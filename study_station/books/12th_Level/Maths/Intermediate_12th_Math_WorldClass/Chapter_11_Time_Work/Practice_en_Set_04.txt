76. A can do a piece of work in 12 days and B can do it in 18 days. If they work together, in how many days will they complete the work?
(a) 6.5 days (b) 8 days (c) 7.2 days (d) 9 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 8 days comes from roughly averaging 12 and 18, but rates, not times, must be combined. A's 1-day work is 1/12 and B's is 1/18; together 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36. Time = 36/5 = 7.2 days, so the answer is 7.2 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (direct and inverse proportions)

77. A and B together can finish a work in 10 days. A alone can finish it in 15 days. In how many days can B alone finish the work?
(a) 25 days (b) 20 days (c) 30 days (d) 35 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students subtract 15 − 10 = 5 and guess near 25, which is wrong because rates, not times, are subtracted. B's 1-day work = 1/10 − 1/15 = 3/30 − 2/30 = 1/30. So B alone takes 30 days, so the answer is 30 days.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-I (PYQ-style)

78. A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a work in 14 days. In how many days can A alone finish it?
(a) 28 days (b) 21 days (c) 18 days (d) 24 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to double 14 to get 28, but that gives B's time, not A's. Let B's rate be x, so A's rate is 2x; combined 3x = 1/14, hence x = 1/42 and A's rate = 2/42 = 1/21. A alone takes 21 days, so the answer is 21 days.
Source: RRB NTPC (PYQ-style)

79. 10 men can complete a work in 12 days. In how many days can 15 men complete the same work?
(a) 8 days (b) 9 days (c) 10 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to use direct proportion and get 18 days; here more men means fewer days, so use inverse proportion. Total work = 10 × 12 = 120 man-days; with 15 men, days = 120/15 = 8. So the answer is 8 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (direct and inverse proportions)

80. A can do a work in 20 days. He works for 5 days and then B finishes the remaining work in 15 days. In how many days can B alone do the whole work?
(a) 25 days (b) 18 days (c) 20 days (d) 30 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to treat B's 15 days as his full time, but B only did the leftover part. In 5 days A completes 5/20 = 1/4 of the work, leaving 3/4. B does 3/4 of the work in 15 days, so full work takes 15 × 4/3 = 20 days. So the answer is 20 days.
Source: SSC CHSL (PYQ-style)

81. A and B can do a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, and A and C in 20 days. In how many days will A, B and C together complete the work?
(a) 15 days (b) 12 days (c) 8 days (d) 10 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Adding the three times and dividing by 3 gives about 15.7 days, a common trap, because rates must be added, not times. Sum of pair rates = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = 5/60 + 4/60 + 3/60 = 12/60 = 1/5, which equals twice the combined rate of all three. Combined rate = 1/10, so all three take 10 days. So the answer is 10 days.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-I (PYQ-style)

82. A can do a work in 16 days and B in 24 days. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?
(a) 14 days (b) 10 days (c) 12 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to compute the remaining fraction and forget to divide by B's rate alone, reporting a fraction instead of days. Combined rate = 1/16 + 1/24 = 3/48 + 2/48 = 5/48; in 4 days they finish 20/48 = 5/12, leaving 7/12. B needs (7/12) ÷ (1/24) = 14 days. So the answer is 14 days.
Source: IBPS PO (PYQ-style)

83. A is 50% more efficient than B. If B can do a work in 30 days, in how many days can A do it?
(a) 18 days (b) 15 days (c) 20 days (d) 25 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to take 50% of 30 and subtract to get 15 days, but efficiency and time are inversely related, not directly. A's rate is 1.5 times B's rate, so A's time = 30/1.5 = 20 days. So the answer is 20 days.
Source: RRB NTPC (PYQ-style)

84. 6 men or 10 women can do a work in 20 days. In how many days can 3 men and 5 women together do the same work?
(a) 18 days (b) 20 days (c) 24 days (d) 15 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to average the two group times, but 3 men and 5 women are exactly half of each full team. 3 men do half of 1/20 per day and 5 women do half of 1/20 per day, so together they do 1/20 per day. Time = 20 days, so the answer is 20 days.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-I (PYQ-style)

85. A pipe can fill a tank in 6 hours and another pipe can empty it in 9 hours. If both are opened together, in how many hours will the tank be filled?
(a) 18 hours (b) 15 hours (c) 12 hours (d) 20 hours
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to add the rates and get a time less than 6 hours, forgetting the second pipe empties the tank. Net rate = 1/6 − 1/9 = 3/18 − 2/18 = 1/18 per hour. The tank fills in 18 hours, so the answer is 18 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (direct and inverse proportions)

86. Two pipes A and B can fill a tank in 12 hours and 16 hours respectively, while pipe C can empty it in 24 hours. If all three are opened together, in how many hours will the tank be filled?
(a) 8 hours (b) 9.6 hours (c) 10 hours (d) 12 hours
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to ignore C and combine only A and B, giving 48/7 ≈ 6.9 hours. Net rate = 1/12 + 1/16 − 1/24 = 8/96 + 6/96 − 4/96 = 10/96 = 5/48. Time = 48/5 = 9.6 hours, so the answer is 9.6 hours.
Source: IBPS Clerk (PYQ-style)

87. A can do a work in 10 days and B in 15 days. They work on alternate days, starting with A. In how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 11 days (b) 13 days (c) 12 days (d) 12.5 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to take the combined time of 6 days and double it blindly without checking the cycle total. In each 2-day cycle, work done = 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6, so 6 cycles = 12 days complete exactly 6 × 1/6 = 1 full work. So the answer is 12 days.
Source: SSC CHSL (PYQ-style)

88. A alone can do a work in 18 days and B alone in 24 days. With the help of C, they finish it in 8 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 48 days (b) 24 days (c) 30 days (d) 36 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to subtract 8 from 18 or 24, which mixes times instead of rates. Combined rate of all three = 1/8; A and B together = 1/18 + 1/24 = 4/72 + 3/72 = 7/72. C's rate = 1/8 − 7/72 = 9/72 − 7/72 = 2/72 = 1/36. So C alone takes 36 days, so the answer is 36 days.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-I (PYQ-style)

89. 12 men can build a wall in 10 days working 8 hours a day. In how many days can 8 men build the same wall working 10 hours a day?
(a) 10 days (b) 12 days (c) 15 days (d) 18 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to adjust only for men and ignore hours, giving 15 days. Total work = 12 × 10 × 8 = 960 man-hours. With 8 men at 10 hours a day, days = 960/(8 × 10) = 12. So the answer is 12 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (direct and inverse proportions)

90. A and B together can do a work in 8 days. B and C together in 12 days, and A, B and C together in 6 days. In how many days can A and C together do the work?
(a) 6 days (b) 10 days (c) 12 days (d) 8 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to average 8 and 12 to get 10, but we must isolate each rate first. C's rate = 1/6 − 1/8 = 1/24; A's rate = 1/6 − 1/12 = 1/12. A and C together = 1/12 + 1/24 = 3/24 = 1/8, so they take 8 days. So the answer is 8 days.
Source: IBPS PO (PYQ-style)

91. A works twice as fast as B and together they finish a work in 8 days. B started the work alone and worked for 6 days; then A joined and they finished the rest together. In how many total days was the work completed?
(a) 10 days (b) 12 days (c) 14 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to add 6 days to the combined time of 8 days and pick 14, ignoring that B's early work reduces the remaining part. Let B's rate be x and A's 2x; 3x = 1/8, so x = 1/24. In 6 days B does 6/24 = 1/4, leaving 3/4. Together they need (3/4) ÷ (1/8) = 6 days; total = 6 + 6 = 12 days. So the answer is 12 days.
Source: RRB NTPC (PYQ-style)

92. A tank is filled by pipe A in 10 hours and by pipe B in 15 hours. Both pipes are opened together but pipe A is closed after 4 hours. In how many more hours will B fill the remaining tank?
(a) 5 hours (b) 6 hours (c) 4 hours (d) 7 hours
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to compute the leftover fraction and report it as hours, or to keep both pipes open in the calculation. In 4 hours, both fill 4 × (1/10 + 1/15) = 4 × 1/6 = 2/3, leaving 1/3. B alone needs (1/3) ÷ (1/15) = 5 hours. So the answer is 5 hours.
Source: SSC CHSL (PYQ-style)

93. 6 men can do a work in 10 days and 9 women can do the same work in 15 days. In how many days can 6 men and 9 women working together complete the work?
(a) 8 days (b) 5 days (c) 6 days (d) 12 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to average 10 and 15 or to add the times, both of which ignore that the two groups work simultaneously. Rate of 6 men = 1/10 per day; rate of 9 women = 1/15 per day. Combined = 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6, so time = 6 days. So the answer is 6 days.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-I (PYQ-style)

94. A can complete a work in 25 days. B is 25% more efficient than A. In how many days will B complete the same work?
(a) 20 days (b) 18 days (c) 22 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to subtract 25% of 25 (about 6) and pick 18 or 19 days, but efficiency and time are inversely proportional. B's rate = 1.25 × A's rate, so B's time = 25/1.25 = 20 days. So the answer is 20 days.
Source: IBPS Clerk (PYQ-style)

95. A and B can do a work in 20 and 30 days respectively. They start together but A leaves 5 days before completion. In how many total days is the work finished?
(a) 15 days (b) 12 days (c) 18 days (d) 14 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to use the combined time of 12 days and add 5, giving 17, which double-counts. Let total time be T days; B works all T days and A works (T − 5) days. So (T − 5)/20 + T/30 = 1; multiplying by 60: 3(T − 5) + 2T = 60, 5T = 75, T = 15. So the answer is 15 days.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-I (PYQ-style)

96. Pipe A can fill a tank in 8 hours. Pipe B can empty the full tank in 12 hours. If the tank is half full and both pipes are opened, in how many hours will the tank be completely filled?
(a) 10 hours (b) 16 hours (c) 8 hours (d) 12 hours
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to compute the full-tank net time of 24 hours and forget that only half the tank needs filling. Net rate = 1/8 − 1/12 = 3/24 − 2/24 = 1/24 per hour. To fill the remaining half: (1/2) ÷ (1/24) = 12 hours. So the answer is 12 hours.
Source: RRB NTPC (PYQ-style)

97. A does half as much work as B in the same time. If together they can finish a work in 12 days, in how many days can B alone finish it?
(a) 20 days (b) 24 days (c) 16 days (d) 18 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to double 12 and pick 24, which actually corresponds to A's time, not B's. Let B's rate be 2x and A's rate be x; combined 3x = 1/12, so x = 1/36 and B's rate = 2/36 = 1/18. B alone takes 18 days, so the answer is 18 days.
Source: SSC CHSL (PYQ-style)

98. 15 workers can complete a project in 24 days. After 8 days, 5 workers leave. In how many total days will the project be completed?
(a) 30 days (b) 32 days (c) 36 days (d) 28 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to scale the whole 24 days by the worker ratio, ignoring that the first 8 days are already done at full strength. Total work = 15 × 24 = 360 worker-days; in 8 days, 120 worker-days are done, leaving 240. With 10 workers, remaining days = 240/10 = 24; total = 8 + 24 = 32 days. So the answer is 32 days.
Source: IBPS PO (PYQ-style)

99. A, B and C can do a work in 10, 20 and 20 days respectively. They work together for 2 days, after which C leaves. In how many more days will A and B finish the remaining work?
(a) 4 days (b) 3 days (c) 5 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to divide the remaining work by the combined rate of all three instead of only A and B. Combined rate of all three = 1/10 + 1/20 + 1/20 = 2/20 + 1/20 + 1/20 = 4/20 = 1/5; in 2 days they finish 2/5, leaving 3/5. A and B together = 1/10 + 1/20 = 3/20, so time = (3/5) ÷ (3/20) = 4 days. So the answer is 4 days.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-I (PYQ-style)

100. A can do a work in 14 days and B in 21 days. They work together, but A works at only half his efficiency. In how many days will they complete the work?
(a) 11 days (b) 10.5 days (c) 9 days (d) 12 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to use A's full rate 1/14, which gives 42/5 = 8.4 days and ignores the reduced efficiency. A's effective rate = 1/28; combined = 1/28 + 1/21 = 3/84 + 4/84 = 7/84 = 1/12. Time = 12 days, so the answer is 12 days.
Source: RRB NTPC (PYQ-style)
