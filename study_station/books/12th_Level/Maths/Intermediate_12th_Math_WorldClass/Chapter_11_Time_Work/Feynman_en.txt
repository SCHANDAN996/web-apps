# The Feynman Technique: Time & Work

### The Great Lego Wall
Imagine building a wall made of 60 Lego bricks.
- Worker A can build the entire 60-brick wall in 10 days. That means A snaps in **6 bricks every day**.
- Worker B can build the 60-brick wall in 15 days. That means B snaps in **4 bricks every day**.

Now, put them in the room together:
In just one single day, A snaps 6 bricks and B snaps 4 bricks. Together, they snap $6 + 4 = 10$ bricks onto the wall!
To finish all 60 bricks:
$60 / 10 = 6$ days! That's it! No fractions, no messy decimals.

### The Magic "Don't Leave!" Trick
What if A gets angry and walks away 2 days before the wall is finished?
Instead of panicking, play a trick on the problem:
Say to A, *"Hey A, pretend you didn't leave! Keep building for those 2 days!"*
In those 2 extra days, A would have snapped $2 \times 6 = 12$ extra bricks.
So add those 12 bricks to the wall: $60 + 12 = 72$ bricks!
Now pretend both A and B worked together from the first second to the last second:
$72 / 10 = 7.2$ days!
The entire problem is solved in five seconds!
