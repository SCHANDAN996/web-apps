Questions 26–50: Medium

26. (Medium) A can do a work in 16 days and B in 12 days. Starting with A, they work on alternate days. In how many days will the work be completed?
(A) 13.75 days  (B) 13.50 days  (C) 14.00 days  (D) 14.25 days
Answer: (A) 13.75 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(16, 12) = 48 units. E_A = 3 units/day, E_B = 4 units/day. In a 2-day cycle, (A + B) do 3 + 4 = 7 units. In 6 full cycles (12 days), 6 * 7 = 42 units are done. Remaining work = 48 - 42 = 6 units. On Day 13, A works and does 3 units. Remaining = 3 units. On Day 14, B does 3/4 of a day's work. Total time = 12 + 1 + 3/4 = 13.75 days. The required value is 13.75 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. (Medium) A can do a work in 16 days and B in 12 days. If B starts the work on the first day and they work on alternate days, in how many days will the work be completed?
(A) 13.75 days  (B) 13.67 days  (C) 14.00 days  (D) 13.50 days
Answer: (B) 13.67 days
Solution: Total work = 48 units. 6 cycles of 2 days = 12 days = 42 units done. Remaining work = 6 units. On Day 13, B works and does 4 units. Remaining = 2 units. On Day 14, A needs 2/3 of a day. Total time = 12 + 1 + 2/3 = 13.67 days (or 13 2/3 days). The required value is 13.67 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

28. (Medium) A, B, and C can do a work in 20, 30, and 60 days respectively. In how many days can A do the work if he is assisted by B and C on every third day?
(A) 12 days  (B) 16 days  (C) 15 days  (D) 18 days
Answer: (C) 15 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(20, 30, 60) = 60 units. E_A = 3, E_B = 2, E_C = 1 unit/day. Day 1: A does 3 units. Day 2: A does 3 units. Day 3: (A + B + C) do 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 units. Work done in a 3-day cycle = 3 + 3 + 6 = 12 units. Cycles required = 60 / 12 = 5 cycles. Total days = 5 * 3 = 15 days. The required value is 15 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

29. (Medium) 3 men or 4 women can complete a work in 43 days. In how many days will 7 men and 5 women complete the same work?
(A) 15 days  (B) 10 days  (C) 14 days  (D) 12 days
Answer: (D) 12 days
Solution: 3 Men = 4 Women => 1 Man = 4/3 Women. Target workforce: 7 Men + 5 Women = 7*(4/3 W) + 5W = 28/3 W + 15/3 W = 43/3 Women. Total work = 4 Women * 43 days = 172 W-days. Time taken = 172 / (43/3) = (172 * 3) / 43 = 4 * 3 = 12 days. The required value is 12 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. (Medium) 12 men can complete a work in 9 days. After they have worked for 6 days, 6 more men join them. How many days will all of them now take to finish the remaining work?
(A) 2 days  (B) 3 days  (C) 4 days  (D) 2.5 days
Answer: (A) 2 days
Solution: Total work = 12 * 9 = 108 man-days. Work completed in 6 days = 12 * 6 = 72 man-days. Remaining work = 108 - 72 = 36 man-days. New number of workers = 12 + 6 = 18 men. Time required = 36 / 18 = 2 days. The required value is 2 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

31. (Medium) A is 30% more efficient than B. How much time will they, working together, take to complete a job which A alone could have done in 23 days?
(A) 11 days  (B) 13 days  (C) 15 days  (D) 12 days
Answer: (B) 13 days
Solution: Efficiency ratio E_A : E_B = 130 : 100 = 13 : 10. Total work = Days of A * E_A = 23 * 13 = 299 units. Combined efficiency = 13 + 10 = 23 units/day. Time required together = 299 / 23 = 13 days. The required value is 13 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

32. (Medium) A can do a piece of work in 80 days. He works by himself for 10 days, and then B alone finishes the remaining work in 42 days. In how many days can both A and B together finish the entire work?
(A) 28 days  (B) 32 days  (C) 30 days  (D) 35 days
Answer: (C) 30 days
Solution: Assume Total work = 80 units => E_A = 1 unit/day. In 10 days, A does 10 units. Remaining work = 80 - 10 = 70 units. B finishes 70 units in 42 days => E_B = 70 / 42 = 5/3 units/day. Combined efficiency = 1 + 5/3 = 8/3 units/day. Time together = 80 / (8/3) = (80 * 3) / 8 = 30 days. The required value is 30 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

33. (Medium) A and B can together finish a work in 30 days. They worked at it for 20 days and then B left. The remaining work was done by A alone in 20 more days. In how many days could A alone finish the whole work?
(A) 50 days  (B) 45 days  (C) 54 days  (D) 60 days
Answer: (D) 60 days
Solution: Work completed in 20 days = 20 / 30 = 2/3 of the work. Remaining work = 1 - 2/3 = 1/3 of the work. A completes 1/3 of the work in 20 days. Time for A alone to complete the whole work = 20 * 3 = 60 days. The required value is 60 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

34. (Medium) 4 men and 6 women can complete a work in 8 days, while 3 men and 7 women can complete it in 10 days. In how many days will 10 women complete it?
(A) 40 days  (B) 35 days  (C) 45 days  (D) 50 days
Answer: (A) 40 days
Solution: 8*(4M + 6W) = 10*(3M + 7W) => 32M + 48W = 30M + 70W => 2M = 22W => 1M = 11W. Total work = 8*(4*11W + 6W) = 8*(50W) = 400 W-days. Time for 10 women = 400 / 10 = 40 days. The required value is 40 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

35. (Medium) A can do a work in 10 days, B in 12 days, and C in 15 days. They all begin together, but A leaves after 2 days and B leaves 3 days before the completion of the work. How long did the entire work last?
(A) 8 days  (B) 7 days  (C) 6 days  (D) 9 days
Answer: (B) 7 days
Solution: Total work = 60 units. E_A = 6, E_B = 5, E_C = 4 units/day. In the first 2 days, all three work: (6 + 5 + 4) * 2 = 30 units. Remaining work = 30 units. For the remaining phase with B and C, since B leaves 3 days before end, add B's 3 days work: Adjusted work = 30 + (3 * 5) = 45 units. Time for second phase = 45 / (5 + 4) = 45 / 9 = 5 days. Total duration = 2 + 5 = 7 days. The required value is 7 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

36. (Medium) A and B can do a piece of work in 45 and 40 days respectively. They began the work together, but A left after some days and B finished the remaining work in 23 days. After how many days did A leave?
(A) 8 days  (B) 10 days  (C) 9 days  (D) 12 days
Answer: (C) 9 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(45, 40) = 360 units. E_A = 8, E_B = 9 units/day. Work done by B alone in the last 23 days = 23 * 9 = 207 units. Work done together before A left = 360 - 207 = 153 units. Days worked together = 153 / (8 + 9) = 153 / 17 = 9 days. The required value is 9 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

37. (Medium) If 6 men and 8 boys can do a work in 10 days, while 26 men and 48 boys can do the same in 2 days, find the time taken by 15 men and 20 boys to do the same work.
(A) 5 days  (B) 6 days  (C) 7 days  (D) 4 days
Answer: (D) 4 days
Solution: 10*(6M + 8B) = 2*(26M + 48B) => 60M + 80B = 52M + 96B => 8M = 16B => 1M = 2B. Total work = 10*(6*2B + 8B) = 200B. Target: 15M + 20B = 15*2B + 20B = 50B. Time required = 200B / 50B = 4 days. The required value is 4 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

38. (Medium) A, B, and C can do a piece of work in 11 days, 20 days, and 55 days respectively. In how many days can the work be completed if A is assisted by B and C on alternate days?
(A) 8 days  (B) 9 days  (C) 10 days  (D) 7 days
Answer: (A) 8 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(11, 20, 55) = 220 units. E_A = 20, E_B = 11, E_C = 4 units/day. Day 1: A + B = 20 + 11 = 31 units. Day 2: A + C = 20 + 4 = 24 units. 2-day cycle = 31 + 24 = 55 units. Number of cycles = 220 / 55 = 4 cycles. Total days = 4 * 2 = 8 days. The required value is 8 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

39. (Medium) 5 men can prepare 10 toys in 6 days working 6 hours a day. In how many days can 12 men prepare 16 toys working 8 hours a day?
(A) 4 days  (B) 3 days  (C) 2 days  (D) 5 days
Answer: (B) 3 days
Solution: Using (M1 * D1 * H1) / W1 = (M2 * D2 * H2) / W2: (5 * 6 * 6) / 10 = (12 * D2 * 8) / 16 => 180 / 10 = 96*D2 / 16 => 18 = 6*D2 => D2 = 3 days. The required value is 3 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

40. (Medium) A does half as much work as B in three-fourth of the time. If together they take 18 days to complete the work, how much time shall B take to do it alone?
(A) 25 days  (B) 36 days  (C) 30 days  (D) 40 days
Answer: (C) 30 days
Solution: A completes 1/2 work in 3/4 of B's time => A completes 1 full work in (3/4 * 2) = 3/2 of B's time. Efficiency ratio E_A : E_B = 1/(3/2) : 1 = 2 : 3. Combined daily efficiency = 2 + 3 = 5 units/day. Total work = 5 * 18 = 90 units. Time for B alone = 90 / 3 = 30 days. The required value is 30 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

41. (Medium) A can do a work in 15 days and B in 20 days. If they work on it together for 4 days, what part of the work is completed?
(A) 8/15  (B) 1/2  (C) 3/5  (D) 7/15
Answer: (D) 7/15
Solution: Total work = 60 units. E_A = 4, E_B = 3 units/day. Combined 4 days' work = (4 + 3) * 4 = 28 units. Fraction completed = 28 / 60 = 7 / 15. The required value is 7/15. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

42. (Medium) 10 men can finish a construction work in 8 days. How many men are needed to finish the same work in half a day?
(A) 160 men  (B) 150 men  (C) 140 men  (D) 180 men
Answer: (A) 160 men
Solution: Using M1 * D1 = M2 * D2: 10 * 8 = M2 * 0.5 => 80 = 0.5 * M2 => M2 = 80 / 0.5 = 160 men. The required value is 160 men. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

43. (Medium) A can do a work in 20 days and B in 25 days. They started together, but B left after 5 days. How many more days will A take to finish the work?
(A) 10 days  (B) 11 days  (C) 12 days  (D) 13 days
Answer: (B) 11 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(20, 25) = 100 units. E_A = 5, E_B = 4 units/day. Work done in 5 days = (5 + 4) * 5 = 45 units. Remaining work = 100 - 45 = 55 units. Additional days for A = 55 / 5 = 11 days. The required value is 11 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

44. (Medium) A is 25% more efficient than B, and B alone takes 25 days to complete a job. In how many days can A and B together finish the job?
(A) 11.00 days  (B) 12.00 days  (C) 11.11 days  (D) 10.50 days
Answer: (C) 11.11 days
Solution: Efficiency ratio E_A : E_B = 125 : 100 = 5 : 4. Total work = 25 * 4 = 100 units. Combined efficiency = 5 + 4 = 9 units/day. Time required together = 100 / 9 = 11.11 days (or 11 1/9 days). The required value is 11.11 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

45. (Medium) A and B together can do a work in 12 days, which B and C can do in 16 days. After A has worked at it for 5 days and B for 7 days, C finishes it in 13 days. In how many days could C alone do the work?
(A) 20 days  (B) 28 days  (C) 30 days  (D) 24 days
Answer: (D) 24 days
Solution: Expressing the work: 5*A + 7*B + 13*C = 5*(A + B) + 2*(B + C) + 11*C = 1. Since (A + B) = 1/12 and (B + C) = 1/16: 5/12 + 2/16 + 11*C = 1 => 5/12 + 1/8 + 11*C = 1 => 13/24 + 11*C = 1 => 11*C = 11/24 => C = 1/24. Time taken by C alone = 24 days. The required value is 24 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

46. (Medium) A work could be completed by a certain number of men in 60 days. If there were 8 more men, it could be completed in 10 days less. What was the original number of men?
(A) 40 men  (B) 35 men  (C) 45 men  (D) 50 men
Answer: (A) 40 men
Solution: M * 60 = (M + 8) * (60 - 10) => 60*M = 50*M + 400 => 10*M = 400 => M = 40 men. The required value is 40 men. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

47. (Medium) A and B can do a piece of work in 30 days and 20 days respectively. If they work on alternate days beginning with A, in how many days will the work be completed?
(A) 25 days  (B) 24 days  (C) 23 days  (D) 26 days
Answer: (B) 24 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(30, 20) = 60 units. E_A = 2, E_B = 3 units/day. In a 2-day cycle, (A + B) do 2 + 3 = 5 units. Total cycles = 60 / 5 = 12 cycles. Total days = 12 * 2 = 24 days. The required value is 24 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

48. (Medium) 8 men can complete a work in 12 days. After 6 days of work, 4 more men join them. How many days will now be needed to complete the remaining work?
(A) 3 days  (B) 5 days  (C) 4 days  (D) 6 days
Answer: (C) 4 days
Solution: Work remaining = 8 men * (12 - 6) days = 48 man-days. New number of men = 8 + 4 = 12 men. Days needed = 48 / 12 = 4 days. The required value is 4 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

49. (Medium) A can do a work in 24 days, B in 9 days, and C in 12 days. B and C start the work together but leave after 3 days. The remaining work was finished by A alone in:
(A) 8 days  (B) 12 days  (C) 9 days  (D) 10 days
Answer: (D) 10 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(24, 9, 12) = 72 units. E_A = 3, E_B = 8, E_C = 6 units/day. Work done by (B + C) in 3 days = (8 + 6) * 3 = 42 units. Remaining work = 72 - 42 = 30 units. Time for A alone = 30 / 3 = 10 days. The required value is 10 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

50. (Medium) A takes twice as much time as B or thrice as much time as C to finish a piece of work. Working together, they can finish the work in 2 days. How long will B alone take to do the work?
(A) 4 days  (B) 6 days  (C) 8 days  (D) 12 days
Answer: (B) 6 days
Solution: Let time taken by A = 6x days => Time for B = 3x days, Time for C = 2x days. Daily efficiencies: E_A = 1, E_B = 2, E_C = 3 units/day. Combined efficiency = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 units/day. Total work = 6 * 2 days = 12 units. Time taken by B alone = 12 / 2 = 6 days. The required value is 6 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
