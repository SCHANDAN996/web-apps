51. A can do a piece of work in 12 days and B can do it in 18 days. They work together for 4 days, after which A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 8 days (d) 7 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 5 days comes from forgetting that A leaves and computing the remaining time as if both still worked together. Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36, so in 4 days they complete 20/36 = 5/9, leaving 4/9. B alone works at 1/18 per day, so B needs (4/9) ÷ (1/18) = 8 days, so the answer is 8 days.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a work in 14 days. In how many days can A alone finish the work?
(a) 28 days (b) 18 days (c) 24 days (d) 21 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 28 days comes from simply doubling 14, which ignores that B also contributes. Let B's rate be x, then A's rate is 2x; together 3x = 1/14, so x = 1/42 and A's rate = 2/42 = 1/21, so the answer is 21 days.
Source: PYQ-style

53. A and B can do a work in 20 and 30 days respectively. They work on alternate days, starting with A. In how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 23 days (b) 24 days (c) 25 days (d) 26 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 25 days comes from averaging the two times or miscounting the last day. In every 2 days the work done is 1/20 + 1/30 = 1/12, so in 22 days (11 pairs) 11/12 is done. On day 23, A adds 1/20, taking the total to 29/30, and on day 24 B adds 1/30, completing the work, so the answer is 24 days.
Source: PYQ-style

54. 12 men can complete a work in 15 days. After working for 5 days, 4 men leave. In how many days will the remaining work be completed?
(a) 12 days (b) 15 days (c) 14 days (d) 13 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 12 days comes from assuming the remaining work still takes 10 days with the reduced workforce. Total work = 12 × 15 = 180 man-days; work done in 5 days = 60 man-days, leaving 120 man-days for 8 men, so days = 120/8 = 15, so the answer is 15 days.
Source: PYQ-style

55. A can do a work in 10 days, B in 15 days and C in 30 days. In how many days will all three together complete the work?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 4 days (d) 7 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 6 days comes from taking an arithmetic average of the three times. Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 + 1/30 = 3/30 + 2/30 + 1/30 = 6/30 = 1/5, so the answer is 5 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (direct and inverse proportions)

56. A pipe can fill a tank in 6 hours and another pipe can empty it in 9 hours. If both are opened together, in how many hours will the tank be filled?
(a) 15 hours (b) 16 hours (c) 18 hours (d) 20 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 15 hours comes from adding 6 and 9, which is meaningless for rates. Net rate = 1/6 − 1/9 = 3/18 − 2/18 = 1/18, so the answer is 18 hours.
Source: PYQ-style

57. A and B together can do a work in 8 days. A alone can do it in 12 days. In how many days can B alone do it?
(a) 20 days (b) 24 days (c) 26 days (d) 22 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 20 days comes from adding or subtracting the given days instead of the rates. B's rate = 1/8 − 1/12 = 3/24 − 2/24 = 1/24, so the answer is 24 days.
Source: PYQ-style

58. 8 men or 12 women can do a work in 25 days. In how many days can 6 men and 11 women do the same work?
(a) 12 days (b) 18 days (c) 15 days (d) 20 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 20 days comes from treating men and women as equally efficient. Since 8 men = 12 women, 6 men = 9 women, giving 9 + 11 = 20 women in total; 12 women take 25 days, so 20 women take (12 × 25)/20 = 15 days, so the answer is 15 days.
Source: PYQ-style

59. A can do a work in 16 days. B is 60% more efficient than A. In how many days can B alone do the work?
(a) 8 days (b) 12 days (c) 9 days (d) 10 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 8 days comes from wrongly halving 16, treating 60% more efficient as twice as fast. B's rate = 1.6 × (1/16) = 1/10, so the answer is 10 days.
Source: PYQ-style

60. A, B and C can do a work in 12, 15 and 20 days respectively. They work together, but C leaves after 2 days. In how many days from the start will the work be completed?
(a) 5 days (b) 8 days (c) 7 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 5 days comes from ignoring C's departure. Combined rate of all three = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = 12/60 = 1/5, so in 2 days they finish 2/5, leaving 3/5 for A and B whose combined rate is 1/12 + 1/15 = 3/20; time = (3/5) ÷ (3/20) = 4 days, total = 2 + 4 = 6 days, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: PYQ-style

61. Two pipes can fill a tank in 20 minutes and 30 minutes respectively, while a third pipe can empty the full tank in 60 minutes. If all three are opened together, in how many minutes will the tank be filled?
(a) 12 minutes (b) 18 minutes (c) 15 minutes (d) 20 minutes
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 12 minutes comes from adding the two filling rates and ignoring the emptying pipe. Net rate = 1/20 + 1/30 − 1/60 = 3/60 + 2/60 − 1/60 = 4/60 = 1/15, so the answer is 15 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

62. A and B together can finish a work in 12 days, while A alone can finish it in 20 days. They work together for 4 days, after which A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?
(a) 15 days (b) 18 days (c) 20 days (d) 24 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 24 days comes from using A's solo time instead of first finding B's rate. B's rate = 1/12 − 1/20 = 5/60 − 3/60 = 1/30; in 4 days together they complete 4/12 = 1/3, leaving 2/3, so B needs (2/3) × 30 = 20 days, so the answer is 20 days.
Source: PYQ-style

63. 15 workers can build a wall in 20 days. How many workers are needed to build the same wall in 12 days?
(a) 20 (b) 22 (c) 28 (d) 25
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 20 comes from wrongly applying direct proportion. Work = 15 × 20 = 300 worker-days, so workers needed = 300/12 = 25, so the answer is 25.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (direct and inverse proportions)

64. A does half as much work as B in the same time, and together they finish a work in 18 days. In how many days can B alone finish it?
(a) 27 days (b) 24 days (c) 30 days (d) 36 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 36 days comes from doubling 18, which ignores A's contribution. Let B's rate be 2x and A's rate be x; together 3x = 1/18, so x = 1/54 and B's rate = 2/54 = 1/27, so the answer is 27 days.
Source: PYQ-style

65. A can complete a work in 25 days. He works for 10 days and then B alone completes the remaining work in 15 days. In how many days can B alone complete the whole work?
(a) 25 days (b) 22 days (c) 20 days (d) 30 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 30 days comes from assuming B worked at double rate for the whole job. In 10 days A does 10/25 = 2/5 of the work, leaving 3/5 which B finishes in 15 days, so B's full time = 15 × (5/3) = 25 days, so the answer is 25 days.
Source: PYQ-style

66. Two pipes can fill a tank in 12 minutes and 18 minutes respectively. Both are opened together, but the first pipe is closed after 4 minutes. In how many more minutes will the tank be full?
(a) 8 minutes (b) 7 minutes (c) 6 minutes (d) 9 minutes
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 6 minutes comes from miscomputing the second pipe's remaining share. Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36, so in 4 minutes 20/36 = 5/9 is filled, leaving 4/9; the second pipe's rate is 1/18, so time = (4/9) × 18 = 8 minutes, so the answer is 8 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

67. A is 50% more efficient than B, and C is 25% more efficient than A. If B alone can do a work in 30 days, in how many days can C alone do it?
(a) 20 days (b) 14 days (c) 18 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 20 days comes from adding the percentages (75%) and applying them directly to 30. A's rate = 1.5 × (1/30) = 1/20, and C's rate = 1.25 × (1/20) = 1/16, so the answer is 16 days.
Source: PYQ-style

68. 6 men and 8 boys can do a work in 10 days, while 26 men and 48 boys can do it in 2 days. In how many days can 15 men and 20 boys do the work?
(a) 5 days (b) 4 days (c) 6 days (d) 7 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 5 days comes from guessing a simple ratio without solving. Let a man's daily work be m and a boy's be b; then 10(6m + 8b) = 2(26m + 48b), giving 8m = 16b, so m = 2b. Total work = 10(12b + 8b) = 200b, and 15 men with 20 boys do 30b + 20b = 50b per day, so days = 200b/50b = 4, so the answer is 4 days.
Source: PYQ-style

69. A and B can do a work in 10 days, B and C in 12 days, and C and A in 15 days. In how many days can A alone do the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 24 days (c) 30 days (d) 40 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 20 days comes from halving a pair time incorrectly. Adding the three pair rates gives 2(A + B + C) = 1/10 + 1/12 + 1/15 = 15/60 = 1/4, so A + B + C = 1/8; A's rate = 1/8 − 1/12 = 1/24, so the answer is 24 days.
Source: PYQ-style

70. A works for 12 days and completes 3/4 of a work. The remaining work is completed by B in 4 days. In how many days can A and B together complete the whole work?
(a) 8 days (b) 6 days (c) 10 days (d) 12 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 12 days comes from using A's full time alone without combining rates. A's full time = 12 × (4/3) = 16 days, and B does 1/4 of the work in 4 days, so B's full time is also 16 days; combined rate = 1/16 + 1/16 = 1/8, so the answer is 8 days.
Source: PYQ-style

71. A alone can do a work in 10 days and B alone in 15 days. They start together, but 2 days before completion A leaves. In how many days is the work finished?
(a) 6 days (b) 6.5 days (c) 8 days (d) 7.2 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 6 days comes from computing only the together-time (1/6 rate gives 6 days) and ignoring A's early exit. In the last 2 days B alone does 2/15 of the work, so the first 13/15 was done together at rate 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6, taking (13/15) × 6 = 5.2 days; total = 5.2 + 2 = 7.2 days, so the answer is 7.2 days.
Source: PYQ-style

72. 4 men can do a work in 12 days working 6 hours a day. In how many days can 8 men do the same work working 9 hours a day?
(a) 3 days (b) 4 days (c) 5 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 6 days comes from using only the number of men and ignoring the hours per day. Total work = 4 × 12 × 6 = 288 man-hours, so days = 288/(8 × 9) = 288/72 = 4, so the answer is 4 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (direct and inverse proportions)

73. A can do a work in 9 days and B in 12 days. They work together for 3 days, then both leave and C finishes the remaining work in 5 days. In how many days can C alone do the whole work?
(a) 12 days (b) 18 days (c) 15 days (d) 10 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 15 days comes from assuming C did half the work in 5 days. Combined rate of A and B = 1/9 + 1/12 = 7/36, so in 3 days they do 21/36 = 7/12, leaving 5/12 which C finishes in 5 days; C's full time = 5 × (12/5) = 12 days, so the answer is 12 days.
Source: PYQ-style

74. A tap can fill a tank in 8 hours. Due to a leak at the bottom, it takes 12 hours to fill. In how many hours can the leak alone empty the full tank?
(a) 16 hours (b) 20 hours (c) 24 hours (d) 30 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 20 hours comes from adding the two times (12 + 8) instead of subtracting rates. Net rate = 1/12 and tap rate = 1/8, so leak rate = 1/8 − 1/12 = 3/24 − 2/24 = 1/24, so the answer is 24 hours.
Source: PYQ-style

75. A, B and C are employed to do a work for Rs 5,292. A and B together finish 13/18 of the work and C finishes the rest. If their wages are divided in proportion to work done, how much does C get?
(a) Rs 1,470 (b) Rs 1,500 (c) Rs 1,620 (d) Rs 1,764
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer Rs 1,764 comes from taking C's share as 6/18 instead of 5/18. C's share of work = 1 − 13/18 = 5/18, so C's wage = (5/18) × 5292 = 5 × 294 = 1470, so the answer is Rs 1,470.
Source: PYQ-style
