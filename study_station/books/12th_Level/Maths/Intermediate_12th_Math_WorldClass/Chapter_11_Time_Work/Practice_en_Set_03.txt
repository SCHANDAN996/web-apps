Questions 51–75: Medium

51. (Medium) A is twice as good a workman as B and together they finish a piece of work in 18 days. In how many days can A alone finish the work?
(A) 27 days  (B) 24 days  (C) 30 days  (D) 36 days
Answer: (A) 27 days
Solution: Ratio of efficiencies of A and B is 2 : 1. Combined efficiency = 2 + 1 = 3 units/day. Total work = 3 * 18 = 54 units. Time taken by A alone = 54 / 2 = 27 days. The required value is 27 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. (Medium) A is 50% more efficient than B. If B alone can complete a work in 30 days, in how many days can they together complete the work?
(A) 15 days  (B) 12 days  (C) 10 days  (D) 18 days
Answer: (B) 12 days
Solution: Efficiency ratio A : B = 150 : 100 = 3 : 2. Total work = Efficiency of B * Days = 2 * 30 = 60 units. Combined efficiency of A and B = 3 + 2 = 5 units/day. Time taken together = 60 / 5 = 12 days. The required value is 12 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

53. (Medium) A can do a work in 12 days and B in 15 days. They undertake to complete the work for Rs. 450. How much should A receive?
(A) Rs. 200  (B) Rs. 225  (C) Rs. 250  (D) Rs. 275
Answer: (C) Rs. 250
Solution: Wages are shared in proportion to work efficiency. Ratio of efficiencies of A and B = 1/12 : 1/15 = 5 : 4. Total parts = 5 + 4 = 9. A's share = (5/9) * 450 = Rs. 250. The required value is Rs. 250. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

54. (Medium) A, B, and C can complete a work in 10, 15, and 30 days respectively. They worked together and received Rs. 1800. What is B's share?
(A) Rs. 900  (B) Rs. 300  (C) Rs. 500  (D) Rs. 600
Answer: (D) Rs. 600
Solution: LCM of 10, 15, 30 = 30 units. Daily efficiencies: A = 3, B = 2, C = 1. Total efficiency = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 units/day. B's share = (2/6) * 1800 = Rs. 600. The required value is Rs. 600. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

55. (Medium) A and B undertook a work for Rs. 880. A can do it in 20 days and B in 25 days. With the help of C, they finish the work in 10 days. How much should C receive?
(A) Rs. 88  (B) Rs. 80  (C) Rs. 96  (D) Rs. 110
Answer: (A) Rs. 88
Solution: Total work = LCM(20, 25, 10) = 100 units. Daily efficiency: A = 5, B = 4. In 10 days, A does 5 * 10 = 50 units, B does 4 * 10 = 40 units. Remaining work done by C = 100 - (50 + 40) = 10 units. Ratio of work done = 50 : 40 : 10 = 5 : 4 : 1. C's share = (1/10) * 880 = Rs. 88. The required value is Rs. 88. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

56. (Medium) A is thrice as efficient as B and is therefore able to finish a piece of work 40 days earlier than B. In how many days can B alone finish the work?
(A) 45 days  (B) 60 days  (C) 50 days  (D) 75 days
Answer: (B) 60 days
Solution: Efficiency ratio A : B = 3 : 1 => Time ratio A : B = 1 : 3. Difference in time = 3 - 1 = 2 units = 40 days => 1 unit = 20 days. Time taken by B alone = 3 * 20 = 60 days. The required value is 60 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

57. (Medium) A is 40% more efficient than B and B is 20% less efficient than C. If A takes 6 days less than C to complete a work, in how many days can B complete the work?
(A) 60 days  (B) 65 days  (C) 70 days  (D) 75 days
Answer: (C) 70 days
Solution: Let efficiency of C = 100. Then efficiency of B = 80. Efficiency of A = 1.4 * 80 = 112. Ratio of efficiencies of A and C = 112 : 100 = 28 : 25. Time ratio A : C = 25 : 28. Difference = 3 units = 6 days => 1 unit = 2 days. Time taken by C = 28 * 2 = 56 days. Total work = 100 * 56 = 5600 units. Time taken by B = 5600 / 80 = 70 days. The required value is 70 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

58. (Medium) A and B together can do a piece of work in 12 days, which B and C together can do in 16 days. After A has worked on it for 5 days and B for 7 days, C finishes it in 13 days. In how many days can C alone complete the work?
(A) 20 days  (B) 30 days  (C) 36 days  (D) 24 days
Answer: (D) 24 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(12, 16) = 48 units. Efficiency: (A + B) = 4 units/day, (B + C) = 3 units/day. Work done = 5A + 7B + 13C = 5(A + B) + 2(B + C) + 11C = 48. Substituting: 5(4) + 2(3) + 11C = 48 => 20 + 6 + 11C = 48 => 11C = 22 => C = 2 units/day. Time taken by C alone = 48 / 2 = 24 days. The required value is 24 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

59. (Medium) A, B, and C can complete a work in 10, 12, and 15 days respectively. They start the work together, but A leaves after 2 days and B leaves 3 days before the completion of the work. For how many days did the total work last?
(A) 7 days  (B) 6 days  (C) 8 days  (D) 9 days
Answer: (A) 7 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(10, 12, 15) = 60 units. Efficiency: A = 6, B = 5, C = 4 units/day. A works for 2 days: 2 * 6 = 12 units. Remaining work = 60 - 12 = 48 units. Let the total time be T days. B works for (T - 3) days and C works for T days: 5(T - 3) + 4T = 48 => 9T - 15 = 48 => 9T = 63 => T = 7 days. The required value is 7 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

60. (Medium) 12 men and 16 boys can do a piece of work in 5 days; 13 men and 24 boys can do it in 4 days. How long will 7 men and 10 boys take to do the work?
(A) 8.50 days  (B) 8.33 days  (C) 7.50 days  (D) 9.00 days
Answer: (B) 8.33 days
Solution: Total work equivalence: 5 * (12M + 16B) = 4 * (13M + 24B) => 60M + 80B = 52M + 96B => 8M = 16B => 1M = 2B. Total work = 5 * (12*2 + 16) = 5 * 40 = 200 boy-days. Target team: 7M + 10B = 7(2) + 10 = 24 boys. Time taken = 200 / 24 = 25/3 = 8.33 days. The required value is 8.33 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

61. (Medium) A can do a piece of work in 24 days. B is 20% more efficient than A. If C can do the work in 10 days less than B, in how many days can B and C together finish the work?
(A) 6.25 days  (B) 7.50 days  (C) 6.67 days  (D) 8.00 days
Answer: (C) 6.67 days
Solution: Ratio of efficiencies of B to A = 120 : 100 = 6 : 5. Time taken by B = 24 * (5/6) = 20 days. Time taken by C = 20 - 10 = 10 days. Total work = LCM(20, 10) = 20 units. Daily efficiency: B = 1, C = 2. Combined efficiency = 1 + 2 = 3 units/day. Time taken together = 20 / 3 = 6.67 days. The required value is 6.67 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

62. (Medium) A and B together can do a piece of work in 8 days, B and C in 12 days, and A, B, and C together in 6 days. In how many days can A and C together complete the work?
(A) 6 days  (B) 10 days  (C) 12 days  (D) 8 days
Answer: (D) 8 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(8, 12, 6) = 24 units. Daily efficiency: A + B + C = 4 units/day. Efficiency of C = (A + B + C) - (A + B) = 4 - 3 = 1 unit/day. Efficiency of A = (A + B + C) - (B + C) = 4 - 2 = 2 units/day. Combined efficiency of A and C = 2 + 1 = 3 units/day. Time taken by A and C together = 24 / 3 = 8 days. The required value is 8 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

63. (Medium) Two workers A and B are engaged to do a piece of work. Working alone, A takes 8 hours more than the time taken by A and B together. B takes 4.5 hours more than the time taken by A and B together. In how many hours will they complete the work together?
(A) 6 hours  (B) 5 hours  (C) 7.5 hours  (D) 8 hours
Answer: (A) 6 hours
Solution: If A takes (T + a) days and B takes (T + b) days, the time taken together is T = sqrt(a * b). Here, a = 8 and b = 4.5. T = sqrt(8 * 4.5) = sqrt(36) = 6 hours. The required value is 6 hours. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

64. (Medium) A, B, and C can complete a work together in 15 days. If the efficiency of A is 50% more than that of B, and B is twice as efficient as C, in how many days can A alone complete the work?
(A) 25 days  (B) 30 days  (C) 35 days  (D) 40 days
Answer: (B) 30 days
Solution: Let efficiency of C = 2 units/day. Then efficiency of B = 2 * 2 = 4 units/day. Efficiency of A = 1.5 * 4 = 6 units/day. Total efficiency = 6 + 4 + 2 = 12 units/day. Total work = 12 * 15 = 180 units. Time taken by A alone = 180 / 6 = 30 days. The required value is 30 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

65. (Medium) A can do a piece of work in 14 days and B in 21 days. They begin together, but 3 days before the completion of the work, A leaves. For how many days was the work carried on?
(A) 9.6 days  (B) 10.5 days  (C) 10.2 days  (D) 11.0 days
Answer: (C) 10.2 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(14, 21) = 42 units. Efficiency: A = 3, B = 2 units/day. In the last 3 days, only B works: 3 * 2 = 6 units. Work done together before A left = 42 - 6 = 36 units. Days worked together = 36 / (3 + 2) = 36 / 5 = 7.2 days. Total time = 7.2 + 3 = 10.2 days. The required value is 10.2 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

66. (Medium) A, B, and C together can complete a work in 12 days. A is twice as efficient as B, and C alone can complete the work in 36 days. In how many days can B alone complete the work?
(A) 48 days  (B) 60 days  (C) 36 days  (D) 54 days
Answer: (D) 54 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(12, 36) = 36 units. Combined efficiency (A + B + C) = 36 / 12 = 3 units/day. Efficiency of C = 36 / 36 = 1 unit/day. Combined efficiency (A + B) = 3 - 1 = 2 units/day. Given A = 2B => 2B + B = 2 => 3B = 2 => B = 2/3 unit/day. Time taken by B alone = 36 / (2/3) = (36 * 3) / 2 = 54 days. The required value is 54 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

67. (Medium) A can do 2/5 of a work in 6 days and B can do 1/3 of the same work in 10 days. In how many days can both A and B together do 4/5 of the work?
(A) 8 days  (B) 9 days  (C) 10 days  (D) 6 days
Answer: (A) 8 days
Solution: Time taken by A for full work = 6 * (5/2) = 15 days. Time taken by B for full work = 10 * 3 = 30 days. Total work = LCM(15, 30) = 30 units. Daily efficiency: A = 2, B = 1. Combined efficiency = 3 units/day. Target work = (4/5) * 30 = 24 units. Time taken = 24 / 3 = 8 days. The required value is 8 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

68. (Medium) A and B can separately do a piece of work in 20 and 30 days respectively. They started the work together, but B left after some days and A finished the remaining work in 5 days. For how many days did B work?
(A) 8 days  (B) 9 days  (C) 10 days  (D) 7 days
Answer: (B) 9 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units. Efficiency: A = 3, B = 2 units/day. Work done by A alone in last 5 days = 5 * 3 = 15 units. Work done by A and B together = 60 - 15 = 45 units. Combined efficiency = 3 + 2 = 5 units/day. Time worked together = 45 / 5 = 9 days. Thus, B worked for 9 days. The required value is 9 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

69. (Medium) Daily wages of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 5. When A works for 15 days and B works for 12 days, they together earn Rs. 3150. What is the daily wage of B?
(A) Rs. 90  (B) Rs. 120  (C) Rs. 150  (D) Rs. 180
Answer: (C) Rs. 150
Solution: Let daily wages of A and B be 3x and 5x respectively. Total earnings = 15 * (3x) + 12 * (5x) = 45x + 60x = 105x. Given 105x = 3150 => x = 30. Daily wage of B = 5 * 30 = Rs. 150. The required value is Rs. 150. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

70. (Medium) A and B can do a piece of work in 45 and 40 days respectively. They began the work together, but A left after some days and B finished the remaining work in 23 days. After how many days did A leave?
(A) 10 days  (B) 8 days  (C) 12 days  (D) 9 days
Answer: (D) 9 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(45, 40) = 360 units. Daily efficiency: A = 8, B = 9 units/day. Work done by B alone in last 23 days = 23 * 9 = 207 units. Work done together = 360 - 207 = 153 units. Combined efficiency = 8 + 9 = 17 units/day. Days they worked together = 153 / 17 = 9 days. Thus, A left after 9 days. The required value is 9 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

71. (Medium) A sum of Rs. 770 is to be divided among A, B, and C such that A's share is 2/9 of B and C's combined share. What is A's share?
(A) Rs. 140  (B) Rs. 150  (C) Rs. 130  (D) Rs. 160
Answer: (A) Rs. 140
Solution: Ratio of A : (B + C) = 2 : 9. Total parts = 2 + 9 = 11 parts. 11 parts = Rs. 770 => 1 part = Rs. 70. A's share = 2 * 70 = Rs. 140. The required value is Rs. 140. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

72. (Medium) A works 3 times as fast as B. If A and B together can complete a work in 12 days, in how many days can B alone complete half of the work?
(A) 36 days  (B) 24 days  (C) 48 days  (D) 18 days
Answer: (B) 24 days
Solution: Efficiency ratio A : B = 3 : 1. Combined efficiency = 3 + 1 = 4 units/day. Total work = 4 * 12 = 48 units. Half of the work = 48 / 2 = 24 units. Time taken by B alone = 24 / 1 = 24 days. The required value is 24 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

73. (Medium) A and B together can finish a work in 30 days. They worked together for 20 days and then B left. The remaining work was done by A alone in 20 more days. In how many days can A alone complete the entire work?
(A) 50 days  (B) 45 days  (C) 60 days  (D) 75 days
Answer: (C) 60 days
Solution: Work done by (A + B) in 20 days = 20 / 30 = 2/3 of work. Remaining work = 1 - 2/3 = 1/3 of work. A completes 1/3 of the work in 20 days. Therefore, time taken by A alone for full work = 20 * 3 = 60 days. The required value is 60 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

74. (Medium) A can do a work in 25 days and B can do it in 20 days. They work together for 5 days and then A goes away. In how many days will B finish the remaining work?
(A) 10 days  (B) 12 days  (C) 9 days  (D) 11 days
Answer: (D) 11 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(25, 20) = 100 units. Daily efficiency: A = 4, B = 5 units/day. Work done in 5 days = 5 * (4 + 5) = 45 units. Remaining work = 100 - 45 = 55 units. Time taken by B to finish remaining work = 55 / 5 = 11 days. The required value is 11 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

75. (Medium) A is twice as efficient as B and C is thrice as efficient as B. If all three working together can complete a job in 5 days, how many days will C take to finish the job alone?
(A) 15 days  (B) 10 days  (C) 12 days  (D) 8 days
Answer: (B) 10 days
Solution: Let efficiency of B = 1 unit/day. Then efficiency of A = 2 units/day and C = 3 units/day. Combined efficiency = 2 + 1 + 3 = 6 units/day. Total work = 6 * 5 = 30 units. Time taken by C alone = 30 / 3 = 10 days. The required value is 10 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
