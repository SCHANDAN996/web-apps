1. A can complete a piece of work in 10 days. What part of the work does he complete in 1 day?
(a) 1/5 (b) 1/10 (c) 1/20 (d) 10
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 10, which is the total number of days, not the daily work. One day's work is always the reciprocal of the total days, so one day's work is 1/10, so the answer is 1/10.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

2. B can do a work in 20 days. In how many days will he complete half of the work?
(a) 10 days (b) 5 days (c) 15 days (d) 40 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students pick 40 days by doubling instead of halving. Since B does 1/20 of the work daily, half the work (1/2) needs (1/2) ÷ (1/20) = 10 days, so the answer is 10 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

3. A can finish a work in 12 days and B can finish the same work in 12 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish it?
(a) 6 days (b) 12 days (c) 24 days (d) 3 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 24 days, obtained by adding the days, which is wrong because days are never added — rates are. Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/12 = 2/12 = 1/6, so they finish in 6 days, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

4. A can do a work in 6 days and B can do it in 12 days. In how many days will they complete the work together?
(a) 4 days (b) 8 days (c) 9 days (d) 18 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 9 days, the average of 6 and 12, but averaging days is invalid. Combined rate = 1/6 + 1/12 = 3/12 = 1/4, so the work is done in 4 days, so the answer is 4 days.

5. If 5 men can complete a work in 8 days, in how many days will 10 men complete the same work?
(a) 4 days (b) 16 days (c) 8 days (d) 2 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 16 days, which comes from direct proportion, but men and days are inversely proportional. Total work = 5 × 8 = 40 man-days, so 10 men need 40/10 = 4 days, so the answer is 4 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

6. A can do a piece of work in 15 days. How much work will he do in 5 days?
(a) 1/5 (b) 1/2 (c) 1/3 (d) 3/4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 1/5, which confuses the days worked with the fraction done. In 5 days A completes 5 × (1/15) = 5/15 = 1/3 of the work, so the answer is 1/3.

7. A and B together can do a work in 12 days. A alone can do it in 20 days. In how many days can B alone do it?
(a) 8 days (b) 32 days (c) 30 days (d) 25 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 8 days, found by subtracting 12 from 20, but days cannot be subtracted — only rates can. B's rate = 1/12 − 1/20 = 5/60 − 3/60 = 2/60 = 1/30, so B alone takes 30 days, so the answer is 30 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

(a) 1/6 (b) 2/3 (c) 1/2 (d) 1/3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 1/6, which is only one hour's work. In 2 hours the pipe fills 2 × (1/6) = 1/3 of the tank, so the answer is 1/3.

9. 8 workers can build a wall in 15 days. How many workers are needed to build it in 10 days?
(a) 10 (b) 16 (c) 14 (d) 12
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 10 workers, matching the 10 days, but this ignores inverse proportion. Total work = 8 × 15 = 120 worker-days, so workers needed = 120/10 = 12, so the answer is 12.

10. A can do a work in 4 days and B can do it in 4 days. Together they will do it in:
(a) 8 days (b) 2 days (c) 4 days (d) 1 day
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 8 days from adding the days, which is never correct. Combined rate = 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2, so together they take 2 days, so the answer is 2 days.

11. A can complete a work in 30 days. He works for 10 days. What fraction of the work is left?
(a) 1/3 (b) 2/3 (c) 1/2 (d) 3/4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 1/3, which is the work done, not the work left. Work done in 10 days = 10/30 = 1/3, so work left = 1 − 1/3 = 2/3, so the answer is 2/3.

12. A tap can fill a tank in 10 hours and another tap can fill it in 15 hours. Both taps together will fill the tank in:
(a) 25 hours (b) 6 hours (c) 12.5 hours (d) 5 hours
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 12.5 hours, the average of 10 and 15, but averaging times is wrong. Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6, so the tank fills in 6 hours, so the answer is 6 hours.

13. If a man does 1/8 of a work in 1 day, in how many days will he complete the whole work?
(a) 4 days (b) 6 days (c) 8 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 16 days from doubling without reason. Total days = reciprocal of daily work = 1 ÷ (1/8) = 8 days, so the answer is 8 days.

14. A and B together finish a work in 6 days. If A alone finishes it in 10 days, B's one day work is:
(a) 1/4 (b) 1/10 (c) 1/6 (d) 1/15
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 1/4, from subtracting the days (10 − 6), which is invalid. B's rate = 1/6 − 1/10 = 5/30 − 3/30 = 2/30 = 1/15, so the answer is 1/15.

15. 12 men can reap a field in 10 days. In how many days will 6 men reap the same field?
(a) 5 days (b) 20 days (c) 15 days (d) 24 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 5 days, from halving the days along with the men, but fewer men need more days. Total work = 12 × 10 = 120 man-days, so 6 men need 120/6 = 20 days, so the answer is 20 days.

16. A can do a work in 5 days. B can do the same work in 10 days. Who is more efficient and by what ratio of daily work?
(a) A, 1:2 (b) B, 2:1 (c) A, 2:1 (d) Both equal
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is "Both equal" since both finish the same work, but they take different times. A's daily work is 1/5 and B's is 1/10, and 1/5 : 1/10 = 2:1, so the answer is A, 2:1.

Solution: The tempting wrong option is 1/3, the work completed, but the question asks what remains. Work done = 6/18 = 1/3, so work remaining = 1 − 1/3 = 2/3, so the answer is 2/3.

20. A and B can do a work in 8 days and 24 days respectively. Working together, they finish it in:
(a) 6 days (b) 12 days (c) 16 days (d) 4 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 16 days, the average of 8 and 24, which is a classic trap. Combined rate = 1/8 + 1/24 = 3/24 + 1/24 = 4/24 = 1/6, so they finish in 6 days, so the answer is 6 days.

21. A can do a work in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work together for 4 days. What fraction of the work is left?
(a) 2/3 (b) 5/9 (c) 1/3 (d) 4/9
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 5/9, which is the work done, not the work left. Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36; in 4 days they do 20/36 = 5/9, so work left = 1 − 5/9 = 4/9, so the answer is 4/9.

22. A can do a work in 10 days and B in 15 days. They work together for 3 days, after which A leaves. In how many days will B finish the remaining work?
(a) 5 days (b) 9 days (c) 7.5 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 6 days, found by giving B the whole 15 days minus 3, ignoring that A also worked. Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6; in 3 days they complete 1/2, leaving 1/2. B needs (1/2) ÷ (1/15) = 7.5 days, so the answer is 7.5 days.
Source: PYQ-style

23. A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a work in 14 days. In how many days can A alone finish it?
(a) 28 days (b) 21 days (c) 42 days (d) 18 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 28 days, which is actually B's time, not A's. Let B's rate be x, so A's rate is 2x; then 3x = 1/14, giving x = 1/42. A's rate = 2/42 = 1/21, so A alone takes 21 days, so the answer is 21 days.
Source: PYQ-style

24. A and B can do a work in 20 and 30 days respectively. They start together, but A leaves 5 days before completion. In how many total days is the work finished?
(a) 18 days (b) 12 days (c) 15 days (d) 10 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 12 days, the simple combined time (1/20 + 1/30 = 1/12), which ignores A leaving early. Let total time be T days; B works all T days and A works (T − 5) days: T/30 + (T − 5)/20 = 1. Multiplying by 60: 2T + 3T − 15 = 60, so 5T = 75 and T = 15, so the answer is 15 days.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A pipe can fill a tank in 12 hours while a leak at the bottom can empty it in 20 hours. With the leak open, in how many hours will the tank be full?
(a) 15 hours (b) 32 hours (c) 8 hours (d) 30 hours
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 8 hours, from subtracting the times (20 − 12), but times cannot be subtracted — only rates. Net rate = 1/12 − 1/20 = 5/60 − 3/60 = 2/60 = 1/30, so the tank fills in 30 hours, so the answer is 30 hours.
Source: PYQ-style
