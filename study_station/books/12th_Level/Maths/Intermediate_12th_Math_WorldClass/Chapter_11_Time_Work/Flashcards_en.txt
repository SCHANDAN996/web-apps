Card 1
Front: If A can finish a job in 10 days, how much work does A do in one day?
Back: 1/10 of the work. One day's work = 1 ÷ (total days).

Card 2
Front: A does a job in 12 days and B in 6 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish?
Back: 4 days. Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/6 = 1/4, so time = 4 days.

Card 3
Front: What is the LCM shortcut for "A in x days, B in y days, together in ? days"?
Back: Take LCM of x and y as total work units. Together time = LCM ÷ (sum of daily units).

Card 4
Front: What does the mnemonic "W = R × T" stand for in Time & Work?
Back: Work = Rate × Time. Fix any two, find the third — the master formula of the chapter.

Card 5
Front: If A is twice as efficient as B, and B alone takes 20 days, how long does A take?
Back: 10 days. Double efficiency → half the time (efficiency and time are inversely proportional).

Card 6
Front: A and B together finish a job in 12 days; A alone in 20 days. How long does B alone take?
Back: 30 days. B's rate = 1/12 − 1/20 = 1/30.

Card 7
Front: What is the trick to remember "efficiency ↑ means time ↓"?
Back: Mnemonic: "Fast runner, short race." More efficiency always shrinks the time — they move opposite.

Card 8
Front: A can do a job in 15 days. He works for 5 days and leaves. What fraction of work remains?
Back: 2/3. Work done = 5 × (1/15) = 1/3, so remaining = 2/3.

Card 9
Front: If 6 men build a wall in 10 days, how many days will 15 men take (same efficiency)?
Back: 4 days. Men × Days is constant: 6 × 10 = 15 × D → D = 4.

Card 10
Front: What is the formula when A and B work together but A leaves after some days?
Back: Split it: (days together × combined rate) + (remaining days × B's rate) = 1 full work.

Card 11
Front: Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours, pipe B empties it in 12 hours. Net filling time when both are open?
Back: 12 hours. Net rate = 1/6 − 1/12 = 1/12.

Card 12
Front: What mnemonic helps you remember that an outlet pipe's rate is subtracted?
Back: Mnemonic: "Inlet ADDS, Outlet ATTACKS." Filling pipes get +, emptying pipes get −.

Card 13
Front: A works in 8 days, B in 12 days. They work on alternate days, A starting. When is the work done?
Back: In 9.5 days (work finishes on day 10, halfway through B's turn). Per 2 days they do 1/8 + 1/12 = 5/24; after 8 days 20/24 done, then A does 3/24 on day 9, B finishes the last 1/24 in half a day.

Card 14
Front: If A can do a job in x days and B in y days, what is the direct formula for time taken together?
Back: xy / (x + y) days — "product over sum."

Card 15
Front: Wages of ₹900 are shared between A and B who worked in the ratio of efficiency 2 : 1. What does A get?
Back: ₹600. Wages are divided in the ratio of work done (efficiency), so A gets 2/3 × 900.

Card 16
Front: What is the one-line trick for "M₁D₁H₁ = M₂D₂H₂" problems?
Back: Mnemonic: "Men-Days-Hours stay glued." Total man-hours for a fixed job never change — balance both sides.

Card 17
Front: A is 50% more efficient than B. If B takes 18 days, how long does A take?
Back: 12 days. Efficiency ratio 3 : 2, so time ratio 2 : 3 → A takes (2/3) × 18 = 12 days.

Card 18
Front: Three workers A, B, C take 10, 15 and 30 days respectively. Together, how long?
Back: 5 days. Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 + 1/30 = 6/30 = 1/5.

Card 19
Front: In "work and wages" problems, what is the golden rule for dividing payment?
Back: Payment is shared in the ratio of work contributed — more work, more money.

Card 20
Front: A does half the work in 6 days. In how many days will he complete the full work?
Back: 12 days. Half work in 6 days → full work needs double the time.
