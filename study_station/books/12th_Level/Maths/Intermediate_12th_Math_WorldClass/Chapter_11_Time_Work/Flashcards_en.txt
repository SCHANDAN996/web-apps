# Flashcards: Time & Work

CARD 1:
Front: How is Total Work determined in the LCM efficiency method?
Back: Total Work is assumed to be the LCM of the days taken by individual workers.

CARD 2:
Front: If A completes a work in x days and B in y days, what is the joint time?
Back: T = (x * y) / (x + y) days.

CARD 3:
Front: What is the relationship between efficiency and time for a fixed work?
Back: Efficiency is inversely proportional to time: E_A : E_B = T_B : T_A.

CARD 4:
Front: What is the golden trick when a person leaves 'd' days before completion?
Back: Add the person's potential work (d * E_person) to the Total Work, then divide by the combined efficiency of all workers.

CARD 5:
Front: State the universal chain rule formula for multi-variable work.
Back: (M1 * D1 * H1 * E1) / W1 = (M2 * D2 * H2 * E2) / W2.

CARD 6:
Front: If A takes 'a' days more than (A+B) together, and B takes 'b' days more than (A+B) together, what is the time taken by (A+B)?
Back: T = sqrt(a * b) days.

CARD 7:
Front: How are wages distributed among workers who work for different number of days?
Back: Strictly in proportion to the total work done by each: (E_i * t_i).

CARD 8:
Front: If 3 Men or 5 Women can do a work in 12 days, what is the ratio of their efficiencies?
Back: 3M = 5W => M / W = 5 / 3. Efficiency ratio is 5 : 3.

CARD 9:
Front: If A is 50% more efficient than B, what is the ratio of time taken by A and B?
Back: E_A : E_B = 150 : 100 = 3 : 2 => T_A : T_B = 2 : 3.

CARD 10:
Front: What is a work cycle in alternate-day work problems?
Back: The set of days required for all participants to take exactly one turn in sequence.
