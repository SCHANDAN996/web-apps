126. A can do a piece of work in 12 days, B in 15 days and C in 20 days. They work together for 2 days, then A leaves. B and C continue for 3 more days, then B leaves. In how many total days from the start will the work be finished?
(a) 10 days (b) 8 days (c) 9 days (d) 11 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to forget that C works in every phase. First 2 days: 2 × (1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20) = 2 × 1/5 = 2/5. Next 3 days: 3 × (1/15 + 1/20) = 3 × 7/60 = 7/20. Done = 8/20 + 7/20 = 15/20, leaving 1/4 for C alone at 1/20 per day, i.e. 5 days. Total = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 days, so the answer is 10 days.
Source: PYQ-style

127. A and B together can do a work in 8 days, and A alone can do it in 12 days. They work on alternate days, A starting. In how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 16 days (b) 15 days (c) 14 days (d) 15½ days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to use the combined rate 1/8 for every single day. B's rate = 1/8 − 1/12 = 1/24, so each 2-day pair completes 1/12 + 1/24 = 1/8. After 7 pairs (14 days), 7/8 is done; on day 15 A adds 1/12, reaching 23/24; on day 16 B does exactly the remaining 1/24 in a full day. So the answer is 16 days.
Source: PYQ-style

128. A is twice as efficient as B, and C is twice as efficient as A. If B alone can do the work in 42 days, in how many days will A, B and C together finish it?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 7 days (d) 8 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to add the time periods instead of the efficiencies. Take B's rate as 1 unit/day; then A = 2 and C = 4 units/day, and total work = 42 × 1 = 42 units. Combined rate = 7 units/day, giving 42/7 = 6 days, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Direct and Inverse Proportions)

129. 12 men can complete a work in 18 days. After working for 6 days, 4 more men join. In how many total days will the work be completed?
(a) 16 days (b) 15 days (c) 16½ days (d) 15¾ days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to finish the remaining work with 12 men instead of 16. Total work = 12 × 18 = 216 man-days; done in 6 days = 72, leaving 144 man-days. With 16 men this takes 144/16 = 9 days. Total = 6 + 9 = 15 days, so the answer is 15 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Direct and Inverse Proportions)

130. A, B and C can do a work in 10, 12 and 15 days respectively. They start together, but A leaves after 2 days and B leaves 3 days before completion. In how many days is the work finished?
(a) 7 days (b) 6 days (c) 6⅔ days (d) 7⅓ days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to subtract B's 3 absent days directly from the answer. Let total time be T days: A works 2 days, B works (T − 3) days, C works T days, so 2/10 + (T − 3)/12 + T/15 = 1. Multiplying by 60: 12 + 5T − 15 + 4T = 60, giving 9T = 63 and T = 7, so the answer is 7 days.
Source: PYQ-style

131. A pipe can fill a tank in 6 hours and another can empty it in 9 hours. They are opened alternately for 1 hour each, starting with the filling pipe. In how many hours will the tank be full?
(a) 30 hours (b) 33 hours (c) 32 hours (d) 31 hours
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to use the net rate 1/6 − 1/9 = 1/18 and answer 36 hours, ignoring the alternation. Each 2-hour cycle adds 1/18, so after 15 cycles (30 hours) the tank holds 15/18 = 5/6. In hour 31 the filling pipe adds 1/6, making exactly 5/6 + 1/6 = 1, so the answer is 31 hours.
Source: PYQ-style

132. A and B can do a work in 20 and 30 days respectively. They work together, but A leaves 5 days before completion. If the total payment is ₹3000, what is B's share?
(a) ₹1200 (b) ₹1800 (c) ₹1500 (d) ₹2000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to split the money in the efficiency ratio 3:2, ignoring that A left early. If total time is T, then (T − 5)/20 + T/30 = 1, giving 3T − 15 + 2T = 60, so T = 15. A worked 10 days completing 10/20 = 1/2 and B worked 15 days completing 15/30 = 1/2, so the work — and the pay — is shared equally. B's share is ₹1500, so the answer is ₹1500.
Source: PYQ-style

133. 2 men or 3 women can do a work in 20 days. In how many days will 4 men and 3 women together do the same work?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 6⅔ days (d) 7 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to read "2 men or 3 women" as "2 men and 3 women". One man's daily work = 1/40 and one woman's = 1/60. Combined rate = 4/40 + 3/60 = 1/10 + 1/20 = 3/20, so time = 20/3 = 6⅔ days, so the answer is 6⅔ days.
Source: PYQ-style

134. A can do a work in 25 days and B is 25% more efficient than A. They work together for 5 days, then C joins and the work finishes in 5 more days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 25 days (b) 40 days (c) 30 days (d) 50 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to take B's time as 25 × 1.25 = 31.25 days; higher efficiency means less time, so B takes 25/1.25 = 20 days. In 10 days A completes 10/25 = 2/5 and B completes 10/20 = 1/2, together 9/10. C does the remaining 1/10 in 5 days, i.e. 1/50 per day, so the answer is 50 days.
Source: PYQ-style

135. Pipe A can fill a tank in 10 hours and pipe B in 15 hours. A is opened at 8 AM and B at 10 AM. At what time will the tank be full?
(a) 3:12 PM (b) 3:00 PM (c) 2:30 PM (d) 2:48 PM
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to compute the combined time from 8 AM for both pipes, ignoring B's late start. By 10 AM, A alone fills 2/10 = 1/5, leaving 4/5. Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6 per hour, so the rest takes (4/5) ÷ (1/6) = 24/5 = 4 hours 48 minutes after 10 AM, so the answer is 2:48 PM.
Source: PYQ-style

136. A does half as much work as B in three-fourths of the time. If together they take 18 days, how many days will B take alone?
(a) 45 days (b) 36 days (c) 40 days (d) 30 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to invert the efficiency ratio. In equal time A does (1/2) ÷ (3/4) = 2/3 of B's work, so A : B = 2 : 3. Together they do 5 parts per day and finish in 18 days, so total work = 90 parts; B alone does 3 parts/day, taking 90/3 = 30 days, so the answer is 30 days.
Source: PYQ-style

137. A, B and C working together earn ₹740 per day. A and B together earn ₹520 per day, and B and C together earn ₹450 per day. If the work lasts 10 days, what is C's total earning?
(a) ₹2000 (b) ₹2400 (c) ₹2200 (d) ₹1800
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to compute C = 740 − 450 = 290, which subtracts the wrong pair. C's daily earning = (A + B + C) − (A + B) = 740 − 520 = ₹220. Over 10 days C earns 220 × 10 = ₹2200, so the answer is ₹2200.
Source: PYQ-style

138. A can build a wall in 12 days while B can demolish it in 18 days. If both work together, in how many days will the wall be fully built?
(a) 24 days (b) 30 days (c) 40 days (d) 36 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to add the rates as if both build. Net rate = 1/12 − 1/18 = 3/36 − 2/36 = 1/36 per day, so the wall is built in 36 days, so the answer is 36 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Direct and Inverse Proportions)

139. 8 men and 12 women can do a work in 10 days, while 6 men and 8 women can do it in 14 days. In how many days will 4 men and 6 women do it?
(a) 20 days (b) 18 days (c) 24 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to assume men and women are equally efficient. From 10(8m + 12w) = 14(6m + 8w) we get 80m + 120w = 84m + 112w, so m = 2w. Total work = 10(16w + 12w) = 280w, and 4 men + 6 women = 8w + 6w = 14w per day, giving 280/14 = 20 days, so the answer is 20 days.
Source: PYQ-style

140. A takes twice as long as B and thrice as long as C to do a work. Working together they finish in 2 days. How long would B take alone?
(a) 9 days (b) 6 days (c) 8 days (d) 12 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to reverse the relation and take B's time as twice A's. Let A take 6x days; then B takes 3x and C takes 2x. Combined rate = 1/6x + 1/3x + 1/2x = 6/6x = 1/x = 1/2, so x = 2 and B takes 3x = 6 days, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: PYQ-style

141. A tap can fill a tank in 6 hours, but a leak at the bottom can empty the full tank in 10 hours. If both are open, in how many hours will the tank be filled?
(a) 12 hours (b) 15 hours (c) 18 hours (d) 20 hours
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to average 6 and 10 or to add the rates. Net rate = 1/6 − 1/10 = 5/30 − 3/30 = 2/30 = 1/15 per hour, so the tank fills in 15 hours, so the answer is 15 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Direct and Inverse Proportions)

142. A and B can do a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, and C and A in 20 days. In how many days will A, B and C together complete the work?
(a) 8 days (b) 10 days (c) 9 days (d) 12 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to treat 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 as the combined rate of all three; that sum counts each person twice. Sum of pair rates = 12/60 = 1/5, so the triple rate = 1/10 per day, giving 10 days, so the answer is 10 days.

143. 40 men can do a work in 30 days. They start together, but at the end of every 10 days 5 men leave. In how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 40 days (b) 38 days (c) 42 days (d) 36 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to use the average number of men over the whole period. Total work = 1200 man-days. First 10 days: 40 × 10 = 400; next 10: 35 × 10 = 350; next 10: 30 × 10 = 300; total 1050. Remaining 150 man-days with 25 men take 6 days, so total = 36 days, so the answer is 36 days.

144. A is thrice as fast as B and finishes a work 40 days earlier than B. In how many days can they finish it working together?
(a) 24 days (b) 18 days (c) 15 days (d) 20 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to take the time ratio as 3:1; efficiency 3:1 means times are 1:3. The difference of 2 parts equals 40 days, so A takes 20 days and B takes 60 days. Together: 1/20 + 1/60 = 4/60 = 1/15, so the answer is 15 days.
Source: PYQ-style

145. Pipes A and B can fill a tank in 20 and 30 minutes respectively. Both are opened together, and A is closed exactly when the tank is 3/4 full. For how long was A open?
(a) 9 minutes (b) 8 minutes (c) 10 minutes (d) 12 minutes
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to divide 3/4 by A's rate alone, giving 15 minutes. While A is open, both pipes run at 1/20 + 1/30 = 1/12 per minute, so filling 3/4 takes (3/4) × 12 = 9 minutes, so the answer is 9 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

146. A can do a work in 18 days. He works for 6 days and leaves, and B finishes the remaining work in 8 days. In how many days can B alone do the whole work?
(a) 10 days (b) 16 days (c) 12 days (d) 14 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to equate B's 8 days with the whole work. A completes 6/18 = 1/3, leaving 2/3 for B. If 2/3 of the work takes B 8 days, the whole work takes 8 × 3/2 = 12 days, so the answer is 12 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Direct and Inverse Proportions)

147. 5 men, 6 women and 7 children earn ₹3120 in a week. Their individual daily wages are in the ratio 4:3:2. What is the total weekly earning of the women?
(a) ₹1080 (b) ₹960 (c) ₹1200 (d) ₹840
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to split ₹3120 directly in the ratio 4:3:2, ignoring the number of people. Total wage-units = 5×4 + 6×3 + 7×2 = 20 + 18 + 14 = 52, and each unit = 3120/52 = ₹60. Women get 18 × 60 = ₹1080, so the answer is ₹1080.
Source: PYQ-style

148. A and B can separately do a work in 9 and 12 days. They work on alternate days, one day each, with A starting. In how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 10¼ days (b) 10 days (c) 10½ days (d) 11 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to assume the work ends exactly at a full day. Each 2-day cycle completes 1/9 + 1/12 = 7/36, so 5 cycles (10 days) complete 35/36. On day 11 it is A's turn, and A needs only (1/36) ÷ (1/9) = 1/4 of a day, so the answer is 10¼ days.
Source: PYQ-style

149. A contractor employs 150 men to finish a work in 60 days. After 30 days, only 2/5 of the work is done. How many extra men must he employ to finish on time?
(a) 100 (b) 75 (c) 90 (d) 120
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to scale the men by the work ratio without adjusting for the remaining 30 days. Work done = 150 × 30 = 4500 man-days = 2/5 of the total, so total work = 11250 man-days and 6750 remain. Men needed = 6750/30 = 225, so extra men = 225 − 150 = 75, so the answer is 75.
Source: PYQ-style

150. A, B and C can do a work in 11, 20 and 55 days respectively. A works with B on the first day, with C on the second day, with B on the third day, and so on alternately. In how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 10 days (b) 9 days (c) 8 days (d) 11 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to average B's and C's rates and treat every day as identical. Odd days (A + B) give 1/11 + 1/20 = 31/220; even days (A + C) give 1/11 + 1/55 = 24/220. Each 2-day block completes 55/220 = 1/4, so 4 blocks finish the work exactly, so the answer is 8 days.
Source: PYQ-style
