126. (Hard) A can do a piece of work in 10 days, B in 12 days, and C in 15 days. They all begin together, but A leaves after 2 days and B leaves 3 days before the completion of the work. How long did the work last?
(A) 7 days  (B) 6.5 days  (C) 7.5 days  (D) 8 days
Answer: (A) 7 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(10, 12, 15) = 60 units. Daily efficiency: A = 6, B = 5, C = 4 units/day. In first 2 days, all three work: 2 * (6 + 5 + 4) = 30 units. Work remaining = 60 - 30 = 30 units. In the last 3 days, only C works: 3 * 4 = 12 units. Work done by (B + C) in the middle phase = 30 - 12 = 18 units. Time for middle phase = 18 / (5 + 4) = 2 days. Total time = 2 + 2 + 3 = 7 days. The required value is 7 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

127. (Hard) Three pipes A, B, and C can fill a cistern in 8 hours. After working together for 2 hours, C is closed and A and B can fill the remaining part in 9 hours. In how many hours can C alone fill the cistern?
(A) 20 hours  (B) 24 hours  (C) 28 hours  (D) 32 hours
Answer: (B) 24 hours
Solution: Work done by (A + B + C) in 2 hours = 2/8 = 1/4 of cistern. Remaining work = 1 - 1/4 = 3/4. Rate of (A + B) = (3/4) / 9 = 1/12 per hour. Rate of (A + B + C) = 1/8 per hour. Rate of C = 1/8 - 1/12 = (3 - 2)/24 = 1/24 per hour. Time taken by C alone = 24 hours. The required value is 24 hours. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

128. (Hard) A and B can complete a work in 24 days. They worked together for 16 days, and then B left. The remaining work was finished by A alone in 10 more days. In how many days can B alone complete the entire work?
(A) 100 days  (B) 90 days  (C) 120 days  (D) 150 days
Answer: (C) 120 days
Solution: Work done by (A + B) in 16 days = 16 / 24 = 2/3. Remaining work = 1/3, done by A in 10 days => Time taken by A alone = 10 * 3 = 30 days. Daily rate of A = 1/30. Daily rate of (A + B) = 1/24. Rate of B = 1/24 - 1/30 = (5 - 4)/120 = 1/120 per day. Time taken by B alone = 120 days. The required value is 120 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

129. (Hard) If 3 men or 6 women can do a piece of work in 16 days, in how many days can 12 men and 8 women do the same work?

Solution: 3 Men = 6 Women => 1 Man = 2 Women. Target workforce: 12 Men + 8 Women = 12(2 Women) + 8 Women = 32 Women. Total work = 6 Women * 16 days = 96 woman-days. Time taken = 96 / 32 = 3 days. The required value is 3 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).

130. (Hard) A, B, and C can do a piece of work in 15, 20, and 30 days respectively. In how many days can A finish the work if he is assisted by B and C on every second day?
(A) 9.44 days  (B) 9.25 days  (C) 9.50 days  (D) 10.00 days
Answer: (A) 9.44 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(15, 20, 30) = 60 units. Daily efficiency: A = 4, B = 3, C = 2 units/day. Day 1: A works = 4 units. Day 2: (A + B + C) work = 4 + 3 + 2 = 9 units. In a 2-day cycle: work done = 4 + 9 = 13 units. In 4 cycles (8 days): work done = 4 * 13 = 52 units. Remaining work = 60 - 52 = 8 units. Day 9: A works and does 4 units. Remaining work = 8 - 4 = 4 units. Day 10: All three work at 9 units/day: time = 4/9 = 0.44 days. Total days = 8 + 1 + 0.44 = 9.44 days. The required value is 9.44 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

131. (Hard) Three pipes A, B, and C are attached to a cistern. A and B can fill it in 20 and 30 hours respectively, while C can empty it in 60 hours. If A is opened at 7 AM, B at 9 AM, and C at 10 AM, at what time will the cistern be completely filled?
(A) 10:30 PM  (B) 10:15 PM  (C) 9:45 PM  (D) 11:00 PM
Answer: (B) 10:15 PM
Solution: Total capacity = LCM(20, 30, 60) = 60 units. Flow rates: A = +3, B = +2, C = -1 unit/hour. From 7 AM to 9 AM (2 hours), only A works: 2 * 3 = 6 units. From 9 AM to 10 AM (1 hour), A and B work: 1 * (3 + 2) = 5 units. Total filled by 10 AM = 6 + 5 = 11 units. Remaining capacity = 60 - 11 = 49 units. From 10 AM onwards, net rate = 3 + 2 - 1 = 4 units/hour. Time required = 49 / 4 = 12.25 hours = 12 hours 15 minutes. 10:00 AM + 12 hours 15 min = 10:15 PM. The required value is 10:15 PM. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

132. (Hard) A and B can do a job in 12 days and 18 days respectively. They work on alternate days with B starting the first day. In how many days will the job be completed?
(A) 14.2 days  (B) 15.0 days  (C) 14.5 days  (D) 14.75 days
Answer: (C) 14.5 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(12, 18) = 36 units. Daily efficiency: A = 3, B = 2 units/day. In a 2-day cycle: B + A = 2 + 3 = 5 units. In 7 cycles (14 days): work done = 7 * 5 = 35 units. Remaining work = 36 - 35 = 1 unit. On Day 15, B works at 2 units/day: time taken = 1 / 2 = 0.5 days. Total time taken = 14 + 0.5 = 14.5 days. The required value is 14.5 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).

133. (Hard) A does 4/5 of a work in 20 days. He then calls in B and they together finish the remaining work in 3 days. How long would B alone take to do the whole work?
(A) 35.0 days  (B) 40.0 days  (C) 42.5 days  (D) 37.5 days
Answer: (D) 37.5 days
Solution: A does 4/5 work in 20 days => Time taken by A alone for full work = 20 * (5/4) = 25 days. Rate of A = 1/25 per day. Remaining work = 1 - 4/5 = 1/5. (A + B) do 1/5 work in 3 days => Rate of (A + B) = (1/5) / 3 = 1/15 per day. Rate of B = 1/15 - 1/25 = (5 - 3)/75 = 2/75 per day. Time taken by B alone = 75 / 2 = 37.5 days. The required value is 37.5 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

134. (Hard) Two pipes A and B can fill a tank in 15 minutes and 20 minutes respectively. An outlet pipe C can empty the full tank in 25 minutes. All three pipes are opened together in the beginning. After 10 minutes, C is closed. In what time will the tank be full?
(A) 12 minutes  (B) 11 minutes  (C) 13 minutes  (D) 14 minutes
Answer: (A) 12 minutes
Solution: Capacity = LCM(15, 20, 25) = 300 units. Flow rates: A = +20, B = +15, C = -12 units/minute. Net filling rate with all open = 20 + 15 - 12 = 23 units/minute. In first 10 minutes, water filled = 10 * 23 = 230 units. Remaining capacity = 300 - 230 = 70 units. After closing C, rate of (A + B) = 20 + 15 = 35 units/minute. Time to fill remaining = 70 / 35 = 2 minutes. Total time = 10 + 2 = 12 minutes. The required value is 12 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Hence, the correct option is (C).

137. (Hard) 25 men can reap a field in 20 days. When should 15 men leave the work, if the whole field is to be reaped in 37.5 days?

Answer: (D) 8.33 days
Solution: Total work = 25 men * 20 days = 500 man-days. Let 25 men work for x days. Then remaining 25 - 15 = 10 men work for (37.5 - x) days. Answer: (A) 15 days

Answer: (B) 9.17 hours
Solution: Capacity = LCM(15, 20, 30) = 60 units. Flow rates: A = 4, B = 3, C = 2 units/hour. Hour 1: (A + B) = 4 + 3 = 7 units. Hour 2: (A + C) = 4 + 2 = 6 units. In 2 hours: 7 + 6 = 13 units. In 4 cycles (8 hours): 4 * 13 = 52 units. Remaining: 60 - 52 = 8 units. In Hour 9, (A + B) work and fill 7 units. Remaining: 8 - 7 = 1 unit. In Hour 10, (A + C) work at 6 units/hour: time = 1 / 6 = 0.17 hours. Total time = 8 + 1 + 0.17 = 9.17 hours. The required value is 9.17 hours. Hence, the correct option is (B).

(A) 50  (B) 65  (C) 55  (D) 60
Answer: (D) 60

149. (Hard) Pipe A alone can fill a tank in 12 hours. Pipe B alone can fill it in 15 hours. Both pipes are opened together. When the tank was 1/3 full, a leak developed in the tank through which 1/3 of the water supplied by both pipes goes out. What is the total time taken to fill the tank?
(A) 8.50 hours  (B) 9.20 hours  (C) 8.75 hours  (D) 8.89 hours
Answer: (D) 8.89 hours
Solution: Total capacity = LCM(12, 15) = 60 units. Combined flow rate of A and B = 5 + 4 = 9 units/hour. First 1/3 of tank = 60 / 3 = 20 units. Time taken for first 1/3 = 20 / 9 = 2.22 hours. Remaining capacity = 60 - 20 = 40 units. After the leak develops, 1/3 of water is lost, so net effective filling rate = (2/3) * 9 = 6 units/hour. Time taken for remaining 40 units = 40 / 6 = 6.67 hours. Total time = 2.22 + 6.67 = 8.89 hours. The required value is 8.89 hours. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

150. (Hard) A, B, and C can complete a piece of work in 24, 32, and 48 days respectively. They started the work together. A left after 4 days and B left 3 days before the completion of the work. For how many days was the work carried on?
(A) 17.5 days  (B) 17.8 days  (C) 18.0 days  (D) 16.5 days
Answer: (B) 17.8 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(24, 32, 48) = 96 units. Daily efficiency: A = 4, B = 3, C = 2 units/day. A worked for 4 days: 4 * 4 = 16 units. Remaining work = 96 - 16 = 80 units done by B and C. Let total days be T. B worked for (T - 3) days and C for T days: 3(T - 3) + 2T = 80 => 5T - 9 = 80 => 5T = 89 => T = 17.8 days. The required value is 17.8 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
