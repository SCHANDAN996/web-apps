76. Six bells commence tolling together and toll at intervals of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12 seconds respectively. In 30 minutes, how many times do they toll together?
(a) 16 (b) 15 (c) 14 (d) 18
Answer: (a) 16
Solution: LCM(2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12) = 120 seconds = 2 minutes. In 30 minutes, they toll (30 / 2) + 1 = 15 + 1 = 16 times (including the initial toll at t = 0). The required value is 16. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

(a) 12:03:00 (b) 12:02:48 (c) 12:02:24 (d) 12:04:00
Answer: (b) 12:02:48
Solution: LCM(4, 6, 8, 14) = 168 seconds = 2 minutes 48 seconds. The time will be 12:02:48. The required value is 12:02:48. Hence, the correct option is (b).

Answer: (c) 8:27:12 hrs

79. A room is 15 m 17 cm long and 9 m 2 cm broad. Find the least number of square tiles required to pave the floor.
(a) 841 (b) 828 (c) 804 (d) 814
Answer: (d) 814
Solution: L = 1517 cm, B = 902 cm. Tile size = HCF(1517, 902) = 41 cm. Number of tiles = (1517 * 902) / (41 * 41) = 37 * 22 = 814 tiles. The required value is 814. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

80. Three cans contain 403 liters, 434 liters, and 465 liters of milk respectively. What is the maximum capacity of a container that can measure the milk of all three cans an exact number of times?
(a) 31 liters (b) 29 liters (c) 37 liters (d) 33 liters
Answer: (a) 31 liters
Solution: HCF(403, 434, 465) = HCF(434 - 403, 465 - 434) = HCF(31, 31) = 31 liters. The required value is 31 liters. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

81. A, B, and C start at the same time in the same direction to run around a circular stadium of circumference 12 km. If their speeds are 4 km/h, 3 km/h, and 2 km/h respectively, when will they meet again at the starting point?
(a) 6 hours (b) 12 hours (c) 24 hours (d) 18 hours
Answer: (b) 12 hours
Solution: Lap times: t1 = 12/4 = 3h, t2 = 12/3 = 4h, t3 = 12/2 = 6h. Meeting time at starting point = LCM(3, 4, 6) = 12 hours. The required value is 12 hours. Hence, the correct option is (b).

82. A farmer has 945 cows and 2475 sheep. He groups them into minimum number of equal flocks having either cows or sheep. Find the total number of flocks.
(a) 45 (b) 68 (c) 76 (d) 84
Answer: (c) 76
Solution: Animals per flock = HCF(945, 2475) = 45. Total number of flocks = (945 + 2475) / 45 = 3420 / 45 = 76 flocks. The required value is 76. Hence, the correct option is (c).

89. Five bells toll together at intervals of 3, 5, 7, 8, and 10 seconds respectively. How many times will they toll together in one hour excluding the start?
(a) 5 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 6
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: LCM(3, 5, 7, 8, 10) = 840 seconds. In 1 hour (3600 seconds), they toll floor(3600 / 840) = 4 times. The required value is 4. Hence, the correct option is (b).

90. Three wheels make 120, 150, and 180 revolutions per minute respectively. After how many seconds will they be in the same relative position again?
(a) 1 second (b) 4 seconds (c) 2 seconds (d) 3 seconds
Answer: (c) 2 seconds
Solution: Periods of one revolution: 60/120 = 1/2 s, 60/150 = 2/5 s, 60/180 = 1/3 s. LCM of fractions = LCM(1, 2, 1) / HCF(2, 5, 3) = 2 / 1 = 2 seconds. The required value is 2 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

100. A boy counts coins by grouping them into piles of 12, 15, or 18. Each time, 5 coins are left over. If he groups them into piles of 17, no coins are left over. Find the least number of coins he could have.
(a) 185 (b) 1445 (c) 365 (d) 725
Answer: (b) 1445
Solution: LCM(12, 15, 18) = 180. Form: 180k + 5. For k = 8, 180(8) + 5 = 1445. 1445 is exactly divisible by 17 (1445 / 17 = 85). The required value is 1445. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
