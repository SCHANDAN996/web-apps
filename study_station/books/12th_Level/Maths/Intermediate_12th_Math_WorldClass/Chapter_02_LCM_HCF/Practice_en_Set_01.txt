1. Find the HCF of 2^3 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^4 and 2^2 * 3^5 * 5^2 * 7^3.
(a) 2^2 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3 (b) 2^3 * 3^5 * 5^2 * 7^4 (c) 2^2 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7^3 (d) 2^3 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3
Answer: (a) 2^2 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3
Solution: HCF takes the minimum power of each common prime factor: 2^min(3,2) * 3^min(2,5) * 5^min(1,2) * 7^min(4,3) = 2^2 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3. The required value is 2^2 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. Find the LCM of 2^3 * 3^2 * 5 and 2^4 * 3 * 5^2 * 7.
(a) 2^3 * 3 * 5 (b) 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7 (c) 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2 (d) 2^3 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7
Answer: (b) 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7
Solution: LCM takes the highest power of all prime factors appearing in either number: 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7. The required value is 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7. Hence, the correct option is (b).

3. The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 12 and 336 respectively. If one number is 84, find the other number.
(a) 36 (b) 56 (c) 48 (d) 42
Answer: (c) 48
Solution: Product of two numbers = LCM * HCF. Other number = (12 * 336) / 84 = 336 / 7 = 48. The required value is 48. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

4. Find the HCF of the fractions 2/3, 4/5, and 6/7.
(a) 12/105 (b) 2/15 (c) 1/105 (d) 2/105
Answer: (d) 2/105
Solution: HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators = HCF(2, 4, 6) / LCM(3, 5, 7) = 2 / 105. The required value is 2/105. Hence, the correct option is (d).

6. What is the HCF of any two consecutive natural numbers n and n + 1?
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) n (d) n + 1
Answer: (b) 1
Solution: Consecutive natural numbers never share any common factor greater than 1 because (n + 1) - n = 1. Hence, gcd(n, n + 1) = 1. The required value is 1. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (c) 2
Solution: Consecutive even integers differ by 2: (2k + 2) - 2k = 2. Both are divisible by 2, so their HCF is 2. The required value is 2. Hence, the correct option is (c).

Answer: (d) 48

9. The product of two coprime numbers is 117. What is their LCM?
(a) 117 (b) 1 (c) 9 (d) 13
Answer: (a) 117
Solution: For coprime numbers, HCF = 1. Since A * B = LCM * HCF, LCM = A * B / 1 = 117. The required value is 117. Hence, the correct option is (a).

10. What is the greatest number that will divide 390, 495, and 300 without a remainder?

Answer: (b) 15
Solution: We need HCF(390, 495, 300). 390 - 300 = 90. Factors of 90 are 90, 45, 30, 15... 15 divides 390, 495, and 300. Thus HCF = 15. The required value is 15. Hence, the correct option is (b).

18. The LCM of two numbers is 495 and their HCF is 5. If the sum of the numbers is 100, find their difference.
(a) 15 (b) 10 (c) 20 (d) 25
Answer: (b) 10
Solution: A + B = 100, A * B = 495 * 5 = 2475. (A - B)^2 = (A + B)^2 - 4AB = 10000 - 4(2475) = 10000 - 9900 = 100. Thus A - B = 10. The required value is 10. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

21. Find the smallest four-digit number which is exactly divisible by 12, 18, 21, and 28.
(a) 1008 (b) 1024 (c) 1080 (d) 1050
Answer: (a) 1008
Solution: LCM(12, 18, 21, 28): 12 = 2^2*3, 18 = 2*3^2, 21 = 3*7, 28 = 2^2*7. LCM = 4 * 9 * 7 = 252. Smallest 4-digit number is 1000. 1000 / 252 = 3 rem 244. Required number = 1000 + (252 - 244) = 1008. The required value is 1008. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

22. Find the greatest number of four digits which is divisible by 15, 25, 40, and 75.
(a) 9400 (b) 9600 (c) 9800 (d) 9000
Answer: (b) 9600
Solution: LCM(15, 25, 40, 75) = 600. Largest 4-digit number is 9999. 9999 / 600 = 16 rem 399. Required number = 9999 - 399 = 9600. The required value is 9600. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

24. How many pairs of positive integers have an HCF of 15 and an LCM of 630?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 4
Answer: (d) 4
Solution: LCM = H * x * y => 630 = 15 * x * y => x * y = 42. Coprime pairs for 42 = 2 * 3 * 7: (1, 42), (2, 21), (3, 14), (6, 7). Exactly 4 pairs. The required value is 4. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

25. The sum of two numbers is 84 and their HCF is 12. How many such pairs of numbers exist?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 1
Answer: (b) 3
Solution: 12x + 12y = 84 => x + y = 7. Coprime pairs summing to 7: (1, 6), (2, 5), (3, 4). Exactly 3 pairs exist. The required value is 3. Hence, the correct option is (b).
