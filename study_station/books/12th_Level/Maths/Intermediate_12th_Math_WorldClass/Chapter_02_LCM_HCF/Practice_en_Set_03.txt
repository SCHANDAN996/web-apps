Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Find the greatest number that will divide 964, 1238, and 1400 leaving remainders 41, 31, and 51 respectively.
(a) 71 (b) 61 (c) 81 (d) 91
Answer: (a) 71
Solution: Subtract remainders: 964 - 41 = 923; 1238 - 31 = 1207; 1400 - 51 = 1349. 1207 - 923 = 284 = 4 * 71. HCF is 71. The required value is 71. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. Find the largest number that divides 220, 284, and 428 leaving the same remainder in each case.
(a) 32 (b) 16 (c) 8 (d) 24
Answer: (b) 16
Solution: Differences: 284 - 220 = 64; 428 - 284 = 144; 428 - 220 = 208. HCF(64, 144, 208) = 16. The required value is 16. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

53. Find the least number which when divided by 24, 32, and 36 leaves remainders 19, 27, and 31 respectively.
(a) 288 (b) 293 (c) 283 (d) 278
Answer: (c) 283
Solution: Constant difference K = 24 - 19 = 32 - 27 = 36 - 31 = 5. LCM(24, 32, 36) = 288. Required number = 288 - 5 = 283. The required value is 283. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

54. Find the least multiple of 7 which leaves a remainder of 4 when divided by 6, 9, 15, and 18.
(a) 184 (b) 274 (c) 94 (d) 364
Answer: (d) 364
Solution: LCM(6, 9, 15, 18) = 90. Form is 90k + 4. For k = 4, 90(4) + 4 = 364, which is divisible by 7 (364 / 7 = 52). The required value is 364. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

55. Find the greatest four-digit number which when divided by 10, 15, 21, and 28 leaves 4, 9, 15, and 22 as remainders respectively.
(a) 9654 (b) 9660 (c) 9666 (d) 9648
Answer: (a) 9654
Solution: Constant difference K = 10 - 4 = 6. LCM(10, 15, 21, 28) = 420. Largest 4-digit multiple of 420 is 9660. Number = 9660 - 6 = 9654. The required value is 9654. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
