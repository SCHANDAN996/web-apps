Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The sum of two numbers is 216 and their HCF is 27. How many such pairs of numbers exist?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: 27x + 27y = 216 => x + y = 8. Coprime pairs summing to 8 with gcd(x, y) = 1 are (1, 7) and (3, 5). Exactly 2 pairs. The required value is 2. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. The product of two numbers is 2028 and their HCF is 13. How many pairs of such numbers exist?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: Product = H^2 * x * y => 2028 = 169 * x * y => x * y = 12. Coprime pairs with gcd(x, y) = 1 are (1, 12) and (3, 4). Exactly 2 pairs. The required value is 2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

28. The difference of two numbers is 24 and their HCF is 12. If the smaller number is 60, find the larger number.
(a) 96 (b) 72 (c) 84 (d) 108
Answer: (c) 84
Solution: Let the numbers be 60 and N. N - 60 = 24 => N = 84. Check HCF(60, 84) = 12. Larger number is 84. The required value is 84. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

29. The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 21 and 4641 respectively. If one of the numbers lies between 200 and 300, find the larger number.
(a) 273 (b) 441 (c) 336 (d) 357
Answer: (d) 357
Solution: x * y = LCM / HCF = 4641 / 21 = 221 = 13 * 17. The numbers are 21 * 13 = 273 and 21 * 17 = 357. The larger number is 357. The required value is 357. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. Two numbers both greater than 29 have an HCF of 29 and an LCM of 4147. Find the sum of the numbers.
(a) 696 (b) 667 (c) 725 (d) 580
Answer: (a) 696
Solution: x * y = 4147 / 29 = 143 = 11 * 13. Since both numbers > 29, numbers are 29 * 11 = 319 and 29 * 13 = 377. Sum = 319 + 377 = 696. The required value is 696. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

31. Find the HCF of 1.08, 0.36, and 0.90.
(a) 0.09 (b) 0.18 (c) 0.36 (d) 0.06
Answer: (b) 0.18
Solution: Writing with equal decimals: 108/100, 36/100, 90/100. HCF(108, 36, 90) = 18. HCF = 18 / 100 = 0.18. The required value is 0.18. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

32. The LCM of two numbers is 12 times their HCF. The sum of HCF and LCM is 403. If one number is 93, find the other number.
(a) 134 (b) 114 (c) 124 (d) 128
Answer: (c) 124
Solution: L = 12H. H + 12H = 13H = 403 => H = 31. L = 12 * 31 = 372. Other number = (31 * 372) / 93 = 372 / 3 = 124. The required value is 124. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

33. Find the greatest number which will divide 43, 91, and 183 so as to leave the same remainder in each case.
(a) 7 (b) 9 (c) 8 (d) 4
Answer: (d) 4
Solution: Take differences: 91 - 43 = 48, 183 - 91 = 92, 183 - 43 = 140. HCF(48, 92, 140) = 4. The required value is 4. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

34. The HCF of two numbers is 11 and their LCM is 7700. If one of the numbers is 275, find the other number.
(a) 308 (b) 286 (c) 318 (d) 298
Answer: (a) 308
Solution: Other number = (HCF * LCM) / First = (11 * 7700) / 275 = 7700 / 25 = 308. The required value is 308. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

35. Find the largest three-digit number which when divided by 6, 9, 12, and 15 leaves a remainder of 3 in each case.
(a) 900 (b) 903 (c) 963 (d) 993
Answer: (b) 903
Solution: LCM(6, 9, 12, 15) = 180. Largest 3-digit multiple of 180 is 900. Required number = 900 + 3 = 903. The required value is 903. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

36. Find the smallest five-digit number which is exactly divisible by 16, 24, 30, and 36.
(a) 10000 (b) 10800 (c) 10080 (d) 10160
Answer: (c) 10080
Solution: LCM(16, 24, 30, 36) = 720. 10000 / 720 = 13 rem 640. Required number = 10000 + (720 - 640) = 10080. The required value is 10080. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

37. The ratio of two numbers is 4 : 5. If their LCM is 180, find the smaller number.
(a) 45 (b) 30 (c) 40 (d) 36
Answer: (d) 36
Solution: LCM = 4 * 5 * H = 180 => 20H = 180 => H = 9. Smaller number = 4 * 9 = 36. The required value is 36. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

38. Find the value of gcd(4^315 - 1, 4^25 - 1).
(a) 1023 (b) 1024 (c) 255 (d) 4095
Answer: (a) 1023
Solution: By identity gcd(a^m - 1, a^n - 1) = a^gcd(m, n) - 1: gcd(315, 25) = 5. So 4^5 - 1 = 1024 - 1 = 1023. The required value is 1023. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

39. If gcd(a, b) = 1, which of the following values can gcd(a + b, a - b) possibly take?
(a) Only 1 (b) 1 or 2 (c) Only 2 (d) Any integer
Answer: (b) 1 or 2
Solution: Let d = gcd(a+b, a-b). Then d divides (a+b)+(a-b)=2a and (a+b)-(a-b)=2b. Since gcd(a,b)=1, d must divide 2. Hence d is either 1 or 2. The required value is 1 or 2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

40. Find the least number which when divided by 12, 16, 24, and 36 leaves a remainder of 7 in each case.
(a) 144 (b) 158 (c) 151 (d) 137
Answer: (c) 151
Solution: LCM(12, 16, 24, 36) = 144. Required number = LCM + remainder = 144 + 7 = 151. The required value is 151. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

41. A number when divided by 3, 5, and 7 leaves remainders 1, 3, and 5 respectively. What is the smallest such positive integer?
(a) 105 (b) 107 (c) 101 (d) 103
Answer: (d) 103
Solution: Differences: 3 - 1 = 2, 5 - 3 = 2, 7 - 5 = 2 (constant K = 2). LCM(3, 5, 7) = 105. Required number = LCM - K = 105 - 2 = 103. The required value is 103. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

42. The product of two numbers is 4107. If their HCF is 37, find the greater number.
(a) 111 (b) 37 (c) 74 (d) 148
Answer: (a) 111
Solution: Product = 37^2 * x * y = 4107 => 1369 * x * y = 4107 => x * y = 3. Since 3 is prime, factors are 1 and 3. Greater number = 37 * 3 = 111. The required value is 111. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

43. The HCF of two numbers is 9 and their LCM is 270. If the sum of the numbers is 99, find their difference.
(a) 18 (b) 9 (c) 27 (d) 36
Answer: (b) 9
Solution: A + B = 99, A * B = 9 * 270 = 2430. (A - B)^2 = 99^2 - 4(2430) = 9801 - 9720 = 81. Thus A - B = 9. The required value is 9. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

44. Find the least number of soldiers in a regiment who can stand in rows of 15, 20, or 25 and also form a solid square.
(a) 400 (b) 1600 (c) 900 (d) 2500
Answer: (c) 900
Solution: LCM(15, 20, 25) = 300 = 2^2 * 3^1 * 5^2. To make all prime exponents even, multiply by 3: 300 * 3 = 900. The required value is 900. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

45. The sum of two numbers is 45 and their difference is 1/9 of their sum. Find their LCM.
(a) 90 (b) 120 (c) 80 (d) 100
Answer: (d) 100
Solution: A + B = 45, A - B = 45 / 9 = 5. Adding gives 2A = 50 => A = 25, B = 20. LCM(25, 20) = 100. The required value is 100. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

46. Three measuring rods are 64 cm, 72 cm, and 96 cm long. Find the least length in cm that can be measured exactly by any one of them.
(a) 576 (b) 288 (c) 864 (d) 432
Answer: (a) 576
Solution: LCM(64, 72, 96) = 2^6 * 3^2 = 64 * 9 = 576 cm. The required value is 576. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

47. Positive integers x and y have x * y = 1800 and gcd(x, y) = 5. How many unordered pairs {x, y} exist?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: x * y = 5^2 * a * b = 1800 => a * b = 72. Coprime pairs for 72 = 2^3 * 3^2 are (1, 72) and (8, 9). Exactly 2 pairs. The required value is 2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

48. The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 13 and 455 respectively. If one number lies between 60 and 80, find that number.
(a) 78 (b) 75 (c) 65 (d) 70
Answer: (c) 65
Solution: x * y = 455 / 13 = 35 = 5 * 7. Numbers are 13 * 5 = 65 and 13 * 7 = 91. The number between 60 and 80 is 65. The required value is 65. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

49. Find the greatest four-digit number which is divisible by each of 12, 15, 18, and 27.
(a) 9640 (b) 9820 (c) 9900 (d) 9720
Answer: (d) 9720
Solution: LCM(12, 15, 18, 27) = 540. 9999 / 540 = 18 with remainder 279. Required number = 9999 - 279 = 9720. The required value is 9720. Hence, the correct option is (d).
