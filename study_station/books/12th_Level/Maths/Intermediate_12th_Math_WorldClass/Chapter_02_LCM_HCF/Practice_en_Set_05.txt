Questions 101–125: Hard

101. Find the HCF of the fractions 9/10, 12/25, 18/35, and 21/40.
(a) 3/1400 (b) 9/1400 (c) 3/700 (d) 1/1400
Answer: (a) 3/1400
Solution: HCF of fractions = HCF(9, 12, 18, 21) / LCM(10, 25, 35, 40) = 3 / 1400. The required value is 3/1400. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

102. Find the LCM of the fractions 2/3, 3/5, 4/7, and 9/13.
(a) 18 (b) 36 (c) 72 (d) 36/1365
Answer: (b) 36
Solution: LCM of fractions = LCM(2, 3, 4, 9) / HCF(3, 5, 7, 13) = 36 / 1 = 36. The required value is 36. Hence, the correct option is (b).

103. Find the value of gcd(3^45 - 1, 3^35 - 1).
(a) 243 (b) 80 (c) 242 (d) 728
Answer: (c) 242
Solution: By identity gcd(a^m - 1, a^n - 1) = a^gcd(m, n) - 1: gcd(45, 35) = 5. So 3^5 - 1 = 243 - 1 = 242. The required value is 242. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

106. The HCF of two numbers is 16 and their LCM is 160. If one of the numbers is 32, find the other number.

107. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7. If their HCF is 8, find the sum of the two numbers.

108. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If their LCM is 75, find their HCF.
(a) 3 (b) 15 (c) 1 (d) 5
Answer: (d) 5
Solution: LCM = 3 * 5 * H = 75 => 15H = 75 => H = 5. The required value is 5. Hence, the correct option is (d).

109. Find the HCF of the algebraic polynomials (x^2 - y^2) and (x^3 - y^3).
(a) x - y (b) x + y (c) x^2 - y^2 (d) 1
Answer: (a) x - y
Solution: (x^2 - y^2) = (x - y)(x + y) and (x^3 - y^3) = (x - y)(x^2 + xy + y^2). Common factor is (x - y). The required value is x - y. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

110. Find the LCM of the polynomials (x^2 - 4) and (x + 2)^2.
(a) (x - 2)(x + 2) (b) (x - 2)(x + 2)^2 (c) (x^2 - 4)^2 (d) (x + 2)^3
Answer: (b) (x - 2)(x + 2)^2
Solution: Factorizations: (x - 2)(x + 2) and (x + 2)^2. Highest powers give LCM = (x - 2)(x + 2)^2. The required value is (x - 2)(x + 2)^2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

111. The HCF of three numbers 12, 18, and N is 6, and their LCM is 360. What is the smallest possible positive integer value of N?
(a) 60 (b) 30 (c) 120 (d) 180
Answer: (c) 120
Solution: 12 = 2^2 * 3, 18 = 2 * 3^2. LCM = 360 = 2^3 * 3^2 * 5. N must supply the factor of 5 and 2^3 while being a multiple of 6 (2*3): N = 2^3 * 3 * 5 = 120. The required value is 120. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

117. Find the LCM of (x^2 - 9) and (x^2 - 5x + 6).

118. If the HCF of two numbers is 1, the numbers are standardly called:

121. Find the greatest positive integer that will divide (n^3 - n) for every positive integer n.
(a) 6 (b) 12 (c) 3 (d) 24
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: n^3 - n = (n - 1)n(n + 1). The product of any three consecutive integers is always divisible by 3! = 6. The required value is 6. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

122. If HCF of two numbers x and y is 15, their LCM is 180, and x + y = 105, find the absolute difference |x - y|.
(a) 25 (b) 15 (c) 30 (d) 10
Answer: (b) 15
Solution: x * y = 15 * 180 = 2700. (x - y)^2 = (x + y)^2 - 4xy = 105^2 - 4(2700) = 11025 - 10800 = 225. Thus |x - y| = 15. The required value is 15. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (c) 31
Solution: gcd(315, 25) = 5. Therefore, gcd(2^315 - 1, 2^25 - 1) = 2^5 - 1 = 32 - 1 = 31. The required value is 31. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

124. If p is a prime number greater than 3, what is the greatest integer that always divides (p^2 - 1)?
(a) 12 (b) 6 (c) 48 (d) 24
Answer: (d) 24
Solution: Every prime p > 3 is of form 6k ± 1. (6k ± 1)^2 - 1 = 36k^2 ± 12k = 12k(3k ± 1). Since k or (3k ± 1) is even, this expression is always divisible by 24. The required value is 24. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

125. Three numbers x, y, z have pairwise HCFs: gcd(x, y) = 3, gcd(y, z) = 2, gcd(z, x) = 5, and gcd(x, y, z) = 1. What is the minimum possible value of their product x * y * z?
(a) 450 (b) 900 (c) 1800 (d) 300
Answer: (b) 900
Solution: Minimum factors: x must contain 3 and 5 (x = 15); y must contain 3 and 2 (y = 6); z must contain 2 and 5 (z = 10). Product = 15 * 6 * 10 = 900. The required value is 900. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
