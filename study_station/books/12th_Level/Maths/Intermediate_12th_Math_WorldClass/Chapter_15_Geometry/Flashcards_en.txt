Card 1
Front: What mnemonic helps remember sin, cos, tan in a right triangle?
Back: "SOH-CAH-TOA" — Sine = Opposite/Hypotenuse, Cosine = Adjacent/Hypotenuse, Tangent = Opposite/Adjacent.

Card 2
Front: What are the five congruence criteria for triangles?
Back: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and RHS (for right triangles).

Card 3
Front: What is the Pythagorean theorem?
Back: In a right triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse.

Card 4
Front: What is the sum of the interior angles of a triangle?
Back: 180°.

Card 5
Front: What is the sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral?
Back: 360°.

Card 6
Front: What is the formula for the sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon?
Back: (n − 2) × 180°.

Card 7
Front: What is the sum of the exterior angles of any polygon?
Back: 360°.

Card 8
Front: What is the area of a triangle?
Back: ½ × base × height.

Card 9
Front: What is the area of a circle?
Back: πr², where r is the radius.

Card 10
Front: What is the circumference of a circle?
Back: 2πr (or πd, where d is the diameter).

Card 11
Front: What is the area of a trapezium?
Back: ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × (perpendicular distance between them) = ½(a + b) × h.

Card 12
Front: Give an example of a Pythagorean triple.
Back: 3, 4, 5 (since 3² + 4² = 5²). Others: 5-12-13, 8-15-17.

Card 13
Front: What is the locus of a point at a fixed distance from a fixed point?
Back: A circle (the fixed point is the centre, the fixed distance is the radius).

Card 14
Front: What is the mnemonic for alternate angles formed by a transversal?
Back: The "Z" pattern — alternate interior angles are equal and form a Z shape.

Card 15
Front: What is the area of a parallelogram?
Back: base × height.

Card 16
Front: What is the area of a rhombus using its diagonals?
Back: ½ × d₁ × d₂, where d₁ and d₂ are the diagonals.

Card 17
Front: What is the measure of each interior angle of a regular hexagon?
Back: 120° (using (n − 2) × 180° ÷ n with n = 6).

Card 18
Front: What is the angle in a semicircle?
Back: 90° — an angle inscribed in a semicircle is always a right angle.

Card 19
Front: What is the relationship between a tangent and the radius at the point of contact?
Back: The tangent is perpendicular (90°) to the radius at the point of contact.

Card 20
Front: What is the length of the diagonal of a square with side a?
Back: a√2.
