76. In a triangle ABC, the angles are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. The smallest angle of the triangle is:
(a) 30° (b) 60° (c) 45° (d) 40°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 30°, which comes from wrongly dividing 180° by 6 parts and taking 1 part. The ratio has 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 parts, so each part is 180° ÷ 9 = 20°, and the smallest angle is 2 × 20° = 40°, so the answer is 40°.

77. The supplement of an angle is four times its complement. The angle is:
(a) 30° (b) 60° (c) 45° (d) 75°
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students pick 45° by guessing, but checking fails: supplement 135° is not four times complement 45°. Let the angle be x; then 180° − x = 4(90° − x), giving 180° − x = 360° − 4x, so 3x = 180° and x = 60°, so the answer is 60°.

78. In triangle ABC, AB = AC and angle A = 80°. Then angle B equals:
(a) 40° (b) 80° (c) 60° (d) 50°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 80°, chosen by those who forget the base angles are equal, not equal to the vertex angle. Since AB = AC, angles B and C are equal, and B + C = 180° − 80° = 100°, so each is 50°, so the answer is 50°.

(a) 6 cm (b) 4 cm (c) 8 cm (d) 5 cm
