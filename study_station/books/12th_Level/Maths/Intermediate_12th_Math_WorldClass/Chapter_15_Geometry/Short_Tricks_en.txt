# 🪄 Geometry — Short Tricks & Memory Aids

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 1 — Angle Sum of Polygon: "Minus 2, Times 180"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Memory Hook:** Imagine the polygon is a pizza. Cut off 2 slices for the "tax man" (−2), then each remaining slice is worth 180°.
- Sum of interior angles = **(n − 2) × 180°**
- Each interior angle of regular polygon = (n − 2) × 180° / n
- **Keyword:** "TAX-2" → subtract 2, multiply by 180.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 2 — Exterior Angles: "The 360 Walk"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Loci Trick:** Picture yourself walking around any polygon. When you return to the start, you've turned one full circle — **360°**, always, no matter how many sides!
- Sum of exterior angles = **360°** (any polygon)
- Each exterior angle of regular polygon = 360°/n
- Number of sides n = 360° ÷ (one exterior angle)

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 3 — Triangle Types by Angles: "ARO"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Acronym: A-R-O** (like "arrow" without the second R)
- **A**cute → **A**ll angles < 90°
- **R**ight → **R**ight angle = 90°
- **O**btuse → **O**ne angle > 90°
**Memory line:** *"An ARROW flies Acute, lands Right, or falls Obtuse."*

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 4 — Pythagoras: "The Hungry Hypotenuse"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Story:** The Hypotenuse is the longest, hungriest side — it eats the squares of BOTH other sides!
- **H² = P² + B²** (Hypotenuse² = Perpendicular² + Base²)
- **Keyword:** "H eats P and B" — H is always alone on one side.
- Common triplets to memorize: **(3,4,5), (5,12,13), (8,15,17), (7,24,25)**

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 5 — Circle Formulas: "Pie Are Squared"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Outrageous line:** *"Pie are not round — Pie are SQUARED!"* (π r²)
- Area = **πr²** ("Pie are squared")
- Circumference = **2πr** ("Two pies in a row" → 2-π-r)
- Semicircle area = πr²/2; Perimeter of semicircle = πr + 2r (don't forget the diameter!)

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 6 — Cyclic Quadrilateral: "Opposite Couples Fight to 180"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Story:** In a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles are like quarrelling couples — they always make up to **180°**.
- ∠A + ∠C = 180°, ∠B + ∠D = 180°
- **Keyword:** "CIRCLE = COUPLES = 180"
- Bonus: Exterior angle of cyclic quad = interior opposite angle.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 7 — Tangent & Radius: "The Polite 90° Handshake"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Loci Trick:** Wherever a tangent touches a circle, the radius rushes out to shake hands — always at exactly **90°**.
- Tangent ⊥ Radius at point of contact
- Two tangents from an external point are **equal** ("Twins from the same house").

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 8 — Angle at Centre: "The Boss Sees Double"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Story:** The centre is the BOSS of the circle. Whatever angle the circumference sees, the boss sees **DOUBLE**.
- Angle at centre = **2 ×** angle at circumference (same arc)
- Angle in a semicircle = **90°** (boss sees 180°, you see half!)

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 9 — Similar Triangles: "AAA — The Battery Test"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Acronym:** Similarity tests = **AAA, SSS, SAS** — think of batteries (AAA) powering your proof!
- **AAA** → all angles equal
- **SSS** → sides proportional
- **SAS** → two sides proportional + included angle equal
- **Keyword:** "Similar = Same SHAPE, different SIZE."

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 10 — Congruence: "S-S-S-A Party, but No A-S-S!"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Memory line:** Congruence tests are **SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, RHS** — but **ASS/AAA is NOT allowed at the party!**
- AAA only proves similarity, never congruence.
- **RHS** = Right angle–Hypotenuse–Side (only for right triangles) — "Royal Highness Special" rule.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 11 — Quadrilateral Areas: "Trap-ezoid = Average Couple × Height"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Story:** A trapezium has two parallel sides (a couple). Their area = the couple's **average** × height.
- Trapezium area = **½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height**
- Parallelogram = base × height ("Just a rectangle that leaned over!")
- Rhombus = **½ × d₁ × d₂** ("Diamonds are cut in half — twice!")

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 12 — Complementary vs Supplementary: "C comes before S, 90 before 180"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Alphabet Trick:** In the dictionary, **C**omplementary comes before **S**upplementary — and **90** comes before **180**.
- Complementary → sum = **90°** ("Corner" of a square = 90°)
- Supplementary → sum = **180°** ("Straight" line = 180°)
- **Keyword:** C-Corner-90, S-Straight-180.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 13 — Triangle Inequality: "The Lazy Shortcut Rule"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Story:** Two sides of a triangle together must beat the third — because going via two sides is the "long way round." A straight line (third side) is always the lazy shortcut!
- Sum of any two sides **>** third side
- Difference of any two sides **<** third side
- **Quick check:** add the two smallest sides — must exceed the largest.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 14 — Chord & Perpendicular: "The Fair Axe"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Story:** A perpendicular dropped from the centre to a chord is a FAIR AXE — it splits the chord into two perfectly equal halves.
- Perpendicular from centre **bisects** the chord.
- Equal chords are **equidistant** from the centre ("Equal chords, equal respect!")

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 15 — Sector & Arc: "The Pizza Fraction"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
**Loci Trick:** A sector is just a pizza slice — everything is the fraction **θ/360** of the whole circle!
- Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr
- Sector area = (θ/360) × πr²
- **Keyword:** "Whatever fraction of the pizza, that fraction of the formula."
