126. In a triangle ABC, the sides are 13 cm, 14 cm and 15 cm. What is the length of the altitude drawn to the side of 14 cm?
(a) 10 cm (b) 13 cm (c) 11 cm (d) 12 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 10 cm comes from wrongly assuming the triangle is right-angled. Using Heron's formula, s = (13 + 14 + 15)/2 = 21, so area = √(21 × 8 × 7 × 6) = √7056 = 84 cm². Altitude to side 14 = (2 × 84)/14 = 12, so the answer is 12 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics (Heron's Formula)

127. Two circles of radii 5 cm and 3 cm touch each other externally. What is the length of their direct common tangent?
(a) 8 cm (b) √34 cm (c) 4√15 cm (d) 2√15 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students wrongly pick 8 cm by simply adding the radii, which gives the distance between centres, not the tangent. Direct common tangent = √(d² − (r₁ − r₂)²) = √(64 − 4) = √60 = 2√15, so the answer is 2√15 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics (Circles)

128. In a circle of radius 10 cm, a chord is at a distance of 6 cm from the centre. What is the length of the chord?
(a) 16 cm (b) 12 cm (c) 8 cm (d) 14 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 8 cm is only half the chord, obtained by forgetting that the perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord. Half-chord = √(10² − 6²) = √64 = 8, so full chord = 2 × 8 = 16, so the answer is 16 cm.

129. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 7. What is the measure of the largest angle?
(a) 90° (b) 100° (c) 120° (d) 105°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 90° comes from wrongly treating the ratio parts as degrees or assuming a right triangle. Sum of parts = 12, so largest angle = (7/12) × 180° = 105°, so the answer is 105°.

130. In triangle ABC, D and E are points on AB and AC such that DE is parallel to BC. If AD = 4 cm, DB = 8 cm and AE = 6 cm, then EC equals:
(a) 12 cm (b) 10 cm (c) 9 cm (d) 8 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 8 cm comes from wrongly setting EC = DB. By the Basic Proportionality Theorem, AD/DB = AE/EC, so 4/8 = 6/EC, giving EC = 12, so the answer is 12 cm.

131. A ladder 25 m long leans against a wall and reaches a window 24 m high. How far is the foot of the ladder from the wall?
(a) 5 m (b) 6 m (c) 8 m (d) 7 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 5 m comes from misremembering the 7-24-25 triplet as 5-24-25. Distance = √(25² − 24²) = √(625 − 576) = √49 = 7, so the answer is 7 m.

132. The diagonals of a rhombus are 24 cm and 10 cm. What is the length of each side of the rhombus?
(a) 12 cm (b) 17 cm (c) 13 cm (d) 15 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 17 cm comes from adding the half-diagonals (12 + 5) instead of applying Pythagoras. Diagonals of a rhombus bisect at right angles, so side = √(12² + 5²) = √169 = 13, so the answer is 13 cm.

133. In a circle, two chords AB and CD intersect inside the circle at P. If AP = 6 cm, PB = 4 cm and CP = 3 cm, then PD equals:
(a) 6 cm (b) 8 cm (c) 9 cm (d) 12 cm
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 6 cm comes from wrongly assuming PD = AP by symmetry. For intersecting chords, AP × PB = CP × PD, so 6 × 4 = 3 × PD, giving PD = 8, so the answer is 8 cm.

134. The area of an equilateral triangle is 16√3 cm². What is the length of its side?
(a) 8 cm (b) 6 cm (c) 4 cm (d) 12 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 4 cm comes from forgetting to take the square root after dividing. Area = (√3/4)a² = 16√3, so a² = 64 and a = 8, so the answer is 8 cm.

135. In triangle ABC, angle A = 90°, AB = 6 cm and AC = 8 cm. What is the length of the median from A to BC?
(a) 4 cm (b) 5 cm (c) 6 cm (d) 7 cm
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 4 cm comes from halving AC instead of the hypotenuse. BC = √(36 + 64) = 10 cm, and in a right triangle the median to the hypotenuse equals half the hypotenuse = 5, so the answer is 5 cm.

136. The angle subtended by an arc at the centre of a circle is 100°. What is the angle subtended by the same arc at any point on the remaining part of the circle?
(a) 50° (b) 100° (c) 40° (d) 80°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 100° comes from forgetting the central angle theorem entirely. The angle at the circumference is half the angle at the centre = 100°/2 = 50°, so the answer is 50°.

137. A regular polygon has each interior angle equal to 156°. How many sides does it have?
(a) 12 (b) 18 (c) 15 (d) 20
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 12 comes from using the interior angle 156° directly in 360/156-type wrong division. Exterior angle = 180° − 156° = 24°, so number of sides = 360/24 = 15, so the answer is 15.

138. In triangle PQR, PQ = 8 cm, QR = 6 cm and angle Q = 90°. A perpendicular from Q meets PR at S. What is the length of QS?
(a) 4.8 cm (b) 5 cm (c) 4 cm (d) 5.2 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 5 cm comes from assuming the altitude equals half the hypotenuse, which is true only for the median. PR = 10 cm; using area, QS = (PQ × QR)/PR = (8 × 6)/10 = 4.8, so the answer is 4.8 cm.

139. The perimeter of a semicircular protractor is 36 cm. What is its diameter? (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 10 cm (b) 12 cm (c) 14 cm (d) 7 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 12 cm comes from wrongly using only the curved part πr = 36. Perimeter of semicircle = πr + 2r = r(22/7 + 2) = 36r/7 = 36, so r = 7 and diameter = 14, so the answer is 14 cm.

140. ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral. If angle A = 75°, what is angle C?
(a) 105° (b) 75° (c) 115° (d) 95°

Solution: The trap option 75° comes from wrongly applying the "opposite angles equal" rule of parallelograms. In a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles are supplementary, so angle C = 180° − 75° = 105°, so the answer is 105°.

141. The sides of a triangle are 7 cm, 24 cm and 25 cm. What is the radius of its circumcircle?
(a) 12 cm (b) 12.5 cm (c) 13 cm (d) 25 cm
Answer: (b)

142. From an external point P, two tangents PA and PB are drawn to a circle with centre O. If angle APB = 60°, what is angle AOB?
(a) 60° (b) 90° (c) 120° (d) 150°
Answer: (c)

143. The area of a rhombus is 240 cm² and one of its diagonals is 16 cm. What is the length of the other diagonal?
(a) 15 cm (b) 30 cm (c) 20 cm (d) 24 cm
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 15 cm comes from forgetting the factor 1/2 in the area formula, i.e., computing 240/16. Area = (1/2)d₁d₂, so 240 = (1/2) × 16 × d₂, giving d₂ = 30, so the answer is 30 cm.
