1. The sum of all interior angles of a triangle is:
(a) 90° (b) 270° (c) 180° (d) 360°
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students confuse this with the angle sum of a quadrilateral, which is 360°, but a triangle has only three angles. By the angle sum property of a triangle, the three interior angles always add up to 180°, so the answer is 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

2. An angle which is greater than 90° but less than 180° is called:
(a) Obtuse angle (b) Right angle (c) Acute angle (d) Reflex angle
Answer: (a)
Solution: An acute angle is less than 90° and a reflex angle is more than 180°, so those options are traps. An angle lying strictly between 90° and 180° is defined as an obtuse angle, so the answer is obtuse angle.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

3. Two angles are complementary. If one of them is 35°, the other is:
(a) 55° (b) 145° (c) 65° (d) 45°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 145°, which comes from subtracting 35° from 180° — that is the rule for supplementary angles, not complementary. Complementary angles sum to 90°, so the other angle is 90° − 35° = 55°, so the answer is 55°.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

4. Two angles are supplementary. If one angle is 110°, the other angle is:
(a) 80° (b) 60° (c) 70° (d) 90°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 80°, obtained by wrongly subtracting from 190° or mixing up the rule. Supplementary angles add up to 180°, so the required angle is 180° − 110° = 70°, so the answer is 70°.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

5. The number of sides of a triangle is:
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 5
Answer: (c)
Solution: A triangle by definition is a closed figure bounded by three line segments. The prefix "tri" itself means three, so the answer is 3.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

6. A triangle with all three sides equal is called:
(a) Equilateral triangle (b) Isosceles triangle (c) Scalene triangle (d) Right-angled triangle
Answer: (a)
Solution: An isosceles triangle has only two equal sides and a scalene triangle has none, so both are traps. A triangle with all three sides equal is an equilateral triangle, so the answer is equilateral triangle.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

7. Each angle of an equilateral triangle measures:
(a) 45° (b) 30° (c) 90° (d) 60°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 90°, which belongs to a right-angled triangle. In an equilateral triangle all three angles are equal, and since they sum to 180°, each angle is 180° ÷ 3 = 60°, so the answer is 60°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

8. In a right-angled triangle, the side opposite the right angle is called the:
(a) Base (b) Perpendicular (c) Median (d) Hypotenuse
Answer: (d)
Solution: Base and perpendicular are the two sides forming the right angle, so they are tempting but wrong. The longest side, opposite the 90° angle, is the hypotenuse, so the answer is hypotenuse.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

9. According to the Pythagoras theorem, in a right-angled triangle:
(a) Hypotenuse = Base + Perpendicular (b) Hypotenuse = Base² + Perpendicular² (c) Hypotenuse² = Base² − Perpendicular² (d) Hypotenuse² = Base² + Perpendicular²
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding the sides directly instead of their squares — lengths do not add, their squares do. The Pythagoras theorem states that the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides, so the answer is Hypotenuse² = Base² + Perpendicular².
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

10. The two sides containing the right angle of a right triangle are 3 cm and 4 cm. The hypotenuse is:
(a) 7 cm (b) 6 cm (c) 5 cm (d) 12 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 7 cm, obtained by simply adding 3 + 4, which ignores the theorem. By Pythagoras, hypotenuse² = 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25, so hypotenuse = 5 cm, so the answer is 5 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

11. The sum of all interior angles of a quadrilateral is:
(a) 360° (b) 270° (c) 180° (d) 540°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 180°, which is the angle sum of a triangle, not a quadrilateral. A quadrilateral can be split into two triangles, giving 2 × 180° = 360°, so the answer is 360°.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

12. A quadrilateral with both pairs of opposite sides parallel is called a:
(a) Trapezium (b) Kite (c) Rhombus only (d) Parallelogram
Answer: (d)
Solution: A trapezium has only one pair of parallel sides and a kite has none, so both are traps. The defining property of a parallelogram is that both pairs of opposite sides are parallel, so the answer is parallelogram.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

13. The diagonals of a rectangle are:
(a) Equal (b) Unequal (c) Perpendicular always (d) Parallel
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "perpendicular always" — that is true for a rhombus or square, not every rectangle. In a rectangle the diagonals are always equal in length and bisect each other, so the answer is equal.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

14. The area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm is:
(a) 60 cm² (b) 32 cm² (c) 16 cm² (d) 30 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 60 cm², which forgets the factor of one-half in the formula. Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 cm², so the answer is 30 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

15. The area of a circle of radius 7 cm is (take π = 22/7):
(a) 44 cm² (b) 154 cm² (c) 144 cm² (d) 308 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 44 cm², which is the circumference 2πr, not the area. Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 154 cm², so the answer is 154 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

16. The circumference of a circle of radius 14 cm is (take π = 22/7):
(a) 88 cm (b) 44 cm (c) 616 cm (d) 22 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 44 cm, which uses only the radius instead of the diameter in 2πr. Circumference = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 14 = 88 cm, so the answer is 88 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

17. The angle subtended by a diameter at any point on the circle is:
(a) 90° (b) 60° (c) 45° (d) 180°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 180°, which is the angle at the centre, not on the circumference. By the theorem, the angle in a semicircle is a right angle, so the answer is 90°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

18. Vertically opposite angles formed by two intersecting lines are always:
(a) Complementary (b) Equal (c) Supplementary (d) Unequal
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is supplementary — that applies to a linear pair, not vertically opposite angles. When two lines intersect, the vertically opposite angles are always equal, so the answer is equal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

19. The sum of angles at a point on one side of a straight line is:
(a) 90° (b) 180° (c) 270° (d) 360°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 360°, which is the sum of angles around a full point, not on one side of a line. Angles forming a linear pair together make a straight angle of 180°, so the answer is 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

20. A polygon with 6 sides is called a:
(a) Pentagon (b) Hexagon (c) Heptagon (d) Octagon
Answer: (b)
Solution: A pentagon has 5 sides and an octagon has 8, so both are traps. The Greek prefix "hexa" means six, so a six-sided polygon is a hexagon, so the answer is hexagon.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

21. In a triangle ABC, ∠A = 50° and ∠B = 60°. Then ∠C is:
(a) 60° (b) 80° (c) 70° (d) 90°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 80°, which comes from an arithmetic slip while subtracting from 180°. Since the three angles sum to 180°, ∠C = 180° − 50° − 60° = 70°, so the answer is 70°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

22. The exterior angle of a triangle is 120° and one of its interior opposite angles is 50°. The other interior opposite angle is:
(a) 60° (b) 80° (c) 70° (d) 50°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 60°, obtained by wrongly subtracting from 180° instead of using the exterior angle property. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles, so the required angle is 120° − 50° = 70°, so the answer is 70°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

23. The ratio of the angles of a triangle is 2 : 3 : 4. The largest angle is:
(a) 60° (b) 100° (c) 90° (d) 80°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 90°, which tempts students who assume the triangle must be right-angled. The parts sum to 9, so each part is 180° ÷ 9 = 20°, and the largest angle is 4 × 20° = 80°, so the answer is 80°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

24. The area of an equilateral triangle of side 6 cm is:
(a) 12√3 cm² (b) 9√3 cm² (c) 18√3 cm² (d) 36√3 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 36√3 cm², which comes from forgetting the √3/4 factor and using the whole square of the side. Area = (√3/4) × side² = (√3/4) × 36 = 9√3 cm², so the answer is 9√3 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

25. The diagonals of a rhombus are 10 cm and 24 cm. Its area is:
(a) 240 cm² (b) 120 cm² (c) 60 cm² (d) 480 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 240 cm², which is just the product of the diagonals without the one-half factor. Area of a rhombus = ½ × d₁ × d₂ = ½ × 10 × 24 = 120 cm², so the answer is 120 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
