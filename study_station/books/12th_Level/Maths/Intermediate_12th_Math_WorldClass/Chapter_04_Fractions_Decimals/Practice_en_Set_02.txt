26. Convert the mixed recurring decimal 0.1bar(6) into a simple fraction in lowest terms.
(a) 1/6 (b) 16/99 (c) 1/5 (d) 16/90
Answer: (a) 1/6
Solution: (16 - 1) / 90 = 15 / 90 = 1/6. The required value is 1/6. Hence, the correct option is (a).

29. Which of the two fractions 11/14 and 13/16 is larger?
(a) 11/14 (b) Both are equal (c) Cannot be determined (d) 13/16
Answer: (d) 13/16
Solution: Differences are equal: 14 - 11 = 3 and 16 - 13 = 3. In proper fractions with equal differences, the fraction with the larger numerator is larger: 13/16 > 11/14. The required value is 13/16. Hence, the correct option is (d).

39. Arrange in descending order: 4/5, 7/9, 11/14, 13/17.
(a) 13/17 > 11/14 > 7/9 > 4/5 (b) 4/5 > 11/14 > 7/9 > 13/17 (c) 4/5 > 7/9 > 11/14 > 13/17 (d) 11/14 > 4/5 > 7/9 > 13/17

40. If 1/3 of a tank is filled in 15 minutes, what fraction of the tank remains empty after 30 minutes at the same rate?

Solution: In 30 minutes, 2/3 of the tank is filled. The remaining empty portion is 1 - 2/3 = 1/3. The required value is 1/3. Hence, the correct option is (c).

46. A student was asked to multiply a number by 3/5, but mistakenly divided it by 3/5. The resulting answer was 32 more than the correct answer. What was the original number?
(a) 30 (b) 25 (c) 45 (d) 60
Answer: (a) 30
Solution: (5/3)x - (3/5)x = 32 => (25 - 9)x / 15 = 32 => 16x = 480 => x = 30. The required value is 30. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
