76. Find the sum of recurring decimals: 8.bar(31) + 0.bar(6) + 0.00bar(2).
(a) 8.98bar(20) (b) 8.97bar(20) (c) 8.99bar(2) (d) 8.982
Answer: (a) 8.98bar(20)
Solution: 0.3131... + 0.6666... = 0.9797... Adding 0.0022... gives 0.982020... Combining with integer 8 gives 8.98bar(20). The required value is 8.98bar(20). Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

77. Which of the following values of n causes the fraction 7/n to produce a NON-terminating repeating decimal: 16, 25, 35, 21?
(a) 16 (b) 21 (c) 25 (d) 35
Answer: (b) 21
Solution: 7/35 = 1/5 terminates. 7/21 = 1/3, whose denominator contains factor 3, producing a non-terminating repeating decimal. The required value is 21. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

80. What is the continued fraction expansion of 43/30?
(a) 1 + 1 / (2 + 1 / (3 + 1/4)) (b) 1 + 1 / (3 + 1 / (2 + 1/4)) (c) 1 + 1 / (2 + 1 / (4 + 1/3)) (d) 1 + 1 / (4 + 1 / (3 + 1/2))
Answer: (a) 1 + 1 / (2 + 1 / (3 + 1/4))
Solution: 43/30 = 1 + 13/30 = 1 + 1/(30/13). 30/13 = 2 + 4/13 = 2 + 1/(13/4). 13/4 = 3 + 1/4. Result is 1 + 1 / (2 + 1 / (3 + 1/4)). The required value is 1 + 1 / (2 + 1 / (3 + 1/4)). Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

81. If x = 0.bar(7) and y = 0.bar(2), find the value of (x + y) / (x - y).
(a) 5/9 (b) 9/5 (c) 7/2 (d) 2/7
Answer: (b) 9/5
Solution: x + y = 7/9 + 2/9 = 1. x - y = 7/9 - 2/9 = 5/9. Ratio = 1 / (5/9) = 9/5. The required value is 9/5. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

82. Which fraction is the greatest among 7/8, 8/9, 9/10, 10/11?
(a) 9/10 (b) 8/9 (c) 10/11 (d) 7/8
Answer: (c) 10/11
Solution: In proper fractions with equal difference (gap = 1), the fraction with the largest numerator is greatest: 10/11. The required value is 10/11. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: State PCS Pattern

Solution: For improper fractions with equal difference (gap = 3), larger numbers yield values closer to 1 (smaller values). Thus 14/11 is the smallest. The required value is 14/11. Hence, the correct option is (d).

86. A drum is 3/4 full of oil. When 15 liters of oil is drawn from it, it remains 7/12 full. Find the total capacity of the drum.
(a) 80 liters (b) 100 liters (c) 90 liters (d) 120 liters
Answer: (c) 90 liters
Solution: Fraction removed = 3/4 - 7/12 = 2/12 = 1/6. Capacity = 15 * 6 = 90 liters. The required value is 90 liters. Hence, the correct option is (c).

93. A student read 3/8 of a book on the first day and 4/5 of the remainder on the second day. If 30 pages remained unread, how many pages were in the book?
(a) 200 (b) 240 (c) 280 (d) 320
Answer: (b) 240
Solution: Remainder after day 1 = 5/8. Unread fraction after day 2 = (1 - 4/5) * 5/8 = (1/5) * 5/8 = 1/8. Total pages = 30 * 8 = 240. The required value is 240. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

100. If 0.bar(x) + 0.bar(y) = 7/9 and x - y = 3 for digits x and y, find the product (x * y).
(a) 12 (b) 10 (c) 8 (d) 14
Answer: (b) 10
Solution: (x + y)/9 = 7/9 => x + y = 7. Together with x - y = 3, we get x = 5, y = 2. Product is 5 * 2 = 10. The required value is 10. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
