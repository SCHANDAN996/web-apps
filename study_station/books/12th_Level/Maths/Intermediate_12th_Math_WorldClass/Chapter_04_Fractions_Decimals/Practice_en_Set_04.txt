76. What is the value of 3/7 + 2/5 − 1/35?
(a) 24/35 (b) 26/35 (c) 30/35 (d) 28/35
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 30/35 comes from adding 1/35 instead of subtracting it. The LCM of 7, 5 and 35 is 35, so 3/7 = 15/35 and 2/5 = 14/35, giving 15/35 + 14/35 − 1/35 = 28/35. The answer is 28/35.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

77. Simplify: 0.6 × 0.06 × 0.006
(a) 0.0216 (b) 0.000216 (c) 0.00216 (d) 0.0000216
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 0.00216 comes from counting only some of the decimal places instead of adding all of them. Since 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 and total decimal places = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6, the decimal goes six digits from the right. The answer is 0.000216.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

78. Which of the following fractions is the largest?
(a) 7/12 (b) 5/8 (c) 13/20 (d) 11/18
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 5/8 feels large because eighths are big chunks, but converting to decimals gives 7/12 ≈ 0.583, 5/8 = 0.625, 11/18 ≈ 0.611 and 13/20 = 0.65. The largest value is 0.65, so the answer is 13/20.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

79. The value of 4.5 ÷ 0.15 is:
(a) 30 (b) 3 (c) 0.3 (d) 300
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 3 comes from ignoring the decimal shift in the divisor. Multiplying numerator and denominator by 100 gives 450 ÷ 15 = 30. The answer is 30.

80. If 3/4 of a number is 36, what is 5/6 of the same number?
(a) 48 (b) 45 (c) 50 (d) 40
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 48 is the number itself, not 5/6 of it. The number = 36 × 4/3 = 48, and then 5/6 × 48 = 40. The answer is 40.

81. Express 0.363636… as a fraction in its simplest form.
(a) 9/25 (b) 36/100 (c) 4/11 (d) 3/8
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 36/100 treats the decimal as terminating at two places. Let x = 0.3636…, then 100x = 36.3636…, so 99x = 36 and x = 36/99 = 4/11. The answer is 4/11.

82. What is the value of 2.5 × 4.4 ÷ 1.1?
(a) 10 (b) 11 (c) 1 (d) 100
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 11 comes from computing 4.4 ÷ 1.1 = 4 and then adding instead of multiplying. Since 4.4 ÷ 1.1 = 4, we get 2.5 × 4 = 10. The answer is 10.

83. A man spends 2/5 of his salary on rent and 1/4 of the remainder on food. What fraction of his salary is left?
(a) 7/20 (b) 3/10 (c) 11/20 (d) 9/20
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 7/20 comes from subtracting 1/4 of the whole salary instead of 1/4 of the remainder. After rent the remainder is 3/5; food takes 1/4 × 3/5 = 3/20, so what is left is 3/5 − 3/20 = 12/20 − 3/20 = 9/20. The answer is 9/20.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

84. The sum of 0.333… and 0.666… is:
(a) 0.99 (b) 0.9 (c) 1 (d) 0.999
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 0.999 treats the decimals as terminating after three places. Since 0.333… = 1/3 and 0.666… = 2/3, the sum is 1/3 + 2/3 = 1. The answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

85. Which fraction lies between 1/4 and 1/2?
(a) 1/5 (b) 2/3 (c) 5/8 (d) 3/8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 1/5 looks close to 1/4 but 1/5 = 0.2, which is less than 1/4 = 0.25. Checking 3/8 = 0.375, it lies between 0.25 and 0.5, while 5/8 and 2/3 exceed 1/2. The answer is 3/8.

87. What is 7/8 of 0.64?
(a) 0.58 (b) 0.54 (c) 0.56 (d) 0.62

88. If 1/3 + 1/x = 1/2, then x equals:
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 3 comes from adding the denominators directly. Rearranging, 1/x = 1/2 − 1/3 = 1/6, so x = 6. The answer is 6.

(a) 0.666333… (b) 0.636363… (c) 0.633633… (d) 0.636336…

90. A fraction becomes 2/3 when 1 is added to its numerator and becomes 1/2 when 1 is added to its denominator. The fraction is:
(a) 4/7 (b) 5/9 (c) 3/5 (d) 7/11
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 4/7 satisfies only one of the two conditions. Testing 5/9: adding 1 to the numerator gives 6/9 = 2/3, and adding 1 to the denominator gives 5/10 = 1/2, so both conditions hold. The answer is 5/9.

92. The difference between 5/6 and 3/4 of 96 is:
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 16 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 12 comes from computing 5/6 − 3/4 = 1/12 and then misreading 1/12 of 96. Here 5/6 × 96 = 80 and 3/4 × 96 = 72, so the difference is 8. The answer is 8.

94. If 0.4 of a number is 48 more than 0.2 of the same number, the number is:
(a) 240 (b) 200 (c) 120 (d) 480
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 120 comes from dividing 48 by 0.4 instead of by the difference of the two parts. Here 0.4x − 0.2x = 48 gives 0.2x = 48, so x = 240. The answer is 240.

95. Simplify: 3/5 ÷ 9/10 × 5/6

97. A tank is 3/8 full. After adding 50 litres it becomes 5/8 full. The capacity of the tank is:
(a) 200 litres (b) 160 litres (c) 250 litres (d) 400 litres
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 400 litres comes from treating 50 litres as 1/8 of the capacity instead of the difference. The rise is 5/8 − 3/8 = 2/8 = 1/4 of capacity, which equals 50 litres, so capacity = 200 litres. The answer is 200 litres.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

98. Which is the smallest among 0.5, 0.55, 0.505, 0.055?
(a) 0.505 (b) 0.5 (c) 0.055 (d) 0.55
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 0.505 looks small because of the zero in the middle, but 0.505 > 0.5. Writing all as 0.500, 0.550, 0.505 and 0.055, the smallest is 0.055. The answer is 0.055.

99. If 2/3 of 3/4 of a number is 120, the number is:
(a) 240 (b) 180 (c) 160 (d) 360
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 180 comes from dividing 120 by 2/3 only and ignoring the second fraction. Since 2/3 × 3/4 = 1/2, half the number is 120, giving 240. The answer is 240.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

100. The value of (2.3)² − (1.7)² is:
(a) 4.0 (b) 2.04 (c) 2.4 (d) 1.2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 2.04 comes from squaring each decimal incorrectly or squaring the difference. Using a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b) = (4.0)(0.6) = 2.4. The answer is 2.4.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
