51. What is the value of 3/4 + 5/6 − 7/12?
(a) 13/12 (b) 11/12 (c) 1 (d) 5/6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The LCM of 4, 6 and 12 is 12. Converting: 9/12 + 10/12 − 7/12 = 12/12 = 1. A common trap is adding numerators and denominators directly, which gives 1/22 — never do that; always use a common denominator. The sum is 12/12, so the answer is 1.

53. A man spends 2/5 of his salary on rent and 1/4 of the remainder on food. What fraction of his salary is left?
(a) 3/10 (b) 7/20 (c) 9/20 (d) 1/2
Answer: (c)
Solution: After rent, remainder = 1 − 2/5 = 3/5. Food = 1/4 of 3/5 = 3/20. Left = 3/5 − 3/20 = 12/20 − 3/20 = 9/20. The trap is taking 1/4 of the whole salary instead of the remainder, which wrongly gives 7/20. The correct fraction left is 9/20.

59. What is the value of 1/2 + 1/6 + 1/12 + 1/20?
(a) 3/4 (b) 5/6 (c) 4/5 (d) 7/10
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each term telescopes: 1/2 = 1 − 1/2, 1/6 = 1/2 − 1/3, 1/12 = 1/3 − 1/4, 1/20 = 1/4 − 1/5. Sum = 1 − 1/5 = 4/5. The trap is using LCM 60 and making an arithmetic slip; the telescoping pattern is faster and safer. The sum is 4/5.
