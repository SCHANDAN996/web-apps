# 📋 Important Formulas — Fractions & Decimals

## 1. Basic Fraction Rules

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Fraction definition | Fraction = Numerator / Denominator | Identify parts of a whole |
| Equivalent fractions | a/b = (a×k)/(b×k) = (a÷k)/(b÷k) | Comparing or simplifying fractions |
| Comparing two fractions | a/b ⋛ c/d → compare a×d with c×b | Cross-multiplication; bigger product = bigger fraction |
| Addition / Subtraction | a/b ± c/d = (a×d ± c×b)/(b×d) | Use when denominators differ; or take LCM of denominators |
| Multiplication | (a/b) × (c/d) = (a×c)/(b×d) | Cancel common factors before multiplying |
| Division | (a/b) ÷ (c/d) = (a/b) × (d/c) | "Keep–Change–Flip": invert the second fraction |
| Reciprocal | Reciprocal of a/b = b/a | Needed for division of fractions |
| Mixed → Improper | N R/D = (N×D + R)/D | Convert before any calculation |
| Improper → Mixed | a/b = Quotient + Remainder/b | For final answers in mixed form |

## 2. Fraction–Decimal–Percentage Conversion

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Fraction → Decimal | Divide numerator by denominator | a/b = a ÷ b |
| Decimal → Fraction | 0.abcd… = abcd…/10ⁿ (n = decimal places) | Then reduce to lowest terms |
| Fraction → Percentage | (a/b) × 100% | Multiply fraction by 100 |
| Percentage → Fraction | x% = x/100 | Reduce to lowest terms |
| Decimal → Percentage | 0.xy = xy% | Shift decimal point 2 places right |
| Percentage → Decimal | x% = x/100 = 0.0x… | Shift decimal point 2 places left |

## 3. Recurring (Repeating) Decimals

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Pure recurring decimal | 0.a̅ = a/9; 0.ab̅ = ab/99; 0.abc̅ = abc/999 | Denominator = as many 9s as repeating digits |
| Mixed recurring decimal | 0.xy̅ = (xy − x)/90; 0.xy z̅ = (xyz − xy)/900 | Denominator: 9s for repeating digits, then 0s for non-repeating |
| General rule | 0.(non-rep)(rep)̅ = [whole number − non-repeating part] / [9s followed by 0s] | One-line conversion for any recurring decimal |

## 4. Operations on Decimals

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Addition / Subtraction | Align decimal points, then add/subtract | Fill missing places with zeros |
| Multiplication | Multiply as whole numbers; decimal places in product = sum of decimal places of factors | 0.2 × 0.03 → 2×3 = 6, places = 1+2 = 3 → 0.006 |
| Division by decimal | Multiply dividend & divisor by 10ⁿ to make divisor whole | 4.5 ÷ 0.15 = 450 ÷ 15 = 30 |
| ×10, ×100, ×1000 | Shift decimal point right by 1, 2, 3 places | Quick multiplication by powers of 10 |
| ÷10, ÷100, ÷1000 | Shift decimal point left by 1, 2, 3 places | Quick division by powers of 10 |

## 5. Comparison & Ordering

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Comparing decimals | Equalize decimal places with zeros, then compare digit by digit from left | 0.5 vs 0.45 → 0.50 > 0.45 |
| Comparing fractions (same numerator) | Smaller denominator → bigger fraction | 3/7 > 3/11 |
| Comparing fractions (same denominator) | Bigger numerator → bigger fraction | 5/9 > 4/9 |
| Fraction between two fractions | (a+c)/(b+d) lies between a/b and c/d | Quick check for "which lies between" questions |
| Ascending/Descending order | Convert all to decimals OR make denominators equal (LCM) | Use decimals for speed in exams |

## 7. Quick-Use Exam Rules

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Sum of fractions of a whole | Sum of all parts = 1 | "Remaining part" problems: remaining = 1 − (sum of given parts) |
| Part of a quantity | Part = Fraction × Total | "2/5 of ₹500" = (2/5) × 500 |
| Finding the whole | Whole = Part ÷ Fraction | If 3/7 of a number is 45, number = 45 × 7/3 |
| Successive fractions spent | Remaining = Total × (1 − f₁) × (1 − f₂) × … | "Spends 1/3, then 1/4 of rest" type problems |
| HCF/LCM of fractions | HCF = HCF(numerators)/LCM(denominators); LCM = LCM(numerators)/HCF(denominators) | Direct formula for fraction HCF–LCM questions |
| Terminating decimal test | a/b terminates ⇔ denominator (in lowest terms) has only factors 2 and/or 5 | 3/40 terminates (40 = 2³×5); 5/12 does not (12 has 3) |
