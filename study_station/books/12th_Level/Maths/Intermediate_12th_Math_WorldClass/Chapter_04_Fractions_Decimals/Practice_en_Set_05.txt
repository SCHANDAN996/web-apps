101. What is the value of 3/4 + 5/6 − 7/12?
(a) 11/12 (b) 5/6 (c) 7/6 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 11/12 comes from forgetting to subtract 7/12 fully. LCM of 4, 6, 12 is 12: 9/12 + 10/12 − 7/12 = 12/12 = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

102. Which of the following fractions is the largest: 7/9, 5/7, 11/14, 3/4?
(a) 7/9 (b) 5/7 (c) 11/14 (d) 3/4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 7/9 looks big but equals about 0.778. Converting: 5/7 ≈ 0.714, 11/14 ≈ 0.786, 3/4 = 0.75. The greatest is 11/14, so the answer is 11/14.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

103. A man spends 2/5 of his salary on rent and 1/4 of the remainder on food. What fraction of his salary is left?
(a) 9/20 (b) 7/20 (c) 3/5 (d) 11/20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 7/20 comes from subtracting 1/4 of the whole salary instead of the remainder. After rent, 3/5 remains; food takes 1/4 × 3/5 = 3/20. Left = 3/5 − 3/20 = 12/20 − 3/20 = 9/20, so the answer is 9/20.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

Solution: The tempting wrong option 1 comes from assuming both brackets are equal. First bracket = 2/3 × 3/4 = 1/2; second = 1/2 × 2/3 = 1/3. Then 1/2 ÷ 1/3 = 3/2, so the answer is 3/2.

115. A tank is 3/8 full. After adding 50 litres it becomes 5/8 full. The capacity of the tank is:
(a) 200 litres (b) 250 litres (c) 300 litres (d) 400 litres
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 400 comes from taking 50 as 1/8 of capacity. Rise = 5/8 − 3/8 = 2/8 = 1/4 of capacity = 50 litres, so capacity = 200 litres, so the answer is 200 litres.

121. The value of (0.75 × 0.75 − 0.25 × 0.25) ÷ (0.75 − 0.25) is:
(a) 1 (b) 0.5 (c) 1.5 (d) 0.25
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 0.5 comes from cancelling the terms wrongly. Numerator = a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b), so the expression = a + b = 0.75 + 0.25 = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

Solution: The tempting wrong option 268.9 comes from shifting the decimal by the wrong number of places. 0.0003718 = 3.718 × 10⁻⁴, so 1/0.0003718 = 0.2689 × 10⁴ = 2689, so the answer is 2689.

123. A fraction becomes 3/5 when 1 is added to both its numerator and denominator, and becomes 3/7 when 2 is subtracted from both. The fraction is:
(a) 7/11 (b) 4/7 (c) 5/9 (d) 3/5
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 4/7 satisfies only the second condition, not the first. Let the fraction be x/y: (x+1)/(y+1) = 3/5 gives 5x − 3y = −2; (x−2)/(y−2) = 3/7 gives 7x − 3y = 8. Subtracting, 2x = 10, so x = 5 and y = 9, so the answer is 5/9.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

124. The value of 0.222… + 0.333… + 0.444… + 0.999… is:
(a) 2 (b) 1.8 (c) 1.9 (d) 2.1
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 1.8 comes from writing 0.999… as 0.9 instead of 1. Each repeating single digit n equals n/9: 2/9 + 3/9 + 4/9 + 9/9 = 18/9 = 2, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

125. A man gave 1/4 of his money to his son, 1/5 of the remainder to his daughter, and the rest to his wife. If the wife got ₹36,000, the total money was:
(a) ₹72,000 (b) ₹60,000 (c) ₹75,000 (d) ₹80,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 72,000 comes from treating the wife's share as 1/2 of the total. Son gets 1/4, remainder 3/4; daughter gets 1/5 × 3/4 = 3/20. Wife gets 3/4 − 3/20 = 12/20 = 3/5. Total = 36,000 × 5/3 = ₹60,000, so the answer is ₹60,000.
Source: PYQ-style
