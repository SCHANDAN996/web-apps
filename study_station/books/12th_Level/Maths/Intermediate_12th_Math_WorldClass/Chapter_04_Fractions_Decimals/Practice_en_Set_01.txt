Questions 1–25: Easy

1. Convert the decimal 0.75 into a simple fraction in lowest terms.
(a) 3/4 (b) 7/10 (c) 4/5 (d) 75/10
Answer: (a) 3/4
Solution: 0.75 = 75 / 100. Dividing numerator and denominator by 25 gives 3/4. The required value is 3/4. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. Convert the pure recurring decimal 0.bar(6) into a simple fraction in lowest terms.

Answer: (b) 2/3
Solution: 0.bar(6) = 6 / 9. Dividing by 3 gives 2/3. The required value is 2/3. Hence, the correct option is (b).

5. Which of the following fractions represents a terminating decimal: 1/6, 3/8, 2/7, 5/12?
(a) 3/8 (b) 1/6 (c) 2/7 (d) 5/12
Answer: (a) 3/8
Solution: The denominator 8 = 2^3 has only prime factor 2, which satisfies the terminating decimal condition 2^m * 5^n. 3/8 = 0.375. The required value is 3/8. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

7. Which of the two fractions 5/8 and 7/12 is greater?
(a) 7/12 (b) Both are equal (c) 5/8 (d) Cannot be determined
Answer: (c) 5/8
Solution: Cross multiplication: 5 * 12 = 60, and 8 * 7 = 56. Since 60 > 56, 5/8 > 7/12. The required value is 5/8. Hence, the correct option is (c).
