1. What is the value of 3/5 + 1/5?
(a) 3/25 (b) 2/5 (c) 4/10 (d) 4/5
Answer: (d)
Solution: When denominators are equal, add only the numerators: 3 + 1 = 4, so the sum is 4/5. The tempting wrong option 4/10 comes from wrongly adding the denominators too, which is never done. So the answer is 4/5.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

2. Which of the following fractions is the smallest?
(a) 1/2 (b) 1/3 (c) 1/4 (d) 1/6
Answer: (d)
Solution: For unit fractions, the larger the denominator, the smaller the fraction, because the whole is divided into more parts. Among 2, 3, 4 and 6, the largest denominator is 6, so the answer is 1/6.

3. Convert 0.75 into a fraction in its simplest form.
(a) 7/5 (b) 3/4 (c) 75/10 (d) 1/4
Answer: (b)
Solution: 0.75 = 75/100, and dividing numerator and denominator by 25 gives 3/4. The option 75/10 is a trap from forgetting that two decimal places mean division by 100, not 10. So the answer is 3/4.

4. What is the value of 2/3 × 9/4?

Answer: (a)
Solution: Multiply numerators and denominators: (2 × 9)/(3 × 4) = 18/12 = 3/2 after dividing by 6. The option 18/7 is a trap from adding denominators instead of multiplying. So the answer is 3/2.

8. Which decimal is the greatest?

Answer: (c)

Answer: (d)
Solution: 1/3 ≈ 0.333 and 1/2 = 0.5, and only 0.4 lies between them. The option 0.3 is a trap because it looks close to 1/3 but is actually smaller than 0.333. So the answer is 0.4.

25. A student spends 2/5 of his money on books and 1/4 on food. What fraction of his money is left?
(a) 1/2 (b) 13/20 (c) 3/9 (d) 7/20
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total spent = 2/5 + 1/4 = 8/20 + 5/20 = 13/20, so the money left is 1 − 13/20 = 7/20. The option 13/20 is a trap because it is the fraction spent, not the fraction left. So the answer is 7/20.
