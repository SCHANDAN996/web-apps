# 🪄 Short Tricks — Fractions & Decimals

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 1 — "KFC" for Dividing Fractions**  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Keep – Flip – Change!**

To divide fractions: **K**eep the first fraction, **F**lip the second (reciprocal), **C**hange ÷ to ×.

Example: 3/4 ÷ 2/5 → Keep 3/4, Flip 2/5 → 5/2, Change to × → 3/4 × 5/2 = 15/8.

🍗 Picture a KFC bucket: you KEEP your chicken, FLIP the lid, CHANGE seats. Never divide directly — always KFC!

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 2 — "Butterfly" for Comparing**     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

To compare a/b and c/d, draw a **butterfly**: cross-multiply a×d and b×c.

Compare 3/7 vs 4/9:
- 3 × 9 = 27 (left wing)
- 7 × 4 = 28 (right wing)

Bigger wing = bigger fraction → 4/9 wins! 🦋

The butterfly's wings never lie — the side with the bigger product has the bigger fraction.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 3 — "DON'T ADD TOPS AND BOTTOMS!"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Memory hook: **"Pizza slices, not pizza boxes!"**

1/2 + 1/3 is NOT 2/5. You can't add slices of different-sized pizzas. First make the slices equal (common denominator = 6): 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

🍕 Chant: *"Same-size slices, then add the tops — the bottom stays put, it never hops!"*

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 4 — "Line Up the Dots" (Decimals)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

For decimal addition/subtraction: **"Dots in a row, then off you go!"**

```
  12.5
+  3.07   ← decimal points stacked like soldiers
------
  15.57
```

Fill empty places with zeros (12.50 + 3.07). The decimal point in the answer drops straight down like an elevator. 🛗

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 5 — "Count the Hops" (× Decimals)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Multiplying decimals: **Multiply like whole numbers, then COUNT THE HOPS.**

0.4 × 0.03:
- Ignore dots: 4 × 3 = 12
- Count decimal places: 0.4 has 1 hop, 0.03 has 2 hops → total 3 hops
- Place the dot 3 hops from the right: 0.012

🐰 The bunny hops left — total hops of both numbers!

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 6 — "÷10 = Left, ×10 = Right"**     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**"Multiply → Move Right (More). Divide → Dive Left (Less)."**

- 4.56 × 100 = 456 (dot moves 2 RIGHT — number gets MORE)
- 4.56 ÷ 100 = 0.0456 (dot moves 2 LEFT — number gets LESS)

Number of zeros = number of moves. **M-R, D-L** — remember "Mister Right, Doctor Left"! 👨‍⚕️

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 7 — "9s in the Denominator"**       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Repeating decimals to fractions: **"One 9 per repeating digit."**

- 0.777… = 7/9 (one repeating digit → one 9)
- 0.454545… = 45/99 (two repeating digits → two 9s)
- 0.123123… = 123/999

🎵 Sing it: *"Repeating digits on top, nines below — that's how pure repeaters go!"*

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 8 — "9s and 0s" (Mixed Repeaters)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

For 0.1666… type decimals: **"9 for repeaters, 0 for non-repeaters."**

0.1666…:
- Repeating part: 6 → one 9
- Non-repeating part: 1 → one 0
- Denominator = 90
- Numerator = (16 − 1) = 15
- Answer: 15/90 = 1/6 ✓

🔑 Rule: Numerator = (whole number formed) − (non-repeating part).

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 9 — "DENOMINATOR = DOWN"**          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Confused which is numerator and which is denominator?

**D**enominator = **D**own (both start with D!). The denominator sits DOWN below the line.

Numerator = Number of parts you have (think "**N**orth" — it's on top).

🏠 Memory palace: The Denominator lives in the Downstairs Dungeon; the Numerator Naps in the North attic.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 10 — "Bigger Bottom = Smaller Slice"**│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Comparing unit fractions (same numerator): **the bigger the denominator, the SMALLER the fraction.**

1/3 > 1/7 > 1/12

🎂 Think: one cake shared by 3 people vs 12 people — more people (bigger denominator) = tinier slice for you!

Reverse trap alert: examiners love asking "which is largest?" with options like 1/9 — the big-looking 9 is the SMALL answer.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 11 — "Half-Quarter-Eighth Ladder"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Instant fraction↔decimal conversions — memorize the ladder:

- 1/2 = 0.5
- 1/4 = 0.25 | 3/4 = 0.75
- 1/8 = 0.125 | 3/8 = 0.375 | 5/8 = 0.625 | 7/8 = 0.875
- 1/5 = 0.2 | 1/20 = 0.05 | 1/25 = 0.04

🪜 Trick: 1/8 = 0.125 — remember "**125 is half of 250, which is half of 500, which is half of 1000**" — the 8-family halving chain!

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 12 — "Percent = Per-CENT = /100"**  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

A **cent**ury = 100 years, a **cent**imetre = 1/100 metre, 100 **cent**s = 1 dollar/rupee-100-paise.

So 37% = 37/100 = 0.37. To convert % → decimal: **"Drop the %, hop two left."**

- 7% = 0.07 (not 0.7! — classic trap)
- 125% = 1.25

💯 CENT is your anchor word — it always means 100.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 13 — "Flip to Kill the Decimal"**   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Dividing BY a decimal (e.g., 6 ÷ 0.25): **shift both dots right equally until the divisor is whole.**

6 ÷ 0.25 → 600 ÷ 25 = 24 ✓

⚖️ Think of a balance scale: whatever you multiply the divisor by, multiply the dividend by the same — the scale stays level, the answer stays true.

Bonus: ÷ 0.25 = × 4 | ÷ 0.5 = × 2 | ÷ 0.2 = × 5 | ÷ 0.125 = × 8. **"Dividing by a tiny decimal is secretly multiplying!"**

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 14 — "HCF-LCM House" (Loci)**       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

For fractions:
- **LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators**
- **HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators**

🏠 Loci trick: Picture a house. **LCM lives in the LARGE Living room** — it takes the LCM of the tops. **HCF Hides in the tiny attic** — it takes the HCF of the tops.

Memory line: *"LCM wants the biggest upstairs; HCF stays humble upstairs — downstairs they swap!"*

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 15 — "0.999… = 1" Shock Card**      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Exam favourite! 0.999… = 9/9 = **1** (using Trick 7: one repeating digit 9 → 9/9).

🤯 Proof in 3 lines:
- x = 0.999…
- 10x = 9.999…
- 10x − x = 9 → 9x = 9 → x = 1

When an option says "0.999… < 1", it's the trap. The infinite repeater IS exactly 1.
