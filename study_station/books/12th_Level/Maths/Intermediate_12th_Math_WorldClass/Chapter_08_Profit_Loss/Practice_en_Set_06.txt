Q126. (Very Hard) A dealer sells a table at 15% profit and a chair at 12% loss, earning Rs 540 in total. If he had sold the table at 12% loss and the chair at 15% profit, he would have gained Rs 180. Find the total cost price of the table and the chair together.
(A) Rs 24,000  (B) Rs 20,000  (C) Rs 25,000  (D) Rs 22,000

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Let table cost be T and chair cost be C. Setting up equations: 0.15T - 0.12C = 540 and -0.12T + 0.15C = 180. Adding both equations gives: 0.03T + 0.03C = 720 => 0.03(T + C) = 720 => T + C = 720 / 0.03 = Rs 24,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 24,000, corresponding to option (A).

Q127. (Very Hard) A person bought a certain number of articles at 4 for Rs 5 and an equal number at 5 for Rs 6. He mixed them together and sold them at 9 for Rs 11. In the whole transaction, he suffered a loss of Rs 3. How many articles did he buy in all?
(A) 1,200  (B) 1,080  (C) 960  (D) 1,140

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Taking 20 articles of each variety (total 40 articles): Cost of first 20 = 20 * (5/4) = Rs 25. Cost of second 20 = 20 * (6/5) = Rs 24. Total Cost Price for 40 articles = Rs 49. Selling Price for 40 articles = 40 * (11/9) = Rs 440/9. Loss on 40 articles = 49 - 440/9 = Re 1/9. Therefore, a loss of Re 1 occurs on 40 * 9 = 360 articles. A loss of Rs 3 occurs on 360 * 3 = 1,080 articles. Psychological Trap: A frequent pitfall is using 20 articles as the total lot rather than 40, or making an algebraic error when dividing by the fractional loss of 1/9. Therefore, the correct value is 1,080, corresponding to option (B).
