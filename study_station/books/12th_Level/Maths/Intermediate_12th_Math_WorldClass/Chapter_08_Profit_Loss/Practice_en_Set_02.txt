Q27. (Medium) A dishonest dealer professes to sell his goods at cost price, but he uses a weight of 950 grams for a kilogram weight. Find his gain percent.
(A) 5%  (B) 5 5/19%  (C) 5.5%  (D) 5 1/4%

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Cost incurred by merchant is for 950 grams, while revenue collected corresponds to 1,000 grams. Profit = 1000 - 950 = 50 grams on a base of 950 grams. Gain percentage = (50 / 950) * 100 = 100 / 19 = 5 5/19% (or approximately 5.26%). Psychological Trap: Students often mistakenly divide the error 50 g by 1,000 g to get 5%, forgetting that profit is evaluated on the actual outlay of 950 g. Therefore, the correct value is 5 5/19%, corresponding to option (B).

(A) Rs 100  (B) Rs 60  (C) Rs 80  (D) Rs 120

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Let initial CP = 4x and SP = 5x (since 25% profit means 5/4 ratio). Reduced quantities: CP = 4x - 20 and SP = 5x - 20. With 33 1/3% profit, SP / CP = 4 / 3. Cross multiplying: 3(5x - 20) = 4(4x - 20) => 15x - 60 = 16x - 80 => x = 20. Original Cost Price = 4x = 4 * 20 = Rs 80. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 80, corresponding to option (C).
