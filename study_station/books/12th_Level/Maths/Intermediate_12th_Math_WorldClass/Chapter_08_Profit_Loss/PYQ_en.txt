# Previous Year Questions (Intermediate / 12th Level Patterns) - Profit & Loss

## Pattern 1: Fundamental Ratio of CP and SP
Q: If the cost price of 25 articles is equal to the selling price of 20 articles, find the profit percentage.
Solution:
25 * CP = 20 * SP => SP / CP = 25 / 20 = 5 / 4.
Profit = 5 - 4 = 1 unit on CP of 4 units.
Profit % = (1 / 4) * 100 = 25%.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

## Pattern 2: Linking MP, CP, Profit %, and Discount %
Q: A trader marks his goods at such a price that after allowing a discount of 12.5%, he still makes a profit of 15%. If the cost price of the article is Rs 1,400, find its marked price.
Solution:
Using MP / CP = (100 + P%) / (100 - D%):
MP / 1400 = (100 + 15) / (100 - 12.5) = 115 / 87.5 = 1150 / 875 = 46 / 35.
MP = 1400 * (46 / 35) = 40 * 46 = Rs 1,840.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

## Pattern 3: Equal Selling Price with Identical Profit and Loss Percentages
Q: A man sells two flats for Rs 19,95,000 each. On one he gains 5% and on the other he loses 5%. What is his overall gain or loss percent in the whole transaction?
Solution:
When SP is identical and gain% = loss% = x:
There is always an overall loss = (x / 10)^2 % = (5 / 10)^2 % = 0.25% loss (or 1/4% loss).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

## Pattern 4: Dishonest Dealer with Underweight Measure
Q: A dishonest grocer sells rice at an 8% profit over cost price and also uses a weight that is 20% less than the actual weight. Find his total gain percentage.
Solution:
Let cost of 1,000 g be Rs 1,000.
He delivers 800 g (cost to grocer = Rs 800).
He sells at 8% profit on marked Rs 1,000, so he collects Rs 1,080.
Net profit = 1,080 - 800 = Rs 280 on an outlay of Rs 800.
Gain % = (280 / 800) * 100 = 35%.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

## Pattern 5: Successive Discounts Combination
Q: Find the single equivalent discount for three successive discounts of 20%, 10%, and 5%.
Solution:
Multiplying factor = (1 - 0.20) * (1 - 0.10) * (1 - 0.05) = 0.80 * 0.90 * 0.95 = 0.684.
Equivalent discount = (1 - 0.684) * 100 = 31.6%.
Source: IBPS PO Pattern

## Pattern 6: "Buy X, Get Y Free" Combined with Extra Discount
Q: A shopkeeper offers a scheme of "Buy 5, Get 3 Free" and in addition provides an extra cash discount of 20%. What is the total effective discount percentage?
Solution:
First scheme multiplier: Customer gets 8 articles for the price of 5 => Factor = 5/8.
Extra discount multiplier: (1 - 0.20) = 4/5.
Combined Factor = (5/8) * (4/5) = 4/8 = 1/2.
Effective discount = (1 - 1/2) * 100 = 50%.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

## Pattern 7: Selling at Two Different Prices
Q: An article is sold at a loss of 10%. Had it been sold for Rs 90 more, there would have been a gain of 8%. Find the cost price of the article.
Solution:
The difference between -10% and +8% is 8 - (-10) = 18% of CP.
18% of CP = Rs 90 => 1% of CP = Rs 5 => CP = 100 * 5 = Rs 500.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

## Pattern 8: Alligation Applied to Stock Split
Q: A merchant has 1,000 kg of sugar, part of which he sells at 8% profit and the rest at 18% profit. He gains 14% on the whole. What quantity was sold at 18% profit?
Solution:
Using alligation:
(18 - 14) : (14 - 8) = 4 : 6 = 2 : 3.
Ratio of quantities = 2 : 3 (sum of parts = 5).
Quantity sold at 18% profit = (3 / 5) * 1,000 = 600 kg.
Source: CDS Pattern

## Pattern 9: Breakage and Spoilage Model
Q: A fruit vendor buys 400 oranges at Rs 2 each. During transport, 40 oranges get spoiled. At what price per orange must he sell the remaining oranges to earn an overall profit of 35%?
Solution:
Total CP = 400 * 2 = Rs 800.
Required revenue for 35% profit = 800 * 1.35 = Rs 1,080.
Remaining salable oranges = 400 - 40 = 360 oranges.
Selling price per orange = 1,080 / 360 = Rs 3.00.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

## Pattern 10: Asymmetric Equal SP Transaction
Q: Two items are sold for Rs 4,800 each. On the first, the seller gains 20%, and on the second, he loses 20%. Find the actual monetary gain or loss.
Solution:
CP of first item = 4,800 / 1.20 = Rs 4,000.
CP of second item = 4,800 / 0.80 = Rs 6,000.
Total CP = 4,000 + 6,000 = Rs 10,000.
Total SP = 4,800 + 4,800 = Rs 9,600.
Net Loss = 10,000 - 9,600 = Rs 400.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
