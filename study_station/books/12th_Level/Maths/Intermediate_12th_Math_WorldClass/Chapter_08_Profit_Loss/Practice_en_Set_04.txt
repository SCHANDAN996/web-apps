Q76. (Hard) A dishonest merchant cheats to the extent of 10% in buying as well as in selling by using false weights. What is his total gain percentage?
(A) 22.22%  (B) 21%  (C) 20%  (D) 25%

Answer: (A)

Solution:
When purchasing, he procures 1,100 g for the cost of 1,000 g. When selling, he delivers only 900 g for the price of 1,000 g. Ratio of Selling Price to Cost Price = 1100 / 900 = 11 / 9. Total gain percentage = [(11 - 9) / 9] * 100 = (2 / 9) * 100 = 22.22%. Psychological Trap: Many examinees erroneously use the net percentage formula 10 + 10 + (10*10)/100 = 21%, which presumes currency markup rather than physical weight cheating on delivery. Therefore, the correct value is 22.22%, corresponding to option (A).
