76. A shopkeeper sells an article at a 20% profit. If the cost price is ₹500, what is the selling price?
(a) ₹600 (b) ₹550 (c) ₹575 (d) ₹625
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option adds only part of the profit or misreads the percentage. Profit = 20% of 500 = ₹100, so SP = 500 + 100 = ₹600, so the answer is ₹600.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
