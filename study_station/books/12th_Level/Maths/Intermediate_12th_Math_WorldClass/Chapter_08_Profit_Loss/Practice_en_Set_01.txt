Q1. (Easy) A person buys an article for Rs 450 and sells it for Rs 540. Find his profit percentage.
(A) 20%  (B) 25%  (C) 18%  (D) 22%

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Cost Price = Rs 450 and Selling Price = Rs 540. Profit = 540 - 450 = Rs 90. Profit percentage = (Profit / CP) * 100 = (90 / 450) * 100 = 20%. Therefore, the correct value is 20%, corresponding to option (A).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q2. (Easy) An almirah was bought for Rs 14,000 and Rs 500 were spent on its transportation and repairs. It was then sold for Rs 16,675. Find the profit percentage.
(A) 16%  (B) 15%  (C) 14%  (D) 18%

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Total effective Cost Price = Purchase Price + Overheads = 14000 + 500 = Rs 14500. Selling Price = Rs 16675. Profit = 16675 - 14500 = Rs 2175. Profit percentage = (2175 / 14500) * 100 = 15%. Therefore, the correct value is 15%, corresponding to option (B).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q3. (Easy) A retailer buys a cycle for Rs 2,400. At what price should he sell it to gain 12.5%?
(A) Rs 2,650  (B) Rs 2,750  (C) Rs 2,700  (D) Rs 2,800

Answer: (C)

Solution:
A gain of 12.5% corresponds to a multiplying fraction of 1/8. Selling Price = CP * (1 + 1/8) = 2400 * (9/8) = 300 * 9 = Rs 2,700. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 2,700, corresponding to option (C).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q4. (Easy) By selling a watch for Rs 1,440, a man loses 10%. At what price should he sell it to gain 15%?
(A) Rs 1,800  (B) Rs 1,900  (C) Rs 1,760  (D) Rs 1,840

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Given that 90% of CP = Rs 1440, we find CP = 1440 / 0.90 = Rs 1600. To gain 15%, the desired Selling Price = CP * 1.15 = 1600 * 1.15 = Rs 1,840. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 1,840, corresponding to option (D).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q5. (Easy) If the cost price of 18 articles is equal to the selling price of 15 articles, find the gain percentage.
(A) 20%  (B) 25%  (C) 16.67%  (D) 15%

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Setting up the equivalence: 18 * CP = 15 * SP => SP / CP = 18 / 15 = 6 / 5. Gain on 5 units of cost is 1 unit. Gain percentage = (1 / 5) * 100 = 20%. Psychological Trap: Candidates often mistakenly divide the difference of 3 by the number of purchased articles (18) to arrive at 16.67%, whereas gain must be calculated on the goods sold (15). Therefore, the correct value is 20%, corresponding to option (A).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q7. (Easy) An item listed at Rs 800 is offered at two successive discounts of 20% and 10%. Find the final selling price.

Q13. (Easy) Find the single discount equivalent to two successive discounts of 15% and 10%.
(A) 23.5%  (B) 25%  (C) 24%  (D) 22.5%

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Using the equivalent discount formula: D_eq = d_1 + d_2 - (d_1 * d_2) / 100 = 15 + 10 - (15 * 10) / 100 = 25 - 1.5 = 23.5%. Therefore, the correct value is 23.5%, corresponding to option (A).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q14. (Easy) A cloth merchant offers a promotional scheme: 'Buy 3 shirts, Get 1 free'. What is the effective discount percentage?
(A) 33.33%  (B) 25%  (C) 20%  (D) 30%

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Customer receives a total of 3 + 1 = 4 shirts while paying only for 3. The 1 free shirt is evaluated over the total basket of 4 shirts. Effective discount percentage = (1 / 4) * 100 = 25%. Psychological Trap: Many examinees incorrectly divide the free item by paid items (1/3) yielding 33.33%, ignoring that discount is based on the total items delivered. Therefore, the correct value is 25%, corresponding to option (B).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q15. (Easy) The marked price of a radio is Rs 4,800. The shopkeeper gives a discount of 10% and gains 8%. If no discount is allowed, his gain percent would be:
(A) 18%  (B) 22%  (C) 20%  (D) 25%

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Using the golden ratio formula: MP / CP = (100 + P) / (100 - D) = 108 / 90 = 6 / 5. When no discount is allowed, Selling Price equals Marked Price. Gain on 5 units of CP is 6 - 5 = 1 unit. Gain percentage = (1 / 5) * 100 = 20%. Therefore, the correct value is 20%, corresponding to option (C).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q16. (Easy) A grocer buys 30 kg of wheat at Rs 15 per kg and 20 kg of wheat at Rs 20 per kg. He mixes them and sells the mixture at Rs 18 per kg. Find his total profit.
(A) Rs 60  (B) Rs 45  (C) Rs 55  (D) Rs 50

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Total Cost Price = (30 * 15) + (20 * 20) = 450 + 400 = Rs 850. Total weight = 50 kg. Total Selling Price = 50 * 18 = Rs 900. Total Profit = 900 - 850 = Rs 50. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 50, corresponding to option (D).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q24. (Easy) A watch was sold at a loss of 10%. If it had been sold for Rs 140 more, there would have been a profit of 4%. What is the cost price of the watch?

Q25. (Easy) The cost price of an article is Rs 640. What must be its marked price so that after giving a discount of 20%, the dealer gains 25%?
(A) Rs 960  (B) Rs 1,000  (C) Rs 1,050  (D) Rs 1,080

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Using the golden ratio formula: MP / CP = (100 + P) / (100 - D) => MP / 640 = 125 / 80 = 25 / 16. Therefore, MP = 640 * (25 / 16) = 40 * 25 = Rs 1,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 1,000, corresponding to option (B).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern
