101. Find the missing number: 4, 9, 19, 39, 79, ?
(a) 159 (b) 149 (c) 169 (d) 139
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option 149 comes from adding 70 to 79, but the differences are 5, 10, 20, 40 — each doubling — so the next difference is 80, not 70. Each term is also previous × 2 + 1: 79 × 2 + 1 = 159, so the answer is 159.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Find the missing number: 7, 12, 19, 28, 39, ?
(a) 50 (b) 52 (c) 51 (d) 54
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 50 assumes a constant difference of 11, but the differences are 5, 7, 9, 11 — increasing by 2 — so the next difference is 13. Adding 13 to 39 gives 52, so the answer is 52.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (pattern)

(a) 64 (b) 81 (c) 100 (d) 121
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option 81 is 9², but the series is squares of consecutive numbers with 16 (4²) missing in the middle — actually it is squares of 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, so the next is 8² = 64.
