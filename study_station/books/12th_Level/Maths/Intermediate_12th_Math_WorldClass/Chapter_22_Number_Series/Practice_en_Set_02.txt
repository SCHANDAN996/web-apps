26. Find the missing number in the series: 4, 9, 19, 39, 79, ?
(a) 159 (b) 149 (c) 169 (d) 139
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice 149 comes from doubling and adding 1 only at the last step, but the rule is consistent: each term is double the previous term plus 1. 4×2+1 = 9, 9×2+1 = 19, 19×2+1 = 39, 39×2+1 = 79, so 79×2+1 = 159, so the answer is 159.
