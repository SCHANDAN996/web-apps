1. Find the next term: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ?
(a) 11 (b) 12 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (b)
Solution: Each term increases by 2. The tempting option 11 comes from adding 1, but the constant difference is 2, so 10 + 2 = 12. The answer is 12.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

2. Find the next term: 5, 10, 15, 20, ?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 30 (d) 25

Solution: This is a simple arithmetic series with common difference 5. Option 24 tempts those who add 4 by mistake, but 20 + 5 = 25. The answer is 25.
