51. Find the missing number: 4, 9, 19, 39, 79, ?
(a) 149 (b) 139 (c) 169 (d) 159
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 149 comes from doubling and adding 1 only at the last step, but the rule is consistent: each term is previous × 2 + 1. Check: 4×2+1 = 9, 9×2+1 = 19, 19×2+1 = 39, 39×2+1 = 79, so 79×2+1 = 159. The answer is 159.
