# 📋 Important Formulas — Trigonometry

## 1. Basic Ratios (in a right-angled triangle)

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Sine | sin θ = Perpendicular / Hypotenuse | Ratio involving opposite side |
| Cosine | cos θ = Base / Hypotenuse | Ratio involving adjacent side |
| Tangent | tan θ = Perpendicular / Base = sin θ / cos θ | When hypotenuse is not given |
| Cosecant | cosec θ = 1 / sin θ | Reciprocal of sine |
| Secant | sec θ = 1 / cos θ | Reciprocal of cosine |
| Cotangent | cot θ = 1 / tan θ = cos θ / sin θ | Reciprocal of tangent |

## 2. Standard Values Table

| θ | 0° | 30° | 45° | 60° | 90° |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sin θ | 0 | 1/2 | 1/√2 | √3/2 | 1 |
| cos θ | 1 | √3/2 | 1/√2 | 1/2 | 0 |
| tan θ | 0 | 1/√3 | 1 | √3 | Not defined |
| cot θ | Not defined | √3 | 1 | 1/√3 | 0 |
| sec θ | 1 | 2/√3 | √2 | 2 | Not defined |
| cosec θ | Not defined | 2 | √2 | 2/√3 | 1 |

## 3. Fundamental Identities

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Pythagorean Identity 1 | sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 | Simplifying sin–cos expressions |
| Pythagorean Identity 2 | 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ | Converting tan ↔ sec |
| Pythagorean Identity 3 | 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ | Converting cot ↔ cosec |
| Derived form | sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 | Factor as (sec θ − tan θ)(sec θ + tan θ) |
| Derived form | cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1 | Factor as (cosec θ − cot θ)(cosec θ + cot θ) |

## 4. Complementary Angle Formulas

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Sine–Cosine | sin(90° − θ) = cos θ | Converting between sin and cos |
| Cosine–Sine | cos(90° − θ) = sin θ | Converting between cos and sin |
| Tangent–Cotangent | tan(90° − θ) = cot θ | Converting between tan and cot |
| Cotangent–Tangent | cot(90° − θ) = tan θ | Converting between cot and tan |
| Secant–Cosecant | sec(90° − θ) = cosec θ | Converting between sec and cosec |
| Cosecant–Secant | cosec(90° − θ) = sec θ | Converting between cosec and sec |

## 5. Signs in Quadrants (ASTC Rule)

| Quadrant | Angles | Positive Ratios |
|---|---|---|
| I | 0°–90° | All (A) |
| II | 90°–180° | Sin, Cosec (S) |
| III | 180°–270° | Tan, Cot (T) |
| IV | 270°–360° | Cos, Sec (C) |

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Negative angles | sin(−θ) = −sin θ; cos(−θ) = cos θ; tan(−θ) = −tan θ | Handling negative angles |
| Supplementary | sin(180° − θ) = sin θ; cos(180° − θ) = −cos θ | Second quadrant values |
| | tan(180° + θ) = tan θ; cos(180° + θ) = −cos θ | Third quadrant values |
| | sin(360° − θ) = −sin θ; cos(360° − θ) = cos θ | Fourth quadrant values |

## 6. Sum and Difference Formulas

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| sin(A + B) | sin A cos B + cos A sin B | Expanding compound angles |
| sin(A − B) | sin A cos B − cos A sin B | Expanding compound angles |
| cos(A + B) | cos A cos B − sin A sin B | Expanding compound angles |
| cos(A − B) | cos A cos B + sin A sin B | Expanding compound angles |
| tan(A + B) | (tan A + tan B) / (1 − tan A tan B) | Compound angle in tan |
| tan(A − B) | (tan A − tan B) / (1 + tan A tan B) | Compound angle in tan |

## 7. Double Angle Formulas

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| sin 2θ | 2 sin θ cos θ = 2 tan θ / (1 + tan²θ) | Doubling an angle |
| cos 2θ | cos²θ − sin²θ = 2cos²θ − 1 = 1 − 2sin²θ | Choose form as per given data |
| tan 2θ | 2 tan θ / (1 − tan²θ) | Doubling in tan |

## 8. Triple Angle Formulas

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| sin 3θ | 3 sin θ − 4 sin³θ | Tripling an angle |
| cos 3θ | 4 cos³θ − 3 cos θ | Tripling an angle |
| tan 3θ | (3 tan θ − tan³θ) / (1 − 3 tan²θ) | Tripling in tan |

## 9. Product-to-Sum Formulas

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| 2 sin A cos B | sin(A + B) + sin(A − B) | Product → sum conversion |
| 2 cos A sin B | sin(A + B) − sin(A − B) | Product → sum conversion |
| 2 cos A cos B | cos(A + B) + cos(A − B) | Product → sum conversion |
| 2 sin A sin B | cos(A − B) − cos(A + B) | Product → sum conversion |

## 10. Sum-to-Product Formulas

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| sin C + sin D | 2 sin((C+D)/2) cos((C−D)/2) | Sum → product conversion |
| sin C − sin D | 2 cos((C+D)/2) sin((C−D)/2) | Difference → product |
| cos C + cos D | 2 cos((C+D)/2) cos((C−D)/2) | Sum → product conversion |
| cos C − cos D | −2 sin((C+D)/2) sin((C−D)/2) | Difference → product (note minus sign) |

## 11. Heights and Distances

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Height from angle of elevation | h = d · tan θ | d = distance from foot of object |
| Distance from angle of elevation | d = h / tan θ = h · cot θ | When height is known |
| Two positions (same side) | h = d · tan α · tan β / (tan β − tan α) | Angles α < β from two points d apart |
| Two positions (opposite sides) | h = d · tan α · tan β / (tan α + tan β) | Object between the two points |

## 12. Radian–Degree Conversion

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Degree → Radian | θ° × (π/180) | Converting degrees to radians |
| Radian → Degree | θ × (180/π) | Converting radians to degrees |
| Key relation | π radians = 180° | Base conversion fact |

**Memory Tip:** For standard values of sin θ, write 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 → divide each by 4 → take square root: √(0/4), √(1/4), √(2/4), √(3/4), √(4/4) = 0, 1/2, 1/√2, √3/2, 1. Cosine is the same row read backwards!
