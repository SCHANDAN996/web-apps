126. A train 240 m long moving at 54 km/h crosses a platform in 30 seconds. What is the length of the platform?
(a) 210 m (b) 180 m (c) 240 m (d) 270 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is forgetting to add the train's own length and directly computing 15 × 30 = 450 m as the platform length. Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s, so total distance covered in 30 s = 450 m = train length + platform length. Platform = 450 − 240 = 210 m, so the answer is 210 m.

127. Walking at 5/6 of his usual speed, a man reaches his office 10 minutes late. What is his usual time to reach the office?
(a) 40 minutes (b) 66 minutes (c) 60 minutes (d) 50 minutes
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong step is taking 10 minutes as the usual time itself. Speed ratio is 5:6, so time ratio is 6:5; the extra 1 part equals 10 minutes, hence usual time = 5 parts = 50 minutes, so the answer is 50 minutes.

128. Two men start from the same point at the same time and walk in opposite directions at 6 km/h and 4 km/h. How far apart will they be after 3 hours?
(a) 24 km (b) 30 km (c) 28 km (d) 32 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is subtracting the speeds (2 km/h) instead of adding them. In opposite directions, relative speed = 6 + 4 = 10 km/h, so separation in 3 hours = 10 × 3 = 30 km, so the answer is 30 km.

(a) 52 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 48 km/h (d) 45 km/h
