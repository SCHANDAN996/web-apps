126. (Hard) A man walks up a moving escalator (going up) in 30 seconds taking 20 steps. The same man walking down the same escalator at the same stepping rate takes 90 seconds. How many steps would he take if the escalator were stationary?
(A) 30 steps  (B) 36 steps  (C) 25 steps  (D) 40 steps
Answer: (A) 30 steps
Solution: Man's stepping rate m = 20 / 30 = 2/3 steps/sec. Going up: N = 30(2/3 + e) = 20 + 30e. Going down: N = 90(2/3 - e) = 60 - 90e. 20 + 30e = 60 - 90e => 120e = 40 => e = 1/3 steps/sec. Stationary steps N = 20 + 30*(1/3) = 30 steps. The required value is 30 steps. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

(A) 120 seconds  (B) 80 seconds  (C) 100 seconds  (D) 150 seconds
Answer: (C) 100 seconds
Solution: Initial distance to cover = 200 meters. Relative speed in same direction = 5 - 3 = 2 m/s. Time taken to catch B = 200 / 2 = 100 seconds. The required value is 100 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (C).

129. (Hard) Two boys A and B start simultaneously from opposite ends of a 90-meter pool. A swims at 3 m/s and B swims at 2 m/s. They turn back immediately upon reaching the opposite ends. Where will they meet for the first time?
(A) 50 meters from A  (B) 60 meters from A  (C) 45 meters from A  (D) 54 meters from A
Answer: (D) 54 meters from A
Solution: Relative speed = 3 + 2 = 5 m/s. Time to first meeting = 90 / 5 = 18 seconds. Distance covered by A = 3 * 18 = 54 meters from A's starting end. The required value is 54 meters from A. Hence, the correct option is (D).

130. (Hard) Two boys A and B start simultaneously from opposite ends of a 90-meter pool at 3 m/s and 2 m/s respectively and swim back and forth. After how many seconds from the start will they meet for the second time?

Answer: (A) 54 seconds
Solution: For the second meeting in back-and-forth motion between two ends, total distance covered together = 3 * D = 3 * 90 = 270 meters. Combined speed = 3 + 2 = 5 m/s. Time = 270 / 5 = 54 seconds. The required value is 54 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (A).

133. (Hard) Two trains A and B start simultaneously from station P to station Q (distance 120 km). Speed of train A is 10 km/h faster than train B. Train A reaches Q, turns back immediately, and meets train B at a point 20 km from Q. Find the speed of train B.
(A) 20 km/h  (B) 30 km/h  (C) 35 km/h  (D) 25 km/h
Answer: (D) 25 km/h
Solution: Distance covered by A = 120 + 20 = 140 km. Distance covered by B = 120 - 20 = 100 km. Ratio of speeds = 140 : 100 = 7 : 5. Difference = 7x - 5x = 2x = 10 km/h => x = 5 km/h. Speed of train B = 5 * 5 = 25 km/h. The required value is 25 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

135. (Hard) A thief steals a car and drives away at 60 km/h. The police start chasing him 15 minutes later at 80 km/h. How many kilometers from the starting point will the police catch the thief?
(A) 55 km  (B) 60 km  (C) 70 km  (D) 65 km
Answer: (B) 60 km
Solution: In 15 minutes (0.25 h), thief covers 60 * 0.25 = 15 km. Relative speed = 80 - 60 = 20 km/h. Time to catch = 15 / 20 = 0.75 hours. Distance from start = 80 * 0.75 = 60 km. The required value is 60 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).

136. (Hard) A man walked at 5 km/h for 2.5 hours and then ran at 10 km/h for 1.5 hours. What was his average speed for the entire journey?
(A) 6.50 km/h  (B) 7.00 km/h  (C) 6.88 km/h  (D) 7.25 km/h
Answer: (C) 6.88 km/h
Solution: Total Distance = (5 * 2.5) + (10 * 1.5) = 12.5 + 15 = 27.5 km. Total Time = 2.5 + 1.5 = 4 hours. Average Speed = 27.5 / 4 = 6.88 km/h. The required value is 6.88 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

137. (Hard) Two cyclists start at the same time from two points 48 km apart and travel towards each other. If they meet after 2 hours, and one travels 4 km/h faster than the other, find the speed of the faster cyclist.
(A) 12 km/h  (B) 16 km/h  (C) 15 km/h  (D) 14 km/h
Answer: (D) 14 km/h
Solution: Relative speed = 48 / 2 = 24 km/h. S1 + S2 = 24 and S1 - S2 = 4. Adding gives 2 S1 = 28 => S1 = 14 km/h. The required value is 14 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).

141. (Hard) A motorboat whose speed is 15 km/h in still water goes 30 km downstream and comes back in a total of 4 hours 30 minutes. What is the speed of the stream in km/h?
(A) 4 km/h  (B) 6 km/h  (C) 3 km/h  (D) 5 km/h
Answer: (D) 5 km/h
Solution: Equation: 30/(15 + v) + 30/(15 - v) = 4.5 => 900 / (225 - v^2) = 4.5 => 225 - v^2 = 200 => v^2 = 25 => v = 5 km/h. The required value is 5 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

142. (Hard) A man can row 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream in 13 hours. Also, he can row 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours. Find the speed of the stream.
(A) 3 km/h  (B) 2 km/h  (C) 4 km/h  (D) 2.5 km/h
Answer: (A) 3 km/h
Solution: Solving 40/U + 55/D = 13 and 30/U + 44/D = 10 yields D = 11 km/h and U = 5 km/h. Speed of stream v = (D - U) / 2 = (11 - 5) / 2 = 3 km/h. The required value is 3 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

144. (Hard) Two trains 200 m and 150 m long are running on parallel tracks. If they run in the same direction, the faster train passes the slower one in 35 seconds. If they run in opposite directions, they pass each other in 5 seconds. What is the speed of the faster train in km/h?
(A) 135 km/h  (B) 150 km/h  (C) 144 km/h  (D) 120 km/h

Solution: Total distance = 200 + 150 = 350 m. Relative speeds: S1 - S2 = 350 / 35 = 10 m/s; S1 + S2 = 350 / 5 = 70 m/s. Adding gives 2 S1 = 80 => S1 = 40 m/s = 40 * (18/5) = 144 km/h. The required value is 144 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
