Questions 51–75: Medium

51. (Medium) In a 100 m race, A can beat B by 25 m and B can beat C by 4 m. In the same race, by how many meters can A beat C?
(A) 28 meters  (B) 29 meters  (C) 27 meters  (D) 30 meters
Answer: (A) 28 meters
Solution: When A runs 100 m, B runs 75 m. When B runs 100 m, C runs 96 m. Therefore, when B runs 75 m, C runs (96 / 100) * 75 = 72 m. Distance by which A beats C = 100 - 72 = 28 meters. The required value is 28 meters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. (Medium) In a 1000 m race, A beats B by 100 m, and in a 500 m race, B beats C by 50 m. By how many meters will A beat C in a 1000 m race?
(A) 180 meters  (B) 190 meters  (C) 200 meters  (D) 175 meters
Answer: (B) 190 meters
Solution: When A covers 1000 m, B covers 900 m. When B covers 500 m, C covers 450 m => C/B = 9/10. When B covers 900 m, C covers (9/10) * 900 = 810 m. Distance by which A beats C = 1000 - 810 = 190 meters. The required value is 190 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

53. (Medium) In a race of 200 meters, A can beat B by 31 meters and C by 18 meters. In a race of 350 meters, by how many meters will C beat B?
(A) 24 meters  (B) 26 meters  (C) 25 meters  (D) 28 meters
Answer: (C) 25 meters
Solution: When A runs 200 m, B runs 200 - 31 = 169 m and C runs 200 - 18 = 182 m. In a 350 m race, when C runs 350 m, B runs (169 / 182) * 350 = (13 / 14) * 350 = 325 m. C beats B by 350 - 325 = 25 meters. The required value is 25 meters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

54. (Medium) A and B take part in a 100 m race. A runs at 5 km/h. A gives B a start of 8 meters and still beats him by 8 seconds. Find the speed of B in km/h.
(A) 4.25 km/h  (B) 4.00 km/h  (C) 4.50 km/h  (D) 4.14 km/h
Answer: (D) 4.14 km/h
Solution: Speed of A = 5 * (5/18) = 25/18 m/s. Time taken by A = 100 / (25/18) = 72 seconds. B covers 100 - 8 = 92 m in 72 + 8 = 80 seconds. Speed of B = 92 / 80 m/s = (92 / 80) * (18/5) = 4.14 km/h. The required value is 4.14 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

55. (Medium) A train X leaves station A at 6 AM and reaches station B at 10 AM. Another train Y leaves station B at 8 AM and reaches station A at 11:30 AM. At what time do the two trains cross each other?
(A) 8:56 AM  (B) 8:48 AM  (C) 9:00 AM  (D) 9:12 AM
Answer: (A) 8:56 AM
Solution: Train X takes 4 hours, Train Y takes 3.5 hours (7/2 hours). Let distance AB = 28 km. Speed of X = 7 km/h, Speed of Y = 8 km/h. In 2 hours (6 to 8 AM), X covers 14 km. Distance remaining at 8 AM = 28 - 14 = 14 km. Relative speed = 7 + 8 = 15 km/h. Time to meet = 14 / 15 hours = 56 minutes. Meeting time = 8:00 AM + 56 min = 8:56 AM. The required value is 8:56 AM. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

56. (Medium) A train leaves station P at 7 AM and reaches station Q at 11 AM. Another train leaves station Q at 8 AM and reaches station P at 11:30 AM. At what time do they cross each other?
(A) 9:15 AM  (B) 9:24 AM  (C) 9:30 AM  (D) 9:36 AM
Answer: (B) 9:24 AM
Solution: Duration: Train 1 = 4 hours, Train 2 = 3.5 hours = 7/2 hours. Let distance = 28 km. Speed 1 = 7 km/h, Speed 2 = 8 km/h. In 1 hour (7 to 8 AM), Train 1 covers 7 km. Remaining distance = 28 - 7 = 21 km. Relative speed = 7 + 8 = 15 km/h. Time to meet = 21 / 15 = 7/5 hours = 1 hour 24 minutes. Meeting time = 8:00 AM + 1 hr 24 min = 9:24 AM. The required value is 9:24 AM. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

57. (Medium) Two trains, one from Howrah to Patna and the other from Patna to Howrah, start simultaneously. After they meet, the trains reach their destinations after 9 hours and 16 hours respectively. What is the ratio of their speeds?
(A) 3 : 4  (B) 16 : 9  (C) 4 : 3  (D) 9 : 16
Answer: (C) 4 : 3
Solution: Using after-meeting formula: S1 / S2 = sqrt(T2 / T1) = sqrt(16 / 9) = 4 / 3. The required value is 4 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

58. (Medium) Two trains A and B start from Delhi and Agra respectively and run towards each other. After passing each other, they take 4 hours 48 minutes and 3 hours 20 minutes to reach Agra and Delhi respectively. If train A is moving at 45 km/h, what is the speed of train B?
(A) 50 km/h  (B) 52 km/h  (C) 60 km/h  (D) 54 km/h
Answer: (D) 54 km/h
Solution: T1 = 24/5 hours, T2 = 10/3 hours. S_A / S_B = sqrt(T2 / T1) = sqrt((10/3) / (24/5)) = sqrt(25/36) = 5/6. Given S_A = 45 km/h => 45 / S_B = 5/6 => S_B = 45 * 6 / 5 = 54 km/h. The required value is 54 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

59. (Medium) A man covers a certain distance by car driving at 30 km/h and he is late by 10 minutes. However, with a speed of 40 km/h, he reaches his office 5 minutes earlier. Find the distance he covers.
(A) 30 km  (B) 25 km  (C) 35 km  (D) 40 km
Answer: (A) 30 km
Solution: Time difference ΔT = 10 min late + 5 min early = 15 minutes = 0.25 hours. Distance = (30 * 40 / 10) * 0.25 = 120 * 0.25 = 30 km. The required value is 30 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

60. (Medium) A man covers a distance by driving at 30 km/h and is late by 10 minutes. With a speed of 40 km/h, he reaches 5 minutes early. What is his exact scheduled time to reach the destination?
(A) 45 minutes  (B) 50 minutes  (C) 55 minutes  (D) 60 minutes
Answer: (B) 50 minutes
Solution: Distance = (30 * 40 / 10) * (15/60) = 30 km. At 30 km/h, time taken = 30 / 30 = 1 hour = 60 minutes. Since he was 10 minutes late, scheduled time = 60 - 10 = 50 minutes. The required value is 50 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

61. (Medium) A man covers a distance by driving at 30 km/h and is late by 10 minutes, but at 40 km/h he is 5 minutes early. What should be his speed to reach exactly on scheduled time?
(A) 35 km/h  (B) 38 km/h  (C) 36 km/h  (D) 40 km/h
Answer: (C) 36 km/h
Solution: Distance = 30 km. Scheduled time = 50 minutes = 5/6 hours. Required speed = 30 / (5/6) = (30 * 6) / 5 = 36 km/h. The required value is 36 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

62. (Medium) A train running at a speed of 60 km/h crosses a platform double its length in 32.4 seconds. What is the length of the platform?
(A) 320 meters  (B) 300 meters  (C) 400 meters  (D) 360 meters
Answer: (D) 360 meters
Solution: Speed = 60 * (5/18) = 50/3 m/s. Total distance in 32.4 seconds = (50/3) * 32.4 = 540 meters. Let train length = L, then platform = 2L. 3L = 540 => L = 180 meters. Length of platform = 2 * 180 = 360 meters. The required value is 360 meters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

63. (Medium) A boat covers 24 km upstream and 36 km downstream in 6 hours, while it covers 36 km upstream and 24 km downstream in 6.5 hours. Find the speed of the current.
(A) 2 km/h  (B) 1.5 km/h  (C) 2.5 km/h  (D) 3 km/h
Answer: (A) 2 km/h
Solution: Equations: 24/U + 36/D = 6 and 36/U + 24/D = 6.5. Solving gives U = 8 km/h and D = 12 km/h. Speed of current v = (D - U) / 2 = (12 - 8) / 2 = 2 km/h. The required value is 2 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

64. (Medium) A boat covers 24 km upstream and 36 km downstream in 6 hours, while it covers 36 km upstream and 24 km downstream in 6.5 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
(A) 9 km/h  (B) 10 km/h  (C) 11 km/h  (D) 12 km/h
Answer: (B) 10 km/h
Solution: Solving gives upstream speed U = 8 km/h and downstream speed D = 12 km/h. Speed of boat in still water u = (D + U) / 2 = (12 + 8) / 2 = 20 / 2 = 10 km/h. The required value is 10 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

65. (Medium) Two trains of lengths 100 meters and 120 meters are running on parallel tracks. When running in the same direction, the faster train passes the slower one in 22 seconds. When running in opposite directions, they pass each other in 5.5 seconds. What is the speed of the faster train in km/h?
(A) 85 km/h  (B) 80 km/h  (C) 90 km/h  (D) 95 km/h
Answer: (C) 90 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 100 + 120 = 220 m. Relative speed in same direction: S1 - S2 = 220 / 22 = 10 m/s. Relative speed in opposite direction: S1 + S2 = 220 / 5.5 = 40 m/s. Adding equations: 2 S1 = 50 => S1 = 25 m/s = 25 * (18/5) = 90 km/h. The required value is 90 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

(A) 50 km/h  (B) 60 km/h  (C) 48 km/h  (D) 54 km/h
Answer: (D) 54 km/h
Solution: Relative speed in same direction: S1 - S2 = 220 / 22 = 10 m/s. Opposite direction: S1 + S2 = 220 / 5.5 = 40 m/s. Solving yields S1 = 25 m/s and S2 = 15 m/s. In km/h: S2 = 15 * (18/5) = 54 km/h. The required value is 54 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).

71. (Medium) A man travels 35 km partly at 4 km/h and partly at 5 km/h. If he covers former distance at 5 km/h and later distance at 4 km/h, he could cover 2 km more in the same time. What is the total time taken to cover the journey?

72. (Medium) In a 500 m race, the ratio of speeds of two contestants A and B is 3 : 4. A has a start of 140 m. Then, A wins by:
(A) 15 meters  (B) 20 meters  (C) 25 meters  (D) 30 meters
Answer: (B) 20 meters
Solution: A needs to cover 500 - 140 = 360 m. Time taken by A = 360 / 3 = 120 units. In 120 units of time, B covers 4 * 120 = 480 m. Since total race is 500 m, A wins by 500 - 480 = 20 meters. The required value is 20 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

73. (Medium) Two trains start at the same time from Aligarh and Delhi and proceed towards each other at 14 km/h and 21 km/h respectively. When they meet, it is found that one train has traveled 70 km more than the other. What is the distance between two stations?
(A) 300 km  (B) 360 km  (C) 350 km  (D) 400 km
Answer: (C) 350 km
Solution: Distance ratio = Speed ratio = 14 : 21 = 2 : 3. Difference in distance = 3 - 2 = 1 unit = 70 km. Total distance = 2 + 3 = 5 units = 5 * 70 = 350 km. The required value is 350 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

74. (Medium) A carriage driving in a fog passed a man who was walking at the rate of 3 km/h in the same direction. He could see the carriage for 4 minutes and it was visible to him up to a distance of 100 meters. What was the speed of the carriage in km/h?
(A) 4.0 km/h  (B) 5.0 km/h  (C) 4.2 km/h  (D) 4.5 km/h
Answer: (D) 4.5 km/h
Solution: Relative speed = 100 m / (4 * 60 s) = 100 / 240 = 5/12 m/s = (5/12) * (18/5) = 1.5 km/h. Since moving in same direction: S_carriage - S_man = 1.5 => S_carriage = 3 + 1.5 = 4.5 km/h. The required value is 4.5 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

75. (Medium) A boat travels upstream from B to A and downstream from A to B in 3 hours. If the speed of the boat in still water is 9 km/h and the speed of the current is 3 km/h, the distance between A and B is:
(A) 10 km  (B) 12 km  (C) 15 km  (D) 14 km
Answer: (B) 12 km
Solution: Downstream speed = 9 + 3 = 12 km/h. Upstream speed = 9 - 3 = 6 km/h. Time equation: d/12 + d/6 = 3 => 3d/12 = 3 => d/4 = 3 => d = 12 km. The required value is 12 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
