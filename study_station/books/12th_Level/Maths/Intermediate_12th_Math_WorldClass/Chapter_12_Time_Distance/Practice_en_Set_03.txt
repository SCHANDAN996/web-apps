51. A train 240 m long crosses a pole in 12 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(a) 72 km/h (b) 80 km/h (c) 60 km/h (d) 90 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = distance/time = 240/12 = 20 m/s. Converting to km/h: 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h. The trap is forgetting the 18/5 conversion, so the answer is 72 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

52. A man covers a certain distance in 4 hours at 45 km/h. How long will he take to cover the same distance at 60 km/h?
(a) 3 hours (b) 2.5 hours (c) 2 hours (d) 3.5 hours
Answer: (a)
Solution: Distance = 45 × 4 = 180 km. Time at 60 km/h = 180/60 = 3 hours. The tempting wrong option 2.5 hours comes from mishandling the inverse speed-time ratio, so the answer is 3 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

53. Two trains of lengths 150 m and 250 m run towards each other on parallel tracks at speeds of 40 km/h and 50 km/h. In how much time will they cross each other?
(a) 14 seconds (b) 20 seconds (c) 18 seconds (d) 16 seconds
Answer: (d)
Solution: Relative speed = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h = 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s. Total length = 150 + 250 = 400 m, so time = 400/25 = 16 seconds. The trap is adding speeds in km/h without converting to m/s, so the answer is 16 seconds.
Source: PYQ-style

54. A car travels the first half of a journey at 30 km/h and the second half at 60 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 45 km/h (b) 42 km/h (c) 48 km/h (d) 40 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: For equal distances, average speed = 2xy/(x+y) = 2 × 30 × 60/90 = 40 km/h. The trap is the simple mean 45 km/h, which ignores that more time is spent at the lower speed, so the answer is 40 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Motion)

55. A boat goes 30 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 3 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
(a) 10 km/h (b) 12 km/h (c) 12.5 km/h (d) 15 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: Downstream speed = 30/2 = 15 km/h; upstream speed = 30/3 = 10 km/h. Boat speed in still water = (15 + 10)/2 = 12.5 km/h. The trap is taking 15 km/h directly, so the answer is 12.5 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

56. A man walks at 5 km/h and reaches his office 10 minutes late. If he walks at 6 km/h, he reaches 10 minutes early. What is the distance to his office?
(a) 8 km (b) 10 km (c) 12 km (d) 15 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let distance be d. Then d/5 − d/6 = 20/60 hours (the total gap between late and early). So d/30 = 1/3, giving d = 10 km. The trap is using only 10 minutes instead of the 20-minute gap, so the answer is 10 km.
Source: PYQ-style

57. A train 180 m long is running at 54 km/h. How long will it take to cross a platform 120 m long?
(a) 15 seconds (b) 18 seconds (c) 20 seconds (d) 24 seconds
Answer: (c)
Solution: Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s. Total distance = 180 + 120 = 300 m, so time = 300/15 = 20 seconds. The trap is using only the train's length and forgetting the platform, so the answer is 20 seconds.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

58. A and B start from the same point and walk in opposite directions at 4 km/h and 5 km/h. After how much time will they be 27 km apart?
(a) 2 hours (b) 2.5 hours (c) 3 hours (d) 3.5 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: Moving in opposite directions, relative speed = 4 + 5 = 9 km/h. Time = 27/9 = 3 hours. The trap is subtracting the speeds, which applies only to the same direction, so the answer is 3 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

59. A cyclist covers a distance of 750 m in 2 minutes 30 seconds. What is his speed in km/h?
(a) 18 km/h (b) 20 km/h (c) 15 km/h (d) 24 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Time = 150 seconds. Speed = 750/150 = 5 m/s = 5 × 18/5 = 18 km/h. The trap is misreading 2 minutes 30 seconds as 230 seconds, so the answer is 18 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Motion)

60. A train passes a man standing on a platform in 8 seconds and passes the 264 m long platform completely in 20 seconds. What is the length of the train?
(a) 160 m (b) 176 m (c) 180 m (d) 200 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let train length be L. Speed = L/8, and crossing the platform gives (L + 264)/20 = L/8. So 8L + 2112 = 20L, giving L = 176 m. The trap is assuming the 20 seconds covers only the platform, so the answer is 176 m.
Source: PYQ-style

61. A man travels 160 km at 64 km/h and the next 160 km at 80 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 70 km/h (b) 71.1 km/h (c) 72 km/h (d) 74 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Time for first part = 160/64 = 2.5 h; second part = 160/80 = 2 h. Average speed = 320/4.5 ≈ 71.1 km/h. The trap is the simple mean 72 km/h, which ignores unequal times, so the answer is 71.1 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Motion)

62. The speed of a boat in still water is 11 km/h and the speed of the stream is 3 km/h. How much time will the boat take to go 56 km upstream?
(a) 7 hours (b) 5 hours (c) 6 hours (d) 8 hours
Answer: (a)
Solution: Upstream speed = 11 − 3 = 8 km/h. Time = 56/8 = 7 hours. The trap is adding the stream speed, which applies only downstream, so the answer is 7 hours.
Source: PYQ-style

63. A bus covers a certain distance in 6 hours at 50 km/h. By how much should its speed increase to cover the same distance in 5 hours?
(a) 8 km/h (b) 15 km/h (c) 10 km/h (d) 12 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: Distance = 50 × 6 = 300 km. Required speed = 300/5 = 60 km/h, so the increase = 60 − 50 = 10 km/h. The trap is answering 60 km/h, the new speed, instead of the increase asked, so the answer is 10 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

64. Two trains start at the same time from stations A and B, 300 km apart, and travel towards each other at 55 km/h and 65 km/h. After how long will they meet?
(a) 2 hours (b) 3.5 hours (c) 3 hours (d) 2.5 hours
Answer: (d)
Solution: Relative speed = 55 + 65 = 120 km/h. Time = 300/120 = 2.5 hours. The trap is using the difference of speeds, which suits same-direction pursuit, so the answer is 2.5 hours.
Source: PYQ-style

65. A man can row 9 km/h in still water. He takes twice as long to row upstream as to row downstream the same distance. What is the speed of the stream?
(a) 3.5 km/h (b) 2 km/h (c) 3 km/h (d) 4 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: Double the time upstream means downstream speed is twice the upstream speed. So 9 + s = 2(9 − s), giving 3s = 9 and s = 3 km/h. The trap is reversing the ratio and taking upstream speed as double, so the answer is 3 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

66. A train 400 m long running at 90 km/h crosses a bridge in 36 seconds. What is the length of the bridge?
(a) 400 m (b) 550 m (c) 450 m (d) 500 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: Speed = 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s. Distance covered in 36 s = 25 × 36 = 900 m = train length + bridge length. Bridge = 900 − 400 = 500 m. The trap is reporting 900 m as the bridge length and forgetting to subtract the train, so the answer is 500 m.
Source: PYQ-style

67. A person travels equal distances at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. What is his average speed?
(a) 48 km/h (b) 52 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 45 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Average speed for equal distances = 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h. The trap is the arithmetic mean 50 km/h, valid only for equal times, so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Motion)

68. A 300 m long train crosses a man walking at 6 km/h in the same direction in 30 seconds. What is the speed of the train?
(a) 36 km/h (b) 42 km/h (c) 45 km/h (d) 40 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Relative speed = 300/30 = 10 m/s = 36 km/h. Since they move in the same direction, train speed = 36 + 6 = 42 km/h. The trap is ignoring the man's motion and answering 36 km/h, so the answer is 42 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

69. A man covers 2/5 of his 100 km journey by bus at 40 km/h, 2/5 by train at 60 km/h, and the rest by cycle at 10 km/h. What is the total time taken?
(a) 3 hours (b) 3 hours 20 minutes (c) 4 hours (d) 3 hours 40 minutes
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bus: 40 km at 40 km/h = 60 min. Train: 40 km at 60 km/h = 40 min. Cycle: remaining 20 km at 10 km/h = 120 min. Total = 220 min = 3 hours 40 minutes. The trap is forgetting the remaining 1/5 of the journey and stopping at 100 minutes, so the answer is 3 hours 40 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

70. A thief runs at 8 km/h and a policeman chases him at 10 km/h. If the thief is initially 400 m ahead, how long will the policeman take to catch him?
(a) 12 minutes (b) 10 minutes (c) 15 minutes (d) 18 minutes
Answer: (a)
Solution: Relative speed = 10 − 8 = 2 km/h = 2000 m per 60 min. Time to cover 400 m = 400/2000 × 60 = 12 minutes. The trap is adding the speeds, which applies to opposite directions, so the answer is 12 minutes.

72. A boat takes 4 hours to go 24 km downstream and 6 hours to return the same distance upstream. What is the speed of the stream?
(a) 2.5 km/h (b) 1 km/h (c) 2 km/h (d) 1.5 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Downstream speed = 24/4 = 6 km/h; upstream speed = 24/6 = 4 km/h. Stream speed = (6 − 4)/2 = 1 km/h. The trap is taking the full difference 2 km/h instead of halving it, so the answer is 1 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

73. A man reaches his destination 20 minutes late when he walks at 3 km/h and 10 minutes early when he walks at 4 km/h. What is the distance to his destination?
(a) 6 km (b) 5 km (c) 4 km (d) 8 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let distance be d. Then d/3 − d/4 = 30/60 = 1/2 hour. So d/12 = 1/2, giving d = 6 km. The trap is using 20 − 10 = 10 minutes instead of the total 30-minute gap, so the answer is 6 km.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Two trains of equal length cross a pole in 10 seconds and 15 seconds respectively. In what time will they cross each other when moving in opposite directions?
(a) 10 seconds (b) 15 seconds (c) 14 seconds (d) 12 seconds
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let each train's length be L. Speeds are L/10 and L/15, so relative speed = L/10 + L/15 = L/6. Total distance = 2L, giving time = 2L/(L/6) = 12 seconds. The trap is averaging 10 and 15 to get 12.5 seconds, so the answer is 12 seconds.
Source: PYQ-style

75. A man travels from A to B at 20 km/h and returns at 30 km/h. If the total journey takes 5 hours, what is the distance between A and B?
(a) 50 km (b) 60 km (c) 70 km (d) 80 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let one-way distance be d. Then d/20 + d/30 = 5, so 5d/60 = 5, giving d = 60 km. The trap is using the average speed 25 km/h × 5 h = 125 km as the one-way distance, so the answer is 60 km.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
