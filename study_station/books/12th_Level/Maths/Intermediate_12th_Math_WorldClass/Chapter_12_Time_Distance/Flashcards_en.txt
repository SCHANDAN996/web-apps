Q: How do you convert speed from km/h to m/s?
A: Multiply the speed in km/h by 5/18.

Q: How do you convert speed from m/s to km/h?
A: Multiply the speed in m/s by 18/5.

Q: What is the formula for average speed when covering two equal distances at speeds x and y?
A: Average Speed = (2xy) / (x + y).

Q: If distance is constant, what is the mathematical relationship between speed and time?
A: Speed is inversely proportional to time: S1 / S2 = T2 / T1.

Q: What is the relative speed of two objects moving in the same direction at speeds S1 and S2?
A: Relative Speed = |S1 - S2|.

Q: What is the relative speed of two objects moving in opposite directions at speeds S1 and S2?
A: Relative Speed = S1 + S2.

Q: When a train of length L passes a platform of length P, what is the total distance covered?
A: Total Distance = L + P.

Q: When a train crosses a vertical pole or stationary man, what is the distance covered?
A: Total Distance = Length of the train itself (L).

Q: What is the speed of a boat in still water (u) in terms of downstream speed (D) and upstream speed (U)?
A: u = (D + U) / 2.

Q: What is the speed of the stream or current (v) in terms of downstream speed (D) and upstream speed (U)?
A: v = (D - U) / 2.

Q: What is the formula for distance when traveling at speeds S1 and S2 results in a time difference of ΔT?
A: Distance = (S1 × S2 / |S1 - S2|) × ΔT.

Q: What is the formula for stoppage time per hour for a bus?
A: Stoppage Time per hour = [(Speed without stoppage - Speed with stoppage) / Speed without stoppage] × 60 minutes.
