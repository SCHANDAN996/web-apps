# Previous Year Questions: Time, Speed and Distance

1. A car travels a distance of 240 km at a speed of 60 km/h and returns at a speed of 40 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey?
(A) 48 km/h  (B) 50 km/h  (C) 45 km/h  (D) 52 km/h
Answer: (A) 48 km/h
Solution: Average Speed for two equal distances = 2xy / (x + y) = (2 * 60 * 40) / (60 + 40) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h. The required value is 48 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. A train 150 meters long passes a telegraph pole in 12 seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
(A) 45 km/h  (B) 50 km/h  (C) 40 km/h  (D) 54 km/h
Answer: (A) 45 km/h
Solution: Speed = Distance / Time = 150 m / 12 s = 12.5 m/s. In km/h = 12.5 * (18/5) = 2.5 * 18 = 45 km/h. The required value is 45 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

3. Walking at 3/4 of his usual speed, a man reaches his office 20 minutes later than usual. What is his usual time to reach the office?
(A) 60 minutes  (B) 50 minutes  (C) 75 minutes  (D) 45 minutes
Answer: (A) 60 minutes
Solution: Speed ratio = 3 : 4 => Time ratio = 4 : 3. Difference in time = 4 - 3 = 1 unit = 20 minutes. Usual time = 3 units = 3 * 20 = 60 minutes. The required value is 60 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

4. A train running at 72 km/h crosses a 250 m long platform in 20 seconds. What is the length of the train?
(A) 150 m  (B) 120 m  (C) 180 m  (D) 200 m
Answer: (A) 150 m
Solution: Speed of train = 72 * (5/18) = 20 m/s. Total distance in 20 seconds = 20 * 20 = 400 m. Length of train = 400 - 250 = 150 m. The required value is 150 m. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

5. A man can row upstream at 8 km/h and downstream at 14 km/h. What is the speed of the stream?
(A) 3 km/h  (B) 2 km/h  (C) 4 km/h  (D) 2.5 km/h
Answer: (A) 3 km/h
Solution: Speed of stream = (Downstream - Upstream) / 2 = (14 - 8) / 2 = 6 / 2 = 3 km/h. The required value is 3 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
