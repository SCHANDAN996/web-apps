1. A car covers 240 km in 4 hours. What is its speed?
(a) 60 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 70 km/h (d) 80 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 240 ÷ 4. Many students divide 4 by 240 by mistake and get a fraction, but speed is always distance over time, so the answer is 60 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

2. A train runs at 72 km/h. What is its speed in m/s?
(a) 20 m/s (b) 18 m/s (c) 15 m/s (d) 25 m/s
Answer: (a)
Solution: To convert km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18. The tempting wrong value 18 m/s comes from reversing the fraction (multiplying by 18/5 gives 20, but dividing wrongly gives 14.4). Here 72 × 5/18 = 20, so the answer is 20 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

3. A man walks at 5 m/s. What is his speed in km/h?
(a) 18 km/h (b) 15 km/h (c) 20 km/h (d) 25 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: To convert m/s to km/h, multiply by 18/5. The value 15 km/h is a trap from multiplying by 3 instead of 18/5. Here 5 × 18/5 = 18, so the answer is 18 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

4. A cyclist covers 45 km at a speed of 15 km/h. How much time does he take?
(a) 2 hours (b) 3 hours (c) 2.5 hours (d) 4 hours
Answer: (b)
Solution: Time = Distance ÷ Speed = 45 ÷ 15. The trap is multiplying 45 × 15, which gives a huge number; time is found by division, so the answer is 3 hours.

5. A bus travels at 40 km/h for 2.5 hours. What distance does it cover?
(a) 80 km (b) 90 km (c) 100 km (d) 120 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: Distance = Speed × Time = 40 × 2.5. The wrong value 80 km comes from taking the time as 2 hours only and ignoring the half hour. So the answer is 100 km.

6. A train 120 m long crosses a pole in 12 seconds. What is its speed?
(a) 15 m/s (b) 12 m/s (c) 10 m/s (d) 20 m/s
Answer: (c)
Solution: To cross a pole, a train covers a distance equal to its own length. Speed = 120 ÷ 12. The trap 12 m/s comes from confusing time with speed. So the answer is 10 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

7. A train 150 m long crosses a platform 250 m long in 20 seconds. Find its speed.
(a) 15 m/s (b) 30 m/s (c) 25 m/s (d) 20 m/s
Answer: (d)
Solution: To cross a platform, the train covers its own length plus the platform length = 150 + 250 = 400 m. The trap 15 m/s comes from using only 250 m or only 150 m. Speed = 400 ÷ 20, so the answer is 20 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

8. A man covers 60 km in 1 hour 30 minutes. What is his speed?
(a) 30 km/h (b) 40 km/h (c) 45 km/h (d) 50 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 hours. Speed = 60 ÷ 1.5. The trap 30 km/h comes from treating 1 hour 30 minutes as 2 hours. So the answer is 40 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

9. Two trains are moving in the same direction at 60 km/h and 40 km/h. What is their relative speed?
(a) 100 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 20 km/h (d) 2400 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: In the same direction, relative speed is the difference of speeds = 60 − 40. The trap 100 km/h is the sum, which applies only to opposite directions. So the answer is 20 km/h.

10. Two cars approach each other at 50 km/h and 70 km/h. What is their relative speed?
(a) 120 km/h (b) 60 km/h (c) 20 km/h (d) 3500 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: In opposite directions, relative speed is the sum of speeds = 50 + 70. The trap 20 km/h is the difference, which applies to the same direction. So the answer is 120 km/h.

11. A boat's speed in still water is 10 km/h and the stream flows at 2 km/h. What is the downstream speed?
(a) 8 km/h (b) 12 km/h (c) 10 km/h (d) 20 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Downstream speed = boat speed + stream speed = 10 + 2. The trap 8 km/h is the upstream speed (subtraction). So the answer is 12 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

12. A boat's speed in still water is 15 km/h and the stream speed is 3 km/h. What is the upstream speed?
(a) 12 km/h (b) 15 km/h (c) 18 km/h (d) 45 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Upstream speed = boat speed − stream speed = 15 − 3. The trap 18 km/h is the downstream speed (addition). So the answer is 12 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

13. A man walks 3 km in 30 minutes. What is his speed in km/h?
(a) 3 km/h (b) 9 km/h (c) 6 km/h (d) 12 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: 30 minutes = 0.5 hour. Speed = 3 ÷ 0.5. The trap 3 km/h comes from treating 30 minutes as 1 hour. So the answer is 6 km/h.

14. A train running at 54 km/h crosses a pole in 10 seconds. What is the length of the train?
(a) 120 m (b) 540 m (c) 180 m (d) 150 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: Convert speed: 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s. Length = speed × time = 15 × 10. The trap 540 m comes from forgetting to convert km/h into m/s. So the answer is 150 m.

15. A person covers a certain distance in 5 hours at 12 km/h. What is the distance?

Answer: (a)
Solution: Distance = Speed × Time = 12 × 5. The trap 50 km comes from multiplying 12 by 4 instead of 5. So the answer is 60 km.

16. A car travels 180 km at 60 km/h. How long does the journey take?
(a) 2 hours (b) 3 hours (c) 2.5 hours (d) 3.5 hours
Answer: (b)
Solution: Time = Distance ÷ Speed = 180 ÷ 60. The trap 2 hours comes from dividing 180 by 90 by mistake. So the answer is 3 hours.

17. A runner covers 400 m in 50 seconds. What is his speed in m/s?
(a) 8 m/s (b) 7 m/s (c) 6 m/s (d) 9 m/s
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = 400 ÷ 50. The trap 7 m/s comes from a division slip (400 ÷ 50 is not 7). So the answer is 8 m/s.

18. A train 200 m long is running at 36 km/h. How long will it take to cross a pole?
(a) 10 seconds (b) 20 seconds (c) 15 seconds (d) 25 seconds
Answer: (b)
Solution: Speed = 36 × 5/18 = 10 m/s. Time = 200 ÷ 10. The trap 10 seconds comes from using 100 m instead of the full 200 m length. So the answer is 20 seconds.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

19. A man goes to office at 30 km/h and returns at 20 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 50 km/h (b) 25 km/h (c) 26 km/h (d) 24 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: For equal distances, average speed = 2xy ÷ (x + y) = 2 × 30 × 20 ÷ 50 = 1200 ÷ 50. The trap 25 km/h is the simple arithmetic mean, which is wrong when speeds differ over equal distances. So the answer is 24 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

20. A bus covers 90 km in 2 hours and then 60 km in 1 hour. What is its average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 45 km/h (b) 55 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 60 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: Average speed = total distance ÷ total time = (90 + 60) ÷ (2 + 1) = 150 ÷ 3. The trap 55 km/h comes from averaging the two speeds 45 and 60 directly. So the answer is 50 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

21. A train 180 m long crosses a platform 120 m long at a speed of 54 km/h. How much time does it take?
(a) 15 seconds (b) 30 seconds (c) 25 seconds (d) 20 seconds
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total distance = 180 + 120 = 300 m. Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s. Time = 300 ÷ 15. The trap 15 seconds comes from using only the platform length or only the train length instead of their sum. So the answer is 20 seconds.
Source: SSC CGL pattern (PYQ-style)

22. Two trains 120 m and 180 m long run towards each other at 40 km/h and 50 km/h. In how much time will they cross each other?
(a) 10 seconds (b) 18 seconds (c) 15 seconds (d) 12 seconds
Answer: (d)
Solution: Relative speed = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h = 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s. Total distance = 120 + 180 = 300 m. Time = 300 ÷ 25. The trap 10 seconds comes from using only one train's length. So the answer is 12 seconds.
Source: RRB NTPC pattern (PYQ-style)

23. A man covers a certain distance at 40 km/h and returns the same distance at 60 km/h. If the total journey takes 5 hours, find the one-way distance.
(a) 100 km (b) 150 km (c) 140 km (d) 120 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let distance be d. Then d/40 + d/60 = 5, so 3d + 2d = 600, giving 5d = 600. The trap 100 km comes from taking average speed 50 km/h and computing 50 × 5 = 250, then halving wrongly. So the answer is 120 km.
Source: SSC CHSL pattern (PYQ-style)

24. A boat goes 24 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 4 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
(a) 6 km/h (b) 9 km/h (c) 8 km/h (d) 12 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Downstream speed = 24 ÷ 2 = 12 km/h; upstream speed = 24 ÷ 4 = 6 km/h. Boat speed = (12 + 6) ÷ 2. The trap 6 km/h is the stream speed or the upstream speed, not the boat's still-water speed. So the answer is 9 km/h.
Source: IBPS Clerk pattern (PYQ-style)

25. A and B start walking towards each other from two places 100 km apart at 6 km/h and 4 km/h respectively. After how many hours will they meet?
(a) 8 hours (b) 12 hours (c) 10 hours (d) 20 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: Since they move towards each other, relative speed = 6 + 4 = 10 km/h. Time = 100 ÷ 10. The trap 20 hours comes from using the difference of speeds (6 − 4 = 2) instead of the sum. So the answer is 10 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
