Questions 1–25: Easy

1. (Easy) Convert a speed of 54 km/h into meters per second (m/s).
(A) 15 m/s  (B) 12 m/s  (C) 18 m/s  (D) 20 m/s
Answer: (A) 15 m/s
Solution: To convert km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18. Speed = 54 * (5/18) = 3 * 5 = 15 m/s. The required value is 15 m/s. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. (Easy) Convert a speed of 25 m/s into kilometers per hour (km/h).
(A) 80 km/h  (B) 90 km/h  (C) 85 km/h  (D) 100 km/h
Answer: (B) 90 km/h
Solution: To convert m/s to km/h, multiply by 18/5. Speed = 25 * (18/5) = 5 * 18 = 90 km/h. The required value is 90 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

3. (Easy) A car covers a distance of 360 km in 6 hours. What is its speed in km/h?
(A) 55 km/h  (B) 65 km/h  (C) 60 km/h  (D) 70 km/h
Answer: (C) 60 km/h
Solution: Speed = Distance / Time = 360 / 6 = 60 km/h. The required value is 60 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

4. (Easy) A train travels at a speed of 72 km/h. How much distance will it cover in 15 minutes?
(A) 16 km  (B) 20 km  (C) 15 km  (D) 18 km
Answer: (D) 18 km
Solution: Time = 15 minutes = 15/60 = 0.25 hours. Distance = Speed * Time = 72 * 0.25 = 18 km. The required value is 18 km. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

5. (Easy) How many seconds will a cyclist take to cover a distance of 500 meters at a speed of 18 km/h?
(A) 100 seconds  (B) 90 seconds  (C) 120 seconds  (D) 80 seconds
Answer: (A) 100 seconds
Solution: Speed in m/s = 18 * (5/18) = 5 m/s. Time = Distance / Speed = 500 / 5 = 100 seconds. The required value is 100 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

6. (Easy) A bus covers 150 km in 3 hours and the next 150 km in 2 hours. What is the average speed of the bus for the entire journey?
(A) 58 km/h  (B) 60 km/h  (C) 62 km/h  (D) 65 km/h
Answer: (B) 60 km/h
Solution: Total Distance = 150 + 150 = 300 km. Total Time = 3 + 2 = 5 hours. Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time = 300 / 5 = 60 km/h. The required value is 60 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

7. (Easy) A person travels from A to B at 20 km/h and returns from B to A at 30 km/h. Find the average speed of the whole journey.
(A) 25 km/h  (B) 22 km/h  (C) 24 km/h  (D) 26 km/h
Answer: (C) 24 km/h
Solution: For two equal distances, Average Speed = (2xy) / (x + y) = (2 * 20 * 30) / (20 + 30) = 1200 / 50 = 24 km/h. The required value is 24 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

8. (Easy) A train 200 meters long is running at a speed of 36 km/h. In how much time will it cross a telegraph post?
(A) 18 seconds  (B) 22 seconds  (C) 25 seconds  (D) 20 seconds
Answer: (D) 20 seconds
Solution: Speed in m/s = 36 * (5/18) = 10 m/s. Distance = Length of train = 200 m. Time = 200 / 10 = 20 seconds. The required value is 20 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

9. (Easy) A train 300 meters long passes a standing man in 15 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 72 km/h  (B) 60 km/h  (C) 64 km/h  (D) 80 km/h
Answer: (A) 72 km/h
Solution: Speed = Distance / Time = 300 / 15 = 20 m/s. In km/h = 20 * (18/5) = 4 * 18 = 72 km/h. The required value is 72 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

10. (Easy) A train 120 meters long crosses a platform 180 meters long in 15 seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
(A) 68 km/h  (B) 72 km/h  (C) 75 km/h  (D) 80 km/h
Answer: (B) 72 km/h
Solution: Total distance = Length of train + Length of platform = 120 + 180 = 300 m. Speed in m/s = 300 / 15 = 20 m/s. In km/h = 20 * (18/5) = 72 km/h. The required value is 72 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

11. (Easy) Two cars start from the same place in opposite directions at speeds of 40 km/h and 50 km/h. What will be the distance between them after 3 hours?
(A) 250 km  (B) 280 km  (C) 270 km  (D) 300 km
Answer: (C) 270 km
Solution: Relative speed in opposite directions = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h. Distance after 3 hours = 90 * 3 = 270 km. The required value is 270 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

12. (Easy) Two boys start running from the same point in the same direction at speeds of 12 km/h and 9 km/h. What will be the distance between them after 2 hours?
(A) 5 km  (B) 8 km  (C) 4 km  (D) 6 km
Answer: (D) 6 km
Solution: Relative speed in the same direction = 12 - 9 = 3 km/h. Distance after 2 hours = 3 * 2 = 6 km. The required value is 6 km. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

13. (Easy) A boat moves downstream at the rate of 12 km/h and upstream at the rate of 8 km/h. Find the speed of the boat in still water.
(A) 10 km/h  (B) 9 km/h  (C) 11 km/h  (D) 12 km/h
Answer: (A) 10 km/h
Solution: Speed of boat in still water u = (Downstream + Upstream) / 2 = (12 + 8) / 2 = 20 / 2 = 10 km/h. The required value is 10 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

14. (Easy) A boat travels downstream at 16 km/h and upstream at 10 km/h. What is the speed of the river current?
(A) 2 km/h  (B) 3 km/h  (C) 4 km/h  (D) 5 km/h
Answer: (B) 3 km/h
Solution: Speed of current v = (Downstream - Upstream) / 2 = (16 - 10) / 2 = 6 / 2 = 3 km/h. The required value is 3 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

15. (Easy) If a man runs at 10 km/h, how much time will he take to cover 750 meters?
(A) 4.0 minutes  (B) 5.0 minutes  (C) 4.5 minutes  (D) 5.5 minutes
Answer: (C) 4.5 minutes
Solution: Speed in m/s = 10 * (5/18) = 25/9 m/s. Time in seconds = 750 / (25/9) = (750 * 9) / 25 = 30 * 9 = 270 seconds. Time in minutes = 270 / 60 = 4.5 minutes. The required value is 4.5 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

16. (Easy) A car travels 80 km at 40 km/h and another 120 km at 60 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire 200 km?
(A) 48 km/h  (B) 52 km/h  (C) 55 km/h  (D) 50 km/h
Answer: (D) 50 km/h
Solution: Time for first part = 80 / 40 = 2 hours. Time for second part = 120 / 60 = 2 hours. Total Distance = 200 km, Total Time = 2 + 2 = 4 hours. Average Speed = 200 / 4 = 50 km/h. The required value is 50 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

17. (Easy) An athlete runs a 200-meter race in 24 seconds. His speed in km/h is:
(A) 30 km/h  (B) 28 km/h  (C) 32 km/h  (D) 25 km/h
Answer: (A) 30 km/h
Solution: Speed = 200 / 24 = 25/3 m/s. In km/h = (25/3) * (18/5) = 5 * 6 = 30 km/h. The required value is 30 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

18. (Easy) If the ratio of speeds of two cars is 4 : 5, find the ratio of time taken by them to cover the same distance.
(A) 4 : 5  (B) 5 : 4  (C) 16 : 25  (D) 25 : 16
Answer: (B) 5 : 4
Solution: When distance is constant, Speed and Time are inversely proportional: T1 / T2 = S2 / S1 = 5 / 4. Ratio of time = 5 : 4. The required value is 5 : 4. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

22. (Easy) Two persons start walking towards each other from two points 30 km apart at speeds of 4 km/h and 6 km/h. In how many hours will they meet?
(A) 2.5 hours  (B) 3 hours  (C) 3.5 hours  (D) 4 hours
