Questions 101–125: Hard

101. (Hard) A train passes two persons walking in the same direction at 2 km/h and 4 km/h in 9 seconds and 10 seconds respectively. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
(A) 22 km/h  (B) 20 km/h  (C) 24 km/h  (D) 25 km/h
Answer: (A) 22 km/h
Solution: Let speed of train be S km/h. Equating train length: (S - 2) * (5/18) * 9 = (S - 4) * (5/18) * 10 => 9(S - 2) = 10(S - 4) => 9S - 18 = 10S - 40 => S = 22 km/h. The required value is 22 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

102. (Hard) A train passes two persons walking in the same direction at 2 km/h and 4 km/h in 9 seconds and 10 seconds respectively. What is the length of the train in meters?
(A) 45 meters  (B) 50 meters  (C) 55 meters  (D) 60 meters
Answer: (B) 50 meters
Solution: From previous relation, train speed S = 22 km/h. Relative speed with first person = 22 - 2 = 20 km/h = 20 * (5/18) = 50/9 m/s. Length of train = (50/9) * 9 = 50 meters. The required value is 50 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

103. (Hard) A train passes two persons walking in the opposite directions at 5 km/h and 10 km/h in 6 seconds and 5 seconds respectively. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 24 km/h  (B) 18 km/h  (C) 20 km/h  (D) 22 km/h
Answer: (C) 20 km/h
Solution: Equating length: (S + 5) * (5/18) * 6 = (S + 10) * (5/18) * 5 => 6(S + 5) = 5(S + 10) => 6S + 30 = 5S + 50 => S = 20 km/h. The required value is 20 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

104. (Hard) A train passes two persons walking in opposite directions at 5 km/h and 10 km/h in 6 seconds and 5 seconds respectively. What is the length of the train in meters?
(A) 40.00 meters  (B) 45.00 meters  (C) 42.50 meters  (D) 41.67 meters
Answer: (D) 41.67 meters
Solution: Train speed = 20 km/h. Relative speed with first person = 20 + 5 = 25 km/h = 25 * (5/18) m/s. Length = 25 * (5/18) * 6 = 125 / 3 = 41.67 meters. The required value is 41.67 meters. Hence, the correct option is (D).

105. (Hard) A train meets with an accident 60 km after starting. It then proceeds at 4/5 of its former speed and arrives 45 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 60 km further, it would have arrived only 33 minutes late. What was the original speed of the train?
(A) 75 km/h  (B) 70 km/h  (C) 80 km/h  (D) 90 km/h
Answer: (A) 75 km/h
Solution: Time saved over 60 km = 45 - 33 = 12 minutes = 1/5 hour. Time equation: 60 / (4/5 S) - 60 / S = 1/5 => 75 / S - 60 / S = 1/5 => 15 / S = 1/5 => S = 75 km/h. The required value is 75 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

106. (Hard) A train meets with an accident 60 km after starting and proceeds at 4/5 of its former speed, arriving 45 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 60 km further, it would have arrived only 33 minutes late. What is the total distance of the journey?
(A) 275 km  (B) 285 km  (C) 300 km  (D) 260 km
Answer: (B) 285 km
Solution: Original speed S = 75 km/h. Reduced speed = 60 km/h. Let distance after accident be D. D/60 - D/75 = 45/60 => D/300 = 3/4 => D = 225 km. Total distance = 60 + 225 = 285 km. The required value is 285 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

107. (Hard) Three runners A, B, and C run along a circular track of length 12 km in the same direction at speeds of 3 km/h, 7 km/h, and 13 km/h respectively. After how many hours will they all meet together at the starting point for the first time?
(A) 10 hours  (B) 14 hours  (C) 12 hours  (D) 8 hours
Answer: (C) 12 hours
Solution: Time taken for one round: A = 12/3 = 4 hours, B = 12/7 hours, C = 12/13 hours. Meeting time at starting point = LCM(4, 12/7, 12/13) = LCM(4, 12, 12) / HCF(1, 7, 13) = 12 / 1 = 12 hours. The required value is 12 hours. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

108. (Hard) Two cyclists A and B start simultaneously from the same point on a 1200 m circular track in the same direction at speeds of 15 m/s and 10 m/s. How many distinct points will they meet each other on the track?

110. (Hard) A boat takes 90 minutes less to travel 36 miles downstream than to travel the same distance upstream. If the boat's speed in still water is 10 mph, find the speed of the stream.
(A) 3 mph  (B) 2 mph  (C) 1.5 mph  (D) 2.5 mph
Answer: (B) 2 mph
Solution: Equation: 36/(10 - v) - 36/(10 + v) = 1.5 => 36 * 2v / (100 - v^2) = 1.5 => 48v = 100 - v^2 => v^2 + 48v - 100 = 0 => (v + 50)(v - 2) = 0 => v = 2 mph. The required value is 2 mph. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

111. (Hard) A man can row 7.5 km/h in still water. If in a river which is flowing at 1.5 km/h, it takes him 50 minutes to row to a place and back, how far off is the place?
(A) 2.5 km  (B) 3.5 km  (C) 3 km  (D) 4 km
Answer: (C) 3 km
Solution: Downstream speed = 7.5 + 1.5 = 9 km/h. Upstream speed = 7.5 - 1.5 = 6 km/h. Time equation: d/9 + d/6 = 50/60 = 5/6 => 5d/18 = 5/6 => d = 3 km. The required value is 3 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

112. (Hard) A car traveled 75% of the distance from town A to town B at its usual speed and the rest at 50% of its usual speed. If the total journey took 4 hours 30 minutes and the distance between town A and B is 180 km, what was the usual speed of the car?
(A) 45 km/h  (B) 55 km/h  (C) 60 km/h  (D) 50 km/h
Answer: (D) 50 km/h
Solution: 75% of 180 = 135 km at speed S. 25% of 180 = 45 km at speed 0.5S. Equation: 135/S + 45/(0.5S) = 4.5 => 135/S + 90/S = 4.5 => 225/S = 4.5 => S = 50 km/h. The required value is 50 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

113. (Hard) A man covered a distance of 61 km in 9 hours partly on foot at 4 km/h and partly on bicycle at 9 km/h. What is the distance covered on foot?
(A) 16 km  (B) 15 km  (C) 18 km  (D) 20 km
Answer: (A) 16 km
Solution: Let time on foot = t hours. Then time on bicycle = 9 - t. Distance: 4t + 9(9 - t) = 61 => 81 - 5t = 61 => 5t = 20 => t = 4 hours. Distance on foot = 4 * 4 = 16 km. The required value is 16 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).

115. (Hard) A train traveling at 72 km/h crosses a 200 m long platform in 22 seconds. How much time will it take to cross a person who is riding a bicycle at 18 km/h in the opposite direction?
(A) 9.0 seconds  (B) 10.0 seconds  (C) 9.6 seconds  (D) 10.5 seconds
Answer: (C) 9.6 seconds
Solution: Speed = 72 * (5/18) = 20 m/s. Train length = 20 * 22 - 200 = 440 - 200 = 240 m. Relative speed in opposite direction = 72 + 18 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Time to cross cyclist = 240 / 25 = 9.6 seconds. The required value is 9.6 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

116. (Hard) Two stations A and B are 110 km apart on a straight line. One train starts from A at 7 AM and travels towards B at 20 km/h. Another train starts from B at 8 AM and travels towards A at 25 km/h. At what time will they meet?
(A) 9:30 AM  (B) 10:30 AM  (C) 11:00 AM  (D) 10:00 AM
Answer: (D) 10:00 AM
Solution: In 1 hour (7 to 8 AM), train from A covers 20 km. Remaining distance at 8 AM = 110 - 20 = 90 km. Relative speed = 20 + 25 = 45 km/h. Time taken to meet = 90 / 45 = 2 hours. Meeting time = 8:00 AM + 2 hours = 10:00 AM. The required value is 10:00 AM. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

117. (Hard) Two points P and Q are 400 km apart. A train starts from P at 6:00 AM at 40 km/h towards Q. Another train starts from Q at 7:00 AM at 50 km/h towards P. At what distance from P will they meet?
(A) 200 km  (B) 180 km  (C) 220 km  (D) 240 km
Answer: (A) 200 km
Solution: In 1 hour, train from P covers 40 km. Remaining distance at 7 AM = 400 - 40 = 360 km. Relative speed = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h. Time to meet after 7 AM = 360 / 90 = 4 hours. Total travel time of first train = 1 + 4 = 5 hours. Distance from P = 40 * 5 = 200 km. The required value is 200 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

118. (Hard) A thief running at 8 km/h is chased by a policeman running at 10 km/h. If the thief is 100 m ahead of the policeman, what is the distance covered by the thief before he is caught?
(A) 350 meters  (B) 400 meters  (C) 450 meters  (D) 500 meters
Answer: (B) 400 meters
Solution: Relative speed = 10 - 8 = 2 km/h = 2 * (5/18) = 5/9 m/s. Time to catch = 100 / (5/9) = 180 seconds = 3 minutes = 3/60 hours. Distance covered by thief = 8 * (3/60) = 0.4 km = 400 meters. The required value is 400 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).

123. (Hard) A boat travels 24 km downstream and returns to the starting point in 5 hours. If the speed of the stream is 2 km/h, find the speed of the boat in still water.
(A) 9 km/h  (B) 11 km/h  (C) 10 km/h  (D) 12 km/h
Answer: (C) 10 km/h
Solution: Equation: 24/(u + 2) + 24/(u - 2) = 5. Testing u = 10: 24/12 + 24/8 = 2 + 3 = 5 hours. Thus, speed of boat in still water = 10 km/h. The required value is 10 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

124. (Hard) A passenger train takes 2 hours less for a journey of 300 km if its speed is increased by 5 km/h from its usual speed. What is its usual speed?
(A) 20 km/h  (B) 30 km/h  (C) 35 km/h  (D) 25 km/h
Answer: (D) 25 km/h
Solution: Equation: 300/S - 300/(S + 5) = 2 => 1500 / [S(S + 5)] = 2 => S(S + 5) = 750. Testing S = 25: 25 * 30 = 750. Thus, usual speed = 25 km/h. The required value is 25 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

125. (Hard) An express train traveled at an average speed of 100 km/h, stopping for 3 minutes after every 75 km. How much time did it take to reach its destination 600 km away?
(A) 6 hours 24 minutes  (B) 6 hours 21 minutes  (C) 6 hours 18 minutes  (D) 6 hours 15 minutes
Answer: (B) 6 hours 21 minutes
Solution: Running time = 600 / 100 = 6 hours. Number of stops before destination = 600 / 75 - 1 = 8 - 1 = 7 stops. Total stoppage time = 7 * 3 = 21 minutes. Total journey time = 6 hours 21 minutes. The required value is 6 hours 21 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
