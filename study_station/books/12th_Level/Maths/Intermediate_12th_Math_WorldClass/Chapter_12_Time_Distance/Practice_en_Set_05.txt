101. A train 240 m long crosses a pole in 12 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(a) 72 km/h (b) 60 km/h (c) 80 km/h (d) 90 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap 60 km/h comes from forgetting the 18/5 conversion factor. Speed = 240/12 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h, so the answer is 72 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept), PYQ-style

102. A man covers a certain distance in 4 hours at 45 km/h. How long will he take to cover the same distance at 60 km/h?
(a) 3.5 hours (b) 2.5 hours (c) 3 hours (d) 4 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap 2.5 hours comes from mishandling the inverse speed-time ratio. Distance = 45 × 4 = 180 km, so time at 60 km/h = 180/60 = 3 hours, so the answer is 3 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept), PYQ-style

103. Two trains of lengths 150 m and 250 m run towards each other on parallel lines at speeds 40 km/h and 50 km/h. In what time will they cross each other?
(a) 18 seconds (b) 14 seconds (c) 12 seconds (d) 16 seconds
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap 12 seconds comes from using only one train's length. Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s and total length = 400 m, so time = 400/25 = 16 seconds, so the answer is 16 seconds.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

104. A car travels the first half of a journey at 30 km/h and the second half at 60 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 50 km/h (b) 40 km/h (c) 48 km/h (d) 45 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting 45 km/h is the simple arithmetic mean, which is wrong when distances (not times) are equal. Average speed = 2xy/(x+y) = 2×30×60/90 = 40 km/h, so the answer is 40 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Motion), PYQ-style

105. A boat goes 24 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 3 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
(a) 10 km/h (b) 9 km/h (c) 11 km/h (d) 12 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap 12 km/h is just the downstream speed. Downstream = 12 km/h and upstream = 8 km/h, so boat speed = (12+8)/2 = 10 km/h, so the answer is 10 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC/Bank pattern)

106. A man walking at 5 km/h reaches his office 10 minutes late. Walking at 6 km/h, he reaches 5 minutes early. What is the distance to his office?
(a) 6 km (b) 10 km (c) 9 km (d) 7.5 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap 6 km comes from taking the time gap as 10 minutes only instead of 10 + 5 = 15 minutes. With distance d, d/5 − d/6 = 1/4 hour, so d/30 = 1/4 and d = 7.5 km, so the answer is 7.5 km.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

107. A train 180 m long is running at 54 km/h. How long will it take to cross a platform 120 m long?
(a) 18 seconds (b) 20 seconds (c) 15 seconds (d) 24 seconds
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap 15 seconds comes from using the wrong total distance. Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s and total distance = 180 + 120 = 300 m, so time = 300/15 = 20 seconds, so the answer is 20 seconds.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept), PYQ-style

108. The ratio of speeds of A and B is 3 : 4. A takes 30 minutes more than B to cover a certain distance. How much time does A take?
(a) 1 hour (b) 1.5 hours (c) 2 hours (d) 2.5 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap 1.5 hours is B's time, not A's. Time ratio is inverse of speed ratio, so times are 4 : 3; the difference of 1 part equals 30 minutes, so A's time = 4 parts = 120 minutes = 2 hours, so the answer is 2 hours.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

109. A cyclist covers 750 m in 2 minutes 30 seconds. What is his speed in km/h?
(a) 15 km/h (b) 20 km/h (c) 24 km/h (d) 18 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap 15 km/h comes from a faulty minute-to-second conversion. Time = 150 seconds, so speed = 750/150 = 5 m/s = 5 × 18/5 = 18 km/h, so the answer is 18 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Motion), PYQ-style

110. A boat goes 36 km downstream in 3 hours and 24 km upstream in 4 hours. What is the speed of the current?
(a) 2 km/h (b) 2.5 km/h (c) 3 km/h (d) 4 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap 2 km/h comes from dividing the difference of speeds by the wrong factor. Downstream speed = 36/3 = 12 km/h and upstream speed = 24/4 = 6 km/h, so current = (12 − 6)/2 = 3 km/h, so the answer is 3 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style (Bank exam pattern)

111. A train passes a man standing on a platform in 8 seconds and crosses the platform 264 m long in 20 seconds. What is the length of the train?
(a) 180 m (b) 176 m (c) 192 m (d) 200 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap 192 m comes from wrongly setting up the proportion. With train length L, (L + 264)/20 = L/8, so 8L + 2112 = 20L and L = 176 m, so the answer is 176 m.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

112. A and B start at the same time from points P and Q towards each other. After meeting, A takes 9 hours and B takes 4 hours to reach the opposite ends. If A's speed is 12 km/h, what is B's speed?
(a) 18 km/h (b) 16 km/h (c) 15 km/h (d) 20 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap 16 km/h comes from using the ratio 4 : 9 directly instead of its square root. Speed ratio A : B = √4 : √9 = 2 : 3, so B's speed = 12 × 3/2 = 18 km/h, so the answer is 18 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

113. A man travels 600 km partly by train at 80 km/h and partly by car at 50 km/h. The total time is 9 hours. What distance did he travel by train?
(a) 300 km (b) 400 km (c) 360 km (d) 450 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap 360 km comes from an arithmetic slip while clearing fractions. With train distance x, x/80 + (600 − x)/50 = 9; multiplying by 400 gives 5x + 4800 − 8x = 3600, so x = 400 km, so the answer is 400 km.
Source: PYQ-style (Bank exam pattern)

114. A goods train leaves a station at 60 km/h. Two hours later, an express train leaves the same station at 90 km/h in the same direction. How far from the station will the express train overtake the goods train?
(a) 300 km (b) 360 km (c) 320 km (d) 400 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap 300 km comes from using the goods train's total time wrongly. The lead is 120 km and relative speed is 30 km/h, so catching time = 4 hours and distance = 90 × 4 = 360 km, so the answer is 360 km.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

115. A man can row 8 km/h in still water. If the river flows at 2 km/h, how long will he take to row 12 km upstream and back?
(a) 3.5 hours (b) 3 hours (c) 3.2 hours (d) 4 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap 3 hours comes from wrongly assuming the current cancels out over the round trip. Upstream speed = 6 km/h and downstream speed = 10 km/h, so time = 12/6 + 12/10 = 2 + 1.2 = 3.2 hours, so the answer is 3.2 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept), PYQ-style

116. Two cars start from the same point in opposite directions at 40 km/h and 55 km/h. After how long will they be 285 km apart?
(a) 2.5 hours (b) 3 hours (c) 3.5 hours (d) 4 hours
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap 3.5 hours comes from subtracting the speeds instead of adding them. Relative speed = 40 + 55 = 95 km/h, so time = 285/95 = 3 hours, so the answer is 3 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept), PYQ-style

117. A train running at 72 km/h crosses a bridge in 30 seconds. If the train is 200 m long, what is the length of the bridge?
(a) 350 m (b) 500 m (c) 400 m (d) 450 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap 500 m comes from adding instead of subtracting the train's length. Speed = 20 m/s, total distance in 30 s = 600 m, so bridge length = 600 − 200 = 400 m, so the answer is 400 m.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC/RRB pattern)

118. A man covers a certain distance. If he walks 3 km/h faster, he takes 40 minutes less; if he walks 2 km/h slower, he takes 40 minutes more. Find the distance.
(a) 40 km (b) 30 km (c) 36 km (d) 24 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap 24 km comes from solving only one of the two conditions. With usual speed s and distance d, d/s − d/(s+3) = 2/3 and d/(s−2) − d/s = 2/3; dividing these gives 3/2 = (s+3)/(s−2), so s = 12 km/h, and then 3d = (2/3)(12)(15) = 120, giving d = 40 km, so the answer is 40 km.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

119. A bus covers a distance of 480 km in 8 hours with two stoppages of 15 minutes each. What is the average running speed of the bus excluding stoppages?
(a) 60 km/h (b) 62 km/h (c) 64 km/h (d) 66 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap 60 km/h is the overall average including stoppages. Running time = 8 − 0.5 = 7.5 hours, so running speed = 480/7.5 = 64 km/h, so the answer is 64 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style (RRB NTPC pattern)

120. A thief steals a car at 2:30 pm and drives at 60 km/h. The owner starts chasing at 3:00 pm at 80 km/h. At what time will the owner catch the thief?
(a) 4:00 pm (b) 5:30 pm (c) 5:00 pm (d) 4:30 pm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap 5:00 pm comes from taking the lead as 40 km instead of 30 km. The thief's lead is 30 km and relative speed is 20 km/h, so catching time = 1.5 hours after 3:00 pm, so the answer is 4:30 pm.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

121. A train overtakes two persons walking in the same direction at 2 km/h and 4 km/h and passes them completely in 9 and 10 seconds respectively. What is the length of the train?
(a) 50 m (b) 54 m (c) 45 m (d) 60 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap 45 m comes from using only the first person's data with a wrong relative speed. With train speed v m/s and length L, L = 9(v − 5/9) and L = 10(v − 10/9); solving gives v = 55/9 m/s and L = 9 × 50/9 = 50 m, so the answer is 50 m.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

122. Two trains start simultaneously from stations A and B, 400 km apart, towards each other. They meet at a point 240 km from A. What is the ratio of their speeds?
(a) 2 : 3 (b) 5 : 3 (c) 3 : 5 (d) 3 : 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap 2 : 3 reverses the ratio by matching the wrong train to the wrong distance. In the same time the trains cover 240 km and 160 km, so the speed ratio = 240 : 160 = 3 : 2, so the answer is 3 : 2.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC/Bank pattern)

123. A man rows to a place 48 km away and back in 14 hours. He finds that he can row 4 km with the stream in the same time as 3 km against the stream. What is the speed of the stream?
(a) 1 km/h (b) 1.5 km/h (c) 2 km/h (d) 2.5 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap 2 km/h is the value of the proportionality factor k, not the stream speed. Speeds are in ratio 4 : 3, so 48/4k + 48/3k = 14 gives k = 2, making speeds 8 and 6 km/h; stream speed = (8 − 6)/2 = 1 km/h, so the answer is 1 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style (Bank PO pattern)

124. A, B and C run around a circular track of 1200 m at speeds of 4 m/s, 6 m/s and 8 m/s respectively, starting together from the same point in the same direction. After how much time will all three meet at the starting point for the first time?
(a) 600 seconds (b) 400 seconds (c) 1200 seconds (d) 300 seconds
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap 300 seconds is only A's lap time, not the common meeting time. Lap times are 300 s, 200 s and 150 s, and their LCM is 600 seconds, so the answer is 600 seconds.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

125. A car travels from P to Q at 40 km/h and returns along the same route at 60 km/h. If the total time for the round trip is 5 hours, what is the distance from P to Q?
(a) 100 km (b) 110 km (c) 150 km (d) 120 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap 100 km comes from wrongly averaging the speeds as 50 km/h and halving 250 km. With distance d, d/40 + d/60 = 5, so 5d/120 = 5 and d = 120 km, so the answer is 120 km.
Source: PYQ-style (Bank PO pattern)
