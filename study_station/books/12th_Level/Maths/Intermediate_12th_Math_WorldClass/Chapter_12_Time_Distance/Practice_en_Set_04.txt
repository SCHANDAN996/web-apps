Questions 76–100: Medium

76. (Medium) Two guns were fired from the same place at an interval of 13 minutes, but a person in a train approaching the place hears the second firing 12 minutes 30 seconds after the first. Find the speed of the train, supposing that sound travels at 330 m/s.
(A) 47.52 km/h  (B) 45.00 km/h  (C) 48.25 km/h  (D) 50.00 km/h
Answer: (A) 47.52 km/h
Solution: Time difference = 13 min - 12 min 30 sec = 30 seconds. Distance covered by sound in 30 seconds = Distance covered by train in 12 min 30 sec (750 seconds). S_train * 750 = 330 * 30 => S_train = 9900 / 750 = 13.2 m/s = 13.2 * (18/5) = 47.52 km/h. The required value is 47.52 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

77. (Medium) Two guns were fired from the same place at an interval of 28 minutes, but a man sitting in a train approaching the place hears the second shot 26 minutes after the first. If the speed of sound is 325 m/s, find the speed of the train in km/h.
(A) 85 km/h  (B) 90 km/h  (C) 80 km/h  (D) 95 km/h
Answer: (B) 90 km/h
Solution: Time difference for sound = 28 - 26 = 2 minutes = 120 seconds. Train travels for 26 minutes = 1560 seconds. Distance of sound in 120 s = 325 * 120 = 39000 m. Speed of train = 39000 / 1560 = 25 m/s = 25 * (18/5) = 90 km/h. The required value is 90 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

78. (Medium) A train leaves a station at 6:00 AM at 40 km/h. Another train leaves the same station at 7:30 AM at 50 km/h in the same direction. At what distance from the station will the second train overtake the first?
(A) 280 km  (B) 320 km  (C) 300 km  (D) 350 km
Answer: (C) 300 km
Solution: In 1.5 hours (6:00 to 7:30 AM), first train covers 40 * 1.5 = 60 km. Relative speed = 50 - 40 = 10 km/h. Time taken to overtake = 60 / 10 = 6 hours. Distance from station = 50 * 6 = 300 km. The required value is 300 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

79. (Medium) A train starts from A at 8:00 AM and reaches B at 12:00 noon. Another train starts from B at 8:30 AM and reaches A at 12:30 PM. At what time do they cross each other?
(A) 10:30 AM  (B) 10:00 AM  (C) 10:20 AM  (D) 10:15 AM
Answer: (D) 10:15 AM
Solution: Both trains take 4 hours. Let distance AB = 40 km. Speed of each train = 10 km/h. In 30 minutes (8:00 to 8:30 AM), Train A covers 5 km. Remaining distance = 40 - 5 = 35 km. Relative speed = 10 + 10 = 20 km/h. Time to meet = 35 / 20 = 1.75 hours = 1 hr 45 min. Meeting time = 8:30 AM + 1 hr 45 min = 10:15 AM. The required value is 10:15 AM. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

80. (Medium) A man takes 6 hours 30 minutes in walking to a certain place and riding back. He would have gained 2 hours 10 minutes by riding both ways. How long would he take to walk both ways?
(A) 8 hours 40 minutes  (B) 8 hours 20 minutes  (C) 9 hours 00 minutes  (D) 8 hours 50 minutes
Answer: (A) 8 hours 40 minutes
Solution: Walk + Ride = 6 hr 30 min = 390 min. Riding both ways saves 2 hr 10 min (130 min), so walking both ways will take 130 min extra. Total time to walk both ways = 390 + 130 = 520 min = 8 hours 40 minutes. The required value is 8 hours 40 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

81. (Medium) A man takes 5 hours 45 minutes in walking to a certain place and riding back. If he walks both ways, he takes 2 hours more. How long would it take him to ride both ways?
(A) 3 hours 30 minutes  (B) 3 hours 45 minutes  (C) 4 hours 00 minutes  (D) 3 hours 15 minutes
Answer: (B) 3 hours 45 minutes
Solution: Walk + Ride = 5 hr 45 min. Walk + Walk = 5 hr 45 min + 2 hr = 7 hr 45 min. Therefore, 1 Walk takes half of 7 hr 45 min = 3 hr 52.5 min. 1 Ride = (5 hr 45 min) - (3 hr 52.5 min) = 1 hr 52.5 min. Ride + Ride = 2 * (1 hr 52.5 min) = 3 hours 45 minutes. The required value is 3 hours 45 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

82. (Medium) A train traveling at 48 km/h completely crosses another train half its length traveling in opposite direction at 42 km/h in 12 seconds. It also passes a railway platform in 45 seconds. Find the length of the railway platform.
(A) 380 meters  (B) 420 meters  (C) 400 meters  (D) 350 meters
Answer: (C) 400 meters
Solution: Relative speed in opposite direction = 48 + 42 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Total length = L + 0.5L = 1.5L = 25 * 12 = 300 m => L = 200 m. Speed of first train = 48 * (5/18) = 40/3 m/s. Platform crossing: (40/3) * 45 = 600 m = L + Platform => Platform = 600 - 200 = 400 meters. The required value is 400 meters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

83. (Medium) A train 108 m long moving at a speed of 50 km/h crosses another train 112 m long coming from opposite direction in 6 seconds. What is the speed of the second train in km/h?
(A) 78 km/h  (B) 85 km/h  (C) 80 km/h  (D) 82 km/h
Answer: (D) 82 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 108 + 112 = 220 m. Relative speed = 220 / 6 m/s = (220 / 6) * (18/5) = 132 km/h. S1 + S2 = 132 => S2 = 132 - 50 = 82 km/h. The required value is 82 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

84. (Medium) A train running at 25 km/h leaves Delhi at 9:00 AM and another train running at 35 km/h leaves Delhi at 2:00 PM in the same direction. How many km from Delhi will they be together?

Answer: (A) 437.5 km
Solution: In 5 hours (9 AM to 2 PM), first train covers 25 * 5 = 125 km. Relative speed = 35 - 25 = 10 km/h. Time taken to overtake = 125 / 10 = 12.5 hours. Distance from Delhi = 35 * 12.5 = 437.5 km. The required value is 437.5 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

85. (Medium) A train passes a man running at 9 km/h in the same direction in 10 seconds. If the length of the train is 125 meters, what is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 50 km/h  (B) 54 km/h  (C) 48 km/h  (D) 60 km/h
Answer: (B) 54 km/h
Solution: Relative speed = 125 / 10 = 12.5 m/s = 12.5 * (18/5) = 45 km/h. S_train - S_man = 45 => S_train - 9 = 45 => S_train = 54 km/h. The required value is 54 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

86. (Medium) A man rows to a place 48 km distant and back in 14 hours. He finds that he can row 4 km with the stream in the same time as 3 km against the stream. Find the rate of the stream.
(A) 1.5 km/h  (B) 2 km/h  (C) 1 km/h  (D) 0.8 km/h
Answer: (C) 1 km/h
Solution: Ratio D / U = 4 / 3 => Let D = 4x, U = 3x. Time equation: 48/(4x) + 48/(3x) = 14 => 12/x + 16/x = 14 => 28/x = 14 => x = 2. D = 8 km/h, U = 6 km/h. Rate of stream v = (8 - 6) / 2 = 1 km/h. The required value is 1 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

87. (Medium) A man rows to a place 48 km distant and back in 14 hours. He finds that he can row 4 km with the stream in the same time as 3 km against the stream. What is the speed of the man in still water?
(A) 6.5 km/h  (B) 7.5 km/h  (C) 8 km/h  (D) 7 km/h
Answer: (D) 7 km/h
Solution: From the equations, downstream speed D = 8 km/h and upstream speed U = 6 km/h. Speed in still water u = (D + U) / 2 = (8 + 6) / 2 = 7 km/h. The required value is 7 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

88. (Medium) A car covers four successive 3 km stretches at speeds of 10 km/h, 20 km/h, 30 km/h, and 60 km/h respectively. Find its average speed for this total distance of 12 km.
(A) 20 km/h  (B) 22 km/h  (C) 18 km/h  (D) 24 km/h
Answer: (A) 20 km/h
Solution: Total Distance = 12 km. Total Time = 3/10 + 3/20 + 3/30 + 3/60 = (18 + 9 + 6 + 3)/60 = 36/60 = 0.6 hours. Average Speed = 12 / 0.6 = 20 km/h. The required value is 20 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

89. (Medium) A motor car starts with the speed of 70 km/h with its speed increasing every two hours by 10 km/h. In how many hours will it cover 345 km?
(A) 4.0 hours  (B) 4.5 hours  (C) 5.0 hours  (D) 4.25 hours
Answer: (B) 4.5 hours
Solution: First 2 hours at 70 km/h = 140 km. Next 2 hours at 80 km/h = 160 km. Distance covered in 4 hours = 140 + 160 = 300 km. Remaining distance = 345 - 300 = 45 km. Speed in 5th hour = 90 km/h. Time for 45 km = 45 / 90 = 0.5 hours. Total time = 4 + 0.5 = 4.5 hours. The required value is 4.5 hours. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

90. (Medium) A plane left 30 minutes later than the scheduled time and in order to reach its destination 1500 km away in time, it had to increase its speed by 250 km/h from its usual speed. Find its usual speed.
(A) 720 km/h  (B) 800 km/h  (C) 750 km/h  (D) 700 km/h
Answer: (C) 750 km/h
Solution: Equation: 1500/S - 1500/(S + 250) = 0.5 => 1500 * 250 / [S(S + 250)] = 0.5 => S(S + 250) = 750000. Testing S = 750: 750 * 1000 = 750000. Thus, usual speed = 750 km/h. The required value is 750 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

91. (Medium) Two men start together to walk a certain distance, one at 4 km/h and the other at 3 km/h. The former arrives half an hour before the latter. Find the distance.
(A) 5 km  (B) 7 km  (C) 8 km  (D) 6 km
Answer: (D) 6 km
Solution: Distance = (S1 * S2 / |S1 - S2|) * ΔT = (4 * 3 / 1) * 0.5 = 12 * 0.5 = 6 km. The required value is 6 km. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (B) 70 meters
Solution: Relative speed with man in opposite direction = 36 + 6 = 42 km/h = 42 * (5/18) = 35/3 m/s. Length of train = Relative speed * Time = (35/3) * 6 = 70 meters. The required value is 70 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).

94. (Medium) A train running at 36 km/h takes 12 seconds to pass a platform and 6 seconds to pass a man walking at 6 km/h in opposite direction. What is the length of the platform?

Solution: Train speed = 36 * (5/18) = 10 m/s. Length of train = (42 * 5/18) * 6 = 70 m. Distance in 12 seconds to pass platform = 10 * 12 = 120 m. Length of platform = 120 - 70 = 50 meters. The required value is 50 meters. Hence, the correct option is (C).

95. (Medium) In a race of 1000 m, A can beat B by 100 m. In a race of 400 m, B beats C by 40 m. In a race of 500 m, by how many meters will A beat C?
(A) 90 meters  (B) 100 meters  (C) 85 meters  (D) 95 meters
Answer: (D) 95 meters
Solution: B/A = 900/1000 = 9/10. C/B = 360/400 = 9/10. In a 500 m race, when A runs 500 m: B runs (9/10) * 500 = 450 m, and C runs (9/10) * 450 = 405 m. Distance by which A beats C = 500 - 405 = 95 meters. The required value is 95 meters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

96. (Medium) A river 3 m deep and 40 m wide is flowing at the rate of 2 km/h. How much water (in cubic meters) will fall into the sea in a minute?
(A) 4000 m^3  (B) 3600 m^3  (C) 4500 m^3  (D) 4200 m^3
Answer: (A) 4000 m^3
Solution: Flow rate = 2 km/h = 2000 m / 60 min = 100/3 m/min. Cross-sectional area = 3 * 40 = 120 m^2. Volume per minute = 120 * (100/3) = 4000 m^3. The required value is 4000 m^3. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

97. (Medium) A man covered a certain distance at some speed. Had he moved 3 km/h faster, he would have taken 40 minutes less. If he had moved 2 km/h slower, he would have taken 40 minutes more. What is the distance in km?
(A) 36 km  (B) 40 km  (C) 42 km  (D) 45 km
Answer: (B) 40 km
Solution: Formula: D = [S(S + 3)/3] * (40/60) = [S(S - 2)/2] * (40/60) => (S + 3)/3 = (S - 2)/2 => 2S + 6 = 3S - 6 => S = 12 km/h. Distance = [12 * 15 / 3] * (2/3) = 60 * (2/3) = 40 km. The required value is 40 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

98. (Medium) A person travels 285 km in 6 hours in two stages. In the first part of the journey, he travels by bus at 40 km/h and in the second part by train at 55 km/h. How much distance did he travel by train?
(A) 150 km  (B) 160 km  (C) 165 km  (D) 175 km
Answer: (C) 165 km
Solution: Let time on train be t hours. Then time on bus = 6 - t hours. 55t + 40(6 - t) = 285 => 15t = 45 => t = 3 hours. Distance traveled by train = 55 * 3 = 165 km. The required value is 165 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

99. (Medium) A boy walking at 10 km/h covers a certain distance in 2 hours 30 minutes. To cover the same distance in 1 hour 40 minutes, what should be his speed in km/h?
(A) 14 km/h  (B) 16 km/h  (C) 18 km/h  (D) 15 km/h
Answer: (D) 15 km/h
Solution: Distance = 10 * 2.5 = 25 km. Target time = 1 hour 40 minutes = 5/3 hours. Required speed = 25 / (5/3) = (25 * 3) / 5 = 15 km/h. The required value is 15 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

100. (Medium) A train takes 18 seconds to pass completely through a station 162 m long and 15 seconds through another station 120 m long. The length of the train is:
(A) 85 meters  (B) 90 meters  (C) 95 meters  (D) 100 meters
Answer: (B) 90 meters
Solution: Speed of train = (162 - 120) / (18 - 15) = 42 / 3 = 14 m/s. Length of train = Speed * 15 - 120 = 14 * 15 - 120 = 210 - 120 = 90 meters. The required value is 90 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
