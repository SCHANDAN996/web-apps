Questions 26–50: Medium

26. (Medium) Walking at 3/4 of his normal speed, a man is 16 minutes late to reach his office. What is his usual time to reach the office?
(A) 48 minutes  (B) 45 minutes  (C) 50 minutes  (D) 54 minutes
Answer: (A) 48 minutes
Solution: Speed ratio = 3 : 4 => Time ratio = 4 : 3. Difference in time = 4 - 3 = 1 unit = 16 minutes. Usual time = 3 units = 3 * 16 = 48 minutes. The required value is 48 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. (Medium) If a student walks from his house to school at 5 km/h, he is late by 10 minutes. If he walks at 6 km/h, he reaches 5 minutes early. Find the distance between his house and school.
(A) 7.0 km  (B) 7.5 km  (C) 8.0 km  (D) 8.5 km
Answer: (B) 7.5 km
Solution: Using formula: Distance = (S1 * S2 / |S1 - S2|) * ΔT. Time difference ΔT = 10 min late + 5 min early = 15 minutes = 15/60 = 0.25 hours. Distance = (5 * 6 / 1) * 0.25 = 30 * 0.25 = 7.5 km. The required value is 7.5 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

28. (Medium) Excluding stoppages, the speed of a bus is 54 km/h and including stoppages, it is 45 km/h. For how many minutes does the bus stop per hour?
(A) 9 minutes  (B) 12 minutes  (C) 10 minutes  (D) 8 minutes
Answer: (C) 10 minutes
Solution: Stoppage time per hour = [(Speed without stoppage - Speed with stoppage) / Speed without stoppage] * 60 = [(54 - 45) / 54] * 60 = (9 / 54) * 60 = 10 minutes. The required value is 10 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (D) 12 minutes
Solution: Stoppage time per hour = [(60 - 48) / 60] * 60 = (12 / 60) * 60 = 12 minutes. The required value is 12 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (D).

30. (Medium) A train 150 meters long moving at a speed of 50 km/h crosses another train 100 meters long coming from the opposite direction at 40 km/h. How long will they take to pass each other completely?
(A) 10 seconds  (B) 12 seconds  (C) 8 seconds  (D) 15 seconds
Answer: (A) 10 seconds
Solution: Total distance = 150 + 100 = 250 meters. Relative speed in opposite direction = 50 + 40 = 90 km/h = 90 * (5/18) = 25 m/s. Time taken = 250 / 25 = 10 seconds. The required value is 10 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

31. (Medium) Two trains 140 m and 160 m long run at speeds of 60 km/h and 40 km/h respectively in opposite directions on parallel tracks. What is the time taken to cross each other?
(A) 10.5 seconds  (B) 10.8 seconds  (C) 11.2 seconds  (D) 12.0 seconds
Answer: (B) 10.8 seconds
Solution: Total distance = 140 + 160 = 300 meters. Relative speed = 60 + 40 = 100 km/h = 100 * (5/18) = 250/9 m/s. Time taken = 300 / (250/9) = (300 * 9) / 250 = 54 / 5 = 10.8 seconds. The required value is 10.8 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

32. (Medium) Two trains 130 meters and 110 meters long are going in the same direction. The faster train travels at 72 km/h and the slower train at 36 km/h. How much time will the faster train take to completely pass the slower train?
(A) 20 seconds  (B) 25 seconds  (C) 24 seconds  (D) 30 seconds
Answer: (C) 24 seconds
Solution: Total distance = 130 + 110 = 240 meters. Relative speed in same direction = 72 - 36 = 36 km/h = 36 * (5/18) = 10 m/s. Time taken = 240 / 10 = 24 seconds. The required value is 24 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (C).

33. (Medium) A train 125 meters long passes a man, running at 5 km/h in the same direction in which the train is going, in 10 seconds. What is the speed of the train?
(A) 45 km/h  (B) 55 km/h  (C) 48 km/h  (D) 50 km/h
Answer: (D) 50 km/h
Solution: Relative speed = Distance / Time = 125 / 10 = 12.5 m/s = 12.5 * (18/5) = 45 km/h. In the same direction, Relative Speed = S_train - S_man => 45 = S_train - 5 => S_train = 50 km/h. The required value is 50 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

34. (Medium) A train 110 meters long passes a man walking at 6 km/h in the direction opposite to that of the train in 6 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 60 km/h  (B) 54 km/h  (C) 66 km/h  (D) 58 km/h
Answer: (A) 60 km/h
Solution: Relative speed in opposite direction = 110 / 6 m/s = (110 / 6) * (18/5) = 66 km/h. S_train + S_man = 66 => S_train = 66 - 6 = 60 km/h. The required value is 60 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

35. (Medium) A motorboat can travel at 10 km/h in still water. It traveled 91 km downstream and then returned to the same spot, taking 20 hours in all. Find the speed of the stream.
(A) 2 km/h  (B) 3 km/h  (C) 4 km/h  (D) 2.5 km/h
Answer: (B) 3 km/h
Solution: Equation: 91/(10 + v) + 91/(10 - v) = 20 => 91 * [20 / (100 - v^2)] = 20 => 100 - v^2 = 91 => v^2 = 9 => v = 3 km/h. The required value is 3 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

36. (Medium) A man can row 6 km/h in still water. It takes him twice as long to row up as to row down the river. Find the rate of the stream.
(A) 1.5 km/h  (B) 3 km/h  (C) 2 km/h  (D) 2.5 km/h
Answer: (C) 2 km/h
Solution: Ratio u / v = (k + 1) / (k - 1) where k = 2. 6 / v = (2 + 1) / (2 - 1) = 3 / 1 => 3v = 6 => v = 2 km/h. The required value is 2 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

37. (Medium) A person travels three equal distances at speeds of 10 km/h, 20 km/h, and 30 km/h respectively. What is his average speed for the whole journey?
(A) 15.50 km/h  (B) 17.00 km/h  (C) 18.25 km/h  (D) 16.36 km/h
Answer: (D) 16.36 km/h
Solution: Average Speed = 3xyz / (xy + yz + zx) = (3 * 10 * 20 * 30) / (200 + 600 + 300) = 18000 / 1100 = 180 / 11 = 16.36 km/h. The required value is 16.36 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

38. (Medium) A thief is noticed by a policeman from a distance of 200 meters. The thief starts running and the policeman chases him. The thief and the policeman run at the rate of 10 km/h and 11 km/h respectively. What is the distance between them after 6 minutes?
(A) 100 meters  (B) 120 meters  (C) 80 meters  (D) 150 meters
Answer: (A) 100 meters
Solution: Relative speed = 11 - 10 = 1 km/h = 1000 m / 60 min = 50/3 m/min. In 6 minutes, distance gained by policeman = (50/3) * 6 = 100 meters. Distance remaining = 200 - 100 = 100 meters. The required value is 100 meters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

39. (Medium) A thief steals a car at 1:30 PM and drives it at 40 km/h. The theft is discovered at 2:00 PM and the owner sets off in another car at 50 km/h. At what time will he overtake the thief?
(A) 3:30 PM  (B) 4:00 PM  (C) 4:30 PM  (D) 5:00 PM
Answer: (B) 4:00 PM
Solution: Distance covered by thief in 30 minutes (0.5 hour) = 40 * 0.5 = 20 km. Relative speed of owner = 50 - 40 = 10 km/h. Time taken to overtake = 20 / 10 = 2 hours. Overtake time = 2:00 PM + 2 hours = 4:00 PM. The required value is 4:00 PM. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

40. (Medium) Two trains start at the same time from points A and B, 300 km apart, and travel towards each other at 60 km/h and 40 km/h. How far from station A will they meet?
(A) 160 km  (B) 200 km  (C) 180 km  (D) 150 km
Answer: (C) 180 km
Solution: Relative speed in opposite direction = 60 + 40 = 100 km/h. Time to meet = 300 / 100 = 3 hours. Distance from A = Speed of train from A * Time = 60 * 3 = 180 km. The required value is 180 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

41. (Medium) A train running at 54 km/h takes 20 seconds to cross a platform. Next, it takes 12 seconds to pass a man walking at 6 km/h in the same direction in which the train is going. Find the length of the train.
(A) 150 meters  (B) 180 meters  (C) 140 meters  (D) 160 meters
Answer: (D) 160 meters
Solution: Speed of train = 54 * (5/18) = 15 m/s. Man speed = 6 * (5/18) = 5/3 m/s. Relative speed = 15 - 5/3 = 40/3 m/s. Length of train = Relative speed * Time = (40/3) * 12 = 160 meters. The required value is 160 meters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

43. (Medium) A man walks at 6 km/h and reaches his destination 20 minutes late. If he increases his speed to 8 km/h, he still reaches 5 minutes late. Find the distance.
(A) 5 km  (B) 6 km  (C) 7 km  (D) 8 km
Answer: (B) 6 km
Solution: Both arrivals are late, so time difference ΔT = 20 - 5 = 15 minutes = 0.25 hours. Distance = (S1 * S2 / |S1 - S2|) * ΔT = (6 * 8 / 2) * 0.25 = 24 * 0.25 = 6 km. The required value is 6 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

46. (Medium) A train passes a station platform in 36 seconds and a man standing on the platform in 20 seconds. If the speed of the train is 54 km/h, what is the length of the platform?

Answer: (A) 240 meters
Solution: Speed = 54 * (5/18) = 15 m/s. Length of train = Speed * Time to pass man = 15 * 20 = 300 meters. Total distance to pass platform = 15 * 36 = 540 meters. Length of platform = 540 - 300 = 240 meters. The required value is 240 meters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

47. (Medium) Two trains are running in opposite directions with the same speed. If the length of each train is 120 meters and they cross each other in 12 seconds, find the speed of each train in km/h.
(A) 32 km/h  (B) 36 km/h  (C) 40 km/h  (D) 45 km/h
Answer: (B) 36 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 120 + 120 = 240 meters. Relative speed = 240 / 12 = 20 m/s. Since speeds are equal, 2S = 20 m/s => S = 10 m/s. In km/h = 10 * (18/5) = 36 km/h. The required value is 36 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

48. (Medium) A car covers a distance of 715 km at a constant speed. If the speed of the car had been 10 km/h more, it would have taken 2 hours less to cover the same distance. What is the original speed of the car?
(A) 50 km/h  (B) 60 km/h  (C) 55 km/h  (D) 65 km/h
Answer: (C) 55 km/h
Solution: Equation: 715/S - 715/(S + 10) = 2 => 7150 / [S(S + 10)] = 2 => S(S + 10) = 3575. Testing S = 55: 55 * 65 = 3575. Thus, original speed = 55 km/h. The required value is 55 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
