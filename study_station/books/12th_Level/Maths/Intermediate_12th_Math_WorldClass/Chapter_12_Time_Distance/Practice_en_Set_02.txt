26. A train 180 m long is running at a speed of 54 km/h. How much time will it take to cross a platform 120 m long?
(a) 15 seconds (b) 20 seconds (c) 18 seconds (d) 24 seconds
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 15 seconds comes from using only the train's length (180 m) instead of adding the platform length. Total distance = 180 + 120 = 300 m. Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s. Time = 300/15 = 20 seconds, so the answer is 20 seconds.

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 50 km/h comes from wrongly adding 5 km/h instead of using the inverse relation. Distance = 45 × 4 = 180 km. Required speed = 180/3 = 60 km/h, so the answer is 60 km/h.

28. Two trains of length 150 m and 100 m are running towards each other on parallel tracks at 40 km/h and 50 km/h. In what time will they cross each other?
(a) 10 seconds (b) 8 seconds (c) 12 seconds (d) 15 seconds
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 15 seconds comes from using the difference of speeds instead of the sum for opposite directions. Relative speed = 40 + 50 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Total length = 250 m. Time = 250/25 = 10 seconds, so the answer is 10 seconds.

29. A car travels the first half of a journey at 40 km/h and the second half at 60 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 45 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 48 km/h (d) 52 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 50 km/h is the simple arithmetic mean, which is wrong when equal distances are covered at different speeds. Average speed = 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h, so the answer is 48 km/h.
