Questions 51–75: Medium

51. A cricketer has an average of 32 runs in 10 innings. How many runs must he score in the next inning so as to increase his average by 4 runs?
(a) 76 (b) 72 (c) 70 (d) 80
Answer: (a) 76
Solution: Target average for 11 innings = 32 + 4 = 36 runs. Total runs required = 11 * 36 = 396. Runs in first 10 innings = 10 * 32 = 320. Runs needed in 11th inning = 396 - 320 = 76. The required value is 76. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. A batsman in his 17th inning makes a score of 85 runs, and thereby increases his average by 3 runs. What is his average after the 17th inning?
(a) 34 (b) 37 (c) 40 (d) 36
Answer: (b) 37
Solution: Let new average after 17th inning be A. The score in 17th inning = A + (17 - 1) * 3 = A + 48 = 85. Therefore, A = 85 - 48 = 37. The required value is 37. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

53. A bowler has a bowling average of 12.4 runs per wicket. In his next match, he takes 5 wickets for 26 runs, thereby decreasing his average by 0.4 runs per wicket. How many wickets had he taken before this match?
(a) 80 (b) 90 (c) 85 (d) 75
Answer: (c) 85
Solution: Performance in current match = 26 / 5 = 5.2 runs/wicket. New overall average = 12.4 - 0.4 = 12.0. By alligation: (12.4 - 12.0) : (12.0 - 5.2) = 0.4 : 6.8 = 1 : 17. The current match represents 1 part (5 wickets), so the previous matches represent 17 parts = 17 * 5 = 85 wickets. The required value is 85. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

54. The batting average for 40 innings of a cricket player is 50 runs. His highest score exceeds his lowest score by 172 runs. If these two innings are excluded, the average of the remaining 38 innings is 48 runs. Find the highest score of the player.
(a) 170 (b) 176 (c) 172 (d) 174
Answer: (d) 174
Solution: Total runs in 40 innings = 40 * 50 = 2,000. Total runs in 38 innings = 38 * 48 = 1,824. Sum of highest and lowest scores = 2,000 - 1,824 = 176. Given Highest - Lowest = 172. Adding the two equations: 2 * Highest = 176 + 172 = 348 => Highest = 174 runs. The required value is 174. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

55. The average age of husband, wife, and their child 3 years ago was 27 years, and that of wife and child 5 years ago was 20 years. What is the present age of the husband?
(a) 40 years (b) 38 years (c) 42 years (d) 45 years
Answer: (a) 40 years
Solution: Present sum of ages of husband, wife, and child = (3 * 27) + (3 * 3) = 81 + 9 = 90 years. Present sum of ages of wife and child = (2 * 20) + (2 * 5) = 40 + 10 = 50 years. Present age of husband = 90 - 50 = 40 years. The required value is 40 years. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

56. The average age of a committee of 8 members is 40 years. A member aged 55 years retired and his place was taken by a new member aged 39 years. What is the present average age of the committee?
(a) 39 years (b) 38 years (c) 37.5 years (d) 38.5 years
Answer: (b) 38 years
Solution: Net change in total age = Incoming age - Outgoing age = 39 - 55 = -16 years. Change in average = -16 / 8 = -2 years. New average = 40 - 2 = 38 years. The required value is 38 years. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

57. The average of 5 consecutive integers starting with m is n. What is the average of 6 consecutive integers starting with (m + 2)?
(a) n + 2 (b) n + 3 (c) n + 2.5 (d) n + 3.5
Answer: (c) n + 2.5
Solution: Average of 5 consecutive integers starting at m is m + 2 = n => m = n - 2. Six consecutive integers starting at m + 2 have average = (m + 2) + 2.5 = m + 4.5. Substituting m = n - 2: (n - 2) + 4.5 = n + 2.5. The required value is n + 2.5. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

58. The average marks obtained by 150 students was 60. If the average marks of passed students was 70 and that of failed students was 20, what is the number of students who passed?
(a) 110 (b) 125 (c) 100 (d) 120
Answer: (d) 120
Solution: By alligation rule: Ratio of Passed to Failed = (60 - 20) : (70 - 60) = 40 : 10 = 4 : 1. Passed students = 150 * (4/5) = 120. The required value is 120. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

59. A batsman has played 14 innings and has an average of 42 runs. In the 15th inning, he scores 98 runs. What is his new average?
(a) 45.73 (b) 46 (c) 44.5 (d) 45
Answer: (a) 45.73
Solution: Surplus over previous average = 98 - 42 = 56 runs. Increase in average across 15 innings = 56 / 15 = 3.73 runs. New average = 42 + 3.73 = 45.73 runs. The required value is 45.73. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

60. A family consists of grandparents, parents, and 3 grandchildren. The average age of the grandparents is 67 years, that of the parents is 35 years, and that of the grandchildren is 6 years. What is the average age of the family?
(a) 32 1/7 years (b) 31 5/7 years (c) 30 years (d) 33 4/7 years
Answer: (b) 31 5/7 years
Solution: Total family members = 2 + 2 + 3 = 7. Total sum of ages = (2 * 67) + (2 * 35) + (3 * 6) = 134 + 70 + 18 = 222 years. Average age = 222 / 7 = 31 5/7 years. The required value is 31 5/7 years. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

61. The average weight of 10 students is 35 kg. If the lightest student weighing 26 kg and the heaviest student weighing 44 kg are excluded, what is the average weight of the remaining 8 students?
(a) 34.5 kg (b) 35.5 kg (c) 35 kg (d) 36 kg
Answer: (c) 35 kg
Solution: Total sum of 10 students = 10 * 35 = 350 kg. Sum of the two excluded students = 26 + 44 = 70 kg. Sum of remaining 8 students = 350 - 70 = 280 kg. Average = 280 / 8 = 35 kg. The required value is 35 kg. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

62. A cricketer scored runs in 5 matches as follows: 45, 0, 78, 102, 55. What is his batting average across these matches if he remained not out in the second match?
(a) 56 (b) 65 (c) 68 (d) 70
Answer: (d) 70
Solution: Total runs scored = 45 + 0 + 78 + 102 + 55 = 280 runs. Since he was not out in 1 match, the number of completed innings (dismissals) = 5 - 1 = 4. Batting average = 280 / 4 = 70. The required value is 70. Hence, the correct option is (d).

64. A car travels 100 km at 50 km/h, 120 km at 60 km/h, and 70 km at 35 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 50 km/h (b) 48.33 km/h (c) 45 km/h (d) 52 km/h
Answer: (b) 48.33 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 100 + 120 + 70 = 290 km. Total time = (100 / 50) + (120 / 60) + (70 / 35) = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 hours. Average speed = 290 / 6 = 48.33 km/h. The required value is 48.33 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

65. The average of 8 numbers is 20. The average of the first two numbers is 15.5 and that of the next three numbers is 21 1/3. If the sixth number is less than the seventh and eighth numbers by 4 and 7 respectively, find the eighth number.
(a) 22 (b) 18 (c) 25 (d) 24
Answer: (c) 25
Solution: Sum of all 8 numbers = 8 * 20 = 160. Sum of first two = 2 * 15.5 = 31. Sum of next three = 3 * (64 / 3) = 64. Sum of remaining three (6th, 7th, 8th) = 160 - (31 + 64) = 160 - 95 = 65. Let 6th be x, then 7th = x + 4, 8th = x + 7. x + (x + 4) + (x + 7) = 65 => 3x + 11 = 65 => 3x = 54 => x = 18. Eighth number = 18 + 7 = 25. The required value is 25. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (d) Rs 52,000
Solution: Manager's salary = New average + (Initial workers) * (Increase) = 12,000 + 20 * 2,000 = 12,000 + 40,000 = Rs 52,000. The required value is Rs 52,000. Hence, the correct option is (d).

Solution: Total score = 10 * 82 = 820. Top 3 total = 3 * 94 = 282. Remaining 7 total = 820 - 282 = 538. Average of 7 students = 538 / 7 = 76.86. The required value is 76.86. Hence, the correct option is (a).

Answer: (b) 37.5
Solution: Total sum of 50 numbers = 50 * 38 = 1,900. Sum of 2 discarded numbers = 45 + 55 = 100. Sum of remaining 48 numbers = 1,900 - 100 = 1,800. New average = 1,800 / 48 = 37.5. The required value is 37.5. Hence, the correct option is (b).

69. The average of 6 numbers is 30. If the average of the first four is 25 and that of the last three is 35, the fourth number is:
(a) 20 (b) 30 (c) 25 (d) 35
Answer: (c) 25
Solution: Total sum of 6 numbers = 6 * 30 = 180. Sum of first 4 numbers = 4 * 25 = 100. Sum of last 3 numbers = 3 * 35 = 105. Since the 4th number is counted in both groups: 4th number = (100 + 105) - 180 = 205 - 180 = 25. The required value is 25. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

70. A cricketer has an average of 45 runs in his 20 innings. His highest score exceeds his lowest score by 150 runs. If these two innings are discarded, the average of the remaining 18 innings is 40 runs. Find his lowest score.
(a) 10 runs (b) 20 runs (c) 12 runs (d) 15 runs
Answer: (d) 15 runs
Solution: Total runs in 20 innings = 20 * 45 = 900. Total runs in 18 innings = 18 * 40 = 720. Sum of highest and lowest = 900 - 720 = 180. Given Highest - Lowest = 150. Subtracting the equations: 2 * Lowest = 180 - 150 = 30 => Lowest = 15 runs. The required value is 15 runs. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

72. The average of five numbers is 65. The average of the first two numbers is 81 and the average of the last two numbers is 38. What is the third number?
(a) 85 (b) 87 (c) 89 (d) 91
Answer: (b) 87
Solution: Sum of all 5 numbers = 5 * 65 = 325. Sum of first two = 2 * 81 = 162. Sum of last two = 2 * 38 = 76. Third number = 325 - (162 + 76) = 325 - 238 = 87. The required value is 87. Hence, the correct option is (b).

73. The average weight of 29 students is 48 kg. By adding the weight of a teacher, the average increases by 500 grams. Find the weight of the teacher.
(a) 62.5 kg (b) 64 kg (c) 63 kg (d) 61 kg
Answer: (c) 63 kg
Solution: Teacher's weight = New average + (Previous students) * (Increase) = (48 + 0.5) + 29 * 0.5 = 48.5 + 14.5 = 63 kg. The required value is 63 kg. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

74. The average of 3 numbers is 77. The first number is twice the second number and the second number is twice the third number. Find the first number.
(a) 128 (b) 136 (c) 140 (d) 132
Answer: (d) 132
Solution: Let the third number be x. Then second = 2x, first = 4x. Sum = 4x + 2x + x = 7x. Given average is 77, sum = 3 * 77 = 231. 7x = 231 => x = 33. First number = 4x = 4 * 33 = 132. The required value is 132. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

75. In a class, the average age of 24 boys is 11 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average age becomes 12 years. Find the teacher's age.
(a) 35 years (b) 36 years (c) 38 years (d) 34 years
Answer: (b) 36 years
Solution: Teacher's age = New Average + (Initial boys) * (Increase in average) = 12 + 24 * (12 - 11) = 12 + 24 = 36 years. The required value is 36 years. Hence, the correct option is (b).
