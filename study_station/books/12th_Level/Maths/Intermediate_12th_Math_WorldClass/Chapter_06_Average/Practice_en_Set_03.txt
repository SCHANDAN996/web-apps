51. The average of 11 numbers is 50. If the average of the first six numbers is 49 and that of the last six numbers is 52, find the sixth number.
(a) 56 (b) 50 (c) 46 (d) 44
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 50 comes from assuming the sixth number equals the overall average, but it is counted twice. Sum of all 11 numbers = 11 × 50 = 550. Sum of first six = 6 × 49 = 294; sum of last six = 6 × 52 = 312. Sixth number = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56, so the answer is 56.
Source: PYQ-style

52. The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one of them weighing 65 kg. Find the weight of the new person.
(a) 76 kg (b) 75 kg (c) 80 kg (d) 85 kg
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students add only 2.5 kg to 65 kg and pick 67.5-type thinking, but the increase applies to all 8 persons. Total increase = 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg. New person's weight = 65 + 20 = 85 kg, so the answer is 85 kg.

53. The average of five consecutive even numbers is 24. Find the largest of these numbers.
(a) 26 (b) 30 (c) 24 (d) 28
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to take 24 as the largest number, but the average of consecutive numbers is the middle one. The five numbers are 20, 22, 24, 26, 28. Largest = 24 + 4 = 28, so the answer is 28.

54. The average age of a class of 30 students is 14 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average becomes 15 years. Find the teacher's age.
(a) 40 years (b) 42 years (c) 45 years (d) 44 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: A common error is adding just 1 year to 14, but the teacher raises the average of all 31 people by 1 year. Students' total age = 30 × 14 = 420; new total = 31 × 15 = 465. Teacher's age = 465 − 420 = 45 years, so the answer is 45 years.

55. The average of 20 numbers is zero. At most how many of them can be greater than zero?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 19 (d) 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer 10 assumes half must be positive and half negative, but there is no such rule. If 19 numbers are positive, the 20th can be a single negative number equal in magnitude to their total, keeping the sum zero. So at most 19 numbers can be positive, so the answer is 19.

56. A batsman makes a score of 87 runs in the 17th innings and thus increases his average by 3. Find his average after the 17th innings.
(a) 42 (b) 36 (c) 39 (d) 40
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to subtract 3 from 87 directly. Let the new average be x. Then 16(x − 3) + 87 = 17x, giving 16x − 48 + 87 = 17x, so x = 39. The average after the 17th innings is 39, so the answer is 39.

57. The average of three numbers is 77. The first number is twice the second and the second is twice the third. Find the first number.
(a) 132 (b) 121 (c) 110 (d) 144
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often set the first number as x instead of the third and get tangled ratios. Let the third number be x; then second = 2x, first = 4x. Sum = 7x = 3 × 77 = 231, so x = 33. First number = 4 × 33 = 132, so the answer is 132.

58. The average marks of 100 students were 40. Later it was found that a score of 53 was misread as 83. Find the correct average.
(a) 39.3 (b) 40.3 (c) 39.7 (d) 40.7

Solution: The trap is to add 30 instead of subtracting, since 83 was wrongly entered in place of 53, inflating the total. Correction = 83 − 53 = 30 too much. Correct total = 4000 − 30 = 3970. Correct average = 3970 ÷ 100 = 39.7, so the answer is 39.7.

60. The average weight of A, B and C is 45 kg. If the average weight of A and B is 40 kg and that of B and C is 43 kg, find the weight of B.
(a) 30 kg (b) 34 kg (c) 36 kg (d) 31 kg
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to average 40 and 43 to guess B, but B is counted in both pairs. A + B + C = 135; A + B = 80; B + C = 86. B = 80 + 86 − 135 = 31 kg, so the answer is 31 kg.

61. The average of 50 numbers is 38. If two numbers, 45 and 55, are discarded, find the average of the remaining numbers.
(a) 37 (b) 38 (c) 37.5 (d) 36.5
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many assume the average stays 38 because 45 and 55 average to 50, but 50 is not the group average. Total = 50 × 38 = 1900; discarded sum = 100. Remaining sum = 1800 over 48 numbers, average = 1800 ÷ 48 = 37.5, so the answer is 37.5.

62. The average age of 24 students and their principal is 15 years. When the principal's age is excluded, the average age decreases by 1 year. Find the principal's age.
(a) 39 years (b) 38 years (c) 40 years (d) 37 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to think the principal is 15 + 1 = 16 years older than average, but removing him drops the average of 24 students by 1. Total of all 25 = 25 × 15 = 375; students' total = 24 × 14 = 336. Principal's age = 375 − 336 = 39 years, so the answer is 39 years.

63. The average of five numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 25. Find the excluded number.

Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer 27 assumes removing a number equal to the average changes nothing, but here the average falls. Sum of five = 135; sum of four = 100. Excluded number = 135 − 100 = 35, so the answer is 35.

64. A cricketer has a certain average for 10 innings. In the 11th innings he scores 108 runs, thereby increasing his average by 6 runs. Find his new average.
(a) 48 (b) 44 (c) 42 (d) 50
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to compute 108 ÷ 6 = 18 and stop, which is meaningless. Let the new average be x: 10(x − 6) + 108 = 11x, so 10x − 60 + 108 = 11x, giving x = 48. The new average is 48, so the answer is 48.

65. The average of 8 numbers is 20. The average of the first two is 15.5 and that of the next three is 21.33 (approximately 64/3). If the sixth number is 4 less than the seventh and 7 less than the eighth, find the eighth number.
(a) 25 (b) 22 (c) 28 (d) 30
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is misreading "4 less than" as the sixth being larger. Sum of all 8 = 160; first two = 31; next three = 64. Last three sum to 160 − 95 = 65. Let sixth = x; then seventh = x + 4, eighth = x + 7. So 3x + 11 = 65, x = 18. Eighth = 18 + 7 = 25, so the answer is 25.

66. The average salary of all workers in a workshop is Rs 8,000. The average salary of 7 technicians is Rs 12,000 and that of the rest is Rs 6,000. Find the total number of workers.
(a) 20 (b) 22 (c) 21 (d) 23
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to assume equal groups because 8,000 lies between 6,000 and 12,000, but it is not the midpoint. Let the rest be n: 7 × 12000 + 6000n = 8000(7 + n), so 84000 + 6000n = 56000 + 8000n, giving n = 14. Total = 7 + 14 = 21, so the answer is 21.

67. Three years ago, the average age of a family of 5 members was 17 years. A baby having been born, the average age of the family is the same today. Find the age of the baby.
(a) 1 year (b) 6 months (c) 3 years (d) 2 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to say the baby is 0 years old, forgetting the 5 older members each aged 3 years. Total age 3 years ago = 85; today those 5 total 85 + 15 = 100. For the average of 6 members to be 17, total must be 102. Baby's age = 102 − 100 = 2 years, so the answer is 2 years.

68. The average of the first 100 positive integers is:
(a) 50 (b) 50.5 (c) 51 (d) 49.5

Solution: The tempting answer 50 comes from dividing 100 by 2, but the average of 1 to n is (n + 1)/2. Average = (100 + 1)/2 = 50.5, so the answer is 50.5.

69. The average marks obtained by 120 candidates were 35. If the average of passed candidates was 39 and that of failed candidates was 15, find the number of candidates who passed.
(a) 90 (b) 100 (c) 110 (d) 80
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to split 120 in half since 35 is near the middle, but 35 is much closer to 39. Let passed = p: 39p + 15(120 − p) = 120 × 35, so 24p + 1800 = 4200, giving p = 100. The number who passed is 100, so the answer is 100.

71. A man travels equal distances at speeds of 40 km/h and 60 km/h. Find his average speed for the whole journey.
(a) 50 km/h (b) 52 km/h (c) 45 km/h (d) 48 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer 50 is the simple mean of 40 and 60, but average speed over equal distances is the harmonic mean. Average speed = 2 × 40 × 60 ÷ (40 + 60) = 4800 ÷ 100 = 48 km/h, so the answer is 48 km/h.

(a) 30 years (b) 28 years (c) 32 years (d) 26 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to add only 2 years to the pair's average of 22, but the increase applies to all 8 men. Total increase = 8 × 2 = 16 years. Sum of new men's ages = 21 + 23 + 16 = 60; average = 30 years, so the answer is 30 years.

73. The average of x and 1/x (x ≠ 0) is M. Find the average of x² and 1/x².
(a) 4M² − 2 (b) M² − 2 (c) 2M² + 1 (d) 2M² − 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to square M directly, forgetting the cross term. x + 1/x = 2M, so x² + 1/x² = (2M)² − 2 = 4M² − 2. Average = (4M² − 2)/2 = 2M² − 1, so the answer is 2M² − 1.

74. The average of 9 observations was 9, that of the 1st of 5 observations was 10 and that of the last 5 was 8. Find the 5th observation.
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 7 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to average 10 and 8 to get 9 without checking totals — here it works only after proper calculation. Total of 9 = 81; first five = 50; last five = 40. Fifth observation = 50 + 40 − 81 = 9, so the answer is 9.

75. In a class, the average score of girls is 73 and that of boys is 71. The average score of the whole class is 71.8. Find the percentage of girls in the class.
(a) 45% (b) 40% (c) 50% (d) 60%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to assume 50% girls since 71.8 looks near the middle, but it is closer to 71. Let girls = g, boys = b: 73g + 71b = 71.8(g + b), so 1.2g = 0.8b, giving g/b = 2/3. Girls' share = 2/5 = 40%, so the answer is 40%.
Source: PYQ-style
