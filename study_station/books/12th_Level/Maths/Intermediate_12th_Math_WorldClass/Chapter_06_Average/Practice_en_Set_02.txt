26. The average weight of 8 men is increased by 1.5 kg when one of the men, who weighs 65 kg, is replaced by a new man. What is the weight of the new man?
(a) 77 kg (b) 75 kg (c) 78 kg (d) 74 kg
Answer: (a) 77 kg
Solution: Weight of new man = Weight of replaced man + n * (Increase in average) = 65 + 8 * 1.5 = 65 + 12 = 77 kg. The required value is 77 kg. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. The average marks of 50 students in an examination was found to be 40. Later on, it was discovered that a score of 53 was misread as 83. What is the correct mean?
(a) 40.6 (b) 39.4 (c) 38.8 (d) 39.6
Answer: (b) 39.4
Solution: Difference = Correct value - Misread value = 53 - 83 = -30. Correction per student = -30 / 50 = -0.6. Correct average = 40 - 0.6 = 39.4. The required value is 39.4. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

28. The average marks of 40 students in a class was calculated as 60. Later it was found that the marks of two students were wrongly entered as 64 and 62 instead of 84 and 42 respectively. Find the actual average marks.
(a) 61.5 (b) 59.5 (c) 60 (d) 62
Answer: (c) 60
Solution: Correct sum of two scores = 84 + 42 = 126. Incorrect sum recorded = 64 + 62 = 126. Since the sum of correct scores equals the sum of incorrect scores, the net change is 0. Actual average remains 60. The required value is 60. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

29. The average temperature of Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday was 40 degrees C. The average temperature of Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday was 41 degrees C. If the temperature on Thursday was 42 degrees C, what was the temperature on Monday?
(a) 38 degrees C (b) 40 degrees C (c) 41 degrees C (d) 39 degrees C
Answer: (d) 39 degrees C
Solution: Difference equation: (Tue + Wed + Thu) - (Mon + Tue + Wed) = 3 * 41 - 3 * 40 = 3 degrees. Thus, Thu - Mon = 3 degrees. Given Thu = 42 degrees, Mon = 42 - 3 = 39 degrees C. The required value is 39 degrees C. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. The average age of 30 boys in a class is 14 years. When the age of the class teacher is included, the average becomes 15 years. Find the age of the class teacher.
(a) 45 years (b) 44 years (c) 46 years (d) 42 years
Answer: (a) 45 years
Solution: Teacher's age = New Average + (Initial number of boys) * (Increase in average) = 15 + 30 * 1 = 45 years. The required value is 45 years. Hence, the correct option is (a).

31. A library has an average of 510 visitors on Sundays and 240 on other days. The average number of visitors per day in a month of 30 days beginning with a Sunday is:
(a) 290 (b) 285 (c) 280 (d) 275
Answer: (b) 285
Solution: A 30-day month starting on Sunday has 5 Sundays (days 1, 8, 15, 22, 29) and 25 other days. Total visitors = (5 * 510) + (25 * 240) = 2550 + 6000 = 8550. Daily average = 8550 / 30 = 285. The required value is 285. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

32. In a class of 60 students, the average weight of 35 girls is 45 kg, and the average weight of 25 boys is 57 kg. Find the average weight of the whole class.
(a) 49 kg (b) 51 kg (c) 50 kg (d) 52 kg
Answer: (c) 50 kg
Solution: Ratio of girls to boys = 35 : 25 = 7 : 5. Weighted average = (7 * 45 + 5 * 57) / (7 + 5) = (315 + 285) / 12 = 600 / 12 = 50 kg. The required value is 50 kg. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

33. The average of 25 results is 18. The average of the first twelve of them is 14 and that of the last twelve is 17. Find the thirteenth result.
(a) 72 (b) 75 (c) 80 (d) 78
Answer: (d) 78
Solution: Total sum of 25 results = 25 * 18 = 450. Sum of first 12 results = 12 * 14 = 168. Sum of last 12 results = 12 * 17 = 204. Thirteenth result = 450 - (168 + 204) = 450 - 372 = 78. The required value is 78. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

34. The average of 11 numbers is 10.9. If the average of the first six is 10.5 and that of the last six is 11.4, find the middle (sixth) number.
(a) 11.5 (b) 11.2 (c) 11.8 (d) 10.8
Answer: (a) 11.5
Solution: Sum of all 11 numbers = 11 * 10.9 = 119.9. Sum of first 6 = 6 * 10.5 = 63.0. Sum of last 6 = 6 * 11.4 = 68.4. Middle number = (63.0 + 68.4) - 119.9 = 131.4 - 119.9 = 11.5. The required value is 11.5. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

35. The average salary of all the 50 employees in a company is Rs 16,000 per month. The average salary of 20 female employees is Rs 14,500. What is the average monthly salary of the remaining male employees?
(a) Rs 16,500 (b) Rs 17,000 (c) Rs 17,500 (d) Rs 18,000
Answer: (b) Rs 17,000
Solution: Total salary = 50 * 16,000 = 800,000. Salary of 20 females = 20 * 14,500 = 290,000. Salary of 30 males = 800,000 - 290,000 = 510,000. Average for males = 510,000 / 30 = Rs 17,000. The required value is Rs 17,000. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

36. The average of 5 consecutive even numbers is M. If the next 2 even numbers are also included, what is the new average?
(a) M + 1 (b) M + 1.5 (c) M + 2 (d) M + 3
Answer: (c) M + 2
Solution: When adding k consecutive even numbers with common difference 2, the average shifts forward by k * (2 / 2) = k. Here k = 2, so the average increases by 2 to M + 2. The required value is M + 2. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

37. The average of 6 consecutive odd numbers is 48. What is the average of the first and last of these numbers?
(a) 47 (b) 49 (c) 50 (d) 48
Answer: (d) 48
Solution: For any arithmetic progression, the average of the whole sequence is exactly equal to the average of the first and last terms: (first + last) / 2 = 48. The required value is 48. Hence, the correct option is (d).

38. A cricketer has an average of 58 runs in 9 innings. How many runs must he score in the 10th inning to raise his average to 61?
(a) 88 (b) 85 (c) 90 (d) 82
Answer: (a) 88
Solution: Required score in 10th inning = Target Average + (Previous Innings) * (Increase) = 61 + 9 * (61 - 58) = 61 + 9 * 3 = 61 + 27 = 88 runs. The required value is 88. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

39. The average age of 8 men increases by 2 years when two of them whose ages are 21 years and 23 years are replaced by two new men. What is the average age of the two new men?
(a) 28 years (b) 30 years (c) 32 years (d) 29 years
Answer: (b) 30 years
Solution: Sum of ages of replaced men = 21 + 23 = 44 years. Total increase for the group = 8 * 2 = 16 years. Sum of ages of new men = 44 + 16 = 60 years. Average age of the two new men = 60 / 2 = 30 years. The required value is 30 years. Hence, the correct option is (b).

40. The average weight of 3 men p, q, and r is 84 kg. Another man s joins the group and the average becomes 80 kg. If another man t, whose weight is 3 kg more than that of s, replaces p, then the average weight of q, r, s, and t becomes 79 kg. What is the weight of p?
(a) 72 kg (b) 78 kg (c) 75 kg (d) 70 kg
Answer: (c) 75 kg
Solution: Weight of p + q + r = 3 * 84 = 252 kg. Weight of p + q + r + s = 4 * 80 = 320 kg => s = 320 - 252 = 68 kg. Then t = 68 + 3 = 71 kg. Weight of q + r + s + t = 4 * 79 = 316 kg. Subtracting (q + r + s + t) from (p + q + r + s): p - t = 320 - 316 = 4 kg => p = t + 4 = 71 + 4 = 75 kg. The required value is 75 kg. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

41. The average of 8 numbers is 14. The average of 6 of these numbers is 16. What is the average of the remaining two numbers?
(a) 9 (b) 7.5 (c) 10 (d) 8
Answer: (d) 8
Solution: Sum of 8 numbers = 8 * 14 = 112. Sum of 6 numbers = 6 * 16 = 96. Sum of remaining 2 numbers = 112 - 96 = 16. Average of remaining 2 numbers = 16 / 2 = 8. The required value is 8. Hence, the correct option is (d).

43. The average weight of 12 parcels is 1.8 kg. Addition of another parcel reduces the average weight by 50 grams. What is the weight of the new parcel?
(a) 1.20 kg (b) 1.15 kg (c) 1.10 kg (d) 1.25 kg
Answer: (b) 1.15 kg
Solution: Total parcels now = 13. New average = 1.8 kg - 0.05 kg = 1.75 kg. Weight of new parcel = New average - (Previous parcels) * (Decrease) = 1.75 - 12 * 0.05 = 1.75 - 0.60 = 1.15 kg. The required value is 1.15 kg. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

44. The average age of a family of 5 members is 24 years. If the youngest member is 8 years old, what was the average age of the family at the time of the birth of the youngest member?
(a) 16 years (b) 18 years (c) 20 years (d) 22 years
Answer: (c) 20 years
Solution: Current total age of 5 members = 5 * 24 = 120 years. 8 years ago, sum of ages of the 4 other members = 120 - 5 * 8 = 120 - 40 = 80 years. Average age of those 4 members at that time = 80 / 4 = 20 years. The required value is 20 years. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

45. A person covers four successive 3 km stretches at speeds of 10, 20, 30, and 60 km/h respectively. Find his average speed for the whole journey.
(a) 25 km/h (b) 30 km/h (c) 18 km/h (d) 20 km/h
Answer: (d) 20 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 4 * 3 = 12 km. Total time = 3/10 + 3/20 + 3/30 + 3/60 = 3 * (6 + 3 + 2 + 1) / 60 = 3 * 12 / 60 = 36 / 60 = 0.6 hours. Average speed = 12 / 0.6 = 20 km/h. The required value is 20 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

48. In an examination, the average mark scored by 120 candidates was 35. The average mark of passed candidates was 39 and that of failed candidates was 15. How many candidates passed the examination?
(a) 90 (b) 105 (c) 100 (d) 96
Answer: (c) 100
Solution: Using rule of alligation: (39 - 35) : (35 - 15) = 4 : 20 = 1 : 5. Thus, Ratio of Passed to Failed = 20 : 4 = 5 : 1. Passed candidates = 120 * (5/6) = 100. The required value is 100. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

50. The average expenditure of a man for the first 4 months is Rs 1,800 and for the next 8 months is Rs 2,000. He saves Rs 5,600 during the year. What is his average monthly income?
(a) Rs 2,200 (b) Rs 2,400 (c) Rs 2,500 (d) Rs 2,600
Answer: (b) Rs 2,400
Solution: Total annual expenditure = (4 * 1800) + (8 * 2000) = 7,200 + 16,000 = 23,200. Total annual income = 23,200 + 5,600 = 28,800. Average monthly income = 28,800 / 12 = Rs 2,400. The required value is Rs 2,400. Hence, the correct option is (b).
