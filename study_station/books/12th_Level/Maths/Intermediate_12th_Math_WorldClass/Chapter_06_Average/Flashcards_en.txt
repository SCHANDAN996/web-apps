# Flashcards: Average Mastery

## Card 1: Fundamental Mean Formula
- **Front:** What is the formula for the Average and Sum of observations?
- **Back:** Average = (Sum of observations) / (Total count n); Sum = Average * n.

## Card 2: Replacement Formula
- **Front:** If one person of weight W_out is replaced by W_in in a group of n members, changing average by Delta A:
- **Back:** W_in = W_out + n * Delta A (where Delta A is positive for increase and negative for decrease).

## Card 3: Consecutive Natural Numbers Average
- **Front:** What is the average of the first n natural numbers?
- **Back:** (n + 1) / 2.

## Card 4: Consecutive Even Numbers Average
- **Front:** What is the average of the first n even natural numbers?
- **Back:** n + 1.

## Card 5: Consecutive Odd Numbers Average
- **Front:** What is the average of the first n odd natural numbers?
- **Back:** Exactly n.

## Card 6: Average Speed for Equal Distance
- **Front:** A vehicle travels distance d at speed u and returns at speed v. What is the average speed?
- **Back:** (2 * u * v) / (u + v) (Harmonic Mean).
