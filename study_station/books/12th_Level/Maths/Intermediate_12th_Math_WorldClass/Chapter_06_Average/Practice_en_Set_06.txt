126. The average of 11 numbers is 50. The average of the first 6 numbers is 49 and that of the last 6 numbers is 52. What is the 6th number?
(a) 46 (b) 48 (c) 50 (d) 56
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting shortcut is to assume the 6th number equals the overall average 50, but it is counted in both groups. Sum of all 11 = 550; first 6 = 294; last 6 = 312. The 6th number = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56, so the answer is 56.
Source: PYQ-style

127. The average age of a group of 30 students is 15 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average increases by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?
(a) 46 years (b) 45 years (c) 50 years (d) 40 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students just add 30 × 1 = 30 to 15 and pick 45, but the new average applies to all 31 people. New total = 31 × 16 = 496; students' total = 30 × 15 = 450. Teacher's age = 496 − 450 = 46, so the answer is 46 years.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics (Statistics)

128. The average of 5 consecutive even numbers is 24. What is the average of the next 5 consecutive even numbers following them?
(a) 34 (b) 30 (c) 29 (d) 32
Answer: (a)
Solution: A common trap is adding 5 to 24 and answering 29, but consecutive even numbers differ by 2, so the next block of five shifts each term by 10. The numbers are 20, 22, 24, 26, 28; the next five are 30, 32, 34, 36, 38 with average 34, so the answer is 34.
Source: PYQ-style

129. A batsman's average for 40 innings is 50 runs. His highest score exceeds his lowest score by 172 runs. If these two innings are excluded, his average for the remaining 38 innings becomes 48. What is his highest score?
(a) 170 (b) 172 (c) 174 (d) 176
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to treat 172 itself as the highest score. Total runs = 40 × 50 = 2000; runs in 38 innings = 38 × 48 = 1824. So highest + lowest = 176 and highest − lowest = 172. Adding gives 2 × highest = 348, so the answer is 174.
Source: PYQ-style

130. The average weight of A, B and C is 50 kg. The average weight of A and B is 45 kg and that of B and C is 52 kg. What is the weight of B?
(a) 44 kg (b) 46 kg (c) 42 kg (d) 48 kg
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is averaging the two pair-averages 45 and 52 to guess B. A + B + C = 3 × 50 = 150; A + B = 90; B + C = 104. Then B = 90 + 104 − 150 = 44, so the answer is 44 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

131. The average of 20 numbers is 15. The average of the first 12 numbers is 14 and that of the next 7 numbers is 16. What is the last number?
(a) 18 (b) 22 (c) 20 (d) 24
Answer: (c)
Solution: Students often divide the remainder by 20, forgetting that 12 + 7 = 19 leaves exactly one number. Total = 20 × 15 = 300; first 12 = 168; next 7 = 112. Last number = 300 − 168 − 112 = 20, so the answer is 20.
Source: PYQ-style

132. The average monthly income of P and Q is Rs 5050, of Q and R is Rs 6250, and of P and R is Rs 5200. What is the monthly income of P?
(a) 3500 (b) 4000 (c) 4050 (d) 5000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is averaging the three averages, which gives the mean of pairs, not P. P + Q = 10100, Q + R = 12500, P + R = 10400. Adding all three: 2(P + Q + R) = 33000, so P + Q + R = 16500 and P = 16500 − 12500 = 4000, so the answer is Rs 4000.
Source: PYQ-style

133. The average age of 8 persons increases by 2 years when two men aged 21 and 23 years are replaced by two new men. What is the average age of the two new men?
(a) 22 years (b) 28 years (c) 30 years (d) 24 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many forget the increase applies to all 8 persons and just average 21 and 23 to get 22. Total increase = 8 × 2 = 16 years. Sum of ages of new men = 21 + 23 + 16 = 60, so the answer is 30 years.
Source: PYQ-style

134. The average of 9 observations was 9, that of the 1st 5 observations was 10 and that of the last 5 observations was 8. What was the 5th observation?
(a) 9 (b) 8 (c) 7 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the 5th observation is the mean of 10 and 8 without proof — here it coincides, but it must be shown. Total = 81; first 5 = 50; last 5 = 40; 5th = 50 + 40 − 81 = 9, so the answer is 9.
Source: PYQ-style

135. A cricketer has a certain average for 10 innings. In the 11th innings he scores 108 runs, increasing his average by 6 runs. What is his new average?
(a) 48 (b) 52 (c) 55 (d) 60
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is dividing 108 by 11 or adding 6 to a guessed average. Let the old average be x: 10x + 108 = 11(x + 6), giving x = 42. New average = 42 + 6 = 48, so the answer is 48.
Source: PYQ-style

136. The average salary of all workers in a workshop is Rs 8000. The average salary of 7 technicians is Rs 12000 and of the rest is Rs 6000. How many workers are there in the workshop?
(a) 20 (b) 21 (c) 22 (d) 23
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming the two groups are equal in size. Let total workers be n: 8000n = 7 × 12000 + (n − 7) × 6000, so 2000n = 42000 and n = 21, so the answer is 21.
Source: PYQ-style

137. The average of three numbers is 77. The first number is twice the second and the second is twice the third. What is the first number?
(a) 33 (b) 66 (c) 77 (d) 132
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many assume the ratio is 2 : 1 : 1 and get stuck; actually the ratio is 4 : 2 : 1. Let the third be x: 4x + 2x + x = 231, so x = 33 and the first number = 4 × 33 = 132, so the answer is 132.
Source: PYQ-style

138. The average marks of 120 candidates were 35. If the average of passed candidates was 39 and that of failed candidates was 15, how many candidates passed?
(a) 90 (b) 110 (c) 100 (d) 120
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is splitting 120 equally between passed and failed. Let passed = p: 39p + 15(120 − p) = 4200, so 24p = 2400 and p = 100, so the answer is 100.
Source: PYQ-style

139. The average of 5 numbers is 7. When 3 new numbers are added, the average of the 8 numbers becomes 8.5. What is the average of the 3 new numbers?
(a) 9 (b) 10.5 (c) 12 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is subtracting the averages directly, 8.5 − 7 = 1.5. Sum of 5 numbers = 35; sum of 8 numbers = 68; sum of the 3 new numbers = 33, so their average = 11, so the answer is 11.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics (Statistics)

140. In a class, the average age of 24 boys and their teacher is 15 years. If the teacher's age is excluded, the average reduces by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?
(a) 38 years (b) 40 years (c) 39 years (d) 41 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 15 + 24 × 1 = 39 mechanically without verifying. Total of 25 people = 25 × 15 = 375; boys' total = 24 × 14 = 336; teacher = 375 − 336 = 39, so the answer is 39 years.
Source: PYQ-style

141. The average of 11 results is 60. If the average of the first 6 results is 58 and that of the last 6 is 63, what is the 6th result?
(a) 65 (b) 60 (c) 64 (d) 66
Answer: (d)
Solution: The common error is taking the mean of 58 and 63. Total = 11 × 60 = 660; first 6 = 348; last 6 = 378; 6th result = 348 + 378 − 660 = 66, so the answer is 66.
Source: PYQ-style

142. A man's average expenditure for the first 5 months is Rs 1200 and for the next 7 months is Rs 1300. He saves Rs 2900 during the year. What is his average monthly income?
(a) Rs 1400 (b) Rs 1500 (c) Rs 1600 (d) Rs 1700
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is averaging 1200 and 1300 directly to get 1250. Total expenditure = 5 × 1200 + 7 × 1300 = 15100; total income = 15100 + 2900 = 18000; monthly average = 1800 ÷ 12 = 1500, so the answer is Rs 1500.
Source: PYQ-style

143. The average of 8 numbers is 25. The average of the first two is 15 and that of the next three is 20. The 6th number is 4 less than the 7th and 7 less than the 8th. What is the 8th number?
(a) 42 (b) 36 (c) 38 (d) 40
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is ignoring the chain of relations among the last three numbers. Total = 8 × 25 = 200; first two = 30; next three = 60; last three = 110. Let the 6th be x: x + (x + 4) + (x + 7) = 110, so 3x = 99, x = 33, and the 8th = 33 + 7 = 40, so the answer is 40.
Source: PYQ-style

144. The average weight of 45 students is 52 kg. It was later found that the weight of one student was misread as 46 kg instead of 64 kg. What is the correct average weight?
(a) 52.4 kg (b) 52.2 kg (c) 52.6 kg (d) 52.8 kg
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding the full difference 18 to the average instead of to the total. Correction to total = 64 − 46 = 18 kg; correct total = 45 × 52 + 18 = 2358; correct average = 2358 ÷ 45 = 52.4, so the answer is 52.4 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

145. The average of 50 numbers is 30. If two numbers, 35 and 40, are discarded, what is the average of the remaining numbers?
(a) 28.5 (b) 29.17 (c) 29.69 (d) 30
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is subtracting the mean of 35 and 40 from 30. Total = 50 × 30 = 1500; remaining sum = 1500 − 75 = 1425 over 48 numbers; average = 1425 ÷ 48 ≈ 29.69, so the answer is 29.69.
Source: PYQ-style

146. The average age of a husband and wife was 23 years when they married 5 years ago. Now they have a child and the average age of the family is 20 years. What is the child's present age?
(a) 1 year (b) 2 years (c) 3 years (d) 4 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is forgetting to age both partners by 5 years each. At marriage, combined age = 46; now it is 46 + 10 = 56. Family of 3 totals 3 × 20 = 60, so the child's age = 60 − 56 = 4, so the answer is 4 years.
Source: PYQ-style

147. The average of 6 observations is 45.5. If one new observation is added, the average becomes 47. What is the new observation?
(a) 54 (b) 56 (c) 50 (d) 58
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing 47 + (47 − 45.5) = 48.5. Sum of 6 observations = 273; sum of 7 = 329; new observation = 329 − 273 = 56, so the answer is 56.
Source: PYQ-style

148. In an exam, the average marks of girls is 73 and of boys is 71. The overall average is 71.8. What is the ratio of girls to boys?
(a) 4 : 5 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 3 : 2 (d) 5 : 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is inverting the alligation ratio. Girls : boys = (71.8 − 71) : (73 − 71.8) = 0.8 : 1.2 = 2 : 3, so the answer is 2 : 3.
Source: PYQ-style

149. The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 61. What is the difference between the highest and the lowest of these numbers?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 6 (d) 10
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing 5 × 2 = 10, but 5 consecutive odd numbers span only 4 gaps of 2. The numbers are 57, 59, 61, 63, 65, and 65 − 57 = 8, so the answer is 8.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

150. A library has an average of 510 visitors on Sundays and 240 on other days. What is the average number of visitors per day in a month of 30 days beginning with a Sunday?
(a) 285 (b) 280 (c) 276 (d) 290
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming only 4 Sundays; a 30-day month starting on Sunday has 5 Sundays (1st, 8th, 15th, 22nd, 29th). Total visitors = 5 × 510 + 25 × 240 = 8550; average = 8550 ÷ 30 = 285, so the answer is 285.
Source: PYQ-style
