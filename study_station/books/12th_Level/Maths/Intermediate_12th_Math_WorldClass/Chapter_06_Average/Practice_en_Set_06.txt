126. The average weight of 12 students increases by 1/3 kg when a student weighing 40 kg is replaced by a new student. What is the weight of the new student?
(a) 44 kg (b) 43 kg (c) 42 kg (d) 45 kg
Answer: (a) 44 kg
Solution: Weight of new student = Replaced weight + n * (Increase) = 40 + 12 * (1/3) = 40 + 4 = 44 kg. The required value is 44 kg. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

127. A person travels 1/3 of the total distance at 20 km/h, 1/4 of the remaining distance at 25 km/h, and the rest at 30 km/h. What is his average speed for the entire trip?
(a) 25 km/h (b) 24 km/h (c) 22.5 km/h (d) 26 km/h
Answer: (b) 24 km/h
Solution: Let total distance be 120 km. First segment = 1/3 * 120 = 40 km at 20 km/h (time = 2 hours). Remaining = 80 km. Second segment = 1/4 * 80 = 20 km at 25 km/h (time = 0.8 hours). Third segment = 60 km at 30 km/h (time = 2 hours). Total time = 2 + 0.8 + 2 = 4.8 hours. Average speed = 120 / 4.8 = 24 km/h. The required value is 24 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

128. The average score of 60 students in an exam is 65. The average of the top 10 students is 85 and that of the bottom 10 students is 35. What is the average of the middle 40 students?
(a) 66.0 (b) 68.0 (c) 67.5 (d) 65.5
Answer: (c) 67.5
Solution: Total score = 60 * 65 = 3,900. Top 10 sum = 10 * 85 = 850. Bottom 10 sum = 10 * 35 = 350. Middle 40 sum = 3,900 - (850 + 350) = 3,900 - 1,200 = 2,700. Average of middle 40 = 2,700 / 40 = 67.5. The required value is 67.5. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

129. The average age of 10 members in a joint family is 33 years. If the youngest member is 9 years old, what was the average age of the family 9 years ago just after the birth of the youngest member?
(a) 26.67 years (b) 23 years (c) 25 years (d) 24 years
Answer: (d) 24 years
Solution: Current total age of 10 members = 10 * 33 = 330 years. 9 years ago, the total age of the 10 members (including the newborn at age 0) = 330 - 10 * 9 = 330 - 90 = 240 years. Average age of the 10 members just after birth = 240 / 10 = 24 years. The required value is 24 years. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

130. A batsman has a certain average of runs for 16 innings. In the 17th inning, he makes a score of 87 runs, thereby increasing his average by 3. What is his average after 17 innings?
(a) 39 (b) 36 (c) 42 (d) 38
Answer: (a) 39
Solution: Let old average be A. New average = A + 3. 17 * (A + 3) = 16 * A + 87 => 17A + 51 = 16A + 87 => A = 36. New average = 36 + 3 = 39. The required value is 39. Hence, the correct option is (a).

131. In a class, the average weight of male students is 65 kg and the average weight of female students is 50 kg. The overall average weight of the class is 60 kg. What percentage of the class are male students?
(a) 60% (b) 66.67% (c) 75% (d) 55%
Answer: (b) 66.67%
Solution: By alligation: Ratio of Males to Females = (60 - 50) : (65 - 60) = 10 : 5 = 2 : 1. Percentage of males = (2 / 3) * 100% = 66.67%. The required value is 66.67%. Hence, the correct option is (b).

132. The average of 8 numbers is 20. The average of the first two numbers is 15.5 and that of the next three numbers is 21 1/3. If the sixth number is 4 less than the seventh and 7 less than the eighth, what is the value of the seventh number?
(a) 18 (b) 25 (c) 22 (d) 20
Answer: (c) 22
Solution: Sum of all 8 numbers = 160. Sum of first two = 31. Sum of next three = 64. Sum of 6th, 7th, 8th = 160 - 95 = 65. Let 6th be x, then 7th = x + 4, 8th = x + 7. 3x + 11 = 65 => 3x = 54 => x = 18. Value of 7th number = 18 + 4 = 22. The required value is 22. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

133. The average marks of 50 students in a section was 72. Two students whose marks were 88 and 76 were wrongly registered as 38 and 67. Find the correct average.
(a) 72.82 (b) 73.50 (c) 72.50 (d) 73.18
Answer: (d) 73.18
Solution: Correct sum = 88 + 76 = 164. Recorded sum = 38 + 67 = 105. Net difference = 164 - 105 = +59. Per student increase = +59 / 50 = +1.18. Correct average = 72 + 1.18 = 73.18. The required value is 73.18. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

134. A cricketer has a bowling average of 16.2 runs per wicket. In his next match, he takes 4 wickets for 28 runs, reducing his bowling average by 0.2 runs per wicket. How many wickets did he take in total after this match?
(a) 184 (b) 180 (c) 176 (d) 190
Answer: (a) 184
Solution: Current match rate = 28 / 4 = 7.0 runs/wicket. Target average = 16.2 - 0.2 = 16.0. By alligation: (16.2 - 16.0) : (16.0 - 7.0) = 0.2 : 9.0 = 1 : 45. Since 1 unit corresponds to 4 wickets, 45 units correspond to 45 * 4 = 180 wickets. Total wickets after this match = 180 + 4 = 184 wickets. The required value is 184. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

135. The average of squares of first 7 natural numbers is:
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 21 (d) 24
Answer: (b) 20
Solution: Formula: (n + 1)(2n + 1) / 6. For n = 7: (7 + 1)(14 + 1) / 6 = (8 * 15) / 6 = 120 / 6 = 20. The required value is 20. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

136. The average of cubes of first 5 natural numbers is:
(a) 40 (b) 50 (c) 45 (d) 42
Answer: (c) 45
Solution: Formula: Average of cubes of first n natural numbers = n(n + 1)^2 / 4. For n = 5: 5 * (6)^2 / 4 = (5 * 36) / 4 = 5 * 9 = 45. The required value is 45. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

137. The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 55. What is the difference between the square of the largest number and the square of the smallest number?
(a) 840 (b) 900 (c) 860 (d) 880
Answer: (d) 880
Solution: The numbers centered at 55 are 51, 53, 55, 57, 59. Largest = 59, smallest = 51. Difference of squares = 59^2 - 51^2 = (59 - 51)(59 + 51) = 8 * 110 = 880. The required value is 880. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

138. The average age of a husband, wife, and their twin sons is 25 years. When the twin sons were born, the average age of the husband and wife was 38 years. Find the present age of the twin sons.
(a) 6 years (b) 5 years (c) 7 years (d) 8 years
Answer: (a) 6 years
Solution: Total present age of all 4 family members = 4 * 25 = 100 years. Let the twin sons be t years old each. At the time of their birth (t years ago), the sum of ages of husband and wife was 2 * 38 = 76 years. Over t years, their combined age increased by 2t years. Total family age today = 76 + 2t + 2t = 76 + 4t = 100 => 4t = 24 => t = 6 years. The required value is 6 years. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

139. A person covered 120 km by train at 60 km/h, 80 km by bus at 40 km/h, and 50 km by bicycle at 25 km/h. What was his average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 40 km/h (b) 41.67 km/h (c) 42 km/h (d) 45 km/h
Answer: (b) 41.67 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 120 + 80 + 50 = 250 km. Total time = (120 / 60) + (80 / 40) + (50 / 25) = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 hours. Average speed = 250 / 6 = 41.67 km/h. The required value is 41.67 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (b).

142. The average score of a student in 8 papers is 56. The highest score exceeds the lowest by 36. If these two papers are excluded, the average of the remaining 6 papers is 55. What is his lowest score?
(a) 41 (b) 40 (c) 42 (d) 38
Answer: (a) 41
Solution: Total sum of 8 papers = 8 * 56 = 448. Total sum of 6 papers = 6 * 55 = 330. Sum of highest and lowest = 448 - 330 = 118. Highest - Lowest = 36. Subtracting: 2 * Lowest = 118 - 36 = 82 => Lowest = 41. The required value is 41. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

143. The average of 7 numbers is 40. The average of the first 4 numbers is 35 and that of the last 4 numbers is 45. Find the fourth number.
(a) 38 (b) 40 (c) 42 (d) 45
Answer: (b) 40
Solution: Total sum = 7 * 40 = 280. Sum of first 4 = 4 * 35 = 140. Sum of last 4 = 4 * 45 = 180. Fourth number = (140 + 180) - 280 = 320 - 280 = 40. The required value is 40. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

146. The average temperature of the first 4 days of a week was 39 degrees C and that of the last 4 days of the week was 41 degrees C. If the average temperature of the whole week was 40 degrees C, find the temperature of the fourth day.
(a) 40 degrees C (b) 38 degrees C (c) 42 degrees C (d) 41 degrees C
Answer: (a) 40 degrees C
Solution: Total sum of week (7 days) = 7 * 40 = 280. Sum of first 4 days = 4 * 39 = 156. Sum of last 4 days = 4 * 41 = 164. Fourth day temperature = (156 + 164) - 280 = 320 - 280 = 40 degrees C. The required value is 40 degrees C. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Solution: Teacher's age = New average + (Initial students) * (Increase) = 15 + 30 * (15 - 14) = 15 + 30 = 45 years. The required value is 45 years. Hence, the correct option is (b).
