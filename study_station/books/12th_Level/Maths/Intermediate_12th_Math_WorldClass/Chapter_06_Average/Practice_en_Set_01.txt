Questions 1–25: Easy

1. Find the average of the first 50 natural numbers.
(a) 25.5 (b) 25 (c) 26 (d) 24.5
Answer: (a) 25.5
Solution: Average of first n natural numbers is given by (n + 1) / 2. Here n = 50, so average = (50 + 1) / 2 = 51 / 2 = 25.5. The required value is 25.5. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. Find the average of the first 40 even natural numbers.
(a) 40 (b) 41 (c) 42 (d) 39
Answer: (b) 41
Solution: Average of first n even natural numbers is n + 1. For n = 40, average = 40 + 1 = 41. The required value is 41. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

3. Find the average of the first 35 odd natural numbers.
(a) 36 (b) 34 (c) 35 (d) 35.5
Answer: (c) 35
Solution: Average of first n odd natural numbers is exactly n. Therefore, for n = 35, the average is 35. The required value is 35. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

4. Find the average of all multiples of 7 between 1 and 50.
(a) 21 (b) 24.5 (c) 35 (d) 28
Answer: (d) 28
Solution: Multiples of 7 between 1 and 50 are 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49. In an arithmetic progression, average = (first term + last term) / 2 = (7 + 49) / 2 = 56 / 2 = 28. The required value is 28. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

5. The average of 5 numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 25. Find the excluded number.
(a) 35 (b) 32 (c) 30 (d) 28
Answer: (a) 35
Solution: Sum of 5 numbers = 5 * 27 = 135. Sum of remaining 4 numbers = 4 * 25 = 100. Excluded number = 135 - 100 = 35. The required value is 35. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

6. The average of six numbers is 20. If one number is removed, the average becomes 15. What is the number removed?
(a) 40 (b) 45 (c) 35 (d) 50
Answer: (b) 45
Solution: Sum of 6 numbers = 6 * 20 = 120. Sum of 5 numbers = 5 * 15 = 75. Removed number = 120 - 75 = 45. The required value is 45. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

7. The average score of 8 students in a test is 72. If a ninth student scores 90 marks, what is the new average score?
(a) 73.5 (b) 75 (c) 74 (d) 72.5
Answer: (c) 74
Solution: Net surplus brought by the new student = 90 - 72 = 18. This surplus is distributed across all 9 students: 18 / 9 = 2. New average = 72 + 2 = 74. The required value is 74. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

8. The average of 7 consecutive numbers is 20. Find the largest of these numbers.
(a) 24 (b) 22 (c) 25 (d) 23
Answer: (d) 23
Solution: For 7 consecutive numbers, the average is the exact 4th number. So 4th number = 20. The 7th (largest) number = 20 + 3 = 23. The required value is 23. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

9. The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 61. What is the difference between the highest and lowest numbers?
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 6 (d) 12
Answer: (a) 8
Solution: For any n consecutive odd numbers, the difference between the largest and smallest is always 2 * (n - 1). Here 2 * (5 - 1) = 8. (The numbers are 57, 59, 61, 63, 65; 65 - 57 = 8). The required value is 8. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

10. The average of 9 numbers is 50. If the average of the first 5 numbers is 54 and that of the last 5 numbers is 52, find the fifth number.
(a) 75 (b) 80 (c) 70 (d) 85
Answer: (b) 80
Solution: Sum of all 9 numbers = 9 * 50 = 450. Sum of first 5 numbers = 5 * 54 = 270. Sum of last 5 numbers = 5 * 52 = 260. The fifth number is included in both sums: Fifth number = (270 + 260) - 450 = 530 - 450 = 80. The required value is 80. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

11. The average age of 24 students and their teacher is 15 years. If the teacher's age is excluded, the average decreases by 1 year. Find the age of the teacher.
(a) 38 years (b) 40 years (c) 39 years (d) 36 years
Answer: (c) 39 years
Solution: Total persons initially = 25. Teacher's age = Old Average + (Total remaining) * (Decrease) = 15 + 24 * 1 = 39 years. The required value is 39 years. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

12. The average weight of a group of 15 members is 40 kg. If a new member weighing 56 kg joins the group, what is the new average weight?
(a) 42 kg (b) 40.5 kg (c) 41.5 kg (d) 41 kg
Answer: (d) 41 kg
Solution: Surplus weight brought by the new member = 56 - 40 = 16 kg. New total members = 16. Increase per member = 16 / 16 = 1 kg. New average = 40 + 1 = 41 kg. The required value is 41 kg. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

13. Find the average of all prime numbers between 20 and 40.
(a) 31 (b) 30 (c) 32 (d) 29.5
Answer: (a) 31
Solution: Prime numbers between 20 and 40 are 23, 29, 31, 37. Sum = 23 + 29 + 31 + 37 = 120. Total count = 4. Average = 120 / 4 = 31. The required value is 31. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

14. The average of 4 numbers is 9. If a fifth number 14 is added, what is the new average?
(a) 11 (b) 10 (c) 9.5 (d) 10.5
Answer: (b) 10
Solution: Sum of 4 numbers = 4 * 9 = 36. New sum = 36 + 14 = 50. Total count = 5. New average = 50 / 5 = 10. The required value is 10. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

15. A car covers the first 60 km at a speed of 30 km/h and the next 60 km at a speed of 60 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey?
(a) 45 km/h (b) 42 km/h (c) 40 km/h (d) 38 km/h
Answer: (c) 40 km/h
Solution: For equal distances, average speed is the harmonic mean: (2 * 30 * 60) / (30 + 60) = 3600 / 90 = 40 km/h. The required value is 40 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

16. If the average of x, y, and z is 14, and the average of y and z is 10, find the value of x.
(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 18 (d) 22
Answer: (d) 22
Solution: Sum of x, y, and z = 3 * 14 = 42. Sum of y and z = 2 * 10 = 20. Therefore, x = 42 - 20 = 22. The required value is 22. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

17. The average of 8 numbers is 12. If each number is multiplied by 3, what will be the new average?
(a) 36 (b) 32 (c) 24 (d) 48
Answer: (a) 36
Solution: If each observation is multiplied by a constant k, the average is also multiplied by k. New average = 12 * 3 = 36. The required value is 36. Hence, the correct option is (a).

18. The average of 10 observations is 25. If 5 is subtracted from each observation, find the new average.
(a) 22 (b) 20 (c) 18 (d) 25
Answer: (b) 20
Solution: Subtracting a constant from every observation decreases the average by the same constant: New average = 25 - 5 = 20. The required value is 20. Hence, the correct option is (b).

19. The average of 6 numbers is 18. If one number is 23 and another is 15, what is the average of the remaining 4 numbers?
(a) 17 (b) 18 (c) 17.5 (d) 16.5
Answer: (c) 17.5
Solution: Sum of all 6 numbers = 6 * 18 = 108. Sum of the two known numbers = 23 + 15 = 38. Sum of remaining 4 numbers = 108 - 38 = 70. Average = 70 / 4 = 17.5. The required value is 17.5. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

20. A student scored 65, 72, 80, and 83 in four tests. What score must he obtain in the fifth test to have an overall average of 75?
(a) 78 (b) 72 (c) 80 (d) 75
Answer: (d) 75
Solution: Total score required in 5 tests = 5 * 75 = 375. Sum of first 4 tests = 65 + 72 + 80 + 83 = 300. Required fifth score = 375 - 300 = 75. The required value is 75. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

21. The average of three numbers is 20. If the first two numbers are 16 and 22, find the third number.
(a) 22 (b) 20 (c) 24 (d) 18
Answer: (a) 22
Solution: Sum of three numbers = 3 * 20 = 60. Sum of first two = 16 + 22 = 38. Third number = 60 - 38 = 22. The required value is 22. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

22. The average weight of 6 persons is increased by 1.5 kg when a new person replaces one of them who weighs 50 kg. What is the weight of the new person?
(a) 56 kg (b) 59 kg (c) 58 kg (d) 60 kg
Answer: (b) 59 kg
Solution: Replacement formula: Weight of new person = Weight of replaced person + n * (Increase in average) = 50 + 6 * 1.5 = 50 + 9 = 59 kg. The required value is 59 kg. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

23. Find the average of squares of the first 5 natural numbers.
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 11 (d) 11.5
Answer: (c) 11
Solution: Average of squares of first n natural numbers = (n + 1)(2n + 1) / 6. For n = 5: (5 + 1)(10 + 1) / 6 = (6 * 11) / 6 = 11. The required value is 11. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

24. The average of first 9 prime numbers is:
(a) 11 (b) 10 8/9 (c) 11 2/9 (d) 11 1/9
Answer: (d) 11 1/9
Solution: First 9 prime numbers are 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23. Their sum is 100. Average = 100 / 9 = 11 1/9. The required value is 11 1/9. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

25. A batsman has a certain average for 11 innings. In the 12th inning, he scores 90 runs and his average increases by 5 runs. What is his new average?
(a) 30 (b) 35 (c) 40 (d) 45
Answer: (b) 35
Solution: Let old average be A. Then 12 * (A + 5) = 11 * A + 90 => 12A + 60 = 11A + 90 => A = 30. New average = A + 5 = 35. The required value is 35. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
