Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The average marks of 100 students were found to be 40. Later on it was discovered that a score of 53 was misread as 83 and another score of 44 was misread as 24. What is the corrected average?
(a) 39.9 (b) 40.1 (c) 39.8 (d) 40.2
Answer: (a) 39.9
Solution: Correct sum of two scores = 53 + 44 = 97. Incorrect sum recorded = 83 + 24 = 107. Net difference = 97 - 107 = -10. Correction per student = -10 / 100 = -0.1. Corrected average = 40 - 0.1 = 39.9. The required value is 39.9. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

77. In an examination, the average score of a student was calculated as 67.6. If he had scored 27 more marks in Mathematics, 10 more marks in Computer Science, 18 more marks in History, and retained other marks, his average would have been 72.6. How many papers were there in the examination?
(a) 10 (b) 11 (c) 12 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 11
Solution: Total additional marks = 27 + 10 + 18 = 55 marks. Increase in average = 72.6 - 67.6 = 5.0 marks. Number of papers = Total additional marks / Increase in average = 55 / 5 = 11. The required value is 11. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

78. The average weight of 38 students is 42 kg. It was found that the weight of one student was read as 46 kg instead of 26 kg, and another was read as 34 kg instead of 54 kg. What is the actual average weight of the class?
(a) 42.5 kg (b) 41.5 kg (c) 42 kg (d) 43 kg
Answer: (c) 42 kg
Solution: Correct sum = 26 + 54 = 80 kg. Incorrect sum recorded = 46 + 34 = 80 kg. Difference = 80 - 80 = 0. Therefore, the actual average weight is unchanged at 42 kg. The required value is 42 kg. Hence, the correct option is (c).

79. The average of 15 numbers is 7. If the average of the first 8 numbers is 6.5 and that of the last 8 numbers is 9.5, find the eighth number.
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 21 (d) 23
Answer: (d) 23
Solution: Total sum of 15 numbers = 15 * 7 = 105. Sum of first 8 = 8 * 6.5 = 52. Sum of last 8 = 8 * 9.5 = 76. The 8th number is included in both sums: 8th number = (52 + 76) - 105 = 128 - 105 = 23. The required value is 23. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

80. The average age of 30 students in a class is 12 years. The average age of a group of 5 of them is 10 years and that of another group of 5 of them is 14 years. What is the average age of the remaining students?
(a) 12 years (b) 11.5 years (c) 12.5 years (d) 13 years
Answer: (a) 12 years
Solution: Total sum of ages of 30 students = 30 * 12 = 360 years. Sum of first 5 = 5 * 10 = 50. Sum of second 5 = 5 * 14 = 70. Sum of these 10 students = 50 + 70 = 120. Sum of remaining 20 students = 360 - 120 = 240 years. Average of remaining 20 = 240 / 20 = 12 years. The required value is 12 years. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

81. In a class of 50 students, 40% are girls and the rest are boys. The average score in mathematics of boys is 62 and that of girls is 68. What is the average score of the entire class in mathematics?
(a) 65.0 (b) 64.4 (c) 64.0 (d) 65.2
Answer: (b) 64.4
Solution: Percentage of boys = 60%, percentage of girls = 40%. Ratio of boys to girls = 3 : 2. Weighted average = (3 * 62 + 2 * 68) / (3 + 2) = (186 + 136) / 5 = 322 / 5 = 64.4. The required value is 64.4. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

82. The average of marks obtained by 100 candidates in a certain examination is 30. If the average marks of passed candidates is 35 and that of the failed candidates is 10, what is the number of candidates who failed the examination?
(a) 25 (b) 15 (c) 20 (d) 30
Answer: (c) 20
Solution: By alligation: Ratio of Passed to Failed = (30 - 10) : (35 - 30) = 20 : 5 = 4 : 1. Total parts = 4 + 1 = 5. Number of failed candidates = 100 * (1 / 5) = 20. The required value is 20. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

83. The average expenditure of a man for the first 3 months was Rs 2,200, for the next 4 months Rs 2,550, and for the remaining 5 months Rs 3,120. If his total savings during the year was Rs 1,260, find his average monthly income.
(a) Rs 2,750 (b) Rs 2,850 (c) Rs 2,900 (d) Rs 2,805
Answer: (d) Rs 2,805
Solution: Total annual expenditure = (3 * 2200) + (4 * 2550) + (5 * 3120) = 6600 + 10200 + 15600 = 32,400. Total annual income = 32,400 + 1,260 = 33,660. Average monthly income = 33,660 / 12 = Rs 2,805. The required value is Rs 2,805. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

84. The average salary per head of all the employees of a workshop is Rs 850. If the average salary of 7 technicians is Rs 1,000 and the average salary of the rest is Rs 780, find the total number of employees in the workshop.
(a) 22 (b) 20 (c) 24 (d) 25
Answer: (a) 22
Solution: By alligation: (1000 - 850) = 150; (850 - 780) = 70. Ratio of Technicians to Non-technicians = 70 : 150 = 7 : 15. Since there are 7 technicians, there are 15 non-technicians. Total employees = 7 + 15 = 22. The required value is 22. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

85. A person covers a distance of 450 km in 9 hours partly by train at 60 km/h and partly by car at 40 km/h. What distance was covered by train?
(a) 240 km (b) 270 km (c) 250 km (d) 280 km
Answer: (b) 270 km
Solution: Average speed overall = 450 / 9 = 50 km/h. By alligation between train (60) and car (40): Ratio of time spent on train to car = (50 - 40) : (60 - 50) = 10 : 10 = 1 : 1. Total time = 9 hours, so time on train = 4.5 hours. Distance by train = 4.5 * 60 = 270 km. The required value is 270 km. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

88. The average marks of 30 students in a class is 52. The average marks of top 6 students is 80 and the average marks of bottom 6 students is 30. What is the average marks of the remaining 18 students?
(a) 50 (b) 48 (c) 52 (d) 54
Answer: (a) 50
Solution: Total sum = 30 * 52 = 1,560. Sum of top 6 = 6 * 80 = 480. Sum of bottom 6 = 6 * 30 = 180. Sum of remaining 18 = 1,560 - (480 + 180) = 1,560 - 660 = 900. Average of remaining 18 = 900 / 18 = 50. The required value is 50. Hence, the correct option is (a).

Solution: New average = 20 + 10% of 20 = 22 years. Coach's age = New average + (Initial players) * (Increase) = 22 + 11 * 2 = 22 + 22 = 44 years. The required value is 44 years. Hence, the correct option is (a).

99. A batsman has a bowling average of 24.85 runs per wicket. He takes 5 wickets for 52 runs in the next match and thereby decreases his average by 0.85. Find the number of wickets taken by him till the last match.
(a) 80 (b) 90 (c) 75 (d) 85
Answer: (d) 85
Solution: Current match performance = 52 / 5 = 10.4 runs/wicket. New overall average = 24.85 - 0.85 = 24.0. By alligation: (24.85 - 24.0) : (24.0 - 10.4) = 0.85 : 13.6 = 1 : 16. Previous wickets = 16 * 5 = 80 wickets. Wickets till last match (including this match) = 80 + 5 = 85 wickets. The required value is 85. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

100. The average of 9 numbers is 60. The average of the first 5 numbers is 55 and that of the last 5 numbers is 68. What is the fifth number?
(a) 70 (b) 75 (c) 80 (d) 65
Answer: (b) 75
Solution: Total sum = 9 * 60 = 540. Sum of first 5 = 5 * 55 = 275. Sum of last 5 = 5 * 68 = 340. Fifth number = (275 + 340) - 540 = 615 - 540 = 75. The required value is 75. Hence, the correct option is (b).
