76. The average of 15 numbers is 40. If each number is increased by 6, what is the new average?
(a) 46 (b) 40 (c) 34 (d) 45
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 40, which ignores the increase. When every number is increased by the same value, the average increases by that same value: 40 + 6 = 46, so the answer is 46.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

77. The average age of 30 students is 12 years. If the teacher's age is included, the average becomes 13 years. What is the teacher's age?
(a) 40 years (b) 43 years (c) 45 years (d) 42 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 42, found by wrongly adding 12 + 30. Total age of students = 30 × 12 = 360; total with teacher = 31 × 13 = 403; teacher's age = 403 − 360 = 43, so the answer is 43 years.
Source: PYQ-style

78. The average of five consecutive even numbers is 24. What is the largest of these numbers?
(a) 28 (b) 30 (c) 26 (d) 32
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 26, which is only the next even number after the average. For consecutive even numbers, the average is the middle (third) number, so the numbers are 20, 22, 24, 26, 28; the largest is 28, so the answer is 28.

79. The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one weighing 55 kg. What is the weight of the new person?
(a) 70 kg (b) 77.5 kg (c) 75 kg (d) 72.5 kg
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 72.5 kg, found by adding only 2.5 × 7. Total increase = 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg, so new person's weight = 55 + 20 = 75 kg, so the answer is 75 kg.

80. The average of 11 results is 50. The average of the first six results is 49 and that of the last six is 52. What is the sixth result?
(a) 54 (b) 56 (c) 60 (d) 58
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 54, from averaging 49 and 52 carelessly. Sum of all 11 = 550; sum of first six = 294; sum of last six = 312; sixth result = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56, so the answer is 56.

81. A batsman has an average of 45 runs after 20 innings. How many runs must he score in his 21st innings to raise his average to 47?
(a) 85 (b) 87 (c) 89 (d) 91
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 89, from adding 2 × 20 to 49 incorrectly. Runs after 20 innings = 900; required total after 21 innings = 21 × 47 = 987; needed = 987 − 900 = 87, so the answer is 87.

82. The average of three numbers is 60. The first number is twice the second and the second is twice the third. What is the first number?
(a) 100 (b) 108 (c) 105 (d) 102.86
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 105, from assuming equal gaps. Let third = x, second = 2x, first = 4x; sum = 7x = 180, so x = 180/7 and first = 720/7 ≈ 102.86, so the answer is 102.86.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

83. The average age of a family of 6 members is 22 years. If the youngest member is 8 years old, what was the average age of the family just before the youngest was born?
(a) 17.5 years (b) 16.4 years (c) 16.8 years (d) 17 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 17, from subtracting 8 from 22 and rounding wrongly. Present total = 132; 8 years ago total of the other 5 members = 132 − 8 − (5 × 8) = 84; average = 84/5 = 16.8, so the answer is 16.8 years.
Source: PYQ-style

84. The average of 20 numbers is zero. At most how many of them can be greater than zero?
(a) 20 (b) 10 (c) 18 (d) 19
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 10, from assuming half must be positive. If 19 numbers are positive, the 20th can be a single negative number balancing their sum, but all 20 cannot be positive since the sum would then exceed zero, so the answer is 19.
Source: PYQ-style

85. The average marks of 100 students were 40. Later it was found that a score of 53 was misread as 83. What is the correct average?
(a) 40.5 (b) 39.5 (c) 40.3 (d) 39.7
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 40.3, from adding the error instead of subtracting. The total was overstated by 83 − 53 = 30, so correct total = 4000 − 30 = 3970 and correct average = 3970/100 = 39.7, so the answer is 39.7.

87. The average salary of all workers in a factory is Rs 8000. The average salary of 7 technicians is Rs 12000 and that of the rest is Rs 6000. How many workers are there in the factory?
(a) 21 (b) 19 (c) 20 (d) 22
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 20, from a wrong ratio split. Let total workers be n: 8000n = 84000 + 6000(n − 7), giving 2000n = 42000, so n = 21, so the answer is 21.

88. A cricketer's average for 11 innings is 40 runs. His average for the first 6 innings is 35 and for the last 6 innings is 42. How many runs did he score in the sixth innings?
(a) 24 (b) 26 (c) 22 (d) 20
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 24, from adding the two averages wrongly. Total = 440; first six = 210; last six = 252; sixth innings = 210 + 252 − 440 = 22, so the answer is 22.

90. The average age of 24 students and their class teacher is 15 years. If the teacher's age is excluded, the average drops by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?

Solution: The tempting wrong option is 40, from adding 25 to 15 directly. Total with teacher = 25 × 15 = 375; total of students = 24 × 14 = 336; teacher's age = 375 − 336 = 39, so the answer is 39 years.

93. The average weight of A, B and C is 45 kg. If the average weight of A and B is 40 kg and that of B and C is 43 kg, what is B's weight?
(a) 31 kg (b) 33 kg (c) 29 kg (d) 35 kg
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 33, from averaging 40 and 43 wrongly. A + B + C = 135; A + B = 80; B + C = 86; B = 80 + 86 − 135 = 31, so the answer is 31 kg.

94. A man buys 5 shirts at Rs 300 each, 4 shirts at Rs 350 each and 3 shirts at Rs 400 each. What is the average price per shirt?
(a) Rs 345 (b) Rs 337.50 (c) Rs 350 (d) Rs 341.67
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 350, from simply averaging 300, 350 and 400 without weights. Total cost = 1500 + 1400 + 1200 = 4100; average = 4100/12 ≈ 341.67, so the answer is Rs 341.67.

95. The average of 9 numbers is 20. If the average of the first five numbers is 18 and that of the last five numbers is 22, what is the fifth number?
(a) 22 (b) 18 (c) 20 (d) 24
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 18, from subtracting the two group sums in the wrong order. Sum of all 9 = 180; sum of first five = 90; sum of last five = 110; the fifth number is counted twice, so fifth = 90 + 110 − 180 = 20, so the answer is 20.
Source: PYQ-style

96. The average temperature for Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday was 35°C, and for Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday it was 37°C. If Thursday's temperature was 38°C, what was Monday's temperature?
(a) 34°C (b) 30°C (c) 32°C (d) 36°C
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 34, from halving the difference wrongly. Mon + Tue + Wed = 105; Tue + Wed + Thu = 111; subtracting, Thu − Mon = 6, so Mon = 38 − 6 = 32, so the answer is 32°C.
Source: PYQ-style

97. The average of 6 numbers is 3.95. The average of two of them is 3.4, while the average of the other two is 3.85. What is the average of the remaining two numbers?
(a) 4.5 (b) 4.6 (c) 4.7 (d) 4.8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 4.5, from rounding errors in subtraction. Total = 23.7; sum of first two pairs = 6.8 + 7.7 = 14.5; remaining sum = 9.2; average = 9.2/2 = 4.6, so the answer is 4.6.
Source: PYQ-style

98. In a class of 60 students, the average weight of boys is 55 kg and that of girls is 45 kg. If the average weight of the whole class is 52 kg, how many boys are there?
(a) 40 (b) 45 (c) 44 (d) 42
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 45, from assuming equal numbers. Let boys = b: 55b + 45(60 − b) = 60 × 52 = 3120, so 10b = 420 and b = 42, so the answer is 42.
Source: PYQ-style

99. The average of 25 observations is 36. It was later found that one observation, 48, was wrongly copied as 84. What is the correct average?
(a) 34.44 (b) 34.56 (c) 35.56 (d) 35.44
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 35.56, from adding the correction instead of subtracting. The total was overstated by 84 − 48 = 36, so correct total = 900 − 36 = 864 and correct average = 864/25 = 34.56, so the answer is 34.56.

100. The average of 10 numbers is 15. If each number is first multiplied by 3 and then decreased by 5, what is the new average?
(a) 40 (b) 50 (c) 45 (d) 35
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 45, from forgetting the subtraction of 5. New average = 3 × 15 − 5 = 45 − 5 = 40, so the answer is 40.
