Questions 126–150: Hard

126. What is the unit digit of 57^336?
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 8 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The unit digits of powers of 7 repeat in a cycle of 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. 336 leaves remainder 4 in this cycle, so the unit digit is 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

127. How many zeros are there at the end of 596! (596 factorial)?
(a) 145 (b) 59 (c) 146 (d) 148
Answer: (c)
Solution: Count the factors of 5: 596/5 + 596/25 + 596/125 + … (whole parts) = 146. There are always more 2s, so the number of trailing zeros is 146. So the answer is 146.
Source: PYQ-style

128. How many factors (divisors) does 198 have?
(a) 24 (b) 14 (c) 5 (d) 12
Answer: (d)
