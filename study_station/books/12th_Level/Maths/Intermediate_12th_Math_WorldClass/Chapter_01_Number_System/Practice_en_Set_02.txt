Questions 26–50: Medium

26. What is the remainder when 19¹⁰⁰ is divided by 20?
(a) 1 (b) 19 (c) 0 (d) 18
Answer: (a) 1
Solution: In modular arithmetic, 19 ≡ -1 (mod 20). Since the exponent 100 is even, (-1)¹⁰⁰ = 1. Dividing by 20 gives a remainder of 1. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. What is the unit digit of the expression (137)¹³ × (47)¹⁵?
(a) 3 (b) 1 (c) 7 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 1
Solution: Both bases have a unit digit of 7. Cyclicity of 7 is 4: 13 mod 4 = 1 so 7¹ ends in 7, and 15 mod 4 = 3 so 7³ ends in 3. Multiplying unit digits gives 7 × 3 = 21, which ends in 1. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

28. How many factors of the number 2⁴ × 3³ × 5² are perfect squares?
(a) 18 (b) 15 (c) 12 (d) 24
Answer: (c) 12
Solution: A factor is a perfect square if all prime exponents are even. For 2⁴, powers are {0, 2, 4} (3 choices); for 3³, powers are {0, 2} (2 choices); for 5², powers are {0, 2} (2 choices). Total square factors = 3 × 2 × 2 = 12. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

29. What is the remainder when (9¹⁹ + 6) is divided by 8?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 7
Answer: (d) 7
Solution: Since 9 ≡ 1 (mod 8), 9¹⁹ ≡ 1¹⁹ = 1. Adding 6 gives (1 + 6) = 7. Dividing by 8 leaves a remainder of 7. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

30. If the 6-digit number 4x573y is divisible by 72, what is the value of (x + y) for the largest value of y?
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 6
Answer: (a) 8
Solution: For divisibility by 8, last three digits 73y must be divisible by 8: 73 mod 8 = 1, so 1y must be 16, giving y = 6. For divisibility by 9, sum 4 + x + 5 + 7 + 3 + 6 = 25 + x must be a multiple of 9, giving x = 2. Thus, x + y = 2 + 6 = 8. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

31. What is the sum of all factors of the number 120?
(a) 320 (b) 360 (c) 240 (d) 280
Answer: (b) 360
Solution: Prime factorization: 120 = 2³ × 3¹ × 5¹. Sum of factors = (1 + 2 + 4 + 8)(1 + 3)(1 + 5) = 15 × 4 × 6 = 360. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

32. What is the remainder when 2⁵⁰ is divided by 7?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: Since 2³ = 8 ≡ 1 (mod 7), write 2⁵⁰ = (2³)¹⁶ × 2² ≡ 1¹⁶ × 4 = 4. The remainder is 4. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

33. How many trailing zeroes are at the end of the product 1 × 2 × 3 × ... × 80?
(a) 16 (b) 18 (c) 20 (d) 19
Answer: (d) 19
Solution: The product equals 80!. Using Legendre formula for prime 5: floor(80/5) + floor(80/25) = 16 + 3 = 19. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

34. If x = 3⁶⁷ + 2, what is the unit digit of x?
(a) 9 (b) 7 (c) 5 (d) 1
Answer: (a) 9
Solution: Cyclicity of 3 is 4: 67 mod 4 = 3, so 3⁶⁷ ends in the same unit digit as 3³ = 27, which is 7. Adding 2 gives 7 + 2 = 9. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

35. What is the remainder when (12¹³ + 23¹³) is divided by 35?
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) 34 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 0
Solution: Trap: Avoid expanding individual terms. For any odd exponent n, (aⁿ + bⁿ) is strictly divisible by (a + b). Since 13 is odd, (12¹³ + 23¹³) is divisible by (12 + 23) = 35, leaving a remainder of 0. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

36. How many factors of 540 are divisible by 6?
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 16
Answer: (c) 12
Solution: Write 540 as 6 × 90. Factors of 540 that are multiples of 6 correspond to total factors of 90: 90 = 2¹ × 3² × 5¹. Total factors = (1 + 1)(2 + 1)(1 + 1) = 2 × 3 × 2 = 12. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

37. What is the remainder when 10! is divided by 11?
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) 9 (d) 10
Answer: (d) 10
Solution: By Wilson theorem, for any prime p, (p - 1)! ≡ (p - 1) (mod p). Here p = 11, so 10! ≡ 10 (mod 11). The remainder is 10. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

38. What is the unit digit of the expression (73)⁵⁴ + (87)⁵⁴?
(a) 8 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (a) 8
Solution: Both exponents are 54: 54 mod 4 = 2. 3² ends in 9, and 7² = 49 ends in 9. Summing unit digits: 9 + 9 = 18, which ends in 8. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

39. A number when divided by 13 leaves remainder 11, and when divided by 17 leaves remainder 9. What is the smallest such positive integer?
(a) 115 (b) 128 (c) 141 (d) 154
Answer: (b) 128
Solution: Testing numbers: 128 = 13 × 9 + 11 (leaves 11), and 128 = 17 × 7 + 9 (leaves 9). Thus, the smallest positive integer is 128. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

40. How many trailing zeroes are at the end of (25! × 35!)?
(a) 12 (b) 13 (c) 14 (d) 15
Answer: (c) 14
Solution: Zeroes in 25! = floor(25/5) + floor(25/25) = 5 + 1 = 6. Zeroes in 35! = floor(35/5) + floor(35/25) = 7 + 1 = 8. Total zeroes in the product = 6 + 8 = 14. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

41. What is the remainder when 5⁹⁹ is divided by 13?
(a) 1 (b) 5 (c) 12 (d) 8
Answer: (d) 8
Solution: By Fermat Little Theorem, 5¹² ≡ 1 (mod 13). Since 99 = 12 × 8 + 3, 5⁹⁹ ≡ 5³ = 125 ≡ 8 (mod 13). The remainder is 8. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

42. What is the value of digit p such that the 7-digit number 653p472 is divisible by 11?
(a) 3 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 3
Solution: Sum of digits at odd places from right: 2 + 4 + 3 + 6 = 15. Sum of digits at even places: 7 + p + 5 = 12 + p. Difference: 15 - (12 + p) = 3 - p. Setting 3 - p = 0 gives p = 3. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

43. What is the product of all factors of the number 72?
(a) 72⁵ (b) 72⁶ (c) 72⁷ (d) 72⁸
Answer: (b) 72⁶
Solution: Prime factorization: 72 = 2³ × 3². Total number of factors = (3 + 1)(2 + 1) = 12. Product of factors formula is N^(T/2) = 72^(12/2) = 72⁶. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

44. What is the unit digit of (17)¹⁹⁹⁹ + (11)¹⁹⁹⁹ - (7)¹⁹⁹⁹?
(a) 7 (b) 9 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (c) 1
Solution: Trap: Since 17 and 7 share the same base unit digit 7 with equal exponents, their unit digits cancel completely: (..7) - (..7) = 0. The remaining term (11)¹⁹⁹⁹ always ends in 1. Thus, the unit digit is 1. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

45. Which of the following numbers will completely divide (23¹⁰ - 1024)?
(a) 11 (b) 13 (c) 17 (d) 21
Answer: (d) 21
Solution: Note that 1024 = 2¹⁰. The expression is (23¹⁰ - 2¹⁰). For even power n, (aⁿ - bⁿ) is divisible by (a - b) = 23 - 2 = 21. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

46. What is the remainder when 7⁸⁴ is divided by 342?
(a) 1 (b) 7 (c) 341 (d) 49
Answer: (a) 1
Solution: Since 7³ = 343 = 342 + 1, write 7⁸⁴ = (7³)²⁸ = (342 + 1)²⁸ ≡ 1²⁸ = 1 (mod 342). The remainder is 1. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

47. If N = 4¹¹ + 4¹² + 4¹³ + 4¹⁴, then N is completely divisible by which of the following?
(a) 11 (b) 17 (c) 19 (d) 23
Answer: (b) 17
Solution: Factor out 4¹¹: N = 4¹¹(1 + 4 + 16 + 64) = 4¹¹ × 85. Since 85 = 5 × 17, the expression is a multiple of 17. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

48. What is the number of distinct prime factors of the number 2310?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 5
Solution: Prime factorization: 2310 = 2 × 3 × 5 × 7 × 11. The distinct prime factors are 2, 3, 5, 7, and 11, giving a total of 5 distinct prime factors. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

49. What is the remainder when 3¹⁰⁰ is divided by 7?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: Cyclicity of powers of 3 mod 7: 3¹=3, 3²=2, 3³=6, 3⁴=4, 3⁵=5, 3⁶=1. Cycle length is 6: 100 = 6 × 16 + 4. Thus, 3¹⁰⁰ ≡ 3⁴ = 81 ≡ 4 (mod 7). The remainder is 4. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

50. How many natural numbers between 1 and 200 are divisible by both 4 and 6?
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 25 (d) 16
Answer: (d) 16
Solution: Trap: A number divisible by both 4 and 6 must be divisible by LCM(4, 6) = 12, not by 4 × 6 = 24. Number of multiples = floor(200/12) = 16. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern
