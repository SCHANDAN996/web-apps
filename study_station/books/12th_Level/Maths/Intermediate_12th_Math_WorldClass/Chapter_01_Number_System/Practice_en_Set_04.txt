Questions 76–100: Medium

76. What is the unit digit of 31^178?
(a) 6 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The unit digits of powers of 1 repeat in a cycle of 1: 1. 178 leaves remainder 1 in this cycle, so the unit digit is 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

77. How many zeros are there at the end of 345! (345 factorial)?
(a) 83 (b) 34 (c) 84 (d) 85
Answer: (c)
Solution: Count the factors of 5: 345/5 + 345/25 + 345/125 + … (whole parts) = 84. There are always more 2s, so the number of trailing zeros is 84. So the answer is 84.
Source: PYQ-style

78. How many factors (divisors) does 148225 have?
