Questions 76–100: Medium

76. What is the remainder when 11¹⁴² is divided by 10?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 9 (d) 0
Answer: (a) 1
Solution: In modular arithmetic, 11 ≡ 1 (mod 10). Therefore, 11¹⁴² ≡ 1¹⁴² = 1 (mod 10). The remainder is 1. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

77. What is the unit digit of (329)⁷⁸?
(a) 9 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 7
Answer: (b) 1
Solution: The base unit digit is 9. The cyclicity of 9 is 2: 9 raised to any positive even power always ends in 1. Since 78 is even, the unit digit is 1. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

78. What is the total number of factors of the number 300?
(a) 12 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 24
Answer: (c) 18
Solution: Prime factorization: 300 = 2² × 3¹ × 5². Total number of factors = (2 + 1)(1 + 1)(2 + 1) = 3 × 2 × 3 = 18. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

79. How many trailing zeroes are at the end of 90!?
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 22 (d) 21
Answer: (d) 21
Solution: Applying Legendre formula for prime 5 in 90!: floor(90/5) + floor(90/25) = 18 + 3 = 21. Thus, 90! ends in 21 trailing zeroes. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

80. What is the remainder when 2¹⁰⁵ is divided by 10?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Dividing an integer by 10 is mathematically equivalent to finding its unit digit. Cyclicity of 2 is 4: 105 mod 4 = 1, so 2¹⁰⁵ ends in 2. The remainder is 2. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

81. Which of the following numbers is completely divisible by 99?
(a) 35721 (b) 35739 (c) 35748 (d) 35757
Answer: (b) 35739
Solution: Divisibility by 99 requires divisibility by both 9 and 11. For 35739, sum of digits 3 + 5 + 7 + 3 + 9 = 27 (divisible by 9). Alternating difference: (9 + 7 + 3) - (3 + 5) = 19 - 8 = 11 (divisible by 11). Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

82. What is the sum of all factors of the number 36?
(a) 81 (b) 84 (c) 91 (d) 96
Answer: (c) 91
Solution: Prime factorization: 36 = 2² × 3². Sum of factors = (1 + 2 + 4)(1 + 3 + 9) = 7 × 13 = 91. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

83. What is the remainder when 3¹⁰² is divided by 28?
(a) 9 (b) 27 (c) 3 (d) 1
Answer: (d) 1
Solution: Notice that 3³ = 27 ≡ -1 (mod 28). Write 3¹⁰² = (3³)³⁴ ≡ (-1)³⁴ = 1 (mod 28). The remainder is 1. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

84. What is the unit digit of (144)⁴⁴ × (155)⁵⁵ × (166)⁶⁶?
(a) 0 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (a) 0
Solution: (144)⁴⁴ ends in 6 (even power of 4), (155)⁵⁵ ends in 5, and (166)⁶⁶ ends in 6. Multiplying unit digits: 6 × 5 × 6 = 180, which has unit digit 0. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

85. How many even factors does the number 72 have?
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 9
Solution: Trap: Avoid assuming half of all factors are even. Prime factorization: 72 = 2³ × 3². Total factors = (3 + 1)(2 + 1) = 12. Odd factors = (2 + 1) = 3. Even factors = 12 - 3 = 9. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

86. What is the remainder when 17¹⁷ is divided by 16?
(a) 16 (b) 0 (c) 1 (d) 15
Answer: (c) 1
Solution: Write 17 as (16 + 1). (16 + 1)¹⁷ ≡ 1¹⁷ = 1 (mod 16). The remainder is 1. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

87. What is the unit digit of (888)⁹²?
(a) 8 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 6
Answer: (d) 6
Solution: Base unit digit is 8. Cyclicity of 8 is 4. Exponent 92 is an exact multiple of 4 (remainder 0). The unit digit is that of 8⁴ = 4096, which is 6. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

88. What is the value of binary number (11001)₂ in decimal base 10?
(a) 25 (b) 27 (c) 29 (d) 31
Answer: (a) 25
Solution: Converting binary to decimal: 1×2⁴ + 1×2³ + 0×2² + 0×2¹ + 1×2⁰ = 16 + 8 + 0 + 0 + 1 = 25. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

89. What is the remainder when 28! is divided by 29?
(a) 1 (b) 28 (c) 0 (d) 27
Answer: (b) 28
Solution: By Wilson theorem, for any prime p, (p - 1)! ≡ (p - 1) (mod p). Here p = 29, so 28! ≡ 28 (mod 29). The remainder is 28. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

90. How many trailing zeroes are at the end of 120!?
(a) 24 (b) 26 (c) 28 (d) 30
Answer: (c) 28
Solution: Using Legendre formula for prime 5 in 120!: floor(120/5) + floor(120/25) = 24 + 4 = 28. Thus, 120! ends in 28 trailing zeroes. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

91. What is the unit digit of (7)⁷³ × (3)³⁷?
(a) 7 (b) 3 (c) 9 (d) 1
Answer: (d) 1
Solution: 73 mod 4 = 1, so 7⁷³ ends in 7. 37 mod 4 = 1, so 3³⁷ ends in 3. Multiplying unit digits: 7 × 3 = 21, which ends in 1. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

92. What is the remainder when (19³⁵ + 35³⁵) is divided by 27?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 26 (d) 19
Answer: (a) 0
Solution: For any odd power n, (aⁿ + bⁿ) is divisible by (a + b). Since 35 is odd, (19³⁵ + 35³⁵) is divisible by (19 + 35) = 54, which is a multiple of 27. The remainder is 0. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (b) 15

94. What is the remainder when 5²⁰⁰ is divided by 26?
(a) 5 (b) 25 (c) 1 (d) 0
Answer: (c) 1
Solution: Notice that 5² = 25 ≡ -1 (mod 26). Write 5²⁰⁰ = (5²)¹⁰⁰ ≡ (-1)¹⁰⁰ = 1 (mod 26). The remainder is 1. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

95. What is the unit digit of the expression (7)⁹⁹ - (3)⁹⁹?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 8 (d) 6
Answer: (d) 6
Solution: Trap: Remember to borrow from tens place when subtracting a larger digit. 99 mod 4 = 3: 7³ ends in 3, and 3³ ends in 7. For (..3) - (..7), borrow 10: 13 - 7 = 6. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

96. If the 6-digit number 34x51y is divisible by 88, what is the value of (x - y)?
(a) 5 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 2
Answer: (a) 5
Solution: Divisibility by 8 requires 51y to be divisible by 8, giving y = 2. Divisibility by 11 requires (2 + 5 + 4) - (1 + x + 3) = 11 - (4 + x) = 7 - x = 0, giving x = 7. Thus, x - y = 7 - 2 = 5. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

97. What is the product of all factors of the number 18?
(a) 18² (b) 18³ (c) 18⁴ (d) 18⁶
Answer: (b) 18³
Solution: Prime factorization: 18 = 2¹ × 3². Total factors T = (1 + 1)(2 + 1) = 6. Product of factors formula is N^(T/2) = 18^(6/2) = 18³. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

98. What is the remainder when 15²⁵ is divided by 16?
(a) 1 (b) 15 (c) 0 (d) 14
Answer: (b) 15
Solution: Since 15 ≡ -1 (mod 16), 15²⁵ ≡ (-1)²⁵ = -1 ≡ 16 - 1 = 15 (mod 16). The remainder is 15. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

99. What is the number of odd factors of the number 200?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 6
Answer: (c) 3
Solution: Prime factorization: 200 = 2³ × 5². Odd factors are formed by ignoring powers of 2: (2 + 1) = 3. Thus, 200 has 3 odd factors. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics
