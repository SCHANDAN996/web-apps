101. What is the remainder when 3¹⁹⁸⁹ is divided by 7?
(a) 6 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: Cyclicity of powers of 3 modulo 7 is 6: 3⁶ ≡ 1 (mod 7). 1989 mod 6 = 3. Therefore, 3¹⁹⁸⁹ ≡ 3³ = 27 ≡ 6 (mod 7). The remainder is 6. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

102. What is the unit digit of the expression (13)²⁵ + (17)²⁵?
(a) 8 (b) 0 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 0
Solution: Exponent 25 mod 4 = 1. 3¹ ends in 3, and 7¹ ends in 7. Summing unit digits: 3 + 7 = 10, which ends in 0. Hence, the correct option is (b).

103. What is the sum of all odd factors of the number 360?
(a) 52 (b) 65 (c) 78 (d) 91
Answer: (c) 78
Solution: Prime factorization: 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5¹. Odd factors are formed exclusively by odd prime bases: (1 + 3 + 9)(1 + 5) = 13 × 6 = 78. Hence, the correct option is (c).

104. How many trailing zeroes are at the end of 130!?
(a) 26 (b) 28 (c) 30 (d) 32
Answer: (d) 32
Solution: Applying Legendre formula for prime 5 in 130!: floor(130/5) + floor(130/25) + floor(130/125) = 26 + 5 + 1 = 32. Thus, 130! ends in 32 trailing zeroes. Hence, the correct option is (d).

105. What is the remainder when (10¹⁰ + 10¹⁰⁰ + 10¹⁰⁰⁰ + 10¹⁰⁰⁰⁰) is divided by 7?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 1
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Note that 10 ≡ 3 (mod 7). The powers of 3 mod 7 have period 6: 10ᵏ mod 6 = 4 for all k ≥ 1. Thus, 3⁴ = 81 ≡ 4 (mod 7). Sum of four terms: 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 16 ≡ 2 (mod 7). Trap: Candidates mistakenly sum the full numbers instead of computing the modular periodic pattern. The remainder is 2. Hence, the correct option is (a).

106. What is the unit digit of 2³³ × 3³³ × 4³³?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: Combine the bases: (2 × 3 × 4)³³ = (24)³³. The base unit digit is 4. The cyclicity of 4 is 2: 4 raised to an odd power always ends in 4. Since 33 is odd, the unit digit is 4. Hence, the correct option is (b).

107. What is the total number of factors of 2⁵ × 3⁴ × 5² that are divisible by 12?
(a) 36 (b) 40 (c) 48 (d) 60
Answer: (c) 48
Solution: Since 12 = 2² × 3¹, divide the number by 12: (2⁵ × 3⁴ × 5²) / (2² × 3¹) = 2³ × 3³ × 5². Factors that are multiples of 12 correspond to total factors of this quotient: (3 + 1)(3 + 1)(2 + 1) = 4 × 4 × 3 = 48. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

108. What is the remainder when 30! is divided by 31?
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) 29 (d) 30
Answer: (d) 30
Solution: 31 is a prime number. By Wilson theorem, (p - 1)! ≡ (p - 1) (mod p). Here p = 31, so 30! ≡ 30 (mod 31). The remainder is 30. Hence, the correct option is (d).

110. If the 9-digit number 83p93678q is divisible by 72, what is the value of (p - q) for the smallest value of q?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 1
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: Divisibility by 8 requires 78q to be divisible by 8: 78 mod 8 = 6, so 6q must be 64, giving q = 4. Divisibility by 9 requires sum 8 + 3 + p + 9 + 3 + 6 + 7 + 8 + 4 = 48 + p to be a multiple of 9, giving p = 6. Thus, p - q = 6 - 4 = 2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

112. What is the remainder when 7¹⁰⁰ is divided by 101?
(a) 7 (b) 100 (c) 0 (d) 1
Answer: (d) 1
Solution: 101 is a prime number and gcd(7, 101) = 1. By Fermat Little Theorem, a^(p - 1) ≡ 1 (mod p). Here p = 101, so 7¹⁰⁰ ≡ 1 (mod 101). The remainder is 1. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

113. How many factors of the number 10800 are perfect squares?
(a) 12 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 24
Answer: (a) 12
Solution: Prime factorization: 10800 = 2⁴ × 3³ × 5². Even exponents: for 2⁴ there are {0, 2, 4} (3 choices); for 3³ there are {0, 2} (2 choices); for 5² there are {0, 2} (2 choices). Total square factors = 3 × 2 × 2 = 12. Hence, the correct option is (a).

119. How many factors of 1200 are multiples of 10?
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 18
Answer: (c) 16
Solution: Divide 1200 by 10: 1200 / 10 = 120. Factors that are multiples of 10 equal total factors of 120: 120 = 2³ × 3¹ × 5¹. Total factors = (3 + 1)(1 + 1)(1 + 1) = 4 × 2 × 2 = 16. Hence, the correct option is (c).

120. What is the remainder when 14! is divided by 17?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (d) 8
Solution: By Wilson theorem, 16! ≡ -1 (mod 17). This gives 16 × 15 × 14! ≡ (-1) × (-2) × 14! = 2 × 14! ≡ -1 ≡ 16 (mod 17). Dividing by 2 yields 14! ≡ 8 (mod 17). The remainder is 8. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

121. What is the remainder when 32^(32^32) is divided by 7?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 6
Answer: (a) 4
Solution: Trap: Always evaluate the modular cycle before processing power towers. 32 ≡ 4 (mod 7). Powers of 4 mod 7 have cycle length 3: 4¹=4, 4²=2, 4³=1. Power 32³² mod 3: 32 ≡ -1 (mod 3) so (-1)³² = 1. Thus, 4¹ = 4. The remainder is 4. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

122. What is the remainder when 7⁸² is divided by 100?
(a) 43 (b) 49 (c) 1 (d) 7
Answer: (b) 49
Solution: By Euler Totient Theorem, phi(100) = 40. Therefore, 7⁴⁰ ≡ 1 (mod 100). Thus, 7⁸² = (7⁴⁰)² × 7² ≡ 1² × 49 = 49 (mod 100). The remainder is 49. Hence, the correct option is (b).

124. What is the remainder when (1! + 2! + 3! + ... + 10!) is divided by 15?

Answer: (c) 3
Solution: For any n ≥ 5, n! is a multiple of 15 (as it contains 3 and 5). Thus, the remainder depends only on 1! + 2! + 3! + 4! = 1 + 2 + 6 + 24 = 33. Dividing 33 by 15 leaves a remainder of 3. Hence, the correct option is (c).

125. What is the highest power of 6 that completely divides 50!?
(a) 25 (b) 24 (c) 23 (d) 22
Answer: (d) 22
Solution: Since 6 = 2 × 3, the power of 6 is limited by prime factor 3. Applying Legendre formula for prime 3: floor(50/3) + floor(50/9) + floor(50/27) = 16 + 5 + 1 = 22. Thus, the highest power of 6 dividing 50! is 22. Hence, the correct option is (d).
