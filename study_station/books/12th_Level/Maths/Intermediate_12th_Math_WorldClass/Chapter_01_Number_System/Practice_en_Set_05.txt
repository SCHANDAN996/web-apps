101. What is the unit digit of 82^224?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 1 (d) 9
Answer: (a)
Solution: The unit digits of powers of 2 repeat in a cycle of 4: 2, 4, 8, 6. 224 leaves remainder 4 in this cycle, so the unit digit is 6. So the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

102. How many zeros are there at the end of 30! (30 factorial)?
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: Count the factors of 5: 30/5 + 30/25 + 30/125 + … (whole parts) = 7. There are always more 2s, so the number of trailing zeros is 7. So the answer is 7.
Source: PYQ-style

104. The HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 270. If one number is 54, what is the other number?
(a) 36 (b) 30 (c) 45 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: HCF × LCM = product of the numbers, so the other number = 6 × 270 ÷ 54 = 30. So the answer is 30.

106. What is the smallest digit that can replace * so that 7*213 is divisible by 11?

Answer: (a)
Solution: A number is divisible by 11 when the difference between the sums of digits in odd and even places is 0 or a multiple of 11. Trying digits from 0 upwards, the smallest that works is 0. So the answer is 0.
