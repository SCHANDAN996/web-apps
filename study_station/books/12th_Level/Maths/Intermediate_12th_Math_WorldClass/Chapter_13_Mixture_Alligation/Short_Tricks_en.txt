# Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Mixture and Alligation

**Box 1 — "Cross and Subtract, Far goes Near"**
> Draw the cross; each quantity is the other price's distance from the mean.

**Box 2 — "Swap the distances"**
> Distances 40 and 60 → shares 60 and 40.

**Box 3 — "Closer means more"**
> The ingredient whose price is closer to the mean is used in larger quantity.

**Box 4 — "Equal only, average only"**
> Simple average of prices only when equal quantities are mixed.

**Box 5 — "Replacement multiplies"**
> Multiply by (1 − x/V) for each round — never subtract a fixed amount.

**Box 6 — "Volume × strength"**
> For solutions, multiply each volume by its percentage before averaging.

**Box 7 — "Split, add, simplify"**
> Adding water or milk: find each part, add to one, then simplify the ratio.

**Box 8 — "Alligation is everywhere"**
> Average speeds, marks of two classes, profit on two parts — same cross.

**Box 9 — "Check the reversed ratio"**
> The upside-down ratio is the examiner's favourite wrong option.

**Box 10 — "Mean must lie between"**
> If your mean is not between the two prices, you made an error.

## Skip Strategy

1. **15 seconds:** direct alligation and mean-price questions.
2. **45 seconds:** three-component mixtures — mark and return if slow.
3. **Use options:** a mixing ratio that does not reproduce the mean when checked is wrong.
