Questions 126–150: Hard

126. In what ratio must rice costing ₹74 per kg be mixed with rice costing ₹98 per kg so that the mixture costs ₹96 per kg?
(a) 11 : 1 (b) 1 : 11 (c) 1 : 12 (d) 74 : 98
Answer: (b)
Solution: By alligation: cheaper : dearer = (98 − 96) : (96 − 74) = 2 : 22 = 1 : 11. The trap is writing the ratio the other way, 11 : 1. So the answer is 1 : 11.
Source: PYQ-style
