126. (Hard) A container contains 90 litres of milk. 18 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water. Next, 18 litres of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water. What is the ratio of milk to water in the container now?
(A) 16 : 9  (B) 14 : 9  (C) 16 : 11  (D) 18 : 7
Answer: (A) 16 : 9
Solution: Fraction replaced each time = 18 / 90 = 1/5.
Fraction of milk remaining = (1 - 1/5)^2 = 16/25.
Remaining milk = 90 * (16/25) = 57.6 litres.
Remaining water = 90 - 57.6 = 32.4 litres.
Ratio of milk to water = 57.6 : 32.4 = 576 : 324 = 16 : 9. The required value is 16 : 9. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

127. (Hard) A vessel is full of a mixture of kerosene and petrol in the ratio 3 : 2. 10 litres of the mixture is drawn off and the vessel is filled with petrol. If the ratio of kerosene to petrol becomes 2 : 3, find the capacity of the vessel.
(A) 25 litres  (B) 30 litres  (C) 35 litres  (D) 40 litres
Answer: (B) 30 litres
Solution: Let the remaining volume after drawing 10 litres be 5x (so Kerosene = 3x, Petrol = 2x).
After adding 10 litres of petrol: Kerosene = 3x, Petrol = 2x + 10.
New ratio: 3x / (2x + 10) = 2 / 3 => 9x = 4x + 20 => 5x = 20 => x = 4.
Remaining volume = 5x = 20 litres.
Total capacity = Remaining volume + Withdrawn volume = 20 + 10 = 30 litres. The required value is 30 litres. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

128. (Hard) Two alloys of copper and tin are made: Alloy A contains copper and tin in the ratio 9 : 1, while Alloy B contains them in the ratio 7 : 3. In what ratio must Alloy A and Alloy B be melted together to produce a new alloy having copper and tin in the ratio 4 : 1?
(A) 1 : 2  (B) 2 : 1  (C) 1 : 1  (D) 3 : 2
Answer: (C) 1 : 1
Solution: Fraction of copper in Alloy A = 9/10.
Fraction of copper in Alloy B = 7/10.
Desired fraction of copper = 4/5 = 8/10.
By rule of alligation:
Ratio (A : B) = (8/10 - 7/10) : (9/10 - 8/10) = 1/10 : 1/10 = 1 : 1. The required value is 1 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

129. (Hard) A milkman mixes 15 litres of water with 60 litres of milk. He sells the mixture at ₹55 per litre. If the cost of pure milk was ₹50 per litre (water is free), what is his profit percentage?
(A) 32.5%  (B) 35.0%  (C) 40.0%  (D) 37.5%
Answer: (D) 37.5%
Solution: Cost price = 60 * 50 = ₹3,000.
Total volume of mixture = 60 + 15 = 75 litres.
Selling price = 75 * 55 = ₹4,125.
Profit = 4,125 - 3,000 = ₹1,125.
Profit percentage = (1125 / 3000) * 100 = 37.5%. The required value is 37.5%. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

130. (Hard) A trader has 50 kg of pulses, part of which he sells at 8% profit and the rest at 18% profit. He gains 14% on the whole. What is the quantity sold at 18% profit?
(A) 30 kg  (B) 20 kg  (C) 25 kg  (D) 35 kg
Answer: (A) 30 kg
Solution: By rule of alligation:
Part at 8% and Part at 18%, Mean profit = 14%.
Ratio (8% : 18%) = (18 - 14) : (14 - 8) = 4 : 6 = 2 : 3.
Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5 parts = 50 kg.
Quantity sold at 18% profit = (3/5) * 50 = 30 kg. The required value is 30 kg. Hence, the correct option is (A).

135. (Hard) A mixture of 80 litres of milk and water contains 25% water. How many litres of water should be added to make water 40% of the new mixture?
(A) 15 litres  (B) 20 litres  (C) 18 litres  (D) 25 litres
Answer: (B) 20 litres
Solution: Quantity of milk in original mixture = 75% of 80 = 60 litres.
Since pure water is added, the quantity of milk remains 60 litres.
In the new mixture, water is 40%, so milk is 60%.
Total volume of new mixture = 60 / 0.60 = 100 litres.
Water added = 100 - 80 = 20 litres. The required value is 20 litres. Hence, the correct option is (B).

136. (Hard) A man covered 180 km in 6 hours, partly by car at 40 km/h and partly by bus at 25 km/h. Find the distance travelled by car.
(A) 70 km  (B) 90 km  (C) 80 km  (D) 100 km
Answer: (C) 80 km
Solution: Average speed = 180 km / 6 h = 30 km/h.
By rule of alligation on speeds (gives time ratio):
Car (40 km/h) and Bus (25 km/h), Mean = 30 km/h.
Time ratio (Car : Bus) = (30 - 25) : (40 - 30) = 5 : 10 = 1 : 2.
Total time = 6 hours => Time travelled by car = (1/3) * 6 = 2 hours.
Distance travelled by car = 2 h * 40 km/h = 80 km. The required value is 80 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

137. (Hard) Three equal vessels are filled with mixtures of milk and water in the ratios 1 : 2, 2 : 3, and 3 : 7. If all three mixtures are combined, what is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(A) 29 : 61  (B) 33 : 57  (C) 35 : 55  (D) 31 : 59
Answer: (D) 31 : 59
Solution: Total parts in each vessel: 3, 5, and 10. LCM(3, 5, 10) = 30 units capacity each.
Vessel 1 (30 units): Milk = (1/3) * 30 = 10, Water = 20.
Vessel 2 (30 units): Milk = (2/5) * 30 = 12, Water = 18.
Vessel 3 (30 units): Milk = (3/10) * 30 = 9, Water = 21.
Total milk = 10 + 12 + 9 = 31 units.
Total water = 20 + 18 + 21 = 59 units.
Ratio of milk to water = 31 : 59. The required value is 31 : 59. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (C) 45

Number of two-wheelers = (9/16) * 80 = 45. The required value is 45. Hence, the correct option is (C).

141. (Hard) A can contains a mixture of two liquids X and Y in the ratio 5 : 3. When 16 litres of the mixture is drawn off and replaced with liquid Y, the ratio of X and Y becomes 3 : 5. What was the total capacity of the can?
(A) 32 litres  (B) 36 litres  (C) 45 litres  (D) 40 litres
Answer: (D) 40 litres
Solution: After withdrawing 16 litres of mixture, ratio of X : Y remains 5 : 3.
Let remaining volumes be 5k and 3k (total remaining = 8k).
Adding 16 litres of Y: X = 5k, Y = 3k + 16.
New ratio: 5k / (3k + 16) = 3 / 5 => 25k = 9k + 48 => 16k = 48 => k = 3.
Remaining volume = 8k = 24 litres.
Total capacity = Remaining volume + Withdrawn volume = 24 + 16 = 40 litres. The required value is 40 litres. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

147. (Hard) A sum of ₹18,000 is divided into two parts such that the simple interest on the first part at 6% per annum for 4 years is equal to the simple interest on the second part at 8% per annum for 3 years. Find the value of the first part.
(A) ₹8,000  (B) ₹9,000  (C) ₹10,000  (D) ₹12,000
Answer: (B) ₹9,000
Solution: Let the two parts be P1 and P2.
Given: P1 * 6 * 4 / 100 = P2 * 8 * 3 / 100 => 24 P1 = 24 P2 => P1 = P2.
Since P1 + P2 = ₹18,000 and P1 = P2, each part is ₹18,000 / 2 = ₹9,000. The required value is ₹9,000. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Solution: By rule of alligation on scores:
Girls (75) and Boys (65), Overall mean = 70.

Total candidates = 120 => Number of girls = 120 / 2 = 60. The required value is 60. Hence, the correct option is (C).
