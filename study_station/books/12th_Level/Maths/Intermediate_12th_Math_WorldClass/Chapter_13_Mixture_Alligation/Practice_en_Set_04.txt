Questions 76–100: Medium

76. (Medium) A container holds 80 litres of milk. 8 litres is taken out and replaced with water. Next, 10 litres of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water. How many litres of milk is left in the container?
(A) 63 litres  (B) 60 litres  (C) 64.8 litres  (D) 66.5 litres
Answer: (A) 63 litres
Solution: After first replacement, remaining milk = 80 * (1 - 8/80) = 80 * (9/10) = 72 litres.
After second replacement, remaining milk = 72 * (1 - 10/80) = 72 * (7/8) = 63 litres. The required value is 63 litres. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

77. (Medium) Three containers of capacities in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3 contain milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1, 3 : 2, and 4 : 1 respectively. If all three containers are emptied into a large vessel, find the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture.
(A) 31 : 14  (B) 32 : 13  (C) 33 : 12  (D) 35 : 11
Answer: (B) 32 : 13
Solution: Total parts in each container: 3, 5, and 5. LCM(3, 5) = 15.
Let container capacities be 15, 30, and 45 units (ratio 1 : 2 : 3).
Container 1 (15 units): Milk = (2/3) * 15 = 10, Water = 5.
Container 2 (30 units): Milk = (3/5) * 30 = 18, Water = 12.
Container 3 (45 units): Milk = (4/5) * 45 = 36, Water = 9.
Total milk = 10 + 18 + 36 = 64 units.
Total water = 5 + 12 + 9 = 26 units.
Ratio of milk to water = 64 : 26 = 32 : 13. The required value is 32 : 13. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

78. (Medium) A dishonest grocer professes to sell sugar at its cost price, but he uses a false weight of 900 grams instead of a kilogram. What is his percentage gain?
(A) 10%  (B) 12.5%  (C) 11.11%  (D) 15%
Answer: (C) 11.11%
Solution: Let the cost price of 1 gram of sugar be ₹1.
Cost Price of 900 grams given to customer = ₹900.
Selling Price charged (for 1000 grams) = ₹1,000.
Profit = ₹1,000 - ₹900 = ₹100.
Profit percentage = (100 / 900) * 100 = 11.11%. The required value is 11.11%. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

79. (Medium) In an examination, the average score of 40 students was 65. The average score of girls was 70 and that of boys was 62. Find the number of girls in the class.
(A) 12  (B) 20  (C) 25  (D) 15
Answer: (D) 15
Solution: By rule of alligation on scores:
Girls (70) and Boys (62), Class average = 65.
Ratio (Girls : Boys) = (65 - 62) : (70 - 65) = 3 : 5.
Total parts = 3 + 5 = 8 parts = 40 students.
Number of girls = (3/8) * 40 = 15. The required value is 15. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

80. (Medium) A merchant blends two varieties of tea costing ₹140 per kg and ₹180 per kg. If he sells the blend at ₹192 per kg and gains 20%, what is the ratio in which the two varieties were blended?
(A) 1 : 1  (B) 1 : 2  (C) 2 : 1  (D) 3 : 2

Solution: Selling price of blend = ₹192 per kg, Profit = 20%.
Cost price of blend = 192 / 1.20 = ₹160 per kg.
By rule of alligation:
Variety 1 (₹140) and Variety 2 (₹180), Mean = ₹160.
Ratio = (180 - 160) : (160 - 140) = 20 : 20 = 1 : 1. The required value is 1 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (A).

81. (Medium) A sum of ₹8,000 was invested in two parts. One part at 8% per annum and the other at 12% per annum simple interest. The total annual interest received was ₹800. What was the amount invested at 8%?
(A) ₹3,500  (B) ₹4,000  (C) ₹4,500  (D) ₹5,000
Answer: (B) ₹4,000
Solution: Overall interest rate = (800 / 8000) * 100 = 10% per annum.
By rule of alligation:
Part 1 (8%) and Part 2 (12%), Mean = 10%.
Ratio = (12 - 10) : (10 - 8) = 2 : 2 = 1 : 1.
Amount invested at 8% = ₹8,000 / 2 = ₹4,000. The required value is ₹4,000. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

82. (Medium) A vessel contains 60 litres of milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio of milk to water 1 : 2?
(A) 40 litres  (B) 50 litres  (C) 60 litres  (D) 70 litres
Answer: (C) 60 litres
Solution: Initial milk = 60 * (2/3) = 40 litres, and water = 20 litres.
In the new mixture, ratio of milk to water is 1 : 2.
Since milk remains 40 litres, 1 part = 40 litres => Water = 2 * 40 = 80 litres.
Water to be added = 80 - 20 = 60 litres. The required value is 60 litres. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

83. (Medium) A person drives 200 km in 4 hours. He drives part of the journey at 40 km/h and the rest at 60 km/h. How many hours did he drive at 60 km/h?
(A) 1.5 hours  (B) 2.5 hours  (C) 3 hours  (D) 2 hours
Answer: (D) 2 hours
Solution: Average speed = 200 km / 4 h = 50 km/h.
By rule of alligation on speeds (gives time ratio):
Speed 1 (40 km/h) and Speed 2 (60 km/h), Mean = 50 km/h.
Time ratio = (60 - 50) : (50 - 40) = 10 : 10 = 1 : 1.
Total time = 4 hours => Time driven at 60 km/h = 4 / 2 = 2 hours. The required value is 2 hours. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

84. (Medium) A 40-litre mixture of milk and water contains 10% water. How many litres of pure milk must be added to make the water percentage 5%?
(A) 40 litres  (B) 30 litres  (C) 35 litres  (D) 45 litres
Answer: (A) 40 litres
Solution: Initial water volume = 40 * 0.10 = 4 litres.
Since pure milk is added, the volume of water remains 4 litres.
In the new mixture, water is 5% = 1/20 of the total volume.
New total volume = 4 / 0.05 = 80 litres.
Pure milk added = 80 - 40 = 40 litres. The required value is 40 litres. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

85. (Medium) Two alloys of gold and silver contain gold in the ratios 7 : 2 and 7 : 11 respectively. If equal quantities of these two alloys are melted together, what will be the ratio of gold to silver in the resulting alloy?
(A) 5 : 3  (B) 7 : 5  (C) 6 : 5  (D) 8 : 5
Answer: (B) 7 : 5
Solution: Total parts: Alloy 1 has 7 + 2 = 9 parts; Alloy 2 has 7 + 11 = 18 parts.
LCM(9, 18) = 18 units.
Take 18 kg of each alloy:
Alloy 1 (18 kg): Gold = (7/9) * 18 = 14 kg, Silver = 4 kg.
Alloy 2 (18 kg): Gold = (7/18) * 18 = 7 kg, Silver = 11 kg.
Total gold = 14 + 7 = 21 kg.
Total silver = 4 + 11 = 15 kg.
Ratio of gold to silver = 21 : 15 = 7 : 5. The required value is 7 : 5. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

88. (Medium) A vessel contains 96 litres of pure milk. 24 litres of milk is removed and replaced with water. This process is repeated one more time. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(A) 9 : 7  (B) 7 : 5  (C) 11 : 7  (D) 13 : 9
Answer: (A) 9 : 7
Solution: Fraction replaced each time = 24 / 96 = 1/4.
Remaining milk = 96 * (1 - 1/4)^2 = 96 * 9/16 = 54 litres.
Water in final mixture = 96 - 54 = 42 litres.
Ratio of milk to water = 54 : 42 = 9 : 7. The required value is 9 : 7. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

90. (Medium) A person had ₹500 in total consisting of 50-paise and 25-paise coins. If there are 1,400 coins in all, how many 50-paise coins did he have?
(A) 500  (B) 550  (C) 600  (D) 650
Answer: (C) 600
Solution: Total value = 500 * 100 = 50,000 paise.
Average value per coin = 50,000 / 1400 = 250 / 7 paise.
By rule of alligation:
50 paise (= 350/7 paise) and 25 paise (= 175/7 paise), Mean = 250/7 paise.
Ratio (50p : 25p) = (250/7 - 175/7) : (350/7 - 250/7) = 75 : 100 = 3 : 4.
Total parts = 3 + 4 = 7 parts = 1,400 coins.
Number of 50-paise coins = (3/7) * 1400 = 600. The required value is 600. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

96. (Medium) A container is filled with 100 litres of wine. 10 litres is removed and replaced with water. This operation is repeated 2 more times (total 3 operations). Find the quantity of wine remaining in the container.
(A) 72.9 litres  (B) 70.5 litres  (C) 72.0 litres  (D) 74.2 litres
Answer: (A) 72.9 litres
Solution: Using replacement formula: Remaining wine = Total * (1 - y/x)^n.
Here x = 100, y = 10, and n = 3.
Remaining wine = 100 * (1 - 10/100)^3 = 100 * (9/10)^3 = 100 * (729/1000) = 72.9 litres. The required value is 72.9 litres. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Cost price per kg = 4200 / 100 = ₹42 per kg.
Selling price per kg for 25% profit = 42 * 1.25 = ₹52.50 per kg. The required value is ₹52.50 per kg. Hence, the correct option is (B).

98. (Medium) A drum contains 80 litres of an ethanol-water mixture containing 75% ethanol. How many litres of water must be added to reduce the ethanol concentration to 60%?
(A) 15 litres  (B) 18 litres  (C) 20 litres  (D) 25 litres
Answer: (C) 20 litres
Solution: Volume of ethanol = 80 * 0.75 = 60 litres.
Since water is added, the volume of ethanol remains 60 litres.
In the new mixture, 60 litres represents 60% of the total volume.
New total volume = 60 / 0.60 = 100 litres.
Water added = 100 - 80 = 20 litres. The required value is 20 litres. Hence, the correct option is (C).

100. (Medium) A 60-litre solution of acid and water contains 80% acid. How many litres of water must be added so that the water content becomes 40%?
(A) 15 litres  (B) 20 litres  (C) 25 litres  (D) 30 litres
Answer: (B) 20 litres
Solution: Quantity of acid = 80% of 60 = 48 litres.
In the new solution, water is 40%, which means acid is 60%.
Total volume of new solution = 48 / 0.60 = 80 litres.
Water added = 80 - 60 = 20 litres. The required value is 20 litres. Hence, the correct option is (B).
