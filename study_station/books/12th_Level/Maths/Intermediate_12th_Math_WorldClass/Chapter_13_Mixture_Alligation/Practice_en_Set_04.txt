Questions 76–100: Medium

76. In what ratio must rice costing ₹48 per kg be mixed with rice costing ₹58 per kg so that the mixture costs ₹50 per kg?
(a) 48 : 58 (b) 5 : 1 (c) 4 : 1 (d) 4 : 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: By alligation: cheaper : dearer = (58 − 50) : (50 − 48) = 8 : 2 = 4 : 1. The trap is writing the ratio the other way, 1 : 4. So the answer is 4 : 1.
Source: PYQ-style

77. 40 litres of a 10% sugar solution is mixed with 40 litres of a 20% sugar solution. What is the sugar concentration of the mixture?
(a) 30% (b) 15% (c) 17% (d) 12%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sugar = 40 × 10% + 40 × 20% = 12 litres in 80 litres, so the concentration is 15%. The trap is the simple average 15%. So the answer is 15%.

79. A vessel contains 80 litres of milk. 10 litres are taken out and replaced with water. This is done 2 times in all. How much milk is left in the vessel?

Answer: (d)
