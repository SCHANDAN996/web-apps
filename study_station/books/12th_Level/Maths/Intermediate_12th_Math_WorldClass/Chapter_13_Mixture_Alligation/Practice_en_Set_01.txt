Questions 1–25: Easy

1. (Easy) In what ratio must a grocer mix two varieties of tea costing Rs. 60 per kg and Rs. 75 per kg to get a mixture worth Rs. 65 per kg?
(A) 2 : 1  (B) 1 : 2  (C) 3 : 1  (D) 3 : 2
Answer: (A) 2 : 1
Solution: By Rule of Alligation: (75 - 65) / (65 - 60) = 10 / 5 = 2 : 1. The required value is 2 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. (Easy) In what ratio must rice at Rs. 9.30 per kg be mixed with rice at Rs. 10.80 per kg so that the mixture be worth Rs. 10.00 per kg?
(A) 7 : 8  (B) 8 : 7  (C) 9 : 7  (D) 6 : 5
Answer: (B) 8 : 7
Solution: By Rule of Alligation: (10.80 - 10.00) / (10.00 - 9.30) = 0.80 / 0.70 = 8 : 7. The required value is 8 : 7. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

3. (Easy) In what ratio must water be mixed with milk costing Rs. 32 per liter to get a mixture worth Rs. 28 per liter? (Assume water is free of cost)
(A) 1 : 8  (B) 2 : 7  (C) 1 : 7  (D) 1 : 6
Answer: (C) 1 : 7
Solution: Cost of water = Rs. 0, Milk = Rs. 32, Mean = Rs. 28. Ratio of Water : Milk = (32 - 28) / (28 - 0) = 4 / 28 = 1 : 7. The required value is 1 : 7. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

4. (Easy) A mixture of 40 liters of milk and water contains 10% water. How much water should be added to it to make 20% water in the new mixture?
(A) 4 liters  (B) 6 liters  (C) 7.5 liters  (D) 5 liters
Answer: (D) 5 liters
Solution: Water initially = 10% of 40 = 4 L. Milk = 36 L. In new mixture, milk is 80%: 0.80 * Total = 36 => Total = 45 L. Water to add = 45 - 40 = 5 liters. The required value is 5 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

5. (Easy) A vessel contains 60 liters of a mixture of alcohol and water in the ratio 2 : 1. How much water should be added to make the ratio 1 : 2?
(A) 60 liters  (B) 50 liters  (C) 40 liters  (D) 70 liters
Answer: (A) 60 liters
Solution: Alcohol = (2/3) * 60 = 40 L, Water = 20 L. For ratio 1 : 2, Water needed = 2 * Alcohol = 2 * 40 = 80 L. Water to be added = 80 - 20 = 60 liters. The required value is 60 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

6. (Easy) In what ratio must a 20% alcohol solution be mixed with a 50% alcohol solution to get a 30% alcohol solution?
(A) 1 : 2  (B) 2 : 1  (C) 3 : 1  (D) 3 : 2
Answer: (B) 2 : 1
Solution: By Alligation: (50 - 30) / (30 - 20) = 20 / 10 = 2 : 1. The required value is 2 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

7. (Easy) How many kg of sugar costing Rs. 9 per kg must be mixed with 28 kg of sugar costing Rs. 7 per kg so that there may be a gain of 10% by selling the mixture at Rs. 9.24 per kg?
(A) 10 kg  (B) 15 kg  (C) 12 kg  (D) 14 kg
Answer: (C) 12 kg
Solution: Mean CP = 9.24 / 1.10 = Rs. 8.40. Ratio of Rs. 9 to Rs. 7 = (8.40 - 7) / (9 - 8.40) = 1.40 / 0.60 = 7 : 3. So Quantity at Rs. 7 : Quantity at Rs. 9 = 7 : 3. Since Quantity at Rs. 7 is 28 kg (7 parts = 28 kg => 1 part = 4 kg), Quantity at Rs. 9 = 3 * 4 = 12 kg. The required value is 12 kg. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

8. (Easy) A container contains 50 liters of milk. From this, 5 liters of milk is taken out and replaced with water. How much milk remains in the container?
(A) 40 liters  (B) 42 liters  (C) 44 liters  (D) 45 liters
Answer: (D) 45 liters
Solution: Remaining milk = 50 * (1 - 5/50) = 50 * (9/10) = 45 liters. The required value is 45 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

10. (Easy) A merchant has 100 kg of sugar, part of which he sells at 7% profit and the rest at 17% profit. He gains 10% on the whole. How much is sold at 7% profit?
(A) 60 kg  (B) 70 kg  (C) 75 kg  (D) 80 kg
Answer: (B) 70 kg
Solution: Ratio of quantities (7% : 17%) = (17 - 10) / (10 - 7) = 7 : 3. Total parts = 10. Quantity at 7% = (7/10) * 100 = 70 kg. The required value is 70 kg. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

11. (Easy) A merchant has 100 kg of sugar, part of which he sells at 7% profit and the rest at 17% profit, gaining 10% overall. How much sugar is sold at 17% profit?
(A) 40 kg  (B) 25 kg  (C) 30 kg  (D) 35 kg
Answer: (C) 30 kg
Solution: Ratio of quantities (7% : 17%) = (17 - 10) / (10 - 7) = 7 : 3. Quantity at 17% = (3/10) * 100 = 30 kg. The required value is 30 kg. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

12. (Easy) A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 1. On adding 5 liters of water, the ratio of milk to water becomes 5 : 2. What is the quantity of milk in the mixture?
(A) 20 liters  (B) 30 liters  (C) 35 liters  (D) 25 liters
Answer: (D) 25 liters
Solution: Milk is 5x, Water is x. Adding 5 L water: 5x / (x + 5) = 5 / 2 => 2(5x) = 5(x + 5) => 5x = 25 => x = 5. Milk = 5x = 25 liters. The required value is 25 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

13. (Easy) A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 1. On adding 5 liters of water, the ratio of milk to water becomes 5 : 2. What was the initial total quantity of the mixture in liters?
(A) 30 liters  (B) 25 liters  (C) 35 liters  (D) 40 liters
Answer: (A) 30 liters
Solution: From the equation, x = 5. Initial total mixture = 5x + x = 6x = 6 * 5 = 30 liters. The required value is 30 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

14. (Easy) How much water must be added to 60 liters of milk at 1.5 liters for Rs. 20 so as to have a mixture worth Rs. 10 per liter?
(A) 15 liters  (B) 20 liters  (C) 25 liters  (D) 30 liters
Answer: (B) 20 liters
Solution: Cost of milk per liter = 20 / 1.5 = 40/3. Water = 0, Mean = 10. Ratio of Water to Milk = (40/3 - 10) / (10 - 0) = (10/3) / 10 = 1 : 3. For 60 L of milk, water needed = 60 / 3 = 20 liters. The required value is 20 liters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

15. (Easy) Two varieties of milk with different prices are mixed in the ratio 2 : 3. The price of the first variety is Rs. 10 per liter and that of the second variety is Rs. 15 per liter. Find the average price of the mixture per liter.
(A) Rs. 12  (B) Rs. 14  (C) Rs. 13  (D) Rs. 12.5
Answer: (C) Rs. 13
Solution: Average Price = (2 * 10 + 3 * 15) / (2 + 3) = (20 + 45) / 5 = 65 / 5 = Rs. 13 per liter. The required value is Rs. 13. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

16. (Easy) A trader mixes 26 kg of rice at Rs. 20 per kg with 30 kg of rice of other variety at Rs. 36 per kg and sells the mixture at Rs. 30 per kg. His profit percent is:
(A) 6%  (B) 4%  (C) 8%  (D) 5%
Answer: (D) 5%
Solution: Total Cost Price = 26 * 20 + 30 * 36 = 520 + 1080 = Rs. 1600. Total Selling Price = (26 + 30) * 30 = 56 * 30 = Rs. 1680. Profit = 1680 - 1600 = Rs. 80. Profit % = (80 / 1600) * 100 = 5%. The required value is 5%. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

17. (Easy) In what ratio must a solution of 70% concentration be mixed with a 40% concentration solution to obtain a 50% concentration solution?
(A) 1 : 2  (B) 2 : 1  (C) 1 : 3  (D) 2 : 3
Answer: (A) 1 : 2
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio (70% : 40%) = (50 - 40) / (70 - 50) = 10 / 20 = 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

18. (Easy) A 50-liter solution of water and salt contains 20% salt. How much water must be evaporated to make the salt content 25%?
(A) 8 liters  (B) 10 liters  (C) 12 liters  (D) 15 liters
Answer: (B) 10 liters
Solution: Salt quantity = 20% of 50 = 10 L. In new solution, 10 L represents 25%: Total Volume = 10 / 0.25 = 40 L. Water evaporated = 50 - 40 = 10 liters. The required value is 10 liters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

19. (Easy) Gold is 19 times as heavy as water and copper is 9 times as heavy as water. In what ratio should these metals be mixed so that the mixture may be 15 times as heavy as water?

Answer: (C) 3 : 2
Solution: Ratio of Gold to Copper = (15 - 9) / (19 - 15) = 6 / 4 = 3 : 2. The required value is 3 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

20. (Easy) A jar contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 4 : 1. When 10 liters of the mixture is taken out and 10 liters of liquid B is poured in, the ratio becomes 2 : 3. What was the quantity of liquid A initially contained in the jar?
(A) 20 liters  (B) 12 liters  (C) 18 liters  (D) 16 liters
Answer: (D) 16 liters
Solution: Let initial A = 4x, B = x. In 10 L drawn, A removed = 8 L, B removed = 2 L. New ratio: (4x - 8) / (x - 2 + 10) = 2/3 => (4x - 8)/(x + 8) = 2/3 => 12x - 24 = 2x + 16 => 10x = 40 => x = 4. Initial A = 4 * 4 = 16 liters. The required value is 16 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (A) 3 : 1

22. (Easy) A mixture of 150 liters of wine and water contains 20% water. How many liters of water must be added so that water may be 25% of the new mixture?
(A) 12 liters  (B) 10 liters  (C) 8 liters  (D) 15 liters
Answer: (B) 10 liters
Solution: Wine = 80% of 150 = 120 L. In new mixture, wine is 75%: 0.75 * Total = 120 => Total = 160 L. Water to add = 160 - 150 = 10 liters. The required value is 10 liters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

23. (Easy) A dishonest milkman professes to sell his milk at cost price but he mixes it with water and thereby gains 25%. The percentage of water in the mixture is:
(A) 25%  (B) 16.67%  (C) 20%  (D) 15%
Answer: (C) 20%
Solution: Water : Milk = 25 : 100 = 1 : 4. Total mixture = 1 + 4 = 5 parts. Percentage of water = (1 / 5) * 100 = 20%. The required value is 20%. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

24. (Easy) A dishonest milkman sells milk at cost price but mixes water and gains 20%. What is the ratio of water to milk?
(A) 5 : 1  (B) 1 : 4  (C) 1 : 6  (D) 1 : 5
Answer: (D) 1 : 5
Solution: Ratio of Water to Milk = Profit % / 100 = 20 / 100 = 1 : 5. The required value is 1 : 5. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

25. (Easy) 300 grams of sugar solution has 40% sugar in it. How much sugar should be added to make it 50% in the solution?
(A) 50 grams  (B) 60 grams  (C) 40 grams  (D) 80 grams
Answer: (B) 60 grams
Solution: Water is constant: 60% of 300 = 180 g. In new solution, water is 50%: Total = 180 / 0.50 = 360 g. Sugar added = 360 - 300 = 60 grams. The required value is 60 grams. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
