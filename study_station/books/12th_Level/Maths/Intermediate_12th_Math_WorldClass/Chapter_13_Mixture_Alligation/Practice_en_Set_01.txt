1. In what ratio must rice costing ₹78 per kg be mixed with rice costing ₹92 per kg so that the mixture costs ₹84 per kg?
(a) 78 : 92 (b) 4 : 3 (c) 4 : 4 (d) 5 : 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: By alligation: cheaper : dearer = (92 − 84) : (84 − 78) = 8 : 6 = 4 : 3. The trap is writing the ratio the other way, 3 : 4. So the answer is 4 : 3.
Source: PYQ-style

2. A vessel contains 40 litres of milk. 10 litres are taken out and replaced with water. This is done 2 times in all. How much milk is left in the vessel?
(a) 22.50 litres (b) 30 litres (c) 20 litres (d) 20.50 litres
Answer: (a)
Solution: Milk left = 40 × (1 − 10/40)^2 = 22.50 litres. The trap is subtracting 10 litres 2 times, which gives 20 litres. So the answer is 22.50 litres.
Source: PYQ-style

3. 10 litres of a 20% sugar solution is mixed with 40 litres of a 25% sugar solution. What is the sugar concentration of the mixture?
(a) 22.50% (b) 26% (c) 45% (d) 24%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sugar = 10 × 20% + 40 × 25% = 12 litres in 50 litres, so the concentration is 24%. The trap is the simple average 22.50%. So the answer is 24%.
