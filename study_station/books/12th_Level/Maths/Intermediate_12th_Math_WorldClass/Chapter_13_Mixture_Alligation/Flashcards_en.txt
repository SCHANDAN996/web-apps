Q: What is the Rule of Alligation ratio formula?
A: Quantity of Cheaper / Quantity of Dearer = (Price of Dearer - Mean Price) / (Mean Price - Price of Cheaper).

Q: Under what condition is the Rule of Alligation valid?
A: The mean price must lie strictly between the prices of the cheaper and dearer components: c < m < d.

Q: What is the formula for the quantity of pure liquid remaining after n successive replacement operations?
A: Remaining Quantity = x * (1 - y/x)^n, where x is the initial volume and y is the volume drawn and replaced each time.

Q: If a milkman adds water free of cost and sells the milk at cost price gaining 25% profit, what is the ratio of water to milk?
A: Water : Milk = 25 : 100 = 1 : 4.

Q: Two vessels contain milk and water in ratios 3:1 and 5:3. If equal quantities are mixed, what is the fraction of milk in the mixture?
A: (3/4 + 5/8) / 2 = (6/8 + 5/8) / 2 = 11/16.

Q: How can alligation be used to find the average speed over two legs of a journey?
A: The alligation rule gives the ratio of the time spent traveling at each speed, not the distance.

Q: When alligation is applied to profit percentages on two parts of goods, what does the resulting ratio represent?
A: It represents the ratio of the cost prices of the two parts.

Q: When alligation is applied to simple interest rates on two parts of an investment, what does the resulting ratio represent?
A: It represents the ratio of the principal amounts invested in each part.

Q: In what ratio must rice at Rs. 30/kg be mixed with rice at Rs. 40/kg to get a mixture worth Rs. 33/kg?
A: (40 - 33) / (33 - 30) = 7 : 3.

Q: A container has 40 liters of milk. 4 liters are removed and replaced with water. This process is done once. How much milk remains?
A: 40 * (1 - 4/40)^1 = 40 * 0.9 = 36 liters.

Q: If a mixture of alcohol and water is 80% alcohol, what is the ratio of alcohol to water?
A: Alcohol : Water = 80 : 20 = 4 : 1.

Q: If two qualities of tea costing Rs. 180/kg and Rs. 260/kg are mixed in the ratio 5 : 3, what is the cost of the mixture?
A: (5 * 180 + 3 * 260) / (5 + 3) = (900 + 780) / 8 = 1680 / 8 = Rs. 210/kg.
