Questions 101–125: Hard

101. (Hard) A container contains 80 litres of pure milk. From this, 20 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated 3 times in total. What is the ratio of milk to water in the container after the third operation?
(A) 27 : 37  (B) 25 : 39  (C) 29 : 35  (D) 31 : 33
Answer: (A) 27 : 37
Solution: Fraction of milk left after each operation = 1 - 20/80 = 3/4.
After 3 operations, quantity of milk left = 80 * (3/4)^3 = 80 * (27/64) = 33.75 litres.
Quantity of water = 80 - 33.75 = 46.25 litres.
Ratio of milk to water = 33.75 : 46.25 = 135 : 185 = 27 : 37. The required value is 27 : 37. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

102. (Hard) A vessel is full of milk. 15 litres of milk is drawn out and replaced with water. Again 15 litres of the mixture is drawn out and replaced with water. The ratio of milk to water now becomes 9 : 16. What was the capacity of the vessel?
(A) 35 litres  (B) 37.5 litres  (C) 40 litres  (D) 45 litres
Answer: (B) 37.5 litres
Solution: Ratio of remaining milk to total mixture = 9 / (9 + 16) = 9 / 25.
Using the formula: Remaining Milk / Total = (1 - 15/x)^2 = 9/25.
Taking square root on both sides: 1 - 15/x = 3/5 => 15/x = 2/5 => x = 37.5 litres. The required value is 37.5 litres. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

103. (Hard) A cask is filled with wine. 8 litres of wine is drawn and replaced with water. This operation is performed three more times (total 4 operations). The ratio of wine to water left in the cask is 16 : 65. What was the original volume of wine in the cask?
(A) 20 litres  (B) 28 litres  (C) 24 litres  (D) 32 litres
Answer: (C) 24 litres
Solution: Ratio of remaining wine to total volume = 16 / (16 + 65) = 16 / 81.
Formula: (1 - 8/x)^4 = 16 / 81 = (2/3)^4.
1 - 8/x = 2/3 => 8/x = 1/3 => x = 24 litres. The required value is 24 litres. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

104. (Hard) Three equal containers are filled with mixtures of milk and water. The ratios of milk to water are 3 : 2, 4 : 1, and 7 : 3. All three containers are emptied into a fourth container. What is the ratio of milk to water in the fourth container?
(A) 2 : 1  (B) 5 : 2  (C) 8 : 3  (D) 7 : 3
Answer: (D) 7 : 3
Solution: Total parts in the three containers are 5, 5, and 10. LCM = 10 units capacity each.
Container 1 (10 units): Milk = (3/5) * 10 = 6, Water = 4.
Container 2 (10 units): Milk = (4/5) * 10 = 8, Water = 2.
Container 3 (10 units): Milk = (7/10) * 10 = 7, Water = 3.
Total milk = 6 + 8 + 7 = 21 units.
Total water = 4 + 2 + 3 = 9 units.
Ratio of milk to water = 21 : 9 = 7 : 3. The required value is 7 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

105. (Hard) A merchant has 120 kg of tea. He sells a part of it at 15% profit and the remaining at 5% loss, earning an overall profit of 10%. How much tea did he sell at 15% profit?
(A) 90 kg  (B) 80 kg  (C) 85 kg  (D) 95 kg
Answer: (A) 90 kg
Solution: By rule of alligation:
Profit (+15%) and Loss (-5%), Mean profit = +10%.
Ratio = (10 - (-5)) : (15 - 10) = 15 : 5 = 3 : 1.
Total parts = 3 + 1 = 4 parts = 120 kg.
Quantity sold at 15% profit = (3/4) * 120 = 90 kg. The required value is 90 kg. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

Solution: By rule of alligation on spirit concentrations:

111. (Hard) In a 60-litre mixture of milk and water, the ratio of milk to water is 7 : 3. How many litres of water must be added so that the resulting mixture contains 40% water?
(A) 8 litres  (B) 12 litres  (C) 10 litres  (D) 15 litres
Answer: (C) 10 litres
Solution: Initial milk = 60 * (7/10) = 42 litres, and water = 18 litres.
Since only water is added, the quantity of milk remains 42 litres.
In the new mixture, water is 40%, which means milk is 60% = 3/5.
Total volume of new mixture = 42 / 0.60 = 70 litres.
Water added = 70 - 60 = 10 litres. The required value is 10 litres. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

112. (Hard) A vessel contains 60 litres of pure milk. From this, 12 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water. Then 12 litres of mixture is taken out and replaced with water. What is the ratio of milk to water remaining in the vessel?
(A) 14 : 9  (B) 16 : 11  (C) 18 : 7  (D) 16 : 9
Answer: (D) 16 : 9
Solution: Fraction of liquid replaced in each step = 12 / 60 = 1/5.
Remaining milk = 60 * (1 - 1/5)^2 = 60 * (4/5)^2 = 60 * 16/25 = 38.4 litres.
Remaining water = 60 - 38.4 = 21.6 litres.
Ratio of milk to water = 38.4 : 21.6 = 384 : 216 = 16 : 9. The required value is 16 : 9. Hence, the correct option is (D).

114. (Hard) A sum of ₹16,000 was lent in two parts: one at 5% per annum and the other at 9% per annum simple interest. If the total annual interest received was ₹1,040, what was the amount lent at 9% per annum?
(A) ₹5,000  (B) ₹6,000  (C) ₹7,000  (D) ₹10,000
Answer: (B) ₹6,000
Solution: Overall interest rate = (1040 / 16000) * 100 = 6.5% per annum.
By rule of alligation:
Part 1 (5%) and Part 2 (9%), Mean = 6.5%.
Ratio (5% : 9%) = (9 - 6.5) : (6.5 - 5) = 2.5 : 1.5 = 5 : 3.
Total parts = 5 + 3 = 8 parts = ₹16,000.
Amount lent at 9% = (3/8) * 16000 = ₹6,000. The required value is ₹6,000. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

115. (Hard) A container contains 72 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3. If 24 litres of the mixture is removed and replaced with 24 litres of pure water, what is the new ratio of milk to water?
(A) 3 : 5  (B) 4 : 5  (C) 5 : 7  (D) 5 : 8
Answer: (C) 5 : 7
Solution: Initial milk = 72 * (5/8) = 45 litres, and water = 27 litres.
In 24 litres of mixture removed: Milk removed = 24 * (5/8) = 15 litres, Water removed = 9 litres.
Remaining milk = 45 - 15 = 30 litres, Remaining water = 27 - 9 = 18 litres.
After adding 24 litres of water: Milk = 30 litres, Water = 18 + 24 = 42 litres.
New ratio of milk to water = 30 : 42 = 5 : 7. The required value is 5 : 7. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

116. (Hard) A car travels 300 km in 6 hours. The first part of the journey was driven at 40 km/h and the remaining part at 70 km/h. How many hours were spent driving at 70 km/h?
(A) 1.5 hours  (B) 2.5 hours  (C) 3 hours  (D) 2 hours
Answer: (D) 2 hours
Solution: Average speed = 300 km / 6 h = 50 km/h.
By rule of alligation on speeds (gives time ratio):
Speed 1 (40 km/h) and Speed 2 (70 km/h), Mean = 50 km/h.
Time ratio = (70 - 50) : (50 - 40) = 20 : 10 = 2 : 1.
Total time = 6 hours => Time at 70 km/h = (1/3) * 6 = 2 hours. The required value is 2 hours. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

118. (Hard) A grocer bought 60 kg of sugar at ₹30 per kg and 40 kg at ₹45 per kg. He mixed them and sold the entire mixture at ₹43.20 per kg. What was his profit percentage?
(A) 15%  (B) 20%  (C) 18%  (D) 25%
Answer: (B) 20%
Solution: Total cost price = (60 * 30) + (40 * 45) = 1800 + 1800 = ₹3,600.
Total weight = 60 + 40 = 100 kg.
Cost price per kg = 3600 / 100 = ₹36 per kg.
Selling price per kg = ₹43.20.
Profit percentage = ((43.20 - 36) / 36) * 100 = (7.20 / 36) * 100 = 20%. The required value is 20%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

(A) 25  (B) 30  (C) 35  (D) 45

Number of goats = (2/5) * 75 = 30. The required value is 30. Hence, the correct option is (B).
