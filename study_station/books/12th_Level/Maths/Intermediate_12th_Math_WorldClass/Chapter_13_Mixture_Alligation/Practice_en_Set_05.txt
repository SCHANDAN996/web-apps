101. In what ratio must rice costing ₹70 per kg be mixed with rice costing ₹100 per kg so that the mixture costs ₹73 per kg?
(a) 9 : 1 (b) 10 : 1 (c) 70 : 100 (d) 9 : 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: By alligation: cheaper : dearer = (100 − 73) : (73 − 70) = 27 : 3 = 9 : 1. The trap is writing the ratio the other way, 1 : 9. So the answer is 9 : 1.
Source: PYQ-style

102. 2 kg of tea at ₹40/kg is mixed with 4 kg of tea at ₹55/kg. What is the price of the mixture per kg?
(a) ₹52 (b) ₹50 (c) ₹55 (d) ₹95/2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mean price = (2 × 40 + 4 × 55) ÷ (2 + 4) = 300 ÷ 6 = ₹50. So the answer is ₹50.
Source: PYQ-style

104. A vessel contains 50 litres of milk. 5 litres are taken out and replaced with water. This is done 2 times in all. How much milk is left in the vessel?
(a) 40.50 litres (b) 45 litres (c) 38.50 litres (d) 40 litres
Answer: (a)
Solution: Milk left = 50 × (1 − 5/50)^2 = 40.50 litres. The trap is subtracting 5 litres 2 times, which gives 40 litres. So the answer is 40.50 litres.
