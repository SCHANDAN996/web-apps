Questions 26–50: Medium

26. (Medium) A container contains 60 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2. How much water must be added to this mixture so that the ratio of milk to water becomes 1 : 1?
(A) 12 litres  (B) 10 litres  (C) 15 litres  (D) 18 litres
Answer: (A) 12 litres
Solution: Quantity of milk = 60 * (3/5) = 36 litres.
Quantity of water = 60 * (2/5) = 24 litres.
Let x litres of water be added. New ratio of milk to water is 1 : 1.
36 / (24 + x) = 1 / 1 => 24 + x = 36 => x = 12 litres. The required value is 12 litres. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. (Medium) A grocer mixes two varieties of pulses costing ₹80 per kg and ₹95 per kg in a certain ratio and sells the mixture at ₹99 per kg, making a profit of 10%. In what ratio did he mix the two varieties?
(A) 2 : 1  (B) 1 : 2  (C) 2 : 3  (D) 3 : 2
Answer: (B) 1 : 2
Solution: Selling price of mixture = ₹99 per kg, Profit = 10%.
Cost price of mixture = 99 / 1.10 = ₹90 per kg.
By the rule of alligation:
Ratio = (95 - 90) : (90 - 80) = 5 : 10 = 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

28. (Medium) In an alloy, copper and zinc are in the ratio 5 : 3. In another alloy, copper and zinc are in the ratio 8 : 5. If equal weights of both alloys are melted together, what will be the ratio of copper to zinc in the resulting alloy?
(A) 127 : 81  (B) 131 : 77  (C) 129 : 79  (D) 133 : 75
Answer: (C) 129 : 79
Solution: Total parts in alloy 1 = 5 + 3 = 8.
Total parts in alloy 2 = 8 + 5 = 13.
LCM of 8 and 13 = 104 units.
Let 104 kg of each alloy be taken:
In alloy 1: Copper = (5/8) * 104 = 65 kg, Zinc = (3/8) * 104 = 39 kg.
In alloy 2: Copper = (8/13) * 104 = 64 kg, Zinc = (5/13) * 104 = 40 kg.
Total copper = 65 + 64 = 129 kg.
Total zinc = 39 + 40 = 79 kg.
Resulting ratio = 129 : 79. The required value is 129 : 79. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

29. (Medium) A vessel contains 80 litres of pure milk. 16 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated one more time (total two operations). How much pure milk is left in the vessel?
(A) 48.6 litres  (B) 50.4 litres  (C) 54.0 litres  (D) 51.2 litres
Answer: (D) 51.2 litres
Solution: Using the replacement formula: Remaining quantity = Total * (1 - y/x)^n.
Here x = 80 litres, y = 16 litres, and n = 2.
Remaining milk = 80 * (1 - 16/80)^2 = 80 * (1 - 1/5)^2 = 80 * (4/5)^2 = 80 * 16 / 25 = 51.2 litres. The required value is 51.2 litres. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. (Medium) The average weight of 60 students in a class is 52 kg. The average weight of boys is 56 kg and that of girls is 46 kg. Find the number of boys in the class.
(A) 36  (B) 32  (C) 34  (D) 38
Answer: (A) 36
Solution: By rule of alligation on average weights:
Boys (56 kg) and Girls (46 kg), Overall mean = 52 kg.
Ratio of Boys : Girls = (52 - 46) : (56 - 52) = 6 : 4 = 3 : 2.
Total parts = 3 + 2 = 5 parts = 60 students.
Number of boys = (3/5) * 60 = 36. The required value is 36. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

31. (Medium) A merchant has 100 kg of sugar, part of which he sells at 7% profit and the rest at 17% profit. He gains 10% on the whole. How much sugar did he sell at 17% profit?
(A) 25 kg  (B) 30 kg  (C) 35 kg  (D) 40 kg
Answer: (B) 30 kg
Solution: By the rule of alligation:
Part at 7% and Part at 17%, Mean profit = 10%.
Ratio (7% : 17%) = (17 - 10) : (10 - 7) = 7 : 3.
Total parts = 7 + 3 = 10.
Quantity sold at 17% profit = (3/10) * 100 = 30 kg. The required value is 30 kg. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

32. (Medium) A person travelled a total distance of 150 km in 5 hours. Part of the journey was covered by car at an average speed of 40 km/h and the remaining distance on a bicycle at an average speed of 15 km/h. Find the distance travelled by car.
(A) 100 km  (B) 110 km  (C) 120 km  (D) 130 km
Answer: (C) 120 km
Solution: Average speed for entire journey = 150 km / 5 h = 30 km/h.
By rule of alligation on speeds (gives time ratio):
Car (40 km/h) and Bicycle (15 km/h), Mean = 30 km/h.
Time ratio (Car : Bicycle) = (30 - 15) : (40 - 30) = 15 : 10 = 3 : 2.
Total time = 5 hours => Time by car = 3 hours.
Distance covered by car = 3 h * 40 km/h = 120 km. The required value is 120 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

33. (Medium) A sum of ₹10,000 was lent partly at 6% per annum simple interest and the remaining at 10% per annum simple interest. If the total interest earned in one year was ₹760, how much money was lent at 10% per annum?
(A) ₹3,600  (B) ₹4,500  (C) ₹6,000  (D) ₹4,000
Answer: (D) ₹4,000
Solution: Overall interest rate = (760 / 10000) * 100 = 7.6% per annum.
By rule of alligation:
Part at 6% and Part at 10%, Mean rate = 7.6%.
Ratio (6% : 10%) = (10 - 7.6) : (7.6 - 6) = 2.4 : 1.6 = 3 : 2.
Total parts = 3 + 2 = 5.
Amount lent at 10% = (2/5) * 10000 = ₹4,000. The required value is ₹4,000. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

34. (Medium) Two vessels A and B contain mixtures of milk and water in the ratio 4 : 5 and 5 : 1 respectively. In what ratio should the quantities from vessels A and B be mixed so that the new mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 4?
(A) 5 : 2  (B) 3 : 2  (C) 4 : 3  (D) 5 : 3
Answer: (A) 5 : 2
Solution: Fraction of milk in Vessel A = 4/9 = 8/18.
Fraction of milk in Vessel B = 5/6 = 15/18.
Desired fraction of milk in mixture = 5/9 = 10/18.
By rule of alligation:
Ratio (A : B) = (15/18 - 10/18) : (10/18 - 8/18) = 5/18 : 2/18 = 5 : 2. The required value is 5 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

35. (Medium) A mixture of 40 litres contains spirit and water in the ratio 3 : 1. How much water must be added to the mixture so that the spirit becomes 60% of the new mixture?
(A) 8 litres  (B) 10 litres  (C) 12 litres  (D) 15 litres
Answer: (B) 10 litres
Solution: Initial spirit = 40 * (3/4) = 30 litres, and water = 10 litres.
Since only water is added, the volume of spirit remains 30 litres.
In the new mixture, spirit represents 60% = 3/5 of total volume.
New total volume = 30 / (3/5) = 50 litres.
Water added = 50 - 40 = 10 litres. The required value is 10 litres. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

36. (Medium) A dishonest milkman professes to sell milk at cost price but mixes water with it and thereby gains 25%. What is the percentage of water in the mixture?
(A) 16.67%  (B) 25%  (C) 20%  (D) 33.33%
Answer: (C) 20%
Solution: Let the cost price of 1 litre of pure milk be ₹1.
To gain 25%, he sells 1.25 litres of mixture for ₹1.25, while purchasing 1 litre of milk for ₹1 (water is free).
In 1.25 litres of mixture, quantity of water = 0.25 litres.
Percentage of water in mixture = (0.25 / 1.25) * 100 = 20%. The required value is 20%. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

37. (Medium) Tea worth ₹120 per kg and ₹130 per kg are mixed with a third variety in the ratio 1 : 1 : 2. If the mixture is worth ₹160 per kg, find the price of the third variety per kg.
(A) ₹185 per kg  (B) ₹190 per kg  (C) ₹200 per kg  (D) ₹195 per kg
Answer: (D) ₹195 per kg
Solution: Let the price of the third variety be ₹x per kg.
Ratio of weights = 1 : 1 : 2. Total weight = 1 + 1 + 2 = 4 kg.
Total cost = 1 * 120 + 1 * 130 + 2 * x = 250 + 2x.
Average cost = (250 + 2x) / 4 = 160.
250 + 2x = 640 => 2x = 390 => x = ₹195 per kg. The required value is ₹195 per kg. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

38. (Medium) A can contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 7 : 5. When 9 litres of mixture are drawn off and the can is filled with liquid B, the ratio of A and B becomes 7 : 9. How many litres of liquid A was contained in the can initially?
(A) 21 litres  (B) 18 litres  (C) 24 litres  (D) 27 litres
Answer: (A) 21 litres
Solution: After withdrawing 9 litres of mixture, ratio of A : B remains 7 : 5.
Adding 9 litres of liquid B changes ratio to 7 : 9.
Amount of A is constant (7 units).
Increase in B = 9 - 5 = 4 units = 9 litres => 1 unit = 9/4 litres.
Total quantity after refilling = 7 + 9 = 16 units = 16 * (9/4) = 36 litres.
Initial capacity was 36 litres.
Initial quantity of liquid A = 36 * (7/12) = 21 litres. The required value is 21 litres. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (A) 45 kg
Solution: By rule of alligation:

(A) 119 : 61  (B) 121 : 59  (C) 123 : 57  (D) 125 : 55

Glass 3: Milk = (3/5) * 60 = 36, Water = 24.
Total milk = 40 + 45 + 36 = 121 units.

Ratio of milk to water = 121 : 59. The required value is 121 : 59. Hence, the correct option is (B).

45. (Medium) The average monthly salary of all employees in an office is ₹16,000. The average monthly salary of officers is ₹32,000 and that of non-officers is ₹12,000. If the number of officers is 8, find the total number of employees in the office.
(A) 36  (B) 44  (C) 48  (D) 40
Answer: (D) 40
Solution: By rule of alligation on salaries:
Officers (₹32,000) and Non-officers (₹12,000), Mean = ₹16,000.
Ratio (Officers : Non-officers) = (16000 - 12000) : (32000 - 16000) = 4000 : 16000 = 1 : 4.
Number of officers = 1 unit = 8.
Number of non-officers = 4 units = 32.
Total employees = 8 + 32 = 40. The required value is 40. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

46. (Medium) A container has 120 litres of pure wine. 30 litres of wine is replaced with water. Again, 30 litres of the mixture is replaced with water. What is the ratio of wine to water remaining in the container?
(A) 9 : 7  (B) 7 : 5  (C) 8 : 7  (D) 16 : 9
Answer: (A) 9 : 7
Solution: Fraction of liquid replaced in each step = 30 / 120 = 1/4.
Remaining wine = 120 * (1 - 1/4)^2 = 120 * (3/4)^2 = 120 * 9/16 = 67.5 litres.
Water remaining = 120 - 67.5 = 52.5 litres.
Ratio of wine to water = 67.5 : 52.5 = 135 : 105 = 9 : 7. The required value is 9 : 7. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

(A) 65%  (B) 70%  (C) 75%  (D) 80%
Answer: (C) 75%
Solution: By rule of alligation:
Passed (45) and Failed (25), Overall mean = 40.

Percentage of candidates who passed = (3 / (3 + 1)) * 100 = 75%. The required value is 75%. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
