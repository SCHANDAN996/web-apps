Questions 26–50: Medium

26. 20 litres of a 10% sugar solution is mixed with 40 litres of a 40% sugar solution. What is the sugar concentration of the mixture?
(a) 25% (b) 50% (c) 30% (d) 27%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sugar = 20 × 10% + 40 × 40% = 18 litres in 60 litres, so the concentration is 30%. The trap is the simple average 25%. So the answer is 30%.
Source: PYQ-style

27. A mixture of 20 litres contains milk and water in the ratio 4 : 1. If 4 litres of water is added, what will be the new ratio of milk to water?
(a) 3 : 1 (b) 4 : 1 (c) 2 : 1 (d) 1 : 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Milk = 16 litres and water = 4 litres. After adding water: 16 : 8 = 2 : 1. So the answer is 2 : 1.
Source: PYQ-style

28. A vessel contains 40 litres of milk. 8 litres are taken out and replaced with water. This is done 2 times in all. How much milk is left in the vessel?
(a) 24 litres (b) 33.60 litres (c) 23.60 litres (d) 25.60 litres
Answer: (d)
Solution: Milk left = 40 × (1 − 8/40)^2 = 25.60 litres. The trap is subtracting 8 litres 2 times, which gives 24 litres. So the answer is 25.60 litres.
Source: PYQ-style

30. In what ratio must rice costing ₹108 per kg be mixed with rice costing ₹116 per kg so that the mixture costs ₹114 per kg?
(a) 2 : 3 (b) 1 : 3 (c) 1 : 4 (d) 108 : 116
Answer: (b)
Solution: By alligation: cheaper : dearer = (116 − 114) : (114 − 108) = 2 : 6 = 1 : 3. The trap is writing the ratio the other way, 3 : 1. So the answer is 1 : 3.
