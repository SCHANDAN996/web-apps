# Previous Year Questions: Mixture and Alligation

1. In what ratio must a grocer mix two varieties of pulses costing Rs. 15 and Rs. 20 per kg respectively, so as to get a mixture worth Rs. 16.50 per kg?
(A) 7 : 3  (B) 3 : 7  (C) 5 : 3  (D) 3 : 5
Answer: (A) 7 : 3
Solution: By Rule of Alligation: (Price of Dearer - Mean Price) / (Mean Price - Price of Cheaper) = (20 - 16.50) / (16.50 - 15) = 3.50 / 1.50 = 7 / 3 = 7 : 3. The required value is 7 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. A container contains 40 liters of milk. From this container, 4 liters of milk was taken out and replaced by water. This process was repeated further two times. How much milk is now contained by the container?
(A) 29.16 liters  (B) 28.00 liters  (C) 30.24 liters  (D) 27.50 liters
Answer: (A) 29.16 liters
Solution: Amount of milk left = 40 * (1 - 4/40)^3 = 40 * (9/10)^3 = 40 * 729 / 1000 = 29.16 liters. The required value is 29.16 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

3. In what ratio must water be mixed with milk costing Rs. 12 per liter to obtain a mixture worth Rs. 8 per liter?
(A) 1 : 2  (B) 2 : 1  (C) 2 : 3  (D) 1 : 3
Answer: (A) 1 : 2
Solution: Cost price of water = Rs. 0. By Alligation: (Milk - Mean) / (Mean - Water) = (12 - 8) / (8 - 0) = 4 / 8 = 1 : 2. Ratio of Water : Milk = 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

4. Two vessels A and B contain milk and water in the ratio 4 : 3 and 2 : 3 respectively. In what ratio should the mixtures from both vessels be mixed to get a new mixture containing half milk and half water?
(A) 7 : 5  (B) 5 : 7  (C) 4 : 5  (D) 3 : 4
Answer: (A) 7 : 5
Solution: Fraction of milk in A = 4/7. In B = 2/5. In mixture = 1/2. By Alligation: (1/2 - 2/5) / (4/7 - 1/2) = (1/10) / (1/14) = 14 / 10 = 7 / 5 = 7 : 5. The required value is 7 : 5. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

5. A merchant has 1000 kg of sugar, part of which he sells at 8% profit and the rest at 18% profit. He gains 14% on the whole. The quantity sold at 18% profit is:
(A) 600 kg  (B) 400 kg  (C) 500 kg  (D) 650 kg
Answer: (A) 600 kg
Solution: By Alligation on profit %: Ratio of quantity at 8% to 18% = (18 - 14) / (14 - 8) = 4 : 6 = 2 : 3. Quantity at 18% = (3 / 5) * 1000 = 600 kg. The required value is 600 kg. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern
