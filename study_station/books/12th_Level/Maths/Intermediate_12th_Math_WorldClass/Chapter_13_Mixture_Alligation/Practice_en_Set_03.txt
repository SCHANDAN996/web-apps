Questions 51–75: Medium

51. (Medium) A container contains 50 litres of pure milk. From this, 5 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated once more. How many litres of milk is left in the container?
(A) 40.5 litres  (B) 38.5 litres  (C) 42.0 litres  (D) 45.0 litres
Answer: (A) 40.5 litres
Solution: Using the replacement formula: Remaining milk = Total * (1 - y/x)^n.
Here x = 50, y = 5, n = 2.
Remaining milk = 50 * (1 - 5/50)^2 = 50 * (9/10)^2 = 50 * 0.81 = 40.5 litres. The required value is 40.5 litres. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. (Medium) A milkman mixes 20 litres of water with 80 litres of milk. If cost price of pure milk is ₹50 per litre and water is available free of cost, find his profit percentage if he sells the mixture at the cost price of pure milk.
(A) 20%  (B) 25%  (C) 30%  (D) 33.33%
Answer: (B) 25%
Solution: Cost Price of 80 litres pure milk = 80 * 50 = ₹4,000.
Total volume of mixture = 80 + 20 = 100 litres.
Selling Price of 100 litres mixture at ₹50 per litre = 100 * 50 = ₹5,000.
Profit = ₹5,000 - ₹4,000 = ₹1,000.
Profit percentage = (1000 / 4000) * 100 = 25%. The required value is 25%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

53. (Medium) A chemist has two solutions of hydrochloric acid: Solution X contains 15% acid and Solution Y contains 40% acid. How many litres of Solution X should be mixed with Solution Y to obtain 50 litres of a 30% acid solution?
(A) 18 litres  (B) 25 litres  (C) 20 litres  (D) 30 litres
Answer: (C) 20 litres
Solution: By rule of alligation on acid percentages:
Solution X (15%) and Solution Y (40%), Desired mean = 30%.
Ratio (X : Y) = (40 - 30) : (30 - 15) = 10 : 15 = 2 : 3.
Total volume = 50 litres, total parts = 2 + 3 = 5.
Volume of Solution X = (2/5) * 50 = 20 litres. The required value is 20 litres. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

54. (Medium) A sum of ₹12,000 is divided into two parts such that simple interest on the first part at 8% per annum for 1 year equals simple interest on the second part at 12% per annum for 1 year. What is the value of the first part?
(A) ₹4,800  (B) ₹6,000  (C) ₹8,400  (D) ₹7,200
Answer: (D) ₹7,200
Solution: Let the two parts be P1 and P2.
Given: P1 * 8 * 1 / 100 = P2 * 12 * 1 / 100 => 8 P1 = 12 P2.
Ratio P1 : P2 = 12 : 8 = 3 : 2.
Total parts = 3 + 2 = 5 parts = ₹12,000.
First part P1 = (3/5) * 12000 = ₹7,200. The required value is ₹7,200. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

55. (Medium) In a 45-litre mixture of alcohol and water, the ratio of alcohol to water is 4 : 1. If 5 litres of water is added to the mixture, what is the new ratio of alcohol to water?
(A) 18 : 7  (B) 16 : 7  (C) 20 : 9  (D) 24 : 11
Answer: (A) 18 : 7
Solution: Quantity of alcohol = 45 * (4/5) = 36 litres.
Quantity of water = 45 * (1/5) = 9 litres.
After adding 5 litres of water, new water quantity = 9 + 5 = 14 litres.
New ratio of alcohol to water = 36 : 14 = 18 : 7. The required value is 18 : 7. Hence, the correct option is (A).

57. (Medium) The average age of all the 50 employees in a company is 32 years. The average age of male employees is 35 years and that of female employees is 25 years. How many female employees work in the company?

Solution: By rule of alligation on average age:

Number of female employees = (3/10) * 50 = 15. The required value is 15. Hence, the correct option is (C).

58. (Medium) Two vessels A and B contain mixtures of milk and water. In vessel A, the ratio of milk to water is 7 : 3, and in vessel B, it is 2 : 3. In what ratio should the contents of vessels A and B be mixed to get a mixture containing milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2?
(A) 1 : 2  (B) 3 : 2  (C) 4 : 3  (D) 2 : 1
Answer: (D) 2 : 1
Solution: Milk fraction in vessel A = 7/10.
Milk fraction in vessel B = 2/5 = 4/10.
Desired milk fraction = 3/5 = 6/10.
By rule of alligation:
Ratio (A : B) = (6/10 - 4/10) : (7/10 - 6/10) = 2/10 : 1/10 = 2 : 1. The required value is 2 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (D).

61. (Medium) A mixture of 75 litres contains milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1. How many litres of water must be added so that the ratio of milk to water becomes 1 : 2?
(A) 50 litres  (B) 60 litres  (C) 75 litres  (D) 80 litres
Answer: (C) 75 litres
Solution: Initial milk = 75 * (2/3) = 50 litres, and water = 25 litres.
In the new mixture, the ratio of milk to water is 1 : 2.
Since milk remains 50 litres, 1 unit = 50 litres => Water = 2 * 50 = 100 litres.
Water to be added = 100 - 25 = 75 litres. The required value is 75 litres. Hence, the correct option is (C).

62. (Medium) In what ratio must water be mixed with spirit costing ₹60 per litre so that selling the mixture at ₹50 per litre yields a profit of 25%?

Solution: Selling price of mixture = ₹50 per litre, Profit = 25%.

Ratio (Water : Spirit) = (60 - 40) : (40 - 0) = 20 : 40 = 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (D).

65. (Medium) A vessel contains 90 litres of a mixture of milk and water containing 20% water. How many litres of pure milk must be added to reduce the water content to 15%?

Solution: Quantity of water in original mixture = 90 * 0.20 = 18 litres.

Quantity of milk added = 120 - 90 = 30 litres. The required value is 30 litres. Hence, the correct option is (C).

(A) 2 hours  (B) 3.5 hours  (C) 4 hours  (D) 3 hours
Answer: (D) 3 hours
Solution: Average speed for entire trip = 60 km / 5 h = 12 km/h.
By rule of alligation on speeds (gives time ratio):
Foot (8 km/h) and Bicycle (18 km/h), Mean = 12 km/h.
Ratio of time (Foot : Bicycle) = (18 - 12) : (12 - 8) = 6 : 4 = 3 : 2.
Total time = 5 hours => Time travelled on foot = 3 hours. The required value is 3 hours. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Cost price per kg = 900 / 5 = ₹180 per kg.
Selling price per kg = ₹216.
Profit percentage = ((216 - 180) / 180) * 100 = (36 / 180) * 100 = 20%. The required value is 20%. Hence, the correct option is (D).

74. (Medium) A jar contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 4 : 1. When 10 litres of the mixture is taken out and 10 litres of liquid B is poured into the jar, the ratio becomes 2 : 3. What was the total quantity of liquid contained in the jar initially?
(A) 16 litres  (B) 24 litres  (C) 28 litres  (D) 20 litres
Answer: (D) 20 litres
Solution: After removing 10 litres of mixture, ratio of A : B is 4 : 1. Let remaining volumes be 4x and 1x.
Adding 10 litres of liquid B: A = 4x, B = x + 10.
New ratio: 4x / (x + 10) = 2 / 3 => 12x = 2x + 20 => 10x = 20 => x = 2.
Remaining volume before adding B = 4x + x = 5x = 10 litres.
Total initial quantity = Remaining volume + Removed volume = 10 + 10 = 20 litres. The required value is 20 litres. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

75. (Medium) A vessel contains 54 litres of pure acid. One-third of the acid is replaced with water. Then one-third of the mixture is again replaced with water. How much pure acid remains in the vessel?
(A) 20 litres  (B) 24 litres  (C) 28 litres  (D) 32 litres
Answer: (B) 24 litres
Solution: Fraction replaced each time = 1/3.
Remaining acid = Initial * (1 - 1/3)^2 = 54 * (2/3)^2 = 54 * 4/9 = 24 litres. The required value is 24 litres. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
