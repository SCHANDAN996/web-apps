Questions 51–75: Medium

51. In what ratio must rice costing ₹72 per kg be mixed with rice costing ₹92 per kg so that the mixture costs ₹88 per kg?
(a) 1 : 5 (b) 2 : 4 (c) 1 : 4 (d) 72 : 92
Answer: (c)
Solution: By alligation: cheaper : dearer = (92 − 88) : (88 − 72) = 4 : 16 = 1 : 4. The trap is writing the ratio the other way, 4 : 1. So the answer is 1 : 4.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A mixture of 20 litres contains milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2. If 4 litres of water is added, what will be the new ratio of milk to water?
(a) 1 : 2 (b) 2 : 1 (c) 1 : 1 (d) 3 : 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Milk = 12 litres and water = 8 litres. After adding water: 12 : 12 = 1 : 1. So the answer is 1 : 1.
Source: PYQ-style

53. A vessel contains 40 litres of milk. 10 litres are taken out and replaced with water. This is done 2 times in all. How much milk is left in the vessel?
(a) 22.50 litres (b) 30 litres (c) 32.50 litres (d) 20 litres
Answer: (a)
Solution: Milk left = 40 × (1 − 10/40)^2 = 22.50 litres. The trap is subtracting 10 litres 2 times, which gives 20 litres. So the answer is 22.50 litres.
Source: PYQ-style

54. 40 litres of a 10% sugar solution is mixed with 20 litres of a 25% sugar solution. What is the sugar concentration of the mixture?
