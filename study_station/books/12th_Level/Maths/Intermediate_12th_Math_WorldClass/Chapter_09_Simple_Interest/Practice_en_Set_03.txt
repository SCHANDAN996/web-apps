Q51. (Medium) A sum of Rs 18,750 is divided into two parts and invested at 4% and 6% per annum simple interest respectively. If the total annual interest received from both parts is Rs 950, find the amount invested at 4%.
(A) Rs 8,750  (B) Rs 10,000  (C) Rs 9,250  (D) Rs 8,500

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Assume the entire sum was invested at 6%. The total interest would be 6% of 18750 = Rs 1,125. The actual interest is Rs 950, giving a deficit of 1125 - 950 = Rs 175. This deficit is due to the 6% - 4% = 2% lower rate on part 1. Therefore, 2% of Part 1 = Rs 175 => Part 1 = (175 / 2) * 100 = Rs 8,750. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 8,750, corresponding to option (A).

Q52. (Medium) Divide Rs 14,400 between P and Q such that the simple interest on P's share after 7 years at 5% per annum equals the simple interest on Q's share after 9 years at the same rate. Find the share of P.
(A) Rs 6,300  (B) Rs 8,100  (C) Rs 7,800  (D) Rs 8,400

Solution:
For equal interest at the same rate: P * 7 = Q * 9 => P / Q = 9 / 7. Total ratio parts = 9 + 7 = 16. Total sum = Rs 14,400. Value of 1 part = 14400 / 16 = Rs 900. P's share = 9 * 900 = Rs 8,100. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 8,100, corresponding to option (B).

Q53. (Medium) A person divides Rs 26,000 among his three sons in such a way that the simple interest on each share at 5% per annum after 2, 3, and 4 years respectively are all equal. Find the share of the first son.
(A) Rs 8,000  (B) Rs 6,000  (C) Rs 12,000  (D) Rs 10,000

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Since the rate is identical (5%), the condition for equal interest SI1 = SI2 = SI3 gives P1 * 2 = P2 * 3 = P3 * 4. Dividing by LCM(2, 3, 4) = 12 gives P1 / 6 = P2 / 4 = P3 / 3. Sum of ratio units = 6 + 4 + 3 = 13 units. Since 13 units = Rs 26,000, 1 unit = Rs 2,000. Therefore, the first son's share P1 = 6 * 2000 = Rs 12,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 12,000, corresponding to option (C).

Solution:
Younger son has 18 - 12 = 6 years until age 18. Elder son has 18 - 14 = 4 years until age 18. Amount for younger son = P1 * [1 + (6 * 5)/100] = 1.30 * P1.
