Q101. (Hard) A sum of Rs 18,750 is divided into two parts such that simple interest on the first part for 4 years at 5% per annum is equal to the simple interest on the second part for 3 years at 10% per annum. Find the value of the first part.
(A) Rs 11,250  (B) Rs 7,500  (C) Rs 10,500  (D) Rs 12,000

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Equating the interests: P1 * (4 * 5) / 100 = P2 * (3 * 10) / 100 => 20 * P1 = 30 * P2 => P1 / P2 = 3 / 2. The ratio of the two parts is 3 : 2. Total ratio units = 3 + 2 = 5 units = Rs 18,750 => 1 unit = Rs 3,750. First part P1 = 3 * 3750 = Rs 11,250. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 11,250, corresponding to option (A).

Q102. (Hard) A father leaves Rs 85,000 to be divided between his two sons aged 12 years and 10 years such that each receives an equal amount when they attain the age of 18 years at 10% simple interest per annum. How much did the elder son receive?
(A) Rs 40,000  (B) Rs 45,000  (C) Rs 48,000  (D) Rs 42,000

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Elder son's investment period = 18 - 12 = 6 years. Amount A1 = P1 * [1 + (10 * 6)/100] = 1.6 * P1. Younger son's investment period = 18 - 10 = 8 years. Amount A2 = P2 * [1 + (10 * 8)/100] = 1.8 * P2. Since A1 = A2, 1.6 * P1 = 1.8 * P2 => P1 / P2 = 1.8 / 1.6 = 9 / 8. Total ratio units = 9 + 8 = 17 units = Rs 85,000 => 1 unit = Rs 5,000. Elder son's share = 9 * 5000 = Rs 45,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 45,000, corresponding to option (B).

Q103. (Hard) A sum of Rs 26,000 is divided into three parts such that the simple interest earned on them after 2 years, 3 years, and 4 years respectively at 5% per annum are all equal. Find the smallest part.
(A) Rs 12,000  (B) Rs 8,000  (C) Rs 6,000  (D) Rs 5,000

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Since the rate is identical (5%), equal interest means: P1 * 2 = P2 * 3 = P3 * 4. The ratio of parts is P1 : P2 : P3 = (1/2) : (1/3) : (1/4). Multiplying by LCM 12 gives 6 : 4 : 3. Sum of ratio units = 6 + 4 + 3 = 13 units = Rs 26,000 => 1 unit = Rs 2,000. Smallest part P3 = 3 * 2000 = Rs 6,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 6,000, corresponding to option (C).

Q104. (Hard) A person invested a total of Rs 100,000 in two schemes: Scheme A offering 8% per annum and Scheme B offering 12% per annum simple interest. If the total annual interest received from both schemes combined was Rs 9,600, how much was invested in Scheme A?
(A) Rs 40,000  (B) Rs 50,000  (C) Rs 65,000  (D) Rs 60,000

Answer: (D)

Solution:
The overall average interest rate = (9600 / 100000) * 100 = 9.6% per annum. Using the rule of alligation: Scheme A (8%) and Scheme B (12%) with mean 9.6%: Ratio of investments = (12 - 9.6) : (9.6 - 8) = 2.4 : 1.6 = 3 : 2. Sum of units = 3 + 2 = 5 units = Rs 100,000 => 1 unit = Rs 20,000. Scheme A investment = 3 * 20000 = Rs 60,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 60,000, corresponding to option (D).

Q105. (Hard) Find the true discount on a bill of Rs 1,860 due after 8 months at 5% simple interest per annum.
(A) Rs 60  (B) Rs 62  (C) Rs 58  (D) Rs 65

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Time T = 8 / 12 = 2/3 year. Rate R = 5%. Formula for Present Worth: PW = (100 * Sum Due) / [100 + (R * T)] = (100 * 1860) / [100 + 5 * (2/3)] = 186000 / (310 / 3) = 186000 * (3 / 310) = 600 * 3 = Rs 1,800. True Discount TD = Sum Due - PW = 1860 - 1800 = Rs 60. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 60, corresponding to option (A).

Q107. (Hard) The banker's gain on a bill due 1 year hence at 10% per annum simple interest is Rs 20. Find the bill amount (sum due).
(A) Rs 2,000  (B) Rs 2,400  (C) Rs 2,200  (D) Rs 2,100

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Banker's Gain BG = (TD * R * T) / 100. Given BG = 20, R = 10, T = 1: 20 = (TD * 10 * 1) / 100 => TD = Rs 200. Present Worth PW = (100 * TD) / (R * T) = (100 * 200) / 10 = Rs 2,000. Sum Due = PW + TD = 2000 + 200 = Rs 2,200. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 2,200, corresponding to option (C).
