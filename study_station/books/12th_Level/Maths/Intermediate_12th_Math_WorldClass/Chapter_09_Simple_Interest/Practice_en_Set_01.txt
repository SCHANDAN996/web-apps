1. Find the simple interest on ₹5,000 at 8% per annum for 3 years.
(a) ₹1,000 (b) ₹1,200 (c) ₹1,400 (d) ₹1,500
Answer: (b)
Solution: SI = P × R × T / 100 = 5000 × 8 × 3 / 100 = 1,200. The tempting wrong option ₹1,500 comes from wrongly using 10% instead of 8%, so the answer is ₹1,200.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

5. The simple interest on a sum at 10% per annum for 2 years is ₹600. Find the sum.
(a) ₹2,500 (b) ₹4,000 (c) ₹3,000 (d) ₹3,500
Answer: (c)
Solution: P = SI × 100 / (R × T) = 600 × 100 / (10 × 2) = 3,000. The trap ₹4,000 comes from dividing 600 by 15% instead of the correct 20% total rate, so the answer is ₹3,000.

21. A sum of money amounts to ₹5,900 in 3 years and ₹6,500 in 5 years at simple interest. Find the rate of interest per annum.
(a) 8% (b) 5% (c) 7% (d) 6%
Answer: (d)
Solution: SI for 2 years = 6,500 − 5,900 = 600, so yearly SI = 300 and SI for 3 years = 900; Principal = 5,900 − 900 = 5,000. Then R = 300 × 100 / 5000 = 6%. The trap 8% comes from dividing the 2-year interest 600 by the principal without halving it first, so the answer is 6%.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

22. A sum of ₹1,550 was lent partly at 5% and partly at 8% per annum simple interest. The total interest received after 3 years was ₹300. The ratio of money lent at 5% to that at 8% is:
(a) 5 : 8 (b) 8 : 5 (c) 16 : 15 (d) 15 : 16
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let x be lent at 5%; then (1550 − x) at 8%. Total SI = [x × 5 × 3 + (1550 − x) × 8 × 3]/100 = 300, so 15x + 37,200 − 24x = 30,000, giving 9x = 7,200 and x = 800. Ratio = 800 : 750 = 16 : 15. The trap 15 : 16 is the same ratio reversed, so the answer is 16 : 15.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

23. A person borrows ₹5,000 at 4% per annum and ₹4,000 at 5% per annum simple interest for the same period. If the total interest paid is ₹800, find the time.
(a) 1 year (b) 1.5 years (c) 2.5 years (d) 2 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: Interest per year = 5000 × 4/100 + 4000 × 5/100 = 200 + 200 = 400, so Time = 800/400 = 2 years. The trap 1 year comes from adding the two principals and applying a single average rate incorrectly, so the answer is 2 years.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

24. The simple interest on a sum is 1/4 of the principal, and the number of years is equal to the rate per cent per annum. Find the rate.
(a) 5% (b) 4% (c) 6% (d) 8%
Answer: (a)
Solution: SI = P/4 and R = T. So P × R × R / 100 = P/4, giving R² = 25 and R = 5%. The trap 4% comes from confusing the fraction 1/4 with the rate itself, so the answer is 5%.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

25. A sum of money becomes 3 times itself in 8 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 5 times itself?
(a) 12 years (b) 18 years (c) 14 years (d) 16 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: To become 3 times, interest = 2P in 8 years, so interest of P takes 4 years. To become 5 times, interest needed = 4P, which takes 4 × 4 = 16 years. The trap 12 years comes from wrongly scaling 8 × 5/3, so the answer is 16 years.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)
