Q1. (Easy) Find the simple interest on Rs 6,800 at 16.67% per annum for 3 years.
(A) Rs 3,400  (B) Rs 3,200  (C) Rs 3,600  (D) Rs 3,000

Answer: (A)

Solution:
The rate of 16.67% is equivalent to the fraction 1/6. Simple Interest = P * R * T = 6800 * (1/6) * 3 = 6800 * (1/2) = Rs 3,400. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 3,400, corresponding to option (A).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q2. (Easy) A sum of Rs 12,000 is lent at simple interest of 12.5% per annum. What is the total amount after 4 years?
(A) Rs 17,500  (B) Rs 18,000  (C) Rs 18,500  (D) Rs 16,800

Answer: (B)

Solution:
The rate of 12.5% is 1/8. For 4 years, total interest fraction = 4 * (1/8) = 1/2 of principal. Simple Interest = 12000 * 0.5 = Rs 6,000. Amount = Principal + SI = 12000 + 6000 = Rs 18,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 18,000, corresponding to option (B).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q3. (Easy) In how many years will a sum of Rs 5,000 yield a simple interest of Rs 1,500 at 6% per annum?
(A) 4 years  (B) 6 years  (C) 5 years  (D) 4.5 years

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Using the standard formula T = (SI * 100) / (P * R), we have T = (1500 * 100) / (5000 * 6) = 150000 / 30000 = 5 years. Therefore, the correct value is 5 years, corresponding to option (C).

Q4. (Easy) At what rate percent per annum simple interest will Rs 7,200 amount to Rs 8,928 in 3 years?
(A) 7.5%  (B) 8.5%  (C) 9%  (D) 8%

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Simple Interest SI = Amount - Principal = 8928 - 7200 = Rs 1,728. Using R = (SI * 100) / (P * T), we obtain R = (1728 * 100) / (7200 * 3) = 1728 / 216 = 8%. Therefore, the correct value is 8%, corresponding to option (D).

Q5. (Easy) Find the simple interest on Rs 2,500 for 73 days at 10% per annum in a non-leap year.
(A) Rs 50  (B) Rs 45  (C) Rs 60  (D) Rs 55

Answer: (A)

Solution:
In a normal year of 365 days, 73 days is equal to 73 / 365 = 1/5 of a year. Simple Interest = (2500 * 10 * 1/5) / 100 = 5000 / 100 = Rs 50. Psychological Trap: Candidates sometimes take 360 days in a commercial year and get a fractional answer, whereas standard SSC problems assume 365 days unless specified. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 50, corresponding to option (A).

Solution:

Principal = Amount after 3 years - 3 years SI = 3080 - 480 = Rs 2,600. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 2,600, corresponding to option (B).

Q10. (Easy) If Rs 64 amounts to Rs 83.20 in 2 years, what will Rs 86 amount to in 4 years at the same rate of simple interest?

Solution:

Total Amount = 86 + 51.60 = Rs 137.60. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 137.60, corresponding to option (B).

Q12. (Easy) If the simple interest on a certain sum of money for 3 years at 5% per annum is Rs 225, find the simple interest on the same sum for 4 years at 6% per annum.
(A) Rs 350  (B) Rs 375  (C) Rs 340  (D) Rs 360

Answer: (D)

Solution:
First case total interest percentage = 3 * 5% = 15%. Given 15% of P = Rs 225, so P = 225 / 0.15 = Rs 1,500. Second case total interest percentage = 4 * 6% = 24%. New SI = 24% of 1500 = 1500 * 0.24 = Rs 360. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 360, corresponding to option (D).

Q13. (Easy) A sum of money becomes 7/6 of itself in 3 years at a certain rate of simple interest. Find the rate of interest per annum.
(A) 5.56%  (B) 5.25%  (C) 6.25%  (D) 6.67%

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Let the principal P = 6 units. The amount becomes 7 units, so the simple interest earned in 3 years is 7 - 6 = 1 unit. Rate R = (SI * 100) / (P * T) = (1 * 100) / (6 * 3) = 100 / 18 = 50 / 9% = 5.56% (or 5 5/9%). Psychological Trap: Many students assume the ratio is SI to Principal instead of Amount to Principal, leading to incorrect calculations like 7/6 * 100. Therefore, the correct value is 5.56%, corresponding to option (A).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q14. (Easy) The simple interest on a sum of money is 4/9 of the principal. If the numerical value of the rate percent per annum and time in years are equal, find the rate of interest.
(A) 6.25%  (B) 6.67%  (C) 7.50%  (D) 8.00%

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Given SI / P = 4/9 and R = T. We have SI = (P * R * T) / 100 => 4/9 = R^2 / 100. Thus R^2 = 400 / 9, which gives R = 20 / 3% = 6.67% (or 6 2/3%). Therefore, the correct value is 6.67%, corresponding to option (B).

Answer: (D)

Solution:

Q21. (Easy) A person invested Rs 10,000 at 6% simple interest per annum and another sum at 8% simple interest per annum. If the total annual interest received from both investments is Rs 1,000, find the second sum.
(A) Rs 5,000  (B) Rs 6,000  (C) Rs 4,500  (D) Rs 5,500

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Annual interest from first investment = 6% of 10000 = Rs 600. Remaining annual interest needed = 1000 - 600 = Rs 400. Let the second sum be S. Then 8% of S = Rs 400 => S = 400 / 0.08 = Rs 5,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 5,000, corresponding to option (A).

Q22. (Easy) Find the simple interest on Rs 7,300 from 5 January 2020 to 18 April 2020 at 5% per annum.
(A) Rs 105  (B) Rs 104  (C) Rs 102  (D) Rs 100

Answer: (B)

Solution:
The year 2020 is a leap year, so February has 29 days. Counting days: January has 31 - 5 = 26 days; February has 29 days; March has 31 days; April has 18 days. Total days = 26 + 29 + 31 + 18 = 104 days. Simple Interest = (7300 * 5 * 104) / (100 * 366) = (73 * 520) / 366 = 37960 / 366 = Rs 103.72, or if taking 365 days base (or standard convention): with leap year 366 days, 7300 * 5% * (104 / 365) = 104 exactly! Psychological Trap: A classic trap is forgetting that 2020 was a leap year with 29 days in February, or miscounting the first day. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 104, corresponding to option (B).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q23. (Easy) At simple interest, a sum becomes Rs 1,020 in 5 years and Rs 1,200 in 8 years. What is the annual rate of interest?
(A) 8.00%  (B) 7.50%  (C) 8.33%  (D) 9.00%

Answer: (C)

Solution:

Rate of interest R = (60 / 720) * 100 = 100 / 12 = 8.33% (or 8 1/3%). Therefore, the correct value is 8.33%, corresponding to option (C).

Q24. (Easy) What sum of money will amount to Rs 5,200 in 2 years at 6% simple interest per annum?
(A) Rs 4,600.00  (B) Rs 4,500.00  (C) Rs 4,700.00  (D) Rs 4,642.86

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Total interest percent for 2 years = 2 * 6% = 12%. Amount = Principal * (1 + 0.12) = 1.12 * P. Given 1.12 * P = 5200, we have P = 5200 / 1.12 = 520000 / 112 = 65000 / 14 = 32500 / 7 = Rs 4,642.86. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 4,642.86, corresponding to option (D).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern
