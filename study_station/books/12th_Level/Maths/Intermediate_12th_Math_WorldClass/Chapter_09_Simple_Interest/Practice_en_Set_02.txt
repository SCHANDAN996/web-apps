Q26. (Medium) A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become four times itself at the same rate?
(A) 24 years  (B) 32 years  (C) 16 years  (D) 20 years

Answer: (A)

Solution:
When a sum doubles, simple interest earned = (2 - 1) = 1 unit in 8 years. To become 4 times, simple interest required = (4 - 1) = 3 units. Since simple interest grows linearly with time, required time = 3 * 8 = 24 years. Psychological Trap: A frequent misconception is assuming simple interest behaves like compound interest and calculating 8 * 2 = 16 or 8 * 4 = 32 years. Therefore, the correct value is 24 years, corresponding to option (A).

Q27. (Medium) A sum of money invested at simple interest triples itself in 12 years. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(A) 16.00%  (B) 16.67%  (C) 15.50%  (D) 17.25%

Answer: (B)

Solution:
When a sum triples, the interest earned is SI = 3P - P = 2P. Rate R = (SI * 100) / (P * T) = (2P * 100) / (P * 12) = 200 / 12 = 50 / 3% = 16.67% (or 16 2/3%). Therefore, the correct value is 16.67%, corresponding to option (B).
