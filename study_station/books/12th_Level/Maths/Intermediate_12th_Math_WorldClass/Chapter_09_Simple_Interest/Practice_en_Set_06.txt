126. A sum of money at simple interest amounts to ₹2,240 in 2 years and ₹2,600 in 5 years. The sum is:
(a) ₹1,880 (b) ₹2,120 (c) ₹2,000 (d) ₹2,400
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is to take ₹2,240 as the principal, but that includes 2 years of interest. Interest for 3 years = 2,600 − 2,240 = ₹360, so interest per year = ₹120. Interest for 2 years = ₹240, hence principal = 2,240 − 240 = ₹2,000. So the answer is ₹2,000.
Source: PYQ-style

127. A sum was invested at simple interest at a certain rate for 3 years. Had it been invested at a rate 4% per annum higher, it would have fetched ₹600 more. The sum is:
(a) ₹4,000 (b) ₹4,500 (c) ₹5,000 (d) ₹6,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is dividing 600 by 4 to get 150 and stopping there. Extra interest = P × 4 × 3/100 = 600, so P = 600 × 100/12 = ₹5,000. So the answer is ₹5,000.
Source: PYQ-style

128. A person lent ₹10,000 in two parts, one at 8% and the other at 10% per annum simple interest. The total interest after 2 years was ₹1,760. The part lent at 8% was:
(a) ₹6,000 (b) ₹5,000 (c) ₹7,000 (d) ₹4,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is splitting equally, which gives interest 800 + 1,000 = ₹1,800, not ₹1,760. Let the 8% part be x: 16x/100 + 20(10,000 − x)/100 = 1,760, so 2,000 − 4x/100 = 1,760, giving x = ₹6,000. So the answer is ₹6,000.
Source: PYQ-style

129. A sum doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become four times itself at the same rate?
(a) 32 years (b) 20 years (c) 16 years (d) 24 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is doubling 8 to get 16, but "four times" means interest = 3P, not 2P. Interest of P takes 8 years, so interest of 3P takes 24 years. So the answer is 24 years.
Source: PYQ-style

130. The simple interest on a sum for 5 years is two-fifths of the sum. The rate of interest per annum is:
(a) 6% (b) 10% (c) 8% (d) 12.5%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is taking 2/5 as 40% and calling that the rate. SI = 2P/5, so P × R × 5/100 = 2P/5, giving R = 40/5 = 8%. So the answer is 8%.

131. A man borrowed ₹8,000 at 10% per annum simple interest and immediately lent the whole sum at 12% per annum simple interest. His gain after 4 years is:
(a) ₹480 (b) ₹560 (c) ₹720 (d) ₹640
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing the gain for one year only, which gives ₹160. Net gain rate = 12 − 10 = 2% per annum, so gain = 8,000 × 2 × 4/100 = ₹640. So the answer is ₹640.
Source: PYQ-style

132. A sum of ₹1,550 was lent partly at 5% and partly at 8% per annum simple interest. The total interest received after 3 years was ₹300. The ratio of money lent at 5% to that at 8% is:
(a) 5 : 8 (b) 16 : 15 (c) 15 : 16 (d) 8 : 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming equal parts, which gives interest of about ₹302, close but wrong. Let the 5% part be x: 15x/100 + 24(1,550 − x)/100 = 300, so 37,200 − 9x = 30,000, giving x = 800 and the other part 750; ratio 800 : 750 = 16 : 15. So the answer is 16 : 15.
Source: PYQ-style

133. The simple interest on a certain sum at 8% per annum for 6 years is ₹1,200 less than the simple interest on the same sum at 10% per annum for 6 years. The sum is:
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹9,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹12,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is using the rate difference of 2% for one year only. Difference in interest = P × (10 − 8) × 6/100 = 1,200, so P = 1,200 × 100/12 = ₹10,000. So the answer is ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

134. A sum of money becomes ₹4,200 in 4 years and ₹4,800 in 6 years at simple interest. The rate of interest per annum is:
(a) 15% (b) 8% (c) 12% (d) 10%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is taking ₹4,200 as the principal. Interest for 2 years = 4,800 − 4,200 = ₹600, so yearly interest = ₹300. Principal = 4,200 − 4 × 300 = ₹3,000, and rate = 300 × 100/3,000 = 10%. So the answer is 10%.
Source: PYQ-style

135. A person invests money in three schemes for 6 years, 10 years and 12 years at 10%, 12% and 15% simple interest respectively. At the end of each scheme he gets the same interest. The ratio of his investments is:
(a) 3 : 4 : 6 (b) 6 : 3 : 2 (c) 2 : 3 : 4 (d) 4 : 3 : 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is taking the ratio of the times or the rates directly. Equal interest means P₁ × 60 = P₂ × 120 = P₃ × 180 (rate × time), so P₁ : P₂ : P₃ = 1/60 : 1/120 : 1/180 = 6 : 3 : 2. So the answer is 6 : 3 : 2.
Source: PYQ-style

136. A sum of ₹12,000 amounts to ₹15,600 in 3 years at simple interest. If the rate of interest is increased by 3% per annum, the new amount after 3 years would be:
(a) ₹16,680 (b) ₹16,920 (c) ₹16,440 (d) ₹17,200
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding 3% of the amount instead of recomputing the interest. Original interest = ₹3,600, so rate = 3,600 × 100/(12,000 × 3) = 10%. New rate = 13%, new interest = 12,000 × 13 × 3/100 = ₹4,680, new amount = ₹16,680. So the answer is ₹16,680.
Source: PYQ-style

137. A man lent ₹5,000 for 2 years and ₹3,000 for 4 years at simple interest at the same rate and received ₹2,200 in all as interest. The rate of interest per annum is:
(a) 8% (b) 9% (c) 10% (d) 12%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is averaging the principals or the times. Total interest = (5,000 × 2 + 3,000 × 4) × R/100 = 22,000R/100 = 2,200, so R = 10%. So the answer is 10%.
Source: PYQ-style

138. A sum was put at simple interest at a certain rate for 4 years. Had it been put at 2% higher rate, it would have fetched ₹56 more. The sum is:

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dividing 56 by 2 to get 28 and building the answer on that. Extra interest = P × 2 × 4/100 = 56, so P = 56 × 100/8 = ₹700. So the answer is ₹700.

139. The simple interest on a sum of money is one-ninth of the principal, and the number of years is equal to the rate percent per annum. The rate per annum is:
(a) 3% (b) 3⅓% (c) 4% (d) 5%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is taking the square root of 9 as 3%. Given SI = P/9 and R = T: P × R × R/100 = P/9, so R² = 100/9 and R = 10/3 = 3⅓%. So the answer is 3⅓%.
Source: PYQ-style

140. A sum of ₹5,400 is divided into two parts such that the amount on the first part at 6% per annum for 5 years equals the amount on the second part at 8% per annum for 5 years, both at simple interest. The first part is:
(a) ₹2,600 (b) ₹2,800 (c) ₹2,700 (d) ₹3,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is dividing ₹5,400 equally. Equal amounts mean P₁ × 1.30 = P₂ × 1.40, so P₁ : P₂ = 14 : 13. First part = 5,400 × 14/27 = ₹2,800. So the answer is ₹2,800.
Source: PYQ-style

141. A man invested one-third of his capital at 7%, one-fourth at 8% and the remainder at 10% per annum simple interest. If his annual income from interest is ₹561, the capital is:
(a) ₹6,000 (b) ₹7,200 (c) ₹6,600 (d) ₹7,500
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is averaging the three rates to 8.33% directly. Let capital = 12k: the parts are 4k, 3k and 5k. Interest = (28 + 24 + 50)k/100 = 102k/100 = 561, so k = 550 and capital = 12 × 550 = ₹6,600. So the answer is ₹6,600.
Source: PYQ-style

142. A sum of money lent at simple interest amounts to ₹1,008 in 2 years and ₹1,164 in 3½ years. The rate of interest per annum is:

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking ₹1,008 as the principal. Interest for 1½ years = 1,164 − 1,008 = ₹156, so yearly interest = ₹104. Principal = 1,008 − 2 × 104 = ₹800, and rate = 104 × 100/800 = 13%. So the answer is 13%.

143. A person borrows ₹5,000 at 4% per annum and ₹4,000 at 5% per annum simple interest for the same period. He pays ₹1,200 as total interest. The period is:
(a) 2 years (b) 4 years (c) 3 years (d) 5 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing interest on ₹9,000 at a guessed average rate. Yearly interest = 5,000 × 4/100 + 4,000 × 5/100 = 200 + 200 = ₹400, so time = 1,200/400 = 3 years. So the answer is 3 years.

144. A sum of money becomes 7/5 of itself in 4 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 2 times itself at the same rate?
(a) 8 years (b) 10 years (c) 12 years (d) 14 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is scaling 4 years by 2/(7/5) directly. Interest in 4 years = 2P/5, so yearly interest = P/10. To double, interest needed = P, which takes 10 years. So the answer is 10 years.

145. Two equal sums were lent at 5% and 6% per annum simple interest. The interest on the first sum for 3 years equals the interest on the second sum for 2 years plus ₹90. Each sum is:
(a) ₹4,000 (b) ₹2,500 (c) ₹3,500 (d) ₹3,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is ignoring the ₹90 difference and equating the two interests. Let each sum be P: 15P/100 = 12P/100 + 90, so 3P/100 = 90, giving P = ₹3,000. So the answer is ₹3,000.

146. A man lent a sum at 12% per annum simple interest for some years and received an amount of ₹14,800. If he had lent it at 15% for the same period, he would have received ₹16,000. The sum and the period are:
(a) ₹10,000, 4 years (b) ₹12,000, 5 years (c) ₹10,000, 6 years (d) ₹12,000, 4 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating the difference ₹1,200 as one year's interest. Difference = P × 3 × T/100 = 1,200, so PT = 40,000. Also P + 12PT/100 = 14,800 gives P + 4,800 = 14,800, so P = ₹10,000 and T = 40,000/10,000 = 4 years. So the answer is ₹10,000, 4 years.
Source: PYQ-style

148. The rate of simple interest on a sum is 6% per annum for the first 3 years, 8% per annum for the next 4 years and 10% per annum for the period beyond 7 years. The total interest earned on ₹6,000 in 10 years is:
(a) ₹4,200 (b) ₹4,800 (c) ₹4,920 (d) ₹4,440

Solution: The trap is applying one average rate for all 10 years. Interest = 6,000 × (6 × 3 + 8 × 4 + 10 × 3)/100 = 6,000 × (18 + 32 + 30)/100 = 6,000 × 80/100 = ₹4,800. So the answer is ₹4,800.

Solution: The trap is scaling 15 by 5/3 to get 25. Trebling means interest = 2P in 15 years, so interest of P takes 7.5 years. Becoming 5 times needs interest = 4P, taking 30 years. So the answer is 30 years.

150. A man deposited ₹8,000 in a bank at 5% per annum simple interest. After 3 years he withdrew the whole amount and deposited it in another bank at 8% per annum simple interest. His total interest earned after 6 years from the start is:
(a) ₹3,360 (b) ₹3,600 (c) ₹4,080 (d) ₹3,408
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 8% on the original ₹8,000 for the second period. First interest = 8,000 × 5 × 3/100 = ₹1,200, so the new principal = ₹9,200. Second interest = 9,200 × 8 × 3/100 = ₹2,208, and total interest = 1,200 + 2,208 = ₹3,408. So the answer is ₹3,408.
Source: PYQ-style
