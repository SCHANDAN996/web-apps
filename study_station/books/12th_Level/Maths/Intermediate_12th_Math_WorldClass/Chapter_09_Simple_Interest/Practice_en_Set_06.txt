Q126. (Hard) A person lent a certain sum of money at 4% simple interest per annum, and in 8 years the interest amounted to Rs 340 less than the sum lent. Find the sum lent.
(A) Rs 500  (B) Rs 480  (C) Rs 520  (D) Rs 550

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Let the sum lent be P. Simple interest for 8 years at 4% = (P * 4 * 8) / 100 = 0.32 * P. Given P - 0.32 * P = 340 => 0.68 * P = 340 => P = 340 / 0.68 = Rs 500. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 500, corresponding to option (A).
