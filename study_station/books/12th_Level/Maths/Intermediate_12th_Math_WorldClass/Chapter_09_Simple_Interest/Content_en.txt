# Chapter 09: Simple Interest (Comprehensive Intermediate / 12th Level Foundation)

## 1. Fundamentals of Simple Interest
Simple Interest (SI) is the fixed rental fee charged on the original principal sum borrowed or invested over a duration of time. Unlike compound interest, the principal remains invariant across all compounding periods.
- **Principal (P):** The capital sum borrowed, lent, or invested.
- **Rate of Interest (R):** The percentage of the principal charged per unit time (usually per annum, p.a.).
- **Time Period (T):** The duration for which the sum is invested (measured in years).
  - If time is given in months: T = (number of months) / 12.
  - If time is given in days: T = (number of days) / 365 (standard year).
- **Basic Formula:**
  SI = (P * R * T) / 100
- **Total Amount (A):**
  A = P + SI = P + (P * R * T) / 100 = P * [ 1 + (R * T) / 100 ]

## 2. Dynamic Rates over Multi-Year Intervals
When a capital sum is subjected to varying interest rates over consecutive time frames:
- Let a principal P be lent at r_1% p.a. for t_1 years, r_2% p.a. for t_2 years, and r_3% p.a. for t_3 years:
  Total SI = (P / 100) * [ (r_1 * t_1) + (r_2 * t_2) + (r_3 * t_3) ]
  Effective Single Rate R_eff = [ (r_1 * t_1) + (r_2 * t_2) + (r_3 * t_3) ] / (t_1 + t_2 + t_3)

## 3. Multiplicity Principles (Doubling, Tripling, n-fold Sums)
- **Time to become 'n' times:**
  If a principal P amounts to n * P in T years at R% p.a.:
  SI = A - P = nP - P = (n - 1)P
  Since SI = (P * R * T) / 100:
  (n - 1)P = (P * R * T) / 100 => R * T = 100 * (n - 1)
  - Time required: T = [ 100 * (n - 1) ] / R
  - Rate required: R = [ 100 * (n - 1) ] / T
- **Two Different Periods Comparison:**
  If a sum becomes n_1 times in T_1 years and n_2 times in T_2 years at the same rate of simple interest:
  (n_1 - 1) / T_1 = (n_2 - 1) / T_2

## 4. Division of Capital into Multiple Investment Channels
- **Case 1: Equal Interests earned across parts:**
  A sum P is split into parts P_1, P_2, P_3 invested at (R_1, T_1), (R_2, T_2), (R_3, T_3):
  P_1 * R_1 * T_1 = P_2 * R_2 * T_2 = P_3 * R_3 * T_3
  Ratio of parts: P_1 : P_2 : P_3 = [ 1 / (R_1 * T_1) ] : [ 1 / (R_2 * T_2) ] : [ 1 / (R_3 * T_3) ]
- **Case 2: Equal Maturity Amounts across parts:**
  P_1 * [ 100 + (R_1 * T_1) ] = P_2 * [ 100 + (R_2 * T_2) ] = P_3 * [ 100 + (R_3 * T_3) ]
  Ratio of parts: P_1 : P_2 : P_3 = [ 1 / (100 + R_1 * T_1) ] : [ 1 / (100 + R_2 * T_2) ] : [ 1 / (100 + R_3 * T_3) ]

## 5. Equal Annual Installments in Simple Interest
When a loan debt of Rs D due after T years is discharged in T equal annual installments of Rs x each at R% p.a. simple interest:
- Each installment paid earns simple interest until the maturity date:
  D = [ x + x*(R*(T-1))/100 ] + [ x + x*(R*(T-2))/100 ] + ... + x
  D = x * T + (x * R / 100) * [ (T - 1) + (T - 2) + ... + 1 ]
  D = x * T + (x * R * T * (T - 1)) / 200
- **Annual Installment Formula:**
  x = (100 * D) / [ 100 * T + (R * T * (T - 1)) / 2 ]

## 6. Alligation Method for Split Interest Investments
When a total capital P is divided into two loans at rates R_1% and R_2%, producing an overall average interest rate of R_m%:
- Ratio of the two investments:
  P_1 : P_2 = | R_2 - R_m | : | R_m - R_1 |

## 7. True Discount (TD) & Banker's Discount (BD)
- **Present Worth (PW):** The sum of money that, with interest at R% for T years, will amount to the Sum Due (SD).
- **Sum Due (SD) = PW + TD**
- **True Discount (TD):** Simple interest on Present Worth:
  TD = (PW * R * T) / 100 = (SD * R * T) / (100 + R * T)
- **Banker's Discount (BD):** Simple interest on the Face Value (Sum Due):
  BD = (SD * R * T) / 100
- **Banker's Gain (BG):**
  BG = BD - TD = Simple Interest on TD = (TD^2) / PW = (TD * R * T) / 100
- Key relation: PW = (TD * BD) / (BD - TD) = (TD * BD) / BG
