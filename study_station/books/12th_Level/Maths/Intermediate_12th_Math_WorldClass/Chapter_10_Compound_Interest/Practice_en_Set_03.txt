51. The difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹4000 for 2 years at 10% per annum is:
(a) ₹80 (b) ₹30 (c) ₹50 (d) ₹40
Answer: (d)
Solution: For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)² = 4000 × (10/100)² = ₹40. So the answer is ₹40.
Source: PYQ-style

52. At what rate per cent per annum will ₹2000 amount to ₹2420 in 2 years at compound interest?
(a) 9% (b) 10% (c) 20% (d) 12%
Answer: (b)
Solution: (1 + r/100)² = 2420/2000 = 121/100, so 1 + r/100 = 11/10 and r = 10%. So the answer is 10%.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Find the compound interest on ₹20,000 for 1 year at 10% per annum, compounded half-yearly.
(a) ₹2050 (b) ₹4100 (c) ₹2150 (d) ₹2000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Half-yearly: rate 5% per half-year for 2 half-years. Amount = 20,000 × (1 + 5/100)² = ₹22,050, so CI = ₹2050. The trap is the yearly figure ₹2000. So the answer is ₹2050.
