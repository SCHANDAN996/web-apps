# PYQ Analysis — Compound Interest

## 1. How Often It Appears

Compound interest appears regularly in SSC CGL/CHSL, banking (IBPS/SBI PO and Clerk), railway and state exams. Most papers carry one or two questions; bank papers often pair it with simple interest. Exact yearly counts vary, so this analysis describes the pattern rather than quoting numbers.

## 2. Sub-topic Weightage (qualitative)

- **CI − SI difference (2 and 3 years)** — very common in SSC.
- **Amount and interest for 2–3 years** — common everywhere.
- **Half-yearly and quarterly compounding** — common in banking.
- **Finding rate or time** — moderate.
- **Population growth and depreciation** — common in SSC and railway.

## 3. Ten High-Yield Questions (PYQ-style, with solutions)

1. CI on ₹8,000 at 5% for 2 years → A = 8000 × 1.1025 = ₹8,820 → **CI ₹820**. Hook: SI option ₹800.
2. CI − SI on ₹5,000 at 10% for 2 years → 5000 × 0.01 = **₹50**.
3. CI − SI on ₹8,000 at 5% for 3 years → 8000 × 0.0025 × 3.05 = **₹61**.
4. ₹10,000 at 20% for 1 year compounded half-yearly → 10% × 2 → **CI ₹2,100**. Hook: ₹2,000.
5. ₹6,250 becomes ₹7,840 in 2 years → **R = 12%**.
6. Population 50,000 grows 10% a year → after 2 years **60,500**.
7. Machine worth ₹40,000 depreciates 10% a year → after 2 years **₹32,400**. Hook: ₹32,000 (simple).
8. A sum doubles in 5 years → becomes 8 times in **15 years**.
9. Rates 10% and 20% in two years on ₹10,000 → 10000 × 1.1 × 1.2 = **₹13,200**.
10. ₹1,000 at 10% for 3 years → A = **₹1,331**, CI ₹331. Hook: amount and interest both in options.

## 4. Skip Guidance (15-second / 45-second rule)

- Questions 2, 3 and 8 should take under 15 seconds with shortcuts.
- Question 5 type with awkward numbers: if the root is not obvious in 45 seconds, mark and return.

## 5. Examiner's Traps Seen Again and Again

1. Simple-interest value placed as an option.
2. Yearly rate used for half-yearly compounding.
3. Amount given as an option when interest is asked.
