76. What is the compound interest on ₹8000 for 2 years at 20% per annum, compounded annually?
(a) ₹11,520 (b) ₹3720 (c) ₹3320 (d) ₹3520
Answer: (d)
Solution: Amount = 8000 × (1 + 20/100)^2 = ₹11,520. CI = amount − principal = ₹3520. The trap is simple interest ₹3200. So the answer is ₹3520.
Source: PYQ-style

77. At what rate per cent per annum will ₹10,000 amount to ₹14,400 in 2 years at compound interest?
(a) 19% (b) 20% (c) 22% (d) 40%
Answer: (b)
Solution: (1 + r/100)² = 14,400/10,000 = 36/25, so 1 + r/100 = 6/5 and r = 20%. So the answer is 20%.
Source: PYQ-style
