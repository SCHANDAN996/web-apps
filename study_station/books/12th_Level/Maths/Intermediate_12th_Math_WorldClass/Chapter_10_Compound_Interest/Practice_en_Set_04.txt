Questions 76–100: Medium

76. (Medium) A sum of money placed at compound interest doubles itself in 4 years. In how many years will it amount to 8 times itself at the same rate?
(A) 12 years  (B) 16 years  (C) 8 years  (D) 10 years
Answer: (A) 12 years
Solution: Under compound interest, growth scales exponentially. Given 2 = (1 + R/100)^4. To become 8 times: 8 = 2^3 = [(1 + R/100)^4]^3 = (1 + R/100)^12. Therefore, required time = 3 * 4 = 12 years. The required value is 12 years. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

80. (Medium) Find the compound interest on Rs 12,000 for 2.5 years at 10% per annum, compounded annually.
(A) Rs 3,246  (B) Rs 3,200  (C) Rs 3,300  (D) Rs 3,180
Answer: (A) Rs 3,246
Solution: Amount = 12000 * (1 + 10/100)^2 * [1 + (0.5 * 10)/100] = 12000 * (1.1)^2 * 1.05 = 12000 * 1.21 * 1.05 = 12000 * 1.2705 = Rs 15,246. Compound interest = 15246 - 12000 = Rs 3,246. The required value is Rs 3,246. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

82. (Medium) Find the compound interest on Rs 31,250 for 2 years at 12% per annum, compounded every 8 months.
(A) Rs 8,100  (B) Rs 8,150  (C) Rs 8,116  (D) Rs 8,080
Answer: (C) Rs 8,116
Solution: Rate per 8 months = 12 * (8/12) = 8%. Total conversion periods = 24 / 8 = 3. Amount = 31250 * (1 + 8/100)^3 = 31250 * (27/25)^3 = 31250 * (19683 / 15625) = 2 * 19683 = Rs 39,366. Compound interest = 39366 - 31250 = Rs 8,116. The required value is Rs 8,116. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

84. (Medium) A sum of money becomes 8 times itself in 3 years at compound interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(A) 100%  (B) 50%  (C) 75%  (D) 200%
Answer: (A) 100%
Solution: A / P = (1 + R/100)^3 => 8 = (1 + R/100)^3. Taking cube root: 2 = 1 + R/100 => R/100 = 1 => R = 100% per annum. The required value is 100%. Hence, the correct option is (A).

86. (Medium) In how many years will a sum of money approximately double itself at 10% compound interest per annum?
(A) 6.5 years  (B) 8.0 years  (C) 7.3 years  (D) 7.0 years
Answer: (C) 7.3 years
Solution: Using the logarithmic formulation: (1.1)^T = 2 => T * log(1.1) = log(2) => T = 0.30103 / 0.04139 = 7.27 ≈ 7.3 years (or using Rule of 72: 72 / 10 = 7.2 years). The required value is 7.3 years. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

99. (Medium) A sum of money amounts to Rs 1,210 in 2 years and to Rs 1,331 in 3 years at compound interest. Find the principal sum.
(A) Rs 900  (B) Rs 1,100  (C) Rs 950  (D) Rs 1,000
Answer: (D) Rs 1,000
Solution: Interest in 3rd year = 1331 - 1210 = Rs 121. Rate R = (121 / 1210) * 100 = 10%. Using Amount at year 2: P * (1 + 10/100)^2 = 1210 => P * 1.21 = 1210 => P = Rs 1,000. The required value is Rs 1,000. Hence, the correct option is (D).
