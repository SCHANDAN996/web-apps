# Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Compound Interest

**Box 1 — "PRN-A"**
> Principal, Rate, n years → Amount = P (1 + R/100)ⁿ.

**Box 2 — "Snowball on snowball"**
> Interest earns interest — that is why CI beats SI after year 1.

**Box 3 — "Half the rate, double the turns"**
> Half-yearly: R/2 and 2n. Quarterly: R/4 and 4n.

**Box 4 — "Square the rate for two"**
> CI − SI for 2 years = P × (R/100)².

**Box 5 — "Three plus for three"**
> CI − SI for 3 years = P × (R/100)² × (3 + R/100).

**Box 6 — "Double, double again"**
> Doubles in n years → 4 times in 2n → 8 times in 3n.

**Box 7 — "Grow with plus, shrink with minus"**
> Population: (1 + R/100)ⁿ. Depreciation: (1 − R/100)ⁿ.

**Box 8 — "Fraction rates are friendlier"**
> 10% = 1/10, 20% = 1/5, 25% = 1/4, 12.5% = 1/8. Multiply by 11/10, 6/5, 5/4, 9/8.

**Box 9 — "Successive rates multiply"**
> 10% then 20% → × 1.1 × 1.2 = × 1.32.

**Box 10 — "Know 1.21, 1.331, 1.44, 1.728"**
> Powers of 1.1 and 1.2 save long multiplication.

## Skip Strategy

1. **15 seconds:** CI − SI and 2-year amounts — use the shortcuts directly.
2. **45 seconds:** rate or time with awkward numbers — mark and return.
3. **Options first:** strike out the simple-interest value and the full amount when the question asks for interest.
