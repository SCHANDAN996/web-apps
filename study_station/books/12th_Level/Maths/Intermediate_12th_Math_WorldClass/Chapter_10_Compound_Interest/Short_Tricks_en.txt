# Short Tricks & Mental Hacks: Compound Interest

### Trick 1: The '2 : 1' Fast 2-Year CI Code
Instead of messy fractions:
- A = R% of P.
- B = R% of A.
- Total CI = 2*A + B.
- CI - SI difference = B.

### Trick 2: The '3 : 3 : 1' Fast 3-Year CI Code
- A = R% of P.
- B = R% of A.
- C = R% of B.
- Total CI = 3*A + 3*B + C.
- CI - SI difference = 3*B + C.

### Trick 3: Instant Rate Recovery from 3-yr / 2-yr Difference Ratio
When both 3-year difference D_3 and 2-year difference D_2 are given:
- D_3 / D_2 = 3 + (R / 100).
- Subtract 3, then multiply remainder by 100 to get R% instantly in 3 seconds!

### Trick 4: The 10% Golden Table
Memorize effective rates for 10% p.a.:
- 1 Year: 10%
- 2 Years: 21%
- 3 Years: 33.1%
- 4 Years: 46.41%

### Trick 5: Power Matching for Multiplier Problems
If a sum doubles in 3 years:
To become 16 times: 16 = 2^4.
Multiply the original time by the power: 3 * 4 = 12 years!
