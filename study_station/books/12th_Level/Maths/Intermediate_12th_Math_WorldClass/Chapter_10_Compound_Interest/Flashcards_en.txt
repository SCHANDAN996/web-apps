# Flashcards: Compound Interest

CARD 1:
Front: What is the primary difference between Simple Interest and Compound Interest?
Back: In Simple Interest, principal remains constant. In Compound Interest, interest from previous periods is added to the principal to earn interest in subsequent periods.

CARD 2:
Front: Write the formula for the difference between CI and SI for 2 years at R% p.a.
Back: Difference D_2 = P * (R / 100)^2 = (P * R^2) / 10,000.

CARD 3:
Front: Write the formula for the difference between CI and SI for 3 years at R% p.a.
Back: Difference D_3 = [ P * R^2 * (300 + R) ] / 10^6.

CARD 4:
Front: What is the ratio of 3-year CI-SI difference to 2-year CI-SI difference?
Back: D_3 / D_2 = (300 + R) / 100 = 3 + (R / 100).

CARD 5:
Front: If interest is compounded half-yearly, how are Rate and Time adjusted?
Back: Rate is halved (R / 2)% and Time is doubled (2T conversion periods).

CARD 6:
Front: If interest is compounded quarterly, how are Rate and Time adjusted?
Back: Rate is divided by 4 (R / 4)% and Time is multiplied by 4 (4T periods).

CARD 7:
Front: What are the Pascal tree ratios for 2-year and 3-year Compound Interest?
Back: 2 years: 2 : 1 (CI = 2A + B). 3 years: 3 : 3 : 1 (CI = 3A + 3B + C).

CARD 8:
Front: If a sum becomes 'k' times in T years at CI, in how many years will it become k^3 times?
Back: In 3T years (powers of multiplier correspond to multiples of time).

CARD 9:
Front: What is the effective annual rate for 10% p.a. compounded half-yearly for 1 year?
Back: Half-yearly rate = 5%. Effective rate = 5 + 5 + (5*5)/100 = 10.25%.

CARD 10:
Front: What is the Rule of 72?
Back: An approximation for money doubling time under compound interest: T_double ≈ 72 / R.

CARD 11:
Front: What is the formula for equal annual installment 'x' to clear debt 'P' in 2 years at R% CI?
Back: P = [ x / (1 + R/100) ] + [ x / (1 + R/100)^2 ].

CARD 12:
Front: What is the mnemonic code for 3-year CI calculation?
Back: 3 : 3 : 1, where A = P*R%, B = A*R%, C = B*R%, giving CI = 3A + 3B + C.
