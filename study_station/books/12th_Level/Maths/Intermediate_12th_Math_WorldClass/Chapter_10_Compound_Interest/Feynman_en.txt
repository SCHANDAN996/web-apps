# The Feynman Technique: Compound Interest

### The Magic Snowball on the Mountain Peak
Imagine you are rolling a tiny snowball down a snowy mountain.
In simple interest, every single second a helpful friend places 10 grams of fresh snow on your snowball. It grows by the exact same 10 grams every second — nice and steady, but slow.

In compound interest, something magical happens!
As the snowball rolls, its own sticky body gathers fresh snow proportional to its current size.
When it is small, it picks up just a pinch. But as it grows larger, its giant surface area sweeps up massive chunks of snow!
The interest earned in year 1 becomes part of the capital in year 2, so it now earns interest on itself!

### The Two Golden Tree Codes
- For 2 years, remember the code **2 : 1**.
  Calculate standard interest on principal (A), then calculate interest on that interest (B). Total is 2A + B.
- For 3 years, remember the code **3 : 3 : 1**.
  Total is 3A + 3B + C.
The difference between CI and SI is simply everything except the first column!
For 2 years, the difference is just B.
For 3 years, the difference is 3B + C.
In five seconds, the hardest exam problem dissolves into child's play!
