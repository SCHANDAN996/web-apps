# Chapter 10: Compound Interest (Intermediate / 12th Level)

## 1. Fundamentals of Compound Interest
Compound Interest (CI) is the interest calculated on the initial principal and also on the accumulated interest of previous periods. Often called "interest on interest", it represents exponential monetary growth.
- **Principal (P):** The initial sum borrowed or invested.
- **Rate of Interest (R):** The annual rate percent charged.
- **Time Period (T or n):** The total duration of the investment.
- **Conversion Frequency (m):** The number of compounding intervals per year.
  - Annual: m = 1, Rate = R%, Time = T years.
  - Semi-annual (Half-yearly): m = 2, Rate = R/2%, Time = 2T conversion periods.
  - Quarterly: m = 4, Rate = R/4%, Time = 4T conversion periods.
  - Monthly: m = 12, Rate = R/12%, Time = 12T conversion periods.
- **Master Amount Formula:**
  A = P * [ 1 + R / (100 * m) ]^(m * T)
- **Compound Interest:**
  CI = Amount - Principal = P * [ (1 + R/100)^T - 1 ]

## 2. The Successive Percentage Method
For two compounding cycles at annual rate r%:
- Net effective compound rate R_eff = r + r + (r * r) / 100 = 2r + (r^2 / 100)%.
- For differing successive rates r_1% and r_2%:
  R_eff = r_1 + r_2 + (r_1 * r_2) / 100 %.
- For three consecutive years (r_1, r_2, r_3):
  R_eff = (r_1 + r_2 + r_3) + [ (r_1*r_2 + r_2*r_3 + r_3*r_1) / 100 ] + [ (r_1 * r_2 * r_3) / 10000 ] %.

## 3. The Tree Method (Pascal's Line Method)
Instead of calculating powers of fractions, resolve interest layer by layer:
- **2-Year Structure (2 : 1 Ratio):**
  - First term A = R% of P (earned twice, in year 1 and year 2).
  - Second term B = R% of A (interest on year 1's interest).
  - Total CI = 2*A + B.
  - Total SI = 2*A.
  - Difference (CI - SI) = B.
- **3-Year Structure (3 : 3 : 1 Ratio):**
  - First term A = R% of P.
  - Second term B = R% of A.
  - Third term C = R% of B.
  - Total CI = 3*A + 3*B + C.
  - Total SI = 3*A.
  - Difference (CI - SI) for 3 years = 3*B + C.

## 4. Analytical Differences Between CI and SI
- **2-Year Difference Formula:**
  D_2 = CI_2 - SI_2 = P * (R / 100)^2 = (P * R^2) / 10000.
- **3-Year Difference Formula:**
  D_3 = CI_3 - SI_3 = P * (R / 100)^2 * [ (300 + R) / 100 ] = [ P * R^2 * (300 + R) ] / 10^6.
- **Ratio of 3-Year to 2-Year Difference:**
  D_3 / D_2 = (300 + R) / 100 = 3 + (R / 100).

## 5. Multiplicity Rules in Exponential Growth
Unlike simple interest where principal growth is linear, compound interest scales powers of multipliers:
- If a capital becomes 'k' times itself in 'T' years:
  - In 2T years, it amounts to k^2 * P.
  - In 3T years, it amounts to k^3 * P.
  - In m*T years, it amounts to k^m * P.
- **Doubling Rule (Rule of 72):**
  Approximate doubling time T_double ≈ 72 / R years.

## 6. Equal Annual Installments (EMI under CI)
When a borrowed debt P is cleared in 'n' equal annual installments of Rs 'x' at R% p.a. compound interest:
- Each installment's present discounted value equals its contribution to loan discharge:
  P = [ x / (1 + R/100) ] + [ x / (1 + R/100)^2 ] + ... + [ x / (1 + R/100)^n ]
- For 2 equal annual installments:
  P = [ x / (1 + R/100) ] * [ 1 + 1 / (1 + R/100) ]
