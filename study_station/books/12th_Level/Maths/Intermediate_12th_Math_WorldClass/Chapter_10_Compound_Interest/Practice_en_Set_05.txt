101. What is the compound interest on ₹20,000 for 2 years at 4% per annum, compounded annually?
(a) ₹1592 (b) ₹1632 (c) ₹1672 (d) ₹21,632
Answer: (b)
Solution: Amount = 20,000 × (1 + 4/100)^2 = ₹21,632. CI = amount − principal = ₹1632. The trap is simple interest ₹1600. So the answer is ₹1632.
Source: PYQ-style

102. At what rate per cent per annum will ₹2000 amount to ₹2880 in 2 years at compound interest?
(a) 22% (b) 20% (c) 21% (d) 19%
Answer: (b)
Solution: (1 + r/100)² = 2880/2000 = 36/25, so 1 + r/100 = 6/5 and r = 20%. So the answer is 20%.
