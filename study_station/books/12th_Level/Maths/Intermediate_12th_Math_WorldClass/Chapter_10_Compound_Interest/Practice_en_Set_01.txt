Questions 1–25: Easy

1. (Easy) Find the compound interest on Rs 10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually.
(A) Rs 2,100  (B) Rs 2,000  (C) Rs 2,200  (D) Rs 2,150
Answer: (A) Rs 2,100
Solution: Amount A = P * (1 + R/100)^T = 10000 * (1 + 10/100)^2 = 10000 * 1.21 = Rs 12,100. Compound interest CI = Amount - Principal = 12100 - 10000 = Rs 2,100. The required value is Rs 2,100. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. (Easy) What will a principal of Rs 8,000 amount to in 2 years at 5% per annum compound interest?
(A) Rs 8,800  (B) Rs 8,820  (C) Rs 8,850  (D) Rs 8,750
Answer: (B) Rs 8,820
Solution: Amount A = P * (1 + R/100)^T = 8000 * (1 + 5/100)^2 = 8000 * (21/20)^2 = 8000 * (441 / 400) = 20 * 441 = Rs 8,820. The required value is Rs 8,820. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

3. (Easy) Find the compound interest on Rs 5,000 for 3 years at 10% per annum, compounded annually.
(A) Rs 1,650  (B) Rs 1,600  (C) Rs 1,655  (D) Rs 1,705
Answer: (C) Rs 1,655
Solution: Using the tree ratio 3 : 3 : 1 for 3 years: First tier A = 10% of 5000 = Rs 500. Second tier B = 10% of 500 = Rs 50. Third tier C = 10% of 50 = Rs 5. Total CI = 3*A + 3*B + C = 3(500) + 3(50) + 5 = 1500 + 150 + 5 = Rs 1,655. The required value is Rs 1,655. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

4. (Easy) Find the compound interest on Rs 16,000 for 1 year at 10% per annum, compounded half-yearly.

Answer: (D) Rs 1,640
Solution: For half-yearly compounding: Rate r = 10/2 = 5% per period; Time periods n = 1 * 2 = 2 half-years. Amount = 16000 * (1 + 5/100)^2 = 16000 * (441 / 400) = 40 * 441 = Rs 17,640. Compound interest = 17640 - 16000 = Rs 1,640. The required value is Rs 1,640. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

23. (Medium) A sum of money at compound interest amounts to Rs 4,840 in 2 years and to Rs 5,324 in 3 years. Find the rate of interest per annum.
(A) 9%  (B) 11%  (C) 10%  (D) 8%
Answer: (C) 10%
Solution: The increase in amount from year 2 to year 3 is simply one year's compound interest on the amount at the end of year 2: Interest earned = 5324 - 4840 = Rs 484. Rate R = (Interest * 100) / Principal = (484 * 100) / 4840 = 10% per annum. The required value is 10%. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

25. (Medium) If the compound interest on a sum for 2 years at 12.5% per annum is Rs 510, find the simple interest on the same sum at the same rate and time.
(A) Rs 450  (B) Rs 480  (C) Rs 500  (D) Rs 460
Answer: (B) Rs 480
Solution: Fraction equivalent of 12.5% is 1/8. Let Principal = 8^2 = 64 units. Simple interest for 2 years = 8 + 8 = 16 units. Compound interest for 2 years = 8 + 8 + 1 = 17 units. Given 17 units = Rs 510 => 1 unit = Rs 30. Simple Interest = 16 * 30 = Rs 480. The required value is Rs 480. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
