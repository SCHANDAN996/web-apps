1. The difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹20,000 for 2 years at 15% per annum is:
(a) ₹1350 (b) ₹450 (c) ₹900 (d) ₹465
Answer: (b)
Solution: For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)² = 20,000 × (15/100)² = ₹450. So the answer is ₹450.
Source: PYQ-style

2. The population of a town is 125,000 now. It increases at 10% per year. What will it be after 2 years?
(a) 151,250 (b) 152,500 (c) 125,000 (d) 148,750
Answer: (a)
Solution: Value after 2 years = 125,000 × (1 + 10/100)² = 151,250. The trap is the simple-interest style change: 150,000. So the answer is 151,250.
Source: PYQ-style
