Questions 26–50: Medium

26. What is the compound interest on ₹20,000 for 2 years at 20% per annum, compounded annually?
(a) ₹8800 (b) ₹28,800 (c) ₹9000 (d) ₹8000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amount = 20,000 × (1 + 20/100)^2 = ₹28,800. CI = amount − principal = ₹8800. The trap is simple interest ₹8000. So the answer is ₹8800.
Source: PYQ-style

27. The difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹12,500 for 2 years at 8% per annum is:
(a) ₹72 (b) ₹240 (c) ₹80 (d) ₹160
Answer: (c)
Solution: For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)² = 12,500 × (8/100)² = ₹80. So the answer is ₹80.
Source: PYQ-style

28. At what rate per cent per annum will ₹1000 amount to ₹1440 in 2 years at compound interest?
(a) 21% (b) 22% (c) 19% (d) 20%
Answer: (d)
Solution: (1 + r/100)² = 1440/1000 = 36/25, so 1 + r/100 = 6/5 and r = 20%. So the answer is 20%.
Source: PYQ-style
