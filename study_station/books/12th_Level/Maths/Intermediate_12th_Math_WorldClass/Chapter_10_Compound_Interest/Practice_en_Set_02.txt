Questions 26–50: Medium

26. (Medium) The difference between compound interest and simple interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is Rs 65. Find the sum.
(A) Rs 6,500  (B) Rs 6,000  (C) Rs 7,000  (D) Rs 6,250
Answer: (A) Rs 6,500
Solution: Difference D = P * (R / 100)^2. Here D = 65, R = 10: 65 = P * (10 / 100)^2 = P * (1 / 100) => P = 65 * 100 = Rs 6,500. The required value is Rs 6,500. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. (Medium) The difference between compound interest and simple interest on a sum of Rs 15,000 for 2 years is Rs 96. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(A) 6%  (B) 8%  (C) 10%  (D) 7.5%
Answer: (B) 8%
Solution: Using D = P * (R / 100)^2: 96 = 15000 * (R / 100)^2 => (R / 100)^2 = 96 / 15000 = 64 / 10000 => R / 100 = 8 / 100 => R = 8% per annum. The required value is 8%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

29. (Medium) The simple interest on a certain sum of money for 2 years at 8% per annum is Rs 400. What is the compound interest on the same sum at the same rate and for the same time?
(A) Rs 420  (B) Rs 412  (C) Rs 424  (D) Rs 416
Answer: (D) Rs 416
Solution: Simple interest per year = 400 / 2 = Rs 200. In the second year, compound interest adds interest on the first year's interest: Additional interest = 8% of 200 = Rs 16. Total compound interest = 400 + 16 = Rs 416. The required value is Rs 416. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. (Medium) The compound interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 4% per annum is Rs 102. Find the simple interest on the same sum at the same rate and time.
(A) Rs 100  (B) Rs 101  (C) Rs 98  (D) Rs 105
Answer: (A) Rs 100
Solution: Effective CI rate for 2 years at 4% = 4 + 4 + (4*4)/100 = 8.16%. Effective SI rate = 4 * 2 = 8%. Ratio CI / SI = 8.16 / 8 = 1.02. Given CI = 102: Simple Interest = 102 / 1.02 = Rs 100. The required value is Rs 100. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

31. (Medium) A sum of money becomes Rs 1,331 in 3 years and Rs 1,464.10 in 4 years at compound interest compounded annually. Find the rate of interest per annum.

Answer: (B) 10%
Solution: Interest earned in the 4th year = 1464.10 - 1331 = Rs 133.10. Rate R = (Interest * 100) / Amount_at_year_3 = (133.10 * 100) / 1331 = 10% per annum. The required value is 10%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

32. (Medium) A sum of Rs 3,200 invested at 10% per annum compounded quarterly amounts to Rs 3,362. Find the time period.
(A) 1 year  (B) 9 months  (C) 6 months  (D) 3 months
Answer: (C) 6 months
Solution: Quarterly rate = 10 / 4 = 2.5% = 1/40. A / P = 3362 / 3200 = 1681 / 1600 = (41 / 40)^2. Since base matches (1 + 1/40)^n = (41/40)^2, number of quarters n = 2. Time in months = 2 * 3 = 6 months. The required value is 6 months. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

33. (Medium) Find the compound interest on Rs 50,000 for 2 years, if the rate of interest for the first year is 8% and for the second year is 9% per annum.

Answer: (D) Rs 8,860
Solution: Net effective compound rate = 8 + 9 + (8 * 9)/100 = 17 + 0.72 = 17.72%. Compound Interest = 50000 * (17.72 / 100) = 500 * 17.72 = Rs 8,860. The required value is Rs 8,860. Hence, the correct option is (D).

34. (Medium) What is the difference between compound interest on Rs 10,000 for 1 year at 20% per annum compounded annually and compounded semi-annually?
(A) Rs 100  (B) Rs 120  (C) Rs 80  (D) Rs 150
Answer: (A) Rs 100
Solution: Annually: CI = 20% of 10000 = Rs 2,000. Semi-annually: Rate = 10% per half-year, 2 periods. Effective rate = 10 + 10 + (10*10)/100 = 21%. CI = 21% of 10000 = Rs 2,100. Difference = 2100 - 2000 = Rs 100. The required value is Rs 100. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

47. (Medium) A sum of money invested at compound interest amounts to Rs 4,500 in 2 years and to Rs 6,750 in 4 years. What is the principal sum?
(A) Rs 3,200  (B) Rs 3,000  (C) Rs 2,800  (D) Rs 3,500
Answer: (B) Rs 3,000
Solution: A2 = P * (1 + R/100)^2 = 4500 and A4 = P * (1 + R/100)^4 = 6750. Dividing gives (1 + R/100)^2 = 6750 / 4500 = 3 / 2. Substituting back into A2: P * (3/2) = 4500 => P = (4500 * 2) / 3 = Rs 3,000. The required value is Rs 3,000. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

50. (Medium) A customer deposits Rs 1,600 each on 1st January and 1st July of a year in a bank offering 10% compound interest per annum compounded half-yearly. At the end of the year, find the total interest earned.
(A) Rs 240  (B) Rs 244  (C) Rs 250  (D) Rs 248
Answer: (B) Rs 244
Solution: Half-yearly rate = 10 / 2 = 5%. The first deposit of Rs 1,600 stays for 2 half-years: Amount_1 = 1600 * (1.05)^2 = 1600 * 1.1025 = Rs 1,764. The second deposit of Rs 1,600 stays for 1 half-year: Amount_2 = 1600 * 1.05 = Rs 1,680. Total maturity value = 1764 + 1680 = Rs 3,444. Total principal deposited = 1600 + 1600 = Rs 3,200. Total interest earned = 3444 - 3200 = Rs 244. The required value is Rs 244. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
