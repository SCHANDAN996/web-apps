126. The difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹10,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum is:
(a) ₹100 (b) ₹110 (c) ₹90 (d) ₹300
Answer: (a)
Solution: For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)² = 10,000 × (10/100)² = ₹100. So the answer is ₹100.
Source: PYQ-style

127. At what rate per cent per annum will ₹10,000 amount to ₹15,625 in 2 years at compound interest?
(a) 27% (b) 25% (c) 50% (d) 26%
Answer: (b)
Solution: (1 + r/100)² = 15,625/10,000 = 25/16, so 1 + r/100 = 5/4 and r = 25%. So the answer is 25%.
Source: PYQ-style
