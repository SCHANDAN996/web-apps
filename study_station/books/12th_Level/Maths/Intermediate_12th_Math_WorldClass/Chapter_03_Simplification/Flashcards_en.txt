Card 1
Front: What does the "B" in BODMAS stand for, and which brackets are solved first?
Back: Brackets. Order inside brackets: bar (—) → small ( ) → curly { } → square [ ].

Card 2
Front: In BODMAS, which is solved first — multiplication or division?
Back: Neither has priority; solve Division and Multiplication together, left to right, as they appear.

Card 3
Front: What is the full form of the mnemonic "BODMAS"?
Back: Brackets, Of, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction.

Card 4
Front: What is the mnemonic trick to remember the order of brackets?
Back: "Bar-Small-Curly-Square" — remember: "Boats Sail Calm Seas" (Bar → ( ) → { } → [ ]).

Card 5
Front: What does "Of" mean in simplification, and what operation replaces it?
Back: "Of" means multiplication (×). Example: 1/2 of 8 = 1/2 × 8 = 4. It is solved before division/multiplication.

Card 6
Front: What is the value of 1 + 2 + 3 + … + n?
Back: n(n+1)/2. Example: 1 to 100 = 100×101/2 = 5050.

Card 7
Front: What is the sum of the first n odd numbers?
Back: n². Example: first 10 odd numbers = 10² = 100.

Card 8
Front: What is the sum of the first n even numbers?
Back: n(n+1). Example: first 10 even numbers = 10×11 = 110.

Card 9
Front: What is the algebraic identity for (a + b)²?
Back: a² + 2ab + b².

Card 10
Front: What is the identity for a² − b², and when is it most useful?
Back: (a + b)(a − b). Useful for products like 102 × 98 = (100+2)(100−2) = 10000 − 4 = 9996.

Card 11
Front: What is the quick trick to square a number ending in 5, like 85²?
Back: Multiply the tens digit by (itself + 1), then attach 25: 8×9 = 72 → 7225.

Card 12
Front: How do you quickly multiply any number by 5?
Back: Halve the number and multiply by 10. Example: 248 × 5 = 124 × 10 = 1240.

Card 13
Front: How do you quickly multiply a number by 25?
Back: Divide by 4 and multiply by 100. Example: 64 × 25 = 16 × 100 = 1600.

Card 14
Front: What is the divisibility rule for 3 and 9?
Back: If the sum of digits is divisible by 3 (or 9), the number is divisible by 3 (or 9).

Card 15
Front: What is the divisibility rule for 11?
Back: If the difference between the sum of digits at odd places and even places is 0 or a multiple of 11, the number is divisible by 11.

Card 16
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember that "Of" beats Division in BODMAS?
Back: "OF = Order First" — 'O' comes before 'D' in BODMAS, so solve "of" before ÷ and ×.

Card 17
Front: In a fraction like (3/4) ÷ (9/8), what is the shortcut?
Back: Multiply by the reciprocal: (3/4) × (8/9) = 24/36 = 2/3.

Card 18
Front: What is the digit-sum (casting out nines) check used for?
Back: Verifying calculations: add digits of each number to a single digit; the digit-sum of the answer must match the digit-sum of the computed expression.

Card 19
Front: What is the value of 0.125 as a fraction, and why is it worth memorising?
Back: 1/8. Common decimal-fraction pairs (0.5=1/2, 0.25=1/4, 0.2=1/5, 0.125=1/8) make simplification much faster.

Card 20
Front: When an expression has both + and − only, what is the safe method?
Back: Add all positive terms together, add all negative terms together, then subtract — avoids sign errors.
