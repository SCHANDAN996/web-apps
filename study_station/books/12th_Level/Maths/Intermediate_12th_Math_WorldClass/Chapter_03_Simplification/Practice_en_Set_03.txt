51. Simplify: 48 ÷ 8 × 3 + 12 − 6
(a) 18 (b) 30 (c) 24 (d) 36
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is doing 8 × 3 first and getting 48 ÷ 24 = 2. Division and multiplication have equal priority and go left to right: 48 ÷ 8 = 6, then 6 × 3 = 18. Then 18 + 12 − 6 = 24, so the answer is 24.
