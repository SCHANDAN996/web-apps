1. Simplify: 48 + 36 ÷ 4 × 3
(a) 63 (b) 75 (c) 81 (d) 90
Answer: (b)
Solution: By BODMAS, division and multiplication come before addition. First 36 ÷ 4 = 9, then 9 × 3 = 27, then 48 + 27 = 75. The trap is doing 48 + 36 first, which gives 84 ÷ 4 × 3 = 63, so the answer is 75.

2. Simplify: 125 − 25 × 4 + 10
(a) 410 (b) 35 (c) 15 (d) 90
Answer: (b)
Solution: Multiplication first: 25 × 4 = 100. Then 125 − 100 + 10 = 35. The trap is subtracting first: 100 × 4 + 10 = 410, so the answer is 35.

3. Simplify: 7² + 3² − 2 × 5
(a) 38 (b) 58 (c) 68 (d) 48
Answer: (d)
Solution: 7² = 49 and 3² = 9, so 49 + 9 = 58. Then 2 × 5 = 10, and 58 − 10 = 48. The trap is forgetting the subtraction, which gives 58, so the answer is 48.

5. Simplify: 15% of 240
(a) 24 (b) 30 (c) 48 (d) 36

7. Simplify: 96 ÷ 8 × 4 − 12
(a) 24 (b) 48 (c) 36 (d) 60
Answer: (c)
Solution: Division and multiplication go left to right: 96 ÷ 8 = 12, then 12 × 4 = 48, then 48 − 12 = 36. The trap is multiplying 8 × 4 = 32 first and dividing 96 ÷ 32 = 3, so the answer is 36.

22. Simplify: (17² − 8²) ÷ 9
(a) 15 (b) 20 (c) 25 (d) 30
Answer: (c)
Solution: Use a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b): (17 + 8)(17 − 8) = 25 × 9 = 225, then 225 ÷ 9 = 25. The trap is squaring wrongly: 289 − 64 = 225 is correct, but some write 289 − 64 = 215, so the answer is 25.
