126. What is 48% of 625 + 35% of 480?
(a) 476 (b) 486 (c) 458 (d) 468
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 486 comes from swapping the digits after a quick mental slip. Compute each part: 48% of 625 = (48 × 625)/100 = 300, and 35% of 480 = (35 × 480)/100 = 168. Adding, 300 + 168 = 468, so the answer is 468.
