101. Simplify: 48 ÷ 8 × 3 + 12 − 5
(a) 31 (b) 13 (c) 19 (d) 25
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding 12 − 5 first or dividing wrongly; follow BODMAS strictly. Division and multiplication first: 48 ÷ 8 = 6, then 6 × 3 = 18, then 18 + 12 − 5 = 25, so the answer is 25.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Simplify: 7.5 × 4 + 2.5 × 4
(a) 40 (b) 30 (c) 35 (d) 45
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing each product separately and mis-adding decimals. Factor out 4: (7.5 + 2.5) × 4 = 10 × 4 = 40, so the answer is 40.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

103. Simplify: 3/5 of 250 + 1/4 of 160
(a) 180 (b) 200 (c) 190 (d) 170
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 180 comes from computing only 3/5 of 250 = 150 and adding 30 by mistake. Correctly, 3/5 × 250 = 150 and 1/4 × 160 = 40, so 150 + 40 = 190, so the answer is 190.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

104. Simplify: 125% of 480 − 25% of 240
(a) 520 (b) 540 (c) 600 (d) 560
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is treating 125% as 1.25 of 400 instead of 480. 125% of 480 = 5/4 × 480 = 600; 25% of 240 = 60. Then 600 − 60 = 540, so the answer is 540.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

105. Simplify: √144 + √81 − √49

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding the numbers under one root: √(144+81−49) = √176, which is not an option but misleads many. Compute each root: 12 + 9 − 7 = 14, so the answer is 14.

106. Simplify: 2³ × 3² − 5²
(a) 67 (b) 57 (c) 47 (d) 37
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 57 comes from computing 2³ = 6 (multiplying instead of raising to power). Correctly, 2³ = 8, 3² = 9, so 8 × 9 = 72, and 72 − 25 = 47, so the answer is 47.

107. Simplify: 0.6 × 0.5 + 0.04 ÷ 0.2
(a) 0.5 (b) 0.32 (c) 0.23 (d) 0.7
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is doing addition before division: (0.3 + 0.04) ÷ 0.2 = 1.7, which is wrong. By BODMAS: 0.6 × 0.5 = 0.3 and 0.04 ÷ 0.2 = 0.2, then 0.3 + 0.2 = 0.5, so the answer is 0.5.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

108. Simplify: 5/6 ÷ 10/3 × 4
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 1/2 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is dividing by multiplying without inverting the second fraction. Correctly, 5/6 ÷ 10/3 = 5/6 × 3/10 = 15/60 = 1/4, then 1/4 × 4 = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

109. Simplify: 18 − [6 − {4 − (8 − 6 + 3)}]
(a) 9 (b) 13 (c) 11 (d) 15
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is removing brackets from outside in, or making a sign error with the double negative. Work from the innermost bracket: 8 − 6 + 3 = 5; then 4 − 5 = −1; then 6 − (−1) = 7; finally 18 − 7 = 11, so the answer is 11.
Source: PYQ-style

110. Simplify: 45% of 360 + 55% of 360
(a) 360 (b) 340 (c) 400 (d) 380
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing each percentage separately and making arithmetic slips. Since 45% + 55% = 100%, the sum is 100% of 360 = 360, so the answer is 360.

111. Simplify: 999 × 99
(a) 98901 (b) 98991 (c) 99801 (d) 99901
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is long multiplication errors. Use 999 × 99 = 999 × (100 − 1) = 99900 − 999 = 98901, so the answer is 98901.

112. Simplify: (17)² − (13)²
(a) 100 (b) 140 (c) 120 (d) 160
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is squaring the difference: (17 − 13)² = 16, which is wrong. Use a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b) = 30 × 4 = 120, so the answer is 120.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

113. Simplify: 2.4 + 3.6 × 2 − 1.8
(a) 10.2 (b) 8.4 (c) 9.6 (d) 7.8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding first: (2.4 + 3.6) × 2 − 1.8 = 10.2, which ignores BODMAS. Correctly, 3.6 × 2 = 7.2, then 2.4 + 7.2 − 1.8 = 7.8, so the answer is 7.8.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

114. Simplify: 7/9 + 5/6 − 1/3
(a) 23/18 (b) 19/18 (c) 21/18 (d) 25/18
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding numerators and denominators directly. The LCM of 9, 6 and 3 is 18: 7/9 = 14/18, 5/6 = 15/18, 1/3 = 6/18, so 14 + 15 − 6 = 23, giving 23/18, so the answer is 23/18.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

115. Simplify: 64 ÷ 0.8 × 0.5
(a) 40 (b) 80 (c) 20 (d) 160
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is multiplying 0.8 × 0.5 = 0.4 first and then dividing: 64 ÷ 0.4 = 160, which is wrong. Division and multiplication go left to right: 64 ÷ 0.8 = 80, then 80 × 0.5 = 40, so the answer is 40.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

119. Simplify: 5 + 5 × 5 ÷ 5 − 5
(a) 10 (b) 0 (c) 5 (d) 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is working strictly left to right and ignoring operation priority. By BODMAS: 5 × 5 = 25, 25 ÷ 5 = 5, then 5 + 5 − 5 = 5, so the answer is 5.

120. Simplify: (0.7)² + (0.3)² + 2 × 0.7 × 0.3
(a) 1.21 (b) 1 (c) 0.58 (d) 0.49
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding only the squares: 0.49 + 0.09 = 0.58, forgetting the middle term. This is (a + b)² = (0.7 + 0.3)² = 1² = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

121. Simplify: 1/(1×2) + 1/(2×3) + 1/(3×4) + 1/(4×5) + 1/(5×6)
(a) 1/6 (b) 5/6 (c) 6/5 (d) 4/5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding fractions with LCM, which is long and error-prone. Use the identity 1/(n(n+1)) = 1/n − 1/(n+1); the series telescopes to 1 − 1/6 = 5/6, so the answer is 5/6.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Simplify: (2.3)³ − 3 × (2.3)² × 1.3 + 3 × 2.3 × (1.3)² − (1.3)³
(a) 0.1 (b) 10 (c) 1 (d) 3.6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is expanding each cube separately, which is tedious and error-prone. Recognise the pattern (a − b)³ with a = 2.3, b = 1.3: (2.3 − 1.3)³ = 1³ = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

123. If x = 3 + 2√2, then the value of √x + 1/√x is
(a) √2 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 2√2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is squaring blindly and losing track. Note x = (√2 + 1)², so √x = √2 + 1 and 1/√x = √2 − 1 (rationalising). Sum = (√2 + 1) + (√2 − 1) = 2√2, so the answer is 2√2.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

124. Simplify: (999² − 1) ÷ 1000
(a) 1000 (b) 998 (c) 997 (d) 999
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing 999² = 998001 and dividing 998000 by 1000 with a slip. Use a² − 1 = (a − 1)(a + 1) = 998 × 1000; dividing by 1000 gives 998, so the answer is 998.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Simplify: 1 + 2 + 3 + … + 50 − (1 + 2 + 3 + … + 20)
(a) 1245 (b) 1275 (c) 1155 (d) 1065
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is applying the sum formula only to the first series and subtracting 20 instead of the whole second sum. Sum to 50 = 50 × 51/2 = 1275; sum to 20 = 20 × 21/2 = 210; 1275 − 210 = 1065, so the answer is 1065.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
