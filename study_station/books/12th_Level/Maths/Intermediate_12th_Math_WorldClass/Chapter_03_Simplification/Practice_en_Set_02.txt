26. Simplify: 48 ÷ 8 × 3 + 12 − 6
(a) 24 (b) 30 (c) 18 (d) 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting trap is dividing 48 by (8 × 3) or adding 12 − 6 first, which gives wrong values like 2 or 20. By BODMAS, division and multiplication go left to right: 48 ÷ 8 = 6, then 6 × 3 = 18, then 18 + 12 − 6 = 24, so the answer is 24.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

27. Simplify: 15% of 240 + 25% of 160
(a) 72 (b) 76 (c) 80 (d) 84
Answer: (b)
Solution: A common slip is computing 15% of 240 as 30 (using 12.5% by mistake), which leads to 70. Correctly, 15% of 240 = 36 and 25% of 160 = 40, giving 36 + 40 = 76, so the answer is 76.
Source: PYQ-style

28. Simplify: (3/5) of (7/9) of 675

Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often multiply 3 × 7 × 675 and divide only by 9, forgetting the 5 in the denominator, which gives 1575. Correctly, 675 × 7/9 = 525, then 525 × 3/5 = 315, so the answer is 315.

29. Simplify: 144 ÷ 12 + 18 × 4 − 20
(a) 60 (b) 64 (c) 62 (d) 66
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is doing 12 + 18 first because addition looks easy, which breaks BODMAS and gives a wrong result. Correctly, 144 ÷ 12 = 12 and 18 × 4 = 72, then 12 + 72 − 20 = 64, so the answer is 64.

Answer: (d)

32. Simplify: (17² − 15²) ÷ 4
(a) 16 (b) 14 (c) 18 (d) 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: Computing full squares is fine, but many miscompute 17² as 279 or divide 64 by 4 wrongly. Using a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b) = 32 × 2 = 64, then 64 ÷ 4 = 16, so the answer is 16.

34. Simplify: 40% of 350 − 30% of 200
(a) 70 (b) 90 (c) 80 (d) 100
Answer: (c)
Solution: Students often subtract the percentages first (40% − 30% = 10%) and apply it to 350, getting 35. Correctly, 40% of 350 = 140 and 30% of 200 = 60, so 140 − 60 = 80, so the answer is 80.

Solution: Quick addition often slips a carry, giving 1100. Using the trick: (1000 − 1) + (100 − 1) + (10 − 1) + 3 = 1110 − 3 + 3 = 1110, so the answer is 1110.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dividing 560 by (14 × 6) = 84, which gives a fraction. By left-to-right order, 560 ÷ 14 = 40, then 40 × 6 = 240, then 240 − 120 = 120, so the answer is 120.

48. Simplify: 15 × 15 − 14 × 16
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many compute both products fully and make a subtraction slip, getting 2 or 3. Using the identity n² − (n−1)(n+1) = 1, here 225 − 224 = 1, so the answer is 1.

50. Simplify: (256 ÷ 16)² ÷ 32

Solution: The trap is squaring 256 first or dividing 16² by 32 incorrectly as 16. Correctly, 256 ÷ 16 = 16, then 16² = 256, then 256 ÷ 32 = 8, so the answer is 8.
