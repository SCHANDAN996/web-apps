26. Find the value of 45 - [38 - {60 / 3 - (6 - 9 / 3)}].
(a) 24 (b) 26 (c) 22 (d) 28
Answer: (a) 24
Solution: 9 / 3 = 3; 6 - 3 = 3; 60 / 3 = 20; 20 - 3 = 17; 38 - 17 = 21; 45 - 21 = 24. The required value is 24. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. Find the value of 25 - [16 - {12 - (9 - 4)}], where (7 - 3) was evaluated first under a vinculum.
(a) 12 (b) 16 (c) 14 (d) 18
Answer: (b) 16
Solution: Vinculum term 7 - 3 = 4. Then 9 - 4 = 5. Braces: 12 - 5 = 7. Box: 16 - 7 = 9. Result: 25 - 9 = 16. The required value is 16. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
