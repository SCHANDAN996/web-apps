Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The table shows the number of students in four classes: Class A = 40, Class B = 50, Class C = 60, Class D = 30. What percentage of the total students are in Class C?
(a) 25% (b) 30% (c) 35% (d) 33.33%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total students = 40 + 50 + 60 + 30 = 180. Percentage in Class C = 60/180 × 100 = 33.33%. The tempting wrong choice 30% comes from dividing 60 by 200, a wrongly assumed total, so the answer is 33.33%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Data Handling)

77. A pie chart shows monthly expenses: Rent 30%, Food 25%, Travel 15%, Savings 20%, Others 10%. If the total monthly income is ₹40,000, how much is spent on Food?
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹12,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹6,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Food = 25% of ₹40,000 = 25/100 × 40,000 = ₹10,000. The trap option ₹12,000 comes from mistakenly using the Rent percentage of 30%, so the answer is ₹10,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Data Handling)

78. The bar graph shows sales (in units) of a shop over 4 months: Jan = 200, Feb = 250, Mar = 300, Apr = 350. What is the average monthly sale?
(a) 300 units (b) 275 units (c) 250 units (d) 280 units
Answer: (b)
Solution: Average = (200 + 250 + 300 + 350)/4 = 1100/4 = 275 units. The trap option 250 is just the February value picked by those who average only two middle terms incorrectly, so the answer is 275 units.

80. A company's production (in tonnes) over 5 years: 2019 = 120, 2020 = 150, 2021 = 180, 2022 = 160, 2023 = 200. What is the percentage increase in production from 2019 to 2023?
(a) 66.67% (b) 80% (c) 75% (d) 60%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Increase = 200 − 120 = 80. Percentage increase = 80/120 × 100 = 66.67%. The trap option 80% comes from treating the increase of 80 directly as the percentage, so the answer is 66.67%.

82. A line graph shows a city's population (in lakhs): 2018 = 5, 2019 = 5.5, 2020 = 6, 2021 = 6.4, 2022 = 7. In which year was the percentage growth over the previous year the highest?
(a) 2021 (b) 2020 (c) 2019 (d) 2022
Answer: (c)
Solution: Growth rates: 2019 = 0.5/5 = 10%; 2020 = 0.5/5.5 ≈ 9.09%; 2021 = 0.4/6 ≈ 6.67%; 2022 = 0.6/6.4 = 9.375%. The trap option 2022 has the largest absolute increase (0.6 lakh) but not the largest percentage, so the answer is 2019.

85. The average of five numbers is 46. If one number, 60, is removed, what is the average of the remaining four numbers?
(a) 44 (b) 42 (c) 43 (d) 42.5
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sum of five = 46 × 5 = 230. Remaining sum = 230 − 60 = 170. New average = 170/4 = 42.5. The trap option 42 comes from dividing 170 by 4 with a rounding slip or using 168, so the answer is 42.5.

89. A batsman's scores in 6 innings: 45, 60, 30, 75, 50, 40. What is the median score?
(a) 45 (b) 50 (c) 47.5 (d) 42.5
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ordered scores: 30, 40, 45, 50, 60, 75. Median = average of 3rd and 4th = (45 + 50)/2 = 47.5. The trap option 50 comes from taking the mean (300/6 = 50) instead of the median, so the answer is 47.5.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics (Statistics)

90. In a factory, production rose from 800 units to 1,000 units, while workers rose from 50 to 60. What is the percentage change in production per worker?
(a) 25% increase (b) 4.17% increase (c) 20% increase (d) No change
Answer: (b)
Solution: Earlier per worker = 800/50 = 16; later = 1000/60 = 16.67. Change = 0.67/16 × 100 ≈ 4.17% increase. The trap option 25% is the production increase alone, ignoring the rise in workers, so the answer is 4.17% increase.

95. A shop's profit (₹ thousand): Jan = 12, Feb = 15, Mar = 10, Apr = 18, May = 20. What is the average monthly profit, and in how many months was profit above this average?

97. The table shows runs scored by two players in 5 matches: Player M: 50, 70, 30, 90, 60; Player N: 60, 60, 60, 60, 60. Which statement is true?
(a) Averages are equal but N is more consistent (b) N has higher average (c) Averages are equal but M is more consistent (d) M has higher average
Answer: (a)
Solution: M's average = 300/5 = 60; N's average = 300/5 = 60. Averages are equal, but N's scores never vary, so N is more consistent. The trap option claiming M is more consistent relies only on M's higher peak score of 90, so the answer is that averages are equal but N is more consistent.

100. The table shows employees in departments: HR = 25, Sales = 75, Tech = 100, Admin = 50. If 20% of Tech employees are transferred to Sales, what is the new ratio of Sales to Tech employees?
(a) 95:80 (b) 19:16 (c) 3:2 (d) 5:4
Answer: (b)
Solution: Transferred = 20% of 100 = 20. New Sales = 75 + 20 = 95; new Tech = 100 − 20 = 80. Ratio = 95:80 = 19:16. The trap option 95:80 is the same ratio left unsimplified, but the fully simplified form is required, so the answer is 19:16.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)
