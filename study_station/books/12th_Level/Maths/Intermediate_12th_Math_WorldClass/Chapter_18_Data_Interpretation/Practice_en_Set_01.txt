1. The table shows the number of students in a school: Class VI = 120, Class VII = 150, Class VIII = 180. What is the total number of students in these three classes?
(a) 420 (b) 450 (c) 480 (d) 500
Answer: (b)
Solution: A common slip is adding only two classes (120 + 150 = 270) or misreading one value. Add all three: 120 + 150 + 180 = 450, so the answer is 450.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Data Handling)

2. In a bar graph, the bar for Monday shows 40 books sold and the bar for Tuesday shows 65 books sold. How many more books were sold on Tuesday than on Monday?
(a) 25 (b) 20 (c) 30 (d) 35
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding the two values (40 + 65 = 105) instead of subtracting. Difference = 65 − 40 = 25, so the answer is 25.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics (Data Handling)

3. A pie chart shows that 25% of a family's income is spent on food. If the monthly income is ₹20,000, how much is spent on food?
(a) ₹5,000 (b) ₹4,000 (c) ₹6,000 (d) ₹2,500
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students compute 20% (₹4,000) by habit. 25% of 20,000 = (25/100) × 20,000 = ₹5,000, so the answer is ₹5,000.

4. The table gives marks of a student: Hindi = 70, English = 80, Maths = 90, Science = 60. What is the average mark?
(a) 75 (b) 72 (c) 80 (d) 70
Answer: (a)
Solution: A tempting error is picking the middle value 80 as the "average". Average = (70 + 80 + 90 + 60)/4 = 300/4 = 75, so the answer is 75.

5. In a pie chart, the central angle for "Rent" is 90°. What percentage of the total does Rent represent?
(a) 25% (b) 15% (c) 30% (d) 20%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating 90° as 90% of something. Percentage = (90/360) × 100 = 25%, so the answer is 25%.

(a) 1/2 (b) 3/5 (c) 2/5 (d) 2/3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is writing 200/300 = 2/3, comparing tea only to coffee instead of the total. Fraction = 200/(200 + 300) = 200/500 = 2/5, so the answer is 2/5.

23. The table shows production of two companies (in thousand units): Year 2020 — X = 250, Y = 200; Year 2021 — X = 300, Y = 260. In which company was the percentage growth from 2020 to 2021 higher?
(a) X (b) Y (c) Both equal (d) Cannot be determined
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is comparing absolute growth: X grew by 50 and Y by 60, but the question asks percentage. X: (50/250) × 100 = 20%; Y: (60/200) × 100 = 30%. Y's percentage growth is higher, so the answer is Y.
