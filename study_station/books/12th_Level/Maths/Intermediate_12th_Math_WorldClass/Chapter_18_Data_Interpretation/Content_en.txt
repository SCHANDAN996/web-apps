# Data Interpretation

## Chapter Outline & Learning Goals

**Chapter Outline:**
- Understanding data interpretation as a language of numbers
- Reading tables, charts, and graphs
- Calculating percentages, ratios, and trends
- Solving problems involving averages, ratios, and proportions

**Learning Goals:**
- Interpret data presented in tables, bar graphs, pie charts, and line graphs
- Calculate averages, ratios, and proportions from given data
- Solve problems involving mean, median, mode, and range
- Compare data sets and draw conclusions from them

**Hook:**
Imagine you are a detective, and the numbers in front of you are your clues. A table of marks, a bar graph of rainfall, a pie chart of expenses — each one hides a story, and your job is to uncover it. In exams like SSC, Railway, and Banking, data interpretation questions work exactly the same way: the answer is always sitting inside the data; you only need to read it correctly and calculate smartly. Master this skill, and you will find that DI questions are some of the quickest marks you can earn.
