101. A family's monthly budget of ₹60,000 is shown in a pie chart: Food 25%, Rent 20%, Education 15%, Transport 10%, Savings 30%. How much is spent on Food?
(a) ₹12,000 (b) ₹20,000 (c) ₹18,000 (d) ₹15,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is picking the Savings slice, which is the largest at 30% and gives ₹18,000, but the question asks only about Food. Food = 25% of ₹60,000 = 25/100 × 60,000 = ₹15,000, so the answer is ₹15,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Maths (Data Handling)

102. A bar graph shows annual sales of a firm: 2019 = ₹240 lakh, 2020 = ₹300 lakh, 2021 = ₹360 lakh, 2022 = ₹420 lakh. What is the percentage increase in sales from 2019 to 2022?
(a) 60% (b) 70% (c) 80% (d) 75%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students divide the increase by the 2022 value and get about 43%, or compare only consecutive years. Increase = 420 − 240 = 180; percentage increase = 180/240 × 100 = 75%, so the answer is 75%.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

103. In a school of 720 students, the ratio of boys to girls is 5 : 4. How many girls are there?
(a) 280 (b) 320 (c) 360 (d) 400
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting error is computing the boys' share, which is 400, because 5 is written first. Girls' share = 4/(5+4) × 720 = 4/9 × 720 = 320, so the answer is 320.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Maths (Ratio and Proportion)

104. The production of a factory (in tonnes) over five years is: 40, 50, 60, 70, 80. What is the average annual production?
(a) 55 tonnes (b) 58 tonnes (c) 65 tonnes (d) 60 tonnes
Answer: (d)
Solution: A quick guess of 55 comes from misreading the middle term; the data is evenly spaced so the average equals the middle value. Sum = 300; average = 300/5 = 60 tonnes, so the answer is 60 tonnes.

105. In a survey of 400 people, 120 prefer tea, 180 prefer coffee and 100 prefer milk. What percentage of people prefer coffee?
(a) 40% (b) 42% (c) 45% (d) 48%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is dividing 180 by 450 or by the tea-plus-milk total; the base must be all 400 people. Percentage = 180/400 × 100 = 45%, so the answer is 45%.

106. A line graph shows a town's population: 2.0 lakh in 2015 and 2.5 lakh in 2020. What is the percentage increase over the period?
(a) 25% (b) 22.5% (c) 20% (d) 30%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dividing the increase 0.5 by the final value 2.5 gives 20%, which is the classic wrong-base trap. Correct base is 2015: 0.5/2.0 × 100 = 25%, so the answer is 25%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Maths (Comparing Quantities)

107. In a pie chart of a company's annual expenditure of ₹90,000 crore, the angle for Raw Materials is 108°. What is the expenditure on Raw Materials?
(a) ₹24,000 crore (b) ₹27,000 crore (c) ₹30,000 crore (d) ₹32,400 crore
Answer: (b)
Solution: Using 108 as a percentage of 360 directly without converting is the trap; the share is 108/360, not 108%. Expenditure = 108/360 × 90,000 = 0.3 × 90,000 = ₹27,000 crore, so the answer is ₹27,000 crore.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CHSL pattern)

108. A firm's exports rose from ₹450 crore to ₹540 crore, while imports rose from ₹300 crore to ₹360 crore. What is the ratio of the percentage increase in exports to that in imports?
(a) 5 : 4 (b) 3 : 2 (c) 2 : 3 (d) 1 : 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The absolute increases (90 and 60) tempt students to answer 3 : 2, but percentages must be compared. Exports: 90/450 = 20%; imports: 60/300 = 20%; ratio = 20 : 20 = 1 : 1, so the answer is 1 : 1.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS PO pattern)

109. A man's average monthly income is ₹25,000 and he spends 80% of it. How much does he save in a year?
(a) ₹48,000 (b) ₹54,000 (c) ₹60,000 (d) ₹72,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is reporting monthly savings ₹5,000 or computing 20% of the annual figure incorrectly as ₹48,000 by a slip. Monthly savings = 20% of 25,000 = ₹5,000; yearly = 5,000 × 12 = ₹60,000, so the answer is ₹60,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Maths (Comparing Quantities)

110. A student's marks in five subjects are 72, 85, 90, 68 and 75. What is his average score?
(a) 78 (b) 77 (c) 76 (d) 79
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rounding each mark before adding gives 77 or 79, which is the trap. Sum = 72 + 85 + 90 + 68 + 75 = 390; average = 390/5 = 78, so the answer is 78.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Maths (Data Handling)

111. In a pie chart of total production of 5,000 units, Company A makes 20% and Company B makes 30%. By how many units does B exceed A?
(a) 500 (b) 400 (c) 600 (d) 750
Answer: (a)
Solution: Computing only B's share (1,500) and forgetting to subtract is the common slip. Difference = (30% − 20%) of 5,000 = 10% of 5,000 = 500 units, so the answer is 500.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

112. A bar graph shows that a city's water consumption fell from 800 million litres to 600 million litres. What is the percentage decrease?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 33.33%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dividing 200 by the final value 600 gives 33.33%, the wrong-base trap. Decrease = 200; percentage = 200/800 × 100 = 25%, so the answer is 25%.

113. In a pie chart, the angles for two items P and Q are 90° and 60° respectively. What is the ratio of their values?
(a) 2 : 3 (b) 3 : 4 (c) 3 : 2 (d) 4 : 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Reversing the order and writing 2 : 3 is the trap, since Q is smaller. Ratio = 90 : 60 = 3 : 2, so the answer is 3 : 2.

114. A pie chart represents a budget of ₹1,20,000. If one sector is 15% of the total, what is its value?
(a) ₹18,000 (b) ₹16,000 (c) ₹15,000 (d) ₹20,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Confusing 15% with 1/8 or reading it as 15,000 directly is the trap. Value = 15/100 × 1,20,000 = ₹18,000, so the answer is ₹18,000.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC MTS pattern)

115. A company's revenue was ₹400 crore in 2018 and grew by 15% each year. What is the approximate percentage increase from 2018 to 2020?
(a) 30% (b) 31.5% (c) 35% (d) 32.25%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Adding 15 + 15 = 30% ignores compounding, which is the trap. 2019: 400 × 1.15 = 460; 2020: 460 × 1.15 = 529; increase = 129/400 × 100 = 32.25%, so the answer is 32.25%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Maths (Compound Interest)

116. Six-monthly sales (in ₹ thousand) are 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60. By how much does the average of the last three months exceed the average of the first three months?
(a) ₹25 thousand (b) ₹30 thousand (c) ₹35 thousand (d) ₹40 thousand
Answer: (b)
Solution: Taking the difference of the two middle terms (40 − 30 = 10) is the trap. First three average = 60/3 = 20; last three average = 150/3 = 50; difference = 30, so the answer is ₹30 thousand.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Maths (Data Handling)

117. In a pie chart of a monthly income of ₹96,000, savings amount to ₹12,000. What is the angle of the savings sector?
(a) 40° (b) 45° (c) 50° (d) 54°
Answer: (b)
Solution: Computing 12,000/96,000 as 12.5% and then stopping, or multiplying by 100 instead of 360, is the trap. Angle = 12,000/96,000 × 360° = 1/8 × 360° = 45°, so the answer is 45°.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

118. Pie chart A has a total of ₹8,000 with Item X at 25%; pie chart B has a total of ₹12,000 with Item X at 20%. What is the combined value of Item X?
(a) ₹4,200 (b) ₹4,800 (c) ₹4,600 (d) ₹4,400
Answer: (d)
Solution: Averaging the percentages (22.5%) and applying to the combined total gives ₹4,500, which is wrong because the totals differ. X in A = 2,000; X in B = 2,400; combined = ₹4,400, so the answer is ₹4,400.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS Clerk pattern)

119. In a colony of 2,000 families, 50% own a scooter, and 40% of scooter owners also own a car. How many families own both?
(a) 300 (b) 350 (c) 400 (d) 450
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding the percentages (90%) or applying 40% to all 2,000 families (800) are the traps. Scooter owners = 1,000; both = 40% of 1,000 = 400, so the answer is 400.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Maths (Comparing Quantities)

120. A bar graph shows yearly output: Year 1 = 100, Year 2 = 140, Year 3 = 154, Year 4 = 192.5. In which year was the percentage increase over the previous year the highest?
(a) Year 4 (b) Year 3 (c) Year 2 (d) Same in all years
Answer: (c)
Solution: Year 4 has the biggest absolute jump (38.5), which tempts students, but percentage growth is what matters. Year 2: 40/100 = 40%; Year 3: 14/140 = 10%; Year 4: 38.5/154 = 25%; the highest is Year 2, so the answer is Year 2.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL Mains pattern)

121. In 2019, a college had 500 students with boys and girls in the ratio 3 : 2. In 2020, the strength became 600 with the ratio 7 : 5. What is the approximate percentage increase in the number of boys?
(a) 16.67% (b) 14.28% (c) 20% (d) 25%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Comparing total strength (500 to 600, i.e. 20%) instead of boys specifically is the trap. Boys in 2019 = 3/5 × 500 = 300; boys in 2020 = 7/12 × 600 = 350; increase = 50/300 × 100 ≈ 16.67%, so the answer is 16.67%.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS PO pattern)

122. A pie chart shows that 25% of 2,400 students in a school study Science. If 60% of the Science students are boys, how many girls study Science?
(a) 220 (b) 240 (c) 260 (d) 360
Answer: (b)
Solution: Computing the boys (360) and stopping is the trap, since the question asks for girls. Science students = 25% of 2,400 = 600; girls = 40% of 600 = 240, so the answer is 240.
Source: PYQ-style (RRB NTPC pattern)

123. A table shows that trader A earns 25% more than trader B. By what percentage does B earn less than A?
(a) 25% (b) 22.5% (c) 20% (d) 30%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Answering 25% by symmetry is the classic trap; the base changes. Let B = 100, then A = 125; B earns less by 25/125 × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Maths (Comparing Quantities)

124. Three classes have average marks of 40, 50 and 60 with 20, 30 and 50 students respectively. What is the combined average mark of all students?
(a) 53 (b) 52 (c) 50 (d) 55
Answer: (a)
Solution: Taking the simple average of 40, 50, 60 gives 50, which ignores class sizes — the weighted-average trap. Total marks = 800 + 1,500 + 3,000 = 5,300; students = 100; average = 53, so the answer is 53.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL Mains pattern)

125. In 2019, a family's total expenditure was ₹50,000 with rent at 24%. In 2020, total expenditure rose to ₹60,000 but rent fell to 20% of it. What is the ratio of rent in 2019 to rent in 2020?
(a) 1 : 1 (b) 6 : 5 (c) 5 : 6 (d) 24 : 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: Using the percentages directly (24 : 20 = 6 : 5) is the trap, because the totals differ. Rent 2019 = 24% of 50,000 = ₹12,000; rent 2020 = 20% of 60,000 = ₹12,000; ratio = 1 : 1, so the answer is 1 : 1.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS PO pattern)
