# 🪄 Short Tricks — Data Interpretation (Memory Tricks)

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 1 — The "Pizza Slice" Rule (Pie Chart)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** "A full pizza is 360°, so every 1% = 3.6°."

**Trick:** Imagine a pizza cut into 100 equal crumbs. Each crumb = 1% = 3.6°.
To convert: **Value% × 3.6 = Degrees** and **Degrees ÷ 3.6 = Value%**

**Keyword:** **"3.6 DEGREE"** — *3.6 is the Degree-Delivery boy between % and °.*

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 2 — "RATIO = RAT" (Ratio Comparison)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** A **RAT** always compares by dividing, never subtracting!

**Trick:** When a question says "ratio of A to B", the rat jumps: **A ÷ B**.
Never subtract. If A = 80, B = 50 → ratio = 80/50 = **8:5**.

**Keyword:** **RAT = Ratio Always Through division.**

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 3 — "UP-DOWN Ladder" (% Change)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** Climbing a ladder: **New − Old, over Old, times 100.**

**Trick:** % Change = **(New − Old) / Old × 100**
- Going UP the ladder = Increase (+)
- Slipping DOWN = Decrease (−)

**Loci Trick:** Picture yourself on a ladder. Your feet = Old value (the base/denominator). Your head = New value. The distance climbed ÷ where you started = % change.

**Keyword:** **"Feet First!"** — the OLD value always sits at the bottom (denominator).

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 4 — "AVG the Average Ant" (Averages)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** An ant named **AVG** carries food equally to every nest-mate.

**Trick:** Average = **Total ÷ Count**. And the reverse weapon: **Total = Average × Count** (most DI questions need THIS one!).

**Keyword:** **"AVG multiplies back"** — given average and count, MULTIPLY to get the total.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 5 — "The 10% Shortcut Stairs"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** Every percentage problem starts on the 10% stair.

**Trick:** Find 10% first (just shift the decimal one place left), then build:
- 5% = half of 10%
- 20% = double of 10%
- 15% = 10% + 5%
- 1% = shift decimal two places left

**Example:** 15% of 240 → 10% = 24, 5% = 12 → **36**

**Keyword:** **"Shift, Halve, Double"** — the three dance steps of percentage.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 6 — "FRACTION FRIENDS" (Fraction ↔ %)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** Your best friends whose phone numbers you know by heart:

| Friend | Number |
|---|---|
| Half-bhai | 1/2 = 50% |
| Third-tiya | 1/3 = 33.33% |
| Quarter-uncle | 1/4 = 25% |
| Fifth-ji | 1/5 = 20% |
| Eighth-aunty | 1/8 = 12.5% |
| Twelfth-tau | 1/12 = 8.33% |

**Trick:** 37.5% = 3/8, 62.5% = 5/8, 87.5% = 7/8 — the **"Eighth Family"** (12.5, 25, 37.5, 50, 62.5, 75, 87.5).

**Keyword:** **"Eighths rule DI"** — most exam percentages are secretly eighths!

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 7 — "BAR = Base Always Reads" (Bar Graphs)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** Before reading any bar, touch its **BASE** (the axis)!

**Trick:** 90% of bar-graph traps are axis traps:
- Check if the scale starts at 0 or not
- Check units (lakhs? crores? thousands?)
- Check if bars are stacked or side-by-side

**Keyword:** **"BASE before BAR"** — read the axis before the bar height.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 8 — "The Approximation Ambulance" 🚑** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** When options are far apart, the ambulance takes the SHORTEST route — round off!

**Trick:** If options differ by more than ~5%, round numbers aggressively:
- 498 × 12 ≈ 500 × 12 = 6000
- 23.7% of 840 ≈ 24% of 840 ≈ 200

**Keyword:** **"Far options = Fast rounding. Close options = Careful calculation."**

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 9 — "LINE = Look At INclinE" (Line Graphs)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** In a line graph, the **SLOPE** tells the story, not the point.

**Trick:**
- Steepest UPWARD slope = highest % growth (usually)
- Flat line = no change
- But beware: steepest slope ≠ highest % change if bases differ!

**Keyword:** **"Slope shows speed, Base shows truth"** — always check the starting value before crowning a "fastest growth".

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 10 — "TABLE = Total And Basic Logic First"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** Before solving ANY table question, scan for **TOTALS row/column**.

**Trick:** Many questions are answered directly from the totals row. If missing, compute totals FIRST — 3 out of 5 questions in a set usually need them.

**Keyword:** **"Total first, questions later"** — 30 seconds of totaling saves 3 minutes of re-adding.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 11 — "The X% of Y = Y% of X Mirror" 🪞** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** Percentages are mirror-twins — they can swap places!

**Trick:** **8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2** (much easier!)
- 16% of 50 = 50% of 16 = 8
- 4% of 75 = 75% of 4 = 3

**Keyword:** **"Swap when stuck"** — if one direction looks ugly, flip it in the mirror.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 12 — "CASELET = Story First, Table Second"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** A caselet is a story hiding a table. Be the detective who **draws the table** before answering.

**Trick:** Never solve a caselet in your head. Convert every sentence into a table cell:
- "A is 20% more than B" → write A = 1.2B in the cell
- Fill totals last

**Keyword:** **"Pen beats memory"** — the moment you write the table, half the caselet solves itself.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 13 — "The 1/x Division Decoder"** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** Division is just a fraction wearing a disguise.

**Trick:** Dividing by 5, 25, 125? Multiply instead:
- ÷ 5 = × 2 then ÷ 10 (e.g., 340 ÷ 5 = 680 ÷ 10 = 68)
- ÷ 25 = × 4 then ÷ 100 (e.g., 350 ÷ 25 = 1400 ÷ 100 = 14)
- ÷ 125 = × 8 then ÷ 1000

**Keyword:** **"2-4-8 the Divide-Gate"** — 5→2, 25→4, 125→8.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 14 — "GROWTH = GRand Old Weight" (Successive %)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** Two successive changes of a% and b% don't just add — the second one grows on the **OLD WEIGHT** of the first!

**Trick:** Net % change = **a + b + (a×b)/100**
- +20% then +10% = 20 + 10 + 2 = **32%** (not 30%!)
- +10% then −10% = 10 − 10 − 1 = **−1%** (not 0%!)

**Keyword:** **"Add, then add the product's hundredth"** — the (ab/100) term is the trap examiners love.

---

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ **TRICK 15 — "DI = Decide & Ignore" (Exam Strategy)** │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

**Memory Hook:** In the exam hall, DI also means **Decide & Ignore**!

**Trick:** Scan all DI sets in 60 seconds:
1. **Decide** — pick sets with direct values (tables, simple bars) first
2. **Ignore** — skip sets with heavy caselets or 3-variable calculations for the end
3. Never spend more than ~1 minute per question on the first pass

**Keyword:** **"Easy sets eat first"** — marks are equal, so grab the cheap ones before the costly ones.
