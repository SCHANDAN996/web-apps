Questions 51–75: Medium

51. The table shows the production of wheat (in lakh tonnes) in four states: Punjab 150, Haryana 110, UP 180, MP 90. What percentage of the total production is contributed by UP?
(a) 33.96% (b) 32.5% (c) 35% (d) 30%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is dividing 180 by a mis-added total of 540 and rounding to 35%. Total = 150 + 110 + 180 + 90 = 530; percentage = (180/530) × 100 = 33.96%, so the answer is 33.96%.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A pie chart shows a company's expenditure: Raw material 120°, Labour 90°, Transport 60°, Others 90°. If total expenditure is ₹7,20,000, what is the expenditure on Labour?
(a) ₹1,60,000 (b) ₹2,00,000 (c) ₹2,40,000 (d) ₹1,80,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is taking 90° out of 270° instead of the full 360°. Labour share = (90/360) × 7,20,000 = 1,80,000, so the answer is ₹1,80,000.
Source: PYQ-style

53. The bar graph shows sales (in ₹ thousand) of a shop: Jan 40, Feb 55, Mar 45, Apr 60. What is the percentage increase in sales from January to April?
(a) 60% (b) 50% (c) 45% (d) 40%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing the increase over April (20/60 ≈ 33%) instead of over January. Increase = 60 − 40 = 20; percentage = (20/40) × 100 = 50%, so the answer is 50%.
Source: PYQ-style

54. The table gives the number of students in three classes: Class X: Boys 30, Girls 20; Class XI: Boys 25, Girls 35; Class XII: Boys 40, Girls 30. What is the ratio of total boys to total girls?
(a) 17:19 (b) 19:17 (c) 19:18 (d) 18:19
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option reverses the ratio, giving girls to boys. Total boys = 30 + 25 + 40 = 95; total girls = 20 + 35 + 30 = 85; ratio = 95:85 = 19:17, so the answer is 19:17.
Source: PYQ-style

55. A line graph shows a city's population (in lakhs): 2018: 12, 2019: 13.2, 2020: 14.5, 2021: 16. What is the average annual population over the four years?
(a) 14 lakh (b) 13.5 lakh (c) 13.925 lakh (d) 13.75 lakh
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is averaging only the first and last values (12 and 16 → 14). Sum = 12 + 13.2 + 14.5 + 16 = 55.7; average = 55.7/4 = 13.925, so the answer is 13.925 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

56. In a pie chart of 360°, the angle for Savings is 54°. If total monthly income is ₹45,000, how much is saved?
(a) ₹6,750 (b) ₹6,000 (c) ₹7,200 (d) ₹5,400
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating 54° as 54% of income. Savings = (54/360) × 45,000 = 0.15 × 45,000 = 6,750, so the answer is ₹6,750.

(a) 72% (b) 70% (c) 68% (d) 75%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is a careless addition giving 360, which pushes the percentage to 72%. Total = 80 + 70 + 60 + 90 + 50 = 350; percentage = (350/500) × 100 = 70%, so the answer is 70%.

58. A company's revenue grew from ₹80 lakh in 2022 to ₹92 lakh in 2023. Expenses grew from ₹60 lakh to ₹72 lakh. By what percentage did profit change?
(a) Increased by 20% (b) No change (c) Increased by 10% (d) Decreased by 10%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is comparing revenue growth (15%) with expense growth (20%) and concluding profit fell. Profit in 2022 = 80 − 60 = 20 lakh; profit in 2023 = 92 − 72 = 20 lakh; change = 0%, so the answer is no change.
Source: PYQ-style

59. The table shows production of cars by a factory: 2019: 5000, 2020: 5500, 2021: 6050, 2022: 6655. The production increases by the same percentage each year. What is that percentage?
(a) 12% (b) 20% (c) 15% (d) 10%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dividing the total growth over three years (33.1%) by 3, giving about 11%, or guessing 15%. Yearly multiplier: 5500/5000 = 1.1, 6050/5500 = 1.1, 6655/6050 = 1.1, i.e. a consistent 10% rise, so the answer is 10%.
Source: PYQ-style

60. A pie chart represents expenses of a family: Food 40%, Rent 25%, Education 15%, Savings 20%. If the family spends ₹12,000 on food, what is the amount saved?
(a) ₹6,000 (b) ₹5,000 (c) ₹7,500 (d) ₹4,500
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking 20% of ₹12,000 (₹2,400) instead of first finding total income. Total income = 12,000/0.40 = 30,000; savings = 20% of 30,000 = 6,000, so the answer is ₹6,000.

61. The bar graph shows exports (in ₹ crore): Textiles 250, Gems 300, Engineering 450, Chemicals 200. Engineering exports are what percent more than Chemicals?
(a) 125% (b) 150% (c) 100% (d) 55.5%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing Engineering as a percentage of Chemicals (225%) or halving the difference. Excess = 450 − 200 = 250; percentage more = (250/200) × 100 = 125%, so the answer is 125%.

62. The table shows the number of employees in departments: HR 40, Sales 120, Tech 160, Admin 80. If 25% of Tech employees are transferred to Sales, what is the new ratio of Sales to Tech?
(a) 4:5 (b) 4:3 (c) 3:2 (d) 2:3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is transferring 25% of Sales instead of Tech, which gives 150:160 and a wrong ratio. Transferred = 25% of 160 = 40; new Sales = 120 + 40 = 160; new Tech = 160 − 40 = 120; ratio = 160:120 = 4:3, so the answer is 4:3.
Source: PYQ-style

64. The table shows income and expenditure (in ₹ thousand) of a firm: 2020: Income 500, Expenditure 400; 2021: Income 600, Expenditure 450; 2022: Income 550, Expenditure 500. In which year was the profit percentage (on expenditure) highest?
(a) 2022 (b) 2020 (c) Same in all three years (d) 2021
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is picking 2020 because its absolute profit of ₹100 thousand looks large on a small base without computing all three. Profit % on expenditure: 2020 = 100/400 = 25%; 2021 = 150/450 = 33.3%; 2022 = 50/500 = 10%; the highest is 33.3% in 2021, so the answer is 2021.

69. A table shows marks obtained by four students in a test of 200 marks: A 150, B 130, C 170, D 110. The pass mark is 60%. How many students passed?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking 60 as the pass mark instead of 60% of 200, which would pass all four. Pass mark = 60% of 200 = 120; A (150), B (130) and C (170) all score 120 or more, while D (110) fails, so the answer is 3.
