126. A family's total annual expenditure is ₹7,20,000, shown in a pie chart with sector angles: Rent 90°, Food 120°, Travel 60°, Education 45°, Miscellaneous 45°. How much more does the family spend on Food than on Education?
(a) ₹1,20,000 (b) ₹1,50,000 (c) ₹1,80,000 (d) ₹90,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing each amount separately and mis-subtracting; instead use the angle difference directly. Difference in angles = 120° − 45° = 75°. Amount = (75/360) × 7,20,000 = 75 × 2,000 = 1,50,000, so the answer is ₹1,50,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Data Handling)

Solution: The tempting error is dividing the increase by the 2023 value instead of the base year 2019. Increase = 900 − 480 = 420. Percentage increase = (420/480) × 100 = 87.5%, so the answer is 87.5%.

131. The population of a town is 2,50,000. It increases by 20% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. What is the population at the end of two years?
