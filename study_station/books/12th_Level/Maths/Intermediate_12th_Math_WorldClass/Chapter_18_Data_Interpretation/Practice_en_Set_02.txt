26. The table shows the number of students in four schools: A = 450, B = 600, C = 350, D = 400. What percentage of the total students are in school B?
(a) 30% (b) 33.33% (c) 35% (d) 25%

Solution: Total students = 450 + 600 + 350 + 400 = 1800. Share of B = 600/1800 × 100 = 33.33%. The trap is 30%, which comes from wrongly taking the total as 2000. So the answer is 33.33%.
