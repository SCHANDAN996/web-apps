126. A bag contains 5 red and 4 blue balls. Two balls are drawn at random without replacement. What is the probability that both balls are of the same colour?
(a) 2/9 (b) 4/9 (c) 1/2 (d) 5/9
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is treating the two draws as independent, which inflates the value. Without replacement: P(both red) = (5/9)(4/8) = 20/72 and P(both blue) = (4/9)(3/8) = 12/72. Total = 32/72 = 4/9, so the answer is 4/9.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

127. Three dice are thrown simultaneously. What is the probability of getting a sum of 10?
(a) 25/216 (b) 7/24 (c) 27/216 (d) 1/6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 25/216 comes from miscounting ordered triples. The patterns giving sum 10 are 1-3-6 (6 arrangements), 1-4-5 (6), 2-2-6 (3), 2-3-5 (6), 2-4-4 (3), 3-3-4 (3), totalling 27 favourable outcomes out of 216, so the answer is 27/216.

128. A speaks truth in 60% of cases and B speaks truth in 70% of cases. What is the probability that they contradict each other in stating the same fact?
(a) 0.18 (b) 0.42 (c) 0.46 (d) 0.54
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing agreement instead of contradiction. Contradiction = A true and B false, or A false and B true = (0.6)(0.3) + (0.4)(0.7) = 0.18 + 0.28 = 0.46, so the answer is 0.46.

129. From a pack of 52 cards, two cards are drawn at random. What is the probability that both are aces or both are kings?
(a) 4/221 (b) 1/221 (c) 8/663 (d) 2/221
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding simple fractions like 4/52 + 4/52 instead of using combinations. P(both aces) = C(4,2)/C(52,2) = 6/1326 and P(both kings) = 6/1326. Total = 12/1326 = 2/221, so the answer is 2/221.

130. The probability that A can solve a problem is 2/3 and that B can solve it is 3/4. If both try independently, what is the probability that the problem is solved?
(a) 11/12 (b) 5/12 (c) 7/12 (d) 1/2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding 2/3 + 3/4, which exceeds 1 and is impossible for a probability. The problem is solved unless both fail: 1 − (1/3)(1/4) = 1 − 1/12 = 11/12, so the answer is 11/12.

131. A box contains 10 bulbs, of which 4 are defective. Three bulbs are drawn one by one without replacement. What is the probability that exactly two are defective?
(a) 3/10 (b) 3/8 (c) 1/5 (d) 7/20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is forgetting that the two defectives and one good bulb can appear in three different orders. Favourable = C(4,2) × C(6,1) = 36 out of C(10,3) = 120, giving 36/120 = 3/10, so the answer is 3/10.

135. A and B throw a die alternately, A starting first. The first to throw a 6 wins. What is the probability that A wins?
(a) 5/11 (b) 1/2 (c) 7/12 (d) 6/11
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming equal chances of 1/2, ignoring that A moves first. P(A wins) = 1/6 + (5/6)(5/6)(1/6) + … = (1/6)/(1 − 25/36) = (1/6)/(11/36) = 6/11, so the answer is 6/11.

140. The probability that it will rain tomorrow is 0.4 and the probability that a student will catch the bus is 0.7, independent of rain. What is the probability that it rains and the student misses the bus?
(a) 0.18 (b) 0.28 (c) 0.10 (d) 0.12
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is multiplying 0.4 × 0.7 = 0.28, which is the probability of rain and catching the bus. Missing the bus has probability 0.3, so the required probability = 0.4 × 0.3 = 0.12, so the answer is 0.12.

141. From a group of 5 boys and 3 girls, a committee of 3 is formed at random. What is the probability that the committee has at least one girl?

Solution: The trap is computing only the exactly-one-girl case. Use the complement: P(no girl) = C(5,3)/C(8,3) = 10/56 = 5/28, so P(at least one girl) = 1 − 5/28 = 23/28, so the answer is 23/28.

150. An urn contains 4 red and 6 green balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. Given that at least one ball is red, what is the probability that both are red?
(a) 1/5 (b) 1/6 (c) 2/15 (d) 3/14
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing P(both red) = C(4,2)/C(10,2) = 2/15 without applying the condition. P(at least one red) = 1 − C(6,2)/C(10,2) = 1 − 15/45 = 30/45. Conditional probability = (6/45)/(30/45) = 6/30 = 1/5, so the answer is 1/5.
