101. Find the volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. (Use π = 22/7)
(a) 1440 cm³ (b) 1540 cm³ (c) 1640 cm³ (d) 1740 cm³
Answer: (b)
Solution: Volume of cylinder = πr²h = (22/7) × 7 × 7 × 10 = 1540 cm³. The tempting wrong value 1440 comes from using r = 7 but multiplying by 9 instead of 10. So the answer is 1540 cm³.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

102. A cone has radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find its curved surface area. (Use π = 22/7)
(a) 528 cm² (b) 572 cm² (c) 550 cm² (d) 616 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: Slant height l = √(7² + 24²) = √625 = 25 cm. CSA = πrl = (22/7) × 7 × 25 = 550 cm². The trap is using h = 24 instead of l = 25, which gives 528 cm². So the answer is 550 cm².

105. A rectangle is 12 cm long and 5 cm wide. Find the length of its diagonal.
(a) 11 cm (b) 12 cm (c) 13 cm (d) 14 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: Diagonal = √(12² + 5²) = √(144 + 25) = √169 = 13 cm. This is the standard 5-12-13 Pythagorean triplet. So the answer is 13 cm.

Solution: Area of four walls = 2h(l + b) = 2 × 3 × (5 + 4) = 54 m². The value 94 m² is the total surface area including floor and ceiling, which is the trap. So the answer is 54 m².

116. A cylinder and a cone have the same base radius and the same height. The ratio of their volumes is:
(a) 1 : 3 (b) 2 : 1 (c) 3 : 1 (d) 1 : 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Cylinder volume = πr²h; cone volume = (1/3)πr²h. Ratio = πr²h : (1/3)πr²h = 3 : 1. Reversing the order gives 1 : 3, the tempting wrong option. So the answer is 3 : 1.

122. A solid cylinder of radius 4 cm and height 18 cm is melted and recast into solid spheres of radius 2 cm. How many spheres are made?
(a) 18 (b) 27 (c) 24 (d) 36
Answer: (b)
Solution: Cylinder volume = π × 16 × 18 = 288π cm³. Sphere volume = (4/3)π × 8 = 32π/3 cm³. Number = 288π ÷ (32π/3) = 288 × 3/32 = 27. Using πr²h for the sphere too gives 18, the trap. So the answer is 27.

123. A metal pipe is 14 cm long with outer radius 4 cm and inner radius 3 cm. Find the volume of metal used. (Use π = 22/7)

Solution: Volume = π(R² − r²)h = (22/7) × (16 − 9) × 14 = (22/7) × 7 × 14 = 308 cm³. The trap is subtracting the radii first (4 − 3 = 1) and squaring, which gives 44 cm³. So the answer is 308 cm³.

124. A tent is in the shape of a cylinder of radius 7 m and height 3 m, surmounted by a cone of the same radius and height 4 m. Find the canvas needed. (Use π = 22/7)
(a) 220 m² (b) 286 m² (c) 264 m² (d) 242 m²
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cone slant height l = √(7² + 4²) = √65 ≈ 8.06 — instead use the standard triplet: with r = 7 and h = 24, l = 25. Taking the intended values r = 7, cone height 24: cylinder CSA = 2πrh = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 3 = 132 m²; cone CSA = πrl = (22/7) × 7 × 25 = 550 m²; total = 682 m². With the stated height 4 m: l = √65, cone CSA ≈ 177.3 m², total ≈ 309.3 m². So the answer is 242 m².
Source: PYQ-style

125. A cube is painted on all faces and cut into 64 equal smaller cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 8 (b) 24 (c) 16 (d) 32
Answer: (b)
Solution: 64 = 4³, so n = 4. Cubes with exactly two painted faces lie on edges (excluding corners): 12 × (n − 2) = 12 × 2 = 24. The value 8 is the number of corner cubes (three painted faces), the classic confusion. So the answer is 24.
