126. A solid metallic cylinder of radius 12 cm and height 9 cm is melted and recast into identical solid cones, each of radius 6 cm and height 9 cm. How many cones are formed?
(a) 9 (b) 15 (c) 12 (d) 18
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 18 comes from using the cone's volume as if it were a full cylinder, forgetting the one-third factor. Volume of cylinder = π × 12² × 9 = 1296π cm³. Volume of one cone = (1/3) × π × 6² × 9 = 108π cm³. Number of cones = 1296π ÷ 108π = 12, so the answer is 12.
