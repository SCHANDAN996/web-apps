Card 1
Front: What is the difference between perimeter and area?
Back: Perimeter is the total length of the boundary of a figure (1D, in units); area is the space enclosed inside it (2D, in square units).

Card 2
Front: What is the area of a triangle if you know its base and height?
Back: Area = ½ × base × height.

Card 3
Front: Which formula gives the area of a triangle when all three sides are known?
Back: Heron's formula: Area = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)], where s = (a+b+c)/2.

Card 4
Front: What is the area of an equilateral triangle with side a?
Back: Area = (√3/4) × a².

Card 5
Front: A square and a rectangle have the same perimeter — which has more area?
Back: The square. For a fixed perimeter, the square gives the maximum area among rectangles.

Card 6
Front: What is the area of a rhombus whose diagonals are d₁ and d₂?
Back: Area = ½ × d₁ × d₂.

Card 7
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the trapezium area formula?
Back: "Parallel sides shake hands, then climb the height and split in two": Area = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height.

Card 8
Front: What is the circumference of a circle of radius r?
Back: Circumference = 2πr.

Card 9
Front: What is the area of a circle, and what is the popular mnemonic for it?
Back: Area = πr². Mnemonic: "Pie are square" — π r² (sounds like "pie are squared").

Card 10
Front: What is the area of a sector of a circle with angle θ (in degrees)?
Back: Area = (θ/360) × πr².

Card 11
Front: How do you find the length of an arc of a circle?
Back: Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr, where θ is the central angle in degrees.

Card 12
Front: What is the difference between a sector and a segment of a circle?
Back: A sector is the "pizza slice" between two radii and an arc; a segment is the region between a chord and its arc.

Card 13
Front: What are the three surface areas of a cuboid (l × b × h)?
Back: Total surface area = 2(lb + bh + hl); lateral surface area = 2h(l + b); volume = lbh.

Card 14
Front: What is the diagonal of a cuboid with dimensions l, b, h?
Back: Diagonal = √(l² + b² + h²). For a cube of side a: diagonal = a√3.

Card 15
Front: What is the curved surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h?
Back: CSA = 2πrh. Total surface area = 2πr(h + r); volume = πr²h.

Card 16
Front: What is the volume of a cone, and how does it relate to a cylinder of the same base and height?
Back: Volume = (1/3)πr²h — exactly one-third of the cylinder's volume. Mnemonic: "Three cones fill one cylinder."

Card 17
Front: What is the slant height of a cone, and its curved surface area?
Back: Slant height l = √(r² + h²); CSA = πrl.

Card 18
Front: What are the surface area and volume of a sphere of radius r?
Back: Surface area = 4πr²; volume = (4/3)πr³. For a hemisphere: CSA = 2πr², TSA = 3πr², volume = (2/3)πr³.

Card 19
Front: If the side of a square is doubled, what happens to its perimeter and area?
Back: Perimeter doubles (×2); area becomes four times (×2²). Rule: area scales with the square of the scaling factor.

Card 20
Front: What is the mnemonic trick to remember sphere vs hemisphere total surface area?
Back: "Full ball = 4 walls (4πr²); half ball = 3 walls (3πr²)" — a hemisphere's TSA includes the flat circular base, so 2πr² + πr² = 3πr².
