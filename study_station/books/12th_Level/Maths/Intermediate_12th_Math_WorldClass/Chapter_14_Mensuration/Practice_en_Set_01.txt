1. The area of a rectangle with length 12 cm and breadth 8 cm is:
(a) 40 cm² (b) 96 cm² (c) 20 cm² (d) 48 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 40 cm² comes from adding 2(12 + 8), which is the perimeter, not the area. Area of rectangle = length × breadth = 12 × 8 = 96 cm², so the answer is 96 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

2. The perimeter of a square of side 15 cm is:
(a) 60 cm (b) 225 cm (c) 30 cm (d) 45 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 225 cm is the area (15 × 15), not the perimeter. Perimeter of square = 4 × side = 4 × 15 = 60 cm, so the answer is 60 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

3. The area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm is:
(a) 30 cm² (b) 60 cm² (c) 16 cm² (d) 32 cm²
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 60 cm² forgets the half in the formula. Area of triangle = ½ × base × height = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 cm², so the answer is 30 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

4. The circumference of a circle of radius 7 cm is (take π = 22/7):
(a) 22 cm (b) 154 cm (c) 88 cm (d) 44 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 154 cm² is the area (πr²), not the circumference. Circumference = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 7 = 44 cm, so the answer is 44 cm.

(a) 12 cm³ (b) 60 cm³ (c) 94 cm³ (d) 47 cm³
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 94 cm³ is the total surface area 2(20 + 15 + 12), not the volume. Volume = l × b × h = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 cm³, so the answer is 60 cm³.

12. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm is (take π = 22/7):

17. The diagonal of a rectangle with sides 6 cm and 8 cm is:
(a) 10 cm (b) 14 cm (c) 12 cm (d) 48 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 14 cm simply adds the two sides. Diagonal = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 cm, so the answer is 10 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

18. The area of an equilateral triangle of side 4 cm is (take √3 = 1.73):
(a) 8 cm² (b) 6.92 cm² (c) 13.84 cm² (d) 4 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 8 cm² wrongly uses ½ × 4 × 4, treating the side as the height. Area = (√3/4) × side² = (1.73/4) × 16 = 6.92 cm², so the answer is 6.92 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

19. The area of a rhombus whose diagonals are 10 cm and 8 cm is:

20. The perimeter of a semicircle of radius 7 cm is (take π = 22/7):
(a) 22 cm (b) 36 cm (c) 44 cm (d) 29 cm
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 22 cm is only the curved part (πr) and forgets the diameter. Perimeter of semicircle = πr + 2r = 22 + 14 = 36 cm, so the answer is 36 cm.

21. The area of a rectangular field is 540 m² and its length is 30 m. The cost of fencing its boundary at ₹12 per metre is:
(a) ₹1,056 (b) ₹1,296 (c) ₹1,152 (d) ₹1,200
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option ₹1,296 comes from using breadth 24 m by misreading the area division. Breadth = 540 ÷ 30 = 18 m; perimeter = 2(30 + 18) = 96 m; cost = 96 × 12 = ₹1,152, so the answer is ₹1,152.
Source: PYQ-style

22. The radius of a circle is increased by 10%. The percentage increase in its area is:
(a) 10% (b) 20% (c) 22% (d) 21%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 20% just doubles 10%, ignoring the cross term. New area factor = 1.1 × 1.1 = 1.21, i.e. an increase of 21%, so the answer is 21%.
Source: PYQ-style

23. A wheel of radius 35 cm makes 100 revolutions. The distance covered is (take π = 22/7):
(a) 110 m (b) 220 m (c) 22 m (d) 440 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 22 m forgets to multiply by the number of revolutions. Circumference = 2 × (22/7) × 35 = 220 cm; distance = 220 × 100 = 22,000 cm = 220 m, so the answer is 220 m.
Source: PYQ-style

24. The ratio of the volumes of two cubes is 8 : 27. The ratio of their total surface areas is:
(a) 4 : 9 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 8 : 27 (d) 16 : 81
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 8 : 27 wrongly assumes surface area scales like volume. Sides ratio = ∛8 : ∛27 = 2 : 3; surface area ratio = 2² : 3² = 4 : 9, so the answer is 4 : 9.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A cylinder and a cone have the same base radius and the same height. The ratio of their volumes is:
(a) 1 : 3 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 3 : 1 (d) 1 : 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 1 : 3 reverses the order, putting the cone first. Cylinder volume = πr²h and cone volume = (1/3)πr²h, so cylinder : cone = 3 : 1, so the answer is 3 : 1.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics
