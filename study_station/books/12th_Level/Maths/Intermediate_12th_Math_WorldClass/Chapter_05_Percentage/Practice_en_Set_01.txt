1. What is 25% of 480?
(a) 96 (b) 160 (c) 140 (d) 120
Answer: (d)
Solution: 25% means one-fourth, so 480 ÷ 4 = 120. The tempting wrong option 96 comes from wrongly taking 20% instead of 25%, so the answer is 120.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

2. Convert 3/5 into a percentage.
(a) 60% (b) 50% (c) 35% (d) 65%
Answer: (a)
Solution: 3/5 × 100 = 60%. The option 35% is a trap for those who just attach % to the numerator and denominator digits, so the answer is 60%.

21. The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the initial population was 10,000, what is the population at the end of two years?
(a) 10,000 (b) 9,900 (c) 9,800 (d) 10,100
Answer: (b)
Solution: After year 1: 10,000 × 1.10 = 11,000. After year 2: 11,000 × 0.90 = 9,900. The option 10,000 is the classic trap of assuming +10% and −10% cancel out, but the decrease applies to a larger base, so the answer is 9,900.
Source: PYQ-style

22. A's salary is 25% more than B's salary. By what percentage is B's salary less than A's?
(a) 25% (b) 15% (c) 20% (d) 30%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let B = 100, then A = 125. B is less than A by 25 on a base of 125, so 25/125 × 100 = 20%. The option 25% is the trap of using the same percentage in reverse, but the base changes, so the answer is 20%.
Source: PYQ-style

23. The price of an article is reduced by 20%. By what percentage must it be increased to restore the original price?
(a) 20% (b) 22% (c) 30% (d) 25%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let original price = 100; reduced price = 80. Required increase = 20 on a base of 80, so 20/80 × 100 = 25%. The option 20% is the trap of reversing with the same figure, so the answer is 25%.

24. In an election, a candidate who got 40% of the votes lost by 200 votes. How many votes were cast in total?
(a) 800 (b) 1,000 (c) 1,200 (d) 1,500
Answer: (b)
Solution: The winner got 60%, so the margin is 60% − 40% = 20% of total votes. Then 20% of total = 200, giving total = 1,000. The option 800 is a trap from dividing 200 by 25% instead of 20%, so the answer is 1,000.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A number is first increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net percentage change?
(a) No change (b) 2% decrease (c) 4% increase (d) 4% decrease
Answer: (d)
Solution: Net change = 20 − 20 − (20 × 20)/100 = −4%, i.e. a 4% decrease. The option "No change" is the trap of assuming equal opposite percentages cancel, but the second operation works on a changed base, so the answer is 4% decrease.
Source: PYQ-style
