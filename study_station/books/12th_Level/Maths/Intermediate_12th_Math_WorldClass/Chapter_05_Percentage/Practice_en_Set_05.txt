101. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The net change in the number is:
(a) No change (b) 2% decrease (c) 4% increase (d) 4% decrease
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is "no change", but the 20% decrease applies to the increased value, not the original. Net change = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%, so the answer is 4% decrease.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

102. If 30% of a number is 45, then 70% of the same number is:
(a) 105 (b) 90 (c) 120 (d) 150
Answer: (a)
Solution: Do not take 70% of 45 directly; first find the whole number: 45 × 100/30 = 150. Then 70% of 150 = 105, so the answer is 105.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

103. The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percentage should consumption be reduced so that expenditure remains the same?
(a) 25% (b) 30% (c) 15% (d) 20%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many pick 25%, but the reduction is calculated on the new price. Required reduction = 25/(100+25) × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.
Source: PYQ-style

104. In an election, a candidate who got 40% of the votes lost by 300 votes. The total number of votes polled was:
(a) 1200 (b) 2000 (c) 1500 (d) 1800
Answer: (c)
Solution: The loser got 40%, so the winner got 60%; the margin is 20% of the votes, not 40%. 20% of total = 300, so total = 1500, so the answer is 1500.
Source: PYQ-style

105. A's salary is 25% more than B's. By what percentage is B's salary less than A's?
(a) 25% (b) 20% (c) 22.5% (d) 33.33%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 25%, but the base changes to A's salary. Required percentage = 25/125 × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

106. If the length of a rectangle is increased by 10% and breadth decreased by 10%, the area:
(a) Increases by 1% (b) Decreases by 1% (c) Remains same (d) Decreases by 2%
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Remains same" is the trap; the changes compound. Net change = 10 − 10 − (10×10)/100 = −1%, so the answer is decreases by 1%.
Source: PYQ-style

107. 40% of a number is added to 120, the result is the number itself. The number is:
(a) 300 (b) 180 (c) 240 (d) 200
Answer: (d)
Solution: Do not equate 40% of the number to 120; the remaining 60% equals 120. Number = 120 × 100/60 = 200, so the answer is 200.
Source: PYQ-style

108. The population of a town increases by 10% every year. If the present population is 12100, the population two years ago was:
(a) 10000 (b) 11000 (c) 9800 (d) 10500
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dividing 12100 by 1.21 (not subtracting 20%) gives the past value. Population = 12100/(1.1 × 1.1) = 10000, so the answer is 10000.
Source: PYQ-style

109. A student scored 32% marks and failed by 40 marks. Another scored 42% and got 30 marks more than passing marks. The maximum marks are:
(a) 500 (b) 600 (c) 700 (d) 800
Answer: (c)
Solution: The gap between 42% and 32% is 10%, which equals 40 + 30 = 70 marks, not 40 alone. Maximum marks = 70 × 100/10 = 700, so the answer is 700.
Source: PYQ-style

110. If 60% of A = 30% of B, and B = 40% of C, then A is what percent of C?
(a) 40% (b) 20% (c) 30% (d) 25%
Answer: (b)
Solution: From 60% of A = 30% of B, A = B/2; and B = 0.4C, so A = 0.2C. Do not add the percentages directly, so the answer is 20%.
Source: PYQ-style

111. A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and expenditure increases by 10%. His savings increase by:
(a) 25% (b) 50% (c) 30% (d) 40%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let income be 100: expenditure 75, savings 25. New income 120, new expenditure 82.5, new savings 37.5. Increase = 12.5/25 × 100 = 50%, so the answer is 50%.
Source: PYQ-style

112. The value of a machine depreciates by 10% annually. If its present value is ₹81000, its value two years ago was:
(a) ₹90000 (b) ₹99000 (c) ₹100000 (d) ₹95000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Subtracting 20% from 81000 is wrong; divide by 0.9 twice. Value = 81000/(0.9 × 0.9) = 100000, so the answer is ₹100000.
Source: PYQ-style

113. In a class, 60% are boys. If 25% of boys and 20% of girls play cricket, what percentage of the class plays cricket?
(a) 23% (b) 22% (c) 24% (d) 25%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Averaging 25% and 20% to get 22.5% ignores the different group sizes. Cricket players = 25% of 60 + 20% of 40 = 15 + 8 = 23%, so the answer is 23%.
Source: PYQ-style

114. A number is first increased by 50% and then the result is decreased by 50%. The final number is what percent of the original?
(a) 100% (b) 90% (c) 80% (d) 75%
Answer: (d)
Solution: "100%" is the trap since the decrease acts on the larger value. Final = 1.5 × 0.5 = 0.75 of original, so the answer is 75%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

115. If x% of y is equal to y% of 100, then x equals:
(a) y (b) 100/y (c) 100 (d) y/100
Answer: (c)
Solution: x% of y = xy/100 and y% of 100 = y. Setting xy/100 = y gives x = 100, so the answer is 100.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

116. The price of an article is reduced by 20%. To restore it to the original price, the new price must be increased by:
(a) 25% (b) 20% (c) 30% (d) 16.67%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 20%, but the increase is on the reduced price. Required increase = 20/80 × 100 = 25%, so the answer is 25%.
Source: PYQ-style

117. In an examination, 35% of students failed in Hindi, 45% failed in English and 20% failed in both. The percentage of students who passed in both subjects is:
(a) 45% (b) 35% (c) 30% (d) 40%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Do not add 35 + 45 + 20; use inclusion-exclusion: failed in at least one = 35 + 45 − 20 = 60%. Passed in both = 100 − 60 = 40%, so the answer is 40%.
Source: PYQ-style

118. A trader marks his goods 40% above cost price and allows a discount of 10%. His profit percentage is:
(a) 30% (b) 24% (c) 28% (d) 26%
Answer: (d)
Solution: 40 − 10 = 30% is the trap because the discount is on the marked price. Profit = 40 − 10 − (40×10)/100 = 26%, so the answer is 26%.
Source: PYQ-style

119. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and the denominator decreased by 20%, the fraction becomes 3/2. The original fraction is:
(a) 5/4 (b) 1 (c) 3/4 (d) 2/3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let the fraction be N/D. Then 1.2N/0.8D = 3/2, so N/D = (3/2) × (0.8/1.2) = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

120. Fresh fruit contains 68% water and dry fruit contains 20% water. How much dry fruit can be obtained from 100 kg of fresh fruit?
(a) 40 kg (b) 45 kg (c) 32 kg (d) 50 kg
Answer: (a)
Solution: The pulp (non-water) stays constant: 32 kg in fresh fruit. In dry fruit, pulp is 80%, so dry fruit = 32 × 100/80 = 40 kg, so the answer is 40 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

121. The price of petrol increased by 20%, then decreased by 10%, and again increased by 10%. The net percentage change is:
(a) 20% increase (b) 18.8% increase (c) 16.4% increase (d) 22% increase
Answer: (b)
Solution: Adding 20 − 10 + 10 = 20% ignores compounding. Net factor = 1.2 × 0.9 × 1.1 = 1.188, so the answer is 18.8% increase.
Source: PYQ-style

122. In a factory, 40% of workers are skilled. If 60% of skilled and 20% of unskilled workers are permanent, what percentage of all workers are temporary?
(a) 68% (b) 60% (c) 64% (d) 72%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Permanent workers = 60% of 40 + 20% of 60 = 24 + 12 = 36%. Temporary = 100 − 36 = 64%, so the answer is 64%.
Source: PYQ-style

123. A man spends 80% of his income. His income increases by 25% and his expenditure increases by 20%. His savings increase by:
(a) 45% (b) 50% (c) 40% (d) 55%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let income be 100: expenditure 80, savings 20. New income 125, new expenditure 96, new savings 29. Increase = 9/20 × 100 = 45%, so the answer is 45%.
Source: PYQ-style

124. The population of a village was 8000. It increased by 10% in the first year and decreased by 10% in the second year, then increased by 5% in the third year. The population at the end of three years is:
(a) 8200 (b) 8400 (c) 8316 (d) 8360
Answer: (c)
Solution: Net is not 5% since changes compound: 8000 × 1.1 × 0.9 × 1.05 = 8000 × 1.0395 = 8316, so the answer is 8316.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Two numbers are respectively 20% and 50% more than a third number. The first number is what percentage of the second number?
(a) 80% (b) 75% (c) 70% (d) 85%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let the third number be 100; first = 120, second = 150. The trap is taking 50 − 20 = 30% difference. Required = 120/150 × 100 = 80%, so the answer is 80%.
Source: PYQ-style
