Questions 101–125: Hard

101. In an examination, 65% of the candidates passed in Mathematics, 48% passed in Physics, and 30% passed in both. What percentage of candidates failed in both subjects?
(a) 17% (b) 15% (c) 18% (d) 20%
Answer: (a) 17%
Solution: Percentage passing in at least one subject = n(M) + n(P) - n(M and P) = 65% + 48% - 30% = 83%. Percentage failing in both = 100% - 83% = 17%. The required value is 17%. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

102. In an examination, 35% of the students failed in English, 25% failed in Mathematics, and 15% failed in both. If 440 students passed in both subjects, find the total number of students who appeared in the examination.
(a) 750 (b) 800 (c) 900 (d) 850
Answer: (b) 800
Solution: Percentage failed in at least one subject = 35% + 25% - 15% = 45%. Percentage passed in both subjects = 100% - 45% = 55%. Given 55% = 440 => Total students = 440 / 0.55 = 800. The required value is 800. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

103. A candidate who gets 20% marks in an examination fails by 30 marks, but another candidate who gets 32% gets 42 marks more than the passing marks. Find the percentage of marks required to pass.
(a) 28% (b) 24% (c) 25% (d) 30%
Answer: (c) 25%
Solution: Difference in percentage = 32% - 20% = 12%. Difference in marks = 42 - (-30) = 72 marks. Maximum marks = 72 / 0.12 = 600 marks. Passing marks = 20% of 600 + 30 = 120 + 30 = 150 marks. Passing percentage = (150 / 600) * 100% = 25%. The required value is 25%. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

104. A candidate secures 30% marks in an exam and fails by 15 marks, while another candidate scores 40% marks and secures 35 marks more than the minimum pass mark. Find the maximum marks of the examination.
(a) 450 (b) 550 (c) 600 (d) 500
Answer: (d) 500
Solution: Percentage difference = 40% - 30% = 10%. Marks difference = 35 + 15 = 50 marks. Maximum marks = 50 / 0.10 = 500. The required value is 500. Hence, the correct option is (d).

105. Fresh fruit contains 68% water and dry fruit contains 20% water. How many kilograms of dry fruit can be obtained from 100 kg of fresh fruit?
(a) 40 kg (b) 32 kg (c) 48 kg (d) 50 kg
Answer: (a) 40 kg
Solution: Pulp content remains constant. In 100 kg fresh fruit, pulp = (100 - 68)% = 32% of 100 kg = 32 kg. In dry fruit, pulp is (100 - 20)% = 80%. Let dry fruit weight be W. 80% of W = 32 kg => W = 32 / 0.80 = 40 kg. The required value is 40 kg. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

106. Fresh grapes contain 80% water by weight, while raisins contain 10% water by weight. What weight of fresh grapes is needed to get 50 kg of raisins?
(a) 200 kg (b) 225 kg (c) 250 kg (d) 210 kg
Answer: (b) 225 kg
Solution: Pulp in 50 kg raisins = 50 * (1 - 0.10) = 45 kg. In fresh grapes, pulp is 20%. Let weight of grapes be G. 20% of G = 45 kg => G = 45 / 0.20 = 225 kg. The required value is 225 kg. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

107. In an alloy of 60 kg, there is 75% copper and the rest is zinc. How much zinc must be added to this alloy to make the percentage of copper 60%?
(a) 12 kg (b) 18 kg (c) 15 kg (d) 20 kg
Answer: (c) 15 kg
Solution: Copper is constant. Initial copper = 60 * 0.75 = 45 kg. In the new mixture, 45 kg represents 60% of total weight W. W = 45 / 0.60 = 75 kg. Zinc to be added = 75 - 60 = 15 kg. The required value is 15 kg. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

108. How many liters of water must be added to 40 liters of a 20% acid solution to decrease the concentration of acid to 16%?
(a) 8 liters (b) 12 liters (c) 15 liters (d) 10 liters
Answer: (d) 10 liters
Solution: Pure acid = 40 * 0.20 = 8 liters. In the new solution, 8 liters of acid must constitute 16% of total volume V. V = 8 / 0.16 = 50 liters. Water added = 50 - 40 = 10 liters. The required value is 10 liters. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

109. In a survey of 1,000 consumers, it was found that 720 like product Alpha and 450 like product Beta. What is the least number of consumers that must like both products?
(a) 170 (b) 150 (c) 180 (d) 200
Answer: (a) 170
Solution: Using Venn formula: n(A U B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A and B). Since total consumers is at most 1,000: 720 + 450 - n(A and B) <= 1,000 => 1,170 - n(A and B) <= 1,000 => n(A and B) >= 170. The required value is 170. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

110. In an examination, 70% of candidates passed in English and 80% passed in Mathematics. If 10% failed in both subjects and 144 candidates passed in both, find the total number of candidates.
(a) 200 (b) 240 (c) 250 (d) 300
Answer: (b) 240
Solution: Candidates passing in at least one subject = 100% - 10% = 90%. Using Venn: n(E U M) = n(E) + n(M) - n(E and M) => 90% = 70% + 80% - n(E and M) => n(E and M) = 150% - 90% = 60%. Given 60% = 144 => Total candidates = 144 / 0.60 = 240. The required value is 240. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

111. A vessel contains 80 liters of a solution containing 15% alcohol. How many liters of pure alcohol must be added to make it a 32% alcohol solution?
(a) 18 liters (b) 24 liters (c) 20 liters (d) 16 liters
Answer: (c) 20 liters
Solution: Non-alcohol component (water) is constant: 80 * (1 - 0.15) = 80 * 0.85 = 68 liters. In new solution, water constitutes 100% - 32% = 68%. Total new volume = 68 / 0.68 = 100 liters. Alcohol added = 100 - 80 = 20 liters. The required value is 20 liters. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

112. One liter of water is evaporated from 6 liters of a sugar solution containing 4% sugar. What is the percentage of sugar in the remaining solution?
(a) 5% (b) 4.5% (c) 5.2% (d) 4.8%
Answer: (d) 4.8%
Solution: Amount of sugar = 6 * 0.04 = 0.24 liters. Remaining volume = 6 - 1 = 5 liters. Percentage of sugar = (0.24 / 5) * 100% = 4.8%. The required value is 4.8%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

113. In an examination, student X scored 36% marks and failed by 24 marks, while student Y scored 48% marks and got 36 marks more than the pass mark. What is the passing percentage for this examination?
(a) 40.8% (b) 41.5% (c) 42% (d) 39.5%
Answer: (a) 40.8%
Solution: Difference in percentage = 48% - 36% = 12%. Difference in marks = 36 - (-24) = 60 marks. Maximum marks = 60 / 0.12 = 500. Pass marks = 36% of 500 + 24 = 180 + 24 = 204. Passing percentage = (204 / 500) * 100% = 40.8%. The required value is 40.8%. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

114. In a class, 60% of students play Cricket, 50% play Football, and 20% play neither. What percentage of students play both games?
(a) 25% (b) 30% (c) 35% (d) 20%
Answer: (b) 30%
Solution: Students who play at least one game = 100% - 20% = 80%. Using Venn: n(C U F) = n(C) + n(F) - n(C and F) => 80% = 60% + 50% - n(C and F) => n(C and F) = 110% - 80% = 30%. The required value is 30%. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

115. A watermelon weighing 20 kg contains 96% water by weight. After being kept in the sun for some days, some water evaporates so that it contains 95% water. What is its present weight?
(a) 15 kg (b) 18 kg (c) 16 kg (d) 17.5 kg
Answer: (c) 16 kg
Solution: Pulp content is constant: 20 * (1 - 0.96) = 20 * 0.04 = 0.8 kg. In present state, water is 95%, so pulp is 5%. Let new weight be W. 5% of W = 0.8 kg => W = 0.8 / 0.05 = 16 kg. The required value is 16 kg. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

120. A sample of 50 liters of glycerine is found to be adulterated to the extent of 20%. How much pure glycerine should be added to it so as to bring down the percentage of impurity to 5%?
(a) 140 liters (b) 160 liters (c) 120 liters (d) 150 liters
Answer: (d) 150 liters
Solution: Amount of impurity is constant: 50 * 0.20 = 10 liters. In the final mixture, this 10 liters must represent 5% of total volume V. V = 10 / 0.05 = 200 liters. Pure glycerine to add = 200 - 50 = 150 liters. The required value is 150 liters. Hence, the correct option is (d).
