Q: If Price of an item increases by a/b, by what fraction must consumption decrease to keep expenditure constant?
A: Consumption must decrease by a / (b + a).

Q: What is the net percentage change when a quantity increases by x% and then decreases by x%?
A: A net decrease of (x^2 / 100)%.

Q: What is the formula for the net percentage change of two successive changes of +x% and +y%?
A: Net change = x + y + (xy / 100)%.

Q: In an election, if the winner gets W% of valid votes in a two-candidate contest, what is the winning margin percentage?
A: (2W - 100)% of valid votes.

Q: How is percentage increase in income related to expenditure and savings?
A: % change in Income is the weighted average of % change in Expenditure and % change in Savings (weights are E and S).

Q: If a town's population P increases by r% annually, what will be the population after n years?
A: P * (1 + r/100)^n.
