Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percentage must a household reduce its consumption so that the total expenditure remains unchanged?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 16 2/3% (d) 22.5%
Answer: (a) 20%
Solution: Using the formula for constant expenditure: Reduction = [r / (100 + r)] * 100% = [25 / 125] * 100% = 20%. The required value is 20%. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. The price of petrol decreased by 20%. By how much percentage can a motorist increase his consumption such that his expenditure remains constant?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 16 2/3% (d) 30%
Answer: (b) 25%
Solution: Using the formula: Increase = [r / (100 - r)] * 100% = [20 / 80] * 100% = 25%. The required value is 25%. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

28. The price of cooking oil increases by 30%, and a family reduces its consumption such that the expenditure increases by only 10.5%. What is the percentage decrease in consumption?
(a) 12% (b) 18% (c) 15% (d) 19.5%
Answer: (c) 15%
Solution: Let initial price and consumption be 100 each, so expenditure = 10,000. New price = 130, new expenditure = 11,050. New consumption = 11,050 / 130 = 85. Percentage decrease = 100 - 85 = 15%. The required value is 15%. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

29. A number is first increased by 20% and then decreased by 15%. What is the net percentage change in the number?
(a) 5% increase (b) 2% decrease (c) 3% increase (d) 2% increase
Answer: (d) 2% increase
Solution: Net change formula: a + b + (ab / 100) = 20 - 15 + (20 * (-15) / 100) = 5 - 3 = +2%. Thus, an increase of 2%. The required value is 2% increase. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. The length of a rectangle is increased by 30% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. What is the percentage change in the area of the rectangle?
(a) 4% increase (b) 10% increase (c) 6% increase (d) 4% decrease
Answer: (a) 4% increase
Solution: Net change in area = l + b + (lb / 100) = 30 - 20 - (30 * 20 / 100) = 10 - 6 = +4%. The required value is 4% increase. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

31. If the radius of a circle is increased by 25%, by what percentage does its area increase?
(a) 50% (b) 56.25% (c) 62.5% (d) 52.5%
Answer: (b) 56.25%
Solution: Since area is proportional to r^2, net percentage change = 25 + 25 + (25 * 25 / 100) = 50 + 6.25 = 56.25%. The required value is 56.25%. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

32. If each side of a cube is increased by 10%, find the percentage increase in its total surface area.
(a) 33.1% (b) 20% (c) 21% (d) 30%
Answer: (c) 21%
Solution: Surface area is proportional to side^2. Percentage increase = 10 + 10 + (10 * 10 / 100) = 21%. The required value is 21%. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

33. If each side of a cube is increased by 10%, find the percentage increase in its volume.
(a) 30% (b) 21% (c) 33% (d) 33.1%
Answer: (d) 33.1%
Solution: Volume is proportional to side^3. Multiplier = (1.1)^3 = 1.331. Percentage increase = (1.331 - 1) * 100% = 33.1%. The required value is 33.1%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

34. The radius of a right circular cylinder is decreased by 20% and its height is increased by 50%. What is the percentage change in its volume?
(a) 4% decrease (b) 4% increase (c) 10% decrease (d) 10% increase
Answer: (a) 4% decrease
Solution: Volume is proportional to r^2 * h. Multiplier = (0.8)^2 * 1.5 = 0.64 * 1.5 = 0.96. Net change = (0.96 - 1) * 100% = -4% (a 4% decrease). The required value is 4% decrease. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

35. Find the single discount equivalent to two successive discounts of 20% and 15%.
(a) 35% (b) 32% (c) 30% (d) 28%
Answer: (b) 32%
Solution: Equivalent discount = d1 + d2 - (d1 * d2 / 100) = 20 + 15 - (300 / 100) = 35 - 3 = 32%. The required value is 32%. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

36. Find the single discount equivalent to three successive discounts of 10%, 20%, and 25%.
(a) 55% (b) 45% (c) 46% (d) 48%
Answer: (c) 46%
Solution: Net multiplier = 0.90 * 0.80 * 0.75 = 0.72 * 0.75 = 0.54. Equivalent discount = (1 - 0.54) * 100% = 46%. The required value is 46%. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

37. The price of an article is reduced by 25%. By what percentage must the new price be increased to restore it to the original price?
(a) 25% (b) 20% (c) 30% (d) 33 1/3%
Answer: (d) 33 1/3%
Solution: Using formula: [r / (100 - r)] * 100% = [25 / 75] * 100% = 33 1/3%. The required value is 33 1/3%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

38. A reduction of 20% in the price of rice enables a customer to obtain 4 kg more for Rs 320. Find the original price per kg.
(a) Rs 20 (b) Rs 16 (c) Rs 24 (d) Rs 18
Answer: (a) Rs 20
Solution: Money saved = 20% of 320 = Rs 64. Reduced price per kg = 64 / 4 = Rs 16. Original price = 16 / 0.80 = Rs 20 per kg. The required value is Rs 20. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

39. An increase of 25% in the price of wheat causes a buyer to purchase 5 kg less for Rs 600. Find the increased price per kg.
(a) Rs 24 (b) Rs 30 (c) Rs 28 (d) Rs 32
Answer: (b) Rs 30
Solution: Extra cost for same quantity = 25% of 600 = Rs 150. This corresponds to the deficit of 5 kg. Increased price per kg = 150 / 5 = Rs 30. The required value is Rs 30. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

40. The tax on a commodity is diminished by 10% and its consumption increases by 10%. Find the effect on revenue.
(a) No change (b) 1% increase (c) 1% decrease (d) 2% decrease
Answer: (c) 1% decrease
Solution: Net change = -10 + 10 - (10 * 10 / 100) = -1%. Revenue decreases by 1%. The required value is 1% decrease. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

41. A man's wage was reduced by 50%. Again, the reduced wage was increased by 50%. Find his net loss percentage.
(a) 0% (b) 20% (c) 50% (d) 25%
Answer: (d) 25%
Solution: Net multiplier = (1 - 0.50) * (1 + 0.50) = 0.50 * 1.50 = 0.75. Loss = (1 - 0.75) * 100% = 25%. The required value is 25%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

42. If the base radius of a cone is increased by 20% and its vertical height is decreased by 25%, what is the percentage change in its volume?
(a) 8% increase (b) 5% increase (c) 4% decrease (d) 8% decrease
Answer: (a) 8% increase
Solution: Volume of cone is proportional to r^2 * h. Multiplier = (1.2)^2 * 0.75 = 1.44 * 0.75 = 1.08. Volume increases by (1.08 - 1) * 100% = 8%. The required value is 8% increase. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

43. The price of an entry ticket to an exhibition is reduced by 25%, which causes total sales of tickets to increase by 40%. What is the overall percentage change in revenue?
(a) 15% increase (b) 5% increase (c) 10% increase (d) 5% decrease
Answer: (b) 5% increase
Solution: Revenue = Price * Quantity. Multiplier = 0.75 * 1.40 = 1.05. Hence, revenue increases by 5%. The required value is 5% increase. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

44. A person gives 30% of his wealth to his daughter, 40% of the remainder to his son, and the remaining Rs 21,000 to charity. Find his total initial wealth.
(a) Rs 60,000 (b) Rs 45,000 (c) Rs 50,000 (d) Rs 55,000
Answer: (c) Rs 50,000
Solution: After giving daughter 30%, remaining is 70%. Son gets 40% of 70% = 28%. Final remainder = 70% - 28% = 42%. 42% = 21,000 => Total = 21,000 / 0.42 = Rs 50,000. The required value is Rs 50,000. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

45. If the side of a square is increased by 30%, find the percentage increase in its perimeter.
(a) 69% (b) 60% (c) 33% (d) 30%
Answer: (d) 30%
Solution: Perimeter = 4 * side. Since perimeter is directly linear with side length, it increases by exactly the same percentage, 30%. The required value is 30%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

46. A student multiplied a number by 3/5 instead of 5/3. What is the percentage error in the calculation?
(a) 64% (b) 36% (c) 40% (d) 50%
Answer: (a) 64%
Solution: Let the number be 15. Correct value = 15 * (5/3) = 25. Incorrect value = 15 * (3/5) = 9. Error = 25 - 9 = 16. Percentage error = (16 / 25) * 100% = 64%. The required value is 64%. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

47. A number is mistakenly divided by 5 instead of being multiplied by 5. Find the percentage error in the result.
(a) 80% (b) 96% (c) 24% (d) 90%
Answer: (b) 96%
Solution: Let the number be 5. Correct value = 5 * 5 = 25. Incorrect value = 5 / 5 = 1. Error = 25 - 1 = 24. Percentage error = (24 / 25) * 100% = 96%. The required value is 96%. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

48. If the price of tea is reduced by 10%, a person can buy 250 grams more tea for Rs 270. Find the original price of tea per kg.
(a) Rs 108 (b) Rs 100 (c) Rs 120 (d) Rs 115
Answer: (c) Rs 120
Solution: Savings = 10% of 270 = Rs 27. This buys 0.25 kg. Reduced price per kg = 27 / 0.25 = Rs 108. Original price = 108 / 0.90 = Rs 120 per kg. The required value is Rs 120. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

49. In an examination, a candidate must secure 33% marks to pass. He gets 125 marks and fails by 40 marks. Find the maximum marks of the examination.
(a) 450 (b) 600 (c) 550 (d) 500
Answer: (d) 500
Solution: Passing marks = 125 + 40 = 165. Since 33% of maximum marks = 165, Maximum marks = 165 / 0.33 = 500. The required value is 500. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

50. The price of an item was first reduced by 20% and later increased by 25%. What is the net percentage change in the price compared to the original price?
(a) 5% increase (b) 0% (No change) (c) 5% decrease (d) 2% increase
Answer: (b) 0% (No change)
Solution: Net multiplier = (1 - 0.20) * (1 + 0.25) = 0.80 * 1.25 = 1.00. Hence, there is no change (0% change). The required value is 0% (No change). Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
