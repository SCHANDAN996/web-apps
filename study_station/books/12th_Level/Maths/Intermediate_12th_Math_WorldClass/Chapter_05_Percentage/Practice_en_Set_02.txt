26. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The net change in the number is:
(a) No change (b) 4% increase (c) 4% decrease (d) 2% decrease
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option is "no change", but the 20% decrease applies to the increased value, not the original. Net change = x + y + xy/100 = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%, so the answer is a 4% decrease.

27. If the price of sugar rises by 25%, by what percentage should consumption be reduced so that expenditure remains the same?
(a) 25% (b) 15% (c) 30% (d) 20%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students pick 25%, but the reduction is on the new, higher price. Required reduction = 25/(100+25) × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.

28. In an election, a candidate who got 40% of the votes lost by 300 votes. The total number of votes polled was:
(a) 1500 (b) 1200 (c) 1800 (d) 2000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The winner got 60%, so the margin is 60% − 40% = 20% of total votes. Do not equate 300 with 40% — that is the trap. 20% of total = 300, so total = 1500, so the answer is 1500.

29. The population of a town increases by 10% every year. If the present population is 1,21,000, what was it two years ago?
(a) 1,01,000 (b) 1,10,000 (c) 99,000 (d) 1,00,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: Simply subtracting 20% of 1,21,000 is wrong because growth is compounded. Population two years ago = 1,21,000 / (1.1 × 1.1) = 1,21,000 / 1.21 = 1,00,000, so the answer is 1,00,000.

31. A student scored 32% marks and failed by 40 marks. Another student scored 42% marks and got 30 marks more than the pass mark. The maximum marks are:
(a) 700 (b) 600 (c) 500 (d) 800

Solution: The gap between the two scores is 42% − 32% = 10%, which equals 40 + 30 = 70 marks. Do not use 40 − 30. So 10% of maximum = 70, giving maximum = 700, so the answer is 700.

37. If 15% of x is equal to 20% of y, then 25% of x is equal to what percent of y?

42. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and the denominator is decreased by 20%, the fraction becomes 3/2. The original fraction is:
(a) 1 (b) 5/4 (c) 4/5 (d) 3/4

Solution: Let the fraction be n/d. Then (1.2n)/(0.8d) = 3/2, so n/d = (3/2) × (0.8/1.2) = (3/2) × (2/3) = 1. Reversing the multiplier gives 9/8-type errors, so the answer is 1.
