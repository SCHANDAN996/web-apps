Questions 51–75: Medium

51. A person spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%. By what percentage do his savings increase?
(a) 50% (b) 40% (c) 30% (d) 25%
Answer: (a) 50%
Solution: Let initial income be 100, expenditure = 75, savings = 25. New income = 120. New expenditure = 75 * 1.10 = 82.5. New savings = 120 - 82.5 = 37.5. Increase in savings = 37.5 - 25 = 12.5. Percentage increase = (12.5 / 25) * 100% = 50%. The required value is 50%. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. A man spends 80% of his income. When his income is increased by 30%, he increases his expenditure by 37.5%. What is the percentage change in his savings?
(a) 5% increase (b) 0% (No change) (c) 10% decrease (d) 7.5% increase
Answer: (b) 0% (No change)
Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 80, savings = 20. New income = 130. New expenditure = 80 + 80 * (3/8) = 80 + 30 = 110. New savings = 130 - 110 = 20. Change in savings = 0%. The required value is 0% (No change). Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

53. If the income tax is increased by 19%, the net income is reduced by 1%. Find the original rate of income tax.
(a) 4% (b) 6% (c) 5% (d) 7.5%
Answer: (c) 5%
Solution: The increase in tax equals the reduction in net income. Thus, 19% of Tax = 1% of Net Income => Tax / Net Income = 1 / 19. Total Income = Tax + Net Income = 1 + 19 = 20. Rate of Tax = (1 / 20) * 100% = 5%. The required value is 5%. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

54. If income tax is increased by 17%, net income decreases by 3%. What was the original rate of income tax?
(a) 12% (b) 18% (c) 20% (d) 15%
Answer: (d) 15%
Solution: Change in Tax = Change in Net Income: 17% of Tax = 3% of Net Income => Tax / Net Income = 3 / 17. Total Income = 3 + 17 = 20. Tax rate = (3 / 20) * 100% = 15%. The required value is 15%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

55. The ratio of expenditure and savings of a person is 5 : 3. If his income increases by 12% and expenditure increases by 15%, find the percentage increase in his savings.
(a) 7% (b) 8% (c) 9% (d) 6%
Answer: (a) 7%
Solution: Let expenditure = 500 and savings = 300, so income = 800. New income = 800 * 1.12 = 896. New expenditure = 500 * 1.15 = 575. New savings = 896 - 575 = 321. Increase in savings = 321 - 300 = 21. Percentage increase = (21 / 300) * 100% = 7%. The required value is 7%. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 60, savings = 40. New income = 115, new expenditure = 60 * 1.05 = 63. New savings = 115 - 63 = 52. Increase in savings = 52 - 40 = 12. Percentage increase = (12 / 40) * 100% = 30%. The required value is 30%. Hence, the correct option is (b).

57. A worker experiences an 8% cut in wages followed by a 12% rise in the subsequent year. What is the overall percentage change in his wages over the two years?
(a) 4% increase (b) 3.04% decrease (c) 3.04% increase (d) 4% decrease
Answer: (c) 3.04% increase
Solution: Using net change: -8 + 12 + (-8 * 12 / 100) = 4 - 0.96 = +3.04%. This is a 3.04% increase. The required value is 3.04% increase. Hence, the correct option is (c).

58. A man spends 20% of his monthly income on house rent, 25% of the remaining on food, and 10% of the balance on education. If he is left with Rs 27,000, find his total monthly income.
(a) Rs 48,000 (b) Rs 60,000 (c) Rs 54,000 (d) Rs 50,000
Answer: (d) Rs 50,000
Solution: Multiplier for remainder = (1 - 0.20) * (1 - 0.25) * (1 - 0.10) = 0.80 * 0.75 * 0.90 = 0.60 * 0.90 = 0.54. Income = 27,000 / 0.54 = Rs 50,000. The required value is Rs 50,000. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

59. An employee contributes 12% of basic salary to provident fund and pays 10% of the remainder as tax. If his net take-home salary is Rs 39,600, find his basic salary.
(a) Rs 50,000 (b) Rs 48,000 (c) Rs 45,000 (d) Rs 52,000
Answer: (a) Rs 50,000
Solution: Remaining fraction = (1 - 0.12) * (1 - 0.10) = 0.88 * 0.90 = 0.792. Basic salary = 39,600 / 0.792 = Rs 50,000. The required value is Rs 50,000. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

60. Monthly income of two persons are in the ratio 4 : 5 and their monthly expenses are in the ratio 7 : 9. If each saves Rs 2,500 per month, find the monthly income of the first person.
(a) Rs 25,000 (b) Rs 20,000 (c) Rs 18,000 (d) Rs 24,000
Answer: (b) Rs 20,000
Solution: Let incomes be 4x and 5x. Then (4x - 2500) / (5x - 2500) = 7 / 9. Cross-multiplying: 9(4x - 2500) = 7(5x - 2500) => 36x - 22,500 = 35x - 17,500 => x = 5,000. Income of first person = 4x = 4 * 5,000 = Rs 20,000. The required value is Rs 20,000. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

61. A person saves 15% of his income. If his income increases by 20% and he still saves the same absolute amount, find the percentage increase in his expenditure.
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 23 9/17% (d) 18 2/3%
Answer: (c) 23 9/17%
Solution: Let original income = 100, savings = 15, expenditure = 85. New income = 120, new savings = 15. New expenditure = 120 - 15 = 105. Increase in expenditure = 105 - 85 = 20. Percentage increase = (20 / 85) * 100% = 400 / 17% = 23 9/17%. The required value is 23 9/17%. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

62. A man spends 70% of his income. If his income increases by 24% and his savings increase by 10%, find the percentage increase in his expenditure.

Answer: (d) 30%
Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 70, savings = 30. Total increase in income = 24. Increase in savings = 10% of 30 = 3. Increase in expenditure = 24 - 3 = 21. Percentage increase in expenditure = (21 / 70) * 100% = 30%. The required value is 30%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

63. An agent gets 5% commission on all sales up to Rs 10,000 and 4% commission on all sales exceeding this amount. If he remits Rs 31,100 to his company after deducting his commission, find total sales.
(a) Rs 32,500 (b) Rs 33,000 (c) Rs 30,000 (d) Rs 35,000
Answer: (a) Rs 32,500
Solution: Suppose the agent got 4% on entire sales. Then commission on first 10,000 would be 1% less, so company would receive 1% of 10,000 = Rs 100 more, total Rs 31,200. Since company gets 96% of total sales, Total sales = 31,200 / 0.96 = Rs 32,500. The required value is Rs 32,500. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

64. A salesman is allowed 9% commission on total sales and an additional bonus of 1% on sales over Rs 20,000. If his total earning is Rs 6,800, find the total sales.
(a) Rs 68,000 (b) Rs 70,000 (c) Rs 75,000 (d) Rs 65,000
Answer: (b) Rs 70,000
Solution: If he received 1% bonus on the entire sales, he would earn 1% of 20,000 = Rs 200 more, making total earnings 6,800 + 200 = Rs 7,000 at a flat 10% rate. Hence, total sales = 7,000 / 0.10 = Rs 70,000. The required value is Rs 70,000. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

65. A person spends 50% of his monthly income on household items and out of the remaining, he spends 50% on transport, 25% on entertainment, 10% on sports, and saves the remaining Rs 900. What is his monthly income?
(a) Rs 10,000 (b) Rs 15,000 (c) Rs 12,000 (d) Rs 14,000
Answer: (c) Rs 12,000
Solution: Remaining after household = 50%. Out of this 50%, he spends 50% + 25% + 10% = 85%, leaving 15%. Thus savings = 15% of 50% = 7.5% of total income. Total income = 900 / 0.075 = Rs 12,000. The required value is Rs 12,000. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (d) 42%
Solution: Let total staff be 100. Females = 40, Males = 60. Unmarried females = 30% of 40 = 12. Unmarried males = 50% of 60 = 30. Total unmarried staff = 12 + 30 = 42, which is 42%. The required value is 42%. Hence, the correct option is (d).

74. A man's salary was reduced by 10% and then the reduced salary was increased by 10%. His current salary compared to original salary is:
(a) 1% more (b) The same (c) 2% less (d) 1% less
Answer: (d) 1% less
Solution: Net change = -10 + 10 - (100 / 100) = -1%. Hence, it is 1% less than original salary. The required value is 1% less. Hence, the correct option is (d).

75. In an exam, passing marks are 40%. A candidate gets 180 marks and fails by 20 marks. What are the maximum marks?
(a) 450 (b) 500 (c) 400 (d) 600
Answer: (b) 500
Solution: Passing marks = 180 + 20 = 200. Maximum marks = 200 / 0.40 = 500. The required value is 500. Hence, the correct option is (b).
