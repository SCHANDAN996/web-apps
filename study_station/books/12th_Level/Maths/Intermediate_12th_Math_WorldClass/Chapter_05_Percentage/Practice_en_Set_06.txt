126. In a college, 20% of the students failed in Mathematics, 15% failed in English, and 10% failed in both. A student is selected at random. If he failed in English, what is the probability that he also failed in Mathematics?
(a) 2/3 (b) 1/2 (c) 3/4 (d) 1/3
Answer: (a) 2/3
Solution: Conditional probability P(Math | English) = P(Math and English) / P(English) = 10% / 15% = 10 / 15 = 2/3. The required value is 2/3. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

127. A company gives 12% commission to its salesman on total sales up to Rs 15,000 and 15% on sales exceeding Rs 15,000. If the salesman deposits Rs 72,350 in the company after deducting his commission, what is the total sales amount?
(a) Rs 82,500 (b) Rs 85,000 (c) Rs 90,000 (d) Rs 80,000
Answer: (b) Rs 85,000
Solution: Suppose the salesman got 15% on total sales. He would get (15% - 12%) = 3% more on the first 15,000, which is Rs 450. Then company would receive 72,350 - 450 = Rs 71,900. Company gets (100% - 15%) = 85% of total sales. Total sales = 71,900 / 0.85 = Rs 85,000. The required value is Rs 85,000. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

128. In an election between two contestants, 10% of voters did not cast their votes and 2,500 votes were declared invalid. The winning candidate secured 55% of the valid votes and won by a margin of 2,000 votes. Find the total number of enrolled voters.
(a) 22,500 (b) 28,000 (c) 25,000 (d) 30,000
Answer: (c) 25,000
Solution: Winner's lead in valid votes = 55% - 45% = 10% of valid votes. 10% of valid votes = 2,000 => Valid votes = 20,000. Total votes polled = 20,000 + 2,500 = 22,500. Since 10% didn't vote, polled votes = 90% of enrolled. Total enrolled = 22,500 / 0.90 = 25,000. The required value is 25,000. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

129. A person invests 25% of his capital at 4%, 40% of his capital at 5%, and the remainder at 10%. What average annual percentage return does he receive on his total capital?
(a) 6.0% (b) 7.0% (c) 5.8% (d) 6.5%
Answer: (d) 6.5%
Solution: Remainder capital percentage = 100% - (25% + 40%) = 35%. Average return = (0.25 * 4%) + (0.40 * 5%) + (0.35 * 10%) = 1.0% + 2.0% + 3.5% = 6.5%. The required value is 6.5%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

130. In an examination paper of 5 subjects, each subject carries a maximum of 100 marks. A student scores in the ratio 5 : 6 : 7 : 8 : 9 across the five subjects. If his overall aggregate score is 70%, in how many subjects did he score more than 75% marks?
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Total maximum marks = 5 * 100 = 500. Total marks scored = 70% of 500 = 350. Sum of ratio parts = 5 + 6 + 7 + 8 + 9 = 35 parts. Value of 1 part = 350 / 35 = 10 marks. Marks in subjects are 50, 60, 70, 80, 90. The subjects with more than 75 marks (75%) are those with 80 and 90, which are exactly 2 subjects. The required value is 2. Hence, the correct option is (a).

131. A manufacturer sells an article to a wholesale dealer at a profit of 10%. The wholesale dealer sells it to a shopkeeper at 20% profit. The shopkeeper sells it to a customer for Rs 56,100 at a loss of 15%. Find the cost of production for the manufacturer.
(a) Rs 48,000 (b) Rs 50,000 (c) Rs 52,000 (d) Rs 54,000
Answer: (b) Rs 50,000
Solution: Let production cost be C. C * 1.10 * 1.20 * 0.85 = 56,100 => C * 1.32 * 0.85 = 56,100 => C * 1.122 = 56,100 => C = 56,100 / 1.122 = Rs 50,000. The required value is Rs 50,000. Hence, the correct option is (b).

133. The price of raw materials has increased by 15%, and labor cost has increased from 25% of the cost of raw materials to 30% of the cost of raw materials. By what percentage should the use of raw material be reduced to keep the total manufacturing cost the same?
(a) 20.5% (b) 18.25% (c) 21.0% (d) 19.73%
Answer: (d) 19.73%
Solution: Initially, let raw material cost = 100. Labor cost = 25. Total cost = 125. Now raw material price is 115, and labor is 30% of raw material, so total cost per unit of raw material = 115 + 0.30 * 115 = 115 * 1.30 = 149.5. To keep expenditure at 125, consumption factor = 125 / 149.5 = 0.80267. Reduction = (1 - 0.80267) * 100% = 19.73%. The required value is 19.73%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

134. A large watermelon weighs 40 kg with 98% water content. After sitting in the sun, water evaporates until the water content is 95%. What is the new weight of the watermelon?
(a) 16 kg (b) 20 kg (c) 24 kg (d) 18 kg
Answer: (a) 16 kg
Solution: Solid pulp weight = 40 * (1 - 0.98) = 40 * 0.02 = 0.8 kg. In the final state, water is 95%, so pulp is 5%. New total weight = 0.8 / 0.05 = 16 kg. The required value is 16 kg. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

135. In a village of 6,000 residents, the number of males increases by 8% and the number of females increases by 10% in one year. As a result, the population becomes 6,520. Find the ratio of males to females in the village originally.
(a) 3 : 2 (b) 2 : 1 (c) 1 : 2 (d) 5 : 4
Answer: (b) 2 : 1
Solution: Total increase = 6,520 - 6,000 = 520. If all 6,000 grew by 8%, the increase would be 6,000 * 0.08 = 480. The excess increase of 520 - 480 = 40 is due to the additional 2% on females. Thus, 2% of females = 40 => Females = 40 / 0.02 = 2,000. Males = 6,000 - 2,000 = 4,000. Ratio of males to females = 4,000 : 2,000 = 2 : 1. The required value is 2 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

136. A man deposits 10% of his salary in a provident fund, spends 20% of the remainder on house rent, and 30% of the balance on children's education. If he is left with Rs 25,200, find his total salary.
(a) Rs 45,000 (b) Rs 48,000 (c) Rs 50,000 (d) Rs 52,000
Answer: (c) Rs 50,000
Solution: Net fraction remaining = (1 - 0.10) * (1 - 0.20) * (1 - 0.30) = 0.90 * 0.80 * 0.70 = 0.504. Total salary = 25,200 / 0.504 = Rs 50,000. The required value is Rs 50,000. Hence, the correct option is (c).

137. In a competitive exam of 150 questions, each carrying 1 mark, candidate scores 70% in the first 90 questions. What percentage of the remaining 60 questions must he answer correctly to achieve an overall score of 60%?
(a) 40% (b) 50% (c) 55% (d) 45%
Answer: (d) 45%
Solution: Total marks required = 60% of 150 = 90 marks. Marks obtained in first 90 questions = 70% of 90 = 63 marks. Marks needed from remaining 60 questions = 90 - 63 = 27 marks. Required percentage = (27 / 60) * 100% = 45%. The required value is 45%. Hence, the correct option is (d).

141. A sample of 40 liters of milk solution contains 10% water. How many liters of pure milk should be added to reduce the water concentration to 8%?

Answer: (d) 10 liters
Solution: Water quantity is constant: 40 * 0.10 = 4 liters. In the new solution, 4 liters represents 8% of total volume V. V = 4 / 0.08 = 50 liters. Pure milk to add = 50 - 40 = 10 liters. The required value is 10 liters. Hence, the correct option is (d).

144. The price of an article was increased by p%. Later, the new price was decreased by p%. If the latest price was Rs 1, then the original price was:
(a) 1 / (1 - p^2) (b) (100 - p^2) / 100 (c) 10,000 / (10,000 - p^2) (d) (10,000 + p^2) / 10,000
Answer: (c) 10,000 / (10,000 - p^2)
Solution: Let original price be P0. P0 * (1 + p/100) * (1 - p/100) = 1 => P0 * [1 - p^2 / 10,000] = 1 => P0 * [(10,000 - p^2) / 10,000] = 1 => P0 = 10,000 / (10,000 - p^2). The required value is 10,000 / (10,000 - p^2). Hence, the correct option is (c).

146. A person gave 40% of his money to his wife, 20% of the remainder to each of his three sons, and half of the rest was spent on other items. If he is left with Rs 12,000, find the amount of money he originally had.
(a) Rs 100,000 (b) Rs 80,000 (c) Rs 120,000 (d) Rs 90,000
Answer: (a) Rs 100,000
Solution: After giving wife 40%, remaining is 60%. Three sons receive 20% each of this remainder, totaling 60% of remainder. So sons take 0.60 * 60% = 36% of total. Balance after sons = 60% - 36% = 24%. Half of this balance is spent, leaving half: 0.50 * 24% = 12% of total. 12% = 12,000 => Total = Rs 100,000. The required value is Rs 100,000. Hence, the correct option is (a).

150. A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 15%. Find the percentage increase in his savings.
(a) 30% (b) 35% (c) 25% (d) 40%
Answer: (b) 35%
Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 75, savings = 25. New income = 120. New expenditure = 75 * 1.15 = 86.25. New savings = 120 - 86.25 = 33.75. Increase in savings = 33.75 - 25 = 8.75. Percentage increase in savings = (8.75 / 25) * 100% = 35%. The required value is 35%. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
