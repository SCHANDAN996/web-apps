126. A number is increased by 20% and then the result is decreased by 20%. If the final number is 96, what was the original number?
(a) 90 (b) 100 (c) 104 (d) 96
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is thinking the two changes cancel out and the answer is 96 itself. Let the original number be N. Then N × 1.20 × 0.80 = 0.96N = 96, giving N = 100, so the answer is 100.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

127. The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that the expenditure remains the same?
(a) 25% (b) 15% (c) 20% (d) 22.5%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 25%, assuming the reduction equals the rise. Required reduction = [r/(100 + r)] × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = 20%. Check: price 100 → 125, consumption 100 → 80 keeps expenditure at 10000, so the answer is 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

128. In an election between two candidates, the winner got 62% of the votes and won by 7200 votes. How many votes did the loser get?
(a) 30000 (b) 18600 (c) 11400 (d) 22800
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 38% of 7200 directly, which gives 2736 — meaningless. Margin = 62% − 38% = 24% of total = 7200, so total votes = 30000. Loser's votes = 38% of 30000 = 11400, so the answer is 11400.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

129. A shopkeeper marks an article 40% above cost price and gives a discount of 25%. What is his profit percentage?
(a) 5% (b) 15% (c) 10% (d) 12%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is subtracting directly: 40 − 25 = 15%. Let CP = 100, MP = 140, SP = 140 × 0.75 = 105. Profit = 5 on 100, so the answer is 5%.

130. The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year, decreases by 10% in the second year, and increases by 10% in the third year. If the population at the end of the third year is 10890, what was it at the start?
(a) 10000 (b) 11000 (c) 10500 (d) 10890
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the changes cancel and answering 10890. Net multiplier = 1.1 × 0.9 × 1.1 = 1.089. Original = 10890/1.089 = 10000, so the answer is 10000.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CHSL pattern)

131. If A's salary is 25% more than B's, then by what percentage is B's salary less than A's?
(a) 22.5% (b) 25% (c) 20% (d) 33.33%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering 25%, forgetting the base changes. Let B = 100, then A = 125. B is less than A by 25 on a base of 125, i.e. 25/125 × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

132. A student scored 32% marks and failed by 40 marks. Another student scored 42% marks and got 30 marks more than the passing marks. What are the maximum marks?
(a) 700 (b) 600 (c) 800 (d) 500
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding 40 + 30 = 70 and treating it as 10% of 1000 without checking. Difference in scores = 42% − 32% = 10% of maximum = 40 + 30 = 70 marks. Maximum = 70 × 10 = 700, so the answer is 700.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

133. Fresh fruit contains 68% water and dry fruit contains 20% water. How much dry fruit can be obtained from 100 kg of fresh fruit?
(a) 45 kg (b) 32 kg (c) 50 kg (d) 40 kg
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 20% of 100 = 20 kg or 68 − 20 = 48 kg. Fruit pulp stays constant: 32% of 100 = 32 kg. In dry fruit, pulp is 80%, so dry fruit = 32/0.8 = 40 kg, so the answer is 40 kg.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS PO pattern)

134. The price of an article is reduced by 20% and its sale increases by 30%. What is the net effect on the revenue?
(a) 6% increase (b) 10% increase (c) 4% decrease (d) 4% increase
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 30 − 20 = 10% increase. Net change = −20 + 30 + (−20 × 30)/100 = 10 − 6 = 4% increase, so the answer is 4% increase.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

135. In a factory, 60% of the workers are men. If 20% of the men and 25% of the women are skilled, what percentage of all workers are unskilled?
(a) 78% (b) 22% (c) 75% (d) 25%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is averaging 20% and 25% to get 22.5% skilled. Skilled men = 12% of total, skilled women = 25% of 40% = 10% of total; total skilled = 22%. Unskilled = 100 − 22 = 78%, so the answer is 78%.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

136. A number is first increased by 10% and then reduced by 10%. The net change in the number is:
(a) No change (b) 1% increase (c) 2% decrease (d) 1% decrease
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "no change", assuming the operations cancel. Net change = 10 − 10 − (10 × 10)/100 = −1%, i.e. a 1% decrease, so the answer is 1% decrease.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

137. The value of a machine depreciates at 10% per annum. If its present value is ₹81000, what was its value 2 years ago?
(a) ₹90000 (b) ₹100000 (c) ₹98000 (d) ₹105000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding 20% to 81000 to get 97200. Value 2 years ago = 81000/(0.9 × 0.9) = 81000/0.81 = 100000, so the answer is ₹100000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

138. In an examination, 35% of students failed in Hindi, 45% failed in English and 20% failed in both. What percentage of students passed in both subjects?
(a) 60% (b) 40% (c) 45% (d) 55%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 100 − (35 + 45) = 20%, ignoring the overlap. Failed in at least one = 35 + 45 − 20 = 60%. Passed in both = 100 − 60 = 40%, so the answer is 40%.
Source: PYQ-style (RRB NTPC pattern)

139. A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%. What is the percentage increase in his savings?
(a) 50% (b) 40% (c) 30% (d) 25%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 20 − 10 = 10%. Let income = 100, expenditure = 75, savings = 25. New income = 120, new expenditure = 82.5, new savings = 37.5. Increase = 12.5/25 × 100 = 50%, so the answer is 50%.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS PO pattern)

140. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and the denominator is decreased by 20%, the fraction becomes 3/2. What is the original fraction?
(a) 1 (b) 5/4 (c) 4/5 (d) 3/4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating 3/2 itself as the original fraction. (1.2N)/(0.8D) = 3/2, so N/D = (3/2) × (0.8/1.2) = (3/2) × (2/3) = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

141. Two numbers are respectively 20% and 50% more than a third number. What percentage is the first number of the second?
(a) 70% (b) 80% (c) 75% (d) 85%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 50 − 20 = 30% or 100 − 30 = 70%. Let the third number = 100; first = 120, second = 150. Required = 120/150 × 100 = 80%, so the answer is 80%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

142. A reduction of 20% in the price of rice enables a buyer to purchase 5 kg more rice for ₹400. What is the reduced price per kg of rice?
(a) ₹20 (b) ₹16 (c) ₹25 (d) ₹18
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is reporting the original price ₹20 instead of the reduced price. Let original price be p; then 400/(0.8p) − 400/p = 5 gives 100/p = 5, so p = 20. Reduced price = 0.8 × 20 = 16, so the answer is ₹16.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CHSL pattern)

143. In a mixture of 60 litres, milk and water are in the ratio 2 : 1. What quantity of water must be added so that milk becomes 50% of the new mixture?
(a) 12 litres (b) 20 litres (c) 10 litres (d) 15 litres
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding water without tracking the total. Milk = 40 litres and stays constant. For milk to be 50% of the mixture, total must be 80 litres, so water added = 80 − 60 = 20 litres, so the answer is 20 litres.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS Clerk pattern)

144. A candidate who gets 30% of the marks fails by 50 marks, while another candidate who gets 45% marks gets 25 marks more than necessary for passing. The passing percentage is:
(a) 38% (b) 35% (c) 33% (d) 40%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is averaging 30% and 45% to get 37.5%. Difference of 15% of maximum = 50 + 25 = 75 marks, so maximum = 500. Passing marks = 150 + 50 = 200, i.e. 200/500 × 100 = 40%, so the answer is 40%.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

145. The salary of a person was reduced by 10%. By what percentage should his reduced salary be raised so as to bring it at par with his original salary?
(a) 10% (b) 12% (c) 9.09% (d) 11.11%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 10%, the same as the reduction. Required increase = [10/(100 − 10)] × 100 = 10/90 × 100 = 11.11%, so the answer is 11.11%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

146. In a village, 30% of the population is literate. If the number of illiterate people is 5600, and 60% of the literates are male, how many literate females are there in the village?
(a) 1440 (b) 2400 (c) 960 (d) 800
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is taking 40% of 5600 = 2240, using the illiterate base. Illiterate = 70% = 5600, so total = 8000 and literates = 2400. Literate females = 40% of 2400 = 960, so the answer is 960.
Source: PYQ-style (RRB NTPC pattern)

147. An article is sold at a profit of 20%. If both the cost price and selling price were ₹100 less, the profit would be 25%. What is the cost price?
(a) ₹400 (b) ₹700 (c) ₹600 (d) ₹500
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is applying 25% to the original cost price instead of the reduced one. Let CP = x, SP = 1.2x. Then 1.2x − 100 = 1.25(x − 100), so 0.05x = 25 and x = 500. Verify: new CP 400, new SP 500, profit 25%, so the answer is ₹500.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS PO pattern)

148. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 3 : 2. If 20% of the boys and 30% of the girls are scholarship holders, what percentage of students are not scholarship holders?
(a) 76% (b) 24% (c) 50% (d) 72%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is averaging 20% and 30% to get 25% holders. Take 300 boys and 200 girls: holders = 60 + 60 = 120 out of 500 = 24%. Non-holders = 76%, so the answer is 76%.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CHSL pattern)

149. A trader sells two articles at ₹960 each. On one he gains 20% and on the other he loses 20%. What is his overall profit or loss percentage?
(a) 4% profit (b) No profit, no loss (c) 4% loss (d) 2% loss
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is "no profit, no loss", since the gain and loss percentages are equal. CP of first = 960/1.2 = 800, CP of second = 960/0.8 = 1200; total CP = 2000, total SP = 1920. Loss = 80/2000 × 100 = 4%, so the answer is 4% loss.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

150. The length of a rectangle is increased by 25% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. What is the percentage change in its area?
(a) 5% increase (b) 5% decrease (c) No change (d) 2.5% increase
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 25 − 20 = 5% increase. Net change = 25 − 20 + (25 × −20)/100 = 5 − 5 = 0%. New area = 1.25 × 0.8 = 1.00 times the original, so the answer is No change.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)
