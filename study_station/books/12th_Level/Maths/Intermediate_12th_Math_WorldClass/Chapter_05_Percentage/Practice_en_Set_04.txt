76. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The net change in the number is:
(a) No change (b) 4% increase (c) 4% decrease (d) 2% decrease
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is "no change", but the 20% decrease applies to the increased value, not the original. Net change = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%, so the answer is 4% decrease.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

77. If the price of sugar rises by 25%, by what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that expenditure remains the same?
(a) 20% (b) 30% (c) 15% (d) 25%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students pick 25%, assuming the reduction equals the rise, but the base changes. Required reduction = 25/(100+25) × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

78. In an election between two candidates, the winner got 62% of the votes and won by 7200 votes. The total number of votes polled was:
(a) 36000 (b) 24000 (c) 30000 (d) 28000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is dividing 7200 by 62, but 7200 is the margin, not the winner's votes. Loser got 38%, so margin = 62 − 38 = 24% of total, and total = 7200 × 100/24 = 30000, so the answer is 30000.
Source: PYQ-style

79. The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the population at the end of the second year is 49500, the initial population was:
(a) 50000 (b) 49500 (c) 50500 (d) 49000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Choosing 49500 assumes the changes cancel, but the decrease acts on the increased population. Net multiplier = 1.1 × 0.9 = 0.99, and initial = 49500/0.99 = 50000, so the answer is 50000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

80. A student scored 32% marks and failed by 40 marks. Another student scored 42% marks and got 30 marks more than the pass marks. The maximum marks are:
(a) 800 (b) 600 (c) 500 (d) 700
Answer: (d)
Solution: A common error is using 40 or 30 alone instead of their sum. The gap of 10% equals 40 + 30 = 70 marks, so maximum = 70 × 100/10 = 700, so the answer is 700.
Source: PYQ-style

81. If A's salary is 25% more than B's salary, then B's salary is what percent less than A's?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 22.5% (d) 15%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 25%, but the base for "less" is A's salary, not B's. Let B = 100, then A = 125, and B is less by 25/125 × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

82. A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%. His savings increase by:
(a) 30% (b) 40% (c) 25% (d) 50%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Students often subtract 10 from 20 to get 10%, ignoring that savings are only 25% of income. Let income = 100, expenditure = 75, savings = 25; new income = 120, new expenditure = 82.5, new savings = 37.5, and increase = 12.5/25 × 100 = 50%, so the answer is 50%.
Source: PYQ-style

83. The value of a machine depreciates at 10% per annum. If its present value is ₹81000, its value 2 years ago was:
(a) ₹95000 (b) ₹90000 (c) ₹100000 (d) ₹98000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding 20% to 81000 gives 97200, which is wrong because depreciation compounds. Value 2 years ago = 81000/(0.9 × 0.9) = 81000/0.81 = 100000, so the answer is ₹100000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

84. In an examination, 35% of students failed in Hindi, 45% failed in English and 20% failed in both. The percentage of students who passed in both subjects is:
(a) 60% (b) 35% (c) 40% (d) 45%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding 35 and 45 to get 80% failed ignores the overlap of 20%. Failed in at least one = 35 + 45 − 20 = 60%, so passed in both = 100 − 60 = 40%, so the answer is 40%.
Source: PYQ-style

85. If 60% of a number is 120 more than 20% of the same number, the number is:
(a) 360 (b) 240 (c) 400 (d) 300
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dividing 120 by 60, but 120 is the difference between two percentages. Difference = 60% − 20% = 40% of the number = 120, so number = 120 × 100/40 = 300, so the answer is 300.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

86. A reduction of 20% in the price of rice enables a buyer to purchase 5 kg more for ₹400. The reduced price per kg of rice is:
(a) ₹20 (b) ₹16 (c) ₹25 (d) ₹18
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many compute the original price (₹20) and stop, but the question asks the reduced price. The 20% saving on ₹400 is ₹80, which buys 5 kg extra, so reduced price = 80/5 = ₹16 per kg, so the answer is ₹16.
Source: PYQ-style

87. Two numbers are respectively 20% and 50% more than a third number. The first number is what percent of the second?
(a) 80% (b) 70% (c) 75% (d) 85%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Taking 20/50 = 40% is wrong because both numbers are measured against the third. Let third = 100; first = 120, second = 150, and required = 120/150 × 100 = 80%, so the answer is 80%.

88. The length of a rectangle is increased by 25% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. The percentage change in its area is:
(a) 5% increase (b) No change (c) 5% decrease (d) 2.5% increase
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is 5% increase (25 − 20), but the changes multiply. Net change = 25 − 20 − (25×20)/100 = 0%, so the area is unchanged, so the answer is No change.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

89. A number is first increased by 10% and then the result is increased by 20%. The overall percentage increase is:
(a) 30% (b) 32% (c) 28% (d) 35%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Simply adding gives 30%, which ignores compounding. Net increase = 10 + 20 + (10×20)/100 = 32%, so the answer is 32%.

90. In a factory, 40% of the workers are women. If 60% of the men and 80% of the women are skilled, what percentage of all workers are skilled?
(a) 64% (b) 70% (c) 68% (d) 72%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Averaging 60 and 80 to get 70% ignores the different group sizes. Men = 60% of workers; skilled men = 36%, skilled women = 32%, and total skilled = 36 + 32 = 68%, so the answer is 68%.

91. The price of an article was increased by 30% and then decreased by 20%. If the final price is ₹1040, the original price was:
(a) ₹960 (b) ₹1100 (c) ₹1000 (d) ₹1050
Answer: (c)
Solution: Assuming a net 10% rise and computing 1040/1.1 ≈ 945 is wrong because the net change is not 10%. Net multiplier = 1.3 × 0.8 = 1.04, and original = 1040/1.04 = ₹1000, so the answer is ₹1000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

92. If 15% of x is equal to 25% of y, then x : y is:
(a) 4 : 5 (b) 3 : 5 (c) 5 : 4 (d) 5 : 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: Reversing the ratio to 3 : 5 is the common trap. 15x = 25y gives x/y = 25/15 = 5/3, so the answer is 5 : 3.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

93. A candidate who scored 30% marks failed by 15 marks, while another who scored 45% marks passed with 30 marks above the pass mark. The pass percentage is:
(a) 35% (b) 33% (c) 40% (d) 32%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Averaging 30 and 45 to get 37.5% is the trap, since the pass mark lies between the two scores but is not their average. The 15% gap equals 15 + 30 = 45 marks, so maximum = 300; pass marks = 30% of 300 + 15 = 105, and 105/300 × 100 = 35%, so the answer is 35%.
Source: PYQ-style

94. The salary of a person was reduced by 10%. By what percent must his reduced salary be raised to restore the original salary?
(a) 11⅑% (b) 9⅑% (c) 10% (d) 11%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Answering 10% is the trap, since the base is now the reduced salary. Required rise = 10/(100 − 10) × 100 = 100/9 = 11⅑%, so the answer is 11⅑%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

95. In a school, 20% of the students are below 10 years of age. The number of students above 10 years is 480. The total number of students in the school is:
(a) 600 (b) 580 (c) 640 (d) 560
Answer: (a)
Solution: Computing 480 as 20% of the total is the trap; 480 represents the 80% above 10 years. Total = 480 × 100/80 = 600, so the answer is 600.

96. Fresh fruit contains 68% water and dry fruit contains 20% water. How much dry fruit can be obtained from 100 kg of fresh fruit?
(a) 38 kg (b) 32 kg (c) 45 kg (d) 40 kg
Answer: (d)
Solution: Taking 32% of 100 kg as the answer confuses pulp with water. Pulp in fresh fruit = 32 kg, which is 80% of dry fruit, so dry fruit = 32/0.8 = 40 kg, so the answer is 40 kg.

98. The price of petrol increased by 20% and then decreased by 10%. If the original price was ₹80 per litre, the final price is:
(a) ₹86 (b) ₹86.40 (c) ₹84 (d) ₹88
Answer: (b)
Solution: Assuming a net 10% rise gives ₹88, but the decrease applies to the increased price. After increase: 80 × 1.2 = 96; after decrease: 96 × 0.9 = ₹86.40, so the answer is ₹86.40.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

99. In an examination, 65% of the total students passed. If the number of students who failed was 420, the total number of students was:
(a) 1100 (b) 1000 (c) 1300 (d) 1200
Answer: (d)
Solution: Computing 420 as 65% of the total is the trap; 420 is the failed 35%. Total = 420 × 100/35 = 1200, so the answer is 1200.

100. A number is divided into two parts such that 80% of the first part is 60% of the second part. The ratio of the first part to the second part is:
(a) 4 : 3 (b) 3 : 4 (c) 2 : 3 (d) 3 : 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Reversing to 4 : 3 is the common error. 0.8 × first = 0.6 × second gives first/second = 0.6/0.8 = 3/4, so the answer is 3 : 4.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)
