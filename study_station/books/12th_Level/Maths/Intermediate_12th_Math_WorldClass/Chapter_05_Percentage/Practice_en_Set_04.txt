Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The population of a town increases by 5% annually. If its present population is 80,000, what will be its population after 2 years?
(a) 88,200 (b) 88,000 (c) 86,400 (d) 89,100
Answer: (a) 88,200
Solution: Population after 2 years = 80,000 * (1 + 5/100)^2 = 80,000 * (21/20)^2 = 80,000 * (441 / 400) = 200 * 441 = 88,200. The required value is 88,200. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

77. The population of a city was 160,000 two years ago. If it increased by 10% in the first year and decreased by 10% in the second year, what is its present population?
(a) 160,000 (b) 158,400 (c) 156,800 (d) 162,000
Answer: (b) 158,400
Solution: Net multiplier = (1 + 0.10) * (1 - 0.10) = 1.10 * 0.90 = 0.99. Present population = 160,000 * 0.99 = 158,400. The required value is 158,400. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

78. The value of a machine depreciates at the rate of 10% per annum. If its present value is Rs 72,900, what was its value 3 years ago?
(a) Rs 90,000 (b) Rs 95,000 (c) Rs 100,000 (d) Rs 110,000
Answer: (c) Rs 100,000
Solution: Let value 3 years ago be V. Present value = V * (1 - 10/100)^3 = V * (9/10)^3 = V * (729 / 1000). Since V * 729 / 1000 = 72,900, V = Rs 100,000. The required value is Rs 100,000. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

79. In an election between two candidates, one got 55% of the total valid votes, and 20% of the votes were declared invalid. If the total number of votes polled was 7,500, find the number of valid votes that the other candidate got.
(a) 3,300 (b) 3,000 (c) 2,850 (d) 2,700
Answer: (d) 2,700
Solution: Total valid votes = 7,500 * (1 - 0.20) = 6,000. The winning candidate got 55%, so the losing candidate got 45% of valid votes. Votes for losing candidate = 6,000 * 0.45 = 2,700. The required value is 2,700. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

80. In an election between two candidates, 10% of voters did not cast their votes and 10% of the votes cast were declared invalid. A candidate got 54% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 1,620 votes. Find the total number of voters on the electoral roll.
(a) 25,000 (b) 20,000 (c) 30,000 (d) 22,500
Answer: (a) 25,000
Solution: Let total enrolled voters be x. Votes cast = 0.90x. Valid votes = 0.90x * 0.90 = 0.81x. Winner got 54% and loser got 46% of valid votes. Winning margin = 54% - 46% = 8% of valid votes. Thus, 0.08 * 0.81x = 1,620 => 0.0648x = 1,620 => x = 1,620 / 0.0648 = 25,000. The required value is 25,000. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

81. In an election, 8% of the voters did not cast their votes. There were only two candidates. The winner obtained 48% of the total enrolled votes and defeated his contestant by 1,100 votes. Find the total number of voters in the election.
(a) 25,000 (b) 27,500 (c) 30,000 (d) 28,000
Answer: (b) 27,500
Solution: Let total enrolled voters be 100%. Votes polled = 100% - 8% = 92%. Winner got 48% of total enrolled votes. Loser got 92% - 48% = 44% of total enrolled votes. Difference between winner and loser = 48% - 44% = 4% of total enrolled votes. Given 4% = 1,100 => Total voters = 1,100 / 0.04 = 27,500. The required value is 27,500. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

82. The population of a city increases at the rate of 4% per annum. There is an additional annual increase of 1% due to immigration. What is the net percentage increase in population after 2 years?
(a) 10% (b) 10.50% (c) 10.25% (d) 11.25%
Answer: (c) 10.25%
Solution: Net annual rate of increase = 4% + 1% = 5%. Over 2 years, net percentage increase = 5 + 5 + (5 * 5 / 100) = 10.25%. The required value is 10.25%. Hence, the correct option is (c).

83. The population of a village was 9,600. In a year, with an increase in male population by 8% and female population by 5%, the population became 10,272. What was the number of males in the village before the increase?
(a) 3,200 (b) 5,800 (c) 6,000 (d) 6,400
Answer: (d) 6,400
Solution: Total increase = 10,272 - 9,600 = 672. If all 9,600 increased by 5%, increase would be 9,600 * 0.05 = 480. Excess increase = 672 - 480 = 192. This excess is due to the additional 3% (8% - 5%) increase in males. Thus, 3% of Males = 192 => Males = 192 / 0.03 = 6,400. The required value is 6,400. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

84. In an election between two contestants, 20% of the voters did not cast their votes and 120 votes were declared invalid. The winner secured 41% of the total voters on the list and defeated his rival by 200 votes. Find the total number of voters on the list.
(a) 4,000 (b) 3,600 (c) 4,500 (d) 5,000
Answer: (a) 4,000
Solution: Let total voters be 100x. Votes polled = 80x. Valid votes = 80x - 120. Winner got 41x. Loser got (80x - 120) - 41x = 39x - 120. Margin: 41x - (39x - 120) = 200 => 2x + 120 = 200 => 2x = 80 => x = 40. Total voters = 100x = 4,000. The required value is 4,000. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

85. The value of a property depreciates every year by 10% of its value at the beginning of that year. If the value of the property at the end of 3 years is Rs 218,700, find its original price.
(a) Rs 270,000 (b) Rs 300,000 (c) Rs 280,000 (d) Rs 320,000
Answer: (b) Rs 300,000
Solution: Value after 3 years = Original * (0.90)^3 = Original * 0.729. Original value = 218,700 / 0.729 = Rs 300,000. The required value is Rs 300,000. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

86. In an election between two candidates, 75% of the voters cast their votes, out of which 2% of the votes were declared invalid. A candidate got 9,261 votes which was 75% of the total valid votes. Find the total number of voters enrolled in that election.
(a) 16,400 (b) 17,200 (c) 16,800 (d) 15,600
Answer: (c) 16,800
Solution: Let total enrolled voters be x. Valid votes = x * 0.75 * 0.98. Winner got 75% of valid votes: x * 0.75 * 0.98 * 0.75 = 9,261 => x * (3/4) * (49/50) * (3/4) = 9,261 => x * (441 / 800) = 9,261 => x = (9,261 * 800) / 441 = 21 * 800 = 16,800. The required value is 16,800. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

89. The present population of a village is 5,500. If the number of males increases by 11% and the number of females increases by 20%, the population becomes 6,330. Find the initial population of females in the village.
(a) 3,000 (b) 2,500 (c) 2,800 (d) 2,200
Answer: (b) 2,500
Solution: Total increase = 6,330 - 5,500 = 830. If all 5,500 increased by 11%, the increase would be 5,500 * 0.11 = 605. Excess increase = 830 - 605 = 225. This excess represents (20% - 11%) = 9% of initial females. Initial females = 225 / 0.09 = 2,500. The required value is 2,500. Hence, the correct option is (b).

90. A company purchased a motor car for Rs 500,000. Its value depreciates at 20% for the first year, 15% for the second year, and 10% for the third year. What is its value at the end of 3 years?
(a) Rs 310,000 (b) Rs 300,000 (c) Rs 306,000 (d) Rs 315,000
Answer: (c) Rs 306,000
Solution: Final value = 500,000 * (1 - 0.20) * (1 - 0.15) * (1 - 0.10) = 500,000 * 0.80 * 0.85 * 0.90 = 400,000 * 0.765 = Rs 306,000. The required value is Rs 306,000. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

91. In a three-candidate election, the winning candidate received 45% of the total votes, the runner-up got 35%, and the third candidate received 9,600 votes (which was all the remaining votes). Find the winning margin of the winner over the runner-up.
(a) 5,200 (b) 4,500 (c) 6,000 (d) 4,800
Answer: (d) 4,800
Solution: Percentage for third candidate = 100% - (45% + 35%) = 20%. Given 20% = 9,600 votes. Winning margin over runner-up = 45% - 35% = 10%. Since 20% = 9,600, 10% = 9,600 / 2 = 4,800 votes. The required value is 4,800. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

92. Two candidates contested an election. The winning candidate secured 58% of the valid votes and won by 4,320 votes. If 10% of the total votes were invalid, find the total number of votes polled.
(a) 30,000 (b) 28,000 (c) 32,000 (d) 27,000
Answer: (a) 30,000
Solution: Margin in terms of valid votes = 58% - 42% = 16% of valid votes. 16% of valid votes = 4,320 => Valid votes = 4,320 / 0.16 = 27,000. Since 10% votes were invalid, valid votes = 90% of total polled votes. Total polled votes = 27,000 / 0.90 = 30,000. The required value is 30,000. Hence, the correct option is (a).

95. In an election between two candidates, 15% of voters did not cast their vote. Of the votes cast, 10% were found invalid. The successful candidate got 55% of the valid votes and won by a margin of 6,120 votes. Find the total number of voters enrolled.
(a) 75,000 (b) 85,000 (c) 90,000 (d) 80,000
Answer: (d) 80,000
Solution: Let enrolled voters be N. Polled votes = 0.85N. Valid votes = 0.85N * 0.90 = 0.765N. Winner's margin = 55% - 45% = 10% of valid votes. Margin = 0.10 * 0.765N = 0.0765N = 6,120. N = 6,120 / 0.0765 = 80,000. The required value is 80,000. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

97. In an election, 10% of people on the voter list did not vote and 60 voters cast their ballot papers blank. There were only two candidates. The winner was supported by 47% of all voters in the list and he got 308 votes more than his rival. Find the number of voters on the list.
(a) 6,000 (b) 6,200 (c) 6,400 (d) 5,800
Answer: (b) 6,200
Solution: Let total voters be 100x. Total valid votes cast = 90x - 60. Winner got 47x. Loser got (90x - 60) - 47x = 43x - 60. Margin: 47x - (43x - 60) = 4x + 60 = 308 => 4x = 248 => x = 62. Total voters = 100x = 6,200. The required value is 6,200. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
