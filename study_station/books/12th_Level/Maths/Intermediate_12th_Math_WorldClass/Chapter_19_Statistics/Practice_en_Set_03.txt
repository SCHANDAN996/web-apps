51. The mean of 20 observations is 15. If each observation is increased by 5, the new mean is:
(a) 20 (b) 15 (c) 25 (d) 75
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is multiplying 15 by 5 to get 75, but adding a constant to every value shifts the mean by that constant, not multiplies it. New mean = 15 + 5 = 20, so the answer is 20.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics (Statistics)

52. The median of the data 12, 17, 8, 21, 14, 9, 25 is:
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 17 (d) 15
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is picking 17, the middle value of the unsorted list; data must first be arranged in order. Sorted: 8, 9, 12, 14, 17, 21, 25; with 7 values the 4th term is the median, so the answer is 14.

53. If the mode of the data 5, 7, 9, 7, 11, 7, 9, 5, 7 is x, then the value of x is:
(a) 7 (b) 9 (c) 5 (d) 11
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is choosing 9 because it appears large, but mode depends only on frequency. Here 7 occurs 4 times, more than any other value, so the answer is 7.

54. The mean of first 10 natural numbers is:
(a) 5.5 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 10
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 5 by taking the middle of 1 and 10 carelessly. Sum = 10×11/2 = 55, mean = 55/10 = 5.5, so the answer is 5.5.

55. For a moderately skewed distribution, if mean = 24 and mode = 20, then the median is:
(a) 22 (b) 21.33 (c) 22.67 (d) 23
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is taking the simple average of mean and mode, which gives 22, but the empirical relation is Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean. So 20 = 3M − 48, giving M = 68/3 ≈ 22.67, so the answer is 22.67.

56. The mean of 5 numbers is 18. If one number is excluded, the mean becomes 16. The excluded number is:
(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 26 (d) 28
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is subtracting the means, 18 − 16 = 2, which ignores the change in count. Total of 5 numbers = 90; total of 4 numbers = 64; excluded number = 90 − 64 = 26, so the answer is 26.

57. The range of the data 25, 18, 42, 33, 10, 27 is:
(a) 42 (b) 30 (c) 32 (d) 28
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is picking 42, the maximum value, instead of the difference. Range = maximum − minimum = 42 − 10 = 32, so the answer is 32.

58. The mean of x, x+2, x+4, x+6, x+8 is 11. The value of x is:
(a) 7 (b) 6 (c) 5 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the middle term x+4 equals x, but the mean of these equally spaced terms is the middle term x+4. So x + 4 = 11, giving x = 7, so the answer is 7.

59. If the mean of a frequency distribution is 8.1 and Σfᵢxᵢ = 162, then Σfᵢ is:
(a) 20 (b) 18 (c) 22 (d) 25
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is dividing 162 by 8 instead of 8.1, giving about 20.25 and confusion. Mean = Σfᵢxᵢ / Σfᵢ, so Σfᵢ = 162/8.1 = 20, so the answer is 20.

60. The median of first 15 odd natural numbers is:
(a) 13 (b) 14 (c) 17 (d) 15

Solution: The trap is answering 14 by halving 28, but the median is the 8th odd number. The nth odd number is 2n − 1, so the 8th is 15, so the answer is 15.

61. In a class of 40 students, the average weight is 50 kg. If the teacher's weight is included, the average rises by 1 kg. The teacher's weight is:
(a) 90 kg (b) 51 kg (c) 91 kg (d) 81 kg
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding just 1 kg to 50, but the teacher must raise every one of the 41 members by 1 kg. Teacher's weight = 50 + 41×1 = 91 kg, so the answer is 91 kg.

(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 3 and 5 both

63. The mean of 100 observations was calculated as 40. Later it was found that one observation 53 was misread as 35. The correct mean is:
(a) 41.8 (b) 40.82 (c) 39.82 (d) 40.18
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding the full 18 to the mean instead of spreading it over 100 observations. Correction = (53 − 35)/100 = 0.18, so correct mean = 40.18, so the answer is 40.18.

64. The class mark of the class interval 20–30 is:
(a) 20 (b) 50 (c) 30 (d) 25

66. The average of 11 results is 50. The average of the first 6 results is 49 and that of the last 6 is 52. The 6th result is:
(a) 50 (b) 52 (c) 46 (d) 56
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is averaging 49 and 52 to get 50.5, but the 6th result is counted in both groups. 6th result = (6×49 + 6×52) − 11×50 = 606 − 550 = 56, so the answer is 56.

68. The variance of the data 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 is:
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is reporting the standard deviation-like value 4 or the mean deviation; variance uses squared deviations. Mean = 6; squared deviations 16, 4, 0, 4, 16 sum to 40; variance = 40/5 = 8, so the answer is 8.

69. If the standard deviation of a data set is 5, then its variance is:
(a) 25 (b) 10 (c) 5 (d) √5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is doubling 5 to get 10, but variance is the square of standard deviation. Variance = 5² = 25, so the answer is 25.

70. The coefficient of variation of a data set with mean 50 and standard deviation 10 is:
(a) 5% (b) 10% (c) 20% (d) 25%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 50/10 = 5%, which inverts the formula. Coefficient of variation = (SD/mean)×100 = (10/50)×100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.

Solution: The trap is subtracting from 100 to get 55, but cumulative frequency grows by adding the next class frequency. 45 + 15 = 60, so the answer is 60.

73. If each observation of a data set is multiplied by 3, the standard deviation of the original data being 4, the new standard deviation is:
(a) 4 (b) 7 (c) 16 (d) 12

Solution: The trap is squaring to get 16, which applies to variance, not standard deviation. Multiplying data by 3 multiplies the SD by 3: 4×3 = 12, so the answer is 12.

74. The average age of 30 students is 12 years. If 5 new students with average age 10 years join, the new average age is:
(a) 11.5 years (b) 11.71 years (c) 11 years (d) 12 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is averaging 12 and 10 to get 11, ignoring the different group sizes. Total age = 30×12 + 5×10 = 410; new average = 410/35 ≈ 11.71 years, so the answer is 11.71 years.

75. For a distribution, the sum of deviations of all observations taken from the mean is:
(a) Always positive (b) Zero (c) Always negative (d) Equal to the mean
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is thinking deviations sum to the mean or stay positive, but positive and negative deviations from the mean exactly cancel out. By definition Σ(xᵢ − mean) = 0, so the answer is zero.
