Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Two vessels A and B contain mixtures of milk and water in the ratio 7 : 5 and 17 : 7 respectively. In what ratio should the contents of these two vessels be mixed so as to result in a mixture containing milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3?
(a) 2 : 1 (b) 1 : 2 (c) 3 : 2 (d) 2 : 3
Answer: (a) 2 : 1
Solution: Milk concentration in vessel A is 7/12 = 14/24. Milk concentration in vessel B is 17/24. The target milk concentration is 5/8 = 15/24. Applying the rule of alligation: Ratio of A to B = (17/24 - 15/24) : (15/24 - 14/24) = 2/24 : 1/24 = 2 : 1. The required value is 2 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

77. A container contains 80 litres of milk. From this container, 8 litres of milk is taken out and replaced by water. This process was repeated further two times. How much milk is now contained by the container?
(a) 56.48 litres (b) 58.32 litres (c) 60.24 litres (d) 54.72 litres
Answer: (b) 58.32 litres
Solution: The fraction of liquid replaced in each cycle is 8/80 = 1/10. The process is carried out a total of 3 times. Remaining quantity of pure milk = 80 * (1 - 1/10)^3 = 80 * (9/10)^3 = 80 * (729 / 1000) = 58.32 litres. The required value is 58.32 litres. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

78. Three glasses of equal volume contain mixtures of spirit and water in the ratio 2 : 3, 3 : 4, and 4 : 5 respectively. The contents of all three glasses are emptied into a large vessel. What is the ratio of spirit to water in the resultant mixture?
(a) 401 : 945 (b) 544 : 401 (c) 401 : 544 (d) 395 : 550
Answer: (c) 401 : 544
Solution: The sums of ratio terms are 5, 7, and 9. Taking LCM(5, 7, 9) = 315 as the equal volume of each glass: Glass 1 has spirit = 315 * 2/5 = 126, water = 189. Glass 2 has spirit = 315 * 3/7 = 135, water = 180. Glass 3 has spirit = 315 * 4/9 = 140, water = 175. Total spirit = 126 + 135 + 140 = 401; Total water = 189 + 180 + 175 = 544. Resultant ratio = 401 : 544. The required value is 401 : 544. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

79. In an alloy of 60 kg, the ratio of zinc to copper is 2 : 1. How much copper should be added to make the ratio 1 : 2?
(a) 40 kg (b) 80 kg (c) 50 kg (d) 60 kg
Answer: (d) 60 kg
Solution: Initial quantity of zinc = 60 * 2/3 = 40 kg, and copper = 20 kg. In the desired mixture, the zinc content remains 40 kg while the ratio of zinc to copper is 1 : 2. Required copper = 2 * 40 = 80 kg. Hence, copper to be added = 80 - 20 = 60 kg. The required value is 60 kg. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

80. A vessel is filled with liquid, 3 parts of which are water and 5 parts syrup. How much of the mixture must be drawn off and replaced with water so that the mixture may be half water and half syrup?
(a) 1/5 (b) 1/4 (c) 1/3 (d) 1/6
Answer: (a) 1/5
Solution: Initial syrup fraction = 5/8. Let fraction x of mixture be drawn off. The remaining syrup is (5/8)(1 - x). The desired syrup fraction is 1/2. Equating gives (5/8)(1 - x) = 1/2 => 5(1 - x) = 4 => 1 - x = 4/5 => x = 1/5. The required value is 1/5. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

81. Two alloys contain tin and iron in the ratio 1 : 2 and 2 : 3 respectively. If the two alloys are mixed in the ratio 3 : 4 by weight, find the ratio of tin to iron in the newly formed alloy.
(a) 14 : 25 (b) 13 : 22 (c) 12 : 23 (d) 11 : 20
Answer: (b) 13 : 22
Solution: Taking weights 3 kg and 4 kg: Alloy 1 gives Tin = 3 * 1/3 = 1 kg, Iron = 3 * 2/3 = 2 kg. Alloy 2 gives Tin = 4 * 2/5 = 1.6 kg, Iron = 4 * 3/5 = 2.4 kg. Combined Tin = 1 + 1.6 = 2.6 kg; Combined Iron = 2 + 2.4 = 4.4 kg. Ratio = 2.6 / 4.4 = 13 : 22. The required value is 13 : 22. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

82. A jar contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 4 : 1. When 10 litres of the mixture is taken out and 10 litres of liquid B is poured into the jar, the ratio becomes 2 : 3. What was the quantity of liquid A initially contained in the jar?
(a) 20 litres (b) 12 litres (c) 16 litres (d) 24 litres
Answer: (c) 16 litres
Solution: Let the initial quantities be 4x and x. In 10 litres drawn off, A = 8 litres and B = 2 litres. After adding 10 litres of B, the new amounts are (4x - 8) and (x - 2 + 10) = (x + 8). Given (4x - 8) / (x + 8) = 2/3 => 12x - 24 = 2x + 16 => 10x = 40 => x = 4. Initial quantity of A = 4 * 4 = 16 litres. The required value is 16 litres. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

83. The ratio of expenditure to savings of a family is 5 : 3. If the income increases by 12% and the expenditure increases by 15%, then by what percent do the savings increase?
(a) 8% (b) 6.5% (c) 7.5% (d) 7%
Answer: (d) 7%
Solution: Let Expenditure = 500, Savings = 300, and Income = 800. New Income = 800 * 1.12 = 896. New Expenditure = 500 * 1.15 = 575. New Savings = 896 - 575 = 321. Increase in savings = 321 - 300 = 21. Percentage increase = (21 / 300) * 100% = 7%. The required value is 7%. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

84. In an examination, the ratio of passes to failures was 4 : 1. If 30 fewer students had appeared and 20 fewer had passed, the ratio of passes to failures would have been 5 : 1. How many students appeared for the examination originally?
(a) 150 (b) 120 (c) 180 (d) 160
Answer: (a) 150
Solution: Let passes = 4x and failures = x, total appeared = 5x. Under the hypothetical condition: Total appeared = 5x - 30, passes = 4x - 20, failures = (5x - 30) - (4x - 20) = x - 10. Given ratio (4x - 20) / (x - 10) = 5/1 => 4x - 20 = 5x - 50 => x = 30. Total appeared originally = 5x = 5 * 30 = 150. The required value is 150. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

85. A, B, and C have incomes in the ratio 3 : 7 : 4 and their spendings are in the ratio 4 : 3 : 5. If A saves Rs 300 out of an income of Rs 2,400, find the savings of B.
(a) Rs 3,850 (b) Rs 4,025 (c) Rs 4,125 (d) Rs 3,975
Answer: (b) Rs 4,025
Solution: A's income = 2400. In ratio 3 : 7 : 4, one income unit = 2400 / 3 = 800. B's income = 7 * 800 = 5600. A's spending = 2400 - 300 = 2100. In spending ratio 4 : 3 : 5, 4 units = 2100 => 1 unit = 525. B's spending = 3 * 525 = 1575. B's savings = 5600 - 1575 = Rs 4,025. The required value is Rs 4,025. Hence, the correct option is (b).

86. Fresh fruit contains 68% water and dry fruit contains 20% water. How much dry fruit can be obtained from 100 kg of fresh fruit?
(a) 32 kg (b) 48 kg (c) 40 kg (d) 36 kg
Answer: (c) 40 kg
Solution: Pulp content remains invariant during evaporation. In 100 kg fresh fruit, pulp = 100 - 68 = 32% => 32 kg pulp. In dry fruit, pulp is 100 - 20 = 80%. Let dry fruit weight be W. Then 80% of W = 32 kg => W = 32 / 0.80 = 40 kg. The required value is 40 kg. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

87. A person distributes his pens among four friends A, B, C, D in the ratio 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/5 : 1/6. What is the minimum number of pens that the person should have?
(a) 60 (b) 65 (c) 54 (d) 57
Answer: (d) 57
Solution: Finding the LCM of denominators 3, 4, 5, 6 gives 60. Multiplying each term by 60: Ratio = 20 : 15 : 12 : 10. The minimum integral number of pens is the sum of these smallest coprime integer parts: 20 + 15 + 12 + 10 = 57 pens. The required value is 57. Hence, the correct option is (d).

89. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7. If 9 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 7 : 11. Find the difference between the two numbers.
(a) 14 (b) 12 (c) 10 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: Let the numbers be 5x and 7x. Setting up equation: (5x - 9) / (7x - 9) = 7 / 11 => 55x - 99 = 49x - 63 => 6x = 36 => x = 6. Difference between numbers = 7x - 5x = 2x = 2 * 6 = 12. The required value is 12. Hence, the correct option is (b).

90. A mixture contains wine and water in the ratio 3 : 2 and another mixture contains them in the ratio 4 : 5. How many litres of the latter must be mixed with 3 litres of the former so that the resulting mixture may contain equal quantities of wine and water?
(a) 4.5 litres (b) 5.2 litres (c) 5.4 litres (d) 6.0 litres
Answer: (c) 5.4 litres
Solution: Wine fraction in first mixture = 3/5. Wine fraction in second mixture = 4/9. Target wine fraction = 1/2. By alligation: (1/2 - 4/9) : (3/5 - 1/2) = (1/18) : (1/10) = 10 : 18 = 5 : 9. Thus, First Volume / Second Volume = 5 / 9 => 3 / V = 5 / 9 => V = 27 / 5 = 5.4 litres. The required value is 5.4 litres. Hence, the correct option is (c).

91. The ratio of total students in three classes A, B, and C is 2 : 3 : 5. If 20 students are admitted to each class, the ratio becomes 4 : 5 : 7. What was the total number of students in the three classes together initially?
(a) 120 (b) 80 (c) 140 (d) 100
Answer: (d) 100
Solution: The increase across all three terms is 4 - 2 = 5 - 3 = 7 - 5 = 2 ratio units. Since each class gains 20 students, 2 units = 20 students => 1 unit = 10 students. Initial total students = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 units = 10 * 10 = 100 students. The required value is 100. Hence, the correct option is (d).

92. A bucket contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 7 : 5. When 9 litres of mixture is drawn off and the bucket is filled with B, the ratio of A and B becomes 7 : 9. How many litres of liquid A was in the bucket initially?
(a) 21 litres (b) 25 litres (c) 18 litres (d) 24 litres
Answer: (a) 21 litres
Solution: After 9 litres of mixture is removed, the remaining ratio of A : B is 7 : 5. After adding 9 litres of pure B, the ratio is 7 : 9. Here A remains unchanged (7 units). The increase in B is 9 - 5 = 4 units = 9 litres => 1 unit = 9/4 litres. Mixture remaining before adding B = (7 + 5) * (9/4) = 27 litres. Total capacity = 27 + 9 = 36 litres. Initial liquid A = 36 * (7/12) = 21 litres. The required value is 21 litres. Hence, the correct option is (a).

93. If x varies inversely as (y^2 - 1) and x = 24 when y = 10, find x when y = 5.
(a) 96 (b) 99 (c) 102 (d) 88
Answer: (b) 99
Solution: Given relation: x * (y^2 - 1) = k. Substituting initial values: k = 24 * (100 - 1) = 24 * 99 = 2376. When y = 5: y^2 - 1 = 25 - 1 = 24. Thus x * 24 = 2376 => x = 2376 / 24 = 99. The required value is 99. Hence, the correct option is (b).

95. A bag contains 50p, 25p, and 10p coins in the ratio 5 : 9 : 4, amounting to Rs 206. Find the number of coins of each type respectively.
(a) 200, 320, 180 (b) 180, 360, 160 (c) 220, 340, 150 (d) 200, 360, 160
Answer: (d) 200, 360, 160
Solution: Value of one ratio set = (5 * 0.50) + (9 * 0.25) + (4 * 0.10) = 2.50 + 2.25 + 0.40 = Rs 5.15. Total number of sets = 206 / 5.15 = 40. Coins: 50p coins = 5 * 40 = 200; 25p coins = 9 * 40 = 360; 10p coins = 4 * 40 = 160. The required value is 200, 360, 160. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

96. In a mixture of 45 litres, the ratio of milk and water is 4 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?
(a) 15 litres (b) 12 litres (c) 18 litres (d) 10 litres
Answer: (a) 15 litres
Solution: Milk = 45 * 4/5 = 36 litres, and water = 9 litres. When water W is added, milk remains 36 litres. Setting up: 36 / (9 + W) = 3 / 2 => 72 = 27 + 3W => 3W = 45 => W = 15 litres. The required value is 15 litres. Hence, the correct option is (a).

97. If a/(b + c) = b/(c + a) = c/(a + b), then what is the value of each ratio if a + b + c != 0?
(a) 1 (b) 1/2 (c) 1/3 (d) 2
Answer: (b) 1/2
Solution: Applying the property of equal ratios (sum of antecedents divided by sum of consequents): Each ratio = (a + b + c) / [(b + c) + (c + a) + (a + b)] = (a + b + c) / [2(a + b + c)]. Since a + b + c != 0, this simplifies directly to 1/2. The required value is 1/2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

98. A gold bar broke into three parts whose weights are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. The value of gold is directly proportional to the square of its weight. If the breakage resulted in a loss of Rs 47,000, find the original value of the gold bar.
(a) Rs 75,000 (b) Rs 68,000 (c) Rs 72,000 (d) Rs 80,000
Answer: (c) Rs 72,000
Solution: Total original weight = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 units. Original value is proportional to 12^2 = 144. Broken pieces have total value proportional to 3^2 + 4^2 + 5^2 = 9 + 16 + 25 = 50. Loss in value = 144 - 50 = 94 units. Given 94 units = Rs 47,000 => 1 unit = Rs 500. Original value = 144 * 500 = Rs 72,000. The required value is Rs 72,000. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

99. What must be added to each term of the ratio 49 : 68 so that it becomes 3 : 4?
(a) 6 (b) 9 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (d) 8
Solution: Let the required number be x. Setting up proportion: (49 + x) / (68 + x) = 3 / 4 => 4(49 + x) = 3(68 + x) => 196 + 4x = 204 + 3x => x = 204 - 196 = 8. The required value is 8. Hence, the correct option is (d).

100. An employer reduces the number of his employees in the ratio 9 : 8 and increases their wages in the ratio 14 : 15. If the original total wage bill was Rs 18,900, find the new total wage bill.
(a) Rs 17,500 (b) Rs 18,000 (c) Rs 18,200 (d) Rs 17,800
Answer: (b) Rs 18,000
Solution: Total wage bill is proportional to (Number of employees * Wage per employee). Ratio of initial to new wage bill = (9 * 14) : (8 * 15) = 126 : 120 = 21 : 20. Given 21 units = Rs 18,900 => 1 unit = 900. New wage bill = 20 * 900 = Rs 18,000. The required value is Rs 18,000. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
