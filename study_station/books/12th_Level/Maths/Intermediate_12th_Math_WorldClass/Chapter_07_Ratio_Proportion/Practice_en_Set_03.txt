51. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 9 is added to each, the ratio becomes 12 : 19. Find the smaller number.
(a) 27 (b) 45 (c) 36 (d) 63
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let the numbers be 3x and 5x. Then (3x + 9)/(5x + 9) = 12/19, so 57x + 171 = 60x + 108, giving 3x = 63. The tempting error is reporting x = 21 as the answer, but the smaller number is 3x, so the answer is 63.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

52. If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, then A : B : C is:
(a) 2 : 4 : 5 (b) 6 : 9 : 15 (c) 8 : 12 : 20 (d) 8 : 12 : 15
Answer: (d)
Solution: Make B equal in both ratios: B = 3 and B = 4, LCM is 12. Multiply the first ratio by 4 and the second by 3: A : B = 8 : 12 and B : C = 12 : 15. The trap is writing 2 : 3 : 5 directly without equalising B, so the answer is 8 : 12 : 15.

53. The ratio of incomes of A and B is 5 : 4 and the ratio of their expenditures is 3 : 2. If each saves ₹1600, find the income of A.
(a) ₹5000 (b) ₹4000 (c) ₹2400 (d) ₹3200
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let incomes be 5x and 4x, expenditures 3y and 2y. Then 5x − 3y = 1600 and 4x − 2y = 1600. From the second, y = 2x − 800; substituting gives 5x − 6x + 2400 = 1600, so x = 800. The trap is assuming savings follow the income ratio, so the answer is ₹4000.

54. What number must be added to each term of the ratio 7 : 11 so that it becomes 4 : 5?
(a) 7 (b) 9 (c) 11 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let the number be x. Then (7 + x)/(11 + x) = 4/5, so 35 + 5x = 44 + 4x, giving x = 9. The trap is cross-multiplying incorrectly as 35 + 5x = 44 + 5x, which gives no solution, so the answer is 9.

Solution: Fourth proportional x satisfies 5 : 8 :: 15 : x, so 5x = 8 × 15 = 120, giving x = 24. The trap is computing the third proportional (8 × 8/5) instead of the fourth, so the answer is 24.

65. If (a + b) : (b + c) : (c + a) = 6 : 7 : 8 and a + b + c = 14, then c equals:
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 4 (d) 7
Answer: (a)
Solution: The three ratios sum to 21 parts = 2(a + b + c) = 28, so 1 part = 4/3. Then a + b = 6 × 4/3 = 8, giving c = 14 − 8 = 6. The trap is taking 1 part = 14/21, forgetting the sum counts each variable twice, so the answer is 6.

66. The ratio of the ages of a father and son is 7 : 2. After 10 years the ratio will be 9 : 4. The present age of the father is:
(a) 49 years (b) 42 years (c) 28 years (d) 35 years

Solution: Let the ages be 7x and 2x. Then (7x + 10)/(2x + 10) = 9/4, so 28x + 40 = 18x + 90, giving 10x = 50, x = 5. The trap is reporting x = 5 or the son's age 10, so the answer is 35 years.

74. A and B started a business investing in the ratio 4 : 5. After 6 months A withdrew half his capital. The ratio in which profit should be divided at the end of the year is:
(a) 4 : 5 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 3 : 5 (d) 5 : 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: A's effective capital-months = 4 × 6 + 2 × 6 = 36; B's = 5 × 12 = 60, giving 36 : 60 = 3 : 5. The trap is keeping the original ratio 4 : 5 and ignoring the withdrawal, so the answer is 3 : 5.

75. The ratio of copper to zinc in alloy P is 5 : 3 and in alloy Q is 3 : 2. Equal weights of P and Q are melted together. The ratio of copper to zinc in the new alloy is:
(a) 8 : 5 (b) 41 : 29 (c) 49 : 31 (d) 4 : 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Take 40 g of each alloy (LCM of 8 and 5). P gives 25 g copper and 15 g zinc; Q gives 24 g copper and 16 g zinc, totalling 49 : 31. The trap is simply adding the ratios to get 8 : 5, so the answer is 49 : 31.
