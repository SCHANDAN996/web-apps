101. If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 6 : 7, then A : C is
(a) 7 : 9 (b) 3 : 7 (c) 2 : 7 (d) 9 : 14
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is writing 3 : 7 directly by ignoring that B has different values in the two ratios. Make B equal: A : B = 3 : 4 = 9 : 12 and B : C = 6 : 7 = 12 : 14, so A : B : C = 9 : 12 : 14, giving A : C = 9 : 14. So the answer is 9 : 14.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

102. The ratio of two numbers is 5 : 7 and their sum is 96. The smaller number is
(a) 40 (b) 35 (c) 56 (d) 48
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 56 is the larger number, not the smaller. Total parts = 5 + 7 = 12, so one part = 96/12 = 8, and the smaller number = 5 × 8 = 40. So the answer is 40.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

103. If 2A = 3B = 4C, then A : B : C is
(a) 2 : 3 : 4 (b) 6 : 4 : 3 (c) 3 : 4 : 6 (d) 4 : 3 : 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is writing 2 : 3 : 4, but the coefficients are inversely related to the values. Let 2A = 3B = 4C = 12 (LCM), then A = 6, B = 4, C = 3, so A : B : C = 6 : 4 : 3. So the answer is 6 : 4 : 3.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

104. Rs 1,860 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. The share of B is
(a) Rs 620 (b) Rs 775 (c) Rs 465 (d) Rs 560
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking B's share as 4/5 of the total instead of 4/12. Total parts = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12, so B's share = (4/12) × 1860 = Rs 620. So the answer is Rs 620.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

105. The fourth proportional to 6, 8 and 15 is
(a) 18 (b) 12 (c) 24 (d) 20
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing the third proportional (8²/6) instead of the fourth. If 6 : 8 :: 15 : x, then x = (8 × 15)/6 = 20. So the answer is 20.

106. The mean proportional between 9 and 25 is
(a) 17 (b) 15 (c) 12 (d) 18
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is taking the arithmetic mean (9 + 25)/2 = 17, but mean proportional means geometric mean. Mean proportional = √(9 × 25) = √225 = 15. So the answer is 15.

107. If a : b = 2 : 3, then (3a + 4b) : (5a + 2b) is
(a) 7 : 8 (b) 8 : 9 (c) 9 : 8 (d) 9 : 7
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is substituting a = 2, b = 3 into only one bracket. Take a = 2k, b = 3k: numerator = 6k + 12k = 18k, denominator = 10k + 6k = 16k, ratio = 18 : 16 = 9 : 8. So the answer is 9 : 8.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

108. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 7 : 5. If there are 36 students in all, the number of girls is
(a) 21 (b) 15 (c) 18 (d) 12
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing boys = 21 and reporting it as the answer. Total parts = 12, one part = 36/12 = 3, so girls = 5 × 3 = 15. So the answer is 15.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

109. If x : y = 5 : 6 and y : z = 4 : 5, then x : y : z is
(a) 10 : 12 : 12 (b) 5 : 6 : 5 (c) 20 : 24 : 25 (d) 10 : 12 : 15
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is writing 5 : 6 : 5 by joining the ratios without equalising y. Make y = 12: x : y = 5 : 6 = 10 : 12 and y : z = 4 : 5 = 12 : 15, so x : y : z = 10 : 12 : 15. So the answer is 10 : 12 : 15.

110. Two numbers are in the ratio 4 : 5. If 8 is added to each, the ratio becomes 6 : 7. The numbers are
(a) 16 and 20 (b) 12 and 15 (c) 20 and 25 (d) 24 and 30
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the difference changes after adding 8; actually the difference stays the same. Let numbers be 4k and 5k: (4k + 8)/(5k + 8) = 6/7 gives 28k + 56 = 30k + 48, so k = 4, and the numbers are 16 and 20. So the answer is 16 and 20.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

111. The third proportional to 12 and 18 is
(a) 30 (b) 24 (c) 27 (d) 36
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing the fourth proportional or the mean proportional √(12 × 18). Third proportional x satisfies 12 : 18 :: 18 : x, so x = 18²/12 = 324/12 = 27. So the answer is 27.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

112. Rs 782 is divided into three parts proportional to 1/2 : 2/3 : 3/4. The largest part is
(a) Rs 204 (b) Rs 306 (c) Rs 272 (d) Rs 340
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using the fractions directly as parts without clearing denominators. Multiply by LCM 12: ratio = 6 : 8 : 9, total = 23 parts, one part = 782/23 = 34, largest = 9 × 34 = Rs 306. So the answer is Rs 306.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

113. If (a + b) : (a − b) = 7 : 3, then a : b is
(a) 2 : 5 (b) 5 : 2 (c) 7 : 3 (d) 10 : 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is guessing 7 : 3 itself. By componendo-dividendo, a/b = (7 + 3)/(7 − 3) = 10/4 = 5/2, so a : b = 5 : 2. So the answer is 5 : 2.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

114. The incomes of A and B are in the ratio 5 : 4 and their expenditures in the ratio 3 : 2. If each saves Rs 1,600, the income of A is
(a) Rs 4,000 (b) Rs 3,200 (c) Rs 4,800 (d) Rs 5,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is setting savings proportional to income, which ignores the expenditure ratio. Let incomes be 5k, 4k and expenditures 3m, 2m: 5k − 3m = 1600 and 4k − 2m = 1600. Solving, k = 800, so A's income = 5 × 800 = Rs 4,000. So the answer is Rs 4,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

115. A bag contains 50 paise, 25 paise and 10 paise coins in the ratio 5 : 9 : 4, amounting to Rs 206. The number of 25 paise coins is
(a) 200 (b) 450 (c) 160 (d) 360
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dividing Rs 206 by the ratio sum without converting coins to value. Value ratio = (5 × 50) : (9 × 25) : (4 × 10) = 250 : 225 : 40 paise; total parts = 515, one part = 20600/515 = 40 paise, so 25-paise coins = 9 × 40 = 360. So the answer is 360.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

116. If a/b = 3/4, then the value of (2a + 3b)/(3a + 5b) is
(a) 17/29 (b) 18/29 (c) 18/31 (d) 15/29
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding numerators and denominators of 3/4 directly. Take a = 3, b = 4: numerator = 6 + 12 = 18, denominator = 9 + 20 = 29, so the value is 18/29. So the answer is 18/29.

117. The ratio of the ages of a father and son is 7 : 2. After 10 years the ratio will be 9 : 4. The present age of the son is
(a) 16 years (b) 12 years (c) 10 years (d) 14 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming the age gap changes with time; it stays constant. Let ages be 7k and 2k: (7k + 10)/(2k + 10) = 9/4 gives 28k + 40 = 18k + 90, so k = 5, and the son's age = 2 × 5 = 10 years. So the answer is 10 years.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

118. If 30% of A = 0.25 of B = 1/5 of C, then A : B : C is
(a) 10 : 12 : 15 (b) 15 : 12 : 10 (c) 6 : 5 : 4 (d) 10 : 15 : 12
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is writing the coefficients 30 : 25 : 20 in order. Let each equal k: A = k/0.30 = 10k/3, B = k/0.25 = 4k, C = 5k; ratio = 10/3 : 4 : 5 = 10 : 12 : 15. So the answer is 10 : 12 : 15.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

119. Rs 4,200 is divided among A, B, C such that A gets half of what B and C together get, and B gets half of what A and C together get. C's share is
(a) Rs 1,600 (b) Rs 1,200 (c) Rs 1,800 (d) Rs 1,400
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming the conditions force unequal shares. A = (B + C)/2 gives 3A = A + B + C = 4200, so A = 1400; similarly B = 1400; hence C = 4200 − 2800 = Rs 1,400. So the answer is Rs 1,400.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

120. The ratio of copper to zinc in alloy P is 5 : 3 and in alloy Q is 3 : 2. Equal weights of P and Q are melted together. The ratio of copper to zinc in the new alloy is
(a) 49 : 31 (b) 8 : 5 (c) 41 : 29 (d) 7 : 5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding the ratios term by term to get 8 : 5, which ignores that the two alloys have different total parts (8 and 5). Take 40 g of each (LCM of 8 and 5): P gives 25 g Cu and 15 g Zn; Q gives 24 g Cu and 16 g Zn; total Cu : Zn = 49 : 31. So the answer is 49 : 31.
Source: PYQ-style

121. A sum of Rs 10,500 is divided among A, B and C such that A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5. The difference between the shares of C and A is
(a) Rs 2,100 (b) Rs 1,800 (c) Rs 2,400 (d) Rs 1,500
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is joining the ratios as 2 : 3 : 5 without equalising B. A : B = 2 : 3 = 8 : 12 and B : C = 4 : 5 = 12 : 15, so A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15, total = 35 parts, one part = 10500/35 = 300, and C − A = 7 × 300 = Rs 2,100. So the answer is Rs 2,100.
Source: PYQ-style

122. The ratio of the number of students in three schools is 3 : 4 : 5. If 20 students are added to each school, the ratio becomes 5 : 6 : 7. The original total number of students is
(a) 240 (b) 360 (c) 120 (d) 480
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming the gaps between terms change after adding 20; they stay equal, which is why the new ratio also has gaps of one part. Let the numbers be 3k, 4k, 5k: (3k + 20)/(4k + 20) = 5/6 gives 18k + 120 = 20k + 100, so k = 10, and the original total = 12k = 120. So the answer is 120.
Source: PYQ-style

123. A and B started a business with capitals in the ratio 4 : 5. After 6 months A withdrew half his capital. The ratio in which the profit is divided at the end of the year is
(a) 4 : 5 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 3 : 5 (d) 7 : 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using 4 : 5 for the whole year, ignoring the withdrawal. A's effective capital-months = 4 × 6 + 2 × 6 = 36; B's = 5 × 12 = 60; profit ratio = 36 : 60 = 3 : 5. So the answer is 3 : 5.
Source: PYQ-style

124. If (a + b) : (b + c) : (c + a) = 5 : 6 : 7 and a + b + c = 36, then the value of c is
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 18
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is dividing 36 in the ratio 5 : 6 : 7 directly, which gives a + b, b + c, c + a — not a, b, c. Sum of the three = 2(a + b + c) = 72, so one part = 72/18 = 4; a + b = 20, b + c = 24, c + a = 28; then c = (a + b + c) − (a + b) = 36 − 20 = 16. So the answer is 16.
Source: PYQ-style

125. A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7 : 3. How much water must be added to 40 litres of the mixture so that the ratio becomes 7 : 5?
(a) 6 litres (b) 12 litres (c) 10 litres (d) 8 litres
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding water to the whole 40 litres ratio-wise instead of keeping milk fixed. Milk = 28 litres, water = 12 litres; milk stays 28, so 7 parts = 28, hence 5 parts water = 20 litres; water to add = 20 − 12 = 8 litres. So the answer is 8 litres.
Source: PYQ-style
