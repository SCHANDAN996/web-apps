126. In a library, the ratio of books on Computer Science, Mathematics, and Physics is 3 : 4 : 5. If their quantities are increased by 20%, 50%, and 40% respectively, what will be the new ratio of the books?
(a) 18 : 30 : 35 (b) 15 : 25 : 30 (c) 12 : 20 : 25 (d) 18 : 28 : 35
Answer: (a) 18 : 30 : 35
Solution: Let the initial number of books be 30, 40, and 50. Increased numbers: Computer Science = 30 * 1.20 = 36; Mathematics = 40 * 1.50 = 60; Physics = 50 * 1.40 = 70. The new ratio is 36 : 60 : 70. Dividing by 2 gives 18 : 30 : 35. The required value is 18 : 30 : 35. Hence, the correct option is (a).

127. A vessel contains a mixture of two liquids P and Q in the ratio 5 : 3. When 16 litres of the mixture is drawn off and replaced by liquid Q, the ratio of P to Q becomes 3 : 5. How many litres of the mixture was contained by the vessel initially?
(a) 45 litres (b) 40 litres (c) 36 litres (d) 48 litres
Answer: (b) 40 litres
Solution: After 16 litres is drawn off, the remaining mixture has P : Q = 5 : 3 = 15 : 9. After adding 16 litres of Q, the ratio becomes 3 : 5 = 15 : 25. Liquid P remains constant at 15 units. The increase in Q is 25 - 9 = 16 units = 16 litres, giving 1 unit = 1 litre. Total volume after replacement = 15 + 25 = 40 units = 40 litres, which equals initial total capacity. The required value is 40 litres. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

128. The incomes of X, Y, and Z are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 2 and their expenditures are in the ratio 5 : 5 : 2. If X saves 1/3 of his income, what is the ratio of savings of X, Y, and Z?
(a) 6 : 10 : 5 (b) 5 : 8 : 6 (c) 5 : 10 : 6 (d) 4 : 10 : 5
Answer: (c) 5 : 10 : 6
Solution: Let incomes be 3I, 4I, 2I and expenditures be 5E, 5E, 2E. X saves 1/3 of income, meaning X spends 2/3 of income: 5E = (2/3)(3I) = 2I => I / E = 5 / 2. Setting I = 5 and E = 2: Incomes are 15, 20, 10; Expenditures are 10, 10, 4. Savings = Incomes - Expenditures: X = 15 - 10 = 5; Y = 20 - 10 = 10; Z = 10 - 4 = 6. Ratio of savings = 5 : 10 : 6. The required value is 5 : 10 : 6. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

129. An alloy contains copper, zinc, and nickel in the ratio 5 : 3 : 2. How much nickel must be added to 100 kg of this alloy so that the new ratio becomes 5 : 3 : 3?
(a) 15 kg (b) 8 kg (c) 12 kg (d) 10 kg
Answer: (d) 10 kg
Solution: Sum of initial parts = 5 + 3 + 2 = 10 parts = 100 kg => 1 part = 10 kg. Initial quantities: Copper = 50 kg, Zinc = 30 kg, Nickel = 20 kg. In the desired ratio 5 : 3 : 3, copper and zinc are 50 kg and 30 kg (5 and 3 parts), so 1 part remains 10 kg. Required nickel = 3 parts = 30 kg. Nickel to add = 30 - 20 = 10 kg. The required value is 10 kg. Hence, the correct option is (d).

130. The ratio of land to water on the whole earth is 1 : 2 and in the Northern Hemisphere it is 2 : 3. What is the ratio of land to water in the Southern Hemisphere?
(a) 4 : 11 (b) 11 : 4 (c) 3 : 7 (d) 4 : 9
Answer: (a) 4 : 11
Solution: Assume the total area of the Earth is 300 units, divided into 150 units Northern and 150 units Southern Hemisphere. On Earth: Land = 100, Water = 200. In Northern Hemisphere (ratio 2 : 3, sum 5): Land = 150 * (2/5) = 60; Water = 150 * (3/5) = 90. Southern Hemisphere: Land = 100 - 60 = 40; Water = 200 - 90 = 110. Ratio of land to water = 40 : 110 = 4 : 11. The required value is 4 : 11. Hence, the correct option is (a).

131. A person bought some oranges at the rate of 5 for a rupee and an equal number at the rate of 4 for a rupee. He mixed them and sold them at the rate of 9 for 2 rupees. What is the ratio of his cost price to selling price for the whole transaction?
(a) 80 : 81 (b) 81 : 80 (c) 9 : 8 (d) 8 : 9
Answer: (b) 81 : 80
Solution: Let the person buy 20 oranges of each type (LCM of 4 and 5). Total oranges = 40. Cost of first 20 oranges = 20 / 5 = Rs 4. Cost of second 20 oranges = 20 / 4 = Rs 5. Total Cost Price = Rs 9. Selling Price of 40 oranges = 40 * (2 / 9) = Rs 80/9. Ratio of Cost Price to Selling Price = 9 : (80/9) = 81 : 80. The required value is 81 : 80. Hence, the correct option is (b).

132. If x : y = 5 : 2, find the value of (8x + 9y) : (8x + 2y).
(a) 22 : 29 (b) 25 : 18 (c) 29 : 22 (d) 27 : 20
Answer: (c) 29 : 22
Solution: Dividing numerator and denominator by y gives [8(x/y) + 9] / [8(x/y) + 2]. Substituting x/y = 5/2: Numerator = 8(5/2) + 9 = 20 + 9 = 29. Denominator = 8(5/2) + 2 = 20 + 2 = 22. Ratio = 29 : 22. The required value is 29 : 22. Hence, the correct option is (c).

133. A cask is full of 60 litres of alcohol. 6 litres are drawn off and replaced with water. Again 6 litres are drawn off and replaced with water, and this is done once more. Find the final ratio of alcohol to water in the cask.
(a) 729 : 1000 (b) 271 : 729 (c) 81 : 19 (d) 729 : 271
Answer: (d) 729 : 271
Solution: Fraction of liquid replaced in each step = 6 / 60 = 1/10. The proportion of alcohol remaining after 3 replacements = (1 - 1/10)^3 = (9/10)^3 = 729 / 1000. Alcohol represents 729 parts out of 1000 total parts. Water fraction = 1000 - 729 = 271 parts. Final ratio of alcohol to water = 729 : 271. The required value is 729 : 271. Hence, the correct option is (d).

134. A, B, and C entered into a business. A contributed 1/3 of the total capital, B contributed as much as A and C together contributed. What is the ratio of their profits at the end of the year?
(a) 2 : 3 : 1 (b) 1 : 3 : 2 (c) 2 : 1 : 3 (d) 3 : 2 : 1
Answer: (a) 2 : 3 : 1
Solution: Let total capital = 1. A = 1/3. Given B = A + C, and A + B + C = 1 => B + B = 1 => 2B = 1 => B = 1/2. Then C = B - A = 1/2 - 1/3 = 1/6. Ratio of their capitals = 1/3 : 1/2 : 1/6. Multiplying by 6 gives the profit ratio: 2 : 3 : 1. The required value is 2 : 3 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (a).

135. Two candles of the same height are lighted at the same time. The first is consumed in 4 hours and the second in 3 hours. Assuming that each candle burns at a constant rate, in how many hours after being lighted was the first candle twice the height of the second?
(a) 2.0 hours (b) 2.4 hours (c) 2.8 hours (d) 1.8 hours
Answer: (b) 2.4 hours
Solution: Let the common initial height be 12 units. Burning rate of first candle = 3 units/hour; second candle = 4 units/hour. After t hours, height of first is 12 - 3t and second is 12 - 4t. Condition: 12 - 3t = 2(12 - 4t) => 12 - 3t = 24 - 8t => 5t = 12 => t = 2.4 hours. The required value is 2.4 hours. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

136. A fox pursues a hare and takes 4 leaps for every 5 leaps of the hare, but 3 leaps of the fox are equal to 4 leaps of the hare. What is the ratio of the speed of the fox to that of the hare?
(a) 15 : 16 (b) 12 : 15 (c) 16 : 15 (d) 5 : 4
Answer: (c) 16 : 15
Solution: Speed equals (Distance per leap) * (Frequency of leaps). Given 3 leaps of fox = 4 leaps of hare, so distance per leap ratio Fox / Hare = 4 / 3. Frequency ratio Fox / Hare = 4 / 5. Ratio of speeds = (4 * 4) : (3 * 5) = 16 : 15. The required value is 16 : 15. Hence, the correct option is (c).

137. The ratio of the number of men, women, and children in a town is 9 : 8 : 3. If 80% of the men, 70% of the women, and 90% of the children are literate, find the ratio of literate persons to illiterate persons in the town.
(a) 9 : 31 (b) 29 : 11 (c) 33 : 7 (d) 31 : 9
Answer: (d) 31 : 9
Solution: Taking men = 90, women = 80, children = 30. Total population = 200. Literate count: Men = 90 * 0.80 = 72; Women = 80 * 0.70 = 56; Children = 30 * 0.90 = 27. Total literate = 72 + 56 + 27 = 155. Illiterate count = 200 - 155 = 45. Ratio of literate to illiterate = 155 : 45 = 31 : 9. The required value is 31 : 9. Hence, the correct option is (d).

138. An amount of money is to be distributed among P, Q, and R in the ratio 2 : 7 : 9. The total of P's and Q's share is equal to R's share. If the difference between P's and Q's share is Rs 1,500, what is R's share?

139. The ratio of incomes of two persons is 5 : 3 and that of their expenditures is 9 : 5. If they save Rs 2,600 and Rs 1,800 respectively, what is the income of the second person?
(a) Rs 8,000 (b) Rs 4,800 (c) Rs 4,500 (d) Rs 5,200
Answer: (b) Rs 4,800
Solution: Let incomes be 5x and 3x. Expenditures are (5x - 2600) and (3x - 1800). Setting ratio: (5x - 2600) / (3x - 1800) = 9 / 5 => 25x - 13000 = 27x - 16200 => 2x = 3200 => x = 1600. Income of second person = 3x = 3 * 1600 = Rs 4,800. The required value is Rs 4,800. Hence, the correct option is (b).

140. If y varies directly as the sum of two quantities, of which one varies directly as x and the other varies inversely as x, and if y = 6 when x = 1, and y = 3.5 when x = 2, find the equation connecting x and y.
(a) 2xy = x^2 + 10 (b) 3xy = 2x^2 + 15 (c) 3xy = x^2 + 17 (d) xy = x^2 + 5
Answer: (c) 3xy = x^2 + 17
Solution: Form of relationship: y = ax + b/x. For x = 1, y = 6 gives a + b = 6. For x = 2, y = 3.5 gives 2a + b/2 = 3.5 => 4a + b = 7. Subtracting gives 3a = 1 => a = 1/3, so b = 17/3. Thus y = x/3 + 17/(3x) => 3y = x + 17/x => 3xy = x^2 + 17. The required value is 3xy = x^2 + 17. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

141. A vessel has 20 litres of pure milk. From this, 4 litres of milk is taken out and replaced by water. Again 4 litres of the mixture is taken out and replaced by water. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(a) 9 : 16 (b) 16 : 25 (c) 25 : 16 (d) 16 : 9
Answer: (d) 16 : 9
Solution: Fraction replaced each cycle = 4 / 20 = 1/5. Milk remaining fraction = (1 - 1/5)^2 = 16 / 25. Thus, out of 25 total parts, milk is 16 parts and water is 25 - 16 = 9 parts. Ratio of milk to water = 16 : 9. The required value is 16 : 9. Hence, the correct option is (d).

If all contents of all three vessels are mixed together, find the ratio of milk to water in the new mixture.
(a) 53 : 17 (b) 17 : 53 (c) 52 : 19 (d) 55 : 16

Vessel 3: Milk = 35 * (4/5) = 28, Water = 7. Total milk = 75 + 56 + 28 = 159; Total water = 30 + 14 + 7 = 51. Ratio = 159 : 51 = 53 : 17. The required value is 53 : 17. Hence, the correct option is (a).

144. A sum of Rs 5,640 is divided among three friends in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. By mistake, it was divided in the ratio 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/5. Who gained the maximum amount and how much?
(a) Third friend, Rs 910 (b) First friend, Rs 850 (c) First friend, Rs 990 (d) Second friend, Rs 80
Answer: (c) First friend, Rs 990
Solution: Correct ratio is 3 : 4 : 5 (sum = 12 parts). Actual shares: First = 5640 * 3/12 = Rs 1410; Second = Rs 1880; Third = Rs 2350. Erroneous ratio: 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/5 = 20 : 15 : 12 (sum = 47 parts). Erroneous shares: First = 5640 * 20/47 = Rs 2400; Second = Rs 1800; Third = Rs 1440. Gain of first friend = 2400 - 1410 = Rs 990, which is the maximum gain. The required value is First friend, Rs 990. Hence, the correct option is (c).

145. If (x^3 + 3x) / (3x^2 + 1) = 341 / 91, find the value of x.
(a) 9 (b) 13 (c) 7 (d) 11
Answer: (d) 11
Solution: Applying Componendo and Dividendo: [(x^3 + 3x) + (3x^2 + 1)] / [(x^3 + 3x) - (3x^2 + 1)] = (341 + 91) / (341 - 91) => (x + 1)^3 / (x - 1)^3 = 432 / 250 = 216 / 125. Taking cube root: (x + 1) / (x - 1) = 6 / 5 => 5x + 5 = 6x - 6 => x = 11. The required value is 11. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

148. A box contains Rs 56 in the form of Rs 1, 50p, and 25p coins. The number of 50p coins is double the number of 25p coins and 4 times the number of Rs 1 coins. How many 50p coins are in the box?
(a) 32 (b) 16 (c) 64 (d) 48
Answer: (c) 64
Solution: Let Rs 1 coins = x. Number of 50p coins = 4x, and 25p coins = 2x. Total value in rupees = (x * 1) + (4x * 0.50) + (2x * 0.25) = x + 2x + 0.5x = 3.5x. Given 3.5x = 56 => x = 56 / 3.5 = 16. Total number of 50p coins = 4x = 4 * 16 = 64. The required value is 64. Hence, the correct option is (c).

149. If a : b = c : d = e : f = 1 : 2, find the value of (pa + qc + re) : (pb + qd + rf).
(a) 2 : 1 (b) p : q (c) r : (p + q) (d) 1 : 2
Answer: (d) 1 : 2
Solution: Given a/b = c/d = e/f = 1/2. By the fundamental property of equal ratios: (p*a + q*c + r*e) / (p*b + q*d + r*f) = a / b = 1 / 2 = 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (d).

150. Two railway tickets from city A to B and three tickets from A to C cost Rs 177, but three tickets from A to B and two tickets from A to C cost Rs 173. What is the ratio of the fare from A to B to the fare from A to C?
(a) 37 : 33 (b) 33 : 37 (c) 31 : 35 (d) 35 : 39
Answer: (b) 33 : 37
Solution: Let fares be x and y. Equations: 2x + 3y = 177 and 3x + 2y = 173. Adding both equations gives 5x + 5y = 350 => x + y = 70. Subtracting the first from the second gives x - y = -4 => y - x = 4. Adding these two simplified equations gives 2y = 74 => y = 37, and x = 70 - 37 = 33. The ratio x : y = 33 : 37. The required value is 33 : 37. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
