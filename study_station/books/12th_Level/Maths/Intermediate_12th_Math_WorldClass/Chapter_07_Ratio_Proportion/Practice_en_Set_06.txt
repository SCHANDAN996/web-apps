126. The incomes of A and B are in the ratio 5 : 3 and their expenditures are in the ratio 9 : 5. If each saves ₹1,300, what is A's income?
(a) ₹6,500 (b) ₹10,400 (c) ₹13,000 (d) ₹7,800
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let incomes be 5x and 3x, expenditures 9y and 5y. Then 5x − 9y = 1300 and 3x − 5y = 1300, so 5x − 9y = 3x − 5y, giving x = 2y; substituting, 3(2y) − 5y = y = 1300, so x = 2600. The tempting wrong option ₹6,500 comes from wrongly taking 5x − 3x = 1300. A's income = 5 × 2600, so the answer is ₹13,000.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

127. If a : b = 2 : 3, b : c = 4 : 5 and c : d = 6 : 7, then a : d is
(a) 4 : 7 (b) 8 : 15 (c) 16 : 35 (d) 12 : 35
Answer: (c)
Solution: a/d = (a/b)(b/c)(c/d) = (2/3)(4/5)(6/7) = 48/105 = 16/35. The trap is taking only the first and last numbers (2 : 7) instead of multiplying the whole chain. Multiplying correctly gives 16 : 35, so the answer is 16 : 35.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Ratio concepts)

128. ₹5,580 is divided among A, B and C so that A gets half of what B and C together get, and B gets one-third of what A and C together get. C's share is
(a) ₹2,790 (b) ₹1,860 (c) ₹1,395 (d) ₹2,325
Answer: (d)
Solution: A = (B + C)/2 means A = total/3 = ₹1,860; B = (A + C)/3 means B = total/4 = ₹1,395. Then C = 5580 − 1860 − 1395 = ₹2,325. The trap is assuming equal shares of ₹1,860 each. So the answer is ₹2,325.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

129. The ratio of two numbers is 3 : 4. If 8 is added to each, the ratio becomes 5 : 6. The sum of the numbers is
(a) 35 (b) 42 (c) 28 (d) 56
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let the numbers be 3x and 4x. (3x + 8)/(4x + 8) = 5/6 gives 18x + 48 = 20x + 40, so x = 4; the numbers are 12 and 16, and 20/24 = 5/6 checks. The trap option 56 doubles the sum by mistake. Sum = 12 + 16, so the answer is 28.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

130. If (3a + 5b) : (3a − 5b) = 5 : 1, then a : b equals
(a) 5 : 3 (b) 5 : 2 (c) 3 : 2 (d) 2 : 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: By componendo-dividendo, 6a/10b = 6/4, so a/b = (6/4)(10/6) = 5/2. The trap is inverting the result to 2 : 5 or misapplying the rule. So a : b = 5 : 2, and the answer is 5 : 2.
Source: PYQ-style (componendo-dividendo, SSC pattern)

131. Three numbers are in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. If their sum is 156, the largest number is
(a) 36 (b) 48 (c) 72 (d) 60
Answer: (c)
Solution: Multiply the ratio by 12 to get 6 : 4 : 3, total 13 parts. Largest = 156 × 6/13 = 72. The trap is treating 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4 as 2 : 3 : 4, which picks the wrong term as largest. So the answer is 72.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

132. A bag contains ₹1, 50-paise and 25-paise coins in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5 (by value). If the total value is ₹240, the number of 25-paise coins is
(a) 320 (b) 400 (c) 480 (d) 240
Answer: (b)
Solution: Total 12 parts = ₹240, so 1 part = ₹20. Value of 25-paise coins = 5 × 20 = ₹100, and number of coins = 100 ÷ 0.25 = 400. The trap is dividing by 0.5, which mixes up the coin denominations. So the answer is 400.
Source: PYQ-style (coin problems, SSC/RRB pattern)

133. If x : y = 3 : 4, then (7x + 3y) : (7x − 3y) equals
(a) 4 : 1 (b) 9 : 11 (c) 11 : 9 (d) 11 : 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: Put x = 3, y = 4: 7x + 3y = 33 and 7x − 3y = 9, giving 33 : 9 = 11 : 3. The trap option 9 : 11 inverts the ratio, and a ratio must always be reduced to lowest terms. So the answer is 11 : 3.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

134. The prices of a scooter and a TV are in the ratio 7 : 5. If the scooter costs ₹8,000 more than the TV, the price of the TV is
(a) ₹28,000 (b) ₹24,000 (c) ₹20,000 (d) ₹32,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Difference of parts = 7 − 5 = 2 parts = ₹8,000, so 1 part = ₹4,000. TV = 5 parts = ₹20,000. The trap option ₹28,000 is the scooter's price, not the TV's. So the answer is ₹20,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

135. A, B and C start a business with capitals in the ratio 4 : 5 : 6. A invests for the whole year, B for 9 months and C for 6 months. The profit-sharing ratio is
(a) 8 : 7 : 6 (b) 16 : 15 : 12 (c) 4 : 5 : 6 (d) 2 : 3 : 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: Profit ratio = capital × time = 4×12 : 5×9 : 6×6 = 48 : 45 : 36 = 16 : 15 : 12. The trap option 4 : 5 : 6 ignores the different time periods. So the answer is 16 : 15 : 12.
Source: PYQ-style (partnership, IBPS/SSC pattern)

136. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 4 : 3. If 10 boys leave and 10 girls join, the ratio becomes 3 : 4. The original number of boys was
(a) 40 (b) 30 (c) 70 (d) 50
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let boys = 4x and girls = 3x. Then (4x − 10)/(3x + 10) = 3/4 gives 16x − 40 = 9x + 30, so 7x = 70 and x = 10; check: (40 − 10)/(30 + 10) = 30/40 = 3/4 ✓. The trap option 70 is the total strength, not the boys. Original boys = 4 × 10, so the answer is 40.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

137. If a/b = c/d = e/f = 2/3, then (3a + 2c + 5e)/(3b + 2d + 5f) equals
(a) 5/6 (b) 2/3 (c) 3/2 (d) 4/9
Answer: (b)
Solution: By the equal-ratios property, each numerator term is 2/3 of the matching denominator term, so the whole expression equals 2/3. The trap option 3/2 is the inverted ratio. So the answer is 2/3.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (properties of equal ratios)

138. ₹1,880 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/5. B's share is
(a) ₹600 (b) ₹720 (c) ₹480 (d) ₹540
Answer: (a)
Solution: Multiply the ratio by 60 to get 20 : 15 : 12, total 47 parts. B's share = 1880 × 15/47 = ₹600. The trap is reading 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/5 as 3 : 4 : 5, which gives a wrong share. So the answer is ₹600.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

139. The third proportional to 12 and 18 is
(a) 27 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Third proportional x satisfies 12 : 18 = 18 : x, so x = 18²/12 = 324/12 = 27. The trap option 24 comes from wrongly averaging or misplacing the terms. So the answer is 27.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

140. The mean proportional between 0.08 and 0.18 is
(a) 0.12 (b) 0.13 (c) 0.0144 (d) 0.16
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mean proportional = √(0.08 × 0.18) = √0.0144 = 0.12. The trap option 0.0144 is the product before taking the square root, and 0.13 is the arithmetic mean. So the answer is 0.12.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

141. If 2A = 3B = 4C, then A : B : C is
(a) 3 : 4 : 6 (b) 2 : 3 : 4 (c) 4 : 3 : 2 (d) 6 : 4 : 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let 2A = 3B = 4C = k. Then A = k/2, B = k/3, C = k/4, and multiplying by 12 gives 6 : 4 : 3. The trap option 2 : 3 : 4 copies the coefficients directly instead of inverting them. So the answer is 6 : 4 : 3.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

142. A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 1. After adding 5 litres of water, the ratio becomes 5 : 2. The quantity of milk in the mixture is
(a) 25 litres (b) 15 litres (c) 20 litres (d) 30 litres
Answer: (a)
Solution: Milk is unchanged; water rises from 1 part to 2 parts, so 1 part = 5 litres and milk = 5 parts = 25 litres. The trap is treating the 5 litres added as 5 parts. So the answer is 25 litres.
Source: PYQ-style (mixture, SSC/RRB pattern)

143. The ratio of the ages of a father and son is 7 : 2. After 10 years the ratio will be 9 : 4. The father's present age is
(a) 35 years (b) 42 years (c) 49 years (d) 28 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let ages be 7x and 2x. (7x + 10)/(2x + 10) = 9/4 gives 28x + 40 = 18x + 90, so x = 5 and father = 35 years. The trap option 49 comes from solving with the ratio inverted. So the answer is 35 years.
Source: PYQ-style (ages, SSC pattern)

144. If (a + b) : (b + c) : (c + a) = 6 : 7 : 8 and a + b + c = 21, then c equals
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 6 (d) 7
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sum of the ratio terms = 21 parts = 2(a + b + c) = 42, so 1 part = 2 and a + b = 12. Then c = 21 − 12 = 9. The trap option 8 is the ratio value of (c + a), not c itself. So the answer is 9.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

145. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7. On subtracting 12 from each, the ratio becomes 3 : 5. The smaller number is
(a) 40 (b) 35 (c) 25 (d) 30
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let the numbers be 5x and 7x. (5x − 12)/(7x − 12) = 3/5 gives 25x − 60 = 21x − 36, so x = 6 and the smaller number = 30. The trap option 35 is the larger number, not the smaller. So the answer is 30.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

146. In a 60-litre mixture of milk and water, water is 20%. How much water must be added so that water becomes 40% of the new mixture?
(a) 15 litres (b) 12 litres (c) 24 litres (d) 20 litres
Answer: (d)
Solution: Milk = 80% of 60 = 48 litres, unchanged. In the new mixture milk is 60%, so total = 48/0.6 = 80 litres and water added = 80 − 60 = 20 litres. The trap is adding 12 litres by ignoring that the total volume grows. So the answer is 20 litres.
Source: PYQ-style (mixture, IBPS/SSC pattern)

147. If x/2 = y/3 = z/5 and x + y + z = 100, then y − x equals
(a) 30 (b) 20 (c) 10 (d) 5
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let x/2 = y/3 = z/5 = k. Then x = 2k, y = 3k, z = 5k; sum = 10k = 100, so k = 10 and y − x = k = 10. The trap option 20 is the value of x, not the difference. So the answer is 10.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

148. The ratio of the areas of two squares is 9 : 16. The ratio of their perimeters is
(a) 9 : 16 (b) 3 : 4 (c) 4 : 3 (d) 81 : 256
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sides are in ratio √9 : √16 = 3 : 4, and perimeters share the same ratio as sides. The trap option 9 : 16 keeps the area ratio for the perimeter, forgetting the square root. So the answer is 3 : 4.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

149. A sum of money is divided among P, Q and R such that P's share : Q's share = 2 : 3 and Q's share : R's share = 4 : 5. If R gets ₹350 more than P, the total sum is
(a) ₹1,400 (b) ₹1,575 (c) ₹2,100 (d) ₹1,750
Answer: (d)
Solution: Combine the ratios: P : Q = 8 : 12 and Q : R = 12 : 15, so P : Q : R = 8 : 12 : 15. R − P = 7 parts = ₹350, so 1 part = ₹50 and total = 35 × 50 = ₹1,750. The trap is adding the ratios 2 : 3 : 4 : 5 instead of combining them through Q. So the answer is ₹1,750.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

150. The ratio of the speeds of two trains is 4 : 5. If the first train covers 320 km in a certain time, the distance covered by the second train in the same time is
(a) 400 km (b) 360 km (c) 384 km (d) 420 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: In equal time, distance is proportional to speed, so the second train covers 320 × 5/4 = 400 km. The trap option 384 km comes from wrongly adding 20% (320 × 1.2) instead of scaling by 5/4. So the answer is 400 km.
Source: PYQ-style (speed-distance, RRB NTPC pattern)
