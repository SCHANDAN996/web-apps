Card 1
Front: What is a ratio, in one line?
Back: A comparison of two quantities of the same kind by division, written as a : b (a/b).

Card 2
Front: In the ratio 3 : 5, what are 3 and 5 called?
Back: 3 is the antecedent (first term) and 5 is the consequent (second term).

Card 3
Front: When can two quantities NOT be compared as a ratio?
Back: When they are of different kinds or different units — e.g., 5 kg vs 2 metres. Convert to the same unit first.

Card 4
Front: What is the duplicate ratio of a : b?
Back: a² : b². (Square both terms.)

Card 5
Front: What is the sub-duplicate ratio of a : b?
Back: √a : √b. (Take square roots of both terms.)

Card 6
Front: What is the triplicate ratio of 2 : 3?
Back: 2³ : 3³ = 8 : 27.

Card 7
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember duplicate, sub-duplicate, triplicate?
Back: "D-S-T = 2-½-3" — Duplicate = power 2, Sub-duplicate = power ½, Triplicate = power 3. Powers go 2, half, 3!

Card 8
Front: What is a proportion?
Back: An equality of two ratios: a : b = c : d, written a : b :: c : d.

Card 9
Front: In a : b :: c : d, which are the extremes and which are the means?
Back: a and d are extremes; b and c are means. Rule: Product of extremes = Product of means (ad = bc).

Card 10
Front: What is the memory trick for "product of extremes = product of means"?
Back: "Outside × Outside = Inside × Inside" — the outer numbers multiply to equal the inner numbers.

Card 11
Front: What is the mean proportion between a and b?
Back: √(ab). If a : x :: x : b, then x² = ab.

Card 12
Front: What is the third proportion to a and b?
Back: x such that a : b :: b : x, so x = b²/a.

Card 13
Front: What is the fourth proportion to a, b, c?
Back: x such that a : b :: c : x, so x = bc/a.

Card 14
Front: State the componendo rule.
Back: If a/b = c/d, then (a + b)/b = (c + d)/d.

Card 15
Front: State the dividendo rule.
Back: If a/b = c/d, then (a − b)/b = (c − d)/d.

Card 16
Front: State componendo-dividendo — the most exam-used rule.
Back: If a/b = c/d, then (a + b)/(a − b) = (c + d)/(c − d).

Card 17
Front: What is the mnemonic for componendo-dividendo?
Back: "C-D = Plus over Minus" — Componendo adds (+), Dividendo subtracts (−); together they give (sum)/(difference).

Card 18
Front: If a/b = c/d = e/f, what does each ratio equal?
Back: (a + c + e)/(b + d + f) — add all numerators and all denominators (addendo rule).

Card 19
Front: A ratio a : b is in simplest form when…?
Back: a and b have no common factor other than 1 (HCF = 1).

Card 20
Front: If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, how do you find A : B : C quickly?
Back: Make B equal in both: A : B = 8 : 12 and B : C = 12 : 15, so A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15.
