# 📋 Key Facts & Figures — Indian Economy (Basic)

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Father of Indian Economics | Dadabhai Naoroji | Confused with Mahatma Gandhi (called "Father of the Nation") | Similar-name confusion |
| "Drain of Wealth" theory | Given by Dadabhai Naoroji in "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India" | Book title swapped with "Hind Swaraj" (Gandhi) | Book-author mismatch |
| Planning Commission | Established 15 March 1950; Chairman = PM | Confused with NITI Aayog (1 January 2015) | Chronology confusion |
| NITI Aayog | Replaced Planning Commission in 2015; Vice-Chairperson is the executive head | "CEO is the head" — wrong; PM is Chairperson | Near-correct statement |
| First Five-Year Plan | 1951–56, based on Harrod-Domar model, focus: agriculture | Model swapped with Mahalanobis (2nd Plan) | Model-plan mismatch |
| Second Five-Year Plan | 1956–61, Mahalanobis model, focus: heavy industries | Attributed to 1st Plan | Model-plan mismatch |
| Plan Holiday | 1966–69 (three Annual Plans) | Called "Plan Vacation" or placed 1965–68 | Terminology trap |
| National Income estimation | CSO (now NSO) estimates it; first scientific estimate by Dadabhai Naoroji (1867–68) | RBI given as estimator | Institution swap |
| GDP at market price | GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) | Formula reversed (GDP = GNP + NFIA) | Formula reversal |
| Base year of GDP series | 2011–12 | Old base years (2004–05) quoted | Outdated fact |
| RBI | Established 1 April 1935 (RBI Act 1934); nationalised 1 January 1949 | "Nationalised in 1935" — wrong | Date swap |
| RBI headquarters | Mumbai (originally Kolkata, shifted 1937) | "Established in Mumbai" — wrong | Near-correct statement |
| First Governor of RBI | Osborne Smith (first Indian: C.D. Deshmukh) | Deshmukh given as first Governor | Similar-name confusion |
| Bank nationalisation | 14 banks in 1969; 6 more in 1980 | "20 banks in 1969" — wrong | Number inflation |
| SBI formation | 1 July 1955 (from Imperial Bank, est. 1921) | Confused with RBI nationalisation year | Date confusion |
| NABARD | Established 12 July 1982 (Shivaraman Committee) | Swapped with SIDBI (1990) | Institution-year mismatch |
| SEBI | Established 1988; statutory status 1992 | "Statutory from 1988" — wrong | Near-correct statement |
| LPG Reforms | New Economic Policy, 24 July 1991; PM: P.V. Narasimha Rao; FM: Manmohan Singh | Credited to Rajiv Gandhi era | Chronology confusion |
| GST | Launched 1 July 2017; 101st Constitutional Amendment; Article 246A | "102nd Amendment" (that is for OBC Commission) | Amendment-number swap |
| GST Council | Article 279A; chaired by Union Finance Minister | "Chaired by PM" — wrong | Near-correct statement |
| First GST country | France (1954) | Canada given as first | Fact substitution |
| Poverty line (India) | Tendulkar Committee (2009), Rangarajan Committee (2014) | Committees' years swapped | Chronology confusion |
| Fiscal deficit | Total expenditure − total receipts (excluding borrowings) | Confused with revenue deficit | Concept swap |
| FRBM Act | 2003 | Quoted as 2005 | Date trap |
| Inflation measurement | WPI (base 2011–12) by DPIIT; CPI by NSO | "RBI publishes WPI" — wrong | Institution swap |
| Repo rate | Rate at which RBI lends to banks | Swapped with reverse repo | Terminology reversal |
| Monetary Policy Committee | 6 members, 3 from RBI; headed by RBI Governor; formed 2016 | "Headed by Finance Minister" — wrong | Near-correct statement |
| Demonetisation | ₹500 and ₹1000 notes withdrawn on 8 November 2016 | ₹2000 also said to be withdrawn that day | Fact addition trap |
| Green Revolution | M.S. Swaminathan (father in India); Norman Borlaug (world) | Names interchanged | Person-role swap |
| White Revolution | Verghese Kurien (Operation Flood) | Confused with Green Revolution | Revolution mismatch |
| HDI | Published by UNDP (since 1990); 3 dimensions: health, education, income | "Published by World Bank" — wrong | Institution swap |
| Currency printing | RBI prints notes (₹2 and above); ₹1 note by Government of India (signed by Finance Secretary) | "RBI prints ₹1 note" — wrong | Near-correct statement |
| Coins minted by | Government of India (4 mints: Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Noida) | Attributed to RBI | Institution swap |
| Budget | Article 112 — Annual Financial Statement | Article 110 (Money Bill) quoted | Article swap |
| First Budget of independent India | R.K. Shanmukham Chetty, 26 November 1947 | John Mathai given as first | Similar-name confusion |
| Budget date | 1 February (since 2017); earlier last working day of February | Old date quoted as current | Outdated fact |
| Fiscal year in India | 1 April – 31 March | January–December quoted | Calendar confusion |
| Disguised unemployment | Found mainly in agriculture; marginal productivity ≈ zero | Associated with industry | Concept misplacement |
| Poverty line concept (world) | $2.15/day (World Bank, extreme poverty) | Old $1.90 figure quoted | Outdated fact |
