🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

1. Five-Year Plans — "PaLaTourist" (Plan Launch Tour)
"PaLaTourist" = **P**lanning Commission **La**unched the **T**welve Plans Tour.
- 1st Plan (1951–56): Agriculture focus (Harrod–Domar model)
- 2nd Plan (1956–61): Industry focus (Mahalanobis model)
- 5th Plan (1974–79): "Garibi Hatao" slogan
- 12th Plan (2012–17): Last Five-Year Plan; NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission in 2015
Trick: "A-I-G-L" — **A**griculture first, **I**ndustry second, **G**aribi Hatao fifth, **L**ast was twelfth.

2. Poverty Line — "Calorie Count"
The poverty line is the minimum income needed to buy a basket of goods providing the required daily calories.
- Rural: 2,400 calories per day
- Urban: 2,100 calories per day
Trick: "Rural needs More" — villagers do heavier physical work, so the rural calorie norm (2,400) is higher than the urban norm (2,100). Remember: **R**ural = **R**eally more (2,400 > 2,100).

3. Planning Bodies — "PC to NITI"
- Planning Commission: 1950 (by a Government of India resolution; non-constitutional body)
- NITI Aayog: 1 January 2015 (National Institution for Transforming India)
Trick: "65 years of Planning, then NITI" — 1950 to 2015 = 65 years of the Planning Commission.

4. National Income — "GDP vs GNP"
GNP = GDP + Net factor income from abroad.
Trick: "G for Global" — GNP adds what Indians earn abroad and subtracts what foreigners earn in India.

5. Inflation Types — "Creeping to Galloping"
Creeping → Walking → Running → Galloping (mild to severe inflation).
Trick: Remember a person speeding up: first creeps, then walks, then runs, then gallops like a horse — prices rise faster at each stage.
