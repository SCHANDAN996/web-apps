126. Which of the following correctly describes the relationship between GDP at factor cost and GDP at market price?
(a) GDP at factor cost = GDP at market price + Net indirect taxes (b) GDP at factor cost = GDP at market price + Depreciation (c) GDP at factor cost = GDP at market price − Net indirect taxes (d) GDP at factor cost = GDP at market price − Net factor income from abroad
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option adds net indirect taxes, but that converts factor cost INTO market price, not the other way round. Market price includes indirect taxes and excludes subsidies, so to reach factor cost we subtract net indirect taxes (indirect taxes − subsidies) from market price, so the answer is GDP at market price − Net indirect taxes.
