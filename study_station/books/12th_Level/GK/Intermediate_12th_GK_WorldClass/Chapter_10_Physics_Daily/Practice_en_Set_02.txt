Questions 26–50: Medium

26. A person standing on a weighing machine inside a lift finds his weight reduced. The lift is:
(a) moving down with acceleration (b) moving up with uniform velocity (c) moving down with uniform velocity (d) at rest
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is moving down with uniform velocity, but uniform velocity produces no pseudo-force, so the reading stays normal. When the lift accelerates downward, apparent weight = m(g − a), which is less than the true weight, so the answer is moving down with acceleration.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics

27. Why does a cricket fielder move his hands backward while catching a fast ball?
(a) To increase the time of impact and reduce the force (b) To decrease the ball's momentum (c) To increase the impulse (d) To reduce the ball's kinetic energy to zero instantly
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is decreasing the momentum — the ball's momentum change is fixed regardless of how the catch is taken. By moving the hands back, the fielder increases the stopping time, and since force = change in momentum ÷ time, the force on the hands decreases, so the answer is to increase the time of impact and reduce the force.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics

28. A convex mirror is preferred as a rear-view mirror in vehicles because:
(a) it gives an erect image with a wider field of view (b) it gives a real image (c) it gives a magnified image (d) it converges light from behind
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is a magnified image — a convex mirror actually forms a diminished image, which is precisely why more of the road fits into view. It always forms an erect, virtual, diminished image with a wide field of view, so the answer is it gives an erect image with a wider field of view.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

29. The sky appears blue because:
(a) the atmosphere absorbs blue light (b) sunlight contains only blue light (c) the ozone layer reflects blue light (d) shorter wavelengths are scattered more than longer wavelengths
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that the atmosphere absorbs blue light — absorption would remove blue, not spread it. Rayleigh scattering is inversely proportional to the fourth power of wavelength, so blue (shorter wavelength) scatters far more than red, so the answer is shorter wavelengths are scattered more than longer wavelengths.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

30. An electric bulb is rated 100 W, 220 V. Its resistance while glowing is:
(a) 2.2 Ω (b) 48.4 Ω (c) 484 Ω (d) 2200 Ω
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option is 48.4 Ω, which comes from wrongly using R = V²/P with V = 22 or dividing incorrectly. Using R = V²/P = 220 × 220 ÷ 100 = 48400 ÷ 100 = 484 Ω, so the answer is 484 Ω.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

31. Which of the following statements about sound is correct?
(a) Sound travels fastest in vacuum (b) Sound is a transverse wave in air (c) Sound travels faster in steel than in air (d) The speed of sound decreases with rise in temperature
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that sound is a transverse wave in air — in air sound is longitudinal, not transverse. Sound needs a medium, travels fastest in solids (about 5960 m/s in steel versus about 343 m/s in air), and its speed increases with temperature, so the answer is sound travels faster in steel than in air.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

32. A piece of ice is floating in a glass of water. When the ice melts completely, the water level will:
(a) rise (b) fall (c) first rise then fall (d) remain unchanged
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is that the level rises, based on the idea that melting adds water. Floating ice displaces water equal to its own weight, and the melted ice occupies exactly that same volume, so the answer is the level remains unchanged.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

33. The working of a pressure cooker is based on the principle that:
(a) boiling point increases with increase in pressure (b) boiling point decreases with increase in pressure (c) pressure has no effect on boiling point (d) steam absorbs all heat inside
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that boiling point decreases with pressure — that is true on mountains where pressure falls, which is why cooking is slow there. Inside a cooker, pressure rises, raising water's boiling point above 100°C so food cooks faster, so the answer is boiling point increases with increase in pressure.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics

34. Which colour of light deviates the most while passing through a glass prism?
(a) Red (b) Green (c) Violet (d) Yellow
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option is red, because red scatters least in the sky and students confuse scattering with refraction. In a prism, deviation increases as wavelength decreases, and violet has the shortest wavelength, so the answer is violet.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

35. A fuse wire is made of a material having:
(a) high resistance and high melting point (b) low resistance and low melting point (c) high resistance and low melting point (d) low resistance and high melting point
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is high resistance with high melting point — a high melting point would stop the wire from melting during overload, defeating its purpose. A fuse must heat up quickly (high resistance) and melt quickly (low melting point) to break the circuit, so the answer is high resistance and low melting point.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

36. When a bus starts suddenly, passengers lean backward. This is an example of:
(a) inertia of motion (b) Newton's third law (c) inertia of direction (d) inertia of rest
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is inertia of motion, because the bus is moving — but the passengers' bodies were at rest and tend to remain at rest while the bus moves forward. This tendency of a body to resist change from rest is inertia of rest, so the answer is inertia of rest.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

37. The image formed by a plane mirror is:
(a) virtual, erect and same size (b) real, erect and same size (c) virtual, inverted and magnified (d) real, inverted and diminished
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is real, erect and same size — a plane mirror's image cannot be obtained on a screen, so it is virtual, not real. The image is virtual, erect, laterally inverted and of the same size as the object, so the answer is virtual, erect and same size.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

38. Which of the following is used to measure electric current in a circuit?
(a) Voltmeter connected in series (b) Ammeter connected in parallel (c) Ammeter connected in series (d) Galvanometer connected in parallel
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option is ammeter connected in parallel — an ammeter has very low resistance, and connecting it in parallel would short the circuit. Current must pass through the measuring device, so an ammeter is always connected in series, so the answer is ammeter connected in series.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

39. A body is thrown vertically upward. At the highest point:
(a) velocity and acceleration are both zero (b) velocity is zero but acceleration is g downward (c) velocity is maximum and acceleration is zero (d) velocity and acceleration are both maximum
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that both velocity and acceleration are zero — gravity never stops acting on the body. At the top, instantaneous velocity is zero, but acceleration due to gravity still acts downward at about 9.8 m/s², so the answer is velocity is zero but acceleration is g downward.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

40. The twinkling of stars is due to:
(a) reflection of starlight by clouds (b) dispersion of starlight by air (c) scattering of starlight by dust (d) atmospheric refraction of starlight
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is scattering by dust, because students link twinkling with the same cause as the blue sky. Twinkling occurs because starlight passes through layers of air of varying density, causing continuous refraction and apparent shifting of the star's position, so the answer is atmospheric refraction of starlight.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

41. Which of the following quantities remains constant for a body moving in uniform circular motion?
(a) Speed (b) Acceleration (c) Velocity (d) Displacement
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is velocity — in circular motion the direction of velocity changes continuously, so velocity is not constant even though its magnitude is. Only the speed (magnitude) stays constant, while acceleration points toward the centre, so the answer is speed.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

42. The device used to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy is:
(a) electric motor (b) dynamo (c) transformer (d) galvanometer
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is electric motor, but a motor does the opposite — it converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. A dynamo (generator) works on electromagnetic induction and converts mechanical rotation into electricity, so the answer is dynamo.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

43. A man pushes a wall and fails to move it. The work done by him on the wall is:
(a) positive (b) zero (c) negative (d) infinite
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is positive work, because the man feels tired — but tiredness comes from internal muscular energy, not from work on the wall. In physics, work = force × displacement, and since the wall does not move, displacement is zero, so the answer is zero.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

44. Which of the following correctly matches the instrument with what it measures?
(a) Barometer — humidity (b) Barometer — atmospheric pressure (c) Hygrometer — atmospheric pressure (d) Ammeter — potential difference
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is hygrometer for atmospheric pressure — a hygrometer measures humidity, not pressure. A barometer measures atmospheric pressure, while an ammeter measures current, so the answer is barometer — atmospheric pressure.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics

45. The phenomenon of mirage in deserts is caused by:
(a) total internal reflection of light (b) scattering of light (c) dispersion of light (d) interference of light
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is scattering, which explains blue sky but not mirages. In a mirage, hot air near the ground is less dense, so light from the sky bends gradually and undergoes total internal reflection, creating the illusion of water, so the answer is total internal reflection of light.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

46. If the distance between two point charges is doubled, the electrostatic force between them becomes:
(a) double (b) half (c) four times (d) one-fourth
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is half, which ignores the square in Coulomb's law. Since F is proportional to 1/r², doubling the distance makes the force 1/2² = 1/4 of the original, so the answer is one-fourth.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

47. Which mirror is used by dentists to examine teeth?
(a) Convex mirror (b) Concave mirror (c) Plane mirror (d) Parabolic mirror
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is plane mirror, since it gives a true-size image — but dentists need a magnified view. A concave mirror forms an erect, magnified, virtual image when the object (tooth) lies within its focal length, so the answer is concave mirror.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

48. The unit of power is:
(a) Joule (b) Newton (c) Watt (d) Pascal
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is joule, which is the unit of energy or work, not power. Power is the rate of doing work, measured in joules per second, which is called the watt, so the answer is watt.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

49. A boatman finds it difficult to push his boat forward by pushing the water with a pole. The physics principle involved is:
(a) Newton's first law (b) Newton's third law (c) Newton's second law (d) law of gravitation
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is Newton's second law, because force and acceleration are involved — but the key idea here is action and reaction. The pole pushes water backward (action) and water pushes the boat forward (reaction), which is Newton's third law, so the answer is Newton's third law.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

50. The frequency of a sound wave is 500 Hz and its speed in air is 340 m/s. Its wavelength is:
(a) 6.8 m (b) 1.47 m (c) 0.34 m (d) 0.68 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 1.47 m, which comes from dividing speed by frequency the wrong way round (340 ÷ 500 inverted). Wavelength = speed ÷ frequency = 340 ÷ 500 = 0.68 m, so the answer is 0.68 m.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science
