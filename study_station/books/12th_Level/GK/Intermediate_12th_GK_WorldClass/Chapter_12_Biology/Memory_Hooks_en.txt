# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Biology: Human Body

---

**Box 1 — Cranial Nerves (12 pairs)**
> **"Oh Oh Oh, To Touch And Feel Very Good Velvet, AH!"**
> Olfactory, Optic, Oculomotor, Trochlear, Trigeminal, Abducens, Facial, Vestibulocochlear, Glossopharyngeal, Vagus, Accessory, Hypoglossal.
> *Trick: The "AH!" at the end reminds you the last two are Accessory and Hypoglossal.*

---

**Box 2 — Bones of the Wrist (Carpals, 8)**
> **"She Looks Too Pretty, Try To Catch Her"**
> Scaphoid, Lunate, Triquetrum, Pisiform, Trapezium, Trapezoid, Capitate, Hamate.
> *Exam trap: Trapezium comes before Trapezoid — "Try To" in that order.*

---

**Box 3 — Fat-Soluble Vitamins**
> **"A DEK of cards is FAT"**
> Vitamins **A, D, E, K** are fat-soluble; the rest (B-complex, C) are water-soluble.
> *Trap: Vitamin C is NOT fat-soluble — examiners love swapping it in.*

---

**Box 4 — Vitamin Deficiency Quick Chain**
> **"A-Night, B1-Beri, C-Scurvy, D-Rickets, K-Klotting fails"**
> A → Night blindness | B1 → Beri-beri | C → Scurvy | D → Rickets | K → delayed blood clotting.

---

**Box 5 — Blood Cells by Size & Count**
> **"RBCs Run Rapidly, WBCs Walk, Platelets are Plenty-tiny"**
> RBC count ~5 million/mm³ (most), WBC ~7,000–8,000 (fewer), Platelets ~2.5 lakh (tiny fragments).
> *Largest WBC = Monocyte; largest cell in body = ovum; longest cell = neuron.*

---

**Box 6 — Heart Chambers & Valves**
> **"LAB RAT"** — **L**eft **A**trium has **B**icuspid (mitral) valve; **R**ight **A**trium has **T**ricuspid valve.
> *Trap: Bicuspid = 2 flaps = Left side. "Bi-Left" both have an L-ish logic — 2 letters apart in the alphabet trick: B(icuspid) → L(eft).*

---

**Box 7 — Universal Blood Groups**
> **"O Gives, AB Takes"**
> O-negative = universal **donor**; AB-positive = universal **recipient**.
> *Memory: "O" looks like an open hand giving; "AB" = "A Beggar" takes from all.*

---

**Box 8 — Digestive Enzyme Sources**
> **"Grandma Salivates, Stomach Peps, Pancreas Does All"**
> Salivary amylase (ptyalin) → starch in mouth; Pepsin → protein in stomach; Pancreatic juice → digests carbs + proteins + fats (the "complete" juice).
> *Bile has NO enzyme — it only emulsifies fats. Classic trap!*

---

**Box 9 — Hormone Glands: Master & Emergency**
> **"Pituitary is the Principal, Adrenal is the Ambulance"**
> Pituitary = master gland (controls others); Adrenal medulla = emergency gland (adrenaline — fight or flight).
> *Largest endocrine gland = Thyroid; mixed gland (endo+exo) = Pancreas.*

---

**Box 10 — Bone Count Story**
> **"A baby is born with ~300 bones, but adults settle at 206 — bones marry and merge!"**
> Smallest bone = **Stapes** (ear — "STirrup is STiny"); Longest = **Femur** ("FEmur = FEet-length").

---

**Box 11 — Kidney Facts**
> **"Nephrons are a Million Minions per kidney"**
> ~1 million nephrons per kidney; functional unit = nephron. Dialysis = artificial kidney.
> *Urea filtered here; urine formed by filtration + reabsorption.*

---

**Box 12 — Respiratory Numbers**
> **"16-20 breaths, 500 mL each"**
> Normal breathing rate = 16–20/min; tidal volume ≈ 500 mL.
> *Trap: Gas exchange happens in ALVEOLI, not bronchi.*

---

**Box 13 — DNA/RNA Contrast**
> **"DNA is Double with Deoxyribose; RNA is a single Rebel with Uracil"**
> DNA: double helix, thymine; RNA: single strand, **uracil replaces thymine**.
> *Watson & Crick proposed the double helix model (1953).*

---

**Box 14 — Normal Body Values Chain**
> **"37-7.4-120/80-72"**
> Body temp 37°C | Blood pH 7.4 | BP 120/80 mmHg | Heartbeat 72/min.
> *Chant it like a phone number — four values, one breath.*

---

**Box 15 — Insulin & Sugar**
> **"Insulin = In-to-the-cell"**
> Insulin pushes glucose INTO cells → lowers blood sugar. Deficiency → Diabetes mellitus. Secreted by **β-cells of Islets of Langerhans** (pancreas).
> *Glucagon does the opposite (α-cells).*

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

1. **The "one blank in four" rule:** If a match-the-following asks you to pair four hormones with four glands and you are sure of only two, and the options don't eliminate cleanly — **skip immediately**. Guessing between two 50-50 options with negative marking costs more than the question earns.

2. **Numeric-value traps:** If a question asks an exact figure (e.g., "How many ml is the tidal volume?" or "Exact WBC count range") and you only remember it vaguely ("somewhere in thousands"), **skip**. Examiners place the correct value next to a look-alike (7,000 vs 70,000). Vague memory + precise options = trap.

3. **"Which is NOT correct" biology statements:** If even one statement involves a fact you have never seen (a rare enzyme, an unfamiliar hormone), don't burn 90 seconds re-reading. Mark it, move on, and return only if time remains — these questions are designed to eat your time, not test your knowledge.
