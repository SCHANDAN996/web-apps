51. Which enzyme present in saliva begins the digestion of starch?
(a) Ptyalin (b) Pepsin (c) Lipase (d) Trypsin
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pepsin acts in the stomach on proteins, not in the mouth, so it is the tempting wrong choice. Salivary amylase, also called ptyalin, starts breaking starch into maltose in the mouth, so the answer is ptyalin.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science

52. The functional unit of the kidney is the —
(a) neuron (b) alveolus (c) nephron (d) villus
Answer: (c)
Solution: Alveolus belongs to the lungs and villus to the small intestine; both are common confusions. Urine formation and filtration occur in the nephron, so the answer is nephron.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

53. Which blood cells are primarily responsible for fighting infections?
(a) White blood cells (b) Red blood cells (c) Platelets (d) Plasma cells
Answer: (a)
Solution: Platelets help in clotting and red blood cells carry oxygen, so neither fights germs. White blood cells (leukocytes) defend the body against infection, so the answer is white blood cells.

54. The normal resting heart rate of a healthy adult is approximately —
(a) 40–50 beats per minute (b) 150–180 beats per minute (c) 120–140 beats per minute (d) 60–100 beats per minute
Answer: (d)
Solution: 40–50 is typical only of trained athletes at rest, which misleads many. A normal adult resting heart rate ranges from 60 to 100 beats per minute, so the answer is 60–100 beats per minute.

55. Which vitamin is synthesized in human skin on exposure to sunlight?
(a) Vitamin D (b) Vitamin C (c) Vitamin A (d) Vitamin K
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vitamin K is made by gut bacteria, which is a frequent confusion. Ultraviolet rays convert a cholesterol derivative in the skin into vitamin D, so the answer is vitamin D.

56. The largest gland in the human body is the —
(a) liver (b) thyroid (c) pancreas (d) pituitary
Answer: (a)
Solution: The pituitary is often wrongly picked because it is called the "master gland," but it is tiny. The liver is the largest gland and produces bile, so the answer is liver.

57. Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination of the body?
(a) Cerebellum (b) Cerebrum (c) Medulla oblongata (d) Hypothalamus
Answer: (a)
Solution: The medulla controls involuntary actions like heartbeat, which tempts many candidates. The cerebellum maintains posture, balance and coordination, so the answer is cerebellum.

58. Insulin is secreted by which cells of the pancreas?
(a) Alpha cells (b) Beta cells of islets of Langerhans (c) Acinar cells (d) Delta cells
Answer: (b)
Solution: Alpha cells secrete glucagon, the opposite hormone, which is the classic trap. Insulin comes from the beta cells of the islets of Langerhans, so the answer is beta cells of islets of Langerhans.

59. The exchange of gases in the lungs takes place in the —
(a) bronchi (b) alveoli (c) bronchioles (d) trachea
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bronchi and trachea are only air passages, not exchange surfaces. The thin-walled alveoli surrounded by capillaries allow oxygen and carbon dioxide exchange, so the answer is alveoli.

60. Which of the following statements about human blood groups is correct?
(a) Group O is the universal recipient (b) Group AB is the universal donor (c) Group A can donate to group B (d) Group O is the universal donor
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is reversing donor and recipient: AB is the universal recipient, not donor. Group O lacks A and B antigens, making it the universal donor, so the answer is group O is the universal donor.

61. The average volume of blood in an adult human body is about —
(a) 2–3 litres (b) 12–14 litres (c) 8–10 litres (d) 4–6 litres
Answer: (d)
Solution: 8–10 litres is an exaggerated figure often quoted loosely. An average adult has roughly 4 to 6 litres of blood (about 7–8% of body weight), so the answer is 4–6 litres.

62. Which hormone is called the "fight or flight" hormone?
(a) Thyroxine (b) Insulin (c) Adrenaline (d) Growth hormone
Answer: (c)
Solution: Thyroxine regulates metabolism and is a common wrong pick. Adrenaline (epinephrine) from the adrenal medulla prepares the body for emergencies, so the answer is adrenaline.

63. The human skeleton of an adult consists of how many bones?
(a) 300 (b) 216 (c) 206 (d) 186
Answer: (c)

64. Which part of the eye controls the amount of light entering it?
(a) Cornea (b) Iris (c) Retina (d) Lens

66. Which of the following statements about arteries is correct?

Solution: The trap is "all arteries carry oxygenated blood" — the pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood. By definition arteries carry blood away from the heart, so the answer is arteries carry blood away from the heart.

69. Which blood component is responsible for clotting?
(a) Plasma (b) Platelets (c) Red blood cells (d) White blood cells

70. The strongest muscle in the human body relative to its function of chewing is the —
(a) masseter (b) biceps (c) gluteus maximus (d) deltoid
Answer: (a)
Solution: The gluteus maximus is the largest muscle, which tempts those who confuse "largest" with "strongest for its task." The masseter, the jaw muscle used in chewing, is famed for its biting force, so the answer is masseter.

71. Which of the following correctly matches the disease with its cause?
(a) Diabetes mellitus — deficiency of thyroxine (b) Anaemia — excess of iron (c) Goitre — deficiency of iodine (d) Kwashiorkor — excess of protein
Answer: (c)
Solution: Diabetes is caused by insulin deficiency, not thyroxine, and kwashiorkor is protein deficiency, not excess — both are reversed traps. Goitre results from iodine deficiency affecting the thyroid, so the answer is goitre — deficiency of iodine.

72. The average human body temperature in Celsius is —
(a) 35°C (b) 40°C (c) 39°C (d) 37°C

(a) haemoglobin (b) chlorophyll (c) melanin (d) keratin

75. Which of the following statements about the human heart is correct?
(a) The heart has three chambers (b) The left ventricle pumps blood to the lungs (c) The right ventricle pumps blood to the lungs (d) The heart has five chambers
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is swapping sides: the left ventricle pumps oxygenated blood to the whole body, not the lungs. The human heart has four chambers, and the right ventricle sends deoxygenated blood to the lungs, so the answer is the right ventricle pumps blood to the lungs.
