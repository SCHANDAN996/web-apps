101. Which enzyme present in saliva begins the digestion of starch?
(a) Amylase (ptyalin) (b) Pepsin (c) Lipase (d) Trypsin
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pepsin is tempting but it acts on proteins in the stomach, not on starch. Salivary amylase (ptyalin) in the mouth converts starch into maltose, so the answer is Amylase (ptyalin).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

102. The normal resting heart rate of a healthy adult human is about:
(a) 36 beats/minute (b) 120 beats/minute (c) 72 beats/minute (d) 100 beats/minute
Answer: (c)
Solution: 72 beats/minute is the average resting heart rate; 36 is far too low and 100–120 would indicate tachycardia, so the answer is 72 beats/minute.
