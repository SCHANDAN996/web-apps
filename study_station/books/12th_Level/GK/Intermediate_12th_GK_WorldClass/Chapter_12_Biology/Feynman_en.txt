# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Biology: Human Body

## The Most Confusing Concept: Double Circulation of Blood

Ask any student: "Does the heart contain pure blood or impure blood?" Most will say "pure on the left, impure on the right" — and then get confused the moment a question asks about the **pulmonary artery** (which carries *impure* blood!) or the **pulmonary vein** (which carries *pure* blood!). Let's fix this forever.

### Explain it like you're 12

Imagine your body is a housing colony, and blood is a **delivery truck** carrying oxygen cylinders.

- The truck loads fresh oxygen cylinders at the **lungs** (the warehouse).
- It delivers them to every house (body cells) and picks up empty cylinders (carbon dioxide).
- Then it must go **back to the warehouse** to reload.

Now here's the problem: one heart, two trips. So nature split the heart into **two pumps side by side**:

1. **Right side of the heart** = the "return trip" pump. It receives tired, oxygen-poor blood from the body and pushes it a short distance — to the lungs.
2. **Left side of the heart** = the "delivery trip" pump. It receives fresh, oxygen-rich blood from the lungs and pushes it with great force to the whole body.

That's why the **left ventricle has the thickest wall** — it has to push blood to your toes, not just next door!

### The trap that fools everyone

We learn: *"Arteries carry pure blood, veins carry impure blood."* **Wrong as a rule!**

The real rule is:
- **Artery** = any vessel carrying blood **AWAY from the heart**.
- **Vein** = any vessel carrying blood **TOWARDS the heart**.

So:
- **Pulmonary artery** → carries *impure* blood (heart → lungs). It's an artery because it goes *away* from the heart.
- **Pulmonary vein** → carries *pure* blood (lungs → heart). It's a vein because it comes *towards* the heart.

These two are the **only exceptions** to the "arteries = pure" shortcut — and examiners love them.

### The Unforgettable Trick

**"A = Away, V = arive (arrive)."**
- **A**rtery = blood goes **A**way from the heart.
- **V**ein = blood arri**V**es at the heart.

And for the exceptions: **"Pulmonary is the rebel pair"** — the pulmonary artery is impure, the pulmonary vein is pure. Just remember: *whatever goes to the lungs is dirty, whatever comes back is clean.*

Full loop in one line:
**Body → Right atrium → Right ventricle → Lungs → Left atrium → Left ventricle → Body.**
(Think: **"B-R-R-L-L-B"** — BRR, it's cold, Let's go Back!)
