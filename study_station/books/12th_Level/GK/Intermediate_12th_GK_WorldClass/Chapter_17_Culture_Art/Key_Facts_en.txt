# Key Facts & Figures — Indian Culture & Art

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classical dances of India (8) | Bharatanatyam (TN), Kathak (UP), Kathakali (Kerala), Mohiniyattam (Kerala), Kuchipudi (AP), Odissi (Odisha), Manipuri (Manipur), Sattriya (Assam) | Yakshagana, Chhau, Bhangra are folk dances, not classical | Near-correct option |
| Sattriya | Recognised as classical dance in 2000 (by Sangeet Natak Akademi) | Often omitted; students count only 7 classical dances | Omission trap |
| Bharatanatyam | Tamil Nadu; oldest classical dance form; linked to Devadasi tradition | Confused with Kuchipudi (Andhra Pradesh) | Similar-name confusion |
| Kathak | Uttar Pradesh; influenced by Mughal courts; Jaipur, Lucknow, Banaras gharanas | Gharanas swapped with Hindustani music gharanas | Cross-topic mixing |
| Kathakali | Kerala; elaborate face makeup, costumes; stories from epics | Confused with Mohiniyattam (also Kerala, but graceful solo female dance) | Same-state confusion |
| Kuchipudi | Andhra Pradesh; named after Kuchipudi village | Wrongly assigned to Tamil Nadu or Karnataka | State mismatch |
| Odissi | Odisha; based on temple sculpture poses (tribhangi) | Confused with Manipuri | Similar-name confusion |
| Manipuri | Manipur; themes of Radha-Krishna (Raslila) | Confused with Sattriya (Assam, Vaishnavite monasteries) | Theme confusion |
| Hindustani vs Carnatic music | Hindustani – North India, Persian influence; Carnatic – South India, more devotional/kriti-based | Swapping regions or features | Reversal trap |
| Tansen | Court musician of Akbar; associated with Dhrupad; ragas like Miyan ki Malhar, Miyan ki Todi attributed to him | Wrongly linked to Shah Jahan or Aurangzeb | Ruler mismatch |
| Amir Khusrau | Credited with inventing sitar and tabla (traditional attribution); Sufi poet, disciple of Nizamuddin Auliya | Instruments' invention is traditional attribution, not proven fact | Overstatement trap |
| Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri | Trinity of Carnatic music | Swapped with Hindustani musicians | Category swap |
| Bismillah Khan | Shehnai maestro; Bharat Ratna (2001) | Instrument confused with flute (Hariprasad Chaurasia) | Instrument swap |
| Ravi Shankar | Sitar; popularised Indian music globally | Confused with Ali Akbar Khan (sarod) | Instrument swap |
| M.S. Subbulakshmi | Carnatic vocalist; first musician to receive Bharat Ratna (1998) | "First musician Bharat Ratna" wrongly given to Ravi Shankar | First-claim trap |
| UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage (India) | Yoga (2016), Kumbh Mela (2017), Durga Puja of Kolkata (2021), Garba of Gujarat (2023), Ramlila, Vedic chanting, Kutiyattam, etc. | Taj Mahal etc. are World Heritage Sites (tangible), not intangible | Category confusion |
| Ajanta Caves | Maharashtra; Buddhist; famous for paintings; c. 2nd century BCE onwards | Confused with Ellora (Hindu, Buddhist, Jain caves; Kailasa temple) | Site swap |
| Ellora – Kailasa temple | Rock-cut monolithic temple, built under Rashtrakuta king Krishna I | Attributed to Pallavas or Cholas | Dynasty mismatch |
| Elephanta Caves | Near Mumbai; famous Trimurti (Maheshmurti) sculpture of Shiva | Confused with Ajanta/Ellora location | Location trap |
| Sanchi Stupa | Madhya Pradesh; built by Ashoka, enlarged later; gateways (toranas) added under Satavahanas | Toranas wrongly attributed to Ashoka himself | Period confusion |
| Konark Sun Temple | Odisha; built by Narasimhadeva I (Eastern Ganga dynasty); chariot of Surya | Attributed to Cholas or Pallavas | Dynasty mismatch |
| Khajuraho Temples | Madhya Pradesh; Chandela dynasty; Nagara style | Attributed to Chalukyas | Dynasty mismatch |
| Brihadeeswara Temple | Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu; built by Rajaraja Chola I; Dravidian style | Confused with Gangaikonda Cholapuram temple (built by Rajendra I) | Similar-name confusion |
| Nagara, Dravida, Vesara | Three temple architecture styles: North, South, Deccan (mixed) | Vesara wrongly called purely South Indian | Style confusion |
| Shore Temple, Mahabalipuram | Pallava dynasty (Narasimhavarman II); structural stone temple | Attributed to Cholas | Dynasty mismatch |
| Hampi | Karnataka; capital of Vijayanagara Empire; Virupaksha temple | Wrongly placed in Tamil Nadu or Andhra Pradesh | State mismatch |
| Fatehpur Sikri | Built by Akbar; Buland Darwaza commemorates Gujarat victory | Buland Darwaza wrongly linked to victory over Bengal | Event mismatch |
| Taj Mahal | Built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal; architect traditionally Ustad Ahmad Lahauri | Wrongly attributed to Akbar or Aurangzeb | Ruler mismatch |
| Red Fort | Built by Shah Jahan (Delhi); PM addresses nation from here on 15 August | Confused with Agra Fort (Akbar) | Similar-name confusion |
| Qutub Minar | Started by Qutb-ud-din Aibak, completed by Iltutmish | "Built entirely by Aibak" is wrong | Completion trap |
| Charminar | Hyderabad; built by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah (1591) | Attributed to Mughals | Dynasty mismatch |
| Gateway of India | Mumbai; built to commemorate visit of King George V (1911) | Confused with India Gate (New Delhi, war memorial) | Similar-name confusion |
| India Gate | New Delhi; designed by Edwin Lutyens; memorial to soldiers of WWI | Designer confused with Herbert Baker (Rashtrapati Bhavan, with Lutyens) | Architect swap |
| Classical languages of India | Tamil (2004), Sanskrit (2005), Kannada (2008), Telugu (2008), Malayalam (2013), Odia (2014); Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese, Bengali added in 2024 | Tamil was the first; order of declaration often swapped | Order trap |
| Natyashastra | Ancient treatise on performing arts, attributed to Bharata Muni | Attributed to Kalidasa or Panini | Author mismatch |
| Kalidasa | Wrote Abhijnanashakuntalam, Meghaduta, Raghuvamsha; court of Chandragupta II (traditionally) | Works swapped with Banabhatta (Harshacharita, Kadambari) | Author-work swap |
| Sangam literature | Ancient Tamil literature; three Sangams (legendary) | Wrongly called Sanskrit literature | Language trap |
| Mahabharata | Attributed to Ved Vyasa; longest epic; Bhagavad Gita is part of it | Gita wrongly called a separate text | Composition trap |
| Ramayana | Attributed to Valmiki; Kamban wrote Tamil Ramavataram | Kamban's version wrongly called a translation of Tulsidas's Ramcharitmanas | Version confusion |
| Ramcharitmanas | Tulsidas; Awadhi language; Bhakti period | Wrongly said to be in Sanskrit or Braj | Language trap |
| Bhakti movement – Shankaracharya | Advaita (non-dualism); 8th century | Confused with Ramanuja (Vishishtadvaita) and Madhva (Dvaita) | Philosophy swap |
| Kabir | Nirguna Bhakti; disciple of Ramananda (traditionally); dohas in Bijak | Called Saguna Bhakti saint | Category swap |
| Guru Nanak | Founder of Sikhism; Guru Granth Sahib compiled by Guru Arjan (5th Guru) | Compilation attributed to Guru Gobind Singh (he added Guru Tegh Bahadur's hymns and declared it eternal Guru) | Attribution trap |
| Sufi orders in India | Chishti (Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti, Ajmer) and Suhrawardi | Saints swapped between orders | Order swap |
| Ajmer Sharif | Dargah of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti; Urs festival | Confused with Nizamuddin Auliya (Delhi) | Saint-place swap |
| Madhubani painting | Bihar (Mithila region); traditionally by women; GI tag | Wrongly assigned to Odisha or Rajasthan | State mismatch |
| Warli painting | Maharashtra; tribal art; geometric figures | Wrongly assigned to Gujarat or MP | State mismatch |
| Pattachitra | Odisha; cloth-based scroll painting, Jagannath themes | Confused with Kalamkari (Andhra Pradesh) | Similar-art confusion |
| Phad painting | Rajasthan; scroll paintings of folk deities (Pabuji, Devnarayan) | Confused with Pichwai (Nathdwara, Krishna themes) | Similar-art confusion |
| Miniature paintings | Mughal, Rajasthani, Pahari (Kangra, Basohli), Deccani schools | Kangra wrongly placed under Rajasthani school | School confusion |
| Raja Ravi Varma | Famous for oleographs of Hindu deities; Kerala | Called a Mughal miniature painter | Category trap |
| Nataraja | Shiva as cosmic dancer; Chola bronze icon | Wrongly linked to Vishnu | Deity swap |
| Lion Capital of Ashoka | Sarnath; adopted as National Emblem; "Satyameva Jayate" from Mundaka Upanishad | Motto wrongly sourced from Rigveda | Source trap |
| Classical music gharanas | Gwalior (oldest), Kirana, Jaipur-Atrauli, Agra, Patiala, Mewati | Gwalior called newest or Patiala called oldest | Order trap |
| Musical instruments – string | Sitar, sarod, veena, santoor, tanpura | Santoor wrongly called percussion (it is a struck string instrument) | Classification trap |
| Instruments – wind | Flute (bansuri), shehnai, nadaswaram | Nadaswaram wrongly called North Indian (it is South Indian) | Region trap |
| Instruments – percussion | Tabla, mridangam, pakhawaj, ghatam | Mridangam (Carnatic) swapped with tabla (Hindustani) | Tradition swap |
| Harappan art | Dancing Girl (bronze, lost-wax method), Priest-King (steatite), seals with unicorn motif | Dancing Girl wrongly said to be terracotta | Material trap |
| Mauryan art | Polished pillars (Ashokan), Didarganj Yakshi, Sarnath capital | Didarganj Yakshi wrongly called Gupta art | Period trap |
| Gandhara art | Greco-Buddhist style; Buddha in Greek-like features; NW region | Confused with Mathura art (indigenous, red sandstone) | School confusion |
| Mathura art | Red sandstone; Buddha and Jain images; Kushan period | Material confused with Gandhara (grey schist) | Material trap |
| Gupta period | "Golden Age" of art; Ajanta paintings, Sarnath Buddha, Deogarh Dashavatara temple | Ajanta paintings span earlier periods too; examiners test over-attribution to Gupta | Overstatement trap |
| Folk theatres | Nautanki (UP), Tamasha (Maharashtra), Jatra (Bengal), Bhavai (Gujarat), Yakshagana (Karnataka) | Yakshagana wrongly assigned to Kerala | State mismatch |
| Puppetry forms | Kathputli (Rajasthan), Bommalattam (TN), Togalu Gombeyaata (Karnataka), Putul Nach (Bengal) | Kathputli wrongly assigned to Gujarat | State mismatch |
| Festivals – harvest | Baisakhi (Punjab), Pongal (TN), Bihu (Assam), Onam (Kerala), Makar Sankranti | Onam wrongly called harvest festival of Tamil Nadu | State mismatch |
| Hornbill Festival | Nagaland; "Festival of Festivals" | Wrongly assigned to Manipur or Mizoram | State mismatch |
| Hemis Festival | Ladakh; monastery festival | Wrongly placed in Sikkim | Region trap |
| National School of Drama | New Delhi; established 1959 | Year confused with Sangeet Natak Akademi (1953) | Institution-year swap |
| Sangeet Natak Akademi | National academy of music, dance, drama; 1953 | Confused with Lalit Kala Akademi (fine arts, 1954) and Sahitya Akademi (literature, 1954) | Akademi swap |
| Indus Valley script | Undeciphered; written right to left (boustrophedon in some) | Claimed to be deciphered — false | False-claim trap |
| Dholavira | Harappan site in Gujarat; signboard with large inscription; UNESCO World Heritage Site (2021) | Wrongly placed in Haryana or Pakistan | Location trap |
| First Buddhist Council | Rajgriha, under Ajatashatru | Venue confused with Vaishali (Second Council) | Council-venue swap |
| Kanishka | Fourth Buddhist Council (Kashmir); patron of Gandhara art | Council wrongly placed at Pataliputra | Venue trap |
