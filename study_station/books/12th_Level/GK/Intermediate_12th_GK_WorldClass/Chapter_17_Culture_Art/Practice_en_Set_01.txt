1. The famous Sun Temple at Konark, built in the shape of a chariot, is located in which state?
(a) Odisha (b) Tamil Nadu (c) Karnataka (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Konark Sun Temple, built by Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty in the 13th century, is in Odisha. Tamil Nadu is a trap because it has many famous temples, but Konark is an Odisha landmark, so the answer is Odisha.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts (An Introduction to Indian Art)

2. Bharatanatyam, one of the oldest classical dance forms of India, originated in which state?
(a) Kerala (b) Andhra Pradesh (c) Karnataka (d) Tamil Nadu
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bharatanatyam originated in the temples of Tamil Nadu and was earlier known as Sadir. Kerala is associated with Kathakali and Mohiniyattam, which is a common confusion, so the answer is Tamil Nadu.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

3. The Ajanta Caves, famous for their paintings, are located in which state?
(a) Madhya Pradesh (b) Maharashtra (c) Gujarat (d) Rajasthan
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Ajanta Caves are located in the Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar) district of Maharashtra and date mainly from the 2nd century BCE to the 6th century CE. Madhya Pradesh has Bhimbetka rock shelters, which is a similar-name trap, so the answer is Maharashtra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

4. Which classical dance form is associated with the state of Uttar Pradesh?
(a) Kuchipudi (b) Odissi (c) Kathak (d) Manipuri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kathak developed in northern India, especially Uttar Pradesh, with famous gharanas of Lucknow and Jaipur. Odissi belongs to Odisha and Kuchipudi to Andhra Pradesh, so the answer is Kathak.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

5. The famous rock-cut Kailasa temple at Ellora was built during the rule of which dynasty?
(a) Rashtrakuta (b) Pallava (c) Chola (d) Chalukya of Badami
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Kailasa temple at Ellora (Cave 16) was built under the Rashtrakuta king Krishna I in the 8th century. The Pallavas built rock-cut temples at Mamallapuram, which is the tempting confusion, so the answer is Rashtrakuta.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

6. Madhubani painting, known for its intricate folk designs, belongs to which state?
(a) Bihar (b) West Bengal (c) Odisha (d) Jharkhand
Answer: (a)
Solution: Madhubani (Mithila) painting comes from the Mithila region of Bihar and traditionally was done by women on walls and floors. Odisha's Pattachitra is a similar folk-art trap, so the answer is Bihar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

7. The Sattriya dance form was introduced by the saint-scholar Sankaradeva in which state?
(a) Assam (b) Manipur (c) Tripura (d) West Bengal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sattriya was introduced in the 15th–16th century by the Vaishnavite saint Sankaradeva in Assam as part of monastery (sattra) worship. Manipur has its own Manipuri dance, which is the confusion, so the answer is Assam.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

8. The Brihadeeswarar Temple at Thanjavur was built by which ruler?
(a) Krishnadevaraya (b) Rajendra Chola I (c) Rajaraja Chola I (d) Narasimhavarman I
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Brihadeeswarar (Rajarajeswara) Temple at Thanjavur was completed around 1010 CE by Rajaraja Chola I. His son Rajendra Chola I built a similar temple at Gangaikonda Cholapuram, which is the classic similar-name trap, so the answer is Rajaraja Chola I.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Our Pasts II)

9. Which of the following is a stringed musical instrument?
(a) Tabla (b) Mridangam (c) Flute (d) Sitar
Answer: (d)
Solution: The sitar is a plucked stringed instrument. Tabla and mridangam are percussion instruments and the flute is a wind instrument, so the answer is Sitar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

10. The Khajuraho group of temples, famous for their sculptures, were mainly built by which dynasty?
(a) Chandela (b) Paramara (c) Solanki (d) Hoysala
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Khajuraho temples in Madhya Pradesh were built mainly by the Chandela dynasty between the 10th and 11th centuries. The Hoysalas built Belur and Halebidu temples, which is the confusion, so the answer is Chandela.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

11. Kuchipudi, a classical dance-drama tradition, originated in which state?
(a) Karnataka (b) Andhra Pradesh (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Kerala
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kuchipudi takes its name from the village of Kuchipudi in Andhra Pradesh, where it developed as a dance-drama tradition. It is often confused with Bharatanatyam of Tamil Nadu, so the answer is Andhra Pradesh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

12. The famous painting style of 'Pattachitra' is associated with which state?
(a) Bihar (b) Odisha (c) Rajasthan (d) Gujarat
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pattachitra is a traditional cloth-based scroll painting of Odisha, centred around Puri and the Jagannath cult. Bihar's Madhubani is the similar folk-art trap, so the answer is Odisha.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

13. The 'Natya Shastra', the foundational Sanskrit text on performing arts, is attributed to which sage?
(a) Panini (b) Patanjali (c) Bharata Muni (d) Valmiki
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Natya Shastra, the ancient treatise on drama, dance and music, is attributed to Bharata Muni. Panini wrote on grammar (Ashtadhyayi) and Patanjali on yoga and grammar, which are the traps, so the answer is Bharata Muni.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

14. The Elephanta Caves, famous for the Trimurti sculpture of Shiva, are located near which city?
(a) Chennai (b) Kolkata (c) Mumbai (d) Kochi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Elephanta Caves are on Elephanta Island in Mumbai Harbour, Maharashtra, and the famous Trimurti Sadashiva sculpture is their highlight. They are often confused with Ellora, which is also in Maharashtra but near Aurangabad, so the answer is Mumbai.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

15. Which classical music tradition is predominantly practised in South India?
(a) Hindustani (b) Carnatic (c) Qawwali (d) Thumri
Answer: (b)
Solution: Carnatic music is the classical tradition of South India, while Hindustani music is the northern tradition. Qawwali and Thumri are semi-classical/devotional forms of the north, so the answer is Carnatic.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

16. The Shore Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is located at which place?
(a) Madurai (b) Kanchipuram (c) Mamallapuram (d) Rameswaram
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Shore Temple was built by the Pallavas at Mamallapuram (Mahabalipuram) in Tamil Nadu in the 8th century. Kanchipuram is famous for the Kailasanathar temple, which is the confusion, so the answer is Mamallapuram.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

17. Which of the following folk dances belongs to Punjab?
(a) Garba (b) Lavani (c) Bihu (d) Bhangra
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bhangra is the energetic folk dance of Punjab, traditionally linked to the harvest festival of Baisakhi. Garba belongs to Gujarat, Bihu to Assam and Lavani to Maharashtra, so the answer is Bhangra.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (contemporary cultural regions)

18. The famous Meenakshi Temple is located in which city?
(a) Thanjavur (b) Madurai (c) Tiruchirappalli (d) Kanchipuram
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Meenakshi-Sundareswarar Temple is located in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, and is a masterpiece of Dravidian architecture with towering gopurams. Thanjavur is famous for the Brihadeeswarar Temple, which is the trap, so the answer is Madurai.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

19. 'Kathakali', the classical dance-drama known for elaborate costumes and face makeup, belongs to which state?
(a) Tamil Nadu (b) Kerala (c) Karnataka (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kathakali is the classical dance-drama of Kerala, noted for its elaborate makeup, costumes and gestures. Yakshagana of Karnataka is a similar dance-drama form and a common confusion, so the answer is Kerala.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

20. The stupa at Sanchi, one of the oldest stone structures in India, was originally commissioned by which ruler?
(a) Ashoka (b) Chandragupta Maurya (c) Harshavardhana (d) Kanishka
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Great Stupa at Sanchi in Madhya Pradesh was originally commissioned by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE and later enlarged. Kanishka is associated with Gandhara art and the Kanishka stupa at Peshawar, which is the trap, so the answer is Ashoka.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

21. Consider the following statements:
1. The Gandhara school of art shows strong Greco-Roman influence.
2. The Mathura school of art primarily used red sandstone.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Neither 1 nor 2 (d) Both 1 and 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to assume only one school had foreign influence; in fact Gandhara art blended Indian themes with Greco-Roman styles, while Mathura art used the local spotted red sandstone. Both statements are factually correct, so the answer is Both 1 and 2.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

22. Consider the following statements about the Ellora Caves:
1. They contain Buddhist, Hindu and Jain rock-cut monuments.
2. They were excavated entirely during the Mauryan period.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Neither 1 nor 2 (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) 1 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 2 is the chronological trap: Ellora's caves were excavated roughly between the 6th and 10th centuries CE, long after the Mauryan period. Statement 1 is correct as Ellora has Buddhist, Hindu and Jain caves, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

23. Match the following classical dances with their states:
1. Mohiniyattam — Kerala
2. Odissi — Odisha
3. Manipuri — Manipur
Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: All three pairs are correctly matched: Mohiniyattam is from Kerala, Odissi from Odisha and Manipuri from Manipur. The trap is to doubt one pair because the names sound similar to other dances, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

24. Consider the following statements about the Qutub Minar:
1. Its construction was begun by Qutb-ud-din Aibak.
2. It was completed by Alauddin Khalji.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Neither 1 nor 2 (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) 1 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: the Qutub Minar was completed by Iltutmish, not Alauddin Khalji, though Alauddin later added the Alai Darwaza to the complex. Statement 1 is correct as Aibak began its construction around 1199, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Our Pasts II)

25. Consider the following statements about Indian classical music:
1. Tansen was a court musician of Akbar.
2. Purandardasa is regarded as the father of Carnatic music.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both statements are correct: Tansen was one of the nine gems (navaratnas) of Akbar's court, and Purandardasa of Karnataka is honoured as the father (pitamaha) of Carnatic music. The trap is to link Purandardasa with Hindustani music, so the answer is Both 1 and 2.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts
