Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Who is the Supreme Commander of the Indian Armed Forces?
(a) The President of India (b) The Prime Minister of India (c) The Chief of Defence Staff (d) The Defence Minister
Answer: (a)
Solution: Article 53(2) of the Constitution declares the President of India as the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces. The Prime Minister and Defence Minister exercise executive control, but the constitutional title belongs to the President, so the answer is the President of India.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

2. Which is the oldest paramilitary force of India?
(a) Border Security Force (b) Assam Rifles (c) Central Reserve Police Force (d) Indo-Tibetan Border Police
Answer: (b)
Solution: The CRPF (1939) and BSF (1965) are often picked, but Assam Rifles was raised in 1835 as the Cachar Levy, making it the oldest paramilitary force of India, so the answer is Assam Rifles.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

3. The headquarters of the Indian Army's Southern Command is located at:
(a) Kolkata (b) Lucknow (c) Pune (d) Udhampur
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lucknow is the Central Command HQ and Udhampur the Northern Command HQ, which confuses many candidates. The Southern Command of the Indian Army is headquartered at Pune, so the answer is Pune.
Source: Indian Army official website

4. INS Vikrant, commissioned in 2022, is India's first:
(a) Nuclear-powered submarine (b) Indigenously built aircraft carrier (c) Stealth frigate (d) Anti-submarine warfare corvette
Answer: (b)
Solution: INS Arihant is the nuclear-powered submarine, a common mix-up. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), built at Cochin Shipyard, is India's first indigenously designed and built aircraft carrier, so the answer is indigenously built aircraft carrier.
Source: Indian Navy official website

5. The Border Security Force (BSF) was raised in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1962 (c) 1971 (d) 1965
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1962 is tempting because of the India-China war, but the BSF was raised on 1 December 1965 after the India-Pakistan war exposed gaps in border guarding, so the answer is 1965.
Source: BSF official website

6. Which missile is a surface-to-surface ballistic missile with a range of over 5,000 km?
(a) Prithvi (b) Agni-V (c) Akash (d) Nag
Answer: (b)
Solution: Akash is a surface-to-air missile and Nag is anti-tank, so they are distractors. Agni-V is India's long-range surface-to-surface ballistic missile with a range beyond 5,000 km, so the answer is Agni-V.
Source: DRDO official website

7. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India also heads which department?
(a) Department of Defence Production (b) Department of Defence Research (c) Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare (d) Department of Military Affairs
Answer: (d)
Solution: The CDS serves as the Secretary of the Department of Military Affairs and is the principal military adviser to the government. The other departments have their own secretaries, so the answer is Department of Military Affairs.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

8. The Indian Air Force was officially established in which year?
(a) 1932 (b) 1911 (c) 1947 (d) 1950
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1947 (independence) is the tempting wrong choice. The Indian Air Force was established on 8 October 1932, which is why Air Force Day is celebrated on 8 October, so the answer is 1932.
Source: Indian Air Force official website

9. The 'Sagar Kavach' exercise is related to:
(a) Air defence (b) Mountain warfare (c) Coastal security (d) Cyber security
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sagar Kavach is a coastal security exercise conducted to check the preparedness of agencies guarding India's coastline, especially after the 26/11 Mumbai attacks, so the answer is coastal security.
Source: Indian Coast Guard / Ministry of Defence

10. Which of the following is India's first nuclear-powered submarine?
(a) INS Chakra (b) INS Kalvari (c) INS Sindhughosh (d) INS Arihant
Answer: (d)
Solution: INS Kalvari is a diesel-electric Scorpene-class submarine, a frequent trap. INS Arihant is India's first indigenously built nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN), so the answer is INS Arihant.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

11. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) is headquartered at:
(a) Bengaluru (b) Hyderabad (c) New Delhi (d) Pune
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bengaluru and Hyderabad host major DRDO labs, which misleads candidates. DRDO headquarters is at DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi, so the answer is New Delhi.
Source: DRDO official website

12. The 'Tejas' aircraft is a:
(a) Heavy transport aircraft (b) Light combat aircraft (c) Helicopter (d) Unmanned aerial vehicle
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tejas, developed by HAL and ADA, is an indigenously designed single-engine light combat aircraft (LCA) of the Indian Air Force, so the answer is light combat aircraft.
Source: HAL / DRDO official website

13. Which force guards the India-Pakistan and India-Bangladesh borders?
(a) Indo-Tibetan Border Police (b) Sashastra Seema Bal (c) Border Security Force (d) Assam Rifles
Answer: (c)
Solution: ITBP guards the India-China border and SSB the India-Nepal and India-Bhutan borders — a classic border-force mix-up. The BSF guards the India-Pakistan and India-Bangladesh borders, so the answer is Border Security Force.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

14. The rank of 'Field Marshal' in the Indian Army has been held by how many officers?
(a) One (b) Two (c) Three (d) Four
Answer: (b)
Solution: Only two officers — Sam Manekshaw (1973) and K.M. Cariappa (1986) — have held the five-star rank of Field Marshal, so the answer is two.
Source: Indian Army official records

15. The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) was launched under the leadership of:
(a) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (b) Vikram Sarabhai (c) Homi J. Bhabha (d) Raja Ramanna
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bhabha and Sarabhai are associated with the nuclear and space programmes respectively, which is the trap. Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam led the IGMDP (1983), which produced Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Nag and Trishul, so the answer is A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.
Source: DRDO official website

16. 'Operation Vijay' (1999) is associated with:
(a) Liberation of Goa (b) 1971 Bangladesh war (c) Kargil conflict (d) Siachen conflict
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 1961 Goa liberation was also called Operation Vijay, which is the chronological trap. In 1999, Operation Vijay was the Indian Army's operation to evict intruders from Kargil, so the answer is Kargil conflict.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

17. The National Security Guard (NSG) is popularly known as:
(a) Garuds (b) Greyhounds (c) Cobras (d) Black Cats
Answer: (d)
Solution: Garud is the special force of the Indian Air Force, a common confusion. The NSG, raised in 1984 for counter-terrorism, is known as the Black Cats because of their black uniforms, so the answer is Black Cats.
Source: NSG / Ministry of Home Affairs

18. India's first nuclear test (1974) at Pokhran was code-named:
(a) Operation Shakti (b) Operation Vijay (c) Smiling Buddha (d) Bandar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Operation Shakti refers to the 1998 Pokhran-II tests, the classic chronological trap. The 1974 test was code-named Smiling Buddha, so the answer is Smiling Buddha.
Source: Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India

19. The 'BrahMos' missile is a joint venture between India and:
(a) Russia (b) Israel (c) France (d) USA
Answer: (a)
Solution: BrahMos is a joint venture between India's DRDO and Russia's NPO Mashinostroyeniya; its name combines the Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers, so the answer is Russia.
Source: BrahMos Aerospace official website

20. The Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) primarily protects:
(a) International borders (b) Coastal areas (c) VVIPs only (d) Vital industrial installations and airports
Answer: (d)
Solution: The CISF guards vital installations such as airports, atomic power plants, space centres and major public sector undertakings, so the answer is vital industrial installations and airports.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

21. Consider the following statements:
1. The Indo-Tibetan Border Police was raised after the 1962 India-China war.
2. The ITBP guards the India-Nepal border.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — the India-Nepal border is guarded by the Sashastra Seema Bal, not the ITBP. The ITBP was raised in 1962 after the India-China war and guards the India-China (LAC) border, so statement 1 alone is correct and the answer is 1 only.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

22. Match the following commands with their headquarters:
1. Western Naval Command — Mumbai
2. Eastern Naval Command — Visakhapatnam
3. Southern Naval Command — Kochi
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: Candidates often swap Visakhapatnam and Kochi. The Western Naval Command is at Mumbai, the Eastern at Visakhapatnam, and the Southern (training command) at Kochi, so all three are correctly matched and the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: Indian Navy official website

23. Consider the following statements about the Agni missile series:
1. Agni missiles are surface-to-air missiles.
2. Agni-I is a short-range missile while Agni-V is a long-range missile.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) Both 1 and 2 (c) 2 only (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 1 is the trap — Agni missiles are surface-to-surface ballistic missiles; the surface-to-air missile in the IGMDP family is Akash. Statement 2 is correct as the series ranges from short-range Agni-I to long-range Agni-V, so the answer is 2 only.
Source: DRDO official website

24. Arrange the following events in chronological order:
1. First Pokhran nuclear test
2. Kargil War
3. Raising of the Border Security Force
4. Liberation of Goa
(a) 4, 3, 1, 2 (b) 3, 4, 1, 2 (c) 4, 1, 3, 2 (d) 3, 1, 4, 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is placing the BSF raising (1965) before Goa's liberation (1961). The correct order is: Goa liberation (1961), BSF raised (1965), Pokhran-I (1974), Kargil War (1999), so the answer is 4, 3, 1, 2.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

25. Consider the following statements:
1. The Chief of Defence Staff is a four-star officer.
2. The first Chief of Defence Staff of India was General Bipin Rawat.
3. The post of CDS was created in 2019.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: All three statements are correct: the CDS is a four-star general/equivalent officer, General Bipin Rawat was the first to hold the post, and the post was created in December 2019 (he assumed office on 1 January 2020), so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India
