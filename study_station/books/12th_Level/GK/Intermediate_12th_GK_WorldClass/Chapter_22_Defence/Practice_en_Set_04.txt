Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) was launched in which year under the leadership of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam?
(a) 1974 (b) 1998 (c) 1991 (d) 1983
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 1974 is the year of India's first nuclear test (Smiling Buddha), which students often confuse with the missile programme. IGMDP was launched in 1983 and developed Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Nag and Trishul missiles, so the answer is 1983.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

77. Which of the following is a surface-to-air missile developed under IGMDP?
(a) Prithvi (b) Akash (c) Agni (d) Nag
Answer: (b)
Solution: Prithvi and Agni are surface-to-surface ballistic missiles, and Nag is an anti-tank guided missile — these are the common traps. Akash is the surface-to-air missile of the IGMDP family, so the answer is Akash.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

78. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, was built at which shipyard?
(a) Mazagon Dock, Mumbai (b) Hindustan Shipyard, Visakhapatnam (c) Garden Reach, Kolkata (d) Cochin Shipyard, Kochi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mazagon Dock builds destroyers and submarines (like INS Visakhapatnam and Scorpene class), which is the usual confusion. INS Vikrant was constructed at Cochin Shipyard Limited, Kochi, and commissioned in September 2022, so the answer is Cochin Shipyard, Kochi.
Source: Indian Navy official website (indiannavy.nic.in)

79. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India heads which department of the Ministry of Defence?
(a) Department of Defence (b) Department of Defence Production (c) Department of Military Affairs (d) Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Department of Defence is headed by the Defence Secretary, a civil servant — this is the classic trap. The CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs, created in 2019, so the answer is Department of Military Affairs.
Source: Ministry of Defence official website (mod.gov.in)

80. The Agni-V missile of India has an approximate range of:
(a) 700 km (b) 2,500 km (c) More than 5,000 km (d) 3,500 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: 700 km matches Agni-I and 2,500 km matches Agni-II/III, which are the confusing options. Agni-V is an intercontinental-range ballistic missile with a range of more than 5,000 km, so the answer is more than 5,000 km.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

81. Which of the following statements about the S-400 Triumf is correct?
(a) It is a long-range air defence missile system purchased from Russia (b) It is a fighter aircraft purchased from France (c) It is an indigenously developed anti-tank missile (d) It is a nuclear-powered submarine leased from Russia
Answer: (a)
Solution: The French purchase refers to Rafale jets, and the leased Russian nuclear submarine refers to INS Chakra — both are deliberate confusions. The S-400 Triumf is a long-range surface-to-air missile system bought from Russia, so the answer is the second statement.
Source: Ministry of Defence / PIB releases

82. The headquarters of the Southern Command of the Indian Army is located at:
(a) Chennai (b) Secunderabad (c) Pune (d) Bengaluru
Answer: (c)
Solution: Secunderabad is a common trap because it hosts a major Army station in the south, but it is not a command headquarters. The Southern Command HQ is at Pune, so the answer is Pune.
Source: Indian Army official website (indianarmy.nic.in)

83. Operation Vijay (1999) is associated with which conflict?
(a) Liberation of Goa (b) Indo-Pak War of 1971 (c) Kargil conflict (d) Siachen operations
Answer: (c)
Solution: The name "Operation Vijay" is a trap because it was also used for the liberation of Goa in 1961. In 1999, Operation Vijay was the Indian Army's operation to evict intruders from Kargil heights, so the answer is the Kargil conflict.
Source: Ministry of Defence official records

84. Which of the following is India's first nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN)?
(a) INS Chakra (b) INS Arihant (c) INS Kalvari (d) INS Sindhughosh
Answer: (b)
Solution: INS Chakra is nuclear-powered but is an attack submarine (SSN), not a ballistic missile submarine — the key trap. INS Kalvari is a diesel-electric Scorpene-class submarine. INS Arihant is India's first indigenous SSBN, so the answer is INS Arihant.
Source: PIB / Indian Navy official website

85. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was formed in which year?
(a) 1958 (b) 1947 (c) 1962 (d) 1972
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1947 tempts students who assume DRDO began with independence, and 1962 tempts those linking it to the China war. DRDO was formed in 1958 by merging the Technical Development Establishments with the Directorate of Technical Development and Production, so the answer is 1958.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

86. The 'Dhanush' artillery gun inducted into the Indian Army is an upgraded version of which foreign gun?
(a) M777 howitzer (b) Pinaka launcher (c) K9 Vajra (d) Bofors FH-77B
Answer: (d)
Solution: K9 Vajra is a South Korean self-propelled gun and M777 is American — both are traps based on recent inductions. Dhanush is the indigenously upgraded version of the Swedish Bofors FH-77B, so the answer is Bofors FH-77B.
Source: PIB / Ministry of Defence releases

87. Which command of the Indian Armed Forces is the only tri-services (joint) command?
(a) Western Command (b) Andaman and Nicobar Command (c) Strategic Forces Command (d) Training Command
Answer: (b)
Solution: Strategic Forces Command is a strong trap because it handles nuclear forces jointly, but it is a functional command, not a full tri-services theatre command. The Andaman and Nicobar Command, headquartered at Port Blair, is India's only tri-services command, so the answer is Andaman and Nicobar Command.
Source: Ministry of Defence official website (mod.gov.in)

88. The Tejas Light Combat Aircraft is manufactured by:
(a) Bharat Electronics Limited (b) Bharat Dynamics Limited (c) Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (d) Mishra Dhatu Nigam
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bharat Electronics makes avionics and Bharat Dynamics makes missiles, so both attract guesses. The Tejas LCA is designed by ADA and manufactured by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited, so the answer is Hindustan Aeronautics Limited.
Source: HAL official website (hal-india.co.in)

89. Consider the chronological order of these nuclear tests: 1. Pokhran-I (Smiling Buddha), 2. Pokhran-II (Operation Shakti). Which is correct?
(a) 1974 and 1996 (b) 1971 and 1999 (c) 1974 and 1998 (d) 1964 and 1998
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1971 is the Bangladesh war year and 1996 is when India refused to sign the CTBT — both are chronological traps. Pokhran-I was conducted in May 1974 and Pokhran-II in May 1998, so the answer is 1974 and 1998.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

90. The rank of 'Field Marshal' in the Indian Army has been held by how many officers?
(a) One (b) Two (c) Three (d) Four
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students answer one, remembering only Sam Manekshaw, or three, adding a wrong name. The rank has been held by two officers — Sam Manekshaw (1973) and K.M. Cariappa (1986), so the answer is two.
Source: Indian Army official website (indianarmy.nic.in)

91. Which of the following statements about the BrahMos missile is NOT correct?
(a) It is a joint venture between India and Russia (b) It is a supersonic cruise missile (c) It can be launched from land, sea and air (d) It is a ballistic missile with a range of over 5,000 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first three statements are all true, which tempts students to pick one of them carelessly. BrahMos is a cruise missile, not a ballistic missile, and its range is a few hundred kilometres, not 5,000 km — so the incorrect statement is the last one.
Source: BrahMos Aerospace official website (brahmos.com)

92. The National Security Guard (NSG) was raised in which year, and under which ministry does it function?
(a) 1965, Ministry of Home Affairs (b) 1986, Ministry of Defence (c) 1984, Ministry of Defence (d) 1984, Ministry of Home Affairs
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is pairing the correct year with the wrong ministry — NSG is a counter-terrorism force, not a military unit. It was raised in 1984 under the Ministry of Home Affairs, so the answer is 1984, Ministry of Home Affairs.
Source: NSG / MHA official website (mha.gov.in)

93. The 'Pinaka' system developed by DRDO is a:
(a) Anti-tank guided missile (b) Multi-barrel rocket launcher (c) Torpedo system (d) Battle tank
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nag is the anti-tank missile and Arjun is the battle tank — students mix up these DRDO product names. Pinaka is a multi-barrel rocket launcher system, so the answer is multi-barrel rocket launcher.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

94. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) INS Vikramaditya — acquired from Russia (b) INS Vikrant — acquired from Russia (c) INS Vikramaditya — indigenously built carrier (d) INS Viraat — acquired from the USA
Answer: (a)
Solution: The similar carrier names are the trap: Vikrant is indigenous while Vikramaditya is the refurbished Russian Admiral Gorshkov, and Viraat came from the UK, not the USA. The only correct match is INS Vikramaditya — acquired from Russia, so the answer is the third option.
Source: Indian Navy official website (indiannavy.nic.in)

95. The Border Roads Organisation (BRO) functions under which ministry?
(a) Ministry of Defence (b) Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (c) Ministry of Home Affairs (d) Ministry of External Affairs
Answer: (a)
Solution: Because BRO builds roads, students often pick the Road Transport ministry — the intended trap. BRO works under the Ministry of Defence and builds roads in border areas, so the answer is Ministry of Defence.
Source: BRO official website (bro.gov.in)

96. Exercise 'Malabar' is a naval exercise in which India participates along with:
(a) Russia and China (b) USA, Japan and Australia (c) France and UK (d) Sri Lanka and Maldives
Answer: (b)
Solution: France is a trap because India holds the Varuna exercise with it. Malabar involves the Quad countries — India, USA, Japan and Australia, so the answer is USA, Japan and Australia.
Source: Indian Navy / PIB releases

97. The Assam Rifles, one of the oldest paramilitary forces of India, is administered by the Ministry of Home Affairs but its operational control in the North-East rests with:
(a) The Indian Army (b) The State Governments (c) The CRPF (d) The BSF
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming that since MHA administers it, a civilian agency also controls operations. Assam Rifles is administered by the MHA but operationally controlled by the Indian Army, so the answer is the Indian Army.
Source: MHA official website (mha.gov.in)

98. Which of the following is the correct order of Indian Air Force ranks from highest to lowest?
(a) Air Commodore, Air Vice Marshal, Air Marshal, Air Chief Marshal (b) Air Marshal, Air Chief Marshal, Air Commodore, Air Vice Marshal (c) Air Chief Marshal, Air Vice Marshal, Air Marshal, Air Commodore (d) Air Chief Marshal, Air Marshal, Air Vice Marshal, Air Commodore
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is swapping Air Marshal and Air Vice Marshal, whose names sound similar in seniority. The correct descending order is Air Chief Marshal, Air Marshal, Air Vice Marshal, Air Commodore, so the answer is the first option.
Source: Indian Air Force official website (indianairforce.nic.in)

99. India's first dedicated military satellite for the Indian Navy, launched in 2013, is:
(a) GSAT-7 (Rukmini) (b) GSAT-6 (c) Cartosat-2 (d) EMISAT
Answer: (a)
Solution: GSAT-6 tempts because of the similar name, but it serves civilian/strategic communication, not the Navy. GSAT-7, named Rukmini, launched in 2013, is the Navy's dedicated satellite, so the answer is GSAT-7 (Rukmini).
Source: ISRO official website (isro.gov.in)

100. The 'Arjun' developed by DRDO is a:
(a) Main battle tank (b) Light combat aircraft (c) Aircraft carrier (d) Nuclear submarine
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tejas is the aircraft, Vikrant the carrier and Arihant the submarine — all are traps based on famous indigenous platforms. Arjun is India's indigenously developed main battle tank, so the answer is main battle tank.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)
