Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Which of the following is the oldest paramilitary force of India?
(a) Assam Rifles (b) Border Security Force (c) Central Reserve Police Force (d) Indo-Tibetan Border Police
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many candidates pick the BSF because it is the largest border force, but it was raised only in 1965. The Assam Rifles traces its origin to 1835 (as Cachar Levy), making it the oldest paramilitary force of India, so the answer is Assam Rifles.
Source: PYQ-style

27. The Integrated Defence Staff (IDS) headquarters is located at:
(a) South Block, New Delhi (b) Hutments, Dalhousie Road, New Delhi (c) Sena Bhawan, New Delhi (d) Kashmir House, New Delhi
Answer: (b)
Solution: South Block houses the Ministry of Defence and service headquarters, which is the tempting wrong choice. The HQ Integrated Defence Staff functions from Hutments on Dalhousie Road, New Delhi, so the answer is Hutments, Dalhousie Road.
Source: PYQ-style

28. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, was built at:
(a) Hindustan Shipyard, Visakhapatnam (b) Garden Reach Shipbuilders, Kolkata (c) Mazagon Dock, Mumbai (d) Cochin Shipyard, Kochi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mazagon Dock builds destroyers and submarines and is a common wrong guess. INS Vikrant was constructed at Cochin Shipyard Limited, Kochi, and commissioned in September 2022, so the answer is Cochin Shipyard, Kochi.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Which missile of India is a submarine-launched ballistic missile (SLBM)?
(a) K-4 (b) Akash (c) Prithvi-II (d) Nag
Answer: (a)
Solution: Prithvi-II is a surface-to-surface missile but is launched from land, which traps many candidates. The K-4 is a submarine-launched ballistic missile developed by DRDO for India's nuclear triad, so the answer is K-4.
Source: PYQ-style

30. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India also heads which department?
(a) Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare (b) Department of Defence (c) Department of Defence Production (d) Department of Military Affairs
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Department of Defence is headed by the Defence Secretary, not the CDS — a frequent confusion. The CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs created in 2019, so the answer is Department of Military Affairs.
Source: PYQ-style

31. Operation Vijay (1999) is associated with:
(a) Liberation of Goa (b) Kargil conflict (c) 1971 Indo-Pak war (d) IPKF operations in Sri Lanka
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 1971 war is a tempting answer, but its operations had different code names. Operation Vijay was the Indian Army's operation to evict intruders from Kargil heights in 1999, so the answer is the Kargil conflict.
Source: PYQ-style

32. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of these events?
P. Raising of Border Security Force
Q. Raising of Indo-Tibetan Border Police
R. Raising of National Security Guard
(a) Q, P, R (b) R, Q, P (c) P, R, Q (d) P, Q, R
Answer: (a)
Solution: Candidates often assume the BSF is the oldest because it is the largest border force, but the ITBP was raised in 1962, before the BSF in 1965, while the NSG came in 1984. The correct order is ITBP, BSF, NSG, so the answer is Q, P, R.
Source: PYQ-style

33. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was formed in:
(a) 1972 (b) 1948 (c) 1962 (d) 1958
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1948 is tempting because the Technical Development Establishment dates to that era, but DRDO as an organisation was formed in 1958 by merging existing bodies, so the answer is 1958.
Source: PYQ-style

34. Which Indian warship class does INS Kolkata belong to?
(a) Talwar-class frigate (b) Shivalik-class frigate (c) Kolkata-class destroyer (d) Delhi-class destroyer
Answer: (c)
Solution: INS Delhi belongs to the Delhi class, and the similar city names cause confusion. INS Kolkata is the lead ship of the Kolkata-class (Project 15A) guided-missile destroyers, so the answer is Kolkata-class destroyer.
Source: PYQ-style

35. The National War Memorial in New Delhi was inaugurated in:
(a) 2016 (b) 2017 (c) 2019 (d) 2021
Answer: (c)
Solution: Some candidates choose 2021, confusing it with other commemorations. The National War Memorial near India Gate was inaugurated by the Prime Minister in February 2019, so the answer is 2019.
Source: PYQ-style

36. Which force is responsible for guarding the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders?
(a) Sashastra Seema Bal (b) Assam Rifles (c) Indo-Tibetan Border Police (d) Border Security Force
Answer: (d)
Solution: SSB guards the Nepal and Bhutan borders, which is the common trap. The BSF guards the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders, so the answer is Border Security Force.
Source: PYQ-style

37. The Agni-V missile has an approximate range of:
(a) 700 km (b) Over 5,000 km (c) 3,500 km (d) 2,500 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: 3,500 km is the range of Agni-IV, which misleads many. Agni-V is an intercontinental-range ballistic missile with a range of over 5,000 km, so the answer is over 5,000 km.
Source: PYQ-style

38. India's first nuclear test (1974) was code-named:
(a) Smiling Buddha (b) Operation Shakti (c) Operation Vijay (d) Pokhran-II
Answer: (a)
Solution: Operation Shakti (Pokhran-II, 1998) is the tempting wrong answer because it is better known. The 1974 test at Pokhran was code-named Smiling Buddha, so the answer is Smiling Buddha.
Source: PYQ-style

39. The headquarters of the Western Naval Command of the Indian Navy is at:
(a) Mumbai (b) Kochi (c) Visakhapatnam (d) Karwar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Karwar (Project Seabird base) is a frequent wrong choice. The Western Naval Command is headquartered at Mumbai, while Visakhapatnam hosts the Eastern Naval Command, so the answer is Mumbai.
Source: PYQ-style

40. Which of the following statements about the National Security Guard (NSG) is correct?
(a) It is a counter-terrorism force under the Ministry of Home Affairs (b) It guards the Indo-China border (c) It functions under the Ministry of Defence (d) It was raised in 1965 after the Indo-Pak war
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ministry of Defence option is tempting because NSG draws personnel from the Army, but the NSG (raised 1984) is a federal counter-terrorism force under the Ministry of Home Affairs, so the answer is that it is a counter-terrorism force under the Ministry of Home Affairs.
Source: PYQ-style

41. The Tejas Light Combat Aircraft is manufactured by:
(a) Bharat Electronics Limited (b) Bharat Dynamics Limited (c) Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (d) Mishra Dhatu Nigam
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bharat Dynamics makes missiles, which confuses some candidates. The Tejas LCA is designed by ADA and manufactured by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited, so the answer is Hindustan Aeronautics Limited.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Which of the following is India's first indigenous nuclear-powered submarine?
(a) INS Chakra (b) INS Kalvari (c) INS Sindhughosh (d) INS Arihant
Answer: (d)
Solution: INS Chakra is nuclear-powered but leased from Russia, which is the classic trap. INS Arihant is India's first indigenously built nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine, so the answer is INS Arihant.
Source: PYQ-style

43. The Andaman and Nicobar Command is significant because it is:
(a) Headquartered in Chennai (b) India's only naval command (c) India's first and only tri-services theatre command (d) Under the Ministry of Home Affairs
Answer: (c)
Solution: Candidates sometimes think it is purely naval because of its island location. Established in 2001 at Port Blair, it is India's first and only tri-services (geographical) command integrating Army, Navy and Air Force, so the answer is India's first and only tri-services theatre command.
Source: PYQ-style

44. The BrahMos missile is a joint venture between India and:
(a) France (b) Russia (c) USA (d) Israel
Answer: (b)
Solution: Israel is a tempting answer because of other joint projects like Barak-8. BrahMos is a joint venture between India's DRDO and Russia's NPO Mashinostroyeniya, named after the Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers, so the answer is Russia.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Which of the following correctly matches the force with the border it guards?
(a) ITBP – India–Myanmar border (b) SSB – India–China border (c) Assam Rifles – India–Myanmar border (d) BSF – India–Nepal border
Answer: (c)
Solution: ITBP guards the India–China border, not Myanmar, which is the trap in the first option. The Assam Rifles guards the India–Myanmar border, so the answer is Assam Rifles – India–Myanmar border.
Source: PYQ-style

46. The rank of 'Field Marshal' in the Indian Army has been held by:
(a) K.M. Cariappa and Sam Manekshaw (b) Only Sam Manekshaw (c) Only K.M. Cariappa (d) Three officers so far
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many believe only Manekshaw held the rank, forgetting Cariappa. Sam Manekshaw (1973) and K.M. Cariappa (1986) are the only two officers to hold the rank of Field Marshal, so the answer is K.M. Cariappa and Sam Manekshaw.
Source: PYQ-style

47. The 'Akash' missile system is a:
(a) Anti-tank guided missile (b) Air-to-air missile (c) Submarine-launched missile (d) Surface-to-air missile
Answer: (d)
Solution: Its use by the Air Force makes some candidates call it air-to-air, but Akash is a medium-range surface-to-air missile system developed by DRDO, so the answer is surface-to-air missile.
Source: PYQ-style

48. The Indian Air Force was officially established on:
(a) 15 August 1947 (b) 8 October 1932 (c) 26 January 1950 (d) 1 April 1935
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1947 is tempting due to independence, but the IAF was established on 8 October 1932, which is why Air Force Day is celebrated on that date, so the answer is 8 October 1932.
Source: PYQ-style

49. Which of the following statements about INS Vikramaditya is NOT correct?
(a) It was acquired from Russia (b) It is a nuclear-powered aircraft carrier (c) It was originally named Admiral Gorshkov (d) It was commissioned into the Indian Navy in 2013
Answer: (b)
Solution: All other statements are true, which makes this negative question tricky. INS Vikramaditya is a conventionally powered (not nuclear-powered) carrier, originally the Russian Admiral Gorshkov, commissioned in 2013, so the answer is that it is a nuclear-powered aircraft carrier.
Source: PYQ-style

50. The 'Mountain Strike Corps' (17 Corps) of the Indian Army is headquartered at:
(a) Leh, Ladakh (b) Dimapur, Nagaland (c) Panagarh, West Bengal (d) Mathura, Uttar Pradesh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Leh is tempting because the corps is meant for mountain warfare along the northern borders, but the 17 Mountain Strike Corps is headquartered at Panagarh, West Bengal, so the answer is Panagarh, West Bengal.
Source: PYQ-style
