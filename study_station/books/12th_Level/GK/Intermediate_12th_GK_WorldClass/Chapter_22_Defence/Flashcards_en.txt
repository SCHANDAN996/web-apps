Card 1
Front: Which is India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, commissioned in 2022?
Back: INS Vikrant (IAC-1), commissioned on 2 September 2022. Don't confuse it with the older INS Vikrant (R11) of 1971 war fame.

Card 2
Front: Which organisation designs and develops missiles like Agni and Prithvi for India?
Back: DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation), headquartered in New Delhi, established in 1958.

Card 3
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the five missiles of the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP)?
Back: "A-P-A-N-T" — Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Nag, Trishul. Remember: "APANT guards the sky."

Card 4
Front: Which is India's longest-range ballistic missile family, and what is the approximate range of Agni-V?
Back: The Agni series; Agni-V has a range of over 5,000 km, making it an intercontinental-range missile.

Card 5
Front: Who is the Supreme Commander of the Indian Armed Forces?
Back: The President of India (Article 53(2)). The President does not exercise operational command directly.

Card 6
Front: Which is the oldest paramilitary force of India?
Back: Assam Rifles, raised in 1835 as "Cachar Levy." Trap alert: many students wrongly pick BSF (1965).

Card 7
Front: Which force guards the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders?
Back: BSF (Border Security Force), raised on 1 December 1965 after the Indo-Pak war.

Card 8
Front: Which force guards the Line of Actual Control with China?
Back: ITBP (Indo-Tibetan Border Police), raised in 1962 after the Sino-Indian war.

Card 9
Front: Examiner's trap: Is the Indian Coast Guard part of the Indian Navy?
Back: No. The Coast Guard (established 1978) works under the Ministry of Defence but is an independent armed force for maritime law enforcement in peacetime — not a branch of the Navy.

Card 10
Front: What is the trick to remember which force guards which border?
Back: "B-P, I-C, S-N, A-My" — BSF: Pakistan/Bangladesh; ITBP: China; SSB: Nepal/Bhutan; Assam Rifles: Myanmar. Match force initials to the neighbour.

Card 11
Front: Where was India's first nuclear test (1974) conducted, and what was its code name?
Back: Pokhran, Rajasthan — code name "Smiling Buddha." The 1998 tests were "Operation Shakti" (Pokhran-II).

Card 12
Front: Which tri-services command is based in the Andaman & Nicobar Islands?
Back: The Andaman and Nicobar Command (established 2001) — India's only integrated theatre command so far.

Card 13
Front: Who was the first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India?
Back: General Bipin Rawat, appointed on 1 January 2020. The CDS post was created in 2019.

Card 14
Front: Which missile is a joint venture between India and Russia?
Back: BrahMos — a supersonic cruise missile; the name combines the Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers.

Card 15
Front: Examiner's trap: Does "INS" prefix apply to submarines as well as ships?
Back: Yes. INS (Indian Naval Ship) is used for all commissioned naval vessels, including submarines like INS Arihant (India's first nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine).

Card 16
Front: Which agency handles India's external intelligence?
Back: RAW (Research and Analysis Wing), formed in 1968. Internal intelligence is handled by the Intelligence Bureau (IB, founded 1887).

Card 17
Front: What is India's nuclear doctrine's key pledge?
Back: "No First Use" (NFU) — India will use nuclear weapons only in retaliation to a nuclear attack on Indian territory or forces, with massive retaliation.

Card 18
Front: Which is the highest wartime gallantry award of India?
Back: Param Vir Chakra. Trick to remember the order: "PVC-MVC-VC" — Param Vir, Maha Vir, Vir Chakra (descending order).

Card 19
Front: Where is the National Defence Academy (NDA) located, and what makes it unique?
Back: Khadakwasla, Pune — it is the world's first tri-services academy where Army, Navy, and Air Force cadets train together.

Card 20
Front: Which exercise series is conducted between the navies of India and the USA (with Japan and Australia joining in recent years)?
Back: Malabar. Trap alert: don't confuse it with "Varuna" (India–France) or "SIMBEX" (India–Singapore).
