# Key Facts & Figures — Indian Defence & Security

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supreme Commander of Armed Forces | President of India (Article 53) | "Prime Minister" given as option | Near-correct statement |
| Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) | Post created in 2019; first CDS — Gen. Bipin Rawat | Confusing CDS with Service Chiefs | Similar names |
| Department of Military Affairs | Headed by CDS; under Ministry of Defence | Placing it under PMO/Home Ministry | Institutional confusion |
| Integrated Defence Staff (HQ IDS) | HQ at New Delhi; supports CDS | Confusing with Theatre Commands | Similar names |
| Indian Army motto | "Seva Paramo Dharma" (Service Before Self) | Swapping mottos of Army/Navy/Air Force | Motto swap |
| Indian Navy motto | "Sham No Varunah" (May the Lord of Waters be auspicious unto us) | Attributing to Air Force | Motto swap |
| Indian Air Force motto | "Nabha Sparsham Deeptam" (Touch the Sky with Glory) — from Bhagavad Gita | Attributing to Navy | Motto swap |
| Army Day | 15 January (Gen. K.M. Cariappa took over as first Indian Commander-in-Chief, 1949) | Confusing with 16 December (Vijay Diwas) | Date confusion |
| Navy Day | 4 December (Operation Trident, 1971 war) | Confusing with 8 December (Submarine Day) | Date confusion |
| Air Force Day | 8 October (IAF established 1932) | Confusing with 15 January | Date confusion |
| Vijay Diwas | 16 December (1971 victory, surrender at Dhaka) | Confusing with Kargil Vijay Diwas (26 July) | Date confusion |
| Kargil Vijay Diwas | 26 July (Operation Vijay, 1999) | Confusing with 16 December | Date confusion |
| First Indian Commander-in-Chief of the Army | Gen. K.M. Cariappa (1949) | Calling him the first "Chief of Army Staff" — the title changed only in 1955 | Title confusion |
| First Chief of Army Staff | Gen. Maharaj Shri Rajendrasinhji Jadeja (1955, when the post of C-in-C was redesignated Chief of Army Staff) | Confusing with Gen. K.M. Cariappa (first Indian C-in-C, 1949) | Similar names |
| First Indian Chief of Naval Staff | Vice Admiral R.D. Katari (1958) | Confusing with Adm. S.M. Nanda | Similar names |
| First Indian Chief of Air Staff | Air Marshal Subroto Mukerjee (first Indian C-in-C of IAF from 1954; became Chief of Air Staff when the title changed in 1955) | Confusing with Air Marshal Arjan Singh | Similar names |
| Only Marshal of the Indian Air Force | Arjan Singh (five-star rank, 2002) | Confusing with Field Marshal rank of Army | Rank confusion |
| Field Marshals of India | Sam Manekshaw (1973) and K.M. Cariappa (1986) | Adding a third name | Fabricated option |
| Param Vir Chakra | Highest wartime gallantry award | Confusing with Ashoka Chakra (peacetime) | Award swap |
| Ashoka Chakra | Highest peacetime gallantry award | Confusing with Param Vir Chakra | Award swap |
| Maha Vir Chakra | Second-highest wartime gallantry award | Confusing order: PVC > MVC > VrC | Order confusion |
| DRDO | Established 1958, HQ New Delhi; works under Ministry of Defence | Confusing with ISRO (Dept. of Space) | Organisation swap |
| Agni-V | Longest-range Indian ballistic missile (over 5,000 km class) | Confusing with Prithvi (short range) | Missile range swap |
| Prithvi series | Short-range ballistic missiles | Confusing with Agni series | Missile range swap |
| BrahMos | Indo-Russian supersonic cruise missile (joint venture) | Calling it a ballistic missile | Missile type swap |
| Akash | Surface-to-air missile (SAM) | Calling it air-to-air (that's Astra) | Missile type swap |
| Astra | Beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile | Confusing with Akash | Missile type swap |
| INS Vikrant (IAC-1) | India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, commissioned 2022 | Confusing with INS Vikramaditya (Russian-built) | Similar names |
| INS Vikramaditya | Aircraft carrier acquired from Russia (ex-Admiral Gorshkov) | Calling it indigenous | Origin swap |
| INS Arihant | India's first nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN) | Confusing with INS Chakra (nuclear attack submarine, leased) | Submarine type swap |
| Project 75 | Conventional (Scorpène-class) submarines built with French collaboration | Confusing with nuclear submarine programme | Programme swap |
| Tejas | Indigenous Light Combat Aircraft (LCA), built by HAL | Confusing with Rafale (French import) | Origin swap |
| Rafale | French-origin fighter (Dassault), inducted 2020 | Calling it indigenous | Origin swap |
| HAL | Hindustan Aeronautics Limited, HQ Bengaluru | Confusing with BEL (Bengaluru too, but electronics) | Organisation swap |
| S-400 Triumf | Air defence system procured from Russia | Confusing with Israeli systems (Barak) | Origin swap |
| Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) | Launched 1983 under Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam | Wrong year or wrong scientist | Date/person swap |
| Nuclear tests | Pokhran-I (1974, "Smiling Buddha"); Pokhran-II (1998, "Operation Shakti") | Swapping the years | Date confusion |
| India's nuclear doctrine | No First Use (NFU); credible minimum deterrence | "First use" given as option | Near-correct statement |
| NSG (National Security Guard) | Counter-terrorism force, "Black Cats", formed 1984 | Confusing with SPG (PM protection) | Force swap |
| BSF | Border Security Force, raised 1965; guards India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders | Assigning it the China border (that's ITBP) | Border-force swap |
| ITBP | Indo-Tibetan Border Police, raised 1962; India–China border | Assigning it the Pakistan border | Border-force swap |
| SSB | Sashastra Seema Bal; India–Nepal and India–Bhutan borders | Assigning it the Bangladesh border | Border-force swap |
| Assam Rifles | Oldest paramilitary force (1835); India–Myanmar border and Northeast | Calling it the newest force | Date confusion |
| CRPF | Central Reserve Police Force; internal security, largest CAPF | Confusing its role with BSF | Role swap |
| Coast Guard | Established 1978; under Ministry of Defence | Placing it under Ministry of Home Affairs | Ministry swap |
| CAPFs under | Ministry of Home Affairs (BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB) | Placing them under MoD | Ministry swap |
| CISF | Central Industrial Security Force; protects airports, vital installations | Confusing with RPF (railways) | Force swap |
| RAW | Research and Analysis Wing, external intelligence, formed 1968 | Confusing with IB (internal intelligence) | Agency swap |
| IB | Intelligence Bureau, internal intelligence; oldest intelligence agency | Confusing with RAW | Agency swap |
| NIA | National Investigation Agency, formed after 2008 Mumbai attacks (Act of 2008) | Wrong year (2001 Parliament attack) | Date confusion |
| CDS Gen. Bipin Rawat | Died in a helicopter crash, December 2021 | Confusing with another officer | Person confusion |
| Second CDS | Gen. Anil Chauhan (appointed 2022) | Confusing with first CDS | Person confusion |
| Operation Trident | Naval attack on Karachi, 1971 war | Confusing with Operation Python (follow-up attack) | Operation swap |
| Operation Vijay (1999) | Kargil operation to evict intruders | Confusing with Operation Vijay 1961 (Goa liberation) | Same-name operations |
| Operation Meghdoot | 1984, Siachen Glacier | Confusing with Operation Cactus (Maldives, 1988) | Operation swap |
| Operation Cactus | 1988, Maldives intervention | Confusing with Operation Pawan (Sri Lanka, IPKF) | Operation swap |
| Siachen Glacier | World's highest battlefield; Indian Army deployed since 1984 | Wrong year (1971) | Date confusion |
| Chief of Army Staff rank | General | "Field Marshal" as regular rank | Rank confusion |
| Chief of Naval Staff rank | Admiral | "Admiral of the Fleet" as regular rank | Rank confusion |
| Chief of Air Staff rank | Air Chief Marshal | "Marshal of the IAF" as regular rank | Rank confusion |
| Defence Minister | Heads Ministry of Defence; civilian control over armed forces | "President heads MoD" | Institutional confusion |
| Territorial Army | Part-time volunteer force; supports regular Army | Calling it a paramilitary force | Classification trap |
| NCC | National Cadet Corps, established 1948 (NCC Act) | Wrong year (1962) | Date confusion |
| Agnipath scheme | Short-service recruitment scheme announced 2022; recruits called "Agniveers" | Confusing with regular permanent commission | Scheme confusion |
| Theatre Commands | Proposed integrated commands replacing single-service commands | Stating they are already fully operational | Near-correct statement |
| Dhanush | Naval variant of Prithvi missile (ship-launched) | Calling it a cruise missile | Missile type swap |
| Nag | Anti-tank guided missile | Confusing with surface-to-air role | Missile type swap |
| Pinaka | Multi-barrel rocket launcher system | Calling it a missile | Weapon type swap |
| LCA Tejas variants | Mk-1, Mk-1A, trainer; single-engine fighter | Confusing with twin-engine AMCA (future) | Variant confusion |
| Arjun | Indigenous Main Battle Tank | Confusing with T-90 (Russian origin) | Origin swap |
| INS Vikrant (old) | First aircraft carrier of India (ex-HMS Hercules), served in 1971 war | Confusing with new INS Vikrant (2022) | Same-name ships |
| Integrated Test Range | Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Island (formerly Wheeler Island), Odisha | Confusing with Sriharikota (ISRO launch site) | Location swap |
| Sriharikota | Satish Dhawan Space Centre — ISRO's launch site | Confusing with missile test range | Location swap |
| National War Memorial | Inaugurated 2019, near India Gate, New Delhi | Confusing with Amar Jawan Jyoti (flame merged with the Memorial's flame in 2022) | Memorial confusion |
| Gallantry award order (wartime) | Param Vir Chakra > Maha Vir Chakra > Vir Chakra | Reversing the order | Order confusion |
| Gallantry award order (peacetime) | Ashoka Chakra > Kirti Chakra > Shaurya Chakra | Reversing the order | Order confusion |
