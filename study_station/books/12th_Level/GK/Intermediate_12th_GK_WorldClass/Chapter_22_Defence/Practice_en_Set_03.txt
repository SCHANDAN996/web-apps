51. Which of the following is the oldest paramilitary force of India?
(a) Assam Rifles (b) Border Security Force (c) Central Reserve Police Force (d) Indo-Tibetan Border Police
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many candidates pick the BSF because it is the largest border-guarding force, but it was raised only in 1965. The Assam Rifles traces its origin to 1835 (as Cachar Levy), making it the oldest paramilitary force of India, so the answer is Assam Rifles.
Source: PYQ-style

52. The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) was launched in which year?
(a) 1973 (b) 1983 (c) 1993 (d) 2003
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap here is 1973, which sounds plausible because missile work began earlier, but the IGMDP under Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was formally launched in 1983, covering Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Nag and Trishul, so the answer is 1983.
Source: DRDO official records

53. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, was commissioned in which year?
(a) 2013 (b) 2018 (c) 2024 (d) 2022
Answer: (d)
Solution: Candidates often confuse the launch year (2013) with commissioning; the carrier was launched in 2013 but commissioned into the Navy on 2 September 2022, so the answer is 2022.
Source: Indian Navy official

54. The headquarters of the Western Naval Command of the Indian Navy is located at:
(a) Kochi (b) Visakhapatnam (c) Karwar (d) Mumbai
Answer: (d)
Solution: Visakhapatnam is a tempting wrong option, but it hosts the Eastern Naval Command; the Western Naval Command is headquartered at Mumbai, while Kochi hosts the Southern Naval Command, so the answer is Mumbai.
Source: Indian Navy official

55. Which missile of India is a submarine-launched ballistic missile (SLBM)?
(a) Prithvi-II (b) Nag (c) Akash (d) K-4
Answer: (d)
Solution: Prithvi-II is a trap because it is a ballistic missile, but it is surface-to-surface and land-based; the K-4 is the SLBM developed for INS Arihant-class submarines, so the answer is K-4.

57. Which of the following is India's first indigenous nuclear-powered submarine?

58. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was established in which year?
(a) 1958 (b) 1948 (c) 1962 (d) 1970

Solution: 1948 is a tempting wrong option because the Defence Science Organisation was set up then, but DRDO in its present form was established in 1958 by merging it with technical development establishments, so the answer is 1958.

59. The Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) primarily guards:
(a) International borders with Pakistan (b) The India-China border (c) Vital industrial installations and airports (d) Parliament House only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The CISF was raised in 1969 to protect public sector undertakings and now also guards airports, Delhi Metro and other vital installations, so the answer is vital industrial installations and airports.

60. Which exercise is a bilateral naval exercise between India and France?
(a) Malabar (b) Konkan (c) Varuna (d) Slinex

Solution: Malabar is a trap because it is a famous naval exercise, but it involves India, USA, Japan and Australia; Varuna is the India-France bilateral naval exercise, while Konkan is with the UK and Slinex with Sri Lanka, so the answer is Varuna.

61. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) post in India was created in which year?
(a) 2017 (b) 2020 (c) 2019 (d) 2021

62. Which of the following is the light combat aircraft developed indigenously by India?
(a) Tejas (b) Rafale (c) Sukhoi-30 MKI (d) Mirage-2000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sukhoi-30 MKI is a trap because it is manufactured in India, but under Russian licence; Tejas is the indigenously designed and developed light combat aircraft by HAL and ADA, so the answer is Tejas.

63. The Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) guards India's borders with which countries?
(a) Pakistan and Afghanistan (b) Nepal and Bhutan (c) Myanmar and Bangladesh (d) China and Nepal
Answer: (b)
Solution: China-Nepal is a trap because SSB sounds like a China-border force, but the India-China border is guarded by the ITBP; the SSB guards the Nepal and Bhutan borders, so the answer is Nepal and Bhutan.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs official

64. India's first nuclear test (Smiling Buddha) was conducted at Pokhran in which year?
(a) 1971 (b) 1974 (c) 1988 (d) 1998
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1998 is a tempting wrong option because Pokhran-II is more famous, but the first test, codenamed Smiling Buddha, was conducted on 18 May 1974, so the answer is 1974.

65. The Andaman and Nicobar Command is significant because it is:
(a) India's only tri-services theatre command (b) The headquarters of the Coast Guard (c) A joint command with Sri Lanka (d) The oldest command of the Indian Army
Answer: (a)
Solution: Established in 2001 at Port Blair, the Andaman and Nicobar Command is India's first and only tri-services command integrating the Army, Navy and Air Force, so the answer is India's only tri-services theatre command.

66. Which of the following statements about the National Security Guard (NSG) is correct?
(a) It is a counter-terrorism force raised in 1984 (b) It was raised in 1965 after the Indo-Pak war (c) It guards the Prime Minister's residence (d) It functions under the Ministry of Defence
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1965 is a trap because several forces were raised around that time, but the NSG was raised in 1984 (following Operation Blue Star) as a federal counter-terrorism force under the Ministry of Home Affairs, so the answer is that it is a counter-terrorism force raised in 1984.
Source: MHA official records

67. The BrahMos missile is a joint venture between India and which country?

Solution: BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile developed jointly by India's DRDO and Russia's NPO Mashinostroyeniya, its name combining the Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers, so the answer is Russia.

68. Which force is responsible for guarding the India-Pakistan and India-Bangladesh borders?

69. The 'Shakti' series of nuclear tests (Pokhran-II) was conducted in which year?

71. Which of the following correctly matches the command with its headquarters?
(a) Northern Army Command – Pune (b) Southern Air Command – Thiruvananthapuram (c) Eastern Army Command – Lucknow (d) Western Air Command – Gandhinagar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The other pairings are traps: the Northern Army Command is at Udhampur, the Eastern Army Command at Kolkata, and the Western Air Command at New Delhi; the Southern Air Command is correctly headquartered at Thiruvananthapuram, so the answer is Southern Air Command – Thiruvananthapuram.

72. The 'Akash' missile system is a:
(a) Anti-tank guided missile (b) Surface-to-air missile (c) Air-to-air missile (d) Submarine-launched missile
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nag is the anti-tank missile and Astra is the air-to-air missile, which are common confusions; Akash is a medium-range surface-to-air missile developed under the IGMDP, so the answer is surface-to-air missile.
Source: DRDO official records

73. Which of the following statements about INS Vikramaditya is correct?
(a) It is an indigenously built aircraft carrier (b) It was decommissioned in 1997 (c) It was acquired from Russia and commissioned in 2013 (d) It is a nuclear-powered submarine
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Indigenously built" is a trap because that describes INS Vikrant (IAC-1); INS Vikramaditya is the former Russian carrier Admiral Gorshkov, refurbished and commissioned into the Indian Navy in November 2013, so the answer is that it was acquired from Russia and commissioned in 2013.
Source: Indian Navy official

74. The 'Dhanush' artillery gun is associated with which of the following?
(a) An imported howitzer from Sweden (b) A surface-to-surface missile (c) An indigenously upgraded version of the Bofors gun (d) An anti-aircraft gun from Israel
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Imported from Sweden" is a trap because the original Bofors FH-77B was Swedish, but Dhanush is the indigenously upgraded 155mm/45-calibre version developed from Bofors technology transfer, so the answer is an indigenously upgraded version of the Bofors gun.
Source: Ministry of Defence official

75. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of these events?
(a) First nuclear test – Pokhran-II – Kargil War (b) First nuclear test – Kargil War – Pokhran-II (c) Kargil War – Pokhran-II – First nuclear test (d) Pokhran-II – First nuclear test – Kargil War
Answer: (a)
Solution: This is a chronological trap: the first nuclear test (Smiling Buddha) was in 1974, Pokhran-II (Shakti) in May 1998, and the Kargil War followed in 1999; placing Kargil before Pokhran-II is the common error, so the answer is first nuclear test – Pokhran-II – Kargil War.
Source: PYQ-style
