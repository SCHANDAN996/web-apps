126. Which of the following statements about the Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) is correct?
(a) The CDS commands all three services directly and replaces the three Service Chiefs (b) The CDS is the head of the Department of Military Affairs and also the Permanent Chairman of the Chiefs of Staff Committee (c) The CDS is a five-star rank officer senior to all Service Chiefs in protocol (d) The CDS heads the Department of Defence in the Ministry of Defence
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the second option: the CDS does not exercise military command over the three services; the Service Chiefs retain operational command of their respective forces. The CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs and is the Permanent Chairman of the Chiefs of Staff Committee, so the answer is that the CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs and chairs the Chiefs of Staff Committee permanently.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

127. Consider the following pairs of missiles and their types:
1. Prithvi — Surface-to-surface ballistic missile
2. Akash — Surface-to-air missile
3. Nag — Anti-tank guided missile
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is dropping pair 3: candidates often doubt Nag, but it is a third-generation fire-and-forget anti-tank guided missile. All three pairings are correct — Prithvi is a surface-to-surface ballistic missile, Akash is a surface-to-air missile, and Nag is an anti-tank guided missile, all developed under DRDO's Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: DRDO, Government of India (official)

128. The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) was conceived under the leadership of:
(a) Homi J. Bhabha (b) Vikram Sarabhai (c) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (d) Raja Ramanna
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Homi Bhabha: he is associated with India's nuclear programme, not the missile programme. Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam led the IGMDP, launched in 1983, which produced the Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Nag and Trishul missiles, so the answer is A. P. J. Abdul Kalam.
Source: DRDO, Government of India (official)

129. Which of the following is India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier?
(a) INS Vikrant (IAC-1) (b) INS Vikramaditya (c) INS Viraat (d) INS Arihant
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is INS Arihant: it is a nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine, not an aircraft carrier. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), built at Cochin Shipyard and commissioned in 2022, is India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, so the answer is INS Vikrant (IAC-1).
Source: Indian Navy / Ministry of Defence (official)

130. With reference to India's nuclear triad, which platform completed India's sea-based nuclear deterrent capability?
(a) INS Chakra (b) INS Arihant (c) INS Sindhughosh (d) INS Kolkata
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is INS Chakra: it is a nuclear-powered attack submarine leased from Russia and does not carry ballistic missiles. INS Arihant, a nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN), completed the sea leg of India's nuclear triad, so the answer is INS Arihant.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

131. Operation Cactus (1988) was conducted by India in which country?
(a) Sri Lanka (b) Maldives (c) Bangladesh (d) Nepal
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Sri Lanka: India's operation there was Operation Pawan (IPKF, 1987). Operation Cactus was the 1988 Indian military intervention that foiled an attempted coup in the Maldives, so the answer is Maldives.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

132. The Agni-V missile belongs to which category?
(a) Cruise missile (b) Medium-range ballistic missile (c) Short-range ballistic missile (d) Intercontinental-range ballistic missile (range over 5,000 km)
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the cruise missile option: cruise missiles like BrahMos fly within the atmosphere on a powered trajectory, whereas Agni-V is a ballistic missile. Agni-V is a long-range ballistic missile with a range exceeding 5,000 km, placing it in the intercontinental class, so the answer is intercontinental-range ballistic missile.
Source: DRDO, Government of India (official)

133. Which of the following statements about the Andaman and Nicobar Command is correct?
(a) It is a command of the Indian Army alone, headquartered at Kolkata (b) It is India's only tri-services theatre command, headquartered at Port Blair (c) It is a naval command under the Western Naval Command (d) It is an air command under the Southern Air Command
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the Army-only option: candidates assume island defence belongs to one service, but the Andaman and Nicobar Command, established in 2001, is India's first and only tri-services command, headed in rotation by officers of the Army, Navy and Air Force. It is headquartered at Port Blair, so the answer is that it is India's only tri-services theatre command, headquartered at Port Blair.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

134. The BrahMos missile is a joint venture between India and:
(a) Israel (b) France (c) United States (d) Russia
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Israel: India-Israel collaboration produced systems like the Barak-8 (MRSAM/LRSAM), not BrahMos. BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile jointly developed by India's DRDO and Russia's NPO Mashinostroyeniya, so the answer is Russia.
Source: BrahMos Aerospace / DRDO (official)

135. Which of the following statements about India's 'No First Use' (NFU) nuclear doctrine is correct?
(a) India commits not to use nuclear weapons first, but retains the right of massive retaliation to a nuclear attack on Indian territory or forces (b) India will use nuclear weapons first only against nuclear-armed states (c) India has renounced the use of nuclear weapons entirely (d) The NFU policy applies only during peacetime
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the first option: it reverses the doctrine — NFU means India will not initiate nuclear use against any state. India's doctrine (2003) pledges no first use with credible minimum deterrence and massive retaliation in response to a nuclear attack, so the answer is that India commits not to use nuclear weapons first but retains the right of massive retaliation.
Source: Ministry of External Affairs / Cabinet Committee on Security (official)

136. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was formed in which year by merging existing technical establishments?
(a) 1958 (b) 1962 (c) 1947 (d) 1971
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 1962: that year is associated with the Sino-Indian war and subsequent defence reforms, which tempts many candidates. DRDO was formed in 1958 by merging the Technical Development Establishment and the Directorate of Technical Development and Production with the Defence Science Organisation, so the answer is 1958.
Source: DRDO, Government of India (official)

137. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of these events?
1. Pokhran-I (Smiling Buddha)
2. Pokhran-II (Operation Shakti)
3. Commissioning of INS Arihant
(a) 1, 2, 3 (b) 2, 1, 3 (c) 1, 3, 2 (d) 3, 2, 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is chronological confusion: Pokhran-I was in 1974, Pokhran-II in May 1998, and INS Arihant was commissioned in 2016. The correct sequence is 1, 2, 3, so the answer is 1, 2, 3.
Source: Department of Atomic Energy / Indian Navy (official)

138. The 'Tejas' aircraft is:
(a) An imported multirole fighter from Russia (b) A jointly developed trainer aircraft with France (c) An indigenously developed light combat aircraft (d) An unmanned combat aerial vehicle
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the Russian fighter option: candidates confuse Tejas with the Sukhoi Su-30 MKI, which is of Russian origin. Tejas is an indigenously designed and developed single-engine light combat aircraft by HAL and ADA, so the answer is an indigenously developed light combat aircraft.
Source: HAL / Aeronautical Development Agency (official)

139. Which paramilitary force is primarily responsible for guarding the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders?
(a) Indo-Tibetan Border Police (b) Assam Rifles (c) Sashastra Seema Bal (d) Border Security Force
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the ITBP: it guards the India–China border, while the SSB guards the India–Nepal and India–Bhutan borders. The Border Security Force guards the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders, so the answer is the Border Security Force.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India (official)

140. The Kargil War of 1999 between India and Pakistan is also known by which Indian operation name?
(a) Operation Meghdoot (b) Operation Vijay (c) Operation Blue Star (d) Operation Trident
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Operation Meghdoot: it was India's 1984 operation to secure the Siachen Glacier, and Operation Trident was a 1971 naval operation. The 1999 Kargil operation to evict Pakistani intruders was Operation Vijay, so the answer is Operation Vijay.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

141. Which of the following statements about the S-400 Triumf system purchased by India is correct?
(a) It is an anti-ballistic missile system jointly developed with the United States (b) It is a long-range surface-to-air missile defence system acquired from Russia (c) It is a submarine-launched missile system acquired from France (d) It is an anti-tank missile system acquired from Israel
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the US joint-venture option: India's indigenous ballistic missile defence (Prithvi Air Defence/Advanced Air Defence) is a separate DRDO programme, not a US collaboration. The S-400 Triumf is a Russian long-range surface-to-air missile system contracted by India in 2018, so the answer is a long-range surface-to-air missile defence system acquired from Russia.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

142. The headquarters of the Integrated Defence Staff (IDS) is located in:
(a) Mumbai (b) Pune (c) New Delhi (d) Secunderabad
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Pune: it hosts the Southern Command of the Army and the National Defence Academy, which misleads candidates. The Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff, which promotes jointness among the three services, is located in New Delhi, so the answer is New Delhi.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

143. Which of the following is India's first nuclear-powered submarine?
(a) INS Arihant (b) INS Kalvari (c) INS Sindhurakshak (d) INS Khanderi
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is INS Kalvari: it is the first of the Scorpene-class conventional (diesel-electric) submarines, not nuclear-powered. INS Arihant is India's first indigenously built nuclear-powered submarine, so the answer is INS Arihant.
Source: Indian Navy / Ministry of Defence (official)

144. The 'Dhanush' missile, the naval variant of the Prithvi family, is launched from which type of platform?
(a) Aircraft (b) Submarines only (c) Ships (surface vessels) (d) Mobile land launchers only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the submarine option: submarine-launched ballistic missiles in India's arsenal are the K-series (like K-15 and K-4), not Dhanush. Dhanush is the ship-launched naval variant of the Prithvi missile family, so the answer is ships (surface vessels).
Source: DRDO, Government of India (official)

145. Consider the following statements about the National Security Guard (NSG):
1. It was established in 1984.
2. It functions under the Ministry of Defence.
3. It is a federal contingency force for counter-terrorism operations.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is statement 2: the NSG functions under the Ministry of Home Affairs, not the Ministry of Defence, although it draws personnel from the Army and CAPFs. Statements 1 and 3 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India (official)

146. The 'Mountain Strike Corps' (17 Corps) of the Indian Army, raised for operations along the northern borders, has its headquarters at:
(a) Udhampur (b) Dimapur (c) Leh (d) Panagarh
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Leh: it hosts the existing XIV Corps (Fire and Fury), which many candidates confuse with the new strike corps. The 17 Mountain Strike Corps is headquartered at Panagarh, West Bengal, so the answer is Panagarh.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

147. Which of the following correctly describes the 'Pinaka' system?
(a) A man-portable air defence system (b) An anti-aircraft gun imported from Sweden (c) A torpedo system for the Indian Navy (d) A multi-barrel rocket launcher system developed by DRDO
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the Swedish gun option: the Bofors howitzer was imported from Sweden, and candidates mix up imported artillery with indigenous systems. Pinaka is an indigenously developed multi-barrel rocket launcher system produced by DRDO, so the answer is a multi-barrel rocket launcher system developed by DRDO.
Source: DRDO, Government of India (official)

148. The Defence Acquisition Council (DAC), the highest decision-making body for defence procurement, is chaired by:
(a) The Raksha Mantri (Union Defence Minister) (b) The Chief of Defence Staff (c) The Defence Secretary (d) The National Security Advisor
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the CDS option: the CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs but does not chair the DAC. The Defence Acquisition Council is chaired by the Raksha Mantri, so the answer is the Raksha Mantri (Union Defence Minister).
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)

149. Which of the following statements about the Assam Rifles is correct?
(a) It guards the India–Pakistan border in the Jammu sector (b) It is the oldest paramilitary force of India, functioning under the administrative control of the Ministry of Home Affairs and operational control of the Army (c) It is a regiment of the regular Indian Army raised in 1947 (d) It functions under the Ministry of Defence in all respects
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the full Ministry of Defence option: although the Army exercises operational control, the Assam Rifles' administrative control lies with the Ministry of Home Affairs. Raised in 1835, it is India's oldest paramilitary force, so the answer is that it is the oldest paramilitary force under MHA's administrative control and the Army's operational control.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India (official)

150. Match the following defence exercises with the partner countries:
1. Exercise Malabar — India, USA, Japan (and Australia in recent editions)
2. Exercise Varuna — France
3. Exercise Mitra Shakti — Sri Lanka
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is pairing 3: candidates often confuse Mitra Shakti (India–Sri Lanka) with SLINEX, the naval exercise with Sri Lanka. All three pairings given are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India (official)
