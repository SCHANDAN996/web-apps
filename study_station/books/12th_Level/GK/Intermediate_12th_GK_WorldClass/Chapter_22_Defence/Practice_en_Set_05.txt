101. Which is the oldest paramilitary force of India?
(a) Border Security Force (b) Indo-Tibetan Border Police (c) Central Reserve Police Force (d) Assam Rifles
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is BSF, which is the largest border-guarding force but was raised only in 1965. Assam Rifles traces its origin to 1835 as the Cachar Levy, making it the oldest paramilitary force of India, so the answer is Assam Rifles.
Source: PYQ-style

102. The headquarters of the Western Naval Command of the Indian Navy is located at:
(a) Kochi (b) Visakhapatnam (c) Karwar (d) Mumbai
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kochi is tempting because it hosts the Southern Naval Command, and Visakhapatnam hosts the Eastern Naval Command. The Western Naval Command is headquartered at Mumbai, so the answer is Mumbai.
Source: Indian Navy official website (indiannavy.nic.in)

103. The headquarters of the Integrated Defence Staff (HQ IDS) is located at:
(a) Kashmir House, New Delhi (b) South Block, New Delhi (c) Sena Bhawan, New Delhi (d) Vayu Bhawan, New Delhi
Answer: (a)
Solution: South Block is tempting because it houses the Ministry of Defence, but HQ IDS, established in 2001 on the recommendations of the Kargil Review Committee, is located at Kashmir House, New Delhi, so the answer is Kashmir House, New Delhi.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Which missile of India is a submarine-launched ballistic missile (SLBM)?
(a) Prithvi-II (b) Agni-V (c) K-4 (d) Akash
Answer: (c)
Solution: Agni-V is tempting because it is India's longest-range missile, but it is a land-based, canisterised ballistic missile. The K-4 is an intermediate-range SLBM developed for the Arihant-class submarines, so the answer is K-4.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

105. The 'Sagar Kavach' exercise is conducted to test:
(a) Coastal security preparedness (b) Anti-submarine warfare (c) Air defence of island territories (d) Deep-sea mining readiness
Answer: (a)
Solution: Anti-submarine warfare is tempting because it is a naval activity, but Sagar Kavach is a coastal security exercise involving the Navy, Coast Guard, marine police and other agencies, conducted after the 26/11 Mumbai attacks exposed coastal gaps, so the answer is coastal security preparedness.
Source: PYQ-style

106. Who was the first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India?
(a) General Manoj Mukund Naravane (b) General Bipin Rawat (c) Admiral Karambir Singh (d) General Dalbir Singh Suhag
Answer: (b)
Solution: General Naravane is tempting because he was Army Chief when the post was created, but the post of CDS was created in December 2019 and General Bipin Rawat assumed office on 1 January 2020 as the first CDS, so the answer is General Bipin Rawat.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

107. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was formed in which year?
(a) 1948 (b) 1958 (c) 1972 (d) 1962
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1948 is tempting because the Technical Development Establishment dates to that era, but DRDO was formed in 1958 by merging the Technical Development Establishment and the Directorate of Technical Development and Production with the Defence Science Organisation, so the answer is 1958.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

108. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, was built at:
(a) Hindustan Shipyard, Visakhapatnam (b) Mazagon Dock, Mumbai (c) Garden Reach Shipbuilders, Kolkata (d) Cochin Shipyard, Kochi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mazagon Dock is tempting because it builds destroyers and submarines for the Navy, but INS Vikrant was designed by the Navy's Warship Design Bureau and built at Cochin Shipyard Limited, Kochi, and commissioned in September 2022, so the answer is Cochin Shipyard, Kochi.
Source: Indian Navy official website (indiannavy.nic.in)

109. The 'Agnipath' scheme announced in 2022 provides for recruitment of Agniveers for an initial service period of:
(a) 3 years (b) 5 years (c) 4 years (d) 7 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: 5 years is tempting because many government schemes run in five-year cycles, but under Agnipath, Agniveers serve for four years including training, after which up to 25% may be retained, so the answer is 4 years.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

110. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of these events?
(a) Kargil War → Indo-Pak War 1971 → Sino-Indian War 1962 (b) Sino-Indian War 1962 → Indo-Pak War 1971 → Kargil War (c) Indo-Pak War 1971 → Sino-Indian War 1962 → Kargil War (d) Kargil War → Sino-Indian War 1962 → Indo-Pak War 1971
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a chronological-confusion trap: the 1971 war is often misremembered as preceding 1962. The Sino-Indian War was in 1962, the Indo-Pak War leading to Bangladesh's liberation in 1971, and the Kargil conflict in 1999, so the answer is Sino-Indian War 1962 → Indo-Pak War 1971 → Kargil War.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

111. The Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) primarily guards:
(a) Vital installations like airports, atomic plants and metro networks (b) Coastal areas (c) The India-Nepal border (d) International borders with Pakistan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Border guarding is tempting because CISF is a Central Armed Police Force, but CISF was raised in 1969 to protect industrial undertakings and now guards airports, nuclear installations, Delhi Metro and other critical infrastructure, so the answer is vital installations like airports, atomic plants and metro networks.
Source: CISF official website (cisf.gov.in)

112. 'Exercise Malabar' is a naval exercise in which India participates along with:
(a) USA, Japan and Australia (b) USA, France and UK (c) Russia, China and Iran (d) Japan, Sri Lanka and Maldives
Answer: (a)
Solution: The France-UK option is tempting because India holds Varuna with France, but Malabar began as an India-US bilateral exercise in 1992, Japan joined permanently in 2015 and Australia in 2020, so the answer is USA, Japan and Australia.
Source: PYQ-style

113. The five-star rank of 'Field Marshal' in the Indian Army has been held by how many officers so far?
(a) Two (b) One (c) Three (d) Four
Answer: (a)
Solution: One is tempting because Sam Manekshaw (1973) is the most famous holder, but K.M. Cariappa was also conferred the rank in 1986, making them the only two officers to hold it, so the answer is Two.
Source: PYQ-style

114. The 'Tejas' aircraft is a:
(a) Heavy transport aircraft (b) Light combat aircraft (c) Unmanned aerial vehicle (d) Trainer aircraft
Answer: (b)
Solution: Trainer aircraft is tempting because Tejas has a trainer variant, but Tejas is an indigenously developed single-engine, multirole light combat aircraft designed by ADA and manufactured by HAL, so the answer is light combat aircraft.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

115. The Andaman and Nicobar Command is significant because it is India's:
(a) Only tri-services theatre command (b) First overseas military base (c) Largest naval dockyard (d) First nuclear submarine base
Answer: (a)
Solution: First overseas base is tempting because of the islands' strategic location, but the Andaman and Nicobar Command, established in 2001 at Port Blair, is India's first and only integrated tri-services command with Army, Navy, Air Force and Coast Guard components, so the answer is only tri-services theatre command.
Source: PYQ-style

116. The 'Shaurya Chakra' is awarded for:
(a) Gallantry in the face of the enemy during war (b) Distinguished service of the most exceptional order (c) Gallantry otherwise than in the face of the enemy (d) Long and meritorious service
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gallantry in war is tempting because Shaurya Chakra is a gallantry award, but wartime gallantry awards are the Param Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra and Vir Chakra; the Shaurya Chakra is a peacetime gallantry award for valour not in the face of the enemy, so the answer is gallantry otherwise than in the face of the enemy.
Source: PYQ-style

117. India's first nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN) is:
(a) INS Chakra (b) INS Kalvari (c) INS Sindhughosh (d) INS Arihant
Answer: (d)
Solution: INS Chakra is tempting because it is also nuclear-powered, but it is an attack submarine (SSN) leased from Russia. INS Arihant, commissioned in 2016, is India's first indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine, so the answer is INS Arihant.
Source: PYQ-style

118. The 'Dhanush' artillery gun inducted into the Indian Army is a:
(a) Anti-tank guided missile (b) 105 mm light field gun (c) Self-propelled rocket launcher (d) 155 mm towed howitzer
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rocket launcher is tempting because of the name's association with missiles, but Dhanush is an indigenously upgraded 155 mm/45 calibre towed howitzer, derived from the Bofors FH-77B technology, so the answer is 155 mm towed howitzer.
Source: PYQ-style

119. The National Security Guard (NSG) was raised in which year, and for what primary role?
(a) 1969, industrial security (b) 1965, border guarding (c) 1984, counter-terrorism and counter-hijacking (d) 1992, coastal security
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1969 is tempting because CISF was raised then, but NSG was raised in 1984 under the NSG Act as a federal contingency force for counter-terrorism, counter-hijacking and hostage-rescue operations, so the answer is 1984, counter-terrorism and counter-hijacking.
Source: PYQ-style

120. The 'BrahMos' missile is a joint venture between India and:
(a) France (b) Israel (c) Russia (d) USA
Answer: (c)
Solution: Israel is tempting because India co-develops Barak-8 with it, but BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile developed by BrahMos Aerospace, a joint venture between India's DRDO and Russia's NPO Mashinostroyeniya, named after the Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers, so the answer is Russia.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

121. Consider the following statements:
1. The Chief of Defence Staff is the permanent Chairman of the Chiefs of Staff Committee.
2. The CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs.
3. The CDS is a member of the Defence Acquisition Council chaired by the Defence Minister.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the almost-correct option 1 and 2 only, which omits statement 3; the CDS is indeed a member of the Defence Acquisition Council. All three statements correctly describe the CDS's roles, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

122. Match the following commands of the Indian Army with their headquarters:
1. Northern Command — P. Udhampur
2. Southern Command — Q. Pune
3. Eastern Command — R. Kolkata
4. Central Command — S. Lucknow
(a) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-S, 4-R (b) 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R, 4-S (c) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R, 4-S (d) 1-R, 2-Q, 3-P, 4-S
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is swapping Northern and Southern Command headquarters; Udhampur is in Jammu and Kashmir and hosts the Northern Command, while Pune hosts the Southern Command, with Eastern at Kolkata and Central at Lucknow, so the answer is 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R, 4-S.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Consider the following statements about the Agni series of missiles:
1. Agni-I is a short-range ballistic missile.
2. Agni-V is an intercontinental-range ballistic missile with a range of over 5,000 km.
3. All Agni missiles are submarine-launched.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is statement 3, which sounds plausible because India has SLBMs, but the Agni series is land-based; the submarine-launched missiles are the K-series. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: DRDO official website (drdo.gov.in)

124. Which of the following pairs is/are correctly matched?
1. Exercise Garuda Shakti — India and Indonesia (Army)
2. Exercise Mitra Shakti — India and Sri Lanka (Army)
3. Exercise Surya Kiran — India and Nepal (Army)
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming one pairing must be wrong because the names sound interchangeable; Mitra Shakti is indeed with Sri Lanka, not Nepal. All three pairs are correctly matched, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Consider the following statements about India's nuclear doctrine:
1. India follows a policy of 'No First Use' of nuclear weapons.
2. India's nuclear doctrine was formalised after the 1998 Pokhran-II tests.
3. The Nuclear Command Authority is headed by the Defence Minister.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is statement 3, an almost-correct statement, since the Defence Minister deals with defence; in fact the Political Council of the Nuclear Command Authority is chaired by the Prime Minister. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: PYQ-style
