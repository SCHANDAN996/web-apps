101. The pH of a solution is 4.5. The solution is
(a) strongly basic (b) neutral (c) weakly acidic (d) strongly acidic
Answer: (c)
Solution: A pH below 7 means acidic, but the tempting trap is to call any pH below 7 "strongly acidic". Strong acidity applies only near pH 0–2; 4.5 is only moderately below 7, so the solution is weakly acidic, so the answer is weakly acidic.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 2

102. Which of the following is used as a moderator in a nuclear reactor?
(a) Heavy water (b) Cadmium (c) Uranium-235 (d) Plutonium
Answer: (a)
Solution: Cadmium is a trap because it is used in reactors too, but as control rods to absorb neutrons, not to slow them. Heavy water (D₂O) slows down fast neutrons, acting as a moderator, so the answer is heavy water.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics, Chapter 13

103. The chemical formula of washing soda is
(a) NaHCO₃ (b) Na₂CO₃ (c) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O (d) NaOH
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is option Na₂CO₃, which is only anhydrous sodium carbonate (soda ash); washing soda is its decahydrate. Baking soda is NaHCO₃, another similar-name trap. Washing soda contains ten water molecules of crystallisation, so the answer is Na₂CO₃·10H₂O.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 2

104. Which gas is produced when dilute hydrochloric acid reacts with zinc metal?
(a) Oxygen (b) Chlorine (c) Hydrogen (d) Carbon dioxide
Answer: (c)
Solution: Carbon dioxide is a tempting trap because acids produce it with carbonates, but zinc is a metal, not a carbonate. Metals above hydrogen in the reactivity series displace hydrogen from dilute acids: Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂, so the answer is hydrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 2

105. The atomic number of an element is 17. Its valency is
(a) 7 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 7, which is the number of valence electrons (configuration 2, 8, 7), not the valency. The element needs one electron to complete its octet, so its valency is 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Chapter 4

106. Which of the following is an amphoteric oxide?
(a) Na₂O (b) Al₂O₃ (c) SO₂ (d) CaO
Answer: (b)
Solution: Na₂O and CaO are basic oxides and SO₂ is acidic; the trap is to assume all metal oxides are only basic. Aluminium oxide reacts with both acids and bases, making it amphoteric, so the answer is Al₂O₃.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 3

107. Brass is an alloy of
(a) copper and zinc (b) copper and tin (c) iron and chromium (d) aluminium and magnesium
Answer: (a)
Solution: Copper and tin is the classic trap — that combination makes bronze, not brass. Brass is an alloy of copper and zinc, so the answer is copper and zinc.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 3

108. The process of coating iron with zinc to prevent rusting is called
(a) galvanisation (b) anodising (c) electroplating with chromium (d) alloying
Answer: (a)
Solution: Electroplating is a near trap, but galvanisation specifically means coating iron or steel with a layer of zinc to protect it from corrosion, so the answer is galvanisation.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 3

109. Which of the following has the highest electron affinity?
(a) Fluorine (b) Iodine (c) Bromine (d) Chlorine
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fluorine is the famous trap here — although it is the most electronegative element, its small size causes electron–electron repulsion, so chlorine actually has the highest electron affinity in the halogen group, so the answer is chlorine.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry, Chapter 3

110. The number of moles in 44 g of CO₂ (molar mass 44 g/mol) is
(a) 1 (b) 0.5 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: Moles = given mass ÷ molar mass = 44 ÷ 44 = 1. The trap is to confuse this with the number of molecules or atoms, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry, Chapter 1

111. Which acid is present in vinegar?
(a) Citric acid (b) Oxalic acid (c) Lactic acid (d) Acetic acid
Answer: (d)
Solution: Citric acid (citrus fruits) and lactic acid (curd) are similar-name traps. Vinegar is a 4–8% solution of acetic acid (ethanoic acid) in water, so the answer is acetic acid.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 4

112. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH₂–OH is
(a) methanol (b) propanol (c) ethanol (d) ethanal
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ethanal is a trap with a similar name — it is an aldehyde (CHO group), not an alcohol. The compound has two carbon atoms with an –OH group, giving ethanol, so the answer is ethanol.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 4

113. Which of the following is a greenhouse gas?
(a) Methane (b) Nitrogen (c) Oxygen (d) Argon
Answer: (a)
Solution: Oxygen and nitrogen make up most of the atmosphere but do not absorb infrared radiation. Methane traps heat in the atmosphere and is a potent greenhouse gas, so the answer is methane.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry, Chapter 14

114. The oxidation number of sulphur in H₂SO₄ is
(a) +4 (b) +2 (c) +6 (d) −2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is +4, which is sulphur's oxidation state in H₂SO₃ (sulphurous acid) — a similar-name compound. In H₂SO₄: 2(+1) + x + 4(−2) = 0, giving x = +6, so the answer is +6.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry, Chapter 8

115. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?
(a) Sodium (b) Gallium only (c) Mercury (d) Caesium
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gallium and caesium melt near 30°C, which makes them traps, but the standard exam answer for a metal liquid at room temperature is mercury, so the answer is mercury.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 3

116. The common name of CaOCl₂ is
(a) bleaching powder (b) baking soda (c) plaster of Paris (d) slaked lime
Answer: (a)
Solution: Slaked lime, Ca(OH)₂, is a trap because bleaching powder is manufactured from it by passing chlorine. The compound CaOCl₂ itself is bleaching powder, so the answer is bleaching powder.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 2

117. Which of the following bonds is present in a molecule of nitrogen (N₂)?
(a) single bond (b) double bond (c) ionic bond (d) triple bond
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each nitrogen atom shares three pairs of electrons to complete its octet, forming a triple bond, which is why N₂ is very stable, so the answer is triple bond.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 4

118. Hard water does not form lather with soap because it contains salts of
(a) sodium and potassium (b) iron and zinc (c) calcium and magnesium (d) ammonium
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sodium and potassium salts are a trap because soaps themselves are sodium/potassium salts of fatty acids. Hardness is caused by dissolved calcium and magnesium salts, which form scum with soap, so the answer is calcium and magnesium.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 4

119. The gas used in the manufacture of ammonia by the Haber process, along with nitrogen, is
(a) oxygen (b) carbon monoxide (c) chlorine (d) hydrogen
Answer: (d)
Solution: In the Haber process, nitrogen and hydrogen combine in a 1:3 ratio over an iron catalyst to form ammonia: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, so the answer is hydrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry, Chapter 9

120. Which of the following is the lightest metal?
(a) Aluminium (b) Lithium (c) Magnesium (d) Sodium
Answer: (b)
Solution: Aluminium is a common trap because it is famous as a "light" structural metal, but by density lithium (0.53 g/cm³) is the lightest metal, so the answer is lithium.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry, Chapter 10

121. An element X has electronic configuration 2, 8, 6. Which statement about X is correct?
(a) It is a metal that forms X²⁺ ions (b) It is a non-metal that gains two electrons to form X²⁻ ions (c) It is a noble gas (d) It belongs to Group 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is reading 6 valence electrons as "loses electrons" like a metal; with 6 valence electrons it is easier to gain 2 than lose 6. The element is sulphur (Group 16), a non-metal forming X²⁻, so the answer is "It is a non-metal that gains two electrons to form X²⁻ ions".
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 5

122. Consider the following statements about the Modern Periodic Table:
1. Elements are arranged in increasing order of atomic mass.
2. There are 7 periods and 18 groups.
3. Elements in the same group have the same number of valence electrons.
Which of the above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 1 is the almost-correct trap — it describes Mendeleev's table; the modern table is arranged by atomic number, not atomic mass. Statements 2 and 3 are correct, so the answer is "2 and 3 only".
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 5

123. A solution turns red litmus blue. On adding excess of another solution, the blue litmus turns red again. The second solution is
(a) sodium hydroxide solution (b) hydrochloric acid (c) sodium chloride solution (d) distilled water
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sodium chloride is a trap because it is neutral and would not reverse the colour. Turning red litmus blue shows the first solution is basic; only an acid can neutralise it and turn the litmus red again, so the answer is hydrochloric acid.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 2

124. Arrange the following metals in decreasing order of reactivity: potassium, iron, gold, zinc.
(a) potassium > iron > zinc > gold (b) potassium > zinc > iron > gold (c) zinc > potassium > iron > gold (d) gold > iron > zinc > potassium
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is swapping iron and zinc — zinc is above iron in the reactivity series. The correct order is K > Zn > Fe > Au, with gold the least reactive, so the answer is "potassium > zinc > iron > gold".
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 3

125. Consider the following statements about carbon:
1. Diamond is a good conductor of electricity.
2. Graphite is used as a lubricant.
3. Fullerene (C₆₀) has a buckminsterfullerene structure.
Which of the above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 1 is the classic trap — it is graphite, not diamond, that conducts electricity, because diamond has no free electrons. Graphite's layered structure makes it a lubricant, and C₆₀ is buckminsterfullerene, so the answer is "2 and 3 only".
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 4
