26. Which of the following is the most abundant element in the Earth's crust?
(a) Oxygen (b) Silicon (c) Aluminium (d) Iron
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students pick silicon because it is the most abundant solid element and forms most rocks, but oxygen (about 46.6% by mass) exceeds silicon (about 27.7%). Aluminium is the most abundant metal, not element. So the answer is Oxygen.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

27. The pH of a solution is 4. If its hydrogen ion concentration is doubled, the new pH will be approximately:
(a) 3.7 (b) 2.0 (c) 4.3 (d) 8.0
Answer: (a)
Solution: Doubling concentration does not halve the pH — pH is logarithmic, so pH decreases by log 2 ≈ 0.3, giving 4 − 0.3 = 3.7. Options 2.0 and 8.0 wrongly assume linear change. So the answer is 3.7.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

28. Which gas is produced when zinc reacts with dilute sulphuric acid?
(a) Oxygen (b) Hydrogen (c) Sulphur dioxide (d) Hydrogen sulphide
Answer: (b)
Solution: Students often confuse this with the reaction of concentrated sulphuric acid, which gives sulphur dioxide; but with dilute acid, zinc displaces hydrogen: Zn + H2SO4 → ZnSO4 + H2. So the answer is Hydrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

29. The atomic number of an element is 17. Its valency is:
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 2 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 7, the number of valence electrons (configuration 2, 8, 7). Valency is the electrons needed to complete the octet, i.e. 8 − 7 = 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

30. Which of the following is used as a moderator in a nuclear reactor?
(a) Cadmium (b) Plutonium (c) Uranium-235 (d) Heavy water
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cadmium is a tempting wrong choice, but cadmium rods are control rods that absorb neutrons, not slow them. Heavy water (D2O) slows down fast neutrons as a moderator. So the answer is Heavy water.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

31. Brass is an alloy of:
(a) Copper and tin (b) Copper and zinc (c) Copper and aluminium (d) Zinc and tin
Answer: (b)
Solution: The classic trap is confusing brass with bronze; bronze is copper and tin, while brass is copper and zinc. So the answer is Copper and zinc.

32. Which of the following acids is present in vinegar?
(a) Citric acid (b) Acetic acid (c) Lactic acid (d) Tartaric acid
Answer: (b)
Solution: Citric acid (citrus fruits), lactic acid (curd) and tartaric acid (tamarind) are common distractors from the same NCERT list. Vinegar is a 4–8% solution of acetic (ethanoic) acid. So the answer is Acetic acid.

33. The number of electrons in 1 coulomb of charge is approximately:
(a) 6.25 × 10^18 (b) 6.022 × 10^23 (c) 1.6 × 10^−19 (d) 9.1 × 10^31
Answer: (a)
Solution: Option 6.022 × 10^23 is Avogadro's number, a frequent confusion. Since one electron carries 1.6 × 10^−19 C, 1 C contains 1/(1.6 × 10^−19) ≈ 6.25 × 10^18 electrons. So the answer is 6.25 × 10^18.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

34. Which of the following is an amphoteric oxide?
(a) Na2O (b) CaO (c) SO2 (d) Al2O3
Answer: (d)
Solution: Na2O and CaO are basic oxides and SO2 is acidic; the trap is assuming all metal oxides are basic. Aluminium oxide reacts with both acids and bases, so it is amphoteric. So the answer is Al2O3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

35. The chemical formula of washing soda is:
(a) NaHCO3 (b) Na2CO3·10H2O (c) Na2CO3 (d) NaOH
Answer: (b)
Solution: Option Na2CO3 is almost correct — it is soda ash (anhydrous). Washing soda is the decahydrate, Na2CO3·10H2O, while NaHCO3 is baking soda. So the answer is Na2CO3·10H2O.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

36. Which of the following elements has the highest electronegativity?
(a) Fluorine (b) Chlorine (c) Oxygen (d) Nitrogen
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chlorine is a tempting answer because it has the highest electron affinity, but electronegativity peaks at fluorine (4.0 on the Pauling scale). So the answer is Fluorine.

37. In the modern periodic table, elements are arranged in increasing order of:
(a) Atomic mass (b) Number of neutrons (c) Atomic number (d) Density
Answer: (c)
Solution: Atomic mass is the trap — Mendeleev used atomic mass, but Moseley's work established atomic number as the basis of the modern periodic law. So the answer is Atomic number.

38. Which gas is known as laughing gas?
(a) Nitrous oxide (b) Nitric oxide (c) Nitrogen dioxide (d) Ammonia
Answer: (a)
Solution: The similar names of nitrogen oxides cause confusion: N2O (nitrous oxide) is laughing gas, NO is nitric oxide, and NO2 is a brown pungent gas. So the answer is Nitrous oxide.

39. The rusting of iron is an example of:
(a) Oxidation (b) Reduction (c) Neutralisation (d) Displacement
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rust is hydrated ferric oxide, Fe2O3·xH2O, formed when iron combines with oxygen and moisture — a slow oxidation (corrosion). It is not a displacement or neutralisation reaction. So the answer is Oxidation.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

40. Which of the following is the lightest metal?
(a) Aluminium (b) Magnesium (c) Sodium (d) Lithium
Answer: (d)
Solution: Aluminium feels light in daily life and is a common wrong pick, but by density lithium (0.53 g/cm³) is the lightest metal — it even floats on kerosene, which is why it is stored in it. So the answer is Lithium.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

41. The common name of calcium oxychloride is:
(a) Plaster of Paris (b) Slaked lime (c) Bleaching powder (d) Quick lime
Answer: (c)
Solution: These similar calcium compounds are a classic trap: quick lime is CaO, slaked lime is Ca(OH)2, Plaster of Paris is CaSO4·½H2O, and bleaching powder is CaOCl2 (calcium oxychloride). So the answer is Bleaching powder.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

42. Which of the following carbon compounds is the hardest naturally occurring substance?
(a) Graphite (b) Fullerene (c) Diamond (d) Charcoal
Answer: (c)
Solution: Graphite is a trap because it is also a carbon allotrope, but it is soft and slippery due to layered structure. Diamond's 3D tetrahedral network of covalent bonds makes it the hardest natural substance. So the answer is Diamond.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

43. The isotopes of an element differ in the number of:
(a) Protons (b) Electrons (c) Neutrons (d) Valence electrons
Answer: (c)
Solution: Isotopes have the same number of protons (same atomic number) but different mass numbers, which means different numbers of neutrons — e.g. protium, deuterium and tritium of hydrogen. So the answer is Neutrons.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

44. Which of the following is used in the manufacture of safety matches?
(a) White phosphorus (b) Phosphorus pentoxide (c) Red phosphorus (d) Phosphoric acid
Answer: (c)
Solution: White phosphorus is the trap — it is highly reactive and poisonous, so it is unsafe. The striking surface of a matchbox contains red phosphorus, which is much less reactive. So the answer is Red phosphorus.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

45. A solution turns red litmus blue. The solution is:
(a) Acidic (b) Neutral (c) Basic (d) Amphoteric
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is reversing the colour change: acids turn blue litmus red, while bases turn red litmus blue. Since red litmus turned blue, the solution is basic. So the answer is Basic.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

46. Which of the following gases is the main component of biogas?
(a) Carbon dioxide (b) Methane (c) Hydrogen (d) Butane
Answer: (b)
Solution: Butane is a distractor because it is the main component of LPG, not biogas. Biogas, produced by anaerobic decomposition of organic waste, contains about 50–75% methane. So the answer is Methane.

47. The number of water molecules in one mole of water is:
(a) 6.022 × 10^23 (b) 18 × 10^23 (c) 3.011 × 10^23 (d) 1.6 × 10^19
Answer: (a)
Solution: Option 18 × 10^23 wrongly multiplies Avogadro's number by the molar mass; 18 g is the mass of one mole, not the particle count. One mole of any substance contains Avogadro's number of particles. So the answer is 6.022 × 10^23.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

48. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?
(a) Gallium (b) Sodium (c) Caesium (d) Mercury
Answer: (d)
Solution: Gallium and caesium melt just above room temperature (about 30°C and 29°C), making them near-correct traps, but mercury is the only metal that is liquid at standard room temperature. So the answer is Mercury.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

49. The process of coating iron with zinc to prevent rusting is called:
(a) Electroplating (b) Galvanisation (c) Anodising (d) Alloying
Answer: (b)
Solution: Electroplating is a near-correct trap since zinc can be electroplated, but the specific term for coating iron/steel with zinc (usually by dipping in molten zinc) is galvanisation. So the answer is Galvanisation.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

50. Which of the following statements about noble gases is correct?
(a) They are highly reactive because of incomplete shells (b) They exist as diatomic molecules (c) They readily form compounds with all elements (d) They have completely filled outermost shells
Answer: (d)
Solution: Option "diatomic molecules" is the trap — noble gases are monatomic, unlike gases such as oxygen or nitrogen. Their chemical inertness comes from completely filled valence shells (duplet for helium, octet for others). So the answer is "They have completely filled outermost shells".
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science
