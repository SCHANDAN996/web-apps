51. World Environment Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 22 April (b) 5 July (c) 16 June (d) 5 June
Answer: (d)
Solution: 22 April is Earth Day, which is the tempting confusion here, but it is a different observance. World Environment Day was established by the UN General Assembly in 1972 during the Stockholm Conference, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: UNEP official website

52. International Yoga Day is celebrated on 21 June because this day marks which event in the Northern Hemisphere?
(a) Winter solstice (b) Spring equinox (c) Autumn equinox (d) Summer solstice
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students pick the spring equinox thinking of balance, but 21 June is the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere, the summer solstice, which is why India proposed this date, so the answer is summer solstice.
Source: UN official website

53. National Science Day in India is observed on 28 February to commemorate which discovery?
(a) Discovery of zero (b) Theory of Relativity (c) Raman Effect (d) Bose-Einstein statistics
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Bose-Einstein statistics is a tempting option because C.V. Raman and S.N. Bose were contemporaries, but National Science Day marks the announcement of the Raman Effect in 1928, for which Raman won the Nobel Prize in 1930, so the answer is Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics; Government of India (DST)

54. World Health Day is observed on 7 April because it marks which event?
(a) Alma-Ata Declaration (b) Eradication of smallpox (c) First World Health Assembly in 1950 (d) Founding of WHO in 1948
Answer: (d)
Solution: The First World Health Assembly is a near-correct trap since the Assembly decided to celebrate the day, but 7 April 1948 is the date the WHO Constitution came into force, so the answer is founding of WHO in 1948.
Source: WHO official website

55. Human Rights Day is observed every year on which date?

56. National Voters' Day in India is celebrated on 25 January to mark which event?
(a) Foundation of the Election Commission of India (b) First general election (c) Adoption of the Constitution (d) First use of EVMs
Answer: (a)

57. World Water Day is observed on which date?
(a) 22 March (b) 22 April (c) 5 June (d) 16 September
Answer: (a)
Solution: 5 June (Environment Day) and 22 April (Earth Day) are classic confusions, but World Water Day was recommended at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit and first observed in 1993, so the answer is 22 March.

58. National Education Day in India is observed on 11 November in memory of which leader?
(a) Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (b) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (c) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (d) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dr. Radhakrishnan is the tempting trap because his birthday (5 September) is Teachers' Day, but 11 November is the birth anniversary of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, India's first Education Minister, so the answer is Maulana Abul Kalam Azad.
Source: Ministry of Education, Government of India

59. World Ozone Day (International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer) is observed on 16 September to mark which event?
(a) Rio Earth Summit (b) Signing of the Kyoto Protocol (c) Signing of the Montreal Protocol (d) Paris Agreement
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kyoto Protocol is the classic confusion since both deal with the atmosphere, but the Montreal Protocol on substances that deplete the ozone layer was signed on 16 September 1987, so the answer is signing of the Montreal Protocol.
Source: UN official website

64. Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) in India is observed on 26 November because on this day the Constituent Assembly did what?
(a) Enforced the Constitution (b) Adopted the Constitution (c) Held its first meeting (d) Signed the Preamble
Answer: (b)
Solution: Enforced the Constitution is the classic trap — enforcement happened on 26 January 1950 — but the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949, so the answer is adopted the Constitution.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Indian Constitution at Work)

Solution: The founding of UNFPA is a plausible-sounding trap, but the day was inspired by 11 July 1987, the approximate date world population crossed five billion (the "Day of Five Billion"), so the answer is world population crossing 5 billion.
Source: UN official website
