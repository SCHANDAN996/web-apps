Card 1
Front: Which day is celebrated as National Youth Day in India, and whose birthday does it mark?
Back: 12 January — the birth anniversary of Swami Vivekananda (born 1863).

Card 2
Front: Army Day (15 January) commemorates which historic first?
Back: It marks the day in 1949 when General (later Field Marshal) K. M. Cariappa became the first Indian Commander-in-Chief of the Indian Army, taking over from the British.

Card 3
Front: Why is 26 January celebrated as Republic Day, even though the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949?
Back: 26 January was chosen because on this date in 1930, the Indian National Congress observed Purna Swaraj (complete independence) Day. The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950.

Card 4
Front: World Environment Day falls on which date, and which UN body leads it?
Back: 5 June, led by the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP). It marks the start of the 1972 Stockholm Conference on the Human Environment.

Card 5
Front: Which two important days fall on 21 June, and why is that date chosen?
Back: International Yoga Day and World Music Day. 21 June is the summer solstice — the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere.

Card 6
Front: Quick mnemonic: "MART" — which four national days does it help you recall?
Back: M — Martyrs' Day (30 January, Gandhi's death anniversary); A — Army Day (15 January); R — Republic Day (26 January); T — Teachers' Day (5 September, Dr. S. Radhakrishnan's birthday).

Card 7
Front: Examiner's trap: Is National Science Day (28 February) linked to C. V. Raman's birthday?
Back: No — the trap! It marks the discovery of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928, not his birthday (which is 7 November). Examiners swap "discovery date" with "birth date."

Card 8
Front: Which day is observed as Kargil Vijay Diwas, and what does it commemorate?
Back: 26 July — it marks India's victory over Pakistan in the Kargil War of 1999 (Operation Vijay).

Card 9
Front: World Health Day (7 April) marks which event?
Back: The founding of the World Health Organization (WHO) in 1948.

Card 10
Front: Hindi Diwas is celebrated on 14 September. What happened on this date in 1949?
Back: The Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in the Devanagari script as the official language of the Union on 14 September 1949.

Card 11
Front: Memory trick: "4-5-6" for UN-related days — what does it stand for?
Back: 4 April — International Mine Awareness Day; 5 June — World Environment Day; 6 August — Hiroshima Day. A simple ascending date chain: 4/4, 5/6, 6/8.

Card 12
Front: Examiner's trap: Gandhi Jayanti (2 October) is also observed internationally as which day?
Back: International Day of Non-Violence (declared by the UN in 2007). The trap: students often confuse it with International Peace Day, which is 21 September.

Card 13
Front: On which date is Human Rights Day observed, and why?
Back: 10 December — the UN General Assembly adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights on this date in 1948.

Card 14
Front: National Voters' Day falls on 25 January. What does it mark?
Back: The foundation day of the Election Commission of India (established 25 January 1950).

Card 15
Front: Which day is celebrated as National Unity Day (Rashtriya Ekta Diwas), and whose birth anniversary is it?
Back: 31 October — the birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. (Trap: 31 October is also Indira Gandhi's death anniversary.)

Card 16
Front: World Population Day (11 July) was inspired by which milestone?
Back: The "Day of Five Billion" — 11 July 1987, when the world population was estimated to reach five billion.

Card 17
Front: Quick mnemonic: "Navy before Air Force" — what are the two dates?
Back: Navy Day — 4 December (marks the 1971 attack on Karachi harbour); Air Force Day — 8 October (IAF founded in 1932). Remember: December comes after October in the calendar, but "Navy" comes before "Air Force" alphabetically — a classic confusion point.

Card 18
Front: Anti-Terrorism Day is observed on 21 May. Whose death anniversary is it?
Back: Former Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi, assassinated on 21 May 1991.

Card 19
Front: Which day is celebrated as Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas), and how does it differ from Republic Day?
Back: 26 November — the day the Constitution was adopted in 1949. Republic Day (26 January) marks the day it came into force in 1950. Examiners love swapping "adopted" and "enforced."

Card 20
Front: International Women's Day and World Consumer Rights Day fall in the same week — what are the dates?
Back: International Women's Day — 8 March; World Consumer Rights Day — 15 March (inspired by John F. Kennedy's 1962 consumer rights address).
