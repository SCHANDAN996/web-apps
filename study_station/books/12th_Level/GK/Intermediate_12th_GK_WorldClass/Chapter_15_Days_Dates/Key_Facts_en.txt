# Key Facts & Figures — Important Days & Dates

|---|---|---|---|
| 26 January — Republic Day | Constitution came into force in 1950 | Confused with 26 November (Constitution Day, 1949 adoption) | Date swap |
| 15 August — Independence Day | India became independent in 1947 | Confused with 26 January | Date swap |

| 1 December — World AIDS Day | Observed since 1988 | Confused with World Health Day | Health-theme mix-up |
| 31 May — World No Tobacco Day | WHO initiative | Confused with 4 February (World Cancer Day) | Health-theme mix-up |
| 4 February — World Cancer Day | Led by UICC | Confused with 7 November (National Cancer Awareness Day, India) | National vs international |
| 22 March — World Water Day | UN observance since 1993 | Confused with 16 September (Ozone Day) | Environment-theme mix-up |
| 16 September — International Day for Preservation of Ozone Layer | Marks Montreal Protocol (1987) | Confused with Environment Day | Environment-theme mix-up |
| 29 August — National Sports Day | Birth anniversary of Major Dhyan Chand (1905) | Confused with other sports-linked days | Personality-date link |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Category | Question Type | Time Rule | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct date recall (e.g., "When is Yoga Day?") | Fact-based | 15 seconds — answer instantly or skip | Yes — highest accuracy per second |
| Personality ↔ day matching (Jayanti/Punyatithi) | Fact-based | 15 seconds | Yes |
| National vs international variants (Youth Day, Teachers' Day) | Fact-based with trap | 15 seconds if memorised as pairs; else skip | Yes |
| Same-name days (Martyrs' Day 30 Jan vs 23 Mar) | Fact-based with trap | 15 seconds if pair memorised | Yes |
| Statement-based questions ("Which of the following pairs is/are correctly matched?") | Analytical | 45 seconds — verify each pair | Moderate — attempt only if 3 of 4 pairs are certain |
| Chronology questions (arrange observances by month) | Analytical | 45 seconds | Moderate |
| Theme-of-the-year questions | Fact-based but volatile | Skip unless certain — themes change yearly | No |
| Double-fact days (1 May = Labour Day + Maharashtra/Gujarat Day) | Fact-based | 15 seconds | Yes |

## Part 3: Data & Formulas

| Item | Data |
|---|---|
| Fixed-date observances | Vast majority of UN/national days — memorise as date–event pairs |
| Shifting-date observances | Defined as "nth weekday of a month" (e.g., World Migratory Bird Day — second Saturday of May and of October); never a fixed date |
| Founding-year anchors | WHO — 1948 (7 April); UN — 1945 (24 October); UDHR — 1948 (10 December); Earth Day — 1970; Environment Day — since 1973; Yoga Day — since 2015 |
| Key birth/death anchors | Gandhi: born 2 Oct 1869, died 30 Jan 1948; Nehru: born 14 Nov 1889; Radhakrishnan: born 5 Sep 1888; Vivekananda: born 12 Jan 1863; Dhyan Chand: born 29 Aug 1905; Kalam: born 15 Oct 1931; Rajiv Gandhi: born 20 Aug 1944, died 21 May 1991 |
| No formulas | This chapter has no numerical formulas; scoring depends entirely on pair-memorisation accuracy |
