101. World Environment Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 16 June (b) 5 July (c) 5 June (d) 22 April
Answer: (c)
Solution: 22 April is Earth Day, which is the tempting confusion here. World Environment Day, led by UNEP since 1973, is observed on 5 June, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: UNEP official website

102. International Yoga Day is celebrated on 21 June because it is:
(a) the day the UN was founded (b) the birthday of Patanjali (c) the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere (d) the shortest day of the year
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students pick Patanjali's birthday, but that is not the stated reason. PM Modi proposed 21 June as it is the summer solstice, the longest day in the Northern Hemisphere, so the answer is the longest day of the year.
Source: UN official website

103. National Science Day in India is observed on 28 February to commemorate:
(a) Homi Bhabha's birthday (b) C.V. Raman's Nobel Prize award (c) the founding of ISRO (d) the discovery of the Raman Effect
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Nobel Prize was awarded in 1930, but the day marks the discovery of the Raman Effect announced on 28 February 1928, so the answer is the discovery of the Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics / Government of India

104. World Health Day is observed on 7 April because:
(a) the first vaccine was discovered (b) WHO was founded on this day in 1948 (c) the UN Charter was signed (d) smallpox was eradicated
Answer: (b)
Solution: Smallpox eradication was declared in 1980, not on 7 April. The day marks the founding of the World Health Organization on 7 April 1948, so the answer is WHO was founded on this day in 1948.
Source: WHO official website

105. Human Rights Day is observed every year on:
(a) 10 December (b) 24 October (c) 26 January (d) 14 November
Answer: (a)
Solution: 24 October is UN Day, a common confusion. Human Rights Day marks the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights on 10 December 1948, so the answer is 10 December.
Source: UN official website

106. National Voters' Day in India is celebrated on 25 January to mark:
(a) the foundation day of the Election Commission of India (b) the first general election (c) the adoption of the Constitution (d) the first use of EVMs
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first general election was held in 1951–52, which tempts many. The Election Commission was established on 25 January 1950, and the day has been observed since 2011, so the answer is the foundation day of the Election Commission of India.
Source: Election Commission of India official website

107. World Water Day is observed on:
(a) 16 September (b) 22 April (c) 5 June (d) 22 March
Answer: (d)
Solution: 22 April is Earth Day and 5 June is World Environment Day — all three are frequently mixed up. World Water Day is observed on 22 March, so the answer is 22 March.
Source: UN official website

108. World Ozone Day (International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer) is observed on:
(a) 16 September (b) 5 June (c) 22 March (d) 11 July
Answer: (a)
Solution: 5 June is Environment Day, the usual trap. 16 September marks the signing of the Montreal Protocol in 1987, so the answer is 16 September.
Source: UN official website

109. National Unity Day (Rashtriya Ekta Diwas) is observed on 31 October, the birth anniversary of:
(a) Subhas Chandra Bose (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (d) Lal Bahadur Shastri

Source: Government of India

110. World Population Day is observed on:

111. Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed in India on:
(a) 26 July (b) 16 December (c) 15 January (d) 7 December
Answer: (a)
Solution: 16 December is Vijay Diwas (1971 war victory), the classic chronological confusion. Kargil Vijay Diwas marks the end of the Kargil conflict on 26 July 1999, so the answer is 26 July.
Source: Government of India

112. National Education Day is observed on 11 November, the birth anniversary of:

Answer: (b)
Solution: Radhakrishnan's birthday (5 September) is Teachers' Day, the tempting trap. 11 November marks the birth anniversary of Maulana Azad, India's first Education Minister, so the answer is Maulana Abul Kalam Azad.
Source: Government of India

113. International Women's Day is observed on:

118. International Day of Non-Violence is observed on 2 October because:
(a) Gandhi was assassinated on this day (b) the UN was founded on this day (c) the Salt March began on this day (d) it is Mahatma Gandhi's birth anniversary
Answer: (d)

Source: UN official website

120. Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) in India is observed on:
(a) 26 January (b) 26 November (c) 15 August (d) 9 December
Answer: (b)
Solution: 26 January is Republic Day, when the Constitution came into force — the classic trap. The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949, observed as Constitution Day since 2015, so the answer is 26 November.
Source: Government of India

121. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) World Environment Day — 16 June (b) World Health Day — 7 May (c) World No Tobacco Day — 31 May (d) World Water Day — 22 April
Answer: (c)
Solution: World Health Day is 7 April (not 7 May), Environment Day is 5 June (not 16 June), and Water Day is 22 March (not 22 April) — each is an almost-correct statement trap. Only World No Tobacco Day on 31 May is correct, so the answer is World No Tobacco Day — 31 May.
Source: WHO/UN official websites

122. Arrange these days in correct chronological order within a calendar year: 1. World Environment Day 2. International Yoga Day 3. World Water Day 4. World Ozone Day
(a) 3, 1, 2, 4 (b) 1, 3, 2, 4 (c) 3, 2, 1, 4 (d) 1, 2, 3, 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is placing Yoga Day (21 June) before Environment Day (5 June). The correct order is Water Day (22 March), Environment Day (5 June), Yoga Day (21 June), Ozone Day (16 September), so the answer is 3, 1, 2, 4.
Source: UN official website

Solution: The trap is the almost-correct date: International Literacy Day is on 8 September, not 8 October. The other three statements are correct, so the answer is World Literacy Day is observed on 8 October.
Source: UNESCO / Government of India

124. Parakram Diwas is observed on 23 January to honour the birth anniversary of:

Source: Government of India

125. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?

Answer: (b)
Solution: Kargil Vijay Diwas is 26 July (not June), Armed Forces Flag Day is 7 December (not January), and Navy Day is 4 December (not November) — all near-miss traps. Vijay Diwas on 16 December marks the 1971 victory, so the answer is Vijay Diwas — 16 December.
Source: Government of India
