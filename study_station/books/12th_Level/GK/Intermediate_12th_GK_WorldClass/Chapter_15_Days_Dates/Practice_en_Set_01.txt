1. When is World Environment Day observed every year?
(a) 5 June (b) 5 July (c) 22 April (d) 16 September
Answer: (a)
Solution: 22 April is Earth Day and 16 September is World Ozone Day, which are common confusions. World Environment Day, led by UNEP since 1973, is observed on 5 June, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science / UNEP official

2. International Women's Day is celebrated on which date?
(a) 8 March (b) 8 February (c) 8 April (d) 8 May
Answer: (a)
Solution: The other dates are distractors with no such observance. International Women's Day is celebrated on 8 March every year, so the answer is 8 March.
Source: UN official website

3. World Health Day is observed on 7 April to mark the founding of which organisation?
(a) UNICEF (b) UNESCO (c) WHO (d) Red Cross
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNICEF and UNESCO are UN agencies but not linked to 7 April. World Health Day marks the founding of the World Health Organization in 1948, so the answer is WHO.
Source: WHO official website

4. National Science Day in India is observed on 28 February to commemorate which discovery?
(a) Theory of Relativity (b) Raman Effect (c) Discovery of electron (d) Radioactivity
Answer: (b)
Solution: The other discoveries were made by foreign scientists and are not linked to this date. National Science Day marks C.V. Raman's discovery of the Raman Effect in 1928, so the answer is Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics

5. When is International Yoga Day celebrated?

Source: UN official website

8. National Youth Day in India is celebrated on 12 January to mark the birth anniversary of which personality?

21. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Earth Day – 22 March (b) World Water Day – 22 April (c) World Health Day – 7 May (d) World Environment Day – 5 June
Answer: (d)
Solution: The traps swap similar dates: Earth Day is 22 April, World Water Day is 22 March, and World Health Day is 7 April, not 7 May. Only World Environment Day on 5 June is correctly matched, so the answer is World Environment Day – 5 June.
Source: UN official website

22. Arrange these observances in correct chronological order within a calendar year: 1. World Health Day 2. World Environment Day 3. Human Rights Day 4. National Science Day
(a) 1, 2, 4, 3 (b) 1, 4, 2, 3 (c) 4, 2, 1, 3 (d) 4, 1, 2, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap options place World Health Day (7 April) before National Science Day (28 February), reversing the year's order. The correct sequence is 28 February, 7 April, 5 June, 10 December, so the answer is 4, 1, 2, 3.
Source: NCERT / UN official website

23. Which day is observed as Martyrs' Day (Shaheed Diwas) in India on 30 January?
(a) Death anniversary of Subhas Chandra Bose (b) Death anniversary of Bhagat Singh (c) Death anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi (d) Death anniversary of Lal Bahadur Shastri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bhagat Singh's martyrdom is observed on 23 March, the most common trap since both are called Martyrs' Day. 30 January marks the assassination of Mahatma Gandhi in 1948, so the answer is death anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: NCERT Class 8 History

24. Which of the following statements about International Yoga Day is correct?
(a) It was first celebrated in 2014 (b) It is observed on 21 July (c) It was first celebrated in 2015 following a UN resolution (d) It marks the birthday of Patanjali
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 2014 — the UN resolution was passed in December 2014, but the first celebration was in 2015; the day is 21 June, not 21 July. The first International Yoga Day was celebrated on 21 June 2015, so the answer is that it was first celebrated in 2015 following a UN resolution.
Source: UN official website

25. Constitution Day (Samvidhan Diwas) in India is observed on 26 November because on this day:
(a) The Constitution came into force (b) The Constitution was adopted by the Constituent Assembly (c) The first Lok Sabha was constituted (d) The Preamble was amended
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that the Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950 (Republic Day), not 26 November. The Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on 26 November 1949, so the answer is that the Constitution was adopted by the Constituent Assembly.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Indian Constitution at Work)
