126. World Environment Day is observed on 5 June to commemorate the opening of which landmark conference?
(a) Stockholm Conference on the Human Environment, 1972 (b) Paris Climate Agreement, 2015 (c) Kyoto Protocol signing, 1997 (d) Rio Earth Summit, 1992
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is the Rio Earth Summit, because it is the most famous environmental conference, but World Environment Day was established by the UN General Assembly in 1972 to mark the opening day of the Stockholm Conference on the Human Environment (5 June 1972), and was first celebrated in 1974. So the answer is Stockholm Conference on the Human Environment, 1972.
Source: United Nations Environment Programme (unep.org)

127. Which of the following pairs of international days and dates is correctly matched?
(a) World No Tobacco Day – 31 May (b) World Tuberculosis Day – 7 April (c) World AIDS Day – 10 December (d) World Health Day – 24 March
Answer: (a)
Solution: This is a chronological-confusion trap: World Health Day is 7 April (not 24 March), World Tuberculosis Day is 24 March (not 7 April), and World AIDS Day is 1 December (not 10 December, which is Human Rights Day). Only World No Tobacco Day on 31 May is correctly matched, so the answer is World No Tobacco Day – 31 May.
Source: World Health Organization (who.int)

128. National Science Day in India is celebrated on 28 February because on this day:
(a) C.V. Raman was awarded the Nobel Prize (b) The first Indian satellite was launched (c) The Raman Effect was discovered by C.V. Raman in 1928 (d) Homi Bhabha founded TIFR
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the Nobel Prize option — C.V. Raman received the Nobel Prize in 1930, but National Science Day marks the discovery of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928; the day has been celebrated since 1987. So the answer is the discovery of the Raman Effect in 1928.
Source: NCERT / Vigyan Prasar, DST (dst.gov.in)

129. Which day is observed as International Day of Non-Violence, and why?
(a) 15 August – India's independence (b) 30 January – martyrdom of Mahatma Gandhi (c) 2 October – birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi (d) 26 January – adoption of the Constitution
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 30 January, which is Gandhi's martyrdom day and is observed in India as Martyrs' Day (Shaheed Diwas), but the UN observes the International Day of Non-Violence on 2 October, Gandhi's birthday, since 2007. So the answer is 2 October – birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: United Nations (un.org)

130. Human Rights Day is observed on 10 December because on this day in 1948:
(a) The UN Charter was signed (b) The International Court of Justice was established (c) The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted (d) The Geneva Conventions were revised
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is the UN Charter, which was signed on 26 June 1945 (observed as UN Charter Day); Human Rights Day marks the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights by the UN General Assembly on 10 December 1948. So the answer is the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
Source: United Nations (un.org)

131. Which of the following is observed on 21 June?
(a) International Yoga Day only (b) Both International Yoga Day and World Music Day (c) World Music Day only (d) International Olympic Day
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming only one observance falls on a date; 21 June is both International Yoga Day (UN, since 2015, proposed by India) and World Music Day (Fête de la Musique, since 1982), while International Olympic Day is 23 June. So the answer is both International Yoga Day and World Music Day.
Source: United Nations (un.org)

132. Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed in India on:
(a) 15 January (b) 16 December (c) 7 December (d) 26 July
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 16 December, which is Vijay Diwas marking the 1971 victory over Pakistan; Kargil Vijay Diwas commemorates the success of Operation Vijay in the Kargil War and is observed on 26 July. So the answer is 26 July.
Source: Ministry of Defence, India (mod.gov.in)

133. National Unity Day (Rashtriya Ekta Diwas) on 31 October commemorates the birth anniversary of:
(a) Subhas Chandra Bose (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Lal Bahadur Shastri (d) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Subhas Chandra Bose, whose birth anniversary (23 January) is Parakram Diwas; National Unity Day since 2014 marks the birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel on 31 October. So the answer is Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.

134. World Population Day, observed on 11 July, was inspired by which event?
(a) The establishment of UNFPA in 1969 (b) The world population reaching 5 billion in 1987 (c) The first World Population Conference in 1974 (d) The world population reaching 7 billion in 2011
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the 7-billion milestone of 2011, but World Population Day was established by the UNDP Governing Council in 1989, inspired by the "Day of Five Billion" on 11 July 1987 when world population crossed five billion. So the answer is the world population reaching 5 billion in 1987.
Source: United Nations (un.org)

135. Which day marks the adoption of the Indian Constitution and is observed as National Law Day (Constitution Day)?
(a) 26 January (b) 26 November (c) 15 August (d) 9 December
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 26 January, when the Constitution came into force (Republic Day); the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949, observed as Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) since 2015. So the answer is 26 November.
Source: Ministry of Law and Justice, India (lawmin.gov.in)

136. International Women's Day is observed on:
(a) 8 March (b) 12 February (c) 24 January (d) 10 December
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap options are related days: 24 January is National Girl Child Day (India) and 10 December is Human Rights Day; International Women's Day has been observed on 8 March, recognised by the UN since 1977. So the answer is 8 March.
Source: United Nations (un.org)

137. Hindi Diwas is celebrated on 14 September because on this day in 1949:
(a) The Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari script as the official language of the Union (b) The Constitution came into force (c) The Official Languages Act was passed (d) Hindi was declared the national language
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "national language" — India has no national language; on 14 September 1949 the Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari script as the official language of the Union, and the Official Languages Act came later, in 1963. So the answer is the Constituent Assembly adopting Hindi as the official language of the Union.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Rajbhasha Vibhag (rajbhasha.gov.in)

138. World Red Cross Day on 8 May marks the birth anniversary of:
(a) Florence Nightingale (b) Clara Barton (c) Henry Dunant (d) Mother Teresa
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Florence Nightingale, whose birthday (12 May) is International Nurses Day; World Red Cross and Red Crescent Day on 8 May honours Henry Dunant, founder of the Red Cross and the first Nobel Peace Prize laureate. So the answer is Henry Dunant.
Source: International Committee of the Red Cross (icrc.org)

139. Which of the following sequences of days is in correct chronological order within a calendar year?
(a) World Meteorological Day – World Water Day – World Health Day (b) World Health Day – World Water Day – World Meteorological Day (c) World Water Day – World Meteorological Day – World Health Day (d) World Water Day – World Health Day – World Meteorological Day
Answer: (c)
Solution: This is a chronological-confusion trap: World Water Day is 22 March, World Meteorological Day is 23 March, and World Health Day is 7 April; since 22 March comes before 23 March, the correct order is Water Day, then Meteorological Day, then Health Day. So the answer is World Water Day – World Meteorological Day – World Health Day.
Source: United Nations / WMO (un.org, wmo.int)

140. National Voters' Day in India is observed on 25 January to mark:

Source: Election Commission of India (eci.gov.in)

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 24 October, which is United Nations Day; the International Day of Peace is observed on 21 September, established by the UN General Assembly in 1981 and fixed to this date in 2001. So the answer is 21 September.

142. National Sports Day in India on 29 August commemorates the birth anniversary of:

(a) The first World Food Conference in 1974 (b) The establishment of the World Food Programme (c) The launch of the Green Revolution (d) The founding of the Food and Agriculture Organization in 1945
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the World Food Programme, which was established in 1961; World Food Day commemorates the founding of the FAO on 16 October 1945 and has been observed since 1981. So the answer is the founding of the Food and Agriculture Organization in 1945.
Source: Food and Agriculture Organization (fao.org)

144. Which of the following pairs is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) World Book and Copyright Day – 23 April (b) International Labour Day – 1 May (c) World Press Freedom Day – 3 May (d) International Literacy Day – 15 September
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that all four sound plausible, but International Literacy Day is observed on 8 September, not 15 September (which is Engineer's Day in India and International Day of Democracy); the other three pairs are correct. So the answer is International Literacy Day – 15 September.
Source: UNESCO (unesco.org)

145. Good Governance Day (Sushasan Diwas), observed in India on 25 December, was announced by the Government of India in 2014 to honour the birth anniversary of which Prime Minister?
(a) Atal Bihari Vajpayee (b) Lal Bahadur Shastri (c) Morarji Desai (d) P.V. Narasimha Rao
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that 25 December is also the birth anniversary of Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya, which can confuse candidates, but Good Governance Day was announced in 2014 specifically to honour former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee, born on 25 December 1924. So the answer is Atal Bihari Vajpayee.
Source: Government of India (india.gov.in)

148. World Ozone Day (International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer) on 16 September marks:
(a) The Kyoto Protocol in 1997 (b) The signing of the Montreal Protocol in 1987 (c) The signing of the Vienna Convention in 1985 (d) The discovery of the ozone hole in 1985
Answer: (b)

149. Which of the following days is observed on 4 February?
(a) World Heart Day (b) World Health Day (c) World Cancer Day (d) World Diabetes Day
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is World Diabetes Day, which is 14 November, and World Heart Day, which is 29 September; World Cancer Day is observed on 4 February, led by the Union for International Cancer Control since 2000. So the answer is World Cancer Day.
Source: Union for International Cancer Control / WHO (uicc.org)

150. National Maritime Day in India on 5 April commemorates:
(a) The launch of INS Vikrant (b) India's first major port at Mumbai (c) The establishment of the Shipping Corporation of India (d) The maiden voyage of SS Loyalty from Mumbai to London in 1919
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the Shipping Corporation of India, but National Maritime Day marks the maiden voyage of SS Loyalty, the first ship of the Scindia Steam Navigation Company, which sailed from Mumbai to London on 5 April 1919; the day has been observed since 1964. So the answer is the maiden voyage of SS Loyalty from Mumbai to London in 1919.
Source: Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, India (shipmin.gov.in)
