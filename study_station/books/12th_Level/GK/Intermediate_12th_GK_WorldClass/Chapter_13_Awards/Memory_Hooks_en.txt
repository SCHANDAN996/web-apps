# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Awards & Honours

---

**Hook 1 — Bharat Ratna Order of Precedence**
*"First Citizen, then PM, then the Ratna shines."*
Bharat Ratna comes **3rd** in the Indian order of precedence — after the President (1st) and Vice President (2nd)... wait, trap! Actually: President → Vice President → Prime Minister → Governors → former Presidents → Deputy PM → **Bharat Ratna awardees (7th position)**. Remember: **"7 = Ratna's heaven."**

---

**Hook 2 — Padma Awards Count the Petals**
*"Shri has 4, Bhushan 3, Vibhushan 2, Ratna 1 — the higher you climb, the fewer the words!"*
- Bharat **Ratna** = 1 word (highest)
- Padma **Vibhushan** = 2nd highest civilian
- Padma **Bhushan** = 3rd
- Padma **Shri** = 4th
Trick: **R-V-B-S** → *"Ratna Very Big Shine"* (descending order).

---

**Hook 3 — First Bharat Ratna Trio (1954)**
*"C. Rajagopalachari, Radhakrishnan, Raman — the 3 R's of '54!"*
All three names start with **R-sound**: **R**ajaji, **R**adhakrishnan, C.V. **R**aman. Three R's, year '54 → **"3R-54"**.

---

**Hook 4 — Nobel Prize Categories**
*"People Love Peaceful Chemistry, Medicine & Economics"*
**P**hysics, **L**iterature, **P**eace, **C**hemistry, **M**edicine (Physiology), **E**conomics.
Trap alert: Economics is NOT an original Nobel — it was added in **1968** by Sweden's central bank. *"Economics came late to the party — 1968!"*

---

**Hook 5 — Nobel Ceremony Cities**
*"Peace in Oslo, everything else in Stockholm."*
Norway = Peace prize; Sweden = all other five. Trick: **"Oslo = Only Peace"** (both start with O-P vibes).

---

**Hook 6 — India's First Nobel (Rabindranath Tagore, 1913)**
*"Tagore sang Gitanjali in 1913 — Literature's first Indian crown."*
Remember: **1913 = 13 = unlucky for Britain, lucky for Tagore.** Category: **Literature**, for *Gitanjali*.

---

**Hook 7 — C.V. Raman's Nobel (1930)**
*"Raman scattered light in '28, Nobel caught it in '30."*
Raman Effect discovered **1928** → Nobel **Physics 1930**. Trick: **"2 years to scatter into Stockholm."**

---

**Hook 8 — Major Dhyan Chand & Sports Awards**
*"Khel Ratna wears Dhyan Chand's name now (2021), Arjuna stays Arjuna."*
- **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna** — highest sports honour (renamed from Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna in 2021)
- **Arjuna Award** — consistent performance
- **Dronacharya** — coaches (*"Drona taught Arjuna — coach before player!"*)
- **Dhyan Chand Award** — lifetime achievement

---

**Hook 9 — Gandhi Peace vs. Nobel Peace**
*"Gandhi never got the Nobel — the biggest miss in history!"*
Mahatma Gandhi was nominated 5 times but never won. Don't confuse: **Indira Gandhi** won the **Bharat Ratna (1971)**, not a Nobel. Trap: "Gandhi Nobel Peace Prize" — always FALSE.

---

**Hook 10 — Jnanpith Award**
*"Jnan = Knowledge = Literature's biggest Indian prize (1965)."*
First Jnanpith went to **G. Sankara Kurup** (Malayalam, 1965). Trick: *"Kurup's knowledge opened the Jnanpith door."*

---

**Hook 11 — Dadasaheb Phalke Award**
*"Phalke = Films = Father of Indian Cinema."*
Highest **film** award; first recipient **Devika Rani (1969)**. Trick: *"The first lady Devika Rani got the first Phalke."*

---

**Hook 12 — Bharat Ratna to Foreigners**
*"Khan and Mandela — two non-Indians wore the Ratna."*
**Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987)** and **Nelson Mandela (1990)**. Trick: **"Frontier Khan first, Mandela followed."**

---

**Hook 13 — Posthumous Bharat Ratna Rule**
*"Lal Bahadur Shastri — the first Ratna after death (1966)."*
Trick: *"Shastri's Jai Jawan Jai Kisan echoed beyond life."*

---

**Hook 14 — Oscar's Indian Connection**
*"Bhanu Athaiya dressed Gandhi (1982) — India's first Oscar."*
Costume design for *Gandhi*. Also: **Satyajit Ray** — Honorary Oscar **1992**, same era as his Bharat Ratna (**1992**). Trick: *"Ray's double crown: Oscar + Ratna, both '92."*

---

**Hook 15 — Param Vir Chakra**
*"PVC = Param Vir = war's highest; Ashoka Chakra = peace's highest."*
Wartime gallantry: **Param Vir > Maha Vir > Vir Chakra** (*"Param is Paramount"*).
Peacetime: **Ashoka > Kirti > Shaurya Chakra** (*"AKS — Ashoka-Kirti-Shaurya, descending like A-K-S in a deck"*).
First PVC: **Major Somnath Sharma (1947)**.

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

1. **"Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?" (Award–Year–Person combos):** If you can verify only 2 of 4 pairs with confidence and the remaining two both look plausible, **skip**. Award-year matching questions are designed with one-digit year shifts (e.g., 1954 vs 1956) — guessing here is a coin toss with negative marking.

2. **"Who was the FIRST recipient of [obscure award]?" questions:** If the award is not Bharat Ratna, Nobel, Jnanpith, Phalke, Khel Ratna, or PVC (the high-yield six), and you haven't revised it, **skip immediately** — first-recipient facts for minor awards cannot be logically derived.

3. **Statement questions mixing civilian and gallantry awards** (e.g., "Padma Vibhushan is awarded for gallantry"): If you feel even 10% doubt about the category, **skip** — examiners deliberately swap "distinguished service" and "acts of valour" to trap partial learners. Come back only if time remains after attempting sure-shot questions.
