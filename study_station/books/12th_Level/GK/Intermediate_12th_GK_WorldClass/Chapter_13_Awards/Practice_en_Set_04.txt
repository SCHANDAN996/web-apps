Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Bharat Ratna was instituted in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1950 (c) 1954 (d) 1956
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1950 is tempting because the Constitution came into force then, but the Bharat Ratna, along with the Padma awards, was instituted by a Presidential notification in January 1954. The first recipients were C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan and C.V. Raman, so the answer is 1954.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Who was the first foreigner to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Nelson Mandela (b) Amartya Sen (c) Mother Teresa (d) Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nelson Mandela is tempting as a famous foreign recipient, but he received it in 1990. Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan of Pakistan (then a Pakistani citizen) received it in 1987, making him the first non-Indian citizen to get the award, so the answer is Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan.
Source: PYQ-style

78. The Padma Vibhushan is the ______ highest civilian award of India.
(a) second (b) first (c) third (d) fourth
Answer: (a)
Solution: The order is Bharat Ratna (first), Padma Vibhushan (second), Padma Bhushan (third) and Padma Shri (fourth). Many candidates confuse Padma Vibhushan with the top award, so the answer is second.
Source: PYQ-style

79. Which of the following is India's highest gallantry award during wartime?
(a) Ashoka Chakra (b) Maha Vir Chakra (c) Param Vir Chakra (d) Kirti Chakra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ashoka Chakra is tempting but it is the highest peacetime gallantry award. The Param Vir Chakra is the highest wartime gallantry decoration, so the answer is Param Vir Chakra.
Source: PYQ-style

80. The first recipient of the Param Vir Chakra was:
(a) Major Somnath Sharma (b) Lance Naik Albert Ekka (c) Captain Vikram Batra (d) Major Dhan Singh Thapa
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vikram Batra is tempting due to Kargil fame, but the first Param Vir Chakra was awarded posthumously to Major Somnath Sharma for the Battle of Badgam in 1947, so the answer is Major Somnath Sharma.
Source: PYQ-style

81. The Nobel Prize is awarded in memory of Alfred Nobel, who belonged to which country?
(a) Norway (b) Switzerland (c) Denmark (d) Sweden
Answer: (d)
Solution: Norway is tempting because the Peace Prize is awarded in Oslo, but Alfred Nobel was a Swedish chemist and the inventor of dynamite, so the answer is Sweden.
Source: PYQ-style

82. The Nobel Peace Prize is awarded in which city?
(a) Stockholm (b) Geneva (c) Copenhagen (d) Oslo
Answer: (d)
Solution: Stockholm is tempting since all other Nobel Prizes are awarded there, but by Nobel's will the Peace Prize alone is awarded in Oslo, Norway, so the answer is Oslo.
Source: PYQ-style

83. Who was the first Indian to win the Nobel Prize?
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Mother Teresa (d) Har Gobind Khorana
Answer: (b)
Solution: C.V. Raman is tempting as the first Indian science laureate (1930), but Rabindranath Tagore won the Literature Nobel in 1913 for Gitanjali, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: PYQ-style

84. The Nobel Prize in Economics was first awarded in which year?
(a) 1901 (b) 1969 (c) 1950 (d) 1978
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1901 is tempting because the original five Nobel Prizes began then, but the Economics prize was established by Sweden's central bank in 1968 and first awarded in 1969, so the answer is 1969.
Source: PYQ-style

85. The Jnanpith Award is given for excellence in which field?
(a) Music (b) Sports (c) Literature (d) Science
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Jnanpith Award, instituted in 1961, is India's highest literary honour; the first recipient was G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam) in 1965, so the answer is Literature.
Source: PYQ-style

86. The first recipient of the Jnanpith Award wrote in which language?
(a) Hindi (b) Bengali (c) Malayalam (d) Tamil
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hindi is tempting as the most widely spoken language, but G. Sankara Kurup won the first Jnanpith (1965) for his Malayalam work 'Odakkuzhal', so the answer is Malayalam.
Source: PYQ-style

87. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award is associated with which field?
(a) Sports (b) Literature (c) Cinema (d) Journalism
Answer: (a)
Solution: It is India's highest sporting honour, renamed in 2021 after hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand (earlier called Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna), so the answer is Sports.
Source: PYQ-style

88. The Arjuna Award was instituted in which year?
(a) 1956 (b) 1991 (c) 1985 (d) 1961
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1956 is tempting because the National Film Awards began around that era, but the Arjuna Award for outstanding sportspersons was instituted in 1961, so the answer is 1961.
Source: PYQ-style

89. The Dronacharya Award is given to:
(a) outstanding sportspersons (b) sports coaches (c) sports journalists (d) sports administrators
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Arjuna Award (for players) is the tempting confusion here; the Dronacharya Award, instituted in 1985, honours eminent coaches who train outstanding sportspersons, so the answer is sports coaches.
Source: PYQ-style

90. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is given for contribution to:
(a) Cinema (b) Classical music (c) Theatre (d) Painting
Answer: (a)
Solution: Theatre is tempting because Phalke worked in performing arts, but the award is India's highest honour in cinema, first given in 1969 to Devika Rani, so the answer is Cinema.
Source: PYQ-style

91. Who was the first recipient of the Dadasaheb Phalke Award?
(a) Devika Rani (b) Satyajit Ray (c) Prithviraj Kapoor (d) B.N. Sircar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Satyajit Ray is tempting as India's most celebrated filmmaker, but he received it in 1984; the first award in 1969 went to Devika Rani, so the answer is Devika Rani.
Source: PYQ-style

92. The Pulitzer Prize is associated with which fields?
(a) Science and medicine (b) Peace and economics (c) Journalism, literature and music (d) Sports and cinema
Answer: (c)
Solution: Established by Joseph Pulitzer and administered by Columbia University since 1917, it honours journalism, literature and musical composition, so the answer is Journalism, literature and music.
Source: PYQ-style

93. The Booker Prize is awarded for fiction written in which language?
(a) French (b) Any language (c) Spanish (d) English
Answer: (d)
Solution: 'Any language' is tempting because of the separate International Booker for translations, but the main Booker Prize is for a novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland, so the answer is English.
Source: PYQ-style

94. The first Indian-origin author to win the Booker Prize was:
(a) Arundhati Roy (b) V.S. Naipaul (c) Salman Rushdie (d) Kiran Desai
Answer: (b)
Solution: Salman Rushdie is tempting for 'Midnight's Children' (1981), but V.S. Naipaul won the Booker in 1971 for 'In a Free State', so the answer is V.S. Naipaul.
Source: PYQ-style

95. The Grammy Awards are given for excellence in:
(a) Films (b) Music recording (c) Television (d) Theatre
Answer: (b)
Solution: Films is tempting because the Grammys are often confused with the Oscars; the Grammy Awards honour achievements in the music recording industry, so the answer is Music recording.
Source: PYQ-style

96. The Academy Awards (Oscars) are presented by an academy based in which country?
(a) United States (b) France (c) United Kingdom (d) Italy
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Oscars are presented by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, headquartered in Beverly Hills, California, since 1929, so the answer is United States.
Source: PYQ-style

97. The first Indian to win an individual Oscar (Academy Award) was:
(a) A.R. Rahman (b) Satyajit Ray (c) Gulzar (d) Bhanu Athaiya
Answer: (d)
Solution: Satyajit Ray is tempting but he received an Honorary Oscar in 1992; Bhanu Athaiya won a competitive Oscar for costume design for 'Gandhi' (1982 ceremony), so the answer is Bhanu Athaiya.
Source: PYQ-style

98. The Ramon Magsaysay Award is often called Asia's:
(a) Oscar (b) Booker Prize (c) Nobel Prize (d) Pulitzer
Answer: (c)
Solution: Instituted in 1957 in memory of Philippine President Ramon Magsaysay, it honours selfless service in Asia and is popularly called Asia's Nobel Prize, so the answer is Nobel Prize.
Source: PYQ-style

99. Which of the following Indians received the Ramon Magsaysay Award?
(a) Vinoba Bhave (b) C.V. Raman (c) Homi Bhabha (d) S. Chandrasekhar
Answer: (a)
Solution: C.V. Raman and Chandrasekhar are tempting as Nobel laureates, but the Magsaysay Award is for public service; Vinoba Bhave received it in 1958 for community leadership, so the answer is Vinoba Bhave.
Source: PYQ-style

100. The Gandhi Peace Prize is awarded by:
(a) United Nations (b) Government of India (c) Government of South Africa (d) Nobel Committee
Answer: (b)
Solution: The United Nations is tempting because peace prizes are often UN-linked, but the Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted by the Government of India in 1995 on the 125th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi, so the answer is Government of India.
Source: PYQ-style
