51. The Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, was instituted in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1950 (c) 1954 (d) 1956
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students pick 1950 because the Constitution came into force that year and the first Republic Day honours feel linked to it, but that is the chronological trap. The Bharat Ratna was formally instituted on 2 January 1954, along with the Padma awards, so the answer is 1954.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Who was the first recipient of the Bharat Ratna award?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (c) C. Rajagopalachari (d) C.V. Raman
Answer: (c)
Solution: C.V. Raman is tempting because he was a Nobel laureate and was honoured in the same first batch, and Radhakrishnan also received it in 1954, but the question asks for the first recipient. In 1954 the award went first to C. Rajagopalachari, followed by Radhakrishnan and C.V. Raman, so the answer is C. Rajagopalachari.
Source: PYQ-style

53. The Padma awards are announced every year on the occasion of which day?
(a) Independence Day (b) Constitution Day (c) Gandhi Jayanti (d) Republic Day
Answer: (d)
Solution: Independence Day is associated with gallantry award announcements, which creates confusion. Padma awards are announced annually on the eve of Republic Day, 26 January, so the answer is Republic Day.
Source: PYQ-style

54. Which is the correct descending order of the Padma awards?
(a) Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan (b) Padma Vibhushan, Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan (c) Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Shri (d) Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing Padma Bhushan above Padma Vibhushan because "Bhushan" sounds grander to some. Padma Vibhushan is the second-highest civilian award after Bharat Ratna, followed by Padma Bhushan and then Padma Shri, so the answer is Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri.
Source: PYQ-style

55. The Nobel Prize was established according to the will of which scientist?
(a) Isaac Newton (b) Alfred Nobel (c) Marie Curie (d) Albert Einstein
Answer: (b)
Solution: Einstein and Curie are distractors because they are famous Nobel winners, not founders. The prizes were established by the 1895 will of Swedish chemist and dynamite inventor Alfred Nobel, so the answer is Alfred Nobel.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

56. In which field is the Nobel Prize NOT awarded among the following?
(a) Physics (b) Mathematics (c) Peace (d) Literature
Answer: (b)
Solution: Students often wrongly think Economics is the missing field, but the Economics prize was added later in 1968 by Sweden's central bank. Alfred Nobel's original will covered Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature and Peace — Mathematics was never included, so the answer is Mathematics.
Source: PYQ-style

57. Who was the first Indian to receive the Nobel Prize?
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Har Gobind Khorana (c) Mother Teresa (d) Rabindranath Tagore
Answer: (d)
Solution: C.V. Raman is the common wrong pick because his 1930 Physics Nobel is very famous, but Tagore won earlier. Rabindranath Tagore received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913 for Gitanjali, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: PYQ-style

58. C.V. Raman was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics for the discovery of which phenomenon?
(a) Photoelectric effect (b) Nuclear fission (c) Raman effect (d) Radioactivity
Answer: (c)
Solution: The photoelectric effect is tempting because Einstein won a Nobel for it, creating a similar-name confusion. C.V. Raman won the 1930 Nobel Prize for his work on the scattering of light, known as the Raman effect, so the answer is Raman effect.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

59. The Param Vir Chakra is India's highest award for which category?
(a) Civilian service (b) Sports excellence (c) Peacetime gallantry (d) Wartime gallantry
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is confusing it with the Ashoka Chakra, which is the highest peacetime gallantry award. The Param Vir Chakra is the highest wartime gallantry decoration, so the answer is wartime gallantry.

60. Which is the highest peacetime gallantry award of India?
(a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Maha Vir Chakra (c) Ashoka Chakra (d) Kirti Chakra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kirti Chakra is a close distractor as it is the second-highest peacetime gallantry award. The Ashoka Chakra ranks first among peacetime gallantry awards, so the answer is Ashoka Chakra.
Source: PYQ-style

61. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award is given for excellence in which field?
(a) Literature (b) Science (c) Cinema (d) Sports
Answer: (d)
Solution: The name Dhyan Chand itself can mislead some into thinking of cinema or arts honours, but he was India's hockey legend. The award, earlier called Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna, is India's highest sporting honour, so the answer is sports.
Source: PYQ-style

62. The Arjuna Award was instituted in which year?
(a) 1954 (b) 1971 (c) 1961 (d) 1985
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1954 is the trap year because that is when Bharat Ratna and Padma awards began, and students merge the dates. The Arjuna Award for outstanding sportspersons was instituted in 1961, so the answer is 1961.
Source: PYQ-style

63. The Dronacharya Award is given to honour whom?
(a) Outstanding sportspersons (b) Sports coaches (c) Sports journalists (d) Sports administrators
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many confuse it with the Arjuna Award, which honours players. Named after the guru of the Pandavas, the Dronacharya Award honours eminent coaches, so the answer is sports coaches.

64. The Jnanpith Award is given for outstanding contribution in which field?
(a) Indian literature (b) Classical music (c) Social service (d) Journalism
Answer: (a)
Solution: Social service is the tempting wrong option because the award's citation mentions service to humanity through writing. Instituted in 1961, the Jnanpith Award honours Indian authors for literary excellence, so the answer is Indian literature.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Who was the first recipient of the Jnanpith Award?
(a) G. Sankara Kurup (b) Kuvempu (c) Ashapurna Devi (d) Sumitranandan Pant
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kuvempu is a frequent wrong choice because he was the first Kannada writer to win it. The first Jnanpith Award in 1965 went to Malayalam poet G. Sankara Kurup for Odakkuzhal, so the answer is G. Sankara Kurup.
Source: PYQ-style

66. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is associated with which field?
(a) Cinema (b) Theatre (c) Painting (d) Dance
Answer: (a)
Solution: Theatre is the trap because Phalke worked in stage arts early in life. Instituted in 1969, it is India's highest award in cinema for lifetime contribution, so the answer is cinema.
Source: PYQ-style

67. Who was the first woman to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Mother Teresa (b) Indira Gandhi (c) M.S. Subbulakshmi (d) Aruna Asaf Ali
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mother Teresa is the classic trap since she was a globally honoured woman who received the Bharat Ratna in 1980, but Indira Gandhi received it earlier, in 1972, so the answer is Indira Gandhi.

68. The Pulitzer Prize is primarily associated with which fields?
(a) Journalism, literature and music (b) Science and medicine (c) Peace and diplomacy (d) Sports and adventure
Answer: (a)
Solution: Peace is the distractor because students mix it up with the Nobel Peace Prize. Established by Joseph Pulitzer's will, the Pulitzer Prizes honour achievements in journalism, literature and musical composition in the USA, so the answer is journalism, literature and music.

69. The Booker Prize is awarded for excellence in which area?
(a) Fiction written in English (b) Poetry in any language (c) Scientific research (d) Documentary films
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming it covers all literature including poetry. The Booker Prize is awarded annually for the best novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland, so the answer is fiction written in English.

70. Mother Teresa received the Nobel Peace Prize in which year?
(a) 1979 (b) 1975 (c) 1972 (d) 1980
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1980 is the chronological trap because she received the Bharat Ratna that year, and students swap the two dates. She was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 1979 for her humanitarian work in Kolkata, so the answer is 1979.

71. The Gandhi Peace Prize is instituted by which body?
(a) United Nations (b) Nobel Foundation (c) Government of India (d) UNESCO
Answer: (c)
Solution: The United Nations is tempting because of the word "peace" and global connotation. The Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted by the Government of India in 1995, so the answer is Government of India.

72. Which award is known as the highest honour in the field of mathematics, often called the "Nobel of Mathematics"?
(a) Fields Medal (b) Abel Prize only in name (c) Turing Award (d) Pulitzer Prize
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Turing Award is the trap because it is called the "Nobel of Computing," a similar-sounding phrase. The Fields Medal, awarded every four years to mathematicians under 40, is regarded as the highest honour in mathematics, so the answer is Fields Medal.

73. The Sahitya Akademi Award is given for books written in how many recognised languages?
(a) 18 (b) 22 (c) 26 (d) 24
Answer: (d)
Solution: 22 is the tempting wrong answer because the Eighth Schedule lists 22 languages, and students stop there. The Sahitya Akademi honours books in 24 languages — the 22 scheduled languages plus English and Rajasthani, so the answer is 24.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Nobel Prize — Norway for all categories (b) Magsaysay Award — Philippines (c) Grammy Award — Literature (d) Oscar — Sports
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Norway option is almost correct but is a trap: only the Peace Prize is awarded in Oslo, Norway, while the other Nobel Prizes are given in Stockholm, Sweden. The Ramon Magsaysay Award is indeed a Philippine honour for Asian contributions, so the answer is Magsaysay Award — Philippines.
Source: PYQ-style

75. The Bharat Ratna has been awarded posthumously to which of the following leaders?
(a) Morarji Desai (b) Lal Bahadur Shastri (c) Gulzarilal Nanda (d) Charan Singh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Morarji Desai is the trap because he received a high honour from Pakistan (Nishan-e-Pakistan), but he never received the Bharat Ratna. Lal Bahadur Shastri was the first person to be awarded the Bharat Ratna posthumously, in 1966, so the answer is Lal Bahadur Shastri.
