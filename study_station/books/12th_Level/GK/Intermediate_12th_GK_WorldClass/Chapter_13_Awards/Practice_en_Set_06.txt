126. The Bharat Ratna was instituted in which year, and who were its first recipients?
(a) 1950; C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan, C.V. Raman (b) 1952; S. Radhakrishnan, C. Rajagopalachari, Govind Ballabh Pant (c) 1954; Jawaharlal Nehru, C.V. Raman, Bhagwan Das (d) 1954; C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan, C.V. Raman
