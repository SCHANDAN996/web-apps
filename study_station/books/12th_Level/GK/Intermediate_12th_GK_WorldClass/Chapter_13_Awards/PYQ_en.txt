# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Awards & Honours

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from This Chapter

Across SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD), Banking (IBPS, SBI), Railway (NTPC, Group D) and State PCS prelims, questions from Awards & Honours follow a stable, recognisable pattern. Exams do not test obscure awards; they repeatedly circle around a small core:

- **Bharat Ratna** — India's highest civilian award: who received it, in which year, and who were the *first* recipients (C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan, C.V. Raman — 1954).
- **Padma Awards hierarchy** — Padma Vibhushan → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri (2nd, 3rd, 4th highest civilian honours respectively). Order-confusion is the single most common trap.
- **Gallantry awards** — Param Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra, Vir Chakra (wartime) vs Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra, Shaurya Chakra (peacetime). Exams love asking "which is the highest *peacetime* gallantry award?"
- **Sports awards** — Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (renamed from Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna in 2021), Arjuna Award, Dronacharya Award, Dhyan Chand Award.
- **Literature & films** — Jnanpith Award (first recipient: G. Sankara Kurup, 1965), Sahitya Akademi Award, Dadasaheb Phalke Award (first recipient: Devika Rani, 1969).
- **International awards** — Nobel Prize (Indian laureates: Tagore 1913, C.V. Raman 1930, Mother Teresa 1979, Amartya Sen 1998, Kailash Satyarthi 2014, Abhijit Banerjee 2019), Booker Prize, Pulitzer, Magsaysay.
- **Fields of Nobel Prizes** — six categories; note there is **no Nobel Prize in Mathematics** (a classic trap).

## 2. Sub-topics That Recur Most Often

In words, not numbers: **civilian awards (Bharat Ratna + Padma)** dominate, followed by **gallantry awards** and **sports awards**. International awards (especially Nobel laureates of Indian origin) appear regularly in Banking and SSC. Film and literature awards appear less often but are predictable — first recipients and recent winners are the usual angles.

## 3. Exam-wise Tendencies

- **SSC (CGL/CHSL/MTS/GD):** Direct factual one-liners — "Who received the Bharat Ratna in year X?", "Which is the highest peacetime gallantry award?"
- **Banking (IBPS/SBI):** Recent award winners (current-affairs linked), Nobel laureates, awards received by banks/economists.
- **Railway (NTPC/Group D):** Simple identification — award-to-field or award-to-first-recipient matching.
- **State PCS:** Awards of that particular state, plus national civilian awards.

## 4. Examiner's Traps (with Examples)

**Trap 1 — Almost Correct (लगभग-सही):** Options where three details are right and one is subtly wrong.
> Q. Which is the highest peacetime gallantry award of India?
> (a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Ashoka Chakra (c) Kirti Chakra (d) Shaurya Chakra
> Answer: (b). Param Vir Chakra is the highest *wartime* award — the word "peacetime" is the trap.

**Trap 2 — Chronological Confusion:** Bharat Ratna instituted in 1954; Padma awards also 1954 (Padma Vibhushan etc.); Arjuna Award 1961; Dronacharya Award 1985; Khel Ratna 1991–92. Exams swap years between options.

**Trap 3 — Similar Names:** Khel Ratna vs Arjuna vs Dronacharya; Kirti Chakra vs Shaurya Chakra; Jnanpith vs Sahitya Akademi. Read the full option before answering.

**Trap 4 — Negative Phrasing:** "Which of the following is NOT a civilian award?" — Param Vir Chakra is the answer, but in a hurry students pick a Padma award.

**Trap 5 — Renaming:** The Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna was renamed **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award in 2021**. Older answer keys say "Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna"; newer papers expect the new name.

## 5. High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions with Solutions

**Q1.** Who among the following was NOT among the first recipients of the Bharat Ratna in 1954?
(a) C. Rajagopalachari (b) S. Radhakrishnan (c) C.V. Raman (d) Jawaharlal Nehru
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nehru received the Bharat Ratna in 1955, not 1954. The first three recipients (1954) were Rajagopalachari, Radhakrishnan and Raman. Trap type: Chronological Confusion. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q2.** The correct descending order of India's civilian awards is:
(a) Bharat Ratna → Padma Bhushan → Padma Vibhushan → Padma Shri
(b) Bharat Ratna → Padma Vibhushan → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri
(c) Padma Vibhushan → Bharat Ratna → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri
(d) Bharat Ratna → Padma Shri → Padma Bhushan → Padma Vibhushan
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vibhushan (2nd) > Bhushan (3rd) > Shri (4th). Option (a) swaps Vibhushan and Bhushan — the classic Almost Correct trap. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q3.** The first recipient of the Jnanpith Award (1965) was:
(a) Mahadevi Verma (b) G. Sankara Kurup (c) Sumitranandan Pant (d) Ramdhari Singh Dinkar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Malayalam poet G. Sankara Kurup won the first Jnanpith for 'Odakkuzhal'. Distractors are all later Jnanpith winners — Similar Names trap. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q4.** The Dronacharya Award is given for excellence in:
(a) Sports performance (b) Sports coaching (c) Lifetime contribution to sports administration (d) Bravery
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dronacharya = coaches; Arjuna/Khel Ratna = players; Dhyan Chand Award = lifetime achievement. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q5.** Which of the following is NOT one of the six Nobel Prize categories?
(a) Physics (b) Peace (c) Mathematics (d) Literature
Answer: (c)
Solution: There is no Nobel Prize in Mathematics. Negative Phrasing trap — underline "NOT" before reading options. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q6.** The first Indian to receive the Nobel Prize was:
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Mother Teresa (d) Amartya Sen
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tagore, Literature, 1913. C.V. Raman (Physics, 1930) is the tempting wrong pick because he was the first Indian *scientist* laureate. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q7.** The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is associated with:
(a) Literature (b) Cinema (c) Journalism (d) Classical music
Answer: (b)
Solution: India's highest award in cinema; first awarded to Devika Rani in 1969. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q8.** The Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award was renamed in 2021 as:
(a) Dhyan Chand Award (b) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (c) Arjuna Award (d) Tenzing Norgay Adventure Award
Answer: (b)
Solution: Renaming trap — the Dhyan Chand *Award* (lifetime achievement) already existed separately; only the Khel Ratna was renamed. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q9.** Which gallantry award ranks immediately below the Param Vir Chakra?
(a) Ashoka Chakra (b) Maha Vir Chakra (c) Vir Chakra (d) Kirti Chakra
Answer: (b)
Solution: Wartime order: PVC > MVC > Vir Chakra. Ashoka Chakra heads the *peacetime* list — mixing the two lists is the trap. Source: PYQ-style.

**Q10.** The Ramon Magsaysay Award is named after a former president of:
(a) Indonesia (b) The Philippines (c) Thailand (d) Japan
Answer: (b)
Solution: Instituted in 1957 in memory of Philippine President Ramon Magsaysay; often called "Asia's Nobel Prize". Source: PYQ-style.

## 6. The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**15-second questions (answer instantly, don't overthink):**
- Q4 (Dronacharya = coaching) and Q7 (Phalke = cinema) are pure recall. If you know the fact, mark and move on in under 15 seconds. Re-reading such questions only invites self-doubt.

**45-second questions (slow down, the trap is in the wording):**
- Q1 and Q5 carry negative phrasing ("NOT") and near-correct options. Spend up to 45 seconds: underline "NOT", verify each option against the fact, then answer. Q2 (order of Padma awards) also deserves 45 seconds — write the order mentally (Vibhushan–Bhushan–Shri) before looking at options, so the Almost Correct option (a) cannot fool you.

**Rule of thumb:** Direct recall → 15 seconds. Negative phrasing, ordering, or "first recipient" questions → 45 seconds. Beyond 45 seconds, mark for review and move on.

## 7. Preparation Takeaways

1. Memorise the two gallantry ladders (wartime/peacetime) and the civilian award order as fixed sequences.
2. Prepare a "first recipients" list: Bharat Ratna (1954), Jnanpith (1965), Dadasaheb Phalke (1969), Arjuna Award (1961).
3. Track Nobel laureates of Indian origin with year and field.
4. Remember the 2021 Khel Ratna renaming — new papers use the new name.
5. For recent winners (current affairs), follow the app's current-affairs section; this chapter covers only the static framework.
