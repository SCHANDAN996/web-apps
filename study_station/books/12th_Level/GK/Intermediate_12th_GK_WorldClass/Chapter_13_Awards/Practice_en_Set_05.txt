101. Which award is India's highest peacetime gallantry award?
(a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Shaurya Chakra (c) Kirti Chakra (d) Ashoka Chakra
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Param Vir Chakra, but it is the highest wartime gallantry award, not peacetime. The Ashoka Chakra is awarded for valour, courageous action or self-sacrifice away from the battlefield, so the answer is Ashoka Chakra.
Source: PYQ-style

102. The Bharat Ratna was instituted in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1950 (c) 1956 (d) 1954
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students pick 1950 because the Constitution came into force then, but the Bharat Ratna was instituted on 2 January 1954, along with the Padma awards, so the answer is 1954.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Who among the following received the Bharat Ratna in 1954, its very first year?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Sardar Patel (c) C. Rajagopalachari (d) B.R. Ambedkar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Jawaharlal Nehru is the tempting wrong choice, but he received it in 1955. In 1954, the first year, the award went to C. Rajagopalachari, Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan and C.V. Raman, so the answer is C. Rajagopalachari.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Which of the following is the correct order of Padma awards from highest to lowest?
(a) Padma Vibhushan, Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan (b) Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan (c) Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Shri (d) Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is confusing Padma Bhushan with Padma Vibhushan; Vibhushan is the higher one. The correct descending order is Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri, so the answer is Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri.
Source: PYQ-style

105. The Nobel Prize is NOT awarded in which of the following fields?
(a) Physics (b) Literature (c) Mathematics (d) Peace
Answer: (c)
Solution: Students often assume every major subject has a Nobel, but Alfred Nobel's will listed only five fields: Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature and Peace; Economics was added later by Sweden's central bank. There is no Nobel in Mathematics, so the answer is Mathematics.
Source: PYQ-style

106. Who was the first Indian to win the Nobel Prize?
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Mother Teresa (c) Amartya Sen (d) Rabindranath Tagore
Answer: (d)
Solution: C.V. Raman is the tempting wrong option as the first Indian Nobel laureate in science (1930), but Rabindranath Tagore won the Literature Nobel in 1913 for Gitanjali, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: NCERT Class 10 / PYQ-style

107. The Jnanpith Award is given for excellence in which field?
(a) Literature (b) Music (c) Sports (d) Science
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Jnanpith Award, instituted in 1961, is India's highest literary honour; the first award (1965) went to Malayalam writer G. Sankara Kurup, so the answer is Literature.
Source: PYQ-style

108. Which award is given for excellence in sports coaching in India?
(a) Dronacharya Award (b) Dhyan Chand Award (c) Khel Ratna (d) Arjuna Award
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Arjuna Award tempts many, but it honours sportspersons for performance, not coaching. Named after Guru Dronacharya of the Mahabharata, the coaching award is the Dronacharya Award, so the answer is Dronacharya Award.
Source: PYQ-style

109. India's highest award in sports, renamed in 2021, is now called:
(a) Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna (b) Arjuna Award (c) Dhyan Chand Award (d) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna, its old name; in August 2021 it was renamed after hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand, so the answer is Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna.
Source: PYQ-style

110. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is associated with which field?
(a) Cinema (b) Theatre (c) Classical dance (d) Painting
Answer: (a)
Solution: It is India's highest award in cinema, instituted in 1969 in memory of Dadasaheb Phalke, the father of Indian cinema; the first recipient was Devika Rani, so the answer is Cinema.
Source: PYQ-style

111. Who was the first woman to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) M.S. Subbulakshmi (b) Mother Teresa (c) Indira Gandhi (d) Lata Mangeshkar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mother Teresa (1980) is the tempting wrong choice, but Indira Gandhi received the Bharat Ratna in 1972, earlier than her, so the answer is Indira Gandhi.
Source: PYQ-style

112. The Gandhi Peace Prize is awarded by:
(a) United Nations (b) UNESCO (c) Government of India (d) Nobel Committee
Answer: (c)
Solution: The UN connection tempts many because of its international character, but the Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted by the Government of India in 1995; its first recipient was Julius Nyerere of Tanzania, so the answer is Government of India.
Source: PYQ-style

113. The Arjuna Award is given for outstanding achievement in which field?
(a) Literature (b) Social service (c) Medicine (d) Sports
Answer: (d)
Solution: Social service tempts because many national awards honour it, but the Arjuna Award, instituted in 1961, honours sportspersons for consistent outstanding performance, so the answer is Sports.
Source: PYQ-style

114. The Pulitzer Prize is associated with which country and field?
(a) USA, Journalism (b) Sweden, Peace (c) France, Literature (d) UK, Science
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sweden tempts because of the Nobel, but the Pulitzer Prize, established by Joseph Pulitzer, is an American award for journalism, literature and musical composition, so the answer is USA, Journalism.
Source: PYQ-style

115. The Booker Prize is awarded for:
(a) Poetry in any language (b) Best novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland (c) Scientific writing (d) Journalism
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is thinking it covers all literature; the Booker Prize specifically honours the best novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland, so the answer is best novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland.
Source: PYQ-style

116. Who was the first Indian citizen to win the Booker Prize?
(a) V.S. Naipaul (b) Salman Rushdie (c) Arundhati Roy (d) Kiran Desai
Answer: (c)
Solution: Salman Rushdie tempts because he won the Booker in 1981, but he is a British citizen, not Indian; Arundhati Roy won in 1997 for "The God of Small Things" as an Indian citizen, so the answer is Arundhati Roy.
Source: PYQ-style

117. The Param Vir Chakra was first awarded posthumously to:
(a) Lt. Col. A.B. Tarapore (b) Major Somnath Sharma (c) Captain Vikram Batra (d) Major Ramaswamy Parameswaran
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vikram Batra (Kargil, 1999) is the tempting modern name, but the first Param Vir Chakra went to Major Somnath Sharma for the Battle of Badgam in 1947, so the answer is Major Somnath Sharma.
Source: PYQ-style

118. The Magsaysay Award is associated with which country?
(a) Japan (b) Thailand (c) Philippines (d) Indonesia
Answer: (c)
Solution: Named after Philippine President Ramon Magsaysay and awarded by the Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation in Manila, it is often called Asia's Nobel Prize, so the answer is Philippines.
Source: PYQ-style

119. Which Indian scientist won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930?
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Venkatraman Ramakrishnan (c) S. Chandrasekhar (d) Hargobind Khorana
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chandrasekhar tempts as another Physics laureate, but he won in 1983; C.V. Raman won in 1930 for the Raman Effect (scattering of light), so the answer is C.V. Raman.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics / PYQ-style

120. The Sahitya Akademi Award is given for books in how many languages?
(a) 24 (b) 15 (c) 18 (d) 22
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 22, the number of scheduled languages in the Eighth Schedule; the Sahitya Akademi additionally honours English and Rajasthani, making 24 languages, so the answer is 24.
Source: PYQ-style

121. Consider the following statements about the Bharat Ratna:
1. It can be awarded posthumously.
2. It is restricted to Indian citizens only.
3. The number of awards is normally restricted to three in a year.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: the Bharat Ratna is NOT restricted to Indian citizens — Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan and Nelson Mandela received it. It can be posthumous (Lal Bahadur Shastri) and is capped at three per year, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Match the awards with their fields:
1. B.C. Roy Award — Medicine
2. Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize — Science and Technology
3. Vyas Samman — Literature
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: All three are correctly matched: the B.C. Roy Award honours medical excellence, the Bhatnagar Prize honours science and technology, and the Vyas Samman honours Hindi literature, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Which of the following pairs is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) Kalinga Prize — Popularisation of science (b) Abel Prize — Mathematics (c) Grammy Award — Literature (d) Oscar — Cinema
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming all famous Western awards are literary; the Grammy honours music, not literature. The Kalinga Prize (UNESCO) is for science popularisation and the Abel Prize is for mathematics, so the answer is Grammy Award — Literature.
Source: PYQ-style

124. Arrange the following gallantry awards in descending order of precedence:
1. Maha Vir Chakra
2. Param Vir Chakra
3. Vir Chakra
(a) 1, 2, 3 (b) 2, 1, 3 (c) 3, 1, 2 (d) 2, 3, 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chronological or alphabetical confusion is the trap here; the wartime gallantry hierarchy runs Param Vir Chakra, then Maha Vir Chakra, then Vir Chakra, so the answer is 2, 1, 3.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Consider the following statements:
1. Mother Teresa received the Nobel Peace Prize before receiving the Bharat Ratna.
2. Amartya Sen received the Bharat Ratna before receiving the Nobel Prize.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 2 is the chronological trap: Amartya Sen won the Nobel in 1998 and the Bharat Ratna in 1999, so the Nobel came first. Mother Teresa won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1979 and the Bharat Ratna in 1980, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: PYQ-style
