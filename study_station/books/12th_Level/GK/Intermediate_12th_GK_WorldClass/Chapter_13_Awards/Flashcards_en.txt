Card 1
Front: Which is India's highest civilian award, and in which year was it instituted?
Back: Bharat Ratna, instituted in 1954. It is awarded for exceptional service in any field, including art, literature, science, and public service.

Card 2
Front: Name the four civilian awards of India in descending order of precedence.
Back: Bharat Ratna → Padma Vibhushan → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri. Mnemonic: "B-V-Bh-Sh" — "Bharat's Very Bright Shining" stars.

Card 3
Front: Which award is given for gallantry in the face of the enemy, and what is its highest form?
Back: Wartime gallantry awards; the highest is Param Vir Chakra (PVC), followed by Maha Vir Chakra and Vir Chakra.

Card 4
Front: What is the highest peacetime gallantry award of India?
Back: Ashoka Chakra, followed by Kirti Chakra and Shaurya Chakra. Trap alert: examiners swap "wartime" and "peacetime" — Param Vir Chakra is wartime, Ashoka Chakra is peacetime.

Card 5
Front: Which is the highest sports award of India, and what was its earlier name?
Back: Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (earlier Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna, renamed in 2021). It is given for outstanding performance over four years.

Card 6
Front: Which award honours lifetime achievement in sports, and who was its first recipient?
Back: Dhyan Chand Award (lifetime achievement in sports). First awarded in 2002; among the first recipients was Shahuraj Birajdar (boxing). Arjuna Award, by contrast, is for consistent performance over four years.

Card 7
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the order of gallantry awards — PVC, MVC, VC?
Back: "Param Maha Vir" — think of a soldier becoming Param (supreme), then Maha (great), then Vir (brave). Descending: PVC > MVC > VC; peacetime mirror: AC > KC > SC.

Card 8
Front: Which is the highest literary award of India, and who gives it?
Back: Jnanpith Award, given by the Bharatiya Jnanpith trust (instituted 1961). Trap: Sahitya Akademi Award is given by the National Academy of Letters — examiners swap the two.

Card 9
Front: Which award is called the "Nobel of Asia"?
Back: Ramon Magsaysay Award (Philippines, instituted 1957), named after Philippine President Ramon Magsaysay. Vinoba Bhave was the first Indian recipient (1958).

Card 10
Front: Who was the first Indian to win the Nobel Prize, and in which field?
Back: Rabindranath Tagore, Nobel Prize in Literature, 1913, for Gitanjali. He was also the first non-European Nobel laureate in Literature.

Card 11
Front: Name the Indian Nobel laureates in Science and their fields.
Back: C.V. Raman (Physics, 1930 — Raman Effect), Har Gobind Khorana (Medicine, 1968), Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar (Physics, 1983), Venkatraman Ramakrishnan (Chemistry, 2009).

Card 12
Front: Which two Indians have won the Nobel Peace Prize?
Back: Mother Teresa (1979) and Kailash Satyarthi (2014, shared with Malala Yousafzai). Amartya Sen won in Economics (1998) — a common trap pairing.

Card 13
Front: What is the Dadasaheb Phalke Award, and who was its first recipient?
Back: India's highest award in cinema, instituted 1969, given for lifetime contribution to Indian cinema. First recipient: Devika Rani (1969).

Card 14
Front: Examiner's trap: Bharat Ratna and Padma awards were suspended twice — when and why?
Back: Suspended 1977–80 and 1992–95 due to court challenges questioning their constitutional validity (Article 18 on titles). The Supreme Court upheld them in 1995 as not being "titles."

Card 15
Front: Which is the highest award for bravery given to children in India?
Back: National Bravery Awards (Bharat Award being the highest), given by the Indian Council for Child Welfare on Republic Day. Do not confuse with gallantry awards for armed forces.

Card 16
Front: What is the Gandhi Peace Prize, and who was its first recipient?
Back: Instituted by the Government of India in 1995 (125th birth anniversary of Gandhi) for contributions to social, economic, and political transformation through non-violence. First recipient: Julius Nyerere (1995).

Card 17
Front: Which award is given to coaches in sports, and what are its two categories?
Back: Dronacharya Award — one category for regular coaching (recent performance) and one for lifetime contribution to coaching. Mnemonic: Dronacharya taught Arjuna — coach award pairs with Arjuna Award for players.

Card 18
Front: Examiner's trap: Can the Bharat Ratna be awarded posthumously, and what about Padma awards?
Back: Yes — Bharat Ratna can be posthumous (e.g., Lal Bahadur Shastri, 1966, the first posthumous recipient). Padma awards are generally not given posthumously, but exceptions are made in highly deserving cases.

Card 19
Front: Which international prize is awarded for mathematics, often called the "Nobel of Mathematics"?
Back: Fields Medal, awarded every four years to mathematicians under 40. Note: there is no Nobel Prize in Mathematics — a classic examiner trap.

Card 20
Front: What do the Padma Vibhushan's three categories of "distinguished service" mean in the award's definition?
Back: Padma Vibhushan: exceptional and distinguished service; Padma Bhushan: distinguished service of a high order; Padma Shri: distinguished service. The wording differences are a favourite statement-based trap.
