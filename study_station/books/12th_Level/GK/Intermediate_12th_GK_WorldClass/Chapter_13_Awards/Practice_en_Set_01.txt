Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which is the highest civilian award of India?
(a) Bharat Ratna (b) Padma Vibhushan (c) Padma Bhushan (d) Padma Shri
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Padma awards are prestigious, but the Bharat Ratna, instituted in 1954, ranks above all of them in the order of precedence. So the answer is Bharat Ratna.
Source: NCERT-based GK / PYQ-style

2. The Bharat Ratna was instituted in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1954 (c) 1950 (d) 1956
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students pick 1950 because the Constitution came into force then, but the Bharat Ratna was actually instituted on 2 January 1954. So the answer is 1954.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Who was the first recipient of the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) C.V. Raman (c) Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (d) C. Rajagopalachari
Answer: (d)
Solution: In 1954, the first year, three people received it — C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan and C.V. Raman. Rajagopalachari is conventionally listed first among them. So the answer is C. Rajagopalachari.
Source: PYQ-style

4. The Padma awards are announced every year on the eve of which day?
(a) Independence Day (b) Republic Day (c) Gandhi Jayanti (d) Constitution Day
Answer: (b)
Solution: Padma awards are announced on the eve of Republic Day (26 January) and conferred by the President later at Rashtrapati Bhavan. So the answer is Republic Day.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Which is the highest gallantry award in India during wartime?
(a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Ashoka Chakra (c) Maha Vir Chakra (d) Vir Chakra
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ashoka Chakra is the highest peacetime gallantry award, which confuses many students; for wartime valour the highest is the Param Vir Chakra. So the answer is Param Vir Chakra.
Source: PYQ-style

6. The Nobel Prize is awarded in memory of Alfred Nobel, who belonged to which country?
(a) Norway (b) Switzerland (c) Denmark (d) Sweden
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Peace Prize ceremony is held in Oslo, Norway, which misleads many, but Alfred Nobel was a Swedish chemist and inventor of dynamite. So the answer is Sweden.
Source: PYQ-style

7. Who was the first Indian to win the Nobel Prize?
(a) Rabindranath Tagore (b) C.V. Raman (c) Mother Teresa (d) Amartya Sen
Answer: (a)
Solution: C.V. Raman won in 1930, but Rabindranath Tagore won the Literature Nobel in 1913 for Gitanjali, making him the first Indian laureate. So the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: NCERT Class 10 / PYQ-style

8. The Nobel Peace Prize is awarded in which city?
(a) Stockholm (b) Geneva (c) Oslo (d) Copenhagen
Answer: (c)
Solution: All other Nobel Prizes are given in Stockholm, Sweden, but the Peace Prize alone is awarded in Oslo, Norway. So the answer is Oslo.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Which award is known as the highest honour in Indian cinema, given for lifetime contribution?

10. The Bharatiya Jnanpith Award is given for excellence in which field?

11. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award is India's highest honour in which field?
(a) Sports (b) Literature (c) Cinema (d) Military service

12. The Arjuna Award is given for outstanding performance in which area?
(a) Sports (b) Classical dance (c) Journalism (d) Social service
Answer: (a)
Solution: Instituted in 1961, the Arjuna Award recognises consistent outstanding performance in sports over the preceding years. So the answer is Sports.

14. The Booker Prize is awarded for excellence in which field?
(a) Fiction writing in English (b) Journalism (c) Scientific research (d) Peace efforts

15. The Oscar Awards are given by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences of which country?
(a) United Kingdom (b) France (c) Italy (d) United States of America

16. The Dronacharya Award is given to whom?
(a) Outstanding sportspersons (b) Best universities (c) Outstanding coaches in sports (d) Retired athletes
Answer: (c)
Solution: Students often confuse it with the Arjuna Award, which is for players; the Dronacharya Award honours coaches who train outstanding sportspersons. So the answer is Outstanding coaches in sports.

17. The Gandhi Peace Prize is instituted by the Government of India for contributions towards which cause?
(a) Economic development (b) Social, economic and political transformation through non-violence (c) Environmental protection (d) Scientific innovation
Answer: (b)
Solution: Instituted in 1995 on the 125th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi, it honours contributions to social, economic and political transformation through non-violence and Gandhian methods. So the answer is Social, economic and political transformation through non-violence.

18. Which of the following is the highest peacetime gallantry award in India?
(a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Kirti Chakra (c) Shaurya Chakra (d) Ashoka Chakra
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Param Vir Chakra tempts many, but it is a wartime award; the Ashoka Chakra is the highest gallantry award for acts of valour other than in the face of the enemy. So the answer is Ashoka Chakra.

20. The Sahitya Akademi Award is given for books written in how many recognised languages?
(a) 18 (b) 22 (c) 24 (d) 26

21. Consider the following statements about the Bharat Ratna:
1. It can be awarded posthumously.
2. The number of awards is restricted to a maximum of three in a year.
3. It can be awarded only to Indian citizens.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — the Bharat Ratna is not restricted to Indian citizens; Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan and Nelson Mandela received it. Statements 1 and 2 are correct. So the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Match the following awards with their fields:
1. Bhatnagar Prize — P. Sports
2. Arjuna Award — Q. Science
3. Sangeet Natak Akademi Award — R. Performing arts
(a) 1-R, 2-P, 3-Q (b) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R (c) 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R (d) 1-Q, 2-R, 3-P
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize is for science and technology, the Arjuna Award for sports, and the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award for music, dance and drama. So the answer is 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R.

23. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of institution of these awards?
(a) Bharat Ratna – Arjuna Award – Padma Shri – Jnanpith Award (b) Padma Shri – Bharat Ratna – Jnanpith Award – Arjuna Award (c) Bharat Ratna – Padma Shri – Arjuna Award – Jnanpith Award (d) Padma Shri – Bharat Ratna – Arjuna Award – Jnanpith Award
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing the Padma Shri first; both Bharat Ratna and Padma awards began in 1954, with Bharat Ratna instituted in January 1954, followed by the Arjuna Award in 1961 and the Jnanpith Award in 1961 (first given 1965). Among the options, Bharat Ratna – Padma Shri – Arjuna Award – Jnanpith Award is the accepted order. So the answer is Bharat Ratna – Padma Shri – Arjuna Award – Jnanpith Award.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Consider the following statements:
1. The Nobel Prize in Economics was one of the original five prizes mentioned in Alfred Nobel's will.
2. Amartya Sen won the Nobel Prize in Economics in 1998.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) Neither 1 nor 2 (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 1 is a classic trap — the Economics prize was established later, in 1968, by Sweden's central bank, and was not in Nobel's original will. Statement 2 is correct. So the answer is 2 only.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (d)
Solution: Mother Teresa (1980) tempts many, but Indira Gandhi received the Bharat Ratna in 1971, making her the first woman recipient. So the answer is Indira Gandhi.
