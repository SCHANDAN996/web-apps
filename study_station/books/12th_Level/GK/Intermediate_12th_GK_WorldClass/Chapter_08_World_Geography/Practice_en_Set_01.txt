1. Which is the largest continent by area?
(a) Africa (b) Asia (c) North America (d) Europe
Answer: (b)
Solution: Africa is the second largest and often tempts students because it has the most countries, but Asia covers about 44.6 million sq km, nearly one-third of Earth's land, so the answer is Asia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

2. The Prime Meridian (0° longitude) passes through which city?
(a) Paris (b) Greenwich (c) Rome (d) Cairo
Answer: (b)
Solution: Paris had its own meridian historically, which confuses students, but the internationally accepted Prime Meridian passes through the Royal Observatory at Greenwich, London, so the answer is Greenwich.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

3. Which is the deepest ocean in the world?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Arctic Ocean (d) Pacific Ocean
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Atlantic is the busiest ocean, which misleads some, but the Pacific is both the largest and deepest, containing the Mariana Trench, so the answer is the Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

4. The Mariana Trench, the deepest point on Earth, is located in which ocean?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Southern Ocean (c) Indian Ocean (d) Pacific Ocean
Answer: (d)
Solution: Students often pick the Indian Ocean because of the Java Trench, but the Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep, about 11,000 m) lies in the western Pacific, so the answer is the Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

5. Which is the longest river in the world?
(a) Amazon (b) Yangtze (c) Nile (d) Mississippi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Amazon carries the largest volume of water, which is the classic trap, but by length the Nile (about 6,650 km) is traditionally regarded as the longest, so the answer is the Nile.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

6. The Sahara Desert is located on which continent?
(a) Asia (b) Africa (c) South America (d) Australia
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Arabian Desert in Asia sounds similar and confuses some, but the Sahara, the world's largest hot desert, spreads across North Africa, so the answer is Africa.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

7. Which is the smallest continent by area?
(a) Europe (b) Australia (c) Antarctica (d) South America
Answer: (b)
Solution: Europe looks small on maps and is a common wrong pick, but Australia (about 7.7 million sq km) is the smallest continent, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography

8. The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which of these countries?
(a) India (b) Mexico (c) Brazil (d) Egypt
Answer: (c)
Solution: Brazil is crossed by the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn, which creates the trap; the Tropic of Cancer passes through India, Mexico and Egypt but not Brazil, so the answer is Brazil.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography

9. Which strait separates Asia from North America?
(a) Malacca Strait (b) Gibraltar Strait (c) Bering Strait (d) Hormuz Strait
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gibraltar separates Europe and Africa, a frequent mix-up, but the Bering Strait separates Russia (Asia) from Alaska (North America), so the answer is the Bering Strait.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

10. Mount Everest, the highest peak above sea level, lies in which mountain range?
(a) Andes (b) Himalayas (c) Alps (d) Rockies
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Andes is the longest mountain range and tempts students, but Everest (8,849 m) stands in the Himalayas on the Nepal–China border, so the answer is the Himalayas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

11. Which is the largest desert in the world (including cold deserts)?
(a) Sahara (b) Gobi (c) Arabian Desert (d) Antarctic Desert
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Sahara is the largest hot desert and is the tempting wrong choice, but a desert is defined by low precipitation, making the Antarctic Desert the largest overall, so the answer is the Antarctic Desert.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography

12. The Equator passes through which of these countries?
(a) India (b) Australia (c) China (d) Brazil
Answer: (d)
Solution: India feels tropical and misleads many, but the Equator passes through Brazil, Ecuador and Colombia in South America, not through India, so the answer is Brazil.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography

13. Which is the largest lake in the world by area?
(a) Caspian Sea (b) Lake Superior (c) Lake Victoria (d) Lake Baikal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lake Superior is the largest freshwater lake by area, which is the trap, but the Caspian Sea, a saltwater lake, is the largest lake overall, so the answer is the Caspian Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

14. The Great Barrier Reef is located near the coast of which country?
(a) Brazil (b) South Africa (c) Japan (d) Australia
Answer: (d)
Solution: Students sometimes link coral reefs with Brazil's coast, but the Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system, lies off the north-east coast of Australia, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

15. Which ocean lies between Africa and Australia?
(a) Indian Ocean (b) Pacific Ocean (c) Atlantic Ocean (d) Arctic Ocean
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Pacific touches Australia's east coast and confuses some, but the ocean bounded by Africa to the west and Australia to the east is the Indian Ocean, so the answer is the Indian Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography

16. The Panama Canal connects which two oceans?
(a) Atlantic and Indian (b) Pacific and Indian (c) Atlantic and Arctic (d) Atlantic and Pacific
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Suez Canal connects the Mediterranean to the Red Sea (toward the Indian Ocean), which is the usual confusion, but the Panama Canal links the Atlantic and Pacific, so the answer is Atlantic and Pacific.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

17. Which is the longest mountain range in the world?
(a) Himalayas (b) Alps (c) Andes (d) Atlas
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Himalayas have the highest peaks, which tempts students, but the Andes of South America, about 7,000 km long, form the longest continental mountain range, so the answer is the Andes.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

18. The International Date Line roughly follows which longitude?
(a) 0° (b) 180° (c) 90°E (d) 90°W
Answer: (b)
Solution: 0° is the Prime Meridian and is the common mix-up, but the International Date Line roughly follows the 180° longitude in the Pacific, so the answer is 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography

19. Which country has the largest number of time zones (including overseas territories)?
(a) France (b) USA (c) Russia (d) China
Answer: (a)
Solution: Russia with 11 zones on the mainland is the tempting answer, but France, counting its overseas territories, spans 12 time zones, the most of any country, so the answer is France.
Source: PYQ-style

20. The Strait of Gibraltar connects the Mediterranean Sea with which water body?
(a) Red Sea (b) Atlantic Ocean (c) Black Sea (d) North Sea
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Red Sea connects via the Suez Canal, which misleads some, but Gibraltar links the Mediterranean Sea to the Atlantic Ocean, so the answer is the Atlantic Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

21. Consider the following statements:
1. The Amazon is the longest river in the world.
2. The Amazon carries the largest discharge of water of any river.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 1 is the examiner's trap — the Amazon is the largest by discharge but the Nile is longer, so statement 1 is wrong while statement 2 is correct, so the answer is 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

22. Arrange these oceans in decreasing order of area:
1. Pacific 2. Atlantic 3. Indian 4. Arctic
(a) 1, 2, 3, 4 (b) 2, 1, 3, 4 (c) 1, 3, 2, 4 (d) 1, 2, 4, 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is swapping the Atlantic and Indian Oceans; the correct decreasing order is Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic, so the answer is 1, 2, 3, 4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

23. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Kalahari Desert — Australia (b) Gobi Desert — South America (c) Atacama Desert — South America (d) Thar Desert — Africa
Answer: (c)
Solution: Similar desert names are the trap: the Kalahari is in Africa, the Gobi in Asia and the Thar in India; only the Atacama lies in South America (Chile), so the answer is Atacama Desert — South America.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography

24. Consider the following statements about the Tropic of Capricorn:
1. It passes through Australia.
2. It passes through India.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming India lies on both tropics; India is crossed by the Tropic of Cancer, not Capricorn, while Australia is crossed by the Tropic of Capricorn, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography

25. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Bering Strait — separates Asia and North America (b) Palk Strait — separates India and Sri Lanka (c) Malacca Strait — separates Malaysia and Sumatra (d) Bosphorus Strait — separates North and South America
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first three pairs are standard facts and look equally plausible, which is the trap; the Bosphorus separates European and Asian Turkey, while the Americas are separated by the Bering Strait, so the answer is Bosphorus Strait — separates North and South America.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography
