126. Which of the following statements about the International Date Line is correct?
(a) It is a perfectly straight line along 180° longitude (b) It passes through the Atlantic Ocean (c) It deviates from 180° longitude to avoid dividing island groups and countries (d) Crossing it westward subtracts one calendar day
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims the Date Line is perfectly straight along 180°, but it actually zigzags around places like Kiribati, Samoa, and the Aleutian Islands so that a single country is not split into two dates. It lies in the Pacific, not the Atlantic, and crossing westward adds a day, not subtracts. So the answer is that it deviates from 180° longitude to avoid dividing island groups and countries.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Practical Work in Geography)

127. Arrange the following oceans in decreasing order of area: 1. Atlantic 2. Pacific 3. Indian 4. Arctic
(a) 1, 2, 3, 4 (b) 2, 1, 3, 4 (c) 2, 3, 1, 4 (d) 2, 1, 4, 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is placing the Indian Ocean above the Atlantic; the Atlantic (about 106 million sq km) is clearly larger than the Indian Ocean (about 70 million sq km). The Pacific is the largest and the Arctic the smallest. So the answer is 2, 1, 3, 4.

128. Which strait separates Asia from North America?
(a) Bering Strait (b) Bosphorus Strait (c) Malacca Strait (d) Davis Strait
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Bosphorus is a trap because it also separates continents, but it divides Europe and Asia (Turkey), not Asia and North America. The Bering Strait separates Russia (Asia) from Alaska (North America) and connects the Arctic Ocean with the Pacific. So the answer is Bering Strait.

130. Which of the following pairs of country and capital is NOT correctly matched?

131. The Tropic of Capricorn passes through which of the following continents?
(a) Only South America and Africa (b) South America, Africa and Asia (c) Africa, Asia and Australia (d) South America, Africa and Australia
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option includes Asia, but the Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S) never touches Asia — that is the Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N). The Capricorn line crosses South America, southern Africa, and Australia. So the answer is South America, Africa and Australia.

132. Which is the deepest oceanic trench in the world, and in which ocean does it lie?
(a) Puerto Rico Trench – Atlantic Ocean (b) Java Trench – Indian Ocean (c) Mariana Trench – Pacific Ocean (d) Peru-Chile Trench – Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Peru-Chile Trench is a trap because it is real and very deep, but it lies in the Pacific, not the Atlantic, and it is not the deepest. The Mariana Trench in the western Pacific contains the Challenger Deep, the deepest known point on Earth. So the answer is Mariana Trench – Pacific Ocean.

133. Which of the following rivers does NOT flow into the Atlantic Ocean?
(a) Nile (b) Congo (c) Amazon (d) Danube
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Danube is a trap because it ends in the Black Sea, but the Black Sea connects to the Atlantic via the Mediterranean, whereas the Nile empties directly into the Mediterranean Sea, which is a marginal sea of the Atlantic system — however, in standard exam classification the Nile is treated as a Mediterranean river, not an Atlantic one, while the Congo and Amazon drain directly into the Atlantic. So the answer is Nile.

135. Which of the following statements about the equator is correct?

142. Which of the following statements about Lake Baikal is correct?
(a) It is the largest freshwater lake by surface area (b) It is the deepest lake in the world, located in Russia (c) It is located in Canada (d) It is a saltwater lake
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is confusing "largest by surface area" with "deepest": Lake Superior has the largest surface area among freshwater lakes, while Lake Baikal in Siberia, Russia, is the deepest (over 1,600 m) and holds the greatest volume of fresh water. So the answer is that it is the deepest lake in the world, located in Russia.

150. Which of the following statements about the continents is correct?
(a) Greenland is geographically part of North America (b) Greenland is a continent (c) Australia is the second smallest continent (d) Antarctica has no ice cover
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating Greenland as a continent or as part of Europe (it belongs politically to Denmark); geographically it sits on the North American tectonic plate and is considered part of North America, while Australia is the smallest continent, not the second smallest. So the answer is that Greenland is geographically part of North America.
