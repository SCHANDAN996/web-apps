Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Which is the largest hot desert in the world?
(a) Sahara Desert (b) Arabian Desert (c) Gobi Desert (d) Kalahari Desert
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Arabian Desert is large but only about one-fourth the Sahara's size, making it the tempting wrong choice. The Sahara in North Africa covers roughly 9 million sq km and is the world's largest hot desert, so the answer is Sahara Desert.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

52. The Tropic of Capricorn does NOT pass through which of the following countries?
(a) Australia (b) Brazil (c) South Africa (d) India
Answer: (d)
Solution: India is the trap here — the Tropic of Cancer, not Capricorn, passes through India, and students often confuse the two tropics. The Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S) crosses Australia, Brazil and South Africa, so the answer is India.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

53. Which ocean is the deepest in the world?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Pacific Ocean (c) Arctic Ocean (d) Indian Ocean
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Atlantic is the second largest and often picked by mistake, but the Pacific is both the largest and the deepest ocean, containing the Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep, about 11,000 m), so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

54. The 'Ring of Fire' is associated with which ocean?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Pacific Ocean (d) Arctic Ocean
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Indian Ocean is tempting because of the 2004 tsunami, but the Ring of Fire is the horseshoe-shaped belt of volcanoes and earthquakes around the Pacific Ocean basin, so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

55. Which strait separates Asia from North America?
(a) Bering Strait (b) Palk Strait (c) Malacca Strait (d) Gibraltar Strait
Answer: (a)
Solution: Gibraltar is a common wrong pick, but it separates Europe from Africa, not Asia from North America. The Bering Strait lies between Russia (Asia) and Alaska (North America), so the answer is Bering Strait.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

56. Which is the longest river in South America?
(a) Amazon (b) Paraná (c) Orinoco (d) São Francisco
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Paraná is long and hosts major dams, which misleads some students, but the Amazon is South America's longest river and carries the largest discharge of any river in the world, so the answer is Amazon.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

57. The Great Barrier Reef is located off the coast of which country?
(a) Australia (b) South Africa (c) Brazil (d) Indonesia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Indonesia has rich coral reefs, making it a tempting option, but the Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system, lies off the north-east coast of Queensland, Australia, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

58. Which of the following countries is landlocked?
(a) Thailand (b) Vietnam (c) Laos (d) Myanmar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Myanmar looks inland on some mental maps but has a long coastline on the Bay of Bengal, which traps many students. Laos is the only landlocked country in mainland Southeast Asia, so the answer is Laos.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (India: People and Economy – map work)

59. The Suez Canal connects which two water bodies?
(a) Red Sea and Persian Gulf (b) Mediterranean Sea and Black Sea (c) Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea (d) Red Sea and Arabian Sea
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Red Sea–Arabian Sea link is tempting because the canal's traffic heads toward the Arabian Sea, but the canal itself joins the Mediterranean Sea (Port Said) to the Red Sea (Suez), so the answer is Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (Fundamentals of Human Geography)

60. Which is the largest lake in the world by surface area?
(a) Lake Superior (b) Caspian Sea (c) Lake Victoria (d) Lake Baikal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lake Superior is the largest freshwater lake by area, which makes it the classic trap, but the Caspian Sea — a saltwater lake despite its name — is the largest lake overall, so the answer is Caspian Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

61. The Atacama Desert, one of the driest places on Earth, is located in which continent?
(a) Africa (b) Asia (c) Australia (d) South America
Answer: (d)
Solution: Africa is tempting because of the Sahara and Namib deserts, but the Atacama lies in northern Chile along the Pacific coast of South America, so the answer is South America.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

62. Which mountain range separates Europe from Asia?
(a) Alps (b) Caucasus only (c) Carpathians (d) Urals
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Caucasus is tempting since it also lies near the Europe–Asia boundary, but the traditional dividing range running north–south through Russia is the Ural Mountains, so the answer is Urals.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

63. The Panama Canal connects which two oceans?
(a) Atlantic and Pacific (b) Pacific and Indian (c) Atlantic and Indian (d) Arctic and Atlantic
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students who confuse Panama with Suez pick Atlantic–Indian, but the Panama Canal in Central America links the Atlantic Ocean (Caribbean side) with the Pacific Ocean, so the answer is Atlantic and Pacific.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (Fundamentals of Human Geography)

64. Which is the smallest continent by land area?
(a) Europe (b) Antarctica (c) Australia (d) South America
Answer: (c)
Solution: Europe is the trap because it is small and densely populated, but Australia (about 7.7 million sq km) is the smallest continent by land area, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

65. The river Nile empties into which sea?
(a) Red Sea (b) Arabian Sea (c) Black Sea (d) Mediterranean Sea
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Red Sea is tempting because Egypt borders it and the Nile flows nearby, but the Nile's delta drains northward into the Mediterranean Sea, so the answer is Mediterranean Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

66. Which country has the largest number of time zones?
(a) Russia (b) USA (c) China (d) France
Answer: (d)
Solution: Russia is the obvious trap with 11 time zones on its mainland, but France, counting its overseas territories, spans 12 time zones — the most of any country, so the answer is France.
Source: PYQ-style

67. The 'Midnight Sun' phenomenon can be observed in which of the following regions?
(a) Near the Equator (b) Within the Arctic Circle (c) Near the Tropic of Cancer (d) Near the Tropic of Capricorn
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tropics are tempting because of their strong sunshine, but the Midnight Sun — the sun visible at midnight in summer — occurs only within the polar circles, such as inside the Arctic Circle, so the answer is Within the Arctic Circle.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

68. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Kilimanjaro – South America (b) Vinson Massif – Asia (c) Mount McKinley (Denali) – Africa (d) Mount Elbrus – Europe
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kilimanjaro is tempting but it is in Africa (Tanzania), not South America — a classic continent-swap trap. Mount Elbrus in the Caucasus is Europe's highest peak, so the answer is Mount Elbrus – Europe.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

69. The Strait of Malacca connects which two water bodies?
(a) South China Sea and Java Sea (b) Andaman Sea (Indian Ocean) and South China Sea (Pacific) (c) Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea (d) Red Sea and Gulf of Aden
Answer: (b)
Solution: The South China Sea–Java Sea option is tempting because both are nearby, but the Malacca Strait specifically links the Andaman Sea (Indian Ocean side) with the South China Sea (Pacific side), so the answer is Andaman Sea (Indian Ocean) and South China Sea (Pacific).
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (Fundamentals of Human Geography)

70. Which is the largest island in the world?
(a) Greenland (b) Madagascar (c) Borneo (d) New Guinea
Answer: (a)
Solution: New Guinea and Borneo are huge and often misranked, but Greenland (about 2.16 million sq km) is the world's largest island — Australia is treated as a continent, so the answer is Greenland.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

71. The Pampas grasslands are found in which country?
(a) Brazil (b) South Africa (c) Argentina (d) Kazakhstan
Answer: (c)
Solution: Brazil is tempting because of its vast interior, but Brazil's grasslands are the Campos; the temperate Pampas lie mainly in Argentina (and Uruguay), so the answer is Argentina.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

72. Which of the following statements about the Dead Sea is correct?
(a) It lies between Israel and Jordan and its shore is the lowest land point on Earth (b) It is the deepest lake in the world (c) It is a freshwater lake (d) It drains into the Mediterranean Sea
Answer: (a)
Solution: The "deepest lake" claim is the trap — that title belongs to Lake Baikal, not the Dead Sea. The Dead Sea is a hypersaline lake between Israel and Jordan whose shore, about 430 m below sea level, is Earth's lowest exposed land, so the answer is that it lies between Israel and Jordan and its shore is the lowest land point on Earth.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

73. Arrange the following oceans in decreasing order of area: 1. Atlantic 2. Indian 3. Pacific 4. Arctic
(a) 1, 3, 2, 4 (b) 3, 2, 1, 4 (c) 3, 1, 2, 4 (d) 3, 1, 4, 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Placing the Indian Ocean above the Atlantic is the common error — the Atlantic (about 106 million sq km) is larger than the Indian (about 70 million sq km). The correct order is Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic, so the answer is 3, 1, 2, 4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

74. The country through which both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn pass is:
(a) Indonesia (b) Brazil (c) Kenya (d) Colombia
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kenya and Indonesia are tempting because the Equator crosses them, but neither reaches the Tropic of Capricorn. Brazil is crossed by both the Equator (north) and the Tropic of Capricorn (south), so the answer is Brazil.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

75. Which African country was formerly known as Abyssinia?
(a) Sudan (b) Ethiopia (c) Somalia (d) Eritrea
Answer: (b)
Solution: Eritrea is the trap because it was once part of the same region and gained independence only in 1993, but the historic name Abyssinia refers to Ethiopia, so the answer is Ethiopia.
Source: PYQ-style
