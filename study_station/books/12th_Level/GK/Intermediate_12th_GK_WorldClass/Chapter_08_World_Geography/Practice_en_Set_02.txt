26. Which is the largest hot desert in the world?
(a) Gobi Desert (b) Arabian Desert (c) Sahara Desert (d) Kalahari Desert
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Gobi is a cold desert, not a hot one, so it is a tempting trap. The Arabian and Kalahari deserts are hot but much smaller. The Sahara, covering most of North Africa, is the largest hot desert, so the answer is Sahara Desert.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography

27. The Great Barrier Reef is located off the coast of which country?
(a) Brazil (b) South Africa (c) Australia (d) Japan
Answer: (c)
Solution: Students often confuse it with reefs near Brazil or the Caribbean. The Great Barrier Reef lies in the Coral Sea off the north-eastern coast of Queensland, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

28. Which river carries the largest volume of water in the world?
(a) Amazon (b) Yangtze (c) Mississippi (d) Nile
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap here is the Nile, which is the longest river but not the largest by discharge. The Amazon discharges more water than the next several rivers combined, so the answer is Amazon.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

29. The Strait of Gibraltar connects the Mediterranean Sea with which water body?
(a) Red Sea (b) Black Sea (c) North Sea (d) Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Red Sea is linked to the Mediterranean by the Suez Canal, a common confusion. Gibraltar lies between Spain and Morocco and joins the Mediterranean to the open ocean, so the answer is Atlantic Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

30. Which country is known as the "Land of the Rising Sun"?
(a) Japan (b) South Korea (c) Thailand (d) China
Answer: (a)
Solution: China is sometimes guessed because of its eastern position, but the name "Nippon" (origin of the sun) belongs to Japan, which lies east of the Asian mainland, so the answer is Japan.
Source: PYQ-style

31. The Tropic of Capricorn does NOT pass through which of the following countries?
(a) Australia (b) Brazil (c) South Africa (d) India
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming both tropics cross India. India is crossed by the Tropic of Cancer, not Capricorn. The Tropic of Capricorn passes through Australia, Brazil and South Africa, so the answer is India.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

32. Which is the deepest oceanic trench in the world?
(a) Mariana Trench (b) Puerto Rico Trench (c) Java Trench (d) Peru-Chile Trench
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Peru-Chile Trench is long but shallower, a frequent mix-up. The Mariana Trench in the western Pacific contains the Challenger Deep, the deepest known point, so the answer is Mariana Trench.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

33. The "Ring of Fire" is associated with which ocean?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Pacific Ocean (d) Arctic Ocean
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Indian Ocean has tsunamis, which misleads some students. The Ring of Fire is the horseshoe-shaped belt of volcanoes and earthquakes around the Pacific plate margins, so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

34. Which of the following is the largest island in the world?
(a) Borneo (b) Greenland (c) New Guinea (d) Madagascar
Answer: (b)
Solution: New Guinea and Borneo are larger than Madagascar, which creates confusion, but Greenland is the largest island (Australia is treated as a continent), so the answer is Greenland.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography

35. The Panama Canal connects which two water bodies?
(a) Atlantic Ocean and Indian Ocean (b) Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea (c) Indian Ocean and Pacific Ocean (d) Atlantic Ocean and Pacific Ocean
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Mediterranean–Red Sea link is the Suez Canal, the classic trap pairing. Panama cuts across Central America joining the two great oceans, so the answer is Atlantic Ocean and Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

36. Which continent has the largest number of countries?
(a) Asia (b) Africa (c) Europe (d) South America
Answer: (b)
Solution: Asia feels bigger, so it is the tempting wrong choice, but Africa has 54 recognised countries, more than any other continent, so the answer is Africa.
Source: PYQ-style

37. The Atacama Desert, one of the driest places on Earth, is located in which continent?
(a) Africa (b) Asia (c) Australia (d) South America
Answer: (d)
Solution: Students often link "driest desert" with the Sahara in Africa. The Atacama lies in northern Chile along the Pacific coast, so the answer is South America.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

38. Which sea separates the Korean Peninsula from Japan?
(a) Yellow Sea (b) Sea of Japan (c) South China Sea (d) Bering Sea
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Yellow Sea lies between China and Korea, which is the near-miss trap. The water body between Korea and the Japanese islands is the Sea of Japan, so the answer is Sea of Japan.
Source: PYQ-style

39. Mount Kilimanjaro, the highest peak of Africa, is located in which country?
(a) Kenya (b) Ethiopia (c) Tanzania (d) Uganda
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kenya is the common wrong guess because the mountain is best viewed from there. The peak itself stands in north-eastern Tanzania, so the answer is Tanzania.
Source: PYQ-style

40. Which of the following rivers flows through the Grand Canyon?
(a) Missouri River (b) Colorado River (c) Columbia River (d) Rio Grande
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Rio Grande is associated with the US–Mexico border and misleads many. The Grand Canyon in Arizona was carved by the Colorado River, so the answer is Colorado River.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

41. The country known as the "Land of a Thousand Lakes" is:
(a) Finland (b) Norway (c) Sweden (d) Canada
Answer: (a)
Solution: Canada has the most lakes in absolute terms, which is the trap. The traditional nickname "Land of a Thousand Lakes" belongs to Finland, so the answer is Finland.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Which is the smallest continent by land area?
(a) Europe (b) Australia (c) Antarctica (d) South America
Answer: (b)
Solution: Europe looks small on maps and tempts students, but it is larger than Australia. Australia is the smallest continent by area, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography

43. The Bering Strait separates which two landmasses?
(a) Asia and North America (b) Europe and Asia (c) North America and South America (d) Africa and Europe
Answer: (a)
Solution: Africa and Europe are separated by the Strait of Gibraltar, a frequent confusion. The Bering Strait lies between Siberia (Asia) and Alaska (North America), so the answer is Asia and North America.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

44. Which of the following countries is landlocked?
(a) Vietnam (b) Thailand (c) Laos (d) Myanmar
Answer: (c)
Solution: All four are South-East Asian neighbours, making them easy to mix up. Vietnam, Thailand and Myanmar all have coastlines; only Laos has no sea access, so the answer is Laos.
Source: PYQ-style

45. The world's largest coral reef system is threatened mainly by:
(a) rising sea temperatures (b) volcanic eruptions (c) earthquakes (d) tsunamis
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tsunamis and earthquakes damage coasts suddenly, which sounds dramatic and tempting, but mass coral bleaching of the Great Barrier Reef is driven by warming oceans, so the answer is rising sea temperatures.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

46. Which African country was formerly known as Abyssinia?
(a) Ethiopia (b) Somalia (c) Sudan (d) Eritrea
Answer: (a)
Solution: Eritrea broke away from Ethiopia in 1993, which confuses students into picking it. Abyssinia was the historical name of the Ethiopian Empire, so the answer is Ethiopia.
Source: PYQ-style

47. The Mekong River does NOT flow through which of these countries?
(a) Cambodia (b) Malaysia (c) Vietnam (d) Laos
Answer: (b)
Solution: Because Malaysia is in South-East Asia, it is the trap option. The Mekong flows through China, Myanmar, Laos, Thailand, Cambodia and Vietnam, but not Malaysia, so the answer is Malaysia.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Which is the largest lake in the world by surface area?
(a) Lake Superior (b) Lake Baikal (c) Lake Victoria (d) Caspian Sea
Answer: (d)
Solution: Lake Superior is the largest freshwater lake by area, and Baikal is the deepest — both are classic traps. The Caspian Sea, though called a sea, is classified as the largest lake, so the answer is Caspian Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

49. The "Roaring Forties" refer to:
(a) a mountain range in South America (b) a gold rush in California (c) warm ocean currents near the equator (d) strong westerly winds in the Southern Hemisphere
Answer: (d)
Solution: The California gold rush of 1849 makes the second option very tempting. In geography, the Roaring Forties are powerful westerly winds between 40° and 50° south latitude, so the answer is strong westerly winds in the Southern Hemisphere.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

50. Which strait separates India from Sri Lanka?
(a) Malacca Strait (b) Hormuz Strait (c) Palk Strait (d) Bab-el-Mandeb
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Malacca Strait is famous but lies between Malaysia and Indonesia, far from India. The narrow channel between Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka is the Palk Strait, so the answer is Palk Strait.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography
