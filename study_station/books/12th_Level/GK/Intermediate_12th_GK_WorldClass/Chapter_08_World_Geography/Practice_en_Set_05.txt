101. Which is the largest hot desert in the world?
(a) Gobi Desert (b) Arabian Desert (c) Sahara Desert (d) Kalahari Desert
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Gobi, which is large but is a cold desert of Central Asia. The Sahara in North Africa, covering about 9 million sq km, is the largest hot desert, so the answer is Sahara Desert.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Our Environment)

102. The Tropic of Capricorn passes through which of the following continents?
(a) Asia, Africa, South America (b) Europe, Africa, Australia (c) Africa, Asia, Australia (d) Africa, Australia, South America
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is including Asia — the Tropic of Cancer passes through Asia, not the Tropic of Capricorn. The Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S) crosses Africa, Australia and South America, so the answer is Africa, Australia, South America.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

103. Which strait separates Asia from North America?
(a) Palk Strait (b) Bering Strait (c) Malacca Strait (d) Gibraltar Strait
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gibraltar is a trap — it separates Europe from Africa, not Asia from North America. The Bering Strait separates Russia (Asia) from Alaska (North America), so the answer is Bering Strait.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

104. The world's deepest oceanic trench, the Mariana Trench, is located in which ocean?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Pacific Ocean (c) Arctic Ocean (d) Indian Ocean
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Indian Ocean is a tempting distractor because it has the Java Trench, but the deepest point on Earth, the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, lies in the Pacific Ocean, so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

105. Which country is the largest producer of coffee in the world?
(a) Colombia (b) Vietnam (c) Brazil (d) Ethiopia
Answer: (c)
Solution: Colombia is the classic trap because of its famous coffee, but it ranks behind Brazil and Vietnam. Brazil has been the world's largest coffee producer for over a century, so the answer is Brazil.
Source: PYQ-style

106. The Great Barrier Reef is located off the coast of which country?
(a) Brazil (b) South Africa (c) Indonesia (d) Australia
Answer: (d)
Solution: Indonesia is a trap since it has extensive coral reefs in the Coral Triangle, but the Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system, lies off the north-east coast of Queensland, Australia, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Our Environment)

107. Which of the following rivers flows through the Grand Canyon?
(a) Mississippi (b) Columbia (c) Missouri (d) Colorado
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Mississippi is the tempting wrong choice as the most famous US river, but it flows through the central plains. The Colorado River carved the Grand Canyon in Arizona, so the answer is Colorado.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

108. The 'Ring of Fire' is associated with which ocean?
(a) Pacific Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Atlantic Ocean (d) Arctic Ocean
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Indian Ocean is a trap because of the 2004 tsunami, but the Ring of Fire is the horseshoe-shaped belt of volcanoes and earthquakes around the margins of the Pacific Ocean, so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

109. Which is the largest landlocked country in the world?
(a) Kazakhstan (b) Mongolia (c) Chad (d) Bolivia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mongolia is the common trap as it is vast and landlocked, but Kazakhstan, with about 2.7 million sq km, is the largest country with no coastline on an open ocean, so the answer is Kazakhstan.
Source: PYQ-style

110. The Suez Canal connects which two water bodies?
(a) Red Sea and Persian Gulf (b) Mediterranean Sea and Black Sea (c) Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea (d) Red Sea and Arabian Sea
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is pairing the Mediterranean with the Black Sea — that is the Bosporus. The Suez Canal (opened 1869) links the Mediterranean Sea with the Red Sea, so the answer is Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (Fundamentals of Human Geography)

111. Which of the following is the longest river in Europe?
(a) Danube (b) Rhine (c) Volga (d) Thames
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Danube is the classic trap — it is the longest river in the European Union region and passes through the most countries, but the Volga in Russia is Europe's longest river, so the answer is Volga.
Source: PYQ-style

112. The Atacama Desert, one of the driest places on Earth, is located in which continent?
(a) South America (b) Asia (c) Australia (d) Africa
Answer: (a)
Solution: Africa is the tempting wrong answer because of the Sahara, but the Atacama lies in northern Chile along the Pacific coast of South America, so the answer is South America.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Our Environment)

113. Which country has the largest number of time zones in the world?
(a) France (b) USA (c) Russia (d) China
Answer: (a)
Solution: Russia is the strong trap with 11 time zones on its mainland, but France, counting its overseas territories spread across the globe, has 12 time zones — the most of any country, so the answer is France.
Source: PYQ-style

114. The Panama Canal connects which two oceans?
(a) Atlantic and Indian (b) Pacific and Indian (c) Atlantic and Arctic (d) Atlantic and Pacific
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is confusing it with the Suez Canal, which links the Mediterranean–Red Sea route toward the Indian Ocean. The Panama Canal joins the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans across Central America, so the answer is Atlantic and Pacific.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (Fundamentals of Human Geography)

115. Which is the smallest continent by land area?
(a) Europe (b) Australia (c) Antarctica (d) South America
Answer: (b)
Solution: Europe is the tempting wrong option as it looks small on maps, but it is larger than Australia. Australia, at about 7.7 million sq km, is the smallest continent, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

116. The Strait of Malacca lies between which two landmasses?
(a) India and Sri Lanka (b) Thailand and Vietnam (c) Java and Borneo (d) Malay Peninsula and Sumatra
Answer: (d)
Solution: India and Sri Lanka is the trap — they are separated by the Palk Strait. The Malacca Strait runs between the Malay Peninsula and the Indonesian island of Sumatra, so the answer is Malay Peninsula and Sumatra.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (India: People and Economy)

117. Which African country was formerly known as Abyssinia?
(a) Sudan (b) Ethiopia (c) Somalia (d) Eritrea
Answer: (b)
Solution: Eritrea is a trap because it was once part of Ethiopia, but the ancient name Abyssinia refers to Ethiopia, one of the few African countries never formally colonised, so the answer is Ethiopia.
Source: PYQ-style

118. The 'Roaring Forties' refer to which of the following?
(a) Ocean currents in the Atlantic (b) A desert region in Australia (c) Strong westerly winds in the Southern Hemisphere (d) Warm winds of the Mediterranean
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is thinking of ocean currents, but the Roaring Forties are powerful westerly winds blowing between 40°S and 50°S latitudes, unobstructed by large landmasses, so the answer is strong westerly winds in the Southern Hemisphere.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

119. Which is the largest island in the world?
(a) Borneo (b) Greenland (c) Madagascar (d) New Guinea
Answer: (b)
Solution: Borneo and New Guinea are traps as they are huge tropical islands, but Greenland, an autonomous territory of Denmark, is the world's largest island (Australia is counted as a continent), so the answer is Greenland.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

120. The Dead Sea is bordered by which countries?
(a) Jordan and Israel (b) Egypt and Israel (c) Syria and Jordan (d) Lebanon and Israel
Answer: (a)
Solution: Egypt is a tempting wrong choice due to its proximity, but the Dead Sea, the lowest land point on Earth, lies between Jordan to the east and Israel (with the West Bank) to the west, so the answer is Jordan and Israel.
Source: PYQ-style

121. Arrange the following straits from west to east: 1. Gibraltar 2. Bosporus 3. Hormuz 4. Malacca
(a) 1, 2, 3, 4 (b) 2, 1, 3, 4 (c) 1, 3, 2, 4 (d) 2, 1, 4, 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The chronological-style trap is placing Bosporus before Gibraltar; Gibraltar is at the western entrance of the Mediterranean, Bosporus at its eastern end (Black Sea), Hormuz at the Persian Gulf, and Malacca in Southeast Asia. The west-to-east order is Gibraltar, Bosporus, Hormuz, Malacca, so the answer is 1, 2, 3, 4.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Pampas — Argentina (b) Prairies — North America (c) Veld — South Africa (d) Steppes — Australia
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that all four are temperate grasslands, so each looks plausible; but the Steppes are the grasslands of Eurasia (Russia, Ukraine, Kazakhstan), while Australia's grasslands are called Downs. The incorrectly matched pair is Steppes — Australia, so the answer is Steppes — Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Our Environment)

123. Consider the following statements about Lake Baikal: 1. It is the deepest lake in the world. 2. It is located in Russia. 3. It is a saltwater lake. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is statement 3, which sounds plausible because Baikal is so deep and ancient, but it is a freshwater lake holding about a fifth of the world's unfrozen fresh water. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

124. Which of the following countries does the Equator NOT pass through?
(a) Kenya (b) Indonesia (c) Nigeria (d) Brazil
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming all large equatorial-looking African countries lie on the Equator; the Equator crosses Kenya, Uganda, Congo and Gabon in Africa, Indonesia in Asia, and Brazil, Colombia and Ecuador in South America — but not Nigeria. The answer is Nigeria.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

125. Match the following deserts with their continents: 1. Gobi — Asia 2. Kalahari — Africa 3. Gibson — Australia 4. Sonoran — South America. Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 only (b) 1, 2 and 4 only (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the Sonoran Desert — it sounds South American but actually lies in North America (south-western USA and north-western Mexico). Gobi (Asia), Kalahari (Africa) and Gibson (Australia) are correctly matched, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3 only.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Our Environment)
