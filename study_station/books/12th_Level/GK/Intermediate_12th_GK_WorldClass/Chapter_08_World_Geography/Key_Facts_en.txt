## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Topic | Question Type | Time Rule | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Superlatives (largest, longest, highest, deepest) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Country nicknames (Rising Sun, Midnight Sun) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Straits, canals, and what they connect | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Latitudes (Tropics, Circles, Equator) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Grasslands & their regions | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Earth's layers & seismic waves | Concept + fact | 15–45 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Solstices, equinoxes, seasons | Analytical (hemisphere logic) | 45 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Longitude–time calculation (4 min/degree) | Analytical (calculation) | 45 sec | ❌ Attempt only if formula is clear |
| Ocean currents (warm/cold, effects on climate) | Analytical | 45 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Statement-based questions (multiple statements) | Analytical (each statement checked) | 45 sec | ❌ Skip if >2 statements doubtful |
| Match-the-following (lake–country, strait–location) | Fact recall in pairs | 15–30 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Negative questions ("which is NOT…") | Careful fact recall | 30 sec | ⚠️ Read twice before answering |
