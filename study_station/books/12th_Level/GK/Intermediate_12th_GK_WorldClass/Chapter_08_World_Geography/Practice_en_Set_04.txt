Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Which of the following rivers crosses the Equator twice?
(a) Nile (b) Amazon (c) Congo (d) Mekong
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Amazon is a tempting choice because it is the largest river, but it flows almost entirely in the Southern Hemisphere and never crosses the Equator. The Congo (Zaire) river flows in a great arc, crossing the Equator once going north and again going south, so the answer is Congo.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

77. The 'Ring of Fire' is associated with which ocean?
(a) Pacific Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Arctic Ocean (d) Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often pick the Indian Ocean because of the 2004 tsunami, but the Ring of Fire is the horseshoe-shaped belt of volcanoes and earthquake zones around the edges of the Pacific plate. About 75% of the world's active volcanoes lie here, so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

78. Which strait separates Asia from North America?
(a) Bering Strait (b) Malacca Strait (c) Gibraltar Strait (d) Bosphorus Strait
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Bosphorus is a trap because it also separates two continents, but it divides Europe and Asia (Turkey), not Asia and North America. The Bering Strait separates Russia (Asia) from Alaska (North America), so the answer is Bering Strait.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

79. The Great Barrier Reef is located off the coast of which country?
(a) Brazil (b) Australia (c) South Africa (d) Japan
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system, lies in the Coral Sea off the north-eastern coast of Queensland, Australia, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

80. Which is the deepest oceanic trench in the world?
(a) Java Trench (b) Peru-Chile Trench (c) Puerto Rico Trench (d) Mariana Trench
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Peru-Chile Trench is long and deep, which tempts many, but the deepest point on Earth, the Challenger Deep, lies in the Mariana Trench in the western Pacific Ocean, so the answer is Mariana Trench.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

81. Which of the following countries is landlocked?
(a) Vietnam (b) Thailand (c) Myanmar (d) Laos
Answer: (d)
Solution: Thailand is a common wrong pick, but it has a long coastline on the Gulf of Thailand. Among the South-East Asian countries listed, only Laos has no sea coast, so the answer is Laos.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (World Geography)

82. The Tropic of Capricorn passes through which of the following continents?
(a) Asia (b) South America (c) Europe (d) North America
Answer: (b)
Solution: Asia is a trap because the Tropic of Cancer passes through it; the Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S) does not. The Tropic of Capricorn crosses South America, Africa and Australia, so the answer is South America.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

83. Which is the largest desert in the world?
(a) Antarctic Desert (b) Gobi (c) Sahara (d) Arabian Desert
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sahara is the classic trap — it is the largest hot desert, not the largest desert overall. Deserts are defined by low precipitation, and the Antarctic Desert is the largest desert in the world, so the answer is Antarctic Desert.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

84. The Suez Canal connects which two water bodies?
(a) Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea (b) Red Sea and Arabian Sea (c) Mediterranean Sea and Black Sea (d) Persian Gulf and Red Sea
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Mediterranean–Black Sea link is a trap; that connection is through the Bosphorus and Dardanelles, not a canal. The Suez Canal (opened 1869) joins the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea, so the answer is Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (World Geography)

85. Which country is known as the 'Land of the Rising Sun'?
(a) China (b) South Korea (c) Japan (d) Thailand
Answer: (c)
Solution: Japan lies to the east of the Asian mainland, where the sun appears to rise first; its name 'Nippon' itself means 'origin of the sun', so the answer is Japan.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science

86. Which of the following is the correct order of the Great Lakes of North America from west to east?
(a) Michigan, Superior, Erie, Huron, Ontario (b) Ontario, Erie, Huron, Michigan, Superior (c) Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, Ontario (d) Superior, Huron, Michigan, Ontario, Erie
Answer: (c)
Solution: Chronological/positional confusion is common here; option (b) simply reverses the true order to trap careless readers. The correct west-to-east sequence is Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, Ontario, so the answer is Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, Ontario.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (World Geography)

87. The 'Pampas' grasslands are found in which country?
(a) Argentina (b) South Africa (c) Australia (d) Kazakhstan
Answer: (a)
Solution: South Africa is tempting because it has the 'Velds', a similar temperate grassland — the similar-name trap. The Pampas are the temperate grasslands of Argentina and Uruguay, so the answer is Argentina.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Life in the Temperate Grasslands)

88. Which ocean is the smallest and shallowest in the world?
(a) Indian Ocean (b) Arctic Ocean (c) Southern Ocean (d) Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Indian Ocean is sometimes wrongly chosen as 'smaller', but it is the third largest. The Arctic Ocean, around the North Pole, is the smallest and shallowest of all oceans, so the answer is Arctic Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

89. Mount Kilimanjaro, the highest peak of Africa, is located in which country?
(a) Kenya (b) Ethiopia (c) Tanzania (d) Uganda
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kenya is the tempting trap because Kilimanjaro is best viewed from the Kenyan side and lies near the Kenya border. However, the mountain itself is entirely within Tanzania, so the answer is Tanzania.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

90. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Strait of Malacca — Red Sea and Gulf of Aden (b) Strait of Hormuz — Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman (c) Strait of Gibraltar — Black Sea and Mediterranean Sea (d) Bab-el-Mandeb — Mediterranean Sea and Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (b)
Solution: The other pairs are deliberately swapped to create confusion: Malacca joins the Andaman Sea and South China Sea, Gibraltar joins the Mediterranean and Atlantic, and Bab-el-Mandeb joins the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden. Only the Hormuz pairing is correct, so the answer is Strait of Hormuz — Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (World Geography)

91. The 'Midnight Sun' phenomenon can be observed in which country?
(a) Japan (b) Norway (c) New Zealand (d) Brazil
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Midnight Sun occurs in regions north of the Arctic Circle (or south of the Antarctic Circle) where the sun does not set in summer. Norway lies partly within the Arctic Circle and is famous as the 'Land of the Midnight Sun', so the answer is Norway.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

92. Which is the longest mountain range in the world?
(a) Andes (b) Himalayas (c) Rockies (d) Alps
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Himalayas are the trap — they are the highest mountains, not the longest. The Andes of South America stretch for about 7,000 km, making them the longest continental mountain range, so the answer is Andes.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

93. Which of the following statements about the Dead Sea is correct?
(a) It is the largest freshwater lake in the world (b) It is the deepest lake in the world (c) It is a salt lake located in South America (d) It lies between Israel and Jordan and is the lowest land point on Earth
Answer: (d)
Solution: The 'deepest lake' option is a trap — that title belongs to Lake Baikal in Russia. The Dead Sea is a hypersaline lake between Israel and Jordan whose shore is the lowest exposed land on Earth, so the answer is that it lies between Israel and Jordan and is the lowest land point on Earth.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

94. The 'Downs' grasslands are found in which country?
(a) Argentina (b) USA (c) Russia (d) Australia
Answer: (d)
Solution: Argentina (Pampas) and Russia (Steppes) are similar-name traps for temperate grasslands. The temperate grasslands of Australia are called the Downs, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (Life in the Temperate Grasslands)

95. Which country has the largest number of time zones in the world?
(a) Russia (b) USA (c) France (d) China
Answer: (c)
Solution: Russia is the classic trap — it spans 11 time zones, the most for a single contiguous landmass. But France, counting its overseas territories spread across the globe, covers 12 time zones, the most of any country, so the answer is France.
Source: PYQ-style

96. The Panama Canal connects which two oceans?
(a) Atlantic and Indian (b) Pacific and Indian (c) Atlantic and Arctic (d) Atlantic and Pacific
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Panama Canal (opened 1914) cuts across the Isthmus of Panama in Central America, linking the Atlantic Ocean (Caribbean Sea) with the Pacific Ocean, so the answer is Atlantic and Pacific.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (World Geography)

97. Which of the following is the largest island in the world?
(a) Greenland (b) Madagascar (c) Borneo (d) New Guinea
Answer: (a)
Solution: Borneo and New Guinea are large and tempting, but Greenland (an autonomous territory of Denmark) is the world's largest island — Australia is treated as a continent, not an island, so the answer is Greenland.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

98. Which of the following countries does the Equator NOT pass through?
(a) Indonesia (b) India (c) Brazil (d) Kenya
Answer: (b)
Solution: Brazil is a trap because it is so large that many assume it lies entirely in the tropics south of the Equator — in fact the Equator crosses northern Brazil. India lies entirely north of the Equator (its southernmost mainland point is around 8°N), so the answer is India.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

99. The 'Sahel' region of Africa is best described as:
(a) A dense equatorial rainforest (b) A high mountain range in East Africa (c) A semi-arid transition zone between the Sahara and the savannas (d) A large freshwater lake basin
Answer: (c)
Solution: The rainforest option is a trap — that describes the Congo Basin, not the Sahel. The Sahel is the semi-arid belt stretching across Africa between the Sahara desert in the north and the savannas in the south, so the answer is a semi-arid transition zone between the Sahara and the savannas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

100. Which is the largest lake in the world by surface area?
(a) Lake Superior (b) Lake Baikal (c) Lake Victoria (d) Caspian Sea
Answer: (d)
Solution: Lake Superior is the trap — it is the largest freshwater lake by surface area, but not the largest lake overall. The Caspian Sea, despite its name, is a saltwater lake and is the largest lake in the world, so the answer is Caspian Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)
