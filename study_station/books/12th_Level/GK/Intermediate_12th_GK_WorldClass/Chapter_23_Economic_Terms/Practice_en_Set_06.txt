126. Consider the following statements about the Phillips Curve:
1. It shows a short-run inverse relationship between inflation and unemployment.
2. In the long run, the Phillips Curve is vertical at the natural rate of unemployment.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Neither 1 nor 2 (d) Both 1 and 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: Both statements are correct. The short-run Phillips Curve shows that lower unemployment is associated with higher inflation, but in the long run expectations adjust and the curve becomes vertical at the natural rate of unemployment, so the answer is both 1 and 2.

127. Which of the following best describes "stagflation"?
(a) High inflation with high economic growth (b) Low inflation with low unemployment (c) Falling prices with rising unemployment (d) High inflation combined with stagnant output and high unemployment
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is falling prices with rising unemployment, which describes deflationary recession, not stagflation. Stagflation is the unusual combination of rising prices with stagnating output and rising unemployment, as seen in the 1970s oil shocks, so the answer is high inflation combined with stagnant output and high unemployment.

130. Which of the following is an example of "transfer payment" in national income accounting?

(a) Income and consumption (b) Inflation and unemployment (c) Tax rates and tax revenue (d) Money supply and price level

138. The "paradox of thrift" suggests that:
(a) Increased saving by all individuals can reduce aggregate income and total saving in the economy (b) Saving always increases national wealth (c) Thrift leads to higher interest rates (d) Consumption has no effect on national income
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the second option, which is true for an individual but not necessarily for the whole economy.
