,# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Economic Terms and Concepts

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from to This Chapter

Economics terminology is one of the most reliable scoring areas across across SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS), Banking (IBPS, SBI PO/Clerk), Railway (NTPC, Group D) and State PCS prelims. Questions are almost always direct, definition-based or match-the-following. The examiner tests whether you can tell to similar-sounding terms apart, not whether you can write an essay.

The pattern, in words:

- **National Income family** (GDP, GNP, NNP, NDP, factor cost vs market price) is the single, most repeated cluster. Examiners love asking "which of the following includes net factor income from abroad" or "GDP at market price minus indirect taxes plus subsidies equals…".
- **Inflation types** (demand-pull, cost-push, stagflation, deflation, disinflation, creeping/g, galloping/hyperinflation) appear again and again and again, usually as "match the term with the situation".
- **Bank and terms** (repo rate, reverse repo, CSL, SLR, bank rate, MSF) are asked as definitional one-liners — "the rate at to RBI lends to banks for the short term is…". Note: the values of these rates change; exams that ask values date the question, so learn the definitions permanently and the values only close to your exam.
- **Budget terms (fiscal deficit, revenue deficit, primary deficit, effective revenue deficit) are a favourite of Banking and SSC CGL — especially the formulas and "which deficit is fiscal minus interest payments".
- **Poverty & unemployment measures** (pline, Tendulkar/Rajan committees, types of unemployment — disguised, structural, frictional, seasonal) recur in SSC and Railway.
- **Trade terms** (balance of trade vs balance of payments, devaluation vs depreciation, FDI vs FII) appear as classic "difference between" traps.
- **Tax terms** (direct vs indirect tax, GST, progressive vs regressive, ad valorem vs specific duty) are steady performers in all exams.
- **Market structures** ( monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition, perfect) appear more Banking and CGL than in Group D.

Exam-wise flavour ( words, not numbers):

- **SSC CGL / CHSL**: definitions + committee names + "which of the following is NOT to negative questions.
- **Bank (IBPS/SBI)**: monetary policy tools, budget deficits, Basel III, financial terms (NPA, SARA, priority sector).
- **Rail NT. / Group D**: the simplest layer — full forms, basic definitions, "who coins the term to style.
- **State PCS**: poverty lines, planning history (NITI Aayog vs Planning Commission), five-year plan facts.
