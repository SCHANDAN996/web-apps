Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Which river forms the boundary between Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh for a considerable stretch?
(a) Betwa (b) Chambal (c) Ken (d) Son
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Son tempts because it also flows along state borders, but it mainly separates the Kaimur range region of Bihar and Uttar Pradesh before joining the Ganga. The Chambal flows along the Uttar Pradesh–Madhya Pradesh boundary for a long stretch before entering Rajasthan, so the answer is Chambal.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

52. The river Luni, which disappears into the Rann of Kutch, originates from which hills?
(a) Western Ghats (b) Vindhya hills (c) Satpura hills (d) Aravalli hills near Ajmer
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Vindhyas are tempting because many peninsular rivers rise there, but the Luni rises in the Aravalli range near Ajmer (from the Nag Pahar) and flows southwest into the Rann of Kutch. Its water turns saline after Balotra, so the answer is Aravalli hills near Ajmer.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

53. Which of the following rivers does NOT form a delta at its mouth?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Narmada (c) Godavari (d) Krishna
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Mahanadi, Godavari and Krishna are east-flowing rivers that build deltas on the Bay of Bengal coast. The Narmada is a west-flowing rift-valley river that forms an estuary, not a delta, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

54. The Tungabhadra river is formed by the confluence of which two rivers?
(a) Tunga and Bhadra (b) Tunga and Koyna (c) Bhadra and Hemavati (d) Tunga and Netravati
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hemavati is tempting because it is a well-known Karnataka river, but it is a tributary of the Kaveri, not of the Tungabhadra. The Tunga and Bhadra rise in the Western Ghats of Karnataka and unite at Koodli to form the Tungabhadra, so the answer is Tunga and Bhadra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

55. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar"?
(a) Kosi (b) Gandak (c) Bagmati (d) Kamla
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Gandak and Bagmati also flood Bihar, which makes them tempting, but the Kosi is called the Sorrow of Bihar because of its frequent course shifts and devastating floods. The Damodar, by contrast, was called the Sorrow of Bengal, so the answer is Kosi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

56. The Indus river enters India (Ladakh) near which place?
(a) Demchok (b) Leh (c) Kargil (d) Dras
Answer: (a)
Solution: Leh is tempting because it is the best-known town on the Indus in Ladakh, but the river enters Indian territory near Demchok in southeastern Ladakh after flowing northwest from its source near Lake Mansarovar. It then flows past Leh, so the answer is Demchok.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

57. Which of the following is the correct west-to-east sequence of these peninsular rivers at their mouths?
(a) Krishna – Godavari – Mahanadi – Kaveri (b) Mahanadi – Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri (c) Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri – Mahanadi (d) Kaveri – Krishna – Godavari – Mahanadi
Answer: (d)
Solution: This is a chronological/positional trap: the Godavari is the longest peninsular river, so many place it first, but length does not fix position. From south to north (equivalently west-to-east along the coast order asked) the mouths run Kaveri (Tamil Nadu), Krishna (Andhra), Godavari (Andhra), Mahanadi (Odisha), so the answer is Kaveri – Krishna – Godavari – Mahanadi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

58. The river Brahmaputra is known by which name in Tibet?
(a) Siang (b) Dihang (c) Tsangpo (d) Lohit
Answer: (c)
Solution: Siang and Dihang are tempting because they are also names of the Brahmaputra, but they apply in Arunachal Pradesh, not Tibet. In Tibet the river is called the Tsangpo, which becomes the Siang/Dihang in Arunachal and the Brahmaputra in Assam, so the answer is Tsangpo.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

59. Which river flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges?
(a) Tapi (b) Narmada (c) Mahi (d) Sabarmati
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Tapi is tempting because it too flows west in a rift valley, but it lies south of the Satpuras, between the Satpura and the Ajanta ranges. The Narmada flows west through the rift valley bounded by the Vindhyas to the north and the Satpuras to the south, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

60. The Bhakra Nangal dam is built on which river?
(a) Sutlej (b) Beas (c) Ravi (d) Chenab
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Beas is tempting because the Beas–Sutlej link project connects the two rivers, but the Bhakra Nangal dam stands on the Sutlej in Himachal Pradesh, creating the Gobind Sagar reservoir. The Pong dam is on the Beas, so the answer is Sutlej.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

61. Which of the following rivers is a tributary of the Ganga that joins it from the south (right bank)?
(a) Ghaghara (b) Gandak (c) Kosi (d) Son
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Ghaghara, Gandak and Kosi are tempting because all are major Ganga tributaries, but all three join from the north (left bank), rising in the Himalayas. The Son rises in the Amarkantak plateau and joins the Ganga from the south near Patna, so the answer is Son.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

62. The Hirakud dam, one of the longest dams in the world, is built across which river?
(a) Krishna (b) Godavari (c) Mahanadi (d) Brahmani
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Brahmani is tempting because it is also an Odisha river, but the Hirakud dam is built across the Mahanadi near Sambalpur in Odisha. It is among the longest earthen dams in the world, so the answer is Mahanadi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

63. Which river is called the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Krishna (b) Kaveri (c) Tungabhadra (d) Godavari
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kaveri is tempting because of its great religious importance in the south, but the title Dakshin Ganga belongs to the Godavari because it is the longest river of peninsular India with the largest peninsular basin. It rises at Trimbakeshwar near Nashik, so the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

64. The Jhelum river flows through which famous valley and city?
(a) Kullu valley, Manali (b) Kangra valley, Dharamshala (c) Kashmir valley, Srinagar (d) Tawi valley, Jammu
Answer: (c)
Solution: Jammu is tempting because the Jhelum is a Jammu & Kashmir river, but Jammu city stands on the Tawi. The Jhelum flows through the Kashmir valley and passes through Srinagar, so the answer is Kashmir valley, Srinagar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

65. Which of the following pairs of river and origin is correctly matched?
(a) Chambal – Amarkantak (b) Ganga – Gangotri glacier as the Ganga (c) Yamuna – Yamunotri glacier (d) Betwa – Mahabaleshwar
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Gangotri option is the trap: the stream rising from the Gangotri glacier is the Bhagirathi, which becomes the Ganga only after meeting the Alaknanda at Devprayag. The Yamuna does rise from the Yamunotri glacier, so the answer is Yamuna – Yamunotri glacier.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

66. The Sardar Sarovar dam is located in which state and on which river?
(a) Madhya Pradesh, Chambal (b) Maharashtra, Tapi (c) Gujarat, Narmada (d) Rajasthan, Mahi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Madhya Pradesh is tempting because most of the Narmada flows through it and the project benefits it greatly, but the Sardar Sarovar dam itself is located at Kevadia in Gujarat on the Narmada river, so the answer is Gujarat, Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

67. Which river forms the famous Jog (Gersoppa) Falls?
(a) Kaveri (b) Kali (c) Netravati (d) Sharavati
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kaveri is tempting because it forms the famous Shivanasamudra falls, but the Jog Falls, among the highest plunge waterfalls in India, are formed by the Sharavati river in Karnataka, so the answer is Sharavati.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

68. The confluence of the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi rivers, where the Ganga is formally born, takes place at:
(a) Rudraprayag (b) Karnaprayag (c) Devprayag (d) Vishnuprayag
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rudraprayag is tempting because it is also a famous Alaknanda confluence, but there the Alaknanda meets the Mandakini. The Bhagirathi joins the Alaknanda at Devprayag, and from that point the combined stream is called the Ganga, so the answer is Devprayag.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

69. Which of the following rivers flows into the Arabian Sea?
(a) Subarnarekha (b) Penna (c) Rushikulya (d) Periyar
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Subarnarekha, Penna and Rushikulya are tempting because all are well-known east-coast rivers, but all three drain into the Bay of Bengal. The Periyar of Kerala flows westward into the Arabian Sea, so the answer is Periyar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

70. The Tehri dam, one of the tallest dams in India, is built on which river?
(a) Alaknanda (b) Bhagirathi (c) Mandakini (d) Yamuna
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Alaknanda is tempting because the dam lies close to the Alaknanda system in Uttarakhand, but the Tehri dam is built on the Bhagirathi river near Tehri, just upstream of its confluence zone, so the answer is Bhagirathi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

71. Which river is the largest tributary of the Ganga by volume of water?
(a) Yamuna (b) Ghaghara (c) Kosi (d) Son
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Yamuna is tempting because it is the longest tributary of the Ganga, but by discharge the Ghaghara (Karnali in Nepal) carries the greatest volume of water among Ganga tributaries. Length and volume are different measures, so the answer is Ghaghara.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

72. The city of Guwahati is situated on the banks of which river?
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Barak (c) Manas (d) Subansiri
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Barak is tempting because it is the other great river system of Assam, but it flows through the Barak valley in southern Assam. Guwahati, the largest city of Assam, stands on the south bank of the Brahmaputra, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

73. Which of the following statements about the Kaveri river is correct?
(a) It rises in the Amarkantak plateau (b) It rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Karnataka (c) It forms an estuary at its mouth (d) It flows through only one state
Answer: (b)
Solution: Amarkantak is the trap because it is the source of the Narmada and Son, not the Kaveri. The Kaveri rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Kodagu (Karnataka), flows through Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, and forms a delta, so the answer is that it rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Karnataka.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

74. The Rihand river, on which the Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar reservoir is built, is a tributary of:
(a) Son (b) Ganga (c) Yamuna (d) Chambal
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ganga is tempting because the Rihand's waters ultimately reach the Ganga, but it does so indirectly. The Rihand joins the Son in Uttar Pradesh, and the Son then joins the Ganga, so the answer is Son.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

75. Which river is associated with the Dhuandhar Falls near Jabalpur?
(a) Narmada (b) Tapi (c) Betwa (d) Ken
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ken is tempting because it also flows through the Jabalpur–Bundelkhand region, but the Dhuandhar Falls, where the river plunges through marble rocks at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur, are on the Narmada, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)
