101. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar"?
(a) Gandak (b) Son (c) Bagmati (d) Kosi
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Gandak and Bagmati also flood Bihar, which makes them tempting, but the Kosi is infamous for frequently shifting its course westward and causing devastating floods, earning it the title "Sorrow of Bihar". So the answer is Kosi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

102. The Tungabhadra river is formed by the confluence of which two rivers?
(a) Varada and Hagari (b) Tunga and Netravati (c) Bhadra and Hemavati (d) Tunga and Bhadra
Answer: (d)
Solution: Hemavati is a tributary of the Kaveri and Netravati is a separate west-flowing river, so those pairs are traps. The Tungabhadra is formed near Koodli in Karnataka by the union of the Tunga and the Bhadra, both rising in the Western Ghats. So the answer is Tunga and Bhadra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

103. Which of the following rivers flows through a rift valley?
(a) Narmada (b) Godavari (c) Krishna (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Godavari and Krishna flow through normal erosional valleys on the Deccan plateau, which is the common confusion. The Narmada flows westward through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges, formed by faulting. So the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

104. The river Luni drains into which of the following?
(a) Arabian Sea (b) Rann of Kutch (c) Gulf of Khambhat (d) It is a tributary of the Chambal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many assume every Rajasthan river reaches the Arabian Sea, but the Luni's water turns saline and it loses itself in the marshy Rann of Kutch without reaching the sea. So the answer is Rann of Kutch.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

105. Which state is drained by the maximum number of major east-flowing peninsular rivers?
(a) Odisha (b) Karnataka (c) Andhra Pradesh (d) Tamil Nadu
Answer: (c)
Solution: Odisha is tempting because the Mahanadi and Brahmani flow through it, but the Godavari, Krishna and Penna all drain through Andhra Pradesh, giving it the largest share of major east-flowing peninsular rivers. So the answer is Andhra Pradesh.

106. The Chambal river is a tributary of which river?
(a) Ganga (b) Betwa (c) Son (d) Yamuna
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Chambal ultimately joins the Ganga system, which tempts many to pick Ganga directly, but it first merges with the Yamuna near Etawah in Uttar Pradesh. So the answer is Yamuna.

107. Which river forms the boundary between Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh for a considerable stretch?
(a) Betwa (b) Ken (c) Son (d) Chambal
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Betwa and Ken are tributaries of the Yamuna flowing through Bundelkhand, which creates confusion. The Chambal forms part of the boundary between Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh (and also between MP and Rajasthan). So the answer is Chambal.

108. The Hirakud Dam, one of the longest dams in the world, is built on which river?
(a) Godavari (b) Mahanadi (c) Damodar (d) Brahmani
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Damodar has famous dams like Maithon and Panchet, which is the trap, but the Hirakud Dam near Sambalpur in Odisha is built across the Mahanadi. So the answer is Mahanadi.

109. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Beas (b) Ravi (c) Sutlej (d) Chenab
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chenab is tempting because it flows through Jammu & Kashmir near the border, but it forms in Himachal Pradesh by the union of the Chandra and Bhaga. The Sutlej rises near Lake Mansarovar in Tibet and enters India through the Shipki La pass. So the answer is Sutlej.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

110. The river known as "Dihang" in Arunachal Pradesh is called what in Assam?
(a) Lohit (b) Subansiri (c) Brahmaputra (d) Manas
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Lohit and Subansiri are tributaries that join the main river in Assam, which is the confusion. The Tsangpo of Tibet becomes the Dihang (Siang) in Arunachal Pradesh and is called the Brahmaputra after entering the Assam valley. So the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

111. Which is the largest river island in the world, located in the Brahmaputra?
(a) Sagar Island (b) Srirangapatna (c) Majuli (d) Divar Island
Answer: (c)
Solution: Srirangapatna is a famous river island but it lies in the Kaveri and is much smaller, which is the trap. Majuli in Assam, formed by the Brahmaputra and its channels, is recognised as the world's largest inhabited river island. So the answer is Majuli.

112. The Kaveri river originates at which place?
(a) Mahabaleshwar (b) Talakaveri (c) Amarkantak (d) Trimbakeshwar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Trimbakeshwar is the tempting option because it is the source of the Godavari, another major peninsular river. The Kaveri rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Kodagu district, Karnataka. So the answer is Talakaveri.

113. Which river is associated with the Dhuandhar Falls near Jabalpur?
(a) Tapi (b) Betwa (c) Narmada (d) Chambal
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chambal has famous ravines, not this waterfall, and the Tapi flows further south, so both are traps. The Dhuandhar Falls, where the river plunges through marble rocks at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur, is on the Narmada. So the answer is Narmada.

114. The Indus Water Treaty (1960) between India and Pakistan was brokered by which organisation?
(a) United Nations (b) World Bank (c) International Court of Justice (d) Commonwealth Secretariat
Answer: (b)
Solution: The United Nations is the natural guess for any international treaty, which is exactly the trap. The Indus Waters Treaty of 1960 was negotiated and brokered by the World Bank (then the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development). So the answer is World Bank.

115. Which of the following is the correct sequence of the following rivers from north to south?
(a) Godavari – Krishna – Penna – Kaveri (b) Krishna – Godavari – Penna – Kaveri (c) Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri – Penna (d) Krishna – Godavari – Kaveri – Penna
Answer: (a)
Solution: The common error is placing the Penna north of the Krishna or south of the Kaveri. The correct north-to-south order of the east-flowing peninsular rivers is Godavari, Krishna, Penna and then Kaveri. So the answer is Godavari – Krishna – Penna – Kaveri.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

116. The Sardar Sarovar Dam is located in which state?

Solution: Most of the Narmada flows through Madhya Pradesh, which makes that option tempting, but the Sardar Sarovar Dam on the Narmada is situated near Kevadia in Gujarat. So the answer is Gujarat.

117. Which river is called the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Krishna (b) Kaveri (c) Mahanadi (d) Godavari
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kaveri is often guessed because of its religious importance in the south, which is the trap. The Godavari, being the longest peninsular river with the largest peninsular basin, is called the Dakshin Ganga or Vriddha Ganga. So the answer is Godavari.

119. Which of the following rivers forms an estuary instead of a delta?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Godavari (c) Narmada (d) Krishna
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Mahanadi, Godavari and Krishna all build large deltas on the east coast, which is the confusion. The west-flowing Narmada empties into the Gulf of Khambhat through an estuary, as the strong tidal action prevents delta formation. So the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

120. The Teesta river, a major tributary of the Brahmaputra system, originates in which state?
(a) Sikkim (b) West Bengal (c) Arunachal Pradesh (d) Assam
Answer: (a)
Solution: West Bengal is tempting because the Teesta flows through it and joins the Brahmaputra system in Bangladesh, but the river originates from the Tso Lhamo lake region in North Sikkim. So the answer is Sikkim.

121. Consider the following statements about the Tapi river:
1. It flows through a rift valley like the Narmada.
2. It originates at Multai in the Betul district of Madhya Pradesh.
3. It drains into the Gulf of Khambhat.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap lies in assuming the Tapi differs from the Narmada in origin or mouth; in fact it parallels the Narmada closely. The Tapi rises at Multai in Betul district, flows west through a rift valley between the Satpura ranges, and empties into the Gulf of Khambhat. So the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

122. Consider the following pairs of tributaries and their parent rivers:
1. Indravati – Godavari
2. Bhima – Krishna
3. Hemavati – Kaveri
4. Pranhita – Mahanadi
How many pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) Only three (b) Only two (c) Only one (d) All four
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is pair 4: the Pranhita sounds like a Mahanadi tributary but it is actually the largest tributary of the Godavari, formed by the Wardha and Wainganga. The Indravati joins the Godavari, the Bhima joins the Krishna, and the Hemavati joins the Kaveri, so three pairs are correct. So the answer is only three.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

123. Consider the following statements about the Ganga river system:
1. The Ganga is formed at Devprayag by the confluence of the Alaknanda and the Bhagirathi.
2. The Ramganga joins the Ganga upstream of the Yamuna confluence at Prayagraj.
3. The Ghaghara is the largest tributary of the Ganga by volume of water.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap because many assume the Yamuna joins first; actually the Ramganga meets the Ganga near Kannauj, well before Prayagraj. The Ganga forms at Devprayag, and the Ghaghara carries more water than any other Ganga tributary. So the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

124. Arrange the following confluences (prayags) of the Alaknanda from upstream to downstream:
1. Rudraprayag 2. Karnaprayag 3. Devprayag 4. Vishnuprayag
(a) 4 – 2 – 1 – 3 (b) 2 – 4 – 1 – 3 (c) 4 – 1 – 2 – 3 (d) 2 – 1 – 4 – 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chronological (here, downstream) confusion is the classic trap with the Panch Prayag. Moving downstream, the Alaknanda first meets the Dhauliganga at Vishnuprayag, then the Pindar at Karnaprayag, then the Mandakini at Rudraprayag, and finally the Bhagirathi at Devprayag. So the answer is 4 – 2 – 1 – 3.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Consider the following statements:
1. The Barak river rises in Manipur and flows through Assam before entering Bangladesh.
2. In Bangladesh, the Barak splits into the Surma and the Kushiyara, which later rejoin the Meghna system.
3. The Barak is a tributary of the Brahmaputra within Indian territory.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: because the Barak drains into the Meghna, which later joins the Padma (Ganga) system, it is often wrongly described as a Brahmaputra tributary; within India it never joins the Brahmaputra. The Barak rises in the Manipur hills, flows through the Barak valley of Assam, and splits into the Surma and Kushiyara in Bangladesh. So the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)
