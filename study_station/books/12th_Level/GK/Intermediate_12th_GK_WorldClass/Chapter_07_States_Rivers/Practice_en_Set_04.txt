76. Which river forms the boundary between the states of Uttar Pradesh and Bihar for a considerable stretch?
(a) Gandak (b) Kosi (c) Son (d) Ghaghara
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Gandak and Kosi tempt many students because both are famous Bihar rivers, but they flow entirely within Bihar after entering India. The Ghaghara (Sarayu) flows along the UP–Bihar border for a long stretch before joining the Ganga near Chhapra, so the answer is Ghaghara.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

77. The Tungabhadra river is formed by the confluence of which two rivers?
(a) Tunga and Koyna (b) Bhadra and Hemavati (c) Tunga and Bhadra (d) Tunga and Hemavati
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Hemavati is a tributary of the Kaveri, not of the Tungabhadra, which is the common trap here. The Tunga and Bhadra rise in the Western Ghats of Karnataka and meet at Koodli to form the Tungabhadra, so the answer is Tunga and Bhadra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

78. Which of the following rivers does NOT flow through Rajasthan?
(a) Betwa (b) Luni (c) Chambal (d) Banas
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chambal is tempting because it is associated with Madhya Pradesh, but it flows through Rajasthan too and forms part of the MP–Rajasthan boundary. The Betwa flows through Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh only, joining the Yamuna, so the answer is Betwa.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

79. The river Kaveri originates at Talakaveri in which state?
(a) Kerala (b) Karnataka (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tamil Nadu is the tempting wrong choice because the Kaveri is culturally the river of Tamil Nadu, but origin and association are different things. Talakaveri lies in the Brahmagiri hills of Kodagu district, Karnataka, so the answer is Karnataka.

80. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Odisha"?
(a) Brahmani (b) Baitarani (c) Mahanadi (d) Subarnarekha
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Brahmani is a frequent distractor because it is also a major Odisha river, but the "sorrow" title belongs to the river whose floods historically devastated the Odisha plains. The Mahanadi's floods earned it the title "Sorrow of Odisha", so the answer is Mahanadi.

81. The Indus river enters India (Ladakh) near which place?
(a) Skardu (b) Leh (c) Kargil (d) Demchok
Answer: (d)
Solution: Leh is the tempting answer because it is the most famous town on the Indus in Ladakh, but the river is already inside India when it reaches Leh. The Indus enters Indian territory near Demchok in Ladakh, so the answer is Demchok.

82. Which of the following is the correct west-to-east sequence of these peninsular rivers?
(a) Mahanadi – Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri (b) Kaveri – Krishna – Godavari – Mahanadi (c) Krishna – Godavari – Kaveri – Mahanadi (d) Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri – Mahanadi
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first option is the trap because it lists the rivers roughly by basin size, not by geography. Moving from west to east along the peninsula's east-flowing rivers, the order is Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri and then Mahanadi in the north-east, so the answer is Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri – Mahanadi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

83. The Chambal river joins the Yamuna in which state?
(a) Uttar Pradesh (b) Madhya Pradesh (c) Rajasthan (d) Bihar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Madhya Pradesh tempts students because most of the Chambal's course and its famous ravines lie there, but the question asks where it joins the Yamuna. The confluence takes place near Etawah in Uttar Pradesh, so the answer is Uttar Pradesh.

84. Which river flows through the Kashmir valley and is known as its lifeline, passing through Srinagar?
(a) Ravi (b) Chenab (c) Jhelum (d) Beas
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chenab is the tempting option because it is the largest river of the region by volume, but it does not flow through the Kashmir valley itself. The Jhelum flows through the Kashmir valley, passing Srinagar, and is called its lifeline, so the answer is Jhelum.

85. The Subarnarekha river flows through which of the following groups of states?
(a) Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha (b) Bihar, Jharkhand, West Bengal (c) Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Odisha (d) West Bengal, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: The second option is the trap because Bihar borders Jharkhand and students assume the river passes through it, but it does not. The Subarnarekha rises in Jharkhand (Ranchi plateau) and flows through West Bengal and Odisha before meeting the Bay of Bengal, so the answer is Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

86. Which of the following rivers forms an estuary instead of a delta?
(a) Godavari (b) Narmada (c) Krishna (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Godavari tempts students as the largest peninsular river, but large east-flowing rivers build deltas, not estuaries. The west-flowing Narmada empties into the Gulf of Khambhat through an estuary, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

87. The river Gandak is known by which name in Nepal?
(a) Trishuli (b) Kali Gandaki (c) Arun (d) Karnali
Answer: (b)
Solution: Karnali is the tempting wrong choice because it is also a major Nepal river, but it is the Nepali name linked to the Ghaghara system, not the Gandak. The Gandak is called Kali Gandaki in Nepal, famous for its deep gorge, so the answer is Kali Gandaki.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

88. Which state is drained by the maximum number of major east-flowing peninsular rivers?
(a) Andhra Pradesh (b) Odisha (c) Karnataka (d) Tamil Nadu
Answer: (a)
Solution: Odisha is tempting because the Mahanadi, Brahmani and Baitarani all flow through it, but Andhra Pradesh receives the Godavari, Krishna and Penna along with parts of other basins. Andhra Pradesh is drained by the largest number of major east-flowing peninsular rivers, so the answer is Andhra Pradesh.

89. The Bhagirathi and Alaknanda meet at which place to form the Ganga?
(a) Rudraprayag (b) Vishnuprayag (c) Karnaprayag (d) Devprayag
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rudraprayag is the classic trap because it is also a famous confluence (Alaknanda–Mandakini), and students confuse the prayags. The Bhagirathi joins the Alaknanda at Devprayag, where the river first takes the name Ganga, so the answer is Devprayag.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

90. Which river forms part of the boundary between Maharashtra and Telangana before joining the Godavari as the Pranhita?
(a) Tapi (b) Narmada (c) Wardha (d) Wainganga
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Narmada tempts students because it is central India's biggest river, but it flows through Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat, far from the Maharashtra–Telangana border. The Wardha unites with the Wainganga to form the Pranhita, which flows along the Maharashtra–Telangana boundary before joining the Godavari, so the answer is Wardha.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

91. The Teesta river, a major tributary of the Brahmaputra, originates in which state?
(a) West Bengal (b) Sikkim (c) Arunachal Pradesh (d) Assam
Answer: (b)
Solution: West Bengal is the tempting answer because the Teesta is heavily discussed there due to water-sharing issues, but origin and downstream importance differ. The Teesta rises from the Tso Lhamo lake region in Sikkim, so the answer is Sikkim.

92. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Sabarmati – Madhya Pradesh (b) Mahi – Rajasthan and Gujarat (c) Luni – Gujarat only (d) Banas – Punjab
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Sabarmati option is the trap because the river is associated with Gujarat (Ahmedabad), though it rises in Rajasthan — it is not a Madhya Pradesh river at all. The Mahi rises in Madhya Pradesh and flows through Rajasthan and Gujarat, so the answer is Mahi – Rajasthan and Gujarat.

93. Which river flows through the maximum number of Indian states along its main course?
(a) Ganga (b) Narmada (c) Brahmaputra (d) Godavari
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Godavari is the tempting choice because its basin spreads over many states, but the question is about the states the river itself flows through, not its basin. The Ganga flows through Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand and West Bengal — five states, more than any other river's main course, so the answer is Ganga.

94. Which river is also known as the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Kaveri (c) Krishna (d) Godavari
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kaveri is the tempting option because of its sacred status in the South, but the title is based on size and length, not sanctity. The Godavari, being the longest peninsular river, is called the Dakshin Ganga, so the answer is Godavari.

95. The confluence of the Alaknanda and Mandakini rivers takes place at:
(a) Nandaprayag (b) Devprayag (c) Rudraprayag (d) Karnaprayag
Answer: (c)
Solution: Devprayag is the trap because it is the most famous prayag (Bhagirathi–Alaknanda), and students apply it to every confluence question. The Mandakini joins the Alaknanda at Rudraprayag, so the answer is Rudraprayag.

96. Which of the following rivers flows through a rift valley?
(a) Kaveri (b) Tapi (c) Krishna (d) Penna
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Kaveri tempts students as a major southern river, but it flows over the Deccan plateau in a normal valley. The Tapi, like the Narmada, flows westward through a rift valley between the Satpura and Ajanta ranges, so the answer is Tapi.

(a) Kali Gandaki (b) Seti (c) Bheri (d) Karnali
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kali Gandaki is the deliberate trap because it is the Nepali name of the Gandak, and examiners exploit the similarity between Gandak and Ghaghara. The Ghaghara is called the Karnali in Nepal, so the answer is Karnali.

100. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Sutlej (b) Jhelum (c) Ravi (d) Beas
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Jhelum is the tempting wrong answer because it is associated with Pakistan, but it originates at Verinag in Jammu & Kashmir, India. The Sutlej rises near Lake Mansarovar in Tibet (China) and enters India through Himachal Pradesh, so the answer is Sutlej.
