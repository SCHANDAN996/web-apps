126. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bengal"?
(a) Kosi (b) Damodar (c) Hooghly (d) Mahananda
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Kosi is the tempting wrong choice because it is also called a "sorrow", but it is the "Sorrow of Bihar", not Bengal. The Damodar, flowing through Jharkhand and West Bengal, earned the title "Sorrow of Bengal" for its devastating floods, so the answer is Damodar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

127. The river known as the "Sorrow of Bihar" is:
(a) Kosi (b) Son (c) Bagmati (d) Gandak
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Damodar is called the "Sorrow of Bengal" and is a common trap here, but it flows through Jharkhand and West Bengal. The Kosi, notorious for shifting its course and causing devastating floods in north Bihar, is called the "Sorrow of Bihar", so the answer is Kosi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

128. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Beas (b) Sutlej (c) Chenab (d) Ravi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Beas, Ravi and Chenab all rise in the Himalayas within Himachal Pradesh, so they are Indian-origin rivers. The Sutlej originates from the Rakshastal–Mansarovar region in Tibet (China) and enters India through the Shipki La pass, so the answer is Sutlej.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

129. The Triveni Sangam at Prayagraj is the confluence of the Ganga, Yamuna and the mythical:
(a) Kaveri (b) Gandak (c) Narmada (d) Saraswati
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kaveri and Narmada are peninsular rivers and have no connection with Prayagraj, and the Gandak joins the Ganga near Patna, not Prayagraj. The third stream at the Sangam is the invisible, mythical Saraswati, so the answer is Saraswati.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

130. Which river flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges?
(a) Narmada (b) Mahanadi (c) Godavari (d) Krishna
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Godavari and Krishna flow over the Deccan plateau in broad valleys, not rift valleys, which is the usual confusion. The Narmada flows westward through a fault-created rift valley bounded by the Vindhyas to the north and the Satpuras to the south, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

131. The largest river island in the world, Majuli, is located in which river?
(a) Ganga (b) Brahmaputra (c) Godavari (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Ganga has many islands in its delta, which tempts students, but none matches Majuli in size. Majuli, in Assam, is a river island of the Brahmaputra and is recognised as the world's largest river island, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

132. Which of the following is the correct north-to-south sequence of the Peninsular rivers' mouths along the east coast?
(a) Krishna – Godavari – Mahanadi – Kaveri (b) Mahanadi – Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri (c) Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri – Mahanadi (d) Kaveri – Krishna – Godavari – Mahanadi
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a positional confusion trap: students often place the Godavari north of the Mahanadi. Moving from north to south along the east coast, the order is Mahanadi (Odisha), Godavari, Krishna (Andhra Pradesh) and Kaveri (Tamil Nadu), so the answer is Mahanadi – Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

133. The Chambal river is a tributary of the:
(a) Ganga (b) Son (c) Yamuna (d) Betwa
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chambal ultimately reaches the Ganga system, which makes the Ganga a tempting answer, but it does not join the Ganga directly. The Chambal joins the Yamuna in Etawah district of Uttar Pradesh, so the answer is Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

134. Which river is known as the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Krishna (b) Kaveri (c) Godavari (d) Tungabhadra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kaveri is often mistakenly chosen because of its sacred status in the south, but the title refers to size and length. The Godavari, the longest Peninsular river with the largest basin among Peninsular rivers, is called the "Dakshin Ganga", so the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

135. The Indus river enters India in which Union Territory/State?
(a) Punjab (b) Ladakh (c) Himachal Pradesh (d) Jammu & Kashmir
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students pick Jammu & Kashmir because the Indus is associated with Kashmir in general knowledge, which is the trap. After originating near Lake Mansarovar in Tibet, the Indus flows northwest and enters India in the Union Territory of Ladakh, so the answer is Ladakh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

136. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Hirakud Dam – Krishna (b) Bhakra Nangal Dam – Ravi (c) Sardar Sarovar Dam – Narmada (d) Nagarjuna Sagar Dam – Kaveri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hirakud is on the Mahanadi (not Krishna), Bhakra Nangal is on the Sutlej (not Ravi), and Nagarjuna Sagar is on the Krishna (not Kaveri) — all three are classic mismatched traps. The Sardar Sarovar Dam in Gujarat is built on the Narmada, so the answer is Sardar Sarovar Dam – Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

137. The river that makes the Dhuandhar Falls near Jabalpur is:
(a) Tapi (b) Betwa (c) Narmada (d) Ken
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Tapi also flows in a rift valley nearby, which confuses students, but its course lies further south. The Narmada plunges through marble rocks at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur forming the Dhuandhar (smoke-like) Falls, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

138. Which river's basin covers the largest area in India?
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Indus (c) Godavari (d) Ganga
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Brahmaputra carries more water, which tempts students to equate discharge with basin area — a classic trap. In terms of drainage basin area within India, the Ganga basin is the largest, covering about a quarter of the country's area, so the answer is Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

139. The Kaveri river originates at Talakaveri in which state?
(a) Karnataka (b) Kerala (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tamil Nadu is tempting because the Kaveri is most associated with that state and its delta, but origin and association are different things. Talakaveri lies in the Brahmagiri hills of Kodagu (Coorg) district in Karnataka, so the answer is Karnataka.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

140. Which of the following rivers flows into the Arabian Sea?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Godavari (c) Kaveri (d) Tapi
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Mahanadi, Godavari and Kaveri are all east-flowing rivers draining into the Bay of Bengal, which is the common confusion. The Tapi (Tapti) flows westward through a rift valley and empties into the Gulf of Khambhat in the Arabian Sea, so the answer is Tapi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

141. The Teesta river, a major tributary of the Brahmaputra system, originates in which state?
(a) Sikkim (b) Arunachal Pradesh (c) West Bengal (d) Assam
Answer: (a)
Solution: West Bengal is tempting because the Teesta flows through it and joins the Brahmaputra (Jamuna) in Bangladesh, but that is its lower course. The Teesta rises from the Tso Lhamo lake region in North Sikkim, so the answer is Sikkim.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

142. Which city is situated on the banks of the Musi river?
(a) Hyderabad (b) Nashik (c) Vijayawada (d) Cuttack
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vijayawada is on the Krishna, Nashik on the Godavari and Cuttack on the Mahanadi — all plausible river cities that act as traps. Hyderabad, the capital of Telangana, stands on the banks of the Musi, a tributary of the Krishna, so the answer is Hyderabad.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

143. The Brahmaputra is known by which name in Tibet?
(a) Tsangpo (b) Dihang (c) Siang (d) Lohit
Answer: (a)
Solution: Siang and Dihang are names of the Brahmaputra within Arunachal Pradesh, not Tibet, and Lohit is a tributary — all are similar-name traps. In Tibet the river is called the Tsangpo, which enters India through Arunachal Pradesh, so the answer is Tsangpo.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

144. Which of the following statements about the Narmada is correct?
(a) It flows through Himachal Pradesh (b) It flows into the Bay of Bengal (c) It is the longest river of India (d) It originates from the Amarkantak plateau
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Narmada flows west into the Arabian Sea (not the Bay of Bengal), the Ganga is the longest river of India, and the Narmada never touches Himachal Pradesh — these are the almost-correct traps. The Narmada rises from the Amarkantak plateau in Anuppur district of Madhya Pradesh, so the answer is that it originates from the Amarkantak plateau.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

145. The Gandak river is known by which name in Nepal?
(a) Bhote Koshi (b) Karnali (c) Arun (d) Kali Gandaki
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Karnali is the Nepali name associated with the Ghaghara system, and the Arun is a tributary of the Kosi — both are similar-name traps from the same Himalayan region. The Gandak is called the Kali Gandaki in Nepal, famous for its deep gorge, so the answer is Kali Gandaki.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

146. Which river forms the famous Jog (Gersoppa) Falls?
(a) Periyar (b) Kali (c) Netravati (d) Sharavati
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kali and Netravati are also rivers of Karnataka's Western Ghats, which makes them attractive but wrong choices. The Jog Falls, one of India's highest plunge waterfalls, is formed by the Sharavati river in Karnataka, so the answer is Sharavati.

147. The Ghaghara river joins the Ganga near which place?
(a) Varanasi (b) Prayagraj (c) Chhapra (d) Kanpur
Answer: (c)
Solution: Prayagraj is tempting because it is the most famous Ganga confluence, but there the Yamuna joins the Ganga — a classic confluence-confusion trap. The Ghaghara, a major Himalayan tributary from Nepal, joins the Ganga near Chhapra in Bihar, so the answer is Chhapra.

148. Which of the following rivers is an example of an antecedent (prior) drainage river that cuts across the Himalayas?
(a) Chambal (b) Mahanadi (c) Indus (d) Betwa
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chambal, Betwa and Mahanadi are consequent streams whose courses follow the slope of the land, so they do not fit. The Indus is older than the Himalayas and maintained its course by cutting deep gorges as the mountains rose, making it a classic antecedent river, so the answer is Indus.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

149. The Loktak Lake, famous for its floating phumdis, is located in which state?
(a) Nagaland (b) Manipur (c) Tripura (d) Mizoram
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mizoram and Tripura have notable lakes and rivers, which creates a north-eastern state confusion trap. Loktak, the largest freshwater lake in north-eastern India, lies in Manipur and is drained by the Manipur river system, so the answer is Manipur.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

150. Which of the following is the correct downstream confluence order of the Yamuna's major tributaries?
(a) Tons – Chambal – Sind – Betwa – Ken (b) Chambal – Tons – Ken – Betwa – Sind (c) Tons – Ken – Betwa – Sind – Chambal (d) Betwa – Sind – Chambal – Ken – Tons
Answer: (a)
Solution: This is a positional-order trap: the Tons joins the Yamuna early near Kalsi in the hills, while the Chambal joins much lower near Etawah, so any option placing Chambal first is wrong. Downstream, the correct order of confluence is Tons, then Chambal, Sind, Betwa and Ken in the Bundelkhand stretch, so the answer is Tons – Chambal – Sind – Betwa – Ken.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)
