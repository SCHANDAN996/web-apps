# Key Facts & Figures — States & Rivers

| Bhagirathi vs Alaknanda | Meet at Devprayag to form the Ganga | Vishnuprayag, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag, Nandaprayag are other Prayags — order matters | Confluence (Prayag) confusion |

| Ganga river system states | Uttarakhand, UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, West Bengal (main stem) | Rajasthan is not on the main Ganga stem | State-river mapping |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Question Type | Time | Strategy | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| River origin / source glacier | 15 sec | Direct recall | ✅ Yes |
| Tributary–river matching | 15 sec | Direct recall | ✅ Yes |
| Dam–river–state matching | 15 sec | Direct recall | ✅ Yes |
| City on river bank | 15 sec | Direct recall | ✅ Yes |
| Epithets (Sorrow of Bihar etc.) | 15 sec | Direct recall | ✅ Yes |
| National Waterway numbers | 15 sec | Direct recall | ✅ Yes |
| Left-bank vs right-bank tributary | 45 sec | Visualise flow direction; eliminate | ⚠️ Medium |
| Sequence of Prayags / confluences | 45 sec | Recall upstream→downstream order | ⚠️ Medium |
| West-flowing vs east-flowing classification | 45 sec | Check each option against list | ⚠️ Medium |
| Statement-based (2–4 statements) | 45 sec | Test each statement; watch for swapped pairs | ❌ No |
| Treaty/dispute year + parties | 45 sec | Verify both year and parties | ⚠️ Medium |
| "Which is NOT correct" negative questions | 45 sec | Read all four; trap is usually one swapped word | ❌ No |

**Score Maximizer sub-topics:** Dam–river–state, city-on-bank, tributary matching, epithets, National Waterways — pure recall, high accuracy, low time.
