Card 1
Front: Which is the longest river of peninsular India, and what is it popularly called?
Back: The Godavari (about 1,465 km), called the "Dakshin Ganga". (Trap: The longest river of India overall is the Ganga, about 2,525 km — examiners swap these two.)

Card 2
Front: Which river is called the "Sorrow of Bihar", and why?
Back: The Kosi river, because it frequently shifts its course and causes devastating floods in Bihar. (Trap: "Sorrow of Bengal" is the Damodar, "Sorrow of Assam" is the Brahmaputra.)

Card 3
Front: Which is the only major Indian river that flows westward into the Arabian Sea through a rift valley — and name its twin?
Back: The Narmada, flowing west through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges; its twin is the Tapi (Tapti), which also flows west into the Gulf of Khambhat.

Card 4
Front: Which state does the Brahmaputra enter first in India, and what is it called there?
Back: It enters Arunachal Pradesh, where it is known as the Dihang (Siang). In Tibet it is called the Tsangpo.

Card 5
Front: Mnemonic trick: How can you remember the Ganga's left-bank tributaries from west to east?
Back: Remember "Ra-Go-Gha-Ga-Ko" — Ramganga, Gomati, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi (left bank, west to east). Right bank: Yamuna, Son. Key point: the Yamuna is the largest tributary of the Ganga.

Card 6
Front: Which river forms the famous Jog (Gersoppa) Falls, and in which state?
Back: The Sharavati river, in Karnataka. Jog Falls is one of India's highest plunge waterfalls.

Card 7
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Chambal is a tributary of the Ganga." True or false?
Back: False. The Chambal is a tributary of the Yamuna, not directly of the Ganga. Similarly, the Betwa and Ken join the Yamuna. Examiners swap "Ganga" and "Yamuna" to confuse.

Card 8
Front: Which river is known as the "Lifeline of Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat" and does NOT form a delta?
Back: The Narmada. It forms an estuary (not a delta) because it flows through a rift valley directly into the Gulf of Khambhat.

Card 9
Front: Which two rivers meet at Devprayag to officially form the Ganga?
Back: The Bhagirathi and the Alaknanda. (Trap: At Vishnuprayag, the Alaknanda meets the Dhauliganga — don't confuse the prayags.)

Card 10
Front: Which is the largest peninsular river by basin area, and where does it originate?
Back: The Godavari, originating at Trimbakeshwar near Nashik, Maharashtra, and emptying into the Bay of Bengal in Andhra Pradesh.

Card 11
Front: Mnemonic trick: How to remember the five rivers of Punjab?
Back: "Jhelum–Chenab–Ravi–Beas–Sutlej" — use "JCRBS". All five belong to the Indus system; under the Indus Waters Treaty (1960), India has rights over the eastern rivers Ravi, Beas and Sutlej.

Card 12
Front: Through which states does the main stem of the Ganga flow?
Back: Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand (along the border) and West Bengal, before entering Bangladesh. The Ganga basin as a whole covers the largest area of any Indian river basin.

Card 13
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Luni is a river of which state — and does it reach the sea?"
Back: The Luni is in Rajasthan and does NOT reach the sea; it ends in the marshy Rann of Kutch. It is an inland-drainage (endorheic) river — a classic trap asking "into which sea does the Luni fall?"

Card 14
Front: Which river is called the "Dakshin Ganga" and which is called "Vridha Ganga" (Old Ganga)?
Back: Both names refer to the Godavari — "Dakshin Ganga" for its size, "Vridha Ganga" for its age among peninsular rivers.

Card 15
Front: On which river is the Hirakud Dam built, and in which state?
Back: On the Mahanadi, in Odisha. It is one of the longest dams in the world (about 25.8 km including dykes).

Card 16
Front: Which river originates from the Amarkantak plateau, and which direction does it flow?
Back: The Narmada originates at Amarkantak (Madhya Pradesh) and flows west. (Trap: The Son also rises near Amarkantak but flows east/northeast to join the Ganga.)

Card 17
Front: Which river forms the boundary between India and Nepal for a long stretch, and joins the Ganga as a left-bank tributary?
Back: The Gandak (called Narayani in Nepal). It joins the Ganga near Patna/Hajipur region in Bihar.

Card 18
Front: Which is the largest river island in the world, and on which river?
Back: Majuli island, on the Brahmaputra river in Assam.

Card 19
Front: The Tungabhadra is a tributary of which river, and which two rivers merge to form it?
Back: It is a tributary of the Krishna, formed by the confluence of the Tunga and Bhadra rivers in Karnataka.

Card 20
Front: Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Odisha", and which dam controls its floods?
Back: The Mahanadi is associated with Odisha's floods, controlled by the Hirakud Dam. (Compare: Damodar = Sorrow of Bengal, controlled by the Damodar Valley Corporation dams.)
