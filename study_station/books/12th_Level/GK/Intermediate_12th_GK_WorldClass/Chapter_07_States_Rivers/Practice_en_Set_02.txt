Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The Gandak river joins the Ganga near which place?
(a) Bhagalpur (b) Sonpur (near Hajipur) (c) Munger (d) Rajmahal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bhagalpur and Munger lie on the Ganga's main channel in Bihar and are tempting because they are famous river towns, but no major northern tributary joins there. The Gandak, rising in Nepal, meets the Ganga at Sonpur near Hajipur, opposite Patna, so the answer is Sonpur (near Hajipur).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

27. The Tungabhadra river is formed by the confluence of which two rivers?
(a) Tunga and Bhadra (b) Tunga and Kaveri (c) Bhadra and Hemavati (d) Koyna and Krishna
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hemavati is a tributary of the Kaveri and Koyna of the Krishna, so those pairs are traps based on similar-sounding river names. The Tungabhadra is formed at Koodli (Karnataka) by the meeting of the Tunga and the Bhadra, so the answer is Tunga and Bhadra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

28. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Chambal (b) Brahmaputra (c) Betwa (d) Ken
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chambal, Betwa and Ken all rise in Madhya Pradesh (Vindhya region), so picking any of them is the trap. The Brahmaputra rises near Lake Mansarovar in Tibet (as the Tsangpo) and enters India through Arunachal Pradesh, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

29. The river known as the "Sorrow of Bengal" before the construction of the Damodar Valley Project is:
(a) Damodar (b) Hooghly (c) Ajay (d) Mayurakshi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hooghly is a distributary of the Ganga and is a navigation channel, not a flood menace, which makes it a tempting wrong choice. The Damodar, flowing through Jharkhand and West Bengal, caused devastating floods until the Damodar Valley Corporation tamed it, so the answer is Damodar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

30. Which river flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges?
(a) Sabarmati (b) Tapi (c) Mahi (d) Narmada
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Tapi also flows in a rift valley, but it lies south of the Satpuras, between the Satpura and Ajanta ranges — that is the trap here. The Narmada flows westward in a rift valley bounded by the Vindhyas to the north and the Satpuras to the south, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

31. The Indus Water Treaty (1960) between India and Pakistan was brokered by:
(a) United Nations (b) USSR (c) USA (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The UN is the tempting answer because it mediates many international disputes, but it had no role in this treaty. The Indus Waters Treaty of 1960 was negotiated and brokered by the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (World Bank), so the answer is World Bank.
Source: PYQ-style

32. Which tributary of the Ganga is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar"?
(a) Gandak (b) Bagmati (c) Kosi (d) Kamla
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Gandak and Bagmati also flood Bihar, which makes them attractive distractors, but the title belongs to one river only. The Kosi, notorious for shifting its course westward and causing repeated floods, is called the "Sorrow of Bihar", so the answer is Kosi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

33. The city of Jabalpur is situated on the banks of which river?
(a) Betwa (b) Chambal (c) Narmada (d) Wainganga
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Wainganga flows through the same region of Madhya Pradesh and is the trap for those who confuse nearby rivers. Jabalpur, famous for the Marble Rocks at Bhedaghat, stands on the Narmada, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

34. Which of the following is the correct sequence of rivers from north to south?
(a) Kaveri – Krishna – Godavari – Mahanadi (b) Krishna – Godavari – Kaveri – Mahanadi (c) Godavari – Mahanadi – Kaveri – Krishna (d) Mahanadi – Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri
Answer: (d)
Solution: This is a positional confusion trap: the Godavari lies south of the Mahanadi, not north of it, which eliminates the third sequence. Moving down the east coast the order is Mahanadi (Odisha), Godavari (Telangana–Andhra), Krishna, and Kaveri (Tamil Nadu), so the answer is Mahanadi – Godavari – Krishna – Kaveri.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

35. The Bhakra Nangal Dam is built on which river?
(a) Ravi (b) Beas (c) Sutlej (d) Chenab
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Beas is the trap because the Beas–Sutlej link project and Pong Dam (on the Beas) confuse candidates. The Bhakra Nangal project, one of India's highest gravity dams, is built on the Sutlej in Himachal Pradesh, so the answer is Sutlej.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Water Resources)

36. Which river is also known as the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Kaveri (c) Krishna (d) Godavari
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kaveri is often wrongly picked because of its sacred status in the South — that is the trap. The Godavari, being the longest peninsular river with the largest peninsular basin, is called the "Dakshin Ganga", so the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

37. The Tehri Dam, one of the tallest dams in India, is constructed on which river?
(a) Bhagirathi (b) Alaknanda (c) Mandakini (d) Yamuna
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Alaknanda is the tempting wrong answer because the Bhagirathi and Alaknanda meet at Devprayag to form the Ganga, and candidates mix the two. The Tehri Dam in Uttarakhand stands on the Bhagirathi, so the answer is Bhagirathi.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Water Resources)

38. Which state is drained by the maximum number of major east-flowing peninsular rivers?
(a) Odisha (b) Tamil Nadu (c) Andhra Pradesh (d) Karnataka
Answer: (c)
Solution: Odisha tempts candidates because of the Mahanadi and Brahmani, but the count of major rivers matters. The Godavari, Krishna and Penna all flow through Andhra Pradesh to the Bay of Bengal, giving it the largest share of major east-flowing rivers, so the answer is Andhra Pradesh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

39. The river Luni, which drains into the Rann of Kutch, is an example of:
(a) An inland drainage river (b) A consequent river (c) An antecedent river (d) A perennial river
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Antecedent" is the trap — that term applies to rivers like the Indus and Sutlej that predate the Himalayas. The Luni rises in the Aravallis and loses itself in the salty Rann of Kutch without reaching the sea, making it a classic inland drainage river, so the answer is an inland drainage river.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

40. Which of the following pairs of cities and rivers is correctly matched?
(a) Cuttack – Godavari (b) Lucknow – Ganga (c) Patna – Son (d) Surat – Tapi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Lucknow stands on the Gomti, Patna on the Ganga, and Cuttack on the Mahanadi — each is a near-miss trap with a river of the same region. Surat in Gujarat is correctly located on the Tapi, so the answer is Surat – Tapi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

41. The Chambal river originates from:
(a) Amarkantak plateau (b) Janapav hills near Mhow (c) Mahabaleshwar (d) Brahmagiri hills
Answer: (b)
Solution: Amarkantak is the trap because the Narmada and Son rise there, and candidates often extend it to the Chambal. The Chambal rises from the Janapav hills near Mhow in Madhya Pradesh, so the answer is Janapav hills near Mhow.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

42. Which river forms the famous Dhuandhar Falls?
(a) Tapi (b) Chambal (c) Narmada (d) Betwa
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chambal is known for ravines, not waterfalls, which makes it a weak but common guess. The Narmada plunges through the Marble Rocks near Jabalpur to form the Dhuandhar (smoke-like) Falls, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

43. The river which does NOT form a delta is:
(a) Mahanadi (b) Narmada (c) Krishna (d) Godavari
Answer: (b)
Solution: All four reach the sea, so candidates hesitate — the trap is assuming every large river makes a delta. The Narmada flows west into the Arabian Sea through an estuary (Gulf of Khambhat) and forms no delta, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

44. The Hirakud Dam, one of the longest dams in the world, is built across the:
(a) Subarnarekha (b) Brahmani (c) Mahanadi (d) Baitarani
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Brahmani and Baitarani are Odisha rivers used as regional traps. The Hirakud Dam near Sambalpur, Odisha, is built across the Mahanadi, so the answer is Mahanadi.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Water Resources)

45. Which of the following rivers is a tributary of the Krishna?
(a) Bhima (b) Penganga (c) Manjra (d) Pranhita
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Penganga, Manjra and Pranhita all belong to the Godavari system — that is the deliberate trap of similar Deccan river names. The Bhima, rising near Bhimashankar in Maharashtra, joins the Krishna, so the answer is Bhima.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

46. The Ganga is formed at Devprayag by the confluence of:
(a) Alaknanda and Mandakini (b) Alaknanda and Bhagirathi (c) Bhagirathi and Yamuna (d) Alaknanda and Pindar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Mandakini (at Rudraprayag) and Pindar (at Karnaprayag) join the Alaknanda at other prayags — that is the trap of confusing the Panch Prayag sequence. The Ganga proper begins at Devprayag where the Alaknanda meets the Bhagirathi, so the answer is Alaknanda and Bhagirathi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

47. Which river's main channel flows through the maximum number of Indian states?
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Godavari (c) Narmada (d) Ganga
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Godavari is the tempting answer because it is long and crosses several Deccan states, but its count is lower. The Ganga's main channel flows through Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand and West Bengal, the highest count for a single river's course, so the answer is Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

48. The Sabarmati river, on whose banks Ahmedabad stands, empties into:
(a) Gulf of Khambhat (b) Arabian Sea near Karachi (c) Bay of Bengal (d) Rann of Kutch
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Rann of Kutch is the trap because the Luni drains there and candidates generalise it to all Gujarat rivers. The Sabarmati flows south through Ahmedabad and empties into the Gulf of Khambhat (Cambay), so the answer is Gulf of Khambhat.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

49. Which of the following statements about the Kaveri is correct?
(a) It rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills (b) It originates at Amarkantak (c) It flows through a rift valley (d) It joins the sea at Kanyakumari
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amarkantak (Narmada/Son) and rift valleys (Narmada/Tapi) are traps borrowed from other rivers' facts. The Kaveri rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Karnataka's Western Ghats and empties into the Bay of Bengal in Tamil Nadu, so the answer is that it rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

50. The "Statue of Unity" on the Sadhu Bet island stands near which river?
(a) Tapi (b) Narmada (c) Mahi (d) Sabarmati
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Sabarmati is the trap because the Statue of Unity is in Gujarat and the Sabarmati is Gujarat's best-known river. The statue stands on Sadhu Bet island in the Narmada, downstream of the Sardar Sarovar Dam, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: PYQ-style
