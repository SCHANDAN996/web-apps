126. Which of the following correctly describes the function of DNA ligase in recombinant DNA technology?
(a) It cuts DNA at specific recognition sequences (b) It separates the two strands of DNA by breaking hydrogen bonds (c) It joins DNA fragments by forming phosphodiester bonds (d) It synthesizes new DNA strands using a template
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is the first one, which describes restriction enzymes, not ligase; many students confuse "cutting" and "joining" enzymes. 132. The pH of a solution changes from 4 to 6. The hydrogen ion concentration:
(a) Decreases 100 times (b) Decreases 2 times (c) Increases 100 times (d) Increases 2 times
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "decreases 2 times" — pH is logarithmic, not linear, so each unit change means a tenfold change in hydrogen ion concentration. 140. In a concave mirror, when an object is placed between the pole and the focus, the image formed is:

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the first option — students recall "magnified" from other cases but forget that a real image cannot form when the object is inside the focus.
