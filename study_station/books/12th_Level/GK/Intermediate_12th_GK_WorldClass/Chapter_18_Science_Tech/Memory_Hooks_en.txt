# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Science & Technology (Basic)

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 1: Vitamins (Fat-Soluble)
**"KEDA" — the fat-loving gang!**
**K** – Vitamin K (clotting)
**E** – Vitamin E (antioxidant)
**D** – Vitamin D (bones, sunlight)
**A** – Vitamin A (eyes, night vision)
🎯 Trick: Fat-soluble vitamins = **A, D, E, K** → read it as **"ADEK" → "A DEK" (Hindi: "आ देख!")** — they come dissolved in fat, so "आ देख" them in oily food!
⚠️ Trap: Vitamin **C** and **B-complex** are water-soluble — they are NOT in this gang.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 2: Vitamin Deficiency Diseases
**"ABC of Deficiency"**
- **A** → **A**ndha-ratri (Night blindness) 👁️
- **B1** → **B**eri-**B**eri
- **C** → **C**curvy… no, **S**curvy! (bleeding gums) 🦷
- **D** → **D** for **D**eformed bones → **Rickets** (children) / Osteomalacia (adults)
🎯 Trick: **"Aankh–Beri–Scurvy–Rickets"** = A, B1, C, D in order!

## 🧠 Mnemonic 3: Electromagnetic Spectrum (Increasing Frequency)
**"Raging Martians Invaded Venus Using X-ray Guns"**
**R**adio → **M**icrowave → **I**nfrared → **V**isible → **U**ltraviolet → **X**-ray → **G**amma
🎯 Remember: frequency ↑ left to right, wavelength ↓ left to right. Gamma = shortest wavelength, highest energy.

## 🧠 Mnemonic 4: Order of Planets
**"My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Noodles"**
**M**ercury, **V**enus, **E**arth, **M**ars, **J**upiter, **S**aturn, **U**ranus, **N**eptune
⚠️ Trap: Pluto was removed in **2006** — it is now a **dwarf planet**. If an option says "9 planets," it's wrong!

## 🧠 Mnemonic 5: SI Units (The Big Seven)
**"SMACK-KM" — Seven base units:**
- **S**econd (time)
- **M**etre (length)
- **A**mpere (current)
- **C**andela (luminous intensity)
- **K**elvin (temperature)
- **K**ilogram (mass)
- **M**ole (amount of substance)
🎯 Trick: **"S-M-A-C-K, K-M"** — smack the exam with these 7!

## 🧠 Mnemonic 6: Common Acids in Daily Life
**"TAM-CV" — Taste the acids!**
- **T**amarind → **T**artaric acid
- **A**nt sting → **F**ormic acid (Methanoic acid) 🐜
- **M**ilk (curd/sour) → **L**actic acid
- **C**itrus fruits → **C**itric acid 🍋
- **V**inegar → **A**cetic acid
⚠️ Trap: Ant/bee sting = **formic acid**, treated with a **base** (baking soda), NOT more acid!

## 🧠 Mnemonic 7: pH Scale
**"7 is Heaven (Neutral)"**
- Below 7 → Acidic (think: **A**cid = **A**ngry, low number)
- Above 7 → Basic/Alkaline
- Exactly 7 → Pure water 🚰
🎯 Blood pH ≈ **7.4** (slightly basic) — a favourite one-liner question!

## 🧠 Mnemonic 8: Newton's Laws
**"I-F-A" — Inertia, Force, Action!**
- **1st Law** → **I**nertia (body resists change)
- **2nd Law** → **F** = ma (Force)
- **3rd Law** → **A**ction–Reaction
🎯 Rocket launching = **3rd law**; passengers jerking forward when a bus brakes = **1st law (inertia of motion)**.

## 🧠 Mnemonic 9: Human Body Numbers
**"206-32-600" — the body code!**
- **206** bones (adult)
- **32** teeth (adult)
- ~**600+** muscles
- Largest gland → **Liver**; Largest organ → **Skin**
- Smallest bone → **Stapes** (ear); Longest bone → **Femur** (thigh)
🎯 Trick: **"Stapes is Small, Femur is Fat-long"** — both start pairs match!

## 🧠 Mnemonic 10: Blood Facts
**"Red-White-Platelet: 120-Long-Short"**
- RBC lifespan → **120 days** (made in bone marrow)
- WBC → the **soldiers** (immunity)
- Platelets → the **plaster** (clotting)
- Universal donor → **O−**; Universal acceptor → **AB+**
🎯 Trick: **"O Gives, AB Takes"** — O donates to all, AB accepts from all.

## 🧠 Mnemonic 12: Indian Space Firsts
**"Aryabhata Opened, Rakesh Flew, Gaganyaan Coming"**

- **1984** → First Indian in space: **Rakesh Sharma** (aboard Soviet Soyuz T-11)
- **2008** → **Chandrayaan-1** (Moon)
- **2014** → **Mangalyaan (MOM)** reached Mars — first Asian nation to do so in first attempt
- **2023** → **Chandrayaan-3** soft-landed near Moon's south pole (23 August 2023)
🎯 Trick: **"75-84-08-14-23"** — say it like a phone number!

## 🧠 Mnemonic 14: Units of Measurement
**"Power-Watt, Force-Newton, Energy-Joule — PW-FN-EJ"**
- Power → **Watt** (think: a 60-**watt** bulb)
- Force → **Newton** (apple fell on Newton!)
- Energy/Work → **Joule**
- Pressure → **Pascal**
- Frequency → **Hertz**
🎯 Trick: **"Watt-N-Joule-Pa-Hz"** — recite as "What? New Jewel, Pa? Hertz!"

## 🧠 Mnemonic 15: Diseases — Bacterial vs Viral
**"TB-Cholera-Typhoid are BACTERIA; Flu-Polio-Rabies are VIRUSES"**
- Bacterial 🦠: TB, Cholera, Typhoid, Plague, Tetanus, Leprosy
- Viral 🧬: Influenza, Polio, Rabies, Measles, Dengue, COVID-19, AIDS
🎯 Trick: **"Bacteria = Treatable by antibiotics; Viruses = NOT"** — if the question asks "which disease can antibiotics cure?", pick the bacterial one!

# ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

### 🚫 Skip Tip 1: The "Odd-One-Out with 4 Similar Names" Trap
If a question lists four scientists/inventors/discoveries and you can confidently recall only two or three, **do not guess between the remaining two** — examiners deliberately pair look-alike names (e.g., Marconi vs Tesla, Fleming vs Florey). If you can't eliminate down to one with certainty, **skip and return later** — negative marking eats more marks than a skipped question.

### 🚫 Skip Tip 2: The "Exact Number" Question
Questions asking precise figures — exact pH of a fluid, exact number of muscles, exact year of a satellite launch — are **all-or-nothing**. If you don't know the number cold, options like 7.35 / 7.4 / 7.45 are designed to trap half-knowledge. **Rule: if you can't recall the number in 15 seconds, skip.** Never "estimate" in a static GK question.

### 🚫 Skip Tip 3: The "Statement I & II" Chronology Trap
When two statements about S&T events are given (e.g., "Aryabhata was launched before Bhaskara-I" / "INSAT came before IRS") and you're unsure of **even one** date, the whole question becomes a coin flip. **Skip immediately** — chronology traps have the highest wrong-answer rate because "almost right" feels right. Attempt only if BOTH facts are solid in memory.

⏱️ **Golden Rule:** 15 seconds to recall → attempt. 45 seconds of doubt → skip. A skipped question costs 0; a wrong guess costs negative marks.
