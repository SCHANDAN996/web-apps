# Key Facts & Figures — Science & Technology (Basic)

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| SI unit of force | Newton (N) = kg·m/s² | Dyne is CGS unit, not SI | Similar-name confusion |
| SI unit of work/energy | Joule (J) | Calorie is a heat unit, not SI energy unit | Similar-name confusion |
| SI unit of power | Watt (W) = J/s | Horsepower (746 W) is not SI | Unit swap |
| SI unit of electric current | Ampere (A) | Volt measures potential difference, not current | Quantity swap |
| SI unit of resistance | Ohm (Ω) | Resistivity unit is Ω·m, not Ω | Near-identical unit |
| Speed of light in vacuum | 3 × 10⁸ m/s | Slower in water/glass; sound needs a medium, light does not | Concept reversal |
| Speed of sound in air (approx.) | ~332 m/s at 0°C | Faster in solids than gases — opposite of light behaviour | Concept reversal |
| Ohm's Law | V = IR | Valid only at constant temperature (ohmic conductors) | Hidden condition |
| pH of neutral solution | 7 (at 25°C) | Blood pH ≈ 7.4 (slightly basic), not exactly 7 | Approximate-value trap |
| Acid + Base | Salt + Water (neutralisation) | Metal + acid gives salt + hydrogen, not water | Reaction mix-up |
| Common name of NaHCO₃ | Baking soda | Na₂CO₃ is washing soda; NaOH is caustic soda | Similar-name confusion |
| Common name of CaO | Quicklime | Ca(OH)₂ is slaked lime; CaCO₃ is limestone | Similar-name confusion |
| Hardest natural substance | Diamond (pure carbon) | Graphite is also pure carbon but softest lubricant | Same-element contrast |
| Gas used in photosynthesis | Carbon dioxide | Oxygen is released, not absorbed | Concept reversal |
| Vitamin synthesized in skin by sunlight | Vitamin D | Vitamin C comes from citrus, not sunlight | Source confusion |
| Deficiency of Vitamin C | Scurvy | Night blindness = Vitamin A; Rickets = Vitamin D; Beri-beri = Vitamin B1 | Deficiency swap |
| Powerhouse of the cell | Mitochondria | Ribosome makes proteins; nucleus holds DNA | Organelle swap |
| Universal blood donor | O negative | Universal acceptor is AB positive — opposite pair | Pair reversal |
| Normal human body temperature | 37°C (98.6°F) | 98.6 is Fahrenheit, not Celsius | Unit swap |
| Largest gland in human body | Liver | Pancreas is the mixed (largest digestive enzyme) gland | Category confusion |
| Smallest bone in human body | Stapes (ear) | Femur is the longest bone | Opposite-pair confusion |
| Number of bones in adult human | 206 | Infants have ~300 bones | Age-based variation |
| Insulin controls | Blood glucose | Secreted by pancreas (beta cells), not liver | Organ swap |
| First vaccine (smallpox) | Edward Jenner, 1796 | Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin — different person | Person swap |
| Penicillin discovered by | Alexander Fleming, 1928 | Jenner = vaccine; Pasteur = rabies vaccine | Person swap |
| Unit of frequency | Hertz (Hz) | Decibel measures sound intensity, not frequency | Quantity swap |
| Convex mirror use | Rear-view mirror (wide field) | Concave mirror used in torches/dentist mirrors | Mirror swap |
| Convex lens use | Magnifying glass, camera | Concave lens corrects myopia (short-sightedness) | Lens swap |
| Myopia corrected by | Concave lens | Hypermetropia needs convex lens | Pair reversal |
| Nuclear fission | Heavy nucleus splits (reactors, atom bomb) | Fusion powers the Sun and hydrogen bomb | Process swap |
| Earth's natural satellite | Moon | Moon has no light of its own — reflects sunlight | Statement trap |
| ISRO headquarters | Bengaluru | Sriharikota is the launch site, not HQ | Place swap |
| First Indian satellite | Aryabhata (1975) | Launched by USSR, not by India itself | Hidden detail |
| Father of Indian space programme | Vikram Sarabhai | Homi Bhabha = father of Indian nuclear programme | Person swap |
| WWW invented by | Tim Berners-Lee (1989, CERN) | Internet (ARPANET) is older than WWW | Timeline confusion |
| 1 byte | 8 bits | 1 KB = 1024 bytes, not 1000 | Numerical trap |
| CPU full form | Central Processing Unit | Called the "brain" of the computer | Terminology |
| RAM nature | Volatile memory | ROM is non-volatile | Pair reversal |
| Greenhouse gas (major) | Carbon dioxide | Methane is more potent per molecule but less abundant | Degree trap |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Topic | Question Type | Time Rule | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| SI units & measurements | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Chemical common names (soda, lime, salts) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Vitamins & deficiency diseases | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Human body facts (bones, glands, organs) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Scientists & discoveries | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Computer basics (memory, units, full forms) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Space & nuclear facts (ISRO, satellites) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Mirror/lens image problems | Analytical (apply ray rules) | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt if confident |
| Ohm's law / circuit numericals | Analytical (formula application) | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt if confident |
| pH & acid-base reasoning | Analytical (concept application) | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt if confident |
| Statement-based physics/chemistry questions | Analytical (eliminate traps) | 45 sec | ⚠️ Read twice |
| Biology process questions (photosynthesis, respiration) | Analytical (sequence/contrast) | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt if confident |

**Strategy:** Attempt all 15-second fact questions first — they form the bulk of S&T questions in SSC/Railway/Bank exams. Return to 45-second analytical questions only in the second pass.

## Part 3: Data & Formulas

| Item | Value / Formula | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Speed formula | Speed = Distance / Time | Units: m/s (SI), km/h (×3.6 conversion) |
| Density | Density = Mass / Volume | SI unit: kg/m³ |
| Ohm's Law | V = I × R | V in volts, I in amperes, R in ohms |
| Electric power | P = V × I = I²R = V²/R | Unit: watt |
| Work | W = F × d (force × displacement) | Unit: joule |
| Kinetic energy | KE = ½mv² | Unit: joule |
| Potential energy | PE = mgh | g ≈ 9.8 m/s² (often taken as 10) |
| Acceleration due to gravity (Earth) | g ≈ 9.8 m/s² | Moon's g ≈ 1/6 of Earth's |
| Speed of light | c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s | In vacuum |
| Speed of sound in air | ~332 m/s at 0°C | Increases ~0.6 m/s per °C rise |
| pH scale | 0–14 | <7 acidic, 7 neutral, >7 basic |
| Normal body temperature | 37°C = 98.6°F | °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32 |
| Normal human blood pH | ~7.4 | Slightly alkaline |
| Normal blood pressure | 120/80 mmHg | Systolic/diastolic |
| Water formula | H₂O | Boils at 100°C, freezes at 0°C (at 1 atm) |
| 1 byte | 8 bits | 1 KB = 1024 B; 1 MB = 1024 KB; 1 GB = 1024 MB |
| 1 horsepower | 746 watts | Non-SI power unit |
| 1 calorie | ≈ 4.2 joules | Heat energy conversion |
| Atomic number | Number of protons | Defines the element |
| Mass number | Protons + Neutrons | Isotopes: same atomic number, different mass number |
| Aryabhata launch year | 1975 | India's first satellite |
| WWW proposal year | 1989 | Tim Berners-Lee at CERN |
| Penicillin discovery year | 1928 | Alexander Fleming |
| Smallpox vaccine year | 1796 | Edward Jenner |
