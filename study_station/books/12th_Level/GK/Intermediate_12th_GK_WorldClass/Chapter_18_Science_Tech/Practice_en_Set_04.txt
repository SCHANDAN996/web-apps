Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Which of the following pairs of vitamin and its deficiency disease is correctly matched?
(a) Vitamin A – Scurvy (b) Vitamin B12 – Beriberi (c) Vitamin C – Night blindness (d) Vitamin D – Rickets
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong options swap the diseases: scurvy is caused by vitamin C deficiency, night blindness by vitamin A deficiency, and beriberi by vitamin B1 (thiamine) deficiency. Rickets in children is caused by deficiency of vitamin D, so the answer is Vitamin D – Rickets.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science (Components of Food)

77. The pH value of human blood is approximately:
(a) 6.4 (b) 5.4 (c) 7.4 (d) 8.4
Answer: (c)
Solution: Blood is slightly alkaline, not acidic, so options below 7 are traps. The pH of normal human blood is about 7.4, so the answer is 7.4.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Acids, Bases and Salts)

78. Which instrument is used to record the waves generated by an earthquake?
(a) Barometer (b) Richter scale (c) Seismograph (d) Ammeter
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the Richter scale, which is a scale for expressing the magnitude of an earthquake, not an instrument that records its waves. A seismograph is the instrument that detects and records seismic waves, so the answer is Seismograph.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science (Some Natural Phenomena)

79. Which of the following is NOT a greenhouse gas?
(a) Oxygen (b) Methane (c) Nitrous oxide (d) Carbon dioxide
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to assume all common atmospheric gases trap heat. Carbon dioxide, methane and nitrous oxide all absorb infrared radiation and warm the atmosphere, while oxygen does not act as a greenhouse gas, so the answer is Oxygen.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

80. The SI unit of electric resistance is:
(a) Volt (b) Ampere (c) Ohm (d) Watt
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is confusing related electrical quantities: volt is the unit of potential difference, ampere of current, and watt of power. Resistance is measured in ohms, so the answer is Ohm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Electricity)

81. Which of the following diseases is caused by a virus?
(a) Measles (b) Tuberculosis (c) Cholera (d) Typhoid
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that all four are infectious diseases, but tuberculosis, cholera and typhoid are caused by bacteria. Measles is caused by a virus, so the answer is Measles.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Why Do We Fall Ill?)

82. The process by which plants lose water in the form of vapour through leaves is called:
(a) Transpiration (b) Respiration (c) Translocation (d) Photosynthesis
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the similar-sounding term translocation, which is the movement of food through phloem, not water loss. Loss of water vapour mainly through stomata of leaves is transpiration, so the answer is Transpiration.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

83. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?
(a) Sodium (b) Mercury (c) Iron (d) Aluminium
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is sodium, which is so soft it can be cut with a knife, but it is still a solid at room temperature, as are aluminium and iron. Mercury is the only metal that is liquid at room temperature, so the answer is Mercury.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Metals and Non-metals)

84. The full form of 'LED' is:
(a) Light Electric Diode (b) Low Energy Device (c) Long Emitting Diode (d) Light Emitting Diode
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap options play on the words "light" and "diode" but rearrange them wrongly. LED stands for Light Emitting Diode, a semiconductor device that emits light when current passes through it, so the answer is Light Emitting Diode.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics (Semiconductor Electronics)

85. Which of the following is the correct order of planets from the Sun?
(a) Venus, Mercury, Earth, Mars (b) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars (c) Mercury, Earth, Venus, Mars (d) Mercury, Venus, Mars, Earth
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a positional confusion trap: Venus and Mercury are often swapped, and Mars is sometimes placed before Earth. The correct sequence from the Sun is Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, so the answer is Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science (Stars and the Solar System)

86. The chemical formula of washing soda is:
(a) NaHCO₃ (b) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O (c) NaOH (d) CaOCl₂
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is NaHCO₃, which is baking soda, a commonly confused similar name; NaOH is caustic soda and CaOCl₂ is bleaching powder. Washing soda is sodium carbonate decahydrate, so the answer is Na₂CO₃·10H₂O.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Acids, Bases and Salts)

87. Which part of the human brain controls balance and posture?
(a) Cerebellum (b) Medulla (c) Cerebrum (d) Hypothalamus
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the cerebrum, the largest part of the brain, which controls thinking and voluntary actions, not balance. The cerebellum coordinates muscular activity, balance and posture, so the answer is Cerebellum.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Control and Coordination)

88. The phenomenon of light bending as it passes from one medium to another is called:
(a) Reflection (b) Diffraction (c) Dispersion (d) Refraction
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dispersion, which is the splitting of white light into colours — a result of refraction but not the bending itself. Bending of light due to change in speed between media is refraction, so the answer is Refraction.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Light – Reflection and Refraction)

89. Which of the following is used as a moderator in a nuclear reactor?
(a) Plutonium (b) Uranium-235 (c) Cadmium (d) Heavy water
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is cadmium, which is used in control rods to absorb neutrons, not to slow them down; uranium and plutonium are fuels. Heavy water slows down fast neutrons as a moderator, so the answer is Heavy water.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Sources of Energy)

90. The deficiency of iodine in the human body causes:
(a) Goitre (b) Anaemia (c) Rickets (d) Scurvy
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is anaemia, which is caused by iron deficiency, another mineral deficiency often confused with iodine. Iodine deficiency enlarges the thyroid gland causing goitre, so the answer is Goitre.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science (Components of Food)

91. Which of the following statements about sound is correct?
(a) Sound travels fastest in vacuum (b) Sound travels faster in solids than in gases (c) Sound cannot travel through liquids (d) Sound travels at the same speed in all media
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the almost-correct-looking claim that sound travels fastest in vacuum — in fact sound cannot travel in vacuum at all because it needs a medium. Sound travels fastest in solids, slower in liquids and slowest in gases, so the answer is that sound travels faster in solids than in gases.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Sound)

92. The gas used in the manufacture of ammonia by the Haber process, along with nitrogen, is:
(a) Carbon dioxide (b) Oxygen (c) Hydrogen (d) Chlorine
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is oxygen, since air is involved in obtaining nitrogen, but oxygen is not a reactant in ammonia synthesis. In the Haber process, nitrogen reacts with hydrogen to form ammonia, so the answer is Hydrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Hydrogen)

93. Which blood group is called the universal donor?
(a) AB (b) O (c) B (d) A
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is AB, which is the universal recipient, not the donor — a classic similar-pair confusion. Group O blood can be donated to persons of any group, so the answer is O.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology (Body Fluids and Circulation)

94. The device used to convert alternating current into direct current is called:
(a) Rectifier (b) Dynamo (c) Transformer (d) Capacitor
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is dynamo, which generates current, and transformer, which only changes AC voltage without converting it to DC. A rectifier converts alternating current into direct current, so the answer is Rectifier.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics (Alternating Current)

95. Which of the following is an example of a non-biodegradable waste?
(a) Cow dung (b) Paper (c) Vegetable peels (d) Plastic bags
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is paper, which feels synthetic but is biodegradable since it is made from plant fibres. Plastic bags cannot be broken down by microorganisms and persist in the environment, so the answer is Plastic bags.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Our Environment)

96. The SI unit of power is:
(a) Joule (b) Newton (c) Watt (d) Pascal
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is joule, which is the unit of energy or work, while power is the rate of doing work. One watt equals one joule per second, so the answer is Watt.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Work and Energy)

97. Which of the following acids is present in vinegar?
(a) Citric acid (b) Lactic acid (c) Acetic acid (d) Oxalic acid
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is citric acid, which is found in citrus fruits like lemon, and lactic acid in curd — both common food acids. Vinegar is a dilute solution of acetic acid, so the answer is Acetic acid.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Acids, Bases and Salts)

98. The first Indian satellite, launched in 1975, was:
(a) Bhaskara (b) Aryabhata (c) Rohini (d) INSAT-1A
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a chronological confusion trap: Rohini (1980) was the first satellite launched by an Indian launch vehicle, and INSAT-1A came in 1982, but neither was first. Aryabhata, launched in 1975, was India's first satellite, so the answer is Aryabhata.
Source: ISRO official website (isro.gov.in)

99. Which cell organelle is known as the powerhouse of the cell?
(a) Mitochondrion (b) Nucleus (c) Ribosome (d) Golgi apparatus
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the nucleus, which controls cell activities but does not produce energy. Mitochondria release energy from food through respiration in the form of ATP, so the answer is Mitochondrion.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

100. The speed of light in vacuum is approximately:
(a) 3 × 10⁴ m/s (b) 3 × 10⁶ m/s (c) 3 × 10¹⁰ m/s (d) 3 × 10⁸ m/s
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap options differ only in the power of ten, a common examiner trick. The speed of light in vacuum is about 300,000 km/s, so the answer is 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Light – Reflection and Refraction)
