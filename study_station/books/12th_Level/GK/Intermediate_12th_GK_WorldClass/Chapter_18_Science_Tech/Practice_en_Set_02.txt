Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Which vitamin is synthesised in the human skin on exposure to sunlight?
(a) Vitamin D (b) Vitamin C (c) Vitamin A (d) Vitamin K
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vitamin K is a common trap because it is linked to blood clotting and is made by gut bacteria, not skin. Sunlight converts 7-dehydrocholesterol in the skin into Vitamin D, so the answer is Vitamin D.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

27. The SI unit of electric current is:
(a) Volt (b) Ohm (c) Ampere (d) Watt
Answer: (c)
Solution: Volt measures potential difference, Ohm measures resistance and Watt measures power — all tempting because they belong to the same topic. Current is measured in Ampere, so the answer is Ampere.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

28. Which gas is commonly known as laughing gas?
(a) Nitrogen dioxide (b) Nitrous oxide (c) Nitric oxide (d) Ammonia
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nitric oxide looks similar in name and is the usual trap, but it is a different compound (NO). Laughing gas is nitrous oxide (N2O), used as a mild anaesthetic, so the answer is Nitrous oxide.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

29. The pH value of a neutral solution at 25°C is:
(a) 0 (b) 14 (c) 1 (d) 7
Answer: (d)
Solution: Students often confuse the ends of the scale: 0 is strongly acidic and 14 is strongly alkaline. A neutral solution like pure water has pH 7, so the answer is 7.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

30. Which of the following diseases is caused by a virus?
(a) Tuberculosis (b) Cholera (c) Typhoid (d) Measles
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tuberculosis, cholera and typhoid are all bacterial diseases and form the trap set here. Measles is caused by a virus, so the answer is Measles.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

31. The instrument used to measure atmospheric pressure is:
(a) Ammeter (b) Thermometer (c) Barometer (d) Hydrometer
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hydrometer is the tempting wrong choice because it also measures a physical property, but it measures the density of liquids. Atmospheric pressure is measured by a barometer, so the answer is Barometer.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics

32. Which organ in the human body produces insulin?
(a) Liver (b) Pancreas (c) Kidney (d) Spleen
Answer: (b)
Solution: Liver is the common trap because it stores glycogen and regulates blood sugar, but it does not secrete insulin. Insulin is secreted by the beta cells of the pancreas, so the answer is Pancreas.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

33. The speed of light in vacuum is approximately:
(a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s (b) 3 × 10⁶ m/s (c) 3 × 10¹⁰ m/s (d) 3 × 10⁴ m/s
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap lies in the powers of ten, which look similar under exam pressure. The accepted value is about 3 × 10⁸ m/s, so the answer is 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

34. Which of the following is a non-renewable source of energy?
(a) Coal (b) Wind energy (c) Solar energy (d) Biogas
Answer: (a)
Solution: Biogas is the tempting option because it comes from waste and seems artificial, but it is renewable. Coal is a fossil fuel that cannot be replenished on a human timescale, so the answer is Coal.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

35. The chemical formula of washing soda is:
(a) NaHCO₃ (b) NaOH (c) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O (d) CaOCl₂
Answer: (c)
Solution: NaHCO₃ is the classic trap because it is also a sodium carbonate compound, but it is baking soda. Washing soda is sodium carbonate decahydrate, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O, so the answer is Na₂CO₃·10H₂O.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

36. Which blood cells help in clotting of blood?
(a) Platelets (b) White blood cells (c) Red blood cells (d) Plasma cells
Answer: (a)
Solution: White blood cells tempt students because they are linked to body defence, but they fight infection, not bleeding. Platelets (thrombocytes) form clots, so the answer is Platelets.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

37. The phenomenon of bending of light as it passes from one medium to another is called:
(a) Reflection (b) Refraction (c) Diffraction (d) Dispersion
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dispersion is the trap because it also involves light changing behaviour in a medium, but it is the splitting of light into colours. Bending due to change of medium is refraction, so the answer is Refraction.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

38. Which of the following acids is present in vinegar?
(a) Acetic acid (b) Citric acid (c) Lactic acid (d) Tartaric acid
Answer: (a)
Solution: Citric acid is the tempting wrong answer because it is found in sour fruits like lemon. Vinegar is a dilute solution of acetic acid, so the answer is Acetic acid.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

39. The unit of frequency is:
(a) Joule (b) Hertz (c) Newton (d) Pascal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Joule (energy), Newton (force) and Pascal (pressure) are all SI units and form a confusing set. Frequency is measured in Hertz, so the answer is Hertz.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

40. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
(a) Venus (b) Mars (c) Jupiter (d) Mercury
Answer: (b)
Solution: Venus is the trap because it is often in the news as the brightest planet, but it is called the Morning/Evening Star. Mars appears red due to iron oxide dust, so the answer is Mars.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

41. The process by which plants make their food is called:
(a) Respiration (b) Fermentation (c) Transpiration (d) Photosynthesis
Answer: (d)
Solution: Transpiration is the trap because it is another plant process, but it is the loss of water vapour from leaves. Food-making using sunlight, carbon dioxide and water is photosynthesis, so the answer is Photosynthesis.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

42. Which of the following is the hardest naturally occurring substance?
(a) Quartz (b) Diamond (c) Graphite (d) Iron
Answer: (b)
Solution: Graphite is the trap because it is chemically the same element as diamond (carbon), yet it is soft and slippery. Diamond is the hardest natural substance, so the answer is Diamond.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

43. The deficiency of iodine in the human body causes:
(a) Night blindness (b) Scurvy (c) Goitre (d) Rickets
Answer: (c)
Solution: Night blindness tempts students because it is also a deficiency disease, but it is caused by lack of Vitamin A. Iodine deficiency enlarges the thyroid gland causing goitre, so the answer is Goitre.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

44. Which of the following is used as a moderator in a nuclear reactor?
(a) Uranium-235 (b) Cadmium (c) Plutonium (d) Heavy water
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cadmium is the trap because it is also used in reactors, but cadmium rods absorb neutrons to control the reaction, not slow them. Heavy water slows down neutrons as a moderator, so the answer is Heavy water.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

45. The full form of DNA is:
(a) Dinucleic acid (b) Deoxyribose nucleic atom (c) Deoxyribonucleic acid (d) Deoxyribonuclear acid
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Deoxyribonuclear acid" is the almost-correct trap that sounds right but is a wrong expansion. The correct expansion is Deoxyribonucleic acid, so the answer is Deoxyribonucleic acid.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

46. Which of the following metals is liquid at room temperature?
(a) Sodium (b) Aluminium (c) Mercury (d) Gallium only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sodium is the trap because it is soft enough to cut with a knife, but it is still solid at room temperature. Mercury is the metal that is liquid at room temperature, so the answer is Mercury.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

47. The ozone layer is found mainly in which layer of the atmosphere?
(a) Stratosphere (b) Troposphere (c) Mesosphere (d) Thermosphere
Answer: (a)
Solution: Troposphere is the tempting answer because it is the layer we live in and where weather occurs. The ozone layer lies mainly in the stratosphere, so the answer is Stratosphere.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

48. Which of the following is a good conductor of electricity?
(a) Rubber (b) Glass (c) Dry wood (d) Copper
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dry wood is the trap because wet wood conducts, but dry wood is an insulator. Copper is a metal with free electrons and conducts electricity well, so the answer is Copper.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

49. The human heart has how many chambers?
(a) Two (b) Three (c) Five (d) Four
Answer: (d)
Solution: Three is the trap because a frog's heart has three chambers and students mix up the two. The human heart has two atria and two ventricles, so the answer is Four.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

50. Which of the following waves is used in radar?
(a) Microwaves (b) Sound waves (c) Ultraviolet rays (d) X-rays
Answer: (a)
Solution: X-rays tempt students because they are also used for detection, but they are used in medical imaging, not radar. Radar works with microwaves (radio waves), so the answer is Microwaves.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics
