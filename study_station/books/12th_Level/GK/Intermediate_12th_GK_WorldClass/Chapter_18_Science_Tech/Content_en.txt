# Science & Technology (Basic)

## 🚀 The Hook: A Rocket That Refused to Give Up

In 1979, India's first attempt to launch a satellite on its own rocket — the SLV-3 — failed. The rocket plunged into the Bay of Bengal. A young scientist named A.P.J. Abdul Kalam took responsibility for the failure. On 18 July 1980 (the first SLV-3 launch was 10 August 1979, about 11 months earlier), the same team placed the Rohini satellite into orbit. That single success taught India a lesson that defines this entire chapter: **science is not about never failing — it is about understanding *why* things work.**

Every competitive exam — SSC, Banking, Railway, State PCS — tests this same understanding. Not advanced research, but the *basics*: units, inventions, discoveries, space missions, defence systems, and everyday science. Master these, and you can expect 4–6 marks (not guaranteed).

## 1. The Language of Science: SI Units

Science needs a common language, and that language is the **SI system (Système International d'Unités)**. There are exactly **7 base units**:

| Quantity | Unit | Symbol |
|---|---|---|
| Length | metre | m |
| Mass | kilogram | kg |
| Time | second | s |
| Electric current | ampere | A |
| Temperature | kelvin | K |
| Amount of substance | mole | mol |
| Luminous intensity | candela | cd |

**Derived units you must know:**
- Force → **newton (N)** = kg·m/s²
- Pressure → **pascal (Pa)** = N/m²
- Energy/Work → **joule (J)**
- Power → **watt (W)** = J/s
- Electric charge → **coulomb (C)**
- Potential difference → **volt (V)**
- Resistance → **ohm (Ω)**
- Frequency → **hertz (Hz)**

🧠 **Active Recall:** Close the book. Can you name all 7 SI base units and their symbols? Which unit measures pressure? Which measures power?

> 🪄 **Mnemonic for the 7 SI units:** **"My Kind Teacher Always Keeps Measuring Carefully"** — **M**etre, **K**ilogram, **T**ime (second), **A**mpere, **K**elvin, **M**ole, **C**andela.

## 4. India's Space Programme (ISRO)

**Key facts:**
- **ISRO** (Indian Space Research Organisation) was founded on **15 August 1969**. Its founder is regarded as **Dr. Vikram Sarabhai**, the "Father of the Indian Space Programme."
- Headquarters: **Bengaluru**.
- First Indian satellite: **Aryabhata** (1975) — launched by the **Soviet Union**.
- **SLV-3** placed **Rohini** satellite in orbit in **1980** (project led by A.P.J. Abdul Kalam).
- **Chandrayaan-1** (2008): discovered evidence of **water molecules on the Moon**.
- **Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan, 2013)**: India became the **first Asian nation** to reach Mars orbit and the **first nation in the world to do so in its first attempt**.
- **Chandrayaan-3** (2023): India became the **fourth country** to soft-land on the Moon and the **first to land near the lunar south pole** (23 August 2023 — now celebrated as **National Space Day**).
- **Aditya-L1** (launched 2023): India's first mission to study the **Sun**, placed at Lagrange Point 1 in January 2024.
- Launch site: **Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota** (Andhra Pradesh).

🧠 **Active Recall:** Who is the Father of the Indian Space Programme? Which mission reached Mars in the first attempt? Where is ISRO's launch site?

## 5. India's Nuclear Programme

- Father of India's nuclear programme: **Dr. Homi J. Bhabha**.
- India's first nuclear reactor: **Apsara** (1956), at BARC, Mumbai — also the first in Asia.
- First nuclear test: **"Smiling Buddha"** at **Pokhran, Rajasthan, 1974**.
- Second series of tests: **Pokhran-II (Operation Shakti), 1998**.
- Nuclear power plants: Tarapur (Maharashtra), Rawatbhata (Rajasthan), Kalpakkam (Tamil Nadu), Narora (UP), Kakrapar (Gujarat), Kaiga (Karnataka), Kudankulam (Tamil Nadu — largest, built with Russian collaboration).

## 6. Defence: Missiles and Systems

India's missile programme was guided by **Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** ("Missile Man of India") under the **Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP)**.

| Missile | Type |
|---|---|
| Prithvi | Surface-to-surface, short range |
| Agni (I–V) | Surface-to-surface ballistic; Agni-V range over 5,000 km |
| Akash | Surface-to-air |
| Nag | Anti-tank guided missile |
| Trishul | Short-range surface-to-air |
| BrahMos | Supersonic cruise missile (India–Russia joint venture) |

🧠 **Active Recall:** Which missile is a joint venture with Russia? Which Agni has the longest range? Who is called the Missile Man of India?

> 🪄 **Mnemonic for IGMDP missiles:** **"PANT"** — **P**rithvi, **A**gni, **N**ag, **T**rishul (plus **A**kash — "PANTA"). Picture a missile wearing pants!

## 7. Everyday Science Basics

- **Vitamin C** deficiency → Scurvy; **Vitamin D** → Rickets; **Vitamin A** → Night blindness; **Vitamin B1** → Beri-beri.
- **pH value** of human blood ≈ **7.4** (slightly alkaline).
- Normal human body temperature: **37°C (98.6°F)**.
- Light year = unit of **distance**, not time.
- Speed of light ≈ **3 × 10⁸ m/s**.
- Sound travels fastest in **solids**, slowest in **gases**.
- **Red blood cells** are produced in the **bone marrow**.
- The **largest gland** in the human body is the **liver**.

## ⚠️ Caution: Examiner's Trap

**Trap 1 — "Almost-True Statement" (लगभग सही कथन)**
*"Aryabhata was launched by India using its own rocket."* — Sounds right, but Aryabhata (1975) was launched by the **Soviet Union**. India's own orbital launch was **Rohini via SLV-3 (1980)**. Examiners swap the launcher to catch you.

**Trap 2 — "Similar Names/Years" (समान नाम/वर्ष)**
**Rutherford discovered the proton; Chadwick discovered the neutron.** Both worked on the atomic nucleus, and examiners routinely interchange them. Also: **Becquerel discovered radioactivity; the Curies discovered radium and polonium** — don't mix these pairs.

**Trap 3 — "Negative Phrasing" (नकारात्मक वाक्यांश)**
*"Which of the following is NOT a base SI unit?"* — Options: metre, kelvin, **newton**, mole. The word "NOT" is hidden in a long question. Newton is a *derived* unit. Always circle the negative word before answering.
