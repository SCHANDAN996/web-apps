26. Where is the headquarters of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) located?
(a) Washington D.C. (b) Geneva (c) New York (d) Paris
Answer: (a)
Solution: New York is tempting because the UN headquarters is there, but the IMF and the World Bank are both headquartered in Washington D.C., USA. The IMF was established in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference and began operations in 1945, so the answer is Washington D.C.
Source: IMF official website (imf.org)

27. How many permanent members does the UN Security Council have?
(a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 5 (d) 7
Answer: (c)
Solution: 15 is tempting because that is the total membership of the Security Council, but only 5 members — China, France, Russia, the UK and the USA — are permanent with veto power; the other 10 are non-permanent members elected for two years, so the answer is 5.
Source: UN official website (un.org)

28. The headquarters of UNESCO is located in which city?
(a) Rome (b) Paris (c) Geneva (d) Vienna
Answer: (b)
Solution: Geneva is tempting because many UN agencies like WHO and ILO are based there, but UNESCO (United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization), founded in 1945, has its headquarters in Paris, France, so the answer is Paris.
Source: UNESCO official website (unesco.org)

29. Which of the following organizations is headquartered in Rome, Italy?
(a) Food and Agriculture Organization (b) World Health Organization (c) International Labour Organization (d) World Trade Organization
Answer: (a)
Solution: WHO and ILO are tempting because they are UN specialized agencies, but both are headquartered in Geneva; the FAO, founded in 1945, has its headquarters in Rome, so the answer is Food and Agriculture Organization.
Source: FAO official website (fao.org)

30. The World Trade Organization (WTO) came into existence on which date?
(a) 1 January 1991 (b) 1 January 1994 (c) 24 October 1945 (d) 1 January 1995
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1 January 1994 is tempting because the Uruguay Round agreement (Marrakesh Agreement) was signed in 1994, but the WTO officially replaced GATT and came into existence on 1 January 1995, so the answer is 1 January 1995.
Source: WTO official website (wto.org)

31. Which country is NOT a permanent member of the UN Security Council?
(a) France (b) Germany (c) Russia (d) China
Answer: (b)
Solution: France is tempting because it is sometimes overlooked among the great powers, but it is a permanent member; Germany, despite being a major economy and a G4 aspirant, is not a permanent member of the Security Council, so the answer is Germany.
Source: UN official website (un.org)

32. The headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO) is in which city?
(a) Geneva (b) The Hague (c) Brussels (d) New York
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Hague is tempting because the International Court of Justice is located there, but the ILO, founded in 1919 under the League of Nations and now a UN specialized agency, is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: ILO official website (ilo.org)

33. The International Court of Justice (ICJ) is located at which place?
(a) Geneva (b) New York (c) Paris (d) The Hague
Answer: (d)
Solution: New York is tempting because most principal organs of the UN are located there, but the ICJ is the only principal organ not in New York — it sits at the Peace Palace in The Hague, Netherlands, so the answer is The Hague.
Source: ICJ official website (icj-cij.org)

34. How many judges serve on the International Court of Justice?
(a) 9 (b) 15 (c) 11 (d) 21
Answer: (b)
Solution: 9 is tempting because it resembles the strength of some national supreme courts, but the ICJ consists of 15 judges elected for nine-year terms by the UN General Assembly and Security Council, so the answer is 15.
Source: ICJ official website (icj-cij.org)

35. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of establishment?
(a) IMF → UN → WTO (b) UN → IMF → WTO (c) WTO → UN → IMF (d) UN → WTO → IMF
Answer: (a)
Solution: Option with UN first is tempting because the UN is the most famous, but the IMF was founded at Bretton Woods in 1944, the UN in 1945, and the WTO in 1995 — a classic chronological-confusion trap, so the answer is IMF → UN → WTO.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

36. The headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO) is located in which city?
(a) London (b) Washington D.C. (c) Geneva (d) Rome
Answer: (c)
Solution: Washington D.C. is tempting because the Pan American Health Organization is there, but the WHO, established in 1948, has its headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: WHO official website (who.int)

37. Which organization is known as the "lender of last resort" for countries facing balance of payments crises?
(a) World Bank (b) Asian Development Bank (c) International Monetary Fund (d) WTO
Answer: (c)
Solution: World Bank is tempting because it also lends to countries, but it focuses on long-term development projects; the IMF provides short-term financial assistance to countries facing balance of payments problems, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Economics (Indian Economic Development)

38. The headquarters of the Asian Development Bank (ADB) is located in which city?
(a) Bangkok (b) Manila (c) Jakarta (d) Singapore
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bangkok is tempting because it hosts several UN regional offices like ESCAP, but the ADB, established in 1966, has its headquarters in Manila, Philippines, so the answer is Manila.
Source: ADB official website (adb.org)

39. How many member countries does the United Nations have (as of 2024)?
(a) 190 (b) 195 (c) 193 (d) 197
Answer: (c)
Solution: 195 is tempting because it is often quoted as the number of countries in the world (including two observer states), but the UN has 193 member states; the Holy See and Palestine are only observer states, so the answer is 193.
Source: UN official website (un.org)

40. The New Development Bank (NDB), established by BRICS countries, is headquartered in which city?
(a) Beijing (b) New Delhi (c) Shanghai (d) Moscow
Answer: (c)
Solution: Beijing is tempting because it is China's capital and hosts the AIIB, but the BRICS New Development Bank, established in 2015, is headquartered in Shanghai, China, so the answer is Shanghai.
Source: NDB official website (ndb.int)

41. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) UNHCR — Geneva (b) UNICEF — Paris (c) WIPO — Vienna (d) IAEA — Rome
Answer: (a)
Solution: UNICEF–Paris is tempting because UNESCO is in Paris, but UNICEF is headquartered in New York; WIPO is in Geneva and IAEA is in Vienna, so the only correct match is UNHCR — Geneva, so the answer is UNHCR — Geneva.
Source: UN official websites of respective agencies

42. The term of a non-permanent member of the UN Security Council is how long?
(a) 1 year (b) 5 years (c) 3 years (d) 2 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: 5 years is tempting because it matches the term of ICJ judges' renewal cycle confusion, but non-permanent members of the Security Council are elected by the General Assembly for a term of 2 years, with five elected each year, so the answer is 2 years.
Source: UN official website (un.org)

43. The headquarters of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) is in which city?
(a) Geneva (b) The Hague (c) Paris (d) Vienna
Answer: (d)
Solution: Geneva is tempting because many UN bodies are based there, but the IAEA, established in 1957, has its headquarters in Vienna, Austria — which also hosts UNIDO and UNODC, so the answer is Vienna.
Source: IAEA official website (iaea.org)

44. Which organization replaced the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT)?
(a) WTO (b) World Bank (c) IMF (d) UNCTAD
Answer: (a)
Solution: UNCTAD is tempting because it also deals with trade, but it is a UN body for trade and development established in 1964; the WTO replaced GATT on 1 January 1995 after the Uruguay Round, so the answer is WTO.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Economics (Indian Economic Development)

45. The headquarters of SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) is located in which city?
(a) New Delhi (b) Colombo (c) Kathmandu (d) Dhaka
Answer: (c)
Solution: New Delhi is tempting because India is the largest SAARC member, but the SAARC Secretariat, established after SAARC's formation in 1985, is located in Kathmandu, Nepal, so the answer is Kathmandu.
Source: SAARC official website (saarc-sec.org)

46. Which of the following statements about the World Bank is correct?
(a) It was established in 1955 at the Bandung Conference (b) It was established in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference (c) It is headquartered in New York (d) It is a specialized agency of the WTO
Answer: (b)
Solution: New York is tempting in the third statement because the UN is there, but the World Bank is headquartered in Washington D.C.; both the World Bank (IBRD) and the IMF were created at the 1944 Bretton Woods Conference, so the answer is "It was established in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference."
Source: World Bank official website (worldbank.org)

47. The headquarters of OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries) is in which city?
(a) Riyadh (b) Doha (c) Geneva (d) Vienna
Answer: (d)
Solution: Riyadh is tempting because Saudi Arabia is the leading OPEC member, but OPEC, founded in 1960 in Baghdad, has had its headquarters in Vienna, Austria since 1965, so the answer is Vienna.
Source: OPEC official website (opec.org)

48. Which UN agency is primarily responsible for the welfare of children worldwide?
(a) UNESCO (b) WFP (c) UNHCR (d) UNICEF
Answer: (d)
Solution: UNESCO is tempting because it works on education which involves children, but its mandate is education, science and culture broadly; UNICEF (United Nations Children's Fund), created in 1946, works specifically for children's welfare, so the answer is UNICEF.
Source: UNICEF official website (unicef.org)

49. The headquarters of the European Union's main institutions, including the European Commission, is in which city?
(a) Strasbourg (b) Brussels (c) Luxembourg (d) Frankfurt
Answer: (b)
Solution: Strasbourg is tempting because the European Parliament holds sessions there, and Frankfurt hosts the European Central Bank, but the European Commission and the Council of the EU are headquartered in Brussels, Belgium, so the answer is Brussels.
Source: European Union official website (europa.eu)

50. Which of the following organizations was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 2020?
(a) World Food Programme (b) WHO (c) UNICEF (d) UNHCR
Answer: (a)
Solution: WHO is tempting because it was prominent during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, but the Nobel Peace Prize 2020 was awarded to the World Food Programme for its efforts to combat hunger and prevent its use as a weapon of war, so the answer is World Food Programme.
Source: Nobel Prize official website (nobelprize.org)
