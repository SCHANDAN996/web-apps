# 🧒 The Feynman Corner — International Organizations

## The Most Confusing Concept: IMF vs. World Bank

Ask any exam aspirant the difference between the **IMF** and the **World Bank**, and watch them freeze. Both were born at the same conference (Bretton Woods, 1944), both are headquartered in Washington D.C., both lend money to countries, and both have nearly the same members. No wonder examiners love trapping you here!

Let me explain it the way I'd explain it to a 12-year-old.

---

### 🏥 Imagine Your Country Is a Person Who Falls Sick

**The IMF is the Emergency Room (ER).**

Your country suddenly can't pay for imports, its currency is crashing, foreign investors are fleeing — it's an *emergency*. You rush to the IMF. The IMF gives you a **short-term loan** to stop the bleeding. But the ER doctor is strict: "Take this medicine (cut government spending, raise interest rates, devalue your currency), or no more help." These strict conditions are called **conditionalities**.

**The World Bank is the Family Doctor for Long-Term Health.**

Your country isn't dying — it's just *underdeveloped*. Bad roads, no electricity in villages, weak schools. The World Bank gives **long-term loans** (sometimes 30+ years, at very low interest) to build dams, highways, schools, and irrigation projects. It's about *growth*, not survival.

### One-Line Memory Anchor

> **IMF = "I'm Monetary Fund" → fixes MONEY crises (short-term).**
> **World Bank = "Build" Bank → builds the country (long-term).**

---

### 📋 Side-by-Side (The Examiner's Trap Zone)

| Feature | IMF | World Bank |
|---|---|---|
| Nickname | "Lender of last resort" | "Development Bank" |
| Purpose | Fix **balance of payments** crises | Fund **development projects** |
| Loan type | Short/medium-term, with strict conditions | Long-term, low/zero interest |
| Members | 190+ countries | 189 countries (IBRD) |
| Head's nationality (by convention) | Always a **European** | Always an **American** |
| Special unit | SDR (Special Drawing Rights) — "paper gold" | IDA gives interest-free loans to poorest nations |

⚠️ **Trap Alert:** The examiners swap these two constantly. "Which body gives long-term loans for dam construction?" — If you answer IMF, you've fallen into the trap. Also remember: **SDR belongs to the IMF, not the World Bank.**

---

### 🗣️ Explain It Back (The Real Feynman Test)

If you can say this out loud without notes, you've mastered it:

*"The IMF is like an emergency room that rescues countries whose money is collapsing, with short-term loans and strict conditions. The World Bank is like a family doctor that slowly builds a country's health with long-term loans for roads, dams, and schools. Both were born at Bretton Woods in 1944 and sit in Washington D.C. — that's why everyone confuses them."*
