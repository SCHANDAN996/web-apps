# Key Facts & Figures — International Organizations

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| UN founded | 24 October 1945, San Francisco Conference; Charter signed 26 June 1945 | Confusing Charter signing date (26 June) with UN Day (24 October) | Date confusion |
| UN Headquarters | New York, USA | Geneva/Vienna/Paris given as distractors | Similar-name trap |
| UN official languages | 6 — English, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Arabic | Hindi often given as a tempting wrong option | Near-correct trap |
| UN founding members | 51 original members; 193 members at present | 51 vs 193 swapped in options | Number swap |
| UN Security Council | 15 members: 5 permanent (USA, UK, France, Russia, China) + 10 non-permanent (2-year term) | Asking which country is NOT permanent; Germany/Japan/India as distractors | Negative statement |
| Veto power | Only the 5 permanent members (P5) hold veto | "All 15 members have veto" — false | Exaggeration trap |
| UN General Assembly | All 193 members; one nation, one vote; resolutions are recommendations | Claiming GA resolutions are binding | Concept reversal |
| UN Secretary-General | Appointed by General Assembly on Security Council's recommendation; 5-year term, renewable | "Elected directly by GA" or "appointed by SC alone" | Process confusion |
| First UN Secretary-General | Trygve Lie (Norway) | Dag Hammarskjöld (2nd) or António Guterres (9th, since 2017) as distractors | Order confusion |
| International Court of Justice | The Hague, Netherlands; 15 judges, 9-year term; principal judicial organ of UN | ICJ confused with ICC (International Criminal Court, also at The Hague but NOT a UN organ) | Similar-name trap |
| UN's 6 principal organs | GA, Security Council, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ, Secretariat | WHO/UNESCO/UNICEF listed as "principal organs" — they are specialized agencies | Category trap |
| Trusteeship Council | Suspended operations in 1994 after Palau's independence | "It was abolished" — false; it is suspended, not dissolved | Near-correct trap |
| WHO | Founded 7 April 1948 (World Health Day); HQ Geneva | 1948 vs 1945 confusion; HQ Paris as distractor | Date/place confusion |
| UNESCO | Founded 1945 (16 November); HQ Paris | HQ confused with Geneva (WHO/ILO/WTO) | Place confusion |
| UNICEF | Created 1946; HQ New York; Nobel Peace Prize 1965 | "Founded 1945" trap | Date confusion |
| ILO | Founded 1919 (Treaty of Versailles); HQ Geneva; Nobel Peace Prize 1969; only tripartite UN agency | Only UN agency older than the UN itself — asked as "oldest specialized agency" | Fact reversal |
| FAO | Founded 1945; HQ Rome; World Food Day 16 October | HQ Geneva as distractor | Place confusion |
| IMF | Founded 1944 (Bretton Woods); operational 1945; HQ Washington D.C. | IMF vs World Bank roles swapped (IMF = short-term balance-of-payments; WB = long-term development) | Role swap |
| World Bank | Founded 1944 (Bretton Woods); HQ Washington D.C.; IBRD + IDA = World Bank | Confusing World Bank Group (5 institutions) with "World Bank" (2 institutions) | Category trap |
| WTO | Established 1 January 1995; HQ Geneva; succeeded GATT (1947) | "WTO founded 1947" — that was GATT | Predecessor trap |
| NATO | Founded 4 April 1949 (Washington Treaty); HQ Brussels; Article 5 = collective defence | HQ Paris as distractor; "NATO is a UN body" — false | Place/category trap |
| SAARC | Established 8 December 1985, Dhaka; Secretariat Kathmandu; 8 members | Afghanistan's membership year (2007) vs founding 7 members | Number confusion |
| SAARC members | Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka | Myanmar/China given as member distractors | False inclusion |
| ASEAN | Founded 8 August 1967, Bangkok Declaration; Secretariat Jakarta; 10 members | India/China as member distractors (they are dialogue partners, not members) | False inclusion |
| European Union | Maastricht Treaty 1993; HQ Brussels; Nobel Peace Prize 2012 | "EU formed by Treaty of Rome (1957)" — that created the EEC | Predecessor trap |
| OPEC | Founded 1960, Baghdad; HQ Vienna | "HQ Riyadh/Dubai" distractors | Place confusion |
| Commonwealth | 56 members; mostly former British territories; Head = British monarch (symbolic) | "All members accept the monarch as head of state" — false; most are republics | Near-correct trap |
| NAM | Founded 1961, Belgrade; key founders: Nehru, Tito, Nasser, Sukarno, Nkrumah | "NAM founded at Bandung 1955" — Bandung was the precursor conference | Predecessor trap |
| Amnesty International | Founded 1961, London; Nobel Peace Prize 1977 | Confused with Human Rights Watch (1978, New York) | Similar-name trap |
| Red Cross (ICRC) | Founded 1863, Geneva by Henry Dunant; Nobel Peace Prize 1901, 1917, 1944, 1963 (shared) | "Founded during World War I" — it predates WWI | Date confusion |
| BRICS | First summit 2009 (as BRIC); South Africa joined 2010 (BRICS); expanded 2024 | Asking original members; South Africa wrongly listed as founder | Membership trap |
| G20 | Founded 1999 (finance ministers); leaders' summit since 2008; India hosted 2023 summit | "G20 has a permanent secretariat" — false | Concept trap |
| Shanghai Cooperation Organisation | Founded 2001; HQ Beijing; India & Pakistan joined 2017 | "India is a founding member" — false | Membership trap |
| Asian Development Bank | Founded 1966; HQ Manila, Philippines | HQ confused with AIIB (Beijing) | Similar-name trap |
| AIIB | Founded 2016 (operations began); HQ Beijing; India is a founding member | "AIIB is a UN agency" — false | Category trap |
| New Development Bank | BRICS bank; founded 2015; HQ Shanghai | HQ confused with AIIB (Beijing) | Place confusion |
| UNHCR | Founded 1950; HQ Geneva; Nobel Peace Prize 1954, 1981 | Confused with UNRWA (Palestine refugees, 1949) | Similar-name trap |
| India's UN role | Founding member (1945, before independence); largest cumulative troop contributor to peacekeeping | "India joined UN in 1947" — false | Date confusion |
| League of Nations | Founded 1920 (Covenant part of Treaty of Versailles); dissolved 1946; HQ Geneva | "USA was a member" — false; US Senate never ratified | Negative statement |
| UN Charter | Preamble begins "We the peoples of the United Nations"; Chapter VII allows enforcement action | Confusing with US Constitution's "We the People" | Similar-phrase trap |
| ECOSOC | 54 members, 3-year terms; coordinates economic/social work | "54 members elected for 5 years" — term length trap | Number swap |
| International Atomic Energy Agency | Founded 1957; HQ Vienna; Nobel Peace Prize 2005 | "IAEA is a specialized agency" — it reports to GA/SC but is not a specialized agency | Category trap |
| Interpol | Founded 1923; HQ Lyon, France | HQ confused with The Hague | Place confusion |
| World Food Programme | Founded 1961; HQ Rome; Nobel Peace Prize 2020 | Confused with FAO (also Rome) | Similar-place trap |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Topic | Question Type | Time Strategy | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founding year + HQ of major organizations (UN, WHO, UNESCO, IMF, WTO, NATO, SAARC) | Pure recall | 15 sec — answer instantly if memorized | ✅ Yes |
| Membership counts (UN 193, UNSC 15, SAARC 8, ASEAN 10, EU 27) | Pure recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| P5 veto powers / UNSC structure | Pure recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Nobel Peace Prize winning organizations | Pure recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Founders of NAM / first Secretary-General | Pure recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| "Which is NOT a member/permanent member/specialized agency" questions | Negative-statement analysis | 45 sec — read every option carefully | ✅ Yes (high accuracy if careful) |
| IMF vs World Bank vs WTO role-based questions | Conceptual comparison | 45 sec | ✅ Yes |
| ICJ vs ICC, GATT vs WTO, League vs UN distinctions | Conceptual comparison | 45 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Statement-based questions (e.g., "GA resolutions are binding", "Trusteeship Council abolished") | Analytical | 45 sec — check each statement word by word | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Chronology questions (order of founding: ILO 1919 → UN 1945 → NATO 1949 → WTO 1995) | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Recent expansion/membership changes (BRICS 2024, SCO 2017) | Recall + currency | 15 sec if revised recently; else skip | ⚠️ Moderate |

## Part 3: Data & Formulas

| Data Point | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| UN founding year | 1945 | Charter signed 26 June; in force 24 October |
| UN members (current) | 193 | 51 founding members |
| UNSC composition | 5 permanent + 10 non-permanent | Non-permanent: 2-year term, elected by GA |
| UN official languages | 6 | Arabic added in 1973 |
| UN Secretary-General term | 5 years (renewable) | No formal limit on terms |
| ICJ judges | 15 judges, 9-year term | One-third elected every 3 years |
| ECOSOC members | 54, 3-year terms | — |
| SAARC members | 8 | Founded 1985; Afghanistan joined 2007 |
| ASEAN members | 10 | Founded 1967 with 5 members |
| EU members | 27 | After Brexit (2020) |
| NATO members | 32 | Finland (2023) and Sweden (2024) latest |
| Commonwealth members | 56 | — |
| G20 members | 19 countries + EU + African Union | AU joined 2023 |
| WTO members | 164 | Founded 1995 |
| IMF/World Bank founding | 1944 | Bretton Woods Conference |
| Voting weight in IMF/World Bank | Proportional to financial quota | Unlike UN's one-nation-one-vote |
| Nobel Peace Prizes to organizations (key) | ICRC (4 times incl. shared), UNHCR (2), UNICEF 1965, ILO 1969, UN & Kofi Annan 2001, IAEA 2005, EU 2012, WFP 2020 | High-yield list |
| League of Nations lifespan | 1920–1946 | Succeeded by UN |
| Chronology anchor | ILO 1919 → League 1920 → UN 1945 → NATO 1949 → EEC 1957 → NAM 1961 → ASEAN 1967 → SAARC 1985 → WTO 1995 | Use for order-based questions |
