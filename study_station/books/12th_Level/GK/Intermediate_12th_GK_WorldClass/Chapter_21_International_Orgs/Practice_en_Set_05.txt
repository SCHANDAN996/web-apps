101. The headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO) is located in:
(a) New York (b) Vienna (c) Paris (d) Geneva
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many UN agencies are in New York, which tempts students to pick it, but the ILO, founded in 1919, has always been headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

102. How many permanent members does the UN Security Council have?
(a) 10 (b) 5 (c) 15 (d) 20
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Council has 15 members in total, which is the trap figure, but only 5 are permanent — China, France, Russia, the UK and the USA; the other 10 are elected for two years, so the answer is 5.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

103. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) was established in which year?
(a) 1919 (b) 1965 (c) 1951 (d) 1944
Answer: (d)
Solution: The IMF formally came into existence in 1945, which confuses many, but it was established (Articles drawn up) at the Bretton Woods Conference in 1944, so the answer is 1944.
Source: IMF official website (imf.org)

104. The headquarters of UNESCO is located in:

106. The World Health Organization (WHO) was established in:
(a) 1945 (b) 1948 (c) 1950 (d) 1955
Answer: (b)
Solution: The UN itself was founded in 1945, which is the trap year, but WHO came into force on 7 April 1948 — the date now observed as World Health Day, so the answer is 1948.
Source: WHO official website (who.int)

108. The World Trade Organization (WTO) replaced which body in 1995?
(a) GATT (b) UNCTAD (c) IMF (d) World Bank
Answer: (a)
Solution: The IMF and World Bank still exist, so they cannot be "replaced"; the WTO succeeded the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT, 1948) on 1 January 1995, so the answer is GATT.
Source: WTO official website (wto.org)

109. The headquarters of the International Court of Justice (ICJ) is located in:
(a) New York (b) The Hague (c) Geneva (d) Brussels
Answer: (b)
Solution: New York hosts the UN headquarters, which misleads many, but the ICJ — the UN's principal judicial organ — sits at the Peace Palace in The Hague, Netherlands, so the answer is The Hague.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

121. Consider the following statements about the UN Security Council:
1. It has 10 non-permanent members elected for two-year terms.
2. Non-permanent members are elected by the General Assembly.
3. A permanent member's veto can block any substantive resolution.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to doubt statement 3, but the veto power of the five permanent members over substantive (non-procedural) matters is real; non-permanent members (10, two-year terms) are indeed elected by the General Assembly, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

122. Match the organizations with their headquarters:
1. UNICEF — New York
2. UNESCO — Paris
3. IMF — Washington D.C.
4. FAO — Geneva
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 only (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is pair 4: FAO is in Rome, not Geneva, so any option containing pair 4 is wrong; UNICEF (New York), UNESCO (Paris) and IMF (Washington D.C.) are correctly matched, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3 only.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

123. Arrange the following in chronological order of their establishment:
1. United Nations
2. League of Nations
3. World Trade Organization
4. GATT
(a) 2, 1, 3, 4 (b) 1, 2, 4, 3 (c) 2, 4, 1, 3 (d) 2, 1, 4, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing GATT after the WTO or the UN before the League; the correct order is League of Nations (1920), United Nations (1945), GATT (1948), WTO (1995), so the answer is 2, 1, 4, 3.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

124. Consider the following statements about the International Court of Justice:
1. It consists of 15 judges elected for nine-year terms.
2. Judges are elected by the Security Council alone.
3. It is the successor of the Permanent Court of International Justice.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is statement 2: ICJ judges are elected by both the General Assembly and the Security Council voting independently, not by the Security Council alone; statements 1 and 3 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.
Source: ICJ official website (icj-cij.org)

125. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Amnesty International — London (b) Commonwealth Secretariat — Geneva (c) NATO — Brussels (d) International Criminal Court — The Hague
Answer: (b)
Solution: All four sound plausible, which is the trap, but the Commonwealth Secretariat is headquartered at Marlborough House in London, not Geneva; the other three pairs are correct, so the answer is Commonwealth Secretariat — Geneva.
Source: Commonwealth official website (thecommonwealth.org)
