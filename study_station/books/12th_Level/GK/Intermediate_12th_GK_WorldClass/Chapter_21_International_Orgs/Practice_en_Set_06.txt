Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Which of the following correctly pairs a UN specialized agency with its headquarters?
(a) UNESCO — Geneva (b) WHO — Paris (c) IMF — Vienna (d) FAO — Rome
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is pairing UNESCO with Geneva — UNESCO is in Paris, while Geneva hosts WHO, ILO and WTO. FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization) is headquartered in Rome, so the answer is FAO — Rome.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

127. How many non-permanent members does the UN Security Council have, and what is their term?
(a) 5 members, 5 years (b) 10 members, 2 years (c) 15 members, 3 years (d) 10 members, 1 year
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is confusing total UNSC strength (15) with non-permanent members. The Council has 5 permanent members plus 10 non-permanent members elected by the General Assembly for two-year terms, so the answer is 10 members, 2 years.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

128. The UN Charter was signed on 26 June 1945 at San Francisco, but the United Nations officially came into existence on:
(a) 26 June 1945 (b) 1 January 1946 (c) 10 December 1945 (d) 24 October 1945
Answer: (d)
Solution: The chronological trap is the signing date, 26 June 1945. The UN came into being only after the Charter was ratified by the required majority including the five permanent members, on 24 October 1945 — now celebrated as UN Day, so the answer is 24 October 1945.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

129. Which of the following is NOT one of the six principal organs of the United Nations?
(a) Trusteeship Council (b) Economic and Social Council (c) United Nations Development Programme (d) International Court of Justice
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that UNDP sounds like a core UN body, but it is a programme/fund, not a principal organ. The six principal organs are the General Assembly, Security Council, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ and Secretariat, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

130. The International Court of Justice is seated at:
(a) Geneva (b) New York (c) Vienna (d) The Hague
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is New York, since most UN organs are based there — the ICJ is the only principal organ NOT located in New York. It sits at the Peace Palace in The Hague, Netherlands, so the answer is The Hague.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

131. Judges of the International Court of Justice are elected for a term of:
(a) 5 years (b) 9 years (c) 6 years (d) Life term
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 6 years, which is the term of the UN Secretary-General's often-confused figures and of some other bodies. The ICJ has 15 judges elected by the General Assembly and Security Council for nine-year terms, with one-third retiring every three years, so the answer is 9 years.
Source: Official website of the International Court of Justice (icj-cij.org)

132. Which country is NOT a permanent member of the UN Security Council?
(a) Germany (b) Russia (c) France (d) United Kingdom
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Germany, a frequent G4 aspirant often mistakenly assumed to be permanent. The five permanent members (P5) with veto power are the USA, Russia, China, France and the UK, so the answer is Germany.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

133. The G4 nations, which support each other's bids for permanent UNSC seats, consist of:
(a) India, Japan, Germany, Brazil (b) India, Japan, France, Brazil (c) India, China, Germany, South Africa (d) India, Japan, Germany, South Africa
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is South Africa, which belongs to the IBSA grouping, not G4. The G4 comprises India, Japan, Germany and Brazil, so the answer is India, Japan, Germany, Brazil.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

134. The headquarters of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) is located in:
(a) New York (b) Washington D.C. (c) Geneva (d) London
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is New York, home of the UN headquarters. Both the IMF and the World Bank — the Bretton Woods twins of 1944 — are headquartered in Washington D.C., so the answer is Washington D.C.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

135. The World Trade Organization (WTO) replaced GATT and came into existence on:
(a) 1 January 1948 (b) 15 April 1994 (c) 1 January 1995 (d) 1 January 1996
Answer: (c)
Solution: The chronological trap is 15 April 1994, the date of the Marrakesh Agreement that created the WTO. The organization itself commenced functioning on 1 January 1995, so the answer is 1 January 1995.
Source: Official website of the WTO (wto.org)

136. Which of the following statements about the World Bank is correct?
(a) Its largest arm, IBRD, was established in 1944 (b) It is headquartered in Geneva (c) It was founded at the San Francisco Conference (d) It lends only to least-developed countries
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the San Francisco Conference, which produced the UN Charter — the World Bank and IMF arose from the Bretton Woods Conference of 1944. The International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) was established in 1944 and began operations in 1946, so the answer is that IBRD was established in 1944.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

137. SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) has its secretariat at:
(a) New Delhi (b) Colombo (c) Kathmandu (d) Dhaka
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Dhaka, where SAARC was founded in 1985 — founding place and headquarters differ here. The SAARC Secretariat is located in Kathmandu, Nepal, so the answer is Kathmandu.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

138. How many member countries does SAARC have?
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 10
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 7, the number of founding members in 1985. Afghanistan joined as the eighth member in 2007, taking the total to eight, so the answer is 8.

139. ASEAN (Association of Southeast Asian Nations) was established in 1967 by the Bangkok Declaration. Its secretariat is at:
(a) Bangkok (b) Kuala Lumpur (c) Singapore (d) Jakarta
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Bangkok, where the founding declaration was signed — again, founding city and headquarters differ. The ASEAN Secretariat is in Jakarta, Indonesia, so the answer is Jakarta.
Source: Official website of ASEAN (asean.org)

140. Which of the following countries is NOT a member of ASEAN?
(a) Myanmar (b) Brunei (c) Timor-Leste (d) Laos
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Brunei, a small state people often forget is a full member since 1984. Timor-Leste (East Timor) has long held observer status and was not a full member in the standard exam framework of ten members, so the answer is Timor-Leste.
Source: Official website of ASEAN (asean.org)

141. The European Union was formally established by which treaty, signed in 1992?
(a) Treaty of Rome (b) Lisbon Treaty (c) Maastricht Treaty (d) Treaty of Paris
Answer: (c)
Solution: The chronological trap is the Treaty of Rome (1957), which created the European Economic Community, not the EU. The Maastricht Treaty of 1992 (effective 1993) formally established the European Union, so the answer is Maastricht Treaty.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

142. The headquarters of the European Union's main institutions (European Commission and Council) is in:
(a) Strasbourg (b) Brussels (c) Luxembourg (d) Frankfurt
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Strasbourg, seat of the European Parliament's plenary sessions, and Frankfurt, home of the European Central Bank. The European Commission and the Council of the EU are based in Brussels, so the answer is Brussels.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

143. The African Union (AU), which replaced the Organisation of African Unity, was launched in 2002. Its headquarters is at:
(a) Nairobi (b) Johannesburg (c) Cairo (d) Addis Ababa
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Nairobi, a major UN office city in Africa. The African Union is headquartered in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia — the same city that hosted the OAU, so the answer is Addis Ababa.
Source: Official website of the African Union (au.int)

144. Which of the following pairs of organization and founding year is INCORRECT?
(a) NATO — 1949 (b) Red Cross — 1919 (c) Amnesty International — 1961 (d) OPEC — 1960
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that 1919 is the founding year of the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies. The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) was founded in 1863 by Henry Dunant and others, so the incorrect pair is Red Cross — 1919.
Source: Official website of the ICRC (icrc.org)

145. The headquarters of NATO (North Atlantic Treaty Organization) is located in:
(a) Washington D.C. (b) Paris (c) London (d) Brussels
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Washington D.C., where the North Atlantic Treaty was signed in 1949. NATO's political headquarters has been in Brussels, Belgium, since 1967 (after moving from Paris), so the answer is Brussels.
Source: Official website of NATO (nato.int)

146. OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries) is headquartered at:
(a) Vienna (b) Baghdad (c) Riyadh (d) Geneva
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Riyadh or Baghdad — OPEC was founded in Baghdad in 1960, but its headquarters is not in any member country of the Gulf. The OPEC Secretariat is in Vienna, Austria, so the answer is Vienna.
Source: Official website of OPEC (opec.org)

147. Which of the following statements about the UN General Assembly is correct?
(a) Each member state has one vote (b) Its decisions on all matters are binding on members (c) It meets only once every two years (d) Its members include only sovereign states recognized by the Security Council
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the binding-power statement — General Assembly resolutions are generally recommendations, not binding law. The Assembly works on the principle of one nation, one vote, with all 193 member states equal, so the answer is that each member state has one vote.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

148. The Secretary-General of the United Nations is appointed by:
(a) The Security Council alone (b) The International Court of Justice (c) The General Assembly on the recommendation of the Security Council (d) The Economic and Social Council
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is "Security Council alone" — the Council only recommends, and its permanent members can block a candidate. Formal appointment is made by the General Assembly upon the Security Council's recommendation, so the answer is the General Assembly on the recommendation of the Security Council.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

149. Which of the following organizations has India as a founding member?
(a) United Nations (b) ASEAN (c) European Union (d) NATO
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is ASEAN — India is a dialogue partner of ASEAN, not a member. India, though not yet independent in 1945, was among the 51 founding members that signed the UN Charter, so the answer is United Nations.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

150. The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) is headquartered at Beijing. India became a full member of the SCO in:
(a) 2017 (b) 2005 (c) 2001 (d) 2019
Answer: (a)
Solution: The chronological trap is 2005, when India became an SCO observer, and 2001, when the SCO itself was founded. India (along with Pakistan) was admitted as a full member at the Astana Summit in June 2017, so the answer is 2017.
Source: Official website of the SCO (eng.sectsco.org)
