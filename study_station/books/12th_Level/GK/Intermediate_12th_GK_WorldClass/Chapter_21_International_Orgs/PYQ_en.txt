# 📊 PYQ Analysis — International Organizations

## How Exams Test This Chapter

International Organizations is one of the most reliable scoring areas in Static GK. Across SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD), Banking (IBPS, SBI), Railway (NTPC, Group D) and State PCS prelims, questions from this chapter appear in almost every paper. The good news: the questions are highly repetitive in *pattern*, even when the exact question changes.

### Sub-topics Examiners Love Most

1. **Headquarters of organizations** — the single most asked sub-topic. UN (New York), UNESCO (Paris), WHO (Geneva), IMF & World Bank (Washington D.C.), WTO (Geneva), ILO (Geneva), FAO (Rome), ICJ (The Hague), NATO (Brussels), SAARC (Kathmandu), ASEAN (Jakarta), OPEC (Vienna), Amnesty International (London).
2. **Founding years and founding conferences** — UN (1945, San Francisco Conference), UN Charter effective 24 October 1945, League of Nations (1920), IMF/World Bank (Bretton Woods, 1944), WTO (1995, replacing GATT), NATO (1949), EU (Maastricht Treaty, 1993), SAARC (1985), NAM (1961, Belgrade).
3. **UN organs and their functions** — six principal organs; which one is in The Hague (ICJ, not New York); which organ's membership includes non-UN members.
4. **Membership numbers and new members** — UN has 193 members; newest member South Sudan (2011); non-member observer states (Vatican City, Palestine).
5. **Heads of organizations** — change frequently; exams ask the *current* head, so this is the riskiest sub-topic (revise close to exam date).
6. **India's connection** — founding member of UN, NAM, Commonwealth; UNSC non-permanent seat terms; India and G20, BRICS, SCO.
7. **Specialized agencies vs. funds/programmes** — UNESCO, WHO, ILO are specialized agencies; UNICEF, UNDP, UNEP are funds/programmes. Examiners exploit this confusion.

### Exam-wise Emphasis (in words, not numbers)

- **SSC (CGL/CHSL/MTS/GD):** Headquarters and founding years dominate. One-liner factual questions. Geneva-based organizations are asked again and again.
- **Banking (IBPS/SBI):** Financial institutions get priority — IMF, World Bank group (IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID), ADB, AIIB, NDB (BRICS Bank), and their heads/headquarters.
- **Railway (NTPC/Group D):** Simple headquarters and full-form questions; occasionally UN Day, member counts.
- **State PCS:** Mix of headquarters plus India's role — UNSC membership, India's contributions, organizations headquartered in India or South Asia (SAARC — Kathmandu).

### The Examiner's Traps (जाल)

1. **Almost Correct:** "UNESCO headquarters — Geneva" (it is Paris; Geneva hosts WHO, ILO, WTO, UNHCR, ITU, WIPO, WMO). Geneva is the decoy city for almost every organization question.
2. **Chronological Confusion:** Swapping founding years — UN 1945 vs. League of Nations 1920 vs. WTO 1995 vs. GATT 1947. Or asking which came first: IMF or WTO.
3. **Negative Phrasing:** "Which of the following is NOT a specialized agency of the UN?" (Answer type: UNICEF — it is a fund/programme.) Students misread "NOT" under time pressure.
4. **Similar Names:** World Bank vs. IMF (both Washington D.C., both Bretton Woods 1944); ICJ (The Hague) vs. ICC — International Criminal Court (also The Hague, but NOT a UN organ); ILO vs. ITU.
5. **City-Country Swap:** "ICJ — Geneva, Switzerland" (wrong: The Hague, Netherlands). "OPEC — Geneva" (wrong: Vienna, Austria).
6. **Outdated Heads:** Options listing a previous Secretary-General or previous WHO chief. Always verify the current head before the exam.

## Top 10 High-Yield Questions (with Solutions and Trap Tags)

**Q1. The headquarters of UNESCO is located at:**
(a) Geneva (b) Paris (c) New York (d) Rome
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** UNESCO (United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization) is headquartered in Paris, France. Geneva (option a) is the classic decoy — it hosts WHO, ILO and WTO, but not UNESCO.
**Trap:** Almost Correct (Geneva decoy).
**⏱ 15/45-second rule:** This is a 15-second question. Headquarters of major bodies must be instant recall; if you need more than 15 seconds, mark and move on.

**Q2. The International Court of Justice is headquartered at:**
(a) Geneva (b) New York (c) The Hague (d) Vienna
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** The ICJ sits at The Hague, Netherlands. It is the only principal organ of the UN not located in New York.
**Trap:** Almost Correct — New York is offered because all other UN organs are there.

**Q3. Which of the following is NOT a specialized agency of the United Nations?**
(a) WHO (b) ILO (c) UNICEF (d) FAO
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** UNICEF is a fund/programme of the UN, not a specialized agency. WHO, ILO and FAO are specialized agencies with their own membership and budgets.
**Trap:** Negative Phrasing — read "NOT" twice before answering.

**Q4. The United Nations officially came into existence on:**
(a) 26 June 1945 (b) 24 October 1945 (c) 1 January 1942 (d) 10 December 1948
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** The UN Charter was *signed* on 26 June 1945 (San Francisco) but came into *force* on 24 October 1945 — celebrated as UN Day. 10 December 1948 is the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
**Trap:** Chronological Confusion — signing date vs. enforcement date.

**Q5. The Bretton Woods Conference (1944) led to the creation of:**
(a) UN and UNESCO (b) IMF and World Bank (c) WTO and GATT (d) FAO and ILO
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** The 1944 Bretton Woods Conference (USA) created the IMF and the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (World Bank). Both are headquartered in Washington D.C.
**Trap:** Similar Names — WTO came much later (1995).

**Q6. The headquarters of SAARC is located at:**
(a) New Delhi (b) Dhaka (c) Kathmandu (d) Colombo
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** SAARC (established 1985, Dhaka Declaration) has its secretariat in Kathmandu, Nepal. New Delhi is the decoy because India is the largest member.
**Trap:** Almost Correct — biggest member's capital offered as bait.
**⏱ 15/45-second rule:** 15-second recall question. If the SAARC secretariat city doesn't come instantly, flag it and return after finishing the section — do not burn 45 seconds here.

**Q7. Which organization replaced GATT in 1995?**
(a) IMF (b) World Bank (c) WTO (d) UNCTAD
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** The World Trade Organization replaced GATT on 1 January 1995 after the Uruguay Round. Headquarters: Geneva.
**Trap:** Chronological Confusion — GATT (1947) vs. WTO (1995).

**Q8. The newest (193rd) member state of the United Nations is:**
(a) Kosovo (b) South Sudan (c) Timor-Leste (d) Montenegro
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** South Sudan joined the UN in July 2011 as the 193rd member. Timor-Leste (2002) and Montenegro (2006) joined earlier; Kosovo is not a UN member.
**Trap:** Chronological Confusion among recent members.

**Q9. The headquarters of OPEC is located at:**
(a) Riyadh (b) Geneva (c) Vienna (d) Doha
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** OPEC (Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries, founded 1960) is headquartered in Vienna, Austria. Riyadh and Doha are decoys because they are oil-country capitals.
**Trap:** Almost Correct — oil-capital decoys plus the Geneva trap.

**Q10. Which of the following is NOT a principal organ of the United Nations?**
(a) International Court of Justice (b) Trusteeship Council (c) UNICEF (d) Economic and Social Council
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** The six principal organs are: General Assembly, Security Council, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ and Secretariat. UNICEF is a fund/programme, not an organ.
**Trap:** Negative Phrasing + Similar Names.

## The 15/45-Second Rule for This Chapter

- **15-second questions (answer instantly or skip):** headquarters, founding years, member counts, full forms. These are pure recall — thinking longer does not help. If not recalled in 15 seconds, mark for review.
- **45-second questions (worth the time):** "NOT"-type questions, match-the-organization-with-headquarters questions, and statement-based questions (e.g., "Which of these statements about the UNSC is correct?"). Here, slow down, underline the negative word, and eliminate options one by one.
- **Golden rule:** Never spend 45 seconds on a headquarters question — either you know it or you don't. Save those seconds for negative-phrasing traps where careful reading wins marks.

## Revision Priority Before the Exam

1. Geneva-based vs. non-Geneva organizations (highest trap density).
2. Founding years in a timeline: 1920 League → 1944 Bretton Woods → 1945 UN → 1947 GATT → 1949 NATO → 1961 NAM → 1985 SAARC → 1993 EU → 1995 WTO.
3. Current heads of UN, WHO, WTO, IMF, World Bank (verify within a month of the exam).
4. India's memberships and UNSC non-permanent terms.
