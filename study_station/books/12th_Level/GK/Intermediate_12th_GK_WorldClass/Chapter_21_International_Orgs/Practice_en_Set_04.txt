76. Which organ of the United Nations is responsible for maintaining international peace and security?
(a) General Assembly (b) Economic and Social Council (c) Security Council (d) Trusteeship Council
Answer: (c)
Solution: The General Assembly is tempting because it is the largest and most visible organ, but it can only make recommendations. Under the UN Charter, primary responsibility for international peace and security rests with the Security Council, so the answer is Security Council.
