1. Where is the headquarters of the United Nations Organization located?
(a) New York (b) Geneva (c) Paris (d) Vienna
Answer: (a)
Solution: Geneva is a common trap because many UN agencies like WHO and ILO are based there, but the main UN headquarters is in New York, USA, established in 1945. So the answer is New York.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

2. How many permanent members does the UN Security Council have?
(a) 15 (b) 10 (c) 5 (d) 7
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Security Council has 15 members in total, which tempts many students, but only 5 are permanent — the USA, Russia, China, France and the UK — each holding veto power. So the answer is 5.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

3. The International Court of Justice is headquartered at —
(a) The Hague (b) Geneva (c) New York (d) Rome
Answer: (a)
Solution: New York is the trap since the UN headquarters is there, but the ICJ, the principal judicial organ of the UN, sits at The Hague in the Netherlands. So the answer is The Hague.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

4. In which year was the United Nations Organization founded?
(a) 1919 (b) 1947 (c) 1945 (d) 1950
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1919 is the trap — that is the year the League of Nations was formed after World War I. The UN was founded on 24 October 1945 after World War II. So the answer is 1945.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

5. Which of the following is the headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO)?
(a) Paris (b) Geneva (c) Washington D.C. (d) London
Answer: (b)
Solution: Paris is tempting because UNESCO is located there, but WHO, founded in 1948, has its headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland. So the answer is Geneva.
Source: Official website of WHO (who.int)

6. The headquarters of UNESCO is located in —
(a) Geneva (b) Vienna (c) New York (d) Paris
Answer: (d)
Solution: Geneva is the trap because WHO and ILO are based there, but UNESCO (United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization) is headquartered in Paris, France. So the answer is Paris.
Source: Official website of UNESCO (unesco.org)

7. Which organization is known as the "lender of last resort" for countries facing balance of payments crises?
(a) World Bank (b) IMF (c) WTO (d) ADB
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank is the trap since it also lends to countries, but it funds long-term development projects; the International Monetary Fund provides short-term assistance for balance of payments problems. So the answer is IMF.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Economics (Indian Economic Development)

8. The World Trade Organization (WTO) was established in —
(a) 1947 (b) 1986 (c) 1995 (d) 2001
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1947 is the trap — that is when GATT, the WTO's predecessor, came into force. The WTO replaced GATT on 1 January 1995. So the answer is 1995.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Economics (Indian Economic Development)

21. Consider the following statements about the UN Security Council:
1. It has 10 non-permanent members elected for two-year terms.
2. Non-permanent members are elected by the General Assembly.
3. India has been a permanent member since 1950.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — India has served several times as a non-permanent member but has never been a permanent member. Statements 1 and 2 are correct: 10 non-permanent members are elected by the General Assembly for two-year terms. So the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

22. Match the organizations with their headquarters:
1. WTO — Geneva
2. UNESCO — Paris
3. FAO — Rome
4. IMF — New York
Which of the above pairs are correctly matched?
(a) 1, 2, 3 and 4 (b) 1 and 4 only (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Pair 4 is the trap — the IMF looks like it should be in New York near the UN, but it is actually in Washington D.C. Pairs 1, 2 and 3 are correctly matched. So the answer is 1, 2 and 3 only.
Source: Official websites of WTO, UNESCO, FAO and IMF

23. Arrange the following organizations in the chronological order of their establishment:
1. United Nations
2. League of Nations
3. WTO
4. GATT
(a) 2, 1, 3, 4 (b) 1, 2, 4, 3 (c) 2, 4, 1, 3 (d) 2, 1, 4, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing GATT after the WTO or the UN before the League. The correct order is: League of Nations (1920), United Nations (1945), GATT (1948), WTO (1995). So the answer is 2, 1, 4, 3.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

24. Consider the following statements about the World Bank and IMF:
1. Both were established at the Bretton Woods Conference of 1944.
2. The World Bank focuses on long-term development financing.
3. The IMF's headquarters is in Geneva.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — Geneva hosts many UN agencies, but the IMF is headquartered in Washington D.C. Statements 1 and 2 are correct: both institutions emerged from Bretton Woods (1944), and the World Bank funds long-term development. So the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Economics (Indian Economic Development)

25. Which of the following statements about the International Court of Justice is correct?
(a) It has 10 judges elected for life terms. (b) It can try individuals for war crimes. (c) It is headquartered in New York along with the UN Secretariat. (d) It has 15 judges elected for nine-year terms by the UN General Assembly and Security Council.
Answer: (d)
Solution: Option (b) is the trap — trying individuals for war crimes is the role of the International Criminal Court, not the ICJ, which settles disputes between states. The ICJ has 15 judges elected for nine-year terms and sits at The Hague. So the answer is "It has 15 judges elected for nine-year terms by the UN General Assembly and Security Council."
Source: Official website of ICJ (icj-cij.org)
