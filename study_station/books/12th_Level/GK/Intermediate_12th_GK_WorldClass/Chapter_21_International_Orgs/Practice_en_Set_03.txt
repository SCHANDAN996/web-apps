Questions 51–75: Medium

51. The headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO) is located in:
(a) Geneva (b) Paris (c) Vienna (d) Rome
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many candidates pick Paris because several UN agencies (UNESCO, OECD) are based there, but the ILO, founded in 1919, has its headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

52. Which of the following is NOT a principal organ of the United Nations?
(a) International Court of Justice (b) Trusteeship Council (c) Economic and Social Council (d) World Health Organization
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is to assume every UN-linked body is a principal organ; WHO is a specialized agency, not a principal organ. The six principal organs are the General Assembly, Security Council, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ and Secretariat, so the answer is World Health Organization.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

53. The Bretton Woods Conference (1944) led to the creation of which pair of institutions?
(a) IMF and World Bank (b) WTO and UNCTAD (c) FAO and UNESCO (d) ADB and AIIB
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is pairing WTO with Bretton Woods; the WTO came only in 1995 from the GATT system. The 1944 Bretton Woods Conference created the IMF and the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (World Bank), so the answer is IMF and World Bank.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

54. How many non-permanent members does the UN Security Council have?
(a) 5 (b) 20 (c) 15 (d) 10
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 15, which is the total membership of the Security Council. Of the 15 members, 5 are permanent and 10 are non-permanent, elected for two-year terms, so the answer is 10.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

55. The headquarters of the World Trade Organization (WTO) is in:
(a) Geneva (b) Washington D.C. (c) New York (d) Brussels
Answer: (a)
Solution: Brussels is tempting because it hosts the EU and NATO, but the WTO, established on 1 January 1995 as successor to GATT, is headquartered in Geneva, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Economics (Indian Economic Development)

56. Which UN agency is primarily responsible for children's welfare worldwide?
(a) UNESCO (b) UNICEF (c) UNHCR (d) WFP
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNHCR is a common wrong pick, but it deals with refugees, not children specifically. UNICEF (United Nations Children's Fund), created in 1946, works for children's welfare, so the answer is UNICEF.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

57. The International Court of Justice is located at:
(a) Geneva (b) The Hague (c) New York (d) Vienna
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Geneva, where many UN bodies sit, but the ICJ, the principal judicial organ of the UN, sits at the Peace Palace in The Hague, Netherlands, so the answer is The Hague.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

58. Which of the following countries is NOT a permanent member of the UN Security Council?
(a) France (b) Russia (c) United Kingdom (d) Germany
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming large economies like Germany or Japan are permanent members; they are not. The five permanent members are China, France, Russia, the UK and the USA, so the answer is Germany.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

59. The headquarters of UNESCO is located in:
(a) Paris (b) Rome (c) London (d) Geneva
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rome is tempting because FAO is there, but UNESCO (United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, 1945) is headquartered in Paris, so the answer is Paris.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

60. The World Health Organization (WHO) was established in which year?
(a) 1945 (b) 1950 (c) 1948 (d) 1951
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 1945, the year the UN itself was founded; WHO came later. WHO was established on 7 April 1948 (observed as World Health Day), with headquarters in Geneva, so the answer is 1948.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

61. The headquarters of the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) is in:
(a) Geneva (b) Paris (c) Rome (d) Vienna
Answer: (c)
Solution: Paris is the trap because UNESCO is there; candidates often mix up the two. FAO, founded in 1945, is headquartered in Rome, Italy, so the answer is Rome.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

62. Which organization replaced the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT) in 1995?
(a) IMF (b) WTO (c) World Bank (d) UNCTAD
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNCTAD is a tempting distractor as it also deals with trade, but it is a UN body founded in 1964, not GATT's successor. The WTO replaced GATT on 1 January 1995, so the answer is WTO.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Economics (Indian Economic Development)

63. The headquarters of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) is in:
(a) New York (b) London (c) Geneva (d) Washington D.C.
Answer: (d)
Solution: New York is the trap because the UN headquarters is there, but the IMF, like the World Bank, is headquartered in Washington D.C., so the answer is Washington D.C.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Economics (Indian Economic Development)

64. Which of the following is the official language count of the United Nations?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 5 (d) 7
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 5, which was the count before Arabic was added in 1973. The six official languages are Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian and Spanish, so the answer is 6.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

65. The headquarters of the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC) is in:
(a) Riyadh (b) Vienna (c) Geneva (d) Doha
Answer: (b)
Solution: Riyadh is the trap because Saudi Arabia is OPEC's largest producer, but OPEC, founded in 1960 at Baghdad, has its headquarters in Vienna, Austria, so the answer is Vienna.

66. The SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation) has its secretariat in:
(a) New Delhi (b) Colombo (c) Dhaka (d) Kathmandu
Answer: (d)
Solution: New Delhi is tempting as India is the largest SAARC member, but the SAARC secretariat, established in 1987, is located in Kathmandu, Nepal, so the answer is Kathmandu.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

67. Which of the following statements about the UN General Assembly is correct?
(a) Its decisions on all matters are binding on members (b) It has 15 members (c) Each member state has one vote (d) It meets only once in five years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the binding-power statement, which confuses the General Assembly with the Security Council; General Assembly resolutions are generally recommendations. In the General Assembly every member state has one vote, so the answer is that each member state has one vote.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

68. The headquarters of the Asian Development Bank (ADB) is in:
(a) Tokyo (b) Manila (c) Singapore (d) Beijing
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tokyo is the trap because Japan is a leading ADB member and hosts the AIIB's rival institutions, but ADB, established in 1966, is headquartered in Manila, Philippines, so the answer is Manila.
Source: PYQ-style

69. The UN Charter was signed in 1945 at:
(a) New York (b) Washington D.C. (c) San Francisco (d) Geneva
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is New York, where the UN headquarters later came up. The Charter was signed on 26 June 1945 at the San Francisco Conference and came into force on 24 October 1945, so the answer is San Francisco.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

70. Which organization is known as the "World Bank's" soft-loan window for the poorest countries?
(a) IBRD (b) IFC (c) IDA (d) MIGA
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is IBRD, the original World Bank arm, which lends to middle-income countries. The International Development Association (IDA), established in 1960, gives concessional loans and grants to the poorest nations, so the answer is IDA.
Source: PYQ-style

71. The headquarters of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) is in:
(a) Vienna (b) Geneva (c) Paris (d) The Hague
Answer: (a)
Solution: Geneva is the usual trap since most UN-related agencies sit there, but the IAEA, set up in 1957, is headquartered in Vienna, Austria, so the answer is Vienna.
Source: PYQ-style

72. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) WFP – Rome (b) UNHCR – Rome (c) UNICEF – Geneva (d) IMF – Geneva
Answer: (a)
Solution: The traps are the other three pairs: UNHCR is in Geneva, UNICEF in New York and the IMF in Washington D.C. The World Food Programme, a 2020 Nobel Peace Prize laureate, is headquartered in Rome, so the answer is WFP – Rome.
Source: PYQ-style

73. The New Development Bank (NDB), set up by BRICS countries, is headquartered in:
(a) New Delhi (b) Beijing (c) Moscow (d) Shanghai
Answer: (d)
Solution: Beijing is the trap because China hosts the AIIB there; candidates confuse the two banks. The BRICS New Development Bank, established in 2015, is headquartered in Shanghai, so the answer is Shanghai.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Which UN body is responsible for maintaining international peace and security and can impose binding sanctions?
(a) General Assembly (b) Economic and Social Council (c) Security Council (d) Secretariat
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the General Assembly, whose resolutions are only recommendations. The Security Council's decisions under Chapter VII of the Charter are binding on all members, so the answer is Security Council.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

75. The headquarters of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) is in:
(a) Jakarta (b) Bangkok (c) Kuala Lumpur (d) Singapore
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bangkok is the trap because ASEAN was founded there in 1967 with the Bangkok Declaration. The ASEAN Secretariat, however, is located in Jakarta, Indonesia, so the answer is Jakarta.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)
