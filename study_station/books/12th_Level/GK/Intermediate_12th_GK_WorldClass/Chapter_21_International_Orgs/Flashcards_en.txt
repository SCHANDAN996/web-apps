Card 1
Front: Which city hosts the headquarters of the United Nations — and which European city hosts its second major office?
Back: New York (USA) is the UN headquarters; Geneva (Switzerland) hosts the second major office (UNOG). Vienna and Nairobi are the other two main UN office cities.

Card 2
Front: How many member states does the UN have, and which country joined most recently?
Back: 193 member states. South Sudan became the 193rd member in July 2011.

Card 3
Front: The UN has six principal organs — can you name all six?
Back: General Assembly, Security Council, ECOSOC (Economic and Social Council), Trusteeship Council, International Court of Justice, and the Secretariat.

Card 4
Front: Which UN organ's work has effectively ended because all trust territories attained self-government?
Back: The Trusteeship Council — it suspended operations in 1994 after Palau (the last trust territory) became independent.

Card 5
Front: How many members does the UN Security Council have, and who are the five permanent members (P5)?
Back: 15 members — 5 permanent (USA, UK, France, Russia, China) with veto power, and 10 non-permanent members elected for 2-year terms.

Card 6
Front: Where is the International Court of Justice located — and why do students get this wrong?
Back: The Hague, Netherlands. Trap: students confuse it with the International Criminal Court (also in The Hague) or assume it sits in New York with the UN HQ. The ICJ is the only principal UN organ not in New York.

Card 7
Front: What is the memory trick for the P5 countries of the Security Council?
Back: "FRUC-US" or "F-R-U-C-US" — France, Russia, UK, China, USA. Another hook: the five victors of World War II.

Card 8
Front: Which specialized agency of the UN is the oldest, founded in 1865?
Back: The International Telecommunication Union (ITU), founded in 1865 in Paris; it became a UN specialized agency in 1947.

Card 9
Front: WHO, UNESCO, UNICEF, FAO — match each to its headquarters city.
Back: WHO — Geneva; UNESCO — Paris; UNICEF — New York; FAO — Rome. Trap: examiners swap WHO and UNICEF headquarters.

Card 10
Front: Where are the headquarters of the World Bank and the IMF, and in which year were both founded?
Back: Both are in Washington, D.C., and both were founded in 1944 at the Bretton Woods Conference (they began operations in 1945/1946).

Card 11
Front: What is the trick to remember the Bretton Woods twins?
Back: "Bretton Woods = Bank + Fund" — the World Bank (IBRD) and IMF were both born at the 1944 Bretton Woods Conference in New Hampshire, USA. Both HQ: Washington, D.C.

Card 12
Front: The WTO replaced which body, and in which year? Where is its HQ?
Back: The WTO replaced GATT (General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade) on 1 January 1995. HQ: Geneva, Switzerland.

Card 13
Front: Examiner's trap: Is the WTO a specialized agency of the United Nations?
Back: No. The WTO is NOT a UN specialized agency — it is an independent organization. Similarly, the ICC (International Criminal Court) is not a UN organ. Don't be fooled by "related to trade/international" wording.

Card 14
Front: Where is NATO headquartered, and in which year was it founded?
Back: Brussels, Belgium; founded in 1949 by the Washington Treaty. Its core principle: Article 5 — collective defence (an attack on one is an attack on all).

Card 15
Front: SAARC: how many members, where is the secretariat, and is Afghanistan a member?
Back: 8 members (Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka). Secretariat: Kathmandu, Nepal. Founded: 1985 (Afghanistan joined in 2007).

Card 16
Front: ASEAN has 10 members — where is its secretariat, and which country joined most recently?
Back: Secretariat: Jakarta, Indonesia. Founded 1967 (Bangkok Declaration). Cambodia was the last country to join (1999). Timor-Leste (East Timor) has observer status and has applied for full membership, but is not yet a full member — so ASEAN still has 10 members.

Card 17
Front: Examiner's trap: The Commonwealth of Nations — is the British monarch automatically its Head, and how many members does it have?
Back: 56 member countries. Trap: the Head of the Commonwealth is NOT hereditary by rule — it was decided in 2018 that Charles (then Prince of Wales) would succeed Queen Elizabeth II. Also, most members are republics, not realms.

Card 18
Front: Where are the headquarters of these: OPEC, Amnesty International, Red Cross (ICRC), INTERPOL?
Back: OPEC — Vienna, Austria; Amnesty International — London, UK; ICRC — Geneva, Switzerland; INTERPOL — Lyon, France.

Card 19
Front: What is the trick to remember the four "Geneva cluster" organizations?
Back: "Geneva = W-W-I-R" — WHO, WTO, ILO, ICRC (Red Cross) all sit in Geneva. Hook: "World's Workers, Trade and Health meet at the Red Cross in Geneva."

Card 20
Front: The UN Charter was signed in which city and year, and when did the UN officially come into existence?
Back: Signed in San Francisco on 26 June 1945; the UN officially came into existence on 24 October 1945 (celebrated as UN Day).
