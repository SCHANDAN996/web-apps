⚠️ Trap: Charter *signed* 26 June 1945, but UN *came into force* 24 October 1945. Examiners swap these two dates!

## ⏭️ SKIP STRATEGY — When to Skip the Question

**Tip 1 — The "Exact Membership Number" Trap:**
If a question asks the *exact current member count* of a fast-changing body (e.g., WTO, NATO, Commonwealth) and you remember only an older figure, **skip it**. One new member (e.g., NATO's recent expansions) makes your "correct" answer wrong. Attempt only if the options are widely spaced (e.g., 28 / 31 / 45 / 60).

**Tip 2 — The "Founder List" Question:**
If asked "which of the following was NOT a founder of OPEC/NAM/ASEAN/SAARC" and you can confidently recall only 3 of the founders, **don't gamble** — the odd one out is usually a plausible-sounding country (e.g., Qatar in OPEC founders, China in NAM, Afghanistan in SAARC founders). Skip unless you can recite the full founder list from your mnemonic.

**Tip 3 — The "Date vs Date" Confusion:**
If the question hinges on two close dates you habitually mix up (UN Charter *signed* 26 June vs *in force* 24 October; GATT 1948 vs WTO 1995) and you haven't revised that pair in the last week, **mark it for review and move on** — a 50-50 guess on a known confusion pair costs negative marking. Come back only if time remains.
