101. The Pench, Kanhan and Wainganga are tributaries of which river system?
(a) Narmada (b) Mahanadi (c) Godavari (d) Krishna
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Mahanadi, because the Wainganga flows through the Chhattisgarh–Maharashtra region close to the Mahanadi basin. In fact the Pench and Kanhan join the Wainganga, which merges with the Wardha to form the Pranhita, the largest tributary of the Godavari, so the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

102. Which of the following is the correct sequence of Himalayan ranges from north to south?
(a) Trans Himalaya – Greater Himalaya – Lesser Himalaya – Shiwalik (b) Shiwalik – Lesser Himalaya – Greater Himalaya – Trans Himalaya (c) Greater Himalaya – Trans Himalaya – Shiwalik – Lesser Himalaya (d) Lesser Himalaya – Greater Himalaya – Trans Himalaya – Shiwalik
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is placing the Shiwaliks first because they are met first when travelling from the plains, but the question asks north to south. From north to south the order is Trans Himalaya, Greater Himalaya (Himadri), Lesser Himalaya (Himachal) and finally the Shiwaliks, so the answer is Trans Himalaya – Greater Himalaya – Lesser Himalaya – Shiwalik.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

103. The Barak river, after entering the plains of Assam, splits into which two streams?
(a) Surma and Kushiyara (b) Dhaleshwari and Singla (c) Jiri and Langai (d) Manu and Khowai
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong options are Dhaleshwari, Jiri, Manu and Khowai, which are indeed rivers of the Barak basin but are tributaries, not the two branches. In the Surma valley the Barak divides into the Surma and the Kushiyara, so the answer is Surma and Kushiyara.

(a) Chota Nagpur Plateau (b) Deccan Plateau (c) Malwa Plateau (d) Meghalaya Plateau
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Deccan Plateau is tempting because it is the largest plateau, but size is not the criterion here. The Chota Nagpur Plateau holds rich deposits of coal, iron ore, mica and bauxite and is called the mineral heartland of India, so the answer is Chota Nagpur Plateau.

107. The 'Duns' are:
(a) Deep gorges in the Eastern Ghats (b) Longitudinal valleys between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwaliks (c) Sand dunes of the Thar desert (d) Deltaic islands of the Sundarbans
Answer: (b)
Solution: The similar-sounding 'dunes' of the Thar is the classic trap based on name similarity. Duns are flat-floored longitudinal structural valleys such as Dehradun, Kotli Dun and Patli Dun lying between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwaliks, so the answer is longitudinal valleys between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwaliks.

108. Which river forms the Dhuandhar Falls near Jabalpur?

Answer: (d)
Solution: Chambal and Betwa are tempting as they are also rivers of Madhya Pradesh, but they flow northward to the Yamuna. The Narmada, flowing through a rift valley, plunges over marble rocks at Jabalpur forming the Dhuandhar Falls, so the answer is Narmada.

121. Consider the following statements about the Narmada river:
1. It flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges.
2. It forms an estuary before joining the Arabian Sea.
3. It forms a large delta at its mouth.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is statement 3, which sounds plausible because most big rivers form deltas; but west-flowing rivers like the Narmada and Tapi form estuaries, not deltas, as they descend through fault valleys into the sea. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.

122. Arrange the following peaks from west to east:
1. Nanda Devi 2. Kanchenjunga 3. Nanga Parbat 4. Namcha Barwa
(a) 3 – 1 – 2 – 4 (b) 1 – 3 – 4 – 2 (c) 3 – 2 – 1 – 4 (d) 1 – 2 – 3 – 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is placing Kanchenjunga last because it is the highest of the four, but height has nothing to do with longitude. From west to east: Nanga Parbat at the western syntaxial bend, Nanda Devi in Uttarakhand, Kanchenjunga in Sikkim, and Namcha Barwa at the eastern syntaxial bend, so the answer is 3 – 1 – 2 – 4.

123. Consider the following statements:
1. The Godavari is the longest river of peninsular India.
2. The Godavari has the largest drainage basin among peninsular rivers.
3. The Godavari is called the 'Dakshin Ganga' because of its size.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 only (d) 2 and 3 only

Solution: The trap is to reject statement 3 thinking 'Dakshin Ganga' refers to the Kaveri, a common mix-up; in NCERT geography the Godavari bears the title 'Dakshin Ganga' owing to its length and basin size. All three statements are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.

125. Consider the following statements about the peninsular plateau:
1. The Deccan plateau is higher in the west and slopes gently towards the east.
2. The Meghalaya plateau is an eastward extension of the peninsular block, separated by the Garo-Rajmahal gap.
3. The general slope of peninsular rivers is from east to west.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is statement 3, which contradicts statement 1; students who accept both fall into the inconsistency. Since the plateau tilts eastward, most peninsular rivers flow from west to east into the Bay of Bengal, making statement 3 wrong, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
