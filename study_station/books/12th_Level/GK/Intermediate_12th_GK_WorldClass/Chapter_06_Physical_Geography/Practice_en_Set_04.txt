Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Tropic of Cancer passes through how many Indian states?
(a) 8 (b) 7 (c) 6 (d) 9
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students count only the well-known states and answer 7, forgetting that the line also crosses Mizoram in the northeast. The Tropic of Cancer passes through Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram, so the answer is 8.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

77. Which is the highest peak of the Satpura range?
(a) Dhupgarh (b) Anamudi (c) Doddabetta (d) Mahendragiri
Answer: (a)
Solution: Anamudi and Doddabetta are tempting because they are famous high peaks, but both belong to the Western Ghats, not the Satpuras. Dhupgarh, near Pachmarhi in Madhya Pradesh, is the highest point of the Satpura range, so the answer is Dhupgarh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

78. The Barak river originates in which state?
(a) Assam (b) Nagaland (c) Mizoram (d) Manipur
Answer: (d)
Solution: Assam is tempting because the Barak flows through the Barak Valley of Assam, but a river's origin and its main valley are different things. The Barak rises in the hills of Manipur and then flows through Mizoram and Assam, so the answer is Manipur.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

79. Which of the following rivers does NOT form a delta?
(a) Narmada (b) Godavari (c) Mahanadi (d) Krishna
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming all major peninsular rivers form deltas. The Narmada flows westward through a rift valley and empties into the Gulf of Khambhat forming an estuary, not a delta, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

80. The 'Duns' are found in which part of the Himalayas?
(a) Between the Greater Himalayas and the Lesser Himalayas (b) Between the Shiwaliks and the Lesser Himalayas (c) Between the Shiwaliks and the Northern Plains (d) Within the Trans-Himalayas
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice places Duns between the plains and the Shiwaliks, but that belt is the Bhabar–Terai zone. Duns are longitudinal valleys lying between the Shiwalik range and the Lesser Himalayas, such as Dehradun, so the answer is between the Shiwaliks and the Lesser Himalayas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

81. Which plateau is known as the 'Mineral Heartland of India'?
(a) Chotanagpur Plateau (b) Deccan Plateau (c) Malwa Plateau (d) Meghalaya Plateau
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Deccan Plateau is tempting because it is the largest plateau, but size is not the criterion here. The Chotanagpur Plateau, rich in coal, iron ore, mica and bauxite, is called the mineral heartland of India, so the answer is Chotanagpur Plateau.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

82. The Kullu Valley of Himachal Pradesh lies between which two ranges?
(a) Zanskar and Ladakh (b) Pir Panjal and Dhauladhar (c) Dhauladhar and Shiwalik (d) Karakoram and Zanskar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dhauladhar–Shiwalik is tempting because both are Himachal ranges, but the Shiwaliks lie far to the south of Kullu. The Kullu Valley lies between the Pir Panjal and the Dhauladhar ranges, so the answer is Pir Panjal and Dhauladhar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

83. Which river is known as the 'Sorrow of Bihar'?
(a) Kosi (b) Gandak (c) Ganga (d) Son
Answer: (a)
Solution: Gandak is tempting because it also floods north Bihar, but the traditional title belongs to the river that has repeatedly shifted its course westward causing devastating floods. The Kosi is called the Sorrow of Bihar, so the answer is Kosi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

84. The Western Ghats are locally known as 'Sahyadri' in which state?
(a) Kerala (b) Tamil Nadu (c) Maharashtra (d) Karnataka
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kerala is tempting because the Ghats are prominent there, but in Kerala they are not called Sahyadri. The name Sahyadri is used for the Western Ghats in Maharashtra, so the answer is Maharashtra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

85. Which of the following is the correct north-to-south sequence of Himalayan ranges?
(a) Shiwalik – Lesser Himalaya – Greater Himalaya – Trans-Himalaya (b) Trans-Himalaya – Greater Himalaya – Lesser Himalaya – Shiwalik (c) Greater Himalaya – Trans-Himalaya – Lesser Himalaya – Shiwalik (d) Lesser Himalaya – Greater Himalaya – Shiwalik – Trans-Himalaya
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a chronological/sequence trap: option with Greater Himalaya before Trans-Himalaya looks plausible but reverses the northernmost order. From north to south the order is Trans-Himalaya (Karakoram–Ladakh–Zanskar), Greater Himalaya, Lesser Himalaya, then Shiwalik, so the answer is Trans-Himalaya – Greater Himalaya – Lesser Himalaya – Shiwalik.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

86. The Loktak Lake, famous for its floating phumdis, is located in which state?
(a) Assam (b) Tripura (c) Meghalaya (d) Manipur
Answer: (d)
Solution: Assam is tempting because it has many wetlands, but Loktak is not there. Loktak, the largest freshwater lake in Northeast India with floating phumdis and the Keibul Lamjao National Park, is in Manipur, so the answer is Manipur.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

87. Which pass connects Srinagar with Leh?
(a) Zoji La (b) Banihal (c) Shipki La (d) Nathu La
Answer: (a)
Solution: Banihal is tempting because it is on the Srinagar side, but it connects the Kashmir Valley with Jammu, not with Leh. Zoji La connects Srinagar with Leh in Ladakh, so the answer is Zoji La.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

88. The 'Bhabar' belt of the Northern Plains is characterised by:
(a) Pebble-studded porous deposits where streams disappear (b) Marshy land with dense vegetation (c) Fine alluvium of the floodplains (d) Old alluvium with calcareous concretions
Answer: (a)
Solution: The marshy description is tempting but that is the Terai belt, which lies just south of Bhabar. The Bhabar is a narrow pebble-and-boulder belt along the Shiwalik foothills where streams disappear into porous deposits, so the answer is pebble-studded porous deposits where streams disappear.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

89. Which river flows through a rift valley?
(a) Chambal (b) Betwa (c) Ken (d) Tapi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Chambal is tempting because of its ravines, but ravines are erosional badlands, not a rift valley. The Tapi, like the Narmada, flows westward through a rift valley between the Satpura and Ajanta ranges, so the answer is Tapi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

90. The highest waterfall of India, the Kunchikal Falls, is on which river?
(a) Sharavati (b) Kaveri (c) Varahi (d) Mandovi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sharavati is the classic trap because the famous Jog Falls are on it, and many assume Jog is India's highest. The Kunchikal Falls, the highest in India, are on the Varahi river in Karnataka, so the answer is Varahi.
Source: Survey of India / NCERT-based reference

91. Which of the following statements about the Deccan Plateau is correct?
(a) It slopes from east to west (b) It is younger than the Himalayas in geological age (c) It is bounded by the Satpura range in the north (d) It is drained only by west-flowing rivers
Answer: (c)
Solution: The east-to-west slope statement is the tempting trap — the plateau actually slopes from west to east, which is why most of its rivers flow east into the Bay of Bengal. The Deccan Plateau is bounded in the north by the Satpura and Vindhya ranges, so the answer is that it is bounded by the Satpura range in the north.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

92. The 'Karewas' of Kashmir are famous for the cultivation of:
(a) Apricot (b) Apple (c) Walnut (d) Saffron
Answer: (d)
Solution: Apple is tempting because Kashmir is famous for apples, but apples grow on slopes and orchards, not specifically on karewa deposits. Karewas are lacustrine deposits of the Kashmir Valley renowned for saffron (kesar) cultivation, so the answer is saffron.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

93. Which river is a tributary of the Indus and flows through the Kashmir Valley?
(a) Chenab (b) Beas (c) Ravi (d) Jhelum
Answer: (d)
Solution: Chenab is tempting because it is also an Indus tributary near Kashmir, but it does not flow through the Kashmir Valley itself. The Jhelum flows through the Kashmir Valley, passing Srinagar, before joining the Chenab, so the answer is Jhelum.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

94. The 'Malwa Plateau' is mainly located in which state?
(a) Rajasthan (b) Gujarat (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Maharashtra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rajasthan is tempting because the plateau extends into its southeastern part, but the bulk of it lies elsewhere. The Malwa Plateau is mainly in Madhya Pradesh, with Indore and Ujjain situated on it, so the answer is Madhya Pradesh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

95. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Anamudi – Eastern Ghats (b) Gurushikhar – Aravalli (c) Dhupgarh – Vindhya (d) Doddabetta – Satpura
Answer: (b)
Solution: Anamudi–Eastern Ghats is the tempting trap; Anamudi is the highest South Indian peak but it lies in the Western Ghats, and similarly Dhupgarh belongs to the Satpuras and Doddabetta to the Nilgiris. Gurushikhar, the highest peak of the Aravallis in Mount Abu, is correctly matched, so the answer is Gurushikhar – Aravalli.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

96. The 'Terai' belt lies to the:
(a) North of Bhabar (b) North of Shiwaliks (c) South of Bhabar (d) South of Bhangar
Answer: (c)
Solution: North of Bhabar is tempting if one reverses the sequence, but north of Bhabar lie the Shiwalik hills themselves. The Terai is a wet, marshy belt lying south of the Bhabar where the disappeared streams re-emerge, so the answer is south of Bhabar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

97. Which river forms the boundary between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges?
(a) Tapi (b) Narmada (c) Mahi (d) Chambal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tapi is tempting because it also flows between hill ranges, but it flows south of the Satpuras. The Narmada flows in the rift valley between the Vindhyas to the north and the Satpuras to the south, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

98. The 'Palk Strait' separates India from:
(a) Maldives (b) Myanmar (c) Indonesia (d) Sri Lanka
Answer: (d)
Solution: Maldives is tempting because it is also an island neighbour of India, but India and Maldives are separated by the Eight Degree Channel. The Palk Strait lies between Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka, so the answer is Sri Lanka.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India)

99. Which of the following is the largest brackish water lagoon of India?
(a) Pulicat Lake (b) Vembanad Lake (c) Chilika Lake (d) Kolleru Lake
Answer: (c)
Solution: Pulicat is tempting because it is also a large coastal lagoon, but it is smaller and partly freshwater-fed. Chilika Lake in Odisha, at the mouth of the Daya river, is India's largest brackish water lagoon, so the answer is Chilika Lake.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

100. The 'Dooars' region of West Bengal is known for:
(a) Coal mining (b) Tea gardens and forests (c) Cotton cultivation (d) Salt production
Answer: (b)
Solution: Coal mining is tempting because West Bengal has the Raniganj coalfield, but that lies in the Damodar valley, not the Dooars. The Dooars are the floodplains and foothills of the eastern Himalayas in West Bengal, famous for tea gardens and dense forests, so the answer is tea gardens and forests.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)
