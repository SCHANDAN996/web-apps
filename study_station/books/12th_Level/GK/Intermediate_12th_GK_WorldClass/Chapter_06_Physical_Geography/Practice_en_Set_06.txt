126. Which of the following statements about the Deccan Trap is correct?
(a) It is composed mainly of granite and gneiss (b) It was formed by folding of sedimentary rocks during the Tertiary period (c) It covers only the state of Maharashtra (d) It was formed by fissure-type volcanic eruptions during the Cretaceous–Eocene period
Answer: (d)
Solution: Option (b) is the tempting trap — folding describes the Himalayas, not the Deccan Trap. The Deccan Trap is a vast basaltic lava plateau formed by fissure eruptions around the Cretaceous–Eocene transition, covering parts of Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh and beyond, so the answer is fissure-type volcanic eruptions during the Cretaceous–Eocene period.
