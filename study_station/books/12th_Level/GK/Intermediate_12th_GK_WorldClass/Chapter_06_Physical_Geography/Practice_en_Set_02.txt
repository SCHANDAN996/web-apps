Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The Tropic of Cancer passes through how many Indian states?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 9 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students count only the well-known states and answer 7, forgetting that the line also crosses Mizoram. The Tropic of Cancer passes through Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram, so the answer is 8.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

27. Which is the highest peak of the Karakoram range located in India?
(a) K2 (Godwin Austen) (b) Nanda Devi (c) Kanchenjunga (d) Kamet
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kanchenjunga is a tempting answer because it is the highest peak of India in the Himalayas, but it lies in Sikkim, not the Karakoram. K2 (8611 m) lies in the Karakoram range in the Ladakh region, so the answer is K2 (Godwin Austen).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

28. The Zoji La pass connects which two regions?
(a) Dehradun and Tibet (b) Manali and Leh (c) Shimla and Kinnaur (d) Srinagar and Leh
Answer: (d)
Solution: Baralacha La on the Manali–Leh route is a common confusion, but that pass lies in Himachal Pradesh. Zoji La lies on the Srinagar–Leh National Highway connecting the Kashmir Valley with Ladakh, so the answer is Srinagar and Leh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

29. Which river is known as the 'Sorrow of Bengal'?
(a) Hooghly (b) Teesta (c) Kosi (d) Damodar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kosi is a trap because it is called the 'Sorrow of Bihar', not Bengal. The Damodar, notorious for devastating floods in West Bengal before the Damodar Valley Corporation dams, is called the 'Sorrow of Bengal', so the answer is Damodar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

30. The Narmada river flows through which type of valley?
(a) V-shaped valley (b) Rift valley (c) Glacial valley (d) Deltaic valley
Answer: (b)
Solution: A V-shaped valley is tempting since most rivers carve such valleys, but the Narmada is special. It flows westward through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges, formed by faulting, so the answer is rift valley.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

31. Which of the following is the largest brackish water lake in India?
(a) Wular Lake (b) Pulicat Lake (c) Chilika Lake (d) Sambhar Lake
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sambhar is a trap because it is India's largest inland saltwater lake, but it is saline, not brackish, and smaller in area. Chilika Lake in Odisha, at the mouth of the Daya river, is India's largest brackish water lagoon, so the answer is Chilika Lake.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

32. The 'Duns' are found in which part of the Himalayas?
(a) Between Great Himalaya and Lesser Himalaya (b) Within the Trans-Himalaya (c) Between Lesser Himalaya and Shiwalik (d) Within the Shiwalik range
Answer: (c)
Solution: Students often place Duns inside the Shiwaliks, but they are longitudinal valleys lying between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwalik range, such as Dehradun and Kotli Dun, so the answer is between Lesser Himalaya and Shiwalik.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

33. Which plateau is known as the 'storehouse of minerals' in India?
(a) Deccan Plateau (b) Malwa Plateau (c) Chota Nagpur Plateau (d) Meghalaya Plateau
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Deccan Plateau is a tempting answer because of its vast size, but the richest concentration of coal, iron ore, mica and bauxite lies in the Chota Nagpur Plateau (Jharkhand region), so the answer is Chota Nagpur Plateau.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

34. The Palk Strait separates India from which country?
(a) Maldives (b) Bangladesh (c) Sri Lanka (d) Myanmar
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Gulf of Mannar confuses many, but it lies south of the Palk Strait between India and Sri Lanka as well. The Palk Strait, between Tamil Nadu and the Jaffna region, separates India from Sri Lanka, so the answer is Sri Lanka.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

35. Which river forms the famous Dhuandhar Falls?
(a) Tapi (b) Betwa (c) Chambal (d) Narmada
Answer: (d)
Solution: Chambal is a trap because it is famous for ravines, not waterfalls. The Narmada falls over marble rocks at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur forming the Dhuandhar (smoke-like) Falls, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

36. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are separated by which channel?
(a) Eight Degree Channel (b) Nine Degree Channel (c) Ten Degree Channel (d) Duncan Passage
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Eight Degree Channel is a trap, but it separates Minicoy (Lakshadweep) from the Maldives, not the two island groups. The Ten Degree Channel separates the Andaman group from the Nicobar group, so the answer is Ten Degree Channel.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

37. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Beas (b) Brahmaputra (c) Chenab (d) Ravi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chenab is a trap since it forms in India by the union of Chandra and Bhaga, but the Brahmaputra rises from the Chemayungdung glacier near Lake Mansarovar in Tibet, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

38. The 'Karewa' deposits of Kashmir are famous for the cultivation of which crop?
(a) Apple (b) Saffron (c) Walnut (d) Barley
Answer: (b)
Solution: Apple is tempting because Kashmir is famous for apples, but Karewas are lacustrine (lake) deposits of the Kashmir Valley specifically renowned for saffron (kesar) cultivation, so the answer is saffron.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

39. Which is the southernmost point of the Indian mainland?
(a) Kanyakumari (b) Indira Point (c) Rameswaram (d) Point Calimere
Answer: (a)
Solution: Indira Point is the classic trap — it is the southernmost point of Indian territory (Great Nicobar), not of the mainland. Kanyakumari (Cape Comorin) in Tamil Nadu is the southernmost tip of the mainland, so the answer is Kanyakumari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

40. The Chambal, Betwa and Ken are tributaries of which river?
(a) Yamuna (b) Ganga (c) Son (d) Gandak
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ganga is a trap because these rivers ultimately join the Ganga system, but they first merge into the Yamuna — Chambal, Betwa and Ken are all right-bank tributaries of the Yamuna, so the answer is Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

41. Which type of soil is best suited for cotton cultivation?
(a) Alluvial soil (b) Laterite soil (c) Black soil (d) Red soil
Answer: (c)
Solution: Alluvial soil is tempting as the most fertile soil, but it suits wheat and rice. Black (regur) soil, formed from Deccan trap lava, retains moisture and is ideal for cotton, so the answer is black soil.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography

42. The 'Bhabar' belt lies along which region?
(a) Foot of the Shiwaliks (b) Middle Ganga plain (c) Delta region of Bengal (d) Malwa plateau edge
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Terai is a trap because it lies just south of Bhabar and is often confused with it. Bhabar is the narrow belt of pebbles and coarse deposits along the foothills of the Shiwaliks where streams disappear, so the answer is foot of the Shiwaliks.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

43. Which pass connects the Kashmir Valley with the Kargil region and is on the Srinagar–Leh highway?
(a) Zoji La (b) Banihal (c) Shipki La (d) Nathu La
Answer: (a)
Solution: Banihal is a trap because it also relates to Kashmir, but it connects Jammu with the Kashmir Valley. Zoji La lies beyond the valley on the Srinagar–Leh highway towards Kargil, so the answer is Zoji La.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

44. The Loktak Lake, famous for phumdis (floating islands), is located in which state?
(a) Manipur (b) Assam (c) Mizoram (d) Nagaland
Answer: (a)
Solution: Assam is a tempting answer due to its many wetlands, but Loktak Lake, the largest freshwater lake of Northeast India with floating phumdis and Keibul Lamjao National Park, is in Manipur, so the answer is Manipur.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

45. Which river is also known as the 'Dakshin Ganga' (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Krishna (b) Mahanadi (c) Kaveri (d) Godavari
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kaveri is a trap because it is sacred like the Ganga in the South, but the Godavari, being the longest peninsular river with the largest basin among peninsular rivers, is called Dakshin Ganga, so the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

46. The 'Meghalaya Plateau' is geologically a part of which landmass?
(a) Deccan Plateau (b) Indo-Gangetic alluvium (c) Himalayan fold mountains (d) Peninsular block separated by the Malda gap
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Deccan Plateau is tempting, but the Meghalaya Plateau is an eastward extension of the peninsular block (Chota Nagpur Plateau), separated from it by the Malda gap (Rajmahal–Garo gap), so the answer is peninsular block separated by the Malda gap.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

47. Which of the following rivers forms an estuary instead of a delta?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Narmada (c) Krishna (d) Godavari
Answer: (b)
Solution: Godavari is a trap as a major east-flowing river, but all east-flowing rivers listed form deltas. The west-flowing Narmada, flowing swiftly through a rift valley with little silt, forms an estuary at the Gulf of Khambhat, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

48. The 'Pir Panjal' range is a part of which Himalayan division?
(a) Great Himalaya (b) Lesser (Middle) Himalaya (c) Shiwalik (d) Trans-Himalaya
Answer: (b)
Solution: Great Himalaya is a trap because Pir Panjal has high snowy peaks, but structurally Pir Panjal, along with Dhauladhar and Mahabharat ranges, belongs to the Lesser (Middle) Himalaya, so the answer is Lesser (Middle) Himalaya.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

49. Which island of India is of coral origin?
(a) Lakshadweep Islands (b) Nicobar Islands (c) Andaman Islands (d) New Moore Island
Answer: (a)
Solution: Andaman is a trap, but the Andaman–Nicobar islands are elevated portions of submerged mountains. The Lakshadweep Islands in the Arabian Sea are built of coral deposits (atolls), so the answer is Lakshadweep Islands.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

50. The 'Sundarbans' delta is formed by the distributaries of which river system?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Ganga–Brahmaputra (c) Godavari (d) Hooghly alone
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hooghly alone is a trap because it flows through the region, but it is only one distributary. The Sundarbans, the world's largest delta, is built jointly by the Ganga and Brahmaputra river systems, so the answer is Ganga–Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)
