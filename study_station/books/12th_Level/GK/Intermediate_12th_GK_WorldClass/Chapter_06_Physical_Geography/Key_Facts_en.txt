**Quick Formula / Rule of Thumb:**
- East-flowing river → Delta | West-flowing river (Narmada, Tapi) → Estuary
- Higher the degree channel number → farther north (8° Channel near Maldives, 9° Channel near Minicoy, 10° Channel between Andaman & Nicobar)
- Himalaya ranges N→S: Himadri → Himachal → Shiwalik (remember: "Great to Small to Outer")
- Plains N→S sequence: Bhabar → Terai → Bhangar/Khadar (floodplain)
- Latitude check: mainland starts at 8°4′ N (Kanyakumari); the whole country starts at 6°45′ N (Indira Point) — read the question wording carefully.
