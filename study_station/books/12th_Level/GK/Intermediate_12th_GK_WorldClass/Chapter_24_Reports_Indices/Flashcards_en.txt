Card 1
Front: Which organization publishes the Human Development Index (HDI)?
Back: The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), through its annual Human Development Report. HDI combines life expectancy, education, and per-capita income (GNI).

Card 2
Front: Which index ranks countries on press freedom, and who publishes it?
Back: The World Press Freedom Index, published by Reporters Without Borders (RSF), headquartered in Paris.

Card 3
Front: The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which two organizations?
Back: Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany). It uses undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting, and child mortality as indicators.

Card 4
Front: Which index measures corruption perception, and which body releases it?
Back: The Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI), released by Transparency International, based in Berlin. Denmark has consistently ranked at the top in recent years.

Card 5
Front: Who publishes the Global Innovation Index (GII)?
Back: The World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO), a UN agency, in partnership with other institutions. Switzerland has topped it repeatedly.

Card 6
Front: Which report by the World Bank was discontinued in 2021 after data irregularities?
Back: The Ease of Doing Business report. It was discontinued in September 2021 after an investigation found data manipulation. This is a favourite examiner trap — the report no longer exists.

Card 7
Front: Which Indian think tank releases indices like the SDG India Index?
Back: NITI Aayog, in collaboration with the UN and other partners. It also publishes the Export Preparedness Index and the School Education Quality Index.

Card 8
Front: What mnemonic helps remember the three dimensions of HDI?
Back: "LEI" — Life expectancy, Education, Income. Remember: "A long, educated, earning life = high HDI."

Card 9
Front: What trick helps recall the four indicators of the Global Hunger Index?
Back: "USWM" — Undernourishment, Stunting, Wasting, Mortality (child). Think: "Hunger wastes the young."

Card 10
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Global Gender Gap Report is published by UNDP." True or false?
Back: False. It is published by the World Economic Forum (WEF). Examiners swap publishing bodies — always match the index to its exact organization.

Card 11
Front: Examiner's trap: "The World Happiness Report is a UN publication." Is this fully correct?
Back: It is published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN), using Gallup data — not by UNDP or the UN Secretariat directly. Finland has topped it for several consecutive years.

Card 12
Front: Which index ranks countries on peace, and who publishes it?
Back: The Global Peace Index, published by the Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP), Sydney. Iceland has ranked first since 2008.

Card 13
Front: Who publishes the Global Competitiveness Report?
Back: The World Economic Forum (WEF), Geneva — the same body behind the Global Gender Gap Report and the Global Risks Report.

Card 14
Front: Which report tracks modern slavery and is published by the Walk Free Foundation?
Back: The Global Slavery Index. The Walk Free Foundation is based in Australia.

Card 15
Front: Which UN agency publishes the State of the World's Children report?
Back: UNICEF. It focuses on children's health, education, and welfare worldwide.

Card 16
Front: The Henley Passport Index ranks what, and on what basis?
Back: It ranks passports by the number of destinations their holders can access without a prior visa, using IATA data.

Card 17
Front: Which index by the Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU) ranks cities on living conditions?
Back: The Global Liveability Index. Vienna has frequently topped it. EIU also publishes the Democracy Index.

Card 18
Front: Which Indian ministry releases the National Family Health Survey (NFHS)?
Back: The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. NFHS-5 was conducted in 2019–21 and provides data on fertility, nutrition, and health indicators.

Card 19
Front: What is the quick trick to remember WEF's headquarters city for its reports?
Back: "WEF = Geneva, where the world meets." WEF (Davos meetings) is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland — unlike the World Bank and IMF, which are in Washington, D.C.

Card 20
Front: Which two Washington, D.C.-based institutions publish the World Development Report and the Global Economic Prospects respectively?
Back: The World Development Report is by the World Bank; Global Economic Prospects is also a World Bank publication. The IMF publishes the World Economic Outlook — a common mix-up in exams.
