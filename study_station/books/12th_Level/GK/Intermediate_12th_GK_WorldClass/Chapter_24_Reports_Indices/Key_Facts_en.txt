# Key Facts & Figures — Reports & Indices

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human Development Index (HDI) | Released by UNDP; India ranked 134 out of 193 in the 2023–24 report (published 2024) | UNDP confused with World Bank; rank confused with older 2021–22 report (132) | Wrong organisation / outdated rank |
| HDI dimensions | Health (life expectancy), Education (mean & expected years of schooling), Standard of living (GNI per capita) | GDP per capita written instead of GNI per capita | Near-correct statement |
| Global Hunger Index (GHI) | Published jointly by Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe; India ranked 105 out of 127 in 2024 | Attributed to FAO or WFP | Wrong organisation |
| Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI) | Released by Transparency International; India ranked 93 out of 180 in 2024 (score 38) | Confused with "ease of doing business" or attributed to World Bank | Wrong organisation |
| World Press Freedom Index | Released by Reporters Without Borders (RSF); India ranked 159 out of 180 in 2024 | Attributed to UNESCO or Amnesty International | Wrong organisation |
| Global Peace Index | Released by Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP), Australia; India ranked 116 in 2024; Iceland most peaceful | Attributed to UN; "most peaceful" country confused | Wrong organisation / rank swap |
| Global Gender Gap Report | Released by World Economic Forum (WEF); India ranked 129 out of 146 in 2024 | Attributed to UN Women | Wrong organisation |
| World Happiness Report | Released by UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN); Finland happiest (2024); India ranked 126 | Attributed to UNDP; happiest country confused with Denmark | Wrong organisation / rank swap |
| Henley Passport Index | Released by Henley & Partners; based on visa-free access; India ranked 80 in 2024 | Confused with "passport power rank" by Arton Capital | Similar-name confusion |
| Global Innovation Index (GII) | Released by WIPO; India ranked 39 out of 133 in 2024; Switzerland top | Attributed to WEF | Wrong organisation |
| Ease of Doing Business | Was released by World Bank; **discontinued in 2021** (last report 2020, India ranked 63) | Questions still treat it as current — trap: "latest EoDB rank" | Outdated/discontinued index |
| Global Competitiveness Report | Released by World Economic Forum (WEF) | Confused with IMD World Competitiveness Ranking | Similar-name confusion |
| Human Capital Index | Released by World Bank | Confused with HDI (UNDP) | Similar-name confusion |
| Global Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) | Released by UNDP + Oxford Poverty & Human Development Initiative (OPHI) | Attributed to World Bank or NITI Aayog (NITI releases the *national* MPI) | Wrong organisation |
| National MPI (India) | Released by NITI Aayog; based on NFHS data | Confused with global MPI (UNDP-OPHI) | National vs global confusion |
| Climate Change Performance Index (CCPI) | Released by Germanwatch; India ranked 7th in 2024 report | Attributed to UNEP | Wrong organisation |
| Environmental Performance Index (EPI) | Released by Yale & Columbia Universities with WEF; biennial | Attributed to UNEP or IPCC | Wrong organisation |
| Global Food Security Index | Released by Economist Impact (Economist Intelligence Unit) | Attributed to FAO | Wrong organisation |
| Logistics Performance Index (LPI) | Released by World Bank; India ranked 38 out of 139 in 2023 | Attributed to WTO | Wrong organisation |
| Global Terrorism Index (GTI) | Released by Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP) | Attributed to UN or Interpol | Wrong organisation |
| World Inequality Report | Released by World Inequality Lab (Paris) | Attributed to Oxfam (Oxfam releases a separate inequality report at Davos) | Similar-name confusion |
| Global Liveability Index | Released by Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU); Vienna top | Attributed to Mercer (Mercer has its own Quality of Living survey) | Similar-name confusion |
| SDG India Index | Released by NITI Aayog; measures states' progress on Sustainable Development Goals | Confused with global SDG Index (SDSN) | National vs global confusion |
| India Innovation Index | Released by NITI Aayog | Confused with Global Innovation Index (WIPO) | National vs global confusion |
| Export Preparedness Index | Released by NITI Aayog | Attributed to Ministry of Commerce | Wrong organisation |
| State Energy & Climate Index | Released by NITI Aayog | Attributed to Ministry of Power | Wrong organisation |
| Fiscal Health Index | Released by NITI Aayog (first edition 2025) | Attributed to RBI or Finance Ministry | Wrong organisation |
| World Economic Outlook | Released by IMF; published twice a year (April & October) | Attributed to World Bank | Wrong organisation |
| Global Financial Stability Report | Released by IMF | Confused with RBI's Financial Stability Report (India) | National vs global confusion |
| Global Economic Prospects | Released by World Bank | Confused with IMF's World Economic Outlook | Similar-name confusion |
| World Development Report | Released by World Bank; annual, theme-based | Attributed to UNDP | Wrong organisation |
| Human Development Report | Released by UNDP; first published 1990; Mahbub ul Haq & Amartya Sen associated | Attributed to World Bank | Wrong organisation |
| World Happiness Report — basis | Based on Gallup World Poll data | "Survey by UN directly" — near-correct trap | Near-correct statement |
| Global Gender Gap — 4 dimensions | Economic participation, Educational attainment, Health & survival, Political empowerment | "Political empowerment" replaced with "social empowerment" | Near-correct statement |
| GHI — 4 indicators | Undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting, child mortality | "Child literacy" inserted as an indicator | Near-correct statement |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Fact/Question Type | Category | Time | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Which organisation releases Index X?" | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes — pure recall, highest ROI in this chapter |
| "India's rank in Index X (year given)" | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes — if memorised with year |
| "Which country topped Index X?" | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| "Which of these is NOT an indicator of GHI/HDI?" | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Match-the-following: Index ↔ Organisation | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Statement-based: "HDI uses GDP per capita" (true/false) | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Trap-heavy — read every word |
| Chronological ordering of reports/first editions | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ Low frequency — skip if unsure |
| National vs global index discrimination (NITI Aayog vs UNDP) | Analytical | 45 sec | ✅ Yes — frequent trap, worth mastering |
| Discontinued/changed indices (EoDB) | Analytical | 45 sec | ✅ Yes — easy marks if known |
| Multi-statement questions (2–3 statements on different indices) | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt only if 2 statements are certain |
