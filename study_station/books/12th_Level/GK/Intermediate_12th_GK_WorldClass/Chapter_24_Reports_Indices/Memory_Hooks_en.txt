# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Reports & Indices

---

**Box 1 — HDI's Three Pillars**
**"Long Life, Long Books, Long Wallet"**
HDI measures: **L**ife expectancy (health), **E**ducation (mean & expected years of schooling), **I**ncome (GNI per capita).
Trick: **L-E-I** → "LEI" — a garland of three flowers. HDI = garland of Life, Education, Income.

---

**Box 2 — Who Publishes HDI?**
**"UNDP Delivers People"**
**U**nited **N**ations **D**evelopment **P**rogramme → **H**uman **D**evelopment **R**eport.
Hook: "The UN's Development Programme develops People — so it measures Human Development."
⚠️ Trap alert: NOT World Bank, NOT IMF. Development in the name = Development in the report.

---

**Box 3 — Corruption Perceptions Index**
**"Transparency sees through Corruption"**
CPI is published by **Transparency International** (Berlin-based NGO).
Hook: To *see* corruption clearly, you need *transparency*. Cleanest countries score highest (100 = very clean, 0 = highly corrupt).
⚠️ Trap: Higher score = LESS corrupt. Examiners flip this!

---

**Box 4 — Global Hunger Index**
**"Hungry? Call the Irish-German duo!"**
GHI is published jointly by **Concern Worldwide (Ireland)** and **Welthungerhilfe (Germany)**.
Hook: "When hunger strikes, the **Concern**ed **Welt** (world) helps."
⚠️ Trap: NOT FAO, NOT WFP. FAO publishes the *State of Food Security and Nutrition* report — different!

---

**Box 5 — Global Gender Gap Report**
**"WEF closes the GAP"**
Published by the **World Economic Forum** (Davos).
Four pillars: **"HEEP"** — **H**ealth, **E**ducation, **E**conomy, **P**olitics.
Hook: "There's a HEEP of gaps to close — Health, Education, Economy, Politics."

---

**Box 6 — World Happiness Report**
**"Happy Finns under the UN Umbrella"**
Published by the **UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN)**; Finland has topped it repeatedly in recent years.
Hook: "Finland = FIN-ish your worries." Gallup data + UN network = happiness ranking.

---

**Box 7 — Ease of Doing Business — DISCONTINUED!**
**"The Bank closed its Business"**
The World Bank's *Doing Business* report was **discontinued in 2021** after data irregularities. If an option treats it as a current annual report, be suspicious.
Hook: "The shop shut down in 2021 — don't list it as open!"

---

**Box 8 — Global Innovation Index**
**"WIPO Invents the Innovation Index"**
Published by **WIPO** (World Intellectual Property Organization) — Geneva.
Hook: "Patents and inventions live at WIPO — so does the Innovation Index." Switzerland has topped it for many consecutive years.

---

**Box 9 — Henley Passport Index**
**"Henley = Hand-luggage ranking"**
Ranks passports by visa-free destinations, using **IATA** data.
Hook: "Henley sounds like 'handily' — how *handily* can you travel?"

---

**Box 10 — Press Freedom Index**
**"Reporters Without Borders rank Reporters"**
Published by **Reporters Without Borders (RSF)**, Paris.
Hook: "Who judges press freedom? The reporters themselves — without borders!"
⚠️ Trap: NOT UNESCO, NOT Amnesty International.

---

**Box 11 — India's NITI Aayog Indices**
**"NITI = National Index Team of India"**
NITI Aayog publishes: **SDG India Index, Export Preparedness Index, School Education Quality Index, Health Index, Composite Water Management Index**.
Hook: "NITI is India's own index factory — SDG, Exports, School, Health, Water."
⚠️ Trap: These are domestic (state-wise) rankings, NOT global reports.

---

**Box 12 — Environmental Performance Index**
**"Yale & Columbia climb the green EPI-ladder"**
EPI is by **Yale & Columbia Universities** with WEF.
Hook: "Two Ivy League professors grade the planet's homework."

---

**Box 13 — Global Peace Index**
**"IEP: Institute for Economics & Peace — Peace has a Price tag"**
Published by the **Institute for Economics and Peace** (Sydney). Iceland tops it consistently.
Hook: "ICE-land = coolest, calmest, most peaceful."

---

**Box 14 — Human Capital Index**
**"World Bank invests in Humans"**
HCI is a **World Bank** product (part of the Human Capital Project).
Hook: "Banks invest capital — the World Bank invests *human* capital."
⚠️ Trap: HDI = UNDP, but Human *Capital* = World Bank. Similar names, different publishers!

---

**Box 15 — The "Publisher Swap" Master Trick**
Examiners' favourite trap is swapping publishers. Lock these pairs:
- **UNDP** → Human Development Report
- **WEF** → Gender Gap, Global Competitiveness, Travel & Tourism
- **World Bank** → Human Capital, (formerly) Doing Business
- **IMF** → World Economic Outlook
- **WIPO** → Global Innovation Index
- **Transparency International** → Corruption Perceptions Index
Hook: **"U-H, W-G, B-C, I-O, W-I, T-C"** — chant it like a train rhythm before the exam.

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

1. **The "Rank-Number" trap:** If a question asks India's *exact rank* (e.g., "India's rank in XYZ Index 2023") and you can't recall it confidently, **skip immediately**. Ranks change every year and near-identical numbers (126 vs 127 vs 132) are designed to punish guessing. Attempt only if you can eliminate two options.

2. **The "Publisher Swap" with one unknown:** If three of four publisher–report pairs look right and one is unfamiliar, don't gamble on the unfamiliar one — but if you know *none* of the pairs, skip. A blind guess here has negative-marking written all over it.

3. **The "Latest Edition" question:** Questions like "Which edition of the report was released in 2024?" or theme-of-the-year questions are pure memory with no logic to fall back on. If you haven't revised that specific fact, **skip in under 15 seconds** and invest the saved time in solvable questions.
