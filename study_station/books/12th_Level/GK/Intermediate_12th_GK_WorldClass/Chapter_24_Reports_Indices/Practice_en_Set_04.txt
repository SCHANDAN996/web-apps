76. Which organisation publishes the Human Development Report (HDR)?
(a) World Bank (b) World Economic Forum (c) United Nations Development Programme (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, not the HDR, which is the tempting confusion here. The Human Development Report, with the Human Development Index (HDI), has been published annually by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) since 1990, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: UNDP official website (hdr.undp.org)

77. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which organisations?
(a) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (b) FAO and WFP (c) UNICEF and WHO (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many candidates pick FAO and WFP because they are UN food agencies, but that is the trap — FAO publishes the State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World report. The Global Hunger Index is published jointly by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

78. The World Happiness Report is published by which body?
(a) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (b) UNDP (c) OECD (d) World Health Organization
Answer: (a)
Solution: UNDP is a common wrong guess because it publishes many UN reports, but it handles the Human Development Report. The World Happiness Report is published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, and Finland has repeatedly topped it, so the answer is UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network.
Source: World Happiness Report official website

79. The Global Gender Gap Report is released by which organisation?
(a) UN Women (b) World Bank (c) International Labour Organization (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (d)
Solution: UN Women sounds plausible because of the word "Gender", which is exactly the examiner's trap. The Global Gender Gap Report is an annual publication of the World Economic Forum (WEF), which also publishes the Global Competitiveness Report, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

80. Which index is released by the Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU)?
(a) Democracy Index (b) Global Peace Index (c) Corruption Perceptions Index (d) Human Freedom Index
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Global Peace Index is a tempting option but it comes from the Institute for Economics and Peace, not the EIU. The Democracy Index is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit, the research arm of The Economist Group, so the answer is Democracy Index.
Source: Economist Intelligence Unit official website

81. The Corruption Perceptions Index is published by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) Transparency International (c) Amnesty International (d) United Nations
Answer: (b)
Solution: Amnesty International is the trap here — it works on human rights, not corruption rankings. The Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI) has been published annually since 1995 by Transparency International, headquartered in Berlin, so the answer is Transparency International.
Source: Transparency International official website

82. The Global Innovation Index is co-published by WIPO along with which partners?
(a) World Bank and IMF (b) Cornell University and INSEAD (c) Oxford University and MIT (d) UNESCO and UNICEF
Answer: (b)
Solution: Oxford and MIT are famous universities, making them a tempting guess, but they are not partners in this index. The Global Innovation Index is published by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) together with Cornell University and INSEAD, so the answer is Cornell University and INSEAD.
Source: WIPO official website

83. The Human Development Index is based on how many dimensions?
(a) Two (b) Four (c) Three (d) Five
Answer: (c)
Solution: Candidates often add "political freedom" or "environment" and pick four, which is the trap. The HDI uses exactly three dimensions — a long and healthy life, access to knowledge, and a decent standard of living (GNI per capita), so the answer is Three.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

84. The 'State of the World's Children' report is published by which UN agency?
(a) UNICEF (b) UNESCO (c) WHO (d) FAO
Answer: (a)
Solution: UNESCO is a common wrong choice because it deals with education of children, but its flagship report is the Global Education Monitoring Report. The State of the World's Children is the flagship publication of UNICEF, so the answer is UNICEF.
Source: UNICEF official website

85. The World Development Report is an annual publication of which institution?
(a) IMF (b) ADB (c) WTO (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The IMF is the tempting distractor because it publishes the World Economic Outlook, which sounds similar. The World Development Report has been published annually by the World Bank since 1978, so the answer is World Bank.

86. The Global Peace Index is released by which institute?
(a) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (b) Institute for Economics and Peace (c) Carnegie Endowment (d) Brookings Institution
Answer: (b)
Solution: SIPRI is the classic trap because it works on peace and arms data, but it publishes military expenditure databases, not this index. The Global Peace Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, headquartered in Sydney, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace official website

87. Which organisation publishes the 'Global Financial Stability Report'?
(a) World Bank (b) Bank for International Settlements (c) International Monetary Fund (d) Financial Stability Board
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Financial Stability Board is the trap — its name matches the report title almost word for word, but it does not publish this report. The Global Financial Stability Report is a flagship publication of the International Monetary Fund, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.
Source: IMF official website

88. The 'Ease of Doing Business' report, discontinued in 2021, was published by which body?
(a) World Economic Forum (b) IMF (c) World Bank (d) UNCTAD
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Economic Forum is a tempting answer because it publishes competitiveness rankings, but the Doing Business report was a World Bank flagship. It was discontinued in 2021 after data irregularities, and the World Bank replaced it with the Business Ready (B-READY) project, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

89. The Global Terrorism Index is published by which organisation?
(a) United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (b) Institute for Economics and Peace (c) Interpol (d) RAND Corporation
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNODC is the trap because it deals with crime, but it publishes the World Drug Report and Global Report on Trafficking in Persons. The Global Terrorism Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, the same body behind the Global Peace Index, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace official website

90. The 'State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World' (SOFI) report is led by which agency?
(a) World Food Programme (b) International Fund for Agricultural Development (c) Food and Agriculture Organization (d) World Health Organization
Answer: (c)
Solution: WFP is tempting because it fights hunger and won the 2020 Nobel Peace Prize, but the SOFI report is led by the FAO along with IFAD, UNICEF, WFP and WHO as partners. The lead publishing agency is the Food and Agriculture Organization, so the answer is Food and Agriculture Organization.
Source: FAO official website

91. Which of the following correctly matches the report with its publisher?
(a) World Economic Outlook — World Bank (b) Global Competitiveness Report — World Economic Forum (c) Human Development Report — World Bank (d) World Happiness Report — UNICEF
Answer: (b)
Solution: Each wrong option swaps the publisher with a similar-sounding institution, which is the standard matching trap. The World Economic Outlook is by the IMF, the Human Development Report is by UNDP, and the World Happiness Report is by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network; only the Global Competitiveness Report is correctly matched with the World Economic Forum, so the answer is Global Competitiveness Report — World Economic Forum.
Source: PYQ-style

92. The Press Freedom Index (World Press Freedom Index) is published by which organisation?
(a) UNESCO (b) Committee to Protect Journalists (c) Amnesty International (d) Reporters Without Borders
Answer: (d)
Solution: UNESCO is the trap because it works on media freedom and observes World Press Freedom Day, but it does not publish this ranking. The World Press Freedom Index is published annually by Reporters Without Borders (RSF), headquartered in Paris, so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: Reporters Without Borders (RSF) official website

93. The Global Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) is jointly released by UNDP and which other body?
(a) Asian Development Bank (b) World Bank (c) UNICEF (d) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative
Answer: (d)
Solution: The World Bank is tempting because it measures extreme income poverty, but the global MPI is a different measure covering health, education and living standards. It is released jointly by UNDP and the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI), so the answer is Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: UNDP/OPHI official website

94. Which Indian institution releases the 'India Innovation Index'?
(a) NITI Aayog (b) Ministry of Science and Technology (c) CSIR (d) FICCI
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ministry of Science and Technology sounds logical, which is the trap, but it does not publish this index. The India Innovation Index, ranking states and union territories on innovation, is released by NITI Aayog (with the Institute for Competitiveness), so the answer is NITI Aayog.
Source: NITI Aayog official website

95. The 'Global Climate Risk Index' was published by which organisation?

96. The 'World Inequality Report' is associated with which institution?
(a) London School of Economics (b) Oxfam International (c) World Inequality Lab (d) Brookings Institution

97. Which report is published by the International Labour Organization (ILO)?
(a) Employment Outlook — OECD (b) State of Working India (c) Global Wage Report only for Europe (d) World Employment and Social Outlook
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Employment Outlook is the trap — it exists but is published by the OECD, not the ILO. The ILO's flagship publication is the World Employment and Social Outlook, and it also publishes the Global Wage Report covering all regions, so the answer is World Employment and Social Outlook.
Source: ILO official website

98. The 'Global Environment Outlook' report is published by which UN body?
(a) UNDP (b) UNEP (c) UNFCCC (d) IPCC
Answer: (b)
Solution: The IPCC is the tempting wrong choice because it publishes famous climate assessment reports, but those are scientific assessments, not this report. The Global Environment Outlook is the flagship environmental assessment of the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), so the answer is UNEP.

99. Arrange these reports in the correct chronological order of their first publication: 1. Human Development Report 2. World Development Report 3. Corruption Perceptions Index
(a) 2, 1, 3 (b) 1, 2, 3 (c) 3, 2, 1 (d) 2, 3, 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: The chronological confusion trap is that the Human Development Report feels older, but it actually began in 1990. The World Development Report started first in 1978, followed by the Human Development Report in 1990, and the Corruption Perceptions Index in 1995, so the answer is 2, 1, 3.
Source: PYQ-style

100. The 'Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) India Index' is released by which body?
(a) NITI Aayog (b) Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (c) United Nations India office (d) Ministry of Rural Development
Answer: (a)
Solution: The UN India office is the trap because the SDGs are a UN framework, but the India-specific index ranking states is a domestic exercise. The SDG India Index is released by NITI Aayog in collaboration with the UN in India, so the answer is NITI Aayog.
Source: NITI Aayog official website
