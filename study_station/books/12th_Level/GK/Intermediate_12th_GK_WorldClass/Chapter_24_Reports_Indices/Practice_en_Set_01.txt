1. Which organisation publishes the Human Development Report (HDR)?
(a) World Bank (b) United Nations Development Programme (c) World Economic Forum (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, not the HDR, which is the tempting confusion. The Human Development Report, with the Human Development Index, has been published by the UNDP since 1990, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: UNDP official website (hdr.undp.org)

2. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which organisations?
(a) FAO and WFP (b) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (c) UNICEF and WHO (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students pick FAO and WFP because they are UN food agencies, but they only supply data. The Global Hunger Index is published jointly by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

3. The World Happiness Report is published by which body?
(a) World Health Organisation (b) UNESCO (c) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (d) OECD
Answer: (c)
Solution: WHO and UNESCO deal with health and culture, not happiness rankings. The World Happiness Report is published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, using Gallup World Poll data, so the answer is UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network.
Source: worldhappiness.report

4. The Global Gender Gap Report is released by which organisation?
(a) World Economic Forum (b) World Bank (c) UN Women (d) UNDP
Answer: (a)
Solution: UN Women sounds like the natural publisher, which is exactly the trap. The Global Gender Gap Report is an annual publication of the World Economic Forum, Geneva, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

5. The Ease of Doing Business report, which was discontinued in 2021, was published by which institution?
(a) World Economic Forum (b) WTO (c) IMF (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The World Economic Forum publishes the Global Competitiveness Report, which students often confuse with this one. The Ease of Doing Business report was a World Bank publication, discontinued after data irregularities, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

6. Which index measures the performance of countries on environmental health and ecosystem vitality?
(a) Environmental Performance Index (b) Global Peace Index (c) Corruption Perceptions Index (d) Human Capital Index
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Human Capital Index measures education and health of workers, not the environment. The Environmental Performance Index, prepared by Yale and Columbia Universities with the WEF, ranks countries on environmental health and ecosystem vitality, so the answer is Environmental Performance Index.
Source: epi.yale.edu

7. The Corruption Perceptions Index is published by which organisation?
(a) Transparency International (b) Amnesty International (c) World Bank (d) United Nations
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amnesty International works on human rights, not corruption rankings — a common mix-up. The Corruption Perceptions Index is published annually by Berlin-based Transparency International, so the answer is Transparency International.
Source: Transparency International official website

8. The Global Peace Index is published by which institute?
(a) United Nations (b) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (c) Institute for Economics and Peace (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (c)
Solution: SIPRI is famous for arms and military expenditure data, which makes it the tempting wrong choice. The Global Peace Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, headquartered in Sydney, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: visionofhumanity.org

9. The Human Development Index combines which three dimensions?
(a) Income, trade, population (b) Education, environment, income (c) Health, employment, housing (d) Health, education, standard of living
Answer: (d)
Solution: Options mentioning trade or environment are distractors; HDI does not measure either. HDI combines life expectancy (health), schooling (education) and GNI per capita (standard of living), so the answer is health, education, standard of living.
Source: UNDP, Human Development Report

10. The World Press Freedom Index is published by which organisation?
(a) UNESCO (b) Reporters Without Borders (c) Amnesty International (d) Committee to Protect Journalists
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNESCO promotes press freedom but does not publish this index — that is the trap. The World Press Freedom Index is published annually by Reporters Without Borders (RSF), based in Paris, so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: rsf.org

11. The Global Innovation Index is co-published by which UN agency?
(a) WTO (b) WIPO (c) UNCTAD (d) UNESCO
Answer: (b)
Solution: WTO deals with trade rules, not innovation rankings. The Global Innovation Index is published by the World Intellectual Property Organisation (WIPO) along with partner institutions, so the answer is WIPO.
Source: wipo.int

12. The Human Capital Index is a publication of which institution?
(a) UNDP (b) IMF (c) World Bank (d) ILO
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNDP publishes HDI, which sounds similar and traps many students. The Human Capital Index, measuring child survival, schooling and health, is a World Bank project launched in 2018, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

13. The Global Competitiveness Report is published by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) OECD (c) World Economic Forum (d) IMF
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Bank published Ease of Doing Business, which students confuse with competitiveness. The Global Competitiveness Report is the flagship index of the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

14. The Henley Passport Index ranks countries on the basis of what?
(a) Number of embassies abroad (b) Number of international treaties signed (c) Size of diplomatic staff (d) Visa-free or visa-on-arrival access of passports
Answer: (d)
Solution: Diplomatic strength options are distractors; the index has nothing to do with embassies. The Henley Passport Index ranks passports by the number of destinations their holders can access without a prior visa, so the answer is visa-free or visa-on-arrival access of passports.
Source: henleyglobal.com

15. The Global Terrorism Index is published by which body?
(a) United Nations Security Council (b) Institute for Economics and Peace (c) Interpol (d) NATO
Answer: (b)
Solution: Interpol and the UNSC deal with security operations, not this statistical index. The Global Terrorism Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, the same body behind the Global Peace Index, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: visionofhumanity.org

16. The World Development Report is published annually by which institution?
(a) IMF (b) WTO (c) World Bank (d) ADB
Answer: (c)
Solution: The IMF publishes the World Economic Outlook, which is the classic confusion here. The World Development Report is the World Bank's flagship annual publication since 1978, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

17. The World Economic Outlook report is published by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) World Economic Forum (c) International Monetary Fund (d) OECD
Answer: (c)
Solution: Despite its name, this report is not from the World Economic Forum — that is the examiner's favourite trap. The World Economic Outlook is published twice a year by the International Monetary Fund, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.
Source: IMF official website

18. The Global Multidimensional Poverty Index is released by UNDP in partnership with which institution?
(a) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (b) London School of Economics (c) Harvard University (d) World Bank
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Bank measures monetary poverty, not this multidimensional index. The Global MPI is released by UNDP together with the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI), so the answer is Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: ophi.org.uk / UNDP

19. The Global Food Security Index is published by which organisation?
(a) Economist Impact (b) FAO (c) World Food Programme (d) IFAD
Answer: (a)
Solution: FAO is the most tempting wrong answer because it is the UN food agency, but it does not publish this index. The Global Food Security Index is published by Economist Impact (Economist Intelligence Unit), so the answer is Economist Impact.
Source: impact.economist.com

20. In India, the NITI Aayog releases which of the following indices?
(a) Global Hunger Index (b) SDG India Index (c) Human Development Index (d) Corruption Perceptions Index
Answer: (b)
Solution: The other three are international indices, not Indian publications — a frequent confusion. NITI Aayog releases the SDG India Index to track states' progress on Sustainable Development Goals, so the answer is SDG India Index.
Source: NITI Aayog official website

21. Consider the following statements about the Human Development Index:
1. It was created by Pakistani economist Mahbub ul Haq and Indian economist Amartya Sen.
2. It uses GDP per capita as its income indicator.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Neither 1 nor 2 (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) 1 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: HDI uses GNI (Gross National Income) per capita, not GDP per capita, so it is false. Statement 1 is correct — Mahbub ul Haq devised HDI with Amartya Sen's contribution, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: UNDP, Human Development Report

22. Match the index with its publisher:
1. Global Peace Index — Institute for Economics and Peace
2. Corruption Perceptions Index — Transparency International
3. World Happiness Report — World Bank
Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Pair 3 is the trap: the World Happiness Report comes from the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, not the World Bank. Pairs 1 and 2 are correctly matched, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Consider the following statements about the Global Hunger Index:
1. It uses undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting and child mortality as indicators.
2. It is published by the Food and Agriculture Organisation.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 only (b) 1 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: the GHI is published by Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe, not the FAO. Statement 1 correctly lists the four GHI indicators, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

24. Arrange the following reports in chronological order of their first publication, from earliest to latest:
1. Human Development Report
2. World Development Report
3. Global Gender Gap Report
(a) 1, 2, 3 (b) 2, 3, 1 (c) 3, 2, 1 (d) 2, 1, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming the Human Development Report is the oldest because it is the most famous. The World Development Report began in 1978, the Human Development Report in 1990, and the Global Gender Gap Report in 2006, so the answer is 2, 1, 3.
Source: World Bank / UNDP / WEF official websites

25. Consider the following statements:
1. The Global Innovation Index is published by WIPO.
2. The World Press Freedom Index is published by UNESCO.
3. The Ease of Doing Business report was discontinued by the World Bank in 2021.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: the World Press Freedom Index is published by Reporters Without Borders, not UNESCO. Statements 1 and 3 are correct — WIPO publishes the GII and the World Bank discontinued Doing Business in 2021, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.
Source: WIPO / RSF / World Bank official websites
