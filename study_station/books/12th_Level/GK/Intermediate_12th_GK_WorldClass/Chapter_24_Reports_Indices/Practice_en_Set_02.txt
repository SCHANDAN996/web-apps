Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published annually by which organisation?
(a) United Nations Development Programme (b) World Bank (c) World Economic Forum (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, which is the tempting wrong choice because both are UN-system bodies. HDI, however, is released by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Which of the following is NOT one of the three dimensions of the Human Development Index?
(a) Life expectancy at birth (b) Gender equality (c) Education (d) Gross National Income per capita
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gender equality is measured by the Gender Inequality Index, not HDI — this is the classic trap of mixing two UNDP indices. HDI uses health (life expectancy), education, and standard of living (GNI per capita), so the answer is Gender equality.
Source: PYQ-style

28. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which organisations?
(a) FAO and WFP (b) UNICEF and WHO (c) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (c)
Solution: FAO and WFP are UN food agencies and sound plausible, but the Global Hunger Index is published by the NGOs Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: PYQ-style

29. The Corruption Perceptions Index is released by which body?
(a) Amnesty International (b) Transparency International (c) World Economic Forum (d) OECD
Answer: (b)
Solution: Amnesty International works on human rights, not corruption rankings — a similar-name trap. The Corruption Perceptions Index is published by Berlin-based Transparency International, so the answer is Transparency International.
Source: PYQ-style

30. The World Press Freedom Index is published annually by which organisation?
(a) UNESCO (b) Amnesty International (c) Reporters Without Borders (d) International Press Institute
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNESCO is a tempting choice as it deals with media freedom, but the index is compiled by Reporters Without Borders (RSF), headquartered in Paris, so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: PYQ-style

31. The Global Competitiveness Report is published by which institution?
(a) World Bank (b) International Monetary Fund (c) WTO (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (d)
Solution: The World Bank is the common trap here since it publishes many development reports. The Global Competitiveness Report comes from the World Economic Forum (Davos), so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: PYQ-style

32. The Ease of Doing Business report, discontinued in 2021, was published by which organisation?
(a) World Economic Forum (b) UNCTAD (c) IMF (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The World Economic Forum publishes the Global Competitiveness Report, which confuses many candidates. The Ease of Doing Business report was a World Bank publication, discontinued after data irregularities, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: PYQ-style

33. The Global Peace Index is published by which institute?
(a) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (b) Institute for Economics and Peace (c) United Nations Peacekeeping (d) Carnegie Endowment
Answer: (b)
Solution: SIPRI is the tempting wrong option because it deals with peace and arms data, but it does not publish this index. The Global Peace Index is released by the Institute for Economics and Peace, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: PYQ-style

34. The World Happiness Report is published by which body?
(a) UNDP (b) WHO (c) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (d) World Bank
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNDP is a frequent wrong guess because it publishes human development reports. The World Happiness Report is published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, so the answer is UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network.
Source: PYQ-style

35. The Global Innovation Index is co-published by WIPO along with which of the following?
(a) Portulans Institute (b) World Bank (c) OECD (d) UNESCO
Answer: (a)
Solution: OECD is a plausible distractor as it works on innovation policy, but the Global Innovation Index is published by WIPO together with the Portulans Institute, so the answer is Portulans Institute.
Source: PYQ-style

36. The Human Development Report was first published in which year?
(a) 1990 (b) 1985 (c) 1995 (d) 2000
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1995 is the trap year, often confused with the Beijing Conference or the first Gender-related indices. The first Human Development Report was launched by UNDP in 1990 under Mahbub ul Haq, so the answer is 1990.
Source: PYQ-style

37. The concept of Human Development was developed under the leadership of which economist?
(a) Mahbub ul Haq (b) Amartya Sen (c) Joseph Stiglitz (d) Jeffrey Sachs
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amartya Sen is the classic trap — he contributed the capability approach, but the Human Development Report was pioneered by Pakistani economist Mahbub ul Haq, so the answer is Mahbub ul Haq.
Source: PYQ-style

38. The Global Gender Gap Report is published by which organisation?
(a) UN Women (b) UNDP (c) World Economic Forum (d) World Bank
Answer: (c)
Solution: UN Women sounds correct because of its gender mandate, but the Global Gender Gap Report is an annual publication of the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: PYQ-style

39. The Global Terrorism Index is published by which institute?
(a) RAND Corporation (b) Institute for Economics and Peace (c) United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (d) Interpol
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNODC deals with crime and drugs and is a tempting distractor. The Global Terrorism Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, the same body behind the Global Peace Index, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: PYQ-style

40. The Environmental Performance Index is prepared by Yale and Columbia Universities in collaboration with which organisation?
(a) UNEP (b) World Bank (c) World Economic Forum (d) IPCC
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNEP is the natural trap since it is the UN's environment agency. The Environmental Performance Index is a biennial report of Yale and Columbia with the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: PYQ-style

41. The State of World Population report is published by which UN agency?
(a) UNFPA (b) UNICEF (c) WHO (d) UNDESA
Answer: (a)
Solution: UNICEF is a common wrong answer as it deals with children and population-adjacent issues. The State of World Population report is published by the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA), so the answer is UNFPA.
Source: PYQ-style

42. The World Development Report is an annual publication of which institution?
(a) IMF (b) Asian Development Bank (c) WTO (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The IMF publishes the World Economic Outlook, which candidates often confuse with this report. The World Development Report is the flagship annual publication of the World Bank, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: PYQ-style

43. The World Economic Outlook report is published how many times a year by the IMF?
(a) Twice (b) Once (c) Thrice (d) Four times
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Once a year" is the tempting answer because most flagship reports are annual. The IMF's World Economic Outlook is published twice a year, usually in April and October, so the answer is Twice.
Source: PYQ-style

44. The Global Financial Stability Report is published by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) Bank for International Settlements (c) Financial Stability Board (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Financial Stability Board is the trap because of the matching name. The Global Financial Stability Report is a semi-annual publication of the International Monetary Fund, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.
Source: PYQ-style

45. The State of the World's Children report is published by which agency?
(a) WHO (b) UNESCO (c) UNICEF (d) Save the Children
Answer: (c)
Solution: Save the Children is a well-known NGO and a tempting distractor. The State of the World's Children is the flagship report of UNICEF, so the answer is UNICEF.
Source: PYQ-style

46. The Global Multidimensional Poverty Index is jointly released by UNDP and which other institution?
(a) World Bank (b) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (c) UNICEF (d) Brookings Institution
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank is the tempting choice since it measures income poverty. The Multidimensional Poverty Index is released by UNDP with the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI), so the answer is Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: PYQ-style

47. The Global Slavery Index is published by which foundation?
(a) Ford Foundation (b) Walk Free Foundation (c) Gates Foundation (d) Open Society Foundations
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Gates Foundation is a plausible guess due to its global development work, but the Global Slavery Index is published by the Australia-based Walk Free Foundation, so the answer is Walk Free Foundation.
Source: PYQ-style

48. The Logistics Performance Index is published by which organisation?
(a) World Trade Organization (b) UNCTAD (c) IMF (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: UNCTAD deals with trade and development and is a common trap. The Logistics Performance Index, covering customs, infrastructure and shipments, is published by the World Bank, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: PYQ-style

49. Arrange these reports in the correct chronological order of their first publication: 1. Human Development Report 2. World Development Report 3. Global Gender Gap Report
(a) 2, 1, 3 (b) 1, 2, 3 (c) 2, 3, 1 (d) 1, 3, 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the Human Development Report is the oldest. The World Development Report began in 1978, the Human Development Report in 1990, and the Global Gender Gap Report in 2006, so the answer is 2, 1, 3.
Source: PYQ-style

50. The Global Food Security Index is published by which organisation?
(a) FAO (b) IFAD (c) World Food Programme (d) Economist Impact
Answer: (d)
Solution: FAO is the tempting wrong answer because it is the UN's food agency. The Global Food Security Index is published by Economist Impact (Economist Intelligence Unit), so the answer is Economist Impact.
Source: PYQ-style
