# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Reports & Indices

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from This Chapter

Reports & Indices is a **high-frequency, low-effort** chapter. Almost every major exam — UPSC Prelims, SSC CGL/CHSL, IBPS PO/Clerk, RRB NTPC, State PCS — asks 1–3 questions from it in the General Awareness section. The questions are almost always **direct one-liners**: "Which organisation publishes X report?" or "What is India's rank in Y index?"

The pattern across exams:

- **Banking exams (IBPS, SBI, RBI):** Heaviest focus. They ask the *publishing organisation*, the *theme of the latest edition*, and *India's rank* — often all three in different shifts.
- **SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS):** Focus on the publishing body and the country/organisation that tops the index. India's rank is asked less often than the topper.
- **UPSC / State PCS:** Less about ranks, more about *who publishes what* and *conceptual understanding* (e.g., what does the index actually measure, which indicators are used).
- **Railway (NTPC, Group D):** Simple matching-type questions — report name ↔ organisation.

## 2. Sub-Topics That Recur Most Often

1. **Publishing organisations** — the single most repeated question type. (World Bank → Ease of Doing Business [discontinued], WEF → Global Gender Gap / Global Competitiveness, UNDP → Human Development Index, Transparency International → Corruption Perceptions Index, WHO/UNESCO/UNICEF flagship reports.)
2. **India's rank** in major indices (HDI, Global Hunger Index, Corruption Perceptions Index, Press Freedom Index, Happiness Report).
3. **Top-ranked country** in each index (e.g., Nordic countries dominate happiness, press freedom, gender gap).
4. **Theme of the latest edition** of a report (asked mainly in Banking exams).
5. **Indices released by Indian bodies** — NITI Aayog (SDG India Index, Innovation Index), Ministry of Education (NIRF rankings), Swachh Survekshan (MoHUA).
6. **Confusing pairs** — Global Competitiveness (WEF) vs Ease of Doing Business (World Bank); Human Development Index (UNDP) vs Human Capital Index (World Bank).

## 3. The Examiner's Traps (जाल)

**Trap 1 — Almost Correct Organisation:** Options list four real organisations, but only one publishes the report asked. E.g., for Global Gender Gap Report, options may include UN Women, UNDP, World Bank — all plausible — but the answer is **World Economic Forum**.

**Trap 2 — Chronological Confusion:** The question asks for the rank in the *latest* report, but one option is India's rank from the *previous* edition. Aspirants who memorised an old rank fall for it.

**Trap 3 — Negative Phrasing:** "Which of the following is **NOT** published by the World Bank?" Students in a hurry miss the NOT and pick the first World Bank report they see.

**Trap 4 — Similar-Sounding Names:** Human Development **Index** (UNDP) vs Human Capital **Index** (World Bank) vs Inequality-adjusted HDI. Also: Global Hunger Index (Concern Worldwide & Welthungerhilfe) vs Global Food Security Index (Economist Impact).

**Trap 5 — Discontinued Reports:** Ease of Doing Business was **discontinued by the World Bank in 2021**. Questions now test whether you know its replacement discussions (B-READY) or trap you into treating it as current.

## 4. High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions with Solutions

**Q1. The Global Gender Gap Report is published by —**
(a) UN Women (b) UNDP (c) World Economic Forum (d) World Bank
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** WEF publishes it annually. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — UN Women looks tailor-made for this report, which is exactly why it is the most-chosen wrong option.

**Q2. Which organisation releases the Human Development Index?**
(a) World Bank (b) UNDP (c) IMF (d) WHO
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** UNDP's Human Development Report Office publishes the HDI. Trap type: *Similar-Sounding Names* — Human Capital Index is World Bank's; don't mix them.

**Q3. The Corruption Perceptions Index is released by —**
(a) Transparency International (b) World Bank (c) Amnesty International (d) OECD
**Answer: (a)**
**Solution:** Berlin-based Transparency International publishes it. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — Amnesty International works on human rights, not corruption rankings.

**Q4. Which of the following is NOT a World Bank publication?**
(a) World Development Report (b) Global Financial Development Report (c) World Economic Outlook (d) Global Economic Prospects
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** World Economic Outlook is an **IMF** publication. Trap type: *Negative Phrasing* — read the NOT twice before answering.

**Q5. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by —**
(a) FAO and WFP (b) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (c) WHO and UNICEF (d) World Bank and IMF
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Irish NGO Concern Worldwide and German NGO Welthungerhilfe publish it. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — FAO/WFP are UN food bodies, the obvious-looking but wrong choice.

**Q6. NIRF rankings in India are released by —**
(a) NITI Aayog (b) UGC (c) Ministry of Education (d) AICTE
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** The National Institutional Ranking Framework is under the Ministry of Education. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — NITI Aayog releases many indices, so it is the planted distractor.

**Q7. The World Happiness Report is published by —**
(a) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (b) UNDP (c) World Bank (d) OECD
**Answer: (a)**
**Solution:** SDSN publishes it; Finland has topped it repeatedly in recent editions. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — UNDP is the habitual wrong pick.

**Q8. The Ease of Doing Business report, discontinued in 2021, was published by —**
(a) WEF (b) World Bank (c) IMF (d) WTO
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** World Bank discontinued it after data irregularities. Trap type: *Chronological Confusion* — questions now test whether you know it no longer exists.

**Q9. The Global Competitiveness Report is released by —**
(a) World Bank (b) IMF (c) World Economic Forum (d) UNCTAD
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** WEF publishes it. Trap type: *Similar-Sounding Names* — students swap it with Ease of Doing Business (World Bank).

**Q10. The World Press Freedom Index is published by —**
(a) UNESCO (b) Reporters Without Borders (RSF) (c) Amnesty International (d) UNHRC
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Paris-based RSF (Reporters Sans Frontières) releases it annually. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — UNESCO deals with media freedom themes, making it the tempting wrong answer.

## 5. The 15/45-Second Rule in Action

**15-second questions (answer instantly, don't overthink):**
Q1, Q2, Q3, Q7, Q10 above are pure recall — "who publishes what." If you have revised the organisation list, these take under 15 seconds. **Rule:** If you know it, mark it and move. If you don't, never spend more than 45 seconds — the answer won't appear by staring.

**45-second questions (need careful reading):**
Q4 and Q8 are the 45-second type. Q4 has negative phrasing — you must verify each option's publisher. Q8 tests a *status change* (discontinued report) — you must recall both the publisher and the 2021 development. **Rule:** For NOT/EXCEPT questions and "latest edition" questions, budget the full 45 seconds; a rushed answer here is a negative-mark answer.

**Skip strategy:** If a question asks for an exact rank you haven't revised (e.g., "India's rank in the latest Global Innovation Index"), skip it rather than guess between two adjacent numbers — rank questions have near-random guess value and carry negative marking in most exams.

## 6. Key Takeaways for Revision

- Master the **organisation ↔ report** table first; it covers the majority of questions.
- For ranks, revise only **India's rank + top country** in the 8–10 biggest indices, and always with the **year** attached.
- Treat every NOT/EXCEPT question as a 45-second question.
- Remember the discontinued/changed items: Ease of Doing Business (discontinued 2021), and check the current publisher status of any index that changed hands recently.
