Questions 126–150: Hard

126. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published annually by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) United Nations Development Programme (c) World Economic Forum (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, not HDI — a common mix-up. HDI has been published by the United Nations Development Programme since 1990, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: UNDP official website

127. Which of the following is NOT one of the three dimensions of the Human Development Index?
(a) Life expectancy at birth (b) Education (c) Gender equality (d) Gross National Income per capita
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gender equality is measured by the Gender Inequality Index, a separate index — this is the tempting trap. HDI's three dimensions are health, education, and standard of living, so the answer is Gender equality.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

128. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which two organisations?
(a) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (b) UNICEF and WHO (c) World Bank and IMF (d) FAO and WFP
Answer: (a)
Solution: FAO and WFP are UN food agencies but they do not publish the GHI — the most common trap. The Global Hunger Index is published by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

129. The World Happiness Report uses data primarily from which source?
(a) Gallup World Poll (b) Pew Research Center surveys (c) Eurobarometer (d) World Values Survey only
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Values Survey is a real survey but is not the primary source — a near-correct trap. The World Happiness Report is based mainly on Gallup World Poll data, so the answer is Gallup World Poll.
Source: World Happiness Report official website

130. The Corruption Perceptions Index is released by which body?
(a) Transparency International (b) World Economic Forum (c) OECD (d) Amnesty International
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amnesty International works on human rights, not corruption rankings — a similar-name trap. The Corruption Perceptions Index is published annually by Berlin-based Transparency International, so the answer is Transparency International.
Source: Transparency International official website

131. In the Corruption Perceptions Index, a score of 100 indicates what?
(a) Very clean (b) Highly corrupt (c) Moderately corrupt (d) Data unavailable
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many candidates assume a high score means high corruption — the classic reversed-scale trap. On the CPI scale of 0 to 100, 0 means highly corrupt and 100 means very clean, so the answer is Very clean.
Source: Transparency International

132. The Global Gender Gap Report is published by which organisation?
(a) UN Women (b) World Bank (c) World Economic Forum (d) UNDP
Answer: (c)
Solution: UN Women works on gender issues but does not publish this report — the most tempting wrong choice. The Global Gender Gap Report is an annual publication of the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

133. Which index measures countries on the basis of pillars such as safety and security, personal freedom, governance, and economic quality?
(a) Legatum Prosperity Index (b) Global Peace Index (c) Human Development Index (d) Ease of Doing Business Index
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Global Peace Index measures only peacefulness, not prosperity pillars — a partial-match trap. The Legatum Prosperity Index assesses prosperity through pillars like safety, freedom, governance and economy, so the answer is Legatum Prosperity Index.
Source: Legatum Institute official website

134. The Global Peace Index is published by which institute?
(a) United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research (b) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (c) Institute for Economics and Peace (d) Carnegie Endowment
Answer: (c)
Solution: SIPRI is a famous peace research body and is the most tempting wrong answer, but it publishes military expenditure data, not the GPI. The Global Peace Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace official website

135. The Ease of Doing Business report, discontinued in 2021, was published by which institution?
(a) World Economic Forum (b) Asian Development Bank (c) World Bank (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Economic Forum publishes the Global Competitiveness Report, which candidates often confuse with this. The Ease of Doing Business report was a World Bank publication, discontinued after data irregularities, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

136. The Global Innovation Index is published by which specialised agency of the United Nations?
(a) UNIDO (b) UNESCO (c) WIPO (d) ITU
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNESCO deals with education, science and culture and is the plausible trap, but it does not publish this index. The Global Innovation Index is published by the World Intellectual Property Organization, so the answer is WIPO.
Source: WIPO official website

137. The World Press Freedom Index is released annually by which organisation?
(a) Committee to Protect Journalists (b) Reporters Without Borders (c) International Press Institute (d) Article 19
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Committee to Protect Journalists is a real media-freedom body and the classic trap, but it does not publish this index. The World Press Freedom Index is published by Reporters Without Borders (RSF), so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: RSF official website

138. Which of the following indices is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU)?
(a) Global Terrorism Index (b) Human Freedom Index (c) Social Progress Index (d) Democracy Index
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Global Terrorism Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, not EIU — a common confusion. The Democracy Index is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit, so the answer is Democracy Index.
Source: EIU official website

139. The Henley Passport Index ranks passports based on what criterion?
(a) Number of destinations a passport holder can visit without a prior visa (b) Number of embassies of the issuing country (c) Strength of the issuing country's economy (d) Security features of the passport
Answer: (a)
Solution: Economic strength correlates with passport power but is not the criterion — a reasoning trap. The Henley Passport Index ranks passports by the number of destinations accessible without a prior visa, so the answer is Number of destinations a passport holder can visit without a prior visa.
Source: Henley & Partners official website

140. The Global Multidimensional Poverty Index is jointly released by UNDP and which other body?
(a) World Bank (b) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (c) International Labour Organization (d) UNICEF
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank publishes monetary poverty estimates but not this index — the tempting wrong option. The global MPI is a joint publication of UNDP and the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative, so the answer is Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: UNDP/OPHI official website

141. The India Innovation Index, which ranks Indian states and union territories on innovation performance, is released by which body?
(a) Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (b) Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (c) NITI Aayog (d) Ministry of Science and Technology
Answer: (c)
Solution: DPIIT deals with industrial promotion and is the plausible trap, but it does not release this index. The India Innovation Index is released by NITI Aayog, so the answer is NITI Aayog.
Source: NITI Aayog official website

142. The Environmental Performance Index is prepared by Yale and Columbia universities in collaboration with which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) World Economic Forum (c) Greenpeace (d) UNEP
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNEP is the UN's environment body and the most tempting wrong answer, but it is not a partner in this index. The EPI is prepared by Yale and Columbia in collaboration with the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: EPI official website

143. Which Indian institution releases the State Energy and Climate Index?
(a) Ministry of Power (b) Bureau of Energy Efficiency (c) Central Electricity Authority (d) NITI Aayog
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Ministry of Power deals with energy and is the plausible trap, but this index is not its publication. The State Energy and Climate Index is released by NITI Aayog, so the answer is NITI Aayog.
Source: NITI Aayog official website

144. The Global Competitiveness Report is published by which body?
(a) International Monetary Fund (b) World Economic Forum (c) World Bank (d) OECD
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank published Ease of Doing Business, which candidates often swap with this — a similar-report trap. The Global Competitiveness Report is the flagship publication of the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

145. Which of the following correctly matches the index with its publisher?
(a) Logistics Performance Index — World Bank (b) Human Freedom Index — United Nations (c) Global Food Security Index — FAO (d) Global Terrorism Index — Interpol
Answer: (a)
Solution: Interpol is a policing body and does not publish the Global Terrorism Index — the trap in the last option. The Logistics Performance Index is published by the World Bank, so the answer is Logistics Performance Index — World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

146. The World Development Report is the flagship annual publication of which institution?
(a) WTO (b) IMF (c) ADB (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The IMF publishes the World Economic Outlook, which sounds similar and is the classic confusion. The World Development Report is published by the World Bank, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

147. Arrange these reports in correct chronological order of their first publication: 1. Human Development Report 2. World Happiness Report 3. Global Gender Gap Report
(a) 3, 1, 2 (b) 1, 2, 3 (c) 2, 1, 3 (d) 1, 3, 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap: many assume the Happiness Report is older than the Gender Gap Report. The Human Development Report began in 1990, the Global Gender Gap Report in 2006, and the World Happiness Report in 2012, so the answer is 1, 3, 2.
Source: PYQ-style

148. The Global Liveability Index, ranking cities on quality of life, is published by which organisation?
(a) Mercer (b) Economist Intelligence Unit (c) World Economic Forum (d) UN-Habitat
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mercer publishes a Quality of Living survey, which is the near-correct trap, but it is a different ranking. The Global Liveability Index is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit, so the answer is Economist Intelligence Unit.
Source: EIU official website

149. India's National Multidimensional Poverty Index is released by which body?
(a) Ministry of Rural Development (b) National Sample Survey Office (c) Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (d) NITI Aayog
Answer: (d)
Solution: MoSPI handles official statistics and is the tempting wrong choice, but it does not release this index. India's national MPI, based on NFHS data, is released by NITI Aayog, so the answer is NITI Aayog.
Source: NITI Aayog official website

150. Which statement about the Human Development Report is correct?
(a) It is published by the World Bank since 1990 (b) It ranks countries only on per capita income (c) It is published once every five years (d) It was first published in 1990, drawing on the capability approach associated with Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first option is an almost-correct trap — the year 1990 is right but the publisher is UNDP, not the World Bank. The HDR was first published in 1990 under Mahbub ul Haq, drawing on Amartya Sen's capability approach, so the answer is It was first published in 1990, drawing on the capability approach associated with Mahbub ul Haq and Amartya Sen.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report
