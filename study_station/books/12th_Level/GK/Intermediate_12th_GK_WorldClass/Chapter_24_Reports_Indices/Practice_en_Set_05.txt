101. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published annually by which organisation?
(a) United Nations Development Programme (b) World Bank (c) World Economic Forum (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Economic Forum is a tempting wrong choice because it publishes the Global Gender Gap and Global Competitiveness reports, but it does not publish HDI. The Human Development Report, containing the HDI, has been published by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) since 1990, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report (official)

102. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which two organisations?
(a) FAO and WFP (b) UNICEF and WHO (c) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (c)
Solution: FAO and WFP are tempting because both are UN food agencies, but they publish the State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World report, not GHI. The Global Hunger Index is a peer-reviewed annual report jointly published by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

103. The three dimensions of the Human Development Index are:
(a) Health, education and standard of living (b) Income, employment and trade (c) Health, wealth and happiness (d) Education, environment and economy
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Health, wealth and happiness" sounds attractive but is not the official framework. HDI measures a long and healthy life (life expectancy), access to knowledge (mean and expected years of schooling) and a decent standard of living (GNI per capita), so the answer is health, education and standard of living.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report (official)

104. The World Happiness Report is published by:
(a) UNDP (b) Gallup (c) World Health Organisation (d) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network
Answer: (d)
Solution: Gallup is the tempting wrong option because its world poll supplies the underlying data, but it does not publish the report. The World Happiness Report is published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, so the answer is UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network.
Source: World Happiness Report (official)

105. The Global Gender Gap Report is released by:
(a) UN Women (b) World Bank (c) UNDP (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (d)
Solution: UN Women is a plausible distractor since it works on gender equality, but it does not publish this index. The Global Gender Gap Report, benchmarking countries on economic participation, education, health and political empowerment, is published by the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum (official)

106. The Corruption Perceptions Index is published by:
(a) Transparency International (b) World Bank (c) Amnesty International (d) OECD
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amnesty International is a tempting wrong choice as a famous watchdog NGO, but it works on human rights, not corruption rankings. The Corruption Perceptions Index, scoring countries on perceived public-sector corruption, is published annually by Berlin-based Transparency International, so the answer is Transparency International.
Source: Transparency International (official)

107. The Ease of Doing Business report, discontinued in 2021, was published by:
(a) World Economic Forum (b) World Bank (c) IMF (d) WTO
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Economic Forum is tempting because of its competitiveness rankings, but the Doing Business report was a flagship World Bank publication. It was discontinued in 2021 after data irregularities were found, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank (official)

108. The Global Peace Index is published by:
(a) United Nations (b) Institute for Economics and Peace (c) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (d) World Bank
Answer: (b)
Solution: SIPRI is the tempting wrong option because it is a famous peace research body, but it publishes data on arms transfers and military expenditure, not the Global Peace Index. The GPI is produced by the Institute for Economics and Peace, headquartered in Sydney, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace (official)

109. In the HDI, the standard of living dimension is measured by:
(a) GDP per capita at current prices (b) Household savings rate (c) Per capita electricity consumption (d) GNI per capita (PPP, US dollars)
Answer: (d)
Solution: GDP per capita is the tempting wrong answer, but HDI deliberately uses Gross National Income per capita at purchasing power parity, not GDP, because GNI captures income accruing to residents. So the answer is GNI per capita (PPP, US dollars).
Source: UNDP Human Development Report (official)

110. The World Press Freedom Index is published by:
(a) UNESCO (b) Reporters Without Borders (c) Amnesty International (d) Committee to Protect Journalists
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNESCO is tempting because it promotes freedom of expression, but it does not publish this ranking. The World Press Freedom Index is published annually by Reporters Without Borders (Reporters Sans Frontières), headquartered in Paris, so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: Reporters Without Borders (official)

111. The Global Innovation Index is co-published by WIPO along with partners; WIPO is headquartered at:
(a) New York (b) Paris (c) Geneva (d) Vienna
Answer: (c)
Solution: New York is tempting as the UN headquarters, but the World Intellectual Property Organization, a specialised UN agency that publishes the Global Innovation Index, is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: WIPO (official)

112. The Global Competitiveness Report was traditionally published by:
(a) World Bank (b) UNCTAD (c) IMF (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (d)
Solution: The World Bank is tempting because of its former Doing Business report, but the Global Competitiveness Report and its index were published by the Geneva-based World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.

113. The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World (SOFI) report is led by:
(a) FAO (b) World Bank (c) WTO (d) IMF
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Bank is a distractor since it funds food programmes, but SOFI is a flagship UN report led by the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) along with IFAD, UNICEF, WFP and WHO, so the answer is FAO.
Source: FAO (official)

114. India's NITI Aayog publishes which of the following indices?
(a) Global Hunger Index (b) Human Development Index (c) SDG India Index (d) Corruption Perceptions Index
Answer: (c)
Solution: The other three are tempting because they are famous indices, but all are published by international bodies, not by any Indian institution. NITI Aayog publishes the SDG India Index to track states' progress on Sustainable Development Goals, so the answer is SDG India Index.
Source: NITI Aayog (official)

115. The Henley Passport Index ranks countries on the basis of:
(a) Visa-free or visa-on-arrival access of passports (b) Number of embassies abroad (c) Number of international airports (d) Tourist arrivals
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tourist arrivals is a tempting wrong option since travel is involved, but the index measures how many destinations a passport holder can access without a prior visa. It is based on IATA data, so the answer is visa-free or visa-on-arrival access of passports.
Source: Henley & Partners (official)

116. The Global Terrorism Index is published by:
(a) Institute for Economics and Peace (b) United Nations Security Council (c) Interpol (d) World Bank
Answer: (a)
Solution: Interpol is the tempting wrong choice as an international policing body, but it does not publish this index. The Global Terrorism Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, the same body behind the Global Peace Index, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace (official)

117. The World Development Report is an annual flagship publication of:
(a) IMF (b) World Bank (c) UNDP (d) Asian Development Bank
Answer: (b)
Solution: The IMF is tempting because it publishes the World Economic Outlook, a similarly named report — a classic similar-names trap. The World Development Report has been published annually by the World Bank since 1978, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank (official)

118. The Environmental Performance Index is prepared by researchers at:
(a) Yale and Columbia Universities (b) Oxford and Cambridge (c) Harvard and MIT (d) Stanford and Princeton
Answer: (a)
Solution: Harvard and MIT are tempting as famous universities, but the EPI is a biennial index developed by Yale University and Columbia University in collaboration with the World Economic Forum, so the answer is Yale and Columbia Universities.
Source: Environmental Performance Index (official)

119. The Global Multidimensional Poverty Index is released by UNDP together with:
(a) World Bank (b) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (c) UNICEF (d) FAO
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank is tempting because it measures monetary poverty, but the global MPI, which counts overlapping deprivations in health, education and living standards, is produced by UNDP with the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI), so the answer is Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: UNDP/OPHI (official)

120. The Logistics Performance Index is published by:

121. Consider the following statements about HDI:
P. It was created by Pakistani economist Mahbub ul Haq with Amartya Sen's contributions.
Q. The first Human Development Report was published in 1990.
R. HDI ranges from 0 to 10.
Which of the above are correct?
(a) P and R only (b) Q and R only (c) P and Q only (d) P, Q and R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is statement R, which looks plausible but is wrong — HDI is a scale from 0 to 1, not 0 to 10. Mahbub ul Haq, with Amartya Sen's capability approach, created the HDI, and the first Human Development Report came out in 1990, so the answer is P and Q only.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report (official)

122. Arrange these reports' first publication years in correct chronological order:
P. World Development Report
Q. Human Development Report
R. Global Gender Gap Report
(a) P, R, Q (b) Q, P, R (c) R, Q, P (d) P, Q, R
Answer: (d)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap is placing the Human Development Report first because it is the most famous. The World Development Report began in 1978, the Human Development Report in 1990, and the Global Gender Gap Report in 2006, so the answer is P, Q, R.
Source: World Bank / UNDP / WEF (official)

123. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Global Gender Gap Report — World Economic Forum (b) World Happiness Report — UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (c) Corruption Perceptions Index — World Bank (d) Global Innovation Index — WIPO
Answer: (c)
Solution: This is an almost-correct-statement trap: the World Bank does publish governance indicators, which makes the pairing look right, but the Corruption Perceptions Index belongs to Transparency International. The other three pairs are correctly matched, so the answer is Corruption Perceptions Index — World Bank.
Source: Transparency International (official)

124. Consider the following statements about the Global Hunger Index:
P. It uses undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting and child mortality as indicators.
Q. It is published by the World Food Programme.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) Both P and Q (b) Q only (c) P only (d) Neither P nor Q
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is statement Q — the World Food Programme works on hunger, so it feels right, but GHI is published by Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe. Statement P correctly lists the four GHI component indicators, so the answer is P only.
Source: Global Hunger Index (official)

125. Which of the following indices is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit?
(a) Global Peace Index (b) Democracy Index (c) World Press Freedom Index (d) Human Development Index
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Global Peace Index is the tempting wrong option because democracy and peace sound related, but GPI comes from the Institute for Economics and Peace. The Democracy Index, classifying regimes as full democracies, flawed democracies, hybrid regimes and authoritarian regimes, is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit, so the answer is Democracy Index.
Source: Economist Intelligence Unit (official)
