## 2. Awards Linked to Books (Static Facts)

- **Nobel Prize in Literature**: Rabindranath Tagore was the first Indian (and first non-European) to win it, in **1913**, for *Gitanjali*.
- **Booker Prize winners of Indian origin**: Salman Rushdie (1981), Arundhati Roy (1997), Kiran Desai (2006), Aravind Adiga (2008), Geetanjali Shree won the **International Booker Prize 2022** for *Tomb of Sand* (translated by Daisy Rockwell) — the first Hindi-language work to win it.
- **Jnanpith Award**: India's highest literary honour; first awarded in 1965 to G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam) for *Odakkuzhal*.
- **Sahitya Akademi Award**: Given annually since 1955 for works in Indian languages.

## 3. The Examiner's Traps (How Questions Trick You)

1. **Similar-sounding authors**: *The Guide* is by **R. K. Narayan**, not R. K. Laxman (the cartoonist). *Malgudi Days* — Narayan again.
2. **Original language confusion**: *Gitanjali* was written in **Bengali**; Tagore himself translated it into English. *Tomb of Sand* was originally written in **Hindi** (*Ret Samadhi*).
3. **Award-year swaps**: Arundhati Roy won the Booker in **1997**, Kiran Desai in **2006** — examiners swap these years in options.
4. **Father–daughter confusion**: Kiran Desai is the daughter of **Anita Desai** (author of *Clear Light of Day*), who was shortlisted for the Booker but never won it.
5. **Autobiography mix-ups**: *Wings of Fire* = Kalam; *Playing It My Way* = Tendulkar; *My Experiments with Truth* = Gandhi. These three are swapped constantly.
6. **Negative questions**: "Which of the following is NOT written by Premchand?" — read the stem twice before answering.
7. **Translated works**: *Godan* and *Gaban* are Premchand's Hindi novels; do not confuse with Sarat Chandra's Bengali works like *Devdas*.

## 4. Quick-Recall One-Liners

- First Indian Nobel Laureate in Literature → **Rabindranath Tagore (1913)**.
- First Jnanpith Award → **G. Sankara Kurup (1965)**.
- First Hindi novel to win International Booker → ***Tomb of Sand* (2022)**.
- *Anandamath* (Bankim Chandra) contains the song **"Vande Mataram"**.
- *Madhushala* → **Harivansh Rai Bachchan**.
- *Midnight's Children* also won the **"Booker of Bookers"** (1993) and **"Best of the Booker"** (2008).
- *Panchatantra* → **Vishnu Sharma** (not Chanakya — a common trap).
- *A Suitable Boy* by Vikram Seth is among the longest single-volume novels in English.
