Card 1
Front: Who wrote the epic "Mahabharata" according to tradition?
Back: Ved Vyasa (also called Krishna Dvaipayana). He is also credited with composing the Puranas.

Card 2
Front: Which ancient Sanskrit play by Kalidasa tells the love story of King Dushyanta and Shakuntala?
Back: Abhijnanashakuntalam (The Recognition of Shakuntala).

Card 3
Front: Who authored "Arthashastra", the ancient treatise on statecraft and economics?
Back: Kautilya (Chanakya / Vishnugupta), advisor of Chandragupta Maurya.

Card 4
Front: "Meghaduta" (The Cloud Messenger) is a famous lyric poem by which classical Sanskrit poet?
Back: Kalidasa.

Card 5
Front: Which book by Jawaharlal Nehru was written as letters to his daughter Indira from prison?
Back: "Glimpses of World History". (Trap: "Letters from a Father to His Daughter" is a different, shorter collection — don't confuse the two.)

Card 6
Front: Who wrote "Discovery of India", and where was it written?
Back: Jawaharlal Nehru, written during his imprisonment at Ahmednagar Fort (1942–46).

Card 7
Front: "My Experiments with Truth" is the autobiography of which national leader?
Back: Mahatma Gandhi (originally written in Gujarati as "Satya na Prayogo").

Card 8
Front: Who wrote "Gitanjali", and what major honour did it bring?
Back: Rabindranath Tagore; he won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913 — the first Asian Nobel laureate.

Card 9
Front: "Godan" and "Gaban" are celebrated novels by which Hindi writer?
Back: Munshi Premchand.

Card 10
Front: Which book by Mulk Raj Anand depicts the life of a sweeper boy named Bakha?
Back: "Untouchable" (1935).

Card 11
Front: Who wrote "The Guide" and "Malgudi Days"?
Back: R. K. Narayan.

Card 12
Front: "Midnight's Children", which won the Booker of Bookers, is by which author?
Back: Salman Rushdie.

Card 13
Front: Which book won Arundhati Roy the Booker Prize in 1997?
Back: "The God of Small Things".

Card 14
Front: Who wrote "Anandamath", the novel that contains the song "Vande Mataram"?
Back: Bankim Chandra Chatterjee.

Card 15
Front: "Panchatantra", the famous collection of animal fables, is attributed to whom?
Back: Vishnu Sharma.

Card 16
Front: Mnemonic: How can you quickly remember that Kautilya wrote Arthashastra?
Back: "KAU-ARTH" — Kautilya + Arthashastra both start with the "Kau/Arth" sound of economics (Artha = wealth). Chanakya counted coins, so he wrote on Artha (economy).

Card 17
Front: Mnemonic: How to remember that Kalidasa wrote both "Meghaduta" and "Abhijnanashakuntalam"?
Back: "KALI's cloud carries a ring" — KALI-dasa → Megha (cloud) + Shakuntala's ring (the token of recognition in the play).

Card 18
Front: Examiner's trap: "India Wins Freedom" is often wrongly attributed to Nehru. Who actually wrote it?
Back: Maulana Abul Kalam Azad. Trap: Nehru wrote "Discovery of India" and "Glimpses of World History" — examiners swap the two leaders' books.

Card 19
Front: Examiner's trap: Both "Train to Pakistan" and "The Company of Women" are by the same author — who, and what is the common wrong guess?
Back: Khushwant Singh. Common wrong guess: Salman Rushdie or Vikram Seth, because all three are famous Indian English novelists.

Card 20
Front: Who wrote "A Suitable Boy", one of the longest novels published in a single volume?
Back: Vikram Seth (1993).
