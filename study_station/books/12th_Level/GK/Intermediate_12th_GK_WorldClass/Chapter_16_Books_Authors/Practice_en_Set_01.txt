1. Who wrote the famous novel "Pride and Prejudice"?
(a) Charlotte Brontë (b) Jane Austen (c) Emily Brontë (d) George Eliot
Answer: (b)
Solution: Charlotte Brontë wrote "Jane Eyre", which is the tempting confusion here, but "Pride and Prejudice" (1813) is the work of Jane Austen, so the answer is Jane Austen.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (Literature background)

2. "The God of Small Things", which won the Booker Prize in 1997, was written by whom?
(a) Kiran Desai (b) Jhumpa Lahiri (c) Arundhati Roy (d) Anita Desai
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kiran Desai also won the Booker (2006, "The Inheritance of Loss"), which confuses many candidates, but the 1997 Booker went to Arundhati Roy for "The God of Small Things", so the answer is Arundhati Roy.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Who is the author of the Indian epic "Ramcharitmanas"?
(a) Valmiki (b) Ved Vyasa (c) Kalidasa (d) Tulsidas
Answer: (d)
Solution: Valmiki wrote the Sanskrit "Ramayana", which is the classic trap; Tulsidas composed "Ramcharitmanas" in Awadhi in the 16th century, so the answer is Tulsidas.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Hindi

4. "Wings of Fire" is the autobiography of which Indian personality?
(a) Vikram Sarabhai (b) Homi Bhabha (c) C.V. Raman (d) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
Answer: (d)
Solution: All four are great Indian scientists, which makes this confusing, but "Wings of Fire" (1999) is the autobiography of former President and missile scientist A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, so the answer is A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Who wrote the play "Abhijnanashakuntalam"?
(a) Kalidasa (b) Bhasa (c) Shudraka (d) Banabhatta
Answer: (a)
Solution: Banabhatta wrote "Kadambari" and "Harshacharita", a frequent mix-up, but "Abhijnanashakuntalam" is the celebrated Sanskrit drama of Kalidasa, so the answer is Kalidasa.
Source: NCERT Class 12 (Sanskrit literature background)

6. "Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone" was written by which author?
(a) J.R.R. Tolkien (b) C.S. Lewis (c) Roald Dahl (d) J.K. Rowling
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tolkien ("The Lord of the Rings") and Lewis ("Narnia") are fantasy authors often confused here, but the Harry Potter series is by J.K. Rowling, so the answer is J.K. Rowling.

7. Who is the author of "Gitanjali", for which the Nobel Prize in Literature was awarded in 1913?
(a) Rabindranath Tagore (b) Bankim Chandra Chatterjee (c) Sarojini Naidu (d) Muhammad Iqbal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bankim Chandra wrote "Anandamath", a common confusion, but "Gitanjali" earned Rabindranath Tagore the 1913 Nobel Prize, the first for an Asian in literature, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: NCERT Class 8 History

8. "Malgudi Days" is a famous work of which Indian English writer?
(a) Mulk Raj Anand (b) Ruskin Bond (c) Raja Rao (d) R.K. Narayan
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mulk Raj Anand ("Untouchable") and Raja Rao ("Kanthapura") form the famous trio with Narayan, which traps candidates, but the fictional town of Malgudi belongs to R.K. Narayan, so the answer is R.K. Narayan.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Who wrote the book "Discovery of India"?
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) B.R. Ambedkar (d) Sardar Patel
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gandhi wrote "My Experiments with Truth", the tempting alternative, but "Discovery of India" was written by Jawaharlal Nehru during his imprisonment in Ahmednagar Fort (1944), so the answer is Jawaharlal Nehru.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History

10. "Mein Kampf" is the autobiographical book of which dictator?
(a) Hitler (b) Stalin (c) Mussolini (d) Napoleon
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mussolini is the near-confusion as a fellow fascist dictator, but "Mein Kampf" ("My Struggle") was written by Adolf Hitler while in prison in 1924, so the answer is Hitler.
Source: NCERT Class 9 History

11. Who authored the Hindi novel "Godaan"?
(a) Mahadevi Verma (b) Harivansh Rai Bachchan (c) Premchand (d) Jaishankar Prasad
Answer: (c)
Solution: Jaishankar Prasad wrote "Kamayani", which confuses some, but "Godaan" (1936), the story of the peasant Hori, is the masterpiece of Munshi Premchand, so the answer is Premchand.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Hindi

12. "A Brief History of Time" is a famous science book written by whom?
(a) Isaac Newton (b) Stephen Hawking (c) Albert Einstein (d) Carl Sagan
Answer: (b)
Solution: Carl Sagan wrote "Cosmos", a close alternative, but "A Brief History of Time" (1988) on cosmology was written by physicist Stephen Hawking, so the answer is Stephen Hawking.

13. Who wrote the ancient Sanskrit treatise on statecraft, the "Arthashastra"?
(a) Kautilya (b) Kalidasa (c) Panini (d) Patanjali
Answer: (a)
Solution: Panini wrote the grammar "Ashtadhyayi", the classic trap in this cluster, but the "Arthashastra" is attributed to Kautilya (Chanakya), so the answer is Kautilya.
Source: NCERT Class 6 History

21. Which of the following book–author pairs is correctly matched?
(a) "Midnight's Children" – Vikram Seth (b) "A Suitable Boy" – Salman Rushdie (c) "The Inheritance of Loss" – Kiran Desai (d) "The Namesake" – Arundhati Roy
Answer: (c)
Solution: The examiner swaps authors among famous Indian English novels: "Midnight's Children" is by Rushdie, "A Suitable Boy" by Vikram Seth, and "The Namesake" by Jhumpa Lahiri. Only the pairing of "The Inheritance of Loss" with Kiran Desai (Booker 2006) is correct, so the answer is "The Inheritance of Loss" – Kiran Desai.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Arrange these books in the correct chronological order of their first publication: P. "Gitanjali" (English edition), Q. "Godan", R. "Anandamath", S. "Discovery of India".
(a) R, P, Q, S (b) P, R, S, Q (c) R, Q, P, S (d) P, Q, R, S
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is placing "Gitanjali" before "Anandamath"; in fact "Anandamath" (1882) came first, then "Gitanjali" (1912), then "Godan" (1936), and finally "Discovery of India" (1946), so the answer is R, P, Q, S.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Which of the following statements about books and authors is NOT correct?
(a) "Harshacharita" was written by Banabhatta. (b) "Rajatarangini" was written by Kalidasa. (c) "Kamasutra" is attributed to Vatsyayana. (d) "Mrichchhakatika" was written by Shudraka.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Three statements are correct, and the trap is the almost-plausible classical name: "Rajatarangini", the chronicle of Kashmir's kings, was written by Kalhana, not Kalidasa, so the answer is "Rajatarangini" was written by Kalidasa.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History

24. Who wrote the book "India Wins Freedom", an account of the freedom struggle?

Answer: (c)
Solution: Ghaffar Khan is the tempting confusion as a fellow Congress leader from the frontier, but "India Wins Freedom" is the memoir of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, India's first Education Minister, so the answer is Maulana Abul Kalam Azad.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Which pair of author and autobiography is INCORRECTLY matched?

Solution: The trap is the similar Bollywood name: "An Unsuitable Boy" is the autobiography of filmmaker Karan Johar, not Shah Rukh Khan; the other three pairs are correct, so the answer is "An Unsuitable Boy" – Shah Rukh Khan.
