51. Who wrote the novel "The Guide", which was also adapted into a famous Hindi film?
(a) R. K. Narayan (b) Mulk Raj Anand (c) Raja Rao (d) Khushwant Singh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mulk Raj Anand wrote "Untouchable" and "Coolie", which tempts many students because both Anand and Narayan were pioneers of Indian English fiction. "The Guide" (1958) is by R. K. Narayan, set in the fictional town of Malgudi, and it won him the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1960, so the answer is R. K. Narayan.

Solution: Students often confuse it with Tagore's plays or his novel "Gora". "Gitanjali" (Song Offerings) is a collection of poems, originally in Bengali and translated into English by Tagore himself, so the answer is poems.
