126. Which of the following statements about the Booker Prize is correct?
(a) It was originally open only to American writers (b) It is awarded only for poetry collections (c) It was first awarded to V. S. Naipaul (d) It was first awarded in 1969 to P. H. Newby for "Something to Answer For"
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is the Naipaul claim — Naipaul did win the Booker, but in 1971 for "In a Free State", not the first award. The Booker Prize was established in 1969, and the first winner was P. H. Newby for "Something to Answer For", so the answer is that it was first awarded in 1969 to P. H. Newby.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Arrange these books in correct chronological order of first publication: 1. Anandamath 2. Gitanjali (English) 3. Godan 4. Untouchable
(a) 1, 2, 3, 4 (b) 2, 1, 3, 4 (c) 2, 1, 4, 3 (d) 1, 2, 4, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing Godan before Untouchable — both are 1930s works, but Untouchable (Mulk Raj Anand) came in 1935 and Godan (Premchand) in 1936. Anandamath (Bankim Chandra) is 1882 and English Gitanjali is 1912, so the correct order is 1, 2, 4, 3.
Source: PYQ-style

130. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Midnight's Children — Salman Rushdie (b) The Guide — R. K. Narayan (c) A Suitable Boy — Vikram Seth (d) The Inheritance of Loss — Arundhati Roy

131. The book "My Experiments with Truth" was originally written by Mahatma Gandhi in which language?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is English, since most readers know the book through its English translation. Gandhi wrote his autobiography originally in Gujarati, serialised in Navajivan; the English translation was done by Mahadev Desai, so the answer is Gujarati.
