# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Books & Authors

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from This Chapter

Books & Authors is one of the most reliable "free marks" areas in SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD), Banking (IBPS, SBI), Railway (NTPC, Group D) and State PCS prelims. The questions are almost always **direct one-liners**: "Who wrote X?" or "Which book is written by Y?" The difficulty does not come from depth — it comes from **confusion between similar names, similar titles, and books by the same author**.

Examiners repeatedly test these sub-areas:

- **Classics of Indian English writing** — *The Guide*, *God of Small Things*, *The Inheritance of Loss*, *Midnight's Children*, *A Suitable Boy*.
- **Autobiographies of famous Indians** — *Wings of Fire* (A.P.J. Abdul Kalam), *My Experiments with Truth* (Gandhi), *Playing It My Way* (Sachin Tendulkar), *An Autobiography / Toward Freedom* (Nehru).
- **Ancient & classical texts** — *Arthashastra* (Kautilya), *Indica* (Megasthenes), *Rajatarangini* (Kalhana), *Akbarnama / Ain-i-Akbari* (Abul Fazl).
- **Hindi literature** — works of Premchand (*Godan*, *Gaban*), Harivansh Rai Bachchan (*Madhushala*), Mahadevi Verma (*Yama*), Ramdhari Singh Dinkar (*Rashmirathi*, *Kurukshetra*).
- **Books by politicians & Nobel/Pulitzer winners** — *Discovery of India* (Nehru), *Gitanjali* (Tagore), *The Argumentative Indian* (Amartya Sen).
- **Award-linked books** — Booker, Jnanpith, Sahitya Akademi winners (asked especially when recently in news).

## 2. The Examiner's Traps (Trap Types)

| Trap Type | How It Works | Example Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Almost Correct** | Option is a real book by a *related* author | *The Guide* credited to Mulk Raj Anand instead of R.K. Narayan |
| **Same Author, Wrong Book** | All four options are by the same author; only one matches the clue | Nehru wrote both *Discovery of India* and *Glimpses of World History* |
| **Chronological Confusion** | Asking which book came first / was written during imprisonment | *Discovery of India* (written in jail, 1942–46) vs *An Autobiography* (1936) |
| **Similar Titles** | Two books with near-identical names by different authors | *My Life* (Bill Clinton) vs *My Life, My Mission* style titles |
| **Negative Phrasing** | "Which of the following is NOT written by…" | Students misread and pick the correct pair |
| **Language Swap** | Original language of the work asked | *Gitanjali* originally in Bengali, not English |
| **Real vs Pen Name** | Author's pen name vs real name | Munshi Premchand = Dhanpat Rai Srivastava; "Kautilya" = Chanakya/Vishnugupta |

## 3. Top 10 High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions with Solutions

**Q1. Who is the author of *The Guide*?**
(a) Mulk Raj Anand (b) R.K. Narayan (c) Raja Rao (d) R.K. Laxman
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *The Guide* (1958) is by R.K. Narayan; it won him the Sahitya Akademi Award. **Trap: Almost Correct** — Mulk Raj Anand and Raja Rao are the other two of the "big three" of early Indian English fiction, deliberately placed to confuse. R.K. Laxman was a cartoonist (Narayan's brother).
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q2. *Wings of Fire* is the autobiography of:**
(a) Vikram Sarabhai (b) Homi Bhabha (c) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (d) C.V. Raman
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** *Wings of Fire* (1999) is Kalam's autobiography, written with Arun Tiwari. **Trap: Same-field confusion** — all four are famous Indian scientists, so field knowledge alone won't save you; only the exact pairing will.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3. Which of the following books was written by Jawaharlal Nehru during his imprisonment?**
(a) *The Discovery of India* (b) *India Wins Freedom* (c) *My Experiments with Truth* (d) *Unhappy India*
**Answer: (a)**
**Solution:** *The Discovery of India* was written by Nehru in Ahmednagar Fort prison (1944). *India Wins Freedom* is by Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, *My Experiments with Truth* by Gandhi, *Unhappy India* by Lala Lajpat Rai. **Trap: Chronological Confusion + Almost Correct** — all four are freedom-era books by national leaders.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4. *Godan* and *Gaban* are famous novels written by:**
(a) Harivansh Rai Bachchan (b) Munshi Premchand (c) Ramdhari Singh Dinkar (d) Maithili Sharan Gupt
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Both are classics of Premchand (real name Dhanpat Rai Srivastava). **Trap: Same-language confusion** — all options are Hindi literary giants; Bachchan is a poet (*Madhushala*), Dinkar an epic poet (*Rashmirathi*).
**Source:** NCERT Class 10 Hindi / PYQ-style

**Q5. Which of the following is NOT written by Jawaharlal Nehru?**
(a) *Glimpses of World History* (b) *An Autobiography* (c) *The Discovery of India* (d) *Hind Swaraj*
**Answer: (d)**
**Solution:** *Hind Swaraj* (1909) is by Mahatma Gandhi. The other three are all Nehru's works. **Trap: Negative Phrasing** — in a hurry, students pick (a) or (c) because they "recognise" them as correct pairs. Always underline NOT/EXCEPT in the question.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6. *Arthashastra*, the ancient treatise on statecraft, was written by:**
(a) Kalhana (b) Banabhatta (c) Kautilya (d) Megasthenes
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** Kautilya (Chanakya/Vishnugupta), Chandragupta Maurya's minister, wrote the *Arthashastra*. **Trap: Almost Correct** — Megasthenes wrote *Indica* (about the same Mauryan period!), Kalhana wrote *Rajatarangini*, Banabhatta wrote *Harshacharita*. Same era, different books.
**Source:** NCERT Class 6/12 History / PYQ-style

**Q7. *The Inheritance of Loss*, winner of the Man Booker Prize (2006), was written by:**
(a) Arundhati Roy (b) Kiran Desai (c) Aravind Adiga (d) Anita Desai
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Kiran Desai won the Booker in 2006. **Trap: Family/Similar-name confusion** — Anita Desai is her mother, also a famous novelist; Arundhati Roy won the Booker for *The God of Small Things* (1997); Aravind Adiga for *The White Tiger* (2008). Examiners love this cluster.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8. *Madhushala* is a celebrated work of:**
(a) Mahadevi Verma (b) Suryakant Tripathi 'Nirala' (c) Harivansh Rai Bachchan (d) Jaishankar Prasad
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** *Madhushala* (1935) is Bachchan's most famous poetry collection. **Trap: Same-movement confusion** — all four belong to the Chhayavad era of Hindi poetry, so "Hindi poet" knowledge is insufficient; exact pairing is needed.
**Source:** NCERT Class 10 Hindi / PYQ-style

**Q9. *Playing It My Way* is the autobiography of which sportsperson?**
(a) Kapil Dev (b) Sunil Gavaskar (c) Sachin Tendulkar (d) M.S. Dhoni
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** Published in 2014, it is Tendulkar's autobiography (with Boria Majumdar). **Trap: Same-field confusion** — Kapil Dev wrote *By God's Decree*; Gavaskar wrote *Sunny Days*. Cricketer autobiographies are a recurring exam theme.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10. *Gitanjali*, for which Rabindranath Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature (1913), was originally written in:**
(a) English (b) Hindi (c) Bengali (d) Sanskrit
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** The original *Gitanjali* is in Bengali; Tagore himself translated it into English. **Trap: Language Swap** — because the Nobel was for the English version, many assume English is the original.
**Source:** NCERT Class 8/10 / PYQ-style

## 4. The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**The rule:** If you know the answer instantly, mark it in **15 seconds** and move on. If unsure, give it a maximum of **45 seconds** of elimination, then mark your best guess (if no negative marking) or skip.

**Applied to Q1 (*The Guide*):** This is a pure recall question. Either you know R.K. Narayan wrote it (15 seconds — mark and go) or you don't. Spending 2 minutes "thinking harder" will not produce the answer. If stuck, eliminate R.K. Laxman (cartoonist, not novelist) in 45 seconds, guess among the remaining three, and move on.

**Applied to Q5 (NOT written by Nehru):** This needs the full 45 seconds, not 15. Read the question twice and circle "NOT". Check each pair: *Glimpses* — Nehru ✓, *Autobiography* — Nehru ✓, *Discovery* — Nehru ✓, *Hind Swaraj* — Gandhi ✗. Mark (d). Rushing this in 15 seconds is exactly how students lose a mark they already knew.

**Strategy summary:**
- Direct "Who wrote X?" → 15 seconds, no overthinking.
- NOT/EXCEPT, match-the-list, or chronological questions → up to 45 seconds with active elimination.
- Never leave blanks where there is no negative marking; Books & Authors guesses between two plausible authors are 50% shots.

## 5. What to Prioritise in Revision

1. **Autobiographies** (Kalam, Tendulkar, Gandhi, Nehru) — asked most often, easiest to memorise.
2. **The Booker/Nobel cluster** — Roy, Kiran Desai, Adiga, Tagore — learn as a group with years.
3. **Ancient texts with authors** — *Arthashastra*, *Indica*, *Rajatarangini*, *Akbarnama*, *Harshacharita*, *Mrichchhakatika* (Shudraka).
4. **Hindi literature one-liners** — one signature work per major writer is enough for most exams.
5. **Pen names / real names** — Premchand, Kautilya, 'Nirala', 'Bachchan' — a favourite trap zone.
