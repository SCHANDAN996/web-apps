101. Who wrote the novel "The Guide", later adapted into a famous Hindi film?
(a) Mulk Raj Anand (b) R. K. Narayan (c) Raja Rao (d) Khushwant Singh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mulk Raj Anand wrote "Untouchable" and "Coolie", which tempts many students because both Anand and Narayan were pioneers of Indian English fiction. "The Guide" (1958) is by R. K. Narayan and won him the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1960, so the answer is R. K. Narayan.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English (Indian Writing in English context)

102. "Gitanjali", for which Rabindranath Tagore received the Nobel Prize in 1913, is a collection of what?
(a) Short stories (b) Poems (c) Plays (d) Essays
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students pick essays because Tagore wrote extensively on education and nationalism, but the Nobel was specifically for "Gitanjali" (Song Offerings), a collection of poems translated into English by Tagore himself, so the answer is poems.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

103. The book "Anandamath", which contains the song "Vande Mataram", was written by whom?
(a) Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar (b) Sarat Chandra Chatterjee (c) Bankim Chandra Chatterjee (d) Dinabandhu Mitra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sarat Chandra Chatterjee is the tempting wrong choice because of the shared surname and his fame for "Devdas" and "Parineeta". "Anandamath" (1882) was written by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee, so the answer is Bankim Chandra Chatterjee.
Source: NCERT Class 8 History (Our Pasts III)

104. Who is the author of the book "My Experiments with Truth"?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) B. R. Ambedkar (c) Sardar Patel (d) Mahatma Gandhi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nehru is a common trap because he wrote the autobiography "Toward Freedom" and "The Discovery of India". "My Experiments with Truth" is the autobiography of Mahatma Gandhi, originally written in Gujarati, so the answer is Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History III)

105. "The Discovery of India" was written by Jawaharlal Nehru while he was imprisoned in which jail?
(a) Cellular Jail, Andaman (b) Yerwada Jail (c) Naini Jail (d) Ahmednagar Fort Jail
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cellular Jail tempts students because it is the most famous colonial prison, but it housed revolutionaries like Savarkar, not Nehru. Nehru wrote "The Discovery of India" during his imprisonment at Ahmednagar Fort Jail (1942–45), so the answer is Ahmednagar Fort Jail.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History

106. Who wrote the Sanskrit play "Abhijnanashakuntalam"?
(a) Bhasa (b) Shudraka (c) Kalidasa (d) Vishakhadatta
Answer: (c)
Solution: Vishakhadatta is a trap because he wrote the political play "Mudrarakshasa", and Shudraka wrote "Mrichchhakatika". "Abhijnanashakuntalam" is the masterpiece of Kalidasa, so the answer is Kalidasa.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History I)

114. "Arthashastra", the ancient treatise on statecraft and economics, is attributed to whom?

121. Consider the following statements about the book "Hind Swaraj":
1. It was written by Mahatma Gandhi in 1909.
2. It was written during Gandhi's return sea voyage from London to South Africa.
3. It was originally written in English.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 3 is the examiner's trap — it sounds plausible because Gandhi wrote for English journals, but "Hind Swaraj" was originally written in Gujarati in 1909 aboard the ship Kildonan Castle during his voyage from London to South Africa, and he later translated it into English himself. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History III)

122. Match the following books with their authors:
Book — Author
1. Rajatarangini — P. Kalhana
2. Kadambari — Q. Banabhatta
3. Mrichchhakatika — R. Shudraka
4. Mudrarakshasa — S. Vishakhadatta
(a) 1-Q, 2-P, 3-S, 4-R (b) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R, 4-S (c) 1-P, 2-R, 3-Q, 4-S (d) 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R, 4-S
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap lies in swapping Kalhana and Banabhatta, since both wrote about kings — but Kalhana wrote "Rajatarangini" (history of Kashmir) while Banabhatta wrote "Kadambari" and "Harshacharita". Shudraka wrote "Mrichchhakatika" and Vishakhadatta wrote "Mudrarakshasa", so the answer is 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R, 4-S.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History I)

123. Arrange the following books in correct chronological order of their first publication:
1. The God of Small Things
2. Gitanjali (English edition)
3. Untouchable
4. Midnight's Children
(a) 2, 4, 3, 1 (b) 3, 2, 4, 1 (c) 2, 3, 4, 1 (d) 3, 2, 1, 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap is placing "Untouchable" before "Gitanjali" because Anand wrote about older social themes. The correct order is: Gitanjali (English, 1912), Untouchable (1935), Midnight's Children (1981), The God of Small Things (1997), so the answer is 2, 3, 4, 1.
Source: PYQ-style

124. Consider the following statements:
1. "The God of Small Things" won the Booker Prize in 1997.
2. Its author Arundhati Roy also wrote "The Ministry of Utmost Happiness".
3. Arundhati Roy won the Nobel Prize in Literature.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the almost-correct trap — students inflate a Booker Prize into a Nobel Prize; Arundhati Roy has never won the Nobel. Statements 1 and 2 are correct: she won the Booker in 1997 for "The God of Small Things" and published "The Ministry of Utmost Happiness" in 2017, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Consider the following pairs of autobiographies and their authors:
1. "Playing It My Way" — Sachin Tendulkar
2. "A Shot at History" — Abhinav Bindra
3. "The Race of My Life" — Milkha Singh
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming one of these similar sports memoirs is mismatched, since exams often swap Milkha Singh's book with "Flying Sikh" titles. All three pairs are correct: Tendulkar wrote "Playing It My Way" (2014), Bindra wrote "A Shot at History" (2011), and Milkha Singh's autobiography is "The Race of My Life" (2013), so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PYQ-style
