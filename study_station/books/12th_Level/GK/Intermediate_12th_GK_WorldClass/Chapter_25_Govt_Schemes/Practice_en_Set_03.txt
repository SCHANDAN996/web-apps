51. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the overdraft facility available to eligible account holders?
(a) Up to ₹10,000 (b) Up to ₹5,000 (c) Up to ₹15,000 (d) Up to ₹20,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many aspirants pick ₹5,000 because the scheme originally offered an overdraft of ₹5,000 at launch in 2014; the limit was later raised. The current overdraft facility for eligible PMJDY account holders is up to ₹10,000, so the answer is up to ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

52. The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G) provides financial assistance for house construction. What is the unit assistance in plain areas?
(a) ₹1.00 lakh (b) ₹1.50 lakh (c) ₹1.20 lakh (d) ₹1.30 lakh
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is ₹1.30 lakh, but that higher amount applies to hilly/difficult areas and IAP districts, not plains. In plain areas the unit assistance under PMAY-G is ₹1.20 lakh, so the answer is ₹1.20 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY provides a health cover of how much per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization?
(a) ₹5 lakh (b) ₹2 lakh (c) ₹3 lakh (d) ₹10 lakh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Aspirants often confuse PM-JAY's cover with the much smaller cover of the older Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana, which PM-JAY subsumed. PM-JAY provides a cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year, so the answer is ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

54. Which of the following correctly matches the scheme with its nodal ministry?
(a) PM-KISAN — Ministry of Rural Development (b) PM-KISAN — Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (c) Ayushman Bharat — Ministry of Rural Development (d) MGNREGA — Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is swapping ministries between two rural schemes: MGNREGA belongs to the Ministry of Rural Development, while PM-KISAN is run by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. Ayushman Bharat is under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, so the answer is PM-KISAN — Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.
Source: PYQ-style

55. Under PM-KISAN, eligible landholding farmer families receive income support of:
(a) ₹4,000 per year in two instalments (b) ₹6,000 per year in three instalments (c) ₹8,000 per year in four instalments (d) ₹10,000 per year in two instalments
Answer: (b)
Solution: The common error is remembering only the ₹2,000 instalment and miscomputing the annual total. PM-KISAN gives ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 each, so the answer is ₹6,000 per year in three instalments.
Source: PYQ-style

56. Arrange the following schemes in chronological order of their launch: 1. MGNREGA 2. Swachh Bharat Mission 3. PM Jan Dhan Yojana 4. Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
(a) 2, 1, 3, 4 (b) 1, 2, 3, 4 (c) 2, 3, 1, 4 (d) 1, 3, 2, 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming Swachh Bharat Mission came before PMJDY because both were launched in 2014; in fact PMJDY was launched on 28 August 2014 and Swachh Bharat Mission on 2 October 2014. MGNREGA (2005) is first and PM-JAY (September 2018) is last, so the correct sequence is 1, 3, 2, 4.
Source: PYQ-style

57. The Stand-Up India scheme facilitates bank loans for setting up greenfield enterprises to:
(a) Only women entrepreneurs of any category (b) All MSME units above ₹5 crore investment (c) Only SC entrepreneurs (d) SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option limits the scheme to women only, but the scheme mandates at least one SC/ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch. Stand-Up India covers SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs with loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore, so the answer is SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs.
Source: PYQ-style

58. Under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana, the 'Kishor' category of loans covers:
(a) ₹10 lakh to ₹20 lakh (b) Up to ₹50,000 (c) ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh (d) ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap lies in confusing the three Mudra categories: Shishu covers up to ₹50,000 and Tarun covers ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh. Kishor covers loans above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5 lakh, so the answer is ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

59. Which scheme aims to provide piped water supply (Har Ghar Jal) to all rural households?
(a) Jal Jeevan Mission (b) Atal Bhujal Yojana (c) Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (d) Jal Shakti Abhiyan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Similar names cause confusion: PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana is about irrigation ("Har Khet Ko Pani"), and Atal Bhujal Yojana concerns groundwater management. The Jal Jeevan Mission (2019) targets functional household tap connections to every rural household, so the answer is Jal Jeevan Mission.
Source: PYQ-style

60. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana primarily provides:
(a) Free electricity connections to BPL households (b) LPG connections to women from poor households (c) Subsidised solar lanterns to rural students (d) CNG stations in rural areas
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is mixing Ujjwala with Saubhagya, which is the scheme for household electrification. PM Ujjwala Yojana (launched 2016 from Ballia, UP) provides deposit-free LPG connections to adult women of poor households, so the answer is LPG connections to women from poor households.
Source: PYQ-style

61. The Saubhagya scheme (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana) is associated with:
(a) Crop insurance (b) Free LPG refills (c) Rural road connectivity (d) Universal household electrification
Answer: (d)
Solution: Aspirants often swap Saubhagya with Ujjwala because both were launched around the same period and target households. Saubhagya (2017) aims at universal household electrification, so the answer is universal household electrification.
Source: PYQ-style

62. Under the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), the maximum premium payable by farmers for Kharif food and oilseed crops is:
(a) 1.5% of the sum insured (b) 2% of the sum insured (c) 3% of the sum insured (d) 5% of the sum insured
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the 1.5% figure, which applies to Rabi crops, not Kharif. Under PMFBY farmers pay a maximum of 2% of the sum insured for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5% for commercial/horticultural crops, so the answer is 2% of the sum insured.
Source: PYQ-style

63. The 'Beti Bachao Beti Padhao' scheme was launched in 2015 from which place?
(a) Rohtak, Haryana (b) Karnal, Haryana (c) Gurugram, Haryana (d) Panipat, Haryana
Answer: (d)
Solution: All four options are Haryana cities, which is the deliberate trap — the state is correct in every option, so only the exact city matters. The scheme was launched by the Prime Minister on 22 January 2015 from Panipat, Haryana, so the answer is Panipat, Haryana.
Source: PYQ-style

64. Which of the following statements about the Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is correct?
(a) It can be opened for a girl child up to the age of 15 years (b) It matures after 15 years from opening (c) It can be opened for a girl child before she attains the age of 10 years (d) Only one account per family is permitted
Answer: (c)
Solution: The almost-correct trap is the age of 15 — deposits can be made for 15 years, but the account must be opened before the girl turns 10. Up to two accounts per family (one per girl child) are allowed, and maturity is 21 years from opening, so the answer is that it can be opened before the girl attains the age of 10 years.
Source: PYQ-style

65. The Atal Pension Yojana (APY) guarantees a minimum monthly pension ranging from:
(a) ₹500 to ₹3,000 (b) ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 (c) ₹2,000 to ₹10,000 (d) ₹3,000 to ₹15,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the lower slab ₹500–₹3,000, which sounds plausible but is not the APY structure. APY offers a guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000 or ₹5,000 after the age of 60, so the answer is ₹1,000 to ₹5,000.
Source: PYQ-style

67. Which scheme provides a cash incentive of ₹5,000 to pregnant women and lactating mothers for their first living child, as maternity benefit?
(a) Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (b) Janani Suraksha Yojana (c) Mission Shakti (d) ICDS
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Janani Suraksha Yojana, which is also a maternity scheme but focuses on promoting institutional delivery through cash assistance, not a fixed ₹5,000 maternity benefit. PM Matru Vandana Yojana provides ₹5,000 (in instalments) for the first living child, so the answer is Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana.

68. The 'Skill India' initiative's flagship scheme for short-term skill training of youth is:

72. Under the National Rural Livelihood Mission (DAY-NRLM), the primary institutional platform for poor rural women is:
(a) Gram Panchayats (b) Self Help Groups (SHGs) (c) Cooperatives (d) Urban Local Bodies
Answer: (b)

73. The 'Poshan Abhiyaan' (National Nutrition Mission) primarily targets:
(a) Mid-day meals in secondary schools (b) Food grain distribution at subsidised rates (c) Reduction of stunting, under-nutrition and anaemia among children, adolescents and women (d) Fortification of edible oil only
Answer: (c)

Poshan Abhiyaan (launched 2018 from Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan) targets reduction of stunting, under-nutrition, low birth weight and anaemia, so the answer is reduction of stunting, under-nutrition and anaemia among children, adolescents and women.

74. Which statement about the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is correct?
(a) It is an accident insurance scheme with ₹2 lakh cover for accidental death/full disability at a premium of ₹20 per year (b) It is a life insurance scheme offering ₹2 lakh cover on natural death (c) It is a health insurance scheme with ₹5 lakh hospitalization cover (d) It is a crop insurance scheme for small farmers
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the life insurance option, which describes PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) — a similar-name scheme launched the same day (9 May 2015). PMSBY is the accident insurance scheme offering ₹2 lakh for accidental death or full disability at an annual premium of ₹20, so the answer is the accident insurance scheme with ₹2 lakh cover at ₹20 per year.
Source: PYQ-style

75. The 'One Nation One Ration Card' scheme is associated with which ministry?
(a) Ministry of Home Affairs (b) Ministry of Rural Development (c) Ministry of Food Processing Industries (d) Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the Ministry of Food Processing Industries, whose similar-sounding name misleads aspirants; it deals with food industries, not the Public Distribution System. One Nation One Ration Card enables portability of PDS benefits and is run by the Department of Food and Public Distribution under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, so the answer is Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution.
Source: PYQ-style
