Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the overdraft facility available to eligible account holders?
(a) Up to ₹15,000 (b) Up to ₹5,000 (c) Up to ₹10,000 (d) Up to ₹20,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many aspirants pick ₹5,000 because the overdraft began at ₹5,000 when the scheme launched in 2014; the limit was later raised. Eligible PMJDY account holders can now avail an overdraft of up to ₹10,000, so the answer is up to ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Which of the following statements about the Ayushman Bharat – PM Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is correct?
(a) It is an insurance scheme only for farmers (b) It provides ₹10 lakh per individual per year for all treatments including OPD (c) It covers only government hospital employees (d) It provides a health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation
Answer: (d)
Solution: The ₹10 lakh per individual claim is the classic trap — the cover is ₹5 lakh per family (not per individual) and it does not cover routine OPD. PM-JAY gives a health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation, so the answer is ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation.
Source: PYQ-style

78. The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) provides income support of how much per year to eligible farmer families?
(a) ₹6,000 in three equal instalments (b) ₹8,000 in four instalments (c) ₹4,000 in two instalments (d) ₹12,000 in a single instalment
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is ₹4,000, which confuses PM-KISAN with some state schemes. PM-KISAN gives ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 each, paid directly into bank accounts, so the answer is ₹6,000 in three equal instalments.
Source: PYQ-style

79. Which ministry implements the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)?
(a) Ministry of Rural Development (b) Ministry of Labour and Employment (c) Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (d) Ministry of Panchayati Raj
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Labour and Employment ministry is the tempting wrong choice because the scheme guarantees employment, but MGNREGA is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development, so the answer is the Ministry of Rural Development.
Source: PYQ-style

80. Under MGNREGA, the guaranteed wage employment per household per financial year is:
(a) 50 days (b) 75 days (c) 100 days (d) 150 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The figure 150 days tempts because additional days are allowed in notified drought or calamity-hit areas, but the statutory guarantee is 100 days of wage employment per rural household per year, so the answer is 100 days.
Source: PYQ-style

81. The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G) replaced which earlier rural housing scheme?
(a) Indira Awaas Yojana (b) Rajiv Awas Yojana (c) Valmiki Ambedkar Awas Yojana (d) Jawahar Rozgar Yojana
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rajiv Awas Yojana tempts because of the similar name, but it was an urban slum-focused scheme. PMAY-G (launched 2016) restructured and replaced the Indira Awaas Yojana for rural housing, so the answer is Indira Awaas Yojana.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Arrange the following schemes in chronological order of launch: 1. Swachh Bharat Mission 2. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana 3. Make in India 4. Digital India
(a) 2, 1, 3, 4 (b) 1, 2, 3, 4 (c) 2, 3, 1, 4 (d) 1, 3, 2, 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming Swachh Bharat (2 October 2014) came first because it is the most famous. The actual order is Jan Dhan (28 August 2014), Make in India (25 September 2014), Swachh Bharat (2 October 2014), Digital India (1 July 2015), so the answer is 2, 3, 1, 4.
Source: PYQ-style

83. The Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, a small savings scheme for the girl child, was launched under which campaign?
(a) Swachh Bharat Mission (b) Stand Up India (c) Skill India (d) Beti Bachao Beti Padhao
Answer: (d)
Solution: Stand Up India tempts because it also involves bank accounts and women beneficiaries, but it is an entrepreneurship loan scheme. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana was launched in January 2015 as part of the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao campaign, so the answer is Beti Bachao Beti Padhao.
Source: PYQ-style

84. Under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana, the 'Kishor' category of loans covers:
(a) Loans up to ₹50,000 (b) Loans above ₹10 lakh and up to ₹20 lakh (c) Loans above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5 lakh (d) Loans above ₹5 lakh and up to ₹10 lakh
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is mixing up the three categories: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishor (₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh), Tarun (₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh). The Kishor category covers loans above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5 lakh, so the answer is loans above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

85. Which of the following statements about the Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is NOT correct?
(a) It is open to Indian citizens aged 18 to 40 years (b) It guarantees a fixed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 after age 60 (c) It is administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (d) It provides a fixed monthly pension immediately upon enrolment
Answer: (d)
Solution: The other three statements are all true, which makes this negative-worded question tricky. The pension under APY starts only after the subscriber turns 60, not on enrolment, so the statement that is NOT correct is that it provides a fixed monthly pension immediately upon enrolment.
Source: PYQ-style

86. The PM Vishwakarma scheme, launched in 2023, primarily supports:
(a) Traditional artisans and craftspeople working with hands and tools (b) Large software exporters (c) Scheduled commercial banks (d) Multinational manufacturing companies
Answer: (a)
Solution: The word "Vishwakarma" tempts some aspirants to link it with big industry or infrastructure companies. PM Vishwakarma supports traditional artisans and craftspeople (such as carpenters, blacksmiths, potters and tailors) with recognition, skill training, toolkit incentives and collateral-free credit, so the answer is traditional artisans and craftspeople working with hands and tools.
Source: PYQ-style

87. The 'Stand Up India' scheme facilitates bank loans to:
(a) All farmers for crop loans (b) Government employees for housing (c) Students for higher education abroad (d) SC/ST and women entrepreneurs for greenfield enterprises
Answer: (d)
Solution: The scheme's name tempts aspirants into thinking it is for all youth or all small businesses. Stand Up India mandates bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and one woman borrower per bank branch for greenfield enterprises, so the answer is SC/ST and women entrepreneurs for greenfield enterprises.
Source: PYQ-style

88. Which scheme aims to provide piped water supply (Har Ghar Jal) to all rural households?
(a) Namami Gange (b) Jal Jeevan Mission (c) Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (d) Atal Bhujal Yojana
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Krishi Sinchayee Yojana tempts because it also deals with water, but it focuses on irrigation ("Per Drop More Crop"). The Jal Jeevan Mission (2019) aims at functional household tap connections for every rural household, so the answer is Jal Jeevan Mission.
Source: PYQ-style

89. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana primarily provides:
(a) Free electricity connections to BPL households (b) LPG connections to women from poor households (c) Solar lanterns to rural students (d) Subsidised piped natural gas to urban homes
Answer: (b)
Solution: Free electricity connections tempt because of the similarly named Saubhagya scheme. Ujjwala (2016) provides deposit-free LPG connections to adult women of poor households, so the answer is LPG connections to women from poor households.
Source: PYQ-style

90. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gram Jyoti Yojana — LPG distribution (b) Poshan Abhiyaan — rural roads (c) Saubhagya — household electrification (d) PM Gram Sadak Yojana — nutrition
Answer: (c)
Solution: The other three options deliberately swap themes — LPG, roads and nutrition — to catch rote learners. Saubhagya (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana, 2017) is the household electrification scheme, so the correctly matched pair is Saubhagya — household electrification.

91. The National Health Mission covers which two sub-missions?
(a) Ayushman Bharat and PM-JAY (b) National Rural Health Mission and National Urban Health Mission (c) Swachh Bharat and Jal Jeevan (d) ICDS and Mid-Day Meal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ayushman Bharat tempts because it is the better-known health initiative, but it is a separate programme. The National Health Mission comprises the National Rural Health Mission and the National Urban Health Mission, so the answer is National Rural Health Mission and National Urban Health Mission.

92. Under the PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana, free foodgrains were distributed at the rate of:
(a) 5 kg per person per month (b) 10 kg per family per month (c) 2 kg per person per week (d) 35 kg per family per month
Answer: (a)

93. The 'One Nation One Ration Card' scheme is associated with which benefit?
(a) Replacement of ration cards by voter ID cards (b) A single ration shop for the whole country (c) Free ration only in the home district (d) Portability of foodgrain entitlements under the National Food Security Act across states
Answer: (d)
Solution: The name tempts aspirants into thinking it means one shop or one card replacing all documents. The scheme enables migrant beneficiaries to draw their NFSA foodgrain entitlement from any fair price shop in the country, so the answer is portability of foodgrain entitlements under the National Food Security Act across states.
Source: PYQ-style

94. Which scheme provides institutional training and stipends to apprentices in industry?
(a) Skill India Digital only (b) National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (c) Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (d) Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana
Answer: (b)
Solution: PMKVY tempts because it is the flagship skilling scheme, but it offers short-term training, not industry apprenticeships with stipend sharing. The National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme supports apprenticeships with government sharing of the stipend, so the answer is the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme.

95. The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is related to:
(a) Crop insurance (b) Life insurance of farmers (c) Tractor subsidies (d) Soil health cards
Answer: (a)
Solution: Life insurance tempts because of the word "Bima", but PMFBY is a crop insurance scheme where farmers pay a low premium (2% for kharif, 1.5% for rabi crops) and the rest is shared by governments, so the answer is crop insurance.
Source: PYQ-style

96. Which of the following statements about the National Food Security Act, 2013 is correct?
(a) It applies only to urban areas (b) It entitles eligible households to subsidised foodgrains under the Targeted Public Distribution System (c) It guarantees free foodgrains to every citizen without any eligibility criteria (d) It was enacted in 2005 along with MGNREGA
Answer: (b)
Solution: The year 2005 tempts because MGNREGA was passed then, but NFSA came in 2013. The Act legally entitles eligible households (priority and Antyodaya categories) to subsidised foodgrains through the TPDS, so the answer is that it entitles eligible households to subsidised foodgrains under the Targeted Public Distribution System.
Source: PYQ-style

97. The 'PM SVANidhi' scheme provides collateral-free working capital loans to:
(a) Cooperative banks (b) Street vendors (c) Software companies (d) Large industries
Answer: (b)
Solution: The word "Nidhi" tempts aspirants to link it with general MSME credit, but PM SVANidhi (2020) specifically gives collateral-free working capital loans (initially up to ₹10,000) to street vendors, so the answer is street vendors.
Source: PYQ-style

98. Which mission focuses on rejuvenation and cleaning of the river Ganga?
(a) Jal Shakti Abhiyan (b) Swachh Bharat Mission (c) Namami Gange (d) AMRUT
Answer: (c)

99. The 'Beti Bachao Beti Padhao' scheme was launched in 2015 from which place?
(a) Panipat, Haryana (b) Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh (c) Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh (d) Jaipur, Rajasthan
Answer: (a)

100. Which of the following schemes is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs?
(a) PM-KISAN (b) PM Awas Yojana – Gramin (c) MGNREGA (d) PM Awas Yojana – Urban
Answer: (d)
Solution: PMAY-Gramin tempts because of the identical name, but it is run by the Ministry of Rural Development — a classic similar-name trap. PMAY-Urban is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, so the answer is PM Awas Yojana – Urban.
