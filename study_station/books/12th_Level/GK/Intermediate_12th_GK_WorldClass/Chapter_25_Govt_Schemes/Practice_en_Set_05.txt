101. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the overdraft facility available to eligible account holders?
(a) Up to ₹10,000 (b) Up to ₹20,000 (c) Up to ₹15,000 (d) Up to ₹5,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many aspirants pick ₹5,000 because the scheme originally offered an overdraft of ₹5,000 at launch in 2014; the limit was later raised. The current overdraft facility for eligible PMJDY account holders is up to ₹10,000, so the answer is ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

102. The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G) aims to provide pucca houses with basic amenities to which group?
(a) All urban slum dwellers only (b) Only farmers owning more than 2 hectares (c) Only government employees in rural areas (d) Houseless households and those living in kutcha/dilapidated houses in rural areas
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the first option, which describes the urban component (PMAY-U). PMAY-G targets rural households that are houseless or living in kutcha and dilapidated houses, identified through SECC 2011 data, so the answer is houseless households and those in kutcha houses in rural areas.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY provides a health cover of how much per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation?
(a) ₹1 lakh (b) ₹2 lakh (c) ₹5 lakh (d) ₹10 lakh
Answer: (c)
Solution: ₹1 lakh is tempting because some state schemes and insurance products use that figure, but PM-JAY, the world's largest government-funded health assurance scheme, gives ₹5 lakh per family per year, so the answer is ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Which of the following schemes provides a legal employment guarantee of 100 days to rural households?
(a) Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (b) Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (c) Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (d) Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana
Answer: (c)
Solution: The other options are skill or credit schemes with no employment guarantee. MGNREGS (enacted 2005) legally guarantees 100 days of wage employment per rural household per year, so the answer is the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Economics

105. Under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN), eligible farmer families receive income support of:
(a) ₹12,000 per year in four instalments (b) ₹10,000 per year in two instalments (c) ₹6,000 per year in three instalments (d) ₹4,000 per year in one instalment
Answer: (c)
Solution: ₹12,000 is a common confusion with annual totals of other benefits, but PM-KISAN pays ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 directly into bank accounts, so the answer is ₹6,000 per year in three instalments.
Source: PYQ-style

106. The 'Beti Bachao Beti Padhao' scheme is jointly implemented by which ministries?
(a) Ministry of Women and Child Development, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Ministry of Education (formerly HRD) (b) Ministry of Health, Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Home Affairs (c) Ministry of Rural Development, Ministry of Labour, Ministry of Social Justice (d) Ministry of Women and Child Development alone
Answer: (a)
Solution: The last option is tempting because WCD is the nodal ministry, but the scheme is a tri-ministerial initiative of WCD, Health and Family Welfare, and Education, so the answer is the three ministries together.
Source: PYQ-style

107. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) primarily provides:
(a) LPG connections to women from poor households (b) Free electricity connections to BPL households (c) Free piped water connections to rural homes (d) Subsidised solar lanterns to students
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first option describes Saubhagya (electricity) and the third describes Jal Jeevan Mission — classic similar-scheme traps. PMUY, launched in 2016, provides deposit-free LPG connections to adult women of poor households, so the answer is LPG connections to women from poor households.
Source: PYQ-style

108. Which scheme was launched to provide piped drinking water ('Har Ghar Jal') to every rural household?
(a) Swachh Bharat Mission (b) Jal Jeevan Mission (c) Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (d) Namami Gange Programme
Answer: (b)
Solution: Krishi Sinchayee is about irrigation ('Per Drop More Crop'), a frequent confusion. The Jal Jeevan Mission (2019) aims at functional household tap connections for every rural household, so the answer is Jal Jeevan Mission.
Source: PYQ-style

109. Under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana, the 'Kishore' category loan covers:
(a) Up to ₹50,000 (b) Above ₹5 lakh and up to ₹10 lakh (c) Above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5 lakh (d) Above ₹10 lakh and up to ₹20 lakh
Answer: (c)
Solution: The three categories are Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh), and Tarun (₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh). Mixing up Shishu and Kishore is the classic trap, so the answer is above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

110. The 'Stand Up India' scheme facilitates bank loans to:
(a) All salaried employees for housing (b) SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs for setting up greenfield enterprises (c) Only large corporations for expansion (d) Students for foreign education
Answer: (b)
Solution: The scheme is often confused with Mudra, but Stand Up India specifically mandates at least one SC/ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch for greenfield enterprises between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore, so the answer is SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs.
Source: PYQ-style

111. The Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) was launched on which date?
(a) 15 August 2014 (b) 26 January 2015 (c) 2 October 2014 (d) 5 June 2015
Answer: (c)
Solution: 15 August 2014 is tempting because the mission was announced in the Prime Minister's Independence Day speech, but it was formally launched on Gandhi Jayanti, 2 October 2014, so the answer is 2 October 2014.
Source: PYQ-style

112. The 'Poshan Abhiyaan' (National Nutrition Mission) primarily targets:
(a) Only school-going children in cities (b) Reduction of stunting, undernutrition, anaemia and low birth weight, especially among children, adolescent girls, pregnant women and lactating mothers (c) Distribution of free fertilizers to farmers (d) Mid-day meals in all private schools
Answer: (b)
Solution: The mid-day meal option confuses it with the Mid-Day Meal Scheme (PM POSHAN). Poshan Abhiyaan, launched in 2018, is an overarching nutrition programme targeting stunting, undernutrition, anaemia and low birth weight, so the answer is the nutrition-focused option.
Source: PYQ-style

113. The Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is open to Indian citizens in which age group?
(a) 18 to 40 years (b) 21 to 50 years (c) 25 to 60 years (d) 18 to 60 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: 18 to 60 is tempting because many insurance schemes use that band, but APY requires entry between 18 and 40 years so that contributions can build a pension payable from age 60, so the answer is 18 to 40 years.
Source: PYQ-style

114. Under the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY), the annual premium and accident death cover are respectively:
(a) ₹12 per year; ₹1 lakh (b) ₹436 per year; ₹5 lakh (c) ₹330 per year; ₹2 lakh (d) ₹20 per year; ₹2 lakh
Answer: (d)
Solution: ₹330 is the annual premium of the life insurance scheme PMJJBY — a classic similar-scheme trap. PMSBY is the accident insurance scheme with a ₹20 annual premium and ₹2 lakh cover for accidental death, so the answer is ₹20 per year and ₹2 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

115. The 'Digital India' programme was launched in which year?
(a) 2014 (b) 2015 (c) 2016 (d) 2017
Answer: (b)
Solution: 2014 is tempting because the government took office then and many schemes date from 2014, but Digital India was launched on 1 July 2015, so the answer is 2015.

116. Which scheme was extended as 'Ujjwala 2.0' with additional features like a free first refill and stove?
(a) PMUY only for urban areas (b) Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana 2.0 (c) PAHAL scheme (d) Give It Up campaign
Answer: (b)
Solution: PAHAL is the LPG subsidy direct-transfer scheme, not a connection scheme — a frequent confusion. Ujjwala 2.0 (2021) extended PMUY with migrant-friendly enrolment, free first refill and stove, so the answer is Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana 2.0.
Source: PYQ-style

117. The 'Skill India' mission's flagship scheme for short-term skill training of youth is:
(a) National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (b) Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan (c) Deen Dayal Antyodaya Yojana (d) Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana
Answer: (d)
Solution: The apprenticeship scheme is related but not the flagship short-term training programme. PMKVY, run by the Ministry of Skill Development, is the flagship scheme for industry-relevant short-term skill training, so the answer is Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana.
Source: PYQ-style

118. The 'Saubhagya' scheme (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana) is related to:
(a) Solar pumps for farmers (b) Free LPG to all households (c) Universal household electrification (d) LED bulb distribution
Answer: (c)
Solution: LED bulb distribution is the UJALA scheme — a similar-name trap. Saubhagya (2017) aimed at providing electricity connections to all willing households, so the answer is universal household electrification.
Source: PYQ-style

119. The 'PM SVANidhi' scheme provides collateral-free working capital loans to:
(a) Small farmers (b) Street vendors (c) Self-help groups in villages (d) Handloom weavers only
Answer: (b)
Solution: Self-help groups are covered under DAY-NRLM, a common mix-up. PM SVANidhi (2020) gives street vendors collateral-free working capital loans initially up to ₹10,000, so the answer is street vendors.
Source: PYQ-style

120. The 'One Nation One Ration Card' scheme enables:
(a) Free ration to all citizens without any card (b) Portability of ration card benefits across states under NFSA (c) A single ration shop in every district (d) Cash transfer instead of foodgrains
Answer: (b)
Solution: The cash transfer option confuses it with direct benefit transfer pilots. The scheme lets NFSA beneficiaries lift their entitled foodgrains from any fair price shop in the country, so the answer is portability of ration card benefits across states.
Source: PYQ-style

121. Consider the following statements about PM-JAY:
1. It is fully funded by the central government for all states.
2. It covers pre- and post-hospitalisation expenses.
3. It subsumed the earlier Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY).
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 1 is the trap: PM-JAY's cost is shared between Centre and states (generally 60:40), not fully centrally funded. Statements 2 and 3 are correct — it covers pre/post-hospitalisation and subsumed RSBY, so the answer is 2 and 3 only.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Arrange the following schemes in chronological order of their launch:
1. MGNREGA
2. Swachh Bharat Mission
3. Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY
4. PM Jan Dhan Yojana
(a) 1, 2, 4, 3 (b) 1, 2, 3, 4 (c) 4, 1, 2, 3 (d) 1, 4, 2, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming Swachh Bharat (announced on 15 August 2014) predates Jan Dhan; in fact PMJDY was launched on 28 August 2014 and SBM on 2 October 2014. The correct chronology is MGNREGA (2005), PMJDY (August 2014), SBM (October 2014), PM-JAY (September 2018), so the answer is 1, 4, 2, 3.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Which of the following statements about the National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM / Aajeevika) is NOT correct?
(a) It provides a legal guarantee of 100 days of wage employment (b) It is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development (c) It promotes self-help groups (SHGs) of rural poor women (d) It supports community institutions and livelihoods promotion
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is merging two rural schemes: the 100-day legal employment guarantee belongs to MGNREGA, not NRLM. NRLM focuses on SHG mobilisation and livelihoods under the Ministry of Rural Development, so the statement that is NOT correct is the 100-day guarantee one.
Source: PYQ-style

124. Consider the following pairs:
1. PM-KUSUM — Solar pumps and solarisation of agriculture
2. PM Fasal Bima Yojana — Crop insurance
3. e-NAM — National electronic agriculture market
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Aspirants often doubt pair 3, confusing e-NAM with a banking platform, but e-NAM is indeed the National Agriculture Market portal integrating mandis. All three pairs are correctly matched, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Consider the following statements about the Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana:
1. The account can be opened in the name of a girl child below 10 years of age.
2. It was launched under the 'Beti Bachao Beti Padhao' campaign.
3. A maximum of four accounts per family is permitted.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: a maximum of two accounts per family (one per girl child, with limited exceptions for twins/triplets) is allowed, not four. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: PYQ-style
