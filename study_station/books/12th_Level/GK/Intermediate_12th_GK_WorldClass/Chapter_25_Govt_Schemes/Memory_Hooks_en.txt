# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Government Schemes & Initiatives

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 1 — PMJDY (Jan Dhan Yojana)

**"Jan Dhan = Janta ka Dhan in the Bank"**

📅 **2014** — the FIRST big scheme of the Modi government.
Hook: **"14 = Dhan ka door"** — 2014 opened the door of bank accounts for every household.
Trick: Zero-balance account + RuPay debit card + ₹1 lakh accident insurance (later ₹2 lakh for accounts opened after 28.08.2018).

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 2 — PMAY (Housing for All)

**"Awas = A Roof Over All"**

📅 **2015** — Urban; Gramin (rural) version restructured in **2016**.
Hook: **"15 sheher, 16 gaon"** — 2015 for cities, 2016 for villages.
Trick: Target year was **2022** ("Housing for All by 2022"). If the option says 2024 or 2030 — trap!

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 3 — Skill India / PMKVY

**"Kaushal = Kaam ka Skill"**

📅 **2015** — launched on **World Youth Skills Day (15 July)**.
Hook: **"15 July, 15 saal ka skill"** — both the year and the date carry 15.
Trick: Ministry = **Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (MSDE)** — not Labour Ministry (classic trap).

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 4 — Swachh Bharat Mission

**"Gandhi's Glasses = Clean India"**

📅 **2 October 2014** — Gandhi Jayanti.
Hook: The logo is **Gandhi's spectacles** — "Bapu ki nazar, saaf sheher."
Trick: Target year **2019** = 150th birth anniversary of Gandhi. Examiner loves swapping 2019 ↔ 2022.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 5 — Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

**"Ayushman = 5 Lakh ka Aashirwad"**

📅 **2018** — launched from **Ranchi, Jharkhand** on **23 September**.
Hook: **"5-5-5"** — ₹5 lakh per family per year, world's largest health assurance scheme.
Trick: It's **insurance/assurance for hospitalisation (secondary & tertiary)** — NOT free OPD, NOT a hospital-building scheme (that's the Health & Wellness Centres pillar).

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 6 — Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

**"Ujjwala = Ujala from LPG, not Lantern"**

📅 **1 May 2016** — launched from **Ballia, Uttar Pradesh**.
Hook: **"May Day = Smoke-Free Day"** — Labour Day gift of smokeless kitchens to BPL women.
Trick: Connection is given in the name of the **woman** of the household — examiners swap it to "head of family (male)".

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 7 — Stand-Up India

**"Stand Up = SC/ST + Women Stand Up as Entrepreneurs"**

📅 **2016** — bank loans **₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore**.
Hook: **"10 to 100"** — 10 lakh to 100 lakh (₹1 crore).
Trick: For **greenfield (first-time)** enterprises of SC/ST and women — not for everyone (trap: "all citizens above 18").

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 8 — Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

**"MUDRA = Micro Units' Dhan, Right Away"**

📅 **2015** — three products: **Shishu (₹50,000), Kishore (₹5 lakh), Tarun (₹10 lakh)**.
Hook: **"Baby → Teen → Young Man"** — Shishu (baby) smallest loan, Kishore (teenager) middle, Tarun (youth) biggest.
Trick: Order trap! Examiners shuffle Shishu/Kishore/Tarun amounts. Remember the growing child.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 9 — Beti Bachao Beti Padhao

**"Beti = Bachao + Padhao (Save + Educate)"**

📅 **22 January 2015** — launched from **Panipat, Haryana**.
Hook: **"Panipat ki Beti"** — Haryana had the worst child sex ratio, so it started there.
Trick: Ministries = **Women & Child Development + Health + HRD (Education)** — a tri-ministry scheme (trap: "only WCD Ministry").

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 10 — Digital India

**"Digital India = 1 July 2015, Digital Bharat"**

📅 **1 July 2015**.
Hook: **"1-7-15: One Nation, Seven Days a Week, Online"**.
Trick: Three pillars — **Digital Infrastructure, Digital Services, Digital Literacy**. Ministry = **MeitY** (Electronics & IT), not Information & Broadcasting.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 11 — Make in India

**"Make in India = Lion of 25 September 2014"**

📅 **25 September 2014** — logo is a **lion made of cogs/gears**.
Hook: **"Gear-Lion roars in 14"**.
Trick: Logo animal = **Lion** (examiners offer tiger/elephant as bait). Focus: manufacturing + FDI, 25 sectors originally.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 12 — PM-KISAN

**"KISAN = ₹6,000 ka Salaana Salaam"**

📅 **2019** (announced in Interim Budget 2019; effective retrospectively from **1 December 2018**).
Hook: **"2-2-2"** — ₹6,000 per year in **three instalments of ₹2,000**.
Trick: Paid **directly into bank accounts (DBT)** to landholding farmer families — trap: "₹6,000 per month" (it's per YEAR).

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 13 — Jal Jeevan Mission

**"Jal Jeevan = Har Ghar Jal by 2024"**

📅 **2019** — announced on **15 August 2019**.
Hook: **"Nal se Jal, 24 ka Chaal"** — tap water to every rural household, target 2024.
Trick: It's about **tap (piped) water connections**, not just wells/handpumps. Ministry = **Jal Shakti**.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 14 — POSHAN Abhiyaan

**"POSHAN = PM's Overarching Scheme for Holistic Nutrition"**

📅 **2018** — launched from **Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan** on **8 March (Women's Day)**.
Hook: **"Jhunjhunu se Poshan ki Shuruaat"**.
Trick: Target = reduce **stunting, undernutrition, anaemia**; National Nutrition Mission is its other name. Ministry = **WCD**.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 15 — Year Ladder (Master Hook)

**"14-15-16-18-19: The Scheme Staircase"**

- **2014**: Jan Dhan, Swachh Bharat, Make in India
- **2015**: PMAY(U), Skill India, Mudra, Digital India, BBBP
- **2016**: Ujjwala, Stand-Up India, PMAY(G)
- **2018**: Ayushman Bharat, POSHAN Abhiyaan
- **2019**: PM-KISAN, Jal Jeevan Mission

Hook: **"14 mein Dhan-Swachh-Make, 15 mein Skill-Mudra-Digital, 16 mein Ujjwala-StandUp, 18 mein Ayushman-POSHAN, 19 mein KISAN-Jal"** — chant it like a train rhythm.

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

**1. The "Exact Amount/Beneficiary Number" Trap:**
If a question asks for a precise figure you haven't memorised (e.g., "How many crore accounts under PMJDY as of a given year?") and all four options are close numbers (11.2 / 12.5 / 13.1 / 14.8 crore) — **skip immediately**. These figures change yearly and guessing between near-identical numbers is a coin toss that costs negative marks.

**2. The "Which Ministry Nodal?" Question with Twin Ministries:**
If you can recall the scheme but are confused between two similar ministries (e.g., Rural Development vs. Housing & Urban Affairs for PMAY; MeitY vs. I&B for Digital India), and you cannot firmly place it — **skip after 45 seconds**. Don't burn 2 minutes re-reading; the confusion won't resolve itself.

**3. The "Launch Date + Place" Combo Question:**
If a question demands BOTH the exact date AND launch city (e.g., "Ujjwala was launched on ___ from ___"), and you remember only one of the two — **skip**. Combo questions are designed so that partial knowledge still leads to the wrong option; examiners pair your known fact with a wrong city in three of the four options.

**Golden Rule:** In scheme questions, attempt only if you can verify at least **two anchors** (year + ministry, or year + place, or amount + beneficiary). One anchor = trap territory.
