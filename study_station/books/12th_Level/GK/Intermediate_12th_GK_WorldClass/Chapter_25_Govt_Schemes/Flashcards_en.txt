Card 1
Front: Which scheme gives ₹6,000 per year to small and marginal farmer families, and in how many instalments?
Back: PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) — ₹6,000 per year paid in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 every four months, directly into bank accounts (DBT).

Card 2
Front: Which scheme provides health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for hospitalisation, and what is its insurance arm called?
Back: Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana). It is the health-insurance arm; the other arm is Health and Wellness Centres (now Ayushman Arogya Mandirs).

Card 3
Front: What is the difference between PMAY-G and PMAY-U?
Back: PMAY-Gramin is the rural housing scheme (implemented with Rural Development Ministry), while PMAY-Urban covers cities (Housing and Urban Affairs Ministry). Both aim at "Housing for All".

Card 4
Front: Which scheme gives free LPG connections to women of poor households, and what is its refill-support phase called?
Back: Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY), launched 2016, gives deposit-free LPG connections to adult women of poor households. Ujjwala 2.0 extended coverage with additional features.

Card 5
Front: MNREGA guarantees how many days of wage employment, and to whom?
Back: 100 days of guaranteed wage employment per financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work. (Enacted 2005; renamed Mahatma Gandhi NREGA in 2009.)

Card 6
Front: Which scheme focuses on "water to every household" through tap connections, and which on water conservation?
Back: Jal Jeevan Mission (2019) — functional household tap connections (Har Ghar Jal). Jal Shakti Abhiyan — a campaign for water conservation and rainwater harvesting.

Card 7
Front: What do Stand-Up India and MUDRA Yojana each support?
Back: Stand-Up India: bank loans (₹10 lakh–₹1 crore) to SC/ST and women entrepreneurs for greenfield enterprises. PM MUDRA Yojana: loans up to ₹10 lakh to micro/small units, classified as Shishu, Kishore, Tarun.

Card 8
Front: Under MUDRA, what are the loan limits of Shishu, Kishore and Tarun?
Back: Shishu: up to ₹50,000; Kishore: ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh; Tarun: ₹5,00,001 to ₹10 lakh.

Card 9
Front: Which scheme is the flagship for sanitation in rural India, and on whose anniversary was it launched?
Back: Swachh Bharat Mission, launched on 2 October 2014 (Gandhi Jayanti). Its rural arm (SBM-Gramin) is run by the Ministry of Jal Shakti; the urban arm by MoHUA.

Card 10
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the three MUDRA categories in order of loan size?
Back: "SKT — Small Kid Tackles": Shishu (smallest), Kishore (middle), Tarun (largest). Remember Shishu = child = smallest loan.

Card 11
Front: What is a quick memory trick for PM-KISAN's instalment structure?
Back: "6-3-2": ₹6,000 a year, 3 instalments, ₹2,000 each. If you remember 6-3-2, you can answer any instalment question.

Card 12
Front: Examiner's trap: A question says "PM-JAY provides ₹5 lakh per person per year." Is this correct?
Back: No. The cover is ₹5 lakh per FAMILY per year (floater basis), not per person. Examiners swap "family" with "person/member" to trap you.

Card 13
Front: Examiner's trap: "MNREGA guarantees 100 days of employment to every individual in rural areas." True or false?
Back: False. The guarantee is per rural HOUSEHOLD, not per individual. Also it is a legal guarantee of work on demand, not automatic employment.

Card 14
Front: Which scheme promotes digital payments and digital literacy, and what is the umbrella programme for e-governance?
Back: Digital India programme (launched 2015) is the umbrella; PMGDISHA (Pradhan Mantri Gramin Digital Saksharta Abhiyan) targets digital literacy in rural households.

Card 15
Front: What is the difference between Skill India's PMKVY and the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme?
Back: PMKVY (Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana) gives short-term skill training and certification; NAPS promotes apprenticeships in industry by sharing stipend costs with employers.

Card 16
Front: Which mission aims to give every urban household access to safe drinking water and sewerage, often paired with Smart Cities in questions?
Back: AMRUT (Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation, 2015). Smart Cities Mission (also 2015) focuses on area-based urban development — don't confuse the two.

Card 17
Front: Which scheme provides pension to unorganised-sector workers after age 60, and what is the pension amount?
Back: PM-SYM (Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan, 2019): assured pension of ₹3,000 per month after age 60, with 50:50 contribution by worker and Government.

Card 18
Front: What is POSHAN Abhiyaan, and which ministry runs it?
Back: National Nutrition Mission (POSHAN Abhiyaan, 2018) — aims to reduce malnutrition, stunting and anaemia, especially among children, adolescent girls and women. Run by the Ministry of Women and Child Development.

Card 19
Front: Which scheme gives collateral-free credit support to street vendors, and what is the first-cycle loan amount?
Back: PM SVANidhi (2020): collateral-free working-capital loan of ₹10,000 for the first cycle to street vendors, with interest subsidy and cashback on digital transactions.

Card 20
Front: What is the one-line trick to separate "Beti Bachao Beti Padhao" from "Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana" in exam questions?
Back: BBBP (2015) is an awareness/behaviour-change campaign (WCD Ministry) against declining child sex ratio; Sukanya Samriddhi is a small-savings deposit scheme for a girl child under Post Office/banks. Campaign vs. savings account — that is the trap.
