26. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), the overdraft facility available to eligible account holders is up to:
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹5,000 (c) ₹15,000 (d) ₹20,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many aspirants pick ₹5,000, which was the overdraft limit in the scheme's early phase; it was later raised. Currently, eligible PMJDY account holders can avail overdraft up to ₹10,000, so the answer is ₹10,000.
