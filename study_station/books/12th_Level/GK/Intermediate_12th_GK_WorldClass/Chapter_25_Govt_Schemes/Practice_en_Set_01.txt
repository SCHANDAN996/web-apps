1. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the overdraft facility available to eligible account holders?
(a) Up to ₹5,000 (b) Up to ₹10,000 (c) Up to ₹15,000 (d) Up to ₹20,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many aspirants pick ₹5,000 because the scheme originally offered ₹5,000 overdraft at launch in 2014, but the limit was later raised. The overdraft facility under PMJDY is up to ₹10,000 for eligible account holders, so the answer is ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

2. The Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) was launched on which date?
(a) 15 August 2014 (b) 28 August 2014 (c) 2 October 2014 (d) 26 January 2015
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 15 August 2014 — that is when the scheme was announced in the Independence Day speech, not launched. PMJDY was formally launched on 28 August 2014, so the answer is 28 August 2014.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) provides a health cover of how much per family per year?
(a) ₹1 lakh (b) ₹5 lakh (c) ₹2 lakh (d) ₹10 lakh
Answer: (b)
Solution: ₹2 lakh tempts because some state schemes offer that amount, but PM-JAY provides a cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation, so the answer is ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Which ministry implements the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)?
(a) Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (b) Ministry of Panchayati Raj (c) Ministry of Rural Development (d) Ministry of Home Affairs
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs is the classic trap — it runs PMAY-Urban, not the rural component. PMAY-Gramin is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development, so the answer is the Ministry of Rural Development.

5. The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) provides income support of how much per year to eligible farmer families?
(a) ₹4,000 (b) ₹10,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹6,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: PM-KISAN gives ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 each directly into bank accounts, so the answer is ₹6,000.

7. Under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana, the 'Kishor' category covers loans of which range?
(a) Up to ₹50,000 (b) ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh (c) ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh (d) ₹10 lakh to ₹20 lakh
Answer: (c)
Solution: The three Mudra categories are Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishor (₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh) and Tarun (₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh). Confusing Kishor with Tarun is the common trap, so the answer is ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

8. The 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' scheme was launched in 2015 from which place?
(a) Panipat, Haryana (b) New Delhi (c) Jaipur, Rajasthan (d) Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: New Delhi is the tempting wrong choice since most schemes are launched there. Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao was launched on 22 January 2015 from Panipat, Haryana — a state then known for a low child sex ratio — so the answer is Panipat, Haryana.
Source: PYQ-style

9. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is related to which of the following?
(a) Free electricity connections (b) LPG connections to women of poor households (c) Free piped water supply (d) Solar lanterns for students
Answer: (b)
Solution: Free electricity connections describe the Saubhagya scheme, which is the trap here. PMUY, launched in 2016, provides deposit-free LPG connections to adult women of poor households, so the answer is LPG connections to women of poor households.
Source: PYQ-style

10. The Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is targeted mainly at workers of which sector?
(a) Organised sector (b) Defence personnel (c) Government employees (d) Unorganised sector
Answer: (d)
Solution: Government employees are covered by the National Pension System, which is the distractor. APY, launched in 2015, targets workers in the unorganised sector, guaranteeing a fixed pension of ₹1,000–₹5,000 per month after age 60, so the answer is the unorganised sector.
Source: PYQ-style

11. The 'Make in India' initiative was launched in which year?
(a) 2014 (b) 2015 (c) 2016 (d) 2017
Answer: (a)
Solution: Make in India was launched on 25 September 2014 to boost manufacturing; Digital India and Skill India followed in 2015, which causes chronological confusion, so the answer is 2014.

12. The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is associated with which of the following?

15. The 'Stand Up India' scheme facilitates bank loans for which group of entrepreneurs?

(a) Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) – 2018 (b) PM-KISAN – 2016 (c) Ujjwala Yojana – 2014 (d) Jal Jeevan Mission – 2017

22. Consider the following statements about the Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY): Statement 1 — It is a life insurance scheme. Statement 2 — It offers a cover of ₹2 lakh. Which is correct?
(a) Both statements (b) Only Statement 2 (c) Only Statement 1 (d) Neither statement
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is confusing PMJJBY with PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (accident insurance). PMJJBY is a one-year renewable life insurance scheme offering ₹2 lakh cover on death for any reason, so both statements are correct and the answer is both statements.

Answer: (c)

24. Which of the following statements about the National Education Policy (NEP) is correct?
(a) It proposes a 5+3+3+4 school structure (b) It was approved in 2018 (c) It retains the 10+2 structure unchanged (d) It was India's first education policy
Answer: (a)
Solution: The traps are the year (NEP was approved in July 2020, not 2018) and the claim that 10+2 continues unchanged. NEP 2020 replaces 10+2 with the 5+3+3+4 structure, so the answer is that it proposes a 5+3+3+4 school structure.

25. The 'PM SVANidhi' scheme provides collateral-free working capital loans to which group?
(a) Small farmers (b) Self-help groups (c) Handloom weavers (d) Street vendors
Answer: (d)
Solution: Self-help groups are the trap — they are covered under DAY-NRLM. PM SVANidhi, launched in 2020, gives collateral-free working capital loans (initially up to ₹10,000) to street vendors affected by the pandemic, so the answer is street vendors.
