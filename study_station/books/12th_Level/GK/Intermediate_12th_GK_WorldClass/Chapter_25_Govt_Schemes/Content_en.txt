# Government Schemes & Initiatives

## 🎯 The Hook: A Bank Account That Changed 50 Crore Lives

In August 2014, a daily-wage worker in a small UP village walked into a bank for the first time in her life. She had no documents except an Aadhaar card, no minimum balance, and no idea what a "zero-balance account" meant. Within 15 minutes, she had a **Jan Dhan account** — her first formal identity in the financial system. By 2024, over **50 crore such accounts** had been opened under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), making it one of the largest financial inclusion drives in world history.

That is the power of a government scheme — and that is exactly why examiners love this chapter. Schemes are where **polity, economy, and current affairs collide**. Let's master them.

## 📌 Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

3. Spot the classic examiner traps: swapped years, swapped ministries, and "almost correct" statements.
4. Link schemes to their **target beneficiaries** and **funding patterns**.

## 🧩 Chunk 1: How to Study Schemes — The 5-Point Formula

Every scheme question in SSC, Banking, or State PCS exams revolves around just **five data points**:

| # | Data Point | Example (PMJDY) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Launch Year** | 2014 |
| 2 | **Ministry** | Ministry of Finance |
| 3 | **Objective** | Financial inclusion — bank account for every household |
| 4 | **Beneficiary** | All unbanked households |
| 5 | **Funding/Nodal Agency** | Central Sector Scheme |

> 🧠 **Active Recall:** Close your eyes. What are the 5 data points of any scheme? Say them aloud before reading on.

**Key distinction you must never confuse:**

- **Central Sector Scheme** → 100% funded by the Central Government (e.g., PM-KISAN).
- **Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS)** → Cost shared between Centre and States, commonly **60:40** (90:10 for North-Eastern and Himalayan states) (e.g., MGNREGA is largely centrally funded but implemented by states; PMAY-G follows a sharing pattern).

## 🧩 Chunk 2: Financial Inclusion & Welfare Schemes

### 1. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) — 2014
- **Launched:** 28 August 2014 | **Ministry of Finance**
- World's largest financial inclusion mission — zero-balance bank accounts, RuPay debit card, accident insurance cover, and overdraft facility.
- Foundation of the **JAM Trinity** — **J**an Dhan + **A**adhaar + **M**obile — used for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

### 2. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) — 2019
- **Launched:** February 2019 | **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare**
- **₹6,000 per year** to eligible farmer families, paid in **three instalments of ₹2,000** via DBT.
- Central Sector Scheme — 100% Centre-funded.

### 3. MGNREGA — 2005
- **Enacted:** 2005 (came into force February 2006) | **Ministry of Rural Development**
- Guarantees **100 days of wage employment per year** to every rural household whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work.
- Legal guarantee — demand-driven, not a target-driven scheme.

> 🧠 **Active Recall:** Which scheme gives ₹6,000/year, and in how many instalments? Which scheme is a *legal guarantee* of work?

## 🧩 Chunk 3: Health, Housing & Sanitation

### 4. Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY — 2018
- **Launched:** September 2018 | **Ministry of Health & Family Welfare**
- Health cover of **₹5 lakh per family per year** for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation.
- World's largest government-funded health insurance/assurance scheme; based on SECC 2011 deprivation criteria.

### 5. Swachh Bharat Mission — 2014
- **Launched:** 2 October 2014 (Gandhi Jayanti) | **Ministry of Jal Shakti (Rural)** & **MoHUA (Urban)**
- Goal: eliminate open defecation; India declared itself **ODF (Open Defecation Free)** on 2 October 2019.
- Phase II (SBM-G) focuses on **ODF Plus** — solid and liquid waste management.

### 6. PM Awas Yojana (PMAY)
- **PMAY-Urban:** 2015 | **Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs** — "Housing for All."
- **PMAY-Gramin:** 2016 (restructured from Indira Awaas Yojana) | **Ministry of Rural Development**.

> 🧠 **Active Recall:** Ayushman Bharat's cover amount? Which ministry runs PMAY-Gramin? (Trap alert — it's NOT the Housing Ministry!)

## 🧩 Chunk 4: Economy, Skills & Infrastructure

### 7. Make in India — 2014
- **Launched:** September 2014 | **DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry**
- Aim: turn India into a global manufacturing hub; originally covered **25 sectors**.

### 8. Skill India / PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) — 2015
- **Launched:** 15 July 2015 (World Youth Skills Day) | **Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship**
- Short-term skill training with monetary rewards and certification.

### 9. Startup India & Standup India — 2016
- **Startup India:** January 2016 — tax benefits, easier compliance, Fund of Funds.
- **Standup India:** April 2016 — bank loans (₹10 lakh–₹1 crore) to **SC/ST and women entrepreneurs** for greenfield enterprises.

### 10. Digital India — 2015
- **Launched:** 1 July 2015 | **Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY)**
- Three pillars: digital infrastructure, digital services, digital literacy.

### 11. Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) — 2016
- Run by **NITI Aayog** — Atal Tinkering Labs in schools, Atal Incubation Centres for startups.

> 🧠 **Active Recall:** Standup India targets which two groups? Which body runs the Atal Innovation Mission?

## 🧩 Chunk 5: Women, Children & Social Security

### 12. Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) — 2015
- **Launched:** 22 January 2015, Panipat (Haryana) | **Ministry of Women & Child Development** (with Health and Education ministries)
- Goal: address declining Child Sex Ratio and promote girls' education.

### 13. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana — 2015
- Small savings scheme for the girl child under BBBP; account can be opened before the girl turns **10**; matures at **21 years** from opening.

### 14. PM Ujjwala Yojana — 2016
- **Launched:** 1 May 2016, Ballia (UP) | **Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas**
- Deposit-free LPG connections to women of poor households.

### 15. Social Security Trio — 2015
- **PMJJBY** (life insurance, ₹2 lakh cover), **PMSBY** (accident insurance, ₹2 lakh cover), **APY — Atal Pension Yojana** (guaranteed pension ₹1,000–₹5,000/month after age 60). All launched **9 May 2015**.

## 🧠 Mnemonics That Actually Work

**Mnemonic 1 — "JAM" for DBT:**
**J**an Dhan + **A**adhaar + **M**obile = the JAM Trinity that delivers subsidies directly.

**Mnemonic 2 — "14-15-16 Ladder" for launch years:**
- **2014:** Jan Dhan, Swachh Bharat, Make in India
- **2015:** Skill India, Digital India, BBBP, PMAY-U, Insurance Trio
- **2016:** Ujjwala, Startup/Standup India, PMAY-G, AIM
- **2018:** Ayushman Bharat | **2019:** PM-KISAN

**Mnemonic 3 — "HARYANA's Daughter" for BBBP:**
Beti Bachao Beti Padhao was launched in **Panipat, Haryana** — remember "Haryana's daughters first."

## 🔗 Static-Dynamic Link Box

**MGNREGA (2005) ↔ Article 41 (Static):** The Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV) direct the State to secure the "right to work." MGNREGA is the closest legislative expression of Article 41 — examiners love asking this linkage.

**PMJDY ↔ RBI's Financial Inclusion drive:** Jan Dhan accounts ride on the banking network built through RBI's earlier "no-frills accounts" (2005) and Business Correspondent model — a static banking concept turned into a mass scheme in 2014.

## ⚠️ Caution: Examiner's Trap

**Trap 1 — "Almost Correct Statement" (लगभग सही कथन):**
*"PM-KISAN provides ₹6,000 per year in two instalments of ₹3,000."*
❌ Wrong! It is **three instalments of ₹2,000**. The amount is right; the instalment structure is the trap.

**Trap 2 — "Similar Names/Ministry Swap" (समान नाम/मंत्रालय भ्रम):**
*"PMAY-Gramin is implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs."*
❌ Wrong! PMAY-**Gramin** → Ministry of **Rural Development**; PMAY-**Urban** → Ministry of **Housing and Urban Affairs**. The scheme names are twins; the ministries are not.

**Trap 3 — "Chronological Confusion" (कालानुक्रमिक भ्रम):**
*"Ayushman Bharat (2018) was launched before MGNREGA (2005)."* — obviously false, but in statement-based questions, examiners pair schemes just **one year apart**: Digital India (2015) vs Startup India (2016), or Ujjwala (2016) vs BBBP (2015). One-year swaps are the deadliest.

**Trap 4 — "Negative Phrasing" (नकारात्मक वाक्यांश):**
*"Which of the following is NOT a feature of PMJDY?"* — Students read fast, miss the "NOT," and pick the first true feature. Always circle the negative word before answering.
