## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Category | Question Type | Time Rule | Score Maximizer? |
|----------|---------------|-----------|------------------|
| Launch year + place of launch | Pure recall (e.g., BBBP from Panipat, 2015) | **15 sec** — answer or skip instantly | ✅ Yes — high frequency, zero calculation |
| Ministry in charge | Recall | **15 sec** | ✅ Yes |
| Financial amounts (PM-KISAN ₹6,000, PM-JAY ₹5 lakh, Mudra slabs) | Recall | **15 sec** | ✅ Yes — but beware number-swap traps |
| Beneficiary / eligibility (age, category, greenfield) | Recall + careful reading | **15 sec** if known; skip if unsure | ✅ Yes |
| Full forms / acronyms (AMRUT, SVANidhi, PM SHRI) | Recall | **15 sec** | ✅ Yes |
| Statement-based: "Which of the following is/are correct about Scheme X?" | Analytical — check each statement for traps | **45 sec** | ⚠️ Attempt only if 2+ statements confidently known |
| Match-the-following (scheme ↔ ministry ↔ year) | Analytical elimination | **45 sec** | ⚠️ Use elimination; one known pair often solves it |
| Chronology questions (arrange schemes by launch year) | Analytical | **45 sec** | ⚠️ Attempt only if 3 of 4 dates are certain |
| Current-status questions ("extended till…", "target year…") | Recall but volatile | **15 sec**; skip if budget changed recently | ❌ Risky — facts change yearly |
| Negative questions ("Which is NOT a feature of…") | Analytical | **45 sec** — underline NOT before answering | ⚠️ Common error zone |

**Skip Strategy:** Skip any question quoting a specific percentage coverage, beneficiary count, or budget allocation you have not verified — examiners plant updated/old figures as distractors. Never guess between two similar amounts (₹1.20L vs ₹1.30L) without elimination.
