# PYQ Analysis — Current Affairs (April – October 2026)

*(How SSC CHSL/MTS, Railway NTPC/Group D, State Police and Agniveer exams ask current affairs. We describe the pattern of questions; we do not quote invented counts.)*

## 1. How exams ask current affairs

- **One-liners:** "Who won…", "Where was … held", "Who has been appointed…".
- **Number questions:** medal tallies, ranks, percentages, counts (Ramsar sites, Padma awards).
- **Firsts and records:** "first Indian to…", "record … time".
- **Pair-matching:** event ↔ host city, award ↔ winner, exercise ↔ partner country.

## 2. Themes examiners return to

- Awards and honours, appointments, sports events, defence tests and exercises, space missions, economy data, environment and heritage, government schemes, international summits.

## 3. Ten high-yield questions (PYQ-style, with answers)

**Q1.** How many Censuses has India conducted since Independence, counting Census-2027?
**Answer:** 8
**Why:** Census-2027 is the 16th Census overall and the 8th since Independence.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q2.** Who was elected President of Vietnam by its parliament in April 2026?
**Answer:** To Lam
**Why:** Communist Party General Secretary To Lam was elected unanimously for a five-year term.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3.** Who was sworn in as the elected President of Myanmar in April 2026?
**Answer:** Min Aung Hlaing
**Why:** Min Aung Hlaing, who took power in 2021, was sworn in as President after a widely criticised election.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4.** According to the ISRO Chairman (April 2026), the G20 satellite for climate and air-pollution study is expected to be launched in which year?
**Answer:** 2027
**Why:** ISRO Chairman V. Narayanan said the G20 satellite is expected to be launched in 2027.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q5.** INS Sunayna's 2026 deployment with a multinational crew from 16 countries was under which initiative?
**Answer:** IOS SAGAR
**Why:** INS Sunayna carried crew from 16 friendly foreign countries under the Indian Ocean Ship (IOS) SAGAR initiative.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6.** Where was the 2026 Review Conference of the Parties to the NPT held?
**Answer:** New York
**Why:** The 11th NPT Review Conference, convened by the UN, began in New York on 27 April 2026.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7.** Who was appointed India's High Commissioner to Bangladesh in April 2026?
**Answer:** Dinesh Trivedi
**Why:** The MEA appointed former Railway Minister Dinesh Trivedi as High Commissioner to Bangladesh.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8.** BrahMos Aerospace's revenue for 2025-26 crossed what amount?
**Answer:** ₹5,200 crore
**Why:** DRDO said BrahMos Aerospace revenue crossed ₹5,200 crore; its new Lucknow facility rolled out its first missiles.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9.** GalaxEye's Mission Drishti (2026) launched the world's first satellite of which type?
**Answer:** OptoSAR
**Why:** PM Modi said the launch of the world's first OptoSAR satellite, the largest privately built satellite in India, was a major achievement.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10.** India lost the Thomas Cup 2026 semi-final to which country?
**Answer:** France
**Why:** India lost 0-3 to France, led by the Popov brothers, in the semi-final at Horsens, Denmark.
**Source:** PYQ-style

## 4. 15-second / 45-second rule

- Name/place questions: 15 seconds — you know it or you skip it.
- Number questions: up to 45 seconds — eliminate outdated figures first.
- Never spend more than 45 seconds on a single current-affairs question.
