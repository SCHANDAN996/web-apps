# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Indian Polity

---

## Mnemonic 1: Fundamental Rights (Articles 12–35)

**"E-F-E-R-C-C" = "Every Free Elephant Runs Carefully, Carefully"**

- **E** — Equality (Art 14–18)
- **F** — Freedom (Art 19–22)
- **E** — Exploitation, Against (Art 23–24)
- **R** — Religion (Art 25–28)
- **C** — Cultural & Educational Rights (Art 29–30)
- **C** — Constitutional Remedies (Art 32)

---

## Mnemonic 2: Fundamental Duties — How Many?

**"42 + 86 = 11"**
- 42nd Amendment (1976) added **10** Fundamental Duties (Art 51A).
- 86th Amendment (2002) added the **11th** (parents to provide education to children aged 6–14).
- Trick: *"42 brought 10, 86 brought 1 more — 11 duties on the floor."*

---

## Mnemonic 3: Schedules of the Constitution (12)

**"TEARS OF OLD PM"** (classic, slightly expanded):

- **T** — Territories (1st Schedule: States & UTs)
- **E** — Emoluments (2nd: Salaries)
- **A** — Affirmations/Oaths (3rd)
- **R** — Rajya Sabha seats (4th)
- **S** — Scheduled Areas & Tribes (5th & 6th)
- **O** — Organisation of powers (7th: Union, State and Concurrent Lists)
- **F** — Formal (scheduled) languages (8th: 22 languages)
- **O** — Order of land reforms (9th)
- **L** — Defection Law (10th: Anti-Defection, 1985)
- **D** — Districts/Panchayats (11th) & **M** — Municipalities (12th)

## Mnemonic 4: Parts Borrowed from Other Countries

**"UK-USA-IRE-CAN-AUS-GER-USSR-SA-FR-JAP"**

- **UK** → Parliamentary system, Rule of Law, Single citizenship
- **USA** → Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, Impeachment of President, Preamble idea
- **Ireland** → DPSP, Nomination of Rajya Sabha members, President's election method
- **Canada** → Federation with strong Centre, Residuary powers with Centre
- **Australia** → Concurrent List, Joint Sitting
- **Germany (Weimar)** → Emergency provisions
- **USSR** → Fundamental Duties, Five-Year Plan ideals
- **South Africa** → Amendment procedure, Rajya Sabha election
- **France** → Republic, ideals of liberty-equality-fraternity
- **Japan** → Procedure established by law

Hook: *"Britain gave the engine, America gave the brakes, Ireland gave the conscience."*

## Mnemonic 5: Writs (Article 32 & 226)

**"Habeas Mandamus Prohibits Certiorari Quo"** — remember by function:

- **Habeas Corpus** — "Produce the body!" (illegal detention)
- **Mandamus** — "We command!" (court orders a public official to do duty)
- **Prohibition** — "Stop!" (higher court stops lower court mid-case)
- **Certiorari** — "Send the records!" (quashes a decided order)
- **Quo Warranto** — "By what authority?" (challenges illegal office-holder)

Hook: *"Body, Command, Stop, Records, Authority — BCSRA: Big Courts Save Rights Always."*

---

## Mnemonic 6: President's Impeachment & Removal Numbers

**"1/4 propose, 2/3 dispose"**
- Impeachment motion needs **1/4th** of total members of a House to sign the proposal.
- Needs **2/3rd of total membership** of each House to pass.
- Only ground: **violation of the Constitution**.

---

## Mnemonic 7: Money Bill vs Ordinary Bill

**"108 is the Joint-Sitting Gate"**
- Deadlock on ordinary bill → Joint Sitting under **Article 108**.
- **Money Bill (Art 110)**: Speaker certifies, Rajya Sabha can only suggest (14 days), **no joint sitting ever**.
- Hook: *"Money talks, but only in the Lok Sabha."*

---

## Mnemonic 8: Panchayati Raj — 73rd & 74th Amendments

**"73 = Village (3-tier), 74 = City (3 types)"**
- 73rd (1992): Panchayats — Gram, Block (Panchayat Samiti), District (Zila Parishad).
- 74th (1992): Municipalities — Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation.
- 11th Schedule = **29 subjects**; 12th Schedule = **18 subjects**.
- Hook: *"29 for the village, 18 for the town — 29+18 = 47, the local-governance crown."*

---

## Mnemonic 9: Important Articles of the Executive

**"52-53-54-55: President's ladder"**
- **52** — There shall be a President.
- **53** — Executive power vested in President.
- **54** — Election of President.
- **55** — Manner of election (proportional representation, single transferable vote).
- **72** — President's pardon power; **74** — Council of Ministers to aid & advise; **123** — Ordinance.
- Hook: *"52 born, 53 powered, 54 elected, 55 counted."*

---

## Mnemonic 10: Emergency Provisions

**"352-356-360: National, State, Financial — N-S-F = 'No State Funds'"**
- **352** — National Emergency (war, external aggression, armed rebellion).
- **356** — President's Rule (failure of constitutional machinery in a state).
- **360** — Financial Emergency (never proclaimed so far).
- Hook: *"352 the nation shakes, 356 a state breaks, 360 the money quakes."*

---

## Mnemonic 11: Amendment of the Constitution (Article 368)

**"Simple, Special, Special+States — the 3 S ladder"**
- Simple majority (ordinary laws, e.g., creation of new states under Art 3).
- Special majority (2/3 present & voting + majority of total membership).
- Special majority **+ ratification by half the states** (federal provisions: election of President, distribution of powers, Supreme Court/High Courts, Art 368 itself).
- Hook: *"Touch the federal bone, and half the states must phone home."*

**"E-U-A-F-C-S" = "Every Uncle Ate Five Crispy Samosas"**
- **E** — Election Commission (Art 324)
- **U** — UPSC (Art 315)
- **A** — Attorney General (Art 76)
- **F** — Finance Commission (Art 280)
- **C** — CAG (Art 148)
- **S** — Scheduled Castes/Tribes Commissions (Art 338, 338A)

## Mnemonic 13: Preamble Keywords Order

**"Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic — Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity"**
Hook: *"3S-D-R, then JLEF — 'Just Lions Eat Fruit'."*
- "Socialist", "Secular", "Integrity" added by **42nd Amendment, 1976**.
- Preamble borrowed from **USA**; called the "identity card of the Constitution" (N.A. Palkhivala).

---

## Mnemonic 14: Vice-President & Rajya Sabha

**"VP = RS Chairman, but NOT a member"**
- Vice-President is **ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha** (Art 64) but is **not a member** of it.
- Can be removed by Rajya Sabha resolution (absolute majority) agreed by Lok Sabha — **no impeachment**.
- Hook: *"He chairs the house he doesn't live in."*

---

## Mnemonic 15: Languages in the 8th Schedule

**"14 → 22"**
- Originally **14** languages; now **22**.
- Added later: Sindhi (21st Amdt, 1967); Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali (71st Amdt, 1992); Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali (92nd Amdt, 2003).
- Hook: *"1967 one, 1992 three, 2003 four — 14+1+3+4 = 22."*

# ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

**Tip 1: The "One-Blank Memory" Rule.**
If a question asks for all members/founders/items of a list (e.g., "Which of these is NOT in the 8th Schedule?" or "Match the borrowed features with countries") and you can recall every item **except one**, do not gamble on the unknown one — the examiner has almost certainly placed the trap there. Skip and return only if time remains.

**Tip 2: Article-Number Confusion = Instant Skip.**
If a question hinges on exact article numbers you keep mixing up (e.g., 352 vs 356 vs 360, or 74 vs 75), and no elimination is possible, skip immediately. A blind guess between two similar article numbers is a 50-50 with negative marking — mathematically a losing bet. Only attempt if you can eliminate at least two options confidently.

**Tip 3: "Current Officeholder + Static Fact" Hybrids.**
If a question mixes a static fact with a current name (e.g., "Who presides over a joint sitting?" — static answer: Speaker) and you know the static rule, answer it. But if it asks for a current officeholder's name you haven't verified recently, skip — these change and are not worth negative marking in a static-polity paper.
