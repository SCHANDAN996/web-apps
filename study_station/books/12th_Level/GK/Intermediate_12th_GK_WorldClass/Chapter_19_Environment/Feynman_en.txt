# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Environment & Ecology (Basic)

## The Hardest Concept, Made Simple: The 10% Law of Energy Flow

Most students can recite "producer → herbivore → carnivore" but freeze when asked: *"If grass fixes 10,000 units of energy as food, how much reaches the eagle?"* Let's fix that forever.

### Imagine a Leaky Bucket Chain

Think of energy in a food chain as water being poured from one bucket to the next — but every bucket has big holes in it.

- **Bucket 1 (Grass/Producer):** Has fixed **10,000 units of energy** as food (this is the energy stored in plant matter — not the total sunlight falling on it).
- **Bucket 2 (Deer/Herbivore):** Eats the grass, but keeps only **10%** = 1,000 units. The other 90% is "lost" — used up in running, breathing, staying warm, or passed out as waste.
- **Bucket 3 (Tiger/Carnivore):** Eats the deer, keeps only **10%** = 100 units.
- **Bucket 4 (Eagle/Top carnivore):** Keeps only **10%** = 10 units.

So out of 10,000 units fixed by producers, the top of the chain gets just **10 units**. That's the **10% Law**, given by **Raymond Lindeman (1942)**.

### Why Does This Matter in Real Life?

This is exactly why:
- **Food chains rarely have more than 4–5 trophic levels** — after that, there's simply not enough energy left to support another level.
- **Tigers are rare, grass is everywhere.** Top predators need huge territories because so little energy reaches them.
- **Vegetarian diets support more people** — eating plants directly (1st consumer level) gives you 10× more energy than eating the animals that ate the plants.

### The Unforgettable Trick 🪄

**"Every step up, drop a zero."**

10,000 → 1,000 → 100 → 10. Just remove one zero at each trophic level. If an exam question gives you energy at any level, divide by 10 going up, multiply by 10 going down.

### Examiner's Trap to Watch Out For ⚠️

- The 10% law applies to **energy transfer between trophic levels** — NOT to the sunlight captured by plants (plants convert only about 1% of sunlight into food energy; that's a different number!). So if a question starts with "sunlight energy," be careful — the 10% law begins from the energy **fixed by producers**.
- Energy flow is **unidirectional** (one-way), but **nutrient cycling is cyclic**. Examiners love swapping these two in statement-based questions.
