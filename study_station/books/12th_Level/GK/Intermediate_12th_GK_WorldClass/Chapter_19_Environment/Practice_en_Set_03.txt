51. Which of the following is the correct sequence of increasing trophic levels in a grassland food chain?
(a) Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake (b) Grass → Deer → Grasshopper → Tiger (c) Grasshopper → Grass → Frog → Snake (d) Snake → Frog → Grasshopper → Grass
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap in option (b) is that it places the deer (herbivore) after the grasshopper and before the tiger, mixing up herbivores and carnivores; option (d) reverses the entire chain. A food chain always starts with a producer, followed by primary, secondary and tertiary consumers. Grass (producer) → grasshopper (herbivore) → frog (carnivore) → snake (top carnivore) is correct, so the answer is Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

52. In an ecosystem, the 10% law of energy transfer was given by:
(a) Charles Elton (b) Raymond Lindeman (c) E.P. Odum (d) A.G. Tansley
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Charles Elton, who is famous for the concept of the ecological pyramid and food chains, not the energy-transfer law. Raymond Lindeman (1942) proposed that only about 10% of energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next, so the answer is Raymond Lindeman.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

53. Which ecological pyramid is always upright?
(a) Pyramid of biomass in a pond (b) Pyramid of numbers in a forest (c) Pyramid of energy (d) Pyramid of biomass in a grassland
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the pyramid of numbers, which can be inverted (e.g., one tree supporting many insects), and the pyramid of biomass, which is inverted in aquatic ecosystems like ponds. The pyramid of energy is always upright because energy decreases at each successive trophic level, so the answer is Pyramid of energy.

(a) Organic pollution increases (b) Dissolved oxygen increases (c) Water temperature decreases (d) Aquatic plants increase
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is dissolved oxygen increase — actually high BOD means microbes consume more oxygen, lowering dissolved oxygen. Higher organic waste means more microbial decomposition and hence higher BOD, so the answer is Organic pollution increases.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is biomagnification, which is the increase in concentration of toxins (like DDT) at higher trophic levels, not nutrient enrichment. Nutrient enrichment of water bodies causing algal blooms is eutrophication, so the answer is Eutrophication.

65. Acid rain is mainly caused by oxides of:
(a) Sulphur and nitrogen (b) Carbon and hydrogen (c) Sodium and potassium (d) Calcium and magnesium
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is carbon — although CO2 forms weak carbonic acid in normal rain, it is not the cause of acid rain. Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides form sulphuric and nitric acids, causing acid rain (pH below 5.6), so the answer is Sulphur and nitrogen.

Solution: The trap is producers, where the pesticide first enters, but its concentration there is lowest. Due to biomagnification, DDT concentration increases at each trophic level and is maximum in top carnivores, so the answer is Top carnivores.
