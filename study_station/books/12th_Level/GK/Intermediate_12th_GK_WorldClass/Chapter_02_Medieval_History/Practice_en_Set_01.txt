1. Who founded the Delhi Sultanate in 1206 CE?
(a) Qutbuddin Aibak (b) Iltutmish (c) Balban (d) Alauddin Khalji
Answer: (a)
Solution: Iltutmish is a tempting option because he consolidated the Sultanate and is sometimes called its real founder, but the actual founder in 1206 was Qutbuddin Aibak, a slave-general of Muhammad Ghori, so the answer is Qutbuddin Aibak.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

2. The Qutub Minar was completed by which ruler?
(a) Qutbuddin Aibak (b) Alauddin Khalji (c) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (d) Iltutmish
Answer: (d)
Solution: Aibak only began the construction of the Qutub Minar, which makes him the tempting wrong choice; it was Iltutmish who completed it, so the answer is Iltutmish.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

3. Who was the first woman ruler of Delhi?
(a) Nur Jahan (b) Jahanara Begum (c) Chand Bibi (d) Razia Sultan
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nur Jahan wielded great influence in the Mughal court but never ruled Delhi as sovereign; Razia Sultan ruled Delhi from 1236 to 1240, so the answer is Razia Sultan.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

4. Alauddin Khalji introduced which system to control prices in the market?
(a) Market control policy (b) Dahsala system (c) Mansabdari system (d) Zamindari system
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Dahsala system is a trap because it was Akbar's land revenue system, not a price-control measure; Alauddin Khalji fixed prices of goods in Delhi's markets, so the answer is the market control policy.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

5. Who wrote the book 'Kitab-ul-Hind'?
(a) Ibn Battuta (b) Al-Biruni (c) Firdausi (d) Amir Khusrau
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ibn Battuta is the tempting wrong option because he too wrote a famous travel account (Rihla), but Kitab-ul-Hind was written by Al-Biruni, who came to India with Mahmud of Ghazni, so the answer is Al-Biruni.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

6. The First Battle of Panipat (1526) was fought between Babur and whom?
(a) Rana Sanga (b) Hemu (c) Ibrahim Lodi (d) Sikandar Lodi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rana Sanga is a trap because Babur fought him at Khanwa in 1527, not at Panipat; at Panipat in 1526 Babur defeated and killed Ibrahim Lodi, so the answer is Ibrahim Lodi.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

7. Who was the founder of the Mughal Empire in India?
(a) Humayun (b) Babur (c) Akbar (d) Jahangir
Answer: (b)
Solution: Akbar is tempting because he greatly expanded and consolidated the empire, but the founder was Babur after his victory at Panipat in 1526, so the answer is Babur.

8. The Second Battle of Panipat (1556) was fought between Akbar's forces and whom?
(a) Ibrahim Lodi (b) Sher Shah Suri (c) Hemu (d) Mahmud Lodi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sher Shah Suri is a trap because he had died in 1545, before this battle; in 1556 Akbar's army under Bairam Khan defeated Hemu, so the answer is Hemu.

9. Din-i-Ilahi was propounded by which Mughal emperor?
(a) Babur (b) Akbar (c) Humayun (d) Aurangzeb
Answer: (b)
Solution: Aurangzeb is a tempting wrong option because of his strong religious policies, but they were orthodox, not syncretic; Akbar propounded Din-i-Ilahi in 1582, so the answer is Akbar.

10. Who built the Taj Mahal?
(a) Shah Jahan (b) Jahangir (c) Akbar (d) Aurangzeb
Answer: (a)
Solution: Jahangir is a trap because he built fine monuments like Akbar's tomb at Sikandra, but the Taj Mahal at Agra was built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal, so the answer is Shah Jahan.

11. The Battle of Haldighati (1576) was fought between Akbar's forces and whom?
(a) Rana Udai Singh (b) Rana Sanga (c) Rana Pratap (d) Amar Singh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rana Sanga is a trap because he fought Babur at Khanwa in 1527, not Akbar; at Haldighati in 1576, Maharana Pratap faced the Mughal army led by Man Singh, so the answer is Rana Pratap.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

12. Who was the founder of the Vijayanagara Empire?
(a) Harihara and Bukka (b) Krishnadevaraya and Achyutaraya (c) Devaraya I and II (d) Narasimha and Tirumala
Answer: (a)
Solution: Krishnadevaraya is the tempting wrong option because he was the empire's most famous ruler, but he belonged to a later dynasty; the empire was founded in 1336 by the brothers Harihara and Bukka, so the answer is Harihara and Bukka.

13. Which traveller visited India during the reign of Muhammad bin Tughlaq?
(a) Marco Polo (b) Ibn Battuta (c) Nicolo Conti (d) Domingo Paes
Answer: (b)
Solution: Marco Polo is a trap because he visited South India earlier, in the 13th century; the Moroccan traveller Ibn Battuta came during Muhammad bin Tughlaq's reign and wrote the Rihla, so the answer is Ibn Battuta.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

14. Who shifted the capital from Delhi to Daulatabad?
(a) Alauddin Khalji (b) Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq (c) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (d) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (d)
Solution: Firoz Shah Tughlaq is tempting because he founded new cities, but he never shifted the capital; Muhammad bin Tughlaq ordered the capital's transfer to Daulatabad, so the answer is Muhammad bin Tughlaq.

15. Sher Shah Suri is famous for building which road?
(a) Grand Trunk Road (b) Silk Route (c) Dakshinapath (d) Uttarapath bypass
Answer: (a)
Solution: Uttarapath is a trap because it was the ancient name of a northern route, but Sher Shah rebuilt and extended the highway from Sonargaon to Peshawar, known as the Grand Trunk Road, so the answer is the Grand Trunk Road.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

Solution: Akka Mahadevi is a trap because she was a famous woman Bhakti saint too, but she was a devotee of Shiva from Karnataka; the Rajput princess devoted to Krishna was Mirabai, so the answer is Mirabai.

(a) Rana Sanga (b) Ibrahim Lodi (c) Medini Rai (d) Hasan Khan Mewati
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ibrahim Lodi is the classic chronological trap because Babur fought him at Panipat in 1526; at Khanwa in 1527 Babur defeated the Rajput confederacy under Rana Sanga, so the answer is Rana Sanga.

21. Consider the following statements about the Mansabdari system:
P. It was introduced by Akbar.
Q. Every mansabdar held a rank called zat only, with no other rank.
R. The sawar rank indicated the number of cavalrymen to be maintained.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) P and R only (b) P and Q only (c) Q and R only (d) P, Q and R
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Part Q, which sounds plausible but is wrong because a mansabdar held two ranks — zat (personal status and salary) and sawar (cavalry contingent); Parts P and R are correct, so the answer is P and R only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

22. Arrange the following battles in correct chronological order:
1. Battle of Khanwa 2. First Battle of Panipat 3. Battle of Chausa 4. Battle of Ghaghra
(a) 2, 4, 1, 3 (b) 1, 2, 3, 4 (c) 2, 1, 4, 3 (d) 1, 2, 4, 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing Ghaghra before Khanwa; the correct order is Panipat (1526), Khanwa (1527), Ghaghra (1529), and Chausa (1539, where Sher Shah defeated Humayun), so the answer is 2, 1, 4, 3.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

23. Which of the following statements about Alauddin Khalji is NOT correct?
(a) He was the first Sultan to attack South India (b) He introduced the dagh (branding of horses) system (c) He built the Alai Darwaza (d) He shifted the capital to Daulatabad
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that all the other statements are genuinely true of Alauddin, making the question confusing; shifting the capital to Daulatabad was done by Muhammad bin Tughlaq, not Alauddin, so the answer is the statement about shifting the capital to Daulatabad.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

24. Match the following travellers with the rulers during whose reign they visited India:
1. Ibn Battuta — i. Krishnadevaraya
2. Domingo Paes — ii. Muhammad bin Tughlaq
3. Abdur Razzaq — iii. Devaraya II
(a) 1-iii, 2-i, 3-ii (b) 1-i, 2-ii, 3-iii (c) 1-ii, 2-i, 3-iii (d) 1-ii, 2-iii, 3-i
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is confusing the two Vijayanagara visitors: Paes (Portuguese) came under Krishnadevaraya while Abdur Razzaq (Persian) came under Devaraya II; Ibn Battuta visited under Muhammad bin Tughlaq, so the answer is 1-ii, 2-i, 3-iii.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History (Our Pasts II)

25. Consider the following statements about Akbar's religious policy:
P. He abolished the jizya tax in 1564.
Q. He built the Ibadat Khana at Fatehpur Sikri in 1575.
R. He issued the Mahzar in 1579.
Which of the above are correct?
(a) P and Q only (b) Q and R only (c) P, Q and R (d) P and R only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is doubting Part P because the pilgrimage tax was abolished in 1563 and jizya in 1564, and students often mix the two dates; all three statements are correct, so the answer is P, Q and R.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History
