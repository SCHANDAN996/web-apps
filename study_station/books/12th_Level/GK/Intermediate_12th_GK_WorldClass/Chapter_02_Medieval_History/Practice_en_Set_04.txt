76. Who was the founder of the Bahmani Kingdom?
(a) Ahmad Shah Wali (b) Muhammad Shah I (c) Firoz Shah Bahmani (d) Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah
Answer: (d)

Answer: (b)
Solution: Gol Gumbaz is the mausoleum of Muhammad Adil Shah at Bijapur (Vijayapura), built by the Adil Shahi dynasty. Hyderabad is associated with the Qutb Shahis, not this monument, so the answer is Bijapur.

78. Which Delhi Sultan first introduced the token currency of bronze and copper?
(a) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (b) Alauddin Khalji (c) Balban (d) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Answer: (a)
