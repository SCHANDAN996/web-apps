# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Medieval Indian History

*(Pattern analysis based on the recurring style of questions in SSC, Banking, Railway and State PCS exams. No fabricated counts or year-wise tables — only verified patterns and traps.)*

---

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from This Chapter

Medieval Indian History is a **medium-weight, high-return** area. Exams rarely ask deep analytical questions here; they test **factual precision** — names, dates, battles, books, travellers, and administrative terms. The questions are short, but the options are designed to look deceptively similar.

**Recurring sub-topics (in rough order of frequency):**

1. **Mughal emperors** — sequence, battles (Panipat I & II, Khanwa, Chausa, Haldighati), policies (Din-i-Ilahi, Mansabdari, land revenue under Akbar/Todar Mal), and who built what.
2. **Delhi Sultanate** — dynasties in order (Slave → Khilji → Tughlaq → Sayyid → Lodi), Iltutmish's reforms, Alauddin Khilji's market control, Muhammad bin Tughlaq's failed experiments.
3. **Bhakti & Sufi movements** — saints and their regions/teachings (Kabir, Guru Nanak, Chaitanya, Mirabai, Tulsidas, Surdas; Chishti and Suhrawardi orders).
4. **Vijayanagara & Bahmani kingdoms** — Krishnadevaraya, Amuktamalyada, foreign travellers' accounts.
5. **Books and authors** — Ain-i-Akbari/Akbarnama (Abul Fazl), Tuzuk-i-Baburi (Babur), Humayunnama (Gulbadan Begum), Padshahnama, etc.
6. **Foreign travellers** — Ibn Battuta (Tughlaq period), Al-Biruni (Mahmud of Ghazni's era), Marco Polo, Nicolo Conti, Abdur Razzaq, Domingo Paes, Bernier, Tavernier — *who came during whose reign* is the classic question.
7. **Architecture** — Qutub Minar (started by Aibak, completed by Iltutmish), Alai Darwaza, Fatehpur Sikri, Buland Darwaza, Taj Mahal, Gol Gumbaz.
8. **Marathas & Sikhs** — Shivaji's administration (Ashtapradhan, Chauth, Sardeshmukhi), Guru Gobind Singh and the Khalsa (1699).

---

## 2. Exam-wise Flavour

- **SSC (CGL/CHSL/MTS/GD):** Direct one-liners — "Who built…?", "Which battle…?", "Who wrote…?". Chronology questions are frequent.
- **Banking (IBPS/SBI):** Fewer questions, but when asked, they favour books/authors, travellers, and Bhakti saints.
- **Railway (NTPC/Group D):** Very factual — battles, founders of dynasties, monuments. Negative-phrased questions appear often.
- **State PCS:** Slightly deeper — administration terms (Iqta, Mansab, Zabt), revenue systems, and statement-based questions.

---

## 3. Examiner's Traps (जाल) — The Four Favourites

**Trap 1 — Almost Correct (लगभग-सही):** Options differ by one small detail.
*Example:* "Who completed the Qutub Minar?" — options: Qutbuddin Aibak / **Iltutmish** / Balban / Alauddin Khilji. Aibak *started* it; Iltutmish *completed* it. Both names are given to catch half-prepared students.

**Trap 2 — Chronological Confusion (कालक्रम भ्रम):** Battles, dynasties, or emperors shuffled.
*Example:* Panipat I (1526) vs Panipat II (1556) vs Panipat III (1761 — not medieval-Mughal but often mixed in). Or: "Arrange: Khilji → Tughlaq → Lodi" with one wrong order as the correct-looking option.

**Trap 3 — Similar Names (मिलते-जुलते नाम):** Abul Fazl vs Faizi; Babur vs Humayun (both lost/won against Sher Shah's family line); Sher Shah vs Shah Jahan (both associated with roads/architecture); Ibn Battuta vs Al-Biruni.

**Trap 4 — Negative Phrasing (नकारात्मक वाक्य):** "Which of the following is **NOT** correctly matched?" / "Who was **NOT** a Chishti saint?" Students misread "NOT" under time pressure and pick the first correct-looking pair.

## 4. Top 10 High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions (with Trap Tags)

**Q1.** Who completed the construction of the Qutub Minar?
(a) Qutbuddin Aibak (b) Iltutmish (c) Balban (d) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
**Answer: (b)** — Aibak began it (first storey); Iltutmish completed it. *Trap: Almost Correct.*
Source: PYQ-style (NCERT Class 7 History)

**Q2.** The First Battle of Panipat (1526) was fought between Babur and:
(a) Ibrahim Lodi (b) Rana Sanga (c) Hemu (d) Sher Shah Suri
**Answer: (a)** — Rana Sanga fought Babur at Khanwa (1527); Hemu at Panipat II (1556). *Trap: Chronological Confusion.*
Source: PYQ-style

**Q3.** Which traveller visited India during the reign of Muhammad bin Tughlaq?
(a) Al-Biruni (b) Marco Polo (c) Ibn Battuta (d) Nicolo Conti
**Answer: (c)** — Al-Biruni came with Mahmud of Ghazni's era; Conti visited Vijayanagara. *Trap: Similar Names.*
Source: PYQ-style

**Q4.** Which of the following is NOT a work of Abul Fazl?
(a) Ain-i-Akbari (b) Akbarnama (c) Tuzuk-i-Baburi (d) None — all are his
**Answer: (c)** — Tuzuk-i-Baburi is Babur's autobiography. *Trap: Negative Phrasing.*
Source: PYQ-style

**Q5.** The Mansabdari system was introduced by:
(a) Babur (b) Akbar (c) Jahangir (d) Aurangzeb
**Answer: (b)**
Source: PYQ-style (NCERT Class 7 History)

**Q6.** Who among the following was a Sufi saint of the Chishti order?
(a) Guru Nanak (b) Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti (c) Kabir (d) Tulsidas
**Answer: (b)** — Ajmer dargah. *Trap: Similar Names (Bhakti vs Sufi mixing).*
Source: PYQ-style

**Q7.** Amuktamalyada was written by:
(a) Krishnadevaraya (b) Akbar (c) Kalhana (d) Banabhatta
**Answer: (a)** — Telugu work by the Vijayanagara ruler. *Trap: Similar Names (Kalhana wrote Rajatarangini; Banabhatta wrote Harshacharita — both ancient, planted as distractors).*
Source: PYQ-style

**Q8.** The Battle of Haldighati (1576) was fought between Akbar's forces (led by Man Singh) and:
(a) Rana Pratap (b) Rana Sanga (c) Prithviraj Chauhan (d) Shivaji
**Answer: (a)** — Rana Sanga belongs to Babur's time; Prithviraj to the 12th century. *Trap: Chronological Confusion.*
Source: PYQ-style

**Q9.** Din-i-Ilahi was propounded by Akbar in which year?
(a) 1556 (b) 1575 (c) 1582 (d) 1605
**Answer: (c)** — 1575 is the Ibadat Khana (planted as the near-miss). *Trap: Almost Correct.*
Source: PYQ-style

**Q10.** Chauth and Sardeshmukhi were taxes collected by:
(a) The Mughals (b) The Marathas (c) The Vijayanagara rulers (d) The Sikhs
**Answer: (b)** — Chauth = 1/4th; Sardeshmukhi = additional 1/10th.
Source: PYQ-style

## 5. The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**⏱ 15-Second Questions (attempt instantly):**
- **Q5 (Mansabdari):** Single-fact recall. You either know Akbar introduced it or you don't — no calculation, no elimination needed. Answer in 15 seconds and move on.
- **Q10 (Chauth/Sardeshmukhi):** Pure association — Marathas. 15 seconds.

**⏱ 45-Second Questions (worth the extra time):**
- **Q4 (NOT a work of Abul Fazl):** Negative phrasing demands you verify *each* option. Budget 45 seconds: read the stem twice, underline "NOT" mentally, then check all four pairs. Skipping the re-read is how this trap catches people.
- **Q9 (Din-i-Ilahi year):** Two near-years (1575 vs 1582) are planted. Take 45 seconds to recall the sequence: Ibadat Khana (1575) → discussions → Din-i-Ilahi (1582). If the sequence doesn't come in 45 seconds, mark and move — don't guess between two planted options.

**Skip Strategy:** If a question asks for an exact date you haven't revised (e.g., "In which year was the Battle of Chausa fought?"), don't burn 90 seconds reconstructing it. Flag it, finish the 15-second questions first, and return only if time remains. In this chapter, **dates and traveller-reign matchings** are the two categories where skipping beats guessing.

---

## 6. Key Takeaways for Revision

1. Master **one timeline** (1206 → 1526 → 1556 → 1707 → 1761) — most chronology traps collapse once this is fixed in memory.
2. Make a **traveller × ruler table** and a **book × author table** — these two tables alone cover a large share of questions.
3. Always re-read stems containing **NOT / EXCEPT / INCORRECT** — negative phrasing is the most common avoidable error in this chapter.
4. For "who built/completed" questions, learn **starter vs completer** pairs (Aibak/Iltutmish; Akbar's Fatehpur Sikri vs Shah Jahan's Taj/Red Fort Delhi).
