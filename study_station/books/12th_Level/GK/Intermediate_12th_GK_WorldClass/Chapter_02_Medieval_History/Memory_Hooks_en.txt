# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Medieval Indian History

---

**Box 1 — Delhi Sultanate Dynasties (in order)**
**"My King Took Some Land"**
**M**amluk (Slave) → **K**hilji → **T**ughlaq → **S**ayyid → **L**odi (1206–1526).

---

**Box 2 — Mughal Emperors (in order)**
**"BHAJAS"** — the six Great Mughals:
**B**abur → **H**umayun → **A**kbar → **J**ahangir → **S**hah Jahan → **A**urangzeb
Sing it like a chant: *Ba-Hu-Ak-Ja-Sha-Au!*

---

**Box 3 — Babur's Four Battles (in order)**
**"PKCG"** — **"Punjab Kings Conquered Ganga"**
**P**anipat (1526) → **K**hanwa (1527) → **C**handeri (1528) → **G**haghra (1529).

---

**Box 4 — Alauddin Khilji's Market Reforms**
**"Daal-ROT"** — he fixed prices of food grains (daal!) and set up separate markets:
**Shahana-i-Mandi** (grain market), plus markets for cloth and for horses/slaves/cattle.
Hook: *"Alauddin was the first 'price-control officer' of India — even a roti had a fixed rate!"*

---

**Box 5 — Iqta vs Jagir (the classic trap)**
**Iqta = Sultanate, Jagir = Mughals.**
Hook: **"I" comes before "J" in the alphabet, and the Sultanate came before the Mughals.**
Iqtadar collected revenue for the Sultan; Jagirdar for the Mughal emperor.

---

**Box 6 — Akbar's Navratnas (Nine Gems)**
**"Birbal Tansen, Faizi Abu'l Fazl, Man Singh Todar Mal, Rahim, Hakim, Mulla"**
Short trick: **"2 Bards, 2 Brothers, 2 Generals, 1 Finance, 1 Doctor, 1 Legend"**
Birbal (wit), Tansen (music), Faizi & Abu'l Fazl (scholar brothers), Man Singh & Rahim (generals), Todar Mal (revenue), Hakim Humam (physician), Mulla Do-Piyaza*.
*Note: Mulla Do-Piyaza is considered a later legend — examiners love this trap!

---

**Box 7 — Din-i-Ilahi (1582)**
Hook: **"Akbar's 'One-Religion Buffet' — take the best from every faith, serve under one emperor."**
Remember: it had **no scriptures, no priests, and died with Akbar**. It was NOT a mass religion — it had only a handful of followers.

---

**Box 8 — Sher Shah Suri's Road**
**"GT Road = Grand Trunk = Sher Shah's Highway"**
Sonargaon (Bengal) to Peshawar — with **sarais** (rest houses) along the route.
Hook: *"Sher Shah built India's first 'highway + motel chain' in just 5 years of rule (1540–45)!"*
Also: his silver currency **Rupiya** — ancestor of today's Rupee.

---

**Box 9 — First Battle of Panipat (1526)**
**"Babur's Gunpowder vs Ibrahim's Elephants"**
Hook: **"Cannons beat Jumbos"** — Babur's artillery (Tulughma tactic) defeated Ibrahim Lodi's huge army.
Trick for the three Panipats: **1526 (Babur wins), 1556 (Akbar's side wins), 1761 (Marathas lose)** — *"Win, Win, Lose."*

---

**Box 10 — Vijayanagara Empire Founders**
**"Harihara & Bukka — the H-B Brothers"** (1336), guided by saint **Vidyaranya**.
Dynasties in order: **"SST"** — **S**angama → **S**aluva → **T**uluva (→ Aravidu).
Hook: *"Sangama sang first, Saluva salvaged, Tuluva towered (Krishnadevaraya!)."*

---

**Box 11 — Krishnadevaraya (1509–1529)**
**"Amuktamalyada = the Telugu King"** — he wrote this Telugu classic.
His court had **Ashtadiggajas** (8 literary giants) — **"8 elephants guard the Telugu throne."**
Foreign travellers: **Domingo Paes & Fernao Nuniz** (Portuguese) visited him.

---

**Box 12 — Bhakti & Sufi Quick Pairs**
**"Kabir weaves, Guru Nanak travels, Chaitanya dances, Mirabai sings."**
- Kabir → weaver of Varanasi, disciple of Ramananda
- Guru Nanak → founded Sikhism (born 1469, Talwandi)
- Chaitanya → Bengal, Krishna-bhakti, kirtans
- Sufi orders: **Chishti** (Ajmer — Khwaja Moinuddin) vs **Suhrawardi** (Multan)
Hook: **"Chishti = Chai in Ajmer; Suhrawardi = Sweet in Multan."**

---

**Box 13 — Shivaji's Administration**
**"Ashtapradhan = 8 ministers"** (*ashta* = eight).
Taxes: **Chauth (1/4th) + Sardeshmukhi (1/10th)**.
Hook: **"Chauth = Chaar (4) → 1/4; Sardeshmukhi = Deshmukh's extra 10%."**
Coronation: **1674, Raigad**.

---

**Box 14 — Famous Buildings (who built what)**
**"Shah Jahan = Marble Man"** — Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Jama Masjid (Delhi), Moti Masjid.
**"Qutub Minar: Started by Aibak, finished by Iltutmish, repaired by Firoz Tughlaq"** — *"A-I-F: Aibak Initiated, Iltutmish Finished, Firoz Fixed."*
**Alai Darwaza** = Alauddin Khilji (his name is IN the monument!).

---

**Box 15 — Travellers' Timeline**
**"Marco Polo came early, Ibn Battuta in Tughlaq's time, Bernier saw Aurangzeb's court."**
- **Marco Polo** (Venetian) → Pandya kingdom, ~1293
- **Ibn Battuta** (Moroccan) → Muhammad bin Tughlaq's court
- **Nicolo Conti** (Italian) → Vijayanagara (Deva Raya I)
- **Abdur Razzaq** (Persian) → Vijayanagara (Deva Raya II)
- **Bernier & Tavernier** (French) → Aurangzeb's reign (Bernier was in India 1656–68)
Hook: **"Polo → Battuta → Conti → Razzaq → Bernier: P-B-C-R-B = 'Please Bring Coffee, Really Big'."**

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

**Tip 1 — The "Four Founders" trap:** If a question asks you to match founders of four dynasties (e.g., Slave, Khilji, Tughlaq, Lodi) and you confidently recall only three, **skip it**. One wrong pairing usually makes two options look correct, and you'll burn 2+ minutes guessing. Mark it, move on, return only if time remains.

**Tip 2 — Exact-year questions with look-alike options:** If options are 1526 / 1556 / 1576 / 1582 and your mind is blank, **skip immediately** — these are pure memory, not logic. No amount of staring will help. (But if you remember the *event*, the year often follows: Haldighati = 1576, Din-i-Ilahi = 1582.)

**Tip 3 — Traveller–ruler matching with unfamiliar names:** If a question pairs travellers (Conti, Razzaq, Paes, Nuniz) with rulers and you only know Ibn Battuta, **skip**. These questions are designed so that half-knowledge creates false confidence. Attempt only if you can eliminate at least two options with certainty.
