# 📋 Key Facts & Figures — Indian Constitution (Basic)

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constitution adopted on | 26 November 1949 (Constitution Day) | Confused with 26 January 1950 (enforcement) | Date confusion |
| Constitution enforced on | 26 January 1950 (Republic Day) | "Adopted" vs "came into force" swapped in options | Date confusion |
| Drafting Committee Chairman | Dr. B.R. Ambedkar | Dr. Rajendra Prasad was Assembly President, not Drafting Chairman | Similar names |
| Constituent Assembly President | Dr. Rajendra Prasad | Swapped with Ambedkar's role | Role confusion |
| First meeting of Constituent Assembly | 9 December 1946 | Sachchidananda Sinha was temporary chairman, not permanent | Near-correct statement |
| Total members of Constituent Assembly | 299 (after partition; originally 389) | 389 quoted as final strength | Number confusion |
| Time taken to make Constitution | 2 years, 11 months, 18 days | Rounded figures like "3 years" given as correct | Approximation trap |
| Total Articles (original) | 395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules | Current figures (448+ Articles, 12 Schedules) quoted as original | Original vs current |
| Schedules at present | 12 | 8 (original) given as current | Original vs current |
| Longest written constitution | India's is the longest written national constitution | "Largest democracy" ≠ "longest constitution" logic trap | Statement trap |
| Borrowed: Parliamentary system | From Britain | Confused with federal structure (Canada) | Source-country mix-up |
| Borrowed: Fundamental Rights | From USA | Confused with DPSP (Ireland) | Source-country mix-up |
| Borrowed: DPSP | From Ireland | Confused with Fundamental Rights (USA) | Source-country mix-up |
| Borrowed: Federal system with strong centre | From Canada | Confused with USA's federalism | Source-country mix-up |
| Borrowed: Concurrent List | From Australia | Confused with residuary powers (Canada) | Source-country mix-up |
| Borrowed: Emergency provisions | From Germany (Weimar Constitution) | Confused with suspension of FRs detail | Source-country mix-up |
| Borrowed: Amendment procedure | From South Africa | Confused with election of Rajya Sabha members (also S. Africa) | Source-country mix-up |
| Borrowed: Republic & ideals of justice | From France (French Revolution) | Liberty, Equality, Fraternity — French origin | Source-country mix-up |
| Preamble words added by 42nd Amendment (1976) | Socialist, Secular, Integrity | "Fraternity" wrongly listed as added word | Negative statement trap |
| Preamble based on | Objectives Resolution by Nehru (13 Dec 1946) | Confused with a foreign constitution's preamble | Near-correct statement |
| Preamble is part of Constitution | Kesavananda Bharati case (1973) | Berubari case (1960) said Preamble NOT a part — old ruling quoted | Case confusion |
| Fundamental Rights in Part | Part III (Articles 12–35) | Part IV (DPSP) given as answer | Part/Article confusion |
| DPSP in Part | Part IV (Articles 36–51) | Confused with Part III | Part/Article confusion |
| Fundamental Duties in Part | Part IV-A, Article 51-A | Added by 42nd Amendment 1976; originally 10, now 11 (86th Amendment 2002) | Number confusion |
| Right to Education | Article 21-A (86th Amendment, 2002) | Confused with Article 45 (DPSP) | Article confusion |
| Right to Constitutional Remedies | Article 32 — "heart and soul" (Ambedkar) | Article 226 (High Court writs) confused with Art 32 | Article confusion |
| Writs count | 5 writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto | Meanings swapped between Mandamus/Prohibition | Meaning swap |
| Citizenship provisions | Part II (Articles 5–11) | Confused with Part III | Part confusion |
| Single citizenship | India has single citizenship (like Britain) | USA's dual citizenship model applied to India | Comparative trap |
| President's election | Article 54 — indirect election by electoral college | Nominated members of Parliament do NOT vote | Negative statement trap |
| Vice-President | Article 63; ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha | "Elected by both Houses including nominated members" — here nominated DO vote | Contrast trap with President |
| Minimum age: President/VP/Governor | 35 years | 25 years (Lok Sabha) / 30 years (Rajya Sabha) mixed up | Age confusion |
| Minimum age: Lok Sabha member | 25 years | Confused with Rajya Sabha (30) | Age confusion |
| Minimum age: Rajya Sabha member | 30 years | Confused with Lok Sabha (25) | Age confusion |
| Lok Sabha maximum strength | 550 (530 States + 20 UTs) | Old figure 552 (with 2 Anglo-Indians) — abolished by 104th Amendment | Outdated figure trap |
| Rajya Sabha maximum strength | 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated) | 245 (current actual) confused with maximum | Max vs actual |
| Money Bill | Article 110; only Lok Sabha; Speaker certifies | Rajya Sabha can only suggest, not amend/reject | Power confusion |
| Speaker decides Money Bill | Decision is final | "President decides" given as option | Authority confusion |
| Joint sitting | Article 108; presided by Speaker | Not applicable to Money Bills & Constitutional Amendment Bills | Exception trap |
| Supreme Court established | 28 January 1950 (inaugurated) | 26 January 1950 given as answer | Date confusion |
| SC judges originally | 8 (1 Chief Justice + 7) | Current strength quoted as original | Original vs current |
| SC judge retirement age | 65 years | High Court (62) confused | Age confusion |
| HC judge retirement age | 62 years | Confused with SC (65) | Age confusion |
| CJI appointment | By President (seniority convention) | "Elected by Parliament" trap | Process trap |
| Judicial review | Articles 13, 32, 226 | "Borrowed from UK" — actually USA | Source-country mix-up |
| Amendment Article | Article 368 (Part XX) | Confused with Article 360 (Financial Emergency) | Article confusion |
| 42nd Amendment (1976) | "Mini Constitution" | 44th Amendment confused with it | Amendment confusion |
| 44th Amendment (1978) | Reversed many 42nd Amendment provisions; Right to Property removed from FR | Right to Property now legal right under Art 300-A | Amendment confusion |
| 73rd Amendment (1992) | Panchayati Raj — Part IX, 11th Schedule | Confused with 74th (Municipalities) | Amendment confusion |
| 74th Amendment (1992) | Municipalities — Part IX-A, 12th Schedule | Confused with 73rd | Amendment confusion |
| 86th Amendment (2002) | Right to Education (Art 21-A); 11th Fundamental Duty | Confused with 42nd Amendment | Amendment confusion |
| 101st Amendment (2016) | GST — Article 246-A | Confused with 100th Amendment (land boundary with Bangladesh) | Amendment confusion |
| 52nd Amendment (1985) | Anti-Defection Law — 10th Schedule | Confused with 91st Amendment (cabinet size 15%) | Amendment confusion |
| National Emergency | Article 352 | Confused with Art 356 (President's Rule) | Article confusion |
| President's Rule | Article 356 | Confused with Art 360 (Financial Emergency) | Article confusion |
| Financial Emergency | Article 360 — never declared in India | "Declared once" trap | Negative statement trap |
| Election Commission | Article 324 | Confused with Art 280 (Finance Commission) | Article confusion |
| Finance Commission | Article 280 — every 5 years | Confused with Art 324 | Article confusion |
| UPSC | Articles 315–323 | Confused with State PSC article numbers | Article confusion |
| CAG | Article 148 — Comptroller and Auditor General | "Elected" trap — CAG is appointed by President | Process trap |
| Attorney General | Article 76 — first law officer | Confused with Solicitor General (not constitutional post) | Post confusion |
| Official language | Hindi in Devanagari script (Art 343) | "National language" — India has NO national language | Concept trap |
| National Anthem adopted | 24 January 1950 (with National Song) | Confused with 26 January 1950 | Date confusion |
| "Sovereign Socialist Secular Democratic Republic" | Preamble order | Words jumbled in options | Order trap |
