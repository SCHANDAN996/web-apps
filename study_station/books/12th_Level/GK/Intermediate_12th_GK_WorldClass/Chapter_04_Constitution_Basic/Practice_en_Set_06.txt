126. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched regarding the source of features in the Indian Constitution?
(a) Directive Principles — Constitution of USA (b) Fundamental Rights — Constitution of Ireland (c) Concurrent List — Constitution of Australia (d) Procedure of Constitutional Amendment — Constitution of Canada
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong options swap the sources: Directive Principles came from Ireland, Fundamental Rights from the USA, and the amendment procedure from South Africa. The Concurrent List was indeed borrowed from the Australian Constitution, so the answer is Concurrent List — Constitution of Australia.

127. Consider the following statements about the Constituent Assembly:
1. It was constituted under the Cabinet Mission Plan of 1946.
2. Its first meeting was held on 9 December 1946.
3. Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected its permanent President on 11 December 1946.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: All three statements are correct. The Assembly was formed under the Cabinet Mission Plan, met first on 9 December 1946, and Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected permanent President two days later on 11 December 1946 — Sachchidananda Sinha was only the temporary chairman, which is the common confusion, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.

128. The idea of a Constituent Assembly for India was first put forward by:
(a) B.R. Ambedkar in 1942 (b) Jawaharlal Nehru in 1936 (c) M.N. Roy in 1934 (d) Mahatma Gandhi in 1940
Answer: (c)
Solution: Nehru is tempting because the Congress officially demanded a Constituent Assembly in 1935–36, but the idea itself was first put forward by M.N. Roy, a pioneer of the communist movement in India, in 1934, so the answer is M.N. Roy in 1934.

129. Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the power and procedure of constitutional amendment?
(a) Article 356 (b) Article 360 (c) Article 368 (d) Article 370
Answer: (c)
Solution: Article 356 (President's Rule) and Article 360 (Financial Emergency) are tempting because they are also famous numbered articles, but the power and procedure of constitutional amendment is laid down in Article 368 in Part XX, so the answer is Article 368.

130. In the Kesavananda Bharati case (1973), the Supreme Court held that:
(a) Parliament cannot amend the Constitution at all (b) Parliament can amend any part of the Constitution but cannot alter its basic structure (c) Fundamental Rights can never be amended under any circumstances (d) The Preamble is not a part of the Constitution
Answer: (b)
Solution: The extreme option is tempting because the Golaknath case (1967) had restricted amendment of Fundamental Rights, but Kesavananda Bharati overruled that position: Parliament may amend any part, including Fundamental Rights, subject to the basic structure doctrine, so the answer is that Parliament can amend but cannot alter the basic structure.

131. The words 'Socialist' and 'Secular' were added to the Preamble by which amendment?
(a) 42nd Amendment, 1976 (b) 24th Amendment, 1971 (c) 44th Amendment, 1978 (d) 52nd Amendment, 1985
Answer: (a)
Solution: The 44th Amendment is tempting because it also made major changes in the same era, but it was the 42nd Constitutional Amendment of 1976 (the 'Mini Constitution') that inserted 'Socialist', 'Secular' and 'Integrity' into the Preamble, so the answer is the 42nd Amendment, 1976.

132. Which of the following is NOT a feature of a typical federal system?
(a) Written Constitution (b) Division of powers between Centre and States (c) Independent judiciary (d) Single citizenship
Answer: (d)
Solution: A written constitution, division of powers and an independent judiciary are classic federal features, so they tempt the reader; single citizenship, however, is a unitary feature borrowed from Britain — federations like the USA have dual citizenship — so the answer is single citizenship.

135. The Ninth Schedule was added to the Constitution by:
(a) The First Amendment, 1951 (b) The Seventh Amendment, 1956 (c) The 42nd Amendment, 1976 (d) The 44th Amendment, 1978
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Seventh Amendment is tempting because it was a major early amendment (reorganisation of states), but the Ninth Schedule, protecting certain laws (mainly land reform laws) from judicial review, was inserted by the very First Constitutional Amendment in 1951, so the answer is the First Amendment, 1951.

139. Which of the following Fundamental Rights is available only to citizens of India and not to foreigners?
(a) Right to life and personal liberty (Article 21) (b) Equality before law (Article 14) (c) Protection in respect of conviction for offences (Article 20) (d) Protection against discrimination (Article 15)
Answer: (d)
Solution: Articles 14, 20 and 21 are tempting because they are famous rights, but they are available to all persons including foreigners; Article 15 (prohibition of discrimination), like Articles 16, 19, 29 and 30, is available only to citizens, so the answer is protection against discrimination under Article 15.

141. Which writ is issued by a court to a person detaining another, commanding him to produce the detained person before the court?
(a) Mandamus (b) Quo Warranto (c) Certiorari (d) Habeas Corpus
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mandamus is tempting as the most commonly tested writ, but it commands a public authority to perform its duty; Habeas Corpus ('to have the body') is the writ requiring production of a detained person before the court, so the answer is Habeas Corpus.

(a) 26 January 1950 (b) 15 August 1947 (c) 26 January 1949 (d) 26 November 1949
Answer: (d)
Solution: 26 January 1950 is the classic trap — that is when the Constitution came into force (Republic Day); the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949, now celebrated as Constitution Day, so the answer is 26 November 1949.

149. Consider the following statements about the Preamble:
1. The Preamble is based on the Objectives Resolution moved by Nehru.
2. The Preamble is enforceable in courts of law.
3. The Preamble has been amended only once so far.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: the Supreme Court has held that the Preamble is part of the Constitution but is NOT enforceable in a court of law; statements 1 (Objectives Resolution, 13 December 1946) and 3 (amended once, by the 42nd Amendment, 1976) are correct, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.

3. Concurrent List — 47 subjects

Solution: The trap is to use the present-day figures of 100, 61 and 52, which resulted from later amendments transferring subjects; as originally enacted in 1950, the Union List had 97 subjects, the State List 66 and the Concurrent List 47, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
