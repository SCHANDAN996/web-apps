# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Modern Indian History

---

## 📦 Box 1: Order of European Arrivals in India
**"P-E-D-D-F" → "Please Every Day Drink Fruit-juice"**
- **P**ortuguese (1498 – Vasco da Gama lands at Calicut)
- **E**nglish (1600 – East India Company formed; 1608 reaches Surat)
- **D**utch (1602 – Dutch East India Company)
- **D**anes (1616 – Tranquebar)
- **F**rench (1664 – French East India Company; Pondicherry 1674)

✅ Exam order asked most: **Portuguese FIRST, French LAST.**
⚠️ Trap: The English company (1600) was formed *before* the Dutch company (1602) — examiners swap these two.

---

## 📦 Box 2: Governor-Generals & Their Big Acts
**"Cornwallis = Permanent, Bentinck = Ban, Dalhousie = Doctrine"**
- **Cornwallis** → **Permanent** Settlement (1793) — "Corn is Permanent in the field"
- **Bentinck** → **Ban** on Sati (1829) — "Bentinck Banned Burning"
- **Dalhousie** → **Doctrine** of Lapse — "Dalhousie Devoured kingdoms"

---

## 📦 Box 3: Doctrine of Lapse Victims (in order)
**"S-J-S-J-N" → "Satara, Jaitpur, Sambalpur, Jhansi, Nagpur"**
- **S**atara (1848) → **J**aitpur (1849) → **S**ambalpur (1849) → **J**hansi (1853) → **N**agpur (1854)
- Hook: **"Sunny Jumps Slowly, Joins Nagpur"**
- ⚠️ Trap: Jhansi was annexed in **1853**, BEFORE Nagpur (1854) — and before the 1857 revolt. The revolt came later, partly because of this annexation.

---

## 📦 Box 4: 1857 — Who Led Where
**"Delhi-Bahadur, Kanpur-Nana, Jhansi-Lakshmi, Lucknow-Begum, Bihar-Kunwar"**
- **D**elhi → **B**ahadur Shah II — "Delhi's Badshah"
- **K**anpur → **N**ana Saheb — "Kanpur ka Nana"
- **J**hansi → Rani **L**akshmi Bai — "Jhansi ki Rani"
- **L**ucknow → **B**egum Hazrat Mahal — "Lucknow's Begum"
- **B**ihar (Arrah) → **K**unwar Singh — "Bihar's Kunwar"

## 📦 Box 5: INC Founding Facts
**"72 delegates met in '85, thanks to Hume's hive"**
- INC founded **1885**, first session at **Bombay (Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College)**
- **72 delegates**, first president **W.C. Bonnerjee**
- Founder: **A.O. Hume** (retired ICS officer)
- Hook: **"HUME = Help Unite Moderate Indians Early"**
- ⚠️ Trap: First session was Bombay, NOT Calcutta (Calcutta was 2nd, 1886).

---

## 📦 Box 6: Moderates vs Extremists Split
**"Surat Split '07 — Surat = Split (both start with S!)"**
- **1907 Surat Session** → Congress split into Moderates & Extremists
- **1905** = Bengal Partition (announced by Curzon)
- **1906** = Muslim League formed (Dhaka) + Calcutta session (Dadabhai Naoroji — "Swaraj" first mentioned)
- Hook: **"5-Partition, 6-League, 7-Split"** — count on fingers!

---

## 📦 Box 7: Gandhi's Early Movements (Chronology)
**"Champaran → Ahmedabad → Kheda → NCM → CDM → QIM"**
**"Champaran's Coolies, Ahmedabad's Mills, Kheda's Crops, then Nation-wide thrills"**
- **1917** Champaran (indigo) — Gandhi's FIRST satyagraha in India
- **1918** Ahmedabad Mill Strike + Kheda Satyagraha
- **1920–22** Non-Cooperation (withdrawn after **Chauri Chaura, 1922**)
- **1930** Civil Disobedience (**Dandi March**, 12 March–6 April, Sabarmati to Dandi)
- **1942** Quit India ("Do or Die")

## 📦 Box 8: Revolutionary Organisations
**"Anushilan in Bengal, HSRA in the North, Ghadar across the Ocean"**
- **Anushilan Samiti** — Bengal (1902)
- **HSRA** — Hindustan Socialist Republican Association, 1928 (Chandrashekhar Azad, Bhagat Singh)
- **Ghadar Party** — 1913, San Francisco, USA (Lala Har Dayal)
- Hook: **"Bhagat Singh HURLED a bomb — HSRA, Assembly, 1929"** (with Batukeshwar Dutt — "to make the deaf hear")

## 📦 Box 9: The Three "Montagu/Cripps/Wavell" Failures
**"Monty promised, Cripps offered, Wavell planned — all flopped"**
- **1917** Montagu Declaration (August Declaration)
- **1942** Cripps Mission — Gandhi called it **"a post-dated cheque on a crashing bank"**
- **1945** Wavell Plan / Shimla Conference — failed on Muslim League's demand
- Hook: **"Cripps = Crashed Cheque"**

## 📦 Box 10: Newspapers & Their Founders
**"Tilak's Kesari (Maratha), Gandhi's Young India, Annie Besant's New India"**
- **Kesari** (Marathi) + **Maratha** (English) → **Tilak**
- **Young India** + **Harijan** → **Gandhi**
- **New India** + **Commonweal** → **Annie Besant**
- **Amrita Bazar Patrika** → Sisir Kumar Ghosh

## 📦 Box 11: Famous Slogans — Who Said What
**"Bose gave blood, Bhagat wanted Inquilab, Gandhi said Do-or-Die"**
- **"Give me blood, I will give you freedom"** → **Subhas Chandra Bose**
- **"Inquilab Zindabad"** → coined by **Hasrat Mohani**, popularised by **Bhagat Singh**
- **"Do or Die"** → **Gandhi** (Quit India, 1942)
- **"Swaraj is my birthright"** → **Tilak**
- **"Jai Jawan Jai Kisan"** → **Lal Bahadur Shastri** (1965)
- ⚠️ Trap: "Inquilab Zindabad" was NOT coined by Bhagat Singh — examiners love this swap!

---

## 📦 Box 12: Round Table Conferences
**"3 RTCs, Gandhi went to only ONE — the 2nd"**
- **1st RTC (1930)** — Congress boycotted
- **2nd RTC (1931)** — Gandhi attended (after Gandhi-Irwin Pact) — FAILED
- **3rd RTC (1932)** — Congress absent again
- Hook: **"Gandhi's middle visit"** — 2nd of 3.
- Aftermath: **Communal Award (1932)** by Ramsay MacDonald → Gandhi's fast → **Poona Pact** (with Ambedkar).

---

## 📦 Box 13: INA & Subhas Chandra Bose
**"Mohan Singh raised it, Bose raised it higher"**
- INA first formed by **Capt. Mohan Singh** (1942, with Japanese help)
- Reorganised & led by **Subhas Chandra Bose** (1943, Singapore)
- **"Azad Hind Government"** — 21 October 1943
- INA trials at **Red Fort (1945)** — Shah Nawaz, Prem Sahgal, Gurbaksh Dhillon
- Hook: **"Mohan made it, Netaji named it Azad"**

---

## 📦 Box 14: Viceroys at Key Moments (Last-Minute Gold)
**"Lytton-Vernacular, Ripon-Ilbert, Curzon-Partition, Mountbatten-Freedom"**
- **Lytton** → Vernacular Press Act (1878) — "Lytton Locked the press"
- **Ripon** → Ilbert Bill (1883), Local Self-Government (1882) — "Ripon = Reform"
- **Curzon** → Bengal Partition (1905) — "Curzon Cut Bengal"
- **Mountbatten** → Last Viceroy, Independence (1947)
- ⚠️ Trap: 1857 revolt happened under **Canning** (who then became FIRST Viceroy, 1858).

## 📦 Box 15: Cabinet Mission & Independence Timeline
**"Mission in March, Interim in September, Freedom in August"**
- **March 1946** — Cabinet Mission (3 members: Pethick-Lawrence, Cripps, Alexander)
- **2 September 1946** — Interim Government (Nehru as Vice-President)
- **20 February 1947** — Attlee's declaration
- **3 June 1947** — Mountbatten Plan
- **15 August 1947** — Independence
- Hook: **"3-6-8: March Mission, June Plan, August Azadi"**

# ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

**Tip 1: The "One-Blank Memory" Rule**
If a question asks you to match **four** founders/leaders with four organisations (e.g., "Match: Brahmo Samaj, Arya Samaj, Ramakrishna Mission, Aligarh Movement") and you are sure of only three — **attempt it** (elimination will save you). But if you can't recall even **two**, **skip immediately**. Matching questions punish partial knowledge brutally; a wrong guess costs negative marks.

**Tip 2: The "Date-Cluster Trap" Rule**
If a question asks for the exact year of an event that sits in a confusing cluster (e.g., Surat Split 1907 vs Bengal Partition 1905 vs Muslim League 1906) and two options are adjacent years (1905/1907), **don't guess between them in under 15 seconds**. Mark it, move on, and return only if another question in the paper jogs your memory. Adjacent-year options are designed to harvest guesses.

**Tip 3: The "Statement-Soup" Rule**
If a question has 4 statements about a Governor-General's entire tenure ("Which of the following happened under Dalhousie?") and you recognise only one statement confidently, **skip**. These questions are built so that each statement is individually plausible — one unknown statement makes the whole question a coin toss. Your time is better spent on slogan/newspaper/movement questions where one solid fact wins the mark.
