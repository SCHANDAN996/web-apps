Card 1
Front: Which event is called the "First War of Independence" and in which year did it occur?
Back: The Revolt of 1857. It began at Meerut on 10 May 1857. V.D. Savarkar called it the "First War of Independence"; the British called it the Sepoy Mutiny.

Card 2
Front: Who founded the Brahmo Samaj, and in which year?
Back: Raja Rammohan Roy founded it in 1828 (as Brahmo Sabha). He is called the "Father of the Indian Renaissance."

Card 3
Front: Which Viceroy was associated with the Partition of Bengal, and when was it announced and later annulled?
Back: Lord Curzon announced the Partition of Bengal in 1905 (effective 16 October 1905). It was annulled in 1911 by King George V, when the capital was shifted from Calcutta to Delhi.

Card 4
Front: Which two organisations were founded in the 1880s — one Indian nationalist, one loyalist counter to it?
Back: The Indian National Congress (1885, founded by A.O. Hume, first session in Bombay, president W.C. Bonnerjee) and, as a loyalist counter, the United Indian Patriotic Association by Syed Ahmed Khan (1888 — trap: don't confuse the two years).

Card 5
Front: Mnemonic trick: How do you remember the order of the four major Gandhi movements?
Back: "N-C-C-Q" — Non-Cooperation (1920), Civil Disobedience (1930), (Gap), Quit India (1942). Remember: "No Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India" — each roughly a decade apart: 1920 → 1930 → 1942.

Card 6
Front: Examiner's trap: Who gave the slogan "Swaraj is my birthright and I shall have it" — and who is often wrongly credited?
Back: Bal Gangadhar Tilak. Students often confuse it with "Do or Die" (Gandhiji, Quit India 1942) or "Jai Hind" (Subhas Chandra Bose). Match each slogan to its leader carefully.

Card 7
Front: What was the immediate cause (greased cartridges) of the Revolt of 1857?
Back: Cartridges of the new Enfield rifle were rumoured to be greased with cow and pig fat, offending both Hindu and Muslim sepoys. Mangal Pandey revolted at Barrackpore on 29 March 1857.

Card 8
Front: Which Act first introduced a portfolio/system of separate departments and is called the beginning of parliamentary government in India?
Back: The Indian Councils Act, 1861. (Trap: Don't confuse with the Charter Act of 1853, which separated legislative and executive functions of the Governor-General's Council.)

Card 9
Front: Who founded the Swaraj Party, and why?
Back: Chittaranjan Das and Motilal Nehru founded it in 1923 (as the Congress-Khilafat Swarajya Party) to enter legislative councils and obstruct them from within, after the Non-Cooperation Movement was withdrawn.

Card 10
Front: Which session of Congress (1929) declared Purna Swaraj as its goal, and who presided over it?
Back: The Lahore Session, December 1929, presided over by Jawaharlal Nehru. 26 January 1930 was then observed as the first Independence Day.

Card 11
Front: Mnemonic trick: How do you remember the three phases of Congress leadership?
Back: "M-E-R" — Moderates (1885–1905), Extremists (1905–1919), Gandhi Era (1919–1947). Remember: "Moderate, Extremist, Rashtrapita (Gandhi)."

Card 12
Front: Examiner's trap: The Dandi March (Salt Satyagraha) — how many kilometres and how many days?
Back: About 240 miles (≈385 km) from Sabarmati Ashram to Dandi, 12 March to 6 April 1930 (24 days), with 78 followers. Trap: options often swap 78 followers with 78 days or 240 km with 240 miles.

Card 13
Front: Who was the first Indian to become a member of the British House of Commons?
Back: Dadabhai Naoroji (elected 1892, Liberal Party, from Finsbury Central). He propounded the "Drain of Wealth" theory in "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India."

Card 14
Front: Which revolutionary threw a bomb in the Central Legislative Assembly in 1929, and what was the motive?
Back: Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt (8 April 1929). The bombs were deliberately harmless — thrown "to make the deaf hear," protesting the Public Safety Bill and Trade Disputes Bill.

Card 15
Front: What did the Cabinet Mission Plan (1946) propose, and why did it fail?
Back: A three-tier federation with grouping of provinces (A, B, C) and a Constituent Assembly. It failed because Congress and the Muslim League disagreed over the grouping scheme and powers of the centre.

Card 16
Front: Who founded the Forward Bloc, and in which year?
Back: Subhas Chandra Bose founded the All India Forward Bloc in 1939 after resigning from the Congress presidency (following the Tripuri crisis with Gandhiji).

Card 17
Front: Which Act introduced dyarchy (diarchy) in the provinces, and which Act abolished it?
Back: The Government of India Act, 1919 (Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms) introduced dyarchy in provinces; the Government of India Act, 1935 abolished provincial dyarchy but proposed it at the centre (never implemented).

Card 18
Front: Who gave the slogan "Inquilab Zindabad," and who popularised "Jai Hind"?
Back: "Inquilab Zindabad" was coined by Hasrat Mohani and popularised by Bhagat Singh. "Jai Hind" was popularised by Subhas Chandra Bose (INA). Trap: examiners swap these two.

Card 19
Front: What was the Mountbatten Plan, and on which date was it announced?
Back: Announced on 3 June 1947 (also called the June 3rd Plan), it proposed the partition of India into two dominions. It led to the Indian Independence Act, 1947, and independence on 15 August 1947.

Card 20
Front: Which was the first Congress session held outside a presidency city, and who presided over the Surat Split session?
Back: The Allahabad Session (1888), presided over by George Yule, was the first session held outside a presidency city. The Surat Session (1907), where Congress split into Moderates and Extremists, was presided over by Rash Behari Ghosh. Trap: students often name Tilak — he was the Extremist leader, not the president.
