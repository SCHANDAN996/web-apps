# 📋 Key Facts & Figures — Modern Indian History

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battle of Plassey | 1757; Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah; foundation of British rule in Bengal | Confused with Battle of Buxar (1764) | Similar events / year swap |
| Battle of Buxar | 1764; British defeated combined forces of Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daulah and Shah Alam II | Wrongly credited as the "first" decisive battle | Chronology confusion |
| Diwani Rights | Granted to East India Company in 1765 by Treaty of Allahabad | Attributed to Treaty of Plassey (no such treaty) | Fabricated treaty name |
| Regulating Act | 1773; first parliamentary control over Company; created Governor-General of Bengal | Confused with Pitt's India Act 1784 | Similar names |
| Warren Hastings | First Governor-General of Bengal (1773–1785) | Called "first Governor-General of India" (that was William Bentinck, 1833) | Title swap |
| Permanent Settlement | 1793, by Cornwallis in Bengal; fixed land revenue with zamindars | Confused with Ryotwari (Munro) and Mahalwari (Holt Mackenzie) | System–region mismatch |
| Subsidiary Alliance | Introduced by Lord Wellesley (1798) | Credited to Dalhousie (who gave Doctrine of Lapse) | Governor-General swap |
| Doctrine of Lapse | Dalhousie; annexed Satara (1848), Jhansi, Nagpur | Applied to Awadh — Awadh was annexed on "misgovernance" (1856), not lapse | Wrong annexation ground |
| Revolt of 1857 | Began at Meerut (10 May 1857); Mangal Pandey at Barrackpore (29 March 1857) | Mangal Pandey shown as leader of the whole revolt | Scale exaggeration |
| Queen's Proclamation | 1858; rule transferred from Company to British Crown | Dated 1857 | Year shift |
| Indian National Congress | Founded 1885; first session at Bombay (Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College); president W.C. Bonnerjee | First session shown at Calcutta; founder shown as Bonnerjee (founder was A.O. Hume) | Place/person swap |
| Partition of Bengal | 1905 by Curzon; annulled 1911 | Annulment year given as 1909 | Year shift |
| Swadeshi Movement | Launched 1905 against Bengal partition | Confused with Non-Cooperation (1920) methods | Movement overlap |
| Muslim League | Founded 1906 at Dhaka (Aga Khan, Salimullah Khan) | Founded year given as 1905 or place as Lucknow | Year/place shift |
| Surat Split | 1907; Congress split into Moderates and Extremists | Placed at Calcutta session 1906 (where Swaraj was first demanded) | Session confusion |
| Morley-Minto Reforms | 1909; separate electorates for Muslims | Separate electorates attributed to 1919 Act | Act swap |
| Lucknow Pact | 1916; Congress–League agreement; Tilak's Home Rule League active | Home Rule Leagues of Tilak and Annie Besant merged into one | Detail merge |
| Rowlatt Act | 1919; "Black Act"; allowed detention without trial | Linked to Jallianwala Bagh as its cause — the massacre (13 April 1919) followed protests against it | Cause–event blur |
| Jallianwala Bagh | 13 April 1919, Amritsar; firing ordered by General Dyer | Hunter Commission shown as punishing Dyer (it only inquired) | Outcome exaggeration |
| Non-Cooperation Movement | 1920–22; withdrawn after Chauri Chaura (5 Feb 1922) | Withdrawal reason given as Moplah rebellion | Wrong trigger |
| Swaraj Party | 1923; C.R. Das and Motilal Nehru | Founded year given as 1922 | Year shift |
| Simon Commission | 1928 (arrived in India); all-white commission; boycotted | Year given as 1927 (it was appointed in 1927, arrived 1928) | Appointment vs arrival |
| Lahore Session | 1929; Purna Swaraj resolution; president Jawaharlal Nehru; 26 Jan 1930 as Independence Day | President shown as Motilal Nehru | Person swap |
| Dandi March | 12 March – 6 April 1930; Sabarmati to Dandi; Salt Law broken; distance about 240 miles (~385 km) | Distance quoted as 240 km instead of 240 miles | Unit distortion |
| Gandhi-Irwin Pact | 1931; Gandhi joined Second Round Table Conference | Shown as outcome of First RTC (Congress boycotted the First RTC, 1930) | Conference sequence |
| Communal Award | 1932 by Ramsay MacDonald; Poona Pact between Gandhi and Ambedkar | Poona Pact shown as ending separate electorates for all — it replaced them with reserved seats for Depressed Classes only | Scope distortion |
| Government of India Act 1935 | Provincial autonomy; proposed federation (never implemented); basis of much of the Constitution | Federation shown as implemented | False implementation |
| Quit India Movement | 8 August 1942; "Do or Die"; launched at Bombay (Gowalia Tank) | Slogan attributed to 1942 but movement dated 1940 | Year shift |
| INA / Azad Hind Fauj | Subhas Chandra Bose; Azad Hind Government 1943; INA originally formed by Mohan Singh (1942) | Bose shown as original founder | Founder swap |
| Cripps Mission | 1942; offered Dominion Status after war; rejected | Shown as offering complete independence | Offer exaggeration |
| Cabinet Mission | 1946; proposed Constituent Assembly; rejected Pakistan demand | Credited with proposing Pakistan | Opposite claim |
| Mountbatten Plan | 3 June 1947; basis of partition | Confused with Cripps/Cabinet Mission plans | Plan swap |
| Independence Act | July 1947 (British Parliament); India independent 15 August 1947 | Dated August 1947 | Month shift |
| First Session of Constituent Assembly | 9 December 1946; Sachchidananda Sinha as temporary chairman; Dr. Rajendra Prasad elected permanent president (11 Dec 1946) | Rajendra Prasad shown as chairman of the first sitting | Role confusion |
