**Box 1 — Grand Slam Order: "AO-French-Wimby-US" → "Aunt Fiona Wears Uniforms"**
The four tennis Grand Slams in calendar order: **A**ustralian Open → **F**rench Open → **W**imbledon → **U**S Open.
*Aunt Fiona Wears Uniforms* — say it once and the order never scrambles in the exam.

**Box 2 — Grand Slam Surfaces: "Hard Clay Grass Hard" → "Hot Chai, Garam Halwa"**
Australian Open (Hard) → French Open (Clay) → Wimbledon (Grass) → US Open (Hard).
Remember: the two "hot" hard courts sandwich the clay and grass — *Hot Chai, Garam Halwa*.

**Box 3 — Olympic Rings: "5 Rings = 5 Continents" → "Olympic HAND"**
Five interlaced rings = five inhabited continents (Africa, the Americas, Asia, Europe, Oceania). Your hand has 5 fingers — make a fist and count the continents. Colours: blue, yellow, black, green, red on white — *"Boys Yellow Black Green Red"*.

**Box 4 — First Modern Olympics: "1896 Athens — Greece gave birth, Greece hosted first"**
Ancient Olympics born in Greece (Olympia, 776 BC); first MODERN Olympics also in Greece — Athens, 1896. Hook: *"The baby returned home after 1500 years."* Founder: Pierre de Coubertin — *"Coubertin Cooked the Olympic Cake."*

**Box 5 — Cricket World Cup Years: "Every 4 years since 1975" → "75, 79, 83… Kapil's 83!"**
First ODI World Cup: 1975 (West Indies won). India first won in **1983** under Kapil Dev. Hook: *"Kapil lifted the cup in '83 — 8 and 3, Kapil's lucky pair."* Then 2011 under Dhoni — *"Dhoni's 11."*

**Box 6 — Hockey Olympic Golds: "India = 8 Golds" → "Hockey Stick looks like a sideways 8 waiting to happen"**
India won 8 Olympic hockey golds (1928, 1932, 1936, 1948, 1952, 1956, 1964, 1980). Hook: *"8 golds — 6 before 1964, then a long wait, last one 1980 Moscow."* Major Dhyan Chand: *"The Wizard with the stick."*

**Box 7 — Football World Cup: "FIFA = 4-year rhythm, Brazil = 5 titles" → "Brazil's HAND of FIFA"**
Brazil has won the FIFA World Cup 5 times — count them on one hand: 1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002. Hook: *"Brazil's five fingers of football."* First World Cup: 1930, Uruguay (host AND winner) — *"Uruguay threw the party and took the cake."*

**Box 8 — National Sports Awards: "Khel Ratna → Arjuna → Dronacharya → Dhyan Chand" → "King Arjun's Guru Drona worships Dhyan"**
Order of prestige: Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (highest) → Arjuna Award → Dronacharya (coaches) → Dhyan Chand Award (lifetime). Hook: *"The King (Ratna) sits above Arjuna, who was taught by Drona, and all bow to Dhyan Chand."* Trap alert: Dronacharya is for COACHES, not players!

## ⏭️ SKIP STRATEGY — When to Skip a Sports Question

**Tip 1 — The "Current Champion" Trap:** If a question asks for the *latest* winner of a tournament (e.g., "Who won the most recent edition of X?") and you haven't revised current affairs recently, **skip it**. Static knowledge won't save you, and guessing between two recent finalists is a coin toss with negative marking.

**Tip 2 — The "Four Names, One Founder" Trap:** If a question lists four people and asks who founded/won something first, and you can confidently eliminate only two, **skip after 45 seconds**. Partial elimination feels close but still gives you a 50% gamble — the exact situation negative marking punishes.

**Tip 3 — The "Number of Titles/Medals" Trap:** Questions like "How many Olympic golds has India won in hockey?" are safe ONLY if the number is a memorised hook (8 golds — the hockey stick). If the number isn't tied to a mnemonic in your head, **skip** — "almost right" numbers (7 vs 8, 4 vs 5) are the examiner's favourite trap options.
