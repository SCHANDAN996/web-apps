Card 1
Front: Which country has won the most Olympic gold medals in men's field hockey, and how many?
Back: India — 8 gold medals (1928, 1932, 1936, 1948, 1952, 1956, 1964, 1980). Don't confuse with total medals; the question usually asks only about golds.

Card 2
Front: The Durand Cup is associated with which sport, and what is special about it?
Back: Football. It is one of the oldest football tournaments in Asia, started in 1888 in Shimla.

Card 3
Front: Which trophy is NOT associated with cricket: Ranji Trophy, Duleep Trophy, Santosh Trophy, Irani Trophy?
Back: Santosh Trophy — it is a football tournament. Examiner's trap: three cricket trophies are listed with one football trophy to confuse you.

Card 4
Front: Who was the first Indian to win an individual Olympic gold medal, and in which event?
Back: Abhinav Bindra — 10m Air Rifle shooting, Beijing Olympics 2008.

Card 5
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the four Grand Slam tennis tournaments in calendar order?
Back: "AO-FR-WI-US" → Australian Open (January), French Open (May–June), Wimbledon (June–July), US Open (August–September). Trick: "Aunty Feeds Walnuts Until September."

Card 6
Front: Where were the first modern Olympic Games held, and in which year?
Back: Athens, Greece — 1896. Trap: don't answer Paris 1900; that was the second edition.

Card 7
Front: Which Indian woman won a bronze medal in boxing at the London 2012 Olympics?
Back: Mary Kom (Manipur), in the flyweight (51 kg) category.

Card 8
Front: The Thomas Cup and Uber Cup belong to which sport, and what is the difference between them?
Back: Badminton. Thomas Cup = men's team championship; Uber Cup = women's team championship. Trap: exams swap the two.

Card 9
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember India's hockey gold streak years?
Back: "28-32-36, then 48-52-56, then 64 and 80" — three pre-war, three post-independence, then Tokyo 1964 and Moscow 1980. Remember: "3 + 3 + 2 = 8 golds."

Card 10
Front: Which country hosted the 2010 Commonwealth Games, and where were the 2022 Games held?
Back: 2010 — Delhi, India. 2022 — Birmingham, England. Trap: many students wrongly pick Melbourne or Gold Coast for 2022.

Card 11
Front: Who is known as the "Flying Sikh" of India, and what was his famous Olympic moment?
Back: Milkha Singh — he finished 4th in the 400m at the 1960 Rome Olympics, missing a medal by a fraction of a second.

Card 12
Front: The term "LBW" belongs to which sport, and what does it stand for?
Back: Cricket — Leg Before Wicket. Trap: exams pair it with terms like "Deuce" (tennis) or "Bogey" (golf) to test sport-term matching.

Card 13
Front: Which Indian won two Olympic medals in wrestling, and in which years?
Back: Sushil Kumar — bronze at Beijing 2008 and silver at London 2012; the first Indian to win two individual Olympic medals after independence.

Card 14
Front: In which sport is the "Sultan Azlan Shah Cup" played, and which country hosts it?
Back: Hockey — hosted by Malaysia (Ipoh). Trap: students often link it to football because of the word "Sultan."

Card 15
Front: What do the five rings of the Olympic symbol represent?
Back: The five inhabited continents united by sport — Africa, the Americas, Asia, Europe, and Oceania. The rings do NOT each represent a specific continent by colour.

Card 16
Front: Which city hosted the 2010 FIFA World Cup final, and which country won it?
Back: Johannesburg (Soccer City), South Africa — Spain won, beating the Netherlands 1–0. It was Spain's first World Cup title.

Card 17
Front: Neeraj Chopra won gold at Tokyo 2020 — in which event, and why was it historic?
Back: Javelin throw — India's first-ever Olympic medal in athletics (track and field), and only the second individual gold after Abhinav Bindra.

Card 18
Front: Examiner's trap: "Wimbledon is played on clay courts." True or false?
Back: False. Wimbledon is played on GRASS. The French Open (Roland Garros) is the clay-court Grand Slam. Surface-swapping is a classic trap.

Card 19
Front: Which is the oldest tennis Grand Slam tournament?
Back: Wimbledon — first held in 1877. Trap: students often pick the US Open (1881), which is the second oldest.

Card 20
Front: The "Aga Khan Cup" and "Beighton Cup" are associated with which sport?
Back: Hockey. Both are historic Indian hockey tournaments; the Beighton Cup (started 1895, Kolkata) is among the oldest hockey tournaments in the world.
