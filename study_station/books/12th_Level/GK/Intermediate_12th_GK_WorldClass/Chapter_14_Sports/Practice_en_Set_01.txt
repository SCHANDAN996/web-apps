1. The Olympic Games are held every how many years?
(a) Two years (b) Three years (c) Four years (d) Five years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The modern Olympic Games follow a four-year cycle called an Olympiad. Two years is tempting because Summer and Winter Olympics alternate every two years, but each edition itself is held every four years, so the answer is four years.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physical Education

2. In which city were the first modern Olympic Games held in 1896?
(a) Paris (b) London (c) Rome (d) Athens
Answer: (d)
Solution: Paris is tempting because the 1900 Olympics were held there and Pierre de Coubertin was French, but the first modern Olympics of 1896 were held in Athens, Greece, the ancient home of the Games, so the answer is Athens.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physical Education

3. How many rings are there in the Olympic symbol?
(a) Four (b) Five (c) Six (d) Seven
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Olympic symbol has five interlaced rings representing the five inhabited continents. Four is tempting as it matches the four-year cycle, but the symbol has five rings, so the answer is five.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physical Education

4. The term "LBW" is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Cricket (c) Football (d) Tennis
Answer: (b)
Solution: LBW stands for "Leg Before Wicket", a mode of dismissal in cricket. Hockey and football use penalty corners and penalty kicks, not LBW, so the answer is cricket.
Source: PYQ-style

5. How many players are there in a football (soccer) team on the field?
(a) 9 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: Eleven is tempting to confuse with cricket, which also has 11, but that coincidence is correct here — a football team fields 11 players including the goalkeeper, so the answer is 11.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physical Education

22. Consider the following statements about the Arjuna Award:
Statement 1: It was instituted in 1961.
Statement 2: It is given for outstanding achievement in sports coaching.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) Only Statement 1 (b) Only Statement 2 (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — it describes the Dronacharya Award, not the Arjuna Award; the Arjuna Award honours sportspersons for outstanding performance. Statement 1 is correct as the award was instituted in 1961, so the answer is Only Statement 1.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Arrange these events in correct chronological order:
1. First modern Olympic Games
2. First FIFA World Cup
3. First Asian Games
(a) 1, 3, 2 (b) 2, 1, 3 (c) 1, 2, 3 (d) 3, 2, 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is swapping the World Cup and Asian Games; the first FIFA World Cup was in 1930 (Uruguay) and the first Asian Games in 1951, while the first modern Olympics were in 1896, giving the order 1, 2, 3, so the answer is 1, 2, 3.
Source: PYQ-style

24. The Davis Cup is associated with which sport, and the Uber Cup with which sport?
(a) Lawn Tennis and Badminton (b) Badminton and Lawn Tennis (c) Table Tennis and Badminton (d) Lawn Tennis and Table Tennis
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is reversing the pair — both are racket-sport team trophies and easy to confuse. The Davis Cup is the men's team event in lawn tennis and the Uber Cup is the women's team event in badminton, so the answer is Lawn Tennis and Badminton.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Consider the following statements about the 1983 Cricket World Cup:
Statement 1: India won the tournament defeating West Indies in the final.
Statement 2: The final was played at Lord's, London.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) Only Statement 1 (b) Only Statement 2 (c) Neither 1 nor 2 (d) Both 1 and 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming one statement must be wrong; in fact India defeated two-time champions West Indies in the 1983 final, and that final was played at Lord's under Kapil Dev's captaincy. Both statements are correct, so the answer is Both 1 and 2.
Source: PYQ-style
