# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Sports & Competitions

## The Most Confusing Concept: "Which Trophy Belongs to Which Sport?"

Ask any exam aspirant: *"Is the Durand Cup for football or hockey?"* and watch them freeze. This is the single most trap-prone area of Sports GK — dozens of trophies, all with similar-sounding names, scattered across cricket, football, hockey, tennis and badminton. Let's fix it the Feynman way.

---

## Explain It Like I'm 12

Imagine your school has many cupboards, and **each cupboard belongs to one class only**. The Class 8 cupboard can never hold Class 10 books. Trophies work exactly like that — **each trophy is permanently locked to one sport**.

The confusion happens because trophy names don't tell you the sport. "Durand" sounds like it could be anything. So instead of memorising 50 random names, we sort them into cupboards:

- **Cricket cupboard:** Ranji Trophy, Duleep Trophy, Irani Trophy, Deodhar Trophy, Vijay Hazare Trophy, Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy
- **Football cupboard:** Durand Cup, Santosh Trophy, Federation Cup, Subroto Cup, I-League / ISL trophies
- **Hockey cupboard:** Sultan Azlan Shah Cup, Aga Khan Cup, Beighton Cup, Rangaswamy Cup
- **Tennis cupboard:** Davis Cup (men's team), Billie Jean King Cup — formerly Fed Cup (women's team), Wimbledon, US Open, French Open, Australian Open
- **Badminton cupboard:** Thomas Cup (men), Uber Cup (women), All England Championship
- **Golf cupboard:** Ryder Cup, Walker Cup
- **Table Tennis cupboard:** Swaythling Cup (men's team), Corbillon Cup (women's team)

---

## A Daily-Life Example

Think of how you never confuse your **school bus** with your **school building** — because you use them differently every day. Now give each trophy a daily "use":

- **Durand Cup = Football.** Trick: *"Durand line is a border — footballers cross the LINE to score a goal."* (Durand Cup, started 1888 in Shimla, is Asia's oldest football tournament.)
- **Santosh Trophy = Football too.** Both big Indian football trophies start with a "person's name" feel.
- **Ranji = Cricket.** Named after Ranjitsinhji, the legendary cricketer — the name itself screams cricket.
- **Thomas Cup = Badminton.** Trick: *"Thomas the Tank Engine SHUTTLES back and forth — like a shuttlecock."*
- **Davis Cup = Tennis.** Trick: *"Davis serves an ACE."*

---

## The Unforgettable Trick: "CR-F-H-T-B" Cupboard Song

Sing it once, remember forever:

> **"Ranji plays Cricket, Durand kicks Football,**
> **Aga Khan rides Hockey, Davis swings Tennis,**
> **Thomas flies Badminton — five cupboards, no confusion at all!"**

And the gender trap the examiner loves:
- **Thomas Cup = Men's** badminton team event; **Uber Cup = Women's**. (Trick: *"U for Uber, U for ladies' Universe."*)
- **Davis Cup = Men's** tennis; **Billie Jean King Cup (Fed Cup) = Women's**.
