Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Who was the first Indian woman to win an Olympic medal?
(a) P.T. Usha (b) Karnam Malleswari (c) Saina Nehwal (d) Mary Kom
Answer: (b)
Solution: P.T. Usha is the tempting wrong choice because she came closest earlier, but she finished fourth at the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics and won no medal. Karnam Malleswari won bronze in weightlifting at the 2000 Sydney Olympics, so the answer is Karnam Malleswari.
Source: PYQ-style

77. The Durand Cup, one of the oldest football tournaments in the world, is associated with which country?
(a) India (b) Brazil (c) England (d) Spain
Answer: (a)
Solution: England is tempting because the tournament was started by a British officer, Sir Mortimer Durand, in 1888, but it has always been held in India (Shimla first, later Kolkata). It is India's oldest football tournament, so the answer is India.
Source: PYQ-style

78. How many players are there in a standard field hockey team on the ground at one time?
(a) 9 (b) 11 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ten is tempting because people subtract the goalkeeper, but the count of 11 already includes the goalkeeper. A hockey team fields 11 players, so the answer is 11.
Source: NCERT Class 6–10 General Knowledge

79. The term 'LBW' is used in which sport?
(a) Football (b) Hockey (c) Cricket (d) Tennis
Answer: (c)
Solution: Football is tempting because it also involves leg contact with a ball, but LBW stands for 'Leg Before Wicket', a mode of dismissal in cricket. So the answer is Cricket.
Source: PYQ-style

80. Which country won the first FIFA World Cup in 1930?
(a) Uruguay (b) Argentina (c) Brazil (d) Italy
Answer: (a)
Solution: Brazil is the classic trap because it has won the most World Cups, but its first title came only in 1958. Uruguay hosted and won the inaugural 1930 tournament, so the answer is Uruguay.
Source: PYQ-style

81. The Thomas Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Badminton (b) Table Tennis (c) Lawn Tennis (d) Squash
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lawn Tennis is tempting because of the similar-sounding Davis Cup, but the Davis Cup is tennis while the Thomas Cup is the world men's team championship in badminton. So the answer is Badminton.
Source: PYQ-style

82. In which year did India win its first Olympic gold medal in hockey after independence?
(a) 1952 (b) 1948 (c) 1956 (d) 1936
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1936 is the chronological trap — India did win gold in 1936, but as a British-ruled team, before independence. The first gold as an independent nation came at the 1948 London Olympics, so the answer is 1948.
Source: PYQ-style

83. The 'Dronacharya Award' in India is given for excellence in which field?
(a) Sports playing performance (b) Sports journalism (c) Sports coaching (d) Sports administration
Answer: (c)
Solution: Playing performance is tempting because the award is often confused with the Khel Ratna, but the Khel Ratna honours players while the Dronacharya Award honours coaches — named after Guru Dronacharya, the teacher. So the answer is Sports coaching.
Source: PYQ-style

84. A standard chessboard has how many squares?
(a) 64 (b) 56 (c) 48 (d) 72
Answer: (a)
Solution: A chessboard is an 8 by 8 grid, giving 64 squares. So the answer is 64.
Source: NCERT Class 6–10 General Knowledge

85. Which Indian cricketer scored the first double century in men's One Day International cricket?
(a) Virender Sehwag (b) Sachin Tendulkar (c) Rohit Sharma (d) MS Dhoni
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rohit Sharma is the trap because he holds the record of three ODI double centuries, but he was not the first. Sachin Tendulkar scored the first men's ODI double century (200 not out) against South Africa in 2010, so the answer is Sachin Tendulkar.
Source: PYQ-style

86. The Ranji Trophy is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Football (c) Badminton (d) Cricket
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Ranji Trophy is India's premier domestic first-class cricket championship, named after Ranjitsinhji. So the answer is Cricket.
Source: PYQ-style

87. In which city were the first modern Olympic Games held in 1896?
(a) Paris (b) London (c) Athens (d) Rome
Answer: (c)
Solution: Paris is tempting because the modern Olympics were revived by the Frenchman Pierre de Coubertin, but the first Games were held in Athens, Greece, honouring the ancient Olympic tradition. So the answer is Athens.
Source: NCERT Class 9–10 General Knowledge

88. How many rings are there in the Olympic symbol?
(a) Four (b) Five (c) Six (d) Seven
Answer: (b)
Solution: The five interlaced rings represent the five inhabited continents united by sport. So the answer is Five.
Source: NCERT Class 6–10 General Knowledge

89. Which of the following is the highest sports honour of India?
(a) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (b) Dronacharya Award (c) Arjuna Award (d) Dhyan Chand Award
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Arjuna Award is the trap because it is older and very famous, but it ranks below the Khel Ratna. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (earlier Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna) is India's highest sporting honour, so the answer is Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award.
Source: PYQ-style

90. The term 'Deuce' is used in which sport?
(a) Cricket (b) Golf (c) Boxing (d) Tennis
Answer: (d)
Solution: Deuce denotes a tied score (40–40) in tennis, after which a player must win two consecutive points. So the answer is Tennis.
Source: PYQ-style

91. Milkha Singh, known as the 'Flying Sikh', was associated with which event?
(a) Long jump (b) Javelin throw (c) Marathon (d) 400 metres sprint
Answer: (d)
Solution: Marathon is tempting because of his long career and the film depiction, but Milkha Singh was a sprinter who finished fourth in the 400 metres at the 1960 Rome Olympics. So the answer is 400 metres sprint.
Source: PYQ-style

92. Which country has won the most Olympic gold medals in men's field hockey?
(a) Pakistan (b) Netherlands (c) India (d) Germany
Answer: (c)
Solution: Pakistan is the trap because of its strong hockey rivalry with India, but India has won 8 Olympic gold medals in men's hockey, the most by any country. So the answer is India.
Source: PYQ-style

93. The Santosh Trophy is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Cricket (c) Football (d) Kabaddi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Santosh Trophy is India's premier inter-state football championship for senior men. So the answer is Football.
Source: PYQ-style

94. In badminton, how many points make a standard game under the rally scoring system?
(a) 15 (b) 11 (c) 25 (d) 21
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fifteen is the trap because the old scoring system used 15 points, but since 2006 a badminton game is played to 21 points under rally scoring. So the answer is 21.
Source: PYQ-style

95. Who was the first Indian to win an individual Olympic gold medal?
(a) Rajyavardhan Singh Rathore (b) Vijay Kumar (c) Gagan Narang (d) Abhinav Bindra
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rajyavardhan Rathore is tempting because he won a shooting medal (silver) earlier in 2004, but it was not gold. Abhinav Bindra won gold in the 10m air rifle at the 2008 Beijing Olympics, so the answer is Abhinav Bindra.
Source: PYQ-style

96. The 'Grand Slam' in tennis consists of how many tournaments?
(a) Three (b) Four (c) Five (d) Six
Answer: (b)
Solution: The four Grand Slam tournaments are the Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon and US Open. So the answer is Four.
Source: NCERT Class 6–10 General Knowledge

97. Which of the following statements about Major Dhyan Chand is correct?
(a) He won an Olympic medal in wrestling (b) He was the first Indian to win an individual Olympic gold (c) He captained the Indian cricket team (d) He won three Olympic gold medals in hockey for India
Answer: (d)
Solution: The individual-gold statement is the almost-correct trap — that distinction belongs to Abhinav Bindra, not Dhyan Chand. Dhyan Chand won Olympic hockey gold in 1928, 1932 and 1936, so the answer is the statement that he won three Olympic gold medals in hockey for India.
Source: PYQ-style

98. The Nehru Cup in India was associated with which sport?
(a) Football (b) Hockey (c) Tennis (d) Golf
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hockey is tempting because India hosts several hockey tournaments, but the Nehru Cup was an international football tournament hosted by India. So the answer is Football.
Source: PYQ-style

99. In which sport did P.V. Sindhu win Olympic medals?
(a) Boxing (b) Wrestling (c) Badminton (d) Shooting
Answer: (c)
Solution: P.V. Sindhu won silver at Rio 2016 and bronze at Tokyo 2020 in badminton, becoming the first Indian woman to win two Olympic medals in consecutive Games. So the answer is Badminton.
Source: PYQ-style

100. The headquarters of the International Olympic Committee (IOC) is located in which city?
(a) Lausanne (b) Geneva (c) Paris (d) Zurich
Answer: (a)
Solution: Geneva is the classic trap because many international bodies (WHO, WTO, UN offices) are based there, but the IOC headquarters is in Lausanne, Switzerland. So the answer is Lausanne.
Source: PYQ-style
