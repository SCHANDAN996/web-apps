# 📋 Key Facts & Figures — Sports & Competitions

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| First modern Olympics | Athens, 1896 (Greece) | Confused with Paris 1900 or ancient Olympia 776 BC | Similar names / chronology |
| Olympic rings | 5 rings, designed by Pierre de Coubertin (1913) | Asked who designed — not "IOC" | Attribution trap |
| Olympic motto | "Citius, Altius, Fortius" (Faster, Higher, Stronger); "Communiter" added in 2021 | Old motto given as fully correct in older papers | Outdated fact trap |
| First Asian Games | New Delhi, 1951 | Confused with 1982 Delhi Asiad | Chronology trap |
| First Commonwealth Games | 1930, Hamilton (Canada), called British Empire Games | "London 1934" as distractor | Place trap |
| FIFA World Cup first edition | 1930, Uruguay (Uruguay won) | Brazil given as first winner | Winner trap |
| Most FIFA World Cup titles | Brazil — 5 | Germany/Italy (4 each) as distractors | Number trap |
| Cricket World Cup first edition | 1975, England; won by West Indies | 1983 (India's win) confused as first | Chronology trap |
| India's first Cricket World Cup | 1983, under Kapil Dev | Confused with 2011 (Dhoni) | Captain trap |
| Hockey World Cup first edition | 1971, Barcelona; won by Pakistan | India 1975 win confused as first edition | Chronology trap |
| India's only Hockey World Cup title | 1975, Kuala Lumpur | "India has 2 titles" is false | Number trap |
| India's Olympic hockey golds | 8 gold medals (1928–1980) | Counted as 6 or 10 | Number trap |
| First Indian individual Olympic gold | Abhinav Bindra, Shooting, Beijing 2008 | Confused with Neeraj Chopra (Tokyo 2020, first athletics gold) | "First" trap |
| First Indian woman Olympic medallist | Karnam Malleswari, Weightlifting, Sydney 2000 (bronze) | P.T. Usha (never won a medal) as distractor | Near-miss trap |
| First Indian at Olympics | Norman Pritchard, 1900 Paris, 2 silver medals | Called "British athlete only" — he represented India | Identity trap |
| Dhyan Chand | Hockey legend; 3 Olympic golds (1928, 1932, 1936); National Sports Day on his birthday (29 August) | National Sports Day date confused with 26 January etc. | Date trap |
| Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award | India's highest sporting honour; renamed from Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna in 2021 | Old name given as current | Renaming trap |
| Arjuna Award | Instituted 1961, for consistent outstanding performance | Year confused with Dronacharya (1985) | Year trap |
| Dronacharya Award | For coaches, instituted 1985 | Swapped with Arjuna Award purpose | Purpose swap |
| Grand Slam tennis tournaments | Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open | "Olympics tennis" added as 5th | List trap |
| Wimbledon surface | Grass | French Open = clay; don't swap | Surface swap |
| French Open venue | Roland Garros, Paris (clay) | Confused with Wimbledon (London) | Venue trap |
| Santosh Trophy | Indian football (state teams) | Confused with Durand Cup (oldest Indian football tournament, 1888) | Tournament swap |
| Ranji Trophy | Domestic cricket (first-class), since 1934–35 | Confused with Irani/Duleep Trophy | Tournament swap |
| Thomas Cup | Badminton (men's team); India won first title in 2022 | Uber Cup = women's team; swapped | Gender swap |
| Uber Cup | Badminton (women's team) | Swapped with Thomas Cup | Gender swap |
| Davis Cup | Tennis (men's team) | Fed Cup (now Billie Jean King Cup) = women's | Gender swap |
| Sultan Azlan Shah Cup | Hockey tournament (Malaysia) | Sport confused with football | Sport trap |
| Neeraj Chopra | Javelin gold, Tokyo 2020 Olympics — India's first athletics Olympic gold | Called "first individual gold" (that is Bindra) | "First" trap |
| Mithali Raj | Highest run-scorer in women's international cricket (ODIs) | Confused with Sachin Tendulkar (men's) | Category trap |
| Viswanathan Anand | India's first Grandmaster (1988); 5-time World Chess Champion | "First GM" confused with later GMs | "First" trap |
| Mary Kom | 6-time World Boxing Champion; Olympic bronze (London 2012) | Olympic medal colour given as silver/gold | Medal-colour trap |
| P.V. Sindhu | Badminton — Olympic silver (Rio 2016), bronze (Tokyo 2020); World Champion 2019 | Medals swapped between editions | Edition swap |
| Saina Nehwal | First Indian woman to win Olympic badminton medal (bronze, London 2012) | Confused with Sindhu | "First" trap |
| IPL started | 2008; first winner Rajasthan Royals | Mumbai Indians (most titles) given as first winner | Winner trap |
| Hockey India League / national game myth | India has NO officially declared national game | "Hockey is the national game" — common false statement | Myth trap |
| Olympic 2024 host | Paris, France | Confused with Los Angeles 2028 | Edition trap |
| Khelo India | Government programme for grassroots sports, launched 2018 | Confused with "Fit India Movement" (2019) | Scheme swap |
| Target Olympic Podium Scheme (TOPS) | Support for elite athletes, started 2014 | Purpose confused with Khelo India | Purpose swap |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Category | Question Type | Time | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tournament first editions & hosts | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Trophy–sport matching | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Award names, years, purposes | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Olympic facts (motto, rings, hosts) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Indian "firsts" (Bindra, Malleswari, Pritchard) | Fact recall with trap check | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Statement-based questions (2–3 statements on awards/tournaments) | Analytical — verify each statement | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt only if sure |
| Chronology ordering (World Cups, Olympics editions) | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt only if sure |
| Match-the-following (player–sport–award) | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt only if sure |
| Negative questions ("Which is NOT correct") | Analytical — read twice | 45 sec | ❌ Skip if any doubt |
