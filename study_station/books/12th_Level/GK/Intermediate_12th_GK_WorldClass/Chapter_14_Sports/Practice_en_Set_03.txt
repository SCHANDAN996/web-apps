Questions 51–75: Medium

51. The Durand Cup, one of the oldest football tournaments in the world, is associated with which country?
(a) England (b) India (c) Spain (d) Brazil
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students pick England because football's oldest competitions (like the FA Cup) are English, but the Durand Cup was started in 1888 in Shimla by Sir Mortimer Durand and is India's oldest football tournament, so the answer is India.
Source: PYQ-style

52. In which sport is the term "googly" used?
(a) Cricket (b) Tennis (c) Hockey (d) Badminton
Answer: (a)
Solution: A googly is a type of delivery bowled by a leg-spin bowler that turns in the opposite direction to the batsman's expectation; it belongs to cricket, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

53. The Thomas Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Badminton (b) Table Tennis (c) Lawn Tennis (d) Squash
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often confuse the Thomas Cup with the Davis Cup; the Davis Cup is for lawn tennis, while the Thomas Cup is the world men's team championship in badminton, so the answer is Badminton.
Source: PYQ-style

54. How many players are there in a standard volleyball team on the court?
(a) 5 (b) 7 (c) 6 (d) 11
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 7, which is the number of players in a kabaddi team on the mat; a volleyball team fields 6 players on the court, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

55. The term "LBW" is used in which game?
(a) Football (b) Hockey (c) Cricket (d) Baseball
Answer: (c)
Solution: LBW stands for "Leg Before Wicket", a mode of dismissal in cricket when the ball hits the batsman's leg in line with the stumps, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

56. The first modern Olympic Games were held in which city?
(a) Athens (b) London (c) Paris (d) Rome
Answer: (a)
Solution: A common trap is Paris, since the International Olympic Committee was founded there in 1894, but the first modern Olympics were held in 1896 in Athens, Greece, so the answer is Athens.
Source: PYQ-style

57. In hockey, how many players of one team are on the field during play?
(a) 9 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: Students sometimes answer 10 by forgetting the goalkeeper; a hockey team has 11 players on the field including the goalkeeper, so the answer is 11.
Source: PYQ-style

58. The Santosh Trophy is associated with which sport in India?
(a) Football (b) Hockey (c) Cricket (d) Kabaddi
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Santosh Trophy is India's premier inter-state football championship, run by the All India Football Federation, so the answer is Football.
Source: PYQ-style

59. Which of the following terms is NOT associated with boxing?
(a) Silly point (b) Uppercut (c) Knockout (d) Ring
Answer: (a)
Solution: Knockout, uppercut and ring are all boxing terms; "silly point" is a fielding position in cricket, so the answer is Silly point.
Source: PYQ-style

60. The Ranji Trophy is related to which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Cricket (c) Football (d) Tennis
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Ranji Trophy, started in 1934–35 and named after Ranjitsinhji, is India's premier domestic first-class cricket championship, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: PYQ-style

61. In which sport did Milkha Singh represent India?
(a) Wrestling (b) Boxing (c) Athletics (d) Swimming
Answer: (c)
Solution: Milkha Singh, known as the "Flying Sikh", was a sprinter who famously finished fourth in the 400 metres at the 1960 Rome Olympics; he was an athlete, so the answer is Athletics.
Source: PYQ-style

62. The term "checkmate" belongs to which game?
(a) Bridge (b) Billiards (c) Chess (d) Polo
Answer: (c)
Solution: Checkmate is the position in chess where the king is under attack and cannot escape, ending the game, so the answer is Chess.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

63. The Federation Cup in India is associated with which sport?
(a) Badminton (b) Football (c) Hockey (d) Basketball
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Federation Cup was a major Indian football knockout tournament; do not confuse it with the Fed Cup, which is a women's tennis team event, so the answer is Football.
Source: PYQ-style

64. How many rings are there on the Olympic flag?
(a) 4 (b) 7 (c) 6 (d) 5
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Olympic flag has five interlaced rings representing the five inhabited continents united by sport, so the answer is 5.
Source: PYQ-style

65. "Dribbling" is a skill used in which of the following pairs of sports?
(a) Swimming and Diving (b) Cricket and Baseball (c) Chess and Bridge (d) Hockey and Football
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dribbling means moving the ball or puck along with controlled touches; it is a core skill in both hockey and football, so the answer is Hockey and Football.
Source: PYQ-style

66. The Davis Cup is awarded in which sport?
(a) Badminton (b) Table Tennis (c) Lawn Tennis (d) Golf
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap here is badminton because of the Thomas Cup; the Davis Cup is the premier international men's team event in lawn tennis, so the answer is Lawn Tennis.
Source: PYQ-style

67. In cricket, what is the maximum number of overs bowled by one bowler in a One Day International (50 overs)?
(a) 8 (b) 15 (c) 12 (d) 10
Answer: (d)
Solution: Some students pick 12 thinking of T20 proportions, but in a 50-over ODI no bowler may bowl more than one-fifth of the overs, i.e. 10 overs, so the answer is 10.
Source: PYQ-style

68. Which country is credited with originating the game of kabaddi as an organised sport?
(a) India (b) Pakistan (c) Bangladesh (d) Iran
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kabaddi has ancient roots in the Indian subcontinent and was developed as an organised competitive sport in India, which also dominates the Kabaddi World Cup, so the answer is India.
Source: PYQ-style

69. The term "deuce" is used in which sport?
(a) Cricket (b) Golf (c) Hockey (d) Tennis
Answer: (d)
Solution: Deuce is the tennis score when both players reach 40–40 in a game; it is not a cricket or hockey term, so the answer is Tennis.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

70. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of these events?
(a) First modern Olympics – Durand Cup – Ranji Trophy (b) Ranji Trophy – Durand Cup – First modern Olympics (c) Durand Cup – First modern Olympics – Ranji Trophy (d) First modern Olympics – Ranji Trophy – Durand Cup
Answer: (c)
Solution: This is a chronological-confusion trap: the Durand Cup began in 1888, the first modern Olympics were held in 1896, and the Ranji Trophy started in 1934–35, so the answer is Durand Cup – First modern Olympics – Ranji Trophy.
Source: PYQ-style

71. In which sport is the "Sultan Azlan Shah Cup" contested?
(a) Football (b) Hockey (c) Cricket (d) Badminton
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Sultan Azlan Shah Cup is an annual international field hockey tournament held in Malaysia, named after a former Malaysian king who was a hockey patron, so the answer is Hockey.
Source: PYQ-style

72. The term "penalty corner" is associated with which game?
(a) Football (b) Hockey (c) Basketball (d) Volleyball
Answer: (b)
Solution: Football has the penalty kick, which tempts many students, but the "penalty corner" is a specific set-piece awarded in field hockey for defensive fouls inside the circle, so the answer is Hockey.
Source: PYQ-style

73. Which Indian woman won a bronze medal in badminton at the London 2012 Olympics?
(a) P. V. Sindhu (b) Ashwini Ponnappa (c) Jwala Gutta (d) Saina Nehwal

Solution: The trap is P. V. Sindhu, who won silver at Rio 2016 and bronze at Tokyo 2020; at London 2012 it was Saina Nehwal who won the bronze, so the answer is Saina Nehwal.

74. A standard game of chess begins with how many pieces on the board in total?
(a) 16 (b) 32 (c) 24 (d) 40
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 16, which is the number of pieces per player; with two players the board starts with 32 pieces in total, so the answer is 32.
Source: PYQ-style

(a) Football (b) Cricket (c) Hockey (d) Athletics
Answer: (a)
