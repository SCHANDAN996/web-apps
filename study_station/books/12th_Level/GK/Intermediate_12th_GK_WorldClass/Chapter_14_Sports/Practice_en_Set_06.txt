126. Which of the following statements about the Olympic Games is correct?
(a) The Olympic motto "Citius, Altius, Fortius" was adopted in 1896 (b) The five Olympic rings represent the five founding nations of the modern Olympics (c) The Olympic flag was first hoisted at the 1896 Athens Games (d) The five Olympic rings represent the five participating continents, and the rings were designed by Pierre de Coubertin
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims the rings represent founding nations — a common trap; the rings symbolise the five inhabited continents united by sport. The flag was first hoisted at the 1920 Antwerp Games, not 1896, and the motto was proposed by Coubertin in 1894 and adopted officially later. The rings were designed by Pierre de Coubertin in 1913, so the answer is the statement about the five continents and Coubertin.
