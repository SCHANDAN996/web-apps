Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Who was the first Indian woman to win a medal at the Olympic Games?
(a) Karnam Malleswari (b) P.T. Usha (c) Mary Kom (d) Saina Nehwal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students pick P.T. Usha because of her famous 1984 Los Angeles near-miss, but she finished fourth and won no medal. Karnam Malleswari won bronze in weightlifting at the Sydney 2000 Olympics, so the answer is Karnam Malleswari.
Source: PYQ-style

27. The Durand Cup, one of the oldest football tournaments in the world, is associated with which country?
(a) England (b) India (c) Spain (d) Brazil
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Durand Cup was started in 1888 in Shimla and is India's oldest football tournament, so the answer is India.
Source: PYQ-style

28. In which year did India win its first Olympic gold medal in hockey as an independent nation?
(a) 1936 (b) 1956 (c) 1952 (d) 1948
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 1936 — India did win gold then, but as British India, not as an independent nation. Independent India's first hockey gold came at the 1948 London Olympics, so the answer is 1948.
