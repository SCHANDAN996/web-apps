Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. B sits at the leftmost end. A sits third from the left. A sits second to the left of C. D sits immediately to the right of A. F is not adjacent to B. Who sits at the rightmost end?
(a) C (b) F (c) E (d) D
Answer: (b)
Solution: Picking C is tempting because A sits second to the left of C, but that only fixes C at position 5. With B at 1, A at 3, D at 4 and C at 5, the remaining seats 2 and 6 go to E and F; F cannot be next to B, so F takes seat 6. The rightmost person is F, so the answer is F.
Source: PYQ-style

27. In a family of six, P is the son of Q. R is the daughter of P. S is the wife of Q. T is the brother of P. U is the son of T. How is U related to R?
(a) Nephew (b) Brother (c) Cousin (d) Uncle
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many pick Brother because U and R are of the same generation, but their fathers T and P are brothers, not the same person. Children of two brothers are cousins, so the answer is Cousin.
Source: PYQ-style

28. Five boxes P, Q, R, S, T are stacked. R is at the top. P is immediately below R. P is above Q. Q is immediately above S. S is immediately above T. Which box is at the bottom?
(a) Q (b) P (c) T (d) S
Answer: (c)
Solution: Choosing Q is tempting since P is above Q, but the S–T pair must also fit below. The order from top to bottom is R, P, Q, S, T. The bottom box is T, so the answer is T.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit around a circular table facing the centre. P sits opposite S. Q sits between P and R. T sits opposite Q. Who sits opposite R?
(a) P (b) U (c) Q (d) T
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is picking T because T is already known to sit opposite Q. Fix P at seat 1, so S is at seat 4; Q between P and R gives Q at 2 and R at 3; T opposite Q is seat 5, leaving U at seat 6, which is opposite R at seat 3. The answer is U.
Source: PYQ-style

30. A is taller than B but shorter than C. D is taller than C. E is shorter than B. Who is the shortest?
(a) B (b) A (c) D (d) E
Answer: (d)
Solution: B looks shortest at first because E is only compared with B, but the full chain is E < B < A < C < D, so E is below everyone. The shortest is E, so the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

31. In a code, MONDAY is written as NPOEBZ. How is FRIDAY written in that code?
(a) GSJEAZ (b) GSJEBZ (c) GSJFCZ (d) HSJEBZ
Answer: (b)
Solution: Each letter shifts forward by one: F→G, R→S, I→J, D→E, A→B, Y→Z. Picking GSJEAZ is the trap from forgetting that A becomes B. The code is GSJEBZ, so the answer is GSJEBZ.
Source: PYQ-style

32. Five exams — Hindi, English, Maths, Science, Social — are held Monday to Friday. Maths is on Wednesday. English is before Hindi. Science is on the last day. English is not on Monday. Which exam is on Monday?
(a) Maths (b) Hindi (c) English (d) Social
Answer: (d)
Solution: English on Monday is the tempting trap, but the clue forbids it. Wednesday is Maths and Friday is Science; English must come before Hindi among Monday, Tuesday and Thursday with English not on Monday, so English is Tuesday and Hindi is Thursday, leaving Social on Monday. The answer is Social.
Source: PYQ-style

33. Pointing to a photo, Ravi says, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the girl related to Ravi?
(a) Aunt (b) Cousin (c) Sister (d) Mother
Answer: (c)
Solution: Cousin tempts those who miss the words "only son" — the grandfather's only son is Ravi's own father, so his daughter is Ravi's sister. The answer is Sister.
Source: PYQ-style

34. Five persons P, Q, R, S and T sit in a row facing south. P sits at the extreme left end from his own point of view. From the viewer's perspective, where does P sit?
(a) Extreme right end (b) Extreme left end (c) Exact middle (d) Second from the left
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming left and right are the same for everyone; a person facing south has his left on the viewer's right side. So P's own extreme left is the viewer's extreme right, and the answer is Extreme right end.
Source: PYQ-style

35. In a queue, Ramesh is 12th from the front and 18th from the back. How many persons are in the queue?
(a) 31 (b) 29 (c) 30 (d) 28
Answer: (b)
Solution: Adding 12 and 18 to get 30 is the classic trap — Ramesh is counted twice. Total = 12 + 18 − 1 = 29, so the answer is 29.
Source: PYQ-style

36. Six people P, Q, R, S, T, U have different heights. P is taller than Q. R is shorter than S but taller than T. U is taller than P. S is shorter than Q. Who is the tallest?
(a) P (b) S (c) U (d) R
Answer: (c)
Solution: P seems tallest until U is placed above P. The full chain is U > P > Q > S > R > T. The tallest is U, so the answer is U.
Source: PYQ-style

37. A clock shows 4:20. What is the angle between the hour and minute hands?
(a) 20° (b) 30° (c) 0° (d) 10°
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many answer 0° assuming the hands overlap at 4:20, but the hour hand has moved past 4. The minute hand is at 120° and the hour hand at 130°, a difference of 10°, so the answer is 10°.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

38. In a group, A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. E is D's mother. How is A related to D?
(a) Mother (b) Daughter (c) Granddaughter (d) Great-granddaughter
Answer: (c)
Solution: Great-granddaughter is the trap for those who count one generation too many. A is C's daughter and D is C's father, so A is D's granddaughter. The answer is Granddaughter.
Source: PYQ-style

39. Five friends W, X, Y, Z, V like five different colours among red, blue, green, yellow and pink. W likes red. X does not like blue or green. Y likes blue. Z likes neither green nor yellow. Which colour does Z like?
(a) Green (b) Blue (c) Pink (d) Yellow
Answer: (c)
Solution: Yellow is tempting because Z's dislikes push that way, but red and blue are already taken by W and Y. Z cannot take green or yellow, and red and blue are gone, so Z must like pink. The answer is Pink.
Source: PYQ-style

40. If yesterday was Wednesday, what day will it be 100 days from today?
(a) Sunday (b) Monday (c) Friday (d) Saturday
Answer: (d)
Solution: Today is Thursday, and 100 divided by 7 leaves a remainder of 2, so the day is Thursday + 2 = Saturday. Forgetting the remainder step leads to Friday; the answer is Saturday.
Source: PYQ-style

41. In a row of students facing north, K is 7th from the left and L is 9th from the right. They interchange positions; now K is 11th from the left. How many students are in the row?
(a) 19 (b) 18 (c) 20 (d) 21
Answer: (a)
Solution: After swapping, K takes L's place, so that position is 11th from the left and 9th from the right. Total = 11 + 9 − 1 = 19. Using K's original 7th position gives the wrong total; the answer is 19.
Source: PYQ-style

42. A man walks 4 km east, turns left and walks 3 km, then turns left again and walks 4 km. How far and in which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) 3 km south (b) 3 km north (c) 7 km north (d) 4 km west
Answer: (b)
Solution: The two 4 km legs cancel each other since they are in opposite directions, leaving only the 3 km northward leg. Adding all legs to get 7 km is the trap; the answer is 3 km north.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

43. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 3 km east (b) 8 km east (c) 3 km west (d) 5 km south
Answer: (a)
Solution: The two 5 km legs cancel each other, leaving only the 3 km eastward leg. Answering 8 km comes from wrongly adding the legs together; the answer is 3 km east.

44. In a certain code, TEACHER is written as VGCEJGT. How is STUDENT written in that code?
(a) UVWFGPV (b) UVWFGPU (c) UVWFGQV (d) UVWFGPW
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each letter shifts forward by two: S→U, T→V, U→W, D→F, E→G, N→P, T→V. The wrong options alter the last one or two letters; the correct code is UVWFGPV, so the answer is UVWFGPV.

45. Four husbands sit in seats 1 to 4 of a row, and their four wives sit in seats 5 to 8 of a parallel row facing them, so that each husband faces his own wife. Mr. A sits in seat 2. Who sits directly in front of Mr. A?
(a) Mrs. B (b) Mrs. A (c) Mrs. C (d) Mrs. D
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is hunting for extra clues about the other couples, but the rule that each husband faces his own wife settles it directly. Whoever sits in front of Mr. A must be his wife, so the answer is Mrs. A.

46. P, Q, R, S, T are five books on a shelf. Q is at the rightmost end. S is immediately to the right of P. R is immediately to the right of S. T is immediately to the left of Q. Which book is at the leftmost end?
(a) S (b) T (c) R (d) P
Answer: (d)
Solution: T being just left of Q tempts placing T first, but the P–S–R block of three must fit before it. The only order satisfying all clues is P, S, R, T, Q. The leftmost book is P, so the answer is P.
Source: PYQ-style

47. In a family, X and Y are brothers. Z is X's sister. W is Z's mother. V is W's husband. How is V related to Y?
(a) Father (b) Uncle (c) Grandfather (d) Brother
Answer: (a)
Solution: Uncle is tempting when solvers miss that W is the mother of all the siblings and V is her husband. V is the father of X, Y and Z, so the answer is Father.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Seven persons sit in a row facing south. G is third from the left end. H is to the immediate right of G. I is at the right end. Who is second from the right end?
(a) H (b) G (c) I (d) Cannot be determined
Answer: (d)
Solution: Facing south reverses left-right intuition, but the real issue is that the middle persons are unconstrained, so the seat second from the right can be taken by more than one person. The answer is Cannot be determined.
Source: PYQ-style

49. A cube is painted red on all faces and cut into 64 equal smaller cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 24 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 16
Answer: (a)
Solution: 12 is the trap — that counts the edges, not the cubes. Each of the 12 edges of a 4×4×4 cube has 2 middle cubes with exactly two painted faces, giving 12 × 2 = 24. The answer is 24.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

50. In a class of 40 students, Sita ranks 8th from the top. Gita is 5 ranks below Sita. What is Gita's rank from the bottom?
(a) 28 (b) 29 (c) 27 (d) 26
Answer: (a)
Solution: Gita is 13th from the top, so her rank from the bottom is 40 − 13 + 1 = 28. Forgetting the +1 gives the trap value 27, but the correct count includes Gita herself, so the answer is 28.
Source: PYQ-style
