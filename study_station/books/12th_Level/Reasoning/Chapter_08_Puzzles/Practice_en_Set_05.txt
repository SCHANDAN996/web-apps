101. Five friends A, B, C, D and E are sitting in a row facing north. A sits at one of the ends. B sits second to the right of A. C sits next to B. D does not sit next to A. Who sits in the middle?
(a) C (b) B (c) D (d) E
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is placing A in the middle of the row; A must be at an end. With A at the left end, B is at position 3, C next to B at position 4, D not next to A so D takes position 5, and E fills position 2: A, E, B, C, D. The middle seat is position 3, so the answer is B.
Source: PYQ-style

102. In a family of six, P is the father of Q. R is the mother of Q. S is the brother of Q. T is the wife of S. U is the daughter of S. How is U related to P?
(a) Granddaughter (b) Daughter (c) Sister (d) Niece
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "niece" — U is the daughter of S, and S is the son of P (since S is the brother of P's child Q). The child of one's son is a granddaughter, so the answer is Granddaughter.

103. Six boxes are stacked one above another. Box M is immediately above box N. Box O is at the top. Box P is immediately below box N. Box Q is above box R but below box P. Which box is at the bottom?
(a) P (b) Q (c) R (d) N
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Q, which sits just above R but is not at the bottom. The full order from top is O, M, N, P, Q, R, so the answer is R.

106. In a code, if MONDAY is written as NPOEBZ, how is FRIDAY written?
(a) GSKEBZ (b) GSJEAZ (c) HSJEBZ (d) GSJEBZ
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each letter shifts +1: F→G, R→S, I→J, D→E, A→B, Y→Z. The trap is GSJEAZ, where the solver forgets to shift the A, so the answer is GSJEBZ.

107. Five exams — Hindi, English, Maths, Science, Social — are held Monday to Friday. Maths is on Wednesday. English is before Hindi. Science is on Friday. Social is not on Monday. Which exam is on Monday?

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Social, but it is barred from Monday. Wednesday = Maths, Friday = Science; of the remaining days, English must come before Hindi, so Monday = English, Tuesday = Hindi, Thursday = Social. So the answer is English.

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is R, which is opposite Q, not opposite T. Numbering seats 1–6: P(1), S(4) opposite P, Q(2) next to P, R(5) opposite Q; T must sit next to S, and seat 5 is taken, so T is at seat 3 and U fills seat 6. The seat opposite T(3) is seat 6, so the answer is U.

110. In a row of students, Ramesh is 12th from the left and Suresh is 18th from the right. If they interchange positions, Ramesh becomes 20th from the left. How many students are in the row?
(a) 36 (b) 39 (c) 37 (d) 38
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 38, from adding 20 + 18 without subtracting the double-counted person. After interchange Ramesh stands at Suresh's place: 20th from left and 18th from right, so total = 20 + 18 − 1 = 37. So the answer is 37.
Source: PYQ-style

111. A clock shows 4:20. What is the angle between the hour and minute hands?
(a) 0° (b) 20° (c) 10° (d) 30°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 20°, from ignoring the hour hand's drift past 4. Minute hand at 20 minutes = 120°; hour hand = 4×30 + 20×0.5 = 130°; difference = 10°. So the answer is 10°.
Source: PYQ-style

113. If 1st January 2001 was a Monday, what day was 1st January 2005?
(a) Saturday (b) Friday (c) Sunday (d) Thursday
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Friday, from counting leap year 2004 as a normal year. Odd days: 2001 = 1, 2002 = 1, 2003 = 1, 2004 = 2; total 5; Monday + 5 = Saturday. So the answer is Saturday.

114. In a certain code, TEACHER is written as VGCEJGT. How is STUDENT written?
(a) UVWFGPW (b) UVXFGPV (c) UVWFGPV (d) UVWFGQV
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each letter shifts +2: S→U, T→V, U→W, D→F, E→G, N→P, T→V. The traps shift one letter wrongly (last letter, or N, or D), so the answer is UVWFGPV.

115. Five friends P, Q, R, S, T like five different colours — red, blue, green, yellow and pink. P likes red. R likes blue. T likes yellow. Q does not like green. Which colour does S like?
(a) Pink (b) Green (c) Blue (d) Yellow
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is pink — but Q cannot take green, so Q must take pink. Red, blue and yellow are already taken by P, R and T, leaving green for S. So the answer is Green.

116. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 8 km, from adding the legs instead of cancelling. North 5 and South 5 cancel out, leaving only the 3 km eastward leg. So the answer is 3 km East.

123. In a code language, "pit na som" means "bring me water", "na jo tod" means "water is life", "tub od pit" means "give me toy". Which word means "me"?
(a) som (b) jo (c) pit (d) na
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is na — sentences 1 and 2 share "na" and the common word "water", so na = water. Sentences 1 and 3 share "pit" and the common word "me", so pit = me. So the answer is pit.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Mr. C, who is fixed at seat 2, not seat 5. Mr. A is at seat 1, Mr. C at seat 2 and Mrs. A at seat 4, but the clues fix nothing about seat 5, to the right of Mrs. A. So the answer is Cannot be determined.

125. A cube is painted red on all faces and cut into 64 equal smaller cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 12 (b) 24 (c) 8 (d) 32
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 12, from counting only one cube per edge. A 4×4×4 cube has 12 edges, and each edge contributes 4 − 2 = 2 cubes with exactly two painted faces: 12 × 2 = 24. So the answer is 24.
