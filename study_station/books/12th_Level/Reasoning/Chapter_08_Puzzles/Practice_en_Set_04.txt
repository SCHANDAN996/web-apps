Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Five friends P, Q, R, S and T are sitting in a row facing north. P sits at the left end. R sits exactly between P and T. S sits to the immediate right of T. Who sits at the right end?
(a) R (b) S (c) Q (d) T
Answer: (c)
Solution: Starting from the left end: P, then R between P and T gives P–R–T, and S immediately right of T gives P–R–T–S. The tempting trap is S, but S sits fourth; the only remaining friend Q takes the fifth seat at the right end, so the answer is Q.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit around a circular table facing the centre. P sits opposite S. Q sits to the immediate left of P. R sits opposite Q. T sits to the immediate right of S. Who sits opposite T?
(a) Q (b) U (c) R (d) P
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fix P at the top with S opposite. Q is immediately left of P, and R sits opposite Q, so four seats are fixed. T is immediately right of S, which fills the fifth seat and leaves only one seat for U, directly across from T. The trap is R, who sits near S but beside T rather than opposite; the person opposite T is U, so the answer is U.
Source: PYQ-style

78. In a family of six, M is the father of N. N is the sister of O. O is the husband of P. Q is the daughter of P. How is M related to Q?
(a) Brother (b) Uncle (c) Grandfather (d) Father
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is Uncle, because N is the sister of Q's father O — but the question asks about M, not N. M is the father of N and O, so M is the father of Q's father, which makes M the grandfather, so the answer is Grandfather.

79. Seven boxes are stacked one above another. Box A is immediately above box B. Box C is at the top. Box D is immediately below box C. Box E is below box B. Box F is between E and G, with G at the bottom. Which box is third from the top?
(a) D (b) A (c) B (d) F
Answer: (b)
Solution: From the top: C is 1st and D is 2nd. A is immediately above B with E below B, giving C, D, A, B, E, and then F between E and G at the bottom: C, D, A, B, E, F, G. The trap is D, which is second, not third; the third box from the top is A, so the answer is A.

Answer: (c)
Solution: B is C's mother and D is C's father, so B and D are husband and wife. The trap Uncle describes A's relation to C, not to D; A is the brother of D's wife, making A the brother-in-law of D, so the answer is Brother-in-law.

82. Eight persons sit in two rows of four facing each other. Row 1 (facing south): P, Q, R, S. Row 2 (facing north): W, X, Y, Z. P faces X. Q sits to the right of P. Y sits to the left of X. Who faces Q?
(a) W (b) X (c) Y (d) Z
Answer: (c)
Solution: Row 1 faces south, so P's right is the viewer's left, and Q sits beside P on that side. Row 2 faces north, so X's left is the viewer's left as well, and Y sits beside X on that side. The trap is X, who faces P, not Q; aligning the rows, the seat opposite Q is occupied by Y, so the answer is Y.

(a) Hindi (b) Geography (c) English (d) History
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixed: Monday Science, Tuesday English, Wednesday Maths, Saturday History. Hindi is immediately before History, so Hindi is on Friday, and Geography fills Thursday. The trap is Geography, which is the only other free subject but belongs on Thursday, so the answer is Hindi.

85. Five cars — Red, Blue, Green, White, Black — are parked in a row. The Red car is at the left end. The Blue car is next to the Red car. The Green car is in the middle. The White car is between the Green car and the Black car. Which car is at the right end?
(a) Green (b) Black (c) Blue (d) White
Answer: (b)
Solution: From the left: Red is 1st, Blue next to Red is 2nd, and Green in the middle is 3rd. White must sit between Green and Black, so White is 4th and Black is 5th. The trap is White, which sits fourth; the Black car is at the right end, so the answer is Black.

Answer: (a)
Solution: Fix A and D opposite each other. B is immediately left of A, and C opposite B fixes four seats. 88. Ramesh walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km. His friend Suresh starts from the same point, walks 3 km east and stops. How far apart are they?
(a) 3 km (b) 6 km (c) 5 km (d) 0 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ramesh goes 5 km north, 3 km east, then 5 km south, ending exactly 3 km east of the start. Suresh also stops 3 km east of the start. The trap is adding the legs to get 6 km; both men finish at the same point, so the distance between them is 0 km, and the answer is 0 km.

95. In a row of 40 students, Asha is 12th from the left and Beena is 15th from the right. How many students sit between them?
(a) 13 (b) 14 (c) 12 (d) 11
Answer: (a)
Solution: Beena's position from the left = 40 − 15 + 1 = 26th. Students between the 12th and 26th positions = 26 − 12 − 1 = 13. The trap is subtracting directly to get 14, which counts one of the two girls; the correct count is 13, so the answer is 13.

97. P, Q, R, S and T each own a different vehicle: car, bike, scooter, bus, cycle. P owns the car. Q does not own the bike or the scooter. R owns the scooter. S does not own the bike. What does T own?
(a) Bus (b) Bike (c) Cycle (d) Scooter
Answer: (b)
Solution: P has the car and R has the scooter. Q cannot have the bike or scooter, so Q takes the bus or cycle; S cannot have the bike, so S takes the other of bus or cycle. The trap is giving T the bus or cycle, but those go to Q and S; the bike is left for T, so the answer is Bike.

98. In a queue, Mohan is 8th from the front and Sohan is 12th from the back. They interchange their positions, and Mohan becomes 15th from the front. How many persons are in the queue?
(a) 25 (b) 27 (c) 24 (d) 26
Answer: (d)
Solution: After swapping, Mohan stands at Sohan's old place, which is 15th from the front and 12th from the back. Total = 15 + 12 − 1 = 26. The trap is adding without subtracting the double-counted person to get 27; the queue has 26 persons, so the answer is 26.

(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 5 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: K = 2, L = 3, M = 6, N = 1 (the only floor below K), and O = 5 (immediately below M). The trap is floor 5, but that belongs to O; the only floor left for P is 4, so the answer is 4.

100. A meeting starts when the hour hand is between 2 and 3 and the minute hand is at 6. It ends after 75 minutes. At what time does it end?
(a) 3:30 (b) 4:00 (c) 3:45 (d) 4:15
Answer: (c)
Solution: The minute hand at 6 means 30 minutes past the hour, and the hour hand between 2 and 3 confirms a start of 2:30. Adding 75 minutes (1 hour 15 minutes) gives 3:45. The trap 4:15 comes from treating 75 minutes as 1 hour 45 minutes; the meeting ends at 3:45, so the answer is 3:45.
Source: PYQ-style
