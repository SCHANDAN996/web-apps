1. Five friends A, B, C, D and E are sitting in a row facing north. A sits at the left end. B sits second to the right of A. C sits at the right end. D sits immediately to the left of C. Who sits in the middle?
(a) A (b) B (c) D (d) E
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is choosing D, which sits 4th, not 3rd. From the left: A is 1st, B is 3rd, C is 5th, D is 4th, so E fills the 2nd seat and the middle (3rd) seat belongs to B, so the answer is B.
Source: PYQ-style

2. In a family, Ravi is the son of Meena. Meena is the wife of Suresh. How is Suresh related to Ravi?
(a) Uncle (b) Father (c) Brother (d) Grandfather
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option Uncle tempts those who overthink the chain, but Meena is Ravi's mother and Suresh is Meena's husband, so the direct link makes Suresh Ravi's father, so the answer is Father.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Six people P, Q, R, S, T and U are sitting around a circular table facing the centre. P is opposite S. Q is to the immediate left of P. R is to the immediate right of P. Who sits between Q and R?
(a) T (b) P (c) U (d) S
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering S because S is opposite P, but opposite does not mean between. Q is on P's immediate left and R is on P's immediate right, so the order is Q, P, R with P seated between Q and R, so the answer is P.

4. Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Mother (b) Aunt (c) Sister (d) Cousin
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option Cousin attracts those who add an extra generation. Grandfather's only son is the man's father, and the father's daughter is the man's sister, so the answer is Sister.

5. Five boxes are stacked one above another. Box M is at the bottom. Box N is immediately above M. Box O is at the top. Box P is immediately below O. Which box is in the middle?
(a) N (b) O (c) P (d) Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is picking P, which is fourth, not third. From bottom the order is M, N, _, P, O, and the only remaining box Q fills the third (middle) position, so the answer is Q.

7. In a row of students, Ram is 7th from the left and 12th from the right. How many students are in the row?
(a) 17 (b) 19 (c) 18 (d) 20
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding 7 + 12 = 19 and forgetting that Ram is counted twice. Total = left position + right position − 1 = 7 + 12 − 1 = 18, so the answer is 18.

8. Six persons sit in a row facing north. X sits third from the left. Y sits at the right end. Z sits between X and Y with exactly one person between X and Z. What is Z's position from the left?
(a) 5th (b) 4th (c) 3rd (d) 6th
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is placing Z at 4th, which leaves no one between X and Z. X is 3rd and Y is 6th; one person between X and Z means Z is 5th with the 4th seat between them, so the answer is 5th.

9. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 5 km North (b) 3 km East (c) 3 km West (d) 8 km East
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding all distances or picking 8 km by combining legs wrongly. The two 5 km legs cancel out, leaving only the 3 km eastward leg, so the answer is 3 km East.

(a) GBUHFS (b) GBUIFR (c) EZSGDQ (d) GBUIFS
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option GBUHFS swaps the last two letters. Each letter shifts one step forward: F→G, A→B, T→U, H→I, E→F, R→S, giving GBUIFS, so the answer is GBUIFS.

13. In a queue, Seema is 9th from the front and Reema is 14th from the back. If they interchange positions, Seema becomes 17th from the front. How many girls are in the queue?
(a) 29 (b) 30 (c) 31 (d) 32
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using Seema's original 9th position, which gives a wrong total. After interchange, Seema takes Reema's place: 17th from front and 14th from back, so total = 17 + 14 − 1 = 30, so the answer is 30.

14. Eight persons sit around a circular table facing the centre. K sits opposite M. L sits second to the right of K. N sits second to the right of M. Who sits opposite L?
(a) K (b) M (c) Cannot be determined (d) N
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering Cannot be determined, but the opposite pairs are fully fixed by symmetry. Number the seats 1 to 8 with K at seat 1, so M is opposite at seat 5; second to the right of K puts L at seat 7, and second to the right of M puts N at seat 3, and seats 7 and 3 are exactly opposite, so the answer is N.

18. In a row of 40 students, Anil is 15th from the left. What is his position from the right?
(a) 26th (b) 27th (c) 24th (d) 25th
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing 40 − 15 = 25 and forgetting the +1. Position from right = 40 − 15 + 1 = 26, so the answer is 26th.

19. A clock shows 3:15. What is the angle between the hour and minute hands?
(a) 0° (b) 7.5° (c) 22.5° (d) 15°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 0°, assuming the hands overlap at 3:15. The minute hand is at 90° and the hour hand at 3 × 30 + 15 × 0.5 = 97.5°, so the difference is 7.5°, so the answer is 7.5°.
Source: PYQ-style

20. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, F sit in a row facing south. A sits at the left end as seen by them. B sits second to the right of A. From the viewer's perspective facing them, where does B sit?
(a) Second from viewer's left (b) At viewer's left end (c) At viewer's right end (d) Second from viewer's right
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is ignoring the facing direction and answering second from viewer's left. For south-facing persons, their right is the viewer's left, so A, at their left end, is at the viewer's right end, and B, second to A's right, becomes second from the viewer's right, so the answer is Second from viewer's right.

23. Five teams played matches on five different days from Monday to Friday. Team X played on Wednesday. Team Y played before X but not on Monday. Team Z played immediately after X. Team W played on Monday. On which day did Team V play?
(a) Tuesday (b) Monday (c) Friday (d) Thursday

Solution: The trap is placing Y on Monday and shifting everything. W is Monday, Y must be Tuesday (before X but not Monday), X is Wednesday, Z is Thursday, leaving Friday for V, so the answer is Friday.

25. Eight friends sit around a square table, four at the corners and four at the middle of the sides, all facing the centre. A sits at a corner. B sits second to the right of A. C sits opposite A. D sits immediately to the left of C. Which of the following must be true?
(a) D sits immediately to the right of A (b) D sits at a corner (c) B and D sit opposite each other (d) B sits at a corner
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming B and D are opposite by symmetry, but they are not. On a square with alternating corner and side seats, moving two seats from a corner lands on another corner, so B is at a corner, while D, immediately left of C (a corner), sits at a side middle, so the answer is B sits at a corner.
Source: PYQ-style
