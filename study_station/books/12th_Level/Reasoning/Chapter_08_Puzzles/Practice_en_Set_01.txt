1. Five friends A, B, C, D and E are sitting in a row facing north. A sits at the left end. B sits second to the right of A. C sits at the right end. D sits immediately to the left of C. Who sits in the middle?
(a) A (b) E (c) D (d) B
Answer: (d)
Solution: From the left: A is 1st, B is 3rd, C is 5th, D is 4th, so E fills the 2nd seat and the middle (3rd) seat belongs to B. The trap is choosing D, which sits 4th, not 3rd. So the answer is B.
Source: PYQ-style

2. In a family, Ravi is the son of Meena. Meena is the wife of Suresh. How is Suresh related to Ravi?
(a) Father (b) Uncle (c) Brother (d) Grandfather
Answer: (a)
Solution: Meena is Ravi's mother and Suresh is Meena's husband, so Suresh is Ravi's father. The trap option Uncle tempts those who overthink the chain, but the direct link gives the answer is Father.

3. Six people P, Q, R, S, T and U are sitting around a circular table facing the centre. P is opposite S. Q is to the immediate left of P. R is to the immediate right of P. Who sits between Q and R?
(a) T (b) S (c) U (d) P
Answer: (d)
Solution: Q is on P's immediate left and R is on P's immediate right, so the order around the table is Q, P, R with P seated between Q and R. The trap is answering S because S is opposite P, but opposite does not mean between. So the answer is P.

7. In a row of students, Ram is 7th from the left and 12th from the right. How many students are in the row?
(a) 17 (b) 18 (c) 19 (d) 20
Answer: (b)
Solution: Total = left position + right position − 1 = 7 + 12 − 1 = 18. The trap is adding 7 + 12 = 19 and forgetting that Ram is counted twice. So the answer is 18.

8. Six persons sit in a row facing north. X sits third from the left. Y sits at the right end. Z sits between X and Y with exactly one person between X and Z. What is Z's position from the left?
(a) 3rd (b) 4th (c) 5th (d) 6th
Answer: (c)
Solution: X is 3rd, Y is 6th. One person between X and Z means Z is 5th, with the 4th seat between them. The trap is placing Z at 4th, which leaves no one between X and Z. So the answer is 5th.

9. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 5 km North (b) 3 km East (c) 3 km West (d) 8 km East
Answer: (b)
Solution: The two 5 km legs cancel out, leaving only the 3 km eastward leg. The trap is adding all distances or picking 8 km by combining legs wrongly. So the answer is 3 km East.

10. In a code, MOTHER is written as NPUIFS. How is FATHER written in that code?
(a) GBUIFS (b) GBUIFR (c) EZSGDQ (d) GBUHFS
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each letter shifts one step forward: F→G, A→B, T→U, H→I, E→F, R→S, giving GBUIFS. The trap option GBUHFS swaps the last two letters. So the answer is GBUIFS.

(a) 29 (b) 30 (c) 31 (d) 32
Answer: (b)
Solution: After interchange, Seema takes Reema's place: 17th from front and 14th from back. Total = 17 + 14 − 1 = 30. The trap is using Seema's original 9th position, which gives a wrong total. So the answer is 30.

14. Eight persons sit around a circular table facing the centre. K sits opposite M. L sits second to the right of K. N sits second to the right of M. Who sits opposite L?
(a) K (b) N (c) Cannot be determined (d) M
Answer: (b)
Solution: Number the seats 1 to 8 with K at seat 1, so M is opposite at seat 5. Second to the right of K puts L at seat 7, and second to the right of M puts N at seat 3; seats 7 and 3 are exactly opposite. The trap is answering Cannot be determined, but the opposite pairs are fully fixed by symmetry. So the answer is N.

(a) 25th (b) 27th (c) 24th (d) 26th

19. A clock shows 3:15. What is the angle between the hour and minute hands?
(a) 0° (b) 22.5° (c) 7.5° (d) 15°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The minute hand is at 90° and the hour hand at 3 × 30 + 15 × 0.5 = 97.5°, so the difference is 7.5°. The trap is answering 0°, assuming the hands overlap at 3:15. So the answer is 7.5°.

20. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, F sit in a row facing south. A sits at the left end as seen by them. B sits second to the right of A. From the viewer's perspective facing them, where does B sit?
(a) Second from viewer's left (b) At viewer's left end (c) Second from viewer's right (d) At viewer's right end
Answer: (c)
Solution: For south-facing persons, their right is the viewer's left, so A, at their left end, is at the viewer's right end, and B, second to A's right, becomes second from the viewer's right. The trap is ignoring the facing direction and answering second from viewer's left. So the answer is Second from viewer's right.

24. In a family of six members P, Q, R, S, T, U, there are two married couples. P is a doctor and the father of T. U is the grandfather of R and is a teacher. Q is the grandmother of T. S is the wife of P. How is R related to P?

Answer: (d)
Solution: P and S are one couple, U and Q the other; T is P's child and Q's grandchild. R is U's grandchild, but R's parent among the younger generation is not specified and R's gender is unknown. The trap is assuming R is T's sibling and answering Son or Daughter. So the answer is Cannot be determined.

25. Eight friends sit around a square table, four at the corners and four at the middle of the sides, all facing the centre. A sits at a corner. B sits second to the right of A. C sits opposite A. D sits immediately to the left of C. Which of the following must be true?
(a) B sits at a corner (b) D sits at a corner (c) B and D sit opposite each other (d) D sits immediately to the right of A
Answer: (a)
Solution: On a square with alternating corner and side seats, moving two seats from a corner lands on another corner, so B is at a corner; D, immediately left of C (a corner), sits at a side middle. The trap is assuming B and D are opposite by symmetry, but they are not. So the answer is B sits at a corner.
