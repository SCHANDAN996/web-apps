51. Six friends P, Q, R, S, T and U are sitting in a row facing north. P sits third to the left of T. U sits second to the right of T. S sits between P and R. Q sits immediately to the left of U. Who sits at the extreme right end?
(a) T (b) R (c) U (d) Q
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is Q, but Q sits immediately to the left of U, so Q cannot be at the end. P third to the left of T gives P _ _ T, and U second to the right of T with Q just left of U fixes T, Q, U together, giving P, S, R, T, Q, U. So the answer is U.
Source: PYQ-style

52. In a family of six members A, B, C, D, E and F, there are two married couples. A is the father of C. F is the wife of A. B is the wife of C. D is the daughter of B. E is the son of C. How is B related to E?
(a) Mother (b) Grandmother (c) Aunt (d) Sister
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "Grandmother", chosen by those who place B in the older generation. A and F are one couple with child C; B is the wife of C, and E is the son of C, so B is E's mother. So the answer is Mother.

53. Five boxes A, B, C, D and E are stacked one above another. A is immediately above C. B is above A but not at the top. D is at the bottom. Which box is at the top?
(a) B (b) A (c) E (d) C
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many pick B because "B is above A", but B is not at the top, so something must be above B. From the bottom: D, then C with A immediately above it, then B above A. The only box left for the top is E, so the answer is E.

54. Six friends A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. A sits at the extreme left end. B sits second to the right of A. F sits at the extreme right end. C sits immediately to the left of F. E sits immediately to the right of B. Who sits second from the left?
(a) C (b) B (c) D (d) E
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is B, but B sits second to the right of A, which puts B third. The row is A, D, B, E, C, F, so the second seat from the left belongs to D. So the answer is D.

(a) C (b) B (c) D (d) A

Solution: The trap is answering C directly, ignoring the renaming chain. Since B is called C, the person originally named B is the one called C. So the answer is B.

(a) Hindi (b) Science (c) Geography (d) English

(a) P (b) S (c) T (d) Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is P, but P lives immediately above Q, so P is on floor 3. R is on floor 5 and T on floor 4; S must be below Q, which fits only with S on floor 1 and Q on floor 2. So the answer is Q.

60. In a row of 40 students, Ram is 12th from the left and Shyam is 15th from the right. How many students sit between them?
(a) 12 (b) 11 (c) 14 (d) 13
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 15 − 12 = 3 or similar. Shyam's position from the left = 40 − 15 + 1 = 26. Students between positions 12 and 26 = 26 − 12 − 1 = 13. So the answer is 13.

61. Five friends A, B, C, D and E have different heights. C is taller than only E. B is taller than A but shorter than D. A is taller than C. Who is the tallest?
(a) B (b) C (c) D (d) A
Answer: (c)
Solution: C is taller than only E, so C is fourth and E is shortest. With A taller than C and D > B > A, the full order is D > B > A > C > E. So the answer is D.

62. A clock shows 4:20. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand?
(a) 20° (b) 30° (c) 0° (d) 10°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 0°, assuming the hands overlap at 4:20. Minute hand at 20 minutes = 120°. Hour hand = 4×30 + 20×0.5 = 130°. Difference = 10°, so the answer is 10°.

65. Five cars Red, Blue, Green, White and Black are parked in a row facing north. Red is at one end. Blue is immediately to the right of Red. Green is at the other end. White is between Blue and Black. Which car is in the middle?
(a) Black (b) Green (c) White (d) Blue
Answer: (c)
Solution: Red must be at the left end so that Blue can be to its right, and Green is at the right end. White sits between Blue and Black, giving: Red, Blue, White, Black, Green. The middle (third) car is White, so the answer is White.

66. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 3 km East (b) 3 km West (c) 8 km East (d) 5 km North
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "3 km West", from confusing the final facing direction with displacement. The 5 km north and 5 km south cancel; the net displacement is 3 km to the east. So the answer is 3 km East.

Answer: (b)
Solution: After interchange, Kunal is at Nikhil's original seat, which is 15th from the left and 10th from the right. Total = 15 + 10 − 1 = 24; the trap is adding 15 + 10 without subtracting the double count. So the answer is 24.
