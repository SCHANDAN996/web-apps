26. If '+' means '÷', '−' means '×', '×' means '−' and '÷' means '+', then the value of 36 + 6 × 3 − 2 ÷ 4 is
(a) 6 (b) 10 (c) 8 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: Replace the signs: 36 ÷ 6 − 3 × 2 + 4. By BODMAS: 36 ÷ 6 = 6 and 3 × 2 = 6, so 6 − 6 + 4 = 4. The trap is working left to right without BODMAS, so the answer is 4.
