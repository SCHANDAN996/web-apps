76. If '+' means '÷', '÷' means '×', '×' means '−' and '−' means '+', then the value of 36 + 6 × 3 − 2 ÷ 4 is
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 14 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: Replace the signs: 36 ÷ 6 − 3 + 2 × 4. The trap is to work left to right; apply BODMAS instead: 36 ÷ 6 = 6 and 2 × 4 = 8, so 6 − 3 + 8 = 11, so the answer is 11.
