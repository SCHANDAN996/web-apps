126. If 'P' means '×', 'Q' means '÷', 'R' means '+' and 'S' means '−', then the value of 18 Q 3 P 2 R 5 S 4 is:
(a) 15 (b) 13 (c) 17 (d) 11
Answer: (b)
Solution: Replacing symbols: 18 ÷ 3 × 2 + 5 − 4. By BODMAS: 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 6 × 2 = 12, then 12 + 5 − 4 = 13. The trap is doing operations strictly left to right and getting 15, so the answer is 13.
Source: PYQ-style

127. If '+' means '−', '×' means '+', '−' means '×' and '÷' stays '÷', then the value of 20 + 5 × 3 − 2 is:
(a) 19 (b) 23 (c) 17 (d) 21
Answer: (d)
Solution: After replacement: 20 − 5 + 3 × 2. By BODMAS: 3 × 2 = 6, then 20 − 5 + 6 = 21. The trap is computing 20 − 5 = 15 and then 15 + 3 × 2 = 36 or ignoring the multiplication priority, so the answer is 21.
