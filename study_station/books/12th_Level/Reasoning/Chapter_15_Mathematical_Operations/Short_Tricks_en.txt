# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Mathematical Operations

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 1: BODMAS the Boss

> **"Big Orders Demand My Attention, Sir!"**
> **B**rackets → **O**rders (powers/roots) → **D**ivision → **M**ultiplication → **A**ddition → **S**ubtraction
> Division and Multiplication are equal partners — solve left to right. Same for Addition and Subtraction.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 2: Bracket Order

> **"Very Old Books"** — solve brackets in this order:
> **V**inculum (bar — ) → **O**rdinary/Small ( ) → **B**races/Curly { } → then Square [ ]
> (Remember: bar first, then ( ), then { }, then [ ].)

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 3: Symbol Swap Questions

> **"Rewrite Before You Fight!"**
> When the question says "× means +, ÷ means −…", **never solve in your head**.
> First rewrite the whole expression with the NEW symbols on paper, then apply BODMAS.
> 90% of errors happen because students calculate with old symbols halfway through.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 4: Balancing the Equation

> **"Options Are Your Answer Key — Feed Them!"**
> In "which signs make the equation correct?" questions, don't derive — **plug in the options**.
> Start with option (a), substitute the signs, check LHS = RHS. Usually solved in under 30 seconds.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 5: The ÷ Trap

> **"Divide First, Cry Never."**
> In any mixed expression, hunt for **÷ and × first** — they are the examiners' favourite trap spots.
> 12 + 6 ÷ 3 = 12 + 2 = **14**, NOT 6. (The "left-to-right blindly" trap!)

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 6: Interchanging Numbers vs Signs

> **"Digits Dance, Signs Stay"** vs **"Signs Dance, Digits Stay."**
> Read the question twice: are the **numbers** swapping positions or the **operators**?
> Examiners mix these two types deliberately. Underline the words "numbers" or "signs" before solving.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 7: Greater/Less Than Chains

> **"All Mouths Eat the Bigger Fish."**
> In A > B, B = C, C < D type questions, convert everything to >, <, = first, draw a single chain,
> and only then check the conclusions. Never compare two elements that aren't connected by the chain.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 8: The "=" Anchor

> **"Equal is the Bridge."**
> In inequality chains, the "=" sign is your bridge — it lets you carry comparisons across.
> No "=" and no direct link between two elements? Answer: **relationship cannot be established.**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 9: Fraction of a Number

> **"Of Means Multiply, Is Means Equal."**
> "2/3 **of** 24 **is** x" → (2/3) × 24 = x. The tiny word "of" hides a multiplication — examiners count on you missing it.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 10: Digit Interchange Check

> **"Swap, Then Shop (the options)."**
> When digits of a number are interchanged (e.g., 1↔9, 2↔8), rewrite each option's number with swapped digits
> and test which satisfies the condition. Testing options beats building equations.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 11: The Zero Warning

> **"Zero Divides Nothing, Nothing Divides Zero… Wait!"**
> 0 ÷ 5 = 0 is fine. 5 ÷ 0 is **undefined** — if an option forces division by zero after symbol swap, reject it instantly.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 12: Negative × Negative

> **"Two Wrongs Make a Right (only in multiplication!)"**
> (−) × (−) = (+), but (−) + (−) = (−). After symbol interchange, signs often flip — recheck every minus twice.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Box 13: Quick Estimation

> **"Ballpark Before You Battle."**
> Before full calculation, estimate: if LHS ≈ 50 and an option gives ≈ 500, eliminate it without computing.
> Saves 20–30 seconds on equation-balancing questions.

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip

1. **The 15-Second Rule:** If after 15 seconds you still can't see which symbols to swap or where to start, mark it and move on. Symbol-interchange questions with 3+ changes are time-traps — return only if time remains.
2. **Skip "All Four Options Look Similar" Equation-Balancers:** If every option needs full substitution and none can be eliminated by estimation, the question costs 60+ seconds for 1 mark — attempt it in the second pass.
3. **Skip Long Inequality Chains with 4+ Variables Under Pressure:** If the chain has more than 3 relations and no "=" bridge is visible in the first read, skip — the risk of a wrong "cannot be determined" call is high, and the time cost is double a normal question.
