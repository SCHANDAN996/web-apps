101. If '+' means '÷', '÷' means '×', '×' means '−' and '−' means '+', then 36 + 6 ÷ 3 × 2 − 4 = ?
(a) 14 (b) 20 (c) 18 (d) 22
Answer: (b)
Solution: Replace the signs first: 36 ÷ 6 × 3 − 2 + 4. By BODMAS: 36 ÷ 6 = 6; 6 × 3 = 18; 18 − 2 + 4 = 20. The trap value 14 comes from applying BODMAS on the original signs before swapping them. So the answer is 20.
Source: PYQ-style

102. If 'A' means '+', 'B' means '−', 'C' means '×' and 'D' means '÷', then 18 D 3 A 5 B 2 C 4 = ?
(a) 5 (b) 7 (c) 3 (d) 9
Answer: (c)
Solution: Substituting: 18 ÷ 3 + 5 − 2 × 4. By BODMAS: 18 ÷ 3 = 6 and 2 × 4 = 8, so 6 + 5 − 8 = 3. The tempting wrong value 7 comes from computing strictly left to right and ignoring multiplication priority. So the answer is 3.
