51. If '+' means '÷', '÷' means '×', '×' means '−' and '−' means '+', then the value of 24 + 6 × 4 − 3 ÷ 2 is
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 4 (d) 10
Answer: (a)
Solution: Replace the symbols: 24 ÷ 6 − 4 + 3 × 2. By BODMAS: 24 ÷ 6 = 4 and 3 × 2 = 6, so 4 − 4 + 6 = 6. The trap is applying the new meanings only to some signs, so the answer is 6.
