1. If '+' means '÷', '÷' means '×', '×' means '−' and '−' means '+', then the value of 36 + 6 ÷ 3 × 2 − 4 is:
(a) 20 (b) 18 (c) 22 (d) 16
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is applying BODMAS before substitution or treating ÷ as weaker than ×. Substituting: 36 ÷ 6 × 3 − 2 + 4 = 6 × 3 − 2 + 4 = 18 − 2 + 4 = 20, so the answer is 20.
Source: PYQ-style

2. If '×' means '+', '÷' means '−', '+' means '×' and '−' means '÷', then 8 × 4 ÷ 2 + 6 − 3 = ?
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is ignoring BODMAS after substitution and working left to right. Substituting: 8 + 4 − 2 × 6 ÷ 3 = 8 + 4 − 4 = 8, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style
