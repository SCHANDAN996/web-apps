Questions 76–100: Medium

76. If A + B means A is the father of B, A − B means A is the sister of B, and A × B means A is the mother of B, then in the expression P × Q + R − S, how is P related to S?
(a) Grandmother (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Sister
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting trap is to read P × Q as making P only a mother and stop there, ignoring the chain. P × Q: P is mother of Q. Q + R: Q is father of R. R − S: R is sister of S, so S is also a child of Q. Thus P is the mother of S's father, so the answer is Grandmother.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Pointing to a photograph, Ravi said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to Ravi?
(a) Cousin (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Aunt
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming "grandfather's only son" could be an uncle; but Ravi's grandfather's only son must be Ravi's own father, since Ravi descends from him. The daughter of Ravi's father is Ravi's sister, so the answer is Sister.

78. If P $ Q means P is the brother of Q, P # Q means P is the mother of Q, and P * Q means P is the daughter of Q, then which expression shows that A is the maternal uncle of B?
(a) B # C $ A (b) A # B $ C (c) A $ C # B (d) A * B # C
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the first option, which says B is mother of C and C is brother of A — that does not connect A to B as uncle at all. In A $ C # B: A is brother of C, and C is mother of B, so A is the brother of B's mother, i.e., the maternal uncle, so the answer is A $ C # B.

79. A is the son of B. C, B's sister, has a son D and a daughter E. F is the maternal uncle of A. How is D related to F?
(a) Son (b) Cousin (c) Brother (d) Nephew
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is calling D a cousin of F because they belong to the same extended family; but cousin applies between members of the same generation. F is the maternal uncle of A, so F is the brother of B; C is B's sister, hence also F's sister. D is the son of F's sister, so the answer is Nephew.

80. If M × N means M is the wife of N, M + N means M is the son of N, and M ÷ N means M is the daughter of N, then in K ÷ L × M + N, how is K related to N?
(a) Mother (b) Daughter (c) Sister (d) Granddaughter
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is reading left to right and calling K the daughter of N directly. K ÷ L: K is daughter of L. L × M: L is wife of M. M + N: M is son of N. So L and M are a couple, M is N's son, and K is their daughter, so the answer is Granddaughter.

81. Introducing a man, a woman said, "His wife is the only daughter of my father." How is the man related to the woman?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the woman has a sister and the man is her brother-in-law; but "only daughter of my father" means the woman herself, since she is the only daughter. So the man's wife is the woman herself, so the answer is Husband.

82. If A @ B means A is the father of B, A & B means A is the sister of B, and A % B means A is the husband of B, then which expression means X is the father-in-law of Y?
(a) X @ Z % Y (b) Y % Z & X (c) X % Z @ Y (d) X & Z @ Y
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the first option, which makes X the husband of Z and Z the father of Y — that makes X a stepfather figure, not a father-in-law. In X @ Z % Y: X is father of Z, and Z is husband of Y, so X is the father of Y's husband, so the answer is X @ Z % Y.

83. P is the brother of Q. R is the mother of Q. S is the father of R. T is the mother of S. How is T related to P?
(a) Grandmother (b) Aunt (c) Mother (d) Great-grandmother
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is stopping at S and calling T a grandmother. R is mother of Q and hence of P, since P and Q are siblings. S is father of R, so S is P's grandfather. T is mother of S, so the answer is Great-grandmother.

84. If D is the brother of B, how is B related to C? To answer, we are told: C is the daughter of A, and A is the wife of D's father. Which additional fact is essential to fix B's gender?
(a) B is the son of A (b) D is the son of A (c) C is the sister of D (d) A is the mother of D
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is thinking D's gender or C's relation fixes B's gender — they do not, since D is already known to be male as a brother. Only a statement that directly assigns B a gender, such as "B is the son of A," resolves whether B is C's brother or sister, so the answer is B is the son of A.

85. If A + B means A is the father of B, A − B means A is the sister of B, and A × B means A is the husband of B, then in P × Q − R + S, how is P related to S?
(a) Brother (b) Uncle (c) Father (d) Grandfather
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is reading P × Q and jumping to "P is the father of S." P × Q: P is husband of Q. Q − R: Q is sister of R. R + S: R is father of S. So Q is the sister of S's father, making Q the aunt of S, and P, being Q's husband, is S's uncle by marriage, so the answer is Uncle.

100. A family has a man, his wife, their three sons with their wives, and three children in each son's family. How many members are there in the family?
(a) 15 (b) 18 (c) 17 (d) 20
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is forgetting the original couple or the sons' wives and getting 15 or 20. Man + wife = 2; three sons + three wives = 6; three sons × three children = 9. Total = 2 + 6 + 9 = 17, so the answer is 17.
