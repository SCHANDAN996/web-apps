51. If 'A + B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A − B' means 'A is the mother of B', 'A × B' means 'A is the brother of B', then which expression means 'P is the paternal uncle of R'?
(a) P × Q − R (b) P + Q × R (c) P × Q + R (d) P − Q × R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is the first option, where P × Q makes P the brother of Q, but Q − R makes Q the mother of R, so P becomes the maternal uncle, not paternal. In the correct expression, P × Q makes P the brother of Q, and Q + R makes Q the father of R, so P is the father's brother, i.e., the paternal uncle of R.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

52. Pointing to a photograph, Ravi said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to Ravi?
(a) Cousin (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Sister
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 'aunt' — many students read "grandfather's only son" as someone other than the father. But Ravi's grandfather's only son must be Ravi's own father, and the father's daughter is Ravi's sister. So the answer is sister.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CHSL pattern)

53. If 'P @ Q' means 'P is the wife of Q', 'P # Q' means 'P is the son of Q', and 'P $ Q' means 'P is the father of Q', then in the expression 'A # B @ C $ D', how is A related to D?
(a) Son (b) Brother (c) Grandson (d) Father
Answer: (b)
Solution: A # B means A is the son of B. B @ C means B is the wife of C, so C is A's father. C $ D means C is the father of D, making A and D children of the same father C. The trap 'grandson' comes from misreading the chain direction, but A is simply the brother of D.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS PO pattern)

54. Introducing a man, a woman said, "His wife is the only daughter of my father." How is the man related to the woman?
(a) Husband (b) Father (c) Brother (d) Brother-in-law
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap 'brother-in-law' tempts students who assume the woman has a sister. But "only daughter of my father" means the woman herself, since she is the only daughter. So the man's wife is the woman herself, making the man her husband.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

55. If 'M × N' means 'M is the sister of N', 'M + N' means 'M is the father of N', and 'M ÷ N' means 'M is the mother of N', which of the following shows that 'T is the maternal aunt of W'?
(a) T + U × W (b) T × U ÷ W (c) T × U + W (d) T ÷ U × W
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the third option: T × U makes T the sister of U, but U + W makes U the father of W, so T becomes the paternal aunt. In the correct expression, T × U makes T the sister of U, and U ÷ W makes U the mother of W, so T is the mother's sister — the maternal aunt of W.
Source: PYQ-style (RRB NTPC pattern)

56. A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. D is the father of A. How is C related to D?
(a) Daughter (b) Sister (c) Mother (d) Wife
Answer: (d)
Solution: A is B's brother and B is C's daughter, so A and B are both children of C. D is A's father, so D is the father of C's children, which makes C the wife of D. The trap 'sister' arises if students wrongly place D and C in the same generation as siblings, but the answer is wife.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC MTS pattern)

57. If 'X → Y' means 'X is the mother of Y', 'X ← Y' means 'X is the father of Y', and 'X ↑ Y' means 'X is the sister of Y', then in 'P ↑ Q ← R', how is P related to R?
(a) Aunt (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Niece
Answer: (a)
Solution: P ↑ Q means P is the sister of Q, and Q ← R means Q is the father of R. So P is the sister of R's father. The trap 'sister' ignores the generation gap created by the father link; P is the paternal aunt of R.
Source: PYQ-style (Bank Clerk pattern)

58. Pointing to a boy, Seema said, "He is the son of the only daughter of my mother's husband." How is the boy related to Seema?

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap 'nephew' tempts students who insert an imaginary sister. Seema's mother's husband is Seema's father, and the only daughter of her father is Seema herself. So the boy is the son of Seema — the answer is son.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

59. If 'A * B' means 'A is the husband of B', 'A / B' means 'A is the daughter of B', and 'A − B' means 'A is the brother of B', then in 'K / L * M − N', how is K related to N?
(a) Niece (b) Daughter (c) Sister (d) Aunt
Answer: (a)
Solution: K / L means K is the daughter of L. L * M means L is the husband of M, so M is K's mother. M − N means M is the brother of N, so N is K's maternal uncle or aunt, making K the niece of N. The trap 'daughter' ignores the last link; the answer is niece.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS Clerk pattern)

60. R is the son of Q. Q is the sister of P. P is the father of S. How is R related to S?
(a) Cousin (b) Brother (c) Uncle (d) Nephew
Answer: (a)
Solution: Q is P's sister, so Q's son R and P's child S are children of two siblings. The trap 'nephew' reverses the generation. Children of a brother and a sister are cousins, so R is the cousin of S.
Source: PYQ-style (RRB NTPC pattern)

61. If 'P □ Q' means 'P is the father of Q', 'P ○ Q' means 'P is the sister of Q', and 'P △ Q' means 'P is the mother of Q', which expression shows 'M is the grandmother of T'?
(a) M □ N △ T (b) M △ N □ T (c) M ○ N □ T (d) M △ N ○ T
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the second option, where M □ N makes M the father of N — a grandfather, not grandmother. In the correct expression, M △ N makes M the mother of N, and N □ T makes N the father of T, so M is the paternal grandmother of T.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CHSL pattern)

63. If 'A > B' means 'A is the brother of B', 'A < B' means 'A is the sister of B', and 'A = B' means 'A is the father of B', then in 'X > Y = Z < W', how is X related to W?
(a) Son (b) Brother (c) Father (d) Uncle
Answer: (d)
Solution: X > Y means X is the brother of Y. Y = Z means Y is the father of Z, and Z < W means Z is the sister of W, so Y is the father of W as well. Therefore X is the brother of W's father. The trap 'son' reverses the chain direction; X is the uncle of W.
Source: PYQ-style (Bank PO pattern)

64. K is the daughter of L. L is the wife of M. N is the son of M. O is the wife of N. How is K related to O?

Answer: (a)
Solution: K and N are both children of L and M, so K is N's sister. O is N's wife, so K is the sister of O's husband. The trap 'sister' ignores that O married into the family; the correct relation is sister-in-law.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC MTS pattern)

65. If 'S + T' means 'S is the son of T', 'S − T' means 'S is the daughter of T', and 'S × T' means 'S is the wife of T', then in 'A + B × C − D', how is A related to D?
(a) Nephew (b) Son (c) Brother (d) Grandson
Answer: (d)
Solution: A + B means A is the son of B. B × C means B is the wife of C, so C is A's father. C − D means C is the daughter of D, so D is C's parent and hence A's grandparent. The trap 'nephew' misreads the last link; A is the grandson of D.
Source: PYQ-style (IBPS PO pattern)
