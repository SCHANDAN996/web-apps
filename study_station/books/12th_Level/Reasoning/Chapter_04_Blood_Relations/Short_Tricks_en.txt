🧠 SHORT TRICKS & MNEMONICS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📦 BOX 1: HOW TO BUILD YOUR OWN MNEMONIC
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A mnemonic works only when it is short, vivid and personal.
Follow these steps for any list you must memorise:

1. Take the FIRST LETTER of each item in the list.
2. Arrange those letters into a word or a funny sentence.
3. Attach a picture in your mind — the sillier, the better.
4. Revise it after 1 day, 1 week and 1 month.

Example pattern (method only): to remember a list of items
A, B, C, D — make a sentence like "Aunt Buys Cakes Daily".
The sentence itself is meaningless; the letters do the work.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

📦 BOX 2: ELIMINATION TRICK FOR MCQs
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
When you are unsure of the answer:

1. Cross out options that are clearly wrong first.
2. Beware of "almost-correct" statements — one wrong word
   (name, date, direction) makes the whole statement false.
3. In negative questions ("which is NOT…"), underline the
   word NOT before reading the options.
4. If two options say opposite things, the answer is often
   one of them — check those two first.
5. Never leave a question blank if there is no negative
   marking; if there is, guess only after eliminating at
   least two options.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

📦 BOX 3: MEMORY HOOKS THAT ALWAYS WORK
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• CHUNKING — break a long list into groups of 3–4 items;
  the brain holds small groups far better than one long chain.
• STORY LINK — link items in a made-up story; recalling the
  story recalls the list in order.
• PLACE METHOD — mentally place each item in a room of your
  house; "walk" through the house during the exam.
• WRITE-RECALL — after reading a fact once, close the book
  and write it from memory; this fixes it far better than
  re-reading.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

📦 BOX 4: EXAM-HALL TIME TRICKS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• First pass: answer only the questions you know instantly.
• Second pass: return to the marked/doubtful ones.
• Do not spend more than about a minute on any single
  question in the first pass.
• Keep the last 5 minutes only for checking marked answers
  and ensuring every bubble/option is filled correctly.
