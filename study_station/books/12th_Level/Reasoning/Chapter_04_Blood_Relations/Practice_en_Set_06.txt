126. If 'A + B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A − B' means 'A is the sister of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the mother of B', then in the expression 'P × Q + R − S', how is P related to S?
(a) Aunt (b) Mother (c) Grandmother (d) Grandfather
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is Mother, which ignores that the chain has two generations. P × Q means P is the mother of Q; Q + R means Q is the father of R; R − S means R is the sister of S, so R and S are children of Q. Hence P is the mother of S's father, so the answer is Grandmother.
Source: PYQ-style

127. In a code, 'M @ N' means 'M is the wife of N', 'M # N' means 'M is the son of N', and 'M $ N' means 'M is the brother of N'. Which expression shows that T is the paternal uncle of V?
(a) T $ U @ V (b) T $ U # V (c) V # U $ T (d) T # U @ V
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is T $ U # V, where U # V makes U the son of V, so T ends up as the brother of V's son, not of V's father. In V # U $ T: V is the son of U, and U is the brother of T, so T is the brother of V's father, so the answer is V # U $ T.

128. 'P × Q' means 'P is the daughter of Q', 'P + Q' means 'P is the husband of Q', 'P ÷ Q' means 'P is the father of Q'. If 'A × B + C ÷ D', how is A related to D?
(a) Daughter (b) Sister (c) Granddaughter (d) Niece
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Daughter, which stops after reading only A × B. A is the daughter of B; B is the husband of C; C is the father of D. So B is the mother of D, and A, being B's daughter, is D's sister, so the answer is Sister.

129. 'X * Y' means 'X is the mother of Y', 'X % Y' means 'X is the brother of Y', 'X & Y' means 'X is the father of Y'. In 'A & B % C * D', how is A related to D?

Solution: The tempting wrong answer is Father, which collapses two generations into one. A & B: A is the father of B. B % C: B is the brother of C. C * D: C is the mother of D. So A is the father of B and C, and C is D's mother, hence A is the father of D's mother, so the answer is Grandfather.

130. 'A → B' means 'A is the son of B', 'A ← B' means 'A is the daughter of B', 'A ↑ B' means 'A is the husband of B'. Which expression means 'K is the mother-in-law of M'?
(a) M ↑ L → K (b) K ← L ↑ M (c) K → L ↑ M (d) M → L ↑ K
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is K ← L ↑ M, which makes K the daughter of L and L the husband of M — that makes K the child of M's spouse, not the mother-in-law. In M ↑ L → K: M is the husband of L, and L is the son of K, so K is the mother of M's wife, so the answer is M ↑ L → K.

Solution: The trap is Sister, which treats R and T as belonging to the same generation. R ⊕ S: R is the sister of S. S ⊗ T: S is the father of T. T ⊖ U: T is the wife of U. So R is the sister of T's father, so the answer is Aunt.
