101. If 'P + Q' means 'P is the father of Q', 'P − Q' means 'P is the mother of Q', and 'P × Q' means 'P is the sister of Q', then how is A related to D in the expression 'A + B − C × D'?
(a) Grandfather (b) Uncle (c) Father (d) Brother
Answer: (a)
Solution: A + B means A is the father of B. B − C means B is the mother of C. C × D means C is the sister of D, so C and D are siblings and both are children of B. The tempting wrong choice is "Father", which ignores that the parent directly above D is B, not A. A is the father of D's mother, so the answer is Grandfather.

102. If 'M × N' means 'M is the wife of N', 'M ÷ N' means 'M is the son of N', and 'M + N' means 'M is the daughter of N', then which expression shows that 'T is the father of R'?
(a) T × S ÷ R (b) T ÷ S × R (c) R + S ÷ T (d) R ÷ S × T
Answer: (d)
Solution: In "R ÷ S × T", R ÷ S means R is the son of S, and S × T means S is the wife of T. So R is the son of S, and S is married to T, making T the father of R. The trap in the first option is that it makes T the wife, not the father. So the answer is "R ÷ S × T".
