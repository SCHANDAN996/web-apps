1. Pointing to a photograph, Ravi said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to Ravi?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Cousin
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option "cousin" is wrong because "only son" removes any uncle's family from the picture. Grandfather's only son is Ravi's father, and the father's daughter is Ravi's sister, so the answer is sister.
Source: PYQ-style

2. If A + B means A is the father of B, A − B means A is the mother of B, and A × B means A is the brother of B, then in P + Q − R, how is P related to R?
(a) Father (b) Grandfather (c) Uncle (d) Brother
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Father" is the trap for those who stop after the first symbol. P + Q means P is the father of Q, and Q − R means Q is the mother of R, so P is the father of R's mother, i.e., the maternal grandfather, so the answer is grandfather.
Source: PYQ-style

4. If M × N means M is the sister of N, M + N means M is the father of N, then which expression shows that T is the daughter of P?

Answer: (a)
Solution: P × T would make P the sister of T, reversing the roles, and T + P would make T the father of P. P + T means P is the father of T, which makes T the child of P, fitting "daughter", so the answer is P + T.
