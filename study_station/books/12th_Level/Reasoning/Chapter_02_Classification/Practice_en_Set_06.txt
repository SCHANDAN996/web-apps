126. Four of the following five are alike in a certain way and so form a group. Which is the one that does not belong to that group?
(a) 121 (b) 169 (c) 196 (d) 225
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is to treat all four as perfect squares and reject the question. Look deeper: 121 = 11², 169 = 13², 225 = 15² — all squares of odd numbers, while 196 = 14² is the square of an even number. Hence the odd one out is 196.
