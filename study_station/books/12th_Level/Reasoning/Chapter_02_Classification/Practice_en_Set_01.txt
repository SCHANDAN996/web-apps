1. Find the odd one out: Apple, Mango, Banana, Potato
(a) Potato (b) Mango (c) Banana (d) Apple
Answer: (a)
Solution: Potato is a vegetable, while Apple, Mango and Banana are all fruits. The tempting trap is to think all are eaten raw, but a potato is not a fruit, so the answer is Potato.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Find the odd one out: Rose, Lotus, Lily, Mango
(a) Rose (b) Mango (c) Lily (d) Lotus
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rose, Lotus and Lily are flowers, whereas Mango is a fruit. Do not be misled by the fact that all come from plants; the category here is flowers, so the answer is Mango.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Find the odd one out: 27, 64, 125, 216
(a) 64 (b) 27 (c) 125 (d) 216
Answer: (a)
Solution: 27 (3³), 125 (5³) and 216 (6³) are cubes of numbers whose cube root is odd or 6; more precisely, 27, 125 and 216 are cubes of 3, 5 and 6, while 64 is both a cube (4³) and a perfect square (8²). The double relationship is the examiner's trap: 64 is the only one that is also a perfect square, so the answer is 64.
Source: PYQ-style
