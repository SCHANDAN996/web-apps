Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Bat (b) Pigeon (c) Parrot (d) Sparrow
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bat is tempting because it flies like the others, but it is a mammal that gives birth to young ones, while pigeon, parrot and sparrow are birds that lay eggs. Flying is the misleading common feature here, so the answer is Bat.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 13 (b) 21 (c) 17 (d) 19
Answer: (b)
Solution: 21 is tempting because it is odd like the others, but 21 = 3 × 7 is composite, while 13, 17 and 19 are prime numbers. Oddness is the trap, so the answer is 21.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

28. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Copper (b) Brass (c) Iron (d) Silver
Answer: (b)
Solution: Brass looks like a metal name alongside the others, but it is an alloy of copper and zinc, while copper, iron and silver are pure elements. So the answer is Brass.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

29. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Mango (b) Rose (c) Marigold (d) Lotus
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rose, marigold and lotus are flowers grown mainly for decoration or worship, while mango is a fruit. All being plants is the trap, so the answer is Mango.
Source: PYQ-style

30. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 27 (b) 144 (c) 125 (d) 64
Answer: (b)
Solution: 27 = 3³, 125 = 5³ and 64 = 4³ are perfect cubes, while 144 = 12² is only a perfect square. Note that 64 is also a perfect square (8²), which is the trap — but it is still a cube, so the answer is 144.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

31. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Ear (b) Eye (c) Throat (d) Nose
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ear, eye and nose are sense organs, while throat is not a sense organ — it is part of the respiratory and digestive passage. All being body parts of the head region is the trap, so the answer is Throat.
Source: NCERT Class 5 EVS

32. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 48 (b) 36 (c) 64 (d) 16
Answer: (a)
Solution: 16 = 4², 36 = 6² and 64 = 8² are squares of even numbers, while 48 is not a perfect square at all. All being even numbers is the trap, so the answer is 48.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

33. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Kolkata (b) Mumbai (c) Chennai (d) Delhi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kolkata, Mumbai and Chennai are port cities on the coast, while Delhi is an inland city with no seaport. All being major capitals or metros is the trap, so the answer is Delhi.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

34. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Triangle (b) Circle (c) Rectangle (d) Square
Answer: (b)
Solution: Triangle, square and rectangle are polygons made of straight line segments, while a circle is a curved figure with no sides or corners. All being plane figures is the trap, so the answer is Circle.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

35. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 9 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 7
Answer: (a)
Solution: 2, 3 and 7 are prime numbers, while 9 = 3 × 3 is composite. The trap is that 9 is odd like 3 and 7, but oddness is not the classifying rule, so the answer is 9.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

36. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Sitar (b) Flute (c) Violin (d) Guitar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sitar, violin and guitar are string instruments, while flute is a wind instrument. All being musical instruments is the trap, so the answer is Flute.
Source: PYQ-style

37. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 11-13 (b) 41-43 (c) 17-19 (d) 23-29
Answer: (d)
Solution: 11-13, 17-19 and 41-43 are twin prime pairs differing by 2, while 23 and 29 differ by 6 and are not even consecutive primes (they are both prime, which is the trap). So the answer is 23-29.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

38. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Apple (b) Banana (c) Grapes (d) Potato
Answer: (d)
Solution: Apple, banana and grapes are fruits, while potato is a vegetable (a modified stem called a tuber). All being food items is the trap, so the answer is Potato.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

39. Choose the odd one out.
(a) May (b) March (c) June (d) January
Answer: (c)
Solution: March, May and January have 31 days, while June has 30 days. All being months is the trap, so the answer is June.
Source: PYQ-style

40. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 8 (b) 27 (c) 100 (d) 64
Answer: (c)
Solution: 8 = 2³, 27 = 3³ and 64 = 4³ are perfect cubes, while 100 is a perfect square (10²) but not a cube. So the answer is 100.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

41. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Lion (b) Tiger (c) Leopard (d) Wolf
Answer: (d)
Solution: Lion, leopard and tiger belong to the cat family (Felidae), while wolf belongs to the dog family (Canidae). All being wild carnivores is the trap, so the answer is Wolf.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

42. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 51 (b) 67 (c) 57 (d) 87
Answer: (b)
Solution: 51 = 3 × 17, 57 = 3 × 19 and 87 = 3 × 29 are all divisible by 3, while 67 is a prime number not divisible by 3. All being two-digit odd-looking numbers is the trap, so the answer is 67.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

43. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Teacher (b) Engineer (c) Hospital (d) Doctor
Answer: (c)
Solution: Engineer, teacher and doctor are professions (persons), while hospital is a place. Doctor is tempting because it relates to a hospital, but it is still a person, so the answer is Hospital.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 132 (b) 396 (c) 482 (d) 264
Answer: (c)
Solution: In 132, 396 and 264, the middle digit equals the sum of the other two digits (1+2=3, 3+6=9, 2+4=6). In 482, 4+2 = 6, which is not 8, so the answer is 482.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Chess (b) Hockey (c) Football (d) Cricket
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hockey, football and cricket are outdoor team games played on a field, while chess is an indoor board game between two individuals. All being games is the trap, so the answer is Chess.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 15 (b) 21 (c) 33 (d) 37
Answer: (d)
Solution: 15, 33 and 21 are odd composite numbers (divisible by 3 or 5), while 37 is a prime number. All being odd is the trap, so the answer is 37.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

47. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Hirakud (b) Yamuna (c) Ganga (d) Godavari
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ganga, Yamuna and Godavari are rivers, while Hirakud is a dam built across the Mahanadi river. All being names linked with rivers is the trap, so the answer is Hirakud.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

48. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 457 (b) 234 (c) 123 (d) 345
Answer: (a)
Solution: In 123, 234 and 345, the three digits are consecutive numbers in increasing order. In 457, the digits are not consecutive (4, 5, 7 skips 6), so the answer is 457.
Source: PYQ-style

49. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Venus (b) Mars (c) Jupiter (d) Moon
Answer: (d)
Solution: Venus, Mars and Jupiter are planets, while the Moon is a natural satellite of the Earth. All being heavenly bodies visible in the sky is the trap, so the answer is Moon.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

50. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 18 (b) 24 (c) 46 (d) 36
Answer: (c)
Solution: 18, 24 and 36 are divisible by both 2 and 3 (that is, divisible by 6), while 46 is divisible only by 2, not by 3. All being even is the trap, so the answer is 46.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics
