51. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Bat (b) Pigeon (c) Sparrow (d) Parrot
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bat looks like a bird and flies, which is the trap, but it is a mammal that gives birth to young ones and suckles them. Sparrow, pigeon and parrot are birds that lay eggs. So the answer is Bat.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

52. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Copper (b) Iron (c) Silver (d) Brass
Answer: (d)
Solution: Brass is tempting because it looks and is used like a metal, but it is an alloy of copper and zinc, not a pure element. Copper, iron and silver are pure metallic elements. So the answer is Brass.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

53. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 121 (b) 144 (c) 150 (d) 169
Answer: (c)
Solution: 121 = 11², 144 = 12² and 169 = 13² are perfect squares. 150 is not a perfect square — the trap is that it sits close to 144 and looks like it belongs to the same number family. So the answer is 150.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

55. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 64 (b) 144 (c) 125 (d) 27
Answer: (b)
Solution: 27 = 3³, 64 = 4³ and 125 = 5³ are perfect cubes. 144 = 12² is a perfect square, not a cube — the trap is that 64 is both a square and a cube, which may confuse. So the answer is 144.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

Answer: (a)
Solution: 132 = 12 × 11, 264 = 24 × 11 and 396 = 36 × 11 are all divisible by 11. 482 leaves a remainder on division by 11 — the trap is that all four are even three-digit numbers. So the answer is 482.
