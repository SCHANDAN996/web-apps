126. In a certain code, "P @ Q" means "P is 5 m north of Q", "P # Q" means "P is 5 m south of Q", "P $ Q" means "P is 5 m east of Q", and "P % Q" means "P is 5 m west of Q". If "A @ B", "B $ C" and "D @ C", then in which direction is A with respect to D?
(a) North-East (b) West (c) East (d) South-West
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming A is north-east of D by adding the north moves; the vertical positions actually match. A is 5 m north of B, B is 5 m east of C, and D is 5 m north of C, so taking C at (0,0): D(0,5), B(5,0), A(5,5). A is exactly east of D, so the answer is East.
