101. A man walks 5 km towards the North, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) 5 km North (b) 3 km West (c) 3 km East (d) 8 km East
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 3 km West, which comes from forgetting that after facing North, a right turn means East. Walking 5 km North, then 3 km East, then 5 km South cancels the North–South movement completely, leaving only the 3 km Eastward shift, so the answer is 3 km East.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Ravi starts from his house and walks 10 m towards the East. He then turns left and walks 5 m, then turns left again and walks 10 m. In which direction is he now facing?
(a) East (b) North (c) West (d) South
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to compute his position instead of the direction he faces. Facing East, a left turn makes him face North; another left turn makes him face West, so the answer is West.
Source: PYQ-style

103. In a coded direction system, 'North' is called 'East', 'East' is called 'South', 'South' is called 'West' and 'West' is called 'North'. A man walks 4 km in the direction called 'South'. In which actual direction does he walk?
(a) East (b) South (c) North (d) West
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to answer West by reversing the code chain. Here 'South' is the code name, and the code says East is called South, so walking in the direction called 'South' means actually walking East, so the answer is East.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Sunita walks 6 km towards the South, then turns left and walks 8 km. How far is she from the starting point?
(a) 10 km (b) 14 km (c) 2 km (d) 12 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 14 km, obtained by simply adding 6 and 8. The two legs are perpendicular, so use Pythagoras: √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10, so the answer is 10 km.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

105. A clock shows 3:00. The hour hand points towards the East. In which direction does the minute hand point?
(a) West (b) North (c) East (d) South
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to answer South by assuming the minute hand follows the hour hand's side. At 3:00 the minute hand points to 12, exactly opposite the hour hand which points to 3; if the hour hand points East, the minute hand points the opposite way, so the answer is North.

108. Priya walks 12 m towards the North, then 5 m towards the East, then 12 m towards the South. What is the shortest distance between her final position and the starting point?
(a) 5 m (b) 17 m (c) 13 m (d) 7 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 13 m, which wrongly applies Pythagoras to 12 and 5. The 12 m North and 12 m South cancel each other, leaving only the 5 m Eastward movement, so the answer is 5 m.

109. A man facing South turns 135° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North-West (b) South-West (c) South-East (d) North-East
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is North-East, obtained by turning anticlockwise by mistake. Clockwise from South: 45° gives South-West, 90° gives West, and 135° gives North-West, so the answer is North-West.

110. Reena walks 4 km East, then 3 km North, then 4 km West. How far is she from the starting point?
(a) 4 km (b) 3 km (c) 5 km (d) 7 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 5 km, from applying Pythagoras to 4 and 3 without cancelling. The 4 km East and 4 km West cancel out, leaving only the 3 km Northward movement, so the answer is 3 km.

111. In a code, 'East' means 'North', 'North' means 'West', 'West' means 'South' and 'South' means 'East'. A traveller is told to go 6 km 'West'. In which actual direction does he travel?
(a) West (b) North (c) South (d) East
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to answer West by ignoring the code. Since 'West' in the code means South, travelling 6 km 'West' means actually travelling South, so the answer is South.

112. Ajay walks 20 m towards the North. He turns left and walks 15 m, then turns left and walks 20 m. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 15 m West (b) 20 m South (c) 15 m East (d) 35 m West
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 15 m East, from confusing left with right while facing North. Facing North, left is West; after walking 15 m West and then 20 m South, the North–South parts cancel, so the answer is 15 m West.

113. One morning, the shadow of a pole falls towards the West. A boy stands facing the pole's shadow. Which direction is behind him?
(a) West (b) South (c) North (d) East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to answer West by ignoring that the boy faces the shadow. In the morning the sun is in the East, so shadows fall West; the boy faces West, hence behind him is East, so the answer is East.

114. Kiran walks 9 km towards the South, then turns right and walks 12 km. What is the shortest distance from the starting point?
(a) 3 km (b) 21 km (c) 18 km (d) 15 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 21 km, from simply adding the two legs. The legs are perpendicular, so distance = √(9² + 12²) = √(81 + 144) = √225 = 15, so the answer is 15 km.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

115. A man walks 8 m North, then 6 m East, then 8 m South, then 6 m West. Where is he with respect to the starting point?
(a) 10 m North-East (b) 8 m North (c) 6 m East (d) At the starting point
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 10 m North-East, from wrongly applying Pythagoras to 8 and 6. The 8 m North cancels the 8 m South and the 6 m East cancels the 6 m West, so he returns exactly to the start, so the answer is 'At the starting point'.
Source: PYQ-style

(a) South-West (b) North-East (c) West (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to answer South, which is the actual direction, not its code. The direction opposite to actual North is actual South, and the code writes South as South-West, so the answer is South-West.

117. Deepak starts walking towards the East. After walking 10 m he turns 45° anticlockwise and walks 10 m. In which direction is he walking in the second leg?
(a) North (b) North-East (c) North-West (d) South-East
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is North, which would need a 90° turn, not 45°. Facing East, a 45° anticlockwise turn points halfway between East and North, so the answer is North-East.

118. Meena walks 5 km North, then 5 km East, then 5 km South. In which direction is she from the starting point?

119. At 6:00 in the evening, a man stands facing the setting sun. His shadow falls in which direction relative to him?

Solution: The trap is 'in front of him', from forgetting that shadows fall away from the sun. The sun sets in the West; facing West, his shadow falls towards the East, which is behind him, so the answer is 'Behind him'.

121. A man walks 3 km North, then 4 km East, then 3 km South, then 2 km West. What is the shortest distance from the starting point?
(a) 5 km (b) 4 km (c) 6 km (d) 2 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 5 km, from applying Pythagoras to 3 and 4 without cancelling the return legs. North and South cancel; the net Eastward shift is 4 − 2 = 2 km, so the answer is 2 km.

122. In a coded system, each direction is replaced by the direction 90° clockwise from it (North becomes East, East becomes South, and so on). A girl is instructed: "Walk 5 km North, then 4 km West." If she follows the coded instructions literally in actual directions, how far is she from the starting point?
(a) √41 km (b) 1 km (c) 9 km (d) 5 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 9 km, from adding the legs as if they were in a straight line. Coded 'North' means actual East (5 km) and coded 'West' means actual North (4 km); these are perpendicular, so distance = √(5² + 4²) = √41, so the answer is √41 km.
Source: PYQ-style

123. P is 40 m South-West of Q. R is 40 m South-East of Q. In which direction is R with respect to P?
(a) North-East (b) West (c) East (d) South-East
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is South-East, from assuming R must be diagonally placed from P. P and R are both 40 m from Q at the same southern level, symmetrically placed to the West and East of Q, so R lies exactly East of P, so the answer is East.
Source: PYQ-style

124. A man walks 10 m towards the North, turns right and walks 10 m, then turns right and walks 10 m, then turns right and walks 10 m. Where is he with respect to the starting point?
(a) 10 m North (b) At the starting point (c) 10 m East (d) 10 m South
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 10 m East, from counting only the first rightward leg. The four legs form a square: 10 m North, 10 m East, 10 m South, 10 m West, so every movement is cancelled and he returns to the start, so the answer is 'At the starting point'.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Village A is to the North of village B. Village C is to the East of village B. Village D is to the North of village C. In which direction is village D with respect to village A, if AB = BC = CD?
(a) North-East (b) North (c) South-East (d) East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is North-East, from assuming D must be diagonally placed from A. Let B be at the origin: A is at (0, 1), C at (1, 0), D at (1, 1). D and A have the same northern level, with D directly to the right of A, so the answer is East.
Source: PYQ-style
