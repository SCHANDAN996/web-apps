26. In a coded language, "P @ Q" means P is to the North of Q, "P # Q" means P is to the South of Q, "P $ Q" means P is to the East of Q, and "P % Q" means P is to the West of Q. If "A @ B $ C", then in which direction is C with respect to A?
(a) South-West (b) North-East (c) North-West (d) South-East
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer is North-East, which comes from misreading "B $ C" as C being East of B. Actually A @ B means A is North of B, and B $ C means B is East of C, so C is West of B. With B at the origin, A is North and C is West, so C is South-West of A, so the answer is South-West.
