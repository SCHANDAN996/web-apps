# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Direction Sense (Coded)

---

**Box 1 — The "NESW Clock"**
Never Eat Soggy Wafers → **N**orth, **E**ast, **S**outh, **W**est — always in CLOCKWISE order. If your clockwise order breaks, your diagram is wrong. Redraw it!

---

**Box 2 — "Code = Costume"**
A coded direction is just a normal direction wearing a costume. Before solving, STRIP the costume: write a small dictionary first —
`@ → North, # → East, $ → South, % → West`
Solve the whole question with real directions only. Never think in symbols.

---

**Box 3 — "Right is Clock, Left is Anti"**
Right turn = move like clock hands (N→E→S→W).
Left turn = move against clock hands (N→W→S→E).
Chant: **"Right-Clock, Left-Lock (anti-lock!)"**

---

**Box 4 — "90-180-270 Trick"**
- 90° turn = 1 step on the compass
- 180° turn = 2 steps = exact OPPOSITE
- 270° turn = 3 steps = same as 1 step the OTHER way
So 270° right = 90° left. Save time — convert big turns!

---

**Box 5 — "Opposite Pairs Hug the Diagonal"**
N↔S and E↔W are opposite pairs.
NE↔SW and NW↔SE are opposite pairs.
Memory hook: **"North-South divorce, East-West divorce — diagonals follow their lead."**

---

**Box 6 — "Decode Once, Use Thrice"**
In coded questions, the same code table applies to ALL sub-questions. Decode it ONCE at the top of your rough space, then every sub-question takes only seconds.

---

**Box 7 — "Shadow Rule: Sun is a Snitch"**
Morning → sun in East → shadow falls WEST.
Evening → sun in West → shadow falls EAST.
Hook: **"Shadow runs AWAY from the Sun"** — like a kid running from a strict teacher.

---

**Box 8 — "Start Point = Home, End Point = School"**
For "how far and in which direction from start" questions: mark start as H (Home) and end as S (School). The question always asks: "Where is School from Home?" Draw a straight line H→S. That's your answer direction.

---

**Box 9 — "Pythagoras Only for 3-4-5 Family"**
If legs are 3 & 4 → diagonal is 5. If 6 & 8 → 10. If 5 & 12 → 13.
Don't calculate — RECOGNIZE the family and write the answer instantly.

---

**Box 10 — "Turn-Turn = Add or Cancel"**
Two turns in the same direction → ADD them (right 90° + right 90° = 180°).
Two turns in opposite directions → SUBTRACT (right 90° + left 90° = 0°, no change).
Hook: **"Same team adds, enemy team cancels."**

---

**Box 11 — "Facing Check Before Every Turn"**
After EVERY move, ask aloud (in your head): "Which way am I facing NOW?" 80% of coded-direction errors happen because students apply a turn to the OLD facing. One question, one second, zero errors.

---

**Box 12 — "Symbol Swap Panic Button"**
If the code says "East is called North," don't panic — just relabel your compass BEFORE reading the moves. The person walks in the REAL direction; only the NAME changes. **"Walk real, name fake."**

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip

1. **15-Second Decode Rule:** If after 15 seconds you still can't build the code dictionary (symbol → direction), SKIP and return later. Coded questions are cheap marks ONLY after decoding — without it, you're guessing.

2. **3+ Moves with 2+ Turns:** If a question has more than 3 movements AND multiple turns AND coding together, it needs a full diagram (45–60 sec). In the first pass of the paper, SKIP it; attempt in the second pass when you have diagram time.

3. **"Possibly / Cannot be determined" Traps:** If the question asks for direction but gives distance-only data (or vice versa), check for 10 seconds whether data is sufficient. If two answers look possible, mark "Cannot be determined" only if you're sure — otherwise SKIP. Never burn 90 seconds proving insufficiency.
