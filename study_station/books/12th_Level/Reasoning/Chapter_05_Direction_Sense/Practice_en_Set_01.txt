1. In a coded language, 'A @ B' means 'A is to the North of B', 'A # B' means 'A is to the South of B', 'A $ B' means 'A is to the East of B', and 'A % B' means 'A is to the West of B'. If 'P @ Q' and 'Q $ R', then in which direction is P with respect to R?
(a) North-East (b) South-East (c) North-West (d) South-West
Answer: (a)
Solution: P @ Q means P is North of Q, and Q $ R means Q is East of R, so R is West of Q. Place Q at the centre: P is above it and R is to its left, so from R, P lies up and to the right. The tempting wrong option North-West comes from misreading Q $ R as "Q is West of R". Hence P is to the North-East of R, so the answer is North-East.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: P is 8 m North of Q; Q is 6 m East of R, so R is 6 m West of Q; R is 8 m South of S, so S is 8 m North of R. Thus S is at the same height as P but 6 m to its West, so the gap is purely horizontal.
