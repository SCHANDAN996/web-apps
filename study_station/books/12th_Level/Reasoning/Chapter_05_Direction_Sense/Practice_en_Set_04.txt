76. In a certain code, P @ Q means Q is 5 m north of P; P # Q means Q is 5 m south of P; P $ Q means Q is 5 m east of P; P & Q means Q is 5 m west of P. If A # B $ C # D, then D is in which direction with respect to A?
(a) North-east (b) South-west (c) South-east (d) North-west
Answer: (c)
Solution: B is 5 m south of A, C is 5 m east of B, and D is 5 m south of C, so D is 10 m south and 5 m east of A. The trap is reading the last symbol from D's side, which wrongly gives north-west. So the answer is South-east.
Source: PYQ-style
