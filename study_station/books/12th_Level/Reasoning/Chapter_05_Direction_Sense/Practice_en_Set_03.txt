51. In a coded language, "North" is written as "East", "East" as "South", "South" as "West" and "West" as "North". A man walks 5 km towards "East" and then turns left and walks 3 km. In which actual direction is he from the starting point?
(a) South-East (b) North-East (c) North-West (d) South-West
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is treating "East" as actual East; here coded "East" means actual North. He walks 5 km actual North, then turns left from North, which is West, and walks 3 km, ending 5 km North and 3 km West of the start, so the answer is North-West.
