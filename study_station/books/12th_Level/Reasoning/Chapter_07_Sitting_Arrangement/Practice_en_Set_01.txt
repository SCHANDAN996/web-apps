1. Five friends A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing north. A sits at the extreme left end. C sits in the middle. D sits to the immediate left of C and B sits to the immediate right of C. Who sits at the extreme right end?
(a) A (b) D (c) E (d) C
Answer: (c)
Solution: From the left the order is A, D, C, B and the last seat goes to E. The trap is picking B because he is "right of C" — he is only immediately right of C, not at the end. So the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a circle facing the centre. P sits opposite S. Q sits to the immediate left of P and T sits to the immediate right of P. R is not an immediate neighbour of Q. Who sits to the immediate right of S?
(a) R (b) U (c) Q (d) T
Answer: (b)
Solution: Facing the centre, a person's left is the next seat clockwise. Q and T sit on either side of P, so R and U take the two seats beside S; R cannot sit next to Q, so R is S's immediate left and U is S's immediate right. So the answer is U.
Source: PYQ-style

3. In a row of students facing north, Ravi is 7th from the left and 12th from the right. How many students are in the row?
(a) 18 (b) 19 (c) 17 (d) 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding 7 + 12 = 19, which counts Ravi twice. Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 7 + 12 − 1, so the answer is 18.
Source: SSC CHSL pattern

4. A, B, C, D, E and F sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits between B and C, with B to the immediate left of A. D sits opposite A. E sits to the immediate right of D. Who sits opposite B?
(a) C (b) F (c) D (d) E
Answer: (b)
Solution: Place A; B is on A's left (clockwise) and C on A's right. D sits opposite A and E is D's immediate right, which puts E opposite C; F takes the last seat, opposite B. The trap is choosing E by mixing up B and C. So the answer is F.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Five girls M, N, O, P and Q sit in a row facing south. (Left and right are taken from the girls' own view.) M sits at the extreme left. N sits second to the right of M. O sits between N and P. Who sits at the extreme right?
(a) O (b) P (c) Q (d) N
Answer: (b)
Solution: From the girls' own left: M is 1st and N is 3rd. O must be next to both N and P, so O is 4th and P is 5th; Q takes the 2nd seat. The trap is turning left and right around because they face south — the question fixes them from the girls' view. So the answer is P.
Source: PYQ-style

6. In a row facing north, X is 5th from the left and Y is 8th from the right. If X and Y interchange positions, X becomes 10th from the left. How many persons are in the row?
(a) 15 (b) 18 (c) 16 (d) 17
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using X's old rank 5 with 8, which gives 12 and matches nothing. After the swap X occupies Y's seat, which is 10th from the left and 8th from the right, so total = 10 + 8 − 1 = 17. So the answer is 17.
Source: SSC CGL pattern

7. Eight persons sit at equal distances around a circular table facing the centre. A sits opposite B. C sits to the immediate left of A and D sits to the immediate left of B. Who sits opposite C?
(a) B (b) A (c) Cannot be determined (d) D
Answer: (d)
Solution: A and B are opposite each other; each has a neighbour on the same (left) side, so C and D are also exactly opposite each other. The trap is answering "cannot be determined" because four people are not placed. So the answer is D.
Source: PYQ-style

8. P, Q, R, S, T are sitting in a row facing north. Q is to the immediate left of R. P is at one end. S is between R and T. If P is at the left end, who is in the middle?
(a) R (b) T (c) S (d) Q
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is picking S because "between R and T" sounds central, but S sits fourth. The order from left is P, Q, R, S, T, so the middle seat is R. So the answer is R.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Six friends sit in a circle facing the centre. K is between J and L. M is opposite K. N is to the immediate left of M. Who is to the immediate right of M?
(a) J (b) N (c) L (d) O
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing N, but N is already fixed on M's left. The six persons are J, K, L, M, N and O; with N on M's immediate left, the only person left for M's immediate right is O. So the answer is O.

11. A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H sit in a row facing north. A is fourth from the left. B is to the immediate right of A. C is at the extreme right. How many persons sit between B and C?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing 8 − 5 = 3 without excluding C's own seat. A is 4th, B is 5th and C is 8th, so the seats between them are the 6th and 7th, holding 2 persons. So the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

12. Five boys sit facing north. Ram is to the right of Shyam. Mohan is to the left of Shyam. Sohan is to the right of Ram. If Rohan is at the extreme left, who is in the middle?
(a) Mohan (b) Ram (c) Shyam (d) Sohan
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing Mohan next to Rohan and then losing track of Shyam's central spot. The order from left is Rohan, Mohan, Shyam, Ram, Sohan, so the middle seat is Shyam. So the answer is Shyam.

13. In a circular arrangement of 6 persons facing the centre, how many persons sit between two persons sitting opposite each other on either side?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dividing 6 by 2 and answering 3. Removing the two opposite persons leaves 4 persons split equally on the two arcs, giving 2 on each side. So the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

14. Seven persons A to G sit in a row facing north. D sits exactly in the middle. B sits second to the left of D. F sits second to the right of D. Who sits at the extreme left?
(a) A (b) Cannot be determined (c) B (d) C
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming the remaining letters fill the gaps in alphabetical order. D is 4th, B is 2nd and F is 6th, but A, C, E and G can occupy positions 1, 3, 5 and 7 in any order, so the extreme-left person is not fixed. So the answer is "Cannot be determined".
Source: SSC CGL pattern

15. Four couples sit around a circular table facing the centre, with each husband opposite his wife. If Mr. A sits to the immediate right of Mrs. B, who sits opposite Mr. A?
(a) Mrs. B (b) Mrs. A (c) Mr. B (d) Cannot be determined
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is picking Mrs. B because she is mentioned sitting next to Mr. A. The given condition fixes that every husband sits opposite his own wife, so Mrs. A sits opposite Mr. A regardless of other placements. So the answer is Mrs. A.
Source: PYQ-style

16. In a row facing north, T is 11th from the left and R is 15th from the right. They interchange seats and now T is 18th from the left. What is R's new position from the right?
(a) 21st (b) 20th (c) 22nd (d) 18th
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering 15th, R's old right rank, but after the swap R moves to T's old seat. Total persons = 18 + 15 − 1 = 32, and T's old seat is 11th from the left, i.e. 32 − 11 + 1 = 22nd from the right. So the answer is 22nd.
Source: PYQ-style

17. Six persons sit in two rows of three, the rows facing each other: A, B, C sit in Row 1 facing south and D, E, F sit in Row 2 facing north. A faces D. B sits to the immediate left of A and E sits to the immediate right of D. Who faces B?
(a) E (b) C (c) F (d) D
Answer: (a)
Solution: A person facing south has east on the left, and a person facing north has east on the right, so B (left of A) and E (right of D) are both on the east side, directly facing each other. The trap is assuming "left" points the same way in both rows. So the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

18. Five persons U, V, W, X and Y sit around a circular table facing the centre. V sits between U and W. X sits to the immediate left of W. Who sits second to the right of V?
(a) U (b) W (c) X (d) Y
Answer: (d)
Solution: X must be on W's left (the next seat clockwise), so W is on V's left and U on V's right; the order clockwise from V is V, W, X, Y, U. Moving right from V we meet U and then Y. The trap is picking X by counting to the left. So the answer is Y.
Source: PYQ-style

19. In a row of 40 students facing north, Sunil is 12th from the left. What is his position from the right?
(a) 27th (b) 29th (c) 30th (d) 28th
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing 40 − 12 = 28 without adding 1 for Sunil himself. Position from right = 40 − 12 + 1 = 29. So the answer is 29th.
Source: RRB Group D pattern

20. A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a circle facing outward (away from the centre). A sits to the immediate left of B. C sits to the immediate right of B. D sits opposite B. E sits to the immediate right of C. Who sits to the immediate left of D?
(a) F (b) A (c) E (d) C
Answer: (c)
Solution: Facing outward, left and right are the reverse of a centre-facing circle. Going round from B towards C: B, C, E, D, F, A — so E and D are neighbours with E on D's left side. The trap is answering C, who is two seats away. So the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

21. Eight persons P, Q, R, S, T, U, V and W sit around a circular table facing the centre. P sits second to the right of T. R sits opposite P. Q sits to the immediate left of T and U sits to the immediate left of R. W sits opposite T. V sits to the immediate right of P. Who sits opposite Q?
(a) W (b) V (c) S (d) U
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fix T; P is two seats to T's right and R is opposite P. Q and U sit on the left of T and R, W is opposite T, V is right of P and S takes the last seat. The seat opposite Q is V's. The trap is picking W, who is opposite T, not Q. So the answer is V.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Six persons sit in a row facing north. A sits second to the left of B. C sits second to the right of B. D sits between A and B. E sits at an end. Who sits third to the left of C?
(a) D (b) A (c) B (d) E
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is counting C itself as the first person, which wrongly gives B. The row is E, A, D, B, F, C (or its mirror); counting left from C, F is first, B is second and D is third. So the answer is D.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit around a circular table facing the centre. A is not an immediate neighbour of B. C sits to the immediate right of A. D sits between B and E. Who sits to the immediate left of B?
(a) A (b) E (c) D (d) C
Answer: (d)
Solution: Going clockwise from A the only order that fits is A, E, D, B, C: C is on A's right, D sits between B and E, and A is not next to B. B's left is the next seat clockwise, which is C. The trap is choosing D because "D is between B and E". So the answer is C.
Source: PYQ-style

24. In a row facing north, there are 21 persons. M is 6th from the left and N is 9th from the right. How many persons sit between M and N?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 5 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing 13 − 6 = 7 without excluding N's own seat. N's position from the left is 21 − 9 + 1 = 13, so persons between positions 6 and 13 = 13 − 6 − 1 = 6. So the answer is 6.
Source: SSC CHSL pattern

25. Seven executives G, H, J, K, L, M and N sit around a circular table facing the centre. G sits second to the right of H. N sits second to the left of H. K sits to the immediate left of J and N sits to the immediate right of J. L sits to the immediate right of H. Who sits to the immediate left of K?
(a) N (b) L (c) M (d) G
Answer: (d)
Solution: Clockwise from H the seats are H, M, N, J, K, G, L: N is second to H's left, J and K follow N, L is on H's right and G is second to H's right. K's immediate left is the next seat clockwise, which is G. The trap is picking M by going the wrong way round. So the answer is G.
Source: PYQ-style
