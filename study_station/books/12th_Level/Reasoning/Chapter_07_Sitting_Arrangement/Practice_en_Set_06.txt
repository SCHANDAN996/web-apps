126. Eight persons L, M, N, O, P, Q, R and S sit around a circular table, facing away from the centre. P sits to the immediate right of R. L sits third to the right of S. N sits between L and O (next to both). M sits opposite P. Who sits to the immediate left of Q?
(a) R (b) M (c) S (d) N
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. Going clockwise: Q, S, R, P, L, N, O, M (facing outside, a person's left is anticlockwise). The trap is S, who is on the other side of Q. So the answer is M.
Source: PYQ-style
