101. Six persons L, M, N, O, P and Q sit in a row facing south. M sits third to the right of N. O sits between M and P (next to both). M sits second to the right of Q. Who sits to the immediate left of M?
(a) P (b) L (c) O (d) Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. As we look at them (they face us), from our left to our right: P, O, M, L, Q, N. The trap is O, who is on the other side of M. So the answer is L.
Source: PYQ-style
