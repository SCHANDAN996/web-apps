76. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a row facing north. S sits between P and Q (next to both). T sits third to the left of P. R sits at the right end. Who sits to the immediate left of P?
(a) R (b) S (c) U (d) T
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: U, T, Q, S, P, R. The trap is R, who is on the other side of P. So the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style
