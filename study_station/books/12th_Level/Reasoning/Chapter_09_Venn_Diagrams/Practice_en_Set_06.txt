126. In a class of 100 students, 60 play cricket, 50 play football and 30 play hockey. 20 play cricket and football, 15 play football and hockey, 10 play cricket and hockey, and 5 play all three games. How many students play at least one of the three games?
(a) 95 (b) 90 (c) 100 (d) 105
Answer: (c)
Solution: By inclusion–exclusion, students playing at least one game = 60 + 50 + 30 − 20 − 15 − 10 + 5 = 100. The trap is to forget adding back the all-three group, which gives 95. Applying the formula fully, the answer is 100.
Source: PYQ-style

127. In a survey of 200 people, 120 read newspaper A, 100 read newspaper B and 80 read newspaper C. 50 read A and B, 40 read B and C, 30 read A and C, and 20 read all three. How many read exactly two newspapers?
(a) 60 (b) 70 (c) 80 (d) 90
Answer: (a)
Solution: Exactly two = (50 − 20) + (40 − 20) + (30 − 20) = 30 + 20 + 10 = 60. The trap is to add the pairwise figures directly (50 + 40 + 30 = 120), which counts the all-three group three times. Subtracting the all-three group from each pairwise figure, the answer is 60.
Source: PYQ-style
