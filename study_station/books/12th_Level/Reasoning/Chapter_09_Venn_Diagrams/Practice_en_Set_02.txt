26. In a class of 60 students, 35 play cricket, 28 play football and 12 play both. How many students play neither game?
(a) 12 (b) 9 (c) 15 (d) 7
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 12 is just the "both" figure copied into the "neither" blank, which ignores the union calculation. Students playing at least one game = 35 + 28 − 12 = 51, so neither = 60 − 51, and the answer is 9.
Source: PYQ-style

27. In a survey of 100 people, 60 like tea, 45 like coffee and 25 like both. How many like only tea?
(a) 25 (b) 35 (c) 40 (d) 20
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to answer 60 (all tea drinkers) or 20 (the coffee-only figure), but "only tea" excludes those who like both. Only tea = 60 − 25, so the answer is 35.

28. In a group, 40 persons speak Hindi, 30 speak English and 15 speak both languages. How many persons speak at least one of the two languages?
(a) 25 (b) 70 (c) 45 (d) 55
Answer: (d)
Solution: The wrong option 70 comes from simply adding without subtracting the overlap, which double-counts the 15 bilingual persons. At least one = 40 + 30 − 15, so the answer is 55.

29. Which Venn diagram best represents the relationship among: Doctors, Men, Fathers?
(a) One circle inside another inside a third (b) Three disjoint circles (c) Two intersecting circles with a third disjoint (d) Three mutually intersecting circles
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing nested circles, which would wrongly claim that all fathers are doctors or all doctors are men. Some doctors are men, some men are fathers, some doctors are fathers, and one person can be all three at once, with no group fully inside another, so the answer is three mutually intersecting circles.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: The trap is three mutually intersecting circles, which wrongly allows a mother who is not a woman. 31. In a school of 120 students, 70 study Mathematics, 50 study Science and 30 study both. How many study exactly one of the two subjects?
(a) 50 (b) 40 (c) 60 (d) 90
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 90 is the union (at least one subject), which includes the 30 studying both. Only Mathematics = 70 − 30 = 40 and only Science = 50 − 30 = 20, giving 40 + 20, so the answer is 60.
