51. In a class of 60 students, 35 play cricket, 28 play football and 12 play both. How many students play neither game?
(a) 12 (b) 9 (c) 15 (d) 7
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 12 comes from confusing "play both" with "play neither". Students playing at least one game = 35 + 28 − 12 = 51, so neither = 60 − 51 = 9. The answer is 9.

52. In a survey of 100 people, 60 read newspaper A, 45 read newspaper B and 20 read both. How many read exactly one newspaper?
(a) 85 (b) 65 (c) 55 (d) 40
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to answer 85, which is the number reading at least one newspaper (60 + 45 − 20). Exactly one = (60 − 20) + (45 − 20) = 40 + 25 = 65, so the answer is 65.
Source: PYQ-style

53. In a group, 40 people like tea, 30 like coffee and 15 like both. If 10 people like neither, what is the total number of people in the group?
(a) 55 (b) 70 (c) 65 (d) 60
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to add 40 + 30 + 10 = 80, forgetting the overlap. People liking at least one = 40 + 30 − 15 = 55, so total = 55 + 10 = 65. The answer is 65.

54. Which Venn diagram best represents the relationship among: Women, Doctors, Mothers?
(a) Three disjoint circles (b) Two circles inside one big circle (c) Three mutually intersecting circles (d) One circle intersecting two disjoint circles
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to think mothers are entirely inside women and draw nested circles. Some women are doctors, some are mothers, some doctors are mothers, and some mothers are doctors — every pair can overlap, so three mutually intersecting circles are needed. The answer is three mutually intersecting circles.

60. In a survey, 70 people like tea, 50 like coffee, 30 like milk; 20 like tea and coffee, 15 like coffee and milk, 10 like tea and milk, and 5 like all three. How many like at least one drink?
(a) 105 (b) 120 (c) 100 (d) 110
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to add 70 + 50 + 30 = 150 and subtract only the pairwise overlaps without adding back the triple overlap. At least one = 70 + 50 + 30 − 20 − 15 − 10 + 5 = 110, so the answer is 110.

61. In a survey, 70 people like tea, 50 like coffee, 30 like milk; 20 like tea and coffee, 15 like coffee and milk, 10 like tea and milk, and 5 like all three. How many like exactly two drinks?
(a) 30 (b) 45 (c) 25 (d) 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to add the pairwise figures 20 + 15 + 10 = 45, but each of those includes the 5 who like all three. Exactly two = (20 − 5) + (15 − 5) + (10 − 5) = 15 + 10 + 5 = 30, so the answer is 30.
