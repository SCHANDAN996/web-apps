76. In a class of 60 students, 35 play cricket, 28 play football and 12 play both. How many students play neither game?
(a) 12 (b) 18 (c) 15 (d) 9
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 12, which is the number playing both games, not neither. Students playing at least one game = 35 + 28 − 12 = 51, so students playing neither = 60 − 51 = 9. The answer is 9.

77. In a survey of 100 people, 60 like tea, 45 like coffee and 25 like both. How many like exactly one of the two drinks?
(a) 50 (b) 60 (c) 45 (d) 55
Answer: (d)
Solution: Only tea = 60 − 25 = 35; only coffee = 45 − 25 = 20. Exactly one = 35 + 20 = 55. Do not subtract 25 from the total of 105 directly, which would wrongly give 80. The answer is 55.

78. Which Venn diagram best represents the relationship: Animals, Dogs, Cats?
(a) One circle inside another inside a third (b) Two intersecting circles inside a big circle (c) Three mutually intersecting circles (d) Two separate circles inside a big circle
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing intersecting circles, but no dog is a cat, so the two inner circles must not overlap. Both dogs and cats are animals, so both circles lie fully inside the big circle. The answer is two separate circles inside a big circle.

80. In a group, 40 persons speak Hindi, 30 speak English and 15 speak both. How many persons are in the group if everyone speaks at least one of these languages?
(a) 70 (b) 60 (c) 55 (d) 45
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is simply adding 40 + 30 = 70, which double-counts the 15 who speak both. Total = 40 + 30 − 15 = 55. The answer is 55.

Solution: Passed in at least one subject = 25 + 20 − 8 = 37. Adding the 5 who failed in both gives 37 + 5 = 42. Forgetting the 5 failures gives the wrong answer 37. The answer is 42.
