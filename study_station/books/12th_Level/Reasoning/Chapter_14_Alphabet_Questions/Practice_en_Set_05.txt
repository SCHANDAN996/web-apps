101. Which letter is 7th to the right of the 13th letter of the English alphabet?
(a) S (b) T (c) U (d) R
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option S comes from mis-counting the 20th letter as S. The 13th letter is M; 7th to its right is the 20th letter, which is T, so the answer is T.
Source: PYQ-style

102. How many letters are there between the 8th letter from the left and the 6th letter from the right in the English alphabet?
(a) 14 (b) 11 (c) 13 (d) 12
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 13 comes from subtracting 8 and 6 from 26 and forgetting that the two end letters themselves are excluded. The 6th from right is the 21st from left; letters between the 8th and 21st = 21 − 8 − 1 = 12, so the answer is 12.
Source: PYQ-style

103. If the English alphabet is written in reverse order (Z, Y, X, …, A), which letter will be 10th from the left?
(a) P (b) Q (c) R (d) S
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option P comes from counting 10 from A in the normal alphabet instead of from Z in the reversed one. Counting Z as 1: Z, Y, X, W, V, U, T, S, R, Q — the 10th letter is Q, so the answer is Q.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Which letter lies exactly midway between E and S in the English alphabet?
(a) K (b) M (c) L (d) N
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option M comes from taking the middle of the whole alphabet instead of the middle of E and S. E is 5th and S is 19th; the midpoint is (5 + 19) ÷ 2 = 12, and the 12th letter is L, so the answer is L.
Source: PYQ-style

105. In the word "CREATIVE", how many pairs of letters have as many letters between them in the word as in the English alphabet?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 2 comes from missing the pair T–V, which has one letter (I) between them in the word and one letter (U) between them in the alphabet. The matching pairs are C–E (one letter between in both), A–E (three letters between in both) and T–V (one letter between in both), so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

106. If the letters of the word "DANGER" are arranged in alphabetical order, which letter will be third from the left?
(a) N (b) E (c) G (d) D
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option G comes from mis-sorting the letters and skipping E. The alphabetical order is A, D, E, G, N, R, so the third letter from the left is E, so the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

107. What should come next in the series: A, C, F, J, O, ?
(a) S (b) T (c) U (d) V
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option T comes from assuming a constant gap of 3. The gaps are +2, +3, +4, +5, so the next gap is +6: O + 6 = U, so the answer is U.
Source: PYQ-style

108. In the series Z, X, U, Q, ?, the gaps increase by one each time. Which letter completes it?
(a) M (b) N (c) K (d) L
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option M comes from taking the last gap as −4 instead of −5. The gaps are −2, −3, −4, so the next gap is −5: Q − 5 = L, so the answer is L.
Source: PYQ-style

109. If A = 1, B = 2, … Z = 26, what is the sum of the letter values of the word "CAB"?
(a) 6 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 5 comes from taking B = 1 by mistake. C = 3, A = 1, B = 2, so the sum is 3 + 1 + 2 = 6, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

110. Which of the following words will appear first in a dictionary?
(a) FRINGE (b) FRIEND (c) FRIDGE (d) FRIGHT
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option FRIEND looks early, but compare the fourth letters: D, E, G, N. Since D comes earliest in the alphabet, FRIDGE comes first, so the answer is FRIDGE.
Source: PYQ-style

111. How many meaningful English words can be formed using the letters E, A, T (each used once)?
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 4 comes from counting a non-word or repeating a word. The valid words are EAT, TEA and ATE — three words, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

112. In a row of letters, P is 9th from the left and Q is 14th from the right. If they interchange positions, P becomes 17th from the left. How many letters are there in the row?
(a) 30 (b) 29 (c) 31 (d) 32
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 31 comes from adding 17 and 14 without subtracting the overlap of 1. After interchange, P sits at Q's old position, which is 17th from the left and 14th from the right, so total = 17 + 14 − 1 = 30, so the answer is 30.
Source: PYQ-style

113. Which letter is 5th to the left of the letter which is 4th to the right of H?
(a) F (b) H (c) E (d) G
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option F comes from subtracting one extra step while combining the two movements. H is the 8th letter; 4th to its right is L (12th), and 5th to the left of L is the 7th letter G, so the answer is G.
Source: PYQ-style

114. If every alternate letter of the alphabet starting from B is removed, which letter will be 8th in the new series?
(a) O (b) P (c) Q (d) N
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option P comes from removing A, C, E… instead of B, D, F…. Removing B, D, F, … leaves A, C, E, G, I, K, M, O, …, and the 8th letter is O, so the answer is O.
Source: PYQ-style

115. In the word "MACHINE", which letter is 3rd to the right of the letter that is 2nd from the left?
(a) I (b) H (c) C (d) N
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option H comes from counting the starting letter as the first step. The 2nd letter from the left is A; moving 3 right gives C(1), H(2), I(3), so the answer is I.
Source: PYQ-style

116. What comes next: B, E, I, N, ?
(a) S (b) U (c) T (d) R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option S comes from assuming the gaps stay at +5. The gaps are +3, +4, +5, so the next gap is +6: N + 6 = T, so the answer is T.
Source: PYQ-style

117. If the word "PLANT" is coded by shifting each letter +2, what is the code?
(a) QNDQW (b) RNCOV (c) RNCPW (d) RNCPV
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option RNCPW comes from shifting T by 3 instead of 2. P→R, L→N, A→C, N→P, T→V, giving RNCPV, so the answer is RNCPV.
Source: PYQ-style

118. How many vowels are there among the first 10 letters of the English alphabet?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 4 comes from counting an extra letter as a vowel. Among A to J, the vowels are A, E and I — three vowels, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

119. In the series A, Z, B, Y, C, X, ?, what comes next?
(a) D (b) W (c) E (d) V
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option W comes from continuing only the reverse half of this interleaved series. The odd positions run A, B, C, D and the even positions run Z, Y, X, W; the 7th term belongs to the forward half, so the answer is D.
Source: PYQ-style

120. Which group of letters is the odd one out?
(a) BDF (b) CEG (c) DFJ (d) ACE
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option CEG looks different because it starts with C, but it follows the same +2 pattern. ACE, BDF and CEG all have a uniform gap of +2, while DFJ has gaps of +2 then +4, so the answer is DFJ.
Source: PYQ-style

121. If the first half of the alphabet is reversed, which letter will be 10th from the left?
(a) C (b) E (c) D (d) B
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option C comes from reversing only the first 11 letters instead of the first 13. Reversing A–M gives M, L, K, J, I, H, G, F, E, D, …, and counting to the 10th position gives D, so the answer is D.
Source: PYQ-style

122. In the word "GARDEN", how many pairs of letters have as many letters between them in the word as in the English alphabet?
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 3 comes from counting A–D, which has one letter between in the word but two letters (B, C) between in the alphabet. The matching pairs are D–E (adjacent in both) and G–D (two letters between in both), so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

123. A series follows the rule: multiply the term number by 2, then subtract 1: 1st term A(1), 2nd term C(3), 3rd term E(5)… What is the 10th term?
(a) Q (b) R (c) T (d) S
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option T comes from computing 2 × 10 = 20 and forgetting to subtract 1. The position is 2 × 10 − 1 = 19, and the 19th letter is S, so the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style

124. If the alphabet is divided into two halves and the second half is placed before the first half, which letter will be 5th to the right of the 15th letter?
(a) G (b) H (c) E (d) F
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option F comes from mis-counting the 15th letter of the new order as A. The new order is N, O, P, …, Z, A, B, …, M; the 15th letter is B, and 5th to its right is G, so the answer is G.
Source: PYQ-style

125. In a certain pattern, each letter is replaced by the letter whose position equals the sum of the digits of the original position squared (e.g., B = 2 → 4 → D). What does F become?
(a) H (b) I (c) G (d) J
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option G comes from adding the digits of 6 instead of squaring first. F = 6; 6² = 36; 3 + 6 = 9, and the 9th letter is I, so the answer is I.
Source: PYQ-style
