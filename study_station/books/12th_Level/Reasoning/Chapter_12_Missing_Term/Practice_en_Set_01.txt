1. Find the missing term in the series: 3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ?
(a) 38 (b) 36 (c) 40 (d) 42
Answer: (a)
Solution: The differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 (consecutive odd numbers). So 27 + 11 = 38. The tempting option 36 comes from wrongly adding 9 again, but the difference must grow by 2 each time, so the answer is 38.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Find the missing term: 5, 10, 20, 40, 80, ?
(a) 120 (b) 160 (c) 140 (d) 180
Answer: (b)
Solution: Each term is double the previous one (×2 pattern). 80 × 2 = 160. Option 120 tempts those who add 40 instead of multiplying, so the answer is 160.

3. Find the missing term: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
