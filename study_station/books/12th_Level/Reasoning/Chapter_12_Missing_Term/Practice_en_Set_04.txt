76. Find the missing term: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ?
(a) 95 (b) 127 (c) 131 (d) 111
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 131 comes from adding 64 instead of doubling, which breaks the pattern. Each term is doubled and 1 added: 3×2+1=7, 7×2+1=15, and so on, so 63×2+1=127, so the answer is 127.
Source: PYQ-style
