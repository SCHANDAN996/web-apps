126. Arrange the following words as they would appear in an English dictionary:
1. Quarantine 2. Quantity 3. Quarter 4. Quarrel
(a) 4, 3, 2, 1 (b) 1, 2, 4, 3 (c) 1, 4, 2, 3 (d) 2, 1, 4, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming all four share "Quar"; in fact Quantity has 'n' as its fourth letter while the other three have 'r', and since 'n' < 'r', Quantity comes first. Among the "Quar-" words, fifth letters are 'a' (Quarantine), 'r' (Quarrel), 't' (Quarter), giving Quarantine, Quarrel, Quarter. So the answer is 2, 1, 4, 3.
