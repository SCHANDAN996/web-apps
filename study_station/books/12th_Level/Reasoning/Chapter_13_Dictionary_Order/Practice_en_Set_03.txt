51. Arrange the following words in dictionary order:
1. Plaster 2. Planet 3. Plastic 4. Plank
(a) 2, 4, 3, 1 (b) 4, 2, 1, 3 (c) 4, 2, 3, 1 (d) 2, 4, 1, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: All words begin with "Pla". Compare the fourth letter: Plank and Planet have n, Plastic and Plaster have s, and n comes before s. Between Planet and Plank, the fifth letters are e and k, so Planet precedes Plank; between Plaster and Plastic, the sixth letters are e and i, so Plaster precedes Plastic. The tempting option puts Plank first by ignoring the fifth letter, so the answer is 2, 4, 1, 3.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Which word will come third in dictionary order?
(a) Grasp (b) Grammar (c) Grand (d) Granary
Answer: (c)
Solution: All start with "Gra". Third letters differ: Grammar has m, Granary and Grand have n, Grasp has s, so Grammar is first and Grasp is last. Between Granary and Grand, the fifth letters are a and d, so Granary comes before Grand. The order is Grammar, Granary, Grand, Grasp, so the answer is Grand.
