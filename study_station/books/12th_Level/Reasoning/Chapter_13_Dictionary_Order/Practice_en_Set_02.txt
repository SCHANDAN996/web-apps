26. Arrange the following words in dictionary order and choose the one that comes FIRST:
1. Pencil  2. Penalty  3. Pension  4. Pendant
(a) Pencil (b) Pension (c) Penalty (d) Pendant
Answer: (c)
Solution: All words start with "Pen", so compare the fourth letter: Pencil (c), Penalty (a), Pension (s), Pendant (d). Since a comes before c, d and s, Penalty comes first. The tempting wrong choice is Pencil because "pen" is the most familiar word, but its fourth letter c comes after a. So the answer is Penalty.
Source: PYQ-style
