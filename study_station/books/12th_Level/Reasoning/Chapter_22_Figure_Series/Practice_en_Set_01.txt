1. In a figure series, a dot moves one corner clockwise in each step inside a square. If the dot is at the top-left corner in the first figure, where will it be in the fifth figure?
(a) Top-right (b) Bottom-right (c) Top-left (d) Bottom-left
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is bottom-right, which assumes the dot moves two corners per step. The dot moves one corner clockwise each step: top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left → top-left. After four moves it returns to the start, so the answer is top-left.
Source: PYQ-style

2. A series shows a triangle, then a square, then a pentagon, then a hexagon. Which figure comes next?
(a) Octagon (b) Heptagon (c) Circle (d) Square
Answer: (b)
Solution: The number of sides increases by one each time: 3, 4, 5, 6. The next figure must have 7 sides, so the answer is heptagon.
Source: PYQ-style

3. In a series, a small circle inside a square alternates between shaded and unshaded: shaded, unshaded, shaded, unshaded. What is the fifth figure?
(a) Unshaded circle (b) No circle (c) Shaded square (d) Shaded circle
Answer: (d)
Solution: Odd positions carry the shaded circle and even positions the unshaded one. The fifth position is odd, so the answer is shaded circle.
Source: PYQ-style

4. A line segment rotates 45° clockwise in each step. Starting vertical, what is its orientation after two steps?
(a) Horizontal (b) Vertical (c) Diagonal at 45° (d) Diagonal at 135°
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vertical is 90°; after one step it becomes a 45° diagonal, and after two steps it becomes 0°, i.e. horizontal. The diagonal option tempts those who stop after one step, so the answer is horizontal.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Find the odd figure out: four figures contain a star inside a circle; in three of them the star touches the circle, in one it does not. Which is the odd one?
(a) The figure where the star touches the circle (b) The figure with the largest star (c) The figure where the star does not touch the circle (d) The figure with the smallest circle
Answer: (c)
Solution: Classification depends on the common property shared by three figures. Three stars touch the circle, so the figure where the star does not touch the circle is the odd one out.

6. A series shows arrows pointing up, right, down, left, up. Which direction comes next?

7. In a series the number of dots in a box goes 1, 2, 4, 8. How many dots appear in the next figure?
(a) 10 (b) 16 (c) 12 (d) 14

8. A square is divided into 4 equal small squares. In each step one more small square is shaded, going left to right, top row first. If two squares are shaded in the second figure, how many are shaded in the fourth figure?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: Shaded squares increase by one per step: 1, 2, 3, 4. By the fourth figure all four small squares are shaded, so the answer is 4.

9. Find the odd one out among: circle, triangle, square, rectangle.
(a) Triangle (b) Circle (c) Square (d) Rectangle
Answer: (b)
Solution: Triangle, square and rectangle are all made of straight line segments; only the circle is a curved figure, so the answer is circle.

10. A figure series shows a plus sign rotating 90° anticlockwise each step. Since a plus sign looks identical after a 90° rotation, what should a candidate check to find the pattern?
(a) The size of the plus sign (b) The colour of the page (c) An attached marker such as a dot on one arm (d) The thickness of the border
Answer: (c)
Solution: A symmetric figure hides rotation, so examiners attach a marker like a dot or arrow to one arm to reveal it. Tracking that marker exposes the rotation, so the answer is an attached marker such as a dot on one arm.

11. In a series, a black dot moves along the sides of a triangle, one vertex per step, clockwise. Starting at the top vertex, where is the dot in the fourth figure?
(a) Bottom-right vertex (b) Bottom-left vertex (c) Top vertex (d) Midpoint of the base
Answer: (c)
Solution: A triangle has 3 vertices, so after 3 moves the dot completes one full cycle and returns to the top vertex in the fourth figure. The bottom vertices tempt those who miscount the cycle, so the answer is top vertex.

21. In a series, two elements change together: a dot moves one corner clockwise each step, and a cross moves one corner anticlockwise each step inside the same square. In the first figure the dot is at top-left and the cross is at top-right. Where are they in the third figure?
(a) Dot at bottom-right, cross at bottom-left (b) Dot at bottom-left, cross at bottom-right (c) Dot at top-right, cross at top-left (d) Both at bottom-right
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is tracking only one element and picking the option where positions are swapped. The dot goes top-left → top-right → bottom-right, while the cross goes top-right → top-left → bottom-left, so the answer is dot at bottom-right, cross at bottom-left.
Source: PYQ-style

22. A series shows figures made of matchsticks: 3 sticks, 5 sticks, 7 sticks, 9 sticks. How many sticks does the sixth figure need?
(a) 11 (b) 13 (c) 15 (d) 12

Solution: The count rises by 2 each step: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13. The option 11 is only the fifth figure, a common one-step-short trap, so the answer is 13.

23. Find the odd figure out: four figures each show a circle divided into equal parts — one into 2 parts, one into 3 parts, one into 4 parts, one into 6 parts. Three figures have all parts shaded alternately starting with a shaded part; one figure starts with an unshaded part. Which is odd?
(a) The figure divided into 2 parts (b) The figure divided into 6 parts (c) The figure divided into 4 parts (d) The figure whose shading starts with an unshaded part
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to classify by the number of parts, which differs in every figure and cannot isolate one. The shared property is the shading pattern starting with a shaded part, so the figure whose shading starts with an unshaded part is the odd one out.
Source: PYQ-style

24. In a series, a square rotates 45° clockwise each step while an inner circle alternates between shaded and unshaded every step. The first figure has an upright square with a shaded circle. What does the fourth figure show?
(a) Square rotated 135°, shaded circle (b) Square rotated 135°, unshaded circle (c) Square rotated 90°, unshaded circle (d) Upright square, shaded circle
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is counting the first figure as step one of the rotation, giving 90°. Rotation applies per move: after three moves the square has turned 3 × 45° = 135°, and the even fourth position carries an unshaded circle, so the answer is square rotated 135°, unshaded circle.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A series shows the number of sides of the outer figure as 3, 4, 5, 6, while the number of inner dots goes 6, 5, 4, 3. What does the fifth figure contain?
(a) 6-sided figure with 3 dots (b) 7-sided figure with 2 dots (c) 7-sided figure with 3 dots (d) 8-sided figure with 2 dots
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the double relationship: sides increase by one while dots decrease by one, and options mixing the two sequences mislead. The fifth figure has 7 sides and 2 dots, so the answer is 7-sided figure with 2 dots.
