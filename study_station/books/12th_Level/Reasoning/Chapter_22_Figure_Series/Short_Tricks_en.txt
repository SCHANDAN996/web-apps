# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Figure Series / Classification

---

**Box 1 — The "ROSE" Scan (First 10 seconds on any series)**

**R**otation → **O**bjects (count) → **S**hading → **E**lements (add/remove)

> "Every figure is a ROSE — smell all four petals before answering!"
> Check Rotation first (most common), then count of objects, then shading, then elements added/removed.

---

**Box 2 — "Clock Thief" for Rotation**

> "The thief steals time — 45°, 90°, 135°…"
> If an arrow/hand rotates, ask: is it stealing the SAME minutes each step (constant) or MORE each step (45→90→135→180)? Constant = simple series; increasing = acceleration series.

---

**Box 3 — "ODD Man Wears Odd Socks" (Classification)**

> In classification, 4 figures wear matching socks, 1 wears odd ones.
> Check ONE property at a time: closed/open, sides (odd/even), symmetry, shading position, line direction. The moment 4 match and 1 doesn't — STOP, that's your answer. Don't keep hunting.

---

**Box 4 — "Count Before You Think"**

> "Fingers before brains!"
> Before any logic, count: lines, dots, circles, triangles, corners. Many series questions crack on pure counting (3,4,5,6… or 5,4,3,2…) — so counting first is never wasted time.

---

**Box 5 — "Mirror Mirror, Water Water"**

> Mirror flips LEFT-RIGHT (like a mirror beside you); Water flips TOP-BOTTOM (like a lake below you).
> Mnemonic: **"Mirror = Left-Right, Water = Up-Down"** — say it twice, never confuse again.

---

**Box 6 — "The Shading Elevator"**

> Shaded part moves like a lift: up one floor each step, or down.
> Track the black/dark region's position (corner 1→2→3→4). If it jumps 2 corners, it's an express lift!

---

**Box 7 — "New Baby, Old Baby" (Element Addition)**

> "Each step, a new baby is born and the oldest may retire."
> Series often add one element per step, or add one + remove one. Ask: "What's NEW here? What's MISSING here?"

---

**Box 8 — "Alternate Twins"**

> "When one pattern fails, split the family into twins: 1-3-5 and 2-4-6."
> If figures 1→2→3 make no sense, check odd positions separately and even positions separately. Two simple patterns often hide inside one confusing series.

---

**Box 9 — "Corner Clock" for Position Tracking**

> Number the 4 corners like a clock: 12, 3, 6, 9.
> A dot moving 12→3→6→9 is +1 step clockwise; 12→6→12 is jumping 2. Say the positions aloud in your head — faster than staring.

---

**Box 10 — "Line Count = Side Count" Trap-Buster**

> "A triangle has 3 sides, not 3 wishes."
> In classification, examiners mix figures where lines inside = sides of figure (4 figures) vs. lines ≠ sides (1 figure). Always compare inner lines with outer sides.

---

**Box 11 — "Symmetry Samurai"**

> "The samurai cuts every figure with his sword — if both halves match, it's symmetric."
> In classification, quickly slash each figure mentally: vertical cut, horizontal cut. 4 will survive the cut, 1 won't.

---

**Box 12 — "Open Mouth, Closed Mouth"**

> "4 figures close their mouth, 1 forgets."
> Open vs. closed figures is the laziest examiner trick — check it in the first 3 seconds of classification.

---

**Box 13 — "45-90-180 Ladder"**

> Rotation angles climb a ladder: 45° (half-step), 90° (full step), 180° (U-turn).
> If a figure flips completely upside down, that's 180° — don't mistake it for a mirror image! 180° rotation ≠ mirror flip. (Mirror = left-right swap; 180° = both swaps.)

---

**Box 14 — "Same Family, Different Face"**

> In classification, ask: "Do 4 figures belong to one family (all curved / all straight / all 4-sided)?"
> The odd one is the outsider at the family photo. Curved vs. straight lines is the #1 family test.

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Leave the Question

**Tip 1 — The 15-Second Rule:**
If after 15 seconds you cannot name the changing property (rotation / count / shading / position), mark and SKIP. Figure series questions are designed to be solved in 20–30 seconds; if the pattern isn't visible by then, staring longer rarely helps. Return only if time remains.

**Tip 2 — Two-Property Confusion = Skip:**
If you find TWO different logics giving TWO different answers, the question is eating your time. Don't debate with the examiner — skip, because re-checking both logics costs 2 minutes for 1 mark.

**Tip 3 — Never Skip Classification Blindly:**
Classification (odd-one-out) questions are usually FASTER than series — the odd property (open/closed, curved/straight, symmetry) appears within 10 seconds. Attempt all classification questions first; skip only the series questions where the pattern stays invisible after the ROSE scan.
