101. Statements: P > Q ≥ R = S; S < T
Conclusions: I. P > T  II. Q ≥ S
(a) Only I follows (b) Both follow (c) Only II follows (d) Neither follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is conclusion I: since S < T and P > S, students assume P > T, but P and T are not directly comparable — P could be less than, equal to, or greater than T. Conclusion II follows directly because Q ≥ R = S means Q ≥ S. So the answer is that only II follows.
