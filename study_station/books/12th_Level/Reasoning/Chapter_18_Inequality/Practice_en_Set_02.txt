26. In the following question, the relationship between different elements is shown in the statements. Decide which of the conclusions follows.
Statements: P > Q ≥ R; R = S < T
Conclusions: I. P > S  II. T > R
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting error is to reject conclusion I because P and S are not directly compared. But P > Q ≥ R and R = S, so P > S, hence I follows. Also T > S = R, so T > R, hence II follows. So the answer is both I and II follow.
