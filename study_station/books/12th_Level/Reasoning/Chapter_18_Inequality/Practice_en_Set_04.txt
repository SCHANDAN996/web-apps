Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Statement: P > Q ≥ R = S < T
Conclusions: I. P > S  II. T > R
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Neither I nor II follows (d) Both I and II follow
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting error is to reject Conclusion II because the chain breaks at S < T; but R = S and S < T together give R < T, i.e., T > R. Also P > Q ≥ R = S gives P > S. Both conclusions follow, so the answer is both I and II follow.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Statement: A ≥ B > C = D ≤ E
Conclusions: I. A > D  II. B > E
(a) Neither I nor II follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Only I follows
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students mark Conclusion II thinking B > C = D ≤ E keeps B greater, but the sign reverses at D ≤ E, so B and E cannot be compared. However A ≥ B > C = D gives A > D. Only I follows, so the answer is only I follows.
