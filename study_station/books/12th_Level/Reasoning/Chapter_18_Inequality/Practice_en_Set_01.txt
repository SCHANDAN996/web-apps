1. In the expression P > Q ≥ R = S, which of the following is definitely true?
(a) P < S (b) P = S (c) P > S (d) P ≥ S
Answer: (c)
Solution: Since P > Q and Q ≥ R = S, we get P > Q ≥ S, so P is strictly greater than S. Option (d) is tempting but "≥" is weaker than what the chain guarantees. So the answer is P > S.

2. Given A ≥ B > C = D, which conclusion follows?
(a) A < D (b) A = D (c) A > D (d) B = D
Answer: (c)
Solution: A ≥ B and B > C = D gives A ≥ B > D, hence A > D. Option (d) fails because B > C = D means B is strictly greater than D. So the answer is A > D.

21. Statements: P ≥ Q = R, R < S, S ≤ T. Conclusions: I. P < T  II. Q < T. Which conclusion(s) follow(s)?
(a) Only II follows (b) Only I follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: Combining: P ≥ Q = R < S ≤ T. For II: Q = R < S ≤ T gives Q < T, so II follows. For I: P ≥ Q but P's relation with T cannot be fixed — P could be greater than, equal to, or less than T — so I does not follow. Option (c) is the trap for those who assume P ≥ Q forces P < T. So the answer is Only II follows.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Statements: A > B ≥ C, C = D, D > E. Conclusions: I. A > E  II. B > D. Which conclusion(s) follow(s)?
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: Combining: A > B ≥ C = D > E. For I: A > B ≥ D > E gives A > E, so I follows. For II: B ≥ C = D means B ≥ D, not definitely B > D, so II does not follow. Option (c) is the trap — "≥" does not guarantee strict ">". So the answer is Only I follows.

Answer: (b)

24. Statements: X ≥ Y > Z, Z = W, W < V. Conclusions: I. X > W  II. Y > V. Which conclusion(s) follow(s)?
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: Combining: X ≥ Y > Z = W < V. For I: X ≥ Y > W gives X > W, so I follows. For II: Y > W and W < V — the sign reverses between Y and V, so Y and V cannot be compared; II does not follow. Option (c) is the trap for those who chain through W ignoring the reversed sign. So the answer is Only I follows.
Source: PYQ-style
