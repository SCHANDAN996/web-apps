100. Find the missing term: 1, 4, 27, 256, ?
(a) 1024 (b) 3125 (c) 625 (d) 1296
Answer: (b)
Solution: Each term is n raised to the power n: 1¹ = 1, 2² = 4, 3³ = 27, 4⁴ = 256, so the next term is 5⁵ = 3125. The trap is 1024, which is 4⁵ and mixes up the base and the power, so the answer is 3125.
Source: PYQ-style
