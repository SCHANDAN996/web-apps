126. Find the missing term: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?, 56
(a) 40 (b) 42 (c) 44 (d) 46
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 40 comes from wrongly adding a constant 10 to 30. The differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, so the next difference is 12, giving 30 + 12 = 42 (terms are n(n+1): 1×2, 2×3, …, 6×7 = 42), so the answer is 42.
