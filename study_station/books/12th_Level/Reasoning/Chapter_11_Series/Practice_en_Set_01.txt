1. Find the next term: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?
(a) 36 (b) 30 (c) 42 (d) 48
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each term is double the previous one: 3×2=6, 6×2=12, 12×2=24, so 24×2=48. The tempting option 36 comes from wrongly adding 12 instead of multiplying by 2, so the answer is 48.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: This is a simple arithmetic series with common difference 5. After 20 comes 20+5=25, so the answer is 25.

21. Find the next term: 2, 3, 5, 9, 17, ?
(a) 31 (b) 35 (c) 34 (d) 33
Answer: (d)
Solution: Examiner's trap — the differences 1, 2, 4, 8 look like simple doubling, so many candidates double 17 to get 34. But the rule is: each term = previous term + next power of 2, i.e. 17+16=33. Equivalently, each term = 2×previous −1. So the answer is 33.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Find the next term: 5, 11, 23, 47, 95, ?
(a) 189 (b) 191 (c) 190 (d) 193
