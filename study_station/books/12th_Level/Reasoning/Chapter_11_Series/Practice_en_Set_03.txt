51. Find the missing term: 7, 12, 19, 28, 39, ?
(a) 48 (b) 50 (c) 54 (d) 52
Answer: (d)
Solution: The differences are 5, 7, 9, 11 — consecutive odd numbers, so the next difference is 13, not 11 again (the tempting trap of repeating the last gap). Adding 13 to 39 gives 52, so the answer is 52.
