101. Statement: "Join our spoken English course and get a job in a multinational company," an advertisement claims.
Assumptions:
I. Spoken English skills help in getting jobs in multinational companies.
II. People who join the course will definitely get jobs.
(a) Neither I nor II is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Only I is implicit (d) Both I and II are implicit
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice assumes the advertisement's guarantee is taken literally, but an assumption must be what the speaker takes for granted, not an exaggerated promise. The advertiser assumes spoken English improves job prospects, which is why the course is advertised; the word "definitely" makes II too extreme, so the answer is Only I is implicit.
Source: PYQ-style
