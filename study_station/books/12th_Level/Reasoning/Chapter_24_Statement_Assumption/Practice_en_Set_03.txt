51. Statement: "Buy our new vitamin tablets and you will never fall sick again," an advertisement claims.
Assumptions:
I. People may be attracted by such a claim and buy the tablets.
II. Vitamin tablets alone can guarantee that a person never falls sick.
(a) Neither I nor II is implicit (b) Only assumption II is implicit (c) Both I and II are implicit (d) Only assumption I is implicit
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is to treat the advertiser's claim as a belief the advertiser holds, but an advertiser need not believe the claim is literally true; he only assumes people will respond to it. Assumption II states a medical fact the advertiser does not necessarily assume. Hence only assumption I is implicit, so the answer is Only assumption I is implicit.
