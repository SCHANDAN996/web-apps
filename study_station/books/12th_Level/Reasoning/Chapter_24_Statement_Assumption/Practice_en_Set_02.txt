26. Statement: The municipal corporation has decided to impose a fine on people who litter in public places.
Assumptions:
I. Imposing a fine may deter people from littering.
II. People are currently littering in public places.
(a) Only assumption II is implicit (b) Only assumption I is implicit (c) Both assumptions I and II are implicit (d) Neither assumption I nor assumption II is implicit
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is to pick only assumption I, thinking the decision is only about deterrence. But a fine is imposed only when the problem exists, so littering must be occurring, and the purpose of the fine is deterrence. So the answer is that both assumptions I and II are implicit.
