1. Statement: "Buy our new shampoo and get silky hair in just one week," an advertisement.
Assumptions:
I. People want silky hair.
II. The shampoo can make hair silky in one week.
(a) Both I and II are implicit (b) Only I is implicit (c) Neither I nor II is implicit (d) Only II is implicit
Answer: (a)
Solution: An advertisement works only if the advertiser assumes people desire the promised benefit, so I is implicit. The specific promise of one week also assumes the product can deliver it, so II is implicit. Hence the answer is that both I and II are implicit.
