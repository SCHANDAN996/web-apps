Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Statement: "The government should ban all private vehicles from city centres to reduce pollution."
Assumptions:
I. Private vehicles are a major source of pollution in city centres.
II. Public transport in city centres is already adequate for all commuters.
III. Banning private vehicles will completely eliminate pollution from city centres.
(a) Only I and III are implicit (b) Only I and II are implicit (c) Only I is implicit (d) All I, II and III are implicit
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting choice is to treat everything connected with the proposal as assumed, but an assumption is only what the speaker must believe while speaking. The speaker must believe private vehicles significantly cause the pollution (I); adequacy of public transport (II) is a separate factual claim the speaker may not hold, and complete elimination of pollution (III) is an extreme outcome no such proposal needs to assume. So the answer is Only I is implicit.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Statement: A company tells its employees, "Work from home will be allowed only for those who complete their projects before deadlines."
Assumptions:
I. Some employees complete projects before deadlines.
II. Work from home is considered a reward by employees.
III. All employees want to work from home.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) All are implicit (c) Only I and II are implicit (d) Only II and III are implicit
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is III — the company need not assume every single employee wants work from home; offering it as a conditional benefit only requires that it is generally valued (II). The rule is meaningful only if at least some employees can meet the condition (I). So the answer is Only I and II are implicit.
