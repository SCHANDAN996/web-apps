76. Statement: "Join our spoken English course and get a job in a multinational company within six months." — an advertisement.
Assumptions:
I. People who join the course may get jobs in multinational companies.
II. Spoken English skills are valued by multinational companies.
(a) Neither I nor II is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Only I is implicit (d) Both I and II are implicit
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting error is to reject I because the promise sounds exaggerated, but an assumption only needs to underlie the statement, not be guaranteed. The ad promises jobs through spoken English, so it assumes both that the course can lead to such jobs and that companies value the skill, so the answer is both I and II are implicit.
