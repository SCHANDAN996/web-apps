126. Statements: All pens are books. Some books are chairs. No chair is a table.
Conclusions: I. Some pens are chairs. II. Some books are not tables.
(a) Only I follows (b) Neither I nor II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Only II follows
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is Conclusion I: "All pens are books" plus "Some books are chairs" does not connect pens to chairs, since the books that are chairs may not be the pens, so I does not follow. For II, all books that are chairs are not tables (no chair is a table), and some books are chairs, so some books are definitely not tables. Hence only II follows, so the answer is (d).
