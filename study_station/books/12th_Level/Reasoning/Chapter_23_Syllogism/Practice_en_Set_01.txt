1. Statements: All pens are books. All books are chairs.
Conclusions: I. All pens are chairs. II. All chairs are pens.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Conclusion II, which merely reverses "All pens are chairs"; a universal statement can never be converted into another universal. Since pens lie inside books and books inside chairs, all pens are certainly chairs, so the answer is Only I follows.
