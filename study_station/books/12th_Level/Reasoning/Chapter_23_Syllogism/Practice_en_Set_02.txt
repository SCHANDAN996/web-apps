Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Statements: All pens are books. All books are chairs.
Conclusions: I. All pens are chairs. II. All chairs are pens.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting trap is Conclusion II — when all pens are chairs, some students assume the reverse also holds, but "all A are B" never converts to "all B are A". From the two statements, pens ⊂ books ⊂ chairs, so every pen is a chair, making I true. Chairs may include many things other than pens, so II fails. Hence only I follows, so the answer is (a).
Source: PYQ-style

27. Statements: Some dogs are cats. Some cats are rats.
Conclusions: I. Some dogs are rats. II. Some rats are dogs.
(a) Only I follows (b) Neither I nor II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Only II follows
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap here is assuming that two "some" chains connect — dogs–cats and cats–rats share "cats", but the cats that are dogs may be completely different from the cats that are rats. No definite link between dogs and rats is established, so neither I nor II can be concluded with certainty, so the answer is (b).
