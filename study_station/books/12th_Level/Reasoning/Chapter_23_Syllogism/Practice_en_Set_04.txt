Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Statements: All pens are books. All books are chairs.
Conclusions: I. All pens are chairs. II. All chairs are pens.
(a) Neither I nor II follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Only I follows
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is Conclusion II, which reverses the chain. From "All pens are books" and "All books are chairs", pens lie inside chairs, so all pens are chairs — I follows. But chairs may include many things other than pens, so II does not follow. Hence the answer is Only I follows.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Conclusion I — two "some" statements with different middle links never give a definite connection between dogs and rats. However, "Some dogs are cats" directly converts to "Some cats are dogs", so II follows. Hence the answer is Only II follows.
