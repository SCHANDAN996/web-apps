1. Statement: Should India make voting compulsory for all citizens?
Arguments:
I. Yes, it will increase voter participation and strengthen democracy.
II. No, forcing people to vote violates their personal freedom.
(a) Both arguments I and II are strong (b) Only argument II is strong (c) Only argument I is strong (d) Neither argument I nor II is strong
Answer: (a)
Solution: Argument I is strong because higher participation genuinely reflects the people's will. Argument II is also strong because compulsion curtails individual liberty, a valid democratic concern. Both address important, relevant aspects, so both arguments are strong.
