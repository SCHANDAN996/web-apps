26. Statement: Should the government make voting compulsory for all eligible citizens?
Arguments:
I. Yes, compulsory voting ensures that election results truly reflect the will of the entire population.
II. No, forcing people to vote violates the individual's freedom of choice, which includes the right not to participate.
(a) Both arguments I and II are strong (b) Only argument II is strong (c) Only argument I is strong (d) Neither argument I nor II is strong
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to treat one side as merely emotional; both rest on core democratic principles. Argument I is strong because higher turnout makes results more representative, and argument II is strong because democratic freedom includes the choice to abstain, so the answer is that both arguments I and II are strong.
