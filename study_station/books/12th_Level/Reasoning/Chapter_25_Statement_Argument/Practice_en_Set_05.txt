101. Statement: Should the government ban the use of plastic bags completely?
Argument I: Yes, plastic bags are a major source of environmental pollution and harm animals.
Argument II: No, banning plastic bags will increase the cost of packaging for small shopkeepers.
(a) Both Arguments I and II are strong (b) Only Argument II is strong (c) Only Argument I is strong (d) Neither Argument I nor Argument II is strong
Answer: (a)
Solution: Argument I is strong because environmental protection is a matter of public interest and plastic pollution is a proven harm. Argument II is also strong because a sudden ban imposes a real economic burden on small traders, which is a valid practical concern. Both sides raise genuine, relevant issues, so the answer is that both Arguments I and II are strong.
