76. Statement: Should the government ban the use of private vehicles in city centres to reduce pollution?
Arguments:
I. Yes, this will significantly reduce air pollution in crowded city centres.
II. No, it will cause great inconvenience to commuters who depend on private vehicles.
(a) Only argument I is strong (b) Neither I nor II is strong (c) Only argument II is strong (d) Both I and II are strong
Answer: (d)
Solution: Argument I is strong because cutting vehicular traffic in congested centres directly reduces emissions, a genuine public-interest benefit. Argument II is also strong because a ban without alternatives imposes real hardship on commuters. Both raise valid, relevant considerations, so the answer is that both I and II are strong.
