51. Statement: Should the government ban the use of private vehicles in city centres to reduce pollution?
Arguments:
I. Yes, vehicular emission is a major contributor to urban air pollution.
II. No, it would cause severe inconvenience to commuters who depend on private vehicles.
(a) Only argument I is strong (b) Both I and II are strong (c) Only argument II is strong (d) Neither I nor II is strong
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to treat inconvenience as trivial and pick only I; but a policy that ignores genuine public hardship is impractical, so II is also strong. Argument I directly addresses the stated objective of cutting pollution. So the answer is both I and II are strong.
