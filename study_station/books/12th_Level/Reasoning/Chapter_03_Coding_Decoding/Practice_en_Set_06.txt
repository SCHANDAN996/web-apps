126. In a certain code, "MASTER" is written as "OCUVGT". How will "PLANET" be written in that code?
(a) QNCPGV (b) RNCPGU (c) RMCPGV (d) RNCPGV
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each letter is shifted forward by 2: M→O, A→C, S→U, T→V, E→G, R→T. The trap is shifting only consonants or mixing +1 and +2, which gives tempting strings like RMCPGV. Applying +2 to PLANET: P→R, L→N, A→C, N→P, E→G, T→V, so the answer is RNCPGV.
