101. A standard die has opposite faces summing to 7. If the top face shows 5 and the front face shows 2, what is on the bottom face?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 6 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is option 1, which comes from subtracting 5 from 6 instead of 7. On a standard die, opposite faces sum to 7, so the face opposite the top face 5 is 7 − 5 = 2, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Two positions of a die are shown. In position 1, faces 1, 2, 3 are visible; in position 2, faces 1, 2, 4 are visible. Which number is opposite to 4?
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is option 5, which is simply the unseen face and cannot be concluded from two views. Faces 1 and 2 are common to both views, so the two non-common faces, 3 and 4, must be opposite each other, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

103. A cube is painted red on all faces and cut into 64 equal smaller cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 8 (b) 24 (c) 12 (d) 36
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is option 12, which counts only the edges and forgets the cubes along each edge. A 4×4×4 cube has 12 edges with (4 − 2) = 2 two-face cubes per edge, giving 12 × 2 = 24, so the answer is 24.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Visualising Solid Shapes)

104. A cube painted on all faces is cut into 27 equal cubes. How many small cubes have no painted face?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 6 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is option 8, which is the number of corner cubes, but corners have three painted faces. For a 3×3×3 cube, unpainted cubes = (3 − 2)³ = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Visualising Solid Shapes)

105. In a standard die, which number is on the face opposite to 4?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 5
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is option 5, which comes from wrongly pairing nearby numbers. Opposite faces of a standard die sum to 7, so the face opposite 4 is 7 − 4 = 3, so the answer is 3.

106. A standard die shows 6 on top and 4 on the front face. Which number is on the back face?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is option 1, which is opposite the top face 6, not the front face. The back face is opposite the front face 4, and 7 − 4 = 3, so the answer is 3.

107. A cube is painted blue on all faces and cut into 125 equal cubes. How many cubes have exactly one painted face?
(a) 64 (b) 48 (c) 36 (d) 54
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is option 36, which comes from using the wrong edge count in the formula. For a 5×5×5 cube, one-face cubes = 6 × (5 − 2)² = 6 × 9 = 54, so the answer is 54.

108. A standard die is placed so that the top shows 3 and the left face shows 2. What is on the right face?
(a) 1 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is option 4, which is opposite the top face 3, not the left face. Opposite pairs are (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), so the right face opposite 2 is 5, so the answer is 5.

109. A cube of side 6 cm is painted green and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have exactly three painted faces?
(a) 6 (b) 4 (c) 8 (d) 12
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is option 12, which counts the edges instead of the corners. Three-face cubes sit only at corners, and every cube has exactly 8 corners whatever its size, so the answer is 8.

(a) 6 (b) 5 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (a)

111. A wooden cube is painted on all sides and cut into 216 equal cubes. How many cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 24 (b) 60 (c) 36 (d) 48
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is option 60, which uses 12 × 5 and forgets to remove the two corner cubes on each edge. Side = 6, so two-face cubes = 12 × (6 − 2) = 48, so the answer is 48.

112. In a die, 1 is opposite 6, 2 is opposite 5, and 3 is opposite 4. Which of the following can NOT be a pair of adjacent faces?
(a) 1 and 2 (b) 3 and 4 (c) 4 and 6 (d) 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming any listed pair can touch; opposite faces never can. Since 3 and 4 are opposite, they can never be adjacent, while the other pairs are non-opposite, so the answer is 3 and 4.
Source: PYQ-style

113. A cube painted red is cut into 64 equal cubes. How many cubes have at least one painted face?
(a) 48 (b) 60 (c) 56 (d) 64
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is option 48, which comes from a wrong count of the inner cubes. Unpainted cubes = (4 − 2)³ = 8, so painted cubes = 64 − 8 = 56, so the answer is 56.

114. A cube painted on all faces is cut into 27 equal cubes. How many small cubes have at least two painted faces?

115. A cube of side 5 cm is painted on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 36 (b) 24 (c) 30 (d) 48

Solution: The trap is option 48, which multiplies 12 × 4 using side minus one instead of side minus two. Two-face cubes = 12 × (5 − 2) = 36, so the answer is 36.

(a) 729 (b) 343 (c) 216 (d) 512

118. Two dice are thrown. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers shown is exactly 7?
(a) 1/6 (b) 1/9 (c) 5/36 (d) 7/36
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is option 7/36, which counts one outcome too many. Favourable outcomes are (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3), (5,2), (6,1) — 6 out of 36, giving 1/6, so the answer is 1/6.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics (Probability)

119. A cube painted on all faces is cut into 27 cubes. The cubes with exactly one painted face are what fraction of all cubes?
(a) 4/9 (b) 1/3 (c) 2/9 (d) 1/9
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is option 1/3, which comes from counting 9 face cubes instead of 6. One-face cubes = 6 × (3 − 2)² = 6, and 6/27 = 2/9, so the answer is 2/9.

120. In a non-standard die, 1 is opposite 2, 3 is opposite 4, and 5 is opposite 6. If 1 is on top and 3 faces you, what is on the bottom?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is option 4, which is the back face opposite 3, not the bottom. The bottom is opposite the top face 1, and 1 is opposite 2, so the answer is 2.

121. A cube is painted red on two opposite faces, green on two opposite faces, and blue on the remaining two opposite faces. It is cut into 64 equal cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two colours on them?
(a) 16 (b) 32 (c) 24 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is option 32, which includes the corner cubes that actually carry three colours. Each of the 12 edges joins two different colours with (4 − 2) = 2 non-corner cubes per edge, giving 24, so the answer is 24.

122. A cube painted on all faces is cut into n³ equal cubes. The number of cubes with no painted face equals the number of cubes with exactly three painted faces. What is n?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is option 2, which solves n³ = 8 instead of (n − 2)³ = 8. Three-face cubes are always 8, and (n − 2)³ = 8 gives n − 2 = 2, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Three views of the same die show: View 1 — 1 top, 2 front, 3 right; View 2 — 2 top, 4 front, 1 right; View 3 — 3 top, 1 front, 5 right. Which number is opposite 2?
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is option 4, but 4 appears together with 2 in View 2, and adjacent faces are never opposite. Face 1 is adjacent to 2, 3, 4, 5 across the views, so 1 is opposite 6; then 2 is adjacent to 1, 3, 4 and cannot be opposite 6, leaving 5, so the answer is 5.
Source: PYQ-style

124. A large cube is formed of 125 identical small cubes. The entire surface is painted, and then the cube is dismantled. If one small cube is picked at random, what is the probability that it has exactly two painted faces?
(a) 36/125 (b) 24/125 (c) 48/125 (d) 12/125
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is option 48/125, which applies the formula for a 6×6×6 cube instead of 5×5×5. Two-face cubes = 12 × (5 − 2) = 36, so the probability is 36/125, so the answer is 36/125.
Source: PYQ-style

125. A cuboid of dimensions 4 cm × 5 cm × 6 cm is painted on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 36 (b) 44 (c) 52 (d) 60
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is option 52, which applies the cube formula 12 × (n − 2) and ignores the unequal dimensions. For a cuboid, two-face cubes = 4[(4 − 2) + (5 − 2) + (6 − 2)] = 4 × 9 = 36, so the answer is 36.
Source: PYQ-style
