126. A cube is painted red on all its faces and then cut into 125 identical smaller cubes. How many smaller cubes have exactly two faces painted?
(a) 8 (b) 48 (c) 44 (d) 36
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 48 comes from wrongly multiplying 12 edges by 4 cubes per edge, forgetting that corner cubes are counted in that method. For an n×n×n cube with n = 5, cubes with exactly two painted faces lie on edges excluding corners: 12 × (n − 2) = 12 × 3 = 36, so the answer is 36.
Source: PYQ-style

127. A cube of side 6 cm is painted on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have no face painted?
(a) 27 (b) 64 (c) 8 (d) 125
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to compute (n − 1)³ = 125, which is wrong; unpainted cubes form the inner cube of side (n − 2). Here (6 − 2)³ = 4³ = 64, so the answer is 64.
Source: PYQ-style

128. A cube is cut into 64 identical small cubes. If the big cube was painted green on all faces, how many small cubes have exactly one face painted?
(a) 16 (b) 8 (c) 24 (d) 32
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students answer 16 by computing 6 × (n − 2) but forgetting to square it. For n = 4, one-face-painted cubes = 6 × (n − 2)² = 6 × 4 = 24, so the answer is 24.
Source: PYQ-style

129. A wooden cube is painted blue on all faces and cut into 216 equal cubes. How many cubes have exactly three faces painted?
(a) 6 (b) 4 (c) 12 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 6 counts faces instead of corners. Three painted faces occur only at corners, and every cube has exactly 8 corners regardless of size, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

130. A cube painted on all faces is cut into 343 small cubes. How many cubes have at least one face painted?
(a) 218 (b) 216 (c) 343 (d) 125
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to answer 343 (all cubes) or 125 (the unpainted inner cubes). Unpainted cubes = (7 − 2)³ = 125, so painted cubes = 343 − 125 = 218, so the answer is 218.
Source: PYQ-style

131. Two positions of the same dice are shown. In position 1, faces 1, 2, 3 are visible meeting at a corner. In position 2, faces 2, 3, 5 are visible meeting at a corner. Which number is on the face opposite to 2?
(a) 1 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to say 5 is opposite 2, but 5 is adjacent to 2 in position 2. Since 1 and 5 both touch the 2–3 edge pair, 1 is opposite 5; then 3 is opposite 6, leaving 4 opposite 2, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

132. On a standard dice, the sum of numbers on opposite faces is 7. If face 2 is on top and face 3 is facing you, which number is on the right face?
(a) 1 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) Cannot be determined uniquely
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to assume a fixed arrangement and pick 1 or 6. Standard dice exist in two mirror-image forms (right-handed and left-handed), so with only top = 2 and front = 3 fixed, the right face can be 1 or 6 depending on the dice handedness, so the answer is "Cannot be determined uniquely".
Source: PYQ-style

133. A dice is thrown four times and the top faces show 2, 3, 5, 6 in four different positions. From these views it is found that 1 is adjacent to 2, 3, 5 and 6. Which number is opposite to 1?
(a) 6 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to pick one of the adjacent numbers. A face is adjacent to exactly four faces and opposite to only one; since 1 is adjacent to 2, 3, 5, 6, the only remaining number 4 must be opposite 1, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

134. A cube is painted red on two opposite faces, green on two opposite faces, and yellow on the remaining two opposite faces. It is cut into 64 small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two colours on them?
(a) 24 (b) 16 (c) 32 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to include corner cubes, but corners carry three colours. Each of the 12 edges has (4 − 2) = 2 two-colour cubes, giving 12 × 2 = 24, so the answer is 24.
Source: PYQ-style

135. A cube is painted red on two opposite faces, green on two opposite faces, and yellow on the remaining two opposite faces, and cut into 64 small cubes. How many small cubes have all three colours?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 6 (d) 12
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to say 4, counting only some corners. Every corner of the cube is a meeting point of one face of each colour, since each colour pair is opposite, so all 8 corners have three colours, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

136. A cube of side 4 cm is painted black on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. The cubes with no paint are separated. How many such cubes are there, and what fraction of the total do they form?
(a) 27, 27/64 (b) 8, 1/4 (c) 8, 1/8 (d) 4, 1/16
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to compute (n − 1)³ = 27. Unpainted cubes = (4 − 2)³ = 8 out of 64 total, and 8/64 = 1/8, so the answer is 8, 1/8.
Source: PYQ-style

137. Two views of a dice are given. View 1 shows A on top, B in front, C on the right. View 2 shows D on top, B in front, C on the right. Which letter is opposite A?
(a) C (b) D (c) B (d) E
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to think B or C is opposite A, but they are adjacent to A in view 1. Since B and C stay fixed while the top changes from A to D, A and D must be on opposite faces, so the answer is D.
Source: PYQ-style

138. A dice has numbers 1 to 6. In one view, 4 is on top and 1 is in front. In another view, 4 is on top and 2 is in front. Which number is opposite to 4?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to pick 1 or 2, but both are adjacent to 4. With 4 on top in both views and front faces 1 and 2, the four side faces around 4 are 1, 2 and the two hidden ones; the only number that can be opposite 4 must not be adjacent — among the options, 3 fits since 1, 2, 5, 6 can all be side faces, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

139. A cube is cut into 1000 small cubes after painting all faces orange. How many cubes have exactly one or two faces painted?
(a) 480 (b) 384 (c) 488 (d) 512
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to include the 8 corner cubes, which have three painted faces, giving the wrong total 488. For n = 10: one-face cubes = 6 × (10 − 2)² = 6 × 64 = 384, and two-face cubes = 12 × (10 − 2) = 96, so the total is 384 + 96 = 480, so the answer is 480.
Source: PYQ-style

140. A standard dice (opposite faces sum to 7) is placed on a table with 1 on top. It is rolled one step east, then one step north. What number is now on top?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to assume a fixed orientation of side faces. Knowing only that 1 is on top (so 6 is at the bottom) does not fix which of 2, 3, 4, 5 faces east or north, so the final top face depends on the initial arrangement, so the answer is "Cannot be determined".
Source: PYQ-style

141. A cube is painted on all faces and cut into 27 equal cubes. The corner cubes are removed. How many of the remaining cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 6 (d) 18
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to subtract corners from the two-face count, but corners were never part of it. For n = 3, two-face cubes = 12 × (3 − 2) = 12, and removing corners does not change this, so the answer is 12.
Source: PYQ-style

142. Three views of the same dice show the following top faces with front faces: (top 1, front 2), (top 2, front 3), (top 3, front 1). Which number is opposite 2?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 6 (d) 5
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to say 1 or 3 is opposite 2, but the views show 2 adjacent to 1 and 3. From the cyclic arrangement 1–2–3 around a corner region, 2 is adjacent to 1, 3, and by rotation also to 6; the only number left that is never adjacent to 2 is 5, so the answer is 5.
Source: PYQ-style

143. A large cube of side 5 cm is painted red and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many cubes have paint on at least two faces?
(a) 44 (b) 36 (c) 52 (d) 48
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to count only edge cubes (36) and forget the 8 corners. At-least-two-faces = two-face cubes + corner cubes = 12 × 3 + 8 = 36 + 8 = 44, so the answer is 44.
Source: PYQ-style

144. A dice is made from a net where the six squares are labelled P, Q, R, S, T, U. In the net, P is opposite R, Q is opposite S. Which pair is opposite?
(a) R and S (b) P and Q (c) T and U (d) P and S
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to mix the given pairs. A cube has exactly three opposite pairs; since P–R and Q–S are two of them, the remaining pair must be T–U, so the answer is T and U.
Source: PYQ-style

145. A cube painted on all faces is cut into 512 small cubes. How many cubes have exactly two painted faces?
(a) 64 (b) 84 (c) 96 (d) 72
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to use (n − 1) = 7 per edge, giving 84. Since 512 = 8³, two-face cubes = 12 × (8 − 2) = 12 × 6 = 72, so the answer is 72.
Source: PYQ-style

146. On a dice, the face with 6 dots is opposite the face with 1 dot, 5 is opposite 2, and 4 is opposite 3. The dice is rolled so that the top face moves to the north side. If 6 was on top initially, which number is now on the bottom?
(a) 1 (b) Cannot be determined (c) 3 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to say 1, assuming the bottom stays fixed. When the top face moves to the north side, the old south face comes to the bottom; since the initial north and south faces are not specified (they could be any of 2, 3, 4, 5), the bottom number cannot be fixed, so the answer is "Cannot be determined".
Source: PYQ-style

147. A cube is painted red on three faces that meet at one corner, and blue on the other three faces. It is cut into 27 small cubes. How many small cubes have both red and blue paint?
(a) 6 (b) 9 (c) 12 (d) 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to count all 12 edges. Red and blue meet only along the 3 edges separating a red face from a blue face, contributing 1 middle cube each (3 cubes), and 3 corners touch faces of both colours; total = 3 + 3 = 6, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

148. Four views of a dice show that face X is adjacent to faces A, B, C and D. If the sixth face is E, which face is opposite X?
(a) A (b) C (c) E (d) B
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to pick one of the adjacent faces. A face has exactly four neighbours and one opposite; since X touches A, B, C, D, the only remaining face E must be opposite X, so the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

149. A cube of side 3 cm, painted on all faces, is cut into 1 cm cubes. All cubes with at least one painted face are removed. The remaining cubes are glued together and painted again on all outer faces. How many cubes now have paint?
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to say 0, thinking the inner cube stays hidden. Only 1 unpainted cube exists ((3 − 2)³ = 1); when painted on all sides, that single cube gets paint on all its faces, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

150. A standard dice (opposite faces sum to 7) shows 2 on top and 4 facing east. It is rolled twice towards the east. What number is on top now?
(a) 3 (b) 6 (c) 5 (d) 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to stop after one roll, which would put 3 on top, since the west face is opposite 4, i.e., 3. Rolling east twice: after the first roll the west face (3) comes on top; after the second roll the bottom face (5, opposite the top 2) comes on top, so the answer is 5.
Source: PYQ-style
