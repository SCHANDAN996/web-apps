76. Statement: Many accidents are occurring at the unmanned railway crossing near the town.
Courses of Action:
I. The railway authorities should deploy a gateman at the crossing immediately.
II. The railway authorities should construct an overbridge or underpass at the crossing.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Neither I nor II follows (d) Both I and II follow
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting choice is to pick only the overbridge as a "permanent" solution, but permanent works take time while accidents continue now. Deploying a gateman addresses the immediate danger, and an overbridge or underpass removes the root cause permanently, so both actions logically follow.
