1. Statement: Many people in the city are suffering from respiratory diseases due to increasing air pollution.
Courses of Action:
I. The government should ban all vehicles in the city immediately.
II. The government should promote public transport and stricter emission norms.
(a) Only I follows (b) Both I and II follow (c) Only II follows (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: Banning all vehicles immediately is an extreme and impractical step that would paralyse the city, so Course I does not follow. Promoting public transport and enforcing emission norms is a practical, logical step, so the answer is Only II follows.
