101. A clock shows 7:25. What time will its image show in a plane mirror held upright beside it?
(a) 5:25 (b) 4:35 (c) 7:25 (d) 5:35
Answer: (b)
Solution: For a mirror image, subtract the time from 11:60: 11:60 − 7:25 = 4:35. The trap is 7:25; so the answer is 4:35.
Source: PYQ-style

102. In the word "TIMBER", how many letters look exactly the same in its water image (mirror placed below the word)?
(a) 3 (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: A capital letter looks the same in this image when its top half matches the bottom half. In "TIMBER" those letters are I, B, E; letters like M, R, T change, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Which of the following words looks exactly the same in a mirror placed on its right (the image reverses the order of the letters and flips each letter)?
(a) MAT (b) DAD (c) PEEP (d) TUT
Answer: (d)
Solution: The word must read the same backwards and use only letters that look the same in a mirror (A H I M O T U V W X Y). Words such as DAD read the same backwards but D flips; so the answer is TUT.
