# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Mirror & Water Images

## The Story of Ravi and the Lake

Imagine a boy named Ravi standing in front of a big mirror in his bedroom, holding a clock in his **right hand**. He looks up — and the "mirror Ravi" is holding the clock in his **left hand**! Ravi didn't switch hands. So what happened?

The mirror didn't flip Ravi upside down. It didn't swap his head and feet. It only swapped **left and right** — the side facing the mirror comes out on the opposite side. That's the whole secret of a **mirror image**: *left becomes right, right becomes left, but top stays top and bottom stays bottom.*

Now imagine Ravi goes to a calm lake. He leans over and looks at his reflection in the water. This time his **head is at the bottom** and his **feet are at the top**! But the clock is still in the same left-right position. That's a **water image**: *top becomes bottom, bottom becomes top, but left stays left and right stays right.*

So remember it like this:

- **Mirror = Left-Right flip** (like a vertical mirror beside you)
- **Water = Top-Bottom flip** (like a horizontal water surface below you)

## The Hardest Part Made Easy: Letters and Numbers

The tricky part in exams is not people — it's **letters, numbers, and clocks**.

Take the letter **b**. In a mirror it looks like **d** (left-right flip). In water it looks like **p** (top-bottom flip). One letter, two different answers — and the examiner knows students mix these up!

**The Unforgettable Trick — "M-W Rule":**

> **M**irror flips **M**iddle-to-side (left ↔ right).
> **W**ater flips **W**eight (top ↔ bottom — like dropping your head down).

Or even simpler: **"Mirror = sideways, Water = upside-down."**

**Clock trick:** For a mirror image of a clock, subtract the time from **11:60** (or from **12:00** if the minutes are 00). For a water image, subtract from **17:90** — but if the minutes are 00, subtract from **18:00** instead. Example: mirror of 3:20 → 11:60 − 3:20 = **8:40**.

**Quick letter check:** Letters like **A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y** look the same in a mirror (vertically symmetric). Letters like **B, C, D, E, H, I, O, X** look the same in water (horizontally symmetric). If an option shows a symmetric letter changed, eliminate it instantly!
