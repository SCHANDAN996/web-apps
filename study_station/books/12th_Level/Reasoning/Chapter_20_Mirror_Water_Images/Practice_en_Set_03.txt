Questions 51–75: Medium

51. A clock shows 8:40. What time will its image show in a plane mirror held upright beside it?
(a) 4:40 (b) 3:20 (c) 4:20 (d) 3:50
Answer: (b)
Solution: For a mirror image, subtract the time from 11:60: 11:60 − 8:40 = 3:20. The trap is 4:40; so the answer is 3:20.
Source: PYQ-style

52. In the word "BOOKED", how many letters look exactly the same in its mirror image (mirror placed on the right)?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 5 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: A capital letter looks the same in this image when its left half matches the right half. In "BOOKED" those letters are O, O; letters like B, D, E, K change, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Which of the following words looks exactly the same in a mirror placed on its right (the image reverses the order of the letters and flips each letter)?
(a) BOB (b) SOS (c) OATH (d) AHA
Answer: (d)
Solution: The word must read the same backwards and use only letters that look the same in a mirror (A H I M O T U V W X Y). Words such as DAD read the same backwards but D flips; so the answer is AHA.
