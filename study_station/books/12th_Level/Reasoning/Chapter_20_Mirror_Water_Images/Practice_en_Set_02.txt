Questions 26–50: Medium

26. A clock shows 10:15. What time will its image show in a plane mirror held upright beside it?
(a) 1:15 (b) 1:45 (c) 2:15 (d) 2:45
Answer: (b)
Solution: For a mirror image, subtract the time from 11:60: 11:60 − 10:15 = 1:45. The trap is 1:15; so the answer is 1:45.
Source: PYQ-style

27. In the word "TOMATO", how many letters look exactly the same in its water image (mirror placed below the word)?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: A capital letter looks the same in this image when its top half matches the bottom half. In "TOMATO" those letters are O, O; letters like A, M, T change, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

28. Which of the following words looks exactly the same in a mirror placed on its right (the image reverses the order of the letters and flips each letter)?

Answer: (d)
Solution: The word must read the same backwards and use only letters that look the same in a mirror (A H I M O T U V W X Y). Words such as DAD read the same backwards but D flips; so the answer is YAY.
