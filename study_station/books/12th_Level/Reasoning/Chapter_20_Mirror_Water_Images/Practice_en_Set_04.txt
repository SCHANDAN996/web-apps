Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The image of a clock in a plane mirror shows 8:10. What is the actual time on the clock?
(a) 4:50 (b) 3:20 (c) 3:50 (d) 8:10
Answer: (c)
Solution: The actual time and its mirror time always add up to 11:60, so 11:60 − 8:10 = 3:50. The trap is 8:10; so the answer is 3:50.
Source: PYQ-style

77. In the word "MAMMOTH", how many letters look exactly the same in its mirror image (mirror placed on the right)?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 7
Answer: (d)
Solution: A capital letter looks the same in this image when its left half matches the right half. In "MAMMOTH" those letters are M, A, M, M, O, T, H; letters like - change, so the answer is 7.
Source: PYQ-style
