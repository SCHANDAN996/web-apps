Questions 1–25: Easy

1. The image of a clock in a plane mirror shows 8:15. What is the actual time on the clock?
(a) 8:15 (b) 4:15 (c) 4:45 (d) 3:45
Answer: (d)
Solution: The actual time and its mirror time always add up to 11:60, so 11:60 − 8:15 = 3:45. The trap is 8:15; so the answer is 3:45.
Source: PYQ-style

2. In the word "BEHIND", how many letters look exactly the same in its water image (mirror placed below the word)?
(a) 6 (b) 5 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: A capital letter looks the same in this image when its top half matches the bottom half. In "BEHIND" those letters are B, E, H, I, D; letters like N change, so the answer is 5.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Which of the following words looks exactly the same in a mirror placed on its right (the image reverses the order of the letters and flips each letter)?
(a) YAY (b) MATH (c) NOON (d) BOB
Answer: (a)
Solution: The word must read the same backwards and use only letters that look the same in a mirror (A H I M O T U V W X Y). Words such as DAD read the same backwards but D flips; so the answer is YAY.
