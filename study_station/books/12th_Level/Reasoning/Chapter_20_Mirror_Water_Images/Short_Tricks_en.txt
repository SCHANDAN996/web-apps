# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Mirror & Water Images

---

**Box 1 — The Lateral Flip Rule**
> 🪞 **"Mirror = Left-Right Swap, Water = Top-Bottom Drop"**
> Mirror image flips LEFT ↔ RIGHT only. Water image flips TOP ↔ BOTTOM only. Nothing else changes. If an option flips both — it's a trap!

---

**Box 2 — Which Side Is the Mirror?**
> 📍 **"Mirror sits on the RIGHT, unless told otherwise"**
> In exams, if no mirror position is shown, assume the mirror is on the **right side** of the figure. The image forms on the other side, laterally inverted.

---

**Box 3 — The Clock Trick**
> 🕐 **"11:60 minus the time — mirror time is clean!"**
> Mirror image of a clock time = **11:60 − given time** (or 12:00 − time, adjusting minutes).
> Example: Mirror of 3:20 → 11:60 − 3:20 = **8:40**.
> Memory hook: *"Eleven-sixty is the mirror's city!"*

---

**Box 4 — Water Image of Clock**
> 💧 **"For water, flip the hands down — 6:30 minus, wear the crown!"**
> Water image of clock time ≈ **6:30 − given time** (when time ≤ 6:30), else use 18:30 − time.
> Example: Water image of 2:20 → 6:30 − 2:20 = **4:10**.

---

**Box 5 — Letter Memory: The Symmetric Seven**
> 🔤 **"A H I M O T U V W X Y — these letters never lie!"**
> These capital letters look the SAME in a mirror (vertical symmetry). If a question asks "which letter is unchanged in mirror?" — hunt from this list.

---

**Box 6 — Water-Symmetric Letters**
> 💦 **"B C D E H I K O X — water keeps them in the mix!"**
> These capitals have horizontal symmetry, so their **water image** looks the same. (Note: K and O depending on font — in standard exam fonts, B C D E H I O X are safe.)

---

**Box 7 — The Number Trap**
> 🔢 **"2 and 5 are mirror twins — swap them and the answer wins!"**
> In digital-style fonts, 2 ↔ 5 look like mirror images of each other. Examiners love hiding this in number-based mirror questions. Check digits one by one, right to left.

---

**Box 8 — Reverse Reading Order**
> 🔄 **"Read the word BACKWARDS first, then flip each letter"**
> For mirror image of a word like "TAXI": reverse the order → "IXAT", then flip each letter's shape. Two steps, never skip the reversal — that is exactly where most students slip!

---

**Box 9 — The Paper-Fold Visualizer**
> 📄 **"Fold the page on the mirror line — what overlaps is the design!"**
> Mentally fold the question paper along the mirror/water line. Whatever the figure would stamp onto the other side IS the answer. Works for every single question type.

---

**Box 10 — Arrow & Direction Trick**
> ➡️ **"East stays East in water, but mirror makes it West, son!"**
> An arrow pointing right (→): mirror image points left (←); water image still points right but flips up/down components. Directions parallel to the mirror line survive; perpendicular ones reverse.

---

**Box 11 — The "Closest Feature" Anchor**
> ⚓ **"What's nearest the mirror stays nearest — anchor it, don't fear it!"**
> Pick the feature of the figure closest to the mirror line. In the image, that same feature must be closest to the line. Eliminate any option where the near feature jumped far away — instant 2-option knockout.

---

**Box 12 — Dot & Circle Positioning**
> 🎯 **"Dot near top in water? Now it's near the bottom, daughter!"**
> For water images, track dots/shading: top becomes bottom, bottom becomes top, left and right stay put. For mirror: left-right swap, top-bottom stay put. Track ONE dot and eliminate.

---

**Box 13 — The Elimination Sprint**
> 🏃 **"Don't draw the answer — kill the wrong ones!"**
> Never try to construct the full image. Check options against 2-3 anchor points (nearest feature, one letter's flip, one dot's position). Three checks usually eliminate three options. Faster and safer.

---

**Box 14 — Mixed Flip = Instant Reject**
> 🚫 **"If it flipped BOTH ways, it's neither mirror nor water — it's garbage!"**
> A figure rotated 180° (both flips) is the examiner's favourite trap. Mirror = one axis only. Water = one axis only. Both-axis flip = wrong, always.

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Leave the Question

**Tip 1 — The 15-Second Rule:**
> If after 15 seconds you cannot identify even ONE anchor point (nearest feature, a flippable letter, a dot's position) to start eliminating — **mark and skip**. Mirror/water questions are meant to be 20–30 second scorers; a confusing figure will eat 2 minutes with no guarantee.

**Tip 2 — Skip Crowded Figures First:**
> If the figure has 6+ elements (multiple dots, arrows, letters AND shading), the chance of a tracking error is high. Attempt it only in the second pass, after securing the easy single-element questions.

**Tip 3 — Skip When Two Options Look Identical:**
> If two options differ only in one tiny detail (e.g., a dot slightly left vs. right) and you can't verify it quickly, don't gamble blindly — mark it, finish the paper, and return with fresh eyes. A rushed guess here is a negative-marking magnet.
