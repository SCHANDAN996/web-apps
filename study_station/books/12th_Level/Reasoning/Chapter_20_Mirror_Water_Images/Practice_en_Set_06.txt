Questions 126–150: Hard

126. A clock shows 2:25. What time will its image show in a plane mirror held upright beside it?
(a) 10:35 (b) 9:35 (c) 2:25 (d) 9:05
Answer: (b)
Solution: For a mirror image, subtract the time from 11:60: 11:60 − 2:25 = 9:35. The trap is 2:25; so the answer is 9:35.
Source: PYQ-style

127. In the word "EXCHANGE", how many letters look exactly the same in its mirror image (mirror placed on the right)?
(a) 6 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: A capital letter looks the same in this image when its left half matches the right half. In "EXCHANGE" those letters are X, H, A; letters like C, E, G, N change, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style
