101. A clock shows 4:20. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand?
(a) 20° (b) 0° (c) 30° (d) 10°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is 0°, assuming the hour hand stays exactly at 4. But the hour hand moves 0.5° per minute, so at 4:20 it has moved 10° past 4; the minute hand is at 120° and the hour hand at 130°, so the answer is 10°.
Source: PYQ-style

102. At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock will the hands of a clock be together?
(a) 7:38²⁄₁₁ (b) 7:35 (c) 7:40 (d) 7:36⁴⁄₁₁
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hands coincide at (60/11) × 7 = 420/11 = 38²⁄₁₁ minutes past 7. The trap is 7:35, which wrongly assumes the hour hand is fixed at 7, so the answer is 7:38²⁄₁₁.
Source: PYQ-style

103. How many times in a day are the hands of a clock at right angles?
(a) 48 (b) 24 (c) 22 (d) 44
Answer: (d)
Solution: In 12 hours the hands are at right angles 22 times, so in 24 hours it is 44 times. The trap is 48, which assumes 2 per hour without noting the lost occurrences near 3 and 9 o'clock, so the answer is 44.
Source: PYQ-style

104. The minute hand of a clock overtakes the hour hand at intervals of 65 minutes of correct time. How much does the clock gain or lose per day?
(a) Gains 10¹⁰⁄₁₄₃ min (b) Loses 10¹⁰⁄₁₄₃ min (c) Loses 5⁵⁄₁₄₃ min (d) Gains 5⁵⁄₁₄₃ min
Answer: (a)
Solution: In a correct clock the hands coincide every 65⁵⁄₁₁ minutes; here they coincide every 65 minutes, which is faster, so the clock gains. Gain per day = (5⁄₁₁ × 1440)/65 = 10¹⁰⁄₁₄₃ minutes, so the answer is gains 10¹⁰⁄₁₄₃ min.
Source: PYQ-style

105. What was the day of the week on 15 August 1947?
(a) Thursday (b) Wednesday (c) Saturday (d) Friday
Answer: (d)
Solution: Odd days: 1600 years = 0, 300 years = 1, 46 years = 11 leap + 35 ordinary = 57 ≡ 1, and 1 Jan to 15 Aug 1947 = 227 days ≡ 3. Total = 5 odd days, so the answer is Friday.

106. If 1 January 2001 was a Monday, what day was 1 January 2005?
(a) Saturday (b) Sunday (c) Thursday (d) Friday
Answer: (a)
Solution: From 2001 to 2005 there are 4 years including one leap year (2004), giving 4 + 1 = 5 odd days; Monday + 5 = Saturday. The trap is Friday, which forgets the extra odd day from the leap year, so the answer is Saturday.
Source: PYQ-style

107. A clock is set right at 6 a.m. It gains 2 minutes every hour. What will it show when the correct time is 9 p.m. the same day?
(a) 9:26 p.m. (b) 9:28 p.m. (c) 9:30 p.m. (d) 9:32 p.m.
Answer: (c)
Solution: From 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. is 15 hours; gain = 15 × 2 = 30 minutes. The faulty clock runs ahead by exactly this gain, so the answer is 9:30 p.m.

109. How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in 12 hours?
(a) 12 (b) 10 (c) 24 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: The hands coincide every 65⁵⁄₁₁ minutes, giving 11 coincidences in 12 hours, not 12, because between 11 and 1 they meet only once (at 12). So the answer is 11.

110. If today is Wednesday, what day will it be after 100 days?
(a) Tuesday (b) Saturday (c) Friday (d) Thursday
Answer: (c)
Solution: 100 ÷ 7 = 14 weeks and 2 odd days; Wednesday + 2 = Friday. The trap is Thursday, which counts only 1 odd day, so the answer is Friday.
Source: PYQ-style

111. At what time between 3 and 4 o'clock are the hands of a clock exactly opposite each other?
(a) 3:45 (b) 3:50 (c) 3:48 (d) 3:49¹⁄₁₁
Answer: (d)
Solution: For opposite hands the minute hand must gain 45 minute-spaces on the hour hand, taking 45 × 12/11 = 540/11 = 49¹⁄₁₁ minutes. The trap is 3:45, which ignores the hour hand's movement, so the answer is 3:49¹⁄₁₁.
Source: PYQ-style

112. Which of the following years is a leap year?
(a) 2100 (b) 2000 (c) 1900 (d) 1800
Answer: (b)
Solution: Century years are leap years only if divisible by 400; 2000 ÷ 400 = 5, while 1900, 2100 and 1800 are divisible by 100 but not by 400. The trap is 1900, which is divisible by 4 but still not a leap year, so the answer is 2000.
Source: PYQ-style

113. The calendar of the year 2014 will be used again in which year?
(a) 2020 (b) 2019 (c) 2025 (d) 2023
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding odd days from 2014 onward: 1, 1, 2, 1, 1, 1, 2, 1, 1, 1, 2 gives a cumulative total of 14, a multiple of 7, after 11 years. So the calendar repeats in 2025, and the answer is 2025.
Source: PYQ-style

114. A watch which gains uniformly was 2 minutes slow at noon on Monday and 4 minutes 48 seconds fast at 2 p.m. on the following Monday. When was it correct?
(a) 2 p.m. Wednesday (b) 3 p.m. Thursday (c) 1 p.m. Friday (d) 2 p.m. Tuesday
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total gain in 170 hours = 2 min + 4 min 48 s = 408 seconds; covering the initial 120-second deficit takes 120/408 × 170 = 50 hours. Monday noon + 50 hours = Wednesday 2 p.m., so the answer is 2 p.m. Wednesday.
Source: PYQ-style

117. If 5 March of a year is Tuesday, what day is 5 July of the same year (non-leap year)?
(a) Thursday (b) Wednesday (c) Saturday (d) Friday
Answer: (d)
Solution: Days from 5 March to 5 July: 26 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 5 = 122 days, and 122 ÷ 7 leaves 3 odd days. Tuesday + 3 = Friday, so the answer is Friday.

118. How many times in a day do the hands of a clock form a straight line (coincide or opposite)?
(a) 44 (b) 24 (c) 22 (d) 48
Answer: (a)
Solution: In 12 hours the hands coincide 11 times and are opposite 11 times, totalling 22 straight-line positions; in 24 hours this is 44. The trap is 48, which assumes 2 per hour without the losses near 12 and 6 o'clock, so the answer is 44.

119. A clock strikes once at 1 o'clock, twice at 2 o'clock and so on. How many times does it strike in 24 hours?
(a) 156 (b) 136 (c) 144 (d) 78
Answer: (a)
Solution: In 12 hours the strikes are 1 + 2 + … + 12 = 78; in 24 hours this doubles to 156. The trap is 78, which counts only 12 hours, so the answer is 156.

120. If the day before yesterday was Saturday, what day will it be the day after tomorrow?
(a) Tuesday (b) Wednesday (c) Friday (d) Thursday
Answer: (b)
Solution: Day before yesterday = Saturday, so today = Monday, and the day after tomorrow = Monday + 2 = Wednesday. The trap is Thursday, which wrongly adds 3 days to today instead of 2, so the answer is Wednesday.
Source: PYQ-style

121. At what time between 5 and 6 o'clock will the angle between the hands be exactly 60° for the second time?
(a) 5:38²⁄₁₁ (b) 5:16⁴⁄₁₁ (c) 5:30 (d) 5:27³⁄₁₁
Answer: (a)
Solution: Solving |150 − 5.5M| = 60 gives M = 180/11 = 16⁴⁄₁₁ minutes (first time) and M = 420/11 = 38²⁄₁₁ minutes (second time). The trap is 5:16⁴⁄₁₁, which is the first occurrence, not the second, so the answer is 5:38²⁄₁₁.

122. A man was born on 29 February 1972. How many birthdays (actual date) had he celebrated by 29 February 2000?
(a) 28 (b) 7 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: Leap years after 1972 up to 2000: 1976, 1980, 1984, 1988, 1992, 1996, 2000 = 7 birthdays. The trap is 8, which counts 1972 itself, the birth year, as a celebrated birthday, so the answer is 7.
Source: PYQ-style

123. The hands of a clock are together at 12. After how many minutes will they be together again, and what is the exact time?
(a) 1:05 (b) 1:05⁵⁄₁₁ (c) 1:06 (d) 1:04⁶⁄₁₁
Answer: (b)
Solution: The minute hand gains 55 minute-spaces over the hour hand in 60 minutes, so to gain 60 minute-spaces it takes 60 × 60/55 = 65⁵⁄₁₁ minutes. The trap is 1:05, which ignores that the hour hand also moves, so the answer is 1:05⁵⁄₁₁.
Source: PYQ-style

124. If 1 January 2024 was a Monday, on which dates of February 2024 will Sunday fall?
(a) 3, 10, 17, 24 (b) 5, 12, 19, 26 (c) 4, 11, 18, 25 (d) 2, 9, 16, 23
Answer: (c)
Solution: January has 31 days, so 1 February = Monday + 3 = Thursday, making the first Sunday 4 February, then 11, 18 and 25. The trap is 3, 10, 17, 24, which comes from taking 1 February as Friday, so the answer is 4, 11, 18, 25.
Source: PYQ-style

125. A clock loses 4 minutes every hour. It was set right at 10 a.m. What is the correct time when this clock shows 4 p.m. the same day?
(a) 4:24 p.m. (b) 4:20 p.m. (c) 4:25⁵⁄₇ p.m. (d) 4:30 p.m.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The faulty clock covers only 56 minutes for every 60 minutes of correct time, so when it shows 6 hours elapsed, the correct elapsed time is 6 × 60/56 = 45/7 = 6⁳⁄₇ hours = 6 hours 25⁵⁄₇ minutes. The trap is 4:24 p.m., which simply adds 6 × 4 = 24 minutes instead of using the ratio, so the answer is 4:25⁵⁄₇ p.m.
Source: PYQ-style
