1. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3:15?
(a) 7.5° (b) 15° (c) 0° (d) 22.5°
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students assume the hands coincide at 3:15 and pick 0°, but the hour hand moves 0.5° per minute, so in 15 minutes it moves 7.5° past 3. The minute hand is at 90° and the hour hand at 97.5°, so the answer is 7.5°.
Source: PYQ-style

2. At what time between 4 o'clock and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 4:20 (b) 4:21 9/11 min (c) 4:22 (d) 4:23 1/11 min
Answer: (b)
Solution: The hands coincide at (60/11) × H minutes past H o'clock. For H = 4, time = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes past 4, so the answer is 4:21 9/11 min.
Source: PYQ-style

3. How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in 12 hours?
(a) 11 (b) 10 (c) 22 (d) 12
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer is 12, but between 11 and 1 the hands coincide only once (at 12 o'clock), so one coincidence is lost. In 12 hours the hands coincide 11 times, so the answer is 11.
Source: PYQ-style

4. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 6:30?
(a) 0° (b) 30° (c) 15° (d) 45°
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many pick 0° thinking the hands overlap, but at 6:30 the hour hand has moved halfway between 6 and 7, i.e. 15° past 6, while the minute hand points at 6. The gap is 15°, so the answer is 15°.
Source: PYQ-style

5. The minute hand of a clock overtakes the hour hand at intervals of how many minutes?
(a) 64 minutes (b) 65 5/11 minutes (c) 60 minutes (d) 66 minutes
Answer: (b)
Solution: The minute hand gains 55 minute-spaces over the hour hand in 60 minutes, so to gain a full 60 spaces it needs 60 × 60/55 = 720/11 = 65 5/11 minutes. Choosing 60 minutes ignores the hour hand's own movement, so the answer is 65 5/11 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

6. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 8:20?
(a) 120° (b) 150° (c) 140° (d) 130°
Answer: (d)
Solution: Angle = |30H − 5.5M| = |240 − 110| = 130°. Since 130° is less than 180°, no correction is needed, so the answer is 130°.
Source: PYQ-style

7. At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock are the hands of a clock at right angles for the first time?
(a) 2:32 8/11 min (b) 2:25 min (c) 2:27 3/11 min (d) 2:30 min
Answer: (c)
Solution: For right angles, time = (5H ± 15) × 12/11 minutes past H. For H = 2, the first right angle is (5×2 + 15) × 12/11 = 25 × 12/11 = 300/11 = 27 3/11 minutes, so the answer is 2:27 3/11 min.
Source: PYQ-style

8. How many times in a day are the hands of a clock in a straight line (coinciding or opposite)?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 44 (d) 48
Answer: (c)
Solution: The hands coincide 11 times and are opposite 11 times in 12 hours, i.e. 22 straight-line positions in 12 hours. In 24 hours this doubles to 44, so the answer is 44.
Source: PYQ-style

9. A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. If it is set right at 6 a.m., what time will it show at 6 p.m. the same day?
(a) 7:00 p.m. (b) 6:55 p.m. (c) 7:05 p.m. (d) 6:05 p.m.
Answer: (a)
Solution: In 12 hours the clock gains 12 × 5 = 60 minutes = 1 hour. So when the true time is 6 p.m., the faulty clock shows 7:00 p.m., so the answer is 7:00 p.m.
Source: PYQ-style

10. If today is Monday, what day will it be after 61 days?
(a) Friday (b) Tuesday (c) Sunday (d) Saturday
Answer: (d)
Solution: 61 ÷ 7 gives remainder 5 (5 odd days). Monday + 5 days = Saturday, so the answer is Saturday.
Source: PYQ-style

11. How many odd days are there in a leap year?
(a) 3 (b) 1 (c) 0 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: A leap year has 366 days = 52 weeks + 2 days, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

12. How many odd days are there in 100 years?
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: 100 years contain 76 ordinary years and 24 leap years, giving 76 + 48 = 124 odd days; 124 mod 7 = 5. The trap is counting 25 leap years, but the 100th year is not a leap year, so the answer is 5.
Source: PYQ-style

13. Which of the following years is a leap year?
(a) 1900 (b) 2000 (c) 2100 (d) 1800
Answer: (b)
Solution: Century years are leap years only if divisible by 400. 1900, 1800 and 2100 are not divisible by 400, but 2000 is, so the answer is 2000.
Source: PYQ-style

14. If 1st January 2001 was a Monday, what day was 1st January 2002?
(a) Monday (b) Tuesday (c) Wednesday (d) Sunday
Answer: (b)
Solution: 2001 is an ordinary year with 1 odd day, so the next year's same date shifts by one day: Monday + 1 = Tuesday, so the answer is Tuesday.
Source: PYQ-style

15. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 10:10?
(a) 115° (b) 120° (c) 125° (d) 110°
Answer: (a)
Solution: Angle = |30 × 10 − 5.5 × 10| = |300 − 55| = 245°. Since this exceeds 180°, subtract from 360°: 360 − 245 = 115°, so the answer is 115°.
Source: PYQ-style

16. The calendar of which year is the same as that of 2007?
(a) 2016 (b) 2017 (c) 2018 (d) 2012
Answer: (c)
Solution: For an ordinary year, the calendar repeats when the accumulated odd days total a multiple of 7. From 2007 to 2018 the accumulated odd days total 14, so the answer is 2018.
Source: PYQ-style

17. How many times in 12 hours are the hands of a clock at right angles?
(a) 24 (b) 44 (c) 11 (d) 22
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is 24 (2 per hour), but between 2–3 and 8–9 o'clock one right angle each is lost, giving 22 in 12 hours, so the answer is 22.
Source: PYQ-style

18. If the day before yesterday was Wednesday, what day will it be the day after tomorrow?
(a) Friday (b) Sunday (c) Saturday (d) Monday
Answer: (b)
Solution: Day before yesterday = Wednesday, so yesterday = Thursday and today = Friday. Then tomorrow = Saturday and the day after tomorrow = Sunday, so the answer is Sunday.
Source: PYQ-style

19. A watch loses 4 minutes every hour. It was set right at 9 a.m. What is the correct time when the watch shows 5 p.m.?
(a) 5:34 2/7 p.m. (b) 5:30 p.m. (c) 5:32 p.m. (d) 5:28 p.m.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The watch covers 56 minutes for every true 60 minutes. When it shows 8 hours elapsed, true elapsed time = 8 × 60/56 = 60/7 = 8 hours 34 2/7 minutes, so true time = 9 a.m. + 8 h 34 2/7 min = 5:34 2/7 p.m., so the answer is 5:34 2/7 p.m.
Source: PYQ-style

20. What was the day of the week on 15th August 1947?
(a) Thursday (b) Saturday (c) Friday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: Odd days up to the end of 1946: 1600 years give 0, next 300 years give 1, and 46 years (35 ordinary + 11 leap) give 57 ≡ 1, totalling 2. Days from 1 Jan to 15 Aug 1947 = 227 ≡ 3 odd days, giving 2 + 3 = 5, which corresponds to Friday, so the answer is Friday.
Source: PYQ-style

21. At what time between 5 and 6 o'clock will the hands of a clock be exactly opposite each other?
(a) 5:54 6/11 min (b) 5:55 min (c) 5:52 8/11 min (d) 6:00
Answer: (d)
Solution: At 5:00 the gap is only 150°, and the minute hand must first close it to 0° and then open it to 180° on the other side, a total gain of 330°. At 5.5° per minute this takes exactly 60 minutes, so the hands are opposite at 6:00; the formula value 54 6/11 minutes is the trap here, so the answer is 6:00.
Source: PYQ-style

22. On what dates of March 2005 did Friday fall, if 1st March 2005 was a Tuesday?
(a) 4, 11, 18, 25 (b) 3, 10, 17, 24, 31 (c) 5, 12, 19, 26 (d) 4, 11, 18, 25, 31
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1st March = Tuesday, so the first Friday is 4th March. Adding 7 repeatedly gives 4, 11, 18, 25; the next Friday would be 1st April, so the answer is 4, 11, 18, 25.
Source: PYQ-style

23. A clock strikes once at 1 o'clock, twice at 2 o'clock, and so on. How many times does it strike in 24 hours?
(a) 78 (b) 144 (c) 156 (d) 136
Answer: (c)
Solution: In 12 hours it strikes 1 + 2 + … + 12 = 78 times. The trap is stopping at 78, but the question asks for 24 hours, so 78 × 2 = 156, so the answer is 156.
Source: PYQ-style

24. If 1st January of a non-leap year falls on a Sunday, on what day will Christmas (25th December) fall in the same year?
(a) Monday (b) Friday (c) Saturday (d) Sunday
Answer: (a)
Solution: From 1 January to 25 December there are 358 days, and 358 mod 7 = 1 odd day. Sunday + 1 = Monday, which matches real calendars such as 2023, so the answer is Monday.
Source: PYQ-style

25. The angle between the hands of a clock is 100° and the time is between 7 and 8 o'clock. Which of these is a possible time?
(a) 7:21 9/11 min (b) 7:25 5/11 min (c) 7:30 (d) 7:20
Answer: (d)
Solution: Angle = |30 × 7 − 5.5M| = 100, so |210 − 5.5M| = 100, giving 5.5M = 110 or 310, i.e. M = 20 or M = 56 4/11. Only M = 20 appears among the options, so the answer is 7:20.
Source: PYQ-style
