26. At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be together?
(a) 21 1/11 minutes past 4 (b) 21 9/11 minutes past 4 (c) 20 minutes past 4 (d) 22 minutes past 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer 20 minutes ignores that the hour hand also moves forward. At 4 o'clock the minute hand is 20 minute-spaces behind; it gains 55 spaces in 60 minutes, so it gains 20 spaces in (20 × 60)/55 = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes. So the answer is 21 9/11 minutes past 4.
Source: PYQ-style

27. The angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3:40 is:
(a) 120° (b) 150° (c) 140° (d) 130°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing only 30 × 3 − 6 × 40 = −150° and taking 150°, which forgets the hour hand's extra movement of 0.5° per minute. Angle = |30 × 3 − (11/2) × 40| = |90 − 220| = 130°. So the answer is 130°.
Source: PYQ-style

28. How many times in a day are the hands of a clock at right angles?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 48 (d) 44
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is doubling 24, the naive "twice per hour" count, which gives 48; around 2–3 and 8–9 o'clock one right angle is lost in each 12-hour cycle. In 12 hours the hands are at right angles 22 times, so in 24 hours it happens 44 times. So the answer is 44.
Source: PYQ-style

29. A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. If it is set right at 6 a.m., what will it show when the correct time is 6 p.m.?
(a) 6:05 p.m. (b) 7:00 p.m. (c) 7:05 p.m. (d) 6:55 p.m.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding only 5 minutes, treating the gain as a one-time error instead of an hourly one. From 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. is 12 correct hours, so the clock gains 5 × 12 = 60 minutes and shows 6 p.m. + 60 minutes. So the answer is 7:00 p.m.
Source: PYQ-style

30. The minute hand of a clock overtakes the hour hand at intervals of 65 minutes of correct time. How much does the clock gain or lose per day?
(a) Gains 11 minutes (b) Gains 10 10/143 minutes (c) Loses 10 10/143 minutes (d) Loses 11 minutes
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is thinking the clock loses time; since the hands meet sooner than the correct interval of 65 5/11 minutes, the clock runs fast. Gain in 65 minutes = 5/11 minute, so gain per day = (5/11) × (24 × 60)/65 = 720/143 = 10 10/143 minutes. So the answer is gains 10 10/143 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

31. What was the day of the week on 15 August 1947?
(a) Wednesday (b) Saturday (c) Thursday (d) Friday
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is miscounting the leap years between year 1 and 1946. Odd days up to 1946 years = 1; days from 1 January to 15 August 1947 = 227 = 3 odd days; total 4 odd days from Monday gives Friday. So the answer is Friday.
Source: PYQ-style

32. If 1 January 2001 was a Monday, what day of the week was 1 January 2006?
(a) Friday (b) Saturday (c) Sunday (d) Monday
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is counting 5 odd days by ignoring the leap year 2004, which would wrongly give Saturday. Odd days = 4 ordinary years × 1 + 1 leap year × 2 = 6, and Monday + 6 = Sunday. So the answer is Sunday.
Source: PYQ-style

33. The calendar of the year 2007 can be used again in which year?
(a) 2016 (b) 2018 (c) 2017 (d) 2015
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is picking 2016 because it is a leap year, but a repeated calendar must match both the starting day and the leap/ordinary nature of the year. Adding odd days from 2007 onward: 1+2+1+1+1+2+1+1+1+2+1 = 14, a multiple of 7, reached in 2018, which is also an ordinary year like 2007. So the answer is 2018.
Source: PYQ-style

34. How many leap years are there in 400 consecutive years of the Gregorian calendar?
(a) 96 (b) 99 (c) 100 (d) 97
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 100 by ignoring the century rule. In 400 years there are 100 multiples of 4, minus 4 century years, plus 1 year divisible by 400, giving 97. So the answer is 97.
Source: PYQ-style

35. If today is Wednesday, what will be the day after 61 days?
(a) Thursday (b) Tuesday (c) Wednesday (d) Monday
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using the quotient 8 instead of the remainder, or counting 61 as 60 and landing on Tuesday. 61 ÷ 7 gives remainder 5, and Wednesday + 5 days = Monday. So the answer is Monday.
Source: PYQ-style

36. At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock will the hands of a clock be in opposite directions (180° apart)?
(a) 5 5/11 minutes past 7 (b) 6 minutes past 7 (c) 10 10/11 minutes past 7 (d) 5 minutes past 7
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is finding the 5-space gain correctly but then dividing by 60 instead of 55. At 7 o'clock the hands are 35 spaces apart; to be 30 spaces apart the minute hand must gain 5 spaces, taking (5 × 60)/55 = 60/11 = 5 5/11 minutes. So the answer is 5 5/11 minutes past 7.
Source: PYQ-style

37. The reflex angle between the hands of a clock at 10:25 is:
(a) 197.5° (b) 192.5° (c) 180° (d) 162.5°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is stopping at 162.5°, which is the smaller angle, not the reflex angle asked. Smaller angle = |30 × 10 − (11/2) × 25| = |300 − 137.5| = 162.5°, so reflex angle = 360° − 162.5° = 197.5°. So the answer is 197.5°.
Source: PYQ-style

38. A watch gains 2 minutes every hour. It is set right at 6 a.m. What will it show when the correct time is 6 p.m. the same day?
(a) 6:12 p.m. (b) 6:02 p.m. (c) 6:24 p.m. (d) 7:00 p.m.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding only 2 minutes, treating the gain as a single error rather than 2 minutes per hour. From 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. is 12 correct hours, so the watch gains 2 × 12 = 24 minutes and shows 6:24 p.m. So the answer is 6:24 p.m.
Source: PYQ-style

39. What was the day of the week on 26 January 1950?
(a) Thursday (b) Tuesday (c) Wednesday (d) Friday
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is counting 49 years as 49 odd days without separating the 12 leap years. Odd days: 1600 years = 0, 300 years = 1, 49 years = 12 × 2 + 37 × 1 = 61 = 5 odd days, plus 26 days of January = 5 odd days; total 0 + 1 + 5 + 5 = 11 = 4 odd days from Monday gives Thursday. So the answer is Thursday.
Source: PYQ-style

40. If the 5th of a month falls 3 days after Sunday, what day will the 25th of that month be?
(a) Wednesday (b) Tuesday (c) Thursday (d) Friday
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding 20 days directly to Wednesday without reducing modulo 7. Three days after Sunday is Wednesday, so the 5th is Wednesday; the 12th, 19th and 26th are also Wednesdays, making the 25th a Tuesday. So the answer is Tuesday.
Source: PYQ-style

41. How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in 12 hours?
(a) 13 (b) 12 (c) 11 (d) 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering 12, assuming one coincidence per hour; between 11 and 1 the hands coincide only once, at 12 o'clock. The hands coincide every 65 5/11 minutes, giving 11 coincidences in 12 hours. So the answer is 11.
Source: PYQ-style

42. The angle between the hands of a clock at 6:30 is:
(a) 15° (b) 0° (c) 30° (d) 22.5°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 0°, assuming the hands overlap at half past six; in fact the hour hand has moved 15° past 6. Angle = |30 × 6 − (11/2) × 30| = |180 − 165| = 15°. So the answer is 15°.
Source: PYQ-style

43. Which of the following years is a leap year?
(a) 2000 (b) 2100 (c) 1900 (d) 1800
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating every year divisible by 4 as leap, which would wrongly include 1900. Century years are leap only when divisible by 400; 2000 ÷ 400 = 5 exactly, while 1800, 1900 and 2100 are not divisible by 400. So the answer is 2000.
Source: PYQ-style

44. If 1 March 2024 was a Friday, what day of the week will 1 March 2025 be?
(a) Sunday (b) Thursday (c) Saturday (d) Friday
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is counting 2 odd days because 2024 is a leap year, forgetting that 29 February 2024 falls before 1 March. The period 1 March 2024 to 1 March 2025 contains 365 days = 1 odd day, and Friday + 1 = Saturday. So the answer is Saturday.
Source: PYQ-style

45. At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 27 3/11 minutes past 2 (b) 25 minutes past 2 (c) 30 minutes past 2 (d) 21 9/11 minutes past 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is gaining only 15 spaces, which ignores the initial 10-space gap at 2 o'clock. The minute hand must gain 10 + 15 = 25 spaces, taking (25 × 60)/55 = 300/11 = 27 3/11 minutes. So the answer is 27 3/11 minutes past 2.
Source: PYQ-style

46. A clock shows 8 a.m. Through how many degrees will the hour hand rotate by 2 p.m. the same day?
(a) 200° (b) 150° (c) 180° (d) 120°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using the minute hand's speed of 6° per minute or counting only 5 hours. From 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. is 6 hours, and the hour hand moves 30° per hour, so 6 × 30° = 180°. So the answer is 180°.
Source: PYQ-style

47. The last day of a century cannot be:
(a) Monday (b) Friday (c) Wednesday (d) Saturday
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming all seven days are equally likely for a century's last day. The odd days in 100, 200, 300 and 400 years are 5, 3, 1 and 0, so the last day can only be Friday, Wednesday, Monday or Sunday; Saturday never occurs. So the answer is Saturday.
Source: PYQ-style

48. If the day before yesterday was Saturday, what day will it be the day after tomorrow?
(a) Thursday (b) Wednesday (c) Friday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is counting from yesterday instead of today, which gives Tuesday. Day before yesterday = Saturday means yesterday was Sunday and today is Monday; day after tomorrow = Monday + 2 days = Wednesday. So the answer is Wednesday.
Source: PYQ-style

49. How many degrees does the minute hand of a clock turn through in 20 minutes?
(a) 120° (b) 60° (c) 110° (d) 100°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is using the hour hand's rate of 0.5° per minute, which gives 10°, or confusing the rate with 30° per 5 minutes. The minute hand moves 6° per minute, so in 20 minutes it turns 20 × 6° = 120°. So the answer is 120°.
Source: PYQ-style

50. If 15 August 2020 was a Saturday, on which day will 15 August 2022 fall?
(a) Sunday (b) Saturday (c) Monday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding 2 odd days for 2020 being a leap year, but the leap day of 2020 fell before August. Each 12-month span from 15 August to the next 15 August contains no 29 February, so each contributes 1 odd day: Saturday → Sunday → Monday. So the answer is Monday.
Source: PYQ-style
