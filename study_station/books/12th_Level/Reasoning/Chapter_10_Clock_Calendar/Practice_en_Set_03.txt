51. At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be together?
(a) 21 9/11 minutes past 4 (b) 20 9/11 minutes past 4 (c) 21 3/11 minutes past 4 (d) 22 9/11 minutes past 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer of 20 minutes past 4 ignores that the hour hand also moves. At 4:00 the minute hand is 20 minutes behind, and it gains 55 minutes in 60 minutes, so it gains 20 minutes in (20 × 60)/55 = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes. So the answer is 21 9/11 minutes past 4.
Source: PYQ-style

52. The angle between the two hands of a clock at 6:30 is:
(a) 15° (b) 0° (c) 30° (d) 45°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is picking 0° thinking both hands point at 6. At 6:30 the hour hand has moved halfway between 6 and 7, i.e. 15° past 6, while the minute hand is exactly at 6. So the answer is 15°.

53. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 8:20?
(a) 120° (b) 130° (c) 140° (d) 110°
Answer: (b)
Solution: Angle = |30H − (11/2)M| = |240 − 110| = 130°. The trap is forgetting the hour hand's extra movement past 8, which gives 120°. So the answer is 130°.
Source: PYQ-style

54. At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 22 9/11 minutes past 7 (b) 25 minutes past 7 (c) 20 minutes past 7 (d) 21 9/11 minutes past 7
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first right angle occurs when the minute hand is 15 minutes behind the hour hand. At 7:00 it is 35 minutes behind, so it must gain 20 minutes: (20 × 60)/55 = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes. So the answer is 21 9/11 minutes past 7.

55. How many times in a day are the hands of a clock in a straight line but opposite in direction?
(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 11 (d) 22
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 24 assumes twice per hour for all 12 hours. In 12 hours the hands are opposite only 11 times (between 5 and 7 it happens once, not twice), so in 24 hours it happens 22 times. So the answer is 22.

56. A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. If it is set right at 6 a.m., what time will it show when the correct time is 6 p.m. the same day?
(a) 6:55 p.m. (b) 7:00 p.m. (c) 6:50 p.m. (d) 7:05 p.m.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding only 5 minutes once. In 12 hours the clock gains 12 × 5 = 60 minutes = 1 hour, so when the true time is 6 p.m. the faulty clock shows 7:00 p.m. So the answer is 7:00 p.m.
Source: PYQ-style

57. A watch loses 2 minutes per hour. It was set correctly at 10 a.m. What is the correct time when the watch shows 5 p.m. the same day?
(a) 5:10 p.m. (b) 5:14 p.m. (c) 5:07 p.m. (d) 5:12 p.m.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is simply adding 7 × 2 = 14 minutes to get 5:14 without checking — but here that shortcut needs verification: the watch covers 58 minutes for every true 60 minutes. Watch elapsed = 7 hours = 420 minutes, so true elapsed = 420 × 60/58 ≈ 434.5 minutes ≈ 7 hours 14.5 minutes, i.e. about 5:14 p.m. So the answer is 5:14 p.m.
Source: PYQ-style

58. The minute hand of a clock overtakes the hour hand at intervals of 65 minutes of correct time. How much does the clock gain or lose per day?
(a) Gains 10 10/143 minutes (b) Loses 10 10/143 minutes (c) Gains 5 5/143 minutes (d) Loses 5 5/143 minutes
Answer: (a)
Solution: In a correct clock the hands coincide every 65 5/11 minutes; here they coincide in 65 minutes, so the clock runs fast, ruling out the loss options. Gain per day = (5/11) × (24 × 60)/65 = 720/143 = 10 10/143 minutes. So the answer is gains 10 10/143 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

59. At what time between 3 and 4 o'clock are the hands of a clock exactly opposite to each other?
(a) 50 minutes past 3 (b) 48 1/11 minutes past 3 (c) 45 minutes past 3 (d) 49 1/11 minutes past 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: At 3:00 the minute hand is 15 minutes behind; to be opposite it must end 30 minutes ahead, i.e. gain 45 minutes. Time = (45 × 60)/55 = 540/11 = 49 1/11 minutes. So the answer is 49 1/11 minutes past 3.
Source: PYQ-style

60. The reflex angle between the hands of a clock at 10:25 is:
(a) 162.5° (b) 197.5° (c) 180° (d) 195°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is reporting the direct angle 162.5° = |300 − 137.5|. The question asks for the reflex angle: 360° − 162.5° = 197.5°. So the answer is 197.5°.

61. If 1st January 2001 was a Monday, what day of the week was 1st January 2005?
(a) Saturday (b) Friday (c) Sunday (d) Thursday
Answer: (a)
Solution: From 2001 to 2005 there are 4 years including one leap year (2004), giving 4 + 1 = 5 odd days. Monday + 5 = Saturday; the trap is forgetting the leap year 2004 and answering Friday. So the answer is Saturday.
Source: PYQ-style

62. What day of the week was 15th August 1947?
(a) Thursday (b) Saturday (c) Friday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: Odd days: 1600 years → 0; 300 years → 1; 46 years = 11 leap + 35 ordinary → 57 ≡ 1; days till 15 August = 227 ≡ 3. Total = 0 + 1 + 1 + 3 = 5 → Friday. So the answer is Friday.

63. If today is Wednesday, what day will it be after 61 days?
(a) Monday (b) Sunday (c) Tuesday (d) Saturday
Answer: (a)
Solution: 61 ÷ 7 = 8 weeks and 5 odd days; the trap is adding the 8 weeks instead of the remainder. Wednesday + 5 = Monday. So the answer is Monday.

64. Which of the following years is a leap year?
(a) 2000 (b) 2100 (c) 1800 (d) 1900
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that 1900, 1800 and 2100 are divisible by 4 but are century years not divisible by 400, so they are not leap years. 2000 is divisible by 400, so it is a leap year. So the answer is 2000.
Source: PYQ-style

65. The calendar of the year 2014 will repeat in which year?
(a) 2019 (b) 2020 (c) 2026 (d) 2025
Answer: (d)
Solution: 2014 is an ordinary year; cumulative odd days from 2015 to 2025 total 11 ordinary + 3 leap contributions = 14 ≡ 0, so the calendar repeats after 11 years. The trap is assuming a fixed 6-year gap for every ordinary year. So the answer is 2025.
Source: PYQ-style

66. If 5th March of a year is Tuesday, what day is 5th July of the same year (non-leap year)?
(a) Saturday (b) Thursday (c) Friday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: Days from 5 March to 5 July: 26 + 30 + 31 + 30 + 5 = 122 days = 17 weeks + 3 odd days. Tuesday + 3 = Friday; the trap is miscounting the remaining days of March. So the answer is Friday.

67. How many odd days are there in 100 years?
(a) 3 (b) 6 (c) 5 (d) 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: 100 years = 76 ordinary + 24 leap years, giving 76 + 48 = 124 ≡ 5 odd days. The trap is counting 25 leap years, but the 100th year is not a leap year unless divisible by 400. So the answer is 5.
Source: PYQ-style

68. If 1st January of a leap year is Sunday, what day is 1st January of the next year?
(a) Monday (b) Sunday (c) Tuesday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: A leap year has 366 days = 52 weeks + 2 odd days, so the next year starts 2 days later. The trap is adding only 1 odd day as in an ordinary year. Sunday + 2 = Tuesday, so the answer is Tuesday.
Source: PYQ-style

69. What was the day of the week on 26th January 1950?

Answer: (c)
Solution: Odd days: 1600 years → 0; 300 years → 1; 49 years = 12 leap + 37 ordinary → 61 ≡ 5; 26 days of January ≡ 5. Total = 0 + 1 + 5 + 5 = 11 ≡ 4 → Thursday. So the answer is Thursday.

70. In a month of 31 days, if the third Thursday falls on the 17th, what day is the 1st of that month?
(a) Sunday (b) Monday (c) Tuesday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: If the 17th is the third Thursday, Thursdays fall on 3rd, 10th, 17th, 24th and 31st. The 1st is two days before Thursday the 3rd, i.e. Tuesday. So the answer is Tuesday.

71. The hands of a clock coincide every:
(a) 65 minutes (b) 65 5/11 minutes (c) 64 minutes (d) 66 minutes
Answer: (b)
Solution: The minute hand gains 55 minutes over the hour hand in 60 minutes, so to gain a full 60 minutes it takes 60 × 60/55 = 720/11 = 65 5/11 minutes. The trap is rounding to 65 minutes. So the answer is 65 5/11 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

72. If the day before yesterday was Saturday, what day will it be the day after tomorrow?
(a) Wednesday (b) Tuesday (c) Friday (d) Thursday
Answer: (a)
Solution: Day before yesterday = Saturday means yesterday was Sunday and today is Monday; the trap is losing count in the chain and landing on Thursday. Day after tomorrow = Monday + 2 = Wednesday. So the answer is Wednesday.
Source: PYQ-style

73. A clock shows 4:15. Through what angle has the hour hand moved from the 4 o'clock position?
(a) 12.5° (b) 15° (c) 30° (d) 7.5°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The hour hand moves 0.5° per minute, so in 15 minutes it moves 7.5°. The trap is using the minute hand's speed of 6° per minute, which would give 90°-style errors. So the answer is 7.5°.
Source: PYQ-style

74. How many times do the hands of a clock form a right angle in 12 hours?
(a) 24 (b) 22 (c) 20 (d) 11
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 24 assumes two right angles in every hour, but between 2–3 and 8–9 o'clock only one right angle occurs in each span. Hence 24 − 2 = 22. So the answer is 22.

75. If 15th August 2020 was Saturday, what day was 15th August 2019?
(a) Friday (b) Tuesday (c) Wednesday (d) Thursday
Answer: (d)
Solution: The span from 15 Aug 2019 to 15 Aug 2020 includes 29 Feb 2020, so it has 366 days = 2 odd days; the trap is subtracting only 1 odd day. Saturday − 2 = Thursday. So the answer is Thursday.
