126. At what time between 4 o'clock and 5 o'clock do the hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 4:20 (b) 4:22 2/11 min (c) 4:21 9/11 min (d) 4:21 6/11 min
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option 4:20 comes from wrongly assuming the hour hand stays fixed at 4; it actually moves ahead as the minute hand advances. Coincidence time = (4 × 5) × 12/11 minutes past 4 = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes, so the answer is 4:21 9/11 min.

Solution: The option 5:43 7/11 min is the second right angle of the hour, a trap for those who forget the question asks for the first. For the first right angle, the minute hand is 15 minute-spaces behind the hour hand: (25 − 15) × 12/11 = 120/11 = 10 10/11 minutes, so the answer is 5:10 10/11 min.

130. A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. It was set right at 8 a.m. What will it show when the correct time is 5 p.m. the same day?

131. What was the day of the week on 15 August 1947?
(a) Saturday (b) Thursday (c) Friday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option Saturday comes from counting 1900 as a leap year, which it is not. Counting odd days: 1600 years = 0, 300 years = 1, 46 years = 11 leap + 35 ordinary = 57 days ≡ 1, and days up to 15 Aug 1947 = 227 ≡ 3; total 0 + 1 + 1 + 3 = 5, which corresponds to Friday, so the answer is Friday.
