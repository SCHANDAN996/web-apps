126. In a row of students, Ravi is 12th from the left end and 18th from the right end. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 28 (b) 31 (c) 30 (d) 29
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding 12 + 18 = 30, which counts Ravi twice. Total = 12 + 18 − 1 = 29, so the answer is 29.
Source: PYQ-style

127. In a class of 45 students, Mohan ranks 20th from the top. What is his rank from the bottom?
(a) 24th (b) 25th (c) 26th (d) 27th
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 45 − 20 = 25 and forgetting the +1. Rank from bottom = 45 − 20 + 1 = 26, so the answer is 26th.
Source: PYQ-style

128. In a row, A is 15th from the left and B is 10th from the right. If they interchange their positions, A becomes 22nd from the left. How many persons are there in the row?
(a) 30 (b) 31 (c) 32 (d) 33
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using A's original 15th position with 10, giving 24. After interchange, A sits at B's place, which is 22nd from left and 10th from right, so Total = 22 + 10 − 1 = 31, so the answer is 31.

143. In a class, the ratio of boys to girls is 3:2 and there are 40 students. Priya, a girl, is 10th from the top, and 5 girls are above her. How many girls are below Priya?
(a) 11 (b) 9 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is subtracting only the 5 girls above, giving 11, and forgetting Priya herself. Girls = (2/5) × 40 = 16, so girls below = 16 − 5 − 1 = 10, so the answer is 10.

148. In a queue of 36 people, Neha is 4th from the front. Behind her, every 3rd person is wearing a cap, counting the person immediately behind her as 1. How many cap-wearers are behind Neha?
(a) 10 (b) 9 (c) 11 (d) 12
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is dividing 32 by 3 and rounding up to 11, but position 33 does not exist. People behind Neha = 36 − 4 = 32, and cap-wearers stand at positions 3, 6, 9, …, 30, which is 32 ÷ 3 = 10 ignoring the remainder, so the answer is 10.
Source: PYQ-style

149. In a row of 45 students, Ankit, a boy, is 12th from the left. There are 8 girls to his left, and the 12 students immediately to his right include 7 girls. If the row has 20 girls in total and all remaining girls stand at the right end, how many girls are at the right end?
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 4

Solution: The trap is forgetting that Ankit is a boy and miscounting the groups. Girls accounted for = 8 + 7 = 15, so remaining girls = 20 − 15 = 5, so the answer is 5.
