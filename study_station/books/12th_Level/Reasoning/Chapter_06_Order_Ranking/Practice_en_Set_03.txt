51. In a row of students, Ravi is 12th from the left end and 18th from the right end. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 28 (b) 30 (c) 29 (d) 31
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding 12 + 18 = 30, which counts Ravi twice. Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 12 + 18 − 1 = 29, so the answer is 29.

52. In a class of 40 students, Mohan ranks 15th from the top. What is his rank from the bottom?
(a) 26th (b) 25th (c) 24th (d) 27th
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing 40 − 15 = 25 and forgetting to add 1. Rank from bottom = total − rank from top + 1 = 40 − 15 + 1 = 26, so the answer is 26th.

75. In a row of 55 students, Rekha is 24th from the left and Meera is 20th from the right. If Seema sits exactly midway between them, what is Seema's position from the left?
(a) 32nd (b) 31st (c) 33rd (d) 30th
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is averaging 24 with the right-side rank 20, which mixes two different reference points. Meera's position from the left = 55 − 20 + 1 = 36th, and the midpoint of 24th and 36th = (24 + 36)/2 = 30th, so the answer is 30th.
