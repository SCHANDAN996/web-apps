1. In a row of students, Ravi is 7th from the left end. What is his position from the right end if there are 20 students in the row?
(a) 13th (b) 12th (c) 15th (d) 14th
Answer: (d)
Solution: Position from right = Total − position from left + 1 = 20 − 7 + 1 = 14. Many students forget to add 1 and pick 13, which is the trap. So the answer is 14th.
Source: PYQ-style

3. In a queue, Asha is 9th from the front and 15th from the back. How many persons are in the queue?
(a) 24 (b) 22 (c) 23 (d) 25
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total = position from front + position from back − 1 = 9 + 15 − 1 = 23. Adding without subtracting 1 gives 24, which counts Asha twice — that is the trap. So the answer is 23.

5. In a row of boys, Suresh is 10th from the left and Ramesh is 12th from the right. If they interchange their positions, Suresh becomes 18th from the left. How many boys are in the row?
(a) 28 (b) 30 (c) 29 (d) 31
Answer: (c)
Solution: After interchange, Suresh is at Ramesh's place, which is 18th from left and 12th from right. Total = 18 + 12 − 1 = 29. The trap is using Suresh's old left position 10, which no longer applies after the swap. So the answer is 29.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The order is S, Q, P, R, T. The middle (3rd) position is occupied by P. The trap is picking Q, who sits next to P but is 2nd, not 3rd. So the answer is P.

21. In a row of students, A is 13th from the left and B is 17th from the right. If they interchange their positions, A becomes 21st from the left. What is B's new position from the right?
(a) 17th (b) 13th (c) 25th (d) 21st
Answer: (c)
Solution: After interchange, A sits at B's original place, which is 21st from left and 17th from right, so total = 21 + 17 − 1 = 37. B now sits at A's original place, 13th from left, so B's new position from right = 37 − 13 + 1 = 25. The trap is answering 13th, which is B's new position from the left, not from the right. So the answer is 25th.
Source: PYQ-style

22. In a class, Rohit's rank is 16th from the top. There are 7 students who failed and they occupy the bottom 7 ranks. If Rohit's rank from the bottom among the passed students is 10th, how many students are in the class?
(a) 33 (b) 34 (c) 31 (d) 32
Answer: (d)
Solution: Passed students = 16 + 10 − 1 = 25. Total students = 25 + 7 = 32. The trap is mis-adding to get 33 by forgetting to subtract 1 while counting the passed students. So the answer is 32.
Source: PYQ-style

23. In a row of boys facing north, A is 10th from the left and B is 9th from the right. If there are 8 boys between A and B, which of the following can be the total number of boys in the row?
(a) 26 (b) 27 (c) 25 (d) 24
Answer: (b)
Solution: If A is left of B: total = 10 + 8 + 9 = 27. If B is left of A, the overlap gives total = 10 + 9 − 8 − 2 = 9, which is impossible since A alone is 10th from the left. The trap is assuming the overlapping case also works. So the answer is 27.
Source: PYQ-style

25. In a row of 45 students, Sunil is 20th from the left and Anil is 30th from the right. How many students are there between Sunil and Anil?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 3 (d) 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Anil's position from the left = 45 − 30 + 1 = 16th. Sunil is 20th from the left, so Anil is to the left of Sunil. Students between 16th and 20th positions = 20 − 16 − 1 = 3. The trap is subtracting directly (20 − 16 = 4) without removing the endpoint. So the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style
