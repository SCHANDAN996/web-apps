Card 1
Front: In a row of students, A is 5th from the left and B is 7th from the right. If there is no one between them, what is the total number of students in the row?
Back: 5 + 7 = 12 students. When positions from opposite ends are given with no overlap, total = (position from left) + (position from right).

Card 2
Front: What is the formula for the total number of people in a row when a person's position from both ends is known?
Back: Total = (position from left) + (position from right) − 1. The person is counted twice, so subtract 1.

Card 3
Front: Ravi is 12th from the top and 18th from the bottom in a class list. How many students are in the class?
Back: 12 + 18 − 1 = 29 students.

Card 4
Front: In a row, P is 8th from the left and Q is 10th from the right, and P and Q interchange their positions. After interchanging, P becomes 15th from the left. How many people are in the row?
Back: After interchange, P takes Q's place, so Q's original position is 15th from the left and 10th from the right. Total = 15 + 10 − 1 = 24.

Card 5
Front: What does "rank from the right" mean if a person is 6th from the left in a row of 20 people?
Back: Position from right = Total − position from left + 1 = 20 − 6 + 1 = 15th from the right.

Card 6
Front: In a queue, Mohan is 9th from the front and Sohan is 14th from the back. There are 5 people between them. What is the total number of people (assuming Mohan is ahead of Sohan)?
Back: Total = 9 + 5 + 14 = 28 people. Add the people between the two when their positions are from opposite ends without overlap.

Card 7
Front: In a row of 40 students, how many students sit between the 10th student from the left and the 12th student from the right?
Back: 12th from right = 40 − 12 + 1 = 29th from left. Students between 10th and 29th = 29 − 10 − 1 = 18.

Card 8
Front: What is the quick formula to convert a position from one end to the other end in a row of N people?
Back: Position from other end = N − (given position) + 1.

Card 9
Front: In an exam merit list, Asha ranks 7th from the top. If 35 students are below her, how many students appeared in the list?
Back: 7 + 35 = 42 students. (Her rank counts herself; the 35 below are additional.)

Card 10
Front: Five friends A, B, C, D, E are standing in a row facing north. C is to the immediate right of A, and D is at the extreme left. Who can be at the extreme right?
Back: B or E — the information fixes only D (extreme left) and the A–C pair; B and E can occupy the remaining spots, so the extreme right cannot be uniquely determined.

Card 11
Front: In a row facing north, "X is to the immediate left of Y" — what does this mean for their positions?
Back: X sits exactly one place before Y on Y's left side; since they face north, left is the viewer's left, so X comes just before Y from the left end.

Card 12
Front: In a row of 25 boys, Ram is shifted 3 places to his right from his original 8th position from the left. What is his new position from the right?
Back: New position from left = 8 + 3 = 11th. From right = 25 − 11 + 1 = 15th.

Card 13
Front: What is the common trap in "interchange of positions" questions in ranking?
Back: After two people swap, each takes the other's position. Use the new rank of one person with the other person's old rank from the opposite end to find the total — do not add both new ranks.

Card 14
Front: In a class, Sita is taller than Gita but shorter than Rita. Mita is taller than Rita. Who is the tallest?
Back: Mita. Order: Mita > Rita > Sita > Gita.

Card 15
Front: In a row of 30 students, how many students are there between the 7th and the 20th student from the left?
Back: 20 − 7 − 1 = 12 students. Subtract 1 because the two endpoint students are not counted.

Card 16
Front: A is 4th from the left and B is 5th from the right in a row, and they are adjacent with A to the left of B. What is the total number of people?
Back: Total = 4 + 5 = 9. Since A is immediately left of B, there is no one between them, so simply add the two positions.

Card 17
Front: What should you check first when positions from two ends are given along with "people between them"?
Back: Check whether the two positions overlap (total would be less than the sum) or not. If they overlap, subtract: Total = (left position) + (right position) − (people between) − 2.

Card 18
Front: In a row of 50 people, a man is 17th from the right. What is his position from the left?
Back: 50 − 17 + 1 = 34th from the left.
