26. In a row of students, Ravi is 12th from the left end and 18th from the right end. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 28 (b) 30 (c) 29 (d) 31
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding 12 + 18 = 30, which counts Ravi twice. Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 12 + 18 − 1 = 29, so the answer is 29.
Source: PYQ-style

27. In a class of 45 students, Meena's rank is 17th from the top. What is her rank from the bottom?
(a) 27 (b) 28 (c) 30 (d) 29
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 45 − 17 = 28, which forgets the +1 adjustment. Rank from bottom = Total − rank from top + 1 = 45 − 17 + 1 = 29, so the answer is 29.
