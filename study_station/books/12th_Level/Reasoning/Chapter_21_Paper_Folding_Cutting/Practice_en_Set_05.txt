101. A square sheet of paper is folded once along the vertical centre line and then once along the horizontal centre line. A single circular hole is punched through all layers at the folded corner. How many holes appear when the sheet is unfolded?
(a) 2 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each fold doubles the number of layers. Two folds create 4 layers, so one punch passes through 4 layers and produces 4 holes on unfolding. The tempting answer 2 counts only one fold, forgetting the second fold also doubles the layers, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

102. A rectangular sheet is folded along its diagonal. A triangular cut is made near the fold. After unfolding, what shape does the cut form?
(a) A triangle (b) A rhombus (c) A square (d) A circle
Answer: (b)
Solution: A cut made on a fold line gets mirrored when unfolded. A triangle mirrored across its base forms a rhombus (a kite-like four-sided figure). Choosing "a triangle" ignores the mirror effect of the fold, so the answer is a rhombus.
Source: PYQ-style

103. A sheet is folded into quarters by folding it in half twice. A hole is punched at the centre of the folded quarter. On unfolding, the holes will be arranged as:
(a) Four holes at the corners of a square (b) Four holes in a straight line (c) Two holes only (d) Eight holes in a circle
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two perpendicular folds create four symmetric layers. A punch at the centre of the folded quarter reflects across both fold lines, giving four holes placed symmetrically at the corners of a square. The trap answer of a straight line would occur only if both folds were in the same direction, so the answer is four holes at the corners of a square.
Source: PYQ-style

104. A circular paper is folded into a semicircle and then into a quarter circle. A small notch is cut at the folded apex. How many notches appear on the unfolded circle?
(a) 2 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: Folding a circle into a semicircle gives 2 layers; folding again into a quarter gives 4 layers. One cut at the apex therefore replicates into 4 symmetric notches. Counting only the visible folded shape gives the wrong answer 2, so the answer is 4.

105. A square sheet is folded along both diagonals, one after the other. A hole is punched through the resulting triangle. How many holes appear on unfolding?

Answer: (a)
Solution: Folding along one diagonal gives 2 layers; folding along the other diagonal of the folded triangle gives 4 layers. One punch through 4 layers yields 4 holes. The trap answer 8 assumes each fold doubles independently without considering the geometry of diagonal folds, so the answer is 4.

106. A paper is folded in half vertically, then the folded paper is folded in half horizontally. Two holes are punched in the folded piece. How many holes appear after unfolding?
(a) 8 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 16
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two folds create 4 layers. Each punch goes through all 4 layers, so each punch produces 4 holes. Two punches produce 2 × 4 = 8 holes. The trap answer 4 counts only one punch, so the answer is 8.

107. A sheet is folded once along the horizontal midline. A semicircular cut is made with its diameter lying exactly on the fold line. After unfolding, the cut becomes:
(a) A semicircle (b) A full circle (c) An ellipse (d) Two separate semicircles
Answer: (b)
Solution: When a shape is cut with its edge on the fold line, unfolding mirrors it across the fold. A semicircle mirrored across its diameter forms a complete circle. The trap answer "two separate semicircles" forgets that the cut is joined along the fold, so the answer is a full circle.

Solution: Unfolding reflects every punched point across the fold line. Since the punch is away from the fold, it produces two holes that are mirror images across the diagonal. The trap answer "at the same point" would hold only if the punch were exactly on the fold, so the answer is mirror images across the diagonal.

Solution: The punch is on the curved edge, away from the fold (the diameter). Unfolding mirrors it across the diameter, giving two holes at opposite points on the circle's edge. 116. A sheet is folded in half horizontally. Two cuts are made: one on the fold line and one away from it. After unfolding, how many cut marks appear in total?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The cut on the fold line opens into a single joined shape (1 mark), while the cut away from the fold is mirrored and appears twice (2 marks). Total = 1 + 2 = 3. The trap answer 4 assumes both cuts double, forgetting that a fold-line cut does not double, so the answer is 3.

117. A paper is folded into quarters. A hole is punched exactly at the corner where the two fold edges meet. After unfolding, the hole appears:
(a) At four different points (b) At the corners of the sheet (c) At two points (d) As one hole at the centre of the sheet
Answer: (d)
Solution: The meeting point of both fold edges corresponds to the centre of the original sheet. A punch there lies on both fold lines, so it does not replicate — it opens into a single hole at the centre. The trap answer "four different points" ignores that the punch is on the folds, so the answer is one hole at the centre of the sheet.

Solution: A punch lying on one fold line is mirrored only across the other fold. The trap answer 4 assumes the punch is away from both folds — this is the examiner's double-condition trap, so the answer is 2.

123. A sheet is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally. Three holes are punched: one at the double-fold corner, one on one fold edge, and one in the interior of the folded piece. How many holes appear after unfolding?

Answer: (d)
Solution: The punch at the double-fold corner lies on both folds, giving 1 hole. The punch on one fold edge doubles once, giving 2 holes. The interior punch doubles twice, giving 4 holes. Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7. The trap answer 12 multiplies all three punches by 4, ignoring the fold-line positions, so the answer is 7.

125. A rectangular sheet is folded in half lengthwise, then the folded strip is folded in half again crosswise, and finally folded along the diagonal of the small rectangle. Two holes are punched in the interior of the final folded shape, away from all fold edges. How many holes appear on complete unfolding?
(a) 16 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: Three folds give 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 layers. Each interior punch passes through all 8 layers, producing 8 holes per punch. Two punches give 2 × 8 = 16 holes. The trap answer 8 counts only one punch — a classic misleading-option trap — so the answer is 16.
Source: PYQ-style
