26. A square sheet of paper is folded once along the vertical midline and then once along the horizontal midline. A single circular hole is punched through all layers at the centre of the folded quarter. How many holes appear when the paper is unfolded?
(a) 2 (b) 8 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is 8, assuming each fold doubles the holes twice more, but there are only two folds. Each fold doubles the number of layers: 2 folds give 4 layers, so one punch makes 4 holes symmetric about both fold lines. So the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

27. A rectangular sheet is folded along its diagonal. A triangular cut is made near the folded edge. When unfolded, the cut will appear as:
(a) A quadrilateral formed by two mirror-image triangles joined along the diagonal (b) One triangle (c) Two separate triangles (d) A circle
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students pick two separate triangles, forgetting the cut is made on the fold itself. A cut on a fold line mirrors across that line and joins into a single symmetric shape — two mirror triangles sharing the diagonal edge form a quadrilateral. So the answer is a quadrilateral formed by two mirror-image triangles joined along the diagonal.

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is choosing the four corners, confusing the folded corner with the sheet's corners. The corner where both folded edges meet corresponds to the centre of the original sheet, and all four layers punch at that same central point. So the answer is at the centre of the sheet.
