76. A square sheet of paper is folded once along its vertical centre line and then once along its horizontal centre line. A single circular hole is punched through all layers at the centre of the folded quarter. How many holes appear when the sheet is unfolded?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each fold doubles the number of layers. Two folds create 4 layers, so one punch passes through 4 layers and produces 4 holes symmetrically placed about both fold lines. The trap is answering 2 by counting only one fold. So the answer is 4.

Solution: A fold acts as a line of symmetry; any cut made on folded paper reflects across the fold line on unfolding. The trap is choosing overlapping marks, which happens only if the cut lies exactly on the fold line. So the answer is mirror images across the diagonal.
