Questions 51–75: Medium

51. A square sheet of paper is folded once along its vertical midline and then once along its horizontal midline. A single circular hole is punched through all layers at the centre of the folded quarter. How many holes appear when the sheet is unfolded?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each fold doubles the layers, so two folds give 4 layers and one punch passes through all 4. The tempting answer 8 assumes three folds, which did not happen here. So the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A rectangular sheet is folded along its diagonal and a triangular cut is made at the folded corner. When unfolded, the cut will appear as:
(a) A single rhombus-shaped hole (b) Two separate triangles (c) A circle (d) A square hole with rounded edges
Answer: (a)
Solution: A cut made on a fold line is mirrored across that fold when opened, and two mirror-image triangles joined along the fold form a rhombus. Two separate triangles would result only if the cut were away from the fold. So the answer is a single rhombus-shaped hole.
Source: PYQ-style

53. A sheet is folded into half, then folded into half again in the same direction. How many layers does the folded paper have?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 8 (d) 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The first fold gives 2 layers and the second doubles them to 4. The trap is to think of three doublings and say 8, but only two folds were made: 2 × 2 = 4. So the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

54. A square paper is folded along both diagonals (one after the other) and a hole is punched at a point not on any fold line. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 2 (b) 8 (c) 16 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: Folding along one diagonal gives 2 layers and folding along the second gives 4 layers, so one punch produces 4 holes. The tempting answer 8 wrongly assumes each diagonal fold doubles the layers twice more. So the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

55. A paper is folded once vertically (left half over right half). A cut is made on the folded edge. After unfolding, the two halves of the design will be:
(a) Identical and facing the same direction (b) Unrelated (c) Rotated by 180° (d) Mirror images of each other
Answer: (d)
Solution: Unfolding reflects the cut across the fold line, so the design on one side is the lateral mirror image of the other. Rotation by 180° is the trap — reflection across a vertical line is not the same as a half-turn. So the answer is mirror images of each other.
Source: PYQ-style

56. A square sheet is folded into quarters (half, then half again). A semicircular cut is made with its diameter lying exactly on one folded edge. On complete unfolding, how many complete circles are formed?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: A semicircle cut on a fold opens into a full circle across that fold, and the second fold duplicates this circle once, giving 2 complete circles. The trap answer 4 counts the semicircles in 4 layers without pairing them across the fold. So the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

57. A strip of paper is folded three times in the same direction (each time in half). If one hole is punched through all layers, how many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 8 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 16
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each fold doubles the layers: 2, then 4, then 8, so one punch through 8 layers gives 8 holes. The trap is to add folds (2 + 2 + 2 = 6) instead of multiplying. So the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

58. A circular sheet of paper is folded into a semicircle and then into a quarter circle. A small notch is cut at the apex (the corner where the folds meet). On unfolding, the notch becomes:
(a) Four holes near the rim (b) A hole on the rim (c) A hole at the centre of the circle (d) A semicircular gap on the edge
Answer: (c)
Solution: The apex of the quarter-folded circle corresponds to the centre of the original circle, so a cut there opens into a single hole at the centre. Holes near the rim would come from cuts at the curved edge, not the apex. So the answer is a hole at the centre of the circle.
Source: PYQ-style

59. A square paper is folded along the horizontal midline, then along the vertical midline. A straight cut is made parallel to one folded edge, away from all fold lines. On unfolding, the cuts will be:
(a) 1 long slit (b) 2 slits only (c) 8 slits (d) 4 separate slits symmetric about both midlines
Answer: (d)
Solution: Two folds create 4 layers, and since the cut is away from fold lines it does not merge with its reflections, appearing as 4 separate mirrored slits. The trap answer 2 counts only one fold. So the answer is 4 separate slits symmetric about both midlines.
Source: PYQ-style

60. In a paper folding problem, a sheet is folded so that the bottom edge meets the top edge, and then the left edge meets the right edge. The folded corner that has no open edges corresponds to which part of the original sheet?
(a) The centre of the sheet (b) The top-right corner (c) The bottom-left corner (d) The midpoint of the top edge
Answer: (a)
Solution: The corner where both fold lines meet — the fully closed corner — maps to the centre of the original sheet, while the outer corners of the folded packet have open edges. Picking an outer corner is the trap, since those corners contain free edges. So the answer is the centre of the sheet.
Source: PYQ-style

61. A sheet is folded once and a design is cut touching the fold line at two points. On unfolding, the design will be:
(a) Two separate identical designs (b) A design rotated by 90° (c) One single symmetric design joined across the fold (d) Four designs
Answer: (c)
Solution: Because the cut touches the fold at two points, the mirrored halves remain connected at those points when opened, forming one continuous symmetric figure. Two separate designs would appear only if the cut never touched the fold. So the answer is one single symmetric design joined across the fold.
Source: PYQ-style

62. A square paper is folded into half vertically, then a punch is made near the open edge (not the fold). On unfolding, the two holes will be located:
(a) At the same spot (b) Symmetrically about the horizontal midline (c) Symmetrically about the vertical midline (d) Diagonally opposite
Answer: (c)
Solution: The single fold is vertical, so unfolding reflects the hole across the vertical midline, placing the two holes symmetrically left and right of it. The horizontal midline is the trap — no horizontal fold was made. So the answer is symmetrically about the vertical midline.
Source: PYQ-style

63. A paper is folded twice: first the upper half down, then the right half to the left. A hole is punched at the top-right corner of the folded packet, which lies on both folded edges. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 1 (c) 8 (d) 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: A punch exactly on a fold line is shared by the layers on both sides of that fold; being on both fold lines, it lies at the meeting point of the folds — the centre of the sheet — so all layers open into a single hole. The trap answer 4 ignores that punches on fold lines merge. So the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

64. A rectangular sheet is folded into thirds (two parallel folds, like a letter). A hole is punched through all layers away from the folds. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: Folding into thirds creates 3 layers, not 4, so one punch through 3 layers gives 3 holes. The trap answer 4 comes from assuming two folds always mean 4 layers — true only when each fold halves the whole stack. So the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

65. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal. A cut is made perpendicular to the fold, starting from the fold and ending inside the paper. On unfolding, the cut becomes:
(a) Two separate slits (b) A V-shaped slit symmetric about the diagonal (c) A single straight slit crossing the diagonal (d) A curved slit
Answer: (c)
Solution: The cut starts on the fold, so on opening, the cut and its mirror image join end to end at the fold, forming one straight slit that crosses the diagonal at right angles. A V-shape would result only if the cut met the fold at an angle other than 90°. So the answer is a single straight slit crossing the diagonal.
Source: PYQ-style

66. A paper is folded in half, then in half again (both folds parallel). A punch is made on the folded packet at a point lying on one folded edge only. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: Two parallel folds give 4 layers, but a punch on a fold line is shared between the two layers meeting at that fold, so the 4 layers produce only 2 distinct holes mirrored across the other fold. The trap answer 4 ignores the merging at the fold line. So the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

67. A transparent sheet with a pattern is folded along its vertical midline from left to right. The pattern on the left half, after folding, will appear on the right half as:
(a) Exactly the same, shifted right (b) Rotated by 90° (c) Upside down (d) Laterally inverted (mirror reversed)
Answer: (d)
Solution: Folding a transparent sheet flips the left half over the vertical line, so the pattern is seen laterally inverted on the right half. Upside down would require a horizontal flip, and rotation is not produced by folding. So the answer is laterally inverted (mirror reversed).
Source: PYQ-style

68. A square paper is folded along the vertical midline and then along the horizontal midline of the folded rectangle. A triangular cut is made at the corner where the two folded edges meet. On unfolding, the hole formed is:
(a) A triangle at the centre (b) A circle at the centre (c) Four triangles at the corners (d) A square/diamond hole at the centre
Answer: (d)
Solution: The corner where both folds meet is the centre of the original sheet, and a triangular cut there is mirrored across both folds so that four mirror-image triangles join into a square or diamond. Four separate triangles would appear only if the cut were away from the folds. So the answer is a square/diamond hole at the centre.
Source: PYQ-style

69. A sheet is folded once horizontally. Two punches are made on the folded sheet, one near the fold and one near the open edge. On unfolding, the total number of holes is:
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each punch passes through 2 layers, so each produces 2 holes on unfolding, giving 2 + 2 = 4 holes. The trap answer 2 counts only the punches, forgetting the doubling by the fold. So the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

70. A circular paper is folded into a semicircle. A straight cut is made connecting two points on the diameter (the folded edge), passing through the interior. On unfolding, the cut-out piece removed is:
(a) A semicircle (b) A symmetric shape about the diameter (c) A triangle (d) A quarter circle
Answer: (b)
Solution: Any cut whose endpoints lie on the fold opens into a shape mirrored across the fold, hence symmetric about the diameter, whatever its exact outline. A semicircle would require a curved cut, which was not stated. So the answer is a symmetric shape about the diameter.
Source: PYQ-style

71. A square sheet is folded into half diagonally, then folded into half again along the other diagonal direction of the folded triangle. A punch is made through all layers at a point not on any fold. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first diagonal fold gives 2 layers and the second fold of the triangle gives 4 layers, so one punch yields 4 holes arranged with diagonal symmetry. The trap answer 8 assumes three doublings. So the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

72. In a folded-paper problem, the last fold made is the first to be opened when reversing the process. This principle is used because:
(a) It saves time in the exam (b) Holes are always on the last fold (c) Unfolding must retrace the folds in reverse order to locate holes correctly (d) The paper is thicker at the last fold
Answer: (c)
Solution: To track where a punch lands on the original sheet, each fold must be undone in the reverse order of folding, reflecting the hole across one fold line at a time; skipping the order misplaces the reflections. Saving time is a benefit, not the reason for the principle. So the answer is that unfolding must retrace the folds in reverse order to locate holes correctly.
Source: PYQ-style

73. A rectangular sheet is folded in half lengthwise (top to bottom). A semicircular cut is made with its diameter on the open edges (not the fold). On unfolding, the result is:
(a) One full circle (b) Two separate semicircular cuts (c) Two full circles (d) One semicircle
Answer: (b)
Solution: Since the cut's diameter lies on the open edge and not on the fold, the cut does not merge with its reflection, giving two separate semicircular cuts mirrored across the fold. One full circle would appear only if the diameter were on the fold. So the answer is two separate semicircular cuts.
Source: PYQ-style

74. A square paper is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally. Three punches are made on the folded quarter: one on both fold lines (corner), one on one fold line, and one away from all folds. Total holes on unfolding:
(a) 12 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 7
Answer: (d)
Solution: The corner punch on both folds gives 1 hole, the punch on one fold line gives 2 holes, and the punch away from folds gives 4 holes, totalling 1 + 2 + 4 = 7. The trap answer 12 multiplies every punch by 4, ignoring that punches on fold lines merge. So the answer is 7.
Source: PYQ-style

75. A sheet is folded twice (half, then half again, folds perpendicular to each other). After punching and unfolding, the holes are found to be symmetric about both the vertical and horizontal midlines. This happens because:
(a) The two perpendicular folds act as two axes of reflection for every punch (b) The holes were on the fold lines (c) The punch was circular (d) The paper was square
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each fold line acts as a mirror when the sheet is opened, so with two perpendicular folds every punch is reflected across both axes and the final pattern is automatically symmetric about both midlines, wherever the punch was made. The shape of the paper or punch is irrelevant. So the answer is that the two perpendicular folds act as two axes of reflection for every punch.
Source: PYQ-style
