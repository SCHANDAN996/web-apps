Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Doctor : Patient :: Teacher : ?
(a) School (b) Student (c) Book (d) Principal
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is School, but that is the place where a teacher works, not the person served. A doctor treats a patient; a teacher teaches a student, so the answer is Student.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Book : Pages :: Wall : ?
(a) Bricks (b) Cement (c) House (d) Paint
Answer: (a)
Solution: House is tempting because a wall belongs to a house, but the relation is whole-to-part, not part-to-whole. A book is made of pages; a wall is made of bricks, so the answer is Bricks.
Source: PYQ-style

28. Thermometer : Temperature :: Barometer : ?
(a) Humidity (b) Pressure (c) Rainfall (d) Wind speed
Answer: (b)
Solution: Humidity is tempting because it is also a weather measure, but it is measured by a hygrometer. A thermometer measures temperature; a barometer measures atmospheric pressure, so the answer is Pressure.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Bird : Nest :: Lion : ?
(a) Cave (b) Hive (c) Burrow (d) Den
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cave is tempting since lions may shelter in caves, but the standard dwelling term for a lion is a den. A bird lives in a nest; a lion lives in a den, so the answer is Den.
Source: PYQ-style

30. Water : Thirst :: Food : ?
(a) Stomach (b) Taste (c) Hunger (d) Energy
Answer: (c)
Solution: Energy is tempting because food gives energy, but the relation is "removes the need". Water removes thirst; food removes hunger, so the answer is Hunger.
Source: PYQ-style

31. Knife : Cut :: Pen : ?
(a) Ink (b) Nib (c) Paper (d) Write
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ink is tempting because a pen contains ink, but the relation is tool-to-function. A knife is used to cut; a pen is used to write, so the answer is Write.

32. Moon : Satellite :: Earth : ?
(a) Star (b) Planet (c) Asteroid (d) Comet
Answer: (b)
Solution: Star is tempting because both shine in the sky, but the Moon is classified as a satellite; the Earth is classified as a planet, so the answer is Planet.

33. Cow : Calf :: Dog : ?
(a) Puppy (b) Cub (c) Kitten (d) Foal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Cub is tempting because many wild animals have cubs, but the young one of a dog is a puppy. A cow's young one is a calf; a dog's young one is a puppy, so the answer is Puppy.
Source: PYQ-style

34. India : Tiger :: USA : ?
(a) Lion (b) Bald Eagle (c) Kangaroo (d) Panda
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lion is tempting as a common national symbol, but the national bird of the USA is the bald eagle. India's national animal is the tiger; the USA's national emblem bird is the bald eagle, so the answer is Bald Eagle.
Source: PYQ-style

35. ACE : GIK :: BDF : ?
(a) IKM (b) HJL (c) GIK (d) HJK
Answer: (b)
Solution: HJK is tempting because it starts correctly, but each letter must shift by +6: A→G, C→I, E→K. Similarly B→H, D→J, F→L, so the answer is HJL.
Source: PYQ-style

36. 8 : 72 :: 9 : ?
(a) 90 (b) 81 (c) 99 (d) 72
Answer: (a)
Solution: 81 is tempting because 9 × 9 = 81, but the rule is n × (n+1): 8 × 9 = 72. So 9 × 10 = 90, and the answer is 90.
Source: PYQ-style

37. 5 : 36 :: 7 : ?
(a) 56 (b) 49 (c) 64 (d) 48
Answer: (c)
Solution: 49 is tempting as 7², but the rule is (n+1)²: (5+1)² = 36. So (7+1)² = 64, and the answer is 64.
Source: PYQ-style

38. Ornithology : Birds :: Entomology : ?
(a) Fish (b) Fossils (c) Insects (d) Snakes
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fish is tempting as a common study subject, but the study of fish is ichthyology. Ornithology is the study of birds; entomology is the study of insects, so the answer is Insects.
Source: PYQ-style

39. Doctor : Stethoscope :: Carpenter : ?
(a) Scalpel (b) Hammer (c) Plough (d) Needle
Answer: (b)
Solution: Scalpel is tempting because it is also a professional tool, but it belongs to a surgeon. A doctor uses a stethoscope; a carpenter uses a hammer, so the answer is Hammer.

40. Flow : River :: Stagnant : ?
(a) Stream (b) Waterfall (c) Canal (d) Pond
Answer: (d)
Solution: Stream is tempting because it contains water, but a stream flows, which breaks the relation. A river is characterised by flow; a pond is characterised by stagnant water, so the answer is Pond.
Source: PYQ-style

41. 12 : 144 :: 15 : ?
(a) 180 (b) 195 (c) 225 (d) 210
Answer: (c)
Solution: 180 is tempting as 12 × 15, but the rule is squaring: 12² = 144. So 15² = 225, and the answer is 225.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Paws : Cat :: Hooves : ?
(a) Horse (b) Snake (c) Dog (d) Bird
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dog is tempting because dogs are common pets, but dogs have paws, not hooves — the relation is foot-type to animal. A cat has paws; a horse has hooves, so the answer is Horse.
Source: PYQ-style

43. Cub : Lion :: Gosling : ?
(a) Goose (b) Hen (c) Duck (d) Swan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Duck is tempting because it is a similar water bird, but a young goose is called a gosling. A cub is the young one of a lion; a gosling is the young one of a goose, so the answer is Goose.
Source: PYQ-style

44. 3 : 27 :: 4 : ?
(a) 16 (b) 48 (c) 81 (d) 64
Answer: (d)
Solution: 16 is tempting as 4², but the rule is cubing: 3³ = 27. So 4³ = 64, and the answer is 64.

45. Farmer : Field :: Teacher : ?
(a) Classroom (b) Book (c) Chalk (d) Student
Answer: (a)
Solution: Student is tempting, but that is the person taught, not the workplace — this is the double-relationship trap. A farmer works in a field; a teacher works in a classroom, so the answer is Classroom.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Melt : Liquid :: Freeze : ?
(a) Cold (b) Gas (c) Vapour (d) Solid
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cold is tempting because freezing involves cold, but cold is a condition, not a state of matter. Melting produces a liquid; freezing produces a solid, so the answer is Solid.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

47. 16 : 56 :: 20 : ?
(a) 60 (b) 100 (c) 70 (d) 80
Answer: (c)
Solution: 80 is tempting as 20 × 4, but the rule is n × 3.5: 16 × 3.5 = 56. So 20 × 3.5 = 70, and the answer is 70.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Curtain : Window :: Lid : ?
(a) Box (b) Door (c) Wall (d) Key
Answer: (a)
Solution: Door is tempting because a curtain and a door both close openings, but the relation is cover-to-object. A curtain covers a window; a lid covers a box, so the answer is Box.
Source: PYQ-style

49. BEGK : DGIM :: ADFJ : ?
(a) CGHL (b) CEHL (c) CFHM (d) CFHL
Answer: (d)
Solution: CEHL is tempting because it looks close, but every letter must shift by +2: B→D, E→G, G→I, K→M. Similarly A→C, D→F, F→H, J→L, so the answer is CFHL.
Source: PYQ-style

50. War : Destruction :: Peace : ?
(a) Silence (b) Treaty (c) Prosperity (d) Army
Answer: (c)
Solution: Treaty is tempting because treaties end wars, but the relation is state-to-result, not state-to-instrument. War results in destruction; peace results in prosperity, so the answer is Prosperity.
