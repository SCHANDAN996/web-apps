101. Doctor : Patient :: Teacher : ?
(a) School (b) Classroom (c) Book (d) Student
Answer: (d)
Solution: A doctor treats a patient; similarly, a teacher teaches a student. School and classroom are places, not the receiver of the action, so the answer is Student.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Bird : Nest :: Lion : ?
(a) Den (b) Cave (c) Forest (d) Burrow
Answer: (a)
Solution: A bird lives in a nest; a lion lives in a den. A cave may shelter many animals but the specific term for a lion's home is den, so the answer is Den.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

103. Water : Thirst :: Food : ?
(a) Hunger (b) Taste (c) Energy (d) Stomach
Answer: (a)
Solution: Water quenches thirst; food removes hunger. Energy is a result, not the direct parallel of thirst, so the answer is Hunger.
