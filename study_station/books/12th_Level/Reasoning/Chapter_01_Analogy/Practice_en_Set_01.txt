Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Doctor : Patient :: Teacher : ?
(a) School (b) Book (c) Student (d) Classroom
Answer: (c)
Solution: A doctor treats a patient; similarly, a teacher teaches a student. School and classroom are places, not the receiver of the action, so the answer is Student.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Bird : Nest :: Lion : ?
(a) Den (b) Hive (c) Burrow (d) Cave
Answer: (a)
Solution: A bird lives in a nest; a lion lives in a den. A cave is a tempting option but the specific term for a lion's home is a den, so the answer is Den.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Book : Pages :: Wall : ?
(a) Cement (b) Bricks (c) Roof (d) Paint
Answer: (b)
Solution: A book is made up of pages; a wall is made up of bricks. Cement is only a binding material, not the unit, so the answer is Bricks.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Water : Thirst :: Food : ?
(a) Hunger (b) Taste (c) Energy (d) Plate
Answer: (a)
Solution: Water removes thirst; food removes hunger. Energy is a result, not the need being satisfied, so the answer is Hunger.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Pen : Write :: Knife : ?
(a) Cut (b) Sharp (c) Blade (d) Steel
Answer: (a)
Solution: A pen is used to write; a knife is used to cut. Sharp and blade describe the knife, not its function, so the answer is Cut.
Source: PYQ-style

6. Moon : Satellite :: Earth : ?
(a) Star (b) Sun (c) Planet (d) Galaxy
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Moon is a satellite; the Earth is a planet. The Earth is not a star, so the answer is Planet.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography

7. Cow : Calf :: Dog : ?
(a) Kitten (b) Cub (c) Puppy (d) Foal
Answer: (c)
Solution: The young one of a cow is a calf; the young one of a dog is a puppy. A kitten is a young cat, so the answer is Puppy.
Source: PYQ-style

8. India : Tiger :: USA : ?
(a) Bald Eagle (b) Lion (c) Kangaroo (d) Panda
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tiger is the national animal of India; the bald eagle is the national bird/animal symbol of the USA. Kangaroo belongs to Australia, so the answer is Bald Eagle.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Eye : See :: Ear : ?
(a) Touch (b) Smell (c) Taste (d) Hear
Answer: (d)
Solution: The eye is the organ of seeing; the ear is the organ of hearing, so the answer is Hear.
Source: PYQ-style

10. Doctor : Stethoscope :: Carpenter : ?
(a) Needle (b) Plough (c) Scalpel (d) Hammer
Answer: (d)
Solution: A stethoscope is the tool of a doctor; a hammer is the tool of a carpenter. A scalpel belongs to a surgeon, so the answer is Hammer.
Source: PYQ-style

11. Hot : Cold :: Day : ?
(a) Night (b) Light (c) Sun (d) Morning
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hot is the opposite of cold; day is the opposite of night. Morning is a part of the day, not its opposite, so the answer is Night.
Source: PYQ-style

12. Army : Soldier :: Navy : ?
(a) Pilot (b) Sailor (c) Farmer (d) Guard
Answer: (b)
Solution: A soldier serves in the army; a sailor serves in the navy. A pilot serves in the air force, so the answer is Sailor.
Source: PYQ-style

13. Flower : Fragrance :: Fire : ?
(a) Smoke (b) Ash (c) Heat (d) Wood
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fragrance is the characteristic property of a flower; heat is the characteristic property of fire. Smoke and ash are by-products, so the answer is Heat.
Source: PYQ-style

14. Mumbai : Maharashtra :: Jaipur : ?
(a) Gujarat (b) Haryana (c) Punjab (d) Rajasthan
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mumbai is the capital of Maharashtra; Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan, so the answer is Rajasthan.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Geography

15. Wheel : Car :: Page : ?
(a) Book (b) Pen (c) Ink (d) Word
Answer: (a)
Solution: A wheel is a part of a car; a page is a part of a book. A word is a part of a page, reversing the order, so the answer is Book.
Source: PYQ-style

16. Fish : Gills :: Man : ?
(a) Heart (b) Lungs (c) Skin (d) Stomach
Answer: (b)
Solution: A fish breathes through gills; a man breathes through lungs. The heart pumps blood but does not breathe, so the answer is Lungs.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science

17. Cricket : Bat :: Tennis : ?
(a) Court (b) Ball (c) Net (d) Racket
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cricket is played with a bat; tennis is played with a racket. Ball and net are common to many games, so the answer is Racket.
Source: PYQ-style

18. Poet : Poem :: Sculptor : ?
(a) Painting (b) Song (c) Statue (d) Novel
Answer: (c)
Solution: A poet creates a poem; a sculptor creates a statue. A painting is made by a painter, so the answer is Statue.
Source: PYQ-style

19. Thermometer : Temperature :: Barometer : ?
(a) Wind speed (b) Humidity (c) Rainfall (d) Atmospheric pressure
Answer: (d)
Solution: A thermometer measures temperature; a barometer measures atmospheric pressure. Humidity is measured by a hygrometer, so the answer is Atmospheric pressure.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science

20. Monday : Tuesday :: January : ?
(a) February (b) Sunday (c) March (d) December
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tuesday comes immediately after Monday; February comes immediately after January. December comes before January, so the answer is February.
Source: PYQ-style

21. Ornithology : Birds :: Entomology : ?
(a) Fishes (b) Snakes (c) Insects (d) Fossils
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ornithology is the study of birds; entomology is the study of insects. The tempting trap is snakes, but the study of snakes is herpetology, so the answer is Insects.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Doctor : Diagnosis :: Judge : ?
(a) Punishment (b) Lawyer (c) Court (d) Verdict
Answer: (d)
Solution: A doctor gives a diagnosis after examining; a judge gives a verdict after hearing a case. Punishment follows the verdict and court is a place, so the answer is Verdict.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Water : Ice :: Milk : ?
(a) Butter (b) Curd (c) Cream (d) Cheese
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ice is obtained by changing the state of water (freezing); curd is obtained by changing the state of milk (fermenting). The trap is butter, which is made from cream, not directly from milk, so the answer is Curd.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Preamble : Constitution :: Preface : ?
(a) Chapter (b) Book (c) Index (d) Cover
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Preamble is the introductory statement of the Constitution; a preface is the introductory statement of a book. The trap is chapter, which is a division inside a book, not what a preface introduces, so the answer is Book.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Virus : Disease :: Exercise : ?
(a) Fatigue (b) Fitness (c) Sweat (d) Pain
Answer: (b)
Solution: A virus causes disease; exercise causes fitness. The trap is fatigue, which is a temporary side effect, not the intended result, so the answer is Fitness.
Source: PYQ-style
