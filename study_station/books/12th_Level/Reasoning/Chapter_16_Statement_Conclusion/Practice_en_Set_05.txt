101. Statements: Some dancers are doctors. No dancer is a singer. All singers are teachers.
Conclusions:
I. Some teachers are not dancers.
II. Some doctors are teachers.
(a) Only conclusion II follows (b) Both I and II follow (c) Only conclusion I follows (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: Draw every Venn diagram that fits the statements: conclusion I is forced in every diagram that fits, while conclusion II can be false. The trap is reading conclusion II as a valid reversal of a statement; so the answer is "Only conclusion I follows".
Source: PYQ-style
