76. Statement: The factory owner announced a Diwali bonus for all the workers.
Conclusions:
I. The factory earned record profits this year.
II. The owner wants to keep the workers happy and motivated.
(a) Only conclusion I follows (b) Neither conclusion I nor II follows (c) Only conclusion II follows (d) Both conclusions I and II follow
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is conclusion I — a bonus does not prove record profits; bonuses are also given out of tradition or to retain workers even in ordinary years. Giving a bonus is a recognised way of keeping workers happy and motivated, so conclusion II is a reasonable direct consequence. Hence only conclusion II follows, which is the correct option.
