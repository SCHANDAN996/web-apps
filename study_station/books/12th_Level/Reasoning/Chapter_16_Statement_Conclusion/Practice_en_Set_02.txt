Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Statements: Some singers are not doctors. Some doctors are teachers.
Conclusions:
I. Some teachers are doctors.
II. No singer is a teacher.
(a) Either I or II follows (b) Only conclusion I follows (c) Only conclusion II follows (d) Both I and II follow
Answer: (b)
Solution: Draw every Venn diagram that fits the statements: conclusion I is forced in every diagram that fits, while conclusion II can be false. The trap is reading conclusion II as a valid reversal of a statement; so the answer is "Only conclusion I follows".
Source: PYQ-style
