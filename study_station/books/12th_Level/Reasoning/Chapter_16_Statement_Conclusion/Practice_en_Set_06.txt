Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Statements: Some doctors are dancers. All doctors are teachers. Some singers are not teachers.
Conclusions:
I. Some dancers are doctors.
II. No dancer is a doctor.
(a) Either I or II follows (b) Only conclusion II follows (c) Only conclusion I follows (d) Both I and II follow
Answer: (c)
Solution: Draw every Venn diagram that fits the statements: conclusion I is forced in every diagram that fits, while conclusion II can be false. The trap is reading conclusion II as a valid reversal of a statement; so the answer is "Only conclusion I follows".
Source: PYQ-style
