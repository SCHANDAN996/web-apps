1. **Use a Clear, Descriptive Subject Line**  
   The subject must summarise the purpose in 5–7 words so the recipient can prioritise without opening.  
   Example: “Leave Application – 12 to 14 Oct – John Doe (Emp ID 4521)”  
   Exception: Internal quick replies in an ongoing thread may keep the original subject with “Re:” prefix.

2. **Address the Recipient Formally Unless Otherwise Established**  
   Start with “Dear Mr./Ms. [Last Name]” or “Dear [Designation]” for first contact or external mail.  
   Example: “Dear Ms. Sharma,”  
   Exception: Established internal colleagues may use first names per organisational culture.

3. **Keep the Message Body Concise and Structured**  
   Use short paragraphs, bullet points for action items, and bold key dates/numbers.  
   Example:  
   - Report due: 20 Oct  
   - Review meeting: 18 Oct, 10:00 AM  
   Exception: Legal, compliance or technical mails may need full narrative; still use headings.

4. **State the Purpose in the First Two Lines**  
   The “BLUF” (Bottom Line Up Front) principle: why you are writing and what you need.  
   Example: “I am writing to request approval for the Q3 marketing budget (₹12 lakh).”  
   Exception: Bad-news or sensitive messages may open with a buffer sentence.

5. **Use Professional Tone — Avoid Slang, Emojis, ALL CAPS**  
   ALL CAPS is perceived as shouting; emojis reduce credibility in formal exams/banking correspondence.  
   Exception: Internal team culture may allow restrained emojis (👍) in quick acknowledgements.

6. **Attach Files Before Composing; Name Them Clearly**  
   Attach first to avoid “forgot attachment” follow-ups. File name: “Q3_Budget_Proposal_v2_20241010.pdf”  
   Exception: Cloud links (OneDrive/SharePoint) preferred for files > 20 MB or collaborative editing.

7. **Limit Recipients — To, Cc, Bcc Discipline**  
   - To: Action required  
   - Cc: Information only, visible to all  
   - Bcc: Mass mail where recipients must not see each other (privacy)  
   Exception: Bcc yourself for record-keeping in sensitive threads.

8. **Reply Within Defined SLA (Service Level Agreement)**  
   Many organisations define internal SLAs (e.g., 24 h external, 4 h urgent internal); consult your department’s policy. Auto-acknowledge if detailed reply needs time.  
   Exception: Leave/out-of-office auto-reply with alternate contact.

9. **Proofread for Grammar, Spelling, Tone, and Attachments**  
   Read aloud; check names, dates, figures, and that every “please find attached” has a file.  
   Exception: Quick mobile replies may use “Sent from phone, please excuse brevity” disclaimer.

10. **Use Official Signature Block**  
    Full name, designation, department, office phone, mobile (optional), official website. No personal quotes.  
    Example:  
    Rajesh Kumar  
    Assistant Section Officer  
    Ministry of Railways | Extn: 2341  
    railways.gov.in  
    Exception: Internal short replies may omit signature if thread already has it.

11. **Encrypt or Password-Protect Sensitive Data**  
    Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, medical info — send password via separate channel (SMS/phone). Use S/MIME or Office 365 Message Encryption where available.  
    Exception: Internal secure network with DLP (Data Loss Prevention) policies may auto-block unencrypted PII.

12. **Do Not Forward Chain Mails, Hoaxes, or Unverified Alerts**  
    Verify with CERT-In advisories (cert-in.org.in) or official vendor bulletins before forwarding security warnings.  
    Exception: Official CERT-In / NIC / RBI circulars forwarded verbatim with reference number.

13. **Manage Mailbox Size — Archive, Delete, Use Rules/Folders**  
    Government mailboxes typically offer 10 GB (NIC) or 50 GB+ (Microsoft 365); verify your organisation’s quota. Auto-archive > 1 year; delete Sent/Deleted items monthly.  
    Exception: Legal/RTI/hold notices require retention — tag “Do Not Delete”.

14. **Log Out / Lock Screen on Shared Computers**  
    Windows: Win + L; Browser: sign out from Outlook Web App / Gmail. Prevents unauthorised access under IT Act Sec. 43/66.  
    Exception: Kiosk mode / thin clients with auto-session timeout enforced by IT policy.

15. **Follow Organisational Email Policy & Record-Keeping Rules**  
    Official emails are public records under RTI Act, 2005. Do not use personal mail (Gmail/Yahoo) for official work.  
    Exception: Emergency communication when official system is down — document and migrate to official ID ASAP.
