76. Which of the following is the full form of NEFT in the context of electronic fund transfer?

80. What does the "M" in MMID stand for in mobile banking?

81. Which protocol ensures secure communication between a web browser and a banking server?
(a) HTTP (b) HTTPS (c) FTP (d) SMTP
Answer: (b)
Solution: Option (a) HTTP transmits data in plain text. HTTPS uses TLS/SSL encryption to protect sensitive banking data. Always check for "https://" and the padlock icon before entering credentials.

82. In the context of debit/credit cards, what does CVV stand for?

Answer: (a)

83. Which of the following is a characteristic of RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)?

Answer: (c)
Solution: Option (a) describes NEFT (batch settlement). RTGS settles individually in real-time, has a minimum of ₹2 lakh (no upper cap), and is available 24×7×365 since December 2020.

84. What is the primary purpose of an OTP (One Time Password) in e-banking?

Answer: (a)
Solution: Option (b) is a common misconception — OTP supplements, not replaces, the static password. It implements "something you have" (phone/email) as the second factor alongside "something you know" (password).

96. A customer receives an SMS: "Your account will be blocked. Click https://bit.ly/xyz to update KYC." What type of attack is this?

Answer: (a)

97. In the context of digital payments, what is a "chargeback"?

99. A UPI user enters the wrong UPI PIN three times consecutively. What happens next?

Answer: (c)

100. Which of the following best describes "Vishing" in banking fraud?
(a) Fraudulent phone calls pretending to be bank officials (b) Fraudulent emails asking for banking credentials (c) Fake websites mimicking bank login pages (d) Stealing card data using a skimming device at ATM
Answer: (a)
Solution: Option (b) is phishing (email), (c) is pharming/spoofed sites, (d) is skimming. Vishing = Voice + Phishing — fraudsters call victims, create urgency ("card blocked", "KYC pending"), and extract OTP, PIN, or CVV. Banks never ask for these on calls.
Source: RBI BEWARE Booklet on Fraud Prevention
