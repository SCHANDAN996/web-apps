51. What does HTTP stand for?
(a) High Speed Text Protocol (b) High Transfer Text Protocol (c) HyperText Transmission Process (d) HyperText Transfer Protocol
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option (b) swaps "Transfer" and "Text" but the correct expansion is HyperText Transfer Protocol, so the answer is HyperText Transfer Protocol.
