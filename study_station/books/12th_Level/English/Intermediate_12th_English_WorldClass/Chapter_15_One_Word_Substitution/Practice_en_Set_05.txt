101. A person who speaks many languages
(a) Linguist (b) Bilingual (c) Polyglot (d) Multilateral
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Linguist" tempts because it relates to language, but a linguist studies language scientifically and need not speak many. "Bilingual" means only two languages. "Polyglot" (poly = many, glot = tongue) means one who speaks many languages, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: NCERT-based vocabulary / PYQ-style

102. A government by the people, for the people
(a) Democracy (b) Autocracy (c) Bureaucracy (d) Theocracy
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Bureaucracy" tempts because it is also a form of government, but it means rule by officials. "Autocracy" is rule by one person; "theocracy" is rule by religious leaders. Rule by the people is Democracy (demos = people, kratos = rule), so the answer is Democracy.
Source: PYQ-style

103. One who does not believe in the existence of God
(a) Agnostic (b) Apostate (c) Ascetic (d) Atheist
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Agnostic" is the classic trap — an agnostic says God's existence cannot be known, while an atheist firmly denies God's existence. "Ascetic" is one who lives without comforts. One who does not believe in God is an Atheist, so the answer is Atheist.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap: atheist vs agnostic)

104. A list of books available in a library
(a) Anthology (b) Glossary (c) Index (d) Catalogue
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Index" tempts, but an index lists topics at the back of one book. "Glossary" explains difficult words; "anthology" is a collection of poems or stories. A list of books in a library is a Catalogue, so the answer is Catalogue.

112. A person who pretends to have more knowledge than he actually has
(a) Hypocrite (b) Charlatan (c) Sycophant (d) Philistine
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Hypocrite" is the trap — a hypocrite pretends to have virtues or beliefs he lacks, not knowledge. "Sycophant" flatters for gain; "philistine" is indifferent to art and culture. One who fakes knowledge or skill is a Charlatan, so the answer is Charlatan.

116. A disease that spreads by contact
(a) Contagious (b) Infectious (c) Chronic (d) Epidemic
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Infectious" is the trap — infectious diseases spread through germs in air, water or food, while contagious diseases spread specifically by physical contact. "Chronic" means long-lasting; "epidemic" means widespread. A disease spreading by contact is Contagious, so the answer is Contagious.
