76. A person who speaks many languages
(a) Multilateral (b) Bilingual (c) Linguist (d) Polyglot
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Linguist" tempts because it relates to language, but it means a scholar of language, not necessarily a speaker of many. "Bilingual" means only two languages. One who speaks many languages is a polyglot, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English vocabulary

77. A government by the people, of the people, for the people
(a) Autocracy (b) Democracy (c) Bureaucracy (d) Theocracy
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Autocracy" is rule by one person and "theocracy" is rule by religious leaders — both are the opposite of people's rule. "Bureaucracy" is rule by officials. Rule by the people is democracy, so the answer is Democracy.
Source: PYQ-style

78. That which cannot be believed
(a) Inevitable (b) Incredible (c) Invisible (d) Invincible
Answer: (b)
Solution: All four options begin with "in-" and look alike, which is the trap. "Invisible" cannot be seen, "invincible" cannot be defeated, "inevitable" cannot be avoided. What cannot be believed is incredible, so the answer is Incredible.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English vocabulary

79. A person who eats human flesh
(a) Herbivore (b) Carnivore (c) Cannibal (d) Omnivore
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Carnivore" tempts because it means flesh-eater, but it refers to animals eating animal flesh, not humans eating humans. A person who eats human flesh is a cannibal, so the answer is Cannibal.
Source: PYQ-style

80. A list of books available in a library
(a) Catalogue (b) Glossary (c) Index (d) Bibliography
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Index" tempts, but it is a list of topics at the end of one book; "bibliography" is a list of books referred to by an author. A library's list of its books is a catalogue, so the answer is Catalogue.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English vocabulary

81. Murder of one's own brother
(a) Fratricide (b) Homicide (c) Patricide (d) Regicide
Answer: (a)
Solution: The "-cide" family confuses many: "homicide" is killing any human, "patricide" is killing one's father, "regicide" is killing a king. Killing one's brother is fratricide, so the answer is Fratricide.
Source: PYQ-style

82. A person who does not believe in the existence of God
(a) Ascetic (b) Agnostic (c) Atheist (d) Apostate
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Agnostic" is the classic trap — an agnostic says God's existence is unknown or unknowable, not that God does not exist. One who denies God's existence is an atheist, so the answer is Atheist.

(a) Cemetery (b) Mortuary (c) Monastery (d) Sanatorium
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Cemetery" tempts, but it is where bodies are buried, not kept temporarily. A place where dead bodies are kept before disposal is a mortuary, so the answer is Mortuary.

(a) Rhetoric (b) Soliloquy (c) Monologue (d) Extempore
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Monologue" tempts, but it only means a long speech by one person and may be fully prepared. An unprepared speech is extempore, so the answer is Extempore.

Answer: (a)
Solution: "Flexible" tempts but means easily bent without breaking — the opposite idea. "Ductile" means able to be drawn into wires. That which breaks easily is fragile, so the answer is Fragile.

Answer: (d)
Solution: "Reptile" tempts because some reptiles swim, but reptiles are land animals by class. "Aquatic" means water-only. An animal living on both land and water is an amphibian, so the answer is Amphibian.

96. Read the sentence: "The new manager was known for his candour; he never hid the truth from his team." The word closest in meaning to the quality described is
(a) Timidity (b) Cunning (c) Frankness (d) Arrogance
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is "cunning", which also involves handling truth but means hiding it cleverly — the opposite of what the sentence shows. "Candour" means open honesty, so the answer is Frankness.
Source: PYQ-style

97. One who does something for the first time; a pioneer in a field
(a) Trailblazer (b) Maverick (c) Veteran (d) Novice
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Novice" tempts because it relates to "first time", but a novice is a beginner, not one who opens a new path for others. A pioneer who leads the way is a trailblazer, so the answer is Trailblazer.
Source: PYQ-style

98. Choose the one word for: "A statement that contradicts itself but may still be true"
(a) Oxymoron (b) Irony (c) Paradox (d) Metaphor
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Oxymoron" is the strong trap — it is a two-word contradiction like "deafening silence", while a paradox is a full statement that seems self-contradictory yet holds truth. The question describes a statement, so the answer is Paradox.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English literature terms

99. The practice of having only one wife or husband at a time
(a) Polygamy (b) Bigamy (c) Polyandry (d) Monogamy
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Bigamy" tempts, but it is the crime of having two spouses at once; "polygamy" means many spouses. One spouse at a time is monogamy, so the answer is Monogamy.
Source: PYQ-style

100. Read the sentence: "His argument was so specious that the audience almost believed it." The word "specious" here means
(a) Apparently true but actually false (b) Genuinely true (c) Completely irrelevant (d) Scientifically proven
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "genuinely true", because the audience "almost believed" it — but "almost" signals it was not true. "Specious" means plausible on the surface yet false, so the answer is Apparently true but actually false.
Source: PYQ-style
