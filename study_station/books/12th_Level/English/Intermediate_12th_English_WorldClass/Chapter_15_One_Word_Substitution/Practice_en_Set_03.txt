51. A person who speaks many languages
(a) Linguist (b) Bilingual (c) Polyglot (d) Multilateral
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bilingual means speaking only two languages, which is the tempting wrong choice; a linguist studies language scientifically. A person who speaks many languages is a polyglot, so the answer is Polyglot.
