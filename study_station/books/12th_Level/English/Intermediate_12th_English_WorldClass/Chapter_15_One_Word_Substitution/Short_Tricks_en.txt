# Short Tricks & Mnemonics

**Short Tricks + Skip Strategy**

One-word substitution is a game of roots and affixes. If you can spot the root, you can often guess the word even if you don't know the answer. The trick is to break the word into parts (root + affix) and match the meaning.

---

### Mnemonic 1: The "Root" of the Matter

| Root | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| -cide | kill | insecticide, homicide, suicide |
| -phile | love | bibliophile, philanthropist |
| -phobe | fear | arachnophobia, claustrophobia |
| -cracy | rule (system) | democracy, bureaucracy |
| -crat | ruler (person) | autocrat, aristocrat |
| -logy | study | biology, geology |
| -graph | write | biography, autograph |
| -mania | madness/craze | kleptomania, megalomania |

**Trick:** -cracy = the *system* of rule; -crat = the *person* who rules. Democracy is the system; a democrat is the person.

---

### Mnemonic 2: People Words — "One who…"

Most exam questions ask for "one who…" words. Group them by the person's action:

| Word | One who… |
|------|----------|
| Optimist | looks at the bright side of things |
| Pessimist | looks at the dark side of things |
| Philanthropist | loves mankind and helps people |
| Misanthropist | hates mankind |
| Bibliophile | loves books |
| Polyglot | knows many languages |
| Orator | makes speeches well |
| Veteran | has long experience of war/service |

**Trick:** Opti- = best (optimist sees the best); Pessi- = worst (pessimist sees the worst).

---

### Mnemonic 3: Killing Words — the "-cide" Family

| Word | Killing of… |
|------|-------------|
| Homicide | a human being |
| Suicide | oneself |
| Fratricide | one's brother |
| Matricide | one's mother |
| Patricide | one's father |
| Infanticide | an infant |
| Regicide | a king |
| Insecticide | insects |

**Trick:** Remember the family tree — *frater* (brother), *mater* (mother), *pater* (father), *rex* (king) + cide (kill).

---

### Mnemonic 4: Fear Words — the "-phobia" Family

| Word | Fear of… |
|------|----------|
| Acrophobia | heights |
| Hydrophobia | water |
| Claustrophobia | closed spaces |
| Xenophobia | foreigners/strangers |
| Arachnophobia | spiders |

**Trick:** *Acro* = top (think acrobat at the top of the tent); *hydro* = water; *claustro* = closed (like a closet).

---

### Skip Strategy

- If the word looks completely unfamiliar, break it into parts and match the root with the meaning given in the question — this works for -cide, -phobia, -phile, -cracy words.
- If you cannot identify any root and none of the options ring a bell, skip and return later — do not guess blindly where negative marking applies.
- Eliminate options whose roots clearly contradict the meaning (e.g., "one who loves books" cannot be a -phobe word).
