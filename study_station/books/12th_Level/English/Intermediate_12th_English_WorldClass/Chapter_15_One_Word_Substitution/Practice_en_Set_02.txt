26. A person who speaks two languages
(a) Polyglot (b) Bilingual (c) Linguist (d) Multilingual
Answer: (b)
Solution: Polyglot tempts because it also relates to languages, but it means one who knows many languages, not exactly two. "Bi" means two, so a speaker of two languages is bilingual, so the answer is Bilingual.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (Vocabulary)

27. One who loves mankind
(a) Misanthrope (b) Humanitarian aid (c) Anthropologist (d) Philanthropist
Answer: (d)
Solution: Misanthrope is the tempting opposite — it means one who hates mankind. "Phil" means love and "anthropos" means man, so the answer is Philanthropist.
Source: PYQ-style

28. A place where dead bodies are kept for identification
(a) Mortuary (b) Cemetery (c) Monastery (d) Sanatorium
Answer: (a)
Solution: Cemetery tempts as it is linked with the dead, but it is a burial ground, not a place for keeping bodies for identification. A mortuary serves exactly that purpose, so the answer is Mortuary.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Government by the people
(a) Autocracy (b) Theocracy (c) Bureaucracy (d) Democracy
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bureaucracy tempts because it is common in exam papers, but it means rule by officials. "Demos" means people and "cracy" means rule, so the answer is Democracy.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Civics

30. A person who does not believe in the existence of God
(a) Agnostic (b) Atheist (c) Ascetic (d) Apostate
Answer: (b)
Solution: Agnostic is the classic trap — an agnostic is unsure whether God exists, while an atheist firmly denies God's existence. The question states clear disbelief, so the answer is Atheist.
Source: PYQ-style

31. That which cannot be read
(a) Illicit (b) Invisible (c) Illegible (d) Illiterate
Answer: (c)
Solution: Illiterate tempts because of similar spelling, but it describes a person who cannot read, not writing that cannot be read. Illegible means unreadable handwriting or text, so the answer is Illegible.
Source: PYQ-style

32. One who eats human flesh
(a) Cannibal (b) Carnivore (c) Herbivore (d) Omnivore
Answer: (a)
Solution: Carnivore tempts as it involves flesh-eating, but it means any flesh-eating animal. Eating human flesh specifically is cannibalism, so the answer is Cannibal.

33. A list of books available in a library
(a) Glossary (b) Catalogue (c) Index (d) Bibliography
Answer: (b)
Solution: Index tempts, but an index is a list of topics at the end of one book. A catalogue lists all books in a collection, so the answer is Catalogue.
Source: PYQ-style

34. Murder of one's own brother
(a) Homicide (b) Regicide (c) Patricide (d) Fratricide
Answer: (d)
Solution: Homicide tempts as the general word for killing a person, but the question specifies a brother. "Frater" means brother, so the answer is Fratricide.

35. A person who can use both hands equally well
(a) Ambivalent (b) Ambiguous (c) Amphibian (d) Ambidextrous
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ambivalent tempts because of the shared prefix "ambi" (both), but it means having mixed feelings. Skill with both hands is ambidexterity, so the answer is Ambidextrous.

36. An animal that lives both on land and in water
(a) Amphibian (b) Aquatic (c) Reptile (d) Mammal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Aquatic tempts, but aquatic animals live only in water. "Amphi" means both, so the answer is Amphibian.

37. A speech delivered without preparation
(a) Soliloquy (b) Extempore (c) Monologue (d) Rhetoric
Answer: (b)
Solution: Monologue tempts as it is also a speech, but it simply means a long speech by one person, prepared or not. An unprepared speech is extempore, so the answer is Extempore.
Source: PYQ-style

38. One who looks at the bright side of things
(a) Pessimist (b) Realist (c) Idealist (d) Optimist
Answer: (d)
Solution: Idealist tempts, but an idealist pursues high ideals, not necessarily a positive outlook. Seeing the bright side defines an optimist, so the answer is Optimist.
Source: PYQ-style

39. A place where birds are kept
(a) Aviary (b) Apiary (c) Aquarium (d) Stable
Answer: (a)
Solution: Apiary is the sound-alike trap — it is a place where bees are kept. "Avis" means bird, so the answer is Aviary.

40. That which cannot be avoided
(a) Inevitable (b) Invincible (c) Incredible (d) Infallible
Answer: (a)
Solution: Invincible tempts due to the shared "in-" prefix, but it means that which cannot be defeated. Unavoidable is inevitable, so the answer is Inevitable.

41. A person who pretends to have more knowledge or skill than he really has
(a) Hypocrite (b) Charlatan (c) Impostor (d) Sycophant
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hypocrite is the tempting option, but a hypocrite pretends to have virtues or beliefs he lacks, not knowledge or skill. A fake expert is a charlatan, so the answer is Charlatan.

42. A word or law no longer in use
(a) Obsolete (b) Ancient (c) Archaic monument (d) Outdated fashion
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ancient tempts, but ancient things may still be in use; obsolete means fallen out of use entirely. So the answer is Obsolete.

43. One who is indifferent to pleasure or pain
(a) Sadist (b) Cynic (c) Stoic (d) Masochist

Solution: Cynic tempts, but a cynic distrusts human motives; indifference to pleasure and pain defines a stoic. So the answer is Stoic.

44. The scientific study of birds
(a) Entomology (b) Zoology (c) Ornithology (d) Anthropology
Answer: (c)
Solution: Zoology tempts as the study of animals generally, but the question asks specifically about birds. "Ornis" means bird, so the answer is Ornithology.

45. A person living at the same time as another
(a) Comrade (b) Colleague (c) Companion (d) Contemporary
Answer: (d)
Solution: Colleague tempts, but a colleague is a co-worker, who need not be of the same age or era. "Con" (together) plus "tempus" (time) gives contemporary, so the answer is Contemporary.
Source: PYQ-style

48. A song sung at a funeral or for the dead
(a) Epic (b) Elegy (c) Ode (d) Ballad
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ode tempts as another poem type, but an ode praises a person or thing. A mournful funeral song is an elegy, so the answer is Elegy.

49. That which can be easily believed
(a) Credible (b) Credulous (c) Incredible (d) Creditable
Answer: (a)
Solution: Credulous is the examiner's favourite trap — it describes a person who believes too easily, not a thing that can be believed. A believable thing is credible, so the answer is Credible.
Source: PYQ-style

50. An office or position with a salary but no work or responsibility
(a) Honorary (b) Voluntary (c) Sinecure (d) Bureaucracy
Answer: (c)
Solution: Honorary tempts, but an honorary post carries no salary, whereas here a salary is paid without work. Such a post is a sinecure, so the answer is Sinecure.
