126. A person who believes that pleasure is the highest good in life is called a —
(a) Stoic (b) Ascetic (c) Hedonist (d) Cynic
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Ascetic" is the tempting wrong choice, but it means the exact opposite — one who gives up pleasure. A "Stoic" endures pain without complaint, and a "Cynic" distrusts human motives. One who pursues pleasure as life's chief goal is a Hedonist, so the answer is Hedonist.

127. A speech delivered without any previous preparation is —
(a) Extempore (b) Soliloquy (c) Rhetoric (d) Monologue
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Soliloquy" tempts many, but it means talking to oneself aloud, not an unprepared speech. "Rhetoric" is the art of persuasive speaking, and a "Monologue" is a long speech by one person. An unprepared speech is Extempore, so the answer is Extempore.

Answer: (d)
Solution: "Entomology" is the classic trap — it is the study of insects, not birds. "Ichthyology" is the study of fish and "Herpetology" of reptiles. The study of birds is Ornithology, so the answer is Ornithology.

(a) Democracy (b) Oligarchy (c) Aristocracy (d) Plutocracy
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Aristocracy" tempts because the nobility are often rich, but it strictly means rule by nobles of birth. "Oligarchy" is rule by a small group, regardless of wealth. Rule by the rich is Plutocracy (Greek "ploutos" = wealth), so the answer is Plutocracy.

Solution: "Agnostic" is the most common trap — an agnostic says God's existence cannot be known, while an atheist positively denies it. A "Pantheist" believes God is in everything. One who denies God's existence is an Atheist, so the answer is Atheist.
