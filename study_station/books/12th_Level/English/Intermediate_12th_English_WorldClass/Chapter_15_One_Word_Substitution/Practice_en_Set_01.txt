1. A person who speaks many languages
(a) Polyglot (b) Bilingual (c) Linguist (d) Multilateral
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Bilingual" tempts because it relates to languages, but it means speaking only two languages. "Linguist" studies language scientifically. A person who speaks many languages is a polyglot, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: NCERT-based vocabulary list

2. One who loves mankind
(a) Philanthropist (b) Misanthrope (c) Optimist (d) Pessimist
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Misanthrope" is the tempting opposite — it means one who hates mankind. "Phil" means love and "anthropos" means man, so the answer is Philanthropist.
Source: Greek roots — phil (love), anthropos (man)

3. A place where dead bodies are kept for identification
(a) Cemetery (b) Mortuary (c) Monastery (d) Sanatorium
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Cemetery" tempts because it deals with the dead, but it is a burial ground, not a place for keeping bodies for identification. That place is a mortuary, so the answer is Mortuary.
Source: PYQ-style

4. One who believes in fate
(a) Fanatic (b) Fatalist (c) Feminist (d) Fundamentalist
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Fanatic" tempts because it sounds similar, but it means a person with extreme, unreasonable enthusiasm. Belief in fate makes one a fatalist, so the answer is Fatalist.
Source: PYQ-style

5. A government by the people
(a) Democracy (b) Autocracy (c) Bureaucracy (d) Monarchy
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Bureaucracy" tempts because it is also a form of government, but it means rule by officials. "Demos" means people and "cracy" means rule, so the answer is Democracy.
Source: Greek roots — demos (people), kratia (rule)

6. One who cannot read or write
(a) Literate (b) Scholar (c) Illiterate (d) Calligrapher
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Scholar" tempts as an opposite-sounding word, but it means a learned person. One unable to read or write is illiterate, so the answer is Illiterate.

7. A list of books available in a library
(a) Catalogue (b) Glossary (c) Index (d) Anthology
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Index" tempts because it is also a list, but it appears at the end of a single book. A list of all books in a collection is a catalogue, so the answer is Catalogue.

8. One who looks at the bright side of things
(a) Pessimist (b) Optimist (c) Idealist (d) Realist
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Idealist" tempts because it sounds positive, but it means one who follows high ideals, not necessarily a positive outlook. One who sees the bright side is an optimist, so the answer is Optimist.
Source: Latin root — optimus (best)

9. Murder of one's own brother
(a) Homicide (b) Patricide (c) Fratricide (d) Regicide
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Homicide" tempts as the general word for killing a person, but the specific killing of a brother is fratricide (Latin "frater" = brother), so the answer is Fratricide.
Source: Latin roots — frater (brother), caedere (to kill)

10. A place where birds are kept
(a) Aviary (b) Apiary (c) Aquarium (d) Stable
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Apiary" tempts because it sounds similar, but it is a place where bees are kept. Birds are kept in an aviary (Latin "avis" = bird), so the answer is Aviary.
Source: Latin root — avis (bird)

11. One who does not believe in the existence of God
(a) Agnostic (b) Ascetic (c) Atheist (d) Apostate
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Agnostic" is the classic examiner's trap — an agnostic is unsure whether God exists, while an atheist firmly denies God's existence. The question states a clear disbelief, so the answer is Atheist.

21. The scientific study of birds
(a) Entomology (b) Zoology (c) Ornithology (d) Anthropology
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Entomology" tempts because it is also a branch of zoology, but it is the study of insects. The study of birds is ornithology (Greek "ornis" = bird), so the answer is Ornithology.
Source: Greek root — ornis (bird)

22. A person who collects stamps
(a) Archaeologist (b) Numismatist (c) Cartographer (d) Philatelist
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Numismatist" is the tempting wrong choice — a numismatist collects coins, not stamps. A stamp collector is a philatelist, so the answer is Philatelist.
Source: Greek roots — philos (loving), ateles (tax-free stamp)

23. A song sung at a funeral or for the dead
(a) Ode (b) Sonnet (c) Elegy (d) Ballad
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Ode" tempts because it is also a poem of deep feeling, but it is written in praise of someone or something. A mournful song for the dead is an elegy, so the answer is Elegy.
Source: PYQ-style

24. One who knows everything
(a) Omnipotent (b) Omniscient (c) Omnipresent (d) Optimist
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Omnipotent" tempts because it shares the prefix "omni-", but it means all-powerful, not all-knowing. "Sciens" means knowing, so the answer is Omniscient.
Source: Latin roots — omnis (all), scire (to know)

Answer: (d)
Solution: "Infectious" is the subtle trap — it spreads through germs in air, water, or food, not necessarily by direct contact. A disease spreading by contact is contagious, so the answer is Contagious.
