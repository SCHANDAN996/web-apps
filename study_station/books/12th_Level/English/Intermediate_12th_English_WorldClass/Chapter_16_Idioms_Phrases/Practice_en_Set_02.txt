26. The idiom "to bite the dust" means:
(a) to eat in a hurry (b) to work very hard (c) to clean the floor (d) to suffer defeat or failure
Answer: (d)
Solution: "To bite the dust" pictures someone falling face-down in defeat, so it means to fail or be defeated. Option (a) is a literal misreading, a classic trap. The answer is to suffer defeat or failure.
