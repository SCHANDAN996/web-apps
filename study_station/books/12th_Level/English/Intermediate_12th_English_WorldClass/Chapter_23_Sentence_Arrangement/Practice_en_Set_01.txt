1. Arrange the parts P, Q, R, S to form a meaningful sentence:
P: to the market
Q: every Sunday
R: my grandmother goes
S: to buy fresh vegetables
(a) QPRS (b) PQRS (c) RQSP (d) RQPS
Answer: (d)
Solution: The subject "my grandmother goes" (Part R) must come first, followed by the time phrase "every Sunday" (Part Q), then the place "to the market" (Part P), and finally the purpose "to buy fresh vegetables" (Part S). The trap is putting the purpose before the place, which makes "to buy fresh vegetables to the market" read awkwardly. The correct order is RQPS.
Source: PYQ-style
