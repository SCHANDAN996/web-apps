51. Arrange the parts to form a meaningful sentence.
P: to succeed in life
Q: hard work
R: is essential
S: for everyone
(a) Q R P S (b) P Q R S (c) S P Q R (d) R S P Q
Answer: (a)
Solution: The subject "hard work" must come first, followed by the verb phrase "is essential", then the infinitive "to succeed in life", and finally "for everyone". Starting with the infinitive leaves the sentence without a subject, which is the trap in the tempting wrong option, so the answer is Q R P S.
