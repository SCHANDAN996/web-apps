101. Arrange the parts P, Q, R, S to form a meaningful sentence:
P: to succeed in life
Q: one must work hard
R: and remain honest
S: in all circumstances
(a) RQPS (b) PSQR (c) QRPS (d) QPRS
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option QRPS puts the purpose phrase "to succeed in life" after "remain honest", which wrongly makes honesty the means to success rather than a parallel duty. The subject clause "one must work hard" opens the sentence, the purpose phrase follows, the second duty "and remain honest" comes next, and "in all circumstances" closes it. So the answer is QPRS.
Source: PYQ-style

R: but also
S: a talented painter
(a) PQSR (b) QPRS (c) PQRS (d) PRQS
Answer: (c)
Solution: Option QPRS breaks the correlative pair by pushing "not only" after the clause, producing the broken sequence "he is a good singer not only but also a talented painter". The fixed structure is "not only … but also …", so "not only" must come first, then the clause "he is a good singer", then "but also", then "a talented painter". So the answer is PQRS.
