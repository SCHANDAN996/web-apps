76. Rearrange the parts to form a meaningful sentence.
P: to succeed in life
Q: one must work hard
R: it is often said that
S: and remain honest
(a) PRQS (b) RPSQ (c) QRPS (d) RQPS
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice starts with "one must work hard" directly, but the sentence needs an opening clause first. "It is often said that" (R) naturally opens the sentence, followed by the advice "one must work hard" (Q), then the purpose "to succeed in life" (P), and the addition "and remain honest" (S). So the answer is RQPS.

77. Rearrange the parts to form a meaningful sentence.
P: the teacher asked the students
Q: why they had not
R: completed their homework
S: on time
(a) QPRS (b) PQRS (c) PRQS (d) RQPS
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is placing the question word "why" first, which breaks the reporting structure. The subject-verb "the teacher asked the students" (P) must come first, then the reported question "why they had not" (Q), then "completed their homework" (R), and finally the time phrase "on time" (S). So the answer is PQRS.
