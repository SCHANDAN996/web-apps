26. Rearrange the parts P, Q, R, S to make a meaningful sentence:
P: to succeed in life
Q: one must work hard
R: and remain honest
S: in all circumstances
(a) PQRS (b) QPRS (c) RQPS (d) SQPR
Answer: (b)
Solution: The sentence must begin with the subject clause "one must work hard" (Q), followed by the purpose "to succeed in life" (P), then "and remain honest" (R), ending with "in all circumstances" (S). Starting with P, as the tempting first option's reverse suggests, leaves the sentence without a subject. The correct order is QPRS, so the answer is QPRS.
