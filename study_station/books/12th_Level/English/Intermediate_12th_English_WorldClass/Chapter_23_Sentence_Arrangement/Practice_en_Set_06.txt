126. Arrange the parts to form a meaningful sentence.
P: in competitive exams
Q: consistent practice
R: is far more important
S: than last-minute cramming
(a) P Q R S (b) S P Q R (c) R S P Q (d) Q R S P
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting choice P Q R S starts with the bare phrase "in competitive exams", leaving "consistent practice" stranded without a verb link and producing a broken opening. The subject is "consistent practice" (Q), followed by the verb phrase "is far more important" (R), the comparison "than last-minute cramming" (S), and the closing phrase "in competitive exams" (P), so the answer is Q R S P.

127. Arrange the parts to form a meaningful sentence.
P: the old bridge
Q: which was built in 1950
R: was declared unsafe
S: after the floods
(a) Q P R S (b) P R Q S (c) R S P Q (d) P Q R S
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is P R Q S, which separates the relative clause "which was built in 1950" from its antecedent "the old bridge" and leaves it dangling after the verb. The relative clause must sit immediately after the noun it describes, then "was declared unsafe" (R) and "after the floods" (S) complete the predicate, so the answer is P Q R S.
