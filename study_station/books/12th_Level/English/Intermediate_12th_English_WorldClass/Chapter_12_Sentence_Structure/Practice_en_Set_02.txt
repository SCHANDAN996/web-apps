26. Choose the correctly structured sentence.
(a) What you are doing now? (b) What are you doing now? (c) What doing you are now? (d) What now you are doing?
Answer: (b)
Solution: In questions with a Wh-word, the auxiliary verb comes before the subject (inversion). "What you are doing" follows statement order, which is the common Hindi-speaker trap, so the answer is "What are you doing now?"

27. Identify the simple sentence.

Answer: (c)
Solution: A simple sentence has only one subject and one finite verb with no conjunction joining clauses. Options with "and" or "although" or "that" contain two clauses, so the answer is "He finished his homework."
