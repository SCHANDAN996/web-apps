Passage 1 (Questions 126–130): Artificial intelligence systems now screen job applications in many large companies. Supporters argue that algorithms remove human prejudice from hiring. Critics counter that algorithms learn from historical data, and if past hiring favoured certain groups, the machine simply automates the old bias at greater speed and scale. The question, therefore, is not whether machines are biased, but whether we audit them as rigorously as we claim to audit ourselves.

126. What is the central concern of the passage?
(a) Machines work faster than humans (b) AI may reproduce human bias hidden in past data (c) Companies should stop using computers (d) Historical data is always accurate
उत्तर: (b)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प गति वाली बात है, जिसका उल्लेख गद्यांश में केवल यह बताने के लिए है कि पूर्वाग्रह और बदतर होता है, मुख्य विचार के रूप में नहीं। लेखक की मूल चिंता यह है कि पूर्वाग्रहयुक्त इतिहास पर प्रशिक्षित एल्गोरिदम उस पूर्वाग्रह को स्वचालित कर देते हैं, इसलिए उत्तर है कि AI पुराने डेटा में छिपे मानवीय पूर्वाग्रह को दोहरा सकता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

Passage 2 (Questions 131–135): Cities are often several degrees warmer than the countryside around them, a phenomenon called the urban heat island effect. Concrete and asphalt absorb sunlight by day and release heat by night, while the loss of trees removes nature's cooling system — shade and evaporation. Planting trees and painting roofs white are cheap remedies, yet they remain rare because their benefits arrive slowly, while election cycles are short.

131. The urban heat island effect refers to:
(a) cities being warmer than surrounding rural areas (b) islands becoming cities (c) heat waves on tropical islands (d) cooling of cities at night
उत्तर: (a)
हल: गद्यांश इसे पहले ही वाक्य में परिभाषित करता है; tropical-island वाला विकल्प रूपक को गलत समझता है। उत्तर है कि शहर आसपास के ग्रामीण क्षेत्रों से अधिक गर्म होते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

132. Why do concrete and asphalt raise city temperatures?
(a) They reflect all sunlight (b) They block the wind (c) They store heat by day and release it at night (d) They are dark in colour only
उत्तर: (c)
हल: परावर्तन (reflection) उसके विपरीत है जो गद्यांश इन सतहों के बारे में कहता है। उत्तर है कि वे दिन में गर्मी संग्रहीत करते हैं और रात में छोड़ते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

133. Trees cool cities through:

उत्तर: (a)

स्रोत: PYQ-style

134. According to the author, cheap remedies are rarely adopted because:

स्रोत: PYQ-style

Passage 3 (Questions 136–140): Economists often celebrate rising GDP, yet a country can grow richer on paper while its rivers die and its citizens work longer hours for the same pay. GDP counts the money spent cleaning an oil spill as a gain, never subtracting the spill itself. Some nations now publish "well-being budgets" that measure health, education and environment alongside output — an admission that what we choose to count shapes what we choose to care about.

136. The author uses the oil spill example to show that:
(a) oil spills are profitable (b) cleaning companies pay high taxes (c) GDP can register destruction as economic gain (d) GDP measures the environment well
उत्तर: (c)
हल: गद्यांश कभी नहीं कहता कि तेल रिसाव कुल मिलाकर लाभदायक हैं; मुद्दा यह है कि सफ़ाई पर खर्च GDP को बढ़ाता है जबकि नुकसान कभी घटाया नहीं जाता। उत्तर है कि GDP विनाश को आर्थिक लाभ के रूप में दर्ज कर सकती है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

137. "Well-being budgets" measure:
(a) only industrial output (b) only government revenue (c) health, education and environment along with output (d) population growth
उत्तर: (c)
हल: गद्यांश तीन अतिरिक्त मापों को स्पष्ट रूप से सूचीबद्ध करता है; केवल-output मापना तो GDP पहले से करती है। उत्तर है health, education and environment along with output।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

138. The sentence "what we choose to count shapes what we choose to care about" implies that:

स्रोत: PYQ-style

स्रोत: PYQ-style

स्रोत: PYQ-style

Passage 4 (Questions 144–150): Sleep, scientists say, is when the brain files the day's memories and washes out metabolic waste. Students who sacrifice sleep to cram often perform worse than those who study less but sleep well. Yet all-night study persists because its cost — foggy thinking — is invisible in the moment, while its benefit, one more chapter read, feels concrete and countable.

(a) organising memories and clearing waste (b) storing fat (c) stopping all brain activity (d) increasing study hours
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Stopping all brain activity" गद्यांश का खंडन करता है, जो नींद के दौरान सक्रिय रखरखाव का काम बताता है। उत्तर है organising memories and clearing waste।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

स्रोत: PYQ-style
