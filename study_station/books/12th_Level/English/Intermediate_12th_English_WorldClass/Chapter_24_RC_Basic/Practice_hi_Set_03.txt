Passage 1 (Questions 51–55):
Ravi had never liked mornings. Every day his alarm rang at six, and every day he pressed the snooze button. But on the day of his first job interview, something changed. He woke up before the alarm, ironed his shirt twice, and reached the bus stop twenty minutes early. "Today," he told himself, "I will not let laziness decide my future." The bus was crowded, but Ravi did not mind. He spent the journey reading his notes and smiling at the thought of a new beginning.

51. What did Ravi usually do when his alarm rang?
(a) He woke up immediately (b) He called his friend (c) He switched off the alarm forever (d) He pressed the snooze button
उत्तर: (d)
हल: गद्यांश में कहा गया है "every day he pressed the snooze button," जो सीधा तथ्य है। विकल्प (a) जाल है क्योंकि वह इंटरव्यू वाले दिन का वर्णन करता है, उसकी सामान्य आदत का नहीं। उत्तर (d) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

52. Why did Ravi wake up before the alarm on that particular day?
(a) The alarm was broken (b) He wanted to catch an early train (c) He had a job interview (d) His mother woke him up
उत्तर: (c)
हल: गद्यांश स्पष्ट कहता है कि वह "the day of his first job interview" था। विकल्प (a) जाल है; कहीं नहीं कहा गया कि अलार्म खराब था — वह बस उससे पहले जाग गया। उत्तर (c) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

उत्तर: (a)
हल: गद्यांश में कहा गया है कि उसने "ironed his shirt twice"। यह सरल स्मरण-प्रश्न है; बाकी संख्याएँ भ्रमित करने वाली हैं। उत्तर (a) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

54. How early did Ravi reach the bus stop?

स्रोत: PYQ-style

Passage 2 (Questions 56–60):
The honeybee is one of nature's hardest workers. A single bee visits about fifty flowers in one trip. Bees collect nectar, which they turn into honey, and pollen, which feeds their young. While collecting food, bees also carry pollen from flower to flower. This process, called pollination, helps plants produce fruits and seeds. Without bees, many of the crops humans depend on would fail.

56. How many flowers does a single bee visit in one trip?
(a) About five (b) About fifteen (c) About five hundred (d) About fifty
उत्तर: (d)
हल: गद्यांश कहता है "about fifty flowers in one trip"। अन्य संख्याएँ भ्रमित करने वाली हैं। उत्तर (d) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

57. What do bees turn nectar into?
(a) Honey (b) Pollen (c) Wax (d) Water
उत्तर: (a)
हल: गद्यांश बताता है कि मधुमक्खियाँ "collect nectar, which they turn into honey"। विकल्प (b) जाल है क्योंकि pollen पास में ही उल्लिखित है, पर pollen बच्चों का भोजन है — वह nectar से नहीं बनता। उत्तर (a) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

58. What is pollination?
(a) Making honey from nectar (b) Carrying pollen from flower to flower (c) Building a beehive (d) Feeding young bees
उत्तर: (b)
हल: गद्यांश pollination को "pollen from flower to flower" ले जाने के रूप में परिभाषित करता है। विकल्प (a) जाल है क्योंकि शहद बनाना गद्यांश में पहले वर्णित है, पर वह अलग प्रक्रिया है। उत्तर (b) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

59. According to the passage, what would happen without bees?
(a) Flowers would grow faster (b) Honey would become cheaper (c) Many crops would fail (d) Plants would need less water
उत्तर: (c)
हल: अंतिम पंक्ति कहती है "many of the crops humans depend on would fail"। अन्य विकल्प गद्यांश से समर्थित नहीं हैं। उत्तर (c) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

60. What does pollen do for bees?
(a) It protects them from rain (b) It builds their hive (c) It helps them fly (d) It feeds their young
उत्तर: (d)
हल: गद्यांश कहता है कि pollen "feeds their young"। विकल्प (b) जाल है; गद्यांश में छत्ते का उल्लेख ही नहीं है। उत्तर (d) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

Passage 3 (Questions 61–65):
Meena's grandmother never threw away anything that could be reused. Old sarees became quilts, glass jars became spice containers, and vegetable peels went into a compost pit behind the house. Meena used to think this was just her grandmother's habit of saving money. But when she studied environmental science in college, she realised her grandmother had been practising "zero waste" long before the term became fashionable. "We called it common sense," her grandmother laughed, "not a movement."

61. What did Meena's grandmother make from old sarees?
(a) Curtains (b) Carpets (c) Bags (d) Quilts
उत्तर: (d)
हल: गद्यांश कहता है "Old sarees became quilts"। अन्य वस्तुओं का उल्लेख नहीं है। उत्तर (d) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

62. Where did the vegetable peels go?
(a) Into the dustbin (b) Into the garden pond (c) Into a compost pit (d) To the neighbour's farm
उत्तर: (c)
हल: गद्यांश बताता है कि छिलके "went into a compost pit behind the house"। विकल्प (a) जाल है क्योंकि फेंक देना आम आदत है, पर दादी माँ पुन: उपयोग योग्य चीज़ें कभी नहीं फेंकती थीं। उत्तर (c) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

63. What did Meena initially think about her grandmother's habits?
(a) That she was being fashionable (b) That she was following a movement (c) That she was saving money (d) That she was wasting time
उत्तर: (c)
हल: मीना "used to think this was just her grandmother's habit of saving money"। विकल्प (b) जाल है क्योंकि "movement" अंत में आता है, पर वह वह है जिसे दादी माँ ने नकारा, न कि जो मीना ने शुरू में सोचा। उत्तर (c) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

64. What subject did Meena study in college?

स्रोत: PYQ-style

65. What did the grandmother call her way of living?

स्रोत: PYQ-style

Passage 4 (Questions 66–70):
Every year, thousands of migratory birds fly to the wetlands of northern India to escape the harsh winters of Siberia and Central Asia. These birds travel thousands of kilometres, guided by the sun, the stars, and the Earth's magnetic field. Scientists are amazed that young birds, making the journey for the first time, can find the same wetlands their parents visited. However, shrinking wetlands and pollution now threaten these ancient travellers, and their numbers are falling.

66. Why do migratory birds fly to northern India?
(a) To find mates (b) To escape harsh winters (c) To learn flying (d) To avoid hunters
उत्तर: (b)
हल: गद्यांश कहता है कि वे "to escape the harsh winters of Siberia and Central Asia" आते हैं। अन्य कारणों का उल्लेख नहीं है। उत्तर (b) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

67. Which of these is NOT mentioned as something that guides the birds?
(a) The sun (b) The smell of wetlands (c) The Earth's magnetic field (d) The stars
उत्तर: (b)
हल: गद्यांश सूर्य, तारों और पृथ्वी के चुंबकीय क्षेत्र को मार्गदर्शक के रूप में सूचीबद्ध करता है। गंध का कहीं उल्लेख नहीं है, इसलिए उत्तर (b) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

68. What amazes the scientists?
(a) The speed of the birds (b) Young birds finding the same wetlands on their first journey (c) The size of the wetlands (d) The number of birds in Siberia
उत्तर: (b)
हल: गद्यांश कहता है कि वैज्ञानिक इस बात से चकित हैं कि "young birds, making the journey for the first time, can find the same wetlands their parents visited"। विकल्प (a) जाल है; गति की कभी चर्चा नहीं हुई। उत्तर (b) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

69. What threatens the migratory birds?
(a) Shrinking wetlands and pollution (b) Too many scientists (c) Cold weather in India (d) Lack of stars in the sky
उत्तर: (a)
हल: अंतिम वाक्य "shrinking wetlands and pollution" को खतरा बताता है। विकल्प (c) जाल है क्योंकि सर्दी का उल्लेख है, पर वह पक्षियों के भारत आने का कारण है, वहाँ खतरा नहीं। उत्तर (a) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

70. What is happening to the number of these birds?
(a) It is rising (b) It doubles every year (c) It is unchanged (d) It is falling

हल: गद्यांश "their numbers are falling" पर समाप्त होता है। अन्य विकल्प गद्यांश का खंडन करते हैं। उत्तर (d) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

Passage 5 (Questions 71–75):
When Arjun opened his small tea stall near the railway station, people laughed at him. "Who will buy tea from a boy?" they said. Arjun did not argue. He simply made sure every cup was hot, every glass was clean, and every customer was greeted with a smile. Within a year, office workers began stopping at his stall every morning. Within three years, he had hired two helpers and opened a second stall. The people who had laughed now asked him for business advice. Arjun always gave the same reply: "Work quietly and let your results make the noise."

71. Where did Arjun open his tea stall?
(a) Near a bus stand (b) Inside an office (c) Near the railway station (d) Near a school
उत्तर: (c)
हल: गद्यांश कहता है कि ठेला "near the railway station" था। विकल्प (a) जाल है क्योंकि बस स्टैंड और रेलवे स्टेशन मिलती-जुलती जगहें हैं, पर केवल रेलवे स्टेशन का उल्लेख है। उत्तर (c) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

72. How did Arjun respond when people laughed at him?
(a) He did not argue and focused on his work (b) He closed the stall (c) He argued with them (d) He complained to the police
उत्तर: (a)
हल: गद्यांश कहता है "Arjun did not argue" और उसने गुणवत्ता व सेवा सुनिश्चित की। विकल्प (c) गद्यांश के कथन का सीधा विपरीत है। उत्तर (a) है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

73. How long did it take Arjun to open a second stall?
(a) One year (b) Three years (c) Two years (d) Five years

स्रोत: PYQ-style

(a) They apologised publicly (b) They stopped drinking tea (c) They opened their own tea stalls (d) They asked him for business advice

स्रोत: PYQ-style

75. What is the main message of Arjun's reply at the end?

उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Work quietly and let your results make the noise" का अर्थ है कि परिणाम शब्दों से ज़ोर से बोलते हैं। विकल्प (d) जाल है — यह "noise" शब्द को शाब्दिक रूप से तोड़ता है, जबकि अर्जुन इसे परिणामों के लिए रूपक के रूप में प्रयोग करता है। उत्तर (b) है।
