# 🪄 Short Tricks + Skip Strategy — Reading Comprehension (Basic)

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic Boxes

**Box 1 — The Detective's Order: "S-Q-P-A"**
> **S**kim the passage → **Q**uestions first (read them) → **P**assage again (hunt) → **A**nswer with proof.
> Trick: *"Smart Queens Prefer Answers"* — never answer from memory, always from the passage.

**Box 2 — Fact vs Inference: "F-I-N-D"**
> **F**act = **F**ound in the text (15 sec) | **I**nference = **I**mplied, not written (45 sec) | **N**ever guess beyond the passage | **D**etective needs evidence.

**Box 3 — Tone Words: "P-N-C"**
> **P**ositive (optimistic, appreciative, hopeful) | **N**egative (critical, pessimistic, sarcastic) | **C**entre/Neutral (objective, analytical, factual).
> Trick: *"Every passage has a mood — find it before the questions find you."*

**Box 4 — Extreme Words = Red Flag: "A-N-E-O"**
> **A**ll, **N**ever, **E**very, **O**nly — options with these words are usually WRONG unless the passage says exactly that.
> Trick: *"Extremes are examiners' traps."*

**Box 5 — Main Idea Hunt: "F-L-M"**
> **F**irst paragraph + **L**ast paragraph + **M**iddle lines of each paragraph (first sentence).
> The main idea hides in these three zones 90% of the time.

**Box 6 — Vocabulary-in-Context: "R-E-A-D"**
> **R**ead the sentence before | **E**xamine the sentence after | **A**ssume a meaning | **D**ouble-check by substituting the option.
> Trick: *"The sentence around the word is its dictionary."*

**Box 7 — Synonym Trap: "Same Word ≠ Same Meaning"**
> If an option repeats the exact word from the passage, be suspicious — examiners love "word-match traps." The correct answer usually **paraphrases**, not copies.

**Box 8 — Title Selection: "B-N-S"**
> A good title is **B**road enough to cover the whole passage, **N**ot too narrow (one paragraph only), **S**pecific enough to fit this passage (not any passage).

**Box 9 — True/False Questions: "E-L-I-M"**
> **E**liminate options one by one | **L**ocate each in the passage | **I**f found and matching → cut it | **M**ark the one that survives.

**Box 10 — Inference Formula: "P + C = I"**
> **P**assage fact + **C**ommon logic = **I**nference.
> If you need outside knowledge to reach an answer, it's NOT a valid inference.

**Box 11 — Author's Purpose: "I-P-E-D"**
> **I**nform | **P**ersuade | **E**ntertain | **D**escribe.
> Ask: "Why did the author write this?" — not "What did the author write?"

**Box 12 — Negative Questions: Circle the NOT**
> The moment you see "EXCEPT / NOT / LEAST," circle it physically. Half the errors in RC come from forgetting the negative word.

**Box 13 — Two-Pass Rule: "Easy First, Hard Later"**
> Pass 1: all fact-based questions (15 sec each). Pass 2: inference/tone questions (45 sec each). Never let one hard question eat three easy ones.

**Box 14 — The 80% Rule for Options**
> An option that is 80% right and 20% wrong is 100% WRONG. One wrong word ("always," "only," "completely") kills the whole option.

**Box 15 — Skimming Speed Trick: "Eyes on Anchors"**
> While skimming, let your eyes catch only: names, numbers, dates, contrast words (but, however, although), and conclusion words (thus, therefore). These are the anchors where questions are born.

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy

**Skip Tip 1 — The 60-Second Bailout:**
If you have read a question twice and located the relevant lines but still can't decide between two options, mark your best guess, flag it, and MOVE ON. Return only if time remains. One stubborn question can cost you three sure-shot ones.

**Skip Tip 2 — Skip "Roman Numeral" Questions for Last:**
Questions like "Which of the following statements are correct? (I), (II), (III)" demand checking every statement against the passage. They take 2–3× longer. Do all direct questions first; attempt Roman-numeral ones in the final minutes.

**Skip Tip 3 — Never Skip Vocabulary Questions:**
Synonym/antonym-in-context questions are the fastest marks in RC (10–15 seconds). Even if you skip the whole passage, attempt these — the answer is always verifiable from one or two surrounding sentences.

## ⚡ The 15/45-Second Rule (Quick Reference)

| Question Type | Time | Signal Words |
|---|---|---|
| Fact / Detail | 15 sec | "According to the passage…", "The author mentions…" |
| Vocabulary in context | 15 sec | "The word 'X' means…" |
| Inference | 45 sec | "It can be inferred…", "The author implies…" |
| Tone / Attitude | 45 sec | "The tone of the passage is…" |
| Main Idea / Title | 45 sec | "The best title…", "The passage is mainly about…" |

> 🏆 **Golden Rule:** The passage is the only truth. Your opinion, your knowledge, your logic — all stay outside the exam hall.
