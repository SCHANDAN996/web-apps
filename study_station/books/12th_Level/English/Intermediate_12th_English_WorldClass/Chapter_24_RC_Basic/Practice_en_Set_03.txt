Passage 1 (Questions 51–55):
Ravi had never liked mornings. Every day his alarm rang at six, and every day he pressed the snooze button. But on the day of his first job interview, something changed. He woke up before the alarm, ironed his shirt twice, and reached the bus stop twenty minutes early. "Today," he told himself, "I will not let laziness decide my future." The bus was crowded, but Ravi did not mind. He spent the journey reading his notes and smiling at the thought of a new beginning.

Solution: The passage says "every day he pressed the snooze button," which is a direct fact. Option (a) is the trap because it describes the interview day, not his usual habit. The answer is (d).
