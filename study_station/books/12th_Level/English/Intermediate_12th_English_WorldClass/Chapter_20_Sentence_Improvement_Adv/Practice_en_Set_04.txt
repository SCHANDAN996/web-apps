76. Choose the option that best improves the underlined part: Scarcely he had reached the station when the train left.
(a) He had scarcely reached (b) Scarcely he reached (c) Scarcely he has reached (d) Scarcely had he reached
Answer: (d)
Solution: When a negative adverb like "scarcely" begins a sentence, the auxiliary must come before the subject (inversion). The tempting choice drops the inversion because it sounds natural in ordinary speech, but grammar demands it here, so the answer is "Scarcely had he reached".

77. Choose the option that best improves the underlined part: No sooner did she finish her speech then the audience started clapping.
(a) when the audience started (b) No improvement (c) then the audience had started (d) than the audience started
Answer: (d)
Solution: "No sooner" always pairs with "than", never with the sound-alike "then" — that confusion is the trap. The correct pairing is "No sooner... than", so the answer is "than the audience started".
