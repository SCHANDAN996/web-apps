1. सही सुधार चुनें: "He is working here since 2015."
(a) He had worked here since 2015. (b) He was working here since 2015. (c) He has been working here since 2015. (d) He works here since 2015.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Since 2015" के साथ अभी तक जारी क्रिया के लिए present perfect continuous चाहिए, simple present नहीं। हिंदी-प्रभावित बोलचाल में simple-present वाला रूप स्वाभाविक लगता है, पर अंग्रेज़ी में गलत है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He has been working here since 2015."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

2. सही सुधार चुनें: "One of my friend has gone to Delhi."
(a) One of my friends have gone to Delhi. (b) One of my friend have gone to Delhi. (c) One of my friends has gone to Delhi. (d) One of my friend is going to Delhi.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "One of" के बाद संज्ञा बहुवचन (friends) होनी चाहिए, पर क्रिया एकवचन (has) रहती है। विद्यार्थी अक्सर बहुवचन संज्ञा देखकर क्रिया को "have" कर देते हैं — यही जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "One of my friends has gone to Delhi."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

3. सही सुधार चुनें: "She is more taller than her sister."
(a) She is much more taller than her sister. (b) She is most taller than her sister. (c) She is taller than her sister. (d) She is more tall than her sister.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Taller" पहले से ही comparative है, इसलिए "more" जोड़ने से double comparative बन जाता है — एक क्लासिक गलती। "More tall" भी गलत है क्योंकि "tall" का comparative -er से बनता है। इसलिए उत्तर है "She is taller than her sister."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 8 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

4. सही सुधार चुनें: "I have seen him yesterday."
(a) I saw him yesterday. (b) I had seen him yesterday. (c) I see him yesterday. (d) I am seeing him yesterday.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Yesterday" जैसे निश्चित भूतकाल-सूचक शब्द के साथ simple past आता है, present perfect कभी नहीं। "Had seen" के लिए कोई दूसरी पूर्व घटना चाहिए होती है। इसलिए उत्तर है "I saw him yesterday."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

5. सही सुधार चुनें: "He denied to help me."
(a) He refused to help me. (b) He denied to helping me. (c) He denied for help me. (d) He denied for helping me.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Deny" के बाद gerund या that-clause आता है, infinitive कभी नहीं; इसलिए "denied to help" को केवल preposition बदलकर ठीक नहीं किया जा सकता — अर्थ बनाए रखने वाला स्वाभाविक सुधार "refused to help" है। बाकी विकल्प गलत "deny + to/for" पैटर्न ही रखते हैं। इसलिए उत्तर है "He refused to help me."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

6. सही सुधार चुनें: "Unless you do not work hard, you will fail."
(a) Unless you worked hard, you will fail. (b) Unless you work hard, you will fail. (c) Until you do not work hard, you will fail. (d) Unless you will not work hard, you will fail.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Unless" अर्थ में पहले से नकारात्मक है, इसलिए "not" जोड़ने से double negative बन जाता है — परीक्षक का पसंदीदा जाल। "Until" समय बताता है, शर्त नहीं। इसलिए उत्तर है "Unless you work hard, you will fail."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

7. सही सुधार चुनें: "The teacher as well as the students were present."
(a) The teacher as well as the students are present. (b) The teacher as well as the students was present. (c) The teacher as well as the students have been present. (d) The teacher as well as the students were being present.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "As well as" के साथ क्रिया पहले कर्ता (the teacher — एकवचन) के अनुसार आती है। क्रिया को पास की बहुवचन संज्ञा "students" से मिलाना ही जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "The teacher as well as the students was present."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

8. सही सुधार चुनें: "He is senior than me by two years."
(a) He is senior from me by two years. (b) He is senior to me by two years. (c) He is more senior than me by two years. (d) He is senior with me by two years.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Senior, junior, superior, inferior जैसे Latin comparatives के साथ "to" आता है, "than" कभी नहीं। "Senior" के बाद "than" लगाना ही लुभावनी गलती है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He is senior to me by two years."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

9. सही सुधार चुनें: "I and Ram went to the market."
(a) Ram and I went to the market. (b) Me and Ram went to the market. (c) Ram and me went to the market. (d) I and Ram go to the market.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: विनम्र अंग्रेज़ी प्रयोग में प्रथम पुरुष अंत में आता है, और कर्ता के रूप में "I" चाहिए, "me" नहीं। "Me and Ram" बोलचाल की आम गलती है। इसलिए उत्तर है "Ram and I went to the market."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 8 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

10. सही सुधार चुनें: "No sooner did he reach the station when the train left."
(a) No sooner did he reach the station then the train left. (b) No sooner he reached the station when the train left. (c) No sooner did he reach the station than the train left. (d) No sooner had he reach the station when the train left.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "No sooner" की जोड़ी "than" के साथ बनती है, "when" या "then" के साथ नहीं — यह निश्चित correlative जोड़ी अक्सर पूछी जाती है। इसलिए उत्तर है "No sooner did he reach the station than the train left."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

11. सही सुधार चुनें: "He is one of the best player in the team."
(a) He is one of the best player of the team. (b) He is one of the best playing in the team. (c) He is one of best player in the team. (d) He is one of the best players in the team.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "One of the + superlative" के बाद बहुवचन संज्ञा आनी चाहिए। "Player" को एकवचन रखना ही जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He is one of the best players in the team."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

12. सही सुधार चुनें: "The furnitures in this room are new."
(a) The furniture's in this room are new. (b) The furniture in this room is new. (c) The furniture in this room are new. (d) The furnitures in this room is new.
उत्तर: (b)

स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 8 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

13. सही सुधार चुनें: "He prevented me to go there."
(a) He prevented me for going there. (b) He prevented me from going there. (c) He prevented me in going there. (d) He prevented me go there.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Prevent" का पैटर्न है "prevent somebody from doing something"। Infinitive "to go" ही लुभावना गलत विकल्प है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He prevented me from going there."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

14. सही सुधार चुनें: "Hardly he had reached home when it started raining."
(a) Hardly he had reached home than it started raining. (b) Hardly he reached home when it started raining. (c) Hardly had he reached home than it started raining. (d) Hardly had he reached home when it started raining.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: जब "hardly" वाक्य की शुरुआत में आए तो inversion आवश्यक है (had he), और "hardly" की जोड़ी "when" के साथ बनती है, "than" के साथ नहीं। इसलिए उत्तर है "Hardly had he reached home when it started raining."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

15. सही सुधार चुनें: "Each of the boys have done his work."

16. सही सुधार चुनें: "He is more cleverer than his brother."
(a) He is much more cleverer than his brother. (b) He is most cleverer than his brother. (c) He is more clever than his brother. (d) He is cleverer than his brother.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Cleverer" पहले से ही comparative है; "more" जोड़ने से double comparative बन जाता है। "More clever" व्याकरणिक रूप से संभव है, पर परीक्षा में मानक सुधार साधारण comparative ही है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He is cleverer than his brother."

17. सही सुधार चुनें: "I am knowing the answer."
(a) I have been knowing the answer. (b) I was knowing the answer. (c) I know the answer. (d) I am know the answer.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Know" एक stative verb है और continuous tenses में प्रयोग नहीं होता। हिंदी भाषी अक्सर "I am knowing" कह देते हैं — यह भाषा-सेतु की क्लासिक गलती है। इसलिए उत्तर है "I know the answer."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 8 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

19. सही सुधार चुनें: "He told that he was tired."
(a) He said that he was tired. (b) He told that he is tired. (c) He told that he had tired. (d) He told that he being tired.
उत्तर: (a)

स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

20. सही सुधार चुनें: "Scarcely had she entered the room than the phone rang."
(a) Scarcely had she entered the room when the phone rang. (b) Scarcely she had entered the room when the phone rang. (c) Scarcely had she entered the room then the phone rang. (d) Scarcely had she enter the room when the phone rang.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Scarcely" की जोड़ी "when" के साथ बनती है, "than" के साथ कभी नहीं (वह "no sooner" की है)। Inversion "had she entered" पहले से सही है। इसलिए उत्तर है "Scarcely had she entered the room when the phone rang."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

21. सही सुधार चुनें: "The principal, along with the teachers, have decided to cancel the tour."
(a) The principal, along with the teachers, were decided to cancel the tour. (b) The principal, along with the teachers, are deciding to cancel the tour. (c) The principal, along with the teachers, have been deciding to cancel the tour. (d) The principal, along with the teachers, has decided to cancel the tour.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: परीक्षक का जाल — बीच का वाक्यांश "along with the teachers" कर्ता नहीं बदलता; क्रिया "the principal" (एकवचन) के अनुसार आती है। पास की बहुवचन संज्ञा देखकर "have" चुनना ही जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "The principal, along with the teachers, has decided to cancel the tour."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

24. सही सुधार चुनें: "The committee has taken their decision."
(a) The committee has taken his decision. (b) The committee have taken their decision. (c) The committee had taken their decision. (d) The committee has taken its decision.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: परीक्षक का जाल — एक इकाई के रूप में कार्य करने वाली collective noun एकवचन सर्वनाम "its" लेती है; "their" तभी ठीक होता जब सदस्य अलग-अलग कार्य करते। इसलिए उत्तर है "The committee has taken its decision."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

25. सही सुधार चुनें: "It is high time we start preparing for the exam."
(a) It is high time we will start preparing for the exam. (b) It is high time we started preparing for the exam. (c) It is high time we have started preparing for the exam. (d) It is high time we are starting preparing for the exam.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "It is high time" के बाद वर्तमान की तात्कालिकता दिखाने के लिए क्रिया simple past में जाती है। Present tense "start" ही लुभावनी गलती है। इसलिए उत्तर है "It is high time we started preparing for the exam."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण
