26. Choose the correct improvement: "He is more wiser than his brother."
(a) more wiser than (b) most wiser than (c) wiser than (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: "More wiser" is a double comparative, a classic examiner trap — "wiser" is already comparative, so "more" is redundant. The correct form is "wiser than", so the answer is (c).
Source: PYQ-style

27. Choose the correct improvement: "She does not know to swim."
(a) know to swim (b) No improvement (c) know swimming to (d) know how to swim
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "know", we use "how to + verb" for skills, not the bare infinitive. "Know to swim" sounds right to Hindi speakers ("usse tairna aata hai") but is wrong in English, so the answer is (d).
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

28. Choose the correct improvement: "I have been living in Delhi since five years."
(a) since five years (b) from five years (c) for five years (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (since 2019), while a period of time takes "for". "Five years" is a duration, so the answer is (c).
Source: PYQ-style

33. Choose the correct improvement: "I prefer tea more than coffee."
(a) to coffee (b) more than coffee (c) over than coffee (d) No improvement
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Prefer" is a Latin comparative and takes "to", never "than" or "more than". The tempting option "more than" is the trap, so the answer is (a).

38. Choose the correct improvement: "He is working here since 2015."
(a) is working (b) No improvement (c) was working (d) has been working
Answer: (d)
Solution: An action that began in the past and continues now takes the present perfect continuous — "has been working". The simple present is wrong with "since", so the answer is (d).

40. Choose the correct improvement: "Hardly he had reached home when it started raining."

Answer: (d)
Solution: When a sentence begins with a negative adverb like "Hardly", inversion is required — auxiliary before subject: "Hardly had he reached". The normal word order is the trap, so the answer is (d).
