126. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "No sooner did the bell ring when the students rushed out."
(a) then the students rushed out (b) as the students rushed out (c) No improvement (d) than the students rushed out
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "No sooner" is always paired with "than", never "when" — the tempting option "then" is wrong because "then" is an adverb of time, not a correlative conjunction. The correct pair is "No sooner…than", so the answer is "than the students rushed out".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

127. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "He is one of the best players that has ever played for India."
(a) who has ever played (b) that have ever played (c) which has ever played (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (b)
हल: In "one of the + plural noun + relative clause", the verb in the relative clause agrees with the plural noun ("players"), so it must be "have". "Has" sounds right because of the nearby "one", which is the classic trap. The correct pair is "that have ever played".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

128. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "Unless you do not work hard, you will fail."
(a) No improvement (b) Until you do not work hard (c) If you do not work hard not (d) Unless you work hard
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Unless" already carries a negative meaning ("if…not"), so adding "not" creates a double negative. "Until" refers to time, not condition, so it is wrong here. The correct form is "Unless you work hard".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

129. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "The teacher, along with his students, were present at the function."
(a) was present at the function (b) have been present at the function (c) were being present at the function (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject ("the teacher", singular), so "were" is wrong even though "students" sits next to the verb — that proximity is the trap. The correct form is "was present at the function".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

130. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "I prefer tea more than coffee."
(a) tea to coffee (b) No improvement (c) tea over coffee (d) tea than coffee
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Prefer" is a Latin comparative and takes "to", not "than" or "more than". "Over" is informal and not accepted in standard exam English. The correct form is "tea to coffee".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

131. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "Hardly had he reached the station then the train left."
(a) No improvement (b) when the train left (c) as the train left (d) than the train left
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Hardly/Scarcely" pair with "when", while "No sooner" pairs with "than" — examiners swap these to trap you. The correct form is "when the train left".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

132. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "Each of the boys have submitted his assignment."
(a) has submitted his assignment (b) have submitted their assignment (c) has submitted their assignments (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Each of" takes a singular verb, so "have" is wrong; "their" also breaks singular agreement. The correct form is "has submitted his assignment".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

133. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "He suggested me to take rest for a week."
(a) suggested that I take rest (b) suggested me taking rest (c) suggested to me take rest (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Suggest" is never followed by an object + infinitive ("suggested me to take" is wrong); it takes a that-clause or a gerund. The tempting "suggested me taking rest" keeps the wrong "me" before the verb. The correct form is "suggested that I take rest".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

134. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "The Ganges is one of the longest river in India."
(a) longest rivers in India (b) longer river in India (c) most long rivers in India (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "One of the + superlative" must be followed by a plural noun — "river" is the error. "Most long" is grammatically wrong. The correct form is "longest rivers in India".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

135. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "Scarcely he had stepped out of the house when it began to rain."
(a) No improvement (b) Scarcely had he stepped out (c) Hardly had he stepped out (d) Scarcely he stepped out
उत्तर: (b)
हल: When a negative adverb like "Scarcely" begins a sentence, inversion is required (auxiliary before subject). "Hardly had he stepped out" changes the tested word unnecessarily; the standard correction keeps "Scarcely" with inversion. The correct form is "Scarcely had he stepped out".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

136. Spot the error: "Neither of the two brothers (P) / have passed (Q) / the entrance examination. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Neither of" takes a singular verb, so Part Q should be "has passed". The plural-looking "brothers" next to the verb is the trap. The error is in Part Q.
स्रोत: PYQ-style

137. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "The committee has taken its decision unanimously."
(a) No improvement (b) has taken their decision (c) have taken its decision (d) have taken their decision
उत्तर: (a)
हल: When a collective noun acts as one unit (shown by "unanimously"), it takes a singular verb and singular pronoun — "has…its" is correct. "Their" would signal divided members, which contradicts "unanimously". No improvement is needed.
स्रोत: PYQ-style

138. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "Being a rainy day, I stayed at home."
(a) As it was a rainy day being (b) Being raining (c) It being a rainy day (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Being a rainy day, I stayed…" is a dangling modifier — it suggests "I" am the rainy day. The fix is to give "being" its own subject: "It being a rainy day". The correct form is "It being a rainy day".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

139. Spot the error: "The principal as well as the teachers (P) / were present (Q) / at the meeting. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
उत्तर: (d)
हल: With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject ("the principal", singular), so Part Q should be "was present". The nearby plural "teachers" is the deliberate trap. The error is in Part Q.
स्रोत: PYQ-style

140. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "He is more wiser than his brother."
(a) more wise than his brother (b) wisest than his brother (c) wiser than his brother (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Wiser" is already comparative; adding "more" makes a double comparative. "More wise" is non-standard, and "wisest" cannot take "than". The correct form is "wiser than his brother".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

141. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "I have been living in Delhi since five years."
(a) for five years (b) from five years (c) since last five years (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Since" is used with a point of time (since 2019), while a period of time takes "for". "From" is wrong in perfect tenses. The correct form is "for five years".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

142. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "No sooner did she finish her speech then the audience started clapping."
(a) when the audience started (b) as the audience started (c) than the audience started (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "No sooner" pairs only with "than"; "when" belongs with "hardly/scarcely" — the swap is the examiner's favourite trap. The correct form is "than the audience started".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

143. Spot the error: "One of my friend (P) / has gone (Q) / to Mumbai yesterday. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "One of" must be followed by a plural noun, so Part P should be "One of my friends". The singular verb "has" in Part Q is correct because the subject is "one". The error is in Part P.

144. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "He insisted to go with me to the market."
(a) insisted for going with me (b) insisted on going with me (c) insisted in going with me (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Insist" takes the preposition "on" followed by a gerund, never an infinitive. "In" and "for" are wrong prepositions here. The correct form is "insisted on going with me".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

145. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "The furniture in this room are very old."
(a) have been very old (b) were very old (c) is very old (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Furniture" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb — the plural-looking sense of "many items" is the trap. The correct form is "is very old".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

146. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "He told me that he will come to the party the next day."
(a) he would have come to the party (b) he will be coming to the party (c) he would come to the party (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (c)
हल: In reported speech with a past reporting verb ("told"), "will" backshifts to "would". "Would have come" wrongly suggests an unreal condition. The correct form is "he would come to the party".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

147. Spot the error: "The number of students (P) / in the class (Q) / are increasing every year. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "The number of" takes a singular verb ("is increasing"); "a number of" would take a plural verb. The plural noun "students" beside the verb is the trap. The error is in Part R.
स्रोत: PYQ-style

148. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "Despite of the heavy rain, the match continued."
(a) In despite of the heavy rain (b) Despite of heavy raining (c) No improvement (d) Despite the heavy rain
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Despite" never takes "of" — that is "in spite of". Mixing the two is a classic sound-right trap. The correct form is "Despite the heavy rain".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

149. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "Walking through the park, the flowers looked beautiful."
(a) the flowers were looking beautiful (b) I saw the flowers looking beautiful (c) the flowers had looked beautiful (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (b)
हल: The original is a dangling modifier — it says the flowers were walking through the park. The subject doing the walking must be named: "I". Tense changes alone do not fix the modifier. The correct form is "I saw the flowers looking beautiful".
स्रोत: PYQ-style

150. रेखांकित भाग के लिए सबसे अच्छा सुधारणा चुनें: "He is senior than me by two years."
(a) more senior than me by two years (b) senior from me by two years (c) No improvement (d) senior to me by two years
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Latin comparatives like "senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior" take "to", never "than", and never take "more". The correct form is "senior to me by two years".
स्रोत: PYQ-style
