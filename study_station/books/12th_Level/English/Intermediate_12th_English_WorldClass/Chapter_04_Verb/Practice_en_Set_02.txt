Questions 26–40: Easy | Questions 41–50: Medium

26. She ______ to school every day.
(a) go (b) goes (c) going (d) gone
Answer: (b)
Solution: With a singular third-person subject "she" in the simple present, the verb takes -s/-es. "Go" is used with plural subjects and I/you.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. They ______ playing cricket in the park now.
(a) are (b) was (c) is (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Now" signals present continuous, and "they" is plural, so "are playing" is correct. "Is" pairs only with singular subjects.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

28. I ______ my homework before the teacher came.
(a) had finished (b) finished (c) finish (d) have finished
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two past actions: the earlier one takes past perfect ("had finished"), the later one simple past ("came"). Simple past "finished" would wrongly suggest both happened together.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

29. The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rise (b) is rising (c) rises (d) has risen
Answer: (c)
Solution: Universal truths are expressed in the simple present tense. "Is rising" would describe an action happening at this moment, not a permanent fact.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

30. He ______ a letter yesterday.
(a) writes (b) wrote (c) write (d) written
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Yesterday" demands the simple past tense, so "wrote" is correct. "Written" is a past participle and cannot stand alone as a finite verb.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

31. We ______ in this city since 2015.
(a) live (b) lived (c) are living (d) have lived
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since 2015" indicates an action that began in the past and continues now, so present perfect "have lived" is required. Simple past "lived" would mean we no longer live there.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

32. The train ______ before we reached the station.
(a) left (b) has left (c) had left (d) leaves
Answer: (c)
Solution: The train's departure happened before our arrival, so the earlier action takes past perfect "had left". This is a classic exam trap where students pick simple past for both actions.
Source: PYQ-style

33. Bread and butter ______ my favourite breakfast.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: When two nouns joined by "and" denote one single dish or idea, the verb is singular. Bread and butter is treated as one meal, so "is" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

34. Each of the boys ______ a prize.
(a) have won (b) win (c) has won (d) are winning
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Each of" is always followed by a plural noun but a singular verb. The plural word "boys" near the verb is the examiner's trap; the true subject is "each".
Source: PYQ-style

35. She ______ TV when I entered the room.
(a) watched (b) watches (c) has watched (d) was watching
Answer: (d)
Solution: An ongoing past action interrupted by another past action takes past continuous: "was watching". Simple past "watched" would not show the action in progress.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

36. Neither Ram nor his brothers ______ present at the meeting.
(a) were (b) is (c) was (d) has been
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "neither…nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject, here "brothers" (plural), so "were" is correct. Choosing "was" by matching "Ram" is the common trap.
Source: PYQ-style

37. I ______ this movie twice already.
(a) see (b) saw (c) have seen (d) am seeing
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Already" and "twice" indicate experience up to the present, so present perfect "have seen" is used. Simple past "saw" needs a definite past time.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

38. The teacher asked the students why they ______ late.
(a) were (b) are (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: In reported speech with a past reporting verb ("asked"), the tense shifts back: present "are" becomes past "were". Keeping "are" breaks the sequence of tenses.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

39. Look! The children ______ in the garden.
(a) play (b) played (c) have played (d) are playing
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Look!" signals an action happening right now, so present continuous "are playing" is correct. Simple present "play" would describe a habit, not a current scene.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

40. He ______ English very well.
(a) speak (b) is speak (c) speaking (d) speaks
Answer: (d)
Solution: A general ability is expressed in simple present; with "he" the verb takes -s: "speaks". Option "is speak" mixes two verb forms incorrectly.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

41. The quality of the mangoes ______ not good.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: The subject is "quality" (singular), not "mangoes". The intervening phrase "of the mangoes" is the examiner's trap; the verb must agree with "quality", so "is".
Source: PYQ-style

42. By next year, she ______ her degree.
(a) will complete (b) has completed (c) completes (d) will have completed
Answer: (d)
Solution: "By next year" points to an action finished before a future deadline, so future perfect "will have completed" is correct. Simple future only says it will happen, not that it will be finished by then.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

43. One of my friends ______ gone to Delhi.
(a) have (b) has (c) are (d) were
Answer: (b)
Solution: "One of + plural noun" takes a singular verb because the real subject is "one". The nearby plural "friends" misleads many candidates into choosing "have".
Source: PYQ-style

44. Hardly ______ he reached home when it started raining.
(a) had (b) has (c) did (d) was
Answer: (a)
Solution: After "hardly/scarcely" at the start of a clause, the past perfect with inversion is used: "Hardly had he reached…". "Did he reach" would wrongly suggest a question form with simple past.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

45. The committee ______ divided in their opinions on this issue.
(a) is (b) was (c) are (d) has been
Answer: (c)
Solution: When a collective noun's members act separately (here, holding different opinions — shown by "their"), the verb is plural. "Is" would be correct only if the committee acted as one body.
Source: PYQ-style

46. No sooner did she see the police ______ she ran away.
(a) than (b) then (c) when (d) that
Answer: (a)
Solution: "No sooner" always pairs with "than", while "hardly/scarcely" pair with "when". Mixing these correlative pairs is a favourite examiner trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

47. He as well as his friends ______ invited to the party.
(a) were (b) are (c) have been (d) was
Answer: (d)
Solution: With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject ("he"), so singular "was" is correct. Treating the whole phrase as plural is the trap.
Source: PYQ-style

48. I wish I ______ a bird and could fly.
(a) were (b) was (c) am (d) will be
Answer: (a)
Solution: Wishes about unreal present situations take the subjunctive "were" for all persons. "Was" sounds right in speech but is grammatically wrong in this hypothetical construction.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

49. Spot the error: The Ganges (P) / is one of the longest rivers (Q) / which flows through India. (R) / No error (S)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part R has the error. The relative pronoun "which" refers to "rivers" (plural), so the verb must be "flow", not "flows". The singular verb is pulled wrongly toward "one".
Source: PYQ-style

50. Spot the error: Many a student (P) / have failed (Q) / in this examination. (R) / No error (S)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q has the error. "Many a + singular noun" always takes a singular verb, so it should be "has failed". The plural sense of the phrase tempts candidates to accept "have".
Source: PYQ-style
