Questions 51–60: Easy | Questions 61–75: Medium

51. Choose the correct form of the verb: She ______ to school every day.
(a) go (b) gone (c) going (d) goes
Answer: (d)
Solution: With a third-person singular subject (she) in the simple present tense, the verb takes -s/-es. "Goes" is correct; "go" would need a plural subject.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. Fill in the blank: They ______ playing cricket now.
(a) is (b) was (c) are (d) were
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Now" signals present continuous tense, and "they" is plural, so "are playing" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

53. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He doesn't like tea. (b) He doesn't likes tea. (c) He don't like tea. (d) He not like tea.
Answer: (a)
Solution: After "doesn't", the main verb stays in its base form — "like", not "likes". "Don't" is wrong with a singular subject.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

54. Fill in the blank: I ______ my homework yesterday.
(a) finish (b) finishing (c) have finished (d) finished
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Yesterday" marks a definite past time, so the simple past "finished" is used. The present perfect cannot be used with a specific past time word.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

55. Choose the correct verb: The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rises (b) rise (c) is rising (d) rose
Answer: (a)
Solution: Universal truths take the simple present tense. "The sun" is singular, so "rises" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

56. Fill in the blank: We ______ the movie last night.
(a) watched (b) watches (c) watch (d) watching
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Last night" indicates completed past action, so the simple past "watched" is required.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

57. Choose the correct form: ______ you ever been to Delhi?
(a) Have (b) Has (c) Had (d) Did
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Ever been" shows present perfect tense; with "you" the auxiliary is "have". "Has" pairs only with he/she/it.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

58. Fill in the blank: The baby ______ every morning at six.
(a) cry (b) cried (c) crying (d) cries
Answer: (d)
Solution: A daily habit takes the simple present; "the baby" is singular, so "cries" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

59. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I am agree with you. (b) I agree with you. (c) I agreed with you now. (d) I am agreeing with you always.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Agree" is a stative verb and is not used in the continuous form; "am agree" wrongly mixes a linking verb with a main verb.
Source: PYQ-style

60. Fill in the blank: Birds ______ in the sky.
(a) flies (b) flying (c) fly (d) flown
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Birds" is plural, so the base form "fly" is used in the simple present. "Flies" would need a singular subject.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

61. Choose the correct option: Neither of the two boys ______ done his work.
(a) have (b) has (c) are (d) were
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb, so "has" is correct. The plural-sounding phrase "two boys" is the trap — the true subject is "neither".
Source: PYQ-style

62. Spot the error: Part P: The quality of the mangoes / Part Q: were not good / Part R: in the market yesterday.
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: The subject is "the quality" (singular), not "mangoes", so Part Q should read "was not good". The intervening phrase "of the mangoes" misleads many candidates.
Source: PYQ-style

63. Fill in the blank: By the time we reached the station, the train ______.
(a) left (b) has left (c) had left (d) leaves
Answer: (c)
Solution: When two past actions occur, the earlier one takes the past perfect. The train left before we reached, so "had left" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

64. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I saw him yesterday. (b) I have seen him yesterday. (c) I had seen him yesterday. (d) I see him yesterday.
Answer: (a)
Solution: A definite past time expression like "yesterday" demands the simple past. The present perfect in the first option is a classic examiner's trap.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Fill in the blank: Each of the students ______ given a certificate.
(a) were (b) have (c) was (d) are
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Each of" is always followed by a singular verb, so "was" is correct despite the plural noun "students".
Source: PYQ-style

66. Choose the correct passive form: "She wrote a letter."
(a) A letter is written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter has been written by her. (d) A letter had written by her.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Simple past active becomes "was/were + past participle" in passive. "A letter" is singular, so "was written" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

67. Spot the error: Part P: One of my friends / Part Q: have gone / Part R: to Mumbai last week.
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "One of" takes a singular verb, and "last week" needs the simple past, so Part Q should be "went". Both the number trap and the tense trap appear here.
Source: PYQ-style

68. Fill in the blank: If it rains tomorrow, we ______ at home.
(a) stay (b) would stay (c) stayed (d) will stay
Answer: (d)
Solution: In a first conditional (real possibility), the if-clause takes the simple present and the main clause takes "will + verb".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

69. Choose the correct option: The committee ______ divided in their opinion.
(a) is (b) was (c) has (d) were
Answer: (d)
Solution: When a collective noun's members act separately (divided in opinion), a plural verb is used — "were". If the committee acted as one body, "was" would be right.
Source: PYQ-style

70. Fill in the blank: He ______ here since 2015.
(a) is living (b) has been living (c) was living (d) lived
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Since 2015" with an action continuing up to now requires the present perfect continuous — "has been living".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

71. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The news is shocking. (b) The news are shocking. (c) The news were shocking. (d) The news have shocked.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" looks plural but is an uncountable singular noun and always takes a singular verb — a favourite sound-right trap of examiners.
Source: PYQ-style

72. Fill in the blank: Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) did I reach (c) I reached (d) had I reached
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "hardly" at the start of a sentence, inversion is used with the past perfect: "Hardly had I reached... when...".
Source: PYQ-style

73. Choose the correct option: Bread and butter ______ his daily breakfast.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: When two nouns joined by "and" form one single idea or dish, the verb is singular. Bread and butter is treated as one meal, so "is" is correct.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Spot the error: Part P: The teacher, as well as the students, / Part Q: were present / Part R: in the assembly.
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject ("the teacher", singular), so Part Q should be "was present". The nearby plural "students" is the trap.
Source: PYQ-style

75. Fill in the blank: I wish I ______ a millionaire.
(a) am (b) was (c) were (d) will be
Answer: (c)
Solution: "I wish" expressing an unreal present desire takes the subjunctive "were" for all persons, not "was".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
