76. Neither of the two boys ______ done his homework.
(a) has (b) have (c) are (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb, so "has" is correct. "Have" is a common trap because "boys" sits right before the verb, but the true subject is "neither".

77. The quality of the mangoes ______ not good.
(a) were (b) was (c) are (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: The subject is "quality" (singular), not "mangoes". The prepositional phrase "of the mangoes" only describes the subject, so the singular verb "was" is needed.

78. Each of the students ______ given a certificate yesterday.
(a) were (b) have been (c) was (d) are
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Each of" is always followed by a plural noun but a singular verb. The past time marker "yesterday" confirms "was".

79. Bread and butter ______ his daily breakfast.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: When two nouns joined by "and" denote one single dish or idea, the verb is singular. "Bread and butter" is one meal, so "is" is correct.

81. Many a soldier ______ laid down his life for the country.

82. The committee ______ divided in their opinion on the new policy.
(a) were (b) was (c) is (d) has been
Answer: (a)

83. Two-thirds of the work ______ been completed.
(a) have (b) has (c) are (d) were
Answer: (b)
Solution: With fractions, the verb agrees with the noun after "of". "Work" is uncountable and singular, so "has" is correct.

84. I wish I ______ a millionaire.
(a) am (b) was (c) will be (d) were
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "I wish", an unreal or imaginary situation takes the subjunctive "were" for all persons. "Was" would suggest a real past fact.

88. I look forward to ______ you at the conference.

98. The furniture in this room ______ very old.

99. Scarcely had he stepped out of the house ______ it began to rain.
