51. The committee _______ its decision after a long discussion.
(a) announcing (b) announce (c) announced (d) announces
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Announces" looks tempting because the committee sounds like a present-day body, but the phrase "after a long discussion" signals a completed past action, so a past tense verb is needed. "Announced" fits the past context, so the answer is announced.

52. Neither of the two boys _______ done his homework.
(a) have (b) were (c) are (d) has
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "two boys" feels plural, but "neither of" takes a singular verb. The correct verb is has, so the answer is has.

53. She is good _______ mathematics.
