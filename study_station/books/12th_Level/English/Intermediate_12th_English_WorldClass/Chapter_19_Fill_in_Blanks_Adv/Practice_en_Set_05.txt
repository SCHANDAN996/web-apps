Fill in the Blanks (Advanced) — Practice Set 5
Questions 101–125 | Medium: 15 | Hard: 10

101. The committee, along with its advisors, ______ submitted its report to the ministry.
(a) has (b) have (c) were (d) are
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting choice is "have" because "advisors" sits right before the verb, but phrases like "along with" do not change the subject. The real subject is "the committee" (singular), so the answer is "has".
Source: PYQ-style

102. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) had I reached (c) did I reach (d) I reached
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Did I reach" tempts learners who know inversion is needed, but "hardly... when" demands the past perfect with inversion. The correct structure is "Hardly had I reached", so the answer is "had I reached".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

103. The manager insisted that the file ______ before noon.
(a) is completed (b) will be completed (c) was completed (d) be completed
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Was completed" looks safe because "insisted" is past tense, but verbs of demand take the subjunctive: the base form of the verb. Hence "insisted that the file be completed", so the answer is "be completed".
Source: PYQ-style

104. Neither the players nor the coach ______ satisfied with the umpire's decision.
(a) were (b) are (c) have been (d) was
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "players" is plural, but with "neither... nor" the verb agrees with the nearer subject, which is "the coach" (singular). So the answer is "was".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

105. She is one of those writers who ______ their readers think deeply.
(a) makes (b) has made (c) is making (d) make
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Makes" tempts because the sentence begins with "She is", but the relative pronoun "who" refers to "writers" (plural), not to "one". The verb must be plural, so the answer is "make".
Source: PYQ-style

106. The data collected from the survey ______ that most voters favour the new policy.
(a) is showing (b) shows (c) show (d) has shown
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Shows" tempts those who treat "data" as singular like "information", but in formal exam English "data" is the plural of "datum" and takes a plural verb. So the answer is "show".
Source: PYQ-style

107. ______ the heavy rain, the match continued without interruption.
(a) Despite (b) Due to (c) Owing to (d) Because of
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Because of" tempts because rain and a stopped match go together, but the sentence says the match continued, so a contrast word is needed. "Despite" shows contrast, so the answer is "Despite".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

108. He spoke in such a low voice ______ nobody could hear him.
(a) which (b) that (c) as (d) so
Answer: (b)
Solution: "So" tempts because of the meaning of result, but the fixed correlative pattern is "such... that". The correct completion is "such a low voice that", so the answer is "that".
Source: PYQ-style

109. By the time we reach the theatre, the film ______.
(a) will have started (b) has started (c) starts (d) will start
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Will start" tempts as a simple future, but "by the time" signals an action completed before a future point, which needs the future perfect. So the answer is "will have started".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

110. The principal ordered that the gates ______ at 9 p.m. sharp.
(a) are closed (b) should closed (c) be closed (d) were closed
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Were closed" tempts because "ordered" is past tense, but after verbs of command the subjunctive base form is used: "ordered that the gates be closed". So the answer is "be closed".
Source: PYQ-style

111. Many a soldier ______ laid down his life for the country.
(a) has (b) have (c) are (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Have" tempts because the phrase talks about many soldiers, but "many a" is grammatically singular and takes a singular verb. So the answer is "has".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

112. Scarcely had she finished her speech ______ the audience started clapping.
(a) than (b) then (c) when (d) that
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Than" tempts because it pairs with "no sooner", but "scarcely" and "hardly" pair with "when". So the answer is "when".
Source: PYQ-style

113. The number of road accidents ______ increased alarmingly this year.
(a) has (b) have (c) are (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "accidents" is plural and nearest to the verb, but "the number of" is a singular phrase meaning "the count". So the answer is "has".
Source: PYQ-style

114. I would rather you ______ the truth yesterday.
(a) tell (b) told (c) have told (d) had told
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Told" tempts because "would rather" with a subject normally takes the simple past, but that rule applies to present or future preferences; with a past time marker like "yesterday", the past perfect is required. So the answer is "had told".
Source: PYQ-style

115. Bread and butter ______ his daily breakfast.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Are" tempts because two nouns are joined by "and", but "bread and butter" names a single combined dish and takes a singular verb. So the answer is "is".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

116. It is high time we ______ serious about climate change.
(a) get (b) have got (c) got (d) will get
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Get" tempts because the sentence speaks of the present, but "it is high time" is followed by the simple past to express urgency. So the answer is "got".
Source: PYQ-style

117. The Ganges ______ one of the longest rivers in India.
(a) is (b) are (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "rivers" appears just before the blank, but the subject is "The Ganges", a single river. So the answer is "is".
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

118. Unless you ______ hard, you cannot clear the examination.
(a) worked (b) had worked (c) will work (d) work
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Will work" tempts because the result clause is future, but conditional clauses with "unless" use the present simple, never "will". So the answer is "work".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

119. Each of the boys ______ given a certificate at the annual function.
(a) were (b) was (c) have been (d) are
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "boys" is plural and adjacent, but "each of" makes the subject singular. So the answer is "was".
Source: PYQ-style

120. No sooner did the bell ring ______ the students rushed out.
(a) when (b) than (c) then (d) that
Answer: (b)
Solution: "When" tempts because it pairs with "hardly" and "scarcely", but "no sooner" always pairs with "than". So the answer is "than".
Source: PYQ-style

121. The furniture in this showroom ______ very expensive.
(a) have been (b) are (c) were (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Are" tempts because a showroom contains many items, but "furniture" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb. So the answer is "is".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

122. He denied ______ the money from the cash box.
(a) taken (b) to take (c) taking (d) take
Answer: (c)
Solution: "To take" tempts because many verbs are followed by the infinitive, but "deny" is always followed by a gerund. So the answer is "taking".
Source: PYQ-style

123. Five thousand rupees ______ a reasonable price for this old bicycle.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "rupees" is plural, but a sum of money treated as one amount takes a singular verb. So the answer is "is".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

124. Had I known about the meeting earlier, I ______ attended it.
(a) will have (b) would have (c) had (d) would
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Had" tempts because the if-clause uses "had known", but the result clause of a third conditional needs "would have" plus the past participle. So the answer is "would have".
Source: PYQ-style

125. The teacher, as well as the students, ______ present at the seminar.
(a) was (b) are (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "students" stands nearest to the blank, but "as well as" does not make the subject plural; the subject is "the teacher" alone. So the answer is "was".
Source: PYQ-style
