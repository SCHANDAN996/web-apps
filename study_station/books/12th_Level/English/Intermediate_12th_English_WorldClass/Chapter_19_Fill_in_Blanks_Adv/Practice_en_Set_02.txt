26. She has been working in this company ______ 2015.
(a) from (b) for (c) since (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "For" is tempting because it also shows time, but "for" is used with a duration (for ten years), while "since" is used with a point of time. 2015 is a point of time, so the answer is "since".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. He is good ______ mathematics.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) with
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Good in" sounds natural to many learners but is incorrect; the fixed preposition with "good" is "at" when talking about skill. So the answer is "at".
Source: PYQ-style

28. The train arrived ______ time.
(a) on (b) at (c) in (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In time" means before a deadline, which is the trap here; "on time" means exactly at the scheduled time, which fits an arriving train. So the answer is "on".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

29. I ______ my homework before the teacher came.
(a) had finished (b) finished (c) have finished (d) finish
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Finished" is tempting, but when two past actions occur, the earlier one takes the past perfect. Finishing happened before the teacher came, so the answer is "had finished".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

30. Neither of the two boys ______ done his work.
(a) have (b) were (c) are (d) has
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "boys" is plural, but "neither of" takes a singular verb. So the answer is "has".
Source: PYQ-style

31. She is ______ honest woman.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (d)
Solution: "A" tempts because "honest" starts with the letter h, but the h is silent and the word begins with a vowel sound. So the answer is "an".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

32. He jumped ______ the river to save the child.
(a) in (b) into (c) on (d) upon
Answer: (b)
Solution: "In" shows position, but movement from outside to inside requires "into". Jumping is movement, so the answer is "into".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

33. You should abide ______ the rules of the school.
(a) with (b) by (c) to (d) on
Answer: (b)
Solution: The verb "abide" has a fixed preposition "by" when it means to follow rules. So the answer is "by".
Source: PYQ-style

34. The Ganges ______ one of the holiest rivers of India.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "rivers" is plural, but the subject is "The Ganges", a single river. So the answer is "is".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

35. I prefer tea ______ coffee.
(a) to (b) over (c) than (d) from
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Than" is the classic trap because prefer shows comparison, but "prefer" always takes "to", never "than". So the answer is "to".
Source: PYQ-style

36. He has been suffering ______ fever since Monday.
(a) from (b) with (c) by (d) of
Answer: (a)
Solution: The fixed phrase is "suffer from" a disease; "suffer with" is used for a person, not an illness. So the answer is "from".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

37. ______ you finish your work, you may go home.
(a) Till (b) Unless (c) Until (d) If
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Unless" would reverse the meaning (you may go home only if you do not finish), which is illogical. A simple condition fits, so the answer is "If".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

38. The teacher asked the students ______ noise.
(a) not to make (b) to not make (c) not make (d) do not make
Answer: (a)
Solution: "To not make" splits the infinitive awkwardly; the correct negative infinitive structure is "not to make". So the answer is "not to make".
Source: PYQ-style

39. He is ______ taller than his brother.
(a) very (b) much (c) more (d) most
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Very" cannot modify a comparative; "much" is used before comparatives like taller. "More taller" would be a double comparative error. So the answer is "much".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

40. We must respect our ______.
(a) elders (b) elder (c) olders (d) eldest
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Olders" is not an English word, and the general class of older people is expressed by the plural "elders". So the answer is "elders".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

41. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) had I reached (b) I had reached (c) I reached (d) did I reach
Answer: (a)
Solution: "I had reached" tempts because the past perfect is needed, but after "hardly" at the start of a sentence, inversion is compulsory. So the answer is "had I reached".
Source: PYQ-style

42. The committee ______ divided in its opinion on the new policy.
(a) were (b) are (c) have (d) was
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Were" tempts because the members disagree, but the pronoun "its" is singular, so the committee is treated as one body here and takes a singular verb. So the answer is "was".
Source: PYQ-style

43. He denied ______ the money from the drawer.
(a) to steal (b) steal (c) stealing (d) stolen
Answer: (c)
Solution: "To steal" tempts because many verbs take the infinitive, but "deny" is always followed by a gerund. So the answer is "stealing".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

44. ______ the rain, the match continued.
(a) Despite of (b) In spite (c) Although (d) Despite
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Despite of" is the most common trap — "despite" never takes "of" (it is "in spite of"). "Although" needs a clause, not a noun. So the answer is "Despite".
Source: PYQ-style

45. She is one of the best players who ______ ever played for the country.
(a) has (b) was (c) is (d) have
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Has" tempts because of "one of", but the relative pronoun "who" refers to "players" (plural), so the verb must be plural. So the answer is "have".
Source: PYQ-style

46. No sooner did he see the police ______ he ran away.
(a) when (b) then (c) than (d) that
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Then" is the sound-right trap; the fixed correlative pair is "no sooner … than". So the answer is "than".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

47. The manager, along with his assistants, ______ attending the meeting.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "assistants" is plural and nearest to the verb, but with "along with" the verb agrees with the first subject, "the manager". So the answer is "is".
Source: PYQ-style

48. I look forward ______ you at the conference.
(a) to meet (b) to meeting (c) for meeting (d) meeting
Answer: (b)
Solution: "To meet" is the big trap — here "to" is a preposition, not part of an infinitive, so it takes a gerund. So the answer is "to meeting".
Source: PYQ-style

49. Unless you ______ hard, you will not pass the examination.
(a) will work (b) worked (c) work (d) would work
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Will work" tempts because the result clause has "will", but in conditional clauses with "unless", we use the present simple for the future. So the answer is "work".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

50. The scenery of Kashmir ______ very beautiful.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Are" tempts because beauty seems to involve many things, but "scenery" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb. So the answer is "is".
