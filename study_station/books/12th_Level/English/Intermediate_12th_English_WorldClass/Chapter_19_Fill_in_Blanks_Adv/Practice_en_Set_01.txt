Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. She has been working in this office ______ 2015.
(a) from (b) for (c) since (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "For" is tempting because it also shows time, but it is used with a duration (for ten years), not a starting point. "Since" is used with a point of time like 2015, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. The teacher asked the students to ______ their notebooks.
(a) take up (b) take after (c) take off (d) take out
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Take off" tempts because it is common, but it means to remove clothes or for a plane to leave. "Take out" means to remove something from a bag or desk, so the answer is take out.
Source: PYQ-style

3. He is ______ honest man.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "A" tempts because "honest" begins with the letter h, but the h is silent and the word starts with a vowel sound. We use "an" before vowel sounds, so the answer is an.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

4. The train ______ before we reached the station.
(a) left (b) had left (c) has left (d) leaves
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Left" tempts because both actions are in the past, but when two past actions occur, the earlier one takes the past perfect. The train left first, so the answer is had left.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

5. I am fond ______ reading novels.
(a) in (b) of (c) at (d) with
Answer: (b)
Solution: "In" tempts because many verbs pair with it, but "fond" takes a fixed preposition. The correct phrase is "fond of", so the answer is of.
Source: PYQ-style

6. Neither of the two boys ______ done his homework.
(a) have (b) were (c) are (d) has
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "boys" looks plural, but "neither of" takes a singular verb. The subject is "neither", so the answer is has.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

7. She is good ______ mathematics.
(a) at (b) in (c) for (d) on
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In" tempts because we say "interested in", but "good" pairs with "at" for skills and subjects. The fixed phrase is "good at", so the answer is at.
Source: PYQ-style

8. The Ganga ______ one of the longest rivers in India.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "rivers" is plural, but the subject is "The Ganga", which is singular. "One of the + plural noun" takes a singular verb, so the answer is is.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

9. He ______ his lunch before I arrived.
(a) finished (b) finishes (c) has finished (d) had finished
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Finished" tempts as a simple past, but his finishing happened before my arrival, an earlier past action. Earlier of two past actions takes past perfect, so the answer is had finished.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

10. You should abide ______ the rules of the school.
(a) with (b) on (c) to (d) by
Answer: (d)
Solution: "With" tempts because "abide" sounds like "agree with", but the fixed phrase is "abide by" meaning to follow. The correct preposition is by, so the answer is by.
Source: PYQ-style

11. Each of the students ______ given a certificate.
(a) was (b) were (c) have (d) are
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "students" is plural, but "each of" makes the subject singular. The verb must agree with "each", so the answer is was.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

12. I have lived in Delhi ______ ten years.
(a) for (b) since (c) from (d) ago
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Since" tempts because the sentence is in perfect tense, but "since" needs a point of time, not a duration. "Ten years" is a duration, so the answer is for.

13. The child is afraid ______ dogs.
(a) of (b) from (c) with (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "From" tempts because fear seems to come from something, but the fixed phrase is "afraid of". The correct preposition is of, so the answer is of.

14. Bread and butter ______ his daily breakfast.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Are" tempts because two nouns are joined by "and", but bread and butter together form one single dish. A combined item takes a singular verb, so the answer is is.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

15. She ______ television when the lights went off.
(a) watched (b) has watched (c) is watching (d) was watching
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Watched" tempts as simple past, but an ongoing action interrupted by another past action takes past continuous. She was in the middle of watching, so the answer is was watching.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

16. He is junior ______ me by two years.
(a) than (b) from (c) to (d) with
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Than" tempts because it is a comparison, but Latin comparatives like junior, senior, superior and inferior take "to", never "than". The correct preposition is to, so the answer is to.

17. The police ______ arrested the thief.
(a) has (b) have (c) is (d) was
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Has" tempts because "police" looks singular, but "police" is always plural in English. It takes a plural verb, so the answer is have.

18. We must respect our ______.
(a) elder (b) eldest (c) elders (d) older
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Elder" tempts as it matches the adjective form, but here we need a noun meaning older people in general. The noun form is "elders", so the answer is elders.

19. ______ you finish your work, you may go home.
(a) If (b) Unless (c) Until (d) Till
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Unless" tempts because it also shows condition, but it means "if not" and would reverse the meaning. The sentence needs a positive condition, so the answer is If.

20. He deals ______ electronic goods.
(a) at (b) with (c) in (d) on
Answer: (c)
Solution: "With" tempts because "deal with" is common, but that means to handle a problem or person. For trading in goods we say "deal in", so the answer is in.

21. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) I have reached (c) I reached (d) had I reached
Answer: (d)
Solution: "I had reached" tempts because the past perfect tense is right, but after "hardly" at the start of a sentence, inversion is required — the helping verb comes before the subject. The correct inverted form is "had I reached", so the answer is had I reached.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English Grammar

22. The manager, along with his assistants, ______ attending the meeting.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "assistants" sits just before the verb, but with "along with" the verb agrees with the first subject, "the manager", which is singular. The intervening phrase is a trap, so the answer is is.
Source: PYQ-style

23. No sooner had she entered the room ______ the phone rang.
(a) than (b) then (c) when (d) that
Answer: (a)
Solution: "When" tempts because it pairs with "hardly" and "scarcely", but "no sooner" always pairs with "than". The correct correlative is than, so the answer is than.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English Grammar

24. The quality of the mangoes ______ not good.
(a) are (b) were (c) was (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "mangoes" is plural and nearest to the verb, but the real subject is "quality", which is singular. The prepositional phrase "of the mangoes" is an intervening trap, so the answer is was.
Source: PYQ-style

25. He is one of the best players who ______ ever played for India.
(a) have (b) has (c) is (d) was
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Has" tempts because "one of" is singular, but the relative pronoun "who" refers to "players", which is plural, so the verb after it must be plural. The correct verb is have, so the answer is have.
