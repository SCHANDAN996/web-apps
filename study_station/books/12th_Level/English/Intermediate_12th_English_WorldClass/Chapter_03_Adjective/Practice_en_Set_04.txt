76. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is more senior than me by two years. (b) He is senior than me by two years. (c) He is senior to me by two years. (d) He is most senior to me by two years.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Senior than" is the tempting error — Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior take "to", not "than". "Most senior" is wrong because only two people are compared, so the answer is "He is senior to me by two years."
