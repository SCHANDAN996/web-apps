126. Choose the sentence with the correct use of adjective.
(a) He is more superior to me in rank. (b) He is superior to me in rank. (c) He is most superior to me in rank. (d) He is superior than me in rank.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Superior" is a Latin comparative adjective and already carries comparison, so "more superior" and "most superior" are wrong, and it takes "to", not "than" — so "superior than" is also wrong. The correct sentence is "He is superior to me in rank."

Answer: (c)
Solution: "Former" and "latter" are correctly used for two things — "former" for the first mentioned, "latter" for the second. Many students wrongly think "latter" should be "last", but "last" is used only for more than two. The sentence has no error.

Answer: (c)

131. Choose the sentence with the correct order of adjectives.
(a) She bought a beautiful black leather bag. (b) She bought a leather black beautiful bag. (c) She bought a black beautiful leather bag. (d) She bought a leather beautiful black bag.
Answer: (a)
Solution: English adjective order is opinion → size → age → shape → colour → origin → material → purpose. "Beautiful" (opinion) comes before "black" (colour), which comes before "leather" (material). The correct order is "a beautiful black leather bag".

Answer: (d)
