1. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She is more taller than her sister. (b) She is most taller than her sister. (c) She is taller than her sister. (d) She is much more taller than her sister.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Taller" is already a comparative, so adding "more" or "most" makes a double comparative, which is wrong. The tempting option with "more taller" sounds natural in speech but is grammatically incorrect, so the answer is "She is taller than her sister."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

2. Identify the adjective in the sentence: "The brave soldier saved the child."
(a) soldier (b) brave (c) saved (d) child
Answer: (b)
Solution: An adjective describes a noun. "Soldier" and "child" are nouns and "saved" is a verb. "Brave" describes the soldier, so the answer is "brave."
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

3. Choose the correct form: "This is the ______ book I have ever read."
(a) best (b) better (c) good (d) most good
Answer: (a)
Solution: Comparing one book against all books ever read needs the superlative degree. "Good" is positive and "better" is comparative; "most good" is not a valid form. The superlative of good is "best," so the answer is "best."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

4. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is senior than me. (b) He is senior from me. (c) He is senior to me. (d) He is senior of me.
Answer: (c)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior and prefer take "to," not "than." The tempting option with "than" follows the normal comparative pattern but is wrong here, so the answer is "He is senior to me."
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

5. Fill in the blank: "There is ______ milk in the jug."
(a) many (b) much (c) few (d) several
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Milk" is uncountable, so it takes "much." "Many," "few" and "several" are used only with countable nouns, so the answer is "much."
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

6. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I have much money. (b) I have many money. (c) I have many monies. (d) I have much moneys.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Money" is uncountable and has no plural form in this sense, so "monies/moneys" options are wrong, and "many" cannot pair with an uncountable noun. "Much" is correct with uncountable nouns, so the answer is "I have much money."
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

7. Identify the type of adjective in: "Each student received a certificate."
(a) Distributive adjective (b) Adjective of quality (c) Demonstrative adjective (d) Adjective of quantity
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Each" refers to members of a group one by one, which is the job of a distributive adjective (each, every, either, neither). It does not show quality, quantity or pointing, so the answer is "Distributive adjective."
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

8. Choose the correct order of adjectives: "She bought a ______ dress."
(a) red beautiful silk (b) beautiful red silk (c) silk red beautiful (d) red silk beautiful
Answer: (b)
Solution: The standard order is opinion → size → age → shape → colour → origin → material. "Beautiful" (opinion) comes before "red" (colour), which comes before "silk" (material), so the answer is "beautiful red silk."
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

9. Fill in the blank: "______ knowledge is a dangerous thing."
(a) A little (b) Little (c) The little (d) Few
Answer: (a)
Solution: "A little" means some (a small amount), which fits the famous saying. "Little" means almost none and would reverse the meaning; "few" is for countable nouns, so the answer is "A little."
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

10. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The Ganga is one of the longest river in India. (b) The Ganga is one of the most longest rivers in India. (c) The Ganga is one of longest rivers in India. (d) The Ganga is one of the longest rivers in India.
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "one of the + superlative," the noun must be plural — "one of the longest rivers." The tempting option with singular "river" is a classic examiner trap, and "most longest" is a double superlative, so the answer is "The Ganga is one of the longest rivers in India."

11. Identify the adjective of quantity in: "He ate some rice."
(a) He (b) ate (c) rice (d) some
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Some" shows how much rice, an indefinite quantity, so it is an adjective of quantity. "Rice" is the noun and "ate" is the verb, so the answer is "some."

12. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) Ram is more wiser than Shyam. (b) Ram is most wiser than Shyam. (c) Ram is wisest than Shyam. (d) Ram is wiser than Shyam.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Wiser" is already comparative; adding "more" or "most" creates a double comparative. "Wisest" is superlative and cannot be used with "than," so the answer is "Ram is wiser than Shyam."

13. Fill in the blank: "This is ______ unique painting."
(a) a (b) an (c) the most (d) more
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Unique" begins with a "yoo" consonant sound, so it takes "a," not "an." Also, unique is an absolute adjective and cannot take "more" or "most," so the answer is "a."

14. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is the best of the two brothers. (b) He is the good of the two brothers. (c) He is the better of the two brothers. (d) He is the most best of the two brothers.
Answer: (c)
Solution: When only two persons or things are compared, the comparative degree is used, not the superlative. "Best" needs three or more, so the answer is "He is the better of the two brothers."
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

15. Identify the demonstrative adjective in: "Those mangoes are sweet."
(a) are (b) mangoes (c) Those (d) sweet
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Those" points out which mangoes, so it is a demonstrative adjective. "Sweet" is an adjective of quality, not demonstrative, so the answer is "Those."

16. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I prefer tea than coffee. (b) I prefer tea over than coffee. (c) I prefer tea to coffee. (d) I prefer tea from coffee.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Prefer" is a Latin comparative and takes "to," never "than." The tempting option with "than" follows the usual comparative pattern but is wrong, so the answer is "I prefer tea to coffee."

17. Fill in the blank: "______ boys in the class passed the test." (meaning: almost none)
(a) A few (b) Few (c) The few (d) Many
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Few" means almost none (negative sense), which matches the required meaning. "A few" means some (positive sense) and "many" means a large number, so the answer is "Few."
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

18. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) No sooner had he come than it rained. (b) No sooner had he come when it rained. (c) No sooner he had come than it rained. (d) No sooner had he came than it rained.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than," and the verb after "had" must be the past participle "come." The tempting option with "when" pairs wrongly, so the answer is "No sooner had he come than it rained."
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

19. Identify the interrogative adjective in: "Which road leads to the station?"
(a) station (b) road (c) leads (d) Which
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Which" asks a question about the noun "road," so it is an interrogative adjective. When "which" stands alone without a noun it is a pronoun, but here it modifies "road," so the answer is "Which."
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

20. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She is the more beautiful girl in the class. (b) She is the most beautiful girl of the class. (c) She is most beautiful girl in the class. (d) She is the most beautiful girl in the class.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The superlative takes "the" before it and "in" (not "of") for places/groups like a class. Dropping "the" or using "more" breaks the rule, so the answer is "She is the most beautiful girl in the class."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

21. Spot the error: "The two first (P) / chapters of this book (Q) / are very interesting. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ordinal numbers (first, second) come before cardinal numbers (two, three), so it should be "the first two chapters." This is a favourite examiner trap because "two first" sounds acceptable in speech. The error is in Part P, so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is comparatively more richer today. (b) He is comparative richer today. (c) He is comparatively richer today. (d) He is comparatively most rich today.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Comparatively" already signals comparison, so "richer" alone is enough; adding "more" makes a double comparative. "Comparative" is an adjective and cannot modify "richer" here, so the answer is "He is comparatively richer today."
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

23. Spot the error: "My house is (P) / farther than (Q) / yours in the lane. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Farther" is correctly used for physical distance, which is exactly the case here. Candidates often think "further" is the only correct form, but "farther" for distance is right, so the answer is No error.
Source: High School English Grammar — Wren & Martin

24. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The gold is a precious metal. (b) The gold is precious metal. (c) A gold is a precious metal. (d) Gold is a precious metal.
Answer: (d)
Solution: Material nouns used in a general sense take no article. "The gold" would mean some specific gold, and "a gold" is impossible for an uncountable noun, so the answer is "Gold is a precious metal."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

25. Fill in the blank: "Of the three sisters, Meena is the ______."
(a) tall (b) tallest (c) taller (d) more tall
Answer: (b)
Solution: Three sisters are being compared, so the superlative degree is required. "Taller" works only for two, and "more tall" is not a valid form, so the answer is "tallest."
