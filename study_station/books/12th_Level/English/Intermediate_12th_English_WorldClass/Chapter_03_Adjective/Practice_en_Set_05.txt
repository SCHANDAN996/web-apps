101. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is more superior to me in rank. (b) He is superior than me in rank. (c) He is superior to me in rank. (d) He is most superior to me in rank.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option uses "than" after "superior", but Latin comparatives like superior, inferior, senior, junior, prior and prefer take "to", not "than", and never take "more/most". So the answer is "He is superior to me in rank."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

102. Spot the error: Part P: The Ganges is / Part Q: one of the longest / Part R: river in India. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: After "one of the + superlative", the noun must be plural — "one of the longest rivers". Many students leave the noun singular because "one" feels singular, which is the trap. So the error is in Part R.

103. Choose the correctly framed sentence.
(a) I have a five-years-old daughter. (b) I have a five-years old daughter. (c) I have a five year old daughter. (d) I have a five-year-old daughter.
Answer: (d)
Solution: When a compound adjective with a number comes before a noun, it is hyphenated and the noun inside stays singular — "a five-year-old daughter". The plural "years" in the other options is the trap. So the answer is "I have a five-year-old daughter."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

104. Choose the correct option: "The ______ were given food and shelter."
(a) poors (b) poorly (c) poorness (d) poor
Answer: (d)
Solution: "The + adjective" denotes a whole class of people (the poor = poor people) and takes a plural verb; adjectives never take a plural "-s", so "poors" is the trap. So the answer is "poor".
Source: PYQ-style

105. Spot the error: Part P: She is / Part Q: more wiser / Part R: than her sister. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part P (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Double comparatives are wrong — "wiser" is already comparative, so "more wiser" is the trap; it should be just "wiser". So the error is in Part Q.

106. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is the best of the two brothers. (b) He is the better of the two brothers. (c) He is the most better of the two brothers. (d) He is good of the two brothers.
Answer: (b)
Solution: When only two persons or things are compared, use the comparative, not the superlative — "best" with "two" is the classic trap. So the answer is "He is the better of the two brothers."

107. Choose the correct order of adjectives: "She bought a ______ vase."
(a) beautiful old Chinese (b) old Chinese beautiful (c) Chinese beautiful old (d) old beautiful Chinese
Answer: (a)
Solution: The standard order is opinion → age → origin (OSASCOMP: Opinion, Size, Age, Shape, Colour, Origin, Material, Purpose). "Beautiful" (opinion) comes before "old" (age) and "Chinese" (origin). So the answer is "beautiful old Chinese".
Source: PYQ-style

108. Spot the error: Part P: No sooner did he arrive / Part Q: than the meeting / Part R: became more livelier. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Livelier" is already comparative; adding "more" makes a double comparative, which is the trap. It should be "more lively" or "livelier". So the error is in Part R.

109. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) My house is more far than yours. (b) My house is further than yours, but his is the furthest of the two. (c) My house is farthest than yours. (d) My house is farther than yours, but his is the farthest.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Farther/farthest" express physical distance; "further" expresses degree or addition. Superlatives never take "than", which is the trap in the wrong options. So the answer is "My house is farther than yours, but his is the farthest."

110. Choose the correct option: "______ information you gave me was not reliable."
(a) Many (b) Much (c) Few (d) Several
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable, so it takes "much", not "many/few/several", which go with countable nouns — that confusion is the trap. So the answer is "Much".

111. Spot the error: Part P: The first two chapters / Part Q: of this book / Part R: are very interested. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Things cause feelings, so they take "-ing" adjectives (interesting); people feel, so they take "-ed" adjectives (interested). Saying the chapters are "interested" is the trap. So the error is in Part R.

112. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is strong enough to lift the box. (b) He is enough strong to lift the box. (c) He is too enough strong to lift the box. (d) Enough he is strong to lift the box.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Enough" comes after an adjective but before a noun — "strong enough", "enough money". Placing it before the adjective is the trap. So the answer is "He is strong enough to lift the box."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

113. Choose the correct option: "Of all the students, Ravi scored the ______ marks."
(a) little (b) less (c) least (d) lesser
Answer: (c)
Solution: Comparing more than two requires the superlative; "less" is comparative and "lesser" is used only attributively (a lesser evil), which is the trap. So the answer is "least".
Source: PYQ-style

114. Spot the error: Part P: Gold is / Part Q: more precious / Part R: than any metal. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: When comparing a member with its own group, use "any other" — otherwise gold would be compared with itself, which is the trap. It should be "than any other metal". So the error is in Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

115. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The weather of Shimla is better than that of Delhi. (b) The weather of Shimla is more better than Delhi's. (c) The weather of Shimla is better than those of Delhi. (d) The weather of Shimla is better than Delhi.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Comparison must be between like things — weather with weather, so "that of Delhi" (that = the weather). Comparing weather directly with the city is the trap. So the answer is "The weather of Shimla is better than that of Delhi."
Source: PYQ-style

116. Choose the correct option: "There is ______ milk left in the fridge; go and buy some."
(a) a little (b) little (c) the little (d) many
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Little" means almost none (negative sense), which fits "go and buy some"; "a little" would mean some is available — the trap. So the answer is "little".

117. Spot the error: Part P: He is / Part Q: the most unique / Part R: player in the team. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: Absolute adjectives like unique, perfect, universal, empty and complete have no degrees of comparison; "most unique" is the trap. It should be "a unique player". So the error is in Part Q.

118. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is my elder brother, and he is older than me. (b) He is my eldest brother, and he is older than me by two years. (c) He is my older brother, and he is elder than me. (d) He is my elder brother, and he is elder than me.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Elder/eldest" are used for family members but never with "than"; "older" takes "than". Mixing "elder than" is the trap. So the answer is "He is my elder brother, and he is older than me."

119. Choose the correct option: "The ______ of the two plans was finally approved."
(a) latest (b) later (c) last (d) latter
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Latter" means the second of two things mentioned; "later" refers to time and "latest/last" to order or recency — the sound-alike trap. So the answer is "latter".
Source: PYQ-style

120. Spot the error: Part P: This is / Part Q: the most perfect / Part R: example of dedication. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Perfect" is an absolute adjective and cannot take "most"; the temptation to intensify it is the trap. It should be "a perfect example". So the error is in Part Q.

121. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I prefer tea to coffee. (b) I prefer tea over than coffee. (c) I prefer tea from coffee. (d) I prefer tea more than coffee.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Prefer" is a Latin comparative and takes "to", never "than" or "more than" — the trap. So the answer is "I prefer tea to coffee."

122. Choose the correct option: "______ students passed the exam this year, so the principal was disappointed."
(a) A few (b) The few (c) Many (d) Few
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Few" means almost none (negative), matching the disappointment; "a few" would mean some passed — the trap. So the answer is "Few".

123. Spot the error: Part P: The population of India / Part Q: is greater than / Part R: any country in the world. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: India must be excluded from the comparison group, so it should be "that of any other country"; omitting "other" compares India with itself, which is the trap. So the error is in Part R.

124. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She is too good to not pass. (b) She is too good not to pass. (c) She is so good to pass. (d) She is enough good to pass.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The structure "too + adjective + not to" means "so good that she cannot fail"; splitting the infinitive ("to not pass") and misplacing "enough" are the traps. So the answer is "She is too good not to pass."
Source: PYQ-style

125. Choose the correct option: "He gave me ______ useful suggestion that solved my problem."
(a) an (b) a (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Useful" begins with a consonant sound /juː/, so it takes "a", not "an" — going by the letter "u" instead of the sound is the trap. Since "suggestion" is a singular countable noun mentioned for the first time, the indefinite article is needed. So the answer is "a".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar
