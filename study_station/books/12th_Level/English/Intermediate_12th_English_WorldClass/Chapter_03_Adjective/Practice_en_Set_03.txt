51. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She is taller than her sister. (b) She is more taller than her sister. (c) She is most taller than her sister. (d) She is much more taller than her sister.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Taller" is already a comparative, so adding "more" or "most" makes it a double comparative, which is wrong. The tempting option with "more taller" sounds natural in speech but is grammatically incorrect, so the answer is "She is taller than her sister."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

52. Fill in the blank: This is the _______ movie I have ever seen.
(a) best (b) better (c) good (d) most good
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Ever seen" shows comparison among all movies, so the superlative degree is needed. "Good" is positive and "better" is comparative; "most good" is not a valid form, so the answer is "best."
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

53. Choose the correctly spelt adjective phrase position: He bought _______.
(a) a beautiful red car (b) a red beautiful car (c) red a beautiful car (d) beautiful a red car
Answer: (a)
Solution: Adjective order in English is opinion before colour (Opinion–Size–Age–Shape–Colour–Origin–Material–Purpose). "Beautiful" (opinion) must come before "red" (colour), so the answer is "a beautiful red car."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

54. Fill in the blank: There is _______ water in the jug.
(a) many (b) few (c) much (d) several
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Water" is uncountable, so it takes "much." "Many," "few," and "several" are used only with countable nouns, so the answer is "much."
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

55. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I have lesser books than you. (b) I have less books than you. (c) I have fewer books than you. (d) I have least books than you.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Books" is countable, so "fewer" is correct; "less" is for uncountable nouns and is the common trap here. "Lesser" and "least" do not fit this comparison, so the answer is "I have fewer books than you."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

56. Fill in the blank: Mount Everest is the _______ peak in the world.
(a) highest (b) higher (c) high (d) most high
Answer: (a)
Solution: Comparing one peak against all peaks in the world requires the superlative. "High" is positive and "higher" is comparative; "most high" is not standard, so the answer is "highest."
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

57. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is senior than me. (b) He is senior to me. (c) He is senior from me. (d) He is senior of me.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior, and prior take "to," not "than." The trap is using "than" by analogy with ordinary comparatives, so the answer is "He is senior to me."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

58. Fill in the blank: _______ students passed the exam; almost everyone failed.
(a) A few (b) The few (c) Few (d) Many
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Almost everyone failed" gives a negative sense, and "few" means "hardly any." "A few" is positive (some), which contradicts the second clause, so the answer is "Few."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

59. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The gold is a precious metal. (b) A gold is a precious metal. (c) Gold is a precious metal. (d) An gold is a precious metal.
Answer: (c)
Solution: Material nouns used in a general sense take no article. "The gold" would refer to specific gold, not the metal in general, so the answer is "Gold is a precious metal."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

60. Fill in the blank: She is _______ honest girl.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the sound is a vowel and takes "an." The trap is choosing "a" by looking at the letter rather than the sound, so the answer is "an."
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

61. Spot the error: Part P: No sooner had I / Part Q: reached the station / Part R: then the train left. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than," never "then." The trap is the similar sound of "then" and "than." Part R must read "than the train left," so the error is in Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

62. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is the best of the two brothers. (b) He is the better of the two brothers. (c) He is the most better of the two brothers. (d) He is good of the two brothers.
Answer: (b)
Solution: When only two persons or things are compared, the comparative degree is used, even with "the." The trap is "best," which needs three or more, so the answer is "He is the better of the two brothers."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

63. Fill in the blank: The _______ of the two sisters is a doctor.
(a) oldest (b) older (c) eldest (d) elder
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Elder" is used for members of the same family and is never followed by "than"; "older" is general. With two sisters, the comparative "elder" fits, so the answer is "elder."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

64. Spot the error: Part P: He is more wiser / Part Q: than / Part R: his brother. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Wiser" is already comparative, so "more wiser" is a double comparative — a classic examiner's trap. Part P should read "He is wiser," so the error is in Part P.

65. Fill in the blank: Delhi is _______ than Jaipur in population.
(a) populous (b) much populous (c) most populous (d) more populous
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Populous" is a long adjective and forms its comparative with "more." "Most" would be superlative and "much populous" is not standard, so the answer is "more populous."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

66. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I prefer tea more than coffee. (b) I prefer tea than coffee. (c) I prefer tea over than coffee. (d) I prefer tea to coffee.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Prefer" already contains the idea of comparison and takes "to," never "than" or "more than." The trap is adding "more" or "than," so the answer is "I prefer tea to coffee."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

67. Fill in the blank: _______ information you gave me was very useful.
(a) Many (b) Much (c) The few (d) Several
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable, so it takes "much." "Many," "the few," and "several" all require countable plural nouns, so the answer is "Much."

68. Spot the error: Part P: The scenery of Kashmir / Part Q: are / Part R: very beautiful. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb. The trap is the plural-looking idea of many sights; Part Q should read "is," so the error is in Part Q.

69. Fill in the blank: This is _______ unique opportunity; do not miss it.
(a) a most (b) the most (c) a (d) more
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Unique" means one of a kind and does not take degrees of comparison, so "most" or "more" before it is wrong. Since "unique" begins with a consonant sound (yu-), it takes "a," so the answer is "a."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

70. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He gave me many advices. (b) He gave me much advices. (c) He gave me an advice. (d) He gave me a lot of advice.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Advice" is uncountable and has no plural "advices" in standard English, nor can it take "an." "A lot of advice" is correct, so the answer is "He gave me a lot of advice."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

71. Fill in the blank: Of all the students, Ravi works the _______.
(a) hard (b) hardest (c) harder (d) most hard
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Of all" signals the superlative degree. "Hard" is positive and "harder" is comparative; "most hard" is not the standard superlative, so the answer is "hardest."
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

72. Spot the error: Part P: My house is / Part Q: farther than / Part R: yours in distance. / Part S: No error
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Farther" is correctly used for physical distance, while "further" is for additional/abstract sense. The sentence is grammatically sound, so the answer is "No error."

73. Fill in the blank: _______ knowledge is a dangerous thing.
(a) Few (b) Little (c) The little (d) A little
Answer: (d)
Solution: The proverb "A little knowledge is a dangerous thing" uses "a little" (some, though not much). "Little" would mean almost none, which reverses the meaning, so the answer is "A little."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar

74. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The rich should help the poor. (b) The rich should helps the poor. (c) The rich should helping the poor. (d) Rich should help poor.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The + adjective" (the rich) denotes a whole class and takes a plural verb; after "should" the base verb "help" is used. The trap is adding "-s" to the verb, so the answer is "The rich should help the poor."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

75. Fill in the blank: He is _______ than any other boy in the class.
(a) tall (b) tallest (c) taller (d) the tallest
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Than any other" requires the comparative degree to compare one boy with the rest. "Tallest" would need "of all," not "than," so the answer is "taller."
Source: Wren & Martin, High School English Grammar
