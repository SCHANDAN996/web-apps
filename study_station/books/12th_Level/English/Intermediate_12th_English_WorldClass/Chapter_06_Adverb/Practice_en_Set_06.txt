126. Choose the correctly framed sentence.
(a) He sings very beautifully (b) He sings very beautiful (c) He very sings beautifully (d) He beautifully very sings
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Beautiful" is an adjective and cannot modify the verb "sings"; the tempting wrong option uses it because it sounds natural in casual speech. The adverb "beautifully" correctly modifies the verb, and "very" modifies "beautifully", so the answer is "He sings very beautifully".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

127. Spot the error: Part P: She spoke / Part Q: enough loudly / Part R: to be heard by everyone. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that "enough loudly" sounds acceptable, but "enough" as an adverb always comes after the adjective or adverb it modifies. The correct form is "loudly enough", so the error is in Part Q.

133. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) Scarcely he had reached the station than the train left (b) Scarcely he had reached the station when the train left (c) Scarcely had he reached the station when the train left (d) Scarcely had he reached the station than the train left
Answer: (c)
Solution: Two traps operate here: after "scarcely" at the start, inversion (had he) is required, and "scarcely" pairs with "when", not "than". Only one option satisfies both, so the answer is "Scarcely had he reached the station when the train left".
