76. Choose the correctly framed sentence.
(a) He speaks English very fluently. (b) He very speaks English fluently. (c) He speaks very fluently English. (d) He fluently speaks very English.
Answer: (a)
Solution: An adverb of manner should not come between the verb and its object; the object "English" must stay next to the verb "speaks". The tempting option wrongly splits verb and object, so the answer is "He speaks English very fluently."

77. Spot the error: P: She sings / Q: enough sweetly / R: to win the prize / S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Enough" as an adverb comes after the adjective or adverb it modifies, so "sweetly enough" is correct, not "enough sweetly". Part P tempts but is fine, so the error is in Part Q.

78. Choose the correct sentence.
