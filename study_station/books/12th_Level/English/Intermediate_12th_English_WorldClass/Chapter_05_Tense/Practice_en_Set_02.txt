26. She ______ to school every day.
(a) go (b) gone (c) going (d) goes
Answer: (d)
Solution: Daily habit = Simple Present. With third person singular "she", the verb takes -s/-es, so "goes".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. They ______ football in the park yesterday evening.
(a) play (b) plays (c) will play (d) played
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Yesterday" signals Simple Past, so the past form "played" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

28. I ______ my homework before my father returned.
(a) finished (b) have finished (c) had finished (d) finish
Answer: (c)
Solution: Two past actions — the earlier one takes Past Perfect ("had finished"), the later one Simple Past ("returned").
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

29. Look! The baby ______.
(a) is crying (b) cries (c) cried (d) has cried
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Look!" shows an action happening right now, so Present Continuous "is crying" is used.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

30. We ______ in this city since 2015.
(a) have lived (b) lived (c) live (d) are living
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Since 2015" shows an action starting in the past and continuing till now — Present Perfect "have lived".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

31. He ______ the letter before the postman arrived.
(a) writes (b) had written (c) wrote (d) has written
Answer: (b)
Solution: The earlier of two past actions takes Past Perfect: "had written" happened before "arrived".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

32. The train ______ at 6 a.m. tomorrow.
(a) is leaving (b) leaves (c) will leave (d) left
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fixed timetables and schedules take Simple Present even for future time — "leaves".

33. While I ______ TV, the lights went off.
(a) watch (b) watched (c) was watching (d) am watching
Answer: (c)
Solution: A longer background action interrupted by a shorter one takes Past Continuous: "was watching".

34. She ______ her lunch already.
(a) has finished (b) finished (c) finishes (d) is finishing
Answer: (a)

39. My brother ______ in Delhi for five years now.

40. Don't disturb me. I ______.

Answer: (c)

41. Spot the error: Part P: I have seen / Part Q: that movie / Part R: last week. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Examiner's trap — a definite past time ("last week") cannot be used with Present Perfect. Part P should be "I saw". Words like yesterday, ago, last week always demand Simple Past.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) I am knowing the answer. (b) I have been knowing the answer. (c) I was knowing the answer. (d) I know the answer.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Know" is a stative verb (state, not action) and is not used in continuous forms. Simple Present "I know" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

43. By the time we reached the station, the train ______.
(a) had left (b) has left (c) left (d) leaves
Answer: (a)
Solution: "By the time + Simple Past" requires Past Perfect for the earlier action: the train "had left" before we reached.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

44. Spot the error: Part P: He is working / Part Q: in this bank / Part R: since 2010. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Examiner's trap — "since 2010" demands a perfect tense, not Present Continuous. Part P should be "He has been working" or "He has worked".

45. She ______ English very well because she ______ in London for two years.
(a) speaks, lived (b) is speaking, lives (c) speaks, is living (d) spoke, had lived
Answer: (a)
Solution: A permanent ability takes Simple Present ("speaks"); a finished past period takes Simple Past ("lived").

46. When I entered the room, he ______ a letter.

Answer: (d)
Solution: "Yesterday" fixes the action in finished past time, so only Simple Past "bought" is correct.

50. He ______ here for two hours before the doctor came.
