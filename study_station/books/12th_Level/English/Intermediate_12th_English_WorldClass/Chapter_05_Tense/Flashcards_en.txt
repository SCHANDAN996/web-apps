Card 1
Front: What are the three main tenses, and how many forms does each have?
Back: Present, Past, and Future — each has 4 forms: Indefinite (Simple), Continuous, Perfect, and Perfect Continuous. Total = 12 tense forms.

Card 2
Front: What is the structure of Present Perfect Tense?
Back: Subject + has/have + V3 (past participle). Use "has" with he/she/it/singular nouns; "have" with I/we/you/they/plural nouns. Example: She has finished her work.

Card 3
Front: Which time markers signal the Present Perfect Tense?
Back: Just, already, yet, recently, lately, ever, never, since, for, so far, up to now. Example: I have just eaten.

Card 4
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember Present Perfect markers?
Back: "JAY-SF" — Just, Already, Yet, Since, For. If you see these words, think Present Perfect (has/have + V3).

Card 5
Front: When do we use Past Perfect Tense?
Back: When two actions happened in the past, the earlier action takes Past Perfect (had + V3) and the later action takes Simple Past. Example: The train had left before I reached the station.

Card 6
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "The patient died before the doctor came." — Is this correct?
Back: No. The earlier action needs Past Perfect: "The patient had died before the doctor came." Trap: both verbs in Simple Past when one action clearly happened first.

Card 7
Front: What is the rule for "since" vs "for" in Perfect Continuous tenses?
Back: "Since" + point of time (Monday, 2010, morning, childhood); "For" + period of time (two hours, five years, a long time). Example: He has been working since 2018 / for six years.

Card 8
Front: Which verbs are NOT used in Continuous tenses?
Back: Stative verbs: know, believe, love, hate, own, belong, understand, seem, want, need, remember, forget. Say "I know him," not "I am knowing him."

Card 9
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "I am having a car." — Correct or wrong?
Back: Wrong. "Have" meaning possession is stative: "I have a car." But "have" meaning eat/drink/experience can be continuous: "I am having lunch."

Card 10
Front: What tense follows "It is time / It is high time"?
Back: Simple Past (with a present meaning). Example: It is high time we went home. Trap: don't use Simple Present after "It is high time."

Card 11
Front: How is Future time expressed with "going to" vs "will"?
Back: "Going to" = planned action or prediction based on evidence (Look at the clouds — it is going to rain). "Will" = instant decision, promise, or general prediction (I will help you).

Card 12
Front: What is the structure of Past Perfect Continuous?
Back: Subject + had been + V1+ing + since/for + time. Example: She had been waiting for two hours when the bus arrived.

Card 13
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember stative (non-continuous) verbs?
Back: "SALES-MEN" — Sense verbs (see, hear), Agree, Love, Emotions (hate, like), Seem, Mind verbs (know, believe), Exist (own, belong), Need/want. These avoid "-ing".

Card 14
Front: In a conditional sentence "If + Simple Past, ...", what comes in the main clause?
Back: Would + V1 (Type 2, unreal present). Example: If I were a bird, I would fly. Note: use "were" for all persons in formal English.

Card 15
Front: What tense is used in time clauses after when, before, after, until, as soon as (future meaning)?
Back: Simple Present, NOT Simple Future. Example: I will call you when he arrives (not "will arrive").

Card 16
Front: What is the difference between "I have lived here for ten years" and "I lived here for ten years"?
Back: Present Perfect = still living here (action continues to present). Simple Past = no longer living here (finished action).

Card 17
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "He is working here since 2015." — Find the error.
Back: "Since 2015" demands Perfect/Perfect Continuous: "He has been working here since 2015." Trap: Present Continuous with since/for is a classic exam error.

Card 18
Front: Which tense is used for universal truths, habits, and timetables?
Back: Simple Present. Examples: The sun rises in the east (truth); He goes to office daily (habit); The train leaves at 6 a.m. (timetable).

Card 19
Front: What is the structure of Future Perfect Tense, and give its common marker?
Back: Subject + will/shall have + V3. Markers: by, by the time, by tomorrow, by 2030. Example: She will have completed the project by Friday.

Card 20
Front: In reported speech, what does Simple Present change into?
Back: Simple Past (backshift). "He said, 'I am busy.'" → He said that he was busy. Present Perfect → Past Perfect; Simple Past → Past Perfect.
