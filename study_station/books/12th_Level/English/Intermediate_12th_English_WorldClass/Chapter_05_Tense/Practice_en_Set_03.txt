51. She ______ to school every day.
(a) goes (b) go (c) going (d) gone
Answer: (a)
Solution: A daily habit takes the simple present tense. With the third-person singular subject "she", the verb takes -s, so "goes" is correct.

52. They ______ football in the park yesterday evening.
(a) play (b) plays (c) played (d) will play
Answer: (c)
Solution: The time marker "yesterday evening" shows a finished past action, so the simple past "played" is needed.

53. Look! The baby ______.
(a) is crying (b) cries (c) cried (d) has cried
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Look!" signals an action happening right now, which takes the present continuous tense: is crying.

54. I ______ my homework before my father returned.
(a) finish (b) finished (c) had finished (d) have finished
Answer: (c)
Solution: Of two past actions, the earlier one takes the past perfect. Finishing homework happened before the father returned, so "had finished" is correct.

Answer: (b)
Solution: "Already" with a just-completed action takes the present perfect: has/have + past participle. With "she" we use "has", and the past participle of eat is "eaten".

67. Spot the error: Part P: I have seen / Part Q: that movie / Part R: last week / Part S: in the cinema hall.
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part P is wrong. The present perfect cannot be used with a definite past time like "last week"; it should be "I saw that movie last week". This is a classic examiner trap.

69. He usually ______ tea, but today he ______ coffee.
(a) drank, is drinking (b) is drinking, drinks (c) drinks, drinks (d) drinks, is drinking
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Usually" marks a habit, so simple present "drinks"; "today" marks an action in progress now, so present continuous "is drinking". Mixing the two correctly is the key skill here.

70. Spot the error: Part P: I am knowing / Part Q: the answer / Part R: to this question / Part S: very well.
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part P is wrong. "Know" is a stative verb and is not used in the continuous form; the correct sentence is "I know the answer". Examiners love trapping students with stative verbs like know, believe, and love.

75. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) She is working here since 2018. (b) She has been working here since 2018. (c) She was working here since 2018. (d) She works here since 2018.
Answer: (b)
Solution: An action that started in the past and is still continuing takes the present perfect continuous with "since": has been working. The continuous tenses in the other options cannot combine with "since 2018" for an action still in progress.
