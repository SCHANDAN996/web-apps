# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Spelling Check

## How Exams Test Spelling

Spelling questions appear in almost every objective English paper — SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, IBPS, RRB NTPC, and State exams. The format is remarkably stable: you are given **four words (or four sentences with one word highlighted)** and asked to pick the correctly or incorrectly spelt one. Occasionally, a sentence contains a misspelt word that you must identify.

**What the pattern analysis shows (in words, not numbers):**

- **Most frequently tested sub-topics:**
  1. Double-letter confusion (occasion/ocassion, accommodate/accomodate, committee/comittee)
  2. -ie- vs -ei- words (receive, believe, height, foreign)
  3. Silent letters (psychology, honest, knife, subtle)
  4. -able vs -ible endings (advisable, permissible, responsible)
  5. -ance/-ence and -ant/-ent pairs (independent, attendance, persistent)
  6. British vs American variants (colour/color, programme/program) — usually both accepted, but exams test the *British* form as standard
  7. Commonly mispronounced words spelt by sound (February, Wednesday, government, environment)
  8. Noun/verb pairs (advice/advise, practice/practise, licence/license)

- **Difficulty trend:** Easier papers (MTS, Group D) test everyday words (beautiful, necessary, tomorrow). Harder papers (CGL, IBPS PO) test less common words (entrepreneur, manoeuvre, questionnaire, bureaucracy).

- **One question per paper is typical** in SSC exams; banking exams sometimes fold spelling into "error spotting" where the error *is* a misspelling.

---

## Examiner's Trap Types in Spelling Questions

| Trap Type | How It Works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Sound-Right Trap** | The wrong option *sounds* exactly like the right word when spoken | "tommorrow", "occured", "seperate" — all sound plausible |
| **Double-Letter Swap** | Doubling the wrong letter or removing a needed double | "acommodate", "neccessary", "embarass" |
| **Analogy Trap** | A spelling that follows a *false* pattern from another word | "maintainance" (by analogy with "attendance") — correct is **maintenance** |
| **Over-thinking Trap** | The simple, familiar spelling is correct, but students pick an exotic-looking option | "questionnaire" looks wrong to some; they pick "questionaire" |

## Top 10 High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions with Solutions

**Q1.** Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occassion (b) Ocasion (c) Occasion (d) Occasionn
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** "Occasion" has double **c**, single **s**. Trap type: *Double-Letter Swap* — option (a) doubles the wrong letter.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q2.** Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Receive (c) Riceive (d) Receeve
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Rule: *i before e, except after c* — after "c" comes **ei**: re-CEI-ve. Trap type: *Sound-Right* — (a) is the single most common spelling error in English.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3.** Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Maintainance (b) Maintenence (c) Maintenance (d) Maintenanse
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** The verb is "maintain" but the noun drops to **-tenance**: maintenance. Trap type: *Analogy Trap* — students copy "attendance/attendance" pattern.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4.** Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Entreprenur (b) Entrepreneur (c) Entrapreneur (d) Entreprenure
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** French-origin word: entre-pre-neur. Break it into chunks to memorise. Trap type: *Sound-Right*.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q5.** Choose the incorrectly spelt word.
(a) Committee (b) Millennium (c) Embarassment (d) Accommodation
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** Correct spelling is **embarrassment** — double r, double s. Mnemonic: "I'm so embarrassed I turned **R**ed **R**eally **S**trongly **S**hy." Trap type: *Double-Letter Swap*.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6.** Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Questionaire (b) Questionnair (c) Questionnaire (d) Questionairre
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** Double **n** + "-aire": question-n-aire. Trap type: *Over-thinking* — the correct form looks odd, so students pick the simpler (a).
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7.** Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Goverment (b) Government (c) Governmant (d) Govarnment
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** The **n** before -ment is silent in speech but present in writing: gover**n**ment. Trap type: *Sound-Right* — we say "gover-ment".
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8.** Choose the incorrectly spelt word.
(a) Necessary (b) Secretary (c) Febuary (d) Library
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** Correct spelling is **February** — the first **r** is silent but must be written. Trap type: *Sound-Right*.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9.** Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Permissable (b) Permisible (c) Permissible (d) Permisable
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** Words from verbs ending in "-mit/-ss" take **-ible**: permit → permissible. Compare: advisable (-able after a full word). Trap type: *-able/-ible confusion*.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10.** Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Manouvre (b) Maneuver (c) Manoeuvre (d) Manovre
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** British standard: **manoeuvre** (American: maneuver). SSC/banking keys accept the British form. Trap type: *Over-thinking* — students avoid the longest option, but here it is correct.
**Source:** PYQ-style

## Applying the 15/45-Second Rule

**15-Second (Fact-based) — Q2 (Receive):**
You either know "i before e except after c" or you don't. Scan options → spot "Recieve" → eliminate → mark (b). **Do not re-read. Do not second-guess.** Target: 10–15 seconds.

**45-Second (Careful-check) — Q5 (Embarrassment):**
This is an "incorrectly spelt" question — you must verify **all four** options, not stop at the first plausible one. Committee ✓ (double m, double t, double e), Millennium ✓ (double l, double n), Embarassment ✗ (needs double r), Accommodation ✓. Underlining each double letter takes ~40 seconds. **Never rush "incorrectly spelt" questions** — they are the 45-second type.

**Rule of thumb:** "Correctly spelt" with familiar words → 15 sec. "Incorrectly spelt" or unfamiliar/rare words → 45 sec. If still unsure at 45 seconds, mark your first instinct and move on — spelling has no partial logic to unlock with more time.
