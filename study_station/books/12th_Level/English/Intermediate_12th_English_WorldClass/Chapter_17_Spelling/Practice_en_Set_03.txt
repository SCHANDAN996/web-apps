51. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Receive (c) Riceive (d) Receeve
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Recieve" is the classic trap because we hear the "ee" sound first, but the rule is "i before e except after c" — after C comes EI. So the answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

52. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Beleive (b) Belive (c) Believe (d) Beleeve
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Beleive" tempts those who over-apply "except after c" — but there is no C here, so plain IE is used. So the answer is Believe.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

53. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occured (b) Occurrd (c) Ocurred (d) Occurred
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Occured" is tempting because many forget the doubling rule: a stressed final syllable with a single vowel + consonant doubles the consonant (ocCUR → occurred), and the word already has double C. So the answer is Occurred.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

54. Choose the correctly spelt word.
