Card 1
Front: Which is correct — "accomodate" or "accommodate"?
Back: Accommodate. Remember: it has double C and double M — "2 Cars, 2 Motorbikes can be accommodated."

Card 2
Front: What is the correct spelling: "occurence", "occurrence", or "occurance"?
Back: Occurrence — double C, double R, and -ence ending.

Card 3
Front: Mnemonic Card — How do you remember the spelling of "necessary"?
Back: "One Collar, two Sleeves" — one C, double S. A shirt is necessary!

Card 4
Front: Which is correct — "definately" or "definitely"?
Back: Definitely. It comes from "finite" — there is no "a" in definitely.

Card 5
Front: Examiner's Trap Card — "The committee has submitted its report." Spot the spelling error in: "The commitee has submited its report."
Back: Two errors: "commitee" → committee (double M, double T, double E) and "submited" → submitted (double T).

Card 6
Front: What is the correct spelling: "seperate" or "separate"?
Back: Separate. Trick: "There's A RAT in sep-A-rate."

Card 7
Front: Which is correct — "recieve" or "receive"?
Back: Receive. Rule: "I before E, except after C" — after C, write E before I.

Card 8
Front: Name two exceptions to the "I before E except after C" rule.
Back: "Weird" and "seize" — both have E before I without a preceding C.

Card 9
Front: What is the correct spelling: "maintainance" or "maintenance"?
Back: Maintenance. The verb is "maintain" but the noun changes to -tenance.

Card 10
Front: Mnemonic Card — How do you remember "embarrass"?
Back: "I go really red when my sister sings" — double R, double S: emba-RR-a-SS.

Card 11
Front: Which is correct — "existance" or "existence"?
Back: Existence. Words ending in -ist usually take -ence: exist → existence, persist → persistence.

Card 12
Front: Examiner's Trap Card — In exams, "questionnaire" is often misspelled. What is the correct spelling?
Back: Questionnaire — double N. Trap options usually offer "questionaire" (single N).

Card 13
Front: What is the correct spelling: "liason" or "liaison"?
Back: Liaison — remember it has two I's: l-i-A-i-s-o-n.

Card 14
Front: Which is correct — "millenium" or "millennium"?
Back: Millennium — double L and double N (from Latin "mille" = thousand, "annus" = year).

Card 15
Front: What is the correct spelling: "beggining", "begining", or "beginning"?
Back: Beginning — double N. Rule: one-syllable stressed ending consonant doubles before -ing (begin → beginning).

Card 16
Front: Which is correct — "priviledge" or "privilege"?
Back: Privilege. There is no "d" in privilege — a very common trap option.

Card 17
Front: What is the correct spelling: "concientious" or "conscientious"?
Back: Conscientious — it contains "science" inside: con-SCIEN-tious.

Card 18
Front: Which is correct — "guarentee" or "guarantee"?
Back: Guarantee. Trick: "A GUARd gives a guarANtee" — G-U-A-R-A-N-T-E-E.

Card 19
Front: What is the correct spelling: "pronounciation" or "pronunciation"?
Back: Pronunciation. The verb is "pronounce" but the noun drops the second "o" — pro-NUN-ciation.

Card 20
Front: Which is correct — "tommorrow" or "tomorrow"?
Back: Tomorrow — one M, double R. Trick: "Tom or row" — TOM-OR-ROW.
