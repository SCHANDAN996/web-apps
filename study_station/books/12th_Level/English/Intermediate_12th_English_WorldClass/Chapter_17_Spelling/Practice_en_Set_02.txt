26. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Riceive (c) Receive (d) Receeve
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Recieve" is the classic trap that breaks the "i before e except after c" rule. After "c", "e" comes before "i", so the answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

27. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occurred (b) Ocurred (c) Occured (d) Occureed
Answer: (a)
Solution: Verbs ending in a single consonant after a stressed vowel double the consonant: occur → occurred. "Occured" with one "r" is the common trap, so the answer is Occurred.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

28. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Seperate (b) Separate (c) Saparate (d) Separite
Answer: (b)
Solution: Remember "there is A RAT in sepARATe". "Seperate" with an "e" is the most frequent error, so the answer is Separate.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

29. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Definately (b) Definitly (c) Definitely (d) Defenitely
Answer: (c)
Solution: The word is built from "finite": de + finite + ly = definitely. "Definately" with an "a" is wrong, so the answer is Definitely.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

30. Choose the correctly spelt word.

31. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Begining (b) Biginning (c) Beginninng (d) Beginning

32. Choose the correctly spelt word.
