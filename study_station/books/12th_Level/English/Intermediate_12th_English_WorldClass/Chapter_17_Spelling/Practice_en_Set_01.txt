Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Receive (c) Riceive (d) Receeve
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Recieve" is the classic trap because we hear "ee" and write "ie" in the wrong order. The rule "i before e except after c" applies here: after C we write EI, so the answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

2. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occured (b) Ocurred (c) Occurrd (d) Occurred
Answer: (d)
Solution: Verbs ending in a single consonant after a stressed vowel double the consonant before "-ed": occur → occurred. "Occured" tempts because the doubling is easy to forget, so the answer is Occurred.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

3. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Separate (b) Seperate (c) Saparate (d) Seprate
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Seperate" is the most common misspelling in exams; remember "there is A RAT in sepARATe". The middle vowel is A, not E, so the answer is Separate.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

4. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Definately (b) Definitly (c) Definitely (d) Defenitely
Answer: (c)
Solution: The word is built from "finite": de + finite + ly = definitely. "Definately" tempts because of the spoken sound, but there is no A in it, so the answer is Definitely.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

5. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Government (b) Goverment (c) Governmant (d) Govarnment
Answer: (a)
Solution: The word keeps the full verb "govern" and adds "-ment": govern + ment = government. "Goverment" drops the N, which is the trap, so the answer is Government.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

6. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Enviroment (b) Envirnment (c) Environment (d) Invironment
Answer: (c)
Solution: Like "government", this word keeps the N before "-ment": environ + ment = environment. "Enviroment" is the tempting short form, so the answer is Environment.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

7. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Beginner (b) Beginer (c) Begginer (d) Beginnar
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Begin" has a stressed final syllable with a single vowel + single consonant, so the N doubles: beginner. "Beginer" tempts by dropping the double letter, so the answer is Beginner.

13. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Writting (b) Wreting (c) Writeing (d) Writing
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Write" drops the silent E before "-ing" and does NOT double the T (the vowel is long): writing. "Writting" tempts by analogy with "sitting", so the answer is Writing.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

14. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Arguement (b) Argument (c) Arguemant (d) Argment

15. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Maintenance (b) Maintenence (c) Maintainance (d) Maintenanse
