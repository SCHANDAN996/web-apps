101. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occurence (b) Ocurrence (c) Occurrence (d) Occurrance
Answer: (c)
Solution: Occurence tempts with a single r, but the verb occur doubles the r before -ence. The correct spelling is occurrence.
