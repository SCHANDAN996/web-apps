26. Choose the correct pronoun: "_____ am going to the market."
(a) Me (b) Mine (c) I (d) Myself
Answer: (c)
Solution: The subject of a verb takes the nominative case, so "I" is correct. "Me" is objective case and cannot be a subject.

27. Choose the correct pronoun: "This book is _____."
(a) her (b) hers (c) she (d) herself
Answer: (b)
Solution: A possessive pronoun that stands alone (without a noun after it) is needed, so "hers" is correct. "Her" is a possessive adjective and must be followed by a noun.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

28. Choose the correct pronoun: "The dog wagged _____ tail."
(a) it's (b) its (c) it (d) itself
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Its" is the possessive form of "it". "It's" means "it is", which makes no sense here — a classic examiner trap.

29. Choose the correct pronoun: "Between you and _____, this plan will fail."
(a) I (b) mine (c) me (d) myself
Answer: (c)
Solution: After a preposition like "between", the objective case "me" is required. "Between you and I" is a very common error tested in exams.

30. Choose the correct pronoun: "She hurt _____ while cutting vegetables."
(a) her (b) hers (c) she (d) herself
Answer: (d)
Solution: When the subject and object are the same person, a reflexive pronoun is used, so "herself" is correct.

31. Choose the correct pronoun: "_____ of the two boys is taller?"
(a) Who (b) Whom (c) Whose (d) Which
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Which" is used when choosing from a limited, known group (the two boys). "Who" is used for open-ended identity questions.

32. Choose the correct pronoun: "Each of the students has submitted _____ assignment."
(a) their (b) his (c) them (d) they

Solution: "Each" is singular, so it takes a singular pronoun "his". "Their" with "each" is a frequent trap in error-spotting questions.

33. Choose the correct pronoun: "It was _____ who called you yesterday."
(a) him (b) his (c) he (d) himself
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "It was", the nominative case "he" is used in formal English because the pronoun complements the subject "it".

34. Choose the correct pronoun: "One should do _____ duty honestly."
(a) his (b) our (c) their (d) one's
Answer: (d)
Solution: The pronoun "one" must be followed by "one's", not "his" or "their". Mixing "one" with "his" is a standard exam trap.

40. Choose the correct pronoun: "_____ of these pens is yours?"

Solution: Selection from a definite set (these pens) takes "which". "Who" refers to persons, not objects.

41. Spot the error: "Every student and every teacher (P) / have submitted (Q) / their forms on time. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Every student and every teacher" joined by "and" still takes a singular verb when "every" precedes each noun, so Part Q should be "has submitted". Examiners trap candidates who assume "and" always makes the subject plural.

(a) are (b) is (c) was (d) has been
Answer: (a)
Solution: In "one of those who...", the relative pronoun "who" refers to the plural "students", so the plural verb "are" follows. This is a classic agreement trap.

43. Choose the correct pronoun: "The committee has submitted _____ report."

47. Spot the error: "The Ganga is (P) / one of the longest river (Q) / in India. (R) / No error (S)"

48. Choose the correct pronoun: "_____ is no use crying over spilt milk."
(a) It (b) There (c) This (d) That
Answer: (a)
Solution: "It" is used as a preparatory subject in expressions like "It is no use...". "There" would change the meaning of the sentence.

(a) one another (b) themselves (c) each other (d) one other
Answer: (c)
Solution: Traditionally, "each other" is used for two persons and "one another" for more than two. Since there are two brothers, "each other" fits best.
