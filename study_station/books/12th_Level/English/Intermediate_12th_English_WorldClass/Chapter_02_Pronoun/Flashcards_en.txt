Card 1
Front: What is a pronoun?
Back: A word that replaces a noun (e.g., he, she, it, they).

Card 2
Front: Which pronoun is used for a boy or a man?
Back: He.

Card 3
Front: Which pronoun is used for a girl or a woman?
Back: She.

Card 4
Front: Which pronoun is used for a group of people?
Back: They.

Card 5
Front: Which pronoun is used for a book or a pen?
Back: It.

Card 6
Front: Which pronoun is used for a country?
Back: It (e.g., India is famous for its culture).

Card 7
Front: Which pronoun is used for a baby whose gender is not known?
Back: It.

Card 8
Front: Which pronoun is used for an animal like a dog or a cat?
Back: It (he/she may be used for pets when the gender is known).

Card 9
Front: What type of pronoun is "myself"?
Back: A reflexive pronoun.

Card 10
Front: What type of pronoun is "who"?
Back: An interrogative pronoun (used to ask questions).

Card 11
Front: What type of pronoun is "this"?
Back: A demonstrative pronoun (this, that, these, those).

Card 12
Front: What type of pronoun is "each"?
Back: A distributive pronoun (each, either, neither).

Card 13
Front: What type of pronoun is "one another"?
Back: A reciprocal pronoun (each other, one another).

Card 14
Front: What type of pronoun is "somebody"?
Back: An indefinite pronoun (somebody, anyone, everything).

Card 15
Front: Which pronoun shows possession in "This book is mine"?
Back: Mine — a possessive pronoun.

Card 16
Front: What is the object form of "I"?
Back: Me.

Card 17
Front: What is the object form of "they"?
Back: Them.

Card 18
Front: Which pronoun is used for a ship in traditional English?
Back: She (traditionally), though "it" is also correct in modern usage.

Card 19
Front: In "The boy who won the race is my friend," what is "who"?
Back: A relative pronoun (who, whom, whose, which, that).

Card 20
Front: Which pronoun is correct: "Ravi and ___ went to school" (I/me)?
Back: I — because it is the subject of the sentence.
