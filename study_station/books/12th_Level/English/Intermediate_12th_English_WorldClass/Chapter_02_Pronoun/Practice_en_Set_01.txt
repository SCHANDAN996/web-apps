Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the correct pronoun: "Ravi and ___ went to the market."
(a) me (b) myself (c) I (d) mine
Answer: (c)
Solution: The pronoun is part of the subject of the verb "went", so the subjective case "I" is needed. "Me" is objective and cannot act as a subject.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. Choose the correct pronoun: "This book belongs to ___."
(a) she (b) hers (c) her (d) herself
Answer: (c)
Solution: After the preposition "to", the objective case is required, so "her" is correct. "She" is subjective and "hers" is possessive, neither fits after a preposition.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

3. Choose the correct pronoun: "Each of the boys has done ___ homework."
(a) their (b) they (c) them (d) his
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Each" is singular, so it takes a singular pronoun "his". "Their" is a common trap but is plural and does not agree with "each".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

4. Choose the correct pronoun: "The dog wagged ___ tail."
(a) its (b) it's (c) their (d) his
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Its" is the possessive form of "it". "It's" means "it is" and is the most common examiner trap here.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

5. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ is my best friend."
(a) She (b) Hers (c) Her (d) Herself
Answer: (a)
Solution: The blank is the subject of the verb "is", so the subjective pronoun "She" is correct. "Her" is objective and "Hers" is possessive.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

6. Choose the correct pronoun: "Between you and ___, this plan will fail."
(a) me (b) I (c) myself (d) mine
Answer: (a)
Solution: After the preposition "between", the objective case "me" is required. "Between you and I" is a classic sound-right trap examiners love.
Source: PYQ-style

7. Choose the correct pronoun: "Nobody came to the party, did ___?"
(a) they (b) he (c) nobody (d) it
Answer: (a)
Solution: In question tags, "nobody/anybody/somebody" take "they" in modern standard English. The tag is also positive because "nobody" is negative in sense.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

8. Choose the correct pronoun: "One should do ___ duty honestly."
(a) his (b) their (c) your (d) one's
Answer: (d)
Solution: The pronoun "one" must be followed by "one's", not "his" or "their". Mixing "one" with "his" is a frequent error-spotting trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

9. Choose the correct pronoun: "This is the boy ___ won the prize."
(a) which (b) who (c) whom (d) whose
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Who" is used as the subject of a relative clause referring to a person. "Which" is for things and "whom" is objective.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

10. Choose the correct pronoun: "The teacher praised the girl ___ answer was correct."
(a) who (b) whose (c) whom (d) which
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Whose" shows possession — the answer belongs to the girl. "Who" would be the subject, but here we need a possessive relative pronoun.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

11. Choose the correct pronoun: "I hurt ___ while cutting vegetables."
(a) me (b) mine (c) I (d) myself
Answer: (d)
Solution: When the subject and object are the same person, a reflexive pronoun is used — "myself". "Me" cannot refer back to the subject "I".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

12. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ of these two shirts do you like?"
(a) What (b) Which (c) Whose (d) Whom
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Which" is used when choosing from a limited, known set (two shirts). "What" is used for open-ended choices.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

13. Choose the correct pronoun: "It is ___ who called you yesterday."
(a) me (b) I (c) mine (d) myself
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "It is", formal grammar uses the subjective case — "It is I". "It is me" is common in speech but is the trap in error-spotting questions.
Source: PYQ-style

14. Choose the correct pronoun: "Neither of the two brothers has brought ___ books."
(a) his (b) their (c) them (d) they
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Neither" is singular, so it takes the singular pronoun "his". "Their" is the attractive but wrong plural option.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

15. Choose the correct pronoun: "The committee has submitted ___ report."
(a) its (b) their (c) his (d) them
Answer: (a)
Solution: A collective noun acting as one unit takes the singular pronoun "its". "Their" would be correct only if the members acted separately.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

16. Choose the correct pronoun: "Let ___ go to the library now."
(a) we (b) our (c) us (d) ours
Answer: (c)
Solution: After the verb "let", the objective case "us" is used. "We" is subjective and cannot be the object of "let".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

17. Choose the correct pronoun: "He is taller than ___."
(a) me (b) I (c) mine (d) myself
Answer: (b)
Solution: In formal grammar, "than" is a conjunction, so the subjective "I" follows (than I am). "Than me" is informal and often marked wrong in exams.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

18. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ book is this on the table?"
(a) Who (b) Whom (c) Whose (d) Which
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Whose" asks about possession — to whom the book belongs. "Who" and "whom" cannot come before a noun like this.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

19. Choose the correct pronoun: "The students ___ were absent must submit leave applications."
(a) which (b) whose (c) who (d) whom
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Who" refers to persons and acts as the subject of "were absent". "Which" is used for things, not people.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

20. Choose the correct pronoun: "Both of them enjoyed ___ at the picnic."
(a) themself (b) theirself (c) theirselves (d) themselves
Answer: (d)
Solution: The correct reflexive pronoun for "they/them" is "themselves". "Theirselves" and "themself" are non-standard forms used as traps.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

21. Choose the correct pronoun: "It was she ___ helped the old man cross the road."
(a) who (b) whom (c) which (d) whose
Answer: (a)
Solution: In the cleft sentence "It was she who…", "who" is the subject of "helped". "Whom" is tempting after a pronoun but is objective and wrong here.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Choose the correct pronoun: "Avinash is one of those students who ___ always punctual."
(a) is (b) are (c) was (d) has been
Answer: (b)
Solution: The relative pronoun "who" refers to "students" (plural), not "one", so the verb is "are". Choosing "is" by matching "one" is the classic trap.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Choose the correct pronoun: "The manager, along with his assistants, has completed ___ work."
(a) their (b) our (c) its (d) his
Answer: (d)
Solution: The true subject is "the manager" (singular); "along with his assistants" is only an intervening phrase. The pronoun must agree with "manager" — "his".
Source: PYQ-style

24. Choose the correct pronoun: "There is no love lost between you and ___."
(a) I (b) mine (c) me (d) myself
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Between" is a preposition and must be followed by the objective case "me". "Between you and I" sounds correct but is grammatically wrong.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Choose the correct pronoun: "Everyone should respect ___ parents."
(a) their (b) its (c) one's (d) his
Answer: (d)
Solution: In traditional exam grammar, "everyone" is singular and takes "his". "Their" is increasingly accepted in modern usage but is the trap option in SSC-style papers.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
