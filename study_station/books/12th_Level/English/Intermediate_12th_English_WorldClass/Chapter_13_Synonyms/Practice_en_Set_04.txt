76. Choose the word closest in meaning to ABUNDANT.
(a) Scarce (b) Costly (c) Plentiful (d) Rare
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Scarce" and "rare" are tempting because they describe quantity, but they mean the opposite of abundant. Abundant means existing in large quantity, i.e. plentiful, so the answer is Plentiful.
Source: PYQ-style
