51. Choose the synonym of ABANDON.
(a) Forsake (b) Keep (c) Hold (d) Protect
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Keep" tempts because it is the opposite idea students often confuse in haste, but the question asks for a similar meaning. Abandon means to give up or leave completely, which matches "forsake", so the answer is Forsake.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

52. Choose the synonym of BRAVE.
(a) Courageous (b) Fearful (c) Timid (d) Weak
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Timid" and "fearful" are antonyms and trap careless readers. Brave means showing courage, so the answer is Courageous.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Choose the synonym of BEGIN.
(a) End (b) Close (c) Finish (d) Commence
Answer: (d)
Solution: "End" and "finish" are opposites and attract quick guessers. Begin means to start, and "commence" is its formal synonym, so the answer is Commence.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English
