101. Choose the word closest in meaning to: ABANDON
(a) cherish (b) maintain (c) forsake (d) guard
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Cherish" and "guard" are near-antonyms that tempt students who confuse the word with "defend". To abandon is to give up or leave completely, so the answer is forsake.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

102. Choose the word closest in meaning to: CANDID
(a) frank (b) timid (c) clever (d) secretive
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Clever" tempts because candid people often sound smart, but the word means open and truthful in speech. The closest synonym is frank.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Choose the word closest in meaning to: OBSOLETE
(a) obscure (b) outdated (c) obstinate (d) obvious
Answer: (b)
Solution: All four options begin with "ob-", a classic sound-alike trap. Obsolete means no longer in use, so the answer is outdated.

106. Choose the word closest in meaning to: METICULOUS
(a) careless (b) hasty (c) painstaking (d) fearful
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Fearful" is a trap because meticulous contains the root "metus" (fear) — originally "fearful of error". In modern usage it means showing great attention to detail, so the answer is painstaking.

Answer: (b)
Solution: "Bloodthirsty" is the classic trap because "sanguine" relates to blood (Latin "sanguis"), and in old medicine a blood-rich temperament was cheerful. In modern English it means hopeful, so the answer is optimistic.
