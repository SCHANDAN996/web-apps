Card 1
Front: What is a synonym?
Back: A word that has the same or nearly the same meaning as another word. Example: "Happy" and "Joyful".

Card 2
Front: Give the synonym of "Abandon".
Back: Forsake / Leave / Desert. (Trap: "Abundant" means plentiful — it is NOT related.)

Card 3
Front: Mnemonic: How to remember "Candid = Frank/Honest"?
Back: Think of a "CANDID camera" — it captures people as they truly are, honestly, without pretence.

Card 4
Front: What is the synonym of "Benevolent"?
Back: Kind / Generous / Charitable. Root: "bene" (good) + "volent" (wishing) = wishing good.

Card 5
Front: Examiner's Trap: "Enormous" — is its synonym "Tiny" or "Huge"?
Back: Huge. Trap: Students confuse it with "enormity" debates or misread under time pressure. Enormous = very large.

Card 6
Front: Give the synonym of "Obsolete".
Back: Outdated / Antiquated / No longer in use. Example: Typewriters are obsolete.

Card 7
Front: What does the root "Mal-" mean? Give two words.
Back: "Mal-" = bad/evil. Words: Malice (ill will), Malnutrition (bad nutrition), Malevolent (wishing harm).

Card 8
Front: Synonym of "Pragmatic"?
Back: Practical / Realistic / Sensible. (Opposite: Idealistic.)

Card 9
Front: Mnemonic: How to remember "Timid = Shy/Fearful"?
Back: "TIMID people hide behind TIMe" — a timid person avoids the spotlight. Or link: Timid → Tim (a scared little boy).

Card 10
Front: Examiner's Trap: "Censure" vs "Censor" — which means "to criticise strongly"?
Back: Censure = strong criticism/condemnation. Censor = to suppress/remove content. Examiners swap these to trap you.

Card 11
Front: Synonym of "Ephemeral"?
Back: Short-lived / Temporary / Fleeting. Example: Fame on social media is often ephemeral.

Card 12
Front: What is the synonym of "Diligent"?
Back: Hardworking / Industrious / Conscientious.

Card 13
Front: Root "Phil-" means what? Give derived words.
Back: "Phil-" = love. Words: Philanthropy (love of humanity), Bibliophile (book lover), Philosophy (love of wisdom).

Card 14
Front: Synonym of "Lucid"?
Back: Clear / Easy to understand / Transparent (in meaning). Root: "luc" = light.

Card 15
Front: What is the synonym of "Reluctant"?
Back: Unwilling / Hesitant / Disinclined.

Card 16
Front: Examiner's Trap: "Apathy" — does it mean sympathy or indifference?
Back: Indifference / lack of interest. Trap: It looks like "sympathy/empathy" but means the opposite. Root: "a-" (without) + "pathos" (feeling).

Card 17
Front: Synonym of "Augment"?
Back: Increase / Enhance / Add to. (Trap: "Argument" is unrelated — spelling similarity trap.)

Card 18
Front: What is the synonym of "Frugal"?
Back: Thrifty / Economical / Careful with money. (Not "miserly" — frugal is positive, miserly is negative.)

Card 19
Front: Root "Cred-" means what? Give derived words.
Back: "Cred-" = believe. Words: Credible (believable), Incredible (unbelievable), Credulous (too ready to believe).

Card 20
Front: Synonym of "Resilient"?
Back: Tough / Able to recover quickly / Flexible. Example: The Indian economy proved resilient after the crisis.
