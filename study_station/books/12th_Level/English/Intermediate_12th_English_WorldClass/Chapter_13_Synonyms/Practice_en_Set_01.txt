1. Choose the word closest in meaning to ABANDON.
(a) Keep (b) Forsake (c) Protect (d) Repair
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Keep" and "protect" are near-opposites, and "repair" is unrelated. To abandon means to give up or leave completely, so the answer is "Forsake".
