# 📋 Important Rules & Tips — Synonyms

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | A synonym must match the **part of speech** of the given word. | "Abandon" (verb) → "Leave" (verb), not "Abandonment" (noun) | Options include the same root in a different form (noun/adjective) to lure you. |
| 2 | Match the **shade of meaning**, not just the broad idea. | "Frugal" = careful with money (positive); "Miserly" = stingy (negative) | Two options look similar; only one matches the tone (positive/negative/neutral). |
| 3 | **Context decides** when a word has multiple meanings. | "Fair" → "Just" (justice) or "Pale" (skin) — read the sentence | The most common meaning is offered even when the sentence needs the rare one. |
| 4 | **Connotation matters**: positive, negative, or neutral. | "Slim" (+), "Skinny" (−), "Thin" (neutral) | A negative word is given as synonym of a positive word. |
| 5 | Learn words in **clusters**, not isolation. | Happy → Glad, Joyful, Elated, Delighted, Content | Options mix cluster members with a near-antonym ("Gloomy"). |
| 6 | **Degree/intensity** must match. | "Angry" → "Furious" is stronger; "Annoyed" is weaker | A word of the same family but wrong intensity is planted. |
| 7 | **Formal vs. informal** register must match. | "Purchase" (formal) ≈ "Buy" (neutral); "Kids" (informal) ≈ "Children" | A slang/colloquial option is offered for a formal word. |
| 8 | Use **Greek/Latin roots** to decode unknown words. | "Bene" = good → Benevolent, Benefit, Benediction | A word with the same spelling start but different root ("Belligerent" — *bellum* = war). |
| 9 | **Prefix changes meaning**, not always to opposite. | "Disinterested" = impartial; "Uninterested" = bored | These two are swapped deliberately — a classic trap. |
| 10 | Beware of **false friends** (sound-alike words). | "Compliment" (praise) vs. "Complement" (complete) | The sound-alike word is placed as an option. |
| 11 | **Phrasal/idiomatic words** need exact equivalents. | "Give up" = "Quit/Abandon", not "Donate" | A literal word-by-word meaning is offered. |
| 12 | When unsure, **eliminate antonyms and unrelated words first**. | For "Candid": eliminate "Secretive", "Biased" → choose "Frank" | Two options remain close; re-check connotation (Rule 4) before final answer. |

## (b) Time Management — The 15/45-Second Rule

| Type | What it looks like | Time | Strategy |
|------|-------------------|------|----------|
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Direct one-word synonym: "Synonym of CANDID?" — you either know it or you don't | 15 seconds | Answer instantly if known. If unknown, try root/prefix decoding once, then mark and move on. Never guess-stare. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Synonym of a word **used in a sentence**, or a word with multiple meanings/shades: "Choose the synonym of FAIR as used in the sentence." | 45 seconds | Read the sentence → identify part of speech and tone → eliminate antonyms → match connotation → answer. |

**Rule of thumb:** If you cannot recall a direct synonym within 15 seconds, no amount of extra staring will help — flag it and return only if time remains.

## (c) Morphology — Root, Affix, Example

| Root / Affix | Origin & Meaning | Derived Words (Synonym clue) |
|--------------|------------------|------------------------------|
| **Bene / Bon** | Latin — good | Benevolent (kind), Benefit (advantage), Bonus (extra), Bonafide (genuine) |
| **Mal** | Latin — bad | Malicious (spiteful), Malady (illness), Malpractice (misconduct) |
| **Dict** | Latin — say/speak | Predict (foretell), Dictate (command), Verdict (judgement) |
| **Port** | Latin — carry | Transport (carry across), Export, Portable (carryable) |
| **Cred** | Latin — believe | Credible (believable), Credulous (gullible), Incredible (unbelievable) |
| **Anti-** (prefix) | Greek — against | Antipathy (dislike), Antidote (remedy), Antagonist (opponent) |
| **Syn- / Sym-** (prefix) | Greek — together | Sympathy (compassion), Synchronize (coincide), Synthesis (combination) |
| **-phobia** (suffix) | Greek — fear | Claustrophobia (fear of closed spaces), Xenophobia (fear of foreigners) |
| **-cide** (suffix) | Latin — kill | Homicide (murder), Pesticide, Suicide (self-killing) |
| **Omni-** (prefix) | Latin — all | Omnipotent (all-powerful), Omniscient (all-knowing), Omnipresent (present everywhere) |

**How to use this in the exam:** Unknown word? Break it → *Mal + volent* = "bad + wishing" → synonym must be negative → eliminate all positive options → pick "Spiteful".
