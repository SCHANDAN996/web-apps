26. Which of the following is a proper noun?
(a) city (b) river (c) mountain (d) Ganga
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Ganga" names a specific river, so it is a proper noun and is capitalised. The other three are common nouns naming a general class.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

27. Identify the abstract noun in the sentence: "Honesty is the best policy."
(a) best (b) policy (c) Honesty (d) is
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Honesty" is a quality that cannot be seen or touched, so it is an abstract noun. "Policy" here is a common noun.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

28. The plural of "child" is:
(a) childs (b) childes (c) childrens (d) children
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Child" has an irregular plural "children". "Childrens" is doubly wrong because children is already plural.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

29. Which word is a collective noun?
(a) soldiers (b) brave (c) army (d) fight
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Army" denotes a group of soldiers taken as one unit, so it is a collective noun. "Soldiers" is an ordinary plural common noun.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

30. The plural of "knife" is:
(a) knifes (b) knife (c) knives (d) knivies
Answer: (c)
Solution: Nouns ending in -fe usually change to -ves: knife → knives, wife → wives. "Knifes" is a common examiner trap.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

31. Identify the material noun: "This ring is made of gold."
(a) ring (b) made (c) gold (d) this
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Gold" is a substance or raw material, hence a material noun. "Ring" is a common noun (a finished object).
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

32. Which of the following nouns is always singular and takes a singular verb?
(a) news (b) scissors (c) trousers (d) spectacles
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" looks plural but is singular: "The news is good." Scissors, trousers and spectacles are plural-only nouns.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

33. The plural of "phenomenon" is:
(a) phenomenons (b) phenomena (c) phenomenas (d) phenomenones
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Phenomenon" is a Greek word; its plural is "phenomena". Similarly: criterion → criteria.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

34. Choose the correctly spelt plural:
(a) tomatos (b) tomato (c) tomaties (d) tomatoes
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nouns ending in -o preceded by a consonant usually add -es: tomato → tomatoes, hero → heroes. (Exceptions: photo, piano.)
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

35. "A ______ of cattle was grazing in the field."
(a) herd (b) swarm (c) flock (d) pack
Answer: (a)
Solution: The standard collective term for cattle is "herd". Swarm is for bees, flock for sheep/birds, pack for wolves or hounds.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

36. Which sentence uses the possessive (apostrophe) correctly?
(a) The boys school is nearby. (b) The boy's school is nearby. (c) The boys' school is nearby (one boy). (d) The boys's school is nearby.
Answer: (b)
Solution: For one boy, the possessive is boy's. Option (c) would mean several boys, contradicting "one boy"; boys's is never correct.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

37. "Furniture" in English grammar is:
(a) countable — we say "furnitures" (b) a proper noun (c) always plural (d) uncountable — no plural form
Answer: (d)
Solution: Furniture is uncountable: "some furniture", "a piece of furniture" — never "furnitures". Same rule applies to luggage, information, advice.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

38. The plural of "ox" is:
(a) oxes (b) oxies (c) ox (d) oxen
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Ox" has the old English plural "oxen". This irregular form is a favourite in SSC and Railway exams.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

39. Identify the countable noun:
(a) book (b) milk (c) water (d) rice
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Book" can be counted — one book, two books. Water, milk and rice are uncountable and take "much", not "many".
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

40. "The ______ of the building is very strong." (base/foundation)
(a) foundation (b) baseless (c) founded (d) founding
Answer: (a)
Solution: Only "foundation" is a noun that fits the blank after "The". "Founded" and "founding" are verb forms; "baseless" is an adjective.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

41. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The sceneries of Kashmir are beautiful. (b) A scenery of Kashmir is beautiful. (c) The scenery of Kashmir are beautiful. (d) The scenery of Kashmir is beautiful.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Scenery" is uncountable and singular — no plural, no "a", and it takes a singular verb. This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He gave me many advices. (b) He gave me a piece of advice. (c) He gave me much advices. (d) He gave me an advice.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Advice" is uncountable; we count it with "a piece of advice". "Advices" and "an advice" are wrong. (Note: "advise" is the verb.)
Source: PYQ-style

43. Spot the error: "One of my friend (P) / has gone (Q) / to Delhi. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "one of" we need a plural noun: "one of my friends". The verb stays singular (has), so Part Q is correct — a typical trap.
Source: PYQ-style

44. "The committee ______ divided in their opinions." Choose the correct verb:
(a) is (b) were (c) was (d) has been
Answer: (b)
Solution: When a collective noun's members act separately (divided opinions), it takes a plural verb — "were". If acting as one unit, use singular.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

45. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The police have arrested the thief. (b) The police has arrested the thief. (c) The police is arresting the thief yesterday. (d) The polices have arrested the thief.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Police" is a plural-only noun and always takes a plural verb: "The police have…". There is no word "polices" in this sense.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Spot the error: "Mathematics (P) / are (Q) / a difficult subject. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Names of subjects ending in -ics (Mathematics, Physics, Economics) are singular: "Mathematics is…". Part Q should be "is".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

47. "She has bought two ______ of bread."
(a) breads (b) loaves (c) loafs (d) sliceses
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bread is uncountable, so we count it in loaves or slices. Loaf → loaves (f → ves). "Breads" and "loafs" are incorrect.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

48. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) My hairs are black. (b) My hair are black. (c) My hair is black. (d) My all hairs is black.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Hair" meaning all the hair on the head is uncountable and singular: "My hair is black." "Hairs" is used only for a few individual strands.
Source: PYQ-style

49. Spot the error: "The Ganges (P) / are (Q) / a holy river. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Names of rivers with a plural-looking form (The Ganges) take a singular verb: "The Ganges is…". Part Q should be "is".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

50. "He is a man of few ______."
(a) word (b) wording (c) words (d) worded
Answer: (c)
Solution: The fixed idiom is "a man of few words" (one who speaks little). The plural noun "words" is required here.
Source: PYQ-style
