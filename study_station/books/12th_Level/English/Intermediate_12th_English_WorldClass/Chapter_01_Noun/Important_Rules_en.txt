# 📋 Important Grammar Rules & Tips — Noun

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Uncountable nouns** (advice, information, furniture, luggage, news, bread, scenery, machinery, stationery, crockery) take **singular verb** and **no 'a/an' or plural -s**. | The information **is** correct. / He gave me **an advice** ❌ → **a piece of advice** ✅ | "He gave me many advices" — looks natural to Hindi speakers but is wrong. |
| 2 | **Plural-looking but singular nouns**: news, physics, mathematics, economics, politics, measles, mumps, billiards, athletics (as a subject). | Mathematics **is** difficult. | "The news are good" — 'news' ends in -s but is singular. |
| 3 | **Singular-looking but plural nouns**: cattle, people, police, poultry, gentry, clergy, peasantry. | The police **have** arrested him. | "The police has come" — police is always plural. |
| 4 | **Nouns same in singular & plural**: sheep, deer, fish, aircraft, species, series, dozen/score/hundred/thousand (when preceded by a number). | Two **sheep** are grazing. / Five **hundred** rupees. | "Five hundreds rupees" — after a numeral, no -s. |
| 5 | **Compound nouns make plural on the main word**: brother**s**-in-law, commander**s**-in-chief, passer**s**-by, maid-servant**s**. | He has two **brothers-in-law**. | "Brother-in-laws" — plural goes on 'brother', not 'law'. |
| 6 | **Collective noun + singular verb** when the group acts as one unit; **plural verb** when members act separately. | The jury **has** given its verdict. / The jury **are** divided in opinion. | "The committee have decided unanimously" — unanimous decision = one unit = singular. |
| 7 | **Possessive case is used mainly with living things**; for non-living things use 'of'. | The **leg of the table** (not "the table's leg"). | "India's population" is accepted idiomatically, but "the room's window" is a trap — prefer "the window of the room". |
| 8 | **Two nouns joined by apostrophe-s**: one 's for joint possession; 's on each for separate possession. | **Ravi and Mohan's** house (one house, jointly owned). / **Ravi's and Mohan's** houses (two houses). | "Ravi's and Mohan's house is big" — two owners + singular 'house' is contradictory. |
| 9 | **'One of the' + plural noun + singular verb.** | One of my **friends has** come. | "One of my friend has come" — noun after 'one of the' must be plural. |
| 10 | **A number of + plural verb; The number of + singular verb.** | **A number of** students **were** absent. / **The number of** students **is** fifty. | Classic swap trap — examiners interchange the two. |
| 11 | **Nouns used only in plural**: scissors, trousers, spectacles, goggles, tongs, pliers, wages, premises, proceeds, alms, thanks, riches, outskirts. | The scissors **are** on the table. | "A scissor is sharp" — always plural; use "a pair of scissors" for singular. |
| 12 | **Hair**: singular when seen as a whole; plural (hairs) when counted individually. | Her **hair is** black. / I found two grey **hairs**. | "Her hairs are beautiful" — trap for describing overall hair. |

## (b) Time Management — 15/45-Second Rule

| Type | What it looks like | Time | Strategy |
|------|-------------------|------|----------|
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Direct rule recall: plural of a noun, countable/uncountable check, 'one of the + plural noun', possessive apostrophe placement. | 15 sec | Spot the keyword (news, police, one of, a number of) → apply the rule instantly → mark and move. Never re-read. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Collective noun verb agreement (unit vs. divided), joint vs. separate possession, error hidden inside a long intervening phrase, sentence with two possible noun errors. | 45 sec | Strip the sentence to subject + verb, ignore the intervening phrase, then test each option against the rule. If still stuck after 45 sec, mark for review and skip. |

**Rule of thumb:** If the answer depends on a single memorised rule → 15 seconds. If you must first *decide which rule applies* → 45 seconds.

## (c) Morphology — Roots & Affixes that Build Nouns

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Noun Formed | Example Sentence |
|--------------|---------|-------------|------------------|
| **-tion / -sion** (suffix) | act or state of | decide → deci**sion**; educate → educa**tion** | His decision was final. |
| **-ness** (suffix) | state or quality | kind → kind**ness**; dark → dark**ness** | Kindness costs nothing. |
| **-ity** (suffix) | state or condition | able → abil**ity**; pure → pur**ity** | Purity of water matters. |
| **-ment** (suffix) | result or action | govern → govern**ment**; move → move**ment** | The movement gained support. |
| **-er / -or / -ar** (suffix) | one who does | teach → teach**er**; act → act**or**; beg → begg**ar** | The actor won an award. |
| **-ist** (suffix) | person skilled in | art → art**ist**; science → scient**ist** | The scientist published a paper. |
| **-ship** (suffix) | state, rank, skill | friend → friend**ship**; leader → leader**ship** | Their friendship lasted years. |
| **-dom** (suffix) | state or domain | free → free**dom**; king → king**dom** | Freedom is precious. |
| **-age** (suffix) | collection or state | marry → marri**age**; bag → bagg**age** | Keep your baggage safe. |
| **-al** (suffix) | act of | arrive → arriv**al**; refuse → refus**al** | His arrival was delayed. |

**Exam tip:** In error-spotting, check whether the suffix matches the word class the sentence needs — e.g., "His **decide** was wrong" ❌ → "His **decision** was wrong" ✅.
