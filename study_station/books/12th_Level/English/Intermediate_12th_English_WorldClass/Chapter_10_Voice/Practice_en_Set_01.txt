1. Choose the correct passive form: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter has been written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter is written by her.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The active sentence is in simple present tense, so the passive uses "is/are + V3". "Was written" is past tense and "is being written" is continuous, both tempting but wrong. Since the verb is simple present, the answer is "A letter is written by her."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

Answer: (a)
Solution: "Killed" is simple past, so the passive needs "was/were + V3". "Is killed" wrongly shifts to present tense. The snake is singular, so the answer is "The snake was killed by him."
