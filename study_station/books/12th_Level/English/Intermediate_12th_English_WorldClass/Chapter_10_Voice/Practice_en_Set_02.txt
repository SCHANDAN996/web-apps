26. Choose the correct passive form: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter has been written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter is written by her.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option "is being written" is continuous, but the active sentence is simple present ("writes"), so the passive must be simple present: is/am/are + V3. "A letter is written by her" matches the tense, so the answer is "A letter is written by her."
