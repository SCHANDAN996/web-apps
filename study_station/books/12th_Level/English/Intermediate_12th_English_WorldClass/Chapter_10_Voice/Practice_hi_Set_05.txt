101. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."
(a) The documents will signed by the manager tomorrow. (b) The documents would be signed by the manager tomorrow. (c) The documents will be sign by the manager tomorrow. (d) The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प past participle से पहले "be" गिरा देता है, जबकि future passive के लिए "will be + V3" चाहिए। "Sign" और "signed" बिना "be" के व्याकरणिक रूप से अधूरे हैं, इसलिए उत्तर है "The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

102. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "Someone has stolen my wallet."
(a) My wallet was been stolen. (b) My wallet is stolen. (c) My wallet has been stolen. (d) My wallet has stole.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Present perfect active कर्मणि में "has/have been + V3" बनता है। "Was been" दोहरी auxiliary की गलती है और "has stole" में गलत participle है, इसलिए उत्तर है "My wallet has been stolen."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

103. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "The teacher is checking the answer sheets."
(a) The answer sheets are checked by the teacher. (b) The answer sheets have been checked by the teacher. (c) The answer sheets are being check by the teacher. (d) The answer sheets are being checked by the teacher.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present continuous passive के लिए "is/are being + V3" चाहिए; "being" गिराने से यह simple present बन जाता है, और "being check" में participle की जगह base form है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The answer sheets are being checked by the teacher."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

104. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "They were repairing the bridge when the storm hit."
(a) The bridge was repaired when the storm hit. (b) The bridge had being repaired when the storm hit. (c) The bridge was being repair when the storm hit. (d) The bridge was being repaired when the storm hit.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Past continuous passive "was/were being + V3" होता है। "Was repaired" जारी होने का भाव खो देता है और "had being" perfect और continuous auxiliaries को गलत तरीके से मिलाता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The bridge was being repaired when the storm hit."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

105. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "You must obey the rules."
(a) The rules must obeyed by you. (b) The rules must be obeyed. (c) The rules must be obey by you. (d) The rules must been obeyed.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Modal passive "modal + be + V3" होता है; "must obeyed" और "must been" इस पैटर्न को तोड़ते हैं। बाध्यता बताने वाले modals में कर्ता "by you" अनावश्यक होने से आमतौर पर हटा दिया जाता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The rules must be obeyed."

106. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "People speak English all over the world."

107. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "The jury gave him a fair trial."
(a) He was given a fair trial by the jury. (b) He is given a fair trial by the jury. (c) A fair trial is gave to him by the jury. (d) He was gave a fair trial by the jury.

108. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "Open the window."

109. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "Who taught you French?"
