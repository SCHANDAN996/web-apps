Card 1
Front: What is the basic formula for converting Active to Passive voice?
Back: Object of active becomes subject + appropriate form of "be" + V3 (past participle) + "by" + subject of active. Example: "She writes a letter" → "A letter is written by her."

Card 2
Front: Convert to passive: "He teaches English." (Present Simple)
Back: "English is taught by him." Present Simple passive = is/am/are + V3.

Card 3
Front: What is the passive form of Past Continuous? Example: "They were building a bridge."
Back: "A bridge was being built by them." Past Continuous passive = was/were + being + V3.

Card 4
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How do you remember the passive structure for continuous tenses?
Back: Remember "BEING comes in CONTINUOUS" — every continuous tense passive inserts "being": is being, was being, are being. Trick: "Continuous = Being + V3."

Card 5
Front: Convert: "She has completed the work." (Present Perfect)
Back: "The work has been completed by her." Perfect tenses passive = has/have/had + been + V3.

Card 6
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "The letter was wrote by him." — What is the error?
Back: "Wrote" is V2; passive always needs V3. Correct: "The letter was written by him." Trap: examiners swap V2 and V3 in passive sentences.

Card 7
Front: Which tenses CANNOT be changed into passive voice?
Back: Perfect Continuous tenses (Present Perfect Continuous, Past Perfect Continuous, Future Perfect Continuous) and Future Continuous have no standard passive form. Example: "He has been reading" has no passive.

Card 8
Front: Convert: "Open the door." (Imperative sentence)
Back: "Let the door be opened." Imperative passive = Let + object + be + V3.

Card 9
Front: Convert: "She can solve this problem." (Modal verb)
Back: "This problem can be solved by her." Modal passive = modal + be + V3 (can be, should be, must be, may be + V3).

Card 10
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "The work must be finish on time." — Spot the error.
Back: After a modal + be, use V3, not V1. Correct: "The work must be finished on time." Trap: modal passives tempt students to keep the base verb.

Card 11
Front: When should the "by + agent" be dropped in passive voice?
Back: When the agent is unknown, obvious, or unimportant. Example: "My wallet has been stolen" (thief unknown) — no "by" phrase needed.

Card 12
Front: Convert: "People speak English worldwide." (General/universal agent)
Back: "English is spoken worldwide." Drop "by people" — vague agents like people, someone, they, everybody are omitted in passive.

Card 13
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: Which verb takes "to" instead of "by" before the agent in passive?
Back: "Know" takes "to": "He is known to me" (not "known by me"). But verbs like surprise, amaze, and annoy take the normal "by" for the agent: "I was surprised by the news." Trick: "KNOWN TO, but surprised BY!"

Card 14
Front: Convert: "Who taught you English?" (Interrogative with 'who')
Back: "By whom was English taught to you?" or "Who was English taught to you by?" In passive questions, "who" becomes "by whom."

Card 15
Front: Convert: "Someone stole my bike yesterday." (Past Simple)
Back: "My bike was stolen yesterday." Past Simple passive = was/were + V3; agent "someone" is dropped.

Card 16
Front: What happens to double-object verbs in passive? Example: "She gave me a book."
Back: Either object can become the subject: "A book was given to me by her" OR "I was given a book by her." Both are correct — examiners test both forms.

Card 17
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "He was elected the president by the committee." vs "He was elected president." — Which article rule applies?
Back: With verbs like elect, make, appoint, crown — no article before a unique post: "He was elected president." Trap: students insert "the" before unique positions.

Card 18
Front: Convert: "The teacher is respected by the students." → Active voice.
Back: "The students respect the teacher." Reverse the formula: subject of passive becomes object; remove "be + V3" and use the active verb form matching the tense (Present Simple here).

Card 19
Front: Convert: "It is said that he is honest." → Alternative passive with the subject "He."
Back: "He is said to be honest." Reporting verbs (say, believe, think, know, report) allow: "It is said that..." = "Subject + is said + to-infinitive."

Card 20
Front: Quick check: "The match will be won by our team." — Identify tense and passive structure.
Back: Future Simple passive = will + be + V3. Structure: modal "will" + "be" + past participle "won." Active form: "Our team will win the match."
