76. Choose the correct passive form: "She teaches us English."
(a) English is being taught to us by her. (b) English was taught to us by her. (c) English is taught to us by her. (d) English has been taught to us by her.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option uses the continuous form, but the original sentence is simple present, so the passive must also be simple present (is/are + V3), not "is being taught". Simple present "teaches" becomes "is taught", so the answer is "English is taught to us by her."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

77. Choose the correct passive form: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."
(a) The documents will be sign by the manager tomorrow. (b) The documents will signed by the manager tomorrow. (c) The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow. (d) The documents would be signed by the manager tomorrow.
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "will", the passive needs "be + V3"; options dropping "be" or using the base verb "sign" are grammatically wrong. "Will sign" becomes "will be signed", so the answer is "The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow."

79. Choose the correct passive form: "Someone has stolen my bicycle."
(a) My bicycle is been stolen. (b) My bicycle has stolen by someone. (c) My bicycle has been stolen. (d) My bicycle had been stolen by someone.
Answer: (c)
Solution: Present perfect passive needs "has/have been + V3"; "has stolen" without "been" is active in form. Also, when the doer ("someone") is unknown or unimportant, we drop the agent. The correct form is "has been stolen", so the answer is "My bicycle has been stolen."
