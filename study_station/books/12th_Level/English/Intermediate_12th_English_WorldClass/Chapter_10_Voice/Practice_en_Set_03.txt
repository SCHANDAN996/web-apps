51. Choose the correct passive form: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter was written by her. (b) A letter is written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option "was written" wrongly changes the tense. The sentence is in simple present, so the passive uses "is + V3". The correct answer is "A letter is written by her."
