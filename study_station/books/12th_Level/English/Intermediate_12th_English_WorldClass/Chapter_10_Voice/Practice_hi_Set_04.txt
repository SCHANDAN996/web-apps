76. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "She teaches us English."
(a) English is being taught to us by her. (b) English was taught to us by her. (c) English is taught to us by her. (d) English has been taught to us by her.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: लुभाने वाला विकल्प continuous रूप इस्तेमाल करता है, लेकिन मूल वाक्य simple present में है, इसलिए passive भी simple present (is/are + V3) में होना चाहिए, "is being taught" में नहीं। Simple present "teaches" का passive "is taught" बनता है, इसलिए उत्तर "English is taught to us by her." है।

77. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."
(a) The documents will be sign by the manager tomorrow. (b) The documents will signed by the manager tomorrow. (c) The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow. (d) The documents would be signed by the manager tomorrow.

78. सही कर्तृवाच्य (active) रूप चुनिए: "The novel was written by Premchand."

79. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "Someone has stolen my bicycle."
(a) My bicycle is been stolen. (b) My bicycle has stolen by someone. (c) My bicycle has been stolen. (d) My bicycle had been stolen by someone.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Present perfect passive में "has/have been + V3" चाहिए; बिना "been" के "has stolen" रूप में active है। साथ ही, जब कर्ता ("someone") अज्ञात या महत्वहीन हो, तो हम agent को हटा देते हैं। सही रूप "has been stolen" है, इसलिए उत्तर "My bicycle has been stolen." है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

80. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "Open the window."
(a) Let the window be opened. (b) The window is opened. (c) The window should opened. (d) Let the window open.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: आज्ञार्थक (imperative) वाक्यों का passive "Let + object + be + V3" से बनता है; "Let the window open" में past participle छूट गया है। आदेश का मानक passive "Let the window be opened" है, इसलिए उत्तर "Let the window be opened." है।

81. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "They are building a new bridge over the river."

83. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "The teacher gave me a book." ("I" से शुरू करें)
(a) I was gave a book by the teacher. (b) I was given a book by the teacher. (c) I am given a book by the teacher. (d) I had given a book by the teacher.
