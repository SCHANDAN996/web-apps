# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Active & Passive Voice, Explained Like You're 12

## The One Idea Behind Everything

Imagine your mom walks into the kitchen and sees an empty plate. Two things she can say:

1. **"Rohan ate the cake."** → She is pointing at WHO did it. (Active)
2. **"The cake has been eaten!"** → She is pointing at WHAT happened. (Passive)

Same event. Same cake. Same Rohan. The only difference is **where the camera is pointing** — at the doer, or at the action/result.

That's it. That is the entire chapter. Everything else is just grammar machinery to move the camera.

## The Daily-Life Example You'll Never Forget

Think of a **football match**:

- **Active:** "Ronaldo kicked the ball." → Camera on Ronaldo (the doer comes first).
- **Passive:** "The ball was kicked by Ronaldo." → Camera on the ball (the receiver comes first).

Now the golden question that converts any sentence:

> **Ask the verb: "By whom?"**
> - If the answer is sitting at the START of the sentence → **Active**.
> - If the answer is missing or hiding after "by" at the END → **Passive**.

Try it: *"The window was broken."* By whom? — silence. Nobody named. **Passive.** ✅

## The Conversion Machine (3 Steps Only)

To flip Active → Passive, do exactly three things:

1. **Swap:** The object jumps to the front, the subject goes to the back (after "by") — or disappears.
2. **Fix the verb:** Use **be + V3** (is/am/are/was/were/been/being + third form).
3. **Match the tense:** The "be" family carries the tense. V3 never changes.

**Watch it work:**
- Active: *She **writes** a letter.*
- Swap: *A letter … she.*
- Verb: present tense → *is* + V3 → *is written*
- Passive: *A letter **is written** by her.* ✅

## The Unforgettable Trick: **"BE + V3 = Passive Key"** 🔑

Whenever your eyes see **any form of BE (is, am, are, was, were, be, been, being) immediately followed by a V3**, shout "PASSIVE!" in your head.

- *is made* → Passive
- *was stolen* → Passive
- *has been sold* → Passive
- *is being repaired* → Passive

No "be + V3"? It can never be passive. This one check solves half the exam questions in under 15 seconds.

## The Two Traps That Fool Even Toppers

**Trap 1 — "By" is not compulsory.**
*"English is spoken here."* No "by anyone" — still 100% passive. The doer is simply unimportant or unknown.

**Trap 2 — Some verbs can NEVER become passive.**
Verbs with no object (intransitive verbs) like *go, sleep, come, die, run* — you can't say *"was gone by him."* No object = nothing to swap = no passive. If an option tries it, eliminate it instantly.
