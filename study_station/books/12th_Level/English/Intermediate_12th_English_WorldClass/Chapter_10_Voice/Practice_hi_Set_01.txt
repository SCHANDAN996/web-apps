1. सही passive form चुनें: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter has been written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter is written by her.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Active वाक्य simple present tense में है, इसलिए passive में "is/are + V3" लगता है। "Was written" past tense है और "is being written" continuous है — दोनों लुभाने वाले पर गलत विकल्प हैं। चूँकि क्रिया simple present में है, उत्तर है "A letter is written by her."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

2. सही passive form चुनें: "He killed the snake."
(a) The snake was killed by him. (b) The snake is killed by him. (c) The snake has been killed by him. (d) The snake will be killed by him.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Killed" simple past है, इसलिए passive में "was/were + V3" चाहिए। "Is killed" गलती से present tense में चला जाता है। Snake एकवचन है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The snake was killed by him."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

3. सही passive form चुनें: "The teacher is teaching the students."
(a) The students are taught by the teacher. (b) The students have been taught by the teacher. (c) The students were being taught by the teacher. (d) The students are being taught by the teacher.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present continuous passive में "is/are being + V3" बनता है। "Are taught" में continuous भाव गायब हो जाता है — यही आम जाल है। उत्तर है "The students are being taught by the teacher."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

4. सही passive form चुनें: "They have completed the work."
(a) The work has been completed by them. (b) The work was completed by them. (c) The work is completed by them. (d) The work had been completed by them.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Present perfect passive में "has/have been + V3" बनता है। "Work" एकवचन है, इसलिए "has been" आएगा; "had been" गलती से past perfect में चला जाता है। उत्तर है "The work has been completed by them."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

उत्तर: (b)

7. सही passive form चुनें: "Open the door."
(a) The door is opened. (b) The door was opened. (c) Let the door be opened. (d) The door should open.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Imperative वाक्यों का passive "Let + object + be + V3" से बनता है। "The door is opened" एक कथन है, आदेश नहीं। उत्तर है "Let the door be opened."

8. सही passive form चुनें: "People speak English all over the world."
(a) English is spoken all over the world. (b) English was spoken all over the world. (c) English is speaking all over the world. (d) English has spoken all over the world.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Simple present "speak" का passive "is spoken" बनता है; "speak" अनियमित क्रिया है (speak–spoke–spoken), इसलिए "is speaking" जाल है। उत्तर है "English is spoken all over the world."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

9. इस passive वाक्य का tense पहचानें: "The boy was punished by the teacher."
(a) Simple present (b) Present perfect (c) Simple past (d) Past continuous
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Was + V3" simple past passive की पहचान है; "was being + V3" past continuous होता — यही भ्रम पैदा करने वाला जाल है। उत्तर है simple past.
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

10. सही passive form चुनें: "Ravi is writing a poem."
(a) A poem is written by Ravi. (b) A poem is being written by Ravi. (c) A poem was being written by Ravi. (d) A poem has been written by Ravi.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Present continuous passive = "is/are being + V3"। "Being" हटाने से चल रही क्रिया का भाव खत्म हो जाता है — यही क्लासिक जाल है। उत्तर है "A poem is being written by Ravi."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

13. सही passive form चुनें: "You must obey the rules."
(a) The rules must obeyed. (b) The rules must been obeyed. (c) The rules must being obeyed. (d) The rules must be obeyed.
उत्तर: (d)

14. सही passive form चुनें: "Someone has stolen my watch."
(a) My watch has stolen. (b) My watch has been stolen. (c) My watch was stealing. (d) My watch is steal.
उत्तर: (b)

18. सही passive form चुनें: "She teaches us grammar."
(a) Grammar is taught to us by her. (b) Both Grammar is taught to us by her and We are taught grammar by her are possible, but We are taught grammar by her is preferred. (c) We are taught grammar by her. (d) Grammar is being taught us by her.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Teach" जैसी क्रियाएँ दो objects लेती हैं; कोई भी subject बन सकता है, पर परीक्षा में person-object वाला passive पसंद किया जाता है। सिर्फ़ एक रूप चुनना ही जाल है। उत्तर है कि दोनों संभव हैं, जिसमें person-वाला रूप पसंदीदा है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

19. सही passive form चुनें: "Who taught you English?"

24. सही passive form चुनें: "Why did he beat the boy?"
