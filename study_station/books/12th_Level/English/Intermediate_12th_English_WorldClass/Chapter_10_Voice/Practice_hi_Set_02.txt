26. सही passive form चुनें: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter has been written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter is written by her.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: लुभाने वाला विकल्प "is being written" continuous है, लेकिन active वाक्य simple present ("writes") में है, इसलिए passive भी simple present होना चाहिए: is/am/are + V3। "A letter is written by her" काल (tense) से मेल खाता है, इसलिए उत्तर "A letter is written by her" है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

27. सही passive form चुनें: "He killed the snake."
(a) The snake was killed by him. (b) The snake is killed by him. (c) The snake has been killed by him. (d) The snake will be killed by him.
