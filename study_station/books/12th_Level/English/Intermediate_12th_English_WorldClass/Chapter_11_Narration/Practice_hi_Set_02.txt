26. He said, "I am happy today."
(a) He said that he was happy that day. (b) He said that he is happy today. (c) He said that he had been happy that day. (d) He said that he was happy today.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: विकल्प (d) में जाल यह है कि "today" को जस का तस रखा गया है; अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) में समय-सूचक शब्द बदलने चाहिए। Present simple "am" का past simple "was" बनता है और "today" का "that day" बनता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he was happy that day."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

27. She said to me, "Do you like tea?"
(a) She asked me did I like tea. (b) She asked me if I liked tea. (c) She asked me that did I like tea. (d) She told me if I liked tea.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: विकल्प (a) में जाल यह है कि प्रश्नवाचक क्रम "did I like" को जस का तस रखा गया है; अप्रत्यक्ष प्रश्नों में साधारण वाक्य-क्रम (statement order) होता है। Yes/no प्रश्नों में "if/whether" आता है, "said to" का "asked" बनता है और "like" का "liked" बनता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She asked me if I liked tea."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

30. She said to him, "Please help me."

हल: विकल्प (a) में जाल यह है कि "told" का प्रयोग किया गया है, जो आदेशों के लिए होता है, विनम्र अनुरोधों के लिए नहीं। "Please" अनुरोध का संकेत है, इसलिए "requested + object + to-infinitive" सही है, और "me" का "her" बनता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She requested him to help her."

32. He said, "Alas! I have lost my purse."
(a) He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his purse. (b) He said with sorrow that he lost his purse. (c) He cried that he has lost his purse. (d) He exclaimed with joy that he had lost his purse.
उत्तर: (a)
