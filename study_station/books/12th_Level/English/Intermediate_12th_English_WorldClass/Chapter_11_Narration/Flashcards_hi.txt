कार्ड 1
सामने: Direct Speech से Indirect Speech बदलते समय Reporting Verb "said" किसमें बदलता है?
पीछे: सामान्य कथन में "said" → "told" (object के साथ) या "said" (बिना object)। जैसे: He said to me, "I am tired." → He told me that he was tired.

कार्ड 2
सामने: 🧠 म्नेमोनिक — "S-P-T" ट्रिक क्या है?
पीछे: Narration बदलने के 3 कदम — **S**peech mark हटाओ, **P**ronoun बदलो, **T**ense पीछे ले जाओ। याद रखें: "SPT — सीधा-परोक्ष तालमेल!"

कार्ड 3
सामने: Present Simple (Direct) Indirect में किस Tense में बदलता है?
पीछे: Past Simple में। जैसे: She said, "I **like** tea." → She said that she **liked** tea.

कार्ड 4
सामने: ⚠️ परीक्षक का जाल — Reporting Verb Present/Future Tense में हो तो Reported Speech का Tense बदलेगा या नहीं?
पीछे: नहीं बदलेगा! He **says**, "I am busy." → He says that he **is** busy. Tense तभी बदलता है जब Reporting Verb Past में हो।

कार्ड 5
सामने: "now" और "today" Indirect Speech में किसमें बदलते हैं?
पीछे: now → then; today → that day. जैसे: He said, "I am busy now." → He said that he was busy then.

कार्ड 6
सामने: Interrogative (प्रश्नवाचक) वाक्य में "said to" किसमें बदलता है और वाक्य का क्रम क्या होता है?
पीछे: "said to" → "asked/enquired of"; वाक्य Assertive क्रम में आता है (Subject + Verb), प्रश्न चिह्न हट जाता है। He said to me, "Where do you live?" → He asked me where I lived.

कार्ड 7
सामने: ⚠️ परीक्षक का जाल — "He asked me where did I live" सही है या गलत?
पीछे: गलत! Indirect question में helping verb subject के बाद नहीं आता। सही: He asked me where I **lived**. ("did" हटता है, verb Past में बदलता है।)

कार्ड 8
सामने: Imperative वाक्य (आदेश/निवेदन) में Reporting Verb कौन से प्रयोग होते हैं?
पीछे: ordered, commanded, requested, advised, forbade — और Reported Speech Infinitive (to + V1) में बदलता है। The teacher said, "Open your books." → The teacher ordered the students to open their books.

कार्ड 9
सामने: निषेध वाले Imperative वाक्य ("Don't...") Indirect में कैसे बदलते हैं?
पीछे: "not to + V1" से। Mother said, "Don't go out." → Mother forbade me to go out / told me not to go out.

कार्ड 10
सामने: 🧠 म्नेमोनिक — Pronoun बदलने का "SON" नियम क्या है?
पीछे: **S** = Subject (1st person → Subject के अनुसार), **O** = Object (2nd person → Object के अनुसार), **N** = No change (3rd person नहीं बदलता)। I said to him, "I will help you." → I told him that I (S) would help him (O).

कार्ड 11
सामने: "this" और "these" Indirect Speech में किसमें बदलते हैं?
पीछे: this → that; these → those. She said, "This is my pen." → She said that that was her pen.

कार्ड 12
सामने: Universal Truth या Habitual Fact का Tense Indirect में बदलता है या नहीं?
पीछे: नहीं बदलता! The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun." → The teacher said that the earth **revolves** around the sun.

कार्ड 13
सामने: Exclamatory वाक्य में "said" किसमें बदलता है?
पीछे: "exclaimed with joy/sorrow/surprise" में; What/How हटाकर "that" लगता है। He said, "What a beautiful day!" → He exclaimed with joy that it was a very beautiful day.

कार्ड 14
सामने: "yesterday", "ago", "last night" Indirect में किसमें बदलते हैं?
पीछे: yesterday → the day before / the previous day; ago → before; last night → the night before. He said, "I met her yesterday." → He said that he had met her the day before.

कार्ड 15
सामने: Present Perfect (Direct) Indirect में किसमें बदलता है?
पीछे: Past Perfect में। She said, "I **have finished** the work." → She said that she **had finished** the work.

कार्ड 16
सामने: ⚠️ परीक्षक का जाल — Past Simple और Past Perfect में से कौन Indirect में बदलता है और कौन नहीं?
पीछे: Past Simple → Past Perfect में बदलता है; लेकिन Past Perfect **नहीं बदलता** (उससे पीछे कोई Tense नहीं)। He said, "I had already eaten." → He said that he had already eaten. (जस का तस)

कार्ड 17
सामने: "shall/will" Indirect Speech में किसमें बदलते हैं?
पीछे: shall → should/would; will → would. He said, "I will come tomorrow." → He said that he would come the next day.

कार्ड 18
सामने: Optative वाक्य (इच्छा/आशीर्वाद) में Reporting Verb क्या होता है?
पीछे: wished/blessed/cursed/prayed + that। She said, "May God bless you!" → She prayed that God might bless me.

कार्ड 19
सामने: Yes/No प्रश्न Indirect में किस conjunction से जुड़ता है?
पीछे: "if" या "whether" से। He said to me, "Are you ready?" → He asked me if/whether I was ready.

कार्ड 20
सामने: "can", "may", "must" Indirect Speech में किसमें बदलते हैं?
पीछे: can → could; may → might; must → had to / must (आदेश में must अक्सर जस का तस)। She said, "I can swim." → She said that she could swim.
