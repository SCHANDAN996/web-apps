101. He said to me, "Do you know where the station is?"
(a) He asked me did I know where the station is. (b) He asked me if I knew where the station was. (c) He asked me that if I knew where the station was. (d) He asked me whether I know where the station was.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प प्रश्न-क्रम "did I know" को वैसा ही रखता है, जबकि reported speech में statement क्रम "I knew" चाहिए। Yes/no प्रश्न में "if/whether" आता है, और "know" का backshift "knew" तथा "is" का "was" होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He asked me if I knew where the station was."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

102. She said, "I was cooking dinner when the lights went out."
(a) She said that she was cooking dinner when the lights had gone out. (b) She said that she had been cooking dinner when the lights went out. (c) She said that she had cooked dinner when the lights went out. (d) She said that she is cooking dinner when the lights went out.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जाल यह है कि "was cooking" को अपरिवर्तित रखा जाए; direct speech का past continuous reported speech में past perfect continuous "had been cooking" बन जाता है। "Went" (past simple) "went" ही रहता है क्योंकि वह पहले से time clause में है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She said that she had been cooking dinner when the lights went out."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

103. The teacher said to the boys, "Why were you absent yesterday?"
(a) The teacher asked the boys that why they had been absent the day before. (b) The teacher asked the boys why were they absent yesterday. (c) The teacher asked the boys why they had been absent the previous day. (d) The teacher asked the boys why they were absent the previous day.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "were they" (प्रश्न-क्रम) और "yesterday" को वैसा ही रखता है; reported speech में statement क्रम "they had been" चाहिए और "yesterday" बन जाता है "the previous day"। Past simple "were absent" का backshift past perfect "had been absent" होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The teacher asked the boys why they had been absent the previous day."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

104. He said, "I may go to Delhi tomorrow."
(a) He said that he may go to Delhi the next day. (b) He said that he may go to Delhi tomorrow. (c) He said that he might go to Delhi tomorrow. (d) He said that he might go to Delhi the next day.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: जाल यह है कि दोनों आवश्यक बदलावों में से केवल एक बदला जाए। "May" का backshift "might" होता है और "tomorrow" बन जाता है "the next day"; दोनों बदलाव साथ होने चाहिए, इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he might go to Delhi the next day."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

105. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
(a) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain. (b) Mother told me that do not go out in the rain. (c) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (d) Mother forbade me going out in the rain.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "said me" प्रयोग करता है, जो गलत है क्योंकि "say" बिना "to" के indirect object नहीं लेता। Negative imperative को "forbade + to-infinitive" या "told me not to go" से report किया जाता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Mother forbade me to go out in the rain."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

106. He said, "Alas! I have lost my wallet."
(a) He cried that he has lost his wallet. (b) He said alas that he lost his wallet. (c) He exclaimed with joy that he had lost his wallet. (d) He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: जाल है "exclaimed with joy", लेकिन "Alas!" दुख व्यक्त करता है, खुशी नहीं। Exclamations को "exclaimed with sorrow/grief" से report किया जाता है और "have lost" का backshift "had lost" होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English

107. She said to him, "Please lend me your pen."
(a) She told him please to lend her his pen. (b) She said him to please lend her his pen. (c) She requested him that lend her his pen. (d) She requested him to lend her his pen.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "please" को reported clause के अंदर रखता है, जबकि "please" reporting verb "requested" में समा जाता है। Polite imperative बनता है "requested + object + to-infinitive", और "me/your" बदलकर "her/his" हो जाते हैं, इसलिए उत्तर है "She requested him to lend her his pen."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

108. He said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
(a) He said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (b) He said that the earth revolved around the sun. (c) He said that the earth revolves around the sun. (d) He said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: जाल है यांत्रिक रूप से "revolved" में backshift करना; सार्वभौमिक सत्य और वैज्ञानिक तथ्य reported speech में tense नहीं बदलते। Present simple जस का तस रहता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that the earth revolves around the sun."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

109. I said to her, "I will help you if you ask me politely."
(a) I told her that I will help her if she asks me politely. (b) I told her that I would help her if she asked me politely. (c) I said her that I would help her if she asked me politely. (d) I told her that I would help her if she would ask me politely.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "said her" प्रयोग करता है, जो अव्याकरणिक है; object के साथ "told her" आता है। "Will" बन जाता है "would" और if-clause में "ask" का backshift "asked" होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "I told her that I would help her if she asked me politely."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

110. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward and capture the post."
(a) The captain ordered the soldiers that march forward and capture the post. (b) The captain said the soldiers march forward and capture the post. (c) The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward and capture the post. (d) The captain requested the soldiers to march forward and captured the post.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: जाल है "requested", जो सैन्य आदेश के लिए बहुत हल्का शब्द है; commands के लिए "ordered/commanded" आता है। दोनों imperative verbs समानांतर to-infinitives "to march... and (to) capture" बन जाते हैं, इसलिए उत्तर है "The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward and capture the post."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

111. She said, "What a beautiful garden this is!"
(a) She told that it was a beautiful garden. (b) She said what a beautiful garden it was. (c) She exclaimed that it is a very beautiful garden. (d) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful garden.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प exclamatory संरचना "what a beautiful garden" को वैसा ही रखता है, जिसे statement में बदलना ज़रूरी है। "What a...!" बन जाता है "exclaimed that... very + adjective", और "is" का backshift "was" होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful garden."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English

112. He said to me, "Did you finish the work which I gave you?"
(a) He asked me if I had finished the work which he had given me. (b) He asked me did I finish the work which I gave you. (c) He asked me whether I finished the work which he gave me. (d) He asked me if I have finished the work which he had given me.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: जाल है "finished" और "gave" को past simple में रखना; दोनों का backshift past perfect "had finished" और "had given" होता है क्योंकि दोनों क्रियाएँ reporting से पहले की हैं। Yes/no प्रश्न में "if" आता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He asked me if I had finished the work which he had given me."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

113. The old man said, "I have lived in this village since my childhood."
(a) The old man said that he had lived in that village since his childhood. (b) The old man said that he has lived in this village since his childhood. (c) The old man said that he lived in that village since his childhood. (d) The old man said that he had lived in this village since his childhood.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "this village" को वैसा ही रखता है, जबकि reported speech में "this" बन जाता है "that"। Present perfect "have lived" का backshift past perfect "had lived" होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The old man said that he had lived in that village since his childhood."

114. She said to me, "Let us go for a walk."
(a) She told me let us go for a walk. (b) She suggested that we should go for a walk. (c) She suggested me to go for a walk. (d) She requested that they should go for a walk.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जाल है "suggested me to go", जो गलत है क्योंकि "suggest" के बाद कभी object + infinitive नहीं आता। "Let us" वाले प्रस्ताव "suggested that we should" या "proposed going" से report होते हैं, इसलिए उत्तर है "She suggested that we should go for a walk."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

115. He said, "I must finish this report today."
(a) He said that he had to finish that report that day. (b) He said that he must finish this report today. (c) He said that he would finish that report that day. (d) He said that he had to finish that report today.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "today" को वैसा ही रखता है, जिसे "that day" बनना चाहिए। Obligation बताने वाला "must" बन जाता है "had to", और "this" बन जाता है "that", इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he had to finish that report that day."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

116. Reported वाक्य के जिस भाग में त्रुटि है, उसे पहचानें: He told me (P) / that he will come (Q) / to the party (R) / the following day. (S)
(a) भाग P (b) भाग Q (c) भाग R (d) कोई त्रुटि नहीं
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जाल है यह सोचना कि "told me" गलत है, जबकि "told + object" बिल्कुल सही है। त्रुटि भाग Q में है: past reporting verb के बाद "will" का backshift "would" होना चाहिए — "that he would come"। इसलिए उत्तर है भाग Q।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

117. She said, "I wrote a letter to my friend last night."
(a) She said that she has written a letter to her friend the night before. (b) She said that she wrote a letter to her friend last night. (c) She said that she had written a letter to her friend the night before. (d) She said that she had written a letter to my friend the night before.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: जाल है "my friend" को वैसा ही रखना, जबकि first person "my" वक्ता "she" के अनुसार बदलकर "her" हो जाता है। Past simple "wrote" का backshift "had written" होता है और "last night" बन जाता है "the night before", इसलिए उत्तर है "She said that she had written a letter to her friend the night before."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

118. The boy said to his mother, "Shall I switch on the fan?"
(a) The boy asked his mother whether he will switch on the fan. (b) The boy asked his mother shall he switch on the fan. (c) The boy told his mother to switch on the fan. (d) The boy asked his mother if he should switch on the fan.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प वाक्य को आदेश में बदल देता है, जबकि "Shall I...?" अनुमति माँगने वाला प्रश्न है, आदेश नहीं। "Shall" बन जाता है "should" और प्रश्न में "if" आता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The boy asked his mother if he should switch on the fan."

119. He said, "I can solve this problem easily."

120. जिस भाग में त्रुटि है, उसे पहचानें: She asked me (P) / that where (Q) / I had put (R) / her keys. (S)
(a) भाग P (b) भाग Q (c) भाग R (d) कोई त्रुटि नहीं
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जाल है "had put" पर शक करना, जबकि past perfect यहाँ सही है क्योंकि क्रिया पूछने से पहले हुई। त्रुटि भाग Q में है: reported speech में wh-प्रश्न के साथ कभी "that" नहीं आता — केवल "where" होना चाहिए। इसलिए उत्तर है भाग Q।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

121. The traveller said, "How beautiful this valley is!"
(a) The traveller exclaimed that the valley was very beautiful. (b) The traveller said how beautiful the valley is. (c) The traveller exclaimed with joy that how beautiful the valley was. (d) The traveller told that the valley was very beautiful.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "exclaimed with joy that how beautiful" प्रयोग करता है, जबकि "exclaimed that" के बाद साधारण statement चाहिए, exclamatory "how" संरचना नहीं। "How beautiful... is!" बन जाता है "was very beautiful", इसलिए उत्तर है "The traveller exclaimed that the valley was very beautiful."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

122. He said to me, "I met your brother here two days ago."
(a) He told me that he had met your brother there two days before. (b) He told me that he met my brother here two days ago. (c) He said me that he had met my brother there two days before. (d) He told me that he had met my brother there two days before.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: जाल है "your brother" को वैसा ही रखना, जबकि second person "your" श्रोता "me" के अनुसार बदलकर "my" हो जाता है। "Met" का backshift "had met", "here" का "there" और "ago" का "before" होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He told me that he had met my brother there two days before."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

123. She said, "I have been waiting for you since morning."
(a) She said that she had been waiting for me since morning. (b) She said that she has been waiting for me since morning. (c) She said that she was waiting for me since morning. (d) She said that she had waited for me since morning.

124. The principal said, "Honesty is the best policy."
(a) The principal said that honesty had been the best policy. (b) The principal said that honesty was the best policy. (c) The principal said that honesty is the best policy. (d) The principal said that honesty is a best policy.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: जाल है "was" में backshift करना; कहावतें और सार्वभौमिक सत्य reported speech में कभी tense नहीं बदलते। साथ ही "the best policy" ही स्थिर रूप है, "a best policy" नहीं, इसलिए उत्तर है "The principal said that honesty is the best policy."

125. He said to her, "If I were you, I would accept the offer."
(a) He said that if he were her, he will accept the offer. (b) He told her that if he was her, he would accept the offer. (c) He advised her to accept the offer. (d) He asked her whether she would accept the offer.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प conditional संरचना को "was her" के साथ रखता है, जबकि "If I were you" एक स्थिर subjunctive है जो कभी "was" नहीं बनता, और ऐसे वाक्य सलाह के रूप में report होना ही बेहतर है। स्वाभाविक reported रूप है "advised + object + to-infinitive", इसलिए उत्तर है "He advised her to accept the offer."
स्रोत: PYQ-style
