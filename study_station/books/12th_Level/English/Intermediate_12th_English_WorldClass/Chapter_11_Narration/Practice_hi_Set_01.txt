1. He said, "I am happy."
सही अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनिए।
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that I was happy. (c) He said that he was happy. (d) He said that he had been happy.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Reported speech में present simple "am" बदलकर past simple "was" हो जाता है, और "I" subject के अनुसार "he" हो जाता है। विकल्प (a) में present tense वैसा ही रखा गया है — यही आम जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he was happy."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी

2. She said to me, "I will help you."
सही अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनिए।
(a) She told me that she would help me. (b) She said me that she will help me. (c) She told me that I would help you. (d) She told to me that she would help me.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Said to me" बदलकर "told me" होता है (कभी "told to me" नहीं), "will" बदलकर "would" होता है, और सर्वनाम "she" तथा "me" में बदलते हैं। विकल्प (d) में "told to me" वाला क्लासिक जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "She told me that she would help me."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी

3. He said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
सही अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनिए।
(a) He said that the earth revolves around the sun. (b) He said that the earth revolved around the sun. (c) He said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (d) He said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य (universal truths) indirect speech में tense नहीं बदलते, इसलिए "revolves" जस का तस रहता है। विकल्प (b) उन विद्यार्थियों को फँसाता है जो tense-shift नियम आँख बंद करके लगा देते हैं — यही जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that the earth revolves around the sun."

7. The teacher said to the boys, "Open your books."
सही अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनिए।
(a) The teacher told the boys that open their books. (b) The teacher said the boys open your books. (c) The teacher ordered the boys to open their books. (d) The teacher asked the boys that they open books.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: आज्ञावाचक वाक्यों (imperatives) को "ordered/asked/told + object + to-infinitive" से बदला जाता है। विकल्प (a) में imperative के साथ "that" लगाया गया है — यह बार-बार होने वाली गलती है। इसलिए उत्तर है "The teacher ordered the boys to open their books."
