51. He said, "I am happy today."
(a) He said that he was happy today (b) He said that he is happy today (c) He said that he had been happy that day (d) He said that he was happy that day
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap keeps "today" unchanged, but time expressions must shift in indirect speech. Present simple "am" changes to past simple "was", and "today" becomes "that day", so the answer is "He said that he was happy that day".

(a) She asked me if I liked tea (b) She asked me whether I like tea (c) She asked me that did I like tea (d) She asked me did I like tea
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap keeps the question order "did I like", but a reported yes/no question becomes assertive with subject before verb. It is introduced by "if/whether" and "like" shifts to "liked", so the answer is "She asked me if I liked tea".
