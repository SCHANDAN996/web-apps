76. He said to me, "I will help you tomorrow."
(a) He told me that he would help me the next day. (b) He told me that he will help me tomorrow. (c) He told me that he would help me tomorrow. (d) He said me that he would help me the next day.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "tomorrow" को वैसा ही रखता है, लेकिन indirect speech में "tomorrow" का "the next day" और "will" का "would" हो जाता है। साथ ही "said to me" का "told me" होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He told me that he would help me the next day."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

77. She said, "I have finished my work."
(a) She said that she has finished her work. (b) She said that she finished her work. (c) She said that she had finished her work. (d) She said that she has been finished her work.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: direct speech का present perfect, indirect speech में past perfect बन जाता है, इसलिए "have finished" का "had finished" होता है। लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प present perfect को वैसा ही रखता है, जो backshift नियम तोड़ता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She said that she had finished her work."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

78. The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
(a) The teacher said that the earth revolved around the sun. (b) The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun. (c) The teacher said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (d) The teacher said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य और वैज्ञानिक तथ्यों का tense reported speech में नहीं बदलता, भले ही reporting verb past में हो। लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प सार्वभौमिक सत्य पर backshift लगा देता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun."

79. He said to her, "Do you like tea?"
(a) He asked her if she likes tea. (b) He asked her that did she like tea. (c) He asked her if she liked tea. (d) He asked her whether she likes tea or not.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: हाँ/नहीं वाले प्रश्न "if" या "whether" से report होते हैं, प्रश्न-रूप statement order में बदल जाता है और "like" का backshift "liked" होता है। लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प गलती से सहायक क्रिया "did" रखता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He asked her if she liked tea."

80. Mother said to me, "Where are you going?"
(a) Mother asked me where was I going. (b) Mother asked me where I am going. (c) Mother asked me where I was going. (d) Mother asked me that where I was going.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: reported wh-questions में inversion समाप्त हो जाता है: "where are you going" का "where I was going" बनता है, जिसमें "are" का backshift "was" होता है। लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प प्रश्न-क्रम वैसा ही रखता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Mother asked me where I was going."

81. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward."
(a) The captain ordered the soldiers that march forward. (b) The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward. (c) The captain said the soldiers to march forward. (d) The captain requested the soldiers to march forward.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: आदेश "ordered/commanded + object + to-infinitive" से report होते हैं। कैप्टन आदेश देता है, अनुरोध नहीं, और "said" यहाँ object के साथ infinitive नहीं ले सकता, इसलिए उत्तर है "The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

82. She said to me, "Please lend me your pen."
(a) She requested me to lend her my pen. (b) She requested me that lend her my pen. (c) She ordered me to lend her my pen. (d) She requested me to lend her your pen.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Please" अनुरोध दर्शाता है, जो "requested + object + to-infinitive" से report होता है; "me" का "her" और "your" का "my" (श्रोता की दृष्टि से) होता है। लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "ordered" लगाता है, जो भाव बदल देता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She requested me to lend her my pen."

83. He said, "Alas! I have lost my wallet."

86. The boy said, "I was playing cricket yesterday."

100. She said to him, "It is time you started working seriously."
