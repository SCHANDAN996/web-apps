126. The teacher said to the boys, "Do not make a noise."
(a) The teacher told the boys do not make a noise. (b) The teacher ordered the boys that do not make a noise. (c) The teacher said the boys not to make a noise. (d) The teacher forbade the boys to make a noise.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option uses "told the boys do not make", which wrongly keeps the imperative structure of direct speech. A negative imperative is reported with "forbade + object + to-infinitive" or "ordered/told + object + not to". So the answer is "The teacher forbade the boys to make a noise."
