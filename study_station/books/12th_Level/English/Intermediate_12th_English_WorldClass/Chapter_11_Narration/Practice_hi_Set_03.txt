51. He said, "I am happy today."
(a) He said that he was happy today (b) He said that he is happy today (c) He said that he had been happy that day (d) He said that he was happy that day
उत्तर: (d)
हल: जाल में "today" अपरिवर्तित रखा गया है, लेकिन indirect speech में समय-वाचक शब्द बदलने चाहिए। Present simple "am" बदलकर past simple "was" हो जाता है और "today" बदलकर "that day" हो जाता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he was happy that day"।
