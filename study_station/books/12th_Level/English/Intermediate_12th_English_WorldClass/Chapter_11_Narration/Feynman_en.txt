# 🧒 Feynman Technique: Direct-Indirect Speech

## The Hardest Concept: "Backshift of Tenses" — Explained to a 12-Year-Old

Imagine your friend Rohan tells you **today**:

> Rohan says, **"I am happy."**

Now, tomorrow, your mother asks you: *"What did Rohan say yesterday?"*

You cannot say: *"Rohan said he **is** happy."* ❌

Why? Because that moment is gone. Yesterday is finished. When you report something **later**, the words must also travel **back in time** — just like you travelled from yesterday to today.

So you say:

> Rohan said that he **was** happy. ✅

**"am" took one step back and became "was".**

That is the whole secret of Indirect Speech: **when the reporting verb is in the past ("said"), every tense inside the quote takes one step back into the past.**

---

## 🚌 The Bus-Stop Trick (Never Forget This!)

Think of tenses as passengers standing at bus stops. When the reporting verb is **"said"** (past), every passenger moves **one stop backwards**:

| Passenger (Direct) | Moves one stop back → (Indirect) |
|---|---|
| am / is / was (simple present/past) | was / were / had been |
| am playing | was playing |
| has played | had played |
| will play | would play |
| can | could |
| may | might |

**Unforgettable line:** *"Said" is a strict teacher — it pushes every tense one step back!*

**But wait — two VIP passengers never move:**
1. **Universal truths** — He said, "The sun rises in the east." → He said that the sun **rises** in the east. (The sun doesn't care about your grammar!)
2. **Past Perfect (had + V3)** — it is already at the last stop; it cannot go further back.

---

## 🧠 Try It Yourself (Daily-Life Example)

Your sister says: **"I have finished my homework."**

Report it in the evening:

> My sister said that she **had finished** her homework.

Notice three changes:
- **I → she** (person changes with the speaker)
- **have finished → had finished** (one step back)
- **my → her** (possessive follows the person)

**The 3-Step Formula:** 👤 Person change → ⏪ Tense backshift → 🔗 Add "that" (for statements).

## ❓ Question for You

Ravi said, **"I will help you tomorrow."**

Convert it to Indirect Speech. (Hint: three things change — *will*, *you*, and *tomorrow*. Can you find all three?)

*(Answer: Ravi said that he **would** help **me** **the next day**.)*
