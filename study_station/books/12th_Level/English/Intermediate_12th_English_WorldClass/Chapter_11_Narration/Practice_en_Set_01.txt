Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. He said, "I am happy."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that I was happy. (c) He said that he was happy. (d) He said that he had been happy.
Answer: (c)
Solution: In reported speech the present simple "am" shifts to past simple "was", and "I" changes to "he" to match the subject. Option (a) wrongly keeps the present tense, a common trap. So the answer is "He said that he was happy."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

2. She said to me, "I will help you."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) She told me that she would help me. (b) She said me that she will help me. (c) She told me that I would help you. (d) She told to me that she would help me.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Said to me" becomes "told me" (never "told to me"), "will" becomes "would", and pronouns adjust to "she" and "me". Option (d) is the classic trap with "told to me". So the answer is "She told me that she would help me."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

3. He said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) He said that the earth revolves around the sun. (b) He said that the earth revolved around the sun. (c) He said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (d) He said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Universal truths do not change tense in indirect speech, so "revolves" stays as it is. Option (b) tempts students who apply the tense-shift rule blindly — that is the trap. So the answer is "He said that the earth revolves around the sun."

4. Ravi said, "I bought a car yesterday."

(a) Ravi said that he bought a car yesterday. (b) Ravi said that he bought a car the day before. (c) Ravi said that he has bought a car the previous day. (d) Ravi said that he had bought a car the day before.
Answer: (d)
Solution: Past simple "bought" shifts to past perfect "had bought", and "yesterday" becomes "the day before / the previous day". Option (b) changes the time word but not the tense — a tempting half-correct trap. So the answer is "Ravi said that he had bought a car the day before."

12. She said, "What a beautiful flower!"
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) She exclaimed with joy that it was a very beautiful flower. (b) She said what a beautiful flower it is. (c) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful flower. (d) She told that the flower is beautiful.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Exclamations are reported with "exclaimed with joy/sorrow + that + statement", and "what a" becomes "very". Option (c) is grammatically acceptable but (a) is the standard complete form expected in exams; (b) wrongly keeps the exclamatory structure. So the answer is "She exclaimed with joy that it was a very beautiful flower."

25. He said to me, "I saw the film last week, but I did not like it."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) He told me that he had seen the film the week before but that he had not liked it. (b) He told me that he saw the film last week but did not like it. (c) He told me that he has seen the film the week before but did not like it. (d) He told me that he had saw the film the week before but had not liked it.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Both past simple verbs shift to past perfect ("had seen", "had not liked") and "last week" becomes "the week before". Option (d) wrongly uses "had saw" — the trap. So the answer is "He told me that he had seen the film the week before but that he had not liked it."
