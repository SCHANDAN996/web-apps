26. The root "port" in "transport" means:
(a) to carry (b) to look (c) to write (d) to speak
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option "to look" belongs to the root "spect", not "port". The root "port" means "to carry", as in "portable" (able to be carried) and "export" (to carry out), so the answer is to carry.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

27. The root "spect" in "inspect" means:
(a) to say (b) to carry (c) to hear (d) to look
Answer: (d)
Solution: "To carry" is the meaning of "port", which is a common confusion. The root "spect" means "to look", as in "spectator" (one who looks on) and "inspect" (to look into), so the answer is to look.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English
