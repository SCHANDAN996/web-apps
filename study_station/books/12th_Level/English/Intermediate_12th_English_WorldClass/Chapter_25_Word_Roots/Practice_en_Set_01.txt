1. The root "port" (Latin) means:
(a) to write (b) to carry (c) to see (d) to speak
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Port" comes from Latin "portare" meaning to carry, as in transport (carry across) and portable (able to be carried). "To write" is the root "scrib/scrip", so the answer is to carry.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (Vocabulary)

2. The root "dict" (Latin) means:
(a) to love (b) to lead (c) to break (d) to say or speak
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Dict" comes from Latin "dicere" meaning to say, seen in dictate, predict, and verdict. "To lead" is "duct", so the answer is to say or speak.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (Vocabulary)

3. The root "bio" (Greek) means:
(a) earth (b) water (c) life (d) light
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Bio" is Greek for life, as in biology (study of life) and biography (writing about a life). "Earth" is "geo", so the answer is life.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Terminology)

4. The root "geo" (Greek) means:
(a) star (b) time (c) sound (d) earth
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Geo" means earth, as in geography (writing about the earth) and geology (study of the earth). "Star" is "aster/astro", so the answer is earth.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Terminology)

5. The root "scrib/scrip" (Latin) means:
(a) to read (b) to write (c) to count (d) to cut
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Scrib/scrip" comes from Latin "scribere" meaning to write, as in describe, manuscript, and inscription. "To read" relates to "leg/lect", so the answer is to write.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (Vocabulary)

6. The root "aud" (Latin) means:
(a) to see (b) to hear (c) to touch (d) to move

8. The root "chron" (Greek) means:
(a) colour (b) people (c) gold (d) time
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Chron" is Greek for time, as in chronology (order of time) and chronic (lasting over time). "Colour" is "chrom", a look-alike trap, so the answer is time.

9. The root "phil" (Greek) means:
(a) fear (b) war (c) love (d) wisdom
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Phil" means love, as in philosophy (love of wisdom) and philanthropy (love of mankind). "Fear" is "phob", so the answer is love.

10. The root "phob" (Greek) means:
(a) love (b) light (c) fear (d) sound
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Phob" means fear, as in phobia and claustrophobia (fear of closed spaces). "Light" is "photo", so the answer is fear.

11. The root "tele" (Greek) means:
(a) near (b) small (c) far or distant (d) fast
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Tele" means far, as in telephone (sound from far) and telescope (seeing far). It does not mean small, so the answer is far or distant.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (Vocabulary)

12. The root "micro" (Greek) means:

Solution: "Micro" means small, as in microscope (seeing small things) and microbe. "Large" is "macro/mega", so the answer is small.

18. The root "manu" (Latin) means:
(a) foot (b) head (c) hand (d) eye
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Manu" means hand, as in manual (done by hand) and manufacture (originally made by hand). "Foot" is "ped", a common confusion, so the answer is hand.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (Vocabulary)

19. The root "ped/pod" (Latin/Greek) means:
(a) hand (b) foot (c) child (d) tooth
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Ped/pod" means foot, as in pedal and tripod (three feet). "Child" is "paed" (as in paediatrician), a sound-alike trap, so the answer is foot.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (Vocabulary)

20. The root "logy" (Greek) means:
(a) the study of (b) the fear of (c) the love of (d) the rule of
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Logy" (from "logos") means study or science, as in biology and zoology. "The rule of" is "cracy/archy", so the answer is the study of.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Terminology)

21. The word "transport" literally means:
(a) to carry across (b) to write down (c) to see through (d) to speak against
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Trans" means across and "port" means carry, so transport means to carry across. "To speak against" would be "contra + dict" (contradict), so the answer is to carry across.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (Vocabulary)

22. The word "bibliophile" means a person who loves:
(a) books (b) money (c) food (d) travel
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Biblio" means book and "phil" means love, so a bibliophile loves books. Money-loving would be linked to "pluto/mammon", so the answer is books.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (Vocabulary)

23. The word "chronological" order means arrangement according to:
(a) time (b) importance (c) size (d) alphabet
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Chron" means time, so chronological order is arrangement by time. Do not confuse it with "chrom" (colour), so the answer is time.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (Vocabulary)

24. [Examiner's Trap] The word "pedestrian" refers to a person who travels:
(a) on foot (b) by bicycle (c) by car (d) by horse
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming "ped" relates to pedals or vehicles; here "ped" means foot, so a pedestrian walks on foot. The bicycle option tempts because of "pedal", but the answer is on foot.
Source: PYQ-style

25. [Examiner's Trap] The word "manuscript" literally means:
(a) printed by machine (b) copied by many (c) spoken aloud (d) written by hand
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "script" suggesting any writing or printing; but "manu" means hand and "script" means written, so a manuscript is handwritten. Printing came later, so the answer is written by hand.
Source: PYQ-style
