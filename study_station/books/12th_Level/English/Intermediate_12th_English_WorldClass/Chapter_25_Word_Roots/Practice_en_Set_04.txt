76. The root "bene" means:
(a) good (b) before (c) bad (d) against
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Mal" means bad, so "benevolent" is often confused with "malevolent" — but "bene" means good, as in benefit, benediction, benefactor. The answer is good.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (vocabulary)

77. The word "chronology" is built from the root "chron", which means:
(a) time (b) colour (c) sound (d) writing
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Chrom" (colour, as in chrome) is the tempting look-alike, but "chron" means time, as in chronic, synchronize, anachronism. The answer is time.
Source: Standard Greek root lists

78. In the word "unbelievable", the suffix is:
(a) un (b) believe (c) unbelieve (d) able
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Un" is tempting but it is a prefix added at the start; a suffix comes at the end. Here "-able" is the suffix meaning "capable of being". The answer is able.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (word formation)

79. The root "dict" (as in predict, verdict) means:
(a) to say or speak (b) to write (c) to lead (d) to see
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Scrib/script" means to write, which tempts many, but "dict" means to say — dictionary, dictate, contradict all involve speaking. The answer is to say or speak.
Source: Standard Latin root lists

80. Which word contains a root meaning "earth"?
(a) Hydrology (b) Geography (c) Biology (d) Astronomy
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Astronomy" tempts because it deals with the planet, but its root "astro" means star. "Geo" means earth, so the answer is Geography.

81. The prefix "anti-" in "antibiotic" means:
(a) against (b) together (c) after (d) before
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Ante-" (before, as in antecedent) is the classic trap because it looks nearly identical, but "anti-" means against — antifreeze, antidote, antisocial. The answer is against.

82. The word "portable" contains the root "port", meaning:
(a) to place (b) a harbour (c) a door (d) to carry
Answer: (d)
Solution: The harbour meaning tempts because of the everyday noun "port", but the Latin root "portare" means to carry — transport, export, import, support. The answer is to carry.
Source: Standard Latin root lists

83. Which of these words is formed only by adding a prefix to a root?
(a) Quickly (b) Rewrite (c) Happiness (d) Carefully
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Quickly", "happiness" and "carefully" all add suffixes (-ly, -ness, -fully), which tempts quick readers. Only "rewrite" adds the prefix "re-" to the root "write". The answer is Rewrite.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (word formation)

85. "Polyglot" refers to a person who:
(a) has many spouses (b) knows many languages (c) studies rocks (d) fears crowds
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Polygamy" (many spouses) tempts because it shares "poly" (many), but "glot" relates to tongue/language. A polyglot knows many languages, so the answer is knows many languages.

86. In "disagreement", how many morphemes (meaningful units) are there?
(a) One (b) Three (c) Two (d) Four
Answer: (b)
Solution: Counting syllables tempts students toward four, but morphemes are meaning units: dis- + agree + -ment = three. The answer is Three.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (word formation)

87. The root "mort" (as in mortal, immortal, mortuary) means:
(a) death (b) birth (c) war (d) mind
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Nat" means birth and misleads some, but "mort" means death — mortgage (dead pledge), mortify, post-mortem. The answer is death.

88. Which word means "a word spelled like another but different in meaning and origin"?
(a) Homophone (b) Synonym (c) Homonym (d) Antonym
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Homophone" tempts because it also shares "homo" (same), but homophones only sound alike; homonyms share spelling. The answer is Homonym.

89. The suffix "-ology" means:
(a) fear of (b) study of (c) love of (d) hatred of
Answer: (b)
Solution: "-phobia" (fear) and "-phile" (love) tempt because they appear in similar word lists, but "-ology" means study of — biology, geology, zoology. The answer is study of.

90. "Autobiography" can be broken into auto + bio + graphy, meaning:
(a) self + earth + study (b) again + life + sound (c) self + life + writing (d) self + book + writing
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Biblio" means book and tempts because of "bibliography", but "bio" means life and "graphy" means writing. The answer is self + life + writing.

95. In "misunderstanding", the root word is:
(a) mis (b) under (c) stand (d) understanding
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Understanding" tempts because it looks like a complete base word, but the true root is "stand"; "under-", "mis-" and "-ing" are all affixes. The answer is stand.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English (word formation)

96. The root "path" (as in sympathy, pathetic, pathology) means:
(a) disease only (b) father (c) road (d) feeling or suffering
Answer: (d)
Solution: The English word "path" (road) is the sound-right trap, but the Greek root "pathos" means feeling or suffering — empathy, antipathy, apathy. The answer is feeling or suffering.
Source: Standard Greek root lists

97. Which pair of words shares the same root?
(a) Biology and Chronicle (b) Predict and Portable (c) Inspect and Spectacle (d) Transport and Dictate
Answer: (c)
Solution: The other pairs look plausible but mix different roots: "predict" has "dict" (say) while "portable" has "port" (carry); "biology" has "bio" (life) while "chronicle" has "chron" (time); "transport" has "port" while "dictate" has "dict". "Inspect" and "spectacle" both come from "spect" (to look), so the answer is Inspect and Spectacle.
Source: PYQ-style

98. The suffix "-ify" (as in simplify, clarify) generally means:
(a) one who (b) full of (c) to make or become (d) without
Answer: (c)
Solution: "-ful" (full of) tempts because of visual similarity, but "-ify" is a verb-forming suffix meaning to make or become — purify, justify, magnify. The answer is to make or become.
Source: Standard suffix lists

99. "Anachronism" (something out of its proper time) is built from ana- (against/back) + chron (time). Which word shares the "chron" root?
(a) Chorus (b) Chrome (c) Chronicle (d) Anchor
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Chrome" is the classic look-alike trap — it comes from "chroma" (colour), not time. "Chorus" and "anchor" are unrelated. A chronicle is a record of events in time, so the answer is Chronicle.
Source: Standard Greek root lists

100. Read the sentence: "The philanthropist's misanthropic brother refused to attend the charity event." Based on the roots "phil" (love), "mis" (hate) and "anthrop" (mankind), the two brothers:
(a) both love humanity (b) both hate humanity (c) neither cares about humanity (d) one loves and one hates humanity
Answer: (d)
Solution: The similar structure of the two words tempts readers into treating them as synonyms, but the prefixes are opposites: "philanthropist" = lover of mankind, "misanthrope" = hater of mankind. The answer is one loves and one hates humanity.
Source: PYQ-style
