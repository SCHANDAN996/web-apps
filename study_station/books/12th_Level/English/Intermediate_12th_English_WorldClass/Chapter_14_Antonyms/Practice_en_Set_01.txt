1. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: ABUNDANT
(a) Plentiful (b) Copious (c) Ample (d) Scarce
Answer: (d)
Solution: Plentiful, ample and copious are all synonyms of abundant, so they are traps for a reader who misses the word OPPOSITE. Scarce means in short supply, so the answer is Scarce.
Source: PYQ-style
