Practice Set 3 — Antonyms (Questions 51–75)
Difficulty: Questions 51–60 Easy | Questions 61–75 Medium

51. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: ANCIENT
(a) Old (b) Antique (c) Modern (d) Aged
Answer: (c)
Solution: Old, antique and aged are all synonyms of ancient, so they are traps for a reader who misses the word OPPOSITE. Ancient means belonging to the distant past, so its opposite is modern.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (Vocabulary)

Answer: (b)
Solution: Enlarge, extend and spread all mean to grow bigger, so they mislead anyone who ignores OPPOSITE. Expand means to increase in size; its opposite is contract, meaning to shrink.
