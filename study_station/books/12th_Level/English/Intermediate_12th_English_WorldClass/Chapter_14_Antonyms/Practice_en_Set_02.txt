26. Choose the antonym of ANCIENT.
(a) Old (b) Antique (c) Modern (d) Aged
Answer: (c)
Solution: Old, antique and aged are synonyms of ancient, not opposites — a classic trap. The opposite of ancient is modern, so the answer is modern.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

27. Choose the antonym of VICTORY.
(a) Defeat (b) Win (c) Triumph (d) Success
Answer: (a)
Solution: Triumph, win and success all mean the same as victory and are placed to confuse. The opposite of victory is defeat, so the answer is defeat.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English
