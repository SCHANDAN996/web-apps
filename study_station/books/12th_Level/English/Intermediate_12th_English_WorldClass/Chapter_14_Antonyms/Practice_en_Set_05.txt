101. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: ABUNDANT
(a) Scarce (b) Plentiful (c) Copious (d) Ample
Answer: (a)
Solution: Plentiful, copious and ample are all synonyms of abundant, so they are tempting traps for a careless reader. The opposite of abundant is scarce, so the answer is Scarce.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: TRANSPARENT
(a) Clear (b) Obvious (c) Opaque (d) Fragile
Answer: (c)
Solution: Clear and obvious are near-synonyms, and fragile relates to breakability, not visibility. The true opposite of transparent is opaque, so the answer is Opaque.
