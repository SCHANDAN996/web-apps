Antonyms — Practice Set 6 (Questions 126–150)
Difficulty: Questions 126–135 Medium | Questions 136–150 Hard

126. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: FRUGAL
(a) thrifty (b) extravagant (c) prudent (d) economical
Answer: (b)
Solution: Thrifty, prudent and economical are all synonyms of frugal, placed here to trap students who read the question as "similar meaning". Frugal means careful with money; its opposite is extravagant, so the answer is extravagant.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: TRANSPARENT
(a) opaque (b) obvious (c) clear (d) fragile
Answer: (a)
Solution: Clear and obvious are near-synonyms, and fragile describes breakability, not visibility. Transparent means allowing light through; its antonym is opaque, so the answer is opaque.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

128. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: HUMBLE
(a) modest (b) arrogant (c) meek (d) timid
Answer: (b)
Solution: Modest and meek are synonyms of humble, and timid means shy, which is not a true opposite. Humble means not proud; its antonym is arrogant, so the answer is arrogant.
Source: PYQ-style

129. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: EXPAND

Answer: (c)
