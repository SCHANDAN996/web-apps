Card 1
Front: What is an antonym? Give one example.
Back: A word with the opposite meaning of another word. Example: Ancient ↔ Modern.

Card 2
Front: Antonym of "Benevolent".
Back: Malevolent (unkind, wishing harm). Root: bene = good, male = bad.

Card 3
Front: Antonym of "Transparent".
Back: Opaque. Trap: "Translucent" (partly clear) is NOT the opposite — it is a middle state.

Card 4
Front: Antonym of "Abundant".
Back: Scarce / Meagre. Remember: Abundant = plenty; Scarce = hardly any.

Card 5
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember "Candid ↔ Evasive"?
Back: "CANDID camera shows everything; EVASIVE people evade (dodge) everything." Candid = frank; Evasive = avoiding direct answers.

Card 6
Front: Antonym of "Expand".
Back: Contract. Trap: "Contact" is a look-alike distractor — read options carefully.

Card 7
Front: Antonym of "Optimistic".
Back: Pessimistic. Optimist sees the glass half full; pessimist sees it half empty.

Card 8
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: What is the antonym of "Guilty" — "Innocent" or "Not guilty"?
Back: Innocent. In vocabulary questions, choose the single opposite WORD, not a legal phrase.

Card 9
Front: Antonym of "Rigid".
Back: Flexible. Rigid = stiff, unbending; Flexible = easily bent or changed.

Card 10
Front: Antonym of "Victory".
Back: Defeat. Also learn the pair: Triumph ↔ Defeat (triumph = a great victory; defeat = loss).

Card 11
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember "Zenith ↔ Nadir"?
Back: "Zenith = Z = top of the sky (highest point); Nadir = N = down at the bottom (lowest point)."

Card 12
Front: Antonym of "Courage".
Back: Cowardice. Related pair: Brave ↔ Timid.

Card 13
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: Antonym of "Genuine" — "Artificial" or "Fake"?
Back: Both mean false, but in exams pick the option that matches the word's register: "Spurious/Artificial" is the standard answer for objects; "Fake" is informal. Choose the most precise formal opposite given.

Card 14
Front: Antonym of "Ancient".
Back: Modern / Contemporary. Pair with: Antique ↔ New.

Card 15
Front: Antonym of "Humble".
Back: Arrogant / Proud. Humble = modest; Arrogant = having an exaggerated sense of self-importance.

Card 16
Front: Antonym of "Permanent".
Back: Temporary. Root: manere (Latin) = to stay; permanent = staying forever.

Card 17
Front: Antonym of "Accept".
Back: Reject. Trap: "Except" is a sound-alike distractor, not an antonym.

Card 18
Front: Antonym of "Scarce".
Back: Plentiful / Abundant. Reverse of Card 4 — examiners test pairs in both directions.

Card 19
Front: Antonym of "Hostile".
Back: Friendly / Amicable. Hostile = unfriendly, aggressive.

Card 20
Front: Quick strategy: If you don't know a word in an antonym question, what should you do?
Back: Break it into root + prefix/suffix (e.g., "in-" often means "not"), eliminate options with the same meaning, and guess between the remaining opposites. Skip if two options still look equally opposite.
