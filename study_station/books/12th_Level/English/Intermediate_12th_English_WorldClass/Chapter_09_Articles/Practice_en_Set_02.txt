26. Choose the correct article: She is ___ honest woman.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the first sound is a vowel sound. The tempting wrong option is "a", chosen by learners who look at the letter 'h' instead of the sound; articles depend on pronunciation, not spelling, so the answer is "an".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. Choose the correct article: ___ sun rises in the east.
(a) A (b) The (c) No article (d) An
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Sun" is a unique object — there is only one — so it takes the definite article. The tempting wrong option is "a", but we never use "a" or "an" with unique things like the sun, the moon, or the earth, so the answer is "The".

Answer: (c)
Solution: In double comparative structures like "the more… the more…" and "the sooner… the better…", the definite article is used before both comparatives. The tempting wrong option is "no article", since comparatives elsewhere take none, but this parallel structure is a fixed exception, so the answer is "The, the".
