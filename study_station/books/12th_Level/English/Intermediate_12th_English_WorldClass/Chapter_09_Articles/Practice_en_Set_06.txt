126. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is an university student. (b) He is the university student. (c) He is a university student. (d) He is university student.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is "an university" — "university" begins with a consonant sound /juː/, so "an" is wrong despite the vowel letter. "A" is used before consonant sounds, so the answer is "He is a university student."
Source: PYQ-style

127. Spot the error: Part P: The honesty / Part Q: is / Part R: the best policy. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Abstract nouns like "honesty" take no article when used in a general sense. The tempting choice is Part R, but "the best policy" is correct because superlatives take "the". The error is in Part P — it should be just "Honesty", so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Fill in the blank: Mount Everest is ___ highest peak in the world.
(a) a (b) no article (c) an (d) the
Answer: (d)
Solution: Superlative adjectives ("highest") always take "the". The trap is leaving the blank empty, which some learners do because "Mount Everest" itself takes no article — but the article belongs to "highest peak", so the answer is "the".

Solution: In the double comparative structure "the more… the more…", "the" is used before both comparatives. The trap is omitting both articles, treating "more" as an ordinary adverb. The fixed idiom requires "the", so the answer is "The, the".

150. Choose the correct sentence:
