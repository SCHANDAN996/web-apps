51. He is ___ honest man.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the first sound is a vowel sound /ɒ/. The tempting option "a" is wrong because the choice of article depends on sound, not spelling. Since the sound is a vowel, the answer is "an".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. ___ sun rises in the east.
(a) A (b) An (c) No article (d) The
Answer: (d)
Solution: "A" and "an" are tempting but wrong because the sun is a unique object — there is only one. Unique things in the universe take the definite article, so the answer is "The".

53. I saw ___ one-eyed man at the station.
(a) an (b) a (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "An" is tempting because "one" starts with the vowel letter 'o', but "one" is pronounced /wʌn/ — it begins with a consonant sound /w/. Articles follow sound, so the answer is "a".

Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting instinct is to remove "the" before comparatives, but the double comparative structure "the more… the more…" correctly uses "the" in both parts. The sentence is correct, so the answer is "No error".
