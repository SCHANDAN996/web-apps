# 📋 Important Grammar Rules & Tips — Articles

## 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use **"a"** before consonant *sounds*, **"an"** before vowel *sounds* — sound matters, not the letter. | a university, a one-rupee coin, an hour, an honest man, an M.P. | "An university" looks right (vowel letter) but is wrong — "university" begins with a /y/ sound. |
| 2 | Use **"a/an"** when mentioning a singular countable noun for the first time (any one, not specific). | I saw **a** dog in the street. | Using "the" for a first, non-specific mention is a common error. |
| 3 | Use **"the"** when the noun is mentioned again or is already known to both speaker and listener. | I saw a dog. **The** dog was black. | Options often swap "a" and "the" between the two sentences. |
| 4 | Use **"the"** before superlatives and ordinal numbers. | **the** best player, **the** first boy, **the** most honest man | "A best player" or "most honest man" (without "the") is the classic trap. |
| 5 | Use **"the"** before unique things — one of their kind. | **the** sun, **the** moon, **the** earth, **the** sky | "A sun" is wrong in ordinary usage; examiners test this with moon/earth. |
| 6 | Use **"the"** before holy/famous books, newspapers, and historical monuments. | **the** Ramayana, **the** Times of India, **the** Taj Mahal | Dropping "the" before newspaper names is a frequent error option. |
| 7 | Use **"the"** before rivers, seas, oceans, mountain *ranges*, and groups of islands. | **the** Ganga, **the** Indian Ocean, **the** Himalayas, **the** Andamans | But **no article** before a single mountain peak or single island: Mount Everest, Sri Lanka. |
| 8 | **No article** before proper nouns — names of persons, countries (single word), states, cities. | India, Ramesh, Mumbai, Bihar | Trap: "The India" is wrong; but countries with words like Republic/Kingdom/States take "the": the USA, the UK, the Republic of Ireland. |
| 9 | **No article** before uncountable nouns and plural nouns used in a general sense. | Water is life. Honesty is the best policy. Children love sweets. | "The honesty is the best policy" is wrong when speaking generally. |
| 10 | **No article** before names of languages, subjects, games, and meals. | Hindi, Physics, cricket, lunch | "The cricket" (as a game) is wrong; but "the Hindi language" is correct. |
| 11 | Use **"the"** before adjectives used as nouns for a whole class of people. | **the** rich, **the** poor, **the** blind, **the** old | These are plural in sense: "The rich *are* not always happy" — not "is". |
| 12 | **No article** before nouns like school, college, hospital, prison, church, bed when used for their *primary purpose*. | He goes to **school** (to study). She is in **hospital** (as a patient). | With "the", the meaning changes: "He went to **the** school" = visited the building, not to study. |

## Quick Memory Tips

- **Sound, not spelling**: an hour, an heir, an honest man (silent "h") vs. a horse, a hotel, a European, a useful book.
- **Abbreviations**: go by the sound of the first letter spoken — an M.A. (em), an S.P. (es), a B.A. (bee).
- **"The" + comparative** in parallel structures: *The more, the merrier.* / *The harder you work, the better you score.*
- **Same → always "the"**: the same book, the same day.
- **Few / a few / the few** and **little / a little / the little** are tested together with articles — remember: "a few/a little" = some (positive), "few/little" = almost none (negative).

## How to Use These Rules in the Exam

1. First check whether the noun is **countable singular** — if yes, it almost always needs a/an/the.
2. Ask: **specific or general?** Specific → "the"; general/uncountable/plural → usually no article.
3. Read the word **aloud in your head** to decide between "a" and "an" — trust the sound.
4. In error-spotting questions, check every blank/article against Rules 1, 4, 7, 8, and 12 first — these are the most frequently tested.
