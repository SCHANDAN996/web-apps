1. Choose the correct article: She is ___ honest woman.
(a) the (b) a (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option is "a", chosen by learners who look at the spelling instead of the sound. "Honest" begins with a silent h, so the first sound is a vowel sound, and "an" is used before vowel sounds, so the answer is "an".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. Choose the correct article: I saw ___ one-eyed man at the station.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "an", because "one" starts with the vowel letter o. But "one" is pronounced with a "w" sound, which is a consonant sound, so the answer is "a".

3. Fill in the blank: ___ Ganga is the holiest river of India.
(a) A (b) The (c) An (d) No article
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "no article", since we do not use articles before most proper nouns. But names of rivers always take the definite article, so the answer is "The".
