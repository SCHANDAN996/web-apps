76. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is an university student. (b) He is a university student. (c) He is the university student. (d) He is university student.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option uses "an" because "university" begins with a vowel letter, but articles follow sound, not spelling. "University" begins with a /juː/ (y) sound, which is consonantal, so it takes "a". Hence the correct sentence is "He is a university student."
