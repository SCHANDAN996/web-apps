Cloze Test — Set 6 (Questions 126–150)
Difficulty: 10 Medium, 15 Hard

Directions (Q. 126–135): In the following passage, there are ten blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank.

Passage: Artificial intelligence is no longer a distant dream; it has quietly __(126)__ into our daily lives. From the moment we wake up to a smartphone alarm to the recommendations we __(127)__ on streaming platforms, algorithms shape our choices. Yet this convenience comes at a __(128)__. Every click, search and purchase leaves a digital footprint that companies __(129)__ to build detailed profiles of users. Critics argue that such data collection, if left __(130)__, could erode personal privacy beyond repair. Governments around the world are now __(131)__ with the challenge of regulating technology without stifling innovation. The European Union's data protection law is often __(132)__ as a model, but enforcement remains uneven. Ultimately, the question is not whether AI will advance — it surely will — but whether society can __(133)__ its benefits while guarding against its risks. This will require not just laws, but a fundamental __(134)__ in how we think about the relationship between humans and machines. The choices we make today will __(135)__ the world our children inherit.

126. Choose the correct word for blank 126.
(a) marched (b) leapt (c) crept (d) fled
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Leapt" and "marched" suggest sudden, visible movement, which contradicts the word "quietly" in the sentence — a classic sound-right trap. "Crept" means moved slowly and unnoticed, matching "quietly" and the idea of gradual entry into daily life, so the answer is crept.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Choose the correct word for blank 127.
(a) receive (b) reject (c) ignore (d) create
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Create" is tempting because users do generate content, but the sentence says algorithms shape our choices, meaning platforms give us recommendations. Users "receive" recommendations on streaming platforms, so the answer is receive.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Choose the correct word for blank 128.
(a) cost (b) price (c) value (d) worth
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Price" sounds right but refers to money paid for goods; the fixed idiom for a disadvantage accompanying a benefit is "at a cost". "Value" and "worth" are positive and contradict the warning tone, so the answer is cost.
Source: PYQ-style

129. Choose the correct word for blank 129.
(a) waste (b) destroy (c) hide (d) harvest
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Hide" is tempting since data collection is secretive, but the sentence needs a verb showing companies use the footprints to build profiles. "Harvest" means to gather and exploit, fitting the context, so the answer is harvest.
Source: PYQ-style

130. Choose the correct word for blank 130.
(a) checked (b) unchecked (c) finished (d) started
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Checked" is the direct opposite trap — if collection were checked, privacy would not erode. The warning "could erode personal privacy" requires the negative condition "unchecked" (uncontrolled), so the answer is unchecked.
Source: PYQ-style

131. Choose the correct word for blank 131.
(a) playing (b) sleeping (c) agreeing (d) grappling
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Agreeing" sounds plausible with "challenge" but one does not agree with a challenge; the idiom is "grapple with a challenge", meaning to struggle to deal with it. So the answer is grappling.
Source: PYQ-style

132. Choose the correct word for blank 132.
(a) blamed (b) sited (c) sighted (d) cited
Answer: (d)
Solution: This is a homophone trap: "sited" (located) and "sighted" (seen) sound identical but are wrong here. "Cited as a model" means quoted or referred to as an example, so the answer is cited.
Source: PYQ-style

133. Choose the correct word for blank 133.
(a) harness (b) hoard (c) harm (d) halt
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Hoard" (store greedily) is a sound-alike trap and clashes with "benefits". "Harness" means to control and use something productively, which pairs naturally with "benefits", so the answer is harness.
Source: PYQ-style

134. Choose the correct word for blank 134.
(a) shift (b) pause (c) delay (d) copy
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Pause" and "delay" suggest stopping, but the passage calls for change, not inaction. "A fundamental shift in how we think" is the standard collocation for a deep change in thinking, so the answer is shift.
Source: PYQ-style

135. Choose the correct word for blank 135.
(a) destroy (b) shape (c) ignore (d) repeat
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Destroy" is an over-negative trap; the passage is balanced, not doomsday. "Shape the world our children inherit" matches the forward-looking, responsible tone, so the answer is shape.
Source: PYQ-style

Directions (Q. 136–145): In the following passage, there are ten blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank.

Passage: Climate change is often described as a future threat, but for millions of farmers in India it is a __(136)__ reality. Unpredictable monsoons have __(137)__ sowing schedules, while rising temperatures reduce crop yields year after year. Small farmers, who own less than two hectares, are the most __(138)__ because they lack savings and insurance. When a harvest fails, many are forced to __(139)__ money from informal lenders at crushing interest rates. This debt trap has __(140)__ consequences: families sell land, children leave school, and migration to cities accelerates. Scientists warn that without urgent adaptation — drought-resistant seeds, better irrigation, crop insurance — the situation will __(141)__ further. Some states have shown that change is possible: community water harvesting in parts of Rajasthan has __(142)__ once-barren land. Yet such success stories remain __(143)__ rather than the norm. What is needed is a national effort that treats climate adaptation not as charity but as an __(144)__ in the country's future. The cost of action, however large, is far smaller than the cost of __(145)__.

136. Choose the correct word for blank 136.
(a) distant (b) unlikely (c) imaginary (d) present
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Distant" is the trap because it echoes "future threat" in the first clause, but the contrast word "but" demands the opposite. The sentence contrasts future threat with a "present" reality, so the answer is present.
Source: PYQ-style

137. Choose the correct word for blank 137.
(a) improved (b) disrupted (c) followed (d) simplified
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Improved" is a tone trap — the whole passage is about harm from climate change. "Disrupted" (thrown into disorder) fits unpredictable monsoons damaging sowing schedules, so the answer is disrupted.
Source: PYQ-style

138. Choose the correct word for blank 138.
(a) vulnerable (b) powerful (c) wealthy (d) careless
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Careless" is tempting because it sounds negative, but the reason given — lack of savings and insurance — is about exposure to harm, not attitude. "Vulnerable" means easily harmed, so the answer is vulnerable.
Source: PYQ-style

139. Choose the correct word for blank 139.
(a) lend (b) donate (c) borrow (d) invest
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Lend" is the direction trap — farmers in crisis take money, they do not give it. One "borrows money from lenders", so the answer is borrow.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Choose the correct word for blank 140.
(a) trivial (b) far-reaching (c) pleasant (d) imaginary
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Trivial" contradicts the serious list that follows (selling land, children leaving school). "Far-reaching" means having wide and serious effects, matching the listed consequences, so the answer is far-reaching.
Source: PYQ-style

141. Choose the correct word for blank 141.
(a) improve (b) vanish (c) worsen (d) stabilize
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Vanish" is the over-thinking trap — problems do not disappear without action. The warning "without urgent adaptation" implies deterioration, so the answer is worsen.
Source: PYQ-style

142. Choose the correct word for blank 142.
(a) destroyed (b) flooded (c) revived (d) abandoned
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Flooded" is a sound-right trap linked to "water harvesting", but flooding barren land is not a success story. "Revived" means brought back to life, matching "success stories", so the answer is revived.
Source: PYQ-style

143. Choose the correct word for blank 143.
(a) causes (b) rules (c) exceptions (d) results
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Rules" is the trap because it pairs with "norm", but "rather than the norm" requires its opposite. "Exceptions rather than the norm" is the standard phrase, so the answer is exceptions.
Source: PYQ-style

144. Choose the correct word for blank 144.
(a) expense (b) obstacle (c) investment (d) accident
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Expense" is tempting because the next sentence mentions cost, but the sentence contrasts charity with something positive and forward-looking. "An investment in the country's future" is the intended contrast, so the answer is investment.
Source: PYQ-style

145. Choose the correct word for blank 145.
(a) action (b) progress (c) success (d) inaction
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Action" is the direct reversal trap — it would make the sentence say action costs more than itself. The logic is that doing nothing costs more than acting, so the answer is inaction.
Source: PYQ-style

Directions (Q. 146–150): In the following passage, there are five blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank.

Passage: The Indian economy today presents a curious paradox. On one hand, it is among the fastest-growing major economies; on the other, job creation has not __(146)__ pace with output growth. Economists call this "jobless growth", a phrase that __(147)__ the frustration of millions of young graduates. The services sector, especially information technology, has __(148)__ impressive revenues, yet it employs a relatively small share of the workforce. Manufacturing, which could absorb semi-skilled workers in large numbers, has remained __(149)__ due to infrastructure gaps and complex regulations. Bridging this gap between growth and employment is perhaps the most __(150)__ policy challenge of our time.

146. Choose the correct word for blank 146.
(a) kept (b) lost (c) made (d) broken
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Made pace" and "broken pace" are collocation traps — the fixed phrase is "keep pace with", meaning to match the speed of something. So the answer is kept.
Source: PYQ-style

147. Choose the correct word for blank 147.
(a) captures (b) hides (c) creates (d) ends
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Creates" is tempting because the phrase is new, but the sentence says the phrase expresses existing frustration. "Captures" means expresses or encapsulates, so the answer is captures.
Source: PYQ-style

148. Choose the correct word for blank 148.
(a) consumed (b) generated (c) avoided (d) reduced
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Consumed" reverses the meaning — a sector produces revenue, it does not eat it. "Generated impressive revenues" is the correct collocation, so the answer is generated.
Source: PYQ-style

149. Choose the correct word for blank 149.
(a) dominant (b) stagnant (c) booming (d) invisible
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Invisible" is an over-statement trap — manufacturing exists but has not grown. "Stagnant" (not growing) matches "infrastructure gaps and complex regulations", so the answer is stagnant.
Source: PYQ-style

150. Choose the correct word for blank 150.
(a) trivial (b) optional (c) amusing (d) pressing
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Trivial" and "optional" contradict the serious tone of the whole passage. "Pressing" means urgent and demanding attention, fitting "policy challenge of our time", so the answer is pressing.
Source: PYQ-style
