Cloze Test — Practice Set 3 (Questions 51–75)
Difficulty: Questions 51–60 Easy | Questions 61–75 Medium

Passage for Questions 51–55:
The Internet has changed the way we live. It has made communication faster and ___51___. People can now ___52___ with friends across the world in seconds. However, spending too much time online can ___53___ our health. Doctors advise us to take regular ___54___ from screens. A balanced use of technology is the ___55___ to a healthy life.

51. Choose the correct word for blank 51.
(a) cheap (b) cheapest (c) cheaper (d) cheaply
Answer: (c)
Solution: The word "faster" is a comparative, and "and" joins parallel forms, so a comparative adjective is needed. "Cheap" is positive degree and breaks the parallelism; "cheapest" is superlative and "cheaply" is an adverb, so the answer is cheaper.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Choose the correct word for blank 52.
(a) connected (b) connects (c) connecting (d) connect
Answer: (d)
Solution: After the modal "can", the base form of the verb is required. "Connects", "connected" and "connecting" cannot follow a modal directly, so the answer is connect.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Choose the correct word for blank 53.
(a) affect (b) improve (c) effect (d) effects
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option "effect" is a noun, but the blank needs a verb after "can". The context is negative (too much screen time), so "improve" is wrong in meaning; "effects" is a plural noun, so the answer is affect.
Source: PYQ-style

54. Choose the correct word for blank 54.
(a) brakes (b) bricks (c) breaths (d) breaks
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Brakes" (stopping devices) is a sound-alike trap; the idiom is "take a break", meaning rest. "Bricks" and "breaths" do not fit the context of resting from screens, so the answer is breaks.
Source: PYQ-style

55. Choose the correct word for blank 55.
(a) key (b) lock (c) door (d) chain
Answer: (a)
Solution: The fixed expression is "the key to success/health". "Lock", "door" and "chain" do not form this collocation, so the answer is key.
Source: PYQ-style

Passage for Questions 56–60:
Ravi was a poor farmer. He worked hard in his fields every day. One morning he found a bag of gold coins ___56___ under a tree. He was an honest man, so he ___57___ the bag to the village headman. The headman praised his ___58___ and gave him a reward. Ravi used the money to ___59___ a small school for the village children. Everyone ___60___ him for his kindness.

56. Choose the correct word for blank 56.
(a) hide (b) hidden (c) hiding (d) hides
Answer: (b)
Solution: The bag "was found hidden" — a past participle is needed to describe the state of the bag. "Hide" is base form, "hiding" is active (the bag did not hide itself), and "hides" is present tense, so the answer is hidden.
Source: PYQ-style

57. Choose the correct word for blank 57.
(a) took (b) takes (c) take (d) taken
Answer: (a)
Solution: The whole passage is in the past tense ("found", "was"), so the past tense verb is required. "Take" and "takes" are present forms and "taken" cannot stand alone as a main verb, so the answer is took.
Source: PYQ-style

58. Choose the correct word for blank 58.
(a) honest (b) honestly (c) honesty (d) dishonesty
Answer: (c)
Solution: After the possessive "his", a noun is needed. "Honest" is an adjective and "honestly" is an adverb; "dishonesty" is the opposite of the context, so the answer is honesty.
Source: PYQ-style

59. Choose the correct word for blank 59.
(a) building (b) built (c) build (d) builds
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Used the money to…" requires the infinitive (to + base verb). "Built", "building" and "builds" cannot follow "to", so the answer is build.
Source: PYQ-style

60. Choose the correct word for blank 60.
(a) praise (b) praised (c) praises (d) praising
Answer: (b)
Solution: The narrative is in past tense, so the verb must be past. "Praise" and "praises" are present forms and "praising" needs an auxiliary, so the answer is praised.
Source: PYQ-style

Passage for Questions 61–65:
Climate change is one of the biggest challenges of our time. Rising temperatures are ___61___ glaciers to melt, which raises sea levels. Coastal cities may soon face serious ___62___. Scientists say we must reduce carbon ___63___ by using cleaner energy. Solar and wind power are good ___64___ to coal. If we act now, we can still ___65___ the worst effects.

61. Choose the correct word for blank 61.
(a) causing (b) making (c) letting (d) forcing
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Making" is tempting but the structure "making glaciers to melt" is ungrammatical — "make" takes the bare infinitive ("make glaciers melt"). "Cause" correctly takes the to-infinitive ("causing glaciers to melt"); "letting" and "forcing" do not fit the natural process described, so the answer is causing.
Source: PYQ-style

62. Choose the correct word for blank 62.
(a) floods (b) flooding (c) flooded (d) flood
Answer: (b)
Solution: After the adjective "serious", a noun is needed; "flooding" (the phenomenon) fits best as an uncountable noun in the standard collocation "face serious flooding". "Flooded" is a verb form and singular "flood" is unnatural here, so the answer is flooding.
Source: PYQ-style

63. Choose the correct word for blank 63.
(a) emissions (b) admissions (c) permissions (d) missions
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Admissions" is a sound-alike trap meaning entry into institutions. The fixed collocation is "carbon emissions" (gases released into the air); "permissions" and "missions" are unrelated, so the answer is emissions.
Source: PYQ-style

64. Choose the correct word for blank 64.
(a) alternates (b) alterations (c) alternatives (d) alliances
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Alterations" (changes/modifications) is a look-alike trap. "Alternatives to" is the correct collocation meaning substitutes; "alternates" means taking turns and "alliances" means partnerships, so the answer is alternatives.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Choose the correct word for blank 65.
(a) ignore (b) avoid (c) welcome (d) increase
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "can", a base verb is needed and the object is "the worst effects" — "avoid the effects" is the natural collocation. "Ignore" is tempting but we cannot ignore effects that will happen; "welcome" and "increase" reverse the meaning, so the answer is avoid.
Source: PYQ-style

Passage for Questions 66–70:
A healthy diet plays a vital role ___66___ our lives. We should eat plenty of fruits and vegetables ___67___ they provide essential vitamins. Junk food, ___68___ the other hand, contains too much fat and salt. Eating it regularly can lead ___69___ obesity and heart disease. Therefore, we must choose our food ___70___.

66. Choose the correct word for blank 66.
(a) at (b) on (c) in (d) for
Answer: (c)
Solution: The fixed phrase is "play a role in something". "On", "at" and "for" do not collocate with "role" in this sense, so the answer is in.
Source: PYQ-style

67. Choose the correct word for blank 67.
(a) unless (b) because (c) but (d) although
Answer: (b)
Solution: The second clause gives the reason for eating fruits and vegetables, so a reason conjunction is needed. "Although" and "but" show contrast and "unless" shows condition, so the answer is because.
Source: PYQ-style

68. Choose the correct word for blank 68.
(a) in (b) by (c) at (d) on
Answer: (d)
Solution: The fixed idiom is "on the other hand". "In the other hand" is a common Hindi-speaker error; "at" and "by" are never used in this idiom, so the answer is on.
Source: PYQ-style

69. Choose the correct word for blank 69.
(a) into (b) to (c) for (d) towards
Answer: (b)
Solution: The verb "lead" takes the preposition "to" — "lead to obesity". "Lead into" means to introduce a topic, and "for"/"towards" do not collocate here, so the answer is to.
Source: PYQ-style

70. Choose the correct word for blank 70.
(a) wisely (b) wisdom (c) wise (d) wiser
Answer: (a)
Solution: The blank modifies the verb "choose", so an adverb is required. "Wise" is an adjective, "wisdom" is a noun and "wiser" is a comparative adjective, so the answer is wisely.
Source: PYQ-style

Passage for Questions 71–75:
Education is the most powerful tool for change. It not only gives us knowledge ___71___ also builds our character. A person who is educated can ___72___ between right and wrong. Sadly, millions of children in the world are still ___73___ of this basic right. Governments must ___74___ that every child goes to school. Only then can we hope to ___75___ poverty and inequality.

71. Choose the correct word for blank 71.
(a) and (b) or (c) yet (d) but
Answer: (d)
Solution: The correlative pair is "not only… but also". "And", "or" and "yet" cannot complete this pair, so the answer is but.
Source: PYQ-style

72. Choose the correct word for blank 72.
(a) distinguish (b) separate (c) divide (d) differ
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Distinguish between right and wrong" is the standard collocation. "Differ" is tempting but it means "to be different" and does not take "between right and wrong" as its object in this sense; "separate" and "divide" imply physical splitting, so the answer is distinguish.
Source: PYQ-style

73. Choose the correct word for blank 73.
(a) stolen (b) robbed (c) deprived (d) denied
Answer: (c)
Solution: The fixed phrase is "deprived of". "Robbed of" is grammatically possible but is used for taking possessions by force, not rights in formal writing; "stolen" does not take "of" with a person as subject, and "denied" takes a direct object without "of", so the answer is deprived.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Choose the correct word for blank 74.
(a) insure (b) reassure (c) assure (d) ensure
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Insure" (relating to insurance) is a sound-alike trap. "Ensure" means to make certain, which fits; "assure" is used with a person as object ("assure somebody"), and "reassure" means to remove doubts, so the answer is ensure.
Source: PYQ-style

75. Choose the correct word for blank 75.
(a) erase (b) delete (c) remove (d) eradicate
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Eradicate" means to destroy completely and is the standard collocation with "poverty". "Erase" is used for marks or writing, "delete" for data, and "remove" is too weak and general for this formal context, so the answer is eradicate.
Source: PYQ-style
