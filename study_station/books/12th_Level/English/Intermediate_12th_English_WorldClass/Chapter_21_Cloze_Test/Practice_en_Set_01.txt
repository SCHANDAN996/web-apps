1. Fill in the blank: The sun rises ___ the east.
(a) on (b) in (c) at (d) from
Answer: (b)
Solution: "On the east" tempts learners who translate from Hindi, but fixed prepositions with directions take "in". The correct phrase is "in the east", so the answer is "in".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

2. Fill in the blank: She has been living in Delhi ___ 2015.
(a) since (b) for (c) from (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "For" is tempting but is used with a period of time (for ten years), while "since" is used with a point of time like a year. Since 2015 is a point of time, the answer is "since".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

3. Fill in the blank: He is fond ___ reading novels.
(a) in (b) of (c) with (d) for
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Fond" always takes the preposition "of"; "fond in" and "fond with" do not exist in standard English. The correct phrase is "fond of", so the answer is "of".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

4. Fill in the blank: The train arrived ___ time.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "In time" means before the deadline, which is a tempting confusion, but "on time" means punctually, at the scheduled moment. The sentence means the train was punctual, so the answer is "on".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

5. Fill in the blank: I am looking forward ___ meeting you.
(a) for (b) at (c) to (d) on
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many learners pick "for" by guessing, but "look forward to" is a fixed phrasal expression followed by a gerund. The correct phrase is "looking forward to meeting", so the answer is "to".

6. Fill in the blank: Neither Ram nor his friends ___ present at the meeting.
(a) was (b) is (c) has been (d) were
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Was" tempts because "Ram" is singular, but with "neither…nor" the verb agrees with the nearer subject, which is "friends" (plural). So the answer is "were".

7. Fill in the blank: The teacher asked the students ___ quiet.
(a) to keep (b) keep (c) keeping (d) kept
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Keep" alone tempts because it follows a verb, but "ask" is followed by an object plus the to-infinitive. The correct structure is "asked the students to keep quiet", so the answer is "to keep".

8. Fill in the blank: He is ___ honest man.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "A" tempts because "honest" begins with the letter h, but the h is silent and the word starts with a vowel sound. Words beginning with a vowel sound take "an", so the answer is "an".

9. Fill in the blank: The Ganga is one of the ___ rivers in India.
(a) long (b) longer (c) most long (d) longest
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Longer" tempts because two things are compared, but "one of the" is always followed by a superlative adjective and a plural noun. The correct form is "one of the longest rivers", so the answer is "longest".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

10. Fill in the blank: If it rains, we ___ at home.
(a) stayed (b) would stay (c) will stay (d) stay
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Would stay" tempts learners who overuse "would" after "if", but this is a first conditional: if + present simple, then will + verb. So the answer is "will stay".

11. Fill in the blank: She sings very ___.
(a) beautifully (b) beautiful (c) beauty (d) beautify
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Beautiful" tempts because it describes her singing, but we need an adverb to modify the verb "sings". The adverb form is "beautifully", so the answer is "beautifully".

12. Fill in the blank: The children were playing ___ the park.
(a) on (b) in (c) at (d) over
Answer: (b)
Solution: "At" tempts for places, but an open enclosed area like a park takes "in" for being inside it. The correct phrase is "in the park", so the answer is "in".

13. Fill in the blank: He ___ his homework before the teacher came.
(a) finished (b) has finished (c) finishes (d) had finished
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Finished" tempts because both actions are past, but when two past actions occur, the earlier one takes the past perfect. Finishing happened before the teacher came, so the answer is "had finished".

14. Fill in the blank: There is ___ water in the glass.
(a) few (b) a few (c) little (d) many
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Few" tempts but is used only with countable plural nouns; water is uncountable. "Little" means almost none, which fits the uncountable noun, so the answer is "little".

15. Fill in the blank: You should abide ___ the rules of the school.
(a) with (b) on (c) to (d) by
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Abide with" tempts because "with" often follows verbs of company, but "abide by" is the fixed phrase meaning to obey. So the answer is "by".

16. Fill in the blank: The poor ___ helped by the government.
(a) are (b) was (c) is (d) has been
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Is" tempts because "the poor" looks singular, but "the + adjective" denoting a class of people is always plural. "The poor" means poor people, so the answer is "are".

17. Fill in the blank: He prevented me ___ going there.
(a) to (b) from (c) for (d) against
Answer: (b)
Solution: "To" tempts by analogy with "stopped me to go", but "prevent" is always followed by "from + gerund". The correct phrase is "prevented me from going", so the answer is "from".

18. Fill in the blank: Each of the boys ___ given a prize.
(a) was (b) were (c) have been (d) are
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "boys" is plural and stands just before the verb, but the true subject is "each", which is singular. So the answer is "was".

19. Fill in the blank: I have been waiting here ___ two hours.
(a) since (b) from (c) for (d) at
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Since" tempts because the sentence is in the perfect continuous tense, but "since" takes a point of time while "two hours" is a duration. Duration takes "for", so the answer is "for".

(a) was (b) are (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "news" ends in s and looks plural, but "news" is an uncountable noun that always takes a singular verb. So the answer is "was".

21. Fill in the blank: The principal, along with the teachers, ___ present at the function.
(a) were (b) are (c) have been (d) was
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "teachers" stands nearest to the verb, but phrases like "along with" do not change the subject; the subject is "the principal" (singular). So the answer is "was".

22. Fill in the blank: Hardly had he reached the station ___ the train left.
(a) then (b) than (c) when (d) that
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Than" tempts because it pairs with "no sooner", but "hardly" and "scarcely" pair with "when". The correct correlative is "hardly…when", so the answer is "when".

23. Fill in the blank: He is one of the best players who ___ ever played for India.
(a) has (b) have (c) was (d) is
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Has" tempts because "one" is singular, but the relative pronoun "who" refers to "players" (plural), not to "one". A plural antecedent takes a plural verb, so the answer is "have".

24. Fill in the blank: Unless you work hard, you ___ succeed.
(a) will not (b) will (c) would (d) can
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Will" tempts because the sentence sounds positive, but "unless" itself carries a negative meaning (if not), so the main clause must be negative to make sense. So the answer is "will not".

25. Fill in the blank: The committee ___ divided in its opinion on the issue.
(a) were (b) are (c) have been (d) was
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Were" tempts because the members disagree, but when a collective noun is viewed as a single body it takes a singular verb; here "its opinion" signals the body as a unit. So the answer is "was".
