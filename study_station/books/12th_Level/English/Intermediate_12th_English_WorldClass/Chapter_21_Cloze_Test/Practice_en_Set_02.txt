26. Fill in the blank: The government has decided to ______ new rules for online shopping.
(a) bring on (b) bring in (c) bring about of (d) bring with
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Bring in" means to introduce something new, such as rules or laws, which fits the context. "Bring on" means to cause something unpleasant, and the other two are not standard phrasal verbs, so the answer is bring in.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Fill in the blank: She has been working here ______ 2015.
(a) for (b) by (c) from (d) since
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (a specific year like 2015). "For" is used with a duration such as "for nine years", which tempts many learners, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

28. Fill in the blank: The children were afraid ______ the dark.
(a) of (b) from (c) with (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: The fixed adjective-preposition combination is "afraid of". No other preposition collocates with "afraid", so the answer is of.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

29. Fill in the blank: Neither of the two boys ______ done his homework.
(a) have (b) are (c) has (d) were
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb, which is a classic examiner trap because "boys" sits right before the blank and tempts "have". The subject is "neither", singular, so the answer is has.
Source: PYQ-style

30. Fill in the blank: He is ______ honest man.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the sound is a vowel sound and takes "an". Learners who look at the letter instead of the sound wrongly pick "a", so the answer is an.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

31. Fill in the blank: The train ______ before we reached the station.
(a) had left (b) left (c) has left (d) leaves
Answer: (a)
Solution: When two past actions occur, the earlier one takes the past perfect. The train's departure happened before our arrival, so the answer is had left.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

32. Fill in the blank: You should abide ______ the rules of the school.
(a) with (b) on (c) to (d) by
Answer: (d)
Solution: The fixed phrase is "abide by", meaning to follow or obey. The other prepositions do not collocate with "abide", so the answer is by.
Source: PYQ-style

33. Fill in the blank: There is ______ water in the pot.
(a) fewer (b) a few (c) many (d) a little
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Water" is uncountable, so "a few", "many" and "fewer" are impossible. "A little" means some water is present, so the answer is a little.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

34. Fill in the blank: The teacher asked the students ______ noise.
(a) not make (b) to not make (c) not to make (d) do not make
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "asked + object", the negative infinitive is "not to make". Placing "not" after "to" splits the infinitive awkwardly here, so the answer is not to make.
Source: PYQ-style

35. Fill in the blank: Delhi is one of the largest ______ in India.
(a) cities (b) city (c) city's (d) cities'
Answer: (a)
Solution: "One of the" is always followed by a plural noun, a favourite examiner trap where learners pick the singular "city". The answer is cities.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

36. Fill in the blank: I look forward ______ meeting you soon.
(a) to (b) for (c) at (d) on
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Look forward to" is a fixed phrase, and here "to" is a preposition, which is why it is followed by the -ing form "meeting". The answer is to.
Source: PYQ-style

37. Fill in the blank: The poor ______ our help.
(a) needs (b) need (c) needing (d) is need
Answer: (b)
Solution: "The poor" refers to poor people as a class and takes a plural verb. Learners treating it as singular pick "needs", so the answer is need.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

38. Fill in the blank: He deals ______ electronic goods.
(a) on (b) with (c) in (d) at
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Deal in" means to trade in something; "deal with" means to handle a person or problem, which is the tempting wrong choice. The context is trade, so the answer is in.
Source: PYQ-style

39. Fill in the blank: Unless you work hard, you ______ pass.
(a) will (b) will not (c) would (d) can
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Unless" already carries a negative meaning (if not), so the main clause needs a negative verb to make sense. The answer is will not.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

40. Fill in the blank: The Ganga ______ into the Bay of Bengal.
(a) falling (b) fall (c) falls (d) is fall
Answer: (c)
Solution: A universal fact about a river takes the simple present tense, and "The Ganga" is singular. The answer is falls.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

41. Fill in the blank: The committee ______ divided in their opinion on the new policy.
(a) was (b) is (c) were (d) has
Answer: (c)
Solution: This is a collective-noun trap. When the members act separately, as "divided in their opinion" shows, the verb is plural; "was" tempts those who treat every collective noun as singular. The answer is were.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Fill in the blank: Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) I reached (c) had I reached (d) I have reached
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "hardly" at the start of a clause, inversion is required, and the earlier action takes the past perfect. "I had reached" sounds right but breaks the inversion rule, so the answer is had I reached.
Source: PYQ-style

43. Fill in the blank: The principal, along with the teachers, ______ present at the meeting.
(a) were (b) are (c) have been (d) was
Answer: (d)
Solution: The intervening phrase "along with the teachers" is the trap; the true subject is "the principal", which is singular. The answer is was.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Fill in the blank: He is senior ______ me by two years.
(a) than (b) to (c) from (d) of
Answer: (b)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior and inferior take "to", not "than". "Than" is the sound-right trap here, so the answer is to.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Fill in the blank: No sooner did she see the police ______ she ran away.
(a) than (b) then (c) when (d) that
Answer: (a)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than"; "when" pairs with "hardly" and "scarcely", which is the tempting confusion. The answer is than.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Fill in the blank: The scenery of Kashmir ______ very beautiful.
(a) are (b) were (c) have been (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb, even though it refers to many sights. The answer is is.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

47. Fill in the blank: I prefer tea ______ coffee.
(a) to (b) over (c) than (d) from
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Prefer" takes "to" when comparing two nouns. "Than" is the common sound-right error, so the answer is to.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Fill in the blank: Each of the students ______ given a certificate.
(a) were (b) was (c) have (d) are
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Each of" takes a singular verb; the plural "students" before the blank is the intervening-phrase trap. The answer is was.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

49. Fill in the blank: He denied ______ the money.
(a) to steal (b) stealing (c) steal (d) stolen
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Deny" is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive. "To steal" tempts learners who apply the common verb + to pattern, so the answer is stealing.
Source: PYQ-style

50. Fill in the blank: The news ______ broadcast an hour ago.
(a) was (b) were (c) are (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" looks plural but is singular, and "an hour ago" fixes the simple past. "Were" is the sound-right trap, so the answer is was.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English
