101. Cloze Test — Read the passage and choose the word that best fits blank (1):
"Artificial intelligence is no longer a distant dream; it has quietly (1) ______ into our daily lives, from the phones we unlock with our faces to the maps that guide us through traffic."
(a) crept (b) leapt (c) fled (d) sank
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Leapt" is tempting because AI is often described as advancing fast, but the adverb "quietly" demands a slow, unnoticed movement, which only "crept" conveys. "Fled" and "sank" are semantically wrong here, so the answer is crept.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Cloze Test — Same theme, blank (2):
"Yet many workers fear that machines will (2) ______ them of their jobs, a worry that economists say is partly justified but often exaggerated."
(a) steal (b) gift (c) rob (d) assure
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Steal" tempts because it relates to taking, but the correct collocation is "rob somebody of something"; "steal" takes the thing as its object, not the person. "Gift" and "assure" reverse the meaning, so the answer is rob.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (3):
"The government has announced a new climate policy that aims to reduce carbon emissions (3) ______ 40 per cent by 2035."
(a) by (b) to (c) with (d) from
Answer: (a)
Solution: "To" is tempting because targets often use "to", but "reduce X by 40 per cent" expresses the size of the reduction, while "reduce to 40 per cent" would mean the final level is 40 per cent of the original — a very different claim. So the answer is by.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (4):
"Although the monsoon arrived late, the farmers did not lose hope; they (4) ______ their fields with great care and waited for the rain."

Answer: (b)
Solution: "Flooded" tempts because rain is mentioned, but flooding fields before rain makes no sense, and "demolished" and "abandoned" contradict "did not lose hope". Only "cultivated" fits the hopeful context, so the answer is cultivated.

106. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (6):
"The new bridge will (6) ______ the travel time between the two cities from six hours to just two."
(a) delay (b) rise (c) reduce (d) extend
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Extend" tempts because travel time is a duration, but the passage shows time falling from six hours to two, so the verb must mean "make less". "Rise" and "delay" move in the wrong direction, so the answer is reduce.

107. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (7):
"______ the heavy rain, the cricket match continued without interruption."
(a) Because (b) Since (c) Unless (d) Despite
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Because" is the classic trap: it would make the sentence say the match continued due to heavy rain, which is illogical. The sentence needs a contrast connector, and only "Despite" gives that, so the answer is Despite.

110. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (10):
"The old manuscript was so (10) ______ that even experts could not read most of it."
(a) illogical (b) illegible (c) illegal (d) illiterate
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Illiterate" is the sound-alike trap: it describes a person who cannot read, not a text that cannot be read. A text that cannot be read is "illegible", so the answer is illegible.

113. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (13):
"Regular exercise not only strengthens the body (13) ______ also sharpens the mind."
(a) but (b) and (c) or (d) yet
Answer: (a)
Solution: "And" tempts because it is the most common connector, but the correlative pair is fixed: "not only … but also". Using "and" breaks the structure, so the answer is but.

114. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (14):
"The company decided to (14) ______ its old products and launch a completely new range."
(a) put off (b) phase in (c) take after (d) phase out
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Phase in" is the mirror-image trap: it means to introduce gradually, which is what happens to the new range, not the old products. Old products are gradually withdrawn, so the answer is phase out.

118. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (18):
"Water is to fish (18) ______ air is to human beings."
(a) which (b) that (c) what (d) so
Answer: (c)
Solution: "That" tempts as a general-purpose connector, but the standard analogy structure in English is "A is to B what C is to D", where "what" introduces the parallel clause. "Which" and "so" cannot fill this frame, so the answer is what.

Solution: "Than" is the classic trap because "no sooner … than" uses "than", but the pair for "hardly/scarcely" is "hardly … when". Mixing the two pairs is a favourite examiner trick, so the answer is when.

125. Cloze Test — Choose the word for blank (25):
