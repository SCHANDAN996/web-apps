1. Choose the correct conjunction: He is poor ______ honest.
(a) and (b) so (c) or (d) but
Answer: (d)
Solution: "And" is tempting because two adjectives are joined, but the ideas contrast (poverty versus honesty), so an adversative conjunction is needed. "But" shows contrast, so the answer is but.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. Fill in the blank: Work hard ______ you will fail.
(a) and (b) but (c) or (d) because
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Because" is tempting as it gives a reason, but the sentence is a warning, not a reason. "Or" means "otherwise", so the answer is or.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Choose the correct option: ______ he was ill, he went to school.
(a) Though (b) So (c) Unless (d) Because
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Because" is tempting since illness explains absence, but here he went despite illness, so a concessive conjunction is required. "Though" expresses concession, so the answer is Though.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

4. Fill in the blank: She is ______ intelligent ______ hardworking.

Answer: (a)
Solution: "Either/or" is tempting as a correlative pair, but the sentence praises both qualities together, not a choice between them. "Not only...but also" adds two positive qualities, so the answer is not only/but also.

21. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) Scarcely had he reached the station when the train left. (b) Scarcely he had reached the station when the train left. (c) Scarcely had he reached the station than the train left. (d) Scarcely he had reached the station than the train left.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The "than" option is tempting because "no sooner" takes "than", but "scarcely" pairs with "when" and needs inversion (had he). Only the sentence with inversion plus "when" is correct, so the answer is "Scarcely had he reached the station when the train left."
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap: scarcely–when vs no sooner–than)

22. Spot the error: Part P: No sooner did the bell ring / Part Q: when the students / Part R: rushed out of the class. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part P looks suspicious because of inversion, but inversion after "no sooner" is correct; the real error is "when" in Part Q, since "no sooner" pairs with "than". Part Q must read "than the students", so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap: sound-right pair confusion)
