26. Choose the correct conjunction: He was tired, ______ he kept working.
(a) so (b) but (c) because (d) or
Answer: (b)
Solution: "So" is tempting because tiredness seems to cause something, but the second clause shows contrast, not result. Contrast needs "but", so the answer is but.

27. Fill in the blank: ______ it was raining, we stayed indoors.
(a) Although (b) Because (c) Unless (d) Until
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Although" is tempting, but it would need a contrast — staying indoors is the natural result of rain, not a contrast. The rain is the reason, so the answer is Because.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

28. Choose the correct pair: ______ you work hard, you will fail.
(a) Unless (b) If (c) Since (d) Because
Answer: (a)
Solution: "If" alone is tempting but would need a negative main clause ("you will not pass"). "Unless" means "if not" and fits the positive warning, so the answer is Unless.

29. Fill in the blank: She is ______ intelligent ______ hardworking.

Answer: (d)
Solution: "Both … or" is tempting but is a wrong pairing — "both" pairs only with "and". Two positive qualities joined emphatically take "not only … but also", so the answer is not only … but also.

41. Spot the error: Scarcely he had (P) / reached the station (Q) / when the train left. (R) / No error (S)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part R is tempting because learners doubt "when" with "scarcely", but "scarcely … when" is correct. The real error is in Part P: after "scarcely" at the start, inversion is required — "Scarcely had he". The error is in Part P.

42. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) No sooner did he see the police than he ran away. (b) No sooner did he see the police when he ran away. (c) No sooner he saw the police than he ran away. (d) No sooner had he saw the police than he ran away.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first option is tempting because "when" sounds natural, but "no sooner" pairs only with "than"; also inversion "did he see" is needed. The correct form is "No sooner did he see the police than he ran away."
