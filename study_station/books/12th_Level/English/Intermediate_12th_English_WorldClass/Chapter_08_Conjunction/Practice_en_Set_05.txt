101. Choose the correct conjunction: "He is poor ______ honest."
(a) and (b) so (c) or (d) but
Answer: (d)
Solution: "And" is tempting because two adjectives are joined, but the two qualities (poor, honest) contrast with each other, so an adversative conjunction is needed. "But" shows contrast, so the answer is "but".

102. Choose the correct option: "______ he worked hard, he failed the exam."
(a) Because (b) Although (c) Unless (d) Since
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Because" is tempting since hard work seems a reason, but the sentence shows a contrast between effort and result, which needs a concessive conjunction. "Although" expresses that contrast, so the answer is "Although".
