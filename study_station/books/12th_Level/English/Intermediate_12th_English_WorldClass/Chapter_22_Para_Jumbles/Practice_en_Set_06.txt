126. Arrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P. Artificial intelligence systems can now diagnose certain diseases with remarkable accuracy.
Q. However, this accuracy depends entirely on the quality of the data they are trained on.
R. If the training data is biased, the diagnosis will be biased too.
S. Therefore, doctors must treat AI as an assistant, not as a replacement for clinical judgement.
(a) PRQS (b) QPRS (c) PQRS (d) PSQR
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting choice is PRQS, but placing R immediately after P breaks the flow because R's "the training data" needs Q's introduction of "data they are trained on" first. P opens with the claim, Q qualifies it, R extends the qualification, and S concludes with "Therefore", so the answer is PQRS.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Arrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.

Q. Nearly half of India's farmland depends on rainfall rather than irrigation.
R. A delayed or weak monsoon can therefore push millions of farmers into distress.
S. This is why the monsoon forecast is watched so anxiously every year.
(a) PQRS (b) QPRS (c) PQSR (d) RQPS
Answer: (a)
Solution: PQSR is tempting, but S's "This is why" must follow the distress described in R, so R must come before S. P introduces the topic, Q supports it with a fact, R draws the consequence with "therefore", and S concludes, so the answer is PQRS.
