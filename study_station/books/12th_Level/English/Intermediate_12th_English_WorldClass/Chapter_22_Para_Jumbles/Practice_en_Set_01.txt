1. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P. The internet has transformed the way people communicate.
Q. Letters that once took weeks now arrive as emails in seconds.
R. Video calls allow families separated by oceans to see each other daily.
S. This revolution in communication has shrunk the world dramatically.
(a) PQRS (b) PRQS (c) QPRS (d) SPRQ
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is starting with S, but "This revolution" needs something before it to refer to. P introduces the topic, Q and R give examples, and S concludes with the overall effect, so the answer is PQRS.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P. He decided to plant a tree in his garden.
Q. Every morning he watered it carefully.
R. Within a few years, it grew into a tall, shady tree.
S. Ravi loved nature from his childhood.
(a) PQRS (b) SPQR (c) SQPR (d) RSPQ
Answer: (b)
Solution: Starting with P is tempting, but "He" is a pronoun that needs Ravi introduced first in S. After S, the events follow time order: deciding, watering, growing, so the answer is SPQR.

(a) QPRS (b) PRQS (c) PQRS (d) PSQR
Answer: (c)
Solution: Any order placing S earlier is a trap because "Hence" marks a conclusion that cannot precede its reasons. P opens generally, Q and R extend the idea to plants and animals, so the answer is PQRS.
