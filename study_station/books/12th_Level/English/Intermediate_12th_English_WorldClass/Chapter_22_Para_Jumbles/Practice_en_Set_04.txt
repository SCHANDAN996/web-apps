76. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P. The internet has transformed the way people access information.
Q. However, this easy access has also made it difficult to distinguish reliable sources from unreliable ones.
R. Before this shift, people depended on libraries and encyclopedias for knowledge.
S. Therefore, developing the skill of evaluating sources has become essential today.
(a) PRQS (b) RPQS (c) PQRS (d) RQPS
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap in RPQS is treating R as an opener, but R says "Before this shift", which is meaningless unless the shift (the internet's transformation in P) has already been mentioned, so P must come first. Q then adds the drawback with "However", and S concludes with "Therefore", so the answer is PRQS.
Source: PYQ-style
