51. Arrange the sentences P, Q, R, S to form a coherent paragraph.
P. He decided to start a small tea stall near the bus stop.
Q. Ramesh lost his job during the lockdown.
R. Within a year, his stall became famous for its ginger tea.
S. With his savings, he bought a stove, utensils and tea leaves.
(a) PQRS (b) QPSR (c) QSPR (d) RQPS
Answer: (b)
Solution: The paragraph tells a story in time order. Q introduces the problem (job loss), P gives his decision, S shows the action using his savings, and R gives the result. Option QSPR wrongly places the purchase before the decision, which breaks the logic, so the answer is QPSR.
