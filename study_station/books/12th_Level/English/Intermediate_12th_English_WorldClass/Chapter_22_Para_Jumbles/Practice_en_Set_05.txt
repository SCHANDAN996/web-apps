101. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P. The internet has transformed how people access information.
Q. However, this abundance of information has created a new problem.
R. Today, a student in a remote village can read the same research paper as a professor in a big city.
S. It is now difficult to separate reliable facts from misleading content.
(a) PSQR (b) PRQS (c) RQPS (d) PQRS
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting choice PQRS fails because R ("Today, a student...") is an illustration of P's claim and must follow P directly. P opens with the general statement, R gives the example, Q introduces the contrast with "However", and S completes the problem Q raises, so the answer is PRQS.
