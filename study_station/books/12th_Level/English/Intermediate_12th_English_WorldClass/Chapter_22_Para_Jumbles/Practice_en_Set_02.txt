26. Rearrange the parts to form a meaningful paragraph.
P. The sun was setting behind the hills.
Q. Birds were returning to their nests.
R. The sky turned orange and pink.
S. It was a beautiful evening.
(a) PRQS (b) SPRQ (c) PSQR (d) QRPS
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting order SPRQ opens with the conclusion "It was a beautiful evening" before anything is described, which breaks the flow. The scene must open with the sun setting, then the sky's colours, then the birds, and end with the concluding remark, so the answer is PRQS.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Rearrange the parts to form a meaningful paragraph.
P. Water is essential for life.
Q. Without water, no plant or animal can survive.
R. Yet people waste water every day.
S. We must learn to conserve it.
(a) PRSQ (b) QPRS (c) SPQR (d) PQRS
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting order PRSQ places the advice before the problem of wastage is even mentioned. The paragraph must move from the general statement to its explanation, then to the contrast signalled by "yet", and close with the advice, so the answer is PQRS.
