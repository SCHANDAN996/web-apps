51. Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error: Part P: One of my friends / Part Q: are going / Part R: to Delhi tomorrow.
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is to think "friends" controls the verb, but the subject is "One", which is singular, so the verb must be "is going". Part Q carries the error, so the answer is Part Q.

54. Select the correctly punctuated indirect speech: He said to me, "I am busy."
(a) He told me that he is busy. (b) He told me that he was busy. (c) He said me that he was busy. (d) He told to me that he was busy.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Said to me" becomes "told me" (not "said me" or "told to me"), and present "am" shifts to past "was" in reported speech, so the answer is "He told me that he was busy."
