# Error Spotting (Advanced) — Practice Set 2
Questions 26–50 | Easy: 26–40 | Medium: 41–50

26. One of my friend (P) / has gone (Q) / to Delhi. (R)
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: "One of" is always followed by a plural noun, so it should be "one of my friends". Part Q tempts students who hunt for a tense error, but "has gone to Delhi" is a correct present perfect. So the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

27. He does not (P) / know how (Q) / to swim. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The sentence is grammatically correct: "does not know" agrees with "he", and "how to swim" is a correct infinitive structure. So the answer is No error.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

28. Each of the boys (P) / have done (Q) / his homework. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Each of" takes a singular verb, so it should be "has done". Part R tempts students who think "his" should be "their", but "his" is correct with "each". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

29. She is more taller (P) / than (Q) / her sister. (R)
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: A double comparative is wrong: "taller" already shows comparison, so "more" must be removed. So the answer is Part P.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

30. I have been living (P) / in this city (Q) / since five years. (R)
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time; a period of time takes "for" — "for five years". So the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

31. The news (P) / are (Q) / very shocking. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "News" looks plural but is singular, so the verb should be "is". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

32. He as well as his brothers (P) / were present (Q) / at the meeting. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject "He", so it should be "was present". The plural "brothers" near the verb is the trap. So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

33. Neither of the two boys (P) / have passed (Q) / the examination. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb, so it should be "has passed". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

34. She told me (P) / that she will (Q) / come the next day. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: In reported speech with a past reporting verb, "will" changes to "would". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

35. The Ganges is (P) / one of the longest (Q) / river in India. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part R
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "one of the", a plural noun is required — "one of the longest rivers". So the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

36. He is senior (P) / than me (Q) / by two years. (R)
(a) No error (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior and inferior take "to", not "than" — "senior to me". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

37. I and Ram (P) / went to (Q) / the market. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: In polite English the first person comes last: "Ram and I". So the answer is Part P.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

38. No sooner did he reach (P) / the station (Q) / when the train left. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "No sooner" is paired with "than", not "when" — "no sooner... than the train left". So the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

39. The teacher asked (P) / the students to not (Q) / make a noise. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (a)
Solution: The negative infinitive is "not to" — "asked the students not to make a noise". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

40. Unless you do not work hard, (P) / you will not (Q) / pass the exam. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Unless" is already negative, so "do not" must become "do" — "Unless you work hard". So the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

41. The number of students (P) / in the class (Q) / are increasing every year. (R)
(a) Part R (b) Part P (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The number of" takes a singular verb ("is increasing"); "a number of" would take a plural verb. The plural word "students" before the verb is the trap. So the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Hardly had he (P) / entered the room (Q) / than the phone rang. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Hardly" and "scarcely" pair with "when", not "than" — "hardly... when the phone rang". So the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

43. Being a rainy day, (P) / I stayed (Q) / at home. (R)
(a) Part R (b) Part P (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a dangling participle: "Being a rainy day" wrongly attaches to "I". The correct form is "It being a rainy day, I stayed at home." So the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

44. He is one of the best players (P) / that has ever played (Q) / for the country. (R)
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part Q (d) Part R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The relative pronoun "that" refers to the plural "players", so the verb should be "have ever played". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Scarcely he had stepped out (P) / of the house (Q) / when it began to rain. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part R (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: When a sentence begins with "Scarcely", inversion is needed: "Scarcely had he stepped out". So the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

46. The principal, along with the teachers, (P) / have decided (Q) / to cancel the tour. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) No error (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject "The principal", so it should be "has decided". The nearby plural "teachers" is the examiner's trap. So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

47. He insisted (P) / to go (Q) / with me. (R)
(a) Part R (b) Part P (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Insist" is followed by "on" plus a gerund — "insisted on going". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

48. Many a student (P) / have failed (Q) / in this test. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Many a" is followed by a singular noun and a singular verb — "has failed". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

49. I prefer (P) / tea more than (Q) / coffee. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Prefer" is followed by "to", not "more than" — "I prefer tea to coffee". So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

50. The committee (P) / has submitted (Q) / its report yesterday. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: A definite past time like "yesterday" requires the simple past, so Part Q should be "submitted", not the present perfect "has submitted". Part R tempts students who want to delete "yesterday", but the time word is fine — the tense is wrong. So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style
