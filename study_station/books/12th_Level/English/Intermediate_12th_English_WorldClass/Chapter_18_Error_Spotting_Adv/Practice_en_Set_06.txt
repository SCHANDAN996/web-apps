126. Spot the error: One of the most (P)/ widely discussed issue (Q)/ in the seminar was (R)/ climate change. (S)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting trap is Part R, because "was" sounds singular and learners suspect the verb. But after "one of the + superlative", the noun must be plural: "issues", while "was" correctly agrees with "one". The error is in Part Q.

127. Spot the error: Neither the manager (P)/ nor the employees (Q)/ was present (R)/ at the meeting. (S)
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) Part R
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many pick Part Q thinking "employees" is wrong, but the noun is fine. With "neither…nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject — "employees" (plural) — so it must be "were present". The error is in Part R.
