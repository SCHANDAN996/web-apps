Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. The train arrived ______ time.
(a) in (b) on (c) at (d) by
Answer: (b)
Solution: "On time" is the fixed expression meaning punctually. "In time" means early enough for something, which changes the meaning.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

2. She has been living in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) for (b) at (c) from (d) since
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (2015). "For" is used with a period of time, such as "for ten years".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

3. He is fond ______ playing cricket.
(a) in (b) at (c) of (d) with
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Fond of" is the correct fixed prepositional phrase. Other prepositions do not collocate with "fond".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

4. The cat is hiding ______ the table.
(a) on (b) above (c) under (d) over
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Under" means directly below, which fits a hiding cat. "On" and "over" suggest the top side, and "above" means higher than, not below.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

5. We go to school ______ bus.
(a) on (b) by (c) with (d) in
Answer: (b)
Solution: "By" is used with means of transport without an article: by bus, by train, by car. "On the bus" would need an article.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

6. The meeting will start ______ 10 a.m.
(a) in (b) at (c) by (d) on
Answer: (b)
Solution: "At" is used with exact clock times. "In" is for months and years, "on" for days and dates.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

7. He jumped ______ the river to save the child.
(a) into (b) on (c) in (d) upon
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Into" shows movement from outside to inside, which matches "jumped". "In" shows position, not movement.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

8. My birthday falls ______ Monday this year.
(a) by (b) at (c) in (d) on
Answer: (d)
Solution: "On" is used with days of the week. "In" is for months and years; "at" is for exact times.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

9. She is afraid ______ spiders.
(a) from (b) with (c) by (d) of
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Afraid of" is the correct fixed phrase. Learners often wrongly write "afraid from" by translating from Hindi.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

10. The book belongs ______ me.
(a) to (b) for (c) with (d) of
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Belong to" is the fixed verb-preposition combination. Note that "belong" is never used in the continuous form.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

11. He has been working here ______ five years.
(a) for (b) since (c) from (d) at
Answer: (a)
Solution: "For" is used with a duration (five years). "Since" needs a starting point, such as "since 2019".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

12. The picture is hanging ______ the wall.
(a) in (b) over (c) at (d) on
Answer: (d)
Solution: "On" is used for something attached to a surface like a wall. "Over" would mean above it without touching.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

13. He died ______ cancer.
(a) of (b) from (c) by (d) with
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Die of" is used for diseases and hunger; "die from" is used for injuries or external causes. This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

14. I will wait here ______ you return.
(a) by (b) since (c) for (d) until
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Until" is used before a clause showing time up to a point. "By" would mean the deadline, not the waiting period.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

15. The children are playing ______ the park.
(a) in (b) at (c) into (d) on
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In" is used for enclosed or open areas like parks. "At the park" suggests a point location, but "playing in" is the natural choice.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

16. He is senior ______ me by two years.
(a) than (b) to (c) from (d) of
Answer: (b)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior take "to", not "than". This is a frequent examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

17. The stars shine ______ night.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) for
Answer: (b)
Solution: "At night" is the fixed expression. Note the contrast: "in the morning/afternoon/evening" but "at night".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

18. She cut the apple ______ a knife.
(a) with (b) by (c) from (d) through
Answer: (a)
Solution: "With" is used for the instrument; "by" is used for the doer or the method. Compare: killed by the robber, killed with a knife.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

19. The shop is closed ______ Sundays.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "On" is used with days, including plural "Sundays" meaning every Sunday. "In" and "at" never pair with days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

20. He apologized ______ his mistake.
(a) about (b) for (c) on (d) at
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Apologize for something" is the correct combination; "apologize to somebody" is used for the person. Mixing the two is a common error.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

21. Spot the error: Part P: He is suffering / Part Q: from fever / Part R: since Monday. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part R (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part P has the error. With "since Monday", the tense must be present perfect continuous: "He has been suffering from fever since Monday." Part Q and Part R are correctly framed.
Source: PYQ-style

22. He insisted ______ going with me.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) for
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Insist on" is the fixed combination, followed by a gerund (going). "Insist in" is a common Hindi-influenced error.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

23. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) He entered into the room quietly. (b) He entered at the room quietly. (c) He entered in the room quietly. (d) He entered the room quietly.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Enter" is never followed by a preposition before a place. "Entered into" is used only for agreements or discussions, e.g., "entered into a contract". This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

24. The two brothers divided the property ______ themselves.
(a) within (b) among (c) between (d) into
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Between" is used for two persons, even with "themselves", when the division is between two individuals. "Among" is used for more than two.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

25. He is good ______ mathematics but weak ______ English.
(a) at, in (b) in, at (c) at, at (d) in, in
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Good at" and "weak in" are the standard fixed combinations. Examiners exploit the mismatch by offering the same preposition for both.
Source: PYQ-style
