# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Preposition

## How Exams Test Prepositions

Across SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD), RRB (NTPC, Group D), Banking (IBPS, SBI) and State exams, prepositions appear in **four recurring formats**:

1. **Fill in the blank** — one preposition missing in a sentence (the most common format).
2. **Spot the error** — a wrong or extra preposition hidden in one part of the sentence.
3. **Sentence improvement** — replace the underlined phrase containing a preposition error.
4. **Idiom/phrase-based** — fixed prepositional phrases (*abide by, consist of, look forward to*).

## Sub-topics That Repeat Most Often

- **Fixed prepositions with verbs/adjectives/nouns** — *depend on, insist on, afraid of, good at, angry with (a person), angry at (a thing)*. This is the single most productive area.
- **Time prepositions** — *in / on / at* (in 2024, on Monday, at 5 pm); *since vs for*; *by vs until*.
- **Place prepositions** — *in / on / at*; *between vs among*; *beside vs besides*.
- **Movement prepositions** — *into vs in, onto vs on, through vs across*.
- **Confusable pairs** — *beside/besides, since/for, between/among, in/into, on/upon, during/for*.

## Examiner's Traps (in words, not numbers)

- **Sound-Right Trap**: The wrong option "sounds natural" to a Hindi speaker — *"discuss about"*, *"comprise of"*, *"order for food"*, *"married with"*. All are wrong; the correct forms take **no preposition** or a different one.
- **Intervening Phrase Trap**: A long phrase between the verb and the blank distracts you from the fixed pairing — *"He insisted, despite our objections, ___ going."* (on).
- **Over-thinking Trap**: The simplest option (*at, in, on*) is correct, but a fancy option (*within, unto*) tempts you.
- **Similar-Word Trap**: *beside/besides, sensible to/sensitive to, different from/different than* — the exam pairs look-alikes.

## Top 10 High-Yield Questions (PYQ-style) with Solutions

**Q1.** He has been living in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) for (b) since (c) from (d) by
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Since* is used with a **point of time** (2015, Monday, January); *for* is used with a **period** (five years). "2015" is a point → *since*. Trap type: **Sound-Right** (Hindi "से" suggests *from*).
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q2.** The teacher was angry ______ the boy's behaviour.
(a) with (b) on (c) at (d) upon
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** *Angry with* a **person**, *angry at* a **thing/behaviour**. Here the object is "behaviour" → *at*. Trap type: **Similar-Word**.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3.** We discussed ______ the plan for two hours.
(a) about (b) on (c) over (d) no preposition needed
**Answer: (d)**
**Solution:** *Discuss* is a transitive verb — it never takes *about*. "We discussed the plan" is correct. Trap type: **Sound-Right** (Hindi "के बारे में चर्चा की").
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4.** The train arrived exactly ______ time.
(a) in (b) on (c) at (d) by
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *On time* = punctual, at the scheduled time. *In time* = before the deadline (with time to spare). Trap type: **Similar-Word** (in time vs on time).
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q5.** She is good ______ mathematics but weak ______ English.
(a) in / at (b) at / in (c) at / at (d) in / in
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Fixed pairings: *good at* a skill/subject; *weak in* a subject. Trap type: **Over-thinking** — students pick one preposition for both blanks.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6.** The committee comprises ______ ten members.
(a) of (b) from (c) with (d) no preposition needed
**Answer: (d)**
**Solution:** *Comprise* = "consist of" — it already contains the idea of "of". Either "comprises ten members" or "is composed of ten members". Trap type: **Sound-Right**.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7.** He jumped ______ the river to save the child.
(a) in (b) into (c) on (d) upon
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Into* shows **movement from outside to inside**; *in* shows position (already inside). Jumping is movement → *into*. Trap type: **Similar-Word**.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8.** I have known him, through all these years of struggle, ______ a long time.
(a) since (b) from (c) for (d) by
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** Ignore the intervening phrase "through all these years of struggle". "A long time" is a **period** → *for*. Trap type: **Intervening Phrase**.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9.** Besides ______ a fine singer, she is also a painter.
(a) being (b) been (c) be (d) to be
**Answer: (a)**
**Solution:** *Besides* (= in addition to) is a preposition, and a preposition is followed by a **gerund (V-ing)** → *being*. Don't confuse with *beside* (= next to). Trap type: **Similar-Word**.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10.** He insisted, despite our repeated requests to stay, ______ leaving at once.
(a) in (b) on (c) at (d) for
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Fixed pairing: *insist on* + gerund. The long intervening clause hides the verb "insisted" from the blank. Trap type: **Intervening Phrase**.
**Source:** PYQ-style

## Applying the 15/45-Second Rule

**15-second question (Fact-based): Q3** — "discussed ______ the plan". This is a pure rule recall: *discuss* takes no preposition. If you know the rule, mark and move in under 15 seconds. Do not re-read the sentence twice.

**45-second question (Inference-based): Q8** — The intervening phrase "through all these years of struggle" must first be mentally bracketed out; then you must classify "a long time" as a period (not a point) and choose *for*. This needs two steps of reasoning — budget up to 45 seconds, and if still unsure, mark for review rather than guess between *since* and *for*.

**Strategy summary:** Fixed-pairing questions (insist on, good at, depend on) are 15-second questions. Questions with intervening phrases, since/for decisions, or beside/besides-type pairs are 45-second questions.
