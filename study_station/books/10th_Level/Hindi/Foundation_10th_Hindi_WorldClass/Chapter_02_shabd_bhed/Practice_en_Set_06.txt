126. Identify the part of speech of the word "राम" in the sentence "राम खेलता है।"
(a) विशेषण (b) संज्ञा (c) क्रिया (d) सर्वनाम
Answer: (b)
Solution: The word "राम" names a specific person, making it a noun (संज्ञा). The tempting option सर्वनाम would indicate a pronoun, but "राम" is a proper noun, not a pronoun, so the answer is संज्ञा।
Source: NCERT Class 10 Hindi
