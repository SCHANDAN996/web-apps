# Word Classes (शब्द भेद) — Feynman Explanation

Think of a sentence as a **construction site**. Every word is a worker with a specific job. Some workers carry bricks (nouns), some mix cement (verbs), some paint the walls (adjectives), and some direct traffic (prepositions/conjunctions). **Word Classes (शब्द भेद)** is simply the roster that tells you which worker does what.

In Hindi grammar, there are **eight main classes** (आठ मुख्य शब्द भेद):

| Class (Hindi) | Class (English) | Job on the Site | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **संज्ञा** | Noun | Names a person, place, thing, or idea | "राम", "दिल्ली", "किताब", "खुशी" |
| **सर्वनाम** | Pronoun | Stands in for a noun | "वह", "यह", "मैं", "तुम" |
| **विशेषण** | Adjective | Describes a noun | "अच्छा", "लाल", "बड़ा", "तीन" |
| **क्रिया** | Verb | Shows action or state | "खाता", "जाता", "है", "था" |
| **क्रिया विशेषण** | Adverb | Describes how/when/where an action happens | "धीरे", "कल", "यहाँ", "बहुत" |
| **सम्बन्धबोधक** | Preposition / Postposition | Shows relation between words | "में", "से", "को", "पर", "के" |
| **समुच्चयबोधक** | Conjunction | Joins words or clauses | "और", "या", "लेकिन", "क्योंकि" |
| **विस्मयादिबोधक** | Interjection | Expresses sudden emotion | "अरे!", "वाह!", "हाय!" |

---

## Everyday Analogies

1. **संज्ञा (Noun) = The Label on a Box**  
   You write "Apples" on a box. The label *is not* the apples, but it tells you what's inside. "सेब" is the label for the fruit.

2. **सर्वनाम (Pronoun) = A Substitute Teacher**  
   When the regular teacher (noun) is absent, a substitute steps in. "राम आया। **वह** खुश है।" — "वह" substitutes "राम".

3. **विशेषण (Adjective) = A Sticky Note on the Box**  
   "Red apples" — "लाल सेब". The sticky note "लाल" adds detail to the noun "सेब".

4. **क्रिया (Verb) = The Engine of a Car**  
   Without an engine, the car (sentence) doesn't move. "पक्षी **उड़ता** है।" — "उड़ता" drives the sentence.

5. **क्रिया विशेषण (Adverb) = The Speedometer / GPS**  
   It tells *how* the engine runs: "पक्षी **तेजी से** उड़ता है।" — "तेजी से" modifies "उड़ता".

6. **सम्बन्धबोधक (Postposition) = A Connector Pipe**  
   Links two tanks: "घर **में** बिल्ली।" — "में" connects "बिल्ली" to "घर".

7. **समुच्चयबोधक (Conjunction) = A Coupling Hook Between Train Cars**  
   "राम **और** श्याम आए।" — "और" couples two nouns.

8. **विस्मयादिबोधक (Interjection) = A Car Horn**  
   Sudden, standalone: "**अरे!** तुम यहाँ हो।"

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Correct Thinking |
|---|---|---|
| Treating "अच्छा" in "वह अच्छा लड़का है" as a noun | It describes "लड़का" → adjective | Ask: *Does it name or describe?* |
| Confusing "में" (postposition) with "में" inside a word like "दिल्ली में" vs "अंदर" | "में" relates two words; "अंदर" is an adverb of place | Check: *Is it linking a noun to something else?* |
| Calling "और" a verb because it "joins" | Joining is not an action/state | Verbs answer "what happens?" |
| Translating Hindi postpositions as English prepositions 1:1 | Hindi uses postpositions (after noun); English uses prepositions (before noun) | "घर **में**" = "in **the house**" — order flips |

## 5 Recall Questions

**Q1.** In the sentence "छोटा बच्चा रो रहा है", which word is the **विशेषण (adjective)**?  
**Options:** (a) बच्चा (b) छोटा (c) रो (d) है  
**Answer:** (b)  
**Solution:** "छोटा" describes the noun "बच्चा" (what kind of child? — small). "बच्चा" is संज्ञा, "रो" is क्रिया, "है" is क्रिया (auxiliary).

**Q2.** Identify the **सर्वनाम (pronoun)** in "मैंने उसे किताब दी।"  
**Options:** (a) मैंने (b) उसे (c) किताब (d) दी  
**Answer:** (b)  
**Solution:** "उसे" stands for a person (him/her) — a pronoun. "मैंने" is also a pronoun (मैं + ने), but "उसे" is the clearer standalone pronoun option. "किताब" is संज्ञा, "दी" is क्रिया.

**Q3.** Which word class does "बहुत" belong to in "वह बहुत तेज दौड़ता है"?  
**Options:** (a) विशेषण (b) क्रिया (c) क्रिया विशेषण (d) सम्बन्धबोधक  
**Answer:** (c)  
**Solution:** "बहुत" modifies the adverb "तेज" (itself a क्रिया विशेषण), answering "to what degree?" — hence क्रिया विशेषण (adverb of degree).

**Q4.** In "राम और श्याम खेल रहे हैं", what is the function of "और"?  
**Options:** (a) क्रिया (b) समुच्चयबोधक (c) विशेषण (d) सर्वनाम  
**Answer:** (b)  
**Solution:** "और" joins two nouns ("राम" and "श्याम") — classic समुच्चयबोधक (conjunction).

**Q5.** Pick the **विस्मयादिबोधक (interjection)** from: "अरे! दरवाजा खोलो।"  
**Options:** (a) दरवाजा (b) खोलो (c) अरे (d) है  
**Answer:** (c)  
**Solution:** "अरे" expresses sudden emotion (surprise/urgency) and stands alone with an exclamation mark — hallmark of विस्मयादिबोधक.

## Quick Self-Check

Next time you read a Hindi sentence, **tag each word mentally**:  
"यह **लाल** गाड़ी **तेजी से** **चलती** **है**।"  
→ "लाल" = विशेषण | "तेजी से" = क्रिया विशेषण | "चलती" = क्रिया | "है" = क्रिया

If you can label every worker on the site, you've mastered शब्द भेद.
