Card 1
Front: What is the term for "parts of speech" in Hindi grammar?
Back: शब्द भेद (Shabd Bhed) — the classification of words based on their function in a sentence.

Card 2
Front: How many main शब्द भेद are there in standard Hindi grammar?
Back: Eight main classes: संज्ञा (Noun), सर्वनाम (Pronoun), विशेषण (Adjective), क्रिया (Verb), क्रिया-विशेषण (Adverb), संबंधबोधक (Preposition), समुच्चयबोधक (Conjunction), विस्मयादिबोधक (Interjection).

Card 3
Front: Identify the संज्ञा (noun) in the sentence: "राम पढ़ता है।"
Back: "राम" — a proper noun (व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा) naming a specific person.

Card 4
Front: Which word is the सर्वनाम (pronoun) in: "वह स्कूल जाता है।"
Back: "वह" — a pronoun (सर्वनाम) replacing the noun "राम" or any specific person.

Card 5
Front: Pick the विशेषण (adjective) from: "लाल फूल खिला है।"
Back: "लाल" — describes the noun "फूल" (quality/colour), hence a गुणवाचक विशेषण.

Card 6
Front: Find the क्रिया (verb) in: "सीता गाती है।"
Back: "गाती" — the main verb (क्रिया) showing action; "है" is the auxiliary verb (सहायक क्रिया).

Card 7
Front: Which word functions as क्रिया-विशेषण (adverb) in: "वह तेज दौड़ता है।"
Back: "तेज" — modifies the verb "दौड़ता" (manner), so it is a रीतिवाचक क्रिया-विशेषण.

Card 8
Front: Identify the संबंधबोधक (preposition) in: "किताब मेज पर है।"
Back: "पर" — shows relation between "किताब" and "मेज"; a संबंधबोधक (postposition in Hindi).

Card 9
Front: What is the समुच्चयबोधक (conjunction) in: "राम और श्याम आए।"
Back: "और" — joins two nouns "राम" and "श्याम"; a संयोजक समुच्चयबोधक.

Card 10
Front: Spot the विस्मयादिबोधक (interjection) in: "अरे! तुम यहाँ हो।"
Back: "अरे" — expresses sudden emotion/surprise; a विस्मयादिबोधक.

Card 11
Front: Classify "पानी" in: "पानी पीओ।"
Back: "पानी" — a द्रव्यवाचक संज्ञा (material noun), uncountable substance.

Card 12
Front: What type of संज्ञा is "भीड़" in: "भीड़ बढ़ रही है।"
Back: "भीड़" — a समूहवाचक संज्ञा (collective noun) denoting a group.

Card 13
Front: Identify the pronoun type for "अपना" in: "यह अपना काम है।"
Back: "अपना" — a reflexive/possessive pronoun (निजवाचक सर्वनाम) showing ownership by the subject.

Card 14
Front: Which विशेषण type is "पाँच" in: "पाँच लड़के खड़े हैं।"
Back: "पाँच" — a संख्यावाचक विशेषण (numeral adjective) indicating definite number.

Card 15
Front: Classify "कल" in: "कल मैं जाऊँगा।"
Back: "कल" — a कालवाचक क्रिया-विशेषण (adverb of time) telling when the action occurs.

Card 16
Front: What is the function of "किंतु" in: "वह आया किंतु रुका नहीं।"
Back: "किंतु" — an adversative conjunction (विरोधसूचक समुच्चयबोधक) showing contrast.

Card 17
Front: Identify the error: "वह अच्छा गाता है।" (intended: "He sings well.")
Back: "अच्छा" is an adjective; the adverb form is "अच्छी तरह" or "अच्छे से". Correct: "वह अच्छी तरह गाता है।"

Card 18
Front: Choose the correct word class for "बहुत" in: "यह बहुत सुंदर है।"
Back: "बहुत" — a परिमाणवाचक क्रिया-विशेषण (adverb of degree) modifying the adjective "सुंदर".
