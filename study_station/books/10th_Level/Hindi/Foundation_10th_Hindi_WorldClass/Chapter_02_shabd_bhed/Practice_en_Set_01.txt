1. Identify the part of speech of the word "राम" in the sentence "राम खेलता है।"
(a) सर्वनाम (b) क्रिया (c) विशेषण (d) संज्ञा
Answer: (d)
Solution: The word "राम" is a proper noun (व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा) naming a specific person; the tempting option "सर्वनाम" would replace a noun, but here the name itself is used, so the answer is संज्ञा.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Hindi Grammar

2. Which of the following words is a "सर्वनाम" (Pronoun)?
(a) सुन्दर (b) पुस्तक (c) वह (d) खाना
Answer: (c)
Solution: "वह" replaces a noun (e.g., "लड़का" → "वह"), making it a सर्वनाम; "पुस्तक" is a संज्ञा, "सुन्दर" is विशेषण, "खाना" is क्रिया/संज्ञा, so the answer is वह.
Source: PYQ-style

3. In the sentence "सीता सुन्दर लड़की है।", the word "सुन्दर" functions as:
(a) संज्ञा (b) सर्वनाम (c) विशेषण (d) क्रिया
Answer: (c)
Solution: "सुन्दर" describes the quality of the noun "लड़की", hence it is a विशेषण (Adjective); "लड़की" is the naming word, so the answer is विशेषण.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Hindi Grammar

4. Choose the "क्रिया" (Verb) in the sentence "बच्चे स्कूल जाते हैं।"
(a) जाते (b) बच्चे (c) हैं (d) स्कूल
Answer: (a)
Solution: "जाते" expresses the action performed by the subject "बच्चे"; "हैं" is an auxiliary verb but the main lexical verb is "जाते", making the answer जाते.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Which word in "वह तेज़ दौड़ता है।" is a "क्रिया विशेषण" (Adverb)?
(a) है (b) वह (c) दौड़ता (d) तेज़
Answer: (d)
Solution: "तेज़" modifies the verb "दौड़ता" by telling how the action is done, hence it is a क्रिया विशेषण; "दौड़ता" is the main verb, so the answer is तेज़.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Hindi Grammar

6. Identify the "संबंधबोधक" (Postposition) in "किताब मेज़ पर है।"
(a) पर (b) मेज़ (c) किताब (d) है
Answer: (a)
Solution: "पर" shows the relationship between "किताब" and "मेज़"; it comes after the noun, functioning as a संबंधबोधक; "मेज़" is a noun, so the answer is पर.
Source: PYQ-style

7. "और" in "राम और श्याम आये।" belongs to which word class?
(a) समुच्चयबोधक (b) विस्मयादिबोधक (c) संबंधबोधक (d) सर्वनाम
Answer: (a)
Solution: "और" joins two nouns "राम" and "श्याम", therefore it is a समुच्चयबोधक (Conjunction); "विस्मयादिबोधक" expresses sudden emotion, so the answer is समुच्चयबोधक.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Hindi Grammar

8. Which of the following is a "विस्मयादिबोधक" (Interjection)?
(a) लेकिन (b) अरे! (c) यदि (d) तो
Answer: (b)
Solution: "अरे!" expresses sudden emotion or surprise, making it a विस्मयादिबोधक; the other options are conjunctions, so the answer is अरे!.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Which of the following is a "निजवाचक सर्वनाम" (Reflexive Pronoun)?
(a) कौन (b) यह (c) स्वयं (d) कोई
Answer: (c)
Solution: "स्वयं" reflects back to the subject for emphasis, making it a निजवाचक सर्वनाम; "यह" is निश्चयवाचक, "कौन" is प्रश्नवाचक, and "कोई" is अनिश्चयवाचक, so the answer is स्वयं.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Hindi Grammar

10. "कौन" in "कौन आया?" is which type of pronoun?
(a) निश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम (b) संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम (c) प्रश्नवाचक सर्वनाम (d) अनिश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम
Answer: (c)
Solution: "कौन" asks a question, hence it is प्रश्नवाचक सर्वनाम; "संबंधवाचक" connects clauses (e.g., जो), so the answer is प्रश्नवाचक सर्वनाम.
Source: PYQ-style

11. In "वह अपनी पुस्तक लाया।", "अपनी" is a:
(a) विशेषण (b) सर्वनाम (c) संज्ञा (d) क्रिया
Answer: (b)
Solution: "अपनी" stands for the possessor (reflexive pronoun) and functions as a सर्वनाम; it may look like an adjective but grammatically it is a pronoun, so the answer is सर्वनाम.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Hindi Grammar

12. Which word is a "सार्वनामिक विशेषण" (Pronominal Adjective) in "यह लड़का चतुर है।"?
(a) यह (b) लड़का (c) चतुर (d) है
Answer: (a)
Solution: "यह" qualifies the noun "लड़का" while retaining pronoun nature, making it सार्वनामिक विशेषण; "चतुर" is a pure विशेषण, so the answer is यह.
Source: PYQ-style

13. Identify the "क्रिया" in "माँ खाना बना रही है।"
(a) माँ (b) बना (c) खाना (d) रही
Answer: (b)
Solution: "बना" is the main verb root showing action; "रही" is auxiliary, "खाना" is object noun, so the answer is बना.

14. "धीरे-धीरे" in "वह धीरे-धीरे चलता है।" is a:
(a) संज्ञा (b) विशेषण (c) क्रिया विशेषण (d) सर्वनाम
Answer: (c)
Solution: The reduplicated form "धीरे-धीरे" modifies the verb "चलता" indicating manner, hence क्रिया विशेषण; a विशेषण would modify a noun, so the answer is क्रिया विशेषण.

15. Choose the correct "समुच्चयबोधक" for the blank: "वह गरीब है ___ ईमानदार।"
(a) लेकिन (b) क्योंकि (c) यदि (d) जब
Answer: (a)
Solution: "लेकिन" shows contrast between two clauses; "क्योंकि" gives reason, "यदि" condition, "जब" time, so the answer is लेकिन.

16. "हाय!" in "हाय! मैं मर गया।" expresses:
(a) Wonder (b) Joy (c) Anger (d) Sorrow
Answer: (d)
Solution: "हाय!" is a विस्मयादिबोधक expressing grief or pain; "वाह!" would express joy, so the answer is Sorrow.

17. Which of the following is NOT a "संज्ञा"?
(a) मैदान (b) दौड़ (c) धावक (d) दौड़ना
Answer: (d)
Solution: "दौड़ना" is an infinitive verb form (क्रिया), while "दौड़" (race), "धावक" (runner), "मैदान" (field) are nouns, so the answer is दौड़ना.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Hindi Grammar

18. In "अच्छे लड़के पढ़ते हैं।", "अच्छे" is a:
(a) विशेषण (b) सर्वनाम (c) संज्ञा (d) क्रिया विशेषण
Answer: (a)
Solution: "अच्छे" qualifies the noun "लड़के" agreeing in gender/number, making it विशेषण; a क्रिया विशेषण would modify a verb, so the answer is विशेषण.
Source: PYQ-style

19. "अंदर" in "बच्चे अंदर खेल रहे हैं।" functions as:
(a) क्रिया विशेषण (b) संज्ञा (c) संबंधबोधक (d) सर्वनाम
Answer: (a)
Solution: "अंदर" modifies the verb "खेल" telling where, hence क्रिया विशेषण (स्थानवाचक); it is not followed by a noun, so not संबंधबोधक, so the answer is क्रिया विशेषण.

20. Identify the "सर्वनाम" in "तुम्हारी पुस्तक कहाँ है?"
(a) पुस्तक (b) तुम्हारी (c) कहाँ (d) है
Answer: (b)
Solution: "तुम्हारी" is a possessive pronoun (पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम का रूप); "कहाँ" is प्रश्नवाचक क्रिया विशेषण, "पुस्तक" संज्ञा, so the answer is तुम्हारी.
Source: PYQ-style

21. Which sentence contains a "संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम"?
(a) कोई नहीं आया। (b) जो आया वह गया। (c) यह पुस्तक मेरी है। (d) कौन आया?
Answer: (b)
Solution: "जो" relates to "वह" as antecedent, making it संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम; "कौन" is प्रश्नवाचक, "कोई" is अनिश्चयवाचक, "यह" is निश्चयवाचक, so the answer is जो आया वह गया।
Source: NCERT Class 10 Hindi Grammar

22. In "वह स्वयं आया।", "स्वयं" is a:
(a) निश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम (b) विशेषण (c) निजवाचक सर्वनाम (d) क्रिया विशेषण
Answer: (c)
Solution: "स्वयं" emphasizes the subject "वह" reflexively, classifying it as निजवाचक सर्वनाम; "निश्चयवाचक" includes यह, वह, so the answer is निजवाचक सर्वनाम.

23. "बहुत" in "यह बहुत अच्छा है।" modifies which part of speech?
(a) Noun (b) Verb (c) Preposition (d) Adjective
Answer: (d)
Solution: "बहुत" intensifies the adjective "अच्छा", hence it is a क्रिया विशेषण functioning as degree modifier; the trap is to think it modifies verb, but here it qualifies adjective, so the answer is Adjective.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Hindi Grammar

24. Identify the part of speech of the word "खुशी" in the sentence "बच्चे खुशी से खेलते हैं।"
(a) क्रिया विशेषण (b) विशेषण (c) क्रिया (d) संज्ञा
Answer: (d)
Solution: "खुशी" is an abstract noun (भाववाचक संज्ञा); the postposition "से" makes the phrase function adverbially, but the word itself remains a noun, so the answer is संज्ञा.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Hindi Grammar

25. In "यदि वह आये तो मुझे बताना।", "यदि" and "तो" are:
(a) सर्वनाम (b) समुच्चयबोधक (c) संज्ञा (d) संबंधबोधक
Answer: (b)
Solution: "यदि...तो" is a correlative conjunction pair showing condition-result, hence समुच्चयबोधक; संबंधबोधक comes after a noun, so the answer is समुच्चयबोधक.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Hindi Grammar
