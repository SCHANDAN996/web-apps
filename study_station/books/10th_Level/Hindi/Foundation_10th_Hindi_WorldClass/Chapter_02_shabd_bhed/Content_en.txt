Word Classes (शब्द भेद) — Hindi Grammar for Competitive Exams

Introduction
In Hindi, every word belongs to a class based on its function in a sentence. These classes are called शब्द भेद (śabda bheda). Understanding them is essential for spotting errors, filling blanks, and answering comprehension questions in exams like SSC GD, MTS, RRB Group D, and state police constable tests. This lesson covers the eight traditional word classes, gives clear definitions, six worked examples, and highlights common mistakes aspirants make.

1. संज्ञा (Noun) — नाम वाले शब्द
A संज्ञा names a person, place, thing, quality, or action.
Examples: "राम", "दिल्ली", "किताब", "ईमानदारी", "दौड़ना" (as verbal noun).
Test tip: If you can put "यह" or "वह" before a word and it makes sense, it is likely a संज्ञा.

2. सर्वनाम (Pronoun) — संज्ञा के स्थान पर प्रयुक्त शब्द
A सर्वनाम replaces a संज्ञा to avoid repetition.
Examples: "वह", "यह", "मैं", "तुम", "अपना", "कौन".
Test tip: Check whether the word stands in place of a noun. "वह जाता है" — here "वह" replaces "राम".

3. विशेषण (Adjective) — संज्ञा या सर्वनाम की विशेषता बताने वाला शब्द
A विशेषण describes quality, quantity, number, or identity of a noun/pronoun.
Examples: "अच्छा", "बड़ा", "दो", "मेरा", "यह".
Test tip: A विशेषण always qualifies a noun or pronoun. In "अच्छा लड़का", "अच्छा" qualifies "लड़का".

4. क्रिया (Verb) — कार्य या अवस्था बताने वाला शब्द
A क्रिया expresses an action, occurrence, or state of being.
Examples: "जाता है", "खाता है", "है", "था", "चलेगा".
Test tip: Ask "क्या किया?" or "क्या होगा?" — the answer is the क्रिया.

5. क्रिया विशेषण (Adverb) — क्रिया, विशेषण या अन्य क्रिया विशेषण की विशेषता बताने वाला शब्द
A क्रिया विशेषण modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb. It answers how, when, where, how much.
Examples: "धीरे", "कल", "यहाँ", "बहुत", "कभी नहीं".
Test tip: In "वह धीरे चलता है", "धीरे" modifies "चलता है".

6. संबंधबोधक (Preposition/Postposition) — संज्ञा/सर्वनाम को अन्य शब्दों से जोड़ने वाला शब्द
Hindi uses postpositions (after the noun) rather than prepositions.
Examples: "में", "से", "को", "पर", "के लिए", "के साथ".
Test tip: They always follow a noun/pronoun. "घर में", "राम से", "मुझे" (मुझ + को).

7. समुच्चयबोधक (Conjunction) — शब्दों या वाक्यों को जोड़ने वाला शब्द
Examples: "और", "या", "लेकिन", "क्योंकि", "अगर...तो".
Test tip: They connect similar elements. "राम और श्याम", "वह आया लेकिन गया नहीं".

8. विस्मयादिबोधक (Interjection) — भावना व्यक्त करने वाला शब्द
Examples: "अरे!", "वाह!", "हाय!", "आह!".
Test tip: They stand alone or at the start of a sentence, followed by an exclamation mark.

Worked Examples

Example 1 — Identify the word class
Sentence: "राम अच्छा लड़का है।"
Question: "अच्छा" किस शब्द भेद का है?
Analysis: "अच्छा" describes the noun "लड़का". It tells quality. Therefore it is a विशेषण.
Answer: विशेषण

Example 2 — Identify the word class
Sentence: "वह कल दिल्ली जायेगा।"
Question: "कल" किस शब्द भेद का है?
Analysis: "कल" tells when the action "जायेगा" happens. It modifies the verb. Therefore it is a क्रिया विशेषण.
Answer: क्रिया विशेषण

Example 3 — Identify the word class
Sentence: "किताब मेज पर रखी है।"
Question: "पर" किस शब्द भेद का है?
Analysis: "पर" follows the noun "मेज" and shows location. It is a संबंधबोधक.
Answer: संबंधबोधक

Example 4 — Identify the word class
Sentence: "मैं और तुम चलेंगे।"
Question: "और" किस शब्द भेद का है?
Analysis: "और" joins two pronouns "मैं" and "तुम". It is a समुच्चयबोधक.
Answer: समुच्चयबोधक

Example 5 — Identify the word class
Sentence: "अरे! यह क्या हो गया?"
Question: "अरे" किस शब्द भेद का है?
Analysis: "अरे" expresses sudden emotion and stands with an exclamation mark. It is a विस्मयादिबोधक.
Answer: विस्मयादिबोधक

Example 6 — Identify the word class
Sentence: "उसने अपनी किताब दी।"
Question: "अपनी" किस शब्द भेद का है?
Analysis: "अपनी" qualifies the noun "किताब" and shows possession. It is a विशेषण (possessive adjective). Note: Some grammars call it सर्वनाम विशेषण, but for exam purposes treat it as विशेषण because it modifies a noun.
Answer: विशेषण

Common Errors and How to Avoid Them

Error 1: Confusing सर्वनाम and विशेषण
Words like "मेरा", "तेरा", "अपना", "यह", "वह" can function as both.
Rule: If the word stands alone in place of a noun → सर्वनाम. If it comes before a noun and qualifies it → विशेषण.
Example: "यह किताब मेरी है।" — "यह" qualifies "किताब" → विशेषण. "यह मेरी है।" — "यह" stands for "किताब" → सर्वनाम.

Error 2: Treating postpositions as prepositions
English uses prepositions (before noun): "on the table". Hindi uses postpositions (after noun): "मेज पर". Do not call "पर" a preposition; call it संबंधबोधक.

Error 3: Misidentifying क्रिया विशेषण as विशेषण
"वह तेज दौड़ता है।" — "तेज" modifies "दौड़ता है" (verb) → क्रिया विशेषण.
"वह तेज धावक है।" — "तेज" modifies "धावक" (noun) → विशेषण.
Always check which word is being modified.

Error 4: Overlooking compound verbs as single क्रिया
"चला गया", "ले आया", "देख लिया" — each pair functions as one क्रिया. Do not split them into two separate verbs for word-class identification.

Error 5: Ignoring the role of "ने", "को", "से" as संबंधबोधक
In "राम ने खाना खाया", "ने" marks the agent. In "मुझे दो", "को" (in "मुझको") marks the object. These are संबंधबोधक, not separate word classes like "case markers".

Error 6: Confusing conjunctions with relative pronouns/correlatives
"जो आया वह गया।" — "जो" is a सर्वनाम (relative pronoun), not a समुच्चयबोधक.
"वह आया और गया।" — "और" is a समुच्चयबोधक.
Relative-correlative pairs (जो...वह, जैसा...वैसा) are pronoun pairs, not conjunctions.

Examination Strategy
- Read the sentence, isolate the target word, ask: "What does it modify or replace?"
- For "Identify the part of speech" questions, first decide noun vs. pronoun vs. adjective vs. verb vs. adverb vs. postposition vs. conjunction vs. interjection.
- Practice with previous paper sentences (NCERT Class 9–10 Hindi grammar chapters are the standard source).
- Remember: Hindi word order is SOV; postpositions follow nouns; adjectives usually precede nouns but can follow for emphasis.
