101. Which of the following words is a "संज्ञा" (noun)?
(a) सुंदर (b) चलना (c) राम (d) जल्दी
Answer: (c)
Solution: The word "चलना" is a verb, "सुंदर" is an adjective, and "जल्दी" is an adverb; only "राम" denotes a person and functions as a noun, so the answer is 'राम'.
