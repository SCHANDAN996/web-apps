26. Identify the word class of the Hindi word "पुस्तक".  
(a) Pronoun (b) Noun (c) Adjective (d) Verb
Answer: (b)  
Solution: "पुस्तक" denotes a concrete object and functions as a noun; a common trap is to confuse it with a pronoun because it can be replaced by "यह", but it remains a noun, so the answer is (b).  
Source: NCERT Class 9 Hindi Grammar
