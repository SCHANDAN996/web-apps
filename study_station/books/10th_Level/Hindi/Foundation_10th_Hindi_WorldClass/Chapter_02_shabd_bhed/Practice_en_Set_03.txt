51. Which of the following words is a "संज्ञा" (Noun)?
(a) "सुन्दर" (b) "और" (c) "चलता" (d) "राम"
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option "सुन्दर" is an adjective (विशेषण) describing quality, "चलता" is a verb form (क्रिया), and "और" is a conjunction (समुच्चयबोधक). "राम" is a proper noun (व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा) naming a specific person, so the answer is "राम".
Source: NCERT Class 6 Hindi Grammar
