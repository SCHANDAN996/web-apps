**Key Facts — Adjectives and Verbs (विशेषण और क्रिया)**  
*Level: 10th Foundation (SSC GD, MTS, RRB Group D, State Police)*  

1. **Definition of विशेषण (Adjective)** — A word that describes or modifies a noun or pronoun. In Hindi it answers “कैसा?”, “कितना?”, “कौन-सा?”. Example: "लाल" in "लाल फूल".  

2. **Definition of क्रिया (Verb)** — A word that expresses an action, occurrence, or state of being. In Hindi it answers “क्या किया?” or “क्या हो रहा है?”. Example: "खाता" in "वह खाता है".  

3. **Types of विशेषण** — मुख्यतः चार प्रकार: गुणवाचक (qualitative), संख्यावाचक (quantitative), सार्वनामिक (pronominal), और परिमाणवाचक (measure).  

4. **गुणवाचक विशेषण (Qualitative Adjective)** — Describes quality: "अच्छा", "बुरा", "सुंदर", "काला". It agrees with the noun in gender, number, and case.  

5. **संख्यावाचक विशेषण (Quantitative Adjective)** — Indicates number or quantity: "एक", "दो", "बहुत", "थोड़ा", "सभी".  

6. **सार्वनामिक विशेषण (Pronominal Adjective)** — Derived from pronouns: "यह", "वह", "मेरा", "तेरा", "अपना", "कौन", "जो". Functions as adjective when placed before a noun.  

7. **परिमाणवाचक विशेषण (Measure Adjective)** — Shows measure/weight: "पाँच किलो", "दो मीटर", "तीन लीटर".  

8. **Agreement of विशेषण with Noun** — In Hindi, qualitative adjectives ending in "आ" (e.g., "अच्छा") change for gender/number: "अच्छा लड़का", "अच्छी लड़की", "अच्छे लड़के", "अच्छी लड़कियाँ". Adjectives not ending in "आ" (e.g., "बड़ा", "छोटा" — actually they do end in आ; better example: "नीला", "पीला") follow same rule. Invariant adjectives like "सुंदर", "बहुत", "कुछ" never change.  

9. **Position of विशेषण** — Usually precedes the noun: "लाल किताब". For emphasis or poetic order it may follow: "किताब लाल है" (here "लाल" is predicate, not attributive).  

10. **Degrees of Comparison** — Hindi uses periphrastic forms: comparative with "से" + "अधिक/ज्यादा" ("राम श्याम से अधिक लंबा है"), superlative with "सबसे" ("राम सबसे लंबा है"). No inflectional -er/-est.  

11. **Definition of क्रिया (Verb) — Root Form (मूल रूप)** — Dictionary form ends in "ना": "खाना", "पीना", "जाना", "करना".  

12. **Finite vs Non-finite Verbs** — Finite (सकर्मक/अकर्मक) show tense, person, number: "खाता है", "खाएँगे". Non-finite (इन्फिनिटिव, पार्टिसिपल, जेरंड) do not: "खाना", "खाकर", "खाते हुए".  

13. **Transitive (सकर्मक) vs Intransitive (अकर्मक) Verbs** — Transitive takes direct object (कर्म): "राम ने आम खाया". Intransitive has no object: "राम सोया". Many verbs can be both: "चलना" (intrans.) vs "चलाना" (trans.).  

14. **Causative Verbs (प्रेरणार्थक क्रिया)** — Formed by adding "आ" or "वा" to root: "खिलाना" (feed) from "खाना", "बुलाना" (call) from "बोलना". Indicates causing someone to do the action.  

15. **Compound Verbs (संयुक्त क्रिया)** — Main verb + auxiliary verb: "जाना" (go) + "देना" = "जाना देना" (let go), "ले जाना" (take away), "देना" (give), "लेना" (take), "रखना" (keep), "पड़ना" (happen to). Adds aspectual nuance.  

16. **Tense System — Three Main Tenses** — वर्तमान काल (Present), भूत काल (Past), भविष्यत् काल (Future). Each has simple, continuous, perfect, perfect-continuous forms.  

17. **Present Tense Forms** — Simple: "खाता है" (habitual); Continuous: "खा रहा है"; Perfect: "खा चुका है"; Perfect Continuous: "खाता रहा है".  

18. **Past Tense Forms** — Simple: "खाया"; Continuous: "खा रहा था"; Perfect: "खा चुका था"; Perfect Continuous: "खाता रहा था".  

19. **Future Tense Forms** — Simple: "खाएगा"; Continuous: "खा रहा होगा"; Perfect: "खा चुका होगा"; Perfect Continuous: "खाता रहा होगा".  

20. **Imperative Mood (आज्ञार्थक)** — Three levels: intimate "खा", familiar "खाओ", polite "खाइए / खाएँ". Negative: "मत खाओ", "न खाएँ".  

21. **Subjunctive / Presumptive (संभावनार्थक)** — Formed with "हो" + future suffix: "खाता हो", "खा रहा हो", "खा चुका हो". Used for doubt, wish, polite request.  

22. **Passive Voice (कर्मणि प्रयोग)** — Object becomes subject; verb takes "जाना" auxiliary: "आम खाया गया" (by राम). Agent marked by "द्वारा" or "से".  

23. **Negation** — "नहीं" before verb: "नहीं खाता"; "न" in imperative: "न खाओ"; "मत" for prohibition: "मत जाओ".  

24. **Common Error — Adjective Agreement** — Wrong: "अच्छा लड़की"; Correct: "अच्छी लड़की". Invariant adjectives like "सुंदर" never change: "सुंदर लड़का / सुंदर लड़की".  

25. **Common Error — Verb Concord with Subject** — Verb agrees with subject in gender/number in past tense (perfective aspect): "लड़का गया", "लड़की गई", "लड़के गए", "लड़कियाँ गईं". In present/future, verb agrees with subject only in number/person, not gender: "लड़का जाता है", "लड़की जाती है".
