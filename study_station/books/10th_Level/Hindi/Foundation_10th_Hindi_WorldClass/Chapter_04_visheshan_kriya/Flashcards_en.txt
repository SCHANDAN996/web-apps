Card 1
Front: What is a विशेषण (adjective) in Hindi grammar?
Back: A विशेषण is a word that describes or qualifies a संज्ञा (noun) or सर्वनाम (pronoun). It tells "कैसा?" (what kind?), "कितना?" (how much?), or "कौन सा?" (which one?). Example: "लाल" in "लाल फूल" (red flower).

Card 2
Front: Identify the विशेषण in the sentence: "वह एक बुद्धिमान लड़का है।"
Back: "बुद्धिमान" — it describes the noun "लड़का" (boy) by telling his quality.

Card 3
Front: What are the three main types of विशेषण based on meaning?
Back: 1. गुणवाचक विशेषण (Qualitative) — e.g., "सुंदर", "बड़ा" 2. परिमाणवाचक विशेषण (Quantitative) — e.g., "थोड़ा", "बहुत" 3. सार्वनामिक विशेषण (Demonstrative/Possessive) — e.g., "यह", "वह", "मेरा", "तेरा"

Card 4
Front: Give an example of गुणवाचक विशेषण showing gender agreement.
Back: Masculine: "अच्छा लड़का" | Feminine: "अच्छी लड़की" — the adjective "अच्छा/अच्छी" changes form to match the noun's gender.

Card 5
Front: What is a क्रिया (verb) in Hindi?
Back: A क्रिया is a word that expresses an action, occurrence, or state of being. It tells what the subject does or is. Example: "खाता" in "राम रोटी खाता है।" (Ram eats bread.)

Card 6
Front: Identify the क्रिया in: "बच्चे खेल रहे हैं।"
Back: "खेल रहे हैं" — the complete verb phrase showing ongoing action (present continuous).

Card 7
Front: What are the two main classifications of क्रिया based on object requirement?
Back: 1. सकर्मक क्रिया (Transitive verb) — takes an object, e.g., "पढ़ना" in "वह किताब पढ़ता है।" 2. अकर्मक क्रिया (Intransitive verb) — no object needed, e.g., "सोना" in "वह सो रहा है।"

Card 8
Front: How does a सकर्मक क्रिया affect the subject's case in perfective tenses?
Back: In perfective tenses (e.g., past perfect), the subject of a transitive verb takes the ने (ne) marker. Example: "राम**ने** खाना खाया।" (Ram ate food.) — "राम" gets ने because "खाना" is transitive.

Card 9
Front: What is the root form (मूल रूप) of the verb "खिलाया"?
Back: "खिलाना" (causative of "खाना") — the root is obtained by removing the tense suffix. For causative verbs, the root ends in ाना.

Card 10
Front: Distinguish between मुख्य क्रिया (main verb) and सहायक क्रिया (auxiliary verb) in: "वह पढ़ रहा है।"
Back: Main verb: "पढ़" (reading action) | Auxiliary verbs: "रहा है" (showing continuous aspect + present tense). Together they form present continuous.

Card 11
Front: What is the difference between "बोलना" and "बुलाना"?
Back: "बोलना" = to speak (intransitive usually) | "बुलाना" = to call/summon (transitive, causative of बोलना). Example: "वह बोलता है।" vs "उसने मुझे बुलाया।"

Card 12
Front: Identify the विशेषण type in: "कई छात्र अनुपस्थित थे।"
Back: "कई" is परिमाणवाचक विशेषण (quantitative adjective) indicating indefinite number. "अनुपस्थित" is गुणवाचक विशेषण (qualitative) describing the students.

Card 13
Front: In "मेरी किताब नई है।", which words are विशेषण and why?
Back: "मेरी" — सार्वनामिक विशेषण (possessive adjective) showing ownership. "नई" — गुणवाचक विशेषण (qualitative) describing the book's condition. Both qualify "किताब".

Card 14
Front: What is the imperative form (आज्ञार्थ) of "जाना" for तुम (informal you)?
Back: "जाओ" — the तुम-level imperative. For आप (formal): "जाइए"; for तू (intimate): "जा"।

Card 15
Front: Spot the error: "वह अच्छा गाना गाती है।"
Back: No error. "अच्छा" agrees with masculine "गाना" (song), not with the feminine subject "वह". The adjective qualifies the object in this transitive construction.

Card 16
Front: What is the difference between "पकाना" and "पकना"?
Back: "पकाना" (transitive/causative) = to cook something. "पकना" (intransitive) = to get cooked/ripen. Example: "माँ खाना पकाती है।" vs "खाना पक गया।"

Card 17
Front: Which विशेषण does NOT change for gender/number?
Back: अव्यय विशेषण (indeclinable adjectives) like "कुछ", "बहुत", "थोड़ा" (when used as quantity), "काफी" — they remain unchanged. Example: "कुछ लड़के", "कुछ लड़कियाँ"।

Card 18
Front: Complete the sentence with correct verb form: "यदि वह मेहनत करेगा, तो वह ________।" (सफल होना)
Back: "सफल होगा" — future tense in both clauses for a likely future condition. The verb "होना" takes future form "होगा" agreeing with masculine singular subject "वह"।
