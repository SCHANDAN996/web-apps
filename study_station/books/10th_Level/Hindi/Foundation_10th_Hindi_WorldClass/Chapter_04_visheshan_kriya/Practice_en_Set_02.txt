26. Identify the adjective in the sentence: "वह एक सुन्दर लड़का है।"
(a) एक (b) सुन्दर (c) वह (d) लड़का
Answer: (b)
Solution: The word "सुन्दर" describes the quality of the noun "लड़का", making it a qualitative adjective. "वह" is a pronoun, "एक" is a numeral adjective but not the qualitative one tested here, and "लड़का" is the noun. The trap is choosing "एक" thinking it is the only adjective, but "सुन्दर" is the qualitative adjective explicitly asked for, so the answer is सुन्दर.
