126. Identify the adjective in the sentence: "वह सुंदर लड़की खेल रही है।"  
(a) सुंदर (b) लड़की (c) खेल (d) है
Answer: (a)  
Solution: The word "सुंदर" describes the noun "लड़की", making it an adjective; the tempting wrong option "लड़की" is a noun, "खेल" is a verb root, and "है" is an auxiliary, so the answer is सुंदर.  
Source: NCERT Class 9 Hindi
