**PYQ Pattern Analysis — Adjectives and Verbs (विशेषण और क्रिया)**  

**1. Question Types That Recur**  
- **Spot the Adjective / Verb**: A sentence is given; you must pick the word that functions as an adjective (विशेषण) or a verb (क्रिया).  
  *Example (PYQ-style)*:  
  In the sentence **"राम अच्छा लड़का है"**, the adjective is **"अच्छा"**.  
  In **"सीता गाती है"**, the verb is **"गाती"**.  

- **Fill in the Blank with Correct Form**: A blank requires the right adjective form (gender/number agreement) or verb tense/voice.  
  *Example (PYQ-style)*:  
  **"लड़का ______ है।"** — Options: (a) सुन्दर (b) सुन्दरी (c) सुन्दरें (d) सुन्दरों → Correct: **"सुन्दर"** (masculine singular).  
  **"वह ______ खाता है।"** — Options: (a) रोटी (b) रोटियाँ (c) रोटी को (d) रोटी ने → Correct: **"रोटी"** (object, but verb form tested elsewhere).  

- **Identify the Kind of Adjective / Verb**: Classify the highlighted word as गुणवाचक (qualitative), संख्यावाचक (quantitative), सार्वनामिक (pronominal), etc., or as सकर्मक (transitive), अकर्मक (intransitive), संयुक्त (compound), etc.  
  *Example (PYQ-style)*:  
  **"कितने लड़के आये?"** — **"कितने"** is संख्यावाचक विशेषण.  
  **"उसने किताब पढ़ी।"** — **"पढ़ी"** is सकर्मक क्रिया (transitive verb).  

- **Transformation / Conversion**: Change adjective to adverb, verb to noun, active to passive, or alter tense/voice while keeping meaning.  
  *Example (PYQ-style)*:  
  Active: **"राम पत्र लिखता है।"** → Passive: **"राम से पत्र लिखा जाता है।"** (verb form changes from **"लिखता"** to **"लिखा जाता"**).  

- **Error Detection**: A sentence contains a mismatch in adjective agreement (gender/number) or verb concord (subject-verb agreement).  
  *Example (PYQ-style)*:  
  **"लड़कियाँ अच्छा गाती हैं।"** — Error: **"अच्छा"** should be **"अच्छी"** (feminine plural agreement).  
  **"वह जाते हैं।"** — Error: **"जाते"** should be **"जाता"** (singular subject).  

- **One-Word Substitution / Vocabulary Link**: An adjective or verb is described; you supply the exact word.  
  *Example (PYQ-style)*:  
  "One who does not speak much" → **"मितभाषी"** (adjective).  
  "To make someone understand" → **"समझाना"** (causative verb).

**2. Common Examiner Traps**  

| Trap | Description | How to Avoid |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Gender/Number Mismatch in Adjectives** | Options include forms that look similar but differ in gender (e.g., **"सुन्दर"** vs **"सुन्दरी"**) or number (e.g., **"अच्छा"** vs **"अच्छे"**). | Always check the noun the adjective qualifies. Match gender (पुल्लिंग/स्त्रीलिंग) and number (एकवचन/बहुवचन). |
| **Verb Concord with Nearest Noun** | In sentences with compound subjects or intervening phrases, the verb may incorrectly agree with the nearest noun instead of the true subject. | Identify the **main subject** (कर्ता) first. Verb must agree with it in person, number, gender. |
| **Transitive vs. Intransitive Confusion** | Options may swap a transitive verb (takes object) with an intransitive one, or misuse the passive construction. | Ask: "Does the action pass to an object?" If yes → सकर्मक. Passive only possible with transitive verbs. |
| **Tense/Aspect Mismatch** | Options mix present, past, future, habitual, continuous, perfect forms. Time adverbs (कल, अब, रोज) are clues. | Spot time markers. **"रोज"** → habitual present (**"जाता है"**); **"कल"** → past (**"गया"**); **"अब"** → present continuous (**"जा रहा है"**). |
| **Causative vs. Simple Verb** | Pairs like **"खाना"** (to eat) vs **"खिलाना"** (to feed), **"पढ़ना"** vs **"पढ़ाना"**. | Causative adds **-आ-** / **-वा-** and implies causing someone else to do the action. |
| **Adjective Used as Noun (Substantive)** | **"अच्छा"** in **"अच्छा आदमी"** (adjective) vs **"अच्छा"** in **"अच्छा ही होगा"** (adverb/noun-like). | Check function: modifies noun → adjective; modifies verb/whole clause → adverb. |
| **Negative Phrasing** | "Which is **not** an adjective?" or "Find the **incorrect** verb form." | Read the stem twice. Underline **नहीं**, **गलत**, **अशुद्ध**. Eliminate correct statements first. |
| **Look-alike Words** | **"करना"** (to do) vs **"करण"** (instrument case suffix / noun), **"होना"** (to be) vs **"होना"** (to happen). | Context decides. Verb forms take personal endings (**करता, करेगा**); nouns take case endings (**करणे, करणा**). |

**3. High-Yield Sub-Topics (Focus Your Revision)**  
1. **Adjective Agreement** — gender, number, case (direct/oblique) with the qualified noun.  
2. **Verb Concord** — subject-verb agreement in person, number, gender; special rules for compound subjects, "या/अथवा", collective nouns.  
3. **Transitivity & Voice** — identifying transitive/intransitive; active ↔ passive conversion; ergative construction in past transitive (**"ने"** construction).  
4. **Tense-Aspect System** — simple present/past/future, habitual, continuous, perfect; time-adverb mapping.  
5. **Causative Verbs** — formation (adding **-आ-**, **-वा-**), meaning shift, double causative.  
6. **Non-Finite Forms** — infinitive (**-ना**), participle (**-ता/-ती/-ते**, **-ा/-ी/-े**, **-कर/-के**), gerund; their syntactic roles.  
7. **Compound Verbs** — vector verbs (**जाना, देना, लेना, रखना, पड़ना, सकना, चुकना**) and nuance they add.  
8. **Adjective Classification** — गुणवाचक, संख्यावाचक, परिमाणवाचक, सार्वनामिक, प्रश्नवाचक; degrees of comparison (तुलनात्मक, अतिशयोक्तिपूर्ण).  

---

**4. Time-Management Tips**  
- **15-second rule** for spot-the-adjective/verb: underline the target word, match gender/number in 5 sec, confirm in 10 sec.  
- **30-second rule** for fill-in-the-blank: read the whole sentence, identify subject & tense, eliminate mismatched options, pick the one that fits agreement.  
- **Error detection**: scan verb first (highest error yield), then adjective agreement, then case markers.
