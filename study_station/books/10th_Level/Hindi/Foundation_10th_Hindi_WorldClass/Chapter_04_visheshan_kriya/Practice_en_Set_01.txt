1. Identify the adjective (विशेषण) in the following sentence: "राम अच्छा लड़का है।"
(a) राम (b) है (c) लड़का (d) अच्छा
Answer: (d)
Solution: The word "अच्छा" describes the quality of the noun "लड़का", making it a गुणवाचक विशेषण (qualitative adjective). "राम" and "लड़का" are nouns, and "है" is a verb. The tempting noun "लड़का" might be chosen by those confusing the described word with the describing word, so the answer is अच्छा.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Which type of adjective (विशेषण) is "थोड़ा" in the sentence: "थोड़ा पानी पीओ।"
(a) सार्वनामिक (b) संख्यावाचक (c) परिमाणवाचक (d) गुणवाचक
Answer: (c)
Solution: "थोड़ा" indicates an indefinite quantity of the noun "पानी", making it a परिमाणवाचक विशेषण (quantitative adjective). It is not a pronoun (सार्वनामिक), a definite numeral (संख्यावाचक), or a quality descriptor (गुणवाचक). The trap is confusing it with "कुछ" which is pronominal, so the answer is परिमाणवाचक.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Select the correct comparative degree (तुलनात्मक डिग्री) form of the adjective "बड़ा".
(a) बड़ा सा (b) बड़ा (c) सबसे बड़ा (d) और बड़ा
Answer: (d)
Solution: In Hindi, the adjective "बड़ा" remains unchanged; the comparative degree is formed by adding "और" or "अधिक" before it, giving "और बड़ा". The positive degree is "बड़ा", the superlative is "सबसे बड़ा", and "बड़ा सा" is an incorrect form. The trap is assuming the adjective itself changes like in English, so the answer is और बड़ा.
Source: PYQ-style

4. In the sentence "वह तेज दौड़ता है।", identify the verb (क्रिया).
(a) वह (b) दौड़ता (c) तेज (d) है
Answer: (b)
Solution: "दौड़ता" is the main action word (मूल क्रिया) showing what the subject does. "तेज" is an adverb (क्रियाविशेषण) modifying the verb, and "है" is an auxiliary verb (सहायक क्रिया) but not the main lexical verb tested here. The trap is selecting "है" as the only visible verb form, so the answer is दौड़ता.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Which of the following is a compound verb (संयुक्त क्रिया)?
(a) खाना (b) खा लेना (c) खाया (d) खाते
Answer: (b)
Solution: "खा लेना" combines the root "खा" (from खाना) with the auxiliary "लेना" to form a संयुक्त क्रिया indicating completion. "खाना" is an infinitive (नामधातु), "खाया" is past tense, and "खाते" is imperfect participle. The infinitive "खाना" looks like a verb form but is a verbal noun, so the answer is खा लेना.
Source: PYQ-style

6. Choose the correct past tense (भूतकाल) form of the verb "जाना" for the subject "लड़के" (plural, masculine).
(a) गया (b) गई (c) गए (d) गये
Answer: (c)
Solution: For plural masculine subject "लड़के", the verb "जाना" takes the form "गए" in simple past. "गया" is singular masculine, "गई" is feminine. Option (d) "गये" is a common spelling error (using 'ये' instead of 'ए'). The trap is the spelling variant, so the answer is गए.

7. Identify the voice (वाच्य) of the sentence: "पत्र लिखा गया।"
(a) कर्तृवाच्य (b) भाववाच्य (c) कर्मवाच्य (d) इनमें से कोई नहीं
Answer: (c)
Solution: The subject "पत्र" (object in active) receives the action, and the verb "लिखा गया" is in passive construction (कर्मवाच्य). There is no explicit doer (कर्ता). "भाववाच्य" focuses on the action itself without subject/object distinction. The trap is confusing passive with impersonal/भाववाच्य, so the answer is कर्मवाच्य.

Answer: (d)

Answer: (d)
Solution: "धीरे" modifies the verb "बोलता" telling how he speaks, functioning as a क्रियाविशेषण (adverb of manner). "बोलता" is the verb. Students often mistake adjectives describing the subject for adverbs, but "धीरे" describes the action, so the answer is धीरे.

The trap is selecting the respectful plural form "करेंगे" often used in formal Hindi for "मैं", but grammatically "करूँगा" is correct for singular, so the answer is करूँगा.

14. Identify the infinitive (नामधातु) form of the verb "पढ़ना".

Answer: (b)

15. In the sentence "राम ने आम खाया।", what is the case of "राम"?
(a) करण कारक (b) कर्ता कारक (c) संप्रदान कारक (d) कर्म कारक
Answer: (b)
Solution: In past transitive construction (ने construction), the doer "राम" takes "ने" but remains the logical subject (कर्ता) in the ergative case. "आम" is the object (कर्म). The trap is thinking "ने" makes it an object/instrument, but it marks the agent, so the answer is कर्ता कारक.

19. Choose the correct passive voice (कर्मवाच्य) of "राम फल खाता है।"
(a) फल राम खाता है। (b) फल खाया गया। (c) राम द्वारा फल खाया जाता है। (d) फल खाया जाता है।
Answer: (c)
Solution: In कर्मवाच्य (passive voice), the object "फल" becomes subject and the original subject "राम" is expressed as agent with "द्वारा". The verb changes to present passive "खाया जाता है". Option (d) omits the agent, making it भाववाच्य (impersonal), not कर्मवाच्य. Option (b) is past tense. Option (a) is just word-order change. The trap is choosing the agentless form (d) which is actually impersonal voice, so the answer is राम द्वारा फल खाया जाता है।
Source: PYQ-style

The trap is choosing "दौड़ा" (past participle) which also functions adjectivally, but "दौड़ता" is the standard present participle adjective form tested in grammar, so the answer is दौड़ता।

23. Identify the degree of comparison in: "यह पुस्तक उससे अच्छी है।"
(a) उत्तम डिग्री (b) तुलनात्मक डिग्री (c) समान डिग्री (d) मूल डिग्री
Answer: (b)
Solution: "अच्छी" (better) compares two entities ("यह पुस्तक" and "वह"), using the comparative marker "उससे" (than her/it). This is तुलनात्मक डिग्री (comparative degree). Positive is "अच्छी", superlative is "सबसे अच्छी". The trap is seeing "अच्छी" (which looks like positive feminine) and missing the comparative construction "उससे", so the answer is तुलनात्मक डिग्री।

24. Choose the correct agreement for the verb in: "किताबें मेज ______ रखी हैं।"
(a) पर (b) में (c) से (d) को
Answer: (a)
Solution: "रखी हैं" agrees with plural feminine subject "किताबें". The postposition "पर" (on) indicates location. "में" (in), "को" (to/accusative), "से" (from/by) change meaning. The trap is choosing "में" for containment, but "मेज पर" (on the table) is the standard collocation for placing books, so the answer is पर।

25. In the sentence "लड़के को पढ़ना चाहिए।", identify the case of "लड़के" and the form of "पढ़ना".
(a) कर्ता कारक, नामधातु (b) कर्ता कारक, क्रिया (c) संप्रदान कारक, नामधातु (d) कर्म कारक, नामधातु
Answer: (c)
Solution: With modal "चाहिए" (should), the experiencer/agent takes dative case "को" (संप्रदान कारक/संबोधन कारक in traditional grammar, but dative subject). "पढ़ना" is infinitive (नामधातु) functioning as the nominal predicate. The trap is treating "लड़के" as nominative subject (कर्ता) because it's the doer semantically, but syntactically it's dative, so the answer is संप्रदान कारक, नामधातु।
Source: PYQ-style
