Important Rules: Adjectives and Verbs (विशेषण और क्रिया)

This section covers 15 core rules frequently tested in SSC GD Hindi language papers. Each rule is stated in simple English, followed by unambiguous Devanagari examples. Hindi words and sentences that are tested verbatim appear in double quotes.

---

Rule 1 — Definition of विशेषण (Adjective)  
A विशेषण qualifies a संज्ञा (noun) or सर्वनाम (pronoun) by describing quality, quantity, number, or identity.  
Examples:  
- "अच्छा लड़का" (good boy) — "अच्छा" describes quality.  
- "पाँच किताबें" (five books) — "पाँच" shows number.  
- "यह घर" (this house) — "यह" points out identity.

Rule 2 — Agreement of विशेषण with Noun (लिंग, वचन, कारक)  
In Hindi, a variable adjective (गुणवाचक विशेषण) must match the noun in gender (लिंग), number (वचन), and case (कारक).  
Examples:  
- Masculine singular direct: "अच्छा लड़का"  
- Masculine plural direct: "अच्छे लड़के"  
- Feminine singular direct: "अच्छी लड़की"  
- Feminine plural direct: "अच्छी लड़कियाँ"  
Common mistake: writing "अच्छा लड़की" (mismatched gender).

Rule 3 — Invariable Adjectives (अविकारी विशेषण)  
Some adjectives never change form regardless of the noun’s gender/number. Common ones: "कुछ", "बहुत", "थोड़ा", "सारा", "प्रत्येक", "कोई", "कई", "अनेक".  
Examples:  
- "कुछ लड़के", "कुछ लड़कियाँ"  
- "बहुत काम", "बहुत किताबें"  
Do not write "कुछे" or "बहुतों" as adjective forms.

Rule 4 — Types of विशेषण by Meaning  
1. गुणवाचक (qualitative): "सुंदर", "बड़ा", "लाल"  
2. संख्यावाचक (numerical): "एक", "दो", "पहला", "दूसरा"  
3. परिमाणवाचक (quantitative): "थोड़ा", "बहुत", "काफी"  
4. सार्वनामिक (pronominal): "यह", "वह", "मेरा", "तेरा", "कौन", "जो"  
Exam tip: Identify the type from the function in the sentence.

Rule 5 — Position of विशेषण  
Usually the adjective precedes the noun: "लाल फूल".  
In emphatic or poetic order it may follow: "फूल लाल है" (predicative use).  
In SSC GD, the attributive position (before noun) is standard for MCQs.

Rule 6 — Definition of क्रिया (Verb)  
A क्रिया expresses action, occurrence, or state of being. It agrees with the subject in person (पुरुष), number (वचन), and gender (लिंग) where applicable.  
Examples:  
- Action: "लड़का दौड़ता है"  
- State: "वह खुश है"

Rule 7 — Main Verb vs. Auxiliary Verb (मुख्य क्रिया और सहायक क्रिया)  
Main verb carries the core meaning; auxiliary helps form tense, mood, or voice.  
Common auxiliaries: "है", "हैं", "था", "थे", "होगा", "होंगे", "जा", "रहा", "चुका".  
Example: "लड़का पढ़ रहा है" — main verb "पढ़", auxiliaries "रहा है".

Rule 8 — Transitive (सकर्मक) vs. Intransitive (अकर्मक) Verbs  
सकर्मक takes a direct object (कर्म): "राम ने किताब पढ़ी" (किताब = object).  
अकर्मक has no object: "राम सोया".  
Test trick: Ask "क्या?" after the verb; if answer exists → सकर्मक.

Rule 9 — Tense Forms (काल) — Three Main Tenses  
1. वर्तमान काल (Present): "पढ़ता है", "पढ़ रहे हैं"  
2. भूत काल (Past): "पढ़ा", "पढ़ रहा था"  
3. भविष्यत् काल (Future): "पढ़ेगा", "पढ़ रहा होगा"  
Each tense has simple, continuous, perfect, perfect-continuous sub-forms.

Rule 10 — Subject-Verb Agreement (कर्ता-क्रिया अनुबंध)  
Verb matches subject in number and person; in past tense transitive verbs, verb agrees with object (कर्म) if subject has "ने".  
Examples:  
- "लड़का जाता है" (singular)  
- "लड़के जाते हैं" (plural)  
- "लड़के ने किताब पढ़ी" (object "किताब" feminine singular → "पढ़ी")  
- "लड़के ने किताबें पढ़ीं" (object plural → "पढ़ीं")

Rule 11 — Imperative Mood (आज्ञार्थ क्रिया)  
Three levels of politeness:  
- Casual: "जा", "खा", "पी" (tu-level)  
- Polite: "जाओ", "खाओ", "पियो" (tum-level)  
- Formal: "जाइए", "खाइए", "पीजिए" (aap-level)  
Negative: "मत जाओ", "न जाइए".

Rule 12 — Causative Verbs (प्रेरणार्थ क्रिया)  
Formed by adding "आ/वा" to root: "पढ़ना" → "पढ़ाना" (make/let read), "खाना" → "खिलाना" (feed).  
Causative of causative: "पढ़वाना" (get someone to make read).  
SSC GD often tests correct causative form: "माँ ने बेटे को दूध पिलाया" (not "पिलवाया" unless third person involved).

Rule 13 — Compound Verbs (संयुक्त क्रिया)  
Root + auxiliary verb giving nuance:  
- "जा बैठा" (sudden action)  
- "ले गया" (completion + direction)  
- "देख लिया" (completion)  
- "रख दिया" (finality)  
Identify the shade of meaning in MCQs.

Rule 14 — Non-Finite Forms (अवैयक्तिक क्रिया रूप)  
1. Infinitive (स्रोत रूप): "पढ़ना", "जाना"  
2. Perfect participle (कर्मण्य क्रियाविशेषण): "पढ़ा हुआ", "गया हुआ"  
3. Imperfect participle (वर्तमान क्रियाविशेषण): "पढ़ता हुआ", "जाता हुआ"  
4. Conjunctive participle (संयोग क्रियाविशेषण): "पढ़कर", "जाकर"  
These do not change for person/number/gender.

Rule 15 — Common Error Patterns Tested in SSC GD  
1. Gender mismatch: "अच्छा लड़की" → correct "अच्छी लड़की"  
2. Number mismatch: "दो लड़का" → correct "दो लड़के"  
3. Wrong auxiliary: "वह पढ़ रहा हूँ" → correct "वह पढ़ रहा है"  
4. Transitivity error: "उसने दरवाजा खुला" (intransitive used transitively) → correct "उसने दरवाजा खोला"  
5. Causative confusion: "मैंने उसे खाना खिलाया" (correct) vs. "मैंने उसे खाना खाया" (wrong meaning)  
6. Tense inconsistency: "कल वह जाता है" → correct "कल वह गया"  
7. Case ending missing with "ने": "राम किताब पढ़ा" → correct "राम ने किताब पढ़ी"  
8. Adjective used as adverb without "से": "वह तेज दौड़ा" → correct "वह तेजी से दौड़ा"  
9. Double negative: "वह नहीं नहीं आया" → avoid.  
10. Wrong participle: "पढ़के" instead of "पढ़कर".

---

Study Tip: Memorise the agreement tables (Rules 2, 10) and the three imperative forms (Rule 11). Practise spotting the 10 error patterns in Rule 15 — they cover 70 % of grammar MCQs in SSC GD.
