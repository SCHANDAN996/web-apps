51. In the sentence "राम सुन्दर लड़का है।", which word functions as an adjective?
(a) लड़का (b) राम (c) सुन्दर (d) है
Answer: (c)
Solution: The word "सुन्दर" describes the noun "लड़का", making it an adjective; the tempting option "लड़का" is a noun, so the answer is सुन्दर.
Source: PYQ-style

52. In the sentence "सीता गाती है।", which word is the main verb?
(a) गाती (b) है (c) गाती है (d) सीता
Answer: (a)
Solution: "गाती" is the finite verb showing action; "है" is an auxiliary, a common trap, so the answer is गाती.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: The adjective "नया" becomes "नई" in feminine singular and plural; "नये" is masculine plural, a common trap, so the answer is नई.
