# Adjectives and Verbs — Feynman-Style Explanation

Think of a sentence as a tiny movie. **Nouns** (संज्ञा) are the actors — "राम", "किताब", "दिल्ली". **Verbs** (क्रिया) are what the actors *do* or *are* — "चलता है", "पढ़ती है", "सुन्दर है". **Adjectives** (विशेषण) are the costume and make-up department — they describe the actors: "लम्बा", "लाल", "बुद्धिमान".

---

## 1. Verb (क्रिया) — The Engine

A verb tells the action or state. In Hindi the verb usually comes at the end.

| Type | Hindi label | Example | Literal gloss |
|------|-------------|---------|---------------|
| Action | कर्मवाच्य क्रिया | "राम **खाता** है।" | Ram **eats**. |
| State | भाववाचक क्रिया | "सीता **सोती** है।" | Sita **sleeps**. |
| Being | अकर्मक क्रिया | "वह **है**।" | He **is**. |

**Everyday analogy**: The verb is the *engine* of a train. Without it the coaches (nouns, adjectives) just sit on the track.

**Key test-point**: Hindi verbs change for **gender** (लिंग), **number** (वचन) and **person** (पुरुष).  
"लड़का **जाता** है।" (masculine singular)  
"लड़की **जाती** है।" (feminine singular)  
"लड़के **जाते** हैं।" (masculine plural)

---

## 2. Adjective (विशेषण) — The Paint

An adjective qualifies a noun or pronoun. It answers "कैसा?" (how?), "कितना?" (how much?), "कौन सा?" (which?).

| Class | Hindi label | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Quality | गुणवाचक विशेषण | "नीला आसमान" ("blue sky") |
| Quantity | परिमाणवाचक विशेषण | "थोड़ा पानी" ("little water") |
| Number | संख्यावाचक विशेषण | "पाँच आम" ("five mangoes") |
| Demonstrative | सार्वनामिक विशेषण | "यह किताब" ("this book") |

**Everyday analogy**: Adjectives are *sticky notes* you paste on nouns. "गरम" sticks to "चाय", "ठंडा" sticks to "पानी".

**Agreement rule**: Adjectives ending in **आ** (like "अच्छा", "बड़ा", "काला") change exactly like verbs:  
"अच्छा **लड़का**", "अच्छी **लड़की**", "अच्छे **लड़के**".  
Adjectives **not** ending in आ (like "नीला", "सुन्दर", "थोड़ा") **never change**.

---

## 3. Common Traps in Exams

1. **Verb–Adjective confusion**  
   "वह **दौड़ता** है।" → "दौड़ता" is verb (action).  
   "वह **तेज** दौड़ता है।" → "तेज" is adjective (quality), does **not** change.

2. **False agreement**  
   Wrong: "नीले आसमान" (आसमान is masculine singular → "नीला")  
   Right: "नीला आसमान"

3. **Post-position effect**  
   After "ने", "को", "से", "का/के/की" the noun/pronoun takes oblique form, and any adjective ending in आ follows it:  
   "अच्छे **लड़के** ने" (not "अच्छा लड़के ने")

## 4. Five Recall Questions

**Q1.** In "सीता मीठा गाती है", which word is the adjective?  
**Options:** (a) सीता (b) मीठा (c) गाती (d) है  
**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** "मीठा" describes how she sings — quality adjective. "गाती" is the verb.

**Q2.** Choose the correct form: "दो ______ आम खरीदे।"  
**Options:** (a) बड़ा (b) बड़े (c) बड़ों (d) बड़ी  
**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** "आम" is masculine plural oblique (after implied post-position), so "बड़ा" → "बड़े".

**Q3.** Which adjective **does not** change for gender/number?  
**Options:** (a) काला (b) सुन्दर (c) अच्छा (d) छोटा  
**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** "सुन्दर" ends in र, not आ — invariant.

**Q4.** Identify the verb in "बच्चे खुशी से खेलते हैं।"  
**Options:** (a) बच्चे (b) खुशी (c) खेलते (d) से  
**Answer:** (c)  
**Explanation:** "खेलते" shows action; "हैं" is auxiliary.

**Q5.** Correct the error: "वह लाल साड़ी पहनती हैं।"  
**Options:** (a) वह लाल साड़ी पहनती है। (b) वह लाल साड़ी पहनते हैं। (c) वह लाल साड़ी पहनता है। (d) वह लाल साड़ी पहनते है।  
**Answer:** (a)  
**Explanation:** Subject "वह" (singular) → verb "पहनती है". "लाल" (invariant) stays "लाल".

---

## 5. One-Minute Revision Card

| Concept | Hook |
|---------|------|
| Verb = Engine | Comes last, changes for gender/number/person. |
| Adjective = Sticky note | Ends in आ → changes; otherwise fixed. |
| Oblique trigger | ने, को, से, का/के/की → noun & आ-ending adjective both shift. |
| Invariant adjectives | नीला, सुन्दर, थोड़ा, बहुत, कौन सा — never change. |

Stick to these patterns and the "Adjectives & Verbs" questions in SSC GD, MTS, CHSL become straightforward.
