# Important Rules: Tense and Voice (काल और वाच्य)

This section presents 15 core rules for Hindi tense (काल) and voice (वाच्य) that frequently appear in SSC GD, MTS, Railway Group D, and state constable exams. Each rule includes clear Hindi examples (in double quotes) and notes on common exceptions or pitfalls.

---

## Rule 1: Three Main Tenses (तीन मुख्य काल)

Hindi verbs are classified into three primary tenses based on time of action:
- **Present Tense (वर्तमान काल)** — action happening now or habitually.
- **Past Tense (भूतकाल)** — action completed in the past.
- **Future Tense (भविष्यत् काल)** — action yet to happen.

**Examples:**
- Present: "वह पढ़ता है।" (He studies / is studying.)
- Past: "वह पढ़ा।" (He studied.)
- Future: "वह पढ़ेगा।" (He will study.)

**Note:** Hindi does not have separate continuous/perfect forms like English; aspect is shown by auxiliary verbs (रहा, चुका, चुका होगा, etc.).

---

## Rule 2: Present Tense — Habitual vs. Continuous (वर्तमान काल — अभ्यस्त बनाम जारी)

- **Habitual (सामान्य/नित्य):** Root + ता/ते/ती + है/हैं/हूँ  
  Example: "मैं रोज दौड़ता हूँ।" (I run daily.)
- **Continuous (जारी/प्रगतिशील):** Root + रहा/रहे/रही + है/हैं/हूँ  
  Example: "वह अभी दौड़ रहा है।" (He is running right now.)

**Common Mistake:** Using "रहा" for habitual actions.  
✗ "मैं रोज दौड़ रहा हूँ।" → ✓ "मैं रोज दौड़ता हूँ।"

---

## Rule 3: Past Tense — Simple vs. Continuous vs. Perfect (भूतकाल — साधारण, जारी, पूर्ण)

| Form | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Simple (साधारण) | Root + ा/े/ी + था/थे/थी | "वह गया।" / "वह गया था।" |
| Continuous (जारी) | Root + रहा/रहे/रही + था/थे/थी | "वह जा रहा था।" |
| Perfect (पूर्ण) | Root + चुका/चुके/चुकी + था/थे/थी | "वह जा चुका था।" |

**Exception:** Intransitive verbs of motion (जाना, आना, सोना) often use simple past for completed action without "था": "वह गया।" (not necessarily "गया था").

---

## Rule 4: Future Tense — Simple vs. Continuous (भविष्यत् काल — साधारण बनाम जारी)

- **Simple Future:** Root + गा/गे/गी + (optional auxiliary)  
  Example: "वह कल आएगा।" (He will come tomorrow.)
- **Future Continuous:** Root + रहा/रहे/रही + होगा/होंगे/होगी  
  Example: "कल इस समय वह पढ़ रहा होगा।" (At this time tomorrow, he will be studying.)

**Note:** The simple future form (आएगा, पढ़ेगा) is also used for presumption: "वह अब तक पहुँच गया होगा।" (He must have reached by now.)

---

## Rule 5: Person, Number, Gender Agreement (पुरुष, वचन, लिंग अनुबंध)

The verb ending must agree with the **subject** in person (पुरुष), number (वचन), and gender (लिंग).

| Subject | Verb Ending (Present Habitual) |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| मैं (1st sg. masc.) | ता + हूँ → "पढ़ता हूँ" |
| हम (1st pl. masc.) | ते + हैं → "पढ़ते हैं" |
| तू (2nd sg. masc.) | ता + है → "पढ़ता है" |
| तुम (2nd pl. masc.) | ते + हो → "पढ़ते हो" |
| आप (2nd hon. sg./pl.) | ते + हैं → "पढ़ते हैं" |
| वह (3rd sg. masc.) | ता + है → "पढ़ता है" |
| वे (3rd pl. masc.) | ते + हैं → "पढ़ते हैं" |
| Feminine singular | ती + है/हूँ/हो → "पढ़ती है" |
| Feminine plural | ती + हैं/हैं → "पढ़ती हैं" |

**Common Mistake:** Mismatching gender.  
✗ "लड़की पढ़ता है।" → ✓ "लड़की पढ़ती है।"

---

## Rule 6: Transitive vs. Intransitive Verbs (सकर्मक बनाम अकर्मक क्रिया)

- **Transitive (सकर्मक):** Takes a direct object (कर्म). Can be passivized.  
  Examples: "पढ़ना" (to read), "लिखना" (to write), "खाना" (to eat).  
  "वह किताब पढ़ता है।" → Passive: "किताब पढ़ी जाती है।"
- **Intransitive (अकर्मक):** No direct object. Cannot be passivized.  
  Examples: "जाना" (to go), "आना" (to come), "सोना" (to sleep), "हँसना" (to laugh).  
  ✗ "जाया जाता है।" (Incorrect passive)

**Exam Trap:** Some verbs are both depending on usage: "चलना" (intransitive: "वह चलता है") vs. "चलाना" (transitive: "वह गाड़ी चलाता है").

---

## Rule 7: Active Voice (कर्तृ वाच्य) — Subject is Doer

Structure: **Subject (कर्ता) + Object (कर्म) + Verb (क्रिया)**  
Verb agrees with subject.

Examples:
- "राम आम खाता है।" (Ram eats mango.)
- "सीता गीत गाती है।" (Sita sings a song.)

**Key:** The subject (राम, सीता) performs the action. Verb matches subject in gender/number.

---

## Rule 8: Passive Voice (कर्म वाच्य) — Object Becomes Subject

Structure: **Object (कर्म) + द्वारा (by-agent, optional) + Verb (passive form)**  
Verb agrees with the new subject (original object).

**Formation:** Transitive verb root + आ/ए/ई + जाना/जाना (auxiliary)  
Present: "आम खाया जाता है।" (Mango is eaten.)  
Past: "आम खाया गया।" (Mango was eaten.)  
Future: "आम खाया जाएगा।" (Mango will be eaten.)

**Agent Marker:** "द्वारा" introduces the doer.  
"आम राम द्वारा खाया जाता है।" (Mango is eaten by Ram.)

**Common Mistake:** Using "ने" in passive.  
✗ "आम राम ने खाया जाता है।" → ✓ "आम राम द्वारा खाया जाता है।"

---

## Rule 9: Impersonal Passive (भाव वाच्य) — No Expressed Subject

Used when the doer is unknown, irrelevant, or general. No "द्वारा" phrase. Verb is singular masculine default.

Examples:
- "यहाँ धूम्रपान मना है।" (Smoking is prohibited here.)
- "कहा जाता है कि…" (It is said that…)
- "माना जाता है कि…" (It is believed that…)

**Note:** This is not a true passive of a transitive sentence; it is a conventional construction with intransitive or transitive verbs used impersonally.

---

## Rule 10: "ने" Construction in Past Transitive (भूतकाल में सकर्मक क्रिया + ने)

In **simple past** and **perfect** tenses of transitive verbs, the subject takes the postposition "ने", and the verb agrees with the **object** (if object is not marked with "को").

| Active (Present) | Active (Past) |
|------------------|---------------|
| "राम आम खाता है।" | "राम ने आम खाया।" |
| "सीता गीत गाती है।" | "सीता ने गीत गाया।" |
| "वे किताबें पढ़ते हैं।" | "उन्होंने किताबें पढ़ीं।" |

**Exception:** If object has "को" (definite animate), verb defaults to masculine singular:  
"राम ने सीता को देखा।" (not "देखी")

---

## Rule 11: Passive of "ने" Sentences — Object Becomes Subject, "ने" → "द्वारा"

Active (Past): "राम ने आम खाया।"  
Passive: "आम राम द्वारा खाया गया।"

**Agreement Shift:** Verb now agrees with "आम" (masc. sg.) → "खाया गया".  
If object were feminine: "सीता ने गीत गाया।" → "गीत सीता द्वारा गाया गया।"  
If object plural feminine: "सीता ने कविताएँ लिखीं।" → "कविताएँ सीता द्वारा लिखी गईं।"

---

## Rule 12: Causative Verbs (प्रेरणार्थक क्रिया) — Not Passive

Causatives are formed by adding ा/व्/आ to root: "पढ़ना" → "पढ़ाना" (to make study), "खाना" → "खिलाना" (to feed). They are **active transitive** verbs, not passive.

Examples:
- "शिक्षक छात्रों को पढ़ाता है।" (Teacher makes students study / teaches students.)
- "माँ बच्चे को खिलाती है।" (Mother feeds the child.)

**Common Mistake:** Treating causative as passive.  
✗ "छात्र पढ़ाए जाते हैं।" (Students are made to study — this is passive of causative, not causative itself.)

---

## Rule 13: Tense Consistency in Complex Sentences (काल सामंजस्य)

In sentences with subordinate clauses, tense must logically align.

- Main clause past → subordinate usually past:  
  "उसने कहा कि वह आएगा।" (He said that he would come.) — Future in subordinate relative to past main verb.
- Habitual present + subordinate present:  
  "वह रोज आता है और काम करता है।" (He comes daily and works.)

**Exam Trap:** Mixing tenses illogically.  
✗ "वह कल आया और कल जाएगा।" (He came yesterday and will go yesterday.) → ✓ "वह कल आया और आज जाएगा।"

---

## Rule 14: Negative Sentences — "नहीं" Placement (नकारात्मक वाक्य)

"नहीं" precedes the main verb (or auxiliary in compound forms).

- Simple: "वह नहीं आता।" (He does not come.)
- Continuous: "वह नहीं आ रहा है।" (He is not coming.)
- Perfect: "वह नहीं आया है।" (He has not come.)
- Passive: "यह काम नहीं किया गया।" (This work was not done.)

**Note:** In past transitive with "ने", "नहीं" goes before verb: "राम ने आम नहीं खाया।"

---

## Rule 15: Interrogative Sentences — Question Word Order (प्रश्नवाचक वाक्य)

Question words (कौन, क्या, कब, कहाँ, क्यों, कैसे, कितना) typically appear **before the verb**, not necessarily at start.

- "तुम कब आओगे?" (When will you come?)
- "वह कहाँ गया?" (Where did he go?)
- "कौन आया?" (Who came?)
- "क्या तुम खाना खाओगे?" (Will you eat food?)

**Yes/No Questions:** Add "क्या" at beginning or use rising intonation:  
"क्या वह आएगा?" / "वह आएगा?" (Will he come?)

**Common Mistake:** Placing question word at end like English.  
✗ "तुम आओगे कब?" → ✓ "तुम कब आओगे?"

## Quick Reference Table: Voice Transformation (Active → Passive)

| Tense | Active (Transitive) | Passive |
|-------|---------------------|---------|
| Present Habitual | "राम पत्र लिखता है।" | "पत्र राम द्वारा लिखा जाता है।" |
| Present Continuous | "राम पत्र लिख रहा है।" | "पत्र राम द्वारा लिखा जा रहा है।" |
| Past Simple | "राम ने पत्र लिखा।" | "पत्र राम द्वारा लिखा गया।" |
| Past Continuous | "राम पत्र लिख रहा था।" | "पत्र राम द्वारा लिखा जा रहा था।" |
| Future Simple | "राम पत्र लिखेगा।" | "पत्र राम द्वारा लिखा जाएगा।" |

**Remember:** Only transitive verbs (सकर्मक क्रिया) can be passivized. Intransitive verbs (जाना, आना, सोना, हँसना) have no passive voice.

---

**Study Tip:** For exams, practice converting 10–15 active sentences to passive daily. Focus on agreement shifts (verb matching new subject) and correct use of "ने" (active past) vs. "द्वारा" (passive agent).
