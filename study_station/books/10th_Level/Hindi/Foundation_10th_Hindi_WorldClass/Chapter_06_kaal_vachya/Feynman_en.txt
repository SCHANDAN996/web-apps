# Tense and Voice — Feynman Explanation

## The Core Idea

Think of **tense (काल)** as a **timestamp** on a photograph. The picture (the action) is the same, but the timestamp tells you *when* it happened — past, present, or future.

Think of **voice (वाच्य)** as the **camera angle**. The same scene can be shot from the doer's side (active) or the receiver's side (passive). The facts don't change; only the focus shifts.

---

## Tense (काल) — The Three Time Zones

| Tense | Hindi Label | Everyday Analogy | Key Markers |
|-------|-------------|------------------|-------------|
| Present | वर्तमान काल | "Live broadcast" — happening now or habitually | "ता है", "ती है", "ते हैं", "रहा है", "चुका है" |
| Past | भूतकाल | "Replay" — already finished | "आ", "ई", "ए", "था", "थी", "थे", "चुका था" |
| Future | भविष्यत् काल | "Schedule" — yet to come | "गा", "गी", "गे", "उंगा", "उंगी", "उंगे" |

**Worked example** — same verb "खाना" (to eat):

- Present: "वह खाता है" (He eats / is eating)
- Past: "वह खाया" (He ate)
- Future: "वह खाएगा" (He will eat)

**Common mistake**: Mixing markers — *"वह खाया है"* (past marker + present auxiliary) is wrong. Use either "खाया" (simple past) or "खा चुका है" (present perfect).

---

## Voice (वाच्य) — Two Camera Angles

| Voice | Hindi Label | Focus | Structure |
|-------|-------------|-------|-----------|
| Active | कर्तृ वाच्य | Doer (कर्ता) | कर्ता + क्रिया |
| Passive | कर्म वाच्य | Receiver (कर्म) | कर्म + "जाना" + क्रिया (past participle) |

**Worked example** — "राम पत्र लिखता है" (Ram writes a letter)

- Active: "राम पत्र लिखता है" — focus on Ram
- Passive: "पत्र राम द्वारा लिखा जाता है" — focus on the letter

**Rule of thumb**: Only transitive verbs (सकर्मक क्रिया) — verbs that take an object — can become passive. "सोना" (to sleep) has no passive because nobody "is slept."

**Common mistake**: Adding "द्वारा" in active voice — *"राम द्वारा पत्र लिखता है"* is wrong. "द्वारा" belongs only in passive.

## 5 Recall Questions

**Q1.** Identify the tense of: "वह पढ़ रहा है"  
(a) भूतकाल (b) वर्तमान काल (c) भविष्यत् काल (d) अनिश्चित काल  
**Answer: (b)**  
**Solution:** "रहा है" marks present continuous (वर्तमान काल). The action is ongoing now.

**Q2.** Change to passive: "सीता गाना गाती है"  
(a) "गाना सीता द्वारा गाया जाता है" (b) "सीता द्वारा गाना गाया जाता है" (c) "गाना सीता गाती है" (d) "सीता गाना गाया जाता है"  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Solution:** Object "गाना" becomes subject; "द्वारा" marks original doer; verb becomes "गाया जाता है" (past participle + जाना + present).

**Q3.** Which verb CANNOT be made passive?  
(a) "पढ़ना" (b) "लिखना" (c) "सोना" (d) "बनाना"  
**Answer: (c)**  
**Solution:** "सोना" (to sleep) is intransitive (अकर्मक) — no object to become passive subject.

**Q4.** Spot the error: "वह कल आएगा था"  
(a) Tense clash (b) Wrong gender (c) Missing object (d) No error  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Solution:** "आएगा" (future) + "था" (past) — impossible combination. Correct: "वह कल आएगा" or "वह कल आने वाला था".

**Q5.** Passive of "बच्चे आम खाते हैं"  
(a) "आम बच्चों द्वारा खाए जाते हैं" (b) "बच्चों द्वारा आम खाए जाते हैं" (c) "आम बच्चे खाते हैं" (d) "बच्चे आम खाए जाते हैं"  
**Answer: (a)**  
**Solution:** Plural object "आम" → plural verb "खाए जाते हैं"; "बच्चे" → "बच्चों द्वारा".

---

## Quick Mental Checklist

- **Tense**: Does the timestamp match the action? (No "था" with "गा")
- **Voice**: Is the verb transitive? If yes, can flip; if no, stay active.
- **Agreement**: In passive, verb agrees with *new* subject (the old object), not the doer.

Keep these two analogies — **timestamp** and **camera angle** — and you'll navigate any tense/voice question in the exam.
