PYQ Pattern Analysis — Tense and Voice (काल और वाच्य)

This chapter tests the candidate’s ability to identify, convert, and correct Hindi verb forms across the three tenses (Present, Past, Future) and two voices (Active, Passive). SSC GD and equivalent 10th-level exams ask 2–4 questions from this topic, usually as:
- Spot the error in a given sentence
- Fill in the blank with the correct verb form
- Convert Active to Passive (or vice versa) without changing tense
- Choose the sentence that correctly uses a given tense/voice

No year-wise frequency or weightage is claimed here; the patterns below reflect the typical syllabus scope for 10th-level Hindi grammar.

---

### 1. Tense (काल) — Recurring Question Patterns

| Pattern | What is Tested | Typical Trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Identify the tense** | Recognise whether the verb is वर्तमान काल, भूतकाल, or भविष्यत् काल | Mixing aspect markers (e.g., "रहा है" vs "रहा था") |
| **Fill the blank with correct form** | Supply the verb that matches the subject, number, gender, and tense indicated by time adverbials (कल, आज, कल) | Ignoring gender/number agreement (e.g., "लड़की *गया*" instead of "गयी") |
| **Spot the error** | One part of the sentence uses a wrong tense form | Near-correct forms like "वह *पढ़ता था*" (habitual past) vs "वह *पढ़ रहा था*" (continuous past) |
| **Match the sentence to tense name** | Given a sentence, pick "वर्तमान काल", "भूतकाल", or "भविष्यत् काल" | Confusing "सामान्य भूतकाल" (simple past) with "पूर्ण भूतकाल" (perfect past) |

**Key markers examiners rely on:**
- वर्तमान काल: "है", "हैं", "हूँ", "रहा है", "रहे हैं", "ता है", "ती है", "ते हैं"
- भूतकाल: "था", "थे", "थी", "थीं", "आ", "ई", "ए", "या", "यी", "ये"
- भविष्यत् काल: "गा", "गी", "गे", "ऊँगा", "ऊँगी", "ओगे", "एगा", "एगी", "एगे"

### 2. Voice (वाच्य) — Recurring Question Patterns

| Pattern | What is Tested | Typical Trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Active → Passive conversion** | Change "राम फल खाता है" to "फल राम द्वारा खाया जाता है" | Forgetting "द्वारा" or mismatching verb agreement with new subject |
| **Passive → Active conversion** | Reverse the above | Dropping the agent or changing tense inadvertently |
| **Identify voice** | Label a sentence as कर्तृवाच्य (Active) or कर्मवाच्य (Passive) | Misidentifying intransitive verbs (e.g., "वह सोता है") as passive |
| **Choose correct passive form** | Given active sentence, pick the grammatically correct passive option | Options with wrong tense carry-over (e.g., present active → past passive) |

**Conversion rules tested repeatedly:**
1. Subject ↔ Object swap
2. Add "द्वारा" after new subject (agent)
3. Verb → "जाना" + past participle (कर्मवाच्य क्रिया) matching new subject in gender/number
4. Tense **must not change**

---

### 3. Common Examiner Traps (All Levels)

1. **Near-correct verb forms** — "वह *पढ़ता है*" (correct) vs "वह *पढ़ता हूँ*" (wrong person); "लड़के *खेलते हैं*" vs "लड़के *खेलता है*".
2. **Gender/number agreement mismatch** — "सीता *गया*" (should be "गयी"), "बच्चे *सोयी*" (should be "सोए").
3. **Tense shift in voice conversion** — Active present "राम पत्र लिखता है" → Passive *past* "पत्र राम द्वारा लिखा गया" (wrong; must be "लिखा जाता है").
4. **Intransitive verbs forced into passive** — "वह *हँसा जाता है*" (incorrect; intransitive verbs have no passive).
5. **Negative phrasing** — "निम्न में से कौन-सा वाक्य *कर्मवाच्य* **नहीं** है?" — tests careful reading.
6. **Mixed tense markers** — "वह *पढ़ रहा था*" (past continuous) vs "वह *पढ़ता था*" (past habitual) — both भूतकाल but different aspects.
7. **Honorific/plural verb with singular subject** — "पिताजी *आते हैं*" (correct honorific) vs "पिताजी *आता है*" (wrong in formal Hindi).

### 4. PYQ-Style Original Examples (Labelled PYQ-style)

**Example 1 — Spot the Error (Tense)**  
Sentence: "वह कल दिल्ली *जाता है*।"  
Error: "जाता है" (वर्तमान काल) clashes with "कल" (past time).  
Correct: "वह कल दिल्ली *गया*।"  
Source: PYQ-style

**Example 2 — Fill in the Blank (Tense Agreement)**  
"लड़कियाँ मैदान में *________*।" (Options: खेलता है / खेलते हैं / खेलती हैं / खेला)  
Correct: "खेलती हैं" — feminine plural subject, present tense.  
Source: PYQ-style

**Example 3 — Active to Passive (Tense Preservation)**  
Active: "पुलिस चोर को *पकड़ती है*।"  
Passive: "चोर को पुलिस द्वारा *पकड़ा जाता है*।"  
Trap option: "चोर को पुलिस द्वारा *पकड़ा गया*" (tense changed to past).  
Source: PYQ-style

**Example 4 — Identify Voice**  
"पत्र लिखा गया।" → कर्मवाच्य (Passive) — agent omitted, verb "जाना + participle".  
"राम पत्र लिखता है।" → कर्तृवाच्य (Active).  
Source: PYQ-style

**Example 5 — Negative Phrasing Trap**  
"निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा वाक्य **भविष्यत् काल** में **नहीं** है?"  
Options:  
(a) "वह कल *आएगा*।"  
(b) "मैं *जाऊँगा*।"  
(c) "तुम *खेलते हो*।"  
(d) "वे *पढ़ेंगे*।"  
Correct: (c) — "खेलते हो" is वर्तमान काल.  
Source: PYQ-style

**Example 6 — Intransitive Verb Passive Trap**  
"निम्न में से कौन-सा वाक्य **कर्मवाच्य** में बदला **नहीं** जा सकता?"  
Options:  
(a) "राम फल खाता है।"  
(b) "सीता गाती है।"  
(c) "वह सोता है।"  
(d) "बच्चे किताब पढ़ते हैं।"  
Correct: (c) — "सोना" is intransitive; no object to become subject.  
Source: PYQ-style

### 5. Solving Tips (15–45 Second Rule)

- **Spot the time word first** (कल, आज, कल, रोज, अभी, तब) — it locks the tense.
- **Check subject gender/number** before choosing verb form.
- **In voice conversion, tick three boxes**: (1) Subject-object swapped, (2) "द्वारा" present, (3) Verb = "जाना" + participle matching new subject, (4) Tense unchanged.
- **Reject any option that changes tense** during voice conversion.
- **Intransitive verbs = no passive** — memorise common ones: सोना, जाना, आना, हँसना, रोना, उठना, बैठना, मरना, जन्म लेना.
- **Negative questions** — underline "नहीं" / "नहीं है" in the stem; pick the odd one out.
