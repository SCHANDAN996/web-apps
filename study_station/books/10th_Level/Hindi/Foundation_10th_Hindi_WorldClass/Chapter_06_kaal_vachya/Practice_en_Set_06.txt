126. Identify the tense of the verb in the sentence: "राम खाता है।"
(a) Present Tense (वर्तमान काल) (b) Past Tense (भूत काल) (c) Future Tense (भविष्यत काल) (d) Dubitative Tense (संदेहार्थक काल)
Answer: (a)
Solution: The verb "खाता है" indicates a habitual action in the present, which is Present Tense. The trap is confusing the 'ता' ending with Past Tense, but the auxiliary "है" marks it as present. So the answer is Present Tense.

129. Change the voice of "राम ने आम खाया।" to Passive Voice (कर्मवाच्य).
(a) "आम राम ने खाया।" (b) "राम आम खाता है।" (c) "आम खाया गया।" (d) "आम राम द्वारा खाया गया।"
Answer: (d)
Solution: In passive voice, the object becomes the subject ("आम"), the verb changes to passive form ("खाया गया"), and the original subject takes "द्वारा". The option "आम खाया गया" omits the agent, which is incomplete for a transitive verb with an expressed agent. So the answer is "आम राम द्वारा खाया गया।"
