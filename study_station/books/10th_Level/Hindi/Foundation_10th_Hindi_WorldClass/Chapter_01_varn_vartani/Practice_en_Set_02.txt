26. Which of the following is the correct spelling of the Hindi word "किताब"?
(a) किताब् (b) कित्ताब (c) किताब (d) किताबं
Answer: (c)
Solution: The word "किताब" uses a simple त and ब without gemination or anusvara; the other options add unnecessary diacritics, so the answer is किताब.
Source: PYQ-style

38. The visarga (ः) in "दुःख" indicates:
(a) Lengthening (b) Nasalisation (c) Aspiration after the vowel (d) Stress
Answer: (c)
Solution: Visarga adds a voiceless breath after the preceding vowel, representing an aspirated release; it does not nasalise or lengthen, so the answer is Aspiration after the vowel.
Source: PYQ-style

39. In the word "कर्म", the half‑consonant (अर्धव्यंजन) is formed by adding halant to which consonant?
(a) क (b) र (c) म (d) None of these
Answer: (b)
Solution: In "कर्म" the conjunct र्म shows the half‑form of र (र्) combined with म; the halant is added to र to create this half‑consonant, while क and म remain full consonants, so the answer is र.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Hindi
