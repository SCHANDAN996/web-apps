Card 1
Front: What is the term for the smallest unit of sound in Hindi?
Back: "वर्ण" (Varṇ) — the basic letter/sound unit. Example: "क", "ख", "ग".

Card 2
Front: How many main vowels (स्वर) are there in standard Hindi?
Back: 11 main vowels: "अ आ इ ई उ ऊ ऋ ए ऐ ओ औ अं अः". (Some counts treat "अं" and "अः" as separate, giving 13.)

Card 3
Front: How many main consonants (व्यंजन) are there in standard Hindi?
Back: 33 main consonants from "क" to "ह", plus 4 combined forms "क्ष त्र ज्ञ श्र" often taught separately.

Card 4
Front: What is the difference between "अनुस्वार" and "अनुनासिक"?
Back: "अनुस्वार" (ं) is a dot above the line representing nasal sound before a consonant; "अनुनासिक" (ँ) is a dot above the vowel (चन्द्रबिन्दु) showing nasalisation of the vowel itself. Example: "हिंदी" (अनुनासिक) vs "हिन्दी" (अनुस्वार before द).

Card 5
Front: When is "चन्द्रबिन्दु" (ँ) used instead of "बिन्दु" (ं)?
Back: "चन्द्रबिन्दु" is used when the vowel is short (अ, इ, उ) or when the nasal sound is purely vocalic; "बिन्दु" is used before conjunct consonants or long vowels in modern standard spelling. Example: "मैं" (ँ), "हिंदी" (ँ) but "संग" (ं).

Card 6
Front: What is "हलन्त" (्) and what does it do?
Back: "हलन्त" (virama) removes the inherent "अ" from a consonant, making it half: "क्" = "क" without "अ". Used in conjuncts like "क् + ष = क्ष".

Card 7
Front: Give an example of a conjunct consonant (संयुक्त व्यंजन) formed with "र" as first member.
Back: "र् + क = र्क" as in "कर्क" (crab); "र् + म = र्म" as in "धर्म".

Card 8
Front: Give an example of a conjunct consonant formed with "र" as second member.
Back: "क् + र = क्र" as in "क्रोध"; "प् + र = प्र" as in "प्रेम".

Card 9
Front: What is the spelling rule for "श्र"?
Back: "श् + र = श्र" — always written as a single conjunct. Example: "श्रेष्ठ", "श्रृंखला".

Card 10
Front: How do you distinguish "ड़" and "ढ़" from "ड" and "ढ" in pronunciation?
Back: "ड़" and "ढ़" are retroflex flaps (ट/ड family) with a quick tongue tap; "ड" and "ढ" are plosives. In spelling, "ड़/ढ़" occur medially/finally in tadbhava words: "बड़ा", "सीढ़ी".

Card 11
Front: What is the correct spelling: "विचार" or "बिचार"?
Back: "विचार" — initial "व" (labiodental) is standard; "ब" (bilabial) is a common dialectal error.

Card 12
Front: Which is correct: "शुद्ध" or "सुद्ध"?
Back: "शुद्ध" — "श" (palatal sibilant) is correct; "स" (dental) is a frequent misspelling.

Card 13
Front: Choose the correctly spelt word: "प्रभाव", "प्रभाब", "प्रभाव्", "प्रभाब्".
Back: "प्रभाव" — correct use of "व" and no halant at word end.

Card 14
Front: Identify the error in "अध्ध्ययन".
Back: Double "द्ध" is wrong; correct is "अध्ययन" (single "ध् + य").

Card 15
Front: What is the rule for writing "ज्ञ"?
Back: "ज् + ञ = ज्ञ" — a conjunct of "ज" and "ञ". Pronounced "ज्ञ" (gya) in most words: "ज्ञान", "विज्ञान".

Card 16
Front: How is the long vowel "ई" marked on a consonant?
Back: With the matra "ी" placed above the consonant: "क + ी = की".

Card 17
Front: What is the difference between "ऋ" and "रि" in spelling?
Back: "ऋ" is a separate vowel letter; "रि" is consonant "र" + short "इ" matra. Words like "ऋषि" use "ऋ"; "रिश्ता" uses "रि".

Card 18
Front: Which word uses "अनुस्वार" correctly before a labial consonant?
Back: "संभव" — "ं" before "भ" (labial) represents "म" sound. Similarly "संकट" (before क), "संगत" (before ग).
