126. Which of the following is the correct spelling of the word "किताब"?  
(a) किताब् (b) किताब (c) कितााब (d) कित्ताब
Answer: (b)  
Solution: The common trap is the double "त" in option (d) and the extra "ा" in option (c); the correct spelling uses a single "त" and proper matra, so the answer is किताब.  
Source: PYQ-style
