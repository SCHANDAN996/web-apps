Letters and Spelling (वर्ण और वर्तनी)

Introduction

Hindi is written in the Devanagari script. Every sound of the language has a fixed written symbol. Mastering the alphabet (वर्णमाला) and the spelling rules (वर्तनी नियम) is the first step to reading, writing, and scoring well in the Hindi section of SSC GD, MTS, RRB Group D, and state constable exams. Questions in these exams test whether you can spot the correctly spelt word, fill the right matra (मात्रा), or choose the word that follows standard spelling conventions. This lesson covers the alphabet, the matra system, the most frequent spelling rules, six worked examples, and a list of common errors to avoid.

1. The Hindi Alphabet (हिंदी वर्णमाला)

The Devanagari alphabet is divided into two broad groups:

- Swar (स्वर) — Vowels: 13 letters
  "अ", "आ", "इ", "ई", "उ", "ऊ", "ऋ", "ए", "ऐ", "ओ", "औ", "अं", "अः"

- Vyanjan (व्यंजन) — Consonants: 33 letters (arranged by place of articulation)
  क ख ग घ ङ | च छ ज झ ञ | ट ठ ड ढ ण | त थ द ध न | प फ ब भ म | य र ल व | श ष स ह | क्ष त्र ज्ञ

Each consonant carries an inherent "अ" sound. When a different vowel follows, the inherent "अ" is replaced by the corresponding matra (vowel sign).

2. Matras (मात्राएँ) — Vowel Signs

There are 11 matras (the 13 vowels minus "अ" and "अः" which have no separate matra form). They are written above, below, before, or after the consonant.

| Vowel | Matra | Example with क |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| आ | ा | का |
| इ | ि | कि |
| ई | ी | की |
| उ | ु | कु |
| ऊ | ू | कू |
| ऋ | ृ | कृ |
| ए | े | के |
| ऐ | ै | कै |
| ओ | ो | को |
| औ | ौ | कौ |
| अं | ं | कं |
| अः | ः | कः |

Rule: The matra "ि" (इ) is written **before** the consonant (e.g., "कि"), but pronounced after it. All other matras follow the consonant in writing.

3. Special Signs

- Anusvar (अनुस्वार) — "ं" (dot above): nasal sound, replaces the nasal consonant of the same class before a consonant. Example: "संग" (saṅga), "हिंदी" (hindī).
- Anunasik (अनुनासिक) — "ँ" (crescent above): nasalisation of the vowel itself. Used in words like "हँसना" (haṁsnā), "मैं" (maĩ).
- Visarg (विसर्ग) — "ः" (two dots): adds a voiceless 'h' sound after the vowel. Example: "दुःख" (duḥkha), "मनः" (manaḥ).
- Halant / Viram (हलंत/विराम) — "्" (slash below): removes the inherent "अ". Used in conjunct consonants (संयुक्त व्यंजन). Example: "क् + त = क्त" in "शक्ति" (śakti).
- Nukta (नुक़्ता) — "़" (dot below): modifies certain consonants for Perso-Arabic sounds. Example: "क़" (q), "ख़" (kh), "ग़" (ġ), "ज़" (z), "फ़" (f).

4. Key Spelling Rules (मुख्य वर्तनी नियम)

Rule 1: Anusvar before a consonant of the same class
When a nasal sound precedes a consonant, write "ं" (Anusvar) instead of the full nasal letter.
- "संग" (not "सङ्ग")
- "अंगूर" (not "अङ्गूर")
- "चंपा" (not "चम्पा" — here म is the class nasal for प, so Anusvar is correct)

Rule 2: Anunasik for vowel nasalisation
When the vowel itself is nasalised (no following consonant), use "ँ" (Anunasik).
- "हँसना" (laugh)
- "मैं" (I)
- "साँप" (snake)

Rule 3: Visarg before क, ख, प, फ, श, ष, स, ह
Visarg is traditionally used before these voiceless consonants.
- "दुःख" (sorrow)
- "मनःस्थिति" (state of mind)
- "निर्माणः" (in isolation or before pause)

Rule 4: Conjunct consonants (संयुक्त व्यंजन)
Two or more consonants join without an intervening vowel. The first consonant takes Halant.
- "क् + त = क्त" → "शक्ति" (power)
- "स् + थ = स्थ" → "स्थान" (place)
- "द् + ध = द्ध" → "बुद्धि" (intellect)
- "ष् + ट = ष्ट" → "पुष्ट" (strong)

Rule 5: Nukta usage (standardised spellings)
Certain loanwords must carry Nukta.
- "क़लम" (pen), not "कलम"
- "ज़रूरी" (necessary), not "जरूरी"
- "फ़र्क" (difference), not "फर्क"
- "ग़लत" (wrong), not "गलत"
Note: In many exam papers, the non-nukta form is still accepted, but the standardised form with Nukta is preferred in official dictionaries.

Rule 6: चन्द्रबिन्दु vs. अनुनासिक
Chandrabindu (ँ) is used when there is space above the letter (no matra above). Anunasik dot (ं) is used when a matra occupies the top space.
- "हँसना" (Chandrabindu — no upper matra)
- "सिंचाई" (Anusvar — matra "ी" occupies top, so dot used)
- "में" (Anusvar — matra "े" occupies top)

Rule 7: Distinction between similar-sounding letters
- ड vs. ड़: "ड" is retroflex stop; "ड़" is retroflex flap (occurs medially/finally). "बड़ा" (big), "कड़वा" (bitter).
- ढ vs. ढ़: "ढ" aspirated stop; "ढ़" aspirated flap. "बढ़ना" (to increase), "कढ़ाई" (embroidery).
- श vs. ष vs. स: "श" (palatal) in "शहर" (city); "ष" (retroflex) in "षट्कोण" (hexagon); "स" (dental) in "सूरज" (sun). Standard words follow etymology; memorise common ones.

5. Worked Examples (कार्य उदाहरण)

Each example shows a typical exam-style question, the correct answer, and the reasoning.

Example 1 — Choose the correctly spelt word.
Options:
(a) "अंगूर"
(b) "अङ्गूर"
(c) "अंगुर"
(d) "अन्गूर"

Answer: (a) "अंगूर"
Solution: Rule 1 — Anusvar "ं" replaces the class nasal (ङ) before a consonant of the same class (ग). Option (b) uses the full nasal letter, which is not standard in modern spelling. Option (c) misses the nasal entirely. Option (d) uses dental न which is wrong class.

Example 2 — Fill the correct matra: "क__ल" (meaning: tomorrow/yesterday — context decides).
Options:
(a) ा → "काल" (time/tomorrow/yesterday)
(b) ि → "किल" (not a standard word)
(c) ी → "कील" (nail)
(d) ु → "कुल" (family/total)

Answer: (a) ा → "काल"
Solution: The word "काल" (time) takes matra "ा" (आ). "कील" (nail) and "कुल" (family) are different words. The blank tests whether you recognise the common word "काल" with long आ.

Example 3 — Identify the word with correct Anunasik/Chandrabindu usage.
Options:
(a) "हंसना"
(b) "हँसना"
(c) "हंस्ना"
(d) "हंन्सना"

Answer: (b) "हँसना"
Solution: The vowel अ in "हँ" is nasalised; no consonant follows the nasalisation. Rule 2 requires Chandrabindu "ँ" because no upper matra is present. Option (a) misses nasalisation. Option (c) misplaces Halant. Option (d) doubles the nasal incorrectly.

Example 4 — Select the correctly spelt conjunct word.
Options:
(a) "शक्ती"
(b) "शक्ति"
(c) "शकती"
(d) "शक्ती"

Answer: (b) "शक्ति"
Solution: The conjunct "क्त" = क् + त. The first consonant क takes Halant, joining with त. Matra "ि" (इ) attaches to the whole conjunct, written before it: "शक्ति". Option (a) and (d) wrongly place "ी" (ई) after. Option (c) misses the conjunct, writing separate क and त.

Example 5 — Choose the word with correct Nukta usage (standard spelling).
Options:
(a) "जरूरी"
(b) "ज़रूरी"
(c) "जरुरी"
(d) "ज़रुरी"

Answer: (b) "ज़रूरी"
Solution: The Perso-Arabic sound /z/ requires Nukta under ज → "ज़". The vowel is ऊ (long), so matra "ू" is needed. Option (a) misses Nukta. Option (c) misses Nukta and shortens vowel. Option (d) has Nukta but shortens vowel.

Example 6 — Spot the error in the following word: "दुःखी"
Options:
(a) Visarg wrong before ख
(b) Matra "ी" wrong after Visarg
(c) No error
(d) Should be "दुखी"

Answer: (c) No error
Solution: "दुःखी" follows Rule 3: Visarg before ख (voiceless velar). The adjective suffix "ी" (feminine/long) attaches after the consonant ख, giving "दुःखी". Option (a) is false — Visarg is correct before ख. Option (b) is false — matra attaches to the consonant, not to Visarg. Option (d) "दुखी" drops Visarg, which is non-standard for the tatsama word.

6. Common Errors to Avoid (सामान्य त्रुटियाँ)

1. Writing full nasal letter instead of Anusvar: "सङ्ग" for "संग", "अङ्ग" for "अंग".
2. Confusing Chandrabindu and Anusvar: "हंसना" for "हँसना"; "में" written as "मँ" (wrong — matra "े" occupies top, so Anusvar "ं" is used).
3. Misplacing the "ि" matra: writing "क़िताब" as "क़तिब" or "क़ीतब". Remember: "ि" is written **before** the consonant/conjunct but pronounced after.
4. Omitting Nukta in standardised words: "क़लम" → "कलम", "ज़मीन" → "जमीन", "फ़ाइल" → "फाइल". Exams following the latest Hindi spelling standards expect the Nukta.
5. Wrong conjunct formation: "शक्ति" written as "शकित" or "शकती"; "विद्या" as "विदया"; "पुस्तक" as "पुस्तक" (correct) but "पुस्तक" misspelt as "पुसतक".
6. Interchanging श, ष, स: "शहर" written as "षहर" or "सहर"; "सूरज" as "शूरज"; "षट्कोण" as "सट्कोण". Learn the common words for each.
7. Interchanging ड/ड़ and ढ/ढ़: "बड़ा" written as "बडा"; "बढ़ना" as "बढना". The flap forms (ड़, ढ़) occur medially/finally after a vowel.
8. Dropping Visarg in tatsama words: "दुःख" → "दुख", "मनः" → "मन". In formal spelling, retain Visarg before क, ख, प, फ, श, ष, स, ह.
9. Writing "अं" (Anusvar) at word end where "अँ" (Anunasik) is needed: "साँप" → "सांप" (wrong nasalisation mark).
10. Confusing "ऋ" and "रि": "ऋषि" (sage) vs. "रिश्ता" (relation). The vowel "ऋ" has its own matra "ृ"; do not replace it with "रि".

7. Quick Revision Checklist

- [ ] 13 Swar, 33 Vyanjan, 11 Matras memorised.
- [ ] Position of "ि" (before consonant) understood.
- [ ] Anusvar vs. Anunasik (Chandrabindu) distinction clear.
- [ ] Visarg usage before voiceless consonants.
- [ ] Conjunct formation with Halant.
- [ ] Nukta letters: क़, ख़, ग़, ज़, फ़, ड़, ढ़.
- [ ] श / ष / स and ड / ड़ / ढ / ढ़ word lists revised.
- [ ] Six worked examples re-read; reasoning internalised.

Practice Tip
In the exam, first read the options aloud mentally. The wrong spelling often "sounds" wrong because the matra or nasal sign is off. For fill-in-the-matra questions, try each matra and see which forms a known dictionary word. For "spot the error" questions, check each sign (matra, Anusvar, Visarg, Nukta, Halant) against the rules above. Speed comes from pattern recognition — drill the 30–40 most common words that appear in previous papers (e.g., "अंगूर", "हँसना", "शक्ति", "ज़रूरी", "दुःख", "बड़ा", "बढ़ना", "शहर", "सूरज", "षट्कोण", "क़लम", "फ़र्क", "ग़लत", "साँप", "में", "मैं", "कील", "कुल", "काल", "पुस्तक", "विद्या", "निर्माण", "मनःस्थिति", "पुष्ट", "बुद्धि", "स्थान", "चंपा", "सिंचाई", "क़िताब", "ज़मीन", "फ़ाइल", "रिश्ता", "ऋषि").
