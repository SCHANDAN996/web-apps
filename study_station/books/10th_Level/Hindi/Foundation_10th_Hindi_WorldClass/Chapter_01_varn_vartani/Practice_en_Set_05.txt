101. Which of the following is a vowel (स्वर) in Hindi?
(a) क (b) अ (c) ख (d) ग
Answer: (b)
Solution: The options क, ख, and ग are all consonants; only अ is a vowel, so the answer is अ.

102. Identify the correct spelling of the Hindi word for "water".
(a) पानी (b) पनि (c) पानि (d) पाणी
Answer: (a)
Solution: The incorrect options miss the long vowel ई or the nasal anusvara; the correct spelling uses पा + नी with the matra ी and anusvara, so the answer is पानी.

103. Which matra represents the long "ई" sound?
(a) ु (b) ी (c) ि (d) ू
Answer: (b)
Solution: The matra ि is short इ, ु is short उ, and ू is long ऊ; only ी denotes the long ई sound, so the answer is ी.

114. What is the function of the halant (्) in Devanagari?

Answer: (b)
Solution: The halant does not mark vowel length, nasality, or aspiration; it removes the inherent अ from a consonant, so the answer is to suppress the inherent vowel of a consonant.

Solution: The combination involves a nasal before a voiced consonant, triggering nasal assimilation (अनुनासिक संधि); visarga, guna, and yan sandhi do not apply here, so the answer is अनुनासिक संधि.
