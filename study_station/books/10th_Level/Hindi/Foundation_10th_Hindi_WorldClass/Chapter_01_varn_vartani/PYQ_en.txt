PYQ Pattern Analysis — वर्ण और वर्तनी / Letters and Spelling

This chapter tests the ability to recognise correct Devanagari letter forms, matra placement, conjuncts (संयुक्त व्यंजन), anusvara/visarga usage, and standard spelling conventions followed in government examinations. Questions are short, objective, and require no composition — only identification of the correctly written word or the error in a given word.

Common Question Patterns

1. Choose the correctly spelt word
   Four options are given; three contain a spelling error (wrong matra, missing/extra nukta, wrong conjunct, confused anusvara/visarga, or homophone confusion). The candidate must pick the one that follows standard Hindi orthography.

   PYQ-style example:
   Which of the following words is correctly spelt?
   (a) "अधिकारी"
   (b) "अधिकरी"
   (c) "अधीकरी"
   (d) "अधिकारी"
   Answer: (a)
   Solution: Option (a) uses the correct matra (ई) and conjunct (क् + र = कृ). Option (b) drops the matra, (c) uses wrong vowel (ई → ी), (d) duplicates (a) but is a distractor.
   Source: PYQ-style

2. Identify the misspelt word
   A sentence or list is given; one word is incorrectly written. The error is typically a single matra/conjunct/nukta mistake.

   PYQ-style example:
   Find the word with a spelling error:
   "सरकार ने नई योजना शुरू की।"
   (a) "सरकार"
   (b) "नई"
   (c) "योजना"
   (d) "शुरू"
   Answer: (d)
   Solution: Correct spelling is "शुरू" (with उ matra); "शुरू" written as "शुरु" (missing matra) is a common error.
   Source: PYQ-style

3. Correct the underlined/highlighted word
   A word in a sentence is underlined. Four alternatives (including the original) are offered; choose the correct spelling.

   PYQ-style example:
   Correct the spelling of the underlined word:
   "वह विद्यालय जाता है।"
   (a) "विद्यालय"
   (b) "विद्यालय"
   (c) "विद्यालय"
   (d) "विद्यालय"
   Answer: (a)
   Solution: The conjunct द् + य = द्य is correctly formed in (a). Other options show common distortions (missing virama, wrong matra).
   Source: PYQ-style

4. Nukta (नुक़्ता) differentiation
   Words that differ only by the presence/absence of nukta (e.g., क/क़, ख/ख़, ग/ग़, ज/ज़, ड/ड़, ढ/ढ़, फ/फ़) are tested. Only the form prescribed in standard Hindi dictionaries is accepted.

   PYQ-style example:
   Select the correctly spelt word:
   (a) "क़ानून"
   (b) "कानून"
   (c) "क़ानुन"
   (d) "कानुन"
   Answer: (a)
   Solution: The Perso-Arabic loanword requires nukta on क → क़. Option (b) omits nukta, (c) and (d) also miss the long ऊ matra.
   Source: PYQ-style

5. Anusvara (ं) vs. Visarga (ः) vs. Chandrabindu (ँ)
   Confusion between nasalisation (अनुस्वार/चन्द्रबिन्दु) and visarga is frequent. The rule: anusvara before a consonant; chandrabindu over a vowel when nasalised; visarga for the aspirated release.

   PYQ-style example:
   Which word uses the correct nasal sign?
   (a) "हँसना"
   (b) "हंसना"
   (c) "हःसना"
   (d) "हंसना"
   Answer: (a)
   Solution: "हँसना" (laugh) takes chandrabindu because the vowel अ is nasalised. "हंसना" (swan) uses anusvara before a conjunct (स् + न). Visarga in (c) is wrong.
   Source: PYQ-style

6. Homophone / near-homophone confusion
   Pairs like "अर्थ/अर्त", "कर्म/करम", "धर्म/धरम", "शुद्ध/शुध्द", "प्रभाव/प्रभाब" appear. Only the standard spelling is correct.

   PYQ-style example:
   Choose the correctly spelt word:
   (a) "प्रभाव"
   (b) "प्रभाब"
   (c) "प्रभाव"
   (d) "प्रभाब"
   Answer: (a)
   Solution: The conjunct भ् + व = भ्व is standard; ब is not used for the v sound.
   Source: PYQ-style

7. Conjunct formation (संयुक्त व्यंजन)
   Errors in half-forms (हलन्त) and ligatures: त् + र = त्र, द् + य = द्य, क् + ष = क्ष, ज् + ञ = ज्ञ, etc. Missing virama or wrong ligature is the trap.

   PYQ-style example:
   Identify the correct conjunct spelling:
   (a) "विद्या"
   (b) "विदया"
   (c) "विद्या"
   (d) "विद्या"
   Answer: (a)
   Solution: द् + य = द्य is the correct ligature. Option (b) writes the two consonants separately, (c) and (d) show malformed ligatures.
   Source: PYQ-style

8. Matra placement (मात्रा) — short vs. long vowels
   Confusion between इ/ई, उ/ऊ, ए/ऐ, ओ/औ. The error changes meaning or creates a non-word.

   PYQ-style example:
   Which word has the correct matra?
   (a) "सीता"
   (b) "सीता"
   (c) "सीता"
   (d) "सीता"
   Answer: (a)
   Solution: Long ई matra is required. Options (b)-(d) show short इ, misplaced matra, or duplicate distractors.
   Source: PYQ-style

Examiner Traps to Watch

- Near-correct distractors: three options look almost identical; only one matra/conjunct/nukta differs.
- Visual similarity: letters like ब/व, द/ध, ट/ठ, ड/ढ, स/श/ष are swapped.
- Silent virama: writing full consonant instead of half-form (e.g., "विदया" for "विद्या").
- Over-correction: adding nukta where not needed (e.g., "क़िताब" for "किताब") or removing required nukta.
- Anusvara/visarga swap in sandhi contexts (e.g., "संस्कृत" vs. "संस्कृतः" in isolation).
- Homophone traps: "करना/करणा", "भरना/भरणा", "चलना/चलना" (same spelling, different meaning — not tested here; only spelling errors are tested).

Preparation Tips

- Memorise the standard spelling of 200–300 high-frequency words used in government notifications (NCERT Class 6–10 Hindi textbooks are the baseline).
- Practise writing conjuncts with virama: क्, ख्, ग्, घ्, च्, छ्, ज्, झ्, ट्, ठ्, ड्, ढ्, त्, थ्, द्, ध्, प्, फ्, ब्, भ्, म्, य्, र्, ल्, व्, श्, ष्, स्, ह्.
- Learn the nukta list: क़, ख़, ग़, ज़, ड़, ढ़, फ़ — and words where they are mandatory vs. prohibited.
- Revise anusvara/visarga/chandrabindu rules with examples: "हँसना", "हंसना", "अतः", "मँहगा", "संगीत".
- Solve 50–100 PYQ-style MCQs from this chapter; analyse every wrong option to internalise the error type.

Source for concepts: NCERT Class 6–10 Hindi (Vasant, Durva, Kritika, Kshitij, Sparsh, Sanchayan) and standard Hindi orthography (Central Hindi Directorate).
