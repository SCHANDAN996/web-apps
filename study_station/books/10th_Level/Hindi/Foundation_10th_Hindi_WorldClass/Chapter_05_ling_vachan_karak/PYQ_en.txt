**PYQ Pattern Analysis — Gender, Number & Case (लिंग, वचन, कारक)**

This section describes the *types* of questions that repeatedly appear in SSC GD and similar 10th-level exams. No year-wise counts or invented statistics are given — only the patterns examiners favour.

---

### 1. Spot the Wrong Gender (लिंग पहचान)

**Pattern**  
A sentence contains one noun whose gender is incorrectly used. You must pick the word that carries the error.

**Common Traps**  
- Nouns ending in **आ** that are feminine (e.g., "दवा", "लता", "माला")  
- Nouns ending in **ई** that are masculine (e.g., "पानी", "हाथी", "नौकर")  
- Tatsama words retaining Sanskrit gender (e.g., "ज्ञान" masculine, "बुद्धि" feminine)  
- Perso-Arabic loans where gender is fixed by usage (e.g., "किताब" feminine, "इल्म" masculine)

**PYQ-style Example**  
> **Question:** Which word in the following sentence has a gender error?  
> "राम ने **दवा** खाया और **पानी** पीया।"  
> (a) राम  
> (b) **दवा**  
> (c) **पानी**  
> (d) खाया  
> **Answer:** (b)  
> **Solution:** "दवा" is feminine; the verb must be "खाई". "पानी" is masculine — "पीया" is correct.  
> **Source:** PYQ-style

### 2. Singular–Plural Mismatch (वचन अशुद्धि)

**Pattern**  
A sentence uses a singular noun with a plural verb (or vice versa), or the plural form of the noun itself is wrong.

**Common Traps**  
- Nouns ending in **आ** → plural in **ए** ("लड़का → लड़के") but some in **आँ** ("पिता → पिताजी/पितागण")  
- Nouns ending in **इ/ई** → plural in **इयाँ/इयाँ** ("नदी → नदियाँ", "देवी → देवियाँ")  
- Nouns ending in **उ/ऊ** → plural in **एँ/ओं** ("गुरु → गुरुएँ/गुरुजन")  
- Unmarked masculines ("घर", "काम") — plural often same form or with **ओं** ("घर → घर/घरों")  
- Feminines in **आ** → plural in **एँ** ("भाषा → भाषाएँ")  
- English loans — usually take **स** or unchanged ("बस → बसें", "ट्रक → ट्रक")

**PYQ-style Example**  
> **Question:** Identify the sentence with a number error.  
> (a) "लड़के खेल रहे हैं।"  
> (b) "नदियाँ बह रही हैं।"  
> (c) "गुरुजन पढ़ा रहे हैं।"  
> (d) "भाषाएँ सीखना आसान है।"  
> **Answer:** (d)  
> **Solution:** "भाषाएँ" is plural; the predicate should be "आसान हैं" or rephrase to singular "भाषा सीखना आसान है।"  
> **Source:** PYQ-style

### 3. Case-Ending (कारक चिह्न) Errors

**Pattern**  
A postposition (कारक चिह्न) is missing, wrong, or redundant. Examiners test the six main कारक: कर्ता (नॉमिनेटिव), कर्म (अक्यूजेटिव), करण (इंस्ट्रूमेंटल), संप्रदान (डेटिव), अपादान (एब्लेटिव), संबंध (जेनिटिव), अधिकरण (लोकेटिव).

**Common Traps**  
- **ने** only with transitive verbs in perfective past ("राम ने खाना खाया") — never with intransitive ("राम सोया", not "राम ने सोया")  
- **को** for definite animate direct object ("मैंने **कुत्ते को** देखा") — omitted for indefinite/inanimate ("मैंने **किताब** पढ़ी")  
- **से** for instrument/agent ("चाकू **से** काटो", "पुलिस **से** डरो") — not **द्वारा** in simple sentences  
- **को** for dative ("राम **को** दो") — not **के लिए** unless purpose  
- **का/के/की** agreement with possessed noun, not possessor ("राम **का** घर", "सीता **के** भाई")  
- **में/पर** for location — **में** = inside, **पर** = on/at

**PYQ-style Example**  
> **Question:** Choose the sentence with a correct case marker.  
> (a) "राम **ने** सोया।"  
> (b) "मैंने **किताब को** पढ़ा।"  
> (c) "वह चाकू **से** काटता है।"  
> (d) "सीता **के लिए** किताब दी।"  
> **Answer:** (c)  
> **Solution:** (a) "ने" cannot be used with intransitive "सोया". (b) "किताब" is inanimate — "को" not needed. (d) Dative requires "को", not "के लिए".  
> **Source:** PYQ-style

### 4. Agreement Chain (संज्ञा–विशेषण–क्रिया अनुबंध)

**Pattern**  
A long noun phrase contains adjective(s) and a verb; all must agree in gender and number with the head noun.

**Common Traps**  
- Adjective in **आ** modifies masculine singular ("अच्छा लड़का") → plural **ए** ("अच्छे लड़के")  
- Adjective in **ई** modifies feminine ("अच्छी लड़की") → plural **इयाँ** ("अच्छी लड़कियाँ")  
- Verb agrees with subject in gender/number only in past perfective (with **ने**); otherwise with subject in person/number

**PYQ-style Example**  
> **Question:** Fill the blank with the correct form.  
> "दोनों **अच्छे** लड़के दौड़ ______।"  
> (a) रहा  
> (b) रहे  
> (c) रही  
> (d) रहीं  
> **Answer:** (b)  
> **Solution:** Subject "लड़के" is masculine plural → verb "दौड़ रहे हैं" (present continuous) or simple past "दौड़े". Option (b) "रहे" fits present continuous.  
> **Source:** PYQ-style

### 5. Pronoun–Antecedent Agreement (सर्वनाम–पूर्ववर्ती अनुबंध)

**Pattern**  
A pronoun (वह, यह, कौन, जिस, अपना, आदि) must match its antecedent in gender, number, and person.

**Common Traps**  
- "अपना" reflects the subject's gender/number ("राम **अपना** काम करता है", "सीता **अपना** काम करती है")  
- "जिस/जो" agree with antecedent ("जो लड़का आया **वह** मेरा भाई है")  
- Honorific plural for elders ("पिताजी **आए**", not "आया")

**PYQ-style Example**  
> **Question:** Spot the agreement error.  
> "सीता ने **अपना** किताब पढ़ी।"  
> (a) सीता ने  
> (b) **अपना**  
> (c) किताब  
> (d) पढ़ी  
> **Answer:** (b)  
> **Solution:** "सीता" is feminine → "अपनी किताब".  
> **Source:** PYQ-style

### 6. Compound/Conjoined Subjects (संयुक्त विषय)

**Pattern**  
Two nouns joined by "और" or "या" — verb agreement rules.

**Common Traps**  
- "और" → plural verb ("राम और श्याम **आए**")  
- "या" → verb agrees with nearest noun ("राम या श्याम **आएगा**")  
- With "के साथ" / "सहित" → verb agrees with first noun ("राम **सहित** श्याम **आया**")

**PYQ-style Example**  
> **Question:** Correct verb form: "राम **तथा** श्याम बाजार ______।"  
> (a) गया  
> (b) गए  
> (c) जाती  
> (d) जाते  
> **Answer:** (b)  
> **Solution:** "तथा" = "और" → compound subject → masculine plural "गए".  
> **Source:** PYQ-style

### 7. Oblique Form Before Postpositions (रूप परिवर्तन)

**Pattern**  
Nouns/adjectives change to oblique form before any postposition (ने, को, से, का, में, पर, आदि).

**Common Traps**  
- Masc. sing. **आ** → **ए** ("लड़का → लड़के ने")  
- Masc. pl. **ए** → **ओं** ("लड़के → लड़कों ने")  
- Fem. sing. **ई** → **इ** ("लड़की → लड़की ने" — no change) but **आ** → **ए** ("भाषा → भाषा में")  
- Fem. pl. **इयाँ/एँ** → **इयों/ओं** ("लड़कियाँ → लड़कियों ने", "भाषाएँ → भाषाओं में")

**PYQ-style Example**  
> **Question:** Choose the correct oblique form.  
> "________ ने दरवाजा खोला।"  
> (a) लड़का  
> (b) लड़के  
> (c) लड़की  
> (d) लड़कियाँ  
> **Answer:** (b)  
> **Solution:** "ने" requires oblique. Masc. sing. "लड़का" → oblique "लड़के".  
> **Source:** PYQ-style

### 8. Traps Examiners Frequently Use

| Trap | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Near-match distractor** | Two options differ only by one matra (e.g., "खाया/खाई", "गया/गए") — tests attention to gender/number. |
| **Transitivity flip** | Same verb used transitively and intransitively in adjacent questions ("राम ने दरवाजा खोला" vs "दरवाजा खुला"). |
| **Honorific confusion** | "आप **हैं**" (plural verb) vs "तुम **हो**" — mixing honorifics with wrong verb. |
| **Case stacking** | "राम **के द्वारा** से" — redundant double marking. |
| **English calque** | "मैं **को** पता है" (wrong) vs "मुझे पता है" — literal translation of "I know". |
| **Frozen forms** | "घर **को** जाओ" (colloquial) vs standard "घर **जाओ**" — examiners expect standard grammar. |

---

### 9. Solving Strategy (15-Second Rule)

1. **Identify the head noun** — gender, number, case.  
2. **Check verb agreement** — tense, transitivity, perfective → **ने**?  
3. **Scan every postposition** — is the preceding word in oblique form?  
4. **Verify pronoun/adjective chain** — all match head noun?  
5. **Eliminate** options with matra mismatches first; they are the quickest wins.

(d) **खाया**  
**Answer:** (d)  
**Solution:** "सीता" feminine → "खाई". "ने" correct (transitive perfective).  
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Question 4 (final)**  
Spot the error: "लड़के **ने** किताब **पढ़ी**।"

(b) **ने**  
(c) किताब

If singular intended → "लड़के ने" (oblique sing. "लड़के" + ने) but verb "पढ़ी" is feminine — "किताब" fem. sing. → "पढ़ी" correct. So error is "लड़के ने" should be "लड़के ने" for singular boy? Actually "लड़का" oblique sing. = "लड़के". So "लड़के ने किताब पढ़ी" = "The boy read the book" — correct. But "लड़के" can also be direct plural. Ambiguous. Better avoid.

**Question 4 (clean)**  
Choose the correct sentence:

**Solution:** "लगना" (to feel) takes dative subject → "राम को".
