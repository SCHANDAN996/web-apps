101. Identify the gender of the noun "लड़का".
(a) नपुंसकलिंग (b) स्त्रीलिंग (c) द्विलिंग (d) पुल्लिंग
Answer: (d)
Solution: The noun "लड़का" denotes a male person, so it belongs to masculine gender (पुल्लिंग). The tempting option स्त्रीलिंग would be for female nouns like "लड़की", so the answer is पुल्लिंग.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Hindi Grammar

102. Identify the gender of the noun "किताब".
(a) नपुंसकलिंग (b) पुल्लिंग (c) स्त्रीलिंग (d) द्विलिंग
Answer: (c)
Solution: The noun "किताब" (book) is treated as feminine in Hindi grammar (स्त्रीलिंग). The tempting option पुल्लिंग is a common trap for learners who assume inanimate objects are masculine, so the answer is स्त्रीलिंग.

103. What is the plural (बहुवचन) form of "बच्चा"?
(a) बच्चा (b) बच्चों (c) बच्चे (d) बच्ची

Solution: The singular "बच्चा" becomes "बच्चे" in direct plural (बहुवचन). The option "बच्चों" is the oblique plural form used with postpositions, not the direct plural, so the answer is बच्चे.

104. What is the plural (बहुवचन) form of "लड़की"?

105. Which case (कारक) is used in the sentence "राम ने किताब पढ़ी" for "राम"?
(a) कर्म कारक (b) संप्रदान कारक (c) करण कारक (d) कर्ता कारक
Answer: (d)
Solution: In "राम ने किताब पढ़ी", "राम" is the doer of the action with the postposition "ने", marking कर्ता कारक (ergative case). The option कर्म कारक would be for the object "किताब", so the answer is कर्ता कारक.

Answer: (d)
Solution: "किताब" is the object receiving the action, hence कर्म कारक (accusative case). The option कर्ता कारक is the trap for those confusing subject and object, so the answer is कर्म कारक.

124. Which of the following sentences incorrectly uses the ergative marker "ने"?
(a) मोहन ने दौड़ा (b) सीता ने गाना गाया (c) राम ने खाना खाया (d) लड़कों ने पत्र लिखा
Answer: (a)
Solution: The ergative "ने" is used only with transitive verbs in perfective past. "दौड़ा" (ran) is intransitive, so "मोहन ने दौड़ा" is incorrect; it should be "मोहन दौड़ा". The other sentences have transitive verbs (खाया, गाया, लिखा) and correctly use "ने", so the answer is मोहन ने दौड़ा.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Hindi Grammar
