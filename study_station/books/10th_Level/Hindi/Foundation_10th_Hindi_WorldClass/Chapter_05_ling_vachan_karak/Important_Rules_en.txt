**Important Rules: Gender, Number & Case (लिंग, वचन और कारक)**  
*For SSC GD / MTS / RRB Group D / State Constable — 10th Level*

---

### Rule 1 — Two Genders in Hindi (लिंग के दो मुख्य रूप)
Hindi nouns have **two grammatical genders**:  
- **पुल्लिंग (Masculine)** — e.g. "लड़का", "घर", "पेड़"  
- **स्त्रीलिंग (Feminine)** — e.g. "लड़की", "किताब", "नदी"  

There is no separate neuter gender; inanimate objects are arbitrarily assigned masculine or feminine.  
*Exception:* A few words like "बच्चा" (child) can be used for both genders; context decides.

---

### Rule 2 — Common Masculine Endings (पुल्लिंग के सामान्य अंत)
Nouns ending in **आ (ā)** are usually masculine:  
"लड़का", "कमरा", "दरवाजा", "पत्थर", "बाजार".  
*Exceptions:* "माता", "पिता" (irregular), "दवा" (feminine), "सजा" (feminine).

---

### Rule 3 — Common Feminine Endings (स्त्रीलिंग के सामान्य अंत)
Nouns ending in **ई (ī)** or **इ (i)** are usually feminine:  
"लड़की", "सखी", "नदी", "भाषा", "कुर्सी", "मेज".  
*Exceptions:* "पानी", "हाथी", "सुनार", "माली" are masculine.

---

### Rule 4 — Gender Pairs by Vowel Change (वowel परिवर्तन से लिंग बदलना)
Many masculine–feminine pairs differ only in the final vowel:  

| Masculine (पुल्लिंग) | Feminine (स्त्रीलिंग) |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| "लड़का"              | "लड़की"              |
| "चाचा"               | "चाची"               |
| "दादा"               | "दादी"               |
| "नाना"               | "नानी"               |
| "घोड़ा"              | "घोड़ी"              |
| "बंदर"               | "बंदरिया"            |

---

### Rule 5 — Gender Pairs by Suffix Addition (प्रत्यय जोड़कर लिंग बदलना)
Some feminines are formed by adding **इन/नी/आनी** to the masculine:  

| Masculine | Feminine |
|-----------|----------|
| "शेर"     | "शेरनी" |
| "बाघ"     | "बाघिन" |
| "राजा"    | "रानी"  |
| "पति"     | "पत्नी" |
| "देव"     | "देवी"  |
| "गायक"   | "गायिका" |

---

### Rule 6 — Completely Different Words (पूरी तरह अलग शब्द)
Some gender pairs share no phonetic similarity:  

| Masculine | Feminine |
|-----------|----------|
| "पिता"    | "माता"   |
| "भाई"    | "बहन"   |
| "पति"     | "पत्नी" |
| "बेटा"    | "बेटी"  |
| "ससुर"    | "सास"   |
| "देवर"    | "देवरानी" |

Memorise these as vocabulary.

---

### Rule 7 — Same Form, Gender Decided by Context (एक रूप, लिंग संदर्भ से)
Some nouns have identical form; gender shows in verb/adjective agreement:  
- "डॉक्टर आया" (masculine) vs "डॉक्टर आई" (feminine)  
- "अध्यापक पढ़ा रहा है" vs "अध्यापक पढ़ा रही है"  
- "मित्र अच्छा है" vs "मित्र अच्छी है"

---

### Rule 8 — Two Numbers: Singular & Plural (दो वचन: एकवचन और बहुवचन)
Hindi has **एकवचन (Singular)** and **बहुवचन (Plural)**. Dual (द्विवचन) exists only in Sanskrit.  

**Plural formation patterns:**  
- Masculine **आ-ending** → **ए (e)**: "लड़का" → "लड़के", "कमरा" → "कमरे"  
- Masculine **consonant-ending** → no change or **ओं (oṃ)**: "घर" → "घर/घरों", "पेड़" → "पेड़/पेड़ों"  
- Feminine **ई/इ-ending** → **इयाँ (iyāṃ)**: "लड़की" → "लड़कियाँ", "नदी" → "नदियाँ"  
- Feminine **consonant-ending** → **एँ (eṃ)**: "किताब" → "किताबें", "मेज" → "मेजें"

---

### Rule 9 — Oblique Form Before Postpositions (कारक चिह्न से पहले तिर्यक रूप)
When a noun is followed by a postposition (ने, को, से, का, में, पर, आदि), it takes the **oblique (तिर्यक) form**:  

| Direct (प्रत्यक्ष) | Oblique (तिर्यक) | Example with "ने" |
|-------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| "लड़का"          | "लड़के"         | "लड़के ने"       |
| "लड़की"          | "लड़की"         | "लड़की ने"       |
| "घर"              | "घर"             | "घर में"          |
| "किताब"          | "किताब"         | "किताब पर"       |

Plural oblique adds **ओं (oṃ)** to masculine, **इयों (iyoṃ)** to feminine:  
"लड़कों ने", "लड़कियों ने", "घरों में", "किताबों में".

---

### Rule 10 — Eight Kārakas (आठ कारक)
The relation of a noun/pronoun to the verb is shown by **कृदन्त/कारक चिह्न (postpositions)**. Eight kārakas:

| कारक | चिह्न (Postposition) | English Name | Question it Answers |
|-------|----------------------|--------------|---------------------|
| कर्ता (Kartā) | ने (ne) — only in perfective transitive | Agent / Subject | Who did it? |
| कर्म (Karma) | को (ko) — definite animate object | Object | Whom/What? |
| करण (Karaṇ) | से (se) | Instrument | By what means? |
| संप्रदान (Sampradān) | को (ko) | Recipient / Dative | To/For whom? |
| अपादान (Apādān) | से (se) | Source / Ablative | From whom/where? |
| संबंध (Sambandh) | का/के/की (kā/ke/kī) | Possessor / Genitive | Whose? |
| अधिकरण (Adhikaraṇ) | में (mẽ), पर (par) | Locative | Where/On what? |
| संबोधन (Sambodhan) | हे, अरे, ओ (no postposition) | Vocative | Calling/Addressing |

---

### Rule 11 — "ने" Only with Perfective Transitive Verbs (ने का प्रयोग)
The postposition **ने** marks the agent **only** when:  
1. Verb is **transitive** (takes an object)  
2. Tense/aspect is **perfective** (perfect, pluperfect, future perfect)  

Examples:  
- "लड़के **ने** किताब **पढ़ी**" (boy read the book — perfective)  
- "लड़का किताब **पढ़ता है**" (boy reads the book — imperfective, no ने)  
- "लड़के **ने** खाना **खाया**" (ate — perfective)  
- "लड़का खाना **खाता है**" (eats — imperfective, no ने)

*Common mistake:* Using ने with intransitive verbs ("लड़के ने सोया" ❌ → "लड़का सोया" ✅) or imperfective transitives.

---

### Rule 12 — "को" for Definite Animate Object & Recipient (को का दोहरा प्रयोग)
**को** marks two different kārakas:  
- **कर्म (Object)** — definite, animate/human: "मैंने **लड़के को** देखा" (I saw the boy)  
- **संप्रदान (Recipient)** — to/for whom: "मैंने **लड़के को** किताब दी" (I gave the book to the boy)  

Inanimate/indefinite objects usually take **no postposition**: "मैंने **किताब** पढ़ी" (not "किताब को").

---

### Rule 13 — "से" for Instrument, Source & Comparison (से के विविध अर्थ)
**से** expresses three kārakas:  
- **करण (Instrument)**: "लड़के ने कलम **से** लिखा" (wrote with a pen)  
- **अपादान (Source)**: "वह दिल्ली **से** आया" (came from Delhi)  
- **Comparison**: "राम श्याम **से** लंबा है" (Ram is taller than Shyam)  

Context decides which kāraka is intended.

---

### Rule 14 — Genitive Agreement (संबंध कारक में अनुबंध)
The genitive postposition **का/के/की** agrees with the **possessed noun** (not the possessor):  

| Possessor | Possessed (Masculine Singular) | Possessed (Masculine Plural/Oblique) | Possessed (Feminine) |
|-----------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------|
| "लड़का"  | "लड़के **का** घर"             | "लड़के **के** घर"                  | "लड़के **की** किताब" |
| "लड़की"  | "लड़की **का** घर"             | "लड़की **के** घर"                  | "लड़की **की** किताब" |

*Common mistake:* "लड़की का घर" (correct) vs "लड़की के घर" (wrong if possessed noun is masculine singular direct).

---

### Rule 15 — Vocative Case (संबोधन कारक) — No Postposition
The vocative uses the **oblique form** (singular) or **plural oblique** directly, often with interjections "हे", "अरे", "ओ":  
- "हे **लड़के**!" (O boy!) — singular oblique  
- "अरे **लड़कों**!" (Hey boys!) — plural oblique  
- "**मित्र**!" (Friend!) — consonant-ending masculine, same as direct  

No postposition (ने, को, से, का, में) is ever used with संबोधन.

### Common Exam Traps (परीक्षा में आम गलतियाँ)

1. **ने with intransitive verb** — "पक्षी ने उड़ा" ❌ → "पक्षी उड़ा" ✅  
2. **ने in imperfective** — "लड़के ने पढ़ता है" ❌ → "लड़का पढ़ता है" ✅  
3. **को with inanimate object** — "मैंने मेज को देखा" ❌ (unless personified) → "मैंने मेज देखी" ✅  
4. **Genitive disagreement** — "लड़की के घर" (for masculine singular "घर") ❌ → "लड़की का घर" ✅  
5. **Wrong plural** — "लड़कियों" (oblique) used as direct subject ❌ → "लड़कियाँ" ✅  
6. **से vs को confusion** — "मैंने उससे किताब ली" (source) vs "मैंने उसे किताब दी" (recipient) — both use different postpositions in Hindi but English "from/to" clarifies.
