126. Identify the gender of the noun "किताब".
(a) पुल्लिंग (b) स्त्रीलिंग (c) नपुंसकलिंग (d) द्विलिंग
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nouns ending in आ are often masculine, but "किताब" is a common feminine noun despite its ending; the trap is the आ ending, so the correct gender is स्त्रीलिंग.

127. What is the plural form of "लड़का"?
(a) लड़कों (b) लड़कियाँ (c) लड़के (d) लड़की
Answer: (c)
Solution: The direct plural of masculine आ-ending nouns changes आ to ए, giving "लड़के"; the tempting wrong option "लड़कों" is the oblique plural used with postpositions, so the correct plural form is लड़के.

(a) संप्रदान (b) कर्ता (c) करण (d) कर्म
Answer: (b)
Solution: Because the verb is transitive and in perfective aspect, the subject takes ने and is कर्ता कारक; the trap is to mistake it for कर्म due to the object "आम", so the correct case is कर्ता.
