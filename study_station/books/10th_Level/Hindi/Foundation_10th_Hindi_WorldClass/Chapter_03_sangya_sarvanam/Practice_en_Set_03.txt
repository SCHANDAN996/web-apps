51. Identify the noun in the sentence: "राम पढ़ता है।"
(a) राम (b) पढ़ता (c) है (d) कोई नहीं
Answer: (a)
Solution: The word "राम" is a proper noun denoting a person, while "पढ़ता" is a verb and "है" is an auxiliary verb. The tempting wrong option is "पढ़ता" because it appears near the subject, but it functions as the predicate. So the correct answer is राम.
