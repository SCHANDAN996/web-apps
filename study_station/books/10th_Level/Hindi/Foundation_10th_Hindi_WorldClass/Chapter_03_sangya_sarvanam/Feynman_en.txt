# Nouns and Pronouns — Feynman-Style Explanation

Think of a **noun (संज्ञा)** as a name-tag. Every person, place, thing, idea or feeling wears one.  
Think of a **pronoun (सर्वनाम)** as a reusable sticky note that replaces the name-tag so you don't have to keep saying the same name over and over.

---

## 1. Noun (संज्ञा) — The Name-Tags

| Type | Hindi Term | Everyday Analogy | Examples (keep exactly as shown) |
|------|------------|------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Proper | व्यक्तिवाचक | Aadhaar card — unique to one person/place | "राम", "दिल्ली", "गंगा" |
| Common | जातिवाचक | Species label — fits many members | "लड़का", "नदी", "पुस्तक" |
| Collective | समूहवाचक | A bouquet — one word for a group | "भीड़", "सेना", "झुंड" |
| Abstract | भाववाचक | A feeling you can't touch | "खुशी", "बहादुरी", "ईमानदारी" |
| Material | द्रव्यवाचक | Raw substance | "सोना", "लोहा", "पानी" |

**Quick test:** Can you put "एक" (one) or "दो" (two) before it? If yes → countable (common/collective/material). If no → usually abstract or proper.

---

## 2. Pronoun (सर्वनाम) — The Sticky Notes

| Type | Hindi Term | When to Use | Examples (keep exactly as shown) |
|------|------------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| Personal | पुरुषवाचक | Pointing to 1st/2nd/3rd person | "मैं", "हम", "तुम", "आप", "वह", "वे" |
| Demonstrative | निश्चयवाचक | Pointing at distance | "यह", "ये", "वह", "वे" |
| Relative | संबंधवाचक | Joining two clauses | "जो", "जिस", "जिन" |
| Interrogative | प्रश्नवाचक | Asking questions | "कौन", "क्या", "किस" |
| Indefinite | अनिश्चयवाचक | Vague quantity | "कोई", "कुछ", "कई" |
| Reflexive | निजवाचक | Action reflects back | "स्वयं", "अपना" |

**Rule of thumb:** A pronoun *always* borrows its gender, number and case from the noun it replaces (its **antecedent**).

---

## 3. Gender (लिंग) & Number (वचन) — The Matching Game

- **Masculine (पुल्लिंग):** "राम", "लड़का", "वह"  
- **Feminine (स्त्रीलिंग):** "सीता", "लड़की", "वह"  
- **Singular (एकवचन):** "लड़का", "वह"  
- **Plural (बहुवचन):** "लड़के", "वे"

**Common trap:** "वह" looks the same for singular masculine/feminine and for honorific plural. Context decides.

---

## 4. Case (कारक) — The Role in the Sentence

| Case | Hindi | Question it Answers | Typical Postposition |
|------|-------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Nominative | कर्ता | Who did it? | — |
| Accusative | कर्म | Whom/what affected? | "को" |
| Instrumental | करण | With what/by what? | "से" |
| Dative | संप्रदान | To/for whom? | "को", "के लिए" |
| Ablative | अपादान | From where? | "से" |
| Genitive | संबंध | Whose? | "का/के/की" |
| Locative | अधिकरण | Where/when? | "में", "पर" |

**Example:** "राम ने पुस्तक पढ़ी" → "राम" (कर्ता), "पुस्तक" (कर्म), "ने" marks instrumental agent in past transitive.

---

## 5. Five Recall Questions

**Q1.** Identify the noun type in: "गंगा पवित्र नदी है।"  
**Answer:** "गंगा" — व्यक्तिवाचक (proper); "नदी" — जातिवाचक (common).  
**Why:** "गंगा" names one specific river; "नदी" is the general class.

**Q2.** Replace the noun with the correct pronoun: "सीता पढ़ती है। ___ होशियार है।"  
**Answer:** "वह"  
**Why:** "सीता" is 3rd person, singular, feminine → "वह" matches gender/number.

**Q3.** Pick the demonstrative pronoun: "___ किताब मेरी है।"  
Options: (a) "कौन" (b) "यह" (c) "जो" (d) "कोई"  
**Answer:** (b) "यह"  
**Why:** "यह" points to a specific nearby object; others are interrogative, relative, indefinite.

**Q4.** Find the case of "राम" in: "राम को किताब दो।"  
**Answer:** संप्रदान (Dative) — answers "to whom?" marked by "को".

**Q5.** Spot the error: "लड़कियाँ स्कूल जाती है।"  
**Correction:** "लड़कियाँ स्कूल जाती **हैं**।"  
**Why:** Plural subject "लड़कियाँ" demands plural verb "हैं", not singular "है".

---

## 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Mixing honorifics:** Using "तुम" with elders — use "आप".  
2. **Gender mismatch:** "सीता आया" → should be "सीता **आई**".  
3. **Case omission:** "राम पुस्तक पढ़ा" (missing "ने" in past transitive) → "राम **ने** पुस्तक पढ़ी".  
4. **Pronoun without antecedent:** Starting a paragraph with "वह गया" — reader doesn't know who "वह" is.  
5. **Overusing "यह/वह" for people:** Prefer "ये/वे" for plural humans; "यह/वह" can sound distant.

---

## 7. One-Minute Mental Checklist

- Noun identified? → Tag its type.  
- Pronoun used? → Trace back to its noun; verify gender/number/case match.  
- Verb agrees with subject in number & gender?  
- Postpositions (कारक चिह्न) present where required?  

Master these patterns and every "spotting error", "fill in the blank" and "sentence improvement" question on nouns/pronouns becomes a simple matching exercise.
