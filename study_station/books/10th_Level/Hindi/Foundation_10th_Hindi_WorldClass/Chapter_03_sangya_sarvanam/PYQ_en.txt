PYQ Pattern Analysis — Nouns and Pronouns (संज्ञा और सर्वनाम)

This section describes the typical ways SSC GD (and similar 10th-level exams) test the concepts covered in this chapter. It contains no statistics, no year-wise tables, and no invented claims. Every illustrative item is labelled PYQ-style.

1. Spot-the-Error on Noun Number (वचन)
   Pattern: A sentence contains a noun whose singular/plural form does not match the verb or the quantifier.
   Common traps:
   - Nouns that look plural but are singular (e.g., "समाचार", "भौतिकी", "मैथ्स") — verb must be singular.
   - Nouns that are always plural (e.g., "कैंची", "चश्मा", "धन्यवाद") — verb must be plural.
   - Collective nouns (e.g., "सेना", "भीड़", "समिति") — usually singular verb unless the members act individually.
   PYQ-style example:
   "समाचार अच्छे हैं।" → Error: "हैं" should be "है" because "समाचार" is singular.

2. Spot-the-Error on Noun Gender (लिंग)
   Pattern: Adjective or verb ending does not agree with the gender of the noun.
   Common traps:
   - Nouns ending in "आ" that are feminine (e.g., "माता", "दादी", "नानी", "चाची").
   - Nouns ending in "ई" that are masculine (e.g., "पुत्री" is feminine, but "हाथी", "सेठी", "मुखिया" are masculine).
   - Persian/Arabic origin nouns where gender is not obvious from form (e.g., "किताब" feminine, "कलम" feminine, "इल्म" masculine).
   PYQ-style example:
   "मेरी बड़ा भाई आया।" → Error: "बड़ा" should be "बड़ी" to agree with feminine "भाई" (here "भाई" is used in sense of sister? Actually "भाई" is masculine; correct trap: "मेरी बड़ी बहन आई" — if "बड़ा" used with "बहन" it's wrong). Better: "उसकी माता बीमार है।" → Correct. Trap: "उसकी माता बीमार हैं।" (plural verb with singular noun).

3. Spot-the-Error on Case / Postposition (कारक / पदबंध)
   Pattern: Wrong postposition (कारक चिह्न) for the case required by the verb.
   Common traps:
   - "ने" (ergative) used only with transitive verbs in perfective past; omitted or added incorrectly.
   - "को" (dative/accusative) with verbs like "चाहिए", "पसंद", "मिलना" — subject takes "को", not "ने".
   - "से" (instrumental/ablative) vs "द्वारा" (agent in passive).
   - "में" vs "पर" for location vs time.
   PYQ-style example:
   "राम ने आम खाया।" (Correct — transitive perfective)
   "राम को आम पसंद है।" (Correct — "पसंद" takes dative subject)
   "राम ने आम पसंद है।" → Error: "ने" should be "को".

4. Pronoun Case Forms (सर्वनाम के रूप)
   Pattern: Wrong oblique form before postpositions, or wrong nominative/oblique distinction.
   Common traps:
   - "मैं" → "मुझ" (मुझको, मुझसे, मेरे), "हम" → "हम" (हमको, हमसे, हमारे)
   - "तू" → "तुझ", "तुम" → "तुम"
   - "यह/वह" → "इस/उस", "ये/वे" → "इन/उन"
   - Reflexive "अपना" must agree with the subject of its clause, not the matrix subject.
   PYQ-style example:
   "मुझको यह पुस्तक पसंद है।" (Correct)
   "मैंको यह पुस्तक पसंद है।" → Error: "मैंको" should be "मुझको".
   "राम ने अपने पुस्तक ली।" → Error: "अपने" should be "अपनी" (agree with "पुस्तक" feminine).

5. Demonstrative / Relative / Interrogative Pronoun Agreement
   Pattern: Mismatch between "जो... वह", "जैसा... वैसा", "जितना... उतना" pairs.
   Common traps:
   - Gender/number mismatch between the relative and correlative.
   - Using "जो" for things and "वह" for persons incorrectly.
   PYQ-style example:
   "जो लड़का यहाँ खड़ा है, वह मेरा भाई है।" (Correct)
   "जो लड़की यहाँ खड़ी है, वह मेरा भाई है।" → Error: "वह" should be "वह" (correlative matches antecedent gender? Actually "वह" is remote demonstrative, can be used for both; better trap: "जो लड़की यहाँ खड़ी है, वह मेरी बहन है।" — correct. Trap: "जो लड़के यहाँ खड़े हैं, वह मेरे भाई हैं।" → "वह" should be "वे").

6. Indefinite Pronouns (अनिश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम)
   Pattern: "कोई", "कुछ", "कई", "सभी", "प्रत्येक", "हर" — verb agreement and case.
   Common traps:
   - "प्रत्येक" and "हर" take singular verb.
   - "सभी" takes plural verb for persons, singular for collective sense.
   - "कोई" in negative sentences → "कोई नहीं" (singular verb).
   PYQ-style example:
   "प्रत्येक विद्यार्थी को पुस्तक मिली।" (Correct — singular)
   "प्रत्येक विद्यार्थियों को पुस्तक मिली।" → Error: "विद्यार्थियों" should be "विद्यार्थी".

7. Fill in the Blank — Single Word (Noun/Pronoun Form)
   Pattern: Choose the correct form of a noun or pronoun to fit the grammatical context.
   Options are given in Devanagari; the stem contains the sentence with a blank.
   PYQ-style example:
   "राम ______ पुस्तक पढ़ रहा है।"
   (a) अपन
   (b) अपनी
   (c) अपने
   (d) अपना
   Answer: (b) अपनी — "पुस्तक" feminine singular, so possessive pronoun takes "ई" ending.

8. Sentence Improvement / Replacement
   Pattern: An underlined segment contains a noun/pronoun error; choose the best replacement.
   PYQ-style example:
   "मुझको यह काम करना है।"
   (a) मुझे
   (b) मैं
   (c) मेरा
   (d) No improvement
   Answer: (a) मुझे — "मुझको" and "मुझे" both correct, but "मुझे" is more common in modern usage; if "मुझको" is underlined as error, "मुझे" is the standard alternative.

9. Common Traps Summary (Examiner's Tricks)
   - Near-homophone nouns: "भाई/बहन", "पुत्र/पुत्री", "दादा/दादी" — gender flip changes adjective/verb.
   - "अपना" vs "अपने/अपनी/अपने" — agreement with possessed noun, not subject.
   - "ने" insertion/omission in perfective past — only transitive verbs.
   - "को" with "चाहिए/पसंद/मिलना/लगना" — subject takes "को", not "ने".
   - Oblique forms before postpositions: "मैं → मुझ", "हम → हम", "तू → तुझ", "तुम → तुम", "यह → इस", "वह → उस", "ये → इन", "वे → उन".
   - Collective nouns: singular verb default; plural only when individuals emphasized.
   - "कोई/कुछ" in negative polarity → "कोई नहीं/कुछ नहीं" with singular verb.

10. How to Approach These Questions (15–45 Second Rule)
    - Identify the verb first — it dictates case (ने/को/से) and number/gender agreement.
    - Locate the noun/pronoun linked to that verb.
    - Check: number (एकवचन/बहुवचन), gender (पुल्लिंग/स्त्रीलिंग), case (कर्ता/कर्म/करण etc.).
    - For pronouns: convert to oblique before any postposition.
    - For "अपना": find the subject of the clause where "अपना" sits; match gender/number of the possessed noun.
    - Eliminate options that violate any one rule; the survivor is correct.
