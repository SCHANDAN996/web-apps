1. Identify the type of the underlined noun in the sentence: "राम स्कूल जाता है।"
(a) जातिवाचक संज्ञा (b) व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा (c) भाववाचक संज्ञा (d) समूहवाचक संज्ञा
Answer: (b)
Solution: The word "राम" denotes a specific person, making it a व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा (proper noun). The trap is to confuse it with a common noun like "लड़का" which is जातिवाचक. So the answer is व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा.

2. Which of the following is a भाववाचक संज्ञा (abstract noun)?
(a) "दया" (b) "सेना" (c) "किताब" (d) "आम"
Answer: (a)
Solution: "दया" (kindness) expresses a quality or state, hence it is an abstract noun. The trap is to pick "सेना" (collective) or "आम" (material) as they are also nouns but not abstract. So the answer is दया.

Answer: (c)

So the answer is नर्तक → नर्तकी.
