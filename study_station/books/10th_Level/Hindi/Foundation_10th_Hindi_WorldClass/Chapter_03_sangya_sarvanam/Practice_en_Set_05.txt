101. Identify the type of the underlined noun in the sentence: "राम पढ़ता है।"
(a) Common noun (b) Collective noun (c) Abstract noun (d) Proper noun
Answer: (d)
Solution: The word "राम" names a specific person, so it is a proper noun (व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा); a common noun like "लड़का" would be जातिवाचक, so the answer is proper noun.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Hindi Grammar

102. Which of the following is an abstract noun (भाववाचक संज्ञा)?
(a) गंगा (b) पुस्तक (c) सेना (d) दया
Answer: (d)
Solution: "दया" expresses a quality or feeling, making it an abstract noun; "पुस्तक" and "गंगा" are concrete nouns and "सेना" is a collective noun, so the answer is दया.

Answer: (c)
Solution: "राम" is the doer of the action marked by "ने" in a past transitive construction, functioning as subject (कर्ता); the object "खाना" would be object (कर्म), so the answer is subject.

Answer: (d)
Solution: "वह" points to a specific person or thing, making it a demonstrative pronoun (निश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम); although it refers to a third person, its grammatical classification is demonstrative, not personal, so the answer is demonstrative pronoun.

Answer: (b)
Solution: "अपना" reflects back to the subject, so it is a reflexive pronoun (निजवाचक/आत्मवाचक सर्वनाम); it is not personal, demonstrative, or indefinite, so the answer is reflexive.

121. Identify the compound (समास) type in "राजकुमार" (राजा + कुमार).
