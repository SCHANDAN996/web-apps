126. Identify the type of the underlined noun in the sentence: "Ram is a good boy."
(a) Proper noun (b) Common noun (c) Abstract noun (d) Collective noun
Answer: (a)
Solution: The word "Ram" denotes a specific person, making it a proper noun. A common trap is to choose common noun because "boy" is a common noun, but the underlined word is "Ram", so the answer is Proper noun.

146. Which sentence correctly uses the reflexive pronoun "अपना"?
