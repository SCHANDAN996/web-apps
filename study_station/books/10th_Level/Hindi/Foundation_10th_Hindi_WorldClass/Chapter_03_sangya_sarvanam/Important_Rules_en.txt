Important Rules: Nouns and Pronouns (संज्ञा और सर्वनाम)

Rule 1 — Definition of Noun (संज्ञा की परिभाषा)
A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, quality, action, or state. In Hindi: "संज्ञा वह शब्द है जिससे किसी व्यक्ति, स्थान, वस्तु, गुण, क्रिया या अवस्था का बोध हो।"
Examples: "राम" (person), "दिल्ली" (place), "किताब" (thing), "ईमानदारी" (quality), "दौड़ना" (action), "बचपन" (state).
Exception: Verbal nouns (क्रिया-संज्ञा) like "पढ़ना" function as nouns but retain verbal force.

Rule 2 — Types of Nouns (संज्ञा के भेद)
Five main types:
(1) व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा (Proper Noun) — specific name: "गंगा", "ताजमहल".
(2) जातिवाचक संज्ञा (Common Noun) — class name: "नदी", "इमारत".
(3) समूहवाचक संज्ञा (Collective Noun) — group: "सेना", "झुंड", "भीड़".
(4) भाववाचक संज्ञा (Abstract Noun) — quality/state: "बहादुरी", "बचपन".
(5) द्रव्यवाचक संज्ञा (Material Noun) — substance: "सोना", "पानी", "लोहा".
Note: A word can shift type by usage: "गंगा" (proper) vs "गंगा" as any holy river (common).

Rule 3 — Gender (लिंग)
Two genders: पुल्लिंग (masculine) and स्त्रीलिंग (feminine). No neuter gender in Hindi.
General patterns:
- Words ending in "आ" → usually masculine: "लड़का", "घोड़ा".
- Words ending in "ई" → usually feminine: "लड़की", "घोड़ी".
- Many nouns change form: "राजा" → "रानी", "भाई" → "बहन", "पिता" → "माता".
- Some same-form words: "शिक्षक" (both), "डॉक्टर" (both); gender shown by verb/adjective.
Exceptions: "पानी" (masculine), "कहानी" (feminine), "आदमी" (masculine), "औरत" (feminine) — do not follow ending rule.

Rule 4 — Number (वचन)
Two numbers: एकवचन (singular) and बहुवचन (plural).
Plural formation:
- Masculine "आ" ending → "ए": "लड़का" → "लड़के", "घोड़ा" → "घोड़े".
- Feminine "ई" ending → "इयाँ": "लड़की" → "लड़कियाँ", "सखी" → "सखियाँ".
- Consonant-ending masculine → "एँ" or unchanged: "पुत्र" → "पुत्रें" (rare), "फल" → "फल", "पत्र" → "पत्रें"/"पत्र".
- Feminine consonant-ending → "एँ": "नदी" → "नदियाँ", "भाषा" → "भाषाएँ".
- "ओ" ending masculine → "ए": "पुत्रो" rare; "सखा" → "सखे".
Exceptions: Some nouns same in both numbers: "श्रृंखला", "पहुँच", "दौड़". Learn by usage.

Rule 5 — Case (कारक)
Eight cases show relation of noun to verb/other words. Case suffixes (कारक चिह्न) attach to noun stem.
1. कर्ता (Nominative) — no suffix: "राम पढ़ता है।"
2. कर्म (Accusative) — "को": "राम ने किताब पढ़ी।" (definite object takes "को")
3. करण (Instrumental) — "से": "राम ने कलम से लिखा।"
4. संप्रदान (Dative) — "को/के लिए": "राम ने श्याम को पुस्तक दी।"
5. अपादान (Ablative) — "से": "राम घर से आया।"
6. संबंध (Genitive) — "का/के/की": "राम का घर।"
7. अधिकरण (Locative) — "में/पर": "राम घर में है।"
8. संबोधन (Vocative) — "हे/ओ/अरे": "हे राम!" / "ओ लड़के!"
Note: "ने" marks transitive agent in perfective tenses (ergative), not a separate case.

Rule 6 — Noun Declension Patterns (संज्ञा के रूप)
Stem changes before case suffixes:
- "आ" stem masculine: "लड़का" → Nom. "लड़का", Acc. "लड़के को", Gen. "लड़के का", Loc. "लड़के में".
- "ई" stem feminine: "लड़की" → Nom. "लड़की", Acc. "लड़की को", Gen. "लड़की की", Loc. "लड़की में".
- Consonant stem masculine: "फल" → Nom. "फल", Acc. "फल को", Gen. "फल का", Loc. "फल में".
- Consonant stem feminine: "नदी" → Nom. "नदी", Acc. "नदी को", Gen. "नदी की", Loc. "नदी में".
- "ओ" stem: "सखा" → Nom. "सखा", Acc. "सखा को", Gen. "सखा का", Loc. "सखा में".
Plural oblique base: "लड़के" (masc.), "लड़कियाँ" (fem.) take suffixes directly: "लड़कों ने", "लड़कियों ने".

Rule 7 — Definition of Pronoun (सर्वनाम की परिभाषा)
A pronoun is a word used in place of a noun to avoid repetition. In Hindi: "सर्वनाम वह शब्द है जो संज्ञा के स्थान पर प्रयुक्त होता है।"
Example: "राम आया। वह खुश है।" Here "वह" replaces "राम".
Agreement: Pronoun matches antecedent in gender, number, and person.

Rule 8 — Personal Pronouns (पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम)
Three persons, each with singular/plural, and case forms:
First person (उत्तम पुरुष): "मैं" (sing.), "हम" (pl.).
Second person (मध्यम पुरुष): "तू" (sing. intimate), "तुम" (sing./pl. familiar), "आप" (sing./pl. honorific).
Third person (अन्य पुरुष): Proximate "यह" (sing.), "ये" (pl.); Remote "वह" (sing.), "वे" (pl.).
Oblique forms (before case suffixes):
"मैं" → "मुझ", "हम" → "हम", "तू" → "तुझ", "तुम" → "तुम", "आप" → "आप",
"यह" → "इस", "ये" → "इन", "वह" → "उस", "वे" → "उन".
Full paradigm example: "मैंने" (I-erg.), "मुझे" (me-acc.), "मेरा" (my-gen.), "मुझसे" (me-instr.), "मुझमें" (me-loc.).

Rule 9 — Demonstrative Pronouns (संकेतवाचक सर्वनाम)
Point to specific persons/things. Two distances:
Proximate (निकट): "यह" (this), "ये" (these).
Remote (दूर): "वह" (that), "वे" (those).
Used as pronouns: "यह मेरा है।" (This is mine.)
Used as adjectives (determiners): "यह किताब मेरी है।" (This book is mine.)
Oblique forms same as third person personal pronouns: "इस", "इन", "उस", "उन".
Common error: Using "यह/वह" for persons without honorific — prefer "ये/वे" or "यह महाशय/वह महाशय" in formal style.

Rule 10 — Interrogative Pronouns (प्रश्नवाचक सर्वनाम)
Ask questions. Main forms:
"कौन" (who — animate), "क्या" (what — inanimate).
Oblique: "किस" (sing.), "किन" (pl.).
Case forms: "किसने" (who-erg.), "किसको/किसे" (whom), "किसका" (whose), "किससे" (from whom), "किसमें" (in whom).
"क्या" also functions as yes/no question particle: "क्या तुम जाओगे?" (Will you go?)
Indefinite use with "भी/कभी": "कोई भी" (anyone), "कुछ भी" (anything).

Rule 11 — Relative Pronouns (संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम)
Introduce relative clauses, correlate with demonstratives.
"जो" (who/which — relative), "जिस" (oblique sing.), "जिन" (oblique pl.).
Correlative pair: "जो ... सो/वह" or "जो ... वही".
Example: "जो आया, वह गया।" (Who came, he went.)
"जिसने मेहनत की, उसी को फल मिला।" (Who worked hard, he got fruit.)
Case forms: "जिसने", "जिसको", "जिसका", "जिससे", "जिसमें".
Agreement: Relative pronoun agrees with antecedent in gender/number; verb in relative clause agrees with relative pronoun.

Rule 12 — Indefinite Pronouns (अनिश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम)
Refer to non-specific persons/things.
"कोई" (someone/anyone — animate), "कुछ" (something/anything — inanimate).
"कोई नहीं" (no one), "कुछ नहीं" (nothing).
"कोई-न-कोई" (someone or other), "कुछ-न-कुछ" (something or other).
"सब" (all), "कुछ" (some), "बहुत" (many/much), "थोड़ा" (little).
"अन्य" (other), "दूसरा" (another).
Oblique forms: "किसी" (from "कोई"), "किन्हीं" (pl. of "कोई").

Rule 13 — Reflexive and Emphatic Pronouns (निजवाचक और बलवाचक सर्वनाम)
"अपना" (own) — reflexive possessive, agrees with subject.
Example: "राम अपनी किताब लाया।" (Ram brought his own book.)
"आप" (self) — emphatic, reinforces noun/pronoun.
Example: "मैं आप जाऊँगा।" (I myself will go.)
"आपस में" (mutually/among themselves): "वे आपस में लड़ते हैं।"
Distinction: "अपना" reflects subject; "उसका" is non-reflexive third person possessive.
Error: "राम उसकी किताब लाया" (Ram brought his [someone else's] book) vs "राम अपनी किताब लाया" (Ram brought his own book).

Rule 14 — Pronoun Case Government (सर्वनाम का कारक प्रयोग)
Pronouns take same case suffixes as nouns but with specific oblique stems.
Key government rules:
- Transitive perfective → ergative "ने": "उसने किया।" (He did.)
- Definite animate object → "को": "मैंने उसे देखा।" (I saw him.)
- Instrument → "से": "उसने कलम से लिखा।"
- Dative/benefactive → "को/के लिए": "मैंने उसे दिया।"
- Ablative → "से": "मैं उससे मिला।"
- Genitive → "का/के/की": "उसका घर।"
- Locative → "में/पर": "उसमें/उस पर।"
Honorific "आप" takes plural verb agreement: "आप कैसे हैं?" (How are you? — plural verb "हैं").

Rule 15 — Common Errors and Traps (सामान्य त्रुटियाँ और जाल)
1. Gender mismatch: "लड़की आया" (wrong) → "लड़की आयी" (correct).
2. Number mismatch: "ये लड़का" (wrong) → "यह लड़का" or "ये लड़के" (correct).
3. Wrong case: "मैं उसे देखा" (wrong, ergative needed) → "मैंने उसे देखा" (correct).
4. Reflexive confusion: "श्याम ने उसकी किताब ली" (Shyam took his [someone else's] book) vs "श्याम ने अपनी किताब ली" (Shyam took his own book).
5. "यह/वह" for persons without honorific in formal writing — use "ये/वे" or "यह महाशय/वह महाशय".
6. "कौन" for things — use "क्या": "कौन सी किताब?" (Which book? — "कौन" as adjective) but "क्या है?" (What is it?).
7. Relative-correlative mismatch: "जो लड़का आया, वह गया" (correct) — "जो" agrees with "लड़का", "वह" agrees with "लड़का".
8. "अपना" with non-subject: "मोहन ने राम को अपनी किताब दी" — "अपना" reflects subject "मोहन", not "राम".
9. Postposition stacking error: "उसके पास से" (from near him) correct; "उसके से" incorrect.
10. "कोई/कुछ" in negative: "कोई नहीं आया" (no one came), not "कोई आया नहीं" (less idiomatic).
