Card 1
Front: What is a "संज्ञा" (Noun) in Hindi grammar?
Back: "संज्ञा" is a word that denotes the name of a person, place, thing, quality, or action. Example: "राम", "दिल्ली", "किताब", "ईमानदारी", "दौड़ना".

Card 2
Front: How many main types of "संज्ञा" are there? Name them.
Back: Five main types: "व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा" (Proper Noun), "जातिवाचक संज्ञा" (Common Noun), "समूहवाचक संज्ञा" (Collective Noun), "भाववाचक संज्ञा" (Abstract Noun), "द्रव्यवाचक संज्ञा" (Material Noun).

Card 3
Front: Give an example of "व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा" and its key feature.
Back: Example: "गंगा", "ताजमहल", "मोहन". Key feature: denotes a specific person, place, or thing; always capitalized in English, but in Hindi identified by uniqueness.

Card 4
Front: Give an example of "जातिवाचक संज्ञा" and how it differs from "व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा".
Back: Example: "लड़का", "नदी", "पहाड़". Difference: "जातिवाचक संज्ञा" denotes a class or kind, not a specific entity. "नदी" is common; "गंगा" is proper.

Card 5
Front: What is "समूहवाचक संज्ञा"? Give two examples.
Back: "समूहवाचक संज्ञा" denotes a group/collection of persons or things taken as one whole. Examples: "सेना" (army), "भीड़" (crowd), "झुंड" (herd), "कक्षा" (class).

Card 6
Front: Define "भाववाचक संज्ञा" with examples.
Back: "भाववाचक संज्ञा" denotes a quality, state, or action considered apart from the object. Examples: "बचपन" (childhood), "ईमानदारी" (honesty), "हँसी" (laughter), "दौड़ना" (running).

Card 7
Front: Define "द्रव्यवाचक संज्ञा" with examples.
Back: "द्रव्यवाचक संज्ञा" denotes a substance or material of which things are made. Examples: "सोना" (gold), "लोहा" (iron), "पानी" (water), "कपास" (cotton).

Card 8
Front: How are "संज्ञा" classified by "लिंग" (Gender)? Give one example each.
Back: Two genders: "पुल्लिंग" (Masculine) — "लड़का", "घोड़ा"; "स्त्रीलिंग" (Feminine) — "लड़की", "घोड़ी". Some nouns are "नपुंसकलिंग" (Neuter) in concept but treated as masculine/feminine in Hindi usage.

Card 9
Front: How are "संज्ञा" classified by "वचन" (Number)? Give examples.
Back: Two numbers: "एकवचन" (Singular) — "लड़का", "किताब"; "बहुवचन" (Plural) — "लड़के", "किताबें". Plural often formed by adding "एँ", "ए", "ओं", "याँ" or changing vowel.

Card 10
Front: What is "कारक" (Case) in relation to "संज्ञा"?
Back: "कारक" shows the relation of a noun/pronoun to the verb. Main cases: "कर्ता" (Nominative), "कर्म" (Accusative), "करण" (Instrumental), "संप्रदान" (Dative), "अपादान" (Ablative), "संबंध" (Genitive), "अधिकरण" (Locative), "संबोधन" (Vocative).

Card 11
Front: What is a "सर्वनाम" (Pronoun)? How does it differ from "संज्ञा"?
Back: "सर्वनाम" is a word used in place of a noun to avoid repetition. Difference: "संज्ञा" names directly; "सर्वनाम" points to a noun already mentioned or understood. Example: "राम आया। वह खुश है।" — "वह" replaces "राम".

Card 12
Front: List the main types of "सर्वनाम" with one example each.
Back: "पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम" (Personal) — "मैं", "तू", "वह"; "निश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम" (Demonstrative) — "यह", "वह"; "अनिश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम" (Indefinite) — "कोई", "कुछ"; "प्रश्नवाचक सर्वनाम" (Interrogative) — "कौन", "क्या"; "संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम" (Relative) — "जो", "जिस"; "निजवाचक सर्वनाम" (Reflexive) — "आप", "स्वयं"; "सार्वनामिक विशेषण" (Pronominal Adjective) — "मेरा", "तेरा", "इसका".

Card 13
Front: Give the "पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम" forms for first person (singular & plural) in "कर्ता कारक".
Back: Singular: "मैं"; Plural: "हम". Note: "हम" can also be honorific singular.

Card 14
Front: Give the "पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम" forms for third person (proximate & remote) in "कर्ता कारक".
Back: Proximate (near): Singular "यह", Plural "ये". Remote (far): Singular "वह", Plural "वे". In spoken Hindi "ये/वे" often used for both.

Card 15
Front: How does "निजवाचक सर्वनाम" "अपना" behave in a sentence? Give an example.
Back: "अपना" reflects back to the subject. Example: "राम अपनी किताब लाया।" — "अपनी" agrees with "किताब" (feminine) and refers back to "राम". It changes by gender/number/case of the noun it qualifies.

Card 16
Front: What is the difference between "यह/वह" as "सर्वनाम" vs "विशेषण"?
Back: As "सर्वनाम" (Pronoun): stands alone — "यह अच्छा है।" As "सार्वनामिक विशेषण" (Pronominal Adjective): qualifies a noun — "यह लड़का अच्छा है।" The form is same; function differs.

Card 17
Front: Identify the "सर्वनाम" in: "कौन आया? जिसने बुलाया, वही आया।"
Back: "कौन" — "प्रश्नवाचक सर्वनाम" (Interrogative); "जिसने" — "संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम" (Relative, oblique case of "जो"); "वही" — "निश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम" (Demonstrative, emphatic "वही" = "he himself").

Card 18
Front: Common error: "वह अपनें घर गया।" Correct it and explain.
Back: Correct: "वह अपने घर गया।" Error: "अपनें" has unnecessary nasalization (ँ). "अपना" is indeclinable in base form before noun; it takes case/gender/number of the noun it qualifies ("घर" masculine singular direct → "अपना").
