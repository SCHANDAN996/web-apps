# Nouns and Pronouns (संज्ञा और सर्वनाम)

## 1. Introduction

In every Hindi sentence, the **naming words** (संज्ञा) and the **substitute words** (सर्वनाम) carry the core meaning. For SSC GD and similar 10th-level exams, you must recognise:
- the gender (लिंग) and number (वचन) of a noun,
- the correct pronoun that replaces it,
- the case (कारक) endings that show the noun's role in the sentence.

This chapter gives clear definitions, six worked examples, and a list of frequent mistakes so you can answer grammar and comprehension questions quickly and accurately.

---

## 2. संज्ञा (Noun) — Definition and Classification

**Definition**  
A word that denotes a person, place, thing, quality, action, or state is called a **संज्ञा**.  
Example: "राम", "दिल्ली", "किताब", "ईमानदारी", "दौड़ना", "बचपन".

### 2.1 Types of Noun (संज्ञा के भेद)

| Type (भेद) | Hindi Term | Meaning | Examples (उदाहरण) |
|------------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| Proper Noun | व्यक्तिवाचक संज्ञा | Specific name of a person/place | "राम", "गंगा", "भारत" |
| Common Noun | जातिवाचक संज्ञा | General name for a class | "लड़का", "नदी", "देश" |
| Collective Noun | समूहवाचक संज्ञा | Group/collection | "भीड़", "सेना", "झुंड" |
| Abstract Noun | भाववाचक संज्ञा | Quality, action, state | "ईमानदारी", "दौड़ना", "बचपन" |
| Material Noun | द्रव्यवाचक संज्ञा | Substance/matter | "सोना", "लोहा", "पानी" |

> **Exam tip**: In SSC GD, a question may ask: *"निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा शब्द भाववाचक संज्ञा है?"* — pick the word that denotes a quality/state, not a concrete object.

### 2.2 Gender (लिंग) and Number (वचन)

| Gender | Masculine (पुल्लिंग) | Feminine (स्त्रीलिंग) |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Ending in **आ** | "लड़का", "घोड़ा" | Change **आ → ई**: "लड़की", "घोड़ी" |
| Ending in consonant | "राजा", "पिता" | Often **इ/ई** added: "रानी", "माता" |
| No fixed rule | "पुत्र", "भाई" | "पुत्री", "बहन" |

**Number (वचन)**  
- Singular (एकवचन): "लड़का"  
- Plural (बहुवचन): "लड़के" (आ → ए), "लड़कियाँ" (ई → इयाँ)

> **Rule**: The verb and pronoun must agree with the noun in gender and number.  
> Example: "लड़का **खेलता** है" (masc. sing.) vs "लड़की **खेलती** है" (fem. sing.).

## 3. सर्वनाम (Pronoun) — Definition and Classification

**Definition**  
A word used **in place of a noun** to avoid repetition is called a **सर्वनाम**.  
Example: "राम आया। **वह** खुश है।" — "वह" replaces "राम".

### 3.1 Types of Pronoun (सर्वनाम के भेद)

| Type | Hindi Term | Function | Examples |
|------|------------|----------|----------|
| Personal | पुरुषवाचक सर्वनाम | Person speaking, spoken to, spoken about | "मैं", "हम", "तुम", "आप", "वह", "वे" |
| Demonstrative | निश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम | Points out specific person/thing | "यह", "ये", "वह", "वे" |
| Interrogative | प्रश्नवाचक सर्वनाम | Asks questions | "कौन", "क्या", "किसका", "कहाँ" |
| Relative | संबंधवाचक सर्वनाम | Relates two clauses | "जो", "जिस", "जिन", "जैसे" |
| Reflexive | निजवाचक सर्वनाम | Refers back to subject | "स्वयं", "अपना", "आप" |
| Indefinite | अनिश्चयवाचक सर्वनाम | Non-specific | "कोई", "कुछ", "कई", "सभी" |

### 3.2 Case Forms (कारक रूप) — Critical for Exams

Pronouns change form according to the **case (कारक)** they are in. The table below shows the most tested forms.

| Case (कारक) | Singular "वह" | Plural "वे" | Singular "यह" | Plural "ये" |
|--------------|----------------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| Nominative (कर्ता) | वह | वे | यह | ये |
| Accusative (कर्म) | उसे | उन्हें | इसे | इन्हें |
| Instrumental (करण) | उससे | उनसे | इससे | इनसे |
| Dative (सम्प्रदान) | उसे | उन्हें | इसे | इन्हें |
| Ablative (अपादान) | उससे | उनसे | इससे | इनसे |
| Genitive (सम्बन्ध) | उसका | उनके | इसका | इनका |
| Locative (अधिकरण) | उसमें | उनमें | इसमें | इनमें |

> **Memory aid**: The **oblique base** for "वह" is "उस-", for "वे" is "उन-", for "यह" is "इस-", for "ये" is "इन-". Add the case suffix (को, से, का, में, etc.) to this base.

## 4. Six Worked Examples

Each example follows the pattern: **Question → Step-by-step reasoning → Answer**.

### Example 1: Identify the Noun Type
**Question**: *"निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा शब्द जातिवाचक संज्ञा है?"*  
Options: (a) "गंगा" (b) "नदी" (c) "भीड़" (d) "ईमानदारी"

**Reasoning**  
- "गंगा" — specific river → व्यक्तिवाचक (Proper).  
- "नदी" — general class of rivers → जातिवाचक (Common).  
- "भीड़" — group → समूहवाचक (Collective).  
- "ईमानदारी" — quality → भाववाचक (Abstract).

**Answer**: (b) "नदी"

---

### Example 2: Gender Transformation
**Question**: *"पुल्लिंग शब्द 'घोड़ा' का स्त्रीलिंग रूप क्या होगा?"*  
Options: (a) "घोड़ी" (b) "घोड़िन" (c) "घोड़िया" (d) "घोड़"

**Reasoning**  
Nouns ending in **आ** change **आ → ई** for feminine.  
"घोड़ा" → "घोड़ी".

**Answer**: (a) "घोड़ी"

---

### Example 3: Plural Formation
**Question**: *"'लड़की' का बहुवचन रूप चुनिए।"*  
Options: (a) "लड़के" (b) "लड़कियाँ" (c) "लड़कीयाँ" (d) "लड़कियों"

**Reasoning**  
Feminine nouns ending in **ई** form plural by **ई → इयाँ**.  
"लड़की" → "लड़कियाँ". Option (d) "लड़कियों" is the oblique plural (used with postpositions), not the direct plural.

**Answer**: (b) "लड़कियाँ"

---

### Example 4: Pronoun Case — Accusative
**Question**: *"वाक्य पूरा कीजिए: 'मैंने ______ देखा।'"*  
Options: (a) "वह" (b) "उसे" (c) "उससे" (d) "उसका"

**Reasoning**  
The verb "देखा" (saw) takes a direct object → **Accusative (कर्म) case**.  
For "वह", accusative singular is "उसे".

**Answer**: (b) "उसे"

---

### Example 5: Relative Pronoun Agreement
**Question**: *"सही वाक्य चुनिए:"*  
(a) "जो लड़का आया, **वह** मेरा भाई है।"  
(b) "जो लड़का आया, **वह** मेरी बहन है।"  
(c) "जो लड़की आई, **वह** मेरा भाई है।"  
(d) "जो लड़के आए, **वह** मेरे भाई हैं।"

**Reasoning**  
- "जो लड़का आया" — masculine singular → pronoun "वह" (masc. sing.), predicate "मेरा भाई" (masc. sing.) → **agrees**.  
- (b) gender mismatch.  
- (c) "लड़की" (fem.) but "वह" + "भाई" (masc.) → mismatch.  
- (d) "लड़के" (plural) but "वह" (singular) → number mismatch.

**Answer**: (a) "जो लड़का आया, **वह** मेरा भाई है।"

---

### Example 6: Reflexive Pronoun Usage
**Question**: *"'अपना' का सही प्रयोग किस वाक्य में है?"*  
(a) "राम **अपना** किताब लाया।"  
(b) "राम **अपनी** किताब लाया।"  
(c) "राम **अपने** किताब लाया।"  
(d) "राम **अपनी** किताब लाई।"

**Reasoning**  
"किताब" is feminine singular → possessive pronoun must be "अपनी" (fem. sing.).  
Subject "राम" is masculine → verb "लाया" (masc. sing.).  
Only (b) matches: "अपनी" (agrees with "किताब") + "लाया" (agrees with "राम").

**Answer**: (b) "राम **अपनी** किताब लाया।"

## 5. Common Errors — What Examiners Test

| Error Type | Wrong Example | Correct Form | Why It’s Wrong |
|------------|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Gender mismatch (pronoun–noun)** | "लड़की **वह** खुश है।" | "लड़की **वह** खुश है।" (pronoun ok) but predicate must be "खुश **है**" — actually the error is usually in adjective/verb: "लड़की **खुश **है**" ✓; "लड़की **खुश **हैं**" ✗ | Verb/adjective must match noun gender. |
| **Number mismatch (pronoun–noun)** | "लड़के **वह** खेल रहा है।" | "लड़के **वे** खेल रहे हैं।" | Plural noun needs plural pronoun & verb. |
| **Wrong oblique base** | "मैं **उसको** जानता हूँ।" (for "यह") | "मैं **इसे** जानता हूँ।" | "यह" → oblique "इस-", not "उस-". |
| **Using nominative for object** | "मोहन **वह** बुलाओ।" | "मोहन **उसे** बुलाओ।" | Object of verb → accusative "उसे". |
| **Reflexive "अपना" agreement error** | "सीता **अपना** भाई आई।" | "सीता **अपना** भाई **आया**।" | "भाई" masc. → verb "आया"; "अपना" agrees with "भाई" (masc. sing.). |
| **Confusing "कौन" (animate) vs "क्या" (inanimate)** | "**क्या** आया?" (for person) | "**कौन** आया?" | "कौन" for persons, "क्या" for things. |
| **Postposition + direct noun (missing oblique)** | "मैं **राम** से मिला।" | "मैं **राम** से मिला।" (actually correct because proper nouns often don't change) but common noun: "मैं **लड़का** से मिला।" ✗ → "मैं **लड़के** से मिला।" ✓ | Common nouns take oblique before postpositions. |
| **Demonstrative distance confusion** | Near object but "वह" used | "यह" for near, "वह" for far | "यह" = this/these; "वह" = that/those. |

## 6. Quick Revision Checklist

1. **Noun identification** — spot proper, common, collective, abstract, material.  
2. **Gender rules** — आ → ई, consonant + इ/ई, irregular pairs.  
3. **Plural rules** — आ → ए, ई → इयाँ, consonant + एँ/ओं.  
4. **Pronoun case table** — memorise oblique bases: उस-, उन-, इस-, इन-.  
5. **Agreement** — pronoun, adjective, verb all match noun in gender & number.  
6. **Reflexive "अपना"** — agrees with the **possessed noun**, not the subject.  
7. **Interrogatives** — "कौन" (who), "क्या" (what), "किसका" (whose), "कहाँ" (where).  
8. **Relative–correlative** — "जो...वह", "जिस...उस", "जैसे...वैसे" — both halves agree with their nouns.

---

## 7. Exam-Day Strategy (15-Second Rule)

- **Spot the noun/pronoun first** — underline it.  
- **Check gender & number** — does the verb/adjective match?  
- **Identify the case** — is the pronoun subject (नॉमिनेटिव), object (एक्यूसेटिव), or after a postposition (oblique)?  
- **Eliminate** options that violate agreement or case.  
- **Mark and move** — do not spend more than 15–20 seconds per grammar MCQ.
