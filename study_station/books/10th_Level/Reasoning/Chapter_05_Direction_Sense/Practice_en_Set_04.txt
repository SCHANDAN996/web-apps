76. At 8 a.m., a man stands in a field. His shadow falls exactly behind him. In which direction is he facing?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: In the morning, the Sun is in the East and shadows fall toward the West. Since his shadow falls behind him, he is facing East.
Source: PYQ-style

77. A person walks 20 m East, then turns left and walks 20 m, and then turns right and walks 10 m. What is the shortest distance from the starting point?
(a) 30 m (b) 10√13 m (c) 20 m (d) 25 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: Net East displacement = 20 + 10 = 30 m, North displacement = 20 m. By Pythagoras theorem, shortest distance = √(30² + 20²) = √1300 = 10√13 m.

78. A boy walks 30 m South, turns right and walks 40 m, turns right and walks 30 m, and finally turns left and walks 20 m. In which direction is he from his starting point?
(a) North (b) East (c) West (d) South
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 30 m South and 30 m North cancel out. Net Westward displacement = 40 + 20 = 60 m. Therefore, he is West of his starting point.

80. A is 5 m North of B. C is 12 m East of A. D is 5 m South of C. What is the shortest distance between A and D?
(a) 13 m (b) 12 m (c) 5 m (d) 10 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: From A to C is 12 m East; from C to D is 5 m South. In right triangle ACD, shortest distance AD = √(12² + 5²) = √169 = 13 m.

81. A person stands facing West. He turns 135° anticlockwise, then 90° clockwise, and then 45° anticlockwise. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (b)
Solution: Net rotation = -135° + 90° - 45° = -90° (anticlockwise). Turning 90° anticlockwise from West (270°) points to South (180°).

82. A man walks 15 m North, then 8 m West, then 15 m South, and finally 6 m East. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 8 m West (b) 2 m East (c) 2 m West (d) 14 m East
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 15 m North and 15 m South cancel out. Net horizontal displacement = 8 - 6 = 2 m West. Therefore, he is 2 m West of his starting point.

84. If 'East' is called 'North', 'West' as 'South', 'North' as 'West', and 'South' as 'East', what will 'North-West' be called?
(a) South-West (b) North-East (c) South-East (d) West-North
Answer: (a)
Solution: North becomes West and West becomes South. Therefore, North-West becomes West-South, which is South-West.

85. A person walks 10 m East, turns left and walks 10 m, then turns about (180°) and walks 20 m. In which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) North-East (b) South-East (c) South-West (d) North-West
Answer: (b)
Solution: East 10 m brings him to (10,0). Walking 10 m North and then 20 m South leaves him at (10,-10). Since x is positive and y is negative, he is South-East of the start.

Answer: (c)
Solution: The final instruction executed is 'Right', which codes for turning towards West. Therefore, he is facing West.

88. Point P is 4 m West of Q. R is 3 m North of Q. S is 4 m East of R. What is the shortest distance between Q and S?
(a) 5 m (b) 7 m (c) 6 m (d) 4 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let Q = (0,0). Then R = (0,3) and S = (4,3). Shortest distance QS = √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 m.

Solution: South-East (135°) shifts 135° anticlockwise to become North (0°). Applying the same 135° anticlockwise shift to West (270°) gives South-East (135°).

93. A person walks 5 m East, 10 m North, 10 m West, 5 m South, and 5 m East. What is the shortest distance from the start?
(a) 10 m (b) 5 m (c) 0 m (d) 15 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: East-West displacement = 5 - 10 + 5 = 0 m. North-South displacement = 10 - 5 = 5 m. Therefore, the shortest distance from the start is 5 m.

Answer: (b)
Solution: Facing South-West (225°): Turning 90° left brings him to South-East (135°). Turning 180° brings him to North-West (315°). Finally turning 90° right brings him to North-East (45°).

98. A woman walks 10 m West, then 6 m North, then 4 m East, and then 3 m South. In which direction is she from her starting point?
(a) South-West (b) North-East (c) North-West (d) South-East

Solution: Net Westward displacement = 10 - 4 = 6 m; net Northward displacement = 6 - 3 = 3 m. Therefore, she is in the North-West direction from her starting point.

99. If 'North' means 'South-East', and 'West' means 'North-East', what does 'South' mean?
(a) South-East (b) South-West (c) North-East (d) North-West

Solution: The directions rotate 135° clockwise. Therefore, South (180°) + 135° = 315°, which is North-West.
