🧠 Mnemonic 1 — The Clockwise Compass Chant

Never Eat Soggy Waffles
North → East → South → West
(Clockwise order. Say it every time you draw a compass. The waffles will thank you.)

🧠 Mnemonic 2 — The Nose Compass

Your nose always knows!
After every turn, physically (or mentally) point your nose in the new facing direction. If the question says “turn right,” twist your nose right. Final nose direction = your answer. Your nose is a built-in compass.

🧠 Mnemonic 3 — Left = Light (West), Right = Red (East) … When Facing North

“When you face North, your left hand holds the sunset (West), your right hand holds the sunrise (East).”
If you ever get confused mid‑question, quickly face North and stretch out your arms. Left = West, Right = East.

🧠 Mnemonic 4 — The Shadow Rule Song

“Sun in the East, shadow runs West; Sun in the West, shadow runs East.”
Sung to the tune of “Twinkle Twinkle Little Star,” this rhyme sticks like glue. At noon, just remember: “Noon sun South, shadow North” (in the northern hemisphere).

🧠 Mnemonic 5 — “About Turn = About Face”

“About turn? Spin your nose 180° — like you just saw a ghost behind you!”
Never mistake it for a left or right turn. It’s a full U‑turn. Your nose should face exactly the opposite direction.

🧠 Mnemonic 6 — The Pythagorean Triplet Family

“Father 3, Mother 4, Baby 5 — the shortest distance family.”
Other famous relatives:
• 5‑12‑13 (the teenager)
• 8‑15‑17 (the giant)
• 7‑24‑25 (the monster).
Remember the triplet, and you can spot the hypotenuse in a second.

🧠 Mnemonic 7 — “Clockwise = Right, Anti‑clockwise = Left” (The Watch Trick)

Imagine a watch on your right hand. The hands move clockwise. So clockwise = right turn. Anti‑clockwise = left turn.
If you struggle, just draw a tiny clock face on your paper.

🧠 Mnemonic 8 — The Intercardinal Directions Menu

“Never Eat Soggy Waffles, But Always Order Pizza With Extra Cheese.”
• N‑E (North‑East)
• S‑E (South‑East)
• S‑W (South‑West)
• N‑W (North‑West)
“But Always Order Pizza With Extra Cheese” — even the intercardinals deserve a cheesy treat.

🧠 Mnemonic 9 — The 45° Turn Trick

“Half a right, you’re in‑between.”
Turning 45° clockwise from North gives North‑East. 45° anti‑clockwise gives North‑West. This is how the compass gently tilts. Think of it as a door half‑open.

🧠 Mnemonic 10 — The “Stop and Update” Mantra

After every single movement, whisper: “Where is my nose pointing now?”
This is the golden rule of direction sense. Don’t rush — stop, update, then move. It’s like saving your game before the next level.

🧠 Mnemonic 11 — The Shadow Time Trap

“Morning? I’m a cowboy — shadow on my WEST. Evening? I’m a detective — shadow on my EAST.”
Cowboys ride into the sunset (west), detectives follow the rising sun (east). This image helps you never mix up morning vs. evening shadow directions.

🧠 Mnemonic 12 — The Two‑Bus Disaster Avoidance

“Two objects moving? Draw two separate arrows — never mix them.”
When a problem gives two people or vehicles, treat them like two different movies playing on the same screen. Draw their paths in different colors or on separate diagrams.

⏭️ Skip Strategy Tips

15‑second “No Compass” Rule
If you can’t draw a clear starting compass and the first turn within 15 seconds, skip immediately. The question will likely snowball and eat your time.

4+ Turns = Danger
When a question has more than four consecutive turns and your diagram already looks like a plate of spaghetti, mark it and return only at the end. The probability of a silly mistake is too high.

Shadow + Time = 10‑second Check
For shadow‑based questions, first confirm the time of day. If the time is not clearly mentioned, or you mix up morning/evening in the first 10 seconds, skip. These questions are designed to trick you under time pressure.
