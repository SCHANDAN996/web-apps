# Short Tricks: Direction Sense

Trick: Solve every question by drawing arrows on paper — never spin it in your head.

Trick: For the shortest distance use Pythagoras — take east/west as one side, north/south as the other; hypotenuse = √(a² + b²).

Trick: Memorise the Pythagorean triples — 3-4-5, 6-8-10, 5-12-13, 9-12-15, 8-15-17. Then the calculation is instant.

Trick: Facing North — right = East, left = West. Facing South — everything reverses.

Trick: Subtract movements in opposite directions — North 10, then South 4 = net North 6.

Trick: Shadow — west in the morning, east in the evening; negligible at noon. Use "which way the shadow falls" to find the facing direction.

Skip Strategy: If a long multi-turn puzzle isn't done in 60 seconds, skip it; do the simple shortest-distance ones first.

💡 Mnemonic: "North up, East right" — always draw the map this way.
