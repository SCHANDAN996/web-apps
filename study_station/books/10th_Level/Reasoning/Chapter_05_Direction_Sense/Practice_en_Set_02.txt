26. At sunrise, Riya starts walking facing the sun. She walks 10 m, then turns right and walks 15 m, then turns left and walks 5 m. In which direction is her shadow falling now?
(a) West (b) East (c) North (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sunrise → Sun in East. Riya faces East initially. East 10 m (still facing East) → Right → South, walks 15 m → Left → East, walks 5 m. Final facing = East. Shadow falls opposite to sun, i.e., West.
Source: PYQ-style

27. In a certain code, all directions are rotated 180°. If 'North' is coded as 'South' and 'East' as 'West', what will 'South-West' be coded as?
(a) North-West (b) North-East (c) South-East (d) South-West
Answer: (b)
Solution: 180° rotation reverses all directions. South-West (225°) → 45° (North-East).
Source: PYQ-style

28. John walks 20 m North, then 20 m East, then 15 m South, then 8 m West. What is the shortest distance from his starting point?
(a) 15 m (b) 17 m (c) 13 m (d) 12 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: Net North = 20 – 15 = 5 m North. Net East = 20 – 8 = 12 m East. Displacement = √(5² + 12²) = 13 m.
Source: PYQ-style

29. A man is facing North. He turns left and walks 10 m, then turns about and walks 5 m, then turns right and walks 10 m. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) West (c) East (d) South
Answer: (d)
Solution: Start North. Left → West, walk 10 m. About turn (180°) → East, walk 5 m. Right → South (from East, right is South). Final facing South. The trap: many confuse ‘about turn’ with a left/right.
Source: PYQ-style

30. At 4 p.m., a man walking towards a market sees his shadow exactly to his right. In which direction is he walking?
(a) North (b) West (c) East (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: 4 p.m. → Sun in West, shadow in East. Shadow to right → East on right → facing North (since right is East when facing North).

31. Six houses A, B, C, D, E, F are located as follows: A is north of B; C is east of B; D is north of C; E is east of D; F is south of E. Where is F with respect to A?
(a) South-West (b) South-East (c) North-East (d) North-West
Answer: (b)
Solution: Place B at origin (0,0). A (0, North) say (0,10). C (10,0). D (10,10). E (20,10). F (20,0) (south of E). A (0,10) to F (20,0): 20 East, 10 South → South-East.
Source: PYQ-style

32. A man is facing North-East. He turns 135° clockwise, then 90° anticlockwise, then 45° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North-West (b) South-West (c) South-East (d) North-East
Answer: (c)
Solution: NE = 45°. +135° → 180° (South). –90° → 90° (East). +45° → 135° (South-East).
Source: PYQ-style

33. A woman walks 30 m South, turns left and walks 30 m, turns left and walks 10 m, turns right and walks 10 m, and finally turns right and walks 10 m. How far is she from the start?
(a) 60 m (b) 40 m (c) 30 m (d) 50 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trace: South 30. Left → East 30. Left → North 10 (net South 20). Right (from North) → East 10 (East total 40). Right (from East) → South 10 (net South 30). Total: East 40, South 30. Distance = √(40²+30²)=50 m.
Source: PYQ-style

34. A girl is standing in front of a mirror. She sees her reflection walking towards the North. In which direction is the girl actually walking?
(a) South (b) North (c) East (d) West
Answer: (a)
Solution: A mirror reverses left and right, not front–back. But direction of movement: if her reflection appears to go North, she must be moving South (because the mirror swaps the apparent direction along the line perpendicular to the mirror). Simple rule: your reflection’s direction is opposite to your actual direction when facing the mirror.
Source: PYQ-style

35. At sunrise, a boy stands facing the sun. He turns 90° anti-clockwise, walks 100 m, then turns 45° clockwise. In which direction is he now facing?
(a) North-West (b) North-East (c) South-East (d) East
Answer: (b)
Solution: Start East. 90° anti-clockwise → North. Then 45° clockwise → North-East.

36. If in a code, directions are rotated by 135° clockwise (so that SE becomes North), then what will 'West' be coded as?
(a) South-East (b) North-West (c) North-East (d) South-West
Answer: (c)
Solution: Starting from East (90°): Turning 135° anticlockwise brings him to North-West (315°). Then turning 90° clockwise brings him to North-East (45°). Therefore, the correct answer is North-East.
Source: PYQ-style

37. At noon, a man stands facing South. He turns left and walks 10 m, then turns right and walks 20 m. In which direction is his shadow falling now? (Northern hemisphere)
(a) East (b) West (c) South (d) North
Answer: (d)
Solution: Noon sun in South. Start South. Left → East, walk 10 m (still East). Right → South, walk 20 m. Final facing South. Shadow falls opposite sun, i.e., North.

38. Mohan is to the North of Rohan. Sohan is to the East of Mohan. Tohan is to the South of Sohan. In which direction is Tohan with respect to Rohan?
(a) East (b) South-East (c) North-East (d) North
Answer: (a)
Solution: Place Rohan (0,0). Mohan (0, North) e.g. (0,10). Sohan (10,10). Tohan (10,0). So Tohan is East of Rohan.

39. A man walks 14 m East, turns right and walks 48 m, turns right and walks 14 m, and turns right and walks 48 m. How far is he from his starting point?
(a) 14 m (b) 0 m (c) 48 m (d) 50 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: Walking 14 m East and 14 m West cancel horizontally. Walking 48 m South and 48 m North cancel vertically. Therefore, he returns to the starting point and the distance is 0 m.
Source: PYQ-style

40. One evening, a man returning from office finds his shadow falling exactly in front of him. In which direction is he walking?
(a) North (b) West (c) East (d) South
Answer: (c)
Solution: Evening sun West, shadow East. Shadow in front means he faces East. So walking East.

41. In a code, the word 'Left' means turn towards North, 'Right' means turn towards East, 'Forward' means turn towards South, and 'Backward' means turn towards West. A man is initially facing North. He executes: Left, Right, Forward. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South
Answer: (d)
Solution: Initially North. Left → turn to North (still North). Right → turn to East (facing East). Forward → turn to South (facing South).

42. A is 20 m South of B. C is 20 m East of B. D is 20 m North of C. E is 20 m West of D. F is 20 m South of E. Where is F with respect to A?
(a) 20 m North (b) 20 m South (c) 20 m East (d) Same point as A
Answer: (a)
Solution: B(0,0). A(0,-20). C(20,0). D(20,20). E(0,20). F(0,0). So F is at B. A is 20 m South of B, so F is 20 m North of A.
Source: PYQ-style

43. One morning, A and B are standing facing each other. A's shadow is falling exactly to the left of B. In which direction is A facing?
(a) South (b) North (c) East (d) West
Answer: (b)
Solution: Morning sun East, shadow West. A's shadow (West) is to B's left. So B's left is West → B is facing South. Since A faces B, A faces North.

44. A boy walks 4 km North, then 2 km West, then 4 km South, then 4 km East. In which direction is he from his starting point?
(a) South-East (b) North-East (c) East (d) West
Answer: (c)
Solution: North 4, South 4 cancel. Net West 2, East 4 → 2 km East. So he is East.

45. A woman walks 5 m North, 12 m West, 9 m South, 4 m East. What is the shortest distance from the start? (in metres)
(a) 8 (b) 5√3 (c) 10 (d) 4√5
Answer: (d)
Solution: Net North = 5 – 9 = –4 (4 m South). Net West = 12 – 4 = 8 m West. Displacement = √(4² + 8²) = √80 = 4√5 m.
Source: PYQ-style

46. A person is facing North. He turns 225° clockwise, then 90° anti-clockwise. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) South-East (b) South-West (c) North-East (d) North-West
Answer: (a)
Solution: North (0°) + 225° clockwise → 225° (South-West). –90° anti-clockwise → 135° (South-East).

47. A boy walks 20 m North, turns left and walks 10 m, turns left and walks 20 m, turns right and walks 5 m. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 15 m East (b) 15 m West (c) 10 m West (d) 10 m East
Answer: (b)
Solution: North 20 → left (West) 10 → left (South) 20 → right (West) 5. Net: North-South cancel; West 10+5=15 m West.

Solution: Rotate 90° clockwise: SW (225°) + 90° = 315° (NW).

49. A man walks 15 m South, 20 m West, 15 m North, and 15 m East. How far is he from the start?
(a) 0 m (b) 5 m East (c) 10 m West (d) 5 m West
Answer: (d)
Solution: South 15 – North 15 = 0; West 20 – East 15 = 5 m West.

50. If 'North' means 'South-West', then what does 'East' mean? (assuming a uniform rotation)
(a) North-East (b) North-West (c) South-West (d) South-East
Answer: (b)
Solution: North (0°) → SW (225°) is a rotation of 225° clockwise (or 135° anti-clockwise). Applying same rotation: East (90°) + 225° = 315° (North-West).
