126. A man is facing West. He turns 135° clockwise, then 90° anti-clockwise, then 45° clockwise, and then 180° anti-clockwise. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) South (b) North (c) East (d) West
Answer: (a)
Solution: West = 270°. +135° clockwise gives 405° ≡ 45° (North-East). -90° anti-clockwise gives 315° (North-West). +45° clockwise gives 360° (North). -180° anti-clockwise gives 180° (South). Therefore, he is facing South.
Source: PYQ-style

127. At 7 AM, two persons A and B stand 100 m apart, facing each other. A’s shadow falls exactly to the left of B. In which direction is A facing?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (b)
Solution: In the morning, the sun is in the East, so all shadows fall to the West. A’s shadow falls to the West. Since this shadow is to B’s left, B’s left must be West. When facing North, a person’s left is West, so B faces North. Since A and B are facing each other, A is facing South.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (b)
Solution: The rotation is 135° clockwise: North (0°) becomes South-East (135°), and East (90°) becomes South-West (225°). Therefore, South (180°) rotated by 135° clockwise gives 180° + 135° = 315°, which is North-West. Hence, South is written as North-West.

136. A man walks 8 m East, turns left and walks 5 m, turns right and walks 7 m, and then turns left and walks 3 m. How far is he from his starting point?
(a) 13 m (b) 15 m (c) 17 m (d) 19 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: Net East displacement = 8 + 7 = 15 m. Net North displacement = 5 + 3 = 8 m. Shortest distance = √(15² + 8²) = √(225 + 64) = √289 = 17 m. Therefore, the shortest distance from the starting point is 17 m.
