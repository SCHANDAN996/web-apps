🧒 The Feynman Way: Direction Sense Made Simple

The Hardest Concept? It’s Not the Directions — It’s Updating Your Facing Direction.

Most students fail direction sense questions because they try to remember “left turn means west if I was facing north” as a fixed rule. But the real trick is to become the person moving. Let’s crack it with a story.

🏍️ The Pizza Delivery Story

Imagine you’re a pizza delivery rider in a strange new city. Your first order is from a house in a huge colony where all roads are straight and at right angles. The instructions on your phone say:

“From the main gate, go straight North for 200 metres. Then turn right and go 100 metres. Then turn left and go 150 metres. Then turn right and go 50 metres. The house will be on your left.”

You start riding. You are facing North. You reach the 200m mark, and stop. Now, you must turn right. Which way is right? It’s East, because you are still facing North. So you turn to face East and go 100m.

Now stop. You are facing East. The next instruction says “turn left”. If you are facing East, your left is North. So you turn North and go 150m. Then “turn right” — from North, right is East. You go 50m. Now you are facing East, and the house is on your left — that’s North. You did it!

The whole trick was: after every move, you paused and thought, “Which way am I facing now?” That’s the only thing you need. Don’t memorize turns; just update your mental compass each time.

🧭 The Unforgettable Trick: The Nose Compass

Here’s a trick that works even if you are blindfolded in the exam hall. Use your own body.

Stand up (or imagine it). Point your nose to where you think the person in the question is facing.

If the question says “turn right”, physically turn your head right. Your nose now points in the new direction.

If it says “turn left”, turn your head left.

If it says “about turn”, spin your nose all the way behind you.

Do this once for each turn, and at the end, whatever direction your nose is pointing — that’s your final facing direction.

Why is it unforgettable? Because your body remembers movement better than your eyes remember words. Years from now, you’ll still be able to solve these questions by just turning your nose.

❓ One Last Question

You are standing in a field at sunrise. You walk 5 steps towards your shadow. Then you turn right and walk 10 steps. Then you turn left and walk 5 steps. At this moment, in which direction is your shadow falling? (Hint: use the nose compass!)

Think it through, and when you’re sure, check the answer yourself by drawing the path and the sun. That “aha!” moment is what makes this chapter yours forever.
