51. A person walks 10 m North, 10 m East, 10 m South, and 10 m West. How far is he from his starting point?
(a) 0 m (b) 10 m (c) 20 m (d) 40 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Walking 10 m in all four directions in sequence forms a closed square path, returning to the starting point. Therefore, the distance is 0 m.

52. Radha walks 8 km South, then turns right and walks 6 km. What is the shortest distance from her starting point?
(a) 14 km (b) 10 km (c) 12 km (d) 2 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: By Pythagoras theorem: Shortest distance = √(8² + 6²) = √(64 + 36) = √100 = 10 km. Therefore, the correct answer is 10 km.

53. A person is facing South. He turns 135° anticlockwise, then 180° clockwise. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) North-West (b) North-East (c) South-West (d) South-East
Answer: (c)
Solution: Starting facing South (180°): Turning 135° anticlockwise brings him to North-East (45°). Turning 180° clockwise brings him to South-West (225°). Therefore, he is facing South-West.

Solution: At sunrise, shadows fall West. Since the shadow is to Mohit's right, Mohit's right faces West, meaning Mohit faces North. Since Sumit is facing Mohit face to face, Sumit is facing South.
