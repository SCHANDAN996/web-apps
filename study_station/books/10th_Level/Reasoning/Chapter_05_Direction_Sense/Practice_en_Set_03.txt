51. A person walks 10 m North, 10 m East, 10 m South, and 10 m West. How far is he from his starting point?
(a) 40 m (b) 10 m (c) 20 m (d) 0 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding all four legs to get 40 m, but that is the total distance walked, not the displacement. Walking 10 m in all four directions in sequence forms a closed square, so he returns to the start and the distance is 0 m.

52. Radha walks 8 km South, then turns right and walks 6 km. What is the shortest distance from her starting point?
(a) 14 km (b) 10 km (c) 12 km (d) 2 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding 8 + 6 = 14 km, which is the path length, not the straight-line distance. By Pythagoras theorem: √(8² + 6²) = √(64 + 36) = √100, so the answer is 10 km.
