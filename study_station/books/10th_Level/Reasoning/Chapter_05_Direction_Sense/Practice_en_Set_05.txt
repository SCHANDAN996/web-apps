101. At 8 a.m., a man stands facing East. He walks 10 m, turns left and walks 20 m, then turns right and walks 5 m. In which direction is his shadow falling now?
(a) West (b) East (c) North (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: In the morning, the Sun is in the East, so all shadows fall West regardless of the turns taken or the person's facing direction.
Source: PYQ-style

102. At 6 p.m., a boy notices that his shadow falls to his left. In which direction is he facing?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (b)
Solution: In the evening, shadows fall toward the East. Since the shadow is to his left, his left side is East. When facing South, the left side points East. Therefore, he is facing South.
Source: PYQ-style

103. A is 5 m East of B. C is 10 m North of B. D is 5 m West of C. In which direction is D with respect to A?
(a) South-West (b) North-East (c) North-West (d) South-East
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let B = (0,0). Then A = (5,0), C = (0,10), and D = (-5,10). From A to D, displacement is 10 m West and 10 m North. Therefore, D is North-West of A.
Source: PYQ-style

104. In a code, 'Left' means East, 'Right' means West, 'Forward' means North, and 'Backward' means South. A person initially facing North turns according to: Left, Forward, Right, Backward. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) West (b) North (c) East (d) South
Answer: (d)
Solution: The final instruction executed is 'Backward', which specifies turning towards South. Therefore, he is facing South.

105. A person walks 30 m South, then turns about (180°) and walks 10 m, then turns left and walks 20 m, and finally turns right and walks 10 m. In which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) South-West (b) North-East (c) North-West (d) South-East
Answer: (a)
Solution: Walking 30 m South gives (0,-30). Turning 180° North for 10 m gives (0,-20). Turning left (West) 20 m gives (-20,-20). Turning right (North) 10 m gives (-20,-10). Therefore, he is South-West of start.
Source: PYQ-style

106. A person is facing North-East. He turns 90° clockwise, then 135° anticlockwise, and then 45° clockwise. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) East (b) North-East (c) South-East (d) North
Answer: (b)
Solution: Net rotation = +90° - 135° + 45° = 0°. Therefore, he is still facing North-East.
Source: PYQ-style

107. A person walks 3 m East, 4 m North, 3 m East, and 4 m North. What is the shortest distance from his starting point?
(a) 7 m (b) 5 m (c) 10 m (d) 14 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: Net East displacement = 3 + 3 = 6 m; net North displacement = 4 + 4 = 8 m. Shortest distance = √(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10 m.
Source: PYQ-style

108. One morning, A and B are standing facing each other. A's shadow falls to B's right. In which direction is A facing?

Solution: In the morning, shadows fall West. B's right is West, so B faces South. Since A is facing B, A is facing North.

109. If 'South-West' is called 'North', what will 'East' be called? (assuming uniform rotation)
(a) South-West (b) North-East (c) North-West (d) South-East
Answer: (a)
Solution: South-West (225°) rotates 135° clockwise to become North (0°). Rotating East (90°) by 135° clockwise yields 225°, which is South-West.
Source: PYQ-style

110. A person walks 20 m North, turns right and walks 15 m, turns left and walks 10 m, turns right and walks 20 m, and turns left and walks 5 m. In which direction is he from the start?

Answer: (b)
Solution: Total Northward displacement = 20 + 10 + 5 = 35 m; total Eastward displacement = 15 + 20 = 35 m. Therefore, he is North-East of his starting point.

114. A person walks 6 m South, turns left and walks 8 m, turns right and walks 6 m, and turns left and walks 8 m. What is the shortest distance from the start?
(a) 10 m (b) 20 m (c) 15 m (d) 25 m
Answer: (b)

(a) South (b) North (c) East (d) West

119. A man walks 6 m West, turns left and walks 8 m, turns right and walks 6 m, and turns left and walks 8 m. What is the straight-line distance from the start?

Answer: (c)
Solution: Total Westward displacement = 6 + 6 = 12 m; total Southward displacement = 8 + 8 = 16 m. Straight-line distance = √(12² + 16²) = √400 = 20 m.

123. A person is facing East. He turns 135° clockwise, then 90° anticlockwise, then 45° clockwise, and then 180°. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) East (b) South (c) North (d) West
Answer: (c)
Solution: Net rotation = +135° - 90° + 45° + 180° = 270° clockwise. Turning 270° clockwise from East (90°) brings him to North (360° ≡ 0°).

124. A person walks 3 m East, 4 m North, 2 m East, 3 m North, 1 m West, and 5 m North. What is the shortest distance from the start?

Solution: With net horizontal displacement 5 m East and vertical displacement 12 m North, by Pythagoras theorem, shortest distance = √(5² + 12²) = √169 = 13 m.
