101. A man walks 12 m East, turns left and walks 9 m, then turns left and walks 12 m. How far is he from his starting point?

A) 12 m
B) 9 m
C) 15 m
D) 21 m

Answer: B) 9 m
Solution: East 12; left → North 9; left → West 12. Net: East‑West cancel, left with 9 m North. Distance = 9 m.

123. At sunrise, A and B are standing facing each other. A’s shadow falls exactly to B’s left. B’s shadow falls exactly to A’s right. In which directions are A and B facing?

A) A – North, B – South
B) A – South, B – North
C) A – East, B – West
D) A – West, B – East

Answer: A) A – North, B – South
Solution: Sunrise sun East, shadows West. A’s shadow West is to B’s left ⇒ B’s left is West ⇒ B faces South. B’s shadow West is to A’s right ⇒ A’s right is West ⇒ A faces North. So A faces North, B faces South.
