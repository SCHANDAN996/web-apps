PYQ Analysis: Direction Sense (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Direction sense gives 1–3 questions per exam. Shortest-distance (Pythagoras) and final-direction questions are most common. The trap is left/right confusion and distance vs displacement.

Q1. A man walks 3 km east, then 4 km north. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 5 km (b) 7 km (c) 1 km (d) 12 km
Answer: (a) 5 km
Solution: Straight distance = √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 km.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. Facing north, a person turns right, then right again. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) East (c) South (d) West
Answer: (c) South
Solution: Right from North = East; right from East = South.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. In the morning, a boy's shadow falls to his left. Which direction is he facing?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (a) North
Solution: The morning shadow falls west. If it is to his left, then his left = west, so he faces North.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. A person walks 10 m north, turns right and walks 10 m, turns right again and walks 10 m. In which direction is he from the start?
(a) North (b) East (c) South (d) West
Answer: (b) East
Solution: N10 → (0,10), right→E10 → (10,10), right→S10 → (10,0). Relative to start he is due East.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. A man walks 6 km east and then 8 km south. How far is he from the start?
(a) 10 km (b) 14 km (c) 2 km (d) 48 km
Answer: (a) 10 km
Solution: √(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10 km.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. Facing east, a person turns left. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (a) North
Solution: Turning left from East gives North.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q7. A car goes 5 km north, then 12 km east. What is its shortest distance from the start?
(a) 13 km (b) 17 km (c) 7 km (d) 60 km
Answer: (a) 13 km
Solution: √(5² + 12²) = √169 = 13 km.
Source: SSC MTS

Q8. Two friends start from the same point. A goes 3 km north, B goes 4 km east. How far apart are they?
(a) 5 km (b) 7 km (c) 1 km (d) 12 km
Answer: (a) 5 km
Solution: Distance between = √(3² + 4²) = 5 km.
Source: RRB Group D

Q9. Facing south, a person turns 90° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) East (c) South (d) West
Answer: (d) West
Solution: 90° clockwise from South is West.
Source: SSC CGL

Q10. Ravi walks 5 km south, turns left and walks 5 km, turns left again. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) East (c) South (d) West
Answer: (a) North
Solution: Facing South, left = East (walks east). Facing East, left = North. So he now faces North.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q11. A man walks 20 m north, turns left and walks 15 m, turns left and walks 20 m. How far is he from the start?
(a) 15 m (b) 20 m (c) 25 m (d) 35 m
Answer: (a) 15 m
Solution: N20 → (0,20), left→W15 → (−15,20), left→S20 → (−15,0). Distance from start = 15 m (due west).
Source: SSC MTS

Q12. A person walks 4 km west, then 3 km north. What is the straight distance from the start?
(a) 5 km (b) 7 km (c) 1 km (d) 12 km
Answer: (a) 5 km
Solution: √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 km.
Source: SSC CGL
