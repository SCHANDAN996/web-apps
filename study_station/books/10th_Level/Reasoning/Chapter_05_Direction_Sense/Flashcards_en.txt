Front: What is the golden rule for solving direction sense?
Back: Draw each step as an arrow on paper; then look at the start and end points.

Front: Where does the sun rise, and which way is the morning shadow?
Back: The sun rises in the east; the morning shadow falls towards the west.

Front: What are the eight directions?
Back: North, South, East, West + NE, NW, SE, SW.

Front: Facing North, which direction after turning right?
Back: East. (Turning left gives West.)

Front: Facing South, which direction after turning right?
Back: West (reversed!).

Front: What is the shortest-distance formula?
Back: Hypotenuse = √(a² + b²) — the Pythagoras theorem.

Front: 3 km east, then 4 km north — straight distance?
Back: √(3²+4²) = √25 = 5 km.

Front: Name some Pythagorean triples.
Back: 3-4-5, 6-8-10, 5-12-13, 9-12-15, 8-15-17.

Front: Which way does the evening shadow fall?
Back: Towards the east (because the sun is in the west).

Front: How long is the shadow at noon?
Back: Shortest (almost negligible).

Front: What to do with movements in opposite directions?
Back: Subtract — North 10, South 4 = net North 6.

Front: On a map, which directions are top, bottom, right, left?
Back: Top North, bottom South, right East, left West.

Front: Left and right depend on what?
Back: The person's current facing direction — not on top/bottom of the map.

Front: Difference between "distance" and "displacement"?
Back: Distance = total path walked; displacement = straight (shortest) distance from start to end.

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Forgetting the facing direction and reversing left and right.
