Set 1: Direction Sense MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Q1. A man walks 8 km east, then 6 km north. How far is he from the start?
(a) 10 km (b) 14 km (c) 2 km (d) 48 km
Answer: (a) 10 km
Solution: √(8² + 6²) = √100 = 10 km.
Source: SSC MTS

Q2. Facing north, a person turns right, then right again. Which direction is he facing?
(a) North (b) East (c) South (d) West
Answer: (c) South
Solution: Right from North = East; right from East = South.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q3. A person walks 5 km west, then 12 km south. What is the straight distance from the start?
(a) 13 km (b) 17 km (c) 7 km (d) 60 km
Answer: (a) 13 km
Solution: √(5² + 12²) = √169 = 13 km.
Source: SSC CGL

Q4. Facing east, a person turns left. Which direction is he facing?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (a) North
Solution: Turning left from East gives North.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q5. A car goes 9 km north, then 12 km east. Shortest distance from the start?
(a) 15 km (b) 21 km (c) 3 km (d) 108 km
Answer: (a) 15 km
Solution: √(9² + 12²) = √225 = 15 km.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. Facing south, a person turns right. Which direction is he facing?
(a) North (b) East (c) South (d) West
Answer: (d) West

Q7. A man walks 3 km south, then 4 km west. How far is he from the start?
(a) 5 km (b) 7 km (c) 1 km (d) 12 km
Answer: (a) 5 km
Solution: √(3² + 4²) = 5 km.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q8. In the morning, which direction does a person's shadow fall?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South
Answer: (b) West
Solution: The sun rises in the east, so the morning shadow falls west.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q9. Facing west, a person turns right. Which direction is he facing?

Q10. A person walks 15 km east, then 8 km north. Shortest distance from the start?

Q11. Facing north, a person turns around (180°). Which direction is he facing?
(a) North (b) East (c) South (d) West

Solution: A 180° turn from North gives South.

Q12. In the evening, which direction does a shadow fall?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South
Answer: (a) East
Solution: In the evening the sun is in the west, so the shadow falls east.

Q14. Two persons start from the same point. One goes 8 km north, the other 6 km south. How far apart are they?
(a) 14 km (b) 2 km (c) 10 km (d) 48 km
Answer: (a) 14 km
Solution: They move in opposite directions, so the distance = 8 + 6 = 14 km.
Source: SSC CGL

Q17. A man walks 6 km east, 8 km north, then 6 km west. How far is he from the start?
(a) 8 km (b) 6 km (c) 20 km (d) 10 km
Answer: (a) 8 km
Solution: East 6 and west 6 cancel out; only 8 km north remains, so the straight distance = 8 km.
Source: SSC MTS

Q18. Facing west, a person turns left. Which direction is he facing?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (b) South

Q19. A person walks 4 km north, 4 km east, then 4 km south. How far is he from the start?
(a) 4 km (b) 8 km (c) 12 km (d) 0 km
Answer: (a) 4 km
Solution: North 4 and south 4 cancel; only 4 km east remains, so distance = 4 km.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q20. Which direction is exactly between North and East?
(a) NE (b) NW (c) SE (d) SW
Answer: (a) NE
Solution: The direction between North and East is North-East (NE).
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q21. (Medium) A man walks 5 km north, 3 km east, then 5 km south. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 3 km East (b) 5 km North (c) 8 km East (d) 2 km West
Answer: (a) 3 km East
Solution: North 5 and south 5 cancel; only 3 km east remains. So he is 3 km east of the start.
Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) Facing north, a person turns 135° clockwise. Which direction is he facing?
(a) East (b) South-East (c) South (d) South-West
Answer: (b) South-East
Solution: 135° clockwise from North lands exactly between East (90°) and South (180°) = South-East.
Source: SSC CGL

Q23. (Medium) A man walks 20 m north, 15 m east, 20 m south, then 15 m east. How far is he from the start?
(a) 30 m (b) 15 m (c) 35 m (d) 25 m
Answer: (a) 30 m
Solution: North 20 and south 20 cancel; east 15 + east 15 = 30 m. So he is 30 m east.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q24. (Medium) At a certain time the shadow of a pole falls exactly towards the north. In which direction is the sun?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (b) South
Solution: A shadow always falls opposite to the sun. If the shadow is north, the sun is in the south.
Source: SSC MTS

Q25. (Medium) Facing east, a man turns 90° anticlockwise, then 180°, then 90° clockwise. Which direction is he finally facing?
(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West
Answer: (d) West
Solution: East → (90° anticlockwise) North → (180°) South → (90° clockwise) West.
Source: SSC CGL
