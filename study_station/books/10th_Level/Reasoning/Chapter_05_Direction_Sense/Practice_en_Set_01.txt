1. A man is facing North. He turns 90° to his right, then 180° to his left, and finally 90° to his right. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) East (c) West (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: East is the tempting answer if you stop after the first right turn, but you must track all three turns: North → right 90° = East → left 180° = West → right 90° = North, so the answer is North.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Sheela walked 20 m towards East, turned left and walked 15 m, then turned right and walked 25 m. In which direction is she from her starting point?
(a) North-East (b) South-East (c) North (d) East
Answer: (a)
Solution: East alone is tempting because both long walks go East, but the 15 m left turn adds a North component: net East = 20 + 25 = 45 m and net North = 15 m, so the answer is North-East.
Source: PYQ-style

3. One morning after sunrise, Ramesh was standing in front of his house. His shadow was falling exactly to his right. In which direction was Ramesh facing?
(a) East (b) North (c) West (d) South
Answer: (d)
Solution: North is tempting if you forget that morning shadows fall toward the West, not the East; if his right side is West, he must be facing South, so the answer is South.

4. A boy runs 50 m towards South, then turns to his right and runs 50 m, then turns to his right again and runs 50 m. In which direction is he from his starting point?
(a) West (b) North (c) South (d) East
Answer: (a)
Solution: South is tempting because he ran South first, but the 50 m South and the final 50 m North cancel out, leaving only the 50 m Westward leg, so the answer is West.

5. A man is facing West. He turns 45° clockwise, then 135° anticlockwise, and then 90° clockwise. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) East (c) West (d) South
Answer: (c)
Solution: North is tempting if you add all angles as clockwise, but anticlockwise must be subtracted: +45° − 135° + 90° = 0°, so he has not moved at all and the answer is West.

(a) West (b) South (c) North (d) East

7. If 'South-East' is called 'North', and 'North-East' is called 'West', what will 'West' be called?
(a) South-East (b) North-West (c) South-West (d) North-East
Answer: (a)
Solution: North-West is tempting if you assume a simple 90° shift, but South-East to North is a 135° anticlockwise shift; applying the same 135° anticlockwise shift to West gives South-East, so the answer is South-East.

10. A cyclist rides 12 km towards North, then turns left and rides 5 km. What is the shortest distance from his starting point?
(a) 17 km (b) 15 km (c) 7 km (d) 13 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: 17 km is tempting if you simply add the two legs, but the shortest distance is the hypotenuse: √(12² + 5²) = √169 = 13, so the answer is 13 km.

Solution: 35 m is tempting if you add the unequal-looking legs, but North 20 m cancels South 20 m and East 15 m cancels West 15 m, bringing him back to the start, so the answer is 0 m.

22. One morning after sunrise, two friends A and B are standing in a field facing each other. A notices that his own shadow is falling exactly to his left. In which direction is B facing?
(a) North (b) South (c) West (d) East
Answer: (b)
Solution: North is tempting if you answer for A instead of B; in the morning shadows fall West, so A's left being West means A faces North, and B, facing A from the opposite side, faces South, so the answer is South.

23. A is 40 m to the North of B. C is 40 m to the East of B. D is 40 m to the North of C. E is 40 m to the East of D. In which direction is E with respect to A?
(a) South-East (b) East (c) North-East (d) North
Answer: (b)
Solution: North-East is tempting because E is reached by going North and East overall, but taking B as (0,0): A = (0,40) and E = (80,40) share the same height, so E lies exactly East of A, and the answer is East.
Source: PYQ-style

24. In the evening at sunset, a man is walking along a straight road and notices that his shadow is falling exactly in front of him. In which direction is he walking?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: West is tempting because the Sun sets in the West, but shadows fall opposite the Sun, i.e. toward the East; since the shadow is in front of him, he is walking toward the East, so the answer is East.
