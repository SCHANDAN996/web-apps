1. A man is facing North. He turns 90° to his right, then 180° to his left, and finally 90° to his right. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) North (b) East (c) West (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: Starting facing North: Turning 90° right points him East. Turning 180° left points him West. Finally, turning 90° right points him North. Therefore, he is facing North.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Sheela walked 20 m towards East, turned left and walked 15 m, then turned right and walked 25 m. In which direction is she from her starting point?
(a) South-East (b) North-East (c) North (d) East
Answer: (b)
Solution: Net displacement: East = 20 + 25 = 45 m, North = 15 m. Therefore, she is in the North-East direction from her starting point.
Source: PYQ-style

3. One morning after sunrise, Ramesh was standing in front of his house. His shadow was falling exactly to his right. In which direction was Ramesh facing?
(a) East (b) North (c) South (d) West
Answer: (c)
Solution: In the morning, the Sun is in the East, so shadows fall toward the West. Since Ramesh's shadow falls to his right, his right side faces West. Therefore, Ramesh was facing South.
Source: PYQ-style

4. A boy runs 50 m towards South, then turns to his right and runs 50 m, then turns to his right again and runs 50 m. In which direction is he from his starting point?
(a) East (b) North (c) South (d) West
Answer: (d)
Solution: Running 50 m South, then 50 m West (right), and then 50 m North (right) cancels the vertical displacement. Therefore, he is directly West of his starting point.
Source: PYQ-style

5. A man is facing West. He turns 45° clockwise, then 135° anticlockwise, and then 90° clockwise. In which direction is he facing now?
(a) West (b) East (c) North (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: Net rotation = +45° (clockwise) - 135° (anticlockwise) + 90° (clockwise) = 0°. Therefore, he is still facing West.
Source: PYQ-style

6. A girl walks 10 m towards North, then turns right and walks 10 m, then turns right again and walks 10 m. In which direction is she from her starting point?
(a) West (b) East (c) North (d) South
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 10 m North and 10 m South movements cancel each other out, leaving only a 10 m East displacement. Therefore, she is to the East of her starting point.

7. If 'South-East' is called 'North', and 'North-East' is called 'West', what will 'West' be called?
(a) South-West (b) North-West (c) South-East (d) North-East
Answer: (c)
Solution: South-East shifts 135° anticlockwise to become North. Applying the same 135° anticlockwise shift to West brings it to South-East.

(a) North (b) South (c) East (d) West

Solution: Facing East: Turning 90° clockwise points South. Turning 180° anticlockwise from South points directly opposite, to North. Therefore, he is facing North.

10. A cyclist rides 12 km towards North, then turns left and rides 5 km. What is the shortest distance from his starting point?
(a) 17 km (b) 13 km (c) 7 km (d) 15 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: Using Pythagoras theorem: Shortest distance = √(12² + 5²) = √(144 + 25) = √169 = 13 km. Therefore, the correct answer is 13 km.

Answer: (b)
Solution: In the morning, shadows fall toward the West. Since the shadow falls to Sunil's right, Sunil's right is West, meaning Sunil is facing South. Since Mohan is coming from the opposite direction, Mohan is facing North.

23. A is 40 m to the North of B. C is 40 m to the East of B. D is 40 m to the North of C. E is 40 m to the East of D. In which direction is E with respect to A?
(a) South-East (b) North-East (c) East (d) North
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let B = (0,0). Then A = (0,40), C = (40,0), D = (40,40), and E = (80,40). Since A and E share the same y-coordinate of 40 and E has x = 80, E is directly East of A.
Source: PYQ-style
