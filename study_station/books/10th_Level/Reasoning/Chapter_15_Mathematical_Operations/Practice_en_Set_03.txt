Q51. (Medium) If '+' means '-', '-' means 'x', 'x' means '/', and '/' means '+', find the value of: 30 / 10 * 2 - 4 + 6
(A) 44 (B) 42 (C) 46 (D) 48

Answer: (A) 44

Solution:
Replacing symbols: 30 + 10 / 2 x 4 - 6. By BODMAS: 10 / 2 = 5, 5 x 4 = 20, 30 + 20 - 6 = 44. Hence, the correct option is (A) with value 44.

Q71. (Hard) ⚠️ Which two signs should be interchanged to make the given equation correct? 20 * 10 / 2 + 3 - 7 = 28
(A) + and * (B) + and - (C) * and / (D) - and /

Answer: (A) + and *

Solution:
Original LHS: 20 x 10 / 2 + 3 - 7 = 100 + 3 - 7 = 96 ≠ 28. Interchanging '+' and '*': 20 + 10 / 2 x 3 - 7. By BODMAS: 20 + 5 x 3 - 7 = 20 + 15 - 7 = 28, which matches RHS. Examiner's Trap: Swapping '+' and '-' yields 100 - 3 + 7 = 104.

Source: SSC CGL
