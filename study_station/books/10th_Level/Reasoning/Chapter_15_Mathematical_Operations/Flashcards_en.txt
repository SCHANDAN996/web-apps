Front: Which rule still applies after changing signs?
Back: BODMAS (Brackets → Of/Order → Divide → Multiply → Add → Subtract).

Front: What is the complete method for mathematical operations?
Back: First give each sign its new meaning, then solve by BODMAS.

Front: If '+' means '×', then 6 + 2 = ?
Back: 6 × 2 = 12.

Front: If '×' means '+', then 5 × 3 = ?
Back: 5 + 3 = 8.

Front: What does "interchange" mean?
Back: Swapping two signs — like + to − and − to + together.

Front: If + = ×, × = +, then 4 + 3 × 2 = ?
Back: 4 × 3 + 2 = 12 + 2 = 14.

Front: After changing signs, which operation comes first?
Back: Brackets, then divide-multiply, then add-subtract (BODMAS).

Front: If '−' means '×', then 5 − 3 = ?
Back: 5 × 3 = 15.

Front: If '÷' means '+', then 12 ÷ 4 = ?
Back: 12 + 4 = 16.

Front: Difference between "substitution" and "interchange"?
Back: Substitution = a new meaning for one sign; interchange = swapping two signs.

Front: What is the full form of BODMAS?
Back: Brackets, Of, Divide, Multiply, Add, Subtract.

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Ignoring the new meaning and solving with the old sign.

Front: In "swap + and −", how many signs change?
Back: Both — + to − and − to +.

Front: How to solve option-checking questions?
Back: Apply each option's signs and see if both sides become equal.

Front: What is the mathematical-operations mnemonic?
Back: "Change the meaning, run BODMAS".
