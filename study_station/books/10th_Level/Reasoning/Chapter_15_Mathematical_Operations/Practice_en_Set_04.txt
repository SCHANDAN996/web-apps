Q76. (Medium) If '+' means '÷', '–' means '×', '×' means '+', and '÷' means '–', find the value of: 40 + 5 – 2 × 6 ÷ 3
(A) 19 (B) 17 (C) 21 (D) 23

Answer: (A) 19

Solution:
Replacing signs: 40 ÷ 5 × 2 + 6 – 3. By BODMAS: 40 ÷ 5 = 8, 8 × 2 = 16, 16 + 6 – 3 = 19. Hence, the correct option is (A) with value 19.

Source: SSC CGL

Q79. (Medium) If a * b = a² + b² – ab, find the value of: 6 * 4
(A) 32 (B) 26 (C) 30 (D) 28

Answer: (D) 28

Solution:
Given rule: a * b = a² + b² – ab. For 6 * 4: 6² + 4² – (6 × 4) = 36 + 16 – 24 = 28. Hence, the correct option is (D) with value 28.

Q82. (Medium) ⚠️ If '+' means '–', '–' means '×', '×' means '÷', and '÷' means '+', find the value of: 15 – 3 + 10 × 2 ÷ 4
(A) 42 (B) 39 (C) 44 (D) 46

Answer: (C) 44

Solution:
Replacing signs: 15 × 3 – 10 ÷ 2 + 4. By BODMAS: First divide 10 ÷ 2 = 5, then multiply 15 × 3 = 45. Then 45 – 5 + 4 = 44. Examiner's Trap: Do not subtract 10 before dividing by 2; division has higher precedence. Hence, the correct option is (C) with value 44.

Q97. (Hard) ⚠️ If '+' means '÷', '–' means '+', '×' means '–', and '÷' means '×', find the value of: 36 + 6 × 4 – 8 ÷ 2

Answer: (B) 18

Solution:
Replacing signs: 36 ÷ 6 – 4 + 8 × 2. By BODMAS: 36 ÷ 6 = 6, and 8 × 2 = 16. Then 6 – 4 + 16 = 18. Examiner's Trap: Do not perform addition before multiplication (evaluating (6 - 4 + 8) × 2 gives incorrect 20). Hence, the correct option is (B) with value 18.

Q99. (Hard) Evaluate the given nested brackets expression: [{(24 ÷ 3) × 2} + 14] – (15 ÷ 5)
(A) 31 (B) 25 (C) 29 (D) 27

Answer: (D) 27

Solution:
Solving innermost bracket: 24 ÷ 3 = 8. Next: 8 × 2 = 16. Then: 16 + 14 = 30. Finally: 30 – (15 ÷ 5) = 30 – 3 = 27. Hence, the correct option is (D) with value 27.

Source: RRB NTPC
