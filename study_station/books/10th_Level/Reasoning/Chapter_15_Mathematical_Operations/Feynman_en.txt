# Feynman Technique: Mathematical Operations Made Crystal Clear

## The Magic Tea Stall Code
Imagine you and your friend visit a quirky tea stall run by an old shopkeeper. On the wooden board hanging by the counter, he has written today's secret code:
- Cup of Tea = 5 coins
- Biscuit = 3 coins
- Toffee = 2 coins
- And here is the riddle: '+' means "add the second item's price and halve the total", while '-' means "subtract the second from the first and double the result."

The shopkeeper smiles and asks: "Son, what is Tea + Biscuit?"
You instantly shout: "8 coins!"
He chuckles: "Wrong! In my shop, you must obey the code."
Your friend grabs a notepad: "Wait! First write the real numbers: Tea = 5, Biscuit = 3. Now the code says '+' means (5 + 3) / 2 = 4 coins!"
The shopkeeper claps: "Spot on! Free toffees for both of you!"

## The Big Idea: Decode First, Calculate Second
In competitive exams, the examiner acts exactly like that playful shopkeeper. They invent arbitrary rules where '+' wears the mask of '-', and 'x' wears the mask of '/'.
The number one reason candidates lose marks is that they start calculating while their brain is still deciphering the symbols!
Always remember:
The meaning of symbols changes; the laws of mathematics never change.
BODMAS rules standard arithmetic, not the masquerading symbols.

## The Feynman Trick: Strip the Costume Before the Dance
Whenever you see a coded expression, imagine each symbol is wearing a fancy costume.
Before doing a single calculation, write down the clean, unmasked expression on your rough sheet. Once the costumes are off, standard BODMAS solves the puzzle effortlessly.

## The Unforgettable RDB Rule
Remember the simple acronym R.D.B.:
1. **R — Replace**: Swap out the fake symbols with the real operators.
2. **D — Decode**: Write down the pure arithmetic expression.
3. **B — BODMAS**: Solve strictly using Brackets, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction.

Whenever exam stress hits, whisper to yourself: "RDB: Replace, Decode, BODMAS!"

## Worked Example: Step-by-Step
Suppose the question says:
*If 'x' means '+', '/' means '-', '+' means 'x', and '-' means '/', what is the value of: 16 / 4 + 2 - 2 x 3 = ?*

**Step 1 (Replace & Decode):**
Replace every symbol systematically:
`16 - 4 x 2 / 2 + 3`

**Step 2 (BODMAS Application):**
- Division first: 2 / 2 = 1 -> Expression becomes: 16 - 4 x 1 + 3
- Multiplication next: 4 x 1 = 4 -> Expression becomes: 16 - 4 + 3
- Addition & Subtraction: 16 - 4 = 12, then 12 + 3 = 15.

Result: 15. Clean, simple, and error-free!
