Q1. (Easy) If '+' means '-', '-' means 'x', 'x' means '/', and '/' means '+', find the value of: 8 / 4 * 2 - 1 + 3
(A) 7 (B) 5 (C) 9 (D) 11

Answer: (A) 7

Solution:
Replacing symbols according to the rule: 8 + 4 / 2 x 1 - 3. Applying BODMAS: division first: 4 / 2 = 2. Multiplication next: 2 x 1 = 2. Addition and subtraction: 8 + 2 - 3 = 7. Hence, the correct option is (A) with value 7.

Source: SSC CGL

Q21. (Medium) ⚠️ Which two signs should be interchanged to make the given equation correct? 12 + 6 / 3 * 2 - 8 = 18
(A) + and * (B) + and - (C) * and / (D) - and /

Answer: (A) + and *

Solution:
Interchanging '+' and '*': The expression becomes 12 x 6 / 3 + 2 - 8. By BODMAS: 12 x 2 + 2 - 8 = 24 + 2 - 8 = 18, which matches the RHS. Examiner's Trap: Swapping '+' and '-' yields 12 - 4 + 8 = 16, which is close but incorrect.

Source: SSC CGL

Q23. (Medium) ⚠️ Which two numbers should be interchanged to make the given equation correct? 8 + 6 x 2 - 4 / 2 = 20
(A) 8 and 4 (B) 6 and 4 (C) 8 and 6 (D) 6 and 2

Answer: (C) 8 and 6

Solution:
Original LHS: 8 + 12 - 2 = 18 != 20. Interchanging numbers 8 and 6: Expression becomes 6 + 8 x 2 - 4 / 2. By BODMAS: 6 + 16 - 2 = 20, which matches the RHS. Examiner's Trap: Swapping 6 and 4 gives 8 + 8 - 3 = 13.

Source: SSC MTS

Q24. (Medium) Select the correct combination of mathematical signs to replace * sequentially and balance the equation: 24 * 4 * 2 * 6 = 18
(A) /, +, x (B) +, /, x (C) x, /, + (D) /, x, +

Answer: (D) /, x, +

Solution:
Testing combination '/, x, +': 24 / 4 x 2 + 6. By BODMAS: 24 / 4 = 6, 6 x 2 = 12, 12 + 6 = 18, which equals 18. Hence, the correct option is (A) with value /, x, +.

Source: RRB NTPC

Q25. (Medium) If 'P' denotes 'greater than' (>), 'Q' denotes 'less than' (<), and 'R' denotes 'equal to' (=), which of the following expressions is mathematically true?
(A) (6 x 2) Q (3 x 3) (B) (8 + 4) P (2 x 5) (C) (10 - 2) R (3 x 4) (D) (15 / 3) P (3 x 2)

Answer: (B) (8 + 4) P (2 x 5)

Solution:
Evaluating each option: (8 + 4) P (2 x 5) translates to 12 > 10, which is True. Other options evaluate to false statements. Hence, the correct option is (B) with expression (8 + 4) P (2 x 5).
