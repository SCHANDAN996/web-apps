1. If '+' means '-', '-' means 'x', 'x' means '/', and '/' means '+', find the value of: 8 / 4 x 2 - 1 + 3
(a) 9 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 11
Answer: (c)
Solution: Replacing the symbols gives 8 + 4 / 2 x 1 - 3. By BODMAS: 4 / 2 = 2, then 2 x 1 = 2, then 8 + 2 - 3 = 7. The trap is solving left to right without BODMAS, which gives 5, so the answer is 7.
