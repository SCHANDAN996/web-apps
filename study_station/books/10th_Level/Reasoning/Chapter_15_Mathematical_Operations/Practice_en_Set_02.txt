Q26. (Medium) If '+' means '-', '-' means 'x', 'x' means '/', and '/' means '+', find the value of: 24 / 6 * 2 - 3 + 5
(A) 28 (B) 26 (C) 30 (D) 32

Answer: (A) 28

Solution:
Replacing symbols: 24 + 6 / 2 x 3 - 5. By BODMAS: 6 / 2 = 3, 3 x 3 = 9, 24 + 9 - 5 = 28. Hence, the correct option is (A) with value 28.

Q46. (Hard) ⚠️ Which two signs should be interchanged to make the given equation correct? 18 * 6 / 3 + 4 - 5 = 21
(A) + and * (B) + and - (C) * and / (D) - and /

Answer: (A) + and *

Solution:
Original LHS: 18 x 6 / 3 + 4 - 5 = 36 + 4 - 5 = 35 ≠ 21. Interchanging '+' and '*': 18 + 6 / 3 x 4 - 5. By BODMAS: 18 + 2 x 4 - 5 = 18 + 8 - 5 = 21, which matches RHS. Examiner's Trap: Swapping '+' and '-' yields 36 - 4 + 5 = 37.

Source: SSC CGL
