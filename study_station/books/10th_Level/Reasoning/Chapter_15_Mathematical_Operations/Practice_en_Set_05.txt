Q101. (Medium) If '+' means '×', '–' means '÷', '×' means '+', and '÷' means '–', find the value of: 32 – 8 + 4 × 12 ÷ 6
(A) 22 (B) 20 (C) 24 (D) 26

Answer: (A) 22

Solution:
Replacing signs: 32 ÷ 8 × 4 + 12 – 6. By BODMAS: 4 × 4 + 12 – 6 = 16 + 12 – 6 = 22. Hence, the correct option is (A) with value 22.

Q116. (Medium) If 6 * 4 = 20, and 8 * 5 = 35, then find the value of: 7 * 6
(A) 40 (B) 34 (C) 38 (D) 36

Answer: (D) 36

Solution:
Pattern: a * b = (a – 1) × b. So 6 * 4 = 5 × 4 = 20; 8 * 5 = 7 × 5 = 35. Thus, 7 * 6 = (7 – 1) × 6 = 36. Hence, the correct option is (D) with value 36.

Q122. (Hard) ⚠️ If '+' means '–', '–' means '×', '×' means '÷', and '÷' means '+', find the value of: 20 – 4 + 18 × 3 ÷ 6
(A) 68 (B) 80 (C) 74 (D) 86

Answer: (B) 80

Solution:
Replacing signs: 20 × 4 – 18 ÷ 3 + 6. By BODMAS: 18 ÷ 3 = 6, 20 × 4 = 80, then 80 – 6 + 6 = 80. Examiner's Trap: In 80 - 6 + 6, addition and subtraction have equal precedence from left to right; do not calculate 80 - (6 + 6) = 68. Hence, the correct option is (B) with value 80.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q123. (Hard) If a # b = (a² – b²) ÷ (a – b), find the value of: 15 # 9
(A) 26 (B) 22 (C) 24 (D) 28

Answer: (C) 24

Solution:
Using identity (a² – b²) ÷ (a – b) = a + b: 15 # 9 = 15 + 9 = 24. Hence, the correct option is (C) with value 24.

Q124. (Hard) Evaluate the given nested brackets expression: 50 – [20 + {12 ÷ 3 × (6 – 4)}]
(A) 26 (B) 20 (C) 24 (D) 22

Answer: (D) 22

Solution:
Solving innermost bracket first: (6 – 4) = 2. Inside curly braces: 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = 4 × 2 = 8. Inside square brackets: 20 + 8 = 28. Finally: 50 – 28 = 22. Hence, the correct option is (D) with value 22.

Source: RRB NTPC
