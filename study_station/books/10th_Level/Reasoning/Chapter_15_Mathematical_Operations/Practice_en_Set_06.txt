Q126. (Medium) If '+' means '÷', '–' means '×', '×' means '–', and '÷' means '+', find the value of: 36 + 4 – 3 × 15 ÷ 5
(A) 17 (B) 15 (C) 19 (D) 21

Answer: (A) 17

Solution:
Replacing signs: 36 ÷ 4 × 3 – 15 + 5. By BODMAS: 9 × 3 – 15 + 5 = 27 – 15 + 5 = 17. Hence, the correct option is (A) with value 17.

Q149. (Hard) Evaluate the given nested brackets expression: 60 – [25 + {18 ÷ 2 × (5 – 3)}]
(A) 21 (B) 15 (C) 19 (D) 17

Answer: (D) 17

Solution:
Innermost bracket: 5 – 3 = 2. Curly braces: 18 ÷ 2 × 2 = 9 × 2 = 18. Square bracket: 25 + 18 = 43. Finally: 60 – 43 = 17. Hence, the correct option is (D) with value 17.

Source: RRB NTPC
