Q26. (Medium) ⚠️ Figure Series:
Problem Figures:
(i) A circle containing a single dot at the centre.
(ii) Two concentric circles, no dot.
(iii) A square containing a single dot at the centre.
(iv) Two concentric squares, no dot.
(v) ?
(A) A triangle with a dot at the centre (B) Two concentric triangles, no dot (C) A square with a dot at the centre (D) A circle with a dot at the centre

Answer: (A) A triangle with a dot at the centre

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Odd positions (1, 3, 5): outer shape changes in sequence (circle → square → triangle) with a single central dot. Even positions (2, 4): concentric duplication of previous shape without dot. Step (v) is odd, requiring a triangle with a central dot. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q28. (Medium) Figure Series (Rotation + Element Count):
Problem Figures:
(i) Equilateral triangle pointing up, with 1 small tail line at its base.
(ii) Square pointing top-right (rotated 45°), with 2 tail lines at its base.
(iii) Pentagon pointing right (rotated 90°), with 3 tail lines at its base.
(iv) ?
(A) Hexagon pointing right, 3 lines (B) Pentagon pointing right, 4 lines (C) Hexagon pointing bottom-right (rotated 135°), 4 lines (D) Square pointing top-right, 4 lines

Answer: (C) Hexagon pointing bottom-right (rotated 135°), 4 lines

Solution:
Sides increase by 1 (3 → 4 → 5 → 6 = hexagon). Rotation is 45° clockwise per step (0° → 45° → 90° → 135° = bottom-right). Number of tail lines equals step number (1, 2, 3, 4). Step 4 is a hexagon pointing bottom-right with 4 lines. Hence, the correct option is (C).
