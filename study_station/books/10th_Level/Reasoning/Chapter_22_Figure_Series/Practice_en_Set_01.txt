Set 1: Figure Series MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. An arrow points up, right, down. Which way next?
(a) up (b) right (c) down (d) left
Answer: (d) left
Solution: 90° clockwise each time: down → left.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. A full turn in 4 steps means each step rotates:
(a) 45° (b) 90° (c) 120° (d) 180°
Answer: (b) 90°
Solution: 360° ÷ 4 = 90°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Sides: triangle, square, pentagon, ? Next?
(a) hexagon (b) heptagon (c) octagon (d) circle
Answer: (a) hexagon
Solution: 3, 4, 5, 6 sides → hexagon.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Dots: 2, 3, 4, ? How many next?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 5
Solution: Add 1 each time: 4 + 1 = 5.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. Figure series is about:
(a) numbers (b) visuals (c) letters (d) words
Answer: (b) visuals
Solution: It is a visual pattern.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. An arrow points right. After 90° clockwise it points:
(a) up (b) right (c) down (d) left
Answer: (c) down
Solution: 90° clockwise from right is down.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. A full turn in 8 steps means each step rotates:
(a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 60° (d) 90°
Answer: (b) 45°
Solution: 360° ÷ 8 = 45°.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Lines: 2, 4, 6, ? How many next?
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 10
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: Add 2 each time: 6 + 2 = 8.
Source: RRB Group D

Q9. 90° clockwise from North gives:
(a) South (b) East (c) West (d) North
Answer: (b) East
Solution: North → East (clockwise).
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q10. 90° anticlockwise from North gives:
(a) South (b) East (c) West (d) North
Answer: (c) West
Solution: North → West (anticlockwise).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q11. An arrow points down. After 180° it points:
(a) up (b) right (c) down (d) left
Answer: (a) up
Solution: 180° from down is up.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q12. Sides: square, pentagon, hexagon, ? Next?
(a) triangle (b) heptagon (c) octagon (d) circle
Answer: (b) heptagon
Solution: 4, 5, 6, 7 sides → heptagon.
Source: SSC MTS

Q13. If a shaded part moves to the next corner clockwise each time, this is a change of:
(a) rotation (b) position (c) size (d) colour
Answer: (b) position
Solution: Moving corner to corner is a change of position.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q14. Dots: 1, 3, 5, ? How many next?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 7
Solution: Add 2 each time: 5 + 2 = 7.
Source: SSC CGL

Q15. When two changes happen together, you should:
(a) ignore one (b) track each element separately (c) guess (d) skip
Answer: (b) track each element separately
Solution: Handle each element's rule, then combine.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q16. An arrow points left. After 90° clockwise it points:
(a) up (b) right (c) down (d) left
Answer: (a) up
Solution: 90° clockwise from left is up.
Source: SSC MTS

Q17. (Medium) A figure rotates 60° each step. How many steps complete a full turn?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: 360° ÷ 60° = 6.
Source: SSC CGL

Q18. (Medium) 180° from East gives:
(a) North (b) South (c) West (d) East
Answer: (c) West
Solution: 180° from East is West.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q19. (Medium) Sides: 3, 5, 7, ? How many next?
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 10 (d) 11
Answer: (b) 9
Solution: Add 2 each time: 7 + 2 = 9.
Source: SSC CGL

Q20. (Medium) An arrow turns 45° clockwise each step, starting from up (North). After 2 steps it points:
(a) North-East (b) East (c) South-East (d) South
Answer: (b) East
Solution: Up → NE (45°) → East (90°). After 2 steps it points East.
Source: SSC MTS
