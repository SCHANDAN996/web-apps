Q1. (Easy) An arrow points up, right, down. Which way next?
(A) left (B) right (C) down (D) up

Answer: (A) left

Solution:
90° clockwise each time: down → left. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q21. (Medium) An arrow points North-East. It rotates 135° clockwise. In which direction does it point now?
(A) South (B) South-West (C) East (D) West

Answer: (A) South

Solution:
Starting from North-East: rotating 45° clockwise reaches East; another 90° clockwise reaches South (45° + 90° = 135°). Therefore, it points South. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) In a series, the number of lines follows the pattern: 3, 6, 4, 7, 5, ? What is the next number of lines?
(A) 6 (B) 8 (C) 9 (D) 10

Answer: (B) 8

Solution:
The series follows an alternating rule: +3, -2, +3, -2, +3. Following 5, we add 3: 5 + 3 = 8 lines. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q23. (Medium) ⚠️ A circle is divided into 4 quadrants. A shaded sector moves 1 quadrant clockwise at each step, while a dot moves 1 quadrant anticlockwise at each step. If both start in the top quadrant (Step 1), in which step will they coincide in the same quadrant again?
(A) Step 2 (B) Step 4 (C) Step 3 (D) Step 5

Answer: (C) Step 3

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Sector positions: Step 1 = Top, Step 2 = Right, Step 3 = Bottom. Dot positions: Step 1 = Top, Step 2 = Left, Step 3 = Bottom. Both meet in the bottom quadrant at Step 3. Thus, they coincide in Step 3. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q24. (Medium) ⚠️ A geometric figure series shows: 3-sided triangle with 1 inner dot, 4-sided square with 2 inner dots, 5-sided pentagon with 3 inner dots. What comes next in the series?
(A) 5-sided pentagon with 4 inner dots (B) 6-sided hexagon with 5 inner dots (C) 7-sided heptagon with 4 inner dots (D) 6-sided hexagon with 4 inner dots

Answer: (D) 6-sided hexagon with 4 inner dots

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Number of sides increases by +1 (3 → 4 → 5 → 6 = hexagon). Number of inner dots is always (sides - 2) or increases by +1 (1 → 2 → 3 → 4). Candidates often miscount dots or confuse (sides - 1) with (sides - 2), incorrectly choosing 5 dots. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q25. (Medium) An outer square rotates 45° clockwise at each step, while an inner triangle rotates 90° anticlockwise at each step. After 4 steps, what is the net orientation of each compared to their starting positions?
(A) Outer square 90°, inner triangle 180° (B) Outer square 180°, inner triangle 360° (original) (C) Both rotate 180° (D) Outer square 360°, inner triangle 180°

Answer: (B) Outer square 180°, inner triangle 360° (original)

Solution:
Outer square: 4 × 45° = 180° clockwise rotation. Inner triangle: 4 × 90° = 360° anticlockwise rotation (returns to original position). Hence, outer square has rotated 180° and inner triangle is back to its original orientation. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: RRB Group D
