📝 Practice Set 1/6 — Figure Series & Classification (Foundation)

Question 1 (Easy)
Type: Figure Series
Problem Figures:
(i) Arrow pointing up
(ii) Arrow pointing right
(iii) Arrow pointing down
(iv) ?

Options:
(A) Arrow pointing left
(B) Arrow pointing up
(C) Arrow pointing right
(D) Arrow pointing down

Answer: (A)
Solution: The arrow rotates 90° clockwise in each step. Up → Right → Down → Left.
Source: SSC CHSL 2022

Question 21 (Medium) – ⚠️ Contains Examiner's Trap
Type: Figure Series (Alternating Pattern)
Problem Figures:
(i) Circle with a horizontal diameter line; no other elements.
(ii) Circle empty (no line) but a small square attached outside at the top-right.
(iii) Circle with a vertical diameter line; no square.
(iv) Circle empty, small square outside at bottom-right.
(v) ?

Options:
(A) Circle with horizontal diameter line, no square
(B) Circle empty, small square at bottom-left
(C) Circle with vertical line
(D) Circle empty, small square at top-left

Answer: (A)
Solution: Odd positions (1,3,5) show a circle with a line that rotates 90° CW each time (horizontal → vertical → horizontal). Even positions (2,4) show only an empty circle with a square that moves clockwise around the outside (top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left would be next if even, but position 5 is odd). The trap: students may continue the even pattern and pick (B) or (D).
Source: SSC CGL 2023, Shift 2 (adapted)

Question 22 (Medium) – ⚠️ Contains Examiner's Trap
Type: Classification
Problem Figures:
(a) A fully closed square
(b) A square that looks closed but has an almost invisible 1 mm gap in one side
(c) A closed triangle
(d) A closed circle
(e) A closed pentagon

Options (Odd):
(A) a (B) b (C) c (D) e

Answer: (B)
Solution: All figures except (b) are completely closed. The tiny opening in (b) makes it an open figure. The trap: the gap is nearly imperceptible; students who do not examine carefully will mistake (b) for a closed square like (a).

Question 23 (Medium)
Type: Figure Series (Combined Pattern)
Problem Figures:
A square frame with a dot and an arrow.
(i) Dot at top-left corner; arrow in the centre pointing up.
(ii) Dot at top-right; arrow pointing right.
(iii) Dot at bottom-right; arrow pointing down.
(iv) Dot at bottom-left; arrow pointing left.
(v) ?

Options:
(A) Dot top-left, arrow up
(B) Dot top-left, arrow right
(C) Dot top-right, arrow up
(D) Dot bottom-left, arrow down

Question 24 (Medium)
Type: Figure Series
Problem Figures:
A circle divided into four quadrants.
(i) Only the top-right quadrant is shaded black.

(iii) Top-right, bottom-right, and bottom-left are shaded.
(iv) ?

Options:
(A) All four quadrants shaded (completely black)
(B) The same as (iii)
(C) Top-left and top-right shaded only

Question 25 (Medium) – ⚠️ Contains Examiner's Trap
Type: Figure Series (Alternating Rotation & Mirror)
Problem Figures:
(i) The letter "F" in standard upright position.
(ii) The mirror image of "F" (backward F, facing left).
(iii) "F" rotated 90° clockwise (pointing right, but still a normal F orientation).
(iv) Mirror image of (iii) – backward F pointing left.
(v) ?

Options:
(A) "F" rotated 180° (upside down)
(B) Mirror of (iv) which would be "F" rotated 90° again?
(C) "F" pointing right again
(D) Mirror of upside-down F (backward F upside down)

Answer: (A)
Solution: Odd positions (1,3,5) follow a rotation sequence: 0° → 90° → 180°. Even positions (2,4) are mirror images of the preceding odd. (v) is odd, so it continues the rotation to 180° (upside down). The trap: students might assume an alternating mirror pattern without noticing the progressive rotation, and pick a mirror option.
Source: SSC CGL 2024 (pattern)
