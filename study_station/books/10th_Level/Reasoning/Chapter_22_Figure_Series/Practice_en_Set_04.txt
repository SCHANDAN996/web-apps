Question 76 (Medium) – ⚠️ Contains Examiner's Trap (Alternating Rotation & Mirror)
Type: Figure Series
Problem Figures:
(i) A right-pointing arrow (→).
(ii) A left-pointing arrow (←) – mirror image of (i).
(iii) A down-pointing arrow (↓) – 90° clockwise rotation from right.
(iv) An up-pointing arrow (↑) – mirror image of (iii).
(v) ?

Options:
(A) Right-pointing arrow (→)
(B) Left-pointing arrow (←)
(C) Down-pointing arrow (↓)
(D) Up-pointing arrow (↑)

Answer: (B)
Solution: Odd positions (1,3,5): arrow rotates 90° clockwise each time (right → down → left). Even positions (2,4): mirror image of the preceding odd figure. Position 5 is odd, so continue the rotation: down → left. The trap: students may assume the mirror pattern continues and choose (A) or (D).
Source: SSC CGL 2022, Shift 2
