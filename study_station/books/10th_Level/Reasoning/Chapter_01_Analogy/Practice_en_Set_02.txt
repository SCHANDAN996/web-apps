26. Sculptor : Chisel :: Author : ?
(a) Book (b) Pen (c) Publisher (d) Ink
Answer: (b)
Solution: A Book is the author's output, not the tool, so it is the tempting trap. A Sculptor uses a Chisel as a tool, and an Author uses a Pen as a tool, so the answer is Pen.

27. 7 : 50 :: 9 : ?
(a) 80 (b) 82 (c) 64 (d) 78
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 80, which comes from 9² − 1. The pattern is n² + 1: 7 × 7 + 1 = 50, so 9 × 9 + 1 = 82, and the answer is 82.
Source: PYQ-style

28. ACE : ZXV :: BDF : ?
(a) WYU (b) YUW (c) YWU (d) UWY
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each letter is replaced by its opposite (positions summing to 27): A→Z, C→X, E→V. So B→Y, D→W, F→U, giving YWU, and the answer is YWU.

34. AB : YZ :: KL : ?
(a) NO (b) PQ (c) XY (d) OP
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is PQ, which just moves letters forward. The pattern is opposite letters written in reverse order: A→Z, B→Y gives ZY, reversed to YZ. K→P, L→O gives PO, reversed to OP, so the answer is OP.
