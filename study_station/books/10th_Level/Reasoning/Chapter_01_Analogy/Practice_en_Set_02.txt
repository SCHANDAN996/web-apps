26. Sculptor : Chisel :: Author : ?
(a) Pen (b) Book (c) Publisher (d) Ink
Answer: (a)
Solution: Step 1: A Sculptor uses a Chisel as a tool to create sculptures. Step 2: An Author uses a Pen (writing instrument) as a tool to create written work. Examiner’s Trap: (A) Book is the output, not the tool. The relationship is tool-user, not product-creator.
Source: PYQ-style

27. 7 : 50 :: 9 : ?
(a) 80 (b) 82 (c) 64 (d) 78
Answer: (b)
Solution: Step 1: 7 × 7 + 1 = 49 + 1 = 50. Step 2: 9 × 9 + 1 = 81 + 1 = 82. Trap: (A) 80 = 9²-1, (C) 64 = 8². The pattern is n²+1. Therefore, the correct answer is 82.
Source: PYQ-style
