126. Architect : Blueprint :: Composer : ?
(a) Score (b) Symphony (c) Orchestra (d) Concert
Answer: (a)
Solution: Step 1: An Architect uses a Blueprint as the detailed written plan for construction. Step 2: A Composer uses a Score as the detailed written plan for a musical performance. “Symphony” is the finished work. Examiner’s Trap: (A) Symphony is the output, not the plan.

127. 4 : 68 :: 5 : ?
(a) 125 (b) 130 (c) 120 (d) 135
Answer: (b)
Solution: Step 1: 4³ + 4 = 64 + 4 = 68. Step 2: 5³ + 5 = 125 + 5 = 130. Examiner’s Trap: (A) 125 is 5³, ignoring the addition. Therefore, the correct answer is 130.
