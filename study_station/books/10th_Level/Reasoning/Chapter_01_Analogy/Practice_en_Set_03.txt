51. Fisherman : Boat :: Pilot : ?
(a) Sky (b) Aeroplane (c) Runway (d) Airport
Answer: (b)
Solution: A Fisherman uses a Boat as the vehicle of his work; similarly a Pilot uses an Aeroplane. Airport is tempting, but it is the workplace, not the tool, so the answer is Aeroplane.
Source: PYQ-style

52. 2 : 10 :: 3 : ?
(a) 30 (b) 9 (c) 12 (d) 28
Answer: (a)
Solution: The pattern is n³ + n: 2³ + 2 = 10, so 3³ + 3 = 30. The trap is 12, which is just 3 × 4 and ignores the cube, so the answer is 30.
Source: PYQ-style

53. BM : CN :: DO : ?
(a) EO (b) PE (c) EP (d) FP
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each letter moves one step forward: B→C and M→N, giving CN. Likewise D→E and O→P, so the answer is EP.
