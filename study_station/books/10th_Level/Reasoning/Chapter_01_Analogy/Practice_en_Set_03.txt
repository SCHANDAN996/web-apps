51. Fisherman : Boat :: Pilot : ?
(a) Aeroplane (b) Airport (c) Runway (d) Sky
Answer: (a)
Solution: Step 1: A Fisherman uses a Boat as a tool/vehicle to perform his work (fishing). Step 2: A Pilot uses an Aeroplane to perform his work (flying). "Airport" is the workplace, not the tool. Examiner’s Trap: Option (B) Airport seems plausible as a place, but the relationship is worker-tool, not worker-workplace.
Source: PYQ-style

52. 2 : 10 :: 3 : ?
(a) 28 (b) 30 (c) 12 (d) 9
Answer: (b)
Solution: Step 1: Pattern: n³ + n. 2³ + 2 = 8 + 2 = 10. Step 2: 3³ + 3 = 27 + 3 = 30. Trap: (C) 12 is 3×4, (D) 9 is 3². The correct pattern requires both cube and addition. Therefore, the correct answer is 30.
Source: PYQ-style

60. Z : A :: Y : ?
(a) C (b) B (c) X (d) A
Answer: (b)
Solution: Step 1: Z is the last letter (26th), A is the first letter (1st). They are extreme opposites. Step 2: Y is the 2nd last (25th), so the 2nd first is B (2nd). The relationship is positional symmetry from ends. Examiner’s Trap: (D) A might be chosen if thinking Z+1=A, then Y+1=Z, but that pattern fails the pair.
