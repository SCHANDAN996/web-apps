🪄 Mnemonics for Analogy – Fun & Unforgettable

📦 Box 1: WING – The Four-Feathered Bird
Imagine a chubby bird with four colourful feathers: W, I, N, G.
Whenever an analogy question lands on your paper, the bird whispers:
“W for Word, I for Integer, N for aN alphabet, G for General Knowledge.”
Classify instantly, and you'll never fumble.

📦 Box 2: The 27 Love Story – “A-Z Forever”
A and Z are madly in love. Their secret code? Their positions always add up to 27.
If you need the opposite letter, just shout “27 minus your number!”
*Example: A(1)+Z(26)=27. P(16)+?=27 → 11(K).*
Love always sums up.

📦 Box 3: The “Because-Therefore” Tango
This is a dance.
Step 1: Look at the first pair and say “Because A... therefore B.”
Step 2: Grab your partner C, and lead with the same because sentence.
If the option fits smoothly like a perfect dance move, you've found your D.
If it stumbles, it's the wrong partner.

📦 Box 4: Tighten the Knot – The Rope Trick
Two answers look right? You’re tied in a loose knot.
Pull the rope tighter – add one more specific detail to the relationship.
Example: “Fish lives in water” → “Fish lives in water, its natural home.”
Now only “Bird lives in sky” fits; “nest” slips out.
Tighten until only one option remains.

📦 Box 5: 15-45 Skip Rope
Analogy is a jump rope game.
If you can’t catch the rhythm in 15 seconds, jump out.
If you’re still tangled after 45 seconds, mark your best guess and run.
The bell rings at 1 minute – never be caught inside.

📦 Box 6: SQUARE-CUBE Factory
In Number Analogies, imagine a factory that makes square blocks (n²) and cubic boxes (n³).
First check: Are the numbers coming from the Square machine or the Cube machine?
Then check if some extra decoration (+1, –1) was added.
*6²=36 → 37 means Square machine + a sticker of 1.*

📦 Box 7: SOS – Same Order or Swapped?
In Letter Analogies, when opposites are at play,
ask yourself: Same Order or Swapped?
AB → ZY? A→Z, B→Y → ZY. Same order.
AB → YZ? Opposites then reversed. Swapped order!
SOS saves you from drowning in letters.

📦 Box 8: The Twin Trap – Two-Faced Options
Beware! The examiner has evil twins among the options.
They look almost identical – like “School” and “Classroom”.
Remember: only the whole twin carries the relationship, not the half.
Spot the difference like a detective, and pick the one that matches the given pair's level.

📦 Box 9: GK Quick Exit Door
If the analogy is about capitals, authors, or inventions,
and your brain flashes a big “?”,
don't play the guessing game.
Exit through the GK Quick Exit Door – skip, save time, stay alive.

📦 Box 10: Reverse Gear Check
After you pick an answer, put your mental car in reverse.
Check: If A → B, does C → your answer exactly?
No extra noise, no mismatched detail.
If it drives smoothly both ways, you’re on the right road.

📦 Box 11: Maths before Madness
Numbers can drive you crazy.
Before you invent complex formulas, try the basics:
+1, –1, ×2, ÷2, square, cube.
Most number analogies are simple patterns wearing a scary mask.
Unmask them with simple maths.

📦 Box 12: The Sherlock Scan
Channel your inner Sherlock.
Give the first pair a 5-second intense stare.
What is their relationship? Worker-tool? Cause-effect? Part-whole?
Once you deduce it, the second pair becomes elementary.
Eliminate options that don't fit the same story.

⚡ Skip Strategy – Know When to Run

15-Second No-Clue Rule: If within 15 seconds you can’t even form a basic relationship sentence, mark the question and jump to the next. Some battles aren't worth your precious time.

45-Second Twin-Trap Rule: If after 45 seconds of tightening the knot, two options still look equally good, pick the one that feels most direct, flag it for review, and move on. Never spend more than 1 minute on a single analogy question.

Zero-Knowledge GK Exit: If the analogy depends on a fact you simply don't know (e.g., a rare capital or inventor), don't ponder. Guessing is a gamble; skipping saves your accuracy.
