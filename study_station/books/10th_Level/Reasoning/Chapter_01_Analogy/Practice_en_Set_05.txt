101. Cobbler : Leather :: Potter : ?
(a) Soil (b) Wheel (c) Clay (d) Pot
Answer: (c)
Solution: A cobbler works with leather as the raw material, so a potter works with clay as the raw material. The tempting option "Pot" is the finished product, not the raw material, so the answer is Clay.

102. 6 : 42 :: 8 : ?
(a) 72 (b) 56 (c) 64 (d) 40
Answer: (a)
Solution: The rule is n × (n + 1): 6 × 7 = 42, so 8 × 9 = 72. The trap is 56, which comes from wrongly multiplying 8 by 7 instead of by 9, so the answer is 72.

103. AZ : BY :: CX : ?
(a) DW (b) WD (c) EV (d) XD
Answer: (a)
Solution: A(1) pairs with Z(26) and B(2) with Y(25) — opposite letters summing to 27. C(3) pairs with X(24), and D(4) pairs with W(23), giving DW. The trap "WD" reverses the letter order, so the answer is DW.

122. 2 : 12 :: 3 : 36 :: 4 : ?

Solution: The rule is n × (n + n²): 2 × 6 = 12 and 3 × 12 = 36, so 4 × (4 + 16) = 4 × 20 = 80. The trap is 48, which wrongly assumes a fixed multiplier of 12, so the answer is 80.
