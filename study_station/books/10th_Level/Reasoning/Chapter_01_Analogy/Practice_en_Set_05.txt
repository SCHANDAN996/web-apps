Solution: Step 1: A Cobbler works with Leather as the raw material. Step 2: A Potter works with Clay as the raw material. “Pot” is the finished product. Examiner’s Trap: (C) Pot is the output; the relationship is worker–raw material.

121. 2 : 6 :: 4 : ?
(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 18 (d) 60
Answer: (a)
Solution: Step 1: Pattern: n² + n. 2² + 2 = 4 + 2 = 6. Step 2: 4² + 4 = 16 + 4 = 20. Examiner’s Trap: (B) 24 is 4! (factorial), a tempting but wrong pattern. Therefore, the correct answer is 20.
Source: PYQ-style

122. 2 : 12 :: 3 : 36 :: 4 : ?
(a) 48 (b) 80 (c) 100 (d) 60
Answer: (b)
Solution: Step 1: 2 × (2 + 4) = 2 × 6 = 12; 3 × (3 + 9) = 3 × 12 = 36. The addition is n²: 2+2²=6, 3+3²=12. So pattern: n × (n + n²) = n² + n³. Step 2: 4 × (4 + 16) = 4 × 20 = 80. Examiner’s Trap: (A) 48 is 4 × 12, assuming a constant multiplier of 12 from 3:36, but the multiplier increases. Therefore, the correct answer is 80.
Source: PYQ-style

124. 3 : 24 :: 5 : ?
