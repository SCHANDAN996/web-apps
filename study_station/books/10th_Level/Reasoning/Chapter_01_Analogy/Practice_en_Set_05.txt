Q122. 2 : 12 :: 3 : 36 :: 4 : ?
(A) 48 (B) 80 (C) 100 (D) 60
Answer: (B) 80
Solution:

Step 1: 2 × (2 + 4) = 2 × 6 = 12; 3 × (3 + 9) = 3 × 12 = 36. The addition is n²: 2+2²=6, 3+3²=12. So pattern: n × (n + n²) = n² + n³.

Step 2: 4 × (4 + 16) = 4 × 20 = 80.

Examiner’s Trap: (A) 48 is 4 × 12, assuming a constant multiplier of 12 from 3:36, but the multiplier increases.
