PYQ Analysis: Analogy (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Analogy is a guaranteed scorer: 2–4 questions appear in almost every exam. The most common types asked are letter analogy, number analogy, and worker–tool relations. The trap is almost always a "near-miss" option or a reversed order.

Q1. Doctor : Patient :: Lawyer : ?
(a) Court (b) Client (c) Judge (d) Case
Answer: (b) Client
Solution: A doctor serves a patient; a lawyer serves a client. Both show a professional and the person they serve.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. Pen : Write :: Knife : ?
(a) Sharp (b) Kitchen (c) Cut (d) Steel
Answer: (c) Cut
Solution: A pen is used to write; a knife is used to cut. This is a tool–function relationship.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. 7 : 49 :: 9 : ?
(a) 63 (b) 72 (c) 81 (d) 18
Answer: (c) 81
Solution: 7² = 49, so 9² = 81. The rule is squaring.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q4. Bird : Nest :: Bee : ?
(a) Honey (b) Hive (c) Flower (d) Sting
Answer: (b) Hive
Solution: A bird lives in a nest; a bee lives in a hive. This is a creature–home relationship.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q5. AZ : BY :: CX : ?
(a) DV (b) DW (c) EW (d) DX
Answer: (b) DW
Solution: First letters go forward (A→B→C→D); second letters go backward (Z→Y→X→W). So CX → DW.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. Thirst : Water :: Hunger : ?
(a) Stomach (b) Eat (c) Food (d) Weakness
Answer: (c) Food
Solution: Thirst is satisfied by water; hunger is satisfied by food. This is a need–remedy relationship.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q7. 5 : 24 :: 8 : ?
(a) 60 (b) 63 (c) 64 (d) 65
Answer: (b) 63
Solution: 5² − 1 = 24, so 8² − 1 = 63. The rule is (square − 1).
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Carpenter : Saw :: Painter : ?
(a) Wall (b) Colour (c) Brush (d) Canvas
Answer: (c) Brush
Solution: A carpenter works with a saw; a painter works with a brush. This is a worker–tool relationship.
Source: RRB Group D

Q9. January : March :: April : ?
(a) May (b) June (c) July (d) August
Answer: (b) June
Solution: From January, skipping one month gives March; from April, skipping one month gives June (gap of 2 months).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. Day : Night :: Victory : ?
(a) War (b) Defeat (c) Win (d) Army
Answer: (b) Defeat
Solution: Day and night are opposites; victory and defeat are opposites. This is an antonym relationship.
Source: SSC MTS

Q11. Hand : Glove :: Foot : ?
(a) Toe (b) Leg (c) Sock (d) Walk
Answer: (c) Sock
Solution: A glove covers a hand; a sock covers a foot. This is an object–covering relationship.
Source: Bank PO

Q12. CUP : LIP :: BIRD : ?
(a) BUSH (b) GRASS (c) FOREST (d) BEAK
Answer: (d) BEAK
Solution: A cup touches the lip while drinking; a bird uses its beak to touch/pick food. Both link an object to the body part it meets in use.
Source: SSC CGL
