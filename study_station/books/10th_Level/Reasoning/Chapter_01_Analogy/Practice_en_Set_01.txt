1. Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?
(a) School (b) Student (c) Book (d) Classroom
Answer: (a)
Solution: Step 1: A Doctor works in a Hospital (workplace relationship). Step 2: Applying the same relationship, a Teacher works in a School. Trap: "Classroom" is a part of school, not the whole workplace.
