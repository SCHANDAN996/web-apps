1. Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?
(a) School (b) Student (c) Book (d) Classroom
Answer: (a)
Solution: A Doctor works in a Hospital, so the relationship is person : workplace. The trap is "Classroom", which is only a part of the workplace, not the whole institution. A Teacher works in a School, so the answer is School.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Fish : Water :: Bird : ?

3. 4 : 16 :: 5 : ?
(a) 25 (b) 30 (c) 20 (d) 10
Answer: (a)
Solution: The relationship is number : its square, since 4² = 16. The trap is 20, which comes from simple multiplication by 5 instead of squaring. Since 5² = 25, the answer is 25.

7. 8 : 27 :: 64 : ?

Answer: (c)
Solution: 8 = 2³ and 27 = 3³, so the relationship is consecutive cubes. The trap is 100, which is a square, not a cube. Since 64 = 4³, the next cube is 5³ = 125, so the answer is 125.
