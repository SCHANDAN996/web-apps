76. Knife : Cut :: Axe : ?
(a) Chop (b) Wood (c) Sharp (d) Tool
Answer: (a)
Solution: Step 1: A Knife is primarily used to Cut (specific action). Step 2: An Axe is primarily used to Chop (specific heavy cutting action). "Wood" is the object, not the action. Examiner’s Trap: (B) Wood may be selected if thinking "object cut", but the relationship is tool to its specific action.

77. 4 : 20 :: 5 : ?
(a) 25 (b) 30 (c) 26 (d) 24
Answer: (b)
Solution: Step 1: 4 × (4 + 1) = 4 × 5 = 20. Step 2: 5 × (5 + 1) = 5 × 6 = 30. Trap: (B) 25 is 5², a common but incorrect square pattern. Therefore, the correct answer is 30.
