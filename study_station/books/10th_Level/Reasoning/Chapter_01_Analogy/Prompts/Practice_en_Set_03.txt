Set 3/6: Analogy MCQs (Foundation)

Instructions: Choose the option that best completes the relationship.

Q51. Fisherman : Boat :: Pilot : ?
(A) Aeroplane (B) Airport (C) Runway (D) Sky
Answer: (A) Aeroplane
Solution:

Step 1: A Fisherman uses a Boat as a tool/vehicle to perform his work (fishing).

Step 2: A Pilot uses an Aeroplane to perform his work (flying). "Airport" is the workplace, not the tool.

Examiner’s Trap: Option (B) Airport seems plausible as a place, but the relationship is worker-tool, not worker-workplace.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

Q52. 2 : 10 :: 3 : ?
(A) 30 (B) 28 (C) 12 (D) 9
Answer: (A) 30
Solution:

Step 1: Pattern: n³ + n. 2³ + 2 = 8 + 2 = 10.

Step 2: 3³ + 3 = 27 + 3 = 30.

Trap: (C) 12 is 3×4, (D) 9 is 3². The correct pattern requires both cube and addition.
Source: IBPS PO 2022

Q53. BM : CN :: DO : ?
(A) EP (B) PE (C) EO (D) FP
Answer: (A) EP
Solution:

Step 1: Each letter is moved forward by 1: B→C, M→N → CN.

Step 2: D→E, O→P → EP.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

Q54. Scissors : Cut :: Needle : ?
(A) Stitch (B) Pierce (C) Sew (D) Thread
Answer: (C) Sew
Solution:

Step 1: The primary function of Scissors is to Cut.

Step 2: The primary function of a Needle is to Sew (joining fabric). "Pierce" is an action but not the functional purpose.

Examiner’s Trap: (A) Stitch is the result of sewing, (B) Pierce is a physical action; the functional verb is sew.
Source: SSC GD 2019

Q58. Ignite : Extinguish :: Start : ?
(A) Stop (B) Begin (C) Continue (D) Halt
Answer: (A) Stop
Solution:

Step 1: Ignite and Extinguish are antonyms (opposite actions).

Step 2: Start and Stop are antonyms. (Halt is synonym of stop, but stop is the direct opposite of start.)
Source: IBPS Clerk 2021

Q59. 8 : 24 :: 9 : ?
(A) 27 (B) 18 (C) 36 (D) 72
Answer: (A) 27
Solution:

Step 1: 8 × 3 = 24.

Step 2: 9 × 3 = 27.
Source: RRB ALP 2021

Q60. Z : A :: Y : ?
(A) B (B) C (C) X (D) A
Answer: (A) B
Solution:

Step 1: Z is the last letter (26th), A is the first letter (1st). They are extreme opposites.

Step 2: Y is the 2nd last (25th), so the 2nd first is B (2nd). The relationship is positional symmetry from ends.

Examiner’s Trap: (D) A might be chosen if thinking Z+1=A, then Y+1=Z, but that pattern fails the pair.
Source: SSC MTS 2020

Q61. Cow : Calf :: Horse : ?
(A) Foal (B) Colt (C) Pony (D) Mare
Answer: (A) Foal
Solution:

Step 1: Cow is an adult animal, Calf is its young offspring.

Step 2: Horse's young is called a Foal. "Colt" specifically refers to a young male horse, not the general term.
Source: IBPS PO 2019

Q62. 10 : 99 :: 11 : ?
(A) 120 (B) 121 (C) 110 (D) 100
Answer: (A) 120
Solution:

Step 1: 10² – 1 = 100 – 1 = 99.

Step 2: 11² – 1 = 121 – 1 = 120.

Trap: (B) 121 is the square without subtracting 1.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

Q63. AB : YZ :: CD : ?
(A) WX (B) XW (C) XY (D) VW
Answer: (A) WX
Solution:

Step 1: AB: A→Z (opposite), B→Y → ZY, reversed to YZ.

Step 2: CD: C→X, D→W → XW, reversed → WX.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

Q64. Toothpaste : Brush :: Soap : ?
(A) Wash (B) Water (C) Body (D) Sponge
Answer: (D) Sponge
Solution:

Step 1: Toothpaste is typically applied using a Brush.

Step 2: Soap is typically applied using a Sponge or washcloth. "Wash" is the action, not the applicator.
Source: RRB NTPC 2019

Q68. Author : Novel :: Poet : ?
(A) Poem (B) Verse (C) Rhyme (D) Song
Answer: (A) Poem
Solution:

Step 1: An Author writes a Novel (complete creative work).

Step 2: A Poet writes a Poem (complete creative work). "Verse" is a part of a poem, not the whole work.

Examiner’s Trap: (B) Verse is a common misdirection; the relationship is creator-complete work, not creator-component.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

Q69. 3 : 28 :: 4 : ?
(A) 65 (B) 64 (C) 63 (D) 32
Answer: (A) 65
Solution:

Step 1: 3³ + 1 = 27 + 1 = 28.

Step 2: 4³ + 1 = 64 + 1 = 65.

Trap: (B) 64 is the cube without adding 1.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

Q70. XYZ : CBA :: PQR : ?
(A) KJI (B) JIK (C) IJK (D) KJL
Answer: (A) KJI
Solution:

Step 1: XYZ → opposite letters: X→C, Y→B, Z→A → CBA.

Step 2: P→K, Q→J, R→I → KJI.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

Q71. Fire : Ashes :: Event : ?
(A) Memory (B) Result (C) Incident (D) Outcome
Answer: (A) Memory
Solution:

Step 1: Fire leaves behind Ashes (a lasting trace).

Step 2: An Event leaves behind a Memory (a lasting mental trace). "Result" is a direct consequence, but not a lingering trace.

Examiner’s Trap: (B) Result seems plausible, but the relationship is cause-remnant, not cause-effect.
Source: UPSC CSAT 2019
