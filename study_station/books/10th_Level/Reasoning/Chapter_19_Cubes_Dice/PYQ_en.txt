PYQ Analysis: Cubes & Dice (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Cubes & dice gives 1–3 questions per exam. Painted-cube counts and opposite-face types are most common. The trap is forgetting sum 7 and the (n−2) formula.

Q1. A painted cube is cut into small cubes. How many small cubes have 3 painted faces?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 12
Answer: (c) 8
Solution: The 3-painted-face pieces are the 8 corners.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. On a standard die, which number is opposite 1?
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 4
Answer: (c) 6
Solution: Opposite faces sum to 7, so 7 − 1 = 6.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. A 3×3×3 painted cube is cut. How many pieces have exactly 1 painted face?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: 6 × (n−2)² = 6 × 1² = 6.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. On a standard die, opposite faces sum to:
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 7
Solution: 1+6, 2+5, 3+4 all equal 7.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q5. In a 3×3×3 painted cube, how many small cubes have no painted face?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 1
Solution: (n−2)³ = 1³ = 1.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. On a standard die, which number is opposite 3?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: 7 − 3 = 4.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. A 4×4×4 painted cube is cut. How many pieces have 3 painted faces?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 24
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: Corners are always 8, whatever n is.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. In a 3×3×3 painted cube, how many pieces have 2 painted faces?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 6 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: 12 × (n−2) = 12 × 1 = 12.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. On a standard die, which number is opposite 2?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (c) 5
Solution: 7 − 2 = 5.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. How many small cubes are there in a 3×3×3 cube?
(a) 9 (b) 18 (c) 27 (d) 36
Answer: (c) 27
Solution: n³ = 3³ = 27.
Source: SSC CGL
