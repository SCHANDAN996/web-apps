⚠️ 3. Common Examiner Traps in Previous Years
1. The Adjacent Face Trap: Assuming that faces visible together can be opposite. Two faces sharing a visible boundary can never be opposite to each other.
2. The "At Least" Painted Trap: In painted cube questions, confusing "at least two faces painted" with "exactly two faces painted". "At least two" requires adding both 2-face painted edge cubes and 3-face painted corner cubes.
3. Open Dice Alternating Rule Trap: Forgetting that in an unfolded dice net, opposite faces lie in the same straight line separated by exactly one square. Diagonal steps do not form opposite pairs.
4. Non-Standard Dice Trap: Assuming opposite faces always sum to 7. Unless the question explicitly states "standard dice", numbers can be arranged in any configuration.
