Q76. (Medium) Two positions of a dice are shown. Position 1: top 5, front 3, right 1. Position 2: top 5, front 6, right 4. Which number is opposite to 5?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 3 (D) 4

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
Common top face 5. Rotating clockwise from 5: Position 1 gives 3 and 1; Position 2 gives 6 and 4. Hence, 3 ↔ 6 and 1 ↔ 4. The remaining number 2 is opposite to 5. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q77. (Medium) A non-standard dice has opposite pairs 1-4, 2-6, 3-5. Which of the following views is possible?
(A) Top 1, Front 4, Right 5 (B) Top 1, Front 2, Right 3 (C) Top 2, Front 1, Right 6 (D) Top 3, Front 5, Right 6

Answer: (B) Top 1, Front 2, Right 3

Solution:
Opposite faces cannot be visible simultaneously. In option B, 1 and 4 appear together (impossible). In C, 2 and 6 appear together (impossible). In D, 3 and 5 appear together (impossible). Option A has no opposite pair visible together, so it is possible. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q78. (Medium) The top view of a solid is a 3×2 grid (3 columns, 2 rows). Row 1 column heights: 1, 3, 2; Row 2: 2, 1, 1. What is the total number of cubes?

Answer: (C) 10

Solution:
Total cubes = sum of column heights = 1 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 10. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q79. (Medium) ⚠️ A painted cube of side 6 cm is cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many small cubes have at least two faces painted?
(A) 48 (B) 72 (C) 64 (D) 56

Answer: (D) 56

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: For n = 6, cubes with exactly 2 faces painted = 12 × (6 - 2) = 48. Cubes with 3 faces painted = 8. 'At least two faces painted' includes both 2-face and 3-face painted cubes: 48 + 8 = 56. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q80. (Medium) An open dice cross net has top 5, middle row from left to right: 1, 3, 6, 2, and bottom 4. When folded, which number is opposite to 3?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 6 (D) 4

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
Top 5 ↔ bottom 4. In the middle row, alternating squares are opposite: 1 ↔ 6 and 3 ↔ 2. Therefore, 3 is opposite to 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: RRB Group D

Q81. (Medium) Two views of a dice: View 1 has top 4, front 6, right 2. View 2 has top 5, front 6, right 3. Which number is opposite to 6?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 3 (D) 4

Answer: (B) 1

Solution:
Face 6 is common on the front in both views. Adjacent faces are 4, 2 (View 1) and 5, 3 (View 2). Thus, 2, 3, 4, 5 are adjacent to 6. The only remaining face 1 is opposite to 6. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CGL

Q82. (Medium) A painted cube is cut into 125 small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one face painted?
(A) 36 (B) 72 (C) 54 (D) 96

Answer: (C) 54

Solution:
For n = 5, cubes with exactly one face painted = 6 × (n - 2)² = 6 × (5 - 2)² = 6 × 9 = 54. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q84. (Medium) In a standard dice, 2 is on top and 3 is in front. Which number is on the right face?
(A) 6 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 1

Answer: (A) 6

Solution:
In a standard dice, 2 is opposite 5 and 3 is opposite 4. The remaining adjacent faces are 1 and 6. In a conventional right-handed standard dice, with 2 on top and 3 in front, the right face is 6. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q85. (Medium) ⚠️ In an open dice net, four squares are in a row from left to right: 1, 2, 3, 4. Square 5 is above square 1, and square 6 is below square 4. When folded, which number is opposite to 1?

Q94. (Medium) A painted cube of side 3 cm is cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many small cubes have at least one face painted?
(A) 25 (B) 24 (C) 26 (D) 27

Q96. (Medium) ⚠️ The front view of a solid shows column heights 3, 1, 2 from left to right. Top view is a 3×2 grid: Row 1: 3, 1, 2; Row 2: 1, 2, 1. How many cubes are completely hidden from the front view?
(A) 3 (B) 2 (C) 1 (D) 4

Answer: (A) 3

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Col 1: front is 3, back is 1 → 1 cube hidden behind. Col 2: front is 1, back is 2 → bottom cube hidden behind (1). Col 3: front is 2, back is 1 → 1 cube hidden behind. Total hidden cubes = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL
