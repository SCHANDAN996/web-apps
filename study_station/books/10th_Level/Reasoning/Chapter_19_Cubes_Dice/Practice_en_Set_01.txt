Q21. (Medium) In an open dice net, four squares are in a horizontal row: 1, 2, 3, 4. Square 5 is above square 2, and square 6 is below square 3. Which face is opposite to 1?
(A) 3 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 6

Answer: (A) 3

Solution:
In a row of four squares in an open dice net, alternating squares are opposite: 1 ↔ 3, 2 ↔ 4, and lone outer squares 5 ↔ 6. Hence 1 is opposite to 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) Two positions of a single dice are shown. In Position 1, faces visible are 1, 2, 3. In Position 2, faces visible are 1, 4, 5. Which number is opposite to 1?
(A) 4 (B) 6 (C) 5 (D) 2

Answer: (B) 6

Solution:
Face 1 is adjacent to 2, 3 (Position 1) and 4, 5 (Position 2). The four adjacent faces to 1 are 2, 3, 4, 5. The only remaining face opposite to 1 is 6. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q23. (Medium) Two positions of a dice are shown. In Position 1, faces visible are 2, 3, 5. In Position 2, faces visible are 3, 5, 6. Which number is opposite to 2?
(A) 4 (B) 1 (C) 6 (D) 5

Answer: (C) 6

Solution:
When two views of a dice have two common faces (3 and 5), the remaining non-common faces are opposite to each other. Therefore, 2 is opposite to 6. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q24. (Medium) ⚠️ A cube of side 4 cm is painted green on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have at least two faces painted green?
(A) 8 (B) 24 (C) 16 (D) 32

Answer: (D) 32

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: 'At least two faces painted' includes both cubes with 2 painted faces (edges) and cubes with 3 painted faces (corners). 2-face painted = 12 × (4 - 2) = 24. 3-face painted = 8. Total = 24 + 8 = 32 cubes. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q25. (Medium) ⚠️ On a standard dice, the top face is 2 and the front face is 3. What number is on the bottom face?
(A) 4 (B) 5 (C) 1 (D) 6

Answer: (B) 5

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Bottom face is always directly opposite to the top face. On a standard dice, opposite faces always sum to 7. Since top is 2, bottom = 7 - 2 = 5, regardless of the front face. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: RRB Group D
