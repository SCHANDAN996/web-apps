26. Two positions of a dice are shown. Position 1: top 5, front 3, right 1. Position 2: top 5, front 6, right 4. Which number is opposite to 5?
(a) 4 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: Face 5 is common on top in both positions, so its four adjacent faces are 3, 1, 6 and 4. The tempting wrong options 1, 4 and 6 are all adjacent to 5, not opposite. The only remaining number is 2, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

27. ⚠️ In a standard dice, 3 is on top and 5 is in front. Which number is on the right face?
(a) 1 (b) 6 (c) Cannot be determined (d) 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is picking 1 or 6 directly. In a standard dice, 3 is opposite 4 and 5 is opposite 2, so the right face must be one of the remaining pair 1 and 6 — but the orientation does not fix which one. Hence the right face cannot be uniquely determined, so the answer is "Cannot be determined".
Source: PYQ-style

28. The top view of a solid is a 3×3 grid with column heights: Row 1: 1, 2, 1; Row 2: 2, 3, 2; Row 3: 1, 2, 1. What is the total number of cubes?
(a) 13 (b) 14 (c) 15 (d) 16
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total cubes = sum of all column heights = 1 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 15. Counting only the 9 columns (the tempting wrong approach) ignores the stacked cubes. So the answer is 15.

29. ⚠️ A cube of side 5 cm is painted on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have at least two faces painted?
(a) 36 (b) 52 (c) 48 (d) 44
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is counting only edge cubes (36) and forgetting the corners. "At least two" means 2-face cubes plus 3-face cubes: 12 × (5 − 2) = 36 edge cubes and 8 corner cubes, giving 36 + 8 = 44. So the answer is 44.
Source: PYQ-style

30. An open dice net has top face 5, middle row from left to right: 1, 3, 6, 2, and bottom face 4. When folded, which number is opposite to 3?
(a) 4 (b) 1 (c) 6 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: In a row of four squares, alternate squares become opposite faces: 1 ↔ 6 and 3 ↔ 2, while top 5 ↔ bottom 4. The tempting option 6 is actually opposite to 1, not 3. So the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

31. Two views of a dice are given: View 1 has top 2, front 6, right 3. View 2 has top 4, front 2, right 5. Which number is opposite to 2?
(a) 1 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: Face 2 is common, and its adjacent faces across the two views are 6, 3, 4 and 5. Options 4, 5 and 6 are tempting but all touch face 2. The only number not adjacent to 2 is 1, so the answer is 1.

32. The top view of a solid is a 3×2 grid with column heights: Row 1: 3, 2, 1; Row 2: 1, 1, 1. How many cubes are there in total?
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total cubes = 3 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 9. The tempting answer 6 comes from counting only the columns and ignoring the stacked cubes. So the answer is 9.

33. In a dice, opposite pairs are (1, 2), (3, 4) and (5, 6). Which of the following sets of three faces cannot meet at a single corner?
(a) 1, 3, 5 (b) 2, 3, 5 (c) 1, 4, 6 (d) 3, 4, 6
Answer: (d)
Solution: Opposite faces can never meet at one corner. The set 3, 4, 6 contains the opposite pair 3 and 4, so these three cannot share a vertex; the other three sets contain no opposite pair. So the answer is 3, 4, 6.
Source: PYQ-style

34. A cube of side 7 cm is painted on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have no face painted?
(a) 125 (b) 64 (c) 216 (d) 343
Answer: (a)
Solution: Unpainted cubes form the inner block: (n − 2)³ = (7 − 2)³ = 5³ = 125. The tempting answer 343 = 7³ counts all cubes, not just the unpainted inner ones. So the answer is 125.
Source: PYQ-style

35. ⚠️ In a standard dice, three visible faces are 1, 4 and 5. Which number is definitely on the bottom face?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) 2 (c) 6 (d) 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming 1 is on top, which would make the bottom 6. But any of 1, 4 or 5 could be the top face, so the bottom could be 6, 3 or 2 respectively. Hence the bottom face cannot be determined, so the answer is "Cannot be determined".

36. In a cross net, top is 3, middle row from left to right is 1, 6, 4, 5, and bottom is 2. When folded, which number is opposite to 6?
(a) 1 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: In the middle row of four, alternate squares are opposite: 1 ↔ 4 and 6 ↔ 5, while top 3 ↔ bottom 2. The tempting option 4 is opposite to 1, not 6. So the answer is 5.

Answer: (a)
Solution: Face 2 is common on the front, and its adjacent faces are 4, 1, 5 and 3. Options 1, 3 and 4 all touch face 2, so they cannot be opposite it. The only remaining number is 6, so the answer is 6.

38. ⚠️ The top view of a solid is a 2×2 grid: front row has left 3, right 1; back row has left 2, right 2. How many cubes are completely hidden when viewed directly from the front?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming all back-column cubes are hidden. Left column: front height 3 hides both back cubes (height 2). Right column: front height 1 hides only the bottom back cube; the upper back cube (height 2) is visible above. Hidden = 2 + 1 = 3, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

39. In a standard dice, 5 is on top and 4 is in front. Which number can be on the right face?
(a) 1 (b) 1 or 6 (c) 6 (d) 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Opposite to 5 is 2 and opposite to 4 is 3, leaving 1 and 6 for the left and right faces. Choosing only 1 or only 6 is the trap, since the dice can be oriented either way. So the right face can be 1 or 6, and the answer is "1 or 6".

40. A cube of side 4 cm is painted on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one face painted?
(a) 12 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 48
Answer: (b)
Solution: One-face cubes lie on face centres: 6 × (n − 2)² = 6 × (4 − 2)² = 6 × 4 = 24. The tempting answer 48 counts the two-face edge cubes instead. So the answer is 24.
Source: PYQ-style

41. In a net, four squares are in a row from left to right: 1, 2, 3, 4. A square 5 is attached above square 2, and a square 6 is attached below square 3. When folded, which number is opposite to 2?
(a) 1 (b) 6 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: In a row of four squares, alternate squares are opposite: 1 ↔ 3 and 2 ↔ 4, while the two attached squares 5 and 6 fold to opposite faces. The tempting option 3 is opposite to 1, not 2. So the answer is 4.

42. A standard dice has top 1, front 2 and right 3 (opposite pairs: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4). If it is rolled forward once (front becomes bottom), what will be the new top face?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 5
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rolling forward brings the back face to the top. The back face is opposite the front face 2, so back = 5. The tempting answer 2 is the old front, which goes to the bottom, not the top. So the answer is 5.
Source: PYQ-style

43. ⚠️ In a dice net, four squares are in a row from left to right: 2, 5, 1, 6. A square 3 is attached above square 5, and a square 4 is attached below square 5. When folded, which number is opposite to 2?
(a) 5 (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is treating the attached squares 3 and 4 as opposites of row squares. In the row of four, alternate squares are opposite: 2 ↔ 1 and 5 ↔ 6, while 3 ↔ 4. So 2 is opposite to 1, and the answer is 1.

44. A standard dice has top 1, front 2, right 3 (opposite pairs: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4). It is rolled right once (right becomes bottom), then rolled forward once (front becomes bottom). What is the final top face?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: After rolling right: top = 4 (old left), front = 2, back = 5. Rolling forward then brings the back face to the top, and the back is still 5. The tempting answer 4 is the top after only the first roll. So the answer is 5.

47. A cube of side 6 cm is painted on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two faces painted?
(a) 36 (b) 60 (c) 48 (d) 72
Answer: (c)
Solution: Two-face cubes lie on the 12 edges, excluding corners: 12 × (n − 2) = 12 × (6 − 2) = 48. The tempting answer 96 (or 72) comes from using the face formula instead of the edge formula. So the answer is 48.

48. A dice has opposite pairs (1, 4), (2, 5) and (3, 6). Initially top is 1, front is 2 and right is 3. If it is rolled forward twice (each time front becomes bottom), what will be the new front face?
(a) 1 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: Initially back = 5 (opposite front 2) and bottom = 4. First roll forward: front = 1, top = 5. Second roll forward: front = 5. The tempting answer 1 is the front after only one roll. So the answer is 5.

50. ⚠️ A painted 3×3×3 cube has 27 small cubes. If all 8 corner cubes are removed, how many of the remaining cubes have exactly two faces painted?
(a) 12 (b) 8 (c) 20 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is thinking that removing corners destroys the edge cubes. The two-face cubes are the 12 edge-middle cubes, 12 × (3 − 2) = 12, and corners were three-face cubes, so removing them changes nothing. So the answer is 12.
