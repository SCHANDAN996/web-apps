Q26. (Medium) Two positions of a dice are shown. Position 1: top 5, front 3, right 1. Position 2: top 5, front 6, right 4. Which number is opposite to 5?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 4 (D) 6

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
Face 5 is common on top in both positions. Moving clockwise from 5: in Position 1 we get 3 and 1; in Position 2 we get 6 and 4. Hence, 3 ↔ 6 and 1 ↔ 4. The remaining number 2 is opposite to 5. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q27. (Medium) ⚠️ In a standard dice, 3 is visible on top and 5 is in front. Which number is on the right face?
(A) 1 (B) Cannot be determined (C) 6 (D) 2

Answer: (B) Cannot be determined

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: In a standard dice, 3 is opposite 4, 5 is opposite 2, and 1 is opposite 6. The common adjacent faces to both 3 and 5 are 1 and 6. Without knowing the exact orientation, the right face can be either 1 or 6. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q28. (Medium) The top view of a solid is a 3×3 grid with heights: Row 1: 1, 2, 1; Row 2: 2, 3, 2; Row 3: 1, 2, 1. What is the total number of cubes?
(A) 13 (B) 14 (C) 15 (D) 16

Answer: (C) 15

Solution:
Total cubes = sum of all column heights = 1 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 15. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q29. (Medium) ⚠️ A painted cube of side 5 cm is cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many small cubes have at least two faces painted?
(A) 36 (B) 52 (C) 48 (D) 44

Answer: (D) 44

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: 'At least two faces painted' includes both cubes with 2 painted faces (edges) and cubes with 3 painted faces (corners). 2-face painted = 12 × (5 - 2) = 36. 3-face painted = 8. Total = 36 + 8 = 44. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q30. (Medium) An open dice net has top face 5, middle row from left to right: 1, 3, 6, 2, and bottom face 4. When folded, which number is opposite to 3?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 6 (D) 4

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
Top face 5 ↔ bottom face 4. In the middle row, alternating squares are opposite: 1 ↔ 6 and 3 ↔ 2. Therefore, 3 is opposite to 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q31. (Medium) Two views of a dice are given: View 1 has top 2, front 6, right 3. View 2 has top 4, front 2, right 5. Which number is opposite to 2?
(A) 5 (B) 1 (C) 4 (D) 6

Answer: (B) 1

Solution:
Face 2 is common. The faces adjacent to 2 in View 1 are 6 and 3; in View 2 they are 4 and 5. Thus, the four adjacent faces to 2 are 3, 4, 5, and 6. The only remaining face 1 must be opposite to 2. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CGL

Q33. (Medium) In a dice, opposite pairs are (1, 2), (3, 4), and (5, 6). Which of the following sets of three faces cannot meet at a single corner?
(A) 1, 3, 5 (B) 3, 5, 6 (C) 2, 4, 6 (D) 1, 2, 3

Answer: (D) 1, 2, 3

Solution:
Opposite faces can never meet at the same corner or be adjacent. In option B, 1 and 2 are an opposite pair, so they cannot meet at a vertex. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: SSC MTS

Q34. (Medium) A painted cube of side 7 cm is cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many cubes have no face painted?
(A) 125 (B) 64 (C) 216 (D) 343

Answer: (A) 125

Solution:
For n = 7, unpainted cubes = (n - 2)³ = (7 - 2)³ = 5³ = 125. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q35. (Medium) ⚠️ In a standard dice, three visible faces are 1, 4, and 5. Which number is definitely on the bottom face?
(A) 2 (B) Cannot be determined (C) 6 (D) 3

Answer: (B) Cannot be determined

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: The three faces 1, 4, and 5 meet at a corner, but without knowing which face is on top, the bottom face could be opposite to 1 (6), opposite to 4 (3), or opposite to 5 (2). Hence, it cannot be determined. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Answer: (C) 5

Solution:
Top 3 ↔ bottom 2. In the middle row, alternating faces are opposite: 1 ↔ 4 and 6 ↔ 5. Therefore, 6 is opposite to 5. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q37. (Medium) Two views of a dice: View 1 has top 4, front 2, right 1. View 2 has top 5, front 2, right 3. Which number is opposite to 2?
(A) 1 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 6

Answer: (D) 6

Solution:
Face 2 is common on the front in both views. Adjacent faces to 2 in View 1 are 4 and 1; in View 2 they are 5 and 3. Thus, faces 1, 3, 4, and 5 are all adjacent to 2. The only remaining face 6 is opposite to 2. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q38. (Medium) ⚠️ The top view of a solid is a 2×2 grid: front row has left 3, right 1; back row has left 2, right 2. How many cubes are completely hidden when viewed directly from the front?
(A) 3 (B) 2 (C) 1 (D) 4

Answer: (A) 3

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Left column: front height is 3, back height is 2, so both cubes in the back column are hidden. Right column: front height is 1, back height is 2, so the bottom cube in the back is hidden while top is visible. Total hidden = 2 + 1 = 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC MTS

Q39. (Medium) In a standard dice, 5 is on top and 4 is in front. Which number can be on the right face?
(A) 1 (B) 1 or 6 (C) 6 (D) 2

Answer: (B) 1 or 6

Solution:
Opposite to 5 is 2, and opposite to 4 is 3. The remaining two adjacent faces are 1 and 6. Therefore, the right face can be either 1 or 6. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q40. (Medium) A painted cube of side 4 cm is cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one face painted?
(A) 12 (B) 36 (C) 24 (D) 48

Answer: (C) 24

Solution:
For n = 4, cubes with exactly one face painted = 6 × (n - 2)² = 6 × (4 - 2)² = 6 × 4 = 24. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q41. (Medium) In a net, four squares are in a row from left to right: 1, 2, 3, 4. A square 5 is attached above square 2, and a square 6 is attached below square 3. When folded, which number is opposite to 2?
(A) 1 (B) 6 (C) 4 (D) 3

Answer: (D) 3

Solution:
In this net, the opposite pairs are: top 5 ↔ bottom 6, 1 ↔ 4, and 2 ↔ 3. Therefore, 2 is opposite to 3. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Q42. (Medium) A standard dice has top 1, front 2, and right 3 (opposite pairs: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4). If rolled forward once (front becomes bottom), what will be the new top face?
(A) 5 (B) 4 (C) 6 (D) 2

Answer: (A) 5

Solution:
Initial state: Top=1, Front=2, Right=3, Back=5, Bottom=6. Rolling forward once brings the back face to the top. Since Back=5, the new top face is 5. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q43. (Medium) ⚠️ In a T-shaped open dice net, squares are arranged as: top row [1]; middle row [2][3][4]; bottom row has [5] below 2 and [6] below 3. When folded, which number is opposite to 3?
(A) 1 (B) 5 (C) 4 (D) 6

Answer: (B) 5

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: In this T-net configuration, the opposite pairs are 1 ↔ 6, 2 ↔ 4, and 3 ↔ 5. Therefore, 3 is opposite to 5. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q44. (Medium) Initial state of a dice: top 1, front 2, right 3 (standard dice). It is rolled right (right becomes bottom), then rolled forward (front becomes bottom). What is the final top face?
(A) 4 (B) 6 (C) 5 (D) 1

Answer: (C) 5

Solution:
Initial: Top=1, Front=2, Right=3, Left=4, Back=5, Bottom=6. Rolling right makes: Top=4, Front=2, Right=1, Left=6, Back=5, Bottom=3. Then rolling forward makes: Top=5, Front=4, Right=1, Left=6, Back=3, Bottom=2. Hence, the final top face is 5. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q48. (Medium) A dice has opposite pairs (1, 4), (2, 5), and (3, 6). Initially, top is 1, front is 2, and right is 3. If rolled forward twice, what will be the new front face?
(A) 1 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 6

Answer: (C) 5

Solution:
Initial state: Top=1, Front=2, Right=3, Left=6, Back=5, Bottom=4. Rolling forward once: Top=5, Front=1, Bottom=2, Back=4. Rolling forward a second time: Top=4, Front=5, Bottom=1, Back=2. Thus, the new front face is 5. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Answer: (D) 6

Solution:
In the vertical column consisting of squares 1(0, 0), 5(0, -1), and 6(0, -2), alternating squares are opposite. Therefore, 1 is opposite to 6. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Q50. (Medium) ⚠️ A painted 3×3×3 cube has 27 small cubes. If all corner cubes are removed, how many of the remaining cubes have exactly two faces painted?
(A) 8 (B) 12 (C) 20 (D) 4

Answer: (B) 12

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Initially, the number of 2-face painted cubes (on edges) is 12 × (3 - 2) = 12. Corner cubes (8) had three painted faces; removing them does not change the count of 2-face painted cubes. Therefore, exactly 12 cubes remain with two painted faces. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: RRB Group D
