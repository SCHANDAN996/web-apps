Q51. (Medium) Two positions of a dice are given. Position 1: top 6, front 2, right 1. Position 2: top 6, front 3, right 4. Which number is opposite to 6?
(A) 5 (B) 3 (C) 2 (D) 1

Answer: (A) 5

Solution:
Moving clockwise from the common top face 6: Position 1 gives 2 and 1; Position 2 gives 3 and 4. Hence, 2 ↔ 3 and 1 ↔ 4. The remaining number 5 is opposite to 6. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q52. (Medium) ⚠️ In a standard dice, the top face is 4 and the front face is 1. Which number can be on the right face?
(A) 2 (B) 2 or 5 (C) 5 (D) 6

Answer: (B) 2 or 5

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: 4 is opposite 3, and 1 is opposite 6. The common adjacent faces to both 4 and 1 are 2 and 5. The right face can be either 2 or 5. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q53. (Medium) Two views of a dice are given: View 1 has top 2, front 4, right 1. View 2 has top 2, front 5, right 3. Which number is opposite to 4?
(A) 1 (B) 3 (C) 5 (D) 6

Answer: (C) 5

Solution:
Face 2 is common on top in both views. Rotating clockwise from face 2: View 1 shows 4 and 1; View 2 shows 5 and 3. Thus, 4 ↔ 5 and 1 ↔ 3. Therefore, 4 is opposite to 5. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q54. (Medium) A cube of side 5 cm is painted and cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two faces painted?
(A) 60 (B) 48 (C) 54 (D) 36

Answer: (D) 36

Solution:
For n = 5, cubes with exactly two faces painted (along edges) = 12 × (n - 2) = 12 × (5 - 2) = 36. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q57. (Medium) An open dice cross net has top 2, middle row from left to right: 5, 3, 1, 4, and bottom 6. When folded, which number is opposite to 3?

Q58. (Medium) Two views of a dice: View 1 has top 3, front 1, right 6. View 2 has top 5, front 1, right 2. Which number is opposite to 1?
(A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 5 (D) 4

Answer: (D) 4

Solution:
Face 1 is common on front in both views. Adjacent faces to 1 are 3, 6 (View 1) and 5, 2 (View 2). The four adjacent faces are 2, 3, 5, 6. The only remaining face 4 is opposite to 1. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: SSC MTS

Q59. (Medium) ⚠️ A painted cube of side 4 cm is cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many small cubes have at least one face painted?
(A) 56 (B) 48 (C) 32 (D) 64

Answer: (A) 56

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Total cubes = 4³ = 64. Unpainted cubes = (4 - 2)³ = 8. At least one face painted = Total - Unpainted = 64 - 8 = 56. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: RRB NTPC

Answer: (B) 3

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: In this net configuration, opposite pairs are: 5 ↔ 6, 1 ↔ 3, and 2 ↔ 4. Therefore, 1 is opposite to 3. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q61. (Medium) Initial state of a standard dice: top 2, front 4, right 1. It is rolled right once (right becomes bottom), then rolled forward once (front becomes bottom). What is the final top face?
(A) 6 (B) 5 (C) 3 (D) 1

Answer: (C) 3

Solution:
In a standard dice, opposite pairs sum to 7: 2 ↔ 5, 4 ↔ 3, 1 ↔ 6. Initial: Top=2, Front=4, Right=1, Left=6, Back=3, Bottom=5. Rolling right: Top=6, Right=2, Bottom=1, Left=5, Front=4, Back=3. Rolling forward: Top=3, Front=6, Bottom=4, Back=1. Hence, the final top face is 3. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC CGL

Q62. (Medium) ⚠️ A 3×3×3 cube is painted and cut into 27 small cubes. If all 8 corner cubes are removed, how many of the remaining cubes have exactly one face painted?
(A) 24 (B) 12 (C) 18 (D) 6

Answer: (D) 6

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Initially, cubes with exactly one face painted = 6 × (3 - 2)² = 6 (at the centers of faces). Corner cubes had 3 painted faces. Removing corners does not affect face-center cubes, so exactly 6 remain. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q65. (Medium) In a standard dice, 6 is on top and 3 is in front. Which number is on the bottom face?
(A) 4 (B) 2 (C) 1 (D) 5

Answer: (C) 1

Solution:
In a standard dice, opposite faces sum to 7. Opposite to 6 is 7 - 6 = 1. The bottom face is always directly opposite to the top face, so it is 1 regardless of the front face. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q68. (Medium) ⚠️ A painted cube is cut into 64 small cubes. How many small cubes have at least two faces painted?
(A) 24 (B) 32 (C) 40 (D) 48

Answer: (B) 32

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: For n = 4, cubes with 2 painted faces = 12 × (4 - 2) = 24. Cubes with 3 painted faces = 8. 'At least two faces' includes both 2-face and 3-face painted cubes: 24 + 8 = 32. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC MTS

Q70. (Medium) A dice initially has top 1, front 2, and right 3 (standard dice: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4). It is rolled forward, then right, then backward. What is the final top face?
(A) 1 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 6

Answer: (D) 6

Solution:
Initial: Top=1, Front=2, Right=3, Left=4, Back=5, Bottom=6. Rolling forward: Top=5, Front=1, Right=3, Left=4, Back=6, Bottom=2. Rolling right: Top=4, Front=1, Right=5, Left=2, Back=6, Bottom=3. Rolling backward: Top=6, Front=4, Right=5, Left=2, Back=3, Bottom=1. Final top = 6. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB Group D

Q71. (Medium) ⚠️ The top view of a solid is a 2×2 grid: front row has 3, 1; back row has 2, 2. How many cubes are completely hidden when viewed directly from the front?
(A) 3 (B) 2 (C) 1 (D) 4

Answer: (A) 3

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Left column: front height is 3, back height is 2 → both cubes behind are hidden. Right column: front height is 1, back height is 2 → the bottom cube behind is hidden, top is visible. Total hidden cubes = 2 + 1 = 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q72. (Medium) In a non-standard dice with opposite pairs A-F, B-E, C-D, the visible faces are top B, front A, and right C. If rolled forward once, what is the new front face?

Solution:

Q75. (Medium) Four views of a dice are given: View 1: 1, 2, 3; View 2: 2, 3, 4; View 3: 3, 4, 5; View 4: 4, 5, 6. Which number is opposite to 3?
(A) 1 (B) 6 (C) 5 (D) 2

Answer: (B) 6

Solution:
From View 1, 3 is adjacent to 1 and 2. From View 2, 3 is adjacent to 2 and 4. From View 3, 3 is adjacent to 4 and 5. Thus, 1, 2, 4, and 5 are all adjacent to 3. The only remaining face 6 is opposite to 3. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: RRB Group D
