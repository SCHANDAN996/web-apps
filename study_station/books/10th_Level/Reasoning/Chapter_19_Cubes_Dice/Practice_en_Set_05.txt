Q101. (Medium) Two positions of a dice are shown. Position 1: top 2, front 4, right 1. Position 2: top 2, front 5, right 3. Which number is opposite to 2?
(A) 6 (B) 5 (C) 4 (D) 1

Answer: (A) 6

Solution:
Common top face 2. Rotating clockwise from 2: Position 1 gives 4, 1; Position 2 gives 5, 3. Thus, 4 ↔ 5 and 1 ↔ 3. The remaining face 6 is opposite to 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q102. (Medium) ⚠️ In a standard dice, 4 is on top and 2 is in front. Which number can be on the right face?
(A) 1 (B) 1 or 6 (C) 3 (D) 6

Answer: (B) 1 or 6

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: 4 is opposite 3, and 2 is opposite 5. The common adjacent faces to 4 and 2 are 1 and 6. Therefore, the right face can be either 1 or 6. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q103. (Medium) The top view of a solid is a 3×3 grid: Row 1: 1, 2, 1; Row 2: 2, 3, 2; Row 3: 1, 2, 1. How many cubes are there in total?
(A) 13 (B) 14 (C) 15 (D) 16

Answer: (C) 15

Solution:
Total cubes = sum of all heights = 1 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 1 = 15. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q104. (Medium) ⚠️ A painted cube of side 5 cm is cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many small cubes have at least two faces painted?
(A) 36 (B) 52 (C) 48 (D) 44

Answer: (D) 44

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: For n = 5, cubes with 2 painted faces = 12 × (5 - 2) = 36. Cubes with 3 painted faces = 8. At least two faces painted = 36 + 8 = 44. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q107. (Medium) A painted cube of side 4 cm is cut into 1 cm small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one face painted?
(A) 12 (B) 36 (C) 24 (D) 48

Answer: (C) 24

Solution:
For n = 4, cubes with exactly one face painted = 6 × (n - 2)² = 6 × (4 - 2)² = 6 × 4 = 24. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q110. (Medium) A painted cube is cut into 216 small cubes. How many cubes have no face painted?

Answer: (B) 64

Solution:

Q114. (Medium) ⚠️ The top view of a solid is a 2×2 grid: front row has 2, 1; back row has 1, 3. How many cubes are completely hidden when viewed directly from the front?

Answer: (B) 2

Solution:

Q121. (Hard) Four views of a dice are given: View 1: top 1, front 2, right 3. View 2: top 2, front 4, right 5. View 3: top 3, front 5, right 6. View 4: top 4, front 1, right 6. Which number is opposite to 1?
(A) 5 (B) 4 (C) 3 (D) 6

Answer: (A) 5

Solution:
From View 1, faces adjacent to 1 are 2 and 3. From View 4, faces adjacent to 1 are 4 and 6. Therefore, faces 2, 3, 4, and 6 are all adjacent to 1. The only remaining face 5 must be opposite to 1. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q122. (Hard) A cube of side 8 cm is painted with three colors: opposite pairs of faces are painted red, blue, and green respectively. It is cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two colors?
(A) 48 (B) 72 (C) 60 (D) 84

Answer: (B) 72

Solution:
For n = 8, all 12 edges lie between faces of different colors (since opposite faces share the same color). Thus, every 2-face painted cube has exactly two colors. Total = 12 × (8 - 2) = 12 × 6 = 72. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q123. (Hard) In an open dice net, opposite pairs are 1-4, 2-3, and 5-6. Which of the following sets of three faces can meet at a single corner?
(A) 1, 4, 2 (B) 2, 3, 5 (C) 1, 2, 5 (D) 5, 6, 3

Answer: (C) 1, 2, 5

Solution:
Opposite faces can never meet at a vertex. 1 and 4 are opposite, 2 and 3 are opposite, and 5 and 6 are opposite. Options A, B, and D each contain an opposite pair. Only 1, 2, and 5 are mutually adjacent and can meet at a corner. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q124. (Hard) A standard dice (opposite faces sum to 7) is placed with top 1, front 2, and right 3. It is rolled forward once (top moves to front), and then rolled right once (top moves to right). What is the final top face?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 5 (D) 4

Answer: (D) 4

Solution:
In a standard dice, opposite pairs sum to 7: 1 ↔ 6, 2 ↔ 5, 3 ↔ 4. Initial: Top=1, Front=2, Right=3, Left=4, Back=5, Bottom=6. Rolling forward: Top=5, Front=1, Bottom=2, Back=6. Rolling right: The left face (4) becomes the new top. Hence, the final top face is 4. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q125. (Hard) A dice has opposite pairs 1-4, 2-5, 3-6. Initially: top 1, front 2, right 3. It is rolled right once (right becomes bottom), and then rolled forward once (front becomes bottom). What is the final top face?
(A) 1 (B) 5 (C) 6 (D) 2

Answer: (B) 5

Solution:
Opposite pairs: 1 ↔ 4, 2 ↔ 5, 3 ↔ 6. Initial: Top=1, Front=2, Right=3, Left=6, Back=5, Bottom=4. Rolling right: Top=6, Right=1, Bottom=3, Left=4 (Front=2 and Back=5 unchanged). Rolling forward: The back face (5) becomes the new top. Hence, the final top face is 5. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: RRB Group D
