उत्तर: B) 3

बाएँ: (बाएँ घुमाने पर left becomes bottom, top becomes left, right becomes top) → T=3, F=1, R=2, L=5, Bk=4, Btm=6
पीछे: (पीछे घुमाने पर back becomes bottom, top becomes back, front becomes top) → T=4, F=3, R=2, L=5, Bk=6, Btm=1
दाएँ: (right becomes bottom, top becomes right, left becomes top) → T=2, F=3, R=6, L=1, Bk=5, Btm=4

T = old L = 5, F = old F = 6, R = old T = 1, L = old Btm = 4, Bk = old Bk = 3, Btm = old R = 2. अंतिम T=5, विकल्प C. तो सही 5। तो क्रम: आगे, बाएँ, पीछे, दाएँ → T=5। विकल्प C)5। मैं गणना संक्षेप में लिखूँगा।
