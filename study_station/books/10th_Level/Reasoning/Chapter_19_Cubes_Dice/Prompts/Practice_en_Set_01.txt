Question: In a standard dice, what is the sum of numbers on opposite faces?
Options: A) 6 B) 7 C) 5 D) 8
Answer: B) 7
Solution: In a standard dice, opposite faces always total 7 (1+6, 2+5, 3+4).
Source: SSC CGL 2021

Question: Which number is opposite to 4 in a standard dice?
Options: A) 2 B) 5 C) 3 D) 1
Answer: C) 3
Solution: In a standard dice, opposite sum = 7, so opposite of 4 is 7-4 = 3.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

Question: Two positions of a dice are shown. Which number is opposite to 2?
Position 1: top 2, front 6, right 4. Position 2: top 5, front 6, right 3.
Options: A) 3 B) 5 C) 4 D) 6
Answer: B) 5
Solution: From positions, 6 is common. In position 1, clockwise from 6 gives 2,4; in position 2, clockwise gives 5,3. So 2 opposite 5, 4 opposite 3.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

Question: A dice has numbers 1,2,3,4,5,6 on its faces. Two views show: (i) 3 adjacent to 1 and 2; (ii) 3 adjacent to 5 and 6. Which number is opposite to 3?
Options: A) 1 B) 2 C) 4 D) 5
Answer: C) 4
Solution: Adjacent faces to 3 are 1,2,5,6. Only 4 is not adjacent, so 4 is opposite to 3.
Source: RRB NTPC 2019

Question: An open dice net is given: cross shape with top 1, middle row left to right: 2,3,4,5, bottom 6. When folded, which pair will be opposite?
Options: A) 1 and 3 B) 2 and 4 C) 3 and 6 D) 5 and 1
Answer: B) 2 and 4
Solution: In cross net, 1 opposite 6; middle row: 1st opposite 3rd (2↔4), 2nd opposite 4th (3↔5). So 2 and 4 are opposite.
Source: SSC CHSL 2018

Question: In a standard dice, if face 1 is on top, what is the sum of the four adjacent faces around it?
Options: A) 14 B) 15 C) 16 D) 13
Answer: A) 14
Solution: Opposite of 1 is 6. Four adjacent faces sum = total sum of all faces (21) - (1+6) = 14.
Source: SSC CPO 2021

Question: From the given two views of a dice, find the number opposite to 6: View 1: 6 on top, 5 in front, 3 on right. View 2: 6 on top, 4 in front, 2 on right.
Options: A) 1 B) 2 C) 3 D) 4
Answer: A) 1
Solution: Using clockwise rule from common top 6: view 1 gives 5,3; view 2 gives 4,2. So 5 opposite 4, 3 opposite 2. Remaining 1 opposite 6.

Question: Which of the following can be a net of a cube?
Options: A) A T-shape with 6 squares B) A line of 6 squares C) A plus sign with extra square D) A shape with 7 squares
Answer: A) A T-shape with 6 squares
Solution: A cube net must have exactly 6 squares connected properly. The T-shape (like 3 in a column, with 3 attached to the middle) is a valid net. Line of 6 can't fold.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

Question: A large cube is painted and cut into 216 smaller cubes of equal size. How many small cubes have no face painted?
Options: A) 64 B) 27 C) 125 D) 8
Answer: A) 64
Solution: Total cubes = 216, so n = ∛216 = 6. No paint = (n-2)³ = (4)³ = 64.

Options: A) 2 B) 3 C) 4 D) 6

Question: A painted cube (n=3) is cut. How many small cubes have at least 2 faces painted?
Options: A) 8 B) 12 C) 20 D) 24

Solution: At least 2 faces = 2-face + 3-face = 12×(3-2)=12 + 8 = 20. (Trap: "only 2" would be 12, "at least 2" includes corners)

Question (Medium): Two different positions of the same dice are shown. In position 1, faces 1,3,5 are visible around a vertex. In position 2, faces 3,2,6 are visible around another vertex. Which number is opposite to 3?
Options: A) 1 B) 2 C) 4 D) 6
Answer: C) 4
Solution: From position 1, 3 is adjacent to 1,5. From position 2, 3 is adjacent to 2,6. Thus 3 is adjacent to 1,2,5,6. Only 4 remains, so opposite to 3 is 4.
Source: SSC CGL 2023 (Medium)

Question (Medium): An irregular solid is formed by stacking cubes. Front view shows heights: left column 3, middle column 2, right column 1. Top view shows a 2x2 grid with the first row having columns of height 3 and 1 from left to right, second row height 2 and 2. How many cubes are there?
Options: A) 8 B) 9 C) 10 D) 11
Answer: A) 8
Solution: Top view grid: (1,1)=3, (1,2)=1; (2,1)=2, (2,2)=2. Sum = 3+1+2+2 = 8. (Trap: Front view may mislead if only seen from one side)
Source: IBPS PO Mains 2021 (Examiner's Trap)
