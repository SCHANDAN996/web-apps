51. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, and F are sitting in a row facing north. A is third to the left of D. B is to the immediate left of C. E sits at the right end. F is to the immediate left of E. D is not a neighbour of E. Who sits second to the right of A?
(a) C (b) B (c) F (d) D
Answer: (a)
Solution: Arrangement (left to right): 1-A, 2-B, 3-C, 4-D, 5-F, 6-E. Second to the right of A(1) is C at seat 3.
Source: PYQ-style
