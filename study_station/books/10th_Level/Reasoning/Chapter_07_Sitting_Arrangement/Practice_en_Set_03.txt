51. Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing south. A sits to the immediate right of E. D sits at one of the ends. E sits second to the right of C. Who sits second to the left of A?
(a) C (b) B (c) D (d) E
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. As we look at them (they face us), from our left to our right: A, E, B, C, D. So the answer is B.
