26. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, and F are sitting in a row facing north. A is second to the left of C. D is to the immediate right of B. E sits at the right end and is an immediate neighbour of F. B is not a neighbour of A. Who sits third to the right of A?
(a) F (b) D (c) E (d) B
Answer: (a)
Solution: Arrangement (left to right): 1-B, 2-A, 3-D, 4-C, 5-F, 6-E. Third to the right of A(2) is F at seat 5.
Source: PYQ-style
