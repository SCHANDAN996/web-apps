26. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. B sits third to the left of E. C sits third to the left of D. A sits third from the left end. Who sits third to the right of A?
(a) F (b) B (c) E (d) D
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: B, C, A, E, D, F. So the answer is F.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Five persons S, T, U, V and W sit around a circular table, facing the centre. W sits to the immediate right of S. U sits third to the left of S. W sits second to the right of T. Who sits to the immediate right of V?
(a) W (b) T (c) U (d) S

Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. Going clockwise: T, V, U, W, S (facing the centre, a person's left is clockwise). The trap is U, who is on the other side of V. So the answer is T.
