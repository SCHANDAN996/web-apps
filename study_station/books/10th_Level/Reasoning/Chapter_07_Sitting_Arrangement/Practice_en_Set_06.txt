126. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. E sits third to the left of D. B sits to the immediate right of A. B sits to the immediate left of D. Who sits to the immediate left of A?
(a) E (b) C (c) D (d) B
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: F, E, A, B, D, C. The trap is B, who is on the other side of A. So the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style
