126. Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G, and H are sitting in a row facing north. B sits fourth to the left of G. A sits exactly between C and D. E sits second to the right of D. H sits at the right end. F sits to the immediate left of H. G is not a neighbour of F. Who sits second to the left of E?
(a) D (b) A (c) B (d) C
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sitting arrangement from left to right: 1-B, 2-C, 3-A, 4-D, 5-G, 6-E, 7-F, 8-H. Second person to the left of E (seat 6) is D at seat 4.
Source: PYQ-style
