1. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. E sits third to the left of D. A sits second to the right of E. C sits to the immediate right of D. F sits to the immediate right of C. Who sits third to the left of F?
(a) C (b) E (c) D (d) A
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: E, B, A, D, C, F. So the answer is A.
Source: PYQ-style
