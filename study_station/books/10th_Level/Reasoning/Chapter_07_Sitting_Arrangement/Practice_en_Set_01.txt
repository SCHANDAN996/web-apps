1. Five persons P, Q, R, S, and T are sitting in a row facing north. Q is to the immediate left of R. S is second to the right of P. T sits at the right end and is an immediate neighbour of S. Who sits exactly in the middle of the row?
(a) R (b) P (c) Q (d) S
Answer: (a)
Solution: Arrangement (left to right): 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R, 4-S, 5-T. T(5) is at the right end adjacent to S(4). S is second to the right of P(2). Q(1) is immediate left of R(3). Exactly in the middle (seat 3) is R.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, and F are sitting in a row facing north. A is second to the left of C. D is to the immediate right of B. E sits at the right end and is an immediate neighbour of F. B is not a neighbour of A. Who sits third to the right of A?

Solution: Arrangement (left to right): 1-B, 2-A, 3-D, 4-C, 5-F, 6-E. E(6) is at the right end with neighbour F(5). A(2) is second to the left of C(4). D(3) is immediate right of B(1). Third to the right of A(2) is seat 5, occupied by F.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Five persons A, B, C, D, and E are sitting around a circular table facing the centre. A is second to the left of B. C is to the immediate right of A. D is to the immediate left of E. Who sits to the immediate left of C?

Solution: Facing centre: left = clockwise. Clockwise 1 to 5: 1-B, 2-C, 3-A, 4-D, 5-E. A(3) is second to the left of B(1). C(2) is immediate right of A(3). Immediate left of C(2) in clockwise direction is B at seat 1.
