101. Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F and G sit in a row facing north. E sits second to the left of G. G sits third to the left of D. C sits to the immediate left of E. Who sits exactly in the middle of the row?
(a) G (b) B (c) E (d) C
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: C, E, B, G, A, F, D. So the answer is G.
Source: PYQ-style
