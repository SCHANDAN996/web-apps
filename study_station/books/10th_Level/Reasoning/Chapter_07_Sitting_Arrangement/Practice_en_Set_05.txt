101. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, and F are sitting in a row facing north. A is second to the left of D. B is to the immediate right of C. E sits at the left end. F is to the immediate right of E. D is not a neighbour of F. Who sits third to the right of C?
(a) D (b) B (c) E (d) F
Answer: (a)
Solution: Arrangement (left to right): 1-E, 2-F, 3-C, 4-A, 5-B, 6-D. Third to the right of C(3) is D at seat 6.
Source: PYQ-style
