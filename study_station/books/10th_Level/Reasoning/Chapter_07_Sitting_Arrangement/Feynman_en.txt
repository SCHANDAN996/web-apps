# Feynman Technique: Sitting Arrangement in Simple Words

Tell your friend: "Stand facing me. Now raise your right hand. Look — your right hand is on my left side! Because we face each other." That is the biggest secret of sitting arrangement — when someone faces us, their left and right are reversed from ours.

**On a round table everyone faces the centre, which is in a way towards us. So their left = our right.**

## The Biggest Trick: Draw the Circle

Draw a circle on paper and mark 6 dots. Read the conditions one by one:
- "A is to the right of B" → first place B, then A to B's right (taking facing-centre into account).
- Seat everyone one at a time.

With the picture before your eyes, no condition is missed.

## Blurting Sheet (Write from Memory)

Close the book and write:
1. Facing the centre on a round table, which way is left?
2. And facing outward?
3. What are the three main types?

Whatever you cannot write, read again.
