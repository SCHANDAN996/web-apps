76. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. E sits to the immediate right of B. A sits third to the right of F. F sits to the immediate right of D. D sits second from the left end. Who sits to the immediate right of C?
(a) B (b) E (c) A (d) D
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: C, D, F, B, E, A. So the answer is D.
