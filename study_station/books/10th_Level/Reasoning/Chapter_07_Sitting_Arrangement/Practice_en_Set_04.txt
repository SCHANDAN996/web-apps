**76.** Six persons A, B, C, D, E, F sit in a row facing north. A is third to the left of D. B is immediate right of C. E sits at extreme left. F sits immediate right of E. D is not a neighbour of F. Who sits second to the right of C?  
- (A) B  
- (B) D  
- (C) E  
- (D) F  

**Answer:** (B) D  
**Solution:** Seats 1–6. E=1, F=2. A 3rd left of D ⇒ D = A+3. D not neighbour of F ⇒ D ≠ 3. D=4 ⇒ A=1 conflict; D=5 ⇒ A=2 conflict; D=6 ⇒ A=3. So A=3,D=6. B immediate right of C ⇒ C,B block (C left of B). Remaining seats 4,5: C=4,B=5. Arrangement: 1‑E,2‑F,3‑A,4‑C,5‑B,6‑D. Second right of C(4) = 6 = D.  
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier-I 2018 (Memory Based)

**77.** Six persons P, Q, R, S, T, U sit around a circle facing centre. P is second to the left of Q. R is immediate right of P. S is second to the right of T. U sits opposite R. Who sits immediate left of U?  
- (A) P  
- (B) Q  
- (C) S  
- (D) T  

**Answer:** (C) S  
**Solution:** 1–6 clockwise. Q=1. P 2nd left → clockwise 1→2→3, P=3. R immediate right → anticlockwise → R=2. U opposite R (2↔5) ⇒ U=5. S 2nd right of T ⇒ S anticlockwise 2 from T. Remaining 4,6. T=6 ⇒ S anticlockwise 6→5→4, S=4. Arrangement: 1‑Q,2‑R,3‑P,4‑S,5‑U,6‑T. Immediate left of U(5) = clockwise neighbour = 4 = S.  
**Source:** IBPS PO Prelims 2019

**78.** (Examiner’s Trap – Double Relationship)  
Six persons P, Q, R, S, T, U sit in a row facing north. P is second to the left of Q and immediate left of R. S is immediate right of T. U sits at extreme left end. R is not a neighbour of T. Who sits second to the right of U?  
- (A) P  
- (B) Q  
- (C) R  
- (D) S  

**Answer:** (C) R  
**Solution:** U=1. P immediate left of R ⇒ P,R block (P left of R). P second left of Q ⇒ Q = P+2 ⇒ P=x, R=x+1, Q=x+2. S immediate right of T ⇒ T,S block. R not neighbour of T. x=2: P=2,R=3,Q=4. T,S at 5,6: T=5,S=6. R=3 not neighbour of T=5. Arrangement: 1‑U,2‑P,3‑R,4‑Q,5‑T,6‑S. Second right of U(1) = 3 = R.  
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier-I 2020

**80.** (Examiner’s Trap – Misleading Options)  
Seven persons P, Q, R, S, T, U, V sit in a row facing north. R sits fourth to the right of P. Q is immediate left of S. U sits second to the left of T. V sits at extreme left. P is not a neighbour of V. Which of the following is definitely true?  
- (A) Q sits at extreme right.  
- (B) T sits immediate left of R.  
- (C) U sits second to the right of V.  
- (D) S sits immediate right of Q.  

**Answer:** (D) S sits immediate right of Q.

**Source:** SSC CGL Tier-II 2020

**84.** (Examiner’s Trap – Misleading Options)

**Solution:** A=1. D=B+3. C‑E block (C left of E). F second left of G ⇒ G=F+2. B not neighbour of A ⇒ B≠2. B=3 ⇒ D=6. C=2,E=3? B=3 conflict. C=4,E=5; F=2,G=4 conflict. B=4 ⇒ D=7. C=2,E=3; F=5,G=7 conflict. C=3,E=4 conflict B. C=5,E=6; F=2,G=4 works. Arrangement: 1‑A,2‑F,3‑B,4‑G,5‑D,6‑C,7‑E. Second left of E(7) = 5 = D, not G. Hence (B) false.  
**Source:** RRB NTPC 2021
