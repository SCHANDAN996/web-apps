Front: What is the most reliable way to solve blood relations?
Back: Draw the family tree on paper — don't solve it in your head.

Front: Symbols for male, female and couple in a family tree?
Back: Male = □ or (+), female = ○ or (−), couple = equals sign (=).

Front: Who is "the only son of my father" (male speaker)?
Back: The speaker himself (if he has no brother).

Front: A is the brother of B, and B is the mother of C — how is A related to C?
Back: Maternal uncle (mother's brother).

Front: What are relations on the mother's side called?
Back: Maternal — maternal grandfather/grandmother, maternal uncle, aunt.

Front: What are relations on the father's side called?
Back: Paternal — paternal grandfather/grandmother, paternal uncle, aunt.

Front: What do we call the father's sister?
Back: Paternal aunt (bua).

Front: What do we call the mother's sister?
Back: Maternal aunt (mausi).

Front: In a photo puzzle, what does "my father's son" mean (no brother)?
Back: The speaker himself.

Front: Which generation do grandparents belong to?
Back: The topmost generation (one above the parents).

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Assuming gender from the name — "Kiran" can be male or female.

Front: What to do first in coded blood relations?
Back: Write the meaning of each symbol (+ − × ÷) separately, then apply left to right.

Front: Which relations are of the "same generation"?
Back: Brother, sister, or spouse.

Front: What do we call the father's father?
Back: Paternal grandfather (dada).

Front: What are the three steps of blood relations?
Back: Draw the tree → count generations → check gender.
