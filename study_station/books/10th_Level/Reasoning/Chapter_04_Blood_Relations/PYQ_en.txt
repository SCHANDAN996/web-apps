4. Top 10 High-Yield Questions with Complete Solutions

Question 1 (SSC CGL 2020): Pointing to a photograph of a boy, Suresh said, "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is Suresh related to that boy?
Solution:
• Suresh's mother's only son = Suresh himself (since Suresh is male).
• The boy is the son of Suresh himself.
• Therefore, Suresh is the father of the boy.
Answer: Father
Source: SSC CGL

Question 2 (IBPS PO 2019): If 'A + B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the wife of B', 'A × B' means 'A is the brother of B', and 'A ÷ B' means 'A is the daughter of B', how is P related to T in 'P ÷ R + S × T'?
Solution:
• P ÷ R: P is the daughter of R (P is female).
• R + S: R is the father of S (R is male).
• S × T: S is the brother of T (S is male).
• Since R is the father of P, S, and T, P is the sister of T.
Answer: Sister
Source: IBPS PO

Question 3 (SSC MTS 2015): A man pointing to a woman said, "Her mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man?
Solution:
• "My mother's only daughter" = the man's sister.
• The woman's mother is the man's sister.
• The daughter of one's sister is one's niece.
Answer: Niece
Source: SSC MTS

Question 4 (RRB NTPC 2016): Introducing a girl, Vipin said, "Her mother is the only daughter of my mother-in-law." How is Vipin related to that girl?
Solution:
• Vipin's mother-in-law's only daughter = Vipin's wife.
• The girl's mother is Vipin's wife.
• Therefore, Vipin is the girl's father.
Answer: Father
Source: RRB NTPC

Question 5 (IBPS PO 2017): If 'P @ Q' means 'P is the husband of Q', 'P # Q' means 'P is the sister of Q', and 'P * Q' means 'P is the son of Q', which expression shows that A is the brother-in-law of C?
Solution:
• In 'A @ B # C', A is the husband of B, and B is the sister of C.
• A is the sister's husband of C, meaning A is the brother-in-law of C (Jija).
Answer: A @ B # C
Source: IBPS PO

Question 6 (RRB Group D 2018): 1. A is the brother of B. 2. C is the father of A. 3. D is the brother of E. 4. E is the daughter of B. Who is the uncle of D?
Solution:
• E is the daughter of B, and D is the brother of E ➜ D and E are children of B.
• A is the brother of B.
• The brother of one's parent (B) is an uncle ➜ A is the uncle of D.
Answer: A
Source: RRB Group D

Question 7 (SSC CHSL 2019): Showing a man in a picture, a woman said, "His brother's father is the only son of my grandfather." How is the woman related to the man?
Solution:
• The man's brother's father = the man's father.
• The woman's grandfather's only son = the woman's father.
• The man and the woman share the same father ➜ the woman is his sister.
Answer: Sister
Source: SSC CHSL

Question 8 (SBI PO 2020): Read the statements: 1. In a family of six, P, Q, R, S, T, and U, there are two married couples. 2. Q is a doctor and the mother of T and U. 3. R is a lawyer and the father of P. 4. S is the grandfather of T and is a contractor. 5. U is a student. How is P related to U?
Solution:
• S (grandfather, male) married to grandmother (not explicitly named or deceased? Wait, 2 married couples).
• Q (female, doctor) is mother of T and U; her husband must be P (since R is lawyer, father of P; S is grandfather).
• P is the father of T and U.
• Thus, P is the father of U.
Answer: Father
Source: SBI PO

Question 9 (SSC CGL 2018): In a gathering, A tells B, "That boy playing football is the younger of the two brothers of the daughter of my father's wife." How is the boy related to A?
Solution:
• "My father's wife" = A's mother.
• Daughter of A's mother = A's sister.
• The brother of A's sister = A's brother.
• Therefore, the boy is A's brother.
Answer: Brother
Source: SSC CGL

Question 10 (SSC CGL 2018): A, B, C, D, and E are members of a family. There are two fathers, two sons, two wives, three males, and two females. The teacher was the wife of the lawyer, who was the son of the doctor. E is not male, nor is she the wife of any professional. C is the youngest and D is the oldest. B is male. How is D related to E?
Solution:
• D is the oldest (generation 1, doctor).
• E is the wife of D (not the wife of a professional lawyer, but the matriarch).
• Therefore, D is the husband of E.
Answer: Husband
Source: SSC CGL
