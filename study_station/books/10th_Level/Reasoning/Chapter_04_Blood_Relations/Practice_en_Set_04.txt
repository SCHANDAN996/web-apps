If ‘A + B’ means ‘A is son of B’, ‘A – B’ means ‘A is daughter of B’, ‘A × B’ means ‘A is brother of B’, then ‘P + Q × R – S’ means:
(A) P is grandson of S
(B) P is son of S
(C) P is nephew of S
(D) P is brother of S
Answer: (A) P is grandson of S
Solution: P + Q → P son of Q. Q × R → Q brother of R, so Q and R are siblings (R can be male or female). R – S → R daughter of S, so R is female. Thus Q and R are siblings, children of S. P is son of Q, hence P is grandson of S.
Source: IBPS PO 2018
