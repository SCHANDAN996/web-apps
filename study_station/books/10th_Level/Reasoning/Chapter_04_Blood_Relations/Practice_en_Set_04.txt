76. Pointing to a girl, a man says, 'She is the daughter of the only daughter-in-law of my father.' How is the girl related to the man?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) Daughter (c) Niece (d) Sister
Answer: (a)
Solution: The father's only daughter-in-law could be the man's wife (if he has no brothers) or his brother's wife (if he has brothers). Without knowing if he has brothers, the exact relation cannot be determined.
Source: PYQ-style

77. If 'A + B' means 'A is the son of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the daughter of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the brother of B', then what does 'P + Q × R – S' mean?
(a) P is the son of S (b) P is the grandson of S (c) P is the nephew of S (d) P is the brother of S
Answer: (b)
Solution: P + Q means P is the son of Q. Q × R means Q is the brother of R. R – S means R is the daughter of S. Q and R are children of S. Since P is the son of Q, P is the grandson of S.
Source: PYQ-style
