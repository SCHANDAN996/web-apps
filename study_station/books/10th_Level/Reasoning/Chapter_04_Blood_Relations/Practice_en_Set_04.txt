76. Pointing to a girl, a man says, 'She is the daughter of the only daughter-in-law of my father.' How is the girl related to the man?
(a) Niece (b) Daughter (c) Sister (d) Cousin
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is "Niece", which would fit only if the daughter-in-law were a brother's wife. Since the father has only one daughter-in-law, she is the man's own wife, so the girl is his daughter.
Source: PYQ-style

77. If 'A + B' means 'A is the son of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the daughter of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the brother of B', then what does 'P + Q × R – S' mean?
(a) P is the son of S (b) P is the brother of S (c) P is the nephew of S (d) P is the grandson of S
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "son of S", which ignores the middle link. P is the son of Q, Q is the brother of R, and R is the daughter of S, so Q and R are children of S; P, being Q's son, is the grandson of S.
