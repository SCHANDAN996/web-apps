Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. A is the father of B and B is the father of C. How is A related to C?
(a) Father (b) Uncle (c) Grandfather (d) Brother
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Father, but A is two generations above C, not one. A is the father of B and B is the father of C, so A is the paternal grandfather of C, so the answer is Grandfather.
Source: PYQ-style

2. X is the sister of Y. Y is the son of Z. How is Z related to X?
(a) Father or Mother (b) Brother (c) Sister (d) Mother
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Mother, but the gender of Z is not given anywhere in the statement. X and Y are children of Z, so Z is either the father or the mother of X, so the answer is Father or Mother.
Source: PYQ-style

3. P is the mother of Q. Q is the brother of R. How is P related to R?
(a) Aunt (b) Grandmother (c) Sister (d) Mother
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Aunt, but Q being the brother of R makes Q and R siblings, not cousins. Since P is the mother of Q, P is also the mother of R, so the answer is Mother.

24. If 'A × B' means 'A is the brother of B' and 'A + B' means 'A is the mother of B', then in 'P × Q + R', how is P related to R?
(a) Uncle (b) Father (c) Brother (d) Grandfather
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Father, but P is not the parent of R; Q is the mother of R. P × Q means P is the brother of Q, and Q + R means Q is the mother of R, so P is the brother of R's mother, that is, the maternal uncle, so the answer is Uncle.
Source: PYQ-style
