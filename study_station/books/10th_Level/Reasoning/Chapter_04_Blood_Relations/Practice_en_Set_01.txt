Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. A is the father of B and B is the father of C. How is A related to C?
(a) Grandfather (b) Father (c) Uncle (d) Brother
Answer: (a)
Solution: A is the father of B and B is the father of C. Therefore, A is the paternal grandfather of C.
Source: PYQ-style

2. X is the sister of Y. Y is the son of Z. How is Z related to X?
(a) Mother (b) Sister (c) Father or Mother (d) Brother
Answer: (b)
Solution: X and Y are children of Z. Since the gender of Z is not specified, Z is either the father or mother of X.
Source: PYQ-style

3. P is the mother of Q. Q is the brother of R. How is P related to R?
(a) Mother (b) Grandmother (c) Sister (d) Aunt
Answer: (c)
Solution: Q is the brother of R, so Q and R are siblings. Since P is the mother of Q, P is also the mother of R.
Source: PYQ-style

24. If 'A + B' means 'A is the sister of B' and 'A × B' means 'A is the father of B', then in 'P × Q + R', how is P related to R?

Solution: Q + R means Q is the sister of R. P × Q means P is the father of Q. Since Q and R are siblings, P is also the father of R.
