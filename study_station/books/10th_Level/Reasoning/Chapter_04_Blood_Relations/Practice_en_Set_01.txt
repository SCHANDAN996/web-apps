Set 1: Blood Relations MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Q1. A is the father of B, and B is the father of C. How is A related to C?
(a) Father (b) Grandfather (c) Uncle (d) Brother
Answer: (b) Grandfather
Solution: A is the father of B, and B is the father of C, so A is C's grandfather.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. X is the sister of Y. Y is the son of Z. How is Z related to X?
(a) Brother (b) Son (c) Father or Mother (d) Uncle
Answer: (c) Father or Mother
Solution: X is the sister of Y, so X is also Z's child. Thus Z is the parent of X.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. P is the mother of Q. Q is the brother of R. How is P related to R?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Grandmother
Answer: (b) Mother
Solution: Q and R are siblings, both children of P. So P is R's mother.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. X is the brother of Y. Y is the sister of Z. How is X related to Z?
(a) Father (b) Brother (c) Uncle (d) Son
Answer: (b) Brother
Solution: X, Y and Z are siblings, so X is the brother of Z.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. A is the son of B. C is the mother of B. How is C related to A?
(a) Mother (b) Sister (c) Grandmother (d) Aunt
Answer: (c) Grandmother
Solution: C is B's mother and A is B's son, so C is A's grandmother.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. Ram is the brother of Mohan. Mohan is the son of Sohan. How is Sohan related to Ram?
(a) Brother (b) Uncle (c) Father (d) Grandfather
Answer: (c) Father
Solution: Ram and Mohan are brothers; Sohan is Mohan's father, so Sohan is also Ram's father.
Source: SSC MTS

Q10. The daughter of your sister is your?

Q11. A is the wife of B. C is the father of B. How is C related to A?

Answer: (b) Father-in-law

Q21. (Medium) A is B's father. C is D's mother. B is married to D. How is A related to C?
(a) Brother (b) Father (c) Related by marriage (samdhi) (d) Uncle
Answer: (c) Related by marriage (samdhi)
Solution: B and D are a married couple; A is B's father and C is D's mother, so A and C are related by marriage (co-parents-in-law, samdhi).
Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) Pointing to a boy, a woman said, "He is the son of the only son of my grandfather." How is the boy related to the woman?
(a) Son (b) Brother (c) Cousin (d) Nephew
Answer: (b) Brother
Solution: Grandfather's only son = the woman's father. The father's son = the woman's brother.
Source: SSC CGL

Q23. (Medium) A is the mother of B and C. D is the husband of A. How is D related to C?
(a) Uncle (b) Brother (c) Father (d) Grandfather
Answer: (c) Father
Solution: A is the mother of C, and D is A's husband, so D is C's father.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q24. (Medium) If 'A + B' means A is the sister of B and 'A × B' means A is the father of B, then in 'P × Q + R', how is P related to R?
(a) Brother (b) Father (c) Uncle (d) Grandfather
Answer: (b) Father
Solution: Q + R: Q is the sister of R. P × Q: P is the father of Q. As Q and R are sisters, P is also the father of R.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q25. (Medium) X is the son of Y's father's sister. How is X related to Y?
(a) Brother (b) Cousin (c) Uncle (d) Nephew
Answer: (b) Cousin
Solution: Y's father's sister is Y's aunt. Her son X is Y's cousin.
Source: SSC CGL
