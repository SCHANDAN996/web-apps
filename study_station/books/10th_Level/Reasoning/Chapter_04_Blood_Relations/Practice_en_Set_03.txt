51. A is the sister of B. B is the brother of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Aunt (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Grandmother
Answer: (a)
Solution: A is the sister of B, and B is the brother of C, so A is the sister of C. Since C is the father of D, A is the paternal aunt of D.

53. If 'A + B' means 'A is the brother of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the sister of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the father of B', then what does 'P × Q + R – S' mean?
(a) P is the grandfather of S (b) P is the uncle of S (c) P is the father of S (d) P is the brother of S
Answer: (c)
Solution: P × Q means P is the father of Q. Q + R means Q is the brother of R. R – S means R is the sister of S. Thus Q, R, and S are siblings, and P is their father. Therefore, P is the father of S.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: The speaker has no siblings, so 'my father's son' refers to himself. Therefore, 'that man's father is myself', which means that man is his son.
