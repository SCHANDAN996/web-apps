51. A is the sister of B. B is the brother of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Aunt (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Grandmother
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Mother, but C is the father, not A. A is the sister of B, and B is the brother of C, so A is the sister of C; since C is the father of D, the answer is Aunt.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Pointing to a woman, a man said, 'She is the daughter of my father's only son.' How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Sister (b) Niece (c) Daughter (d) Wife
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Sister, but the phrase 'my father's only son' refers to the man himself, not to a brother. The daughter of the man himself is his daughter, so the answer is Daughter.
Source: PYQ-style

53. If 'A + B' means 'A is the brother of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the sister of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the father of B', then what does 'P × Q + R – S' mean?
(a) P is the grandfather of S (b) P is the uncle of S (c) P is the brother of S (d) P is the father of S
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is grandfather, which would need two generations, but there is only one father-symbol here. P × Q makes P the father of Q, and Q + R – S shows Q, R and S are siblings, so the answer is P is the father of S.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: The tempting wrong option is Himself, but the speaker has no siblings, so 'my father's son' is the speaker himself, making the photographed man's father the speaker. Hence the photograph is of his son, so the answer is His son.

56. If 'A @ B' means 'A is the husband of B', 'A # B' means 'A is the daughter of B', and 'A $ B' means 'A is the brother of B', then what does 'M # N @ O # P' mean?
(a) M is the niece of P (b) M is the sister of P (c) M is the daughter of P (d) M is the granddaughter of P
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is daughter, but there are two daughter-steps, not one. M is the daughter of N, N is the husband of O, and O is the daughter of P, so M is one generation below O, and the answer is M is the granddaughter of P.
