Key Rules/Approaches
Rule Name	Description	Example
Anchor First	Mark the speaker or the person pointing as “ME” to fix a starting point.	“She is my mother's…” → Draw yourself as anchor.
Symbol Drawing	Use □ for male, ○ for female, — for marriage, │ for parent-child. Never assume gender from names.	“Kamal is brother of Rani” → Draw Kamal as □ because “brother” confirms gender.
Generation Row Rule	Place every generation on a separate horizontal line to avoid cross-generational mixing.	Grandparents top row, parents second, children third.
Step-by-Step Link (Lego Tower)	Always attach the new person to the last person you drew, not to an earlier one, to keep the chain unbroken.	“A’s mother’s brother” → Draw A’s mother, then her brother.
Direction Awareness	Read “A is B’s son” as A→B, not B→A. In coded relations, never reverse the operator.	“P $ Q = P is father of Q” means P’s line goes down to Q.
“Only” Keyword Superpower	“Only” collapses possibilities; there are no other siblings.	“My father’s only son” = the speaker himself (if male).
Dual Role Check	If a person appears in multiple statements, they may play two roles (e.g., both brother and son). Always check.	“A is the only brother of B and also the son of C.” → A is both brother and son.
Backward Tracing for Final Answer	For “How is A related to B?”, trace from B to A in the tree, naming each relation.	Tree shows B’s father’s son = A, so A is B’s brother.
Coded Relation Rule	Translate each coded operator separately into a visual link; never try to mentally combine them.	“P × Q + R” → draw P (wife of Q), then Q (brother of R).
Time Management
Rule	Description
15‑Second Rule	If within 15 seconds you haven’t identified the anchor and drawn the first relationship, mark the question for review and move on.
45‑Second Rule	Spend a maximum of 45 seconds drawing the full tree and tracing the answer. If the answer isn’t clear by then, eliminate obviously wrong choices, make an educated guess, and proceed.

No mathematical formulas exist for this chapter.	Blood Relations is a purely visual-logical topic. The “formula” is the family‑tree mapping method: □/○ symbols + connecting lines.
