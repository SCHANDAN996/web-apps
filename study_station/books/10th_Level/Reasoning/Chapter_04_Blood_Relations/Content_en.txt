# Blood Relations

At a wedding your father says — "That lady standing in front is the mother of your grandmother's only daughter-in-law." Who is she? Think a little — your grandmother's only daughter-in-law = your mother; your mother's mother = your maternal grandmother. These are blood-relation questions — untangling a puzzle of relationships.

**Definition:** In blood-relation questions, the relationships between family members are given, and we must find an unknown relationship — such as "How is X related to Y?"

🧠 Recall: What matters most in solving blood relations — memorising, or drawing the family tree?

## The Golden Rule: Draw the Family Tree

Never solve it only in your head — draw a small family tree on paper.

**Notation:**
- For a male, use (+) or a square □
- For a female, use (−) or a circle ○
- For a married couple, use an equals sign (=)
- Keep the same generation on the same line; the next generation below.

**Mind the generation:**
- Top: grandparents
- Middle: parents, uncles, aunts
- Bottom: sons, daughters, nephews, nieces

## Main Types

**1. Direct Relation**
"A is the brother of B, and B is the mother of C" → A is the maternal uncle of C.

**2. Pointing to a Photo**
"A man pointed to a photo and said — his father is the son of my father." → Since 'the son of my father' = the man himself (if he has no brother), the photo is of his son/daughter.

**3. Coded Relations**
Relationships are given as symbols: A + B (A is the father of B), A − B (A is the wife of B), etc. First understand the code, then apply it.

🧠 Recall: Who is "the only son of my father" (when the speaker is male)?

## The Examiner's Trap

⚠️ **Trap 1 — assuming gender:** Do not assume gender from the name. "Kiran" can be male or female. Take only what is given.

⚠️ **Trap 2 — reversing generation:** Between "father's father" (grandfather) and "son's son" (grandson), watch the direction carefully.

⚠️ **Trap 3 — maternal vs paternal:** Relations on the mother's side (maternal grandfather, maternal uncle) differ from the father's side (paternal grandfather, paternal uncle).
