101. Pointing to a woman, a man said, 'She is the daughter of the only daughter of my father.' How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Niece (b) Daughter (c) Sister (d) Wife
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Daughter, but the chain goes through the father's daughter, not the man himself. The only daughter of the man's father is the man's sister, and her daughter is his niece, so the answer is Niece.
Source: PYQ-style

102. If 'A + B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the brother of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the sister of B', then in 'P + Q – R × S', how is P related to S?
(a) Uncle (b) Brother (c) Grandfather (d) Father
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Uncle, which students pick by over-reading the chain. P is the father of Q; Q is the brother of R; R is the sister of S, so Q, R and S are siblings and P is the father of all of them, so the answer is Father.
Source: PYQ-style
