101. Pointing to a woman, a man said, 'She is the daughter of the only daughter of my father.' How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Niece (b) Daughter (c) Sister (d) Wife
Answer: (a)
Solution: The only daughter of the man's father is the man's sister. Her daughter is his niece.
Source: PYQ-style

102. If 'A + B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the brother of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the sister of B', then in 'P + Q – R × S', how is P related to S?
(a) Uncle (b) Father (c) Grandfather (d) Brother
Answer: (b)
Solution: P + Q means P is the father of Q. Q – R means Q is the brother of R. R × S means R is the sister of S. Q, R, and S are siblings, and P is their father. Therefore, P is the father of S.
Source: PYQ-style
