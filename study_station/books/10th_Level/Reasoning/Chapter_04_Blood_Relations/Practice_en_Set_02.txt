26. A is the sister of B. B is the brother of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Grandmother (b) Aunt (c) Sister (d) Mother
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is Mother, but C is the father of D, not A. A, B and C are siblings, so A is the paternal aunt of D, so the answer is Aunt.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Pointing to a photograph, a woman said, 'His brother's father is the only son of my grandfather.' How is the woman related to the person in the photograph?
(a) Mother (b) Sister (c) Aunt (d) Cousin
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Aunt, but the brother's father is the father of the person in the photograph, and that father is the only son of the woman's grandfather, so he is also the woman's father. Hence the woman is his sister, so the answer is Sister.

28. If 'A + B' means 'A is the son of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the daughter of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the brother of B', then what does 'P + Q × R – S' mean?
(a) P is the nephew of S (b) P is the son of S (c) P is the grandson of S (d) P is the brother of S
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Son, but P is the son of Q, not of S. Q is the brother of R and R is the daughter of S, so Q and R are children of S, making P the grandson of S, so the answer is Grandson.
