26. A is the sister of B. B is the brother of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Aunt (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Grandmother
Answer: (a)
Solution: A is the sister of B, and B is the brother of C, so A, B, and C are siblings. Since C is the father of D, A is the paternal aunt of D.

27. Pointing to a photograph, a woman said, 'His brother's father is the only son of my grandfather.' How is the woman related to the person in the photograph?

Solution: The brother's father is the father of the person in the photograph. That father is the only son of the woman's grandfather, meaning he is also the woman's father. Therefore, the woman is the sister of the person in the photograph.

28. If 'A + B' means 'A is the son of B', 'A – B' means 'A is the daughter of B', and 'A × B' means 'A is the brother of B', then what does 'P + Q × R – S' mean?
(a) P is the nephew of S (b) P is the son of S (c) P is the grandson of S (d) P is the brother of S
Answer: (c)
Solution: P + Q means P is the son of Q. Q × R means Q is the brother of R. R – S means R is the daughter of S. So Q and R are children of S. Since P is the son of Q, P is the grandson of S.

Answer: (c)
Solution: P – Q means P is the brother of Q. Q × R means Q is the wife of R. R + S means R is the father of S. Since Q is married to R, Q is the mother of S. Since P is the brother of Q (the mother of S), P is the maternal uncle of S.
