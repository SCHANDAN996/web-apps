🧠 Mnemonic Boxes for Blood Relations

🔲🧍 The Symbol Story: "Square Boy, Round Girl"

A square boy married a round girl.
They connected with a horizontal line (—) of love.
Then came a baby — a vertical line (│) dropped straight down.
That’s it: □ Male, ○ Female, — Marriage, │ Child.
No other symbol needed in any family ever.

🍳 The P-M Trick: "Papa’s Plate, Mummy’s Kitchen"

Paternal = Papa’s side.
Maternal = Mummy’s side.
Imagine: Papa’s family eats on a Plate; Mummy’s family cooks in the Kitchen.
Now, “father’s brother” → Papa’s side → Chacha/Tau.
“Mother’s brother” → Mummy’s side → Mama.
P for Papa, M for Mummy — never mix their plates again.

🧱 The Lego Tower Chant: "Brick by Brick, Never Quick"

"Chain is long, don’t panic —
Brick by brick, never quick.
New person? Click to the last,
Not to the first or the past."
Build your family tree like a Lego tower: one block at a time, each clicked to the one just before.

👁️ "Only Is Lonely" — The Superpower Word

"Only" is a lonely kid — it means no siblings.
So "My father’s only son" → no other brother → it’s ME!
"Only" crushes branches. Circle it like a superpower button every time you see it.

🚦 Direction Rule: "Read From the Arrow, Not the Sparrow"

“A is the son of B” means arrow goes A → B.
If you read B → A, you’re flying with the sparrow, not the arrow.
In coded relations: always read the operator left to right as given.
Never reverse — the examiner wants you to.

🧵 Coded Relations: "Code by Code, Draw a Road"

Each little code like +, –, @, $ is a stepping stone.
Don’t jump across the river — step on every stone.
Code by code, draw a road.
Translate one symbol → draw that relation → next symbol.
Mental mixing = mental mess.

📏 Generation Row Rule: "Gens on Steps, Don't Mix the Reps"

Draw each generation on a separate horizontal step.
Great-grandparents top step, then grandparents, parents, children.
Never put an uncle and a cousin on the same line — they're different steps.
"Gens on steps, no mess-ups."

🔙 Backward Tracing: "Start at the Question’s End, Trace Back to the Friend"

Question asks: "How is A related to B?"
Start from B, put your finger on B, and walk back to A through the lines.
The path tells the relation: "B’s father’s son = A" → A is brother.
"End to friend, the answer will send."

⚓ Anchor First: "Find the Speaker, Never the Seeker"

Every puzzle has a speaker or pointer. That person is your anchor.
Mark them "ME" and start the tree from there.
"Find the one who talks or points — that's where the map starts."

🎭 Dual Role Alert: "One Person, Two Hats — Watch for Rats!"

If the same person appears in multiple statements, they might be playing two roles.
E.g., "A is brother of B and son of C" — A is both brother and son.
"One person, two hats, watch for rats!"
Always check: is this person pulling double duty?

👗 Gender Trap: "Name’s Not Game — Wait for the Claim"

"Kamal", "Rajan", "Bobby" may be male or female in exam logic.
Never assume gender from name.
Wait for "brother", "sister", "he", "she", "wife", "husband" — the real gender claim.
"Name’s not game, wait for the claim."

👥 Brother-in-Law Jungle: "Bro-in-law Has Many Paw — Draw Before You Awe"

Brother-in-law = sister’s husband, wife’s brother, or husband’s brother.
Three possible animals, one name.
Don’t be awed by the term — draw the tree to see which paw fits.

⏱️ Time’s Up Tricks:

15-Second Snap Rule: "Fifteen ticks, no fix? Skip the tricks."
If within 15 seconds you can’t identify the anchor and draw the first relation, mark it and move on.

45-Second Grip Rule: "Grip it, rip it in forty-five — or let it slide."
Spend max 45 seconds per Blood Relations question. If the answer isn't clear by then, eliminate obviously wrong options, make an educated guess, and proceed.

🪄 Skip Strategy Summary
Condition	Action
Anchor not found or first link not drawn within 15 seconds	Skip and review later if time permits
Question uses 5+ coded operators or very long chains taking over 45 seconds	Mark, guess logically, move on
Statement has contradictory relationships (e.g., same person both father and brother to someone without clarification)	Don’t overthink — "Cannot be determined" may be the trap, but if stuck, skip
