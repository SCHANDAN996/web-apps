Q1. (Easy) What is the position of the letter A in the English alphabet?
(A) 1 (B) 0 (C) 2 (D) 26

Answer: (A) 1

Solution:
A is the first letter of the English alphabet, so its position is 1. Hence, the correct value is 1.

Source: SSC CGL

Q2. (Easy) What is the opposite letter of B in the English alphabet?
(A) X (B) Y (C) Z (D) W

Answer: (B) Y

Solution:
Position of B is 2. Opposite letter position = 27 - 2 = 25, which corresponds to Y.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. (Easy) According to the EJOTY mnemonic, what is the position of J in the alphabet?
(A) 8 (B) 9 (C) 10 (D) 11

Answer: (C) 10

Solution:
In the EJOTY rule (E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25), the position of J is 10. Hence, the answer is 10.

Source: SSC MTS

Q4. (Easy) If CAT is coded as DBU, how will DOG be coded in that language?
(A) EQG (B) EQH (C) EPG (D) EPH

Answer: (D) EPH

Solution:
Each letter shifts forward by +1: D(+1)=E, O(+1)=P, G(+1)=H. Thus, DOG is coded as EPH.

Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. (Easy) Find the next letter in the series: B, D, F, H, ?
(A) J (B) I (C) K (D) L

Answer: (A) J

Solution:
Positions are B(2), D(4), F(6), H(8). Each term increases by +2. Next position = 8 + 2 = 10, which is J.

Source: RRB Group D

Q6. (Easy) Which letter is 5th from the right end of the English alphabet?

Answer: (B) V

Solution:
From the right end: 27 - 5 = 22nd from left, which corresponds to V.

Source: SSC CGL

Q7. (Easy) Complete the analogy: ABC : DEF :: GHI : ?
(A) JLK (B) IJK (C) JKL (D) KLM

Answer: (C) JKL

Solution:
Each letter shifts forward by +3: G(+3)=J, H(+3)=K, I(+3)=L. Hence, the missing term is JKL.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q8. (Easy) Find the odd one out from the given options:

Answer: (D) A

Solution:
A is a vowel, while B, C, and D are consonants. Hence, A is the odd one out.

Source: SSC MTS

Q9. (Easy) If PEN is coded as 16-5-14, then BAT will be coded as:

Q10. (Easy) Find the next letter in the series: Z, X, V, T, ?
(A) S (B) R (C) Q (D) P

Answer: (B) R

Solution:
Positions decrease by 2: Z(26), X(24), V(22), T(20). Next position is 20 - 2 = 18, which is R.

Q11. (Easy) Complete the analogy: B : Y :: C : ?
(A) V (B) W (C) X (D) Z

Answer: (C) X

Solution:
B and Y are opposite letters (sum = 27). Similarly, the opposite letter of C is X (3 + 24 = 27).

Source: SSC CGL

Q12. (Easy) What comes next in the sequence: AB, EF, IJ, ?
(A) OP (B) LM (C) NO (D) MN

Answer: (D) MN

Solution:
First letters increase by +4: A(1), E(5), I(9) → M(13). Second letters increase by +4: B(2), F(6), J(10) → N(14). Hence, MN.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q15. (Easy) Find the next letter in the alternating series: A, Z, B, Y, C, X, ?
(A) E (B) W (C) D (D) V

Answer: (C) D

Solution:
Two intertwined series: odd terms increase (A, B, C, next D), even terms decrease (Z, Y, X). Next is 7th term → D.

Q17. (Easy) Complete the analogy: ACE : GIK :: MOQ : ?
(A) SUW (B) STV (C) SUV (D) SVW

Answer: (A) SUW

Solution:
Each letter advances by +6: M(13)+6=S(19), O(15)+6=U(21), Q(17)+6=W(23). Hence, SUW.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q20. (Easy) If FRIEND is coded as GSJFOE, then ENEMY will be coded as:
(A) FOFPZ (B) FODNZ (C) FOFMZ (D) FOFNZ

Q21. (Medium) Find the next letter in the series: A, C, F, J, ?
(A) O (B) P (C) M (D) N

Answer: (A) O

Solution:
Positional values increase with increasing gaps: A(1)+2=C(3), C(3)+3=F(6), F(6)+4=J(10), next gap is +5 → 10+5=15, which corresponds to O.

Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) ⚠️ Complete the analogy: BDF : HJL :: NPR : ?
(A) TVW (B) TVX (C) TVY (D) TVZ

Answer: (B) TVX

Solution:
Each letter advances by +6: B(2)+6=H(8), D(4)+6=J(10), F(6)+6=L(12). Applying to NPR: N(14)+6=T(20), P(16)+6=V(22), R(18)+6=X(24) → TVX. Examiner's Trap: Adding +5 to the last letter gives TVW, which is an incorrect trap.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q23. (Medium) ⚠️ In a certain code, RAT is written as JAH. How will DOG be written in that code?
(A) XLU (B) WLT (C) XMU (D) WMT

Answer: (C) XMU

Solution:
Rule: Take opposite letter then add +1. R(opposite I=9)+1=J(10); A(opposite Z=26)+1=A(1); T(opposite G=7)+1=H(8) → JAH. For DOG: D(opposite W=23)+1=X(24); O(opposite L=12)+1=M(13); G(opposite T=20)+1=U(21) → XMU. Examiner's Trap: Forgetting the +1 step yields WLT.

Source: SSC MTS

Q24. (Medium) Find the odd one out among the following letters: H, J, P, T
(A) T (B) H (C) J (D) P

Answer: (D) P

Solution:
Positional values: H=8, J=10, P=16, T=20. Among these, P(16) is a perfect square (4 squared = 16), while none of the others are perfect squares. Hence, P is the odd one.

Source: RRB NTPC

Q25. (Medium) Find the next term in the alphanumeric series: 2Z5, 7Y10, 12X15, 17W20, ?
(A) 23V25 (B) 22V25 (C) 22U25 (D) 22V24

Answer: (B) 22V25

Solution:
First number increases by +5: 2, 7, 12, 17 → 22. Middle letter moves backward: Z, Y, X, W → V. Last number increases by +5: 5, 10, 15, 20 → 25. Thus, the next term is 22V25.

Source: RRB Group D
