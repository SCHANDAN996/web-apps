22. ⚠️ ACE : GIK :: MOQ : ?
(A) SUW (B) STV (C) SUV (D) SVW

Answer: (A) SUW

Solution:
Examiner’s Trap: All letters move +6, but if you mistakenly move the last letter only +5, you get V, making (B) tempting.
A→G (+6), C→I (+6), E→K (+6); apply exactly to M→S (+6), O→U (+6), Q→W (+6) → SUW.

Source: PYQ-style

23. ⚠️ In a certain code, RAT is written as JAH. How is DOG written?
(A) XMU (B) WLT (C) XLU (D) WMT

Answer: (A) XMU

Solution:
Examiner’s Trap: First, take the opposite letter, then add 1 (with wrap-around).
R(18) → opposite I(9) +1 = J(10); A(1) → opposite Z(26) +1 = A(27→1); T(20) → opposite G(7) +1 = H(8) → JAH.
DOG: D(4) → opposite W(23) +1 = X(24); O(15) → opposite L(12) +1 = M(13); G(7) → opposite T(20) +1 = U(21) → XMU.
Trap option (B) WLT is obtained if the +1 step is skipped.

Source: PYQ-style
