PYQ Analysis: Alphabet Questions (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Alphabet questions give 1–3 questions per exam. Position and count-from-right types are most common. The trap is mixing left/right and the "between" double count.

Q1. Which is the 10th letter from the left in the alphabet?
(a) I (b) J (c) K (d) H
Answer: (b) J
Solution: J = 10th position.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. Which is the 5th letter from the right?
(a) U (b) V (c) W (d) T
Answer: (b) V
Solution: From right = 27 − 5 = 22nd from left = V.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. How many letters are there in the English alphabet?
(a) 24 (b) 25 (c) 26 (d) 27
Answer: (c) 26
Solution: A to Z is 26 letters.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q4. How many letters are there between C and H?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: 8 − 3 − 1 = 4 letters (D, E, F, G).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q5. Which is the 3rd letter to the right of L?
(a) N (b) O (c) P (d) M
Answer: (b) O
Solution: L(12) + 3 = 15 = O.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. What is the position of O in the alphabet?
(a) 14 (b) 15 (c) 16 (d) 13
Answer: (b) 15
Solution: EJOTY — O = 15.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. Which is the 15th letter from the left?
(a) N (b) O (c) P (d) M
Answer: (b) O
Solution: O = 15th position.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q8. Which is the 20th letter from the left?
(a) S (b) T (c) U (d) R
Answer: (b) T
Solution: EJOTY — T = 20.
Source: SSC CGL

Q9. Which is the 8th letter from the right?
(a) R (b) S (c) T (d) Q
Answer: (b) S
Solution: From right = 27 − 8 = 19th from left = S.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q10. Which letter is exactly in the middle of A and G?
(a) C (b) D (c) E (d) F
Answer: (b) D
Solution: Middle = (1 + 7) ÷ 2 = 4 = D.
Source: SSC CGL
