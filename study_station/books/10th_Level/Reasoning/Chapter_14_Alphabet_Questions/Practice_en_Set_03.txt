Q51. (Medium) Find the next term in the series: AZ, CX, EV, ?
(A) GT (B) GS (C) FT (D) FS

Answer: (A) GT

Solution:
First letters shift by +2: A(1)→C(3)→E(5)→G(7). Second letters shift by -2: Z(26)→X(24)→V(22)→T(20). Thus, the next term is GT.

Source: SSC CGL

Q52. (Medium) ⚠️ Find the odd one out among the given letter clusters: ACE, BDF, GIK, MOQ
(A) ACE (B) BDF (C) GIK (D) MOQ

Answer: (B) BDF

Solution:
All letters in ACE (1,3,5), GIK (7,9,11), and MOQ (13,15,17) have odd alphabetical positions. In BDF (2,4,6), all letters have even positions. Examiner's Trap: In all groups the difference between letters is 2, so only odd/even positions distinguish them.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q53. (Medium) In a certain code, 'FLOWER' is written as 'GMPXFS'. How will 'GARDEN' be written in that code?
(A) HBSFDP (B) HBSEFO (C) HBSFEO (D) HBSFDO

Answer: (C) HBSFEO

Solution:
Each letter advances by +1: F→G, L→M, O→P, W→X, E→F, R→S. For GARDEN: G→H, A→B, R→S, D→E, E→F, N→O → HBSFEO.

Source: SSC MTS

Q55. (Medium) How many such pairs of letters are there in the word 'CREATE' each of which has as many letters between them as in the English alphabet?
(A) 1 (B) 0 (C) 2 (D) 3

Answer: (A) 1

Solution:
Letters are C(1), R(2), E(3), A(4), T(5), E(6). Pair C and E: C(3) and E(5) have 1 letter between (D) in alphabet; in word, R is between them (1 letter). Hence, the total number of such letter pairs is 1.

Source: RRB Group D

Q56. (Medium) ⚠️ In a certain code language, 'CAT' is written as 'XZG'. How will 'MAN' be written in that code?
(A) MZN (B) NZM (C) NZN (D) OZM

Answer: (B) NZM

Solution:
Each letter is replaced by its opposite letter: C(3)↔X(24), A(1)↔Z(26), T(20)↔G(7). For MAN: M(13)↔N(14), A(1)↔Z(26), N(14)↔M(13) → NZM. Examiner's Trap: Forgetting to take the opposite of M and writing MZN is a common error.

Source: SSC CGL

Q57. (Medium) Complete the analogy: IKM : OQS :: LNP : ?
(A) RUV (B) RSU (C) RTV (D) STV

Answer: (C) RTV

Solution:
Each letter advances by +6: I(9)+6=O(15), K(11)+6=Q(17), M(13)+6=S(19). For LNP: L(12)+6=R(18), N(14)+6=T(20), P(16)+6=V(22) → RTV.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q58. (Medium) Find the next letter in the series: Z, X, V, T, ?
(A) P (B) Q (C) S (D) R

Answer: (D) R

Solution:
Positions decrease by 2 each time: Z(26), X(24), V(22), T(20) → 20 - 2 = 18 = R.

Source: SSC MTS

Q59. (Medium) In the word 'EXAMINATION', if all vowels are removed, which letter will be 5th from the left?
(A) N (B) M (C) T (D) X

Answer: (A) N

Solution:
Original word: E X A M I N A T I O N. Vowels are E, A, I, A, I, O. Remaining consonants in order: X, M, N, T, N. The 5th consonant from the left is N.

Q63. (Medium) How many such pairs of letters are there in the word 'TEACHER' each of which has as many letters between them as in the English alphabet?

Solution:

Q66. (Medium) ⚠️ Complete the analogy: PRT : VXZ :: KMO : ?
(A) QTV (B) QRT (C) PSU (D) QSU

Answer: (D) QSU

Solution:
Each letter advances by +6: P(16)+6=V(22), R(18)+6=X(24), T(20)+6=Z(26). For KMO: K(11)+6=Q(17), M(13)+6=S(19), O(15)+6=U(21) → QSU. Examiner's Trap: Adding +5 to the middle letter yields QRT.

Source: SSC CGL

Q67. (Medium) Which letter is 7th to the left of the 18th letter from the right in the English alphabet?
(A) B (B) C (C) D (D) E

Answer: (A) B

Solution:
18th from right = 27 - 18 = 9th from left (I). 7th to the left of 9th is 9 - 7 = 2nd from left, which is B.
