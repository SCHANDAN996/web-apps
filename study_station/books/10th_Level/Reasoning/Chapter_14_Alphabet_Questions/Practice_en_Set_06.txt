Q126. (Medium) ⚠️ In a certain code, 'HELLO' is written as 'QNNGJ'. How will 'WORLD' be written in that code?
(A) FNTQY (B) YQTNF (C) FNTQZ (D) FNSQY

Answer: (A) FNTQY

Solution:
Pattern: Advance each letter by +2, then reverse the entire word. HELLO: H(+2)=J, E(+2)=G, L(+2)=N, L(+2)=N, O(+2)=Q → JGNNQ, reversed = QNNGJ. For WORLD: W(+2)=Y, O(+2)=Q, R(+2)=T, L(+2)=N, D(+2)=F → YQTNF, reversed = FNTQY. Examiner's Trap: Forgetting to reverse the word gives YQTNF.

Source: SSC CGL

Q127. (Medium) ⚠️ Find the odd one out among the given groups: NPR, TVX, MOQ, PRT
(A) NPR (B) MOQ (C) TVX (D) PRT

Answer: (B) MOQ

Solution:
NPR (14,16,18), TVX (20,22,24), and PRT (16,18,20) consist entirely of letters in even positions. MOQ (13,15,17) consists entirely of letters in odd positions. Examiner's Trap: All clusters have identical internal gaps of +2, so odd/even position is crucial.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q128. (Medium) Find the next term in the series: A2, C5, F10, J17, ?
(A) N26 (B) O25 (C) O26 (D) P26

Answer: (C) O26

Solution:
Letters: A(1)→C(3)→F(6)→J(10) with increasing differences +2, +3, +4 → next is +5 = 15 = O. Numbers follow n^2 + 1: 1^2+1=2, 2^2+1=5, 3^2+1=10, 4^2+1=17 → 5^2+1=26. Thus, O26.

Source: SSC MTS

Q129. (Medium) Complete the alphanumeric series: AC3, CE8, EG15, GI24, ?
(A) HK35 (B) IK36 (C) IJ35 (D) IK35

Answer: (D) IK35

Solution:
First letters advance by +2: A, C, E, G → I. Second letters advance by +2: C, E, G, I → K. Numbers are product of positions: 1x3=3, 3x5? Actually differences: 3(+5)=8(+7)=15(+9)=24(+11)=35. Thus, IK35.

Source: RRB NTPC

Q130. (Medium) ⚠️ In a certain code, 'ACT' is written as 'BEW'. How will 'DOG' be written in that code?
(A) EQJ (B) EPH (C) DPH (D) EQK

Answer: (A) EQJ

Solution:
Pattern: 1st letter +1, 2nd letter +2, 3rd letter +3. ACT: A(+1)=B, C(+2)=E, T(+3)=W → BEW. For DOG: D(+1)=E, O(+2)=Q, G(+3)=J → EQJ. Examiner's Trap: Applying a uniform +1 shift gives EPH.

Q132. (Medium) If the first half of the English alphabet (A to M) is written in reverse order while the second half remains unchanged, which letter will be 7th to the right of the 15th letter from the right?
(A) T (B) R (C) S (D) Q

Answer: (C) S

Solution:
7th to the right of the 15th letter from the right is the (15 - 7) = 8th letter from the right end. In the unchanged second half (N to Z), counting 8 letters backward from Z gives: Z(1), Y(2), X(3), W(4), V(5), U(6), T(7), S(8) → S.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q133. (Medium) In a certain code, 'PRAISE' is written as 'DRHZQO'. How will 'LAUNCH' be written in that code?
(A) GBMSZK (B) GBMTZJ (C) GBNTZK (D) GBMTZK

Answer: (D) GBMTZK

Solution:
Pattern: Reverse the word, then subtract 1 from each letter. PRAISE reversed = ESIARP; subtracting 1: E→D, S→R, I→H, A→Z, R→Q, P→O → DRHZQO. For LAUNCH: reversed = HCNUAL; subtracting 1: H→G, C→B, N→M, U→T, A→Z, L→K → GBMTZK.

Q134. (Medium) Find the next term in the series: Z1, X3, V6, T10, ?
(A) R15 (B) S15 (C) R14 (D) S14

Answer: (A) R15

Solution:
Letters decrease by -2: Z(26)→X(24)→V(22)→T(20)→R(18). Numbers increase by consecutive differences: 1(+2)=3, 3(+3)=6, 6(+4)=10, next is +5: 10 + 5 = 15. Thus, R15.

Q135. (Medium) ⚠️ If 'HUNGRY' is coded as 'IVMHSZ', how will 'THIRST' be coded?
(A) UJHSTU (B) UIHSTU (C) UIHSTV (D) UIHSSU

Answer: (B) UIHSTU

Solution:
Pattern in HUNGRY → IVMHSZ: H(+1)=I, U(+1)=V, N(-1)=M (3rd letter decreases by 1), G(+1)=H, R(+1)=S, Y(+1)=Z. All letters shift by +1 except the 3rd letter which shifts by -1. For THIRST: T(+1)=U, H(+1)=I, I(-1)=H, R(+1)=S, S(+1)=T, T(+1)=U → UIHSTU. Examiner's Trap: Adding +1 to all letters yields UJISTU.

Source: RRB Group D

Q142. (Medium) Find the next term in the sequence: C4, F9, I16, L25, ?
