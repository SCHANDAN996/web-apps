101. Find the next term: AC, EG, IK, ?
(A) MO (B) MP (C) NO (D) NP

Answer: (A) MO

Solution:
First letters: A(1), E(5), I(9) → +4 → M(13). Second letters: C(3), G(7), K(11) → +4 → O(15). So MO.

Source: SSC CHSL 2020 Tier-1

102. ⚠️ Find the odd one out:
(A) ACE (B) BDF (C) GIK (D) PRT

Answer: (B) BDF

Solution:
ACE: A(1), C(3), E(5) – odd positions; GIK: 7,9,11 – odd; PRT: 16,18,20 – even; BDF: 2,4,6 – even. But BDF is the only group with all even positions starting from 2, while others are odd or start from 16. The most distinct is BDF as the only set with smallest even numbers.
Trap: Some might pick PRT as the only even set, but BDF is also even. However, ACE and GIK share odd, BDF stands out as the set that is first consecutive even numbers.

Source: SSC MTS 2021

103. In a certain code, 'CHAIR' is written as 'DIBJS'. How is 'TABLE' written?
(A) UBCMF (B) UBCNF (C) UBDMF (D) UBCMG

Answer: (A) UBCMF

Solution:
Each letter +1: C+1=D, H+1=I, A+1=B, I+1=J, R+1=S → DIBJS. TABLE: T+1=U, A+1=B, B+1=C, L+1=M, E+1=F → UBCMF.

Source: SSC CGL 2019 Tier-1

104. How many such pairs of letters are there in the word 'NATURE' each of which has as many letters between them in the word as in the English alphabet?
(A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 3

Answer: (B) 1

Solution:
Word: N(1), A(2), T(3), U(4), R(5), E(6). Positions: T=20, U=21 → diff 1 (0 between); in word T(3) U(4) adjacent (0) → valid. Other pairs fail.

Source: SSC CHSL 2020 Tier-1

105. What comes next: Z1, Y2, X3, W4, ?
(A) V5 (B) V6 (C) U5 (D) U6

Answer: (A) V5

Solution:
Letters: Z, Y, X, W → –1 → V. Numbers: 1,2,3,4 → +1 → 5. So V5.

Source: SSC MTS 2019

106. If 'MOON' is coded as 'LNNM', then 'STAR' is coded as:
(A) RSZQ (B) RSZR (C) RTZQ (D) RSYQ

Answer: (A) RSZQ

Solution:
Each letter –1: M→L, O→N, O→N, N→M. S→R, T→S, A→Z, R→Q → RSZQ.

Source: SSC CHSL 2021

107. If the first half of the English alphabet (A to M) is reversed, then which letter will be 6th to the left of the 10th letter from the right in the new series?
(A) J (B) K (C) L (D) I

Answer: (B) K

Solution:
New series: M L K J I H G F E D C B A N O P Q R S T U V W X Y Z.
10th from right: Z(1), Y(2), X(3), W(4), V(5), U(6), T(7), S(8), R(9), Q(10) → Q.
6th left of Q: Q → P(1), O(2), N(3), M(4), L(5), K(6) → K.

Source: SSC CGL 2018 Tier-2

108. ⚠️ ACE : GIK :: BDF : ?
(A) HJL (B) HJM (C) HKN (D) IKM

Answer: (A) HJL

Solution:
Each letter +6: A+6=G, C+6=I, E+6=K. B(2)+6=H(8), D(4)+6=J(10), F(6)+6=L(12) → HJL.
Trap: Option (B) HJM appears if the last letter is incorrectly moved by +5 (F+5=K? no, F+5=K not J). The trap is designed to catch students who add +5 instead of +6 on the last letter.

Source: SSC MTS 2020

110. If all the letters of the word 'EXCELLENT' are arranged in alphabetical order, which letter will be fifth from the right?
(A) L (B) E (C) T (D) N

Answer: (A) L

Solution:
Word: E X C E L L E N T → letters: C, E, E, E, L, L, N, T, X.
Alphabetical order: C, E, E, E, L, L, N, T, X. Fifth from right: X(1), T(2), N(3), L(4), L(5) → L.

Source: SSC MTS 2018

111. If PEN = 35 and BAT = 23, then DOG = ?
(A) 26 (B) 27 (C) 28 (D) 25

Answer: (A) 26

Solution:
Sum of positional values: P=16+E=5+N=14 = 35; B=2+A=1+T=20 = 23. DOG: D=4+O=15+G=7 = 26.

Source: SSC CGL 2020 Tier-1

112. Which letter is 7th to the right of the 12th letter from the left in the English alphabet?
(A) S (B) R (C) T (D) Q

Answer: (A) S

Solution:
12th from left = L(12). 7th right of L: L(12) → M13, N14, O15, P16, Q17, R18, S19 → S.

Source: SSC CHSL 2019

113. How many such pairs of letters are there in the word 'BRIDGE' each of which has as many letters between them in the word as in the alphabet?
(A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 3

Answer: (B) 1

Solution:
Word: B(1), R(2), I(3), D(4), G(5), E(6). I=9, G=7 → diff 2 → 1 letter between (H). In word, between I(3) and G(5): D(4) → 1 letter. Valid. Other pairs fail.

Source: SSC MTS 2020

114. ⚠️ If 'CYCLE' is coded as 'BXBKD', then 'MOTOR' is coded as:
(A) LNSNQ (B) LNSNR (C) LNSOQ (D) LNSNP

Answer: (A) LNSNQ

Solution:
Each letter –1: C→B, Y→X, C→B, L→K, E→D → BXBKD. MOTOR: M→L, O→N, T→S, O→N, R→Q → LNSNQ.
Trap: Option (D) LNSNP appears if one mistakenly subtracts 1 from O but counts P as the result instead of N? Actually O-1=N, not P. The trap can be a simple arithmetic error.

Source: SSC CHSL 2020

115. What comes next: 2Z5, 7Y10, 12X15, 17W20, ?

Answer: (A) 22V25

Solution:
First number: +5 (2,7,12,17 → 22). Letter: Z, Y, X, W → –1 → V. Last number: +5 (5,10,15,20 → 25). So 22V25.

120. If 'ABCD' is written as 'ZYXW', then 'PQRS' is written as:

121. ⚠️ (Hard) In a certain code, 'BASKET' is written as 'CBTJFU'. How will 'TACKLE' be written in that code?
(A) UBDJMF (B) UBDLMF (C) UBDJNF (D) UBDKMF

Answer: (A) UBDJMF

Solution:
Pattern in BASKET → CBTJFU:
B(+1)=C, A(+1)=B, S(+1)=T, K(-1)=J, E(+1)=F, T(+1)=U.
So the rule: all letters +1 except the 4th letter, which gets –1.
Apply to TACKLE (6 letters):
T(+1)=U, A(+1)=B, C(+1)=D, K(-1)=J, L(+1)=M, E(+1)=F → UBDJMF.
Trap: If a uniform +1 is applied, the result would be UBDLMF (option B). Many students will not spot the irregularity in the 4th position.

Source: SSC CGL 2018 Tier-2

122. (Hard) How many such pairs of letters are there in the word 'REVOLUTION' each of which has as many letters between them in the word as in the English alphabet?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4

Answer: (C) 3

Solution:
Word: R(1), E(2), V(3), O(4), L(5), U(6), T(7), I(8), O(9), N(10). Positions: R=18, E=5, V=22, O=15, L=12, U=21, T=20, I=9, O=15, N=14.
Check valid pairs:

R (1st) and O (4th): R=18, O=15, diff 3 → 2 letters between (16,17). In word, between R and O: E, V → 2 letters. Valid.

U (6th) and T (7th): U=21, T=20, diff 1 → 0 between. In word, adjacent (0 between). Valid.

O (9th) and N (10th): O=15, N=14, diff 1 → 0 between. In word, adjacent → valid.
Thus, total 3 pairs.

Source: SSC CGL 2021 Tier-2

123. (Hard) If the first half of the English alphabet (A to M) is reversed and the second half (N to Z) is also reversed, then what will be the 7th letter to the left of the 10th letter from the right in the new arrangement?
(A) Q (B) P (C) O (D) R

Answer: (B) P

Solution:
First half reversed: M L K J I H G F E D C B A
Second half reversed: Z Y X W V U T S R Q P O N
Combined: M L K J I H G F E D C B A Z Y X W V U T S R Q P O N
10th from right: from right end N(1), O(2), P(3), Q(4), R(5), S(6), T(7), U(8), V(9), W(10) → W.
7th left of W: W → V(1), U(2), T(3), S(4), R(5), Q(6), P(7) → P.

Source: SSC CGL 2019 Tier-2

124. (Hard) In a certain code language, 'PAINT' is written as 'QZJOU'. How is 'BRUSH' written in that code?
(A) CQVTI (B) CQTVI (C) CQUTI (D) CQVTH

Answer: (A) CQVTI

Solution:
PAINT → QZJOU:
P(16)+1=Q(17), A(1)-1=Z(26), I(9)+1=J(10), N(14)+1=O(15), T(20)+1=U(21).
Pattern: first letter +1, second letter –1 (with wrap), third +1, fourth +1, fifth +1.
Apply to BRUSH:
B(2)+1=C(3), R(18)-1=Q(17), U(21)+1=V(22), S(19)+1=T(20), H(8)+1=I(9) → CQVTI.

Source: Bank PO 2019 (Adapted)

125. (Hard) Find the next term in the series: BMX, DNW, FOV, ?
(A) HNU (B) HOU (C) HPU (D) GPU

Answer: (C) HPU

Solution:
First letters: B(2), D(4), F(6) → +2 → H(8).
Second letters: M(13), N(14), O(15) → +1 → P(16).
Third letters: X(24), W(23), V(22) → –1 → U(21).
Thus, HPU.

Source: SSC CGL 2020 Tier-2
