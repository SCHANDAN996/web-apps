Q76. (Medium) Find the next term in the series: BD, FH, JL, ?
(A) NP (B) NO (C) NQ (D) MP

Answer: (A) NP

Solution:
First letters advance by +4: B(2)→F(6)→J(10)→N(14). Second letters advance by +4: D(4)→H(8)→L(12)→P(16). Thus, the next term is NP.

Source: SSC CGL

Q77. (Medium) ⚠️ Find the odd one out among the given groups: NPR, TVX, MOQ, PRT
(A) NPR (B) MOQ (C) TVX (D) PRT

Answer: (B) MOQ

Solution:
All letters in NPR (14,16,18), TVX (20,22,24), and PRT (16,18,20) have even positions. In MOQ (13,15,17), all letters have odd positions. Examiner's Trap: In all groups the difference between letters is 2, so checking odd vs even is essential.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q78. (Medium) If 'DESK' is coded as 'CDRJ', then how will 'LAMP' be coded?
(A) KZMO (B) KZLP (C) KZLO (D) KZLN

Answer: (C) KZLO

Solution:
Each letter shifts back by -1: D→C, E→D, S→R, K→J. For LAMP: L→K, A→Z, M→L, P→O → KZLO.

Q79. (Medium) How many such pairs of letters are there in the word 'PROBLEM' each of which has as many letters between them as in the English alphabet?
(A) 0 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 1

Answer: (D) 1

Solution:
In 'PROBLEM', letters P (position 16) and L (position 12) have 3 letters between them in the alphabet (M, N, O). In the word 'PROBLEM', between P(1) and L(5) there are exactly 3 letters (R, O, B). Hence, the total number of such letter pairs is 1.

Q87. (Medium) In the word 'MATHEMATICS', if all vowels are removed, which consonant will be 5th from the right?

Answer: (D) H

Solution:
Consonants of MATHEMATICS in order: M, T, H, M, T, C, S (7 consonants). Counting from the right: S(1st), C(2nd), T(3rd), M(4th), H(5th). Hence, H is 5th from the right.

Q88. (Medium) ⚠️ Find the odd one out among the letter clusters: HJN, JLP, LNR, NPR
(A) NPR (B) HJN (C) JLP (D) LNR

Answer: (A) NPR

Solution:
In HJN: H(+2)→J(+4)→N. In JLP: J(+2)→L(+4)→P. In LNR: L(+2)→N(+4)→R. In NPR: N(+2)→P(+2)→R (pattern is +2, +2 instead of +2, +4). Examiner's Trap: All letters are even, so checking internal intervals is necessary.

Source: SSC MTS

Q90. (Medium) Which letter is 5th to the left of the 10th letter from the right in the English alphabet?
(A) M (B) K (C) L (D) N

Answer: (C) L

Solution:
10th from right = 27 - 10 = 17th from left (Q). 5th to the left of 17th is 17 - 5 = 12th from left, which is L.
