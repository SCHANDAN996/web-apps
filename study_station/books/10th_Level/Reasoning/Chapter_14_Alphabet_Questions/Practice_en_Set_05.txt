Q101. (Medium) Find the next term in the series: AC, EG, IK, ?
(A) MO (B) MP (C) NO (D) NP

Answer: (A) MO

Solution:
First letters shift by +4: A(1)→E(5)→I(9)→M(13). Second letters shift by +4: C(3)→G(7)→K(11)→O(15). Thus, MO.

Source: SSC CGL

Q102. (Medium) ⚠️ Find the odd one out among the given letter clusters: ACE, GIK, MOQ, BDF
(A) ACE (B) BDF (C) GIK (D) MOQ

Answer: (B) BDF

Solution:
All letters in ACE (1,3,5), GIK (7,9,11), and MOQ (13,15,17) occupy odd positions. In BDF (2,4,6), all letters occupy even positions. Examiner's Trap: All groups have a difference of +2 between letters, so odd/even position is the distinguishing factor.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q105. (Medium) Find the next term in the sequence: Z1, Y2, X3, W4, ?

Q107. (Medium) If the first half of the English alphabet (A to M) is written in reverse order while the second half remains unchanged, which letter will be 6th to the left of the 10th letter from the right?
(A) D (B) B (C) C (D) A

Answer: (C) C

Solution:
Rearranged series: M L K J I H G F E D C B A N O P Q R S T U V W X Y Z. 10th letter from the right is Q (position 27 - 10 = 17th from left). The 6th letter to the left of 17th is 17 - 6 = 11th from left. In the reversed first half, the 11th letter is C.

Q121. (Hard) ⚠️ In a certain code, 'BASKET' is written as 'CBTJFU'. How will 'TACKLE' be written in that code?
(A) UBDJMF (B) UBDLMF (C) UBDJNF (D) UBDKMF

Answer: (A) UBDJMF

Solution:

Source: SSC CGL
