Q26. (Medium) Find the next term in the series: ABD, EFH, IJL, ?
(A) MNP (B) MNO (C) MNR (D) MNS

Answer: (A) MNP

Solution:
First letters shift by +4: A(1)→E(5)→I(9)→M(13). Second letters by +4: B(2)→F(6)→J(10)→N(14). Third letters by +4: D(4)→H(8)→L(12)→P(16). Thus, MNP.

Source: SSC CGL

Q27. (Medium) ⚠️ Find the odd one out among the given letters: H, J, P, T
(A) H (B) P (C) J (D) T

Answer: (B) P

Solution:
Positional values are H=8, J=10, P=16, T=20. Among these, P(16) is a perfect square (4 squared = 16), while the others are not. Examiner's Trap: All numbers are even, so one must look for the square property.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q28. (Medium) In a certain code, 'PART' is written as 'KZIG'. How will 'SAME' be written in that code?
(A) HZNU (B) HZMV (C) HZNV (D) HZMU

Answer: (C) HZNV

Solution:
Each letter is replaced by its opposite letter: P↔K, A↔Z, R↔I, T↔G. For SAME: S↔H, A↔Z, M↔N, E↔V → HZNV.

Source: SSC MTS

Q29. (Medium) ⚠️ If 'BLUE' is coded as 'DPYG', then 'GREEN' is coded as:
(A) IVHJQ (B) IVHIP (C) IUIIP (D) IVIIP

Answer: (D) IVIIP

Solution:
Pattern is +2, +4, +4, +2: B(+2)=D, L(+4)=P, U(+4)=Y, E(+2)=G. For GREEN: G(+2)=I, R(+4)=V, E(+4)=I, E(+4)=I, N(+2)=P → IVIIP. Examiner's Trap: A uniform +2 shift gives ITGGP, which is wrong.

Source: RRB NTPC

Q30. (Medium) Find the next letter in the series: B, E, I, N, ?
(A) T (B) S (C) U (D) R

Answer: (A) T

Solution:
The differences increase: B(2)+3=E(5), E(5)+4=I(9), I(9)+5=N(14). Next difference is +6: 14 + 6 = 20, which is T.

Source: RRB Group D

Q31. (Medium) Complete the analogy: DFH : JLN :: PRT : ?
(A) VXW (B) VXZ (C) VXY (D) WXZ

Answer: (B) VXZ

Solution:
Each letter advances by +6: D(4)+6=J(10), F(6)+6=L(12), H(8)+6=N(14). For PRT: P(16)+6=V(22), R(18)+6=X(24), T(20)+6=Z(26) → VXZ.

Source: SSC CGL

Q32. (Medium) Find the next letter in the decreasing sequence: Z, X, V, T, ?
(A) S (B) Q (C) R (D) P

Answer: (C) R

Solution:
Positional values decrease by 2 each time: Z(26), X(24), V(22), T(20) → 20 - 2 = 18, which is R.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q33. (Medium) Find the odd one out among the letters: C, F, I, L
(A) L (B) C (C) F (D) I

Answer: (D) I

Solution:
C(3), F(6), I(9), L(12) are all multiples of 3. Among them, I is a vowel while C, F, and L are consonants. Hence, I is the odd one.

Source: SSC MTS

Q34. (Medium) If AB = 3 and CD = 7, then what is the value of EF?
(A) 11 (B) 12 (C) 13 (D) 14

Answer: (A) 11

Solution:
Sum of alphabetical positions: AB = 1+2 = 3; CD = 3+4 = 7; EF = 5+6 = 11. Hence, the correct value is 11.

Source: RRB NTPC

Q35. (Medium) How many such pairs of letters are there in the word 'BAND' which have as many letters between them as in the English alphabet?
(A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 3

Answer: (B) 1

Solution:
In 'BAND', the pair is B and A (positions 2 and 1 in alphabet, difference 1, 0 letters between; in the word they are adjacent, 0 letters between). Hence, the total number of such letter pairs is 1.

Source: RRB Group D

Q36. (Medium) In a certain code, 'MONKEY' is written as 'LNMJDX'. How will 'TIGER' be written in that code?
(A) SHEDQ (B) SHFDP (C) SHFDQ (D) SHFCP

Answer: (C) SHFDQ

Solution:
Each letter shifts back by -1: M→L, O→N, N→M, K→J, E→D, Y→X. For TIGER: T→S, I→H, G→F, E→D, R→Q → SHFDQ.

Source: SSC CGL

Q40. (Medium) Find the next letter in the series: B, E, H, K, ?

Q41. (Medium) If 'HONEY' is coded as 'IPOFZ', then how will 'SUGAR' be coded?

Answer: (D) TVHBS

Solution:

Q42. (Medium) How many letters are there between the 10th letter from the left and the 6th letter from the right in the English alphabet?
(A) 10 (B) 8 (C) 9 (D) 11

Answer: (A) 10

Solution:
10th from left is J(10). 6th from right is 27 - 6 = 21st letter, U(21). Letters between J and U are K(11) to T(20), which is 20 - 11 + 1 = 10 letters. Hence, the answer is 10.

Source: SSC CHSL

Q44. (Medium) Find the missing term in the sequence: AD, FI, KN, ?
(A) PQ (B) PR (C) PS (D) PT

Answer: (C) PS

Solution:
First letters shift by +5: A(1)→F(6)→K(11)→P(16). Second letters shift by +5: D(4)→I(9)→N(14)→S(19). Thus, the missing term is PS.

Q45. (Medium) ⚠️ In a certain code, 'RAT' is written as 'IZG'. How will 'DOG' be written in that code?

Q48. (Hard) Which letter is 8th to the left of the 12th letter from the right in the English alphabet?
(A) F (B) H (C) G (D) I

Answer: (C) G

Solution:
12th from right = 27 - 12 = 15th from left (O). 8th to the left of 15th is 15 - 8 = 7th from left, which is G.

Q50. (Hard) ⚠️ Find the odd one out among the given groups of letters: BDG, CEF, FHK, JLO
(A) BDG (B) CEF (C) FHK (D) JLO

Answer: (B) CEF

Solution:
In BDG: B(+2)→D(+3)→G. In FHK: F(+2)→H(+3)→K. In JLO: J(+2)→L(+3)→O. In CEF: C(+2)→E(+1)→F (pattern is +2, +1 instead of +2, +3). Examiner's Trap: All groups start with +2, so one must inspect the second difference.

Source: RRB Group D
