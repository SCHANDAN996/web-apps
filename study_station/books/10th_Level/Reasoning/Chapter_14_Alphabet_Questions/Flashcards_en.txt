Front: How many letters are in the English alphabet?
Back: 26 letters (A to Z).

Front: What are the EJOTY mnemonic values?
Back: E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25.

Front: What is the formula for the position from the right?
Back: Position from right = 27 − position from left.

Front: Which is the 10th letter from the left?
Back: J (J=10).

Front: Which is the 5th letter from the right?
Back: V (27−5 = 22 = V).

Front: Formula for the number of letters between two letters?
Back: Bigger position − smaller position − 1.

Front: How many letters are between C and H?
Back: 8 − 3 − 1 = 4 letters.

Front: Which is the "3rd letter to the right of L"?
Back: O (12 + 3 = 15 = O).

Front: How do you find the middle letter?
Back: The average of the two positions (add and divide by 2).

Front: What is the sum of a reverse-alphabet pair?
Back: 27 (like A↔Z, B↔Y, C↔X).

Front: Which is the 15th letter from the left?
Back: O (O=15).

Front: What is the position of P?
Back: 16.

Front: Difference between "after" and "nth"?
Back: "3rd after C" = C+3; "3rd from the left" = just 3.

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Mixing up counting from the left and from the right.

Front: What is the alphabet-question mnemonic?
Back: "Remember EJOTY, right from 27".
