51. If 'GREEN' is coded as 'TIVVM' and 'BLUE' is coded as 'YOFV', then how is 'RED' coded?
(a) IUW (b) IWV (c) IVW (d) IVV
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each letter is replaced by its mirror letter in the alphabet (A↔Z, B↔Y, …), so R→I, E→V, D→W. Option IWV swaps the last two letters, a common slip, so the answer is IVW.
