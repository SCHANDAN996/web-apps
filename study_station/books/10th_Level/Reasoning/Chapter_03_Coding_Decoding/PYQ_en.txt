PYQ Analysis: Coding-Decoding (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Coding-decoding gives 2–4 questions per exam. Letter-shift and reverse coding are the most common; number coding appears in banking. The trap is direction (+1 vs −1) and mixed rules.

Q1. If CAT is coded as DBU, then DOG is coded as?
(a) EPH (b) EPG (c) FPH (d) EQH
Answer: (a) EPH
Solution: Each letter moves +1. D→E, O→P, G→H = EPH.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. If MANGO is coded as NBOHP, then FRUIT is coded as?
(a) GSVJU (b) GSVJT (c) GTVJU (d) HSVJU
Answer: (a) GSVJU
Solution: Each letter moves +1. F→G, R→S, U→V, I→J, T→U = GSVJU.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. If TABLE is coded as ELBAT, then CHAIR is coded as?
(a) RIAHC (b) RIACH (c) RAIHC (d) IRAHC
Answer: (a) RIAHC
Solution: The word is reversed. CHAIR reversed = RIAHC.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q4. If A=1, B=2, …, then the sum of the letters of CAB is?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: C=3, A=1, B=2. Sum = 3+1+2 = 6.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q5. If RED is coded as 27, then GREEN is coded as?
(a) 49 (b) 45 (c) 52 (d) 47
Answer: (a) 49
Solution: R+E+D = 18+5+4 = 27. GREEN = 7+18+5+5+14 = 49.
Source: Bank PO

Q6. If DELHI is coded as CDKGH, then WORLD is coded as?
(a) VNQKC (b) VNQKD (c) VMQKC (d) WNQKC
Answer: (a) VNQKC
Solution: Each letter moves −1. W→V, O→N, R→Q, L→K, D→C = VNQKC.
Source: SSC CGL

Q7. If NAME is coded as EMAN, then GAME is coded as?
(a) EMAG (b) EMGA (c) AMEG (d) EAMG
Answer: (a) EMAG
Solution: The word is reversed. GAME reversed = EMAG.
Source: SSC MTS

Q8. In a code, 'water' is called 'food' and 'food' is called 'tree'. Where does a fish live?
(a) tree (b) food (c) water (d) river
Answer: (b) food
Solution: A fish lives in water; here 'water' is called 'food'. So the answer is 'food'.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q9. If SUN is coded as TVO, then MOON is coded as?
(a) NPPO (b) NPPN (c) NOPO (d) NPON
Answer: (a) NPPO
Solution: Each letter moves +1. M→N, O→P, O→P, N→O = NPPO.
Source: RRB Group D

Q10. If A is coded as D and B as E (each +3), then CAT is coded as?
(a) FDW (b) FDV (c) EDW (d) FCW
Answer: (a) FDW
Solution: Each letter moves +3. C→F, A→D, T→W = FDW.
Source: SSC CGL

Q11. If HELP is written using letter positions as 8-5-12-16, then WORD is?
(a) 23-15-18-4 (b) 23-14-18-4 (c) 22-15-18-4 (d) 23-15-17-4
Answer: (a) 23-15-18-4
Solution: W=23, O=15, R=18, D=4.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q12. If CAT is coded as XZG using opposite letters (A↔Z), then DOG is coded as?
(a) WLT (b) WKT (c) VLT (d) WLS
Answer: (a) WLT
Solution: Opposite of each letter (sum 27): D→W, O→L, G→T = WLT.
Source: SSC CGL
