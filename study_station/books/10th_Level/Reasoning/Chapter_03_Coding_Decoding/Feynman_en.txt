🧒 The Feynman Technique: Understanding Reverse Coding Like a 12-Year-Old

Story: Mirror Town and the Secret Friends

Imagine a quiet little town called "Alphabet City". In this town, there are exactly 26 houses built in a straight line along one long avenue.
The first house belongs to 'A', the second to 'B', the third to 'C'... all the way to the 26th house at the far end, which belongs to 'Z'.

Right in the exact middle of town stands a giant, magical two-sided mirror.

Whenever 'A' stands in front of the mirror, he looks across town and sees his reflection friend standing at the very opposite end: 'Z'.
When 'B' looks in, he sees 'Y'. When 'C' looks in, she sees 'X'.
The two houses directly in front of the middle mirror — 'M' (House 13) and 'N' (House 14) — stand side-by-side, perfectly balanced in the center!

The Secret Formula of the Mirror:

To find the mirror-friend of any letter, there is a simple mathematical secret:
Subtract the letter's house number from 27!

Examples:
• A = 1  ➜  27 – 1 = 26  ➜  Z
• B = 2  ➜  27 – 2 = 25  ➜  Y
• C = 3  ➜  27 – 3 = 24  ➜  X
• M = 13 ➜  27 – 13 = 14 ➜  N
• T = 20 ➜  27 – 20 = 7  ➜  G

Using this secret code, children in Alphabet City send each other coded notes.
If someone sends the cipher "ZIV", you can decode it in seconds:
• Z (26) ➜ 27 – 26 = 1  ➜ A
• I (9)  ➜ 27 – 9 = 18  ➜ R
• V (22) ➜ 27 – 22 = 5  ➜ E
The secret message is "ARE"!

The Rule You Must Never Forget: The "27 Minus" Magic

Whenever an exam asks for reverse or opposite letter coding, do not waste time writing the alphabet backwards.
Simply subtract the letter's rank from 27:
• Rank of K is 11 ➜ 27 – 11 = 16 ➜ P. (K pairs with P)
• Rank of G is 7  ➜ 27 – 7 = 20  ➜ T. (G pairs with T)

The Most Common Mistake:
Students often confuse reversing the word order with replacing letters with their opposites.
Remember: The sequence of letters in the word stays in its normal order; you only replace each individual letter with its mirror partner!
