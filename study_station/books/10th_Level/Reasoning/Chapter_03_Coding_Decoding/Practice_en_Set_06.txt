126. In a certain code, 'CAT' is written as 'DBU' and 'DOG' is written as 'EPH'. How will 'FISH' be written in that code?
(a) GJSI (b) GJTH (c) FJTI (d) GJTI
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each letter is shifted one step forward in the alphabet: F→G, I→J, S→T, H→I. The tempting option GJTH wrongly keeps the last letter unshifted, so the answer is GJTI.
