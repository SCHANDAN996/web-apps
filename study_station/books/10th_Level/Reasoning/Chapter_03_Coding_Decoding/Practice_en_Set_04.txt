76. In a certain code, DOG is written as CNF. How will CAT be written in that code?
(a) BZS (b) AZS (c) BZR (d) BZT
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each letter is shifted one step backward in the alphabet: D→C, O→N, G→F. The tempting option BZR wrongly shifts T forward instead of backward. Applying the same rule, C→B, A→Z, T→S, so the answer is BZS.
