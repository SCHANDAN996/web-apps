126. If the following words are arranged in dictionary order, which word will come second?
Words: Garland, Garden, Garment, Garlic, Garage
(a) Garden (b) Garment (c) Garland (d) Garlic
Answer: (a)
Solution: In dictionary order the words are Garage, Garden, Garland, Garlic, Garment. The trap is Garland, which differs only a few letters later; the word in second place is Garden, so the answer is Garden.
