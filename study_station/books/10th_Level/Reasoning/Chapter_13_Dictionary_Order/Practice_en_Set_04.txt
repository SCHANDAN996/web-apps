(a) Spoon (b) Sport (c) Spot (d) Spray
Answer: (b)
Solution: In dictionary order the words are Spoon, Sport, Spot, Spray. The trap is Spot, which differs only a few letters later; the word in second place is Sport, so the answer is Sport.
