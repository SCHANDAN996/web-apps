101. If the following words are arranged in dictionary order, which word will come first?
Words: Planet, Plastic, Play, Plate, Plant
(a) Plastic (b) Planet (c) Play (d) Plant
Answer: (b)
Solution: In dictionary order the words are Planet, Plant, Plastic, Plate, Play. The trap is Plant, which differs only a few letters later; the word in first place is Planet, so the answer is Planet.
Source: PYQ-style
