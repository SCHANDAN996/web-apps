1. Which word will come first in dictionary order?
(a) Penalty (b) Pendant (c) Penguin (d) Pencil
Answer: (a)
Solution: In dictionary order the words are Penalty, Pencil, Pendant, Penguin. The trap is Pencil, which differs only a few letters later; the word in first place is Penalty, so the answer is Penalty.
Source: PYQ-style
