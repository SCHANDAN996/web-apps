Set 1: Dictionary Order MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–25: Medium

Q1. Which comes first: Cat, Car, Call, Cap?
(a) Call (b) Cap (c) Car (d) Cat
Answer: (a) Call
Solution: Third letters l, p, r, t → Call first.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. Which comes first: Dog, Doll, Door, Dot?
(a) Doll (b) Dog (c) Door (d) Dot
Answer: (b) Dog
Solution: After Do, g < l < o < t → Dog first.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Between 'car' and 'care', which comes first?
(a) care (b) same (c) car (d) cannot say
Answer: (c) car
Solution: The shorter word comes first.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Which comes last: Apple, Ant, Age, Angle?

Answer: (d) Apple
Solution: Order: Age, Angle, Ant, Apple → Apple last.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q21. (Medium) Which word comes first in dictionary order: Star, Stand, Stage, Stamp?
(a) Stage (b) Stamp (c) Stand (d) Star
Answer: (a) Stage
Solution: After 'Sta', third letters are g (Stage), m (Stamp), n (Stand), r (Star). Since g < m < n < r, Stage comes first.
Source: SSC MTS

Q22. (Medium) Between 'light' and 'lightning', which comes first?

Answer: (b) light
Solution: The shorter prefix word ends first, so 'light' comes first.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q25. (Medium) Which word comes first in dictionary order: Blue, Black, Blow, Blind?

Source: NCERT Reasoning
