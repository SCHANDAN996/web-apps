# Short Tricks: Dictionary Order

Trick: First look at each word's first letter; if the same, the second, then the third — until you find a difference.

Trick: The shorter word comes first — between 'car' and 'card', 'car' comes first (nothing follows it).

Trick: Remember the alphabet positions (A=1…Z=26); "which letter comes first" is settled instantly.

Trick: Group words by their first 2-3 letters, then order within each group.

Trick: If reverse dictionary order is asked, first arrange normally, then reverse.

Trick: Eliminate options as you go — remove any word that clearly shouldn't come first.

Skip Strategy: If ordering 6+ long words is confusing, do the shorter-list questions first.

💡 Mnemonic: "First letter, then the next" — the whole rule of dictionary order.
