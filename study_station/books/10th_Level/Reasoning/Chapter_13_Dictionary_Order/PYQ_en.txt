PYQ Analysis: Dictionary Order (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Dictionary order gives 1–2 questions per exam. Arranging words and finding first/last are most common. The trap is stopping at the first letter and the shorter-word rule.

Q1. Which word comes first in the dictionary: Cat, Car, Call, Cap?
(a) Call (b) Cap (c) Car (d) Cat
Answer: (a) Call
Solution: All start with Ca; third letters l, p, r, t → Call comes first.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. Which word comes first: Book, Bag, Ball, Bat?
(a) Bag (b) Ball (c) Bat (d) Book
Answer: (a) Bag
Solution: Bag, Ball, Bat start with Ba; Book has 'o'. Among the Ba words, g < l < t, so Bag is first.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Between 'car' and 'card', which comes first?
(a) car (b) card (c) same (d) cannot say
Answer: (a) car
Solution: The shorter word comes first when one is the start of the other.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Which word comes last: Apple, Ant, Angle, Age?
(a) Age (b) Angle (c) Ant (d) Apple
Answer: (d) Apple
Solution: Order: Age, Angle, Ant, Apple (Ap comes after An). Apple is last.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. Which word is third in dictionary order: Dog, Doll, Door, Dot?
(a) Dog (b) Doll (c) Door (d) Dot
Answer: (c) Door
Solution: Order: Dog, Doll, Door, Dot. The third is Door.
Source: SSC MTS

Q6. In dictionary order, words are compared based on:
(a) length (b) alphabet position of letters (c) number of vowels (d) last letter
Answer: (b) alphabet position of letters
Solution: Words are ordered by the alphabet position, letter by letter.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q7. Which word comes first: Sun, Sit, Sad, Set?
(a) Sad (b) Set (c) Sit (d) Sun
Answer: (a) Sad
Solution: All start with S; second letters a, e, i, u → Sad is first.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Which word comes first: Table, Tea, Ten, The?
(a) Table (b) Tea (c) Ten (d) The
Answer: (a) Table
Solution: Ta comes before Te and Th, so Table is first.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. Between 'love' and 'lover', which comes first?
(a) love (b) lover (c) same (d) cannot say
Answer: (a) love
Solution: The shorter word comes first.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. Which word comes last: Red, Rat, Ring, Run?
(a) Rat (b) Red (c) Ring (d) Run
Answer: (d) Run
Solution: Order: Rat, Red, Ring, Run (Ra, Re, Ri, Ru). Run is last.
Source: RRB Group D
