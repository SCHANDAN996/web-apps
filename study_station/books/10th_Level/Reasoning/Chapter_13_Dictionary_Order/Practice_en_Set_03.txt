Arrange the given words in dictionary order and select the correct sequence.
Words: Ambition, Ambiguous, Ambassador, Amber
(A) Ambassador, Amber, Ambiguous, Ambition
(B) Amber, Ambassador, Ambiguous, Ambition
(C) Ambassador, Amber, Ambition, Ambiguous
(D) Amber, Ambiguous, Ambassador, Ambition
Answer: (A)
Solution: All begin ‘Amb’, third letter ‘b’. Fourth letters: Ambassador (a), Amber (e), Ambiguous (i), Ambition (i). a < e < i. Among i-words: Ambiguous (fifth g) < Ambition (fifth t) because g < t. So Ambassador, Amber, Ambiguous, Ambition.
Examiner’s Trap: Jumping to the fifth letter without noticing the fourth‑letter split (e vs i) can lead to wrong ordering.
