51. Which word will come last in dictionary order?
(a) Tiger (b) Tide (c) Ticket (d) Tickle
Answer: (a)
Solution: In dictionary order the words are Ticket, Tickle, Tide, Tiger. The trap is Tide, which differs only a few letters later; the word in last place is Tiger, so the answer is Tiger.
Source: PYQ-style
