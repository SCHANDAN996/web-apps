26. Which word will come first in dictionary order?
(a) Rain (b) Raise (c) Rank (d) Rapid
Answer: (a)
Solution: In dictionary order the words are Rain, Raise, Rank, Rapid. The trap is Raise, which differs only a few letters later; the word in first place is Rain, so the answer is Rain.
Source: PYQ-style
