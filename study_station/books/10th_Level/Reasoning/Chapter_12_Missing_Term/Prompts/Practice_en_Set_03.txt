Set 3: Missing Term – Foundation Level (Medium 51-75)
Bilingual (English / हिंदी)

Q51. Find the missing term: 5, 9, 15, 23, 33, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 5, 9, 15, 23, 33, ?
(A) 41 (B) 43 (C) 45 (D) 47
Answer: (C) 45
Solution: Differences: +4, +6, +8, +10 → next +12 → 33+12=45.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

Q52. Find the missing term: 2, 5, 14, 41, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 2, 5, 14, 41, ?
(A) 86 (B) 112 (C) 122 (D) 124
Answer: (C) 122
Solution: Pattern: ×3 –1. 2×3–1=5, 5×3–1=14, 14×3–1=41, 41×3–1=122.
Source: IBPS Clerk 2018

Q53. Find the missing term: 2, 8, 4, 16, 8, 32, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 2, 8, 4, 16, 8, 32, ?
(A) 12 (B) 16 (C) 20 (D) 64
Answer: (B) 16
Solution: ⚠️ परीक्षक का जाल – एकांतर संक्रिया (Alternating operations).
Odd positions: ÷2, Even positions: ×4. Or simply: 2×4=8, 8÷2=4, 4×4=16, 16÷2=8, 8×4=32, 32÷2=16. Many candidates miss the alternating rule and pick 64 (continuing ×4).
Source: SSC CPO 2020

Q54. Find the missing term: 0, 7, 26, 63, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 0, 7, 26, 63, ?
(A) 100 (B) 124 (C) 126 (D) 128
Answer: (B) 124
Solution: n³ – 1: 1³–1=0, 2³–1=7, 3³–1=26, 4³–1=63, 5³–1=124.
Source: RRB NTPC 2016

Q55. Find the missing term: 0, 4, 18, 48, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 0, 4, 18, 48, ?
(A) 80 (B) 96 (C) 100 (D) 120
Answer: (C) 100
Solution: n³ – n²: 1³–1²=0, 2³–2²=4, 3³–3²=18, 4³–4²=48, 5³–5²=100.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

Q56. Find the missing term: 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, ?
(A) 15 (B) 17 (C) 18 (D) 19
Answer: (C) 18
Solution: Each term is sum of previous two (Fibonacci-like). 7+11=18.
Source: UPSC CSAT 2016

Q57. Find the missing term: B, E, I, N, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: B, E, I, N, ?
(A) Q (B) R (C) S (D) T
Answer: (D) T

Q58. Find the missing term: 3D, 5G, 7J, 9M, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 3D, 5G, 7J, 9M, ?
(A) 11O (B) 11P (C) 13P (D) 11Q
Answer: (B) 11P
Solution: Numbers +2: 3,5,7,9,11. Letters: D(4), G(7), J(10), M(13) → +3 → P(16).
Source: IBPS RRB 2017

Q59. Find the missing number in the matrix:
5 4 41
6 5 61
7 6 ?
आव्यूह में लुप्त संख्या ज्ञात करें:
5 4 41
6 5 61
7 6 ?
(A) 71 (B) 75 (C) 81 (D) 85
Answer: (D) 85
Solution: Row pattern: first² + second² = third. 7²+6²=49+36=85.
Source: SSC CGL 2017

Q60. Find the missing number in the matrix:
4 5 29
6 7 55
8 9 ?
आव्यूह में लुप्त संख्या ज्ञात करें:
4 5 29
6 7 55
8 9 ?
(A) 89 (B) 88 (C) 90 (D) 91
Answer: (A) 89
Solution: ⚠️ परीक्षक का जाल – बदलता जोड़ (Varying addition).
Pattern: (first × second) + (a number increasing by 4). Row1: 4×5+9=29, Row2: 6×7+13=55, Row3: 8×9+17=89. Students often try product+constant and fail.
Source: SSC CPO 2022

Q61. Find the missing term: 12, 18, 27, 40.5, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 12, 18, 27, 40.5, ?
(A) 54.5 (B) 60.75 (C) 61.25 (D) 62
Answer: (B) 60.75
Solution: ×1.5 each time. 40.5 × 1.5 = 60.75.
Source: IBPS PO 2015

Q62. Find the missing term: AZ, BY, CX, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: AZ, BY, CX, ?
(A) DW (B) DV (C) EW (D) DY
Answer: (A) DW
Solution: First letter +1, second letter –1 (opposite pair sum 27). A+1=B, Z–1=Y; B+1=C, Y–1=X; C+1=D, X–1=W → DW.
Source: SSC CHSL 2016

Q63. Find the missing term: A1Z, B2Y, C3X, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: A1Z, B2Y, C3X, ?
(A) D4W (B) D4V (C) E4W (D) D5W
Answer: (A) D4W
Solution: First letter +1, number +1, last letter –1. So C+1=D, 3+1=4, X–1=W → D4W.
Source: RRB Group D 2018

Q64. Find the missing term: 1, 15, 2, 14, 3, 13, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 1, 15, 2, 14, 3, 13, ?
(A) 4 (B) 5 (C) 12 (D) 14
Answer: (A) 4
Solution: Odd positions: 1, 2, 3, ? → 4. Even positions: 15, 14, 13 (decreasing). So the next (7th) term is odd-position, hence 4.
Source: SSC CGL 2014

Q65. Find the missing term: 3, 4, 10, 33, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 3, 4, 10, 33, ?
(A) 100 (B) 126 (C) 136 (D) 148
Answer: (C) 136
Solution: ×1 +1, ×2 +2, ×3 +3, ×4 +4. 3×1+1=4, 4×2+2=10, 10×3+3=33, 33×4+4=136.
Source: SBI PO 2017

Q68. Find the missing term: D, W, F, U, H, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: D, W, F, U, H, ?
(A) R (B) S (C) T (D) Q
Answer: (B) S
Solution: ⚠️ परीक्षक का जाल – आगे-पीछे का खेल (Forward-reverse alternation).
Odd positions: D(4), F(6), H(8) → +2. Even positions: W(23), U(21), ? → –2 → S(19). Students trying continuous pattern between consecutive letters get confused.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

Q69. Find the missing term: 1, 2, 6, 15, 31, ?
लुप्त पद ज्ञात करें: 1, 2, 6, 15, 31, ?

Answer: (C) 56
Solution: Differences: +1², +2², +3², +4², +5² → 31+25=56.
Source: UPSC CSAT 2014
