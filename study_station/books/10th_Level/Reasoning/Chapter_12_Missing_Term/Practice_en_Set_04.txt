Questions 76–100: Medium

Q76. (Medium) Find the missing term: 7, 13, 25, 49, ?
(a) 97 (b) 73 (c) 79 (d) 89
Answer: (a) 97
Solution: ×2 –1 pattern: 7×2–1=13, 13×2–1=25, 25×2–1=49, 49×2–1=97.
Source: SSC CGL

Q84. (Medium) Find the missing term: 5, 7, 13, 25, 45, ?
(a) 75 (b) 73 (c) 77 (d) 79
Answer: (a) 75
Solution: Differences: +2, +6, +12, +20 → next +30 (gaps increasing by 4,6,8,10). 45+30=75. Or pattern: n³ – n²? 2³-2²=4 no. So difference of differences: +4, +6, +8, +10. So answer 75.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q85. (Medium) Find the missing term: 8, 4, 4, 6, 12, ?
(a) 24 (b) 30 (c) 32 (d) 36
Answer: (b) 30
Solution: (Changing multiplier pattern). ×0.5, ×1, ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5. 8×0.5=4, 4×1=4, 4×1.5=6, 6×2=12, 12×2.5=30. Students may try additive pattern and get stuck. Thus, the missing value is 30.
Source: RRB Group D

Q86. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:

(a) 28 (b) 32 (c) 30 (d) 34
Answer: (c) 30
Solution: Row pattern: (First × Second) + Second = Third. Row 1: (2 × 4) + 4 = 8 + 4 = 12. Row 2: (3 × 5) + 5 = 15 + 5 = 20. Row 3: (4 × 6) + 6 = 24 + 6 = 30.

Q92. (Medium) Find the missing term: 1, 2, 9, 28, 65, ?
(a) 126 (b) 128 (c) 130 (d) 132
Answer: (a) 126
Solution: n³ + 1: 0³+1=1? No. Actually 1³+0=1? Not. Check: 1=1³, 2=1³+1, 9=2³+1, 28=3³+1, 65=4³+1, next 5³+1=126. So pattern: n³ + 1 with n starting 0? 0³+1=1, 1³+1=2, 2³+1=9, 3³+1=28, 4³+1=65, 5³+1=126. Yes.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q93. (Medium) Find the missing term: 5, 25, 50, 250, 500, ?
