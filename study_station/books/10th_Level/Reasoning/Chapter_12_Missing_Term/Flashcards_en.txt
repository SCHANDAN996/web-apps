Front: What is the first step to solve a missing term?
Back: Catch the rule of the terms around the blank.

Front: What is the main difference between missing term and series?
Back: A series asks for the next term; missing term asks you to fill a middle (or table/figure) ?.

Front: Where can the rule be in a matrix?
Back: In the row, the column, or the diagonal.

Front: What is the ? in 3, 6, ?, 12, 15?
Back: 9 (add 3 each time).

Front: How is the centre number in a figure formed?
Back: By the sum, product, or difference of the outer numbers.

Front: What to write first in a missing-letter question?
Back: Each letter's position number (A=1…Z=26).

Front: What is the ? in 2, 4, ?, 8, 10?
Back: 6 (add 2 each time).

Front: Which letter is the ? in A, C, ?, G, I?
Back: E (add 2 positions each time).

Front: How do you confirm the rule?
Back: Check the rule across the whole row/column — if it breaks anywhere, it's wrong.

Front: What is the ? in 2, 6, ?, 54?
Back: 18 (multiply by 3 each time).

Front: What is the ? in 1, ?, 9, 16, 25?
Back: 4 (perfect squares: 2² = 4).

Front: Which series rules apply to missing term?
Back: Add, multiply, squares, cubes, Fibonacci — all of them.

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Looking only at the row — ignoring the column/diagonal.

Front: Strategy if a matrix is tangled?
Back: Skip it and do the plain-sequence questions first.

Front: What is the missing-term mnemonic?
Back: "Rule from around, answer in the blank".
