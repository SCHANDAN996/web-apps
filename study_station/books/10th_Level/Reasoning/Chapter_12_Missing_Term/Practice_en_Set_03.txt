Set 3: Missing Term MCQs (English)
Questions 51–75: Medium

Q51. (Medium) Find the missing term: 5, 9, 15, 23, 33, ?
(a) 45 (b) 41 (c) 43 (d) 47
Answer: (a) 45
Solution: Differences: +4, +6, +8, +10 → next +12 → 33+12=45.
Source: SSC CGL

Q52. (Medium) Find the missing term: 2, 5, 14, 41, ?
(a) 86 (b) 122 (c) 112 (d) 124
Answer: (b) 122
Solution: Pattern: ×3 –1. 2×3–1=5, 5×3–1=14, 14×3–1=41, 41×3–1=122.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q53. (Medium) Find the missing term: 2, 8, 4, 16, 8, 32, ?
(a) 12 (b) 20 (c) 16 (d) 64
Answer: (c) 16
Solution: Alternating pattern: multiply by 4, then divide by 2. 2 × 4 = 8, 8 ÷ 2 = 4, 4 × 4 = 16, 16 ÷ 2 = 8, 8 × 4 = 32, 32 ÷ 2 = 16.
Source: SSC MTS

Q54. (Medium) Find the missing term: 0, 7, 26, 63, ?
(a) 100 (b) 126 (c) 128 (d) 124
Answer: (d) 124
Solution: n³ – 1: 1³–1=0, 2³–1=7, 3³–1=26, 4³–1=63, 5³–1=124.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q55. (Medium) Find the missing term: 0, 4, 18, 48, ?
(a) 100 (b) 80 (c) 96 (d) 120
Answer: (a) 100
Solution: n³ – n²: 1³–1²=0, 2³–2²=4, 3³–3²=18, 4³–4²=48, 5³–5²=100.
Source: RRB Group D

Q56. (Medium) Find the missing term: 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, ?
(a) 15 (b) 18 (c) 17 (d) 19
Answer: (b) 18
Solution: Each term is sum of previous two (Fibonacci-like). 7+11=18.
Source: SSC CGL

Q57. (Medium) Find the missing term: B, E, I, N, ?
(a) Q (b) R (c) T (d) S
Answer: (c) T
Solution: Positions: B(2), E(5), I(9), N(14) → differences +3, +4, +5 → next +6 → 20 = T.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q58. (Medium) Find the missing term: 3D, 5G, 7J, 9M, ?
(a) 11O (b) 13P (c) 11Q (d) 11P
Answer: (d) 11P
Solution: Numbers +2: 3,5,7,9,11. Letters: D(4), G(7), J(10), M(13) → +3 → P(16). Thus, the missing value is 11P.
Source: SSC MTS

Q59. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:
5 4 41
6 5 61
7 6 ?
(a) 85 (b) 71 (c) 75 (d) 81
Answer: (a) 85
Solution: Row pattern: first² + second² = third. 7²+6²=49+36=85.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q60. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:
4 5 29
6 7 55
8 9 ?
(a) 88 (b) 89 (c) 90 (d) 91
Answer: (b) 89
Solution: (Varying addition). Pattern: (first × second) + (a number increasing by 4). Row1: 4×5+9=29, Row2: 6×7+13=55, Row3: 8×9+17=89. Students often try product+constant and fail. Thus, the missing value is 89.
Source: RRB Group D

Q61. (Medium) Find the missing term: 12, 18, 27, 40.5, ?
(a) 54.5 (b) 61.25 (c) 60.75 (d) 62
Answer: (c) 60.75
Solution: ×1.5 each time. 40.5 × 1.5 = 60.75.
Source: SSC CGL

Q62. (Medium) Find the missing term: AZ, BY, CX, ?
(a) DV (b) EW (c) DY (d) DW
Answer: (d) DW
Solution: First letter +1, second letter –1 (opposite pair sum 27). A+1=B, Z–1=Y; B+1=C, Y–1=X; C+1=D, X–1=W → DW.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q63. (Medium) Find the missing term: A1Z, B2Y, C3X, ?
(a) D4W (b) D4V (c) E4W (d) D5W
Answer: (a) D4W
Solution: First letter +1, number +1, last letter –1. So C+1=D, 3+1=4, X–1=W → D4W.
Source: SSC MTS

Q64. (Medium) Find the missing term: 1, 15, 2, 14, 3, 13, ?
(a) 5 (b) 4 (c) 12 (d) 14
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: Odd positions: 1, 2, 3, ? → 4. Even positions: 15, 14, 13 (decreasing). So the next (7th) term is odd-position, hence 4.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q65. (Medium) Find the missing term: 3, 4, 10, 33, ?
(a) 100 (b) 126 (c) 136 (d) 148
Answer: (c) 136
Solution: ×1 +1, ×2 +2, ×3 +3, ×4 +4. 3×1+1=4, 4×2+2=10, 10×3+3=33, 33×4+4=136.
Source: RRB Group D

Q66. (Medium) Find the missing term: 4, 6, 10, 18, 34, ?

Solution: Differences: +2, +4, +8, +16 → next +32 → 34+32=66. (Powers of 2 added)
Source: SSC CGL

Q67. (Medium) Find the missing term: 10, 11, 15, 24, 40, ?
(a) 65 (b) 60 (c) 63 (d) 67
Answer: (a) 65
Solution: Differences: +1, +4, +9, +16 (squares) → next +25 → 40+25=65.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q68. (Medium) Find the missing term: D, W, F, U, H, ?
(a) R (b) S (c) T (d) Q
Answer: (b) S
Solution: - (Forward-reverse alternation). Odd positions: D(4), F(6), H(8) → +2. Even positions: W(23), U(21), ? → –2 → S(19). Students trying continuous pattern between consecutive letters get confused. Thus, the missing value is S.
Source: SSC MTS

Q69. (Medium) Find the missing term: 1, 2, 6, 15, 31, ?
(a) 48 (b) 52 (c) 56 (d) 60
Answer: (c) 56
Solution: Differences: +1², +2², +3², +4², +5² → 31+25=56.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q70. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:
2 3 11
4 5 25
6 7 ?
(a) 41 (b) 43 (c) 45 (d) 47
Answer: (d) 47
Solution: Row pattern: (first × second) + 5 = third. 2×3+5=11, 4×5+5=25, 6×7+5=47.
Source: RRB Group D

Q71. (Medium) Find the missing term: 800, 400, 200, 100, ?

Answer: (a) 50
Solution: ÷2 each time. 100÷2=50.
Source: SSC CGL

Q72. (Medium) Find the missing term: C, G, K, O, ?

Answer: (b) S
Solution: Positions +4: C(3)→G(7)→K(11)→O(15)→S(19).

Q73. (Medium) Find the missing term: 2B, 3E, 5H, 7K, ?
(a) 11M (b) 13N (c) 11N (d) 11O
Answer: (c) 11N
Solution: Numbers: primes 2,3,5,7,11. Letters: B(2)→E(5)→H(8)→K(11)→N(14) (+3). Thus, the missing value is 11N.
Source: SSC MTS

Q74. (Medium) Find the missing term: 6, 13, 28, 59, ?

Q75. (Medium) Find the missing term: 5, 8, 17, 44, ?

Solution: ×3 –7: 5×3–7=8, 8×3–7=17, 17×3–7=44, 44×3–7=125.
