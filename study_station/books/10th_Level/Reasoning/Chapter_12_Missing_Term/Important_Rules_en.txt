📋 Important Rules & Approaches
Rule / Approach	Description	Example
Constant Difference Check	Pehle dekho kya lagataar antar (gap) same hai. Agar haan, to agla pad = antim pad + antar.	5, 9, 13, 17 → +4 → 21
Second-Order Difference	Agar antar badal raha hai, to antar ka antar nikalo. Agar wo constant hai, to pattern wahi hai.	4, 6, 10, 16, 24 → antar +2,+4,+6,+8 → agla antar +10 → 34
Multiplication / Division Pattern	Check karo kya har pad kisi fixed number se multiply/divide ho raha hai.	2, 6, 18, 54 → ×3 → 162
Mixed Operation (×a ± b)	Kabhi-kabhi har pad ko multiply karke kuch joda/ghataya jaata hai. Isko bhi check karo.	3, 8, 23, 68 → ×3–1 → 203
Squares / Cubes Series	Sankhyaayein agar 1,4,9,16,25 ya 1,8,27,64 jaisi lagein to n² ya n³ pattern ho sakta hai. n²±1 bhi common hai.	0,3,8,15,24 → n²–1 → 35
Fibonacci / Sum of Previous	Har pad apne pichhle do pado ka yog ho.	1,1,2,3,5,8 → 13
Prime Number Series	Har pad keval 1 aur swayam se vibhajya ho.	2,3,5,7,11 → 13
Odd-Even Split (Double Series)	Agar koi ek pattern nahi milta, to odd aur even positions ko alag karke dekho.	3,21,5,18,7,15 → odd: 3,5,7→9; even: 21,18,15→12
Alphabet Position Value	A=1, B=2,... Z=26. EJOTY anchor yaad rakho.	E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25
Opposite Letter Rule	Kisi bhi akshar aur uske opposite ka yog 27 hota hai.	H (8) → 27–8=19 = S
Alphanumeric – Split First	Akshar aur sankhya ke bhaag alag karo, dono ke apne pattern dhundho.	A2, C4, E8, G16 → letters +2, numbers ×2 → I32
Matrix – Row/Column Logic	3×3 matrix me pehle row-wise (+,×) dekho, fir column-wise, fir diagonal.	Row1: 5+7=12; Row2: ?+8=16 → ?=8
Verify Full Series Rule	Jo bhi pattern pakdo, use kam se kam teen terms par test karo.	Keval pehle do terms se mat maano.
Beware of Decoy Patterns	Kuch series me beech me pattern badalta dikhta hai – ho sakta hai do different operations alternate ho rahe hon.	Isliye odd-even split ya alag-alag block test karo.
⏱️ Time Management Rules
Situation	Time Rule	Action
Pattern dikhte hi samajh aaya (simple addition/multiplication)	15-second rule	Turant solve karo, next question jump karo.
Pattern thoda complex lage (difference of difference, ya double series jaisa)	45-second rule	45 second se zyada lag raha hai to question mark karke skip karo. Last mein time bache to return karo.
Pattern bilkul nahi mil raha	15-second rule	15 second mein koi clue nahi milta to tukka lagao (agar negative marking nahi) ya skip karo. Reasoning me stuck hoke time waste mat karo.

Formula/Expression	Use Case
No fixed formula for Missing Term.	Ye chapter pattern recognition par aadharit hai. Koi ek sutra nahi hai jo sabhi sawaalon par fit ho. Har prashn mein alag pattern hota hai.
Basics used: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, Squares (n²), Cubes (n³), Fibonacci recurrence, Prime numbers.	Sirf ye operations patterns me upayog hote hain.
