PYQ Analysis: Missing Term (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Missing term gives 1–3 questions per exam. Sequence and matrix types are most common. The trap is checking only the row and ignoring column/diagonal rules.

Q1. 3, 6, ?, 12, 15
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 10 (d) 7
Answer: (b) 9
Solution: Add 3 each time: 6 + 3 = 9.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. 2, 4, ?, 8, 10
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: Add 2 each time: 4 + 2 = 6.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. In a matrix, rows are (2, 3, 5), (4, 5, 9), (6, 7, ?). Find ?.
(a) 11 (b) 13 (c) 12 (d) 10
Answer: (b) 13
Solution: The third column = first + second: 6 + 7 = 13.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. 5, 10, ?, 20, 25
(a) 12 (b) 15 (c) 18 (d) 14
Answer: (b) 15
Solution: Add 5 each time: 10 + 5 = 15.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. 1, ?, 9, 16, 25
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: Perfect squares: 2² = 4.
Source: SSC MTS

Q6. A, C, ?, G, I
(a) D (b) E (c) F (d) H
Answer: (b) E
Solution: Add 2 positions each time: C → E.
Source: SSC CGL

Q7. 2, 6, ?, 54
(a) 12 (b) 18 (c) 24 (d) 27
Answer: (b) 18
Solution: Multiply by 3 each time: 6 × 3 = 18 (then 18 × 3 = 54).
Source: Bank Clerk

Q8. 10, ?, 30, 40
(a) 15 (b) 20 (c) 25 (d) 18
Answer: (b) 20
Solution: Add 10 each time: 10 + 10 = 20.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q9. 100, 50, ?, 12.5
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 35
Answer: (b) 25
Solution: Divide by 2 each time: 50 ÷ 2 = 25.
Source: RRB Group D

Q10. 1, 4, ?, 10, 13
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 7
Solution: Add 3 each time: 4 + 3 = 7.
Source: SSC CGL
