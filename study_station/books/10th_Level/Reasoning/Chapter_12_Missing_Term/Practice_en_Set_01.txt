Set 1: Missing Term MCQs (English) — Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–25: Medium

1. Find the missing term: 5, 10, 15, 20, ?
(a) 24 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 35
Answer: (b)
Solution: The series increases by 5 each time, so the next term is 20 + 5. The option 30 tempts those who add 10 by mistake. The answer is 25.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Find the missing term: 3, 7, 11, 15, ?
(a) 19 (b) 18 (c) 20 (d) 21
Answer: (a)
Solution: The constant difference is +4, so the next term is 15 + 4. The option 18 tempts those who add only 3. The answer is 19.

3. Find the missing term: 40, 35, 30, 25, ?
(a) 20 (b) 22 (c) 21 (d) 15

Solution: Each term decreases by 5, so the next term is 25 − 5. The option 15 tempts those who subtract 10 instead of 5. The answer is 20.

4. Find the missing term: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?
(a) 30 (b) 49 (c) 36 (d) 35
Answer: (c)
Solution: These are squares of consecutive numbers: 1², 2², 3², 4², 5², so the next is 6². The option 49 tempts those who jump to 7², skipping 6². The answer is 36.

17. Find the missing number in the matrix:
3  5  8
4  6  10
5  7  ?
(a) 11 (b) 12 (c) 13 (d) 14
Answer: (b)
Solution: In each row, first number + second number = third number, so 5 + 7 gives the missing value. The option 13 tempts those who add 6 to 7 by following the column instead of the row. The answer is 12.

23. Find the missing number in the matrix:
5  12  13
7  24  25
9  40  ?
(a) 45 (b) 49 (c) 51 (d) 41
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each row is a Pythagorean triple: 5²+12²=13² and 7²+24²=25², so 9²+40²=1681=41². The option 49 tempts those who simply add 9 and 40. The answer is 41.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

24. Find the missing term: ACE, BDF, CEG, ?

Answer: (c)
Solution: Each letter of a term moves +1 to form the next term: A→B→C→D, C→D→E→F, E→F→G→H, giving DFH. The option DGH tempts those who move the middle letter +2 by mistake. The answer is DFH.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Find the missing term: 4, 6, 9, 13.5, ?

Answer: (b)
Solution: The pattern is ×1.5 each time: 4×1.5=6, 6×1.5=9, 9×1.5=13.5, so the next is 13.5×1.5. The option 18 tempts those who look for additive differences like +2, +3, +4.5 and misapply them. The answer is 20.25.
