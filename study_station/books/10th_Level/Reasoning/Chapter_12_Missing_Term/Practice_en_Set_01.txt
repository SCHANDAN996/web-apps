Set 1: Missing Term MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–25: Medium

Q1. Find the missing term: 5, 10, 15, 20, ?
(a) 25 (b) 24 (c) 30 (d) 35
Answer: (a) 25
Solution: The series increases by 5 each time. Thus, the missing term is 25.
Source: SSC MTS

Q2. Find the missing term: 3, 7, 11, 15, ?
(a) 18 (b) 19 (c) 20 (d) 21
Answer: (b) 19
Solution: The constant difference is +4. Thus, the missing term is 19.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. Find the missing term: 40, 35, 30, 25, ?
(a) 21 (b) 22 (c) 20 (d) 15
Answer: (c) 20
Solution: Each term decreases by 5. Thus, the missing term is 20.
Source: RRB Group D

Q4. Find the missing term: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?
(a) 30 (b) 49 (c) 35 (d) 36
Answer: (d) 36
Solution: These are squares of consecutive numbers: 1², 2², 3², 4², 5², 6² = 36.
Source: SSC CGL

Q5. Find the missing term: A, C, E, G, ?
(a) I (b) H (c) J (d) K
Answer: (a) I
Solution: Every letter moves +2 places. A (+2)→C (+2)→E (+2)→G (+2)→I.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. Find the missing term: Z, X, V, T, ?
(a) S (b) R (c) Q (d) P
Answer: (b) R
Solution: Each letter moves –2 places. Z (–2)→X (–2)→V (–2)→T (–2)→R.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. Find the missing term: 2, 6, 18, 54, ?
(a) 108 (b) 160 (c) 162 (d) 150
Answer: (c) 162
Solution: Each term is multiplied by 3. Thus, the missing term is 162.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q8. Find the missing term: A1, B2, C3, D4, ?
(a) F5 (b) E6 (c) D5 (d) E5
Answer: (d) E5

Source: RRB Group D

Q9. Find the missing term: 2, 3, 5, 8, ?
(a) 12 (b) 10 (c) 11 (d) 13
Answer: (a) 12
Solution: Differences: +1, +2, +3, so next difference +4 → 8+4=12.
Source: SSC CGL

Q10. Find the missing term: 1, 8, 27, 64, ?
(a) 100 (b) 125 (c) 144 (d) 150
Answer: (b) 125
Solution: These are cubes: 1³, 2³, 3³, 4³, 5³ = 125.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q11. Find the missing term: B, D, F, H, ?
(a) I (b) K (c) J (d) L
Answer: (c) J
Solution: Positions: B(2), D(4), F(6), H(8), next J(10) → +2 each time.
Source: SSC MTS

Q12. Find the missing term: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, ?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 9 (d) 8
Answer: (d) 8
Solution: Fibonacci: each term is the sum of the previous two. 3+5=8.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q13. Find the missing term: 100, 96, 92, 88, ?
(a) 84 (b) 86 (c) 82 (d) 80
Answer: (a) 84

Source: RRB Group D

Q14. Find the missing term: 0, 3, 8, 15, ?
(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 22 (d) 25
Answer: (b) 24
Solution: Pattern: n² – 1. 1²–1=0, 2²–1=3, 3²–1=8, 4²–1=15, 5²–1=24.
Source: SSC CGL

Q15. Find the missing term: E, J, O, T, ?
(a) X (b) V (c) Y (d) W
Answer: (c) Y
Solution: Positions: E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, next Y=25 (multiples of 5).

Q16. Find the missing term: 2B, 3C, 4D, 5E, ?

Q17. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:

Solution: Row pattern: first + second = third. 5+7=12.

Q20. (Medium) Find the missing term: AB, BC, CD, DE, ?

Q21. (Medium) Find the missing terms: 3, 21, 5, 18, 7, 15, ?, ?
(a) 9, 12 (b) 9, 13 (c) 8, 12 (d) 8, 14
Answer: (a) 9, 12
Solution: Double Series. Split odd and even positions: Odd: 3, 5, 7, ? → +2 → 9. Even: 21, 18, 15, ? → –3 → 12. Thus, the missing term is 9, 12.
Source: SSC MTS

Q22. (Medium) Find the missing term: 3, 8, 23, 68, ?
(a) 198 (b) 203 (c) 202 (d) 208
Answer: (b) 203
Solution: Pattern: ×3 – 1. 3×3–1=8, 8×3–1=23, 23×3–1=68, 68×3–1=203.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q23. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:
5 12 13
7 24 25
9 40 ?
(a) 45 (b) 49 (c) 41 (d) 51
Answer: (c) 41
Solution: Pythagoras triple. Each row: a² + b² = c². 5²+12²=169=13², 7²+24²=625=25², 9²+40²=1681=41².
Source: RRB Group D

Q24. (Medium) Find the missing term: ACE, BDF, CEG, ?
(a) DGH (b) CFH (c) EGI (d) DFH
Answer: (d) DFH
Solution: Each letter increases by 1 in the next term: A→B→C→D, C→D→E→F, E→F→G→H. Thus, the missing term is DFH.
Source: SSC CGL

Q25. (Medium) Find the missing term: 4, 6, 9, 13.5, ?
(a) 18 (b) 20.25 (c) 19.5 (d) 22
Answer: (b) 20.25
Solution: Decimal multiplier. Pattern: ×1.5 each time. 4×1.5=6, 6×1.5=9, 9×1.5=13.5, 13.5×1.5=20.25. Many candidates try additive differences (+2, +3, +4.5...) and fail. Thus, the missing term is 20.25.
Source: RRB NTPC
