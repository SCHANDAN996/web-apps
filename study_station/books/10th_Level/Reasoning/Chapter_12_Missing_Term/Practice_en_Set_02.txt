Set 2: Missing Term MCQs (English)
Questions 26–50: Medium

Q26. (Medium) Find the missing term: 6, 11, 21, 36, 56, ?
(a) 81 (b) 71 (c) 76 (d) 86
Answer: (a) 81
Solution: Differences: +5, +10, +15, +20 → increasing by +5 each time. Next difference = +25 → 56+25=81.
Source: SSC CGL

Q27. (Medium) Find the missing term: 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, ?
(a) 63 (b) 65 (c) 64 (d) 66
Answer: (b) 65
Solution: Pattern: ×2 –1. 3×2–1=5, 5×2–1=9, 9×2–1=17, 17×2–1=33, 33×2–1=65.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q28. (Medium) Find the missing term: 2, 3, 7, 22, 89, ?
(a) 445 (b) 448 (c) 446 (d) 450
Answer: (c) 446
Solution: Pattern: ×1 +1, ×2 +1, ×3 +1, ×4 +1, ×5 +1. 2×1+1=3, 3×2+1=7, 7×3+1=22, 22×4+1=89, 89×5+1=446.
Source: SSC MTS

Q29. (Medium) Find the missing term: 8, 24, 12, 36, 18, 54, ?
(a) 26 (b) 28 (c) 72 (d) 27
Answer: (d) 27
Solution: Alternating pattern: multiply by 3, then divide by 2. 8 × 3 = 24, 24 ÷ 2 = 12, 12 × 3 = 36, 36 ÷ 2 = 18, 18 × 3 = 54, 54 ÷ 2 = 27.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q30. (Medium) Find the missing term: 9, 16, 25, 36, ?

Source: RRB Group D

Q32. (Medium) Find the missing term: 120, 60, 30, 15, ?

Source: SSC CHSL

Q33. (Medium) Find the missing term: 1, 6, 15, 28, 45, ?
(a) 60 (b) 72 (c) 78 (d) 66
Answer: (d) 66
Solution: Differences: +5, +9, +13, +17 → increasing by +4 each time. Next diff = +21 → 45+21=66. Or pattern: n×(2n–1) i.e. 1×1=1, 2×3=6, 3×5=15, 4×7=28, 5×9=45, 6×11=66.
Source: SSC MTS

Q34. (Medium) Find the missing term: A, E, I, M, ?

Answer: (a) Q
Solution: Position: A=1, E=5, I=9, M=13 → +4 each → Q=17.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q35. (Medium) Find the missing term: Z, X, V, T, R, ?
(a) P (b) N (c) O (d) Q

Solution: –2 positions each time. Z(26)→X(24)→V(22)→T(20)→R(18)→N(16).
Source: RRB Group D

Q36. (Medium) Find the missing term: AB, CD, EF, GH, ?
(a) HI (b) JK (c) IJ (d) IH
Answer: (c) IJ
Solution: Each pair moves +2 in starting letter. AB→(A+2=C) CD, CD→(C+2=E) EF, EF→(E+2=G) GH, GH→(G+2=I) IJ.
Source: SSC CGL

Q37. (Medium) Find the missing term: A1, D4, G9, J16, ?
(a) M24 (b) L25 (c) M26 (d) M25
Answer: (d) M25
Solution: Letters: A(1)→D(4)→G(7)→J(10)→M(13) (+3 each). Numbers: squares of 1,2,3,4,5 → 1,4,9,16,25. Thus, the missing value is M25.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q38. (Medium) Find the missing term: 10, 22, 46, 94, ?

Q39. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:
2 3 13
4 5 41
6 7 ?
(a) 84 (b) 85 (c) 83 (d) 86
Answer: (b) 85
Solution: Row pattern: first² + second² = third. 2²+3²=4+9=13, 4²+5²=16+25=41, 6²+7²=36+49=85.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q42. (Medium) Find the missing term: 0, 2, 6, 12, 20, ?

Q47. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:
3 4 19
5 6 41
7 8 ?
(a) 70 (b) 71 (c) 69 (d) 68
Answer: (b) 71
Solution: Row pattern: (First × Second) + (First + Second) = Third. Row 1: (3 × 4) + (3 + 4) = 12 + 7 = 19. Row 2: (5 × 6) + (5 + 6) = 30 + 11 = 41. Row 3: (7 × 8) + (7 + 8) = 56 + 15 = 71.
Source: SSC CHSL
