26. Find the missing term: 6, 11, 21, 36, 56, ?
(a) 86 (b) 71 (c) 76 (d) 81
Answer: (d)
Solution: The differences are +5, +10, +15, +20, rising by 5 each time, so the next difference is +25. Choosing 76 is the trap of adding only +20 again, but the difference itself must grow, so the answer is 81.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Find the missing term: 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, ?
(a) 65 (b) 63 (c) 64 (d) 66
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each term is double the previous term minus 1: 3×2−1=5, 5×2−1=9, and so on up to 33×2−1. Picking 64 is the trap of simply doubling 33 and forgetting the −1, so the answer is 65.
Source: PYQ-style
