# Short Tricks: Missing Term

Trick: First catch the rule of the terms around the blank, then apply the same to the ?.

Trick: In a matrix, the rule may be in three places — row, column, or diagonal. Check all three.

Trick: In a figure (circle/triangle), the centre number is often formed by the sum, product, or difference of the outer numbers.

Trick: Confirm by checking the rule across the whole row/column — if it breaks anywhere, the rule is wrong.

Trick: For a missing letter, write the position number (A=1…Z=26), then fill using the number rule.

Trick: All number-series rules apply here too — add, multiply, squares, cubes, Fibonacci.

Skip Strategy: If a tangled figure/matrix rule isn't found in 40 seconds, skip it; do the plain-sequence ones first.

💡 Mnemonic: "Rule from around, answer in the blank" — the whole method of missing term.
