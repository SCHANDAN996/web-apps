Set 5: Missing Term MCQs (English)
Questions 101–125: Medium

Q101. (Medium) Find the missing term: 11, 15, 21, 29, 39, ?
(a) 51 (b) 49 (c) 53 (d) 55
Answer: (a) 51
Solution: Differences: +4, +6, +8, +10 → next +12 → 39+12=51.
Source: SSC CGL

Q102. (Medium) Find the missing term: 3, 8, 18, 38, 78, ?
(a) 148 (b) 158 (c) 154 (d) 162
Answer: (b) 158
Solution: ×2 +2, then increment: 3×2+2=8, 8×2+2=18, 18×2+2=38, 38×2+2=78, 78×2+2=158.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q107. (Medium) Find the missing term: 6, 24, 12, 48, 24, 96, ?
(a) 50 (b) 72 (c) 48 (d) 100
Answer: (c) 48
Solution: . Alternating: ×4 then ÷2. 6×4=24, 24÷2=12, 12×4=48, 48÷2=24, 24×4=96, 96÷2=48. Many try a single rule and pick 72.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q108. (Medium) Find the missing number in the matrix:
3 5 16
4 7 29
5 9 ?
(a) 44 (b) 48 (c) 50 (d) 46
Answer: (d) 46
Solution: Row pattern: (First × Second) + 1 = Third. Row 1: (3 × 5) + 1 = 15 + 1 = 16. Row 2: (4 × 7) + 1 = 28 + 1 = 29. Row 3: (5 × 9) + 1 = 45 + 1 = 46.
Source: SSC MTS

Q123. (Medium) Find the missing term: 2, 12, 36, 80, 150, ?

Source: SSC MTS

Q124. (Medium) Find the missing term: 6, 20, 56, 132, ?
(a) 260 (b) 284 (c) 296 (d) 272
Answer: (d) 272
Solution: Differences: +14, +36, +76 → second diff: +22, +40 → next +58? 76+58=134, 132+134=266 not an option. Better: pattern: 2×3=6, 4×5=20, 7×8=56, 11×12=132, next 16×17=272. So factors increasing: 2×3, 4×5, 7×8, 11×12, 16×17 (gaps 2,3,4,5 between first factors). Answer 272.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q125. (Medium) Find the missing term: 5, 9, 25, 89, ?
(a) 357 (b) 345 (c) 365 (d) 375
Answer: (b) 345
Solution: Differences between consecutive terms are powers of 4: +4 (4¹), +16 (4²), +64 (4³). Next difference is +256 (4⁴): 89 + 256 = 345.
Source: RRB Group D
