126. Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G, and H sit around a circular table. E faces the center and his immediate neighbors face outwards. The facing directions alternate. A sits second to the left of B. F sits opposite G. H sits to the immediate right of A. Who sits second to the left of E?
(a) B (b) A (c) C (d) D
Answer: (a)
Solution: With alternating directions, a unique valid arrangement is obtained in which B sits second to the left of E.

Answer: (b)
Solution: Without overlap, total = 4 + 3 + 6 = 13; with overlap, total = 4 + 6 - 3 - 2 = 5. Since relative positions are not specified, it cannot be determined.
