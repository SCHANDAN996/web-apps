26. Five persons A, B, C, D, and E are sitting in a row facing north. A is to the immediate left of B. C is to the immediate right of D. E sits at the extreme right end. B does not sit at any extreme end. Who sits at the extreme left end?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) A (c) C (d) D
Answer: (a)
Solution: E=5. Two valid arrangements are possible: (1) A, B, D, C, E or (2) D, C, A, B, E. Both satisfy all conditions. Therefore, who sits at the extreme left end cannot be determined.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Six friends P, Q, R, S, T, and U are sitting in a row facing north. P sits at the extreme left end. R sits second to the right of T. S is to the immediate left of T. Only one person sits between Q and U. Who sits at the fifth position from the left end?
(a) Q (b) R (c) S (d) U
Answer: (b)
Solution: Arrangement (left to right): 1-P, 2-S, 3-T, 4-(Q/U), 5-R, 6-(U/Q). The fifth person from the left end is R.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (d)
Solution: A=1, D=4, B=6, C=2. Remaining seats 3 and 5 are for E and F. To the immediate left of D (seat 4) is seat 5, which could be occupied by E or F. Thus, it cannot be determined.

30. Five persons P, Q, R, S, and T have different heights. P is taller than Q but shorter than R. S is taller than T. T is not the shortest. Who is the shortest?
(a) Q (b) P (c) R (d) T
Answer: (a)
Solution: R > P > Q and S > T. Since T is not the shortest, and R, P, S are taller than others, only Q can be the shortest.

Answer: (b)
Solution: R=Friday(5), V=Sunday(7). Exactly one person between R and S, so S=Wednesday(3). S takes leave on Wednesday.

32. A walks 6 metres North from point X, turns left and walks 8 metres, then turns left and walks 6 metres, and again turns left and walks 8 metres. How far is he from point X?
(a) 10 m (b) 14 m (c) 0 m (d) 20 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: Walking 6m North, 8m West, 6m South, and 8m East forms a complete rectangle returning to point X. Distance = 0 m.

36. Six persons are sitting in two parallel rows, 3 in each row. Row-1 [A, B, C] faces South and Row-2 [P, Q, R] faces North. B is at the left end of Row-1. C is to the immediate right of B. In Row-2, R is to the immediate left of P, and Q is to the immediate left of R. Who sits opposite C?
(a) P (b) Q (c) R (d) A
Answer: (c)
Solution: Row-1 (facing South, left to right): B(3), C(2), A(1). Row-2 (facing North, left to right): Q(1), R(2), P(3). Opposite C (seat 2) is R (seat 2).

Solution: Thursday=Hindi. English is on Wednesday. The consecutive Science-Math pair falls on Monday and Tuesday. Therefore, Science is on Monday.

38. Ravi walks 6 metres East from point P, turns left and walks 8 metres North, turns right and walks 6 metres East, then turns left and walks 8 metres North. How far is he from the starting point P?
(a) 20 m (b) 10 m (c) 14 m (d) 28 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total East = 6 + 6 = 12 m. Total North = 8 + 8 = 16 m. Distance = √(12² + 16²) = √400 = 20 m.
Source: PYQ-style

45. A certain number of persons are sitting in a row facing north. A sits at the extreme left end. B sits third to the right of A. Only two persons sit between B and C. C sits at the extreme right end. What is the total number of persons in the row?
