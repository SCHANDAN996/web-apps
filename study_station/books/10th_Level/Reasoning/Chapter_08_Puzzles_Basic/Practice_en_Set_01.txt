Set 1: Puzzles MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. A, B, C live on 3 floors. C is on top, A at the bottom. Who is on the middle floor?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) none
Answer: (b) B
Solution: C on top, A at bottom, so B is on the middle floor.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. P, Q, R, S live on floors 1–4. P is on top (4), S at bottom (1). Q lives above R. On which floor is Q?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (b) 3
Solution: Floors 2 and 3 remain for Q and R. Q above R → Q = 3, R = 2.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Three people have tasks on Monday, Wednesday, Friday. A is on Monday, C on Friday. When is B's task?
(a) Monday (b) Wednesday (c) Friday (d) Sunday
Answer: (b) Wednesday
Solution: Monday and Friday are taken, so B is on Wednesday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. T, P, Q, R, S live on floors 1–5. T on 5, P on 1, Q on 3. R lives above S. On which floor is R?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 5
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: Floors 2 and 4 remain for R and S. R above S → R = 4, S = 2.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. In a puzzle, which clue should be filled first?
(a) Negative (b) Definite (c) Relative (d) Random
Answer: (b) Definite
Solution: Definite clues are certain and fix positions first.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. Three people like red, blue, green. X likes blue, Y does not like red. What does Z like?
(a) Red (b) Blue (c) Green (d) Cannot say
Answer: (a) Red
Solution: X likes blue; Y (not red) must like green; so Z likes red.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. On a 5-floor building: A2, B4, C5, D1, E3. Who lives immediately below B?
(a) A (b) C (c) D (d) E
Answer: (d) E
Solution: B is on floor 4, so immediately below (floor 3) is E.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. In a floor puzzle, "immediately above" means:
(a) any higher floor (b) the very next higher floor (c) the top floor (d) two floors up
Answer: (b) the very next higher floor
Solution: "Immediately above" is the floor directly above.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q9. A test is on Tuesday. What day is 2 days after Tuesday?
(a) Wednesday (b) Thursday (c) Friday (d) Monday
Answer: (b) Thursday
Solution: Wednesday, Thursday — 2 days after Tuesday is Thursday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. In a 5-floor building (top = 5), a person lives on floor 2. How many people live above him?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (b) 3
Solution: Floors 3, 4, 5 are above floor 2, so 3 people live above.
Source: RRB Group D

Q11. Three boxes P, Q, R are stacked. P on top, R at the bottom. Which box is in the middle?
(a) P (b) Q (c) R (d) none
Answer: (b) Q
Solution: P on top, R at bottom, so Q is in the middle.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q12. W, X, Y, Z live on floors 1–4. W on 4, X on 1. Y lives above Z. On which floor is Y?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (b) 3
Solution: Floors 2 and 3 remain for Y and Z. Y above Z → Y = 3, Z = 2.
Source: SSC MTS

Q13. Five people have tasks Monday–Friday. E is on Friday, D immediately before E. When is D's task?
(a) Monday (b) Wednesday (c) Thursday (d) Friday
Answer: (c) Thursday
Solution: Immediately before Friday is Thursday.
Source: SSC CGL

Q14. In a 6-floor building (top = 6, bottom = 1), a person lives on floor 5. How many people live below him?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 2
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: Floors 1, 2, 3, 4 are below floor 5, so 4 people live below.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q15. In a puzzle, a clue like "X is not on Monday" is which type?
(a) Definite (b) Negative (c) Relative (d) Random
Answer: (b) Negative
Solution: It rules out a possibility, so it is a negative clue.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q16. Three people live in Delhi, Mumbai, Pune. P is in Delhi, Q is not in Mumbai. Where does R live?
(a) Delhi (b) Mumbai (c) Pune (d) Cannot say
Answer: (b) Mumbai
Solution: P in Delhi; Q (not Mumbai) must be in Pune; so R is in Mumbai.
Source: SSC MTS

Q17. (Medium) A, B, C, D live on floors 1–4. D is at the bottom (1). C is above A, and A is above B. Who lives on the top floor?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) D
Answer: (c) C
Solution: D on 1. Above D: B, A, C with C > A > B → B = 2, A = 3, C = 4. So C is on top.
Source: SSC CGL

Q18. (Medium) An event is on Sunday. What day was it one day before?
(a) Friday (b) Saturday (c) Monday (d) Thursday
Answer: (b) Saturday
Solution: One day before Sunday is Saturday.
Source: RRB Group D

Q19. (Medium) P, Q, R, S, T live on floors 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 respectively. Who lives between Q and S?
(a) P (b) R (c) T (d) none
Answer: (b) R
Solution: Q on 4, S on 2. The floor between (floor 3) is R.
Source: SSC CGL

Q20. (Medium) Three people live on 3 floors. B is in the middle, and A lives above B. Where is A?
(a) Top (b) Middle (c) Bottom (d) Cannot say
Answer: (a) Top
Solution: B in the middle, A above B → A is on the top floor.
Source: SSC MTS
