1. A, B, C live on 3 floors (1, 2, 3). C is on top, A at the bottom. Who is on the middle floor?
(a) B (b) A (c) C (d) D
Answer: (a)
Solution: C is on top (3), A at the bottom (1); so B is on the middle floor (2).
Source: PYQ-style

2. P, Q, R, S live on floors 1–4. P is on top (4), S at bottom (1). Q lives above R. On which floor is Q?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: Floors 2 and 3 remain for Q and R. Q lives above R, so Q = 3, R = 2.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Three persons have events on Monday, Wednesday, Friday. A is on Monday, C on Friday. When is B's event?
(a) Monday (b) Friday (c) Wednesday (d) Thursday
Answer: (c)
Solution: Monday and Friday are assigned, so B is on Wednesday.
Source: PYQ-style

4. T, P, Q, R, S live on floors 1–5. T on 5, P on 1, Q on 3. R lives above S. On which floor is R?

5. In a puzzle, which type of clue should be placed first?

Answer: (a)
Solution: Definite clues fix exact positions directly, so they should be placed first.

7. In a 5-floor building: A on 2, B on 4, C on 5, D on 1, E on 3. Who lives immediately below B?

17. A, B, C, D live on floors 1–4. D is at the bottom (1). C lives above A, and A lives above B. Who lives on the top floor?

Solution: D is on 1. Above D: C > A > B, giving B=2, A=3, C=4. Thus C is on top.

21. Five friends P, Q, R, S, and T have different heights. P is taller than Q but shorter than R. S is taller than T but shorter than Q. Who is the tallest?
(a) R (b) P (c) Q (d) S
Answer: (a)
Solution: Height order: R > P > Q > S > T. Therefore, R is the tallest.

22. Four persons A, B, C, and D live in a 4-floor building (floors 1 to 4). A lives on an even-numbered floor. B lives immediately above A. C lives on floor 1. On which floor does D live?
(a) Floor 2 (b) Floor 4 (c) Floor 3 (d) Floor 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: C=1. A is on an even floor with B immediately above, so A=2 and B=3. The remaining floor 4 is for D.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Five boxes K, L, M, N, and O are stacked one above another. K is immediately above L. M is immediately below L. N is above O. If M is at the bottom, which box is second from the bottom?
(a) K (b) M (c) L (d) N
Answer: (c)
Solution: M is at the bottom (1). Immediately above M is L (2), and above L is K (3). So the second box from the bottom is L.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Four friends P, Q, R, and S take exams on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. P takes the exam before Q. R takes the exam on Thursday. S takes the exam immediately after P. On which day does P take the exam?
(a) Tuesday (b) Wednesday (c) Thursday (d) Monday
Answer: (d)
Solution: R is on Thursday. Free days: Mon, Tue, Wed. S is immediately after P, so P=Monday, S=Tuesday, Q=Wednesday. P takes exam on Monday.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Five students A, B, C, D, and E took an exam. A scored more than B. C scored more than D. B scored more than C. E scored the highest marks. Who scored the lowest marks?
(a) A (b) D (c) B (d) C
Answer: (b)
Solution: Order of marks: E > A > B > C > D. Lowest marks were scored by D.
