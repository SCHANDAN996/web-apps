76. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, and F are sitting in a row facing north. A sits second to the left of B. C sits exactly between A and B. D sits to the immediate right of E. F sits at one of the extreme ends. E does not sit adjacent to A. Who sits to the immediate left of A?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) C (c) D (d) F
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two different valid arrangements satisfy the conditions: (F, A, C, B, E, D) where F is to the immediate left of A, and (F, E, D, A, C, B) where D is to the immediate left of A. Hence, it cannot be determined.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F, and G live on floors 1 to 7 of a building (1 = ground, 7 = top). A lives on an odd-numbered floor. B lives immediately above C. D lives three floors below E. F lives on the 6th floor. G lives between A and F. Only one person lives between B and D. Who lives on the 3rd floor?
(a) B (b) A (c) C (d) D
Answer: (b)
Solution: Arrangement (1 to 7): 1-C, 2-B, 3-A, 4-D, 5-G, 6-F, 7-E. The person living on the 3rd floor is A.

78. Eight persons P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, and W are sitting around a circular table facing the center. P sits opposite Q. T sits exactly between Q and U. R sits opposite U. W sits opposite T. S sits second to the left of P. Who sits to the immediate right of Q?
(a) U (b) V (c) T (d) W
Answer: (c)
Solution: Arrangement clockwise: Q, T, U, S, P, W, R, V. Facing center, the person sitting to the immediate right of Q is T.

79. Six persons are sitting in two parallel rows. In Row-1, A, B, and C face south; in Row-2, P, Q, and R face north. A sits opposite the person who sits to the immediate left of R. B sits at the left end of Row-1. Who sits opposite C?
(a) P (b) Q (c) Cannot be determined (d) R
Answer: (d)
Solution: Row-1 (facing south): B(1), A(2), C(3). Row-2 (facing north): 1, 2, R(3). Immediate left of R is position 2, opposite A. Thus, opposite C sits R.

80. Five friends A, B, C, D, and E have different heights. A is taller than B but not shorter than C. B is taller than only D. E is shorter than A but taller than B. Who is the tallest?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) A (c) D (d) E
Answer: (a)
Solution: The height order gives A > E > B > D, but the relative order between A and C is not uniquely fixed. Thus, the tallest cannot be determined.

81. A person walks 30 meters north from point X, then turns left and walks 40 meters, then turns left again and walks 30 meters. How far is he now from his starting point X?
(a) 30 m (b) 40 m (c) 50 m (d) 70 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: 30 m north and 30 m south cancel out. The net displacement is 40 m west. Distance from starting point = 40 m.

84. In a row of boys, A is 4th from the left end and B is 6th from the right end. If exactly 3 boys sit between A and B, and A sits to the left of B, how many total boys are in the row?
(a) 13 (b) 11 (c) 15 (d) 17
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total boys = (A from left) + (boys between) + (B from right) = 4 + 3 + 6 = 13.

89. A person walks 15 meters north from point A, turns right and walks 20 meters, turns left and walks 10 meters, turns right and walks 5 meters, and finally turns right and walks 25 meters. How far is he from point A?
(a) 20 m (b) 25 m (c) 30 m (d) 35 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: North = 15 + 10 = 25 m, South = 25 m (net vertical = 0). East = 20 + 5 = 25 m. Net distance = 25 m.

92. A certain number of persons are sitting in a row facing north. A is 4th from the left end. B is 5th from the right end. Only one person sits between A and B. How many total persons are in the row?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) 6 (c) 10 (d) 11
Answer: (a)
Solution: With overlapping, total = 4 + 5 - 1 - 2 = 6; without overlapping, total = 4 + 1 + 5 = 10. Since both are valid, it cannot be determined.
