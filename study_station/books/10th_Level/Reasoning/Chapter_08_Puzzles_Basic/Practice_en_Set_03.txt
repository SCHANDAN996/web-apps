51. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, and F are sitting in a row facing north. B sits exactly between A and C. D sits second to the right of E. F sits at one of the extreme ends. C does not sit at any extreme end. Who sits to the immediate left of A?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) B (c) D (d) E
Answer: (a)
Solution: Multiple valid arrangements satisfy all given clues, leading to different persons to the immediate left of A. Thus, it cannot be determined.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Seven boxes A, B, C, D, E, F, and G are kept one above another (1 = bottom, 7 = top). A is on floor 1. B is immediately above E. G is three boxes above C. D is immediately below F. Only one box is between D and B. Which box is on the 5th floor?
(a) A (b) G (c) C (d) F
Answer: (b)
Solution: Arrangement (1 to 7): 1-A, 2-C, 3-E, 4-B, 5-G, 6-D, 7-F. The box on the 5th floor is G.

55. Five friends P, Q, R, S, and T have different heights. P is taller than Q but shorter than R (R > P > Q). S is taller than T (S > T). R is not the tallest person. Who is the shortest?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) P (c) Q (d) T
Answer: (a)
Solution: No comparison is given between T and Q. Either T or Q could be the shortest. Thus, it cannot be determined.

56. Seven boxes P, Q, R, S, T, U, and V are kept one above another (1 = bottom, 7 = top). P is on floor 6. Q is three boxes above R. S is immediately above T. U is at the top (floor 7). V is on floor 5 and R is on floor 1. Which box is on the 2nd floor?
(a) R (b) T (c) S (d) V
Answer: (b)
Solution: R=1, Q=4, V=5, P=6, U=7. Remaining floors are 2 and 3. S is immediately above T, so T=2 and S=3. Box on the 2nd floor is T.

57. Ravi walks 6 metres North, turns right and walks 8 metres, turns right and walks 6 metres, then turns left and walks 8 metres. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 10 m (b) 12 m (c) 16 m (d) 20 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: North = 6 m, South = 6 m (net North-South = 0). East = 8 + 8 = 16 m. Net distance = 16 m.

60. Five persons A, B, C, D, and E have different heights. A is taller than B. C is shorter than D but taller than E (D > C > E). A is not the tallest person. B is not the shortest. Who is the tallest?

61. A certain number of persons are sitting in a row facing north. A is 3rd from the left end. B is 5th from the right end. Only two persons sit between A and B. What is the total number of persons in the row?
(a) 9 (b) 11 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (c)
Solution: A is 3rd from left. Two persons between them means B is at position 6 (leaving 4 and 5). Total persons = 3 + 2 + 5 = 10.

67. A person walks 24 metres North from point A, then turns right and walks 7 metres East. What is the shortest distance from the starting point A?
(a) 25 m (b) 15 m (c) 20 m (d) 31 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: By Pythagoras theorem: Distance = √(24² + 7²) = √(576 + 49) = √625 = 25 m.
Source: PYQ-style
