Key Rules/Approaches	Description	Example
Identify Puzzle Type	Spot trigger words to classify as Arrangement, Comparison, Scheduling, or Floor.	"Left/right" → Arrangement; "taller/heavier" → Comparison; "Monday-Friday" → Scheduling.
Definite Clues First	Place clues that fix a position or value directly before using relative clues.	"A lives on floor 3" → place A on floor 3 at once.
Clean Diagram	Draw a simple diagram with one slot per entity; use clear labels and arrows.	Floor: 5 down to 1 vertical stack. Row: 1 to 5 horizontal slots with L/R.
"Immediately" vs "Any"	The word "immediately/just" means zero gap; otherwise gaps are allowed.	"A lives above B" allows intermediate floors; "immediately above" means no gap.
Build Cases (Max 2)	If a clue allows two possibilities, create Case 1 and Case 2. Eliminate with later clues.	Q at floor 2 or 4 → test Case 1: Q=2; Case 2: Q=4.
Relative Positioning Care	Use relative clues like "two floors between A and B" to test valid pairs.	In a 5-floor building, |A - B| = 3 implies floors (1, 4) or (2, 5).
No Assumptions	Never assume gender, exact order, or absence of gaps unless explicitly stated.	If not stated, do not assume two persons are on adjacent floors.
Comparison Ladder	Draw a vertical ranking line with > symbols for height, weight, or marks.	A > B > C means A is highest, B middle, C lowest.
Scheduling Grid	List days of the week in chronological order (Mon to Sun) as fixed column headers.	Assign events or people into day slots based on definite day clues first.
Cross-Check Final Arrangement	Verify your completed arrangement against all original statements before answering.	Re-read each clue and confirm there is no contradiction.
Skip Irrelevant Details	If a secondary attribute is not asked in the question stem, do not waste time resolving it.	If only floor number is asked, ignore unasked clothing color details.

Time Management

Rule	Time Limit	Action
15-Second Rule	15 seconds	If puzzle type and starting clues are not identifiable within 15 seconds, mark and skip to the next question.
45-Second Rule	45 seconds	If more than 2 cases remain open or no definite position is fixed after 45 seconds, skip immediately.
2-Minute Set Drill	2 minutes per set	20 sec diagram + 20 sec definite clues + 30 sec relative cases + 30 sec eliminate + 20 sec answers.
Overall Strategy	—	Solve simple, single-variable puzzles first. Leave multi-variable or branching puzzles for the end.

Logical/Mathematical Formulas
No mathematical formulas for this chapter. Puzzles rely entirely on systematic logical arrangement and case elimination.
