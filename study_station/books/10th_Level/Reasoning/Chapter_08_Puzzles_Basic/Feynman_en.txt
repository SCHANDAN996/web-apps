# Feynman Technique: Puzzles in Simple Words

Tell your younger brother: "A game — three friends live on three floors. Ram on top, Mohan at the bottom. So where is Shyam? In the middle! See, from just two clues the third fell into place by itself."

That is all a puzzle is. **Keep filling each clue into a table — the cell left over is the answer.**

## The Biggest Trick: Definite First, Then the Rest

Not all clues are equal:
- "Ram on top" — a definite clue, fill it at once.
- "Shyam not on floor 1" — negative, cross it out there.
- "Sita immediately below Gita" — a pair, keep them together.

After filling the definite clues, the rest usually fall into place on their own.
