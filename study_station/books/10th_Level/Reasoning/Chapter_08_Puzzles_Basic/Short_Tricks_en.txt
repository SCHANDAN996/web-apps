🪄 Short Tricks + Skip Strategy

Mnemonic Boxes

1. The Four Puzzle Beasts
Arrangement, Comparison, Scheduling, Direction — remember the first letters: A-C-S-D.
“Always Carry a Super Dog.” Picture a super dog sniffing out puzzle types.

2. D.R.A.C. – Dracula’s Secret
Diagram → Read definite clues → Add relative clues → Cross-check.
Imagine Dracula drawing a neat diagram, pinning down victims (definite clues), adding relatives (new vampires), then checking his work in a mirror (cross-check, no reflection!).

3. Left-Right in Circle: C-LAC, O-LC
Centre → Left = Anticlockwise. Outside → Left = Clockwise.
“Centre lovers LAC, Outside lovers LC (Elsie).” Think of a centre-facing person named Lac, and an outside-facing person named Elsie.

4. Immediate vs. Ghostly Gaps
When no “immediately” is given, invisible ghosts sit in the gaps.
“If the clue doesn’t say ‘immediate’, a ghost might be sitting there!”

5. The 2-Minute Pizza Drill
0:00–0:20 – Prepare the base (diagram).
0:20–0:40 – Add definite toppings (clues).
0:40–1:10 – Add relative toppings (cases).
1:10–1:40 – Remove burnt cases.
1:40–2:00 – Serve (answer).
Picture baking a puzzle pizza in exactly 2 minutes.

6. Definite Clues = Gold Coins, Relative Clues = Silver Strings
Gold coins you place first — they lock positions. Silver strings you tie later — they connect people.
“Gold before silver.”

7. Case Building: The Twin Monsters
When a clue fits two ways, you get twin monsters (Case 1 & Case 2). Feed them more clues; one will die (be eliminated).
“Feed the twins until one starves.”

8. Comparison Ladder
Draw a vertical ladder; put the tallest on top, shortest at bottom.
“Climb the > ladder.” Each “>” is a rung. A > B > C means A on top, B middle, C bottom.

9. Parallel Rows: Mirror Trick
Row1 facing South, Row2 facing North — the left end of Row1 is opposite the right end of Row2.
“Opposite ends attract.” Imagine a magnet: South-left sticks to North-right.

10. Floor/Building: Numbering from Heaven or Earth
Decide early: Is floor 1 bottom or top?
“Heaven is top, Earth is bottom. Write it down before you drown.”

11. The ‘Exactly Between’ Banana
“Exactly between” often means an equal number of bananas on either side. If you can’t split the bananas equally, the person isn’t exactly between.
“Bananas split evenly or it’s a trap.”

12. Negation Ninjas
Negative clues (“not at end”, “not neighbour”) are silent ninjas that eliminate wrong cases.
“Let the ninjas slice away bad cases.”

13. Direction-Distance: Dancer’s Turn
Left turn = anticlockwise, Right turn = clockwise.
“Left is loose (anticlockwise), Right is tight (clockwise).” Dance it out.

14. The Skip-It Crocodile
If a puzzle shows its teeth (more than 2 cases, unclear pattern), let the crocodile eat it and move on.
“Don’t wrestle a crocodile; skip it in 15 seconds.”

Skip Strategy Tips

15-Second Rule
If you cannot identify the puzzle type and draw a basic diagram within 15 seconds, skip the set immediately. Return only if time allows.

45-Second Rule
If more than 2 cases are still open after 45 seconds, or no definite clue is pinned, skip the entire puzzle set (all linked questions).

The 2-Minute Alarm
Set a mental alarm: the whole 3-question set must be solved in 2 minutes. At the 2-minute mark, if answers aren't found, mark any remaining questions for review and move on. Never let one puzzle steal time from five others.
