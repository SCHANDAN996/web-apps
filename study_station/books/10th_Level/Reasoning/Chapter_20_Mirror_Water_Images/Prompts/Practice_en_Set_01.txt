Question 21
A figure consists of a vertical line with a small arrowhead at its top pointing left. The mirror is placed on the left side of the figure. Which of the following correctly describes the mirror image?

A. Vertical line with arrowhead at top pointing left
B. Vertical line with arrowhead at bottom pointing right
C. Vertical line with arrowhead at top pointing right
D. Vertical line with arrowhead at bottom pointing left

Answer: C
Solution: Mirror on left flips left-right. The arrowhead at top pointing left will become pointing right, but still at top. So a vertical line with arrowhead at top pointing right.

Examiner’s Trap: Option A keeps arrow direction same (common mistake). Option B flips top-bottom mistakenly. Option D flips both.
