🪄 Short Tricks + Skip Strategy

1. The Cross-Hand Mirror Trick

Trick: Extend both hands in front. Cross them as if shaking hands with your opposite self. This is what a vertical mirror does – left becomes right. Now, turn both hands upside down and clap. This is water – top becomes bottom. Your body will never forget this.

2. “Mirror Paltay Left-Right, Water Ultay Top-Bottom” Rhyme

Trick: This Hindi rhyme sticks like glue. Mirror paltay left-right, Water ultay top-bottom site! Chant it thrice before every mock test.

3. The Love Letter “MOM” Story

Trick: Imagine a love letter signed “MOM”. In a mirror (vertical), it stays MOM because M and O are vertically symmetric. But in a calm lake, the same letter “MOM” looks like “WOW” – as if the water is saying “WOW!” to the reflection. Remember: Mirror – M remains M. Water – M becomes W (WOW!).

4. The Clock Food Menu

Trick: Mirror clock: “12 o’clock lunch minus real time”. Water clock: “18:30 dinner minus real time”. If you want mirror time, eat lunch at 12. If you want water time, eat dinner at 18:30. The result is your reflection meal time.

5. M-W Swap: Man and Woman

Trick: In water, M (Man) turns into W (Woman), and W (Woman) turns into M (Man) – a perfect gender swap! So “MOM” becomes “WOW” and “WOW” becomes “MOM”. A magical lake that swaps genders.

6. 6-9 Trick: The Upside-Down Number Plate

Trick: Imagine a car number plate “69” falling into a river. When you look from above, it reads “96” – the water flipped it top to bottom. But if you put the same plate in front of a vertical mirror, it won’t swap to 96; the digits will individually reverse like strange symbols. So remember, water exchanges 6 and 9; mirror does not.

7. The “BOX” and the Magic Pond

Trick: The word “BOX” held over a pond: B flips upside down to ᗺ, O stays O, X stays X, so the reflection reads ᗺOX. Now hold the same “BOX” beside a mirror: B becomes ᗺ (that’s mirror, not water!), O remains O, X remains X, but the order reverses (mirror on right) giving XOᗺ. So water image keeps order, mirror reverses order. Use this to spot the difference.

8. “8 is Great, 0 is Hero”

Trick: Digits 8 and 0 are symmetric in both mirror and water. So they never change. Think “8 is great, 0 is hero, they stay the same no matter the mirror or lake.”

9. The Arrow’s Tale

Trick: Imagine an arrow pointing to your right. A mirror on your right will point it left. An arrow pointing up, over water, will point down. But an arrow pointing right over water still points right (top-bottom flip doesn’t affect horizontal direction). So remember: mirror flips left-right only; water flips up-down only.

10. The L-Shape Dance

Trick: Draw an L (vertical line with horizontal leg at bottom pointing right). Mirror on right: the L becomes a backward L (leg points left, still at bottom). Water below: the L becomes a 7 shape (leg now at top pointing right). So mirror moves the leg left, water moves the leg up.

11. The Symmetry Password: “A H I M O T U V W X Y”

Trick: To remember the vertically symmetric capital letters, use the phrase: “A HIM OUT VWXY”. “A HIM OUT” sounds like “a him out”, and then add “VWXY” as the Volkswagen Beetle model. So “A HIM OUT VWXY” – all these letters look same in mirror.

12. The Two-Step 180° Trick

Trick: If you take a water image and then a mirror image (or vice versa), the result is the same as rotating the object 180° (upside down and reversed). Remember “Water + Mirror = 180° spin.” This trick often helps solve hard questions quickly.

Skip Strategy Tips

Skip Strategy 1: The 15-Second Rule

If you cannot recall the flip rule (mirror or water) or the symmetry of a specific letter/digit within 15 seconds, mark the question and move on. Chances are you’ll waste more time and still guess wrong. Return only after completing the section.

Skip Strategy 2: The 45-Second Complex Figure Rule

When faced with a complex geometric figure (multiple arrows, dots, intricate shapes) that demands the dot method, give it a max of 45 seconds. If you haven’t narrowed to two options by then, skip. These questions are designed to consume time; the extra minute can fetch you 2-3 easy questions elsewhere.

Skip Strategy 3: The “Too Many Traps” Skip

If a question combines mirror and water in a sequential way (e.g., “first mirror, then water”) or asks for the original word from a given reflection image with rare characters, be extra cautious. If you aren’t 100% sure of the character mappings in 30 seconds, skip. These questions are high-risk and often eat up precious exam time. You can always attempt them after attempting all straightforward ones.
