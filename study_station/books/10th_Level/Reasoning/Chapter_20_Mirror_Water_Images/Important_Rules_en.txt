📋 Important Rules & Approaches
1. Key Rules / Approaches
Rule Name	Description	Example
Vertical Mirror Flip	Flips left ↔ right; does not flip top ↔ bottom.	Arrow pointing right → points left; arrow pointing up → still points up.
Horizontal Mirror / Water Flip	Flips top ↔ bottom; does not flip left ↔ right.	Arrow pointing up → points down; arrow pointing right → still points right.
Letter Vertical Symmetry	Letters A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y remain same in vertical mirror.	Mirror of "MOM" → "MOM".
Digit Vertical Symmetry	Digits 0, 1, 8 remain same in vertical mirror.	Mirror of 8 → 8.
Water Image Letter Trick	In water, M ↔ W and W ↔ M because top-bottom flip exchanges them.	Water image of "MOM" → "WOW".
Water Image Digit Swap	In water, 6 ↔ 9 due to top‑bottom reversal; other digits change shape.	Water image of 69 → 96.
Clock – Mirror Image Formula	Mirror time = 12:00 – Actual time (use 11:60 if minutes ≠ 00).	Actual 4:25 → 11:60 – 4:25 = 7:35.
Clock – Water Image Formula	Water time = 18:30 – Actual time (adjust to 12‑hour format if needed).	Actual 7:40 → 18:30 – 7:40 = 10:50.
Dot Method (Shapes)	For vertical mirror: keep vertical distance, flip horizontal distance across mirror line. For water: keep horizontal, flip vertical.	L‑shape with mirror on right: horizontal flip gives backward L.
Mnemonic Trick	Cross hands (right‑left) for mirror; upside‑down clap for water.	Acts as a quick body memory to avoid confusing the two flips.
Trap Check	Always verify letter/digit chart; double‑check arrow directions, small notches, and subtle curves.	Two identical‑looking options differ only by a tiny arrow.
2. Time Management
Rule	Description	Action
15‑Second Rule	If you can’t recall the correct flip rule or symmetry for a given letter/digit within 15 seconds,	Mark the question, skip, and return later.
45‑Second Rule	If a complex shape question (dot method) takes more than 45 seconds,	Skip immediately; do it after finishing easier ones.
First 5‑Second Check	In clock problems, spend 5 seconds confirming whether it's mirror or water to apply correct formula.	Wrong formula → instant wrong answer; prevent by quick label check.
Option Elimination	Eliminate obviously wrong options by checking one or two key points (e.g., arrow direction, letter M/W) before full solve.	Saves time by reducing choices.
