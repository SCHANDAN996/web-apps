Q76. (Medium) A clock shows 1:40. What time will its vertical mirror image show?
(A) 10:20 (B) 11:20 (C) 10:40 (D) 11:40

Answer: (A) 10:20

Solution:
Mirror time formula: 11:60 - 1:40 = 10:20. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q77. (Medium) How many letters in the word 'CHOICE' remain unchanged in their water image?
(A) Only 4 letters (B) All 6 letters (C) Only 3 letters (D) Only 2 letters

Answer: (B) All 6 letters

Solution:
Each of the letters C, H, O, I, and E has horizontal reflection symmetry. Therefore, all 6 letters look identical in their water image. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q78. (Medium) A left-pointing arrow has a small square at its tail. When reflected in a vertical mirror placed to its right, the mirror image shows:
(A) Right-pointing arrow with square at head (B) Left-pointing arrow with square at tail (C) Right-pointing arrow with square at tail (D) Left-pointing arrow with square at head

Answer: (C) Right-pointing arrow with square at tail

Solution:
A vertical mirror laterally inverts direction: the left-pointing arrow points right, while the square remains at the tail end. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q79. (Medium) Which of the following capital letters looks identical in BOTH vertical mirror and water images?
(A) R (B) N (C) P (D) X

Answer: (D) X

Solution:
The letter X has both vertical and horizontal axes of symmetry, so it remains unchanged in both mirror and water reflections. N, P, and R lack one or both symmetries. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q80. (Medium) What is the vertical mirror image of the word 'MAX' when a mirror is placed to its right?
(A) XAM (B) MAX (C) WAX (D) X∀W

Answer: (A) XAM

Solution:
Mirror on the right reverses letter order from right to left: X remains X, A remains A, and M remains M. The reflected word reads XAM. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q82. (Medium) Which of the following words looks identical in its vertical mirror image?

Q83. (Medium) A circle has a horizontal diameter drawn across it. A vertical mirror is placed to its left. The mirror image will show:
(A) No line (B) Vertical diameter (C) Diagonal line (D) Horizontal diameter unchanged

Solution:
A horizontal line has bilateral symmetry across a vertical mirror line, so its orientation remains completely horizontal and unchanged. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Q85. (Medium) A right-pointing arrow is first reflected in a vertical mirror, and the resulting image is then reflected in a water surface. The final image points:
(A) Right (B) Left (C) Upward (D) Downward

Answer: (B) Left

Solution:
The vertical mirror flips left-right, so the right-pointing arrow points left. The water surface flips top-bottom, which leaves horizontal direction unaffected. Thus, the arrow points left. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q87. (Medium) A clock shows 4:50. What is its water image time?
(A) 6:10 (B) 7:40 (C) 8:10 (D) 1:40

Answer: (D) 1:40

Solution:
Water time formula: 17:90 - 4:50 = 13:40. In standard 12-hour format, 13:40 is 1:40. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q88. (Medium) Which of the following capital letters has NEITHER vertical mirror symmetry NOR horizontal water symmetry?

Solution:
Letter F has neither vertical nor horizontal symmetry. Letter H has both, A has vertical symmetry, and E has horizontal symmetry. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q89. (Medium) A triangle has a small arrow on its left edge pointing outward. When a vertical mirror is placed to its right, the mirror image shows the arrow:
(A) On the left edge pointing outward (B) On the right edge pointing outward (C) On the right edge pointing inward (D) On the left edge pointing inward

Answer: (B) On the right edge pointing outward

Solution:
Lateral inversion swaps left and right: the left edge moves to the right edge, and the outward pointing direction remains outward (to the right). Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q91. (Medium) What is the vertical mirror image of the word 'BOX' when a mirror is placed to its right?
(A) XOB (B) ᗺOX (C) BOX (D) XOᗺ

Answer: (D) XOᗺ

Solution:

Q92. (Medium) ⚠️ A clock shows 12:30. What time does its vertical mirror image show?
(A) 11:30 (B) 12:30 (C) 11:00 (D) 12:00

Answer: (A) 11:30

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: On a 12-hour clock, 12:30 is treated as 0:30. Subtracting from 11:60 yields 11:60 - 0:30 = 11:30. Many candidates incorrectly subtract directly from 12:00. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q94. (Medium) A vertical line has a small circle attached to its top end. When reflected across a horizontal mirror placed below, the circle appears at the:

Solution:
A horizontal mirror inverts vertical orientation: the top end flips to become the bottom end. Hence, the circle is at the bottom end. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q97. (Medium) ⚠️ When a mirror is placed horizontally directly below an object, the resulting image is equivalent to a:
(A) Vertical mirror image (lateral inversion) (B) Water image (vertical inversion) (C) 180-degree rotation (D) Completely unchanged image

Answer: (B) Water image (vertical inversion)

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: A mirror placed horizontally (at the bottom or top) flips the object vertically (top to bottom), which is precisely the effect of a water image. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q99. (Medium) A figure shows a semicircle with its straight diameter on the left and curved arc on the right. When reflected across a vertical mirror on its right, the mirror image has:
(A) Straight diameter at bottom (B) Straight diameter on left, curved arc on right (C) Straight diameter at top (D) Straight diameter on right, curved arc on left

Answer: (D) Straight diameter on right, curved arc on left

Solution:
Vertical mirror reflection laterally inverts left and right: the straight diameter on the left reflects to the right, and the curved arc reflects to the left. Hence, the correct option is (D).
