Q51. (Medium) A clock shows 2:45. What time will its vertical mirror image show?
(A) 9:15 (B) 9:45 (C) 10:15 (D) 10:45

Answer: (A) 9:15

Solution:
Mirror time formula: 11:60 - 2:45 = 9:15. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q52. (Medium) What is the water image of the capital letter 'M'?
(A) M (B) W (C) N (D) E

Answer: (B) W

Solution:
A water surface reflects an image by flipping it vertically top-to-bottom. The letter M inverts vertically into the letter W. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q53. (Medium) An upward-pointing arrow has a small triangle at its base. When reflected in a vertical mirror placed to its left, the mirror image shows:
(A) Downward-pointing arrow with triangle at base (B) Downward-pointing arrow with triangle at head (C) Upward-pointing arrow with triangle at base (D) Upward-pointing arrow with triangle at head

Answer: (C) Upward-pointing arrow with triangle at base

Solution:
A vertical mirror flips left and right laterally. The vertical orientation (pointing upward) and the position of the triangle at the base remain unchanged. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q54. (Medium) ⚠️ Which of the following two-digit numbers looks identical in its vertical mirror image?
(A) 08 (B) 80 (C) 11 (D) 88

Answer: (D) 88

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: A vertical mirror reverses the sequence of digits in addition to flipping each digit laterally. Thus, 80 becomes 08. Only a palindrome composed of symmetrical digits, such as 88, remains identical. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q55. (Medium) Which of the following four-letter words looks identical in its water image?
(A) CODE (B) TIME (C) TIT (D) PLAY

Answer: (A) CODE

Solution:
Letters C, O, D, and E all possess horizontal symmetry. In water reflection, the left-to-right order of letters is preserved, so CODE remains identical. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: RRB Group D

Q56. (Medium) A clock shows 5:05. What is the time in its water image?
(A) 7:25 (B) 1:25 (C) 6:55 (D) 8:25

Answer: (B) 1:25

Solution:
Water time formula: 18:30 - 5:05 = 13:25. On a 12-hour clock, 13:25 corresponds to 1:25. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CGL

Q57. (Medium) What is the vertical mirror image of the word 'MIRROR' when a mirror is placed to its right?
(A) ᴙOЯЯIM (B) MIRROR (C) ᴙOᴙᴙIM (D) ЯOЯЯIM

Answer: (C) ᴙOᴙᴙIM

Solution:
A mirror on the right reverses letter order from right to left: R becomes ᴙ, O stays O, R becomes ᴙ, R becomes ᴙ, I stays I, and M stays M. Hence, the resulting word is ᴙOᴙᴙIM. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q58. (Medium) A rectangle has a diagonal line drawn from its top-left to bottom-right corner. When placed above water, the diagonal line in the water image runs from:
(A) Bottom-right to top-left (B) Top-left to bottom-right (C) Top-right to bottom-left (D) Bottom-left to top-right

Answer: (D) Bottom-left to top-right

Solution:
Water reflection inverts the top and bottom: the top-left vertex reflects to the bottom-left vertex, and the bottom-right vertex reflects to the top-right vertex. Thus, the diagonal connects bottom-left to top-right. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: SSC MTS

Q59. (Medium) The water image of a clock shows 11:10. What is the actual time?
(A) 7:20 (B) 6:50 (C) 8:20 (D) 5:10

Answer: (A) 7:20

Solution:
Actual time = 18:30 - Water time = 18:30 - 11:10 = 7:20. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q60. (Medium) What is the vertical mirror image of the word 'KITE' when a mirror is placed to its right?
(A) ETIꓘ (B) ƎTIꓘ (C) ƎTIK (D) ETIK

Answer: (B) ƎTIꓘ

Solution:
Mirror on the right reverses order: E reflects to Ǝ, T remains T, I remains I, and K reflects to ꓘ. Therefore, the resulting image is ƎTIꓘ. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q61. (Medium) A figure consists of a square with a small circle at its centre and a diagonal cross (X). A vertical mirror is placed to its right. Which component remains exactly unchanged?
(A) Cross only (B) Circle only (C) Both circle and cross (D) Neither component

Answer: (C) Both circle and cross

Solution:
Both the circle at the centre and the symmetric diagonal cross have bilateral symmetry across the vertical axis, so both components look identical in the mirror reflection. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC CGL

Q62. (Medium) A clock shows 4:50. What time does its water image show?
(A) 2:50 (B) 2:40 (C) 1:50 (D) 1:40

Answer: (D) 1:40

Solution:
Water time formula: Since minutes > 30, subtract from 17:90. 17:90 - 4:50 = 13:40, which represents 1:40 on a standard clock. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Q64. (Medium) A flagpole stands vertically with a flag pointing to the right. When placed above water, the water image shows the flag pointing:
(A) Pointing left (B) Pointing right (C) Pointing downward (D) Pointing upward

Answer: (B) Pointing right

Solution:
Water reflection inverts top and bottom vertically, but lateral (left-right) directions remain unaffected. Therefore, the flag continues pointing to the right. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q65. (Medium) What is the water image of the letter pair 'MW'?
(A) WW (B) MW (C) WM (D) MM

Answer: (C) WM

Solution:
In a water image, top and bottom invert while sequence stays left-to-right: M inverts to W, and W inverts to M. Thus, MW becomes WM. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q67. (Medium) What is the vertical mirror image of the word 'HELLO' when a mirror is placed to its right?
(A) O⅂⅂ƎH (B) O⅃⅃ƎH (C) O⅂⅂EH (D) OL⅂ƎH

Answer: (A) O⅂⅂ƎH

Solution:
Mirror on the right reverses the order and reflects each letter: O remains O, L reflects to ⅂, L reflects to ⅂, E reflects to Ǝ, and H remains H. Thus, the result is O⅂⅂ƎH. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q68. (Medium) An isosceles triangle points upward. When placed directly above a water surface, the triangle in the water image points:
(A) Upward (B) Downward (C) To the left (D) To the right

Answer: (B) Downward

Solution:
A water surface functions as a horizontal mirror, causing a vertical flip. Consequently, an upward-pointing triangle reflects to point downward. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC MTS

Q69. (Medium) Which of the following capital letters has BOTH vertical mirror symmetry and horizontal water symmetry?
(A) B (B) A (C) H (D) M

Answer: (C) H

Solution:
The letter H has both vertical and horizontal axes of symmetry, so it looks identical in both mirror and water images. Letters A and M have only vertical symmetry, while B has only horizontal symmetry. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q71. (Medium) What is the vertical mirror image of the word 'CROWD' when a mirror is placed to its right?
(A) ᗡWOᴙↃ (B) ᗡWOЯC (C) ᗡWOᴙC (D) ᗡWOЯↃ

Answer: (A) ᗡWOᴙↃ

Solution:
Mirror on the right reverses the letter order and laterally inverts each letter: D becomes ᗡ, W remains W, O remains O, R becomes ᴙ, and C becomes Ↄ. Thus, the image is ᗡWOᴙↃ. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q72. (Medium) An arrow points to the right. First, its water image is taken, and then a vertical mirror image of that water image is taken. The final image shows the arrow pointing:
(A) Right (B) Left (C) Upward (D) Downward

Answer: (B) Left

Solution:
Water reflection keeps horizontal directions unchanged, so the arrow still points right. The subsequent vertical mirror laterally inverts left and right, causing the arrow to point left. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q74. (Medium) When a clock shows exactly 6:00, what time will appear in its vertical mirror image?
(A) 7:00 (B) 12:00 (C) 5:00 (D) 6:00

Answer: (D) 6:00

Solution:
At 6:00, the minute hand points to 12 and the hour hand points to 6, both lying exactly on the vertical line of symmetry. Thus, the mirror time is 6:00. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Q75. (Medium) A semicircle has its flat diameter at the bottom with a dot at its midpoint. When placed above water, where does the dot appear in the water image?
(A) At the bottom curved edge (B) At the midpoint of the flat diameter, now at the top (C) Inside the curved region (D) Outside the semicircle

Answer: (B) At the midpoint of the flat diameter, now at the top

Solution:
Water reflection flips the figure vertically top-to-bottom. The flat diameter moves from the bottom to the top, and the dot remains positioned at its midpoint at the top. Hence, the correct option is (B).
