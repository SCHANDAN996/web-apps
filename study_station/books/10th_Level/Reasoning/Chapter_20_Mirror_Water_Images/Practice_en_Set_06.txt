Q126. (Hard) ⚠️ A clock shows 12:00. First, its image is taken in a vertical mirror. Then that mirror image is reflected in still water. What time does the final image display?
(A) 6:30 (B) 12:00 (C) 6:00 (D) 5:30

Answer: (A) 6:30

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: The vertical mirror of 12:00 is 12:00. The subsequent water reflection of 12:00 is 18:30 - 12:00 = 6:30. Sequential orthogonal reflections invert both axes (a 180° rotation), rather than canceling each other. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q127. (Hard) A square contains a circle touching its top edge. When reflected across a horizontal mirror placed directly below the square, the circle in the reflected square touches the:
(A) Top edge (B) Bottom edge (C) Left edge (D) Right edge

Answer: (B) Bottom edge

Solution:
A horizontal mirror (water reflection) vertically inverts top and bottom. The circle originally touching the top edge reflects to touch the bottom edge of the reflected square. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q128. (Hard) What is the water image of the word 'MIRROR'?
(A) WIRROR (B) MIЯЯOЯ (C) WIᴙᴙOᴙ (D) MIᴙᴙOᴙ

Answer: (C) WIᴙᴙOᴙ

Solution:
Water reflection inverts each letter top-to-bottom while leaving the left-to-right order unchanged: M becomes W, I remains I, R becomes ᴙ, R becomes ᴙ, O remains O, and R becomes ᴙ. Thus, the image is WIᴙᴙOᴙ. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q130. (Hard) An upward-pointing arrow is first reflected across a horizontal mirror (water surface), and then that image is reflected across a vertical mirror. The final arrow points:
(A) Downward (B) Upward (C) To the left (D) To the right

Answer: (A) Downward

Solution:
The horizontal mirror flips top to bottom, making the arrow point downward. The vertical mirror flips left and right, leaving the downward vertical orientation unchanged. Thus, the final arrow points downward. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q136. (Hard) Which of the following capital letters, when reflected in water and then reflected across a vertical mirror, results in the original letter?
(A) C (B) B (C) X (D) P

Answer: (C) X

Solution:
A water reflection followed by a vertical mirror reflection is equivalent to a 180° rotation. Only letters with both vertical and horizontal symmetry (H, I, O, X) return to their original appearance under this operation. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q145. (Hard) When an object undergoes two successive reflections across two parallel vertical mirrors, the final image is:

Q149. (Hard) ⚠️ Reflecting an object first across a vertical mirror and then across a horizontal mirror is geometrically equivalent to:
(A) Returning to the original identical image (B) A 90-degree clockwise rotation (C) A single reflection across a diagonal axis (D) A 180-degree rotation (point reflection)

Answer: (D) A 180-degree rotation (point reflection)

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Reflecting across two mutually perpendicular mirror planes negates both x and y coordinates (x -> -x, y -> -y), which is exactly a 180° rotation about the origin (point inversion). Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q150. (Hard) A regular hexagon has a dot placed at each of its 6 vertices. A vertical mirror is placed along the straight line connecting two opposite vertices. In the mirror image, how many dots lie exactly on the mirror line?
(A) 0 (B) 2 (C) 1 (D) 3

Answer: (B) 2

Solution:
The mirror line passes directly through two opposite vertices. Points lying on the line of reflection reflect onto themselves, so exactly 2 dots lie on the mirror line. Hence, the correct option is (B).
