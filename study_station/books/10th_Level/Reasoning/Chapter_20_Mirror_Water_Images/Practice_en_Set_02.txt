Q26. (Medium) A clock shows 4:20. If a vertical mirror is placed on its left side, what time will the mirror image show?
(A) 7:40 (B) 8:40 (C) 8:20 (D) 7:20

Answer: (A) 7:40

Solution:
Mirror time formula: 11:60 - 4:20 = 7:40. The position of the vertical mirror (left or right) does not affect the formula. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q27. (Medium) Which of the following words looks identical in its water image?
(A) ROAD (B) HIDE (C) MIND (D) WALK

Answer: (B) HIDE

Solution:
All letters H, I, D, and E possess horizontal reflection symmetry, and a water surface preserves the left-to-right order. Thus, HIDE remains identical. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q28. (Medium) A square has a small black dot at its top-right corner. When reflected in a horizontal mirror placed below the square, the dot appears at the:
(A) Top-left corner (B) Bottom-left corner (C) Bottom-right corner (D) Top-right corner

Answer: (C) Bottom-right corner

Solution:
A horizontal mirror (water reflection) inverts top and bottom while leaving left and right unchanged. Therefore, the top-right corner reflects to the bottom-right corner. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q29. (Medium) Which of the following capital letters does NOT look identical in its water image?
(A) C (B) H (C) E (D) F

Answer: (D) F

Solution:
The letters C, H, and E have horizontal symmetry and look unchanged in water. The letter F lacks horizontal symmetry and inverts upside down. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q30. (Medium) What is the vertical mirror image of the word 'CAR' when a mirror is placed to its right?
(A) ᴙAƆ (B) ƆAᴙ (C) CAR (D) RAC

Answer: (A) ᴙAƆ

Solution:
A mirror on the right reverses the sequence of letters and laterally inverts each letter: R becomes first as ᴙ, A remains A, and C becomes last as Ɔ. Thus, the image is ᴙAƆ. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: RRB Group D

Q31. (Medium) ⚠️ Does the capital letter 'S' remain identical in its water image?
(A) Yes, it is completely unchanged (B) No, it inverts to Ƨ (C) It turns into Z (D) It turns into the digit 5

Answer: (B) No, it inverts to Ƨ

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: The letter S has 180-degree rotational symmetry, not reflection symmetry. When flipped vertically in water, the top loop flips to the bottom and reverses direction, forming Ƨ. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CGL

Q32. (Medium) A clock shows 11:45. What time will be seen in its water image?
(A) 8:45 (B) 7:15 (C) 6:45 (D) 5:15

Answer: (C) 6:45

Solution:
Water time formula: Subtract from 17:90 when minutes exceed 30. 17:90 - 11:45 = 6:45. Hence, the correct option is (C).
