4. Top 10 High-Yield Questions with Solutions

Question 1:
If a clock shows 11:50, what time will appear in its mirror image?
Solution:
Formula: Mirror Time = 11:60 - Actual Time
Calculation: 11:60 - 11:50 = 00:10, which corresponds to 12:10.
Examiner's Trap: Students often get confused by '00:10' and mistake it for 1:10 or 12:00. Subtracting from 11:60 gives 00:10, representing 12:10.

Question 2:
Find the correct water image of the word "QUALITY".
Solution:
In a water image, inversion is strictly vertical (top ↔ bottom), while the left-to-right order of letters remains unchanged. Each letter is flipped vertically, reading from left to right: Q-U-A-L-I-T-Y.
Examiner's Trap: Students often laterally reverse the whole word as in a mirror image. In water reflection, the first letter remains on the left, but upside down.

Question 3:
Find the mirror image of a figure having an arrow pointing left and a square on the right, when the mirror is placed on the right.
Solution:
When a vertical mirror is on the right, left and right swap: the arrow moves to the right and points right, while the square moves to the left.
Examiner's Trap: Look out for options where positions are swapped but the arrow's pointing direction is mistakenly left unchanged.

Question 4:
How many capital letters of the English alphabet have identical water images?
Solution:
Letters with horizontal symmetry remain identical in water reflection: B, C, D, E, H, I, K, O, X (total 9 letters).
Examiner's Trap: Do not confuse mirror symmetry (A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y - 11 letters) with water symmetry. Letters like A, M, W change completely in water reflection.

Question 5:
Find the water image of the time 8:20 on a clock without numbers (dots only).
Solution:
Formula: Water Time = 18:30 - Actual Time
Calculation: 18:30 - 8:20 = 10:10.
Examiner's Trap: If minutes exceed 30, borrow 60 minutes and subtract from 17:90. For minutes <= 30, subtract directly from 18:30.

Question 6:
If the clock displays 4:45, what is its water image?
Solution:
Since minutes (45) exceed 30, use 17:90:
17:90 - 4:45 = 13:45, which converts to 1:45 (or approximately 1:15 depending on hour hand position).
Examiner's Trap: Clock water images are optical approximations because the hour hand position does not strictly match a physically running clock mechanism.

Question 7:
Which number from 0 to 9 has identical mirror and water images?
Solution:
The digits 0 and 8 possess both vertical and horizontal symmetry, remaining unchanged in both mirror and water reflections.
Examiner's Trap: Digit 1 is often drawn with an asymmetric serif; standard symmetrical digits are 0 and 8.

Question 8:
Find the mirror image of "BRIDGE" when the mirror is placed vertically to its right.
Solution:
The word is read in reverse order from right to left: E-G-D-I-R-B, with each individual letter laterally inverted.
Examiner's Trap: Common mistake is flipping each letter in place without reversing the letter sequence.

Question 9:
What is the mirror image of a shape when the mirror is placed horizontally below it?
Solution:
A mirror placed horizontally below an object produces an exact water image (vertical flip, top becomes bottom, left-right unchanged).
Examiner's Trap: A mirror placed horizontally is NOT a vertical lateral inversion; it acts identically to a water reflection surface.

Question 10:
Find the water image of "DISC8".
Solution:
Letters and digits inverted vertically: D, I, C, 8 have horizontal symmetry and look identical; S flips vertically (top and bottom curves invert). Sequence stays from left to right.
Examiner's Trap: Reversing the order of characters is a mirror trap; in water reflection, the sequence stays left-to-right.
