Q101. (Medium) A clock shows 3:15. What time will appear in its vertical mirror image?
(A) 8:45 (B) 9:45 (C) 8:15 (D) 9:15

Answer: (A) 8:45

Solution:
Mirror time formula: 11:60 - 3:15 = 8:45. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q102. (Medium) What is the water image of the combination A3B?
(A) A3ᗺ (B) ∀Ɛᗺ (C) ∀3B (D) ∀ƐB

Answer: (B) ∀Ɛᗺ

Solution:
In a water image, each character inverts vertically top-to-bottom while maintaining order: A inverts to ∀, 3 inverts to Ɛ, and B inverts to ᗺ. Hence, the result is ∀Ɛᗺ. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q103. (Medium) What is the vertical mirror image of the word 'CLIP' when a mirror is placed to its right?
(A) qILC (B) PI⅂Ↄ (C) qI⅂Ↄ (D) pILC

Answer: (C) qI⅂Ↄ

Solution:
Mirror on the right reverses letter order from right to left: P reflects to q, I remains I, L reflects to ⅂, and C reflects to Ↄ. Thus, the resulting image is qI⅂Ↄ. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q106. (Medium) Which of the following capital letters does NOT look identical in its water image?
(A) H (B) N (C) E (D) C

Answer: (B) N

Solution:
Letters H, E, and C have horizontal reflection symmetry and remain unchanged in water. Letter N has rotational symmetry, so it inverts into И. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CGL

Q107. (Medium) A downward-pointing arrow is placed in front of a vertical mirror on its right side. The mirror image will show the arrow pointing:
(A) To the left (B) Upward (C) Downward (D) To the right

Answer: (C) Downward

Solution:
A vertical mirror produces lateral inversion (left-right swap) only; vertical directions remain completely unaffected. Therefore, the arrow continues pointing downward. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Q109. (Medium) ⚠️ Does the capital letter 'N' remain identical in its water image?
(A) No, it inverts into И (B) Yes, it is completely unchanged (C) It turns into Z (D) It turns into M

Answer: (A) No, it inverts into И

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: The letter N possesses 180-degree rotational symmetry, not reflection symmetry. When reflected across a horizontal axis, the diagonal stroke reverses direction, forming И. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q121. (Hard) A clock shows 9:45. First, its water image is taken, and then the vertical mirror image of that water image is taken. What time does the final image display?
(A) 3:15 (B) 8:45 (C) 9:45 (D) 10:15

Answer: (A) 3:15

Solution:
Water image of 9:45: 17:90 - 9:45 = 8:45. Then vertical mirror image of 8:45: 11:60 - 8:45 = 3:15. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q122. (Hard) The vertical mirror image of a certain English word with mirror on the right is 'ЯAMOƧ'. What is the original word?
(A) RAMOS (B) SOMAR (C) SOMRA (D) ROMAS

Answer: (B) SOMAR

Solution:
To find the original word from its mirror image, reverse the sequence and un-mirror each letter: Ƨ becomes S, O remains O, M remains M, A remains A, and Я becomes R. The original word is SOMAR. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q124. (Hard) A north-east pointing arrow (upward and to the right) is placed above a still water surface. In what direction does its water image point?
(A) North-east (B) North-west (C) South-west (D) South-east

Answer: (D) South-east

Solution:
Water reflection inverts the vertical component (north becomes south) while the horizontal component remains unchanged (east remains east). Thus, the arrow points south-east. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Q125. (Hard) ⚠️ A clock's hands at exactly 12:00 are first reflected across a vertical mirror, and the resulting image is then reflected in water. What time does the final image display?
(A) 12:00 (B) 6:30 (C) 6:00 (D) 12:30

Answer: (B) 6:30

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: A vertical mirror reflection followed by a water (horizontal) reflection is equivalent to a 180-degree rotation, not a cancellation. At 12:00, the mirror image is 12:00; its water reflection is 18:30 - 12:00 = 6:30. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: RRB Group D
