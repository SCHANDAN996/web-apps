101. A clock shows 3:15. What time will appear in its vertical mirror image?

B. 9:45
C. 8:15
D. 9:15

🔍 Step‑by‑Step Solutions & Sources

101. Mirror time = 11:60 – 3:15 = 8:45.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

102. Water: A→∀, 3→Ɛ, B→ᗺ → ∀Ɛᗺ.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

103. Mirror each character: C→Ↄ, L→⅂, I→I, P→q. Reverse order: q I ⅂ Ↄ → qI⅂Ↄ.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

104. Water flips top‑bottom: 7→7, 2→2, 9→6 → 726.
Source: RRB Group D 2019

105. For 12:45, treat as 0:45. Mirror = 12:00 – 0:45 = 11:15.
Source: SSC MTS 2021

106. Water: C→Ɔ, A→∀, N→N (symmetric), D→ᗡ, Y→Y → Ɔ∀NᗡY.
Source: SSC CGL 2023

107. Vertical mirror flips left‑right only; down arrow remains down.
Source: SSC GD 2022

108. Mirror time = 11:60 – 6:50 = 5:10.
Source: SSC CPO 2020

109. N is horizontally symmetric (top‑bottom flip), so water image remains N.
Source: RRB NTPC 2021

110. Mirror on left flips left‑right: right‑pointing arrow → left‑pointing; circle at tail stays with tail, now on left side.
Source: UP Police 2019

111. Water: P→b, Q→ᗡ, R→ᴙ, S→S → bᗡᴙS.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

112. Actual time = 18:30 – 2:20 = 16:10 = 4:10 PM.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

113. ZOO: mirror right → reverse order O O Z, mirror each: O→O, O→O, Z→Ƨ → OOƧ.
Source: SSC MTS 2022

114. Water: W→M, X→X, Y→Y, Z→Z → MXYZ.
Source: IBPS Clerk 2020

115. Mirror time = 11:60 – 10:15 = 1:45.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

116. Water above flips top‑bottom; top vertex dot goes to bottom vertex.
Source: NIFT Entrance 2021

117. SCHOOL: S→Ƨ, C→Ↄ, H→H, O→O, O→O, L→⅂. Reverse: ⅂ O O H Ↄ Ƨ → ⅂OOHↃƧ.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

118. Water time = 18:30 – 1:15 = 17:15 = 5:15 PM.
Source: SSC CPO 2022

119. Mirror on right: left‑pointing arrow → right‑pointing; circle at tip remains at tip (now right side).
Source: RRB ALP 2020

120. 3’s water image (top‑bottom flip) is Ɛ.
Source: SSC GD 2021

121. Original 9:45 → water image = 18:30 – 9:45 = 8:45 → vertical mirror of 8:45 = 12:00 – 8:45 = 3:15. Examiner’s trap: Students may think two reflections cancel out, or apply wrong order of transformations.
Source: SSC CGL Tier‑II 2020

122. Given mirror image ЯAMOƧ. Reverse order: Ƨ O M A Я. Un‑mirror each: Ƨ→S, O→O, M→M, A→A, Я→R → SOMAR.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

123. From water image 7:40, actual = 18:30 – 7:40 = 10:50. Then vertical mirror of 10:50 = 11:60 – 10:50 = 1:10. Examiner’s trap: Multi‑step calculation with two different formulas; easy to mix up.
Source: SSC CHSL 2022

124. North‑east arrow flips top‑bottom: vertical component reverses, horizontal unchanged → south‑east.
Source: NID Entrance 2020

125. Water image HƎ⅂⅂O. Un‑flip top‑bottom each character: H→H, Ǝ→E, ⅂→L, ⅂→L, O→O → HELLO.
Source: SSC CGL 2021
