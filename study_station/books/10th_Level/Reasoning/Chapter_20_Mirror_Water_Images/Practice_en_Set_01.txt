Q1. (Easy) A mirror image reverses:
(A) Left-right (B) Top-bottom (C) Both (D) None

Answer: (A) Left-right

Solution:
A vertical mirror laterally reverses left and right, keeping top and bottom unchanged. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q2. (Easy) A water image reverses:
(A) Left-right (B) Top-bottom (C) Both (D) None

Answer: (B) Top-bottom

Solution:
A water image vertically inverts top and bottom, while left and right remain unchanged. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. (Easy) What is the mirror time of 4:00 on a clock?
(A) 7:00 (B) 9:00 (C) 8:00 (D) 6:00

Answer: (C) 8:00

Solution:
Mirror time = 11:60 - 4:00 = 7:60 = 8:00. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q24. (Medium) ⚠️ If a clock displays 11:50, what time will appear in its mirror image?
(A) 00:10 (B) 1:10 (C) 11:10 (D) 12:10

Answer: (D) 12:10

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: 11:60 - 11:50 = 00:10. On a 12-hour clock, 00:10 represents 12:10. Therefore, the required mirror time is 12:10. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q25. (Medium) ⚠️ A mirror is placed horizontally beneath the word 'KICK'. What is the resulting image?
(A) KCIK (B) KICK (C) KCIC (D) None of these

Answer: (B) KICK

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: A mirror placed horizontally beneath an object forms a water image (vertical flip), not a lateral mirror image. The sequence stays left-to-right (K-I-C-K). Since K, I, and C all have horizontal symmetry, the image remains 'KICK'. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: RRB Group D
