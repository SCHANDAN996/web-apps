Q101. (Medium) Statement: A large number of private schools are charging fees in excess of the government-mandated cap.
Courses of Action:
I. The education department should conduct audits and impose penalties on erring schools.
II. All private schools should be taken over by the government.
(A) Only I follows (B) Only II follows (C) Both I and II follow (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Only I follows

Solution:
I is a direct, feasible regulatory step. II is an extreme, impractical nationalisation that does not logically follow from a fee-cap violation. Only I follows. Hence, the correct option is (A).
