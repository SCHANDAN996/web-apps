Q126. (Hard) Statement: The majority of citizens in a city are suffering from respiratory diseases, and a study attributes 70% of the pollution to vehicular emissions.
Courses of Action:
I. The city administration should phase out all private diesel and petrol vehicles within a year and replace them with electric vehicles.
II. An immediate odd-even vehicle rationing scheme should be introduced during peak pollution months, along with an expansion of public transport.
(A) Only II follows (B) Only I follows (C) Both I and II follow (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Only II follows

Solution:
I is an extreme and impractical deadline; a year is unrealistic for complete replacement without infrastructure, and it ignores economic feasibility. II is a balanced, immediate, and tested measure to quickly reduce emissions, coupled with strengthening alternatives. Hence only II follows. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q127. (Hard) ⚠️ Statement: A sudden and severe flash flood has devastated several villages, destroying homes and crops.
Courses of Action:
I. Immediate rescue, relief, and temporary shelter should be provided to the affected people.
II. A high-level committee should be set up immediately to investigate the causes and recommend long-term flood prevention measures.
(A) Only I follows (B) Both I and II follow (C) Only II follows (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (B) Both I and II follow

Solution:
I is the emergency life-saving step. II, though a future-looking action, is an immediate administrative decision to set up a committee; starting the investigation now is logical and standard practice. Both are independent and do not conflict. The trap is to discard II as not urgent, but initiating an enquiry is a concrete immediate step. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q132. (Hard) Statement: A few cases of a rare contagious disease have been detected in a remote village.
Courses of Action:
I. The entire village should be quarantined, and all movement banned indefinitely.
II. All villagers should be forcibly vaccinated with an experimental vaccine.
(A) Only I follows (B) Only II follows (C) Neither I nor II follows (D) Both I and II follow

Answer: (C) Neither I nor II follows

Solution:
I uses "indefinitely", making it an extreme, unscientific measure without defined criteria. II is unethical and impractical without proven vaccine efficacy and consent. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Hence, the correct option is (A).
