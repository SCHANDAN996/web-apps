Set 1: Course of Action MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. Problem: A school has poor exam results. Action: Arrange extra classes for weak students. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Extreme (d) Unrelated
Answer: (a) Should be taken
Solution: Related, practical, and solves the problem.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. Problem: A city is very dirty. Action: Demolish the whole city. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Always (d) Required
Answer: (b) Should not be taken
Solution: Extreme and impractical.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Problem: Traffic jams are frequent. Action: Widen the roads and add signals. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Extreme (d) Unrelated

Solution: Related, practical, reduces jams.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. What is the main aim of a course of action?
