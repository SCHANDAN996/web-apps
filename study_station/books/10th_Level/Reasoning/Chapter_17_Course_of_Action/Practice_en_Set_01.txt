Q1. (Easy) Statement: Many students of a school fell ill after consuming food from the canteen.
Courses of Action:
I. The canteen should be closed temporarily and a food safety inspection ordered.
II. All students should be advised to bring homemade food.
(A) Only I follows (B) Only II follows (C) Both I and II follow (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Only I follows

Solution:
I is a direct, practical administrative step to immediately address the hygiene crisis and prevent further illness. II is a general advice, not an immediate action that the school administration can enforce as a solution; it does not directly address the contaminated canteen. Hence only I follows. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL
