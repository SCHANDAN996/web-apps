PYQ Analysis: Course of Action (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Course of action gives 1–2 questions per exam. The trap is treating extreme actions as correct and picking unrelated ones.

Q1. Problem: Dengue is spreading in a city. Action: Run a cleanliness and anti-mosquito drive. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Cannot say (d) Extreme
Answer: (a) Should be taken
Solution: It is directly related, practical, and not extreme.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. Problem: Students fail an exam. Action: Ban all students from school forever. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Always (d) Sometimes
Answer: (b) Should not be taken
Solution: It is extreme and does not solve the problem.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. What matters most in a course of action?
(a) Punishment (b) Solving the problem (c) Cost (d) Speed
Answer: (b) Solving the problem
Solution: The main aim is to solve or reduce the problem.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q4. Problem: A road has frequent accidents. Action: Put up speed breakers and signals. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Extreme (d) Unrelated
Answer: (a) Should be taken
Solution: It is related, practical, and prevents accidents.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q5. An extreme course of action is:
(a) usually correct (b) usually not correct (c) always correct (d) always required
Answer: (b) usually not correct
Solution: Overly harsh or impossible actions are usually wrong.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. Problem: A factory pollutes a river. Action: Install a water-treatment plant. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Extreme (d) Unrelated
Answer: (a) Should be taken
Solution: It is related, practical, and reduces pollution.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. Problem: A city faces a water shortage. Action: Import all citizens' water from abroad daily. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Always (d) Required
Answer: (b) Should not be taken
Solution: It is impractical and extreme.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q8. A good course of action usually does what?
(a) only punishes (b) prevents or solves the problem (c) ignores the problem (d) delays action
Answer: (b) prevents or solves the problem
Solution: Good actions prevent or solve, not just punish.
Source: SSC CGL

Q9. Problem: Many people are illiterate in a village. Action: Open adult-education centres. Should it be taken?
(a) Should be taken (b) Should not be taken (c) Extreme (d) Unrelated
Answer: (a) Should be taken
Solution: It is related, practical, and addresses the problem.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. An action not related to the problem should be:
(a) taken (b) not taken (c) always taken (d) taken partly
Answer: (b) not taken
Solution: Unrelated actions are not correct courses of action.
Source: SSC CGL
