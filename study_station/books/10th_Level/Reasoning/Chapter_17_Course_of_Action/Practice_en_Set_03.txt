Q51. (Medium) Statement: A sudden shortage of onions has led to a sharp price rise across the country.
Courses of Action:
I. The government should immediately release buffer stocks of onions to stabilize prices.
II. A permanent ban on onion exports should be imposed to prevent future shortages.
(A) Only I follows (B) Only II follows (C) Both I and II follow (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Only I follows

Solution:
I is a direct, temporary administrative measure to control the current crisis. II is an extreme, permanent trade restriction that is not justified by a short-term shortage and may harm farmers’ income. Hence only I follows. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q52. (Medium) ⚠️ Statement: A severe drought has affected several districts, causing acute water and food shortages.
Courses of Action:
I. Immediate relief measures like water tankers and food camps should be organized.
II. A long-term water conservation and watershed management plan should be initiated.
(A) Only I follows (B) Both I and II follow (C) Only II follows (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (B) Both I and II follow

Solution:
I addresses the immediate human distress; II tackles the root cause for future prevention. Both are practical, complementary administrative actions. The trap is to dismiss II as a future plan, but in disaster management, simultaneous initiation of relief and planning is standard. Thus both follow. Hence, the correct option is (B).
