Set 1: Statement & Course of Action (1–25)
Foundation Level • Verbal Reasoning

Instructions: In each question below is given a statement followed by two courses of action numbered I and II. A course of action is a step or administrative decision to be taken for improvement, follow‑up, or further action in regard to the problem, policy, etc. On the basis of the information given in the statement, you have to assume everything in the statement to be true and then decide which of the suggested courses of action logically follow(s) for pursuing.

Give answer:
(a) if only I follows
(b) if only II follows
(c) if both I and II follow
(d) if neither I nor II follows

Easy Level (1–20)

1.
Statement: Many students of a school fell ill after consuming food from the canteen.
Courses of Action:
I. The canteen should be closed temporarily and a food safety inspection ordered.
II. All students should be advised to bring homemade food.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) only I follows
Step‑by‑step solution:
I is a direct, practical administrative step to immediately address the hygiene crisis and prevent further illness. II is a general advice, not an immediate action that the school administration can enforce as a solution; it does not directly address the contaminated canteen. Hence only I follows.
Source: SSC CHSL 2022

24. (Examiner's Trap – Dual Connection)
Statement: A major bridge collapsed during peak traffic hours, leading to loss of lives and severe traffic jams.
Courses of Action:
I. Rescue and relief operations should be started immediately.
II. An inquiry committee should be set up to find the cause of the collapse.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (c) both I and II follow
Step‑by‑step solution: Both are valid and independent courses. I addresses the immediate disaster response; II is a standard follow‑up administrative step to prevent recurrence. The trap is to think that only I is immediate, but II can also be initiated simultaneously. Thus both follow.
Source: UPSC CSAT 2018 (adapted)

25. (Examiner's Trap – Misleading extreme option that sounds strong)
Statement: The forest cover in the state has decreased by 15% in the last decade due to illegal logging.
Courses of Action:
I. Strict patrolling in forest areas and heavy penalties for illegal logging should be enforced.
II. All timber-based industries in the state should be shut down.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) only I follows
Step‑by‑step solution: I is a targeted, practical measure to combat illegal logging. II is a disproportionate reaction that would affect legal industries and the economy without a clear link to stopping illegal logging. Only I follows.
Source: SSC CGL 2023
