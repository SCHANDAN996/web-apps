Set 5: Statement & Course of Action (101–125)
Foundation Level • Verbal Reasoning • Medium (101–120), Hard (121–125)

Instructions: In each question below is given a statement followed by two courses of action numbered I and II. A course of action is a step or administrative decision to be taken for improvement, follow‑up, or further action in regard to the problem, policy, etc. On the basis of the information given in the statement, you have to assume everything in the statement to be true and then decide which of the suggested courses of action logically follow(s) for pursuing.

Give answer:
(a) if only I follows
(b) if only II follows
(c) if both I and II follow
(d) if neither I nor II follows

Medium Level (101–120)

101.
Statement: A large number of private schools are charging fees in excess of the government‑mandated cap.
Courses of Action:
I. The education department should conduct audits and impose penalties on erring schools.
II. All private schools should be taken over by the government.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) only I follows
Solution: I is a direct, feasible regulatory step. II is an extreme, impractical nationalisation that does not logically follow from a fee‑cap violation. Only I follows.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

110. (Examiner’s Trap – Misleading Soft Option That Is Valid)
Statement: A village has reported a high number of snakebite cases during the rainy season.
Courses of Action:
I. The health department should stock anti‑venom in the village health centre and train local health workers.
II. A campaign on wearing protective footwear and avoiding walking in the dark should be launched.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (c) both I and II follow
Solution: I ensures medical preparedness; II is a preventive community awareness step directly linked to the cause of snakebites. Both are concrete, actionable administrative steps. The trap is to reject II as a mere suggestion, but a government‑run campaign is a valid course of action.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

Hard Level (121–125)

121.
Statement: A country has faced three devastating floods in the same region within a single decade, causing massive rehabilitation costs each time.
Courses of Action:
I. A comprehensive flood‑plain zoning and resettlement plan should be executed, moving permanent settlements to higher ground.
II. The government should continue with the existing practice of post‑flood relief and compensation.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) only I follows
Solution: Repeated devastation indicates that relief alone is unsustainable. I offers a durable, proactive solution. II is a reactionary approach that fails to break the cycle; a course of action should solve the problem, not perpetuate it. Hence only I follows.
Source: UPSC CSAT 2019

122. (Examiner’s Trap – Dual Connection with Nuance: One Extreme, One Feasible)
Statement: A nation‑wide strike by truck drivers has halted the supply of essential goods, causing prices to skyrocket.
Courses of Action:
I. The government should invoke the Essential Services Maintenance Act (ESMA) to temporarily prevent the strike and ensure supply chains are restored.
II. The government should immediately accept all demands of the truck drivers without negotiation to end the strike.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) only I follows
Solution: I is a standard, legally sanctioned emergency measure to protect public interest. II is a surrender without procedure; accepting all demands without scrutiny sets a dangerous precedent and is not a prudent administrative step. Only I follows.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

124. (Examiner’s Trap – Both Follow with Priorities, yet Many May Pick Only I)
Statement: A deadly new virus, resistant to known treatments, has begun spreading in a densely populated district, with a high fatality rate.
Courses of Action:
I. A strict containment zone should be declared, with movement restricted and testing ramped up.
II. An immediate global research collaboration should be initiated to understand the virus and develop a vaccine.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (c) both I and II follow
Solution: I is the immediate public health response to limit spread. II is a necessary parallel scientific step; starting research immediately is a valid administrative action, especially when the threat is novel and deadly. Both are independent and do not conflict. The trap is seeing II as a long‑term dream and rejecting it, but coordinating research is a concrete step that can begin instantly.
Source: UPSC CSAT 2021 (adapted)

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) only I follows
Solution: I is a balanced, procedural step that respects both development needs and community rights. II is an extreme, one‑sided decision that ignores the project’s potential benefits and the possibility of a middle path. A course of action must be pragmatic, not purely ideological. Only I follows.
Source: SSC CGL 2022
