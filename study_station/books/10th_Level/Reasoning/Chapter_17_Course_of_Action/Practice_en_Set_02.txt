Q26. (Medium) Statement: The number of child labourers in the district has risen sharply in the last two years, despite a legal ban.
Courses of Action:
I. The labour department should conduct raids and ensure strict enforcement of the ban with rehabilitation measures.
II. All factories in the district should be closed until child labour is completely eradicated.
(A) Only I follows (B) Only II follows (C) Both I and II follow (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Only I follows

Solution:
I is a direct, practical enforcement action that targets the problem. II is an extreme, blanket measure that would disrupt the economy and punish compliant factories without addressing root causes. Hence only I follows. Hence, the correct option is (A).
