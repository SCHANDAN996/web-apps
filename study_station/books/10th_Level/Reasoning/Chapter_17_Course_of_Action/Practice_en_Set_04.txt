Q76. (Medium) Statement: Cyber frauds targeting senior citizens have increased significantly.
Courses of Action:
I. A dedicated cyber crime helpline and awareness campaign for the elderly should be launched.
II. All online transactions by senior citizens should be blocked.
(A) Only I follows (B) Only II follows (C) Both I and II follow (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Only I follows

Solution:
I is a targeted, practical administrative response. II is an extreme, patronising restriction that removes digital access entirely and fails to address the root crime. Only I follows. Hence, the correct option is (A).
