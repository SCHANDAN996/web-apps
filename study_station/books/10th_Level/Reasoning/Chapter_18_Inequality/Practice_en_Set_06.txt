Q126. (Hard) ⚠️ In a certain code: ‘A @ B’ ⇒ A ≥ B, ‘A # B’ ⇒ A ≤ B, ‘A % B’ ⇒ A > B, ‘A $ B’ ⇒ A < B, ‘A * B’ ⇒ A = B
Statements: P % Q, Q $ R, R * S, S # T, T @ U, U % V, V $ W
Conclusions:
I. P % W
II. Q $ U
(A) Neither I nor II follows (B) Only II follows (C) Either I or II follows (D) Only I follows

Answer: (A) Neither I nor II follows

Solution:
Decoded: P > Q < R = S ≤ T ≥ U > V < W. Between P and W, signs reverse at Q and V. Between Q and U, signs reverse at T. Neither follows. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q127. (Hard) Statements: A > B ≥ C < D = E ≤ F; G = F > H; I ≤ H; J > I
Conclusions:
I. A > E
II. C < G
III. F > I
IV. B < J
(A) I and III follow (B) II and III follow (C) II, III and IV follow (D) All follow

Answer: (B) II and III follow

Solution:
I: A > B ≥ C < D = E reverses at C (false). II: C < D = E ≤ F = G ⇒ C < G (true). III: F = G > H ≥ I ⇒ F > I (true). IV: B and J have breaks at C and H (false). Thus II and III follow. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q128. (Hard) ⚠️ Which set of symbols should replace the blanks in ‘P _ Q _ R _ S _ T’ so that both ‘P > S’ and ‘Q ≤ T’ are definitely true?
(A) >, >, >, ≤ (B) >, =, >, ≤ (C) None of these (D) ≥, >, =, ≤

Answer: (C) None of these

Solution:
In (a), (b), and (c): P > S requires Q to be strictly less than P, but Q ≤ T requires Q ≤ R ≤ S ≤ T; when Q > R, Q ≤ T cannot be guaranteed. Hence none of the given options satisfies both conditions simultaneously. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q130. (Hard) Statements: A ≥ B > C = D; E < D ≤ F = G; H > G = I; J ≤ I

I. A > E
II. C < H
III. B ≤ F
IV. J < H

Solution:
