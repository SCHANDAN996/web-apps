Q101. (Medium) Statements: A > B ≥ C = D ≤ E < F
Conclusions:
I. A > D
II. B < F
III. C < F
(A) I and III follow (B) Only I follows (C) I and II follow (D) All follow

Answer: (A) I and III follow

Solution:
I: A > B ≥ C = D ⇒ A > D (true). II: B ≥ C = D ≤ E < F reverses at D; no definite relation between B and F (false). III: C = D ≤ E < F ⇒ C < F (true). Thus I and III follow. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q102. (Medium) In a certain code: ‘A @ B’ ⇒ A ≤ B, ‘A # B’ ⇒ A ≥ B, ‘A $ B’ ⇒ A < B, ‘A % B’ ⇒ A > B, ‘A * B’ ⇒ A = B

Conclusions:
I. P % S
II. P * S

Answer: (B) Neither I nor II follows

Solution:
Decoded: P ≤ Q > R = S. At Q, signs reverse. No definite relationship exists between P and S. Both > and = are not complementary because < is omitted. Neither follows. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q103. (Medium) Statements: M > N = O ≥ P; Q = R < P; S ≥ Q
Conclusions:
I. O > S
II. N > R
III. M > Q
(A) Only I follows (B) I and III follow (C) II and III follow (D) All follow

Answer: (C) II and III follow

Solution:
O ≥ P > Q ≤ S reverses at Q; O and S relation is undetermined (I false). N = O ≥ P > R ⇒ N > R (II true). M > N = O ≥ P > Q ⇒ M > Q (III true). Thus II and III follow. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q104. (Medium) Which expression ensures that ‘A > C’ and ‘B ≤ D’ are both definitely true?
(A) >, <, ≤ (B) ≥, >, = (C) <, =, ≤ (D) >, =, ≤

Answer: (D) >, =, ≤

Solution:
In (a): A > B = C ≤ D ⇒ A > C (as A > B = C), and B = C ≤ D ⇒ B ≤ D. Both conditions are definitely true. Hence, the correct option is (D).
