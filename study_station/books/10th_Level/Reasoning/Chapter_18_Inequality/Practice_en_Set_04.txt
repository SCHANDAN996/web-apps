Q76. (Medium) ⚠️ In a certain code: ‘A # B’ ⇒ A ≥ B, ‘A @ B’ ⇒ A ≤ B, ‘A * B’ ⇒ A = B, ‘A % B’ ⇒ A > B, ‘A $ B’ ⇒ A < B
Statements: P # Q, Q * R, R # S
Conclusions:
I. P % S
II. P * S
(A) Either I or II follows (B) Only II follows (C) Only I follows (D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Either I or II follows

Solution:
Decoded: P ≥ Q = R ≥ S ⇒ P ≥ S. Either P > S (I) or P = S (II) must hold. Both together form a complementary pair. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q77. (Medium) Statements: A > B ≥ C < D = E; F < E ≤ G
Conclusions:
I. A > C
II. B < G
III. D ≤ G
(A) II and III follow (B) I and III follow (C) I and II follow (D) All follow

Answer: (B) I and III follow

Solution:
From A > B ≥ C, A > C holds (I true). Chain between B and G reverses at C (B ≥ C < D = E ≤ G), so no definite relation (II false). D = E ≤ G ⇒ D ≤ G holds (III true). Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q78. (Medium) Which of the following expressions ensures ‘X < Z’ and ‘Y ≥ W’ are definitely true?

Answer: (C) X < Y = Z ≥ W

Solution:

Q80. (Medium) Statements: M = N ≥ O > P; Q < P ≤ R

II. N > R

Solution:
