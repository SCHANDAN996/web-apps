Q128. Which set of symbols should replace the blanks in ‘P _ Q _ R _ S _ T’ so that both ‘P > S’ and ‘Q ≤ T’ are definitely true?

Answer: (d) None of these
Solution:
(a) P > Q > R > S ≤ T ⇒ P > S true, but Q ≤ T? Q > R > S ≤ T, break, not definite.

(c) P > Q ≥ R = S ≤ T ⇒ P > S true, Q ≤ T? Q ≥ R = S ≤ T, not definite.
Thus no combination works.
Trap: Students may pick (c) assuming Q ≥ R = S ≤ T implies Q ≤ T, but it does not guarantee Q ≤ T (Q could be greater).
