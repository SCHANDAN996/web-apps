Q26. Statements: P ≥ Q < R = S ≤ T; U > T ≥ V
Conclusions: I. Q < U II. P > S
(a) Only I follows
(b) Only II follows
(c) Both follow
(d) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) Only I follows
Solution:
Combine: Q < R = S ≤ T < U ⇒ Q < U (True). From P ≥ Q < R = S, we cannot determine relation between P and S; P could be less than, equal to, or greater than S depending on values (no definite). Thus II does not follow.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

Q27. Statements: A < B ≤ C = D; E ≥ D > F; G = F ≥ H
Conclusions: I. A < E II. B > H
(a) Only I follows
(b) Only II follows
(c) Both follow
(d) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) Only I follows
Solution:
A < B ≤ C = D ≤ E ⇒ A < E (True). B ≤ C = D > F ≥ H ⇒ B > H is not definite; B could be ≤ H if B ≤ D and D > F ≥ H doesn't give B > H necessarily (e.g., B=D=5, F=4, H=4, B>H holds; but B could be 3, D=5, F=4, H=4, then B=3 not >4). So II is not necessarily true.
Source: SSC CGL 2022
