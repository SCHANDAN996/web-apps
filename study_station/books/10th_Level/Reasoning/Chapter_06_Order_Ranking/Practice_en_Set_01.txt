1. A is 5th from the top and 12th from the bottom. How many students are in the class?
(a) 16 (b) 17 (c) 15 (d) 18
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total = 5 + 12 − 1 = 16.
Source: PYQ-style

2. In a class of 50, A is 20th from the top. What is A's rank from the bottom?
(a) 30 (b) 31 (c) 29 (d) 32
Answer: (b)
Solution: From bottom = 50 − 20 + 1 = 31.
Source: PYQ-style
