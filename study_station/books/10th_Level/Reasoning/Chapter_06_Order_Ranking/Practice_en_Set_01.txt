Set 1: Order & Ranking MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Q1. A is 5th from the top and 12th from the bottom. How many students are in the class?
(a) 16 (b) 17 (c) 15 (d) 18
Answer: (a) 16
Solution: Total = 5 + 12 − 1 = 16.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. In a class of 50, A is 20th from the top. What is A's rank from the bottom?
(a) 30 (b) 31 (c) 29 (d) 32
Answer: (b) 31
Solution: From bottom = 50 − 20 + 1 = 31.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. A is 4th and B is 10th from the same end. How many persons are between them?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 7
Answer: (a) 5
Solution: Between = 10 − 4 − 1 = 5.
Source: SSC CHSL
