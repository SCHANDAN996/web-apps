51. In a row of students, Ankit is 12th from the left and Sumit is 18th from the right. If there are 8 persons between them, what could be the total number of persons in the row?
(a) 38 or 20 (b) 38 or 18 (c) 36 or 20 (d) 38 or 22
Answer: (a)
Solution: Case 1 (Non-overlap): Total = 12 + 18 + 8 = 38. Case 2 (Overlap): Total = (12 + 18) - 8 - 2 = 20. Overlap is valid since min(12, 18) = 12 >= 8 + 2. Thus, the total can be 38 or 20.
Source: PYQ-style

52. In a queue of 50 people, Rohit is 15th from the front and Soham is 20th from the back. How many people are there between Rohit and Soham?
(a) 13 (b) 15 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (b)
Solution: Total = 50. Sum of ranks = 15 + 20 = 35. Since Total > 35, number of people between them = 50 - 35 = 15.

Solution: Since C is 20th from left and A is 14th from left, persons between A and C = 20 - 14 - 1 = 5. So there are also 5 persons between C and B. B’s rank from left = 20 + 5 + 1 = 26th. Total persons = 26 + 16 - 1 = 41.
