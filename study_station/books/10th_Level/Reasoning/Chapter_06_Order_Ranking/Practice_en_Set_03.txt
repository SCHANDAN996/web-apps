51. In a row of students, Ankit is 12th from the left. Sumit is 18th from the right. If there are 8 students between them, what is the total number of students in the row?

(a) 38
(b) 36 or 20
(c) 38 or 20
(d) Cannot be determined

Answer: (c) 38 or 20

Solution:
Case 1 – Ankit left of Sumit: Total = Left + Right + Between = 12 + 18 + 8 = 38.
Case 2 – Sumit left of Ankit: Total = Left + Right – Between – 2 = 12 + 18 – 8 – 2 = 20.
Both are possible because the relative order is not given.
