🧠 Order & Ranking – Fun Mnemonics & Skip Strategy
📦 Mnemonic Box 1: The “Luv-Rav” Total Formula

Rule: T = Left + Right – 1
Mnemonic: “Luv aur Rav ek saath khade, Total mein se ek minus karein!”
Imagine Luv and Rav are best friends. They stand in a queue, but they're holding hands – you counted one extra hand! So always subtract 1.

📦 Mnemonic Box 2: Opposite End Rank – “Total Se Minus, Plus One Fun”

Rule: Rank from other end = Total – Given Rank + 1
Mnemonic: “Total mein se diya hua ghataao, phir ek jodo – ulta rank paao!”
Think of it as a reverse camera: you subtract the given rank from total, then add 1 to flip to the other side.

📦 Mnemonic Box 3: Between Two Persons (Same End) – “Gap Me Se Ek Gap”

Rule: Persons between = |Pos1 – Pos2| – 1
Mnemonic: “Difference lo, ek ghato, beech ke log gino!”
Picture two cars parked, the space between them is the difference of their parking numbers minus the space the car itself occupies.

📦 Mnemonic Box 4: Convert Opposite Ends First! – “Dono Ko Ek Side Lao”

Rule: If ranks are from opposite ends, first convert both to the same end.
Mnemonic: “Left-Right mila ke mat lado, dono ko ek taraf karo!”
Never argue left vs right; bring them to the same side like two warring friends forced to sit together.

📦 Mnemonic Box 5: Overlap Check – “Jab Sum Bada Ho Total Se”

Rule: If L + R > T, persons overlap.
Mnemonic: “L+R T ko cross kare, overlap ka rasta pakde!”
If the sum of ranks from opposite ends exceeds total, they’ve crossed paths. Imagine two trains crossing – they overlapped!

📦 Mnemonic Box 6: Overlap Between Formula – “Sum Se T Ghato, Do Ghato”

Rule: Persons between = (L₁ + R₂) – T – 2
Mnemonic: “Sum of opposite, minus total, minus two – the magic between!”
Think of it as a magic spell: “Summinus Totalminus Two! Abracadabra, between who!”

📦 Mnemonic Box 7: Interchange – “Nayi Rank = Purani Dusre Ki”

Rule: After swap, new rank of one from an end = other’s old rank from that same end.
Mnemonic: “Adla-badli mein yaad rahe, nayi rank dusre ki purani hai!”
Just like exchanging seats in a class, the new position is the old position of the friend you swapped with.

📦 Mnemonic Box 8: Interchange Total Finder – “Naya Left + Unchanged Right – 1”

Rule: T = New Left (of swapped person) + Unchanged Right (of the same person) – 1
Mnemonic: “Jo badla uska naya left, jo na badla uska right, dono jodo, ek ghatao – total tight!”
One rank changed, one didn’t. Add the new left and the old right (that stayed), minus 1.

📦 Mnemonic Box 9: Minimum Total – “Ek Hi Aadmi Maan Lo”

Rule: Min total = L + R – 1 (assume same person)
Mnemonic: “Minimum nikaalna hai to dono ko ek kar do, Jaise Batman aur Bruce Wayne ek hain!”
Just like Bruce Wayne is Batman, consider both ranks point to the same person for minimal total.

📦 Mnemonic Box 10: Maximum Total – “Door-Door Khade Hain”

Rule: Max total = L + R (no overlap, opposite extremes)
Mnemonic: “Maximum mein door-door, dono chhoron par khade, beech mein koi nahin!”
For maximum, imagine them at the two extreme ends, with no one between, so total = L + R.

📦 Mnemonic Box 11: “Cannot be Determined” – “Dost Ya Dushman?”

Rule: If relative order not specified, answer may be “Cannot be determined”.
Mnemonic: “Aage-peechhe ka pata nahin, to answer hamesha 'pata nahin'!”
If you don’t know whether A is before B or after, don’t guess — embrace the uncertainty.

📦 Mnemonic Box 12: Middle Position – “Odd Hai To Seedha Hai”

Rule: Middle rank (odd T) = (T+1)/2 from both ends.
Mnemonic: “Total odd to middle chod, aadha kar aur ek aadha jod!”
If total is odd, the middle is just (Total+1)/2 — like finding the center of a samosa!

📦 Mnemonic Box 13: Persons to One Side – “Total Mein Se Left Ghato”

Rule: Persons to the right of a person = Total – Left rank.
Mnemonic: “Right wale ginne hain? Total se left minus karo, bas itna sa kaam!”
No need to add 1; just subtract left rank from total.

📦 Mnemonic Box 14: Interchange Movement Trick – “Movement = Between + 1”

Rule: When two swap and one’s new rank is given, movement = |new – old| = persons between + 1.
Mnemonic: “Kitna sarka? Between+1! Jaise Ludo mein chakka lag gaya!”
The shift in position is exactly the number of people between them plus 1, like jumping over pawns in Ludo.

📦 Mnemonic Box 15: The 15/45 Second Rule Song

Mnemonic:
“15 second mein formula yaad, 45 mein swap-sa bad.
Na aaye toh chhod aage badh, time ki karna mat barbaad!”
Set to a catchy beat, this reminds you: Solve direct formula in 15s, interchange/overlap in 45s, else skip!

⏭️ Skip Strategy Tips

The 15‑Second Sniff Test: If you read the question and can’t immediately decide whether it’s T = L+R–1 or simple conversion within 15 seconds, mark it and skip. Return only if time permits.

The 45‑Second Interchange Trap: Interchange or ambiguous “between” problems often require drawing a line and testing two cases. If your first attempt doesn’t yield a clear answer within 45 seconds, leave it. The cognitive load can eat up time for 3 easy questions elsewhere.

Ambiguous Order = Instant Skip: Any question that says “A is 5th from left, B is 4th from right” without specifying their relative positions, and options include “Cannot be determined,” it’s often a trick. If you don’t spot a unique answer in 30 seconds, skip — the examiner wants you to waste time.
