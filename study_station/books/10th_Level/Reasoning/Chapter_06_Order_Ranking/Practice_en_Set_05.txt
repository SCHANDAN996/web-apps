101. In a row of 40 students, A is 16th from the left end. What is A’s position from the right end?
(a) 25th (b) 23rd (c) 24th (d) 26th
Answer: (a)
Solution: Position from right = Total - Left + 1 = 40 - 16 + 1 = 25th. Therefore, A is 25th.
Source: PYQ-style

102. In a class of 45 students, A is 12th from the top and B is 18th from the bottom. How many students are there between A and B?
(a) 13 (b) 15 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (b)
Solution: Total = 45. Sum of ranks = 12 + 18 = 30. Since Total > 30, students between A and B = 45 - 30 = 15.
Source: PYQ-style

103. In a queue, P is 12th from the front and Q is 15th from the back. If there are 6 persons between them, what could be the total number of persons in the queue?
(a) 33 or 21 (b) 31 or 19 (c) 33 or 19 (d) 35 or 19
Answer: (c)
Solution: Case 1 (Non-overlap): Total = 12 + 15 + 6 = 33. Case 2 (Overlap): Total = (12 + 15) - 6 - 2 = 19. Both cases are valid since min(12, 15) = 12 >= 6 + 2. Thus, the total can be 33 or 19.
Source: PYQ-style

(a) 32 (b) 33 (c) 35 (d) 34
Answer: (d)
Solution: Since C is midway between A and B, there are also 4 boys between C and B. Position of B from left = 11 + 4 + 1 + 4 + 1 = 21. Total boys = 21 + 14 - 1 = 34.
