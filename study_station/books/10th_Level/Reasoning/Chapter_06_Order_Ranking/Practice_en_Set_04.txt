76. In a row of 43 students, A is 15th from the left and B is 11th from the right. If they interchange their positions, what will be A’s new position from the right end?
(a) 11th (b) 12th (c) 13th (d) 14th
Answer: (a)
Solution: When A and B interchange positions, A occupies B’s original spot. Since B was originally 11th from the right, A’s new position from the right end is 11th. Therefore, A is 11th.

Solution: Total persons = Front of P + Back of Q + Between = 13 + 18 + 4 = 35.

78. In a class of 55 students, A is 9th from the top and B is 12th from the bottom. How many students are there between A and B?
(a) 32 (b) 33 (c) 34 (d) 35
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total = 55. Sum of ranks = 9 + 12 = 21. Since Total > 21, students between A and B = 55 - 21 = 34.

81. In a row of boys, A is 14th from the left end and B is 9th from the right end. C is exactly in the middle of A and B. If C is 12th from the right end, what is the total number of boys in the row (assuming no overlap)?
(a) 29 (b) 28 (c) 30 (d) 31
Answer: (b)
Solution: From the right, B is 9th and C is 12th, so boys between B and C = 12 - 9 - 1 = 2. Since C is midway, boys between A and C = 2. C’s position from left = 14 + 2 + 1 = 17th. Total boys = 17 + 12 - 1 = 28.

84. In a row, A is 15th from the left and B is 14th from the right. If there are 9 persons between them, what could be the total number of persons in the row?
(a) 38 or 18 (b) 38 or 20 (c) 36 or 18 (d) 38 or 16
Answer: (a)
Solution: Case 1 (Non-overlap): Total = 15 + 14 + 9 = 38. Case 2 (Overlap): Total = (15 + 14) - 9 - 2 = 18. Overlap is valid since min(15, 14) = 14 >= 9 + 2. Thus, the total can be 38 or 18.

85. In a row of 42 children, P is 10th from the left and Q is 17th from the right. R is exactly in the middle of P and Q. What is R’s position from the left end?
(a) 17th (b) 18th (c) 19th (d) 20th
Answer: (b)
Solution: Q’s position from left = 42 - 17 + 1 = 26th. Since R is midway between P (10th) and Q (26th), R’s position from left = (10 + 26) / 2 = 18th. Therefore, R is 18th.
