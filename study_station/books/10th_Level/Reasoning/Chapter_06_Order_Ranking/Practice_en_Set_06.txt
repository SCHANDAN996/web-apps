126. In a row, A is 10th from the left and B is 12th from the right. If they interchange their positions, A becomes 18th from the left. What is the total number of persons in the row?
(a) 29 (b) 27 (c) 28 (d) 30
Answer: (a)
Solution: After interchange, A occupies B’s original spot (18th from left and 12th from right). Total persons = 18 + 12 - 1 = 29.
Source: PYQ-style

127. In a row of 40 persons, B is 12th from the right end. C is 6th to the left of B. D is 4th to the right of C. What is D’s position from the left end?
(a) 25th (b) 27th (c) 26th (d) 28th
Answer: (b)
Solution: B’s position from left = 40 - 12 + 1 = 29th. C’s position from left = 29 - 6 = 23rd. D’s position from left = 23 + 4 = 27th. Therefore, D is 27th.
Source: PYQ-style

128. In a queue, P is 15th from the front and Q is 17th from the back. R is exactly in the middle of P and Q. If there are 4 persons between P and R, what could be the total number of persons in the queue?
(a) 41 or 23 (b) 39 or 21 (c) 41 or 21 (d) 43 or 21
Answer: (c)
Solution: Case 1 (P ahead): Q front = 15 + 4 + 1 + 4 + 1 = 25. Total = 25 + 17 - 1 = 41. Case 2 (Q ahead): Q front = 15 - (4 + 1 + 4 + 1) = 5. Total = 5 + 17 - 1 = 21. Both are valid. Thus, total is 41 or 21.
Source: PYQ-style

129. In a class of 50 students, A is 16th from the top and B is 22nd from the bottom. How many students are there between A and B?
(a) 10 (b) 11 (c) 13 (d) 12
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total = 50. Sum of ranks = 16 + 22 = 38. Since Total > 38, students between A and B = 50 - 38 = 12.

Answer: (d)
