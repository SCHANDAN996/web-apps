26. In a row of 50 students, A is 15th from the left. B is 16th from the right. C is exactly in the middle of A and B. What is C’s position from the left?
(a) 25th (b) 23rd (c) 24th (d) 26th
Answer: (a)
Solution: B’s rank from left = 50 - 16 + 1 = 35th. Since C is midway between A (15th) and B (35th), C’s position from left = (15 + 35) / 2 = 25th. Therefore, C is 25th.
Source: PYQ-style

27. In a class, Ravi’s rank from the top is 17th and from the bottom is 23rd. If 5 new students join the class and 2 students leave, what is the new total number of students in the class?
(a) 40 (b) 42 (c) 41 (d) 43
Answer: (b)
Solution: Initial total = 17 + 23 - 1 = 39. After 5 join and 2 leave, new total = 39 + 5 - 2 = 42. Therefore, the new total is 42.

(a) 29 (b) 30 (c) 31 (d) 32
Answer: (c)
Solution: Since R is midway between P and Q and there are 3 children between P and R, there are also 3 children between R and Q. Position of Q from left = 15 + 3 + 1 + 3 + 1 = 23. Given Q is 9th from right, Total = 23 + 9 - 1 = 31.

Answer: (d)
Solution: Case 1 (Non-overlap): Total = 7 + 8 + 5 = 20. Case 2 (Overlap): Total = (7 + 8) - 5 - 2 = 8. Since min(7, 8) = 7 >= 5 + 2, overlap is valid. Hence, the total can be 20 or 8.
