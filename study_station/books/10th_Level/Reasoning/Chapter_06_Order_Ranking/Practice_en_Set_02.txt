50. In a row of 80 persons, A is 35th from left. B is 42nd from right. If they interchange, what will be A’s new position from left? ⚠️ (Examiner’s Trap – confusing left/right in interchange)

(a) 39th
(b) 38th
(c) 37th
(d) 36th

Answer: (a) 39th

Solution:
Total=80. B right=42 → B left = 80-42+1=39. When they interchange, A moves to B’s original place. So A’s new left = B’s original left = 39.
Trap: Many students compute A’s new left as total - A new right +1, but A’s new right is B’s old right (42), giving 80-42+1=39, which is same numerically but may cause confusion. The direct shortcut: A’s new left = B’s old left.
