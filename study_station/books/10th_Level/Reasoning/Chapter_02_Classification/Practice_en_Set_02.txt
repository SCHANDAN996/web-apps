26. Choose the odd one out:
(a) Copper (b) Aluminium (c) Brass (d) Zinc
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is zinc, which is a component of brass but is itself a pure metal. Copper, zinc and aluminium are pure elemental metals, while brass is an alloy of copper and zinc, so the answer is Brass.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

27. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 216 (b) 225 (c) 125 (d) 343
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 216, which looks like an ordinary even number but is 6³. Since 125 = 5³, 216 = 6³ and 343 = 7³ are perfect cubes while 225 = 15² is a perfect square, the answer is 225.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

28. Choose the odd one out:
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Narmada (c) Yamuna (d) Ganga
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Yamuna, which is only a tributary but still drains eastward into the Ganga system. Ganga, Yamuna and Brahmaputra flow east into the Bay of Bengal, while Narmada flows west into the Arabian Sea, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

29. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 27 (b) 100 (c) 8 (d) 64
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 64, which is both a square and a cube, but it is still a perfect cube. Since 8 = 2³, 27 = 3³ and 64 = 4³ are cubes while 100 = 10² is only a square, the answer is 100.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

30. Choose the odd one out:

31. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 29 (b) 37 (c) 51 (d) 17
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that 51 is odd and looks prime, but 51 = 3 × 17. Since 17, 29 and 37 are prime numbers while 51 is composite, the answer is 51.

33. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 12 (b) 36 (c) 50 (d) 48
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 36, which is a perfect square, but it shares divisibility by 6 with 12 and 48. Since 12, 36 and 48 are divisible by 6 while 50 is divisible by neither 3 nor 6, the answer is 50.

45. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 36 (b) 25 (c) 48 (d) 16
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 25, the only odd number, but it is still a perfect square like 16 and 36. Since 16 = 4², 25 = 5² and 36 = 6² are perfect squares while 48 is not, the answer is 48.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

46. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 396 (b) 482 (c) 132 (d) 264
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that all four look like random three-digit numbers, hiding the digit rule. In 132, 396 and 264 the middle digit equals the sum of the other two digits (1+2=3, 3+6=9, 2+4=6), while in 482, 4+2=6 which is not 8, so the answer is 482.
Source: PYQ-style

47. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 14-195 (b) 12-144 (c) 13-169 (d) 11-121
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 195, which is just one less than 196 and looks plausible. In three pairs the second number is the square of the first: 11²=121, 12²=144 and 13²=169, but 14²=196, not 195, so the answer is 14-195.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 2, 4, 8 (b) 5, 25, 125 (c) 4, 16, 32 (d) 3, 9, 27
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 2, 4, 8, which fits the power pattern perfectly. In three triples the terms are the first three powers of the base: 2,2²,2³; 3,3²,3³; 5,5²,5³, while 4, 16, 32 breaks the pattern because 4³=64, not 32, so the answer is 4, 16, 32.
Source: PYQ-style

49. Choose the odd one out:

Solution: The trap is 730, which sits just one above 729 = 9³ and looks like a cube. Since 343 = 7³, 512 = 8³ and 1331 = 11³ are perfect cubes while 730 is not, the answer is 730.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
