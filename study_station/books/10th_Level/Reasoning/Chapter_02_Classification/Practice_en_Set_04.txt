76. Choose the odd one out.
(a) BGL
(b) DIP
(c) FKR
(d) HMT
Answer: (d) HMT
Solution: Positions: B(2) G(7) L(12) → +5,+5; D(4) I(9) N(14) → +5,+5; F(6) K(11) P(16) → +5,+5; H(8) M(13) S(19) → +5,+6 — wait H(8) to M(13) is +5, M(13) to S(19) is +6. Wait the option is HMT, but H(8) M(13) T(20) would be +5,+7. The option says HMT, so letters are H, M, T. H(8) M(13) difference +5, M(13) T(20) difference +7. So HMT breaks the constant +5 gap.
Source: PYQ-style
