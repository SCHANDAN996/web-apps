Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the odd one out:
(a) Potato (b) Banana (c) Mango (d) Apple
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to treat all four as food items and stop there. Apple, banana, and mango are fruits, while potato is a tuber vegetable that grows underground, so the answer is Potato.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 23 (b) 17 (c) 19 (d) 21
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to look for a sequence gap, but the real rule is divisibility. 17, 19, and 23 are prime numbers, while 21 = 3 × 7 is composite, so the answer is 21.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

3. Choose the odd one out:
(a) LOR (b) PRU (c) JMP (d) NQT
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to check only the first two letters. In JMP, LOR, and NQT the letter positions increase by 3 each time (J+3=M, M+3=P and so on), while PRU increases by 2 then 3, so the answer is PRU.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 49 (b) 36 (c) 72 (d) 25
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to check even-odd, which splits them two-two and decides nothing. 25, 36, and 49 are perfect squares (5², 6², 7²), while 72 is not a perfect square, so the answer is 72.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

6. Choose the odd one out:
(a) AZ (b) EV (c) IS (d) CX
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to look for vowels, but each pair has one. In AZ, EV, and CX the two letters are equidistant from the ends of the alphabet (1–26, 5–22, 3–24), while I(9) and S(19) are not, so the answer is IS.

12. Choose the odd one out:
(a) A1B (b) E5F (c) G8H (d) C3D
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to check only that a number sits between two letters, which all four share. In A1B, C3D, and E5F the number equals the position of the first letter (A=1, C=3, E=5), but G is the 7th letter, not 8, so the answer is G8H.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Choose the odd one out:

Solution: The trap is to check only that each pair mixes an early and a late letter, which all four do. In AZ, BY, and CX the two letters are mirror positions from opposite ends of the alphabet (1–26, 2–25, 3–24, each summing to 27), while D(4) and V(22) sum to 26, so the answer is DV.
