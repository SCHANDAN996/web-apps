Set 1: Classification MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Q1. Find the odd one: Apple, Mango, Carrot, Banana
(a) Apple (b) Mango (c) Carrot (d) Banana
Answer: (c) Carrot
Solution: Apple, mango and banana are fruits; a carrot is a vegetable.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. Find the odd one: 2, 4, 6, 7, 8
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (c) 7
Solution: 2, 4, 6, 8 are even; 7 is odd.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Find the odd one: Cricket, Hockey, Chess, Football
(a) Cricket (b) Hockey (c) Chess (d) Football
Answer: (c) Chess
Solution: Cricket, hockey and football are outdoor field sports; chess is an indoor board game.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Find the odd one: 5, 10, 15, 22, 25
(a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 22 (d) 25
Answer: (c) 22
Solution: 5, 10, 15, 25 are multiples of 5; 22 is not.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. Find the odd one: Delhi, Mumbai, Nepal, Chennai
(a) Delhi (b) Mumbai (c) Nepal (d) Chennai

Solution: Delhi, Mumbai and Chennai are cities; Nepal is a country.

Q6. Find the odd one: Cobra, Python, Lizard, Whale

Q7. Find the odd one: 16, 25, 36, 49, 50

Q21. (Medium) Find the odd one: ABC, DEF, GHI, JLM
(a) ABC (b) DEF (c) GHI (d) JLM
Answer: (d) JLM
Solution: ABC, DEF, GHI are three consecutive letters; JLM skips K (J, L, M). Odd = JLM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) Find the odd one: PR, MO, JL, GH
(a) PR (b) MO (c) JL (d) GH
Answer: (d) GH
Solution: PR, MO, JL each have a gap of 2 (one letter skipped); GH has a gap of 1. Odd = GH.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q23. (Medium) Find the odd one: 21, 33, 45, 53, 57
(a) 33 (b) 45 (c) 53 (d) 57
Answer: (c) 53
Solution: 21, 33, 45, 57 are divisible by 3; 53 is a prime number. Odd = 53.
Source: SSC CGL

Q24. (Medium) Find the odd one: January, March, June, August
(a) January (b) March (c) June (d) August
Answer: (c) June
Solution: January, March and August have 31 days; June has 30 days.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q25. (Medium) Find the odd one: 5, 7, 11, 15, 13
(a) 7 (b) 11 (c) 15 (d) 13
Answer: (c) 15
Solution: 5, 7, 11, 13 are prime; 15 = 3×5 is composite.
Source: SSC CGL
