101. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 225 (b) 256 (c) 300 (d) 289
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that 300 looks like a round, special number, but it fails the square test. 225 = 15², 256 = 16², and 289 = 17² are all perfect squares, while 300 is not the square of any whole number, so the answer is 300.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Choose the odd one out:
(a) Apple (b) Banana (c) Tomato (d) Orange
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming tomato belongs with fruits because it grows on a plant like the others. Botanically tomato is a fruit, but in everyday and culinary classification it is treated as a vegetable, while apple, banana, and orange are eaten as sweet fruits, so the answer is Tomato.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 17 (b) 27 (c) 19 (d) 23
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that 27 is odd like the others, so it blends in unless you test divisibility by 3. 17, 19, and 23 are prime numbers, whereas 27 = 3 × 9 is composite, so the answer is 27.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Choose the odd one out:
(a) Ganga (b) Narmada (c) Yamuna (d) Godavari
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that all four are major Indian rivers, so direction of flow is the deciding clue. Ganga, Yamuna, and Godavari are east-flowing rivers that drain into the Bay of Bengal, while Narmada flows westward into the Arabian Sea, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

118. Choose the odd one out:
(a) (9, 80) (b) (7, 50) (c) (11, 122) (d) (13, 170)
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that 80 is a round number that looks plausible next to a square. In the other three pairs the second number is the square of the first plus 1: 7²+1 = 50, 11²+1 = 122, 13²+1 = 170, but 9²+1 = 82, not 80, so the answer is the pair (9, 80).
Source: PYQ-style
