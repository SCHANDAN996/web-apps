51. Choose the odd one out:
(a) HS (b) GT (c) EV (d) IQ
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is EV because E and V look unrelated, but in GT, HS and EV the two letters are opposite letters whose positions add to 27 (G=7, T=20; H=8, S=19; E=5, V=22). IQ gives 9+17=26, breaking the rule, so the answer is IQ.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 100 (b) 8 (c) 27 (d) 64
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is 8 because it is the smallest, but 8 = 2³, 27 = 3³ and 64 = 4³ are perfect cubes, while 100 is only a perfect square, not a cube. So the answer is 100.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

54. Choose the odd one out:

Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option is B2D because the number looks too small, but in B2D, E5G and H8J the middle number equals the position of the first letter (B=2, E=5, H=8) and the last letter is two places ahead. K is the 11th letter, not the 12th, so the answer is K12M.

55. Choose the odd one out:
(a) (12, 36) (b) (8, 24) (c) (18, 56) (d) (15, 45)
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option is (8, 24) because the numbers are smallest, but in the other three pairs the second number is exactly three times the first (12×3=36, 15×3=45, 8×3=24). Since 18×3=54, not 56, the answer is (18, 56).

64. Choose the odd one out:
(a) 5, 25, 125 (b) 3, 9, 27 (c) 6, 36, 212 (d) 4, 16, 64
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option is 3, 9, 27 because the numbers are smallest, but in the other triples the second number is the square and the third is the cube of the first (3²=9, 3³=27; 5²=25, 5³=125; 4²=16, 4³=64). Since 6³=216, not 212, the answer is 6, 36, 212.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

72. Choose the odd one out:

Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is 1001 because it is the only four-digit number, which is exactly the trap — the real test is cubes: 343 = 7³, 512 = 8³ and 729 = 9³ are perfect cubes, while 1001 = 7 × 11 × 13 is not. So the answer is 1001.
