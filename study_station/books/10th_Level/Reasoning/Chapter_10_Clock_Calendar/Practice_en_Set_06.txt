Set 6: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English)
Questions 126–150: Hard

Q126. (Hard) At what time between 9 and 10 o'clock will the hands of a clock be symmetric about the vertical line passing through 12 and 6?
(a) 9:13 11/13 (b) 9:15 1/13 (c) 9:18 2/13 (d) 9:21 9/13
Answer: (a) 9:13 11/13
Solution: Symmetry condition about 12-6: Minute angle = 360° - Hour angle. 6M = 360 - (270 + 0.5M) => 6.5M = 90 => M = 180/13 = 13 11/13 minutes. Time = 9:13 11/13.
Source: SSC CGL

Q127. (Hard) A clock gains 6 minutes in every 12 hours of true time. It was set right at 12:00 noon on Monday. When it indicates 1:00 PM on Saturday, what is the actual true time?
(a) 1:00 PM Saturday (b) 12:00 noon Saturday (c) 12:30 PM Saturday (d) 11:00 AM Saturday
Answer: (b) 12:00 noon Saturday
Solution: Faulty clock rate = 121 / 120. From Mon noon to Sat 1 PM is 121 clock hours. True hours = 121 * (120/121) = 120 hours. Monday noon + 120 hours = Saturday 12:00 noon.
Source: SSC CGL

Q128. (Hard) What is the smaller angle between the hands of a clock at 12:30?
(a) 180° (b) 195° (c) 165° (d) 150°
Answer: (c) 165°
Solution: |30(12) - 5.5(30)| = |360 - 165| = 195°. Smaller angle = 360° - 195° = 165°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q129. (Hard) At what times between 6 and 7 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 20 minutes apart?
(a) 6:12 4/11 and 6:52 8/11 (b) 6:15 and 6:50 (c) 6:18 2/11 and 6:48 (d) 6:10 10/11 and 6:54 6/11
Answer: (d) 6:10 10/11 and 6:54 6/11
Solution: 20 minutes apart = 120°: |180 - 5.5M| = 120 => 5.5M = 60 => M = 120/11 = 10 10/11 min, and 5.5M = 300 => M = 600/11 = 54 6/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q130. (Hard) A clock is set right at 8:00 AM on Sunday. It loses 2 minutes in the first hour and gains 4 minutes in the second hour, repeating this pattern. What time will it show at 8:00 AM on Monday?
(a) 8:24 AM (b) 7:36 AM (c) 8:12 AM (d) 8:00 AM
Answer: (a) 8:24 AM
Solution: In every 2-hour cycle, net gain = -2 + 4 = +2 minutes. In 24 hours, there are 12 cycles. Total gain = 12 * 2 = 24 minutes. Time shown = 8:24 AM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q131. (Hard) What day of the week was 2nd October 1869 (the birth date of Mahatma Gandhi)?
(a) Sunday (b) Saturday (c) Friday (d) Monday
Answer: (b) Saturday
Solution: Date = 2, Oct code = 0, Century code (1800s) = 2, Year = 69, Leap = 17. Total = 2 + 0 + 2 + 69 + 17 = 90 mod 7 = 6 = Saturday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q132. (Hard) A clock shows 11:45. What is the time shown in its water image?
(a) 12:15 (b) 12:45 (c) 6:45 (d) 11:15
Answer: (c) 6:45
Solution: Water image time = 18:30 - 11:45 = 17:90 - 11:45 = 6:45.
Source: SSC MTS

Q133. (Hard) At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock will the hands of a clock form an angle of 10° for the first time?
(a) 2:12 8/11 (b) 2:10 10/11 (c) 2:14 6/11 (d) 2:09 1/11
Answer: (d) 2:09 1/11
Solution: |60 - 5.5M| = 10 => 60 - 5.5M = 10 => 5.5M = 50 => M = 100/11 = 9 1/11 minutes. Time = 2:09 1/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q134. (Hard) A clock loses 1% of its time each day. If set right at 12 noon on Sunday, what is the approximate true time when the clock indicates 12 noon on the next Sunday?
(a) 1:41 PM (b) 12:36 PM (c) 1:12 PM (d) 12:50 PM
Answer: (a) 1:41 PM
Solution: Clock elapsed = 7 days = 168 hours. True hours = 168 / 0.99 ≈ 169.697 hours = 168 hours + 1 hr 41 min 48 sec. True time ≈ 1:41 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q135. (Hard) At what times between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 15 minutes apart?
(a) 4:10 and 4:40 (b) 4:05 5/11 and 4:38 2/11 (c) 4:06 6/11 and 4:36 4/11 (d) 4:15 and 4:45
Answer: (b) 4:05 5/11 and 4:38 2/11
Solution: 15 minutes apart = 90°: |120 - 5.5M| = 90 => 5.5M = 30 => M = 60/11 = 5 5/11 min, and 5.5M = 210 => M = 420/11 = 38 2/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q136. (Hard) A clock is set right at 6:00 AM. It loses 1 minute in the 1st hour, 2 minutes in the 2nd hour, 3 minutes in the 3rd hour, and so on. What time will it show at 6:00 PM on the same day?
(a) 4:48 PM (b) 5:12 PM (c) 4:42 PM (d) 5:24 PM
Answer: (c) 4:42 PM
Solution: Total loss in 12 hours = 1 + 2 + ... + 12 = 78 minutes = 1 hour 18 minutes. Time shown = 6:00 PM - 1 hr 18 min = 4:42 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q137. (Hard) A clock takes 6 seconds to strike 4 o'clock. How many seconds will it take to strike 12 o'clock?
(a) 18 seconds (b) 20 seconds (c) 24 seconds (d) 22 seconds
Answer: (d) 22 seconds
Solution: To strike 4, there are 3 intervals = 6 seconds => 1 interval = 2 seconds. To strike 12, there are 11 intervals = 11 * 2 = 22 seconds.
Source: SSC MTS

Q138. (Hard) If 15th August 1947 was Friday, what day of the week was 15th August 1948?
(a) Sunday (b) Saturday (c) Monday (d) Friday
Answer: (a) Sunday
Solution: The period includes 29th February 1948 (a leap year). Total days = 366 = 2 odd days. Friday + 2 days = Sunday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q139. (Hard) A clock gains 12 minutes every day. If set right at 12 noon on Monday, after how many days will it show the correct time again?
(a) 30 days (b) 60 days (c) 90 days (d) 120 days
Answer: (b) 60 days
Solution: To show correct time again, it must gain 12 hours = 720 minutes. Days = 720 / 12 = 60 days.
Source: SSC CGL

Q140. (Hard) At what times between 6 and 7 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 18 minutes apart?
(a) 6:10 10/11 and 6:54 6/11 (b) 6:15 and 6:50 (c) 6:13 1/11 and 6:52 4/11 (d) 6:18 and 6:48
Answer: (c) 6:13 1/11 and 6:52 4/11
Solution: 18 minutes apart = 108°: |180 - 5.5M| = 108 => 5.5M = 72 => M = 144/11 = 13 1/11 min, and 5.5M = 288 => M = 576/11 = 52 4/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q141. (Hard) A clock gains 15 minutes per day. It is set right at 12 noon. After how many days will it show the correct time again?
(a) 24 days (b) 72 days (c) 36 days (d) 48 days
Answer: (d) 48 days
Solution: Days required = 720 / 15 = 48 days.
Source: SSC MTS

Q142. (Hard) At what time between 10 and 11 o'clock will the hands of a clock be in a straight line but not pointing in the same direction?
(a) 10:21 9/11 (b) 10:38 2/11 (c) 10:54 6/11 (d) 10:43 7/11
Answer: (a) 10:21 9/11
Solution: |300 - 5.5M| = 180 => 300 - 5.5M = 180 => 5.5M = 120 => M = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes. Time = 10:21 9/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q143. (Hard) Which year after 2004 will have the exact same calendar as 2004?
(a) 2012 (b) 2032 (c) 2020 (d) 2048
Answer: (b) 2032
Solution: The calendar of a leap year repeats after 28 years. 2004 + 28 = 2032.
Source: RRB Group D

Q144. (Hard) A clock is set right at 1:00 PM on Sunday. It gains 2 minutes per hour. What time will it show at 1:00 PM on Tuesday?
(a) 2:30 PM (b) 2:40 PM (c) 2:36 PM (d) 2:24 PM
Answer: (c) 2:36 PM
Solution: From Sunday 1 PM to Tuesday 1 PM = 48 hours. Total gain = 48 * 2 = 96 minutes = 1 hour 36 minutes. Time shown = 1:00 PM + 1 hr 36 min = 2:36 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q145. (Hard) How many leap years are there between 1801 and 1900 (both inclusive)?
(a) 25 (b) 26 (c) 23 (d) 24
Answer: (d) 24
Solution: Leap years run from 1804 to 1896: (1896 - 1804)/4 + 1 = 24. Year 1900 is a century year not divisible by 400, so it is not a leap year.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q146. (Hard) A clock gains 15 minutes per hour. It was set right at 12:00 noon. What is the actual true time when the clock indicates 6:00 PM on the same day?
(a) 4:48 PM (b) 5:00 PM (c) 5:12 PM (d) 5:24 PM
Answer: (a) 4:48 PM
Solution: The clock covers 75 minutes for every 60 true minutes (ratio 5 : 4). True elapsed time = 6 * (4/5) = 4.8 hours = 4 hours 48 minutes. True time = 12 noon + 4 hr 48 min = 4:48 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q147. (Hard) At what time between 12 and 1 o'clock will the hands of a clock form an angle of 110° for the first time?
(a) 12:22 8/11 (b) 12:20 (c) 12:24 (d) 12:25 5/11
Answer: (b) 12:20
Solution: Using H = 0: |0 - 5.5M| = 110 => 5.5M = 110 => M = 20 minutes. Time = 12:20.
Source: SSC CGL

Q148. (Hard) A clock takes 5 seconds to strike 5 o'clock. How many seconds will it take to strike 10 o'clock?
(a) 10 seconds (b) 12.5 seconds (c) 11.25 seconds (d) 9 seconds
Answer: (c) 11.25 seconds
Solution: To strike 5, there are 4 intervals = 5 seconds => 1 interval = 1.25 seconds. To strike 10, there are 9 intervals = 9 * 1.25 = 11.25 seconds.
Source: SSC CGL

Q149. (Hard) If the day before yesterday was Friday, what day of the week will it be 10 days from today?
(a) Thursday (b) Friday (c) Saturday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (d) Wednesday
Solution: Day before yesterday = Friday => Yesterday = Saturday => Today = Sunday. 10 days from today: 10 mod 7 = 3 odd days. Sunday + 3 days = Wednesday.
Source: RRB Group D

Q150. (Hard) A clock gains 18 minutes every day. It was set right at 12:00 noon on Monday. What is the true time when the clock indicates 6:00 PM on Wednesday?
(a) 5:30 PM (b) 5:20 PM (c) 5:40 PM (d) 5:10 PM
Answer: (b) 5:20 PM
Solution: Clock elapsed time = 54 hours. Ratio True : Faulty = 1440 : 1458 = 80 : 81. True hours = 54 * (80/81) = 53 hours 20 minutes. Monday noon + 53 hr 20 min = Wednesday 5:20 PM.
Source: SSC CGL
