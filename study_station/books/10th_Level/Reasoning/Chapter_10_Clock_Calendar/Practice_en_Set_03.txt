Set 3: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English)
Questions 51–75: Medium

Q51. (Medium) At what time between 1 and 2 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 1:21 9/11 (b) 1:05 5/11 (c) 1:38 2/11 (d) 1:54 6/11
Answer: (a) 1:21 9/11
Solution: Condition |30(1) - 5.5M| = 90 => 5.5M - 30 = 90 => 5.5M = 120 => M = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes. Time = 1:21 9/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q52. (Medium) A clock is set right at 12 noon on Monday. It loses 2 minutes per hour. What is the true time when the clock indicates 6:00 PM on Tuesday?
(a) 7:00 PM (b) 7:02 PM (c) 6:48 PM (d) 7:24 PM
Answer: (b) 7:02 PM
Solution: Clock shows 30 hours elapsed. True time to clock time ratio = 60 : 58. True minutes = 30 * 60 * (60/58) ≈ 1862.07 min = 31 hr 2 min. True time = 7:02 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q53. (Medium) A watch shows 8:30. If the minute hand points towards North-East, in which direction does the hour hand point?
(a) North (b) South (c) South-East (d) East
Answer: (c) South-East
Solution: At 8:30, minute hand is at 180° and hour hand is at 255° (75° clockwise ahead). If minute hand points NE (45°), hour hand points 45° + 75° = 120°, which is South-East.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q54. (Medium) What day of the week was 2nd October 2005?
(a) Monday (b) Saturday (c) Tuesday (d) Sunday
Answer: (d) Sunday
Solution: Date = 2, Oct code = 0, Century code (2000s) = 6, Year = 5, Leap = 1. Total = 2 + 0 + 6 + 5 + 1 = 14 mod 7 = 0 = Sunday.
Source: SSC MTS

Q55. (Medium) At what times between 9 and 10 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 5 minutes apart?
(a) 9:43 7/11 and 9:54 6/11 (b) 9:38 2/11 and 9:49 1/11 (c) 9:44 and 9:56 (d) 9:40 5/11 and 9:52 3/11
Answer: (a) 9:43 7/11 and 9:54 6/11
Solution: 5 minutes apart = 30°: |270 - 5.5M| = 30 => 5.5M = 240 => M = 480/11 = 43 7/11 min, and 5.5M = 300 => M = 600/11 = 54 6/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q56. (Medium) A clock gains 15 minutes per day. If it is set right at 12 noon, what is the true time when it indicates 12 noon the next day?
(a) 11:50 AM (b) 11:45 AM (c) 11:40 AM (d) 11:30 AM
Answer: (b) 11:45 AM
Solution: In 24 hours, the clock gains 15 minutes. When the clock completes 24 hours, the true elapsed time is 24 hours minus 15 minutes = 23 hours 45 minutes, i.e., 11:45 AM.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q57. (Medium) If 1st January 2005 was Saturday, what day of the week was 1st January 2006?
(a) Saturday (b) Monday (c) Sunday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (c) Sunday
Solution: 2005 is an ordinary year with 365 days = 1 odd day. Saturday + 1 day = Sunday.
Source: RRB Group D

Q58. (Medium) At what times between 7 and 8 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles?
(a) 7:20 and 7:55 (b) 7:16 4/11 and 7:49 1/11 (c) 7:25 and 7:50 (d) 7:21 9/11 and 7:54 6/11
Answer: (d) 7:21 9/11 and 7:54 6/11
Solution: |210 - 5.5M| = 90 => 210 - 5.5M = 90 => 5.5M = 120 => M = 21 9/11 min, and 5.5M - 210 = 90 => 5.5M = 300 => M = 54 6/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q59. (Medium) What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 10:10?
(a) 115° (b) 120° (c) 130° (d) 110°
Answer: (a) 115°
Solution: |30(10) - 5.5(10)| = |300 - 55| = 245°. Reflex = 245°, smaller angle = 360° - 245° = 115°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q60. (Medium) A clock is set right at 5:00 AM on Sunday. It loses 2 minutes per hour for the first 12 hours, and then gains 1 minute per hour for the next 12 hours. What time will it show at 5:00 AM on Monday?
(a) 5:12 AM (b) 4:48 AM (c) 5:00 AM (d) 4:36 AM
Answer: (b) 4:48 AM
Solution: First 12 hours loss = 12 * 2 = 24 min. Next 12 hours gain = 12 * 1 = 12 min. Net loss = 24 - 12 = 12 minutes. Time shown = 5:00 AM - 12 min = 4:48 AM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q61. (Medium) If 15th August 2022 was Monday, what day of the week will 15th August 2025 be?
(a) Thursday (b) Wednesday (c) Friday (d) Saturday
Answer: (c) Friday
Solution: From 15 Aug 2022 to 15 Aug 2025 is 3 years. Leap year 2024 has 29 Feb in this period, so odd days = 1 + 2 + 1 = 4. Monday + 4 days = Friday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q62. (Medium) At what time between 5 and 6 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the second time?
(a) 5:38 2/11 (b) 5:41 8/11 (c) 5:46 4/11 (d) 5:43 7/11
Answer: (d) 5:43 7/11
Solution: Second right angle: 5.5M - 30(5) = 90 => 5.5M = 240 => M = 480/11 = 43 7/11 minutes. Time = 5:43 7/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q63. (Medium) What is the water image time of a clock showing 6:15?
(a) 12:15 (b) 5:45 (c) 6:45 (d) 12:45
Answer: (a) 12:15
Solution: Water image time = 18:30 - 6:15 = 12:15.
Source: SSC MTS

Q64. (Medium) If today is Wednesday, what day was it 100 days ago?
(a) Wednesday (b) Monday (c) Thursday (d) Friday
Answer: (b) Monday
Solution: 100 mod 7 = 2 odd days. Wednesday - 2 days = Monday.
Source: RRB Group D

Q65. (Medium) A clock strikes once at 1, twice at 2, and so on, and also strikes once at every half hour. How many times will it strike between 12 noon and 12 midnight inclusive?
(a) 90 (b) 78 (c) 102 (d) 89
Answer: (c) 102
Solution: Strikes at 12 noon (12) + strikes from 1 to 12 midnight (78) = 90 hourly strikes. Half-hour strikes: 12:30 to 11:30 = 12 strikes. Total = 90 + 12 = 102 strikes.
Source: SSC CGL

Q66. (Medium) At what time between 6 and 7 o'clock will the hands of a clock be pointing in opposite directions?
(a) 6:54 6/11 (b) 6:32 8/11 (c) 6:48 (d) 6:00
Answer: (d) 6:00
Solution: At exactly 6:00, the angle between the hands is 180°. Hence, the hands are opposite at 6:00 on the boundary.
Source: SSC MTS

Q67. (Medium) What day of the week was 26th January 1950?
(a) Thursday (b) Friday (c) Wednesday (d) Saturday
Answer: (a) Thursday
Solution: Date = 26, Jan code = 0, Century code (1900s) = 0, Year = 50, Leap = 12. Total = 26 + 0 + 0 + 50 + 12 = 88 mod 7 = 4 = Thursday.
Source: SSC CGL

Q68. (Medium) A watch loses 5 minutes per day. If it shows the correct time at 12 noon today, after how many days will it show the correct time again?
(a) 288 days (b) 144 days (c) 72 days (d) 120 days
Answer: (b) 144 days
Solution: To show the correct time again, the clock must lose 12 hours = 720 minutes. Number of days = 720 / 5 = 144 days.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q69. (Medium) At what time between 12 and 1 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 12:15 (b) 12:18 (c) 12:16 4/11 (d) 12:10 10/11
Answer: (c) 12:16 4/11
Solution: Using H = 0: |0 - 5.5M| = 90 => 5.5M = 90 => M = 180/11 = 16 4/11 minutes. Time = 12:16 4/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q70. (Medium) If 4th April 2023 was Tuesday, what day of the week was 4th April 2022?
(a) Sunday (b) Saturday (c) Tuesday (d) Monday
Answer: (d) Monday
Solution: From 4 April 2022 to 4 April 2023 is 365 days = 1 odd day. Tuesday - 1 day = Monday.
Source: RRB Group D

Q71. (Medium) At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock will the hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 2:10 10/11 (b) 2:11 9/11 (c) 2:10 (d) 2:09 3/11
Answer: (a) 2:10 10/11
Solution: Coincidence formula: M = 60H/11 = 60(2)/11 = 120/11 = 10 10/11 minutes. Time = 2:10 10/11.
Source: SSC MTS

Q72. (Medium) A clock is set right at 6:00 AM on Monday. It gains 8 minutes per day. What is the true time when the clock indicates 6:00 AM on Wednesday?
(a) 5:36 AM (b) 5:44 AM (c) 5:52 AM (d) 5:48 AM
Answer: (b) 5:44 AM
Solution: In 2 days (48 hours), the clock gains 2 * 8 = 16 minutes. True time = 6:00 AM - 16 min = 5:44 AM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q73. (Medium) If the hands of a clock are 15 minute spaces apart, what is the angle between them?
(a) 60° (b) 75° (c) 90° (d) 30°
Answer: (c) 90°
Solution: 1 minute space = 360° / 60 = 6°. Therefore, 15 minute spaces = 15 * 6° = 90°.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q74. (Medium) What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 4:40?
(a) 80° (b) 120° (c) 110° (d) 100°
Answer: (d) 100°
Solution: Angle = |30(4) - 5.5(40)| = |120 - 220| = 100°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q75. (Medium) After how many years does the calendar of a leap year repeat?
(a) 11 years (b) 28 years (c) 6 years (d) 12 years
Answer: (b) 28 years
Solution: The calendar of a leap year repeats exactly after 28 years.
Source: RRB Group D
