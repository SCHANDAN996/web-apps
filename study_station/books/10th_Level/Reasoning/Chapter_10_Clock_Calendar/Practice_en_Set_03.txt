Set 3: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English) — Questions 51–75: Medium

51. At what time between 1 and 2 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 1:05 5/11 (b) 1:21 9/11 (c) 1:38 2/11 (d) 1:54 6/11
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting shortcut of adding 15 minutes to 1:05 gives a wrong result because the hour hand also moves. Using |30(1) − 5.5M| = 90 gives 5.5M = 120, so M = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes, so the answer is 1:21 9/11.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A clock is set right at 12 noon on Monday. It loses 2 minutes per hour. What is the true time when the clock indicates 6:00 PM on Tuesday?
(a) 7:02 PM (b) 7:00 PM (c) 6:48 PM (d) 7:24 PM
Answer: (a)
Solution: Simply adding 30 × 2 = 60 minutes to get 7:00 PM is the trap, because the clock loses time continuously on a growing interval. The clock shows 30 hours while true hours are 30 × 60/58 ≈ 31 hours 2 minutes, so the answer is 7:02 PM.
Source: PYQ-style

53. A watch shows 8:30. If the minute hand points towards North-East, in which direction does the hour hand point?
(a) South-East (b) South (c) North (d) East
Answer: (a)
Solution: Assuming the hour hand points exactly at 8 is the trap; at 8:30 it has moved halfway towards 9. The hour hand is 75° clockwise from the minute hand, so 45° + 75° = 120°, which is South-East, so the answer is South-East.
Source: PYQ-style

54. What day of the week was 2nd October 2005?
(a) Monday (b) Saturday (c) Sunday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: Miscounting leap years between 2000 and 2005 is the usual trap; only 2004 is a leap year. Odd days from 1 Jan 2000 (Saturday) to 2 Oct 2005 total 1, giving Saturday + 1, so the answer is Sunday.
Source: PYQ-style

55. At what times between 9 and 10 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 5 minutes apart?
(a) 9:40 5/11 and 9:52 3/11 (b) 9:38 2/11 and 9:49 1/11 (c) 9:44 and 9:56 (d) 9:43 7/11 and 9:54 6/11
Answer: (d)
Solution: Taking 5 minutes apart as 5° instead of 30° is the trap; each minute space is 6°. Solving |270 − 5.5M| = 30 gives M = 480/11 and M = 600/11, so the answer is 9:43 7/11 and 9:54 6/11.
Source: PYQ-style

56. A clock gains 15 minutes per day. If it is set right at 12 noon, what is the true time when it indicates 12 noon the next day?
(a) 11:50 AM (b) 11:30 AM (c) 11:40 AM (d) 11:45 AM
Answer: (d)
Solution: Subtracting a full 15 minutes from noon is close but slightly off; the exact ratio gives 24 × 1440/1455 hours ≈ 23 hours 45 minutes. The true elapsed time is about 23 hours 45 minutes, so the answer is 11:45 AM.
Source: PYQ-style

57. If 1st January 2005 was Saturday, what day of the week was 1st January 2006?
(a) Saturday (b) Sunday (c) Monday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (b)
Solution: Treating 2005 as a leap year is the trap; 2005 is an ordinary year with 365 days = 1 odd day. Saturday + 1 day, so the answer is Sunday.
Source: PYQ-style

58. At what times between 7 and 8 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles?
(a) 7:20 and 7:55 (b) 7:21 9/11 and 7:54 6/11 (c) 7:25 and 7:50 (d) 7:16 4/11 and 7:49 1/11
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rounding to whole minutes like 7:20 and 7:55 is the trap; exact answers come in elevenths. Solving |210 − 5.5M| = 90 gives M = 120/5.5 = 21 9/11 and M = 300/5.5 = 54 6/11, so the answer is 7:21 9/11 and 7:54 6/11.
Source: PYQ-style

59. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 10:10?
(a) 115° (b) 120° (c) 130° (d) 110°
Answer: (a)
Solution: Computing |300 − 55| = 245° and stopping there is the trap; the question wants the smaller angle. The smaller angle is 360° − 245° = 115°, so the answer is 115°.
Source: PYQ-style

60. A clock is set right at 5:00 AM on Sunday. It loses 2 minutes per hour for the first 12 hours, and then gains 1 minute per hour for the next 12 hours. What time will it show at 5:00 AM on Monday?
(a) 5:12 AM (b) 4:36 AM (c) 5:00 AM (d) 4:48 AM
Answer: (d)
Solution: Assuming the loss and gain cancel to zero is the trap; the rates differ. Net change = −24 + 12 = −12 minutes, so the clock shows 5:00 AM minus 12 minutes, so the answer is 4:48 AM.
Source: PYQ-style

61. If 15th August 2022 was Monday, what day of the week will 15th August 2025 be?
(a) Friday (b) Wednesday (c) Thursday (d) Saturday
Answer: (a)
Solution: Counting 3 odd days for 3 years is the trap; the leap year 2024 contributes 2 odd days. Total odd days = 1 + 2 + 1 = 4, and Monday + 4 days, so the answer is Friday.
Source: PYQ-style

62. At what time between 5 and 6 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the second time?
(a) 5:38 2/11 (b) 5:43 7/11 (c) 5:46 4/11 (d) 5:41 8/11
Answer: (b)
Solution: Solving 150 − 5.5M = 90 gives the first right angle (5:10 10/11), which is not asked. For the second, 5.5M − 150 = 90 gives M = 240/5.5 = 480/11 = 43 7/11 minutes, so the answer is 5:43 7/11.
Source: PYQ-style

63. What is the mirror image time of a clock showing 4:30?
(a) 8:30 (b) 7:30 (c) 6:30 (d) 7:00
Answer: (b)
Solution: Subtracting from 12:00 instead of 11:60 is the trap, which wrongly gives 8:30. Mirror image = 11:60 − 4:30 = 7:30, so the answer is 7:30.
Source: PYQ-style

64. If today is Wednesday, what day was it 100 days ago?
(a) Wednesday (b) Thursday (c) Monday (d) Friday
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding 2 odd days instead of subtracting is the trap, since the question asks about the past. 100 mod 7 = 2, and Wednesday − 2 days, so the answer is Monday.
Source: PYQ-style

65. A clock strikes once at 1, twice at 2, and so on, and also strikes once at every half hour. How many times will it strike between 12 noon and 12 midnight inclusive?
(a) 102 (b) 78 (c) 90 (d) 89
Answer: (a)
Solution: Forgetting the 12 half-hour strikes gives 90, which is the trap. Hourly strikes = 12 + (1 + 2 + … + 12) = 12 + 78 = 90, plus 12 half-hour strikes, so the answer is 102.
Source: PYQ-style

66. At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock point in opposite directions?
(a) 4:49 1/11 (b) 4:43 7/11 (c) 4:50 (d) 4:54 6/11
Answer: (d)
Solution: Setting the angle to 90° instead of 180° is the trap and gives 4:43 7/11. Solving 5.5M − 120 = 180 gives M = 300/5.5 = 54 6/11 minutes, so the answer is 4:54 6/11.
Source: PYQ-style

67. What day of the week was 26th January 1950?
(a) Wednesday (b) Friday (c) Thursday (d) Saturday
Answer: (c)
Solution: Using the wrong century code for the 1900s is the trap; the 1900s code is 0. Total = 26 + 0 (Jan) + 0 (century) + 50 + 12 (leaps) = 88, and 88 mod 7 = 4, so the answer is Thursday.
Source: PYQ-style

68. A watch loses 5 minutes per day. If it shows the correct time at 12 noon today, after how many days will it show the correct time again?
(a) 288 days (b) 72 days (c) 144 days (d) 120 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Using 24 hours (1440 minutes) instead of 12 hours is the trap; a clock repeats after losing 12 hours = 720 minutes. Days = 720 / 5, so the answer is 144 days.
Source: PYQ-style

69. At what time between 12 and 1 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 12:15 (b) 12:18 (c) 12:10 10/11 (d) 12:16 4/11
Answer: (d)
Solution: Guessing 12:15 is the trap; the minute hand must gain 90° on the hour hand at relative speed 5.5° per minute. M = 90/5.5 = 180/11 = 16 4/11 minutes, so the answer is 12:16 4/11.
Source: PYQ-style

70. If 4th April 2023 was Tuesday, what day of the week was 4th April 2022?
(a) Sunday (b) Saturday (c) Monday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding one odd day instead of subtracting is the trap when moving backwards in time. The gap is 365 days = 1 odd day, and Tuesday − 1 day, so the answer is Monday.
Source: PYQ-style

71. At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock will the hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 2:11 9/11 (b) 2:10 10/11 (c) 2:10 (d) 2:09 3/11
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rounding to 2:10 is the trap; the exact value is a fraction in elevenths. M = 60H/11 = 120/11 = 10 10/11 minutes, so the answer is 2:10 10/11.
Source: PYQ-style

72. A clock is set right at 6:00 AM on Monday. It gains 8 minutes per day. What is the true time when the clock indicates 6:00 AM on Wednesday?
(a) 5:44 AM (b) 5:36 AM (c) 5:52 AM (d) 5:48 AM
Answer: (a)
Solution: Subtracting exactly 16 minutes is a slight approximation but the exact ratio 48 × 1440/1448 ≈ 47 hours 44 minutes confirms it. True time ≈ 6:00 AM minus 16 minutes, so the answer is 5:44 AM.
Source: PYQ-style

73. If the hands of a clock are 15 minute spaces apart, what is the angle between them?
(a) 60° (b) 75° (c) 30° (d) 90°
Answer: (d)
Solution: Multiplying 15 by 5° (confusing hour spaces with minute spaces) is the trap. Each minute space is 360°/60 = 6°, so 15 × 6° = 90°, so the answer is 90°.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

74. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 4:40?
(a) 80° (b) 120° (c) 100° (d) 110°
Answer: (c)
Solution: Taking the hour hand as fixed at 4 (120°) and ignoring its movement gives 120°, which is the trap. Angle = |30(4) − 5.5(40)| = |120 − 220| = 100°, so the answer is 100°.
Source: PYQ-style

75. After how many years does the calendar of a leap year repeat?
(a) 28 years (b) 11 years (c) 6 years (d) 12 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: Applying the ordinary-year rules of 6 or 11 years is the trap; those do not apply to leap years. A leap year's calendar repeats exactly after 28 years, so the answer is 28 years.
Source: PYQ-style
