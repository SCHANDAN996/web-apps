Set 4: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English)
Questions 76–100: Medium

Q76. (Medium) At what time between 3 and 4 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the second time?
(a) 3:32 8/11 (b) 3:27 3/11 (c) 3:16 4/11 (d) 3:49 1/11
Answer: (a) 3:32 8/11
Solution: At 3:00 hands are at 90°. Between 3 and 4, the hands form a right angle when 5.5M - 90 = 90 => 5.5M = 180 => M = 360/11 = 32 8/11 minutes. Time = 3:32 8/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q77. (Medium) A watch loses 2 minutes per hour. If it shows the correct time at 12 noon, what is the true time when the watch shows 6:00 PM on the same day?
(a) 6:10 PM (b) 6:12 PM (c) 6:08 PM (d) 6:15 PM
Answer: (b) 6:12 PM
Solution: Clock shows 6 hours elapsed. True time to clock time ratio = 60 : 58 = 30 : 29. True elapsed minutes = 6 * 60 * (30/29) ≈ 372.4 minutes ≈ 6 hours 12 minutes. True time = 6:12 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q78. (Medium) A clock shows 3:40. What is the time shown in its mirror image?
(a) 2:50 (b) 7:20 (c) 8:20 (d) 10:20
Answer: (c) 8:20
Solution: Mirror image time = 11:60 - 3:40 = 8:20.
Source: SSC MTS

Q79. (Medium) If 10th May 2023 was Wednesday, what day of the week was 10th May 2024?
(a) Thursday (b) Wednesday (c) Saturday (d) Friday
Answer: (d) Friday
Solution: From 10 May 2023 to 10 May 2024 includes 29 Feb 2024 (a leap year). Total days = 366 = 2 odd days. Wednesday + 2 days = Friday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q80. (Medium) At what times between 11 and 12 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles?
(a) 11:10 10/11 and 11:43 7/11 (b) 11:05 5/11 and 11:38 2/11 (c) 11:16 4/11 and 11:49 1/11 (d) 11:21 9/11 and 11:54 6/11
Answer: (a) 11:10 10/11 and 11:43 7/11
Solution: |330 - 5.5M| = 90 => 330 - 5.5M = 90 => M = 240/5.5 = 43 7/11 min, and 330 - 5.5M = 270 (reflex 90°) => 5.5M = 60 => M = 10 10/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q81. (Medium) A clock gains 10 minutes per day. If set right at 12 noon on Monday, what is the true time when the clock indicates 12 noon on Thursday?
(a) 11:40 AM (b) 11:30 AM (c) 11:20 AM (d) 11:50 AM
Answer: (b) 11:30 AM
Solution: In 3 days (72 hours), the clock gains 3 * 10 = 30 minutes. True time = 12 noon - 30 minutes = 11:30 AM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q82. (Medium) If 1st January 2024 was Monday, what day of the week was 31st December 2024?
(a) Monday (b) Wednesday (c) Tuesday (d) Thursday
Answer: (c) Tuesday
Solution: 2024 is a leap year (366 days). In a leap year, the last day of the year is 1 day ahead of the first day. Monday + 1 day = Tuesday.
Source: SSC MTS

Q83. (Medium) A clock shows 8:20. What will be its water image time?
(a) 2:40 (b) 3:40 (c) 9:40 (d) 10:10
Answer: (d) 10:10
Solution: Water image time = 18:30 - 8:20 = 10:10.
Source: SSC MTS

Q84. (Medium) At what time between 6 and 7 o'clock will the hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 6:32 8/11 (b) 6:38 2/11 (c) 6:27 3/11 (d) 6:30
Answer: (a) 6:32 8/11
Solution: Coincidence formula: M = 60H/11 = 60(6)/11 = 360/11 = 32 8/11 minutes. Time = 6:32 8/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q85. (Medium) What day of the week was 5th September 2014?
(a) Saturday (b) Friday (c) Sunday (d) Thursday
Answer: (b) Friday
Solution: Date = 5, Sep code = 5, Century code (2000s) = 6, Year = 14, Leap = 3. Total = 5 + 5 + 6 + 14 + 3 = 33 mod 7 = 5 = Friday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q86. (Medium) A clock is set right at 7:00 AM on Sunday. It gains 4 minutes in the first 3 hours and loses 2 minutes in the next 3 hours, repeating this cycle. What time will it show at 7:00 PM on Sunday?
(a) 7:08 PM (b) 7:02 PM (c) 7:04 PM (d) 7:10 PM
Answer: (c) 7:04 PM
Solution: From 7 AM to 7 PM = 12 hours = two 6-hour cycles. Net gain per 6 hours = 4 - 2 = 2 minutes. Total gain in 12 hours = 2 * 2 = 4 minutes. Time shown = 7:04 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q87. (Medium) At what times between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 18 minutes apart?
(a) 4:03 1/11 and 4:40 4/11 (b) 4:05 5/11 and 4:43 7/11 (c) 4:07 7/11 and 4:45 1/11 (d) 4:02 2/11 and 4:41 5/11
Answer: (d) 4:02 2/11 and 4:41 5/11
Solution: 18 minutes apart = 18 * 6° = 108°: |120 - 5.5M| = 108 => 5.5M = 12 => M = 24/11 = 2 2/11 min, and 5.5M = 228 => M = 456/11 = 41 5/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q88. (Medium) A clock shows 5:55. What is the time shown in its mirror image?
(a) 6:05 (b) 5:05 (c) 6:55 (d) 7:05
Answer: (a) 6:05
Solution: Mirror image time = 11:60 - 5:55 = 6:05.
Source: SSC MTS

Q89. (Medium) If 14th November 2022 was Monday, what day of the week was 14th November 2019?
(a) Friday (b) Thursday (c) Saturday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (b) Thursday
Solution: From 2019 to 2022 is 3 years including leap year 2020 (29 Feb included). Odd days = 2 + 1 + 1 = 4. Monday - 4 days = Thursday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q90. (Medium) A clock is set right at 12 noon. It gains 30 minutes every hour. How many times will it show the correct time in the next 24 hours?
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (c) 1
Solution: The clock gains 30 min per hour = 0.5 hour/hour. To gain 12 hours (showing correct time again), time required = 12 / 0.5 = 24 hours. Thus, it shows correct time exactly 1 time (at t = 24 hours).
Source: SSC CGL

Q91. (Medium) At what time between 3 and 4 o'clock will the hands of a clock be in a straight line but pointing in opposite directions?
(a) 3:16 4/11 (b) 3:21 9/11 (c) 3:27 3/11 (d) 3:49 1/11
Answer: (d) 3:49 1/11
Solution: Opposite condition: 5.5M - 30(3) = 180 => 5.5M = 270 => M = 540/11 = 49 1/11 minutes. Time = 3:49 1/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q92. (Medium) A clock strikes once at 1, twice at 2, and so on, and once at every half hour. How many times will it strike in 24 hours?
(a) 180 (b) 156 (c) 312 (d) 324
Answer: (a) 180
Solution: In 12 hours, strikes = (1 + ... + 12) + 12 = 78 + 12 = 90. In 24 hours, total strikes = 90 * 2 = 180 times.
Source: SSC MTS

Q93. (Medium) A watch gains 5 seconds in 2 minutes and was set right at 6:00 AM. What time will it show at 6:00 PM on the same day?
(a) 6:25 PM (b) 6:30 PM (c) 6:20 PM (d) 6:15 PM
Answer: (b) 6:30 PM
Solution: From 6 AM to 6 PM = 12 hours = 720 minutes. Gain rate = 2.5 seconds per minute. Total gain = 720 * 2.5 = 1800 seconds = 30 minutes. Time shown = 6:30 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q94. (Medium) At what times between 6 and 7 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 15 minutes apart?
(a) 6:15 and 6:50 (b) 6:18 2/11 and 6:48 8/11 (c) 6:16 4/11 and 6:49 1/11 (d) 6:21 9/11 and 6:54 6/11
Answer: (c) 6:16 4/11 and 6:49 1/11
Solution: 15 minutes apart = 90°: |180 - 5.5M| = 90 => 5.5M = 90 => M = 16 4/11 min, and 5.5M = 270 => M = 49 1/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q95. (Medium) A clock is set right at 6:00 AM. It loses 5 minutes in 24 hours. What is the approximate true time when the clock indicates 6:00 AM the next day?
(a) 5:55 AM (b) 6:00 AM (c) 5:50 AM (d) 6:05 AM
Answer: (d) 6:05 AM
Solution: The clock loses 5 minutes in 24 hours. When the faulty clock shows 24 hours elapsed, true elapsed time is approximately 24 hours + 5 minutes = 6:05 AM.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q96. (Medium) What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 8:24?
(a) 108° (b) 102° (c) 96° (d) 114°
Answer: (a) 108°
Solution: Angle = |30(8) - 5.5(24)| = |240 - 132| = 108°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q97. (Medium) If 29th February 2020 was Saturday, what day of the week was 28th February 2020?
(a) Thursday (b) Friday (c) Saturday (d) Sunday
Answer: (b) Friday
Solution: 28 February is the day immediately preceding 29 February. Saturday - 1 day = Friday.
Source: RRB Group D

Q98. (Medium) A clock shows 2:50. What will be the time in its water image?
(a) 10:10 (b) 9:10 (c) 3:40 (d) 4:10
Answer: (c) 3:40
Solution: Water image time = 18:30 - 2:50 = 17:90 - 2:50 = 15:40 = 3:40.
Source: SSC MTS

Q99. (Medium) Which year will have the same calendar as the year 2021?
(a) 2026 (b) 2032 (c) 2025 (d) 2027
Answer: (d) 2027
Solution: 2021 is an ordinary year following a leap year (2020). Its calendar repeats after 6 years: 2021 + 6 = 2027.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q100. (Medium) At what times between 5 and 6 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 24 minutes apart?
(a) 5:02 and 5:54 (b) 5:01 1/11 and 5:53 5/11 (c) 5:03 2/11 and 5:52 3/11 (d) 5:05 5/11 and 5:55
Answer: (b) 5:01 1/11 and 5:53 5/11
Solution: 24 minutes apart = 144°: |150 - 5.5M| = 144 => 5.5M = 6 => M = 12/11 = 1 1/11 min, and 5.5M = 294 => M = 588/11 = 53 5/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL
