4. Top 10 High-Yield Questions (with Solutions)

🥇 Q1. SSC CGL Tier 1
Question: At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles?
Answer: At 4 hours 5 5/11 minutes and 4 hours 38 2/11 minutes.
Solution: Right angle condition: |30H - 5.5M| = 90°. For H = 4: 120 - 5.5M = 90 => 5.5M = 30 => M = 60/11 = 5 5/11 min. Second right angle: 120 - 5.5M = -90 => 5.5M = 210 => M = 420/11 = 38 2/11 min. Both occur between 4 and 5 o'clock.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Providing rounded off choices like 4:15 or 4:45 to mislead candidates who guess without using fractional minutes.
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip — solve using the angle formula within 45 seconds.

🥈 Q2. SSC CGL
Question: If a clock shows 11 hours 50 minutes, what time will it show in a plane mirror?
Answer: 12:10 (or 12 hours 10 minutes).
Solution: Mirror time = 11:60 - 11:50 = 0:10, which corresponds to 12:10 on a standard 12-hour clock.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Confusing 0:10 with 1:10 or subtracting from 12:60 instead of 11:60.
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip — solve in 15 seconds.

🥉 Q3. SSC CGL
Question: Which of the following calendar years will have the same calendar as the year 2009?
Answer: 2015.
Solution: 2009 is an ordinary year. Counting odd days from 2009: 2009(1) + 2010(1) + 2011(1) + 2012(2) + 2013(1) + 2014(1) = 7 days = 0 odd days. Thus, the calendar repeats in 2015.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Blindly applying the 6/11/28 year repetition rule without verifying leap year boundaries.
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip — count odd days in 45 seconds.

🏅 Q4. SSC CGL Tier 2
Question: A clock was 5 minutes slow at 8:00 AM on Sunday. It gained uniformly and was 5 minutes fast at 8:00 PM on Monday. When did it show the correct time?
Answer: Monday at 2:00 AM.
Solution: Total time from Sunday 8:00 AM to Monday 8:00 PM = 36 hours. Total gain = 5 min (slow) + 5 min (fast) = 10 minutes. Time to gain 5 minutes (to become correct) = (5/10) * 36 = 18 hours. Sunday 8:00 AM + 18 hours = Monday 2:00 AM.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Considering only a 5-minute difference instead of the total 10-minute swing between slow and fast.
⏱️ Time Rule: Borderline — if the logic is not clear in 45 seconds, mark for review.

🏅 Q5. SSC CGL Tier 1
Question: If exactly 1 year and 2 days from today is Tuesday, what day is today? (Assume the year is not a leap year).
Answer: Saturday.
Solution: An ordinary year has 365 days = 1 odd day. Total shift = 1 year + 2 days = 1 + 2 = 3 odd days. Moving backward 3 days from Tuesday gives Saturday.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Forgetting to add the 1 odd day of the ordinary year.
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip — solve in 30 seconds.

🏅 Q6. SSC CHSL
Question: If a year is not a leap year, how are the first and last days of that year related?
Answer: Both days are the same.
Solution: An ordinary year has 365 days. The number of days after January 1st to December 31st is 364 days = 52 exact weeks = 0 odd days. Hence, the last day is identical to the first day.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Thinking that 1 odd day in the year means December 31 is 1 day ahead (1 odd day is the shift to Jan 1 of the following year).
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip — 15 seconds theoretical question.

🏅 Q7. SSC CGL
Question: What is the reflex angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock at 10:40?
Answer: 280° (reflex angle) or 80° (acute angle).
Solution: Angle theta = |30 * 10 - 5.5 * 40| = |300 - 220| = 80°. The reflex angle is 360° - 80° = 280°.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Habitually picking the smaller angle 80° instead of the requested reflex angle 280°.
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip — 30 seconds, underline "reflex".

🏅 Q8. SSC Stenographer
Question: What day of the week was 14 September 1947?
Answer: Sunday.
Solution: Date = 14, Month code (Sep) = 5, Century code (1900s) = 0, Year = 47, Leap years = 11. Sum = 14 + 5 + 0 + 47 + 11 = 77. 77 mod 7 = 0 = Sunday.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Arithmetic errors in month code or leap year count.
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip if century and month codes are memorized — 45 seconds.

🏅 Q9. SSC CHSL
Question: In a month of 29 days, the second Thursday falls on the 13th. What is the second to last day of the month?
Answer: Friday.
Solution: 2nd Thursday = 13th => 1st Thursday = 6th => 3rd Thursday = 20th => 4th Thursday = 27th. Then 28th = Friday, 29th = Saturday. The second to last day (28th) is Friday.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Mistaking "second to last day" as the 27th instead of the 28th.
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip — 45 seconds.

🏅 Q10. SSC CGL
Question: A clock gains 6 minutes every hour and was set right at 8:00 AM. What time will it show at 6:00 PM on the same day?
Answer: 7:00 PM.
Solution: From 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM = 10 hours elapsed. Total gain = 10 * 6 minutes = 60 minutes = 1 hour. At correct time 6:00 PM, the clock shows 6:00 PM + 1 hour = 7:00 PM.
🧠 Psychological Trap: Confusing gain per hour with total gain over the period.
⏱️ Time Rule: Do not skip — solve in 30 seconds.
