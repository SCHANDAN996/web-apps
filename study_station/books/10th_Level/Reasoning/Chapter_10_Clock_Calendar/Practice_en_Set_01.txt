Set 1: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. What is the angle between the hands at 2 o'clock?
(a) 30° (b) 60° (c) 90° (d) 45°
Answer: (b) 60°
Solution: angle = |30 × 2| = 60°.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. What is the (smaller) angle between the hands at 9 o'clock?
(a) 90° (b) 270° (c) 180° (d) 120°
Answer: (a) 90°
Solution: |30 × 9| = 270°; the smaller angle = 360 − 270 = 90°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Each hour on a clock is how many degrees?
(a) 30° (b) 15° (c) 6° (d) 12°
Answer: (a) 30°
Solution: 360° ÷ 12 = 30°.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q4. What is the angle between the hands at 12 o'clock?
(a) 0° (b) 90° (c) 180° (d) 360°
Answer: (a) 0°
Solution: Both hands point to 12, so the angle is 0°.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q5. Is 2000 a leap year?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot say (d) Never
Answer: (a) Yes
Solution: 2000 is a century year divisible by 400, so it is a leap year.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. What is the angle between the hands at 6:30?
(a) 15° (b) 30° (c) 0° (d) 45°
Answer: (a) 15°
Solution: |30 × 6 − 5.5 × 30| = |180 − 165| = 15°.
Source: SSC CGL

Q7. How many odd days are in an ordinary year?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 0 (d) 3
Answer: (a) 1
Solution: 365 = 52 weeks + 1 day.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q8. What is the angle between the hands at 5 o'clock?

Answer: (b) 150°
Solution: |30 × 5| = 150°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q9. How many days does February have in a leap year?

Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q10. What is the angle between the hands at 4:20?
(a) 10° (b) 20° (c) 0° (d) 30°
Answer: (a) 10°
Solution: |30 × 4 − 5.5 × 20| = |120 − 110| = 10°.
Source: SSC CGL

Q11. How many degrees does the hour hand move in one minute?
(a) 6° (b) 1° (c) 0.5° (d) 30°
Answer: (c) 0.5°
Solution: 30° per hour ÷ 60 = 0.5° per minute.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q13. Is 2023 a leap year?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot say (d) Always
Answer: (b) No

Q14. Which of these months has 30 days?
