Set 1: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–25: Medium

Q1. What is the angle between the hands at 2 o'clock?
(a) 60° (b) 30° (c) 90° (d) 45°
Answer: (a) 60°
Solution: angle = |30 × 2| = 60°.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. What is the (smaller) angle between the hands at 9 o'clock?
(a) 270° (b) 90° (c) 180° (d) 120°
Answer: (b) 90°
Solution: |30 × 9| = 270°; the smaller angle = 360 − 270 = 90°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Each hour on a clock is how many degrees?
(a) 15° (b) 6° (c) 30° (d) 12°
Answer: (c) 30°
Solution: 360° ÷ 12 = 30°.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q4. What is the angle between the hands at 12 o'clock?
(a) 90° (b) 180° (c) 360° (d) 0°
Answer: (d) 0°
Solution: Both hands point to 12, so the angle is 0°.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q5. Is 2000 a leap year?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot say (d) Never
Answer: (a) Yes
Solution: 2000 is a century year divisible by 400, so it is a leap year.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. What is the angle between the hands at 6:30?
(a) 30° (b) 15° (c) 0° (d) 45°
Answer: (b) 15°
Solution: |30 × 6 − 5.5 × 30| = |180 − 165| = 15°.
Source: SSC CGL

Q7. How many odd days are in an ordinary year?
(a) 2 (b) 0 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (c) 1
Solution: 365 = 52 weeks + 1 day.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q8. What is the angle between the hands at 5 o'clock?

Answer: (d) 150°

Source: SSC MTS

Q9. How many days does February have in a leap year?

Q10. What is the angle between the hands at 4:20?
(a) 20° (b) 10° (c) 0° (d) 30°
Answer: (b) 10°
Solution: |30 × 4 − 5.5 × 20| = |120 − 110| = 10°.
Source: SSC CGL

Q11. How many degrees does the hour hand move in one minute?
(a) 6° (b) 1° (c) 0.5° (d) 30°
Answer: (c) 0.5°
Solution: 30° per hour ÷ 60 = 0.5° per minute.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q13. Is 2023 a leap year?

Answer: (a) No

Q14. Which of these months has 30 days?

Q16. How many times do the clock hands overlap in 12 hours?
(a) 12 (b) 22 (c) 24 (d) 11

Q19. (Medium) What is the (smaller) angle between the hands at 12:30?
(a) 180° (b) 150° (c) 165° (d) 195°
Answer: (c) 165°
Solution: |30 × 12 − 5.5 × 30| = |360 − 165| = 195°; smaller = 360 − 195 = 165°.
Source: SSC CGL

Q20. (Medium) Is 2100 a leap year?
(a) Yes (b) Cannot say (c) Always (d) No
Answer: (d) No
Solution: 2100 is a century year not divisible by 400, so it is not a leap year.
Source: SSC MTS

Q21. (Medium) How many degrees does the minute hand of a clock rotate in one minute?
(a) 6° (b) 30° (c) 0.5° (d) 12°
Answer: (a) 6°
Solution: The minute hand completes 360° in 60 minutes, so in 1 minute it rotates 360° / 60 = 6°.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q22. (Medium) What is the smaller angle between the hands of a clock at 8 o'clock?
(a) 240° (b) 120° (c) 150° (d) 90°
Answer: (b) 120°
Solution: |30 × 8| = 240°; smaller angle = 360° − 240° = 120°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q23. (Medium) How many times are the hands of a clock at right angles in 12 hours?
(a) 11 (b) 24 (c) 22 (d) 44
Answer: (c) 22
Solution: Clock hands form right angles 2 times in most hours, but only once between 2-3 and 8-9, totaling 22 times in 12 hours.
Source: SSC CGL

Q24. (Medium) If today is Monday, what day of the week will it be after 61 days?
(a) Sunday (b) Tuesday (c) Friday (d) Saturday
Answer: (d) Saturday
Solution: 61 ÷ 7 = 8 weeks remainder 5 days. Monday + 5 days = Saturday.
Source: RRB Group D

Q25. (Medium) If a clock shows 3:15, what will be the time seen in a mirror?
(a) 9:45 (b) 8:45 (c) 8:15 (d) 7:45
Answer: (b) 8:45
Solution: Mirror time = 11:60 − 3:15 = 8:45.
Source: SSC CHSL
