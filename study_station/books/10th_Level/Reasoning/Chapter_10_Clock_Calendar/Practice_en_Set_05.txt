Set 5: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English)
Questions 101–125: Medium to Hard

Q101. (Medium) At what time between 5 and 6 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 5:10 10/11 (b) 5:21 9/11 (c) 5:43 7/11 (d) 5:16 4/11
Answer: (a) 5:10 10/11
Solution: |150 - 5.5M| = 90 => 150 - 5.5M = 90 => 5.5M = 60 => M = 120/11 = 10 10/11 minutes. Time = 5:10 10/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q102. (Medium) A clock shows 6:50. What is the time shown in its mirror image?
(a) 4:10 (b) 5:10 (c) 5:50 (d) 6:10
Answer: (b) 5:10
Solution: Mirror image time = 11:60 - 6:50 = 5:10.
Source: SSC MTS

Q103. (Medium) What day of the week was 25th December 2010?
(a) Sunday (b) Friday (c) Saturday (d) Thursday
Answer: (c) Saturday
Solution: Date = 25, Dec code = 5, Century code = 6, Year = 10, Leap = 2. Total = 25 + 5 + 6 + 10 + 2 = 48 mod 7 = 6 = Saturday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q104. (Medium) A clock gains 6 minutes every day. If set right at 12 noon on Monday, after how many days will it show the correct time again?
(a) 60 days (b) 90 days (c) 150 days (d) 120 days
Answer: (d) 120 days
Solution: To show correct time again, it must gain 12 hours = 720 minutes. Days = 720 / 6 = 120 days.
Source: SSC CGL

Q105. (Medium) A clock shows 2:40. What is the time shown in its water image?
(a) 3:50 (b) 9:20 (c) 8:20 (d) 10:10
Answer: (a) 3:50
Solution: Water image time = 18:30 - 2:40 = 17:90 - 2:40 = 15:50 = 3:50.
Source: SSC MTS

Q106. (Medium) At what time between 8 and 9 o'clock will the hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 8:38 2/11 (b) 8:43 7/11 (c) 8:44 6/11 (d) 8:40 1/11
Answer: (b) 8:43 7/11
Solution: M = 60H/11 = 60(8)/11 = 480/11 = 43 7/11 minutes. Time = 8:43 7/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q107. (Medium) If 1st January 2024 was Monday, what day of the week was 1st January 2025?
(a) Tuesday (b) Thursday (c) Wednesday (d) Sunday
Answer: (c) Wednesday
Solution: 2024 is a leap year (366 days = 2 odd days). Monday + 2 days = Wednesday.
Source: RRB Group D

Q108. (Medium) A clock strikes the number of the hour at each hour, and strikes once at each quarter-hour (15, 30, 45 minutes). How many times will it strike from 8:00 AM to 12:00 noon (both inclusive)?
(a) 60 (b) 58 (c) 64 (d) 62
Answer: (d) 62
Solution: Hourly strikes: 8 + 9 + 10 + 11 + 12 = 50. Quarter-hour strikes: 3 per hour * 4 intervals = 12 strikes. Total = 50 + 12 = 62 strikes.
Source: SSC CGL

Q109. (Medium) What is the smaller angle between the hands of a clock at 2:50?
(a) 145° (b) 155° (c) 135° (d) 140°
Answer: (a) 145°
Solution: |30(2) - 5.5(50)| = |60 - 275| = 215°. Smaller angle = 360° - 215° = 145°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q110. (Medium) A clock shows 8:15. What will be the time in its water image?
(a) 3:45 (b) 10:15 (c) 4:45 (d) 2:45
Answer: (b) 10:15
Solution: Water image time = 18:30 - 8:15 = 10:15.
Source: SSC MTS

Q111. (Medium) At what time between 1 and 2 o'clock will the hands of a clock point in opposite directions?
(a) 1:42 8/11 (b) 1:36 4/11 (c) 1:38 2/11 (d) 1:45 6/11
Answer: (c) 1:38 2/11
Solution: 5.5M - 30(1) = 180 => 5.5M = 210 => M = 420/11 = 38 2/11 minutes. Time = 1:38 2/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q112. (Medium) What day of the week was 5th June 2015?
(a) Thursday (b) Saturday (c) Sunday (d) Friday
Answer: (d) Friday
Solution: Date = 5, June code = 4, Century code = 6, Year = 15, Leap = 3. Total = 5 + 4 + 6 + 15 + 3 = 33 mod 7 = 5 = Friday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q113. (Medium) A clock is set right at 8:00 AM. It loses 5 minutes per hour. What is the approximate true time when the clock indicates 11:00 AM on the same day?
(a) 11:16 AM (b) 11:12 AM (c) 11:20 AM (d) 11:25 AM
Answer: (a) 11:16 AM
Solution: Clock elapsed time = 3 hours = 180 min. True to clock ratio = 60 : 55 = 12 : 11. True minutes = 180 * (12/11) ≈ 196.36 min = 3 hr 16 min. True time = 11:16 AM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q114. (Medium) At what times between 3 and 4 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 8 minutes apart?
(a) 3:10 10/11 and 3:30 (b) 3:07 7/11 and 3:25 1/11 (c) 3:05 5/11 and 3:28 4/11 (d) 3:12 and 3:32
Answer: (b) 3:07 7/11 and 3:25 1/11
Solution: 8 minutes apart = 48°: |90 - 5.5M| = 48 => 5.5M = 42 => M = 84/11 = 7 7/11 min, and 5.5M = 138 => M = 276/11 = 25 1/11 min.
Source: SSC CGL

Q115. (Medium) Which year after 2019 will have the exact same calendar as the year 2019?
(a) 2025 (b) 2026 (c) 2030 (d) 2024
Answer: (c) 2030
Solution: 2019 is an ordinary year. Odd days: 2019(1)+2020(2)+2021(1)+2022(1)+2023(1)+2024(2)+2025(1)+2026(1)+2027(1)+2028(2)+2029(1) = 14 mod 7 = 0. Calendar repeats in 2030 (11 years later).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q116. (Medium) A clock is set right at 10:00 AM on Monday. It gains 4 minutes per hour. What is the true time when the clock indicates 6:00 PM on the same day?
(a) 5:45 PM (b) 5:20 PM (c) 6:30 PM (d) 5:30 PM
Answer: (d) 5:30 PM
Solution: Clock shows 8 hours elapsed. Clock runs 64 minutes for every 60 true minutes. True hours = 8 * (60/64) = 7.5 hours = 7 hr 30 min. True time = 10:00 AM + 7 hr 30 min = 5:30 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q117. (Medium) A clock shows 7:55. What is the time shown in its mirror image?
(a) 4:05 (b) 5:05 (c) 3:55 (d) 6:05
Answer: (a) 4:05
Solution: Mirror image time = 11:60 - 7:55 = 4:05.
Source: SSC MTS

Q118. (Medium) How many times do the hands of a clock coincide between 10:00 AM and 10:00 PM?
(a) 12 (b) 11 (c) 10 (d) 13
Answer: (b) 11
Solution: In any 12-hour period, clock hands coincide exactly 11 times.
Source: SSC MTS

Q119. (Medium) A clock gains 15 seconds per hour. It was set right at 8:00 AM. What time will it show at 8:00 PM on the same day?
(a) 8:06 PM (b) 8:02 PM (c) 8:03 PM (d) 8:05 PM
Answer: (c) 8:03 PM
Solution: From 8 AM to 8 PM = 12 hours. Total gain = 12 * 15 sec = 180 seconds = 3 minutes. Time shown = 8:03 PM.
Source: RRB Group D

Q120. (Medium) What day of the week was 1st January 1900?
(a) Tuesday (b) Sunday (c) Wednesday (d) Monday
Answer: (d) Monday
Solution: By standard Gregorian calendar reckoning, 1st January 1900 was a Monday.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q121. (Hard) How many leap years are there in a period of 400 consecutive years?
(a) 97 (b) 100 (c) 96 (d) 98
Answer: (a) 97
Solution: In 400 years, 400 / 4 = 100 years are divisible by 4. However, 3 century years (100, 200, 300) are not leap years because they are not divisible by 400. Total = 100 - 3 = 97 leap years.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q122. (Hard) If 1st January 2000 was Saturday, what day of the week was 31st December 2000?
(a) Monday (b) Sunday (c) Saturday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (b) Sunday
Solution: 2000 is a leap year (366 days). In a leap year, the last day is 1 day ahead of the first day. Saturday + 1 day = Sunday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q123. (Hard) Which year after 2024 will have the exact same calendar as 2024?
(a) 2032 (b) 2048 (c) 2052 (d) 2028
Answer: (c) 2052
Solution: The calendar of a leap year repeats after 28 years. 2024 + 28 = 2052.
Source: RRB Group D

Q124. (Hard) The mirror image of a clock shows 3:15. What is the actual time?
(a) 9:45 (b) 8:15 (c) 9:15 (d) 8:45
Answer: (d) 8:45
Solution: Actual time = 11:60 - 3:15 = 8:45.
Source: SSC MTS

Q125. (Hard) A clock gains 30 minutes every day. If it shows the correct time at 12 noon today, after how many days will it show the correct time again?
(a) 12 days (b) 24 days (c) 36 days (d) 48 days
Answer: (b) 24 days
Solution: To show the correct time again, the clock must gain 12 hours = 720 minutes. Days = 720 / 30 = 24 days.
Source: SSC CGL
