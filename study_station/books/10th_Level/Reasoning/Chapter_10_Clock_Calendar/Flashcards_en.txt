Front: How many degrees is each hour on a clock?
Back: 30° (360° ÷ 12).

Front: How many degrees does the minute hand move per minute?
Back: 6° (360° ÷ 60).

Front: How many degrees does the hour hand move per minute?
Back: 0.5° (30° ÷ 60).

Front: What is the formula for the angle between the hands?
Back: angle = |30 × hours − 5.5 × minutes|.

Front: Angle between hands at 3 o'clock?
Back: 90° (a right angle).

Front: Angle between hands at 6 o'clock?
Back: 180° (a straight line).

Front: What is the angle at 3:30?
Back: |30×3 − 5.5×30| = |90−165| = 75°.

Front: How many odd days in an ordinary year?
Back: 1 odd day (365 = 52 weeks + 1).

Front: How many odd days in a leap year?
Back: 2 odd days (366 = 52 weeks + 2).

Front: What is the basic leap-year rule?
Back: A year exactly divisible by 4 (like 2024).

Front: When is a century year a leap year?
Back: When divisible by 400 — 2000 yes, 1900 no.

Front: Angle between hands at 4 o'clock?
Back: 120° (4 × 30).

Front: How many times do the hands meet in 24 hours?
Back: 22 times.

Front: Which months have 30 days?
Back: April, June, September, November.

Front: What is the clock-angle mnemonic?
Back: "Each hour 30, each minute five-and-a-half".
