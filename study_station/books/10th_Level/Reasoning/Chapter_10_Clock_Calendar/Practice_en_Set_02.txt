Set 2: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English)
Questions 26–50: Medium

26. At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 2:27 3/11 (b) 2:25 3/11 (c) 2:27 9/11 (d) 2:30
Answer: (a)
Solution: Using |30H − 5.5M| = 90 with H = 2: |60 − 5.5M| = 90 gives 5.5M = 150, so M = 300/11 = 27 3/11 minutes. The trap is using 5.5M = 210 (the second right angle), which gives 2:38 2/11, or miscomputing the division. So the answer is 2:27 3/11.
Source: PYQ-style

27. A watch gains 5 seconds in 3 minutes and was set right at 8:00 AM. What time will it show at 10:00 PM on the same day?
(a) 10:24:00 PM (b) 10:22:40 PM (c) 10:23:20 PM (d) 10:25:00 PM
Answer: (c)
Solution: From 8 AM to 10 PM is 14 hours = 840 minutes; gain = (5/3) × 840 = 1400 seconds = 23 min 20 sec. The trap is computing gain as 5 sec per minute, which wrongly gives 10:25:00 PM. So the answer is 10:23:20 PM.
Source: PYQ-style

28. If 15th August 2024 is Thursday, what will be the day of the week on 15th August 2028?
(a) Wednesday (b) Thursday (c) Friday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (d)
Solution: From 15 Aug 2024 to 15 Aug 2028 the odd days are 1 + 1 + 1 + 2 = 5, since 29 Feb 2028 falls in the last interval. The trap is counting only one leap day for all four years or forgetting 2028's February, giving Thursday or Friday. Thursday + 5 = Tuesday, so the answer is Tuesday.
Source: PYQ-style

29. A clock is set right at 5:00 AM. It loses 16 minutes in 24 hours. What will be the true time when the clock indicates 10:00 PM on the 4th day?
(a) 10:48 PM (b) 11:24 PM (c) 11:30 PM (d) 11:00 PM
Answer: (d)
Solution: The faulty clock shows 89 hours elapsed; since 1424 minutes of this clock equal 1440 true minutes, true elapsed time = 89 × (1440/1424) = 90 hours. The trap is adding the lost time directly instead of using the ratio. 5:00 AM + 90 hours = 11:00 PM on the 4th day, so the answer is 11:00 PM.
Source: PYQ-style

30. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 9:45?
(a) 22.5° (b) 45° (c) 67.5° (d) 90°
Answer: (a)
Solution: Angle = |30 × 9 − 5.5 × 45| = |270 − 247.5| = 22.5°. The trap is ignoring the hour hand's movement past 9 and computing |270 − 270| = 0° or using 30 × 9 − 6 × 45 wrongly. So the answer is 22.5°.
Source: PYQ-style

31. At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock will the hands of a clock be in the same straight line but pointing in opposite directions?
(a) 7:04 5/11 (b) 7:06 5/11 (c) 7:05 5/11 (d) 7:05
Answer: (c)
Solution: Opposite hands need |30 × 7 − 5.5M| = 180, so 210 − 5.5M = 180 and M = 60/11 = 5 5/11 minutes. The trap is setting the angle to 0° (coincidence), which gives 7:38 2/11. So the answer is 7:05 5/11.
Source: PYQ-style

32. If 1st January 2007 was Monday, what day of the week was 1st January 2008?
(a) Monday (b) Tuesday (c) Wednesday (d) Sunday
Answer: (b)
Solution: 2007 is an ordinary year with 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 odd day, so the next 1st January shifts by one day. The trap is treating 2007 as a leap year and adding 2 days, giving Wednesday. Monday + 1 = Tuesday, so the answer is Tuesday.
Source: PYQ-style

33. At what time between 5:30 and 6:00 will the hands of a clock be at right angles?
(a) 5:40 (b) 5:44 3/11 (c) 5:43 7/11 (d) 5:45
Answer: (c)
Solution: For the right angle after 5:30: 5.5M − 30 × 5 = 90, so 5.5M = 240 and M = 480/11 = 43 7/11 minutes. The trap is using 5.5M = 60 (the earlier right angle at 5:10 10/11), which falls before 5:30. So the answer is 5:43 7/11.
Source: PYQ-style

34. A clock gains 15 minutes per day. It is set right at 12 noon. What time will it indicate at 4:00 AM the next day?
(a) 4:10 AM (b) 4:15 AM (c) 4:05 AM (d) 4:20 AM
Answer: (a)
Solution: From 12 noon to 4:00 AM is 16 hours; gain = (15/24) × 16 = 10 minutes. The trap is applying the full 15-minute daily gain to only 16 hours, giving 4:15 AM. So the answer is 4:10 AM.
Source: PYQ-style

35. How many times in a day (24 hours) are the hands of a clock straight, pointing either in the same direction or in opposite directions?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 48 (d) 44
Answer: (d)
Solution: The hands coincide 22 times and are opposite 22 times in 24 hours, and both cases form a straight line, giving 44. The trap is answering 22 by counting only coincidences, or 48 by wrongly doubling 24. So the answer is 44.
Source: PYQ-style

36. Which of the following is not a leap year?
(a) 800 (b) 700 (c) 1200 (d) 2000
Answer: (b)
Solution: Century years must be divisible by 400 to be leap years; 800, 1200 and 2000 all qualify, but 700 does not. The trap is checking divisibility by 4 only, which would wrongly make 700 a leap year. So the answer is 700.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

37. What day of the week was 1st January 1901?
(a) Wednesday (b) Tuesday (c) Monday (d) Thursday
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1st January 1900 was a Monday, and 1900 is not a leap year (not divisible by 400), so it contributes 1 odd day. The trap is treating 1900 as a leap year and shifting by 2 days, giving Wednesday. Monday + 1 = Tuesday, so the answer is Tuesday.
Source: PYQ-style

38. At what time between 6 and 7 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the second time?
(a) 6:47 5/11 (b) 6:49 1/11 (c) 6:48 (d) 6:49 5/11
Answer: (b)
Solution: For the second right angle: 5.5M − 30 × 6 = 90, so 5.5M = 270 and M = 540/11 = 49 1/11 minutes. The trap is solving 180 − 5.5M = 90, which gives the first right angle at 6:16 4/11. So the answer is 6:49 1/11.
Source: PYQ-style

39. A clock shows 9:54. What is its mirror image time?
(a) 3:06 (b) 3:54 (c) 2:54 (d) 2:06
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mirror image time = 11:60 − 9:54 = 2:06. The trap is subtracting from 12:00 without borrowing correctly, which gives 3:06. So the answer is 2:06.
Source: PYQ-style

40. How many odd days are there in 300 years?
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 0
Answer: (c)
Solution: 100 years contain 5 odd days, so 300 years contain 15 odd days, and 15 mod 7 = 1. The trap is answering 15 without reducing modulo 7, or assuming 300 years contain 3 leap-century days. So the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

41. A clock strikes once at 1 o'clock, twice at 2 o'clock, and so on up to twelve times at 12 o'clock. It also strikes once at every half hour. How many times will it strike in 12 hours?
(a) 89 (b) 78 (c) 102 (d) 90
Answer: (d)
Solution: Hourly strikes total 1 + 2 + … + 12 = 78, and the 12 half-hour strikes add 12 more, giving 90. The trap is forgetting the half-hour strikes and answering 78. So the answer is 90.
Source: PYQ-style

42. If the day before yesterday was Sunday, what day will it be three days after the day after tomorrow?
(a) Friday (b) Sunday (c) Saturday (d) Monday
Answer: (b)
Solution: Day before yesterday = Sunday means today is Tuesday; the day after tomorrow is Thursday, and three days after Thursday is Sunday. The trap is miscounting "day after tomorrow" as tomorrow, which gives Saturday. So the answer is Sunday.
Source: PYQ-style

43. At what times between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 10 minutes apart?
(a) 4:10 10/11 and 4:32 8/11 (b) 4:21 9/11 and 4:43 7/11 (c) 4:20 and 4:44 (d) 4:18 2/11 and 4:41 8/11
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ten minutes apart means 60° apart: |120 − 5.5M| = 60 gives M = 120/11 = 10 10/11 or M = 360/11 = 32 8/11. The trap is converting 10 minutes to 10° instead of 60°, which yields wrong times. So the answer is 4:10 10/11 and 4:32 8/11.
Source: PYQ-style

44. A clock is set right at 12 noon on Sunday. It loses 3 minutes in the first hour and gains 3 minutes in the second hour, repeating this pattern. What time will it show at 12 noon on Wednesday?
(a) 11:54 AM (b) 12:06 PM (c) 12:00 noon (d) 12:12 PM
Answer: (c)
Solution: Every 2-hour cycle has net change of zero, and Sunday noon to Wednesday noon is 72 hours, an exact multiple of 2. The trap is multiplying only the loss (or only the gain) by 72 hours, giving a wrong offset. So the answer is 12:00 noon.
Source: PYQ-style

45. If 15th August 2014 was Friday, what was the day of the week on 15th August 2024?
(a) Friday (b) Wednesday (c) Saturday (d) Thursday
Answer: (d)
Solution: The 10-year span includes leap days of 2016, 2020 and 2024 (29 Feb 2024 falls before 15 Aug), so odd days = 10 + 3 = 13 ≡ 6. The trap is counting only two leap years, giving Wednesday. Friday + 6 = Thursday, so the answer is Thursday.
Source: PYQ-style

46. At what time between 8 and 9 o'clock will the hands of a clock be in a straight line but not together?
(a) 8:21 9/11 (b) 8:10 10/11 (c) 8:10 9/11 (d) 8:20
Answer: (b)
Solution: Opposite hands need 30 × 8 − 5.5M = 180, so 5.5M = 60 and M = 120/11 = 10 10/11 minutes. The trap is solving for coincidence (angle 0°), which gives 8:43 7/11. So the answer is 8:10 10/11.
Source: PYQ-style

47. A clock is set right at 8:00 AM on Monday. It gains 10 minutes in 24 hours. What is the true time when the clock indicates 5:00 PM on Tuesday?
(a) 4:35 PM (b) 4:49 PM (c) 4:46 PM (d) 4:52 PM
Answer: (c)
Solution: The faulty clock shows 33 hours elapsed; since 1450 faulty minutes equal 1440 true minutes, true time = 33 × (144/145) ≈ 32.77 hours ≈ 32 h 46 min. The trap is subtracting a flat 10 minutes instead of scaling, giving 4:50 PM. 8:00 AM + 32 h 46 min = 4:46 PM, so the answer is 4:46 PM.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Which year after 2020 will have the exact same calendar as the year 2020?
(a) 2048 (b) 2032 (c) 2026 (d) 2054
Answer: (a)
Solution: A leap year's calendar repeats after 28 years within the same century pattern, so 2020 + 28 = 2048. The trap is adding 6 or 11 years (rules for ordinary years), giving 2026 or 2031. So the answer is 2048.
Source: PYQ-style

49. If 8th February 2020 was Saturday, what day of the week was 8th February 2019?
(a) Friday (b) Saturday (c) Wednesday (d) Thursday
Answer: (a)
Solution: From 8 Feb 2019 to 8 Feb 2020 is 365 days because 29 Feb 2020 falls after 8 Feb, so only 1 odd day passes. The trap is counting 2020's leap day, which would shift by 2 and give Thursday. Saturday − 1 = Friday, so the answer is Friday.
Source: PYQ-style

50. A clock shows 4:40. What time will its image in a plane mirror show?
(a) 7:20 (b) 8:20 (c) 7:40 (d) 6:20
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mirror image time = 11:60 − 4:40 = 7:20. The trap is subtracting from 12:00 directly, which gives 8:20. So the answer is 7:20.
Source: PYQ-style
