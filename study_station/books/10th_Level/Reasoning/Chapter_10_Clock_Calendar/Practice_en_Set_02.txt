Set 2: Clock & Calendar MCQs (English)
Questions 26–50: Medium

Q26. (Medium) At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the first time?
(a) 2:27 3/11 (b) 2:25 3/11 (c) 2:27 9/11 (d) 2:30
Answer: (a) 2:27 3/11
Solution: Using |30H - 5.5M| = 90 with H = 2: |60 - 5.5M| = 90 => 5.5M = 150 => M = 300/11 = 27 3/11 minutes. Thus, the hands form a right angle at 2:27 3/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q27. (Medium) A watch gains 5 seconds in 3 minutes and was set right at 8:00 AM. What time will it show at 10:00 PM on the same day?
(a) 10:24:00 PM (b) 10:23:20 PM (c) 10:22:40 PM (d) 10:25:00 PM
Answer: (b) 10:23:20 PM
Solution: From 8 AM to 10 PM = 14 hours = 840 minutes. Gain rate = 5/3 sec per min. Total gain = (5/3) * 840 = 1400 seconds = 23 min 20 sec. Time shown = 10:23:20 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q28. (Medium) If 15th August 2024 is Thursday, what will be the day of the week on 15th August 2028?
(a) Wednesday (b) Thursday (c) Tuesday (d) Friday
Answer: (c) Tuesday
Solution: From 15 Aug 2024 to 15 Aug 2028 is 4 years. Odd days: 2024-25 (1), 2025-26 (1), 2026-27 (1), 2027-28 (includes 29 Feb 2028 = 2). Total odd days = 5. Thursday + 5 days = Tuesday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q29. (Medium) A clock is set right at 5:00 AM. It loses 16 minutes in 24 hours. What will be the true time when the clock indicates 10:00 PM on the 4th day?
(a) 10:48 PM (b) 11:24 PM (c) 11:30 PM (d) 11:00 PM
Answer: (d) 11:00 PM
Solution: Clock elapsed time = 3 days (72 h) + 17 h = 89 hours. True time to clock time ratio = 1440 : 1424 = 90 : 89. True time elapsed = (90/89) * 89 = 90 hours. 5:00 AM + 90 hours = 11:00 PM on the 4th day.
Source: SSC CGL

Q30. (Medium) What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 9:45?
(a) 22.5° (b) 45° (c) 67.5° (d) 90°
Answer: (a) 22.5°
Solution: Angle = |30H - 5.5M| = |30(9) - 5.5(45)| = |270 - 247.5| = 22.5°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q31. (Medium) At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock will the hands of a clock be in the same straight line but pointing in opposite directions?
(a) 7:04 5/11 (b) 7:05 5/11 (c) 7:06 5/11 (d) 7:05
Answer: (b) 7:05 5/11
Solution: Opposite condition: |30(7) - 5.5M| = 180 => 210 - 5.5M = 180 => 5.5M = 30 => M = 60/11 = 5 5/11 minutes. Time = 7:05 5/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q32. (Medium) If 1st January 2007 was Monday, what day of the week was 1st January 2008?
(a) Monday (b) Wednesday (c) Tuesday (d) Sunday
Answer: (c) Tuesday
Solution: The year 2007 is an ordinary year with 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 odd day. Monday + 1 day = Tuesday.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q33. (Medium) At what time between 5:30 and 6:00 will the hands of a clock be at right angles?
(a) 5:40 (b) 5:44 3/11 (c) 5:45 (d) 5:43 7/11
Answer: (d) 5:43 7/11
Solution: For hands at right angles after 5:30: 5.5M - 30(5) = 90 => 5.5M = 240 => M = 480/11 = 43 7/11 minutes. Time = 5:43 7/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q34. (Medium) A clock gains 15 minutes per day. It is set right at 12 noon. What time will it indicate at 4:00 AM the next day?
(a) 4:10 AM (b) 4:15 AM (c) 4:05 AM (d) 4:20 AM
Answer: (a) 4:10 AM
Solution: Time elapsed from 12 noon to 4:00 AM = 16 hours. Gain in 16 hours = (15 / 24) * 16 = 10 minutes. Time shown = 4:10 AM.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q35. (Medium) How many times in a day (24 hours) are the hands of a clock straight (pointing either in the same direction or opposite directions)?
(a) 22 (b) 44 (c) 48 (d) 24
Answer: (b) 44
Solution: The hands coincide 22 times and are opposite 22 times in 24 hours. Both represent a straight line: 22 + 22 = 44 times.
Source: SSC MTS

Q36. (Medium) Which of the following is not a leap year?
(a) 800 (b) 1200 (c) 700 (d) 2000
Answer: (c) 700
Solution: Century years must be divisible by 400 to be leap years. 700 is not divisible by 400, so it is not a leap year.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q37. (Medium) What day of the week was 1st January 1901?
(a) Wednesday (b) Thursday (c) Monday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (d) Tuesday
Solution: 31 December 1900 had 1 odd day (Monday). Therefore, 1st January 1901 was Tuesday.
Source: SSC CGL

Q38. (Medium) At what time between 6 and 7 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at right angles for the second time?
(a) 6:49 1/11 (b) 6:47 5/11 (c) 6:48 (d) 6:49 5/11
Answer: (a) 6:49 1/11
Solution: Second right angle: 5.5M - 30(6) = 90 => 5.5M = 270 => M = 540/11 = 49 1/11 minutes. Time = 6:49 1/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q39. (Medium) A clock shows 9:54. What is its mirror image time?
(a) 3:06 (b) 2:06 (c) 2:54 (d) 3:54
Answer: (b) 2:06
Solution: Mirror image time = 11:60 - 9:54 = 2:06.
Source: SSC MTS

Q40. (Medium) How many odd days are there in 300 years?
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 0
Answer: (c) 1
Solution: 100 years have 5 odd days; 200 years have (5*2 mod 7) = 3; 300 years have (5*3 mod 7) = 1 odd day.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q41. (Medium) A clock strikes once at 1 o'clock, twice at 2 o'clock, and so on. It also strikes once at every half hour. How many times will it strike in 12 hours?
(a) 89 (b) 78 (c) 102 (d) 90
Answer: (d) 90
Solution: Hourly strikes: 1 + 2 + ... + 12 = 78 strikes. Half-hourly strikes: 12 times. Total strikes = 78 + 12 = 90 times.
Source: SSC CGL

Q42. (Medium) If the day before yesterday was Sunday, what day will it be three days after the day after tomorrow?
(a) Sunday (b) Friday (c) Saturday (d) Monday
Answer: (a) Sunday
Solution: Day before yesterday = Sunday => Yesterday = Monday => Today = Tuesday. Tomorrow = Wednesday => Day after tomorrow = Thursday. Three days after Thursday = Sunday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q43. (Medium) At what times between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be 10 minutes apart?
(a) 4:21 9/11 and 4:43 7/11 (b) 4:10 10/11 and 4:32 8/11 (c) 4:20 and 4:44 (d) 4:18 2/11 and 4:41 8/11
Answer: (b) 4:10 10/11 and 4:32 8/11
Solution: 10 minutes apart means 60° apart: |120 - 5.5M| = 60 => 5.5M = 60 => M = 120/11 = 10 10/11 min, and 5.5M = 180 => M = 360/11 = 32 8/11 min. Times: 4:10 10/11 and 4:32 8/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q44. (Medium) A clock is set right at 12 noon on Sunday. It loses 3 minutes in the first hour and gains 3 minutes in the second hour, repeating this pattern. What time will it show at 12 noon on Wednesday?
(a) 11:54 AM (b) 12:06 PM (c) 12:00 noon (d) 12:12 PM
Answer: (c) 12:00 noon
Solution: Every 2 hours, the net loss/gain is zero. From Sunday noon to Wednesday noon = 72 hours (an even number). Net shift is 0, so it shows 12:00 noon.
Source: SSC CGL

Q45. (Medium) If 15th August 2014 was Friday, what was the day of the week on 15th August 2024?
(a) Friday (b) Wednesday (c) Saturday (d) Thursday
Answer: (d) Thursday
Solution: From 2014 to 2024 is 10 years including leap years 2016, 2020, and 2024 (Feb 29 included). Odd days = 10 + 3 = 13 mod 7 = 6 days. Friday + 6 days = Thursday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q46. (Medium) At what time between 8 and 9 o'clock will the hands of a clock be in a straight line but not together?
(a) 8:10 10/11 (b) 8:21 9/11 (c) 8:10 9/11 (d) 8:20
Answer: (a) 8:10 10/11
Solution: Hands in straight line opposite: |30(8) - 5.5M| = 180 => 240 - 5.5M = 180 => 5.5M = 60 => M = 120/11 = 10 10/11 minutes. Time = 8:10 10/11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q47. (Medium) A clock is set right at 8:00 AM on Monday. It gains 10 minutes in 24 hours. What is the true time when the clock indicates 5:00 PM on Tuesday?
(a) 4:35 PM (b) 4:46 PM (c) 4:49 PM (d) 4:52 PM
Answer: (b) 4:46 PM
Solution: Faulty clock elapsed time = 33 hours. True time : Faulty time = 1440 : 1450 = 144 : 145. True hours = (144/145) * 33 ≈ 32.772 hours = 32 hours 46 minutes. True time = 4:46 PM.
Source: SSC CGL

Q48. (Medium) Which year after 2020 will have the exact same calendar as the year 2020?
(a) 2026 (b) 2032 (c) 2048 (d) 2054
Answer: (c) 2048
Solution: The calendar of a leap year repeats after 28 years. 2020 + 28 = 2048.
Source: RRB Group D

Q49. (Medium) If 8th February 2020 was Saturday, what day of the week was 8th February 2019?
(a) Thursday (b) Saturday (c) Wednesday (d) Friday
Answer: (d) Friday
Solution: From 8 Feb 2019 to 8 Feb 2020 is 365 days because 29 Feb 2020 falls after 8 Feb. 365 days = 1 odd day. Saturday - 1 day = Friday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q50. (Medium) At what time between 11 and 12 o'clock will the hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 11:60 (b) 12:00 (c) 11:65 5/11 (d) 11:59
Answer: (b) 12:00
Solution: Formula M = 60H/11. For H = 11: M = 660/11 = 60 minutes past 11, which is exactly 12:00.
Source: SSC MTS
