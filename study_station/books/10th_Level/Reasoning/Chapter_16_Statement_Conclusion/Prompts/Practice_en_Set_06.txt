📝 Practice Set 6/6: Statement & Conclusion (Basic)

Total Questions: 25 (Q126–Q150, All Hard)
Instructions: In each question, one or more statements are given followed by two conclusions I and II. Assume the statements to be true, even if they conflict with commonly known facts. Decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s). Mark:

(A) Only conclusion I follows

(B) Only conclusion II follows

(C) Both I and II follow

(D) Neither I nor II follows

126. Statement: Some A are B. Some B are C. No C is D.
Conclusions:
I. Some A are not D.
II. No A is D.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Either I or II (D) Neither I nor II
Answer: (D) Neither I nor II follows
Solution: There is no direct link from A to D. A could overlap with the B that are C (which are not D), but A could also be entirely outside that set. So I is not definite. II is also not forced; A might have no relation with D.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

127. Statement: Only a few teachers are researchers. All researchers are scholars. Some scholars are authors.

Answer: (D) Neither I nor II follows
Solution: Teachers who are researchers are scholars. But we do not know if those scholars are among the 'some scholars are authors'. Non-researcher teachers could still be authors. So neither conclusion is definite.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

No existence of trained dogs is assured. So neither conclusion follows due to possible emptiness of friendly/trained dogs.
Source: SSC CGL 2017 (Examiner's Trap: existential fallacy)
