📝 Practice Set 4/6: Statement & Conclusion (Basic)

Total Questions: 25 (Q76–Q100, All Medium)
Instructions: In each question, one or more statements are given followed by two conclusions I and II. Assume the statements to be true, even if they conflict with commonly known facts. Decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s). Mark:

(A) Only conclusion I follows

(B) Only conclusion II follows

(C) Both I and II follow

(D) Neither I nor II follows

76. Statement: All buses are vehicles. Some vehicles are not cars.
Conclusions:
I. Some buses are not cars.
II. Some cars are not vehicles.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (D) Neither I nor II follows
Solution: I: Buses could be among the cars (the statement "some vehicles are not cars" does not specify which vehicles; buses could be in the part that are cars). II: "Some vehicles are not cars" does not imply that some cars are not vehicles; all cars could be vehicles. So neither follows.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

77. Statement: All that glitters is not gold.
Conclusions:
I. Some things that glitter are gold.
II. Some things that glitter are not gold.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (B) Only II follows
Solution: The idiomatic statement means "Not everything that glitters is gold," i.e., some glittering things are not gold. II follows. I is not definite; it's possible that no glittering thing is gold.
Source: SSC CGL 2017 (Examiner's Trap: idiomatic expression)

78. Statement: Some cats are not pets. All pets are animals.
Conclusions:
I. Some cats are animals.
II. Some cats are not animals.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Either I or II (D) Neither I nor II
Answer: (D) Neither I nor II follows
Solution: "Some cats are not pets" does not say any cat is a pet; cats might have no relation to pets or animals. So we cannot conclude I. II is also not forced; the cats that are not pets could still be animals.

95. Statement: All pencils are black. Some black things are not pens.
Conclusions:
I. Some pencils are pens.
II. No pencil is a pen.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Either I or II (D) Neither I nor II
Answer: (C) Either I or II follows
Solution: Pencils are a subset of black. The part of black that is "not pens" might include all, some, or none of the pencils. In all possible cases, either some pencils are pens (if they fall outside "not pens") or no pencil is a pen (if they fall entirely within "not pens"). So either I or II is true.
Source: SSC CGL 2018 (Examiner's Trap: complementary pair)
