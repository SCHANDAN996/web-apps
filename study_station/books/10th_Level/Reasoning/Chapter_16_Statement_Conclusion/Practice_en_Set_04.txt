Q76. (Medium) Statements: All kings are brave. All brave people are noble.
Conclusions:
I. All kings are noble.
II. Some noble people are kings.
(A) Both conclusions I and II follow (B) Only conclusion II follows (C) Only conclusion I follows (D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Both conclusions I and II follow

Solution:
Kings ⊂ Brave ⊂ Noble. Thus all kings are noble (I follows) and some noble people are kings (II follows). Hence, the correct option is (A).
