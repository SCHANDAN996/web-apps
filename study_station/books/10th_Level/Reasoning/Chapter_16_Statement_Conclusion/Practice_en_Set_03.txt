Q51. (Medium) Statements: All shirts are garments. All garments are cloth.
Conclusions:
I. All shirts are cloth.
II. Some cloth are shirts.
(A) Both conclusions I and II follow (B) Only conclusion II follows (C) Only conclusion I follows (D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Both conclusions I and II follow

Solution:
Shirts ⊂ Garments ⊂ Cloth. Hence all shirts are cloth (I follows) and some cloth are shirts (II follows). Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q52. (Medium) Statements: Some birds are swimmers. Some swimmers are fish.
Conclusions:
I. Some birds are fish.
II. No bird is a fish.
(A) Only conclusion I follows (B) Either conclusion I or II follows (C) Only conclusion II follows (D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: (B) Either conclusion I or II follows

Solution:
Birds and fish may or may not overlap. Since they form an Either-Or pair ('Some are' / 'No is'), one of them must follow. Hence, the correct option is (B).
