Q26. (Medium) Statements: All trees are plants. Some plants are herbs.
Conclusions:
I. Some trees are herbs.
II. No tree is a herb.
(A) Either conclusion I or II follows (B) Only conclusion II follows (C) Only conclusion I follows (D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Either conclusion I or II follows

Solution:
Trees and herbs may or may not overlap. They form a complementary pair ('Some' and 'No'). Hence, either conclusion I or II must follow. Hence, the correct option is (A).
