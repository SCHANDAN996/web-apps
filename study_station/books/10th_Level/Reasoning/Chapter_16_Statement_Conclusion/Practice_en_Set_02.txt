📝 Practice Set 2/6: Statement & Conclusion (Basic)

Total Questions: 25 (Q26–Q50, All Medium)
Instructions: In each question below, a statement/statements is/are given followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. Assume the statements to be true, even if they contradict commonly known facts. Decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s). Mark your answer as:

(A) Only conclusion I follows

(B) Only conclusion II follows

(C) Both I and II follow

(D) Neither I nor II follows

26. Statement: All trees are plants. Some plants are herbs.
Conclusions:
I. Some trees are herbs.
II. No tree is a herb.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Either I or II (D) Neither I nor II
Answer: (C) Either I or II
Solution: The tree set can be completely inside the ‘herb’ part of plants or completely outside it. In all possible cases, one of I or II must hold. So, either conclusion follows.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Statement: Some actors are dancers. All dancers are singers.
Conclusions:
I. Some actors are singers.
II. All singers are actors.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (A) Only I follows
Solution: Some actors are dancers + All dancers are singers ⇒ Some actors are singers. II is an invalid reversal.
Source: Bank PO 2019

30. Statement: All engineers are graduates. Some graduates are not employed.
Conclusions:
I. Some engineers are not employed.
II. Some employed are graduates.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (D) Neither I nor II follows
Solution: Engineers may be among the employed graduates; we cannot say for sure that some are not employed. II: ‘Some not employed’ does not imply ‘some are employed’ (all could be unemployed). Hence, neither follows.
