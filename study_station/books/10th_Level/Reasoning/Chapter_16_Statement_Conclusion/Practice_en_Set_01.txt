Set 1: Statement & Conclusion MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. Statement: All apples are fruits. Conclusion: Some fruits are apples. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) Never
Answer: (a) Follows
Solution: "All A are B" gives "Some B are A".
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. Statement: All cars are vehicles. Conclusion: All vehicles are cars. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Always (d) Sometimes
Answer: (b) Does not follow
Solution: "All A are B" does not give "All B are A".
Source: SSC MTS
