📝 Practice Set 1/6: Statement & Conclusion (Basic)

Total Questions: 25 (Q1–Q20 Easy, Q21–Q25 Medium)
Instructions: In each question below, a statement is given followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. You have to assume the statement to be true, even if it seems to contradict commonly known facts. Decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statement. Mark your answer as:

(A) Only conclusion I follows

(B) Only conclusion II follows

(C) Both I and II follow

(D) Neither I nor II follows

1. Statement: All roses are flowers.
Conclusions:
I. Some flowers are roses.
II. All flowers are roses.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (A) Only I follows
Solution: From "All roses are flowers", we can safely convert it to "Some flowers are roses" (Conversion rule). Conclusion II is an invalid reversal.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

21. (Medium) Statement: All cups are plates. Some plates are saucers.
Conclusions:
I. Some cups are saucers.
II. No cup is a saucer.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Either I or II (D) Neither I nor II
Answer: (D) Neither I nor II
Solution: From "All cups are plates" and "Some plates are saucers", we cannot directly link cups and saucers. The cup set might be entirely inside the plates that are not saucers, or might overlap with saucers. So neither follows definitely. However, I and II are not an either-or pair because both could be false (if cups are among plates that are not saucers). So (D).
Source: Bank PO 2019
