Q101. (Medium) Statements: All birds are feathered. All feathered creatures are animals.
Conclusions:
I. All birds are animals.
II. Some animals are birds.
(A) Both conclusions I and II follow (B) Only conclusion II follows (C) Only conclusion I follows (D) Neither conclusion I nor II follows

Answer: (A) Both conclusions I and II follow

Solution:
Birds ⊂ Feathered ⊂ Animals. Both conclusions follow by transitive inclusion. Hence, the correct option is (A).
