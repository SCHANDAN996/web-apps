# Short Tricks: Statement & Conclusion

Trick: Always take the statement as 100% true — even if it seems false in the real world. Keep your knowledge aside.

Trick: "All A are B" definitely gives "Some B are A". But not "All B are A".

Trick: "No A is B" definitely gives "No B is A" (both directions).

Trick: A conclusion that only "may be" does not "follow" — a conclusion must be certain.

Trick: Chain (syllogism) — "All A are B; All B are C" → "All A are C" follows.

Trick: Check with a Venn diagram — a conclusion true in every possible picture is correct.

Skip Strategy: Skip tangled two-statement-two-conclusion puzzles; do the single-statement ones first.

💡 Mnemonic: "Statement true, knowledge aside, only the certain is a conclusion" — three golden rules.
