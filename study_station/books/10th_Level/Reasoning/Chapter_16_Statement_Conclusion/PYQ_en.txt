PYQ Analysis: Statement & Conclusion (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Statement & conclusion gives 1–3 questions per exam. The trap is mixing in your own knowledge and treating "may be" as a conclusion.

Q1. Statement: All roses are flowers. Conclusion: Some flowers are roses. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) Partly
Answer: (a) Follows
Solution: "All A are B" always gives "Some B are A".
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. Statement: All dogs are animals. Conclusion: All animals are dogs. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Always (d) Sometimes
Answer: (b) Does not follow
Solution: "All A are B" does not give "All B are A".
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Statement: No student is lazy. Conclusion: No lazy person is a student. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) Partly
Answer: (a) Follows
Solution: "No A is B" gives "No B is A".
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Statement: Some books are pens. Conclusion: Some pens are books. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Never (d) Cannot say
Answer: (a) Follows
Solution: "Some A are B" gives "Some B are A".
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. A conclusion should be drawn based on:
(a) your general knowledge (b) only the given statement (c) real-world facts (d) common sense
Answer: (b) only the given statement
Solution: Conclusions must come only from the statement, not outside knowledge.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. Statement: All A are B. Which conclusion definitely follows?
(a) All B are A (b) Some B are A (c) No B is A (d) Some A are not B
Answer: (b) Some B are A
Solution: "All A are B" gives "Some B are A".
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. Statements: All cats are mammals. All mammals are animals. Conclusion: All cats are animals. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) Partly
Answer: (a) Follows
Solution: Chain: cats ⊂ mammals ⊂ animals, so all cats are animals.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Statement: Some men are doctors. Conclusion: Some doctors are men. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Never (d) Cannot say
Answer: (a) Follows
Solution: "Some A are B" gives "Some B are A".
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. Statement (assumed true): All birds fly. Conclusion: Some flying creatures are birds. Does it follow?
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow (c) Cannot say (d) False
Answer: (a) Follows
Solution: Taking the statement as true, "all birds fly" gives "some flying creatures are birds".
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. A conclusion that is only "may be true" is:
(a) a valid conclusion (b) not a valid conclusion (c) always correct (d) sometimes required
Answer: (b) not a valid conclusion
Solution: A conclusion must follow with certainty, not merely be possible.
Source: SSC CGL
