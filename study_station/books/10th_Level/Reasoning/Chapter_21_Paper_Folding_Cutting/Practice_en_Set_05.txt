Q101. (Medium) A square paper is folded vertically in half (left half over right half), then horizontally in half (top half over bottom half). A semicircle is cut out on the bottom folded edge with its diameter along that edge. How many complete circular holes will appear upon fully unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 4 (D) 8

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
The bottom folded edge lies on the original horizontal midline. Cutting a semicircle along this crease removes semicircles from adjacent layers. Unfolding across the vertical fold reflects them horizontally to form 2 complete circles. Unfolding across the horizontal fold does not duplicate them as they lie on the midline, yielding 2 complete circular holes. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q102. (Medium) A square paper is folded along a diagonal. Then the right-angled corner of the resulting triangle is folded over to the midpoint of the hypotenuse. A small square is cut out along the newly formed folded edge away from any corner. How many square holes will appear upon full unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 4 (C) 6 (D) 8

Answer: (B) 4

Solution:
The first fold gives 2 layers, and the second fold gives 4 layers. The cut is made along a folded crease away from shared corners. Unfolding both creases reflects the cut across both axes, producing 4 symmetric square holes. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q103. (Medium) A square paper is folded along the vertical midline, then the left half is folded over the right half again in an accordion fold. A hole is punched at the midpoint of the left folded edge of this packet. How many holes appear upon full unfolding?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4

Answer: (C) 3

Solution:
The accordion fold creates 4 layers. The left folded edge joins two layers at the crease, while the remaining two layers are distinct flaps. Unfolding across the creases results in 1 merged hole on the crease and 2 separate holes, giving exactly 3 holes in total. Hence, the correct option is (C).
