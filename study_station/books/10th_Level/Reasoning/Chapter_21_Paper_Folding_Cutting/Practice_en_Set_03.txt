Q51. (Medium) A square paper is folded vertically in half (left over right), then horizontally in half (top over bottom). A semicircle is cut out on the bottom folded edge with its diameter along that edge. How many complete circular holes will appear upon fully unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 4 (D) 8

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
The bottom folded edge is part of the original horizontal midline. Cutting a semicircle along this crease joins with its reflection across the horizontal fold line to form a complete circle. Unfolding vertically produces 2 complete circular holes. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q52. (Medium) A square paper is folded along a diagonal. Then the right-angled corner of the resulting triangle is folded down to the midpoint of the hypotenuse. A hole is punched at the midpoint of the newly formed crease. How many holes appear upon complete unfolding?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 4 (D) 8

Answer: (B) 2

Solution:
First fold gives 2 layers; second fold gives 4 layers. The punch on the crease affects the layers meeting at that fold, producing 2 symmetric holes when unfolded along that crease, which reflect across the diagonal to yield 2 holes. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q53. (Medium) ⚠️ A square paper is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally. The resulting quarter-square is folded along its diagonal. A hole is punched at the midpoint of this diagonal crease. How many holes will appear upon complete unfolding?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 4 (D) 8

Answer: (C) 4

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Three successive folds create 2³ = 8 layers. However, because the punch is placed directly on the diagonal crease line, the hole merges across that fold. Thus, exactly 4 holes appear on the unfolded sheet. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q54. (Medium) ⚠️ A square paper is folded along a diagonal, then one acute corner of the triangle is folded over to the right-angled corner. A small square is cut out with one edge lying along the folded crease. How many square holes will appear upon full unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 4 (C) 8 (D) No square holes (only rectangular holes)

Answer: (D) No square holes (only rectangular holes)

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Cutting a square with one side on the folded crease causes each pair of squares on adjacent layers to merge across the crease upon unfolding, forming a rectangle (width = side, length = 2 × side). Therefore, zero square holes are formed. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC
