Q26. (Medium) A square paper is folded along the vertical midline, then folded along the horizontal midline. On the resulting quarter-square, a semicircle is cut along the right folded edge with its diameter on that edge. How many complete circular holes will appear upon fully unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 4 (D) 8

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
The first fold gives 2 layers; the second gives 4 layers. The right folded edge represents the original vertical midline. A semicircle cut along this folded edge merges with its reflection across the vertical midline, producing 2 full circles. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q27. (Medium) A circular paper is folded in half along a diameter to form a semicircle. A small equilateral triangle is cut out from the curved outer rim. Upon completely unfolding, what pattern appears?
(A) A single triangle on the rim (B) Two symmetric triangles on opposite sides of the diameter (C) Four triangles along the rim (D) A diamond at the center

Answer: (B) Two symmetric triangles on opposite sides of the diameter

Solution:
The diameter crease line acts as a plane mirror. The single triangular cut on the curved rim reflects across the diameter, producing two matching triangular cuts on opposite sides of the diameter. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL
