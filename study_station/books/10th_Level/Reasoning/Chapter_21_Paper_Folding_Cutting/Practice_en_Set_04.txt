Q76. (Medium) A square paper is folded in half along the vertical midline, then in half along the horizontal midline. A semicircle is cut out on the left folded edge with its diameter along that edge. How many complete circular holes will appear upon fully unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 1 (C) 4 (D) 8

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
The left folded edge lies on the original vertical midline. Cutting a semicircle along this crease removes semicircles from adjacent layers. Unfolding horizontally joins each pair across the vertical fold into complete circles, yielding 2 complete circular holes along the vertical midline. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q77. (Medium) A square paper is folded along a diagonal to form a triangle. Then one acute corner of this triangle is folded over to the right-angled corner. A small square is cut out at the midpoint of the longest folded edge. How many square holes will appear upon unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 4 (C) 6 (D) 8

Answer: (B) 4

Solution:
The first fold yields 2 layers, and the second fold yields 4 layers. Cutting along the folded crease reflects across the folds without merging at corners, resulting in 4 symmetric square holes upon full unfolding. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q82. (Medium) ⚠️ A square paper is folded along a diagonal. A small square is cut out on the folded edge (the diagonal) such that one of its sides lies entirely along the crease. What shape of hole will appear upon fully unfolding the paper?
(A) A single square hole (B) Two separate square holes (C) A rectangular hole whose length is twice the side of the square (D) A rhombus-shaped hole

Answer: (C) A rectangular hole whose length is twice the side of the square

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: The paper has 2 layers. When a square is cut with one side along the diagonal fold line, both square cuts merge across the crease when unfolded. The resulting hole is a single rectangle with width equal to the square's side and length equal to twice the side. Hence, the correct option is (C).
