Q126. (Hard) A square paper is folded in half vertically (left over right), then horizontally (top over bottom), and finally along the diagonal from the central folded corner to the outer open corner. A hole is punched at the midpoint of the hypotenuse (the folded edge) of this triangular packet. How many holes appear upon full unfolding?
(A) 8 (B) 6 (C) 4 (D) 16

Answer: (A) 8

Solution:
Three successive folds create 2³ = 8 layers. The midpoint of the hypotenuse is not a shared vertex of all folds, so unfolding across all three creases reflects the hole into 8 distinct positions symmetrically. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL
