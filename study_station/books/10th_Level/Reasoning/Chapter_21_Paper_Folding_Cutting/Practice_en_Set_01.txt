Q1. (Easy) A square paper is folded in half along a vertical midline (left half over right half). Two small square holes are cut on the right half away from the crease. When completely unfolded, what pattern of square holes appears?
(A) Two pairs of symmetric square holes on both sides of the midline (B) A single square hole in the middle (C) Two square holes stacked on the midline (D) Four square holes at the four outer corners

Answer: (A) Two pairs of symmetric square holes on both sides of the midline

Solution:
The paper has 2 layers. Holes cut away from the fold line do not merge across the crease. Each cut reflects across the vertical fold line, creating symmetric matching holes on both halves. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q2. (Easy) A square paper is folded along a diagonal. A triangular cut is made on the resulting triangle with its base lying along the diagonal fold line. Upon unfolding, what shape will be formed?
(A) A single triangle (B) A diamond (rhombus) formed by two triangles joined base-to-base (C) Two separate triangles on opposite sides (D) A square centered at the corner

Answer: (B) A diamond (rhombus) formed by two triangles joined base-to-base

Solution:
The base of the cut triangle lies on the fold line. When unfolded, the triangle reflects across the diagonal, merging at the shared base to form a rhombus. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. (Easy) ⚠️ A square paper is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally. A small square is cut out from the corner where both folded edges meet. What pattern appears upon unfolding?
(A) Two square holes along the horizontal crease (B) Four square holes at the four outer corners (C) A single central square hole (D) Four separate square holes in the quadrants

Answer: (C) A single central square hole

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: The corner where both folded edges meet corresponds to the exact center of the original square sheet. Cutting from this corner removes material from the center of all four layers, forming a single square hole at the center. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Source: SSC MTS

Q4. (Easy) A square paper is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally. A small circular hole is punched at the open corner opposite both folded edges. What pattern of holes appears upon unfolding?
(A) One circle at the center (B) Two circles along the diagonal (C) Four quadrant circles (D) One circle at each of the four corners

Answer: (D) One circle at each of the four corners

Solution:
The open corner corresponds to the four outer corners of the original unfolded square. Punching a hole through all four layers at this corner creates one hole at each of the four corners. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Q7. (Easy) A square paper is folded in half along a vertical midline. A semicircle is cut out with its diameter lying along the fold line. What shape appears when the paper is unfolded?

Answer: (C) A single complete circular hole

Q8. (Easy) A square paper is folded along a diagonal, and the resulting triangle is folded in half again by bringing its acute corners together. A hole is punched 1 cm away from the right-angled corner. How many holes appear upon full unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 8 (C) 6 (D) 4

Answer: (D) 4

Solution:
Two successive folds produce 2² = 4 layers. The punch is made through all 4 layers away from any crease intersection, resulting in 4 symmetrically placed holes. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Q21. (Medium) ⚠️ A square paper is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally (producing 4 layers). A hole is punched along the horizontal crease line, away from the intersection of the two folds. How many holes appear upon unfolding?

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
Examiner's Trap: Students often apply 2² = 4. However, because the punch is made directly on the horizontal crease line, the two halves across that crease merge into a single hole. When unfolded vertically, it reflects to the other half, producing exactly 2 holes. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q25. (Medium) A square paper is folded in half along the vertical midline. The right layer is folded backward in an accordion pleat. Then the bottom half is folded upward. A small triangular notch is cut from the upper-left corner of the final packet. How many triangular holes appear upon complete unfolding?
(A) 2 (B) 8 (C) 4 (D) 16

Answer: (B) 8

Solution:
The first fold gives 2 layers, the accordion fold gives 4 layers, and folding the bottom half up gives 2 × 4 = 8 layers. Cutting through all 8 layers at this corner yields 8 triangular holes. Hence, the correct option is (B).
