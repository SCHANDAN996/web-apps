Q26. (Medium)
A square paper is folded along the vertical centre line. A semi‑circle is cut out from the folded paper such that the diameter of the semi‑circle lies exactly on the fold line and the curved portion extends to the right. What is the shape of the hole after the paper is opened?

A. A full circle touching the centre of the paper
B. A horizontal oval
C. A full circle with a vertical slit
D. Two separate semi‑circles, one on each half, their diameters parallel

Correct Answer: A
Step‑by‑step:

Single fold, fold line vertical. The semi‑circle’s diameter is on the fold, so the cut straddles the fold line.

On opening, the semi‑circle is reflected across the fold. The two halves join seamlessly because they share the diameter.

The resulting hole is a perfect full circle, centred on the fold line.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

Q27. (Medium)
A paper is folded once diagonally. A small square is cut out from the resulting triangle, with one side of the square lying on the fold line. When the paper is unfolded, which of the following best describes the hole?

A. A square
B. A rectangle whose length is twice its width
C. A rhombus
D. Two separate squares joined at one corner

Correct Answer: B
Step‑by‑step:

Diagonal fold. The square cut has one side exactly on the fold line.

Mirrored across the diagonal, the square produces a rectangle. The side on the fold becomes the centre line; the width equals the side of the square, and the length equals twice the side.
Source: RRB Group‑D 2018

Q28. (Medium)
A paper is folded first vertically, then horizontally. A cut is made that removes a small isosceles right triangle from the corner that is formed by the two folded edges (i.e., the corner where both fold lines meet). After fully opening, how many triangular cut‑outs are visible?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 4
D. 8

Correct Answer: C
Step‑by‑step:

The cut removes a triangle at the corner that lies on both fold lines.

Unfold last fold (horizontal): the triangle is reflected, producing two triangles sharing the horizontal fold line.

Unfold first fold (vertical): each of those two triangles is reflected across the vertical fold, resulting in four triangles, one at each corner of the original square.
Source: SSC CGL 2020 (Medium)

Q29. (Medium)
A circular paper is folded into a semi‑circle, then into a quarter‑circle. A punch is made through all layers at a point exactly on the curved edge of the quarter‑circle (away from any fold). How many holes will be there when the paper is opened completely?

A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8

Correct Answer: B
Step‑by‑step:

Two folds: first yields semi‑circle, second yields quarter‑circle. The punch is on the curved edge, not touching any fold line.

Undo the second fold (the last fold): the hole is mirrored across that fold line, resulting in two holes along the curved edge of the semi‑circle.

Undo the first fold: each of those two holes is mirrored across the first fold line, giving a total of four holes symmetrically placed along the original circle’s circumference.
Source: RRB NTPC 2019

Q30. (Medium)
A square paper is folded once horizontally. On the folded rectangle, a cut is made that removes a strip parallel to the fold line, starting from the open edge and ending 1 cm before the fold. After unfolding, what is the shape of the cut‑out area?

A. A single horizontal strip across the paper
B. Two separate horizontal strips, one above and one below the fold, with a gap in the middle
C. A rectangle spanning the width of the paper
D. Two vertical strips

Correct Answer: B
Step‑by‑step:

Fold is horizontal. The cut strip is parallel to the fold but does not touch it (ends 1 cm before).

The strip is entirely on one side of the fold line.

Mirroring across the fold produces an identical strip on the other half, but because the cut did not reach the fold line, the two strips are separated by an uncut region along the fold.

Thus, unfolded paper shows two separate horizontal strips, symmetric about the fold line.
Source: SSC GD 2019

Q31. (Medium – Examiner’s Trap: Touch/Not‑touch illusion)
A square paper is folded along the vertical centre line. A small circle is cut out on the folded paper such that its edge is extremely close to the fold line but does not actually touch it. What is the result after opening?

A. Two separate circles, very close to the centre line
B. One elongated oval hole
C. A single circle exactly on the centre line
D. Two circles that overlap to form a figure‑8

Correct Answer: A
Step‑by‑step:

The cut circle does not touch the fold. Therefore the mirror image creates a second circle on the other half, separated by a thin gap.

The two circles are separate, not joined. Options B, C, D assume some merging, which would only happen if the circle touched the fold.
Trap: Proximity creates the illusion of merging; the examiner exploits the tendency to assume touching when very close.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021 (Medium)

Q37. (Medium – Examiner’s Trap: Rotation vs Reflection)
