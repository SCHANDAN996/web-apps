126. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Square, Rhombus, Quadrilateral?
(a) Square inside Rhombus, Rhombus inside Quadrilateral (b) Square inside Quadrilateral, Rhombus inside Quadrilateral, separate (c) Rhombus inside Quadrilateral, Square inside Rhombus (d) Square and Rhombus overlapping inside Quadrilateral
Answer: (a)
Solution: All squares are rhombuses (a square is an equilateral rectangle/rhombus), and all rhombuses are quadrilaterals. Hence, three concentric nested circles: Square inside Rhombus inside Quadrilateral.

(a) Only I and II follow (b) All I, II, III follow (c) Only II and III follow (d) Only I and III follow
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pencils that are pens cannot be erasers (I follows). Sharpeners that are erasers cannot be pens (II follows). The possibility of all sharpeners being erasers is valid (III follows). All follow.
