26. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Cricket, Bat, Ball?
(a) Three separate circles (b) One large circle containing two separate circles (c) Three overlapping circles (d) One circle inside another, third separate
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is the large circle containing two circles, but bat and ball are equipment, not types of cricket. None of the three is a subclass of another, so the answer is three separate circles.

27. Choose the Venn diagram that correctly depicts: Doctors, Stethoscope, Hospital.
(a) Three overlapping circles (b) One circle inside another, both inside a third (c) One large circle containing one circle, third overlapping (d) Three separate circles
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to overlap doctors with hospitals because doctors work there, but a workplace is not a class relationship. A stethoscope is a tool, a doctor is a person, a hospital is a place, so the answer is three separate circles.

30. In a group of 150 people, 90 like tea, 70 like coffee, 50 like both. How many like neither?
(a) 30 (b) 20 (c) 40 (d) 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to subtract only the "both" figure from the total. People liking at least one = 90 + 70 − 50 = 110, so neither = 150 − 110, and the answer is 40.

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is II, which feels true in real life, but syllogisms use only the given statements; the apples that are not oranges could be anything. Some apples are oranges and all oranges are fruits, so some apples are fruits, and the answer is only I follows.
