101. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Shirt, Cotton, Fabric?
(a) Cotton inside Fabric, Shirt overlapping Fabric (b) Shirt inside Fabric, Cotton inside Fabric, separate (c) Shirt inside Cotton, Cotton inside Fabric (d) Cotton inside Fabric, Shirt separate
Answer: (a)
Solution: All cotton is fabric. Shirt is made of fabric; some shirts are cotton, some are not. So Shirt partially overlaps Fabric, and Cotton is a subset of Fabric.

105. [Trap] Which Venn diagram represents the sets Chair, Table, Wood?
(a) Chair and Table separate, Wood overlapping both (b) Chair and Table inside Wood, separate (c) Chair and Table overlapping Wood, all three overlap (d) Chair inside Table, Wood separate
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chair and Table are distinct furniture items (disjoint classes). Wood is a material; chairs and tables can be made of wood, so Wood overlaps with both Chair and Table. Trap: Many assume Chair and Table are made of wood, so they might put them inside Wood, but that would mean all chairs and tables are wood, which is false.

121. [Hard] Statements: All apples are fruits. No fruit is a vegetable. Some vegetables are green. Conclusions: I. Some green things are not fruits. II. Some apples are not vegetables.

Solution: II: All apples are fruits, no fruit is vegetable → no apple is vegetable. That means all apples are not vegetables, so “some apples are not vegetables” is true (in fact all). So II follows. This is hard because of the conversion of “No fruit is vegetable” to “No vegetable is fruit”, allowing the chain.

124. [Hard] In a housing society of 500 families, each family owns at most one type of vehicle: car, bike, or cycle. The data from a Venn diagram (three mutually exclusive sets? Actually "at most one" means no family owns more than one type, so sets are disjoint). The number of families owning car is 180, bike is 210, cycle is 110. How many families own exactly one vehicle?
(a) 400 (b) 300 (c) 250 (d) 500
Answer: (d)
Solution: Since "at most one" means no overlap, the sets are disjoint. The numbers given sum to 180+210+110 = 500, which equals total families. So all families own exactly one vehicle (no one owns none, because total matches). Thus exactly one vehicle = 500.
Source: PYQ-style
