101. Which Venn diagram best represents the relationship among: Women, Mothers, Doctors?
(a) Women inside Mothers, Doctors separate (b) Mothers inside Women, Doctors completely separate (c) Three separate circles (d) Mothers inside Women, Doctors overlapping Women
Answer: (d)
Solution: Every mother is a woman, so Mothers lies inside Women. Some women are doctors and some are not, so Doctors overlaps Women. The trap is keeping Doctors fully separate, ignoring that many doctors are women. So the answer is Mothers inside Women with Doctors overlapping Women.
Source: PYQ-style

103. In a group of 300 people, 180 know Hindi, 140 know English, 80 know both. How many know neither language?
(a) 60 (b) 40 (c) 50 (d) 70
Answer: (a)
Solution: People knowing at least one language = 180 + 140 − 80 = 240. Neither = 300 − 240 = 60. The trap is forgetting to subtract the overlap, which gives 320 and an impossible result. So the answer is 60.

124. [Hard] In a housing society of 500 families, each family owns at most one type of vehicle among car, bike and cycle. The number of families owning a car is 180, a bike is 210, and a cycle is 110. How many families own exactly one vehicle?
(a) 400 (b) 500 (c) 250 (d) 300
Answer: (b)
Solution: "At most one" means the three sets are disjoint, so total owners = 180 + 210 + 110 = 500, which equals all families. Hence every family owns exactly one vehicle. The trap is hunting for an overlap to subtract when the condition forbids any overlap. So the answer is 500.

125. [Hard] Statements: Some A are B. All B are C. No C is D. Conclusions: I. Some A are C. II. Some A are not D. III. Some B are D.
(a) Both I and II follow (b) Only I follows (c) All three follow (d) Only II follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: The A that are B are also C, so some A are C and I follows. Those same A are C, and no C is D, so some A are not D and II follows; but all B are C and no C is D, so no B is D and III fails. The trap is accepting III by ignoring the universal negative between C and D. So the answer is that both I and II follow.
Source: PYQ-style
