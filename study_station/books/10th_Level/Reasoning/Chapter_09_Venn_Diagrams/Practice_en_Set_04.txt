76. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Sparrow, Bird, Ostrich?
(a) Sparrow inside Ostrich, Ostrich inside Bird (b) Sparrow inside Bird, Ostrich inside Bird, no overlap between Sparrow and Ostrich (c) Three separate circles (d) Sparrow and Ostrich overlapping inside Bird
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is the overlapping one, but a sparrow can never be an ostrich, so the two subsets must be disjoint. Both are distinct types of birds, so the answer is two separate small circles inside one large Bird circle.

Answer: (d)
