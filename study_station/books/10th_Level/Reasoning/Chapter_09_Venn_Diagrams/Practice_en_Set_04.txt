76. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Sparrow, Bird, Ostrich?
(a) Sparrow inside Bird, Ostrich inside Bird, no overlap between Sparrow and Ostrich (b) Sparrow inside Ostrich, Ostrich inside Bird (c) Three separate circles (d) Sparrow and Ostrich overlapping inside Bird
Answer: (a)
Solution: Both sparrow and ostrich are distinct types of birds. They are subsets of Bird, and they are disjoint (a sparrow cannot be an ostrich). So the correct diagram is one large circle ‘Bird’ containing two separate smaller circles.
Source: PYQ-style
