76. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Sparrow, Bird, Ostrich?
निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा वेन आरेख गौरैया, पक्षी, शुतुरमुर्ग के बीच संबंध को सर्वोत्तम रूप से दर्शाता है?
(A) Sparrow inside Bird, Ostrich inside Bird, no overlap between Sparrow and Ostrich
(B) Sparrow inside Ostrich, Ostrich inside Bird
(C) Three separate circles
(D) Sparrow and Ostrich overlapping inside Bird

Answer: (A) Sparrow inside Bird, Ostrich inside Bird, no overlap between Sparrow and Ostrich.
Solution: Both sparrow and ostrich are distinct types of birds. They are subsets of Bird, and they are disjoint (a sparrow cannot be an ostrich). So the correct diagram is one large circle ‘Bird’ containing two separate smaller circles.
