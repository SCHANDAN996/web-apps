Set 1: Venn Diagrams MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. 40 people like apples, 30 like oranges, 10 like both. How many like at least one?
(a) 60 (b) 70 (c) 80 (d) 50
Answer: (a) 60
Solution: n(A∪B) = 40 + 30 − 10 = 60.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. From Q1's data, how many like only apples?
(a) 30 (b) 40 (c) 20 (d) 10
Answer: (a) 30
Solution: Only apples = 40 − 10 = 30.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Which relationship fits: Dog, Cat, Animal?
(a) Three separate (b) Dog and Cat separate, both inside Animal (c) All overlapping (d) Nested
Answer: (b) Dog and Cat separate, both inside Animal
Solution: Both are animals but different from each other.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. 20 like A, 15 like B, 5 like both. How many like at least one?
(a) 30 (b) 35 (c) 25 (d) 40
Answer: (a) 30
Solution: n(A∪B) = 20 + 15 − 5 = 30.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. Out of 50 people, 20 like A, 15 like B, 5 like both. How many like neither?
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 15 (d) 30
Answer: (a) 20
Solution: At least one = 20 + 15 − 5 = 30. Neither = 50 − 30 = 20.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. The formula for the number in A or B is:
(a) n(A) + n(B) (b) n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B) (c) n(A) − n(B) (d) n(A) × n(B)
Answer: (b) n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B)
Solution: Subtract the shared part once.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q7. 30 like tea, 20 like coffee, 8 like both. How many like only coffee?
(a) 12 (b) 8 (c) 20 (d) 22
Answer: (a) 12
Solution: Only coffee = 20 − 8 = 12.
Source: SSC MTS

Q8. Which diagram shows "all cats are animals"?
(a) Two separate circles (b) Two overlapping circles (c) Cat circle inside Animal (d) Animal circle inside Cat
Answer: (c) Cat circle inside Animal
Solution: "All cats are animals" → the Cat circle sits fully inside the Animal circle.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q13. Which relationship fits: Rose, Flower, Plant?
(a) Three separate (b) All overlapping (c) Rose inside Flower inside Plant (d) Two separate, one inside
Answer: (c) Rose inside Flower inside Plant

Q14. Out of 100 people, 70 like A, 60 like B, 40 like both. How many like neither?

Solution: At least one = 70 + 60 − 40 = 90. Neither = 100 − 90 = 10.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q18. (Medium) Which diagram fits: some men are doctors, some doctors are engineers, some engineers are men?
