1. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: India, Asia, Earth?
(a) One circle inside another, and both inside a third (concentric circles) (b) Three separate circles (c) Two separate circles inside one large circle (d) Three mutually overlapping circles
Answer: (a)
Solution: India is entirely within Asia, and Asia is entirely on Earth. Hence, they form three concentric nested circles.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Which Venn diagram shows the correct relationship among: Pen, Pencil, Eraser?
(a) Three mutually overlapping circles (b) Three separate (mutually disjoint) circles (c) One large circle containing two smaller circles (d) Two intersecting circles, third separate
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pen, pencil, and eraser are distinct stationery items with no subset or overlap relation. Hence, three separate circles.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Select the Venn diagram that best illustrates the relationship: Women, Mothers, Engineers.
(a) Three completely separate circles (b) Two separate circles inside a large circle (c) One circle (Mothers) completely inside another (Women), and a third circle (Engineers) intersecting both (d) Three separate circles with no nested subset
Answer: (c)
Solution: All mothers are women (Mothers ⊂ Women). Some women and some mothers are engineers. Hence, the Engineers circle intersects both circles.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Which Venn diagram best represents the classes: Doctors, Surgeons, Neurologists?
(a) Three concentric circles (b) Two overlapping circles inside a large circle (c) Three mutually overlapping circles (d) One large circle (Doctors) containing two smaller non-overlapping circles
Answer: (d)
Solution: Both surgeons and neurologists are doctors, but they represent distinct medical specializations. Hence, two disjoint circles inside a large circle.

5. Choose the correct diagram for: Players, Singers, Doctors.
(a) Three mutually overlapping circles (triple intersection) (b) Three completely separate circles (c) One circle inside another, third separate (d) Two overlapping circles, third separate
Answer: (a)
Solution: A person can be any combination of player, singer, and doctor (including all three). Hence, three mutually overlapping circles.

6. In a geometric Venn diagram: Triangle = Teachers, Circle = Graduates, Rectangle = Women. Numbers are: only teachers = 4, only graduates = 6, only women = 7, teachers and graduates (not women) = 3, graduates and women (not teachers) = 5, all three = 1. How many graduate women are NOT teachers?
(a) 6 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: Graduate women who are not teachers corresponds to the region common to Circle and Rectangle outside Triangle = 5.

7. In a Venn diagram, circle A = Men, circle B = Employed, circle C = Educated. Which region represents men who are educated but NOT employed?
(a) Region only inside circle A (b) Intersection of A and B (c) Intersection of A and C excluding B (d) Intersection of all three circles
Answer: (c)
Solution: Men (A) who are educated (C) but not employed (outside B) corresponds to (A ∩ C) excluding B.

Solution: The region common to the circle and triangle outside the square contains the value 4.

9. In a survey, 55% of people like tea, 45% like coffee, and 20% like both. What percentage of people like neither tea nor coffee?
(a) 20% (b) 15% (c) 25% (d) 30%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Union = 55% + 45% - 20% = 80%. Neither = 100% - 80% = 20%.

10. In a Venn diagram of families using three energy sources: LPG, Electricity, and Solar, how many families use only LPG and Solar (and NOT Electricity)?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 10 (d) 15
Answer: (b)
Solution: The intersection of LPG and Solar circles excluding Electricity yields 12.

11. Statements: All flowers are plants. Some plants are trees. Conclusions: I. Some trees are flowers. II. Some plants are flowers.
(a) Only conclusion I follows (b) Both conclusions I and II follow (c) Only conclusion II follows (d) Neither conclusion follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: Since Flowers ⊂ Plants, Some plants are definitely flowers (II follows). There is no guaranteed overlap between Trees and Flowers (I does not follow).
Source: PYQ-style

12. Statements: No book is a pen. All pens are pencils. Conclusions: I. Some books are pencils. II. Some pencils are not books.
(a) Only conclusion I follows (b) Both conclusions follow (c) Neither conclusion follows (d) Only conclusion II follows
Answer: (d)
Solution: Pens ⊂ Pencils, and no pen is a book. Those pencils which are pens can never be books. Hence, 'Some pencils are not books' definitely follows.
Source: PYQ-style

13. Statements: Some actors are singers. All singers are dancers. Conclusions: I. Some actors are dancers. II. All dancers are singers.
(a) Only conclusion I follows (b) Only conclusion II follows (c) Both conclusions follow (d) Neither conclusion follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: Actors who are singers are necessarily dancers, so Some actors are dancers (I follows). All dancers are singers is not necessarily true (II does not follow).

14. Statements: All mangoes are fruits. No fruit is a vegetable. Conclusions: I. No mango is a vegetable. II. Some vegetables are mangoes.
(a) Only conclusion II follows (b) Only conclusion I follows (c) Both conclusions follow (d) Neither conclusion follows
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mangoes ⊂ Fruits, and Fruits and Vegetables are completely disjoint. Thus, no mango can be a vegetable. Only I follows.
Source: PYQ-style

15. Statements: Some cats are dogs. All dogs are rats. No rat is a bat. Conclusions: I. Some cats are rats. II. No cat is a bat.
(a) Only conclusion II follows (b) Both conclusions follow (c) Only conclusion I follows (d) Neither conclusion follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: The part of cats that are dogs is inside rats, so Some cats are rats (I follows). Cats outside dogs and rats could intersect bats, so II is not definite.
Source: PYQ-style

18. Which option fits the words: Car, Vehicle, Petrol?

19. In a Venn diagram with three intersecting circles A, B, and C, how many distinct bounded regions are formed?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 7 (d) 9
Answer: (c)
Solution: Three intersecting circles form exactly 7 distinct bounded regions: 3 individual only, 3 pairwise only, and 1 triple intersection.

20. If 'only A' = 5, 'only B' = 7, 'only C' = 9, 'A and B only' = 3, 'A and C only' = 2, 'B and C only' = 4, and 'all three' = 1, how many total elements are in set A?
(a) 9 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total in A = only A + (A∩B only) + (A∩C only) + all three = 5 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 11.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Statements: All windows are doors. No door is a wall. Some walls are roofs. Conclusions: I. Some roofs are windows. II. No window is a wall.
(a) Only conclusion I follows (b) Only conclusion II follows (c) Both conclusions follow (d) Neither conclusion follows
Answer: (b)
Solution: Windows ⊂ Doors, and Doors ∩ Walls = ∅, which implies Windows ∩ Walls = ∅ (II follows). Roofs and Windows have no definite intersection (I does not follow).
Source: PYQ-style
