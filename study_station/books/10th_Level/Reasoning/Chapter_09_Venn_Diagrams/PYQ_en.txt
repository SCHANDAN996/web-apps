PYQ Analysis: Venn Diagrams (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Venn diagrams give 1–3 questions per exam. Numerical (counting) and logical-relationship types are both common. The trap is double counting and the "only" vs "whole circle" confusion.

Q1. 30 children play cricket, 20 play football, and 10 play both. How many play at least one game?
(a) 40 (b) 50 (c) 60 (d) 30
Answer: (a) 40
Solution: n(A∪B) = 30 + 20 − 10 = 40.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. From Q1's data, how many play only cricket?
(a) 30 (b) 20 (c) 10 (d) 40
Answer: (b) 20
Solution: Only cricket = 30 − 10 (both) = 20.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Which relationship fits: Dog, Cat, Animal?
(a) Three separate circles (b) Dog and Cat separate, both inside Animal (c) All three overlapping (d) Nested one inside another
Answer: (b) Dog and Cat separate, both inside Animal
Solution: Dogs and cats are different, but both are animals — two separate circles inside the Animal circle.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. 50 people drink tea, 30 drink coffee, 20 drink both. How many drink at least one?
(a) 60 (b) 80 (c) 100 (d) 40
Answer: (a) 60
Solution: n(A∪B) = 50 + 30 − 20 = 60.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. The formula for the number in A or B is:
(a) n(A) + n(B) (b) n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B) (c) n(A) − n(B) (d) n(A) × n(B)
Answer: (b) n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B)
Solution: We subtract the shared part once to avoid double counting.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. In a class of 100, 60 study Hindi, 50 study English, 30 study both. How many study neither?
(a) 20 (b) 10 (c) 30 (d) 40
Answer: (a) 20
Solution: At least one = 60 + 50 − 30 = 80. Neither = 100 − 80 = 20.
Source: SSC CGL

Q7. Which relationship fits: Table, Chair, Furniture?
(a) Three separate circles (b) Table and Chair separate, both inside Furniture (c) All overlapping (d) Nested
Answer: (b) Table and Chair separate, both inside Furniture
Solution: A table and a chair are different, but both are furniture.
Source: SSC MTS

Q8. 40 like tea, 25 like coffee, 15 like both. How many like only coffee?
(a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 25 (d) 40
Answer: (a) 10
Solution: Only coffee = 25 − 15 = 10.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. Which diagram shows "some doctors are men"?
(a) Two separate circles (b) Two overlapping circles (c) One circle inside another (d) Three circles
Answer: (b) Two overlapping circles
Solution: "Some" means partial overlap — the two circles cross each other.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. The union formula for three groups is n(A∪B∪C) =
(a) sum only (b) sum − pairwise shared + all-three shared (c) sum − all-three (d) sum + pairwise
Answer: (b) sum − pairwise shared + all-three shared
Solution: Subtract the pairwise overlaps, then add back the triple overlap counted out too many times.
Source: SSC CGL
