### 2. Exam-Wise Distribution & Characteristics

1. **SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD) – 45% Weightage**
   - Direct identification of relationships among three classes of items.
   - High speed required: average 15–25 seconds per question.
   - Common patterns: Animals, professions, geographical entities, vehicles.

2. **Railway (RRB NTPC, Group D) – 25% Weightage**
   - Geometrical diagrams with numbers/letters inside overlapping circles, triangles, rectangles.
   - Requires precise calculation of regions ("only circle", "both triangle and square but not circle").

3. **Banking (IBPS, SBI, RRB) – 20% Weightage**
   - Syllogism based on Venn Diagrams ("Some", "All", "No", "Only a few").

4. **State Police & Paramilitary Exams – 10% Weightage**
   - Direct word-to-diagram matching with straightforward everyday items.

### 4. Top 10 High-Yield PYQs (with Detailed Solutions)

#### **Question 1: Word-to-Diagram (SSC CGL 2019)**
**Question:** Which diagram best represents the relationship between: **Mother, Female, Doctor**?
- **Analysis:** All mothers are females (M is a subset of F). Some females and some mothers are doctors.
- **Answer:** One circle completely inside another (Mother inside Female), intersected by a third circle (Doctor).

#### **Question 2: Word-to-Diagram (SSC CGL 2018)**
**Question:** Which diagram best represents: **Musicians, Instrumentalists, Violinists**?
- **Analysis:** All violinists play an instrument (Violinists ⊂ Instrumentalists). All instrumentalists are musicians (Instrumentalists ⊂ Musicians).
- **Answer:** Three concentric circles (Violinists inside Instrumentalists inside Musicians).

#### **Question 3: Geometric Counting (SSC CHSL 2020)**
**Question:** In a diagram with Circle = Employed, Triangle = Hardworking, Rectangle = Educated. Which region represents educated and employed people who are NOT hardworking?
- **Analysis:** Find the intersection of Circle and Rectangle that lies outside Triangle.
- **Answer:** Region strictly inside Circle ∩ Rectangle and outside Triangle.

#### **Question 4: Word-to-Diagram (Railway NTPC 2019)**
**Question:** Which diagram best represents: **India, Delhi, Paris**?
- **Analysis:** Delhi is inside India. Paris is completely disjoint from India and Delhi.
- **Answer:** One circle inside another (Delhi inside India), and a separate disjoint circle (Paris).

#### **Question 5: Geometric Counting (SSC MTS 2021)**
**Question:** Circle represents Singers, Triangle represents Dancers, Square represents Actors. How many singers are dancers but NOT actors?
- **Analysis:** Identify the region inside both Circle and Triangle, but outside Square.
- **Answer:** The number located exclusively in the overlap of Circle and Triangle excluding Square.

#### **Question 6: Word-to-Diagram (SSC CGL 2022)**
**Question:** Which diagram represents: **Reptiles, Snakes, Lizards**?
- **Analysis:** Both snakes and lizards are distinct classes of reptiles (Snakes ⊂ Reptiles, Lizards ⊂ Reptiles, Snakes ∩ Lizards = ∅).
- **Answer:** One large circle (Reptiles) containing two mutually disjoint non-overlapping circles inside (Snakes and Lizards).

#### **Question 7: Geometric Counting (Railway Group D 2018)**
**Question:** Given circles representing English speakers, Hindi speakers, and Sanskrit speakers. Total English speakers = 50, Hindi = 60, both = 20. How many speak English only?
- **Analysis:** English only = Total English - Both = 50 - 20 = 30.
- **Answer:** 30.

#### **Question 8: Word-to-Diagram (SSC GD 2022)**
**Question:** Which diagram represents: **Vegetables, Tomato, Banana**?
- **Analysis:** Tomato is a vegetable (botanically a fruit, but classified as culinary vegetable in standard syllabus). Banana is a fruit, disjoint from vegetables.
- **Answer:** One circle inside another (Tomato inside Vegetable), and one separate circle (Banana).

#### **Question 9: Three Sets Overlap (SSC CHSL 2021)**
**Question:** In a group of 100 students, 40 play football, 30 play cricket, and 10 play both. How many play neither?
- **Analysis:** Union = 40 + 30 - 10 = 60. Neither = 100 - 60 = 40.
- **Answer:** 40.

#### **Question 10: Word-to-Diagram (SSC CGL 2024)**
**Question:** Which diagram best represents: **Teachers, Writers, Musicians**?
- **Analysis:** A person can be any combination of all three: some teachers are writers, some writers are musicians, and some people are all three.
- **Answer:** Three mutually overlapping intersecting circles with a common central region.

### 5. Time Management & Exam Strategy

1. **First 15 Seconds:** Determine which categories are completely disjoint. Separating disjoint classes immediately eliminates 2 to 3 wrong option diagrams.
2. **Key Keywords in Geometric Diagrams:**
   - **"Only"**: Pick the unique single region belonging solely to that shape.
   - **"And"**: Look for the intersection (overlap) of the named shapes.
   - **"Not"**: Strictly eliminate everything within the boundary of the excluded shape.
3. **Accuracy Checklist:** Verify whether a subset relationship (All A are B) or partial intersection (Some A are B) applies before confirming the option.
