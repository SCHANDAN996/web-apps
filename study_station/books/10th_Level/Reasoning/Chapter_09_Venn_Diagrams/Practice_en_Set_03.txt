51. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Sun, Moon, Stars?
निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा वेन आरेख सूर्य, चंद्रमा, तारों के बीच संबंध को सर्वोत्तम रूप से दर्शाता है?
(A) Three separate circles
(B) One large circle containing two overlapping circles
(C) Three overlapping circles
(D) One circle inside another, third separate

Answer: (A) Three separate circles.
Solution: Sun is a star, but "Stars" as a class includes many celestial bodies. Sun is a member of Stars; Moon is a natural satellite; they are distinct classes with no subset relationships among the given words as general categories. In standard logical Venn, Sun, Moon, and Stars are treated as disjoint because Moon is not a star, Sun is a specific star but the class "Stars" is larger and "Sun" is not a subclass; so three separate circles.
Source: PYQ-style

56. Statements: All pencils are pens. Some pens are erasers. No eraser is a sharpener.
Conclusions: I. Some pencils are erasers. II. Some pens are not sharpeners.
कथन: सभी पेंसिल पेन हैं। कुछ पेन इरेज़र हैं। कोई इरेज़र शार्पनर नहीं है।
निष्कर्ष: I. कुछ पेंसिल इरेज़र हैं। II. कुछ पेन शार्पनर नहीं हैं।
(A) Only I follows
(B) Only II follows
(C) Both I and II follow
(D) Neither I nor II follows

Answer: (B) Only II follows.
Solution: I: Pencils ⊆ Pens. Some pens are erasers does not guarantee that the pencil part overlaps erasers. So I false. II: The pens that are erasers (some pens) are definitely not sharpeners (since no eraser is sharpener). So those pens are not sharpeners. Hence II true.
Source: PYQ-style

(A) 14
(B) 12
(C) 10

Solution: At least two = (exactly two) + (all three) = (4+5+3) + 2 = 12+2 = 14.

(A) Only I follows
(B) Only II follows
(C) Both I and II follow
(D) Neither I nor II follows

66. [Trap] Which Venn diagram best fits Fruit, Apple, Seed?
फल, सेब, बीज के लिए सर्वश्रेष्ठ वेन आरेख कौन-सा है?
(A) Apple inside Fruit, Seed inside Apple
(B) Apple inside Fruit, Seed separate
(C) Seed inside Fruit, Apple inside Seed
(D) Apple inside Fruit, Seed overlapping Fruit

Answer: (A) Apple inside Fruit, Seed inside Apple.
Solution: All apples are fruits. Seeds are a part of an apple; in class logic, a seed is not a subclass of apple but can be considered a component. However, standard competitive exams often treat "Seed" as a part, so they might show it separate. But the most common answer in such word grouping: Apple ⊆ Fruit, and Seed is a part of Apple, so nested. Many students get confused because seed is also a part of fruit directly. Still, the best representation is Apple inside Fruit, Seed inside Apple (since seeds are inside apples).
Trap: Part-whole relationship might confuse; students may think Seed is separate from Apple or Fruit, but the most plausible logical nesting is used.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Statements: All dogs are animals. Some animals are wild. No wild creature is pet.
Conclusions: I. Some dogs are wild. II. No dog is pet.
कथन: सभी कुत्ते जानवर हैं। कुछ जानवर जंगली हैं। कोई जंगली प्राणी पालतू नहीं है।
निष्कर्ष: I. कुछ कुत्ते जंगली हैं। II. कोई कुत्ता पालतू नहीं है।
(A) Only I follows
(B) Only II follows
(C) Both I and II follow
(D) Neither I nor II follows
