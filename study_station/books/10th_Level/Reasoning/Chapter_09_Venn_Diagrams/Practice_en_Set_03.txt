51. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Sun, Moon, Stars?
(a) Sun inside Stars, Moon separate (b) Three separate circles (c) Three overlapping circles (d) Moon inside Stars, Sun separate
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is three separate circles, but the Sun is itself a star, so the Sun circle lies inside the Stars circle. The Moon is a natural satellite, not a star, so it stays separate. Hence the answer is Sun inside Stars with Moon separate.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Choose the Venn diagram for Mammals, Whales, Fishes.
(a) Three overlapping circles (b) Whales inside Fishes, Mammals separate (c) Whales inside Mammals, Fishes overlapping Mammals (d) Whales inside Mammals, Fishes separate from Mammals
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is putting whales with fishes because whales live in water, but biologically all whales are mammals. Fishes form a separate class with no overlap with mammals. So the answer is Whales inside Mammals with Fishes separate.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

53. In a group of 200 people, 120 speak Hindi, 100 speak English, 60 speak both. How many speak neither?
(a) 20 (b) 10 (c) 40 (d) 30
Answer: (c)
Solution: People speaking at least one language = 120 + 100 − 60 = 160. The trap is forgetting to subtract the overlap, which gives 220 and an absurd result. Neither = 200 − 160, so the answer is 40.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

54. In a Venn diagram, circle A = Men, circle B = Players, circle C = Artists. Regions: only Men = 10, only Players = 8, only Artists = 12, Men and Players only = 5, Players and Artists only = 4, Men and Artists only = 6, all three = 3. How many are either men or players but not artists?
(a) 21 (b) 28 (c) 25 (d) 23
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding the all-three region, but it lies inside Artists and must be excluded. Take regions inside Men or Players but outside Artists: 10 + 8 + 5. So the answer is 23.

(a) Both I and II follow (b) Only II follows (c) Only I follows (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming the pencil part of pens must touch the eraser part; it need not, so I fails. The pens that are erasers can never be sharpeners, so some pens are definitely not sharpeners. Hence only II follows.

(a) 10 (b) 14 (c) 12 (d) 16

Solution: The trap is forgetting the all-three region, which is also in at least two sets. At least two = (4 + 5 + 3) + 2. So the answer is 14.

61. In a survey, 70% like cricket, 60% like football, 40% like both. What percentage like at most one?
(a) 50% (b) 70% (c) 40% (d) 60%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 70 + 60 − 40 = 90%, which is at least one, not at most one. At most one = 100% − those who like both = 100% − 40%. So the answer is 60%.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics
