126. Which of the following Venn diagrams best represents the relationship among: Square, Rhombus, Quadrilateral?
निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा वेन आरेख वर्ग, समचतुर्भुज, चतुर्भुज के बीच संबंध को सर्वोत्तम रूप से दर्शाता है?

127. Statements: Some pencils are pens. No pen is an eraser. All erasers are sharpeners.
Conclusions: I. Some pencils are not erasers. II. Some sharpeners are not pens. III. All sharpeners being erasers is a possibility.
कथन: कुछ पेंसिल पेन हैं। कोई पेन इरेज़र नहीं है। सभी इरेज़र शार्पनर हैं।
निष्कर्ष: I. कुछ पेंसिल इरेज़र नहीं हैं। II. कुछ शार्पनर पेन नहीं हैं। III. सभी शार्पनर के इरेज़र होने की संभावना है।
(A) Only I and II follow
(B) Only II and III follow
(C) Only I and III follow
(D) All I, II, III follow

Answer: (D) All I, II, III follow.
Solution: I: The pencils that are pens are not erasers (since no pen is an eraser), so some pencils are not erasers. True. II: All erasers are sharpeners, and no eraser is a pen. Therefore those sharpeners that are erasers are not pens, so some sharpeners are not pens. True. III: "All sharpeners are erasers" is possible because the given statement "All erasers are sharpeners" does not prevent the sharpener set from being exactly the eraser set. No contradiction arises. So it is a possibility. Hence all follow.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

(B) Only III follows
(C) Only I and II follow
(D) All follow

Since A can be entirely placed inside D without touching B, "All A are D" is a possibility. So only III follows.

निष्कर्ष: I. कुछ बिल्लियाँ जानवर नहीं हैं। II. कुछ पेड़ जानवर नहीं हैं। III. कुछ बिल्लियों के पौधे होने की संभावना है।
(A) Only II follows
(B) Only II and III follow
(C) Only I and III follow
(D) All follow

Answer: (B) Only II and III follow.
Solution: I: "Some cats are not dogs" does not imply they are not animals. Cats could still all be animals. So I false. II: Some plants are trees, and plants are not animals (since no animal is plant implies plant ∩ animal = ∅). So those trees are not animals. True. III: "Some cats are plants" is a possibility. Cats and plants have no direct contradiction; cats are not forced to be animals by the statements (we only know some cats are not dogs, not that all cats are animals). Actually, do we know cats are animals? No statement says "All cats are animals". So cats could be plants? Real-world knowledge aside, logically it's possible. So III is a possibility. Thus II and III follow.

What is the ratio of the number of persons who are either post‑graduates or urban but not govt. employees to those who are govt. employees but neither post‑graduate nor urban?
*नीचे दिए गए वेन आरेख में P = स्नातकोत्तर, Q = सरकारी कर्मचारी, R = शहरी। संख्याएँ: केवल P=33, केवल Q=27, केवल R=36, P∩Q केवल=15, Q∩R केवल=18, P∩R केवल=12, तीनों=9। उन व्यक्तियों का अनुपात कितना है जो या तो स्नातकोत्तर हैं या शहरी लेकिन सरकारी कर्मचारी नहीं, और जो सरकारी कर्मचारी हैं लेकिन न तो स्नातकोत्तर और न ही शहरी?*

Answer: (B) 81:27 which simplifies to 3:1, but the options are likely unsimplified. (B) 81:27.
