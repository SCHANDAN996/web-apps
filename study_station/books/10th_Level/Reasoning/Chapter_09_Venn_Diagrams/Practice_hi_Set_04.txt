Conclusions: I. Some actors are singers. II. Some dancers are not painters.

100. [जाल] *कथन: सभी शिक्षक स्नातक हैं। कोई स्नातक छात्र नहीं है। कुछ छात्र एथलीट हैं।*
निष्कर्ष: I. कोई शिक्षक छात्र नहीं है। II. कुछ एथलीट स्नातक नहीं हैं।
Statements: All teachers are graduates. No graduate is a student. Some students are athletes.
Conclusions: I. No teacher is a student. II. Some athletes are not graduates.
(a) केवल I अनुसरण करता है (b) दोनों अनुसरण करते हैं (c) केवल II अनुसरण करता है (d) कोई भी अनुसरण नहीं करता
उत्तर: (b)
हल: I: Teacher ⊆ Graduate, Graduate ∩ Student = ∅ → Teacher ∩ Student = ∅. True. II: Some students are athletes, and students are not graduates (since no graduate is student). So those athletes (who are students) are not graduates. Hence some athletes are not graduates. True. Trap: The possibility that athletes could also be graduates might confuse, but the existence of student-athletes forces some athletes to be non-graduates.
स्रोत: PYQ-शैली
