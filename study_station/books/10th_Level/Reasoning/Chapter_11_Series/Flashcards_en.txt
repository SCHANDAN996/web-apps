Front: What is the first step to solve a series?
Back: Write the difference (or ratio) between consecutive terms.

Front: What comes after 2, 5, 8, 11?
Back: 14 (add 3 each time).

Front: What comes after 3, 6, 12, 24?
Back: 48 (multiply by 2 each time).

Front: Which letter comes after A, C, E, G?
Back: I (add 2 positions each time).

Front: What type of series is 1, 4, 9, 16?
Back: Square series (1², 2², 3², 4²).

Front: What type of series is 1, 8, 27?
Back: Cube series (1³, 2³, 3³).

Front: What to write first in a letter series?
Back: Each letter's position number (A=1…Z=26).

Front: What is the rule of the Fibonacci series?
Back: Each term = sum of the previous two (1,1,2,3,5,8…).

Front: What are the first few terms of the prime series?
Back: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13…

Front: How do you spot an alternating series?
Back: One pattern on odd positions, another on even — check them separately.

Front: What comes after 2, 3, 5, 8, 12?
Back: 17 (differences +1,+2,+3,+4,+5).

Front: What is an alphanumeric series?
Back: Letters and numbers together — like A1, B2, C3, D4.

Front: How do you solve "wrong term" questions?
Back: Catch the full rule first, then the term that breaks it is the answer.

Front: If the plain difference doesn't work, what to check?
Back: Ratio (×), squares/cubes, or the difference-of-differences.

Front: What is the series mnemonic?
Back: "Write the difference, catch the rule".
