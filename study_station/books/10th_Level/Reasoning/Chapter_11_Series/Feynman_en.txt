# Feynman Technique: Series in Simple Words

Tell your younger brother: "A detective game — I say numbers, you catch the hidden rule. 2, 4, 6, 8… what's the rule? Each time 2 is added! So the next? 10. That's all a series is — sniffing out the hidden rule."

**Every time, write the difference between consecutive terms — the rule is often hidden right there.**

## The Biggest Trick: Write the Difference Below

Series: 3, 6, 12, 24
- Write the difference/ratio below: ×2, ×2, ×2 — rule found!
- Next = 24 × 2 = 48.

If the plain difference isn't constant, look at the difference-of-differences, or check squares/cubes.
