Q126. (Hard) (Hard) 2, 6, 15, 31, 56, ?
(a) 92 (b) 88 (c) 96 (d) 100
Answer: (a) 92
Solution: Differences: +4, +9, +16, +25 (squares) → next +36 → 56+36=92.

Q130. (Hard) (Hard) Find the wrong term: 6, 14, 30, 64, 126, 254

Answer: (a) 64

Q132. (Hard) (Hard) A, E, J, P, ?
(a) B (b) C (c) A (d) D
Answer: (c) A
Solution: Positions: A=1→E=5 (+4), E→J=10 (+5), J→P=16 (+6). Differences increase by 1 each time? Then next +7 → 23=W. Not in options. Instead, differences are consecutive primes: 4,5,7,11 → 16+11=27 → 27−26=1=A.
