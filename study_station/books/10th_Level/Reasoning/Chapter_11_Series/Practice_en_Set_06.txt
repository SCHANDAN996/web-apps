Questions 126–150: Hard

126. 2, 6, 15, 31, 56, ?
(a) 88 (b) 92 (c) 96 (d) 100
Answer: (b)
Solution: The differences are +4, +9, +16, +25, i.e. consecutive squares 2², 3², 4², 5². The next difference is 6² = 36, not 32, which is the trap behind 88. So the answer is 56 + 36 = 92.
Source: PYQ-style

127. 1, 3, 7, 15, 31, ?
(a) 62 (b) 65 (c) 64 (d) 63
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each term is previous × 2 + 1: 1×2+1=3, 3×2+1=7, 7×2+1=15, 15×2+1=31. The trap is doubling only, which gives 62. So the answer is 31×2+1 = 63.
Source: PYQ-style

130. Find the wrong term in the series: 6, 14, 30, 64, 126, 254
(a) 14 (b) 64 (c) 30 (d) 126
Answer: (b)
Solution: The pattern is 2ⁿ−2: 2³−2=6, 2⁴−2=14, 2⁵−2=30, 2⁶−2=62, 2⁷−2=126, 2⁸−2=254. The fourth term should be 62, not 64; 64 looks tempting because it is a familiar power of 2. So the wrong term is 64.
Source: PYQ-style

131. 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, ?
(a) 64 (b) 56 (c) 72 (d) 81
Answer: (a)
Solution: These are squares of natural numbers: 1², 2², 3², …, 7², so the next is 8². The trap is 81, which is 9² and skips a step. So the answer is 64.

132. Z, X, V, T, ?

141. B, G, L, Q, V, ?

Answer: (b)
Solution: Letters move forward by 5: B(2)→G(7)→L(12)→Q(17)→V(22)→27, and 27−26=1=A. The trap is Z, from stopping at 26 instead of wrapping around. So the answer is A.

143. 2, 15, 4, 12, 6, 9, 8, ?
(a) 5 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: This is a double series: odd positions 2, 4, 6, 8 increase by 2, while even positions 15, 12, 9 decrease by 3. The 8th term belongs to the even sub-series, and the trap is 5, from wrongly extending the odd sub-series. So the answer is 9−3 = 6.
