# Short Tricks: Series

Trick: Always write the difference (or ratio) between consecutive terms below — the rule appears there.

Trick: If the difference isn't constant, check — ×multiply, squares (1,4,9,16), cubes (1,8,27), or the "difference of differences".

Trick: In a letter series, write each letter's position (A=1…Z=26); then apply the same number rule.

Trick: Spot an alternating series — one pattern on odd positions, another on even. Check the two separately.

Trick: Memorise the prime series (2,3,5,7,11) and Fibonacci (1,1,2,3,5,8 — sum of the previous two).

Trick: In "wrong term" questions, first catch the full rule, then the term that breaks it is the answer.

Skip Strategy: If a very tangled series isn't solved in 40 seconds, skip it; do the simple add/multiply ones first.

💡 Mnemonic: "Write the difference, catch the rule" — the two steps of series.
