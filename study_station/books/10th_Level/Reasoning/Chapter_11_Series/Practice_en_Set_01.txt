Set 1: Series MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–25: Medium

Q1. 1, 3, 5, 7, ?
(a) 9 (b) 8 (c) 10 (d) 11
Answer: (a) 9
Solution: Add 2 each time: 7 + 2 = 9.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. 5, 10, 20, 40, ?
(a) 60 (b) 80 (c) 50 (d) 100
Answer: (b) 80
Solution: Multiply by 2 each time: 40 × 2 = 80.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. B, D, F, H, ?
(a) I (b) K (c) J (d) G
Answer: (c) J
Solution: Skip one letter each time (+2): H → J.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. 1, 8, 27, ?
(a) 36 (b) 48 (c) 81 (d) 64
Answer: (d) 64
Solution: Cubes: 4³ = 64.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. 10, 20, 30, 40, ?
(a) 50 (b) 60 (c) 45 (d) 55
Answer: (a) 50
Solution: Add 10 each time: 40 + 10 = 50.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. 3, 6, 9, 12, ?
(a) 18 (b) 15 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 15
Solution: Add 3 each time: 12 + 3 = 15.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. A, B, D, G, ?
(a) J (b) I (c) K (d) L
Answer: (c) K
Solution: Positions 1, 2, 4, 7 with differences +1,+2,+3; next +4 = 11 = K.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. 2, 4, 6, 8, ?
(a) 9 (b) 12 (c) 11 (d) 10
Answer: (d) 10
Solution: Add 2 each time: 8 + 2 = 10.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q9. 100, 90, 80, 70, ?
(a) 60 (b) 65 (c) 50 (d) 55
Answer: (a) 60
Solution: Subtract 10 each time: 70 − 10 = 60.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?
(a) 30 (b) 36 (c) 49 (d) 32
Answer: (b) 36
Solution: Perfect squares: 6² = 36.
Source: RRB Group D

Q11. C, F, I, L, ?
(a) N (b) M (c) O (d) P
Answer: (c) O
Solution: Add 3 positions each time: L (12) → O (15).
Source: SSC CGL

Q12. 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, ?
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 15 (d) 13
Answer: (d) 13
Solution: Prime numbers: after 11 comes 13.
Source: SSC MTS

Q13. 5, 15, 45, ?
(a) 135 (b) 90 (c) 60 (d) 120
Answer: (a) 135
Solution: Multiply by 3 each time: 45 × 3 = 135.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q14. A, C, E, G, I, ?
(a) J (b) K (c) L (d) H
Answer: (b) K
Solution: Add 2 each time: I → K.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q15. 1, 2, 4, 7, 11, ?
(a) 15 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 18
Answer: (c) 16
Solution: Differences 1, 2, 3, 4, then 5: 11 + 5 = 16.
Source: SSC CGL

Q16. Z, Y, X, W, ?
(a) U (b) T (c) S (d) V
Answer: (d) V
Solution: Go back 1 letter each time: W → V.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q17. (Medium) 7, 14, 21, 28, ?
(a) 35 (b) 32 (c) 30 (d) 36
Answer: (a) 35
Solution: Add 7 each time: 28 + 7 = 35.
Source: SSC CGL

Q18. (Medium) 2, 4, 8, 14, 22, ?
(a) 30 (b) 32 (c) 28 (d) 34
Answer: (b) 32
Solution: Differences 2, 4, 6, 8, then 10: 22 + 10 = 32.
Source: SSC MTS

Q19. (Medium) A2, C4, E6, ?
(a) F8 (b) G7 (c) G8 (d) H8
Answer: (c) G8
Solution: Letters +2 (A,C,E,G) and numbers +2 (2,4,6,8) → G8.
Source: SSC CGL

Q20. (Medium) 81, 27, 9, ?
(a) 6 (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (d) 3
Solution: Divide by 3 each time: 9 ÷ 3 = 3.
Source: RRB Group D

Q21. (Medium) What comes next in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
(a) 42 (b) 40 (c) 44 (d) 46
Answer: (a) 42
Solution: Differences are +4, +6, +8, +10. Next difference is +12: 30 + 12 = 42.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q22. (Medium) Which letter replaces the question mark in the series: Z, X, V, T, ?
(a) S (b) R (c) Q (d) P
Answer: (b) R
Solution: Position values: 26, 24, 22, 20 (each step is -2). Next is 18 = R.
Source: SSC MTS

Q23. (Medium) What is the next prime number in the series: 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, ?
(a) 25 (b) 27 (c) 29 (d) 31
Answer: (c) 29
Solution: This is a consecutive prime number series. The next prime after 23 is 29.
Source: RRB Group D

Q24. (Medium) Which pair comes next in the series: AZ, BY, CX, DW, ?
(a) FU (b) EU (c) FV (d) EV
Answer: (d) EV
Solution: Each pair consists of an alphabetical letter and its reverse pair (sum = 27): A-Z, B-Y, C-X, D-W, E-V.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q25. (Medium) What is the next number in the series: 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, ?
(a) 45 (b) 49 (c) 48 (d) 50
Answer: (b) 49
Solution: The series represents squares of consecutive integers: 2², 3², 4², 5², 6², next is 7² = 49.
Source: NCERT Reasoning
