Set 4/6 – Medium Level MCQs (76–100)

76. 7, 12, 19, 28, 39, ?
(A) 50 (B) 51 (C) 52 (D) 53
Answer: (C) 52
Solution: Differences: +5, +7, +9, +11 → next +13 → 39+13=52.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

77. 2, 8, 18, 32, 50, ?
(A) 60 (B) 68 (C) 72 (D) 76
Answer: (C) 72
Solution: 2×1²=2, 2×2²=8, 2×3²=18, 2×4²=32, 2×5²=50, 2×6²=72.
Source: Railway Group D 2019

78. 5, 7, 11, 19, 35, ?
(A) 67 (B) 68 (C) 69 (D) 70
Answer: (A) 67
Solution: ×2 − 3: 5×2−3=7, 7×2−3=11, 11×2−3=19, 19×2−3=35, 35×2−3=67.
Source: IBPS PO Prelims 2018

79. M, P, T, Y, ?

Source: SSC CGL 2017

81. Find the wrong term: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 38
(A) 5 (B) 10 (C) 17 (D) 38
Answer: (D) 38
Solution: Pattern n²+1: 1²+1=2, 2²+1=5, 3²+1=10, 4²+1=17, 5²+1=26, 6²+1=37 (not 38).
Source: IBPS Clerk 2020

82. ⚠️ Trap: 2, 6, 4, 12, 8, 24, ?
(A) 16 (B) 48 (C) 32 (D) 12
Answer: (A) 16
Solution: Double series – odd positions: 2, 4, 8 (×2 each) → next 16; even positions: 6, 12, 24 (×2). The 7th term is odd position, so 16.
Trap: If you blindly follow the even-position pattern, you might pick 48.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

89. ⚠️ Trap: 4, 6, 9, 13.5, ?
(A) 18 (B) 20 (C) 20.25 (D) 22
Answer: (C) 20.25
Solution: Multiply by 1.5: 4×1.5=6, 6×1.5=9, 9×1.5=13.5, 13.5×1.5=20.25.
Trap: The decimal answer often causes candidates to look for a different whole-number pattern, but the constant multiplier is the correct approach.
*Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 2019*

100. ⚠️ Trap: 4, 6, 9, 14, 21, ?
(A) 30 (B) 31 (C) 32 (D) 33
Answer: (C) 32
Solution: Differences: +2, +3, +5, +7 (consecutive primes) → next +11 → 32.
Trap: If the differences are mistaken for odd numbers (+2, +3, +5, +7, +9), the next term would be 30 (option A), which is a classic distractor.
Source: SSC CGL 2019
