PYQ Analysis: Series (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Series gives 2–4 questions per exam. Number and letter series are most common. The trap is assuming a single rule and missing an alternating pattern.

Q1. 2, 5, 8, 11, ?
(a) 13 (b) 14 (c) 15 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 14
Solution: Add 3 each time: 11 + 3 = 14.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. 3, 6, 12, 24, ?
(a) 36 (b) 48 (c) 30 (d) 42
Answer: (b) 48
Solution: Multiply by 2 each time: 24 × 2 = 48.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. A, C, E, G, ?
(a) H (b) I (c) J (d) K
Answer: (b) I
Solution: Skip one letter each time (+2): G → I.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. 1, 4, 9, 16, ?
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 24 (d) 36
Answer: (b) 25
Solution: Perfect squares: 5² = 25.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. 2, 4, 8, 16, ?
(a) 24 (b) 32 (c) 20 (d) 28
Answer: (b) 32
Solution: Multiply by 2 each time: 16 × 2 = 32.
Source: SSC MTS

Q6. 5, 10, 15, 20, ?
(a) 25 (b) 30 (c) 24 (d) 22
Answer: (a) 25
Solution: Add 5 each time: 20 + 5 = 25.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q7. A1, B2, C3, ?
(a) D4 (b) D5 (c) E4 (d) C4
Answer: (a) D4
Solution: Letters go A, B, C, D and numbers 1, 2, 3, 4 → D4.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q8. 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, ?
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 6 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: Fibonacci — each term is the sum of the previous two: 3 + 5 = 8.
Source: SSC CGL

Q9. Z, X, V, T, ?
(a) S (b) R (c) Q (d) U
Answer: (b) R
Solution: Go back 2 letters each time: T → R.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. 2, 6, 12, 20, ?
(a) 28 (b) 30 (c) 32 (d) 26
Answer: (b) 30
Solution: Differences are 4, 6, 8, then 10: 20 + 10 = 30.
Source: SSC CGL
