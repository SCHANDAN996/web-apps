Set 5: Compound Interest – MCQs (101–125). Questions 101–120 are Medium; 121–125 are Hard.

101. A sum of money at compound interest amounts to ₹14,520 in 2 years and to ₹15,972 in 3 years. Find the rate percent per annum.
(a) 8% (b) 9% (c) 12% (d) 10%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The amount in the 3rd year is the 2nd year amount grown by one year of interest, so 1 + R/100 = 15972/14520 = 1.1, giving R = 10%. The trap is dividing the difference ₹1,452 by ₹14,520 and misreading it as something other than 10%. So the answer is 10%.
Source: PYQ-style

102. What is the compound interest on ₹25,000 at 8% p.a. for 2 years, compounded half-yearly?
(a) ₹4,246.40 (b) ₹4,080 (c) ₹4,161.60 (d) ₹4,320
Answer: (a)
Solution: Half-yearly compounding means rate 4% per half-year over 4 periods: CI = 25000 × (1.04)⁴ − 25000 = 29246.46 − 25000 ≈ 4246.46. The trap is using annual compounding, which gives ₹4,160, close to the wrong option ₹4,161.60. So the answer is ₹4,246.40.
Source: PYQ-style

103. The difference between the compound interest and simple interest on a certain sum at 10% p.a. for 2 years is ₹250. Find the sum.
(a) ₹20,000 (b) ₹30,000 (c) ₹22,000 (d) ₹25,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: For 2 years, CI − SI = P(R/100)², so 250 = P × 0.01, giving P = 25000. The trap is using P(R/100)² × 2, which wrongly gives ₹12,500. So the answer is ₹25,000.
Source: PYQ-style

104. A sum of ₹8,000 is invested at compound interest. The rates for the first and second years are 5% and 10% respectively. Find the amount after 2 years.
(a) ₹9,200 (b) ₹9,240 (c) ₹9,280 (d) ₹9,300
Answer: (b)
Solution: Amount = 8000 × 1.05 × 1.10 = 8000 × 1.155 = 9240. The trap is applying 10% first and then 5% on the original principal, or averaging the rates to 7.5%, which gives ₹9,245. So the answer is ₹9,240.
Source: PYQ-style

105. A town's population increases at the rate of 10% per annum. If the present population is 66,550, what was it 3 years ago?
(a) 52,000 (b) 48,000 (c) 55,000 (d) 50,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: Population 3 years ago = 66550 / (1.1)³ = 66550 / 1.331 = 50000. The trap is multiplying 66550 by (1.1)³ instead of dividing, which gives about 88,578. So the answer is 50,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

106. A sum doubles itself at compound interest in 10 years. In how many years will it become 8 times?
(a) 20 years (b) 30 years (c) 50 years (d) 40 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: 8 times = 2³, and each doubling takes 10 years, so total time = 3 × 10 = 30 years. The trap is thinking 8 times needs 8 × 10 = 80 years or proportionally 40 years. So the answer is 30 years.
Source: PYQ-style

107. Find the effective annual rate of interest equivalent to 15% p.a. compounded half-yearly.
(a) 15.5625% (b) 15.50% (c) 15.75% (d) 15.25%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Effective rate = (1 + 7.5/100)² − 1 = (1.075)² − 1 = 0.155625 = 15.5625%. The trap is simply adding 7.5% + 7.5% = 15% or rounding to 15.5%. So the answer is 15.5625%.
Source: PYQ-style

108. The simple interest on ₹6,000 for 2 years at a certain rate is ₹960. What would be the compound interest on the same sum for 2 years at the same rate?
(a) ₹1,012.80 (b) ₹1,000 (c) ₹998.40 (d) ₹1,020
Answer: (c)
Solution: From SI, R = (960 × 100)/(6000 × 2) = 8%. Then CI = 6000 × (1.08)² − 6000 = 6998.40 − 6000 = 998.40. The trap is assuming CI equals SI for 2 years, giving ₹960. So the answer is ₹998.40.
Source: PYQ-style

109. A sum of ₹20,000 is invested at 10% p.a. compound interest for 1 year, compounded quarterly. Find the interest.
(a) ₹2,000 (b) ₹2,075.50 (c) ₹2,050 (d) ₹2,155
Answer: (b)
Solution: Quarterly compounding: rate 2.5% per quarter over 4 quarters, CI = 20000 × (1.025)⁴ − 20000 ≈ 22076.26 − 20000 ≈ 2076.26, closest to ₹2,075.50. The trap is treating it as simple annual interest, giving ₹2,000. So the answer is ₹2,075.50.
Source: PYQ-style

110. At what rate percent per annum will a sum of ₹10,000 become ₹13,310 in 3 years, compounded annually?
(a) 8% (b) 9% (c) 10% (d) 12%
Answer: (c)
Solution: 13310/10000 = 1.331 = (1 + R/100)³, and ∛1.331 = 1.1, so R = 10%. The trap is computing (13310 − 10000)/10000/3 = 11.03% as if it were simple interest. So the answer is 10%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

111. A machine is depreciating at 12.5% per annum. If its present value is ₹98,000, what was its value 2 years ago?
(a) ₹1,12,000 (b) ₹1,20,000 (c) ₹1,28,000 (d) ₹1,15,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Present value = Original × (0.875)² = 0.765625 × Original = 98000, so Original = 98000/0.765625 = 128000. The trap is adding back 25% of 98000, giving ₹1,22,500. So the answer is ₹1,28,000.
Source: PYQ-style

112. The compound interest on a sum for 2 years at 5% p.a. is ₹410. The sum is:
(a) ₹3,800 (b) ₹4,200 (c) ₹4,000 (d) ₹4,400
Answer: (c)
Solution: CI = P[(1.05)² − 1] = 0.1025P = 410, so P = 410/0.1025 = 4000. The trap is dividing 410 by 0.10 (the simple interest rate for 2 years), giving ₹4,100. So the answer is ₹4,000.
Source: PYQ-style

113. A sum of money becomes 4 times in 10 years at compound interest. In how many years will it become 16 times?
(a) 20 years (b) 15 years (c) 25 years (d) 30 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: 4 times = 2² in 10 years means the doubling time is 5 years; 16 times = 2⁴ needs 4 × 5 = 20 years. The trap is scaling directly: 16/4 × 10 = 40 years. So the answer is 20 years.
Source: PYQ-style

114. A sum of ₹5,000 is lent at 10% p.a. compound interest, compounded annually. After 2 years, the amount is reinvested at 20% p.a. compound interest for 1 year. What is the final amount?
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹7,500 (c) ₹7,000 (d) ₹7,260
Answer: (d)
Solution: After 2 years at 10%: 5000 × 1.21 = 6050; then at 20% for 1 year: 6050 × 1.2 = 7260. The trap is applying 20% to the original ₹5,000 for the last year, giving ₹7,210. So the answer is ₹7,260.
Source: PYQ-style

115. The difference between CI and SI on ₹12,500 for 2 years at 8% p.a. is:
(a) ₹80 (b) ₹100 (c) ₹120 (d) ₹72
Answer: (a)
Solution: CI − SI for 2 years = P(R/100)² = 12500 × 0.0064 = 80. The trap is computing the full CI − SI over 3 years or using P(R/100)² × R/100, which gives ₹6.40. So the answer is ₹80.
Source: PYQ-style

116. A tree's height increases by 25% each year. If its present height is 156.25 cm, what was its height 2 years ago?
(a) 80 cm (b) 100 cm (c) 90 cm (d) 120 cm
Answer: (b)
Solution: Height 2 years ago = 156.25 / (1.25)² = 156.25 / 1.5625 = 100 cm. The trap is subtracting 50% of 156.25, giving about 78 cm. So the answer is 100 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

117. In how many years will ₹8,000 amount to ₹9,261 at 5% p.a. compound interest?
(a) 3 (b) 2.5 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: 9261/8000 = 1.157625 = (1.05)³, so the time is 3 years. The trap is treating ₹1,261 as simple interest: 1261 = 8000 × 5 × T/100 gives T ≈ 3.15, tempting 2.5 or 4. So the answer is 3 years.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

118. A sum of ₹15,000 is invested at 6% p.a. compound interest for 2 years, compounded half-yearly. Find the amount.
(a) ₹16,800 (b) ₹16,950 (c) ₹16,878 (d) ₹16,750
Answer: (c)
Solution: Half-yearly: rate 3% per half-year over 4 periods, A = 15000 × (1.03)⁴ ≈ 15000 × 1.12550881 ≈ 16882.63, nearest to ₹16,878. The trap is annual compounding, which gives ₹16,854. So the answer is ₹16,878.
Source: PYQ-style

119. The ratio of the amount after 3 years to the amount after 2 years on a certain sum at compound interest is 21:20. The rate percent is:
(a) 5% (b) 8% (c) 4% (d) 10%
Answer: (a)
Solution: A₃/A₂ = 1 + R/100 = 21/20 = 1.05, so R = 5%. The trap is computing (21 − 20)/21 ≈ 4.76% and picking 4%. So the answer is 5%.
Source: PYQ-style

120. The simple interest on a sum for 3 years at 5% p.a. is ₹1,500. What is the compound interest on the same sum for 3 years at the same rate?
(a) ₹1,520 (b) ₹1,550 (c) ₹1,600 (d) ₹1,576.25
Answer: (d)
Solution: P = (1500 × 100)/(5 × 3) = 10000; CI = 10000 × (1.05)³ − 10000 = 11576.25 − 10000 = 1576.25. The trap is assuming CI equals SI for 3 years, giving ₹1,500. So the answer is ₹1,576.25.
Source: PYQ-style

121. A sum of ₹50,000 is borrowed at 12% p.a. compound interest, to be repaid in two equal annual installments. What is each installment? (approx)
(a) ₹27,500 (b) ₹29,600 (c) ₹28,100 (d) ₹30,200
Answer: (b)
Solution: If each installment is x, then 50000 = x/1.12 + x/(1.12)² = x(0.892857 + 0.797194) = 1.690051x, so x ≈ 29586, closest to ₹29,600. The trap is simply dividing 50000 × 1.12² / 2 ≈ ₹31,360. So the answer is ₹29,600.
Source: PYQ-style

122. The compound interest on a sum for 2 years at 10% p.a. is ₹1,680. The sum is then reinvested at 20% p.a. compound interest for 1 year, compounded half-yearly. Find the total interest earned over 3 years.
(a) ₹4,150 (b) ₹4,000 (c) ₹3,720 (d) ₹3,800
Answer: (c)
Solution: From 0.21P = 1680, P = 8000; amount after 2 years = 9680. Reinvested half-yearly at 20% for 1 year: 9680 × (1.1)² = 11712.80. Total interest = 11712.80 − 8000 = 3712.80 ≈ ₹3,720. The trap is adding the second year's interest on ₹9,680 at a flat 20%, giving 1680 + 1936 = ₹3,616. So the answer is ₹3,720.
Source: PYQ-style

123. A person invests ₹1,00,000 at 10% p.a. compounded half-yearly. After 1.5 years, the rate changes to 20% p.a. compounded quarterly. What is the total amount after 3 years from the start? (approx)
(a) ₹1,55,000 (b) ₹1,58,000 (c) ₹1,60,200 (d) ₹1,52,800
Answer: (a)
Solution: First 1.5 years = 3 half-years at 5%: A = 100000 × (1.05)³ = 115762.50. Next 1.5 years = 6 quarters at 5%: A = 115762.50 × (1.05)⁶ ≈ 115762.50 × 1.3401 ≈ 155132. The trap is applying 20% for the whole 3 years or mixing up the period counts. So the answer is ₹1,55,000.
Source: PYQ-style

124. The difference between the compound interest and simple interest on a sum for 3 years at 20% p.a. is ₹1,000. Find the sum.
(a) ₹8,200 (b) ₹7,500 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹7,812.50
Answer: (d)
Solution: For 3 years, CI − SI = P(R/100)²(3 + R/100) = P × 0.04 × 3.2 = 0.128P = 1000, so P = 7812.50. The trap is using the 2-year formula P(R/100)² = 1000, which gives ₹25,000. So the answer is ₹7,812.50.
Source: PYQ-style

125. A machine depreciates at 20% p.a. for the first 2 years and then at 10% p.a. for the next year. If its present value is ₹1,00,000, what was its value 3 years ago? (approx)
(a) ₹1,85,000 (b) ₹1,73,600 (c) ₹1,92,000 (d) ₹1,90,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: Present = Original × 0.8 × 0.8 × 0.9 = 0.576 × Original = 100000, so Original = 100000/0.576 ≈ 173611 ≈ ₹1,73,600. The trap is adding back 50% of ₹1,00,000, giving ₹1,50,000. So the answer is ₹1,73,600.
Source: PYQ-style
