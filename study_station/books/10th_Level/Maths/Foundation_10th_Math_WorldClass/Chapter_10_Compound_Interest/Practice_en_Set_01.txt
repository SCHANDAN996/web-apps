Q24. A sum of ₹5000 is borrowed at 10% p.a. compound interest, compounded annually. It is repaid in two equal annual installments. Find the amount of each installment (approx).
(a) ₹2500 (b) ₹2881 (c) ₹3000 (d) ₹2750

Q25. The population of a town increases by 10% annually. If the present population is 66550, what was it 3 years ago?
(a) 50000 (b) 55000 (c) 60000 (d) 45000

Answer Key with Step-by-Step Solutions

1. (b) ₹210
Solution: Amount = 1000(1 + 10/100)² = 1000 × 1.21 = ₹1210. CI = 1210 – 1000 = ₹210. (NCERT)

2. (b) ₹5832
Solution: A = 5000(1.08)² = 5000 × 1.1664 = ₹5832. (NCERT)

3. (b) ₹20
Solution: CI – SI for 2 years = P(R/100)² = 2000 × (10/100)² = 2000 × 0.01 = ₹20. (SSC)

4. (b) ₹102.50
Solution: SI = 100 = P × 5 × 2/100 ⇒ P = ₹1000. CI = 1000(1.05)² – 1000 = 1102.50 – 1000 = ₹102.50. (SSC)

5. (a) ₹10000
Solution: P = 12100 / (1.1)² = 12100 / 1.21 = ₹10000. (NCERT)

6. (b) ₹1245
Solution: Half‑yearly: rate = 7.5% per period, time = 2 periods. A = 8000(1.075)² = 8000 × 1.155625 = ₹9245. CI = 9245 – 8000 = ₹1245. (NCERT)

7. (b) 5%
Solution: 22050/20000 = 1.1025 = (1 + R/100)². √1.1025 = 1.05 ⇒ R = 5%. (SSC)

8. (b) 27040
Solution: Population = 25000(1.04)² = 25000 × 1.0816 = 27040. (NCERT)

9. (a) ₹65610
Solution: Value = 81000(1 – 0.1)² = 81000 × 0.81 = ₹65610. (NCERT)

10. (b) ₹1230
Solution: A = 12000(1.05)² = 12000 × 1.1025 = 13230. CI = 13230 – 12000 = ₹1230. (NCERT)

11. (b) ₹46.50
Solution: CI – SI for 3 years = P(R/100)² (3 + R/100) = 1500 × 0.01 × (3 + 0.1) = 1500 × 0.01 × 3.1 = ₹46.50. (SSC)

12. (b) 6%
Solution: 5618/5000 = 1.1236. √1.1236 = 1.06 ⇒ R = 6%. (NCERT)

13. (b) 30 years
Solution: Doubles in 10 years ⇒ 2¹ in 10 years. 8 times = 2³, so time = 3 × 10 = 30 years. (SSC)

14. (b) ₹510
Solution: Half‑yearly: rate = 4%, n = 2. A = 6250(1.04)² = 6250 × 1.0816 = ₹6760. CI = 6760 – 6250 = ₹510. (NCERT)

15. (b) 10.25%
Solution: Effective rate = (1 + 10/200)² – 1 = (1.05)² – 1 = 0.1025 = 10.25%. (SSC)

16. (b) ₹763.20
Solution: SI = 720 ⇒ P = (720 × 100)/(12 × 2) = ₹3000. CI = 3000(1.12)² – 3000 = 3000 × 1.2544 – 3000 = 3763.20 – 3000 = ₹763.20. (IBPS)

17. (a) ₹8000
Solution: P = 8820 / (1.05)² = 8820 / 1.1025 = ₹8000. (NCERT)

18. (b) 3 years
Solution: 1852.20 / 1600 = 1.157625. (1.05)³ = 1.157625, so T = 3. (NCERT)

19. (b) ₹1909.82
Solution: Quarterly: rate per quarter = 5%, n = 8. A = 4000(1.05)⁸ = 4000 × 1.477455 = ₹5909.82. CI = 5909.82 – 4000 = ₹1909.82. (SSC)

20. (a) 250 cm
Solution: Present height = initial height × (1.2)² = 360 ⇒ initial height = 360 / 1.44 = 250 cm. (NCERT)

21. (a) ₹11088
Solution: Amount = 8000 × 1.05 × 1.10 × 1.20 = 8000 × 1.386 = ₹11088. (SSC CGL)

22. (b) ₹2400
Solution: CI = P[(1.2)² – 1] = 0.44P = 2640 ⇒ P = 6000. SI = 6000 × 20% × 2 = 6000 × 0.4 = ₹2400. (SSC)

23. (c) 10%
Solution: Amount ratio = 22000/20000 = 1.10. So 1 + R/100 = 1.1 ⇒ R = 10%. (RRB)

24. (b) ₹2881 (approx)
Solution: 5000 = x/1.1 + x/(1.1)² = x(1/1.1 + 1/1.21) = x(0.90909 + 0.82645) = 1.73554x ⇒ x = 5000/1.73554 ≈ ₹2880.95 ≈ ₹2881. (SSC)

25. (a) 50000
Solution: Population 3 years ago = 66550 / (1.1)³ = 66550 / 1.331 = 50000. (NCERT)
