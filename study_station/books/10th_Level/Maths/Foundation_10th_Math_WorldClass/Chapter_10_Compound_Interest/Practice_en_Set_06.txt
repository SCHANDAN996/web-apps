126. (a) 5%, ₹3,809.52
Solution: Ratio of 3‑yr to 2‑yr amount = 4410/4200 = 1.05 ⇒ rate = 5%. Principal = 4200/(1.05)² = 4200/1.1025 = ₹3809.52. (SSC CGL)

127. (c) ₹1,200
Solution: CI = P(1.2² – 1) = 0.44P = 1320 ⇒ P = 3000. SI = 3000 × 20% × 2 = ₹1200. (SSC)

128. (b) ₹10,000
Solution: For 3 years, CI – SI = P(R/100)²(3 + R/100) → 310 = P(0.1)² × 3.1 = 0.031P ⇒ P = 310/0.031 = 10000. (SSC CGL)

129. (a) ₹1,54,200
Solution: After 1st year: 1,00,000 × 1.10 = 1,10,000.
2nd year (half‑yearly 20%): rate 10% half‑yr, 2 periods → factor 1.1² = 1.21, amount = 1,10,000 × 1.21 = 1,33,100.
3rd year (quarterly 15%): rate 3.75% per quarter, 4 periods → (1.0375)⁴ ≈ 1.15865, amount = 1,33,100 × 1.15865 ≈ 1,54,200. (SSC/Bank PO)

130. (b) ₹49,960
Solution: Let installment = x. PV = x/1.12 + x/1.12² + x/1.12³ = x(0.892857 + 0.797194 + 0.711780) = 2.401831x = 1,20,000 ⇒ x ≈ 1,20,000/2.401831 ≈ 49,960. (SSC)

131. (a) ₹7,50,000
Solution: Present value = Original × (0.85)² × (0.80)² = Original × 0.4624 = 3,46,800 ⇒ Original = 3,46,800/0.4624 = 7,50,000. (SSC)

132. (b) 21.55%
Solution: Effective rate = (1 + 20/400)⁴ – 1 = (1.05)⁴ – 1 = 1.21550625 – 1 = 0.21550625 ≈ 21.55%. (SSC)

133. (a) ₹1,000, 10%
Solution: CI for 3rd year alone = 331 – 210 = 121. Let amount after 2 years = A₂, then A₂ × r = 121 and A₂ – P = 210. Also P = A₂/(1+r)². Solving gives r = 0.1, P = 1000, rate = 10%. (SSC CGL)

134. (a) ₹17,200
Solution: CI amount = 2,00,000 × 1.05 × 1.10 × 1.20 = 2,00,000 × 1.386 = 2,77,200, CI = 77,200. SI at 10% for 3 years = 2,00,000 × 0.3 = 60,000. Difference = 17,200. (SSC)

135. (b) ₹6,407
Solution: Half‑yearly rate = 12.5% = 1/8, factor = 1.125. After 4 half‑years, amount = 32000 × (1.125)⁴ = 32000 × (6561/4096) = 51257.8125. Interest in 5th half‑year = 51257.8125 × 0.125 = 6407.226 ≈ 6407. (SSC)

136. (a) ₹1,312
Solution: SI = 1200 ⇒ P = 1200/(0.12×2) = 5000. CI half‑yearly: rate 6%, 4 periods, amount = 5000 × (1.06)⁴ ≈ 5000 × 1.262477 = 6312.38, CI ≈ 1312. (Bank PO)

137. (a) ₹189
Solution: SI = 1,00,000 × 5% × 1.5 = 7,500. CI half‑yearly: rate 2.5% per half, 3 periods, amount = 1,00,000 × (1.025)³ = 1,07,689.0625, CI = 7,689.06. Difference = 7,689.06 – 7,500 = 189.06 ≈ 189. (SSC)

138. (a) 1,00,000

139. (a) ₹9,800
