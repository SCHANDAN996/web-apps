Q51. (Medium) If the four-digit number 4x65 is completely divisible by 9, what is the value of digit x?
(A) 3 (B) 2 (C) 4 (D) 5

Answer: (A) 3

Solution:
By the divisibility rule for 9, the sum of all digits must be a multiple of 9. Sum = 4 + x + 6 + 5 = 15 + x. The smallest single digit x making 15 + x divisible by 9 is x = 18 – 15 = 3. Therefore, the value of x is 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).
