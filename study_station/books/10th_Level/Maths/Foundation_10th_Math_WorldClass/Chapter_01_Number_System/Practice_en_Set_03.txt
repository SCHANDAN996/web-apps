Q52. The LCM of 24, 36, and 40 is
(a) 720 (b) 360 (c) 480 (d) 240
Answer: (b) 360
Solution: 24 = 2³×3, 36 = 2²×3², 40 = 2³×5. LCM = 2³×3²×5 = 8×9×5 = 360.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q53. The HCF of 48 and 180 expressed as a linear combination is
(a) 48 × 4 – 180 × 1 (b) 180 × 1 – 48 × 3 (c) 180 × 4 – 48 × 15 (d) 48 × 4 – 180 × 1
Answer: (d) 48 × 4 – 180 × 1
Solution: HCF(48,180) = 12. 48×4 = 192, 192 – 180 = 12. So 48×4 – 180×1 = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 10

Q54. The number of three-digit numbers divisible by 7 is
(a) 128 (b) 127 (c) 129 (d) 126
Answer: (a) 128
Solution: Smallest 3-digit divisible by 7: 105 (7×15). Largest: 994 (7×142). Count = 142 – 15 + 1 = 128.
Source: SSC CGL

Q57. If x and y are two prime numbers such that x < y and x + y = 24, then how many such pairs exist?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (c) 3
Solution: Primes <24: 5+19=24, 7+17=24, 11+13=24. Also 2+22 no (22 not prime), 3+21 no. So three pairs: (5,19), (7,17), (11,13).
Source: Bank PO

Q58. The least number which is a perfect square and also divisible by 8, 12, and 15 is
(a) 3600 (b) 2400 (c) 14400 (d) 900
Answer: (a) 3600
Solution: LCM of 8,12,15 = 120. To make it a perfect square, multiply by missing factors to get even exponents: 120 = 2³×3×5 → need 2×3×5 = 30 → 120×30=3600 = 60².
Source: SSC CGL

Q62. The product of two irrational numbers is

Answer: (c) can be rational or irrational

Q63. The greatest 4-digit number that is divisible by 15, 25, 40, and 75 is
(a) 9600 (b) 9800 (c) 9000 (d) 9900
Answer: (a) 9600
Solution: LCM of 15,25,40,75: 15=3×5, 25=5², 40=2³×5, 75=3×5². LCM = 2³×3×5² = 600. Largest 4-digit = 9999. 9999 ÷ 600 = 16 remainder 399. 9999 – 399 = 9600.
Source: SSC CGL

Q71. The digits of a two-digit number differ by 2, and the number is 7 times the sum of its digits. The number is
(a) 42 (b) 53 (c) 35 (d) 24
Answer: (a) 42
Solution: Let number = 10a + b. Given a – b = 2 (or b – a = 2) and 10a + b = 7(a+b) → 10a + b = 7a + 7b → 3a = 6b → a = 2b. Substitute a=2b into a – b = 2 gives 2b – b = b = 2, a = 4. Number = 42.
Source: SSC CGL

Q72. The LCM of two numbers is 45 times their HCF. If one number is 125 and the sum of their LCM and HCF is 1150, find the other number.
(a) 225 (b) 250 (c) 200 (d) 275
Answer: (a) 225
Solution: Let HCF = h, LCM = 45h. Sum: h + 45h = 46h = 1150 ⇒ h = 25. LCM = 45×25 = 1125. Product of numbers = LCM×HCF = 1125×25 = 28125. Other number = 28125 ÷ 125 = 225.
Source: SSC CGL

Q74. What is the least number by which 4320 must be multiplied to make it a perfect cube?
(a) 25 (b) 50 (c) 75 (d) 100
Answer: (b) 50
Solution: Prime factorization: 4320 = 2⁵ × 3³ × 5. For a perfect cube, exponents must be multiples of 3. Need 2¹ (to make 2⁶), 5² (to make 5³). Multiply by 2 × 5² = 50.
Source: SSC CGL

Q75. The product of three consecutive positive integers is always divisible by
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (d) 6
Solution: Among any three consecutive integers, at least one is even (divisible by 2) and exactly one is divisible by 3. So product divisible by 2×3=6.
Source: NCERT Class 10
