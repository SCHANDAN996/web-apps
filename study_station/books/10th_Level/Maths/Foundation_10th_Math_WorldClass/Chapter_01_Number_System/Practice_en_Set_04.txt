Q76. If a number 357y is divisible by 4, then the digit y can be
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) both (a) and (c)
Answer: (d) both (a) and (c)
Solution: A number is divisible by 4 if its last two digits form a number divisible by 4. The last two digits are 7y. For 7y to be divisible by 4, possibilities: 72 (y=2) and 76 (y=6). So y = 2 or 6.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q77. The HCF of 204, 1190, and 1445 is

Q81. A two-digit number is such that the product of its digits is 12. When 36 is added to the number, the digits interchange their places. The number is
(a) 34 (b) 43 (c) 26 (d) 62
Answer: (c) 26
Solution: Let number = 10a + b. Product ab = 12. Reversed number = 10b + a. Given: 10a + b + 36 = 10b + a → 9a – 9b = –36 → b – a = 4. Pairs with product 12 and difference 4 (b > a): (2,6) gives 2×6=12, 6–2=4. So number = 26.
Source: Bank PO

Q82. The total number of factors of 540 is

Answer: (b) 24
Solution: 540 = 2² × 3³ × 5¹. Number of factors = (2+1)(3+1)(1+1) = 3×4×2 = 24.

Q97. The largest 4-digit number exactly divisible by 9, 12, 15, and 18 is

Answer: (c) 9900? Let's compute: LCM of 9,12,15,18. 9=3²,12=2²×3,15=3×5,18=2×3². LCM = 2²×3²×5 = 180. Largest 4-digit = 9999. 9999 ÷ 180 = 55 remainder 99? 180×55=9900, remainder 99. So 9999-99=9900. So answer (c) 9900.

Q98. A number consists of two digits whose sum is 8. If 18 is added to the number, the digits are reversed. The number is
(a) 35 (b) 53 (c) 26 (d) 62
Answer: (a) 35
Solution: Let number = 10a + b, a+b=8. Reversed = 10b + a. 10a+b+18 = 10b+a → 9a – 9b = –18 → b – a = 2. Solving a+b=8 and b–a=2 gives b=5, a=3. Number = 35.
Source: Bank PO
