Q76. If the four-digit number 357y is completely divisible by 4, then the digit y can be which of the following?
(a) 2 only (b) 6 only (c) 4 (d) Either 2 or 6
Answer: (d) Either 2 or 6
Solution: A number is divisible by 4 if the number formed by its last two digits is divisible by 4. Here, the last two digits are 7y. The possible two-digit numbers in the 70s divisible by 4 are 72 (y = 2) and 76 (y = 6). Since both 2 and 6 satisfy the condition, digit y can be Either 2 or 6. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL

Q77. What is the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 204, 1190, and 1445?
(a) 17 (b) 34 (c) 51 (d) 1
Answer: (a) 17
Solution: Finding prime factorizations: 204 = 2² × 3 × 17, 1190 = 2 × 5 × 7 × 17, and 1445 = 5 × 17². The only common prime factor among all three numbers is 17. Thus, the HCF of the numbers is 17. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q78. What is the unit digit of the sum (246)^99 + (135)^100?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (b) 1
Solution: Any positive integral power of a number ending in 6 always ends in 6, so the unit digit of (246)^99 is 6. Similarly, any power of a number ending in 5 always ends in 5, so the unit digit of (135)^100 is 5. Adding these unit digits gives 6 + 5 = 11, which means the required unit digit is 1. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC MTS

Q79. Which of the following numbers represents a rational number?
(a) √2 + √2 (b) √2 × √3 (c) √18 / √2 (d) π
Answer: (c) √18 / √2
Solution: Simplifying the expression in option (c): √18 / √2 = √(18 / 2) = √9 = 3, which is an integer and therefore rational. In contrast, √2 + √2 = 2√2 (irrational), √2 × √3 = √6 (irrational), and π is transcendental and irrational. Thus, the rational number is √18 / √2. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q80. How many prime numbers lie strictly between 50 and 80?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 7
Answer: (d) 7
Solution: Listing all prime numbers between 50 and 80: 53, 59, 61, 67, 71, 73, and 79. Counting them gives exactly seven primes. Therefore, the total count of prime numbers between 50 and 80 is 7. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB Group D

Q81. In a two-digit number, the product of the digits is 12. If 36 is added to the number, the digits interchange their places. What is the original number?
(a) 34 (b) 26 (c) 43 (d) 62
Answer: (b) 26
Solution: Let the two-digit number be 10a + b. We are given a × b = 12. Adding 36 reverses the digits: (10a + b) + 36 = 10b + a, which simplifies to 9b – 9a = 36 or b – a = 4. The pair of single digits satisfying a × b = 12 and b – a = 4 is a = 2 and b = 6. Thus, the original number is 26. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL

Q82. What is the total number of factors (divisors) of the number 540?
(a) 24 (b) 20 (c) 28 (d) 30
Answer: (a) 24
Solution: Prime factorization of 540: 540 = 54 × 10 = (2 × 3³) × (2 × 5) = 2² × 3³ × 5¹. The total number of positive factors is obtained by multiplying one more than each prime exponent: (2 + 1)(3 + 1)(1 + 1) = 3 × 4 × 2 = 24. Thus, the total number of factors of 540 is 24. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q83. When simplified to a mixed surd in its simplest form, √243 is equal to:
(a) 3√27 (b) 27√3 (c) 9√3 (d) 3√81
Answer: (c) 9√3
Solution: Factoring the radicand into square factors: 243 = 81 × 3 = 9² × 3. Taking the square root: √243 = √(9² × 3) = 9√3. Therefore, the simplified mixed surd is 9√3. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q84. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 5, 7, and 9 leaves a remainder of 3 in each case?
(a) 312 (b) 315 (c) 321 (d) 318
Answer: (d) 318
Solution: The required number is of the form LCM(5, 7, 9) × k + 3. Since 5, 7, and 9 are pairwise coprime, LCM(5, 7, 9) = 5 × 7 × 9 = 315. For the smallest positive integer (k = 1), the number is 315 + 3 = 318. Hence, the required smallest number is 318. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q85. What is the unit digit of the expression 7^1 + 7^2 + 7^3 + ... + 7^100?
(a) 7 (b) 0 (c) 3 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 0
Solution: The unit digits of powers of 7 follow a cycle of length 4: 7, 9, 3, 1. The sum of unit digits in each 4-term cycle is 7 + 9 + 3 + 1 = 20, which ends in 0. Since 100 terms form exactly 25 complete cycles of 4 terms each, the overall unit digit is 0. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D

Q86. The decimal expansion of the rational number 13 / 3125 will terminate after how many decimal places?
(a) 5 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 7
Answer: (a) 5
Solution: The fraction 13 / 3125 is in irreducible simplest form. The denominator factorizes as 3125 = 5⁵ = 2⁰ × 5⁵. The number of decimal places after which the terminating decimal terminates equals the maximum exponent of 2 and 5, which is max(0, 5) = 5. Thus, it terminates after 5 decimal places. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q87. The LCM of two numbers is 14 times their HCF. The sum of the LCM and HCF is 600. If one of the numbers is 280, what is the other number?
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 80 (d) 140
Answer: (c) 80
Solution: Let HCF = h. Then LCM = 14h. Given: h + 14h = 600 ⇒ 15h = 600 ⇒ h = 40. Therefore, LCM = 14 × 40 = 560. Using the identity Product of numbers = HCF × LCM: 280 × Other = 40 × 560 = 22400. Other number = 22400 / 280 = 80. Hence, the other number is 80. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q88. What is the number of trailing zeros in the product 50! = 1 × 2 × 3 × ... × 50?
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 15 (d) 8
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: The number of trailing zeros in n! is determined by the highest power of 5 dividing n!, given by Legendre's formula: floor(50 / 5) + floor(50 / 25) = 10 + 2 = 12. Thus, the total number of trailing zeros is 12. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC MTS

Q89. What is the smallest natural number by which 1200 must be divided so that the quotient becomes a perfect cube?
(a) 75 (b) 100 (c) 300 (d) 150
Answer: (d) 150
Solution: Prime factorization of 1200: 1200 = 12 × 100 = (2² × 3) × (2² × 5²) = 2⁴ × 3¹ × 5². For the quotient to be a perfect cube, every prime exponent must be a multiple of 3. We must divide out the non-multiple-of-3 parts: 2¹ (leaving 2³), 3¹ (leaving 3⁰), and 5² (leaving 5⁰). The required divisor is 2¹ × 3¹ × 5² = 2 × 3 × 25 = 150. Dividing 1200 by 150 gives 8 = 2³, which is a perfect cube. (Dividing by 75 gives 16, a square but not a cube). Hence, the smallest divisor is 150. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q90. What is the sum of all prime numbers lying between 10 and 30?
(a) 112 (b) 120 (c) 102 (d) 128
Answer: (a) 112
Solution: The prime numbers between 10 and 30 are 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, and 29. Adding these prime numbers together: 11 + 13 + 17 + 19 + 23 + 29 = 112. Therefore, the sum is 112. Hence, the correct option is (a).

Q91. If the six-digit number 6x5432 is completely divisible by 3, which of the following is a possible value for digit x?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: By the divisibility rule for 3, the sum of all digits must be a multiple of 3. Sum = 6 + x + 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 20 + x. For 20 + x to be divisible by 3, possible single-digit values of x are 1 (sum = 21), 4 (sum = 24), and 7 (sum = 27). Among the given options, the only matching value is 4. Thus, a possible value for x is 4. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL

Q92. What is the mathematical classification of the sum of the two irrational numbers √5 and 2√5?
(a) A rational number (b) An irrational number (c) An integer (d) A terminating decimal
Answer: (b) An irrational number
Solution: Adding the two numbers: √5 + 2√5 = (1 + 2)√5 = 3√5. Since √5 is an irrational number and 3 is a non-zero rational number, their product 3√5 is strictly irrational. Thus, the sum is An irrational number. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q93. The HCF and LCM of two positive numbers are 9 and 360 respectively. If one of the numbers is 45, what is the value of the other number?
(a) 81 (b) 90 (c) 36 (d) 72
Answer: (d) 72
Solution: Using the fundamental relationship Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM: 45 × Other = 9 × 360 = 3240. Dividing by 45 gives: Other number = 3240 / 45 = 72. Therefore, the value of the other number is 72. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC MTS

Q94. When the repeating decimal 0.2333... is converted to a rational fraction in its simplest form, the result is:
(a) 7/30 (b) 23/99 (c) 21/90 (d) 2/9
Answer: (a) 7/30
Solution: Let x = 0.2333... Multiplying by 10 gives 10x = 2.333... and multiplying by 100 gives 100x = 23.333... Subtracting the two equations: 100x – 10x = 23.333... – 2.333... ⇒ 90x = 21 ⇒ x = 21 / 90. Dividing numerator and denominator by 3 gives 7 / 30. Thus, the simplest rational fraction is 7/30. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q95. What is the unit digit of the product (57)^41 × (32)^20?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: For 57^41, the unit digit depends on 7. Powers of 7 have cyclicity 4 (7, 9, 3, 1). Since 41 mod 4 = 1, 57^41 ends in 7. For 32^20, the unit digit depends on 2. Powers of 2 have cyclicity 4 (2, 4, 8, 6). Since 20 mod 4 = 0, 32^20 ends in 6. The product ends in 7 × 6 = 42, which has a unit digit of 2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D

Q96. What is the remainder when the product 345 × 456 × 567 is divided by 10?
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 0 (d) 6
Answer: (c) 0
Solution: The remainder when any positive integer is divided by 10 is equal to its unit digit. The unit digits of the three factors are 5, 6, and 7. Multiplying them: 5 × 6 = 30 (unit digit 0), and 0 × 7 = 0. Since the unit digit of the product is 0, dividing by 10 leaves a remainder of 0. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL

Q97. What is the largest four-digit number that is completely divisible by 9, 12, 15, and 18?
(a) 9990 (b) 9945 (c) 9972 (d) 9900
Answer: (d) 9900
Solution: First find LCM(9, 12, 15, 18): 9 = 3², 12 = 2² × 3, 15 = 3 × 5, 18 = 2 × 3². Thus, LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 4 × 9 × 5 = 180. The largest 4-digit number is 9999. Dividing 9999 by 180: 9999 = 180 × 55 + 99. Subtracting the remainder 99 gives 9999 – 99 = 9900. Hence, the largest 4-digit number divisible by all four numbers is 9900. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q98. A number consists of two digits whose sum is 8. If 18 is added to the number, the digits are reversed. What is the number?
(a) 35 (b) 53 (c) 26 (d) 62
Answer: (a) 35
Solution: Let the number be 10a + b with a + b = 8. Adding 18 reverses the digits: (10a + b) + 18 = 10b + a ⇒ 9b – 9a = 18 ⇒ b – a = 2. Adding the two equations (a + b = 8 and b – a = 2) yields 2b = 10 ⇒ b = 5, which gives a = 3. Thus, the original two-digit number is 35. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC MTS

Q99. Which of the following numbers is an irrational number?
(a) √25 (b) √2.5 (c) √(4/9) (d) √0.01
Answer: (b) √2.5
Solution: Evaluating each root: √25 = 5 (rational), √(4/9) = 2/3 (rational), and √0.01 = 0.1 = 1/10 (rational). However, √2.5 = √(25/10) = √(5/2) = √10 / 2. Since 10 is not a perfect square, √10 is irrational, making the entire expression irrational. Thus, the irrational number is √2.5. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q100. What is the total number of prime factors (counting multiplicities) of 2³ × 3² × 5 × 7?
(a) 6 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 4
Answer: (c) 7
Solution: The number of prime factors counting repetitions equals the sum of the exponents of all prime factors in the canonical prime factorization. Here, the prime factors are 2 with exponent 3, 3 with exponent 2, 5 with exponent 1, and 7 with exponent 1. Total count = 3 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 7. Thus, the total number of prime factors is 7. Hence, the correct option is (c).
