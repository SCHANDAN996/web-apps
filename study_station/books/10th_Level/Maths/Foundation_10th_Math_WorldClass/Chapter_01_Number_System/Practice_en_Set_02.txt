Q26. Which of the following numbers is divisible by both 4 and 6, but not by 8?
(a) 312 (b) 432 (c) 528 (d) 612
Answer: (d) 612
Solution:

Divisible by 4: last two digits divisible by 4.
312 → 12÷4=3 ✓, 432 → 32÷4=8 ✓, 528 → 28÷4=7 ✓, 612 → 12÷4=3 ✓.

Divisible by 6: divisible by 2 (even) and sum of digits divisible by 3. All are even; check sum of digits: 312 (6)✓, 432 (9)✓, 528 (15)✓, 612 (9)✓.

Not by 8: last three digits divisible by 8.
312 → 312÷8=39 (divisible), out.
432 → 432÷8=54 (divisible), out.
528 → 528÷8=66 (divisible), out.
612 → 612÷8=76.5 (not divisible). So 612 qualifies.
