Q26. (Medium) Which of the following numbers is divisible by both 4 and 6, but NOT by 8?
(A) 612 (B) 312 (C) 432 (D) 528

Answer: (A) 612

Solution:
A number divisible by both 4 and 6 must be a multiple of LCM(4, 6) = 12. For 612: 612 / 12 = 51 (divisible by 12), and 612 / 8 = 76.5 (not divisible by 8).
