Q126. If p and q are prime numbers such that p + q = 99, what is the value of the product p × q?
(a) 194 (b) 485 (c) 1940 (d) 4850
Answer: (a) 194
Solution: The sum of two integers is odd (99) if and only if one integer is even and the other is odd. The only even prime number is 2, so one of the primes must be 2. Setting p = 2 gives q = 99 – 2 = 97, which is indeed prime. Multiplying the two primes gives p × q = 2 × 97 = 194. Thus, the product is 194. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q127. What is the remainder when (2^100 + 3^100) is divided by 5?
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: Using modular arithmetic modulo 5: 2⁴ = 16 ≡ 1 (mod 5), so 2^100 = (2⁴)²⁵ ≡ 1²⁵ ≡ 1 (mod 5). Similarly, 3⁴ = 81 ≡ 1 (mod 5), so 3^100 = (3⁴)²⁵ ≡ 1²⁵ ≡ 1 (mod 5). Adding the congruences gives 2^100 + 3^100 ≡ 1 + 1 ≡ 2 (mod 5). Therefore, the remainder is 2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q128. How many positive integers n exist such that n² + 96 is a perfect square?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: Let n² + 96 = m² for some positive integer m > n. Rearranging gives m² – n² = (m – n)(m + n) = 96. Both factors must have the same parity; since 96 is even, both must be even. Let m – n = 2a and m + n = 2b, giving 4ab = 96 ⇒ ab = 24 with 1 ≤ a < b. The factor pairs (a, b) of 24 are (1, 24), (2, 12), (3, 8), and (4, 6). Each pair yields a unique positive integer n = b – a (values: 23, 10, 5, 2). Thus, there are exactly 4 positive integers. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q129. What is the digit in the unit place of the product 7^71 × 8^72 × 9^73?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 2
Answer: (d) 2
Solution: Finding unit digits: 7 has cyclicity 4 (7, 9, 3, 1); 71 mod 4 = 3, so 7^71 ends in 3. 8 has cyclicity 4 (8, 4, 2, 6); 72 mod 4 = 0, so 8^72 ends in 6. 9 has cyclicity 2 (9, 1); 73 is odd, so 9^73 ends in 9. Multiplying unit digits: 3 × 6 = 18 (unit digit 8), and 8 × 9 = 72 (unit digit 2). Therefore, the digit in the unit place is 2. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q130. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 5, 6, 7, and 8 leaves a remainder of 3 in each case, but is completely divisible by 9?
(a) 1683 (b) 843 (c) 2523 (d) 3363
Answer: (a) 1683
Solution: LCM(5, 6, 7, 8) = 840. The number must be of the form 840k + 3. For divisibility by 9: 840k + 3 ≡ 3k + 3 ≡ 3(k + 1) ≡ 0 (mod 9) because 840 = 93 × 9 + 3. For 3(k + 1) to be a multiple of 9, k + 1 must be a multiple of 3. The smallest positive integer is k = 2. Substituting k = 2: 840 × 2 + 3 = 1680 + 3 = 1683. Dividing 1683 by 9 gives 187 with 0 remainder. Hence, the required smallest number is 1683. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB Group D

Q131. What is the total number of factors of 3600 that are perfect squares?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 10 (d) 15
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: Prime factorization of 3600: 3600 = 36 × 100 = (2² × 3²) × (2² × 5²) = 2⁴ × 3² × 5². A divisor is a perfect square if and only if all exponents in its prime factorization are even. For 2⁴, the even exponents can be 0, 2, or 4 (3 choices). For 3², the even exponents can be 0 or 2 (2 choices). For 5², the even exponents can be 0 or 2 (2 choices). The total number of perfect square factors is 3 × 2 × 2 = 12. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL

Q132. The decimal expansion of 1/7 is the repeating decimal 0.142857 (with period 6). What digit appears in the 2024th decimal place?

Q133. If x and y are positive integers satisfying the equation x² – y² = 105, how many distinct pairs (x, y) exist?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 10 (d) 4
Answer: (d) 4
Solution: Factoring gives (x – y)(x + y) = 105. Since x and y are positive integers, 0 < x – y < x + y, and both factors must have the same parity (both odd, since 105 is odd). Prime factors of 105 = 3 × 5 × 7. The total number of divisors of 105 is (1 + 1)(1 + 1)(1 + 1) = 8. Dividing into pairs (d1, d2) with d1 < d2 gives 8 / 2 = 4 pairs: (1, 105), (3, 35), (5, 21), and (7, 15). Each pair gives unique positive integers x = (d1 + d2)/2 and y = (d2 – d1)/2. Hence, there are exactly 4 pairs. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC MTS

Q134. What is the sum of all two-digit positive integers that leave a remainder of 3 when divided by 7?
(a) 676 (b) 726 (c) 776 (d) 826
Answer: (a) 676
Solution: Numbers leaving remainder 3 when divided by 7 have the form 7k + 3. The smallest two-digit term occurs at k = 1: 7(1) + 3 = 10. The largest two-digit term satisfies 7k + 3 ≤ 99 ⇒ 7k ≤ 96 ⇒ k = 13: 7(13) + 3 = 94. The sequence is an arithmetic progression with first term a = 10, common difference d = 7, last term l = 94, and number of terms n = 13 – 1 + 1 = 13. Using the sum formula: S = n(a + l) / 2 = 13 × (10 + 94) / 2 = 13 × 104 / 2 = 13 × 52 = 676. Thus, the sum is 676. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q135. What is the largest power of 12 that completely divides 100! (factorial 100)?
(a) 47 (b) 48 (c) 49 (d) 50
Answer: (b) 48
Solution: Since 12 = 2² × 3, the power of 12 dividing 100! is min(floor(power of 2 / 2), power of 3). Using Legendre's formula for 3: floor(100/3) + floor(100/9) + floor(100/27) + floor(100/81) = 33 + 11 + 3 + 1 = 48. For 2: floor(100/2) + floor(100/4) + floor(100/8) + floor(100/16) + floor(100/32) + floor(100/64) = 50 + 25 + 12 + 6 + 3 + 1 = 97. Power of 2² is floor(97 / 2) = 48. Taking the minimum gives min(48, 48) = 48. Therefore, the largest power of 12 is 48. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D

Q136. Which of the following numbers is an irrational number (not a rational number)?
(a) 0.16 (with 6 repeating) (b) ∛8 (c) √8 (d) 0.5
Answer: (c) √8
Solution: Evaluating each number: 0.1666... = 1/6 (rational fraction), ∛8 = 2 (rational integer), and 0.5 = 1/2 (rational fraction). However, √8 = √(4 × 2) = 2√2. Since √2 is irrational, 2√2 is irrational. Thus, the irrational number is √8. Hence, the correct option is (c).

Q138. If n = 2¹² × 3⁹ × 5³ × 7³, what is the total number of factors of n that are perfect cubes?
(a) 80 (b) 60 (c) 100 (d) 120
Answer: (a) 80
Solution: A divisor is a perfect cube if and only if all prime exponents in its prime factorization are multiples of 3. For 2¹², the possible exponents are 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 (5 choices). For 3⁹, the possible exponents are 0, 3, 6, 9 (4 choices). For 5³, the possible exponents are 0, 3 (2 choices). For 7³, the possible exponents are 0, 3 (2 choices). Total number of perfect cube factors = 5 × 4 × 2 × 2 = 80. Thus, the number of factors is 80. Hence, the correct option is (a).

Q139. What is the remainder when the factorial series 1! + 2! + 3! + ... + 100! is divided by 12?
(a) 5 (b) 9 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 9
Solution: Notice that for any n ≥ 4, n! contains 4! = 24 as a factor, which is a multiple of 12. Therefore, n! ≡ 0 (mod 12) for all n ≥ 4. The remainder is determined solely by the sum of the first three terms: 1! + 2! + 3! = 1 + 2 + 6 = 9. Dividing 9 by 12 leaves a remainder of 9. Hence, the remainder is 9. Hence, the correct option is (b).

Q140. What is the smallest positive integer which when increased by 17 is completely divisible by both 520 and 468?
(a) 4680 (b) 4653 (c) 4663 (d) 4673
Answer: (c) 4663
Solution: Prime factorizations: 520 = 2³ × 5 × 13, and 468 = 2² × 3² × 13. LCM(520, 468) = 2³ × 3² × 5 × 13 = 8 × 9 × 5 × 13 = 4680. The required number N satisfies N + 17 = 4680 ⇒ N = 4680 – 17 = 4663. Thus, the smallest number is 4663. Hence, the correct option is (c).

Q141. If a and b are rational numbers such that (3 + √5) / (3 – √5) = a + b√5, then what is the value of a + b?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 7 (d) 5
Answer: (d) 5
Solution: Rationalizing the denominator: (3 + √5)² / ((3 – √5)(3 + √5)) = (9 + 5 + 6√5) / (9 – 5) = (14 + 6√5) / 4 = 7/2 + (3/2)√5. Comparing coefficients gives a = 7/2 and b = 3/2. Adding the two rational numbers: a + b = 7/2 + 3/2 = 10/2 = 5. Therefore, the value of a + b is 5. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL

Q142. How many positive integers from 1 to 1000 are divisible by 2, 3, and 5, but not by 7?
(a) 29 (b) 27 (c) 28 (d) 30
Answer: (a) 29
Solution: A number divisible by 2, 3, and 5 is a multiple of LCM(2, 3, 5) = 30. The number of multiples of 30 up to 1000 is floor(1000 / 30) = 33. Among these, those also divisible by 7 are multiples of LCM(30, 7) = 210. The number of multiples of 210 up to 1000 is floor(1000 / 210) = 4 (these are 210, 420, 630, and 840). Subtracting these gives 33 – 4 = 29 integers. Thus, there are 29 such integers. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q144. What is the remainder when 10^98 is divided by 10^96 + 1?

Answer: (c) 10^96 – 99
Solution: Let the divisor be d = 10^96 + 1, so 10^96 = d – 1. Then 10^98 = 10^96 × 100 = 100(d – 1) = 100d – 100 = 99d + (d – 100). Because d = 10^96 + 1 > 100, the remainder r = d – 100 = (10^96 + 1) – 100 = 10^96 – 99, which satisfies 0 ≤ r < d. Thus, the remainder is 10^96 – 99. Hence, the correct option is (c).

Q147. For any integer n > 1, why is n⁴ + 4 always a composite number?
(a) It factorizes as (n² + 2)² (b) It factorizes as (n² + 2n + 2)(n² – 2n + 2) (c) It factorizes as (n² + 2)(n² – 2) (d) It factorizes as (n + 1)⁴
Answer: (b) It factorizes as (n² + 2n + 2)(n² – 2n + 2)
Solution: By Sophie Germain's identity: n⁴ + 4 = n⁴ + 4n² + 4 – 4n² = (n² + 2)² – (2n)² = (n² + 2n + 2)(n² – 2n + 2). For any integer n > 1, the smaller factor is n² – 2n + 2 = (n – 1)² + 1 > 1. Because n⁴ + 4 factors into the product of two integers strictly greater than 1, it is composite. Thus, the algebraic factorization is It factorizes as (n² + 2n + 2)(n² – 2n + 2). Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q148. When simplified, the nested radical expression √(28 + √300) is equal to:

Q150. If x = (√5 – √3) / (√5 + √3) and y = (√5 + √3) / (√5 – √3), what is the value of x + y?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 16 (d) 32
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: Rationalizing the terms: x = (√5 – √3)² / ((√5 + √3)(√5 – √3)) = (5 + 3 – 2√15) / (5 – 3) = (8 – 2√15) / 2 = 4 – √15. Similarly, y = (√5 + √3)² / 2 = (8 + 2√15) / 2 = 4 + √15. Adding the two expressions gives x + y = (4 – √15) + (4 + √15) = 4 + 4 = 8. Thus, the value of x + y is 8. Hence, the correct option is (b).
