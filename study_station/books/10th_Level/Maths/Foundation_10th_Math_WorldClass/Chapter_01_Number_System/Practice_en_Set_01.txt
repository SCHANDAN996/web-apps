Set 1: Number System MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Q1. Which of the following is a natural number?
(a) –5 (b) 0 (c) 7 (d) ½
Answer: (c) 7
Solution: Natural numbers are counting numbers starting from 1, i.e. {1,2,3,…}. –5 is an integer, 0 is a whole number, ½ is a rational fraction. Only 7 fits.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q2. The smallest whole number is
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) –1 (d) 2
Answer: (b) 0
Solution: Whole numbers = {0,1,2,3,…}. The smallest is 0.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q3. Which symbol represents the set of integers?
(a) N (b) W (c) Z (d) Q
Answer: (c) Z
Solution: N = natural, W = whole, Z = integers (from German "Zahlen"), Q = rational.
Source: NCERT Class 7

Q4. The number 0 is
(a) natural (b) whole but not integer (c) integer (d) irrational
Answer: (c) integer
Solution: 0 is a whole number and also an integer. It is not natural. So "integer" correctly describes it.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q5. Every integer is a rational number. True or false?
(a) True (b) False
Answer: (a) True
Solution: Any integer n can be written as n/1, which is a ratio of two integers with denominator not zero. Hence every integer is rational.
Source: NCERT Class 8

Q6. Which of the following is an irrational number?
(a) √4 (b) 3.14 (c) √3 (d) 0.333…
Answer: (c) √3
Solution: √4 = 2, rational. 3.14 terminates, rational. 0.333… = 1/3, rational. √3 is non-terminating non-repeating, hence irrational.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q7. π (pi) is
(a) rational (b) irrational (c) integer (d) whole
Answer: (b) irrational
Solution: π is a non-terminating, non-repeating decimal; cannot be expressed as a fraction of integers. Hence irrational.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q8. The rational number 3/5 is also a/an
(a) integer (b) natural number (c) whole number (d) real number
Answer: (d) real number
Solution: All rationals are real numbers. 3/5 is not an integer, natural, or whole number.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q9. The only even prime number is
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (c) 2
Solution: 2 is prime (factors 1 and 2). All other even numbers are divisible by 2, hence have at least three factors and are composite.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q10. Which number is neither prime nor composite?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 1
Solution: By definition, prime numbers have exactly two distinct positive factors; composites have more than two. 1 has only one factor (itself), so it is neither.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q11. 91 is
(a) prime (b) composite (c) neither prime nor composite (d) irrational
Answer: (b) composite
Solution: 91 = 7 × 13. It has factors 1, 7, 13, 91, hence composite.
Source: SSC CGL

Q12. Which of the following is a prime number?
(a) 21 (b) 27 (c) 29 (d) 33
Answer: (c) 29
Solution: 21 = 3×7, 27 = 3×9, 33 = 3×11. 29 has no divisors other than 1 and 29.
Source: Bank PO

Q13. The set of rational numbers is denoted by
(a) R (b) Z (c) Q (d) N
Answer: (c) Q
Solution: Q stands for "quotient". R = real, Z = integers, N = natural.
Source: NCERT Class 8

Q14. The number 0.75 expressed as a rational number in simplest form is
(a) 3/4 (b) 75/100 (c) 15/20 (d) 7/5
Answer: (a) 3/4
Solution: 0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4 after dividing numerator and denominator by 25.
Source: NCERT Class 7

Q15. All natural numbers are whole numbers. True or false?
(a) True (b) False
Answer: (a) True
Solution: Natural numbers {1,2,3,…} are a subset of whole numbers {0,1,2,3,…}.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q16. Which of the following is a terminating decimal?
(a) 1/3 (b) 2/7 (c) 5/8 (d) 1/11
Answer: (c) 5/8
Solution: 1/3 = 0.333… (repeating), 2/7 ≈ 0.285714… (repeating), 1/11 = 0.0909… (repeating). 5/8 = 0.625 (terminates).
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q17. The number –7 belongs to which of the following sets?
(a) Natural (b) Whole (c) Integer (d) None
Answer: (c) Integer
Solution: Negative numbers are integers, not natural or whole.
Source: NCERT Class 7

Q18. √25 is
(a) irrational (b) rational (c) neither (d) a surd
Answer: (b) rational
Solution: √25 = 5, which can be written as 5/1, so rational.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q19. The sum of two rational numbers is always
(a) rational (b) irrational (c) integer (d) whole
Answer: (a) rational
Solution: Rational numbers are closed under addition: a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/bd, which is rational.
Source: NCERT Class 8

Q20. 0.333… expressed as a fraction in lowest terms is
(a) 1/3 (b) 3/9 (c) 33/100 (d) 1/2
Answer: (a) 1/3
Solution: Let x = 0.333…, then 10x = 3.333…. Subtract: 9x = 3, so x = 1/3.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q21. (Medium) If a number is divided by 7, it leaves remainder 3. What will be the remainder when its square is divided by 7?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Let the number be N = 7k + 3. Then N² = 49k² + 42k + 9 = 7(7k² + 6k + 1) + 2. So remainder is 2.
Source: SSC CGL

Q22. The unit digit of 3²⁰²⁴ is
(a) 3 (b) 9 (c) 7 (d) 1
Answer: (d) 1
Solution: Cyclicity of 3: 3¹=3, 3²=9, 3³=27(unit 7), 3⁴=81(unit 1). Cycle length = 4. Divide 2024 by 4: remainder 0, so unit digit = same as 3⁴ = 1.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q23. (Medium) How many prime numbers are there between 40 and 50?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (b) 3
Solution: Numbers 41, 43, 47 are prime. 42, 44, 45, 46, 48, 49, 50 are composite. So count = 3.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q24. (Medium) If the 5-digit number 312x5 is divisible by 9, the digit x is
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 7
Answer: (d) 7
Solution: Divisibility by 9: sum of digits must be a multiple of 9. Sum = 3+1+2+x+5 = 11+x. For 11+x = 18, x = 7.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q25. (Medium) Which of the following statements is always false?
(a) The sum of two prime numbers is even.
(b) The product of two irrational numbers is irrational.
(c) Every rational number is a real number.
(d) Every integer is a whole number.
Answer: (d) Every integer is a whole number.
Solution: (a) Not always true (2+3=5, odd), but not always false either. (b) Not always true (√2 × √2 = 2), but not always false. (c) Always true. (d) Always false because negative integers (like -3) are not whole numbers.
Source: SSC CGL
