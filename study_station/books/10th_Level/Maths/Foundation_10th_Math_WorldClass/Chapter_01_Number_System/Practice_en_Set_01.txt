Q1. (Easy) Which of the following is a natural number?
(A) 7 (B) –5 (C) 0 (D) 1/2

Answer: (A) 7

Solution:
Natural numbers are positive counting numbers starting from 1, i.e., {1, 2, 3, ...}. While –5 is a negative integer, 0 is a whole number, and 1/2 is a fraction, the number 7 is a natural number. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q2. (Easy) What is the smallest whole number?
(A) 1 (B) 0 (C) –1 (D) 2

Answer: (B) 0

Solution:
Whole numbers include zero and all natural numbers: W = {0, 1, 2, 3, ...}. The smallest whole number is 0. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q5. (Easy) Which of the following statements about integers is correct?
(A) Every integer is a rational number (B) Every rational number is an integer (C) No integer is a rational number (D) Only positive integers are rational numbers

Answer: (A) Every integer is a rational number

Solution:
Any integer n can be expressed in the form p/q as n/1, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0. Therefore, every integer is a rational number. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Q6. (Easy) Which of the following is an irrational number?
(A) √4 (B) √3 (C) 3.14 (D) 0.333...

Answer: (B) √3

Solution:
√4 = 2 (rational), 3.14 is a terminating decimal 314/100 (rational), and 0.333... = 1/3 (rational). √3 has a non-terminating, non-repeating decimal expansion, making it an irrational number. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CGL

Q10. (Easy) Which number is neither prime nor composite?

Solution:
A prime number must have exactly two distinct positive divisors, and a composite number must have more than two. The number 1 has only one positive divisor (itself), so 1 is neither prime nor composite. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Q16. (Easy) Which of the following fractions represents a terminating decimal?
(A) 1/11 (B) 1/3 (C) 2/7 (D) 5/8

Answer: (D) 5/8

Solution:
A rational number in lowest terms has a terminating decimal if and only if the prime factorization of its denominator contains only 2s and/or 5s. Here, the denominator of 5/8 is 8 = 2³, so 5/8 = 0.625 (terminating). Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: SSC CGL

Q21. (Medium) If a positive integer N leaves a remainder of 3 when divided by 7, what will be the remainder when N² is divided by 7?
(A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 5

Answer: (A) 2

Solution:
Let N = 7k + 3. Then N² = (7k + 3)² = 49k² + 42k + 9 = 7(7k² + 6k + 1) + 2. Dividing 9 by 7 gives a remainder of 2. Thus, the remainder is 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).

Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) (⚠️ Examiner's Trap) Find the unit digit of the expression 3²⁰²⁴.
(A) 3 (B) 1 (C) 7 (D) 9

Answer: (B) 1

Solution:
The powers of 3 have a repeating unit-digit cyclicity of 4: 3¹ = 3, 3² = 9, 3³ = 27 (unit 7), 3⁴ = 81 (unit 1). Dividing the exponent 2024 by 4 gives a remainder of 0 (exact multiple). For remainder 0, the unit digit corresponds to 3⁴, which is 1. Hence, the unit digit is 1. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: SSC CHSL

Q24. (Medium) If the five-digit number 312x5 is completely divisible by 9, find the value of digit x.
(A) 6 (B) 4 (C) 5 (D) 7

Answer: (D) 7

Solution:
A number is divisible by 9 if the sum of its digits is a multiple of 9. Sum = 3 + 1 + 2 + x + 5 = 11 + x. The smallest single digit x making 11 + x a multiple of 9 is x = 18 - 11 = 7. Therefore, the missing digit x is 7. Hence, the correct option is (D).

Source: RRB NTPC

Q25. (Medium) (⚠️ Examiner's Trap) Which of the following statements is ALWAYS false?
(A) The sum of two prime numbers is even (B) Every integer is a whole number (C) The product of two irrational numbers is irrational (D) Every rational number is a real number

Answer: (B) Every integer is a whole number

Solution:
Negative integers (such as –1, –2, –3) are integers but not whole numbers, so 'Every integer is a whole number' is always false. By contrast, the sum of two primes can be even (3+5=8), the product of two irrationals can be irrational (√2 × √3 = √6), and every rational is real. Hence, the correct option is (B).

Source: RRB Group D
