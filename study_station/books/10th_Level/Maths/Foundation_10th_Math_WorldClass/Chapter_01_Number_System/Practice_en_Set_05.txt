Questions 101–125: Medium to Hard

Q101. Which of the following four-digit numbers is completely divisible by 8?
(a) 5216 (b) 3124 (c) 7138 (d) 9230
Answer: (a) 5216
Solution: By the divisibility rule for 8, a number is divisible by 8 if the number formed by its last three digits is divisible by 8. Testing the options: for 5216, the last three digits form 216, and 216 / 8 = 27 (divisible with remainder 0). For the others, 124, 138, and 230 are not divisible by 8. Therefore, the number divisible by 8 is 5216. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q102. What is the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of 18, 24, and 32?
(a) 144 (b) 288 (c) 432 (d) 576
Answer: (b) 288
Solution: Writing prime factorizations: 18 = 2¹ × 3², 24 = 2³ × 3¹, and 32 = 2⁵. To find the LCM, take the highest power of each prime factor occurring in the numbers: LCM = 2⁵ × 3² = 32 × 9 = 288. Thus, the LCM is 288. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q103. What is the unit digit of the exponential expression 4^257?
(a) 2 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: The unit digit of powers of 4 follows a pattern of length 2: 4¹ = 4 (odd exponent) and 4² = 16 (even exponent ends in 6). Since the exponent 257 is an odd integer, the expression ends in 4. Hence, the unit digit is 4. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q104. The value of the square root √50 lies between which two consecutive integers?
(a) 5 and 6 (b) 6 and 7 (c) 4 and 5 (d) 7 and 8
Answer: (d) 7 and 8
Solution: Evaluating squares of consecutive integers: 7² = 49 and 8² = 64. Since 49 < 50 < 64, taking square roots gives 7 < √50 < 8. Therefore, √50 lies between 7 and 8. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q105. If a positive integer is completely divisible by both 4 and 6, then it must always be divisible by:
(a) 8 (b) 24 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (d) 12
Solution: A number divisible by two numbers must be divisible by their Least Common Multiple (LCM). Prime factors: 4 = 2² and 6 = 2 × 3, so LCM(4, 6) = 2² × 3 = 12. Thus, the number is always divisible by 12. (It is not necessarily divisible by 24, as 12 is divisible by both 4 and 6 but not by 24). Hence, it must be divisible by 12. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB Group D

Q106. When the fraction 7 / 80 is converted to a terminating decimal, how many decimal places does it have?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 5
Answer: (a) 4
Solution: The fraction 7 / 80 is in irreducible form. Factoring the denominator gives 80 = 2⁴ × 5¹. The number of decimal places of a terminating decimal equals the maximum of the exponents of 2 and 5 in the denominator: max(4, 1) = 4. Indeed, 7 / 80 = 0.0875, which has 4 decimal places. Thus, the number of decimal places is 4. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q107. The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is 51. What is the smallest of these three numbers?
(a) 13 (b) 15 (c) 17 (d) 11
Answer: (b) 15
Solution: Let the three consecutive odd numbers be x, x + 2, and x + 4. Their sum is x + (x + 2) + (x + 4) = 3x + 6. Setting this equal to 51 gives 3x + 6 = 51 ⇒ 3x = 45 ⇒ x = 15. The three numbers are 15, 17, and 19. Therefore, the smallest of them is 15. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q108. What is the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 84 and 126?
(a) 21 (b) 14 (c) 42 (d) 28
Answer: (c) 42
Solution: Prime factorizations of both numbers: 84 = 2² × 3 × 7 and 126 = 2 × 3² × 7. Taking the lowest power of each common prime factor gives HCF = 2¹ × 3¹ × 7¹ = 42. Thus, the HCF is 42. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q109. Which of the following numbers is a rational number lying strictly between 1/3 and 1/2?
(a) 7/12 only (b) 3/5 (c) 1/6 (d) Both 2/5 and 5/12
Answer: (d) Both 2/5 and 5/12
Solution: In decimal form, 1/3 ≈ 0.333... and 1/2 = 0.500. Now test the values: 2/5 = 0.400, which lies between 0.333... and 0.500; and 5/12 ≈ 0.4167, which also lies between 0.333... and 0.500. Since both 2/5 and 5/12 lie within the given open interval, the correct choice is Both 2/5 and 5/12. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q110. What is the smallest four-digit number that is exactly divisible by 18, 24, and 30?
(a) 1080 (b) 1020 (c) 1100 (d) 1200
Answer: (a) 1080
Solution: Prime factorizations: 18 = 2 × 3², 24 = 2³ × 3, 30 = 2 × 3 × 5. LCM(18, 24, 30) = 2³ × 3² × 5 = 8 × 9 × 5 = 360. We find the smallest four-digit multiple of 360: 360 × 2 = 720 (3 digits), while 360 × 3 = 1080 (4 digits). Hence, the smallest four-digit number divisible by all three numbers is 1080. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB Group D

Q111. When the repeating decimal 0.444... is expressed as a rational fraction in simplest form, the result is:
(a) 2/5 (b) 4/9 (c) 44/99 (d) 1/2
Answer: (b) 4/9
Solution: Let x = 0.444... Multiplying by 10 gives 10x = 4.444... Subtracting the first equation from the second yields 10x – x = 4.444... – 0.444... ⇒ 9x = 4 ⇒ x = 4/9. Thus, the simplest rational fraction is 4/9. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL

Q112. What is the unit digit of the product (13)^24 × (17)^36?
(a) 3 (b) 7 (c) 1 (d) 9
Answer: (c) 1
Solution: For 13^24, the base ends in 3, whose powers have cyclicity 4 (3, 9, 7, 1). Since 24 mod 4 = 0, 13^24 ends in 1. For 17^36, the base ends in 7, whose powers also have cyclicity 4 (7, 9, 3, 1). Since 36 mod 4 = 0, 17^36 ends in 1. Multiplying unit digits: 1 × 1 = 1. Thus, the unit digit is 1. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q113. The sum of two positive prime numbers is 30. How many distinct pairs of such prime numbers exist?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (d) 3
Solution: Testing pairs of prime numbers summing to 30: (7, 23), (11, 19), and (13, 17). Pairs like (3, 27) and (5, 25) do not qualify because 27 and 25 are composite. Thus, there are exactly 3 valid prime pairs. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC MTS

Q114. What is the total number of positive factors (divisors) of 198?
(a) 12 (b) 8 (c) 10 (d) 14
Answer: (a) 12
Solution: Prime factorization of 198: 198 = 2 × 99 = 2¹ × 3² × 11¹. The total number of positive factors is obtained by adding 1 to each exponent and multiplying: (1 + 1)(2 + 1)(1 + 1) = 2 × 3 × 2 = 12. Thus, the total number of factors is 12. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q115. What is the least positive integer which when divided by 8, 12, 16, and 24 leaves a remainder of 5 in each case?
(a) 49 (b) 53 (c) 77 (d) 101
Answer: (b) 53
Solution: The required number is of the form LCM(8, 12, 16, 24) × k + 5. Prime factorizations: 8 = 2³, 12 = 2² × 3, 16 = 2⁴, 24 = 2³ × 3. LCM = 2⁴ × 3 = 16 × 3 = 48. For the smallest positive integer (k = 1), the number is 48 + 5 = 53. Hence, the least number is 53. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D

Q116. The product of the radicals √27 × √3 is equal to:
(a) 3 (b) 27 (c) 9 (d) √30
Answer: (c) 9
Solution: Using the product property of radicals: √27 × √3 = √(27 × 3) = √81. Since 81 = 9², √81 = 9. Therefore, the product is 9. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL

Q117. If the five-digit number 372x4 is completely divisible by 6, what is the smallest possible value of digit x?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 2
Answer: (d) 2
Solution: A number is divisible by 6 if it is divisible by both 2 and 3. The last digit is 4 (even), so it is divisible by 2 for any digit x. For divisibility by 3, the sum of digits must be a multiple of 3: Sum = 3 + 7 + 2 + x + 4 = 16 + x. The smallest single digit x making 16 + x a multiple of 3 is x = 18 – 16 = 2. Thus, the smallest possible value of x is 2. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q118. The product of two positive numbers is 4107 and their LCM is 111. What is their HCF?
(a) 37 (b) 27 (c) 39 (d) 111
Answer: (a) 37
Solution: Using the fundamental identity for any two numbers: Product of numbers = HCF × LCM. Substituting the given values gives 4107 = HCF × 111 ⇒ HCF = 4107 / 111 = 37. Therefore, the HCF is 37. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC MTS

Q119. Which of the following pairs of numbers represents twin primes?
(a) (17, 21) (b) (41, 43) (c) (23, 29) (d) (31, 37)
Answer: (b) (41, 43)
Solution: Twin primes are pairs of prime numbers that have a difference of exactly 2. Examining the pairs: 41 and 43 are both prime numbers, and 43 – 41 = 2. In contrast, 21 is composite in (17, 21), while 29 – 23 = 6 and 37 – 31 = 6. Thus, the only twin prime pair is (41, 43). Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q120. After how many decimal places does the decimal expansion of 31 / 16 terminate?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: The fraction 31 / 16 is in irreducible form. The denominator factorizes as 16 = 2⁴ = 2⁴ × 5⁰. The number of decimal places after which the decimal terminates equals max(4, 0) = 4. Indeed, 31 / 16 = 1.9375 (4 digits after the decimal point). Thus, it terminates after 4 decimal places. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB Group D

Q121. If n is a positive integer such that n² is completely divisible by 12, then which of the following statements must always be true?
(a) n must be divisible by 12 (b) n must be divisible by 4 (c) n must be divisible by 9 (d) n must be divisible by 6
Answer: (d) n must be divisible by 6
Solution: Prime factorization of 12 is 2² × 3. If 12 divides n², then 2² divides n² (meaning 2 divides n) and 3 divides n² (meaning 3 divides n). Because 2 and 3 both divide n, their product 6 must divide n. However, n does not need to be divisible by 12 (for instance, if n = 6, n² = 36 is divisible by 12, but 6 is not divisible by 12). Hence, n must be divisible by 6. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL

Q122. What is the remainder when 7^2024 is divided by 5?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 1
Solution: Modulo 5 arithmetic: 7 ≡ 2 (mod 5), so 7^2024 ≡ 2^2024 (mod 5). The powers of 2 modulo 5 cycle with period 4: 2¹ ≡ 2, 2² ≡ 4, 2³ ≡ 3, 2⁴ ≡ 1 (mod 5). Since 2024 is divisible by 4 (2024 = 4 × 506), 2^2024 ≡ (2⁴)^506 ≡ 1^506 ≡ 1 (mod 5). Hence, the remainder is 1. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q123. When simplified, the radical expression √(8 + 2√15) is equal to:
(a) √6 + √2 (b) √5 + √3 (c) √5 + 1 (d) √7 + 1
Answer: (b) √5 + √3
Solution: Let √(8 + 2√15) = √a + √b. Squaring both sides: a + b + 2√(ab) = 8 + 2√15. Comparing rational and radical components: a + b = 8 and ab = 15. The numbers satisfying this system are 5 and 3. Therefore, √(8 + 2√15) = √5 + √3. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC MTS

Q124. How many integers lying strictly between 100 and 500 are divisible by 7 but not by 21?
(a) 34 (b) 40 (c) 38 (d) 42
Answer: (c) 38
Solution: First count multiples of 7 strictly between 100 and 500: smallest is 105 = 7 × 15, largest is 497 = 7 × 71. Total count of multiples of 7 = 71 – 15 + 1 = 57. Next count multiples of 21 (which are divisible by both 7 and 3): smallest is 105 = 21 × 5, largest is 483 = 21 × 23. Total count of multiples of 21 = 23 – 5 + 1 = 19. Subtracting: 57 – 19 = 38. Hence, there are exactly 38 such integers. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q125. The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. If the number is divided by the positive difference of its digits, the quotient is 12 with no remainder. What is the two-digit number?
(a) 54 (b) 36 (c) 45 (d) 63
Answer: (b) 36
Solution: Let the two-digit number be 10a + b with a + b = 9 (so b = 9 – a). The difference of the digits is |a – b| = |2a – 9|. Given (10a + b) / |2a – 9| = 12 ⇒ 9a + 9 = 12|2a – 9|. If 2a – 9 < 0 (i.e. a ≤ 4): 9a + 9 = 12(9 – 2a) = 108 – 24a ⇒ 33a = 99 ⇒ a = 3. Then b = 9 – 3 = 6. Checking: 36 / |3 – 6| = 36 / 3 = 12. Thus, the required two-digit number is 36. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D
