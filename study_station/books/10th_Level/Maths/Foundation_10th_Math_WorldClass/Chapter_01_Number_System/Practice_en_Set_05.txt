Q101. Which of the following numbers is divisible by 8?
(a) 3124 (b) 5216 (c) 7138 (d) 9230
Answer: (b) 5216
Solution: Divisibility by 8: last three digits must be divisible by 8. 124÷8=15.5, 216÷8=27, 138÷8=17.25, 230÷8=28.75. So only 5216 works.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q102. The LCM of 18, 24, and 32 is
(a) 288 (b) 144 (c) 576 (d) 432
Answer: (a) 288
Solution: 18=2×3², 24=2³×3, 32=2⁵. LCM = 2⁵×3² = 32×9 = 288.
Source: NCERT Class 6

Q124. (Hard) How many numbers between 100 and 500 are divisible by 7 but not by 21?
(a) 34 (b) 38 (c) 40 (d) 42
Answer: (b) 38
Solution: Count multiples of 7 between 100 and 500: smallest 105 (7×15), largest 497 (7×71). Count = 71-15+1 = 57. Multiples of 21 (LCM 21): from 105 (21×5) to 483 (21×23). Count = 23-5+1 = 19. So not by 21 = 57 - 19 = 38.
Source: Bank PO

Q125. (Hard) The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 9. If the number is divided by the difference of its digits, the quotient is 12. Find the number.
(a) 54 (b) 45 (c) 36 (d) 63
Answer: (c) 36
Solution: Let number = 10a+b, a+b=9. Difference = |a-b|. Quotient = 12. So (10a+b) / |a-b| = 12. Try a+b=9, number 10a+(9-a)=9a+9. Difference |a-(9-a)| = |2a-9|. Equation: (9a+9) / |2a-9| = 12 → 9a+9 = 12 |2a-9|. Test a from 1 to 8. a=3: 9(3)+9=36, |2*3-9|=3, 36/3=12 works. So number 36 (a=3,b=6).
Source: SSC CGL
