Questions 26–45 (Easy), Questions 46–50 (Medium)

26. A car travels at a constant speed of 60 km/h. What distance does it cover in 3 hours?
(a) 120 km (b) 150 km (c) 180 km (d) 200 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: Distance = Speed × Time = 60 × 3 = 180 km. The trap is multiplying by the wrong time or dividing instead; so the answer is 180 km.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

27. A cyclist covers 144 km in 8 hours. What is his speed in km/h?
(a) 16 km/h (b) 18 km/h (c) 20 km/h (d) 24 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 144 ÷ 8 = 18 km/h. Multiplying 144 by 8 or dividing 8 by 144 gives absurd values; so the answer is 18 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

28. Convert 72 km/h into m/s.
(a) 15 m/s (b) 18 m/s (c) 24 m/s (d) 20 m/s
Answer: (d)
Solution: Multiply by 5/18: 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s. The trap is using 18/5, which gives 259.2 m/s; so the answer is 20 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Units)

29. Convert 10 m/s into km/h.
(a) 36 km/h (b) 30 km/h (c) 42 km/h (d) 50 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Multiply by 18/5: 10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h. Using 5/18 instead gives about 2.8 km/h, which is the reverse trap; so the answer is 36 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Units)

30. A boy walks at 5 km/h for 2 hours and then at 4 km/h for 3 hours. Find the total distance covered.
(a) 22 km (b) 20 km (c) 25 km (d) 28 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: First part: 5 × 2 = 10 km; second part: 4 × 3 = 12 km; total = 22 km. Adding the speeds and multiplying by total time gives 45 km, which is wrong; so the answer is 22 km.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

31. A train 150 m long is running at 54 km/h. How much time will it take to cross a pole?
(a) 10 sec (b) 8 sec (c) 5 sec (d) 12 sec
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s; time = 150 ÷ 15 = 10 s. Forgetting to convert km/h to m/s gives 150 ÷ 54, a common trap; so the answer is 10 sec.
Source: PYQ-style

32. A man runs at 9 km/h. How far does he run in 20 seconds?
(a) 50 m (b) 40 m (c) 60 m (d) 80 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = 9 × 5/18 = 2.5 m/s; distance = 2.5 × 20 = 50 m. Using 9 m/s directly gives 180 m, the unit trap; so the answer is 50 m.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

33. A car covers a certain distance in 5 hours at 40 km/h. How much time will it take to cover the same distance at 50 km/h?
(a) 4 hours (b) 3.5 hours (c) 4.5 hours (d) 5 hours
Answer: (a)
Solution: Distance = 40 × 5 = 200 km; time at 50 km/h = 200 ÷ 50 = 4 hours. Assuming time stays the same when speed changes is the trap; so the answer is 4 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

34. Two trains moving in opposite directions have speeds of 60 km/h and 40 km/h. Their relative speed is:
(a) 20 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 80 km/h (d) 100 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: For opposite directions, relative speed = 60 + 40 = 100 km/h. Subtracting gives 20 km/h, which applies only to the same direction; so the answer is 100 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

35. A train crosses a man standing on the platform in 12 seconds. If the speed of the train is 60 km/h, what is its length?
(a) 150 m (b) 200 m (c) 180 m (d) 220 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: Speed = 60 × 5/18 = 50/3 m/s; length = (50/3) × 12 = 200 m. Multiplying 60 by 12 directly gives 720, the unit trap; so the answer is 200 m.
Source: PYQ-style

36. A person walks 360 metres in 4 minutes. His speed in km/h is:
(a) 5.4 km/h (b) 6.0 km/h (c) 6.5 km/h (d) 7.2 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = 360 ÷ 240 = 1.5 m/s; in km/h = 1.5 × 18/5 = 5.4 km/h. Forgetting that 4 minutes is 240 seconds is the trap; so the answer is 5.4 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

37. A bus travels at 45 km/h for 2 hours and then at 60 km/h for 1 hour. What is its average speed for the entire journey?
(a) 48 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 52 km/h (d) 54 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Total distance = 90 + 60 = 150 km; total time = 3 h; average = 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h. Taking the simple mean of 45 and 60 gives 52.5 km/h, the classic trap; so the answer is 50 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

38. A car moving at 54 km/h crosses a platform in 20 seconds. If the car's length is 5 m, what is the length of the platform?
(a) 245 m (b) 300 m (c) 295 m (d) 305 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s; total distance = 15 × 20 = 300 m; platform = 300 − 5 = 295 m. Forgetting to subtract the car's length gives 300 m, the tempting trap; so the answer is 295 m.
Source: PYQ-style

39. A train 300 m long is running at a speed of 90 km/h. How much time will it take to cross a bridge 200 m long?
(a) 16 sec (b) 18 sec (c) 20 sec (d) 24 sec
Answer: (c)
Solution: Speed = 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s; total distance = 300 + 200 = 500 m; time = 500 ÷ 25 = 20 s. Using only the bridge length gives 8 s, the trap; so the answer is 20 sec.
Source: PYQ-style

40. A runner completes a 400-metre race in 50 seconds. What is his speed in km/h?
(a) 28.0 km/h (b) 28.8 km/h (c) 30.2 km/h (d) 32.4 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Speed = 400 ÷ 50 = 8 m/s; in km/h = 8 × 18/5 = 28.8 km/h. Leaving the answer in m/s or using 5/18 are the traps; so the answer is 28.8 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

41. Two trains of lengths 200 m and 300 m are running in opposite directions at 50 km/h and 40 km/h. How long will they take to cross each other?
(a) 15 sec (b) 20 sec (c) 18 sec (d) 25 sec
Answer: (b)
Solution: Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s; total distance = 200 + 300 = 500 m; time = 500 ÷ 25 = 20 s. Using only one train's length gives 8 or 12 s, the trap; so the answer is 20 sec.
Source: PYQ-style

42. A car goes from town A to town B at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 45 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 48 km/h (d) 52 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: For equal distances, average = (2 × 60 × 40) ÷ (60 + 40) = 48 km/h. The simple mean 50 km/h is the classic trap; so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

43. A bus covers a distance of 240 km in 4 hours. What is its speed in m/s? (approximately)
(a) 12.5 m/s (b) 16.67 m/s (c) 15.2 m/s (d) 18.0 m/s
Answer: (b)
Solution: Speed = 240 ÷ 4 = 60 km/h = 60 × 5/18 ≈ 16.67 m/s. Stopping at 60 km/h or dividing by 5/18 are the traps; so the answer is 16.67 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Units)

44. A man walks at 6 km/h and covers a certain distance in 40 minutes. How much time would he take if he walks at 8 km/h?
(a) 30 min (b) 25 min (c) 20 min (d) 35 min
Answer: (a)
Solution: Distance = 6 × 40/60 = 4 km; time at 8 km/h = 4 ÷ 8 = 0.5 h = 30 min. Using 40 minutes directly as hours is the trap; so the answer is 30 min.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

45. A train 250 m long passes a platform 350 m long in 30 seconds. The speed of the train (in km/h) is:
(a) 60 km/h (b) 80 km/h (c) 72 km/h (d) 90 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total distance = 250 + 350 = 600 m; speed = 600 ÷ 30 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h. Using only the platform length gives 42 km/h, the trap; so the answer is 72 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

46. A train crosses a pole in 10 seconds and a 180 m long platform in 22 seconds. Find the length of the train.
(a) 120 m (b) 200 m (c) 180 m (d) 150 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let length be L and speed v. Then L = 10v and L + 180 = 22v, so 180 = 12v, giving v = 15 m/s and L = 150 m. Assuming the platform time covers only the platform is the trap; so the answer is 150 m.
Source: PYQ-style

47. A car travels the first half of a journey at 40 km/h and the second half at 60 km/h. The average speed for the whole journey is:
(a) 45 km/h (b) 52 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 48 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: For equal distances, average = (2 × 40 × 60) ÷ (40 + 60) = 48 km/h. The arithmetic mean 50 km/h is the tempting trap; so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Two trains start from stations A and B, 600 km apart, at the same time and travel towards each other. Their speeds are 50 km/h and 70 km/h. After how many hours will they meet?
(a) 4 hours (b) 4.5 hours (c) 5.5 hours (d) 5 hours
Answer: (d)
Solution: Relative speed = 50 + 70 = 120 km/h; meeting time = 600 ÷ 120 = 5 hours. Dividing 600 by one speed alone is the trap; so the answer is 5 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

49. A train 100 m long running at 36 km/h takes 25 seconds to cross a bridge. Find the length of the bridge.
(a) 125 m (b) 175 m (c) 150 m (d) 200 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: Speed = 36 × 5/18 = 10 m/s; total distance = 10 × 25 = 250 m; bridge = 250 − 100 = 150 m. Reporting 250 m as the bridge length is the trap; so the answer is 150 m.
Source: PYQ-style

50. A man covers a distance of 40 km. He covers the first 20 km at 8 km/h, the next 15 km at 6 km/h, and the last 5 km at 4 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 7.5 km/h (b) 6.8 km/h (c) 7.2 km/h (d) 6.4 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: Times are 20/8 = 2.5 h, 15/6 = 2.5 h, and 5/4 = 1.25 h, totalling 6.25 h; average = 40 ÷ 6.25 = 6.4 km/h. Averaging the three speeds gives 6 km/h, the trap; so the answer is 6.4 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style
