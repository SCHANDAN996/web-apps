1. A 360 m long train crosses a standing man in 24 seconds and a platform in 55 seconds. What is the length of the platform?
(a) 410 m (b) 445 m (c) 480 m (d) 465 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting shortcut is to subtract times (55 − 24 = 31 s) and multiply by a guessed speed, but you must first get the speed from the man: 360/24 = 15 m/s. Crossing the platform needs 15 × 55 = 825 m, so the platform is 825 − 360 = 465 m, so the answer is 465 m.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Car A leaves Delhi for Shimla at 8:30 a.m. at 40 km/h and car B leaves at 9:00 a.m. at 50 km/h. At what distance from Delhi does B catch A?
(a) 200 km (b) 45 km (c) 80 km (d) 100 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is writing one equation from 9:00 a.m. without adjusting for A's head start. In 30 minutes A gains 20 km; relative speed is 50 − 40 = 10 km/h, so B needs 2 hours and covers 50 × 2 = 100 km, so the answer is 100 km.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Two cars cover the same distance at 60 km/h and 108 km/h. The faster car takes 2 hours less. Find the distance.
(a) 270 km (b) 240 km (c) 300 km (d) 330 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students fumble the fraction subtraction D/60 − D/108 = 2 under pressure. Using D = (ΔT × S1 × S2)/(S2 − S1) = (2 × 60 × 108)/48 = 270 km, so the answer is 270 km.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Amit covers 50 km in 9 hours, partly on foot at 5 km/h and partly by bicycle at 10 km/h. What distance did he cover by bicycle?
(a) 11 km (b) 13 km (c) 12 km (d) 10 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming equal time on foot and bicycle. Average speed is 50/9 km/h; allegation on speeds gives time ratio (cycle : walk) = 1 : 8, so cycle time is 1 hour and cycle distance is 10 × 1 = 10 km, so the answer is 10 km.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Two cities are 465 km apart. Car P leaves the first city at 10 a.m. at 65 km/h; car Q leaves the second city at 11 a.m. at 35 km/h, moving towards P. When do they meet?
(a) 3 p.m. (b) 2 p.m. (c) 4 p.m. (d) 1 p.m.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is ignoring that P travels alone for the first hour. By 11 a.m. P has covered 65 km, leaving 400 km; relative speed is 65 + 35 = 100 km/h, so they meet 4 hours after 11 a.m., so the answer is 3 p.m.
Source: PYQ-style

6. Two cyclists X and Y start together from A to B at 6 km/h and 8 km/h. Y halts for 15 minutes on the way, yet reaches 10 minutes before X. Find the distance AB.
(a) 6 km (b) 16.5 km (c) 8 km (d) 10 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is writing D/6 − D/8 = 25 minutes and forgetting the halt must be added to Y's travel time. Speed ratio 3 : 4 gives time ratio 4 : 3; with the 15-minute halt, 4t − (3t + 15) = 10 gives t = 25 minutes, so X takes 100 minutes and the distance is 6 × 100/60 = 10 km, so the answer is 10 km.
Source: PYQ-style

7. A train 108 m long moving at 128 km/h crosses another train 148 m long standing on a parallel track. How long does it take?
(a) 8 s (b) 7.5 s (c) 7.2 s (d) 9 s
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is hunting for a second speed because "two trains" suggests the relative-speed formula; a stationary train is just a long pole. Distance is 108 + 148 = 256 m and speed is 128 × 5/18 = 320/9 m/s, giving 256 × 9/320 = 7.2 s, so the answer is 7.2 s.
Source: PYQ-style

8. A train crosses a man standing on a platform in 25 seconds and the whole platform in 42 seconds. The train's speed is 72 km/h. Find the platform's length.
(a) 300 m (b) 340 m (c) 270 m (d) 370 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using 42 seconds with the train's length alone. Speed is 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s; the extra 42 − 25 = 17 seconds is exactly the time to cover the platform, so its length is 20 × 17 = 340 m, so the answer is 340 m.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Train A at 63 km/h crosses a 199.5 m long platform in 21 seconds. Train B is 257 m long and moves at 54 km/h in the opposite direction. How long do the two trains take to cross each other completely?
(a) 13.08 s (b) 18 s (c) 12 s (d) 16 s
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is rounding early or forgetting to first extract train A's length from the platform data. Train A's speed is 17.5 m/s, so its length is 17.5 × 21 − 199.5 = 168 m; combined length is 425 m and relative speed is 17.5 + 15 = 32.5 m/s, giving 425/32.5 ≈ 13.08 s, so the answer is 13.08 s.
Source: PYQ-style

10. Convert 90 km/h into metres per second.
(a) 20 m/s (b) 25 m/s (c) 30 m/s (d) 15 m/s
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is multiplying by 18/5 instead of 5/18, which inflates the value. Since 1 km/h = 5/18 m/s, 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s, so the answer is 25 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

11. A man covers a certain distance at 40 km/h and returns the same distance at 60 km/h. His average speed for the whole journey is:
(a) 48 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 52 km/h (d) 45 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking the simple mean (40 + 60)/2 = 50 km/h, which is wrong because the times differ. For equal distances, average speed = 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 48 km/h, so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

12. Walking at 5 km/h a boy reaches school 7 minutes late; walking at 6 km/h he reaches 5 minutes early. The distance to school is:
(a) 5 km (b) 7 km (c) 6 km (d) 8 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using only one of the two time gaps instead of their total difference of 12 minutes. D/5 − D/6 = 12/60 gives D/30 = 1/5, so D = 6 km, so the answer is 6 km.
Source: PYQ-style

13. Two trains 120 m and 180 m long run in the same direction at 60 km/h and 40 km/h. How long does the faster train take to completely pass the slower one?
(a) 54 s (b) 45 s (c) 60 s (d) 36 s
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding the speeds, which applies only to opposite directions. Relative speed is 20 km/h = 50/9 m/s and the distance is 120 + 180 = 300 m, so time = 300 × 9/50 = 54 s, so the answer is 54 s.
Source: PYQ-style

14. Two men cover the same distance at speeds in the ratio 3 : 4. The slower man takes 30 minutes more than the faster one. The distance is:
(a) 5 km (b) 8 km (c) 6 km (d) 4 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is treating the time ratio as 3 : 4 instead of inverting it to 4 : 3. D/3 − D/4 = 1/2 hour gives D/12 = 1/2, so D = 6 km, so the answer is 6 km.
Source: PYQ-style

15. A 150 m long train running at 54 km/h crosses an electric pole in:
(a) 10 s (b) 12 s (c) 8 s (d) 15 s
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is forgetting the unit conversion and computing 150/54 directly. Speed is 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s, so time = 150/15 = 10 s, so the answer is 10 s.

16. A covers a distance in 4 hours while B covers the same distance in 5 hours. A's speed is 5 km/h more than B's. The distance is:
(a) 80 km (b) 100 km (c) 90 km (d) 120 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is setting up D/5 − D/4 = 5 with the difference reversed. D/4 − D/5 = 5 gives D/20 = 5, so D = 100 km, so the answer is 100 km.

17. Walking at 3/4 of his usual speed, a man reaches his office 20 minutes late. His usual time to reach the office is:
(a) 80 minutes (b) 45 minutes (c) 60 minutes (d) 90 minutes
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is taking 3/4 of 20 minutes as the answer. At 3/4 speed the time becomes 4/3 of usual, so the extra 1/3 of usual time equals 20 minutes, giving usual time = 60 minutes, so the answer is 60 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

18. Two trains 120 m and 180 m long run towards each other at 72 km/h and 90 km/h. The time they take to cross each other is:
(a) 6 s (b) 6.67 s (c) 8 s (d) 7.5 s
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is subtracting the speeds; for opposite directions they add. Relative speed is 162 km/h = 45 m/s and total length is 300 m, so time = 300/45 ≈ 6.67 s, so the answer is 6.67 s.
Source: PYQ-style

19. A car travels 240 km. If its speed were 10 km/h more, it would take 2 hours less. The original speed of the car is:
(a) 20 km/h (b) 40 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 30 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is testing 40 km/h first because it looks central; at 40 km/h the times are 6 h and 4.8 h, a gap of only 1.2 h. At 30 km/h the times are 8 h and 6 h, exactly 2 hours apart, so the answer is 30 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

20. A man goes from home to town at 30 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. His average speed for the round trip is:
(a) 45 km/h (b) 40 km/h (c) 42 km/h (d) 48 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the arithmetic mean 45 km/h, which ignores that more time is spent at the lower speed. Average speed = 2 × 30 × 60/90 = 40 km/h, so the answer is 40 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

21. A train crosses a 200 m long platform in 30 seconds and a pole in 10 seconds. The length of the train is:
(a) 150 m (b) 120 m (c) 100 m (d) 200 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming the platform time alone gives the train's length. From the pole, L = 10v; from the platform, L + 200 = 30v, so 20v = 200, v = 10 m/s and L = 100 m, so the answer is 100 m.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Two men start from the same point at the same time and walk in opposite directions at 4 km/h and 6 km/h. How far apart are they after 3 hours?
(a) 30 km (b) 18 km (c) 12 km (d) 24 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is subtracting the speeds, which would give the gap only if they moved in the same direction. In opposite directions the gap grows at 4 + 6 = 10 km/h, so after 3 hours they are 30 km apart, so the answer is 30 km.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

23. A thief runs at 8 km/h and a policeman chases him at 10 km/h. If the thief is 200 m ahead, the policeman will catch him in:
(a) 4 minutes (b) 10 minutes (c) 6 minutes (d) 8 minutes
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is dividing 200 m by 10 km/h, ignoring that the thief also moves. Relative speed is 10 − 8 = 2 km/h, so time = 0.2/2 = 0.1 hour = 6 minutes, so the answer is 6 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

24. A journey of 300 km is covered partly at 50 km/h and partly at 60 km/h, taking a total of 5.5 hours. The distance covered at 50 km/h is:
(a) 120 km (b) 150 km (c) 180 km (d) 100 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is splitting time equally, which would not satisfy the total distance. If t hours are at 50 km/h, then 50t + 60(5.5 − t) = 300 gives t = 3 hours, so the distance at 50 km/h is 150 km, so the answer is 150 km.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A 250 m long train running at 90 km/h crosses a bridge 750 m long in:
(a) 36 s (b) 50 s (c) 45 s (d) 40 s
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using only the bridge length (750 m) and forgetting the train must also clear its own length. Total distance is 250 + 750 = 1000 m and speed is 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s, so time = 1000/25 = 40 s, so the answer is 40 s.
Source: PYQ-style
