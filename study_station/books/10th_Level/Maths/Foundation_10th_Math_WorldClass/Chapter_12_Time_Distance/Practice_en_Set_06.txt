126. A train takes 25 seconds to cross a platform of length 300 m, and 15 seconds to cross a pole. The length of the train (in metres) is:
A) 250 m
B) 300 m
C) 400 m
D) 450 m
Correct Answer: D) 450 m
Solution:
Let length = L m, speed = v m/s.
For pole: L = 15v.
For platform: L + 300 = 25v.
Substituting L: 15v + 300 = 25v ⇒ 10v = 300 ⇒ v = 30 m/s, L = 15 × 30 = 450 m.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2

127. Two trains of lengths 200 m and 300 m are running on parallel tracks in the same direction at 72 km/h and 54 km/h respectively. The time taken by the faster train to completely overtake the slower one is:
A) 50 sec
B) 60 sec
C) 75 sec
D) 100 sec
Correct Answer: D) 100 sec
Solution:
Relative speed = (72 – 54) km/h = 18 km/h = 18 × (5/18) = 5 m/s.
Total distance = 200 + 300 = 500 m.
Time = 500 / 5 = 100 seconds.
Source: SSC CHSL

131. A train after travelling 100 km meets with an accident and then proceeds at 3/4 of its original speed, arriving at its destination 35 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 30 km further, it would have reached only 25 minutes late. The original speed of the train is:
A) 40 km/h

132. A train covers a certain distance at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 10 km/h more, it would have taken 2 hours less. If the speed had been 5 km/h less, it would have taken 2 hours more. The distance (in km) is:
A) 100 km
B) 120 km
C) 140 km
D) 150 km
Correct Answer: B) 120 km
Solution:
Let original speed = v km/h, distance = d km.
d/v – d/(v+10) = 2 ⇒ d(v+10 – v)/(v(v+10)) = 2 ⇒ 10d/(v(v+10)) = 2 ⇒ d = v(v+10)/5.
d/(v-5) – d/v = 2 ⇒ 5d/(v(v-5)) = 2 ⇒ d = 2v(v-5)/5.
Equating: v(v+10) = 2v(v-5) ⇒ v+10 = 2v – 10 ⇒ v = 20 km/h.
Distance d = 20×30/5 = 120 km.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2

137. A train after traveling 75 km meets with an accident and then proceeds at 3/4 of its speed, arriving 50 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 30 km farther, it would have been 40 minutes late. The original speed (in km/h) is:

139. A jeep covers a distance of 300 km in 5 hours. It covers the first part at 50 km/h and the second part at 75 km/h. The distance covered at 50 km/h (in km) is:

Correct Answer: C) 150 km
Solution:

143. A train leaves station A at 9:00 am and reaches station B at 5:00 pm the same day, covering 480 km. Another train leaves station B at 10:30 am and reaches station A at 6:30 pm. At what time do they meet?
