126. A train takes 25 seconds to cross a platform of length 300 m, and 15 seconds to cross a pole. The length of the train (in metres) is:
(a) 250 m (b) 300 m (c) 450 m (d) 400 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students divide 300 by 10 seconds and stop at the speed, forgetting to find the length. From the pole, L = 15v; from the platform, L + 300 = 25v, so 10v = 300, v = 30 m/s and L = 15 × 30, so the answer is 450 m.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Two trains of lengths 200 m and 300 m run on parallel tracks in the same direction at 72 km/h and 54 km/h. The time taken by the faster train to completely overtake the slower one is:
(a) 100 sec (b) 60 sec (c) 75 sec (d) 50 sec
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is using only one train's length; for a complete overtake the faster train must cover the sum of both lengths. Relative speed = 18 km/h = 5 m/s, so time = 500/5, so the answer is 100 sec.
Source: PYQ-style

128. A motor boat can travel 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours. It can travel 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream in 13 hours. The speed of the boat in still water (in km/h) is:
(a) 14 km/h (b) 10 km/h (c) 12 km/h (d) 8 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting error is averaging the two journey times instead of solving for the two speeds. With upstream x and downstream y: 30/x + 44/y = 10 and 40/x + 55/y = 13; eliminating x gives y = 11, then x = 5, and still-water speed = (11 + 5)/2, so the answer is 8 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

129. A man covers three equal distances of 30 km each at 30 km/h, 40 km/h and 60 km/h respectively. His average speed for the entire journey (in km/h) is:
(a) 48 km/h (b) 42 km/h (c) 45 km/h (d) 40 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is taking the arithmetic mean (30 + 40 + 60)/3 = 43.3, which is wrong for equal distances. Total time = 1 + 0.75 + 0.5 = 2.25 h for 90 km, giving 90/2.25, so the answer is 40 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

130. A car covers 1/3 of a distance at 60 km/h, 1/2 of the distance at 40 km/h, and the remaining distance at 50 km/h. If the total time taken is 7 hours 42 minutes, the total distance (in km) is:
(a) 300 km (b) 320 km (c) 400 km (d) 360 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: Students often forget the remaining fraction is 1/6, not 1/3. Time = D/180 + D/80 + D/300 = 77D/3600 hours = 7.7 hours, giving D = 7.7 × 3600/77, so the answer is 360 km.
Source: PYQ-style

131. A train after travelling 100 km meets with an accident and then proceeds at 3/4 of its original speed, arriving 35 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 30 km further, it would have reached only 25 minutes late. The original speed of the train (in km/h) is:
(a) 40 km/h (b) 45 km/h (c) 48 km/h (d) 60 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using the full 35-minute delay; only the 10-minute difference matters, caused by 30 km being run at full speed instead of 3/4 speed. Time saved = 40/v − 30/v = 10/v = 1/6 hour, so the answer is 60 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

132. A train covers a certain distance at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 10 km/h more, it would have taken 2 hours less; if 5 km/h less, 2 hours more. The distance (in km) is:
(a) 100 km (b) 120 km (c) 140 km (d) 150 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: A common slip is setting up only one equation; both conditions are needed. They give d = v(v+10)/5 and d = 2v(v−5)/5, so v + 10 = 2v − 10, v = 20 km/h and d = 20 × 30/5, so the answer is 120 km.
Source: PYQ-style

133. A boat takes 1 hour longer to travel 36 km upstream than to travel the same distance downstream. If the speed of the stream is 3 km/h, the speed of the boat in still water (in km/h) is:
(a) 12 km/h (b) 15 km/h (c) 18 km/h (d) 21 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is writing the time difference with the wrong sign or forgetting the stream affects both directions. 36/(x−3) − 36/(x+3) = 1 gives 216 = x² − 9, so x² = 225, so the answer is 15 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

134. Two places P and Q are 320 km apart. A train leaves P at 6 am at 80 km/h. Another train leaves Q at 7 am at 40 km/h. At what time do they meet?
(a) 9:00 am (b) 8:30 am (c) 9:30 am (d) 10:00 am
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is using the full 320 km with relative speed, ignoring the head start. By 7 am the first train has covered 80 km, leaving 240 km at relative speed 120 km/h, i.e. 2 hours after 7 am, so the answer is 9:00 am.
Source: PYQ-style

135. A man rows to a place 40 km away and back in 14 hours. He can row 4 km upstream in the same time as 10 km downstream. The speed of the stream (in km/h) is:
(a) 3 km/h (b) 2 km/h (c) 1 km/h (d) 4 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the ratio 4:10 is the speed ratio of boat to stream; it is the ratio of upstream to downstream speeds. From 4/(x−y) = 10/(x+y), x = 7y/3; then 40/(4y/3) + 40/(10y/3) = 42/y = 14, so the answer is 3 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

136. A person rides a bicycle at 15 km/h and reaches his destination 1 hour late; at 20 km/h he reaches 1 hour early. The distance (in km) is:
(a) 120 km (b) 100 km (c) 80 km (d) 150 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking the time gap as 1 hour instead of 2 hours (late plus early). d/15 − d/20 = 2 gives d/60 = 2, so the answer is 120 km.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

137. A train after travelling 75 km meets with an accident and then proceeds at 3/4 of its speed, arriving 50 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 30 km farther, it would have been 40 minutes late. The original speed (in km/h) is:
(a) 40 km/h (b) 45 km/h (c) 60 km/h (d) 50 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is equating the whole 50-minute delay to the speed equation; only the 10-minute difference over 30 km counts. Time saved = 40/v − 30/v = 10/v = 1/6 hour, so the answer is 60 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

138. A train passes a man standing on a platform in 8 seconds and passes a 264 m long platform in 20 seconds. The length of the train (in m) is:
(a) 200 m (b) 180 m (c) 176 m (d) 220 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 264/20 as the speed; the platform time covers train plus platform. L = 8v and L + 264 = 20v give v = 22 m/s and L = 8 × 22, so the answer is 176 m.
Source: PYQ-style

139. A jeep covers 300 km in 5 hours, travelling the first part at 50 km/h and the second part at 75 km/h. The distance covered at 50 km/h (in km) is:
(a) 100 km (b) 120 km (c) 150 km (d) 180 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is splitting time equally (2.5 h each), which gives 312.5 km, not 300 km. From 50t + 75(5 − t) = 300, t = 3 hours, so the distance at 50 km/h is 50 × 3, so the answer is 150 km.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

140. A motorboat goes 24 km upstream and 28 km downstream in 6 hours. It can go 30 km upstream and 21 km downstream in 6.5 hours. The speed of the boat in still water (in km/h) is:
(a) 10 km/h (b) 8 km/h (c) 12 km/h (d) 14 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is reporting the downstream speed 14 km/h as the boat's speed. Eliminating the upstream term gives downstream v = 14 km/h, then upstream u = 6 km/h, and still-water speed = (14 + 6)/2, so the answer is 10 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

141. A car travels at 45 km/h for 2 hours, then at 60 km/h for 3 hours, and finally at 75 km/h for 1 hour. The average speed for the whole journey (in km/h) is:
(a) 57.5 km/h (b) 55 km/h (c) 60 km/h (d) 62.5 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is averaging the three speeds directly, which gives 60 km/h. Total distance = 90 + 180 + 75 = 345 km in 6 hours, giving 345/6, so the answer is 57.5 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

142. Two trains of equal length 120 m take 10 seconds and 15 seconds respectively to cross a telegraph post. In how much time (in seconds) will they cross each other travelling in opposite directions?
(a) 8 sec (b) 10 sec (c) 15 sec (d) 12 sec
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is averaging 10 and 15 seconds. Speeds are 12 m/s and 8 m/s, relative speed 20 m/s over a combined length of 240 m, giving 240/20, so the answer is 12 sec.

143. A train leaves station A at 9:00 am and reaches station B at 5:00 pm, covering 480 km. Another train leaves B at 10:30 am and reaches A at 6:30 pm. At what time do they meet?
(a) 1:30 pm (b) 2:15 pm (c) 2:00 pm (d) 1:45 pm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is ignoring the 90 km the first train covers before 10:30 am. Both speeds are 60 km/h; remaining 390 km at relative speed 120 km/h takes 3 h 15 min after 10:30 am, so the answer is 1:45 pm.

144. A man rows at 8 km/h in still water and the river flows at 2 km/h. He rows to a place and back in 4 hours. How far is the place (in km)?
(a) 12 km (b) 15 km (c) 16 km (d) 18 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using the still-water speed for both legs, giving 16 km. With downstream 10 km/h and upstream 6 km/h, d/10 + d/6 = 4 gives 8d/30 = 4, so the answer is 15 km.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

145. The speeds of A and B are in the ratio 3:4 and B's speed is 40 km/h. A starts 40 minutes earlier than B, and B overtakes A exactly 2 hours after B starts. The speed of A (in km/h) is:
(a) 40 km/h (b) 36 km/h (c) 30 km/h (d) 45 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is taking A's speed as 40 km/h by confusing the ratio order. Since 4 parts = 40 km/h, one part = 10, so A = 30 km/h; check: A travels 8/3 h covering 80 km, equal to B's 80 km in 2 h, so the answer is 30 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

146. A train overtakes a cyclist travelling at 18 km/h in the same direction in 12 seconds, and another cyclist at 27 km/h in the same direction in 15 seconds. The length of the train (in m) is:
(a) 180 m (b) 150 m (c) 200 m (d) 225 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming the train's speed is given; it must be found first. With v in m/s, (v − 5) × 12 = (v − 7.5) × 15 gives v = 17.5 m/s, so L = 12.5 × 12, so the answer is 150 m.
Source: PYQ-style

147. A boat goes 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours; in 13 hours it goes 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream. The speed of the current (in km/h) is:
(a) 4 km/h (b) 3 km/h (c) 5 km/h (d) 6 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is reporting the boat's speed (8 km/h) instead of the current. Solving gives upstream 5 km/h and downstream 11 km/h, so current = (11 − 5)/2, so the answer is 3 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

148. A person walks a certain distance and rides back, taking a total of 5 hours. He could walk both ways in 7 hours. How long would it take him to ride both ways?
(a) 2 hours (b) 3 hours (c) 4 hours (d) 5 hours
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is halving 5 hours directly. Walking one way takes 7/2 = 3.5 h, so riding one way takes 5 − 3.5 = 1.5 h, and riding both ways takes 2 × 1.5, so the answer is 3 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

149. A train running at 72 km/h crosses a platform in 30 seconds and a man standing on the platform in 18 seconds. The length of the platform (in m) is:
(a) 240 m (b) 200 m (c) 180 m (d) 300 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking 20 × 30 = 600 m as the platform length; that includes the train. Train length = 20 × 18 = 360 m, so platform = 600 − 360, so the answer is 240 m.
Source: PYQ-style

150. A man covers one-third of a journey at 30 km/h, one-third at 40 km/h, and the remaining one-third at 50 km/h. If the total time is 4 hours 42 minutes, the total distance (in km) is:
(a) 120 km (b) 240 km (c) 180 km (d) 300 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is converting 4 h 42 min as 4.42 h instead of 4.7 h. With each part d km, time = d(1/30 + 1/40 + 1/50) = 47d/600 = 47/10, so d = 60 km and total = 3d, so the answer is 180 km.
Source: PYQ-style
