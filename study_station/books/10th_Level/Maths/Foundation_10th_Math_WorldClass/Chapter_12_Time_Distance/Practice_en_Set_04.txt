76. A car covers the first 40 km of its journey at 60 km/h and the next 40 km at 40 km/h. What is the average speed of the entire journey?
A) 45 km/h
B) 48 km/h
C) 50 km/h
D) 52 km/h
Correct Answer: B) 48 km/h
Solution:
Time for first part = 40/60 = 2/3 h.
Time for second part = 40/40 = 1 h.
Total distance = 80 km, total time = 2/3 + 1 = 5/3 h.
Average speed = 80 / (5/3) = 80 × 3/5 = 48 km/h.
Source: NCERT Exemplar (Class 7)

81. Two trains of lengths 150 m and 200 m are running on parallel tracks at speeds of 60 km/h and 40 km/h respectively in the same direction. How long will the faster train take to completely overtake the slower train?
A) 35 sec
B) 42 sec
C) 50 sec
D) 63 sec
Correct Answer: D) 63 sec
Solution:
Relative speed = (60 - 40) km/h = 20 km/h = 20 × (5/18) = 100/18 = 50/9 m/s.
Total distance to cover = sum of lengths = 150 + 200 = 350 m.
Time = 350 / (50/9) = 350 × 9/50 = 63 seconds.

82. A car travels from A to B at 45 km/h and returns from B to A at 55 km/h. Find the average speed for the round trip.
A) 49.5 km/h
B) 50 km/h
C) 48.9 km/h
D) 49.09 km/h
Correct Answer: A) 49.5 km/h
Solution:
Let distance one way = d km.
Time AB = d/45, time BA = d/55.
Average speed = 2d / (d/45 + d/55) = 2 / (1/45 + 1/55) = 2 / ((55+45)/(45×55)) = 2 × (45×55)/100 = (2×2475)/100 = 4950/100 = 49.5 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7

84. A man covers a certain distance on scooter. Had he moved 3 km/h faster, he would have taken 40 minutes less. If he had moved 2 km/h slower, he would have taken 40 minutes more. Find the distance.
A) 20 km
B) 30 km
C) 40 km
D) 50 km
Correct Answer: C) 40 km
Solution:
Let original speed = v km/h, time = t hours, distance = d = v t.
Condition 1: (v+3)(t - 40/60) = d => (v+3)(t - 2/3) = vt
Condition 2: (v-2)(t + 40/60) = d => (v-2)(t + 2/3) = vt
Expand: v t - 2v/3 + 3t - 2 = v t => -2v/3 + 3t = 2 (1)
Second: v t + 2v/3 - 2t - 4/3 = v t => 2v/3 - 2t = 4/3 (2)
Add (1) and (2): ( -2v/3+2v/3) + (3t - 2t) = 2 + 4/3 => t = 10/3 hours.
From (2): 2v/3 - 2(10/3) = 4/3 => 2v/3 - 20/3 = 4/3 => 2v/3 = 24/3 => 2v/3 = 8 => v = 12 km/h.
Distance = 12 × 10/3 = 40 km.
Source: SSC CGL Exam

B) 12 min
C) 15 min
D) 20 min
Correct Answer: B) 12 min
Solution:
