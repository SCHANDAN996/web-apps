76. A car covers the first 40 km of its journey at 60 km/h and the next 40 km at 40 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire journey?
(a) 45 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 48 km/h (d) 52 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is taking the simple mean of 60 and 40, which gives 50 km/h, but average speed over equal distances is the harmonic mean. Total time = 40/60 + 40/40 = 2/3 + 1 = 5/3 h, so average speed = 80 ÷ (5/3) = 48 km/h, so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

77. A train 200 m long passes a pole in 10 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(a) 60 km/h (b) 72 km/h (c) 64 km/h (d) 80 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Speed = 200/10 = 20 m/s. The trap is multiplying by 5/18 instead of 18/5, which would give about 5.5 km/h; converting correctly, 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h, so the answer is 72 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science (Motion and Time)

78. A person walks at 5 km/h for 6 hours and then at 4 km/h for 12 hours. Find his average speed for the whole journey.
(a) 4.67 km/h (b) 4.50 km/h (c) 4.33 km/h (d) 4.25 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is averaging the two speeds to get 4.5 km/h, ignoring that more time is spent at the slower speed. Total distance = 30 + 48 = 78 km and total time = 18 h, so average speed = 78/18 ≈ 4.33 km/h, so the answer is 4.33 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

79. A cyclist covers 12 km in 40 minutes. Find his speed in km/h.
(a) 12 km/h (b) 18 km/h (c) 16 km/h (d) 15 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is treating 40 minutes as 0.4 hour, which gives 30 km/h; actually 40 min = 2/3 h. Speed = 12 ÷ (2/3) = 18 km/h, so the answer is 18 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

80. A boat goes 10 km upstream in 2 hours and the same distance downstream in 1 hour. Find the speed of the stream.
(a) 1.5 km/h (b) 4 km/h (c) 3 km/h (d) 2.5 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: Upstream speed = 5 km/h and downstream speed = 10 km/h. The trap is halving the sum (7.5 km/h), which gives the boat's speed in still water, not the stream's; stream speed = (10 − 5)/2 = 2.5 km/h, so the answer is 2.5 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

81. Two trains of lengths 150 m and 200 m run on parallel tracks at 60 km/h and 40 km/h in the same direction. How long will the faster train take to completely overtake the slower train?
(a) 42 sec (b) 50 sec (c) 70 sec (d) 63 sec
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using only the faster train's length (150 m), but overtaking requires covering both lengths, 350 m. Relative speed = 20 km/h = 50/9 m/s, so time = 350 × 9/50 = 63 seconds, so the answer is 63 sec.
Source: PYQ-style

82. A car travels from A to B at 45 km/h and returns at 55 km/h. Find the average speed for the round trip.
(a) 49.5 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 48.5 km/h (d) 51 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the arithmetic mean of 45 and 55, which is 50 km/h; for equal distances the average is 2xy/(x+y) = 2×45×55/100 = 49.5 km/h, so the answer is 49.5 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

83. If a person travels at 30 km/h, he reaches his destination 10 minutes late; at 40 km/h, he reaches 5 minutes early. Find the distance of the journey.
(a) 20 km (b) 30 km (c) 28 km (d) 25 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The difference between the two times is 15 minutes = 1/4 h, so d/30 − d/40 = 1/4, giving d/120 = 1/4. The trap is using 5 minutes instead of the full 15-minute gap, which gives 10 km; solving correctly, d = 30 km, so the answer is 30 km.
Source: PYQ-style

84. A man covers a certain distance on a scooter. Had he moved 3 km/h faster, he would have taken 40 minutes less; had he moved 2 km/h slower, he would have taken 40 minutes more. Find the distance.
(a) 40 km (b) 30 km (c) 50 km (d) 60 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let speed be v and time t. From the two conditions, −2v/3 + 3t = 2 and 2v/3 − 2t = 4/3; adding gives t = 10/3 h and then v = 12 km/h. The trap is assuming the distance equals v × 40 minutes; actually distance = 12 × 10/3 = 40 km, so the answer is 40 km.
Source: PYQ-style

85. A 300 m long train crosses a platform 200 m long in 25 seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.
(a) 60 km/h (b) 72 km/h (c) 80 km/h (d) 90 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using only the train's length (300 m), which gives 43.2 km/h; crossing a platform needs 300 + 200 = 500 m. Speed = 500/25 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h, so the answer is 72 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

86. Ravi rows a certain distance downstream in 6 hours and returns the same distance in 9 hours. If the stream flows at 3 km/h, find Ravi's speed in still water.
(a) 12 km/h (b) 15 km/h (c) 18 km/h (d) 21 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Since distances are equal, 6(x + 3) = 9(x − 3), giving 6x + 18 = 9x − 27, so x = 15. The trap is setting the times proportional to the speeds the wrong way round, which yields no valid answer; the correct still-water speed is 15 km/h, so the answer is 15 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

87. A person covers half of his journey at 30 km/h and the remaining half at 20 km/h, taking 5 hours in total. Find the total distance.
(a) 100 km (b) 140 km (c) 150 km (d) 120 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let the total distance be 2d; then d/30 + d/20 = 5, so 5d/60 = 5 and d = 60. The trap is reporting d = 60 km as the final answer, forgetting it is only half the journey; total distance = 2 × 60 = 120 km, so the answer is 120 km.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

88. Without stoppages a train travels at 60 km/h; with stoppages it covers 48 km/h on average. How many minutes per hour does the train stop?
(a) 10 min (b) 20 min (c) 15 min (d) 12 min
Answer: (d)
Solution: The train loses 60 − 48 = 12 km of travel each hour, and covering 12 km at 60 km/h takes 12/60 h = 12 minutes. The trap is computing 12/48 = 15 minutes using the reduced speed; the correct stoppage time is 12 minutes, so the answer is 12 min.
Source: PYQ-style

89. A boy walks from home to school at 4 km/h and returns at 3 km/h, taking 7 hours in all. Find the distance between home and school.
(a) 12 km (b) 10 km (c) 14 km (d) 15 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: d/4 + d/3 = 7 gives 7d/12 = 7, so d = 12. The trap is solving 7d/12 = 7 and then doubling the result to 24 km, but d here is already the one-way distance; the distance is 12 km, so the answer is 12 km.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

90. A train running at 80 km/h crosses a 350 m long tunnel in 36 seconds. Find the length of the train.
(a) 450 m (b) 300 m (c) 200 m (d) 500 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = 80 × 5/18 = 200/9 m/s, so distance covered in 36 s = 800 m. The trap is reporting 800 m as the train's length, forgetting the tunnel; train length = 800 − 350 = 450 m, so the answer is 450 m.
Source: PYQ-style

91. A car covers 420 km at a certain speed. If the speed had been 10 km/h more, it would have taken 1 hour less. Find the original speed.
(a) 60 km/h (b) 70 km/h (c) 80 km/h (d) 50 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: 420/v − 420/(v+10) = 1 gives v(v+10) = 4200, i.e. v² + 10v − 4200 = 0, so v = (−10 + 130)/2 = 60. The trap is taking the negative root or guessing 70 km/h, which gives a time difference of only 6/7 hour; the original speed is 60 km/h, so the answer is 60 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

92. A train covers 10 km in 12 minutes. If its speed is decreased by 5 km/h, how much time will it take to cover the same distance?
(a) 13 min (b) 14 min (c) 13 min 20 sec (d) 14 min 24 sec
Answer: (c)
Solution: Original speed = 10 ÷ (12/60) = 50 km/h; new speed = 45 km/h, so time = 10/45 h = 2/9 × 60 = 13⅓ minutes. The trap is rounding 13⅓ down to 13 minutes; ⅓ minute is 20 seconds, so the answer is 13 min 20 sec.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

93. A boat takes 5 hours to go 15 km upstream and 3 hours to go the same 15 km downstream. Find the speed of the boat in still water.
(a) 7 km/h (b) 5 km/h (c) 6 km/h (d) 4 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: Upstream speed = 15/5 = 3 km/h and downstream speed = 15/3 = 5 km/h. The trap is computing (5 − 3)/2 = 1 km/h, which is the stream's speed; boat speed in still water = (3 + 5)/2 = 4 km/h, so the answer is 4 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

94. A man covers a certain distance at a fixed speed. Had he moved 5 km/h faster, he would have taken 1 hour less; had he moved 3 km/h slower, he would have taken 1 hour more. Find the distance.
(a) 45 km (b) 50 km (c) 75 km (d) 60 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: From (v+5)(t−1) = vt we get −v + 5t = 5, and from (v−3)(t+1) = vt we get v − 3t = 3; adding gives 2t = 8, so t = 4 h and v = 15 km/h. The trap is stopping at v = 15 or t = 4 as the answer; distance = 15 × 4 = 60 km, so the answer is 60 km.
Source: PYQ-style

95. A train, after travelling 50 km, meets with an accident and then proceeds at 3/4 of its original speed, arriving 35 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 24 km farther on, it would have been only 25 minutes late. Find the original speed of the train.
(a) 42 km/h (b) 54 km/h (c) 48 km/h (d) 60 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 10-minute difference in delay comes from the 24 km stretch: 24/(3v/4) − 24/v = 32/v − 24/v = 8/v = 1/6 h, so v = 48. The trap is equating 24 km directly with 10 minutes to get 144 km/h; the original speed is 48 km/h, so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

96. A man can row at 6 km/h in still water. The river flows at 2 km/h, and he takes 3 hours to row to a place and back. How far is the place?
(a) 8 km (b) 6 km (c) 9 km (d) 12 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: Downstream speed = 8 km/h and upstream speed = 4 km/h, so d/8 + d/4 = 3 gives 3d/8 = 3 and d = 8. The trap is using the still-water speed 6 km/h for the round trip, giving 9 km; the correct distance is 8 km, so the answer is 8 km.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

97. A person travels from P to Q at 20 km/h and returns at 30 km/h, taking 5 hours in total. Find the distance from P to Q.
(a) 55 km (b) 50 km (c) 60 km (d) 65 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: d/20 + d/30 = 5 gives 5d/60 = 5, so d = 60. The trap is using the average speed 25 km/h over 5 hours to get 125 km for one way, but 125 km is roughly the round trip; the one-way distance is 60 km, so the answer is 60 km.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

98. A 150 m long train runs at 60 km/h. In how much time will it pass a man running at 12 km/h in the opposite direction?
(a) 6 sec (b) 7.5 sec (c) 8 sec (d) 9 sec
Answer: (b)
Solution: Relative speed in opposite directions = 60 + 12 = 72 km/h = 20 m/s, so time = 150/20 = 7.5 s. The trap is subtracting the speeds (48 km/h), which gives 11.25 s; the correct time is 7.5 seconds, so the answer is 7.5 sec.

99. The speed of a boat in still water is 15 km/h and the current flows at 3 km/h. What distance does the boat travel downstream in 12 minutes?
(a) 3.6 km (b) 3.0 km (c) 4.2 km (d) 2.4 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: Downstream speed = 15 + 3 = 18 km/h and time = 12/60 = 1/5 h, so distance = 18/5 = 3.6 km. The trap is using the still-water speed 15 km/h, which gives 3.0 km; the downstream distance is 3.6 km, so the answer is 3.6 km.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

100. A car starts at 60 km/h and increases its speed by 10 km/h after every hour. What is its average speed over a 4-hour journey?
(a) 70 km/h (b) 80 km/h (c) 75 km/h (d) 85 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hourly distances are 60, 70, 80 and 90 km, totalling 300 km in 4 hours. The trap is averaging only the first and last speeds' midpoint incorrectly or taking 80 km/h (the third hour's speed); average speed = 300/4 = 75 km/h, so the answer is 75 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style
