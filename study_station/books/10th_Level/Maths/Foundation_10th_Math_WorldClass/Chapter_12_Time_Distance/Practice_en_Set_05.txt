101. A car travels the first 50 km at 25 km/h and the next 50 km at 75 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 35 km/h (b) 40 km/h (c) 37.5 km/h (d) 45 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is taking the simple mean of 25 and 75, which gives 50 km/h, but average speed is total distance divided by total time. Times are 50/25 = 2 h and 50/75 = 2/3 h, so average speed = 100 ÷ (8/3) = 37.5 km/h, so the answer is 37.5 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

102. A train 250 m long passes a pole in 12.5 seconds. Find its speed in km/h.
(a) 72 km/h (b) 68 km/h (c) 60 km/h (d) 80 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students forget the 18/5 conversion and stop at 20 m/s. Speed = 250/12.5 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h, so the answer is 72 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science (Motion and Time)

103. A man walks at 6 km/h for 2 hours and at 4 km/h for 3 hours. Find his average speed for the entire journey.
(a) 4.8 km/h (b) 5.0 km/h (c) 5.2 km/h (d) 4.5 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is the simple average of 6 and 4, i.e. 5 km/h, but the times differ. Distances are 12 km and 12 km, total 24 km in 5 h, giving 24/5 = 4.8 km/h, so the answer is 4.8 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

104. A cyclist covers 15 km in 50 minutes. His speed in km/h is:
(a) 15 km/h (b) 16 km/h (c) 18 km/h (d) 20 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is treating 50 minutes as half an hour, which wrongly gives 30 km/h-type thinking; 50 min = 5/6 h. Speed = 15 ÷ (5/6) = 15 × 6/5 = 18 km/h, so the answer is 18 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

105. A boat goes 12 km upstream in 4 hours and returns the same distance downstream in 2 hours. Find the speed of the stream.
(a) 1 km/h (b) 1.5 km/h (c) 2 km/h (d) 3 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is halving the downstream speed or taking the difference 6 − 3 = 3 directly as the stream speed; the stream speed is half the difference. Upstream = 3 km/h, downstream = 6 km/h, stream = (6 − 3)/2 = 1.5 km/h, so the answer is 1.5 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

106. Two trains of lengths 120 m and 180 m move in opposite directions at 54 km/h and 36 km/h. Find the time taken to cross each other.
(a) 10 sec (b) 15 sec (c) 12 sec (d) 20 sec
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding the speeds but forgetting to convert km/h to m/s. Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s, total distance = 300 m, time = 300/25 = 12 sec, so the answer is 12 sec.
Source: PYQ-style

107. A person goes from A to B at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Find his average speed for the entire trip.
(a) 45 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 52 km/h (d) 48 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is the arithmetic mean 50 km/h, but for equal distances the harmonic formula applies. Average speed = 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h, so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

108. A train leaves station P at 8:00 am and reaches station Q at 12:00 noon. If the distance between the stations is 240 km, find the speed of the train.
(a) 55 km/h (b) 60 km/h (c) 70 km/h (d) 65 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The only trap is miscounting the travel time; it is 4 hours, not 3 or 5. Speed = 240/4 = 60 km/h, so the answer is 60 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

109. If a car increases its speed by 10 km/h, it takes 1 hour less to cover a distance of 300 km. Find the original speed.
(a) 60 km/h (b) 55 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 65 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is setting up 300/(v+10) − 300/v = 1 with the wrong sign; the original time is larger. From 300/v − 300/(v+10) = 1 we get v(v+10) = 3000, i.e. v² + 10v − 3000 = 0, giving v = 50 km/h, so the answer is 50 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics (Quadratic Equations)

110. A man covers 24 km in 3 hours, partly on foot at 4 km/h and partly on bicycle at 10 km/h. Find the distance covered on foot.
(a) 5 km (b) 7 km (c) 6 km (d) 4 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming equal distances of 12 km each, which would need 3 + 1.2 = 4.2 hours, not 3. Solving x/4 + (24 − x)/10 = 3 gives 5x + 48 − 2x = 60, so x = 4 km, so the answer is 4 km.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Linear Equations)

111. A 400 m long train crosses a platform 300 m long in 35 seconds. Find the speed of the train.
(a) 60 km/h (b) 72 km/h (c) 68 km/h (d) 80 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using only the train's length 400 m instead of train plus platform. Total distance = 700 m, speed = 700/35 = 20 m/s = 72 km/h, so the answer is 72 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

112. A boat covers 20 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 5 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water.
(a) 7 km/h (b) 6 km/h (c) 8 km/h (d) 5 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking half the difference, which gives the stream speed, not the boat speed. Downstream = 10 km/h, upstream = 4 km/h, boat speed = (10 + 4)/2 = 7 km/h, so the answer is 7 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

113. A person travels the first third of a distance at 10 km/h, the second third at 20 km/h, and the last third at 30 km/h. What is the average speed?
(a) 180/11 km/h (b) 15 km/h (c) 18 km/h (d) 20 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the simple mean of 10, 20, 30, i.e. 20 km/h, which ignores the different times. With total distance 3d, time = d/10 + d/20 + d/30 = 11d/60, so average speed = 3d ÷ (11d/60) = 180/11 km/h, so the answer is 180/11 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

114. A train running at 90 km/h passes a man walking in the same direction at 18 km/h in 6 seconds. Find the length of the train.
(a) 160 m (b) 100 m (c) 140 m (d) 120 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding the speeds; for the same direction we subtract them. Relative speed = 90 − 18 = 72 km/h = 20 m/s, length = 20 × 6 = 120 m, so the answer is 120 m.
Source: PYQ-style

115. The ratio of the speeds of two trains is 3:4. If the second train covers 480 km in 6 hours, find the speed of the first train.
(a) 80 km/h (b) 90 km/h (c) 100 km/h (d) 60 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is reporting 80 km/h, which is the second train's speed, not the first. Second train = 480/6 = 80 km/h, so first train = (3/4) × 80 = 60 km/h, so the answer is 60 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics (Ratio and Proportion)

116. A cyclist, after riding some distance, meets with an accident and then covers the rest at 2/3 of his original speed, arriving 20 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 10 km farther on, he would have been only 10 minutes late. Find his original speed.
(a) 25 km/h (b) 30 km/h (c) 35 km/h (d) 40 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is comparing the full journey times; only the differing 10 km stretch matters, where the delay difference is 10 minutes. For 10 km: 10/(2v/3) − 10/v = 5/v = 1/6 h, giving v = 30 km/h, so the answer is 30 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

117. A boat can travel 30 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 3 hours. The speed of the current is:
(a) 2.5 km/h (b) 3 km/h (c) 5 km/h (d) 2 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking the difference 15 − 10 = 5 km/h as the current speed; the current is half the difference. Downstream = 15 km/h, upstream = 10 km/h, current = (15 − 10)/2 = 2.5 km/h, so the answer is 2.5 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

118. A car completes a journey in 8 hours. It covers half the distance at 40 km/h and the rest at 60 km/h. What is the total distance?
(a) 400 km (b) 384 km (c) 432 km (d) 480 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using the average speed 50 km/h × 8 = 400 km, but equal distances need the harmonic mean 48 km/h. With half-distance d: d/40 + d/60 = 8 gives 5d/120 = 8, so d = 192 and total = 384 km, so the answer is 384 km.
Source: NCERT Exemplar Class 8 Mathematics

119. Without stoppages, a train travels at 72 km/h. With stoppages, its speed is 60 km/h. How many minutes does the train stop per hour?
(a) 8 min (b) 10 min (c) 12 min (d) 15 min
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing 12/60 = 12 minutes; the lost 12 km must be measured at the running speed of 72 km/h. Time to cover 12 km at 72 km/h = 12/72 h = 10 minutes, so the answer is 10 min.
Source: PYQ-style

120. A person walks from home to office at 5 km/h and is late by 30 minutes. If he walks at 6 km/h, he is late by only 10 minutes. Find the distance between home and office.
(a) 10 km (b) 12 km (c) 15 km (d) 20 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking the difference of lateness as the full time; the difference of the two travel times is 20 minutes = 1/3 h. Then d/5 − d/6 = 1/3 gives d/30 = 1/3, so d = 10 km, so the answer is 10 km.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Linear Equations)

121. Two trains start at the same time from stations P and Q, 300 km apart, and travel towards each other at 60 km/h and 40 km/h. A bird starts with the faster train, flies at 100 km/h to the other train, turns back immediately, and keeps flying between them until the trains meet. What total distance does the bird fly?
(a) 250 km (b) 280 km (c) 320 km (d) 300 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is summing an infinite series of back-and-forth trips, which is unnecessary. The trains meet in 300/(60 + 40) = 3 hours, and the bird flies the whole time at 100 km/h, covering 300 km, so the answer is 300 km.
Source: PYQ-style

122. A man rows a boat at 10 km/h in still water and the river flows at 5 km/h. He rows to a place and returns in 4 hours. What is the distance of the place?
(a) 12 km (b) 18 km (c) 15 km (d) 20 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using the still-water speed 10 km/h for both legs, which would give 20 km. Downstream = 15 km/h, upstream = 5 km/h, and d/15 + d/5 = 4 gives 4d/15 = 4, so d = 15 km, so the answer is 15 km.
Source: PYQ-style

123. A car covers 480 km in a certain time. If it had travelled 8 km/h slower, it would have taken 2 hours longer. Find its original speed.
(a) 40 km/h (b) 56 km/h (c) 52 km/h (d) 48 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is writing the equation with the time difference reversed in sign; the slower speed gives the longer time. From 480/(v − 8) − 480/v = 2 we get v² − 8v − 1920 = 0, i.e. (v − 48)(v + 40) = 0, so v = 48 km/h, so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics (Quadratic Equations)

124. A train passes a platform 200 m long in 20 seconds and a man standing on the platform in 10 seconds. The length of the train is:
(a) 200 m (b) 150 m (c) 250 m (d) 300 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the platform time alone gives the train length; the extra 10 seconds is exactly the time to cover the 200 m platform. Speed = 200/10 = 20 m/s, and train length = 20 × 10 = 200 m, so the answer is 200 m.
Source: PYQ-style

125. A car travels from A to B at 30 km/h and returns at 20 km/h, taking a total of 1 hour 40 minutes. Later it makes the same round trip with both speeds increased by 10 km/h. How much time is saved?
(a) 40 min (b) 50 min (c) 30 min (d) 60 min
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is finding the distance 20 km and then stopping, or assuming the saving is proportional to the speed rise. Original time = 100 min; new time = 20/40 + 20/30 = 7/6 h = 70 min, so the saving is 30 min, so the answer is 30 min.
Source: PYQ-style
