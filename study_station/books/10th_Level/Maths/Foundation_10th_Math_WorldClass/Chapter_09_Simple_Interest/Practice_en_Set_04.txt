**76. A sum of ₹ 12,500 amounts to ₹ 15,500 in 4 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest?**
(A) 5% (B) 6% (C) 7% (D) 8%
**Correct Answer: (B) 6%**
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
SI = Amount – Principal = 15500 – 12500 = ₹3000.
SI = (P × R × T)/100 → 3000 = (12500 × R × 4)/100 → 3000 = 500R → R = 6%.
**Source:** SSC CHSL
