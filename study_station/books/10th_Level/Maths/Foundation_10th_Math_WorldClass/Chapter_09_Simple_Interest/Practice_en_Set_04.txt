Set 4: Simple Interest Multiple Choice Questions (Medium to Hard)
Questions 76–100: Medium to Hard Level

76. Nitin borrowed some money at 6% per annum for the first 3 years, 9% per annum for the next 5 years, and 13% per annum for the period beyond 8 years. If the total interest paid by him at the end of 11 years is ₹8,160, how much money did he borrow?
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹7,500 (c) ₹8,500 (d) ₹9,000
Answer: (a) ₹8,000
Solution: Total effective rate = (3 × 6%) + (5 × 9%) + (3 × 13%) = 18% + 45% + 39% = 102%. 102% of P = 8160 => P = (8160 × 100) ÷ 102 = ₹8,000.
Source: SSC CGL

77. A sum of ₹10,000 is lent partly at 8% and the remaining at 10% per annum simple interest. If the average annual interest is 9.2%, find the two parts.
(a) ₹4,500, ₹5,500 (b) ₹4,000, ₹6,000 (c) ₹3,500, ₹6,500 (d) ₹5,000, ₹5,000
Answer: (b) ₹4,000, ₹6,000
Solution: By rule of alligation: Ratio = (10 - 9.2) : (9.2 - 8) = 0.8 : 1.2 = 2 : 3. Part 1 = 10000 × (2/5) = ₹4,000, Part 2 = ₹6,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

78. If a person lends ₹4,000 at 5% simple interest per annum, how much more should he invest at 8% per annum so as to have an overall return of 6% per annum on the entire investment?
(a) ₹1,500 (b) ₹1,800 (c) ₹2,000 (d) ₹2,500
Answer: (c) ₹2,000
Solution: (4000 × 0.05) + 0.08x = 0.06(4000 + x) => 200 + 0.08x = 240 + 0.06x => 0.02x = 40 => x = ₹2,000.
Source: RRB Group D

79. A sum amounts to ₹3,600 in 3 years and ₹4,200 in 4.5 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 12.5% (b) 14 2/7% (c) 15% (d) 16 2/3%
Answer: (d) 16 2/3%
Solution: SI for 1.5 years = 4200 - 3600 = ₹600 => SI for 1 year = ₹400. SI for 3 years = ₹1,200 => Principal = 3600 - 1200 = ₹2,400. Rate = (400 ÷ 2400) × 100 = 16 2/3%.
Source: SSC MTS

80. With a given rate of simple interest, the ratio of principal to amount for a certain period of time is 4 : 5. After 3 more years, with the same rate of interest, the ratio of principal to amount becomes 5 : 7. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 5% (b) 6% (c) 4% (d) 7%
Answer: (a) 5%
Solution: Let Principal = 20 units (LCM of 4 and 5). Initially: Amount = 25 units => SI = 5 units. After 3 years: Amount = 28 units => SI = 8 units. Increase in SI in 3 years = 3 units => 1 year SI = 1 unit. Rate = (1 ÷ 20) × 100 = 5%.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

81. A person borrows ₹7,000 at simple interest. After 3 years, he pays ₹3,000 to the lender and at the end of 5 years from start, he pays ₹5,450 to clear all dues. What was the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 4% (b) 5% (c) 6% (d) 7%
Answer: (b) 5%
Solution: Total amount paid = 3000 + 5450 = ₹8,450 => Total interest = 8450 - 7000 = ₹1,450. Total effective principal × time = (7000 × 3) + (4000 × 2) = 21000 + 8000 = 29,000. Rate = (1450 × 100) ÷ 29000 = 5%.
Source: SSC GD

82. If the simple interest on a certain sum at 4% per annum for 8 months is the same as the simple interest on ₹450 at 5% per annum for 16 months, find the sum.
(a) ₹1,000 (b) ₹1,050 (c) ₹1,125 (d) ₹1,200
Answer: (c) ₹1,125
Solution: P × 4 × (8/12) = 450 × 5 × (16/12) => P × 32 = 450 × 80 => P = (450 × 80) ÷ 32 = 36000 ÷ 32 = ₹1,125.
Source: SSC CGL

83. A sum of ₹1,550 was lent partly at 5% and partly at 8% simple interest per annum. The total interest received after 3 years was ₹300. The ratio of money lent at 5% to that lent at 8% is:
(a) 5 : 8 (b) 8 : 5 (c) 15 : 16 (d) 16 : 15
Answer: (d) 16 : 15
Solution: Average annual interest = 300 ÷ 3 = ₹100. 5% of 1550 = 77.5; 8% of 1550 = 124. By alligation: Ratio = (124 - 100) : (100 - 77.5) = 24 : 22.5 = 48 : 45 = 16 : 15.
Source: RRB NTPC

84. A man borrowed a sum at 4% per annum for the first 2 years, 6% per annum for the next 4 years, and 8% per annum for the period beyond 6 years. If he paid a total simple interest of ₹1,120 for 9 years, how much did he borrow?
(a) ₹2,000 (b) ₹1,555 (c) ₹2,400 (d) ₹2,500
Answer: (a) ₹2,000
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) incorrectly applies 8% to the entire 9 years! Slabs: 2 yrs @ 4% = 8%; 4 yrs @ 6% = 24%; remaining (9 - 6) = 3 yrs @ 8% = 24%. Total rate = 8% + 24% + 24% = 56%. P = (1120 × 100) ÷ 56 = ₹2,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

85. What annual payment will discharge a debt of ₹1,092 due in 3 years at 12% simple interest per annum?
(a) ₹300 (b) ₹325 (c) ₹350 (d) ₹375
Answer: (b) ₹325
Solution: Debt formula: Debt = 3x + [x × 12 × (2 + 1)] ÷ 100 = 3x + 0.36x = 3.36x. 3.36x = 1092 => x = 1092 ÷ 3.36 = ₹325.
Source: SSC CGL

86. A certain sum amounts to ₹5,200 in 5 years and to ₹5,680 in 7 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 5% (b) 5.5% (c) 6% (d) 6.5%
Answer: (c) 6%
Solution: SI for 2 years = 5680 - 5200 = ₹480 => SI for 1 year = ₹240. SI for 5 years = ₹1,200. Principal = 5200 - 1200 = ₹4,000. Rate = (240 ÷ 4000) × 100 = 6%.
Source: SSC MTS

87. A sum of money becomes 3 times itself in 6 years at simple interest. How many times of itself will it become in 15 years at the same rate?
(a) 4 times (b) 5 times (c) 5.5 times (d) 6 times
Answer: (d) 6 times
Solution: (n1 - 1)/t1 = (n2 - 1)/t2 => (3 - 1)/6 = (n2 - 1)/15 => 2/6 = (n2 - 1)/15 => 1/3 = (n2 - 1)/15 => n2 - 1 = 5 => n2 = 6 times.
Source: RRB Group D

88. If the rate of simple interest is 12% per annum, what is the present value of ₹1,240 due in 2 years?
(a) ₹1,000 (b) ₹980 (c) ₹1,050 (d) ₹1,100
Answer: (a) ₹1,000
Solution: Amount = P × [1 + (12 × 2)/100] = 1.24P = 1240 => P = 1240 ÷ 1.24 = ₹1,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

89. An investor divided ₹2,600 among three investments at 4%, 6% and 8% simple interest per annum respectively. If the annual interest from all three investments is equal, find the sum invested at 4%.
(a) ₹1,000 (b) ₹1,200 (c) ₹800 (d) ₹600
Answer: (b) ₹1,200
Solution: 4x = 6y = 8z => 2x = 3y = 4z. Divide by 12: x/6 = y/4 = z/3. Ratio x : y : z = 6 : 4 : 3. Sum of terms = 13. Investment at 4% = 2600 × (6/13) = ₹1,200.
Source: SSC CGL

90. Simple interest on a certain sum is 16/25 of the principal. If the number representing rate percent per annum and time in years are equal, find the rate percent per annum.
(a) 6% (b) 7% (c) 8% (d) 10%
Answer: (c) 8%
Solution: Given R = T. Then R² = (SI ÷ P) × 100 = (16/25) × 100 = 64 => R = 8%.
Source: SSC CHSL

91. A person invested 1/3 of his capital at 7%, 1/4 at 8% and the remainder at 10% simple interest per annum. If his total annual income from interest is ₹561, find the total capital.
(a) ₹6,000 (b) ₹6,200 (c) ₹6,400 (d) ₹6,600
Answer: (d) ₹6,600
Solution: Remainder = 1 - (1/3 + 1/4) = 5/12. Annual interest = P × [(1/3 × 7%) + (1/4 × 8%) + (5/12 × 10%)] = P × (102 ÷ 1200) = 0.085 P. 0.085 P = 561 => P = ₹6,600.
Source: RRB NTPC

92. If a sum of ₹1,600 gives a simple interest of ₹252 in 2 years and 4 months, what is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 6 3/4% (b) 6 1/2% (c) 7% (d) 7 1/4%
Answer: (a) 6 3/4%
Solution: Time = 2 + 4/12 = 7/3 years. Rate = (252 × 100) ÷ [1600 × (7/3)] = 25200 ÷ (11200/3) = (252 × 3) ÷ 112 = 6.75% = 6 3/4%.
Source: SSC GD

93. A mobile phone is sold for ₹10,000 cash or ₹2,000 cash down payment and ₹8,400 after 6 months. What is the rate of simple interest charged per annum?
(a) 8% (b) 10% (c) 12% (d) 9%
Answer: (b) 10%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 8% incorrectly calculates interest on the full ₹10,000 price! Balance borrowed = 10000 - 2000 = ₹8,000. Interest for 6 months (0.5 yr) = 8400 - 8000 = ₹400. Rate = (400 × 100) ÷ (8000 × 0.5) = 10%.
Source: SSC MTS

94. A sum of money at simple interest doubles itself in 7 years. In how many years will it become 4 times of itself at the same rate?
(a) 14 years (b) 18 years (c) 21 years (d) 28 years
Answer: (c) 21 years
Solution: (2 - 1) ÷ 7 = (4 - 1) ÷ T => 1/7 = 3/T => T = 21 years.
Source: SSC CGL

95. Two equal sums are lent at the same time at 8% and 7% simple interest per annum respectively. The first sum is recovered 6 months earlier than the second and the amount in each case is ₹2,560. Find each sum.
(a) ₹1,800 (b) ₹1,900 (c) ₹2,200 (d) ₹2,000
Answer: (d) ₹2,000
Solution: Let time for 7% loan be T years. Then time for 8% loan is (T - 0.5) years. Equal amounts on equal principals mean equal interests: 8(T - 0.5) = 7T => 8T - 4 = 7T => T = 4 years. Amount = P[1 + (7 × 4)/100] = 1.28P = 2560 => P = ₹2,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

96. What is the difference in simple interest on ₹6,000 for 1.5 years at 4% per annum when calculated annually versus semi-annually?
(a) ₹0 (b) ₹2.40 (c) ₹4.80 (d) ₹6.00
Answer: (a) ₹0
Solution: In simple interest, interest is always calculated only on the original principal regardless of compounding periods, so SI remains identical in both cases, difference is ₹0.
Source: RRB Group D

97. A person lent ₹5,000 partly at 5% and partly at 6% simple interest per annum. If the 6% part yields ₹80 more interest per year than the 5% part, find the amount lent at 6%.
(a) ₹2,500 (b) ₹3,000 (c) ₹3,500 (d) ₹2,000
Answer: (b) ₹3,000
Solution: 0.06x - 0.05(5000 - x) = 80 => 0.11x - 250 = 80 => 0.11x = 330 => x = ₹3,000.
Source: SSC CGL

98. If ₹64 amounts to ₹83.20 in 2 years, what will ₹86 amount to in 4 years at the same rate of simple interest per annum?
(a) ₹132.40 (b) ₹135.20 (c) ₹137.60 (d) ₹140.00
Answer: (c) ₹137.60
Solution: SI on 64 in 2 years = 83.20 - 64 = ₹19.20 => Rate = (19.20 × 100) ÷ (64 × 2) = 15%. SI on 86 in 4 years = (86 × 15 × 4) ÷ 100 = ₹51.60. Amount = 86 + 51.60 = ₹137.60.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

99. If the annual rate of simple interest decreases from 11.5% to 10%, a moneylender's yearly income decreases by ₹55.50. His capital is:
(a) ₹3,500 (b) ₹3,600 (c) ₹3,800 (d) ₹3,700
Answer: (d) ₹3,700
Solution: Decrease in rate = 11.5% - 10% = 1.5%. 1.5% of P = 55.50 => P = (55.50 × 100) ÷ 1.5 = ₹3,700.
Source: SSC CHSL

100. In how many years will a sum of ₹800 at 10% per annum simple interest produce the same interest as ₹600 at 8% per annum for 5 years?
(a) 2.5 years (b) 3 years (c) 3.5 years (d) 4 years
Answer: (b) 3 years
Solution: SI on ₹600 at 8% for 5 years = (600 × 8 × 5) ÷ 100 = ₹240. (800 × 10 × T) ÷ 100 = 80T = 240 => T = 3 years.
Source: SSC MTS
