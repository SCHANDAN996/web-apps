**52. A sum of ₹15,000 is lent at 8% p.a. simple interest for 4 years. After 2 years, the borrower pays back ₹8,000. How much amount will he have to pay at the end of 4 years to clear the loan?**
(A) ₹11,200 (B) ₹12,480 (C) ₹13,600 (D) ₹14,200
**Correct Answer: (B) ₹12,480**
**Solution:**
Interest for first 2 years = 15000 × 8 × 2/100 = 2400.
Amount due after 2 years = 15000 + 2400 = 17400.
He pays ₹8000, so remaining = 17400 – 8000 = 9400 (this is the new principal).
Interest for next 2 years on ₹9400 = 9400 × 8 × 2/100 = 1504.
Final amount = 9400 + 1504 = ₹ 12,480.
**Source:** IBPS PO

**75. A sum of money at simple interest becomes 3/2 of itself in 2.5 years. The rate per annum is:**
(A) 12% (B) 16% (C) 18% (D) 20%
**Correct Answer: (D) 20%**
**Solution:**
Amount = (3/2)P ⇒ SI = (3/2 – 1)P = (1/2)P.
SI = (P × R × 2.5)/100 = (2.5PR)/100.
Set (2.5PR)/100 = P/2 ⇒ 2.5R/100 = 1/2 ⇒ R = (100/2)/2.5 = 50/2.5 = 20%.
**Source:** SSC CGL
