Set 3: Simple Interest Multiple Choice Questions (Medium)
Questions 51–75: Medium Level

51. A sum was lent at simple interest at a certain rate for 2 years. Had it been lent at 3% higher rate, it would have fetched ₹300 more. What is the sum?
(a) ₹5,000 (b) ₹4,500 (c) ₹5,500 (d) ₹6,000
Answer: (a) ₹5,000
Solution: Additional interest = (P × 3 × 2) ÷ 100 = 6P / 100 = 300 => P = (300 × 100) ÷ 6 = ₹5,000.
Source: SSC CGL

52. In what time will ₹8,000 amount to ₹9,600 at 5% per annum simple interest?
(a) 3 years (b) 4 years (c) 5 years (d) 4.5 years
Answer: (b) 4 years
Solution: SI = 9600 - 8000 = ₹1,600. Time = (1600 × 100) ÷ (8000 × 5) = 160000 ÷ 40000 = 4 years.
Source: SSC CHSL

53. If the simple interest on ₹1,400 for 3 years is ₹63 more than the simple interest on ₹1,050 for the same time at the same rate, what is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 5% (b) 5.5% (c) 6% (d) 6.5%
Answer: (c) 6%
Solution: Difference in principal = 1400 - 1050 = ₹350. (350 × R × 3) ÷ 100 = 63 => 10.5R = 63 => R = 6%.
Source: RRB Group D

54. A sum of money becomes 5 times itself in 20 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 9 times itself?
(a) 32 years (b) 35 years (c) 36 years (d) 40 years
Answer: (d) 40 years
Solution: (5 - 1) ÷ 20 = (9 - 1) ÷ T => 4/20 = 8/T => 1/5 = 8/T => T = 40 years.
Source: SSC MTS

55. A lends ₹2,500 to B and a certain sum to C at the same time at 7% per annum simple interest. If after 4 years, A altogether receives ₹1,120 as interest from B and C, what is the sum lent to C?
(a) ₹1,500 (b) ₹1,400 (c) ₹1,600 (d) ₹1,800
Answer: (a) ₹1,500
Solution: SI from B = (2500 × 7 × 4) ÷ 100 = ₹700. SI from C = 1120 - 700 = ₹420. Sum lent to C = (420 × 100) ÷ (7 × 4) = ₹1,500.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

56. A person borrows ₹5,000 for 2 years at 4% per annum simple interest. He immediately lends it to another person at 6.25% per annum for 2 years. Find his gain in the transaction per year.
(a) ₹100 (b) ₹112.50 (c) ₹125 (d) ₹150
Answer: (b) ₹112.50
Solution: Rate difference = 6.25% - 4% = 2.25%. Gain per year = 5000 × (2.25 ÷ 100) = ₹112.50.
Source: SSC GD

57. A certain sum of money amounts to ₹756 in 2 years and ₹873 in 3.5 years at simple interest. Find the original principal.
(a) ₹550 (b) ₹580 (c) ₹600 (d) ₹620
Answer: (c) ₹600
Solution: SI for 1.5 years = 873 - 756 = ₹117 => SI for 1 year = ₹78. SI for 2 years = ₹156. Principal = 756 - 156 = ₹600.
Source: SSC CGL

58. The simple interest on a sum of money is equal to the principal, and the number of years is equal to the rate percent per annum. Find the rate percent per annum.
(a) 8% (b) 12% (c) 15% (d) 10%
Answer: (d) 10%
Solution: Given SI = P and T = R. Formula: P = (P × R × R) ÷ 100 => R² = 100 => R = 10%.
Source: RRB NTPC

59. What is the simple interest on ₹8,000 at an interest rate of 4% per quarter for 1 year?
(a) ₹1,280 (b) ₹320 (c) ₹640 (d) ₹960
Answer: (a) ₹1,280
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) ₹320 incorrectly considers 4% annually without multiplying by 4 quarters! Annual rate = 4% × 4 = 16%. SI = (8000 × 16 × 1) ÷ 100 = ₹1,280.
Source: SSC CHSL

60. A sum of ₹1,875 was lent at 4% per annum simple interest. If the total interest earned was ₹375, find the time period in years.
(a) 4 years (b) 5 years (c) 6 years (d) 7 years
Answer: (b) 5 years
Solution: Time = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × R) = (375 × 100) ÷ (1875 × 4) = 37500 ÷ 7500 = 5 years.
Source: SSC CGL

61. A sum was invested for 3 years at simple interest. If the interest rate was 4% for the first year, 5% for the second year, and 6% for the third year, and the total interest earned was ₹900, find the sum.
(a) ₹5,000 (b) ₹5,500 (c) ₹6,000 (d) ₹6,500
Answer: (c) ₹6,000
Solution: Total effective rate = 4% + 5% + 6% = 15%. 15% of P = 900 => P = (900 × 100) ÷ 15 = ₹6,000.
Source: SSC MTS

62. A person deposited ₹400 for 2 years, ₹550 for 4 years, and ₹1,200 for 6 years at the same rate of simple interest. If he received a total interest of ₹1,020, what was the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 8% (b) 9% (c) 12% (d) 10%
Answer: (d) 10%
Solution: Total equivalent principal for 1 year = (400 × 2) + (550 × 4) + (1200 × 6) = 800 + 2200 + 7200 = ₹10,200. Rate = (1020 × 100) ÷ 10200 = 10%.
Source: RRB Group D

63. A sum of money invested at simple interest amounts to ₹10,000 in 4 years and to ₹12,000 in 6 years. What will it amount to in 8 years?
(a) ₹14,000 (b) ₹13,500 (c) ₹14,500 (d) ₹15,000
Answer: (a) ₹14,000
Solution: Interest earned in 2 years (from year 4 to 6) = 12000 - 10000 = ₹2,000. In next 2 years (year 8), Amount = 12000 + 2000 = ₹14,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

64. If the ratio of principal and the amount after 1 year at simple interest is 10 : 12, what is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 15% (b) 20% (c) 25% (d) 12%
Answer: (b) 20%
Solution: P = 10 units, Amount = 12 units => SI = 12 - 10 = 2 units. Rate = (2 ÷ 10) × 100 = 20%.
Source: SSC CGL

65. How much time will it take for an amount of ₹450 to yield ₹81 as simple interest at 4.5% per annum?
(a) 3 years (b) 3.5 years (c) 4 years (d) 4.5 years
Answer: (c) 4 years
Solution: Time = (81 × 100) ÷ (450 × 4.5) = 8100 ÷ 2025 = 4 years.
Source: SSC CHSL

66. An amount of ₹7,000 was divided into two parts and lent at 6% and 4% per annum simple interest. If the total interest earned in 5 years was ₹1,600, find the amount lent at 6%.
(a) ₹1,500 (b) ₹1,800 (c) ₹2,500 (d) ₹2,000
Answer: (d) ₹2,000
Solution: Annual interest = 1600 ÷ 5 = ₹320. 0.06x + 0.04(7000 - x) = 320 => 280 + 0.02x = 320 => 0.02x = 40 => x = ₹2,000.
Source: RRB NTPC

67. A sum of ₹2,800 is divided into two parts such that the interest on the first part at 9% for 5 years is equal to the interest on the second part at 10% for 6 years. Find the first part.
(a) ₹1,600 (b) ₹1,200 (c) ₹1,400 (d) ₹1,800
Answer: (a) ₹1,600
Solution: x × 9 × 5 = (2800 - x) × 10 × 6 => 45x = 60(2800 - x) => 3x = 4(2800 - x) => 7x = 11200 => x = ₹1,600.
Source: SSC GD

68. A person borrows ₹1,200 at 8% per annum and ₹1,800 at 10% per annum simple interest for the same period. He paid a total interest of ₹1,380. How many years did he borrow the money for?
(a) 4 years (b) 5 years (c) 6 years (d) 7 years
Answer: (b) 5 years
Solution: Total interest per year = (1200 × 8)/100 + (1800 × 10)/100 = 96 + 180 = ₹276. Number of years = 1380 ÷ 276 = 5 years.
Source: SSC MTS

69. What annual installment will discharge a debt of ₹6,450 due in 4 years at 5% simple interest per annum?
(a) ₹1,400 (b) ₹1,450 (c) ₹1,500 (d) ₹1,600
Answer: (c) ₹1,500
Solution: Debt formula: Debt = 4x + [x × 5 × (3 + 2 + 1)] ÷ 100 = 4x + 0.3x = 4.3x. 4.3x = 6450 => x = 6450 ÷ 4.3 = ₹1,500.
Source: SSC CGL

70. At simple interest, ₹800 amounts to ₹920 in 3 years. If the rate of interest is increased by 3%, what will the amount become in the same time?
(a) ₹960 (b) ₹980 (c) ₹990 (d) ₹992
Answer: (d) ₹992
Solution: Additional interest = (800 × 3 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹72. New Amount = 920 + 72 = ₹992.
Source: SSC CHSL

71. A person borrowed ₹10 and agreed to return it in 11 monthly installments of ₹1 each. What is the rate of simple interest per annum?
(a) 21 9/11% (b) 10% (c) 15% (d) 18 2/11%
Answer: (a) 21 9/11%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) 10% wrongly treats ₹1 interest on ₹10 flat for 1 year! Monthly balances: 10, 9, 8... 1; sum = 55. Total interest = 11 - 10 = ₹1. Monthly rate r = 100/55 = 20/11%. Annual rate = 12 × (20/11) = 240/11% = 21 9/11%.
Source: RRB Group D

72. If ₹5,000 amounts to ₹5,700 in 1 year at simple interest, what will ₹7,000 amount to in 2 years at the same rate?
(a) ₹8,500 (b) ₹8,960 (c) ₹9,100 (d) ₹9,200
Answer: (b) ₹8,960
Solution: SI on 5000 in 1 year = ₹700 => Rate = (700 ÷ 5000) × 100 = 14%. SI on 7000 in 2 years = (7000 × 14 × 2) ÷ 100 = ₹1,960. Amount = 7000 + 1960 = ₹8,960.
Source: SSC CGL

73. A father left ₹1,30,000 to be divided between his two sons aged 14 and 16 years such that they receive equal amounts at the age of 18 years, at 10% simple interest per annum. How much did the younger son receive initially?
(a) ₹55,000 (b) ₹58,000 (c) ₹60,000 (d) ₹70,000
Answer: (c) ₹60,000
Solution: Younger son time = 18 - 14 = 4 years; Amount = P1 × [1 + (10 × 4)/100] = 1.4 P1. Elder son time = 2 years; Amount = 1.2 P2. 1.4 P1 = 1.2 P2 => P1 / P2 = 6 / 7. Younger son share = 130000 × (6/13) = ₹60,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

74. A sum of money amounts to ₹9,800 after 5 years and ₹12,005 after 8 years at the same rate of simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 10% (b) 11% (c) 15% (d) 12%
Answer: (d) 12%
Solution: SI for 3 years = 12005 - 9800 = ₹2,205 => SI for 1 year = ₹735. SI for 5 years = 735 × 5 = ₹3,675. Principal = 9800 - 3675 = ₹6,125. Rate = (735 ÷ 6125) × 100 = 12%.
Source: SSC CHSL

75. If the simple interest on a certain sum of money for 3 years at 12.5% per annum is ₹3,500 less than the principal, find the principal.
(a) ₹5,200 (b) ₹5,600 (c) ₹6,000 (d) ₹6,400
Answer: (b) ₹5,600
Solution: SI = (P × 12.5 × 3) ÷ 100 = 0.375P. P - 0.375P = 3500 => 0.625P = 3500 => P = 3500 ÷ 0.625 = ₹5,600.
Source: SSC MTS
