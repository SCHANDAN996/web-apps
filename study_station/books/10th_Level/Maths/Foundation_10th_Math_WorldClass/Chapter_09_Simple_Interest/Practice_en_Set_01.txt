Set 1: Simple Interest Multiple Choice Questions (Easy to Medium)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Find the simple interest on ₹4,000 at 5% per annum for 3 years.
(a) ₹600 (b) ₹550 (c) ₹650 (d) ₹700
Answer: (a) ₹600
Solution: Formula: SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100 = (4000 × 5 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹600.
Source: SSC CGL

2. What sum of money will earn a simple interest of ₹180 in 2 years at 6% per annum?
(a) ₹1,200 (b) ₹1,500 (c) ₹1,800 (d) ₹2,000
Answer: (b) ₹1,500
Solution: P = (SI × 100) ÷ (R × T) = (180 × 100) ÷ (6 × 2) = 18000 ÷ 12 = ₹1,500.
Source: SSC CHSL

3. At what rate percent per annum will ₹2,500 amount to ₹3,000 in 4 years at simple interest?
(a) 4% (b) 6% (c) 5% (d) 5.5%
Answer: (c) 5%
Solution: SI = Amount - Principal = 3000 - 2500 = ₹500. Rate = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × T) = (500 × 100) ÷ (2500 × 4) = 5%.
Source: RRB Group D

4. In how many years will a principal of ₹1,600 yield a simple interest of ₹480 at 7.5% per annum?
(a) 2 years (b) 3 years (c) 5 years (d) 4 years
Answer: (d) 4 years
Solution: T = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × R) = (480 × 100) ÷ (1600 × 7.5) = 48000 ÷ 12000 = 4 years.
Source: SSC MTS

5. Find the simple interest on ₹7,200 at 8% per annum for 2.5 years.
(a) ₹1,440 (b) ₹1,400 (c) ₹1,480 (d) ₹1,520
Answer: (a) ₹1,440
Solution: SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100 = (7200 × 8 × 2.5) ÷ 100 = 72 × 20 = ₹1,440.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

6. A person borrows ₹5,000 at 12% per annum simple interest. How much total amount will he pay back after 3 years?
(a) ₹6,500 (b) ₹6,800 (c) ₹7,000 (d) ₹7,200
Answer: (b) ₹6,800
Solution: SI = (5000 × 12 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹1,800. Amount = Principal + SI = 5000 + 1800 = ₹6,800.
Source: SSC GD

7. A sum of money lent at simple interest amounts to ₹880 in 2 years and to ₹920 in 3 years. What is the original sum?
(a) ₹750 (b) ₹780 (c) ₹800 (d) ₹820
Answer: (c) ₹800
Solution: SI for 1 year = 920 - 880 = ₹40. SI for 2 years = 40 × 2 = ₹80. Principal = Amount after 2 years - SI for 2 years = 880 - 80 = ₹800.
Source: SSC CGL

8. What is the simple interest on ₹6,000 at 10% per annum for 9 months?
(a) ₹5,400 (b) ₹600 (c) ₹500 (d) ₹450
Answer: (d) ₹450
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) ₹5,400 incorrectly multiplies by 9 years without converting months to years! Time T = 9/12 = 3/4 year. SI = (6000 × 10 × 3) ÷ (100 × 4) = ₹450.
Source: RRB NTPC

9. In how many years will a sum of money double itself at 10% per annum simple interest?
(a) 10 years (b) 8 years (c) 12 years (d) 15 years
Answer: (a) 10 years
Solution: Using the (n - 1) rule: T = [(n - 1) × 100] ÷ R = [(2 - 1) × 100] ÷ 10 = 10 years.
Source: SSC CHSL

10. At what rate percent per annum simple interest will a sum become triple of itself in 16 years?
(a) 10% (b) 12.5% (c) 15% (d) 16%
Answer: (b) 12.5%
Solution: Using (n - 1) rule: R = [(n - 1) × 100] ÷ T = [(3 - 1) × 100] ÷ 16 = 200 ÷ 16 = 12.5%.
Source: SSC CGL

11. The simple interest on a sum of money for 5 years is 2/5 of the principal. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 6% (b) 7% (c) 8% (d) 9%
Answer: (c) 8%
Solution: SI / P = 2/5. Rate = [(SI ÷ P) × 100] ÷ T = [(2/5) × 100] ÷ 5 = 40 ÷ 5 = 8%.
Source: SSC MTS

12. A sum of ₹3,000 is lent partly at 4% and partly at 6% simple interest per annum. If the total annual interest received is ₹150, how much money was lent at 4%?
(a) ₹1,200 (b) ₹1,400 (c) ₹1,600 (d) ₹1,500
Answer: (d) ₹1,500
Solution: Let the amount at 4% be x. Then 0.04x + 0.06(3000 - x) = 150 => 180 - 0.02x = 150 => 0.02x = 30 => x = ₹1,500.
Source: RRB Group D

13. Find the simple interest on ₹10,000 from 1st January 2023 to 15th March 2023 at 7.3% per annum.
(a) ₹146 (b) ₹150 (c) ₹140 (d) ₹160
Answer: (a) ₹146
Solution: Number of days = 31 (Jan) + 28 (Feb) + 14 (Mar) = 73 days. T = 73/365 = 1/5 year. SI = (10000 × 7.3 × 1) ÷ (100 × 5) = 730 ÷ 5 = ₹146.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

14. If the simple interest for 3 years at 4% per annum is ₹240 less than the simple interest for 5 years at the same rate on the same sum, find the sum.
(a) ₹2,500 (b) ₹3,000 (c) ₹3,200 (d) ₹3,500
Answer: (b) ₹3,000
Solution: Time difference = 5 - 3 = 2 years. Difference in SI = (P × 4 × 2) ÷ 100 = 8P / 100 = ₹240 => P = (240 × 100) ÷ 8 = ₹3,000.
Source: SSC CGL

15. If ₹1,200 amounts to ₹1,440 in 4 years at simple interest, what will it amount to in 4 years if the rate of interest is increased by 3%?
(a) ₹1,520 (b) ₹1,560 (c) ₹1,584 (d) ₹1,600
Answer: (c) ₹1,584
Solution: Additional interest generated = (1200 × 3 × 4) ÷ 100 = ₹144. New Amount = 1440 + 144 = ₹1,584.
Source: SSC CHSL

16. A sum of money at simple interest doubles in 7 years. In how many years will it become 4 times of itself?
(a) 14 years (b) 18 years (c) 20 years (d) 21 years
Answer: (d) 21 years
Solution: Formula: (n1 - 1) ÷ t1 = (n2 - 1) ÷ t2 => (2 - 1) ÷ 7 = (4 - 1) ÷ t2 => 1/7 = 3/t2 => t2 = 21 years.
Source: RRB NTPC

17. Find the simple interest on ₹8,000 at 6.25% per annum for 2 years.
(a) ₹1,000 (b) ₹950 (c) ₹1,050 (d) ₹1,100
Answer: (a) ₹1,000
Solution: Since 6.25% = 1/16, SI = 8000 × (1/16) × 2 = 8000 ÷ 8 = ₹1,000.
Source: SSC GD

18. A person invested ₹2,000 at 5% simple interest per annum. If he received an amount of ₹2,600, find the time period in years.
(a) 5 years (b) 6 years (c) 7 years (d) 8 years
Answer: (b) 6 years
Solution: SI = 2600 - 2000 = ₹600. Time = (600 × 100) ÷ (2000 × 5) = 60000 ÷ 10000 = 6 years.
Source: SSC MTS

19. At what rate of simple interest per annum will the interest on ₹6,400 for 3 years be ₹1,152?
(a) 5% (b) 5.5% (c) 6% (d) 6.5%
Answer: (c) 6%
Solution: Rate = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × T) = (1152 × 100) ÷ (6400 × 3) = 1152 ÷ 192 = 6%.
Source: SSC CGL

20. The simple interest on a sum of money is 9/16 of the principal. If the numerical values of the rate percent and time in years are equal, what is the rate of interest?
(a) 6% (b) 6.5% (c) 7% (d) 7.5%
Answer: (d) 7.5%
Solution: Let R = T. Then R² = (SI ÷ P) × 100 = (9/16) × 100 = 900 ÷ 16 = 225 ÷ 4 => R = 15/2 = 7.5%.
Source: SSC CHSL

21. An investor deposited ₹5,000 at 8% per annum simple interest for 4 years. What is the total amount he receives at maturity?
(a) ₹6,600 (b) ₹1,600 (c) ₹6,400 (d) ₹6,800
Answer: (a) ₹6,600
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) ₹1,600 is only the interest, not the total amount! SI = (5000 × 8 × 4) ÷ 100 = ₹1,600. Total Amount = Principal + SI = 5000 + 1600 = ₹6,600.
Source: RRB Group D

22. A certain sum amounts to ₹756 in 2 years and to ₹873 in 3.5 years at simple interest. Find the annual rate of interest.
(a) 12% (b) 13% (c) 14% (d) 15%
Answer: (b) 13%
Solution: Interest for 1.5 years = 873 - 756 = ₹117 => Interest for 1 year = 117 ÷ 1.5 = ₹78. Interest for 2 years = ₹156. Principal = 756 - 156 = ₹600. Rate = (78 ÷ 600) × 100 = 13%.
Source: SSC CGL

23. A sum was put at simple interest at a certain rate for 3 years. Had it been put at 2% higher rate, it would have fetched ₹360 more. Find the sum.
(a) ₹5,000 (b) ₹5,500 (c) ₹6,000 (d) ₹6,500
Answer: (c) ₹6,000
Solution: Additional interest = (P × 2 × 3) ÷ 100 = 6P / 100 = ₹360 => P = (360 × 100) ÷ 6 = ₹6,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

24. A person lends ₹10,000 in two parts: one at 8% per annum and the other at 10% per annum simple interest. If the total annual interest is ₹920, how much was lent at 10%?
(a) ₹4,000 (b) ₹4,500 (c) ₹5,000 (d) ₹6,000
Answer: (d) ₹6,000
Solution: Let amount at 10% be y. 0.08(10000 - y) + 0.10y = 920 => 800 + 0.02y = 920 => 0.02y = 120 => y = ₹6,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

25. In how many years will a sum of money become 5 times itself at 16% per annum simple interest?
(a) 20 years (b) 25 years (c) 30 years (d) 24 years
Answer: (b) 25 years
Solution: Using (n - 1) rule: T = [(n - 1) × 100] ÷ R = [(5 - 1) × 100] ÷ 16 = 400 ÷ 16 = 25 years.
Source: SSC MTS
