Set 5: Simple Interest Multiple Choice Questions (Advanced Level)
Questions 101–125: Advanced Level

101. A sum of ₹1,875 is lent into two parts, one at 4% and the other at 6% simple interest per annum. If the annual income from both parts is equal, find the sum lent at 4%.
(a) ₹1,125 (b) ₹750 (c) ₹1,050 (d) ₹1,200
Answer: (a) ₹1,125
Solution: 4x = 6y => x : y = 3 : 2. Sum lent at 4% = 1875 × (3/5) = ₹1,125.
Source: SSC CGL

102. A person borrows ₹5,000 for 2 years at 4% per annum simple interest. He immediately lends it to another person at 6 1/4% per annum simple interest for 2 years. Find his total gain in the transaction.
(a) ₹200 (b) ₹225 (c) ₹250 (d) ₹275
Answer: (b) ₹225
Solution: Gain rate per year = 6.25% - 4% = 2.25%. Total gain for 2 years = (5000 × 2.25 × 2) ÷ 100 = ₹225.
Source: SSC CHSL

103. If ₹12,000 is divided into two parts such that simple interest on the first part for 3 years at 12% per annum is equal to simple interest on the second part for 4.5 years at 16% per annum, find the greater part.
(a) ₹6,000 (b) ₹7,200 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹9,000
Answer: (c) ₹8,000
Solution: P1 × 12 × 3 = P2 × 16 × 4.5 => 36 P1 = 72 P2 => P1 = 2 P2 => P1 : P2 = 2 : 1. Greater part P1 = 12000 × (2/3) = ₹8,000.
Source: RRB NTPC

104. At simple interest, a certain sum amounts to ₹1,012 in 2.5 years and to ₹1,067.20 in 4 years. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 3% (b) 3.5% (c) 5% (d) 4%
Answer: (d) 4%
Solution: SI for 1.5 years = 1067.20 - 1012 = ₹55.20 => 1 year SI = ₹36.80. SI for 2.5 years = 36.80 × 2.5 = ₹92. Principal = 1012 - 92 = ₹920. Rate = (36.80 ÷ 920) × 100 = 4%.
Source: SSC MTS

105. If simple interest on ₹1,400 for 3 years is more than the simple interest on ₹1,200 for the same period by ₹60, find the rate of interest per annum.
(a) 10% (b) 8% (c) 12% (d) 9%
Answer: (a) 10%
Solution: Difference in SI = [(1400 - 1200) × R × 3] ÷ 100 = 60 => (200 × 3R) ÷ 100 = 60 => 6R = 60 => R = 10%.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

106. A father left ₹1,30,000 to be divided between his two sons aged 14 and 16 years such that they get equal amounts when they attain the age of 18 years, at 10% per annum simple interest. Find the share of the younger son.
(a) ₹55,000 (b) ₹60,000 (c) ₹65,000 (d) ₹70,000
Answer: (b) ₹60,000
Solution: Younger son time = 18 - 14 = 4 years; Elder son time = 18 - 16 = 2 years. Amounts equal: Y × [1 + (10×4)/100] = E × [1 + (10×2)/100] => 1.4Y = 1.2E => Y/E = 6/7. Younger share = 130000 × (6/13) = ₹60,000.
Source: SSC GD

107. Simple interest on a sum of money is 9/16 of the principal. If the number of years is equal to the rate percent per annum, find the time in years.
(a) 5.5 years (b) 6 years (c) 7.5 years (d) 8 years
Answer: (c) 7.5 years
Solution: T = R => T² = (SI ÷ P) × 100 = (9/16) × 100 = 900 ÷ 16 = 225 ÷ 4 => T = 15/2 = 7.5 years.
Source: SSC CGL

108. What annual installment will discharge a debt of ₹6,450 due in 4 years at 5% simple interest per annum?
(a) ₹1,612.50 (b) ₹1,400 (c) ₹1,450 (d) ₹1,500
Answer: (d) ₹1,500
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) incorrectly divides ₹6,450 directly by 4 without considering interest savings! Installment formula: Debt = 4x + [x × 5 × (3 + 2 + 1)] ÷ 100 = 4x + 0.30x = 4.30x. 4.30x = 6450 => x = 6450 ÷ 4.30 = ₹1,500.
Source: SSC CHSL

109. If the annual simple interest rate increases by 2.5%, the annual interest on a certain sum increases by ₹250. What is the sum?
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹8,000 (c) ₹12,000 (d) ₹12,500
Answer: (a) ₹10,000
Solution: 2.5% of P = 250 => P = (250 × 100) ÷ 2.5 = ₹10,000.
Source: RRB Group D

110. A person borrows ₹8,000 at 5% per annum simple interest and immediately lends it to someone else at 7% per annum simple interest for 3 years. What is his total gain from this transaction?
(a) ₹400 (b) ₹480 (c) ₹560 (d) ₹600
Answer: (b) ₹480
Solution: Net gain rate = 7% - 5% = 2% per year. Total gain for 3 years = (8000 × 2 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹480.
Source: SSC CGL

111. A certain sum amounts to ₹7,560 in 4 years and to ₹8,730 in 7 years at simple interest. Find the sum and the rate percent per annum.
(a) ₹5,500, 6% (b) ₹5,800, 7% (c) ₹6,000, 6.5% (d) ₹6,200, 6.5%
Answer: (c) ₹6,000, 6.5%
Solution: SI for 3 years = 8730 - 7560 = ₹1,170 => 1 year SI = ₹390. SI for 4 years = 390 × 4 = ₹1,560. Principal = 7560 - 1560 = ₹6,000. Rate = (390 ÷ 6000) × 100 = 6.5%.
Source: SSC MTS

112. In how many years will a sum of money become 5 times of itself at 16% per annum simple interest?
(a) 15 years (b) 20 years (c) 22 years (d) 25 years
Answer: (d) 25 years
Solution: Time = [(N - 1) × 100] ÷ R = [(5 - 1) × 100] ÷ 16 = 400 ÷ 16 = 25 years.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

113. A sum was put at simple interest at a certain rate for 2 years. Had it been put at 3% higher rate, it would have fetched ₹300 more. Find the sum.
(a) ₹5,000 (b) ₹4,500 (c) ₹5,500 (d) ₹6,000
Answer: (a) ₹5,000
Solution: Increase in interest = (P × 3 × 2) ÷ 100 = 6% of P. 6% of P = 300 => P = (300 × 100) ÷ 6 = ₹5,000.
Source: RRB NTPC

114. Two equal sums are invested for 2 years and 3 years at 8% per annum simple interest respectively. If the difference in their simple interests is ₹96, find each sum.
(a) ₹1,000 (b) ₹1,200 (c) ₹1,400 (d) ₹1,500
Answer: (b) ₹1,200
Solution: Difference in time = 1 year. SI difference = (P × 8 × 1) ÷ 100 = 8% of P = 96 => P = (96 × 100) ÷ 8 = ₹1,200.
Source: SSC CHSL

115. A lent ₹5,000 to B for 2 years and ₹3,000 to C for 4 years on simple interest at the same rate of interest and received ₹2,200 in all from both as interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 8% (b) 9% (c) 10% (d) 12%
Answer: (c) 10%
Solution: (5000 × R × 2) ÷ 100 + (3000 × R × 4) ÷ 100 = 2200 => 100R + 120R = 220R = 2200 => R = 10%.
Source: SSC CGL

116. The simple interest on a sum of money is ₹240 less than the principal when lent at 5% per annum for 8 years. What is the sum?
(a) ₹300 (b) ₹350 (c) ₹360 (d) ₹400
Answer: (d) ₹400
Solution: SI = (P × 5 × 8) ÷ 100 = 0.40P. P - 0.40P = 0.60P = 240 => P = 240 ÷ 0.60 = ₹400.
Source: SSC GD

117. A sum of ₹4,000 amounts to ₹5,000 in 5 years at simple interest. If the rate of interest is increased by 2% per annum, what will the new amount be in the same period?
(a) ₹5,400 (b) ₹5,100 (c) ₹5,200 (d) ₹5,500
Answer: (a) ₹5,400
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) mistakenly increases total amount by 2% (₹5,100)! Additional interest = (Principal × Increase × Time) ÷ 100 = (4000 × 2 × 5) ÷ 100 = ₹400. New Amount = 5000 + 400 = ₹5,400.
Source: SSC MTS

118. What sum of money will produce ₹143 interest in 3 years and 3 months at 2 1/2% simple interest per annum?
(a) ₹1,650 (b) ₹1,760 (c) ₹1,800 (d) ₹1,850
Answer: (b) ₹1,760
Solution: Time = 3 + 3/12 = 13/4 years. Rate = 5/2%. SI = [P × (5/2) × (13/4)] ÷ 100 = 65P ÷ 800 = 143 => P = (143 × 800) ÷ 65 = 11 × 160 = ₹1,760.
Source: RRB Group D

119. If simple interest on a certain sum for 15 months at 7 1/2% per annum exceeds the simple interest on the same sum for 8 months at 12 1/2% per annum by ₹32.50, find the sum.
(a) ₹2,800 (b) ₹3,000 (c) ₹3,120 (d) ₹3,250
Answer: (c) ₹3,120
Solution: First effective rate = (15/12) × (15/2)% = 75/8%. Second effective rate = (8/12) × (25/2)% = 25/3%. Difference = 75/8% - 25/3% = 25/24% = 25/2400. (25/2400)P = 32.50 => P = (32.50 × 2400) ÷ 25 = ₹3,120.
Source: SSC CGL

120. A sum was invested at simple interest at x% per annum for 3 years. Had it been invested at (x + 3)% per annum, it would have fetched ₹216 more. Find the sum.
(a) ₹2,000 (b) ₹2,100 (c) ₹2,250 (d) ₹2,400
Answer: (d) ₹2,400
Solution: Increase in interest = (P × 3 × 3) ÷ 100 = 9% of P. 9% of P = 216 => P = (216 × 100) ÷ 9 = ₹2,400.
Source: SSC CHSL

121. If the ratio of principal and simple interest at the end of 5 years is 10 : 3, what is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 6% (b) 5% (c) 7% (d) 8%
Answer: (a) 6%
Solution: SI ÷ P = 3 ÷ 10. Rate = (SI × 100) ÷ (P × T) = (3 × 100) ÷ (10 × 5) = 300 ÷ 50 = 6%.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

122. A person borrows ₹10,000 from two lenders. For one loan, he pays 6% per annum and for the other, he pays 8% per annum. After 1 year, he pays a total interest of ₹680. How much did he borrow at 6%?
(a) ₹5,500 (b) ₹6,000 (c) ₹6,500 (d) ₹4,000
Answer: (b) ₹6,000
Solution: Overall interest rate = (680 ÷ 10000) × 100 = 6.8%. By alligation: Ratio = (8 - 6.8) : (6.8 - 6) = 1.2 : 0.8 = 3 : 2. Sum at 6% = 10000 × (3/5) = ₹6,000.
Source: RRB NTPC

123. If ₹500 amounts to ₹580 in 2 years at simple interest, what will ₹650 amount to in 3 years at the same rate of simple interest per annum?
(a) ₹780 (b) ₹796 (c) ₹806 (d) ₹820
Answer: (c) ₹806
Solution: SI on 500 for 2 years = ₹80 => Rate = (80 × 100) ÷ (500 × 2) = 8%. SI on 650 for 3 years at 8% = (650 × 8 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹156. Amount = 650 + 156 = ₹806.
Source: SSC CGL

124. At simple interest, a sum becomes 4 times of itself in 12 years. In how many years will it become 8 times of itself at the same rate?
(a) 21 years (b) 24 years (c) 26 years (d) 28 years
Answer: (d) 28 years
Solution: (n1 - 1)/t1 = (n2 - 1)/t2 => (4 - 1)/12 = (8 - 1)/T => 3/12 = 7/T => 1/4 = 7/T => T = 28 years.
Source: SSC MTS

125. A person lent a certain sum of money at 4% simple interest per annum and in 8 years the interest amounted to ₹340 less than the sum lent. Find the sum lent.
(a) ₹450 (b) ₹500 (c) ₹550 (d) ₹600
Answer: (b) ₹500
Solution: SI = (P × 4 × 8) ÷ 100 = 0.32P. P - 0.32P = 0.68P = 340 => P = 340 ÷ 0.68 = ₹500.
Source: SSC CHSL
