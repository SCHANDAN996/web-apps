**26. A sum of money at simple interest amounts to ₹815 in 3 years and to ₹854 in 4 years. The sum is:**
(A) ₹650 (B) ₹690 (C) ₹698 (D) ₹700
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹698**
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
SI for 1 year = 854 – 815 = ₹39.
SI for 3 years = 39 × 3 = ₹117.
Principal (Sum) = Amount after 3 years – SI = 815 – 117 = ₹698.
**Source:** SSC CGL

**27. The simple interest on a certain sum for 8 months at 5% per annum is ₹60. Find the sum.**
(A) ₹1200 (B) ₹1500 (C) ₹1800 (D) ₹2000
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹1800**
**Solution:**
Time = 8 months = 8/12 = 2/3 year.
SI = (P × 5 × 2/3) / 100 = (10P/3) / 100 = P/30.
Given P/30 = 60 ⇒ P = 60 × 30 = ₹1800.
**Source:** Railway Group D

**28. A sum of money doubles itself in 6 years at simple interest. The rate of interest per annum is:**
(A) 12% (B) 16.67% (C) 10% (D) 8%
**Correct Answer: (B) 16.67%**
**Solution:**
Sum doubles ⇒ n = 2, so SI = (2–1)P = P.
Using SI = (P × R × T)/100, we have P = (P × R × 6)/100 ⇒ R = 100/6 = 16.67%.
**Source:** SSC CHSL

**29. A sum of money becomes 4 times in 12 years at simple interest. Find the rate.**
(A) 20% (B) 25% (C) 30% (D) 15%
**Correct Answer: (B) 25%**
**Solution:**
n = 4 ⇒ SI = (4–1)P = 3P.
3P = (P × R × 12)/100 ⇒ R = (3 × 100)/12 = 25%.
**Source:** IBPS Clerk
