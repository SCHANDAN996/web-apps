Set 2: Simple Interest Multiple Choice Questions (Easy to Medium)
Questions 26–45: Easy | Questions 46–50: Medium

26. At what rate percent per annum will a sum of money double itself in 5 years at simple interest?
(a) 20% (b) 15% (c) 25% (d) 10%
Answer: (a) 20%
Solution: Using (n - 1) rule: Rate = [(2 - 1) × 100] ÷ 5 = 100 ÷ 5 = 20%.
Source: SSC CGL

27. A sum of ₹1,500 is lent at 10% per annum for 3 years. Find the simple interest.
(a) ₹400 (b) ₹450 (c) ₹500 (d) ₹550
Answer: (b) ₹450
Solution: SI = (1500 × 10 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹450.
Source: SSC CHSL

28. A sum of money becomes 8/5 of itself in 5 years at a certain rate of simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 10% (b) 11% (c) 12% (d) 14%
Answer: (c) 12%
Solution: Amount = 8/5 P => SI = (8/5 - 1)P = 3/5 P. Rate = [(3/5) × 100] ÷ 5 = 60 ÷ 5 = 12%.
Source: RRB Group D

29. If the simple interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 4% per annum is ₹80, what is the principal sum?
(a) ₹800 (b) ₹900 (c) ₹1,200 (d) ₹1,000
Answer: (d) ₹1,000
Solution: P = (SI × 100) ÷ (R × T) = (80 × 100) ÷ (4 × 2) = 8000 ÷ 8 = ₹1,000.
Source: SSC MTS

30. The simple interest on ₹5,000 for 3 years at 6% per annum is:
(a) ₹900 (b) ₹850 (c) ₹950 (d) ₹1,000
Answer: (a) ₹900
Solution: SI = (5000 × 6 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹900.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

31. In how many years will ₹3,000 yield ₹1,080 as simple interest at 12% per annum?
(a) 2.5 years (b) 3 years (c) 3.5 years (d) 4 years
Answer: (b) 3 years
Solution: Time = (1080 × 100) ÷ (3000 × 12) = 1080 ÷ 360 = 3 years.
Source: SSC GD

32. If a sum amounts to ₹2,160 in 2 years and to ₹2,400 in 4 years at simple interest, what is the principal?
(a) ₹1,800 (b) ₹1,850 (c) ₹1,920 (d) ₹1,950
Answer: (c) ₹1,920
Solution: SI for 2 years = 2400 - 2160 = ₹240. Principal = Amount after 2 years - SI for 2 years = 2160 - 240 = ₹1,920.
Source: SSC CGL

33. At what rate percent per annum will the simple interest on a sum of money be 1/9 of the principal, and the number of years be equal to the rate percent per annum?
(a) 3% (b) 4% (c) 5% (d) 3 1/3%
Answer: (d) 3 1/3%
Solution: Given R = T. Then R² = (SI ÷ P) × 100 = (1/9) × 100 = 100/9 => R = 10/3% = 3 1/3%.
Source: RRB NTPC

34. A sum of money becomes 3 times itself in 10 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 20% (b) 30% (c) 25% (d) 15%
Answer: (a) 20%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) 30% incorrectly assumes SI = 3P instead of SI = (3 - 1)P = 2P! Rate = [(3 - 1) × 100] ÷ 10 = 200 ÷ 10 = 20%.
Source: SSC CHSL

35. A man borrowed ₹8,000 from two moneylenders. For one loan, he paid 6% per annum and for the other 8% per annum simple interest. At the end of 1 year, he paid ₹560 as total interest. How much did he borrow at 6%?
(a) ₹3,500 (b) ₹4,000 (c) ₹4,500 (d) ₹5,000
Answer: (b) ₹4,000
Solution: Let the amount at 6% be x. Then 0.06x + 0.08(8000 - x) = 560 => 640 - 0.02x = 560 => 0.02x = 80 => x = ₹4,000.
Source: SSC CGL

36. The simple interest on a sum at 5% per annum for 3 years and 4 years differs by ₹42. What is the sum?
(a) ₹800 (b) ₹820 (c) ₹840 (d) ₹860
Answer: (c) ₹840
Solution: Difference of 1 year interest = (P × 5 × 1) ÷ 100 = 0.05P = 42 => P = 42 ÷ 0.05 = ₹840.
Source: SSC MTS

37. A sum of money at simple interest amounts to ₹1,012 in 2.5 years and to ₹1,067.20 in 4 years. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 3% (b) 3.5% (c) 5% (d) 4%
Answer: (d) 4%
Solution: SI for 1.5 years = 1067.20 - 1012 = ₹55.20 => SI for 1 year = ₹36.80. SI for 2.5 years = 36.80 × 2.5 = ₹92. Principal = 1012 - 92 = ₹920. Rate = (36.80 ÷ 920) × 100 = 4%.
Source: RRB Group D

38. If the annual rate of simple interest increases from 10% to 12.5%, a person's yearly interest income increases by ₹1,250. What is his principal investment?
(a) ₹50,000 (b) ₹45,000 (c) ₹55,000 (d) ₹60,000
Answer: (a) ₹50,000
Solution: Rate difference = 12.5% - 10% = 2.5%. 2.5% of P = ₹1,250 => P = (1250 × 100) ÷ 2.5 = ₹50,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

39. A person invests ₹5,000 for 2 years at 4% per annum simple interest. What is the total amount received at maturity?
(a) ₹5,200 (b) ₹5,400 (c) ₹5,600 (d) ₹5,800
Answer: (b) ₹5,400
Solution: SI = (5000 × 4 × 2) ÷ 100 = ₹400. Total Amount = 5000 + 400 = ₹5,400.
Source: SSC CGL

40. A sum of money doubles itself in 12 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 8% (b) 8.5% (c) 8 1/3% (d) 9%
Answer: (c) 8 1/3%
Solution: Rate = [(2 - 1) × 100] ÷ 12 = 100 ÷ 12 = 25/3% = 8 1/3%.
Source: SSC CHSL

41. In how many years will the simple interest on ₹900 at 6% per annum be equal to the simple interest on ₹540 at 5% per annum for 4 years?
(a) 3 years (b) 4 years (c) 1.5 years (d) 2 years
Answer: (d) 2 years
Solution: SI from second loan = (540 × 5 × 4) ÷ 100 = ₹108. (900 × 6 × T) ÷ 100 = 54T = 108 => T = 2 years.
Source: RRB NTPC

42. What principal will amount to ₹1,210 in 3 years at 7% per annum simple interest?
(a) ₹1,000 (b) ₹950 (c) ₹1,050 (d) ₹1,100
Answer: (a) ₹1,000
Solution: Amount = P × [1 + (7 × 3)/100] = 1.21P = 1210 => P = 1210 ÷ 1.21 = ₹1,000.
Source: SSC GD

43. Find the simple interest on ₹2,500 at 5% per annum for 219 days.
(a) ₹150 (b) ₹75 (c) ₹85 (d) ₹95
Answer: (b) ₹75
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) incorrectly handles days conversion! Time T = 219/365 = 3/5 year. SI = (2500 × 5 × 3) ÷ (100 × 5) = ₹75.
Source: SSC MTS

44. A sum of money becomes 7/6 of itself in 3 years at a certain rate of simple interest. The rate of interest per annum is:
(a) 5% (b) 6% (c) 5 5/9% (d) 6 1/4%
Answer: (c) 5 5/9%
Solution: SI = (7/6 - 1)P = 1/6 P. Rate = [(1/6) × 100] ÷ 3 = 100 ÷ 18 = 50/9% = 5 5/9%.
Source: SSC CGL

45. If simple interest on ₹600 for 4 years and on ₹600 for 2 years together is ₹180, what is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 4% (b) 4.5% (c) 6% (d) 5%
Answer: (d) 5%
Solution: Total SI = (600 × R × 4)/100 + (600 × R × 2)/100 = 24R + 12R = 36R = 180 => R = 5%.
Source: SSC CHSL

46. A sum of ₹12,000 is divided into two parts such that the simple interest on the first part for 3 years at 12% per annum is equal to that on the second part for 4.5 years at 16% per annum. Find the first part.
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹7,000 (c) ₹6,000 (d) ₹9,000
Answer: (a) ₹8,000
Solution: x × 3 × 12 = (12000 - x) × 4.5 × 16 => 36x = 72(12000 - x) => x = 2(12000 - x) => 3x = 24000 => x = ₹8,000.
Source: RRB Group D

47. A person invested equal sums of money at 4% and 5% simple interest per annum. At the end of 4 years, the total interest earned was ₹720. Find the total sum invested.
(a) ₹3,600 (b) ₹4,000 (c) ₹4,500 (d) ₹5,000
Answer: (b) ₹4,000
Solution: Total SI = (P × 4 × 4)/100 + (P × 5 × 4)/100 = 36P / 100 = 720 => P = ₹2,000. Total sum invested = 2P = ₹4,000.
Source: SSC CGL

48. A certain sum amounts to ₹2,240 in 2 years and to ₹2,600 in 5 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 5% (b) 5.5% (c) 6% (d) 6.5%
Answer: (c) 6%
Solution: SI for 3 years = 2600 - 2240 = ₹360 => SI for 1 year = ₹120. SI for 2 years = ₹240. Principal = 2240 - 240 = ₹2,000. Rate = (120 ÷ 2000) × 100 = 6%.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

49. At simple interest, a sum becomes 4 times of itself in 15 years. In how many years will it become 7 times of itself?
(a) 24 years (b) 25 years (c) 28 years (d) 30 years
Answer: (d) 30 years
Solution: (n1 - 1) ÷ t1 = (n2 - 1) ÷ t2 => (4 - 1) ÷ 15 = (7 - 1) ÷ t2 => 3/15 = 6/t2 => 1/5 = 6/t2 => t2 = 30 years.
Source: SSC CHSL

50. Out of a sum of ₹8,500, a part was lent at 6% per annum and the rest at 10% per annum simple interest. If the total interest received in 4 years was ₹2,400, what was the sum lent at 10%?
(a) ₹2,000 (b) ₹2,250 (c) ₹2,500 (d) ₹2,750
Answer: (b) ₹2,250
Solution: Annual interest = 2400 ÷ 4 = ₹600. Let amount at 10% be y. 0.06(8500 - y) + 0.10y = 600 => 510 + 0.04y = 600 => 0.04y = 90 => y = ₹2,250.
Source: SSC MTS
