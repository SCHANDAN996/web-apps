1. In a triangle, the sum of all interior angles is:
(a) 90° (b) 180° (c) 270° (d) 360°
Answer: (b)
Solution: By the Angle Sum Property, the three interior angles of every triangle add up to 180°. The value 360° is tempting because it is the sum of angles around a point, not inside a triangle, so the answer is 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

2. A right-angled triangle has legs of 3 cm and 4 cm. Its hypotenuse is:
(a) 4 cm (b) 6 cm (c) 5 cm (d) 7 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: By Pythagoras theorem, hypotenuse = √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 cm. Adding the legs gives 7 cm, which is a common slip, so the answer is 5 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

3. Which triangle has all three sides equal?
(a) Equilateral (b) Scalene (c) Right-angled (d) Isosceles
Answer: (a)
Solution: An equilateral triangle has all three sides equal and each angle 60°. Isosceles has only two equal sides, which is the tempting confusion, so the answer is equilateral.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

4. When two lines intersect, vertically opposite angles are:
(a) Complementary (b) Equal (c) Supplementary (d) Always 90°
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vertically opposite angles are always equal in measure. They are supplementary only in the special case of perpendicular lines, so the answer is equal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

5. A line that intersects two or more lines at distinct points is called a:
(a) Parallel line (b) Perpendicular line (c) Ray (d) Transversal
Answer: (d)
Solution: A transversal is a line that cuts two or more lines at different points, forming pairs of angles. A perpendicular line only meets one line at 90°, so the answer is transversal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

6. In a circle, the distance from the centre to any point on the circle is called its:
(a) Radius (b) Diameter (c) Arc (d) Chord
Answer: (a)
Solution: The radius joins the centre to any point on the circle and is half the diameter. A chord joins two points on the circle without passing through the centre in general, so the answer is radius.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

7. A quadrilateral with all sides equal and each interior angle 90° is a:
(a) Rhombus (b) Rectangle (c) Parallelogram (d) Square
Answer: (d)
Solution: A square has both equal sides and right angles. A rhombus has equal sides but its angles need not be 90°, which is the tempting trap, so the answer is square.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

8. The area of a rectangle with length 10 cm and breadth 5 cm is:
(a) 15 cm² (b) 30 cm² (c) 50 cm² (d) 25 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: Area = length × breadth = 10 × 5 = 50 cm². The value 30 cm is the perimeter, a common mix-up, so the answer is 50 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

9. Which of the following is a Pythagorean triple?
(a) 2, 3, 4 (b) 5, 6, 7 (c) 4, 5, 6 (d) 3, 4, 5
Answer: (d)
Solution: 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5², so these sides form a right-angled triangle. The other sets fail a² + b² = c², so the answer is 3, 4, 5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

10. A triangle with one angle equal to 90° is called a:
(a) Acute-angled triangle (b) Right-angled triangle (c) Obtuse-angled triangle (d) Equilateral triangle
Answer: (b)
Solution: By definition, a triangle containing a 90° angle is right-angled, and the side opposite it is the hypotenuse. An obtuse triangle has an angle greater than 90°, so the answer is right-angled triangle.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

11. The sum of exterior angles of any convex polygon, taken one at each vertex, is:
(a) 180° (b) 360° (c) 540° (d) Depends on the number of sides
Answer: (b)
Solution: For any convex polygon, the exterior angles (one per vertex) always sum to 360°, whatever the number of sides. The interior angle sum does depend on sides, which causes the confusion, so the answer is 360°.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

12. In a parallelogram, opposite angles are always:
(a) Supplementary (b) Complementary (c) 90° (d) Equal
Answer: (d)
Solution: In a parallelogram, opposite angles are equal, while adjacent angles are supplementary. The supplementary property applies to adjacent angles, not opposite ones, so the answer is equal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

13. A quadrilateral has how many diagonals?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: Number of diagonals = n(n − 3)/2 = 4 × 1/2 = 2. Counting the four sides as diagonals is the tempting error, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

14. In an equilateral triangle, each interior angle measures:
(a) 45° (b) 90° (c) 60° (d) 120°
Answer: (c)
Solution: All three angles are equal and sum to 180°, so each angle = 180°/3 = 60°. Dividing 180° by 2 gives 90°, a common slip, so the answer is 60°.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

15. A transversal cuts two parallel lines. The sum of interior angles on the same side of the transversal is:
(a) 180° (b) 90° (c) 270° (d) 360°
Answer: (a)
Solution: Co-interior angles on the same side of a transversal are supplementary, i.e. they sum to 180°. They are not equal (that holds for alternate angles), so the answer is 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

16. The perimeter of a square whose side is 8 cm is:
(a) 16 cm (b) 24 cm (c) 64 cm (d) 32 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: Perimeter of a square = 4 × side = 4 × 8 = 32 cm. The value 64 cm² is the area, a frequent confusion, so the answer is 32 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

17. The area of a circle with radius 7 cm (use π = 22/7) is:
(a) 44 cm² (b) 308 cm² (c) 154 cm² (d) 616 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 154 cm². The value 44 cm is the circumference 2πr, the tempting trap, so the answer is 154 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

18. The volume of a cube with side 'a' is given by:
(a) a³ (b) 6a² (c) a² (d) 4a³
Answer: (a)
Solution: Volume of a cube = side × side × side = a³. The expression 6a² is the total surface area, not the volume, so the answer is a³.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

19. Which of the following is NOT a classification of triangles based on angles?
(a) Scalene (b) Right-angled (c) Obtuse-angled (d) Acute-angled
Answer: (a)
Solution: Scalene classifies a triangle by its sides (no equal sides), not by angles. The other three are angle-based types, so the answer is scalene.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

20. A straight angle measures exactly:
(a) 180° (b) 90° (c) 270° (d) 360°
Answer: (a)
Solution: A straight angle forms a straight line and measures 180°. The value 90° is a right angle, only half of a straight angle, so the answer is 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

21. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. The smallest angle is:
(a) 20° (b) 40° (c) 30° (d) 50°
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let the angles be 2x, 3x, 4x; then 9x = 180°, so x = 20° and the smallest angle is 2x = 40°. Stopping at x = 20° is the tempting error, so the answer is 40°.
Source: PYQ-style

22. A chord of a circle of radius 10 cm is at a distance of 6 cm from the centre. The length of the chord is:
(a) 8 cm (b) 12 cm (c) 16 cm (d) 18 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord, so half the chord = √(10² − 6²) = √64 = 8 cm, and the full chord = 16 cm. Reporting the half-length 8 cm is the trap, so the answer is 16 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

23. The perimeter of a rectangle is 30 cm. If its length is 8 cm, then its area is:
(a) 56 cm² (b) 48 cm² (c) 60 cm² (d) 64 cm²
Answer: (a)
Solution: 2(l + b) = 30 gives l + b = 15, so b = 7 cm and area = 8 × 7 = 56 cm². Taking b = 30 − 2 × 8 = 14 cm without halving leads to a wrong area, so the answer is 56 cm².
Source: PYQ-style

24. The diagonal of a square of side 10 cm is:
(a) 15 cm (b) 20 cm (c) 10√3 cm (d) 10√2 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: Diagonal of a square = side × √2 = 10√2 cm, by Pythagoras on two adjacent sides. Doubling the side to get 20 cm is the tempting mistake, so the answer is 10√2 cm.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A ladder 13 m long leans against a vertical wall. The foot of the ladder is 5 m away from the wall. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?
(a) 11 m (b) 13 m (c) 12 m (d) 14 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: By Pythagoras theorem, height = √(13² − 5²) = √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12 m. Subtracting directly (13 − 5 = 8) or using 13 m itself ignores the right triangle, so the answer is 12 m.
Source: PYQ-style
