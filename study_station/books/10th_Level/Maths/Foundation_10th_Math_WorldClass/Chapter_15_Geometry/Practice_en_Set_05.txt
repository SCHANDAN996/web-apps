The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. The smallest angle is:
(A) 20° (B) 30° (C) 40° (D) 50°
Answer: (B) 30°
Solution: Let angles be x, 2x, 3x. x + 2x + 3x = 180° ⇒ 6x = 180° ⇒ x = 30°.

(A) 12 cm (B) 24 cm (C) 26 cm (D) 20 cm
