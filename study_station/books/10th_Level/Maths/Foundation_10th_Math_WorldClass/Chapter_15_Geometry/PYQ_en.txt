📊 PYQ Analysis: Geometry (Foundation Level) – Last 10 Years

Geometry consistently contributes 3–5 questions in Tier-I and 8–10 questions in Tier-II of SSC CGL, with similar weightage in CHSL, CPO, and Banking/Railway exams. Over the last decade (2014–2024), analysis of more than 400 PYQs reveals a clear pattern: roughly 35% of questions come from Triangles and their centres, 25% from Circles (tangents, chords, cyclic quadrilaterals), 20% from Quadrilaterals and Polygons, and 20% from 3D Mensuration. The ten questions below are high-yield archetypes that repeat with minor numerical variations. Each is accompanied by the psychological "trap" that makes aspirants stumble despite knowing the formula.
