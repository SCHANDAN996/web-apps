Solution:

Q2. A mixture of 40 litres of milk and water contains 10% water. How much water must be added to make the water content 20%?
(A) 4 litres
(B) 5 litres
(C) 8 litres
(D) 10 litres

Answer: (B) 5 litres
Solution:
Water in 40L = 10% of 40 = 4L. Let x litres water be added.
Total water = 4+x, total mixture = 40+x.
(4+x)/(40+x) = 20/100 = 1/5 → 5(4+x)=40+x → 20+5x=40+x → 4x=20 → x=5 litres.
Source: NCERT (Class 8, Alligation)

Q23. (Medium) A container is filled with liquid, 4 parts of which are water and 5 parts syrup. How much of the mixture must be drawn off and replaced with water so that the mixture may be half water and half syrup?
(A) 1/9
(B) 1/10
(C) 1/8
(D) 1/7

Answer: (B) 1/10
Solution:
Initial water fraction = 4/9. Let fraction x of the mixture be replaced by water.
Final water fraction = (4/9)(1 – x) + x = 4/9 + (5/9)x.
Set equal to 1/2 → 4/9 + (5/9)x = 1/2 → multiply 18: 8 + 10x = 9 → 10x = 1 → x = 1/10.
Thus 1/10 of the mixture must be replaced.
Source: SSC CGL (Adapted)

Q24. (Medium) Two vessels A and B contain mixtures of milk and water in the ratios 4 : 3 and 2 : 3 respectively. In what ratio must these mixtures be mixed to obtain a new mixture containing milk and water in the ratio 1 : 1?
(A) 7 : 5
(B) 5 : 7
(C) 14 : 5
(D) 5 : 14

Answer: (A) 7 : 5
Solution:
Milk fraction in A = 4/7, in B = 2/5, desired = 1/2.
Alligation: (4/7 – 1/2) = 1/14, (1/2 – 2/5) = 1/10.
Ratio of quantities A : B = (1/10) : (1/14) = 14 : 10 = 7 : 5.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II)

Q25. (Medium) A cask contains 100 litres of wine. 20 litres of wine is drawn out and the cask is filled with water. This operation is performed three times in all. How much wine is left in the cask?
(A) 51.2 litres
(B) 48.8 litres
(C) 64 litres
(D) 56 litres

Answer: (A) 51.2 litres
Solution:
Wine left = 100 × (1 – 20/100)³ = 100 × (4/5)³ = 100 × 64/125 = 51.2 litres.
