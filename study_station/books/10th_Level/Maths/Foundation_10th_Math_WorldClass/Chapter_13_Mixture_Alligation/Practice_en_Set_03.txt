Q51. A shopkeeper mixes two varieties of wheat costing ₹18 per kg and ₹24 per kg. He sells the mixture at ₹27 per kg and earns a profit of 20%. In what ratio must the two varieties be mixed?
(A) 1 : 3
(B) 3 : 1
(C) 2 : 3
(D) 3 : 2

Answer: (A) 1 : 3
Solution:
SP = ₹27/kg, Profit = 20% → CP = 27 × 100/120 = ₹22.5/kg.
Alligation: cheaper (₹18) and dearer (₹24) to get mean ₹22.5.
(24 – 22.5) = 1.5, (22.5 – 18) = 4.5 → ratio of cheaper : dearer = 1.5 : 4.5 = 1 : 3.
Thus ₹18/kg variety : ₹24/kg variety = 1 : 3.
Source: SSC CGL (Adapted)

Q52. A vessel contains a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 5 : 2. 14 litres of the mixture is drawn out and replaced with water. The new ratio of milk to water becomes 5 : 3. What was the initial quantity of the mixture?
(A) 84 litres
(B) 98 litres
(C) 112 litres
(D) 126 litres

Answer: (C) 112 litres
Solution:
Let initial mixture = 7x litres (milk = 5x, water = 2x).
14 L removed: milk out = (5/7)×14 = 10 L, water out = 4 L.
After adding 14 L water: milk = 5x – 10, water = 2x – 4 + 14 = 2x + 10.
Given ratio 5 : 3 → (5x – 10)/(2x + 10) = 5/3 → 15x – 30 = 10x + 50 → 5x = 80 → x = 16.
Initial mixture = 7 × 16 = 112 litres.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II)

Q54. A cask contains a mixture of liquids A and B in the ratio 7 : 3. When 15 litres of the mixture is drawn off and the cask is filled with B, the ratio of A to B becomes 7 : 9. What is the capacity of the cask?
(A) 30 litres
(B) 35 litres
(C) 40 litres
(D) 45 litres

Answer: (C) 40 litres
Solution:
Let capacity = x L. Initially A = 0.7x, B = 0.3x.
15 L removed: A out = 0.7×15 = 10.5 L, B out = 4.5 L.
After filling with B: A = 0.7x – 10.5, B = 0.3x – 4.5 + 15 = 0.3x + 10.5.
Ratio 7:9 → (0.7x – 10.5)/(0.3x + 10.5) = 7/9 → 9(0.7x – 10.5) = 7(0.3x + 10.5)
→ 6.3x – 94.5 = 2.1x + 73.5 → 4.2x = 168 → x = 40 litres.
Source: SSC CGL (Adapted)

Q64. A solution of 120 litres contains acid and water in the ratio 4 : 1. Some quantity of the solution is taken out and replaced with water so that the ratio becomes 3 : 2. How many litres were replaced?
(A) 24 litres
(B) 30 litres
(C) 36 litres
(D) 40 litres

Answer: (B) 30 litres
Solution:
Acid = (4/5)×120 = 96 L, water = 24 L.
Let x L be replaced with water.
Acid left = 96 – (4/5)x, water left = 24 – (1/5)x + x = 24 + (4/5)x.
Given acid : water = 3 : 2 → (96 – 4x/5) / (24 + 4x/5) = 3/2
→ 2(96 – 4x/5) = 3(24 + 4x/5) → 192 – 8x/5 = 72 + 12x/5 → 120 = 20x/5 = 4x → x = 30 L.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II)

Q68. A vessel is full of a mixture of kerosene and petrol in which kerosene forms 20%. 20 litres of the mixture is taken out and the vessel is filled with petrol. If the kerosene is now 15%, what is the capacity of the vessel?
(A) 60 litres
(B) 70 litres
(C) 80 litres
(D) 90 litres

Answer: (C) 80 litres
Solution:
Let capacity = V L. Kerosene initially = 0.2V.
20 L mixture removed → kerosene removed = 20% of 20 = 4 L.
Kerosene left = 0.2V – 4. After filling with petrol, total volume = V.
Kerosene % = 15% → (0.2V – 4)/V = 0.15 → 0.2V – 4 = 0.15V → 0.05V = 4 → V = 80 L.

Solution:

Q71. A container is filled with a liquid which is 5 parts water and 11 parts syrup. How much of the mixture must be drawn off and replaced with water so that the mixture becomes half water and half syrup?
(A) 1/11
(B) 3/11
(C) 5/16
(D) 7/16

Answer: (B) 3/11
Solution:
Initial water fraction = 5/16, target water fraction = 1/2.
Let fraction x of the mixture be replaced by water.
Final water fraction = (5/16)(1 – x) + x = 1/2 → 5/16 – (5/16)x + x = 1/2
→ 5/16 + (11/16)x = 1/2 → (11/16)x = 8/16 – 5/16 = 3/16 → 11x = 3 → x = 3/11.
Thus 3/11 of the mixture must be replaced.
Source: SSC CGL (Adapted)

Q75. A vessel contains a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 3 : 1. 8 litres of the mixture is drawn off and replaced with water. Now, 8 litres of the new mixture is again drawn off and replaced with water. If the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture is 25 : 23, find the initial quantity of the mixture.
(A) 40 litres
(B) 48 litres
(C) 56 litres
(D) 64 litres

Answer: (B) 48 litres
Solution:
Let initial quantity = 4x litres (milk = 3x, water = x).
After first replacement, milk = 3x – (3/4)×8 = 3x – 6, water = x + 6.
After second replacement, milk = (3x – 6) × (1 – 8/(4x)) = (3x – 6) × (x – 2)/x.
Total volume remains 4x. Milk fraction = (3x – 6)(x – 2) / (4x^2).
Given milk : water = 25 : 23 → milk fraction = 25/48.
So (3x – 6)(x – 2) / (4x^2) = 25/48 → 48(3x – 6)(x – 2) = 100 x^2
→ 144 (x – 2)^2 = 100 x^2 → (x – 2)^2 / x^2 = 100/144 = (10/12)^2 → (x – 2)/x = 5/6
→ 6x – 12 = 5x → x = 12. Thus initial quantity = 48 litres.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II, Adapted)
