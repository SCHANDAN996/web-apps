(1) 180 (2) 360 (3) 120 (4) 720

Answer: (1) 180
Solution: Total digits = 6, with 3 repeated twice and 4 repeated twice. Distinct arrangements = 6! / (2! × 2!) = 720 / 4 = 180.

Q99. In how many ways can 3 red, 2 blue and 1 green ball be arranged in a row?
(1) 60 (2) 720 (3) 120 (4) 360

Answer: (1) 60
Solution: Total balls = 6, with 3 identical red and 2 identical blue. Number of arrangements = 6! / (3! × 2!) = 720 / (6×2) = 720 / 12 = 60.

Q100. If ¹⁵C₆ = ¹⁵Cₓ, and x ≠ 6, then x equals:
(1) 9 (2) 8 (3) 10 (4) 12

Answer: (1) 9
Solution: Using symmetry, ¹⁵Cₓ = ¹⁵C_{15-x}. Given ¹⁵C₆ = ¹⁵Cₓ ⇒ 15 - x = 6 ⇒ x = 9.
