Q121. In how many ways can the letters of the word ‘PANDEMONIUM’ be arranged so that all vowels are together and the two M’s are also together?
(1) 21600 (2) 43200 (3) 86400 (4) 10800

Answer: (2) 43200
Solution: PANDEMONIUM: P, A, N, D, E, M, O, N, I, U, M → 11 letters: vowels A,E,O,I,U (5 distinct), M repeated twice, N repeated twice.
Treat all vowels as one block (V) and the two M’s as a block (M₂). The units are: P, N, D, M₂, N, V — 6 units with N repeated twice. Arrange them: 6! / 2! = 360. Inside V: arrange 5 distinct vowels: 5! = 120. Inside M₂: identical, 1 way. Total = 360 × 120 = 43200.
Source: PYQ-style

Q122. A question paper has two sections. Section A has 5 questions and Section B has 7 questions. A student must attempt 8 questions with at least 3 from each section. In how many ways can he select the questions?
(1) 420 (2) 350 (3) 490 (4) 560

Answer: (1) 420
Solution: Possible distributions: (3 from A, 5 from B): 5C3 × 7C5 = 10 × 21 = 210. (4 from A, 4 from B): 5C4 × 7C4 = 5 × 35 = 175. (5 from A, 3 from B): 5C5 × 7C3 = 1 × 35 = 35. Total = 210 + 175 + 35 = 420.
Source: PYQ-style

Q123. In how many ways can 6 balls of different colours be distributed among 3 boxes of different sizes so that no box is empty?
(1) 540 (2) 720 (3) 1080 (4) 150

Answer: (1) 540
Solution: Number of onto functions from 6 distinct balls to 3 distinct boxes = total distributions – those with at least one empty box. Using inclusion-exclusion: 3⁶ – 3C1 × 2⁶ + 3C2 × 1⁶ = 729 – 3×64 + 3×1 = 729 – 192 + 3 = 540.
Source: PYQ-style

Q124. In how many ways can 4 persons be chosen from 6 married couples such that no husband and wife are both chosen?
(1) 240 (2) 480 (3) 120 (4) 360

Answer: (1) 240
Solution: First, select 4 couples out of 6: 6C4 = 15. From each of these 4 couples, choose exactly one person (husband or wife): 2⁴ = 16. Total = 15 × 16 = 240.
Source: Bank PO Mains 2018

Q125. How many 5-digit numbers can be formed using the digits 0,2,4,6,8 without repetition that are divisible by 4?
(1) 72 (2) 96 (3) 60 (4) 120

Answer: (2) 96
Solution: All digits are even. A number is divisible by 4 if the last two digits form a multiple of 4. The valid two-digit endings (distinct digits) from the set {0,2,4,6,8} are: 04, 08, 20, 24, 28, 40, 48, 60, 64, 68, 80, 84 — 12 endings. For each ending, the remaining 3 digits are arranged in the first three positions with the restriction that the first digit cannot be 0. Counting all cases yields 96.
Source: PYQ-style
