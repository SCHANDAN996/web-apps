Q51. How many ways can the letters of the word 'REMOTE' be arranged such that the vowels are always together?
(1) 72 (2) 144 (3) 36 (4) 120

Answer: (1) 72
Solution: Vowels in REMOTE: E, O, E (E repeated twice). Treat vowels as one block. Total units = R, M, T, [vowel block] = 4 units. These can be arranged in 4! = 24 ways. Within the block, arrange E, O, E: 3!/2! = 3 ways. Total = 24 × 3 = 72.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

Q52. From 5 boys and 4 girls, a team of 3 is to be selected such that exactly 2 are girls. Number of ways is:
(1) 30 (2) 40 (3) 20 (4) 60

Answer: (1) 30
Solution: Choose 2 girls from 4: 4C2 = 6. Choose 1 boy from 5: 5C1 = 5. Total = 6 × 5 = 30.
Source: IBPS Clerk Mains 2019

Q53. In how many ways can 5 different keys be placed on a key ring?
(1) 24 (2) 12 (3) 60 (4) 120

Answer: (2) 12
Solution: Number of circular permutations of n distinct items on a ring (where flipping the ring gives the same arrangement) is (n-1)!/2. Here n=5 → (5-1)!/2 = 4!/2 = 24/2 = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.4

Q54. In how many ways can the letters of the word 'ARRANGE' be arranged so that the two R's are always together?
(1) 360 (2) 180 (3) 720 (4) 120

Answer: (1) 360
Solution: Treat the two R's as a single block 'RR'. Letters become A, A, RR, N, G, E (6 units, A repeated twice). Number of arrangements = 6! / 2! = 720 / 2 = 360.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

Q55. How many 3-digit even numbers can be formed using the digits 1,2,3,4,5,6,7 without repetition?
(1) 90 (2) 120 (3) 60 (4) 210

Answer: (1) 90
Solution: Unit digit must be even: 2,4,6 → 3 choices. Then hundreds digit: any of the remaining 6 digits, tens digit: any of the remaining 5 digits. Total = 3 × 6 × 5 = 90.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

Q56. A student must attempt 5 out of 8 questions in an exam. If the first 3 questions are compulsory, in how many ways can the student choose the questions?
(1) 10 (2) 20 (3) 56 (4) 36

Answer: (1) 10
Solution: Since first 3 are compulsory, the student must select 2 more from the remaining 5 questions. Number of ways = 5C2 = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.4

Q57. How many numbers between 100 and 1000 can be formed with the digits 0,1,2,3,4,5 if no digit is repeated?
(1) 100 (2) 120 (3) 80 (4) 150

Answer: (1) 100
Solution: Numbers are 3-digit. Hundreds place: cannot be 0, so 5 choices (1-5). Tens place: any of the remaining 5 digits (including 0). Units place: any of the remaining 4 digits. Total = 5 × 5 × 4 = 100.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.1

Q58. In how many ways can 7 persons be seated in a row if 3 particular persons always sit together?
(1) 720 (2) 5040 (3) 240 (4) 120

Answer: (1) 720
Solution: Treat the 3 particular persons as a block. Then we have 5 units (4 others + 1 block) which can be arranged in 5! = 120 ways. The 3 persons within the block can be arranged in 3! = 6 ways. Total = 120 × 6 = 720.
Source: SSC CPO 2021

Q59. A man has 5 friends. In how many ways can he invite one or more of them to a party?
(1) 31 (2) 32 (3) 5 (4) 10

Answer: (1) 31
Solution: Each friend can be invited or not, giving 2⁵ = 32 possibilities. Excluding the case of inviting none → 32 – 1 = 31.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.4

Q60. How many distinct permutations can be made from the letters of the word 'BANANA'?
(1) 60 (2) 120 (3) 720 (4) 45

Answer: (1) 60
Solution: BANANA has 6 letters with A repeated 3 times and N repeated twice. Number of permutations = 6! / (3! × 2!) = 720 / (6 × 2) = 720 / 12 = 60.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.3

Q61. In how many ways can 4 different rings be worn on 3 fingers of one hand if any finger can hold any number of rings?
(1) 81 (2) 64 (3) 24 (4) 12

Answer: (1) 81
Solution: Each ring has 3 independent choices of a finger. Total ways = 3⁴ = 81.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.1

Q62. A committee of 4 is to be formed from 4 men and 5 women. In how many ways can it be done if the committee must contain exactly 2 women?
(1) 60 (2) 120 (3) 80 (4) 100

Answer: (1) 60
Solution: Choose 2 women from 5: 5C2 = 10. Choose 2 men from 4: 4C2 = 6. Total = 10 × 6 = 60.
Source: SSC CGL 2017

Q63. If ²ⁿP₂ = 30, then the value of n is:
(1) 3 (2) 4 (3) 5 (4) 6

Answer: (1) 3
Solution: ²ⁿP₂ = (2n)(2n-1) = 30. Checking small values: if n=3, 2n=6, 6×5=30. Hence n=3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.3

Q64. How many 4-digit even numbers can be formed using the digits 0,1,2,3,4,5 without repetition?
(1) 156 (2) 312 (3) 120 (4) 180

Answer: (1) 156
Solution: An even number ends in 0,2,4.
Case 1: unit=0 → thousands:5 choices, hundreds:4, tens:3 → 5×4×3 = 60.
Case 2: unit=2 → thousands:4 (not 0,2), hundreds:4 (0 allowed), tens:3 → 4×4×3 = 48.
Case 3: unit=4 → same as case 2: 48.
Total = 60+48+48 = 156.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

Q65. In how many ways can 4 boys and 2 girls be seated in a row such that no two girls sit together?
(1) 480 (2) 240 (3) 720 (4) 120

Answer: (1) 480
Solution: First arrange the 4 boys: 4! = 24 ways. They create 5 gaps (_ B1 _ B2 _ B3 _ B4 _). Choose 2 of these 5 gaps for the 2 girls and arrange them: 5P2 = 20. Total = 24 × 20 = 480.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

Q66. A polygon has 10 sides. The number of its diagonals is:
(1) 35 (2) 45 (3) 30 (4) 40

Answer: (1) 35
Solution: Number of diagonals in an n-sided polygon = n(n-3)/2. For n=10 → 10×7/2 = 35.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.4

Q67. A club has 6 members. In how many ways can a president, secretary, and treasurer be chosen if no member can hold more than one post?
(1) 120 (2) 20 (3) 60 (4) 240

Answer: (1) 120
Solution: The three posts are distinct, so we use permutation: 6P3 = 6 × 5 × 4 = 120.

Q69. How many 5-digit numbers can be formed using digits 1,2,3,4,5 without repetition that are divisible by 5?
(1) 24 (2) 120 (3) 60 (4) 48

Answer: (1) 24
Solution: A number divisible by 5 must end in 5 (since 5 is the only multiple of 5 in the set). Fix unit digit = 5. Remaining 4 digits can be arranged in 4! = 24 ways.
Source: SSC CGL 2016

Q70. In how many ways can 8 persons be seated around a round table if two particular persons refuse to sit together?
(1) 3600 (2) 2520 (3) 1440 (4) 720

Answer: (1) 3600
Solution: Total circular arrangements of 8 persons = (8-1)! = 7! = 5040. Arrangements with the two together: treat them as block → (7-1)! × 2! = 6! × 2 = 720 × 2 = 1440. Therefore, not together = 5040 – 1440 = 3600.
Source: SSC CPO 2019

Q71. If ⁿC₈ = ⁿC₅, then n equals:
(1) 13 (2) 12 (3) 15 (4) 10

Answer: (1) 13
Solution: Using the property ⁿCᵣ = ⁿC_{n-r}, we have n-8 = 5 ⇒ n = 13.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.4

Q73. A bag contains 3 red, 4 blue, and 2 green balls. In how many ways can 2 balls be drawn such that both are of the same colour?
(1) 10 (2) 9 (3) 36 (4) 18

Answer: (1) 10
Solution: Both red: 3C2 = 3; both blue: 4C2 = 6; both green: 2C2 = 1. Total = 3+6+1 = 10.
Source: IBPS PO Pre 2019

Q74. In how many ways can the letters of the word 'SUCCESS' be arranged such that no two S's are together?
(1) 120 (2) 24 (3) 96 (4) 60

Answer: (1) 120
Solution: SUCCESS has S (3 times), U, C (2 times), E. First arrange the non-S letters: U, C, C, E → 4!/2! = 12 ways. They create 5 gaps (U, C, C, E). Choose 3 gaps out of 5 to place the identical S's: 5C3 = 10. Total = 12 × 10 = 120.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

Q75. How many 4-digit numbers can be formed from the digits 0,2,4,6,8 without repetition?
(1) 96 (2) 120 (3) 48 (4) 24

Answer: (1) 96
Solution: Thousands digit cannot be 0, so 4 choices (2,4,6,8). Hundreds digit: any of the remaining 4 digits (including 0). Tens: 3 choices, Units: 2. Total = 4 × 4 × 3 × 2 = 96.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Ex 7.1
