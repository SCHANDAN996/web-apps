26. In how many ways can 4 boys and 2 girls be seated in a row such that the two girls always sit together?
(a) 480 (b) 720 (c) 120 (d) 240
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is arranging all 6 people freely (720) and forgetting the "together" condition. Treat the 2 girls as one block: 5 units arrange in 5! = 120 ways, and the girls swap inside the block in 2! = 2 ways, so the answer is 240.
Source: PYQ-style

27. How many 4-digit numbers can be formed using the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 without repetition such that the number is divisible by 4?
(a) 24 (b) 48 (c) 36 (d) 60
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students take any 4 digits (5P4 = 120) and divide by 4, which is wrong. Divisibility by 4 depends on the last two digits: valid endings are 12, 24, 32, 52 (4 choices), and the first two digits come from the remaining 3 digits in 3 × 2 = 6 ways, so the answer is 24.
Source: PYQ-style

28. In how many ways can 5 persons sit around a circular table if two particular persons must always sit together?
(a) 12 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 48
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is using 5! = 120 (linear) instead of circular counting. Treat the pair as one block: 4 units around a circle give (4 − 1)! = 6 ways, times 2! = 2 inside the block, so the answer is 12.
Source: PYQ-style

29. From 6 men and 4 women, a committee of 5 is to be formed containing exactly 3 men. In how many ways can this be done?
(a) 120 (b) 240 (c) 60 (d) 180
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is choosing only the men (6C3 = 20) and forgetting the 2 women needed to complete the committee. Multiply 6C3 = 20 by 4C2 = 6, so the answer is 120.
Source: PYQ-style

30. In how many ways can 5 persons be seated in a row if two particular persons never sit together?
(a) 96 (b) 48 (c) 120 (d) 72
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is reporting 48, which is actually the number of arrangements where the two sit together. Total arrangements are 5! = 120 and together-arrangements are 4! × 2! = 48, so the answer is 120 − 48 = 72.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

31. A bag contains 5 red and 4 blue balls. In how many ways can 3 balls be drawn such that at least one ball is red?
(a) 74 (b) 84 (c) 80 (d) 76
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding separate cases (1 red, 2 red, 3 red) invites arithmetic slips; the complement is cleaner. Total draws are 9C3 = 84 and all-blue draws are 4C3 = 4, so the answer is 84 − 4 = 80.
Source: PYQ-style

32. How many 4-digit numbers divisible by 5 can be formed using the digits 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 without repetition?
(a) 96 (b) 120 (c) 108 (d) 84
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is treating both endings alike and ignoring that 0 cannot lead. Ending in 0 gives 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 numbers; ending in 5 gives 4 × 4 × 3 = 48 numbers, so the answer is 60 + 48 = 108.
Source: PYQ-style

33. In how many ways can 7 persons be seated around a circular table such that two particular persons never sit together?
(a) 600 (b) 480 (c) 840 (d) 720
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 720, which counts all circular arrangements without any restriction. Together-arrangements are (6 − 1)! × 2! = 240, so the answer is 720 − 240 = 480.
Source: PYQ-style

34. A man has 4 friends. In how many ways can he invite one or more of them to a party?
(a) 16 (b) 15 (c) 24 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 16, which includes the case where nobody is invited. Each friend has 2 choices (invited or not), giving 2⁴ = 16, and removing the "none invited" case, the answer is 15.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

35. In how many ways can the letters of the word MONDAY be arranged so that the two vowels are never together?
(a) 600 (b) 720 (c) 480 (d) 240
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering 240, which is the count where the vowels are together. Total arrangements are 6! = 720 and together-arrangements are 5! × 2! = 240, so the answer is 720 − 240 = 480.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

36. From 5 men and 4 women, a committee of 5 is to be formed containing at least 3 men. In how many ways can this be done?
(a) 45 (b) 81 (c) 80 (d) 120
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is stopping at the 3-men case (60) and missing the other valid cases. Add 5C3 × 4C2 = 60, 5C4 × 4C1 = 20, and 5C5 × 4C0 = 1, so the answer is 81.
Source: PYQ-style

37. How many 3-digit odd numbers can be formed using the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 without repetition?
(a) 120 (b) 210 (c) 150 (d) 180
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is filling the hundreds place first and losing track of odd choices for the units place. Fix the units digit first: 4 odd choices, then 6 choices for hundreds and 5 for tens, so the answer is 4 × 6 × 5 = 120.
Source: PYQ-style

38. In how many distinct ways can 5 identical red balls and 4 identical blue balls be arranged in a row?
(a) 210 (b) 120 (c) 24 (d) 126
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 9! = 362880-style thinking, ignoring that identical balls make many arrangements the same. Divide by the repetitions: 9!/(5! × 4!), so the answer is 126.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

39. How many different signals can be made by hoisting some of 5 different flags in a vertical stack if at least 2 flags must be used?
(a) 325 (b) 300 (c) 320 (d) 120
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is counting only the 5-flag case (120) and ignoring smaller stacks. Add 5P2 + 5P3 + 5P4 + 5P5 = 20 + 60 + 120 + 120, so the answer is 320.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

40. A student must choose 5 courses out of 9, and 2 particular courses are compulsory. In how many ways can the choice be made?
(a) 21 (b) 84 (c) 42 (d) 35
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 9C5 = 126-style selections without fixing the compulsory courses. With 2 courses fixed, only 3 more are chosen from the remaining 7: 7C3, so the answer is 35.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

41. How many 4-letter codes can be formed from the letters A to J if no letter is repeated and the code must start with a vowel?
(a) 5040 (b) 1512 (c) 2160 (d) 720
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is filling all four places freely (10 × 9 × 8 × 7 = 5040) and ignoring the vowel condition. The first place has 3 vowel choices (A, E, I), then 9 × 8 × 7 = 504 for the rest, so the answer is 3 × 504 = 1512.
Source: PYQ-style

42. In how many ways can the letters of the word BALLOON be arranged so that the two L's are together?
(a) 180 (b) 720 (c) 360 (d) 120
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 7!/(2! × 2!) = 1260 without applying the together condition. Treat LL as one block: 6 units with O repeated twice give 6!/2!, so the answer is 360.
Source: PYQ-style

43. A polygon has 44 diagonals. How many sides does it have?
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 8 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using nC2 = 44 directly, which counts sides plus diagonals. Diagonals equal n(n − 3)/2 = 44, giving n² − 3n − 88 = 0 and n = 11, so the answer is 11.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

44. Six items in column A are to be matched with six items in column B, each item of B used exactly once. How many different matchings are possible?
(a) 36 (b) 720 (c) 120 (d) 30
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 6 × 6 = 36, which allows reuse of column B items. The first item has 6 choices, the next 5, and so on, giving 6!, so the answer is 720.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

45. In how many ways can 3 different letters be posted in 4 letter boxes if each box can hold any number of letters?
(a) 64 (b) 24 (c) 81 (d) 12
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing 3⁴ = 81 by reversing letters and boxes. Each of the 3 letters independently has 4 box choices, giving 4³, so the answer is 64.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

46. How many 5-digit telephone numbers can be formed using digits 0 to 9 if each number must start with 67 and no digit repeats?
(a) 720 (b) 30240 (c) 10080 (d) 336
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is arranging all 10 digits freely instead of fixing the first two places. With 6 and 7 used, the last three places take 8 × 7 × 6 arrangements, so the answer is 336.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

47. A committee of 3 is to be formed from 3 boys and 2 girls, and it must contain at least one girl. In how many ways can this be done?
(a) 9 (b) 7 (c) 6 (d) 10
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 10, the total number of committees, without removing the all-boy case. Total committees are 5C3 = 10 and the all-boy committee is 1, so the answer is 9.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

48. If nC2 = 36, what is the value of n?
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 10 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is solving n(n − 1) = 36 and getting confused between n and n − 1. Since n(n − 1)/2 = 36 gives n(n − 1) = 72 = 9 × 8, the answer is 9.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

49. In how many ways can the letters of the word DAUGHTER be arranged so that the vowels always come together?
(a) 720 (b) 2160 (c) 4320 (d) 1440
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering 720, which arranges the 6 units but forgets the vowels' internal order. The block of 3 vowels plus 5 consonants gives 6! = 720 arrangements, times 3! = 6 inside the block, so the answer is 4320.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

50. A committee of 6 is to be formed from 5 men and 6 women with exactly 3 men included. In how many ways can this be done?
(a) 200 (b) 400 (c) 500 (d) 300
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding 5C3 and 6C3 instead of multiplying the two independent choices. Choose 3 men in 5C3 = 10 ways and 3 women in 6C3 = 20 ways, so the answer is 10 × 20 = 200.
Source: PYQ-style
