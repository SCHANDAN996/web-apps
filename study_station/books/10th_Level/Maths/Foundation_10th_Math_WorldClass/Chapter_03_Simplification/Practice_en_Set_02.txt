26. Simplify: (18 ÷ 3) × 2 + 5 – 1
(a) 16 (b) 12 (c) 18 (d) 14
Answer: (a) 16
Solution: Bracket first: (18 ÷ 3) = 6. Then multiplication: 6 × 2 = 12. Finally addition and subtraction: 12 + 5 – 1 = 16.
Source: NCERT Class 7

27. Simplify: 30 – 5 × 2 + 4 ÷ 2
(a) 20 (b) 22 (c) 24 (d) 18
Answer: (b) 22
Solution: Multiplication and division first: 5 × 2 = 10 and 4 ÷ 2 = 2. Then 30 – 10 + 2 = 20 + 2 = 22.
Source: NCERT Class 7

28. Simplify: 4 of 6 ÷ 3 + 2 × 5
(a) 14 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 20
Answer: (c) 18
Solution: "Of" first: 4 of 6 = 24. Then division: 24 ÷ 3 = 8. Multiplication: 2 × 5 = 10. Finally 8 + 10 = 18.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

29. Simplify: 5 ÷ 1/5 of 25 – 1
(a) 624 (b) 24 (c) 4 (d) 0
Answer: (d) 0
Solution: Common Trap: Dividing first gives (5 ÷ 1/5) = 25, then 25 of 25 – 1 = 624 (incorrect). In BODMAS, "Of" precedes Division: 1/5 of 25 = 5, then 5 ÷ 5 = 1, and 1 – 1 = 0.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. Simplify: 1/2 + 2/3 ÷ 4/5 × 3/4
(a) 1 1/8 (b) 1 1/4 (c) 7/8 (d) 1 3/8
Answer: (a) 1 1/8
Solution: Division and multiplication left to right: 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 5/6. Then 5/6 × 3/4 = 5/8. Finally 1/2 + 5/8 = 4/8 + 5/8 = 9/8 = 1 1/8.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

31. Simplify: [8 + {4 × (6 ÷ 3)}] – 5
(a) 9 (b) 11 (c) 13 (d) 15
Answer: (b) 11
Solution: Innermost bracket: 6 ÷ 3 = 2. Then {4 × 2} = 8. Next [8 + 8] = 16. Finally 16 – 5 = 11.
Source: NCERT Class 7

32. Simplify: (5/6 of 72) ÷ 4 + 1/3
(a) 14 1/3 (b) 16 2/3 (c) 15 1/3 (d) 15 2/3
Answer: (c) 15 1/3
Solution: "Of" first: 5/6 of 72 = 60. Then division: 60 ÷ 4 = 15. Finally 15 + 1/3 = 15 1/3.
Source: RRB Group D Pattern

33. Simplify: 12.5% of 800 + 2/5 of 75
(a) 110 (b) 120 (c) 140 (d) 130
Answer: (d) 130
Solution: 12.5% = 1/8, so 1/8 × 800 = 100. 2/5 of 75 = 30. Sum = 100 + 30 = 130.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

34. Simplify: (256)¹⁄² + (27)¹⁄³ – 10
(a) 9 (b) 7 (c) 11 (d) 13
Answer: (a) 9
Solution: Evaluate roots: √256 = 16 and ∛27 = 3. Expression becomes 16 + 3 – 10 = 19 – 10 = 9.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

35. Simplify: [{(16 ÷ 4) of 3} + 5] × 2 – 10
(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 28 (d) 32
Answer: (b) 24
Solution: Innermost bracket: 16 ÷ 4 = 4. Then 4 of 3 = 12. Next {12 + 5} = 17. Then [17] × 2 = 34. Finally 34 – 10 = 24.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

36. Simplify: 0.75 ÷ 0.25 × 2 + 1
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (c) 7
Solution: Left to right: 0.75 ÷ 0.25 = 3. Then 3 × 2 = 6. Finally 6 + 1 = 7.
Source: NCERT Class 7

37. Simplify: 2/3 + 1/4 of (16/5 ÷ 4/15)
(a) 2 1/3 (b) 3 1/3 (c) 2 2/3 (d) 3 2/3
Answer: (d) 3 2/3
Solution: Innermost division: 16/5 ÷ 4/15 = 16/5 × 15/4 = 12. Then 1/4 of 12 = 3. Finally 2/3 + 3 = 11/3 = 3 2/3.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

38. Simplify: (144 ÷ 12) + (3 × 5) – (2 of 4)
(a) 19 (b) 17 (c) 21 (d) 23
Answer: (a) 19
Solution: Evaluate parts: 144 ÷ 12 = 12, 3 × 5 = 15, and 2 of 4 = 8. Expression: 12 + 15 – 8 = 27 – 8 = 19.
Source: NCERT Class 7

39. Simplify: 5 1/3 ÷ 4/9 of 1 1/2 + 2/5
(a) 7 4/5 (b) 8 2/5 (c) 8 1/5 (d) 9 2/5
Answer: (b) 8 2/5
Solution: Improper fractions: 5 1/3 = 16/3 and 1 1/2 = 3/2. Evaluate 'of': 4/9 × 3/2 = 2/3. Then division: 16/3 ÷ 2/3 = 16/3 × 3/2 = 8. Finally 8 + 2/5 = 8 2/5.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

40. Simplify: (49)¹⁄² × (64)¹⁄³ + (81)¹⁄²
(a) 33 (b) 35 (c) 37 (d) 39
Answer: (c) 37
Solution: Calculate roots: √49 = 7, ∛64 = 4, and √81 = 9. Multiply then add: 7 × 4 + 9 = 28 + 9 = 37.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

41. Simplify: 12 + 8 ÷ 4 × (5 – 3) – 1
(a) 13 (b) 17 (c) 11 (d) 15
Answer: (d) 15
Solution: Bracket first: (5 – 3) = 2. Then division & multiplication left to right: 8 ÷ 4 = 2, 2 × 2 = 4. Finally 12 + 4 – 1 = 15.
Source: NCERT Class 7

42. Simplify: (2/5 of 75) ÷ (1/2 of 18) × 3
(a) 10 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 15
Answer: (a) 10
Solution: Brackets: 2/5 of 75 = 30 and 1/2 of 18 = 9. Expression: 30 ÷ 9 × 3 = (30/9) × 3 = 10.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

43. Simplify: [ { (3 + 2) × 4 } ÷ 5 ] + 6 – 2
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: Innermost: 3 + 2 = 5. Next 5 × 4 = 20. Then 20 ÷ 5 = 4. Finally 4 + 6 – 2 = 8.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

44. Simplify: 1.2 + 2.3 – 0.5 × 2 ÷ 0.1
(a) –5.5 (b) –7.5 (c) –6.5 (d) –4.5
Answer: (c) –6.5
Solution: Multiply and divide left to right: 0.5 × 2 = 1, then 1 ÷ 0.1 = 10. Expression becomes 1.2 + 2.3 – 10 = 3.5 – 10 = –6.5.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

45. Simplify: (3 1/2 – 1 1/4) ÷ (1/2) + 3
(a) 6.5 (b) 8.0 (c) 7.0 (d) 7.5
Answer: (d) 7.5
Solution: Inside bracket: 3 1/2 – 1 1/4 = 7/2 – 5/4 = 9/4. Division: 9/4 ÷ 1/2 = 9/4 × 2 = 4.5. Finally 4.5 + 3 = 7.5.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

46. Simplify: (2⁴ ÷ 2²) + (3³ ÷ 3²) × 2
(a) 10 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 14
Answer: (a) 10
Solution: Index laws: 2⁴ ÷ 2² = 2² = 4 and 3³ ÷ 3² = 3¹ = 3. Expression: 4 + 3 × 2 = 4 + 6 = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 8

47. Simplify: 5/8 of 4/15 ÷ 2/9 × 3/4
(a) 7/16 (b) 9/16 (c) 5/16 (d) 3/8
Answer: (b) 9/16
Solution: "Of" first: 5/8 × 4/15 = 1/6. Then division: 1/6 ÷ 2/9 = 1/6 × 9/2 = 3/4. Finally 3/4 × 3/4 = 9/16.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

48. Simplify: (1.44)¹⁄² + (0.125)¹⁄³ × 10 – 1
(a) 4.8 (b) 6.0 (c) 5.2 (d) 5.6
Answer: (c) 5.2
Solution: Roots: √1.44 = 1.2 and ∛0.125 = 0.5. Multiply: 0.5 × 10 = 5. Expression: 1.2 + 5 – 1 = 5.2.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

49. Simplify: 12 1/2% of 800 + 33 1/3% of 750
(a) 300 (b) 325 (c) 375 (d) 350
Answer: (d) 350
Solution: Convert percentages to fractions: 12 1/2% = 1/8 and 33 1/3% = 1/3. Then (1/8 × 800) + (1/3 × 750) = 100 + 250 = 350.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

50. Simplify: (15 ÷ 5 of 3) × 4 + 2 – 1
(a) 37 (b) 5 (c) 11 (d) 17
Answer: (b) 5
Solution: Psychological Trap: Performing division first inside the bracket gives (15 ÷ 5) = 3, then 3 of 3 = 9 and 9 × 4 + 2 – 1 = 37 (incorrect). By BODMAS, "Of" has precedence over division: 5 of 3 = 15, so (15 ÷ 15) = 1, then 1 × 4 + 2 – 1 = 5.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
