51. Simplify: 36 ÷ 6 × 2 + 4 – 3
(a) 13 (b) 4 (c) 15 (d) 10
Answer: (a) 13
Solution: Common Trap: Multiplying 6 × 2 = 12 first gives 36 ÷ 12 + 1 = 4 (incorrect). In BODMAS, division and multiplication have equal priority and are evaluated left to right: (36 ÷ 6) × 2 = 6 × 2 = 12, then 12 + 4 – 3 = 13.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. Simplify: (15 – 3 of 4) × 2 + 1
(a) 5 (b) 7 (c) 9 (d) 11
Answer: (b) 7
Solution: "Of" first inside bracket: 3 of 4 = 12. Bracket evaluates to 15 – 12 = 3. Then multiply and add: 3 × 2 + 1 = 6 + 1 = 7.
Source: NCERT Class 7

53. Simplify: 1.5 + 2/5 × 10 – 1/2
(a) 4.0 (b) 4.5 (c) 5.0 (d) 5.5
Answer: (c) 5.0
Solution: Multiplication first: 2/5 × 10 = 4. Convert 1/2 = 0.5. Expression: 1.5 + 4 – 0.5 = 5.5 – 0.5 = 5.0.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

54. Simplify: 4/7 of 14 + 3/8 of 24 – 5
(a) 10 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 12
Answer: (d) 12
Solution: "Of" first: 4/7 × 14 = 8 and 3/8 × 24 = 9. Expression: 8 + 9 – 5 = 17 – 5 = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 7

55. Simplify: [25 – {5 + (8 ÷ 2)}] × 3
(a) 48 (b) 42 (c) 36 (d) 54
Answer: (a) 48
Solution: Innermost bracket: 8 ÷ 2 = 4. Curly bracket: {5 + 4} = 9. Square bracket: [25 – 9] = 16. Finally 16 × 3 = 48.
Source: RRB Group D Pattern

56. Simplify: (0.2)² + (0.3)³ ÷ 0.027
(a) 1.40 (b) 1.04 (c) 0.14 (d) 1.004
Answer: (b) 1.04
Solution: Powers: (0.2)² = 0.04 and (0.3)³ = 0.027. Division: 0.027 ÷ 0.027 = 1. Sum: 0.04 + 1 = 1.04.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

57. Simplify: 125% of 48 + 0.5 of 64 – 20
(a) 68 (b) 76 (c) 72 (d) 80
Answer: (c) 72
Solution: 125% = 5/4, so 5/4 × 48 = 60. 0.5 of 64 = 32. Expression: 60 + 32 – 20 = 92 – 20 = 72.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

58. Simplify: 7/8 ÷ 3/4 × 1/4 of 16
(a) 3 1/3 (b) 4 1/3 (c) 5 2/3 (d) 4 2/3
Answer: (d) 4 2/3
Solution: "Of" first: 1/4 of 16 = 4. Expression becomes 7/8 ÷ 3/4 × 4. Division left to right: 7/8 × 4/3 = 7/6. Finally 7/6 × 4 = 28/6 = 14/3 = 4 2/3.
Source: NCERT Class 7

59. Simplify: 3 1/5 + 2 2/3 – 1 1/2
(a) 4 11/30 (b) 4 7/30 (c) 3 19/30 (d) 4 1/6
Answer: (a) 4 11/30
Solution: Improper fractions: 16/5 + 8/3 – 3/2. Common denominator 30: (96 + 80 – 45)/30 = 131/30 = 4 11/30.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

60. Simplify: (√81 – √16) × (√25 + √9)
(a) 35 (b) 40 (c) 45 (d) 50
Answer: (b) 40
Solution: Square roots: √81 = 9, √16 = 4, √25 = 5, √9 = 3. Expression: (9 – 4) × (5 + 3) = 5 × 8 = 40.
Source: NCERT Class 8

61. Simplify: 18 ÷ 3 of (1 + 2)
(a) 18 (b) 6 (c) 2 (d) 1
Answer: (c) 2
Solution: Psychological Trap: Dividing first gives (18 ÷ 3) = 6, then 6 of 3 = 18 (incorrect). By BODMAS, bracket (1 + 2) = 3 is solved first, then "Of" precedes division: 3 of 3 = 9, so 18 ÷ 9 = 2.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

62. Simplify: (5/6 + 1/3) ÷ (1/2 of 1/3)
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 7
Answer: (d) 7
Solution: Numerator: 5/6 + 2/6 = 7/6. Denominator: 1/2 of 1/3 = 1/6. Expression: 7/6 ÷ 1/6 = 7/6 × 6 = 7.
Source: NCERT Class 7

63. Simplify: 20% of 400 + 15% of 200 – 10% of 380
(a) 72 (b) 78 (c) 82 (d) 68
Answer: (a) 72
Solution: Calculate percentages: 20% of 400 = 80, 15% of 200 = 30, 10% of 380 = 38. Expression: 80 + 30 – 38 = 110 – 38 = 72.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

64. Simplify: 3.2 × 1.5 + 4.8 ÷ 0.6
(a) 11.8 (b) 12.8 (c) 13.8 (d) 10.8
Answer: (b) 12.8
Solution: Multiplication and division first: 3.2 × 1.5 = 4.8 and 4.8 ÷ 0.6 = 8. Addition: 4.8 + 8 = 12.8.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

65. Simplify: [{(8 ÷ 4) of 3} × 2] – 7
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (c) 5
Solution: Innermost: 8 ÷ 4 = 2. Then 2 of 3 = 6. Next 6 × 2 = 12. Finally 12 – 7 = 5.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

66. Simplify: (2 1/4 – 1 1/6) ÷ 1 1/8
(a) 1 (b) 27/26 (c) 25/27 (d) 26/27
Answer: (d) 26/27
Solution: Subtract inside bracket: 9/4 – 7/6 = 13/12. Divide by 1 1/8 = 9/8: (13/12) × (8/9) = (13 × 2)/(3 × 9) = 26/27.
Source: NCERT Class 7

67. Simplify: ∛27 + ∛64 – √25
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Calculate roots: ∛27 = 3, ∛64 = 4, and √25 = 5. Expression: 3 + 4 – 5 = 2.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

68. Simplify: 0.6 ÷ 0.2 × 0.5 + 1

70. Simplify: [12 + (3 × 5 – 7) ÷ 2] × 2
(a) 28 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 32
Answer: (d) 32
Solution: Innermost bracket: 3 × 5 – 7 = 15 – 7 = 8. Division: 8 ÷ 2 = 4. Square bracket: 12 + 4 = 16. Finally 16 × 2 = 32.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

71. Simplify: (7/9 of 27) ÷ 3 + 2
(a) 9 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 10
Answer: (a) 9
Solution: "Of" first inside bracket: 7/9 × 27 = 21. Division: 21 ÷ 3 = 7. Finally 7 + 2 = 9.

72. Simplify: 2² + 3² + (√49 – 1)²
(a) 45 (b) 49 (c) 51 (d) 53
Answer: (b) 49
Solution: Powers and roots: 2² = 4, 3² = 9, and (√49 – 1)² = (7 – 1)² = 6² = 36. Sum: 4 + 9 + 36 = 49.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

73. Simplify: (4/3 + 2/3) ÷ 2 × 5/2
(a) 2.0 (b) 3.0 (c) 2.5 (d) 3.5
Answer: (c) 2.5
Solution: Bracket sum: 4/3 + 2/3 = 6/3 = 2. Left to right: 2 ÷ 2 = 1, then 1 × 5/2 = 2.5.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

74. Simplify: 12.5% of 1600 + 33 1/3% of 270 – 16 2/3% of 240
(a) 240 (b) 260 (c) 270 (d) 250
Answer: (d) 250
Solution: Fraction equivalents: 12.5% = 1/8 (1/8 × 1600 = 200), 33 1/3% = 1/3 (1/3 × 270 = 90), 16 2/3% = 1/6 (1/6 × 240 = 40). Expression: 200 + 90 – 40 = 250.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

75. Simplify: (3.5 × 2.4 – 1.2) ÷ 1.5 + 6
(a) 10.4 (b) 10.8 (c) 11.2 (d) 9.8
Answer: (b) 10.8
Solution: Bracket: 3.5 × 2.4 = 8.4, and 8.4 – 1.2 = 7.2. Division: 7.2 ÷ 1.5 = 4.8. Finally 4.8 + 6 = 10.8.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
