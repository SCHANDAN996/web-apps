Set 1: Simplification MCQs (Easy to Medium)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Simplify: 12 + 8 ÷ 4
(a) 5 (b) 14 (c) 20 (d) 17
Answer: (b) 14
Solution:
Division first: 8 ÷ 4 = 2. Then addition: 12 + 2 = 14.
Source: NCERT Class 6

2. Simplify: 20 – 3 × 5
(a) 5 (b) 85 (c) 17 (d) 15
Answer: (a) 5
Solution:
Multiplication before subtraction: 3 × 5 = 15, then 20 – 15 = 5.
Source: NCERT Class 6

3. Simplify: 18 ÷ 3 × 2
(a) 3 (b) 12 (c) 2 (d) 6
Answer: (b) 12
Solution:
Division and multiplication are equal priority; go left to right: 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 6 × 2 = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 7

4. Simplify: 30 – 5 + 2
(a) 23 (b) 27 (c) 33 (d) 37
Answer: (b) 27
Solution:
Addition and subtraction are equal; work left to right: 30 – 5 = 25, then 25 + 2 = 27.
Source: NCERT Class 6

5. Simplify: 4 ÷ 2 of 2
(a) 1 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 1
Solution:
"Of" has higher priority than division: 2 of 2 = 4, then 4 ÷ 4 = 1.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

6. Simplify: 3 of 4 ÷ 6
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 12
Answer: (a) 2
Solution:
"Of" first: 3 of 4 = 12, then 12 ÷ 6 = 2.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

7. Simplify: (2 + 3) × 4
(a) 14 (b) 20 (c) 24 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 20
Solution:
Bracket first: 2 + 3 = 5, then 5 × 4 = 20.
Source: NCERT Class 6

8. Simplify: 10 – (4 – 1)
(a) 7 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 13
Answer: (a) 7
Solution:
Inner bracket first: 4 – 1 = 3, then 10 – 3 = 7.
Source: NCERT Class 6

9. Simplify: 2 × {3 + (4 – 2)}
(a) 10 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 14
Answer: (a) 10
Solution:
Innermost bracket (4 – 2) = 2, then 3 + 2 = 5, finally 2 × 5 = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 7

10. Simplify: 0.5 + 1/4
(a) 0.75 (b) 0.25 (c) 3/5 (d) 2/3
Answer: (a) 0.75
Solution:
Convert to fractions: 0.5 = 1/2, so 1/2 + 1/4 = 3/4 = 0.75.
Source: NCERT Class 7

11. Simplify: 1/2 ÷ 0.25
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 0.5 (d) 0.125
Answer: (a) 2
Solution:
0.25 = 1/4. 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 2.
Source: NCERT Class 7

12. Simplify: √16 + 3²
(a) 13 (b) 25 (c) 7 (d) 10
Answer: (a) 13
Solution:
Orders first: √16 = 4, 3² = 9, then 4 + 9 = 13.
Source: NCERT Class 8

13. Simplify: 5 + 2 × 3 – 1
(a) 10 (b) 14 (c) 12 (d) 16
Answer: (a) 10
Solution:
Multiplication first: 2 × 3 = 6. Then 5 + 6 – 1 = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 6

14. Simplify: 100 ÷ 4 × 5
(a) 125 (b) 5 (c) 80 (d) 20
Answer: (a) 125
Solution:
Left to right: 100 ÷ 4 = 25, 25 × 5 = 125.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

15. Simplify: 8 ÷ 2(2 + 2)
(a) 16 (b) 1 (c) 8 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 16
Solution:
Bracket first: (2 + 2) = 4. Expression becomes 8 ÷ 2 × 4. Left to right: 8 ÷ 2 = 4, 4 × 4 = 16. (Implicit multiplication after bracket is treated as normal multiplication.)
Source: Viral / BODMAS Application

16. Simplify: 15 ÷ 3 of 5

Answer: (a) 1
Solution:
"Of" first: 3 of 5 = 15. Then 15 ÷ 15 = 1.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

Medium Level (Q21–Q25)

21. Simplify: [20 – {4 + (6 ÷ 3)}] ÷ 2
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 6
Answer: (a) 7
Solution:
Innermost bracket: (6 ÷ 3) = 2. Then {4 + 2} = 6. Next [20 – 6] = 14. Finally 14 ÷ 2 = 7.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-I Pattern

22. Simplify: 5/6 of 24 + 3/4 ÷ 1/8 – 5
(a) 19 (b) 21 (c) 23 (d) 25
Answer: (b) 21
Solution:
"Of" first: 5/6 × 24 = 20. Next division: 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8/1 = 6. Then expression: 20 + 6 – 5 = 21.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

23. Simplify: (2 1/2 + 3 1/3) × 6/5
(a) 7 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 7
Solution:
Convert mixed fractions: 2 1/2 = 5/2, 3 1/3 = 10/3. Sum: 5/2 + 10/3 = (15 + 20)/6 = 35/6. Multiply: 35/6 × 6/5 = 35/5 = 7.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

24. Simplify: 125% of 400 + 1/5 of 750 – 100
(a) 550 (b) 600 (c) 650 (d) 700
Answer: (a) 550
Solution:
125% of 400 = 1.25 × 400 = 500. 1/5 of 750 = 150. Then 500 + 150 – 100 = 550.
Source: Banking (RRB) Pattern

25. Simplify: [ { (12 ÷ 4) × 3 } + 5 ] × 2 – 6
(a) 28 (b) 24 (c) 32 (d) 22
Answer: (d) 22
Solution:
Innermost bracket: (12 ÷ 4) = 3, then 3 × 3 = 9. Next {9} + 5 = 14. Then [14] × 2 = 28. Finally 28 – 6 = 22.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern
