76. Simplify: 45 ÷ 9 × 3 + 2 – 5
(a) 12 (b) 15 (c) 8 (d) 10
Answer: (a) 12
Solution: Common Trap: Multiplying 9 × 3 = 27 first gives 45 ÷ 27 – 3 (incorrect). Division and multiplication share equal precedence and are evaluated from left to right: (45 ÷ 9) × 3 = 5 × 3 = 15, then 15 + 2 – 5 = 12.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

77. Simplify: (24 – 6 of 3) × 2 + 1
(a) 11 (b) 13 (c) 15 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 13
Solution: "Of" first inside bracket: 6 of 3 = 18. Bracket: 24 – 18 = 6. Multiplication & addition: 6 × 2 + 1 = 13.
Source: NCERT Class 7

78. Simplify: 2.4 + 1.6 × 2 – 3.2 ÷ 0.8
(a) 2.4 (b) 3.2 (c) 1.6 (d) 4.0
Answer: (c) 1.6
Solution: Multiplication and division first: 1.6 × 2 = 3.2 and 3.2 ÷ 0.8 = 4. Expression: 2.4 + 3.2 – 4 = 5.6 – 4 = 1.6.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

79. Simplify: 5/6 of 48 ÷ 4 + 3/5
(a) 9 3/5 (b) 10 1/5 (c) 11 3/5 (d) 10 3/5
Answer: (d) 10 3/5
Solution: "Of" first: 5/6 of 48 = 40. Division: 40 ÷ 4 = 10. Finally 10 + 3/5 = 10 3/5.
Source: RRB Group D Pattern

80. Simplify: [36 – {12 + (8 ÷ 4)}] × 2
(a) 44 (b) 40 (c) 48 (d) 52
Answer: (a) 44
Solution: Innermost bracket: 8 ÷ 4 = 2. Then {12 + 2} = 14. Next [36 – 14] = 22. Finally 22 × 2 = 44.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

81. Simplify: 0.125 + 3/4 – 0.5
(a) 0.250 (b) 0.375 (c) 0.500 (d) 0.625
Answer: (b) 0.375
Solution: Decimals: 3/4 = 0.75. Expression: 0.125 + 0.75 – 0.5 = 0.875 – 0.5 = 0.375.
Source: NCERT Class 7

82. Simplify: (√64 + √36) × (√49 – √16)
(a) 36 (b) 38 (c) 42 (d) 45
Answer: (c) 42
Solution: Roots: √64 = 8, √36 = 6 (sum = 14); √49 = 7, √16 = 4 (diff = 3). Product: 14 × 3 = 42.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

83. Simplify: 3 1/4 ÷ 2 1/2 of 1 3/5
(a) 1/2 (b) 3/4 (c) 5/8 (d) 13/16
Answer: (d) 13/16
Solution: Improper fractions: 3 1/4 = 13/4, 2 1/2 = 5/2, 1 3/5 = 8/5. 'Of' first: 5/2 × 8/5 = 4. Division: 13/4 ÷ 4 = 13/16.
Source: NCERT Class 7

84. Simplify: (2.5 × 1.6 + 0.4) ÷ 0.2
(a) 22 (b) 20 (c) 24 (d) 18
Answer: (a) 22
Solution: Inside bracket: 2.5 × 1.6 = 4.0, and 4.0 + 0.4 = 4.4. Division: 4.4 ÷ 0.2 = 22.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

85. Simplify: 4/7 of ( 14/15 ÷ 2/3 ) + 1/5
(a) 4/5 (b) 1 (c) 1 1/5 (d) 3/5
Answer: (b) 1
Solution: Inside bracket: 14/15 ÷ 2/3 = 14/15 × 3/2 = 7/5. 'Of': 4/7 × 7/5 = 4/5. Sum: 4/5 + 1/5 = 1.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

86. Simplify: 15% of 460 + 25% of 280 – 20% of 250
(a) 69 (b) 79 (c) 89 (d) 99
Answer: (c) 89
Solution: Calculate percentages: 15% of 460 = 69, 25% of 280 = 70, and 20% of 250 = 50. Expression: 69 + 70 – 50 = 89.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

87. Simplify: (1.44)¹⁄² × (0.064)¹⁄³ + (0.25)¹⁄²
(a) 0.88 (b) 1.02 (c) 0.94 (d) 0.98
Answer: (d) 0.98
Solution: Roots: √1.44 = 1.2, ∛0.064 = 0.4, √0.25 = 0.5. Multiplication first: 1.2 × 0.4 = 0.48. Sum: 0.48 + 0.5 = 0.98.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

88. Simplify: 18 ÷ 9 of 2 × 3 + 1
(a) 4 (b) 13 (c) 7 (d) 10
Answer: (a) 4
Solution: Psychological Trap: Dividing first gives (18 ÷ 9) = 2, then 2 of 2 = 4 and 4 × 3 + 1 = 13 (incorrect). By BODMAS, "Of" takes priority over division: 9 of 2 = 18, then 18 ÷ 18 = 1, giving 1 × 3 + 1 = 4.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

89. Simplify: (4 2/5 – 2 1/3) ÷ 1 1/15
(a) 1 1/2 (b) 1 15/16 (c) 2 (d) 1 3/4
Answer: (b) 1 15/16
Solution: Improper fractions: 4 2/5 = 22/5 and 2 1/3 = 7/3. Difference: (66 – 35)/15 = 31/15. Divide by 1 1/15 = 16/15: (31/15) × (15/16) = 31/16 = 1 15/16.
Source: NCERT Class 7

90. Simplify: (3.1 × 2.5 + 1.25) ÷ 2.5
(a) 4.2 (b) 4.6 (c) 3.6 (d) 3.8
Answer: (c) 3.6
Solution: Inside bracket: 3.1 × 2.5 = 7.75, and 7.75 + 1.25 = 9.0. Division: 9.0 ÷ 2.5 = 3.6.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

91. Simplify: [ { ( 25 ÷ 5 ) of 4 } + 3 ] – 10
(a) 11 (b) 15 (c) 9 (d) 13
Answer: (d) 13
Solution: Innermost bracket: 25 ÷ 5 = 5. Then 5 of 4 = 20. Next 20 + 3 = 23. Finally 23 – 10 = 13.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

92. Simplify: 2/3 + 1/6 of ( 4/5 ÷ 2/15 ) – 1/2
(a) 1 1/6 (b) 1 1/3 (c) 1 1/2 (d) 1 2/3
Answer: (a) 1 1/6
Solution: Bracket division: 4/5 ÷ 2/15 = 4/5 × 15/2 = 6. 'Of': 1/6 of 6 = 1. Expression: 2/3 + 1 – 1/2 = 4/6 + 6/6 – 3/6 = 7/6 = 1 1/6.
Source: NCERT Class 7

93. Simplify: 0.8 × 0.5 ÷ 0.2 + 0.6
(a) 2.2 (b) 2.6 (c) 2.0 (d) 2.8
Answer: (b) 2.6
Solution: Multiply and divide left to right: 0.8 × 0.5 = 0.4, then 0.4 ÷ 0.2 = 2. Finally 2 + 0.6 = 2.6.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

94. Simplify: (5/6 of 36) ÷ (2/3 of 9) + 1
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 7
Answer: (c) 6
Solution: Evaluate brackets: 5/6 of 36 = 30 and 2/3 of 9 = 6. Division: 30 ÷ 6 = 5. Finally 5 + 1 = 6.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

95. Simplify: √121 – ∛216 + √64

97. Simplify: 12 1/2% of 640 + 8 1/3% of 720

Solution: Fractions: 12 1/2% = 1/8 (1/8 × 640 = 80) and 8 1/3% = 1/12 (1/12 × 720 = 60). Sum: 80 + 60 = 140.

98. Simplify: [ { (18 ÷ 3) × 2 } – 5 ] of 3
(a) 18 (b) 24 (c) 21 (d) 27
Answer: (c) 21
Solution: Innermost: 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Multiply: 6 × 2 = 12. Subtract: 12 – 5 = 7. Finally 7 of 3 = 21.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

99. Simplify: 3/8 ÷ (5/6 – 1/3) × 4/3
(a) 1/2 (b) 3/4 (c) 5/8 (d) 1
Answer: (d) 1
Solution: Bracket: 5/6 – 1/3 = 1/2. Division: 3/8 ÷ 1/2 = 3/8 × 2 = 3/4. Finally 3/4 × 4/3 = 1.
Source: NCERT Class 7

100. Simplify: 1.8 × 1.8 + 0.2 × 0.2 + 2 × 1.8 × 0.2
(a) 3.6 (b) 4.0 (c) 4.2 (d) 3.2
Answer: (b) 4.0
Solution: Identity a² + 2ab + b² = (a + b)². Here a = 1.8 and b = 0.2. So (1.8 + 0.2)² = 2.0² = 4.0.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
