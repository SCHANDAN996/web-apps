Flashcards: Simplification

Card 1
Front: What’s the secret code BODMAS that every math problem obeys?
Back: B = Brackets, O = Of/Orders (powers & roots), D = Division, M = Multiplication, A = Addition, S = Subtraction. It’s the unbreakable order of operations.

Card 2
Front: Division and multiplication are standing side by side. Who moves first?
Back: Neither is “more important.” You go from left to right, whichever appears first. Like a relay race where the baton passes along the line.

Card 3
Front: Is “of” just a fancy way of saying “multiply”?
Back: No! “Of” is stronger—it acts like an invisible bracket and must be solved before division and multiplication. E.g., 4 ÷ 2 of 2 → 2 of 2 = 4, then 4 ÷ 4 = 1.

Card 4
Front: Solve 10 – 2 × 3. Why does your gut scream “24” but the truth is 4?
Back: Multiplication hijacks the show first: 2 × 3 = 6, then 10 – 6 = 4. The left-to-right instinct tricks you if you ignore BODMAS.

Card 5
Front: What’s the infamous “3-second trap” hidden in 18 ÷ 3 × 2?
Back: The urge to multiply 3 × 2 first gives 18 ÷ 6 = 3, but the correct path is 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 6 × 2 = 12. Left-to-right for equal powers saves the day.

Card 6
Front: You see a nest of brackets: [20 – {4 + (6 ÷ 3)}]. Where do you start?
Back: Always from the innermost bracket: (6 ÷ 3) = 2, then {4 + 2} = 6, finally [20 – 6] = 14. Like peeling an onion from the inside out.

Card 7
Front: Simplify: 2 + {3 × (4 – 2)} without getting lost.
Back: Innermost first: 4 – 2 = 2; then 3 × 2 = 6; finally 2 + 6 = 8.

Card 8
Front: 30 – 5 + 2 = 27, but if you add first you get 23. Why is 27 right?
Back: Addition and subtraction are equal partners. When they stand together, you work left to right: 30 – 5 = 25, then 25 + 2 = 27. No jumping the queue.

Card 9
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic attack! How do you remember the left-to-right rule for D-M and A-S using a grumpy gatekeeper?
Back: Picture “Bhola Of Delhi Mandir” shouting: “Brackets kholo, Order lo, Divide karo, Multiply karo, Add karo, Subtract karo. Left se Right chalo, nahi to bhool jaoge!” The weird image sticks.

Card 10
Front: Fractions and decimals are mixed: 0.5 + 1/4. What’s the golden rescue rule?
Back: Convert everything to fractions for perfect accuracy: 0.5 = 1/2, so 1/2 + 1/4 = 3/4. Decimals can be sneaky; fractions never lie.

Card 11
Front: Crack 1/2 ÷ 0.25 × 1/3 step by step.
Back: First, 0.25 = 1/4. Then 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 2. Finally, 2 × 1/3 = 2/3.

Card 12
Front: √16 + 3² looks like one big operation. What’s the catch?
Back: Powers and roots are “Orders” (the O in BODMAS). Solve them individually first: √16 = 4, 3² = 9, then add → 13. Never apply the root to the whole sum.

Card 13
Front: What’s the hidden danger of the word “of” in 4 ÷ 2 of 2?
Back: “Of” creates an invisible bracket: 2 of 2 = 4. Then 4 ÷ 4 = 1. Treating it as normal multiplication gives the wrong 4.

Card 14
Front: In BODMAS, what does the letter “O” really stand for?
Back: “Of” (like half of 10) and also Orders (exponents, roots). It’s the second highest priority after Brackets.

Card 15
Front: Can Addition ever beat Subtraction in a simplification battle?
Back: Never. They are equals. When both appear, the one on the left goes first. It’s not about who’s stronger, it’s about who’s standing first in line.

Card 16
Front: Simplify: [12 ÷ (4 – 1)] × 2. What’s the final number?
Back: Innermost bracket: (4 – 1) = 3; then [12 ÷ 3] = 4; finally 4 × 2 = 8.

Card 17
Front: 🧠 Another mnemonic: How does a relay race of four friends explain left-to-right order?
Back: Addy (Addition), Subby (Subtraction), Multi (Multiplication), and Divvy (Division) run a relay. When equal-strength runners stand together, the one on the left runs first. Like a conveyor belt: slide left to right.

Card 18
Front: Solve 5 of 6 ÷ 3 + 1 in your head. Can you get 11 without a pen?
Back: 5 of 6 = 30 (of first); 30 ÷ 3 = 10; 10 + 1 = 11. Yes, if you respect the sequence.
