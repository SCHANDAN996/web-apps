Set 6: Simplification MCQs (Hard / Multi-step & Traps)
Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Simplify: (5/9 of 27) ÷ (3/7 of 14) + (2 1/2 - 1 3/4) × 4/5
(a) 29/10 (b) 31/10 (c) 27/10 (d) 33/10
Answer: (b) 31/10
Solution: 5/9 of 27 = 15, 3/7 of 14 = 6, 15 ÷ 6 = 5/2. 2 1/2 - 1 3/4 = 5/2 - 7/4 = 3/4. 3/4 × 4/5 = 3/5. Sum = 5/2 + 3/5 = (25 + 6)/10 = 31/10.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

127. Simplify: [12 + (3 × 4 - 2) ÷ 5] of 2 - 3
(a) 25 (b) 23 (c) 27 (d) 21
Answer: (a) 25
Solution: Inside parentheses: 3 × 4 = 12, 12 - 2 = 10, 10 ÷ 5 = 2. Bracket: 12 + 2 = 14. "of": 14 of 2 = 28. Then 28 - 3 = 25.
Source: NCERT Class 8

128. Simplify: (2.6)² - (1.4)²
(a) 5.2 (b) 4.8 (c) 4.4 (d) 5.6
Answer: (b) 4.8
Solution: Common Trap: Calculating 2.6² = 6.76 and 1.4² = 1.96 manually is time-consuming and prone to arithmetic error. Using algebraic identity a² – b² = (a – b)(a + b) = (2.6 – 1.4)(2.6 + 1.4) = 1.2 × 4.0 = 4.8.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

129. Simplify: 1 + 1/(1 + 1/(1 + 1/2))
(a) 7/5 (b) 8/5 (c) 9/5 (d) 6/5
Answer: (b) 8/5
Solution: Innermost: 1 + 1/2 = 3/2. 1/(3/2) = 2/3. 1 + 2/3 = 5/3. 1/(5/3) = 3/5. 1 + 3/5 = 8/5.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

130. Simplify: (4/9 of 3/5) ÷ (2/15 of 5/6) + (1/2 - 1/3) × 12
(a) 21/5 (b) 23/5 (c) 22/5 (d) 24/5
Answer: (c) 22/5
Solution: 4/9 × 3/5 = 12/45 = 4/15. 2/15 × 5/6 = 10/90 = 1/9. 4/15 ÷ 1/9 = 4/15 × 9 = 36/15 = 12/5. (1/2 - 1/3) = 1/6, ×12 = 2. Sum = 12/5 + 2 = 12/5 + 10/5 = 22/5.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

131. Simplify: [{(16 ÷ 8) of 3} + 7] × 2 - 5
(a) 25 (b) 23 (c) 19 (d) 21
Answer: (d) 21
Solution: Innermost: 16 ÷ 8 = 2. 2 of 3 = 6. 6 + 7 = 13. 13 × 2 = 26. 26 - 5 = 21.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

132. Simplify: (0.6̅ + 0.3̅) × 1.8 ÷ 0.9

133. Simplify: √(2.25) + ∛(0.064) ÷ 0.4 × 0.5

Answer: (a) 2

134. Simplify: (3 1/2 - 1 3/4) ÷ (7/4) + (2/5 of 15/6)
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (c) 2
Solution: 3 1/2 - 1 3/4 = 7/2 - 7/4 = 7/4. 7/4 ÷ 7/4 = 1. 2/5 × 15/6 = 30/30 = 1. Sum = 1 + 1 = 2.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

135. Simplify: (125% of 64) ÷ (0.5 of 8) + (16 2/3% of 180)
(a) 60 (b) 55 (c) 45 (d) 50
Answer: (d) 50
Solution: 125% of 64 = 80. 0.5 of 8 = 4, 80 ÷ 4 = 20. 16 2/3% = 1/6, 1/6 × 180 = 30. Sum = 20 + 30 = 50.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

136. Simplify: [ (7/8 - 3/4) ÷ 1/16 ] of 5
(a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 5 (d) 20
Answer: (a) 10
Solution: 7/8 - 3/4 = 7/8 - 6/8 = 1/8. 1/8 ÷ 1/16 = 2. 2 of 5 = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 7

137. Simplify: (2.4 × 1.6 + 1.6 × 0.6) ÷ 2
(a) 1.6 (b) 3.2 (c) 2.4 (d) 2.0
Answer: (c) 2.4
Solution: Factor 1.6: 1.6 × (2.4 + 0.6) = 1.6 × 3 = 4.8. 4.8 ÷ 2 = 2.4.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

138. Simplify: 1/(2 + 1/(3 + 1/4))
(a) 19/30 (b) 17/30 (c) 11/30 (d) 13/30
Answer: (d) 13/30
Solution: Psychological Trap: Inverting fractions from top to bottom causes inversion errors. Always evaluate continued fractions strictly from the bottom up: 3 + 1/4 = 13/4, then 1/(13/4) = 4/13, next 2 + 4/13 = 30/13, and finally 1/(30/13) = 13/30.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

139. Simplify: (5/6 + 1/2) ÷ (1/4 of 2/3) + 1/3
(a) 20/3 (b) 25/3 (c) 23/3 (d) 26/3
Answer: (b) 25/3
Solution: 5/6 + 1/2 = 5/6 + 3/6 = 8/6 = 4/3. 1/4 of 2/3 = 1/4 × 2/3 = 1/6. 4/3 ÷ 1/6 = 4/3 × 6 = 8. 8 + 1/3 = 25/3.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

140. Simplify: (3.14 + 2.86) × 0.5 ÷ 0.25

Solution: 3.14 + 2.86 = 6. 6 × 0.5 = 3. 3 ÷ 0.25 = 12.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

141. Simplify: [{(24 ÷ 6) of 3} ÷ 2] + (3/4 - 1/2) × 8/5
(a) 31/5 (b) 28/5 (c) 6 (d) 32/5
Answer: (d) 32/5
Solution: 24 ÷ 6 = 4, 4 of 3 = 12, 12 ÷ 2 = 6. 3/4 - 1/2 = 1/4, × 8/5 = 2/5. Sum = 6 + 2/5 = 30/5 + 2/5 = 32/5.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

142. Simplify: (2.5² - 1.5²) ÷ (2.5 - 1.5)
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 3.5 (d) 4.5
Answer: (a) 4
Solution: (2.5² - 1.5²) = (2.5 - 1.5)(2.5 + 1.5) = 1 × 4 = 4. Denominator (2.5 - 1.5) = 1, result = 4.
Source: NCERT Class 8

143. Simplify: (4/9 of 3/8) ÷ (5/12 of 6/15) + 3/10

144. Simplify: (0.83̅ - 0.16̅) × 1.5

145. Simplify: √(0.81) + ∛(0.027) × √(0.25)

146. Simplify: (12 1/2% of 320) ÷ (33 1/3% of 72) + 1/2

Solution: 12 1/2% = 1/8 → 320/8 = 40. 33 1/3% = 1/3 → 72/3 = 24. 40 ÷ 24 = 5/3. 5/3 + 1/2 = 10/6 + 3/6 = 13/6.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

147. Simplify: (3/5 of 5/9) ÷ (2/3 of 3/4) + 1/2

Solution: 3/5 × 5/9 = 15/45 = 1/3. 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2. 1/3 ÷ 1/2 = 2/3. 2/3 + 1/2 = 4/6 + 3/6 = 7/6.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

148. Simplify: (∛216 + √144 - ∛512) × (√49 - √36)

Answer: (c) 10
Solution: ∛216 = 6, √144 = 12, ∛512 = 8 → 6 + 12 - 8 = 10. √49 - √36 = 7 - 6 = 1. Product = 10 × 1 = 10.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

149. Simplify: (1.8 × 1.8 - 0.2 × 0.2) ÷ (1.8 - 0.2) + 0.4

Answer: (a) 2.4
Solution: (1.8² - 0.2²) = (1.8 - 0.2)(1.8 + 0.2) = 1.6 × 2 = 3.2. Divide by (1.8 - 0.2) = 1.6 gives 2. 2 + 0.4 = 2.4.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
