Set 5: Simplification MCQs (Hard / Advanced Foundation)
Questions 101–125: Hard

101. Simplify: 24 ÷ 8 × 3 + 5 – 2
(a) 12 (b) 4 (c) 10 (d) 14
Answer: (a) 12
Solution: Common Trap: Multiplying 8 × 3 = 24 first gives 24 ÷ 24 = 1, then 1 + 5 – 2 = 4 (incorrect). In BODMAS, division and multiplication have equal priority and are evaluated left to right: (24 ÷ 8) × 3 = 3 × 3 = 9, then 9 + 5 – 2 = 12.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

102. Simplify: (18 – 4 of 3) × 2 + 2
(a) 10 (b) 14 (c) 22 (d) 18
Answer: (b) 14
Solution: "Of" first: 4 of 3 = 12. Bracket: 18 – 12 = 6. Multiply & add: 6 × 2 + 2 = 12 + 2 = 14.
Source: NCERT Class 7

103. Simplify: 3.5 + 1/4 of 16 – 0.5
(a) 6.5 (b) 7.5 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (c) 7
Solution: "Of" first: 1/4 of 16 = 4. Expression: 3.5 + 4 – 0.5 = 7.5 – 0.5 = 7.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

104. Simplify: [45 – {15 + (18 ÷ 3)}] ÷ 3
(a) 6 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 8
Answer: (d) 8
Solution: Innermost: 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Curly: {15 + 6} = 21. Square: [45 – 21] = 24. Division: 24 ÷ 3 = 8.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

105. Simplify: 0.75 ÷ 0.15 × 2 + 0.5
(a) 10.5 (b) 11.5 (c) 9.5 (d) 10.0
Answer: (a) 10.5
Solution: Left to right: 0.75 ÷ 0.15 = 5. Multiply: 5 × 2 = 10. Add: 10 + 0.5 = 10.5.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

106. Simplify: (3/5 of 45) + (2/3 of 36) – 10
(a) 37 (b) 41 (c) 39 (d) 43
Answer: (b) 41
Solution: "Of" operations: 3/5 of 45 = 27, and 2/3 of 36 = 24. Expression: 27 + 24 – 10 = 51 – 10 = 41.
Source: NCERT Class 7

107. Simplify: √169 – ∛125 + √36
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (c) 14
Solution: Roots: √169 = 13, ∛125 = 5, √36 = 6. Expression: 13 – 5 + 6 = 8 + 6 = 14.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

108. Simplify: 3 1/3 + 2 1/2 – 1 1/4
(a) 4 1/12 (b) 3 11/12 (c) 4 5/12 (d) 4 7/12
Answer: (d) 4 7/12
Solution: Improper fractions: 10/3 + 5/2 – 5/4. Common denominator 12: (40 + 30 – 15)/12 = 55/12 = 4 7/12.
Source: NCERT Class 7

109. Simplify: (2.4 × 1.5) ÷ 0.6 + 1.6
(a) 7.6 (b) 8.4 (c) 6.8 (d) 7.2
Answer: (a) 7.6
Solution: Inside bracket: 2.4 × 1.5 = 3.6. Division: 3.6 ÷ 0.6 = 6. Addition: 6 + 1.6 = 7.6.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

110. Simplify: 25% of 480 + 12.5% of 640 – 10% of 300
(a) 160 (b) 170 (c) 180 (d) 190
Answer: (b) 170
Solution: Percentages: 25% of 480 = 120, 12.5% of 640 = 80, 10% of 300 = 30. Sum: 120 + 80 – 30 = 170.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

111. Simplify: (5/6 of 24) ÷ (1/3 of 18) + 2
(a) 4 2/3 (b) 6 (c) 5 1/3 (d) 4 1/3
Answer: (c) 5 1/3
Solution: "Of" in brackets: 5/6 of 24 = 20 and 1/3 of 18 = 6. Division: 20 ÷ 6 = 10/3. Sum: 10/3 + 2 = 16/3 = 5 1/3.
Source: NCERT Class 8

112. Simplify: [ { (15 ÷ 5) of 4 } + 3 ] × 2 – 6
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 28 (d) 24
Answer: (d) 24
Solution: Innermost: 15 ÷ 5 = 3. Next 3 of 4 = 12. Add 3: 12 + 3 = 15. Multiply: 15 × 2 = 30. Subtract: 30 – 6 = 24.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

113. Simplify: (0.2)² + (0.8)² + 2 × (0.2) × (0.8)
(a) 1.0 (b) 0.64 (c) 1.44 (d) 0.96
Answer: (a) 1.0
Solution: Identity a² + 2ab + b² = (a + b)². Here a = 0.2 and b = 0.8. So (0.2 + 0.8)² = 1.0² = 1.0.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

114. Simplify: (2 1/2 ÷ 1 1/4) × (3 1/3 ÷ 1 1/9)
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 5
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: First bracket: 5/2 ÷ 5/4 = 2. Second bracket: 10/3 ÷ 10/9 = 3. Product: 2 × 3 = 6.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

115. Simplify: (√81 + √16) × (√25 – √9)
(a) 20 (b) 24 (c) 26 (d) 30
Answer: (c) 26
Solution: Roots: √81 = 9, √16 = 4 (sum = 13); √25 = 5, √9 = 3 (diff = 2). Product: 13 × 2 = 26.
Source: NCERT Class 7

116. Simplify: 1.25 × 4.8 + 3.4 ÷ 1.7
(a) 7.4 (b) 7.6 (c) 8.2 (d) 8.0
Answer: (d) 8.0
Solution: Multiply: 1.25 × 4.8 = 6. Divide: 3.4 ÷ 1.7 = 2. Sum: 6 + 2 = 8.0.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

117. Simplify: (4/7 of 35) ÷ (2/5 of 15) + 1/2
(a) 3 5/6 (b) 3 1/6 (c) 4 1/6 (d) 2 5/6
Answer: (a) 3 5/6
Solution: Brackets: 4/7 of 35 = 20 and 2/5 of 15 = 6. Division: 20 ÷ 6 = 10/3. Sum: 10/3 + 1/2 = 20/6 + 3/6 = 23/6 = 3 5/6.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

118. Simplify: ∛0.027 + ∛0.008 × √0.25
(a) 0.3 (b) 0.4 (c) 0.5 (d) 0.6
Answer: (b) 0.4
Solution: Roots: ∛0.027 = 0.3, ∛0.008 = 0.2, √0.25 = 0.5. Multiply first: 0.2 × 0.5 = 0.1. Sum: 0.3 + 0.1 = 0.4.
Source: NCERT Class 8

119. Simplify: 16 2/3% of 240 + 33 1/3% of 180
(a) 90 (b) 110 (c) 100 (d) 120
Answer: (c) 100
Solution: Fractions: 16 2/3% = 1/6 (1/6 × 240 = 40) and 33 1/3% = 1/3 (1/3 × 180 = 60). Sum: 40 + 60 = 100.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

120. Simplify: [ 12 + { 3 × (4 + 2) } ÷ 3 – 4 ] ÷ 2
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 7
Answer: (d) 7
Solution: Innermost: 4 + 2 = 6. Multiply: 3 × 6 = 18. Divide: 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Square bracket: 12 + 6 – 4 = 14. Finally 14 ÷ 2 = 7.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

121. Simplify: 3 3/4 ÷ (2 1/2 of 1 1/5) + (1/4 – 1/6) × 12
(a) 9/4 (b) 7/3 (c) 11/4 (d) 5/2
Answer: (a) 9/4
Solution: 'Of' inside bracket: 5/2 × 6/5 = 3. Division: 15/4 ÷ 3 = 5/4. Second part: (1/4 – 1/6) × 12 = 1/12 × 12 = 1. Sum: 5/4 + 1 = 9/4.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

122. Simplify: [ (3 1/2 – 2 1/3) ÷ 1 1/6 ] of 2 1/4 + 1/2
(a) 2 1/4 (b) 2 3/4 (c) 3 1/4 (d) 3 1/2
Answer: (b) 2 3/4
Solution: Bracket subtraction: 7/2 – 7/3 = 7/6. Bracket division: 7/6 ÷ 7/6 = 1. 'Of' operation: 1 of 2 1/4 = 9/4. Finally 9/4 + 1/2 = 9/4 + 2/4 = 11/4 = 2 3/4.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

123. Simplify: (0.83̄ + 0.16̄) ÷ 0.3 + 0.25 × 4
(a) 4 (b) 14/3 (c) 13/3 (d) 5
Answer: (c) 13/3
Solution: Recurring decimals: 0.83̄ = 5/6 and 0.16̄ = 1/6. Sum: 5/6 + 1/6 = 1. Division: 1 ÷ 0.3 = 10/3. Multiplication: 0.25 × 4 = 1. Total: 10/3 + 1 = 13/3.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

124. Simplify: 1 + 1 / [ 1 + 1 / ( 1 + 1/2 ) ]
(a) 5/3 (b) 7/5 (c) 4/3 (d) 8/5
Answer: (d) 8/5
Solution: Psychological Trap: Inverting from top to bottom leads to calculation errors. Always evaluate continued fractions strictly from the bottom up: 1 + 1/2 = 3/2, then 1/(3/2) = 2/3, next 1 + 2/3 = 5/3, then 1/(5/3) = 3/5, and finally 1 + 3/5 = 8/5.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

125. Simplify: (5/6 × 3/7 + 2/9 ÷ 1/3) ÷ (1.25 × 0.8)
(a) 41/42 (b) 43/42 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (b) 43/42
Solution: Numerator: 5/6 × 3/7 = 5/14, and 2/9 ÷ 1/3 = 2/3. Sum: 5/14 + 2/3 = (15 + 28)/42 = 43/42. Denominator: 1.25 × 0.8 = 1.0. Result: (43/42) ÷ 1 = 43/42.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
