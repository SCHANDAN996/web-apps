Set 2: Percentage Multiple Choice Questions (Medium to Hard)
Questions 26–45: Medium | Questions 46–50: Hard

26. A number is first increased by 15% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net percentage change?
(a) 3.5% increase (b) 3.5% decrease (c) 5% increase (d) 2.5% increase
Answer: (a) 3.5% increase
Solution: Net change = a + b + (ab/100) = 15 - 10 - (15 × 10)/100 = 5 - 1.5 = +3.5% (increase).
Source: SSC GD

27. In an examination, 65% of the total examinees passed. If the number of failures is 420, find the total number of examinees.
(a) 1000 (b) 1200 (c) 1400 (d) 1500
Answer: (b) 1200
Solution: Failed percentage = 100% - 65% = 35%. Total examinees = 420 / 0.35 = 1200.
Source: SSC MTS

28. The population of a town increases by 5% annually. If its present population is 44,100, what was it 2 years ago?
(a) 38,000 (b) 39,500 (c) 40,000 (d) 42,000
Answer: (c) 40,000
Solution: Let population 2 years ago be P. P × (1 + 5/100)² = 44100 => P × (21/20)² = 44100 => P × 441/400 = 44100 => P = 40,000.
Source: RRB Group D

29. A person spends 60% of his income. If his income increases by 25% and his expenditure increases by 10%, find the percentage increase in his savings.
(a) 35% (b) 40% (c) 45% (d) 47.5%
Answer: (d) 47.5%
Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 60, savings = 40. New income = 125. New expenditure = 60 × 1.10 = 66. New savings = 125 - 66 = 59. Increase in savings = 59 - 40 = 19. Percentage increase = (19 / 40) × 100 = 47.5%.
Source: SSC CGL

30. Two numbers A and B are such that 25% of A is equal to 20% of B. What is the ratio A : B?
(a) 4 : 5 (b) 5 : 4 (c) 1 : 2 (d) 5 : 6
Answer: (a) 4 : 5
Solution: 25% of A = 20% of B => 0.25 A = 0.20 B => A / B = 20 / 25 = 4 / 5.
Source: SSC MTS

31. A reduction of 20% in the price of rice enables a person to buy 5 kg more rice for ₹160. What was the original price per kg?
(a) ₹6.40 (b) ₹8.00 (c) ₹10.00 (d) ₹7.50
Answer: (b) ₹8.00
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) ₹6.40 is the reduced (new) price! Money saved = 20% of 160 = ₹32. Reduced price = 32 / 5 = ₹6.40 per kg. Original price = 6.40 / 0.80 = ₹8.00 per kg.
Source: RRB Group D

32. The salary of an employee is first increased by 20% and then reduced by 10%. What is the net change in his salary?
(a) 10% increase (b) 8% decrease (c) 8% increase (d) 12% increase
Answer: (c) 8% increase
Solution: Net change = 20 - 10 - (20 × 10)/100 = 10 - 2 = +8% (increase).
Source: IBPS Clerk

33. A student secured 450 marks out of 600 and failed by 30 marks. What is the pass percentage?
(a) 70% (b) 72% (c) 75% (d) 80%
Answer: (d) 80%
Solution: Pass marks = 450 + 30 = 480. Pass percentage = (480 / 600) × 100 = 80%.
Source: SSC GD

34. In an election between two candidates, the winner got 55% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 120 votes. If 20% of the total votes were invalid, find the total number of votes polled.
(a) 1500 (b) 1200 (c) 1800 (d) 2000
Answer: (a) 1500
Solution: Winner received 55% and loser received 45% of valid votes. Difference = 10% of valid votes = 120 => Valid votes = 1200. Since 20% were invalid, Valid votes = 80% of total votes => Total votes = 1200 / 0.80 = 1500.
Source: SSC MTS

35. The price of petrol is increased by 25%. By what percentage should the consumption be reduced so that the expenditure on petrol does not increase?
(a) 16 2/3% (b) 20% (c) 25% (d) 15%
Answer: (b) 20%
Solution: Required reduction = [r / (100 + r)] × 100 = [25 / 125] × 100 = 20%.
Source: SSC CGL

36. A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 16% and his expenditure increases by 8%. Find the percentage increase in his savings.
(a) 32% (b) 36% (c) 40% (d) 45%
Answer: (c) 40%
Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 75, savings = 25. New income = 116. New expenditure = 75 × 1.08 = 81. New savings = 116 - 81 = 35. Increase in savings = 35 - 25 = 10. Percentage increase = (10 / 25) × 100 = 40%.
Source: SSC CHSL

37. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 25% and its denominator is decreased by 20%, the fraction becomes 15/16. Find the original fraction.
(a) 4/5 (b) 12/25 (c) 9/20 (d) 3/5
Answer: (d) 3/5
Solution: Let original fraction be N/D. (N × 1.25) / (D × 0.80) = 15/16 => (N/D) × (125/80) = 15/16 => (N/D) × (25/16) = 15/16 => N/D = (15/16) × (16/25) = 15/25 = 3/5.
Source: RRB NTPC

38. A number is mistakenly divided by 5/6 instead of being multiplied by 5/6. What is the percentage error in the result?
(a) 44% (b) 36% (c) 56% (d) 64%
Answer: (a) 44%
Solution: Let number be x. Correct result = (5/6)x. Mistaken result = x / (5/6) = (6/5)x. Error = (6/5 - 5/6)x = (11/30)x. Percentage error = [(11/30) / (5/6)] × 100 = (11/25) × 100 = 44%.
Source: SSC CGL

39. The ratio of the incomes of A and B is 5:4, and the ratio of their expenditures is 4:3. If each saves ₹2,000, find A's income.
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹10,000 (c) ₹12,000 (d) ₹15,000
Answer: (b) ₹10,000
Solution: Let incomes be 5x and 4x, expenditures be 4y and 3y. Savings = 5x - 4y = 2000 and 4x - 3y = 2000. Ratio difference in income and expenditure parts is (5-4) = (4-3) = 1 unit. 1 unit = ₹2,000. A's income = 5 × 2000 = ₹10,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

40. In a class, 30% of the students are girls. If there are 42 boys, how many total students are there in the class?
(a) 50 (b) 55 (c) 60 (d) 70
Answer: (c) 60
Solution: Percentage of boys = 100% - 30% = 70%. Total students = 42 / 0.70 = 60.
Source: NCERT Class 8

41. A is 20% more than B, and B is 25% more than C. By what percentage is A more than C?
(a) 40% (b) 45% (c) 48% (d) 50%
Answer: (d) 50%
Solution: Let C = 100. B = 125. A = 125 × 1.20 = 150. A is (150 - 100) = 50 more than C => 50%.
Source: IBPS Clerk

42. The price of a commodity is cut by 10%. To restore it to its original value, the new price must be increased by:
(a) 11 1/9% (b) 10% (c) 9 1/11% (d) 12%
Answer: (a) 11 1/9%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) 10% is incorrect because the base is now smaller! Required increase = [r / (100 - r)] × 100 = [10 / (100 - 10)] × 100 = 100/9 = 11 1/9%.
Source: SSC GD

43. After spending 40% on rent, 15% on food, and 25% on other items, a person saves ₹4,800. What is his total income?
(a) ₹20,000 (b) ₹24,000 (c) ₹25,000 (d) ₹30,000
Answer: (b) ₹24,000
Solution: Total expenditure = 40% + 15% + 25% = 80%. Savings = 100% - 80% = 20%. 20% of income = ₹4,800 => Total income = 4800 / 0.20 = ₹24,000.
Source: SSC MTS

44. In a school, 40% of the students play cricket, 30% play football, and 10% play both games. What percentage of students play neither cricket nor football?
(a) 30% (b) 35% (c) 40% (d) 45%
Answer: (c) 40%
Solution: Students playing at least one game = n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A ∩ B) = 40 + 30 - 10 = 60%. Students playing neither = 100% - 60% = 40%.
Source: RRB Group D

45. The length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its breadth is decreased by 10%. What is the net percentage change in its area?
(a) 10% increase (b) 8% decrease (c) 12% increase (d) 8% increase
Answer: (d) 8% increase
Solution: Net percentage change = l + b + (lb/100) = 20 - 10 - (20 × 10)/100 = 10 - 2 = +8% (increase).
Source: SSC CHSL

46. A student has to secure 40% marks to pass an examination. He gets 145 marks and fails by 35 marks. What are the maximum marks of the examination?
(a) 450 (b) 400 (c) 500 (d) 550
Answer: (a) 450
Solution: Passing marks = 145 + 35 = 180. 40% of Maximum Marks = 180 => Maximum Marks = (180 / 40) × 100 = 450.
Source: SSC MTS

47. In a class of 200 students, 60% are boys and the rest are girls. If 30% of boys and 75% of girls passed the examination, find the total number of students who failed.
(a) 96 (b) 104 (c) 110 (d) 120
Answer: (b) 104
Solution: Boys = 60% of 200 = 120; Girls = 80. Failed boys = (100% - 30%) of 120 = 70% of 120 = 84. Failed girls = (100% - 75%) of 80 = 25% of 80 = 20. Total failed students = 84 + 20 = 104.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

48. The present price of a book is ₹250. It was increased by 25% from last year. What was its price last year?
(a) ₹180 (b) ₹190 (c) ₹200 (d) ₹220
Answer: (c) ₹200
Solution: Let last year's price be P. P × 1.25 = 250 => P = 250 / 1.25 = ₹200.
Source: RRB Group D

49. A person spends 20% of his income on education, 30% on food, and 10% on conveyance. If he saves ₹3,600, then what is his total income?
(a) ₹7,500 (b) ₹8,000 (c) ₹8,500 (d) ₹9,000
Answer: (d) ₹9,000
Solution: Total expenditure = 20% + 30% + 10% = 60%. Savings = 100% - 60% = 40%. 40% of income = 3600 => Income = 3600 / 0.40 = ₹9,000.
Source: IBPS Clerk

50. The value of a machine depreciates at the rate of 10% per annum. If its present value is ₹81,000, what was its value 2 years ago?
(a) ₹90,000 (b) ₹1,00,000 (c) ₹1,10,000 (d) ₹1,20,000
Answer: (b) ₹1,00,000
Solution: Let value 2 years ago be V. V × (1 - 10/100)² = 81000 => V × (9/10)² = 81000 => V × 81/100 = 81000 => V = ₹1,00,000.
Source: SSC MTS
