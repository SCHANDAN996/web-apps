76. If the price of a commodity increases by 20%, by what percentage must a household reduce its consumption so that the total expenditure remains unchanged?
(a) 16 2/3% (b) 20% (c) 25% (d) 15%
Answer: (a) 16 2/3%
Solution: Required percentage reduction = [r / (100 + r)] × 100 = [20 / (100 + 20)] × 100 = (20 / 120) × 100 = 100/6 = 16 2/3%.
Source: SSC MTS

77. A student scored 35% marks and failed by 40 marks. Another student scored 50% marks and secured 5 marks more than the pass marks. What are the maximum marks of the examination?
(a) 250 (b) 300 (c) 350 (d) 400
Answer: (b) 300
Solution: Difference in percentage = 50% - 35% = 15%. Total difference in marks = 40 + 5 = 45. 15% of Maximum Marks = 45 => Maximum Marks = (45 / 15) × 100 = 300.
Source: SSC CGL

78. A number is increased by 15% and then decreased by 15%. What is the net percentage change in the number?
(a) No change (b) 2.25% increase (c) 2.25% decrease (d) 3% decrease
Answer: (c) 2.25% decrease
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 'No change' is incorrect because the base decreases after the increase! Net change = -(r²/100)% = -(15²/100)% = -2.25% (2.25% decrease).
Source: SSC GD

79. In a town, 30% of the population are children, 45% are adults, and the remaining 5,000 residents are senior citizens. What is the total population of the town?
(a) 15,000 (b) 16,000 (c) 18,000 (d) 20,000
Answer: (d) 20,000
Solution: Percentage of senior citizens = 100% - (30% + 45%) = 25%. 25% of Total Population = 5,000 => Total Population = 5000 / 0.25 = 20,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

80. A man spends 35% of his monthly income on rent, 25% on food, and saves the remaining ₹16,000. What is his total monthly income?
(a) ₹40,000 (b) ₹36,000 (c) ₹42,000 (d) ₹45,000
Answer: (a) ₹40,000
Solution: Total expenditure = 35% + 25% = 60%. Savings = 100% - 60% = 40%. 40% of income = ₹16,000 => Total income = 16000 / 0.40 = ₹40,000.
Source: RRB Group D

81. If 10% of A is equal to 15% of B, then by what percentage is A greater than B?
(a) 40% (b) 50% (c) 60% (d) 75%
Answer: (b) 50%
Solution: 0.10 A = 0.15 B => A / B = 15 / 10 = 3 / 2 = 1.5. A is (1.5 - 1) × 100 = 50% more than B.
Source: SSC CHSL

82. The value of a car depreciates by 10% each year. If its present value is ₹72,900, what was its value 2 years ago?
(a) ₹80,000 (b) ₹85,000 (c) ₹90,000 (d) ₹1,00,000
Answer: (c) ₹90,000
Solution: Let value 2 years ago be V. V × (1 - 10/100)² = 72900 => V × (9/10)² = 72900 => V × 81/100 = 72900 => V = 72900 / 0.81 = ₹90,000.
Source: SSC MTS

83. A candidate who gets 20% marks fails by 30 marks. Another candidate who gets 32% marks obtains 42 marks more than the pass marks. What is the pass percentage?
(a) 20% (b) 22% (c) 24% (d) 25%
Answer: (d) 25%
Solution: Percentage difference = 32% - 20% = 12%. Marks difference = 30 + 42 = 72. Maximum Marks = 72 / 0.12 = 600. Pass marks = 20% of 600 + 30 = 120 + 30 = 150. Pass percentage = (150 / 600) × 100 = 25%.
Source: RRB NTPC

84. A number when increased by 25% becomes 225. What is the original number?
(a) 180 (b) 160 (c) 175 (d) 190
Answer: (a) 180
Solution: Let the number be x. 1.25 × x = 225 => x = 225 / 1.25 = 180.
Source: NCERT Class 8

85. The income of A is 25% more than that of B. By what percentage is B's income less than A's income?
(a) 16 2/3% (b) 20% (c) 25% (d) 15%
Answer: (b) 20%
Solution: Percentage less = [r / (100 + r)] × 100 = [25 / (100 + 25)] × 100 = (25 / 125) × 100 = 20%.
Source: SSC GD

86. In an election between two candidates, 10% of the total votes were invalid. The winning candidate secured 60% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 1,800 votes. Find the total number of votes polled.
(a) 8,000 (b) 9,000 (c) 10,000 (d) 12,000
Answer: (c) 10,000
Solution: Winner = 60%, Loser = 40% of valid votes. Majority = (60 - 40)% = 20% of valid votes = 1,800 => Valid votes = 1800 / 0.20 = 9,000. Total votes = 9000 / 0.90 = 10,000.
Source: SSC CGL

87. The price of sugar rises by 50%. By what percentage must consumption be reduced so that total expenditure remains the same?
(a) 25% (b) 30% (c) 50% (d) 33 1/3%
Answer: (d) 33 1/3%
Solution: Reduction % = [r / (100 + r)] × 100 = [50 / 150] × 100 = 100/3 = 33 1/3%.
Source: SSC CHSL

88. A man spends 70% of his income. His income increases by 24% and his expenditure increases by 20%. Find the percentage increase in his savings.
(a) 33 1/3% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 35%
Answer: (a) 33 1/3%
Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 70, savings = 30. New income = 124. New expenditure = 70 × 1.20 = 84. New savings = 124 - 84 = 40. Increase in savings = 40 - 30 = 10. Percentage increase = (10 / 30) × 100 = 33 1/3%.
Source: SSC MTS

89. 30% of a number when added to 40% of the same number gives 140. What is the number?
(a) 180 (b) 200 (c) 220 (d) 250
Answer: (b) 200
Solution: (30% + 40%) of x = 70% of x = 140 => 0.70x = 140 => x = 140 / 0.70 = 200.
Source: RRB Group D

90. The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the population after 2 years is 9,900, what was the initial population?
(a) 9,500 (b) 9,800 (c) 10,000 (d) 10,500
Answer: (c) 10,000
Solution: Let initial population be P. P × (1 + 0.10) × (1 - 0.10) = 9900 => P × 1.10 × 0.90 = 9900 => P × 0.99 = 9900 => P = 9900 / 0.99 = 10,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

91. Two numbers are respectively 20% and 50% more than a third number. What is the ratio between the first two numbers?
(a) 2 : 5 (b) 3 : 5 (c) 5 : 4 (d) 4 : 5
Answer: (d) 4 : 5
Solution: Let third number be 100. First number = 120, second number = 150. Ratio = 120 : 150 = 4 : 5.
Source: SSC GD

92. An article's price is reduced by 16 2/3%. To bring it back to its original price, by what percentage must the new price be increased?
(a) 20% (b) 16 2/3% (c) 25% (d) 18%
Answer: (a) 20%
Solution: 16 2/3% = 1/6. Price reduces from 6 to 5. To restore from 5 to 6, required increase = 1/5 × 100 = 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8

93. In a class of 60 students, 40% are boys. If 25% of the boys and 50% of the girls are in the school football team, how many students are NOT in the football team?
(a) 32 (b) 36 (c) 40 (d) 44
Answer: (b) 36
Solution: Boys = 40% of 60 = 24. Girls = 60 - 24 = 36. Boys in team = 25% of 24 = 6. Girls in team = 50% of 36 = 18. Total team members = 6 + 18 = 24. Students not in team = 60 - 24 = 36.
Source: SSC CGL

94. A's salary is 30% more than B's salary, and B's salary is 20% less than C's salary. If C's salary is ₹5,000, what is A's salary?
(a) ₹4,800 (b) ₹5,000 (c) ₹5,200 (d) ₹5,400
Answer: (c) ₹5,200
Solution: C = ₹5,000. B = 5000 × (1 - 0.20) = 5000 × 0.80 = ₹4,000. A = 4000 × (1 + 0.30) = 4000 × 1.30 = ₹5,200.
Source: SSC MTS

95. A number is mistakenly divided by 4/5 instead of being multiplied by 4/5. What is the percentage error in the calculation relative to the correct value?
(a) 36% (b) 44% (c) 50% (d) 56.25%
Answer: (d) 56.25%
Solution: Trap alert: Do not calculate error relative to the mistaken value! Correct value = (4/5)x = 0.8x. Mistaken value = x / (4/5) = (5/4)x = 1.25x. Error = 1.25x - 0.8x = 0.45x. Percentage error = (0.45x / 0.8x) × 100 = (45 / 80) × 100 = 56.25%.
Source: RRB NTPC

96. In a mixture of 80 litres, milk and water are in the ratio 3:2. What percentage of the mixture is water?
(a) 40% (b) 30% (c) 35% (d) 45%
Answer: (a) 40%
Solution: Total parts = 3 + 2 = 5 parts. Water part = 2/5. Percentage of water = (2 / 5) × 100 = 40%.
Source: NCERT Class 8

97. If the price of petrol increases by 33 1/3%, by what percentage must a person decrease his petrol consumption so that his expenditure remains unchanged?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 33 1/3%
Answer: (b) 25%
Solution: 33 1/3% = 1/3. Required percentage reduction = [ (1/3) / (1 + 1/3) ] × 100 = [ (1/3) / (4/3) ] × 100 = (1/4) × 100 = 25%.
Source: SSC CHSL

98. A student has to secure 33% to pass an examination. He gets 75 marks and fails by 24 marks. What are the maximum marks of the examination?
(a) 250 (b) 280 (c) 300 (d) 350
Answer: (c) 300
Solution: Passing marks = 75 + 24 = 99 marks. 33% of Maximum Marks = 99 => Maximum Marks = (99 / 33) × 100 = 300.
Source: SSC GD

99. The price of an item is increased by 15%. By what percentage should a family reduce its consumption so that its total expenditure on the item decreases by 8%?
(a) 12% (b) 15% (c) 18% (d) 20%
Answer: (d) 20%
Solution: Let initial price = 100, consumption = 100, expenditure = 10,000. New price = 115. Desired new expenditure = 10,000 × (1 - 0.08) = 9,200. New consumption = 9200 / 115 = 80. Reduction in consumption = 100 - 80 = 20%.
Source: SSC CGL

100. In a class, 60% of the students are boys and the rest are girls. If 70% of the boys and 85% of the girls passed the examination, what is the overall pass percentage of the class?
(a) 74% (b) 76% (c) 78% (d) 80%
Answer: (b) 76%
Solution: Let total students = 100. Boys = 60, Girls = 40. Passed boys = 60 × 0.70 = 42. Passed girls = 40 × 0.85 = 34. Total passed students = 42 + 34 = 76 => Overall pass percentage = 76%.
Source: Delhi Police Constable
