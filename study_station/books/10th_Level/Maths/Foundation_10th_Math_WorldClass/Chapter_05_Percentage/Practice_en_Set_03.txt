Set 3: Percentage Multiple Choice Questions (Medium to Hard)
Questions 51–70: Medium | Questions 71–75: Hard

51. If the population of a town increases at the rate of 10% per annum and its present population is 79,860, what was its population 2 years ago?
(a) 66,000 (b) 68,000 (c) 70,000 (d) 72,000
Answer: (a) 66,000
Solution: Let population 2 years ago be P. P × (1.10)² = 79860 => P × 1.21 = 79860 => P = 79860 / 1.21 = 66,000.
Source: SSC CGL

52. A person spends 80% of his income. If his income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%, find the percentage increase in his savings.
(a) 50% (b) 60% (c) 70% (d) 80%
Answer: (b) 60%
Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 80, savings = 20. New income = 120. New expenditure = 80 × 1.10 = 88. New savings = 120 - 88 = 32. Increase in savings = 32 - 20 = 12. Percentage increase = (12 / 20) × 100 = 60%.
Source: SSC CHSL

53. The price of coffee increases by 25%. By what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that the total expenditure remains unchanged?
(a) 15% (b) 18% (c) 20% (d) 25%
Answer: (c) 20%
Solution: Reduction % = [r / (100 + r)] × 100 = [25 / (100 + 25)] × 100 = (25 / 125) × 100 = 20%.
Source: SSC MTS

54. A number is first increased by 20% and then decreased by 25%. What is the net percentage change in the number?
(a) 5% decrease (b) 5% increase (c) 10% increase (d) 10% decrease
Answer: (d) 10% decrease
Solution: Net change = 20 - 25 + [20 × (-25)] / 100 = -5 - 5 = -10% (10% decrease).
Source: RRB Group D

55. In an election between two candidates, 20% of the total votes polled were invalid. The winner secured 70% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 2,400 votes. Find the total number of votes polled.
(a) 7500 (b) 8000 (c) 10000 (d) 12000
Answer: (a) 7500
Solution: Valid votes = 80% of total. Winner = 70% and loser = 30% of valid votes. Majority difference = (70 - 30)% = 40% of valid votes = 2400 => Valid votes = 2400 / 0.40 = 6000. Total votes = 6000 / 0.80 = 7500.
Source: SSC CGL

56. Two successive discounts of 15% and 10% are given on an article. What is the single equivalent discount percentage?
(a) 25% (b) 23.5% (c) 24% (d) 22.5%
Answer: (b) 23.5%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 25% is the simple sum (15% + 10%), which is incorrect! Equivalent discount = d1 + d2 - (d1 × d2)/100 = 15 + 10 - (15 × 10)/100 = 25 - 1.5 = 23.5%.
Source: SSC GD

57. A student scored 30% marks and failed by 50 marks. Another student scored 45% marks and obtained 25 marks more than the pass marks. What is the pass percentage?
(a) 35% (b) 38% (c) 40% (d) 42%
Answer: (c) 40%
Solution: Difference in marks percentage = 45% - 30% = 15%. Total difference in marks = 50 + 25 = 75 marks. 15% of Maximum Marks = 75 => Maximum Marks = 500. Pass marks = 30% of 500 + 50 = 150 + 50 = 200. Pass percentage = (200 / 500) × 100 = 40%.
Source: RRB NTPC

58. A person's annual income increases by ₹12,000, but the income tax rate decreases from 10% to 8%. If he pays the same amount of tax as before, what was his original annual income?
(a) ₹40,000 (b) ₹42,000 (c) ₹45,000 (d) ₹48,000
Answer: (d) ₹48,000
Solution: Let original income be I. 0.10 × I = 0.08 × (I + 12000) => 0.10I = 0.08I + 960 => 0.02I = 960 => I = 960 / 0.02 = ₹48,000.
Source: SSC CGL

59. In a club, 60% of the members are men and 40% are women. If 15% of the men and 20% of the women are aged above 50 years, what percentage of the total club members are aged above 50 years?
(a) 17% (b) 16% (c) 18% (d) 20%
Answer: (a) 17%
Solution: Let total members = 100. Men = 60, Women = 40. Men above 50 = 15% of 60 = 9. Women above 50 = 20% of 40 = 8. Total above 50 = 9 + 8 = 17 members => 17%.
Source: SSC CHSL

60. The radius of a circle is increased by 10%. By what percentage does its area increase?
(a) 20% (b) 21% (c) 22% (d) 25%
Answer: (b) 21%
Solution: Area is proportional to r². New area = π(1.10r)² = 1.21πr², which is a 21% increase. Formula: 10 + 10 + (10 × 10)/100 = 21%.
Source: RRB Group D

61. A number is divided into two parts such that one part is 20% of the other part. What percentage of the whole number is the smaller part?
(a) 20% (b) 15% (c) 16 2/3% (d) 18%
Answer: (c) 16 2/3%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 20% is the ratio to the other part, NOT to the whole number! Let larger part = 100. Smaller part = 20. Whole number = 100 + 20 = 120. Required percentage = (20 / 120) × 100 = 100/6 = 16 2/3%.
Source: NCERT Class 8

62. A reduction of 12.5% in the price of a television enables a customer to buy 1 extra television for ₹70,000. What was the original price per television?
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹8,750 (c) ₹9,500 (d) ₹10,000
Answer: (d) ₹10,000
Solution: Let original price be P. Reduced price = 0.875P = (7/8)P. Equation: 70000 / [(7/8)P] - 70000 / P = 1 => (70000/P) × (8/7 - 1) = 1 => (70000/P) × (1/7) = 1 => 10000 / P = 1 => P = ₹10,000.
Source: SSC CGL

63. In an examination, a candidate secured 42% marks and failed by 21 marks, while another candidate secured 50% marks and got 15 marks more than the pass marks. What are the maximum marks of the examination?
(a) 450 (b) 400 (c) 500 (d) 600
Answer: (a) 450
Solution: Percentage difference = 50% - 42% = 8%. Marks difference = 21 + 15 = 36 marks. 8% of Maximum Marks = 36 => Maximum Marks = (36 / 8) × 100 = 450.
Source: SSC CHSL

64. A's income is 20% more than B's, and B's income is 25% less than C's. By what percentage is A's income less than C's income?
(a) 5% less (b) 10% less (c) 15% less (d) 10% more
Answer: (b) 10% less
Solution: Let C's income = 100. B's income = 100 - 25 = 75. A's income = 75 × 1.20 = 90. A is 10 less than 100, which is 10% less than C's income.
Source: SSC MTS

65. The length of a rectangle is increased by 30% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. What is the net percentage change in its area?
(a) 4% decrease (b) 6% increase (c) 4% increase (d) 10% increase
Answer: (c) 4% increase
Solution: Net change in area = 30 - 20 - (30 × 20)/100 = 10 - 6 = +4% (increase).
Source: IBPS Clerk

66. A's salary is 25% more than B's salary, and B's salary is 20% less than C's salary. A's salary is what percentage of C's salary?
(a) 95% (b) 105% (c) 110% (d) 100%
Answer: (d) 100%
Solution: Let C = 100. B = 100 × 0.80 = 80. A = 80 × 1.25 = 100. Therefore, A's salary is 100% of C's salary.
Source: SSC MTS

67. In a school, 30% of the students are girls. If 70% of the girls and 80% of the boys passed the examination, what is the overall pass percentage of the school?
(a) 77% (b) 75% (c) 79% (d) 80%
Answer: (a) 77%
Solution: Let total students = 100. Girls = 30, Boys = 70. Passed girls = 30 × 0.70 = 21. Passed boys = 70 × 0.80 = 56. Total passed = 21 + 56 = 77 => 77%.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

68. An article is sold for ₹680 after a discount of 15%. What was its marked price?
(a) ₹750 (b) ₹800 (c) ₹850 (d) ₹900
Answer: (b) ₹800
Solution: Selling price = Marked Price × (1 - 0.15) = 0.85 × MP => 680 = 0.85 × MP => MP = 680 / 0.85 = ₹800.
Source: SSC GD

69. A's weight is 40% of B's weight, and C's weight is 50% of A's weight. What percentage of B's weight is C's weight?
(a) 15% (b) 18% (c) 20% (d) 25%
Answer: (c) 20%
Solution: Let B = 100. A = 40. C = 50% of 40 = 20. Therefore, C is 20% of B.
Source: RRB Group D

70. The population of a village is 15,000. It increases by 10% in the first year and by 20% in the second year. What will be the population after 2 years?
(a) 18,000 (b) 19,200 (c) 19,500 (d) 19,800
Answer: (d) 19,800
Solution: Population after 2 years = 15000 × 1.10 × 1.20 = 15000 × 1.32 = 19,800.
Source: SSC MTS

71. A person spends 50% of his income on household expenses, 20% on children's education, and saves the remaining ₹6,000. What is his total income?
(a) ₹20,000 (b) ₹18,000 (c) ₹22,000 (d) ₹25,000
Answer: (a) ₹20,000
Solution: Total expenditure = 50% + 20% = 70%. Savings = 100% - 70% = 30%. 30% of income = ₹6,000 => Income = 6000 / 0.30 = ₹20,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

72. If 15% of A is equal to 20% of B, what is the ratio A : B?
(a) 3 : 4 (b) 4 : 3 (c) 2 : 3 (d) 5 : 4
Answer: (b) 4 : 3
Solution: 15% of A = 20% of B => 0.15 A = 0.20 B => A / B = 0.20 / 0.15 = 20 / 15 = 4 / 3.
Source: RRB Group D

73. A student scored 75% in the first test and 60% in the second test. If both tests carried equal maximum marks, what was his overall percentage?
(a) 65% (b) 66.5% (c) 67.5% (d) 70%
Answer: (c) 67.5%
Solution: With equal weightage, overall percentage = (75 + 60) / 2 = 135 / 2 = 67.5%.
Source: SSC GD

74. A number is first increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net change in the number?
(a) No change (b) 1% increase (c) 2% decrease (d) 1% decrease
Answer: (d) 1% decrease
Solution: Net change = 10 - 10 - (10 × 10)/100 = -1% (1% decrease).
Source: SSC MTS

75. An employee's salary is increased successively by 25% and then by 20% to become ₹30,000. What was his original salary?
(a) ₹18,000 (b) ₹20,000 (c) ₹22,000 (d) ₹24,000
Answer: (b) ₹20,000
Solution: Let original salary be S. S × 1.25 × 1.20 = 30000 => S × 1.50 = 30000 => S = 30000 / 1.50 = ₹20,000.
Source: IBPS Clerk
