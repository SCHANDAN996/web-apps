51. If the population of a town increases at the rate of 10% per annum and its present population is 79,860, what was its population 2 years ago?
(a) 72,000 (b) 68,000 (c) 70,000 (d) 66,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dividing 79,860 by 1.20 instead of 1.21, treating two years of growth as simple 20%. Population 2 years ago = 79860 / (1.10)² = 79860 / 1.21 = 66,000, so the answer is 66,000.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A person spends 80% of his income. If his income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%, find the percentage increase in his savings.
(a) 50% (b) 70% (c) 60% (d) 80%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming savings also rise by only 10% or 20% like the other figures. Let income = 100, so expenditure = 80 and savings = 20. New income = 120, new expenditure = 88, new savings = 32. Increase = (12/20) × 100 = 60%, so the answer is 60%.
Source: PYQ-style

53. The price of coffee increases by 25%. By what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that the total expenditure remains unchanged?
(a) 15% (b) 18% (c) 25% (d) 20%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing 25%, thinking the reduction equals the price rise. Reduction % = [r / (100 + r)] × 100 = (25/125) × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

54. A number is first increased by 20% and then decreased by 25%. What is the net percentage change in the number?
(a) 10% decrease (b) 5% increase (c) 10% increase (d) 5% decrease
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing 25% − 20% = 5% decrease, ignoring that the 25% cut applies to the increased value. Net change = 20 − 25 + (20 × (−25))/100 = −10%, so the answer is 10% decrease.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

55. In an election between two candidates, 20% of the total votes polled were invalid. The winner secured 70% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 2,400 votes. Find the total number of votes polled.
(a) 7500 (b) 8000 (c) 10000 (d) 12000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking 40% of total votes as 2400, forgetting the invalid votes. Valid votes = 80% of total; majority margin = (70 − 30)% = 40% of valid votes = 2400, so valid votes = 6000 and total votes = 6000 / 0.80 = 7500. The answer is 7500.
Source: PYQ-style

56. Two successive discounts of 15% and 10% are given on an article. What is the single equivalent discount percentage?
(a) 25% (b) 23.5% (c) 24% (d) 22.5%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding 15% + 10% = 25%, but the second discount applies to the already reduced price. Equivalent discount = 15 + 10 − (15 × 10)/100 = 25 − 1.5 = 23.5%, so the answer is 23.5%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

57. A student scored 30% marks and failed by 50 marks. Another student scored 45% marks and obtained 25 marks more than the pass marks. What is the pass percentage?
(a) 35% (b) 38% (c) 40% (d) 42%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is stopping at maximum marks (500) and reporting 45% or guessing. Here 45% − 30% = 15% equals 50 + 25 = 75 marks, so maximum marks = 500. Pass marks = 30% of 500 + 50 = 200, giving pass percentage = 200/500 × 100 = 40%. The answer is 40%.
Source: PYQ-style

58. A person's annual income increases by ₹12,000, but the income tax rate decreases from 10% to 8%. If he pays the same amount of tax as before, what was his original annual income?
(a) ₹40,000 (b) ₹48,000 (c) ₹45,000 (d) ₹42,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is setting 10% of I equal to 8% of I instead of 8% of (I + 12000). Solving 0.10I = 0.08(I + 12000) gives 0.02I = 960, so I = ₹48,000. The answer is ₹48,000.
Source: PYQ-style

59. In a club, 60% of the members are men and 40% are women. If 15% of the men and 20% of the women are aged above 50 years, what percentage of the total club members are aged above 50 years?
(a) 20% (b) 16% (c) 18% (d) 17%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is averaging 15% and 20% to get 17.5% or adding them. Take 100 members: men above 50 = 15% of 60 = 9, women above 50 = 20% of 40 = 8, total = 17 out of 100. The answer is 17%.
Source: PYQ-style

60. The radius of a circle is increased by 10%. By what percentage does its area increase?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 22% (d) 21%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is doubling 10% to get 20%, forgetting the cross term. New area = π(1.10r)² = 1.21πr², i.e. 10 + 10 + (10 × 10)/100 = 21% increase. The answer is 21%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

61. A number is divided into two parts such that one part is 20% of the other part. What percentage of the whole number is the smaller part?
(a) 20% (b) 16 2/3% (c) 15% (d) 18%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 20%, which is the smaller part's ratio to the larger part, not to the whole. Let larger part = 100, smaller = 20, whole = 120. Required percentage = (20/120) × 100 = 16 2/3%. The answer is 16 2/3%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

62. A reduction of 12.5% in the price of a television enables a customer to buy 1 extra television for ₹70,000. What was the original price per television?
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹8,750 (c) ₹10,000 (d) ₹9,500
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is picking ₹8,750, which is the reduced price, not the original. Reduced price = (7/8)P, and 70000/((7/8)P) − 70000/P = 1 gives (70000/P)(1/7) = 1, so P = ₹10,000. The answer is ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

63. In an examination, a candidate secured 42% marks and failed by 21 marks, while another candidate secured 50% marks and got 15 marks more than the pass marks. What are the maximum marks of the examination?
(a) 600 (b) 400 (c) 500 (d) 450
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is subtracting 21 − 15 = 6 instead of adding the two gaps. Here 50% − 42% = 8% equals 21 + 15 = 36 marks, so maximum marks = (36/8) × 100 = 450. The answer is 450.
Source: PYQ-style

64. A's income is 20% more than B's, and B's income is 25% less than C's. By what percentage is A's income less than C's income?
(a) 10% less (b) 5% less (c) 15% less (d) 10% more
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is netting 25% − 20% = 5% directly. Let C = 100, then B = 75 and A = 75 × 1.20 = 90, which is 10 below C's 100. The answer is 10% less.
Source: PYQ-style

65. The length of a rectangle is increased by 30% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. What is the net percentage change in its area?
(a) 4% increase (b) 6% increase (c) 4% decrease (d) 10% increase
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing 30% − 20% = 10% increase, ignoring the product term. Net change = 30 − 20 − (30 × 20)/100 = 10 − 6 = +4%. The answer is 4% increase.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

66. A's salary is 25% more than B's salary, and B's salary is 20% less than C's salary. A's salary is what percentage of C's salary?
(a) 95% (b) 100% (c) 110% (d) 105%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is netting 25% − 20% = 5% and choosing 105%. Let C = 100, then B = 80 and A = 80 × 1.25 = 100, exactly equal to C. The answer is 100%.
Source: PYQ-style

67. In a school, 30% of the students are girls. If 70% of the girls and 80% of the boys passed the examination, what is the overall pass percentage of the school?
(a) 77% (b) 75% (c) 79% (d) 80%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is averaging 70% and 80% to get 75%, ignoring the 30:70 weighting. Out of 100 students: passed girls = 21, passed boys = 56, total passed = 77. The answer is 77%.

68. An article is sold for ₹680 after a discount of 15%. What was its marked price?
(a) ₹800 (b) ₹750 (c) ₹850 (d) ₹900
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding 15% of 680 to 680, giving ₹782, instead of dividing. Marked price = 680 / 0.85 = ₹800. The answer is ₹800.

69. A's weight is 40% of B's weight, and C's weight is 50% of A's weight. What percentage of B's weight is C's weight?
(a) 15% (b) 18% (c) 25% (d) 20%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is averaging 40% and 50% or taking 50% of 40 as 25. Let B = 100, then A = 40 and C = 50% of 40 = 20. The answer is 20%.

70. The population of a village is 15,000. It increases by 10% in the first year and by 20% in the second year. What will be the population after 2 years?
(a) 18,000 (b) 19,800 (c) 19,500 (d) 19,200
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is applying a flat 30% increase, giving 19,500. Population = 15000 × 1.10 × 1.20 = 15000 × 1.32 = 19,800. The answer is 19,800.
Source: PYQ-style

71. A person spends 50% of his income on household expenses, 20% on children's education, and saves the remaining ₹6,000. What is his total income?
(a) ₹18,000 (b) ₹20,000 (c) ₹22,000 (d) ₹25,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is treating ₹6,000 as 50% or 20% of income. Savings = 100% − 70% = 30%, so income = 6000 / 0.30 = ₹20,000. The answer is ₹20,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

72. If 15% of A is equal to 20% of B, what is the ratio A : B?
(a) 3 : 4 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 4 : 3 (d) 5 : 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is inverting the ratio and writing 3 : 4. From 0.15A = 0.20B, A/B = 20/15 = 4/3. The answer is 4 : 3.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

73. A student scored 75% in the first test and 60% in the second test. If both tests carried equal maximum marks, what was his overall percentage?
(a) 67.5% (b) 66.5% (c) 65% (d) 70%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is rounding to 70% or mis-adding the scores. With equal maximum marks, overall percentage = (75 + 60)/2 = 67.5%. The answer is 67.5%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

74. A number is first increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net change in the number?
(a) No change (b) 1% increase (c) 1% decrease (d) 2% decrease
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming the two equal changes cancel out to no change. Net change = 10 − 10 − (10 × 10)/100 = −1%. The answer is 1% decrease.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

75. An employee's salary is increased successively by 25% and then by 20% to become ₹30,000. What was his original salary?
(a) ₹18,000 (b) ₹22,000 (c) ₹20,000 (d) ₹24,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is dividing 30,000 by 1.45 (adding the rises), which gives a wrong figure. Original salary = 30000 / (1.25 × 1.20) = 30000 / 1.50 = ₹20,000. The answer is ₹20,000.
Source: PYQ-style
