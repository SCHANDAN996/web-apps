1. What is 20% of 150?
(A) 20 (B) 30 (C) 40 (D) 50

Solution: 20% of 150 = (20/100) × 150 = 0.2 × 150 = 30.

21. Two successive discounts of 10% and 20% are given on an item. What is the single equivalent discount?
(A) 28% (B) 30% (C) 25% (D) 32%
Answer: (A) 28%
Solution: Net discount = A + B – (A×B)/100 = –10 –20 + (10×20)/100 = –30 + 2 = –28%, i.e., 28% discount.
Source: PYQ-style

22. In an election, candidate A got 60% of the valid votes. 20% of the total votes were invalid. If the total number of votes was 5000, how many valid votes did candidate B get?
(A) 1200 (B) 1600 (C) 2000 (D) 2400
Answer: (B) 1600
Solution: Invalid votes = 20% of 5000 = 1000. Valid votes = 5000 – 1000 = 4000. A got 60% of 4000 = 2400. B got 4000 – 2400 = 1600.
Source: PYQ-style

23. The price of sugar per kg decreases by 20%. By what percentage should the consumption be increased so that the expenditure remains the same?

Solution: Required % increase in consumption = [r/(100–r)] × 100 = (20/80) × 100 = 25%.

24. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 3:2. If 20% of boys and 25% of girls are athletes, what percentage of the total students are athletes?
(A) 21% (B) 22% (C) 24% (D) 25%
Answer: (B) 22%
Solution: Let total students = 500 (boys 300, girls 200). Athlete boys = 20% of 300 = 60, athlete girls = 25% of 200 = 50. Total athletes = 110. % = (110/500) × 100 = 22%.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A number is multiplied by 4/5 instead of 5/4. What is the percentage error in the calculation?
(A) 36% (B) 45% (C) 64% (D) 56%
Answer: (A) 36%
Solution: Correct value = x × 5/4 = 1.25x. Wrong value = x × 4/5 = 0.8x. Error = 0.45x. % error = (0.45x / 1.25x) × 100 = (0.45/1.25) × 100 = 36%.
Source: PYQ-style
