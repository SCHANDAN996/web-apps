Set 1: Percentage Multiple Choice Questions (Easy to Medium)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. What is 20% of 150?
(a) 30 (b) 20 (c) 40 (d) 50
Answer: (a) 30
Solution: 20% of 150 = (20/100) × 150 = 0.2 × 150 = 30.
Source: NCERT Class 7

2. Convert 25% into a fraction in its simplest form.
(a) 1/5 (b) 1/4 (c) 1/2 (d) 3/4
Answer: (b) 1/4
Solution: 25% = 25/100 = 1/4.
Source: NCERT Class 7

3. What is 50% of 500?
(a) 200 (b) 300 (c) 250 (d) 350
Answer: (c) 250
Solution: 50% of 500 = (50/100) × 500 = 500 / 2 = 250.
Source: NCERT Class 7

4. If 10% of a number is 15, what is the number?
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 140 (d) 150
Answer: (d) 150
Solution: Let the number be x. 0.10 × x = 15 => x = 15 / 0.10 = 150.
Source: NCERT Class 8

5. Convert 0.75 into a percentage.
(a) 75% (b) 7.5% (c) 750% (d) 0.75%
Answer: (a) 75%
Solution: 0.75 × 100% = 75%.
Source: NCERT Class 7

6. A student scored 60 marks out of 80. What is his percentage of marks?
(a) 70% (b) 75% (c) 80% (d) 65%
Answer: (b) 75%
Solution: Percentage = (Obtained Marks / Total Marks) × 100 = (60 / 80) × 100 = (3/4) × 100 = 75%.
Source: NCERT Class 7

7. What is 5% of 200?
(a) 5 (b) 15 (c) 10 (d) 20
Answer: (c) 10
Solution: 5% of 200 = (5/100) × 200 = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 7

8. The price of a shirt is ₹40. It increases by 10%. What is the new price of the shirt?
(a) ₹4 (b) ₹48 (c) ₹50 (d) ₹44
Answer: (d) ₹44
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) ₹4 is just the increase amount, not the new price! Increase = 10% of 40 = ₹4. New price = 40 + 4 = ₹44.
Source: SSC MTS

9. Convert 3/4 into a percentage.
(a) 75% (b) 60% (c) 80% (d) 70%
Answer: (a) 75%
Solution: (3/4) × 100% = 3 × 25% = 75%.
Source: NCERT Class 7

10. A number is increased by 20% to become 120. What was the original number?
(a) 90 (b) 100 (c) 110 (d) 96
Answer: (b) 100
Solution: Let original number be x. 1.20 × x = 120 => x = 120 / 1.20 = 100.
Source: NCERT Class 8

11. 35% of 200 is:
(a) 60 (b) 65 (c) 70 (d) 75
Answer: (c) 70
Solution: 35% of 200 = (35/100) × 200 = 35 × 2 = 70.
Source: NCERT Class 7

12. Which of the following is equal to 0.125 expressed as a percentage?
(a) 1.25% (b) 125% (c) 0.125% (d) 12.5%
Answer: (d) 12.5%
Solution: 0.125 × 100% = 12.5%.
Source: NCERT Class 7

13. A basket contains 40 apples. If 25% of them are rotten, how many apples are good?
(a) 30 (b) 10 (c) 28 (d) 32
Answer: (a) 30
Solution: Good apples = (100% - 25%) of 40 = 75% of 40 = (3/4) × 40 = 30.
Source: SSC GD

14. If the population of a town increases from 2,000 to 2,200, what is the percentage increase?
(a) 8% (b) 10% (c) 12% (d) 15%
Answer: (b) 10%
Solution: Increase = 2200 - 2000 = 200. Percentage increase = (200 / 2000) × 100 = 10%.
Source: NCERT Class 8

15. What percent of 50 is 25?

17. A man spends 30% of his salary on rent. If his salary is ₹20,000, how much does he spend on rent?
(a) ₹6,000 (b) ₹5,000 (c) ₹7,000 (d) ₹6,500
Answer: (a) ₹6,000
Solution: Expenditure on rent = 30% of 20000 = (30/100) × 20000 = ₹6,000.
Source: SSC MTS

18. The cost of an item is reduced by 10% to ₹90. What was its original price?
(a) ₹99 (b) ₹100 (c) ₹110 (d) ₹95
Answer: (b) ₹100
Solution: Let original price be P. (1 - 0.10) × P = 90 => 0.90P = 90 => P = ₹100.
Source: NCERT Class 8

19. Express 1/5 as a percentage.
(a) 15% (b) 25% (c) 20% (d) 10%
Answer: (c) 20%

20. A student needs 40% to pass an examination. He scores 20 marks and fails by 10 marks. What are the maximum marks of the examination?
(a) 60 (b) 70 (c) 80 (d) 75
Answer: (d) 75
Solution: Passing marks = 20 + 10 = 30 marks. 40% of Maximum Marks = 30 => Maximum Marks = (30 / 40) × 100 = 75.
Source: RRB Group D

21. Two successive discounts of 10% and 20% are given on an article. What is the single equivalent discount percentage?
(a) 28% (b) 30% (c) 26% (d) 32%
Answer: (a) 28%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) 30% is the direct sum (10% + 20%), which ignores the compounding effect! Equivalent discount = d1 + d2 - (d1 × d2)/100 = 10 + 20 - (10 × 20)/100 = 30 - 2 = 28%.
Source: SSC GD

22. In an election between two candidates, candidate A got 60% of the valid votes. 20% of the total votes polled were declared invalid. If the total number of votes polled was 5,000, how many valid votes did candidate B receive?
(a) 1500 (b) 1600 (c) 2000 (d) 2400
Answer: (b) 1600
Solution: Total valid votes = 80% of 5000 = 4000. Candidate B received (100% - 60%) = 40% of valid votes = 0.40 × 4000 = 1600.
Source: SSC MTS

23. The price of sugar decreases by 20%. By what percentage must consumption be increased so that the total expenditure remains unchanged?
(a) 20% (b) 22.5% (c) 25% (d) 30%
Answer: (c) 25%
Solution: Required % increase = [r / (100 - r)] × 100 = [20 / (100 - 20)] × 100 = (20 / 80) × 100 = 25%.
Source: RRB Group D

24. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 3:2. If 20% of the boys and 25% of the girls are athletes, what percentage of the total students are athletes?
(a) 20% (b) 24% (c) 25% (d) 22%
Answer: (d) 22%
Solution: Let boys = 300 and girls = 200. Total students = 500. Boy athletes = 20% of 300 = 60. Girl athletes = 25% of 200 = 50. Total athletes = 60 + 50 = 110. Percentage = (110 / 500) × 100 = 22%.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

25. A student multiplied a number by 4/5 instead of 5/4. What is the percentage error in the calculation?
(a) 32% (b) 36% (c) 40% (d) 45%
Answer: (b) 36%
Solution: Let the number be 20 (LCM of 4 and 5). Correct value = 20 × (5/4) = 25. Incorrect value = 20 × (4/5) = 16. Error = 25 - 16 = 9. Percentage error = (9 / 25) × 100 = 36%.
Source: SSC GD
