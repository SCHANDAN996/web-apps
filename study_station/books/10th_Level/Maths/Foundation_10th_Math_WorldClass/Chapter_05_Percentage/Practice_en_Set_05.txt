101. A man saves 25% of his income. His income increases by 20% but he still saves the same amount. By what percentage has his expenditure increased?
(A) 26 ⅔%
(B) 20%
(C) 25%
(D) 30%
Answer: (A) 26 ⅔%
Solution: Let original income = 100, savings = 25, expenditure = 75. New income = 120, savings = 25 (same), so new expenditure = 95. Increase in expenditure = 20, % = (20/75)×100 = 80/3 = 26 ⅔%.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

102. A number is first increased by 50% and then decreased by 50%. The net change is:
(A) 25% increase
(B) 25% decrease
(C) 50% decrease
(D) No change
Answer: (B) 25% decrease
Solution: Net change = 50 – 50 + (50×–50)/100 = 0 – 25 = –25%, so 25% decrease.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

113. In a mixture, 40% is water. If 20 litres of water is added, the water becomes 50%. The original volume of the mixture was:
(A) 80 L
(B) 100 L
(C) 120 L
(D) 150 L
Answer: (B) 100 L
Solution: Let original volume = V, water = 0.4V. After adding 20 L, (0.4V+20)/(V+20) = 0.5 → 0.4V+20 = 0.5V+10 → 0.1V = 10 → V = 100.

118. A number is divided into two parts such that 50% of the first part equals 30% of the second part. If the whole number is 160, the larger part is what percent of the whole?
(A) 55%
(B) 60%
(C) 62.5%
(D) 65%
Answer: (C) 62.5%
Solution: Let first = a, second = 160 – a. 0.5a = 0.3(160–a) → 0.5a = 48 – 0.3a → 0.8a = 48 → a = 60, second = 100. Larger part = 100, % = (100/160)×100 = 62.5%.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

120. In an exam, 80% passed in English, 70% passed in Maths, and 15% failed in both. What percentage passed in both?
(A) 55%
(B) 60%
(C) 65%
(D) 70%
Answer: (C) 65%
Solution: Failed in at least one = failed in English (20%) + failed in Maths (30%) – failed in both (15%) = 35%. So passed in at least one = 65%. But question asks passed in both. Actually, passed at least one = 100 – failed in both = 85%. Then passed both = Pass(E) + Pass(M) – Pass(E∪M) = 80 + 70 – 85 = 65%.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

121. (Hard) In an examination, 65% students passed in Mathematics, 55% passed in Physics and 40% passed in both. If the number of students who failed in both is 120, find the total number of students.
(A) 400
(B) 500
(C) 600
(D) 700
Answer: (C) 600
Solution: Passed in at least one = 65 + 55 – 40 = 80%. Failed in both = 100 – 80 = 20%. So 20% of total = 120 → total = 600.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-II 2022

122. (Hard) A shopkeeper sells an article at a profit of x%. If the cost price had been 20% less, the profit percentage would have been 30 percentage points more than the original profit percentage. Find x.
(A) 15%
(B) 20%
(C) 25%
(D) 30%
Answer: (B) 20%
Solution: Let CP = 100, SP = 100 + x. New CP = 80, profit% = (100 + x – 80)/80 × 100 = (5/4)(20 + x). Given (5/4)(20 + x) = x + 30 → 5(20 + x) = 4(x + 30) → 100 + 5x = 4x + 120 → x = 20.
Source: SSC CGL 2023

125. (Hard) A container has 100 litres of mixture of milk and water in the ratio 4:1. 20 litres of the mixture is drawn out and replaced by water. This process is repeated once more. What is the final percentage of milk in the container?
(A) 48.8%
(B) 51.2%
(C) 54%
(D) 56.4%
Answer: (B) 51.2%
Solution: Initial milk = 80 L. After one replacement, milk = 80 × (1 – 20/100) = 64 L. After second, milk = 64 × 0.8 = 51.2 L. Milk % = 51.2%.
