Set 5: Percentage Multiple Choice Questions (Medium to Hard)
Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard

101. A man saves 25% of his income. His income increases by 20%, but his savings amount remains unchanged. By what percentage does his expenditure increase?
(a) 26 2/3% (b) 25% (c) 28% (d) 30%
Answer: (a) 26 2/3%
Solution: Let income = 100, savings = 25, expenditure = 75. New income = 120. Savings remain 25. New expenditure = 120 - 25 = 95. Increase in expenditure = 95 - 75 = 20. Percentage increase = (20 / 75) × 100 = 80/3 = 26 2/3%.
Source: SSC CGL

102. A number is first increased by 50% and then decreased by 50%. What is the net percentage change in the number?
(a) No change (b) 25% decrease (c) 25% increase (d) 10% decrease
Answer: (b) 25% decrease
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 'No change' is incorrect because the base changes! Net change = -(50²/100)% = -25% (25% decrease).
Source: SSC GD

103. In a school, 45% of the students are girls. If 60% of the girls and 70% of the boys passed an examination, what is the overall pass percentage of the school?
(a) 63.5% (b) 64.5% (c) 65.5% (d) 66.5%
Answer: (c) 65.5%
Solution: Girls = 45%, Boys = 55%. Passed girls = 45 × 0.60 = 27%. Passed boys = 55 × 0.70 = 38.5%. Overall pass percentage = 27 + 38.5 = 65.5%.
Source: SSC MTS

104. The price of a commodity is increased by 25%. To keep the total expenditure unchanged, by what percentage must the consumption be reduced?
(a) 15% (b) 16 2/3% (c) 25% (d) 20%
Answer: (d) 20%
Solution: Reduction % = [r / (100 + r)] × 100 = [25 / 125] × 100 = 20%.
Source: RRB Group D

105. If 35% of a number is 175, what is the number?
(a) 500 (b) 450 (c) 550 (d) 600
Answer: (a) 500
Solution: Let number be x. 0.35x = 175 => x = 175 / 0.35 = 500.
Source: NCERT Class 7

106. A student who gets 20% of the maximum marks fails by 20 marks. Another student who gets 40% of the maximum marks secures 20 marks more than the pass marks. What are the maximum marks?
(a) 180 (b) 200 (c) 220 (d) 250
Answer: (b) 200
Solution: Percentage difference = 40% - 20% = 20%. Marks difference = 20 + 20 = 40. 20% of Maximum Marks = 40 => Maximum Marks = (40 / 20) × 100 = 200.
Source: SSC CHSL

107. If 30% of A is equal to 20% of B, what is the ratio A : B?
(a) 3 : 2 (b) 1 : 2 (c) 2 : 3 (d) 3 : 4
Answer: (c) 2 : 3
Solution: 30% of A = 20% of B => 0.30 A = 0.20 B => A / B = 20 / 30 = 2 / 3.
Source: RRB Group D

108. The population of a town increases by 5% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. What is the net percentage change in the population after 2 years?
(a) 5% decrease (b) 4.5% decrease (c) 5% increase (d) 5.5% decrease
Answer: (d) 5.5% decrease
Solution: Net change = 5 - 10 + [5 × (-10)] / 100 = -5 - 0.5 = -5.5% (5.5% decrease).
Source: SSC MTS

109. A shopkeeper gives two successive discounts of 20% and 15% on the marked price of an item. What is the single equivalent discount percentage?
(a) 32% (b) 35% (c) 30% (d) 28%
Answer: (a) 32%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) 35% is the simple sum (20% + 15%), which is wrong! Equivalent discount = 20 + 15 - (20 × 15)/100 = 35 - 3 = 32%.
Source: SSC GD

110. In an election between two candidates, 10% of the total votes polled were invalid. The winning candidate secured 60% of the valid votes and won by 450 votes. What was the total number of votes polled?
(a) 2400 (b) 2500 (c) 2700 (d) 3000
Answer: (b) 2500
Solution: Winner = 60%, Loser = 40% of valid votes. Difference = 20% of valid votes = 450 => Valid votes = 450 / 0.20 = 2250. Total votes = 2250 / 0.90 = 2500.
Source: SSC CGL

111. A's salary is 20% less than B's salary, and B's salary is 30% more than C's salary. If C's salary is ₹5,000, what is A's salary?
(a) ₹4,800 (b) ₹5,000 (c) ₹5,200 (d) ₹5,500
Answer: (c) ₹5,200
Solution: C = ₹5,000. B = 5000 × 1.30 = ₹6,500. A = 6500 × (1 - 0.20) = 6500 × 0.80 = ₹5,200.
Source: SSC CHSL

112. The price of a television is first reduced by 10% and then further reduced by 20%. If the final price is ₹18,000, what was the original price?
(a) ₹22,000 (b) ₹24,000 (c) ₹26,000 (d) ₹25,000
Answer: (d) ₹25,000
Solution: Let original price be P. P × 0.90 × 0.80 = 18000 => P × 0.72 = 18000 => P = 18000 / 0.72 = ₹25,000.
Source: RRB NTPC

113. In a mixture, 40% is water. When 20 litres of water is added to it, water becomes 50% of the total mixture. What was the initial quantity of the mixture?
(a) 100 L (b) 80 L (c) 120 L (d) 150 L
Answer: (a) 100 L
Solution: Let initial mixture = x litres. Water initially = 0.40x. Non-water part = 0.60x. In the new mixture, non-water is 50%: 0.60x = 0.50(x + 20) => 0.60x = 0.50x + 10 => 0.10x = 10 => x = 100 litres.
Source: SSC CGL

114. A man's salary is increased by 15% and then decreased by 10%. What is the net percentage change in his salary?
(a) 5% increase (b) 3.5% increase (c) 3.5% decrease (d) 4% increase
Answer: (b) 3.5% increase
Solution: Net change = 15 - 10 - (15 × 10)/100 = 5 - 1.5 = +3.5% (increase).
Source: SSC MTS

115. In a class, 60% of the students are boys and there are 30 girls. What is the total number of students in the class?
(a) 60 (b) 70 (c) 75 (d) 80
Answer: (c) 75
Solution: Girls percentage = 100% - 60% = 40%. 40% of Total = 30 => Total = 30 / 0.40 = 75 students.
Source: NCERT Class 8

116. The difference between 75% of a number and 45% of the same number is 135. What is the number?
(a) 350 (b) 400 (c) 420 (d) 450
Answer: (d) 450
Solution: (75% - 45%) of x = 30% of x = 135 => 0.30x = 135 => x = 135 / 0.30 = 450.
Source: SSC GD

117. If 25% of A = 30% of B = 40% of C, what is the ratio A : B : C?
(a) 24 : 20 : 15 (b) 15 : 20 : 24 (c) 12 : 10 : 8 (d) 8 : 10 : 12
Answer: (a) 24 : 20 : 15
Solution: 25A = 30B = 40C => 5A = 6B = 8C = k. A = k/5, B = k/6, C = k/8. LCM(5, 6, 8) = 120. A : B : C = 120/5 : 120/6 : 120/8 = 24 : 20 : 15.
Source: RRB NTPC

118. A number is divided into two parts such that 50% of the first part equals 30% of the second part. If the whole number is 160, what percentage of the whole number is the larger part?
(a) 55% (b) 62.5% (c) 60% (d) 65%
Answer: (b) 62.5%
Solution: 0.50a = 0.30b => a/b = 3/5. Total parts = 3 + 5 = 8 parts. Larger part = 5/8 of the whole number. Required percentage = (5/8) × 100 = 62.5%.
Source: SSC CHSL

119. The price of petrol is increased by 40%. By what percentage should a family reduce its consumption so that its total expenditure increases by only 12%?
(a) 15% (b) 18% (c) 20% (d) 25%
Answer: (c) 20%
Solution: Let original price = 100, consumption = 100, expenditure = 10,000. New price = 140. New expenditure = 10,000 × 1.12 = 11,200. New consumption = 11,200 / 140 = 80. Reduction = 100 - 80 = 20%.
Source: SSC CGL

120. In an examination, 80% candidates passed in English, 70% passed in Mathematics, and 15% failed in both subjects. What percentage of candidates passed in both subjects?
(a) 55% (b) 60% (c) 70% (d) 65%
Answer: (d) 65%
Solution: Percentage passed in at least one subject = 100% - 15% = 85%. By principle of inclusion-exclusion: n(E ∪ M) = n(E) + n(M) - n(E ∩ M) => 85% = 80% + 70% - n(E ∩ M) => n(E ∩ M) = 150% - 85% = 65%.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

121. In an examination, 65% of students passed in Mathematics, 55% passed in Physics, and 40% passed in both subjects. If the number of students who failed in both subjects is 120, find the total number of students.
(a) 600 (b) 500 (c) 700 (d) 800
Answer: (a) 600
Solution: Passed in at least one subject = 65% + 55% - 40% = 80%. Failed in both subjects = 100% - 80% = 20%. 20% of Total = 120 => Total students = 120 / 0.20 = 600.
Source: SSC CGL

122. A shopkeeper sells an article at a profit of x%. If the cost price had been 20% less, the profit percentage would have been 30 percentage points more than the original profit percentage. Find x.
(a) 15% (b) 20% (c) 25% (d) 30%
Answer: (b) 20%
Solution: Let original CP = 100. Then SP = 100 + x. New CP = 80. New profit percentage = [(SP - 80) / 80] × 100 = [(20 + x) / 80] × 100 = 25 + 1.25x. Given: 25 + 1.25x = x + 30 => 0.25x = 5 => x = 20%.
Source: SSC CGL

123. A student's percentage scores in five subjects are 70%, 60%, 80%, 50%, and 40%. The maximum marks for these subjects are 100, 100, 50, 150, and 200 respectively. What is his overall percentage of total marks?
(a) 52% (b) 53 1/3% (c) 54 1/6% (d) 55%
Answer: (c) 54 1/6%
Solution: Marks obtained = (0.70×100) + (0.60×100) + (0.80×50) + (0.50×150) + (0.40×200) = 70 + 60 + 40 + 75 + 80 = 325. Total maximum marks = 100 + 100 + 50 + 150 + 200 = 600. Overall percentage = (325 / 600) × 100 = 325 / 6 = 54 1/6%.
Source: SSC CHSL

124. In an election, 12% of the registered voters did not cast their votes. The winning candidate secured 60% of the polled votes and defeated the rival candidate by 1,760 votes. Find the total number of registered voters.
(a) 8,000 (b) 9,000 (c) 12,000 (d) 10,000
Answer: (d) 10,000
Solution: Let registered voters = V. Polled votes = 0.88V. Winner got 60% and loser got 40% of polled votes. Margin = 20% of 0.88V = 0.176V = 1760 => V = 1760 / 0.176 = 10,000.
Source: SSC CGL

125. A container contains 100 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 4:1. 20 litres of the mixture is drawn out and replaced with water. This process is repeated once more. What is the final percentage of milk in the container?
(a) 48.8% (b) 51.2% (c) 54.0% (d) 56.4%
Answer: (b) 51.2%
Solution: Initial milk = 4/5 × 100 = 80 litres. Final milk = Initial milk × (1 - 20/100)² = 80 × (0.80)² = 80 × 0.64 = 51.2 litres. Since total volume remains 100 litres, percentage of milk = 51.2%.
Source: SSC CGL
