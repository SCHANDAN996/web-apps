126. A number is first increased by 10%, then decreased by 20%, and then increased by 30%. What is the net percentage change in the number?
(a) 14.4% increase (b) 12% increase (c) 15% increase (d) 10% increase
Answer: (a) 14.4% increase
Solution: Net multiplier = 1.10 × 0.80 × 1.30 = 1.144, which represents a 14.4% increase.
Source: SSC CGL

127. If 15% of A = 10% of B, and 20% of B = 25% of C, find the ratio A : B : C.
(a) 15 : 20 : 25 (b) 20 : 30 : 24 (c) 10 : 15 : 18 (d) 12 : 18 : 15
Answer: (b) 20 : 30 : 24
Solution: 15A = 10B => A/B = 2/3 = 20/30. 20B = 25C => B/C = 5/4 = 30/24. Therefore, A : B : C = 20 : 30 : 24.
Source: SSC CHSL

128. In an election between two candidates, 25% of the total votes were invalid. Candidate A got 60% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 12,000 votes. Find the total number of votes polled.
(a) 70,000 (b) 75,000 (c) 80,000 (d) 90,000
Answer: (c) 80,000
Solution: Valid votes = 75% of total. Candidate A = 60%, Candidate B = 40% of valid votes. Majority = (60 - 40)% = 20% of valid votes = 12,000 => Valid votes = 12000 / 0.20 = 60,000. Total votes = 60,000 / 0.75 = 80,000.
Source: SSC CGL

129. The price of a commodity rises by 25%. By what percentage should a family reduce its consumption so that its total expenditure increases by only 5%?
(a) 12% (b) 14% (c) 15% (d) 16%
Answer: (d) 16%
Solution: Let initial price = 100, consumption = 100, expenditure = 10,000. New price = 125. New expenditure = 10,000 × 1.05 = 10,500. New consumption = 10,500 / 125 = 84. Percentage reduction in consumption = 100 - 84 = 16%.
Source: SSC MTS

130. A student scores 25% marks and fails by 30 marks. Another student scores 45% marks and fails by 10 marks. What is the pass percentage of the examination?
(a) 55% (b) 50% (c) 60% (d) 65%
Answer: (a) 55%
Solution: Percentage difference = 45% - 25% = 20%. Difference in marks = 30 - 10 = 20 marks. 20% of Maximum Marks = 20 => Maximum Marks = 100. Pass marks = 25 + 30 = 55. Pass percentage = (55 / 100) × 100 = 55%.
Source: RRB Group D

131. A person spends 60% of his income. His income increases by 30% and his savings increase by 50%. By what percentage does his expenditure increase?
(a) 15% (b) 16 2/3% (c) 18% (d) 20%
Answer: (b) 16 2/3%
Solution: Let income = 100, expenditure = 60, savings = 40. New income = 130. New savings = 40 × 1.50 = 60. New expenditure = 130 - 60 = 70. Increase in expenditure = 70 - 60 = 10. Percentage increase = (10 / 60) × 100 = 100/6 = 16 2/3%.
Source: SSC CGL

132. In a club, 40% of the members are men and 60% are women. If 25% of the men and 30% of the women prefer tea, and the total number of members who prefer tea is 560, find the total number of members in the club.
(a) 1,800 (b) 1,900 (c) 2,000 (d) 2,200
Answer: (c) 2,000
Solution: Tea preferring members = (0.40 × 0.25 + 0.60 × 0.30) × Total = (0.10 + 0.18) × Total = 0.28 × Total = 560 => Total = 560 / 0.28 = 2,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

133. The price of a shirt is increased by x% and then decreased by x%. If the net result is a decrease of 4%, find the value of x.
(a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 25 (d) 20
Answer: (d) 20
Solution: Net percentage change = -(x²/100)% = -4% => x² = 400 => x = 20.
Source: SSC GD

134. A person's salary increases by 10% per annum for two consecutive years, becoming ₹12,100. What was his original salary?
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹9,500 (c) ₹10,500 (d) ₹11,000
Answer: (a) ₹10,000
Solution: Let original salary be S. S × (1.10)² = 12100 => S × 1.21 = 12100 => S = 12100 / 1.21 = ₹10,000.
Source: RRB NTPC

135. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 3:2. If 20% of the boys and 30% of the girls are scholarship holders, and 912 students do NOT hold scholarships, what is the total number of students in the school?
(a) 1,100 (b) 1,200 (c) 1,300 (d) 1,400
Answer: (b) 1,200
Solution: Let total students = 5x (Boys = 3x, Girls = 2x). Non-scholarship boys = 80% of 3x = 2.4x. Non-scholarship girls = 70% of 2x = 1.4x. Total non-scholarship = 2.4x + 1.4x = 3.8x = 912 => x = 912 / 3.8 = 240. Total students = 5 × 240 = 1,200.
Source: SSC CHSL

136. A reduction of 20% in the price of sugar enables a person to buy 5 kg more sugar for ₹400. What was the original price of sugar per kg?
(a) ₹16 (b) ₹18 (c) ₹20 (d) ₹24
Answer: (c) ₹20
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) ₹16 is the reduced price, not the original price! Money saved = 20% of 400 = ₹80. Reduced price = 80 / 5 = ₹16 per kg. Original price = 16 / (1 - 0.20) = 16 / 0.80 = ₹20 per kg.
Source: SSC MTS

137. The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year, increases by 20% in the second year, and decreases by 30% in the third year. If the population after 3 years is 46,200, what was the initial population?
(a) 45,000 (b) 48,000 (c) 52,000 (d) 50,000
Answer: (d) 50,000
Solution: Let initial population be P. P × 1.10 × 1.20 × 0.70 = 46200 => P × 0.924 = 46200 => P = 46200 / 0.924 = 50,000.
Source: SSC CGL

138. In a mixture of alcohol and water, alcohol is 60%. When 20 litres of the mixture is taken out and replaced by water, the percentage of alcohol becomes 45%. What was the original volume of the mixture?
(a) 80 L (b) 70 L (c) 75 L (d) 90 L
Answer: (a) 80 L
Solution: Let total volume be V litres. Initial alcohol = 0.60V. Alcohol removed in 20 L = 0.60 × 20 = 12 L. Since total volume remains V: 0.60V - 12 = 0.45V => 0.15V = 12 => V = 12 / 0.15 = 80 litres.
Source: SSC CHSL

139. A's income is 20% more than B's, and B's income is 30% less than C's. If the total combined income of A, B, and C is ₹2,540, find A's income.
(a) ₹720 (b) ₹840 (c) ₹960 (d) ₹1,080
Answer: (b) ₹840
Solution: Let C = 100. B = 70. A = 70 × 1.20 = 84. Ratio A : B : C = 84 : 70 : 100 = 42 : 35 : 50. Total parts = 42 + 35 + 50 = 127 parts. 1 part = 2540 / 127 = ₹20. A's income = 42 × 20 = ₹840.
Source: SSC MTS

140. A candidate gets 35% marks and fails by 25 marks. Another candidate gets 45% marks and gets 15 marks more than the pass marks. What are the maximum marks of the examination?
(a) 350 (b) 380 (c) 400 (d) 450
Answer: (c) 400
Solution: Percentage difference = 45% - 35% = 10%. Marks difference = 25 + 15 = 40 marks. 10% of Maximum Marks = 40 => Maximum Marks = (40 / 10) × 100 = 400.
Source: RRB Group D

141. The length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its area increases by only 8%. What is the percentage change in its breadth?
(a) 8% decrease (b) 12% decrease (c) 5% decrease (d) 10% decrease
Answer: (d) 10% decrease
Solution: Area = L × B. New Area = 1.08 = 1.20 × (1 + b/100) => 1 + b/100 = 1.08 / 1.20 = 0.90 => b = -10% (10% decrease).
Source: SSC GD

142. In a college, 50% of the students are boys and 50% are girls. 20% of the boys and 30% of the girls passed a test. If 750 students passed the test, find the total number of students in the college.
(a) 3,000 (b) 2,500 (c) 3,500 (d) 4,000
Answer: (a) 3,000
Solution: Let total students = T. Passed students = (0.50 × 0.20 + 0.50 × 0.30) × T = (0.10 + 0.15) × T = 0.25T = 750 => T = 750 / 0.25 = 3,000.
Source: SSC CGL

143. A person spends 90% of his income. His income increases by 50% and his expenditure increases by 40%. Find the percentage increase in his savings.
(a) 120% (b) 140% (c) 150% (d) 160%
Answer: (b) 140%
Solution: Let initial income = 100, expenditure = 90, savings = 10. New income = 150. New expenditure = 90 × 1.40 = 126. New savings = 150 - 126 = 24. Increase in savings = 24 - 10 = 14. Percentage increase = (14 / 10) × 100 = 140%.
Source: SSC CHSL

144. A number is divided into two parts such that 60% of the first part equals 40% of the second part. If the whole number is 250, what is the difference between the two parts?
(a) 40 (b) 45 (c) 50 (d) 55
Answer: (c) 50
Solution: 0.60a = 0.40b => 3a = 2b => a/b = 2/3. Parts ratio = 2:3. Sum of parts = 5 units = 250 => 1 unit = 50. Difference = (3 - 2) units = 1 unit = 50.
Source: SSC MTS

145. A number is increased by 40% and then decreased by 30%. If the resulting number is 2 less than the original number, what is the original number?
(a) 80 (b) 90 (c) 120 (d) 100
Answer: (d) 100
Solution: Net multiplier = 1.40 × 0.70 = 0.98. Decrease = 1 - 0.98 = 0.02 of original number. 0.02x = 2 => x = 2 / 0.02 = 100.
Source: RRB NTPC

146. The cost of a book is first increased by 30% and then reduced by 25%. If the final price is ₹78, what was the original price of the book?
(a) ₹80 (b) ₹75 (c) ₹85 (d) ₹90
Answer: (a) ₹80
Solution: Let original price be P. P × 1.30 × 0.75 = 78 => P × 0.975 = 78 => P = 78 / 0.975 = ₹80.
Source: SSC GD

147. In a school, 60% of the students are boys and the rest are girls. On a rainy day, 20% of the boys and 15% of the girls were absent. If 615 students were present, find the total number of students in the school.
(a) 700 (b) 750 (c) 800 (d) 850
Answer: (b) 750
Solution: Present percentage = (0.60 × 0.80) + (0.40 × 0.85) = 0.48 + 0.34 = 0.82 (82%). 82% of Total = 615 => Total = 615 / 0.82 = 750.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

148. A's salary is 20% less than B's salary, and B's salary is 25% more than C's salary. If C's salary is ₹10,000, what is A's salary?
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹9,000 (c) ₹10,000 (d) ₹12,000
Answer: (c) ₹10,000
Solution: C = ₹10,000. B = 10000 × 1.25 = ₹12,500. A = 12500 × (1 - 0.20) = 12500 × 0.80 = ₹10,000.
Source: SSC CGL

149. The price of petrol increases by 20%. By what percentage must a person reduce his petrol consumption so that his total expenditure increases by only 5%?
(a) 15% (b) 10% (c) 11.5% (d) 12.5%
Answer: (d) 12.5%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 15% is the simple subtraction (20% - 5%), which is mathematically incorrect! Let price = 100, consumption = 100. New price = 120. Desired expenditure = 105. New consumption = 105 / 120 = 7/8 = 87.5. Reduction = 100 - 87.5 = 12.5%.
Source: SSC CHSL

150. A mixture contains 75 litres of milk and 25 litres of water. What percentage of the current water content must be added so that water becomes 40% of the new mixture?
(a) 80% (b) 100% (c) 120% (d) 150%
Answer: (b) 100%
Solution: Milk = 75 L. In new mixture, milk is (100% - 40%) = 60%. New total volume = 75 / 0.60 = 125 litres. New water volume = 125 - 75 = 50 litres. Water added = 50 - 25 = 25 litres. Percentage of current water added = (25 / 25) × 100 = 100%.
Source: SSC CGL
