Set 1: Number Series MCQs (Foundation Level)
Questions 1–20 (Easy) | Questions 21–25 (Medium)

Q1. Find the next term in the series:
5, 10, 15, 20, ?
(A) 22 (B) 25 (C) 30 (D) 28
Answer: (B) 25
Solution: The series increases by 5 each time: 5+5=10, 10+5=15, 15+5=20, so 20+5=25.
Source: NCERT Class 6 pattern

Q2. What comes next?
2, 4, 6, 8, ?
(A) 10 (B) 12 (C) 9 (D) 11
Answer: (A) 10
Solution: Constant difference of +2. 8+2=10.
Source: NCERT Class 6 pattern

Q3. Complete the series:
100, 90, 80, 70, ?
(A) 65 (B) 60 (C) 50 (D) 55
Answer: (B) 60
Solution: Subtraction of 10 each step. 70–10=60.
Source: NCERT Class 5 pattern

Q4. Give the next number:
3, 6, 12, 24, ?
(A) 36 (B) 48 (C) 30 (D) 40
Answer: (B) 48
Solution: Each term is multiplied by 2. 24×2=48.
Source: NCERT Class 7 pattern

Q5. Next term:
1, 4, 9, 16, ?
(A) 20 (B) 25 (C) 24 (D) 23
Answer: (B) 25
Solution: Squares of natural numbers: 1²,2²,3²,4², so 5²=25.
Source: NCERT Class 8 pattern

Q6. Find the missing term:
1, 8, 27, 64, ?
(A) 100 (B) 125 (C) 81 (D) 144
Answer: (B) 125
Solution: Cubes: 1³,2³,3³,4³, so 5³=125.
Source: NCERT Class 8 pattern

Q7. Complete the series:
7, 12, 17, 22, ?
(A) 26 (B) 27 (C) 28 (D) 25
Answer: (B) 27
Solution: Constant difference +5. 22+5=27.
Source: NCERT Class 6 pattern

Q8. Next term:
11, 22, 33, 44, ?
(A) 50 (B) 55 (C) 66 (D) 52
Answer: (B) 55

Source: NCERT Class 6 pattern

Q9. What comes after 0, 5, 10, 15, ?
(A) 18 (B) 20 (C) 25 (D) 16
Answer: (B) 20
Solution: +5 each step. 15+5=20.
Source: NCERT Class 5 pattern

Q10. Find the next term:
2, 6, 18, 54, ?
(A) 108 (B) 162 (C) 100 (D) 120
Answer: (B) 162
Solution: Multiply by 3 each time. 54×3=162.
Source: NCERT Class 7 pattern

Q11. Complete: 0, 3, 8, 15, 24, ?
(A) 34 (B) 35 (C) 36 (D) 33
Answer: (B) 35
Solution: Pattern: n²–1. 1²–1=0, 2²–1=3, 3²–1=8, 4²–1=15, 5²–1=24, 6²–1=35.
Source: NCERT Class 8 pattern

Q12. Next term: 1, 3, 5, 7, ?
(A) 8 (B) 9 (C) 10 (D) 11
Answer: (B) 9
Solution: Odd numbers, +2 each. 7+2=9.
Source: NCERT Class 6 pattern

Q13. Find the missing number: 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, ?
(A) 48 (B) 64 (C) 56 (D) 60
Answer: (B) 64
Solution: Powers of 2. 2⁵=32, so next 2⁶=64.
Source: NCERT Class 8 pattern

Q14. What comes next in: 50, 45, 40, 35, ?
(A) 30 (B) 32 (C) 25 (D) 28
Answer: (A) 30
Solution: Decreasing by 5. 35–5=30.
Source: NCERT Class 5 pattern

Q15. Complete the series: 1, 2, 4, 7, 11, ?
(A) 14 (B) 15 (C) 16 (D) 17
Answer: (C) 16
Solution: Differences: +1, +2, +3, +4, next +5 → 11+5=16.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1 (easy pattern)

Q16. Next term: 0, 1, 4, 9, ?
(A) 12 (B) 16 (C) 15 (D) 14
Answer: (B) 16
Solution: Squares: 0²,1²,2²,3², so 4²=16.
Source: NCERT Class 8 pattern

Q17. Find the next: 120, 60, 30, 15, ?
(A) 5 (B) 7.5 (C) 10 (D) 12
Answer: (B) 7.5
Solution: Divide by 2 each time. 15/2 = 7.5.
Source: NCERT Class 7 pattern

Q18. What comes after 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
(A) 35 (B) 36 (C) 37 (D) 38
Answer: (C) 37
Solution: Pattern: n²+1. 1²+1=2, 2²+1=5, 3²+1=10, 4²+1=17, 5²+1=26, 6²+1=37.
Source: NCERT Class 8 pattern

Q19. Next term: 3, 7, 11, 15, ?
(A) 17 (B) 18 (C) 19 (D) 20
Answer: (C) 19
Solution: Constant difference +4. 15+4=19.
Source: NCERT Class 6 pattern

Q20. Complete: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ?
(A) 10 (B) 11 (C) 13 (D) 12
Answer: (C) 13
Solution: Fibonacci series: each term sum of previous two. 5+8=13.
Source: NCERT Class 10 pattern

Q21. Find the next term: 4, 5, 7, 11, 19, ?
(A) 35 (B) 37 (C) 29 (D) 31
Answer: (A) 35
Solution: Differences: +1(2⁰), +2(2¹), +4(2²), +8(2³). Next difference 2⁴=16. 19+16=35.
Source: SSC CGL 2023 Tier 1

Q22. What comes next in the series: 6, 11, 21, 36, 56, ?
(A) 76 (B) 81 (C) 71 (D) 86
Answer: (B) 81
Solution: Differences: +5, +10, +15, +20 (multiples of 5, increasing by 5). Next difference +25 → 56+25=81.
Source: Banking/ SSC pattern

Q23. Complete the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
(A) 40 (B) 38 (C) 42 (D) 44
Answer: (C) 42
Solution: Pattern: n(n+1). 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42.
Source: NCERT Class 8 pattern

Q24. Find the next term: 5, 7, 10, 15, 23, ?
(A) 34 (B) 33 (C) 35 (D) 32
Answer: (C) 35
Solution: Differences: +2, +3, +5, +8. These differences increase by +1, +2, +3 (difference of differences). Next difference +8+4=12 → 23+12=35.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1 (medium)

Q25. Next term: 3, 8, 23, 68, ?
(A) 203 (B) 143 (C) 123 (D) 183
Answer: (A) 203
Solution: Pattern: ×3 –1. 3×3–1=8, 8×3–1=23, 23×3–1=68, 68×3–1=203.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1 (medium)
