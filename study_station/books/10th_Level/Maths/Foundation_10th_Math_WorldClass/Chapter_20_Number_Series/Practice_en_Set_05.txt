101. Find the next term: 7, 13, 25, 43, 67, ?
(a) 93 (b) 101 (c) 99 (d) 97
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 101 comes from wrongly adding 34, but the differences are +6, +12, +18, +24, rising by 6 each time, so the next difference is 30. 67 + 30 = 97, so the answer is 97.
Source: PYQ-style

102. What comes next? 3, 9, 21, 45, ?
(a) 89 (b) 95 (c) 93 (d) 87
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to look for simple differences (+6, +12, +24) and guess 45 + 48 = 93 by luck, but the clean rule is ×2 + 3: 3×2+3=9, 9×2+3=21, 21×2+3=45, 45×2+3=93, so the answer is 93.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Complete the series: 2, 7, 24, 77, ?
(a) 238 (b) 236 (c) 242 (d) 234
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many pick 234 by computing 77×3+3, but the added numbers are the odd numbers 1, 3, 5, 7: 2×3+1=7, 7×3+3=24, 24×3+5=77, 77×3+7=238, so the answer is 238.

125. (Hard) What comes next? 2, 3, 10, 15, 26, ?
(a) 35 (b) 36 (c) 37 (d) 34
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting 36 comes from spotting 6², but the series alternates: odd positions are n²+1 (1²+1=2, 3²+1=10, 5²+1=26) and even positions are n²−1 (2²−1=3, 4²−1=15). The next term is at an even position: 6²−1=35, so the answer is 35.
Source: PYQ-style
