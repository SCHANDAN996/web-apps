Set 6: Number Series MCQs (Foundation Level) — Questions 126–150 (All Hard)

126. Find the next term: 1, 4, 27, 256, ?
(a) 1024 (b) 3125 (c) 3120 (d) 2048
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 1024 is just 4⁵, but the pattern is n raised to itself: 1¹, 2², 3³, 4⁴, so the next term is 5⁵ = 3125.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (powers pattern)

127. What comes next? 6, 9, 18, 45, ?
(a) 135 (b) 150 (c) 120 (d) 140
Answer: (a)
Solution: Multipliers increase by 0.5 each step: ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5, so next is ×3. The trap 120 assumes a constant ×2.67 or wrong multiplier; 45 × 3 = 135, so the answer is 135.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Complete the series: 1, 4, 11, 26, 57, 120, ?
(a) 235 (b) 247 (c) 251 (d) 243
Answer: (b)
Solution: Differences are 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, each one less than a power of 2 (4−1, 8−1, …). The trap 243 comes from doubling 120 and adding 3; the next difference is 128−1 = 127, so 120 + 127 = 247.
Source: PYQ-style

131. Next term: 7, 8, 16, 43, 107, ?

136. What comes next? 2, 5, 10, 50, 500, ?

Answer: (d)
Solution: Each term is the product of the previous two: 2×5=10, 5×10=50, 10×50=500. The trap 5000 multiplies by 10 only; 50 × 500 = 25000, so the answer is 25000.

138. Complete the series: 6, 15, 35, 77, 143, ?
(a) 215 (b) 235 (c) 221 (d) 227
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each term is the product of consecutive primes: 2×3, 3×5, 5×7, 7×11, 11×13, so next is 13×17. The trap 227 treats differences as growing by 8; 13 × 17 = 221, so the answer is 221.

139. Next term: 2, 4, 16, 256, ?
(a) 4096 (b) 8192 (c) 65536 (d) 32768
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each term is the square of the previous one: 2²=4, 4²=16, 16²=256. The trap 4096 is 256 × 16 instead of squaring; 256² = 65536, so the answer is 65536.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Find the next term: 3, 10, 5, 16, 8, 24, 12, 34, ?
(a) 23 (b) 18 (c) 19 (d) 17
Answer: (d)
Solution: Two interleaved series: odd positions 3, 5, 8, 12 rise by +2, +3, +4, so next is +5; even positions 10, 16, 24, 34 rise by +6, +8, +10. The trap 18 adds 6 to the odd series; 12 + 5 = 17, so the answer is 17.
Source: PYQ-style

141. What comes next? 1, 3, 1, 9, 1, 27, 1, ?

Source: PYQ-style

143. Complete the series: 2, 1, 3, 2, 5, 3, 8, 4, 13, 5, ?

Answer: (c)

149. Find the next term: 1, 1, 4, 8, 9, 27, 16, 64, 25, ?
(a) 125 (b) 100 (c) 144 (d) 121
Answer: (a)
Solution: Squares and cubes of natural numbers alternate: 1², 1³, 2², 2³, …, 5², so next is 5³. The trap 121 is 11², mixing patterns; 5³ = 125, so the answer is 125.

Solution: Squares and cubes alternate from 1 onward: 1², 2³, 3², 4³, 5², 6³, 7², so next is 8³. The trap 343 is 7³, using the wrong base; 8³ = 512, so the answer is 512.
