Set 3: Number Series MCQs (Foundation Level)
Questions 51–75 (All Medium)

Q51. Find the next term in the series:
2, 3, 6, 11, 18, ?
(A) 27 (B) 29 (C) 25 (D) 30
Answer: (A) 27
Solution: Differences: +1, +3, +5, +7 (odd numbers increasing by 2). Next difference = 9. 18 + 9 = 27.
Source: NCERT Class 8 / SSC

Q52. What comes next?
8, 15, 24, 35, 48, ?
(A) 60 (B) 63 (C) 65 (D) 62
Answer: (B) 63
Solution: Pattern: n² – 1, starting with n=3. 3²–1=8, 4²–1=15, 5²–1=24, 6²–1=35, 7²–1=48, 8²–1=63.
Source: NCERT Class 8

Q53. Next term:
1, 2, 6, 15, 31, ?
(A) 54 (B) 56 (C) 58 (D) 60
Answer: (B) 56
Solution: Differences: +1 (1²), +4 (2²), +9 (3²), +16 (4²). Next difference = 5² = 25. 31 + 25 = 56.
Source: SSC CGL (medium)

Q54. Complete the series:
10, 19, 37, 73, ?
(A) 139 (B) 145 (C) 155 (D) 149
Answer: (B) 145
Solution: Pattern: ×2 – 1. 10×2–1=19, 19×2–1=37, 37×2–1=73, 73×2–1=145.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1

Q55. Next term:
3, 8, 18, 38, 78, ?
(A) 148 (B) 158 (C) 168 (D) 154
Answer: (B) 158
Solution: Pattern: ×2 + 2. 3×2+2=8, 8×2+2=18, 18×2+2=38, 38×2+2=78, 78×2+2=158.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1

Q56. Complete the series:
100, 50, 52, 26, 28, ?
(A) 16 (B) 14 (C) 12 (D) 18
Answer: (B) 14
Solution: Alternating operations: ÷2, +2, ÷2, +2. 100 ÷ 2 = 50; 50 + 2 = 52; 52 ÷ 2 = 26; 26 + 2 = 28; 28 ÷ 2 = 14.
Source: Banking / SSC

Q57. Next term:
4, 7, 14, 25, 40, ?
(A) 55 (B) 59 (C) 61 (D) 57
Answer: (B) 59
Solution: Differences: +3, +7, +11, +15 (increase by 4). Next difference = 19. 40 + 19 = 59.
Source: SSC CGL (medium)

Q58. What comes after 6, 9, 13.5, 20.25, ?
(A) 30.375 (B) 27.5 (C) 32.25 (D) 29.5
Answer: (A) 30.375
Solution: Each term multiplied by 1.5. 6 × 1.5 = 9; 9 × 1.5 = 13.5; 13.5 × 1.5 = 20.25; 20.25 × 1.5 = 30.375.
Source: Banking (IBPS)

Q59. Find the next term:
1, 4, 10, 22, 46, ?
(A) 92 (B) 90 (C) 94 (D) 96
Answer: (C) 94
Solution: Pattern: ×2 + 2. 1×2+2=4, 4×2+2=10, 10×2+2=22, 22×2+2=46, 46×2+2=94.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1

Q60. Next term:
729, 243, 81, 27, ?
(A) 6 (B) 9 (C) 3 (D) 12
Answer: (B) 9
Solution: Divide by 3 each time. 27 ÷ 3 = 9.
Source: NCERT Class 8 / Railways

Q61. Complete the series:
5, 12, 23, 38, 57, ?
(A) 78 (B) 80 (C) 82 (D) 76
Answer: (B) 80
Solution: Differences: +7, +11, +15, +19 (increase by 4). Next difference = 23. 57 + 23 = 80.
Source: SSC CGL (medium)

Q62. What comes next?
2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, ?
(A) 33 (B) 34 (C) 35 (D) 32
Answer: (B) 34
Solution: Fibonacci series: each term is the sum of the two previous terms. 13+21=34.
Source: NCERT Class 10 / SSC

Q63. Next term:
4, 18, 48, 100, ?
(A) 160 (B) 180 (C) 200 (D) 220
Answer: (B) 180
Solution: Pattern: n² × (n-1)? Check: 2²×1=4, 3²×2=18, 4²×3=48, 5²×4=100, next 6²×5=36×5=180.
Source: SSC / Banking

Q64. Find the next number:
7, 10, 16, 25, 37, ?
(A) 48 (B) 50 (C) 52 (D) 54
Answer: (C) 52
Solution: Differences: +3, +6, +9, +12 (multiples of 3). Next difference = 15. 37+15=52.
Source: NCERT / SSC

Q65. Next term:
1/2, 3/4, 5/8, 7/16, ?
(A) 9/32 (B) 8/32 (C) 9/30 (D) 11/32
Answer: (A) 9/32
Solution: Numerators: 1,3,5,7 (odd) → next 9. Denominators: 2,4,8,16 (double) → next 32.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1

Q66. Complete the series:
0.5, 1.5, 4.5, 13.5, ?
(A) 27.5 (B) 40.5 (C) 45.5 (D) 54
Answer: (B) 40.5
Solution: Multiply by 3 each time. 13.5 × 3 = 40.5.
Source: Banking (SBI PO)

Q67. Next term:
11, 15, 22, 34, 53, ?
(A) 78 (B) 81 (C) 84 (D) 79
Answer: (B) 81
Solution: Differences: +4, +7, +12, +19. Differences of these: +3, +5, +7. Next diff of diff = 9, so next difference = 19+9=28. 53+28=81.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1

Q68. Find the next term:
5, 6, 10, 19, 35, ?
(A) 55 (B) 58 (C) 60 (D) 62
Answer: (C) 60
Solution: Differences: +1 (1²), +4 (2²), +9 (3²), +16 (4²). Next difference = 5² = 25. 35+25=60.
Source: SSC / Banking

Q69. What comes after 2, 7, 27, 107, ?
(A) 427 (B) 417 (C) 437 (D) 407
Answer: (A) 427
Solution: Pattern: ×4 – 1. 2×4–1=7, 7×4–1=27, 27×4–1=107, 107×4–1=427.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 2

Q70. Complete the series:
3, 5, 15, 45, 113, ?
(A) 235 (B) 243 (C) 255 (D) 230
Answer: (B) 243
Solution: Differences: +2, +10, +30, +68. These are of the form n³ + n: 1³+1=2, 2³+2=10, 3³+3=30, 4³+4=68. Next difference = 5³+5=130. 113+130=243.
Source: SSC CGL

Q71. Next term:
12, 24, 72, 288, ?
(A) 1440 (B) 1152 (C) 1200 (D) 1080
Answer: (A) 1440
Solution: Multiply by increasing integers: ×2, ×3, ×4, next ×5. 288 × 5 = 1440.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1

Q74. Complete the series:
2, 10, 26, 50, ?
(A) 76 (B) 82 (C) 78 (D) 86
Answer: (B) 82
Solution: Pattern: n²+1 where n are odd numbers: 1²+1=2, 3²+1=10, 5²+1=26, 7²+1=50, next 9²+1=82.
Source: NCERT / SSC

Q75. What comes next?
1, 8, 9, 64, 25, 216, ?
(A) 36 (B) 49 (C) 64 (D) 100
Answer: (B) 49
Solution: Alternating squares and cubes: 1², 2³, 3², 4³, 5², 6³, next is 7² = 49.
Source: SSC CGL (alternating pattern)
