Set 3 — Mensuration MCQs (All Medium: 51–75)

51. A rectangle has area 96 cm² and one of its sides is 12 cm. A square has the same perimeter as this rectangle. The side of the square is
(a) 10 cm (b) 12 cm (c) 8 cm (d) 14 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: Breadth = 96/12 = 8 cm, so perimeter = 2(12+8) = 40 cm. The trap is taking 8 cm (the breadth) as the square's side. Side of square = 40/4, so the answer is 10 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

52. The area of a rhombus is 216 cm² and one diagonal is 24 cm. Its perimeter is
(a) 72 cm (b) 50 cm (c) 60 cm (d) 48 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: From (1/2)×24×d₂ = 216, the other diagonal is 18 cm. The trap is adding the diagonals (24+18=42) or doubling a half-diagonal. Side = √(12²+9²) = 15 cm, so perimeter = 4×15, and the answer is 60 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

53. The length and breadth of a rectangle are in the ratio 4 : 3. If its area is 300 cm², then its perimeter is
(a) 60 cm (b) 70 cm (c) 80 cm (d) 50 cm
Answer: (b)
Solution: 12x² = 300 gives x = 5, so sides are 20 cm and 15 cm. The trap is computing 2(4+3)×5 = 70 by luck or stopping at 20+15 = 35. Perimeter = 2(20+15), so the answer is 70 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

54. A circular park of radius 21 m has a 7 m wide road all around it. The area of the road (π=22/7) is
(a) 1078 m² (b) 1540 m² (c) 616 m² (d) 1386 m²
Answer: (a)
Solution: Outer radius = 28 m; road area = (22/7)(28²−21²) = (22/7)×343. The trap is using πR² alone (2464) or πr² (1386). The difference gives 22×49, so the answer is 1078 m².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

55. The perimeter of a sector of a circle of radius 7 cm is 25 cm. The area of the sector is
(a) 77 cm² (b) 19.25 cm² (c) 38.5 cm² (d) 154 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: Arc length = 25 − 2×7 = 11 cm, giving θ = 90°. The trap is forgetting the two radii in the perimeter and taking arc = 25 cm. Area = (1/4)×(22/7)×49, so the answer is 38.5 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

56. A cuboid of dimensions 9 cm × 12 cm × 16 cm is melted and recast into a cube. The surface area of the cube is
(a) 648 cm² (b) 1728 cm² (c) 432 cm² (d) 864 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: Volume = 9×12×16 = 1728 cm³, so cube edge = ∛1728 = 12 cm. The trap is reporting the volume 1728 instead of the surface area. Surface area = 6×144, so the answer is 864 cm².
Source: PYQ-style

57. The area of a trapezium is 180 cm² and its height is 12 cm. If one parallel side is 20 cm, the other parallel side is
(a) 15 cm (b) 25 cm (c) 5 cm (d) 10 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: 180 = (1/2)(20+b)×12 gives 20+b = 30. The trap is forgetting the 1/2 factor, which yields a negative or wrong side. Solving, b = 30−20, so the answer is 10 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

58. The volume of a cylinder is 1540 cm³ and its radius is 7 cm (π=22/7). Its total surface area is
(a) 880 cm² (b) 616 cm² (c) 748 cm² (d) 1232 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: (22/7)×49×h = 1540 gives h = 10 cm. The trap is computing only the curved surface area, 2πrh = 440, or only base areas. TSA = 2πr(r+h) = 44×17, so the answer is 748 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

59. The height of a cone is 24 cm and the diameter of its base is 14 cm. Its curved surface area (π=22/7) is
(a) 616 cm² (b) 1100 cm² (c) 528 cm² (d) 550 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: Radius = 7 cm and slant height = √(24²+7²) = 25 cm. The trap is using the height instead of slant height, giving 528 cm². CSA = (22/7)×7×25, so the answer is 550 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

60. A sphere of radius 7 cm is cut into two equal halves. The total surface area of one hemisphere is
(a) 616 cm² (b) 462 cm² (c) 308 cm² (d) 924 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using only the curved part 2πr² = 308 cm² and forgetting the new flat circular face. TSA of hemisphere = 3πr² = 3×(22/7)×49, so the answer is 462 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

61. The perimeter of an equilateral triangle is 45 cm. Its area (√3=1.732) is approximately
(a) 97.4 cm² (b) 112.5 cm² (c) 84.3 cm² (d) 73.2 cm²
Answer: (a)
Solution: Side = 15 cm. The trap is using (1/2)×base×side = 112.5 cm² instead of the equilateral formula. Area = (1.732/4)×225, so the answer is 97.4 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

62. The radii of two cylinders are in the ratio 2:3 and their heights are in the ratio 5:3. The ratio of their volumes is
(a) 4:9 (b) 20:27 (c) 8:27 (d) 10:9
Answer: (b)
Solution: Volume ∝ r²h, so the ratio is (4×5):(9×3). The trap is using r×h instead of r²×h, which gives 10:9. The correct ratio is 20:27, so the answer is 20:27.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

63. A solid metal sphere of radius 9 cm is melted and drawn into a wire of diameter 2 mm. The length of the wire (in metres) is (π=22/7)
(a) 243 m (b) 972 m (c) 486 m (d) 108 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sphere volume = (4/3)π×729 = 972π cm³; wire radius = 0.1 cm, so length = 972π/(0.01π) = 97200 cm. The trap is forgetting to convert cm to m, leaving 97200. Converting, the answer is 972 m.
Source: PYQ-style

64. The diagonal of a cube is 4√3 m. Its capacity (in litres) is
(a) 32000 L (b) 16000 L (c) 64000 L (d) 8000 L
Answer: (c)
Solution: a√3 = 4√3 gives a = 4 m, so volume = 64 m³. The trap is reporting 64 and forgetting that 1 m³ = 1000 L. Capacity = 64×1000, so the answer is 64000 L.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

65. The circumference of a circle is 88 cm. The area of its quadrant (one-fourth of the circle) is (π=22/7)
(a) 616 cm² (b) 38.5 cm² (c) 308 cm² (d) 154 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: 2πr = 88 gives r = 14 cm. The trap is computing the full circle area, 616 cm², instead of one-fourth. Quadrant area = (1/4)×(22/7)×196, so the answer is 154 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

66. A room is 8 m long, 6 m wide, and 5 m high. The cost of whitewashing its four walls and ceiling at ₹12 per m² is
(a) ₹2256 (b) ₹2400 (c) ₹2112 (d) ₹1880
Answer: (a)
Solution: Walls = 2×5×(8+6) = 140 m² and ceiling = 48 m², total 188 m². The trap is including the floor too, giving 236 m². Cost = 188×12, so the answer is ₹2256.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

67. A conical vessel of radius 6 cm and height 8 cm is filled with water. The water is poured into a cylindrical vessel of radius 4 cm. The height of water in the cylinder is
(a) 8 cm (b) 9 cm (c) 6 cm (d) 12 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: Cone volume = (1/3)π×36×8 = 96π cm³; cylinder base = 16π cm². The trap is dropping the 1/3 factor, which gives 18 cm. Height = 96π/16π, so the answer is 6 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

68. The perimeter of a rectangle is 50 cm and its length is 15 cm. The area of a square whose side equals the breadth of the rectangle is
(a) 100 cm² (b) 225 cm² (c) 400 cm² (d) 150 cm²
Answer: (a)
Solution: Breadth = 25 − 15 = 10 cm. The trap is using the length 15 cm as the square's side, giving 225 cm². Square area = 10², so the answer is 100 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

69. A solid is made by joining a cone and a hemisphere of equal radius 3 cm. The height of the cone is 4 cm. The total volume of the solid (π=3.14) is
(a) 75.36 cm³ (b) 94.2 cm³ (c) 113.04 cm³ (d) 131.88 cm³
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hemisphere volume = (2/3)×3.14×27 = 56.52 cm³ and cone volume = (1/3)×3.14×9×4 = 37.68 cm³. The trap is reporting only one part, such as 56.52 or 37.68. Total = 56.52+37.68, so the answer is 94.2 cm³.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

70. A circle is inscribed in a square of side 28 cm. The area of the region outside the circle but inside the square (π=22/7) is
(a) 196 cm² (b) 224 cm² (c) 168 cm² (d) 252 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: Square area = 784 cm² and circle area = (22/7)×14² = 616 cm². The trap is taking the circle's radius as 28 cm instead of 14 cm. Difference = 784−616, so the answer is 168 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

71. The length, breadth, and height of a cuboid are in the ratio 5:4:2 and its volume is 2560 cm³. Its total surface area is
(a) 1024 cm² (b) 1216 cm² (c) 1408 cm² (d) 960 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: 40x³ = 2560 gives x = 4, so dimensions are 20, 16, 8 cm. The trap is computing only the sum of three faces (608) without doubling. TSA = 2(320+128+160), so the answer is 1216 cm².
Source: PYQ-style

72. A hollow cylindrical pipe has inner radius 4 cm, outer radius 5 cm, and length 20 cm. The volume of the metal used (π=22/7) is approximately
(a) 628.57 cm³ (b) 754.29 cm³ (c) 440 cm³ (d) 565.71 cm³
Answer: (d)
Solution: Metal volume = π(R²−r²)h = (22/7)×9×20. The trap is using (R−r)² = 1 instead of R²−r² = 9. This gives 3960/7, so the answer is 565.71 cm³.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

73. A triangle and a parallelogram share the same base and have equal area. If the altitude of the parallelogram is 8 cm, the altitude of the triangle is
(a) 8 cm (b) 4 cm (c) 12 cm (d) 16 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: (1/2)×base×h = base×8 gives h/2 = 8. The trap is assuming equal altitudes (8 cm) because the areas are equal. Solving, h = 2×8, so the answer is 16 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

74. The diagonal of a square is 10√2 cm. A rectangle has the same area as the square, and its length is 12 cm. The breadth of the rectangle is
(a) 25/3 cm (b) 10 cm (c) 20/3 cm (d) 8 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: a√2 = 10√2 gives side 10 cm and area 100 cm². The trap is taking the diagonal 10√2 as the side. Breadth = 100/12, so the answer is 25/3 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

75. A cylindrical iron rod of length 14 cm and base radius 7 cm is melted and recast into small spheres of radius 3.5 cm. How many spheres are obtained? (π=22/7)
(a) 12 (b) 8 (c) 6 (d) 10
Answer: (a)
Solution: Cylinder volume = π×49×14 = 686π cm³; each sphere = (4/3)π×(3.5)³ = (343/6)π cm³. The trap is dividing radii or volumes incorrectly, e.g. using sphere volume without the 4/3 factor. Number = 686×6/343, so the answer is 12.
Source: PYQ-style
