Set 1 — Mensuration MCQs (Easy: 1–20 | Medium: 21–25)

1. The length and breadth of a rectangle are 15 cm and 8 cm respectively. What is its area?
(A) 120 cm²
(B) 46 cm²
(C) 60 cm²
(D) 92 cm²

Answer: (A) 120 cm²
Solution: Area = length × breadth = 15 × 8 = 120 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 6

2. A square has a side of 9 m. Find its perimeter.
(A) 18 m
(B) 36 m
(C) 81 m
(D) 27 m

Answer: (B) 36 m
Solution: Perimeter of square = 4 × side = 4 × 9 = 36 m.
Source: NCERT Class 6

3. What is the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 7 cm?
(A) 84 cm²
(B) 42 cm²
(C) 38 cm²
(D) 21 cm²

Answer: (B) 42 cm²
Solution: Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 12 × 7 = 42 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7

4. The radius of a circle is 21 cm. Find its circumference. (Take π = 22/7)
(A) 66 cm
(B) 132 cm
(C) 264 cm
(D) 42 cm

Answer: (B) 132 cm
Solution: Circumference = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 21 = 132 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 7

5. Find the area of a circle with radius 7 cm. (π = 22/7)
(A) 154 cm²

13. The circumference of a circle is 176 cm. Find its radius. (π = 22/7)

16. The cost of fencing a square field of side 50 m at the rate of ₹6 per metre is:
(A) ₹300

17. A rectangular garden is 30 m long and 20 m wide. A 1 m wide path runs around it on the outside. What is the area of the path?
(A) 104 m²
(B) 100 m²
(C) 96 m²
(D) 108 m²

Answer: (A) 104 m²
Solution: Outer dimensions: length 30+2=32 m, breadth 20+2=22 m. Area of path = (32×22) – (30×20) = 704 – 600 = 104 m².
Source: NCERT Class 7

19. A square and a circle have equal perimeters. If the side of the square is 11 cm, then the radius of the circle is:
(A) 3.5 cm
(B) 7 cm

Answer: (B) 7 cm
Solution: Square perimeter = 4 × 11 = 44 cm = circumference. 2πr = 44 → r = 44/(2 × 22/7) = 7 cm.

21. A copper wire when bent into a square encloses an area of 121 cm². If the same wire is bent into a circle, the difference between the area of the circle and the area of the square is:
(A) 33 cm²
(B) 154 cm²
(C) 77 cm²
(D) 44 cm²

Answer: (A) 33 cm²
Solution: Square area = a² = 121 → a = 11 cm. Wire length = perimeter = 4a = 44 cm. For circle, circumference = 44 → 2πr = 44 → r = 7 cm. Circle area = πr² = (22/7)×49 = 154 cm². Difference = 154 – 121 = 33 cm².
Source: SSC CGL (adapted)

22. The perimeter of a semicircle (including diameter) is 36 cm. Its area is: (π = 22/7)
(A) 308 cm²
(B) 77 cm²
(C) 154 cm²
(D) 38.5 cm²

Answer: (B) 77 cm²
Solution: Perimeter = πr + 2r = r(22/7 + 2) = r(36/7) = 36 → r = 7 cm. Area = ½ πr² = ½ × (22/7) × 49 = 77 cm².
Source: SSC CHSL 2024

23. If the length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its breadth is decreased by 20%, then the percentage change in its area is:
(A) 4% increase
(B) 4% decrease
(C) No change
(D) 2% decrease

Answer: (B) 4% decrease
Solution: New length = 1.2L, new breadth = 0.8B. New area = 1.2 × 0.8 × L × B = 0.96 times original area. Change = (0.96 – 1) × 100 = –4%, i.e., 4% decrease.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 2

24. The radii of two circles are in the ratio 3 : 5. What is the ratio of their areas?
(A) 3 : 5
(B) 9 : 25
(C) 6 : 10
(D) 5 : 3

Answer: (B) 9 : 25
Solution: Area ∝ r². So ratio of areas = (3)² : (5)² = 9 : 25.
Source: NCERT Class 10

25. The perimeter of a rectangle is 82 m and its area is 400 m². Find the breadth of the rectangle.
(A) 25 m
(B) 20 m
(C) 16 m
(D) 18 m

Answer: (C) 16 m
Solution: l + b = 41, lb = 400. (l – b)² = (l + b)² – 4lb = 41² – 1600 = 1681 – 1600 = 81 → l – b = 9. Solving l + b = 41 and l – b = 9 gives l = 25, b = 16. Breadth = 16 m.
Source: SSC CHSL 2024
