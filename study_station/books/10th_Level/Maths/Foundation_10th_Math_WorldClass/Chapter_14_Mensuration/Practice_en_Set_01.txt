Set 1 — Mensuration MCQs (Easy: 1–20 | Medium: 21–25)

1. The length and breadth of a rectangle are 15 cm and 8 cm respectively. What is its area?
(a) 60 cm² (b) 46 cm² (c) 120 cm² (d) 92 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 46 cm² comes from adding the sides twice, i.e., computing the perimeter 2(15 + 8) = 46 instead of the area. Area = length × breadth = 15 × 8, so the answer is 120 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

2. A square has a side of 9 m. Find its perimeter.
(a) 36 m (b) 81 m (c) 18 m (d) 27 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 81 m is the area (9 × 9), not the perimeter. Perimeter of a square = 4 × side = 4 × 9, so the answer is 36 m.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

3. What is the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and height 7 cm?
(a) 84 cm² (b) 42 cm² (c) 38 cm² (d) 21 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 84 cm² forgets the factor ½ and just multiplies base by height. Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 12 × 7, so the answer is 42 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

4. The radius of a circle is 21 cm. Find its circumference. (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 66 cm (b) 264 cm (c) 42 cm (d) 132 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 66 cm uses πr instead of 2πr, giving only half the circumference. Circumference = 2 × (22/7) × 21, so the answer is 132 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

5. Find the area of a circle with radius 7 cm. (π = 22/7)
(a) 44 cm² (b) 308 cm² (c) 154 cm² (d) 102 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option 44 cm² is the circumference 2πr, not the area. Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7, so the answer is 154 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

6. A rectangular field measures 25 m by 15 m. Its perimeter is:
(a) 80 m (b) 375 m (c) 40 m (d) 100 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 40 m adds length and breadth only once, forgetting that a rectangle has two of each side. Perimeter = 2(25 + 15), so the answer is 80 m.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

7. The side of a square is 13 cm. Its area is:
(a) 52 cm² (b) 169 cm² (c) 26 cm² (d) 144 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 52 cm is the perimeter 4 × 13, not the area. Area = side² = 13 × 13, so the answer is 169 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

8. The sides of a triangle are 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm. Its perimeter is:
(a) 16 cm (b) 18 cm (c) 15 cm (d) 20 cm
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 15 cm comes from wrongly averaging or dropping a side; a perimeter is simply the sum of all three sides. Perimeter = 5 + 6 + 7, so the answer is 18 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

9. A semicircle has radius 14 cm. Find its area. (π = 22/7)
(a) 616 cm² (b) 308 cm² (c) 154 cm² (d) 462 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 616 cm² is the area of the full circle πr², forgetting the factor ½ for a semicircle. Area = ½ × (22/7) × 14 × 14 = ½ × 616, so the answer is 308 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

10. The diameter of a circle is 10 cm. Its circumference (π = 22/7) is:
(a) 31.4 cm (b) 35 cm (c) 40 cm (d) 220/7 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 31.4 cm uses π = 3.14, but the question fixes π = 22/7. Circumference = πd = (22/7) × 10, so the answer is 220/7 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

11. A right-angled triangle has perpendicular sides 9 cm and 12 cm. Its area is:
(a) 54 cm² (b) 45 cm² (c) 108 cm² (d) 60 cm²
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option 108 cm² forgets the ½ and just multiplies the two perpendicular sides. Area = ½ × 9 × 12, so the answer is 54 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

12. The area of a rectangle is 96 cm² and its breadth is 8 cm. What is its length?
(a) 10 cm (b) 14 cm (c) 12 cm (d) 8 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 10 cm comes from guessing a round number instead of dividing. Length = area ÷ breadth = 96 ÷ 8, so the answer is 12 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

13. The circumference of a circle is 176 cm. Find its radius. (π = 22/7)
(a) 7 cm (b) 14 cm (c) 28 cm (d) 21 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option 14 cm divides by 4π instead of 2π, halving the correct radius. From 2πr = 176, r = 176 × 7 ÷ 44, so the answer is 28 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

14. Find the perimeter of an equilateral triangle of side 10 cm.
(a) 20 cm (b) 40 cm (c) 30 cm (d) 15 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 20 cm multiplies the side by 2 instead of 3, forgetting that all three sides are equal. Perimeter = 3 × 10, so the answer is 30 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

15. The diagonal of a square is 12√2 cm. The area of the square is:
(a) 72 cm² (b) 120 cm² (c) 288 cm² (d) 144 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 288 cm² comes from squaring the diagonal directly; the area is d²/2, not d². Side = 12√2 ÷ √2 = 12 cm, so the answer is 144 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

16. The cost of fencing a square field of side 50 m at the rate of ₹6 per metre is:
(a) ₹1200 (b) ₹600 (c) ₹200 (d) ₹300
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option ₹300 multiplies the rate by just one side (50 × 6), ignoring that fencing covers the whole perimeter. Perimeter = 4 × 50 = 200 m and cost = 200 × 6, so the answer is ₹1200.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

17. A rectangular garden is 30 m long and 20 m wide. A 1 m wide path runs around it on the outside. What is the area of the path?
(a) 108 m² (b) 100 m² (c) 96 m² (d) 104 m²
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 100 m² adds only 1 m to each dimension instead of 2 m (the path is on both sides). Outer dimensions are 32 m × 22 m, and path area = 704 − 600, so the answer is 104 m².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

18. The base of a triangle is 8 cm and its height is half of the base. The area of the triangle is:
(a) 32 cm² (b) 16 cm² (c) 24 cm² (d) 64 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 32 cm² forgets the ½ in the triangle formula after correctly finding height = 4 cm. Area = ½ × 8 × 4, so the answer is 16 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

19. A square and a circle have equal perimeters. If the side of the square is 11 cm, then the radius of the circle is: (π = 22/7)
(a) 7 cm (b) 3.5 cm (c) 14 cm (d) 5.5 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option 14 cm is the diameter, not the radius. Square perimeter = 4 × 11 = 44 cm = 2πr, giving r = 44 × 7 ÷ 44, so the answer is 7 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

20. The area of a square is 64 cm². If a rectangle has the same area and its length is 16 cm, its breadth is:
(a) 6 cm (b) 8 cm (c) 2 cm (d) 4 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 8 cm is the side of the square (√64), not the breadth of the rectangle. Breadth = 64 ÷ 16, so the answer is 4 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

21. A copper wire when bent into a square encloses an area of 121 cm². If the same wire is bent into a circle, the difference between the area of the circle and the area of the square is: (π = 22/7)
(a) 77 cm² (b) 154 cm² (c) 33 cm² (d) 44 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option 154 cm² is the circle's area itself, not the difference asked for. Side of square = 11 cm, wire length = 44 cm, so 2πr = 44 gives r = 7 cm and circle area = 154 cm²; difference = 154 − 121, so the answer is 33 cm².
Source: PYQ-style

22. The perimeter of a semicircle (including the diameter) is 36 cm. Its area is: (π = 22/7)
(a) 308 cm² (b) 154 cm² (c) 38.5 cm² (d) 77 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 154 cm² uses r = 7 in the full-circle formula or solves πr = 36 wrongly; the perimeter here is πr + 2r. So r(22/7 + 2) = 36 gives r = 7 cm, and area = ½ × (22/7) × 49, so the answer is 77 cm².
Source: PYQ-style

23. If the length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its breadth is decreased by 20%, then the percentage change in its area is:
(a) 4% decrease (b) 4% increase (c) No change (d) 2% decrease
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option "No change" assumes +20% and −20% cancel out, but they act on different bases. New area = 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96 of the original, so the answer is 4% decrease.
Source: PYQ-style

24. The radii of two circles are in the ratio 3 : 5. What is the ratio of their areas?
(a) 9 : 25 (b) 6 : 10 (c) 5 : 3 (d) 3 : 5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 3 : 5 keeps the ratio of radii unchanged, but area varies as the square of the radius. Ratio of areas = 3² : 5², so the answer is 9 : 25.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

25. The perimeter of a rectangle is 82 m and its area is 400 m². Find the breadth of the rectangle.
(a) 25 m (b) 16 m (c) 20 m (d) 18 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 25 m is the length, not the breadth asked for. Here l + b = 41 and lb = 400, so (l − b)² = 41² − 1600 = 81, giving l − b = 9; solving gives l = 25 and b = 16, so the answer is 16 m.
Source: PYQ-style
