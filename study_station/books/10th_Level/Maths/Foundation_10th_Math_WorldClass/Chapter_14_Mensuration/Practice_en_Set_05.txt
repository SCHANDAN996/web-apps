101. The perimeter of a rectangle is 40 cm and its length is 4 cm more than its breadth. The area of the rectangle is
(A) 96 cm²
(B) 80 cm²
(C) 120 cm²
(D) 84 cm²

Answer: (A) 96 cm²
Solution: Let breadth = b, length = b+4. Perimeter = 2(b + b+4) = 4b+8 = 40 → b = 8 cm, l = 12 cm. Area = 12×8 = 96 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 8

102. The area of a circle is equal to the area of a rectangle of length 14 cm and breadth 11 cm. The circumference of the circle (π=22/7) is
(A) 44 cm
(B) 88 cm
(C) 22 cm
(D) 132 cm

Answer: (A) 44 cm
Solution: Rectangle area = 14×11 = 154 cm². πr² = 154 → r² = 49 → r = 7 cm. Circumference = 2πr = 2×(22/7)×7 = 44 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 7

103. A square and a circle have equal perimeters. If the area of the square is 121 cm², the area of the circle (π=22/7) is
(A) 154 cm²
(B) 144 cm²
(C) 196 cm²
(D) 77 cm²

Answer: (A) 154 cm²
Solution: Square side = √121 = 11 cm, perimeter = 44 cm = circle circumference. 2πr = 44 → r = 7 cm. Area = πr² = 154 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7

104. The radii of two circles are in the ratio 2:3. If the sum of their areas is 117π cm², the radius of the larger circle is
(A) 9 cm
(B) 12 cm
(C) 6 cm
(D) 15 cm

Answer: (A) 9 cm
Solution: Let radii 2k, 3k. Areas = 4k²π and 9k²π. Sum = 13k²π = 117π → k² = 9 → k=3. Larger radius = 9 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 7

105. The area of a circular garden is 38.5 m². The cost of fencing it at ₹20 per metre (π=22/7) is
(A) ₹440
(B) ₹220
(C) ₹110
(D) ₹330

Answer: (A) ₹440
Solution: πr² = 38.5 → (22/7)r² = 38.5 → r² = 12.25 → r = 3.5 m. Circumference = 2πr = 2×(22/7)×3.5 = 22 m. Cost = 22×20 = ₹440.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1

106. A triangle has sides of lengths 6 cm, 8 cm, and 10 cm. Its area is
(A) 24 cm²
(B) 30 cm²

Answer: (A) 24 cm²
Solution: It is a right‑angled triangle (6²+8²=10²). Area = ½×6×8 = 24 cm².

107. The area of an equilateral triangle is 16√3 cm². Its perimeter is

108. The base of a parallelogram is twice its height. If its area is 128 cm², the base is
(A) 16 cm
(B) 8 cm
(C) 32 cm
(D) 12 cm

Answer: (A) 16 cm
Solution: Let height = h, base = 2h. Area = 2h × h = 2h² = 128 → h² = 64 → h = 8, base = 16 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8

109. A rhombus has diagonals of lengths 12 cm and 16 cm. Its perimeter is
(A) 40 cm
(B) 56 cm
(C) 28 cm
(D) 80 cm

Answer: (A) 40 cm
Solution: Side = √((d₁/2)²+(d₂/2)²) = √(6²+8²) = 10 cm. Perimeter = 4×10 = 40 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8

110. The height of a cone is 9 cm and the diameter of its base is 14 cm. Its volume (π=22/7) is
(A) 462 cm³

(D) 616 cm³

Answer: (A) 462 cm³
Solution: r = 7 cm. Volume = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3)×(22/7)×49×9 = 22×7×3 = 462 cm³.

111. A cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 20 cm is completely filled with water. This water is poured into a rectangular tank of base dimensions 22 cm by 10 cm. The height of water in the tank (π=22/7) is
(A) 14 cm
(B) 22 cm
(C) 7 cm
(D) 28 cm

Answer: (A) 14 cm
Solution: Cylinder volume = πr²h = (22/7)×49×20 = 3080 cm³. Tank base area = 22×10 = 220 cm². Height = 3080/220 = 14 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 9

112. The diameter of a sphere is decreased by 50%. The percentage decrease in its surface area is
(A) 75%
(B) 50%
(C) 87.5%
(D) 25%

Answer: (A) 75%
Solution: Surface area ∝ (diameter)². New diameter = 0.5d → new SA = (0.5)² = 0.25 times. Decrease = 1−0.25 = 0.75 = 75%.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 2

113. A solid metallic cone of radius 7 cm and height 15 cm is melted and recast into a solid cylinder of radius 5 cm. The height of the cylinder (π=22/7) is
(A) 9.8 cm
(B) 7 cm
(C) 14 cm
(D) 4.9 cm

Answer: (A) 9.8 cm
Solution: Cone volume = (1/3)π×49×15 = 245π cm³. Cylinder volume = π×25×h = 25πh. 25πh = 245π → h = 245/25 = 9.8 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10

114. The total surface area of a cube is numerically one‑third of its volume. The edge length of the cube is
(A) 18 units

Answer: (A) 18 units
Solution: TSA = 6a², volume = a³. Given a³ = 3×6a² = 18a² → a = 18.

116. The circumferences of two circles are in the ratio 5:3. The ratio of their areas is

117. The perimeter of a semicircle (including the diameter) is 72 cm. Its area (π=22/7) is

Answer: (A) 308 cm²

118. A solid cylinder of radius 5 cm and height 8 cm is melted and recast into small cones of radius 1 cm and height 2 cm. The number of cones obtained is
(A) 300
(B) 200
(C) 150
(D) 400

Answer: (A) 300
Solution: Cylinder volume = π×25×8 = 200π cm³. One cone volume = (1/3)π×1×2 = (2π/3) cm³. Number = 200π / (2π/3) = 300.
Source: NCERT Class 10

119. The curved surface area of a cone is 550 cm² and its slant height is 25 cm. Its height (π=22/7) is

120. The dimensions of a cuboid are in the ratio 2:1:1. If its volume is 54 cm³, its total surface area is
(A) 90 cm²
(B) 108 cm²
(C) 72 cm²
(D) 126 cm²

Answer: (A) 90 cm²
Solution: Let dimensions be 2x, x, x. Volume = 2x³ = 54 → x=3. Dimensions = 6,3,3 cm. TSA = 2(6×3+3×3+3×6) = 2(18+9+18) = 90 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 8

121. A solid is formed by joining a cone on top of a cylinder of same base radius. The total height of the solid is 14 cm and the base radius is 3 cm. If the height of the cone is 4 cm, the total surface area of the solid (π=22/7) is
(A) 264 cm²
(B) 330 cm²
(C) 198 cm²
(D) 396 cm²

Answer: (A) 264 cm²
Solution: Cylinder height = 14−4 = 10 cm. Slant height of cone l = √(3²+4²)=5 cm. TSA = cylinder CSA (2πrh) + cone CSA (πrl) + base area (πr²). No base area inside? The base of the cone is open, so we add only bottom base. So: 2π×3×10 + π×3×5 + π×9 = 60π+15π+9π = 84π = 84×(22/7) = 264 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 10 (Combined Shapes)

122. A toy is made by joining a hemisphere and a cone of the same base radius 7 cm. The height of the cone is 24 cm. The total surface area of the toy (excluding the base) (π=22/7) is
(A) 858 cm²
(B) 1012 cm²
(C) 462 cm²
(D) 704 cm²

Answer: (A) 858 cm²
Solution: Slant height of cone l = √(7²+24²)=25 cm. CSA of cone = π×7×25 = 175π. CSA of hemisphere = 2π×49 = 98π. Total exposed area = 175π+98π = 273π = 273×(22/7) = 858 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 10

123. A solid is composed of a cylinder with hemispherical ends. The total length of the solid is 20 cm and the diameter of each hemisphere is 14 cm. Its total surface area (π=22/7) is
(A) 880 cm²
(B) 792 cm²
(C) 968 cm²
(D) 1056 cm²

Answer: (A) 880 cm²
Solution: Radius = 7 cm. Cylinder length = 20 − 2×7 = 6 cm. CSA of cylinder = 2π×7×6 = 84π. CSA of two hemispheres = 2×2π×7² = 196π. Total = 280π = 280×(22/7)=880 cm².
Source: SSC CGL Tier 2

124. A solid hemisphere of radius 9 cm is melted and drawn into a wire of diameter 3 mm. The length of the wire (in metres) is
(A) 216 m
(B) 108 m
(C) 324 m
(D) 432 m

Answer: (A) 216 m
Solution: Hemisphere volume = (2/3)π×9³ = 486π cm³. Wire radius = 1.5 mm = 0.15 cm, cross‑section area = π×(0.15)² = 0.0225π cm². Length = 486π / (0.0225π) = 21600 cm = 216 m.
Source: SSC CGL

125. A cylindrical water pipe has external diameter 16 cm and thickness 2 cm. Water flows through it at a speed of 3.5 m/s. The volume of water discharged in 1 minute (in litres) is (π=22/7)
(A) 2376 L
(B) 1584 L
(C) 3168 L
(D) 792 L

Answer: (A) 2376 L
Solution: Inner radius = 8−2 = 6 cm. Cross‑section area = π×6² = 36π cm² = 36π×10⁻⁴ m². Flow speed = 3.5 m/s. Discharge per second = area×speed = 36π×10⁻⁴×3.5 = 36×(22/7)×10⁻⁴×3.5 = 36×22×10⁻⁴×0.5 = 18×22×10⁻⁴ = 396×10⁻⁴ m³/s. In 60 s, volume = 396×10⁻⁴×60 = 23760×10⁻⁴ = 2.376 m³ = 2376 L.
Source: NCERT Class 10 (Application)
