71. If P(A) = 0.6, P(B) = 0.5, and P(A ∪ B) = 0.8, find P(A ∩ B).
(A) 0.1  (B) 0.2  (C) 0.3  (D) 0.4
Answer: (C)
Solution: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) – P(A ∩ B) ⇒ 0.8 = 0.6 + 0.5 – P(A∩B) ⇒ P(A∩B) = 1.1 – 0.8 = 0.3.
Source: NCERT Class 11, Ex 14.2.
