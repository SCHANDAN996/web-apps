18. If P(E) = 0.65, what is P(not E)?
(A) 0.35  (B) 0.45  (C) 0.55  (D) 0.25
Answer: (A)
Solution: P(not E) = 1 – P(E) = 1 – 0.65 = 0.35.
Source: NCERT Class 10, Ex 15.1 Q5 (complement concept).

19. A and B are mutually exclusive events. If P(A) = 0.3 and P(B) = 0.4, find P(A or B).
(A) 0.12  (B) 0.7  (C) 0.1  (D) 0.58
Answer: (B)
Solution: For mutually exclusive events, P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) = 0.3 + 0.4 = 0.7.
Source: NCERT Class 11 (Probability), but simplified.
