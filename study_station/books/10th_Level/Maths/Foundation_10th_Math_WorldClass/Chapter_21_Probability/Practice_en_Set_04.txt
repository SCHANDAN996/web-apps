76. A fair coin is tossed 3 times. What is the probability of getting at least 2 heads?
(a) 5/8 (b) 3/8 (c) 1/4 (d) 1/2
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total outcomes = 2³ = 8. Favourable: exactly 2 heads (HHT, HTH, THH = 3 ways) plus 3 heads (HHH = 1 way) = 4 ways. The trap is counting only the 3 two-head cases and getting 3/8, so the answer is 1/2.

77. A bag contains 4 red, 3 blue and 2 green balls. Two balls are drawn at random without replacement. Find the probability that both are blue.
(a) 1/4 (b) 1/6 (c) 1/12 (d) 2/9
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total balls = 9, so total ways to draw 2 = 9C2 = 36. Ways to draw 2 blue from 3 = 3C2 = 3, giving 3/36. The trap is computing (3/9)×(3/9) = 1/9 as if replacement occurred, so the answer is 1/12.
