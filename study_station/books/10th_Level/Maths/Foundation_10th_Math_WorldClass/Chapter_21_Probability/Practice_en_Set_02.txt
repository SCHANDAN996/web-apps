34. A and B are two independent events. If P(A) = 0.4 and P(B) = 0.5, what is P(A ∩ B)?
(A) 0.9  (B) 0.2  (C) 0.1  (D) 0.45
Answer: (B)
Solution: For independent events, P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B) = 0.4 × 0.5 = 0.2.

35. For two events A and B, P(A) = 0.5, P(B) = 0.4, and P(A ∪ B) = 0.7. Find P(A ∩ B).
(A) 0.1  (B) 0.2  (C) 0.3  (D) 0.9
Answer: (B)
Solution: Using addition rule: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) – P(A ∩ B) ⇒ 0.7 = 0.5 + 0.4 – P(A ∩ B) ⇒ P(A ∩ B) = 0.2.
Source: NCERT Class 11, Exercise 14.2 (concept).
