Q126. The sum of two numbers is 200, their LCM is 420, and their HCF is 20. What are the two numbers?
(a) 60 and 140 (b) 80 and 120 (c) 40 and 160 (d) 70 and 130
Answer: (a) 60 and 140
Solution: Let the numbers be 20a and 20b with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Their sum is 20(a + b) = 200 ⇒ a + b = 10. Their LCM is 20ab = 420 ⇒ ab = 21. The co-prime numbers satisfying a + b = 10 and ab = 21 are a = 3 and b = 7. The numbers are 20(3) = 60 and 20(7) = 140. Thus, the numbers are 60 and 140. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q127. The LCM of two numbers is 15 times their HCF. The difference between their LCM and HCF is 224. If one of the numbers is 80, what is the other number?
(a) 36 (b) 48 (c) 64 (d) 72
Answer: (b) 48
Solution: Let HCF = h, then LCM = 15h. Given: 15h – h = 224 ⇒ 14h = 224 ⇒ h = 16. Therefore, LCM = 15 × 16 = 240. Using the product formula: 80 × Other = 240 × 16 = 3840 ⇒ Other number = 3840 / 80 = 48. Hence, the other number is 48. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q128. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 1305, 4665, and 6905 leaving the same remainder in each case?
(a) 560 (b) 2240 (c) 1120 (d) 3360
Answer: (c) 1120
Solution: For equal remainders, the divisor must divide the pairwise differences: 4665 – 1305 = 3360, 6905 – 4665 = 2240, and 6905 – 1305 = 5600. Factoring: 3360 = 1120 × 3, 2240 = 1120 × 2, and 5600 = 1120 × 5. Because 2, 3, and 5 are mutually co-prime, HCF(3360, 2240, 5600) = 1120. (Trap: Taking HCF of the original numbers gives 5, which fails to find the greatest equal-remainder divisor). Thus, the greatest number is 1120. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q129. What is the least positive integer which when divided by 9, 11, and 13 leaves remainders of 8, 10, and 12 respectively?
(a) 1285 (b) 1287 (c) 1288 (d) 1286
Answer: (d) 1286
Solution: The difference between each divisor and its remainder is constant: 9 – 8 = 1, 11 – 10 = 1, and 13 – 12 = 1. Since 9, 11, and 13 are pairwise co-prime, LCM(9, 11, 13) = 9 × 11 × 13 = 1287. Subtracting the constant difference 1: 1287 – 1 = 1286. Hence, the least number is 1286. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q130. What is the LCM of the fractions 2/3, 8/9, 16/81, and 10/27?
(a) 80/3 (b) 16/3 (c) 80/81 (d) 16/81
Answer: (a) 80/3
Solution: LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators divided by HCF of denominators. Numerators: 2, 8, 16, 10 ⇒ LCM(2, 8, 16, 10) = 80. Denominators: 3, 9, 81, 27 ⇒ HCF(3, 9, 81, 27) = 3. Therefore, the LCM is 80/3. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB Group D

Q131. What is the least multiple of 7 greater than 1000 which leaves a remainder of 4 when divided by 6, 9, 15, and 18?
(a) 1444 (b) 1624 (c) 1084 (d) 1264
Answer: (b) 1624
Solution: LCM(6, 9, 15, 18) = 90. Any such number has the form 90k + 4. We need 90k + 4 ≡ 0 (mod 7) ⇒ 6k + 4 ≡ 0 ⇒ 6k ≡ 3 ⇒ –k ≡ 3 ⇒ k ≡ 4 (mod 7). For 90k + 4 > 1000, we require k ≥ 12. The smallest integer k ≥ 12 satisfying k ≡ 4 (mod 7) is k = 18. Substituting k = 18 gives: 90(18) + 4 = 1620 + 4 = 1624. Check: 1624 / 7 = 232 (divisible by 7) and 1624 mod 90 = 4. Thus, the least multiple of 7 is 1624. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL

Q132. The sum of two numbers is 192 and their LCM is 15 times their HCF. What are the two numbers?
(a) 60 and 132 (b) 48 and 144 (c) 72 and 120 (d) 84 and 108
Answer: (c) 72 and 120
Solution: Let the numbers be ha and hb with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Then LCM = hab = 15h ⇒ ab = 15. The sum is h(a + b) = 192. For the co-prime pair a = 3 and b = 5: a + b = 8 ⇒ h = 192 / 8 = 24. The numbers are 24(3) = 72 and 24(5) = 120. (Check: 72 + 120 = 192, HCF = 24, LCM = 360 = 15 × 24). Thus, the numbers are 72 and 120. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q133. Three numbers are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5 and their HCF is 120. What is the LCM of the three numbers?
(a) 1800 (b) 2400 (c) 3000 (d) 3600
Answer: (d) 3600
Solution: Because 2, 3, and 5 are mutually co-prime, their LCM is 2 × 3 × 5 × HCF = 30 × 120 = 3600. Thus, their LCM is 3600. Hence, the correct option is (d).

Q134. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 7, 8, and 9 leaves remainders of 1, 2, and 3 respectively?
(a) 498 (b) 504 (c) 510 (d) 492
Answer: (a) 498
Solution: The difference between each divisor and its remainder is constant: 7 – 1 = 6, 8 – 2 = 6, and 9 – 3 = 6. Since 7, 8, and 9 are pairwise co-prime, LCM(7, 8, 9) = 7 × 8 × 9 = 504. The required number is LCM – constant difference = 504 – 6 = 498. (Trap: Adding 6 gives 510, which leaves wrong remainders). Thus, the smallest number is 498. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q135. Two runners start simultaneously from the same point on a circular track of length 4200 m running in opposite directions with speeds of 15 m/s and 20 m/s respectively. After what time will they meet at the starting point for the first time?
(a) 12 min (b) 14 min (c) 16 min (d) 20 min
Answer: (b) 14 min
Solution: Time taken by the first runner to complete one lap = 4200 / 15 = 280 seconds. Time taken by the second runner to complete one lap = 4200 / 20 = 210 seconds. Regardless of direction, they meet at the starting point at intervals equal to LCM(280, 210). Factoring: 280 = 70 × 4 and 210 = 70 × 3 ⇒ LCM = 70 × 12 = 840 seconds. In minutes: 840 / 60 = 14 minutes. Hence, they meet at the starting point after 14 min. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D

Q136. What is the least positive integer which when divided by 15, 20, and 25 leaves remainders of 10, 15, and 20 respectively?
(a) 305 (b) 300 (c) 295 (d) 290
Answer: (c) 295
Solution: Constant difference between divisor and remainder: 15 – 10 = 5, 20 – 15 = 5, and 25 – 20 = 5. LCM(15, 20, 25) = 2² × 3 × 5² = 300. Subtracting the constant difference gives: 300 – 5 = 295. Therefore, the least number is 295. Hence, the correct option is (c).

Q137. The HCF of two numbers is 23. The other two co-prime factors of their LCM are 9 and 11. What is the smaller of the two numbers?

Solution: The two numbers are HCF × a and HCF × b. Substituting: 23 × 9 = 207 and 23 × 11 = 253. The smaller of the two numbers is 207. Hence, the smaller number is 207. Hence, the correct option is (d).

Q138. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 43, 91, and 183 so as to leave the same remainder in each case?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 12
Answer: (a) 4
Solution: For equal remainders, find the HCF of the pairwise differences: 91 – 43 = 48, 183 – 91 = 92, and 183 – 43 = 140. Factoring: 48 = 4 × 12, 92 = 4 × 23, and 140 = 4 × 35. Since 23 is a prime number that does not divide 12 or 35, HCF(48, 92, 140) = 4. Thus, the greatest divisor is 4. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC MTS

Q139. The LCM of two numbers is 120 and their sum is 54. What is their HCF?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: Let the numbers be ha and hb with gcd(a, b) = 1. Then h(a + b) = 54 and hab = 120. Dividing gives ab / (a + b) = 120 / 54 = 20 / 9. Thus, a + b = 9 and ab = 20. The co-prime factors satisfying this system are a = 4 and b = 5. Then h = 54 / (4 + 5) = 54 / 9 = 6. The numbers are 6(4) = 24 and 6(5) = 30 (Check: sum = 54, LCM = 120, HCF = 6). Therefore, their HCF is 6. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q141. Three numbers are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5 and their LCM is 1080. What is the smallest of the three numbers?
(a) 36 (b) 108 (c) 180 (d) 72
Answer: (d) 72
Solution: Let the numbers be 2x, 3x, and 5x. Because 2, 3, and 5 are co-prime, LCM = 2 × 3 × 5 × x = 30x. Given 30x = 1080 ⇒ x = 36. The smallest number is 2x = 2(36) = 72. Therefore, the smallest number is 72. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL

Q142. The product of two positive numbers is 2304 and their LCM is 192. What is their HCF?

Q143. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 989 and 1327 leaving remainders of 5 and 7 respectively?
(a) 12 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 48
Answer: (b) 24
Solution: Subtracting remainders: 989 – 5 = 984 and 1327 – 7 = 1320. Using the Euclidean algorithm for HCF(984, 1320): 1320 = 984 × 1 + 336, 984 = 336 × 2 + 312, 336 = 312 × 1 + 24, and 312 = 24 × 13 + 0. Thus, HCF(984, 1320) = 24. Hence, the greatest number is 24. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC MTS

Q144. The HCF of two numbers is 29 and their LCM is 4147. If one of the numbers is 319, what is the value of the other number?
(a) 348 (b) 361 (c) 377 (d) 406
Answer: (c) 377
Solution: Using the product formula: 319 × Other = 29 × 4147. Notice 319 = 29 × 11. Dividing by 319 gives: Other number = 4147 / 11 = 377. (Check: 377 = 29 × 13, and 11 and 13 are co-prime). Thus, the other number is 377. Hence, the correct option is (c).

Q145. What is the least positive multiple of 9 which, when divided by 5, 7, and 11, leaves a remainder of 2 in each case?
(a) 772 (b) 1157 (c) 1947 (d) 387
Answer: (d) 387
Solution: The number has the form LCM(5, 7, 11) × k + 2 = 385k + 2 and must be divisible by 9. Modulo 9: 385k + 2 ≡ 7k + 2 ≡ 0 (mod 9) ⇒ 7k ≡ 7 ⇒ k ≡ 1 (mod 9). For the smallest positive integer, choose k = 1: 385(1) + 2 = 387. Check: 387 / 9 = 43 (divisible by 9), and 387 leaves remainder 2 when divided by 5, 7, and 11. Therefore, the least multiple of 9 is 387. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB Group D

Q146. The LCM of two numbers is 12 times their HCF. If the positive difference of the two numbers is 20, what is the sum of the two numbers?
(a) 140 (b) 120 (c) 160 (d) 180
Answer: (a) 140
Solution: Let the numbers be ha and hb with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Then LCM = hab = 12h ⇒ ab = 12. The difference is h(b – a) = 20. The co-prime factor pairs of 12 are (1, 12) with difference 11 (giving h = 20/11, non-integer) and (3, 4) with difference 1 (giving h = 20). With h = 20, the numbers are 20(3) = 60 and 20(4) = 80. Their sum is 60 + 80 = 140. Thus, the sum of the numbers is 140. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q147. What is the smallest positive integer that, when divided by 4, 5, 6, and 7, leaves remainders of 2, 3, 4, and 5 respectively?
(a) 420 (b) 418 (c) 422 (d) 416
Answer: (b) 418
Solution: The difference between each divisor and its remainder is constant: 4 – 2 = 2, 5 – 3 = 2, 6 – 4 = 2, and 7 – 5 = 2. Prime factorizations: 4 = 2², 5 = 5, 6 = 2 × 3, and 7 = 7. LCM(4, 5, 6, 7) = 2² × 3 × 5 × 7 = 420. Subtracting the constant difference 2 gives: 420 – 2 = 418. Hence, the smallest number is 418. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q148. The product of two positive numbers is 54000 and their HCF is 30. How many distinct pairs of such numbers exist?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: Let the numbers be 30a and 30b with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Their product is 30a × 30b = 900ab = 54000 ⇒ ab = 54000 / 900 = 60. Prime factorization: 60 = 2² × 3¹ × 5¹. Co-prime factor pairs (a, b) with a < b are: (1, 60), (3, 20), (4, 15), and (5, 12). There are exactly 4 pairs. Thus, 4 pairs exist. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q149. What is the smallest perfect square positive integer that is completely divisible by each of 15, 18, 20, and 24?
(a) 1800 (b) 7200 (c) 14400 (d) 3600
Answer: (d) 3600
Solution: Prime factorizations: 15 = 3 × 5, 18 = 2 × 3², 20 = 2² × 5, and 24 = 2³ × 3. LCM = 2³ × 3² × 5¹ = 360. To form a perfect square, every prime factor exponent must be even. Multiplying 360 by 2¹ × 5¹ = 10 gives: 360 × 10 = 3600 = 60². Therefore, the smallest perfect square is 3600. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q150. The LCM of three numbers 12, 18, and x is 72, and their HCF is 6. What is the value of x?
(a) 18 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 48
Answer: (b) 24
Solution: Prime factorizations: 12 = 2² × 3 and 18 = 2 × 3². The LCM of 12 and 18 is 36 = 2² × 3². Given LCM(12, 18, x) = 72 = 2³ × 3², the prime factor 2³ must come from x. Since HCF(12, 18, x) = 6 = 2 × 3, x must contain 3¹ as a factor. Setting x = 2³ × 3¹ = 24 satisfies both HCF(12, 18, 24) = 6 and LCM(12, 18, 24) = 72. Therefore, the value of x is 24. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D
