131. Find the least multiple of 7 greater than 1000 which leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6.
(A) 1141 (B) 1111 (C) 1127 (D) 1151

132. The sum of two numbers is 192 and their LCM is 15 times their HCF. Find the numbers. (Both numbers are greater than 10).
(A) 12 and 180 (B) 72 and 120 (C) 96 and 96 (D) 48 and 144

133. Three numbers are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5 and their HCF is 120. Find the smallest of the numbers.
(A) 240 (B) 120 (C) 60 (D) 360

134. Find the smallest number which when divided by 7, 8, and 9 leaves remainders 5, 1, and 2 respectively.
(A) 425 (B) 419 (C) 431 (D) 407

135. Two runners start together on a circular track of length 4200 m with speeds 18 km/h and 24 km/h. After what minimum time (in minutes) will they meet again at the starting point?
(A) 42 min (B) 35 min (C) 49 min (D) 56 min

136. Find the least number which when divided by 15, 20, and 25 leaves remainders 7, 12, and 17 respectively.
(A) 292 (B) 300 (C) 308 (D) 285

(A) 322 (B) 299 (C) 207 (D) 414

138. Find the greatest number that will divide 43, 91, and 183 so as to leave the same remainder in each case.

(A) 152 (B) 76 (C) 38 (D) 114

144. The HCF of two numbers is 29 and their LCM is 4147. If one number is 319, find the other.
(A) 377 (B) 333 (C) 406 (D) 348

145. Find the least multiple of 9 which, when divided by 5, 7, and 11, leaves a remainder of 2 in each case.
(A) 1935 (B) 1932 (C) 1944 (D) 1947

146. The LCM of two numbers is 20 times their HCF. If the difference of the two numbers is 36, find their sum. (Given both numbers are positive integers.)
(A) 84 (B) 90 (C) 96 (D) 108

147. Find the smallest number that, when divided by 4, 5, 6, and 7, leaves remainders 2, 3, 4, and 5 respectively.
(A) 418 (B) 420 (C) 422 (D) 424

148. The product of two numbers is 21600 and their HCF is 30. How many such pairs of numbers exist?
(A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 5

149. Find the smallest perfect square exactly divisible by each of 15, 18, 20, and 24.
(A) 3600 (B) 14400 (C) 7200 (D) 21600
