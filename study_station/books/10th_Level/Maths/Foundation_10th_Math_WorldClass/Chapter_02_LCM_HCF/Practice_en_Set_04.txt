Questions 76–100: All Medium

Q76. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If their LCM is 225, what is their HCF?
(a) 15 (b) 30 (c) 45 (d) 60
Answer: (a) 15
Solution: Let the numbers be 3x and 5x. Because 3 and 5 are co-prime, their LCM is 3 × 5 × x = 15x. Given 15x = 225 ⇒ x = 15. The common factor x is the HCF = 15. Thus, their HCF is 15. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q77. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 47, 67, and 107 leaving a remainder of 7 in each case?
(a) 10 (b) 20 (c) 15 (d) 25
Answer: (b) 20
Solution: The required number must divide (47 – 7) = 40, (67 – 7) = 60, and (107 – 7) = 100 exactly. Factoring: 40 = 2³ × 5, 60 = 2² × 3 × 5, and 100 = 2² × 5². Common prime factors with lowest powers: 2² × 5¹ = 4 × 5 = 20. (Trap: Taking HCF of the original numbers misses the remainder subtraction step). Thus, the greatest number is 20. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q78. What is the LCM of the fractions 2/3, 4/9, and 8/27?
(a) 2/27 (b) 4/3 (c) 8/3 (d) 16/9
Answer: (c) 8/3
Solution: LCM of fractions equals LCM of numerators divided by HCF of denominators. Numerators: 2, 4, 8 ⇒ LCM(2, 4, 8) = 8. Denominators: 3, 9, 27 ⇒ HCF(3, 9, 27) = 3. Therefore, the LCM is 8/3. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q79. The sum of two numbers is 132 and their HCF is 12. How many distinct pairs of such numbers exist?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (d) 5
Solution: Let the numbers be 12a and 12b with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Their sum is 12(a + b) = 132 ⇒ a + b = 11. Since 11 is prime, all positive integer pairs (a, b) with a < b are co-prime: (1, 10), (2, 9), (3, 8), (4, 7), and (5, 6). Counting them gives exactly 5 pairs. Thus, there are 5 such pairs. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q80. What is the least perfect square positive integer that is completely divisible by 12, 15, and 20?
(a) 900 (b) 3600 (c) 1800 (d) 2400
Answer: (a) 900
Solution: Prime factorizations: 12 = 2² × 3, 15 = 3 × 5, and 20 = 2² × 5. LCM(12, 15, 20) = 2² × 3¹ × 5¹ = 60. To make 60 a perfect square, each prime factor must have an even exponent. Multiplying by 3 × 5 = 15 gives: 60 × 15 = 900 = 30². Hence, the least perfect square is 900. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB Group D

Q81. What is the HCF of the decimal numbers 0.54, 1.8, and 7.2?
(a) 0.09 (b) 0.18 (c) 0.36 (d) 1.8
Answer: (b) 0.18
Solution: Writing with two decimal places as fractions over 100: 54/100, 180/100, and 720/100. Finding HCF of the numerators: 54 = 2 × 3³, 180 = 2² × 3² × 5, and 720 = 2⁴ × 3² × 5. The common prime factors with lowest powers are 2¹ × 3² = 18. Dividing by 100 gives 18 / 100 = 0.18. Therefore, the HCF is 0.18. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL

Q82. The LCM of two numbers is 336 and their HCF is 6. If one of the numbers is 48, what is the other number?
(a) 36 (b) 40 (c) 42 (d) 44
Answer: (c) 42
Solution: Using the product formula: 48 × Other = LCM × HCF = 336 × 6 = 2016. Dividing by 48 gives: Other number = 2016 / 48 = 42. Thus, the other number is 42. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q83. The ratio of two numbers is 4 : 7 and their LCM is 140. What is the sum of the two numbers?
(a) 45 (b) 50 (c) 60 (d) 55
Answer: (d) 55
Solution: Let the numbers be 4k and 7k. Since 4 and 7 are co-prime, LCM = 4 × 7 × k = 28k. Given 28k = 140 ⇒ k = 5. The numbers are 4(5) = 20 and 7(5) = 35. Their sum is 20 + 35 = 55. Hence, the sum of the two numbers is 55. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC MTS

Q84. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 98, 138, and 178 leaving a remainder of 8 in each case?
(a) 10 (b) 15 (c) 20 (d) 25
Answer: (a) 10
Solution: Subtracting 8 from each number gives 98 – 8 = 90, 138 – 8 = 130, and 178 – 8 = 170. Factoring: 90 = 2 × 3² × 5, 130 = 2 × 5 × 13, and 170 = 2 × 5 × 17. The common prime factors are 2¹ × 5¹ = 10. Thus, HCF(90, 130, 170) = 10. Therefore, the greatest number is 10. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q85. Three bells toll at intervals of 9, 12, and 15 seconds respectively. If they toll together at 12:00:00 PM, at what time will they toll together next?
(a) 12:02 PM (b) 12:03 PM (c) 12:05 PM (d) 12:06 PM
Answer: (b) 12:03 PM
Solution: Prime factorizations: 9 = 3², 12 = 2² × 3, and 15 = 3 × 5. LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 180 seconds. Converting to minutes: 180 seconds = 3 minutes. Adding 3 minutes to 12:00:00 PM gives 12:03 PM. Thus, they will toll together at 12:03 PM. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D

Q86. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 16, 20, 24, and 28 leaves a remainder of 5 in each case?
(a) 1680 (b) 1675 (c) 1685 (d) 1690
Answer: (c) 1685
Solution: Prime factorizations: 16 = 2⁴, 20 = 2² × 5, 24 = 2³ × 3, and 28 = 2² × 7. LCM = 2⁴ × 3 × 5 × 7 = 16 × 105 = 1680. Adding the remainder 5 gives: 1680 + 5 = 1685. Hence, the smallest number is 1685. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL

Q87. The HCF of two numbers is 5 and their LCM is 360. If one of the numbers is 45, what is the other number?
(a) 36 (b) 45 (c) 50 (d) 40
Answer: (d) 40
Solution: Using the product formula: 45 × Other = 5 × 360 = 1800. Dividing by 45: Other number = 1800 / 45 = 40. Therefore, the other number is 40. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q88. The sum of two positive numbers is 384 and their HCF is 48. How many distinct pairs of such numbers exist?
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Let the numbers be 48a and 48b with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Their sum is 48(a + b) = 384 ⇒ a + b = 384 / 48 = 8. Co-prime pairs (a, b) summing to 8 with a < b are: (1, 7) and (3, 5). (Pairs (2, 6) and (4, 4) have gcd > 1). Thus, there are exactly 2 pairs. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC MTS

Q89. What is the HCF of the fractions 5/7, 10/21, and 15/28?
(a) 5/42 (b) 5/84 (c) 15/84 (d) 5/28
Answer: (b) 5/84
Solution: HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators divided by LCM of denominators. Numerators: 5, 10, 15 ⇒ HCF(5, 10, 15) = 5. Denominators: 7 = 7, 21 = 3 × 7, 28 = 2² × 7 ⇒ LCM(7, 21, 28) = 2² × 3 × 7 = 84. Therefore, the HCF is 5/84. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q90. What is the LCM of the decimal numbers 0.2, 0.25, and 0.4?
(a) 1 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 0.5
Answer: (c) 2
Solution: Writing as fractions: 0.2 = 1/5, 0.25 = 1/4, 0.4 = 2/5. LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators (1, 1, 2) / HCF of denominators (5, 4, 5) = 2 / 1 = 2. Alternatively, multiplying by 100 gives 20, 25, 40: LCM(20, 25, 40) = 200, and dividing by 100 gives 200 / 100 = 2. Thus, the LCM is 2. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB Group D

Q91. Three runners complete one round of a circular track in 10 seconds, 12 seconds, and 15 seconds respectively. If they start together, after how many seconds will they next meet at the starting point?
(a) 30 s (b) 45 s (c) 90 s (d) 60 s
Answer: (d) 60 s
Solution: The runners will meet at the starting point at an elapsed time equal to LCM(10, 12, 15). Prime factorizations: 10 = 2 × 5, 12 = 2² × 3, and 15 = 3 × 5. LCM = 2² × 3 × 5 = 60 seconds. Hence, they will meet after 60 s. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL

Q92. What is the largest positive integer that divides 124, 187, and 250, leaving remainders of 4, 7, and 10 respectively?
(a) 60 (b) 30 (c) 40 (d) 50
Answer: (a) 60
Solution: Subtracting the respective remainders: 124 – 4 = 120, 187 – 7 = 180, and 250 – 10 = 240. Finding HCF(120, 180, 240): 120 = 2³ × 3 × 5, 180 = 2² × 3² × 5, and 240 = 2⁴ × 3 × 5. Common prime factors with lowest powers: 2² × 3¹ × 5¹ = 4 × 3 × 5 = 60. Thus, the largest number is 60. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q93. The LCM of two numbers is 15 times their HCF. If the sum of their LCM and HCF is 256, what is the product of the two numbers?
(a) 2048 (b) 3840 (c) 2560 (d) 4096
Answer: (b) 3840
Solution: Let HCF = h, then LCM = 15h. Sum = h + 15h = 16h = 256 ⇒ h = 16. Then LCM = 15 × 16 = 240. By the product rule: Product of numbers = LCM × HCF = 240 × 16 = 3840. Therefore, the product of the numbers is 3840. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC MTS

Q94. What is the smallest positive integer which, when increased by 5, is completely divisible by 8, 12, and 18?
(a) 77 (b) 72 (c) 67 (d) 80
Answer: (c) 67
Solution: Let the number be N. Then N + 5 must be a multiple of LCM(8, 12, 18). Prime factorizations: 8 = 2³, 12 = 2² × 3, and 18 = 2 × 3². LCM = 2³ × 3² = 8 × 9 = 72. Setting N + 5 = 72 gives N = 72 – 5 = 67. (Trap: Adding 5 instead of subtracting gives 77, but 77 + 5 = 82 is not divisible by 8, 12, or 18). Thus, the smallest number is 67. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q95. The product of two co-prime numbers is 253. What is their LCM?
(a) 11 (b) 23 (c) 276 (d) 253
Answer: (d) 253
Solution: By definition, the HCF of any two co-prime numbers is 1. Using LCM × HCF = Product of numbers ⇒ LCM × 1 = 253 ⇒ LCM = 253. Hence, their LCM is 253. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB Group D

Q96. Three traffic lights change after every 48 seconds, 72 seconds, and 108 seconds respectively. If they all change together at 8:00:00 AM, at what time will they next change together?
(a) 8:07:12 AM (b) 8:06:48 AM (c) 8:08:24 AM (d) 8:09:00 AM
Answer: (a) 8:07:12 AM
Solution: Prime factorizations: 48 = 2⁴ × 3, 72 = 2³ × 3², and 108 = 2² × 3³. LCM(48, 72, 108) = 2⁴ × 3³ = 16 × 27 = 432 seconds. Converting to minutes: 432 seconds = 7 minutes and 12 seconds. Adding to 8:00:00 AM gives 8:07:12 AM. Thus, they will next change together at 8:07:12 AM. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q97. What is the LCM of the decimal numbers 0.12, 0.18, and 0.24?
(a) 0.36 (b) 0.72 (c) 0.48 (d) 1.44
Answer: (b) 0.72
Solution: Multiplying by 100 gives 12, 18, and 24. Prime factorizations: 12 = 2² × 3, 18 = 2 × 3², and 24 = 2³ × 3. LCM(12, 18, 24) = 2³ × 3² = 8 × 9 = 72. Dividing by 100 gives 72 / 100 = 0.72. Therefore, the LCM is 0.72. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q98. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 6. If their HCF is 8, what is their LCM?
(a) 120 (b) 180 (c) 240 (d) 360
Answer: (c) 240
Solution: Because 5 and 6 are co-prime, the numbers are 5 × 8 = 40 and 6 × 8 = 48. Their LCM is 5 × 6 × HCF = 30 × 8 = 240. Thus, their LCM is 240. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q99. What is the greatest four-digit number that is completely divisible by 15, 25, and 40?
(a) 9800 (b) 9900 (c) 9950 (d) 9600
Answer: (d) 9600
Solution: Prime factorizations: 15 = 3 × 5, 25 = 5², and 40 = 2³ × 5. LCM(15, 25, 40) = 2³ × 3 × 5² = 8 × 3 × 25 = 600. The largest 4-digit number is 9999. Dividing 9999 by 600: 9999 = 600 × 16 + 399 (remainder = 399). Subtracting the remainder gives 9999 – 399 = 9600. Hence, the greatest four-digit number is 9600. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q100. Two numbers are 28 and 42. If their LCM is 84, what is their HCF?
(a) 7 (b) 14 (c) 21 (d) 6
Answer: (b) 14
Solution: Using the fundamental relationship: Product of numbers = LCM × HCF ⇒ 28 × 42 = 84 × HCF ⇒ 1176 = 84 × HCF. Dividing by 84 gives: HCF = 1176 / 84 = 14. Therefore, their HCF is 14. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D
