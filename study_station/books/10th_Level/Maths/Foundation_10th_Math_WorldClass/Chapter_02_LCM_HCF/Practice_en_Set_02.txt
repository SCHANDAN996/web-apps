Q26. The LCM of two numbers is 182 and their HCF is 13. If one of the numbers is 26, what is the value of the other number?
(a) 91 (b) 78 (c) 104 (d) 65
Answer: (a) 91
Solution: Using the product rule: Product of numbers = LCM × HCF ⇒ 26 × Other = 182 × 13 = 2366. Dividing by 26 gives: Other number = 2366 / 26 = 91. Thus, the other number is 91. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q27. Three numbers are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4 and their LCM is 240. What is their HCF?
(a) 10 (b) 20 (c) 15 (d) 40
Answer: (b) 20
Solution: Let the numbers be 2x, 3x, and 4x. The LCM of the coefficients 2, 3, and 4 is LCM(2, 3, 4) = 12 (not 2 × 3 × 4 = 24, because 2 divides 4). Thus, the LCM of the numbers is 12x. Given 12x = 240 ⇒ x = 20. The numbers are 40, 60, and 80, whose HCF is x = 20. (Trap: Taking 2 × 3 × 4 = 24 gives 24x = 240 ⇒ x = 10, which is incorrect because 2 and 4 are not co-prime). Hence, their HCF is 20. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q28. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 70 and 125, leaving remainders of 5 and 8 respectively?
(a) 15 (b) 17 (c) 13 (d) 65
Answer: (c) 13
Solution: The required number must exactly divide (70 – 5) = 65 and (125 – 8) = 117. Factoring the differences: 65 = 5 × 13 and 117 = 3² × 13. The common prime factor is 13, so HCF(65, 117) = 13. Therefore, the greatest number is 13. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q29. The LCM of two numbers is 12 times their HCF. The sum of their LCM and HCF is 403. If one of the numbers is 93, what is the other number?
(a) 186 (b) 155 (c) 217 (d) 124
Answer: (d) 124
Solution: Let HCF = h, then LCM = 12h. Given: h + 12h = 403 ⇒ 13h = 403 ⇒ h = 31. Therefore, LCM = 12 × 31 = 372. By the product formula: 93 × Other = 372 × 31 = 11532 ⇒ Other number = 11532 / 93 = 124. Thus, the other number is 124. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q30. What is the HCF of the fractions 2/3, 4/5, and 6/7?
(a) 2/105 (b) 1/105 (c) 2/35 (d) 1/35
Answer: (a) 2/105
Solution: The HCF of fractions equals HCF of numerators divided by LCM of denominators. Numerators are 2, 4, and 6: HCF(2, 4, 6) = 2. Denominators are 3, 5, and 7: LCM(3, 5, 7) = 3 × 5 × 7 = 105. Therefore, the HCF is 2/105. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB Group D

Q31. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 15, 20, and 25 leaves a remainder of 8 in each case?
(a) 300 (b) 308 (c) 292 (d) 608
Answer: (b) 308
Solution: Prime factorizations: 15 = 3 × 5, 20 = 2² × 5, and 25 = 5². LCM(15, 20, 25) = 2² × 3 × 5² = 4 × 3 × 25 = 300. Adding the common remainder 8 gives: 300 + 8 = 308. Hence, the smallest number is 308. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL

Q32. Two numbers have an LCM of 231 and an HCF of 11. If the first number is 33, what is the second number?
(a) 55 (b) 88 (c) 77 (d) 99
Answer: (c) 77
Solution: Using the product formula: First × Second = LCM × HCF ⇒ 33 × Second = 231 × 11 = 2541. Dividing by 33 gives: Second number = 2541 / 33 = 77. Thus, the second number is 77. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q33. What is the least positive integer which is a perfect square and is completely divisible by each of 8, 12, 15, and 20?
(a) 14400 (b) 2400 (c) 4800 (d) 3600
Answer: (d) 3600
Solution: Prime factorizations: 8 = 2³, 12 = 2² × 3, 15 = 3 × 5, and 20 = 2² × 5. LCM(8, 12, 15, 20) = 2³ × 3¹ × 5¹ = 120. To make 120 a perfect square, all prime exponents must be made even. The missing prime factors are 2¹, 3¹, and 5¹, so we multiply by 2 × 3 × 5 = 30: 120 × 30 = 3600 = 60². Therefore, the least perfect square is 3600. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC MTS

Q34. Four bells toll at intervals of 8, 12, 16, and 28 seconds respectively. If they all toll together at 1:00 AM, at what time will they toll together again?
(a) 1:05:36 AM (b) 1:06:12 AM (c) 1:04:48 AM (d) 1:05:12 AM
Answer: (a) 1:05:36 AM
Solution: The bells toll together after an interval equal to the LCM of 8, 12, 16, and 28 seconds. Prime factorizations: 8 = 2³, 12 = 2² × 3, 16 = 2⁴, and 28 = 2² × 7. LCM = 2⁴ × 3 × 7 = 16 × 21 = 336 seconds. Converting to minutes: 336 seconds = 5 minutes and 36 seconds. Adding to 1:00:00 AM gives 1:05:36 AM. Hence, they toll together at 1:05:36 AM. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q35. The HCF of two numbers is 11 and their LCM is 7700. If one of the numbers is 275, what is the other number?
(a) 280 (b) 308 (c) 292 (d) 260
Answer: (b) 308
Solution: Using the product formula: 275 × Other = HCF × LCM = 11 × 7700 = 84700. Dividing by 275 gives: Other number = 84700 / 275 = 308. Thus, the other number is 308. Hence, the correct option is (b).

Q36. Find the least positive integer which when divided by 12, 16, 24, and 36 leaves a remainder of 7 in each case.
(a) 144 (b) 145 (c) 151 (d) 155
Answer: (c) 151
Solution: Prime factorizations: 12 = 2² × 3, 16 = 2⁴, 24 = 2³ × 3, and 36 = 2² × 3². LCM(12, 16, 24, 36) = 2⁴ × 3² = 16 × 9 = 144. Adding the remainder 7 gives 144 + 7 = 151. Therefore, the required least number is 151. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL

Q37. The ratio of two positive numbers is 4 : 5 and their LCM is 140. What are the two numbers?
(a) 20, 25 (b) 40, 50 (c) 24, 30 (d) 28, 35
Answer: (d) 28, 35
Solution: Let the numbers be 4x and 5x. Because 4 and 5 are co-prime, their LCM is 4 × 5 × x = 20x. Given 20x = 140 ⇒ x = 7. The numbers are 4(7) = 28 and 5(7) = 35. Hence, the numbers are 28, 35. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q38. What is the HCF of the decimal numbers 0.63, 1.05, and 2.1?
(a) 0.21 (b) 0.105 (c) 1.05 (d) 0.42
Answer: (a) 0.21
Solution: Expressing each number with two decimal places as integers divided by 100: 63/100, 105/100, and 210/100. Finding the HCF of the numerators: 63 = 3² × 7, 105 = 3 × 5 × 7, and 210 = 2 × 3 × 5 × 7. Common prime factors are 3¹ × 7¹ = 21. Dividing by 100 gives 21 / 100 = 0.21. Thus, the HCF is 0.21. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC MTS

Q39. The LCM of two numbers is 495 and their HCF is 5. If the sum of the two numbers is 100, what are the two numbers?
(a) 25, 75 (b) 45, 55 (c) 35, 65 (d) 30, 70
Answer: (b) 45, 55
Solution: Let the numbers be 5a and 5b with gcd(a, b) = 1. Then LCM = 5ab = 495 ⇒ ab = 99. The sum is 5(a + b) = 100 ⇒ a + b = 20. The co-prime factors of 99 that sum to 20 are a = 9 and b = 11 (since 9 × 11 = 99 and 9 + 11 = 20). The numbers are 5(9) = 45 and 5(11) = 55. Thus, the numbers are 45, 55. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q40. What is the greatest positive integer that exactly divides 306, 450, and 540?
(a) 12 (b) 30 (c) 18 (d) 36
Answer: (c) 18
Solution: The greatest common divisor is the HCF. Factoring: 306 = 2 × 3² × 17, 450 = 2 × 3² × 5², and 540 = 2² × 3³ × 5. Common prime factors with lowest powers are 2¹ and 3²: HCF = 2 × 9 = 18. Thus, the greatest number is 18. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB Group D

Q41. What is the LCM of the fractions 1/3, 5/6, and 4/9?
(a) 5/18 (b) 5/3 (c) 20/9 (d) 20/3
Answer: (d) 20/3
Solution: LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators divided by HCF of denominators. Numerators are 1, 5, and 4: LCM(1, 5, 4) = 20. Denominators are 3, 6, and 9: HCF(3, 6, 9) = 3. Therefore, the LCM is 20/3. Hence, the correct option is (d).

Q42. The sum of two numbers is 84 and their HCF is 12. How many distinct pairs of such numbers exist?
(a) 3 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 3
Solution: Let the two numbers be 12a and 12b with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Their sum is 12a + 12b = 84 ⇒ 12(a + b) = 84 ⇒ a + b = 7. Pairs of positive integers (a, b) with a < b and sum 7 are: (1, 6), (2, 5), and (3, 4). All three pairs are mutually co-prime (gcd is 1). The corresponding pairs of numbers are (12, 72), (24, 60), and (36, 48), each having HCF = 12. Thus, there are exactly 3 pairs. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q43. A rectangular field of length 120 m and width 75 m is to be partitioned into identical square plots of the maximum possible size. What is the minimum number of square plots obtained?
(a) 90 (b) 40 (c) 60 (d) 120
Answer: (b) 40
Solution: For maximum plot size, the side of the square must be the HCF of length and width: HCF(120, 75). 120 = 15 × 8 and 75 = 15 × 5, so HCF = 15 m. The minimum number of square plots equals Total Area divided by Area of one plot: (120 × 75) / (15 × 15) = 8 × 5 = 40 plots. Thus, the minimum number of plots is 40. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC MTS

Q44. The product of two positive numbers is 4107 and their HCF is 37. What is their LCM?
(a) 4107 (b) 1517 (c) 111 (d) 303
Answer: (c) 111
Solution: Using the fundamental identity: Product of numbers = HCF × LCM ⇒ 4107 = 37 × LCM. Dividing by 37 gives: LCM = 4107 / 37 = 111. Therefore, their LCM is 111. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q45. What is the smallest 5-digit number that is completely divisible by 12, 15, and 18?
(a) 10020 (b) 10100 (c) 10200 (d) 10080
Answer: (d) 10080
Solution: First find LCM(12, 15, 18): 12 = 2² × 3, 15 = 3 × 5, and 18 = 2 × 3². LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 180. The smallest 5-digit number is 10000. Dividing 10000 by 180: 10000 = 180 × 55 + 100 (remainder = 100). To find the next multiple, add 180 – 100 = 80: 10000 + 80 = 10080. Hence, the smallest divisible 5-digit number is 10080. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB Group D

Q46. The HCF of two three-digit numbers is 17 and their LCM is 714. What is the sum of the two numbers?
(a) 221 (b) 238 (c) 255 (d) 289
Answer: (a) 221
Solution: Let the numbers be 17a and 17b with gcd(a, b) = 1. Then LCM = 17ab = 714 ⇒ ab = 42. Factor pairs of 42 with gcd 1: (1, 42), (2, 21), (3, 14), and (6, 7). For both numbers to have three digits (≥ 100): 17 × 3 = 51 (only 2 digits, fails constraint), whereas 17 × 6 = 102 and 17 × 7 = 119 are both three-digit numbers. Adding the two numbers gives 102 + 119 = 221. (Trap: Using factor pair (3, 14) gives 51 and 238 whose sum is 289, but 51 is not a three-digit number). Thus, the sum is 221. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q47. What is the least positive integer which when divided by 5, 6, 7, and 8 leaves a remainder of 3 in each case, but is completely divisible by 9?
(a) 1173 (b) 1683 (c) 1203 (d) 1023
Answer: (b) 1683
Solution: LCM(5, 6, 7, 8) = 840. The number has the form 840k + 3. For divisibility by 9: 840k + 3 ≡ 3k + 3 = 3(k + 1) ≡ 0 (mod 9) because 840 = 93 × 9 + 3. This requires k + 1 to be a multiple of 3, so smallest k = 2. For k = 2: 840(2) + 3 = 1680 + 3 = 1683. Dividing 1683 by 9 gives 187 with 0 remainder. Hence, the least number is 1683. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q48. The LCM of two positive numbers is 4 times their HCF. If the product of the two numbers is 64, what is their HCF?
(a) 16 (b) 8 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: Let HCF = h, then LCM = 4h. By the product formula: Product of numbers = LCM × HCF ⇒ 64 = 4h × h = 4h² ⇒ h² = 16 ⇒ h = 4. Therefore, their HCF is 4. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q49. A, B, and C start running simultaneously on a circular track of length 3600 m at speeds of 12 m/s, 18 m/s, and 20 m/s respectively in the same direction. After what minimum time will they meet again for the first time at the starting point?
(a) 20 min (b) 25 min (c) 40 min (d) 30 min
Answer: (d) 30 min
Solution: Time taken by each person to complete one full lap: Time for A = 3600 / 12 = 300 seconds, Time for B = 3600 / 18 = 200 seconds, and Time for C = 3600 / 20 = 180 seconds. They will meet at the starting point at the LCM of their lap times: LCM(300, 200, 180). Prime factorizations: 300 = 2² × 3 × 5², 200 = 2³ × 5², and 180 = 2² × 3² × 5. LCM = 2³ × 3² × 5² = 8 × 9 × 25 = 1800 seconds. Converting to minutes: 1800 / 60 = 30 minutes. Thus, they will meet after 30 min. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q50. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 148, 246, and 623 leaving remainders of 4, 6, and 11 respectively?
(a) 16 (b) 12 (c) 18 (d) 24
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: The required number must divide (148 – 4) = 144, (246 – 6) = 240, and (623 – 11) = 612 exactly. Factoring each number: 144 = 2⁴ × 3², 240 = 2⁴ × 3 × 5, and 612 = 2² × 3² × 17. The common prime factors with lowest powers are 2² and 3¹. HCF = 2² × 3 = 4 × 3 = 12. Therefore, the greatest number is 12. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D
