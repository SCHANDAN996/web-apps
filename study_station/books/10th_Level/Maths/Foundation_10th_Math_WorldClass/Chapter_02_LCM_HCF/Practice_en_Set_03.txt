Set 3: LCM & HCF MCQs (English)
Questions 51–75: All Medium

Q51. The LCM of two numbers is 252 and their HCF is 6. If one of the numbers is 42, what is the value of the other number?
(a) 36 (b) 48 (c) 28 (d) 30
Answer: (a) 36
Solution: Using the product formula: Product of numbers = LCM × HCF ⇒ 42 × Other = 252 × 6 = 1512. Dividing by 42: Other number = 1512 / 42 = 36. Thus, the other number is 36. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q52. Three numbers are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5 and their LCM is 2400. What is their HCF?
(a) 20 (b) 40 (c) 30 (d) 60
Answer: (b) 40
Solution: Let the numbers be 3x, 4x, and 5x. Because 3, 4, and 5 are pairwise co-prime, LCM(3, 4, 5) = 3 × 4 × 5 = 60. So LCM of the numbers is 60x. Given 60x = 2400 ⇒ x = 40. The common factor x is the HCF = 40. Hence, their HCF is 40. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q53. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 85 and 153, leaving remainders of 5 and 9 respectively?
(a) 13 (b) 17 (c) 16 (d) 19
Answer: (c) 16
Solution: The required number must divide (85 – 5) = 80 and (153 – 9) = 144 exactly. Factoring: 80 = 2⁴ × 5 and 144 = 2⁴ × 3². The common factor with lowest power is 2⁴ = 16. Thus, HCF(80, 144) = 16. Therefore, the greatest number is 16. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q54. The product of two positive numbers is 2028 and their HCF is 13. What is their LCM?
(a) 169 (b) 144 (c) 132 (d) 156
Answer: (d) 156
Solution: Using the fundamental identity: LCM × HCF = Product of numbers ⇒ LCM × 13 = 2028 ⇒ LCM = 2028 / 13 = 156. Hence, the LCM is 156. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q55. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 24, 36, and 48 leaves a remainder of 8 in each case?
(a) 152 (b) 144 (c) 136 (d) 160
Answer: (a) 152
Solution: Prime factorizations: 24 = 2³ × 3, 36 = 2² × 3², and 48 = 2⁴ × 3. LCM(24, 36, 48) = 2⁴ × 3² = 16 × 9 = 144. Adding the remainder 8 gives: 144 + 8 = 152. Thus, the smallest number is 152. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB Group D

Q56. The ratio of two positive numbers is 5 : 6 and their LCM is 480. What are the two numbers?
(a) 50 and 60 (b) 80 and 96 (c) 60 and 72 (d) 100 and 120
Answer: (b) 80 and 96
Solution: Let the numbers be 5k and 6k. Since 5 and 6 are co-prime, LCM = 5 × 6 × k = 30k. Given 30k = 480 ⇒ k = 16. The numbers are 5(16) = 80 and 6(16) = 96. Thus, the numbers are 80 and 96. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL

Q57. The LCM of two numbers is 14 times their HCF. The sum of the LCM and HCF is 600. If one of the numbers is 280, what is the other number?
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 80 (d) 72
Answer: (c) 80
Solution: Let HCF = h, then LCM = 14h. Given: h + 14h = 600 ⇒ 15h = 600 ⇒ h = 40. Therefore, LCM = 14 × 40 = 560. By the product formula: 280 × Other = 560 × 40 = 22400 ⇒ Other number = 22400 / 280 = 80. Hence, the other number is 80. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q58. What is the HCF of the fractions 3/4, 5/6, and 7/8?
(a) 1/12 (b) 1/16 (c) 1/48 (d) 1/24
Answer: (d) 1/24
Solution: HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators divided by LCM of denominators. Numerators: 3, 5, 7 are pairwise co-prime, so HCF(3, 5, 7) = 1. Denominators: 4 = 2², 6 = 2 × 3, 8 = 2³. LCM(4, 6, 8) = 2³ × 3 = 24. Therefore, the HCF is 1/24. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC MTS

Q59. The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 23 and 1449 respectively. If one of the numbers is 161, what is the value of the other number?
(a) 207 (b) 217 (c) 184 (d) 230
Answer: (a) 207
Solution: Using the product formula: 161 × Other = 23 × 1449 = 33327. Notice 161 = 23 × 7, so dividing by 161 gives: Other number = 1449 / 7 = 207. Thus, the other number is 207. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q60. What is the least perfect square positive integer which is completely divisible by 9, 15, and 20?
(a) 1800 (b) 900 (c) 3600 (d) 2025
Answer: (b) 900
Solution: Prime factorizations: 9 = 3², 15 = 3 × 5, and 20 = 2² × 5. LCM(9, 15, 20) = 2² × 3² × 5¹ = 180. To make 180 a perfect square, each prime factor must have an even exponent. Multiplying by 5 gives 180 × 5 = 900 = 30². Hence, the least perfect square is 900. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D

Q61. Four bells toll at intervals of 10, 15, 20, and 30 seconds respectively. If they all toll together at 10:00 AM, at what time will they toll together next?
(a) 10:00:30 AM (b) 10:02 AM (c) 10:01 AM (d) 10:10 AM
Answer: (c) 10:01 AM
Solution: The bells toll together after an interval equal to the LCM of 10, 15, 20, and 30 seconds. Prime factorizations: 10 = 2 × 5, 15 = 3 × 5, 20 = 2² × 5, and 30 = 2 × 3 × 5. LCM = 2² × 3 × 5 = 60 seconds = 1 minute. Adding 1 minute to 10:00 AM gives 10:01 AM. Hence, they will toll together at 10:01 AM. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL

Q62. The sum of two positive numbers is 216 and their HCF is 27. How many distinct pairs of such numbers exist?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (d) 2
Solution: Let the numbers be 27a and 27b with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Their sum is 27(a + b) = 216 ⇒ a + b = 8. Co-prime pairs (a, b) with sum 8 and a < b are (1, 7) and (3, 5). (Pairs (2, 6) and (4, 4) have gcd > 1). Thus, there are exactly 2 pairs. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q63. A rectangular floor measuring 90 m by 60 m is to be paved entirely with identical square tiles of the largest possible size. What is the minimum number of tiles required?
(a) 6 (b) 30 (c) 15 (d) 10
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: For maximum tile size, the side of the square tile must be HCF(90, 60) = 30 m. The question asks for the minimum number of tiles, not the side length. Area of one tile = 30 × 30 = 900 m². Total floor area = 90 × 60 = 5400 m². Minimum number of tiles = 5400 / 900 = 6 tiles. (Trap: Answering 30 confuses the tile dimension with the count of tiles). Hence, the minimum number of tiles is 6. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC MTS

Q64. What is the LCM of the decimal numbers 0.24, 0.36, and 0.48?
(a) 0.72 (b) 1.44 (c) 2.88 (d) 1.20
Answer: (b) 1.44
Solution: Converting to integers by multiplying by 100: 24, 36, and 48. Prime factorizations: 24 = 2³ × 3, 36 = 2² × 3², and 48 = 2⁴ × 3. LCM(24, 36, 48) = 2⁴ × 3² = 144. Dividing by 100 yields 144 / 100 = 1.44. Therefore, the LCM is 1.44. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q65. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 7, 8, and 9 leaves a remainder of 4 in each case?
(a) 504 (b) 500 (c) 508 (d) 512
Answer: (c) 508
Solution: Since 7, 8, and 9 are pairwise co-prime, LCM(7, 8, 9) = 7 × 8 × 9 = 504. Adding the common remainder 4 gives: 504 + 4 = 508. Thus, the smallest number is 508. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB Group D

Q66. The LCM of two numbers is 5 times their HCF. If the product of the two numbers is 1620, what is their HCF?
(a) 15 (b) 20 (c) 12 (d) 18
Answer: (d) 18
Solution: Let HCF = h, then LCM = 5h. By the product rule: Product of numbers = LCM × HCF ⇒ 1620 = 5h × h = 5h² ⇒ h² = 324 ⇒ h = 18. Therefore, their HCF is 18. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL

Q67. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 121, 226, and 259 leaving the same remainder in each case?
(a) 3 (b) 7 (c) 9 (d) 11
Answer: (a) 3
Solution: When numbers leave the same remainder, the divisor must divide the pairwise differences of the numbers: 226 – 121 = 105, 259 – 226 = 33, and 259 – 121 = 138. Finding HCF(105, 33, 138): 105 = 3 × 5 × 7, 33 = 3 × 11, and 138 = 2 × 3 × 23. The only common factor is 3. (Trap: Taking HCF of the original numbers gives 1, which misses the common remainder divisor). Hence, the greatest divisor is 3. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q68. Two numbers are in the ratio 8 : 9. If their HCF is 6, what is their LCM?
(a) 408 (b) 432 (c) 386 (d) 412
Answer: (b) 432
Solution: Because 8 and 9 are co-prime, the numbers are 8 × 6 = 48 and 9 × 6 = 54. Their LCM is 8 × 9 × HCF = 72 × 6 = 432. Thus, their LCM is 432. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC MTS

Q69. In a school assembly, students can be arranged in rows of 12, 15, or 24, with 3 students left over in each case. What is the minimum number of students present?
(a) 120 (b) 117 (c) 123 (d) 243
Answer: (c) 123
Solution: The minimum number is LCM(12, 15, 24) + 3. Prime factorizations: 12 = 2² × 3, 15 = 3 × 5, and 24 = 2³ × 3. LCM = 2³ × 3 × 5 = 120. Adding the 3 remainder students gives 120 + 3 = 123. Hence, the minimum number of students is 123. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q70. What is the HCF of 136, 170, and 255?
(a) 34 (b) 51 (c) 68 (d) 17
Answer: (d) 17
Solution: Factoring each number: 136 = 8 × 17 = 2³ × 17, 170 = 10 × 17 = 2 × 5 × 17, and 255 = 15 × 17 = 3 × 5 × 17. The only common prime factor among all three numbers is 17. Thus, the HCF is 17. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB Group D

Q71. Two co-prime numbers have an LCM of 391. What are the two numbers?
(a) 17 and 23 (b) 13 and 31 (c) 7 and 53 (d) 11 and 35
Answer: (a) 17 and 23
Solution: For any two co-prime numbers, their LCM equals their product: a × b = 391. Factoring 391 gives 391 = 17 × 23, where both 17 and 23 are prime numbers and hence co-prime. Therefore, the numbers are 17 and 23. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q72. What is the least number that must be subtracted from 1936 so that the remaining number is completely divisible by 9, 10, and 15?
(a) 36 (b) 46 (c) 56 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 46
Solution: First find LCM(9, 10, 15): 9 = 3², 10 = 2 × 5, 15 = 3 × 5. LCM = 2 × 3² × 5 = 90. Dividing 1936 by 90: 1936 = 90 × 21 + 46 (remainder is 46). Subtracting the remainder 46 gives 1936 – 46 = 1890, which is divisible by 90. Hence, the least number to subtract is 46. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q73. Three machines sound alarms at intervals of 5 minutes, 8 minutes, and 12 minutes respectively. If they all sound together at 9:00 AM, at what time will they sound together next?
(a) 10:00 AM (b) 10:20 AM (c) 11:00 AM (d) 11:40 AM
Answer: (c) 11:00 AM
Solution: The alarms will sound together after an interval equal to LCM(5, 8, 12). Factoring: 5 = 5, 8 = 2³, and 12 = 2² × 3. LCM = 2³ × 3 × 5 = 120 minutes = 2 hours. Adding 2 hours to 9:00 AM gives 11:00 AM. Thus, they will sound together at 11:00 AM. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q74. What is the smallest four-digit number that is completely divisible by 18, 24, and 32?
(a) 1008 (b) 1248 (c) 1296 (d) 1152
Answer: (d) 1152
Solution: Prime factorizations: 18 = 2 × 3², 24 = 2³ × 3, and 32 = 2⁵. LCM(18, 24, 32) = 2⁵ × 3² = 32 × 9 = 288. The smallest 4-digit number is 1000. Dividing 1000 by 288: 1000 = 288 × 3 + 136 (remainder = 136). The required smallest 4-digit number is 1000 + (288 – 136) = 1152. (Check: 288 × 4 = 1152). Therefore, the number is 1152. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q75. The LCM of two numbers is 28 times their HCF. The sum of the LCM and HCF is 1740. If one of the numbers is 480, what is the value of the other number?
(a) 180 (b) 210 (c) 240 (d) 300
Answer: (b) 210
Solution: Let HCF = h, then LCM = 28h. Given: h + 28h = 1740 ⇒ 29h = 1740 ⇒ h = 60. Therefore, LCM = 28 × 60 = 1680. Using the product formula: 480 × Other = 1680 × 60 = 100800 ⇒ Other number = 100800 / 480 = 210. Thus, the value of the other number is 210. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D
