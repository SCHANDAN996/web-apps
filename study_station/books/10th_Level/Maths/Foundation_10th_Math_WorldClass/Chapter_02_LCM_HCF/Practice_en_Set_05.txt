Set 5: LCM & HCF MCQs (English)
Questions 101–125: Medium & Hard

Q101. The product of two positive numbers is 2160 and their HCF is 6. What is their LCM?
(a) 360 (b) 180 (c) 720 (d) 90
Answer: (a) 360
Solution: Using the fundamental identity: LCM = Product of numbers / HCF = 2160 / 6 = 360. Thus, their LCM is 360. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q102. The ratio of two positive numbers is 7 : 8 and their HCF is 11. What is the sum of the two numbers?
(a) 154 (b) 165 (c) 176 (d) 187
Answer: (b) 165
Solution: The two numbers are 7 × 11 = 77 and 8 × 11 = 88. Their sum is 77 + 88 = 165. Hence, the sum of the numbers is 165. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q103. What is the greatest positive integer that divides 38, 50, and 74 leaving a remainder of 2 in each case?
(a) 18 (b) 24 (c) 12 (d) 36
Answer: (c) 12
Solution: Subtracting 2 from each number gives 38 – 2 = 36, 50 – 2 = 48, and 74 – 2 = 72. Factoring: 36 = 2² × 3², 48 = 2⁴ × 3, and 72 = 2³ × 3². Common prime factors with lowest powers are 2² × 3¹ = 12. Thus, HCF(36, 48, 72) = 12. Therefore, the greatest number is 12. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q104. What is the LCM of the fractions 2/5, 4/15, and 6/25?
(a) 2/25 (b) 6/5 (c) 12/25 (d) 12/5
Answer: (d) 12/5
Solution: LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators divided by HCF of denominators. Numerators: 2, 4, 6 ⇒ LCM(2, 4, 6) = 12. Denominators: 5, 15, 25 ⇒ HCF(5, 15, 25) = 5. Therefore, the LCM is 12/5. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q105. The product of two positive numbers is 3072 and their HCF is 16. How many distinct pairs of such numbers exist?
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Let the numbers be 16a and 16b with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Their product is 16a × 16b = 256ab = 3072 ⇒ ab = 3072 / 256 = 12. The co-prime factor pairs (a, b) of 12 with a < b are (1, 12) and (3, 4). (Pair (2, 6) is not co-prime as gcd(2, 6) = 2). Thus, there are exactly 2 pairs. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB Group D

Q106. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 20, 25, 35, and 40 leaves remainders of 14, 19, 29, and 34 respectively?
(a) 1400 (b) 1394 (c) 1406 (d) 1390
Answer: (b) 1394
Solution: Notice that the difference between each divisor and its corresponding remainder is constant: 20 – 14 = 6, 25 – 19 = 6, 35 – 29 = 6, and 40 – 34 = 6. The required number has the form LCM(20, 25, 35, 40) – 6. Prime factorizations: 20 = 2² × 5, 25 = 5², 35 = 5 × 7, and 40 = 2³ × 5. LCM = 2³ × 5² × 7 = 8 × 25 × 7 = 1400. Subtracting the constant difference 6: 1400 – 6 = 1394. (Trap: Adding 6 gives 1406, which leaves incorrect remainders). Thus, the smallest number is 1394. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CGL

Q107. Three numbers are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3 and their HCF is 12. What is their LCM?
(a) 36 (b) 48 (c) 72 (d) 144
Answer: (c) 72
Solution: Since the HCF is 12, the numbers are 1 × 12 = 12, 2 × 12 = 24, and 3 × 12 = 36. Finding their LCM: 12 = 2² × 3, 24 = 2³ × 3, and 36 = 2² × 3². LCM = 2³ × 3² = 8 × 9 = 72. Hence, their LCM is 72. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q108. The LCM of two numbers is 495 and their HCF is 5. If the sum of the two numbers is 100, what is the positive difference between the two numbers?
(a) 5 (b) 15 (c) 20 (d) 10
Answer: (d) 10
Solution: Let the numbers be 5a and 5b with gcd(a, b) = 1. Then LCM = 5ab = 495 ⇒ ab = 99. The sum is 5(a + b) = 100 ⇒ a + b = 20. The co-prime factors of 99 summing to 20 are a = 11 and b = 9. The numbers are 5(11) = 55 and 5(9) = 45. Their positive difference is 55 – 45 = 10. Thus, the difference between the numbers is 10. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC MTS

Q109. What is the least positive number that must be added to 10000 so that the sum is completely divisible by 24, 30, and 36?
(a) 80 (b) 100 (c) 120 (d) 60
Answer: (a) 80
Solution: Prime factorizations: 24 = 2³ × 3, 30 = 2 × 3 × 5, and 36 = 2² × 3². LCM(24, 30, 36) = 2³ × 3² × 5 = 8 × 9 × 5 = 360. Dividing 10000 by 360: 10000 = 360 × 27 + 280 (remainder is 280). To make it divisible, add 360 – 280 = 80: 10000 + 80 = 10080. Therefore, the least number to add is 80. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q110. What is the HCF of the decimal numbers 2.4, 3.6, and 4.8?
(a) 0.6 (b) 1.2 (c) 2.4 (d) 0.12
Answer: (b) 1.2
Solution: Expressing each number as a fraction over 10: 24/10, 36/10, and 48/10. The HCF of the numerators (24, 36, 48) is 12. Dividing by 10 gives 12 / 10 = 1.2. Hence, the HCF is 1.2. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D

Q111. The LCM of two numbers is 420 and their HCF is 15. If one of the numbers is 60, what is the other number?
(a) 90 (b) 120 (c) 105 (d) 75
Answer: (c) 105
Solution: Using the product formula: 60 × Other = LCM × HCF = 420 × 15 = 6300. Dividing by 60 gives: Other number = 6300 / 60 = 105. Thus, the other number is 105. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC CGL

Q112. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7 and their LCM is 315. What is their HCF?
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 9
Answer: (d) 9
Solution: Let the numbers be 5k and 7k. Since 5 and 7 are co-prime, LCM = 5 × 7 × k = 35k. Given 35k = 315 ⇒ k = 315 / 35 = 9. The common factor k is the HCF = 9. Therefore, their HCF is 9. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q113. What is the greatest four-digit number that is completely divisible by 15, 20, and 25?
(a) 9900 (b) 9800 (c) 9600 (d) 9950
Answer: (a) 9900
Solution: Prime factorizations: 15 = 3 × 5, 20 = 2² × 5, and 25 = 5². LCM(15, 20, 25) = 2² × 3 × 5² = 300. The largest 4-digit number is 9999. Dividing 9999 by 300: 9999 = 300 × 33 + 99 (remainder = 99). Subtracting the remainder gives 9999 – 99 = 9900. Hence, the greatest number is 9900. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC MTS

Q114. Three bells toll at intervals of 9, 12, and 15 minutes respectively. If they toll together at 10:00 AM, at what time will they next toll together?
(a) 12:00 PM (b) 1:00 PM (c) 1:30 PM (d) 2:00 PM
Answer: (b) 1:00 PM
Solution: Prime factorizations: 9 = 3², 12 = 2² × 3, and 15 = 3 × 5. LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 180 minutes = 3 hours. Adding 3 hours to 10:00 AM gives 1:00 PM. Thus, they will toll together at 1:00 PM. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q115. What is the smallest perfect cube positive integer that is completely divisible by 6, 9, and 12?
(a) 108 (b) 144 (c) 216 (d) 324
Answer: (c) 216
Solution: Prime factorizations: 6 = 2 × 3, 9 = 3², and 12 = 2² × 3. LCM(6, 9, 12) = 2² × 3² = 36. For a perfect cube, every prime exponent must be a multiple of 3. Raising the exponents of 2 and 3 to 3 gives 2³ × 3³ = 8 × 27 = 216. Since 216 = 6³ is a perfect cube and divisible by 36, the smallest perfect cube is 216. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB Group D

Q116. The sum of two positive numbers is 120 and their HCF is 15. How many distinct pairs of such numbers exist?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (d) 2
Solution: Let the numbers be 15a and 15b with gcd(a, b) = 1 and a < b. Their sum is 15(a + b) = 120 ⇒ a + b = 8. Co-prime pairs (a, b) summing to 8 with a < b are (1, 7) and (3, 5). (Pairs (2, 6) and (4, 4) have gcd > 1). Thus, there are exactly 2 pairs. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: SSC CGL

Q117. What is the HCF of the decimal numbers 1.75, 7.5, and 3.25?
(a) 0.25 (b) 0.5 (c) 0.75 (d) 1.25
Answer: (a) 0.25
Solution: Writing as fractions with denominator 100: 175/100, 750/100, and 325/100. Finding HCF of numerators: 175 = 5² × 7, 750 = 2 × 3 × 5³, and 325 = 5² × 13. The common factor with lowest power is 5² = 25. Dividing by 100 gives 25 / 100 = 0.25. Therefore, the HCF is 0.25. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q118. The LCM of two numbers is 20 times their HCF. The sum of their LCM and HCF is 252. What is the product of the two numbers?
(a) 2400 (b) 2880 (c) 3120 (d) 3360
Answer: (b) 2880
Solution: Let HCF = h, then LCM = 20h. Given: h + 20h = 252 ⇒ 21h = 252 ⇒ h = 12. Therefore, LCM = 20 × 12 = 240. Using the product formula: Product of numbers = LCM × HCF = 240 × 12 = 2880. Thus, the product is 2880. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC MTS

Q119. What is the smallest positive integer which when divided by 12, 15, 20, and 27 leaves remainders of 10, 13, 18, and 25 respectively?
(a) 540 (b) 542 (c) 538 (d) 536
Answer: (c) 538
Solution: The difference between each divisor and its remainder is constant: 12 – 10 = 2, 15 – 13 = 2, 20 – 18 = 2, and 27 – 25 = 2. Prime factorizations: 12 = 2² × 3, 15 = 3 × 5, 20 = 2² × 5, and 27 = 3³. LCM(12, 15, 20, 27) = 2² × 3³ × 5 = 4 × 27 × 5 = 540. Subtracting the constant difference 2 gives: 540 – 2 = 538. Therefore, the required smallest number is 538. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q120. What is the HCF of the fractions 2/3, 8/9, and 64/81?
(a) 4/9 (b) 2/9 (c) 64/3 (d) 2/81
Answer: (d) 2/81
Solution: HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators divided by LCM of denominators. Numerators: 2, 8, 64 ⇒ HCF(2, 8, 64) = 2. Denominators: 3, 9, 81 ⇒ LCM(3, 9, 81) = 81. Therefore, the HCF is 2/81. Hence, the correct option is (d).

Q121. (Hard) What is the greatest positive integer that divides 445, 572, and 699 leaving remainders of 4, 5, and 6 respectively?
(a) 63 (b) 21 (c) 42 (d) 84
Answer: (a) 63
Solution: Subtracting the respective remainders: 445 – 4 = 441, 572 – 5 = 567, and 699 – 6 = 693. Factoring: 441 = 3² × 7², 567 = 3⁴ × 7, and 693 = 3² × 7 × 11. Common prime factors with lowest powers: 3² × 7¹ = 9 × 7 = 63. Thus, HCF(441, 567, 693) = 63. Therefore, the greatest number is 63. Hence, the correct option is (a).
Source: SSC CGL

Q122. (Hard) The HCF of two numbers is 23 and the other two factors of their LCM are 13 and 14. What is the larger of the two numbers?
(a) 299 (b) 322 (c) 276 (d) 345
Answer: (b) 322
Solution: When the HCF of two numbers is H and the remaining co-prime factors of their LCM are a and b, the two numbers are H × a and H × b. Here, H = 23, a = 13, and b = 14. The larger number is 23 × 14 = 322 (the smaller is 23 × 13 = 299). Hence, the larger number is 322. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q123. (Hard) A rectangular courtyard 3.78 m long and 5.25 m wide is to be paved exactly with identical square tiles of the largest possible size. What is the minimum number of tiles required?
(a) 300 (b) 600 (c) 450 (d) 750
Answer: (c) 450
Solution: Converting dimensions to centimeters: length = 378 cm, width = 525 cm. The side length of the largest square tile is HCF(378, 525). Factoring: 378 = 2 × 3³ × 7 and 525 = 3 × 5² × 7. Common factors with lowest powers are 3¹ × 7¹ = 21 cm. Number of tiles required = (378 × 525) / (21 × 21) = (378 / 21) × (525 / 21) = 18 × 25 = 450 tiles. Thus, the minimum number of tiles is 450. Hence, the correct option is (c).
Source: SSC MTS

Q124. (Hard) The LCM of 24, 36, and a third positive integer x is 144. What is the smallest possible value of x?
(a) 24 (b) 48 (c) 18 (d) 16
Answer: (d) 16
Solution: Factoring the numbers: 24 = 2³ × 3, 36 = 2² × 3², and LCM = 144 = 2⁴ × 3². In the prime factorization of the LCM, the factor 2 has exponent 4, whereas the highest exponent of 2 in 24 and 36 is only 3. Therefore, the factor 2⁴ must be contributed by x. To minimize x, set any other prime factors to their lowest non-negative powers, giving x = 2⁴ = 16. (Trap: Choosing 24 or 48 gives higher values; 16 is the minimal integer supplying 2⁴). Thus, the smallest value of x is 16. Hence, the correct option is (d).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q125. (Hard) What is the least positive multiple of 7 which leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6?
(a) 420 (b) 301 (c) 721 (d) 511
Answer: (b) 301
Solution: The number must be of the form LCM(2, 3, 4, 5, 6) × k + 1 = 60k + 1, and must also be completely divisible by 7. In modular arithmetic modulo 7: 60k + 1 ≡ 4k + 1 ≡ 0 (mod 7) ⇒ 4k ≡ –1 ≡ 6 (mod 7). Multiplying by 2: 8k ≡ k ≡ 12 ≡ 5 (mod 7). The smallest positive integer is k = 5. Substituting k = 5: 60(5) + 1 = 300 + 1 = 301. Check: 301 / 7 = 43 (divisible by 7) and 301 = 60 × 5 + 1. Hence, the least multiple of 7 is 301. Hence, the correct option is (b).
Source: RRB Group D
