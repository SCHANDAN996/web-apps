# Short Tricks & Mnemonics: Average (औसत)

### Box 1: SAD Panda (मूल औसत सूत्र)
The SAD Panda sits under a bamboo tree, repeating:
“Sum, Amount, Divide!”
• **S**um = Add all values together.
• **A**mount = Count how many items there are.
• **D**ivide = Divide Sum by Amount.
Panda cries if you forget to Divide at the end. To make him happy, always do the D step last.
Reverse magic: Multiply Average × Amount to get Sum — Panda hugs his bamboo.

---

### Box 2: Triangle of Power (तीन सुनहरे रूप)
Draw a triangle with S on top, A and N below:
      [ S ]
    [A] × [N]
• Cover S: S = A × N
• Cover A: A = S ÷ N
• Cover N: N = S ÷ A

---

### Box 3: NINO the Rhino (समावेश / Inclusion)
When a new number joins the herd:
$$\text{New Value} = (\text{New Avg} \times \text{New Count}) - (\text{Old Avg} \times \text{Old Count})$$
Remember: The new rhino pays for the entire herd's deficit!

---

### Box 4: WALT the Walrus (भारित औसत / Weighted Average)
WALT shouts: “Never average the averages directly unless group sizes are equal!”
$$A_w = \frac{w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2 + \dots}{w_1 + w_2 + \dots}$$
Always weight by group count.

---

### Box 5: Sew the Sum (त्रुटि सुधार / Error Correction)
When a wrong number was added:
$$\text{Correct Sum} = \text{Recorded Sum} - \text{Wrong Value} + \text{Correct Value}$$
Then divide by total count to get the corrected average. Fix the sum first!

---

### Box 6: First-Last Half (क्रमागत संख्याएँ / Consecutive AP)
For any consecutive numbers or arithmetic sequence:
$$\text{Average} = \frac{\text{First Term} + \text{Last Term}}{2}$$
Odd number of terms? The average is the exact middle term!

---

### Box 7: NOVA (एक नई प्रविष्टि का प्रभाव / Effect of One New Entry)
$$\text{New Average} = \text{Old Average} + \frac{\text{New Entry} - \text{Old Average}}{\text{New Total Count}}$$
A direct one-line shortcut when adding a single item.

---

### Box 8: Not Out? No Divide! (बल्लेबाज़ी औसत / Cricket Batting Average)
$$\text{Batting Average} = \frac{\text{Total Runs Scored}}{\text{Total Innings Dismissed (Out)}}$$
If a batsman is Not Out in an innings, his runs count in the numerator, but the denominator does NOT increase!

---

### Box 9: The Scale Trick (प्रतिस्थापन / Replacement)
When person A of weight W is replaced by person B and average increases by $\Delta$:
$$\text{Weight of B} = \text{Weight of A} + (N \times \Delta)$$
If average decreases, subtract $(N \times \Delta)$.

---

### Box 10: Ally the Gator (मिश्रण / Allegation Method)
When two groups with averages $A_1$ and $A_2$ combine to form average $A_m$:
$$\frac{N_1}{N_2} = \frac{A_2 - A_m}{A_m - A_1}$$
Use the criss-cross cross method to find counts instantly.

---

### Box 11: Hippo’s Harmonic (औसत चाल / Average Speed for Equal Distance)
When covering equal distances at speeds $x$ and $y$:
$$\text{Average Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x + y}$$
Do not take arithmetic mean $(x + y)/2$!

---

### Box 12: Copycat Chameleon (स्थिरांक संक्रियाएँ / Constant Operations)
If every observation in a dataset is:
• Increased by $k$ $\implies$ New average = $\text{Old} + k$
• Decreased by $k$ $\implies$ New average = $\text{Old} - k$
• Multiplied by $k$ $\implies$ New average = $\text{Old} \times k$
• Divided by $k$ $\implies$ New average = $\text{Old} \div k$
The average behaves exactly like a copycat!
