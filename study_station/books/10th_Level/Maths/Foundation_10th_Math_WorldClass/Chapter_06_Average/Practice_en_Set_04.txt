76. The average weight of a group of 20 boys was calculated to be 54 kg. Later it was found that one weight was recorded as 45 kg instead of the correct weight 55 kg. What is the correct average weight?
(a) 54.5 kg (b) 53.5 kg (c) 55.0 kg (d) 54.0 kg
Answer: (a) 54.5 kg
Solution: Difference in weight = 55 - 45 = +10 kg. Increase per boy = +10 ÷ 20 = +0.5 kg. Correct average = 54 + 0.5 = 54.5 kg.
Source: SSC CGL

77. A person travels three equal distances at speeds of 12 km/h, 15 km/h, and 20 km/h respectively. What is his average speed for the entire journey?
(a) 14 km/h (b) 15 km/h (c) 16 km/h (d) 15.67 km/h
Answer: (b) 15 km/h
Solution: Let each distance = LCM(12, 15, 20) = 60 km. Total distance = 3 × 60 = 180 km. Total time = (60/12) + (60/15) + (60/20) = 5 + 4 + 3 = 12 hours. Average speed = 180 ÷ 12 = 15 km/h.
Source: SSC CHSL

78. The average of 5 consecutive even numbers is 28. What is the average of the first and the last of these five numbers?
(a) 26 (b) 30 (c) 28 (d) 32
Answer: (c) 28
Solution: Trap alert: Do not waste time manually listing numbers! In any arithmetic progression, the average of the first and last terms is always identical to the overall average of the sequence. Therefore, Average = 28.
Source: RRB Group D

79. The average age of 36 students in a class is 14 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average age increases by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?
(a) 48 years (b) 49 years (c) 50 years (d) 51 years
Answer: (d) 51 years
Solution: Teacher's age = New Average + (Old Count × Increase) = 15 + (36 × 1) = 15 + 36 = 51 years. (Or (37 × 15) - (36 × 14) = 555 - 504 = 51 years).
Source: SSC MTS

80. The average of 5 numbers is 7. If three new numbers are added, the new average becomes 8.5. What is the average of the three new numbers?
(a) 11 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 13
Answer: (a) 11
Solution: Total sum of 5 numbers = 5 × 7 = 35. Total sum of 8 numbers = 8 × 8.5 = 68. Sum of three new numbers = 68 - 35 = 33. Average of new numbers = 33 ÷ 3 = 11.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

81. In a factory, the average salary of all 60 employees is ₹29,000. If the average salary of 20 technicians is ₹35,000, what is the average salary of the remaining 40 non-technical workers?
(a) ₹25,000 (b) ₹26,000 (c) ₹27,000 (d) ₹28,000
Answer: (b) ₹26,000
Solution: Total salary of all 60 employees = 60 × 29000 = ₹1,740,000. Total salary of 20 technicians = 20 × 35000 = ₹700,000. Total salary of 40 workers = 1,740,000 - 700,000 = ₹1,040,000. Average salary = 1,040,000 ÷ 40 = ₹26,000.
Source: SSC CGL

82. The average score of a batsman after 25 innings was 56 runs. In his 26th innings, he was dismissed for only 4 runs. What is his new batting average?
(a) 52 (b) 53 (c) 54 (d) 55
Answer: (c) 54
Solution: Total runs after 25 innings = 25 × 56 = 1400. Total runs after 26 innings = 1400 + 4 = 1404. New average = 1404 ÷ 26 = 54 runs.
Source: SSC CHSL

83. The average of four numbers A, B, C, and D is 30. If A = 24, B = 28, and C = 32, what is the value of D?
(a) 32 (b) 34 (c) 35 (d) 36
Answer: (d) 36
Solution: Total sum of four numbers = 4 × 30 = 120. Sum of A, B, C = 24 + 28 + 32 = 84. D = 120 - 84 = 36.
Source: RRB NTPC

84. The average of six numbers is x. If each of the first three numbers is increased by 4, and each of the remaining three numbers is decreased by 2, what is the new average?
(a) x + 1 (b) x + 2 (c) x - 1 (d) x
Answer: (a) x + 1
Solution: Net change in total sum = (3 × 4) + [3 × (-2)] = 12 - 6 = +6. Change in average = +6 ÷ 6 = +1. New average = x + 1.
Source: SSC MTS

85. The average age of a committee of 8 members is 40 years. A member aged 55 years resigns and is replaced by a new member aged 39 years. What is the new average age of the committee?
(a) 39 years (b) 38 years (c) 37 years (d) 38.5 years
Answer: (b) 38 years
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 39 years simply assumes the average drops by only 1 year! Difference in sum = 39 - 55 = -16 years. Change in average = -16 ÷ 8 = -2 years. New average = 40 - 2 = 38 years.
Source: SSC GD

86. If the average of a, b, c is 11, the average of c, d, e is 17, and the average of all five numbers a, b, c, d, e is 13, what is the value of c?
(a) 17 (b) 18 (c) 19 (d) 21
Answer: (c) 19
Solution: a + b + c = 3 × 11 = 33. c + d + e = 3 × 17 = 51. a + b + c + d + e = 5 × 13 = 65. Now, (a + b + c) + (c + d + e) = (a + b + c + d + e) + c => 33 + 51 = 65 + c => 84 = 65 + c => c = 84 - 65 = 19.
Source: SSC CGL

87. The average weight of 6 men is 68 kg. When a new man weighing 82 kg joins the group, what is the new average weight of the 7 men?
(a) 68.5 kg (b) 69 kg (c) 69.5 kg (d) 70 kg
Answer: (d) 70 kg
Solution: Total weight of 6 men = 6 × 68 = 408 kg. With the new man, total weight = 408 + 82 = 490 kg. New average weight = 490 ÷ 7 = 70 kg.
Source: SSC CHSL

88. A student scored an average of 80 marks across 5 subjects. In two additional subjects, he scored 85 and 75 marks. What is his overall average marks across all 7 subjects?
(a) 80 (b) 78 (c) 82 (d) 84
Answer: (a) 80
Solution: Total marks in first 5 subjects = 5 × 80 = 400. Sum of 2 additional subjects = 85 + 75 = 160. Total marks in 7 subjects = 400 + 160 = 560. Overall average = 560 ÷ 7 = 80 marks.
Source: RRB Group D

89. The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 27. If the next 2 odd numbers are also included, what will be the average of all 7 odd numbers?
(a) 28 (b) 29 (c) 30 (d) 31
Answer: (b) 29
Solution: For consecutive odd numbers (difference d = 2), adding the next k terms increases the average by (k × d) ÷ 2 = (2 × 2) ÷ 2 = 2. New average = 27 + 2 = 29.
Source: SSC MTS

90. The average monthly salary of 12 employees is ₹18,000. If two employees earning ₹15,000 and ₹21,000 leave the organization, what is the average monthly salary of the remaining 10 employees?
(a) ₹17,500 (b) ₹18,500 (c) ₹18,000 (d) ₹19,000
Answer: (c) ₹18,000
Solution: Sum of salaries of the 2 leaving employees = 15000 + 21000 = ₹36,000. Their combined average = 36000 ÷ 2 = ₹18,000. Since their average equals the overall average, the average of the remaining 10 employees remains exactly ₹18,000.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

91. The average of 10 observations is 48. Later, it was discovered that one observation 66 was mistakenly misread as 36. What is the corrected average?
(a) 49 (b) 50 (c) 52 (d) 51
Answer: (d) 51
Solution: Correction = Correct value - Misread value = 66 - 36 = +30. Increase in average = +30 ÷ 10 = +3. Corrected average = 48 + 3 = 51.
Source: SSC CGL

92. The average marks of Section A (30 students) is 60, and that of Section B (20 students) is 70. What is the combined average marks of both sections?
(a) 64 (b) 65 (c) 66 (d) 63
Answer: (a) 64
Solution: Combined Average = (30 × 60 + 20 × 70) ÷ (30 + 20) = (1800 + 1400) ÷ 50 = 3200 ÷ 50 = 64.
Source: SSC CHSL

93. The average age of 4 brothers is 15 years. If the age of their father is added, the average age increases by 5 years. What is the age of the father?
(a) 38 years (b) 40 years (c) 42 years (d) 45 years
Answer: (b) 40 years
Solution: Total age of 4 brothers = 4 × 15 = 60 years. New average for 5 persons = 15 + 5 = 20 years. Total age of 5 persons = 5 × 20 = 100 years. Father's age = 100 - 60 = 40 years.
Source: RRB NTPC

94. An airplane flies along the four sides of a square territory at speeds of 100 km/h, 200 km/h, 300 km/h, and 600 km/h respectively. What is the average speed of the plane around the entire perimeter?
(a) 150 km/h (b) 180 km/h (c) 200 km/h (d) 240 km/h
Answer: (c) 200 km/h
Solution: Let the length of each side = LCM(100, 200, 300, 600) = 600 km. Total distance = 4 × 600 = 2400 km. Total time = (600/100) + (600/200) + (600/300) + (600/600) = 6 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 12 hours. Average speed = 2400 ÷ 12 = 200 km/h.
Source: SSC GD

95. The average of 9 numbers is 32. If the average of the first 4 numbers is 25 and that of the last 4 numbers is 35, what is the fifth number?
(a) 42 (b) 44 (c) 46 (d) 48
Answer: (d) 48
Solution: Total sum of 9 numbers = 9 × 32 = 288. Sum of first 4 = 4 × 25 = 100. Sum of last 4 = 4 × 35 = 140. Fifth number = 288 - (100 + 140) = 288 - 240 = 48.
Source: SSC MTS

96. The average age of a husband, wife, and their son was 42 years. The son got married, and a child was born exactly 1 year after the marriage. When the child became 5 years old, the average age of the family (5 members) became 36 years. What was the age of the bride at the time of marriage?
(a) 25 years (b) 24 years (c) 26 years (d) 27 years
Answer: (a) 25 years
Solution: Child is 5 years old and born 1 year after marriage => Marriage took place 6 years ago. Sum of husband, wife, son 6 years ago = 3 × 42 = 126 years. Their present sum = 126 + (3 × 6) = 144 years. Present total of all 5 members = 5 × 36 = 180 years. Present age of bride = 180 - (144 + 5) = 31 years. Bride's age at marriage (6 years ago) = 31 - 6 = 25 years.
Source: SSC CGL

97. The average score of 50 candidates was calculated as 40. Later, it was discovered that two scores 38 and 42 were mistakenly misread as 83 and 57 respectively. Also, the actual number of candidates was found to be 40, not 50! What is the correct average score?
(a) 50.0 (b) 51.5 (c) 52.0 (d) 53.5
Answer: (b) 51.5
Solution: Initial recorded sum = 50 × 40 = 2000. Correct total sum = 2000 - (38 + 42) + (83 + 57) = 2000 - 80 + 140 = 2060. Since the actual count of candidates was 40, the correct average score is 2060 ÷ 40 = 51.5.
Source: SSC CGL

98. In an examination, the average marks obtained by students who passed was 39, and that by those who failed was 15. If the average marks of all students who appeared in the examination was 35, what percentage of the students passed?
(a) 80% (b) 82.5% (c) 83 1/3% (d) 85%
Answer: (c) 83 1/3%
Solution: By allegation: Passed (39) and Failed (15), Mean (35). Ratio Passed : Failed = (35 - 15) : (39 - 35) = 20 : 4 = 5 : 1. Percentage of students passed = [5 ÷ (5 + 1)] × 100 = (5/6) × 100 = 83 1/3%.
Source: SSC CHSL

99. A batsman in his 18th innings scores 98 runs and thereby increases his batting average by 2.5 runs. What is his batting average after the 18th innings?
(a) 53.0 (b) 54.5 (c) 56.0 (d) 55.5
Answer: (d) 55.5
Solution: Let old average = x. 17x + 98 = 18(x + 2.5) => 17x + 98 = 18x + 45 => x = 98 - 45 = 53. New average after 18th innings = 53 + 2.5 = 55.5 runs.
Source: SSC CGL

100. The average of 8 numbers is 20. The average of the first two numbers is 15.5, and that of the next three is 21 1/3. If the sixth number is 4 less than the seventh and 7 less than the eighth number, what is the eighth number?
(a) 22 (b) 25 (c) 27 (d) 28
Answer: (b) 25
Solution: Total sum of 8 numbers = 8 × 20 = 160. Sum of first two = 2 × 15.5 = 31. Sum of next three = 3 × (64/3) = 64. Sum of first five = 31 + 64 = 95. Sum of remaining three (6th, 7th, 8th) = 160 - 95 = 65. Let 6th = x, 7th = x + 4, 8th = x + 7. Equation: x + (x + 4) + (x + 7) = 3x + 11 = 65 => 3x = 54 => x = 18. Eighth number = 18 + 7 = 25.
Source: SSC CGL
