Find the number of girls.(a) 20(b) 25(c) 30(d) 40Answer: (a) 20Solution: Let boys = b, girls = 60 – b. Total weight = 60 × 42 = 2520. Also, 45b + 36(60 – b) = 2520 ⇒ 45b + 2160 – 36b = 2520 ⇒ 9b = 360 ⇒ b = 40. Girls = 60 – 40 = 20.Source: SSC CHSL 201877.
