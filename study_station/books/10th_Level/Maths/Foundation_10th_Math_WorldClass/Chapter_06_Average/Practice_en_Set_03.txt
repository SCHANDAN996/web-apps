51. The average marks of 60 students in a class is 62. The average marks of the boys is 60 and that of the girls is 65. What is the number of boys in the class?
(a) 36 (b) 24 (c) 30 (d) 40
Answer: (a) 36
Solution: By allegation: Ratio Boys : Girls = (65 - 62) : (62 - 60) = 3 : 2. Total parts = 3 + 2 = 5 parts = 60 => 1 part = 12. Number of boys = 3 × 12 = 36.
Source: SSC CGL

52. A car travels 150 km at 75 km/h, the next 120 km at 60 km/h, and the final 60 km at 30 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey?
(a) 50 km/h (b) 55 km/h (c) 60 km/h (d) 52 km/h
Answer: (b) 55 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 150 + 120 + 60 = 330 km. Total time = (150/75) + (120/60) + (60/30) = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 hours. Average speed = 330 ÷ 6 = 55 km/h.
Source: SSC CHSL

53. The average of 10 consecutive odd integers is 36. What is the sum of the smallest and the largest of these integers?
(a) 70 (b) 74 (c) 72 (d) 68
Answer: (c) 72
Solution: For an arithmetic progression, Average = (First + Last) ÷ 2. Therefore, First + Last = 2 × Average = 2 × 36 = 72.
Source: RRB Group D

54. A batsman scored 92 runs in his 15th innings and his average increased by 4 runs. What was his batting average before the 15th innings?
(a) 36 (b) 30 (c) 28 (d) 32
Answer: (d) 32
Solution: Let old average = x. 14x + 92 = 15(x + 4) => 14x + 92 = 15x + 60 => x = 92 - 60 = 32 runs.
Source: SSC MTS

55. The average age of 12 players in a cricket team is 25 years. If the captain's age is excluded, the average age of the remaining players decreases by 1 year. What is the captain's age?
(a) 36 years (b) 34 years (c) 38 years (d) 40 years
Answer: (a) 36 years
Solution: Total age of 12 players = 12 × 25 = 300 years. Total age of remaining 11 players = 11 × 24 = 264 years. Captain's age = 300 - 264 = 36 years.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

56. The average of 20 observations was calculated as 65. Later, it was discovered that two observations 85 and 45 were mistakenly copied as 58 and 54 respectively. What is the correct average?
(a) 64.1 (b) 65.9 (c) 66.8 (d) 65.0
Answer: (b) 65.9
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 64.1 mistakenly subtracts the correction! Sum of correct values = 85 + 45 = 130. Sum of wrong values = 58 + 54 = 112. Net change in sum = +18. Change in average = +18 ÷ 20 = +0.9. Correct average = 65 + 0.9 = 65.9.
Source: SSC CGL

57. The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when one person weighing 65 kg is replaced by a new person. What is the weight of the new person?
(a) 75 kg (b) 80 kg (c) 85 kg (d) 90 kg
Answer: (c) 85 kg
Solution: Total increase in weight = 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg. Weight of new person = Replaced weight + Total increase = 65 + 20 = 85 kg.
Source: IBPS Clerk

58. The average of 5 numbers is 18. If one number is removed, the average becomes 16. If instead a different number is added to the original 5 numbers, the average becomes 20. What is the sum of the removed number and the added number?
(a) 50 (b) 52 (c) 54 (d) 56
Answer: (d) 56
Solution: Original sum = 5 × 18 = 90. Removed number = 90 - (4 × 16) = 90 - 64 = 26. Added number = (6 × 20) - 90 = 120 - 90 = 30. Sum of removed and added numbers = 26 + 30 = 56.
Source: SSC CHSL

59. The average monthly salary of all staff in a company is ₹15,000. The average salary of 10 officers is ₹25,000, and that of the remaining non-officers is ₹13,000. How many non-officers are there in the company?
(a) 50 (b) 40 (c) 60 (d) 45
Answer: (a) 50
Solution: By allegation: Officers : Non-officers = (15000 - 13000) : (25000 - 15000) = 2000 : 10000 = 1 : 5. Since officers = 10, non-officers = 5 × 10 = 50.
Source: RRB NTPC

60. In an examination, a student's average score was 63 marks per paper. If he had obtained 20 more marks in Geography and 2 more marks in History, his average would have been 65 marks per paper. How many papers were there in the examination?
(a) 10 (b) 11 (c) 12 (d) 13
Answer: (b) 11
Solution: Total increase in marks = 20 + 2 = 22 marks. Increase in average = 65 - 63 = 2 marks. Number of papers = Total increase in marks ÷ Increase in average = 22 ÷ 2 = 11 papers.
Source: SSC CGL

61. A cricket batsman played 10 innings, scored a total of 400 runs, and was 'not out' in 2 of those innings. What is his official batting average?
(a) 40 (b) 45 (c) 50 (d) 55
Answer: (c) 50
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 40 divides by total innings (10), which is incorrect! Cricket batting average is defined as Total Runs ÷ Number of Completed (Out) Innings. Completed innings = 10 - 2 = 8. Batting average = 400 ÷ 8 = 50 runs.
Source: SSC CHSL

62. The average age of a family of 6 members is 22 years. If the age of the youngest member is 7 years, what was the average age of the family members at the time of the birth of the youngest member?
(a) 15 years (b) 16 years (c) 17 years (d) 18 years
Answer: (d) 18 years
Solution: Current total age of 6 members = 6 × 22 = 132 years. 7 years ago, total age of the other 5 members = 132 - (6 × 7) = 132 - 42 = 90 years. Average age of the 5 members at that time = 90 ÷ 5 = 18 years.
Source: SSC MTS

63. The average of 7 consecutive integers is 20. What is the largest of these integers?
(a) 23 (b) 22 (c) 24 (d) 25
Answer: (a) 23
Solution: For an odd number of consecutive integers, the average is the middle (4th) number. The numbers are 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23. The largest integer is 23.
Source: RRB Group D

64. Of four numbers, the average of the first three numbers is twice the fourth number. If the average of all four numbers is 14, what is the fourth number?
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 10
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: Let fourth number = x. Sum of first 3 numbers = 3 × 2x = 6x. Total sum of all 4 numbers = 6x + x = 7x. Given total = 4 × 14 = 56 => 7x = 56 => x = 8.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

65. The average of m numbers is n², and the average of n numbers is m². What is the average of all (m + n) numbers combined?
(a) m + n (b) m - n (c) mn (d) m / n
Answer: (c) mn
Solution: Total sum = (m × n²) + (n × m²) = mn(n + m) = mn(m + n). Combined average = mn(m + n) ÷ (m + n) = mn.
Source: SSC CGL

66. Three numbers are such that each is twice the previous number. If their average is 28, what is the smallest number?
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 14 (d) 12
Answer: (d) 12
Solution: Let the numbers be x, 2x, 4x. Average = (x + 2x + 4x) ÷ 3 = 7x ÷ 3 = 28 => x = (28 × 3) ÷ 7 = 12. The smallest number is 12.
Source: SSC GD

67. The average of 15 numbers is 40. If each of the 15 numbers is divided by 5, what will be the new average?
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 7 (d) 6
Answer: (a) 8
Solution: If each observation is divided by a constant k, the new average is also divided by k: New Average = 40 ÷ 5 = 8.
Source: SSC MTS

68. The average score of 40 students is 52. If the highest and lowest scores (which differ by 60 marks) are excluded, the average of the remaining 38 students becomes 51. What is the highest score?
(a) 98 (b) 101 (c) 104 (d) 108
Answer: (b) 101
Solution: Total sum of 40 students = 40 × 52 = 2080. Total sum of 38 students = 38 × 51 = 1938. Highest + Lowest = 2080 - 1938 = 142. Given H - L = 60 => 2H = 142 + 60 = 202 => H = 101 marks.
Source: SSC CGL

69. The average temperature from Monday to Wednesday was 37°C, and from Tuesday to Thursday was 34°C. If the temperature on Thursday was 4/5 of that on Monday, what was the temperature on Thursday?
(a) 34°C (b) 35°C (c) 36°C (d) 38°C
Answer: (c) 36°C
Solution: M + T + W = 3 × 37 = 111. T + W + Th = 3 × 34 = 102. Subtracting: M - Th = 111 - 102 = 9. Since Th = (4/5)M => M - (4/5)M = (1/5)M = 9 => M = 45°C. Temperature on Thursday = 45 - 9 = 36°C.
Source: SSC CHSL

70. A customer bought 5 shirts at ₹450 each, 4 pairs of trousers at ₹750 each, and 11 pairs of socks at ₹150 each. What was the average price per item purchased?
(a) ₹325 (b) ₹335 (c) ₹340 (d) ₹345
Answer: (d) ₹345
Solution: Total expenditure = (5 × 450) + (4 × 750) + (11 × 150) = 2250 + 3000 + 1650 = ₹6,900. Total items = 5 + 4 + 11 = 20 items. Average price = 6900 ÷ 20 = ₹345.
Source: RRB NTPC

71. The average of 11 numbers is 10.9. If the average of the first six numbers is 10.5 and that of the last six numbers is 11.4, what is the middle (sixth) number?
(a) 11.5 (b) 11.2 (c) 11.8 (d) 12.0
Answer: (a) 11.5
Solution: Total sum of 11 numbers = 11 × 10.9 = 119.9. Sum of first 6 = 6 × 10.5 = 63.0. Sum of last 6 = 6 × 11.4 = 68.4. Sum of first and last 6 = 63.0 + 68.4 = 131.4. Middle number = 131.4 - 119.9 = 11.5.
Source: SSC CGL

72. In a class, the average weight of boys is 60 kg and that of girls is 45 kg. If the average weight of the entire class is 54 kg and there are 18 girls, how many boys are there in the class?
(a) 24 (b) 27 (c) 30 (d) 36
Answer: (b) 27
Solution: Ratio Boys : Girls = (54 - 45) : (60 - 54) = 9 : 6 = 3 : 2. Since 2 units = 18 girls => 1 unit = 9. Number of boys = 3 × 9 = 27 boys.
Source: SSC CHSL

73. A cricket bowler whose bowling average is 24.85 runs per wicket takes 5 wickets for 52 runs in his final match, thereby decreasing (improving) his average by 0.85 runs per wicket. How many wickets had he taken before this match?
(a) 72 (b) 76 (c) 80 (d) 84
Answer: (c) 80
Solution: Let initial wickets = w. New average = 24.85 - 0.85 = 24.0. Equation: (24.85w + 52) ÷ (w + 5) = 24.0 => 24.85w + 52 = 24w + 120 => 0.85w = 68 => w = 68 ÷ 0.85 = 80 wickets.
Source: SSC CGL

74. The average age of 8 men increases by 2 years when two of them aged 21 years and 23 years are replaced by two new women. What is the average age of the two new women?
(a) 26 years (b) 28 years (c) 32 years (d) 30 years
Answer: (d) 30 years
Solution: Total increase in age = 8 × 2 = 16 years. Sum of ages of replaced men = 21 + 23 = 44 years. Sum of ages of two women = 44 + 16 = 60 years. Average age of women = 60 ÷ 2 = 30 years.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

75. A cricketer has a batting average of 40 runs across 50 innings. His highest score exceeds his lowest score by 172 runs. If these two innings are excluded, the average of the remaining 48 innings is 38 runs. What was his highest score?
(a) 170 (b) 174 (c) 176 (d) 172
Answer: (b) 174
Solution: Total runs in 50 innings = 50 × 40 = 2000. Total runs in 48 innings = 48 × 38 = 1824. Highest + Lowest = 2000 - 1824 = 176. Given H - L = 172 => 2H = 176 + 172 = 348 => H = 174 runs.
Source: SSC CGL
