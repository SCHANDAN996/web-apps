### Top 10 High-Yield PYQs with Detailed Solutions & Trap Analysis

#### Q1. [SBI PO 2020] Inclusion — "Just Add the Difference" Fallacy
A cricketer has a batting average of 42 runs in 13 matches. If his average in the first 5 matches is 54 runs, what is his average score in the last 8 matches?
*   (a) 37
*   (b) 39
*   (c) 34.5
*   (d) 33.5

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. Total runs in 13 matches: 13 × 42 = 546 runs.
2. Total runs in first 5 matches: 5 × 54 = 270 runs.
3. Total runs in last 8 matches: 546 - 270 = 276 runs.
4. Average of last 8 matches: 276 ÷ 8 = 34.5 runs.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (c) 34.5**

**Examiner Trap:**
Students often compute 42 - (54 - 42) = 30 and guess nearby options, ignoring the unequal weighting of 5 matches vs 8 matches.

#### Q2. [SSC CGL 2022] Weighted Average — The "Equal Group" Trap
In an examination, the average marks of 40 students in Class A is 75, and the average marks of 60 students in Class B is 85. What is the combined average of both classes?
*   (a) 80
*   (b) 81
*   (c) 79
*   (d) 82

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. Class sizes ratio A : B = 40 : 60 = 2 : 3.
2. Weighted average = (2 × 75 + 3 × 85) / (2 + 3) = (150 + 255) / 5 = 405 / 5 = 81.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (b) 81**

**Examiner Trap:**
Option (a) 80 is the simple unweighted average (75 + 85) / 2 = 80. Examiners place 80 at option (a) to catch hasty test-takers.

#### Q3. [RRB Group D 2018] Allegation / Mixture — Overcomplication Trap
The average salary of all 120 employees in an office is ₹12,000. If the average salary of officers is ₹20,000 and that of non-officers is ₹10,000, find the number of officers.
*   (a) 20
*   (b) 24
*   (c) 30
*   (d) 36

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. Allegation method:
   Officers (20,000)        Non-Officers (10,000)
                    12,000
   (12,000 - 10,000) = 2,000 : (20,000 - 12,000) = 8,000
   Ratio = 2000 : 8000 = 1 : 4.
2. Total parts = 1 + 4 = 5 parts = 120 employees => 1 part = 24.
3. Number of officers = 1 × 24 = 24.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (b) 24**

**Examiner Trap:**
Setting up long linear equations often leads to arithmetic slips under exam stress. Allegation yields the answer in 15 seconds.

#### Q4. [SSC CHSL 2020] Error Correction — "Fix the Sum First" Rule
The average of 50 numbers was calculated as 36. Later it was found that a number 48 was mistakenly entered as 23. What is the correct average?
*   (a) 36.5
*   (b) 36.2
*   (c) 35.5
*   (d) 37.0

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. Net error in sum = Correct value - Mistaken value = 48 - 23 = +25.
2. Net change in average = +25 / 50 = +0.5.
3. Correct average = 36 + 0.5 = 36.5.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (a) 36.5**

**Examiner Trap:**
Subtracting 25 from the sum instead of adding gives option (c) 35.5. Always check whether the correct number is larger or smaller.

#### Q5. [IBPS PO 2019] Group Average — Sub-Group Decomposition
The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one of them weighing 65 kg. What is the weight of the new person?
*   (a) 75 kg
*   (b) 80 kg
*   (c) 85 kg
*   (d) 90 kg

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. Total increase in weight = 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg.
2. Weight of new person = Replaced weight + Total increase = 65 + 20 = 85 kg.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (c) 85 kg**

**Examiner Trap:**
Multiplying 2.5 by 7 (excluding replaced person) gives 17.5 + 65 = 82.5 kg. Remember the replacement keeps the total count at 8!

#### Q6. [SSC CPO 2019] Algebraic Average Systems
The average age of A and B is 20 years. If A is replaced by C, the average age becomes 19 years. The average age of C and B is 19 years, and that of A and C is 21 years. Find the age of A.
*   (a) 22 years
*   (b) 20 years
*   (c) 24 years
*   (d) 18 years

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. A + B = 40
2. B + C = 38
3. A + C = 42
4. Adding all: 2(A + B + C) = 120 => A + B + C = 60.
5. Age of A = (A + B + C) - (B + C) = 60 - 38 = 22 years.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (a) 22 years**

#### Q7. [RRB NTPC 2019] Effect of Replacement
A library has an average of 510 visitors on Sundays and 240 on other days. What is the average number of visitors per day in a month of 30 days beginning with a Sunday?
*   (a) 275
*   (b) 280
*   (c) 285
*   (d) 290

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. Month begins with a Sunday: Sundays fall on 1st, 8th, 15th, 22nd, 29th (total 5 Sundays).
2. Other days = 30 - 5 = 25 days.
3. Total visitors = (5 × 510) + (25 × 240) = 2550 + 6000 = 8550.
4. Average per day = 8550 / 30 = 285.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (c) 285**

**Examiner Trap:**
Assuming 4 Sundays in a month gives (4 × 510 + 26 × 240) / 30 = 276. Always count exact Sundays in a 30-day month starting with Sunday.

#### Q8. [IBPS Clerk 2020] Ratio from Combined Average
In an examination, the average score of girls is 73 and that of boys is 71. The average score of all students is 71.8. What is the ratio of number of girls to boys?
*   (a) 2 : 3
*   (b) 3 : 2
*   (c) 4 : 5
*   (d) 3 : 4

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. By allegation:
   Girls (73)         Boys (71)
             71.8
   (71.8 - 71) = 0.8 : (73 - 71.8) = 1.2
   Ratio Girls : Boys = 0.8 : 1.2 = 2 : 3.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (a) 2 : 3**

#### Q9. [SSC CGL 2023] Consecutive Numbers
The average of 7 consecutive numbers is 20. The largest of these numbers is:
*   (a) 22
*   (b) 23
*   (c) 24
*   (d) 25

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. For an odd count of consecutive integers, the average is the central number (4th number).
2. Numbers: 17, 18, 19, [20], 21, 22, 23.
3. Largest number = 20 + 3 = 23.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (b) 23**

#### Q10. [SBI Clerk 2021] Batting Average & "Not Out" Nuance
A batsman in his 17th innings makes a score of 85 runs and thereby increases his average by 3. What is his average after the 17th innings?
*   (a) 34
*   (b) 37
*   (c) 39
*   (d) 40

**Step-by-Step Solution:**
1. Let average after 16 innings = x.
2. Total runs in 16 innings = 16x.
3. Total runs in 17 innings = 16x + 85.
4. New average = x + 3 => (16x + 85) / 17 = x + 3.
5. 16x + 85 = 17x + 51 => x = 34.
6. New average after 17th innings = 34 + 3 = 37.
*   **✅ Correct Answer: (b) 37**

**Examiner Trap:**
Students frequently solve for x and choose option (a) 34, which is the OLD average before the 17th innings. The question explicitly asks for the average AFTER the 17th innings!
