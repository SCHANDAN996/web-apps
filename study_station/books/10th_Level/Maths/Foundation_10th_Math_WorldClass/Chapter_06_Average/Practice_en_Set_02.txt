A man travels for 3 hours at 60 km/h and for the next 2 hours at 80 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?(a) 68 km/h(b) 70 km/h(c) 72 km/h(d) 66.67 km/hAnswer: (a) 68 km/hSolution: Distance covered in 3 h = 3 × 60 = 180 km. In 2 h = 2 × 80 = 160 km. Total distance = 340 km, total time = 5 h. Average speed = 340 / 5 = 68 km/h.Source: NCERT Class 7
