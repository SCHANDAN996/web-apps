26. The average age of 24 students and their class teacher is 15 years. If the teacher's age is excluded, the average age of the students decreases by 1 year. What is the age of the teacher?
(a) 39 years (b) 38 years (c) 40 years (d) 42 years
Answer: (a) 39 years
Solution: Total age of 25 persons = 25 × 15 = 375 years. Age of 24 students = 24 × (15 - 1) = 24 × 14 = 336 years. Teacher's age = 375 - 336 = 39 years.
Source: SSC CGL

27. The average of 8 consecutive integers is 10.5. What is the largest of these integers?
(a) 13 (b) 14 (c) 15 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 14
Solution: The 8 consecutive integers centered around 10.5 are 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14. The largest integer is 14.
Source: SSC CHSL

28. A batsman has a certain batting average in 16 innings. In his 17th innings, he scores 87 runs, thereby increasing his average by 3 runs. What is his new average?
(a) 36 (b) 38 (c) 39 (d) 42
Answer: (c) 39
Solution: Let old average = x. 16x + 87 = 17(x + 3) => 16x + 87 = 17x + 51 => x = 87 - 51 = 36. New average = 36 + 3 = 39 runs.
Source: SSC MTS

29. The average score of 50 students in a test is 38. If two students who scored 45 and 55 marks are excluded, what is the average score of the remaining 48 students?
(a) 36.5 (b) 37.0 (c) 38.0 (d) 37.5
Answer: (d) 37.5
Solution: Initial total sum = 50 × 38 = 1900. Sum of excluded students = 45 + 55 = 100. Sum of remaining 48 students = 1900 - 100 = 1800. New average = 1800 ÷ 48 = 37.5.
Source: RRB Group D

30. The average of 5 numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 25. What was the excluded number?
(a) 35 (b) 30 (c) 32 (d) 38
Answer: (a) 35
Solution: Total sum of 5 numbers = 5 × 27 = 135. Total sum of remaining 4 numbers = 4 × 25 = 100. Excluded number = 135 - 100 = 35.
Source: SSC GD

31. The average of 25 numbers is 40. Later, it was discovered that a number 64 was mistakenly misread as 46 during the calculation. What is the correct average?
(a) 39.28 (b) 40.72 (c) 41.44 (d) 40.18
Answer: (b) 40.72
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 39.28 subtracts the error instead of adding it! Difference = Correct value - Misread value = 64 - 46 = +18. Correction per number = +18 ÷ 25 = +0.72. Correct average = 40 + 0.72 = 40.72.
Source: SSC CGL

32. The average of three numbers A, B, and C is 20. If A + B = 35 and B + C = 45, what is the value of B?
(a) 15 (b) 18 (c) 20 (d) 25
Answer: (c) 20
Solution: A + B + C = 3 × 20 = 60. Now (A + B) + (B + C) = 35 + 45 = 80 => (A + B + C) + B = 80 => 60 + B = 80 => B = 20.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

33. The average weight of 15 students increases by 1.2 kg when a student weighing 40 kg is replaced by a new student. What is the weight of the new student?
(a) 52 kg (b) 54 kg (c) 56 kg (d) 58 kg
Answer: (d) 58 kg
Solution: Total increase in weight = 15 × 1.2 = 18 kg. Weight of new student = Weight of replaced student + Total increase = 40 + 18 = 58 kg.
Source: SSC CHSL

34. A library has an average of 510 visitors on Sundays and 240 visitors on other weekdays. What is the average number of visitors per day in a 30-day month beginning with a Sunday?
(a) 285 (b) 275 (c) 290 (d) 300
Answer: (a) 285
Solution: Sundays in the month: 1st, 8th, 15th, 22nd, 29th (5 Sundays). Other days = 30 - 5 = 25 days. Total visitors = (5 × 510) + (25 × 240) = 2550 + 6000 = 8550. Average per day = 8550 ÷ 30 = 285.
Source: RRB NTPC

35. A person drives from Town A to Town B at a speed of 60 km/h and returns from Town B to Town A at a speed of 40 km/h. What is his average speed for the round trip?
(a) 50 km/h (b) 48 km/h (c) 45 km/h (d) 52 km/h
Answer: (b) 48 km/h
Solution: For equal distances, Average Speed = (2 × u × v) ÷ (u + v) = (2 × 60 × 40) ÷ (60 + 40) = 4800 ÷ 100 = 48 km/h.
Source: SSC MTS

36. The average age of a husband and wife at the time of their marriage 5 years ago was 25 years. Today, with a child born in the interval, the average age of the family is 21 years. How old is the child?
(a) 1 year (b) 2 years (c) 3 years (d) 4 years
Answer: (c) 3 years
Solution: Combined age of husband and wife 5 years ago = 2 × 25 = 50 years. Present age of husband and wife = 50 + (2 × 5) = 60 years. Present total age of family (3 members) = 3 × 21 = 63 years. Age of child = 63 - 60 = 3 years.
Source: SSC CGL

37. The average monthly salary of 20 workers in an office is ₹8,000. If the manager's salary is included, the average increases by ₹500. What is the manager's monthly salary?
(a) ₹16,500 (b) ₹17,000 (c) ₹18,000 (d) ₹18,500
Answer: (d) ₹18,500
Solution: Total salary of 20 workers = 20 × 8000 = ₹160,000. New average for 21 persons = ₹8,500. Total salary of 21 persons = 21 × 8500 = ₹178,500. Manager's salary = 178,500 - 160,000 = ₹18,500.
Source: SSC CHSL

38. The average of 9 numbers is 50. The average of the first 5 numbers is 54 and that of the last 5 numbers is 52. What is the fifth number?
(a) 80 (b) 75 (c) 85 (d) 90
Answer: (a) 80
Solution: Total of 9 numbers = 9 × 50 = 450. Sum of first 5 = 5 × 54 = 270. Sum of last 5 = 5 × 52 = 260. Sum of both sets = 270 + 260 = 530. Fifth number = 530 - 450 = 80.
Source: RRB Group D

39. A train covers the first 60 km at a speed of 30 km/h and the next 60 km at a speed of 60 km/h. What is the average speed of the train for the total 120 km journey?
(a) 45 km/h (b) 40 km/h (c) 42 km/h (d) 38 km/h
Answer: (b) 40 km/h
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 45 km/h is the simple arithmetic mean (30 + 60)/2, which is mathematically invalid for speeds over equal distances! Total distance = 120 km. Total time = (60/30) + (60/60) = 2 + 1 = 3 hours. Average speed = 120 ÷ 3 = 40 km/h.
Source: SSC MTS

40. The average of 7 numbers is 30. If the average of the first 3 numbers is 25 and that of the last 3 numbers is 35, what is the fourth number?
(a) 25 (b) 28 (c) 30 (d) 32
Answer: (c) 30
Solution: Total sum = 7 × 30 = 210. Sum of first 3 = 3 × 25 = 75. Sum of last 3 = 3 × 35 = 105. Fourth number = 210 - (75 + 105) = 210 - 180 = 30.
Source: SSC GD

41. The average of 12 numbers is 15. If each number is decreased by 3, what will be the new average?
(a) 15 (b) 14 (c) 13 (d) 12
Answer: (d) 12
Solution: When each observation is decreased by a constant k, the new average also decreases by k: New Average = 15 - 3 = 12.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

42. In an examination, the average marks obtained by boys is 71 and that by girls is 73. If the average score of all candidates is 71.8, what is the ratio of the number of boys to girls?
(a) 3 : 2 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 4 : 3 (d) 3 : 4
Answer: (a) 3 : 2
Solution: Using allegation: (Girls' Avg - Overall Avg) : (Overall Avg - Boys' Avg) = (73 - 71.8) : (71.8 - 71) = 1.2 : 0.8 = 3 : 2. Ratio of Boys to Girls = 3 : 2.
Source: SSC CGL

43. The average score of 100 students was found to be 40. Later, it was discovered that the marks of two students were misread as 53 and 83 instead of 83 and 53 respectively. What is the correct average?
(a) 39.5 (b) 40.0 (c) 40.5 (d) 41.0
Answer: (b) 40.0
Solution: Sum of correctly read marks = 83 + 53 = 136. Sum of incorrectly read marks = 53 + 83 = 136. Since the sum of errors is zero (136 - 136 = 0), the total sum remains unchanged, and the correct average is 40.0.
Source: SSC CHSL

44. The average age of 30 students in a class is 14 years. When 10 new students join the class whose average age is 16 years, what is the new average age of the entire class?
(a) 14.2 years (b) 14.4 years (c) 14.5 years (d) 14.8 years
Answer: (c) 14.5 years
Solution: Total age of 30 students = 30 × 14 = 420 years. Total age of 10 new students = 10 × 16 = 160 years. Total age of 40 students = 420 + 160 = 580 years. New average = 580 ÷ 40 = 14.5 years.
Source: RRB NTPC

45. The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 61. What is the difference between the highest and the lowest of these numbers?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 10 (d) 8
Answer: (d) 8
Solution: The 5 consecutive odd numbers centered at 61 are 57, 59, 61, 63, 65. Difference = 65 - 57 = 8 (or simply (5 - 1) × 2 = 8).
Source: SSC MTS

46. Out of 4 numbers, the average of the first three numbers is 16 and that of the last three numbers is 15. If the last number is 20, what is the first number?
(a) 23 (b) 21 (c) 25 (d) 24
Answer: (a) 23
Solution: Let the numbers be a, b, c, d. a + b + c = 3 × 16 = 48. b + c + d = 3 × 15 = 45. Subtracting equations: (a + b + c) - (b + c + d) = a - d = 48 - 45 = 3. Since d = 20, a = 20 + 3 = 23.
Source: SSC CGL

47. The average weight of 3 men A, B, and C is 84 kg. When D joins them, the average weight becomes 80 kg. A man E, who weighs 3 kg more than D, replaces A, and the average weight of B, C, D, and E becomes 79 kg. What is the weight of A?
(a) 72 kg (b) 75 kg (c) 78 kg (d) 80 kg
Answer: (b) 75 kg
Solution: A + B + C = 3 × 84 = 252 kg. A + B + C + D = 4 × 80 = 320 kg => D = 320 - 252 = 68 kg. Weight of E = 68 + 3 = 71 kg. B + C + D + E = 4 × 79 = 316 kg => B + C = 316 - 68 - 71 = 177 kg. Weight of A = 252 - (B + C) = 252 - 177 = 75 kg.
Source: SSC CGL

48. A cricket bowler's bowling average was 12.4 runs per wicket. In his next match, he takes 5 wickets for 26 runs, and his average decreases (improves) by 0.4 runs per wicket. How many wickets had he taken before this match?
(a) 75 (b) 80 (c) 85 (d) 90
Answer: (c) 85
Solution: Let initial wickets = w. Total runs = 12.4w. New average = 12.4 - 0.4 = 12.0. Equation: (12.4w + 26) ÷ (w + 5) = 12.0 => 12.4w + 26 = 12w + 60 => 0.4w = 34 => w = 34 ÷ 0.4 = 85 wickets.
Source: SSC CHSL

49. A student's average marks in 4 subjects was 75. In his 5th subject, he scored 20 marks more than the overall average of all 5 subjects. What did he score in the 5th subject?
(a) 85 (b) 90 (c) 95 (d) 100
Answer: (d) 100
Solution: Let overall average of 5 subjects = A. Total marks = 5A. Sum of 4 subjects = 4 × 75 = 300. Score in 5th subject = A + 20. Equation: 300 + (A + 20) = 5A => 320 = 4A => A = 80. Score in 5th subject = 80 + 20 = 100 marks.
Source: SSC CGL

50. The average of 10 numbers is calculated. If each of the first 5 numbers is increased by 2, and each of the remaining 5 numbers is decreased by 4, what is the net change in the average?
(a) Decreases by 0.5 (b) Decreases by 1 (c) Increases by 1 (d) No change
Answer: (b) Decreases by 1
Solution: Net change in total sum = (5 × 2) + [5 × (-4)] = 10 - 20 = -10. Net change in average = -10 ÷ 10 = -1 (decreases by 1).
Source: RRB Group D
