101. The average marks of 14 students was calculated as 71. Later it was found that the marks of one student were recorded as 42 instead of 56, and of another student as 74 instead of 32. What is the correct average?
(a) 69 (b) 68 (c) 70 (d) 73
Answer: (a) 69
Solution: Net error in sum = (56 + 32) - (42 + 74) = 88 - 116 = -28. Correction in average = -28 ÷ 14 = -2. Correct average = 71 - 2 = 69.
Source: SSC CGL

102. The average age of 24 students in a class is 10 years. If the teacher's age is included, the average age increases by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?
(a) 34 years (b) 35 years (c) 36 years (d) 38 years
Answer: (b) 35 years
Solution: Teacher's age = New Average + (Old Count × Increase) = 11 + (24 × 1) = 35 years. (Or 25 × 11 - 24 × 10 = 275 - 240 = 35 years).
Source: SSC CHSL

103. The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 21. What is the product of the smallest and largest of these numbers?
(a) 399 (b) 415 (c) 425 (d) 435
Answer: (c) 425
Solution: The 5 consecutive odd numbers centered at 21 are 17, 19, 21, 23, 25. Smallest = 17, Largest = 25. Product = 17 × 25 = 425.
Source: RRB Group D

104. A car travels the first 120 km at 40 km/h and the next 240 km at 80 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey of 360 km?
(a) 52 km/h (b) 55 km/h (c) 58 km/h (d) 60 km/h
Answer: (d) 60 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 120 + 240 = 360 km. Total time = (120/40) + (240/80) = 3 + 3 = 6 hours. Average speed = 360 ÷ 6 = 60 km/h.
Source: SSC MTS

105. In an examination, the average score of 50 students in Section A is 65, and that of 50 students in Section B is 75. Since the section sizes are equal, what is the combined average score?
(a) 70 (b) 68 (c) 72 (d) 75
Answer: (a) 70
Solution: When group sizes are equal, the combined average is simply the arithmetic mean of the two averages: (65 + 75) ÷ 2 = 70.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

106. A batsman has a batting average of 45 runs across 9 innings. How many runs must he score in his 10th innings so that his overall batting average becomes 50 runs?
(a) 50 (b) 95 (c) 85 (d) 90
Answer: (b) 95
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 50 is just the target average, which fails to lift the previous 9 innings! Total runs in 9 innings = 9 × 45 = 405. Total runs needed in 10 innings = 10 × 50 = 500. Required score = 500 - 405 = 95 runs.
Source: SSC CGL

107. The average salary of 15 workers is ₹12,000. If 3 new workers join with salaries ₹10,000, ₹14,000, and ₹12,000 respectively, what is the new average salary of all 18 workers?
(a) ₹11,500 (b) ₹12,500 (c) ₹12,000 (d) ₹13,000
Answer: (c) ₹12,000
Solution: Average of the 3 new workers = (10000 + 14000 + 12000) ÷ 3 = 36000 ÷ 3 = ₹12,000. Since their average equals the original average, the overall average of all 18 workers remains ₹12,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

108. The average of 6 numbers is 30. If the average of the first 4 numbers is 25 and that of the last 3 numbers is 35, what is the fourth number?
(a) 20 (b) 22 (c) 24 (d) 25
Answer: (d) 25
Solution: Total sum of 6 numbers = 6 × 30 = 180. Sum of first 4 = 4 × 25 = 100. Sum of last 3 = 3 × 35 = 105. Sum of first 4 and last 3 = 100 + 105 = 205. The fourth number is counted in both sets: 4th number = 205 - 180 = 25.
Source: RRB NTPC

109. The average weight of 12 men increases by 1 kg when a man weighing 60 kg is replaced by another man. What is the weight of the new man?
(a) 72 kg (b) 70 kg (c) 74 kg (d) 75 kg
Answer: (a) 72 kg
Solution: Total increase in weight = 12 × 1 = 12 kg. Weight of new man = Weight of replaced man + Total increase = 60 + 12 = 72 kg.
Source: SSC MTS

110. A mock test consists of two sections. Section 1 has 30 questions with an average accuracy of 80%, and Section 2 has 20 questions with an average accuracy of 60%. What is the candidate's overall accuracy percentage on the test?
(a) 70% (b) 72% (c) 74% (d) 68%
Answer: (b) 72%
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 70% is the unweighted average (80 + 60)/2, which assumes equal section lengths! Weighted Accuracy = (30 × 80 + 20 × 60) ÷ (30 + 20) = (2400 + 1200) ÷ 50 = 3600 ÷ 50 = 72%.
Source: SSC GD

111. The average of 11 consecutive integers is 50. What is the smallest of these integers?
(a) 40 (b) 44 (c) 45 (d) 46
Answer: (c) 45
Solution: In 11 consecutive integers, the average is the middle (6th) term = 50. Smallest term = 50 - 5 = 45.
Source: SSC CGL

112. The average of three numbers x, y, and z is 24. If the average of y and z is 20, what is the value of x?
(a) 24 (b) 28 (c) 30 (d) 32
Answer: (d) 32
Solution: x + y + z = 3 × 24 = 72. y + z = 2 × 20 = 40. Therefore, x = (x + y + z) - (y + z) = 72 - 40 = 32.
Source: SSC CHSL

113. If each of the numbers 12, 16, 20, 24, and 28 is increased by 7, what will be the new average of the numbers?
(a) 27 (b) 25 (c) 29 (d) 28
Answer: (a) 27
Solution: The original numbers form an AP with middle term 20 (average = 20). When each number is increased by 7, the new average = 20 + 7 = 27.
Source: RRB Group D

114. The average of 7 numbers is 8. If one new number is added to the set, the average becomes 9. What is the added number?
(a) 15 (b) 16 (c) 17 (d) 18
Answer: (b) 16
Solution: Total sum of 7 numbers = 7 × 8 = 56. Total sum of 8 numbers = 8 × 9 = 72. Added number = 72 - 56 = 16.
Source: SSC MTS

115. The average age of A, B, and C is 26 years. If the average age of A and C is 29 years, what is the age of B?
(a) 18 years (b) 19 years (c) 20 years (d) 22 years
Answer: (c) 20 years
Solution: Total age of A, B, C = 3 × 26 = 78 years. Total age of A and C = 2 × 29 = 58 years. Age of B = 78 - 58 = 20 years.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

116. The average temperature for Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday was 40°C. The average temperature for Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday was 41°C. If the temperature on Thursday was 42°C, what was the temperature on Monday?
(a) 37°C (b) 38°C (c) 40°C (d) 39°C
Answer: (d) 39°C
Solution: (T + W + Th) - (M + T + W) = (3 × 41) - (3 × 40) = 123 - 120 = 3°C => Th - M = 3°C. Since Th = 42°C => 42 - M = 3 => M = 39°C.
Source: SSC CGL

117. The average marks of 100 students is 50. If the average marks of boys is 60 and that of girls is 40, how many boys are there in the group?
(a) 50 (b) 45 (c) 55 (d) 60
Answer: (a) 50
Solution: By allegation: Boys (60) and Girls (40), Mean (50). Ratio Boys : Girls = (50 - 40) : (60 - 50) = 10 : 10 = 1 : 1. Boys = 100 ÷ 2 = 50.
Source: SSC CHSL

118. A batsman scores 80 runs in his 6th match, thereby increasing his batting average by 4 runs. What is his batting average after 6 matches?
(a) 56 (b) 60 (c) 64 (d) 62
Answer: (b) 60
Solution: Let average after 5 matches = x. 5x + 80 = 6(x + 4) => 5x + 80 = 6x + 24 => x = 56. Average after 6 matches = 56 + 4 = 60 runs.
Source: RRB NTPC

119. The average of 9 numbers is 18. If one number is multiplied by 3 and the other 8 numbers remain unchanged, the new sum becomes 180. What was the value of that number?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 12
Answer: (c) 9
Solution: Original sum = 9 × 18 = 162. New sum = 180. Difference = 180 - 162 = 18. Multiplying the number by 3 adds 2 times the number to the sum: 2x = 18 => x = 9.
Source: SSC GD

120. The average of 25 numbers is 18. If the average of the first 12 numbers is 14 and that of the last 12 numbers is 17, what is the 13th number?
(a) 72 (b) 74 (c) 76 (d) 78
Answer: (d) 78
Solution: Total sum of 25 numbers = 25 × 18 = 450. Sum of first 12 = 12 × 14 = 168. Sum of last 12 = 12 × 17 = 204. 13th number = 450 - (168 + 204) = 450 - 372 = 78.
Source: SSC MTS

121. The average of 12 numbers is 42. The average of the first 4 numbers is 38 and that of the next 5 numbers is 44. The 10th number is 2 less than the 11th and 4 less than the 12th number. What is the average of the 10th and 12th numbers?
(a) 44 (b) 43 (c) 45 (d) 46
Answer: (a) 44
Solution: Total sum of 12 numbers = 12 × 42 = 504. Sum of first 4 = 4 × 38 = 152. Sum of next 5 = 5 × 44 = 220. Sum of remaining three numbers (10th, 11th, 12th) = 504 - (152 + 220) = 132. Let 10th = x, 11th = x + 2, 12th = x + 4. x + (x + 2) + (x + 4) = 3x + 6 = 132 => 3x = 126 => x = 42. 10th = 42, 12th = 46. Average of 10th and 12th = (42 + 46) ÷ 2 = 44.
Source: SSC CGL

122. A batsman in his 12th innings makes a score of 63 runs, and thereby increases his batting average by 2 runs. What is his batting average after the 12th innings?
(a) 39 (b) 41 (c) 42 (d) 45
Answer: (b) 41
Solution: Let average after 11 innings = x. 11x + 63 = 12(x + 2) => 11x + 63 = 12x + 24 => x = 39. Average after 12th innings = 39 + 2 = 41 runs.
Source: SSC CGL

123. The average age of 5 members of a committee is the same today as it was 3 years ago, because an older member has been replaced by a younger member. How much younger is the new member than the replaced member?
(a) 12 years (b) 14 years (c) 15 years (d) 18 years
Answer: (c) 15 years
Solution: Over 3 years, the total age of 5 members increases naturally by 5 × 3 = 15 years. Since the average remains unchanged, the replacement must reduce the total sum by exactly 15 years: Difference = 15 years.
Source: SSC CHSL

124. The average scores of three classes A, B, and C are 83, 76, and 85 respectively. The average score of classes A and B combined is 79, and that of classes B and C combined is 81. What is the average score of all three classes combined?
(a) 80.5 (b) 81.0 (c) 82.0 (d) 81.5
Answer: (d) 81.5
Solution: For A and B: Ratio A : B = (79 - 76) : (83 - 79) = 3 : 4. For B and C: Ratio B : C = (85 - 81) : (81 - 76) = 4 : 5. Combined ratio A : B : C = 3 : 4 : 5. Combined average = (3 × 83 + 4 × 76 + 5 × 85) ÷ (3 + 4 + 5) = (249 + 304 + 425) ÷ 12 = 978 ÷ 12 = 81.5.
Source: SSC CGL

125. A student's score in mathematics was mistakenly entered as 88 instead of 68. Due to this error, the average score of the entire class increased by 0.5 marks. What was the total number of students in the class?
(a) 35 (b) 40 (c) 45 (d) 50
Answer: (b) 40
Solution: Increase in total sum = 88 - 68 = 20 marks. Increase per student = 0.5 marks. Number of students = Total increase ÷ Increase per student = 20 ÷ 0.5 = 40 students.
Source: SSC CGL
