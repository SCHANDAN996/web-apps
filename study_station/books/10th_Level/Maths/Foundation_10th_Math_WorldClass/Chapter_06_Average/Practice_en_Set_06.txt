126. The average marks of 120 candidates in an examination was 35. The average marks of passed candidates was 39 and that of failed candidates was 15. What is the number of candidates who passed the examination?
(a) 100 (b) 90 (c) 110 (d) 105
Answer: (a) 100
Solution: By allegation: Ratio Passed : Failed = (35 - 15) : (39 - 35) = 20 : 4 = 5 : 1. Total parts = 5 + 1 = 6 parts = 120 => 1 part = 20. Passed candidates = 5 × 20 = 100.
Source: SSC CGL

127. In a class of 50 students, 40% are girls. The average marks of the whole class is 64.4 and that of the boys is 62. What is the average marks of the girls?
(a) 66 (b) 68 (c) 70 (d) 72
Answer: (b) 68
Solution: Girls = 40% of 50 = 20. Boys = 30. Ratio Boys : Girls = 3 : 2. Total marks = 50 × 64.4 = 3220. Total marks of boys = 30 × 62 = 1860. Total marks of girls = 3220 - 1860 = 1360. Average marks of girls = 1360 ÷ 20 = 68.
Source: SSC CHSL

128. The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 95. What is the fourth number when arranged in descending order?
(a) 97 (b) 95 (c) 93 (d) 91
Answer: (c) 93
Solution: The 5 consecutive odd numbers centered at 95 are 91, 93, 95, 97, 99. In descending order: 99, 97, 95, 93, 91. The 4th number is 93.
Source: RRB Group D

129. A cricketer has a mean score of 60 runs in 10 innings. How many runs should he score in his 11th innings to raise his mean score by 4 runs?
(a) 64 (b) 74 (c) 84 (d) 104
Answer: (d) 104
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 64 simply adds 4 to the current average, forgetting that the 11th innings must raise the score of all 10 previous innings! Total runs in 10 innings = 10 × 60 = 600. New average = 60 + 4 = 64. Total runs needed in 11 innings = 11 × 64 = 704. Required runs = 704 - 600 = 104 runs.
Source: SSC MTS

130. The average weight of 3 men A, B, and C is 65 kg. If another man D joins them, the average weight becomes 68 kg. What is the weight of D?
(a) 77 kg (b) 75 kg (c) 79 kg (d) 81 kg
Answer: (a) 77 kg
Solution: Total weight of A, B, C = 3 × 65 = 195 kg. Total weight of A, B, C, D = 4 × 68 = 272 kg. Weight of D = 272 - 195 = 77 kg.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

131. The average of 7 consecutive numbers is 20. If each number is multiplied by 3, what will be the new average?
(a) 54 (b) 60 (c) 63 (d) 66
Answer: (b) 60
Solution: When each observation in a data set is multiplied by a constant k, the average is also multiplied by k: New Average = 20 × 3 = 60.
Source: SSC CGL

132. The average of 13 numbers is 68. The average of the first 7 numbers is 63 and that of the last 7 numbers is 70. What is the seventh number?
(a) 43 (b) 45 (c) 47 (d) 49
Answer: (c) 47
Solution: Total sum of 13 numbers = 13 × 68 = 884. Sum of first 7 = 7 × 63 = 441. Sum of last 7 = 7 × 70 = 490. Sum of first and last 7 = 441 + 490 = 931. Seventh number = 931 - 884 = 47.
Source: SSC CHSL

133. The average age of 30 boys in a class is 15 years. One boy aged 20 years left the class, and two new boys whose ages differ by 5 years joined. If the new average age of the class remains 15 years, what is the age of the younger newcomer?
(a) 12 years (b) 14 years (c) 16 years (d) 15 years
Answer: (d) 15 years
Solution: Total age initially = 30 × 15 = 450 years. New count = 30 - 1 + 2 = 31 boys. New total age = 31 × 15 = 465 years. Sum of ages of two newcomers = 465 - (450 - 20) = 465 - 430 = 35 years. Let younger = y, older = y + 5 => 2y + 5 = 35 => 2y = 30 => y = 15 years.
Source: RRB NTPC

134. A car travels 80 km at a speed of 40 km/h and returns the same distance of 80 km at a speed of 60 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the round trip?
(a) 48 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 45 km/h (d) 52 km/h
Answer: (a) 48 km/h
Solution: For equal distances, Average Speed = (2 × u × v) ÷ (u + v) = (2 × 40 × 60) ÷ (40 + 60) = 4800 ÷ 100 = 48 km/h.
Source: SSC GD

135. The average of 10 numbers is 23. Later, it was discovered that a number 32 was mistakenly misread as 23 during calculations. What is the correct average?
(a) 22.1 (b) 23.9 (c) 24.2 (d) 23.0
Answer: (b) 23.9
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 22.1 subtracts the difference instead of adding it! Since the correct number 32 is larger than the recorded number 23, the sum must increase by 32 - 23 = +9. Increase per item = +9 ÷ 10 = +0.9. Correct average = 23 + 0.9 = 23.9.
Source: SSC MTS

136. The average score of 20 students is 75 marks. If the highest score of 95 marks and the lowest score of 55 marks are excluded, what is the average score of the remaining 18 students?
(a) 72 (b) 74 (c) 75 (d) 78
Answer: (c) 75
Solution: Total sum of 20 students = 20 × 75 = 1500. Sum of highest and lowest = 95 + 55 = 150. Sum of remaining 18 students = 1500 - 150 = 1350. Average of remaining students = 1350 ÷ 18 = 75 marks.
Source: SSC CGL

137. The average temperature of the first four days of a week was 39°C and that of the last four days was 40°C. If the average temperature for the entire week of 7 days was 39.5°C, what was the temperature on the fourth day?
(a) 38.5°C (b) 39.0°C (c) 40.0°C (d) 39.5°C
Answer: (d) 39.5°C
Solution: Sum of first 4 days = 4 × 39 = 156°C. Sum of last 4 days = 4 × 40 = 160°C. Total sum of all 7 days = 7 × 39.5 = 276.5°C. Temperature on 4th day = (156 + 160) - 276.5 = 316 - 276.5 = 39.5°C.
Source: SSC CHSL

138. The average age of 10 men increases by 2 years when two of them aged 25 years and 35 years are replaced by two new men. What is the average age of the two new men?
(a) 40 years (b) 38 years (c) 42 years (d) 45 years
Answer: (a) 40 years
Solution: Total increase in age = 10 × 2 = 20 years. Sum of ages of replaced men = 25 + 35 = 60 years. Sum of ages of new men = 60 + 20 = 80 years. Average age of new men = 80 ÷ 2 = 40 years.
Source: RRB Group D

139. The average of 5 consecutive integers is 18. What is the sum of the square of the largest number and the square of the smallest number?
(a) 640 (b) 656 (c) 676 (d) 692
Answer: (b) 656
Solution: The 5 consecutive integers centered at 18 are 16, 17, 18, 19, 20. Smallest = 16, Largest = 20. Sum of their squares = 16² + 20² = 256 + 400 = 656.
Source: SSC MTS

140. The average monthly salary of 8 workers is ₹16,000. If each worker is given an annual bonus increment of ₹2,500 in their monthly salary, what will be the new average monthly salary?
(a) ₹18,000 (b) ₹18,250 (c) ₹18,500 (d) ₹19,000
Answer: (c) ₹18,500
Solution: When each observation is increased by a constant k, the new average also increases by k: New Average = 16000 + 2500 = ₹18,500.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

141. The average of three numbers is 77. The first number is twice the second, and the second number is twice the third. What is the difference between the largest and the smallest number?
(a) 88 (b) 92 (c) 96 (d) 99
Answer: (d) 99
Solution: Let third number = x. Second = 2x. First = 4x. Total sum = x + 2x + 4x = 7x. Given total = 3 × 77 = 231 => 7x = 231 => x = 33. Largest = 4 × 33 = 132, Smallest = 33. Difference = 132 - 33 = 99.
Source: SSC CGL

142. In a family of 8 members, the average age of the 5 adults is 44 years, and the average age of the 3 children is 12 years. What is the average age of the whole family?
(a) 32 years (b) 30 years (c) 34 years (d) 35 years
Answer: (a) 32 years
Solution: Total age of adults = 5 × 44 = 220 years. Total age of children = 3 × 12 = 36 years. Total age of family = 220 + 36 = 256 years. Average age of family = 256 ÷ 8 = 32 years.
Source: SSC CHSL

143. The average of 10 numbers is 15. If each number is divided by 3 and then increased by 2, what will be the new average of the numbers?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 7
Solution: Linear transformation property of mean: New Average = (Old Average ÷ 3) + 2 = (15 ÷ 3) + 2 = 5 + 2 = 7.
Source: RRB NTPC

144. A batsman has a batting average of 30 runs in his first 14 innings. How many runs must he score in his 15th innings to make his overall average 32 runs?
(a) 50 (b) 55 (c) 60 (d) 65
Answer: (c) 60
Solution: Total runs in 14 innings = 14 × 30 = 420. Total runs needed in 15 innings = 15 × 32 = 480. Required score = 480 - 420 = 60 runs.
Source: SSC GD

145. The average marks of 35 students in a class is 40. The average marks of 15 of these students is 30. What is the average marks of the remaining 20 students?
(a) 45.0 (b) 46.0 (c) 47.0 (d) 47.5
Answer: (d) 47.5
Solution: Total marks of 35 students = 35 × 40 = 1400. Total marks of 15 students = 15 × 30 = 450. Total marks of remaining 20 students = 1400 - 450 = 950. Average marks of remaining students = 950 ÷ 20 = 47.5.
Source: SSC MTS

146. The average age of 11 cricket players is 28 years. Out of these, the average ages of 3 groups of 3 players each are 25 years, 28 years, and 30 years respectively. In the remaining two players, the captain is 11 years older than the wicketkeeper. What is the age of the wicketkeeper?
(a) 24 years (b) 22 years (c) 26 years (d) 28 years
Answer: (a) 24 years
Solution: Total age of 11 players = 11 × 28 = 308 years. Sum of 9 players = (3 × 25) + (3 × 28) + (3 × 30) = 75 + 84 + 90 = 249 years. Sum of captain and wicketkeeper = 308 - 249 = 59 years. Given Captain - Wicketkeeper = 11 => 2 × Wicketkeeper = 59 - 11 = 48 => Wicketkeeper's age = 24 years.
Source: SSC CGL

147. A bowler's bowling average was 15 runs per wicket. In his next match, he took 4 wickets for 30 runs, and his bowling average decreased (improved) by 0.5 runs per wicket. What was the total number of wickets taken by him including this match?
(a) 56 (b) 60 (c) 64 (d) 68
Answer: (b) 60
Solution: Let wickets before match = w. Equation: (15w + 30) ÷ (w + 4) = 14.5 => 15w + 30 = 14.5w + 58 => 0.5w = 28 => w = 56 wickets. Total wickets including this match = 56 + 4 = 60 wickets.
Source: SSC CGL

148. In an institution, there are 3 groups of students A, B, and C containing 20, 30, and 50 students respectively. The average marks obtained by group A is 70, group B is 60, and group C is 50. What is the average marks of all three groups combined?
(a) 55 (b) 56 (c) 57 (d) 58
Answer: (c) 57
Solution: Ratio of students in groups = 20 : 30 : 50 = 2 : 3 : 5. Weighted Average = (2 × 70 + 3 × 60 + 5 × 50) ÷ (2 + 3 + 5) = (140 + 180 + 250) ÷ 10 = 570 ÷ 10 = 57 marks.
Source: SSC CHSL

149. The average of 9 numbers is 60. The average of the first 5 numbers is 55 and that of the next 3 numbers is 65. What is the ninth number?
(a) 65 (b) 68 (c) 72 (d) 70
Answer: (d) 70
Solution: Total sum of 9 numbers = 9 × 60 = 540. Sum of first 5 numbers = 5 × 55 = 275. Sum of next 3 numbers = 3 × 65 = 195. Sum of first 8 numbers = 275 + 195 = 470. Ninth number = 540 - 470 = 70.
Source: SSC CGL

150. The average weight of 8 persons increases by 1.5 kg when one of them weighing 65 kg is replaced by a new person. What is the weight of the new person?
(a) 75 kg (b) 77 kg (c) 79 kg (d) 81 kg
Answer: (b) 77 kg
Solution: Total increase in weight = 8 × 1.5 = 12 kg. Weight of new person = Weight of replaced person + Total increase = 65 + 12 = 77 kg.
Source: SSC CGL
