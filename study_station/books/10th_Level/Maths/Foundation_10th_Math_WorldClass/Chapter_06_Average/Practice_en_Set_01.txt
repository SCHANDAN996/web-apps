Set 1: Average Multiple Choice Questions (Easy to Medium)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. What is the average of 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50?
(a) 30 (b) 25 (c) 35 (d) 40
Answer: (a) 30
Solution: Sum = 10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50 = 150. Average = 150 ÷ 5 = 30.
Source: NCERT Class 6

2. The average of 5 numbers is 20. What is the sum of these numbers?
(a) 80 (b) 100 (c) 120 (d) 150
Answer: (b) 100
Solution: Total Sum = Average × Number of items = 20 × 5 = 100.
Source: NCERT Class 6

3. What is the average of the first 10 natural numbers (1, 2, 3, ..., 10)?
(a) 5.0 (b) 6.0 (c) 5.5 (d) 6.5
Answer: (c) 5.5
Solution: For consecutive natural numbers from 1 to n, Average = (n + 1) ÷ 2 = (10 + 1) ÷ 2 = 5.5.
Source: NCERT Class 7

4. What is the average of the first 5 multiples of 3?
(a) 6 (b) 12 (c) 15 (d) 9
Answer: (d) 9
Solution: Multiples of 3 are 3, 6, 9, 12, 15. In an arithmetic progression with odd count, the average is the middle number = 9.
Source: NCERT Class 7

5. The average of 4 numbers is 15. If three of the numbers are 12, 15, and 18, what is the fourth number?
(a) 15 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 20
Answer: (a) 15
Solution: Total sum = 4 × 15 = 60. Sum of three numbers = 12 + 15 + 18 = 45. Fourth number = 60 - 45 = 15.
Source: SSC MTS

6. What is the average of the first 10 whole numbers (0, 1, 2, ..., 9)?
(a) 4.0 (b) 4.5 (c) 5.0 (d) 5.5
Answer: (b) 4.5
Solution: First 10 whole numbers are 0 to 9. Average = (First + Last) ÷ 2 = (0 + 9) ÷ 2 = 4.5.
Source: NCERT Class 6

7. A student scored 60, 70, and 80 marks in three terminal tests. What is his average score?
(a) 65 (b) 75 (c) 70 (d) 80
Answer: (c) 70
Solution: Average = (60 + 70 + 80) ÷ 3 = 210 ÷ 3 = 70.
Source: NCERT Class 7

8. A batsman has a batting average of 32 runs in 10 innings. How many runs must he score in his 11th innings to raise his average to 35 runs?
(a) 35 (b) 45 (c) 55 (d) 65
Answer: (d) 65
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 35 is simply the desired average, which forgets that the new score must cover the shortfall of all previous innings! Total runs in 10 innings = 10 × 32 = 320. Total runs needed in 11 innings = 11 × 35 = 385. Required score = 385 - 320 = 65 runs.
Source: SSC CGL

9. What is the average of the first 5 consecutive even numbers (2, 4, 6, 8, 10)?
(a) 6 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: For an odd number of consecutive terms in AP, the average is the middle term = 6. (Or (2 + 10) ÷ 2 = 6).
Source: NCERT Class 6

10. The average of 7 numbers is 18. If one number equal to 18 is excluded from the set, what is the average of the remaining 6 numbers?
(a) 17 (b) 18 (c) 19 (d) 20
Answer: (b) 18
Solution: When an excluded item has value equal to the original average, the average of the remaining items remains unchanged = 18. Mathematically: (7 × 18 - 18) ÷ 6 = (126 - 18) ÷ 6 = 108 ÷ 6 = 18.
Source: RRB Group D

11. The average age of 5 boys is 12 years. If the teacher aged 30 years is included, what is the new average age?
(a) 14 years (b) 16 years (c) 15 years (d) 18 years
Answer: (c) 15 years
Solution: Total age of boys = 5 × 12 = 60 years. With teacher, total age = 60 + 30 = 90 years. New average = 90 ÷ 6 = 15 years.
Source: SSC GD

12. The average of 10 numbers is 20. If 5 is added to each number, what will be the new average?
(a) 20 (b) 22 (c) 30 (d) 25
Answer: (d) 25
Solution: If a constant k is added to each observation in a data set, the new average increases by k: New Average = 20 + 5 = 25.
Source: SSC MTS

13. The average of 8 numbers is 14. If each number is multiplied by 2, what will be the new average?
(a) 28 (b) 16 (c) 24 (d) 32
Answer: (a) 28
Solution: If each observation is multiplied by k, the new average becomes k times the old average: New Average = 14 × 2 = 28.
Source: RRB NTPC

14. What is the average of all odd numbers between 0 and 20?
(a) 9 (b) 10 (c) 11 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 10
Solution: The odd numbers are 1, 3, 5, ..., 19. Average of AP = (First + Last) ÷ 2 = (1 + 19) ÷ 2 = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 7

15. The average of 10 items was found to be 15. Later, it was discovered that one item was incorrectly recorded as 36 instead of 26. What is the correct average?
(a) 14.5 (b) 15.5 (c) 14.0 (d) 13.5
Answer: (c) 14.0
Solution: Initial total sum = 10 × 15 = 150. Correct total sum = 150 - 36 + 26 = 140. Correct average = 140 ÷ 10 = 14.0.
Source: SSC CHSL

16. What is the average of the first 5 prime numbers?
(a) 5.0 (b) 5.2 (c) 5.4 (d) 5.6
Answer: (d) 5.6
Solution: The first 5 prime numbers are 2, 3, 5, 7, and 11. Sum = 2 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 11 = 28. Average = 28 ÷ 5 = 5.6.
Source: NCERT Class 7

17. A man spends ₹120 on Monday, ₹150 on Tuesday, and ₹180 on Wednesday. What is his average daily expenditure for these three days?
(a) ₹150 (b) ₹140 (c) ₹160 (d) ₹170
Answer: (a) ₹150
Solution: Total expenditure = 120 + 150 + 180 = ₹450. Average = 450 ÷ 3 = ₹150.
Source: SSC GD

18. The recorded temperatures of a city for 4 days were 30°C, 32°C, 35°C, and 39°C. What was the average temperature?
(a) 33°C (b) 34°C (c) 35°C (d) 36°C
Answer: (b) 34°C
Solution: Sum = 30 + 32 + 35 + 39 = 136°C. Average = 136 ÷ 4 = 34°C.
Source: NCERT Class 7

19. The average of 6 consecutive odd numbers is 24. What is the largest of these numbers?
(a) 27 (b) 28 (c) 29 (d) 31
Answer: (c) 29
Solution: The average of 6 consecutive odd numbers lies between the 3rd and 4th number. So the numbers are 19, 21, 23, 25, 27, 29. The largest number is 29.
Source: SSC MTS

20. A car travels the first 100 km at a speed of 50 km/h and the next 100 km at a speed of 25 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey?
(a) 37.5 km/h (b) 35 km/h (c) 40 km/h (d) 33 1/3 km/h
Answer: (d) 33 1/3 km/h
Solution: For equal distances, Average Speed = (2 × u × v) ÷ (u + v) = (2 × 50 × 25) ÷ (50 + 25) = 2500 ÷ 75 = 100/3 = 33 1/3 km/h.
Source: RRB Group D

21. In a class, 10 boys have an average score of 40 marks, and 20 girls have an average score of 70 marks. What is the combined average score of the entire class?
(a) 60 (b) 55 (c) 65 (d) 50
Answer: (a) 60
Solution: Trap alert: Option (b) 55 is the simple average (40 + 70)/2, which assumes equal numbers of boys and girls! Weighted Average = (10 × 40 + 20 × 70) ÷ (10 + 20) = (400 + 1400) ÷ 30 = 1800 ÷ 30 = 60 marks.
Source: SSC CGL

22. The average weight of 10 men increases by 1.5 kg when a man weighing 50 kg is replaced by a new man. What is the weight of the new man?
(a) 60 kg (b) 65 kg (c) 70 kg (d) 55 kg
Answer: (b) 65 kg
Solution: Total increase in weight = 10 × 1.5 = 15 kg. Weight of new man = Weight of replaced man + Total increase = 50 + 15 = 65 kg.
Source: SSC CHSL

23. The average of 11 results is 50. If the average of the first 6 results is 49 and that of the last 6 results is 52, what is the sixth result?
(a) 52 (b) 54 (c) 56 (d) 58
Answer: (c) 56
Solution: Total of 11 results = 11 × 50 = 550. Total of first 6 = 6 × 49 = 294. Total of last 6 = 6 × 52 = 312. Sum of first and last 6 = 294 + 312 = 606. Sixth result = 606 - 550 = 56.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

24. The average age of three friends is 24 years, and their ages are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. What is the age of the youngest friend?
(a) 12 years (b) 15 years (c) 20 years (d) 18 years
Answer: (d) 18 years
Solution: Total age = 3 × 24 = 72 years. Total ratio units = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 units = 72 => 1 unit = 6 years. Youngest friend = 3 × 6 = 18 years.
Source: RRB Group D

25. The average of 5 consecutive integers is m. If the next 2 consecutive integers are also included, what will be the new average?
(a) m + 0.5 (b) m + 1 (c) m + 1.5 (d) m + 2
Answer: (b) m + 1
Solution: When the next k consecutive integers are added to an arithmetic progression of common difference 1, the average increases by k/2. For k = 2, increase = 2/2 = 1. New average = m + 1.
Source: SSC CGL
