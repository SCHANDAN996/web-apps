Flashcard 1
Q: If 5 friends have 100 rupees and share equally, what is each one's share called?
A: Average. It is the equal-share value.

Flashcard 2
Q: What is the single golden formula for average?
A: Average = Total Sum ÷ Number of items (A = S ÷ N).

Flashcard 3
Q: If you know Average and Number of items, how do you find the Total Sum?
A: Sum = Average × Number of items (S = A × N).

Flashcard 4
Q: If you know Total Sum and Average, how do you find how many items there are?
A: Number of items = Total Sum ÷ Average (N = S ÷ A).

Flashcard 5
Q: After 10 innings, a batsman's average is 45. He wants to raise it to 50 after 11 innings. How many runs must he score in the 11th inning?
A: 100 runs, not 50. Because (11 × 50) - (10 × 45) = 550 - 450 = 100.

Flashcard 6
Q: In an inclusion problem, what is the one-step formula for the new entry's value?
A: New value = (New Average × New Count) - (Old Average × Old Count).

Flashcard 7
Q: 4 boys average 50 kg, 6 girls average 40 kg. Why is the combined average NOT 45 kg?
A: Because the groups are unequal in size. The correct weighted average is 44 kg.

Flashcard 8
Q: What is the formula for Weighted Average?
A: Weighted Average = (w₁·x₁ + w₂·x₂ + …) ÷ (w₁ + w₂ + …), where w is the weight (count) and x is the average of each group.

Flashcard 9
Q: Find the average of numbers 1, 2, 3, …, 100 without adding them all.
A: (First + Last) ÷ 2 = (1 + 100) ÷ 2 = 50.5. Works for any arithmetic progression!

Flashcard 10
Q: A student wrote 65 instead of 56 in a sum of 20 numbers, getting an average of 40. What is the correct average?
A: First fix the sum: Wrong sum = 800. Correct sum = 800 - 65 + 56 = 791. Correct average = 791 ÷ 20 = 39.55.

Flashcard 11
Q: What is the mnemonic 'SAD' for computing an average?
A: Sum all values, note the Amount (how many numbers), then Divide. SAD = Sum, Amount, Divide.

Flashcard 12
Q: Why is the panda in the mnemonic 'SAD Panda' sad?
A: Because when a student forgets to Divide at the end, the panda cries. Always finish with the Division step to make the panda happy.

Flashcard 13
Q: A class of 15 students has an average score of 72. A new student joins, and the average rises to 74. What was the new student's score?
A: New score = (74 × 16) - (72 × 15) = 1184 - 1080 = 104.

Flashcard 14
Q: How do you quickly find the average of an odd count of consecutive integers?
A: The average is simply the middle number. E.g., for 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, the average is 13.

Flashcard 15
Q: What is the trap when adding one item to a group? Is 'just add the difference' correct?
A: No! The new item must cover the gap for all items in the expanded group. Formula: New value = Target Average + (Old Count × Increase in Average).

Flashcard 16
Q: A batsman has 8 innings with average 30. What should he score in the 9th innings to bring his average to 35?
A: 9 × 35 = 315; 8 × 30 = 240; required score = 315 - 240 = 75 runs.

Flashcard 17
Q: What is the shortcut formula for the effect of one new value on the average?
A: New Average = Old Average + [(New Value - Old Average) ÷ New Total Count].

Flashcard 18
Q: In the 'SAD' mnemonic, what happens if you multiply Average and Amount?
A: You get the Total Sum. The panda happily hugs his bamboo!

Flashcard 19
Q: The average of 10 numbers is 25. If one number is removed, the average of the remaining 9 is 24. What was the removed number?
A: Total of 10 numbers = 250; total of 9 numbers = 216. Removed number = 250 - 216 = 34.

Flashcard 20
Q: A fruit seller's average profit over 4 days is ₹30. How much profit on day 5 makes the 5-day average ₹40?
A: 5 × 40 = 200; 4 × 30 = 120. Fifth day profit = 200 - 120 = ₹80. He must earn ₹80, not ₹40.
