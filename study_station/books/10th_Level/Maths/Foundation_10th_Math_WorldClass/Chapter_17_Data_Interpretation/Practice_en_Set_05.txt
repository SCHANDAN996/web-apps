Q112.
If the budget for Education is increased by 20% next year while the total budget remains the same, and the increase is taken from Others, what will be the new angle for Others?
(a) 20°
(b) 30°
(c) 40°
(d) 50°

Correct Answer: (c) 40°
Solution:

Q113.

(a) 10.5%
(b) 11.5%
(c) 12.5%
(d) 13.5%

Correct Answer: (c) 12.5%
Solution:
CAGR = [(90/50)^(1/5) - 1] × 100.
90/50 = 1.8. 1.8^(0.2). Approx: 1.8^(1/5). 1.12^5 ≈ 1.76, 1.125^5 ≈ 1.80. So about 12.5%. So (c).
