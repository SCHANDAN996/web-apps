The table below shows the number of participants in different events at a school sports day.

Event	Boys	Girls
Race	45	30
Relay	35	25
Jumps	40	20
Throws	30	15

What percentage of total participants were girls? (Approx.)
(a) 33.3%
(b) 37.5%
(c) 40%
(d) 42.5%

Correct Answer: (b) 37.5%
Solution:
Total boys = 45+35+40+30 = 150. Total girls = 30+25+20+15 = 90.
Total participants = 240. Girls % = (90/240)×100 = 37.5%.
