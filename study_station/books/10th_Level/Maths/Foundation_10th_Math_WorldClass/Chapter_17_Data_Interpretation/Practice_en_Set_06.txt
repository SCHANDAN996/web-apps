126. The table shows the marks obtained by five students in three subjects. Some data are missing.

Student A: Maths 80, Science 75, English 70
Student B: Maths 60, Science 80, English 65
Student C: Maths ?, Science 70, English 80
Student D: Maths 75, Science 60, English 75
Student E: Maths 65, Science 85, English 90

The average marks in Science for all five students is 74 and the average marks in English is 76. Student C's total marks in all three subjects is 210. What is C's marks in Maths?
(a) 65 (b) 60 (c) 70 (d) 75
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting trap is to set up equations from the Science and English averages, but those averages involve only the known entries and say nothing about C's Maths mark. C's total is 210, so Maths = 210 − 70 − 80 = 60, so the answer is 60.

127. A pie chart shows a person's monthly expenditure of ₹50,000: Food 30%, Rent 25%, Transport 20%, Entertainment 15%, Savings 10%. Next month, Food rises by 20%, Rent by 10%, Entertainment falls by 40%, Transport is unchanged, and total expenditure stays the same. By what amount does the savings decrease?
(a) ₹1000 (b) ₹1500 (c) ₹1750 (d) ₹1250
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to add the percentage changes (20 + 10 − 40) and apply them to savings directly. New Food = 18,000, Rent = 13,750, Entertainment = 4,500, Transport = 10,000, totalling 46,250, so new savings = 3,750 against the old 5,000, so the answer is ₹1250.

128. Sales (in units) of products A and B over six months are: Jan 100 and 80, Feb 120 and 90, Mar 150 and 110, Apr 130 and 120, May 160 and 140, Jun 180 and 150. In which month is the ratio of A's sales to B's sales closest to the ratio of total A sales to total B sales over the six months?
(a) June (b) March (c) April (d) January
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to pick January because its ratio 1.25 looks close without computing the overall ratio. Total A = 840 and total B = 690, giving 1.217; June's ratio is 1.20 (difference 0.017), closer than January's 0.033, so the answer is June.

(a) Both statements together are needed (b) Statement I alone is sufficient (c) Statement II alone is sufficient (d) Neither statement is sufficient

130. A company had 500 employees in 2020, split as X 30%, Y 40%, Z 30%. In 2021 the total rose by 20% with shares X 25%, Y 50%, Z 25%. In 2022 the total rose by another 10% over 2021 with X : Y : Z = 2 : 5 : 3. What is the percentage increase in department Y's employees from 2020 to 2022?
(a) 55% (b) 65% (c) 70% (d) 60%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to add the two total increases (20% + 10% = 30%) and apply that to Y. Y in 2020 = 200; 2021 total = 600, Y = 300; 2022 total = 660, Y = 5/10 × 660 = 330; increase = 130 over 200, so the answer is 65%.

133. What is the area of a rectangle?
Statement I: The perimeter of the rectangle is 20 cm.
Statement II: The length of the diagonal is √52 cm.
(a) Statement I alone is sufficient (b) Statement II alone is sufficient (c) Neither statement is sufficient (d) Both statements together are needed
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to assume the diagonal alone fixes the sides, but infinitely many rectangles share a diagonal. From Statement I, l + b = 10; from Statement II, l² + b² = 52; then (l + b)² = 100 gives 2lb = 48, so the area is 24 cm² and the answer is Both statements together are needed.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

134. Five machines produce 800, 1200, 1000, 1500 and 900 units with defective rates of 5%, 8%, 10%, 4% and 6% respectively. What is the overall defective percentage for all machines combined (approximately)?
(a) 6.2% (b) 6.6% (c) 7.0% (d) 6.48%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to average the five percentages, getting 6.6%, which ignores the different production volumes. Defective units are 40 + 96 + 100 + 60 + 54 = 350 out of 5400 total, so the answer is 6.48%.

138. The noon temperatures of a city for 7 days are 32, 34, 36, 35, 33, 31, 30 °C. On the 8th day the temperature falls so that the 8-day average becomes 32.5 °C. By how much did the 8th day's temperature fall compared to the 7th day?
(a) 0.5 °C (b) 2 °C (c) 1 °C (d) 1.5 °C
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to continue the recent pattern of 1–2 degree drops without computing. The 7-day sum is 231; the 8-day total must be 8 × 32.5 = 260, so the 8th day is 29 °C, a fall of 1 °C from 30 °C, so the answer is 1 °C.

142. In Term 1, a class of 200 students had a pass percentage of 70%. In Term 2, the number of students who passed rose by 20% over Term 1 and the pass percentage was 80%. How many students failed in Term 2?
(a) 36 (b) 48 (c) 40 (d) 42
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to apply 80% to the original 200 students, getting 40 failures. Term 2 pass count is 140 × 1.2 = 168, so the Term 2 strength is 168 / 0.8 = 210 and failures are 210 − 168, so the answer is 42.

143. A car's speed is recorded as: 9:00 AM 40 km/h, 10:00 AM 60 km/h, 11:30 AM 50 km/h, 1:00 PM 70 km/h. Assuming constant acceleration between consecutive readings, what is the average speed from 9 AM to 1 PM?
(a) 57.5 km/h (b) 52.5 km/h (c) 55.625 km/h (d) 60 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to average the four readings (55 km/h) or treat all gaps as equal, but the intervals are 1, 1.5 and 1.5 hours. Distances are 50 × 1 = 50, 55 × 1.5 = 82.5 and 60 × 1.5 = 90 km, totalling 222.5 km in 4 hours, so the answer is 55.625 km/h.

(a) ₹80,000 (b) ₹1,00,000 (c) ₹90,000 (d) ₹1,10,000

149. A student's day is divided as: Sleep 25%, School 30%, Homework 10%, Play 15%, Others 20%. The student cuts Play time by 40% and adds the saved time equally to Homework and Others. What is the new percentage of time spent on Homework?
(a) 12% (b) 14% (c) 15% (d) 13%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to add the whole 6 percentage points cut from Play to Homework, or to split it wrongly. Play loses 40% of 3.6 hours = 1.44 hours; half of that, 0.72 hour, joins Homework's 2.4 hours to give 3.12 hours out of 24, so the answer is 13%.

150. A company's total sales and regional shares are: 2019 — ₹400 lakh with X 40%, Y 35%, Z 25%; 2020 — ₹500 lakh with X 30%, Y 50%, Z 20%. The profit margin stayed 20% of sales in both years. What was the percentage increase in profit from region Y from 2019 to 2020?
(a) 78.6% (b) 72.5% (c) 68.4% (d) 82%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to compute the sales increase of region Y and then wrongly apply the 20% margin again, or to compare total profits. Y's profit rose from 20% of 140 = ₹28 lakh to 20% of 250 = ₹50 lakh, an increase of 22 over 28, so the answer is 78.6%.
