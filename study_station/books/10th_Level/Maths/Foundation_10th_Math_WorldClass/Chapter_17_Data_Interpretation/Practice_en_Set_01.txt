Q1.
The table below shows the sales (in kg) of two shops over four months.

Month	Shop A	Shop B
Jan	120	100
Feb	150	110
Mar	130	120
Apr	140	130

What was the total sales of Shop A in all four months?
(a) 440 kg
(b) 540 kg
(c) 460 kg
(d) 520 kg

Correct Answer: (b) 540 kg
Solution:
Total of Shop A = 120 + 150 + 130 + 140 = 540 kg.
Source: NCERT Class 8

Q2.
Using the same table, in which month was the difference in sales between Shop A and Shop B maximum?
(a) Jan
(b) Feb
(c) Mar
(d) Apr

Correct Answer: (b) Feb
Solution:
Differences: Jan = 120−100=20; Feb = 150−110=40; Mar = 130−120=10; Apr = 140−130=10. Maximum in Feb.

Q21.
The following table gives the total number of students in a school and the percentage of boys over four years.

Year	Total Students	Boys (%)
2018	400	55%
2019	500	50%
2020	600	45%
2021	550	48%

In which year was the number of girls maximum?
(a) 2018
(b) 2019
(c) 2020
(d) 2021

Correct Answer: (c) 2020
Solution:
Girls = Total × (100 − Boys%) / 100.
2018: 400 × 0.45 = 180
2019: 500 × 0.50 = 250
2020: 600 × 0.55 = 330
2021: 550 × 0.52 = 286
Maximum girls (330) in 2020.
Source: SSC CGL

Q24.
A line graph shows profit percentage of a company (Profit % = (Income − Expenditure)/Expenditure × 100).

Year	Profit %
2013	20%
2014	25%
2015	30%

If the expenditure in 2013 was ₹100 lakh and the expenditure in 2014 was 10% more than in 2013, what was the income in 2014?
(a) ₹120 lakh
(b) ₹125 lakh
(c) ₹137.5 lakh
(d) ₹140 lakh

Correct Answer: (c) ₹137.5 lakh
Solution:
Expenditure 2014 = 100 × 1.10 = 110 lakh.
Profit 25% → Income = Expenditure × (1 + 25/100) = 110 × 1.25 = 137.5 lakh.
Source: IBPS PO

Q25.
Question: What is the total number of apples sold?
Statement I: 40% of the total apples were sold on Monday, which is 50 apples.
Statement II: On Tuesday, 60 apples were sold.

Choose the correct option:
(a) Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not sufficient.
(b) Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not sufficient.
(c) Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
(d) Statements I and II together are not sufficient.

Correct Answer: (a) Statement I alone is sufficient.
Solution:
From I: 40% = 50 → total = 50/0.40 = 125 apples. So total is known from I alone.
Statement II only gives Tuesday’s sale, no relation to total.
Thus I alone is sufficient.
Source: UPSC CSAT
