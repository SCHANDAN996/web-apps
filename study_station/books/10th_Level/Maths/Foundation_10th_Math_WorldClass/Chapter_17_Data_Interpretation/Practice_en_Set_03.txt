51. The monthly income and expenditure of five families are: A — income ₹50,000, expenditure ₹30,000; B — ₹60,000 and ₹40,000; C — ₹45,000 and ₹35,000; D — ₹70,000 and ₹50,000; E — ₹55,000 and ₹35,000. Which family has the highest savings percentage? (Savings = Income − Expenditure)
(a) E (b) A (c) D (d) B
Answer: (b)
Solution: Family E tempts because its savings of ₹20,000 equal family A's in rupees, but E's income base is larger. Savings percentages: A = 20000/50000 = 40%, B = 33.3%, C = 22.2%, D = 28.6%, E = 36.4%, so the answer is A.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

52. The monthly savings of five families are ₹20,000, ₹20,000, ₹10,000, ₹20,000 and ₹20,000. What is the average monthly saving of these five families?
(a) ₹18,000 (b) ₹22,000 (c) ₹16,000 (d) ₹20,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The most frequent value ₹20,000 tempts, but the average is pulled down by the single ₹10,000 value. Sum = 20+20+10+20+20 = ₹90,000 and average = 90,000/5, so the answer is ₹18,000.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

53. A company sold 800 units in Q1, 1000 in Q2, 1200 in Q3 and 900 in Q4. If the profit is ₹50 per unit, what is the total profit for the year?
(a) ₹1,95,000 (b) ₹1,90,000 (c) ₹1,85,000 (d) ₹2,00,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rounding the total to 4000 units gives the tempting ₹2,00,000, but the exact total is 800+1000+1200+900 = 3900 units. Profit = 3900 × 50, so the answer is ₹1,95,000.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

54. A company's sales were 800 units in the first quarter of a year and 1200 units in the third quarter of the same year. By what percent did sales increase from the first quarter to the third quarter?
(a) 60% (b) 40% (c) 50% (d) 75%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dividing the increase 400 by the later value 1200 gives about 33%, the wrong-base trap; the base must be the first quarter's 800. Percentage = (400/800) × 100, so the answer is 50%.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

55. A school's annual budget of ₹4,50,000 is shown in a pie chart: Salaries 120°, Infrastructure 90°, Resources 60°, Maintenance 45°, Miscellaneous 45°. What amount is allocated to Resources?
(a) ₹75,000 (b) ₹60,000 (c) ₹80,000 (d) ₹90,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Using the 90° Infrastructure sector by mistake gives ₹1,12,500, which is not even listed. Resources fraction = 60/360 = 1/6 and amount = 4,50,000/6, so the answer is ₹75,000.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

56. In a school's budget pie chart, Salaries get 120° and Infrastructure gets 90°. What is the ratio of the amount spent on Salaries to that on Infrastructure?
(a) 3:2 (b) 5:3 (c) 5:4 (d) 4:3
Answer: (d)
Solution: Misreading 120:90 as 3:2 is the trap; dividing both by 30 gives 4:3. Amounts are proportional to angles, so the answer is 4:3.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

57. The number of visitors to a website (in thousands) over six months was: 25, 28, 32, 30, 35, 38. What was the median number of visitors?
(a) 30,000 (b) 32,000 (c) 31,000 (d) 33,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Taking the middle value 32 without ordering and averaging the two middle terms is the trap. Ordered values: 25, 28, 30, 32, 35, 38; median = (30+32)/2 = 31 thousand, so the answer is 31,000.

58. The number of visitors to a website grew from 25 thousand in January to 38 thousand in June. What is the percentage growth over this period?
(a) 55% (b) 50% (c) 48% (d) 52%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dividing the growth 13 by the final value 38 gives about 34%, the wrong-base trap; the base is January's 25. Growth = (13/25) × 100, so the answer is 52%.

59. Marks of three students in three tests (each out of 50): P — 35, 40, 42; Q — 42, 38, 40; R — 30, 45, 36. Who has the highest average?
(a) P (b) Q (c) R (d) Both P and Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: Student P looks strong with a rising trend, but the total decides the average. Averages: P = 117/3 = 39, Q = 120/3 = 40, R = 111/3 = 37, so the answer is Q.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

60. A family's monthly expenses of ₹96,000 are distributed as: Food 30%, Rent 25%, Transport 15%, Savings 20%, Others 10%. How much more is spent on Food than on Transport?
(a) ₹16,000 (b) ₹18,000 (c) ₹14,400 (d) ₹12,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Computing Food alone (₹28,800) and forgetting to subtract Transport is the trap. Difference in percentage = 30% − 15% = 15% and 15% of 96,000, so the answer is ₹14,400.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

61. A family's monthly budget is ₹96,000, of which 20% is savings. If savings are increased by 25% while the total budget stays the same, the new savings are what percent of the total?
(a) 22% (b) 23% (c) 25% (d) 24%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding 20% + 25% = 45% is the trap; the 25% rise applies to the savings amount, not the budget. New savings = 19,200 + 4,800 = ₹24,000, so the answer is 25%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

62. Trees planted in a city: 2016 — 400, 2017 — 500, 2018 — 450, 2019 — 600, 2020 — 650. In which year was the percentage increase over the previous year the maximum?
(a) 2017 (b) 2019 (c) 2018 (d) 2020
Answer: (b)
Solution: Picking 2020 for the highest total is the trap; percentage increase depends on the jump over the previous year. Increases: 2017 = 25%, 2018 = decrease, 2019 = 150/450 = 33.3%, 2020 = 8.3%, so the answer is 2019.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

63. The numbers of trees planted in a city over five consecutive years were 400, 500, 450, 600 and 650. What is the average number of trees planted per year?
(a) 540 (b) 500 (c) 520 (d) 560
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dividing the total by 4 instead of 5 gives 650, the trap. Total = 2600 and average = 2600/5, so the answer is 520.

64. The 6 AM temperatures (°C) of a city on six days were: 22, 24, 26, 23, 25, 27. What is the range of the temperatures?
(a) 5°C (b) 7°C (c) 6°C (d) 4°C
Answer: (a)
Solution: Subtracting two middle values like 26 − 22 = 4 tempts, but range uses only the extremes. Range = 27 − 22, so the answer is 5°C.

65. The 6 AM temperatures (°C) of a city on six days were 22, 24, 26, 23, 25 and 27, and the reading of 23°C was recorded on Thursday. By how much is Thursday's temperature less than the average of all six days?
(a) 0.5°C (b) 1.5°C (c) 1°C (d) 2°C
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rounding the average 24.5 down to 24 gives the tempting 1°C. Sum = 147, average = 147/6 = 24.5°C and difference = 24.5 − 23, so the answer is 1.5°C.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

66. Total production of a factory was 500 tonnes in 2018 and 600 tonnes in 2019. Product B's share was 35% in 2018 and 45% in 2019. What is the difference in production of B between 2019 and 2018?
(a) 95 tonnes (b) 90 tonnes (c) 85 tonnes (d) 100 tonnes
Answer: (a)
Solution: Applying 45% to the 2018 total of 500 is the trap; each year's share applies to that year's total. B in 2018 = 175 tonnes, B in 2019 = 270 tonnes, so the answer is 95 tonnes.

67. What is the total number of books sold by a shopkeeper? Statement I: Fiction books sold are 40% of the total. Statement II: Non-fiction books sold are 120.
(a) Statement I alone is sufficient (b) Statement II alone is sufficient (c) Neither is sufficient (d) Both together are sufficient
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement I gives only a percentage and Statement II only an absolute number, so each alone is insufficient. Combined, non-fiction = 60% of total = 120, giving total = 200, so the answer is that both together are sufficient.

68. Employees in three departments of a firm: 2020 — IT 120, HR 80, Finance 100; 2021 — IT 150, HR 90, Finance 110. What is the percentage increase in total employees from 2020 to 2021?
(a) 16.67% (b) 18% (c) 20% (d) 22.5%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dividing the increase 50 by the 2021 total 350 gives about 14%, the wrong-base trap. Totals are 300 and 350, and (50/300) × 100, so the answer is 16.67%.

69. The number of employees in three departments of a firm changed from 2020 to 2021 as follows: IT — 120 to 150; HR — 80 to 90; Finance — 100 to 110. Which department had the highest percentage increase in employees?
(a) Finance (b) All equal (c) HR (d) IT
Answer: (d)
Solution: HR and Finance have equal absolute increases of 10, which tempts "all equal", but the bases differ. Percentages: IT = 30/120 = 25%, HR = 12.5%, Finance = 10%, so the answer is IT.

70. A pie chart shows energy sources: Coal 180°, Hydro 60°, Nuclear 45°, Solar 45°, Wind 30°. What percent of energy comes from Coal?
(a) 45% (b) 50% (c) 55% (d) 60%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Reading the angle 180 as a percentage directly is the trap. Coal fraction = 180/360 = 1/2, so the answer is 50%.

71. In an energy pie chart, Coal is 180° and Nuclear is 45° out of 360°. If total energy generated is 10,000 MWh, how much energy comes from these two non-renewable sources together?
(a) 6,500 MWh (b) 7,250 MWh (c) 7,000 MWh (d) 6,250 MWh
Answer: (d)
Solution: Taking only Coal's 50% and getting 5,000 MWh is the trap; both sectors must be added. Combined angle = 225°, fraction = 62.5% of 10,000, so the answer is 6,250 MWh.

72. Sales of a shop (in ₹ lakh) over six days: 50, 60, 55, 70, 65, 80. What is the difference between the highest and the lowest sale?
(a) ₹30 lakh (b) ₹25 lakh (c) ₹35 lakh (d) ₹20 lakh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Confusing the range with the average (about 63) is the trap. Highest = 80 and lowest = 50, so the answer is ₹30 lakh.

74. What is the ratio of boys to girls in a class? Statement I: There are 20 boys in the class. Statement II: The number of girls is 60% of the number of boys.
(a) Statement I alone is sufficient (b) Both statements together are required (c) Statement II alone is sufficient (d) Neither is sufficient
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement I gives only a count of boys with no link to girls, so it is insufficient alone. Statement II alone gives girls = 0.6 × boys, fixing boys : girls = 5 : 3 without needing Statement I, so the answer is that Statement II alone is sufficient.

(a) ₹220 crore (b) ₹200 crore (c) ₹160 crore (d) ₹180 crore
Answer: (b)
Solution: Confusing Machinery's 25% share with Electronics' 20% gives ₹160 crore, the trap. Machinery = 25% of 800, so the answer is ₹200 crore.
