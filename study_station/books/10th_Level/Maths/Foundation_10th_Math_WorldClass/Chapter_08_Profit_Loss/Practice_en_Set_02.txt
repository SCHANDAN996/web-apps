28. A dishonest milkman adds water to milk and claims to sell it at cost price. If he makes a profit of 25%, how much water (in ml) does he add to 1 litre of pure milk?
(A) 250 ml
(B) 200 ml
(C) 300 ml
(D) 150 ml

✅ Answer: (A) 250 ml
Solution: Profit% = (Water/Qty of pure milk) × 100 = 25% ⇒ Water / 1000 = 0.25 ⇒ Water = 250 ml. Alternatively, ratio milk:water = 4:1 ⇒ 250 ml water per 1000 ml milk.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

29. A trader sells two articles at $600 each, one at a profit of 20% and the other at a loss of 20%. What is his overall loss percentage?
(A) 4%
(B) 5%
(C) 2%
(D) 3%

✅ Answer: (A) 4%
Solution: Loss% = (x²/100)% = (20²/100)% = 4%.
Source: SBI Clerk Mains 2018

30. The cost price of 20 articles is the same as the selling price of x articles. If the profit is 25%, then the value of x is:
(A) 16
(B) 15
(C) 18
(D) 12

✅ Answer: (A) 16
Solution: CP of 20 = SP of x. Profit% = ((20 – x)/x) × 100 = 25 ⇒ (20 – x)/x = 0.25 ⇒ 20 – x = 0.25x ⇒ 20 = 1.25x ⇒ x = 16.
Source: SSC CGL 2019 (Tier 1)

31. A shopkeeper fixes the marked price of a shirt at 50% above its cost price. He then gives a discount of 20% on it. If the cost price is $800, what is his profit or loss percent?
(A) 20% profit
(B) 15% profit
(C) 10% profit
(D) 25% profit

✅ Answer: (A) 20% profit
Solution: CP = 800, MP = 800 × 1.5 = 1200. SP = 1200 × 0.8 = 960. Profit = 160, Profit% = (160/800) × 100 = 20%.
Source: IBPS Clerk Prelims 2020
