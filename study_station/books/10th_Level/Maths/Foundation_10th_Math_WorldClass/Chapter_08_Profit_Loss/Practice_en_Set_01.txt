22. A dishonest vendor professes to sell his goods at cost price but uses a weight of 800 gm for 1 kg. His gain percent is:
(A) 20%
(B) 25%
(C) 30%
(D) 15%

✅ Answer: (B) 25%

25. A person bought some articles for $600. He sold one-third of them at a loss of 20%. At what profit percent must he sell the remaining articles so that he gains 16% on the whole transaction?
(A) 32%
(B) 36%
(C) 34%
(D) 40%

✅ Answer: (C) 34%
Solution: Total CP = 600, required total SP = 600 × 1.16 = 696. One-third cost = 200, sold at 20% loss → SP1 = 200 × 0.8 = 160. Remaining cost = 400, required SP2 = 696 – 160 = 536. Profit on remaining = 536 – 400 = 136. Profit% = (136/400)×100 = 34%.
Source: PYQ-style
