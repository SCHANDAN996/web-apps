51. A trader marks his goods 35% above cost price and allows a discount of 20%. What is his profit percent?
(A) 8%
(B) 10%
(C) 12%
(D) 15%

✅ Answer: (A) 8%
Solution: Let CP = 100. MP = 135. SP after 20% discount = 135 × 0.8 = 108. Profit = 8, so 8%.

54. By selling 12 oranges for a rupee, a fruit seller loses 20%. How many oranges per rupee should he sell to gain 20%?
(A) 8
(B) 10
(C) 6
(D) 9

✅ Answer: (A) 8
Solution: Current SP per orange = 1/12 rupee. Loss 20% means SP = 80% of CP, so CP per orange = (1/12)/0.8 = 1/9.6 = 5/48. To gain 20%, new SP = 1.2 × CP = 1.2 × 5/48 = 6/48 = 1/8 rupee per orange. So 8 oranges per rupee.
Source: SSC CGL 2018 (Tier 1)
