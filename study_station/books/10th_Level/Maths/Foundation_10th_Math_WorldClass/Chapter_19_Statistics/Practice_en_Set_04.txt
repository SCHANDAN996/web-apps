Q98. If the mode and median of a data set are 25 and 30 respectively, then the mean is:
A. 30
B. 32.5
C. 35
D. 37.5

✅ Correct Answer: B. 32.5
Step‑by‑step: Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean → 25 = 3×30 – 2 Mean → 25 = 90 – 2 Mean → 2 Mean = 65 → Mean = 32.5.
