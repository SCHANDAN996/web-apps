Q126. The mean of 20 observations is 15. On verification, it was found that two observations 18 and 23 were wrongly taken as 28 and 12. The correct mean is:
A. 14.95
B. 15.05
C. 15.00
D. 15.10

✅ Correct Answer: B. 15.05
Step-by-step: Incorrect sum = 20 × 15 = 300. Correction = (18 + 23) – (28 + 12) = 41 – 40 = +1. Correct sum = 300 + 1 = 301. Correct mean = 301/20 = 15.05.
Source: SSC CGL 2017
