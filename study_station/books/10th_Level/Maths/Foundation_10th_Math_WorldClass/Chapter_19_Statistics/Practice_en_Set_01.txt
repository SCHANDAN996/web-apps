📝 Set 1: Statistics MCQs (1–25)

Q1. The science of collecting, organising, presenting, analysing, and interpreting data is called:
A. Algebra
B. Geometry
C. Statistics
D. Arithmetic

✅ Correct Answer: C. Statistics
Step-by-step: By definition, Statistics is the branch of mathematics dealing with data handling.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q2. In a dataset of 10 students' marks: 12, 15, 11, 18, 12, 15, 12, 18, 15, 12, the most frequent mark is:
A. 18
B. 15
C. 12
D. 11

✅ Correct Answer: C. 12
Step-by-step: Mode is the most frequent value. Tally: 11(1), 12(4), 15(3), 18(2). 12 appears 4 times.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q3. The mean of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 is:
A. 5
B. 6
C. 7
D. 8

✅ Correct Answer: B. 6
Step-by-step: Sum = 2+4+6+8+10 = 30, n=5, Mean = 30/5 = 6.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q4. The middlemost value of a data set when arranged in order is called:
A. Mean
B. Mode
C. Median
D. Range

✅ Correct Answer: C. Median
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q5. The median of 3, 1, 5, 2, 4 is:
A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 5

✅ Correct Answer: C. 3
Step-by-step: Arrange ascending: 1,2,3,4,5. n=5 (odd), median position = (5+1)/2 = 3rd term = 3.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q6. The mode of 5, 6, 7, 6, 5, 8, 6 is:
A. 5
B. 6
C. 7
D. 8

✅ Correct Answer: B. 6
Step-by-step: Frequency: 5(2), 6(3), 7(1), 8(1). Mode = 6.
Source: NCERT Class 9

A. Median

Q10. The mode of 2, 2, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5 is:
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5

✅ Correct Answer: C. 4
Step-by-step: Frequency: 2(2), 3(1), 4(3), 5(1). Mode = 4.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q11. If the mean of 5, x, 3, 7 is 5, then x is:
A. 3
B. 5
C. 4
D. 6

✅ Correct Answer: B. 5
Step-by-step: Sum = 5+x+3+7 = 15+x. Mean = (15+x)/4 = 5 → 15+x = 20 → x=5.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q12. The median of an even number of observations is the:
A. First term
B. Average of the two middle terms
C. Last term
D. Most frequent term

✅ Correct Answer: B. Average of the two middle terms
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q13. The arithmetic mean of first 5 natural numbers is:
A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5

✅ Correct Answer: B. 3
Step-by-step: 1+2+3+4+5=15, n=5, Mean = 3.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q14. In a data set, if the mean = 15 and the median = 17, then mode (using empirical relation) is approximately:
A. 21
B. 19
C. 17
D. 15

✅ Correct Answer: A. 21
Step-by-step: Mode = 3 Median - 2 Mean = 3×17 - 2×15 = 51 - 30 = 21.
Source: NCERT Class 10

Q15. The collection of numerical figures giving some information is called:
A. Frequency
B. Data
C. Range
D. Class mark

✅ Correct Answer: B. Data
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q16. The marks of a student in 6 subjects are: 72, 85, 90, 68, 74, 81. The mean marks are:
A. 76.5
B. 78.33
C. 80
D. 75

✅ Correct Answer: B. 78.33
Step-by-step: Sum = 72+85+90+68+74+81 = 470; n=6; Mean = 470/6 ≈ 78.33.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q17. The class mark of the class interval 30 – 40 is:
A. 30
B. 35
C. 40
D. 10

✅ Correct Answer: B. 35
Step-by-step: Class mark = (30+40)/2 = 35.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q18. The mode of a set of observations is the value which:
A. Occurs most frequently
B. Is the average
C. Is the middle value
D. Is minimum

Q19. For the data: 8, 9, 10, 11, 11, 12, 13, the median is:
A. 9
B. 10
C. 11

Source: NCERT Class 9

Q20. In a grouped frequency distribution, the mid-value of a class is called:
A. Class limit
B. Class width
C. Class mark
D. Frequency

✅ Correct Answer: C. Class mark
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q21. The mean of 10 observations is 20. If each observation is increased by 5, the new mean will be:
A. 20
B. 25
C. 15
D. 100

✅ Correct Answer: B. 25
Step-by-step: If each observation is increased by a constant k, the mean also increases by k. So new mean = 20+5 = 25.
Source: SSC MTS 2019

Q22. Find the median of the following data: 41, 45, 32, 55, 50, 48, 50.
A. 45
B. 48
C. 50
D. 55

✅ Correct Answer: B. 48
Step-by-step: Sort: 32,41,45,48,50,50,55. n=7 odd, median = 4th term = 48.
Source: RRB NTPC 2016

Q23. The empirical relationship between three measures of central tendency for a moderately skewed distribution is:
A. Mode = 3 Mean – 2 Median
B. Median = 3 Mode – 2 Mean
C. Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean
D. Mean = 3 Mode – 2 Median

✅ Correct Answer: C. Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean
Source: NCERT Class 10

Q24. The mean of the following data: 5, 8, 7, 6, 9, 5 is 6.5. If 5 is replaced by 11, what will be the new mean?
A. 7.0
B. 7.5
C. 8.0
D. 6.5

✅ Correct Answer: B. 7.5
Step-by-step: Original sum = 6.5×6 = 39. New sum = 39 – 5 + 11 = 45. New mean = 45/6 = 7.5.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

✅ Correct Answer: B. 16
