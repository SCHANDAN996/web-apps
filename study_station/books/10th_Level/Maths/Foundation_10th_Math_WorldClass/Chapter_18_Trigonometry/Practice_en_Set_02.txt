Set 2: Trigonometry (Foundation) – Medium Questions (26–50)

26. If sec θ + tan θ = 2, then sec θ − tan θ equals:
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 1/2 (d) 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 2, which just repeats the given value. Using sec²θ − tan²θ = 1, we get (sec θ + tan θ)(sec θ − tan θ) = 1, so 2 × (sec θ − tan θ) = 1, giving sec θ − tan θ = 1/2. So the answer is 1/2.
Source: PYQ-style

27. If sin θ = 3/5 and θ is acute, then the value of (sec θ − tan θ)/(sec θ + tan θ) is:
(a) 1/2 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 1/4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 4, which is the reciprocal obtained by inverting the ratio. The identity (sec θ − tan θ)/(sec θ + tan θ) = (1 − sin θ)/(1 + sin θ) gives (1 − 3/5)/(1 + 3/5) = (2/5)/(8/5) = 1/4. So the answer is 1/4.
Source: PYQ-style

28. The value of (sin 30° + cos 60°)/tan 45° is:
(a) 0 (b) 1/2 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 1/2, which is just sin 30° alone. Here sin 30° + cos 60° = 1/2 + 1/2 = 1, and dividing by tan 45° = 1 leaves 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

29. The minimum value of 3 sin²θ + 4 cos²θ is:
(a) 3 (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 4, the larger coefficient, but that is the maximum. Writing the expression as 3 sin²θ + 4(1 − sin²θ) = 4 − sin²θ, the minimum occurs when sin²θ = 1, giving 4 − 1 = 3. So the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

30. From a point on the ground, the angle of elevation of the top of a tower is 30°. On walking 30 m towards the tower, the angle becomes 60°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 30√3 m (b) 15√3 m (c) 15 m (d) 30 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 30√3 m, which comes from using tan 60° = h/30 directly. Let the final distance be x; then h = x√3 and h = (x + 30)/√3, so 3x = x + 30, x = 15, and h = 15√3 m. So the answer is 15√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

31. If a = sin θ + cos θ and b = sin θ − cos θ, then a² + b² equals:
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 1, by wrongly applying sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 only once. Expanding, a² = 1 + 2 sin θ cos θ and b² = 1 − 2 sin θ cos θ, and their sum is 2. So the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

32. The shadow of a 15 m high pole is 5√3 m long. The angle of elevation of the sun is:
(a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 75° (d) 60°
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 30°, obtained by inverting the ratio. Here tan θ = 15/(5√3) = √3, and tan 60° = √3, so θ = 60°. So the answer is 60°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

33. If tan θ = 4/3 and θ is acute, then the value of (1 − cos θ)/(1 + cos θ) is:
(a) 1/4 (b) 4/9 (c) 1/9 (d) 9/4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 1/9, which comes from using cos θ = 4/5 instead of 3/5. From the 3-4-5 triangle, cos θ = 3/5, so (1 − 3/5)/(1 + 3/5) = (2/5)/(8/5) = 1/4. So the answer is 1/4.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

34. If sin(A + B) = 1 and cos(A − B) = √3/2, with A + B ≤ 90° and A > B, then A and B are:
(a) A = 45°, B = 15° (b) A = 75°, B = 15° (c) A = 60°, B = 30° (d) A = 30°, B = 15°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is A = 45°, B = 15°, which satisfies A − B = 30° but not A + B = 90°. Here sin(A + B) = 1 gives A + B = 90°, and cos(A − B) = √3/2 gives A − B = 30°; solving gives A = 60°, B = 30°. So the answer is A = 60°, B = 30°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

35. The value of tan 25°/cot 65° is:
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) tan²25° (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is tan²25°, from multiplying instead of recognising the complementary pair. Since cot 65° = tan(90° − 65°) = tan 25°, the ratio is tan 25°/tan 25° = 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

36. If sin θ = 1/3 and θ is acute, then cot θ equals:
(a) √2/2 (b) 2√2 (c) 2√2/3 (d) 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 3, which is cosec θ, not cot θ. Here cos θ = √(1 − 1/9) = 2√2/3, so cot θ = cos θ/sin θ = (2√2/3)/(1/3) = 2√2. So the answer is 2√2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

37. A man observes the top of a tower from a point 90 m away from its foot. If the angle of elevation is 30°, the height of the tower is:
(a) 30 m (b) 90√3 m (c) 45 m (d) 30√3 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 90√3 m, from multiplying by √3 instead of dividing. Since tan 30° = h/90, we get h = 90/√3 = 30√3 m. So the answer is 30√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

38. If sin θ − cos θ = 0, then the value of sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ is:
(a) 1/4 (b) 1 (c) 3/4 (d) 1/2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 1, by wrongly applying sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 to the fourth powers. Here sin θ = cos θ means θ = 45°, so sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ = (1/√2)⁴ + (1/√2)⁴ = 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2. So the answer is 1/2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

39. The value of cos 1° · cos 2° · cos 3° … cos 90° is:
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 1/2 (d) √3/2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 1, assuming the cosines pair up nicely. The product contains the factor cos 90° = 0, and any product with a zero factor is zero. So the answer is 0.
Source: PYQ-style

40. If cot θ = 7/24 and θ is acute, then sin θ is:
(a) 7/25 (b) 7/24 (c) 25/24 (d) 24/25
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 7/25, which swaps the adjacent and opposite sides. With cot θ = 7/24, the hypotenuse is √(7² + 24²) = 25, so sin θ = 24/25. So the answer is 24/25.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

41. The value of (1 − tan²30°)/(1 + tan²30°) equals:
(a) sin 60° (b) cos 60° (c) tan 60° (d) cot 60°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is sin 60°, since 60° = 2 × 30° suggests the double-angle sine. With tan 30° = 1/√3, the expression is (1 − 1/3)/(1 + 1/3) = (2/3)/(4/3) = 1/2, which equals cos 60°. So the answer is cos 60°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

42. A ladder leans against a wall making an angle of 60° with the ground. If the foot of the ladder is 2 m from the wall, the length of the ladder is:
(a) 4 m (b) 2/√3 m (c) 1 m (d) 2√3 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 2√3 m, from using tan 60° instead of cos 60°. Here cos 60° = 2/L, so 1/2 = 2/L, giving L = 4 m. So the answer is 4 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

43. If sin θ + cos θ = 7/5, then sin θ cos θ equals:
(a) 12/25 (b) 24/25 (c) 1/5 (d) 2/5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 24/25, which is 2 sin θ cos θ, not sin θ cos θ. Squaring gives 1 + 2 sin θ cos θ = 49/25, so 2 sin θ cos θ = 24/25 and sin θ cos θ = 12/25. So the answer is 12/25.
Source: PYQ-style

44. The value of sin 18°/cos 72° is:
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) tan 18°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is tan 18°, from treating cos 72° as cos 18°. Since cos 72° = sin(90° − 72°) = sin 18°, the ratio is sin 18°/sin 18° = 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

45. If √3 tan θ = 1 and θ is acute, then θ is:
(a) 15° (b) 30° (c) 60° (d) 45°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 60°, since tan 60° = √3 appears in the equation. Here tan θ = 1/√3, and tan 30° = 1/√3, so θ = 30°. So the answer is 30°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

46. The angle of elevation of the top of a building from a point 15 m from its foot is 60°. The height of the building is:
(a) 45 m (b) 15/√3 m (c) 15√3 m (d) 30 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 15/√3 m, from dividing by √3 instead of multiplying. Since tan 60° = h/15, we get h = 15√3 m. So the answer is 15√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

47. If sin 3θ = cos(θ − 6°), where 3θ and θ − 6° are acute, then θ is:
(a) 18° (b) 27° (c) 24° (d) 30°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 18°, from setting 3θ = θ − 6° + 90° incorrectly. Since sin 3θ = cos(90° − 3θ), we get 90° − 3θ = θ − 6°, so 4θ = 96° and θ = 24°. So the answer is 24°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

48. The value of (sin²63° + sin²27°)/(cos²17° + cos²73°) is:
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 1/2 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 2, from adding the two identity-sums instead of dividing. Since sin 27° = cos 63°, the numerator is sin²63° + cos²63° = 1; since cos 73° = sin 17°, the denominator is cos²17° + sin²17° = 1, so the ratio is 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

49. If tan θ = 1/√3 and θ is acute, then cos²θ/(1 − sin²θ) equals:
(a) 0 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 3, from computing cos²30° = 3/4 and forgetting the denominator. Since 1 − sin²θ = cos²θ, the ratio cos²θ/cos²θ is 1 for any acute θ. So the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

50. A kite flies at a height of 75 m with its string inclined at 60° to the horizontal. Assuming the string is straight, its length is:
(a) 75√3 m (b) 50√3 m (c) 75/√3 m (d) 150 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 75√3 m, from multiplying by √3 instead of using sin 60°. Here sin 60° = 75/L, so L = 75 × 2/√3 = 150/√3 = 50√3 m. So the answer is 50√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics
