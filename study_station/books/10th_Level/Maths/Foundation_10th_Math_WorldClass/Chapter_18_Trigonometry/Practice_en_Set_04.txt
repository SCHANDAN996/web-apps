Set 4: Trigonometry (Foundation) – Medium Questions (76–100)

76. If sin θ = 3/5, then the value of sec θ + tan θ is:
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 1/2 (d) 5/2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding sec θ and tan θ with wrong cos θ; here cos θ = √(1 − 9/25) = 4/5, so sec θ = 5/4 and tan θ = 3/4. Their sum is 8/4, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

77. The value of (sin 70°/cos 20°) + (cosec 20°/sec 70°) is:
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) −1 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students stop after the first term equals 1 and pick 1. But sin 70° = cos 20° makes the first term 1, and sec 70° = cosec 20° makes the second term 1 as well, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

78. If tan θ = a/b, then (a sin θ − b cos θ)/(a sin θ + b cos θ) equals:
(a) (a² − b²)/(a² + b²) (b) 0 (c) a²/(a² + b²) (d) b²/(a² + b²)
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dividing numerator and denominator by b cos θ converts the expression into (a tan θ − b)/(a tan θ + b); substituting tan θ = a/b gives (a²/b − b)/(a²/b + b). The trap is forgetting to divide by cos θ, so the answer is (a² − b²)/(a² + b²).
Source: PYQ-style

79. A kite is flying with its string inclined at 45° to the ground. If the string is 100 m long, the height of the kite is:
(a) 50 m (b) 100√2 m (c) 50√2 m (d) 100 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using cos 45° on the wrong side or taking height = string. Height = 100 sin 45° = 100/√2, so the answer is 50√2 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

80. If sin θ + cosec θ = 2, then sin²θ + cosec²θ equals:
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: Squaring blindly gives 4 − 2 = 2 only after checking; here x + 1/x = 2 forces x = 1, so sin θ = cosec θ = 1. Then 1 + 1 = 2, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

81. The value of (1 − tan²45°)/(1 + tan²45°) is:
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) 1/2 (d) √3/2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Since tan 45° = 1, the numerator becomes 1 − 1 = 0 while the denominator is 2. The trap is computing only the denominator and choosing 1, so the answer is 0.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

82. From a point on the ground, the angle of elevation of the bottom of a 10 m high window is 30° and of its top is 45°. The height of the bottom of the window above the ground is:
(a) 10(√3 + 1) m (b) 10(√3 − 1) m (c) 5(√3 − 1) m (d) 5(√3 + 1) m
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let the bottom height be h and distance d; then d = h√3 from tan 30°, and h + 10 = d from tan 45°. So h(√3 − 1) = 10, giving h = 10/(√3 − 1); the trap is forgetting to rationalise, so the answer is 5(√3 + 1) m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

83. If sin θ + cos θ = √2 cos θ, then cos θ − sin θ equals:
(a) √2 cos θ (b) √2 sin θ (c) cos θ (d) sin θ
Answer: (b)
Solution: Squaring the given relation yields 2 sin θ cos θ = 2cos²θ − 1, so (cos θ − sin θ)² = 1 − 2 sin θ cos θ = 2 sin²θ. The trap is assuming the result is √2 cos θ by symmetry, so the answer is √2 sin θ.
Source: PYQ-style

84. If sec θ = x + 1/(4x), then sec θ + tan θ is:
(a) 2x (b) x/2 (c) x (d) 1/(2x)
Answer: (a)
Solution: tan²θ = sec²θ − 1 = (x − 1/(4x))², so tan θ = x − 1/(4x) for acute θ. Adding cancels the fraction terms; the trap is subtracting instead of adding, so the answer is 2x.
Source: PYQ-style

85. From a point 20 m away from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of its top is 60°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 20√3 m (b) 10√3 m (c) 40√3 m (d) 20/√3 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: tan 60° = h/20 gives h = 20√3. The trap is dividing by √3 (using cot by mistake), so the answer is 20√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

86. The value of (cos 80°/sin 10°) + (cos 59° cosec 31°)/(sin 14° sec 76°) is:
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: cos 80° = sin 10° makes the first term 1; cos 59° = sin 31° and cos 76° = sin 14° make the second fraction 1/1 = 1. The trap is treating the second fraction as 0, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

87. If sin θ = a/√(a² + b²), then cos θ is:
(a) √(a² + b²)/a (b) a/b (c) b/√(a² + b²) (d) √(a² + b²)/b
Answer: (c)
Solution: cos²θ = 1 − a²/(a² + b²) = b²/(a² + b²), and θ acute makes cos θ positive. The trap is inverting the ratio, so the answer is b/√(a² + b²).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

88. If sin θ = 3/5, then cosec θ − cot θ equals:
(a) 3/5 (b) 1/3 (c) 5/3 (d) 4/3
Answer: (b)
Solution: cosec θ = 5/3 and, with cos θ = 4/5, cot θ = 4/3; the difference is 1/3. The trap is adding instead of subtracting to get 3, so the answer is 1/3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

89. From the top of a lighthouse, the angle of depression of a ship sailing towards it changes from 30° to 60° in 2 minutes. The time the ship will take to reach the lighthouse from the second position is:
(a) 2 minutes (b) 1 minute (c) 3 minutes (d) 4 minutes
Answer: (b)
Solution: Distances from the foot are h√3 and h/√3, so the ship covered 2h/√3 in 2 minutes, i.e. h/√3 per minute. The remaining distance is exactly h/√3; the trap is assuming equal angles mean equal times, so the answer is 1 minute.
Source: PYQ-style

90. If sin θ + cos θ = √2 sin(90° − θ), then tan θ equals:
(a) √2 + 1 (b) √2 − 1 (c) 1 (d) 2 − √2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Since sin(90° − θ) = cos θ, the equation gives sin θ = (√2 − 1) cos θ. The trap is replacing sin(90° − θ) with sin θ, so the answer is √2 − 1.
Source: PYQ-style

91. The expression 1/(sec θ − tan θ) − 1/cos θ equals:
(a) tan θ (b) 1 (c) sec θ (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rationalising, 1/(sec θ − tan θ) = sec θ + tan θ, and 1/cos θ = sec θ, so the difference is tan θ. The trap is thinking the two terms cancel to 0, so the answer is tan θ.
Source: PYQ-style

92. If sin θ = √3/2 and cos θ = 1/2, then θ is:
(a) 60° (b) 45° (c) 30° (d) 90°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is swapping the standard values and picking 30°, where sin is 1/2. Both sin 60° = √3/2 and cos 60° = 1/2 match, so the answer is 60°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

93. The expression √[(1 + sin θ)/(1 − sin θ)] is equal to:
(a) sec θ − tan θ (b) cosec θ + cot θ (c) cosec θ − cot θ (d) sec θ + tan θ
Answer: (d)
Solution: Multiplying numerator and denominator by (1 + sin θ) gives (1 + sin θ)²/cos²θ, whose square root is (1 + sin θ)/cos θ = sec θ + tan θ. The trap is choosing the minus form, so the answer is sec θ + tan θ.
Source: PYQ-style

94. The angle of elevation of the sun when the shadow of a pole is √3 times its height is:
(a) 15° (b) 30° (c) 45° (d) 60°
Answer: (b)
Solution: tan θ = height/shadow = 1/√3, which corresponds to 30°. The trap is inverting the ratio and picking 60°, so the answer is 30°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

95. If sec θ + tan θ = 3, then sin θ equals:
(a) 5/13 (b) 3/5 (c) 4/5 (d) 12/13
Answer: (c)
Solution: Using sec θ − tan θ = 1/3 and solving gives sec θ = 5/3 and tan θ = 4/3, hence cos θ = 3/5 and sin θ = tan θ · cos θ = 4/5. The trap is reporting cos θ instead of sin θ, so the answer is 4/5.
Source: PYQ-style

96. The value of sin²1° + sin²2° + … + sin²89° is:
(a) 44 (b) 45 (c) 44.5 (d) 45.5
Answer: (c)
Solution: Pairing sin²θ with sin²(90° − θ) = cos²θ gives 44 pairs each summing to 1, plus the middle term sin²45° = 1/2. The trap is counting 45 pairs, so the answer is 44.5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

97. If 5 tan θ = 4, then (5 sin θ − 3 cos θ)/(5 sin θ + 2 cos θ) equals:
(a) 1/2 (b) 1/3 (c) 1/4 (d) 1/6
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dividing numerator and denominator by cos θ gives (5 tan θ − 3)/(5 tan θ + 2) = (4 − 3)/(4 + 2). The trap is adding in the numerator too, so the answer is 1/6.
Source: PYQ-style

98. The shadow of a tower is 45 m longer when the sun's altitude is 30° than when it is 60°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 45√3 m (b) 45(√3 − 1) m (c) 45(√3 + 1) m (d) 45√3/2 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: Shadows are h√3 and h/√3, so h(√3 − 1/√3) = 45, i.e. 2h/√3 = 45. The trap is using h√3 alone as the difference, so the answer is 45√3/2 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

99. The value of 2 tan 30°/(1 + tan²30°) is:
(a) tan 60° (b) cos 60° (c) sin 60° (d) sin 30°
Answer: (c)
Solution: With tan 30° = 1/√3, the expression is (2/√3)/(4/3) = √3/2, which equals sin 60°. The trap is quoting the formula as sin 30° instead of sin 2θ with θ = 30°, so the answer is sin 60°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

100. If sin θ = m/n (n > m > 0), then (tan θ + sec θ)/(tan θ − sec θ) equals:
(a) (n + m)/(n − m) (b) (n − m)/(n + m) (c) (n + m)/(m − n) (d) (m − n)/(n + m)
Answer: (c)
Solution: tan θ + sec θ = (m + n)/√(n² − m²) and tan θ − sec θ = (m − n)/√(n² − m²), so the ratio is (n + m)/(m − n). The trap is ignoring that tan θ − sec θ is negative and choosing the positive-looking form, so the answer is (n + m)/(m − n).
Source: PYQ-style
