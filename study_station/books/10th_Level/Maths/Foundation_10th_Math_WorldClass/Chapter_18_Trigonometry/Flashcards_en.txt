🃏 Flashcards: Trigonometry (English)

Card 1
Q: What bizarre sentence instantly unlocks the definitions of sin, cos, and tan?
A: "Silly Old Harry Caught A Herring Trawling Off Alaska."
Sin = Opposite/Hypotenuse, Cos = Adjacent/Hypotenuse, Tan = Opposite/Adjacent.

Card 2
Q: In the mnemonic "Silly Old Harry Caught A Herring Trawling Off Alaska," what part gives tan's formula?
A: "Trawling Off Alaska" → Tan = Opposite/Adjacent.

Card 3
Q: Which trig ratio connects the opposite side to the hypotenuse?
A: Sine (sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse).

Card 4
Q: If you're standing 20 m from a tree and looking up at a 45° angle, which ratio lets you find the tree's height with no extra steps?
A: Tangent (tan 45° = height / 20, so height = 20 m).

Card 5
Q: What's the finger-counting trick that gives sin values of 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90° without memorising a table?
A: For angles 0°,30°,45°,60°,90° write numbers 0,1,2,3,4 below them; sin θ = √(that number)/2. So sin 30° = √1/2 = 1/2, etc.

Card 6
Q: sin 30° = ?
A: 1/2

Card 7
Q: tan 45° = ?
A: 1

Card 8
Q: cos 60° = ?
A: 1/2 (it's the reverse of sin 30°).

Card 9
Q: Why does sin²θ + cos²θ always equal exactly 1?
A: It's Pythagoras' theorem in disguise: on a unit circle, (cos θ)² + (sin θ)² = radius² = 1.

Card 10
Q: If a magic pond has radius 1 and a frog sits exactly at the centre, what do the fly's east and north distances (x,y) represent?
A: x = cos θ, y = sin θ, where θ is the angle from east.

Card 11
Q: If sin θ = 5/13, what is cos θ? (No triangle drawing!)
A: cos θ = √(1 − 25/169) = √(144/169) = 12/13.

Card 12
Q: What's the lightning-fast way to find tan θ when you already know sin θ and cos θ?
A: tan θ = sin θ / cos θ. Example: if sin θ=3/5 and cos θ=4/5, tan θ=3/4.

Card 13
Q: A pilot looks down from the cockpit to a runway. The angle between the horizontal and the line of sight is called the ___.
A: Angle of depression.

Card 14
Q: How are the angle of elevation from a boat to a cliff top and the angle of depression from the cliff top to the boat related?
A: They are equal (alternate interior angles formed by a horizontal line).

Card 15
Q: cos 90° = ?
A: 0

Card 16
Q: Why is cos 30° exactly equal to sin 60°?
A: Because cos θ = sin(90° − θ). For θ=30°, 90°−30°=60°.

Card 17
Q: In a height-and-distance problem, what is the absolute first step before using any ratio?
A: Draw the right triangle and label the known angle and sides (opposite, adjacent, hypotenuse).

Card 18
Q: Is sin²30° + sin²60° equal to 1? Why?
A: Yes, because sin 60° = cos 30°, so the expression becomes sin²30° + cos²30° = 1 (using the Pythagorean identity).
