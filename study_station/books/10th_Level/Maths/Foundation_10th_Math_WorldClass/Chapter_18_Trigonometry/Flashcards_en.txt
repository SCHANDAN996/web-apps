🃏 Flashcards: Trigonometry (English)

Card 1
Front: What sentence instantly unlocks the definitions of sin, cos, and tan?
Back: "Silly Old Harry Caught A Herring Trawling Off Alaska."
Sin = Opposite/Hypotenuse, Cos = Adjacent/Hypotenuse, Tan = Opposite/Adjacent.

Card 2
Front: In the mnemonic "Silly Old Harry Caught A Herring Trawling Off Alaska," what part gives tan's formula?
Back: "Trawling Off Alaska" → Tan = Opposite/Adjacent.

Card 3
Front: Which trig ratio connects the opposite side to the hypotenuse?
Back: Sine (sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse).

Card 4
Front: If you're standing 20 m from a tree and looking up at a 45° angle, which ratio lets you find the tree's height with no extra steps?
Back: Tangent (tan 45° = height / 20, so height = 20 m).

Card 5
Front: What's the finger-counting trick that gives sin values of 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90° without memorising a table?
Back: For angles 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90° write numbers 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 below them; sin θ = √(that number)/2. So sin 30° = √1/2 = 1/2, sin 45° = √2/2, etc.

Card 6
Front: sin 30° = ?
Back: 1/2

Card 7
Front: tan 45° = ?
Back: 1

Card 8
Front: cos 60° = ?
Back: 1/2 (it's the reverse of sin 30°).

Card 9
Front: Why does sin²θ + cos²θ always equal exactly 1?
Back: It's Pythagoras' theorem in disguise: on a unit circle, (cos θ)² + (sin θ)² = radius² = 1.

Card 10
Front: If a pond has radius 1 and a frog sits exactly at the centre, what do a fly's east and north distances (x, y) on the edge represent?
Back: x = cos θ, y = sin θ, where θ is the angle measured from east.

Card 11
Front: If sin θ = 5/13 and θ is an acute angle, what is cos θ? (No triangle drawing!)
Back: cos θ = √(1 − 25/169) = √(144/169) = 12/13. (Without the acute-angle condition, cos θ = ±12/13; in exams, θ is acute unless stated otherwise.)

Card 12
Front: What's the lightning-fast way to find tan θ when you already know sin θ and cos θ?
Back: tan θ = sin θ / cos θ. Example: if sin θ = 3/5 and cos θ = 4/5, tan θ = 3/4.

Card 13
Front: A pilot looks down from the cockpit to a runway. The angle between the horizontal and the line of sight is called the ___.
Back: Angle of depression.

Card 14
Front: How are the angle of elevation from a boat to a cliff top and the angle of depression from the cliff top to the boat related?
Back: They are equal (alternate interior angles formed by parallel horizontal lines).

Card 15
Front: cos 90° = ?
Back: 0

Card 16
Front: Why is cos 30° exactly equal to sin 60°?
Back: Because cos θ = sin(90° − θ). For θ = 30°, 90° − 30° = 60°.

Card 17
Front: In a height-and-distance problem, what is the absolute first step before using any ratio?
Back: Draw the right triangle and label the known angle and sides (opposite, adjacent, hypotenuse).

Card 18
Front: Is sin²30° + sin²60° equal to 1? Why?
Back: Yes, because sin 60° = cos 30°, so the expression becomes sin²30° + cos²30° = 1 (using the Pythagorean identity).
