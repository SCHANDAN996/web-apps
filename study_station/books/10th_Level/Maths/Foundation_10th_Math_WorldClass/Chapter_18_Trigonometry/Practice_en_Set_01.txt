Set 1: Trigonometry (Foundation) – Questions 1–20 Easy, 21–25 Medium

1. What is the value of sin 30°?
(a) 0 (b) √3/2 (c) 1/2 (d) 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: From the standard angle table, sin 30° = 1/2. The tempting option √3/2 is the value of sin 60°, not sin 30°, so the answer is 1/2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

2. In a right triangle, if sin θ = 3/5, then cos θ equals:
(a) 4/3 (b) 5/4 (c) 3/4 (d) 4/5
Answer: (d)
Solution: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 gives cos²θ = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25, so cos θ = 4/5 since θ is acute. The option 3/4 is tan θ, not cos θ, so the answer is 4/5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

3. tan 45° = ?
(a) 0 (b) 1/√3 (c) 1 (d) √3
Answer: (c)
Solution: tan 45° = sin 45° / cos 45° = (√2/2)/(√2/2) = 1. The option 1/√3 is tan 30°, a common mix-up, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

4. cos 0° equals:
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 1/2 (d) √3/2
Answer: (b)
Solution: At 0° the unit-circle point is (1, 0), so cos 0° = 1. The option 0 is sin 0°, not cos 0°, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

5. If sin θ = cos θ for an acute angle θ, then θ = ?
(a) 0° (b) 30° (c) 45° (d) 60°
Answer: (c)
Solution: sin θ = cos θ implies tan θ = 1, which holds at θ = 45°. The options 30° and 60° give unequal sine and cosine values, so the answer is 45°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

6. The value of sin² 60° + cos² 60° is:
(a) 1 (b) 1/2 (c) 2 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: The identity sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 holds for every angle, including 60°. The option 1/2 comes from wrongly adding sin 60° + cos 60° instead of the squares, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

7. In a right triangle, the side opposite to angle θ is 5 units and the hypotenuse is 13 units. Then sin θ =
(a) 13/5 (b) 12/13 (c) 5/12 (d) 5/13
Answer: (d)
Solution: sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse = 5/13. The option 5/12 uses the adjacent side (12) instead of the hypotenuse, so the answer is 5/13.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

8. cos 90° =
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 1/2 (d) √3/2
Answer: (a)
Solution: At 90° the unit-circle point is (0, 1), so cos 90° = 0. The option 1 is sin 90°, a frequent confusion, so the answer is 0.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

9. If tan θ = 1 for an acute angle θ, then θ =
(a) 30° (b) 60° (c) 45° (d) 90°
Answer: (c)
Solution: tan 45° = 1 from the standard table. The option 30° gives tan θ = 1/√3, not 1, so the answer is 45°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

10. In a right triangle, sin θ is the ratio of:
(a) Opposite/Hypotenuse (b) Adjacent/Hypotenuse (c) Opposite/Adjacent (d) Adjacent/Opposite
Answer: (a)
Solution: By definition, sin θ = opposite side / hypotenuse. The option Adjacent/Hypotenuse is cos θ, so the answer is Opposite/Hypotenuse.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

11. sin (90° − 60°) equals:
(a) 1/2 (b) √3/2 (c) 1 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: sin (90° − 60°) = sin 30° = 1/2; equivalently, sin (90° − θ) = cos θ gives cos 60° = 1/2. The option √3/2 is sin 60°, so the answer is 1/2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

12. If cos θ = 4/5 for an acute angle θ, then sin θ =
(a) 5/4 (b) 3/5 (c) 5/3 (d) 4/3
Answer: (b)
Solution: sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ = 1 − 16/25 = 9/25, so sin θ = 3/5 since θ is acute. The option 5/4 is sec θ, not sin θ, so the answer is 3/5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

13. The reciprocal of cos θ is:
(a) sec θ (b) tan θ (c) cot θ (d) sin θ
Answer: (a)
Solution: sec θ = 1 / cos θ by definition. The option cot θ is cos θ / sin θ, not the reciprocal, so the answer is sec θ.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

14. From a point on the ground 20 m away from the foot of a tower, the angle of elevation of the top is 45°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 20 m (b) 10 m (c) 20√3 m (d) 40 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: tan 45° = height / 20 gives height = 20 × 1 = 20 m. The option 20√3 m results from wrongly using tan 60°, so the answer is 20 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

15. cot 30° =
(a) 1/√3 (b) √3 (c) 1 (d) 0
Answer: (b)
Solution: cot 30° = 1 / tan 30° = 1/(1/√3) = √3. The option 1/√3 is tan 30°, the reciprocal trap, so the answer is √3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

16. cos 60° + sin 30° =
(a) 1 (b) 1/2 (c) 0 (d) √3
Answer: (a)
Solution: cos 60° = 1/2 and sin 30° = 1/2, so the sum is 1. The option 1/2 is just one of the terms, not the sum, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

17. The identity 1 + tan²θ equals:
(a) cot²θ (b) sec²θ (c) cosec²θ (d) sin²θ
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dividing sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 by cos²θ gives 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ. The option cosec²θ belongs to the identity 1 + cot²θ, so the answer is sec²θ.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

18. Which trigonometric ratio directly gives the adjacent side when the hypotenuse and the angle are known?
(a) sin (b) cos (c) cot (d) tan
Answer: (b)
Solution: cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse, so adjacent = hypotenuse × cos θ. The option sin relates the opposite side to the hypotenuse, so the answer is cos.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

19. The maximum possible value of sin θ is:
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) √2 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: For any angle, −1 ≤ sin θ ≤ 1, so the maximum is 1. Options like √2 or 2 are impossible since the hypotenuse is always the longest side, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

20. If sin θ = 3/5 for an acute angle θ, then tan θ =
(a) 3/5 (b) 4/3 (c) 5/3 (d) 3/4
Answer: (d)
Solution: cos θ = √(1 − 9/25) = 4/5, so tan θ = (3/5)/(4/5) = 3/4. The option 4/3 is cot θ, the inverted ratio, so the answer is 3/4.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

21. If sin θ + sin²θ = 1, then the value of cos²θ + cos⁴θ is:
(a) 0 (b) 1/2 (c) 2 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: From sin θ + sin²θ = 1 we get sin θ = 1 − sin²θ = cos²θ. Then cos²θ + cos⁴θ = sin θ + sin²θ = 1 by the given equation. The option 2 comes from wrongly doubling the expression, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

22. From the top of a 60 m high building, the angle of depression of a car on the ground is 30°. The distance of the car from the base of the building is:
(a) 60 m (b) 60/√3 m (c) 60√3 m (d) 120 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The angle of depression equals the angle of elevation from the car, so tan 30° = 60 / distance, giving distance = 60√3 m. The option 60/√3 m comes from inverting the ratio, so the answer is 60√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

23. If 5 tan θ = 4, then the value of (5 sin θ − 4 cos θ) / (5 sin θ + 4 cos θ) is:
(a) 1/4 (b) 0 (c) 1 (d) 8/9
Answer: (b)
Solution: 5 tan θ = 4 gives 5 sin θ = 4 cos θ, so the numerator 5 sin θ − 4 cos θ = 0 while the denominator is positive. The option 1 comes from assuming numerator equals denominator, so the answer is 0.
Source: PYQ-style

24. If sec θ + tan θ = 3, then sec θ − tan θ equals:
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 9 (d) 1/3
Answer: (d)
Solution: Since sec²θ − tan²θ = 1, we have (sec θ + tan θ)(sec θ − tan θ) = 1, so 3 × (sec θ − tan θ) = 1. The option 3 wrongly repeats the given sum, so the answer is 1/3.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A 20 m high pole casts a shadow of length 20√3 m. The angle of elevation of the sun is:
(a) 60° (b) 45° (c) 30° (d) 90°
Answer: (c)
Solution: tan θ = height / shadow = 20 / (20√3) = 1/√3, so θ = 30°. The option 60° results from swapping height and shadow in the ratio, so the answer is 30°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics
