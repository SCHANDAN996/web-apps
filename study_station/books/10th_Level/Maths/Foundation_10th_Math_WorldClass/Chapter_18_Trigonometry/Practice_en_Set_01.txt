Q1. What is the value of sin 30°?
(a) 0
(b) 1/2
(c) √3/2
(d) 1

Answer: (b) 1/2

Solution:
Using the standard angle table, sin 30° = 1/2.

Source: NCERT Class 10 Maths, Ex 8.2

Q2. In a right triangle, if sin θ = 3/5, then cos θ equals:
(a) 4/5
(b) 5/4
(c) 3/4
(d) 4/3

Answer: (a) 4/5

Solution:
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 ⇒ (3/5)² + cos²θ = 1 ⇒ 9/25 + cos²θ = 1 ⇒ cos²θ = 16/25 ⇒ cos θ = 4/5 (since θ is acute, cos is positive).

Source: NCERT Class 10 Maths, Ex 8.1

Q3. tan 45° = ?
(a) 0
(b) 1
(c) √3
(d) 1/√3

Answer: (b) 1

Solution:
tan 45° = sin 45° / cos 45° = (√2/2)/(√2/2) = 1.

Source: NCERT Class 10 Maths, Ex 8.2

Q12. If cos θ = 4/5, then sin θ =
(a) 3/5
(b) 5/4
(c) 5/3
(d) 4/3

Answer: (a) 3/5

Solution:

Q20. If sin θ = 3/5, then tan θ =

(b) 4/3
(c) 5/3
(d) 3/5

Answer: (a) 3/4

Solution:

Medium Questions (21–25)

Q21. If sin θ + sin²θ = 1, then the value of cos²θ + cos⁴θ is:
(a) 0
(b) 1
(c) 2
(d) –1

Answer: (b) 1

Solution:
Given sin θ + sin²θ = 1 ⇒ sin θ = 1 – sin²θ = cos²θ.
So, cos²θ = sin θ.
Then cos²θ + cos⁴θ = sin θ + sin²θ = 1 (using the given equation).

Source: SSC CGL (adapted)

Q22. From the top of a 60 m high building, the angle of depression of a car on the ground is 30°. The distance of the car from the base of the building is:
(a) 60 m
(b) 60√3 m
(c) 60/√3 m
(d) 120 m

Answer: (b) 60√3 m

Solution:
Angle of depression = angle of elevation from car = 30°.
tan 30° = opposite/adjacent = 60 / distance ⇒ 1/√3 = 60/d ⇒ d = 60√3 m.

Source: NCERT Class 10 Maths, Ex 9.1

Q23. If 5 tan θ = 4, then the value of (5 sin θ – 4 cos θ) / (5 sin θ + 4 cos θ) is:
(a) 0
(b) 1/4
(c) 1
(d) 8/9

Answer: (a) 0

Solution:
tan θ = 4/5 ⇒ sin θ / cos θ = 4/5 ⇒ 5 sin θ = 4 cos θ.
Substitute in numerator: 5 sin θ – 4 cos θ = 0.
Denominator is non-zero, so overall value = 0.

Source: Competitive exam (typical)

Q24. If sec θ + tan θ = 3, then sec θ – tan θ equals:
(a) 1
(b) 1/3
(c) 3
(d) 9

Answer: (b) 1/3

Solution:
We know sec²θ – tan²θ = 1 ⇒ (sec θ + tan θ)(sec θ – tan θ) = 1.
Given sec θ + tan θ = 3, so 3 × (sec θ – tan θ) = 1 ⇒ sec θ – tan θ = 1/3.

Source: SSC CGL (common)

Q25. A 20 m high pole casts a shadow of length 20√3 m. The angle of elevation of the sun is:
(a) 30°
(b) 45°
(c) 60°
(d) 90°

Answer: (a) 30°

Solution:
tan θ = height / shadow = 20 / (20√3) = 1/√3 ⇒ θ = 30°.

Source: NCERT Class 10 Maths, Ex 9.1
