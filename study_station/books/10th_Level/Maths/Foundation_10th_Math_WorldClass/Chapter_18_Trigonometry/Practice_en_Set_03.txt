51. If sec θ + tan θ = 2, then sin θ equals:
(a) 4/5 (b) 3/5 (c) 5/13 (d) 12/13
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 4/5, which is cos θ, not sin θ. Since (sec θ − tan θ)(sec θ + tan θ) = 1, we get sec θ − tan θ = 1/2. Adding gives sec θ = 5/4 so cos θ = 4/5, and subtracting gives tan θ = 3/4, hence sin θ = tan θ · cos θ = (3/4)(4/5) = 3/5. So the answer is 3/5.
Source: PYQ-style

52. If tan θ + cot θ = 2, then sin θ cos θ is:
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 1/2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 1, confusing sin θ cos θ with sin²θ + cos²θ. Now tan θ + cot θ = (sin²θ + cos²θ)/(sin θ cos θ) = 1/(sin θ cos θ) = 2, so sin θ cos θ = 1/2. So the answer is 1/2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

53. In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is 10 cm and one acute angle is 30°. The length of the side opposite to this angle is:
(a) 5√3 cm (b) 10√3 cm (c) 5 cm (d) 10/√3 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 5√3 cm, which is the side adjacent to 30°, not opposite. Since sin 30° = opposite/hypotenuse = 1/2, the opposite side = 10 × 1/2 = 5 cm. So the answer is 5 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

54. The value of sin²5° + sin²10° + sin²15° + … + sin²85° is:
(a) 8.5 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 9.5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is counting 9 pairs and answering 9, but there are 17 terms, not 18. Pair sin²5° with sin²85° = cos²5° to get 1; this gives 8 pairs summing to 8, plus the middle term sin²45° = 1/2. Total = 8.5. So the answer is 8.5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

55. If cos(40° + x) = sin 30° and 0° < x < 50°, then x is:
(a) 10° (b) 30° (c) 20° (d) 40°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is writing cos(40° + x) = cos 30° and getting x = −10°, forgetting sin 30° = cos 60°. Since sin 30° = 1/2 = cos 60°, and 40° + x is acute, 40° + x = 60°, giving x = 20°. So the answer is 20°.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

56. From the top of a cliff 50 m high, the angle of depression of a boat is 30°. The distance of the boat from the base of the cliff is:
(a) 25√3 m (b) 50/√3 m (c) 50√3 m (d) 100 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using tan 30° = distance/50, which inverts the ratio and gives 50/√3. The angle of depression equals the angle of elevation from the boat, so tan 30° = 50/d, i.e. 1/√3 = 50/d, giving d = 50√3 m. So the answer is 50√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

57. If sin A = 8/17 and A is acute, then sec A + tan A equals:
(a) 5/3 (b) 17/15 (c) 15/8 (d) 3/5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking cos A = 17/15 by mixing up sec and cos. Here cos A = √(1 − 64/289) = 15/17, so sec A = 17/15 and tan A = 8/15. Then sec A + tan A = 25/15 = 5/3. So the answer is 5/3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

58. If sec θ − tan θ = 1/4, then sec θ + tan θ is:
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 1/2 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is thinking the value stays 1/4 or becomes 1, forgetting the product identity. Since (sec θ − tan θ)(sec θ + tan θ) = sec²θ − tan²θ = 1, we get (1/4)(sec θ + tan θ) = 1, so sec θ + tan θ = 4. So the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

59. If sin θ − cos θ = 0 and 0° ≤ θ ≤ 90°, then sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ is:
(a) 1/4 (b) 1/2 (c) 3/4 (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 1 by confusing sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ with sin²θ + cos²θ. Here sin θ = cos θ gives θ = 45°, so each of sin 45° and cos 45° is 1/√2, and sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ = 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2. So the answer is 1/2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

60. A tree breaks in a storm and the broken part bends so that its top touches the ground, making an angle of 30° with the ground. The foot of the tree is 8 m from the point where the top touches the ground. The original height of the tree was:
(a) 8/√3 m (b) 8√3 m (c) 16 m (d) 16/√3 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is reporting only the standing stump 8/√3 m and forgetting the broken part. The stump h satisfies tan 30° = h/8, so h = 8/√3; the broken part L satisfies cos 30° = 8/L, so L = 16/√3. Total height = 8/√3 + 16/√3 = 24/√3 = 8√3 m. So the answer is 8√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

61. If sin θ + sin²θ = 1, then cos²θ + cos⁴θ equals:
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) 2 (d) −1
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is trying to solve for θ explicitly, which is unnecessary. From the given, sin θ = 1 − sin²θ = cos²θ, so cos²θ + cos⁴θ = sin θ + sin²θ = 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

62. The value of tan 1° · tan 2° · tan 3° … tan 89° is:
(a) 1 (b) √3 (c) 1/2 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 0, thinking one factor must be zero — but tan 0° is not in the product. Pair tan θ with tan(90° − θ) = cot θ; each pair multiplies to 1, and the middle term tan 45° = 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

63. A tower is 100√3 m tall. The angle of elevation of its top from a point on the ground is 60°. The distance of that point from the base of the tower is:
(a) 100 m (b) 100√3 m (c) 300 m (d) 100/√3 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is multiplying by √3 instead of dividing, giving 300 m. Since tan 60° = height/distance, √3 = 100√3/d, so d = 100 m. So the answer is 100 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

64. If 7 sin²θ + 3 cos²θ = 4 and θ is acute, then tan θ is:
(a) 0 (b) √3 (c) 1 (d) 1/√3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is getting sin²θ = 1/4 and then writing tan θ = 1/2 directly. Writing 3 cos²θ = 3(1 − sin²θ) gives 4 sin²θ = 1, so sin θ = 1/2 and θ = 30°. Hence tan θ = tan 30° = 1/√3. So the answer is 1/√3.
Source: PYQ-style

65. The expression cos⁴θ − sin⁴θ is equal to:
(a) 1 (b) cos²θ − sin²θ (c) 2 cos²θ + 1 (d) sin²θ − cos²θ
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 1 by treating it like sin²θ + cos²θ. Factor as a difference of squares: cos⁴θ − sin⁴θ = (cos²θ − sin²θ)(cos²θ + sin²θ) = cos²θ − sin²θ, since the second bracket is 1. So the answer is cos²θ − sin²θ.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

66. If sin θ = a/b (with b > a > 0) and θ is acute, then cos θ is:
(a) √(a² + b²)/b (b) a/√(b² − a²) (c) √(b² − a²)/b (d) b/√(a² + b²)
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using √(a² + b²), which applies the Pythagoras formula with the wrong sign. Since cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ = 1 − a²/b² = (b² − a²)/b², for acute θ we get cos θ = √(b² − a²)/b. So the answer is √(b² − a²)/b.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

67. (cosec θ − cot θ)² is equal to:
(a) (1 − cos θ)/(1 + cos θ) (b) (1 − sin θ)/(1 + sin θ) (c) (1 + sin θ)/(1 − sin θ) (d) (1 + cos θ)/(1 − cos θ)
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is flipping the fraction to (1 + cos θ)/(1 − cos θ). Now cosec θ − cot θ = (1 − cos θ)/sin θ; squaring gives (1 − cos θ)²/sin²θ = (1 − cos θ)²/[(1 − cos θ)(1 + cos θ)] = (1 − cos θ)/(1 + cos θ). So the answer is (1 − cos θ)/(1 + cos θ).
Source: PYQ-style

68. If cot θ = 1/√3, then (1 − cos²θ)/(1 + cos²θ) equals:
(a) 1/5 (b) 3/5 (c) 1/3 (d) 2/3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is taking θ = 30° because cot 30° = √3 looks similar; actually cot θ = 1/√3 means tan θ = √3, so θ = 60°. Then cos²60° = 1/4, and the ratio is (1 − 1/4)/(1 + 1/4) = (3/4)/(5/4) = 3/5. So the answer is 3/5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

69. From a point on the ground, the angles of elevation of the bottom and the top of a transmission tower fixed on a 20 m high building are 45° and 60° respectively. The height of the tower is:
(a) 20(√3 + 1) m (b) 20(√3 − 1) m (c) 20√3 m (d) 20/√3 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 20√3 m, which is the total height of building plus tower, not the tower alone. From tan 45° = 20/d, the distance d = 20 m; from tan 60° = (20 + h)/20, we get 20 + h = 20√3, so h = 20(√3 − 1) m. So the answer is 20(√3 − 1) m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

70. If (sin θ + cos θ)/(sin θ − cos θ) = 3, then tan θ is:
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering 3, the number on the right side, without solving. Cross-multiplying: sin θ + cos θ = 3 sin θ − 3 cos θ, so 4 cos θ = 2 sin θ, giving sin θ/cos θ = 2. So the answer is 2.

71. sin(90° − θ) · sec θ equals:
(a) sin θ (b) cos θ (c) tan θ (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is writing sin(90° − θ) = sin θ, which would wrongly give tan θ. Actually sin(90° − θ) = cos θ and sec θ = 1/cos θ, so the product is cos θ · (1/cos θ) = 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

72. A man walks 50 m towards the foot of a tower and finds that the angle of elevation of its top changes from 30° to 60°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 50 m (b) 25 m (c) 50√3 m (d) 25√3 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 50√3 m by forgetting the man walked only part of the original distance. Let height be h and initial distance d: tan 30° = h/d gives d = h√3, and tan 60° = h/(d − 50) gives √3(h√3 − 50) = h, so 3h − 50√3 = h and h = 25√3 m. So the answer is 25√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

73. If cos θ = 3/5 and θ is acute, then sin θ + tan θ equals:
(a) 15/32 (b) 4/3 (c) 17/15 (d) 32/15
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is inverting the correct sum and picking 15/32. Here sin θ = 4/5 and tan θ = 4/3, so sin θ + tan θ = 4/5 + 4/3 = (12 + 20)/15 = 32/15. So the answer is 32/15.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

74. (1 + tan²θ)/(1 + cot²θ) is equal to:
(a) sin²θ (b) cos²θ (c) tan²θ (d) cot²θ
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering cot²θ by inverting the ratio. Using 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ and 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ, the expression becomes sec²θ/cosec²θ = sin²θ/cos²θ = tan²θ. So the answer is tan²θ.
Source: PYQ-style

75. If sin A = 3/5 and A is acute, then the value of 3 cot A + 4 tan A is:
(a) 7 (b) 5 (c) 9 (d) 12
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is swapping tan and cot, which gives 3·(3/4) + 4·(4/3), a non-integer. Here cos A = 4/5, so cot A = 4/3 and tan A = 3/4; then 3 cot A + 4 tan A = 3·(4/3) + 4·(3/4) = 4 + 3 = 7. So the answer is 7.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics
