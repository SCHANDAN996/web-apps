Hard Questions (126–150)

Question 126. If cosec θ − cot θ = √2, then cosec θ + cot θ equals:
(a) 1/√2
(b) √2
(c) 2√2
(d) 1/(2√2)
Answer: (a)
Solution: From the identity cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1, we get (cosec θ − cot θ)(cosec θ + cot θ) = 1. So √2 × (cosec θ + cot θ) = 1, giving cosec θ + cot θ = 1/√2. Option (b) is the trap — it is the given value, not the required one.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 127. If sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ = 1/2, then θ (0° < θ < 90°) equals:
(a) 30°
(b) 45°
(c) 60°
(d) 75°
Answer: (b)
Solution: sin⁴θ + cos⁴θ = (sin²θ + cos²θ)² − 2sin²θcos²θ = 1 − ½sin²2θ. Given 1 − ½sin²2θ = ½, so sin²2θ = 1. For acute θ, 2θ = 90°, hence θ = 45°.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 128. The value of sin²5° + sin²10° + sin²15° + ⋯ + sin²90° is:
(a) 8.5
(b) 9
(c) 9.5
(d) 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: There are 18 terms from 5° to 90°. Using the pairing rule sin²θ + sin²(90° − θ) = 1, the 8 pairs from 5° to 40° sum to 8. The remaining terms are sin²45° = 0.5 and sin²90° = 1. Total = 8 + 0.5 + 1 = 9.5.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 129. If sin θ + cos θ = √2, then sin⁶θ + cos⁶θ equals:
(a) 1/4
(b) 1/2
(c) 3/4
(d) 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: Squaring: 1 + 2sinθcosθ = 2, so sinθcosθ = 1/2. Now sin⁶θ + cos⁶θ = (sin²θ + cos²θ)³ − 3sin²θcos²θ(sin²θ + cos²θ) = 1 − 3(1/4) = 1/4.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 130. If cos θ + sec θ = 5/2, then cos²θ + sec²θ equals:
(a) 9/4
(b) 17/4
(c) 25/4
(d) 13/4
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let x = cos θ, so x + 1/x = 5/2. Squaring: x² + 1/x² + 2 = 25/4, hence x² + 1/x² = 25/4 − 2 = 17/4. Option (c) is the trap — forgetting to subtract 2 after squaring.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 131. From the top of a 60 m high lighthouse, the angles of depression of two ships on the same side are 30° and 45°. The distance between the ships is:
(a) 60(√3 + 1) m
(b) 60(√3 − 1) m
(c) 60√3 m
(d) (60√3 − 60)/√3 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: Distance of the nearer ship (45°): d₁ = 60 cot 45° = 60 m. Distance of the farther ship (30°): d₂ = 60 cot 30° = 60√3 m. Distance between ships = d₂ − d₁ = 60√3 − 60 = 60(√3 − 1) m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Exercise 9.1

Question 132. If sin θ + sin²θ = 1, then the value of cos¹²θ + 3cos¹⁰θ + 3cos⁸θ + cos⁶θ is:
(a) 0
(b) 1
(c) 2
(d) 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Given sin θ = 1 − sin²θ = cos²θ. The expression is the binomial expansion of (cos⁴θ + cos²θ)³. Since cos²θ = sin θ and cos⁴θ = sin²θ, the expression = (sin²θ + sin θ)³ = 1³ = 1.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 133. The value of cos²1° + cos²2° + ⋯ + cos²89° is:
(a) 44
(b) 44.5
(c) 45
(d) 45.5
Answer: (b)
Solution: Since cos²θ + cos²(90° − θ) = cos²θ + sin²θ = 1, the 44 pairs from 1° to 44° sum to 44. The middle term is cos²45° = 0.5. Total = 44.5. Option (a) is the trap — forgetting the middle term.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 134. If sec θ + tan θ = 3, then sin θ + cos θ equals:
(a) 7/5
(b) 5/7
(c) 3/5
(d) 4/5
Answer: (a)
Solution: sec θ − tan θ = 1/3. Adding: 2sec θ = 3 + 1/3 = 10/3, so sec θ = 5/3 and cos θ = 3/5. Subtracting: 2tan θ = 3 − 1/3 = 8/3, so tan θ = 4/3 and sin θ = tan θ · cos θ = (4/3)(3/5) = 4/5. Hence sin θ + cos θ = 4/5 + 3/5 = 7/5.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 135. If tan θ + cot θ = a, then tan³θ + cot³θ equals:
(a) a³ − 3a
(b) a³ + 3a
(c) a³
(d) a³ − 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let x = tan θ, so cot θ = 1/x and x + 1/x = a. Cubing: x³ + 1/x³ + 3(x + 1/x) = a³, hence x³ + 1/x³ = a³ − 3a. Therefore tan³θ + cot³θ = a³ − 3a.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 136. If x sin³θ + y cos³θ = sin θ cos θ and x sin θ − y cos θ = 0, then x² + y² equals:
(a) 0
(b) 1
(c) 2
(d) sin²θ + cos²θ
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let x sin θ = y cos θ = k. Then x = k/sin θ and y = k/cos θ. Substituting in the first equation: k sin²θ + k cos²θ = k = sin θ cos θ. So x = cos θ and y = sin θ, giving x² + y² = cos²θ + sin²θ = 1.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 137. A man standing on top of a tower watches a car approaching at uniform speed. The angle of depression of the car changes from 30° to 45° in 12 minutes. In how many more minutes will the car reach the tower?
(a) 6(√3 + 1) minutes
(b) 6(√3 − 1) minutes
(c) 12 minutes
(d) 12(√3 − 1) minutes
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let the tower height be h. Initial distance d₁ = h cot 30° = h√3; after 12 minutes, d₂ = h cot 45° = h. Distance covered in 12 minutes = h(√3 − 1), so speed = h(√3 − 1)/12. Remaining distance = h. Remaining time = h ÷ [h(√3 − 1)/12] = 12/(√3 − 1) = 6(√3 + 1) minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 138. If sin θ − cos θ = 0, then the value of sin⁶θ + cos⁶θ is:
(a) 1/4
(b) 1/2
(c) 3/4
(d) 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: sin θ = cos θ implies θ = 45°, so sin 45° = cos 45° = 1/√2. Then sin⁶θ = cos⁶θ = (1/√2)⁶ = 1/8, and the sum = 1/8 + 1/8 = 1/4.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 139. If 2sin²θ − 7sin θ + 3 = 0 and 0° < θ < 90°, then θ equals:
(a) 30°
(b) 45°
(c) 60°
(d) 90°
Answer: (a)
Solution: Factorising: (2sin θ − 1)(sin θ − 3) = 0, so sin θ = 1/2 or sin θ = 3 (impossible, since sin θ ≤ 1). Hence sin θ = 1/2, giving θ = 30°.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 140. If cosec θ − sin θ = m and sec θ − cos θ = n, then the value of (m²n)^(2/3) + (mn²)^(2/3) is:
(a) 1
(b) m + n
(c) √(mn)
(d) (√m + √n)²
Answer: (a)
Solution: m = cosec θ − sin θ = (1 − sin²θ)/sin θ = cos²θ/sin θ, and n = sec θ − cos θ = sin²θ/cos θ. Then m²n = (cos⁴θ/sin²θ)(sin²θ/cos θ) = cos³θ, so (m²n)^(2/3) = cos²θ. Similarly mn² = sin³θ, so (mn²)^(2/3) = sin²θ. Sum = sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 141. A kite string is 100 m long and makes an angle of 60° with the horizontal. If the string is replaced by another string making 45° with the horizontal while the kite stays at the same height, the length of the new string will be:
(a) 50√6 m
(b) 100√2 m
(c) 100√2/√3 m
(d) 100√3 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Height of the kite h = 100 sin 60° = 100 × (√3/2) = 50√3 m. New string length L = h/sin 45° = 50√3 × √2 = 50√6 m.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 142. The value of tan 1° · tan 2° · tan 3° ⋯ tan 89° is:
(a) 0
(b) 1
(c) √3
(d) undefined
Answer: (b)
Solution: tan θ · tan(90° − θ) = tan θ · cot θ = 1. The terms from 1° to 44° pair with those from 89° to 46°, each pair giving 1, and tan 45° = 1. Hence the product = 1.

Question 143. If tan 15° = 2 − √3, then cot 75° equals:
(a) 2 + √3
(b) 2 − √3
(c) 1
(d) √3
Answer: (b)
Solution: cot 75° = tan(90° − 75°) = tan 15° = 2 − √3. Option (a) is the trap — it is the value of cot 15°, not cot 75°.

Question 144. If cos θ + sec θ = 2, then the value of cos²⁰¹⁶θ + sec²⁰¹⁶θ is:
(a) 0
(b) 1
(c) 2
(d) 2²⁰¹⁶
Answer: (c)
Solution: cos θ + 1/cos θ = 2 gives (cos θ − 1)² = 0, so cos θ = 1 and sec θ = 1. Hence the sum = 1 + 1 = 2, for any positive power.

Question 145. The shadow of a pole is 10 m longer when the sun's altitude is 30° than when it is 60°. The height of the pole is:
(a) 5√3 m
(b) 10√3 m
(c) 5 m
(d) 10 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let the height be h. Shadow at 60° = h/√3; shadow at 30° = h√3. Difference = h√3 − h/√3 = 2h/√3 = 10, so h = 5√3 m.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 146. If sin θ : cos θ = 3 : 4, then sec θ − tan θ equals:
(a) 1/2
(b) 1/3
(c) 1/4
(d) 3/4
Answer: (a)
Solution: sin θ/cos θ = 3/4 gives tan θ = 3/4. Then sec θ = √(1 + tan²θ) = 5/4. Hence sec θ − tan θ = 5/4 − 3/4 = 2/4 = 1/2.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 147. If sin θ + cosec θ = 2, then the value of sin⁴θ + cosec⁴θ is:
(a) 1
(b) 2
(c) 4
(d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: sin θ + 1/sin θ = 2 gives sin θ = 1, so cosec θ = 1. Hence sin⁴θ + cosec⁴θ = 1 + 1 = 2.

Question 148. From the top of a 200 m high cliff, the angle of depression of a ship is 30°. After some time it becomes 60°. The distance travelled by the ship in this interval is:
(a) 400/√3 m
(b) 200√3 m
(c) 200/√3 m
(d) 400√3 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Initial distance d₁ = 200 cot 30° = 200√3; later distance d₂ = 200 cot 60° = 200/√3. Distance travelled = d₁ − d₂ = 200√3 − 200/√3 = 200 × (2/√3) = 400/√3 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Exercise 9.1

Question 149. If sin θ + cos θ = (√3 + 1)/2, then sin θ cos θ equals:
(a) √3/4
(b) 1/4
(c) √3/2
(d) 1/2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Squaring: 1 + 2sinθcosθ = ((√3 + 1)/2)² = (3 + 1 + 2√3)/4 = (4 + 2√3)/4 = 1 + √3/2. Hence 2sinθcosθ = √3/2, so sinθcosθ = √3/4.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 150. If x = a cos θ + b sin θ and y = a sin θ − b cos θ, then x² + y² equals:
(a) a² + b²
(b) a² − b²
(c) ab
(d) a²b²
Answer: (a)
Solution: x² + y² = (a cos θ + b sin θ)² + (a sin θ − b cos θ)² = a²cos²θ + b²sin²θ + 2ab sinθcosθ + a²sin²θ + b²cos²θ − 2ab sinθcosθ = a²(cos²θ + sin²θ) + b²(sin²θ + cos²θ) = a² + b². The cross terms cancel — that is the key step.
Source: PYQ-style
