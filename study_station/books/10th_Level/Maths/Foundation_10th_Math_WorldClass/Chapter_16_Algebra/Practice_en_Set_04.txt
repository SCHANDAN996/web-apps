76. Solve the system: 3x + 2y = 12, 5x – 3y = 1.
(A) x = 2, y = 3
(B) x = 3, y = 2
(C) x = 1, y = 4
(D) x = 4, y = 0
Answer: (A) x = 2, y = 3
Solution:
Multiply first equation by 3: 9x + 6y = 36. Multiply second by 2: 10x – 6y = 2. Add: 19x = 38 → x = 2. Substitute into 3(2)+2y=12 → 6+2y=12 → 2y=6 → y=3.
Source: NCERT Class 10

77. If a + b = 7 and ab = 12, find a² + b².
(A) 25
(B) 37
(C) 49
(D) 13
Answer: (A) 25
Solution:
a² + b² = (a + b)² – 2ab = 7² – 2(12) = 49 – 24 = 25.
Source: NCERT Class 9

78. The sum of two numbers is 8 and the sum of their reciprocals is 8/15. Find the numbers.
(A) 3 and 5
(B) 2 and 6
(C) 4 and 4
(D) 1 and 7
Answer: (A) 3 and 5
Solution:
Let numbers be x, y. x + y = 8, 1/x + 1/y = (x+y)/(xy) = 8/xy = 8/15 → xy = 15. Numbers are roots of t² – 8t + 15 = 0 → (t-3)(t-5)=0 → 3 and 5.
Source: NCERT Class 10

79. If x + 1/x = 4, find x² + 1/x².
(A) 14
(B) 16
(C) 18
(D) 12
Answer: (A) 14
Solution:
Square both sides: (x + 1/x)² = 4² → x² + 1/x² + 2 = 16 → x² + 1/x² = 14.
Source: NCERT Class 9

80. The present age of a father is 3 times the age of his son. After 12 years, the father’s age will be twice that of his son. The son’s present age is:
(A) 12 years
(B) 10 years
(C) 15 years
(D) 8 years
Answer: (A) 12 years
Solution:
Let son = s years, father = 3s. After 12 years: 3s + 12 = 2(s + 12) → 3s + 12 = 2s + 24 → s = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 10

81. If a/4 = b/5 = c/6, find the value of (a + b + c)/b.
(A) 3
(B) 5
(C) 4
(D) 2
Answer: (A) 3
Solution:
Let a = 4k, b = 5k, c = 6k. Then (a+b+c)/b = (4k + 5k + 6k)/(5k) = 15k/5k = 3.

84. Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If each is increased by 10, the ratio becomes 5:7. The numbers are:
(A) 15 and 25
(B) 9 and 15
(C) 18 and 30
(D) 12 and 20
Answer: (A) 15 and 25
Solution:
Let numbers be 3x, 5x. Then (3x+10)/(5x+10) = 5/7 → 7(3x+10) = 5(5x+10) → 21x + 70 = 25x + 50 → 4x = 20 → x = 5. Numbers = 15, 25.
Source: NCERT Class 10

85. Solve: 2x + 3y = 8, 3x + 2y = 7.
(A) x = 1, y = 2
(B) x = 2, y = 1
(C) x = 4, y = 0
(D) x = 3, y = –1
Answer: (A) x = 1, y = 2
Solution:
Add equations: 5x + 5y = 15 → x + y = 3. Subtract second from first: –x + y = 1 → y – x = 1. Solving x+y=3, y–x=1: add → 2y=4 → y=2, then x=1.
Source: NCERT Class 10

86. The denominator of a fraction is 3 more than twice the numerator. The sum of numerator and denominator is 18. The fraction is:
(A) 5/13
(B) 3/9
(C) 7/17
(D) 4/11
Answer: (A) 5/13
Solution:
Let numerator = x, denominator = 2x+3. Sum: x + (2x+3) = 18 → 3x + 3 = 18 → 3x = 15 → x = 5. Denominator = 2(5)+3=13. Fraction = 5/13.
Source: NCERT Class 10

88. A man is twice as old as his son. 20 years ago, he was 12 times as old as his son. Find the man’s present age.

91. A boat takes 2 hours to go 16 km downstream and 4 hours to return the same distance upstream. Find the speed of the boat in still water (km/h).
(A) 6
(B) 4
(C) 8
(D) 5
Answer: (A) 6
Solution:
Downstream speed = 16/2 = 8 km/h, upstream speed = 16/4 = 4 km/h. Speed in still water = (8+4)/2 = 6 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 10

92. The sum of two numbers is 9 and the sum of their squares is 41. Find the numbers.
(A) 4 and 5
(B) 3 and 6
(C) 2 and 7
(D) 1 and 8
Answer: (A) 4 and 5
Solution:
Let numbers be x, y. x+y=9, x²+y²=41. (x+y)²=81 → 2xy=81–41=40 → xy=20. Numbers are roots of t²–9t+20=0 → (t–4)(t–5)=0 → 4,5.
Source: NCERT Class 9

93. If (3x – 2y) : (2x + 3y) = 5 : 6, find x : y.
(A) 27 : 8
(B) 8 : 27
(C) 5 : 6
(D) 6 : 5
Answer: (A) 27 : 8
Solution:
6(3x – 2y) = 5(2x + 3y) → 18x – 12y = 10x + 15y → 8x = 27y → x/y = 27/8, so x : y = 27 : 8.

94. If x + 1/x = a, then x² + 1/x² equals:

95. Evaluate 101² – 99².
(A) 400
(B) 200
(C) 2
(D) 4
Answer: (A) 400
Solution:
a² – b² = (a – b)(a + b) = (101 – 99)(101 + 99) = 2 × 200 = 400.

96. If x/y = 3/4, find (3x + 2y)/(5x – 3y).

97. Solve: 0.2x + 0.3y = 1.3, 0.4x + 0.5y = 2.3.
(A) x = 2, y = 3
(B) x = 3, y = 2
(C) x = 1, y = 4
(D) x = 0, y = 5
Answer: (A) x = 2, y = 3
Solution:
Multiply equations by 10: 2x + 3y = 13, 4x + 5y = 23.
Multiply first by 2: 4x + 6y = 26.
Subtract second: (4x+6y) – (4x+5y) = 26 – 23 → y = 3.
Then 2x + 9 = 13 → x = 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10

98. A fraction becomes 1/2 when 1 is added to the numerator, and becomes 1/3 when 1 is added to the denominator. The fraction is:
(A) 3/8
(B) 5/12
(C) 2/5
(D) 4/9
Answer: (A) 3/8
Solution:
Let fraction = x/y. (x+1)/y = 1/2 → 2x + 2 = y.
x/(y+1) = 1/3 → 3x = y + 1.
Substitute y: 3x = (2x+2) + 1 → 3x = 2x + 3 → x = 3, y = 2(3)+2 = 8. Fraction = 3/8.
Source: NCERT Class 10
