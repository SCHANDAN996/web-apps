Set 4: Algebra MCQs — Questions 76–100 (All Medium – 25 Questions)

76. Solve the system: 3x + 2y = 12, 5x – 3y = 1.
(a) x = 2, y = 3 (b) x = 3, y = 2 (c) x = 1, y = 4 (d) x = 4, y = 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: The pair x = 3, y = 2 satisfies the first equation but fails the second, which is the common trap of checking only one equation. Multiply the first equation by 3 and the second by 2, then add: 19x = 38, so x = 2, and substituting gives y = 3, so the answer is x = 2, y = 3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

77. If a + b = 7 and ab = 12, find a² + b².
(a) 37 (b) 13 (c) 49 (d) 25
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students wrongly square the sum and report 49, forgetting to subtract 2ab. Using a² + b² = (a + b)² – 2ab = 49 – 24 = 25, so the answer is 25.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

78. The sum of two numbers is 8 and the sum of their reciprocals is 8/15. Find the numbers.
(a) 3 and 5 (b) 4 and 4 (c) 2 and 6 (d) 1 and 7
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to test pairs that only satisfy the sum condition, like 2 and 6, whose reciprocals add to 2/3, not 8/15. Since (x + y)/(xy) = 8/15 gives xy = 15, the numbers are roots of t² – 8t + 15 = 0, so the answer is 3 and 5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

79. If x + 1/x = 4, find x² + 1/x².
(a) 14 (b) 18 (c) 12 (d) 16
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting error is to square 4 and write 16, ignoring the cross term. Squaring gives x² + 1/x² + 2 = 16, so x² + 1/x² = 14, and the answer is 14.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

80. The present age of a father is 3 times the age of his son. After 12 years, the father's age will be twice that of his son. The son's present age is:
(a) 15 years (b) 10 years (c) 12 years (d) 8 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: A common slip is writing 3s + 12 = 2s + 12, adding 12 to only one side of the comparison. Correctly, 3s + 12 = 2(s + 12) gives s = 12, so the son's present age is 12 years.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

81. If a/4 = b/5 = c/6, find the value of (a + b + c)/b.
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Students often add the denominators 4 + 5 + 6 = 15 and stop, or divide by a instead of b. Taking a = 4k, b = 5k, c = 6k gives (15k)/(5k) = 3, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Solve for x: (x + 3)/2 – (x – 1)/3 = 1
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) –5 (d) –3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is expanding –2(x – 1) as –2x – 2 instead of –2x + 2, which leads to x = 5. Multiplying by 6 gives 3x + 9 – 2x + 2 = 6, so x + 11 = 6 and the answer is x = –5.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

83. If x + 1/x = √5, find x² + 1/x².
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) 7 (d) √5
Answer: (b)
Solution: Squaring √5 gives 5, and forgetting to subtract 2 makes 5 the tempting wrong choice. Since x² + 1/x² = 5 – 2 = 3, the answer is 3.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

84. Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If each is increased by 10, the ratio becomes 5:7. The numbers are:
(a) 15 and 25 (b) 9 and 15 (c) 18 and 30 (d) 12 and 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to increase only one number or to cross-multiply incorrectly, giving pairs like 12 and 20. Solving 7(3x + 10) = 5(5x + 10) gives x = 5, so the numbers are 15 and 25.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

85. Solve: 2x + 3y = 8, 3x + 2y = 7.
(a) x = 2, y = 1 (b) x = 4, y = 0 (c) x = 1, y = 2 (d) x = 3, y = –1
Answer: (c)
Solution: Because the coefficients are symmetric, students often swap the values and pick x = 2, y = 1, which fails the first equation. Adding gives x + y = 3 and subtracting gives y – x = 1, so y = 2 and x = 1; the answer is x = 1, y = 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

86. The denominator of a fraction is 3 more than twice the numerator. The sum of numerator and denominator is 18. The fraction is:
(a) 5/13 (b) 7/17 (c) 3/9 (d) 4/11
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking the denominator as 2x – 3 or misreading "3 more than twice", which yields 7/11-type errors. With numerator x and denominator 2x + 3, the sum gives 3x + 3 = 18, so x = 5 and the fraction is 5/13.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

87. If (a + b)² = 49 and a² + b² = 29, find ab.
(a) 10 (b) 20 (c) 15 (d) 5
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many forget the factor 2 and report 2ab = 20 as ab = 20. Since 49 = 29 + 2ab, we get 2ab = 20 and ab = 10, so the answer is 10.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

88. A man is twice as old as his son. 20 years ago, he was 12 times as old as his son. Find the man's present age.
(a) 40 years (b) 48 years (c) 44 years (d) 36 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is writing 2s – 20 = 12s – 20, subtracting 20 from only one age. Correctly, 2s – 20 = 12(s – 20) gives 10s = 220, so s = 22 and the man's present age is 44 years.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

89. If a + b + c = 11 and ab + bc + ca = 28, find a² + b² + c².
(a) 55 (b) 45 (c) 75 (d) 65
Answer: (d)
Solution: The common error is subtracting 28 instead of 2 × 28 = 56 from 121, giving 93, or halving wrongly to get 55. Using (a + b + c)² = a² + b² + c² + 2(ab + bc + ca), we get 121 – 56 = 65, so the answer is 65.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

90. If x + y = 5 and xy = 6, find x³ + y³.
(a) 25 (b) 45 (c) 35 (d) 30
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is cubing the sum and reporting 125, or subtracting only 3xy = 18. Using x³ + y³ = (x + y)³ – 3xy(x + y) = 125 – 90 = 35, the answer is 35.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

91. A boat takes 2 hours to go 16 km downstream and 4 hours to return the same distance upstream. Find the speed of the boat in still water (km/h).
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 5 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often average the times or pick the downstream speed 8 itself. Downstream speed = 8 km/h and upstream = 4 km/h, so still-water speed = (8 + 4)/2 = 6 km/h; the answer is 6.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

92. The sum of two numbers is 9 and the sum of their squares is 41. Find the numbers.
(a) 1 and 8 (b) 3 and 6 (c) 2 and 7 (d) 4 and 5
Answer: (d)
Solution: The pair 3 and 6 tempts because 3 + 6 = 9, but 9 + 36 = 45 ≠ 41. From (x + y)² = 81 we get xy = 20, so the numbers are roots of t² – 9t + 20 = 0, namely 4 and 5.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

93. If (3x – 2y) : (2x + 3y) = 5 : 6, find x : y.
(a) 8 : 27 (b) 6 : 5 (c) 5 : 6 (d) 27 : 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cross-multiplying the wrong way gives the inverted ratio 8 : 27, the classic trap. Correctly, 6(3x – 2y) = 5(2x + 3y) gives 8x = 27y, so x : y = 27 : 8.
Source: PYQ-style

94. If x + 1/x = a, then x² + 1/x² equals:
(a) a² + 2 (b) a² – 2 (c) a² (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting choice a² ignores the middle term 2·x·(1/x) = 2 produced on squaring. Since a² = x² + 1/x² + 2, we get x² + 1/x² = a² – 2, so the answer is a² – 2.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

95. Evaluate 101² – 99².
(a) 200 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 400
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing (101 – 99)² = 4, confusing a² – b² with (a – b)². Using a² – b² = (a – b)(a + b) = 2 × 200 = 400, the answer is 400.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

96. If x/y = 3/4, find (3x + 2y)/(5x – 3y).
(a) 3/17 (b) 17/3 (c) 5/7 (d) 7/5
Answer: (b)
Solution: Inverting the ratio by taking x = 4k, y = 3k is the common slip and gives a different value. With x = 3k, y = 4k, the expression is (9k + 8k)/(15k – 12k) = 17k/3k, so the answer is 17/3.
Source: PYQ-style

97. Solve: 0.2x + 0.3y = 1.3, 0.4x + 0.5y = 2.3.
(a) x = 3, y = 2 (b) x = 1, y = 4 (c) x = 2, y = 3 (d) x = 0, y = 5
Answer: (c)
Solution: Decimal mishandling often swaps the values, making x = 3, y = 2 tempting, but it fails 2x + 3y = 13. Clearing decimals gives 2x + 3y = 13 and 4x + 5y = 23; doubling the first and subtracting gives y = 3, then x = 2, so the answer is x = 2, y = 3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

98. A fraction becomes 1/2 when 1 is added to the numerator, and becomes 1/3 when 1 is added to the denominator. The fraction is:
(a) 5/12 (b) 3/8 (c) 2/5 (d) 4/9
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is adding 1 to the wrong term in one of the equations, which produces 2/5. From (x + 1)/y = 1/2 we get y = 2x + 2, and from x/(y + 1) = 1/3 we get 3x = y + 1; solving gives x = 3, y = 8, so the fraction is 3/8.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

99. If a – b = 5 and ab = 36, find a² + b².
(a) 72 (b) 25 (c) 61 (d) 97
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using (a – b)² = a² + b² + 2ab, which subtracts 72 and gives a negative result. Correctly, (a – b)² = a² + b² – 2ab gives 25 = a² + b² – 72, so a² + b² = 97 and the answer is 97.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

100. The sum of three consecutive even numbers is 78. The smallest number is:
(a) 26 (b) 24 (c) 28 (d) 22
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many divide 78 by 3 and report 26, but that is the middle number, not the smallest. Taking the numbers as x – 2, x, x + 2 gives 3x = 78, so x = 26 and the smallest number is 24.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
