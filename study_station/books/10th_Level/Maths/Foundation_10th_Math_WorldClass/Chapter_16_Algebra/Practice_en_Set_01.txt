1. Solve: 3x + 7 = 22
(A) 3
(B) 5
(C) 7
(D) 15
Answer: (B) 5
Solution:
3x = 22 – 7 = 15 ⇒ x = 15/3 = 5.
Source: NCERT Class 8

6. Use identity: 105² – 95² = ?
(A) 200
(B) 2000
(C) 1000
(D) 20000
Answer: (B) 2000
Solution:
a² – b² = (a – b)(a + b).
(105 – 95)(105 + 95) = 10 × 200 = 2000.
Source: NCERT Class 8

22. If a + b = 7 and a – b = 3, find a² + b².
(A) 29
(B) 20
(C) 40
(D) 58
Answer: (A) 29
Solution:
Method 1: Solve a = (7+3)/2 = 5, b = 2 → a² + b² = 25 + 4 = 29.
Method 2: (a+b)² + (a–b)² = 2(a² + b²) → 49 + 9 = 58 = 2(a² + b²) → a² + b² = 29.
Source: NCERT Class 9

23. If x = √3 + √2, find x + 1/x.
(A) 2√3
(B) 2√2
(C) 2
(D) √3 – √2
Answer: (A) 2√3
Solution:
1/x = 1/(√3+√2) = (√3–√2)/((√3)²–(√2)²) = √3–√2.
x + 1/x = (√3+√2) + (√3–√2) = 2√3.
Source: PYQ-style

24. The denominator of a fraction is 1 more than twice the numerator. If the sum of numerator and denominator is 16, find the fraction.
(A) 5/11
(B) 5/6
(C) 6/7
(D) 7/15
Answer: (A) 5/11
Solution:
Let numerator = x. Then denominator = 2x + 1.
Sum: x + (2x + 1) = 16 → 3x + 1 = 16 → 3x = 15 → x = 5.
Denominator = 11. Fraction = 5/11.
Source: NCERT Class 8 (adapted)
