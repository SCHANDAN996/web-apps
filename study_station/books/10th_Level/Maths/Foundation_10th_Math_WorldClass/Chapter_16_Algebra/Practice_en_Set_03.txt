51. If x + 1/x = 6, find x² + 1/x².
(A) 34
(B) 36
(C) 38
(D) 32
Answer: (A) 34
Solution:
Square both sides: (x + 1/x)² = 6² → x² + 1/x² + 2 = 36 → x² + 1/x² = 34.
Source: NCERT Class 9

55. If x – 1/x = 2, then x³ – 1/x³ equals:
(A) 14
(B) 8
(C) 6
(D) 10
Answer: (A) 14
Solution:
We know (x – 1/x)³ = x³ – 1/x³ – 3(x – 1/x). So 2³ = x³ – 1/x³ – 3(2) → 8 = (x³ – 1/x³) – 6 → x³ – 1/x³ = 14.
Source: NCERT Class 9

56. Find the value of (2.3)² – (1.7)².
(A) 2.4
(B) 4.0
(C) 0.6
(D) 2.0
Answer: (A) 2.4
Solution:
a² – b² = (a – b)(a + b) = (2.3 – 1.7)(2.3 + 1.7) = 0.6 × 4 = 2.4.
Source: NCERT Class 8

57. The numerator of a fraction is 3 less than the denominator. If 2 is added to both, the fraction becomes 2/3. Find the fraction.
(A) 4/7
(B) 5/8
(C) 3/6
(D) 7/10
Answer: (A) 4/7
Solution:
Let denominator = x, numerator = x – 3. Then (x – 3 + 2)/(x + 2) = 2/3 → (x – 1)/(x + 2) = 2/3 → 3(x – 1) = 2(x + 2) → 3x – 3 = 2x + 4 → x = 7. Numerator = 4. Fraction = 4/7.
Source: NCERT Class 10

58. If 5x + 4y = 18 and 4x + 5y = 18, then x + y = ?

(D) 5
Answer: (A) 4
Solution:
Add both equations: (5x+4y)+(4x+5y) = 18+18 → 9x+9y = 36 → 9(x+y) = 36 → x+y = 4.

61. The product of two consecutive even numbers is 168. The smaller number is:
(A) 12
(B) 14
(C) 10
(D) 16
Answer: (A) 12
Solution:
Let numbers be 2n and 2n+2. Then 2n(2n+2) = 168 → 4n(n+1) = 168 → n(n+1) = 42 → n = 6. Thus numbers are 12 and 14, smaller = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 10

63. Five years ago, the age of a man was 7 times the age of his son. After 5 years, his age will be 3 times the age of his son. The son’s present age is:
(A) 10 years
(B) 12 years
(C) 8 years
(D) 15 years
Answer: (A) 10 years
Solution:
Let son's present age = x, man = y. 5 years ago: y – 5 = 7(x – 5) → y – 5 = 7x – 35 → y = 7x – 30. After 5 years: y + 5 = 3(x + 5) → (7x – 30) + 5 = 3x + 15 → 7x – 25 = 3x + 15 → 4x = 40 → x = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 10

68. The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 11. If the digits are reversed, the number exceeds the original by 27. The original number is:
(A) 47
(B) 74
(C) 56
(D) 65
Answer: (A) 47
Solution:
Let number = 10a+b, a+b=11. Reversed = 10b+a. Then (10b+a) – (10a+b) = 27 → 9b – 9a = 27 → b – a = 3. Solve a+b=11, b–a=3 → 2b=14 → b=7, a=4. Number = 47.
Source: NCERT Class 10

70. A fraction becomes 1/3 when 1 is subtracted from the numerator and it becomes 1/4 when 8 is added to its denominator. The fraction is:
(A) 5/12
(B) 3/10
(C) 4/9
(D) 6/15
Answer: (A) 5/12
Solution:
Let fraction = x/y. (x–1)/y = 1/3 → 3x – 3 = y. x/(y+8) = 1/4 → 4x = y+8. Substitute y: 4x = (3x–3)+8 → 4x = 3x+5 → x=5, then y=3(5)-3=12. Fraction 5/12.
Source: NCERT Class 10
