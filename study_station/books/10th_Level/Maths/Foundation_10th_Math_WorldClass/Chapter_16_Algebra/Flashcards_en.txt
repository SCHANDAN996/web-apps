🃏 Flashcards – English

Card 1

Front: What is the secret identity of 'x' in algebra?

Back: 'x' is just a placeholder — an empty box waiting for a number to fill it. It's the detective's prime suspect.

Card 2

Front: Why can't you "solve" 5x + 3, but you can solve 5x + 3 = 23?

Back: 5x + 3 is an expression (no equals sign). 5x + 3 = 23 is an equation (has =). Only equations make a claim that can be solved.

Card 3

Front: A number crosses the magic "=" river. What must it pay?

Back: It must flip its sign: + becomes –, – becomes +, × becomes ÷, ÷ becomes ×. This is the Transposition method.

Card 4

Front: In the riddle "5 pens + 3 notebooks cost 55, 3 pens + 5 notebooks cost 49", what's the first move?

Back: Multiply the first equation by 3 and the second by 5, so the coefficients of x (pens) become equal (15). Then subtract to eliminate x.

Card 5

Front: What is the forgotten piece in (a+b)² = a² + b² + ???

Back: + 2ab. Many forget the two identical rectangles (a×b and b×a) that fill the gaps when expanding the square.

Card 6

Front: What's the visual trick to never forget (a+b)²?

Back: Draw a square, split it with a vertical line at 'a' and a horizontal line at 'a'. You see four pieces: a² (big), b² (small), and two rectangles of a×b. Their sum is a² + 2ab + b².

Card 7

Front: (a – b)² looks like (a+b)² but with a mood swing. What's the formula?

Back: a² – 2ab + b². The middle sign flips because now the two rectangles are "missing" or negative.

Card 8

Front: What's the magic trick for a² – b² without expanding?

Back: a² – b² = (a + b)(a – b). The difference of two squares equals the product of sum and difference.

Card 9

Front: Expand (x + 3)(x + 5) in one breath — what's the shortcut?

Back: (x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a+b)x + ab. Here a=3, b=5 → x² + 8x + 15. Middle coefficient is sum, last is product.

Card 10

Front: In the "garden and tiles" Feynman story, why is (a+b)² never just a² + b²?

Back: Because you forget the two rectangular tile strips where old garden meets new extension — each a×b, together 2ab. The story shows (a+b)² breaks into four pieces.

Card 11

Front: What is the special name for formulas like (a+b)² that hold true for any value of a and b?

Back: Algebraic Identities. They are universally true shortcuts.

Card 12

Front: How does the Distributive Law turn 102 × 9 into a mental math breeze?

Back: a(b+c) = ab + ac. 102 × 9 = (100+2)×9 = 900 + 18 = 918. Break numbers into friendly parts.

Card 13

Front: Translate: "The sum of twice a number and 7 is 25." What's the equation?

Back: Let the number be x. Twice the number = 2x, sum with 7 = 2x + 7. "Is 25" gives 2x + 7 = 25.

Card 14

Front: What is the biggest trap when removing parentheses: –(x – y) = ?

Back: –(x – y) = –x + y. The minus sign flips the sign of every term inside, including –y becoming +y. Many write –x – y incorrectly.

Card 15

Front: Why is 3x² + 2x + 1 = 0 NOT a linear equation?

Back: Because the highest power of x is 2. Linear equations only have power 1 (like 2x + 5 = 0).

Card 16

Front: In the "A Boy and a Cat" mnemonic for (a+b)², how many meeting rooms do they build?

Back: Two meeting rooms, each of size a×b. Together they form the 2ab part. (Story: Boy builds a², Cat builds b², and they build two shared rooms — 2ab.)

Card 17

Front: What's the elimination method's golden rule for two linear equations?

Back: Make coefficients of one variable equal (by multiplying equations), then add or subtract to eliminate it. This reduces to one variable.

Card 18

Front: After solving x, how do you check if your answer is correct without redoing everything?

Back: Substitute your value back into the original equation. If both sides equal, it's correct. Like 5(3)+4=19 → 19=19 for x=3.

Card 19

Front: What mnemonic helps remember the order of transposition?

Back: "Move add/subtract first, then multiply/divide" — the reverse of BODMAS when crossing the equals sign.

Card 20

Front: A shopkeeper says, "I sold a total of 20 pens and pencils. Pens are 3 more than pencils." How do you frame the equation?

Back: Let pencils = x. Pens = x + 3. Total: x + (x+3) = 20 → 2x + 3 = 20. Two statements yield two relations.
