Q26. A is twice as efficient as B, and B is thrice as efficient as C. If C alone can finish a work in 36 days, in how many days can A alone finish the same work?
(a) 4 days (b) 6 days (c) 9 days (d) 12 days

Answer: (b) 6 days

Solution:
Efficiencies: A : B = 2 : 1, B : C = 3 : 1 → A : B : C = 6 : 3 : 1.
C takes 36 days, so total work = 1 × 36 = 36 units (efficiency units scaled).
A's efficiency = 6 → A's time = 36/6 = 6 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ adapted

Q27. A, B and C can complete a work in 12, 15 and 20 days respectively. They start working together, but C leaves after 2 days. In how many more days will A and B together finish the remaining work?
(a) 4 days (b) 5 days (c) 6 days (d) 8 days

Answer: (a) 4 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(12, 15, 20) = 60 units.
Eff: A = 5, B = 4, C = 3. Combined = 12.
Work done in 2 days = 12 × 2 = 24. Remaining = 36.
A + B eff = 9. Time = 36/9 = 4 days.
Source: NCERT based

Q28. 10 men can complete a piece of work in 15 days. After working for 5 days, 5 more men join them. In how many days will the remaining work be completed?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 6⅔ days (d) 7 days

Answer: (c) 6⅔ days

Solution:
Total work = 10 × 15 = 150 man-days.
Work done in 5 days = 10 × 5 = 50 man-days.
Remaining work = 100 man-days.
Now 15 men. Time = 100 / 15 = 20/3 = 6⅔ days.
Source: SSC CHSL PYQ

Q29. A and B together can do a piece of work in 20 days. B and C together in 24 days. A and C together in 30 days. In how many days can all three together complete the work?
(a) 12 days (b) 14 days (c) 16 days (d) 18 days

Answer: (c) 16 days

Solution:
2(A + B + C)'s 1-day work = 1/20 + 1/24 + 1/30 = (6 + 5 + 4)/120 = 15/120 = 1/8.
(A + B + C)'s 1-day work = 1/16.
All three take 16 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q30. A and B can complete a work in 15 days and 10 days respectively. They started the work together, but B left after 2 days. A continued alone and completed the remaining work. How many total days did A work?
(a) 8 days (b) 10 days (c) 12 days (d) 14 days

Answer: (c) 12 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(15, 10) = 30 units.
A eff = 2, B eff = 3.
Together 2 days: work done = 5 × 2 = 10 units. Remaining = 20.
A alone takes 20/2 = 10 days. Total A worked = 2 + 10 = 12 days.
Source: NCERT based
