📝 Practice MCQs: Time & Work — Set 1 (Q1–Q25)

Q1. A can complete a piece of work in 10 days, and B can complete the same work in 15 days. If they work together, how many days will they take to finish the work?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 7 days (d) 8 days

Answer: (b) 6 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM of 10 and 15 = 30 units.
A's efficiency = 30/10 = 3 units/day.
B's efficiency = 30/15 = 2 units/day.
Combined efficiency = 3 + 2 = 5 units/day.
Time = 30/5 = 6 days.
Source: NCERT based

Q2. A alone can do a job in 20 days, B alone in 30 days. How long will they take together?
(a) 10 days (b) 12 days (c) 15 days (d) 18 days

Answer: (b) 12 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units.
A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2. Combined eff = 5.
Time = 60/5 = 12 days.
Source: SSC CHSL PYQ adapted

Q3. A is twice as efficient as B. B alone can finish a work in 30 days. In how many days can A alone finish the same work?
(a) 15 days (b) 20 days (c) 12 days (d) 10 days

Answer: (a) 15 days

Solution:
Efficiency ratio A : B = 2 : 1 → Time ratio A : B = 1 : 2.
B takes 30 days → A takes 30/2 = 15 days.
Source: NCERT

Q4. 8 men can complete a work in 12 days. How many men are needed to complete the same work in 8 days?
(a) 10 men (b) 12 men (c) 16 men (d) 14 men

Answer: (b) 12 men

Solution:
M₁D₁ = M₂D₂ (work constant)
⇒ 8 × 12 = M₂ × 8 ⇒ M₂ = 12 men.
Source: NCERT

Q5. A can do a work in 18 days, B in 24 days. They work together for 6 days. What fraction of the work is left?
(a) 5/12 (b) 7/12 (c) 1/3 (d) 1/2

Answer: (a) 5/12

Solution:
Total work = LCM(18, 24) = 72 units.
A's eff = 4, B's eff = 3, together = 7.
Work done in 6 days = 6 × 7 = 42.
Remaining work = 30/72 = 5/12.
Source: NCERT

Q6. A, B and C can complete a work in 10, 12 and 15 days respectively. In how many days will they finish the work together?
(a) 3 days (b) 4 days (c) 5 days (d) 6 days

Answer: (b) 4 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(10, 12, 15) = 60 units.
Eff: A = 6, B = 5, C = 4, total = 15.
Time = 60/15 = 4 days.
Source: NCERT

Q7. 12 men can reap a field in 7 days. In how many days can 14 men reap the same field?
(a) 6 days (b) 8 days (c) 5 days (d) 10 days

Answer: (a) 6 days

Solution:
12 × 7 = 14 × D ⇒ D = 84/14 = 6 days.
Source: NCERT

Q8. A and B together can complete a work in 10 days. B alone can complete it in 15 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 25 days (c) 30 days (d) 35 days

Answer: (c) 30 days

Solution:
1/A + 1/15 = 1/10 ⇒ 1/A = 1/10 – 1/15 = (3 – 2)/30 = 1/30 ⇒ A takes 30 days.
Source: NCERT

Q9. A can do a work in 8 days, B in 12 days. With the help of C, they finish the work in 3 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 6 days (b) 8 days (c) 10 days (d) 12 days

Answer: (b) 8 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(8, 12) = 24 units.
A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2, (A + B + C) eff = 24/3 = 8.
C's eff = 8 – 3 – 2 = 3.
C alone time = 24/3 = 8 days.
Source: SSC

Q10. A is thrice as efficient as B. B completes a work in 45 days. In how many days will A alone complete the same work?
(a) 10 days (b) 12 days (c) 15 days (d) 20 days

Answer: (c) 15 days

Solution:
Efficiency A:B = 3:1 → Time A:B = 1:3.
B takes 45 days ⇒ A takes 45/3 = 15 days.
Source: NCERT

Q11. 20 men can dig a well in 15 days. How many days will 30 men take to dig the same well?
(a) 8 days (b) 10 days (c) 12 days (d) 15 days

Answer: (b) 10 days

Solution:
20 × 15 = 30 × D ⇒ D = 300/30 = 10 days.
Source: NCERT

Q12. A and B can do a piece of work in 4 days and 6 days respectively. If they work on alternate days starting with A, in how many days will the work be finished?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 7 days (d) 8 days

Answer: (a) 5 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(4, 6) = 12 units.
A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2. 2‑day cycle: 3 + 2 = 5 units.
After 2 cycles (4 days): 10 units. Day 5 (A’s turn): remaining 2 units done by A. Total 5 days.
Source: NCERT

Q13. A, B and C can complete a work in 6, 8 and 12 days respectively. If they work together, in what ratio should their daily wages be divided?
(a) 4 : 3 : 2 (b) 3 : 4 : 2 (c) 2 : 3 : 4 (d) 4 : 2 : 3

Answer: (a) 4 : 3 : 2

Solution:
Daily efficiencies: A = 1/6, B = 1/8, C = 1/12. LCM of denominators = 24.
A's daily = 4, B's = 3, C's = 2. Wages ∝ daily work, so ratio = 4 : 3 : 2.
Source: NCERT based

Q14. 18 men can complete a work in 20 days. How many men are required to complete the same work in 15 days?
(a) 20 men (b) 22 men (c) 24 men (d) 30 men

Answer: (c) 24 men

Solution:
18 × 20 = M × 15 ⇒ M = 360/15 = 24 men.

Q15. A can do a work in 25 days, B in 20 days. They work together for 5 days, then A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?
(a) 8 days (b) 9 days (c) 10 days (d) 11 days

Answer: (d) 11 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(25, 20) = 100 units.
A's eff = 4, B's eff = 5, together = 9.
Work in 5 days = 5 × 9 = 45. Remaining = 55.
B's days = 55/5 = 11 days.
Source: NCERT

Q16. A can do a certain work in the same time in which B and C together can do it. A and B together can do it in 10 days, and C alone in 50 days. In how many days can B alone do the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 25 days (c) 30 days (d) 35 days

Answer: (b) 25 days

Solution:
Given: A's daily = B's + C's. A + B = 1/10, C = 1/50.
Let B's daily = b, then A's = b + 1/50.
(b + 1/50) + b = 1/10 ⇒ 2b + 1/50 = 1/10 ⇒ 2b = 1/10 – 1/50 = 4/50 = 2/25 ⇒ b = 1/25.
B alone takes 25 days.
Source: SSC CPO

Q17. 6 men and 8 boys can do a piece of work in 10 days. 26 men and 48 boys can do the same work in 2 days. How many days will 15 men and 20 boys take to complete the same work?
(a) 2 days (b) 3 days (c) 4 days (d) 5 days

Answer: (c) 4 days

Solution:
Let 1 man's 1‑day work = M, 1 boy's = B.
(6M + 8B) × 10 = (26M + 48B) × 2
⇒ 60M + 80B = 52M + 96B ⇒ 8M = 16B ⇒ M = 2B.
Total work = (6×2B + 8B) × 10 = 200B.
15M + 20B = 15×2B + 20B = 50B.
Time = 200B / 50B = 4 days.
Source: SSC CGL

Q18. A, B and C together can finish a work in 4 days. A alone in 12 days, B alone in 18 days. In how many days can C alone finish the work?
(a) 8 days (b) 9 days (c) 10 days (d) 12 days

Answer: (b) 9 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(4, 12, 18) = 36 units.
(A + B + C) eff = 36/4 = 9.
A eff = 3, B eff = 2, so C eff = 9 – 3 – 2 = 4.
C's time = 36/4 = 9 days.
Source: NCERT

Q19. A and B can do a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, C and A in 20 days. How many days will A alone take to complete the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 25 days (c) 30 days (d) 40 days

Answer: (c) 30 days

Solution:
(A + B) = 1/12, (B + C) = 1/15, (C + A) = 1/20.
Adding: 2(A + B + C) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = (5 + 4 + 3)/60 = 12/60 = 1/5.
(A + B + C) = 1/10.
A's daily = (A + B + C) – (B + C) = 1/10 – 1/15 = 1/30.
A alone = 30 days.
Source: SSC

Q20. If 10 men or 20 women can do a piece of work in 15 days, in how many days can 5 men and 10 women do the same work?
(a) 10 days (b) 12 days (c) 15 days (d) 20 days

Answer: (c) 15 days

Solution:
10 men = 20 women ⇒ 1 man = 2 women.
5 men + 10 women = 5×2 women + 10 women = 20 women.
20 women take 15 days (given). So, time = 15 days.
Source: NCERT

Q21. (Medium) A is 30% more efficient than B. B alone can complete a work in 26 days. In how many days will A alone complete the work?
(a) 18 days (b) 20 days (c) 22 days (d) 24 days

Answer: (b) 20 days

Solution:
Efficiency A = 130% of B → E_A : E_B = 130 : 100 = 13 : 10.
Time ratio A : B = 10 : 13.
B's time = 26 days (13 units) → 1 unit = 2 days.
A's time = 10 units = 20 days.
Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) A and B together can complete a work in 8 days. They started working together but after 3 days, B left and A finished the remaining work in 10 days. In how many days can B alone complete the work?
(a) 12 days (b) 14 days (c) 16 days (d) 20 days

Answer: (c) 16 days

Solution:
Together 1 day work = 1/8.
Work done in 3 days = 3/8, remaining = 5/8.
A completes 5/8 in 10 days → A's daily = (5/8)/10 = 1/16.
B's daily = 1/8 – 1/16 = 1/16.
B alone = 16 days.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q23. (Medium) 4 men and 6 women can complete a work in 8 days, while 3 men and 7 women can complete it in 10 days. In how many days will 10 women alone complete the work?
(a) 30 days (b) 35 days (c) 40 days (d) 45 days

Answer: (c) 40 days

Solution:
Let M, W be 1‑day work of a man, woman.
(4M + 6W) × 8 = (3M + 7W) × 10
⇒ 32M + 48W = 30M + 70W
⇒ 2M = 22W ⇒ M = 11W.
Total work = (4×11W + 6W) × 8 = 50W × 8 = 400W.
10 women's efficiency = 10W, time = 400W/10W = 40 days.
Source: IBPS PO

Q24. (Medium) A alone can do a work in 20 days, B alone in 30 days. They work together for some days, then B leaves. A alone completes the remaining work in 10 days. For how many days did B work?
(a) 4 days (b) 6 days (c) 8 days (d) 10 days

Answer: (b) 6 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units.
A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2.
Let B work for x days (together with A).
A works for (x + 10) days.
Total work: 3(x + 10) + 2x = 60 ⇒ 5x + 30 = 60 ⇒ 5x = 30 ⇒ x = 6.
B worked for 6 days.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q25. (Medium) A is 20% more efficient than B, and B is 25% more efficient than C. Together A, B and C complete a work in 24 days. In how many days will A alone complete the work?
(a) 48 days (b) 50 days (c) 60 days (d) 72 days

Answer: (c) 60 days

Solution:
A = 1.2 B, B = 1.25 C.
Take C = 4 units (efficiency), then B = 5, A = 1.2 × 5 = 6.
Ratio A : B : C = 6 : 5 : 4, combined = 15.
Total work = 15 × 24 = 360 units.
A's time = 360/6 = 60 days.
Source: SSC CGL
