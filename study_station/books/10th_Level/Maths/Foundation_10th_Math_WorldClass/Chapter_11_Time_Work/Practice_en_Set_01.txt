📝 Practice MCQs: Time & Work — Set 1 (Q1–Q25)

1. A can complete a piece of work in 10 days, and B can complete the same work in 15 days. If they work together, how many days will they take to finish the work?
(a) 5 days (b) 7 days (c) 6 days (d) 8 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total work = LCM of 10 and 15 = 30 units. A's efficiency = 3 units/day, B's = 2 units/day. Combined efficiency = 5 units/day, so time = 30/5 = 6 days. The trap is to add the times, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

2. A alone can do a job in 20 days, B alone in 30 days. How long will they take together?
(a) 10 days (b) 12 days (c) 15 days (d) 18 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: Total work = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units. A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2, combined eff = 5, time = 60/5 = 12 days. The trap is to take the average of 20 and 30, so the answer is 12 days.

3. A is twice as efficient as B. B alone can finish a work in 30 days. In how many days can A alone finish the same work?
(a) 20 days (b) 15 days (c) 12 days (d) 10 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: Efficiency ratio A : B = 2 : 1, so time ratio A : B = 1 : 2. B takes 30 days, so A takes 30/2 = 15 days. The trap is to halve B's time, so the answer is 15 days.

4. 8 men can complete a work in 12 days. How many men are needed to complete the same work in 8 days?
(a) 10 men (b) 16 men (c) 12 men (d) 14 men
Answer: (c)
Solution: M₁D₁ = M₂D₂, so 8 × 12 = M₂ × 8 ⇒ M₂ = 12 men. The trap is to use 8 × 12 = 96, so the answer is 12 men.

5. A can do a work in 18 days, B in 24 days. They work together for 6 days. What fraction of the work is left?
(a) 1/2 (b) 7/12 (c) 1/3 (d) 5/12
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total work = LCM(18, 24) = 72 units. A's eff = 4, B's eff = 3, together = 7. Work done in 6 days = 6 × 7 = 42. Remaining work = 30/72 = 5/12. The trap is to compute the work done, so the answer is 5/12.

6. A, B and C can complete a work in 10, 12 and 15 days respectively. In how many days will they finish the work together?
(a) 4 days (b) 3 days (c) 5 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total work = LCM(10, 12, 15) = 60 units. Eff: A = 6, B = 5, C = 4, total = 15. Time = 60/15 = 4 days. The trap is to take the average of the times, so the answer is 4 days.

(a) 5 days (b) 8 days (c) 6 days (d) 10 days

Solution: 12 × 7 = 14 × D ⇒ D = 84/14 = 6 days. The trap is to multiply 12 × 7, so the answer is 6 days.

8. A and B together can complete a work in 10 days. B alone can complete it in 15 days. In how many days can A alone complete the work?
(a) 30 days (b) 25 days (c) 20 days (d) 35 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1/A + 1/15 = 1/10 ⇒ 1/A = 1/10 – 1/15 = (3 – 2)/30 = 1/30 ⇒ A takes 30 days. The trap is to take the difference, so the answer is 30 days.

9. A can do a work in 8 days, B in 12 days. With the help of C, they finish the work in 3 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 6 days (b) 10 days (c) 8 days (d) 12 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total work = LCM(8, 12) = 24 units. A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2, (A + B + C) eff = 24/3 = 8. C's eff = 8 – 3 – 2 = 3. C alone time = 24/3 = 8 days. The trap is to take the average of 8 and 12, so the answer is 8 days.

12. A and B can do a piece of work in 4 days and 6 days respectively. If they work on alternate days starting with A, in how many days will the work be finished?
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 7 days (d) 8 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total work = LCM(4, 6) = 12 units. A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2. 2‑day cycle: 3 + 2 = 5 units. After 2 cycles (4 days): 10 units. Day 5 (A’s turn): remaining 2 units done by A. Total 5 days. The trap is to take the average of 4 and 6, so the answer is 5 days.

13. A, B and C can complete a work in 6, 8 and 12 days respectively. If they work together, in what ratio should their daily wages be divided?
(a) 3 : 4 : 2 (b) 4 : 3 : 2 (c) 2 : 3 : 4 (d) 4 : 2 : 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Daily efficiencies: A = 1/6, B = 1/8, C = 1/12. LCM of denominators = 24. A's daily = 4, B's = 3, C's = 2. Wages ∝ daily work, so ratio = 4 : 3 : 2. The trap is to take the average of the times, so the answer is 4 : 3 : 2.

Answer: (b)
Solution: Total work = LCM(25, 20) = 100 units. A's eff = 4, B's eff = 5, together = 9. Work in 5 days = 5 × 9 = 45. Remaining = 55. B's days = 55/5 = 11 days. The trap is to take the average of 25 and 20, so the answer is 11 days.

16. A can do a certain work in the same time in which B and C together can do it. A and B together can do it in 10 days, and C alone in 50 days. In how many days can B alone do the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 30 days (c) 25 days (d) 35 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Given: A's daily = B's + C's. A + B = 1/10, C = 1/50. Let B's daily = b, then A's = b + 1/50. (b + 1/50) + b = 1/10 ⇒ 2b + 1/50 = 1/10 ⇒ 2b = 1/10 – 1/50 = 4/50 = 2/25 ⇒ b = 1/25. B alone takes 25 days. The trap is to take the average of 10 and 50, so the answer is 25 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

17. 6 men and 8 boys can do a piece of work in 10 days. 26 men and 48 boys can do the same work in 2 days. How many days will 15 men and 20 boys take to complete the same work?
(a) 4 days (b) 3 days (c) 2 days (d) 5 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let 1 man's 1‑day work = M, 1 boy's = B. (6M + 8B) × 10 = (26M + 48B) × 2 ⇒ 60M + 80B = 52M + 96B ⇒ 8M = 16B ⇒ M = 2B. Total work = (6×2B + 8B) × 10 = 200B. 15M + 20B = 15×2B + 20B = 50B. Time = 200B / 50B = 4 days. The trap is to take the average of 10 and 2, so the answer is 4 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

18. A, B and C together can finish a work in 4 days. A alone in 12 days, B alone in 18 days. In how many days can C alone finish the work?
(a) 8 days (b) 12 days (c) 10 days (d) 9 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total work = LCM(4, 12, 18) = 36 units. (A + B + C) eff = 36/4 = 9. A eff = 3, B eff = 2, so C eff = 9 – 3 – 2 = 4. C's time = 36/4 = 9 days. The trap is to take the average of 4, 12 and 18, so the answer is 9 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

19. A and B can do a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, C and A in 20 days. How many days will A alone take to complete the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 25 days (c) 40 days (d) 30 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: (A + B) = 1/12, (B + C) = 1/15, (C + A) = 1/20. Adding: 2(A + B + C) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = (5 + 4 + 3)/60 = 12/60 = 1/5. (A + B + C) = 1/10. A's daily = (A + B + C) – (B + C) = 1/10 – 1/15 = 1/30. A alone = 30 days. The trap is to take the average of 12, 15 and 20, so the answer is 30 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

20. If 10 men or 20 women can do a piece of work in 15 days, in how many days can 5 men and 10 women do the same work?

Answer: (d)
Solution: 10 men = 20 women ⇒ 1 man = 2 women. 5 men + 10 women = 5×2 women + 10 women = 20 women. 20 women take 15 days (given). So, time = 15 days. The trap is to take the average of 10 and 20, so the answer is 15 days.

21. A is 30% more efficient than B. B alone can complete a work in 26 days. In how many days will A alone complete the work?
(a) 18 days (b) 24 days (c) 22 days (d) 20 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Efficiency A = 130% of B → E_A : E_B = 130 : 100 = 13 : 10. Time ratio A : B = 10 : 13. B's time = 26 days (13 units) → 1 unit = 2 days. A's time = 10 units = 20 days. The trap is to take the average of 26 and 30, so the answer is 20 days.

22. A and B together can complete a work in 8 days. They started working together but after 3 days, B left and A finished the remaining work in 10 days. In how many days can B alone complete the work?
(a) 12 days (b) 14 days (c) 16 days (d) 20 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Together 1 day work = 1/8. Work done in 3 days = 3/8, remaining = 5/8. A completes 5/8 in 10 days → A's daily = (5/8)/10 = 1/16. B's daily = 1/8 – 1/16 = 1/16. B alone = 16 days. The trap is to take the average of 8 and 10, so the answer is 16 days.

23. 4 men and 6 women can complete a work in 8 days, while 3 men and 7 women can complete it in 10 days. In how many days will 10 women alone complete the work?
(a) 40 days (b) 35 days (c) 30 days (d) 45 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let M, W be 1‑day work of a man, woman. (4M + 6W) × 8 = (3M + 7W) × 10 ⇒ 32M + 48W = 30M + 70W ⇒ 2M = 22W ⇒ M = 11W. Total work = (4×11W + 6W) × 8 = 50W × 8 = 400W. 10 women's efficiency = 10W, time = 400W/10W = 40 days. The trap is to take the average of 8 and 10, so the answer is 40 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

24. A alone can do a work in 20 days, B alone in 30 days. They work together for some days, then B leaves. A alone completes the remaining work in 10 days. For how many days did B work?
(a) 4 days (b) 10 days (c) 8 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total work = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units. A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2. Let B work for x days (together with A). A works for (x + 10) days. Total work: 3(x + 10) + 2x = 60 ⇒ 5x + 30 = 60 ⇒ 5x = 30 ⇒ x = 6. B worked for 6 days. The trap is to take the average of 20 and 30, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

25. A is 20% more efficient than B, and B is 25% more efficient than C. Together A, B and C complete a work in 24 days. In how many days will A alone complete the work?
(a) 48 days (b) 60 days (c) 50 days (d) 72 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: A = 1.2 B, B = 1.25 C. Take C = 4 units (efficiency), then B = 5, A = 1.2 × 5 = 6. Ratio A : B : C = 6 : 5 : 4, combined = 15. Total work = 15 × 24 = 360 units. A's time = 360/6 = 60 days. The trap is to take the average of 20 and 30, so the answer is 60 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
