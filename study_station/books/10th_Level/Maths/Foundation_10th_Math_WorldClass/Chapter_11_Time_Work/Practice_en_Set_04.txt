76. A and B together can do a work in 15 days. B alone can do it in 20 days. In how many days can A alone do it?
(a) 60 days (b) 40 days (c) 50 days (d) 30 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting shortcut "20 − 15 = 5 days" is wrong because times cannot be subtracted directly. A's 1-day work = 1/15 − 1/20 = (4 − 3)/60 = 1/60, so the answer is 60 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

77. A is thrice as efficient as B. Working together they can finish a work in 12 days. In how many days can A alone finish it?
(a) 16 days (b) 14 days (c) 18 days (d) 20 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Do not divide 12 by 3 to get 4 days — that ignores B's contribution. Efficiency ratio A : B = 3 : 1, so total work = 4 × 12 = 48 units and A alone takes 48/3, so the answer is 16 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

78. 20 men can do a work in 25 days. After working for 5 days, 5 more men are hired. In how many total days will the work be completed?
(a) 20 days (b) 23 days (c) 22 days (d) 21 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Simply scaling 25 men for the whole work gives 20 days, but that forgets the first 5 days had only 20 men. Work = 500 man-days; 100 done in 5 days, remaining 400 done by 25 men in 16 days, so the answer is 21 days.
Source: PYQ-style

79. A and B together can do a piece of work in 8 days, B and C together in 12 days, and A and C together in 24 days. In how many days can all three together do the work?
(a) 6 days (b) 7 days (c) 8 days (d) 9 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding 1/8 + 1/12 + 1/24 = 1/4 and directly taking 4 days is the trap — that sum counts each person twice. Half of 1/4 is 1/8, so the answer is 8 days.
Source: PYQ-style

80. A can do a work in 10 days and B in 15 days. They start together, but after 3 days A leaves. In how many more days will B complete the remaining work?
(a) 5.5 days (b) 6 days (c) 7.5 days (d) 7 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Taking half of 15 days (7.5 assumed as B's solo time from scratch) misleads some into picking 6; the correct method uses work = 30 units, A = 3, B = 2. In 3 days they finish 15 units, leaving 15 units for B at 2 units/day, so the answer is 7.5 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

81. A is 30% more efficient than B. B alone can do a work in 23 days. In how many days will A and B together do the work?
(a) 13 days (b) 11 days (c) 12 days (d) 10 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Reducing 23 days by 30% gives about 16 days, which is wrong because that would be A's solo time only roughly, not the combined time. Efficiency ratio A : B = 13 : 10, work = 230 units, combined rate 23, so the answer is 10 days.
Source: PYQ-style

82. 3 men and 4 women can complete a work in 14 days, while 4 men and 6 women can complete the same work in 10 days. In how many days will 6 men and 8 women complete the work?
(a) 5 days (b) 7 days (c) 6 days (d) 8 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: Solving the full man-woman equations is unnecessary here and averaging 14 and 10 to get 12 is wrong. Since 6 men and 8 women is exactly double the group of 3 men and 4 women, the time halves, so the answer is 7 days.
Source: PYQ-style

83. A and B can do a piece of work in 24 days and 30 days respectively. They work together for 8 days, then A leaves. B is joined by C and they complete the remaining work in 6 days. In how many days can C alone do the whole work?
(a) 24 days (b) 30 days (c) 36 days (d) 40 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: Assuming C works at the same rate as B is the common trap. Work = 120 units, A = 5, B = 4; in 8 days they do 72 units, leaving 48 units done by B and C in 6 days at 8 units/day, so C's rate is 4 and the answer is 30 days.
Source: PYQ-style

84. A, B and C alone can do a work in 10 days, 15 days and 20 days respectively. They work together and complete the work. If the total wages paid are ₹2600, what is C's share?
(a) ₹400 (b) ₹500 (c) ₹600 (d) ₹800
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dividing ₹2600 equally or in the ratio 10 : 15 : 20 is wrong — wages follow efficiency, not time. Efficiency ratio = 1/10 : 1/15 : 1/20 = 6 : 4 : 3, so C gets (3/13) × 2600, and the answer is ₹600.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

85. 10 men can do a piece of work in 12 days. How many men are needed to complete the same work in 8 days?
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 15 (d) 18
Answer: (c)
Solution: Using direct proportion (10 × 8/12) gives about 7 men, which is wrong because men and days vary inversely. By M₁D₁ = M₂D₂, M₂ = 10 × 12/8, so the answer is 15 men.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

86. A can do a work in 10 days and B in 15 days. With the help of C, they complete the work in 4 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 12 days (b) 10 days (c) 15 days (d) 20 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Subtracting 4 from 10 or 15 gives meaningless answers; rates must be compared. Work = 30 units, A = 3, B = 2, and all three together = 30/4 = 7.5 units/day, so C = 2.5 units/day and the answer is 12 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

87. A and B can do a work in 30 days, B and C in 40 days, and A and C in 60 days. In how many days can A alone do the work?
(a) 60 days (b) 70 days (c) 80 days (d) 90 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Taking the average of 30, 40 and 60 is a trap. Adding the three pair-rates gives 2(A+B+C) = 1/30 + 1/40 + 1/60 = 3/40, so A+B+C = 3/80; subtracting B+C = 1/40 gives A = 1/80, so the answer is 80 days.
Source: PYQ-style

88. A is 40% less efficient than B. If B alone can complete a work in 16 days, in how many days will A and B together complete it?
(a) 8 days (b) 9 days (c) 10 days (d) 12 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: Halving 16 days to 8 days ignores that A is slower than B. Efficiency ratio A : B = 60 : 100 = 3 : 5, work = 5 × 16 = 80 units, combined rate 8, so the answer is 10 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

89. 12 men can do a work in 15 days. After working for 6 days, 4 men leave. In how many more days will the remaining men complete the work?
(a) 12 days (b) 13.5 days (c) 14 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: Using 12 men for the remaining work gives 9 days, and assuming the leftover time stays 9 days is the trap. Work = 180 man-days, 72 done, 108 left with 8 men, so the answer is 13.5 days.
Source: PYQ-style

90. A can do a work in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work on alternate days, starting with B. In how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 14.5 days (b) 14 days (c) 15 days (d) 15.5 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Starting with A instead of B changes the last step and gives 14⅓ days, which is the trap. Work = 36 units, A = 3, B = 2; each 2-day cycle completes 5 units, so 7 cycles (14 days) finish 35 units, and B does the last 1 unit in half a day, so the answer is 14.5 days.
Source: PYQ-style

91. A can do a certain work in 20 days. B is 25% more efficient than A. In how many days will B alone do the same work?
(a) 15 days (b) 22 days (c) 18 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Reducing 20 days by 25% gives 15 days, which is wrong because efficiency and time are inversely, not directly, related. Efficiency ratio A : B = 4 : 5, so time ratio is 5 : 4, and the answer is 16 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

92. A and B together can do a work in 12 days. A alone can do it in 20 days. They work together for 6 days, then B leaves. In how many days will A alone complete the remaining work?
(a) 12 days (b) 10 days (c) 14 days (d) 16 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: Taking half of 20 days because half the time has passed is the trap. Work = 60 units, A+B = 5 units/day, A = 3 units/day; in 6 days they finish 30 units, leaving 30 units for A, so the answer is 10 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

93. 5 men or 10 women can do a piece of work in 12 days. In how many days will 8 men and 4 women complete the same work?
(a) 6 days (b) 4 days (c) 5 days (d) 3 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Adding 8 + 4 = 12 workers and comparing with 12 days directly is wrong because men and women have different rates. Since 5M = 10W, M = 2W; work = 120W and 8M + 4W = 20W, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

94. A, B and C can do a piece of work in 10 days, 15 days and 20 days respectively. They all start together. After 2 days A leaves; B and C continue for 3 more days, and then A rejoins and the work is finished. In how many total days is the work done?
(a) 9 days (b) 7 days (c) 8 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Assuming the work ends exactly when A rejoins is the trap. Work = 60 units, A = 6, B = 4, C = 3; first 2 days give 26 units, next 3 days give 21 units, leaving 13 units done by all three in 1 day, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: PYQ-style

95. A can do a work in 25 days and B in 20 days. They began together, but A left after some days and B completed the remaining work by working alone for 11 days. After how many days from the start did A leave?
(a) 2 days (b) 3 days (c) 4 days (d) 5 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: Subtracting 11 from 20 or 25 gives wrong guesses. Work = 100 units, A = 4, B = 5; B alone does 55 units in 11 days, leaving 45 units done together at 9 units/day, so the answer is 5 days.
Source: PYQ-style

96. A can do a work in 18 days and B in 24 days. With the help of C, they finish the work in 8 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 24 days (b) 36 days (c) 30 days (d) 40 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: Taking the average of 18 and 24 is a trap. Work = 72 units, A = 4, B = 3, and all three = 72/8 = 9 units/day, so C = 2 units/day and the answer is 36 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

97. A is twice as efficient as B. Working together they can finish a work in 14 days. In how many days can B alone finish it?
(a) 42 days (b) 28 days (c) 35 days (d) 21 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Doubling 14 to 28 days gives A's share of the logic wrong — 28 days would be A's solo time only if A were the slower one. Efficiency ratio A : B = 2 : 1, work = 3 × 14 = 42 units, B's rate is 1, so the answer is 42 days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

98. 15 men can do a work in 20 days. After working for 8 days, 5 men leave. In how many more days will the remaining men complete the work?
(a) 18 days (b) 16 days (c) 15 days (d) 20 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: Keeping the remaining 12 days unchanged is the trap because the workforce has shrunk. Work = 300 man-days, 120 done in 8 days, leaving 180 man-days for 10 men, so the answer is 18 days.
Source: PYQ-style

99. A completes 2/3 of a work in 10 days, and B completes 3/5 of the same work in 6 days. In how many days will they complete the whole work together?
(a) 8 days (b) 6 days (c) 9 days (d) 10 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: Adding 10 + 6 = 16 days or averaging them is the trap. A's full time = 10 × 3/2 = 15 days and B's full time = 6 × 5/3 = 10 days; combined rate = 1/15 + 1/10 = 1/6, so the answer is 6 days.
Source: PYQ-style

100. A can do a work in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work together and are paid ₹1500 in total. What is A's share of the wages?
(a) ₹600 (b) ₹750 (c) ₹1000 (d) ₹900
Answer: (d)
Solution: Splitting ₹1500 equally as ₹750 each is the trap because wages follow work done, not headcount. Efficiency ratio A : B = 1/12 : 1/18 = 3 : 2, so A gets (3/5) × 1500, and the answer is ₹900.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
