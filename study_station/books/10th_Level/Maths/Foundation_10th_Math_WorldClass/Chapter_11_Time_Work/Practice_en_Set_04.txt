Q76. A and B together can do a work in 15 days. B alone can do it in 20 days. In how many days can A alone do it?
(a) 30 days (b) 40 days (c) 50 days (d) 60 days

Answer: (d) 60 days

Solution:
A+B 1-day work = 1/15, B's 1-day work = 1/20.
A's 1-day work = 1/15 – 1/20 = (4 – 3)/60 = 1/60.
A takes 60 days.
Source: NCERT
