Set 6: Fractions & Decimals MCQs (Hard / Multi-step Exam Models)
Questions 126–150: Hard

126. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 200% and the denominator is increased by 300%, the resultant fraction is 9/20. What was the original fraction?
(a) 3/5 (b) 4/5 (c) 2/5 (d) 3/4
Answer: (a) 3/5
Solution: Increasing numerator by 200% makes it 300% = 3x. Increasing denominator by 300% makes it 400% = 4y. Equation: (3x)/(4y) = 9/20 ⇒ x/y = (9/20) × (4/3) = 3/5.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

127. If 1 is added to both the numerator and denominator of a fraction, it becomes 4/5. If 5 is subtracted from both, it becomes 1/2. Find the original fraction.
(a) 5/7 (b) 7/9 (c) 6/7 (d) 8/11
Answer: (b) 7/9
Solution: Equations: 5(x + 1) = 4(y + 1) ⇒ 5x – 4y = –1. And 2(x – 5) = y – 5 ⇒ y = 2x – 5. Substituting gives 5x – 4(2x – 5) = –1 ⇒ –3x + 20 = –1 ⇒ 3x = 21 ⇒ x = 7, y = 9. Fraction = 7/9.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

128. A student was asked to find 3/14 of a certain sum of money. By mistake, he calculated 3/4 of it. If his answer was ₹150 more than the correct answer, find the sum.
(a) ₹240 (b) ₹260 (c) ₹280 (d) ₹320
Answer: (c) ₹280
Solution: Psychological Trap: Dividing ₹150 directly by 3/14 or 3/4 is incorrect. Difference between fractions: 3/4 – 3/14 = (21 – 6)/28 = 15/28. Given (15/28) × Sum = 150 ⇒ Sum = 150 × 28/15 = ₹280.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

129. Evaluate: (0.05³ + 0.04³) / (0.05² – 0.05 × 0.04 + 0.04²)
(a) 0.01 (b) 0.90 (c) 0.009 (d) 0.09
Answer: (d) 0.09
Solution: Using identity (a³ + b³) / (a² – ab + b²) = a + b. Here a = 0.05 and b = 0.04, so sum = 0.05 + 0.04 = 0.09.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

130. If x + 1/x = 5/2, find the value of x where x > 1.
(a) 2 (b) 5/2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Equation: 2x² – 5x + 2 = 0 ⇒ (2x – 1)(x – 2) = 0. Since x > 1, x = 2.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

131. Find the product of the fractional equivalents of 0.75, 0.8, and 0.625.
(a) 1/4 (b) 3/8 (c) 5/16 (d) 1/2
Answer: (b) 3/8
Solution: Fraction equivalents: 0.75 = 3/4, 0.8 = 4/5, and 0.625 = 5/8. Product: (3/4) × (4/5) × (5/8) = 3/8 = 0.375.
Source: NCERT Class 7

132. A father leaves 1/2 of his estate to his wife, 1/3 to his son, and the remaining ₹50,000 to his daughter. What was the total value of his estate?
(a) ₹2,50,000 (b) ₹2,00,000 (c) ₹3,00,000 (d) ₹3,50,000
Answer: (c) ₹3,00,000
Solution: Fraction distributed: 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6. Daughter's share: 1 – 5/6 = 1/6. Given (1/6) × Total = 50,000 ⇒ Total = ₹3,00,000.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

133. Simplify the continued fraction: 1 / [ 1 + 1 / ( 1 + 1 / ( 1 + 1/2 ) ) ]
(a) 5/8 (b) 8/5 (c) 5/13 (d) 8/13
Answer: (d) 8/13
Solution: Bottom-up: 1 + 1/2 = 3/2 ⇒ 2/3. 1 + 2/3 = 5/3 ⇒ 3/5. 1 + 3/5 = 8/5. Finally: 1 / (1 + 5/8) = 1 / (13/8) = 8/13.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

134. Which of the following numbers is the greatest: (0.5)², √0.49, ∛0.008, 0.23?
(a) √0.49 (b) (0.5)² (c) ∛0.008 (d) 0.23
Answer: (a) √0.49
Solution: Calculate values: (0.5)² = 0.25, √0.49 = 0.70, ∛0.008 = 0.20, and 0.23. The greatest is √0.49 = 0.70.
Source: NCERT Class 8

135. If the sum of a fraction and its reciprocal is 25/12, what is the fraction?
(a) 2/3 (b) 4/3 (c) 5/6 (d) 3/5
Answer: (b) 4/3
Solution: Let the fraction be x. Then x + 1/x = 25/12 ⇒ 12x² – 25x + 12 = 0 ⇒ (4x – 3)(3x – 4) = 0. The fraction is 4/3 (or 3/4).
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

136. Evaluate: (1.5³ + 4.7³ + 3.8³ – 3 × 1.5 × 4.7 × 3.8) / (1.5² + 4.7² + 3.8² – 1.5×4.7 – 4.7×3.8 – 3.8×1.5)
(a) 8.5 (b) 9.0 (c) 10.0 (d) 11.2
Answer: (c) 10.0
Solution: Using identity (a³ + b³ + c³ – 3abc) / (a² + b² + c² – ab – bc – ca) = a + b + c. Sum = 1.5 + 4.7 + 3.8 = 10.0.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

137. In a fruit basket, 1/3 of the fruits are apples, 2/5 are bananas, and the remaining 16 fruits are oranges. How many total fruits are in the basket?
(a) 45 (b) 50 (c) 54 (d) 60
Answer: (d) 60
Solution: Apples and bananas = 1/3 + 2/5 = 11/15. Oranges fraction = 1 – 11/15 = 4/15. Given (4/15) × Total = 16 ⇒ Total = 16 × 15/4 = 60.
Source: RRB Group D Pattern

138. Simplify: (0.356² – 0.144²) / (0.356 – 0.144)
(a) 0.5 (b) 0.212 (c) 0.4 (d) 0.55
Answer: (a) 0.5
Solution: Using identity (a² – b²) / (a – b) = a + b. Here 0.356 + 0.144 = 0.500 = 0.5.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

139. If 3/x + 4/y = 1 and 2/x + 3/y = 7/10, find the value of x.
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 10
Answer: (b) 5
Solution: Let u = 1/x and v = 1/y. 3u + 4v = 1 and 2u + 3v = 0.7. Multiplying by 3 and 4 gives v = 0.1 ⇒ y = 10, and u = 0.2 ⇒ x = 1/0.2 = 5.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

140. Find the sum: 1/(2×4) + 1/(4×6) + 1/(6×8) + 1/(8×10) + 1/(10×12)
(a) 1/6 (b) 5/12 (c) 5/24 (d) 7/24
Answer: (c) 5/24
Solution: Common difference is 2: (1/2) × (1/2 – 1/12) = (1/2) × (5/12) = 5/24.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

141. A piece of cloth was 15 metres long. A tailor cut 3 pieces of length 2 1/4 metres each and 2 pieces of length 1 7/8 metres each. What length of cloth remains?
(a) 3.5 metres (b) 5 metres (c) 4 metres (d) 4.5 metres
Answer: (d) 4.5 metres
Solution: Length cut: 3 × 9/4 + 2 × 15/8 = 27/4 + 15/4 = 42/4 = 10.5 metres. Remaining: 15 – 10.5 = 4.5 metres.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

142. What is the recurring decimal representation of the fraction 5/11?
(a) 0.45̄ (b) 0.4̄5 (c) 0.54̄ (d) 0.455̄
Answer: (a) 0.45̄
Solution: Dividing 5 by 11: 5 ÷ 11 = 0.454545... = 0.45̄ (bar on both 4 and 5).
Source: NCERT Class 7

143. If x/y = 3/4, find the value of (2x + 3y) / (3x – 2y).
(a) 12 (b) 18 (c) 15 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 18
Solution: Substitute x = 3 and y = 4: (2×3 + 3×4) / (3×3 – 2×4) = (6 + 12) / (9 – 8) = 18 / 1 = 18.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

144. If 2/5 of a cistern is filled in 1 minute, how much more time is needed to fill the rest of the cistern?
(a) 1 min (b) 2 min (c) 1 min 30 sec (d) 2 min 30 sec
Answer: (c) 1 min 30 sec
Solution: Remaining fraction = 1 – 2/5 = 3/5. Rate = 2/5 per minute. Additional time = (3/5) ÷ (2/5) = 3/2 minutes = 1.5 minutes = 1 minute 30 seconds.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

145. Evaluate: (0.125 + 0.027) / (0.25 – 0.15 + 0.09)
(a) 0.2 (b) 0.5 (c) 0.6 (d) 0.8
Answer: (d) 0.8
Solution: Note that 0.125 = 0.5³ and 0.027 = 0.3³. Expression is (a³ + b³) / (a² – ab + b²) = a + b = 0.5 + 0.3 = 0.8.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

146. If 3/4 of 4/5 of 5/6 of a number is 24, find the number.
(a) 48 (b) 36 (c) 60 (d) 72
Answer: (a) 48
Solution: Multiply fractions: (3/4) × (4/5) × (5/6) = 3/6 = 1/2. So (1/2) × Number = 24 ⇒ Number = 48.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

147. Express 0.235̄ (bar on 35 only) as a fraction in simplest form.
(a) 235/999 (b) 233/990 (c) 233/900 (d) 235/990
Answer: (b) 233/990
Solution: Formula: (235 – 2)/990 = 233/990.
Source: NCERT Class 9

148. Evaluate the product: (1 – 1/2²)(1 – 1/3²)(1 – 1/4²)...(1 – 1/10²)
(a) 1/2 (b) 9/20 (c) 11/20 (d) 10/21
Answer: (c) 11/20
Solution: Psychological Trap: Trying to find common denominators of squares directly leads to messy arithmetic. Factor each term using a² – b²: (1 – 1/n)(1 + 1/n). The product splits into: [1/2 × 2/3 × ... × 9/10] × [3/2 × 4/3 × ... × 11/10] = (1/10) × (11/2) = 11/20.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

149. If 1/3 of a number plus 1/4 of the same number equals 28, find the number.
(a) 36 (b) 42 (c) 56 (d) 48
Answer: (d) 48
Solution: (1/3 + 1/4)x = 28 ⇒ (7/12)x = 28 ⇒ x = 28 × 12/7 = 48.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

150. Simplify the continued fraction: 1 / [ 2 + 1 / ( 3 + 1 / ( 1 + 1/4 ) ) ]
(a) 15/43 (b) 19/43 (c) 21/43 (d) 17/43
Answer: (b) 19/43
Solution: Bottom-up: 1 + 1/4 = 5/4 ⇒ 4/5. 3 + 4/5 = 19/5 ⇒ 5/19. 2 + 5/19 = 43/19. Finally: 1 / (43/19) = 19/43.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
