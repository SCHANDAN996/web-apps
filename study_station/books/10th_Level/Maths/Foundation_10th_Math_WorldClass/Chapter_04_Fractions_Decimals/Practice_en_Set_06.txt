Set 6: Fractions & Decimals MCQs (Hard / Multi-step Exam Models)
Questions 126–150: Hard

126. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 200% and the denominator is increased by 300%, the resultant fraction is 9/20. What was the original fraction?
(a) 3/5 (b) 4/5 (c) 2/5 (d) 3/4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating "increased by 200%" as "becomes 200%" — it becomes 300% = 3x, and the denominator becomes 400% = 4y. Then (3x)/(4y) = 9/20 gives x/y = (9/20) × (4/3) = 3/5, so the original fraction is 3/5.
Source: PYQ-style

127. If 1 is added to both the numerator and denominator of a fraction, it becomes 4/5. If 5 is subtracted from both, it becomes 1/2. Find the original fraction.
(a) 5/7 (b) 7/9 (c) 6/7 (d) 8/11
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is testing only one condition; the fraction must satisfy both. From 5(x + 1) = 4(y + 1) we get 5x – 4y = –1, and from 2(x – 5) = y – 5 we get y = 2x – 5. Substituting: –3x + 20 = –1, so x = 7, y = 9, and the fraction is 7/9.
Source: PYQ-style

128. A student was asked to find 3/14 of a certain sum of money. By mistake, he calculated 3/4 of it. If his answer was ₹150 more than the correct answer, find the sum.
(a) ₹280 (b) ₹260 (c) ₹240 (d) ₹320
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is dividing ₹150 by 3/4 or 3/14 directly; ₹150 is the difference of the two calculations. Difference of fractions: 3/4 – 3/14 = (21 – 6)/28 = 15/28, so (15/28) × Sum = 150, giving Sum = 150 × 28/15 = ₹280.
Source: PYQ-style

129. Evaluate: (0.05³ + 0.04³) / (0.05² – 0.05 × 0.04 + 0.04²)
(a) 0.09 (b) 0.90 (c) 0.009 (d) 0.01
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is cubing and squaring the decimals separately, which invites place-value errors like 0.009. Use the identity (a³ + b³)/(a² – ab + b²) = a + b with a = 0.05, b = 0.04, so the value is 0.05 + 0.04 = 0.09.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

130. If x + 1/x = 5/2, find the value of x where x > 1.
(a) 4 (b) 5/2 (c) 3 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is picking 5/2 because it appears in the equation; it is the sum, not x. Multiplying by 2x gives 2x² – 5x + 2 = 0, i.e. (2x – 1)(x – 2) = 0, and since x > 1, the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

131. Find the product of the fractional equivalents of 0.75, 0.8, and 0.625.
(a) 1/4 (b) 3/8 (c) 5/16 (d) 1/2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is converting 0.625 wrongly as 3/5; it is 5/8. Product: (3/4) × (4/5) × (5/8) = 3/8 after cancellation, so the answer is 3/8.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

132. A father leaves 1/2 of his estate to his wife, 1/3 to his son, and the remaining ₹50,000 to his daughter. What was the total value of his estate?
(a) ₹2,50,000 (b) ₹2,00,000 (c) ₹3,00,000 (d) ₹3,50,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is equating ₹50,000 to 1/2 or 1/3 of the estate; it is the leftover fraction. Distributed fraction = 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6, so the daughter gets 1/6, and Total = 50,000 × 6 = ₹3,00,000.
Source: PYQ-style

133. Simplify the continued fraction: 1 / [ 1 + 1 / ( 1 + 1 / ( 1 + 1/2 ) ) ]
(a) 8/5 (b) 5/8 (c) 5/13 (d) 8/13
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is stopping one step early or inverting at the end, which yields 8/5. Work bottom-up: 1 + 1/2 = 3/2, invert to 2/3; 1 + 2/3 = 5/3, invert to 3/5; 1 + 3/5 = 8/5, invert to 5/8, so the value is 5/8.
Source: PYQ-style

134. Which of the following numbers is the greatest: (0.5)², √0.49, ∛0.008, 0.23?
(a) √0.49 (b) (0.5)² (c) ∛0.008 (d) 0.23
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming squaring increases a number; for decimals below 1 it shrinks, so (0.5)² = 0.25. Values: √0.49 = 0.70, ∛0.008 = 0.20, and 0.23, so the greatest is √0.49 = 0.70.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

135. If the sum of a fraction and its reciprocal is 25/12, what is the fraction?
(a) 2/3 (b) 4/3 (c) 5/6 (d) 3/5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is choosing a fraction whose sum with its reciprocal is not checked; only 4/3 gives 4/3 + 3/4 = 25/12. Solving 12x² – 25x + 12 = 0 gives (4x – 3)(3x – 4) = 0, so the fraction is 4/3 (or equivalently 3/4), and among the options the answer is 4/3.
Source: PYQ-style

136. Evaluate: (1.5³ + 4.7³ + 3.8³ – 3 × 1.5 × 4.7 × 3.8) / (1.5² + 4.7² + 3.8² – 1.5×4.7 – 4.7×3.8 – 3.8×1.5)
(a) 8.5 (b) 10.0 (c) 9.0 (d) 11.2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing the cubes directly, which is long and error-prone. The expression matches (a³ + b³ + c³ – 3abc)/(a² + b² + c² – ab – bc – ca) = a + b + c, so the value is 1.5 + 4.7 + 3.8 = 10.0.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

137. In a fruit basket, 1/3 of the fruits are apples, 2/5 are bananas, and the remaining 16 fruits are oranges. How many total fruits are in the basket?
(a) 45 (b) 50 (c) 60 (d) 54
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding 1/3 + 2/5 incorrectly as 3/8; with LCM 15 it is 11/15. Oranges are 1 – 11/15 = 4/15 of the total, so Total = 16 × 15/4 = 60 fruits.
Source: PYQ-style

138. Simplify: (0.356² – 0.144²) / (0.356 – 0.144)
(a) 0.55 (b) 0.212 (c) 0.4 (d) 0.5
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is cancelling the denominator wrongly and reporting a – b = 0.212. Using (a² – b²)/(a – b) = a + b, the value is 0.356 + 0.144 = 0.5.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

139. If 3/x + 4/y = 1 and 2/x + 3/y = 7/10, find the value of x.
(a) 5 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 10
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is reporting y = 10 instead of x, since the elimination finds v first. Put u = 1/x, v = 1/y: 3u + 4v = 1 and 2u + 3v = 0.7 give v = 0.1 and u = 0.2, so x = 1/0.2 = 5.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Find the sum: 1/(2×4) + 1/(4×6) + 1/(6×8) + 1/(8×10) + 1/(10×12)
(a) 1/6 (b) 5/12 (c) 5/24 (d) 7/24
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is forgetting the factor 1/(common difference) = 1/2, which gives the wrong answer 5/12. Telescoping: (1/2) × (1/2 – 1/12) = (1/2) × (5/12) = 5/24, so the sum is 5/24.
Source: PYQ-style

141. A piece of cloth was 15 metres long. A tailor cut 3 pieces of length 2 1/4 metres each and 2 pieces of length 1 7/8 metres each. What length of cloth remains?
(a) 3.5 metres (b) 5 metres (c) 4.5 metres (d) 4 metres
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is mishandling the mixed fractions, e.g. reading 1 7/8 as 17/8 instead of 15/8. Length cut = 3 × 9/4 + 2 × 15/8 = 27/4 + 15/4 = 42/4 = 10.5 m, so the remaining length is 15 – 10.5 = 4.5 metres.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

142. What is the recurring decimal representation of the fraction 5/11?
(a) 0.45̄ (b) 0.4̄5 (c) 0.54̄ (d) 0.455̄
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is placing the bar on only one digit; both digits of the cycle repeat. Dividing 5 by 11 gives 0.454545…, so the representation is 0.45̄ with the bar over both 4 and 5.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

143. If x/y = 3/4, find the value of (2x + 3y) / (3x – 2y).
(a) 12 (b) 9 (c) 15 (d) 18
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is substituting x = 4, y = 3 (reversing the ratio), which changes the denominator. With x = 3, y = 4: (6 + 12)/(9 – 8) = 18/1, so the value is 18.
Source: PYQ-style

144. If 2/5 of a cistern is filled in 1 minute, how much more time is needed to fill the rest of the cistern?
(a) 1 min (b) 2 min (c) 1 min 30 sec (d) 2 min 30 sec
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing the time for the whole cistern (2.5 min) instead of the remaining part. Remaining fraction = 3/5 at a rate of 2/5 per minute, so extra time = (3/5) ÷ (2/5) = 3/2 minutes = 1 minute 30 seconds.
Source: PYQ-style

145. Evaluate: (0.125 + 0.027) / (0.25 – 0.15 + 0.09)
(a) 0.2 (b) 0.5 (c) 0.6 (d) 0.8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding numerator and denominator as plain decimals and dividing 0.152 by 0.19 approximately. Recognise 0.125 = 0.5³ and 0.027 = 0.3³, so the expression is (a³ + b³)/(a² – ab + b²) = a + b = 0.5 + 0.3 = 0.8.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

146. If 3/4 of 4/5 of 5/6 of a number is 24, find the number.
(a) 72 (b) 36 (c) 60 (d) 48
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is multiplying 24 by 1/2 instead of dividing by it. The fractions cancel: (3/4) × (4/5) × (5/6) = 1/2, so (1/2) × Number = 24 and the number is 48.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

147. Express 0.235̄ (bar on 35 only) as a fraction in simplest form.
(a) 235/999 (b) 233/990 (c) 233/900 (d) 235/990
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using 999 in the denominator, which applies only when all three digits repeat. With one non-repeating and two repeating digits: (235 – 2)/990 = 233/990, so the fraction is 233/990.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

148. Evaluate the product: (1 – 1/2²)(1 – 1/3²)(1 – 1/4²)...(1 – 1/10²)
(a) 11/20 (b) 9/20 (c) 1/2 (d) 10/21
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is multiplying numerators and denominators directly, which becomes unmanageable. Factor each term as (1 – 1/n)(1 + 1/n); the product telescopes to (1/2 × 2/3 × … × 9/10) × (3/2 × 4/3 × … × 11/10) = (1/10) × (11/2) = 11/20, so the answer is 11/20.
Source: PYQ-style

149. If 1/3 of a number plus 1/4 of the same number equals 28, find the number.
(a) 36 (b) 42 (c) 56 (d) 48
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding 1/3 + 1/4 as 2/7; with LCM 12 it is 7/12. Then (7/12)x = 28 gives x = 28 × 12/7 = 48, so the number is 48.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

150. Simplify the continued fraction: 1 / [ 2 + 1 / ( 3 + 1 / ( 1 + 1/4 ) ) ]
(a) 15/43 (b) 21/43 (c) 19/43 (d) 17/43
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is an arithmetic slip in the middle step, e.g. writing 3 + 4/5 as 17/5 instead of 19/5. Bottom-up: 1 + 1/4 = 5/4, invert to 4/5; 3 + 4/5 = 19/5, invert to 5/19; 2 + 5/19 = 43/19, invert to 19/43, so the value is 19/43.
Source: PYQ-style
