26. Simplify: 3/4 + 5/6 – 7/12
(a) 1 (b) 11/12 (c) 5/6 (d) 2/3
Answer: (a) 1
Solution: LCM of 4, 6, and 12 is 12. Convert fractions: (9 + 10 – 7)/12 = 12/12 = 1.
Source: NCERT Class 6

27. Evaluate: 2 1/3 × 1 2/7 ÷ 1 1/2
(a) 3/2 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 5/2
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: Convert to improper fractions: 7/3 × 9/7 ÷ 3/2. Multiply first: 7/3 × 9/7 = 3. Then divide: 3 ÷ 3/2 = 3 × 2/3 = 2.
Source: NCERT Class 7

28. Simplify: 0.125 ÷ 0.5 × 0.4
(a) 0.01 (b) 0.04 (c) 0.1 (d) 0.25
Answer: (c) 0.1
Solution: Left to right: 0.125 ÷ 0.5 = 0.25. Then 0.25 × 0.4 = 0.100 = 0.1.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

29. Find the value of: (3.6 × 0.48) / (0.12 × 0.09)
(a) 80 (b) 120 (c) 140 (d) 160
Answer: (d) 160
Solution: Separate factors: (3.6 / 0.09) × (0.48 / 0.12) = 40 × 4 = 160.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. Evaluate: (1/2 + 2/3) ÷ (3/4 – 1/6)
(a) 2 (b) 7/6 (c) 3/2 (d) 12/7
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: Numerator: 1/2 + 2/3 = 7/6. Denominator: 3/4 – 1/6 = 9/12 – 2/12 = 7/12. Division: 7/6 ÷ 7/12 = 7/6 × 12/7 = 2.
Source: NCERT Class 7

31. Simplify: 0.008 ÷ 0.04 + 0.5 × 0.2
(a) 0.2 (b) 0.3 (c) 0.25 (d) 0.4
Answer: (b) 0.3
Solution: Division: 0.008 ÷ 0.04 = 0.2. Multiplication: 0.5 × 0.2 = 0.1. Sum: 0.2 + 0.1 = 0.3.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

32. Simplify: 5 1/4 – 3 2/3 + 1 1/6
(a) 2 1/4 (b) 3 1/4 (c) 2 3/4 (d) 2 1/2
Answer: (c) 2 3/4
Solution: Improper fractions: 21/4 – 11/3 + 7/6. LCM of 4, 3, 6 is 12: (63 – 44 + 14)/12 = 33/12 = 11/4 = 2 3/4.
Source: NCERT Class 7

33. Evaluate: 0.35 × 0.02 ÷ 0.007
(a) 0.01 (b) 0.1 (c) 10 (d) 1
Answer: (d) 1
Solution: Multiply: 0.35 × 0.02 = 0.0070. Divide: 0.007 / 0.007 = 1.
Source: RRB Group D Pattern

34. Simplify: (4/5 of 25/16) ÷ 5/8
(a) 2 (b) 5/4 (c) 1 (d) 4/5
Answer: (a) 2
Solution: 'Of' first: 4/5 × 25/16 = 5/4. Division: 5/4 ÷ 5/8 = 5/4 × 8/5 = 2.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

35. Evaluate: 0.75 + 1.25 ÷ 0.5 – 0.25 × 4
(a) 2.50 (b) 2.25 (c) 3.00 (d) 1.75
Answer: (b) 2.25
Solution: Division & multiplication first: 1.25 ÷ 0.5 = 2.5, and 0.25 × 4 = 1. Expression: 0.75 + 2.5 – 1 = 3.25 – 1 = 2.25.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

36. Simplify: (3/8 + 1/4) of 16/5 – 1/2
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3/2 (d) 5/4
Answer: (c) 3/2
Solution: Bracket sum: 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8. 'Of': 5/8 × 16/5 = 2. Subtraction: 2 – 1/2 = 3/2.
Source: NCERT Class 7

37. Evaluate: 1.08 ÷ 0.12 × 0.5 – 1.5
(a) 1.5 (b) 2.5 (c) 4.5 (d) 3.0
Answer: (d) 3.0
Solution: Left to right: 1.08 ÷ 0.12 = 9. Multiply: 9 × 0.5 = 4.5. Subtract: 4.5 – 1.5 = 3.0.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

38. Simplify: (2/3 ÷ 4/9) × (5/6 ÷ 10/3)
(a) 3/8 (b) 1/2 (c) 3/4 (d) 5/8
Answer: (a) 3/8
Solution: First bracket: 2/3 × 9/4 = 3/2. Second bracket: 5/6 × 3/10 = 1/4. Product: 3/2 × 1/4 = 3/8.
Source: NCERT Class 7

39. Find the value of: 0.064 ÷ 0.008 + 0.16 ÷ 0.04
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: First division: 0.064 ÷ 0.008 = 8. Second division: 0.16 ÷ 0.04 = 4. Sum: 8 + 4 = 12.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

40. Simplify: (3 1/2 + 2 1/4) ÷ (1 1/2 – 1/4)
(a) 4 1/5 (b) 5 1/5 (c) 4 3/5 (d) 5
Answer: (c) 4 3/5
Solution: Numerator: 7/2 + 9/4 = 23/4. Denominator: 3/2 – 1/4 = 5/4. Division: (23/4) ÷ (5/4) = 23/5 = 4 3/5.
Source: NCERT Class 7

41. Calculate: 0.6 × 0.6 + 0.4 × 0.4 + 2 × 0.6 × 0.4
(a) 0.8 (b) 0.96 (c) 1.2 (d) 1.0
Answer: (d) 1.0
Solution: Using identity a² + b² + 2ab = (a + b)². Here (0.6 + 0.4)² = 1.0² = 1.0.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

42. Evaluate: (5/7 of 2.8) + (3/4 of 1.6)
(a) 3.2 (b) 2.8 (c) 3.6 (d) 4.0
Answer: (a) 3.2
Solution: First part: 5/7 × 2.8 = 5 × 0.4 = 2.0. Second part: 3/4 × 1.6 = 3 × 0.4 = 1.2. Sum: 2.0 + 1.2 = 3.2.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

43. Find the value of: (0.85 × 0.85 – 0.15 × 0.15) / (0.85 + 0.15)
(a) 0.6 (b) 0.7 (c) 0.8 (d) 1.0
Answer: (b) 0.7
Solution: Using identity (a² – b²) / (a + b) = a – b. Here 0.85 – 0.15 = 0.70 = 0.7.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

44. Evaluate: 3/5 ÷ 3/5 of 3/5
(a) 1 (b) 3/5 (c) 1 2/3 (d) 9/25
Answer: (c) 1 2/3
Solution: Common Trap: Dividing first gives (3/5 ÷ 3/5) = 1, then 1 of 3/5 = 3/5 (incorrect). In BODMAS, "Of" takes precedence over division: 3/5 of 3/5 = 9/25, then 3/5 ÷ 9/25 = 3/5 × 25/9 = 5/3 = 1 2/3.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

45. Find the product: 0.02 × 0.03 × 0.04 = ?
(a) 0.0024 (b) 0.00024 (c) 0.024 (d) 0.000024
Answer: (d) 0.000024
Solution: Multiply numbers: 2 × 3 × 4 = 24. Total decimal places = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 places. Product = 0.000024.
Source: NCERT Class 6

46. Evaluate: (1/3 + 1/4 + 1/6) ÷ (1/2 – 1/3)
(a) 4.5 (b) 3.5 (c) 5.0 (d) 4.0
Answer: (a) 4.5
Solution: Numerator: (4 + 3 + 2)/12 = 9/12 = 3/4. Denominator: 1/2 – 1/3 = 1/6. Division: 3/4 ÷ 1/6 = 3/4 × 6 = 9/2 = 4.5.
Source: Banking (IBPS) Pattern

47. Simplify: 0.375 ÷ 0.025 + 0.5 ÷ 0.05
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 15
Answer: (b) 25
Solution: First division: 0.375 ÷ 0.025 = 15. Second division: 0.5 ÷ 0.05 = 10. Sum: 15 + 10 = 25.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

48. Evaluate: 4 1/5 ÷ 2 1/10 × 1/2
(a) 2 (b) 1/2 (c) 1 (d) 4
Answer: (c) 1
Solution: Improper fractions: 21/5 ÷ 21/10 × 1/2. Left to right: 21/5 × 10/21 = 2. Then 2 × 1/2 = 1.
Source: NCERT Class 7

49. Simplify: (0.5³ + 0.3³) / (0.5² – 0.5 × 0.3 + 0.3²)
(a) 0.2 (b) 0.5 (c) 0.6 (d) 0.8
Answer: (d) 0.8
Solution: Using identity (a³ + b³) / (a² – ab + b²) = a + b. Here a = 0.5 and b = 0.3, so sum = 0.5 + 0.3 = 0.8.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

50. Evaluate: 1/2 ÷ (1/2 of 1/2)
(a) 1/2 (b) 2 (c) 1/4 (d) 1
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: Psychological Trap: Performing division first gives (1/2 ÷ 1/2) = 1, then 1 of 1/2 = 1/2 (incorrect). Bracket with "Of" must be evaluated first: 1/2 of 1/2 = 1/4. Then 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 2.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
