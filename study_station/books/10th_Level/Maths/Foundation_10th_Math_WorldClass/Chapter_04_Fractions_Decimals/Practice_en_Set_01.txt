Set 1: Fractions & Decimals MCQs (Easy to Medium)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which of the following is a proper fraction?
(a) 5/7 (b) 7/5 (c) 9/4 (d) 11/3
Answer: (a) 5/7
Solution: A proper fraction is one where numerator < denominator. Among the options, only 5/7 has numerator (5) less than denominator (7).
Source: NCERT Class 6

2. Convert 0.75 into a fraction in simplest form.
(a) 1/2 (b) 3/4 (c) 4/5 (d) 7/10
Answer: (b) 3/4
Solution: 0.75 = 75/100. Dividing both numerator and denominator by their HCF 25 gives 3/4.
Source: NCERT Class 7

3. What is the decimal representation of 5/8?
(a) 0.58 (b) 0.62 (c) 0.625 (d) 0.65
Answer: (c) 0.625
Solution: Dividing 5 by 8: 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625. Thus, the decimal form is 0.625.
Source: NCERT Class 7

4. Compare 2/3 and 3/4. Which fraction is strictly larger?
(a) Both are equal (b) 2/3 (c) Cannot be determined (d) 3/4
Answer: (d) 3/4
Solution: By cross-multiplication: 2 × 4 = 8 and 3 × 3 = 9. Since 9 > 8, 3/4 is strictly greater than 2/3.
Source: NCERT Class 6

5. Find the sum: 1/4 + 2/4 = ?
(a) 3/4 (b) 2/4 (c) 3/8 (d) 1/2
Answer: (a) 3/4
Solution: Like fractions have equal denominators: add the numerators directly: (1 + 2)/4 = 3/4.
Source: NCERT Class 6

6. If 3/5 of a number is 15, what is the number?
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 35
Answer: (b) 25
Solution: Let the number be x. Then (3/5)x = 15 ⇒ x = 15 × (5/3) = 5 × 5 = 25.
Source: SSC GD Pattern

7. Convert the mixed number 1 2/3 into an improper fraction.
(a) 4/3 (b) 7/3 (c) 5/3 (d) 3/5
Answer: (c) 5/3
Solution: Multiply whole by denominator and add numerator: 1 × 3 + 2 = 5. The denominator remains 3, giving 5/3.
Source: NCERT Class 6

8. Express 0.6 as a fraction in its lowest terms.
(a) 6/10 (b) 2/3 (c) 3/4 (d) 3/5
Answer: (d) 3/5
Solution: 0.6 = 6/10. Dividing numerator and denominator by 2 gives 3/5.
Source: NCERT Class 7

9. Which is the smallest fraction among 2/5, 3/7, and 4/9?
(a) 2/5 (b) 3/7 (c) 4/9 (d) All are equal
Answer: (a) 2/5
Solution: Converting to decimals: 2/5 = 0.400, 3/7 ≈ 0.428, and 4/9 ≈ 0.444. The smallest is 0.400, which is 2/5.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

10. Multiply and simplify: 2/3 × 9/4 = ?
(a) 1/2 (b) 3/2 (c) 4/3 (d) 2/3
Answer: (b) 3/2
Solution: Multiply numerators and denominators: (2 × 9) / (3 × 4) = 18/12. Dividing by 6 gives 3/2.
Source: NCERT Class 7

11. Calculate: 5 ÷ 1/3 = ?
(a) 5/3 (b) 1/15 (c) 15 (d) 8
Answer: (c) 15
Solution: Dividing by a fraction is multiplying by its reciprocal: 5 ÷ 1/3 = 5 × (3/1) = 15.
Source: NCERT Class 7

12. Find the sum: 0.2 + 0.02 + 0.002 = ?
(a) 0.6 (b) 0.24 (c) 0.0222 (d) 0.222
Answer: (d) 0.222
Solution: Aligning decimal points: 0.200 + 0.020 + 0.002 = 0.222.
Source: NCERT Class 6

13. Calculate the product: 0.4 × 0.5 = ?
(a) 0.2 (b) 2.0 (c) 0.02 (d) 0.002
Answer: (a) 0.2
Solution: Psychological Trap: Multiplying 4 × 5 = 20 and placing 1 decimal place gives 2.0 (incorrect). Since 0.4 and 0.5 each have 1 decimal place, the product must have 1 + 1 = 2 decimal places: 0.20 = 0.2.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

14. Evaluate: 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = ?
(a) 1/8 (b) 2 (c) 1/4 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: Common Trap: Multiplying numerators and denominators directly gives 1/8 (incorrect). In division of fractions, keep the first fraction and multiply by the reciprocal of the second: 1/2 × 4/1 = 2.
Source: SSC GD Pattern

15. A pizza is divided into 8 equal slices. If 3 slices are eaten, what fraction remains?
(a) 3/8 (b) 1/2 (c) 5/8 (d) 3/5
Answer: (c) 5/8
Solution: Total slices = 8. Slices left = 8 – 3 = 5. Fraction remaining = 5/8.
Source: NCERT Class 6

16. Express 25 paise as a fraction of one rupee.
(a) 1/2 (b) 1/5 (c) 1/10 (d) 1/4
Answer: (d) 1/4
Solution: 1 Rupee = 100 paise. Therefore, 25 paise / 100 paise = 25/100 = 1/4.
Source: NCERT Class 6

17. Find the product: 0.07 × 1000 = ?
(a) 70 (b) 7 (c) 700 (d) 0.7
Answer: (a) 70
Solution: Multiplying by 1000 shifts the decimal point 3 places to the right: 0.07 × 1000 = 70.0 = 70.
Source: NCERT Class 7

18. Which of the following is an improper fraction?
(a) 7/9 (b) 9/7 (c) 3/5 (d) 2/3
Answer: (b) 9/7
Solution: An improper fraction has numerator ≥ denominator. Only 9/7 satisfies this condition (9 > 7).
Source: NCERT Class 6

19. Write 2.5 as a mixed number in simplest form.
(a) 2 1/4 (b) 2 3/4 (c) 2 1/2 (d) 2 1/5
Answer: (c) 2 1/2
Solution: 2.5 = 2 + 0.5 = 2 + 5/10 = 2 1/2.
Source: NCERT Class 7

20. Evaluate: 1/3 + 1/6 = ?
(a) 2/9 (b) 1/9 (c) 2/3 (d) 1/2
Answer: (d) 1/2
Solution: LCM of 3 and 6 is 6. Convert: 2/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.
Source: NCERT Class 6

21. Arrange in ascending order: 3/5, 0.55, 0.5, 7/12.
(a) 0.5 < 0.55 < 7/12 < 3/5 (b) 0.5 < 7/12 < 0.55 < 3/5 (c) 0.55 < 0.5 < 7/12 < 3/5 (d) 0.5 < 3/5 < 0.55 < 7/12
Answer: (a) 0.5 < 0.55 < 7/12 < 3/5
Solution: Convert each to decimal: 3/5 = 0.60, 0.55 = 0.55, 0.5 = 0.50, 7/12 ≈ 0.5833. Ascending order: 0.50 < 0.55 < 0.5833 < 0.60 → 0.5 < 0.55 < 7/12 < 3/5.
Source: SSC GD Pattern

22. Simplify: 2/3 of (3/4 + 1/2)
(a) 1/2 (b) 5/6 (c) 5/12 (d) 7/12
Answer: (b) 5/6
Solution: Inside bracket: 3/4 + 1/2 = 3/4 + 2/4 = 5/4. 'Of' multiplication: 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

23. A container had 3/4 litre of oil. If 1/3 litre was used, how much oil is left?
(a) 1/4 litre (b) 1/2 litre (c) 5/12 litre (d) 7/12 litre
Answer: (c) 5/12 litre
Solution: Subtract: 3/4 – 1/3 = (9 – 4)/12 = 5/12 litre.
Source: Railway (RRB Group D) Pattern

24. A wire of length 2.5 metres is cut into 5 equal pieces. What is the length of each piece in centimetres?
(a) 0.5 cm (b) 5 cm (c) 25 cm (d) 50 cm
Answer: (d) 50 cm
Solution: 2.5 metres = 2.5 × 100 = 250 cm. Length of each piece = 250 ÷ 5 = 50 cm.
Source: State Police Exam Pattern

25. If 1/3 of a number is 5 more than 1/4 of the number, find the number.
(a) 40 (b) 60 (c) 30 (d) 20
Answer: (b) 60
Solution: Let the number be x. Equation: (1/3)x – (1/4)x = 5 ⇒ (4x – 3x)/12 = 5 ⇒ x/12 = 5 ⇒ x = 60.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern
