51. A man spends 2/5 of his income on food, 1/4 on rent, and saves the remaining ₹7,000. What is his total income?
(a) ₹20,000 (b) ₹18,000 (c) ₹24,000 (d) ₹16,000
Answer: (a) ₹20,000
Solution: Total fraction spent: 2/5 + 1/4 = 13/20. Fraction saved: 1 – 13/20 = 7/20. So (7/20) × Income = 7,000 ⇒ Income = 7,000 × 20/7 = ₹20,000.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. In a school, 4/7 of the students are boys. If the number of girls is 210, find the total number of students in the school.
(a) 420 (b) 490 (c) 560 (d) 350
Answer: (b) 490
Solution: Fraction of girls = 1 – 4/7 = 3/7. Given (3/7) × Total = 210 ⇒ Total = 210 × 7/3 = 490.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

53. A water tank is 3/4 full. When 15 litres of water is drawn out, it becomes 1/2 full. What is the total capacity of the tank?
(a) 45 litres (b) 50 litres (c) 60 litres (d) 75 litres
Answer: (c) 60 litres
Solution: Fraction removed: 3/4 – 1/2 = 1/4. So (1/4) × Capacity = 15 litres ⇒ Capacity = 15 × 4 = 60 litres.
Source: RRB Group D Pattern

54. Arrange the fractions in descending order: 3/4, 5/6, 7/9, 11/12.
(a) 11/12 > 7/9 > 5/6 > 3/4 (b) 5/6 > 11/12 > 3/4 > 7/9 (c) 3/4 > 7/9 > 5/6 > 11/12 (d) 11/12 > 5/6 > 7/9 > 3/4
Answer: (d) 11/12 > 5/6 > 7/9 > 3/4
Solution: Convert to decimals: 11/12 ≈ 0.917, 5/6 ≈ 0.833, 7/9 ≈ 0.778, 3/4 = 0.750. Descending order: 11/12 > 5/6 > 7/9 > 3/4.
Source: NCERT Class 7

55. A student was asked to multiply a number by 3/2, but he divided it by 3/2 instead. If his answer was 10 less than the correct answer, find the number.
(a) 12 (b) 15 (c) 18 (d) 24
Answer: (a) 12
Solution: Let the number be x. Correct = (3/2)x, Wrong = x ÷ (3/2) = (2/3)x. Difference: (3/2 – 2/3)x = (5/6)x = 10 ⇒ x = 10 × 6/5 = 12.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

56. A pole has 1/5 of its length in mud, 1/3 in water, and the remaining 7 metres above water. Find the total length of the pole.
(a) 12 m (b) 15 m (c) 18 m (d) 20 m
Answer: (b) 15 m
Solution: Fraction submerged: 1/5 + 1/3 = 8/15. Remaining fraction = 1 – 8/15 = 7/15. So (7/15) × Length = 7 m ⇒ Length = 15 m.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

57. If 4/5 of an estate is valued at ₹40,000, what is the value of 3/8 of the estate?
(a) ₹15,000 (b) ₹16,500 (c) ₹18,750 (d) ₹20,000
Answer: (c) ₹18,750
Solution: Total value = 40,000 × 5/4 = ₹50,000. Value of 3/8 = 50,000 × 3/8 = ₹18,750.
Source: NCERT Class 7

58. Which of the following fractions lies strictly between 1/3 and 1/2?
(a) 1/4 (b) 3/5 (c) 2/7 (d) 5/12
Answer: (d) 5/12
Solution: Converting to decimals: 1/3 ≈ 0.333 and 1/2 = 0.500. Here 5/12 ≈ 0.417, which lies strictly between 0.333 and 0.500.
Source: NCERT Class 7

59. A person spent 1/3 of his money and then 1/4 of the remainder. If he is left with ₹600, how much money did he have originally?
(a) ₹1,200 (b) ₹1,440 (c) ₹1,000 (d) ₹1,500
Answer: (a) ₹1,200
Solution: Psychological Trap: Adding 1/3 + 1/4 = 7/12 gives wrong answer ₹1,440. The second expense is of the remainder: after spending 1/3, remainder is 2/3. He then spends 1/4 of 2/3 = 1/6. Total spent = 1/3 + 1/6 = 1/2. Left = 1/2 of money = 600 ⇒ Original money = ₹1,200.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

60. Out of 120 apples in a basket, 1/4 are rotten. If 2/3 of the good apples are sold, how many good apples remain?
(a) 20 (b) 30 (c) 40 (d) 25
Answer: (b) 30
Solution: Rotten apples = 120 × 1/4 = 30. Good apples = 120 – 30 = 90. Sold = 90 × 2/3 = 60. Remaining good apples = 90 – 60 = 30.
Source: SSC GD Pattern

61. Which of the following fractions is the largest: 7/8, 11/12, 15/16, 19/20?
(a) 7/8 (b) 11/12 (c) 19/20 (d) 15/16
Answer: (c) 19/20
Solution: Each fraction has the same difference of 1 between denominator and numerator (8 – 7 = 1, 12 – 11 = 1, etc.). When the difference is identical, the fraction with the largest numerator is the largest: 19/20.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

62. If a train covers 3/5 of a journey in 3 hours, how many more hours will it take to complete the remaining journey at the same speed?
(a) 1 hour (b) 1.5 hours (c) 3 hours (d) 2 hours
Answer: (d) 2 hours
Solution: 3/5 of journey in 3 hours ⇒ 1/5 of journey takes 1 hour. Remaining journey = 1 – 3/5 = 2/5, which will take 2 hours.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

63. A book has 240 pages. Ramesh reads 3/8 of the book on Monday and 1/3 of the remaining pages on Tuesday. How many pages are still unread?
(a) 100 (b) 80 (c) 120 (d) 90
Answer: (a) 100
Solution: Monday: 240 × 3/8 = 90 pages read; remainder = 150 pages. Tuesday: 150 × 1/3 = 50 pages read. Unread = 150 – 50 = 100 pages.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

64. What fraction must be added to 2/3 of 3/4 to make it equal to 1?
(a) 1/3 (b) 1/2 (c) 1/4 (d) 2/5
Answer: (b) 1/2
Solution: 2/3 of 3/4 = (2/3) × (3/4) = 1/2. Required fraction = 1 – 1/2 = 1/2.
Source: NCERT Class 7

65. If 0.125 of a quantity of grain is 25 kg, what is the weight of 0.6 of the same quantity?
(a) 100 kg (b) 150 kg (c) 120 kg (d) 140 kg
Answer: (c) 120 kg
Solution: 0.125 = 1/8. Total quantity = 25 × 8 = 200 kg. Weight of 0.6 = 200 × 0.6 = 120 kg.
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

66. A fuel tank is 2/5 full. When 35 litres of fuel is added, it becomes 3/4 full. What is the capacity of the tank?
(a) 80 litres (b) 90 litres (c) 120 litres (d) 100 litres
Answer: (d) 100 litres
Solution: Fraction added: 3/4 – 2/5 = (15 – 8)/20 = 7/20. So (7/20) × Capacity = 35 litres ⇒ Capacity = 35 × 20/7 = 100 litres.
Source: RRB Group D Pattern

67. Arrange the fractions in ascending order: 4/5, 7/10, 11/15, 13/20.
(a) 13/20 < 7/10 < 11/15 < 4/5 (b) 7/10 < 13/20 < 11/15 < 4/5 (c) 13/20 < 11/15 < 7/10 < 4/5 (d) 11/15 < 13/20 < 7/10 < 4/5
Answer: (a) 13/20 < 7/10 < 11/15 < 4/5
Solution: LCM of 5, 10, 15, 20 is 60: 4/5 = 48/60, 7/10 = 42/60, 11/15 = 44/60, 13/20 = 39/60. Ascending order: 39/60 < 42/60 < 44/60 < 48/60 ⇒ 13/20 < 7/10 < 11/15 < 4/5.
Source: NCERT Class 7

68. A cricket team won 2/3 of the matches it played and lost 1/4 of them. If 2 matches ended in a tie, how many matches were played in total?
(a) 18 (b) 24 (c) 30 (d) 36
Answer: (b) 24
Solution: Fraction of won + lost = 2/3 + 1/4 = 11/12. Tied matches = 1 – 11/12 = 1/12. Given (1/12) × Total = 2 ⇒ Total = 24 matches.
Source: State Police Exam Pattern

69. If 2/3 of A is equal to 4/5 of B, then what is the ratio A : B?
(a) 5 : 6 (b) 8 : 15 (c) 6 : 5 (d) 15 : 8
Answer: (c) 6 : 5
Solution: (2/3)A = (4/5)B ⇒ A/B = (4/5) / (2/3) = 4/5 × 3/2 = 6/5. Hence, A : B = 6 : 5.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

70. A rope of length 13 1/2 metres is cut into pieces of length 3/4 metre each. How many pieces are obtained?
(a) 15 (b) 16 (c) 20 (d) 18
Answer: (d) 18
Solution: Total length = 13 1/2 = 27/2 metres. Number of pieces = (27/2) ÷ (3/4) = 27/2 × 4/3 = 18 pieces.
Source: NCERT Class 6

71. In an examination, a candidate answered 4/5 of the questions correctly. If he answered 12 questions incorrectly, what was the total number of questions?
(a) 60 (b) 50 (c) 48 (d) 72
Answer: (a) 60
Solution: Fraction of incorrect questions = 1 – 4/5 = 1/5. Given (1/5) × Total = 12 ⇒ Total = 60 questions.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

72. Which of the following decimals is the greatest: 0.5, 0.55, 0.505, 0.055?
(a) 0.505 (b) 0.55 (c) 0.5 (d) 0.055
Answer: (b) 0.55
Solution: Equalizing to three decimal places: 0.550 > 0.505 > 0.500 > 0.055. The greatest is 0.55.
Source: NCERT Class 6

73. A container has 40 litres of pure milk. If 1/4 of it is replaced by water, what is the ratio of milk to water in the resulting mixture?
(a) 2 : 1 (b) 4 : 1 (c) 3 : 1 (d) 5 : 2
Answer: (c) 3 : 1
Solution: Milk removed = 40 × 1/4 = 10 litres. Milk remaining = 30 litres. Water added = 10 litres. Ratio = 30 : 10 = 3 : 1.
Source: Banking Exam Pattern

74. A walker covered 3 1/2 km in the morning and 2 3/4 km in the evening. What was the total distance covered?
(a) 5 1/2 km (b) 6 km (c) 5 3/4 km (d) 6 1/4 km
Answer: (d) 6 1/4 km
Solution: Sum = 3 1/2 + 2 3/4 = 7/2 + 11/4 = 14/4 + 11/4 = 25/4 = 6 1/4 km.
Source: NCERT Class 6

75. When 3/4 of a number is subtracted from the number itself, the result is 15. What is the number?
(a) 45 (b) 60 (c) 20 (d) 30
Answer: (b) 60
Solution: Psychological Trap: Subtracting raw fraction 3/4 from x gives x – 3/4 = 15 ⇒ x = 15 3/4 (incorrect). The question states '3/4 of the number', so equation is x – (3/4)x = (1/4)x = 15 ⇒ x = 60.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
