Set 1: Ratio & Proportion Multiple Choice Questions (Easy to Medium)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. What is the simplest form of the ratio 24 : 36?
(a) 2 : 3 (b) 3 : 2 (c) 4 : 6 (d) 1 : 2
Answer: (a) 2 : 3
Solution: Dividing both terms by their HCF, which is 12: 24 ÷ 12 = 2 and 36 ÷ 12 = 3. The simplest form is 2 : 3.
Source: NCERT Class 6

2. What is the ratio of 75 paise to ₹3 in simplest form?
(a) 1 : 3 (b) 1 : 4 (c) 1 : 5 (d) 25 : 1
Answer: (b) 1 : 4
Solution: Convert ₹3 to paise: ₹3 = 300 paise. The ratio is 75 : 300. Dividing both terms by 75 gives 1 : 4.
Source: NCERT Class 6

3. If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, what is the combined ratio A : B : C?
(a) 8 : 10 : 15 (b) 6 : 12 : 15 (c) 8 : 12 : 15 (d) 10 : 12 : 15
Answer: (c) 8 : 12 : 15
Solution: LCM of common term B (3 and 4) is 12. Multiply first ratio by 4 and second by 3: A : B = 8 : 12 and B : C = 12 : 15. Therefore, A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15.
Source: NCERT Class 7

4. Find the missing value of x in the proportion 3 : 5 :: 12 : x.
(a) 15 (b) 18 (c) 25 (d) 20
Answer: (d) 20
Solution: By cross-product rule: Product of extremes = Product of means. 3 × x = 5 × 12 => 3x = 60 => x = 20.
Source: NCERT Class 7

5. Divide ₹720 between Ram and Shyam in the ratio 4 : 5. How much money does Ram receive?
(a) ₹320 (b) ₹400 (c) ₹360 (d) ₹300
Answer: (a) ₹320
Solution: Total ratio parts = 4 + 5 = 9 parts = ₹720 => 1 part = ₹80. Ram's share = 4 × ₹80 = ₹320.
Source: SSC MTS

6. What is the mean proportional between 9 and 16?
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: Mean proportional between a and b is given by √(a × b) = √(9 × 16) = √144 = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 7

7. What is the third proportional to 4 and 8?
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 20
Answer: (c) 16
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 12 incorrectly adds the arithmetic difference of 4! In proportion, the third proportional c satisfies 4 : 8 :: 8 : c. Product of extremes = Product of means => 4 × c = 8 × 8 = 64 => c = 64 ÷ 4 = 16.
Source: SSC CGL

8. What is the fourth proportional to 3, 7, and 9?
(a) 18 (b) 19 (c) 20 (d) 21
Answer: (d) 21
Solution: In a : b :: c : d, product of extremes equals product of means: 3 : 7 :: 9 : d => 3 × d = 7 × 9 = 63 => d = 63 ÷ 3 = 21.
Source: SSC CHSL

9. What is the duplicate ratio of 3 : 4?
(a) 9 : 16 (b) 6 : 8 (c) 27 : 64 (d) √3 : 2
Answer: (a) 9 : 16
Solution: Duplicate ratio of a : b is a² : b². Here, 3² : 4² = 9 : 16.
Source: NCERT Class 7

10. What is the sub-duplicate ratio of 25 : 36?
(a) 6 : 5 (b) 5 : 6 (c) 125 : 216 (d) 50 : 72
Answer: (b) 5 : 6
Solution: Sub-duplicate ratio of a : b is √a : √b. Here, √25 : √36 = 5 : 6.
Source: NCERT Class 7

11. What is the triplicate ratio of 2 : 3?
(a) 4 : 9 (b) 6 : 9 (c) 8 : 27 (d) 16 : 81
Answer: (c) 8 : 27
Solution: Triplicate ratio of a : b is a³ : b³. Here, 2³ : 3³ = 8 : 27.
Source: NCERT Class 7

12. What is the sub-triplicate ratio of 27 : 64?
(a) 9 : 16 (b) 3 : 8 (c) 4 : 3 (d) 3 : 4
Answer: (d) 3 : 4
Solution: Sub-triplicate ratio of a : b is ∛a : ∛b. Here, ∛27 : ∛64 = 3 : 4.
Source: NCERT Class 7

13. Find the compound ratio of 2 : 3, 6 : 7, and 14 : 15.
(a) 8 : 15 (b) 4 : 15 (c) 16 : 45 (d) 7 : 15
Answer: (a) 8 : 15
Solution: Compound ratio is the ratio of product of antecedents to product of consequents: (2 × 6 × 14) : (3 × 7 × 15) = 168 : 315. Dividing by 21 gives 8 : 15.
Source: SSC GD

14. Divide ₹620 among A, B, and C in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/5. What is the share of A?
(a) ₹200 (b) ₹300 (c) ₹120 (d) ₹240
Answer: (b) ₹300
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) incorrectly divides using the denominators 2 : 3 : 5 directly! To convert fractions into integers, multiply each term by the LCM of denominators (LCM of 2, 3, 5 is 30): (1/2 × 30) : (1/3 × 30) : (1/5 × 30) = 15 : 10 : 6. Total units = 15 + 10 + 6 = 31 units = ₹620 => 1 unit = ₹20. A's share = 15 × ₹20 = ₹300.
Source: SSC CGL

15. If 2A = 3B = 4C, what is the ratio A : B : C?
(a) 2 : 3 : 4 (b) 4 : 3 : 2 (c) 6 : 4 : 3 (d) 3 : 4 : 6
Answer: (c) 6 : 4 : 3
Solution: Divide all coefficients by their LCM, which is 12: 2A/12 = 3B/12 = 4C/12 => A/6 = B/4 = C/3. Therefore, A : B : C = 6 : 4 : 3.
Source: RRB Group D

16. Two cyclists cover distances in the ratio 3 : 4 in times that are in the ratio 4 : 5. What is the ratio of their speeds?
(a) 12 : 20 (b) 16 : 15 (c) 9 : 20 (d) 15 : 16
Answer: (d) 15 : 16
Solution: Speed = Distance ÷ Time. Speed ratio = (3/4) : (4/5) = (3 × 5) : (4 × 4) = 15 : 16.
Source: SSC MTS

17. The ratio of the present ages of A and B is 3 : 4. Five years hence, the ratio of their ages will become 4 : 5. What is A's present age?
(a) 15 years (b) 20 years (c) 25 years (d) 18 years
Answer: (a) 15 years
Solution: Let present ages be 3x and 4x. Equation: (3x + 5) ÷ (4x + 5) = 4/5 => 5(3x + 5) = 4(4x + 5) => 15x + 25 = 16x + 20 => x = 5. A's present age = 3 × 5 = 15 years.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

18. A box contains ₹1, 50-paise, and 25-paise coins in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. If the total value of the coins is ₹180, how many 50-paise coins are in the box?
(a) 80 (b) 120 (c) 160 (d) 100
Answer: (b) 120
Solution: Let the number of coins be 2x, 3x, and 4x. Value in rupees: (2x × 1) + (3x × 0.50) + (4x × 0.25) = 2x + 1.5x + 1x = 4.5x. Given 4.5x = 180 => x = 40. Number of 50-paise coins = 3 × 40 = 120 coins.
Source: SSC CGL

19. The ratio of monthly income to expenditure of a family is 11 : 9. If the family saves ₹4,000 per month, what is their monthly income?
(a) ₹18,000 (b) ₹20,000 (c) ₹22,000 (d) ₹24,000
Answer: (c) ₹22,000
Solution: Savings = Income - Expenditure = 11 units - 9 units = 2 units = ₹4,000 => 1 unit = ₹2,000. Monthly income = 11 × ₹2,000 = ₹22,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

20. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 9 is subtracted from each, the ratio of the new numbers becomes 12 : 23. What is the smaller number?
(a) 27 (b) 30 (c) 36 (d) 33
Answer: (d) 33
Solution: Let numbers be 3x and 5x. Equation: (3x - 9) ÷ (5x - 9) = 12/23 => 23(3x - 9) = 12(5x - 9) => 69x - 207 = 60x - 108 => 9x = 99 => x = 11. Smaller number = 3 × 11 = 33.
Source: RRB NTPC

21. The three interior angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. What is the measure of the largest angle?
(a) 80° (b) 60° (c) 75° (d) 90°
Answer: (a) 80°
Solution: Sum of angles in a triangle is 180°. Ratio units = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 units = 180° => 1 unit = 20°. Largest angle = 4 × 20° = 80°.
Source: NCERT Class 7

22. A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7 : 2. When 5 liters of water is added to the mixture, the ratio of milk to water becomes 7 : 3. What is the quantity of milk in the mixture?
(a) 30 liters (b) 35 liters (c) 40 liters (d) 45 liters
Answer: (b) 35 liters
Solution: Since only water is added, the milk quantity remains constant (7 units). Water increases from 2 units to 3 units, an increase of 1 unit = 5 liters. Milk quantity = 7 × 5 = 35 liters.
Source: SSC MTS

23. What number must be added to each of 6, 7, 15, and 17 so that the resulting numbers are in proportion?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (c) 3
Solution: (6 + x)(17 + x) = (7 + x)(15 + x) => 102 + 23x + x² = 105 + 22x + x² => 23x - 22x = 105 - 102 => x = 3.
Source: SSC CGL

24. In a school, the ratio of boys to girls is 5 : 3. If there are 240 girls in the school, what is the total number of students in the school?
(a) 400 (b) 560 (c) 600 (d) 640
Answer: (d) 640
Solution: Number of girls = 3 units = 240 => 1 unit = 80. Total students = 5 + 3 = 8 units = 8 × 80 = 640 students.
Source: RRB Group D

25. If x : y = 3 : 4, what is the value of (5x - 2y) : (7x + 2y)?
(a) 5 : 29 (b) 7 : 29 (c) 7 : 31 (d) 9 : 29
Answer: (b) 7 : 29
Solution: Substitute x = 3 and y = 4: (5 × 3 - 2 × 4) : (7 × 3 + 2 × 4) = (15 - 8) : (21 + 8) = 7 : 29.
Source: SSC CHSL
