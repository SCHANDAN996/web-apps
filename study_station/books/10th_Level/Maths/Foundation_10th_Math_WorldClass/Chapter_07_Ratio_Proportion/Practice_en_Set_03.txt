51. If (x / 5) = (y / 8), what is the ratio (x + 5) : (y + 8)?
(a) 5 : 8 (b) 8 : 5 (c) 1 : 1 (d) 13 : 8
Answer: (a) 5 : 8
Solution: Let x/5 = y/8 = k. Then x = 5k and y = 8k. (x + 5)/(y + 8) = (5k + 5)/(8k + 8) = 5(k + 1) / [8(k + 1)] = 5/8 = 5 : 8.
Source: SSC CGL

52. What is the mean proportional between 0.08 and 0.18?
(a) 0.10 (b) 0.12 (c) 0.14 (d) 0.16
Answer: (b) 0.12
Solution: Mean proportional = √(0.08 × 0.18) = √(0.0144) = 0.12.
Source: SSC CHSL

53. If A : B = 1/2 : 3/8, B : C = 1/3 : 5/9, and C : D = 5/6 : 3/4, what is the combined ratio A : B : C : D?
(a) 4 : 3 : 5 : 6 (b) 8 : 6 : 9 : 10 (c) 8 : 6 : 10 : 9 (d) 6 : 8 : 10 : 9
Answer: (c) 8 : 6 : 10 : 9
Solution: Simplify ratios: A : B = 4 : 3, B : C = 3 : 5, C : D = 10 : 9. Since B is 3 in both, A : B : C = 4 : 3 : 5 = 8 : 6 : 10. Combining with C : D = 10 : 9 yields A : B : C : D = 8 : 6 : 10 : 9.
Source: SSC CGL

54. A bag contains ₹1, 50-paise, and 25-paise coins whose values are in the ratio 13 : 11 : 7. If the total number of coins in the bag is 378, how many 50-paise coins are there?
(a) 66 (b) 78 (c) 168 (d) 132
Answer: (d) 132
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 66 mistakenly treats the value ratio as coin count ratio! Value ratio is 13 : 11 : 7. Coin count ratio = (13 × 1) : (11 × 2) : (7 × 4) = 13 : 22 : 28. Total units = 13 + 22 + 28 = 63 units = 378 => 1 unit = 6. Number of 50-paise coins = 22 × 6 = 132 coins.
Source: SSC CGL

55. If (a + b) : √(ab) = 4 : 1, where a > b > 0, what is the ratio a : b?
(a) (2 + √3) : (2 - √3) (b) (3 + √2) : (3 - √2) (c) 4 : 1 (d) (√3 + 1) : (√3 - 1)
Answer: (a) (2 + √3) : (2 - √3)
Solution: Rewrite as (a + b)/(2√(ab)) = 2/1. By componendo and dividendo: (a + b + 2√(ab))/(a + b - 2√(ab)) = (2 + 1)/(2 - 1) = 3/1 => (√a + √b)² / (√a - √b)² = 3/1 => (√a + √b)/(√a - √b) = √3/1. Applying componendo and dividendo again: √a / √b = (√3 + 1)/(√3 - 1). Squaring gives a/b = (2 + √3)/(2 - √3).
Source: SSC CGL

56. The ratio of zinc to copper in a brass alloy is 13 : 7. How much zinc is present in 100 kg of this brass alloy?
(a) 60 kg (b) 65 kg (c) 70 kg (d) 75 kg
Answer: (b) 65 kg
Solution: Total ratio parts = 13 + 7 = 20 parts = 100 kg => 1 part = 5 kg. Zinc quantity = 13 × 5 = 65 kg.
Source: RRB Group D

57. A sum of ₹4,270 is divided among A, B, and C such that 3 times A's share = 4 times B's share = 7 times C's share. How much is C's share?
(a) ₹720 (b) ₹780 (c) ₹840 (d) ₹910
Answer: (c) ₹840
Solution: 3A = 4B = 7C. Dividing by LCM(3, 4, 7) = 84: A/28 = B/21 = C/12 => A : B : C = 28 : 21 : 12. Total parts = 28 + 21 + 12 = 61 parts = ₹4,270 => 1 part = ₹70. C's share = 12 × ₹70 = ₹840.
Source: SSC CHSL

58. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7. If each number is increased by 6, the ratio becomes 3 : 4. What is the difference between the two original numbers?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (d) 12
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 6 is simply the value of x, not the difference (2x)! Equation: (5x + 6) ÷ (7x + 6) = 3/4 => 4(5x + 6) = 3(7x + 6) => 20x + 24 = 21x + 18 => x = 6. Original numbers are 5 × 6 = 30 and 7 × 6 = 42. Difference = 42 - 30 = 12.
Source: SSC MTS

59. If x : y = 2 : 1, what is the value of (x² - y²) : (x² + y²)?
(a) 3 : 5 (b) 5 : 3 (c) 1 : 3 (d) 2 : 5
Answer: (a) 3 : 5
Solution: Substitute x = 2 and y = 1: (2² - 1²) : (2² + 1²) = (4 - 1) : (4 + 1) = 3 : 5.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

60. In an office, the ratio of male to female employees is 5 : 3. If there are 12 more male employees than female employees, what is the total number of employees in the office?
(a) 40 (b) 48 (c) 54 (d) 60
Answer: (b) 48
Solution: Difference in ratio = 5 - 3 = 2 units = 12 => 1 unit = 6 employees. Total employees = (5 + 3) × 6 = 8 × 6 = 48 employees.
Source: SSC GD

61. If x / y = 3 / 4, what is the value of the algebraic fraction (2x + 3y) / (3x + 4y)?
(a) 17/25 (b) 16/25 (c) 18/25 (d) 19/25
Answer: (c) 18/25
Solution: Substitute x = 3 and y = 4: (2 × 3 + 3 × 4) ÷ (3 × 3 + 4 × 4) = (6 + 12) ÷ (9 + 16) = 18 / 25.
Source: SSC CGL

62. The ratio of the present ages of two friends is 4 : 5. Six years ago, the sum of their ages was 42 years. What is the present age of the elder friend?
(a) 24 years (b) 26 years (c) 28 years (d) 30 years
Answer: (d) 30 years
Solution: Sum of their present ages = 42 + (2 × 6) = 42 + 12 = 54 years. Total ratio units = 4 + 5 = 9 units = 54 => 1 unit = 6 years. Present age of the elder friend = 5 × 6 = 30 years.
Source: RRB NTPC

63. When x is subtracted from each of the numbers 21, 38, 55, and 106, the resulting numbers are in proportion. What is the value of x?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 4
Solution: (21 - x)(106 - x) = (38 - x)(55 - x) => 2226 - 127x + x² = 2090 - 93x + x² => 127x - 93x = 2226 - 2090 => 34x = 136 => x = 4.
Source: SSC CGL

64. An amount of ₹750 is distributed among A, B, and C such that A : B = 5 : 2 and B : C = 7 : 13. How much money does B receive?
(a) ₹120 (b) ₹140 (c) ₹160 (d) ₹180
Answer: (b) ₹140
Solution: A : B : C = (5 × 7) : (2 × 7) : (2 × 13) = 35 : 14 : 26. Total parts = 35 + 14 + 26 = 75 parts = ₹750 => 1 part = ₹10. B's share = 14 × ₹10 = ₹140.
Source: SSC CHSL

65. If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 6 : 5, what is the ratio A : (A+C)?
(a) 9 : 17 (b) 9 : 18 (c) 9 : 19 (d) 10 : 19
Answer: (c) 9 : 19
Solution: A : B : C = (3 × 6) : (4 × 6) : (4 × 5) = 18 : 24 : 20 = 9 : 12 : 10. A : (A+C) = 9 : (9 + 10) = 9 : 19.
Source: SSC MTS

66. A 45-liter mixture contains alcohol and water in the ratio 4 : 1. How much water must be added to the mixture so that the ratio of alcohol to water becomes 3 : 2?
(a) 10 liters (b) 12 liters (c) 18 liters (d) 15 liters
Answer: (d) 15 liters
Solution: Alcohol = (4/5) × 45 = 36 liters, Water = 9 liters. In the new mixture, alcohol is 3 units = 36 liters => 1 unit = 12 liters. Water needed = 2 units = 2 × 12 = 24 liters. Water to add = 24 - 9 = 15 liters.
Source: SSC CGL

67. The monthly incomes of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 2, and their expenditures are in the ratio 5 : 3. If both save ₹1,000 each per month, what is the monthly income of A?
(a) ₹6,000 (b) ₹4,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹5,000
Answer: (a) ₹6,000
Solution: Equation: (3x - 1000) ÷ (2x - 1000) = 5/3 => 3(3x - 1000) = 5(2x - 1000) => 9x - 3000 = 10x - 5000 => x = 2000. Monthly income of A = 3 × 2000 = ₹6,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

68. The monthly salaries of A, B, and C are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. If C's monthly salary is ₹1,200 more than A's monthly salary, what is B's annual salary?
(a) ₹12,000 (b) ₹14,400 (c) ₹16,800 (d) ₹18,000
Answer: (b) ₹14,400
Solution: Difference between C and A = 5 - 2 = 3 units = ₹1,200 => 1 unit = ₹400. B's monthly salary = 3 × 400 = ₹1,200. B's annual salary = 12 × ₹1,200 = ₹14,400.
Source: RRB Group D

69. If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5, what is the ratio a² : b² : bc?
(a) 16 : 36 : 40 (b) 12 : 36 : 45 (c) 16 : 36 : 45 (d) 16 : 32 : 45
Answer: (c) 16 : 36 : 45
Solution: a : b : c = (2 × 4) : (3 × 4) : (3 × 5) = 8 : 12 : 15. Then a² = 64, b² = 144, bc = 12 × 15 = 180. Dividing 64 : 144 : 180 by 4 gives 16 : 36 : 45.
Source: SSC CGL

70. The sides of a triangle are in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4, and its perimeter is 104 cm. What is the length of the longest side?
(a) 32 cm (b) 36 cm (c) 42 cm (d) 48 cm
Answer: (d) 48 cm
Solution: Multiply by LCM(2, 3, 4) = 12: Ratio of sides = 6 : 4 : 3. Perimeter = 6 + 4 + 3 = 13 units = 104 cm => 1 unit = 8 cm. Longest side = 6 × 8 = 48 cm.
Source: SSC MTS

71. A jar contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 7 : 5. When 9 liters of the mixture is drawn off and replaced by liquid B, the ratio of A and B becomes 7 : 9. How many liters of liquid A were in the jar initially?
(a) 21 liters (b) 24 liters (c) 27 liters (d) 18 liters
Answer: (a) 21 liters
Solution: After 9 liters is drawn off, the remaining mixture has A : B = 7 : 5. Adding 9 liters of B changes it to 7 : 9. Liquid A is unchanged (7 units). Liquid B increases by 9 - 5 = 4 units = 9 liters => 1 unit = 9/4 liters. Remaining volume = 12 units = 12 × (9/4) = 27 liters. Total original volume = 27 + 9 = 36 liters. Initial quantity of A = (7/12) × 36 = 21 liters.
Source: SSC CGL

72. The ratio of the number of boys to girls in a school of 720 students is 7 : 5. How many more girls should be admitted to make the ratio 1 : 1?
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 140 (d) 150
Answer: (b) 120
Solution: Boys = (7/12) × 720 = 420. Girls = (5/12) × 720 = 300. For 1 : 1 ratio, the number of girls must equal the number of boys (420). Additional girls needed = 420 - 300 = 120 girls.
Source: SSC CHSL

73. An employer reduces the number of employees in the ratio 9 : 8 and increases their wages in the ratio 14 : 15. In what ratio is the total wage bill changed?
(a) 20 : 21 (b) 21 : 19 (c) 21 : 20 (d) 18 : 17
Answer: (c) 21 : 20
Solution: Total wage bill = (Number of employees) × (Wage per employee). Initial bill : New bill = (9 × 14) : (8 × 15) = 126 : 120 = 21 : 20.
Source: SSC CGL

74. If (a + b) / c = (b + c) / a = (c + a) / b = k, and a + b + c ≠ 0, what is the value of k?
(a) 1 (b) 1/2 (c) 3 (d) 2
Answer: (d) 2
Solution: By equal ratio theorem, k = (Sum of numerators) ÷ (Sum of denominators) = [(a + b) + (b + c) + (c + a)] ÷ (a + b + c) = 2(a + b + c) ÷ (a + b + c) = 2.
Source: SSC CGL

75. In a colored picture, blue and yellow colors are used in the ratio 4 : 3 overall. If in the upper half the ratio of blue to yellow is 2 : 3, what is the ratio of blue to yellow in the lower half?
(a) 24 : 9 (b) 26 : 9 (c) 28 : 9 (d) 25 : 8
Answer: (b) 26 : 9
Solution: Let total area = 70 units (Blue = 40, Yellow = 30). Upper and lower halves each have 35 units. In upper half (ratio 2 : 3, total 5 units = 35 => 1 unit = 7): Blue = 14, Yellow = 21. In lower half: Blue = 40 - 14 = 26, Yellow = 30 - 21 = 9. Ratio Blue : Yellow in lower half = 26 : 9.
Source: SSC CGL
