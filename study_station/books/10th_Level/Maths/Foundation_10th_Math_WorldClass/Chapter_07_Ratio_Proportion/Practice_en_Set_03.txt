51. If (x / 5) = (y / 8), what is the ratio (x + 5) : (y + 8)?
(a) 8 : 5 (b) 5 : 8 (c) 1 : 1 (d) 13 : 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let x/5 = y/8 = k. Then x = 5k and y = 8k, so (x + 5)/(y + 8) = (5k + 5)/(8k + 8) = 5(k + 1) / [8(k + 1)] = 5/8. The tempting 13 : 8 comes from wrongly adding the numerators and denominators separately, so the answer is 5 : 8.
Source: PYQ-style

52. What is the mean proportional between 0.08 and 0.18?
(a) 0.10 (b) 0.16 (c) 0.14 (d) 0.12
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mean proportional = √(0.08 × 0.18) = √0.0144 = 0.12. The trap is taking the arithmetic mean (0.08 + 0.18)/2 = 0.13, which is not even listed cleanly, so the answer is 0.12.
Source: PYQ-style

53. If A : B = 1/2 : 3/8, B : C = 1/3 : 5/9, and C : D = 5/6 : 3/4, what is the combined ratio A : B : C : D?
(a) 8 : 6 : 10 : 9 (b) 8 : 6 : 9 : 10 (c) 4 : 3 : 5 : 6 (d) 6 : 8 : 10 : 9
Answer: (a)
Solution: Simplify: A : B = 4 : 3, B : C = 3 : 5, C : D = 10 : 9. Since B is 3 in both, A : B : C = 4 : 3 : 5 = 8 : 6 : 10, and combining with C : D = 10 : 9 gives 8 : 6 : 10 : 9. The trap is swapping the last two terms, so the answer is 8 : 6 : 10 : 9.
Source: PYQ-style

54. A bag contains ₹1, 50-paise, and 25-paise coins whose values are in the ratio 13 : 11 : 7. If the total number of coins in the bag is 378, how many 50-paise coins are there?
(a) 66 (b) 78 (c) 132 (d) 168
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting value 66 comes from wrongly treating the value ratio as the coin-count ratio. Coin count ratio = (13 × 1) : (11 × 2) : (7 × 4) = 13 : 22 : 28; total = 63 units = 378, so 1 unit = 6 and 50-paise coins = 22 × 6 = 132. The answer is 132.
Source: PYQ-style

55. If (a + b) : √(ab) = 4 : 1, where a > b > 0, what is the ratio a : b?
(a) 4 : 1 (b) (3 + √2) : (3 - √2) (c) (2 + √3) : (2 - √3) (d) (√3 + 1) : (√3 - 1)
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rewrite as (a + b)/(2√(ab)) = 2/1. By componendo-dividendo, (√a + √b)²/(√a - √b)² = 3/1, so (√a + √b)/(√a - √b) = √3, giving √a/√b = (√3 + 1)/(√3 - 1). Squaring: a/b = (4 + 2√3)/(4 - 2√3) = (2 + √3)/(2 - √3). The trap is stopping at the unsquared form, so the answer is (2 + √3) : (2 - √3).
Source: PYQ-style

56. The ratio of zinc to copper in a brass alloy is 13 : 7. How much zinc is present in 100 kg of this brass alloy?
(a) 65 kg (b) 60 kg (c) 70 kg (d) 75 kg
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total parts = 13 + 7 = 20 parts = 100 kg, so 1 part = 5 kg and zinc = 13 × 5 = 65 kg. The trap is using 7 parts (copper's share of 35 kg misread), so the answer is 65 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

57. A sum of ₹4,270 is divided among A, B, and C such that 3 times A's share = 4 times B's share = 7 times C's share. How much is C's share?
(a) ₹720 (b) ₹780 (c) ₹910 (d) ₹840
Answer: (d)
Solution: 3A = 4B = 7C; dividing by LCM(3, 4, 7) = 84 gives A : B : C = 28 : 21 : 12. Total = 61 parts = ₹4,270, so 1 part = ₹70 and C = 12 × 70 = ₹840. The trap is taking the ratio as 3 : 4 : 7 directly, so the answer is ₹840.
Source: PYQ-style

58. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7. If each number is increased by 6, the ratio becomes 3 : 4. What is the difference between the two original numbers?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting value 6 is just x, not the difference 2x. (5x + 6)/(7x + 6) = 3/4 gives 20x + 24 = 21x + 18, so x = 6; the numbers are 30 and 42. The difference is 42 - 30 = 12, so the answer is 12.
Source: PYQ-style

59. If x : y = 2 : 1, what is the value of (x² - y²) : (x² + y²)?
(a) 1 : 3 (b) 5 : 3 (c) 3 : 5 (d) 2 : 5
Answer: (c)
Solution: Substitute x = 2, y = 1: (4 - 1) : (4 + 1) = 3 : 5. The trap is inverting the ratio to 5 : 3, so the answer is 3 : 5.

60. In an office, the ratio of male to female employees is 5 : 3. If there are 12 more male employees than female employees, what is the total number of employees in the office?
(a) 40 (b) 54 (c) 48 (d) 60
Answer: (c)
Solution: Difference = 5 - 3 = 2 units = 12, so 1 unit = 6 and total = 8 × 6 = 48. The trap is multiplying 12 by 5 or 3 instead of using the difference, so the answer is 48.

61. If x / y = 3 / 4, what is the value of the algebraic fraction (2x + 3y) / (3x + 4y)?
(a) 17/25 (b) 18/25 (c) 16/25 (d) 19/25
Answer: (b)
Solution: Substitute x = 3, y = 4: (6 + 12)/(9 + 16) = 18/25. The trap is adding numerators and denominators of the original ratio wrongly, so the answer is 18/25.

62. The ratio of the present ages of two friends is 4 : 5. Six years ago, the sum of their ages was 42 years. What is the present age of the elder friend?
(a) 24 years (b) 26 years (c) 28 years (d) 30 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: Present sum = 42 + 2 × 6 = 54 years; 9 units = 54, so 1 unit = 6 and the elder friend is 5 × 6 = 30 years. The trap is adding only 6 instead of 12 to the past sum, so the answer is 30 years.

63. When x is subtracted from each of the numbers 21, 38, 55, and 106, the resulting numbers are in proportion. What is the value of x?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: (21 - x)(106 - x) = (38 - x)(55 - x) gives 2226 - 127x = 2090 - 93x, so 34x = 136 and x = 4. The trap is a sign error while cancelling the x² terms, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

64. An amount of ₹750 is distributed among A, B, and C such that A : B = 5 : 2 and B : C = 7 : 13. How much money does B receive?
(a) ₹140 (b) ₹120 (c) ₹160 (d) ₹180
Answer: (a)
Solution: A : B : C = (5 × 7) : (2 × 7) : (2 × 13) = 35 : 14 : 26; total = 75 parts = ₹750, so 1 part = ₹10 and B = 14 × 10 = ₹140. The trap is failing to make B's value common in both ratios, so the answer is ₹140.
Source: PYQ-style

65. If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 6 : 5, what is the ratio A : (A + C)?
(a) 9 : 17 (b) 9 : 18 (c) 10 : 19 (d) 9 : 19
Answer: (d)
Solution: A : B : C = 18 : 24 : 20 = 9 : 12 : 10, so A : (A + C) = 9 : (9 + 10) = 9 : 19. The trap is using B + C or A + B in the second term, so the answer is 9 : 19.

66. A 45-liter mixture contains alcohol and water in the ratio 4 : 1. How much water must be added to the mixture so that the ratio of alcohol to water becomes 3 : 2?
(a) 10 liters (b) 15 liters (c) 18 liters (d) 12 liters
Answer: (b)
Solution: Alcohol = 36 liters, water = 9 liters. In the new ratio, 3 units = 36 liters, so 1 unit = 12 liters and water needed = 24 liters; water to add = 24 - 9 = 15 liters. The trap is reporting 24 liters (total water) instead of the water added, so the answer is 15 liters.
Source: PYQ-style

67. The monthly incomes of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 2, and their expenditures are in the ratio 5 : 3. If both save ₹1,000 each per month, what is the monthly income of A?
(a) ₹6,000 (b) ₹4,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹5,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: (3x - 1000)/(2x - 1000) = 5/3 gives 9x - 3000 = 10x - 5000, so x = 2000 and A's income = 3 × 2000 = ₹6,000. The trap is reporting B's income ₹4,000, so the answer is ₹6,000.
Source: PYQ-style

68. The monthly salaries of A, B, and C are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. If C's monthly salary is ₹1,200 more than A's monthly salary, what is B's annual salary?
(a) ₹12,000 (b) ₹16,800 (c) ₹14,400 (d) ₹18,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: C - A = 3 units = ₹1,200, so 1 unit = ₹400 and B's monthly salary = 3 × 400 = ₹1,200; annual = 12 × 1,200 = ₹14,400. The trap is stopping at the monthly figure ₹1,200 or ₹12,000, so the answer is ₹14,400.
Source: PYQ-style

69. If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5, what is the ratio a² : b² : bc?

Answer: (b)

70. The sides of a triangle are in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4, and its perimeter is 104 cm. What is the length of the longest side?
(a) 48 cm (b) 36 cm (c) 42 cm (d) 32 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: Multiplying by LCM(2, 3, 4) = 12, the sides are in ratio 6 : 4 : 3; 13 units = 104 cm, so 1 unit = 8 cm and the longest side = 6 × 8 = 48 cm. The trap is treating 1/2 as the smallest share instead of the largest, so the answer is 48 cm.

71. A jar contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 7 : 5. When 9 liters of the mixture is drawn off and replaced by liquid B, the ratio of A and B becomes 7 : 9. How many liters of liquid A were in the jar initially?
(a) 27 liters (b) 24 liters (c) 21 liters (d) 18 liters
Answer: (c)
Solution: Liquid A is unchanged at 7 units while B rises from 5 to 9 units, so 4 units = 9 liters and 1 unit = 9/4 liters. Remaining mixture = 12 × 9/4 = 27 liters, so original volume = 27 + 9 = 36 liters and initial A = (7/12) × 36 = 21 liters. The trap is taking 27 liters (the remaining mixture) as the answer, so the answer is 21 liters.
Source: PYQ-style

72. The ratio of the number of boys to girls in a school of 720 students is 7 : 5. How many more girls should be admitted to make the ratio 1 : 1?
(a) 100 (b) 150 (c) 140 (d) 120
Answer: (d)
Solution: Boys = (7/12) × 720 = 420 and girls = 300; for a 1 : 1 ratio, girls must rise to 420, so 120 more girls are needed. The trap is using the ratio difference 2 units on the new total instead of the fixed boy count, so the answer is 120.

73. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 9 is added to each number, the ratio becomes 5 : 8. What is the sum of the original numbers?
(a) 189 (b) 216 (c) 198 (d) 243
Answer: (b)
Solution: (3x + 9)/(5x + 9) = 5/8 gives 24x + 72 = 25x + 45, so x = 27; the numbers are 81 and 135. The trap is reporting x = 27 or one number alone instead of the sum, so the answer is 81 + 135 = 216.
Source: PYQ-style

74. If (a + b) / c = (b + c) / a = (c + a) / b = k, and a + b + c ≠ 0, what is the value of k?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 1/2
Answer: (b)
Solution: By the equal-ratio theorem, k = [(a + b) + (b + c) + (c + a)] / (a + b + c) = 2(a + b + c)/(a + b + c) = 2. The trap is forgetting the factor 2 in the numerator sum, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

75. In a colored picture, blue and yellow colors are used in the ratio 4 : 3 overall. If in the upper half the ratio of blue to yellow is 2 : 3, what is the ratio of blue to yellow in the lower half?
(a) 26 : 9 (b) 24 : 9 (c) 28 : 9 (d) 25 : 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let total area = 70 units (blue = 40, yellow = 30); each half = 35 units. Upper half: 2 : 3 over 5 units gives blue = 14, yellow = 21; lower half: blue = 40 - 14 = 26, yellow = 30 - 21 = 9. The trap is assuming the lower half keeps a simple ratio, so the answer is 26 : 9.
Source: PYQ-style
