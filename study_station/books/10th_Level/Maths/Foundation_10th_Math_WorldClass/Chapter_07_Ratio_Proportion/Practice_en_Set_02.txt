26. If A : B = 3 : 4, B : C = 8 : 9, and C : D = 15 : 16, what is the ratio A : D?
(a) 5 : 8 (b) 3 : 8 (c) 5 : 12 (d) 7 : 12
Answer: (a) 5 : 8
Solution: A/D = (A/B) × (B/C) × (C/D) = (3/4) × (8/9) × (15/16) = (24/36) × (15/16) = (2/3) × (15/16) = 30/48 = 5/8. Ratio A : D = 5 : 8.
Source: SSC CGL

27. What is the mean proportional between (3 + √2) and (12 - √32)?
(a) √14 (b) 2√7 (c) 4√7 (d) 7
Answer: (b) 2√7
Solution: Simplify the second term: 12 - √32 = 12 - 4√2 = 4(3 - √2). Product = (3 + √2) × 4(3 - √2) = 4 × (3² - (√2)²) = 4 × (9 - 2) = 28. Mean proportional = √28 = 2√7.
Source: SSC CGL

28. What is the third proportional to (x² - y²) and (x - y)?
(a) x + y (b) x - y (c) (x - y)/(x + y) (d) (x + y)/(x - y)
Answer: (c) (x - y)/(x + y)
Solution: Let the third proportional be c. (x² - y²) : (x - y) :: (x - y) : c => c = (x - y)² ÷ (x² - y²) = (x - y)² ÷ [(x - y)(x + y)] = (x - y)/(x + y).
Source: SSC CHSL

29. The ratio of incomes of A and B is 5 : 4, and the ratio of their expenditures is 3 : 2. If each saves ₹1,600 at the end of the month, what is the monthly income of A?
(a) ₹3,200 (b) ₹3,600 (c) ₹4,800 (d) ₹4,000
Answer: (d) ₹4,000
Solution: Let incomes be 5x and 4x. Equation: (5x - 1600) ÷ (4x - 1600) = 3/2 => 2(5x - 1600) = 3(4x - 1600) => 10x - 3200 = 12x - 4800 => 2x = 1600 => x = 800. A's income = 5 × 800 = ₹4,000.
Source: SSC MTS

30. A bag contains ₹1, 50-paise, and 25-paise coins in the ratio 5 : 6 : 8. If the total money in the bag is ₹240, what is the total number of 25-paise coins?
(a) 192 (b) 120 (c) 144 (d) 160
Answer: (a) 192
Solution: Total value in rupees = (5x × 1) + (6x × 0.50) + (8x × 0.25) = 5x + 3x + 2x = 10x. 10x = 240 => x = 24. Number of 25-paise coins = 8 × 24 = 192 coins.
Source: RRB NTPC

31. The ratio of boys to girls in a school was 4 : 5. When 100 girls left the school, the ratio of boys to girls became 6 : 7. How many boys are in the school?
(a) 1000 (b) 1200 (c) 1500 (d) 1400
Answer: (b) 1200
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 1000 comes from confusing the number of girls with boys! Let boys = 4x and girls = 5x. Equation: 4x ÷ (5x - 100) = 6/7 => 28x = 6(5x - 100) => 28x = 30x - 600 => 2x = 600 => x = 300. Number of boys = 4 × 300 = 1200.
Source: SSC CGL

32. A 60-liter mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1. How many liters of water must be added to this mixture so that the ratio of milk to water becomes 1 : 2?
(a) 40 liters (b) 50 liters (c) 60 liters (d) 80 liters
Answer: (c) 60 liters
Solution: Initial milk = (2/3) × 60 = 40 liters, water = 20 liters. In new mixture, milk : water = 1 : 2. Since milk quantity remains 40 liters (1 unit), water needed = 2 × 40 = 80 liters. Water to add = 80 - 20 = 60 liters.
Source: SSC CHSL

33. An amount of ₹1,050 is divided among A, B, and C such that A receives 2/5 as much as B and C together receive. How much money does A receive?
(a) ₹200 (b) ₹250 (c) ₹350 (d) ₹300
Answer: (d) ₹300
Solution: A = (2/5)(B + C) => A : (B + C) = 2 : 5. Total parts = 2 + 5 = 7 parts = ₹1,050 => 1 part = ₹150. A's share = 2 × 150 = ₹300.
Source: RRB Group D

34. If (a + b) : (b + c) : (c + a) = 6 : 7 : 8 and a + b + c = 14, what is the value of c?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 5
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: Sum of ratios = 2(a + b + c) = (6 + 7 + 8)k = 21k. Since a + b + c = 14, 2 × 14 = 21k => k = 28/21 = 4/3. a + b = 6k = 6 × (4/3) = 8. c = (a + b + c) - (a + b) = 14 - 8 = 6.
Source: SSC CGL

35. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 8 is added to each number, the ratio becomes 2 : 3. What is the sum of the two original numbers?
(a) 48 (b) 64 (c) 72 (d) 80
Answer: (b) 64
Solution: Trap alert: Option (d) 80 is the sum after adding 8 to both numbers (64 + 16 = 80)! Equation: (3x + 8) ÷ (5x + 8) = 2/3 => 3(3x + 8) = 2(5x + 8) => 9x + 24 = 10x + 16 => x = 8. Original numbers are 3 × 8 = 24 and 5 × 8 = 40. Sum of original numbers = 24 + 40 = 64.
Source: SSC MTS

36. A and B invest in a business in the ratio 3 : 5. If 10% of the total profit goes to a charity fund and A's share of the remaining profit is ₹810, what was the total profit?
(a) ₹2,000 (b) ₹2,200 (c) ₹2,400 (d) ₹2,700
Answer: (c) ₹2,400
Solution: Let total profit = P. Remaining profit after charity = 0.90P. A's share = (3/8) × 0.90P = 810 => 0.90P = (810 × 8) ÷ 3 = 2160 => P = 2160 ÷ 0.90 = ₹2,400.
Source: SSC CGL

37. The duplicate ratio of 4 : 5 is compounded with the sub-duplicate ratio of 25 : 16. What is the resulting ratio?
(a) 1 : 1 (b) 5 : 4 (c) 16 : 25 (d) 4 : 5
Answer: (d) 4 : 5
Solution: Duplicate ratio of 4 : 5 = 16 : 25. Sub-duplicate ratio of 25 : 16 = 5 : 4. Compounded ratio = (16 × 5) : (25 × 4) = 80 : 100 = 4 : 5.
Source: SSC CHSL

38. If (5x² - 3y²) : xy = 11 : 2, what is the positive ratio x : y?
(a) 3 : 2 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 5 : 2 (d) 2 : 5
Answer: (a) 3 : 2
Solution: Cross-multiplying: 2(5x² - 3y²) = 11xy => 10x² - 11xy - 6y² = 0. Factoring: (2x - 3y)(5x + 2y) = 0. For positive values, 2x - 3y = 0 => 2x = 3y => x/y = 3/2.
Source: SSC CGL

39. What number must be subtracted from each term of the ratio 17 : 33 so that the ratio becomes 1 : 3?
(a) 7 (b) 9 (c) 11 (d) 8
Answer: (b) 9
Solution: Equation: (17 - x) ÷ (33 - x) = 1/3 => 3(17 - x) = 33 - x => 51 - 3x = 33 - x => 2x = 18 => x = 9.
Source: SSC MTS

40. A sum of money is divided among P, Q, and R in the ratio 7 : 9 : 4. If P receives ₹1,500 more than R, how much money does Q receive?
(a) ₹3,500 (b) ₹4,000 (c) ₹4,500 (d) ₹5,000
Answer: (c) ₹4,500
Solution: Difference between P and R = 7 - 4 = 3 units = ₹1,500 => 1 unit = ₹500. Q's share = 9 units = 9 × ₹500 = ₹4,500.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

41. If a : b = c : d = e : f = 1 : 2, what is the value of (pa + qc + re) : (pb + qd + rf)?
(a) 1 : 4 (b) p : q (c) 2 : 1 (d) 1 : 2
Answer: (d) 1 : 2
Solution: By the theorem on equal ratios, if a/b = c/d = e/f = k, then (pa + qc + re)/(pb + qd + rf) = k = 1/2. Therefore, the ratio is 1 : 2.
Source: SSC CGL

42. In a public library, the ratio of fiction books to non-fiction books is 7 : 3. If there are 1,470 fiction books in the library, how many non-fiction books are there?
(a) 630 (b) 590 (c) 670 (d) 720
Answer: (a) 630
Solution: 7 units = 1,470 => 1 unit = 210 books. Number of non-fiction books = 3 units = 3 × 210 = 630 books.
Source: SSC CHSL

43. Ten years ago, the ratio of the ages of a father and his son was 3 : 1. Today, the ratio of their ages is 2 : 1. What is the present age of the father?
(a) 36 years (b) 40 years (c) 44 years (d) 48 years
Answer: (b) 40 years
Solution: Let present ages be 2x and x. Ten years ago: (2x - 10) ÷ (x - 10) = 3/1 => 2x - 10 = 3x - 30 => x = 20. Father's present age = 2 × 20 = 40 years.
Source: RRB Group D

44. Two positive numbers are in the ratio 4 : 7. If their Highest Common Factor (HCF) is 16, what is the sum of the two numbers?
(a) 160 (b) 168 (c) 176 (d) 184
Answer: (c) 176
Solution: Numbers are 4 × 16 = 64 and 7 × 16 = 112. Sum of the two numbers = (4 + 7) × 16 = 11 × 16 = 176.
Source: SSC MTS

45. An alloy contains copper and zinc in the ratio 5 : 3. If 1.5 kg of zinc is melted into 16 kg of this alloy, what will be the new ratio of copper to zinc?
(a) 3 : 2 (b) 5 : 4 (c) 7 : 5 (d) 4 : 3
Answer: (d) 4 : 3
Solution: In 16 kg alloy: Copper = (5/8) × 16 = 10 kg, Zinc = (3/8) × 16 = 6 kg. After adding 1.5 kg zinc: New zinc = 6 + 1.5 = 7.5 kg. Ratio of copper to zinc = 10 : 7.5 = 20 : 15 = 4 : 3.
Source: SSC GD

46. The ratio of incomes of A, B, and C is 3 : 7 : 4, and that of their expenditures is 4 : 3 : 5. If A saves ₹300 out of an income of ₹2,400, what is the monthly savings of B?
(a) ₹4,025 (b) ₹3,950 (c) ₹4,125 (d) ₹4,200
Answer: (a) ₹4,025
Solution: A's income = 3 units = ₹2,400 => 1 income unit = ₹800. B's income = 7 × 800 = ₹5,600. A's expenditure = 2400 - 300 = ₹2,100 = 4 expenditure units => 1 exp unit = ₹525. B's expenditure = 3 × 525 = ₹1,575. B's savings = Income - Expenditure = 5600 - 1575 = ₹4,025.
Source: SSC CGL

47. A sum of ₹1,200 is divided among A, B, and C such that A gets half of what B and C together receive, and B gets one-third of what A and C together receive. How much money does C receive?
(a) ₹450 (b) ₹500 (c) ₹550 (d) ₹600
Answer: (b) ₹500
Solution: A = (1/2)(B + C) => A : (B + C) = 1 : 2 => A = (1/3) × 1200 = ₹400. B = (1/3)(A + C) => B : (A + C) = 1 : 3 => B = (1/4) × 1200 = ₹300. C's share = 1200 - (A + B) = 1200 - 700 = ₹500.
Source: SSC CGL

48. Equal quantities of three mixtures of milk and water with ratios 2 : 1, 3 : 2, and 4 : 3 are mixed together into a single large vessel. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(a) 185 : 122 (b) 190 : 125 (c) 193 : 122 (d) 197 : 118
Answer: (c) 193 : 122
Solution: Let volume of each mixture = LCM(3, 5, 7) = 105 liters. In 1st: Milk = (2/3) × 105 = 70, Water = 35. In 2nd: Milk = (3/5) × 105 = 63, Water = 42. In 3rd: Milk = (4/7) × 105 = 60, Water = 45. Total milk = 70 + 63 + 60 = 193 liters. Total water = 35 + 42 + 45 = 122 liters. Ratio = 193 : 122.
Source: SSC CHSL

49. A bag contains 50-paise, 25-paise, and ₹1 coins in the ratio 5 : 8 : 1. If the total value of all coins is ₹55, what is the total number of coins in the bag?
(a) 120 (b) 130 (c) 150 (d) 140
Answer: (d) 140
Solution: Total value in rupees = (5x × 0.50) + (8x × 0.25) + (1x × 1) = 2.5x + 2x + 1x = 5.5x. 5.5x = 55 => x = 10. Total coins = (5 + 8 + 1)x = 14 × 10 = 140 coins.
Source: SSC CGL

50. The ratio of land to water on the whole earth is 1 : 2, and in the Northern Hemisphere it is 2 : 3. What is the ratio of land to water in the Southern Hemisphere?
(a) 3 : 11 (b) 4 : 11 (c) 5 : 11 (d) 2 : 9
Answer: (b) 4 : 11
Solution: Let total surface of Earth = 30 units (10 Land, 20 Water). Northern and Southern Hemispheres are each 15 units. In Northern Hemisphere (ratio 2 : 3, total 5 units = 15 => 1 unit = 3): Land = 6, Water = 9. In Southern Hemisphere: Land = 10 - 6 = 4 units, Water = 20 - 9 = 11 units. Ratio Land : Water = 4 : 11.
Source: SSC CGL
