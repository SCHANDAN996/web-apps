126. If (5a - 3b) : (4a - 2b) = 2 : 3, what is the ratio a : b?
(a) 5 : 7 (b) 7 : 5 (c) 3 : 4 (d) 4 : 3
Answer: (a) 5 : 7
Solution: Cross-multiplying: 3(5a - 3b) = 2(4a - 2b) => 15a - 9b = 8a - 4b => 7a = 5b => a/b = 5/7 = 5 : 7.
Source: SSC CGL

127. What is the mean proportional between 0.04 and 0.36?
(a) 0.10 (b) 0.12 (c) 0.15 (d) 0.18
Answer: (b) 0.12
Solution: Mean proportional = √(0.04 × 0.36) = √(0.0144) = 0.12.
Source: SSC CHSL

128. If A : B = 4 : 5 and B : C = 15 : 16, what is the ratio A : C?
(a) 1 : 2 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 3 : 4 (d) 5 : 6
Answer: (c) 3 : 4
Solution: A/C = (A/B) × (B/C) = (4/5) × (15/16) = 60/80 = 3/4 = 3 : 4.
Source: RRB Group D

129. What is the third proportional to 9 and 15?
(a) 21 (b) 24 (c) 27 (d) 25
Answer: (d) 25
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 21 mistakenly adds the arithmetic difference of 6! Third proportional c satisfies 9 : 15 :: 15 : c => 9 × c = 15 × 15 = 225 => c = 225 ÷ 9 = 25.
Source: SSC CGL

130. A sum of ₹1,800 is divided among A, B, and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. What is the share of B?
(a) ₹600 (b) ₹400 (c) ₹800 (d) ₹500
Answer: (a) ₹600
Solution: Total ratio units = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 units = ₹1,800 => 1 unit = ₹200. B's share = 3 × ₹200 = ₹600.
Source: SSC MTS

131. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 8 : 5. If there are 150 more boys than girls, what is the total number of students enrolled in the school?
(a) 400 (b) 650 (c) 250 (d) 600
Answer: (b) 650
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 400 is only the number of boys, not total students! Difference = 8 - 5 = 3 units = 150 students => 1 unit = 50 students. Total students = (8 + 5) × 50 = 13 × 50 = 650 students.
Source: SSC CGL

132. If 3A = 5B = 6C, what is the ratio A : B : C?
(a) 10 : 5 : 6 (b) 6 : 10 : 5 (c) 10 : 6 : 5 (d) 5 : 6 : 10
Answer: (c) 10 : 6 : 5
Solution: Divide each term by LCM(3, 5, 6) = 30: 3A/30 = 5B/30 = 6C/30 => A/10 = B/6 = C/5 => A : B : C = 10 : 6 : 5.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

133. In a 40-liter mixture, the ratio of milk to water is 3 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 2 : 1?
(a) 8 liters (b) 10 liters (c) 6 liters (d) 5 liters
Answer: (d) 5 liters
Solution: Initial milk = (3/4) × 40 = 30 liters, water = 10 liters. In the new mixture, milk is 2 units = 30 liters => 1 unit = 15 liters. Water required = 1 unit = 15 liters. Water to add = 15 - 10 = 5 liters.
Source: SSC CHSL

134. If x : y = 3 : 2, what is the value of the algebraic ratio (2x² + 3y²) : (3x² - 2y²)?
(a) 30 : 19 (b) 28 : 19 (c) 30 : 17 (d) 26 : 19
Answer: (a) 30 : 19
Solution: Substitute x = 3 and y = 2: (2 × 9 + 3 × 4) : (3 × 9 - 2 × 4) = (18 + 12) : (27 - 8) = 30 : 19.
Source: RRB NTPC

135. The ratio of the present ages of P and Q is 5 : 7. Four years hence, the ratio of their ages will become 3 : 4. What is P's present age?
(a) 16 years (b) 20 years (c) 24 years (d) 28 years
Answer: (b) 20 years
Solution: Let present ages be 5x and 7x. Equation: (5x + 4) ÷ (7x + 4) = 3/4 => 4(5x + 4) = 3(7x + 4) => 20x + 16 = 21x + 12 => x = 4. P's present age = 5 × 4 = 20 years.
Source: SSC GD

136. A bag contains ₹1, 50-paise, and 25-paise coins in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. If the total money in the bag is ₹125, how many ₹1 coins are in the bag?
(a) 50 (b) 40 (c) 60 (d) 80
Answer: (c) 60
Solution: Total value in rupees = (3x × 1) + (4x × 0.50) + (5x × 0.25) = 3x + 2x + 1.25x = 6.25x. Given 6.25x = 125 => x = 20. Number of ₹1 coins = 3 × 20 = 60 coins.
Source: SSC CGL

137. What number must be added to each term of the ratio 7 : 11 so that the resulting ratio becomes 4 : 5?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (d) 9
Solution: Equation: (7 + x) ÷ (11 + x) = 4/5 => 5(7 + x) = 4(11 + x) => 35 + 5x = 44 + 4x => x = 9.
Source: SSC MTS

138. What is the fourth proportional to 4, 9, and 12?
(a) 27 (b) 24 (c) 30 (d) 36
Answer: (a) 27
Solution: 4 : 9 :: 12 : d => 4 × d = 9 × 12 = 108 => d = 108 ÷ 4 = 27.
Source: SSC CGL

139. The monthly incomes of A and B are in the ratio 5 : 3, and their monthly expenditures are in the ratio 9 : 5. If each saves ₹2,600 per month, what is the monthly income of A?
(a) ₹24,000 (b) ₹26,000 (c) ₹28,000 (d) ₹30,000
Answer: (b) ₹26,000
Solution: Equation: (5x - 2600) ÷ (3x - 2600) = 9/5 => 5(5x - 2600) = 9(3x - 2600) => 25x - 13000 = 27x - 23400 => 2x = 10400 => x = 5200. Income of A = 5 × 5200 = ₹26,000.
Source: SSC CHSL

140. If a : b = 2 : 5 and c : b = 3 : 4, what is the combined ratio a : b : c?
(a) 8 : 20 : 12 (b) 6 : 20 : 15 (c) 8 : 20 : 15 (d) 10 : 20 : 15
Answer: (c) 8 : 20 : 15
Solution: Here b is common. LCM of b values (5 and 4) is 20: a : b = 8 : 20 and b : c = 20 : 15. Therefore, a : b : c = 8 : 20 : 15.
Source: RRB Group D

141. If (a + b) : (b + c) : (c + a) = 4 : 7 : 9 and a + b + c = 30, what is the value of a?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (d) 9
Solution: Summing ratios: 2(a + b + c) = (4 + 7 + 9)k = 20k. Since a + b + c = 30, 2 × 30 = 20k => k = 3. Then b + c = 7k = 7 × 3 = 21. a = (a + b + c) - (b + c) = 30 - 21 = 9.
Source: SSC MTS

142. What is the sub-triplicate ratio of 64 : 125?
(a) 4 : 5 (b) 5 : 4 (c) 16 : 25 (d) 2 : 5
Answer: (a) 4 : 5
Solution: Sub-triplicate ratio of a : b is ∛a : ∛b. Here, ∛64 : ∛125 = 4 : 5.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

143. A sum of ₹3,150 is divided among A, B, and C in the ratio 1/3 : 1/5 : 1/6. What is the share of A?
(a) ₹1,200 (b) ₹1,500 (c) ₹900 (d) ₹750
Answer: (b) ₹1,500
Solution: Multiply by LCM(3, 5, 6) = 30: Ratio = 10 : 6 : 5. Total parts = 10 + 6 + 5 = 21 parts = ₹3,150 => 1 part = ₹150. A's share = 10 × ₹150 = ₹1,500.
Source: SSC CGL

144. If x : y = 5 : 2, what is the value of the algebraic ratio (8x + 9y) : (8x + 2y)?
(a) 27 : 22 (b) 29 : 20 (c) 29 : 22 (d) 31 : 22
Answer: (c) 29 : 22
Solution: Substitute x = 5 and y = 2: (8 × 5 + 9 × 2) : (8 × 5 + 2 × 2) = (40 + 18) : (40 + 4) = 58 : 44 = 29 : 22.
Source: SSC CHSL

145. The interior angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 3 : 5 : 9 : 13. What is the measure of the smallest angle?
(a) 24° (b) 30° (c) 40° (d) 36°
Answer: (d) 36°
Solution: Sum of angles in a quadrilateral is 360°. Total ratio parts = 3 + 5 + 9 + 13 = 30 parts = 360° => 1 part = 12°. Smallest angle = 3 × 12° = 36°.
Source: RRB NTPC

146. Two alloys A and B contain zinc and tin in the ratio 5 : 2 and 3 : 4 respectively. 7 kg of alloy A and 21 kg of alloy B are melted together to form a new alloy. What is the ratio of zinc and tin in the new alloy?
(a) 1 : 1 (b) 2 : 1 (c) 1 : 2 (d) 3 : 4
Answer: (a) 1 : 1
Solution: In 7 kg of A: Zinc = (5/7) × 7 = 5 kg, Tin = 2 kg. In 21 kg of B: Zinc = (3/7) × 21 = 9 kg, Tin = 12 kg. Combined: Total Zinc = 5 + 9 = 14 kg. Total Tin = 2 + 12 = 14 kg. Ratio Zinc : Tin = 14 : 14 = 1 : 1.
Source: SSC CGL

147. A, B, and C start a business with investments in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. After 2 months, A doubles his investment capital. If the total annual profit is ₹3,780, what is B's share of the profit?
(a) ₹720 (b) ₹840 (c) ₹960 (d) ₹1,050
Answer: (b) ₹840
Solution: Initial investment ratio = 6 : 4 : 3. A's total investment-months = (6 × 2) + (12 × 10) = 12 + 120 = 132 units. B's = 4 × 12 = 48 units. C's = 3 × 12 = 36 units. Profit ratio = 132 : 48 : 36 = 11 : 4 : 3. Total units = 11 + 4 + 3 = 18 units = ₹3,780 => 1 unit = ₹210. B's share = 4 × ₹210 = ₹840.
Source: SSC CGL

148. A vessel contains 60 liters of pure milk. 12 liters of milk is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated one more time (total of 2 replacements). What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(a) 16 : 7 (b) 25 : 16 (c) 16 : 9 (d) 9 : 16
Answer: (c) 16 : 9
Solution: Remaining milk = 60 × (1 - 12/60)² = 60 × (4/5)² = 60 × (16/25) = 38.4 liters. Water = 60 - 38.4 = 21.6 liters. Ratio Milk : Water = 38.4 : 21.6 = 384 : 216 = 16 : 9.
Source: SSC CHSL

149. The ratio of incomes of A and B is 3 : 5, and the ratio of their expenditures is 1 : 2. If each saves ₹5,000 at the end of the month, what is the monthly income of B?
(a) ₹15,000 (b) ₹20,000 (c) ₹30,000 (d) ₹25,000
Answer: (d) ₹25,000
Solution: Equation: (3x - 5000) ÷ (5x - 5000) = 1/2 => 2(3x - 5000) = 5x - 5000 => 6x - 10000 = 5x - 5000 => x = 5000. Income of B = 5 × 5000 = ₹25,000.
Source: SSC CGL

150. A hound pursues a hare and takes 3 leaps for every 4 leaps of the hare, but 2 leaps of the hound are equal in distance to 3 leaps of the hare. What is the ratio of the speed of the hound to that of the hare?
(a) 8 : 9 (b) 9 : 8 (c) 3 : 4 (d) 6 : 5
Answer: (b) 9 : 8
Solution: Distance per leap: 2 Hound leaps = 3 Hare leaps => Hound leap / Hare leap = 3/2. Leap frequency: Hound / Hare = 3/4. Ratio of speed = (Frequency × Leap distance) = (3 × 3) : (4 × 2) = 9 : 8.
Source: SSC CGL
