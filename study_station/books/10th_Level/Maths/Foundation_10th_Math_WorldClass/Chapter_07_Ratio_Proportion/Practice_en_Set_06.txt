Q126. A vessel contains milk and water in the ratio 5:4. When 18 litres of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water, milk becomes 50% of the mixture. Find the initial quantity of milk (in litres).
A) 80
B) 90
C) 100
D) 120

Answer: C) 100
Solution: Let milk = 5x, water = 4x, total = 9x. After removal: milk = 5x – (5/9)×18 = 5x – 10; water = 4x – (4/9)×18 + 18 = 4x – 8 + 18 = 4x + 10. Milk = 50% of mixture ⇒ (5x–10) / 9x = 1/2 ⇒ 10x–20 = 9x ⇒ x = 20. Milk = 5×20 = 100 L.
Source: PYQ-style

Q127. The ratio of incomes of A and B is 7:5 and their expenditures are in the ratio 5:4. If A saves ₹4,000 and B saves ₹2,000, what is A's income?
A) ₹12,000
B) ₹14,000
C) ₹16,000
D) ₹18,000

Answer: B) ₹14,000
Solution: A = 7x, B = 5x; expenses A = 5y, B = 4y.
7x – 5y = 4000 …(i)
5x – 4y = 2000 …(ii)
Multiply (i) by 4: 28x – 20y = 16000; (ii) by 5: 25x – 20y = 10000. Subtract: 3x = 6000 ⇒ x = 2000. A's income = 7×2000 = 14000.
Source: Bank PO 2017 (adapted)

Q128. A, B, C start a business with investments in the ratio 4:5:6. After 3 months, A withdraws half of his capital, B adds 1/5th of his initial capital, and C doubles his capital. If the annual profit is ₹1,44,000, find B's share.
A) ₹40,320
B) ₹44,160
C) ₹46,080
D) ₹52,800

Answer: B) ₹44,160
Solution: Let initial capitals: A=4k, B=5k, C=6k.
A: first 3 months 4k, next 9 months 2k → effective = 4k×3 + 2k×9 = 12k+18k = 30k.
B: first 3 months 5k, next 9 months 5k + (1/5)×5k = 6k → effective = 5k×3 + 6k×9 = 15k+54k = 69k.
C: first 3 months 6k, next 9 months 12k → effective = 6k×3 + 12k×9 = 18k+108k = 126k.
Profit ratio A:B:C = 30:69:126 = 10:23:42. Total parts = 75. B's share = (23/75)×144000 = 44160.
Source: CAT 2018 (adapted)

Q129. A vessel contains milk and water in the ratio 4:1. When 10 litres of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water, the ratio becomes 2:3. Find the initial quantity of milk (in litres).
A) 12
B) 16
C) 20
D) 24

Answer: B) 16
Solution: Let milk=4x, water=x. Removal: milk removed = (4/5)×10=8, water removed=2.
Milk left = 4x–8, water left = x–2+10 = x+8.
New ratio (4x–8)/(x+8) = 2/3 ⇒ 12x–24 = 2x+16 ⇒ 10x = 40 ⇒ x=4. Milk = 4×4=16 L.
Source: PYQ-style

Q130. The ratio of present ages of a mother and daughter is 9:2. Ten years ago, the product of their ages was 800. Find the mother's present age (in years).
A) 72
B) 81
C) 90
D) 99

Answer: C) 90
Solution: Present mother=9x, daughter=2x.
(9x–10)(2x–10)=800 ⇒ 18x²–90x–20x+100=800 ⇒ 18x²–110x–700=0 ⇒ 9x²–55x–350=0.
Discriminant Δ = 3025+12600=15625, √Δ=125. x = (55+125)/(2×9)=180/18=10. Mother = 9×10=90.
Source: PYQ-style

Q131. A vessel contains 80 litres of pure milk. 10 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated two more times (thrice in all). Find the quantity of milk in the final mixture (correct to two decimal places).
A) 51.20 litres
B) 53.59 litres
C) 58.32 litres
D) 64.80 litres

Answer: B) 53.59 litres
Solution: Milk after n operations = initial × (1 – replacement/volume)ⁿ = 80 × (1 – 10/80)³ = 80 × (7/8)³ = 80 × 343/512 = 27440/512 = 1715/32 ≈ 53.59375 litres ≈ 53.59 litres.
Source: Bank PO 2017

Q132. Three numbers are such that if the first is increased by 20%, the second is decreased by 25%, and the third is increased by 40%, they become equal. If the sum of the original numbers is 1210, find the first number.
A) 300
B) 350
C) 400
D) 420

Answer: B) 350
Solution: Let the equal value = k. Then first = k/1.2 = 5k/6, second = k/0.75 = 4k/3, third = k/1.4 = 5k/7.
Sum = k(5/6 + 4/3 + 5/7) = k(35/42 + 56/42 + 30/42) = (121k/42) = 1210 ⇒ k = 1210 × 42 / 121 = 10 × 42 = 420.
First number = 5×420/6 = 350.
Source: PYQ-style

Q133. A, B, C enter a partnership. A invests 4 times as much as B, and B invests 1.5 times as much as C. After 4 months, A doubles his investment, B halves his, and C triples his. If the annual profit is ₹1,98,000, find A's share.
A) ₹1,32,000
B) ₹1,48,500
C) ₹1,56,000
D) ₹1,62,000

Answer: B) ₹1,48,500
Solution: Let C's initial = x, then B = 1.5x, A = 4×1.5x = 6x.
First 4 months: A=6x, B=1.5x, C=x. Next 8 months: A=12x, B=0.75x, C=3x.
Effective capital: A = 6x×4 + 12x×8 = 24x + 96x = 120x; B = 1.5x×4 + 0.75x×8 = 6x+6x = 12x; C = x×4 + 3x×8 = 4x+24x = 28x.
Profit ratio A:B:C = 120:12:28 = 30:3:7. Total parts = 40. A's share = (30/40)×198000 = ₹1,48,500.
Source: CAT 2019 (adapted)

Q134. In a bag, the number of ₹1, 50p and 25p coins are in the ratio 5:3:2. Some ₹1 coins are removed and an equal number of 50p coins are added. As a result, the total value decreases by ₹13 and the ratio of ₹1 to 50p coins becomes 4:5. Find the original number of 50p coins.
A) 45
B) 54
C) 63
D) 72

Answer: B) 54
Solution: Let original coins: ₹1=5x, 50p=3x, 25p=2x. Remove n ₹1 coins, add n 50p coins. Value change = –n + 0.5n = –0.5n = –13 ⇒ n = 26.
New ₹1 count = 5x–26, 50p count = 3x+26. Ratio = (5x–26)/(3x+26) = 4/5 ⇒ 25x–130 = 12x+104 ⇒ 13x = 234 ⇒ x = 18. Original 50p coins = 3×18 = 54.
Source: RBI Grade B 2018 (adapted)

Q135. Two alloys A and B contain copper and zinc in the ratios 5:3 and 3:7 respectively. In what ratio must A and B be mixed to obtain an alloy containing copper and zinc in the ratio 7:9?
A) 5:9
B) 9:5
C) 11:15
D) 15:11

Answer: C) 11:15
Solution: Copper fraction in A = 5/8, in B = 3/10, desired = 7/16.
Using alligation:
Ratio of A:B = (desired – B) : (A – desired) = (7/16 – 3/10) : (5/8 – 7/16)
= (70/160 – 48/160) : (10/16 – 7/16) = (22/160) : (3/16) = (22/160)×(16/3) = (22×16)/(160×3) = 352/480 = 11/15.
Thus A:B = 11:15.
Source: PYQ-style

Q136. A and B together can complete a work in 10 days. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves and B finishes the remaining work in 10 days. If they are paid ₹3,000 for the whole work, how much does A receive?
A) ₹400
B) ₹480
C) ₹600
D) ₹720

Answer: B) ₹480
Solution: (A+B)'s 1 day work = 1/10. In 4 days, work done = 4/10 = 2/5. Remaining = 3/5 done by B in 10 days ⇒ B's 1 day work = (3/5)/10 = 3/50.
A's 1 day work = 1/10 – 3/50 = 2/50 = 1/25.
A worked 4 days ⇒ A's work = 4/25; B worked 14 days ⇒ B's work = 14 × (3/50) = 21/25.
Payment ratio A:B = 4/25 : 21/25 = 4:21. A's share = (4/25)×3000 = ₹480.
Source: PYQ-style

Q139. In a 1 km race, A beats B by 50 metres. In an 800 metre race, B beats C by 40 metres. By how many metres will A beat C in a 600 metre race?
A) 45 m
B) 50.5 m
C) 58.5 m
D) 60 m

Answer: C) 58.5 m
Solution: Speed ratio A:B = 1000:950 = 20:19. Speed ratio B:C = 800:760 = 20:19.
Thus A:C = (20×20):(19×19) = 400:361.
In 600 m race, when A runs 600 m, C runs (361/400)×600 = 361×1.5 = 541.5 m.
A beats C by 600 – 541.5 = 58.5 m.
Source: PYQ-style

Q140. A vessel contains milk and water in the ratio 7:5. When 9 litres of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water, the ratio becomes 7:9. Find the initial quantity of milk (in litres).
A) 18
B) 21
C) 28
D) 35

Answer: B) 21
Solution: Let milk=7x, water=5x. Removal: milk removed = (7/12)×9 = 21/4 = 5.25, water removed = (5/12)×9 = 15/4 = 3.75.
Milk left = 7x – 5.25, water left = 5x – 3.75 + 9 = 5x + 5.25.
Ratio: (7x–5.25)/(5x+5.25) = 7/9 ⇒ 63x – 47.25 = 35x + 36.75 ⇒ 28x = 84 ⇒ x = 3. Milk = 7×3 = 21 L.
Source: PYQ-style

Q141. The cost of a piece of cloth varies directly as the square of its length. A piece of cloth 12 m long costs ₹432. What is the cost of a piece 16 m long?
A) ₹576
B) ₹648
C) ₹768
D) ₹864

Answer: C) ₹768
Solution: Cost ∝ (length)² ⇒ C₁/C₂ = (L₁/L₂)². 432/C₂ = (12/16)² = 9/16. C₂ = 432 × 16/9 = 48 × 16 = ₹768.

Q149. A vessel is filled with liquid, 3 parts of which are water and 5 parts are syrup. What fraction of the mixture must be drawn off and replaced with water so that the mixture becomes half water and half syrup?
A) 1/3
B) 1/4
C) 1/5
D) 2/5

Answer: C) 1/5
Solution: Let total volume = V. Syrup initial = 5/8 V. After replacing fraction f with water, syrup left = (5/8)V (1–f). We want syrup = 1/2 V. So (5/8)(1–f) = 1/2 ⇒ 1–f = (1/2)×(8/5) = 4/5 ⇒ f = 1/5.
Source: PYQ-style

Q150. The ratio of incomes of A and B is 7:4, and their expenditures are in the ratio 5:3. If A saves ₹2,000 and B saves ₹1,000, what is A's income?
A) ₹5,000
B) ₹6,000
C) ₹7,000
D) ₹8,000

Answer: C) ₹7,000
Solution: A=7x, B=4x; exp A=5y, B=3y.
7x–5y = 2000 …(i)
4x–3y = 1000 …(ii)
Multiply (i) by 3: 21x–15y = 6000; (ii) by 5: 20x–15y = 5000. Subtract: x = 1000. A's income = 7×1000 = ₹7,000.
Source: PYQ-style
