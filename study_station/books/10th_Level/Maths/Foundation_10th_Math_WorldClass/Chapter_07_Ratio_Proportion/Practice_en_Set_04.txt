76. If (2x + 3y) : (3x + 5y) = 18 : 29, what is the ratio x : y?
(a) 3 : 4 (b) 4 : 3 (c) 2 : 3 (d) 3 : 5
Answer: (a) 3 : 4
Solution: Cross-multiplying: 29(2x + 3y) = 18(3x + 5y) => 58x + 87y = 54x + 90y => 58x - 54x = 90y - 87y => 4x = 3y => x/y = 3/4 = 3 : 4.
Source: SSC CGL

77. What is the mean proportional between 0.32 and 0.02?
(a) 0.06 (b) 0.08 (c) 0.16 (d) 0.04
Answer: (b) 0.08
Solution: Mean proportional = √(0.32 × 0.02) = √(0.0064) = 0.08.
Source: SSC CHSL

78. If A : B = 3 : 5 and B : C = 2 : 3, what is the combined ratio A : B : C?
(a) 6 : 10 : 12 (b) 6 : 8 : 15 (c) 6 : 10 : 15 (d) 9 : 10 : 15
Answer: (c) 6 : 10 : 15
Solution: LCM of B's values (5 and 2) is 10. A : B = 6 : 10 and B : C = 10 : 15. Therefore, A : B : C = 6 : 10 : 15.
Source: RRB Group D

79. Two numbers are in the ratio 2 : 3. If 2 is subtracted from the first and 2 is added to the second, the ratio becomes 1 : 2. What is the sum of the two original numbers?
(a) 24 (b) 28 (c) 32 (d) 30
Answer: (d) 30
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 24 comes from incorrectly applying subtraction to both terms! Equation: (2x - 2) ÷ (3x + 2) = 1/2 => 2(2x - 2) = 3x + 2 => 4x - 4 = 3x + 2 => x = 6. Original numbers are 2 × 6 = 12 and 3 × 6 = 18. Sum = 12 + 18 = 30.
Source: SSC CGL

80. A sum of ₹3,700 is divided among A, B, and C such that (A's share / B's share) = (B's share / C's share) = 3/4. How much money does A receive?
(a) ₹900 (b) ₹1,200 (c) ₹1,600 (d) ₹1,000
Answer: (a) ₹900
Solution: A : B = 3 : 4 = 9 : 12, B : C = 3 : 4 = 12 : 16. Combined ratio A : B : C = 9 : 12 : 16. Total parts = 9 + 12 + 16 = 37 parts = ₹3,700 => 1 part = ₹100. A's share = 9 × ₹100 = ₹900.
Source: SSC MTS

81. In a competitive examination, the ratio of selected to unselected candidates was 9 : 2. If 80 fewer candidates had applied and 20 fewer were selected, the ratio of selected to unselected candidates would have been 5 : 1. How many candidates had applied for the examination originally?
(a) 2520 (b) 3080 (c) 2800 (d) 3300
Answer: (b) 3080
Solution: Trap alert: Option (c) 2800 calculates based on unselected remaining constant, which is wrong! Let selected = 9x, unselected = 2x => Total applied = 11x. If 80 fewer applied: New total = 11x - 80. If 20 fewer selected: New selected = 9x - 20. Then new unselected = (11x - 80) - (9x - 20) = 2x - 60. Ratio: (9x - 20) ÷ (2x - 60) = 5/1 => 9x - 20 = 10x - 300 => x = 280. Total candidates applied = 11x = 11 × 280 = 3080.
Source: SSC CGL

82. If a : b = c : d, then what is the value of (ma + nc) : (mb + nd)?
(a) m : n (b) a : d (c) a : b (d) b : d
Answer: (c) a : b
Solution: Let a/b = c/d = k => a = bk and c = dk. (ma + nc)/(mb + nd) = (mbk + ndk)/(mb + nd) = k(mb + nd)/(mb + nd) = k = a/b = a : b.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

83. The ratio of the number of coins of 25-paise, 50-paise, and ₹1 is 8 : 4 : 3. If their total value is ₹140, how many 25-paise coins are there?
(a) 120 (b) 140 (c) 150 (d) 160
Answer: (d) 160
Solution: Value in rupees = (8x × 0.25) + (4x × 0.50) + (3x × 1) = 2x + 2x + 3x = 7x. 7x = 140 => x = 20. Number of 25-paise coins = 8 × 20 = 160 coins.
Source: SSC CHSL

84. If (x - 2) : (x + 6) is the duplicate ratio of 1 : 3, what is the value of x?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (a) 3
Solution: Duplicate ratio of 1 : 3 is 1² : 3² = 1 : 9. Equation: (x - 2) ÷ (x + 6) = 1/9 => 9(x - 2) = x + 6 => 9x - 18 = x + 6 => 8x = 24 => x = 3.
Source: RRB NTPC

85. In an alloy, the ratio of copper to zinc is 7 : 3. If 28 kg of copper is used to prepare the alloy, how much zinc is required?
(a) 10 kg (b) 12 kg (c) 14 kg (d) 15 kg
Answer: (b) 12 kg
Solution: Copper is 7 units = 28 kg => 1 unit = 4 kg. Required zinc = 3 units = 3 × 4 = 12 kg.
Source: SSC GD

86. The ratio of the present ages of a father and his son is 6 : 1. Five years hence, the ratio of their ages will become 7 : 2. What is the present age of the son?
(a) 4 years (b) 6 years (c) 5 years (d) 7 years
Answer: (c) 5 years
Solution: Let present ages be 6x and x. Equation: (6x + 5) ÷ (x + 5) = 7/2 => 2(6x + 5) = 7(x + 5) => 12x + 10 = 7x + 35 => 5x = 25 => x = 5. Son's present age = 5 years.
Source: SSC CGL

87. Find the value of x in the proportion 0.75 : x :: 5 : 8.
(a) 1.15 (b) 1.25 (c) 1.10 (d) 1.20
Answer: (d) 1.20
Solution: Product of extremes = Product of means => 5 × x = 0.75 × 8 => 5x = 6.0 => x = 6.0 ÷ 5 = 1.20.
Source: SSC MTS

88. The weekly incomes of two persons are in the ratio 9 : 7, and their weekly expenditures are in the ratio 4 : 3. If each saves ₹200 per week, what is the sum of their weekly incomes?
(a) ₹3,200 (b) ₹2,800 (c) ₹3,000 (d) ₹3,400
Answer: (a) ₹3,200
Solution: Equation: (9x - 200) ÷ (7x - 200) = 4/3 => 3(9x - 200) = 4(7x - 200) => 27x - 600 = 28x - 800 => x = 200. Incomes are 9 × 200 = ₹1,800 and 7 × 200 = ₹1,400. Sum = 1800 + 1400 = ₹3,200.
Source: SSC CGL

89. A person distributes his pens among four friends A, B, C, and D in the ratio 1/3 : 1/4 : 1/5 : 1/6. What is the minimum number of pens that the person should have?
(a) 55 (b) 57 (c) 60 (d) 62
Answer: (b) 57
Solution: LCM of denominators (3, 4, 5, 6) is 60. Integer ratio = (1/3 × 60) : (1/4 × 60) : (1/5 × 60) : (1/6 × 60) = 20 : 15 : 12 : 10. Minimum total pens = 20 + 15 + 12 + 10 = 57 pens.
Source: SSC CHSL

90. If A : B = 2 : 3, B : C = 4 : 5, and C : D = 6 : 7, what is the ratio A : B : C : D?
(a) 16 : 22 : 30 : 35 (b) 14 : 24 : 30 : 35 (c) 16 : 24 : 30 : 35 (d) 16 : 24 : 28 : 35
Answer: (c) 16 : 24 : 30 : 35
Solution: A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15. Multiply by 2: 16 : 24 : 30. C : D = 6 : 7, multiply by 5: 30 : 35. Therefore, A : B : C : D = 16 : 24 : 30 : 35.
Source: RRB Group D

91. If x² + 4y² = 4xy, what is the ratio x : y?
(a) 1 : 2 (b) 1 : 1 (c) 4 : 1 (d) 2 : 1
Answer: (d) 2 : 1
Solution: Rearranging: x² - 4xy + 4y² = 0 => (x - 2y)² = 0 => x - 2y = 0 => x = 2y => x/y = 2/1 = 2 : 1.
Source: SSC MTS

92. In a 60-liter mixture, the ratio of milk and water is 2 : 1. If this ratio is to be made 1 : 2, what quantity of water must be further added?
(a) 60 liters (b) 40 liters (c) 50 liters (d) 70 liters
Answer: (a) 60 liters
Solution: Initial milk = 40 liters, water = 20 liters. In the new ratio 1 : 2, milk remains 40 liters (1 unit). Required water = 2 × 40 = 80 liters. Water to add = 80 - 20 = 60 liters.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

93. The salaries of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 5. If each receives a salary increment of ₹5,000, the new ratio becomes 2 : 3. What is B's present salary before increment?
(a) ₹20,000 (b) ₹25,000 (c) ₹30,000 (d) ₹35,000
Answer: (b) ₹25,000
Solution: Equation: (3x + 5000) ÷ (5x + 5000) = 2/3 => 3(3x + 5000) = 2(5x + 5000) => 9x + 15000 = 10x + 10000 => x = 5000. B's present salary = 5 × 5000 = ₹25,000.
Source: SSC CGL

94. If (a + b) : (a - b) = 7 : 3, what is the ratio (a² + b²) : (a² - b²)?
(a) 25 : 21 (b) 27 : 21 (c) 29 : 21 (d) 29 : 19
Answer: (c) 29 : 21
Solution: By componendo and dividendo: a/b = (7 + 3)/(7 - 3) = 10/4 = 5/2. Then a² = 25 and b² = 4. (a² + b²) : (a² - b²) = (25 + 4) : (25 - 4) = 29 : 21.
Source: SSC CHSL

95. What number must be added to each of 10, 18, 22, and 38 so that the resulting numbers are in proportion?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (d) 2
Solution: (10 + x)(38 + x) = (18 + x)(22 + x) => 380 + 48x + x² = 396 + 40x + x² => 48x - 40x = 396 - 380 => 8x = 16 => x = 2.
Source: RRB NTPC

96. The ratio of the incomes of A and B is 5 : 3, and the ratio of their expenditures is 9 : 5. If the income of A is twice the expenditure of B, what is the ratio of their savings?
(a) 1 : 1 (b) 2 : 1 (c) 3 : 2 (d) 4 : 3
Answer: (a) 1 : 1
Solution: Let incomes be 5x, 3x and expenditures be 9y, 5y. Given 5x = 2(5y) = 10y => x = 2y. A's income = 10y, expenditure = 9y => A's savings = 10y - 9y = y. B's income = 6y, expenditure = 5y => B's savings = 6y - 5y = y. Ratio of savings = y : y = 1 : 1.
Source: SSC CGL

97. An alloy contains copper and tin in the ratio 3 : 2. In another alloy, copper and tin are in the ratio 4 : 5. If equal weights of both alloys are melted together, what is the ratio of copper to tin in the resulting alloy?
(a) 45 : 43 (b) 47 : 43 (c) 43 : 47 (d) 49 : 41
Answer: (b) 47 : 43
Solution: Let weight of each alloy = LCM(5, 9) = 45 kg. In 1st alloy: Copper = (3/5) × 45 = 27 kg, Tin = 18 kg. In 2nd alloy: Copper = (4/9) × 45 = 20 kg, Tin = 25 kg. Resulting alloy: Copper = 27 + 20 = 47 kg, Tin = 18 + 25 = 43 kg. Ratio = 47 : 43.
Source: SSC CGL

98. Two vessels contain milk and water in the ratio 7 : 5 and 7 : 9 respectively. If equal volumes from both vessels are mixed together, what is the ratio of milk to water in the new mixture?
(a) 47 : 49 (b) 48 : 47 (c) 49 : 47 (d) 51 : 45
Answer: (c) 49 : 47
Solution: Let volume from each vessel = LCM(12, 16) = 48 liters. From 1st: Milk = (7/12) × 48 = 28 L, Water = 20 L. From 2nd: Milk = (7/16) × 48 = 21 L, Water = 27 L. Total Milk = 28 + 21 = 49 L. Total Water = 20 + 27 = 47 L. Ratio = 49 : 47.
Source: SSC CHSL

99. A sum of money is divided among A, B, and C such that A gets ₹20 more than B, and B gets ₹30 more than C. If the ratio of shares of A and C is 5 : 3, what is the total sum divided?
(a) ₹285 (b) ₹295 (c) ₹315 (d) ₹305
Answer: (d) ₹305
Solution: Let C's share = c. Then B = c + 30 and A = (c + 30) + 20 = c + 50. Given A / C = 5/3 => (c + 50)/c = 5/3 => 3c + 150 = 5c => 2c = 150 => c = 75. Shares: C = 75, B = 105, A = 125. Total sum = 125 + 105 + 75 = ₹305.
Source: SSC CGL

100. The ratio of incomes of A and B is 4 : 3, and their expenditures are in the ratio 3 : 2. If A saves ₹6,000 and B saves ₹6,000 per month, what is the total combined monthly income of both?
(a) ₹36,000 (b) ₹42,000 (c) ₹48,000 (d) ₹54,000
Answer: (b) ₹42,000
Solution: Equation: (4x - 6000) ÷ (3x - 6000) = 3/2 => 2(4x - 6000) = 3(3x - 6000) => 8x - 12000 = 9x - 18000 => x = 6000. Combined income = (4 + 3)x = 7 × 6000 = ₹42,000.
Source: SSC CGL
