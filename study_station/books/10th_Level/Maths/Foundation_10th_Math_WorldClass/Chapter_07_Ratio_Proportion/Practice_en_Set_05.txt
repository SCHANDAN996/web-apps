Q101. Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If 9 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 12:23. Find the larger number.
A) 45
B) 55
C) 65
D) 75

Answer: B) 55
Solution: Let numbers be 3x, 5x. (3x–9)/(5x–9) = 12/23 → 23(3x–9) = 12(5x–9) → 69x – 207 = 60x – 108 → 9x = 99 → x = 11. Larger = 5×11 = 55.
Source: PYQ-style
