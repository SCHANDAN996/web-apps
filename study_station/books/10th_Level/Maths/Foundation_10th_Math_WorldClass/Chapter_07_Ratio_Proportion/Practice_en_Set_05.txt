101. If (3x + 2y) : (3x - 2y) = 5 : 2, what is the ratio x : y?
(a) 14 : 9 (b) 9 : 14 (c) 7 : 9 (d) 9 : 7
Answer: (a) 14 : 9
Solution: Cross-multiplying: 2(3x + 2y) = 5(3x - 2y) => 6x + 4y = 15x - 10y => 15x - 6x = 4y + 10y => 9x = 14y => x/y = 14/9 = 14 : 9.
Source: SSC CGL

102. What is the mean proportional between 6 and 54?
(a) 16 (b) 18 (c) 24 (d) 27
Answer: (b) 18
Solution: Mean proportional = √(6 × 54) = √324 = 18.
Source: SSC CHSL

103. If A : B = 5 : 7 and B : C = 6 : 11, what is the ratio A : B : C?
(a) 30 : 42 : 70 (b) 35 : 42 : 77 (c) 30 : 42 : 77 (d) 25 : 42 : 77
Answer: (c) 30 : 42 : 77
Solution: Multiply first ratio by 6 and second by 7: A : B = 30 : 42 and B : C = 42 : 77. Therefore, A : B : C = 30 : 42 : 77.
Source: RRB Group D

104. What is the third proportional to 16 and 36?
(a) 56 (b) 64 (c) 72 (d) 81
Answer: (d) 81
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) 56 incorrectly adds the difference 36 - 16 = 20 to 36! Third proportional c satisfies 16 : 36 :: 36 : c => 16 × c = 36 × 36 = 1296 => c = 1296 ÷ 16 = 81.
Source: SSC CGL

105. A sum of ₹2,400 is divided among A, B, and C in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. How much more money does C receive than A?
(a) ₹400 (b) ₹300 (c) ₹500 (d) ₹600
Answer: (a) ₹400
Solution: Total ratio units = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 units = ₹2,400 => 1 unit = ₹200. Difference between C and A = 5 - 3 = 2 units = 2 × ₹200 = ₹400.
Source: SSC MTS

106. The weekly wages of two workers are in the ratio 4 : 5. If their wages are increased by ₹50 each, the ratio of their wages becomes 9 : 11. What is the original weekly wage of the first worker?
(a) ₹500 (b) ₹400 (c) ₹450 (d) ₹350
Answer: (b) ₹400
Solution: Trap alert: Option (a) ₹500 is the wage of the second worker! Equation: (4x + 50) ÷ (5x + 50) = 9/11 => 11(4x + 50) = 9(5x + 50) => 44x + 550 = 45x + 450 => x = 100. Original wage of the first worker = 4 × 100 = ₹400.
Source: SSC CGL

107. If a : b = 5 : 3, what is the value of the algebraic ratio (8a - 5b) : (8a + 5b)?
(a) 3 : 11 (b) 4 : 11 (c) 5 : 11 (d) 6 : 11
Answer: (c) 5 : 11
Solution: Substitute a = 5 and b = 3: (8 × 5 - 5 × 3) : (8 × 5 + 5 × 3) = (40 - 15) : (40 + 15) = 25 : 55 = 5 : 11.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

108. In a mixture of 28 liters, the ratio of milk to water is 5 : 2. If 2 liters of water is added to the mixture, what will be the new ratio of milk to water?
(a) 5 : 3 (b) 3 : 1 (c) 4 : 1 (d) 2 : 1
Answer: (d) 2 : 1
Solution: Milk = (5/7) × 28 = 20 liters, Water = 8 liters. After adding 2 liters of water: New water = 8 + 2 = 10 liters. Milk remains 20 liters. New ratio = 20 : 10 = 2 : 1.
Source: SSC CHSL

109. If (x + 3) : (2x - 1) = 3 : 5, what is the value of x?
(a) 18 (b) 16 (c) 20 (d) 22
Answer: (a) 18
Solution: Cross-multiplying: 5(x + 3) = 3(2x - 1) => 5x + 15 = 6x - 3 => 6x - 5x = 15 + 3 => x = 18.
Source: RRB NTPC

110. In an examination, 420 candidates appeared. The ratio of passed candidates to failed candidates was 5 : 2. How many candidates passed the examination?
(a) 280 (b) 300 (c) 320 (d) 350
Answer: (b) 300
Solution: Total parts = 5 + 2 = 7 parts = 420 candidates => 1 part = 60. Passed candidates = 5 × 60 = 300 candidates.
Source: SSC GD

111. If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 8 : 10, what is the ratio A : C in simplest form?
(a) 1 : 2 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 3 : 5 (d) 4 : 5
Answer: (c) 3 : 5
Solution: A/C = (A/B) × (B/C) = (3/4) × (8/10) = 24/40 = 3/5 = 3 : 5.
Source: SSC CGL

112. The ratio of the ages of a mother and her daughter is 7 : 3. If the difference between their ages is 28 years, what is the present age of the mother?
(a) 42 years (b) 45 years (c) 52 years (d) 49 years
Answer: (d) 49 years
Solution: Difference in ratio = 7 - 3 = 4 units = 28 years => 1 unit = 7 years. Mother's age = 7 × 7 = 49 years.
Source: SSC MTS

113. A box contains 280 coins consisting of ₹1, 50-paise, and 25-paise coins. The ratio of their monetary values is 8 : 4 : 3. What is the total number of 50-paise coins in the box?
(a) 80 (b) 70 (c) 90 (d) 100
Answer: (a) 80
Solution: Monetary value ratio is 8 : 4 : 3. Coin count ratio = (8 × 1) : (4 × 2) : (3 × 4) = 8 : 8 : 12 = 2 : 2 : 3. Total units = 2 + 2 + 3 = 7 units = 280 coins => 1 unit = 40. Number of 50-paise coins = 2 × 40 = 80 coins.
Source: SSC CGL

114. Find the fourth proportional to the three numbers 0.12, 0.21, and 8.
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 18
Answer: (b) 14
Solution: 0.12 : 0.21 :: 8 : d => 0.12 × d = 0.21 × 8 => d = (0.21 × 8) ÷ 0.12 = 1.68 ÷ 0.12 = 14.
Source: SSC CHSL

115. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 4. If their Least Common Multiple (LCM) is 180, what is the greater number?
(a) 45 (b) 50 (c) 60 (d) 75
Answer: (c) 60
Solution: Let the numbers be 3x and 4x. Since 3 and 4 are co-prime, LCM = 3 × 4 × x = 12x = 180 => x = 15. The greater number is 4 × 15 = 60.
Source: RRB Group D

116. The ratio of copper to zinc in brass is 5 : 2. To 17.5 kg of brass, 1.25 kg of zinc is added. What is the new ratio of copper to zinc?
(a) 5 : 3 (b) 3 : 2 (c) 7 : 4 (d) 2 : 1
Answer: (d) 2 : 1
Solution: In 17.5 kg brass: Copper = (5/7) × 17.5 = 12.5 kg, Zinc = 5.0 kg. After adding 1.25 kg zinc: New zinc = 5.0 + 1.25 = 6.25 kg. Copper remains 12.5 kg. New ratio = 12.5 : 6.25 = 2 : 1.
Source: SSC MTS

117. If (x / y) = (3 / 5), what is the value of the algebraic ratio (5x + 2y) : (5x - 2y)?
(a) 5 : 1 (b) 4 : 1 (c) 6 : 1 (d) 7 : 2
Answer: (a) 5 : 1
Solution: Substitute x = 3 and y = 5: (5 × 3 + 2 × 5) : (5 × 3 - 2 × 5) = (15 + 10) : (15 - 10) = 25 : 5 = 5 : 1.
Source: Delhi Police Constable

118. What is the compound ratio of 3 : 5, 25 : 9, and 7 : 14?
(a) 4 : 5 (b) 5 : 6 (c) 5 : 7 (d) 6 : 7
Answer: (b) 5 : 6
Solution: Compound ratio = (3 × 25 × 7) : (5 × 9 × 14) = 525 : 630 = 5 : 6.
Source: SSC CGL

119. An amount of ₹1,530 is divided between A and B such that 4/7 of A's share is equal to 2/5 of B's share. What is B's share?
(a) ₹800 (b) ₹850 (c) ₹900 (d) ₹950
Answer: (c) ₹900
Solution: (4/7)A = (2/5)B => A/B = (2/5) ÷ (4/7) = (2/5) × (7/4) = 7/10 => A : B = 7 : 10. Total parts = 7 + 10 = 17 parts = ₹1,530 => 1 part = ₹90. B's share = 10 × ₹90 = ₹900.
Source: SSC CHSL

120. The monthly incomes of two persons are in the ratio 5 : 4, and their expenditures are in the ratio 3 : 2. If each saves ₹600 per month, what is the monthly expenditure of the first person?
(a) ₹600 (b) ₹750 (c) ₹800 (d) ₹900
Answer: (d) ₹900
Solution: Let incomes be 5x and 4x. Equation: (5x - 600) ÷ (4x - 600) = 3/2 => 2(5x - 600) = 3(4x - 600) => 10x - 1200 = 12x - 1800 => 2x = 600 => x = 300. Income of first person = 5 × 300 = ₹1,500. Expenditure of first person = Income - Savings = 1500 - 600 = ₹900.
Source: RRB NTPC

121. The ratio of incomes of A, B, and C is 7 : 9 : 12, and their expenditures are in the ratio 8 : 9 : 15. If A saves 1/4 of his income, what is the ratio of savings of A, B, and C?
(a) 56 : 99 : 69 (b) 56 : 95 : 69 (c) 52 : 99 : 69 (d) 56 : 99 : 72
Answer: (a) 56 : 99 : 69
Solution: Incomes = 7x, 9x, 12x. Expenditures = 8y, 9y, 15y. A's savings = 7x/4 => A's expenditure = (3/4)(7x) = 21x/4 = 8y => y = 21x/32. A's savings = 56x/32. B's savings = 9x - 9y = 9x - 9(21x/32) = 99x/32. C's savings = 12x - 15(21x/32) = 69x/32. Ratio of savings = 56 : 99 : 69.
Source: SSC CGL

122. A vessel contains a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 4 : 1. If 10 liters of the mixture is removed and replaced by 10 liters of water, the ratio of milk to water becomes 2 : 3. What was the initial quantity of milk in the vessel?
(a) 14 liters (b) 16 liters (c) 18 liters (d) 20 liters
Answer: (b) 16 liters
Solution: After removing 10 L, milk and water remain 4k and 1k. When 10 L water is added: 4k ÷ (k + 10) = 2/3 => 12k = 2k + 20 => 10k = 20 => k = 2. Remaining volume = 5k = 10 liters. Initial total volume = 10 + 10 = 20 liters. Initial milk = (4/5) × 20 = 16 liters.
Source: SSC CGL

123. In three vessels each of 2 liters capacity, the ratios of milk to water are 2 : 1, 3 : 1, and 3 : 2 respectively. If all three vessels are completely emptied into a single large vessel, what is the ratio of milk to water in the large vessel?
(a) 117 : 63 (b) 119 : 61 (c) 121 : 59 (d) 123 : 57
Answer: (c) 121 : 59
Solution: Milk in 1st = 2 × (2/3) = 4/3 L, Water = 2/3 L. Milk in 2nd = 2 × (3/4) = 3/2 L, Water = 1/2 L. Milk in 3rd = 2 × (3/5) = 6/5 L, Water = 4/5 L. Total Milk = 4/3 + 3/2 + 6/5 = 121/30 L. Total Water = 2/3 + 1/2 + 4/5 = 59/30 L. Ratio Milk : Water = 121 : 59.
Source: SSC CHSL

124. If (x + y) : (y + z) : (z + x) = 7 : 8 : 9 and x + y + z = 36, what is the value of z?
(a) 11 (b) 12 (c) 14 (d) 15
Answer: (d) 15
Solution: Sum of ratios = 2(x + y + z) = (7 + 8 + 9)k = 24k. Since x + y + z = 36, 2 × 36 = 24k => k = 3. Then x + y = 7k = 7 × 3 = 21. z = (x + y + z) - (x + y) = 36 - 21 = 15.
Source: SSC CGL

125. The ratio of students in three classes is 2 : 3 : 5. If 20 students are added to each class, the ratio becomes 4 : 5 : 7. What was the total number of students in all three classes originally?
(a) 80 (b) 100 (c) 120 (d) 150
Answer: (b) 100
Solution: In each class, the ratio term increases by 4 - 2 = 5 - 3 = 7 - 5 = 2 units. Given 2 units = 20 students => 1 unit = 10 students. Total students originally = (2 + 3 + 5) units = 10 × 10 = 100 students.
Source: SSC CGL
