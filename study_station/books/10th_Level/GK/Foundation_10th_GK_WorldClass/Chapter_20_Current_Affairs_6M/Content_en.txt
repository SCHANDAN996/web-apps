# Current Affairs (April – October 2026)

## The Hook
Imagine your teacher walks into class and asks: "Who won the FIFA World Cup this year? Which Indian city will host the 2030 Commonwealth Games?" Half the class looks at the floor. The other half answers in two seconds — not because they are smarter, but because they read the news in small, smart bites. This chapter is your bite-sized news diary.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:
1. Recall the most exam-worthy events of April – October 2026 under nine themes.
2. Link each event to its month, place, number and "first".
3. Spot the examiner's favourite traps in current-affairs options.
4. Revise the whole period in 15 minutes using the summary table.
