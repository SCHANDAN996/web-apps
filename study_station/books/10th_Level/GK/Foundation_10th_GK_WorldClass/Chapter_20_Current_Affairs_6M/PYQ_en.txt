# PYQ Analysis — Current Affairs (April – October 2026)

*(How school quizzes, Navodaya/Sainik-style tests, state Class 10 GK papers and the first rung of SSC/Railway exams ask current affairs. We describe the pattern of questions; we do not quote invented counts.)*

## 1. How exams ask current affairs

- **One-liners:** "Who won…", "Where was … held", "Who has been appointed…".
- **Number questions:** medal tallies, ranks, percentages, counts (Ramsar sites, Padma awards).
- **Firsts and records:** "first Indian to…", "record … time".
- **Pair-matching:** event ↔ host city, award ↔ winner, exercise ↔ partner country.

## 2. Themes examiners return to

- Awards and honours, appointments, sports events, defence tests and exercises, space missions, economy data, environment and heritage, government schemes, international summits.

## 3. Ten high-yield questions (PYQ-style, with answers)

**Q1.** Census-2027, whose first phase began on 1 April 2026, will be India's ____ Census in the series.
**Answer:** 16th
**Why:** It is the 16th Census in the series and the 8th since Independence, and the first conducted digitally.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q2.** India has been the world's second-largest producer of which commodity since 2018?
**Answer:** Steel
**Why:** India became the world's second-largest steel producer in 2018 and has retained the position.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3.** Who took oath as the first BJP Chief Minister of West Bengal in May 2026?
**Answer:** Suvendu Adhikari
**Why:** Governor R. N. Ravi administered the oath to Suvendu Adhikari at the Brigade Parade Grounds, Kolkata.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4.** Who took oath as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu in May 2026?
**Answer:** C. Joseph Vijay
**Why:** PM Modi congratulated Thiru C. Joseph Vijay on taking oath as the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q5.** Which film actor was conferred the Padma Vibhushan posthumously in 2026?
**Answer:** Dharmendra
**Why:** Actor Dharmendra Singh Deol was honoured with the Padma Vibhushan posthumously; his wife Hema Malini received it.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6.** Who took charge as the 27th Chief of the Naval Staff in May 2026?
**Answer:** Admiral Krishna Swaminathan
**Why:** Admiral Krishna Swaminathan succeeded Admiral Dinesh K. Tripathi as the 27th Chief of the Naval Staff.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7.** Which team won the IPL 2026 title?
**Answer:** Royal Challengers Bengaluru
**Why:** Defending champions RCB beat Gujarat Titans by five wickets in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad, to retain the title.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8.** Which country topped the medal table at the World Yogasana Championship 2026, held in Ahmedabad?
**Answer:** India
**Why:** India stayed at the top of the medal table, winning five gold medals at the first World Yogasana Championship in Ahmedabad.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9.** Who became the first Indian to win the Norway Chess title, in June 2026?
**Answer:** R. Praggnanandhaa
**Why:** R. Praggnanandhaa won the Norway Chess 2026 title, a first for an Indian.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10.** In June 2026, Narendra Modi became India's longest-serving elected Prime Minister, surpassing whose record?
**Answer:** Jawaharlal Nehru
**Why:** PM Modi, who first took oath on 26 May 2014, surpassed Pandit Nehru's tenure as an elected Prime Minister (1952–1964).
**Source:** PYQ-style

## 4. 15-second / 45-second rule

- Name/place questions: 15 seconds — you know it or you skip it.
- Number questions: up to 45 seconds — eliminate outdated figures first.
- Never spend more than 45 seconds on a single current-affairs question.
