🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Mnemonic 1: The Three Sectors
Acronym: P-S-T = "Pick, Shape, Transport"

Pick → Primary (pick from nature: crops, fish, wood)

Shape → Secondary (shape into goods: sugar, cloth, buildings)

Transport → Tertiary (move, sell and serve: transport, banking, trade)

Visual: A farmer picks cotton, a weaver shapes it into a shirt, a truck transports it to a shop.

🧠 Mnemonic 2: GDP vs GNP
Rule: "Domestic = Door, National = Nagrik"

Domestic → inside the door (border) of India — whoever produces.

National → by India's nagrik (citizens) — wherever they are.

So GNP = GDP + what our nagriks earn abroad − what foreigners earn here (NFIA).

🧠 Mnemonic 3: "Net means Nibbled"
Every "Net" figure has been nibbled by depreciation.

NDP = GDP − Depreciation; NNP = GNP − Depreciation.

Visual: A mouse nibbling a machine — the nibbled part is depreciation.

🧠 Mnemonic 4: RBI Dates
Number chain: "35 – 49 – 69 – 80"

35 → RBI set up (1 April 1935)

49 → RBI nationalised (1 January 1949)

69 → 14 banks nationalised

80 → 6 more banks nationalised

Say it like a cricket score: "35 for 49, 69 all out at 80!"

🧠 Mnemonic 5: The ₹1 Note Exception
Rhyme: "One rupee, one exception, Finance Secretary's signature."

All other notes → RBI Governor.

₹1 note and coins → Government of India.

🧠 Mnemonic 6: CRR vs SLR
Rule: "C for Central, S for Self"

CRR → cash parked with the Central bank (RBI).

SLR → liquid assets kept by the bank itSelf (cash, gold, government securities).

🧠 Mnemonic 7: Repo Rate and Inflation
Arrow song: "Repo UP, Prices DOWN; Repo DOWN, Prices UP."

Visual: A see-saw — repo on one side, prices on the other.

🧠 Mnemonic 8: MPC
"Six seats, four target, two-six fence"

6 members; 4% CPI target; band 2% to 6%.

Governor holds the casting vote — "the captain breaks the tie".

🧠 Mnemonic 9: Five-Year Plans
"1 Krishi, 2 Karkhana, 3 Kharab, Holiday, 5 Garibi Hatao"

1st (1951–56) → agriculture (Harrod-Domar)

2nd (1956–61) → heavy industry (Mahalanobis)

3rd (1961–66) → failed (wars, drought)

1966–69 → Plan Holiday

5th (1974–79) → Garibi Hatao

Last plan → 12th (2012–17)

🧠 Mnemonic 10: Planning Commission to NITI
"Fifty to Fifteen" — 1950 Planning Commission → 2015 NITI Aayog.

Both chaired by the Prime Minister. The Planning Commission was born from a Cabinet resolution — "Resolution, not Constitution".

🧠 Mnemonic 11: Important Articles
Phone number trick: "112 – 280 – 279A"

112 → Budget (Annual Financial Statement)

280 → Finance Commission

279A → GST Council

🧠 Mnemonic 12: Deficits
"Fiscal minus Interest = Primary" → "FIP": Fiscal − Interest = Primary.

Visual: A fish (Fiscal) eats an insect (Interest) and becomes a Prince (Primary).

🧠 Mnemonic 13: LPG of 1991
"1991 cooking gas: LPG" — Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation.

PM P.V. Narasimha Rao, FM Manmohan Singh — "Rao ran, Singh signed the budget."

🧠 Mnemonic 14: Headquarters
"Washington's Twins, Geneva's Judge"

IMF and World Bank → twins born at Bretton Woods (1944), both in Washington, D.C.

WTO → the trade judge in Geneva (1995).

🧠 Mnemonic 15: Types of Unemployment
"Disguised = Dhoka (hidden), Seasonal = Season, Structural = Skill mismatch, Frictional = Friction while switching jobs"

⏭️ Skip Strategy

Tip 1: If a numerical question on GDP/GNP/NNP gives many figures and you cannot recall which formula applies within 15 seconds, mark it and move on; come back after the fact-based questions.

Tip 2: If a statement-based question has three statements and you are sure about only one, eliminate options using that one statement. If two options still remain and nothing else helps, skip — guessing costs marks in negative-marking exams.

Tip 3: Never skip date-pair questions (RBI 1935/1949, NITI 2015, GST 2017, WTO 1995). They are 15-second score maximizers — answer them first.
