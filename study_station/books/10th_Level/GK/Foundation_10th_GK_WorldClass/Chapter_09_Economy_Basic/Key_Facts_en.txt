1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Three sectors	Primary (agriculture, fishing, forestry), Secondary (manufacturing, construction), Tertiary (services: banking, transport, trade).	Placing banking or transport in the secondary sector.	Almost True Statement
Largest sector vs largest employer	NCERT: tertiary is the largest producing sector in GDP; primary still employs the most workers.	"The tertiary sector employs the most people in India."	Reverse Causation
GDP vs GNP	GDP = production within the country's borders; GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad.	Defining GDP as "production by citizens of a country".	Similar Name
NDP / NNP	NDP = GDP − Depreciation; NNP = GNP − Depreciation.	Subtracting indirect taxes instead of depreciation.	Almost True Statement
National Income	NNP at factor cost (Market price − Indirect taxes + Subsidies).	Calling GDP at market price "National Income".	Similar Name
RBI set up	1 April 1935 under RBI Act, 1934; central office moved Kolkata → Mumbai in 1937.	Giving 1934 (the Act year) as the year of establishment.	Chronological Confusion
RBI nationalised	1 January 1949.	Offering 1969 (year of bank nationalisation).	Similar Name/Year
₹1 note	Issued by Government of India; signed by Finance Secretary. All other notes by RBI.	"All notes are signed by the RBI Governor."	Almost True Statement
Repo rate	Rate at which RBI lends short-term to banks; raised to fight inflation.	"Repo rate is cut to control inflation."	Reverse Causation
CRR vs SLR	CRR = cash kept with RBI; SLR = liquid assets kept by the bank itself.	Swapping where CRR and SLR are kept.	Similar Name
Monetary Policy Committee	Formed 2016; 6 members; Governor has casting vote; decides repo rate.	Saying MPC has 5 or 7 members, or is chaired by the Finance Minister.	Almost True Statement
Inflation target	CPI inflation 4% with band 2%–6%; retained in March 2026 for 2026–31.	Saying the target is based on WPI.	Similar Name
WPI vs CPI	WPI by Office of Economic Adviser (DPIIT), no services; CPI (Combined) by NSO (MoSPI).	Saying WPI is released by RBI or NSO.	Similar Name
Disinflation vs Deflation	Disinflation = inflation slowing but positive; deflation = prices falling.	Using deflation for "slower price rise".	Almost True Statement
Planning Commission	15 March 1950, by Cabinet resolution; PM is chairman.	Calling it a constitutional or statutory body.	Almost True Statement
NITI Aayog	1 January 2015, replaced Planning Commission; PM is chairperson.	Giving 2014 (announcement year) as the year of formation.	Chronological Confusion
Five-Year Plans	1st: agriculture (Harrod-Domar); 2nd: heavy industry (Mahalanobis); 12th (2012–17) last.	Linking the Mahalanobis model to the 1st Plan.	Chronological Confusion
Budget	Annual Financial Statement, Article 112; presented on 1 February since 2017.	Writing Article 110 (Money Bill) for the Budget.	Similar Name
Deficits	Primary deficit = Fiscal deficit − Interest payments.	Subtracting interest from revenue deficit.	Almost True Statement
GST	101st Amendment Act, 2016; from 1 July 2017; GST Council Art. 279A, chaired by Union Finance Minister.	Giving 122nd (the Bill number) as the Amendment number.	Similar Name
Finance Commission	Article 280; every 5 years; constituted by the President.	Confusing with NITI Aayog or Planning Commission.	Similar Name
1991 reforms	LPG; New Industrial Policy 24 July 1991; PM P.V. Narasimha Rao, FM Manmohan Singh.	Naming Manmohan Singh as Prime Minister in 1991.	Chronological Confusion
FEMA	FEMA 1999 replaced FERA 1973.	Reversing the order (FERA replaced FEMA).	Chronological Confusion
Disguised unemployment	More workers than needed; marginal productivity zero; common in agriculture.	Calling it seasonal unemployment.	Similar Name
WTO	1 January 1995; replaced GATT; HQ Geneva.	Placing WTO HQ in Washington (that is IMF/World Bank).	Similar Name
HDI	Released by UNDP; first Human Development Report 1990 (Mahbub ul Haq).	Saying HDI is released by the World Bank.	Similar Name
SEBI	Set up 1988; statutory powers in 1992.	Giving 1992 as the year SEBI was first set up.	Chronological Confusion
2. Time Management (15/45-Sec Rule)
Fact / Topic	Type (15s Fact / 45s Analytical)	Score Maximizer?	Reason
Dates: RBI 1935/1949, NITI 2015, GST 2017, WTO 1995	15s (Fact)	Yes	Direct one-liners; asked again and again.
Who issues/signs the ₹1 note	15s (Fact)	Yes	Single exception; easy marks once learnt.
GDP, GNP, NDP, NNP formula questions	45s (Analytical)	Yes	Needs a small calculation; follow formula step by step.
Fiscal, revenue and primary deficit calculation	45s (Analytical)	Yes	Two-step subtraction; units must match.
Effect of repo/CRR/OMO change on money supply	45s (Analytical)	Yes	Cause-effect chain; reverse causation trap.
Sector classification of a given job	15s (Fact)	Yes	Ask: nature, factory or service?
Five-Year Plan and its model/focus	15s (Fact)	Yes	1 krishi, 2 karkhana.
Types of unemployment from a situation	45s (Analytical)	Yes	Read the situation carefully: seasonal vs disguised.
Inflation types (disinflation, stagflation)	45s (Analytical)	Moderate	Definitions look alike; read the key word.
HQ of international bodies	15s (Fact)	Yes	Fixed pairs; quick recall.
Statement-based questions on RBI/MPC	45s (Analytical)	Moderate	Each statement must be checked; watch "all/only".
3. Data & Formulas
Data / Formula	Value / Detail
GNP	GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)
NDP	GDP − Depreciation
NNP	GNP − Depreciation
Factor Cost	Market Price − Indirect Taxes + Subsidies
National Income	NNP at Factor Cost
Per Capita Income	National Income ÷ Population
GDP Deflator	(Nominal GDP ÷ Real GDP) × 100
GDP (expenditure method)	C + I + G + (X − M)
Fiscal Deficit	Total Expenditure − (Revenue Receipts + Non-debt Capital Receipts)
Revenue Deficit	Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts
Primary Deficit	Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments
M1 (Narrow money)	Currency with public + Demand deposits + Other deposits with RBI
M3 (Broad money)	M1 + Time deposits with banks
MPC	6 members; CPI target 4% (2%–6%)
Poverty line calorie norm (NCERT)	2400 rural, 2100 urban (per person per day)
MGNREGA	100 days of wage employment per rural household per year (Act of 2005)
Financial year	1 April – 31 March
Sensex / Nifty	30 companies / 50 companies
