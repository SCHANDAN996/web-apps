Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Agriculture, fishing and forestry belong to which sector of the economy?
(a) Primary (b) Secondary (c) Tertiary (d) Quaternary
Answer: (a)
Solution: Activities that use natural resources directly form the primary sector. Secondary means manufacturing, and tertiary means services.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Economics

2. Making sugar from sugarcane is an activity of which sector?
(a) Secondary (b) Primary (c) Tertiary (d) Quaternary
Answer: (a)
Solution: Turning a raw material (sugarcane) into a new product (sugar) is manufacturing, which is the secondary sector. Growing the sugarcane would be primary.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Economics

3. In which year was the Reserve Bank of India established?
(a) 1935 (b) 1947 (c) 1949 (d) 1969
Answer: (a)
Solution: Trap: 1949 is the year RBI was nationalised, not set up. RBI began working on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934, so the answer is 1935.
Source: RBI official website

4. Where is the central office of the Reserve Bank of India located?
(a) Mumbai (b) Kolkata (c) New Delhi (d) Chennai
Answer: (a)
Solution: RBI's central office was first in Kolkata but moved permanently to Mumbai in 1937. Kolkata is the tempting wrong option.
Source: RBI official website

5. Who issues the ₹1 currency note in India?
(a) Government of India (Ministry of Finance) (b) Reserve Bank of India (c) State Bank of India (d) SEBI
Answer: (a)
Solution: The ₹1 note is issued by the Government of India and carries the Finance Secretary's signature. All other notes are issued by the RBI.
Source: PYQ-style

6. What is the full form of GDP?
(a) Gross Domestic Product (b) Gross Development Product (c) General Domestic Production (d) Gross Domestic Price
Answer: (a)
Solution: GDP stands for Gross Domestic Product — the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country in a year.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Economics

7. NITI Aayog came into existence on:
(a) 15 March 1950 (b) 1 January 2015 (c) 1 April 2015 (d) 26 January 2014
Answer: (b)
Solution: NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission on 1 January 2015. 15 March 1950 is the date the Planning Commission was set up.
Source: PIB (Government of India)

8. Who is the Chairperson of NITI Aayog?
(a) President of India (b) Prime Minister (c) Union Finance Minister (d) Governor of RBI
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Prime Minister is the Chairperson of NITI Aayog, just as the PM was Chairman of the old Planning Commission.
Source: PIB (Government of India)

9. From which date was GST implemented in India?
(a) 1 April 2017 (b) 1 July 2017 (c) 1 July 2016 (d) 1 January 2018
Answer: (b)
Solution: GST was rolled out from 1 July 2017. The trap is 1 April 2017, which is only the start of a financial year.
Source: PIB (Government of India)

10. Which of the following is an indirect tax?
(a) Income tax (b) Goods and Services Tax (c) Corporation tax (d) Capital gains tax
Answer: (b)
Solution: GST is an indirect tax because the seller collects it and its burden passes to the buyer. Income, corporation and capital gains taxes are direct taxes.
Source: PYQ-style

11. The financial year in India runs from:
(a) 1 January to 31 December (b) 1 April to 31 March (c) 1 July to 30 June (d) 1 October to 30 September
Answer: (b)
Solution: India's financial (fiscal) year runs from 1 April to 31 March, and the Union Budget is prepared for this period.
Source: PYQ-style

12. Where is the headquarters of the World Trade Organization (WTO)?
(a) Washington, D.C. (b) Geneva (c) New York (d) Vienna
Answer: (b)
Solution: The WTO is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland. Washington, D.C. is the trap — it is the home of the IMF and the World Bank.
Source: PYQ-style

13. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published by:
(a) World Bank (b) United Nations Development Programme (c) International Monetary Fund (d) World Trade Organization
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNDP publishes the HDI in its Human Development Report. The World Bank is a common wrong choice.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Economics

14. In the 1991 economic reforms, LPG stands for:
(a) Liberalisation, Planning, Globalisation (b) Licensing, Planning, Growth (c) Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation (d) Land, Production, Growth
Answer: (c)
Solution: The New Economic Policy of 1991 is known as LPG — Liberalisation, Privatisation and Globalisation.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Economics

15. Operation Flood is associated with:
(a) Fish production (b) Oilseed production (c) Milk production (d) Wheat production
Answer: (c)
Solution: Operation Flood, led by Dr. Verghese Kurien, increased milk production and is linked with the White Revolution.
Source: PYQ-style

16. Who is known as the Father of the Green Revolution in India?
(a) Verghese Kurien (b) Norman Borlaug (c) M.S. Swaminathan (d) P.C. Mahalanobis
Answer: (c)
Solution: M.S. Swaminathan led the Green Revolution in India. Norman Borlaug is the father of the Green Revolution worldwide — the trap option.
Source: PYQ-style

17. Under the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR), banks keep a part of their deposits as cash with:
(a) Themselves (b) State Bank of India (c) Reserve Bank of India (d) Ministry of Finance
Answer: (c)
Solution: CRR is kept as cash with the RBI. Keeping liquid assets with the bank itself is SLR — the classic swap trap.
Source: PYQ-style

18. A person working in a call centre is employed in which sector?
(a) Primary (b) Secondary (c) Tertiary (d) Agriculture and allied
Answer: (c)
Solution: A call centre provides a service, not a good, so it belongs to the tertiary (service) sector.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Economics

19. In which year was the Planning Commission of India set up?
(a) 1947 (b) 1951 (c) 1950 (d) 1952
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1951 is the start of the First Plan and 1952 is when the NDC was formed. The Planning Commission was set up by a Cabinet resolution on 15 March 1950.
Source: PIB (Government of India)

20. MGNREGA guarantees wage employment to a rural household for how many days in a year?
(a) 50 (b) 200 (c) 150 (d) 100
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 guarantees at least 100 days.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Economics

21. Consider the following statements:
I. GDP counts the value of final goods and services produced within the geographical boundary of a country.
II. GNP is equal to GDP plus net factor income from abroad.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I only (b) II only (c) Neither I nor II (d) Both I and II
Answer: (d)
Solution: GDP is about production inside the border, whoever produces it. GNP adds what citizens earn abroad minus what foreigners earn here (NFIA). Both statements are correct.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Macroeconomics

22. In which year was the Reserve Bank of India nationalised?
(a) 1935 (b) 1980 (c) 1969 (d) 1949
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 1969 and 1980 are years when commercial banks were nationalised, and 1935 is when RBI was set up. RBI itself was nationalised on 1 January 1949.
Source: RBI official website

23. The repo rate is the rate at which:
(a) commercial banks lend to the public (b) the government borrows from the public (c) the RBI borrows money from commercial banks (d) the RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks
Answer: (d)
Solution: Repo rate is the rate at which RBI lends short-term money to banks against government securities. Option (c) describes the reverse repo rate — a common swap trap.
Source: RBI official website

24. Net National Product (NNP) at factor cost is also known as:
(a) Gross Domestic Product (b) Disposable Income (c) Personal Income (d) National Income
Answer: (d)
Solution: NNP at factor cost is called National Income. Calling GDP at market price "national income" is an almost-true trap.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Macroeconomics

25. Which Five-Year Plan was based on the P.C. Mahalanobis model?
(a) First Plan (b) Fifth Plan (c) Third Plan (d) Second Plan
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Second Plan (1956–61) focused on heavy industry using the Mahalanobis model. The First Plan was based on the Harrod-Domar model and focused on agriculture.
Source: PYQ-style
