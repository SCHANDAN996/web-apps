26. Which of the following is NOT a function of the Reserve Bank of India?
(a) Opening savings accounts for the general public like a commercial bank (b) Acting as banker to the government (c) Issuing currency notes (d) Keeping the country's foreign exchange reserves
Answer: (a)
Solution: RBI is the bank of banks and of the government; it does not open savings accounts for ordinary people like a commercial bank. The other three are core RBI functions.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Economics

27. Under the Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR), banks keep a part of their deposits in the form of:
(a) Liquid assets such as cash, gold and approved government securities kept with themselves (b) Cash deposited with the RBI only (c) Loans given to farmers (d) Deposits held in foreign banks
Answer: (a)
Solution: SLR assets stay with the bank itself in liquid form. Option (a) describes CRR — the usual swap trap.
Source: RBI official website

28. When the RBI sells government securities in the open market, the money supply in the economy:
(a) Decreases (b) Increases (c) Remains unchanged (d) First doubles and then falls
Answer: (a)
Solution: Buyers pay money to the RBI for the securities, so money is pulled out of the market and money supply decreases. Buying securities would do the opposite.
Source: RBI official website

29. How many members are there in the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of India?
(a) 6 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 7
Answer: (a)
Solution: The MPC has three members from the RBI, including the Governor, and three appointed by the Central Government. The Governor holds the casting vote. Total members: 6.
Source: RBI official website

30. The inflation target notified for the RBI is based on which index?
(a) Consumer Price Index (Combined) (b) Wholesale Price Index (c) GDP deflator (d) Index of Industrial Production
Answer: (a)
Solution: Under flexible inflation targeting, the target is set in terms of CPI (Combined) inflation. WPI is the tempting wrong option.
Source: RBI official website

31. Disinflation means:
(a) A slowdown in the rate of inflation while prices still rise (b) A fall in the general price level (c) Inflation along with high unemployment (d) Very high and uncontrolled inflation
Answer: (a)
Solution: In disinflation prices still rise, only more slowly. A fall in the price level is deflation — an almost-true trap.
Source: PYQ-style

32. A situation of high inflation, high unemployment and slow economic growth together is called:
(a) Deflation (b) Stagflation (c) Disinflation (d) Reflation
Answer: (b)
Solution: Stagflation combines stagnation (slow growth, unemployment) with inflation.
Source: PYQ-style

33. The Wholesale Price Index (WPI) in India is released by:
(a) Reserve Bank of India (b) Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT (c) National Statistical Office (d) NITI Aayog
Answer: (b)
Solution: WPI is released by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry. NSO releases CPI, which is the similar-name trap.
Source: PYQ-style

34. The National Statistical Office (NSO) works under which ministry?
(a) Ministry of Finance (b) Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (c) Ministry of Commerce and Industry (d) Ministry of Home Affairs
Answer: (b)
Solution: NSO is part of the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI). It estimates national income and releases CPI.
Source: PYQ-style

35. The National Statistical Office (NSO) was formed in 2019 by merging:
(a) RBI and SBI (b) CSO and NSSO (c) Planning Commission and NDC (d) CACP and FCI
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Central Statistics Office (CSO) and the National Sample Survey Office (NSSO) were merged to form the NSO.
Source: PYQ-style

36. Primary deficit is equal to:
(a) Revenue deficit minus interest payments (b) Fiscal deficit minus interest payments (c) Fiscal deficit plus interest payments (d) Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts
Answer: (b)
Solution: Primary deficit = Fiscal deficit − Interest payments. Option (d) is the formula for revenue deficit.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Macroeconomics

37. Under which Article of the Constitution is the Union Budget presented as the "Annual Financial Statement"?
(a) Article 110 (b) Article 112 (c) Article 266 (d) Article 280
Answer: (b)
Solution: Article 110 defines a Money Bill and Article 280 deals with the Finance Commission. The Annual Financial Statement is under Article 112.
Source: Constitution of India

38. The Finance Commission is constituted under which Article?
(a) Article 112 (b) Article 280 (c) Article 270 (d) Article 324
Answer: (b)
Solution: Article 324 is about the Election Commission. The Finance Commission is set up by the President every five years under Article 280.
Source: Constitution of India

39. The GST Council has been constituted under which Article of the Constitution?
(a) Article 246A (b) Article 269A (c) Article 279A (d) Article 280
Answer: (c)
Solution: Article 246A gives the power to levy GST and Article 269A deals with inter-State GST, which makes them tempting. The GST Council is under Article 279A.
Source: Constitution of India

40. Who chairs the GST Council?
(a) Prime Minister (b) Governor of RBI (c) Union Finance Minister (d) President of India
Answer: (c)
Solution: The GST Council is chaired by the Union Finance Minister, and the Finance Ministers of the States are its members.
Source: PIB (Government of India)

41. GST was introduced through which Constitutional Amendment Act?
(a) 42nd (b) 73rd (c) 101st (d) 122nd
Answer: (c)
Solution: 122nd was the number of the Bill, which is the trap. It became the Constitution 101st Amendment Act after passage, so the answer is 101st.
Source: PIB (Government of India)

42. Which law replaced the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (FERA)?
(a) SEBI Act, 1992 (b) Competition Act, 2002 (c) FEMA, 1999 (d) FRBM Act, 2003
Answer: (c)
Solution: FERA (1973) controlled foreign exchange strictly; after the reforms it was replaced by the Foreign Exchange Management Act, FEMA, 1999.
Source: PYQ-style

43. The MRTP Act, 1969 was replaced by:
(a) FEMA (b) Companies Act (c) Competition Act, 2002 (d) Consumer Protection Act
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Monopolies and Restrictive Trade Practices (MRTP) Act was replaced by the Competition Act, which set up the Competition Commission of India.
Source: PYQ-style

44. In which year was the New Industrial Policy announced, starting the LPG reforms?
(a) 1956 (b) 1980 (c) 1991 (d) 2001
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1956 was the year of the Industrial Policy Resolution of the planning era. The New Industrial Policy was announced on 24 July 1991.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Who was the Prime Minister of India when the 1991 economic reforms were launched?
(a) Rajiv Gandhi (b) Atal Bihari Vajpayee (c) Manmohan Singh (d) P.V. Narasimha Rao
Answer: (d)
Solution: P.V. Narasimha Rao was Prime Minister. Dr. Manmohan Singh was the Finance Minister then — a chronological-confusion trap.
Source: PYQ-style

46. A farm labourer in a dry area gets no work for four to five months after the harvest. This is an example of:
(a) Disguised unemployment (b) Structural unemployment (c) Frictional unemployment (d) Seasonal unemployment
Answer: (d)
Solution: Lack of work during certain months of the year is seasonal unemployment. Disguised unemployment means more people working than needed.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Economics

47. According to NCERT, what was the calorie requirement used for the rural poverty line in India?
(a) 2100 calories (b) 1800 calories (c) 2700 calories (d) 2400 calories
Answer: (d)
Solution: The urban norm was 2100 calories per person per day; the rural norm is higher at 2400.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Economics

48. The International Monetary Fund and the World Bank were created at:
(a) Marrakesh Conference, 1994 (b) Paris Peace Conference, 1919 (c) Geneva Conference, 1947 (d) Bretton Woods Conference, 1944
Answer: (d)
Solution: Both institutions came out of the Bretton Woods Conference held in the USA in 1944.
Source: NCERT Class 10 History

49. Consider the following statements about the Planning Commission:
I. It was a constitutional body.
II. Its Chairman was the Prime Minister.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I only (b) Neither I nor II (c) Both I and II (d) II only
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Planning Commission was set up by a Cabinet resolution in 1950, so it was neither constitutional nor statutory. The Prime Minister was its Chairman.
Source: PYQ-style

50. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) WTO – Geneva (b) IMF – Washington, D.C. (c) Asian Development Bank – Manila (d) New Development Bank – Beijing
Answer: (d)
Solution: The New Development Bank (set up by BRICS) is headquartered in Shanghai. Beijing is the headquarters of the AIIB — the similar-name trap.
Source: PYQ-style
