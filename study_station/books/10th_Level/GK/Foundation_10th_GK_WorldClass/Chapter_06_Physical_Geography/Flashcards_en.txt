Card 1
Front: I am the belt where rivers descending from the Himalayas suddenly play hide-and-seek underground because of huge boulders. Who am I?
Back: Bhabar. The porous pebble bed makes water disappear; rivers re-emerge later in Terai.

Card 2
Front: In which physiographic division would you find the famous "Duns" like Dehradun, and why are they like natural stadiums between mountains?
Back: Shivaliks (Outer Himalayas). Duns are intermontane valleys formed between the Shivalik ranges and the Lesser Himalayas.

Card 3
Front: Which mnemonic helps you recall the six physiographic divisions from north to south: "Hina's Pretty Parrot Dances Cheerfully Inside"?
Back: Himalayas, Northern Plains, Peninsular Plateau, Desert, Coastal Plains, Islands. Each capital letter stands for one division, in order from north to south.

Card 4
Front: My name means "black cotton soil," and I hold moisture so well that farmers love me for cotton. Which soil am I, and which volcanic rock gave birth to me?
Back: Regur (black soil), formed from the weathering of Deccan Trap basalt.

Card 5
Front: I am the place with the highest average annual rainfall in India, yet I'm not Cherrapunji. Who am I?
Back: Mawsynram, Meghalaya. It receives more average annual rainfall than nearby Cherrapunji. (Examiner trap: outdated Cherrapunji record.)

Card 6
Front: What is the clever trick to remember the sequence of alluvial landforms — Bhabar → Terai → Bhangar → Khadar — using the sentence "Babbar Took Bhang Khadi"?
Back: Each word's first letter corresponds: Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar, Khadar. It's the order from north to south in the Northern Plains.

Card 7
Front: I am the only major river in the Thar Desert that never meets the sea but ends in the Rann of Kutch. What's my name?
Back: Luni River. It's an inland drainage river.

Card 8
Front: Which channel separates Minicoy Island from the rest of Lakshadweep, and why do examiners love asking about it alongside the 10° Channel?
Back: The 9° Channel separates Minicoy from the main Lakshadweep group; the 10° Channel separates Andaman from Nicobar. (Memory: 9 for Lakshadweep–Minicoy, 10 for Andaman–Nicobar.)

Card 9
Front: Name the mnemonic that helps you list the four major west-flowing peninsular rivers: Narmada, Tapi, Mahi, Sabarmati.
Back: "NaTa MaSa" — Na (Narmada), Ta (Tapi), Ma (Mahi), Sa (Sabarmati). All four flow west into the Arabian Sea.

Card 10
Front: If a question says "Tapi river forms a large delta in Gujarat," why should your trap-detector beep loudly?
Back: West-flowing peninsular rivers like Tapi form estuaries, not deltas. Only east-flowing peninsular rivers (Godavari, Krishna, Mahanadi, Kaveri) form deltas. (Examiner trap: reversed fact.)

Card 11
Front: I am India's only active volcano, and I sit quietly in the Andaman Sea. Who am I?
Back: Barren Island. It's part of the Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

Card 12
Front: Which mountain range is the oldest in India, and its highest peak Gurushikhar stands at 1722 m?
Back: The Aravalli Range. It is one of the oldest fold mountain systems in the world.

Card 13
Front: You read "The Terai belt is characterized by highly porous soil where rivers disappear." Is this statement correct? If not, what's the flaw?
Back: Incorrect. Rivers disappear in Bhabar, not Terai. In Terai, they re-emerge because the fine, less permeable soil forces water to the surface, creating swamps. (Examiner trap: almost-correct statement with a swapped term.)

Card 14
Front: Which river flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges and ends in an estuary, not a delta?
Back: Narmada. It's the largest west-flowing peninsular river and forms an estuary at the Gulf of Khambhat.

Card 15
Front: I am the highest peak of the Western Ghats, often called the "Everest of South India." Who am I and in which state do I reside?
Back: Anamudi (2695 m), located in Kerala. It is also the highest peak in peninsular India.

Card 16
Front: What is the correct west-to-east order of these Himalayan passes: Jelep La, Khardung La, Bum La, Shipki La, Nathu La?
Back: Khardung La (Ladakh) → Shipki La (Himachal Pradesh) → Nathu La (Sikkim) → Jelep La (Sikkim) → Bum La (Arunachal Pradesh). Memory trick: "Khardung's Ship Nears Jelep's Bum."

Card 17
Front: Which rock system contains the bulk of India's coal reserves, and in which three river valleys are they mainly found?
Back: Gondwana rocks. The main coal-bearing valleys are Damodar, Mahanadi, and Godavari.

Card 18
Front: I am the largest coastal lagoon in India, famous for migratory birds. On which coast am I located, and what's my name?
Back: Chilika Lake, on the eastern coastal plain (Odisha). It is a brackish water lagoon.
