Front: I am the belt where rivers descending from the Himalayas suddenly play hide-and-seek underground because of huge boulders. Who am I?
Back: Bhabar. The porous pebble bed makes water disappear; rivers re-emerge later in Terai.

Front: In which physiographic division would you find the famous "Duns" like Dehradun, and why are they like natural stadiums between mountains?
Back: Shivaliks (Outer Himalayas). Duns are intermontane valleys formed by the folding of the Shivalik ranges.

Front: Which mnemonic helps you recall the six physiographic divisions from north to south: "Hina's Pet Parrot Dances Coolly In Moonlight"?
Back: Himalayas, Northern Plains, Peninsular, Desert, Coastal, Islands. Each capital letter stands for a division.

Front: My name means "black cotton soil," and I hold moisture so well that farmers love me for cotton. Which soil am I, and which volcanic rock gave birth to me?
Back: Regur (black soil), born from the Deccan Trap basalt.

Front: I am the world's wettest place in terms of average annual rainfall (~1187 cm), yet I'm not Cherrapunji. Who am I?
Back: Mawsynram, Meghalaya. It surpassed Cherrapunji in recent decades. (Examiner trap: outdated Cherrapunji record.)

Front: What is the clever trick to remember the sequence of alluvial landforms — Bhabar → Terai → Bhangar → Khadar — using the sentence "Babbar Took Bhang Khadi"?
Back: Each word's first letter corresponds: Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar, Khadar. It's the order from north to south in the Northern Plains.

Front: I am the only river in the Thar Desert that never meets the sea but ends in the Rann of Kutch. What's my name?
Back: Luni River. It's an inland drainage river.

Front: Which channel separates Minicoy Island from the rest of Lakshadweep, and why do examiners love asking about it alongside the 10° Channel?
Back: 9° Channel separates Minicoy from main Lakshadweep; 10° Channel separates Andaman from Nicobar. (Memory: 9 for L9kshadweep-Minicoy, 10 for A10dan & Nic10bar.)

Front: Name the mnemonic that helps you list the four major west-flowing peninsular rivers in order: Narmada, Tapi, Mahi, Sabarmati.
Back: "Nana TaMa SoS" — Na(Narmada), Ta(Tapi), Ma(Mahi), Sa(Sabarmati). SoS reminds you it's west-flowing rivers.

Front: If a question says "Tapi river forms a large delta in Gujarat," why should your trap-detector beep loudly?
Back: West-flowing peninsular rivers like Tapi form estuaries, not deltas. Only east-flowing peninsular rivers (Godavari, Krishna) form deltas. (Examiner Trap: reverse causation.)

Front: I am India's only active volcano, and I sit quietly in the Andaman Sea. Who am I?
Back: Barren Island. It's part of Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

Front: Which mountain range is the oldest in India, and its highest peak Gurushikhar stands at 1722 m?
Back: The Aravalli Range. It's considered one of the oldest fold mountain systems in the world.

Front: You read "The Terai belt is characterized by highly porous soil where rivers disappear." Is this statement correct? If not, what's the flaw?
Back: Incorrect. Rivers disappear in Bhabar, not Terai. In Terai, they re-emerge due to fine impervious soil creating swamps. (Examiner trap: almost correct statement with a swapped term.)

Front: Which river flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges and ends in an estuary, not a delta?
Back: Narmada. It's the largest west-flowing peninsular river and forms an estuary at the Gulf of Khambhat.

Front: I am the highest peak of the Western Ghats, often called the "Everest of South India." Who am I and in which state do I reside?
Back: Anaimudi (2695 m), located in Kerala's Anaimalai Hills. I'm also the highest point in peninsular India.

Front: What mnemonic would help you remember the Himalayan passes from west to east: Jelep La, Khardung La, Bum La, Shipki La, Nathu La?
Back: "JKB Ship Ki Nautanki Boli" — J(Khardung), K(?), actually a bit off. Better: "Jelep La, Khardung La, Bum La, Shipki La, Nathu La" can be recalled as "JKB Ship Ki Nautanki Boli" where J=Jelep, K=Khardung, B=Bum, Ship= Shipki, Nautanki= Nathu La. Use any memorable phrase.

Front: Which rock system contains 98% of India's coal reserves, and in which three river valleys are they mainly found?
Back: Gondwana rocks. The main coal-bearing valleys are Damodar, Mahanadi, and Godavari.

Front: I am the largest saltwater lagoon in India, famous for migratory birds. Which coastal plain am I part of, and what's my name?
Back: Chilika Lake, located on the eastern coastal plain (Odisha). It's a brackish water lagoon, not a lake.
