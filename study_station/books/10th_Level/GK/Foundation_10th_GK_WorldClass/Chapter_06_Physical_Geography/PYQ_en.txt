# Past Year Question (PYQ) Pattern Analysis: Physical Geography

## 1. Exam Weightage & Topic Trends
Physical Geography of India and the World is a fundamental topic across SSC, Railway, and State Government examinations:
- **Physiographic Divisions of India:** High frequency of questions on the Northern Mountains (Himalayan ranges: Himadri, Himachal, Shiwalik, and major mountain passes), Northern Plains (Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar, Khadar), and Peninsular Plateau (Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats, Deccan Traps).
- **Drainage System:** Classification of Himalayan vs Peninsular rivers, west-flowing rivers forming estuaries (Narmada, Tapi) vs east-flowing deltaic rivers (Ganga, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri), and major tributaries.
- **Climate & Rainfall:** The Indian Monsoon mechanism (South-West Monsoon Arabian Sea vs Bay of Bengal branches, Retreating North-East monsoon in Tamil Nadu), highest rainfall regions (Mawsynram in Khasi Hills), and Western Disturbances.
- **Islands & Channels:** Latitudinal channels (10° Channel between Andaman and Nicobar, 9° Channel between Minicoy and Lakshadweep, 8° Channel between Minicoy and Maldives), Duncan Passage, and Palk Strait.
- **Mountain Peaks & Geomorphology:** Highest peaks (Kanchenjunga, Anamudi in Western Ghats, Doddabetta in Nilgiris), oldest fold mountains (Aravalli range), and volcanic islands (Barren Island).

## 2. Common Question Archetypes & Exam Traps
1. **River Tributaries & Estuary Traps:** Confusing east-flowing with west-flowing rivers. Remembering that Narmada and Tapi flow through rift valleys into the Arabian Sea forming estuaries, not deltas.
2. **Channel Degree Numbers:** Direct factual confusion between 8°, 9°, and 10° Channels.
3. **Plains Soil Zones:** Confusing coarse boulder-filled Bhabar with marshy Terai, or old alluvium Bhangar (with Kankar) with fresh flood plain Khadar.
4. **Mountain Peak Heights & Ranges:** Anamudi (highest peak of South India in Anaimalai Hills) vs Doddabetta (Nilgiri Hills).

## 3. High-Yield Practice Questions (PYQ-Style)

1. Which physiographic region of the Northern Plains lies immediately south of the Shiwaliks and is characterized by the disappearance of streams under coarse boulders and pebbles?
(a) Khadar (b) Bhangar (c) Bhabar (d) Terai
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bhabar is a narrow belt of about 8 to 16 km width lying parallel to the Shiwaliks where rivers deposit boulders and streams sink underground due to porous alluvium.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Which of the following major Indian rivers flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges and forms an estuary rather than a delta?
(a) Godavari (b) Krishna (c) Narmada (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Narmada river flows westward through a linear rift valley between the Vindhyas and Satpuras and empties into the Arabian Sea forming an estuary at the Gulf of Khambhat.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Mawsynram, the place that receives the highest average annual rainfall in the world, is situated in which mountain range?
(a) Garo Hills (b) Khasi Hills (c) Jaintia Hills (d) Patkai Bum
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mawsynram is located in the East Khasi Hills district of Meghalaya, where funnel-shaped hills trap moisture-laden monsoon winds from the Bay of Bengal.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Which water body or channel separates the Andaman Islands from the Nicobar Islands in the Bay of Bengal?
(a) 8° Channel (b) 9° Channel (c) 10° Channel (d) Duncan Passage
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 10° Channel lies along the 10° North latitude line and separates Little Andaman in the north from Car Nicobar in the south.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Which of the following is considered the oldest fold mountain system in India?
(a) The Aravalli Range (b) The Satpura Range (c) The Western Ghats (d) The Shiwalik Range
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Aravalli Range in north-western India is one of the oldest fold mountain systems in the world, dating back to the Precambrian era and currently existing as residual mountains.
Source: PYQ-style

6. Which mountain peak is the highest point in South India and the Peninsular Plateau?
(a) Doddabetta (b) Anamudi (c) Guru Shikhar (d) Mahendragiri
Answer: (b)
Solution: Anamudi (height 2,695 meters), located in the Anaimalai Hills of Kerala within the Western Ghats, is the highest peak in South India and the entire Peninsular Plateau.
Source: PYQ-style

7. The Western Ghats and the Eastern Ghats meet each other at which of the following mountain ranges?
(a) Anaimalai Hills (b) Cardamom Hills (c) Nilgiri Hills (d) Palani Hills
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Nilgiri Hills (Blue Mountains) form the junction where the Eastern Ghats join the Western Ghats, with Doddabetta as their highest peak.
Source: PYQ-style

8. The Majuli river island, recognized as the world's largest inhabited river island, is formed by which river?
(a) Ganga (b) Brahmaputra (c) Indus (d) Godavari
Answer: (b)
Solution: Majuli is a large fluvial riverine island in the Brahmaputra River in Assam, formed by the changing course and braided channels of the river.
Source: PYQ-style

9. The Barren Island, the only confirmed active volcano in South Asia, is located in which Union Territory of India?
(a) Lakshadweep (b) Andaman and Nicobar Islands (c) Puducherry (d) Daman and Diu
Answer: (b)
Solution: Barren Island is situated in the Andaman Sea about 138 km northeast of Port Blair and represents the only active volcano in South Asia.
Source: PYQ-style

10. What is the newly formed, fertile alluvium of the flood plains in the Northern Plains of India called?
(a) Bhangar (b) Khadar (c) Bhabar (d) Reh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Khadar is the newer, younger alluvium deposited by flood waters on river floodplains every year, making it extremely fertile for intensive agriculture.
Source: PYQ-style
