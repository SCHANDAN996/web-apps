📋 खंड 2: मुख्य तथ्य एवं आँकड़े (Key Facts & Figures)

1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Rivers disappear in Bhabar, reappear in Terai	Bhabar: porous boulder belt; Terai: marshy resurgence zone.	Statement swaps Bhabar and Terai, e.g., "In Terai, rivers disappear due to porosity."	Almost Correct Statement (90% correct, one term reversed)
West-flowing peninsular rivers form estuaries, not deltas	Narmada, Tapi, Mahi, Sabarmati have estuaries. East-flowing Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery form deltas.	"Tapi river built a large delta in Gujarat" – incorrect.	Reverse Causation (expect delta due to sediment load)
Mawsynram has highest average annual rainfall (~1187 cm)	Overtook Cherrapunji in recent decades.	"Cherrapunji is the wettest place on Earth."	Chronological Confusion (outdated fact)
Narmada flows in a rift valley between Vindhya and Satpura	Forms estuary, not delta; one of the few westward-flowing rift valley rivers.	Confusing Narmada's mouth with delta or mixing with Tapi's location.	Similar Names/Concepts (Narmada-Tapi similarity)
Anaimudi (2695 m) is the highest peak of Western Ghats and South India	Located in Anaimalai Hills, Kerala.	Mistaking Doddabetta (Nilgiris) or Mahendragiri (Eastern Ghats) as the highest.	Similar Names/Concepts (multiple peaks)
Black soil (Regur) from Deccan Trap basalt	Ideal for cotton, moisture-retentive.	Attributing black soil to alluvial deposits.	Almost Correct Statement
10° Channel separates Andaman & Nicobar; 9° Channel separates Minicoy from main Lakshadweep; 8° Channel separates Minicoy from Maldives	"9° Channel separates Andaman and Nicobar."	Swapping the degree channels.	Similar Names/Numbers (8°, 9°, 10°)
Aravalli Range is the oldest fold mountain in India	Gurushikhar (1722 m) is the highest peak.	Calling Vindhyas or Satpuras the oldest.	Chronological Confusion
Peninsular rivers are seasonal, old, and have graded profiles; Himalayan rivers are perennial, young, and antecedent.	Himalayan: perennial, large deltas; Peninsular: rain-fed, some form deltas (east), some estuaries (west).	Calling all Peninsular rivers deltas-forming or perennial.	Overgeneralization
Barren Island is India's only active volcano	Located in Andaman Sea.	Naming Narcondam or others as active.	Similar Names
