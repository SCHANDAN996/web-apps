Consider the following statements:

The Greater Himalayas are composed mainly of recent alluvial deposits.

The Lesser Himalayas are famous for hill stations like Shimla and Nainital.

The Shivalik range is known for 'Duns' like Dehradun.
Which is/are correct? (b) 2 and 3. That's a trap: Almost Correct Statement (1 is completely wrong). I'll use that.

So answer (b) 2 and 3 only.

Trap type: Almost Correct Statement (1 is fabricated; students might confuse with plains).

Source: SSC CGL 2020 style.

(b) The alluvial land between two converging rivers
Solution: Doab is a Persian term meaning "two waters", referring to the tract lying between two confluent rivers, e.g., Ganga-Yamuna Doab.
Source: Railway Group D 2019

(c) Penneru
Solution: Penneru (Pennar) is an east-flowing river. Sabarmati, Mahi, and Tapi are west-flowing.
Source: SSC CHSL 2018 (Trap: Negative Phrasing "NOT")

(c) North Andaman
Solution: Saddle Peak (738 m) is located in North Andaman Island, the highest point in the Andaman and Nicobar group.
Source: UPSC 2015

(c) Cotton
Solution: Regur (black soil) is ideal for cotton due to its moisture-retentive property. It is often called 'black cotton soil'.
Source: SSC CGL 2017

(a) Water divide
Solution: A water divide is an elevated boundary separating neighbouring drainage basins, e.g., the Western Ghats form the main water divide of Peninsular rivers.
Source: IBPS SO 2020

(c) Lipulekh
Solution: Lipulekh pass connects Uttarakhand with Tibet. Nathu La is in Sikkim, Shipki La in Himachal, Bomdi La in Arunachal.
Source: UPSC 2016 (Trap: Similar Names – multiple passes)

(b) The Aravallis are parallel to the monsoon winds and do not cause uplift
Solution: The Aravallis lie parallel to the moisture-laden Arabian Sea branch of the southwest monsoon, so they do not force the air to rise and cool, resulting in a rain-shadow area.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

(b) 1 and 3 only
Solution: Narmada → Gulf of Khambhat (correct); Tapi → Gulf of Khambhat (not Kachchh); Mahi → Gulf of Khambhat (correct). So 1 and 3 are correct.
Source: UPSC 2019 (Trap: Almost Correct – swapping gulfs)

(b) Kerala
Solution: Vembanad Lake, the longest lake in India, is located in Kerala.
Source: SSC MTS 2020

(b) Continental-continental plate collision
Solution: The Himalayas were formed by the collision of the Indian Plate with the Eurasian Plate, a continental-continental convergence.
Source: Railway NTPC 2021

(b) An isolated residual hill in the desert
Solution: Inselbergs are isolated residual hills rising abruptly from a plain, commonly found in arid and semi-arid regions like the Thar Desert.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

(c) The retreating monsoon brings heavy rainfall to the Malabar coast.
Solution: Retreating monsoon (October–December) gives rainfall to the Coromandel coast (Tamil Nadu), not the Malabar coast (which receives rain from the southwest monsoon). So (c) is incorrect.
Source: UPSC 2020 (Trap: Almost Correct – Malabar vs Coromandel)

(b) Sharavati
Solution: Jog Falls, the highest plunge waterfall in India, is on the Sharavati River in Karnataka.
Source: SSC CHSL 2017

(a) Saffron
Solution: Karewas of Kashmir are known for the cultivation of saffron, almond, and other dry fruits.
Source: UPSC 2016

(b) Wide continental shelf and emergent coast
Solution: The East Coast has a wider continental shelf and is emergent (raised coast) with large deltas, while the West Coast is narrow and submerged.
Source: Banking PO 2019

(b) South Andaman and Little Andaman
Solution: Duncan Passage separates South Andaman from Little Andaman. The 10° Channel separates Andaman from Nicobar.
Source: SSC CGL 2020 (Trap: Similar Names – Duncan Passage vs 10° Channel)

(a) Brahmagiri Hills
Solution: The Kaveri river originates from Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri Hills of Karnataka.
Source: RRB NTPC 2020

(a) 1 and 2 only
Solution: Statement 1 is correct; statement 2 is correct; statement 3 is incorrect because Anaimudi is the highest peak of the Western Ghats, not Eastern Ghats. Eastern Ghats' highest is Mahendragiri.
Source: UPSC 2021 (Trap: Almost Correct – mixing peaks)

(b) Uttarakhand
Solution: The Valley of Flowers National Park is located in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand.
Source: SSC CGL 2016

(b) North-east trade winds picking up moisture from the Bay of Bengal
Solution: During the retreating monsoon, the northeast trade winds pick up moisture from the Bay of Bengal and cause rainfall on the Coromandel coast including Tamil Nadu.
Source: UPSC 2018

(b) Madhya Pradesh
Solution: The Baghelkhand plateau lies in the northeastern part of Madhya Pradesh, extending into parts of Uttar Pradesh.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

(a) Srinagar and Leh
Solution: Zoji La connects Kashmir Valley to Ladakh, i.e., Srinagar with Leh.
Source: Railway Group D 2021

(c) Organic matter and nitrogen
Solution: Laterite soils are heavily leached; they are rich in iron and aluminium oxides but poor in organic matter, nitrogen, phosphorus, and lime.
Source: IBPS SO 2020

(b) The plateau region with rolling plains
Solution: In Karnataka, the term 'Maidan' refers to the eastern plateau region with rolling plains, distinct from the coastal plain and the Western Ghats.
Source: SSC CGL 2022
