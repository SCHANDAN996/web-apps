4. Top 10 High-Yield Questions (with Solutions, Trap Type & 15/45-Second Rule)

Q1. UPSC 2020: Consider the following statements:

In the Bhabar region, rivers disappear due to high porosity.

The Terai region is characterized by marshy land and the re-emergence of underground streams.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 2 only
(c) Both 1 and 2
(d) Neither 1 nor 2

Solution: Statement 1 is correct (Bhabar's boulders make rivers disappear). Statement 2 is correct (Terai has marshy re-emergence). Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2.

Trap Type: Almost Correct Statement (Examiner might swap Bhabar and Terai in one statement to create a false pair, but here both are individually correct. However, if statement 1 said "Terai" instead of "Bhabar," it would be a trap.)

15/45-Second Rule: 15-sec fact-based recall for Bhabar and Terai features.

Q2. SSC CGL 2019: Which of the following rivers forms an estuary?
(a) Godavari
(b) Krishna
(c) Narmada
(d) Mahanadi

Solution: Narmada, a west-flowing peninsular river, forms an estuary. East-flowing rivers (Godavari, Krishna, Mahanadi) form deltas. Answer: (c) Narmada.

Trap Type: Reverse Causation (students often associate all peninsular rivers with deltas; Narmada is the exception.)

15/45-Second Rule: 15 sec – fact-based.

Q3. UPSC 2021: With reference to the Mawsynram, consider the following:

It is located in the Khasi Hills of Meghalaya.

It receives the highest average annual rainfall in the world.

Cherrapunji holds the second-highest rainfall record in the same district.
Which of the statements are correct?
(a) 1 and 2
(b) 2 and 3
(c) 1 and 3
(d) All of the above

Solution: Statement 1 is correct; statement 2 is correct (Mawsynram, not Cherrapunji); statement 3 is correct (Cherrapunji is second). Answer: (d) All of the above.

Trap Type: Chronological Confusion (outdated Cherrapunji record).

15/45-Second Rule: 15 sec.

Q4. Railway NTPC 2022: The 10° Channel separates which of the following?
(a) Minicoy and Maldives
(b) Andaman and Nicobar
(c) Lakshadweep and Maldives
(d) Minicoy and main Lakshadweep

Solution: 10° Channel separates Andaman and Nicobar. Answer: (b).

Trap Type: Similar Numbers (8°, 9°, 10° channels often interchanged).

15/45-Second Rule: 15 sec.

Q5. UPSC 2018: Which of the following is the oldest mountain range in India?
(a) Aravalli
(b) Satpura
(c) Vindhya
(d) Western Ghats

Solution: Aravalli is the oldest fold mountain range in India. Answer: (a).

Trap Type: Similar Names/Concepts (confusing with other old ranges).

15/45-Second Rule: 15 sec.

Q6. SSC CHSL 2020: Arrange the following alluvial landforms from north to south:

Khadar

Bhabar

Bhangar

Terai
(a) 2,4,3,1
(b) 2,4,1,3
(c) 4,2,3,1
(d) 2,3,4,1

Solution: North to south order: Bhabar → Terai → Bhangar → Khadar. So 2,4,3,1. Answer: (a).

Trap Type: Almost Correct (reversing Bhangar and Khadar, or Terai and Bhabar).

15/45-Second Rule: 15 sec with mnemonic.

Q7. UPSC 2019: Consider the following pairs:
River : Flows into

Narmada : Gulf of Khambhat

Tapi : Gulf of Kachchh

Mahi : Gulf of Khambhat
Which of the pairs is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 3
(c) 2 and 3
(d) 1, 2 and 3

Solution: Narmada → Gulf of Khambhat (correct). Tapi → Gulf of Khambhat (not Kachchh). Mahi → Gulf of Khambhat (correct). So 1 and 3 correct. Answer: (b).

Trap Type: Almost Correct (slight change in Gulf name; students often confuse the two gulfs in Gujarat.)

15/45-Second Rule: 45 sec analytical (need to recall exact gulfs for each river).

Q8. Banking PO 2021: The Deccan Trap is famous for which type of soil?
(a) Alluvial
(b) Red
(c) Laterite
(d) Black

Solution: Deccan Trap (basalt) weathers into black soil (regur). Answer: (d).

Trap Type: Overgeneralization (linking black soil to alluvial origin).

15/45-Second Rule: 15 sec.

Q9. Railway Group D 2020: Which of the following is NOT a west-flowing peninsular river?
(a) Sabarmati
(b) Mahi
(c) Tapi
(d) Penneru

Solution: Penneru (Pennar) is an east-flowing river. Others are west-flowing. Answer: (d).

Trap Type: Negative Phrasing (NOT), and mixing east/west flowing.

15/45-Second Rule: 15 sec if you know Penneru is east-flowing; 45 sec if you need to eliminate.

Q10. UPSC 2017: With reference to the Sundarbans delta, consider the following:

It is the largest delta in the world.

It is formed by the Ganga and Brahmaputra rivers.

It is a UNESCO World Heritage site.
Which of the statements are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

Solution: All three statements are correct. Answer: (d).

Trap Type: None significant, but sometimes examiners add false details about other deltas being largest to confuse.

15/45-Second Rule: 15 sec.

5. 15/45-Second Rule Applied

Q1 (Fact-based, 15 sec): Questions on basic facts like "10° Channel separates?" – if you recall mnemonic "L8 M9 A10," answer in 5 seconds. Mark as Score Maximizer.

Q7 (Analytical, 45 sec): Matching rivers with their mouths (Gulf of Khambhat vs Kachchh) requires recalling precise geography: Tapi empties into Gulf of Khambhat, not Kachchh. This is a 45-second question; if unsure, skip and come back after easy ones.

Skip advice: If a PYQ demands arranging four minor peaks of Western Ghats or matching obscure tributaries, and you are not 100% sure, mark for review and move on. First secure all 15-second fact-based MCQs before attempting analytical puzzles.
