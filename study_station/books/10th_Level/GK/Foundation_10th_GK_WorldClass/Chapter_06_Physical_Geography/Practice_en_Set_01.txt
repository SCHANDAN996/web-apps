Set 1/6 – Physical Geography of India MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which of the following is considered one of the oldest fold mountain ranges of India?
(a) Vindhya
(b) Satpura
(c) Aravali
(d) Western Ghats
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Aravali hills are among the oldest mountains of India; today they are highly eroded and found as broken hills from Gujarat to Delhi. The Himalayas, by contrast, are young fold mountains.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

2. What is the main feature of the 'Bhabar' belt of the Northern Plains?
(a) Rivers turn into marshes and swamps
(b) Streams disappear among the pebbles and boulders
(c) Fresh alluvium is deposited every year
(d) It is a fertile belt free of pebbles
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rivers coming down from the mountains deposit pebbles in the Bhabar belt, and all the streams disappear in it. The marshy belt where they re-emerge is the Terai — a common swap in exams.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

3. The 'Duns' (such as Dehra Dun) are longitudinal valleys lying between which two ranges?
(a) Himadri and Himachal
(b) Terai and Bhabar
(c) Shiwaliks and Terai
(d) Himachal (Lesser Himalaya) and Shiwaliks
Answer: (d)
Solution: The longitudinal valleys between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwaliks are called Duns, e.g. Dehra Dun, Kotli Dun and Patli Dun.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

4. In Maharashtra, the Western Ghats are locally known as:
(a) Nilgiri
(b) Anaimalai
(c) Cardamom Hills
(d) Sahyadri
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Western Ghats are called Sahyadri in Maharashtra. Nilgiri, Anaimalai and Cardamom are names of other parts of the Ghats further south.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment)

5. The Bhagirathi, the headwater of the Ganga, is fed by which glacier?
(a) Gangotri
(b) Yamunotri
(c) Siachen
(d) Zemu
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Bhagirathi is fed by the Gangotri Glacier and is joined by the Alaknanda at Devprayag. Yamunotri is the source of the Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

6. Which of the following rivers forms an estuary instead of a delta?
(a) Godavari
(b) Mahanadi
(c) Narmada
(d) Kaveri
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Narmada and the Tapi are the only long rivers that flow west and make estuaries. The Godavari, Mahanadi and Kaveri flow east and make deltas.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

7. The Luni, the only large river of the Indian desert, finally drains into:
(a) the Rann of Kachchh
(b) the Gulf of Khambhat
(c) the Sambhar Lake
(d) the Bay of Bengal
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Luni flows west and then south-west to join the Rann of Kachchh. It is an ephemeral river and does not reach the open sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment)

8. Which channel separates Minicoy from the main Lakshadweep group of islands?
(a) 8° Channel
(b) 9° Channel
(c) 10° Channel
(d) Duncan Passage
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Nine Degree Channel divides the Lakshadweep group, with Minicoy to its south. The 8° Channel lies between Minicoy and the Maldives; the 10° Channel separates Andaman from Nicobar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment)

9. India's only active volcano is found on:
(a) Narcondam Island
(b) Great Nicobar
(c) Car Nicobar
(d) Barren Island
Answer: (d)
Solution: India's only active volcano is on Barren Island in the Andaman and Nicobar group. Narcondam is a volcanic island but is not listed as active.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

10. The black soil of the Deccan is formed mainly from the weathering of which rock?
(a) Granite
(b) Basalt (Deccan Trap lava)
(c) Limestone
(d) Sandstone
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Deccan Trap is of volcanic origin; its igneous (basalt) rocks have denuded over time to form black soil.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

11. Which place receives the highest average rainfall in the world?
(a) Cherrapunji
(b) Mahabaleshwar
(c) Mawsynram
(d) Agumbe
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mawsynram, in the southern ranges of the Khasi Hills (Meghalaya), receives the highest average rainfall in the world. Choosing Cherrapunji is the classic outdated-fact trap.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

12. Which is the largest Peninsular river of India?
(a) Godavari
(b) Krishna
(c) Narmada
(d) Kaveri
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Godavari is the largest Peninsular river (about 1500 km) and also has the largest drainage basin among them, so it is called the Dakshin Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

13. Which water body separates the Andaman Islands from the Nicobar Islands?
(a) 8° Channel
(b) 9° Channel
(c) 10° Channel
(d) Palk Strait
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Andaman group in the north and the Nicobar group in the south are separated by the Ten Degree Channel.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment)

14. In the Northern Plains, 'Bhangar' refers to:
(a) New alluvium of the floodplains
(b) Older alluvium lying above the floodplains
(c) A marshy forested belt
(d) A belt of boulders at the foothills
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bhangar is the older alluvium that lies above the floodplains as terraces and contains kankar. Khadar is the newer alluvium of the floodplains.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

15. Which of the following is NOT a west-flowing Peninsular river?
(a) Tapi
(b) Mahi
(c) Sabarmati
(d) Pennar
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Pennar flows east into the Bay of Bengal. The Tapi, Mahi and Sabarmati drain into the Arabian Sea. Read the word NOT carefully.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment)

16. The Sundarban Delta is formed by the waters of which rivers?
(a) Ganga and Brahmaputra
(b) Ganga and Yamuna
(c) Godavari and Krishna
(d) Mahanadi and Brahmani
Answer: (a)
Solution: The river carrying the waters of the Ganga and the Brahmaputra flows into the Bay of Bengal, and the delta formed is the Sundarban Delta, named after the Sundari tree.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

17. Chilika Lake, the largest salt water lake of India, lies in which state?
(a) Kerala
(b) Tamil Nadu
(c) Odisha
(d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Chilika lies in Odisha, south of the Mahanadi delta. Pulicat (Andhra Pradesh–Tamil Nadu) and Vembanad (Kerala) are other coastal lakes.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

18. Guru Shikhar on Mount Abu is the highest peak of which range?
(a) Aravali
(b) Vindhya
(c) Satpura
(d) Western Ghats
Answer: (a)
Solution: Guru Shikhar, on Mount Abu in Rajasthan, is the highest peak of the Aravali range.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment)

19. The word 'laterite' comes from the Latin word 'later', which means:
(a) Stone
(b) Sand
(c) Clay
(d) Brick
Answer: (d)
Solution: Laterite is derived from the Latin 'later' meaning brick. The soil forms where heavy rain causes intense leaching.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India-II)

20. Which is the highest peak of the Western Ghats?
(a) Doda Betta
(b) Anai Mudi
(c) Mahendragiri
(d) Guru Shikhar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Anai Mudi (2,695 m) is the highest peak of the Western Ghats, followed by Doda Betta (2,637 m). Mahendragiri is the highest peak of the Eastern Ghats.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

21. Consider the following statements:
1. The Himalayas consist of three parallel ranges – Himadri, Himachal and Shiwaliks.
2. The Shiwaliks are the oldest range of the Himalayas.
3. The Duns lie between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwaliks.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong: the Shiwaliks are the outermost range made of unconsolidated sediments brought down by rivers, not the oldest.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

22. Which of the following peaks is located in the Eastern Ghats?
(a) Anai Mudi
(b) Doda Betta
(c) Mahendragiri
(d) Guru Shikhar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mahendragiri (1,501 m) is the highest peak of the Eastern Ghats. Anai Mudi and Doda Betta are in the Western Ghats, and Guru Shikhar is in the Aravalis.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

23. Statement: "In the Terai belt, rivers disappear underground because the ground is very porous." Choose the correct evaluation.
(a) True, because the Terai is made of boulders
(b) False, because streams disappear in the Bhabar and re-emerge in the Terai
(c) True, because the Terai has fresh alluvium
(d) False, because Terai rivers fall directly into the sea
Answer: (b)
Solution: Streams disappear in the pebbly Bhabar belt and re-emerge to the south, creating the wet, swampy Terai. The statement swaps Bhabar and Terai.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Narmada – flows in a rift valley
(b) Godavari – forms an estuary
(c) Tapi – flows in a rift valley parallel to the Narmada
(d) Damodar – drains the Chotanagpur plateau
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Godavari flows east into the Bay of Bengal and makes a delta, not an estuary. The other three pairs are correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

25. Arrange the following rivers in decreasing order of length:
1. Godavari
2. Krishna
3. Narmada
4. Kaveri
(a) 1, 3, 2, 4
(b) 2, 1, 3, 4
(c) 1, 2, 3, 4
(d) 1, 2, 4, 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Godavari (about 1500 km) is longer than Krishna (about 1400 km), which is longer than Narmada (about 1312 km), and Kaveri (about 760 km) is the shortest. So the order is 1, 2, 3, 4.
Source: NCERT Class 9 and Class 11 Geography
