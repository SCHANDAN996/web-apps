126. Arrange the following divisions of the Himalayas from west to east:
1. Nepal Himalaya
2. Punjab Himalaya
3. Assam Himalaya
4. Kumaon Himalaya
(a) 2, 1, 4, 3
(b) 4, 2, 1, 3
(c) 2, 4, 1, 3
(d) 2, 4, 3, 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: West to east: Punjab (Indus–Satluj), Kumaon (Satluj–Kali), Nepal (Kali–Teesta) and Assam (Teesta–Dihang), i.e. 2, 4, 1, 3.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

127. Consider the following statements about the Northern Plains:

133. Assertion: Mawsynram receives the highest average rainfall in the world.
Reason: Mawsynram lies in the southern ranges of the Khasi Hills, which face the moist winds of the Bay of Bengal branch of the monsoon.
Choose the correct option.
(a) Both Assertion and Reason are true, and Reason is the correct explanation of Assertion
(b) Both Assertion and Reason are true, but Reason is not the correct explanation of Assertion
(c) Assertion is true, but Reason is false
(d) Assertion is false, but Reason is true
Answer: (a)
Solution: Both are true and linked: the moist Bay of Bengal winds are forced to rise over the Khasi Hills, so Mawsynram on their southern ranges gets very heavy rain.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)

134. Assertion: The Narmada forms a large delta at the Gulf of Khambhat.
Reason: The Narmada flows westwards in a rift valley formed due to faulting.
Choose the correct option.
(a) Both Assertion and Reason are true, and Reason is the correct explanation of Assertion
(b) Both Assertion and Reason are true, but Reason is not the correct explanation of Assertion
(c) Assertion is true, but Reason is false
(d) Assertion is false, but Reason is true
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Reason is true, but the Assertion is false: the Narmada makes an estuary, not a delta. The trap reverses the delta–estuary fact.
Source: PYQ-style

136. Consider the following statements:

2. The Eastern Ghats stretch from the Mahanadi valley to the Nilgiris in the south.

(b) 1 and 2 only

137. Which soil develops on crystalline igneous rocks in areas of low rainfall in the eastern and southern parts of the Deccan plateau?
(a) Laterite soil
(b) Red and yellow soil
(c) Black soil
(d) Arid soil
Answer: (b)
Solution: Red soil develops on crystalline igneous rocks in low-rainfall areas of the eastern and southern Deccan plateau. Laterite needs heavy rain and intense leaching.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India-II)

138. In the Chambal basin, land made unfit for cultivation by deep gullies is called:
(a) Ravines
(b) Bhabar
(c) Doab
(d) Kayal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Running water cuts deep channels (gullies) and the land becomes bad land; in the Chambal basin such lands are called ravines.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India-II)

140. Which of the following statements about the Indus is correct?
(a) It rises in Tibet near Lake Mansarovar
(b) It flows into the Bay of Bengal
(c) The Indus Water Treaty was signed in 1971
(d) India can use 80 per cent of the water of the Indus system

145. Winter rain in Punjab and Haryana, which helps the rabi crop, is brought mainly by:
(a) The retreating monsoon
(b) Western disturbances from the Mediterranean region
(c) The Arabian Sea branch of the monsoon
(d) Tropical cyclones of the Bay of Bengal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Low-pressure systems from the Mediterranean Sea and western Asia move into India with the westerly flow and cause winter rain in the north-western plains.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India-I)
