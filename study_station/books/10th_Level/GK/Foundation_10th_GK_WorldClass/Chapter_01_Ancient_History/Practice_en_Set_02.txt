Set 2/6 – Ancient Indian History MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Which of the following Vedas is often called the ‘book of magical formulas’?
(A) Rig Veda
(B) Sama Veda
(C) Yajur Veda
(D) Atharva Veda
Answer: (D)
Solution: Atharva Veda contains spells, charms, and magical formulas for healing, success, and protection. Rig Veda is hymns, Sama Veda is melodies, Yajur Veda is prose mantras.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

27. In the Later Vedic period, the term ‘Rashtra’ replaced which earlier political unit?
(A) Jana
(B) Kula
(C) Grama
(D) Vish
Answer: (A)
Solution: Early Vedic society was organised into ‘Jana’ (tribe). In the Later Vedic period, territorial identity grew, and the term ‘Rashtra’ became common.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2017

28. The Indus script was written in which style?
(A) Only left-to-right
(B) Only right-to-left
(C) Boustrophedon
(D) Only top-to-bottom
Answer: (C)
Solution: Indus script was written in the boustrophedon style—alternating lines left-to-right and right-to-left.
Source: Railway NTPC 2019

29. Consider the following statements:

The Harappan people were completely unfamiliar with the horse.

Surkotada has yielded evidence of horse bones.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(A) 1 only
(B) 2 only
(C) Both 1 and 2
(D) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (B)
Solution: Statement 1 is incorrect because Surkotada (Gujarat) has reported horse bones, though the presence of horses in the Harappan period is still debated. Statement 2 is correct as per the findings.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2015
⚠️ Trap Type: Almost True Statement – many recall that the Harappan civilisation did not use horses widely and may mark statement 1 as correct, but the absolute “completely unfamiliar” makes it false.

30. Who among the following was the first to apply the term ‘Ekarat’ to a ruler?
(A) Chandragupta Maurya
(B) Mahapadma Nanda
(C) Ashoka
(D) Bimbisara
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Puranas refer to Mahapadma Nanda as ‘Ekarat’, the sole sovereign who destroyed many Kshatriya families and unified large parts of India.
Source: SSC CHSL 2018 (adapted)
⚠️ Trap Type: Similar Name/Chronological Confusion – students often confuse the title with Mauryan rulers.

31. The ‘Gayatri Mantra’ is found in which Mandala of the Rig Veda?
(A) First Mandala
(B) Third Mandala
(C) Ninth Mandala
(D) Tenth Mandala
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Gayatri Mantra, dedicated to Savitr, appears in the third Mandala of the Rig Veda (3.62.10).
Source: SSC CPO 2020

32. The ‘Boghazkoi inscription’ is associated with which Vedic deity?
(A) Agni
(B) Varuna
(C) Indra
(D) Mitra
Answer: (C)
Solution: A treaty inscription found at Boghazkoi (Turkey) mentions the names of Vedic deities Indra, Mitra, Varuna, and Nasatyas, confirming the presence of Rigvedic elements in West Asia around 1400 BCE. Indra is prominently mentioned.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2018

33. Which of the following pairs of Ashokan edicts and their content is NOT correctly matched?
(A) Major Rock Edict I – Prohibition of animal sacrifice
(B) Major Rock Edict II – Medical treatment for humans and animals
(C) Major Rock Edict XIII – Kalinga war and Dhamma Vijay
(D) Major Rock Edict VII – Appointment of Dhamma Mahamatras
Answer: (D)
Solution: The appointment of Dhamma Mahamatras is mentioned in Major Rock Edict V, not VII. Edict VII deals with tolerance among sects.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2019 (adapted)
⚠️ Trap Type: Almost True Statement – all statements look correct but one has a small shift in edict number.

34. The ‘Maski inscription’ is historically significant because it:
(A) is the first Sanskrit royal inscription
(B) mentions the personal name of Ashoka
(C) records the battle of Narmada
(D) is the earliest example of Brahmi script
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Maski (Karnataka) minor rock edict is one of the few inscriptions where Ashoka’s personal name appears alongside his title Devanampiya Piyadassi.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

35. Which of the following Gupta temples is an early example of stone temple architecture with a shikhara?
(A) Kailashnath Temple, Kanchi
(B) Dashavatara Temple, Deogarh
(C) Brihadeshwara Temple, Thanjavur
(D) Shore Temple, Mahabalipuram
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Dashavatara Temple at Deogarh (Uttar Pradesh) is a Gupta-era stone temple with a simple shikhara. Others are later constructions.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2016

36. The earliest known epigraphical evidence of ‘Sati’ in India is found in the:
(A) Junagadh inscription of Rudradaman
(B) Eran pillar inscription of Bhanugupta
(C) Aihole inscription of Pulakeshin II
(D) Allahabad pillar inscription of Samudragupta
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Eran (Madhya Pradesh) pillar inscription of Bhanugupta (c. 510 CE) records the first epigraphic evidence of Sati practice.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2020

37. Consider the following statements about the Nalanda University:

It was established by Kumaragupta I of the Gupta dynasty.

Hiuen Tsang studied and taught at Nalanda.

It was destroyed by Bakhtiyar Khilji in the 12th century.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(A) 1 and 2 only
(B) 2 and 3 only
(C) 1 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (D)
Solution: All three statements are correct. Kumaragupta I founded it, Hiuen Tsang spent many years there, and Bakhtiyar Khilji destroyed it around 1193 CE.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

38. The ‘Stupa’ at Sanchi was originally a brick structure enlarged during the period of the:
(A) Mauryas
(B) Shungas
(C) Kushanas
(D) Guptas
Answer: (B)
Solution: The original brick Stupa at Sanchi was built by Ashoka, but it was enlarged and given a stone casing during the Shunga period.
Source: Railway NTPC 2020

39. Which of the following sites is NOT associated with Jainism?
(A) Shravanabelagola
(B) Udayagiri-Khandagiri
(C) Amaravati
(D) Pavapuri
Answer: (C)
Solution: Amaravati is a famous Buddhist stupa site in Andhra Pradesh. Shravanabelagola (Gomateshwara), Udayagiri-Khandagiri (Jain caves), and Pavapuri (Mahavira’s nirvana) are Jain sites.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

40. The ‘Mrichchhakatika’ (The Little Clay Cart) was written by:
(A) Kalidasa
(B) Shudraka
(C) Bhasa
(D) Vishakhadatta
Answer: (B)
Solution: Shudraka wrote Mrichchhakatika. Kalidasa wrote Abhijnanashakuntalam, Bhasa wrote Svapnavasavadatta, Vishakhadatta wrote Mudrarakshasa.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

41. Which one of the following is the correct chronological order of the rulers of the Haryanka dynasty?
(A) Bimbisara – Udayin – Ajatashatru
(B) Bimbisara – Ajatashatru – Udayin
(C) Ajatashatru – Bimbisara – Udayin
(D) Udayin – Bimbisara – Ajatashatru
Answer: (B)
Solution: Haryanka order: Bimbisara → Ajatashatru (son) → Udayin (who founded Pataliputra).
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2015

42. The doctrine of ‘Anekantavada’ is a core concept of:
(A) Buddhism
(B) Jainism
(C) Ajivika sect
(D) Charvaka philosophy
Answer: (B)
Solution: Anekantavada is the Jain principle of many-sidedness or non-absolutism. Syadvada is its corollary.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

43. Which of the following statements about the Chalukya king Pulakeshin II is correct?
(A) He was defeated by Harsha.
(B) He wrote the Aihole inscription.
(C) His court poet was Banabhatta.
(D) He successfully resisted Harsha’s southward advance.
Answer: (D)
Solution: Pulakeshin II defeated Harsha on the Narmada, not the other way. The Aihole inscription was composed by his court poet Ravikirti, not by the king. Banabhatta was Harsha’s court poet.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2017
⚠️ Trap Type: Reverse Causation and Similar Name – reversing the winner of the Narmada battle, mixing up poets.

44. The earliest use of the term ‘Tamraparni’ for Sri Lanka is found in:
(A) Arthashastra
(B) Indica of Megasthenes
(C) Asokan inscriptions
(D) Harshacharita
Answer: (C)
Solution: Ashoka’s Major Rock Edict II and XIII refer to Sri Lanka as ‘Tamraparni’.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

45. With reference to the religious practices in India, the ‘Ajivika’ sect was:
(A) Founded by Makkhali Gosala
(B) A contemporary and rival of Jainism and Buddhism
(C) Believed in Niyati (fate)
(D) All of the above
Answer: (D)
Solution: Ajivika sect was founded by Makkhali Gosala, a contemporary of Mahavira and Buddha, and believed in absolute determinism (Niyati).
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2018

46. The term ‘Bhagadugha’ in the Vedic period referred to:
(A) Army chief
(B) Tax collector
(C) Royal priest
(D) Charioteer
Answer: (B)
Solution: In Later Vedic administration, Bhagadugha was the officer who collected the ‘Bhaga’ share of produce.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2019

47. Which of the following Puranas contains the earliest list of Maurya rulers?
(A) Vishnu Purana
(B) Vayu Purana
(C) Bhagavata Purana
(D) Brahmanda Purana
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Vayu Purana gives a detailed list of Maurya kings, including Chandragupta and Ashoka.
Source: SSC CPO 2020

48. The ‘Jataka tales’ are part of which Buddhist Pitaka?
(A) Vinaya Pitaka
(B) Sutta Pitaka
(C) Abhidhamma Pitaka
(D) None of the above
Answer: (B)
Solution: Jataka tales (stories of Buddha’s previous births) are part of the Khuddaka Nikaya, which is a section of the Sutta Pitaka.
Source: Railway Group D 2021

49. Consider the following pairs of Harappan sites and their location:

Kalibangan – Rajasthan

Surkotada – Gujarat

Ropar – Haryana

Alamgirpur – Uttar Pradesh
Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(A) 1, 2 and 3 only
(B) 1, 2 and 4 only
(C) 2 and 4 only
(D) 1, 3 and 4 only
Answer: (B)
Solution: Ropar (Rupnagar) is in Punjab, not Haryana. Kalibangan (Rajasthan), Surkotada (Gujarat), Alamgirpur (UP) are correctly matched.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2022 (adapted)
⚠️ Trap Type: Almost True Statement – three look correct, but Ropar’s location is off; students often misplace it.

50. The ‘Mahavastu’ is a sacred text of which Buddhist school?
(A) Theravada
(B) Mahayana
(C) Mahasanghika
(D) Sarvastivada
Answer: (C)
Solution: Mahavastu (‘Great Story’) is a text of the Mahasanghika sect, written in hybrid Sanskrit. It narrates the life of the Buddha.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2016
