1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Mehrgarh – Earliest farming	Neolithic site in Balochistan (c. 7000 BCE); earliest evidence of agriculture in Indian subcontinent.	Replacing Mehrgarh with Burzahom or Chirand as "earliest farming".	Similar Name
Burzahom – Pit dwellings	Neolithic site in Kashmir; people lived in underground pits.	Asking "pit dwellings" and offering Lothal or Kalibangan.	Almost True Statement
Lothal – Dockyard	Harappan site in Gujarat; dockyard and rice husk found.	Confusing with Dholavira (water management) or Mohenjodaro (Great Bath).	Similar Name
Iron in Later Vedic Age	Later Vedic texts call iron "shyama ayas" or "krishna ayas" (dark/black metal).	Placing iron use in Early Vedic or Harappan period.	Chronological Confusion
Mahapadma Nanda – "Ekarat"	First Nanda ruler, called "Ekarat" (sole sovereign) in the Puranas; built Magadha's first big empire before the Mauryas.	Giving the title "Ekarat" to Chandragupta Maurya, or calling Mahapadma a Maurya.	Almost True Statement
Ashoka’s edicts – Scripts	Mostly in Brahmi (left to right); north-western ones in Kharoshthi (right to left).	"All Ashokan edicts are in Brahmi." (False)	Negative Phrasing (all/none)
Kanishka’s Buddhist Council	Fourth council held in Kashmir (Kundalavana); patron Kanishka; Mahavibhasha compiled.	Placing 4th council in Pataliputra or under Ashoka.	Chronological Confusion
Samudragupta – Napoleon of India	Gupta ruler; Allahabad Pillar Inscription by Harishena details his conquests.	Confusing with Chandragupta Maurya or Harsha.	Similar Name
Gupta Golden Age – Reason	Cultural flourishing (literature, art, science), not coins.	Statement "called Golden Age because of gold coins" – wrong reason. First gold coins: Kushanas; most spectacular gold coins: Guptas (NCERT).	Reverse Causation
Rudradaman’s Junagadh Inscription	Earliest long royal inscription in Sanskrit (Shaka Kshatrapa, c. 150 CE).	Asking "first Sanskrit inscription" and giving Gupta ruler as answer.	Chronological Confusion
Harsha’s defeat at Narmada	Defeated by Chalukya king Pulakeshin II; Hiuen Tsang visited Harsha.	Confusing Narmada battle with Alexander’s Jhelum battle; mixing Fa-hien with Harsha.	Similar Name/Event
Third Buddhist Council	Held at Pataliputra under Ashoka; Abhidhamma Pitaka added.	Saying Abhidhamma was compiled at 1st or 2nd council.	Chronological Confusion
First Jain Council	Held at Pataliputra under Sthulabhadra; Shvetambara canon compiled at 2nd council (Vallabhi).	Swapping locations of Jain councils with Buddhist ones.	Similar Name

Samudragupta Allahabad inscription	15s	Yes	One-to-one mapping; no complexity.

Sapta Sindhu region vs Saraswati identification	45s (Analytical)	Moderate	Map-based and interpretive questions can take time.
