126. Consider the following statements regarding the Neolithic site of Mehrgarh:
1. It provides the earliest evidence of wheat and barley cultivation in the subcontinent.
2. Evidence of dental drilling has been found here.
3. The site shows a transition from semi-nomadic herding to settled agriculture.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mehrgarh (c. 7000 BCE, Balochistan) has yielded early wheat and barley, drilled human teeth indicating prehistoric dentistry, and clear layers showing the shift from herding to farming. The trap is assuming dentistry is a much later invention, but all three statements are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PYQ-style

127. With reference to Vedic geography, the river 'Sadanira' mentioned in the Shatapatha Brahmana has been identified with:
(a) Gomati (b) Ghaggar (c) Gandak (d) Saryu
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Shatapatha Brahmana describes Videgha Mathava's migration across the Sadanira, which formed the boundary between Kosala and Videha; it is identified with the modern Gandak. Ghaggar is tempting because of its Vedic fame, but it is identified with the Saraswati, so the answer is Gandak.
Source: PYQ-style

128. The early Buddhist texts mention 'Ashtamahasthanas'. These refer to:
(a) Eight great cities of the Mahajanapada period (b) Eight sacred places associated with the life of the Buddha (c) Eight chapters of the Vinaya Pitaka (d) Eight great monastic universities
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ashtamahasthanas are the eight great pilgrimage sites linked to Buddha's birth, enlightenment, first sermon, mahaparinirvana and four miracles. The "great cities" option is tempting because both lists contain eight items, but the correct answer is the eight sacred places of Buddha's life.
Source: PYQ-style

129. Which of the following is the correct chronological sequence of the composition of these texts?
(a) Rig Veda, Brahmanas, Upanishads, Aranyakas (b) Rig Veda, Brahmanas, Aranyakas, Upanishads (c) Rig Veda, Upanishads, Brahmanas, Aranyakas (d) Rig Veda, Aranyakas, Brahmanas, Upanishads
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vedic literature developed in the order Samhitas (Rig Veda earliest), then Brahmanas, then Aranyakas, then Upanishads. The common trap is reversing Aranyakas and Upanishads, but the Upanishads grew out of the Aranyaka layer, so the correct sequence is Rig Veda, Brahmanas, Aranyakas, Upanishads.
Source: PYQ-style

130. Which site in Maharashtra is famous for a bronze hoard including a chariot with animal figures?
(a) Chanhudaro (b) Kalibangan (c) Banawali (d) Daimabad
Answer: (d)
Solution: Daimabad (Maharashtra) yielded the famous bronze hoard including a chariot with animals, though its cultural affiliation with the Harappans is debated. Chanhudaro is tempting as a craft centre, but it is known for bead-making and lies in Sindh, so the answer is Daimabad.
Source: PYQ-style

131. The works of Panini, Pingala, and Kalidasa belong respectively to which disciplines?
(a) Astronomy, grammar, medicine (b) Dance, astronomy, drama (c) Grammar, prosody, dance (d) Grammar, prosody, poetry/drama
Answer: (d)
Solution: Panini wrote the Ashtadhyayi (grammar), Pingala the Chandahshastra (prosody), and Kalidasa composed poetry and drama. The dance option is a distractor playing on Pingala's metres being used in dance, so the answer is grammar, prosody, poetry/drama.
Source: PYQ-style

132. In Jain tradition, the lion is the symbol (lanchhana) of which Tirthankara?
(a) Rishabhanatha (b) Parshvanatha (c) Mahavira (d) Neminatha
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each Tirthankara has a cognizance symbol: Rishabhanatha has the bull, Parshvanatha the serpent, Neminatha the conch, and Mahavira the lion. The serpent option is tempting because Parshvanatha is often shown with a snake hood, so the answer is Mahavira.
Source: PYQ-style

133. The 'Uttarapatha' in ancient India referred to:
(a) The path of the monsoon winds (b) The southern trade route (c) The northern route connecting Taxila to Tamralipti (d) The royal highway built by Ashoka
Answer: (c)
Solution: Uttarapatha was the great northern trade route running from Taxila in the northwest to the port of Tamralipti in Bengal; the southern route was Dakshinapatha. The Ashokan highway option is a tempting confusion, so the answer is the northern route from Taxila to Tamralipti.
Source: PYQ-style

134. The 'Velvikkudi grant' gives crucial information about the revival of the Pandya kingdom under:
(a) Nedunjeliyan (b) Arikesari Maravarman (c) Kadungon (d) Jatila Parantaka
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Velvikkudi copper plates describe how Kadungon ended the Kalabhra interregnum and revived Pandya rule in the sixth century CE. Nedunjeliyan is tempting as a famous Sangam-era Pandya, but the grant credits Kadungon, so the answer is Kadungon.
Source: PYQ-style

135. Which Buddhist council led to the formal division of the Sangha into Sthaviravada and Mahasanghika?
(a) First Council (b) Fourth Council (c) Second Council (d) Third Council
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Second Council at Vaishali (c. 383 BCE) produced the first great schism over disciplinary rules, splitting the Sangha into Sthaviravada and Mahasanghika. The First Council merely compiled the teachings, so the answer is the Second Council.
Source: PYQ-style

136. The Acharanga Sutra and Kalpa Sutra are sacred texts of the:
(a) Mahayana Buddhists (b) Digambara Jains (c) Theravada Buddhists (d) Shvetambara Jains
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Acharanga Sutra (monastic conduct) and Kalpa Sutra (lives of the Tirthankaras) belong to the Shvetambara Jain canon. Digambaras reject the Shvetambara Agamas, so the answer is Shvetambara Jains.
Source: PYQ-style

137. In the Gupta period, the term 'Agrahara' denoted:
(a) A merchant guild (b) Tax-free land granted to Brahmins (c) A coastal port (d) The royal treasury
Answer: (b)
Solution: Agrahara was a tax-free grant of land or a village made to Brahmins for religious and educational purposes. The merchant guild (shreni) is a tempting confusion from the same economic vocabulary, so the answer is tax-free land granted to Brahmins.
Source: PYQ-style

138. The 'Nasik inscription' of Gautami Balashri eulogizes:
(a) Chandragupta Maurya (b) Ashoka (c) Rudradaman I (d) Gautamiputra Satakarni
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Nasik prashasti, issued by his mother Gautami Balashri, praises Gautamiputra Satakarni for restoring Satavahana glory and defeating the Shakas. Rudradaman I is tempting as the Shaka rival, but the eulogised king is Gautamiputra Satakarni.
Source: PYQ-style

139. The doctrine of 'Syadvada' in Jainism is most accurately described as:
(a) The theory of conditioned predication or relativity of knowledge (b) The theory of non-violence (c) The path of total renunciation (d) The doctrine of karma
Answer: (a)
Solution: Syadvada, the doctrine of 'maybe', holds that truth is relative to standpoint and can be expressed in seven modes (saptabhangi naya). Non-violence is ahimsa, a different principle, so the answer is the theory of conditioned predication.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Which of the following groups constitutes the 'Prasthanatrayi' of Vedanta philosophy?
(a) Puranas, Dharmashastras, Vedangas (b) Manusmriti, Arthashastra, Mahabhashya (c) Brahmasutras, Bhagavad Gita, Principal Upanishads (d) Ramayana, Mahabharata, Harivamsa
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Prasthanatrayi comprises the Upanishads (shruti prasthana), the Bhagavad Gita (smriti prasthana) and the Brahmasutras (nyaya prasthana). The other groups mix smritis and epics, so the answer is Brahmasutras, Bhagavad Gita, Principal Upanishads.
Source: PYQ-style

141. Consider the following statements about the Harappan civilization:
1. Rice husk has been found at Lothal and Rangpur.
2. Evidence of a ploughed field comes from Kalibangan.
3. A terracotta model of a plough has been found at Banawali.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rice remains occur at Lothal and Rangpur, Kalibangan shows a pre-Harappan ploughed field with furrows, and Banawali yielded a terracotta plough model. The trap is assuming rice was unknown to the Harappans, but all three statements are correct.
Source: PYQ-style

142. The earliest epigraphic reference to image worship in Vaishnavism is found in the:
(a) Besnagar inscription of Heliodorus (b) Junagadh inscription of Rudradaman I (c) Allahabad pillar inscription (d) Hathigumpha inscription
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Besnagar (Vidisha) Garuda pillar inscription of Heliodorus (c. 110 BCE), a Greek ambassador turned Bhagavata, records devotion to Vasudeva and is the earliest inscriptional evidence of Vaishnava worship. The Junagadh inscription is later and secular in theme, so the answer is the Besnagar inscription of Heliodorus.
Source: PYQ-style

143. Consider the following statements about the Ashokan pillars:
1. The pillars are monoliths of polished Chunar sandstone.
2. They carry edicts relating to Dhamma.
3. The pillar capitals include animals like lions, elephants, and bulls.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ashokan pillars are single blocks of polished Chunar sandstone bearing Dhamma edicts, with capitals such as the Sarnath lions, the Sankissa elephant and the Rampurva bull. The trap is thinking the capitals were only lions, but all three statements are correct.

144. In Sangam literature, the 'Palai' tinai represents:
(a) Hills and forests (b) Arid/desert region (c) Coastal region (d) Riverine agricultural tract
Answer: (b)
Solution: The five tinais are Kurinji (hills), Mullai (forest), Marutam (riverine plains), Neytal (coast) and Palai (parched land, linked with separation). The coastal option is Neytal, so the answer is the arid region.
Source: PYQ-style

145. Which of the following statements is correct regarding the 'Barabar caves'?
(a) They were built during the Gupta period for Buddhist monks. (b) They are the earliest rock-cut caves in India, dedicated by Ashoka to Ajivikas. (c) They contain paintings from the Jataka tales. (d) They are located in the Western Ghats.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Barabar caves (Bihar) are India's oldest rock-cut caves, donated by Ashoka and Dasharatha to the Ajivika sect. The trap is associating all early caves with Buddhism and Jataka paintings (Ajanta), so the correct statement is that they are the earliest rock-cut caves, dedicated by Ashoka to Ajivikas.
Source: PYQ-style

146. In ancient Indian history, the 'Parivrajaka' referred to:
(a) A class of tax collectors (b) Royal messengers (c) Foreign ambassadors (d) Wandering ascetics who renounced household life
Answer: (d)
Solution: Parivrajakas were wandering mendicants who renounced household life and often criticised Vedic ritualism, many joining heterodox sects. The official-sounding distractors are wrong, so the answer is wandering ascetics who renounced household life.

147. The Prayaga Prashasti inscribed on the Allahabad pillar, which eulogizes Samudragupta, was composed by:
(a) Harisena (b) Banabhatta (c) Kalidasa (d) Vishakhadatta
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Allahabad pillar prashasti of Samudragupta was composed by his court poet and minister Harisena; the same pillar also carries an earlier Ashokan edict. Banabhatta is tempting as another famous royal biographer, but he wrote Harsha's Harshacharita, so the answer is Harisena.

148. The 'Gathasaptasati' is a collection of Prakrit poems attributed to a king of which dynasty?
(a) Gupta (b) Maurya (c) Satavahana (d) Kushana
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Gathasaptasati, 700 lyrical verses in Maharashtri Prakrit on love and rural life, is ascribed to the Satavahana king Hala. The Gupta option tempts because of Sanskrit kavya, but the answer is Satavahana.

149. Consider the following pairs of Vedic deities and natural phenomena:

2. Parjanya – Rain

(a) 1, 2, 3 and 4 (b) 1, 3 and 4 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 only (d) 2, 3 and 4 only

150. The 'Sapta Sindhu' region in the Rigveda refers to the land of the:
(a) Seven sacred rivers of eastern India (b) Seven great Mahajanapadas (c) Indus and its five tributaries along with the Saraswati (d) Seven hills of the Deccan
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sapta Sindhu denotes the Indus, its five tributaries (Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) and the Saraswati. The Mahajanapada option belongs to a later period, so the answer is the Indus, its five tributaries and the Saraswati.
