76. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of the four Buddhist councils?
(A) Rajgriha, Vaishali, Pataliputra, Kashmir
(B) Vaishali, Rajgriha, Kashmir, Pataliputra
(C) Rajgriha, Pataliputra, Vaishali, Kashmir
(D) Kashmir, Rajgriha, Vaishali, Pataliputra

Answer: (A)
Solution: 1st council – Rajgriha; 2nd – Vaishali; 3rd – Pataliputra; 4th – Kashmir. The mnemonic is R V P K.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Consider the following statements about Ashoka’s Dhamma:

It was identical with Buddhism.

It emphasised moral conduct and tolerance.

It completely abolished capital punishment.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(A) 2 only
(B) 1 and 2 only
(C) 2 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (A)
Solution: Statement 1 is false; Dhamma was Ashoka’s own code of ethics, not Buddhism itself. Statement 3 is false; capital punishment was not abolished, only delayed. Statement 2 is correct.
Source: PYQ-style
⚠️ Trap Type: Almost True Statement – many equate Ashoka’s Dhamma with Buddhism; also, the exaggeration in statement 3 (completely abolished) tests factual precision.

78. The ‘Atharvaveda’ primarily deals with:
(A) Hymns to deities
(B) Melodies for sacrifices
(C) Magic, spells and healing
(D) Prose rituals

Answer: (C)
Solution: The Atharvaveda is a collection of charms, spells, and magical formulas for healing, success, and protection.
Source: PYQ-style

79. Which of the following Harappan sites is located in Haryana?
(A) Dholavira
(B) Rakhigarhi

Solution: Rakhigarhi, the largest Indian Harappan site, is situated in Haryana. Dholavira and Lothal are in Gujarat; Kalibangan in Rajasthan.

(A) The Gupta provincial administration

84. Which of the following rulers issued the first royal inscription in Sanskrit?
(A) Ashoka
(B) Chandragupta II
(C) Rudradaman I
(D) Samudragupta

Answer: (C)
Solution: Rudradaman I, a Shaka Kshatrapa, inscribed the Junagadh rock edict in correct Sanskrit. Ashoka used Prakrit; the Guptas came later.
Source: PYQ-style
⚠️ Trap Type: Chronological Confusion – many choose a Gupta ruler, assuming Sanskrit started with the ‘Golden Age’.

90. Consider the following pairs of rulers and foreign visitors:

Chandragupta Maurya – Megasthenes

Harshavardhana – Fa-hien

Chandragupta II – Hiuen Tsang
Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(A) 1 only
(B) 1 and 2 only
(C) 2 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (A)
Solution: Only pair 1 is correct. Fa-hien visited during Chandragupta II’s reign, not Harsha’s. Hiuen Tsang visited Harsha, not Chandragupta II.
Source: PYQ-style
⚠️ Trap Type: Similar Name – mixing the two Chinese pilgrims and their host kings is a very common error.

91. The Virupaksha temple at Pattadakal was built by:
(A) Queen Lokamahadevi of the Chalukyas to commemorate Vikramaditya II’s victory over Kanchi

(C) Narasimhavarman II of the Pallavas

Source: PYQ-style

92. In the Rig Veda, the term ‘Gavishthi’ refers to:
(A) Search for cows (cattle raid)
(B) The royal coronation
(C) An assembly of elders
(D) A fire sacrifice

Answer: (A)
Solution: ‘Gavishthi’ literally means ‘desire for cows’ and denotes cattle raids, highlighting the importance of cattle in Rigvedic society.
Source: PYQ-style

98. With reference to the Rigvedic period, the term ‘Vis’ refers to:
(A) The tribal king
(B) The clan or larger social unit
(C) A cattle raid
(D) The village headman

Answer: (B)
Solution: In the Rigvedic period, the social hierarchy was Kula (family) → Grama (village) → Vis (clan) → Jana (tribe).
Source: PYQ-style

99. The ‘Dasavatara temple’ at Deogarh is an example of which architectural style?
(A) Nagara
(B) Dravida
(C) Vesara
(D) Gandhara

Answer: (A)
Solution: The Deogarh temple of the Gupta period is an early Nagara-style temple with a curvilinear shikhara.
Source: PYQ-style
