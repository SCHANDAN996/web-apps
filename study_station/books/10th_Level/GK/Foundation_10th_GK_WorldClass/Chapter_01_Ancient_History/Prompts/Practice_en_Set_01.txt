Set 1/6 – Ancient Indian History MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which of the following Neolithic sites is famous for pit-dwellings?
(A) Mehrgarh
(B) Burzahom
(C) Chirand
(D) Lothal
Answer: (B)
Solution: Burzahom in Kashmir is known for underground pit-dwellings. Mehrgarh is the earliest farming site, Chirand in Bihar, and Lothal is a Harappan site with a dockyard.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

2. The Great Bath is located at which Harappan site?
(A) Harappa
(B) Mohenjodaro
(C) Lothal
(D) Dholavira
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Great Bath, a large public water tank, was discovered at Mohenjodaro.
Source: Railway NTPC 2020

3. The term ‘Shyam Ayas’ in the Shatapatha Brahmana refers to:
(A) Copper
(B) Iron
(C) Silver
(D) Gold
Answer: (B)
Solution: ‘Shyam Ayas’ literally means ‘dark metal’, which is iron. It is mentioned in the Later Vedic text Shatapatha Brahmana.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

4. Who among the following was the royal physician of Bimbisara?
(A) Charaka
(B) Sushruta
(C) Jivaka
(D) Vagbhata
Answer: (C)
Solution: Jivaka was the royal physician to King Bimbisara of the Haryanka dynasty.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2017

5. The first Buddhist Council was held at:
(A) Vaishali
(B) Pataliputra
(C) Rajgriha
(D) Kashmir
Answer: (C)
Solution: The first Buddhist Council was held at Rajgriha under the patronage of Ajatashatru, presided over by Mahakashyapa.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

6. Which Mauryan ruler is known for the Kalinga War?
(A) Chandragupta Maurya
(B) Bindusara
(C) Ashoka
(D) Brihadratha
Answer: (C)
Solution: Ashoka fought the Kalinga War in 261 BCE; the bloodshed transformed him towards Dhamma.
Source: Railway Group D 2019

7. The ‘Allahabad Prashasti’ is associated with which Gupta ruler?
(A) Chandragupta I
(B) Samudragupta
(C) Chandragupta II
(D) Skandagupta
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Allahabad Pillar Inscription, composed by Harishena, glorifies Samudragupta’s military conquests.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

8. Fa-hien, the Chinese pilgrim, visited India during the reign of:
(A) Ashoka
(B) Harshavardhana
(C) Chandragupta II
(D) Skandagupta
Answer: (C)
Solution: Fa-hien visited India during the Gupta period under Chandragupta II ‘Vikramaditya’.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2016

9. The famous ‘Junagadh Rock Inscription’ belongs to which ruler?
(A) Ashoka
(B) Samudragupta
(C) Rudradaman I
(D) Harsha
Answer: (C)
Solution: Rudradaman I, a Shaka Kshatrapa, issued the first royal Sanskrit inscription at Junagadh. Ashoka’s inscriptions were in Prakrit.
Source: SSC CHSL 2018
⚠️ Trap Type: Similar Name/Chronological Confusion – students often pick Ashoka or a Gupta ruler as the issuer of the first Sanskrit inscription.

10. Which of the following is NOT a site of the Indus Valley Civilization?
(A) Kalibangan
(B) Surkotada
(C) Pataliputra
(D) Dholavira
Answer: (C)
Solution: Pataliputra (modern Patna) was the capital of Magadha/Maurya, not an Indus Valley site.
Source: IBPS Clerk 2020

11. The words ‘Satyameva Jayate’ in the State Emblem of India are taken from:
(A) Rig Veda
(B) Mundaka Upanishad
(C) Bhagavad Gita
(D) Arthashastra
Answer: (B)
Solution: ‘Satyameva Jayate’ comes from the Mundaka Upanishad, later adopted in the Lion Capital of Ashoka.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

12. Who among the following is known as the ‘Napoleon of India’?
(A) Chandragupta Maurya
(B) Samudragupta
(C) Harsha
(D) Ashoka
Answer: (B)
Solution: Samudragupta is often called the Napoleon of India due to his extensive military conquests.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2018

13. The Mahajanapada ‘Vajji’ had which type of government?
(A) Monarchy
(B) Republic (Ganasangha)
(C) Theocracy
(D) Oligarchy
Answer: (B)
Solution: Vajji (with capital Vaishali) was a Ganasangha or republican state, unlike Magadha which was a monarchy.
Source: Railway NTPC 2020

14. Which Veda is a collection of melodies and chants?
(A) Rig Veda
(B) Sama Veda
(C) Yajur Veda
(D) Atharva Veda
Answer: (B)
Solution: Sama Veda is the book of melodies and chants. Rig Veda is hymns, Yajur Veda is prose mantras, Atharva Veda contains spells and charms.
Source: SSC MTS 2019

15. The third Buddhist Council was patronised by:
(A) Ajatashatru
(B) Kanishka
(C) Ashoka
(D) Harsha
Answer: (C)
Solution: The third council was held at Pataliputra under Ashoka, presided over by Moggaliputta Tissa.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

16. Which metal was first used by the Vedic people, marking the Later Vedic period?
(A) Copper
(B) Bronze
(C) Iron
(D) Silver
Answer: (C)
Solution: Iron (Shyam Ayas) became common in the Later Vedic period, enabling large-scale agriculture.
Source: Railway Group D 2021

17. Lothal, a famous Harappan site, is known for its:
(A) Great Bath
(B) Dockyard
(C) Stone bust of a bearded priest
(D) Ploughed field
Answer: (B)
Solution: Lothal (Gujarat) had a dockyard. Great Bath is at Mohenjodaro, ploughed field at Kalibangan.
Source: SSC CGL 2017

18. The title ‘Devanampiya Piyadassi’ was adopted by:
(A) Chandragupta Maurya
(B) Bindusara
(C) Ashoka
(D) Kanishka
Answer: (C)
Solution: Ashoka used this title in his inscriptions, meaning ‘Beloved of the Gods, He who looks on with affection’.
Source: IBPS SO 2020

19. The Gupta era began in:
(A) 78 CE
(B) 319 CE
(C) 606 CE
(D) 150 BCE
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Gupta era started in 319 CE with the accession of Chandragupta I.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

20. The ‘Milinda Panho’ is a record of a dialogue between:
(A) Kanishka and Vasumitra
(B) Menander and Nagasena
(C) Ashoka and Moggaliputta Tissa
(D) Harsha and Hiuen Tsang
Answer: (B)
Solution: ‘Milinda Panho’ (Questions of Milinda) is a Pali text recording the discussion between Indo-Greek king Menander (Milinda) and Buddhist monk Nagasena.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2015

21. (Medium) Consider the following pairs:

Haryanka : Bimbisara

Nanda : Mahapadma

Maurya : Chandragupta
Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(A) 1 and 2 only
(B) 2 and 3 only
(C) 1, 2 and 3
(D) 1 only
Answer: (C)
Solution: All three are correctly matched. Bimbisara was Haryanka, Mahapadma Nanda founded the Nanda dynasty, and Chandragupta Maurya founded the Maurya dynasty. The trap here could be thinking Mahapadma was Maurya, but he is not.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2022
⚠️ Trap Type: Almost True Statement – mixing dynasty names can lead to wrong matches if one is unsure about Nanda’s founder.

22. (Medium) The famous ‘Aihole inscription’ of Pulakeshin II was composed by:
(A) Harishena
(B) Ravikirti
(C) Banabhatta
(D) Kalidasa
Answer: (B)
Solution: The Aihole prashasti of Chalukya king Pulakeshin II was written by his court poet Ravikirti. Harishena composed the Allahabad prashasti.
Source: SSC CGL 2021 (modified)

23. (Medium) Arrange the following Buddhist councils in chronological order:

Vaishali

Rajgriha

Pataliputra

Kashmir
Select the correct answer using the codes:
(A) 2-1-3-4
(B) 1-2-4-3
(C) 2-3-1-4
(D) 3-2-1-4
Answer: (A)
Solution: Order: Rajgriha (1st) → Vaishali (2nd) → Pataliputra (3rd) → Kashmir (4th).
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2016
⚠️ Trap Type: Chronological Confusion – many candidates mix the 3rd and 4th council locations or patrons.

24. (Medium) Which of the following statements about the Gupta period is/are correct?

Aryabhata wrote the Surya Siddhanta.

The Iron Pillar at Mehrauli belongs to Chandragupta II.

Kalidasa adorned the court of Samudragupta.
Select the correct answer:
(A) 1 and 2 only
(B) 2 only
(C) 2 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (B)
Solution: Statement 1 is false: Surya Siddhanta is an earlier astronomical text, not by Aryabhata (he wrote Aryabhatiya). Statement 3 is false: Kalidasa is associated with Chandragupta II, not Samudragupta. Only statement 2 is correct.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2019 (adapted)
⚠️ Trap Type: Almost True Statement – mixing up the ruler-patron associations and attributing a wrong text to Aryabhata.

25. (Medium) The ‘Mattavilasa Prahasana’ was written by a king of which dynasty?
(A) Gupta
(B) Pallava
(C) Chalukya
(D) Rashtrakuta
Answer: (B)
Solution: Mahendravarman I of the Pallava dynasty authored this satirical play.
Source: SSC CPO 2020
