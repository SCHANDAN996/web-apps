Set 3/6 – Ancient Indian History MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Consider the following statements about the Rigvedic period:

The term ‘Rashtra’ denoted a territorial state.

The king’s power was limited by the assemblies called Sabha and Samiti.

The battle of ten kings (Dasharajna) was fought on the banks of the river Parushni.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(A) 1 and 2 only
(B) 2 and 3 only
(C) 1 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (B)
Solution: Statement 1 is incorrect: ‘Rashtra’ (territorial state) is a Later Vedic term; in the Rigvedic period, the political unit was ‘Jana’. Statements 2 and 3 are correct. The Sabha and Samiti acted as checks on royal power, and the Dasharajna was fought on the Parushni (Ravi).
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2017
⚠️ Trap Type: Chronological Confusion – projecting Later Vedic terminology into the Early Vedic period.

52. Which of the following pairs of Harappan sites and the rivers on which they are situated is NOT correctly matched?
(A) Harappa – Ravi
(B) Mohenjodaro – Indus
(C) Kalibangan – Ghaggar
(D) Lothal – Mahi

Answer: (D)
Solution: Lothal is situated on the river Bhogava, not Mahi. Harappa (Ravi), Mohenjodaro (Indus), Kalibangan (Ghaggar) are correctly matched.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

53. The ‘Sutlej-Yamuna divide’ in the context of the Indus Valley Civilization refers to:
(A) The region where the earliest farming began
(B) The presence of a large number of Harappan sites along the now-dry course of the Saraswati
(C) The location of the main copper mines used by Harappans
(D) The route used by Aryans to enter India

Answer: (B)
Solution: The area between the Sutlej and Yamuna rivers, drained by the dry bed of the Ghaggar-Hakra (identified with the ancient Saraswati), contains a dense concentration of Harappan sites.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2019

54. With reference to the Mahajanapada period, which of the following was the capital of Kosala?
(A) Ujjain
(B) Shravasti
(C) Vaishali
(D) Kaushambi

Answer: (B)
Solution: Kosala, one of the 16 Mahajanapadas, had its capital at Shravasti (also associated with Saket/Ayodhya). Ujjain was the capital of Avanti, Vaishali of Vajji, and Kaushambi of Vatsa.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

55. Consider the following: “He was the first Nanda ruler, described in the Puranas as ‘Ekarat’, and is credited with destroying the Kshatriyas.” This refers to:
(A) Dhanananda
(B) Mahapadma Nanda
(C) Kalashoka
(D) Bimbisara

Answer: (B)
Solution: Mahapadma Nanda, the founder of the Nanda dynasty, is called ‘Ekarat’ (sole sovereign) in the Puranas. Dhanananda was the last Nanda, Kalashoka belonged to the Shishunaga dynasty, and Bimbisara to the Haryanka.
Source: SSC CGL 2021
⚠️ Trap Type: Similar Name – Dhanananda is also a Nanda ruler but the epithet ‘Ekarat’ and the description refer to Mahapadma.

56. The ‘Saptanga theory’ of the state was propounded in:
(A) Arthashastra
(B) Manusmriti
(C) Shukranitisara
(D) Mahabharata

Answer: (A)
Solution: The Arthashastra of Kautilya (Chanakya) elaborates the Saptanga theory, listing the seven limbs (organs) of the state.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2016

57. Which of the following Ashokan inscriptions mentions the Kalinga war and its impact?
(A) Major Rock Edict I
(B) Major Rock Edict XIII
(C) Minor Rock Edict I
(D) Bhabru Edict

Answer: (B)
Solution: Major Rock Edict XIII describes the Kalinga war and Ashoka’s subsequent remorse and adoption of Dhamma Vijay.
Source: SSC CPO 2020

58. The ‘Hathigumpha inscription’ is associated with which ruler?
(A) Ashoka
(B) Kharavela
(C) Chandragupta II
(D) Pulakeshin II

Answer: (B)
Solution: The Hathigumpha (Elephant Cave) inscription in the Udayagiri hills (Odisha) records the achievements of King Kharavela of the Mahameghavahana dynasty.
Source: Railway NTPC 2021

59. With reference to the Gupta administration, the term ‘Kumaramatya’ refers to:
(A) Crown prince
(B) A high-ranking provincial official
(C) Commander of the cavalry
(D) Minister for foreign affairs

Answer: (B)
Solution: Kumaramatyas were a cadre of high-ranking officers, often of royal lineage, who served as provincial governors or in other important administrative positions. The literal meaning (‘prince-minister’) misleads many into selecting (A).
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2018
⚠️ Trap Type: Almost True Statement – the prefix ‘Kumara’ suggests prince, but the term denoted a distinct official class.

60. The ‘Mandsaur inscription’ provides information about:
(A) The silk weavers’ guild during the Gupta period
(B) The conquests of Samudragupta
(C) The administration of Chandragupta II
(D) The Hun invasion under Skandagupta

Answer: (A)
Solution: The Mandsaur (Madhya Pradesh) inscription of the time of Kumaragupta I records the construction of a Sun temple by a guild of silk weavers, illustrating the vitality of guilds in the Gupta economy.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2015

61. Which of the following statements about the Chalcolithic cultures of India is correct?
(A) They were primarily iron-using communities.
(B) They buried their dead in urns with distinctive black-and-red ware pottery.
(C) They were unaware of cattle domestication.
(D) The Ahar-Banas culture is located in the Ganga valley.

Answer: (B)
Solution: Chalcolithic (copper-stone) cultures used copper, not iron (eliminates A). They practised cattle domestication (eliminates C). Ahar-Banas is in Rajasthan, not the Ganga valley (eliminates D). Distinctive Black-and-Red Ware (BRW) and urn burials are characteristic of these cultures.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2017

62. The ‘Sangam literature’ was composed during the period of which dynasty’s patronage in the Tamil region?
(A) Pallavas
(B) Cholas
(C) Pandyas
(D) Cheras

Answer: (C)
Solution: The three Sangams (assemblies of poets) were convened under the patronage of the Pandya kings of Madurai. The Cholas, Cheras, and later Pallavas also patronised literature, but the Sangam corpus is specifically linked to the Pandyas.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

63. In Buddhism, the term ‘Arhat’ refers to:
(A) A future Buddha
(B) A fully ordained monk
(C) One who has attained Nirvana
(D) A bodhisattva who delays his own salvation

Answer: (C)
Solution: An Arhat (or Arahant) is a person who has attained enlightenment and freedom from the cycle of rebirth. The concept of a Bodhisattva who postpones Nirvana to help others is a Mahayana ideal (D).
Source: IBPS PO 2019

64. The ‘Ajanta cave paintings’ mostly depict themes from:
(A) Jain Tirthankaras
(B) Hindu epics
(C) Jataka tales and Buddha’s life
(D) Daily life of the Gupta period

Answer: (C)
Solution: The Ajanta murals, created from the 2nd century BCE to the 6th century CE, predominantly illustrate the Jataka stories (previous lives of the Buddha) and scenes from the life of Gautama Buddha.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

65. Which of the following sites has yielded the earliest evidence of rice cultivation in the Indian subcontinent?
(A) Mehrgarh
(B) Lothal
(C) Koldihwa
(D) Burzahom

Answer: (C)
Solution: Koldihwa (Uttar Pradesh) has provided evidence of rice dating to c. 6500 BCE. Lothal also shows rice husk, but it is of a much later Harappan date. Mehrgarh is associated with wheat and barley.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2019
⚠️ Trap Type: Almost True Statement – many recall rice husk at Lothal and select it, forgetting that Koldihwa is far older.

66. The ‘Rummindei pillar inscription’ of Ashoka records:
(A) Ashoka’s visit to Lumbini and tax concession
(B) The Kalinga war
(C) The construction of the Great Stupa at Sanchi
(D) The third Buddhist council

Answer: (A)
Solution: The Rummindei (Lumbini) pillar states that Ashoka visited the Buddha’s birthplace and reduced the tax rate to one-eighth.
Source: Railway Group D 2020

67. Consider the following pairs of ancient Indian texts and their authors:

Harshacharita – Banabhatta

Gaudavaho – Vakpati

Vikramankadevacharita – Bilhana
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(A) 1 only
(B) 1 and 2 only
(C) 1 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (D)
Solution: All are correctly matched. Harshacharita is the biography of Harsha by Banabhatta. Gaudavaho is a Prakrit poem by Vakpati celebrating Yasovarman. Vikramankadevacharita by Bilhana is a Sanskrit work on the Chalukya king Vikramaditya VI.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2016

68. With reference to the Vedic sacrifices, the ‘Rajasuya’ was:
(A) A coronation ceremony of the king
(B) A sacrifice performed to assert imperial supremacy over other kings
(C) A horse sacrifice
(D) A ritual for prosperity of cattle

Answer: (A)
Solution: Rajasuya was the royal consecration or coronation rite. The Ashvamedha (horse sacrifice) was performed to claim imperial sovereignty. Vajapeya was also associated with royal power.
Source: SSC CPO 2019
⚠️ Trap Type: Similar Name – Rajasuya and Ashvamedha are often confused; both are royal ceremonies but with distinct meanings.

69. The ‘Nagara style’ of temple architecture is associated with which region?
(A) South India
(B) North India
(C) Deccan
(D) Eastern India only

Answer: (B)
Solution: The Nagara style, characterised by a curvilinear shikhara, developed in North India. The Dravida style (pyramidal vimana) is the counterpart in South India.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

70. Which of the following rulers adopted the title ‘Gangaikonda’?
(A) Rajendra Chola I
(B) Rajaraja Chola I
(C) Pulakeshin II
(D) Amoghavarsha

Answer: (A)
Solution: Rajendra Chola I assumed the title ‘Gangaikonda Chola’ (the Chola who conquered the Ganga) after his successful north Indian campaign.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

71. The ‘Brihadratha’, the last Maurya ruler, was assassinated by:
(A) Pushyamitra Shunga
(B) Vasudeva Kanva
(C) Simuka
(D) Kharavela

Answer: (A)
Solution: Pushyamitra Shunga, the commander-in-chief, killed Brihadratha and founded the Shunga dynasty. Vasudeva Kanva later overthrew the Shungas.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2015
⚠️ Trap Type: Chronological Confusion – mixing the sequence of dynasties: Maurya → Shunga → Kanva.

72. The ‘Dhamma Mahamatras’ appointed by Ashoka were:
(A) Officers in charge of military expeditions
(B) Special officers to propagate Dhamma and look after the welfare of the people
(C) Provincial governors
(D) Tax collectors

Answer: (B)
Solution: Major Rock Edict V records the institution of Dhamma Mahamatras, a new cadre of officers created by Ashoka to spread ethical teachings and attend to the welfare of all sections of society.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

73. The ‘Udayagiri and Khandagiri caves’ are associated with which religious sect?
(A) Buddhist
(B) Ajivika
(C) Jain
(D) Hindu

Answer: (C)
Solution: The twin hills of Udayagiri and Khandagiri in Bhubaneswar (Odisha) were excavated during the reign of Kharavela and are famous for their Jain rock-cut shelters.
Source: Railway NTPC 2020

74. With reference to ancient Indian astronomy, the work ‘Panchasiddhantika’ was composed by:
(A) Aryabhata
(B) Varahamihira
(C) Brahmagupta
(D) Bhaskara I

Answer: (B)
Solution: Varahamihira (6th century CE), in his Panchasiddhantika, summarises five earlier astronomical treatises. Aryabhata authored Aryabhatiya, Brahmagupta wrote Brahmasphutasiddhanta, Bhaskara I wrote Mahabhaskariya.
Source: UPSC CSE (Prelims) 2017
⚠️ Trap Type: Similar Name – the word ‘Siddhanta’ appears in the works of many astronomers, leading to confusion.

75. Consider the following statements:

The Satavahanas used Prakrit as their official language.

The earliest Satavahana inscriptions were in Sanskrit.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(A) 1 only
(B) 2 only
(C) Both 1 and 2
(D) Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: (A)
Solution: The Satavahanas patronised Prakrit and used it in their inscriptions. Statement 2 is incorrect; the earliest royal Sanskrit inscription in India is the Junagadh inscription of Rudradaman (Shaka), much later than early Satavahanas.
Source: SSC CGL 2020
⚠️ Trap Type: Chronological Confusion – ascribing early Sanskrit use to the Satavahanas instead of the Shakas.
