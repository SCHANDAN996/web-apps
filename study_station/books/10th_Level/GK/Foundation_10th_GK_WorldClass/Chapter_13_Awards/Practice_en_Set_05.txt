Set 5/6 – Awards & Honours MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard

101. In which year was the Arjuna Award instituted?
(a) 1951 (b) 1961 (c) 1985 (d) 1991
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Arjuna Award was instituted in 1961. Trap: 1985 is the Dronacharya Award (for coaches). So the answer is 1961.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

102. The Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (MAKA) Trophy was started in:
(a) 1947–48 (b) 1950–51 (c) 1956–57 (d) 1991–92
Answer: (c)
Solution: The MAKA Trophy began in 1956–57. Trap: 1991–92 is when the Khel Ratna began. So the answer is 1956–57.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

103. 'Raja Harishchandra' (1913) was made by:
(a) Satyajit Ray (b) V. Shantaram (c) Dadasaheb Phalke (d) Prithviraj Kapoor
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dhundiraj Govind (Dadasaheb) Phalke, the 'Father of Indian Cinema', made 'Raja Harishchandra' in 1913. India's highest film award is named after him.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

104. Who received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for the year 2018?
(a) Rajinikanth (b) Mithun Chakraborty (c) Mohanlal (d) Amitabh Bachchan
Answer: (d)
Solution: Amitabh Bachchan received the Phalke Award for 2018. Rajinikanth got it for 2019, Mithun Chakraborty for 2022 and Mohanlal for 2023.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

105. Asha Parekh received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for which year?
(a) 2019 (b) 2020 (c) 2021 (d) 2022
Answer: (b)
Solution: Asha Parekh received it for 2020. Trap: Waheeda Rehman, another actress of the same era, received it for 2021. So the answer is 2020.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

106. The Jnanpith Award was established in 1961. In which year was it first presented?
(a) 1961 (b) 1965 (c) 1968 (d) 1976
Answer: (b)
Solution: It was first given in 1965, to G. Sankara Kurup. Trap: 1961 is the year it was established; 1968 is Sumitranandan Pant's year and 1976 Ashapoorna Devi's. So the answer is 1965.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

107. The 58th Jnanpith Award (for 2023) was given jointly to:
(a) Jagadguru Rambhadracharya and Vinod Kumar Shukla (b) Gulzar and Jagadguru Rambhadracharya (c) Sumitranandan Pant and Gulzar (d) Vinod Kumar Shukla and Gulzar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 58th Jnanpith went to Gulzar (Urdu) and Jagadguru Rambhadracharya (Sanskrit). Trap: Vinod Kumar Shukla received the 59th (for 2024).
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

108. Jagadguru Rambhadracharya received the 58th Jnanpith Award for his writing in which language?
(a) Urdu (b) Sanskrit (c) Pali (d) Hindi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rambhadracharya was honoured for Sanskrit and Gulzar for Urdu. Trap: Hindi belongs to Vinod Kumar Shukla, the 59th winner.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

109. G. Sankara Kurup, the first Jnanpith winner, wrote in which language?
(a) Telugu (b) Kannada (c) Tamil (d) Malayalam
Answer: (d)
Solution: G. Sankara Kurup wrote in Malayalam; he won the first Jnanpith (1965) for 'Odakkuzhal' (The Bamboo Flute).
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

110. The Sahitya Akademi (National Academy of Letters) was set up in:
(a) 1947 (b) 1954 (c) 1961 (d) 1969
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Sahitya Akademi was set up on 12 March 1954 and first gave its award in 1955. Trap: 1961 is the year the Jnanpith Award was established. So the answer is 1954.
Source: Sahitya Akademi

111. Besides the 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule, the Sahitya Akademi Award is also given for books in:
(a) English and Rajasthani (b) English and Tulu (c) Rajasthani and Bhojpuri (d) English and Bhojpuri
Answer: (a)
Solution: The award covers 24 languages: the 22 Eighth Schedule languages plus English and Rajasthani.
Source: Sahitya Akademi

112. Who was the first recipient of the Saraswati Samman (1991)?
(a) Sumitranandan Pant (b) Gulzar (c) Harivansh Rai Bachchan (d) Mahadevi Verma
Answer: (c)
Solution: Harivansh Rai Bachchan received the first Saraswati Samman for his four-part autobiography. Trap: Sumitranandan Pant was the first Hindi winner of the Jnanpith, a different award.
Source: K. K. Birla Foundation

113. The Vyas Samman for Hindi literature is given by:
(a) Sahitya Akademi (b) Ministry of Education (c) Bharatiya Jnanpith (d) K. K. Birla Foundation
Answer: (d)
Solution: The K. K. Birla Foundation gives both the Saraswati Samman and the Vyas Samman (Hindi literature).
Source: K. K. Birla Foundation

114. The Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for young scientists was given (from 1958) by:
(a) ICMR (b) CSIR (c) DRDO (d) ISRO
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize was given by CSIR from 1958. From 2024 it became the 'Vigyan Yuva–Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar' category of the Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar.
Source: Ministry of Science & Technology — Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar

115. Who received the first Vigyan Ratna (2024) under the Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar?
(a) C. N. R. Rao (b) Venkatraman Ramakrishnan (c) Prof. G. Padmanabhan (d) Dr. M. S. Swaminathan
Answer: (c)
Solution: Biochemist Prof. G. Padmanabhan received the first Vigyan Ratna in 2024. Trap: C. N. R. Rao is a scientist with the Bharat Ratna (2014), a different award.
Source: Ministry of Science & Technology — Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar

116. Which of the following is NOT a category of the Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar?
(a) Vigyan Bhushan (b) Vigyan Team (c) Vigyan Shri (d) Vigyan Ratna
Answer: (a)
Solution: The four categories are Vigyan Ratna, Vigyan Shri, Vigyan Yuva–Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar and Vigyan Team. Trap: 'Vigyan Bhushan' sounds right because of the Padma Bhushan, but it does not exist.
Source: Ministry of Science & Technology — Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar

117. María Corina Machado, winner of the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize, is from which country?
(a) Chile (b) Brazil (c) Colombia (d) Venezuela
Answer: (d)
Solution: María Corina Machado of Venezuela won the Nobel Peace Prize in 2025.
Source: nobelprize.org

118. László Krasznahorkai, winner of the 2025 Nobel Prize in Literature, is from:
(a) Poland (b) Austria (c) Romania (d) Hungary
Answer: (d)
Solution: László Krasznahorkai of Hungary won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 2025.
Source: nobelprize.org

119. The 2025 Nobel Prize in Chemistry (Susumu Kitagawa, Richard Robson, Omar Yaghi) was for work on:
(a) The Raman Effect (b) Metal–organic frameworks (c) Peripheral immune tolerance (d) The structure of the ribosome
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 2025 Chemistry prize was for metal–organic frameworks. Trap: peripheral immune tolerance was the 2025 Physiology or Medicine prize.
Source: nobelprize.org

120. For which film did A. R. Rahman win two Oscars in 2009?
(a) RRR (b) Gandhi (c) Lagaan (d) Slumdog Millionaire
Answer: (d)
Solution: A. R. Rahman won two Oscars for 'Slumdog Millionaire'; Resul Pookutty and Gulzar also won for the same film. Trap: 'Gandhi' brought Bhanu Athaiya her Oscar in 1983, and RRR's 'Naatu Naatu' won in 2023.
Source: Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

121. Arrange the following Bharat Ratna recipients in the order in which they received the award:
I. Indira Gandhi
II. Lal Bahadur Shastri
III. Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan
IV. Mother Teresa
(a) II, I, IV, III (b) I, II, IV, III (c) II, I, III, IV (d) I, II, III, IV
Answer: (a)
Solution: Shastri (1966) → Indira Gandhi (1971) → Mother Teresa (1980) → Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987). Trap: Indira Gandhi is the 'first woman', but Shastri's posthumous award came five years earlier.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

122. Consider the following statements about the Padma awards:
I. They were instituted in 1954 along with the Bharat Ratna.
II. Their present names were adopted on 8 January 1955.
III. Doctors in government service are not eligible for them.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I only (b) I and II only (c) II and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (b)
Solution: I and II are correct. III is wrong: government servants are not eligible, but doctors and scientists are the exception. Trap: III looks like the general rule, but it hides the exception.
Source: padmaawards.gov.in (Ministry of Home Affairs)

123. Which of the following pairs (award – given by / country) is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) Ramon Magsaysay Award – Thailand (b) Jnanpith Award – Bharatiya Jnanpith (c) Saraswati Samman – K. K. Birla Foundation (d) Kalinga Prize – UNESCO
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ramon Magsaysay Award is given in the Philippines, not Thailand. The other three pairs are correct.
Source: PYQ-style

124. Arrange the following Nobel laureates in the order in which they won the prize:
I. Venkatraman Ramakrishnan
II. Har Gobind Khorana
III. Abhijit Banerjee
IV. S. Chandrasekhar
(a) II, I, IV, III (b) IV, II, I, III (c) II, IV, I, III (d) II, IV, III, I
Answer: (c)
Solution: Khorana (1968) → Chandrasekhar (1983) → Ramakrishnan (2009) → Banerjee (2019). Trap: Ramakrishnan (2009) and Banerjee (2019) are both recent and easy to swap.
Source: nobelprize.org

125. Consider the following statements about the Param Vir Chakra:
I. It has been awarded 21 times, 14 of them posthumously.
II. It has not been awarded after 1999.
III. Its first recipient was Captain Vikram Batra.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I and II only (b) II and III only (c) I and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (a)
Solution: I and II are correct. III is wrong: the first recipient was Major Somnath Sharma (1947); Vikram Batra received it for the Kargil War (1999).
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in
