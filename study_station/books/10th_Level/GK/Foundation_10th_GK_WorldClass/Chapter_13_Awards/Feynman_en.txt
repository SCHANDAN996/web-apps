🧒 Feynman Corner: "Instituted", "renamed" and "first given" — why one award can have three different years

This is the fact that confuses students most in this chapter. A question asks, "When was the Khel Ratna started?" and the options show 1991, 2021 and 2024. All three years are real and connected to the Khel Ratna. Only one answers the question. Let's make it simple.

The daily-life example: a school prize

Imagine your school decides in June to start a new prize called "Best Reader". That is the year it is instituted — the day the idea becomes a rule.

But the first prize is given only at the annual function in December — that is when it is first given. Sometimes there is a long gap. The Jnanpith Award was set up in 1961, but the first author received it only in 1965.

Years later, the school renames the prize after a famous old teacher: "Sharma Sir Reading Award". The prize is the same — same trophy, same purpose — only the name changes. That is the year of renaming. The Khel Ratna started in 1991–92 and was renamed after Major Dhyan Chand in 2021. The Padma awards started in 1954 and got their present names in 1955. The Ashoka Chakra series started in 1952 and got the names Ashoka, Kirti and Shaurya in 1967.

And every year new students win it — that is the award year. Khel Ratna 2024 went to Manu Bhaker, D. Gukesh, Harmanpreet Singh and Praveen Kumar, but it was presented in January 2025. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award "for 2023" was given to Mohanlal in September 2025. The award year and the ceremony year are often different!

The unforgettable trick

"Born, Baptised, Birthday."
Born = instituted (the rule is made).
Baptised = renamed (a new name for the same award).
Birthday = each year's award (the year it is for — not always the year of the ceremony).

When you see a year in a question, ask: is it asking when the award was born, when it was baptised, or whose birthday it is?

Blurting Sheet

Now, without looking up, write down everything you remember about this concept: the three kinds of years, and for each of these awards write the year it was born and (if any) the year it was renamed — Bharat Ratna, Padma awards, Param Vir Chakra, Ashoka Chakra series, Khel Ratna, Jnanpith, Dadasaheb Phalke. Then write two awards where the award year and the ceremony year are different.

Then check: Did you write 1952 and 1967 for the Ashoka Chakra series? Did you write 1961 and 1965 for the Jnanpith?
