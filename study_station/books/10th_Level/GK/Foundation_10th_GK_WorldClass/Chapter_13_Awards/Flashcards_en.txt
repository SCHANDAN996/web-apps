🃏 Flashcard Deck: Awards & Honours (Foundation)

Card 1
Front: A statesman, a philosopher and a physicist were the first to receive India's highest civilian honour. Name the award, the year and the three people.
Back: Bharat Ratna, 1954 — C. Rajagopalachari, Dr. S. Radhakrishnan and Dr. C. V. Raman.

Card 2
Front: The Bharat Ratna carries no money. What exactly does the recipient get?
Back: A Sanad (certificate) signed by the President and a peepal-leaf-shaped medallion.

Card 3
Front: Before 1966 nobody could receive the Bharat Ratna after death. Who was the first posthumous recipient?
Back: Lal Bahadur Shastri, in 1966.

Card 4
Front: The rule says "maximum three a year". In which year did five people receive the Bharat Ratna, and who were they?
Back: 2024 — Karpoori Thakur, L. K. Advani, Chaudhary Charan Singh, P. V. Narasimha Rao and M. S. Swaminathan.

Card 5
Front: Mnemonic check: what does "RRR" stand for in this chapter?
Back: The first three Bharat Ratna recipients (1954) — Rajagopalachari, Radhakrishnan, Raman.

Card 6
Front: Padma Vibhushan, Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan — put them in order, highest first.
Back: Padma Vibhushan > Padma Bhushan > Padma Shri (Mnemonic "V-B-S — Very Big Star").

Card 7
Front: Mnemonic check: what are "P-M-V" and "A-K-S"?
Back: War-time gallantry: Param Vir, Maha Vir, Vir Chakra. Peace-time gallantry: Ashoka, Kirti, Shaurya Chakra.

Card 8
Front: Who designed the Param Vir Chakra, and whose weapon appears on it?
Back: Savitri Khanolkar; four replicas of Indra's Vajra (made from sage Dadhichi's bones).

Card 9
Front: Who was the first recipient of the Param Vir Chakra?
Back: Major Somnath Sharma of the Kumaon Regiment (posthumous) — battle of Badgam, Kashmir, 3 November 1947.

Card 10
Front: India's highest sports award changed its name in 2021. What was it called before, and when did it start?
Back: Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna, started in 1991–92 (first recipient Viswanathan Anand); renamed Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna on 6 August 2021.

Card 11
Front: In the Mahabharata, one was the student and one the teacher. Which sports award goes to coaches?
Back: The Dronacharya Award (1985). The Arjuna Award (1961) goes to players.

Card 12
Front: Who was the first person to receive the Dadasaheb Phalke Award, and in which year?
Back: Devika Rani, in 1969 — the birth centenary year of Dadasaheb Phalke.

Card 13
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Jnanpith Award is given by the Government of India and was first given in 1961." What is wrong?
Back: Both parts. It is given by the Bharatiya Jnanpith (a private trust); it was established in 1961 but first given in 1965 (Almost True Statement + Chronological Confusion).

Card 14
Front: Which award did Harivansh Rai Bachchan receive first of all, for his autobiography?
Back: The first Saraswati Samman (1991), given by the K. K. Birla Foundation.

Card 15
Front: Where is the Nobel Peace Prize presented, and where are the others presented?
Back: Peace — Oslo (Norway); all others — Stockholm (Sweden), on 10 December.

Card 16
Front: Name the first Indian Nobel laureate and the first Indian to win the Nobel in science.
Back: Rabindranath Tagore (Literature, 1913); C. V. Raman (Physics, 1930).

Card 17
Front: Which award is called "Asia's Nobel Prize", and who was the first Indian to win it?
Back: The Ramon Magsaysay Award (Philippines, 1957); Vinoba Bhave, 1958.

Card 18
Front: Examiner's trap: "Geetanjali Shree won the Booker Prize in 2022." True or false?
Back: False. She won the International Booker Prize (for translated fiction) with "Tomb of Sand" (Similar Name).

Card 19
Front: Who was the first Indian to win an Oscar, and for what?
Back: Bhanu Athaiya, in 1983, for costume design of the film "Gandhi".

Card 20
Front: Which Indian scientist was the first winner of the World Food Prize, started by Norman Borlaug?
Back: M. S. Swaminathan, in 1987.
