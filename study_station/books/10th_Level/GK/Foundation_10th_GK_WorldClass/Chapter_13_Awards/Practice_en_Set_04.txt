Set 4/6 – Awards & Honours MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Fields Medal is awarded in the field of:
(a) Physics (b) Chemistry (c) Economics (d) Mathematics
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Fields Medal is a mathematics award given every four years to mathematicians under 40. Trap: there is no Nobel Prize for mathematics, so this award is often confused with Nobel fields.
Source: PYQ-style

77. The Fields Medal is given to mathematicians below what age?
(a) 35 years (b) 40 years (c) 45 years (d) 50 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Fields Medal goes to mathematicians under 40 and is given every four years. So the answer is 40 years.
Source: PYQ-style

78. In which year did Manjul Bhargava, a mathematician of Indian origin, win the Fields Medal?
(a) 2010 (b) 2014 (c) 2018 (d) 2022
Answer: (b)
Solution: Manjul Bhargava won the Fields Medal in 2014 — the same year Kailash Satyarthi won the Nobel Peace Prize. So the answer is 2014.
Source: PYQ-style

79. The Abel Prize for mathematics is given by which country?
(a) Denmark (b) Norway (c) Sweden (d) Finland
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Abel Prize is a Norwegian award for mathematics. Trap: Sweden gives most Nobel Prizes, so it looks right, but the Abel Prize is Norway's.
Source: PYQ-style

80. The Grammy Award is associated with:
(a) Music (b) Cinema (c) Architecture (d) Journalism
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Grammy is a music award given by the Recording Academy of the USA. Trap: the Oscar is the film award.
Source: PYQ-style

81. The Bharat Ratna medallion is shaped like:
(a) A peepal leaf (b) A five-pointed star (c) The Ashoka Chakra (d) A lotus
Answer: (a)
Solution: The medallion is shaped like a peepal leaf, with a sun and the words 'Bharat Ratna' in Devanagari on the front, and the State Emblem with 'Satyameva Jayate' on the back.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

82. Which of the following statements about the Bharat Ratna is correct?
(a) The Prime Minister recommends names directly to the President (b) It carries a cash prize of ₹1 crore (c) It can be given only to Indian citizens (d) Up to five awards are allowed every year under the rules
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Prime Minister recommends names to the President; no formal nomination is needed. The award has no cash, has gone to non-citizens (Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan, Nelson Mandela), and the rule limit is three a year — five in 2024 was an exception.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

83. During which periods was the Bharat Ratna suspended?
(a) 1977–1980 and 1992–1995 (b) 1971–1974 and 1990–1993 (c) 1975–1977 and 1999–2001 (d) 1980–1983 and 1995–1997
Answer: (a)
Solution: The award was suspended from 1977 to 1980 and from 1992 to 1995 (because of court cases). So the answer is 1977–1980 and 1992–1995.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

84. In which case did the Supreme Court (1995) hold that the Bharat Ratna and Padma awards are not 'titles' barred by Article 18?
(a) Balaji Raghavan case (b) Kesavananda Bharati case (c) Minerva Mills case (d) Golaknath case
Answer: (a)
Solution: In the Balaji Raghavan case (1995) the Supreme Court upheld the awards but said they must not be used as a prefix or suffix to a name. The other cases are about amending the Constitution.
Source: Constitution of India, Article 18; Supreme Court, Balaji Raghavan case (1995)

85. Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the abolition of titles?
(a) Article 21 (b) Article 17 (c) Article 18 (d) Article 14
Answer: (c)
Solution: Article 18 abolishes titles. Trap: Article 17 abolishes untouchability — the numbers are next to each other, so the answer is Article 18.
Source: Constitution of India, Article 18; Supreme Court, Balaji Raghavan case (1995)

86. The Padma awards are announced every year on the eve of:
(a) Independence Day (b) National Unity Day (c) Gandhi Jayanti (d) Republic Day
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Padma awards are announced on 25 January, the eve of Republic Day, and presented by the President at Rashtrapati Bhavan.
Source: padmaawards.gov.in (Ministry of Home Affairs)

87. What is the maximum number of Padma awards in a year (excluding posthumous awards and those to foreigners, NRIs and OCIs)?
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 150 (d) 200
Answer: (b)
Solution: The total should not exceed 120 in a year; posthumous awards and awards to foreigners, NRIs and OCIs are not counted. So the answer is 120.
Source: padmaawards.gov.in (Ministry of Home Affairs)

88. Government servants are not eligible for Padma awards. Who are the exceptions?
(a) Army officers and police officers (b) Judges and diplomats (c) Doctors and scientists (d) Teachers and professors
Answer: (c)
Solution: Government servants, including PSU employees, are not eligible — except doctors and scientists.
Source: padmaawards.gov.in (Ministry of Home Affairs)

89. In which year did Mother Teresa receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) 1971 (b) 1979 (c) 1980 (d) 1987
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mother Teresa received the Bharat Ratna in 1980. Trap: 1979 is the year of her Nobel Peace Prize. So the answer is 1980.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

90. In which year was Nelson Mandela of South Africa awarded the Bharat Ratna?
(a) 1987 (b) 1990 (c) 1995 (d) 2000
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nelson Mandela received the Bharat Ratna in 1990. Trap: 1987 is the year of Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan, the first non-Indian citizen to receive it. So the answer is 1990.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

91. Which founder of Banaras Hindu University received the Bharat Ratna in 2015?
(a) Rabindranath Tagore (b) Madan Mohan Malaviya (c) Annie Besant (d) Syed Ahmad Khan
Answer: (b)
Solution: Madan Mohan Malaviya, founder of BHU, received the Bharat Ratna in 2015 along with Atal Bihari Vajpayee.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

92. Which of the following received the Bharat Ratna in 2019?
(a) Karpoori Thakur (b) Bhupen Hazarika (c) C. N. R. Rao (d) Atal Bihari Vajpayee
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 2019 recipients were Pranab Mukherjee, Nanaji Deshmukh and Bhupen Hazarika. Trap: Karpoori Thakur got it in 2024, Vajpayee in 2015 and C. N. R. Rao in 2014.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

93. Which scientist received the Bharat Ratna in 2014, the same year as Sachin Tendulkar?
(a) Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (b) C. N. R. Rao (c) Dr. M. S. Swaminathan (d) Dr. C. V. Raman
Answer: (b)
Solution: Scientist C. N. R. Rao received the Bharat Ratna in 2014 along with Sachin Tendulkar. Trap: Abdul Kalam got it in 1997, C. V. Raman in 1954 and M. S. Swaminathan in 2024.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

94. Which of the following received the Param Vir Chakra for the Kargil War (1999)?
(a) Major Somnath Sharma (b) Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon (c) Savitri Khanolkar (d) Captain Vikram Batra
Answer: (d)
Solution: Captain Vikram Batra received the Param Vir Chakra (posthumously) for Kargil. Trap: Somnath Sharma was honoured for 1947, Sekhon for the 1971 war, and Savitri Khanolkar designed the medal.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

95. The Param Vir Chakra medal carries four replicas of:
(a) Indra's Vajra (b) The Sudarshan Chakra (c) Lord Shiva's trident (d) The lotus
Answer: (a)
Solution: It shows four replicas of Indra's Vajra (the weapon made from the bones of sage Dadhichi) around the State Emblem.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

96. The Param Vir Chakra hangs from a ribbon of which colour?
(a) Red (b) Purple (c) Green (d) Blue
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Param Vir Chakra hangs from a purple ribbon.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

97. Major Somnath Sharma, the first Param Vir Chakra recipient, belonged to which regiment?
(a) Rajputana Rifles (b) Sikh Regiment (c) Kumaon Regiment (d) Gorkha Rifles
Answer: (c)
Solution: Major Somnath Sharma was of the Kumaon Regiment; he was honoured posthumously for the battle at Badgam on 3 November 1947.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

98. Which chess player received the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna for 2024 (presented on 17 January 2025)?
(a) R. Praggnanandhaa (b) Viswanathan Anand (c) Koneru Humpy (d) D. Gukesh
Answer: (d)
Solution: D. Gukesh was one of the four Khel Ratna awardees for 2024. Trap: Viswanathan Anand was the first Khel Ratna winner, back in 1991–92.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

99. Praveen Kumar, one of the Khel Ratna awardees for 2024, competes in:
(a) Para archery (b) Para badminton (c) Para shooting (d) Para high jump
Answer: (d)
Solution: Praveen Kumar is a para high jumper. The other Khel Ratna awardees for 2024 were Manu Bhaker (shooting), D. Gukesh (chess) and Harmanpreet Singh (hockey).
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

100. In which year was the Dronacharya Award instituted?
(a) 1961 (b) 1985 (c) 1991 (d) 1956
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Dronacharya Award was instituted in 1985. Trap: 1961 is the Arjuna Award, 1991–92 the Khel Ratna and 1956–57 the MAKA Trophy. So the answer is 1985.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards
