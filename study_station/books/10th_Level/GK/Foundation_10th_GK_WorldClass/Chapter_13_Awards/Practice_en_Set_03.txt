Set 3/6 – Awards & Honours MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 51–75: Medium

51. In which year did S. Chandrasekhar win the Nobel Prize in Physics?
(a) 1930 (b) 1968 (c) 1983 (d) 1998
Answer: (c)
Solution: S. Chandrasekhar won the Physics Nobel in 1983. Trap: 1930 is C. V. Raman's Physics prize — both are Physics laureates. So the answer is 1983.
Source: nobelprize.org

52. Venkatraman Ramakrishnan won the Nobel Prize in 2009 in which field?
(a) Economics (b) Chemistry (c) Physiology or Medicine (d) Physics
Answer: (b)
Solution: Venkatraman Ramakrishnan won the 2009 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
Source: nobelprize.org

53. Kailash Satyarthi shared the 2014 Nobel Peace Prize with:
(a) Esther Duflo (b) María Corina Machado (c) Malala Yousafzai (d) Aung San Suu Kyi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kailash Satyarthi shared the 2014 Peace Prize with Malala Yousafzai of Pakistan. Trap: Esther Duflo shared the 2019 Economics prize with Abhijit Banerjee; María Corina Machado won the Peace Prize in 2025.
Source: nobelprize.org

54. Abhijit Banerjee shared the 2019 Nobel Prize in Economics with:
(a) Joel Mokyr and Peter Howitt (b) Esther Duflo and Michael Kremer (c) Amartya Sen and Esther Duflo (d) Malala Yousafzai and Michael Kremer
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 2019 Economics prize went to Abhijit Banerjee, Esther Duflo and Michael Kremer. Trap: Joel Mokyr and Peter Howitt won in 2025; Amartya Sen won alone in 1998.
Source: nobelprize.org

55. Ronald Ross, who was born in India, won the 1902 Nobel Prize in Medicine for his work on:
(a) Tuberculosis (b) Plague (c) Malaria (d) Cholera
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ronald Ross won the 1902 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for his work on malaria.
Source: nobelprize.org

56. Rudyard Kipling, who was born in India, won the Nobel Prize in 1907 in:
(a) Physiology or Medicine (b) Peace (c) Physics (d) Literature
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rudyard Kipling won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1907. Trap: Ronald Ross, also born in India, won in Medicine (1902).
Source: nobelprize.org

57. Nobel Prizes are presented every year on 10 December because it is:
(a) The date of Alfred Nobel's will (b) Alfred Nobel's death anniversary (c) Alfred Nobel's birth anniversary (d) The date the first prizes were announced
Answer: (b)
Solution: 10 December is the death anniversary of Alfred Nobel, and the prizes are presented on that day.
Source: nobelprize.org

58. For which work did Rabindranath Tagore receive the Nobel Prize in Literature (1913)?
(a) Chokher Bali (b) Gora (c) Gitanjali (d) Kabuliwala
Answer: (c)
Solution: Tagore received the 1913 Nobel Prize in Literature for 'Gitanjali'. The other three are also his works, but the prize is linked with Gitanjali.
Source: nobelprize.org

59. Who was the first Indian to receive the Ramon Magsaysay Award?
(a) Jayaprakash Narayan (b) Vinoba Bhave (c) Mother Teresa (d) Kiran Bedi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vinoba Bhave received it in 1958 for the Bhoodan movement — the first Indian winner. Trap: Mother Teresa received it later, in 1962.
Source: Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation

60. In which year was the Ramon Magsaysay Award established?
(a) 1947 (b) 1957 (c) 1962 (d) 1969
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Ramon Magsaysay Award was established in 1957 in the Philippines; its first Indian winner, Vinoba Bhave, got it in 1958. So the answer is 1957.
Source: Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation

61. The Booker Prize, for the best novel written in English, is awarded in which country?
(a) United Kingdom (b) United States (c) France (d) Sweden
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Booker Prize is a United Kingdom award, given since 1969. Trap: the Pulitzer Prize is the American award.
Source: The Booker Prizes (thebookerprizes.com)

62. Arundhati Roy won the Booker Prize in 1997 for:
(a) The God of Small Things (b) The Inheritance of Loss (c) Midnight's Children (d) The White Tiger
Answer: (a)
Solution: Arundhati Roy won the 1997 Booker for 'The God of Small Things'. The others: Salman Rushdie (Midnight's Children, 1981), Aravind Adiga (The White Tiger, 2008), Kiran Desai (The Inheritance of Loss, 2006).
Source: The Booker Prizes (thebookerprizes.com)

63. Aravind Adiga won the Booker Prize in 2008 for:
(a) The God of Small Things (b) The Inheritance of Loss (c) Midnight's Children (d) The White Tiger
Answer: (d)
Solution: Aravind Adiga won the 2008 Booker Prize for 'The White Tiger'. Trap: 'The Inheritance of Loss' won two years earlier (2006) for Kiran Desai.
Source: The Booker Prizes (thebookerprizes.com)

64. In 2022, Geetanjali Shree's 'Tomb of Sand' (Hindi 'Ret Samadhi') won the:
(a) International Booker Prize (b) Jnanpith Award (c) Pulitzer Prize (d) Booker Prize
Answer: (a)
Solution: It won the International Booker Prize, given for fiction translated into English — the first Hindi book to win it. Trap: the Booker Prize is for novels written originally in English.
Source: The Booker Prizes (thebookerprizes.com)

65. Who translated Geetanjali Shree's 'Ret Samadhi' into English as 'Tomb of Sand'?
(a) Kiran Desai (b) Daisy Rockwell (c) Jhumpa Lahiri (d) Deepa Bhasthi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Daisy Rockwell translated 'Ret Samadhi'. Trap: Deepa Bhasthi translated Banu Mushtaq's Kannada 'Heart Lamp', which won the International Booker in 2025.
Source: The Booker Prizes (thebookerprizes.com)

66. Banu Mushtaq's 'Heart Lamp', winner of the International Booker Prize 2025, was originally written in:
(a) Telugu (b) Malayalam (c) Tamil (d) Kannada
Answer: (d)
Solution: 'Heart Lamp' was written in Kannada and translated by Deepa Bhasthi; it was the first short-story collection to win the International Booker.
Source: The Booker Prizes (thebookerprizes.com)

67. Who was the first Indian to win an Oscar?
(a) Bhanu Athaiya (b) Resul Pookutty (c) Satyajit Ray (d) A. R. Rahman
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bhanu Athaiya won in 1983 for costume design in 'Gandhi'. Trap: Satyajit Ray's Honorary Oscar came in 1992; Rahman and Pookutty won in 2009.
Source: Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

68. Bhanu Athaiya won the Oscar in 1983 in which category?
(a) Best Sound Mixing (b) Best Original Score (c) Best Costume Design (d) Best Original Screenplay
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bhanu Athaiya won the Oscar for Best Costume Design for the film 'Gandhi'.
Source: Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

69. In 2023, the song 'Naatu Naatu' from the film RRR won the Oscar for:
(a) Best Documentary Short (b) Best Visual Effects (c) Best Original Score (d) Best Original Song
Answer: (d)
Solution: 'Naatu Naatu' won Best Original Song (music M. M. Keeravani, lyrics Chandrabose). Trap: 'The Elephant Whisperers' won Best Documentary Short the same year.
Source: Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

70. 'The Elephant Whisperers' (director Kartiki Gonsalves, producer Guneet Monga) won which Oscar in 2023?
(a) Best Documentary Feature (b) Best Original Song (c) Best International Feature Film (d) Best Documentary Short
Answer: (d)
Solution: It won Best Documentary Short. Trap: 'Documentary Feature' is the category for full-length documentaries, which sounds almost the same.
Source: Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

71. Who was the first winner of the World Food Prize (1987)?
(a) Norman Borlaug (b) M. S. Swaminathan (c) Verghese Kurien (d) C. Subramaniam
Answer: (b)
Solution: India's M. S. Swaminathan was the first World Food Prize laureate in 1987. Trap: Norman Borlaug founded the prize (1986); he was not its first winner.
Source: World Food Prize Foundation

72. The Kalinga Prize for popularising science is given by:
(a) WHO (b) UNEP (c) UNESCO (d) UNICEF
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kalinga Prize is given by UNESCO for popularising science; it was first given in 1952.
Source: UNESCO — Kalinga Prize

73. The Kalinga Prize was set up with a grant from:
(a) J. R. D. Tata (b) Vikram Sarabhai (c) Biju Patnaik (d) Homi J. Bhabha
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kalinga Prize was set up with a grant from Biju Patnaik of Odisha — hence the name 'Kalinga', the old name of the Odisha region.
Source: UNESCO — Kalinga Prize

74. The Pulitzer Prize, for journalism, literature and music, is associated with:
(a) The Nobel Foundation, Sweden (b) Columbia University, USA (c) UNESCO, Paris (d) Oxford University, UK
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Pulitzer Prize is an American award administered by Columbia University.
Source: PYQ-style

75. Jhumpa Lahiri won the Pulitzer Prize for fiction in 2000 for:
(a) Tomb of Sand (b) The Inheritance of Loss (c) The White Tiger (d) Interpreter of Maladies
Answer: (d)
Solution: Jhumpa Lahiri won the 2000 Pulitzer Prize for fiction for 'Interpreter of Maladies'. Trap: the other three are Booker or International Booker winners.
Source: PYQ-style
