Set 2/6 – Awards & Honours MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Who was the first non-Indian citizen to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (b) Mother Teresa (c) Julius Nyerere (d) Nelson Mandela
Answer: (a)
Solution: Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan, the 'Frontier Gandhi', received it in 1987. Trap: Nelson Mandela came next, in 1990; Mother Teresa was an Indian citizen.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

27. Which of the following did NOT receive the Bharat Ratna in 2024?
(a) Dr. M. S. Swaminathan (b) Karpoori Thakur (c) Pranab Mukherjee (d) Chaudhary Charan Singh
Answer: (c)
Solution: The five recipients of 2024 were Karpoori Thakur, L. K. Advani, Chaudhary Charan Singh, P. V. Narasimha Rao and M. S. Swaminathan. Trap: Pranab Mukherjee is also a recent recipient, but of 2019.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

28. How many people received the Bharat Ratna in 2024?
(a) Three (b) Four (c) Two (d) Five
Answer: (d)
Solution: Five people received it in 2024, although the rule allows a maximum of three in a year. Trap: 'three' is the normal limit, and four were given in 1999.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

29. The first Bharat Ratna awards (1954) went to all of the following EXCEPT:
(a) Dr. C. V. Raman (b) Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan (c) Jawaharlal Nehru (d) C. Rajagopalachari
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 1954 trio was Rajagopalachari, Radhakrishnan and C. V. Raman ('RRR'). Jawaharlal Nehru received it a year later, in 1955.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

30. Who designed the Param Vir Chakra?
(a) Nandalal Bose (b) Savitri Khanolkar (c) M. F. Husain (d) Amrita Sher-Gil
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Param Vir Chakra was designed by Savitri Khanolkar. Its design shows four replicas of Indra's Vajra around the State Emblem.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

31. Who was the first recipient of the Param Vir Chakra?
(a) Rifleman Sanjay Kumar (b) Major Somnath Sharma (c) Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon (d) Captain Vikram Batra
Answer: (b)
Solution: Major Somnath Sharma of the Kumaon Regiment received the first Param Vir Chakra, posthumously, for the battle at Badgam, Kashmir, on 3 November 1947. Trap: Vikram Batra and Sanjay Kumar won it in the Kargil War of 1999.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

32. Who is the only Indian Air Force recipient of the Param Vir Chakra?
(a) Captain Vikram Batra (b) Major Somnath Sharma (c) Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon (d) Grenadier Yogendra Singh Yadav
Answer: (c)
Solution: Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon (1971 war) is the only Air Force recipient. The other three were Army soldiers.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

33. The Param Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra and Vir Chakra were instituted on:
(a) 15 August 1947 (b) 26 January 1950 (c) 4 January 1952 (d) 27 January 1967
Answer: (b)
Solution: They were instituted on 26 January 1950, with effect from 15 August 1947. Trap: 15 August 1947 is only the date from which they took effect; 1952 and 1967 belong to the Ashoka Chakra series. So the answer is 26 January 1950.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

34. In which year did the Ashoka Chakra Class I, II and III series get the names Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra and Shaurya Chakra?
(a) 1950 (b) 1952 (c) 1967 (d) 1971
Answer: (c)
Solution: The series was instituted on 4 January 1952 as Ashoka Chakra Class I, II and III and renamed on 27 January 1967. Trap: 1952 is the year of institution, not renaming. So the answer is 1967.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

35. In which year was the Khel Ratna renamed after Major Dhyan Chand?
(a) 2019 (b) 2020 (c) 2021 (d) 2022
Answer: (c)
Solution: It was renamed the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna on 6 August 2021. Trap: 2021 is only the renaming year; the award itself started in 1991–92. So the answer is 2021.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

36. Who was the first recipient of the Khel Ratna (then the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna)?
(a) Leander Paes (b) Dhanraj Pillay (c) Sachin Tendulkar (d) Viswanathan Anand
Answer: (d)
Solution: Chess player Viswanathan Anand was the first Khel Ratna awardee (1991–92).
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

37. The Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (MAKA) Trophy is given to:
(a) The best state in national games (b) The best university in inter-university sport (c) The best sports journalist (d) The best sports coach
Answer: (b)
Solution: The MAKA Trophy (from 1956–57) goes to the best-performing university in inter-university sport.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

38. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award was instituted in 1969 because that year was:
(a) The birth centenary year of Dadasaheb Phalke (b) The year 'Raja Harishchandra' was released (c) The year the National Film Awards began (d) The year India's first sound film was released
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1969 was the birth centenary year of Dhundiraj Govind (Dadasaheb) Phalke. Trap: 'Raja Harishchandra', the film Phalke made, came in 1913.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

39. Who received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for the year 2023 (presented on 23 September 2025)?
(a) Mohanlal (b) Rajinikanth (c) Mithun Chakraborty (d) Waheeda Rehman
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mohanlal received the Phalke Award for 2023. Trap: Mithun Chakraborty got it for 2022, Waheeda Rehman for 2021 and Rajinikanth for 2019.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

40. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award consists of:
(a) A bronze statue of Vagdevi and a citation (b) A Swarna Kamal (Golden Lotus), a shawl and ₹10 lakh (c) A gold medal and ₹1 crore (d) A peepal-leaf medallion and a Sanad
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Phalke Award carries a Swarna Kamal, a shawl and ₹10 lakh. Trap: the peepal-leaf medallion belongs to the Bharat Ratna and the Vagdevi statue to the Jnanpith Award.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

41. Who was the first Hindi writer to receive the Jnanpith Award?
(a) Harivansh Rai Bachchan (b) Munshi Premchand (c) Sumitranandan Pant (d) Mahadevi Verma
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sumitranandan Pant was the first Hindi writer to win it, in 1968, for 'Chidambara'. Trap: Harivansh Rai Bachchan was the first winner of the Saraswati Samman, a different award.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

42. Who was the first woman to receive the Jnanpith Award?
(a) Mahadevi Verma (b) Amrita Pritam (c) Mahasweta Devi (d) Ashapoorna Devi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ashapoorna Devi (Bengali) was the first woman to receive the Jnanpith, in 1976. The other three won it in later years.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

43. Vinod Kumar Shukla, who received the 59th Jnanpith Award (for 2024), is the first Jnanpith winner from which state?
(a) Jharkhand (b) Odisha (c) Chhattisgarh (d) Madhya Pradesh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hindi writer Vinod Kumar Shukla is the first writer from Chhattisgarh to receive the Jnanpith Award.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

44. The Jnanpith Award includes a bronze statue of:
(a) The Ashoka Lion Capital (b) Nataraja (c) Vagdevi (Saraswati) (d) Lord Buddha
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Jnanpith Award carries a bronze statue of Vagdevi (Saraswati), a citation and cash.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

45. The Sahitya Akademi Award is given for books in how many languages?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 18 (d) 28
Answer: (b)
Solution: It covers 24 languages: the 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule plus English and Rajasthani. Trap: 22 is only the Eighth Schedule count. So the answer is 24.
Source: Sahitya Akademi

46. Who was the first recipient of the Gandhi Peace Prize?
(a) Baba Amte (b) Nelson Mandela (c) Desmond Tutu (d) Julius Nyerere
Answer: (d)
Solution: Julius Nyerere of Tanzania was the first recipient of the Gandhi Peace Prize, instituted by the Government of India in 1995. Trap: Nelson Mandela is the name students guess, but he was not the first.
Source: Ministry of Culture — Gandhi Peace Prize

47. The Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted in 1995 to mark Mahatma Gandhi's:
(a) 100th birth anniversary (b) 125th birth anniversary (c) 50th death anniversary (d) 150th birth anniversary
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gandhi was born on 2 October 1869; his 125th birth anniversary fell on 2 October 1994, and the prize was instituted in that anniversary year, in 1995. The prize carries ₹1 crore. Trap: the 150th birth anniversary came in 2019. So the answer is 125th birth anniversary.
Source: Ministry of Culture — Gandhi Peace Prize

48. The Gandhi Peace Prize for 2021 (announced in June 2023) was given to:
(a) Vivekananda Kendra (b) Gita Press, Gorakhpur (c) Akshaya Patra Foundation (d) Ramakrishna Mission
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Gandhi Peace Prize for 2021 went to Gita Press, Gorakhpur.
Source: Ministry of Culture — Gandhi Peace Prize

49. The Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences was first awarded in:
(a) 1901 (b) 1968 (c) 1969 (d) 1979
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Economics prize was set up in 1968 and first awarded in 1969. Trap: 1968 is the year it was set up; it was not part of Nobel's original five prizes of 1901. So the answer is 1969.
Source: nobelprize.org

50. Har Gobind Khorana won the Nobel Prize in 1968 in which field?
(a) Physiology or Medicine (b) Chemistry (c) Economics (d) Physics
Answer: (a)
Solution: Har Gobind Khorana won the 1968 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. Trap: Venkatraman Ramakrishnan's 2009 prize was in Chemistry; Khorana's was in Physiology or Medicine.
Source: nobelprize.org
