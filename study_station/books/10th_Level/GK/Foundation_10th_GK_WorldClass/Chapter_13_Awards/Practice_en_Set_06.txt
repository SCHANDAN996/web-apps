Set 6/6 – Awards & Honours MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Consider the following statements about the Bharat Ratna:
I. The rule allows a maximum of three awards in a year, but five were given in 2024.
II. The award carries no money.
III. Mahatma Gandhi was its first posthumous recipient.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I only (b) II and III only (c) I and II only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (c)
Solution: I and II are correct. III is wrong: Mahatma Gandhi never received the Bharat Ratna; the first posthumous recipient was Lal Bahadur Shastri (1966).
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

127. Which of the following pairs (Bharat Ratna recipient – year) is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar – 1990 (b) Jawaharlal Nehru – 1955 (c) Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam – 2001 (d) Dr. Rajendra Prasad – 1962
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam received the Bharat Ratna in 1997. Trap: 2001 is the year of Lata Mangeshkar and Ustad Bismillah Khan.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

128. Arrange the peace-time gallantry awards from LOWEST to HIGHEST:
I. Kirti Chakra
II. Ashoka Chakra
III. Shaurya Chakra
(a) II, I, III (b) III, I, II (c) I, III, II (d) III, II, I
Answer: (b)
Solution: From lowest to highest: Shaurya Chakra → Kirti Chakra → Ashoka Chakra. Trap: the question asks for the lowest first — the reverse of the usual 'highest first' order (Ashoka > Kirti > Shaurya).
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

129. Which of the following statements about India's gallantry awards is correct?
(a) The Param Vir Chakra was instituted on 15 August 1947 (b) The Vir Chakra ranks above the Maha Vir Chakra (c) The peace-time series was first instituted in 1952 as Ashoka Chakra Class I, II and III (d) The Kirti Chakra is a war-time gallantry award
Answer: (c)
Solution: The peace-time series began on 4 January 1952 as Ashoka Chakra Class I, II and III. The Kirti Chakra is peace-time; Maha Vir ranks above Vir; and the Param Vir Chakra was instituted on 26 January 1950 (only effective from 15 August 1947).
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

130. Among the five Bharat Ratna recipients of 2024, who received the award in his lifetime (not posthumously)?
(a) Chaudhary Charan Singh (b) Lal Krishna Advani (c) P. V. Narasimha Rao (d) Karpoori Thakur
Answer: (b)
Solution: Of the 2024 recipients, only L. K. Advani received it in his lifetime; Karpoori Thakur, Charan Singh, Narasimha Rao and M. S. Swaminathan received it posthumously.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

131. Which of the following pairs (Booker Prize winner – book) is correctly matched?
(a) Arundhati Roy – Midnight's Children (b) Salman Rushdie – The White Tiger (c) Kiran Desai – The Inheritance of Loss (d) Aravind Adiga – The God of Small Things
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kiran Desai won the 2006 Booker for 'The Inheritance of Loss'. Correct pairs for the others: Rushdie – Midnight's Children (1981), Adiga – The White Tiger (2008), Roy – The God of Small Things (1997).
Source: The Booker Prizes (thebookerprizes.com)

132. Consider the following statements:
I. Geetanjali Shree's 'Tomb of Sand' was the first Hindi book to win the International Booker Prize (2022).
II. Banu Mushtaq's 'Heart Lamp' was the first short-story collection to win the International Booker Prize (2025).
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I only (b) II only (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both are correct. Trap: students who think the prize went to the 'Booker Prize' (English novels) may reject statement I — but both books won the International Booker, which is for translated fiction.
Source: The Booker Prizes (thebookerprizes.com)

133. The music of the Oscar-winning song 'Naatu Naatu' (2023) was composed by:
(a) M. M. Keeravani (b) Chandrabose (c) A. R. Rahman (d) Resul Pookutty
Answer: (a)
Solution: M. M. Keeravani composed the music; Chandrabose wrote the lyrics. Trap: Chandrabose is linked with the same song but as lyricist; Rahman and Pookutty won for 'Slumdog Millionaire' (2009).
Source: Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences

134. In 1992, Satyajit Ray received which two honours?
(a) The Nobel Prize and the Bharat Ratna (b) An Honorary Oscar and the Bharat Ratna (c) An Honorary Oscar and the Ramon Magsaysay Award (d) The Dadasaheb Phalke Award and the Padma Vibhushan
Answer: (b)
Solution: In 1992 Satyajit Ray received an Honorary Oscar and the Bharat Ratna. Trap: Ray never won a Nobel; and Bhanu Athaiya, not Ray, was the first Indian to win a competitive Oscar (1983).
Source: PYQ-style

135. Consider the following statements about the Nobel Prize:
I. It was first awarded in 1901.
II. The prize in Economics was one of the five prizes named in Alfred Nobel's will.
III. All Nobel Prizes except the Peace Prize are presented in Stockholm.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I and II only (b) I and III only (c) II and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (b)
Solution: I and III are correct. II is wrong: the will named Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature and Peace; Economics was set up later (1968) by Sweden's central bank and first given in 1969.
Source: nobelprize.org

136. Which Indian was both the first World Food Prize laureate (1987) and a Bharat Ratna recipient of 2024?
(a) Norman Borlaug (b) Chaudhary Charan Singh (c) Dr. M. S. Swaminathan (d) C. N. R. Rao
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dr. M. S. Swaminathan won the first World Food Prize (1987) and received the Bharat Ratna posthumously in 2024. Trap: Norman Borlaug founded the World Food Prize and is not Indian; Charan Singh also got the Bharat Ratna in 2024 but not the World Food Prize.
Source: World Food Prize Foundation

137. Which of the following pairs (Khel Ratna awardee for 2024 – sport) is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) Manu Bhaker – Shooting (b) D. Gukesh – Chess (c) Praveen Kumar – Para javelin throw (d) Harmanpreet Singh – Hockey
Answer: (c)
Solution: Praveen Kumar is a para high jumper, not a javelin thrower. The other three pairs are correct.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

138. Arrange the following awards in the order in which they were instituted (earliest first):
I. Dronacharya Award
II. Gandhi Peace Prize
III. Arjuna Award
IV. Dadasaheb Phalke Award
(a) III, IV, I, II (b) IV, III, I, II (c) III, I, IV, II (d) I, III, IV, II
Answer: (a)
Solution: Arjuna Award (1961) → Dadasaheb Phalke Award (1969) → Dronacharya Award (1985) → Gandhi Peace Prize (1995). Trap: Arjuna and Dronacharya are paired in memory, but they were started 24 years apart.
Source: PYQ-style

139. Consider the following statements about the Jnanpith Award:
I. It is given by the Ministry of Culture, Government of India.
II. Vinod Kumar Shukla received the 59th Jnanpith Award (for 2024) for Hindi.
III. Its first winner, G. Sankara Kurup, wrote in Malayalam.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I and II only (b) I and III only (c) II and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (c)
Solution: II and III are correct. I is wrong: the Jnanpith is given by the Bharatiya Jnanpith, a private trust, not by the government.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

140. Arrange the following Indian Ramon Magsaysay Award winners in the order in which they received the award:
I. Kiran Bedi
II. Vinoba Bhave
III. Arvind Kejriwal
IV. Mother Teresa
(a) II, IV, I, III (b) IV, II, I, III (c) II, I, IV, III (d) II, IV, III, I
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vinoba Bhave (1958) → Mother Teresa (1962) → Kiran Bedi (1994) → Arvind Kejriwal (2006). Trap: Mother Teresa is a famous early name, but Vinoba Bhave was the first Indian winner.
Source: Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation

141. Which of the following awards is given by the Government of India?
(a) World Food Prize (b) Gandhi Peace Prize (c) Kalinga Prize (d) Ramon Magsaysay Award
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted by the Government of India in 1995, though it can go to people and bodies from any country (its first winner was Julius Nyerere of Tanzania). The Magsaysay is from the Philippines, the Kalinga Prize from UNESCO and the World Food Prize from the foundation set up by Norman Borlaug.
Source: Ministry of Culture — Gandhi Peace Prize

142. Consider the following statements about the Dadasaheb Phalke Award:
I. It is given by the Ministry of Information & Broadcasting at the National Film Awards.
II. Its first recipient was Devika Rani.
III. It is named after the maker of 'Raja Harishchandra' (1913).
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I and II only (b) II and III only (c) I and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (d)
Solution: All three are correct: the Ministry of I&B gives it at the National Film Awards, Devika Rani was the first recipient (1969), and Dadasaheb Phalke made 'Raja Harishchandra' in 1913.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

143. Which of the following India-born persons won a Nobel Prize BEFORE Rabindranath Tagore (1913)?
(a) C. V. Raman (b) Har Gobind Khorana (c) Ronald Ross (d) S. Chandrasekhar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ronald Ross, born in India, won the Medicine prize in 1902 for his work on malaria (Rudyard Kipling, also India-born, won Literature in 1907). Trap: Tagore is the first Indian laureate, but not the first India-born one; Raman (1930), Khorana (1968) and Chandrasekhar (1983) all came after 1913.
Source: nobelprize.org

144. Consider the following statements:
I. Sachin Tendulkar was the first sportsperson to receive the Bharat Ratna.
II. He received it in 2014, the same year as C. N. R. Rao.
III. Major Dhyan Chand received the Bharat Ratna before Sachin Tendulkar.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I only (b) I and II only (c) II and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (b)
Solution: I and II are correct. III is wrong: since Sachin was the first sportsperson to receive the Bharat Ratna, no sportsperson — Dhyan Chand included — got it before him. Dhyan Chand's name was given to the Khel Ratna, India's highest sports award, in 2021.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

145. Which pair of Kargil War (1999) Param Vir Chakra awardees were BOTH honoured posthumously?
(a) Captain Vikram Batra and Lt. Manoj Kumar Pandey (b) Grenadier Yogendra Singh Yadav and Rifleman Sanjay Kumar (c) Captain Vikram Batra and Rifleman Sanjay Kumar (d) Lt. Manoj Kumar Pandey and Grenadier Yogendra Singh Yadav
Answer: (a)
Solution: Captain Vikram Batra and Lt. Manoj Kumar Pandey were honoured posthumously; Grenadier Yogendra Singh Yadav and Rifleman Sanjay Kumar received it in their lifetime.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

146. The 2025 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (Mary Brunkow, Fred Ramsdell, Shimon Sakaguchi) was for discoveries concerning:

147. Which of the following was NOT among the winners of the 2025 Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences?

148. Arrange the following honours in descending order of precedence (highest first):
I. Padma Shri
