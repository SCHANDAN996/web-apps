Set 1/6 – Awards & Honours MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which is the highest civilian award of India?
(a) Padma Shri (b) Param Vir Chakra (c) Bharat Ratna (d) Padma Vibhushan
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Bharat Ratna is India's highest civilian award. Trap: the Param Vir Chakra is the highest gallantry (war-time) award, not a civilian one; Padma Vibhushan is the second-highest civilian award.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

2. In which year was the Bharat Ratna instituted?
(a) 1950 (b) 1952 (c) 1954 (d) 1955
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Bharat Ratna was instituted on 2 January 1954. Trap: 1955 is the year the Padma awards got their present names, not the year the Bharat Ratna began. So the answer is 1954.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

3. The Dronacharya Award is given to:
(a) Sports coaches (b) Outstanding sportspersons (c) Sports journalists (d) Universities that do best in sports
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Dronacharya Award (1985) honours coaches who produce medal winners — Dronacharya was the teacher in the Mahabharata. The Arjuna Award goes to players and the MAKA Trophy to universities.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

4. The Arjuna Award is given for:
(a) Umpiring in sports (b) Lifetime work in sports journalism (c) Consistent outstanding performance by sportspersons (d) Coaching sportspersons
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Arjuna Award (1961) goes to players for consistent outstanding performance. Trap: coaches get the Dronacharya Award — Arjuna was the student, Dronacharya the teacher.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

5. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is given in the field of:
(a) Classical music (b) Literature (c) Cinema (d) Journalism
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is India's highest award in cinema, named after the 'Father of Indian Cinema', Dadasaheb Phalke.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

6. The Jnanpith Award is given for outstanding contribution to:
(a) Science (b) Social service (c) Sports (d) Literature
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Jnanpith Award is India's highest literary award, given by the Bharatiya Jnanpith.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

7. The Ramon Magsaysay Award is given by which country?
(a) Malaysia (b) Philippines (c) Thailand (d) Indonesia
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Ramon Magsaysay Award, set up in 1957, is given in the Philippines and is often called 'Asia's Nobel Prize'. Trap: all four options are South-East Asian countries.
Source: Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation

8. Who was the first Indian to win a Nobel Prize?
(a) Mother Teresa (b) Har Gobind Khorana (c) C. V. Raman (d) Rabindranath Tagore
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rabindranath Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913 for 'Gitanjali' — the first Indian and first Asian laureate. Trap: C. V. Raman came later, in 1930 (Physics).
Source: nobelprize.org

9. The Nobel Peace Prize is presented in which city?
(a) Copenhagen (b) Oslo (c) Geneva (d) Stockholm
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Peace Prize is presented in Oslo (Norway); all other Nobel Prizes are presented in Stockholm (Sweden). Trap: Stockholm is right for every prize except Peace.
Source: nobelprize.org

10. In which field did C. V. Raman win the Nobel Prize (1930)?
(a) Literature (b) Physics (c) Chemistry (d) Physiology or Medicine
Answer: (b)
Solution: C. V. Raman won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930 for the Raman Effect.
Source: nobelprize.org

11. Which is India's highest war-time gallantry award?
(a) Ashoka Chakra (b) Vir Chakra (c) Param Vir Chakra (d) Kirti Chakra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The war-time ladder is Param Vir Chakra > Maha Vir Chakra > Vir Chakra. Trap: the Ashoka Chakra is the highest peace-time gallantry award.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

12. Which is India's highest peace-time gallantry award?
(a) Shaurya Chakra (b) Param Vir Chakra (c) Ashoka Chakra (d) Maha Vir Chakra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The peace-time ladder is Ashoka Chakra > Kirti Chakra > Shaurya Chakra. Param Vir and Maha Vir Chakra are war-time awards.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

13. Who was the first woman to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Lata Mangeshkar (b) Sarojini Naidu (c) Mother Teresa (d) Indira Gandhi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Indira Gandhi received the Bharat Ratna in 1971 — the first woman to get it. Trap: Mother Teresa received it later, in 1980, and Lata Mangeshkar in 2001.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

14. Who was the first sportsperson to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Major Dhyan Chand (b) Milkha Singh (c) Viswanathan Anand (d) Sachin Tendulkar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sachin Tendulkar received the Bharat Ratna in 2014 — the first sportsperson and the youngest recipient. Trap: the country's highest sports award is named after Dhyan Chand, but that is the Khel Ratna, not the Bharat Ratna.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

15. Who was the first recipient of the Dadasaheb Phalke Award?
(a) Satyajit Ray (b) Prithviraj Kapoor (c) Devika Rani (d) Raj Kapoor
Answer: (c)
Solution: Devika Rani, the 'First Lady of Indian Cinema', received the first Dadasaheb Phalke Award in 1969. Trap: the other three are also Phalke Award winners, but of later years.
Source: Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — National Film Awards

16. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna was earlier known as:
(a) Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna (b) Dronacharya Khel Ratna (c) Arjuna Award (d) Bharat Khel Gaurav
Answer: (a)
Solution: The award began in 1991–92 as the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna and was renamed after hockey wizard Major Dhyan Chand on 6 August 2021.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports — National Sports Awards

17. The Nobel Prizes were first awarded in:
(a) 1895 (b) 1901 (c) 1913 (d) 1969
Answer: (b)
Solution: The first Nobel Prizes were given in 1901. Trap: 1895 is the year of Alfred Nobel's will; 1969 is when the Economics prize was first given. So the answer is 1901.
Source: nobelprize.org

18. Alfred Nobel, whose will started the Nobel Prizes, is known for inventing:
(a) Penicillin (b) Dynamite (c) The telephone (d) The radio
Answer: (b)
Solution: Alfred Nobel was a Swedish chemist who invented dynamite; his will of 1895 created the Nobel Prizes.
Source: nobelprize.org

19. Mother Teresa won the Nobel Prize in 1979 in the field of:
(a) Peace (b) Literature (c) Physiology or Medicine (d) Economics
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mother Teresa won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1979. She received the Bharat Ratna the next year, in 1980.
Source: nobelprize.org

20. Amartya Sen won the Nobel Prize in 1998 in which field?
(a) Physics (b) Economics (c) Peace (d) Literature
Answer: (b)
Solution: Amartya Sen won the Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences in 1998.
Source: nobelprize.org

21. Who was the first recipient of the Jnanpith Award?
(a) Rabindranath Tagore (b) Sumitranandan Pant (c) G. Sankara Kurup (d) Ashapoorna Devi
Answer: (c)
Solution: G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam) received the first Jnanpith Award in 1965 for 'Odakkuzhal'. Trap: Sumitranandan Pant (1968) was the first Hindi writer to win it, not the first winner overall.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

22. Which of the following is a peace-time gallantry award?
(a) Vir Chakra (b) Kirti Chakra (c) Maha Vir Chakra (d) Param Vir Chakra
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ashoka, Kirti and Shaurya Chakra are peace-time awards. Trap: all four names end in 'Chakra'; Param Vir, Maha Vir and Vir Chakra are war-time awards.
Source: Ministry of Defence — gallantryawards.gov.in

23. Who was the first person to receive the Bharat Ratna posthumously?
(a) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Lal Bahadur Shastri (d) Mahatma Gandhi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lal Bahadur Shastri was the first posthumous recipient (1966); posthumous awards were allowed only from 1966. Trap: Mahatma Gandhi never received the Bharat Ratna; Nehru got it in his lifetime (1955).
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs — Bharat Ratna

24. Which is the correct order of the Padma awards, from highest to lowest?
(a) Padma Shri – Padma Bhushan – Padma Vibhushan (b) Padma Vibhushan – Padma Bhushan – Padma Shri (c) Padma Bhushan – Padma Vibhushan – Padma Shri (d) Padma Vibhushan – Padma Shri – Padma Bhushan
Answer: (b)
Solution: The order is Padma Vibhushan > Padma Bhushan > Padma Shri (mnemonic 'Very Big Star'). All three rank below the Bharat Ratna.
Source: padmaawards.gov.in (Ministry of Home Affairs)

25. The Saraswati Samman is given by:
(a) Ministry of Culture (b) Sahitya Akademi (c) K. K. Birla Foundation (d) Bharatiya Jnanpith
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Saraswati Samman (from 1991) is given by the K. K. Birla Foundation. Trap: the Jnanpith trophy is a statue of Saraswati (Vagdevi), so students wrongly link the Saraswati Samman with the Bharatiya Jnanpith.
Source: K. K. Birla Foundation
