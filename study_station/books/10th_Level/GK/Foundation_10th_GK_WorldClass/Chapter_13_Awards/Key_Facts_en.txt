📋 Key Facts & Figures: Awards & Honours

1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Bharat Ratna	Highest civilian award; instituted 2 January 1954; no money, Sanad + peepal-leaf medallion.	"Bharat Ratna carries a cash prize."	Almost True Statement
First Bharat Ratna (1954)	C. Rajagopalachari, S. Radhakrishnan, C. V. Raman.	Offering Jawaharlal Nehru (1955) or Rajendra Prasad (1962).	Chronological Confusion
First posthumous Bharat Ratna	Lal Bahadur Shastri, 1966.	Offering Mahatma Gandhi (never received it).	Almost True Statement
First woman Bharat Ratna	Indira Gandhi, 1971.	Offering Mother Teresa (1980).	Chronological Confusion
First non-Indian citizen	Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan, 1987; Nelson Mandela 1990.	Offering Mandela as the first.	Chronological Confusion
Bharat Ratna 2024	Karpoori Thakur, L. K. Advani, Charan Singh, P. V. Narasimha Rao, M. S. Swaminathan.	Adding a name from 2019 (Pranab Mukherjee) to the 2024 list.	Negative Phrasing
Padma awards	1954; three classes renamed Vibhushan, Bhushan, Shri on 8 January 1955.	"Padma awards were instituted in 1955."	Chronological Confusion
Padma eligibility	Govt servants not eligible, except doctors and scientists; cap 120 a year.	"Doctors in government service cannot get Padma awards."	Almost True Statement
War-time gallantry	Param Vir > Maha Vir > Vir Chakra; instituted 26 January 1950.	Putting Kirti Chakra in the war-time list.	Similar Name
Peace-time gallantry	Ashoka > Kirti > Shaurya Chakra; 1952, renamed 1967.	Ranking Shaurya above Kirti.	Almost True Statement
Param Vir Chakra	Designer Savitri Khanolkar; Indra's Vajra motif; first: Maj. Somnath Sharma.	Offering Abdul Hamid or Vikram Batra as the first.	Chronological Confusion
Khel Ratna	1991–92; renamed Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna on 6 August 2021; first: Viswanathan Anand.	"Started in 2021."	Chronological Confusion
Arjuna vs Dronacharya	Arjuna (1961) for players; Dronacharya (1985) for coaches.	Swapping player and coach.	Similar Name
Dadasaheb Phalke	1969; first: Devika Rani; for 2023: Mohanlal.	Offering Prithviraj Kapoor or Raj Kapoor as first.	Similar Name
Jnanpith	By Bharatiya Jnanpith (trust); est. 1961, first given 1965 to G. Sankara Kurup.	"Given by the Ministry of Culture" or "first given in 1961".	Almost True Statement
Saraswati Samman	K. K. Birla Foundation, 1991; first: Harivansh Rai Bachchan.	"Given by Bharatiya Jnanpith".	Similar Name
Gandhi Peace Prize	1995 (125th birth anniversary); first: Julius Nyerere; ₹1 crore.	Offering Nelson Mandela as first.	Almost True Statement
Nobel Prize	First 1901; Peace in Oslo, rest in Stockholm; Economics from 1969.	"All Nobel Prizes are given in Stockholm."	Almost True Statement
First Indian Nobel	Rabindranath Tagore, Literature, 1913.	Offering C. V. Raman (1930).	Chronological Confusion
Ramon Magsaysay	Philippines, 1957; first Indian: Vinoba Bhave (1958).	Offering Mother Teresa (1962).	Chronological Confusion
Booker vs International Booker	Booker: novel in English; International Booker: translated fiction (Geetanjali Shree 2022, Banu Mushtaq 2025).	"Geetanjali Shree won the Booker Prize."	Similar Name
First Indian Oscar	Bhanu Athaiya, 1983, costume design for "Gandhi".	Offering Satyajit Ray (Honorary, 1992) or A. R. Rahman (2009).	Chronological Confusion
