Set 1/6 – Books & Authors MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Who wrote the Arthashastra?
(a) Megasthenes (b) Kalidasa (c) Kautilya (d) Banabhatta
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Arthashastra, a book on statecraft, was written by Kautilya (Chanakya), adviser of Chandragupta Maurya. Trap: Megasthenes wrote Indica about the same Maurya court.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History Part I)

2. Abhijnanashakuntalam was written by:
(a) Kalidasa (b) Bhasa (c) Shudraka (d) Harsha
Answer: (a)
Solution: Abhijnanashakuntalam is Kalidasa's most famous play. Bhasa wrote Swapnavasavadattam and Shudraka wrote Mrichchhakatika.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Who wrote the Ramcharitmanas?
(a) Surdas (b) Kabir (c) Valmiki (d) Tulsidas
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tulsidas wrote the Ramcharitmanas in Awadhi. Trap: Valmiki wrote the original Sanskrit Ramayana, not the Ramcharitmanas.
Source: PYQ-style

4. "Gitanjali", for which the Nobel Prize in Literature was given in 1913, was written by:
(a) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay (d) Sarojini Naidu
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rabindranath Tagore received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913; Gitanjali is his best-known collection of poems. Bankim and Sarat Chandra were also Bengali writers, which makes them tempting.
Source: nobelprize.org

5. The novel "Godan" was written by:
(a) Premchand (b) Jaishankar Prasad (c) Phanishwar Nath Renu (d) Harivansh Rai Bachchan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Godan (1936) is Premchand's last complete novel. Prasad wrote Kamayani, Renu wrote Maila Anchal and Bachchan wrote Madhushala.
Source: PYQ-style

6. "The Discovery of India" was written by:
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (c) Jawaharlal Nehru (d) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: (c)
Solution: Jawaharlal Nehru wrote The Discovery of India in Ahmednagar Fort jail in 1944. Trap: Azad wrote "India Wins Freedom", a similar-sounding title.
Source: PYQ-style

7. "The Story of My Experiments with Truth" is the autobiography of:
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Mahatma Gandhi (c) Rajendra Prasad (d) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gandhi wrote his autobiography in Gujarati; it was translated into English by Mahadev Desai. Nehru's autobiography is simply called "An Autobiography".
Source: PYQ-style

8. "Wings of Fire" is the autobiography of:
(a) Vikram Sarabhai (b) Homi J. Bhabha (c) C. V. Raman (d) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam
Answer: (d)
Solution: Wings of Fire is A. P. J. Abdul Kalam's autobiography, written with Arun Tiwari. All four options are scientists, so only the fact helps.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Who wrote "Indica"?
(a) Megasthenes (b) Fa-Hien (c) Hiuen Tsang (d) Al-Biruni
Answer: (a)
Solution: Megasthenes, the Greek ambassador at the court of Chandragupta Maurya, wrote Indica. The other three are travellers of much later periods.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History Part I)

10. The novel "Anandamath" was written by:
(a) Rabindranath Tagore (b) Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay (c) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (d) Dinabandhu Mitra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay wrote Anandamath (1882), which contains "Vande Mataram". Trap: Sarat Chandra, the author of Devdas, has a similar name.
Source: PYQ-style

11. "Madhushala" was written by:
(a) Ramdhari Singh Dinkar (b) Harivansh Rai Bachchan (c) Suryakant Tripathi Nirala (d) Sumitranandan Pant
Answer: (b)
Solution: Madhushala is a famous collection of Hindi verses by Harivansh Rai Bachchan. Dinkar wrote Rashmirathi.
Source: PYQ-style

12. Who wrote the "Baburnama"?
(a) Humayun (b) Gulbadan Begum (c) Abul Fazl (d) Babur
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Baburnama is the memoir of Babur himself, written in Turkish. Gulbadan Begum, his daughter, wrote the Humayun-nama.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History Part II)

13. "Long Walk to Freedom" is the autobiography of:
(a) Nelson Mandela (b) Martin Luther King Jr. (c) Barack Obama (d) Desmond Tutu
Answer: (a)
Solution: Long Walk to Freedom is Nelson Mandela's autobiography. Desmond Tutu was also a South African leader, which makes (d) tempting.
Source: PYQ-style

14. "Das Kapital" was written by:
(a) Adam Smith (b) Vladimir Lenin (c) Karl Marx (d) Jean-Jacques Rousseau
Answer: (c)
Solution: Das Kapital is Karl Marx's book on capitalism. Adam Smith wrote The Wealth of Nations, and Rousseau wrote The Social Contract.
Source: PYQ-style

15. "Hamlet" was written by:
(a) Charles Dickens (b) William Shakespeare (c) John Milton (d) William Wordsworth
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hamlet is a tragedy by William Shakespeare. Milton wrote Paradise Lost and Dickens wrote Oliver Twist.
Source: PYQ-style

16. "On the Origin of Species" was written by:
(a) Isaac Newton (b) Charles Darwin (c) Gregor Mendel (d) Jean-Baptiste Lamarck
Answer: (b)
Solution: Charles Darwin published On the Origin of Species in 1859, explaining natural selection. Lamarck had an earlier, different theory of evolution.
Source: PYQ-style

17. "Hind Swaraj" was written by:
(a) Bal Gangadhar Tilak (b) Lala Lajpat Rai (c) Mahatma Gandhi (d) Gopal Krishna Gokhale
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gandhi wrote Hind Swaraj in 1909. Trap: Tilak's slogan "Swaraj is my birthright" pulls students towards (a).
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History Part III)

18. "Playing It My Way" is the autobiography of:
(a) Rahul Dravid (b) Kapil Dev (c) Sunil Gavaskar (d) Sachin Tendulkar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Playing It My Way is Sachin Tendulkar's autobiography. Kapil Dev wrote Straight from the Heart and Sunil Gavaskar wrote Sunny Days.
Source: PYQ-style

19. The "Panchatantra" is traditionally credited to:
(a) Vishnu Sharma (b) Narayana Pandit (c) Kalidasa (d) Valmiki
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Panchatantra stories are credited to Vishnu Sharma. Trap: Narayana Pandit wrote the Hitopadesha, a similar collection of animal fables.
Source: PYQ-style

20. "Malgudi Days" was written by:
(a) Mulk Raj Anand (b) Raja Rao (c) R. K. Narayan (d) Ruskin Bond
Answer: (c)
Solution: R. K. Narayan set many stories in Malgudi, an imaginary town. Mulk Raj Anand wrote Untouchable and Raja Rao wrote Kanthapura.
Source: PYQ-style

21. Who wrote "Harshacharita"?
(a) Harshavardhana (b) Banabhatta (c) Kalidasa (d) Bhasa
Answer: (b)
Solution: Banabhatta, the court poet of King Harsha, wrote the Harshacharita about him. Trap: Harsha's name is in the title, but he wrote other works — Ratnavali, Nagananda and Priyadarshika.
Source: PYQ-style

22. "Rajatarangini", a history of the kings of Kashmir, was written by:
(a) Kalidasa (b) Bilhana (c) Jayadeva (d) Kalhana
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kalhana wrote the Rajatarangini in the 12th century. Trap: Kalidasa and Bilhana sound similar to Kalhana.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Who wrote the "Ain-i-Akbari"?
(a) Akbar (b) Faizi (c) Abul Fazl (d) Badauni
Answer: (c)
Solution: Abul Fazl wrote the Ain-i-Akbari as the third volume of his Akbarnama. Trap: Akbar's name is in the title, and Faizi was Abul Fazl's brother.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History Part II)

24. Which of the following was NOT written by Kalidasa?
(a) Meghadutam (b) Mrichchhakatika (c) Raghuvamsham (d) Kumarasambhavam
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mrichchhakatika ("The Little Clay Cart") was written by Shudraka. Meghadutam, Raghuvamsham and Kumarasambhavam are all Kalidasa's works.
Source: PYQ-style

25. "The God of Small Things", which won the Booker Prize in 1997, was written by:
(a) Kiran Desai (b) Anita Desai (c) Arundhati Roy (d) Jhumpa Lahiri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Arundhati Roy won the Booker Prize for The God of Small Things. Trap: Kiran Desai won the Booker in 2006 for a different novel, The Inheritance of Loss.
Source: thebookerprizes.com
