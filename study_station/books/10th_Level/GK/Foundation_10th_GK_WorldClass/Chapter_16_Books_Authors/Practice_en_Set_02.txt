Set 2/6 – Books & Authors MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The "Ashtadhyayi", a famous work on Sanskrit grammar, was written by:
(a) Patanjali (b) Panini (c) Kautilya (d) Amarasimha
Answer: (b)
Solution: Panini wrote the Ashtadhyayi, the classic rule-book of Sanskrit grammar. Trap: Patanjali wrote the Mahabhashya, a commentary on Panini's grammar.
Source: PYQ-style

27. The "Mahabhashya", a great commentary on Panini's grammar, was written by:
(a) Patanjali (b) Panini (c) Bharata (d) Vatsyayana
Answer: (a)
Solution: Patanjali wrote the Mahabhashya on Panini's Ashtadhyayi. Trap: Panini wrote the original grammar, not the commentary.
Source: PYQ-style

28. The "Natyashastra", the oldest Indian text on drama, music and dance, is credited to:
(a) Bharata (Bharatamuni) (b) Kalidasa (c) Bhasa (d) Panini
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Natyashastra is credited to Bharata (Bharatamuni). Kalidasa and Bhasa wrote plays, but not this treatise on the art of drama.
Source: PYQ-style

29. "Mudrarakshasa", a play about Chanakya and Chandragupta Maurya, was written by:
(a) Kalidasa (b) Shudraka (c) Vishakhadatta (d) Bhasa
Answer: (c)
Solution: Vishakhadatta wrote Mudrarakshasa. Trap: it is often slipped into lists of Kalidasa's works, but it is not his.
Source: PYQ-style

30. "Mrichchhakatika" ("The Little Clay Cart") was written by:
(a) Bhasa (b) Harsha (c) Kalidasa (d) Shudraka
Answer: (d)
Solution: Shudraka wrote Mrichchhakatika. Kalidasa wrote Abhijnanashakuntalam and Bhasa wrote Swapnavasavadattam.
Source: PYQ-style

31. "Swapnavasavadattam" was written by:
(a) Bharavi (b) Bhasa (c) Dandin (d) Magha
Answer: (b)
Solution: Swapnavasavadattam is a Sanskrit play by Bhasa. Bharavi wrote Kiratarjuniyam, Dandin wrote Dashakumaracharita and Magha wrote Shishupalavadha.
Source: PYQ-style

32. The plays "Ratnavali", "Nagananda" and "Priyadarshika" were written by:
(a) Banabhatta (b) Kalidasa (c) Samudragupta (d) Harshavardhana
Answer: (d)
Solution: King Harshavardhana himself wrote these three Sanskrit plays. Trap: Banabhatta was his court poet and wrote the Harshacharita, not these plays.
Source: PYQ-style

33. "Buddhacharita", a life of the Buddha in Sanskrit verse, was written by:
(a) Nagarjuna (b) Ashvaghosha (c) Vasubandhu (d) Kalidasa
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ashvaghosha, linked with the court of Kanishka, wrote the Buddhacharita. Nagarjuna and Vasubandhu were Buddhist philosophers, which makes them tempting.
Source: PYQ-style

34. The Prayag Prashasti (Allahabad Pillar inscription) praising Samudragupta was composed by:
(a) Harishena (b) Ravikirti (c) Banabhatta (d) Kalidasa
Answer: (a)
Solution: Harishena, a court poet of Samudragupta, composed the Prayag Prashasti. Trap: Ravikirti composed the Aihole inscription of Pulakeshin II, another famous prashasti.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History Part I)

35. The "Aryabhatiya" was written by:
(a) Varahamihira (b) Brahmagupta (c) Aryabhata (d) Bhaskaracharya
Answer: (c)
Solution: Aryabhata wrote the Aryabhatiya on mathematics and astronomy. The other three were also astronomer-mathematicians, so the name in the title is the clue.
Source: PYQ-style

36. "Brihat Samhita" was written by:
(a) Aryabhata (b) Charaka (c) Sushruta (d) Varahamihira
Answer: (d)
Solution: Varahamihira wrote the Brihat Samhita and the Pancha Siddhantika. Trap: "Samhita" also appears in Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita, which are medical texts.
Source: PYQ-style

37. Which ancient Indian text is best known for its description of surgery?
(a) Charaka Samhita (b) Brihat Samhita (c) Ashtadhyayi (d) Sushruta Samhita
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Sushruta Samhita, credited to Sushruta, is known for its account of surgery. Trap: the Charaka Samhita is a text on medicine in general.
Source: PYQ-style

38. "Lilavati", a famous work on mathematics, was written by:
(a) Brahmagupta (b) Aryabhata (c) Bhaskaracharya (d) Varahamihira
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lilavati is part of the Siddhanta Shiromani of Bhaskaracharya (Bhaskara II). Brahmagupta wrote the Brahmasphutasiddhanta.
Source: PYQ-style

39. The "Tirukkural" was written by:
(a) Ilango Adigal (b) Thiruvalluvar (c) Sattanar (d) Tolkappiyar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Thiruvalluvar wrote the Tirukkural, a Tamil book of couplets. Ilango Adigal wrote Silappadikaram and Sattanar wrote Manimekalai.
Source: PYQ-style

40. The Tamil epic "Silappadikaram" was written by:
(a) Ilango Adigal (b) Thiruvalluvar (c) Kamban (d) Sattanar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ilango Adigal wrote Silappadikaram, the story of Kannagi and Kovalan. Trap: Sattanar wrote Manimekalai, its companion epic.
Source: PYQ-style

41. "Amarakosha", a well-known Sanskrit dictionary, was compiled by:
(a) Panini (b) Hemachandra (c) Amarasimha (d) Kalhana
Answer: (c)
Solution: Amarasimha compiled the Amarakosha; the name of the book carries his name. Panini is linked with grammar, not this dictionary.
Source: PYQ-style

42. "Gita Govinda" was written by:
(a) Jayadeva (b) Chandidas (c) Vidyapati (d) Surdas
Answer: (a)
Solution: Jayadeva wrote the Sanskrit poem Gita Govinda on Radha and Krishna. Chandidas, Vidyapati and Surdas also wrote on Krishna, which makes them tempting.
Source: PYQ-style

43. "Prithviraj Raso" was written by:
(a) Jayanaka (b) Chand Bardai (c) Kalhana (d) Amir Khusrau
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chand Bardai, the court poet of Prithviraj Chauhan, wrote the Prithviraj Raso. Trap: Jayanaka wrote a different work on the same king, the Prithvirajavijaya.
Source: PYQ-style

44. The "Hitopadesha" was written by:
(a) Vishnu Sharma (b) Somadeva (c) Narayana Pandit (d) Kshemendra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Narayana Pandit wrote the Hitopadesha. Trap: Vishnu Sharma is credited with the Panchatantra, a similar book of animal fables.
Source: PYQ-style

45. "Kadambari" was written by:
(a) Banabhatta (b) Dandin (c) Subandhu (d) Bhasa
Answer: (a)
Solution: Banabhatta, Harsha's court poet, wrote both Kadambari and Harshacharita. Dandin and Subandhu were other Sanskrit prose writers.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Megasthenes came to the court of Chandragupta Maurya as the ambassador of:
(a) Alexander (b) Seleucus Nicator (c) Darius (d) Antiochus
Answer: (b)
Solution: Megasthenes was sent by Seleucus Nicator, Alexander's general who ruled the eastern part of his empire. Trap: Alexander had died before Megasthenes came to India.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History Part I)

47. Who is called the "Adi Kavi" (first poet) and credited with the Ramayana?
(a) Ved Vyasa (b) Kalidasa (c) Valmiki (d) Tulsidas
Answer: (c)
Solution: Valmiki is called the Adi Kavi and is credited with the Sanskrit Ramayana. Trap: Ved Vyasa is credited with the Mahabharata, and Tulsidas wrote the Ramcharitmanas much later.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Kalidasa, the author of Meghadutam, is generally linked with which period?
(a) Maurya period (b) Kushana period (c) Mughal period (d) Gupta period
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kalidasa is generally placed in the Gupta period, the age of classical Sanskrit literature. The Maurya period is linked with Kautilya, not Kalidasa.
Source: PYQ-style

49. "Tolkappiyam" is a work on:
(a) Tamil grammar (b) astronomy (c) medicine (d) Mughal history
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tolkappiyam, by Tolkappiyar, is the oldest surviving work on Tamil grammar. It is a Tamil classic, so medicine and Mughal history are clearly ruled out.
Source: PYQ-style

50. The "Charaka Samhita" deals mainly with:
(a) surgery (b) medicine (c) astronomy (d) grammar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Charaka Samhita is a text on medicine (Ayurveda). Trap: surgery is the speciality of the Sushruta Samhita.
Source: PYQ-style
