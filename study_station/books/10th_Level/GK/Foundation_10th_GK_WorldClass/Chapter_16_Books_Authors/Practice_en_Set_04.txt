Set 4/6 – Books & Authors MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 76–100: Medium

76. "Satyarth Prakash" (1875) was written by:
(a) Raja Ram Mohan Roy (b) Swami Vivekananda (c) Swami Dayananda Saraswati (d) Keshab Chandra Sen
Answer: (c)
Solution: Swami Dayananda Saraswati, founder of the Arya Samaj, wrote Satyarth Prakash. The other three were also reformers, so only the fact helps.
Source: PYQ-style

77. "Gulamgiri" (1873) was written by:
(a) Jyotiba Phule (b) B. R. Ambedkar (c) E. V. Ramasamy Periyar (d) Sree Narayana Guru
Answer: (a)
Solution: Jyotiba Phule wrote Gulamgiri against caste oppression. Trap: Ambedkar wrote a different anti-caste book, Annihilation of Caste (1936).
Source: PYQ-style

78. The play "Neel Darpan" (1860) by Dinabandhu Mitra showed the misery of:
(a) jute mill workers (b) indigo farmers (c) tea garden labourers (d) handloom weavers
Answer: (b)
Solution: Neel Darpan described the suffering of indigo farmers under European planters. "Neel" means indigo, which is the clue.
Source: PYQ-style

79. "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India", which put forward the "Drain Theory", was written by:
(a) R. C. Dutt (b) Gopal Krishna Gokhale (c) Dadabhai Naoroji (d) Pherozeshah Mehta
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dadabhai Naoroji wrote this book (1901) and explained the drain of wealth from India to Britain. R. C. Dutt also criticised British economic policy, which makes (a) tempting.
Source: PYQ-style

80. "Gita Rahasya" was written in Mandalay jail by:
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) Aurobindo Ghosh (c) Vinoba Bhave (d) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bal Gangadhar Tilak wrote Gita Rahasya while imprisoned in Mandalay (Burma). Gandhi is a tempting option because he too wrote and spoke much on the Gita.
Source: PYQ-style

81. "The Indian War of Independence 1857" was written by:
(a) V. D. Savarkar (b) Bhagat Singh (c) Bal Gangadhar Tilak (d) Lala Lajpat Rai
Answer: (a)
Solution: V. D. Savarkar wrote this book and called the revolt of 1857 a war of independence. The other three were freedom fighters but did not write this book.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Lala Lajpat Rai wrote "Unhappy India" as a reply to which book?
(a) The Discovery of India (b) Mother India (c) Hind Swaraj (d) Gulliver's Travels
Answer: (b)
Solution: Unhappy India was Lala Lajpat Rai's reply to Katherine Mayo's "Mother India", an American writer's book attacking Indian society. Trap: The Discovery of India (Nehru) and Hind Swaraj (Gandhi) are famous freedom-struggle books, but they were not the target of this reply.
Source: PYQ-style

83. "Annihilation of Caste" (1936) was written by:
(a) Jyotiba Phule (b) Mahatma Gandhi (c) B. R. Ambedkar (d) E. V. Ramasamy Periyar
Answer: (c)
Solution: B. R. Ambedkar wrote Annihilation of Caste. Trap: Jyotiba Phule also wrote against caste, but his book is Gulamgiri.
Source: PYQ-style

84. "Why I Am an Atheist" was written by:
(a) Chandrashekhar Azad (b) Bhagat Singh (c) Sukhdev (d) Ram Prasad Bismil
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bhagat Singh wrote the essay Why I Am an Atheist while in jail. The other three were also revolutionaries, so only the fact helps.
Source: PYQ-style

85. "The Indian Struggle" was written by:
(a) Subhas Chandra Bose (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Mahatma Gandhi (d) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
Answer: (a)
Solution: Subhas Chandra Bose wrote The Indian Struggle. Bose also began an autobiography, An Indian Pilgrim, which he did not finish.
Source: PYQ-style

86. "India Wins Freedom" was written by:
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Rajendra Prasad (c) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (d) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
Answer: (d)
Solution: Maulana Abul Kalam Azad wrote India Wins Freedom. Trap: Rajendra Prasad wrote "India Divided", a similar-sounding title.
Source: PYQ-style

87. Jawaharlal Nehru wrote "Glimpses of World History" in the form of letters to:
(a) his wife Kamala (b) his father Motilal (c) his daughter Indira (d) his sister Vijaya Lakshmi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Glimpses of World History is a collection of Nehru's letters from jail to his daughter Indira. The other three were also close family members, which makes them tempting.
Source: PYQ-style

88. The newspapers "Kesari" (Marathi) and "Maratha" (English) were started by:
(a) Gopal Krishna Gokhale (b) Bal Gangadhar Tilak (c) Lala Lajpat Rai (d) Aurobindo Ghosh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bal Gangadhar Tilak ran Kesari and Maratha. Gokhale was also from Maharashtra, which makes (a) tempting.
Source: PYQ-style

89. The newspaper "Al-Hilal" was started by:
(a) Muhammad Ali Jinnah (b) Sir Syed Ahmad Khan (c) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (d) Muhammad Iqbal
Answer: (c)
Solution: Maulana Abul Kalam Azad started Al-Hilal. The other three were also Muslim leaders or thinkers of the period, which makes them tempting.
Source: PYQ-style

90. The newspaper "Mooknayak" was started by:
(a) B. R. Ambedkar (b) Jyotiba Phule (c) Mahatma Gandhi (d) E. V. Ramasamy Periyar
Answer: (a)
Solution: B. R. Ambedkar started Mooknayak ("leader of the voiceless"). Gandhi's papers were Young India, Navajivan and Harijan.
Source: PYQ-style

91. Which of the following newspapers was NOT associated with Mahatma Gandhi?
(a) Young India (b) Harijan (c) Navajivan (d) Kesari
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kesari was Bal Gangadhar Tilak's newspaper. Young India, Harijan, Navajivan (and Indian Opinion in South Africa) were Gandhi's.
Source: PYQ-style

92. What was the real name of the Hindi writer Premchand?
(a) Sampooran Singh Kalra (b) Dhanpat Rai Srivastava (c) Ramdhari Singh (d) Suryakant Tripathi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Premchand's real name was Dhanpat Rai Srivastava. Sampooran Singh Kalra is Gulzar, Ramdhari Singh is Dinkar and Suryakant Tripathi is Nirala.
Source: PYQ-style

93. "Kamayani" was written by:
(a) Maithili Sharan Gupt (b) Sumitranandan Pant (c) Jaishankar Prasad (d) Mahadevi Verma
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kamayani is Jaishankar Prasad's famous epic poem. Trap: Maithili Sharan Gupt wrote Saket, another well-known long poem.
Source: PYQ-style

94. "Saket" and "Bharat-Bharati" were written by:
(a) Maithili Sharan Gupt (b) Jaishankar Prasad (c) Ramdhari Singh Dinkar (d) Bhartendu Harishchandra
Answer: (a)
Solution: Both are works of Maithili Sharan Gupt. Prasad wrote Kamayani and Dinkar wrote Rashmirathi.
Source: PYQ-style

95. "Rashmirathi" was written by:
(a) Harivansh Rai Bachchan (b) Suryakant Tripathi Nirala (c) Sumitranandan Pant (d) Ramdhari Singh Dinkar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rashmirathi, a poem on Karna, is by Ramdhari Singh Dinkar. Bachchan wrote Madhushala.
Source: PYQ-style

96. "Maila Anchal" was written by:
(a) Shrilal Shukla (b) Phanishwar Nath Renu (c) Bhisham Sahni (d) Premchand
Answer: (b)
Solution: Phanishwar Nath Renu wrote Maila Anchal. Shrilal Shukla wrote Raag Darbari and Bhisham Sahni wrote Tamas.
Source: PYQ-style

97. The Hindi novel "Tamas" was written by:
(a) Shrilal Shukla (b) Phanishwar Nath Renu (c) Bhisham Sahni (d) Agyeya
Answer: (c)
Solution: Tamas is Bhisham Sahni's novel. Trap: Raag Darbari (Shrilal Shukla) and Maila Anchal (Renu) are other famous Hindi novels.
Source: PYQ-style

98. "Andher Nagari" was written by:
(a) Premchand (b) Jaishankar Prasad (c) Bhartendu Harishchandra (d) Maithili Sharan Gupt
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bhartendu Harishchandra, called the father of modern Hindi, wrote Andher Nagari and Bharat Durdasha. Premchand is known mainly for novels and stories.
Source: PYQ-style

99. The Bengali novel "Devdas" was written by:
(a) Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay (b) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (c) Rabindranath Tagore (d) Dinabandhu Mitra
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay wrote Devdas, Parineeta and Srikanta. Trap: Bankim Chandra (Anandamath) has a very similar name.
Source: PYQ-style

100. The Punjabi novel "Pinjar" was written by:
(a) Krishna Sobti (b) Ismat Chughtai (c) Mahadevi Verma (d) Amrita Pritam
Answer: (d)
Solution: Amrita Pritam wrote Pinjar. Mahadevi Verma was a Hindi poet (Yama), and the other two were also women writers, which makes them tempting.
Source: PYQ-style
