🃏 Flashcard Deck: Books & Authors (Foundation)

Card 1
Front: A prisoner in Ahmednagar Fort wrote the story of his country in 1944. Who, and which book?
Back: Jawaharlal Nehru — The Discovery of India (published 1946).

Card 2
Front: A Maurya-age book on running a kingdom — and a Greek ambassador's account of the same court. Name both with authors.
Back: Arthashastra — Kautilya (Chanakya); Indica — Megasthenes.

Card 3
Front: A cloud carries a lover's message across India. Which poem, and whose?
Back: Meghadutam — Kalidasa.

Card 4
Front: Kalidasa or Kalhana — who wrote the history of the kings of Kashmir?
Back: Kalhana — Rajatarangini (12th century). Kalidasa wrote plays and poems. (Examiner's trap: similar names.)

Card 5
Front: The book is named after King Harsha. Did Harsha write Harshacharita?
Back: No. Banabhatta, his court poet, wrote it. Harsha himself wrote the plays Ratnavali, Nagananda and Priyadarshika.

Card 6
Front: Mnemonic check: what does "Shakuntala sent a Megh to Raghu and Kumar" stand for?
Back: Kalidasa's four big works — Abhijnanashakuntalam, Meghadutam, Raghuvamsham, Kumarasambhavam.

Card 7
Front: Which ancient book is linked with surgery?
Back: Sushruta Samhita — Sushruta. (Charaka Samhita is on medicine.)

Card 8
Front: A traveller came with Mahmud of Ghazni and wrote about India in Arabic. Who, and which book?
Back: Al-Biruni — Kitab-ul-Hind (Tahqiq-i-Hind).

Card 9
Front: A Moroccan traveller served under Muhammad bin Tughlaq. Name him and his book.
Back: Ibn Battuta — Rihla.

Card 10
Front: Which Mughal emperor wrote his own memoir, and in which language?
Back: Babur — Baburnama (Tuzuk-i-Baburi), in Turkish.

Card 11
Front: Babur's daughter wrote a book about her brother. Name her and the book.
Back: Gulbadan Begum — Humayun-nama.

Card 12
Front: Ain-i-Akbari — was it written by Akbar? (Examiner's trap)
Back: No. Abul Fazl wrote it; it is the third volume of his Akbarnama.

Card 13
Front: Which novel gave India the song "Vande Mataram"?
Back: Anandamath (1882) — Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay.

Card 14
Front: Gandhi wrote a small book in 1909 on a ship. Name it.
Back: Hind Swaraj (Indian Home Rule).

Card 15
Front: Which book did Tilak write in Mandalay jail?
Back: Gita Rahasya.

Card 16
Front: A book of 1873 dedicated to the fight against slavery — who wrote "Gulamgiri"?
Back: Jyotiba Phule.

Card 17
Front: Mnemonic check: "Nehru's jail had a DGA." What are D, G and A?
Back: The Discovery of India, Glimpses of World History, An Autobiography.

Card 18
Front: What was Premchand's real name, and which is his last complete novel?
Back: Dhanpat Rai Srivastava — Godan (1936).

Card 19
Front: Which Indian won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913, and for which collection?
Back: Rabindranath Tagore — Gitanjali.

Card 20
Front: Whose autobiographies are "Wings of Fire" and "Long Walk to Freedom"?
Back: A. P. J. Abdul Kalam; Nelson Mandela.
