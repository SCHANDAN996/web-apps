Set 5/6 – Books & Authors MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard

101. The novels "Untouchable" and "Coolie" were written by:
(a) Raja Rao (b) Mulk Raj Anand (c) R. K. Narayan (d) Khushwant Singh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mulk Raj Anand wrote Untouchable and Coolie. Raja Rao wrote Kanthapura and R. K. Narayan wrote Malgudi Days.
Source: PYQ-style

102. "Kanthapura" was written by:
(a) Raja Rao (b) Mulk Raj Anand (c) Ruskin Bond (d) Amitav Ghosh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kanthapura is Raja Rao's novel. Trap: Mulk Raj Anand, another early Indian novelist in English, wrote Untouchable.
Source: PYQ-style

103. "Train to Pakistan" was written by:
(a) Salman Rushdie (b) Vikram Seth (c) Amitav Ghosh (d) Khushwant Singh
Answer: (d)
Solution: Khushwant Singh wrote Train to Pakistan, a novel on the Partition. The other three are also Indian-origin writers in English.
Source: PYQ-style

104. "The Room on the Roof" and "The Blue Umbrella" were written by:
(a) R. K. Narayan (b) Anita Desai (c) Ruskin Bond (d) Vikram Seth
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both are works of Ruskin Bond. R. K. Narayan is linked with Malgudi, and Vikram Seth with A Suitable Boy.
Source: PYQ-style

105. "A Suitable Boy" was written by:
(a) Amitav Ghosh (b) Vikram Seth (c) Aravind Adiga (d) Salman Rushdie
Answer: (b)
Solution: A Suitable Boy is Vikram Seth's novel. Amitav Ghosh wrote The Shadow Lines and Adiga wrote The White Tiger.
Source: PYQ-style

106. In which year did Salman Rushdie win the Booker Prize for "Midnight's Children"?
(a) 1981 (b) 1997 (c) 2006 (d) 2008
Answer: (a)
Solution: Midnight's Children won the Booker Prize in 1981. Trap: 1997, 2006 and 2008 are the years of Arundhati Roy, Kiran Desai and Aravind Adiga.
Source: thebookerprizes.com

107. "The White Tiger", which won the Booker Prize in 2008, was written by:
(a) Kiran Desai (b) Arundhati Roy (c) Aravind Adiga (d) Salman Rushdie
Answer: (c)
Solution: Aravind Adiga won the 2008 Booker Prize for The White Tiger. Kiran Desai won in 2006 and Arundhati Roy in 1997.
Source: thebookerprizes.com

108. "The Golden Threshold" is a collection of poems by:
(a) Toru Dutt (b) Sarojini Naidu (c) Amrita Pritam (d) Mahadevi Verma
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Golden Threshold is by Sarojini Naidu, the "Nightingale of India". Toru Dutt was another Indian woman poet in English, which makes (a) tempting.
Source: PYQ-style

109. "Ignited Minds" and "India 2020" are books by:
(a) Vikram Sarabhai (b) Homi J. Bhabha (c) C. V. Raman (d) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam
Answer: (d)
Solution: A. P. J. Abdul Kalam wrote Ignited Minds and, with Y. S. Rajan, India 2020. All four options are scientists, so only the fact helps.
Source: PYQ-style

110. "The Republic" was written by:
(a) Aristotle (b) Socrates (c) Plato (d) Homer
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Republic is Plato's work. Trap: Socrates is the main speaker in it but did not write it; Aristotle wrote Politics.
Source: PYQ-style

111. "The Prince" was written by:
(a) Niccolò Machiavelli (b) Jean-Jacques Rousseau (c) Dante (d) Miguel de Cervantes
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Prince is Machiavelli's book on how rulers gain and keep power. Rousseau wrote The Social Contract and Cervantes wrote Don Quixote.
Source: PYQ-style

112. "The Wealth of Nations" (1776) was written by:
(a) Karl Marx (b) Adam Smith (c) Charles Darwin (d) Jean-Jacques Rousseau
Answer: (b)
Solution: Adam Smith wrote The Wealth of Nations. Trap: Karl Marx also wrote on economics, but his book is Das Kapital.
Source: PYQ-style

113. "The Social Contract" was written by:
(a) Niccolò Machiavelli (b) Plato (c) Aristotle (d) Jean-Jacques Rousseau
Answer: (d)
Solution: Jean-Jacques Rousseau wrote The Social Contract. Machiavelli wrote The Prince, Plato wrote the Republic and Aristotle wrote Politics.
Source: PYQ-style

114. "Paradise Lost" was written by:
(a) Dante (b) John Milton (c) William Shakespeare (d) Homer
Answer: (b)
Solution: John Milton wrote Paradise Lost. Trap: Dante wrote the Divine Comedy, another great poem about heaven and hell.
Source: PYQ-style

115. "War and Peace" and "Anna Karenina" were written by:
(a) Victor Hugo (b) Charles Dickens (c) Leo Tolstoy (d) Ernest Hemingway
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both novels are by the Russian writer Leo Tolstoy. Victor Hugo wrote Les Misérables and Dickens wrote Oliver Twist.
Source: PYQ-style

116. George Orwell, the author of "Animal Farm", was born in:
(a) Motihari, Bihar (b) Bombay (c) Calcutta (d) Madras
Answer: (a)
Solution: George Orwell was born in Motihari, Bihar. Trap: Rudyard Kipling, author of The Jungle Book, was born in Bombay.
Source: PYQ-style

117. "The Jungle Book" and "Kim" were written by:
(a) George Orwell (b) Rudyard Kipling (c) Jonathan Swift (d) Daniel Defoe
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rudyard Kipling wrote The Jungle Book and Kim. Swift wrote Gulliver's Travels and Defoe wrote Robinson Crusoe.
Source: PYQ-style

118. "The Diary of a Young Girl" was written by:
(a) Helen Keller (b) Malala Yousafzai (c) Florence Nightingale (d) Anne Frank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Diary of a Young Girl is Anne Frank's diary. Trap: Helen Keller's autobiography is The Story of My Life.
Source: PYQ-style

119. "The Race of My Life" is the autobiography of:
(a) P. T. Usha (b) Milkha Singh (c) M. C. Mary Kom (d) Abhinav Bindra
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Race of My Life is Milkha Singh's autobiography. Mary Kom's is Unbreakable and Abhinav Bindra's is A Shot at History.
Source: PYQ-style

120. "Unbreakable" is the autobiography of:
(a) Saina Nehwal (b) P. T. Usha (c) M. C. Mary Kom (d) Sania Mirza
Answer: (c)
Solution: Unbreakable is the autobiography of the boxer M. C. Mary Kom. All four are sportswomen, so only the fact helps.
Source: PYQ-style

121. Match List I (book) with List II (author):
A. Rihla  B. Kitab-ul-Hind  C. Si-Yu-Ki  D. Indica
1. Megasthenes  2. Hiuen Tsang  3. Al-Biruni  4. Ibn Battuta
(a) A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1 (b) A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1 (c) A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2 (d) A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rihla – Ibn Battuta, Kitab-ul-Hind – Al-Biruni, Si-Yu-Ki – Hiuen Tsang, Indica – Megasthenes. Trap: (b) swaps the two Arabic-language writers, Ibn Battuta and Al-Biruni.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Consider the following statements:
1. Rabindranath Tagore received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913.
2. Tagore was the first Asian to receive the Nobel Prize in Literature.
3. Tagore wrote "Saare Jahan Se Achha".
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is the trap: Saare Jahan Se Achha was written by Muhammad Iqbal; Tagore wrote Jana Gana Mana.
Source: nobelprize.org

123. Which of the following pairs is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) Anandamath – Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (b) Neel Darpan – Dinabandhu Mitra (c) Gulamgiri – Jyotiba Phule (d) Hind Swaraj – Bal Gangadhar Tilak
Answer: (d)
Solution: Hind Swaraj (1909) was written by Mahatma Gandhi. Trap: Tilak's slogan "Swaraj is my birthright" makes the wrong pair look right.
Source: PYQ-style

124. Arrange these books in the order in which they were published (earliest first):
1. Hind Swaraj  2. Anandamath  3. Satyarth Prakash  4. Annihilation of Caste
(a) 2-3-1-4 (b) 3-2-1-4 (c) 3-1-2-4 (d) 2-1-3-4
Answer: (b)
Solution: Satyarth Prakash (1875) → Anandamath (1882) → Hind Swaraj (1909) → Annihilation of Caste (1936). Trap: (a) puts Anandamath before Satyarth Prakash; the two are only seven years apart.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Who was the first writer in English to receive the Jnanpith Award?
(a) R. K. Narayan (b) Salman Rushdie (c) Amitav Ghosh (d) Vikram Seth
Answer: (c)
Solution: Amitav Ghosh received the 54th Jnanpith Award (for 2018), the first given to a writer in English. R. K. Narayan is the most famous older name, which makes (a) tempting, but he never received it.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith
