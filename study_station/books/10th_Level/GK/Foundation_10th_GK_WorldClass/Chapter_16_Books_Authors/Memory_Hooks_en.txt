🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Mnemonic 1: Kalidasa's four big works
Sentence: "Shakuntala sent a Megh (cloud) to Raghu and Kumar."
Shakuntala → Abhijnanashakuntalam | Megh → Meghadutam | Raghu → Raghuvamsham | Kumar → Kumarasambhavam
Visual: Shakuntala on a riverbank, waving a cloud off towards two princes.

🧠 Mnemonic 2: Kalidasa vs Kalhana
Rule: "Kalhana has an H for History (of Kashmir)."
Kalhana → Rajatarangini, history of Kashmir. Kalidasa → plays and poems.

🧠 Mnemonic 3: Panini vs Patanjali
Rule: "Panini made the Pattern, Patanjali Polished it."
Panini → Ashtadhyayi (grammar rules). Patanjali → Mahabhashya (the great commentary on Panini) and the Yoga Sutra.

🧠 Mnemonic 4: Charaka vs Sushruta
Rule: "Sushruta = Surgery" — both start with S.
Charaka → Charaka Samhita (medicine).

🧠 Mnemonic 5: Bana and Harsha
Rule: "Bana was Harsha's fan."
Banabhatta wrote Harshacharita and Kadambari. Harsha himself wrote three plays — "RNP": Ratnavali, Nagananda, Priyadarshika ("Ration, Naan, Paneer").

🧠 Mnemonic 6: Travellers in order
Sentence: "My Friend Hari Ate Mango Ice-cream Before lunch."
Megasthenes → Fa-Hien → Hiuen Tsang → Al-Biruni → Marco Polo → Ibn Battuta → Bernier.

🧠 Mnemonic 7: Al-Biruni vs Ibn Battuta
Rule: "Al-Biruni = Book of Hind; Battuta = Rihla on the Road."
Al-Biruni → Kitab-ul-Hind (came with Mahmud of Ghazni). Ibn Battuta → Rihla ("journey"), Muhammad bin Tughlaq.

🧠 Mnemonic 8: Abul Fazl
Rule: "Abul Fazl did Akbar a Fazl (favour)."
Akbarnama, with the Ain-i-Akbari as its third volume. Faizi was his brother — a poet, not the author of the Ain.

🧠 Mnemonic 9: Diary or report card?
Rule: "Only B and J kept a diary."
Babur (Baburnama, Turkish) and Jahangir (Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri) wrote their own memoirs. Humayun-nama → Gulbadan Begum; Padshahnama → Abdul Hamid Lahori.

🧠 Mnemonic 10: Bankim vs Sarat
Rule: "Bankim = B for Bharat Mata" → Anandamath, Vande Mataram. "Sarat = Sad Devdas" → Devdas, Parineeta.

🧠 Mnemonic 11: Freedom-struggle books by year
Chant: "60 Neel, 73 Gulam, 75 Satyarth, 82 Ananda, 1909 Swaraj."
Neel Darpan 1860 → Gulamgiri 1873 → Satyarth Prakash 1875 → Anandamath 1882 → Hind Swaraj 1909.

🧠 Mnemonic 12: Nehru's prison trio
Rule: "Nehru's jail had a DGA."
Discovery of India, Glimpses of World History, An Autobiography.

🧠 Mnemonic 13: Tilak's jail book
Rule: "Tilak read the Gita in Mandalay" → Gita Rahasya. Gandhi → Hind Swaraj (1909).

🧠 Mnemonic 14: Chhayavad pillars
Rule: "PPNM — Prasad, Pant, Nirala, Mahadevi."
Kamayani (Prasad), Chidambara (Pant), Ram ki Shakti Puja (Nirala), Yama (Mahadevi).

🧠 Mnemonic 15: Booker winners of Indian origin
Chant: "Rushdie 81, Roy 97, Desai 06, Adiga 08" — "Midnight's Children, Small Things, Inheritance, Tiger".

⏭️ Skip Strategy

1. If a "match the following" lists four books of regional languages you have never studied and you cannot place even two pairs with certainty, skip it in the first round — elimination works only when two pairs are sure.

2. If a question asks for the exact year a lesser-known book was published and the options are within a few years of each other, skip unless you have learnt that year — these are low-return questions in the Foundation exams.

3. If an awards question asks for the "latest" winner and you are not sure which year the exam is counting, skip it; do not guess between two recent winners.
