📋 Key Facts & Figures: Books & Authors

1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Arthashastra	Kautilya (Chanakya); statecraft, Maurya period.	Offering Megasthenes (Indica) or Vishakhadatta (Mudrarakshasa, a play about Chanakya).	Similar Name
Indica	Megasthenes, Greek envoy at Chandragupta Maurya's court.	Offering Fa-Hien or Arrian.	Chronological Confusion
Kalidasa	Abhijnanashakuntalam, Meghadutam, Raghuvamsham, Kumarasambhavam, Malavikagnimitram.	Slipping Mudrarakshasa or Mrichchhakatika into his list.	Negative Phrasing
Rajatarangini	Kalhana; history of Kashmir, 12th century.	Offering Kalidasa (sound-alike).	Similar Name
Harshacharita	Banabhatta, court poet of Harsha.	Saying Harsha wrote it (he wrote Ratnavali, Nagananda, Priyadarshika).	Almost True Statement
Ashtadhyayi	Panini; Sanskrit grammar.	Offering Patanjali (Mahabhashya, a commentary on it).	Similar Name
Sushruta Samhita	Sushruta; surgery.	Offering Charaka (medicine).	Similar Name
Kitab-ul-Hind	Al-Biruni, came with Mahmud of Ghazni; in Arabic.	Offering Ibn Battuta (Rihla).	Similar Name
Rihla	Ibn Battuta, Moroccan, time of Muhammad bin Tughlaq.	Placing him in the Mughal period.	Chronological Confusion
Akbarnama / Ain-i-Akbari	Abul Fazl; Ain is the third volume.	"Ain-i-Akbari was written by Akbar" or by Faizi.	Almost True Statement
Humayun-nama	Gulbadan Begum, daughter of Babur.	Offering Humayun himself.	Almost True Statement
Baburnama	Babur, in Turkish.	Saying it was written in Persian.	Almost True Statement
Padmavat	Malik Muhammad Jayasi, in Awadhi.	Offering Tulsidas (also Awadhi).	Similar Name
Anandamath	Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (1882); Vande Mataram.	Offering Sarat Chandra (Devdas).	Similar Name
Hind Swaraj	Gandhi, 1909.	Offering Tilak (Swaraj slogan) or "Hindu Swaraj".	Almost True Statement
Gita Rahasya	Tilak, Mandalay jail.	Offering Gandhi (wrote on the Gita too).	Similar Name
Gulamgiri	Jyotiba Phule, 1873.	Offering Ambedkar (Annihilation of Caste).	Similar Name
Discovery of India	Nehru, Ahmednagar Fort, 1944.	Offering Azad (India Wins Freedom) or Rajendra Prasad (India Divided).	Similar Name
Godan	Premchand.	Offering Jaishankar Prasad or Sarat Chandra.	Similar Name
Kamayani	Jaishankar Prasad.	Offering Maithili Sharan Gupt (Saket).	Similar Name
The Inheritance of Loss	Kiran Desai, Booker 2006.	Offering Anita Desai (her mother).	Similar Name
Gitanjali	Tagore; Nobel 1913.	Offering 1905 or 1919.	Chronological Confusion
First Jnanpith	G. Sankara Kurup, 1965 (Malayalam).	Offering Sumitranandan Pant (first Hindi, 1968).	Almost True Statement

2. Time Management (15/45-Sec Rule)
Question Type	Example	Time	Score Maximizer?
Direct book–author one-liner	Who wrote Arthashastra?	15 sec	Yes
Autobiography of a famous person	Wings of Fire is whose autobiography?	15 sec	Yes
Traveller and his book / ruler	Ibn Battuta came during whose reign?	15 sec	Yes
Pen name	Real name of Premchand?	15 sec	Yes
"Which is NOT written by X?"	Which is NOT a work of Kalidasa?	45 sec	No (read carefully)
Match the following (4 pairs)	Book – Author list	45 sec	No
Arrange books / travellers in order	Megasthenes, Fa-Hien, Hiuen Tsang, Al-Biruni	45 sec	No
Multi-statement (2–3 statements)	Statements on Booker winners	45 sec	No
