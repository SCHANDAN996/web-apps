Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Consider the following statements about "Indica":
1. It was written by Megasthenes, the ambassador of Seleucus Nicator.
2. The original book survives complete today.
3. Megasthenes wrote about the court of Chandragupta Maurya.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is the trap: the original Indica is lost; we know it only through quotations by later Greek writers.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Which of the following works were written by Banabhatta?
1. Harshacharita  2. Kadambari  3. Ratnavali  4. Nagananda
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 3 and 4 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: Banabhatta wrote Harshacharita and Kadambari. Trap: Ratnavali and Nagananda were written by his patron, King Harshavardhana.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Match List I (book) with List II (author):
A. Akbarnama  B. Humayun-nama  C. Padshahnama  D. Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh
1. Gulbadan Begum  2. Abdul Hamid Lahori  3. Abdul Qadir Badauni  4. Abul Fazl
(a) A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3 (b) A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4 (c) A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3 (d) A-1, B-4, C-3, D-2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Akbarnama – Abul Fazl, Humayun-nama – Gulbadan Begum, Padshahnama – Abdul Hamid Lahori, Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh – Badauni. Trap: (b) gives the Akbarnama to Badauni, Akbar's critic.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History Part II)

129. Which of the following is NOT a work of Amir Khusrau?
(a) Khazain-ul-Futuh (b) Tughlaq Nama (c) Nuh Sipihr (d) Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi was written by Ziauddin Barani. Trap: "Tughlaq" in option (b) links it with the Tughlaq age, but Tughlaq Nama is indeed Khusrau's.
Source: PYQ-style

130. Consider the following statements about the Baburnama:
1. Babur wrote it himself in Turkish.
2. It was translated into Persian by Abdul Rahim Khan-i-Khanan.
3. It is also called the Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is the trap: the Baburnama's other name is Tuzuk-i-Baburi; Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri is Jahangir's memoir.
Source: PYQ-style

131. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Silappadikaram – Sattanar (b) Manimekalai – Ilango Adigal (c) Tirukkural – Thiruvalluvar (d) Tolkappiyam – Kamban
Answer: (c)
Solution: Tirukkural is by Thiruvalluvar. Trap: (a) and (b) swap the authors — Silappadikaram is by Ilango Adigal and Manimekalai by Sattanar; Tolkappiyam is by Tolkappiyar.
Source: PYQ-style

132. Which of the following was NOT written by Kalidasa?
(a) Malavikagnimitram (b) Vikramorvashiyam (c) Mudrarakshasa (d) Ritusamhara
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mudrarakshasa was written by Vishakhadatta. Malavikagnimitram, Vikramorvashiyam and Ritusamhara are Kalidasa's lesser-known works, placed here to confuse.
Source: PYQ-style

133. Consider the following statements:
1. The Charaka Samhita is mainly a text on surgery.
2. The Sushruta Samhita is known for its description of surgery.
3. Lilavati is a part of Bhaskaracharya's Siddhanta Shiromani.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statements 2 and 3 are correct. Statement 1 is the trap: the Charaka Samhita is on medicine; surgery is the speciality of the Sushruta Samhita.
Source: PYQ-style

134. Arrange these travellers in the order of their visits to India (earliest first):
1. Ibn Battuta  2. Megasthenes  3. François Bernier  4. Hiuen Tsang
(a) 2-4-1-3 (b) 4-2-1-3 (c) 2-1-4-3 (d) 2-4-3-1
Answer: (a)
Solution: Megasthenes (Maurya age) → Hiuen Tsang (Harsha) → Ibn Battuta (Muhammad bin Tughlaq) → Bernier (Mughal age). Trap: (d) places Bernier before Ibn Battuta, but Bernier came in the Mughal period.
Source: PYQ-style

135. Which of the following books was NOT written by Jawaharlal Nehru?
(a) The Discovery of India (b) Glimpses of World History (c) An Autobiography (d) India Divided
Answer: (d)
Solution: India Divided was written by Rajendra Prasad. The other three are Nehru's three big books.

137. Consider the following statements about the Jnanpith Award:
1. The first Jnanpith Award (1965) went to G. Sankara Kurup for Odakkuzhal.
2. Sumitranandan Pant was the first Hindi writer to receive it (1968).
3. Ramdhari Singh Dinkar received it for Rashmirathi.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is the trap: Dinkar received the Jnanpith (1972) for Urvashi, not for his better-known Rashmirathi.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith

138. Which of the following poets was NOT one of the four pillars of Chhayavad?
(a) Jaishankar Prasad (b) Sumitranandan Pant (c) Mahadevi Verma (d) Maithili Sharan Gupt
Answer: (d)
Solution: The four pillars of Chhayavad are Prasad, Pant, Nirala and Mahadevi Verma. Maithili Sharan Gupt (Saket) is a great Hindi poet but not one of the four. Trap: students often forget Mahadevi Verma and pick (c).
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (d)

140. Consider the following statements about the Booker Prize:
1. Arundhati Roy won it in 1997 for The God of Small Things.
2. Anita Desai won it in 2006 for The Inheritance of Loss.
3. Aravind Adiga won it in 2008 for The White Tiger.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

141. "Tomb of Sand", which won the International Booker Prize in 2022, is a translation of a Hindi novel ("Ret Samadhi") written by:
(a) Geetanjali Shree (b) Banu Mushtaq (c) Arundhati Roy (d) Kiran Desai
Answer: (a)
Solution: Geetanjali Shree wrote Ret Samadhi; Daisy Rockwell translated it as Tomb of Sand. Trap: Banu Mushtaq won the International Booker in 2025 for Heart Lamp, translated from Kannada.
Source: thebookerprizes.com

142. Which of the following pairs is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) The Social Contract – Jean-Jacques Rousseau (b) The Prince – Niccolò Machiavelli (c) Divine Comedy – John Milton (d) Robinson Crusoe – Daniel Defoe
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Divine Comedy was written by Dante. Trap: John Milton wrote Paradise Lost, a poem on a similar religious theme.

144. Mahatma Gandhi's idea of "Sarvodaya" came from his reading of "Unto This Last", written by:
(a) Leo Tolstoy (b) Karl Marx (c) John Ruskin (d) Charles Dickens

146. Which of the following was NOT written by Rabindranath Tagore?
(a) Gora (b) Ghare Baire (c) Kabuliwala (d) Srikanta

Solution: Srikanta was written by Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay. Gora, Ghare Baire and Kabuliwala are all Tagore's works.

147. "The Story of My Experiments with Truth" was originally written in which language, and who translated it into English?

148. "Majma-ul-Bahrain" was written by which Mughal prince, who also translated the Upanishads into Persian?
(a) Aurangzeb (b) Dara Shikoh (c) Humayun (d) Jahangir
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dara Shikoh wrote Majma-ul-Bahrain and translated the Upanishads into Persian as Sirr-i-Akbar. Trap: Aurangzeb was his brother and rival for the throne.
Source: PYQ-style

149. Consider the following statements:
1. "Jana Gana Mana" was written by Rabindranath Tagore.

3. "Saare Jahan Se Achha" was written by Muhammad Iqbal.
4. "Vande Mataram" is from the novel Anandamath.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 3 and 4 only (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

Source: PYQ-style

150. Which of the following correctly names a Sanskrit play about Chanakya and Chandragupta Maurya, with its author?
(a) Mrichchhakatika – Shudraka (b) Mudrarakshasa – Vishakhadatta (c) Mudrarakshasa – Kalidasa (d) Arthashastra – Kautilya
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vishakhadatta wrote Mudrarakshasa about Chanakya and Chandragupta. Trap: (c) has the right play but the wrong author, and the Arthashastra is a book on statecraft, not a play.
Source: PYQ-style
