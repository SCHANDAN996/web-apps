Set 4/6 – Indian Culture & Art MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The masked dance Chhau is performed mainly in which group of states?
(a) Punjab, Haryana and Himachal Pradesh (b) Kerala, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu (c) Odisha, Jharkhand and West Bengal (d) Gujarat, Rajasthan and Maharashtra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Chhau has three styles — Mayurbhanj (Odisha), Seraikela (Jharkhand) and Purulia (West Bengal). UNESCO added it to the Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2010.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Cheraw, the bamboo dance, is a folk dance of:
(a) Tripura (b) Mizoram (c) Assam (d) Himachal Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: In Cheraw, dancers step in and out of bamboo poles that are clapped together; it belongs to Mizoram. Hojagiri is from Tripura.
Source: PYQ-style

78. Ghoomar is a folk dance of:
(a) Rajasthan (b) Gujarat (c) Punjab (d) Uttar Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ghoomar, with its whirling skirts, is a folk dance of Rajasthan. Kalbelia is another famous dance of Rajasthan.
Source: PYQ-style

79. Yakshagana, a dance-drama form, belongs to:
(a) Kerala (b) Tamil Nadu (c) Andhra Pradesh (d) Karnataka
Answer: (d)
Solution: Yakshagana is the traditional dance-drama of coastal Karnataka. Trap: its heavy costumes look like Kathakali, which is from Kerala.
Source: PYQ-style

80. Hojagiri is a folk dance of:
(a) Manipur (b) Sikkim (c) Tripura (d) Meghalaya
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hojagiri is performed by the Reang (Bru) community of Tripura, with dancers balancing pots and lamps.
Source: PYQ-style

81. Raut Nacha is a folk dance of:
(a) Jharkhand (b) Chhattisgarh (c) Bihar (d) Odisha
Answer: (b)
Solution: Raut Nacha is performed by the Yadav (Raut) community of Chhattisgarh. Karma is common to Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh, and Jat-Jatin belongs to Bihar.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Rouf is a folk dance of:
(a) Jammu and Kashmir (b) Himachal Pradesh (c) Uttarakhand (d) Punjab
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rouf is performed by women in Kashmir. Nati is the folk dance of Himachal Pradesh.
Source: PYQ-style

83. Nautanki, a popular form of folk theatre, is mainly associated with:
(a) Bihar (b) Madhya Pradesh (c) Rajasthan (d) Uttar Pradesh
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nautanki is the folk theatre of Uttar Pradesh. Tamasha belongs to Maharashtra and Bhavai to Gujarat.
Source: PYQ-style

84. Tamasha is a traditional folk theatre of:
(a) Gujarat (b) Maharashtra (c) Karnataka (d) West Bengal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tamasha, with song and dance (often Lavani), is the folk theatre of Maharashtra. Jatra is from West Bengal and Bhavai from Gujarat.
Source: PYQ-style

85. Jatra, a form of folk theatre, is popular in:
(a) Assam (b) Gujarat (c) West Bengal (d) Punjab
Answer: (c)
Solution: Jatra is the open-air folk theatre of West Bengal and Odisha.
Source: PYQ-style

86. Kathputli, the traditional string-puppet show, belongs to:
(a) Rajasthan (b) Andhra Pradesh (c) Kerala (d) Assam
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kathputli are the string puppets of Rajasthan. Trap: Andhra Pradesh is known for Tholu Bommalata, which uses shadow puppets, not string puppets.
Source: PYQ-style

87. Kerala Kalamandalam, the famous centre for Kathakali, was founded by:
(a) Rukmini Devi Arundale (b) Kelucharan Mohapatra (c) Vallathol Narayana Menon (d) Uday Shankar
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Malayalam poet Vallathol Narayana Menon founded Kerala Kalamandalam. Rukmini Devi Arundale founded Kalakshetra in Chennai.
Source: PYQ-style

88. Pandit Ram Narayan made which bowed instrument famous as a solo instrument?
(a) Violin (b) Esraj (c) Dilruba (d) Sarangi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ram Narayan raised the sarangi from an accompanying instrument to a solo concert instrument.
Source: PYQ-style

89. Ustad Alla Rakha is remembered as a master of the:
(a) Sitar (b) Tabla (c) Pakhawaj (d) Santoor
Answer: (b)
Solution: Alla Rakha was a great tabla player who often accompanied Ravi Shankar; his son Zakir Hussain also became a tabla legend.
Source: PYQ-style

90. Ustad Vilayat Khan is associated with which instrument?
(a) Sitar (b) Sarod (c) Shehnai (d) Flute
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vilayat Khan was one of the greatest sitar players. Trap: Ali Akbar Khan and Amjad Ali Khan played the sarod.
Source: PYQ-style

91. The "Mohan Veena" is associated with:
(a) Ali Akbar Khan (b) Lalgudi Jayaraman (c) Vishwa Mohan Bhatt (d) Pannalal Ghosh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Pandit Vishwa Mohan Bhatt plays the Mohan Veena, a modified slide guitar. Lalgudi Jayaraman was a violinist and Pannalal Ghosh a flautist.
Source: PYQ-style

92. U. Srinivas was a famous Carnatic musician who played the:
(a) Veena (b) Ghatam (c) Nadaswaram (d) Mandolin
Answer: (d)
Solution: U. Srinivas made the mandolin a Carnatic concert instrument. The ghatam is a clay pot and the nadaswaram a South Indian wind instrument.
Source: PYQ-style

93. The Bibi ka Maqbara, called the "Taj of the Deccan", is located in:
(a) Aurangabad (b) Bijapur (c) Hyderabad (d) Bidar
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Bibi ka Maqbara in Aurangabad (Maharashtra) was built by Aurangzeb's son Azam Shah for his mother. Trap: Bijapur is famous for the Gol Gumbaz and Hyderabad for the Charminar.
Source: PYQ-style

94. The Alai Darwaza near the Qutb Minar in Delhi was built by:
(a) Iltutmish (b) Balban (c) Alauddin Khalji (d) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (c)
Solution: Alauddin Khalji built the Alai Darwaza as a gateway in the Qutb complex. Iltutmish completed the Qutb Minar itself.
Source: PYQ-style

95. Humayun's Tomb in Delhi is important in architecture because it is:
(a) the first Mughal building in Agra (b) the first garden-tomb of the Indian subcontinent (c) a temple built in the Nagara style (d) a fort built by Shah Jahan
Answer: (b)
Solution: Humayun's Tomb, built after his death in Akbar's reign, is the first garden-tomb of the subcontinent and inspired later tombs such as the Taj Mahal.
Source: whc.unesco.org (Humayun's Tomb, Delhi)

96. The Jantar Mantar observatory at Jaipur was built by:
(a) Sawai Pratap Singh (b) Man Singh I (c) Rana Kumbha (d) Sawai Jai Singh II
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sawai Jai Singh II built the Jantar Mantar observatories, including the one at Jaipur, a World Heritage site since 2010. Trap: Sawai Pratap Singh built the Hawa Mahal.
Source: whc.unesco.org (The Jantar Mantar, Jaipur)

97. The Vijay Stambha (Tower of Victory) at Chittorgarh was built by:
(a) Rana Kumbha (b) Rana Sanga (c) Maharana Pratap (d) Rana Ratan Singh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rana Kumbha of Mewar built the Vijay Stambha at Chittorgarh to celebrate a victory. Maharana Pratap and Rana Sanga were later rulers of Mewar.
Source: PYQ-style

98. The Gateway of India in Mumbai was built to mark:
(a) India's independence in 1947 (b) the visit of King George V and Queen Mary in 1911 (c) the end of the First World War (d) the opening of the first railway line
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Gateway of India commemorates the landing of King George V and Queen Mary in Bombay in 1911. Trap: India Gate in Delhi is the war memorial.
Source: PYQ-style

99. Chhath, celebrated mainly in Bihar, is dedicated to the worship of:
(a) Lord Shiva (b) Goddess Durga (c) the Sun god (d) Lord Ganesha
Answer: (c)
Solution: During Chhath, devotees offer prayers (arghya) to the setting and the rising Sun, standing in rivers and ponds.
Source: PYQ-style

100. Ugadi is celebrated as the new year in:
(a) Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka (b) Punjab and Haryana (c) West Bengal and Odisha (d) Gujarat and Rajasthan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ugadi marks the new year in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka. Trap: in Maharashtra the same new year day is called Gudi Padwa.
Source: PYQ-style
