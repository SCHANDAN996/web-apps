Set 3/6 – Indian Culture & Art MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Which of the following singers belonged to the Kirana gharana?
(a) Pandit Bhimsen Joshi (b) Ustad Bade Ghulam Ali Khan (c) Pandit Jasraj (d) Ustad Faiyaz Khan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bhimsen Joshi was the best-known singer of the Kirana gharana. Bade Ghulam Ali Khan belonged to Patiala, Pandit Jasraj to Mewati and Faiyaz Khan to Agra.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Which is regarded as the oldest gharana of Khayal singing?
(a) Kirana (b) Patiala (c) Gwalior (d) Agra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Gwalior gharana is regarded as the oldest Khayal gharana. Trap: Gwalior is also linked with Tansen, which makes students think only of Dhrupad.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Ustad Bade Ghulam Ali Khan is associated with which gharana?
(a) Kirana (b) Patiala (c) Jaipur-Atrauli (d) Mewati
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bade Ghulam Ali Khan was the great singer of the Patiala gharana. Kirana is linked with Bhimsen Joshi and Mewati with Pandit Jasraj.
Source: PYQ-style

54. In the Natyashastra's classification of instruments, the tabla is a:
(a) Tata vadya (b) Sushira vadya (c) Ghana vadya (d) Avanaddha vadya
Answer: (d)
Solution: Avanaddha vadya are instruments with a skin stretched over them, such as the tabla, mridangam and pakhawaj. Tata are string, sushira wind and ghana solid instruments.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

55. The flute and the shehnai belong to which group of instruments?
(a) Tata vadya (b) Sushira vadya (c) Avanaddha vadya (d) Ghana vadya
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sushira vadya are wind instruments played by blowing air, such as the flute, shehnai and nadaswaram.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

56. Which of the following is a ghana vadya (solid instrument struck to make sound)?
(a) Manjira (b) Sitar (c) Shehnai (d) Mridangam
Answer: (a)
Solution: The manjira (small cymbals) are solid instruments struck together, so they are ghana vadya. The sitar is tata, the shehnai sushira and the mridangam avanaddha.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

57. Who was the first musician to be awarded the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Lata Mangeshkar (b) Pandit Ravi Shankar (c) M. S. Subbulakshmi (d) Ustad Bismillah Khan
Answer: (c)
Solution: M. S. Subbulakshmi received the Bharat Ratna in 1998, before Ravi Shankar (1999) and Lata Mangeshkar and Bismillah Khan (both 2001). Trap: Lata Mangeshkar is the most famous name, but not the first.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs (list of Bharat Ratna awardees)

58. The Bhimbetka rock shelters, with prehistoric paintings, are located in:
(a) Madhya Pradesh (b) Maharashtra (c) Chhattisgarh (d) Karnataka
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bhimbetka is in Madhya Pradesh and became a World Heritage site in 2003. Trap: Ajanta and Ellora, the famous painted and carved caves, are in Maharashtra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

59. The Bhimbetka rock shelters were discovered by:
(a) Alexander Cunningham (b) V. S. Wakankar (c) John Marshall (d) R. D. Banerji
Answer: (b)
Solution: The archaeologist V. S. Wakankar discovered the Bhimbetka rock shelters. John Marshall and R. D. Banerji are linked with the Harappan sites, and Cunningham with the Archaeological Survey of India.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

60. The Ellora caves contain shrines of which religions?
(a) Only Buddhist (b) Buddhist and Jain only (c) Hindu and Jain only (d) Buddhist, Hindu and Jain
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ellora has Buddhist, Brahmanical (Hindu) and Jain caves side by side. Trap: Ajanta is the mainly Buddhist site, which makes "Only Buddhist" look right.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

61. The Kailasa temple at Ellora, cut out of a single rock, was made under which dynasty?
(a) Chalukya (b) Pallava (c) Rashtrakuta (d) Chola
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kailasa temple (Cave 16) was made under the Rashtrakuta king Krishna I in the 8th century.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

62. The painting "Bani Thani", often called the "Mona Lisa of India", belongs to which school?
(a) Kishangarh (b) Kangra (c) Basohli (d) Mughal
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Bani Thani" is a Kishangarh (Rajasthani) painting by Nihal Chand. Kangra and Basohli are Pahari schools.
Source: PYQ-style

63. The famous painting "Bharat Mata" was made by:
(a) Rabindranath Tagore (b) Abanindranath Tagore (c) Nandalal Bose (d) Raja Ravi Varma
Answer: (b)
Solution: Abanindranath Tagore, father of the Bengal School, painted "Bharat Mata". Trap: Rabindranath Tagore, the poet from the same family, also painted, but "Bharat Mata" is not his work.
Source: PYQ-style

64. Raja Ravi Varma, famous for oil paintings of gods and epic scenes, was from:
(a) Bengal (b) Maharashtra (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Kerala
Answer: (d)
Solution: Raja Ravi Varma came from Kilimanoor in Kerala; prints of his paintings spread all over India.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Nandalal Bose is remembered for illustrating:
(a) the original Constitution of India (b) the Hamzanama (c) the Ajanta murals (d) the Babur-nama
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nandalal Bose and his students from Santiniketan decorated the pages of the original Constitution of India. The Hamzanama was painted in Akbar's workshop, and the Ajanta murals are ancient.
Source: PYQ-style

66. In the Nagara style of temple architecture, the curved tower over the sanctum is called the:
(a) Gopuram (b) Vimana (c) Shikhara (d) Mandapa
Answer: (c)
Solution: Nagara temples of North India have a curved shikhara. Trap: the vimana is the stepped tower of Dravida temples, and the gopuram is the southern gateway.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

67. The tall gateways in the outer walls of Dravida-style temples are called:
(a) Shikhara (b) Gopurams (c) Toranas (d) Amalakas
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gopurams are the huge gate-towers of South Indian temples. Toranas are the carved gateways of stupas such as Sanchi, and the amalaka is the ribbed stone on top of a Nagara shikhara.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

68. Which of the following is an example of the Vesara (Deccan) style of temple architecture?
(a) Hoysala temples of Belur (b) Kandariya Mahadeva temple, Khajuraho (c) Brihadeshwara temple, Thanjavur (d) Lingaraja temple, Bhubaneswar
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Hoysala temples of Belur and Halebidu mix northern and southern features, which is called the Vesara style. Khajuraho and Lingaraja are Nagara; Thanjavur is Dravida.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

69. The Shore Temple at Mahabalipuram was built by the:
(a) Cholas (b) Cheras (c) Pandyas (d) Pallavas
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Shore Temple was built by the Pallavas (Narasimhavarman II). The Cholas came later and built the great temple at Thanjavur.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

70. The Sun Temple at Konark was built by:
(a) Rajaraja I (b) Narasimhadeva I (c) Anantavarman Chodaganga (d) Krishnadeva Raya
Answer: (b)
Solution: Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty built the Konark temple in the 13th century. Trap: Anantavarman Chodaganga was also an Eastern Ganga king, but he is linked with the Jagannath temple at Puri.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

71. Hampi in Karnataka was the capital of which empire?
(a) Vijayanagara (b) Chola (c) Maratha (d) Bahmani
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara empire; the stone chariot of its Vitthala temple appears on the ₹50 note.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History

72. The Great Stupa of Sanchi is in which state?
(a) Bihar (b) Uttar Pradesh (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Maharashtra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sanchi is in Madhya Pradesh; the stupa was begun in Ashoka's time and its carved gateways (toranas) are famous. It appears on the ₹200 note.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

73. The Dilwara Jain temples, famous for their marble carving, are at:
(a) Palitana (b) Mount Abu (c) Shravanabelagola (d) Ranakpur
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Dilwara temples are at Mount Abu in Rajasthan. Trap: Ranakpur is another marble Jain temple of Rajasthan, Palitana is in Gujarat and Shravanabelagola in Karnataka.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Wangala, the harvest festival of the Garo people, is celebrated in:
(a) Assam (b) Nagaland (c) Mizoram (d) Meghalaya
Answer: (d)
Solution: Wangala is the post-harvest festival of the Garos of Meghalaya. The Hornbill Festival belongs to Nagaland.
Source: PYQ-style

75. Losar, the Tibetan Buddhist new year festival, is celebrated mainly in:
(a) Ladakh (b) Kerala (c) Goa (d) Punjab
Answer: (a)
Solution: Losar is celebrated in the Buddhist regions of Ladakh, Sikkim and Arunachal Pradesh. Hemis is another festival of Ladakh.
Source: PYQ-style
