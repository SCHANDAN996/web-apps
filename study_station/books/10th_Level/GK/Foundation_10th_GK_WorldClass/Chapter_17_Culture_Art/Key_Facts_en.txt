📋 Key Facts & Figures: Indian Culture & Art

1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
8 classical dances	Recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, Kuchipudi, Odissi, Manipuri, Sattriya.	Slipping Chhau, Garba or Yakshagana into a "classical" list.	Negative Phrasing
Kerala has two	Kathakali and Mohiniyattam are both from Kerala.	Giving Kathakali to Karnataka or Andhra Pradesh.	Similar Name
Kathak vs Kathakali	Kathak — North India (UP); Kathakali — Kerala.	Matching Kathak with Kerala.	Similar Name
Sattriya	Assam; created by Srimanta Sankardev; recognised as classical in 2000.	Calling it a folk dance, or dating its creation to 2000.	Chronological Confusion
Kuchipudi	Named after a village in Krishna district, Andhra Pradesh; Tarangam on a brass plate.	Matching it with Tamil Nadu.	Almost True Statement
Natyashastra	Text on drama, dance, music by Bharata Muni; four groups of instruments.	Crediting it to Kalidasa or calling it a Mughal text.	Similar Name
Carnatic music	Purandara Dasa = father; Trinity = Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Shastri.	Putting Tansen or Purandara Dasa inside the Trinity.	Almost True Statement
Gharanas	Gwalior oldest Khayal gharana; Kirana — Bhimsen Joshi; Patiala — Bade Ghulam Ali Khan.	Linking Bhimsen Joshi with Patiala.	Similar Name
Instrument–player	Shehnai – Bismillah Khan; Santoor – Shivkumar Sharma; Sarod – Amjad Ali Khan; Flute – Hariprasad Chaurasia.	Sitar vs sarod: Ravi Shankar (sitar) vs Amjad Ali Khan (sarod).	Similar Name
Bharat Ratna musicians	M. S. Subbulakshmi (1998) was the first musician to receive it.	Offering Lata Mangeshkar (2001) as the first.	Chronological Confusion
Bhimbetka	Prehistoric rock paintings, Madhya Pradesh; World Heritage 2003.	Placing it in Maharashtra (Ajanta's state).	Almost True Statement
Ellora	Buddhist, Hindu and Jain caves; Kailasa temple (Rashtrakuta Krishna I).	"Ellora caves are only Buddhist" (that is closer to Ajanta).	Almost True Statement
Folk paintings	Madhubani – Bihar; Warli – Maharashtra; Pattachitra – Odisha; Kalamkari – Andhra Pradesh.	Swapping Warli and Madhubani.	Similar Name
Temple styles	Nagara – shikhara (north); Dravida – vimana + gopuram (south); Vesara – mix (Deccan).	Calling the southern tower a shikhara.	Almost True Statement
Brihadeshwara, Thanjavur	Built by the Chola king Rajaraja I.	Offering Rajendra I (who built Gangaikonda Cholapuram).	Similar Name
Konark	Narasimhadeva I, Eastern Ganga dynasty, 13th century; chariot with 24 wheels, 7 horses.	Offering a Chola or Pallava king.	Almost True Statement
Qutb Minar	Begun by Qutbuddin Aibak, completed by Iltutmish.	"Built by Aibak" (only begun).	Almost True Statement
Charminar	Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, 1591, Hyderabad.	Offering Muhammad Adil Shah (Gol Gumbaz).	Similar Name
World Heritage sites (2026)	45 — 37 cultural, 7 natural, 1 mixed (Khangchendzonga).	Giving older counts (42 in 2023, 43 in 2024, 44 in 2025).	Chronological Confusion
First WH sites (1983)	Ajanta, Ellora, Agra Fort, Taj Mahal.	Adding Qutb Minar or Red Fort (1993, 2007).	Chronological Confusion
