Set 5/6 – Indian Culture & Art MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard

101. Madhubani (Mithila) painting originated in which state?
(a) Bihar (b) Uttar Pradesh (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Rajasthan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Madhubani painting originated in the Mithila region of Bihar. It uses natural dyes and geometric patterns and holds a Geographical Indication (GI) tag.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Warli painting, characterized by simple geometric shapes like circles, triangles and squares, belongs to:
(a) Maharashtra (b) Gujarat (c) Odisha (d) Karnataka
Answer: (a)
Solution: Warli folk painting is practiced by the Warli tribe in the North Sahyadri region of Maharashtra. Rice paste and gum are typically used on mud walls.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Pattachitra, a traditional cloth-based scroll painting, is the folk art of:
(a) Odisha (b) Kerala (c) Himachal Pradesh (d) Tamil Nadu
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pattachitra ('patta' means cloth, 'chitra' means picture) is centered in Raghurajpur, Odisha, depicting themes of Lord Jagannath and Krishna.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Kalamkari painting, which uses a tamarind pen to draw designs on cotton fabric, is traditional to:
(a) Andhra Pradesh (b) Kerala (c) Rajasthan (d) Punjab
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kalamkari ('kalam' means pen, 'kari' means work) has two historic styles in Andhra Pradesh: Srikalahasti (temple themes) and Machilipatnam (block-printed designs).
Source: PYQ-style

105. The famous miniature painting "Bani Thani", often called the "Indian Mona Lisa", belongs to which school?
(a) Kishangarh school (b) Mewar school (c) Kangra school (d) Bundi school
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Bani Thani" was painted by artist Nihal Chand under the patron King Sawai Samant Singh of the Kishangarh school of Rajasthan.
Source: PYQ-style

106. Phad painting, where epic stories of folk deities like Pabuji and Devnarayan are painted on long scrolls of cloth, belongs to:
(a) Rajasthan (b) Gujarat (c) Haryana (d) Madhya Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Phad painting is an indigenous scroll painting tradition of Bhilwara and Shahpura in Rajasthan, traditionally carried and recited by Bhopa balladeers.
Source: PYQ-style

107. Which annual cultural festival of Nagaland is celebrated every year from 1 to 10 December and is called the "Festival of Festivals"?
(a) Wangala Festival (b) Hornbill Festival (c) Losar Festival (d) Chapchar Kut
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Hornbill Festival is held at the Naga Heritage Village, Kisama near Kohima, Nagaland. Trap: Wangala is celebrated in Meghalaya and Chapchar Kut in Mizoram.
Source: PYQ-style

108. The Chapchar Kut festival is celebrated upon completion of bamboo clearance for Jhum cultivation in:
(a) Nagaland (b) Mizoram (c) Tripura (d) Manipur
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chapchar Kut is the most joyful spring festival of the Mizo people in Mizoram, celebrated after clearing jungles for Jhum (slash and burn) farming.
Source: PYQ-style

109. Wangala, the "100 Drums Festival" celebrated to honour the sun and fertility god Saljong, belongs to which tribe and state?
(a) Khasis of Assam (b) Garo tribe of Meghalaya (c) Apatanis of Arunachal Pradesh (d) Lepchas of Sikkim
Answer: (b)
Solution: Wangala is the post-harvest Thanksgiving festival of the Garo tribe in Meghalaya, celebrated with the beat of 100 traditional long drums.
Source: PYQ-style

110. Losar is celebrated as the New Year festival mainly by the Buddhist communities of:
(a) Kerala and Tamil Nadu (b) Ladakh and Arunachal Pradesh (c) Gujarat and Rajasthan (d) Punjab and Haryana
Answer: (b)
Solution: Losar marks the Tibetan and Buddhist New Year, celebrated with religious ceremonies and mask dances in Ladakh, Arunachal Pradesh (Monpa tribe), and Sikkim.
Source: PYQ-style

111. Which four-day harvest festival of Tamil Nadu features the preparation of boiled sweet rice in earthen pots and includes Mattu day for honoring cattle?
(a) Onam (b) Pongal (c) Ugadi (d) Vishu
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pongal is the 4-day Tamil harvest festival (Bhogi Pongal, Surya Pongal, Mattu Pongal, Kaanum Pongal). Trap: Onam is celebrated in Kerala, and Vishu marks the Malayali new year.
Source: PYQ-style

112. The annual Ambubachi Mela, associated with the belief of the menstrual cycle of the goddess, is held at which temple in Assam?
(a) Umananda Temple (b) Kamakhya Temple (c) Navagraha Temple (d) Hayagriva Madhava Temple
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ambubachi Mela is held annually during the monsoon at the Kamakhya Temple atop Nilachal Hill in Guwahati, Assam.
Source: PYQ-style

113. The world-famous Pushkar Fair, held annually in Rajasthan around Kartik Purnima, is primarily known as a:
(a) Handicrafts and silk trade fair (b) Camel and livestock trading fair (c) Boat race and water sports festival (d) Tribal weapon and arrow display fair
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pushkar Fair in Ajmer district, Rajasthan, is one of the world's largest camel and cattle trading fairs, combined with sacred dips in the holy Pushkar Lake.
Source: PYQ-style

114. The grand Brihadisvara Temple at Thanjavur (Tamil Nadu), an apex achievement of Dravidian temple architecture, was built by:
(a) Rajendra Chola I (b) Narasimhavarman II (c) Rajaraja Chola I (d) Kulottunga Chola I
Answer: (c)
Solution: Brihadisvara (Peruvudaiyar Koyil) was built by the Chola emperor Rajaraja Chola I between 1003 and 1010 AD. Trap: his son Rajendra Chola I built Gangaikonda Cholapuram.
Source: PYQ-style

115. The Sun Temple at Konark (Odisha), designed in the shape of a colossal 24-wheeled chariot pulled by 7 horses, was built by which ruler?
(a) Anantavarman Chodaganga (b) Kharavela (c) Narasimhadeva I (d) Prataparudra Deva
Answer: (c)
Solution: King Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty built the Konark Sun Temple in the 13th century. It is also known as the "Black Pagoda".
Source: PYQ-style

116. The Kandariya Mahadeva Temple, celebrated for its intricate shikhara and sculptural ornamentation, is located in which group of temples?
(a) Hampi Group of Monuments (b) Pattadakal Group of Monuments (c) Khajuraho Group of Monuments (d) Mahabalipuram Group of Monuments
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kandariya Mahadeva is the largest and most ornate temple at Khajuraho in Madhya Pradesh, built by the Chandela ruler Vidyadhara in the Nagara style.
Source: PYQ-style

117. The famous monolithic rock-cut Kailasa Temple (Cave 16) at Ellora was excavated under the patronage of which Rashtrakuta king?
(a) Dantidurga (b) Amoghavarsha I (c) Krishna I (d) Govinda III
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kailasa Temple at Ellora (Maharashtra) was carved top-down from a single basalt cliff under Rashtrakuta King Krishna I in the 8th century AD.
Source: PYQ-style

118. The Pancha Rathas (Five Chariots) rock-cut monolithic shrines at Mahabalipuram were built during the reign of which dynasty?
(a) Chola dynasty (b) Pandya dynasty (c) Pallava dynasty (d) Chera dynasty
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Pancha Rathas at Mamallapuram (Mahabalipuram, Tamil Nadu) were carved during the reign of Pallava king Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla) in the 7th century.
Source: PYQ-style

119. Nuakhai is an agricultural festival welcoming the new crop season, primarily celebrated in:
(a) Punjab (b) Assam (c) Odisha (d) Kerala
Answer: (c)
Solution: Nuakhai ('nua' means new, 'khai' means food/eat) is celebrated particularly in Western Odisha on the day after Ganesh Chaturthi to offer the first harvest of paddy to deities.
Source: PYQ-style

120. In temple architecture, the circumambulatory walkway surrounding the sanctum sanctorum (garbhagriha) is called:
(a) Antarala (b) Mandapa (c) Shikhara (d) Pradakshina Patha
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Pradakshina Patha is the enclosed or open gallery around the sanctum for clockwise circumambulation. Mandapa is the pillared hall and Antarala is the vestibule.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 6

121. Match the following folk theatre forms with their respective states:
1. Bhavai - A. Goa
2. Dashavatar - B. Gujarat
3. Bhand Pather - C. Jammu and Kashmir
4. Maach - D. Madhya Pradesh
Which matching code is correct?
(a) 1-A, 2-B, 3-C, 4-D (b) 1-B, 2-C, 3-A, 4-D (c) 1-C, 2-A, 3-B, 4-D (d) 1-B, 2-A, 3-C, 4-D
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bhavai is from Gujarat/Rajasthan; Dashavatar is practiced in the Konkan/Goa region; Bhand Pather is from Jammu & Kashmir; Maach belongs to Malwa in Madhya Pradesh.

122. Consider the following pairs of traditional handloom sarees / fabrics and their states:
1. Paithani - Maharashtra
2. Chanderi - Madhya Pradesh
3. Pochampally Ikat - Telangana
4. Kasavu - Kerala
How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?
(a) Only one pair (b) Only two pairs (c) Only three pairs (d) All four pairs
Answer: (d)
Solution: All four are GI-tagged traditional textiles: Paithani from Maharashtra, Chanderi from Madhya Pradesh, Pochampally Ikat from Telangana, and Kasavu sarees from Kerala.
Source: PYQ-style

123. The Shore Temple at Mahabalipuram differs from the earlier rock-cut temples of the Pallavas because it is:
(a) Completely made of unbaked mud bricks (b) Carved into the interior of a subterranean cave (c) A Buddhist vihara carved out of granite boulders (d) One of the earliest freestanding structural stone temples in South India
Answer: (d)
Solution: Unlike the rock-cut cave temples and monolithic rathas of early Pallava times, the Shore Temple at Mahabalipuram was built as a freestanding structural stone temple by Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha).
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 6

124. With reference to the ancient cave paintings of Bhimbetka, which of the following statements is NOT correct?
(a) It is located in the Raisen district of Madhya Pradesh (b) The oldest rock art belongs to the Upper Palaeolithic period (c) Animal motifs like bison, boar, and deer are prominently depicted (d) The paintings depict scenes from the Ramayana and Mahabharata epics
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bhimbetka paintings belong to the prehistoric Stone Age (Upper Palaeolithic and Mesolithic); they depict hunting, dancing, and animals, not epic mythological scenes.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 1

125. Which of the following sites represents the "Vesara" or hybrid style of temple architecture combining Nagara and Dravida elements?
(a) Sun Temple of Modhera (b) Lingaraja Temple of Bhubaneswar (c) Meenakshi Temple of Madurai (d) Pattadakal and Badami temples of the Chalukyas
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Vesara (hybrid) style flourished under the Badami Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, and Hoysalas in Karnataka, blending Dravidian vimana elements with northern Nagara tower profiles.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 6
