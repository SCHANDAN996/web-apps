🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Mnemonic 1: The 8 classical dances
Sentence: "Big Kids Keep Moving, Kids Only Make Sounds"
Big → Bharatanatyam (Tamil Nadu) | Kids → Kathak (North India) | Keep → Kathakali (Kerala) | Moving → Mohiniyattam (Kerala) | Kids → Kuchipudi (Andhra Pradesh) | Only → Odissi (Odisha) | Make → Manipuri (Manipur) | Sounds → Sattriya (Assam)

🧠 Mnemonic 2: Kerala's double
"Kerala's K-M pair": Kathakali and Mohiniyattam — Kerala keeps two. If a question offers Kerala for either of them, it is right.

🧠 Mnemonic 3: Kathak vs Kathakali
"Kathak is short, North is near; Kathakali is long, it goes to the far South."
The shorter name = North India; the longer name = Kerala.

🧠 Mnemonic 4: Sattriya — "Sattra + Sankardev + 2000"
Three S-words in one breath: Sattriya, Sattras (monasteries of Assam), Sankardev. Add the year "2000" as the date of classical status — not the date of birth.

🧠 Mnemonic 5: Four groups of instruments
"Tea, Sugar, And Ghee" — Tata (string), Sushira (wind), Avanaddha (drum), Ghana (solid).

🧠 Mnemonic 6: Instrument masters
"Bismillah blows, Ravi plucks, Shiv strikes, Hari breathes, Amjad strums the sarod."
Bismillah Khan – shehnai | Ravi Shankar – sitar | Shivkumar Sharma – santoor | Hariprasad Chaurasia – flute | Amjad Ali Khan – sarod

🧠 Mnemonic 7: Carnatic Trinity
"T-D-S, all from Tiruvarur" — Tyagaraja, Dikshitar, Syama Shastri. And the "Pitamaha" stands outside the Trinity: Purandara Dasa.

🧠 Mnemonic 8: Folk paintings and states
"My Wise Pet Kitten" — Madhubani (Bihar), Warli (Maharashtra), Pattachitra (Odisha), Kalamkari (Andhra Pradesh).
Extra: "Phad of Rajasthan is a long scroll, like a long Rajasthani road."

🧠 Mnemonic 9: Temple styles
"North curves, South steps, Gate is South, Deccan mixes."
Shikhara – Nagara (north) | Vimana + Gopuram – Dravida (south) | Vesara – Deccan

🧠 Mnemonic 10: Qutb Minar's two builders
"Aibak Aarambh, Iltutmish Itishri" — Aibak began (aarambh), Iltutmish ended (itishri = the end).

🧠 Mnemonic 11: Deccan monuments
"Char-Quli, Gol-Adil" — Charminar by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah; Gol Gumbaz by Muhammad Adil Shah. Both are "Muhammad", so remember the second name.

🧠 Mnemonic 12: Konark in numbers
"24 hours, 7 days" — 24 wheels like the hours of a day, 7 horses like the days of a week. Built by Narasimhadeva I (Eastern Ganga).

🧠 Mnemonic 13: First four World Heritage sites (1983)
"AETA" — Ajanta, Ellora, Taj, Agra Fort. Two caves from Maharashtra, two Mughal buildings from Agra.

🧠 Mnemonic 14: North-East festivals
"Hornbill's Nest in Nagaland, Wangala Wins in Meghalaya" — Hornbill = Nagaland, Wangala = Meghalaya.

⏭️ Skip Strategy

1. If a "match the following" gives four little-known folk dances of one region and you are sure of fewer than two pairs, skip it in the first round — the options are built so that one wrong pair changes everything.

2. If a question asks for the exact UNESCO count "as of" a year you have not studied, do not guess from an old memory. Count only if you know the additions of that year; otherwise skip.

3. If a question names a lesser-known artist and asks for a minor award year, skip it unless you are certain — these are low-return questions in the Foundation exams.
