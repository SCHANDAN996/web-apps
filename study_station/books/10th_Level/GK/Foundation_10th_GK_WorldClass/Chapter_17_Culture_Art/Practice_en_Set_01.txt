Set 1/6 – Indian Culture & Art MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Bharatanatyam is a classical dance of which state?
(a) Kerala (b) Tamil Nadu (c) Karnataka (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bharatanatyam belongs to Tamil Nadu and grew out of the temple dance tradition called Sadir. Kerala's classical dances are Kathakali and Mohiniyattam.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi (list of classical dances)

2. Kathakali is a classical dance-drama of:
(a) Kerala (b) Karnataka (c) Odisha (d) Manipur
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kathakali, with its heavy make-up and large head-dresses, is from Kerala. Trap: the name looks like Kathak, which belongs to North India.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi (list of classical dances)

3. Bihu is the main folk dance and festival of which state?
(a) Meghalaya (b) Tripura (c) Assam (d) Nagaland
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bihu is celebrated and danced in Assam; Rongali (Bohag) Bihu marks the Assamese new year in April.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Garba is a folk dance of:
(a) Rajasthan (b) Maharashtra (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Gujarat
Answer: (d)
Solution: Garba is danced in Gujarat during Navratri; UNESCO added "Garba of Gujarat" to its Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2023. Rajasthan's famous folk dance is Ghoomar.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Bhangra is a popular folk dance of:
(a) Punjab (b) Haryana (c) Himachal Pradesh (d) Jammu and Kashmir
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bhangra (men) and Giddha (women) are the folk dances of Punjab. Nati is from Himachal Pradesh and Rouf from Jammu and Kashmir.
Source: PYQ-style

6. Odissi classical dance originated in:
(a) West Bengal (b) Odisha (c) Assam (d) Bihar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Odissi comes from Odisha and is based on the tribhanga (three-bend) posture. Assam's classical dance is Sattriya.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi (list of classical dances)

7. Ustad Bismillah Khan is famous for playing the:
(a) Sitar (b) Sarod (c) Shehnai (d) Tabla
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bismillah Khan was the great master of the shehnai, a wind instrument. Trap: many "Ustad … Khan" names — Vilayat Khan played the sitar and Amjad Ali Khan the sarod.
Source: PYQ-style

8. Pandit Ravi Shankar is associated with which instrument?
(a) Sitar (b) Santoor (c) Flute (d) Violin
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ravi Shankar was a world-famous sitar player and received the Bharat Ratna in 1999. The santoor is linked with Shivkumar Sharma and the flute with Hariprasad Chaurasia.
Source: PYQ-style

9. The Taj Mahal at Agra was built by:
(a) Akbar (b) Shah Jahan (c) Jahangir (d) Humayun
Answer: (b)
Solution: Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. It was among India's first World Heritage sites (1983).
Source: NCERT Class 7 History

10. The Charminar is located in which city?
(a) Bengaluru (b) Bijapur (c) Lucknow (d) Hyderabad
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Charminar was built in Hyderabad by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah in 1591. Bijapur (Vijayapura) is famous for the Gol Gumbaz.
Source: PYQ-style

11. Madhubani painting is associated with which state?
(a) Odisha (b) Bihar (c) Maharashtra (d) Gujarat
Answer: (b)
Solution: Madhubani (Mithila) painting comes from the Mithila region of Bihar. Warli belongs to Maharashtra and Pattachitra to Odisha.
Source: PYQ-style

12. Pongal is a harvest festival mainly celebrated in:
(a) Kerala (b) Karnataka (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Pongal is the harvest festival of Tamil Nadu. Kerala's harvest festival is Onam.
Source: PYQ-style

13. Onam is the main festival of:
(a) Kerala (b) Tamil Nadu (c) Goa (d) Odisha
Answer: (a)
Solution: Onam is celebrated in Kerala and is linked with the legend of King Mahabali; boat races are part of it.
Source: PYQ-style

14. The Sun Temple of Konark is in which state?
(a) West Bengal (b) Andhra Pradesh (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Odisha
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Konark Sun Temple is in Odisha; it was built in the 13th century by King Narasimhadeva I and appears on the ₹10 note.
Source: whc.unesco.org (Sun Temple, Konârak)

15. The Hornbill Festival is held every year in:
(a) Mizoram (b) Nagaland (c) Manipur (d) Sikkim
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Hornbill Festival is held in Nagaland, from 1 to 10 December, at Kisama near Kohima. Trap: all options are North-Eastern states with tribal festivals.
Source: PYQ-style

16. The Natyashastra, the ancient text on drama, dance and music, is attributed to:
(a) Kalidasa (b) Panini (c) Bharata Muni (d) Valmiki
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Natyashastra is attributed to Bharata Muni. Kalidasa was a poet-dramatist, Panini a grammarian and Valmiki the author of the Ramayana.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

17. The Ajanta Caves, famous for Buddhist wall paintings, are in:
(a) Maharashtra (b) Madhya Pradesh (c) Karnataka (d) Gujarat
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ajanta is in Maharashtra; its paintings show Jataka stories. Trap: Bhimbetka, with prehistoric rock paintings, is in Madhya Pradesh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

18. Pandit Hariprasad Chaurasia is known for playing the:
(a) Tabla (b) Santoor (c) Sarangi (d) Flute
Answer: (d)
Solution: Hariprasad Chaurasia is a master of the bansuri (bamboo flute). The sarangi is linked with Pandit Ram Narayan.
Source: PYQ-style

19. Lavani is a folk dance of:
(a) Gujarat (b) Maharashtra (c) Karnataka (d) Goa
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lavani, performed to the beat of the dholki, is a folk form of Maharashtra. Gujarat's famous dance is Garba.
Source: PYQ-style

20. The Red Fort in Delhi was built by:
(a) Akbar (b) Aurangzeb (c) Shah Jahan (d) Babur
Answer: (c)
Solution: Shah Jahan built the Red Fort in his new capital Shahjahanabad (Delhi); it appears on the ₹500 note.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History

21. How many classical dance forms are recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 10 (d) 12
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Akademi recognises Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, Kuchipudi, Odissi, Manipuri and Sattriya. Counting them gives 8.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi (list of classical dances)

22. The construction of the Qutb Minar was completed by:
(a) Qutbuddin Aibak (b) Alauddin Khalji (c) Iltutmish (d) Balban
Answer: (c)
Solution: Qutbuddin Aibak began the Qutb Minar and his successor Iltutmish completed it. Trap: "built by Aibak" is only half true; the word "completed" points to Iltutmish.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History

23. Which pair of classical dances both belong to Kerala?
(a) Kathakali and Mohiniyattam (b) Kathak and Kathakali (c) Kuchipudi and Mohiniyattam (d) Bharatanatyam and Kathakali
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kathakali and Mohiniyattam are both from Kerala. Kathak is from North India, Kuchipudi from Andhra Pradesh and Bharatanatyam from Tamil Nadu.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi (list of classical dances)

24. The Brihadeshwara Temple at Thanjavur was built by the Chola king:
(a) Rajendra I (b) Rajaraja I (c) Krishnadeva Raya (d) Narasimhavarman II
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rajaraja I built the Brihadeshwara (Rajarajeshvara) temple at Thanjavur. Trap: his son Rajendra I built the temple at Gangaikonda Cholapuram; Narasimhavarman II was a Pallava and Krishnadeva Raya a Vijayanagara king.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History

25. Which of the following was among India's first World Heritage sites, inscribed in 1983?
(a) Qutb Minar (b) Red Fort Complex (c) Ellora Caves (d) Group of Monuments at Hampi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The first four Indian sites (1983) were Ajanta, Ellora, Agra Fort and the Taj Mahal. Hampi was added in 1986, Qutb Minar in 1993 and the Red Fort Complex in 2007.
Source: whc.unesco.org (India)
