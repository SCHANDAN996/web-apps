📊 PYQ Pattern Analysis: Indian Culture & Art

Exams this chapter serves: SSC GD, SSC MTS, RRB Group D, state police / constable exams, and the general-awareness part of other state-level tests.

Note: this is a pattern analysis — what kinds of questions these exams like to ask from this chapter and how they trap you. We do not give year-wise counts or percentages, because no reliable data for them exists. The model questions below are written in the exam style; their sources are the textbooks or official lists they test.

1. What Exams Like to Ask (Sub-topics, in plain words)

Dance and state — the most common one-liner: "Lavani is a folk dance of which state?" (Bihu–Assam, Garba–Gujarat, Ghoomar–Rajasthan, Cheraw–Mizoram, Raut Nacha–Chhattisgarh).

Classical dances — the list of eight, the two Kerala dances, Sattriya of Assam, and "which is NOT a classical dance".

Musicians and instruments — "Bismillah Khan is associated with which instrument?" (shehnai, santoor, sarod, sitar, tabla, flute pairs).

Musicians and dancers by name — a famous person is given, the field or state is asked (Birju Maharaj – Kathak, Kelucharan Mohapatra – Odissi, Rukmini Devi – Bharatanatyam).

Paintings and states — Madhubani, Warli, Pattachitra, Kalamkari, Phad, Gond.

Monuments and builders — Charminar, Gol Gumbaz, Qutb Minar, Buland Darwaza, Brihadeshwara, Konark, Hawa Mahal.

Festivals and states — Hornbill, Bihu, Onam, Pongal, Nuakhai, Wangala, Losar.

UNESCO — the latest site added, the first sites (1983), the count "as of" a year, and Intangible Heritage items such as Garba, Durga Puja and Kumbh Mela.

2. Exam-wise Style (qualitative)

SSC GD / SSC MTS — short, direct one-liners: dance–state, musician–instrument, monument–builder. Speed matters.

RRB Group D — similar direct questions, often with "Which of the following is NOT correctly matched?" pairs.

State police / constable exams — direct facts plus extra weight on the home state's dances, fairs, festivals and monuments.

Higher exams that use these same basics (CHSL, NTPC, CGL) add statement-based questions, current UNESCO inscriptions and award-winning artists.

3. Top 10 High-Yield Model Questions with Solutions and Trap Analysis

Question 1
Lavani is a popular folk dance of:
(a) Gujarat (b) Maharashtra (c) Rajasthan (d) Punjab
Answer: (b) Maharashtra
Trap Type: Similar Name — Gujarat (Garba) and Rajasthan (Ghoomar) are neighbouring western states with famous folk dances.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact. Score maximizer.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 2
Which of the following is NOT one of the eight classical dances recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi?
(a) Sattriya (b) Mohiniyattam (c) Chhau (d) Manipuri
Answer: (c) Chhau
Trap Type: Negative Phrasing — Chhau is on UNESCO's list, so it "feels" classical.
15/45-sec Rule: 45 seconds — read "NOT" twice, then tick the eight in your head.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi

Question 3
Ustad Bismillah Khan is associated with:
(a) Sarod (b) Shehnai (c) Sitar (d) Tabla
Answer: (b) Shehnai
Trap Type: Similar Name — many "Ustad … Khan" names: Amjad Ali Khan (sarod), Vilayat Khan (sitar).
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 4
The Qutb Minar was completed by:
(a) Qutbuddin Aibak (b) Iltutmish (c) Alauddin Khalji (d) Balban
Answer: (b) Iltutmish
Trap Type: Almost True Statement — Aibak began it; the word "completed" decides the answer.
15/45-sec Rule: 15 seconds, if you read the verb carefully.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History

Question 5
Warli painting is associated with which state?
(a) Bihar (b) Odisha (c) Maharashtra (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (c) Maharashtra
Trap Type: Similar Name — Madhubani (Bihar), Pattachitra (Odisha) and Kalamkari (Andhra Pradesh) are the other famous folk paintings.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 6
The Brihadeshwara Temple at Thanjavur was built by:
(a) Rajendra I (b) Rajaraja I (c) Narasimhavarman I (d) Krishna I
Answer: (b) Rajaraja I
Trap Type: Similar Name — Rajendra I was his son and built Gangaikonda Cholapuram.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History

Question 7
Consider the following statements:
I. Sattriya is a classical dance of Assam.
II. Sattriya was created in the 20th century and recognised as classical in 2000.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (a) Only I
Trap Type: Chronological Confusion — recognition came in 2000, but the dance was created by Sankardev in the 15th–16th century.
15/45-sec Rule: 45 seconds — check each statement separately.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi

Question 8
The Hornbill Festival is celebrated in:
(a) Mizoram (b) Manipur (c) Nagaland (d) Meghalaya
Answer: (c) Nagaland
Trap Type: Similar Name — four North-East states with tribal festivals.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 9
Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Charminar – Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah (b) Gol Gumbaz – Muhammad Adil Shah (c) Buland Darwaza – Akbar (d) Hawa Mahal – Shah Jahan
Answer: (d) Hawa Mahal – Shah Jahan
Trap Type: Negative Phrasing — the Hawa Mahal was built by Sawai Pratap Singh (1799).
15/45-sec Rule: 45 seconds — check all four pairs.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 10
As of 2026, which is the only "mixed" (cultural + natural) World Heritage site of India?
(a) Kaziranga National Park (b) Khangchendzonga National Park (c) Western Ghats (d) Sundarbans National Park
Answer: (b) Khangchendzonga National Park
Trap Type: Almost True Statement — the other three are natural sites only.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: whc.unesco.org

4. How to Use This Analysis

Make four lists — dance–state, instrument–player, painting–state, monument–builder. These are the quickest marks in this chapter.

Every time you see "NOT", "except" or "incorrectly matched", slow down — that is a 45-second question, not a 15-second one.

UNESCO counts change every July. Learn the count with its year ("45 as of 2026") and the newest additions; an old guidebook may give an old number.
