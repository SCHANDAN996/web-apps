126. In the Natyashastra, Bharata Muni classified Abhinaya (histrionic representation) into how many types?
(a) Four (b) Six (c) Eight (d) Nine
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bharata Muni divided Abhinaya into four distinct categories: Angika (body gestures), Vachika (speech/song), Aharya (costume/makeup), and Sattvika (involuntary emotional expression).
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

127. The famous 72 Melakarta ragas classification system in Carnatic music was formulated by:
(a) Venkatamakhin (b) Purandara Dasa (c) Tyagaraja (d) Ramamatya
Answer: (a)
Solution: Venkatamakhin formulated the 72 Melakarta (parent raga) system in his 17th-century treatise Chaturdandi Prakasika. Trap: Purandara Dasa is the 'Pitamaha' of Carnatic music.
Source: PYQ-style

128. In the iconic bronze image of Nataraja from the Chola period, what does the crushed dwarf figure beneath Shiva's right foot symbolize?
(a) Apasmara Purusha, symbolizing spiritual ignorance and illusion (b) Yama, the god of death and time (c) Ravana, symbolizing arrogance of strength (d) Mahishasura, the demon of deceit
Answer: (a)
Solution: Shiva's right foot tramples upon the dwarf demon Apasmara Purusha, symbolizing triumph over ignorance (avidya) and ego. The surrounding aureole of flames represents cosmic time.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 7

129. Which ancient rock-cut cave in the Barabar Hills (Bihar), dedicated to the Ajivika sect, features the earliest known stone chaitya arch facade?
(a) Lomas Rishi Cave (b) Sudama Cave (c) Karle Chaitya Cave (d) Bhaja Cave
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lomas Rishi Cave in the Barabar Hills (Jehanabad district, Bihar) was excavated during the Mauryan period (3rd century BC) for Ajivika ascetics; its facade imitates contemporary wooden curved roofs.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 2

130. The "Lost-wax technique" (Cire-perdue), famously used for casting Chola bronzes and the Mohenjo-daro Dancing Girl, is known in traditional Indian terminology as:
(a) Madhuchhishtavidhana (b) Shilparatnakara (c) Vastushastra (d) Chitrasutra
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Sanskrit text Manasara describes the lost-wax casting process as Madhuchhishtavidhana ('madhuchhishta' means beeswax).
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 7

131. Consider the following statements about the Gandhara and Mathura schools of ancient Indian sculpture:
I. Gandhara sculptures were predominantly carved out of grey or bluish schist stone.
II. Mathura school sculptures predominantly used spotted red sandstone from Sikri.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Both I and II (b) Only I (c) Only II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (a)
Solution: Under the Kushana Empire, both schools flourished: Gandhara in the northwest used grey schist stone with Greco-Roman drapery, while Mathura used indigenous spotted red sandstone with spiritual indigenous features.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 3

132. The "Attakkatha" refers to the literary text and script used in which traditional dance-drama form of Kerala?
(a) Mohiniyattam (b) Kathakali (c) Koodiyattam (d) Ottan Thullal
Answer: (b)
Solution: The literature of Kathakali is called Attakkatha, written in Manipravalam (a hybrid of Sanskrit and Malayalam) dramatizing stories from the Mahabharata and Ramayana. Trap: Koodiyattam uses pure Sanskrit plays.
Source: PYQ-style

133. The 10 Thaat classification system of Hindustani classical music was organized and standardized in the modern era by:
(a) Pandit Vishnu Digambar Paluskar (b) Pandit Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande (c) Ustad Faiyaz Khan (d) Pandit Omkarnath Thakur
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pandit V. N. Bhatkhande classified Hindustani ragas into 10 parent scales called Thaats (Bilawal, Kalyan, Khamaj, Kafi, Asavari, Bhairav, Bhairavi, Todi, Poorvi, Marwa).
Source: PYQ-style

134. Which of the following traditional theatres is the ONLY surviving Sanskrit theatre tradition, recognized by UNESCO as Masterpiece of Oral and Intangible Heritage in 2008?
(a) Yakshagana (b) Koodiyattam (c) Therukoothu (d) Mudiyettu
Answer: (b)
Solution: Koodiyattam of Kerala is over 1800 years old and is the only surviving specimen of ancient Sanskrit theatre in India, performed in temple theatres called Koothambalams.
Source: PYQ-style

135. In Kalinga (Odisha) temple architecture, the sanctum tower with a curvilinear vertical profile is called:
(a) Pidha Deula (b) Rekha Deula (c) Khakhara Deula (d) Jagamohana
Answer: (b)
Solution: In Odishan temples, the main sanctum tower is the curvilinear Rekha Deula, while the stepped pyramidal assembly hall is the Pidha Deula (or Jagamohana), and barrel-vaulted shrines are Khakhara Deula.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 6

136. In 2023, the "Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas" was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site. It includes temples at which three locations in Karnataka?
(a) Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal (b) Belur, Halebidu and Somanathapura (c) Hampi, Bijapur and Bidar (d) Sringeri, Udupi and Gokarna
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Hoysala ensemble comprises the Chennakeshava Temple at Belur, the Hoysaleswara Temple at Halebidu, and the Keshava Temple at Somanathapura, noted for stellate (star-shaped) platforms and soapstone carvings.
Source: PYQ-style

137. Which festival of Uttarakhand, celebrated in the village of Saloor Dungra with mask dances honouring the deity Bhumiyal, was inscribed on UNESCO's ICH list in 2009?
(a) Phool Dei (b) Ramman (c) Nanda Devi Raj Jat (d) Harela
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ramman is a unique religious festival and ritual theatre celebrated by the villagers of Saloor Dungra in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand.
Source: PYQ-style

138. The ancient treatise "Abhinaya Darpana", a fundamental text on classical dance gestures and postures, was composed by:
(a) Bharata Muni (b) Nandikeshvara (c) Sharngadeva (d) Matanga Muni
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nandikeshvara authored the Abhinaya Darpana, detailing hastas (single- and double-hand gestures), head movements (shiro bheda), and eye movements (drishti bheda) used extensively in Bharatanatyam.
Source: PYQ-style

139. Which of the following is an example of an open-air rock relief sculpture in Tamil Nadu that depicts celestial beings, animals, and ascetics witnessing the descent of the Ganga or Arjuna's penance?
(a) Udayagiri rock relief of Varaha (b) Elephanta Maheshmurti panel (c) Descent of the Ganga / Arjuna's Penance at Mahabalipuram (d) Kailasa temple relief at Ellora
Answer: (c)
Solution: Carved on two massive granite boulders at Mahabalipuram under the Pallavas, this gigantic open-air bas-relief depicts the myth of Bhagiratha bringing the river Ganga down to Earth.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 6

140. Mudiyettu, inscribed on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2010, is a traditional ritual dance-drama of Kerala based on the mythological battle between:
(a) Lord Rama and Ravana (b) Lord Krishna and Kamsa (c) Goddess Kali and the demon Darika (d) Lord Shiva and Tripurasura
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mudiyettu is enacted in Kali temples of Kerala (Bhagavathi Kavus) depicting the fierce duel between Goddess Bhadrakali and the demon king Darika.
Source: PYQ-style

141. The Brihaddesi, a landmark musicological text from the 6th to 8th century that defined the word "Raga" for the first time in Indian music, was written by:
(a) Bharata Muni (b) Sharngadeva (c) Matanga Muni (d) Venkatamakhin
Answer: (c)
Solution: Matanga Muni in his treatise Brihaddesi gave the earliest formal definition of the musical concept of Raga ("that which colors the mind of listeners").
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

142. The "Tarana" in Hindustani music, characterized by rhythmic mnemonic syllables such as 'ta-na-na' and 'dhir-dhir', is widely attributed to the innovation of:
(a) Swami Haridas (b) Tansen (c) Amir Khusrau (d) Raja Man Singh Tomar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Amir Khusrau, court poet of the Delhi Sultanate, is credited with originating musical forms like Tarana, Qawwali, and the classical style of Khayal.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

143. Which school of Rajasthani miniature painting is famous for depicting hunting scenes, dense foliage, and night skies with deep green backgrounds, heavily developed in Hadoti region?
(a) Kishangarh school (b) Mewar school (c) Kota and Bundi schools (d) Bikaner school
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Bundi and Kota schools in the Hadoti region of southeastern Rajasthan are celebrated for their vibrant portrayal of nature, thick forests, wildlife, and hunting expeditions.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

144. The famous bronze image of the "Dancing Girl" discovered at Mohenjo-daro belongs to which archaeological age and period?
(a) Copper Age, Early Harappan period (b) Iron Age, Vedic period (c) Bronze Age, Mature Harappan period (d) Stone Age, Neolithic period
Answer: (c)
Solution: Found at Mohenjo-daro by Ernest Mackay in 1926, the 10.5-cm lost-wax bronze Dancing Girl dates to around 2500 BC during the Mature Harappan (Bronze Age) civilization.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 2

145. In ancient Indian temple architecture, the architectural term "Vimana" in the Dravidian style specifically refers to:
(a) The colossal entrance gateway to the temple complex (b) The pillared open audience hall for public rituals (c) The water tank situated within the temple courtyard (d) The entire mountain-like stepped pyramidal tower rising directly above the sanctum sanctorum
Answer: (d)
Solution: In Dravidian architecture, the main sanctum tower rising in progressive tiers (talas) is called the Vimana. Trap: the grand multi-storeyed entrance gate is the Gopuram.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 6

146. Which of the following pairs of ancient Indian texts and their primary subject matter is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) Natyashastra - Performing arts, theatre, music, and dance (b) Sangeeta Ratnakara - Treatise on Indian classical music traditions (c) Manasara - Vastu shastra and architectural principles (d) Silappadikaram - Treatise on Buddhist temple stone architecture
Answer: (d)
Solution: Silappadikaram is one of the Five Great Epics of Sangam Tamil literature, composed by Ilango Adigal (telling the story of Kannagi and Kovalan), not an architectural treatise.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

147. Rabindranath Tagore founded Visva-Bharati in 1921 at which site in West Bengal, which was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2023?

148. Consider the following classical music treatises in chronological order:
I. Natyashastra of Bharata Muni
II. Brihaddesi of Matanga Muni
III. Sangeeta Ratnakara of Sharngadeva
IV. Chaturdandi Prakasika of Venkatamakhin
Which sequence correctly represents their historical chronological order from earliest to latest?
(a) II, I, III, IV (b) I, III, II, IV (c) III, I, II, IV (d) I, II, III, IV
Answer: (d)
Solution: Natyashastra (circa 2nd century BC–2nd century AD), Brihaddesi (circa 6th–8th century AD), Sangeeta Ratnakara (13th century AD), and Chaturdandi Prakasika (17th century AD).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts

149. The famous Sun Temple of Modhera in Gujarat, featuring a magnificent stepped Surya Kund reservoir, was constructed during the reign of which dynasty?
(a) Gurjara-Pratihara dynasty (b) Paramara dynasty (c) Rashtrakuta dynasty (d) Solanki (Chalukya of Gujarat) dynasty
Answer: (d)
Solution: King Bhima I of the Solanki (Chalukya) dynasty built the Sun Temple at Modhera in 1026–1027 AD. It represents the Maru-Gurjara architectural style.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art, Ch 6

150. "Kalamezhuthu", an ancient ritual art form where sacred floor designs and cosmic deities are drawn using five natural pigment powders, belongs to:
(a) Tamil Nadu (b) Karnataka (c) Andhra Pradesh (d) Kerala
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kalamezhuthu is practiced in temples and sacred groves (Kavus) of Kerala; natural powders made from rice flour (white), charcoal (black), turmeric (yellow), green leaves (green), and lime-turmeric mix (red) are used.
Source: PYQ-style
