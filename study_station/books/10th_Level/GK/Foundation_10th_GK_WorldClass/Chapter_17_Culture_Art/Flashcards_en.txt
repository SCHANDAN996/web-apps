🃏 Flashcard Deck: Indian Culture & Art (Foundation)

Card 1
Front: One state, two classical dances. Which state, and which dances?
Back: Kerala — Kathakali and Mohiniyattam.

Card 2
Front: An old dance from Assam's monasteries became "classical" only in 2000. Which dance, and who created it?
Back: Sattriya — created by Srimanta Sankardev in the sattras (Vaishnava monasteries) of Assam.

Card 3
Front: A dancer balances on the rim of a brass plate. Which classical dance, and which state?
Back: Kuchipudi (the Tarangam item) — Andhra Pradesh.

Card 4
Front: Which ancient text is the root of Indian dance and music, and who is it credited to?
Back: The Natyashastra — Bharata Muni.

Card 5
Front: The Natyashastra sorts instruments into four groups. Name them.
Back: Tata (string), Sushira (wind), Avanaddha (skin drums), Ghana (solid, struck).

Card 6
Front: Who is the "father" of Carnatic music, and who form its "Trinity"?
Back: Purandara Dasa; Trinity — Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Shastri.

Card 7
Front: Mnemonic check: "Bismillah blows, Ravi plucks, Shiv strikes" — decode it.
Back: Bismillah Khan – shehnai; Ravi Shankar – sitar; Shivkumar Sharma – santoor.

Card 8
Front: Who was the first musician to receive the Bharat Ratna, and in which year?
Back: M. S. Subbulakshmi, 1998.

Card 9
Front: India's oldest paintings are not in a palace but in rock shelters. Where?
Back: Bhimbetka, Madhya Pradesh (World Heritage site since 2003).

Card 10
Front: Which caves hold Buddhist, Hindu and Jain shrines together, and what is cut out of a single rock there?
Back: Ellora (Maharashtra) — the Kailasa temple, made under the Rashtrakuta king Krishna I.

Card 11
Front: White stick figures on mud walls — which painting and which state? Fish and flowers in bright colours from Mithila — which state?
Back: Warli — Maharashtra; Madhubani — Bihar.

Card 12
Front: Mnemonic check: "North curves, South steps, Gate is South" — what does it teach?
Back: Nagara temples have a curved shikhara; Dravida temples have a stepped vimana and tall gopurams (gateways).

Card 13
Front: A temple shaped like the Sun god's chariot. Which temple, who built it, how many wheels and horses?
Back: Konark Sun Temple, Odisha — Narasimhadeva I (Eastern Ganga), 13th century — 24 wheels, 7 horses.

Card 14
Front: Examiner's trap: "Qutbuddin Aibak built the Qutb Minar." What is missing?
Back: Aibak only began it; Iltutmish completed it (Almost True Statement).

Card 15
Front: Examiner's trap: "Chhau is one of India's 8 classical dances." True or false?
Back: False. Chhau (Odisha, Jharkhand, West Bengal) is a famous folk/martial dance on UNESCO's list, but not one of the 8 classical dances.

Card 16
Front: How many World Heritage sites did India have in 2026, and which is the only mixed site?
Back: 45 (37 cultural, 7 natural, 1 mixed); the mixed site is Khangchendzonga National Park, Sikkim.

Card 17
Front: Which four Indian sites were the first to enter the World Heritage list, and when?
Back: Ajanta, Ellora, Agra Fort and Taj Mahal — 1983.

Card 18
Front: Charminar and Gol Gumbaz — who built each?
Back: Charminar — Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah (Hyderabad, 1591); Gol Gumbaz — Muhammad Adil Shah (Bijapur).

Card 19
Front: A festival named after a bird is held every December in Nagaland. Which festival?
Back: The Hornbill Festival (1–10 December, Kisama, near Kohima).
