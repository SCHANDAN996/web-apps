Set 2/6 – Biology: Human Body MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Who discovered the nucleus of the cell?
(a) Robert Hooke (b) Rudolf Virchow (c) Robert Brown (d) Matthias Schleiden
Answer: (c)
Solution: Robert Brown discovered the nucleus in 1831. Trap: Robert Hooke has a similar first name, but he named the cell in 1665.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

27. Who stated that all cells arise from pre-existing cells?
(a) Robert Hooke (b) Rudolf Virchow (c) Theodor Schwann (d) Robert Brown
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rudolf Virchow (1855) added this idea to the cell theory. Schleiden and Schwann had earlier proposed that all plants and animals are made of cells.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

28. Which cell organelles are known as the suicide bags of the cell?
(a) Lysosomes (b) Ribosomes (c) Mitochondria (d) Plastids
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lysosomes contain powerful digestive enzymes; when the cell is damaged they may burst and digest the cell itself, hence the name suicide bags.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

29. Proteins are made in the cell on the:
(a) Lysosomes (b) Golgi apparatus (c) Vacuoles (d) Ribosomes
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ribosomes are the sites of protein synthesis. Rough endoplasmic reticulum looks rough because ribosomes are attached to it.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

30. Which of the following is present in a plant cell but absent in an animal cell?
(a) Nucleus (b) Cell wall (c) Mitochondria (d) Cell membrane
Answer: (b)
Solution: Plant cells have a rigid cell wall outside the cell membrane; animal cells do not. Nucleus, mitochondria and cell membrane are found in both.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

31. The cell wall of plant cells is mainly made of:
(a) Starch (b) Protein (c) Cellulose (d) Glycogen
Answer: (c)
Solution: The plant cell wall is mainly composed of cellulose, which gives strength to the plant. Glycogen is a storage carbohydrate in animals.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

32. Chromosomes, which carry hereditary information, are made up of:
(a) DNA and protein (b) Starch and fat (c) Cellulose (d) Glucose
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chromosomes are found in the nucleus and are made of DNA and protein. DNA carries the information for inheritance of features from parents to the next generation.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

33. Which cell organelle stores, modifies and packages materials and also forms lysosomes?
(a) Mitochondria (b) Nucleus (c) Ribosome (d) Golgi apparatus
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Golgi apparatus packages and dispatches materials to various places in and out of the cell, and it is also involved in forming lysosomes.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

34. Blood is which type of tissue?
(a) Epithelial tissue (b) Fluid connective tissue (c) Muscular tissue (d) Nervous tissue
Answer: (b)
Solution: Blood has a fluid matrix called plasma in which cells float, so it is a fluid connective tissue. It connects and transports materials between parts of the body.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Tissues)

35. A tendon connects:
(a) Muscle to bone (b) Bone to bone (c) Muscle to muscle (d) Bone to cartilage
Answer: (a)
Solution: A tendon is a tough connective tissue that joins a muscle to a bone. Trap: a ligament, the similar-sounding term, joins bone to bone.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Tissues)

36. A ligament connects:
(a) Muscle to bone (b) Nerve to muscle (c) Bone to bone (d) Skin to muscle
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ligaments are elastic connective tissues that join two bones. Remember "LBB – TMB": Ligament Bone-Bone, Tendon Muscle-Bone.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Tissues)

37. Which type of muscle is found in the heart and keeps contracting rhythmically throughout life?
(a) Smooth muscle (b) Skeletal muscle (c) Voluntary muscle (d) Cardiac muscle
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cardiac muscles are involuntary and show rhythmic contraction and relaxation throughout life. Skeletal muscles are voluntary.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Tissues)

38. The muscles in the walls of the alimentary canal, which we cannot control at will, are:
(a) Striated muscles (b) Smooth muscles (c) Cardiac muscles (d) Skeletal muscles
Answer: (b)
Solution: Smooth (unstriated) muscles are involuntary and line organs such as the alimentary canal and blood vessels. Cardiac muscle is found only in the heart.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Tissues)

39. Which is the longest cell in the human body?
(a) Nerve cell (neuron) (b) Red blood cell (c) White blood cell (d) Muscle cell of the heart
Answer: (a)
Solution: A nerve cell (neuron) with its long axon is the longest cell of the human body. Red blood cells are tiny disc-shaped cells.
Source: PYQ-style

40. Salivary amylase present in saliva begins the digestion of:
(a) Proteins (b) Fats (c) Starch (d) Vitamins
Answer: (c)
Solution: Salivary amylase breaks down starch into simple sugar in the mouth. Protein digestion begins in the stomach with pepsin.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

41. Which acid is present in the gastric juice of the stomach?
(a) Sulphuric acid (b) Hydrochloric acid (c) Nitric acid (d) Acetic acid
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gastric glands release hydrochloric acid, which makes the medium acidic for pepsin to act and helps kill germs.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

42. What is the role of mucus secreted in the stomach?
(a) It digests proteins (b) It digests fats (c) It makes the food alkaline (d) It protects the inner lining of the stomach from the acid
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mucus forms a protective layer over the inner lining of the stomach so that the acid does not damage it. Pepsin, not mucus, digests proteins.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

43. Bile is stored in the:
(a) Gall bladder (b) Liver (c) Pancreas (d) Small intestine
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bile is made by the liver and stored in the gall bladder until it is released into the small intestine. Trap: the liver makes bile but does not store it.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

44. What does bile do in digestion?
(a) Digests proteins with the help of an enzyme (b) Converts starch into sugar (c) Breaks large fat globules into small droplets (d) Absorbs water from food
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bile salts emulsify fats, breaking large globules into smaller droplets so that lipase can act on them. Bile contains no digestive enzyme.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

45. Trypsin, an enzyme that digests proteins, is secreted by the:
(a) Stomach (b) Pancreas (c) Liver (d) Large intestine
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pancreatic juice contains trypsin for digesting proteins and lipase for fats. Trap: pepsin, the other protein-digesting enzyme, comes from the stomach.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

46. Finger-like projections called villi, which increase the surface area for absorption, are found in the:
(a) Stomach (b) Oesophagus (c) Large intestine (d) Small intestine
Answer: (d)
Solution: The inner lining of the small intestine has numerous villi that increase the surface area for absorbing digested food.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

47. Which is the longest part of the human alimentary canal?
(a) Small intestine (b) Large intestine (c) Oesophagus (d) Stomach
Answer: (a)
Solution: The small intestine is the longest part of the alimentary canal. It completes digestion and absorbs digested food.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

48. What is the main function of the large intestine?
(a) Digestion of proteins (b) Production of bile (c) Absorption of excess water (d) Secretion of insulin
Answer: (c)
Solution: The large intestine absorbs extra water from the undigested food; the rest is removed from the body as faeces.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Life Processes)

49. Consider the following statements:
Statement I: Mitochondria are known as the powerhouse of the cell.
Statement II: Plastids are found in animal cells.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I only (b) II only (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mitochondria release energy as ATP, so Statement I is correct. Plastids are present only in plant cells, so Statement II is wrong.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)

50. Smooth endoplasmic reticulum helps in the manufacture of:
(a) Proteins (b) Fat molecules (lipids) (c) Starch (d) DNA
Answer: (b)
Solution: Smooth ER helps manufacture fat molecules (lipids). Proteins are made on ribosomes attached to rough ER.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (The Fundamental Unit of Life)
