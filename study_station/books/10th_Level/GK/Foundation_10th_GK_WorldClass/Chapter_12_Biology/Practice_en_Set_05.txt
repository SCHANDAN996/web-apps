Set 5/6 – Biology: Human Body MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard

101. What is the chemical name of Vitamin C?
(a) Retinol (b) Ascorbic acid (c) Calciferol (d) Thiamine
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vitamin C is ascorbic acid. Retinol is Vitamin A, calciferol is Vitamin D and thiamine is Vitamin B1.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Retinol is the chemical name of which vitamin?
(a) Vitamin D (b) Vitamin K (c) Vitamin E (d) Vitamin A
Answer: (d)
Solution: Retinol is Vitamin A, which is needed for good eyesight; its deficiency causes night blindness.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Beriberi is caused by the deficiency of:
(a) Vitamin B1 (thiamine) (b) Vitamin B12 (c) Vitamin C (d) Vitamin D
Answer: (a)
Solution: Beriberi results from a lack of Vitamin B1 (thiamine). Vitamin B12 deficiency causes pernicious anaemia.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science (Components of Food)

104. Pellagra is caused by the deficiency of:
(a) Vitamin A (b) Vitamin K (c) Niacin (Vitamin B3) (d) Iron
Answer: (c)
Solution: Pellagra, which affects the skin, digestion and nerves, is caused by a lack of niacin, a member of the Vitamin B group (B3).
Source: PYQ-style

105. Which vitamin contains the metal cobalt?
(a) Vitamin B1 (b) Vitamin B12 (c) Vitamin C (d) Vitamin K
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vitamin B12 (cobalamin) contains cobalt. Trap: iron is the metal in haemoglobin, not in any vitamin.
Source: PYQ-style

106. Which vitamin is essential for the normal clotting of blood?
(a) Vitamin A (b) Vitamin C (c) Vitamin D (d) Vitamin K
Answer: (d)
Solution: Vitamin K helps blood to clot; its deficiency delays clotting. Platelets also help in clotting, but among vitamins the answer is K.
Source: PYQ-style

107. Which vitamin is made by the human skin in the presence of sunlight?
(a) Vitamin D (b) Vitamin A (c) Vitamin E (d) Vitamin B12
Answer: (a)
Solution: The skin makes Vitamin D when exposed to sunlight; this vitamin helps the body use calcium for bones and teeth.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science (Components of Food)

108. Deficiency of iron in the diet causes:
(a) Goitre (b) Rickets (c) Anaemia (d) Night blindness
Answer: (c)
Solution: Iron is needed to make haemoglobin; its shortage causes anaemia. Goitre is caused by lack of iodine.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science (Components of Food)

109. Kwashiorkor in children is mainly caused by the deficiency of:
(a) Carbohydrates (b) Proteins (c) Fats (d) Vitamin C
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kwashiorkor is a protein-deficiency disease of children, marked by swelling of the body. A shortage of both protein and calories causes marasmus.
Source: PYQ-style

110. Marasmus in children is caused by the deficiency of:
(a) Both proteins and calories (energy) (b) Only Vitamin A (c) Only iodine (d) Only calcium
Answer: (a)
Solution: Marasmus occurs when a child's diet lacks both proteins and energy-giving food over a long time, leading to severe wasting.
Source: PYQ-style

111. Which of the following diseases is caused by bacteria?
(a) Polio (b) Measles (c) Influenza (d) Typhoid
Answer: (d)
Solution: Typhoid is a bacterial disease. Polio, measles and influenza are caused by viruses.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Why Do We Fall Ill)

112. AIDS is caused by:
(a) A bacterium (b) A protozoan (c) A virus called HIV (d) A fungus
Answer: (c)
Solution: AIDS is caused by the Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV), which damages the body's immune system.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Why Do We Fall Ill)

113. Dengue is spread by the bite of which mosquito?
(a) Aedes (b) Culex (c) Female Anopheles (d) Housefly
Answer: (a)
Solution: The dengue virus is carried by the Aedes mosquito, which breeds in clean stagnant water. Female Anopheles spreads malaria.
Source: PYQ-style

114. Filaria (elephantiasis) is spread by which mosquito?
(a) Aedes (b) Culex (c) Female Anopheles (d) Sandfly
Answer: (b)
Solution: Filarial worms are spread by the Culex mosquito. Remember "A-M, A-D, C-F": Anopheles–Malaria, Aedes–Dengue, Culex–Filaria.
Source: PYQ-style

115. Kala-azar is spread by the:
(a) Housefly (b) Tsetse fly (c) Rat flea (d) Sandfly
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kala-azar is caused by the protozoan Leishmania and is spread by the sandfly. The tsetse fly spreads sleeping sickness.
Source: PYQ-style

116. Sleeping sickness is spread by the:
(a) Sandfly (b) Culex mosquito (c) Tsetse fly (d) Housefly
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sleeping sickness is caused by the protozoan Trypanosoma and spread by the tsetse fly. The sandfly spreads kala-azar.
Source: PYQ-style

117. Plague spreads to human beings mainly through the bite of the:
(a) Rat flea (b) Mosquito (c) Sandfly (d) Tsetse fly
Answer: (a)
Solution: Plague is a bacterial disease that spreads from infected rats to human beings through the bite of rat fleas.
Source: PYQ-style

118. Ringworm, a common skin infection, is caused by a:
(a) Virus (b) Fungus (c) Protozoan (d) Worm
Answer: (b)
Solution: Despite its name, ringworm is a fungal skin infection, not a worm. Trap: the word "worm" in the name.
Source: PYQ-style

119. Antibiotics are effective against diseases caused by:
(a) Viruses (b) Worms (c) All microbes equally (d) Bacteria
Answer: (d)
Solution: Antibiotics block biochemical pathways that bacteria need; viruses do not use these pathways, so antibiotics do not work against viral diseases such as the common cold.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Why Do We Fall Ill)

120. Who discovered penicillin, the first antibiotic?
(a) Edward Jenner (b) Louis Pasteur (c) Alexander Fleming (d) Robert Koch
Answer: (c)
Solution: Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin in 1928. Trap: Edward Jenner is known for the smallpox vaccine (1796), not an antibiotic.
Source: PYQ-style

121. Which of the following pairs of vitamin and deficiency disease is correctly matched?
(a) Vitamin B1 – Scurvy (b) Vitamin D – Night blindness (c) Vitamin B12 – Pernicious anaemia (d) Vitamin K – Rickets
Answer: (c)
Solution: Vitamin B12 deficiency causes pernicious anaemia. The correct pairs for the others are B1–beriberi, D–rickets, K–delayed clotting, and A–night blindness.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Which of the following is NOT a viral disease?
(a) Measles (b) Tuberculosis (c) Chickenpox (d) Rabies
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tuberculosis is caused by a bacterium; measles, chickenpox and rabies are viral diseases. Trap: the word NOT reverses the usual question.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Why Do We Fall Ill)

123. Consider the following statements:
Statement I: Malaria is caused by a virus that is spread by the female Anopheles mosquito.
Statement II: Dengue is spread by the Aedes mosquito.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) I only (b) II only (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement I is an almost-correct trap: the vector is right, but malaria is caused by the protozoan Plasmodium, not a virus. Statement II is correct.
Source: PYQ-style

124. Which of the following pairs of pathogen and disease is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) Plasmodium – Malaria (b) Leishmania – Kala-azar (c) Entamoeba – Amoebic dysentery (d) Trypanosoma – Typhoid
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trypanosoma causes sleeping sickness; typhoid is caused by a bacterium. The other three pairs are correct.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Arrange the following events in chronological order: discovery of penicillin; development of the smallpox vaccine; declaration of smallpox eradication by WHO.
(a) Penicillin, smallpox vaccine, smallpox eradication (b) Smallpox vaccine, penicillin, smallpox eradication (c) Smallpox vaccine, smallpox eradication, penicillin (d) Smallpox eradication, penicillin, smallpox vaccine
Answer: (b)
Solution: Jenner developed the smallpox vaccine in 1796, Fleming discovered penicillin in 1928, and WHO declared smallpox eradicated in 1980.
Source: PYQ-style
