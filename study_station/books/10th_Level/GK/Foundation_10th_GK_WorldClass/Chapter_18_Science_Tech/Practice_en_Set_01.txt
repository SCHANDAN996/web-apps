Set 1/6 – Science & Technology MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Who invented the telephone?
(a) Thomas Alva Edison (b) Alexander Graham Bell (c) Guglielmo Marconi (d) John Logie Baird
Answer: (b)
Solution: Alexander Graham Bell patented the telephone in 1876. Trap: Edison is linked with the electric bulb, Marconi with radio and Baird with television.
Source: PYQ-style

2. The practical incandescent electric bulb is credited to:
(a) Michael Faraday (b) James Watt (c) Thomas Alva Edison (d) Alessandro Volta
Answer: (c)
Solution: Thomas Alva Edison developed the practical incandescent lamp in 1879. Faraday is known for electromagnetic induction and Volta for the electric battery.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Penicillin, the first antibiotic, was discovered by:
(a) Louis Pasteur (b) Edward Jenner (c) Alexander Fleming (d) Robert Koch
Answer: (c)
Solution: Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin in 1928. Trap: Pasteur made the rabies vaccine and Jenner the smallpox vaccine — both are linked with medicine, but not with penicillin.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science, Microorganisms

4. The SI unit of force is:
(a) joule (b) newton (c) watt (d) pascal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Force is measured in newton (N). Joule is the unit of work/energy, watt of power and pascal of pressure.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Force and Laws of Motion

5. The SI unit of power is:
(a) watt (b) joule (c) volt (d) ampere
Answer: (a)
Solution: Power is the rate of doing work, and its SI unit is the watt (W), equal to one joule per second.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Work and Energy

6. The SI unit of electric current is:
(a) volt (b) ohm (c) ampere (d) coulomb
Answer: (c)
Solution: Electric current is measured in ampere (A), which is one of the seven SI base units. Volt is for potential difference, ohm for resistance and coulomb for charge.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Electricity

7. Which part is called the "brain" of the computer?
(a) Monitor (b) Hard disk (c) CPU (d) Keyboard
Answer: (c)
Solution: The CPU (Central Processing Unit) processes all instructions and controls the other parts, so it is called the brain of the computer.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

8. One byte is equal to how many bits?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 16 (d) 1024
Answer: (b)
Solution: A group of 4 bits is a nibble and 1024 bytes make a kilobyte. One byte is 8 bits.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

9. Which of the following is an input device?
(a) Monitor (b) Printer (c) Speaker (d) Keyboard
Answer: (d)
Solution: A keyboard sends data into the computer, so it is an input device. Monitor, printer and speaker give results out, so they are output devices.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

10. The headquarters of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is in:
(a) Bengaluru (b) Sriharikota (c) Thiruvananthapuram (d) Ahmedabad
Answer: (a)
Solution: ISRO's headquarters is in Bengaluru. Trap: Sriharikota is the launch centre, Thiruvananthapuram has the Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre and Ahmedabad has the Space Applications Centre.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

11. India's first satellite was:
(a) Bhaskara (b) Rohini (c) Aryabhata (d) INSAT-1A
Answer: (c)
Solution: Aryabhata, launched on 19 April 1975, was India's first satellite. Rohini was the satellite placed in orbit by India's own SLV-3 in 1980.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

12. Who was the first Indian to go into space?
(a) Kalpana Chawla (b) Rakesh Sharma (c) Sunita Williams (d) Shubhanshu Shukla
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rakesh Sharma flew on the Soviet Soyuz T-11 on 3 April 1984. Trap: Kalpana Chawla was the first woman of Indian origin in space (1997), and Shubhanshu Shukla went to the ISS in 2025.
Source: PYQ-style

13. Who is known as the father of India's space programme?
(a) Homi J. Bhabha (b) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (c) Vikram Sarabhai (d) Satish Dhawan
Answer: (c)
Solution: Vikram Sarabhai led INCOSPAR and the founding of ISRO, and is called the father of the Indian space programme. Homi Bhabha is the father of India's nuclear programme.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

14. Who is known as the father of India's nuclear programme?
(a) Homi J. Bhabha (b) C. V. Raman (c) Meghnad Saha (d) Vikram Sarabhai
Answer: (a)
Solution: Homi J. Bhabha was the first chairman of the Atomic Energy Commission (1948) and founded TIFR; BARC is named after him.
Source: PYQ-style

15. National Science Day is celebrated in India on:
(a) 11 May (b) 28 February (c) 23 August (d) 22 December
Answer: (b)
Solution: 11 May is Technology Day, 23 August is Space Day and 22 December is Mathematics Day. National Science Day falls on 28 February, the day C. V. Raman announced the Raman Effect.
Source: PYQ-style

16. The Raman Effect is related to:
(a) Scattering of light (b) Reflection of sound (c) Radioactivity (d) Magnetism
Answer: (a)
Solution: C. V. Raman showed that light changes its wavelength when it is scattered by molecules — the Raman Effect. He won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930.
Source: PYQ-style

17. Who is popularly called the "Missile Man of India"?
(a) Vikram Sarabhai (b) Homi J. Bhabha (c) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (d) Satish Dhawan
Answer: (c)
Solution: A. P. J. Abdul Kalam led India's missile programme (IGMDP) and was project director of SLV-3; he later became the 11th President of India.
Source: PYQ-style

18. The first powered aeroplane flight (1903) was made by:
(a) Wright brothers (b) Montgolfier brothers (c) Lumière brothers (d) Graham Bell
Answer: (a)
Solution: Orville and Wilbur Wright made the first powered aeroplane flight in 1903. Trap: the Montgolfier brothers flew hot-air balloons, and the Lumière brothers are known for early cinema.
Source: PYQ-style

19. X-rays were discovered by:
(a) Henri Becquerel (b) Wilhelm Röntgen (c) Marie Curie (d) J. J. Thomson
Answer: (b)
Solution: Wilhelm Röntgen discovered X-rays in 1895. Becquerel discovered radioactivity (1896) and Thomson the electron (1897).
Source: PYQ-style

20. Satish Dhawan Space Centre, India's main launch site, is at:
(a) Thumba, Kerala (b) Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh (c) Chandipur, Odisha (d) Bengaluru, Karnataka
Answer: (b)
Solution: SDSC SHAR is at Sriharikota in Andhra Pradesh. Thumba was the site of India's first sounding rocket launch in 1963.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

21. Chandrayaan-3's Vikram lander made a soft landing on the Moon on:
(a) 14 July 2023 (b) 23 August 2023 (c) 2 September 2023 (d) 22 July 2019
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chandrayaan-3 was launched on 14 July 2023 and landed near the lunar south pole on 23 August 2023. Trap type: chronological confusion between launch date and landing date.
Source: ISRO / PIB, 2023

22. Which of the following is NOT an SI base unit?
(a) kelvin (b) candela (c) newton (d) mole
Answer: (c)
Solution: The seven base units are metre, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole and candela. Newton is a derived unit (kg m s^-2). Trap type: negative phrasing.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics, Units and Measurement

23. India's Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) was launched in 2013 by:
(a) PSLV-C25 (b) SLV-3 (c) LVM3-M4 (d) GSLV Mk III-M1
Answer: (a)
Solution: LVM3-M4 launched Chandrayaan-3 and GSLV Mk III-M1 launched Chandrayaan-2. The Mars Orbiter Mission lifted off on 5 November 2013 on PSLV-C25.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

24. National Technology Day (11 May) commemorates:
(a) Launch of Aryabhata (b) Pokhran-II nuclear tests of 1998 (c) Formation of ISRO (d) Landing of Chandrayaan-3
Answer: (b)
Solution: On 11 May 1998 India carried out the Pokhran-II nuclear tests (Operation Shakti); the day is observed as National Technology Day. Trap: Pokhran-I took place on 18 May 1974.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

25. Consider the following statements about computer memory:
1. RAM is volatile memory.
2. ROM loses its contents when power is switched off.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: RAM loses its data when power is switched off, so it is volatile. ROM is non-volatile and keeps its contents, so statement 2 is wrong. Trap type: almost true statement.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System
