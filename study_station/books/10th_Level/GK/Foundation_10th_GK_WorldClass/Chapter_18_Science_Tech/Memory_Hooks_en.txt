🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Mnemonic 1: The 7 SI base units
Sentence: "My Kind Sister Always Keeps Mangoes Cool"
My → metre | Kind → kilogram | Sister → second | Always → ampere | Keeps → kelvin | Mangoes → mole | Cool → candela
Visual: a sister putting a basket of mangoes in a cool corner — seven mangoes, seven units.

🧠 Mnemonic 2: ISRO timeline as a phone number
"62-63-69 / 72-75 / 80-84"
INCOSPAR 1962 – Thumba rocket 1963 – ISRO 1969 / Department of Space 1972 – Aryabhata 1975 / SLV-3 1980 – Rakesh Sharma 1984.
Say it like a mobile number three times and it sticks.

🧠 Mnemonic 3: Atom's three discoverers
"Tom found the Electron, Ruth found the Nucleus, Chad found the Neutron"
J. J. Thomson (electron, 1897) → Rutherford (nucleus, 1911) → Chadwick (neutron, 1932) — also the order of discovery.

🧠 Mnemonic 4: Mendel vs Mendeleev
"Short name, short plant (pea); long name, long table (periodic table)."
Mendel → heredity; Mendeleev → periodic table (1869).

🧠 Mnemonic 5: Salk vs Sabin
"Salk — Sui (needle); Sabin — Swallow"
Salk's polio vaccine is injected; Sabin's is taken by mouth.

🧠 Mnemonic 6: Computer generations
"Very Tired Indians Make AI"
Vacuum tube → Transistor → IC → Microprocessor → AI. Each generation, the part gets smaller.

🧠 Mnemonic 7: RAM vs ROM
"RAM = Run-Away Memory" — runs away when the power goes (volatile).
"ROM = Remains On Memory" — stays even without power (non-volatile).

🧠 Mnemonic 8: Light-year
"Light-year walks a road, not a clock." — it is a unit of distance.

🧠 Mnemonic 9: IGMDP's five missiles
"Papa Aaj Aakash Tak Nache" — Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Trishul, Nag.
Type check: Prithvi and Agni hit the ground (surface-to-surface); Akash and Trishul hit the sky (surface-to-air); Nag bites tanks (anti-tank).

🧠 Mnemonic 10: BrahMos
"Brahma + Moscow" — Brahmaputra + Moskva rivers; an India–Russia joint venture, not an IGMDP missile.

🧠 Mnemonic 11: The four science days
"Raman in Feb, Bombs in May, Moon in August, Maths in December"
28 Feb — Science Day | 11 May — Technology Day | 23 Aug — Space Day | 22 Dec — Mathematics Day.

🧠 Mnemonic 12: Moon–Mars–Sun missions
"C1 at 08, Mars at 13, C2 at 19, C3 and Sun at 23"
Chandrayaan-1 2008 → Mangalyaan 2013 (Mars orbit 2014) → Chandrayaan-2 2019 → Chandrayaan-3 and Aditya-L1 2023.

🧠 Mnemonic 13: Lab cities
"Food in Mysuru, Drugs in Lucknow, Cells in Hyderabad, Ocean in Goa"
CFTRI → Mysuru | CDRI → Lucknow | CCMB → Hyderabad | NIO → Goa.

🧠 Mnemonic 14: Pokhran dates
"74 smiled, 98 showed Shakti" — Pokhran-I 1974 "Smiling Buddha"; Pokhran-II 1998 "Operation Shakti".

⏭️ Skip Strategy

1. If a question asks for the exact launch vehicle number (like PSLV-C37 or GSLV-F16) of a mission you never studied, and two options look equally likely, skip it in the first round — the return is low and the options are built to look alike.

2. If a "match the following" has four lesser-known institutes and you are sure of fewer than two pairs, do not guess blindly. Elimination works only when you know at least two pairs.

3. If a numerical unit-conversion question needs more than one step and you are short of time, mark it and come back later; finish all the 15-second inventor, unit and full-form questions first.
