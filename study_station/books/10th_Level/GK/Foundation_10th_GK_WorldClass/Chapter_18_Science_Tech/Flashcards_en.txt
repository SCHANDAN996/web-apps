🃏 Flashcard Deck: Science & Technology (Foundation)

Card 1
Front: A rocket part once travelled to its launch pad on a bicycle. From which village did India launch its first sounding rocket, and when?
Back: Thumba, near Thiruvananthapuram (Kerala), on 21 November 1963.

Card 2
Front: India's first satellite was built at home but rode a foreign rocket. Name it, its date and the country that launched it.
Back: Aryabhata — 19 April 1975 — launched by the Soviet Union.

Card 3
Front: Which day did a scattering of light turn into a national celebration?
Back: 28 February — National Science Day, marking C. V. Raman's discovery of the Raman Effect (1928). He won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930.

Card 4
Front: Mnemonic check: what does "My Kind Sister Always Keeps Mangoes Cool" stand for?
Back: The 7 SI base units — metre, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, candela.

Card 5
Front: Mnemonic check: what is "62 – 63 – 69 – 72 – 75 – 80 – 84"?
Back: INCOSPAR (1962) – Thumba launch (1963) – ISRO (1969) – Department of Space (1972) – Aryabhata (1975) – SLV-3 (1980) – Rakesh Sharma in space (1984).

Card 6
Front: It has "year" in its name but measures no time. What is it?
Back: The light-year — a unit of distance, about 9.46 × 10^15 m.

Card 7
Front: Which part is called the "brain" of the computer, and what are its two main units?
Back: The CPU — with the ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) and the CU (Control Unit).

Card 8
Front: Power cut! Which memory forgets everything, and which remembers?
Back: RAM is volatile and loses its data; ROM is non-volatile and keeps it.

Card 9
Front: Who discovered the electron, the nucleus and the neutron?
Back: J. J. Thomson (electron, 1897), Ernest Rutherford (nucleus, 1911), James Chadwick (neutron, 1932).

Card 10
Front: Examiner's trap: one grew peas, one arranged elements. Who is who?
Back: Gregor Mendel — laws of heredity (pea plants). Dmitri Mendeleev — periodic table (1869). Trap type: Similar Name.

Card 11
Front: India became the fourth nation to soft-land on the Moon, and the first near its south pole. Which mission, and on what date?
Back: Chandrayaan-3 — landed on 23 August 2023 (launched 14 July 2023 by LVM3-M4). The site is "Shiv Shakti Point".

Card 12
Front: Which Indian mission reached Mars orbit on its very first attempt, and when did it arrive?
Back: The Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) — launched 5 November 2013 by PSLV-C25, entered Mars orbit on 24 September 2014.

Card 13
Front: Aditya-L1 does not go around the Moon. Where does it sit, and since when?
Back: In a halo orbit around the Sun–Earth Lagrange point L1, since 6 January 2024. It is India's first solar observatory (launched 2 September 2023).

Card 14
Front: Why is 11 May special for Indian science?
Back: It is National Technology Day, marking the Pokhran-II nuclear tests of 11 May 1998 (Operation Shakti; more tests on 13 May).

Card 15
Front: Name the five missiles of the IGMDP (started 1983).
Back: Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Trishul and Nag. BrahMos is NOT one of them — it is an India–Russia joint venture.

Card 16
Front: Examiner's trap: "Oral polio vaccine was developed by Jonas Salk." True or false?
Back: False. Salk made the injected polio vaccine; Albert Sabin made the oral one. Trap type: Almost True Statement.

Card 17
Front: CFTRI, CDRI and CCMB — match each with its city.
Back: CFTRI — Mysuru; CDRI — Lucknow; CCMB — Hyderabad.

Card 18
Front: In 2025, which Indian became the first to visit the International Space Station, and on which mission?
Back: Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla, on the Axiom-4 mission (launched 25 June 2025, returned 15 July 2025).
