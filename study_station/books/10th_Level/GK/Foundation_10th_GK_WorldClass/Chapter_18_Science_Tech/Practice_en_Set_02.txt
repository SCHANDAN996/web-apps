Set 2/6 – Science & Technology MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The electron was discovered by:
(a) James Chadwick (b) J. J. Thomson (c) Ernest Rutherford (d) Eugen Goldstein
Answer: (b)
Solution: J. J. Thomson discovered the electron in 1897 using cathode rays. Chadwick discovered the neutron and Rutherford the atomic nucleus.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Structure of the Atom

27. The neutron was discovered in 1932 by:
(a) J. J. Thomson (b) Niels Bohr (c) James Chadwick (d) Ernest Rutherford
Answer: (c)
Solution: James Chadwick discovered the neutron, a particle with no charge, in 1932. Trap type: similar names — all four options are atomic scientists.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Structure of the Atom

28. The first vaccine, against smallpox, was developed by:
(a) Louis Pasteur (b) Edward Jenner (c) Robert Koch (d) Jonas Salk
Answer: (b)
Solution: Edward Jenner developed the smallpox vaccine in 1796 using cowpox. Pasteur later made the rabies vaccine and Salk the polio vaccine.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science, Microorganisms

29. The vaccine against rabies was developed by:
(a) Louis Pasteur (b) Alexander Fleming (c) Edward Jenner (d) Karl Landsteiner
Answer: (a)
Solution: Louis Pasteur developed the rabies vaccine; he is also known for pasteurisation. Fleming discovered penicillin and Landsteiner the blood groups.
Source: PYQ-style

30. Radioactivity was discovered by:
(a) Marie Curie (b) Henri Becquerel (c) Wilhelm Röntgen (d) Pierre Curie
Answer: (b)
Solution: Henri Becquerel discovered radioactivity in 1896 from uranium salts. Trap: the Curies discovered the radioactive elements radium and polonium, and Röntgen discovered X-rays.
Source: PYQ-style

31. The World Wide Web (WWW) was invented by:
(a) Bill Gates (b) Tim Berners-Lee (c) Charles Babbage (d) Steve Jobs
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tim Berners-Lee proposed the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN. Babbage is the father of the computer; Gates and Jobs founded Microsoft and Apple.
Source: PYQ-style

32. The SI unit of electrical resistance is:
(a) volt (b) ampere (c) ohm (d) watt
Answer: (c)
Solution: Resistance is measured in ohm (Ω). By Ohm's law, resistance = potential difference / current, so 1 ohm = 1 volt per ampere.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Electricity

33. The SI unit of pressure is:
(a) newton (b) pascal (c) joule (d) bar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pressure is force per unit area, and its SI unit is the pascal (Pa), equal to one newton per square metre. Bar is also a pressure unit, but not the SI unit.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics, Units and Measurement

34. Hertz is the SI unit of:
(a) Frequency (b) Power (c) Wavelength (d) Energy
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hertz (Hz) measures frequency — the number of oscillations per second. Power is in watt and energy in joule; wavelength is a length, measured in metre.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Sound

35. A "light-year" is a unit of:
(a) Time (b) Speed (c) Distance (d) Intensity of light
Answer: (c)
Solution: A light-year is the distance light travels in one year, about 9.46 × 10^15 m. Trap type: almost true statement — the word "year" makes students think of time.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics, Units and Measurement

36. One kilobyte (KB) is equal to:
(a) 1000 bits (b) 1024 bits (c) 1000 bytes (d) 1024 bytes
Answer: (d)
Solution: In computer memory, one kilobyte is 1024 bytes (2^10). Trap: "1024 bits" has the right number but the wrong unit.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

37. Third-generation computers were based on:
(a) Vacuum tubes (b) Transistors (c) Integrated circuits (d) Artificial intelligence
Answer: (c)
Solution: The generations run: vacuum tubes (first), transistors (second), integrated circuits (third), microprocessors (fourth) and AI (fifth).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

38. ISRO was established in which year?
(a) 1962 (b) 1972 (c) 1975 (d) 1969
Answer: (d)
Solution: INCOSPAR was formed in 1962, the Department of Space in 1972, and Aryabhata was launched in 1975. ISRO itself was set up in August 1969.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

39. India's first satellite Aryabhata was launched with the help of:
(a) USA (b) Soviet Union (c) France (d) Japan
Answer: (b)
Solution: Aryabhata was built by ISRO and launched by the Soviet Union on 19 April 1975. Trap type: almost true statement — "India launched it" sounds right but the rocket was Soviet.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

40. Chandrayaan-1, India's first mission to the Moon, was launched in:
(a) 2005 (b) 2013 (c) 2019 (d) 2008
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mangalyaan was launched in 2013 and Chandrayaan-2 in 2019. Chandrayaan-1 lifted off on 22 October 2008 on PSLV-C11.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

41. India's Mars Orbiter Mission entered the orbit of Mars on:
(a) 5 November 2013 (b) 24 September 2014 (c) 22 October 2008 (d) 14 July 2023
Answer: (b)
Solution: The mission was launched on 5 November 2013 and reached Mars orbit on 24 September 2014. Trap type: chronological confusion between launch and arrival.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

42. India's first nuclear test, Pokhran-I ("Smiling Buddha"), was conducted in:
(a) 1998 (b) 1971 (c) 1974 (d) 1984
Answer: (c)
Solution: Pokhran-II (Operation Shakti) took place in 1998. The first test, Pokhran-I, was carried out in Rajasthan on 18 May 1974.
Source: PYQ-style

43. The headquarters of DRDO is located in:
(a) Bengaluru (b) Hyderabad (c) Pune (d) New Delhi
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Defence Research and Development Organisation, formed in 1958, has its headquarters in New Delhi. Bengaluru is the headquarters of ISRO.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

44. Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC) is located at:
(a) Trombay, Mumbai (b) Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu (c) Tarapur, Maharashtra (d) Kota, Rajasthan
Answer: (a)
Solution: BARC is at Trombay in Mumbai. Kalpakkam has the Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research, and Tarapur has India's first nuclear power station.
Source: BARC (barc.gov.in)

45. The name "BrahMos" comes from:
(a) Brahma and Mars (b) Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers (c) Brahmin and Moscow (d) Brahmaputra and Mahanadi rivers
Answer: (b)
Solution: BrahMos, a supersonic cruise missile made by an India–Russia joint venture, is named after the Brahmaputra (India) and Moskva (Russia) rivers.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

46. The Akash missile is a:
(a) Surface-to-surface missile (b) Surface-to-air missile (c) Anti-tank missile (d) Air-to-air missile
Answer: (b)
Solution: Akash is a surface-to-air missile developed under IGMDP. Prithvi and Agni are surface-to-surface, Nag is anti-tank and Astra is air-to-air.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

47. Nag, developed under IGMDP, is a/an:
(a) Anti-tank guided missile (b) Intercontinental ballistic missile (c) Surface-to-air missile (d) Cruise missile
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nag is an anti-tank guided missile. Mnemonic: "Nag bites tanks". Trishul and Akash are the surface-to-air missiles of IGMDP.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

48. Har Gobind Khorana won the Nobel Prize in 1968 in the field of:
(a) Physics (b) Chemistry (c) Physiology or Medicine (d) Economics
Answer: (c)
Solution: Har Gobind Khorana shared the 1968 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for work on the genetic code. Trap: C. V. Raman's and S. Chandrasekhar's prizes were in Physics.
Source: PYQ-style

49. National Space Day is observed in India on:
(a) 23 August (b) 15 August (c) 14 July (d) 19 April
Answer: (a)
Solution: 14 July 2023 was Chandrayaan-3's launch date and 19 April 1975 Aryabhata's launch. National Space Day marks the Chandrayaan-3 landing of 23 August.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

50. Who is called the "Father of the Computer"?
(a) Alan Turing (b) Charles Babbage (c) John von Neumann (d) Blaise Pascal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Charles Babbage designed the Analytical Engine, which had the basic parts of a modern computer, so he is called the father of the computer.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System
