Set 4/6 – Science & Technology MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 76–100: Medium

76. India's first lunar mission, Chandrayaan-1, which confirmed the presence of water molecules on the Moon, was launched in:
(a) 2008 (b) 2013 (c) 2019 (d) 2023
Answer: (a)
Solution: While Mangalyaan was launched in 2013 and Chandrayaan-3 in 2023, India's first lunar mission Chandrayaan-1 was launched on 22 October 2008.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

77. India's Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) was successfully inserted into Martian orbit in:
(a) 2014 (b) 2011 (c) 2017 (d) 2020
Answer: (a)
Solution: Launched in November 2013 by PSLV-C25, Mangalyaan successfully entered orbit around Mars on 24 September 2014 on its maiden attempt.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

78. The supersonic cruise missile "BrahMos" is a joint venture between India and which country?
(a) Russia (b) France (c) United States (d) Israel
Answer: (a)
Solution: BrahMos is a joint venture between India (DRDO) and Russia (NPO Mashinostroyeniya), named after the Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

79. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was formed in which year?
(a) 1958 (b) 1969 (c) 1974 (d) 1983
Answer: (a)
Solution: DRDO was established in 1958 by merging the Technical Development Establishment and the Directorate of Technical Development and Production with the Defence Science Organisation.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

80. Apsara, the first nuclear research reactor in India and Asia, attained criticality at Trombay in:
(a) 1956 (b) 1962 (c) 1974 (d) 1984
Answer: (a)
Solution: Apsara reactor went critical on 4 August 1956 at Trombay under the guidance of Dr. Homi J. Bhabha.
Source: BARC (barc.gov.in)

81. PARAM 8000, widely regarded as India's first indigenous supercomputer, was built in 1991 by:
(a) C-DAC, Pune (b) DRDO, Delhi (c) BARC, Mumbai (d) ISRO, Bengaluru
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) in Pune built PARAM 8000 in 1991 under the leadership of Dr. Vijay Bhatkar.
Source: C-DAC (cdac.in)

82. India's solar observatory mission, Aditya-L1, was placed in a halo orbit around which Lagrange point?
(a) Lagrange Point 2 (L2) (b) Lagrange Point 1 (L1) (c) Lagrange Point 4 (L4) (d) Lagrange Point 5 (L5)
Answer: (b)
Solution: Aditya-L1 was launched by PSLV-C57 in September 2023 to study the Sun from a halo orbit around the Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 1 (L1), about 1.5 million km from Earth.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

83. "Vyommitra", a female-looking humanoid robot developed for the Gaganyaan human spaceflight programme, was designed by:
(a) DRDO (b) ISRO (c) CSIR (d) C-DAC
Answer: (b)
Solution: ISRO Inertial Systems Unit (IISU) developed Vyommitra to fly aboard uncrewed test missions of Gaganyaan to monitor life support and environmental parameters.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

84. NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation), India's regional satellite navigation system, was formerly known as:
(a) GAGAN (b) IRNSS (c) INSAT (d) RISAT
Answer: (b)
Solution: NavIC is the operational name for the Indian Regional Navigation Satellite System (IRNSS), which uses a constellation of 7 satellites to provide positioning services.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

85. Which missile developed under the IGMDP programme is an anti-tank guided missile (ATGM) featuring "fire-and-forget" capability?
(a) Prithvi (b) Nag (c) Akash (d) Trishul
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nag is India's third-generation anti-tank guided missile with fire-and-forget capability. Trap: Prithvi is a surface-to-surface ballistic missile and Akash is surface-to-air.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

86. India's indigenous Light Combat Aircraft (LCA) developed by Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA) and manufactured by HAL is named:
(a) Marut (b) Tejas (c) Rafale (d) Sukhoi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tejas is India's single-engine, delta-wing multirole supersonic light fighter aircraft. Trap: Marut was India's earlier 1960s jet fighter, while Rafale is French.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

87. India conducted its first underground nuclear test, code-named "Smiling Buddha" (Pokhran-I), in:
(a) 1964 (b) 1974 (c) 1984 (d) 1998
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pokhran-II (Operation Shakti) took place in 1998, whereas India conducted its first peaceful nuclear test (Smiling Buddha) on 18 May 1974.
Source: BARC (barc.gov.in)

88. The Unified Payments Interface (UPI), which revolutionized instant mobile payments in India, was developed by:
(a) Reserve Bank of India (RBI) (b) National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) (c) State Bank of India (SBI) (d) NITI Aayog
Answer: (b)
Solution: NPCI launched UPI in 2016 to enable real-time interbank peer-to-peer and peer-to-merchant mobile transactions using virtual payment addresses.
Source: PYQ-style

89. Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC SHAR), the primary spaceport of India, is located at:
(a) Thumba, Kerala (b) Wheeler Island, Odisha (c) Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh (d) Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sriharikota is a barrier island in the Tirupati district of Andhra Pradesh separating Pulicat Lake from the Bay of Bengal; it serves as India's primary orbital launch site.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

90. In the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP), "Akash" is classified as a:
(a) Short-range surface-to-surface missile (b) Long-range anti-ship cruise missile (c) Medium-range surface-to-air missile (d) Air-to-air beyond-visual-range missile
Answer: (c)
Solution: Akash is a medium-range mobile surface-to-air missile defence system developed by DRDO. Prithvi is surface-to-surface and Astra is air-to-air.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

91. Dr. Homi Jehangir Bhabha is universally recognized as the father of:
(a) India's Space Programme (b) India's Missile Programme (c) India's Nuclear Programme (d) India's Supercomputing Programme
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dr. Homi J. Bhabha founded the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR) and the Atomic Energy Establishment at Trombay. Trap: Vikram Sarabhai is father of space programme.
Source: BARC (barc.gov.in)

92. Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant, the highest capacity nuclear plant in India, is located in which state?
(a) Karnataka (b) Maharashtra (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Gujarat
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant is in the Tirunelveli district of Tamil Nadu, utilizing Russian VVER-1000 pressurized water reactors.
Source: PYQ-style

93. An Internet Protocol version 4 (IPv4) address consists of how many bits?
(a) 8 bits (b) 16 bits (c) 32 bits (d) 64 bits
Answer: (c)
Solution: An IPv4 address is a 32-bit numeric address written in four dot-separated octets (e.g. 192.168.1.1). Trap: IPv6 uses 128 bits.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer Networks

94. Which type of computer memory is the fastest and placed directly inside the Central Processing Unit (CPU)?
(a) RAM (b) ROM (c) Registers (d) Hard Disk
Answer: (c)
Solution: Registers are small, high-speed storage locations located directly inside the CPU; next fastest is CPU cache, followed by primary memory (RAM).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

95. Agni-V, the long-range surface-to-surface ballistic missile tested by DRDO, is classified as an:
(a) Anti-radiation cruise missile (b) Submarine-launched torpedo (c) Short-range air-defence missile (d) Intercontinental Ballistic Missile (ICBM)
Answer: (d)
Solution: Agni-V is a three-stage solid-fuel intercontinental ballistic missile capable of striking targets at ranges exceeding 5,000 kilometres.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

96. The monazite sands of the Kerala coast are exceptionally rich in which radioactive element, critical for Stage-3 of India's nuclear programme?
(a) Uranium (b) Radium (c) Plutonium (d) Thorium
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kerala's beach placer sands contain abundant monazite, a major source of thorium-232, which will be utilized in advanced heavy water reactors.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography, Minerals and Energy Resources

97. Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam served as the chief coordinator for which historic event in May 1998?
(a) Launch of Aryabhata (b) Launch of SLV-3 (c) Operation Smiling Buddha (d) Operation Shakti (Pokhran-II nuclear tests)
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dr. Kalam, as head of DRDO and scientific adviser to the Defence Minister, along with Dr. R. Chidambaram of DAE, led the five nuclear weapon tests under Operation Shakti in 1998.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

98. The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In), the national nodal agency for responding to computer security incidents, operates under which ministry?
(a) Ministry of Home Affairs (b) Ministry of Defence (c) Ministry of Science and Technology (d) Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology
Answer: (d)
Solution: CERT-In is designated under Section 70B of the IT Act, 2000, under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
Source: PYQ-style

99. In computer networking, what is the size of an IPv6 address?
(a) 32 bits (b) 48 bits (c) 64 bits (d) 128 bits
Answer: (d)
Solution: While an IPv4 address uses 32 bits and a MAC address uses 48 bits, an IPv6 address consists of 128 bits.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer Networks

100. The device invented by Sir Jagadish Chandra Bose to measure micro-growth in plants was named:
(a) Seismograph (b) Barograph (c) Chronometer (d) Crescograph
Answer: (d)
Solution: J. C. Bose invented the crescograph in the early 20th century to magnify and record plant growth and movements up to 10,000 times.
Source: PYQ-style
