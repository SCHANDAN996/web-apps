Set 5/6 – Science & Technology MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard

101. India's first nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN), which completed India's nuclear triad, is named:
(a) INS Arihant (b) INS Kalvari (c) INS Vikrant (d) INS Chakra
Answer: (a)
Solution: INS Arihant is India's first indigenously designed and built nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine, completing the sea leg of the nuclear triad.
Source: Indian Navy (indiannavy.nic.in)

102. The U R Rao Satellite Centre (URSC), the lead centre of ISRO for design, development, and assembly of satellites, is located in:
(a) Bengaluru (b) Thiruvananthapuram (c) Ahmedabad (d) Hyderabad
Answer: (a)
Solution: URSC (formerly ISRO Satellite Centre) is located in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Space Applications Centre (SAC) is in Ahmedabad.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

103. Which Indian physicist worked with Albert Einstein to formulate the quantum statistics that gave rise to the term "Boson"?
(a) Satyendra Nath Bose (b) Jagadish Chandra Bose (c) Meghnad Saha (d) Homi Bhabha
Answer: (a)
Solution: Satyendra Nath Bose derived the Planck radiation law without classical electrodynamics, leading to Bose-Einstein statistics; Paul Dirac named "Bosons" in his honour.
Source: PYQ-style

104. India acquired the advanced long-range surface-to-air missile defence system "S-400 Triumf" from which country?
(a) Russia (b) France (c) Israel (d) United States
Answer: (a)
Solution: India signed an intergovernmental agreement with Russia to procure five squadrons of the S-400 Triumf air defence missile system.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

105. The extremely fast, small memory buffer situated between the Central Processing Unit (CPU) and main memory (RAM) is known as:
(a) Cache memory (b) Virtual memory (c) Secondary memory (d) Optical memory
Answer: (a)
Solution: Cache memory stores frequently accessed program instructions and data to speed up CPU processing operations.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

106. Dr. M. S. Swaminathan, honoured with the Bharat Ratna in 2024, is celebrated as the father of:
(a) Green Revolution in India (b) White Revolution in India (c) Blue Revolution in India (d) Yellow Revolution in India
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dr. M. S. Swaminathan led the introduction of high-yielding semi-dwarf wheat and rice varieties in India, spearheading the Green Revolution.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

107. Project 75 of the Indian Navy involved the construction of six diesel-electric attack submarines of which class at Mazagon Dock?
(a) Arihant class (b) Kalvari class (c) Shishumar class (d) Sindhughosh class
Answer: (b)
Solution: Project 75 built six Kalvari-class (Scorpene design) conventional attack submarines: Kalvari, Khanderi, Karanj, Vela, Vagir, and Vagsheer.
Source: Indian Navy (indiannavy.nic.in)

108. The ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPRC), where liquid rocket engines and cryogenic stages are tested and assembled, is situated at:
(a) Sriharikota (b) Mahendragiri (c) Aluva (d) Hassan
Answer: (b)
Solution: IPRC is located at Mahendragiri in Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu. Master Control Facility (MCF) is located at Hassan and Bhopal.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

109. Meghnad Saha is celebrated in the history of astrophysics for developing which fundamental formula?
(a) Theory of relativity (b) Thermal ionization equation (c) Uncertainty principle (d) Photoelectric equation
Answer: (b)
Solution: Meghnad Saha formulated the Saha ionization equation in 1920, relating the ionization state of an element in a stellar atmosphere to temperature and pressure.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics

110. An email or fraudulent website designed to deceive users into disclosing sensitive personal credentials like passwords is an example of:
(a) Spoofing (b) Phishing (c) Spying (d) Denial of service
Answer: (b)
Solution: Phishing is a social engineering attack where malicious actors impersonate reputable institutions to steal login credentials and financial details.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Cyber Safety

111. Dr. Verghese Kurien is renowned as the "Father of the White Revolution" in India for creating the cooperative dairy model known as:
(a) Safal (b) Amul (c) Mother Dairy (d) Vita
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dr. Verghese Kurien organized the Anand Milk Union Limited (Amul) cooperative model in Gujarat and headed Operation Flood.
Source: PYQ-style

112. Communication satellites in a circular Geostationary Earth Orbit (GEO) rotate at an altitude of approximately:
(a) 2000 km (b) 36000 km (c) 400 km (d) 12000 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: Low Earth Orbit (LEO) is below 2000 km, while a geostationary orbit matches Earth's 24-hour rotation at an altitude of roughly 36000 km.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics, Gravitation

113. India's fastest artificial intelligence (AI) supercomputer, ranked among the top global AI systems and installed at C-DAC Pune, is:
(a) Pratyush (b) AIRAWAT (c) Mihir (d) PARAM Shivay
Answer: (b)
Solution: AIRAWAT (AI Research, Analytics and Knowledge Dissemination Platform) is an AI supercomputer deployed at C-DAC Pune under the National AI Mission.
Source: MeitY (meity.gov.in)

114. The mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan identified 1729 as a remarkable number because it is the smallest number expressible as:
(a) The product of three consecutive prime numbers (b) The square of the sum of the first nine natural numbers (c) The sum of two cubes in two different ways (d) The sum of four consecutive squares
Answer: (c)
Solution: In a conversation with G. H. Hardy, Ramanujan pointed out that 1729 is the smallest number expressible as the sum of two cubes in two different ways: 1 cubed plus 12 cubed, and 9 cubed plus 10 cubed.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

115. A malicious software that locks computer data through encryption and extorts financial payment for the unlock key is called:
(a) Spyware (b) Adware (c) Ransomware (d) Keylogger
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ransomware encrypts user files and displays a ransom demand (such as in Bitcoin) to restore access to the decrypted files.
Source: CERT-In (cert-in.org.in)

116. Tarapur Atomic Power Station (TAPS), the oldest commercial nuclear power facility in India commissioned in 1969, is situated in:
(a) Gujarat (b) Rajasthan (c) Maharashtra (d) Uttar Pradesh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Tarapur Atomic Power Station was built in the Palghar district of Maharashtra as India's first commercial nuclear power facility.
Source: NPCIL (npcil.nic.in)

117. India's first experimental geostationary communication satellite, launched in 1981 on an Ariane rocket, was named:
(a) Rohini (b) Bhaskara (c) APPLE (d) INSAT-1A
Answer: (c)
Solution: APPLE (Ariane Passenger Payload Experiment) was launched on 19 June 1981, providing early experience in operating three-axis stabilized communication satellites.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

118. In digital electronics, which logic gate outputs a binary 1 ONLY when all of its input values are binary 1?
(a) OR gate (b) NOT gate (c) AND gate (d) XOR gate
Answer: (c)
Solution: An AND gate implements logical conjunction: its output is 1 if and only if all inputs are 1. An OR gate outputs 1 if any input is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics, Semiconductor Electronics

119. India's first indigenously developed New Generation Anti-Radiation Missile (NGARM) for the Indian Air Force is named:
(a) Helina (b) Astra (c) Rudram-1 (d) Pralay
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rudram-1 is an air-to-surface anti-radiation missile developed by DRDO to destroy enemy surveillance radar systems and communication stations.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

120. ISRO's workhorse launch vehicle, the Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle (PSLV), uses how many propulsion stages?
(a) Two stages (liquid and cryogenic) (b) Three stages (solid, liquid, cryogenic) (c) Single stage to orbit (d) Four alternating stages (solid and liquid)
Answer: (d)
Solution: PSLV employs four stages in sequence: first stage solid propellant, second stage liquid (Vikas engine), third stage solid, and fourth stage liquid.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

121. In the second stage of India's Three-Stage Nuclear Power Programme, Fast Breeder Reactors (FBR) utilize which primary fissile fuel?
(a) Natural Uranium-238 (b) Thorium-232 directly (c) Heavy water and Deuterium (d) Plutonium-239
Answer: (d)
Solution: Stage 2 utilizes Plutonium-239 recovered from Stage 1 spent fuel to breed more fissile material (Plutonium from Uranium-238 or Uranium-233 from Thorium).
Source: DAE (dae.gov.in)

122. Which powerful cryogenic rocket engine, developed indigenously by the Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre (LPSC), powers the upper stage of the LVM3 launch vehicle?
(a) Vikas engine (b) CE-7.5 cryogenic engine (c) Dhawan-1 engine (d) CE-20 cryogenic engine
Answer: (d)
Solution: While CE-7.5 powers the GSLV Mk II upper stage, the heavier LVM3 (GSLV Mk III) uses the high-thrust CE-20 cryogenic engine.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

123. Consider the following software translators:
1. Compiler - Translates entire source code into object code in one single batch
2. Interpreter - Translates and executes source code line-by-line sequentially
3. Assembler - Converts assembly language mnemonics into binary machine code
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only 1 (b) Only 1 and 2 (c) Only 2 and 3 (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: All three definitions are correct: Compilers process whole source files, interpreters execute statements step-by-step, and assemblers translate low-level assembly language.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

124. The earth-observing synthetic aperture radar satellite mission "NISAR" is a collaborative joint project between ISRO and:
(a) Roscosmos (b) JAXA (c) ESA (d) NASA
Answer: (d)
Solution: NISAR (NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar) is a joint mission between NASA and ISRO to map the Earth using dual L-band and S-band radar.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

125. The flight-test codenamed "Mission Divyastra" in March 2024 demonstrated which technological milestone for India's Agni-V missile?
(a) Hypersonic air-breathing scramjet flight (b) Submarine-launched cruise flight (c) Underwater stealth torpedo launch (d) Multiple Independently Targetable Re-entry Vehicle (MIRV) technology
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mission Divyastra verified that a single Agni-V missile can deploy multiple nuclear warheads targeted at distinct geographic locations through MIRV technology.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in), 2024
