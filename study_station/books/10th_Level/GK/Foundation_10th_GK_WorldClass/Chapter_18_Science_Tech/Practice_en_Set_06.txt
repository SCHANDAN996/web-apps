Set 6/6 – Science & Technology MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Heavy water (D2O) in Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (PHWRs) serves what primary function?
(a) Both moderator and coolant (b) Control rod absorbing neutrons (c) Primary fissile fuel (d) Biological radiation shield
Answer: (a)
Solution: In PHWRs, heavy water (deuterium oxide, D2O) acts simultaneously as a moderator to slow down fast neutrons and as a coolant to transfer heat from the reactor core.
Source: BARC (barc.gov.in)

127. Which of the following satellite frequency bands operates in the highest frequency range?
(a) Ka-band (b) Ku-band (c) C-band (d) L-band
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ka-band operates in the 26.5–40 GHz range, providing very high data rates. Ku-band is 12–18 GHz, C-band is 4–8 GHz, and L-band is 1–2 GHz.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

128. What is the fundamental unit of information in quantum computing, capable of existing in a superposition of states?
(a) Qubit (b) Quantum byte (c) Q-gate (d) Quantum chip
Answer: (a)
Solution: While classical computers use binary bits (0 or 1), a quantum computer uses quantum bits (qubits) that can represent 0, 1, or any superposition of both simultaneously.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

129. The revolutionary gene-editing technology "CRISPR-Cas9" was adapted from an antiviral defence mechanism found naturally in:
(a) Bacteria (b) Mammalian liver cells (c) Plant chloroplasts (d) Fungi
Answer: (a)
Solution: CRISPR-Cas9 is an adaptive immune system in bacteria that recognizes and cuts foreign viral DNA; Emmanuelle Charpentier and Jennifer Doudna won the 2020 Nobel Prize for adapting it.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Biotechnology

130. In communication networks, the protocol responsible for mapping an IP address to a physical MAC address is:
(a) ARP (Address Resolution Protocol) (b) DHCP (Dynamic Host Configuration Protocol) (c) DNS (Domain Name System) (d) FTP (File Transfer Protocol)
Answer: (a)
Solution: ARP maps a 32-bit logical IPv4 address to a 48-bit physical MAC hardware address on a local network.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer Networks

131. In space mission mechanics, a "Hohmann transfer orbit" is designed to:
(a) Transfer a spacecraft between two circular orbits with minimal fuel consumption (b) Maintain a satellite in a stationary hover directly over the North Pole (c) Increase the payload capacity of a solid rocket booster (d) Decelerate a planetary lander during terminal descent
Answer: (a)
Solution: A Hohmann transfer orbit is an elliptical trajectory used to transition a spacecraft between two orbits using two impulse burns, achieving maximum fuel efficiency.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

132. The "Kavach" system developed by the Indian Railways in collaboration with RDSO is an indigenously developed:
(a) Satellite internet tracking device (b) Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system (c) Electric locomotive regenerative braking motor (d) High-speed vacuum maglev tube
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kavach is an indigenous Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system that prevents train collisions (SPAD - Signal Passed at Danger and head-on collisions) by automatically applying brakes.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

133. India's prototype fast breeder reactor (PFBR, 500 MWe), built at Kalpakkam (Tamil Nadu), uses which liquid metal as its primary coolant?
(a) Liquid Mercury (b) Liquid Sodium (c) Liquid Lead (d) Liquid Lithium
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fast breeder reactors do not use water as it would slow down neutrons; they utilize liquid sodium, which has excellent heat transfer properties and does not moderate neutrons.
Source: IGCAR (igcar.gov.in)

134. Which Indian deep-sea submersible mission is being developed under the "Samudrayaan" project by the National Institute of Ocean Technology (NIOT)?
(a) SAGAR 3000 (b) MATSYA 6000 (c) SAMUDRA 5000 (d) VARUNA 4000
Answer: (b)
Solution: MATSYA 6000 is an indigenous manned submersible designed to carry three humans to a depth of 6,000 metres in the ocean to explore deep-sea mineral resources.
Source: Ministry of Earth Sciences (moes.gov.in)

135. The "Prithvi Air Defence" (PAD) and "Advanced Air Defence" (AAD) interceptors developed by DRDO form part of India's:
(a) Anti-satellite space weapon fleet (b) Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD) shield (c) Naval electronic warfare jamming suite (d) Submarine periscope detection system
Answer: (b)
Solution: India's BMD system has a two-tier interceptor shield: PAD for exo-atmospheric (outside atmosphere) and AAD for endo-atmospheric (inside atmosphere) interception.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

136. What is the fundamental working principle behind an optical fibre communication cable?
(a) Total diffuse scattering (b) Total internal reflection (c) Atmospheric refraction (d) Photoelectric emission
Answer: (b)
Solution: Optical fibres transmit light signals over long distances with minimal loss using total internal reflection, occurring when light hits the core-cladding boundary at an angle greater than the critical angle.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Light

137. Which country's space agency successfully retrieved soil and rock samples from the far side of the Moon in 2024 for the first time in human history?
(a) United States (b) China (Chang'e 6) (c) Russia (d) Japan
Answer: (b)
Solution: In June 2024, China's Chang'e 6 robotic lunar exploration mission returned the first-ever samples collected from the Moon's South Pole-Aitken basin on the lunar far side.
Source: PYQ-style

138. The "LiFi" (Light Fidelity) technology, an alternative to WiFi, transmits data wirelessly using:
(a) High-frequency microwaves (b) Visible light emitted by LEDs (c) Infrared laser beams in vacuum (d) Ultrasonic acoustic waves
Answer: (b)
Solution: LiFi is a bidirectional wireless communication technology that modulates the intensity of visible light from LED bulbs at speeds imperceptible to the human eye.
Source: PYQ-style

139. In the context of computer architecture and processing, a "RISC" processor differs from "CISC" because RISC features:
(a) Complex multi-clock instructions requiring microcode (b) Infinite register sets with variable length encoding (c) Reduced and simplified instruction set executing in fewer clock cycles (d) Hardware-only execution of decimal arithmetic
Answer: (c)
Solution: RISC (Reduced Instruction Set Computer) focuses on simple, uniform-length instructions that can be pipelined and executed in a single clock cycle, in contrast to CISC.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

140. Which indigenously developed navigation satellite system provides differential GPS corrections over the Indian airspace to support civil aviation?
(a) NavIC (b) GLONASS (c) GAGAN (GPS Aided GEO Augmented Navigation) (d) Beidou
Answer: (c)
Solution: GAGAN is a Space Based Augmentation System (SBAS) jointly developed by ISRO and the Airports Authority of India (AAI) to provide satellite navigation accuracy for civil aviation.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

141. The Scramjet (Supersonic Combusting Ramjet) engine tested by ISRO and DRDO differs from conventional jet engines because it:
(a) Carries its own liquid oxygen oxidizer onboard like a rocket (b) Utilizes nuclear thermal fission to heat liquid hydrogen (c) Operates efficiently at supersonic airflow speeds without rotating compressor blades (d) Relies exclusively on solid propellant grains
Answer: (c)
Solution: A scramjet engine breathes atmospheric oxygen and maintains supersonic airflow throughout the combustion chamber without mechanical rotating compressors or turbines.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

142. The "Bose-Einstein Condensate" (BEC), often termed the fifth state of matter, is formed when bosons are:
(a) Heated to millions of degrees Celsius in a tokamak (b) Accelerated to near the speed of light in a cyclotron (c) Cooled to temperatures extremely close to absolute zero (d) Subjected to ultra-high gravitational fields
Answer: (c)
Solution: In a Bose-Einstein Condensate, atoms cooled close to absolute zero (0 Kelvin) collapse into the lowest quantum state, behaving as a single macroscopic super-atom.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Matter in Our Surroundings

143. Which layer of the Open Systems Interconnection (OSI) 7-layer reference model is responsible for end-to-end flow control, error recovery, and data segmentation (e.g. TCP and UDP)?
(a) Network Layer (b) Data Link Layer (c) Transport Layer (d) Session Layer
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Transport Layer (Layer 4) provides reliable end-to-end communication services, segmentation, and flow control. The Network Layer (Layer 3) handles routing and IP addressing.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Computer Science, Computer Networks

144. In modern genetics, the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 2023 was awarded to Katalin Kariko and Drew Weissman for discoveries enabling the development of:
(a) Recombinant insulin therapy (b) CRISPR-Cas9 base editors (c) Effective nucleoside-modified mRNA vaccines against COVID-19 (d) Monoclonal antibodies for malaria
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kariko and Weissman discovered that nucleoside base modifications prevent inflammatory reactions and boost protein production, paving the way for mRNA vaccines.
Source: PYQ-style

145. In computer security, what term describes a vulnerability in software that is exploited by malicious hackers before the software vendor becomes aware of it or issues a security patch?
(a) Trojan backdoor (b) Denial-of-Service exploit (c) SQL Injection bug (d) Zero-Day vulnerability
Answer: (d)
Solution: A Zero-Day vulnerability is an unpatched software flaw unknown to the developers, giving defenders zero days to fix it before an attack occurs.
Source: CERT-In (cert-in.org.in)

146. In nuclear reactors, which of the following materials is commonly used to make "Control Rods" because of its extremely high neutron-absorption capacity?
(a) Heavy water (b) Graphite (c) Zirconium alloy (d) Boron or Cadmium
Answer: (d)
Solution: Control rods are made of neutron-absorbing materials such as boron carbide or cadmium; inserting them into the reactor core absorbs neutrons and slows down the fission chain reaction.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics, Nuclei

149. In artificial intelligence and machine learning, what does the acronym "LLM" stand for?
(a) Logical Learning Machine (b) Linear Language Module (c) Layered Learning Mechanism (d) Large Language Model
Answer: (d)
Solution: A Large Language Model (LLM) is an AI neural network trained on vast text datasets to understand, generate, and process natural human language.
Source: MeitY (meity.gov.in)

150. India's dedicated multi-wavelength space astronomy observatory satellite, launched by ISRO aboard PSLV-C30 in 2015, is:
(a) Cartosat-1 (b) Oceansat-2 (c) Megha-Tropiques (d) AstroSat
Answer: (d)
Solution: AstroSat was launched on 28 September 2015 into an equatorial orbit; it observes the universe simultaneously in optical, ultraviolet, low and high-energy X-ray wavebands.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)
