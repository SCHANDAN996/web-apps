Set 3/6 – Science & Technology MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 51–75: Medium

51. The double-helix structure of DNA was proposed in 1953 by:
(a) Watson and Crick (b) Mendel and Morgan (c) Banting and Best (d) Pierre and Marie Curie
Answer: (a)
Solution: James Watson and Francis Crick proposed the double-helix model of DNA in 1953. Banting and Best are linked with insulin, and the Curies with radium.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Blood groups (A, B, O) were discovered by:
(a) William Harvey (b) Karl Landsteiner (c) Robert Koch (d) Edward Jenner
Answer: (b)
Solution: Karl Landsteiner discovered the ABO blood groups. Trap: William Harvey described the circulation of blood, which is a different blood-related discovery.
Source: PYQ-style

53. The periodic table of elements (1869) was given by:
(a) Gregor Mendel (b) Dmitri Mendeleev (c) John Dalton (d) Antoine Lavoisier
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dmitri Mendeleev arranged the known elements by atomic mass in 1869. Trap type: similar names — Gregor Mendel gave the laws of heredity.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry, Classification of Elements

54. The laws of inheritance were worked out by experiments on pea plants by:
(a) Charles Darwin (b) Gregor Mendel (c) Dmitri Mendeleev (d) Hugo de Vries
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gregor Mendel studied pea plants and gave the laws of inheritance. Trap type: similar names — Mendeleev made the periodic table.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Heredity

55. Electromagnetic induction, the principle behind the electric generator, was discovered by:
(a) Michael Faraday (b) Isaac Newton (c) Alessandro Volta (d) Thomas Edison
Answer: (a)
Solution: Michael Faraday discovered electromagnetic induction in 1831: a changing magnetic field produces current in a coil. Generators work on this principle.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Magnetic Effects of Electric Current

56. Dynamite was invented by:
(a) Alfred Nobel (b) James Watt (c) Rudolf Diesel (d) Robert Boyle
Answer: (a)
Solution: Alfred Nobel invented dynamite in 1867. His will set up the Nobel Prizes, first awarded in 1901.
Source: PYQ-style

57. The printing press with movable metal type was developed in Europe by:
(a) Johannes Gutenberg (b) William Caxton (c) Galileo Galilei (d) Johannes Kepler
Answer: (a)
Solution: Johannes Gutenberg developed the printing press with movable metal type around 1440 in Germany. Caxton later set up printing in England.
Source: NCERT Class 10 History, Print Culture and the Modern World

58. The first public demonstration of television was given by:
(a) John Logie Baird (b) Guglielmo Marconi (c) Alexander Graham Bell (d) Samuel Morse
Answer: (a)
Solution: John Logie Baird gave the first public demonstration of television in 1926. Marconi is linked with radio and Morse with the telegraph code.
Source: PYQ-style

59. Kelvin is the SI unit of:
(a) Heat (b) Temperature (c) Energy (d) Pressure
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kelvin (K) is the SI base unit of temperature. Trap: heat is a form of energy, so it is measured in joule, not kelvin.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Matter in Our Surroundings

60. The joule is the SI unit of:
(a) Force (b) Power (c) Work and energy (d) Momentum
Answer: (c)
Solution: Work and energy are both measured in joule (J); 1 joule = 1 newton × 1 metre. Force is in newton and power in watt.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Work and Energy

61. "Knot" is a unit used to measure:
(a) Depth of sea (b) Speed of ships (c) Distance between stars (d) Intensity of sound
Answer: (b)
Solution: A knot is one nautical mile per hour and is used for the speed of ships and aircraft. Depth of sea is measured in fathoms and sound level in decibels.
Source: PYQ-style

62. The power of a lens is measured in:
(a) Watt (b) Dioptre (c) Lux (d) Candela
Answer: (b)
Solution: The power of a lens is the reciprocal of its focal length in metres, and its unit is the dioptre (D). Trap: watt is the unit of power in mechanics and electricity, not of a lens.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Light – Reflection and Refraction

63. Decibel (dB) is a unit used for:
(a) Sound level (loudness) (b) Frequency of sound (c) Speed of sound (d) Wavelength of sound
Answer: (a)
Solution: The decibel measures the level (loudness) of sound. Frequency is measured in hertz and speed in metre per second.
Source: PYQ-style

64. Which of the following is an output device?
(a) Scanner (b) Mouse (c) Printer (d) Microphone
Answer: (c)
Solution: A printer gives the result of processing on paper, so it is an output device. Scanner, mouse and microphone send data into the computer.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

65. The full form of ROM is:
(a) Random Only Memory (b) Read Only Memory (c) Read Output Memory (d) Run Only Memory
Answer: (b)
Solution: ROM stands for Read Only Memory. It is non-volatile and stores the start-up instructions of a computer.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

66. HTML stands for:
(a) HyperText Markup Language (b) High Transfer Machine Language (c) HyperText Main Link (d) Home Tool Markup Language
Answer: (a)
Solution: HTML is HyperText Markup Language, used to create web pages. Trap: HTTP (HyperText Transfer Protocol) is the rule for sending those pages over the web.
Source: PYQ-style

67. First-generation computers used:
(a) Transistors (b) Integrated circuits (c) Vacuum tubes (d) Microprocessors
Answer: (c)
Solution: First-generation computers such as ENIAC (1946) used vacuum tubes. Transistors came in the second generation and ICs in the third.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Computer Science, Computer System

68. India's first sounding rocket was launched from Thumba in:
(a) 1963 (b) 1969 (c) 1975 (d) 1980
Answer: (a)
Solution: ISRO was formed in 1969, Aryabhata went up in 1975 and SLV-3 flew in 1980. The first sounding rocket rose from Thumba on 21 November 1963.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

69. Thumba, the site of India's first rocket launch, is near:
(a) Sriharikota (b) Thiruvananthapuram (c) Chennai (d) Balasore
Answer: (b)
Solution: Thumba Equatorial Rocket Launching Station (TERLS) is near Thiruvananthapuram in Kerala. The Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre grew from it.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

70. SLV-3, India's first satellite launch vehicle, placed which satellite in orbit in 1980?
(a) Aryabhata (b) Bhaskara (c) Rohini (d) INSAT-1B
Answer: (c)
Solution: On 18 July 1980 SLV-3 put the Rohini satellite into orbit from Sriharikota. Trap: Aryabhata (1975) and Bhaskara were launched by Soviet rockets.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

71. Who was the project director of SLV-3?
(a) Vikram Sarabhai (b) Satish Dhawan (c) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (d) K. Kasturirangan
Answer: (c)
Solution: A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was the project director of SLV-3, which successfully placed Rohini in orbit in 1980. He later led the missile programme.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

72. Chandrayaan-2 was launched in 2019 by:
(a) PSLV-C11 (b) GSLV Mk III (c) PSLV-C25 (d) SLV-3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chandrayaan-2 was launched on 22 July 2019 by GSLV Mk III (now called LVM3). PSLV-C11 launched Chandrayaan-1 and PSLV-C25 the Mars Orbiter Mission.
Source: ISRO (isro.gov.in)

73. On the Moon, "Tiranga Point" is the name given to:
(a) The landing site of Chandrayaan-3 (b) The spot where Chandrayaan-2's lander crashed (c) The impact site of Chandrayaan-1's probe (d) The landing site of Aditya-L1
Answer: (b)
Solution: The spot where Chandrayaan-2's Vikram lander crash-landed in 2019 was named Tiranga Point. Trap type: similar names — Chandrayaan-3's landing site is Shiv Shakti Point.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in), 2023

74. INS Vikrant, commissioned on 2 September 2022, is India's first indigenous:
(a) Nuclear submarine (b) Aircraft carrier (c) Destroyer (d) Frigate
Answer: (b)
Solution: INS Vikrant, built at Cochin Shipyard, is India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier. Trap type: almost true statement — it is a warship, but specifically an aircraft carrier.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in), 2022

75. C. V. Raman was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in:
(a) 1928 (b) 1913 (c) 1983 (d) 1930
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Raman Effect was announced in 1928, and 1983 is S. Chandrasekhar's Nobel year. Raman received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930.
Source: PYQ-style
