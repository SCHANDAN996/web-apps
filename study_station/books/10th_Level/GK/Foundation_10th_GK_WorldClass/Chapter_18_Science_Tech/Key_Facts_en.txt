📋 Key Facts & Figures: Science & Technology (Basic)

1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Telephone	Alexander Graham Bell, 1876.	Offering Edison (bulb) or Marconi (radio).	Similar Name
Electron / nucleus / neutron	J. J. Thomson (1897) / Rutherford (1911) / Chadwick (1932).	Swapping Thomson and Chadwick.	Similar Name
Mendel vs Mendeleev	Mendel — laws of heredity; Mendeleev — periodic table (1869).	Giving the periodic table to Mendel.	Similar Name
Polio vaccines	Salk — injected; Sabin — oral.	"Oral polio vaccine was developed by Salk."	Almost True Statement
Penicillin	Alexander Fleming, 1928 — first antibiotic.	Offering Pasteur (rabies vaccine) or Jenner (smallpox vaccine).	Similar Name
Raman Effect	Announced 28 February 1928; Nobel Physics 1930; National Science Day.	Offering 1928 as the Nobel year.	Chronological Confusion
SI base units	7: metre, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, candela.	Putting newton or joule in the list.	Negative Phrasing
Light-year	Unit of distance (about 9.46 × 10^15 m).	"Light-year is a unit of time."	Almost True Statement
RAM	Volatile main memory; ROM is non-volatile.	"RAM keeps data after power-off."	Almost True Statement
Computer generations	Vacuum tube → transistor → IC → microprocessor → AI.	Placing microprocessors in the third generation.	Chronological Confusion
INCOSPAR / ISRO / DOS	1962 / August 1969 / June 1972.	Giving 1962 as ISRO's founding year.	Chronological Confusion
Aryabhata	19 April 1975; built by ISRO, launched by the USSR.	"Launched by an Indian rocket."	Almost True Statement
SLV-3	18 July 1980, Rohini satellite; project director Kalam.	Offering PSLV as India's first launch vehicle.	Chronological Confusion
Rakesh Sharma	First Indian in space, 3 April 1984, Soyuz T-11.	Offering Kalpana Chawla (Indian-origin woman, 1997).	Similar Name
Chandrayaan-3	Launched 14 July 2023 (LVM3-M4); landed 23 August 2023 near the lunar south pole.	Saying "north pole", or giving the launch date as the landing date.	Almost True Statement
Mars Orbiter Mission	Launched 5 November 2013 (PSLV-C25); Mars orbit 24 September 2014.	Mixing up launch year and arrival year.	Chronological Confusion
Aditya-L1	Launched 2 September 2023 (PSLV-C57); halo orbit at L1 on 6 January 2024.	"Aditya-L1 studies the Moon."	Similar Name
Pokhran	Pokhran-I 18 May 1974; Pokhran-II 11 and 13 May 1998.	National Technology Day on 13 May or 18 May.	Chronological Confusion
IGMDP missiles	Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Trishul, Nag (1983, under Kalam).	Including BrahMos (India–Russia joint venture).	Negative Phrasing
INS Vikrant	First indigenous aircraft carrier, commissioned 2 September 2022.	Calling it a submarine or a destroyer.	Almost True Statement
Institutions	CDRI Lucknow, CFTRI Mysuru, CCMB Hyderabad, NIO Goa, NCL Pune.	Placing CDRI in Chennai or CFTRI in Lucknow.	Similar Name
