126. Consider the following statements regarding the Indian Councils Act 1892:
It introduced the principle of election for the first time in Indian legislative bodies.
The Act allowed non‑official members to discuss the annual financial statement.
It granted separate electorates to Muslims.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 1892 Act did not introduce separate electorates; that came in 1909. It did allow discussion of the budget and used an indirect election system, thus for the first time introduced a (limited) principle of election. Statement 1 is correct in that context. Statement 3 is wrong. Hence (b).
Source: PYQ-style

128. With reference to the Cripps Mission (1942), consider the following proposals:
India would be given Dominion Status immediately after the war.

The defence of India would be handed over to an Indian Commander‑in‑Chief right away.
Which of the above proposals were actually made by the Cripps Mission?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Cripps Mission offered a post‑war Dominion Status with the right of provinces to secede. It did not agree to immediate transfer of defence; that was a key reason for the deadlock. Statement 3 is wrong.
