126. Consider the following statements regarding the Indian Councils Act 1892:

It introduced the principle of election for the first time in Indian legislative bodies.

The Act allowed non‑official members to discuss the annual financial statement.

It granted separate electorates to Muslims.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (b) 1 and 2 only
Step-by-step: The 1892 Act did not introduce separate electorates; that came in 1909. It did allow discussion of the budget and used an indirect election system, thus for the first time introduced a (limited) principle of election. Statement 1 is correct in that context. Statement 3 is wrong. Hence (b).
Source: PYQ-style
🧪 Trap Type: Chronological Confusion (many students associate separate electorates with the 1892 Act because of the expanded councils, but it was 1909.)

150. Which of the following statements is/are correct regarding the “Drain of Wealth” theory?

It was most systematically articulated by Dadabhai Naoroji.

Select the correct answer:
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3
Step-by-step: Naoroji’s Poverty and Un‑British Rule and Dutt’s Economic History of India both highlighted the drain. Home Charges (interest on loans, salaries of British officials, etc.) were a key component.
Source: PYQ-style
