126. Consider the following statements regarding the Indian Councils Act 1892:
It introduced, though indirectly, the principle of election for the first time in Indian legislative bodies.
The Act allowed members to discuss the annual financial statement under certain restrictions.
It granted separate electorates to Muslims.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting trap is statement 3 — separate electorates for Muslims came with the Morley–Minto Reforms of 1909, not 1892. The 1892 Act did permit budget discussion and used nomination on the recommendation of certain bodies, which amounted to an indirect election principle, so statements 1 and 2 are correct.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming Gandhi led every famous satyagraha; the Bardoli Satyagraha of 1928 was led by Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, who earned the title "Sardar" there. The other three pairs are correctly matched, so the incorrectly matched pair is Bardoli Satyagraha – Mahatma Gandhi.

128. With reference to the Cripps Mission (1942), consider the following proposals:
India would be given Dominion Status after the war.
Provinces could opt out of the proposed Indian Union.
The defence of India would be handed over to an Indian Commander-in-Chief immediately.
Which of the above proposals were actually made by the Cripps Mission?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is statement 3 — the Cripps proposals kept defence with the British during the war, and this refusal to transfer real power immediately was a key reason Congress rejected the offer. Dominion Status after the war and the right of provinces to secede were both offered, so the correct combination is 1 and 2.

129. The Ryotwari system of land revenue, under which the cultivator paid revenue directly to the state, was introduced mainly by:
(a) Lord Cornwallis (b) Thomas Munro (c) Lord Wellesley (d) Warren Hastings
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Cornwallis, who is famous for the Permanent Settlement (zamindari) in Bengal — the opposite of ryotwari. Thomas Munro introduced the Ryotwari system in the Madras Presidency around 1820, so the answer is Thomas Munro.

(a) Temple entry and removal of untouchability (b) Minimum wages for industrial labour (c) Abolition of the salt tax (d) Peasant tenancy rights
