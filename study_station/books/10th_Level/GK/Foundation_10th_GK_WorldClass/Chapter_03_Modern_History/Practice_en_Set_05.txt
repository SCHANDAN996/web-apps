📝 Practice Set 5 (Questions 101–125)

Medium (Q101–Q120)

101. Who among the following was the first Indian to become a member of the Viceroy’s Executive Council?
(a) Satyendra Prasanna Sinha
(b) B. R. Ambedkar
(c) Tej Bahadur Sapru
(d) C. Rajagopalachari

Answer: (a) Satyendra Prasanna Sinha
Step-by-step: Under the Morley‑Minto Reforms (1909), Sinha was appointed as the Law Member, becoming the first Indian in the Viceroy’s Executive Council.
Source: PYQ-style

102. The Kheda Satyagraha (1918) was related to:
(a) Indigo cultivation
(b) Revenue remission during famine
(c) Plague bonus for workers
(d) Forest rights

Answer: (b) Revenue remission during famine
Step-by-step: Gandhi led peasants in Kheda district, Gujarat, demanding the suspension of land revenue after crop failure and famine.
Source: PYQ-style

(b) Indian Councils Act 1909
(c) Government of India Act 1919
(d) Government of India Act 1935

Answer: (b) Indian Councils Act 1909
Step-by-step: The Morley‑Minto Reforms (1909) introduced separate electorates for Muslims at the central and provincial levels.
Source: PYQ-style

104. The Lucknow Pact (1916) was an agreement between:
(a) Congress and the Muslim League
(b) Congress and the British government
(c) Muslim League and the British government
(d) Congress and the Home Rule League

Answer: (a) Congress and the Muslim League
Step-by-step: The Pact was signed during the joint session in Lucknow, outlining joint demands for self‑government and agreeing to separate electorates for Muslims.
Source: Banking 2020

105. Arrange the following events in chronological order:

Dandi March

Gandhi‑Irwin Pact

First Round Table Conference

Poona Pact

Codes:
(a) 1, 2, 3, 4
(b) 1, 3, 2, 4
(c) 1, 3, 4, 2
(d) 3, 1, 2, 4

Answer: (b) 1, 3, 2, 4
Step-by-step: Dandi March (March‑April 1930), First Round Table Conference (Nov 1930–Jan 1931), Gandhi‑Irwin Pact (March 1931), Poona Pact (September 1932). Thus order: 1, 3, 2, 4.
Source: PYQ-style
🧪 Trap Type: Chronological Confusion (students often place the Poona Pact before the Gandhi‑Irwin Pact or misplace the First Round Table Conference).

Hard (Q121–Q125)

121. Which of the following was not a recommendation of the Simon Commission?
(a) Abolition of dyarchy in the provinces
(b) Continuation of separate electorates
(c) Establishment of a federal system at the centre
(d) Extension of responsible government in the provinces

Answer: (c) Establishment of a federal system at the centre
Step-by-step: The Simon Commission (1927–30) recommended provincial autonomy, abolition of dyarchy, continuation of separate electorates, and a unitary centre. A federal system was proposed later by the Round Table Conferences and the 1935 Act.
Source: PYQ-style
🧪 Trap Type: Nearly True Statement (many students mistakenly associate the federal idea with the Simon Commission; in reality, it recommended a strong unitary centre).

122. Consider the following statements regarding the Indian National Army (INA):

The INA was formed by Subhas Chandra Bose in Singapore in 1943.

The INA fought alongside the British in the Burma campaign.

The Rani of Jhansi Regiment was an all‑women infantry unit of the INA.

The INA trials were held at the Red Fort in Delhi in 1945–46.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1, 2, and 3 only
(b) 2 and 4 only
(c) 1, 3, and 4 only
(d) 1, 2, 3, and 4

Answer: (c) 1, 3, and 4 only
Step-by-step: Statement 2 is incorrect: the INA fought against the British, not alongside them, during the Burma campaign. All other statements are historically accurate.
Source: PYQ-style
🧪 Trap Type: Nearly True Statement (the false statement 2 appears plausible because both the INA and British were in Burma; reading “alongside” quickly can cause error).

124. With reference to the August Declaration of 1917, consider the following statements:

It promised the gradual development of self‑governing institutions in India.

It was issued by Lord Chelmsford.

It directly led to the Montagu‑Chelmsford Reforms.

Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2, and 3

Answer: (b) 1 and 3 only
Step-by-step: The declaration was made by Edwin Montagu, the Secretary of State for India, not by the Viceroy Lord Chelmsford. Hence statement 2 is incorrect. Statements 1 and 3 are correct.
Source: PYQ-style
🧪 Trap Type: Similar Name/Title Confusion (students often attribute the declaration to the Viceroy rather than the Secretary of State).

(a) The first session held in a rural area
(b) The session that adopted the demand for Pakistan
(c) The session where the Quit India Movement was launched
(d) The session that accepted the Cabinet Mission Plan

Answer: (a) The first session held in a rural area
Step-by-step: The 1936 session, presided over by Jawaharlal Nehru, was held in the village of Faizpur (Maharashtra), marking the first time a Congress session was organised in a rural setting.
Source: PYQ-style
