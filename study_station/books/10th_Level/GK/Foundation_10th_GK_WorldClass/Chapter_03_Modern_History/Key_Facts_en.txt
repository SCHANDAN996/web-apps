📊 PYQ विश्लेषण – आधुनिक भारतीय इतिहास (Trap Analysis & 15/45-Second Rule)
1. High-Yield Facts with Examiner’s Trap Analysis
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Morley-Minto Reforms (1909)	Introduced separate electorate for Muslims	"Separate electorate for Dalits was first introduced by the 1909 Act." (False: Muslims, not Dalits)	Chronological Confusion / Community Swap
Communal Award (1932)	Gave separate electorate to Depressed Classes	"The Poona Pact retained the separate electorate for Depressed Classes." (False: Poona Pact replaced it with reserved seats)	Reverse Causation / Nearly True Statement
Poona Pact (1932)	Replaced separate electorate with reserved seats in joint electorates	Often wrongly stated that Poona Pact gave separate electorate. Watch for "Poona Pact provided separate electorates" – this is false.	Nearly True Statement (90% correct but 10% fatal)
Doctrine of Lapse	Annexed states without natural heir (Dalhousie)	"Subsidiary Alliance was used to annex states without heir." (False, that was Doctrine of Lapse)	Reverse Causation / Policy Mix-up
Subsidiary Alliance	Stationing British troops, control foreign affairs	See above – mixing with Doctrine of Lapse	Reverse Causation
Revolt of 1857 – "First War of Independence"	Savarkar's term, not contemporary official term	"The British officially declared 1857 as the First War of Independence." (False)	Nearly True Statement / Anachronism
Partition of Bengal (1905)	Lord Curzon	"Partition of Bengal was annulled by Lord Curzon in 1911." (False: annulled by Hardinge II)	Chronological Confusion / Wrong Governor-General
Gandhi-Irwin Pact (1931)	Suspension of Civil Disobedience, Round Table Conference	"Gandhi-Irwin Pact led to the withdrawal of the Non-Cooperation Movement." (False: it was related to CDM, not NCM)	Chronological Confusion / Movement Mix-up
Chauri-Chaura Incident (1922)	Gandhi withdrew Non-Cooperation Movement after violence	"Gandhi withdrew the Civil Disobedience Movement after Chauri-Chaura." (False, CDM was 1930, NCM was 1922)	Chronological Confusion / Movement Swap
Quit India Movement (1942)	"Do or Die" slogan	"Quit India Movement was launched after the failure of the Cripps Mission (1942) and also after the Cabinet Mission (1946)." (Only Cripps, not Cabinet)	Chronological Compounding
First session of INC (1885)	Bombay, W.C. Bonnerjee president	"The first session of INC was held in 1885 at Calcutta." (False, Bombay)	Place Swap
2. Time Management – 15/45-Second Rule
Topic/Fact Type	Time Budget	Reason & Strategy
15-Second Facts (Direct Recall)
Year of Battle of Plassey, Buxar	15 sec	Pure memory, no analysis needed. Recall & mark.
Founder of Brahmo Samaj	15 sec	Single name. No multiple links.
Year of Morley-Minto Reforms	15 sec	Date-based.
First Viceroy of India (Canning)	15 sec	Single fact.
INC first session city & president	15 sec	Direct recall.
Slogans – "Do or Die", "Dilli Chalo"	15 sec	One-to-one mapping.
Match leader with revolt centre (1857)	15 sec	Memorize the 5 pairs. Quick match.
45-Second Analytical Facts (Score Maximizers)
Sequence of British Acts (1773–1935) and their provisions	45 sec	Order, cause-effect, linking provisions – high weightage. Score Maximizer
Separate Electorate timeline and the Poona Pact modification	45 sec	Chronological reasoning and consequence. Score Maximizer
Comparison of Moderates vs Extremists	45 sec	Requires understanding of ideology and change.
Doctrine of Lapse vs Subsidiary Alliance – differentiate	45 sec	Policy comparison. Common trap area. Score Maximizer
Gandhian movements – causes, withdrawal, outcomes	45 sec	Multi-step reasoning.
Revolutionaries – organizations and key actions (Kakori, Assembly bombing)	45 sec	Multiple names and events interlinked. Score Maximizer
Constitutional progress – which Act introduced what	45 sec	Application of knowledge in "which of the following is correct" type MCQs.
