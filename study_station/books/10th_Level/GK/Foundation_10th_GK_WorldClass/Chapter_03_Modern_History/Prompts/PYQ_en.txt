4. Top 10 High-Yield Questions with Solutions & Trap Analysis

Q1. With reference to the revolt of 1857, consider the following statements:

It was a planned, pan-India uprising with a unified command.

The British at the time officially termed it the "First War of Independence."
Which of the above statements is/are correct? (UPSC-style)

(a) 1 only

(b) 2 only

(c) Both 1 and 2

(d) Neither 1 nor 2

Answer: (d) Neither 1 nor 2.
Trap Type: Nearly True Statement (90% correct). Statement 1 is false as it lacked coordination; statement 2 is false as the British called it "Sepoy Mutiny". "First War of Independence" was Savarkar's later term.
15/45-Second Rule: This is an analytical question requiring understanding of the nature of the revolt. Allocate 45 seconds. Scan both statements for the exaggerated "planned, pan-India" and the anachronistic label.

Q2. Arrange the following events in chronological order: (SSC style)

Gandhi-Irwin Pact

Poona Pact

Communal Award

Third Round Table Conference

(a) 1, 3, 2, 4

(b) 1, 2, 3, 4

(c) 3, 1, 2, 4

(d) 1, 3, 4, 2

Answer: (a) 1 (1931), 3 (1932), 2 (1932 after CA), 4 (1932). So 1, 3, 2, 4.
Trap Type: Chronological Confusion. The Poona Pact came immediately after Communal Award to modify it. Many mix the order.
15/45-Second Rule: Fact-based chronological order. 15 seconds if you know the years.

Q3. The Doctrine of Lapse was introduced by which Governor-General? (Banking style)

(a) Lord Wellesley

(b) Lord Dalhousie

(c) Lord Cornwallis

(d) Lord Hastings

Answer: (b) Lord Dalhousie.
Trap Type: Policy Mix-up (Reverse Causation). Wellesley is associated with Subsidiary Alliance, but examiners might place him as option (a) to confuse.
15-Second Rule: Pure factual recall.

Q4. Consider the following pairs: (UPSC-style matching)
Movement Founder

Brahmo Samaj : Raja Ram Mohan Roy

Arya Samaj : Swami Vivekananda

Ramakrishna Mission : Swami Dayanand Saraswati
Which of the above is/are correctly matched?

(a) 1 only

(b) 1 and 2 only

(c) 2 and 3 only

(d) 1, 2 and 3

Answer: (a) 1 only. Arya Samaj – Dayanand, Ramakrishna Mission – Vivekananda.
Trap Type: Similar Name/Founder Confusion. Swapping founders between Arya Samaj and Ramakrishna Mission is a classic trap.

Q5. The phrase "Do or Die" is associated with which movement? (Railway style)

(a) Non-Cooperation Movement

(b) Civil Disobedience Movement

(c) Quit India Movement

(d) Home Rule Movement

Answer: (c) Quit India Movement.
Trap Type: Movement-Slogan Swap. "Do or Die" and Quit India are sometimes wrongly matched with Civil Disobedience in memory.

Q6. With reference to the Government of India Act 1935, which of the following statements is correct? (UPSC style)

(a) It abolished Dyarchy at the provincial level.

(b) It introduced Dyarchy at the Centre.

(c) The federal scheme was fully implemented.

(d) It gave full provincial autonomy without restrictions.

Answer: (b) It introduced Dyarchy at the Centre. (It abolished provincial dyarchy, introduced provincial autonomy, but dyarchy was at Centre; federal scheme never implemented.)
Trap Type: Nearly True Statement. Options (a) and (d) are partially true but not entirely correct. Option (a) says "abolished Dyarchy at provincial level" – true, but the Act introduced it at the Centre, and option (b) is more specific. Often such framing makes one choose (a). The actual correct statement is (b), which is less obvious.
45-Second Rule: Requires detailed knowledge of the Act's provisions.

Q7. The Partition of Bengal (1905) was annulled by: (SSC style)

(a) Lord Curzon

(b) Lord Minto

(c) Lord Hardinge

(d) Lord Chelmsford

Answer: (c) Lord Hardinge II.
Trap Type: Chronological Confusion / Wrong Governor-General. Curzon partitioned, Hardinge annulled. Examiner often places Curzon as the first option to tempt.

Q8. Which of the following leaders is NOT correctly matched with the 1857 revolt centre? (Negative phrasing trap)

(a) Begum Hazrat Mahal – Lucknow

(b) Rani Lakshmibai – Jhansi

(c) Nana Sahib – Kanpur

(d) Tantia Tope – Delhi

Answer: (d) Tantia Tope was associated with Kanpur and Gwalior, not Delhi.
Trap Type: Negative Phrasing + Place Swap. "NOT correctly matched" is often missed by rushing students. Tantia Tope in Delhi is a common error.

Q9. The "Drain of Wealth" theory was propounded by: (Banking style)

(a) Mahatma Gandhi

(b) Dadabhai Naoroji

(c) Gopal Krishna Gokhale

(d) Bal Gangadhar Tilak

Answer: (b) Dadabhai Naoroji.
Trap Type: Name Confusion among Moderates.

Q10. In the context of the Indian National Movement, the Poona Pact (1932) is known for: (UPSC style)

(a) Granting separate electorates to the Depressed Classes.

(b) Reserving seats for the Depressed Classes in joint electorates.

(c) Giving separate electorates to Muslims.

(d) Creating a federal structure for India.

Answer: (b) Reserving seats for the Depressed Classes in joint electorates.
Trap Type: Reverse Causation / Nearly True Statement. Option (a) is the Communal Award; Poona Pact modified it.
15-Second Rule: Direct recall if you clearly remember the reversal. Avoid (a) which is a lure.
