51. Which of the following statements about the Rowlatt Act (1919) is correct?
(a) It was opposed by all Indian members of the Imperial Legislative Council. (b) It allowed detention without trial for a maximum of six months. (c) It was repealed in 1922 after the Non-Cooperation Movement. (d) It was passed before the Jallianwala Bagh massacre.
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rowlatt Act came into effect in March 1919; Jallianwala Bagh occurred on 13 April 1919. Indian members opposed it but "all" is incorrect (some nominated members supported it). It was never officially repealed. Detention was indefinite, not six months. Thus (d) is correct.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Arrange the following events in chronological order:
Moplah Rebellion
Chauri Chaura incident
Eka Movement
Bardoli Satyagraha
Codes:
(a) 2 → 1 → 3 → 4 (b) 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 (c) 1 → 3 → 2 → 4 (d) 2 → 3 → 1 → 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: Moplah Rebellion (1921, Malabar), Eka Movement (1921–22, UP), Chauri Chaura (Feb 1922), Bardoli Satyagraha (1928, Gujarat). Order: 1, 3, 2, 4.

54. The dual system of government (Dyarchy) in the provinces was introduced by which Act?
(a) Indian Councils Act 1892 (b) Morley‑Minto Reforms 1909 (c) Montagu‑Chelmsford Reforms 1919 (d) Government of India Act 1935
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 1919 Act divided provincial subjects into “transferred” and “reserved” categories, known as Dyarchy.

60. With reference to the Indian Councils Act 1909, consider the following statements:

2. It increased the size of legislative councils and allowed discussion of the budget.
3. It introduced dyarchy in the provincial governments.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Separate electorates for Muslims and increased council size/powers were features of the 1909 Act (statements 1 and 2). Dyarchy was introduced by the Government of India Act 1919, not the 1909 Act (statement 3 is incorrect).

74. With reference to the Indian National Congress, consider the following statements:
Dadabhai Naoroji was the first Parsi president of the INC.
The INC demanded complete independence for the first time at its 1906 Calcutta session.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Naoroji was the first Parsi president (Calcutta, 1906). However, the 1906 session demanded Swaraj (self‑rule), not complete independence. Complete independence (Purna Swaraj) was demanded at the 1929 Lahore session. So 2 is wrong.

75. The “Wavell Plan” (1945) proposed:
(a) Grant of Dominion Status after the war. (b) An interim government with equal Muslim and non‑Muslim members. (c) Partition of India. (d) Complete transfer of power to a Constituent Assembly.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Wavell Plan, discussed at the Shimla Conference (1945), proposed that the Viceroy’s Executive Council be reconstituted with equal numbers of caste Hindus and Muslims (and other minorities represented). It was rejected.
