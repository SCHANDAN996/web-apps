🃏 Flashcard Deck: States & Rivers (Foundation)

Card 1
Front: A man's fast changed India's map in 1953. Which state was born, and why is it special?
Back: Andhra State (1 October 1953), after Potti Sriramulu's fast — the first state formed on the basis of language.

Card 2
Front: How many states and Union Territories does India have, and since when has this count stood?
Back: 28 states and 8 UTs, since 26 January 2020 (when Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu merged).

Card 3
Front: Two head-streams meet in Uttarakhand and a new name is born. Where, and which two?
Back: At Devprayag — the Bhagirathi and the Alaknanda meet to form the Ganga.

Card 4
Front: One river, four names. What does the Brahmaputra call itself in Tibet, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam and Bangladesh?
Back: Tsangpo → Dihang (Siang) → Brahmaputra → Jamuna.

Card 5
Front: Mnemonic check: what does "Jo Chalega Raste Bina Sahara" stand for?
Back: The five Punjab rivers from west to east — Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej.

Card 6
Front: Mnemonic check: what is "Vi-Na-Ka-Ru-De"?
Back: The five Prayags of the Alaknanda, upstream to downstream — Vishnuprayag, Nandprayag, Karnaprayag, Rudraprayag, Devprayag.

Card 7
Front: The Tropic of Cancer is a long guest. How many states does it visit, and which are the first and last?
Back: 8 states — from Gujarat in the west to Mizoram in the east.

Card 8
Front: This tiny state touches three countries. Which state, and which countries?
Back: Sikkim — Nepal, China (Tibet) and Bhutan.

Card 9
Front: Which river is the "Dakshin Ganga", and where does it rise?
Back: The Godavari — the largest peninsular river (about 1500 km), rising near Trimbakeshwar in Nashik district.

Card 10
Front: Most peninsular rivers run east. Which two long rivers break the rule, and what do they form at their mouths?
Back: The Narmada and the Tapi — they flow west through rift valleys and form estuaries, not deltas.

Card 11
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Krishnaraja Sagar dam is on the Krishna." True or false?
Back: False. KRS dam is on the Kaveri (Karnataka). The name "Krishnaraja" is the trap (Similar Name).

Card 12
Front: Examiner's trap: "Himachal Pradesh became a full state in 1966." What is wrong?
Back: In 1966 it only received hill areas of Punjab. It became a full state on 25 January 1971 (Chronological Confusion).

Card 13
Front: Which river is the "Sorrow of Bihar", and which is the "Sorrow of Bengal"?
Back: Kosi — Sorrow of Bihar; Damodar — Sorrow of Bengal.

Card 14
Front: Under the Indus Waters Treaty (1960), which three rivers did India get?
Back: The eastern rivers — Ravi, Beas and Sutlej. (In April 2025 India put the treaty in abeyance.)

Card 15
Front: Born in the same month, a week apart: which three states were created in November 2000, in order?
Back: Chhattisgarh (1 Nov), Uttarakhand (9 Nov), Jharkhand (15 Nov).

Card 16
Front: Hirakud, Bhakra, Tehri, Sardar Sarovar — match each dam with its river.
Back: Hirakud – Mahanadi; Bhakra – Sutlej; Tehri – Bhagirathi; Sardar Sarovar – Narmada.

Card 17
Front: Which river ends in a salt marsh instead of the sea?
Back: The Luni of Rajasthan — it rises near Ajmer and ends in the Rann of Kutch.

Card 18
Front: Why does the Kaveri have a steadier flow than most peninsular rivers?
Back: Its upper basin gets south-west monsoon rain and its lower basin gets north-east (retreating) monsoon rain.
