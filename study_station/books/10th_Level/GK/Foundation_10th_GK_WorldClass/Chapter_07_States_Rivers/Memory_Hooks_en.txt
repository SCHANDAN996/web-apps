🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Mnemonic 1: Five Rivers of Punjab (west to east)
Sentence: "Jo Chalega Raste Bina Sahara"
Jo → Jhelum | Chalega → Chenab | Raste → Ravi | Bina → Beas | Sahara → Sutlej
Visual: a man walking east across Punjab without a walking stick, crossing five rivers one by one.

🧠 Mnemonic 2: Indus Waters Treaty — India's rivers
Rule: "India got the RBS bank account" — Ravi, Beas, Sutlej (the eastern rivers).
The western three (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) went mainly to Pakistan.

🧠 Mnemonic 3: Panch Prayag of the Alaknanda (upstream to downstream)
Chant: "Vi-Na-Ka-Ru-De" — "Vinay Ka Rude Dost"
Vishnuprayag (Dhauliganga) → Nandprayag (Nandakini) → Karnaprayag (Pindar) → Rudraprayag (Mandakini) → Devprayag (Bhagirathi — the Ganga is born).

🧠 Mnemonic 4: Brahmaputra's four names
Story: "Tea Drinkers Bring Jam" — Tsangpo (Tibet) → Dihang (Arunachal) → Brahmaputra (Assam) → Jamuna (Bangladesh).
Visual: a tea-loving traveller carrying a jar of jam from Tibet to Bangladesh.

🧠 Mnemonic 5: East-coast river mouths (north to south)
Sentence: "My Good Kind Kitten" — Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri.

🧠 Mnemonic 6: River sources — the "MAT-TB" map
Mahabaleshwar → Krishna | Amarkantak → Narmada (and Son) | Trimbakeshwar → Godavari | Talakaveri (Brahmagiri) → Kaveri | Betul (Multai) → Tapi
Trap check: Mahabaleshwar is NOT the Mahanadi — the Mahanadi rises in Chhattisgarh.

🧠 Mnemonic 7: Tropic of Cancer — 8 states (west to east)
Sentence: "Good Rice Makes Children Jump With Tasty Mango"
Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram.

🧠 Mnemonic 8: The 2000 trio, in order of birth
"CUJ — Cute Ugly Joker": Chhattisgarh (1 Nov) → Uttarakhand (9 Nov) → Jharkhand (15 Nov).

🧠 Mnemonic 9: Sorrows of the East
"Damodar sounds like Dum Dum (near Kolkata)" → Damodar = Sorrow of Bengal.
The other one, the Kosi from Nepal, = Sorrow of Bihar. Picture: "Kosi floods the Bihar fields; Damodar drums in Bengal."

🧠 Mnemonic 10: States bordering Bangladesh
"MATMW" → "Mother And Tea Make Warmth": Meghalaya, Assam, Tripura, Mizoram, West Bengal (WB has the longest border).

🧠 Mnemonic 11: States bordering Myanmar
"AN-MM" → "A New Mobile Model": Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram.

🧠 Mnemonic 12: Dam–river quick pairs
"Hira (diamond) in the Maha-river" → Hirakud on the Mahanadi.
"Bhakra Sat-lu-j" → Bhakra on the Sutlej; "Pong pongs on the Beas".
"Tehri is a Bhagi (lucky) dam" → Tehri on the Bhagirathi.
"Sardar walks by the Narmada" → Sardar Sarovar on the Narmada.

🧠 Mnemonic 13: Estuary twins
"N.T. = No Delta, Tidal mouth" → Narmada and Tapi form estuaries, flow west, rift valleys.

🧠 Mnemonic 14: Name changes with years
"Madras 69, Mysore 73, Uttaranchal 07, Orissa 11" — read it like a cricket scorecard: 69, 73, 07, 11.

⏭️ Skip Strategy

1. If a question asks you to arrange five or more river confluences or state-formation dates and you cannot place even two of them with certainty, skip it in the first round — it eats 45 seconds or more and the options are built to confuse.

2. If a "match the following" lists four dams of rivers you have never studied (small regional dams), do not guess blindly. Use elimination only when you are sure about at least two pairs; otherwise skip.

3. If a question asks for an exact river length or area figure in kilometres and the options are close to each other, skip unless you know it from NCERT — these are low-return questions in the Foundation exams.
