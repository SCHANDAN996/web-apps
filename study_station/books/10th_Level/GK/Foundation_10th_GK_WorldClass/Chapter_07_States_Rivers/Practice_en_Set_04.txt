Set 4/6 – States & Rivers MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Tropic of Cancer passes through how many Indian states?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (c)
Solution: It passes through Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram, that is 8 states.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

77. The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which of the following states?
(a) Tripura (b) Mizoram (c) Odisha (d) Jharkhand
Answer: (c)
Solution: Odisha lies wholly south of the Tropic of Cancer. Tripura, Mizoram and Jharkhand are among the eight states it crosses. Trap type: negative phrasing.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

78. Which Indian state shares international borders with Nepal, Bhutan and China?
(a) Sikkim (b) West Bengal (c) Arunachal Pradesh (d) Assam
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sikkim touches Nepal (west), China (north and east) and Bhutan (south-east). West Bengal touches Nepal, Bhutan and Bangladesh, but not China.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

79. Which of the following states does NOT share a border with Bangladesh?
(a) Tripura (b) Meghalaya (c) Manipur (d) Mizoram
Answer: (c)
Solution: The five states bordering Bangladesh are West Bengal, Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram. Manipur borders Myanmar, not Bangladesh.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

80. Which state has the longest border with Bangladesh?
(a) West Bengal (b) Assam (c) Tripura (d) Meghalaya
Answer: (a)
Solution: West Bengal has the longest stretch of the India–Bangladesh border. Trap: Tripura is surrounded by Bangladesh on three sides, but its border is shorter.
Source: PYQ-style

81. Which of the following states does NOT border Myanmar?
(a) Nagaland (b) Manipur (c) Arunachal Pradesh (d) Tripura
Answer: (d)
Solution: Myanmar borders four states — Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur and Mizoram. Tripura borders Bangladesh only.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

82. Which of the following states does NOT share a border with Nepal?
(a) Uttarakhand (b) Himachal Pradesh (c) Bihar (d) Sikkim
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nepal borders Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal and Sikkim. Himachal Pradesh borders China (Tibet), not Nepal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

83. Which state has the longest coastline in India?
(a) Gujarat (b) Andhra Pradesh (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Maharashtra
Answer: (a)
Solution: Gujarat, with the Gulf of Kachchh and the Gulf of Khambhat, has the longest coastline among Indian states. Andhra Pradesh has the longest coastline on the east coast.
Source: PYQ-style

84. Kohima is the capital of which state?
(a) Manipur (b) Nagaland (c) Mizoram (d) Meghalaya
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kohima is the capital of Nagaland. Imphal is Manipur's capital, Aizawl Mizoram's and Shillong Meghalaya's.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

85. Aizawl is the capital of which state?
(a) Tripura (b) Manipur (c) Meghalaya (d) Mizoram
Answer: (d)
Solution: Aizawl is the capital of Mizoram. Agartala is the capital of Tripura.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

86. In 2024, the capital of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands was renamed Sri Vijaya Puram. What was its earlier name?
(a) Port Blair (b) Car Nicobar (c) Havelock (d) Diglipur
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Government of India renamed Port Blair as Sri Vijaya Puram in September 2024.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

87. Kavaratti is the capital of which Union Territory?
(a) Puducherry (b) Andaman and Nicobar Islands (c) Lakshadweep (d) Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kavaratti is the capital of Lakshadweep, the coral islands in the Arabian Sea. Daman is the capital of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

88. On which date did Ladakh become a separate Union Territory?
(a) 5 August 2019 (b) 31 October 2019 (c) 26 January 2020 (d) 15 August 2019
Answer: (b)
Solution: Trap: 26 January 2020 is the date of the Dadra–Daman merger. Under the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, the UTs of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh came into being on 31 October 2019.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

89. Which is the largest Union Territory of India by area?
(a) Jammu & Kashmir (b) Ladakh (c) Andaman and Nicobar Islands (d) Delhi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Since 31 October 2019, Ladakh is the largest UT by area. Before 2019, the Andaman and Nicobar Islands held this position, which is the trap.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

90. Which is the smallest Union Territory of India by area?
(a) Lakshadweep (b) Chandigarh (c) Puducherry (d) Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lakshadweep, a group of small coral islands, is the smallest UT by area. Chandigarh is small too, but larger than Lakshadweep.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

91. Kota in Rajasthan is located on which river?
(a) Betwa (b) Banas (c) Mahi (d) Chambal
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kota is on the Chambal, which has the Gandhi Sagar, Rana Pratap Sagar and Jawahar Sagar dams and the Kota barrage.
Source: PYQ-style

92. Ujjain, a city of the Kumbh Mela, stands on which river?
(a) Shipra (b) Narmada (c) Chambal (d) Betwa
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ujjain is on the Shipra (Kshipra), a tributary of the Chambal. Trap: Narmada is the best-known river of Madhya Pradesh, but it flows far south of Ujjain.
Source: PYQ-style

93. Ayodhya is situated on the banks of which river?
(a) Gomti (b) Gandak (c) Sarayu (Ghaghara) (d) Son
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ayodhya is on the Sarayu, the name of the Ghaghara in this stretch. Lucknow is on the Gomti.
Source: PYQ-style

94. Ludhiana in Punjab is located near which river?
(a) Sutlej (b) Beas (c) Ravi (d) Jhelum
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ludhiana lies near the Sutlej. The Ravi flows near Amritsar district along the border, and the Jhelum is in Kashmir.
Source: PYQ-style

95. Hyderabad stands on which river?
(a) Krishna (b) Musi (c) Godavari (d) Tungabhadra
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hyderabad is on the Musi, a tributary of the Krishna. Trap: students pick the Krishna itself.
Source: PYQ-style

96. Cuttack, the old capital of Odisha, stands on which river?
(a) Brahmani (b) Baitarani (c) Subarnarekha (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cuttack stands at the head of the Mahanadi delta, where the river divides into branches.
Source: PYQ-style

97. Guwahati is located on the banks of which river?
(a) Barak (b) Teesta (c) Brahmaputra (d) Subansiri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Guwahati is on the south bank of the Brahmaputra in Assam. The Barak flows in southern Assam (Silchar).
Source: PYQ-style

98. Kolkata is located on the banks of which river?
(a) Hooghly (b) Damodar (c) Padma (d) Teesta
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kolkata is on the Hooghly, a distributary of the Ganga. Padma is the Ganga's name in Bangladesh.
Source: PYQ-style

99. The Taj Mahal city of Agra stands on which river?
(a) Ganga (b) Yamuna (c) Chambal (d) Gomti
Answer: (b)
Solution: Agra is on the Yamuna, as are Delhi and Mathura. The Chambal joins the Yamuna further downstream.
Source: PYQ-style

100. Consider the following statements:
I. Delhi lies on the banks of the Yamuna.
II. Varanasi and Patna both lie on the Ganga.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both statements are correct. Delhi is on the Yamuna, while Varanasi (Uttar Pradesh) and Patna (Bihar) are on the Ganga.
Source: PYQ-style
