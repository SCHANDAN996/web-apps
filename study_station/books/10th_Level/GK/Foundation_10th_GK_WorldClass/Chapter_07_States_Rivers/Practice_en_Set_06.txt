Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Arrange the mouths of the following east-flowing rivers from north to south along the east coast:
I. Krishna
II. Mahanadi
III. Kaveri
IV. Godavari
(a) II, IV, I, III (b) IV, II, I, III (c) II, I, IV, III (d) IV, I, II, III
Answer: (a)
Solution: From north to south: Mahanadi (Odisha), Godavari and then Krishna (both Andhra Pradesh), and Kaveri (Tamil Nadu). Trap: Godavari and Krishna deltas are neighbours, and the Godavari lies to the north.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

127. Arrange the following left-bank tributaries in the order in which they join the Ganga, from west to east:
I. Kosi
II. Gomti
III. Gandak
IV. Ghaghara
(a) II, III, IV, I (b) IV, II, III, I (c) II, IV, III, I (d) II, IV, I, III
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Gomti joins near Varanasi–Ghazipur, the Ghaghara near Chhapra, the Gandak opposite Patna (Hajipur) and the Kosi last, in eastern Bihar.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

128. Arrange the five rivers of the Punjab region from west to east:
I. Ravi
II. Jhelum
III. Sutlej
IV. Chenab
V. Beas
(a) II, I, IV, V, III (b) II, IV, I, V, III (c) IV, II, I, III, V (d) II, IV, V, I, III
Answer: (b)
Solution: West to east: Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej. Memory hook: 'Jo Chalega Raste Bina Sahara' (J-C-R-B-S).
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

129. Which state is crossed by the Tropic of Cancer and also shares borders with both Bangladesh and Myanmar?
(a) Tripura (b) Mizoram (c) Manipur (d) Meghalaya
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mizoram meets all three conditions. Tripura is on the Tropic and borders Bangladesh but not Myanmar; Manipur borders Myanmar only; Meghalaya is not on the Tropic.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

130. Consider the following statements about Sikkim:
I. It is the least populous state of India (Census 2011).
II. It is the smallest state of India by area.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement I is correct. Statement II is an almost-correct trap: Goa is the smallest state by area; Sikkim is second smallest.
Source: Census of India 2011 (Office of the Registrar General of India)

131. Uttar Pradesh shares its boundary with how many Indian states (not counting Union Territories)?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (c)
Solution: Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand and Bihar — 8 states; Delhi (a UT) and Nepal are extra.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

132. Assam shares its boundary with how many Indian states?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 7
Answer: (d)
Solution: Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Meghalaya and West Bengal — 7 states.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

133. Which pair of city and river is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Nashik – Godavari (b) Jabalpur – Narmada (c) Surat – Sabarmati (d) Cuttack – Mahanadi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Surat is on the Tapi; the Sabarmati flows past Ahmedabad. Trap type: almost-correct pair, as both cities are in Gujarat.
Source: PYQ-style

134. Consider the following statements about the Brahmaputra:
I. It rises in Tibet, east of Lake Mansarovar.
II. In Assam, the Dihang is joined by the Dibang and the Lohit to form the Brahmaputra.
III. In Bangladesh it is known as the Jamuna.
Which of the statements given are correct?
(a) I and II only (b) II and III only (c) I and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (d)
Solution: All three statements match NCERT: source east of Mansarovar, Dihang + Dibang + Lohit form the Brahmaputra in Assam, and its Bangladesh name is Jamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

135. Which pair of river and its source is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Tapi – Multai (b) Godavari – Trimbakeshwar (c) Mahanadi – Mahabaleshwar (d) Kaveri – Talakaveri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mahabaleshwar is the source of the Krishna. The Mahanadi rises near Sihawa in Chhattisgarh. Trap type: similar name ('Maha-' in both).
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

136. According to NCERT, which is the largest freshwater lake of India?
(a) Wular (b) Loktak (c) Dal (d) Chilika
Answer: (a)
Solution: NCERT names the Wular Lake (Jammu & Kashmir), formed by tectonic activity, as the largest freshwater lake. Trap: Chilika is larger but is a brackish-water lagoon.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

137. Which of the following correctly describes Chilika?
(a) A freshwater lake in Andhra Pradesh (b) A brackish-water lagoon in Odisha (c) A salt lake in Rajasthan (d) An ox-bow lake in Bihar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chilika is a brackish-water lagoon on the Odisha coast. Sambhar is the salt lake of Rajasthan, and Kolleru is a freshwater lake in Andhra Pradesh.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

138. The Farakka Barrage, built to divert Ganga water into the Hooghly, is located in which state?
(a) Bihar (b) Jharkhand (c) Uttar Pradesh (d) West Bengal
Answer: (d)
Solution: Farakka is in West Bengal; NCERT notes the Ganga flows east till Farakka, the northernmost point of its delta, where it bifurcates and the Bhagirathi-Hooghly flows south.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

139. The Rihand Dam (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar) in Uttar Pradesh is on the Rihand, which is a tributary of:
(a) Son (b) Yamuna (c) Betwa (d) Ghaghara
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Rihand is a tributary of the Son, which in turn joins the Ganga from the south. Trap: Betwa is a Yamuna tributary of the same southern upland.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Which of the following join the Yamuna from its right (southern) bank?
I. Chambal
II. Betwa
III. Ken
IV. Hindon
(a) II, III and IV only (b) I, II and III only (c) I and IV only (d) I, II, III and IV
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Chambal, Betwa and Ken come from the Malwa plateau and Vindhyan uplands in the south. The Hindon is a left-bank tributary from the Doab side.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment), Ch. 3 Drainage System

141. The Gobind Sagar reservoir is formed by which dam?
(a) Pong Dam on the Beas (b) Ranjit Sagar Dam on the Ravi (c) Bhakra Dam on the Sutlej (d) Tehri Dam on the Bhagirathi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Bhakra Dam on the Sutlej in Himachal Pradesh forms the Gobind Sagar. Trap: the Pong Dam on the Beas forms a different reservoir (Maharana Pratap Sagar).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India II), Ch. 3 Water Resources

142. The Pong Dam is built on which river?
(a) Beas (b) Sutlej (c) Ravi (d) Chenab
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Pong Dam is on the Beas in Kangra district, Himachal Pradesh. Trap type: similar region — Bhakra on the Sutlej is close by.
Source: PYQ-style

143. Consider the following statements about Chandigarh:
I. It is a Union Territory.
II. It is the capital of both Punjab and Haryana.
III. It lies within Himachal Pradesh.
Which of the statements given are correct?
(a) I, II and III (b) II and III only (c) I and III only (d) I and II only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Chandigarh is a UT and the shared capital of Punjab and Haryana. It is not part of Himachal Pradesh, so statement III is wrong.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

144. The Teesta river flows through which states of India before entering Bangladesh?
(a) Sikkim and West Bengal (b) Assam and Meghalaya (c) Arunachal Pradesh and Assam (d) Bihar and West Bengal
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Teesta rises in Sikkim, flows through north Bengal (near Siliguri–Jalpaiguri) and joins the Brahmaputra in Bangladesh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment), Ch. 3 Drainage System

145. Which of the following rivers rise beyond the Himalayas, in Tibet?
I. Indus
II. Sutlej
III. Brahmaputra
IV. Yamuna
(a) I and III only (b) I, III and IV only (c) I, II and III only (d) II, III and IV only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Indus, Sutlej and Brahmaputra rise in Tibet near Mansarovar and cut gorges through the Himalayas. The Yamuna rises at Yamunotri inside India.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

146. Consider the following statements about the Godavari:
I. Its basin is the largest among the peninsular rivers.
II. About half of its basin lies in Maharashtra.
III. It flows into the Arabian Sea.
Which of the statements given are correct?
(a) I, II and III (b) I and II only (c) II and III only (d) I and III only
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statements I and II are from NCERT. Statement III is wrong: although it rises near the west coast, the Godavari flows east into the Bay of Bengal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

147. Why does the Kaveri have a more even flow through the year than most peninsular rivers?
(a) Its upper catchment gets south-west monsoon rain and its lower catchment gets north-east (retreating) monsoon rain (b) It is fed by Himalayan glaciers (c) It flows through a rift valley (d) It receives water diverted from the Krishna
Answer: (a)
Solution: The upper Kaveri basin (Karnataka) gets summer monsoon rain and the lower basin (Tamil Nadu) gets winter north-east monsoon rain, so its flow is steadier. It is not snow-fed.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment), Ch. 3 Drainage System

148. Consider the following statements:
I. The Narmada flows through Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Gujarat.
II. The Tapi flows through Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Gujarat.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both are correct. The Narmada mainly drains Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat and touches Maharashtra along a short border stretch; the Tapi crosses all three states before reaching the sea at Surat.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment), Ch. 3 Drainage System

149. Which river crosses the Tropic of Cancer twice?
(a) Narmada (b) Sabarmati (c) Luni (d) Mahi
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Mahi rises in Madhya Pradesh, loops through Rajasthan and enters Gujarat, crossing the Tropic of Cancer twice before reaching the Gulf of Khambhat.
Source: PYQ-style

150. Arrange the following states in the chronological order of their formation:
I. Nagaland
II. Haryana
III. Sikkim
IV. Goa
(a) I, II, III, IV (b) II, I, III, IV (c) I, III, II, IV (d) II, III, I, IV
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nagaland came first (December 1963), then Haryana (November 1966), Sikkim (May 1975) and Goa (May 1987). Trap type: chronological confusion between Nagaland and Haryana.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Politics in India since Independence), Ch. 1 Challenges of Nation Building
