Set 1/6 – States & Rivers MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which is the largest state of India by area?
(a) Madhya Pradesh (b) Maharashtra (c) Rajasthan (d) Uttar Pradesh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rajasthan is the largest state by area. Trap: Madhya Pradesh was the largest until Chhattisgarh was carved out of it in 2000; it is now second.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

2. Which is the smallest state of India by area?
(a) Sikkim (b) Goa (c) Tripura (d) Manipur
Answer: (b)
Solution: Goa is the smallest state by area. Trap: Sikkim is the smallest by population (Census 2011), not by area.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 1 India – Size and Location

3. The Bhagirathi and the Alaknanda meet to form the Ganga at:
(a) Rudraprayag (b) Haridwar (c) Devprayag (d) Karnaprayag
Answer: (c)
Solution: The two head-streams join at Devprayag in Uttarakhand and the river is called the Ganga from there. Haridwar is where the Ganga leaves the mountains and enters the plains.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

4. The Bhagirathi is fed by which glacier?
(a) Yamunotri (b) Gangotri (c) Siachen (d) Zemu
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Bhagirathi is fed by the Gangotri glacier (snout at Gaumukh). Yamunotri is the source of the Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

5. The Yamuna joins the Ganga at:
(a) Varanasi (b) Kanpur (c) Patna (d) Prayagraj
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Yamuna meets the Ganga at Prayagraj (Allahabad); the meeting point is called the Sangam.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

6. Which river is called the 'Sorrow of Bihar' because of its frequent floods and shifting course?
(a) Gandak (b) Son (c) Kosi (d) Ghaghara
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kosi is the 'Sorrow of Bihar'. Trap: the Damodar is the 'Sorrow of Bengal' — do not mix the two.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment), Ch. 3 Drainage System

7. Majuli, a large inhabited river island, lies in which river?
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Ganga (c) Godavari (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Majuli lies in the Brahmaputra in Assam and is one of the largest inhabited riverine islands in the world.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

8. In Tibet, the Brahmaputra is known as the:
(a) Dihang (b) Tsangpo (c) Jamuna (d) Padma
Answer: (b)
Solution: It is the Tsangpo in Tibet, the Dihang (Siang) in Arunachal Pradesh and the Jamuna in Bangladesh. Padma is the name of the Ganga in Bangladesh.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

9. Which of the following rivers flows westward into the Arabian Sea?
(a) Godavari (b) Krishna (c) Narmada (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Narmada flows west through a rift valley into the Arabian Sea. The Godavari, Krishna and Mahanadi flow east into the Bay of Bengal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

10. Because of its length and large basin, which river is called the 'Dakshin Ganga'?
(a) Narmada (b) Godavari (c) Krishna (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Godavari is the largest peninsular river (about 1500 km) and is called the Dakshin Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

11. The Hirakud Dam is built on which river?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Godavari (c) Damodar (d) Son
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hirakud Dam is on the Mahanadi near Sambalpur in Odisha.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India II), Ch. 3 Water Resources

12. The Bhakra Nangal project is built on which river?
(a) Beas (b) Ravi (c) Sutlej (d) Chenab
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bhakra Nangal is on the Sutlej; its reservoir is the Gobind Sagar. Trap: the Pong Dam is on the Beas.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India II), Ch. 3 Water Resources

13. The Tehri Dam in Uttarakhand is built on which river?
(a) Alaknanda (b) Bhagirathi (c) Yamuna (d) Mandakini
Answer: (b)
Solution: Tehri Dam is on the Bhagirathi. Trap: the Alaknanda is the other head-stream of the Ganga but does not have the Tehri Dam.
Source: PYQ-style

14. The Kaveri rises in:
(a) Mahabaleshwar (b) Trimbakeshwar (c) Amarkantak (d) Brahmagiri range (Kodagu, Karnataka)
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kaveri rises in the Brahmagiri range of the Western Ghats (Talakaveri, Kodagu). Mahabaleshwar is the source of the Krishna, Trimbakeshwar of the Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

15. The Narmada rises in the:
(a) Amarkantak hills, Madhya Pradesh (b) Multai, Madhya Pradesh (c) Mahabaleshwar, Maharashtra (d) Janapav hills, Madhya Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Narmada rises in the Amarkantak hills. Multai (Betul) is the source of the Tapi and Janapav of the Chambal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

16. Which city stands on the banks of the Gomti?
(a) Kanpur (b) Lucknow (c) Agra (d) Patna
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lucknow is on the Gomti. Kanpur and Patna are on the Ganga; Agra is on the Yamuna.
Source: PYQ-style

17. Which river flows through Srinagar?
(a) Chenab (b) Ravi (c) Jhelum (d) Indus
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Jhelum flows through Srinagar and the Wular Lake in the Kashmir Valley.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

18. What is the capital of Arunachal Pradesh?
(a) Itanagar (b) Dispur (c) Kohima (d) Aizawl
Answer: (a)
Solution: Itanagar is the capital of Arunachal Pradesh. Dispur is the capital of Assam, Kohima of Nagaland and Aizawl of Mizoram.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

19. Which city is the common capital of Punjab and Haryana?
(a) Amritsar (b) Chandigarh (c) Ambala (d) Ludhiana
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chandigarh is a Union Territory and also the capital of both Punjab and Haryana.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

20. Telangana became a separate state of India in which year?
(a) 2000 (b) 2011 (c) 2014 (d) 2019
Answer: (c)
Solution: Under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, Telangana became India's 29th state in 2014.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs) notification

21. Which was the first state formed on the basis of language, in 1953?
(a) Andhra State (b) Maharashtra (c) Gujarat (d) Kerala
Answer: (a)
Solution: Andhra State was created on 1 October 1953 after the death of Potti Sriramulu during his fast for a Telugu-speaking state. Maharashtra and Gujarat came later, in 1960.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Politics in India since Independence), Ch. 1 Challenges of Nation Building

22. Which pair of dam and river is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Nagarjuna Sagar – Krishna (b) Mettur – Kaveri (c) Sardar Sarovar – Tapi (d) Hirakud – Mahanadi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sardar Sarovar Dam is on the Narmada in Gujarat, not on the Tapi. Trap type: almost-correct pair, since both rivers are west-flowing and end in the Gulf of Khambhat.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India II), Ch. 3 Water Resources

23. Gujarat and Maharashtra were created by dividing the Bombay State in:
(a) 1956 (b) 1960 (c) 1966 (d) 1972
Answer: (b)
Solution: The bilingual Bombay State was split into Maharashtra and Gujarat on 1 May 1960. Trap: the States Reorganisation Act belongs to 1956, while this split came in 1960.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Politics in India since Independence), Ch. 1 Challenges of Nation Building

24. Which of the following rivers does NOT belong to the Indus river system?
(a) Jhelum (b) Chenab (c) Beas (d) Gomti
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas and Sutlej are Indus tributaries. The Gomti is a left-bank tributary of the Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

25. Consider the following statements about the Narmada:
I. It flows westward through a rift valley between the Vindhya and the Satpura ranges.
II. It forms a large delta before entering the sea.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement I is correct. Statement II is wrong: the Narmada (like the Tapi) forms an estuary, not a delta.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage
