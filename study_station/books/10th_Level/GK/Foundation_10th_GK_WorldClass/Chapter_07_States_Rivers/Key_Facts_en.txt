2. Time Management (15/45-Sec Rule)
Question Type	Example	Time	Score Maximizer?
Capital / city–river one-liner	Kohima is the capital of?	15 sec	Yes
Dam–river pair	Hirakud is on which river?	15 sec	Yes
Source of a river	Where does the Krishna rise?	15 sec	Yes
"Sorrow of …", "Dakshin Ganga" type titles	Sorrow of Bihar?	15 sec	Yes
Year of formation / renaming	Mysore renamed Karnataka in?	15 sec	Yes
Negative-phrased border question	Which state does NOT border Myanmar?	45 sec	No (read carefully)
Arrange in order (formation dates, river mouths, Prayags)	Mahanadi–Godavari–Krishna–Kaveri, north to south	45 sec	No
Multi-statement (2–3 statements)	Statements on the Indus	45 sec	No
Combined conditions	State on the Tropic of Cancer bordering Bangladesh and Myanmar	45 sec	No
