Set 3/6 – States & Rivers MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 51–75: Medium

51. The Godavari rises from the slopes of the Western Ghats in which district?
(a) Satara (Maharashtra) (b) Nashik (Maharashtra) (c) Kodagu (Karnataka) (d) Dhamtari (Chhattisgarh)
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Godavari rises near Trimbakeshwar in Nashik district. Satara district has Mahabaleshwar, the source of the Krishna.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

52. Which is the largest (longest) river of Peninsular India?
(a) Krishna (b) Narmada (c) Godavari (d) Kaveri
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Godavari (about 1500 km) is the largest peninsular river; its basin is also the largest. The Krishna (about 1400 km) comes second.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

53. The Krishna rises from a spring near:
(a) Mahabaleshwar (b) Trimbakeshwar (c) Talakaveri (d) Amarkantak
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Krishna rises from a spring near Mahabaleshwar in the Western Ghats. Trimbakeshwar is the Godavari's source.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

54. The Tungabhadra is a tributary of which river?
(a) Godavari (b) Krishna (c) Kaveri (d) Pennar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Tungabhadra (formed by the Tunga and the Bhadra) joins the Krishna. Hampi stands on its bank.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

55. Which of the following is NOT a tributary of the Krishna?
(a) Bhima (b) Koyna (c) Musi (d) Indravati
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Bhima, Koyna, Musi, Tungabhadra and Ghatprabha are Krishna tributaries. The Indravati joins the Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

56. Which of the following is NOT a tributary of the Godavari?
(a) Wainganga (b) Penganga (c) Kabini (d) Manjra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Wainganga, Penganga, Wardha, Manjra and Purna are Godavari tributaries. The Kabini is a tributary of the Kaveri.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

57. The Amaravati, Bhavani, Hemavati and Kabini are tributaries of:
(a) Kaveri (b) Krishna (c) Godavari (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (a)
Solution: NCERT lists the Amaravati, Bhavani, Hemavati and Kabini as the main tributaries of the Kaveri.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

58. The Mahanadi rises in the highlands of which state?
(a) Odisha (b) Chhattisgarh (c) Jharkhand (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Mahanadi rises in the highlands of Chhattisgarh and flows through Odisha to the Bay of Bengal. Trap: Hirakud Dam and Cuttack are in Odisha, so students pick Odisha.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

59. The Nagarjuna Sagar dam is built on which river?
(a) Godavari (b) Kaveri (c) Tungabhadra (d) Krishna
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nagarjuna Sagar is on the Krishna, on the Telangana–Andhra Pradesh border. The Tungabhadra has its own dam near Hospet.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India II), Ch. 3 Water Resources

60. The Mettur dam on the Kaveri is located in which state?
(a) Karnataka (b) Tamil Nadu (c) Kerala (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mettur dam is in Salem district of Tamil Nadu. Trap: the Krishnaraja Sagar dam on the Kaveri is in Karnataka.
Source: PYQ-style

61. The Krishnaraja Sagar (KRS) dam near Mysuru is built on which river?
(a) Kaveri (b) Krishna (c) Tungabhadra (d) Sharavathi
Answer: (a)
Solution: KRS dam is on the Kaveri. Trap type: similar name — 'Krishnaraja' tempts students to choose the Krishna.
Source: PYQ-style

62. The Sivasamudram (Shivanasamudra) falls are formed by which river?
(a) Sharavathi (b) Narmada (c) Kaveri (d) Godavari
Answer: (c)
Solution: NCERT: the Kaveri makes the second biggest waterfall in India, known as Sivasamudram. The Sharavathi forms the Jog Falls.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

63. The Jog Falls in Karnataka are on which river?
(a) Kaveri (b) Sharavathi (c) Tungabhadra (d) Periyar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Jog Falls are formed by the west-flowing Sharavathi in Shivamogga district, Karnataka.
Source: PYQ-style

64. The Dhuandhar falls and the Marble Rocks near Jabalpur are on which river?
(a) Tapi (b) Son (c) Chambal (d) Narmada
Answer: (d)
Solution: NCERT: near Jabalpur the Narmada flows through a deep gorge of marble rocks and forms the Dhuandhar falls.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

65. The Sardar Sarovar dam on the Narmada is located in which state?
(a) Madhya Pradesh (b) Maharashtra (c) Gujarat (d) Rajasthan
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sardar Sarovar dam is in Gujarat. Trap: the Narmada rises in Madhya Pradesh and the Indira Sagar dam is there, but Sardar Sarovar is in Gujarat.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India II), Ch. 3 Water Resources

66. Why do the Narmada and the Tapi form estuaries instead of deltas?
(a) They flow through rift valleys over hard rock and do not deposit much silt at their mouths (b) They fall into the Bay of Bengal (c) They are fed by Himalayan glaciers (d) They are the shortest rivers of India
Answer: (a)
Solution: Both flow west through rift valleys over hard rock and carry little silt to their mouths, so they form estuaries. They fall into the Arabian Sea, not the Bay of Bengal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

67. Which is the longest river of Kerala, on which the Idukki dam is built?
(a) Pamba (b) Bharathapuzha (c) Periyar (d) Chaliyar
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Periyar is the longest river of Kerala, and the Idukki arch dam is built on it. The Bharathapuzha is the second longest.
Source: PYQ-style

68. The Mandovi and the Zuari are the main rivers of which state?
(a) Goa (b) Kerala (c) Odisha (d) Maharashtra
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Mandovi and the Zuari are short west-flowing rivers of Goa; Panaji stands on the Mandovi estuary.
Source: PYQ-style

69. Vijayawada is located on the banks of which river?
(a) Godavari (b) Krishna (c) Kaveri (d) Pennar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vijayawada is on the Krishna (Prakasam Barrage). Rajahmundry is the big city on the Godavari.
Source: PYQ-style

70. Rajahmundry (Rajamahendravaram) in Andhra Pradesh stands on which river?
(a) Godavari (b) Krishna (c) Mahanadi (d) Tungabhadra
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rajahmundry is on the Godavari, shortly before the river splits into its delta. Trap: Vijayawada on the Krishna is the nearby big river city.
Source: PYQ-style

71. Tiruchirappalli (Tamil Nadu) is located on which river?
(a) Vaigai (b) Tamraparni (c) Pennar (d) Kaveri
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tiruchirappalli is on the Kaveri; the river island of Srirangam lies here. Madurai is on the Vaigai.
Source: PYQ-style

72. Madurai is located on the banks of which river?
(a) Vaigai (b) Kaveri (c) Periyar (d) Palar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Madurai stands on the Vaigai in Tamil Nadu. The Periyar is a river of Kerala.
Source: PYQ-style

73. Consider the following statements:
I. Most major rivers of the Peninsula flow eastward into the Bay of Bengal.
II. The Western Ghats form the main water divide of Peninsular India.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both are correct (NCERT). The Western Ghats lie close to the west coast, so most big rivers flow east; only the Narmada and Tapi are long west-flowing rivers.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I), Ch. 3 Drainage

74. Which of the following rivers flows westward into the Arabian Sea?
(a) Pennar (b) Vaigai (c) Mahanadi (d) Mahi
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Mahi flows west through Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan and Gujarat into the Gulf of Khambhat (Arabian Sea). The Pennar, Vaigai and Mahanadi go to the Bay of Bengal.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment), Ch. 3 Drainage System

75. The Godavari flows into the sea mainly through which state?
(a) Odisha (b) Andhra Pradesh (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Karnataka
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Godavari forms its delta in Andhra Pradesh (East and West Godavari region) and enters the Bay of Bengal there.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India: Physical Environment), Ch. 3 Drainage System
