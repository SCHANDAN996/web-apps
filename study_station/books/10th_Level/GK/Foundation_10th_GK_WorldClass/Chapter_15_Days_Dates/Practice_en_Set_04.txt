Set 4/6 – Important Days & Dates MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer (16 September) marks the signing in 1987 of the:
(a) Kyoto Protocol (b) Montreal Protocol (c) Ramsar Convention (d) CITES
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Montreal Protocol, which controls substances that damage the ozone layer, was signed on 16 September 1987. The Ramsar Convention (1971) is linked to World Wetlands Day and CITES (1973) to World Wildlife Day.
Source: United Nations Environment Programme

77. The International Day of Peace is observed on:
(a) 2 October (b) 24 October (c) 21 June (d) 21 September
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 2 October is the International Day of Non-Violence, 24 October is UN Day and 21 June is the International Day of Yoga. The International Day of Peace falls on 21 September.
Source: United Nations

78. The International Day of Older Persons is observed on:
(a) 1 October (b) 11 October (c) 1 July (d) 14 November
Answer: (a)
Solution: 11 October is the International Day of the Girl Child, 1 July is National Doctors' Day and 14 November is Children's Day. The International Day of Older Persons falls on 1 October.
Source: United Nations

79. World Post Day (9 October) marks the founding of the Universal Postal Union in 1874 at:
(a) Geneva (b) Paris (c) Bern (d) New York
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Universal Postal Union (UPU) was founded at Bern, Switzerland, on 9 October 1874. Geneva is also in Switzerland, which makes it a tempting wrong choice.
Source: Universal Postal Union

80. World Mental Health Day is observed on:
(a) 10 December (b) 7 April (c) 10 October (d) 1 October
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 10 December is Human Rights Day — same date number, different month — and 7 April is World Health Day. World Mental Health Day falls on 10 October.
Source: World Health Organization

81. World Diabetes Day (14 November) is observed on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Alexander Fleming (b) Frederick Banting (c) Robert Koch (d) Edward Jenner
Answer: (b)
Solution: Frederick Banting, co-discoverer of insulin, was born on 14 November. Trap: the same date is Children's Day in India (Nehru's birthday), so one date carries two days.
Source: World Health Organization

82. World Toilet Day is observed on:
(a) 2 October (b) 14 November (c) 3 December (d) 19 November
Answer: (d)
Solution: 2 October is Gandhi Jayanti, 14 November World Diabetes Day and 3 December the International Day of Persons with Disabilities. World Toilet Day falls on 19 November.
Source: United Nations

83. The International Day of Persons with Disabilities is observed on:
(a) 3 December (b) 1 December (c) 10 December (d) 3 May
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1 December is World AIDS Day, 10 December Human Rights Day and 3 May World Press Freedom Day. The International Day of Persons with Disabilities falls on 3 December.
Source: United Nations

84. International Civil Aviation Day (7 December) marks the signing of the Chicago Convention in the year:
(a) 1949 (b) 1945 (c) 1971 (d) 1944
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 7 December is also India's Armed Forces Flag Day, observed since 1949 — do not mix the two years. The Chicago Convention on civil aviation was signed on 7 December 1944.
Source: International Civil Aviation Organization

85. International Mountain Day is observed on:
(a) 10 December (b) 11 December (c) 11 July (d) 21 March
Answer: (b)
Solution: 10 December is Human Rights Day, 11 July World Population Day and 21 March the International Day of Forests. International Mountain Day falls on 11 December.
Source: United Nations

86. Ambedkar Jayanti, the birth anniversary of Dr B. R. Ambedkar, is observed on:
(a) 13 April (b) 26 November (c) 14 April (d) 24 April
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 13 April is the date of the Jallianwala Bagh massacre (1919), and 26 November is Constitution Day. Dr B. R. Ambedkar was born in 1891, and his birth anniversary falls on 14 April.
Source: Government of India

87. National Maritime Day (5 April) remembers the 1919 voyage of the ship SS Loyalty from:
(a) Kolkata to Singapore (b) Mumbai to London (c) Chennai to Colombo (d) Kochi to Aden
Answer: (b)
Solution: On 5 April 1919 the SS Loyalty of the Scindia Steam Navigation Company sailed from Mumbai to London, a landmark for Indian shipping.
Source: Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Government of India

88. Anti-Terrorism Day (21 May) is observed on the death anniversary of:
(a) Indira Gandhi (b) Lal Bahadur Shastri (c) Rajiv Gandhi (d) Jawaharlal Nehru
Answer: (c)
Solution: Former Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi was killed on 21 May 1991, and that day is observed as Anti-Terrorism Day. The other three are former Prime Ministers not linked to this day.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

89. National Handloom Day (7 August) remembers the launch of the Swadeshi Movement on 7 August 1905 in:
(a) Calcutta (b) Bombay (c) Lahore (d) Madras
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Swadeshi Movement was launched in Calcutta on 7 August 1905. Trap: Chennai (Madras) is where National Handloom Day was first celebrated in 2015, not where the movement began.
Source: Ministry of Textiles, Government of India

90. Partition Horrors Remembrance Day (Vibhajan Vibhishika Smriti Diwas) is observed on:
(a) 15 August (b) 16 December (c) 9 August (d) 14 August
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 15 August is Independence Day and 9 August is August Kranti Diwas. Partition Horrors Remembrance Day was declared in 2021 and falls on 14 August.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

91. National Space Day (23 August) marks:
(a) the launch of the satellite Aryabhata (b) the arrival of Mangalyaan at Mars (c) the touchdown of Chandrayaan-3's Vikram lander near the Moon's south pole (d) the launch of Chandrayaan-1
Answer: (c)
Solution: Chandrayaan-3's Vikram lander touched down near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023; National Space Day was first observed on 23 August 2024.
Source: Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)

92. National Consumer Day in India is observed on:
(a) 15 March (b) 24 December (c) 14 December (d) 24 April
Answer: (b)
Solution: Trap: 15 March is World Consumer Rights Day and 14 December is National Energy Conservation Day. The Consumer Protection Act, 1986 received the President's assent on 24 December, which is National Consumer Day.
Source: Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India

93. National Energy Conservation Day is observed on:
(a) 14 November (b) 5 June (c) 14 December (d) 11 May
Answer: (c)
Solution: 14 November is Children's Day, 5 June World Environment Day and 11 May National Technology Day. National Energy Conservation Day falls on 14 December.
Source: Bureau of Energy Efficiency, Ministry of Power

94. The Jallianwala Bagh massacre took place at Amritsar on:
(a) 14 April 1919 (b) 13 April 1920 (c) 23 March 1931 (d) 13 April 1919
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 14 April is Ambedkar Jayanti, and 23 March 1931 is when Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were hanged. General Dyer's troops fired on the crowd at Jallianwala Bagh on 13 April 1919.
Source: NCERT Class 10 History (India and the Contemporary World II), Ch. Nationalism in India

95. On 23 March 1931 Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were hanged in:
(a) Lahore jail (b) Cellular Jail, Port Blair (c) Yerwada jail, Pune (d) Mandalay jail
Answer: (a)
Solution: The three revolutionaries were hanged in Lahore jail on 23 March 1931, and 23 March is remembered as Shaheed Diwas. Trap: Martyrs' Day on 30 January is for Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: Ministry of Culture, Government of India

96. The Quit India resolution was passed by the Congress at Bombay on:
(a) 9 August 1942 (b) 15 August 1942 (c) 8 August 1942 (d) 8 August 1940
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 9 August, August Kranti Diwas, is the day the mass movement began. The resolution itself was passed at Bombay a day earlier, on 8 August 1942.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History III), Ch. Mahatma Gandhi and the Nationalist Movement

97. Utkal Divas (1 April) marks the year in which Odisha became a separate province:
(a) 1912 (b) 1936 (c) 1956 (d) 1949
Answer: (b)
Solution: Trap: 1912 is when Bihar was separated from the Bengal Presidency (Bihar Diwas, 22 March). Odisha became a separate province on 1 April 1936.
Source: Government of Odisha

98. Bihar Diwas is celebrated on:
(a) 1 April (b) 30 March (c) 22 March (d) 15 November
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1 April is Utkal Divas (Odisha), 30 March Rajasthan Diwas and 15 November Jharkhand's foundation day. Bihar was separated from the Bengal Presidency in 1912, and Bihar Diwas falls on 22 March.
Source: Government of Bihar

99. Telangana was formed as a separate state on:
(a) 1 November 2000 (b) 15 November 2000 (c) 1 May 1960 (d) 2 June 2014
Answer: (d)
Solution: 15 November 2000 is Jharkhand's formation and 1 May 1960 that of Maharashtra and Gujarat. Telangana was formed on 2 June 2014.
Source: Government of Telangana

100. Jharkhand was formed on 15 November 2000. Which national day is also observed on 15 November?
(a) Children's Day (b) Janjatiya Gaurav Divas (c) National Education Day (d) Constitution Day
Answer: (b)
Solution: 15 November is the birth anniversary of Bhagwan Birsa Munda, declared Janjatiya Gaurav Divas in 2021. Trap: Children's Day is one day earlier, on 14 November.
Source: Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India (PIB)
