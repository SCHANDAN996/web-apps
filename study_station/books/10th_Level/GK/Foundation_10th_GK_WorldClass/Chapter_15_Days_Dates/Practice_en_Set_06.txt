126. Arrange the events behind these Indian days in chronological order (earliest first):
1. Pokhran nuclear tests (National Technology Day)
2. Discovery of the Raman Effect (National Science Day)
3. 73rd Constitutional Amendment comes into force (National Panchayati Raj Day)
4. Founding of the Election Commission of India (National Voters' Day)
(a) 2, 4, 3, 1 (b) 4, 2, 3, 1 (c) 2, 3, 4, 1 (d) 2, 4, 1, 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Raman Effect 1928, Election Commission 1950, 73rd Amendment in force 1993, Pokhran 1998 — so the order is 2, 4, 3, 1. Trap: the 73rd Amendment (1993) comes before Pokhran-II (1998).
Source: Government of India

127. Consider the following statements:
1. The Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution of India on 26 November 1949.
2. The Election Commission of India was founded on 25 January 1950, one day before the Constitution came into force.
3. The Government of India declared 26 November as Constitution Day in 2010.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong: Constitution Day was declared in November 2015, the 125th birth anniversary year of Dr B. R. Ambedkar.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Political Science (Democratic Politics I), Ch. Constitutional Design

128. All four of the following days fall in December. Which one is NOT a birth anniversary?
(a) Kisan Diwas (b) Good Governance Day (c) Veer Baal Diwas (d) National Mathematics Day
Answer: (c)
Solution: Veer Baal Diwas (26 December) marks the martyrdom of Sahibzada Zorawar Singh and Sahibzada Fateh Singh. Kisan Diwas (Charan Singh), Good Governance Day (Vajpayee) and National Mathematics Day (Ramanujan) are all birth anniversaries.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

131. Consider the following statements about World Environment Day:
1. It was decided at the UN Conference on the Human Environment held at Stockholm in 1972.
2. It was first observed in 1973.
3. It is observed on the same day as Earth Day.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

132. Which of the following days remember an event of the year 1945?
1. World Food Day
2. United Nations Day
3. Hiroshima Day
4. World Health Day
Select the correct answer:
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 only (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: FAO was founded in 1945 (World Food Day), the UN Charter came into force in 1945 (UN Day) and Hiroshima was bombed in 1945. Trap: WHO was founded later, in 1948, so World Health Day is out.
Source: United Nations

133. Which of the following correctly lists the international days observed on 22, 23 and 24 March, in that order?
(a) World Water Day, World Meteorological Day, World TB Day (b) World Meteorological Day, World Water Day, World TB Day (c) World Water Day, World TB Day, World Meteorological Day (d) International Day of Forests, World Water Day, World TB Day
Answer: (a)
Solution: Use the hook "Water 22, Weather 23, TB 24". Trap: the International Day of Forests is 21 March, one day before World Water Day.
Source: United Nations

134. Which one of the following state formation days is correctly matched?
(a) Bihar – 1 April 1936 (b) Odisha – 22 March 1912 (c) Telangana – 2 June 2014 (d) Jharkhand – 1 November 2000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Telangana was formed on 2 June 2014. Trap: the Bihar and Odisha dates are swapped (Bihar 22 March 1912, Odisha 1 April 1936), and Jharkhand was formed on 15 November 2000, not 1 November.
Source: Government of India

135. Which one of the following days does NOT mark the founding of an organisation?
(a) World Post Day (b) World Food Day (c) World Health Day (d) World Diabetes Day
Answer: (d)
Solution: World Diabetes Day (14 November) is the birth anniversary of Frederick Banting, co-discoverer of insulin. The other three mark the founding of the UPU (1874), FAO (1945) and WHO (1948).
Source: World Health Organization

136. Consider the following statements:

139. Which two days in December remember the 1971 India–Pakistan war?
(a) Navy Day and Vijay Diwas (b) Armed Forces Flag Day and Vijay Diwas (c) Navy Day and Armed Forces Flag Day (d) Vijay Diwas and Kisan Diwas
Answer: (a)
Solution: Navy Day (4 December) remembers Operation Trident on Karachi harbour, and Vijay Diwas (16 December) the surrender in Dhaka — both 1971. Trap: Armed Forces Flag Day (7 December) has been observed since 1949 and is not linked to the 1971 war.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

140. Consider the following statements:

(a) 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is the classic trap: the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950.
Source: NCERT Class 10 History (India and the Contemporary World II), Ch. Nationalism in India

141. Which of the following days are observed on the birth anniversary of a scientist?
1. World Blood Donor Day
2. World Diabetes Day
3. World Tuberculosis Day
4. National Science Day
(a) 3 and 4 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: World Blood Donor Day is Karl Landsteiner's birthday and World Diabetes Day is Frederick Banting's. Trap: World TB Day marks Robert Koch's announcement of the TB germ (1882), and National Science Day marks the Raman Effect discovery — both are event days.
Source: World Health Organization

142. Match the agreements with the days that remember them:
A. Ramsar Convention   B. CITES   C. Montreal Protocol   D. Chicago Convention
1. 16 September   2. 7 December   3. 2 February   4. 3 March
(a) A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2 (b) A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1 (c) A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2 (d) A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2

Solution: Ramsar – World Wetlands Day, 2 February; CITES – World Wildlife Day, 3 March; Montreal Protocol – Ozone Day, 16 September; Chicago Convention – International Civil Aviation Day, 7 December. So A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2.
Source: United Nations

145. 25 January is observed in India as:

150. Consider the following statements about World Population Day:
