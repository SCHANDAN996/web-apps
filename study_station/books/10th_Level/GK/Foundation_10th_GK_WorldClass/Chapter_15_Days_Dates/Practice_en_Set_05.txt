Set 5/6 – Important Days & Dates MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard

101. Rajasthan Diwas is celebrated on:
(a) 1 May (b) 30 March (c) 1 November (d) 22 March
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1 May is Maharashtra and Gujarat Day, 1 November is the foundation day of several states and 22 March is Bihar Diwas. Rajasthan Diwas (1949) falls on 30 March.
Source: Government of Rajasthan

102. 1 November is the foundation day of several Indian states. Which one of the following does NOT celebrate its formation on 1 November?
(a) Karnataka (b) Kerala (c) Telangana (d) Madhya Pradesh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka (Karnataka Rajyotsava) and Kerala (Kerala Piravi) all mark their formation on 1 November. Telangana was formed on 2 June 2014, so it is the odd one out.
Source: Government of India

103. International Labour Day (May Day) is observed on:
(a) 1 June (b) 1 October (c) 1 July (d) 1 May
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1 June is World Milk Day, 1 October the International Day of Older Persons and 1 July National Doctors' Day. International Labour Day falls on 1 May.
Source: International Labour Organization

104. World Cancer Day is observed on:
(a) 4 February (b) 2 February (c) 4 March (d) 1 December
Answer: (a)
Solution: 2 February is World Wetlands Day and 1 December is World AIDS Day. World Cancer Day falls on 4 February.
Source: World Health Organization

105. The International Day of Yoga (21 June) was declared by the:
(a) World Health Organization (b) UNESCO (c) UN General Assembly (d) Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India
Answer: (c)
Solution: India proposed the day, and the UN General Assembly declared it on 11 December 2014. Trap: India proposed it, but the declaration was made by the UN General Assembly, not by an Indian ministry.
Source: United Nations

106. International Mother Language Day (21 February) was proclaimed by:
(a) UNICEF (b) UNESCO (c) WHO (d) UNDP
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNESCO proclaimed International Mother Language Day in November 1999, and it has been observed since 2000. It is a language and culture day, so it belongs to UNESCO.
Source: UNESCO

107. World Environment Day (5 June) was decided at the UN Conference on the Human Environment held at:
(a) Rio de Janeiro, 1992 (b) Ramsar, 1971 (c) Montreal, 1987 (d) Stockholm, 1972
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: Ramsar (1971) is linked to wetlands and Montreal (1987) to the ozone layer. World Environment Day was decided at Stockholm in 1972 and first observed in 1973; the conference itself was in 1972.
Source: United Nations Environment Programme

108. 26 January was chosen as Republic Day because on 26 January 1930:
(a) Indians celebrated Purna Swaraj as "Independence Day" (b) the Constitution was adopted (c) the Dandi March began (d) Gandhi returned from South Africa
Answer: (a)
Solution: After the Lahore Congress demanded Purna Swaraj, 26 January 1930 was celebrated across India as Independence Day, so the Constitution was brought into force on the same date in 1950. Trap: the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949, not on 26 January.
Source: NCERT Class 10 History (India and the Contemporary World II), Ch. Nationalism in India

109. The demand for Purna Swaraj (complete independence) was formally made at which session of the Congress in December 1929?
(a) Lucknow (b) Lahore (c) Karachi (d) Haripura
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Lahore Congress of December 1929, presided over by Jawaharlal Nehru, formalised the demand for Purna Swaraj. This is the story behind 26 January.
Source: NCERT Class 10 History (India and the Contemporary World II), Ch. Nationalism in India

110. On 14 September 1949 (Hindi Diwas) the Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi as the official language of the Union in which script?
(a) Brahmi (b) Gurmukhi (c) Devanagari (d) Kharosthi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi written in the Devanagari script as the official language of the Union on 14 September 1949. Brahmi and Kharosthi are ancient scripts; Gurmukhi is used for Punjabi.
Source: Department of Official Language, Ministry of Home Affairs

111. World Hindi Day (10 January) remembers the first World Hindi Conference, held at:
(a) Nagpur, 1975 (b) New Delhi, 1975 (c) Nagpur, 2006 (d) Bhopal, 1975
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first World Hindi Conference was held at Nagpur on 10 January 1975. Trap: 2006 is the year from which World Hindi Day has been observed, not the year of the conference.
Source: Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India

112. Army Day (15 January) marks the day in 1949 when the first Indian Commander-in-Chief of the Indian Army took over. Who was he?
(a) Sam Manekshaw (b) K. S. Thimayya (c) Arjan Singh (d) K. M. Cariappa
Answer: (d)
Solution: General (later Field Marshal) K. M. Cariappa took over as the first Indian Commander-in-Chief on 15 January 1949. Sam Manekshaw was Army chief in the 1971 war, and Arjan Singh was an Air Force officer.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

113. Navy Day (4 December) commemorates which operation of the 1971 war?
(a) Operation Vijay (b) Operation Trident (c) Operation Meghdoot (d) Operation Blue Star
Answer: (b)
Solution: Operation Trident was the Indian Navy's attack on Karachi harbour in the 1971 war, remembered on 4 December. Trap: Operation Vijay is the Kargil operation of 1999 (Kargil Vijay Diwas, 26 July).
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

114. The Indian Air Force, whose day is celebrated on 8 October, was set up in:
(a) 1947 (b) 1950 (c) 1932 (d) 1928
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 1947 is the year of independence, not of the Air Force's founding. The Indian Air Force was set up on 8 October 1932.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

115. The Indian Coast Guard, whose day is 1 February, was raised in:
(a) 1977 (b) 1971 (c) 1947 (d) 1986
Answer: (a)
Solution: Trap: 1971 is the year of the war remembered on Navy Day and Vijay Diwas. The Indian Coast Guard was raised on 1 February 1977.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

116. On 21 April 1947 Sardar Patel addressed the first batch of officers at Metcalfe House, Delhi, and called the civil services the "steel frame of India". This day is now observed as:
(a) National Unity Day (b) Good Governance Day (c) Constitution Day (d) Civil Services Day
Answer: (d)
Solution: 21 April is Civil Services Day. Trap: National Unity Day is also linked to Sardar Patel, but it is his birth anniversary, 31 October.
Source: Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances

117. Which one of the following pairs of "day" and "organisation whose founding it marks" is INCORRECT?
(a) World Post Day – Universal Postal Union (b) World Telecommunication and Information Society Day – UNESCO (c) World Health Day – World Health Organization (d) World Food Day – Food and Agriculture Organization
Answer: (b)
Solution: World Telecommunication and Information Society Day (17 May) marks the founding of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) in 1865, not UNESCO. The other three pairs are correct.
Source: International Telecommunication Union

118. The Government of India declared 26 November as Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) in:
(a) 1949 (b) 2015 (c) 2005 (d) 2014
Answer: (b)
Solution: Trap: 1949 is the year the Constitution was adopted, not the year the day was declared. It was declared Constitution Day in November 2015, the 125th birth anniversary year of Dr B. R. Ambedkar.
Source: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India

119. Rashtriya Ekta Diwas (National Unity Day, 31 October) has been observed since:
(a) 2015 (b) 1975 (c) 2008 (d) 2014
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 2008 is when National Education Day began. Rashtriya Ekta Diwas, on Sardar Patel's birth anniversary, has been observed since 2014.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

120. National Science Day (28 February) was declared in 1986. In which year was it first celebrated?
(a) 1928 (b) 1986 (c) 1998 (d) 1987
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 1928 is the year of the Raman Effect and 1986 the year of the declaration. National Science Day was first celebrated in 1987.
Source: Department of Science & Technology, Government of India

121. Arrange the following international agreements in chronological order (earliest first):
1. Ramsar Convention (World Wetlands Day)
2. Montreal Protocol (Ozone Day)
3. CITES (World Wildlife Day)
4. Chicago Convention (International Civil Aviation Day)
(a) 1, 4, 3, 2 (b) 4, 3, 1, 2 (c) 4, 1, 3, 2 (d) 1, 3, 4, 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Chicago Convention 1944, Ramsar 1971, CITES 1973, Montreal Protocol 1987 — so the order is 4, 1, 3, 2. Trap: Ramsar and CITES are only two years apart; Ramsar comes first.
Source: United Nations

122. Consider the following statements about the International Day of Yoga:
1. It was proposed by India.
2. The UN General Assembly declared it on 11 December 2014.
3. It was first observed on 21 June 2014.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is the trap: the day was declared in December 2014, so the first observance was on 21 June 2015.
Source: United Nations

123. Match the dates with the organisation or event they mark:
A. 9 October   B. 16 October   C. 24 October   D. 17 May
1. FAO founded (1945)   2. UN Charter came into force (1945)   3. UPU founded (1874)   4. ITU founded (1865)
(a) A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4 (b) A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4 (c) A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3 (d) A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
Answer: (d)
Solution: World Post Day 9 October (UPU), World Food Day 16 October (FAO), UN Day 24 October (UN Charter), World Telecommunication Day 17 May (ITU). So A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4.
Source: United Nations

124. Most days are either "person days" (a birth anniversary) or "event days". Which one of the following is an event day and NOT a birth anniversary?
(a) Statistics Day, 29 June (b) Engineers' Day, 15 September (c) National Science Day, 28 February (d) National Doctors' Day, 1 July
Answer: (c)
Solution: National Science Day marks the discovery of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928, not C. V. Raman's birthday. The other three are birth anniversaries of P. C. Mahalanobis, M. Visvesvaraya and B. C. Roy.
Source: Department of Science & Technology, Government of India

125. Which one of the following pairs of national and international days is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) National Youth Day – 12 January; International Youth Day – 12 August (b) National Milk Day – 1 June; World Milk Day – 26 November (c) Teachers' Day – 5 September; World Teachers' Day – 5 October (d) National Girl Child Day – 24 January; International Day of the Girl Child – 11 October
Answer: (b)
Solution: The milk dates are swapped: India's National Milk Day is 26 November (Dr Verghese Kurien) and FAO's World Milk Day is 1 June. The other three pairs are correct.
Source: Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying
