📊 PYQ Pattern Analysis: Important Days & Dates

Exams this chapter serves: SSC GD, SSC MTS, RRB Group D, state police / constable exams, and the general-awareness part of other state-level tests.

Note: this is a pattern analysis — what kinds of questions these exams like to ask from this chapter and how they trap you. We do not give year-wise counts or percentages, because no reliable data for them exists. The model questions below are written in the exam style; their sources are the official bodies or textbooks behind each fact. Yearly "themes" of days are current affairs and are not covered here.

1. What Exams Like to Ask (Sub-topics, in plain words)

Direct date of a national day — the most common one-liner: "When is National Science Day / National Voters' Day / Army Day celebrated?"

Whose birth anniversary — "29 August is celebrated as National Sports Day in honour of whom?" (Dhyan Chand), Teachers' Day, Engineers' Day, Children's Day, National Unity Day, Good Governance Day, Kisan Diwas, National Mathematics Day.

The event behind a day — Raman Effect (28 February), Pokhran (11 May), Election Commission (25 January), 73rd Amendment (24 April), Swadeshi Movement (7 August), Chandrayaan-3 (23 August).

International days — World Health Day, World Environment Day, World Water Day, International Day of Yoga, Human Rights Day, World Population Day, World Food Day; often with the organisation behind the day (WHO, FAO, UNESCO, UN).

Defence days — Army, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard, Kargil Vijay Diwas, Vijay Diwas, Armed Forces Flag Day.

Look-alike pairs — Hindi Diwas vs World Hindi Day; Teachers' Day vs World Teachers' Day; National vs International Youth Day and Girl Child Day; Constitution Day vs Republic Day.

Newly declared days — Parakram Diwas, Janjatiya Gaurav Divas, Veer Baal Diwas, Partition Horrors Remembrance Day, National Space Day — usually asked with the person or event behind them.

2. Exam-wise Style (qualitative)

SSC GD / SSC MTS — short, direct one-liners: "On which date is … observed?" or "… is observed on the birth anniversary of whom?" Speed matters.

RRB Group D — similar direct questions, often with "Which of the following is correctly matched?" pairs of day and date.

State police / constable exams — direct facts plus the state's own foundation day and regional figures.

Higher exams that use the same basics (CHSL, NTPC, CGL) add statement-based questions and the organisation or year behind a day.

3. Top 10 High-Yield Model Questions with Solutions and Trap Analysis

Question 1
National Science Day is celebrated in India on:
(a) 11 May (b) 28 February (c) 7 November (d) 15 September
Answer: (b) 28 February
Trap Type: Similar Name — 11 May is National Technology Day; 7 November is C. V. Raman's birth anniversary, which the examiner offers to catch students who think the day is his birthday.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact. Score maximizer.
Source: Department of Science & Technology, Government of India

Question 2
World Hindi Day is observed every year on:
(a) 14 September (b) 10 January (c) 21 February (d) 26 January
Answer: (b) 10 January
Trap Type: Similar Name — 14 September is Hindi Diwas, India's own day; 21 February is International Mother Language Day.
15/45-sec Rule: 15 seconds — but only if you stop and notice the word "World".
Source: Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India

Question 3
The Constitution of India was adopted by the Constituent Assembly on:
(a) 26 January 1950 (b) 15 August 1947 (c) 26 November 1949 (d) 26 January 1930
Answer: (c) 26 November 1949
Trap Type: Chronological Confusion — 26 January 1950 is when it came into force; 26 January 1930 is Purna Swaraj Day.
15/45-sec Rule: 15 seconds if you read the verb "adopted" carefully.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Political Science, Ch. Constitutional Design

Question 4
National Sports Day (29 August) is observed on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Milkha Singh (b) Major Dhyan Chand (c) P. T. Usha (d) K. D. Jadhav
Answer: (b) Major Dhyan Chand
Trap Type: Similar Name — all four are sports icons; only one was born on 29 August.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India

Question 5
Which of the following days is NOT a birth anniversary of the person it honours?
(a) Teachers' Day (b) National Unity Day (c) Martyrs' Day (30 January) (d) Children's Day
Answer: (c) Martyrs' Day (30 January)
Trap Type: Negative Phrasing — 30 January is the day Mahatma Gandhi was assassinated (1948), not his birthday.
15/45-sec Rule: 45 seconds — mark each option P (birthday) or E (event) first.
Source: PYQ-style

Question 6
World Health Day is celebrated on 7 April because on this day:
(a) the UN Charter came into force (b) the World Health Organization was founded (c) the Red Cross was founded (d) penicillin was discovered
Answer: (b) the World Health Organization was founded
Trap Type: Almost Correct Statement — the Red Cross is also health-related, but World Red Cross Day is 8 May.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: World Health Organization

Question 7
Consider the following statements:
I. Army Day is observed on 15 January.
II. Navy Day is observed on 4 December.
III. Air Force Day is observed on 8 October.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I and II (b) Only II and III (c) Only I and III (d) I, II and III
Answer: (d) I, II and III
Trap Type: Almost Correct Statement — students doubt one correct line and drop it; 7 December (Flag Day) is the usual decoy for Navy Day.
15/45-sec Rule: 45 seconds — check each line separately.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

Question 8
The International Day of Yoga was first observed in:
(a) 2014 (b) 2015 (c) 2016 (d) 2013
Answer: (b) 2015
Trap Type: Chronological Confusion — the UN declared the day on 11 December 2014; the first observance was on 21 June 2015.
15/45-sec Rule: 15 seconds.
Source: United Nations (resolution 69/131)

Question 9
Vijay Diwas, observed on 16 December, commemorates India's victory in:
(a) the Kargil war of 1999 (b) the 1971 war (c) the 1965 war (d) Operation Polo of 1948
Answer: (b) the 1971 war
Trap Type: Similar Name — "Kargil Vijay Diwas" (26 July) remembers 1999.
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

Question 10
National Voters' Day is observed on 25 January because on this day in 1950:
(a) the first general election was held (b) the Election Commission of India was founded (c) the Constitution came into force (d) the right to vote at 18 was given
Answer: (b) the Election Commission of India was founded
Trap Type: Chronological Confusion — the Constitution came into force the very next day, 26 January 1950; voting age was lowered to 18 much later (61st Amendment, 1988).
15/45-sec Rule: 15-second fact.
Source: Election Commission of India

4. How to Use This Analysis

Build a 12-month calendar page and fill it in every week — dates stick when they sit next to their neighbours.

For every day, write one word for its "story" (birthday of whom, or what happened). Questions often ask the story, not the date.

Every time you see "World" or "International", or "NOT" / "incorrectly matched", slow down — that is where the traps are.
