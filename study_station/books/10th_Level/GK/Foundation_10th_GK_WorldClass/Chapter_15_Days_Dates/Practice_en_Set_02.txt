Set 2/6 – Important Days & Dates MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 26–50: Medium

26. World Hindi Day is observed every year on:
(a) 14 September (b) 21 February (c) 10 January (d) 26 November
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 14 September is Hindi Diwas, India's own day. World Hindi Day remembers the first World Hindi Conference held at Nagpur and falls on 10 January.
Source: Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India

27. National Mathematics Day (22 December) is celebrated on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Aryabhata (b) Srinivasa Ramanujan (c) C. R. Rao (d) Shakuntala Devi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Srinivasa Ramanujan was born on 22 December 1887. The day was announced in December 2011 and has been observed from 2012. Aryabhata was an ancient astronomer-mathematician whose birth date is not known.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

28. Good Governance Day (25 December) is observed on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Lal Bahadur Shastri (b) Rajiv Gandhi (c) Chaudhary Charan Singh (d) Atal Bihari Vajpayee
Answer: (d)
Solution: Former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee was born on 25 December 1924, and the day has been observed since 2014. Trap: Chaudhary Charan Singh's birth anniversary is two days earlier — Kisan Diwas.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

29. Kisan Diwas (National Farmers' Day) on 23 December honours the birth anniversary of:
(a) Chaudhary Charan Singh (b) M. S. Swaminathan (c) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (d) Lal Bahadur Shastri
Answer: (a)
Solution: Former Prime Minister Chaudhary Charan Singh, known as a champion of farmers, was born on 23 December. Trap: M. S. Swaminathan is linked with the Green Revolution, and Shastri gave "Jai Jawan Jai Kisan", but neither is this day.
Source: Government of India

30. National Education Day (11 November) honours India's first Education Minister:
(a) Dr Zakir Husain (b) Dr S. Radhakrishnan (c) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (d) Humayun Kabir
Answer: (c)
Solution: Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, India's first Education Minister, was born on 11 November 1888; the day has been observed since 2008. Trap: Dr Radhakrishnan's birthday is Teachers' Day.
Source: Ministry of Education, Government of India

31. Parakram Diwas is observed on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Bhagat Singh (b) Chandrashekhar Azad (c) Lala Lajpat Rai (d) Subhas Chandra Bose
Answer: (d)
Solution: In January 2021 the government declared Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose's birth anniversary, 23 January, as Parakram Diwas. The other three are freedom fighters with different dates.
Source: Ministry of Culture, Government of India (PIB)

32. Veer Baal Diwas (26 December) commemorates the martyrdom of:
(a) Khudiram Bose (b) Sahibzada Zorawar Singh and Sahibzada Fateh Singh (c) Guru Tegh Bahadur (d) Banda Singh Bahadur
Answer: (b)
Solution: Announced on 9 January 2022, Veer Baal Diwas honours the younger sons of Guru Gobind Singh, Sahibzada Zorawar Singh and Sahibzada Fateh Singh. Khudiram Bose was a young revolutionary but is not linked to this day.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

33. Janjatiya Gaurav Divas (15 November) is observed on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Tantya Bhil (b) Sidhu Murmu (c) Bhagwan Birsa Munda (d) Alluri Sitarama Raju
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Union Cabinet declared 15 November, Birsa Munda's birth anniversary, as Janjatiya Gaurav Divas in November 2021. The others are also tribal heroes, but the day is linked to Birsa Munda.
Source: Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India (PIB)

34. Statistics Day (29 June) honours the birth anniversary of:
(a) C. R. Rao (b) P. V. Sukhatme (c) D. R. Gadgil (d) P. C. Mahalanobis
Answer: (d)
Solution: Prof. Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis, father of Indian statistics, was born on 29 June 1893; the day was notified in 2007. C. R. Rao was a famous statistician too, but the day is for Mahalanobis.
Source: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation

35. National Doctors' Day (1 July) honours:
(a) Dr Bidhan Chandra Roy (b) Dr Yellapragada Subbarow (c) Dr Har Gobind Khorana (d) Dr Anandi Gopal Joshi
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1 July is both the birth and death anniversary of Dr B. C. Roy, physician and former Chief Minister of West Bengal. The others are noted scientists or doctors but are not linked to this day.
Source: Government of India

36. National Milk Day (26 November) is observed on the birth anniversary of:
(a) M. S. Swaminathan (b) Norman Borlaug (c) Dr Verghese Kurien (d) Tribhuvandas Patel
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dr Verghese Kurien, father of the White Revolution, was born on 26 November 1921. Trap: Swaminathan and Borlaug are linked with the Green Revolution, not milk.
Source: Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying

37. Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (9 January) marks the day when:
(a) Gandhi left India for South Africa (b) the Dandi March began (c) Gandhi began the Champaran Satyagraha (d) Gandhi returned to India from South Africa
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mahatma Gandhi, the greatest "pravasi", returned to India from South Africa on 9 January 1915. The day has been celebrated since 2003.
Source: Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India

38. National Girl Child Day in India is observed on:
(a) 11 October (b) 8 March (c) 14 November (d) 24 January
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 11 October is the UN's International Day of the Girl Child. India's National Girl Child Day, started in 2008 by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, is 24 January.
Source: Ministry of Women and Child Development

39. Civil Services Day is observed on:
(a) 21 April (b) 24 April (c) 21 May (d) 11 May
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sardar Patel addressed the first batch of officers at Metcalfe House, Delhi, calling them the "steel frame of India"; that day, Civil Services Day, is 21 April.
Source: Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances

40. National Panchayati Raj Day (24 April) marks the coming into force of the:
(a) 42nd Constitutional Amendment (b) 74th Constitutional Amendment (c) 73rd Constitutional Amendment (d) 61st Constitutional Amendment
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: the 74th Amendment deals with urban local bodies. Panchayati Raj Day marks 24 April 1993, when the 73rd Amendment came into force.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Political Science (Democratic Politics II), Ch. Federalism

41. Indian Air Force Day is celebrated on:
(a) 15 January (b) 4 December (c) 8 October (d) 1 February
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 15 January is Army Day and 4 December is Navy Day. The Indian Air Force was set up in 1932, and Air Force Day is 8 October.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

42. Indian Coast Guard Day is observed on:
(a) 1 February (b) 4 December (c) 7 December (d) 21 October
Answer: (a)
Solution: Trap: 4 December is Navy Day and 7 December is Armed Forces Flag Day. The Indian Coast Guard was raised in 1977, and its day is 1 February.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

43. Vijay Diwas (16 December) commemorates India's victory in:
(a) the 1962 war (b) the 1965 war (c) the 1999 Kargil conflict (d) the 1971 war
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: Kargil (1999) has its own day, 26 July. On 16 December the Pakistani army surrendered in Dhaka and Bangladesh was born — the war of 1971.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

44. Armed Forces Flag Day is observed on:
(a) 4 December (b) 16 December (c) 15 January (d) 7 December
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 4 December is Navy Day and 16 December is Vijay Diwas. Armed Forces Flag Day, observed since 1949, falls on 7 December.
Source: Kendriya Sainik Board, Ministry of Defence

45. Police Commemoration Day (21 October) remembers policemen killed in 1959 at:
(a) Galwan Valley (b) Nathu La (c) Hot Springs, Ladakh (d) Tawang
Answer: (c)
Solution: On 21 October 1959 ten policemen were killed by Chinese troops at Hot Springs in Ladakh. Trap: Galwan Valley is linked to a clash in 2020, not to this day.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

46. World Population Day is observed on:
(a) 11 July (b) 11 June (c) 12 August (d) 1 October
Answer: (a)
Solution: World Population Day, started by the UNDP Governing Council in 1989, is observed on 11 July. 12 August is International Youth Day and 1 October is the International Day of Older Persons.
Source: United Nations Population Fund

47. World Food Day (16 October) marks the founding of which organisation?
(a) World Food Programme (b) UNICEF (c) World Health Organization (d) Food and Agriculture Organization
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) was founded on 16 October 1945. Trap: the World Food Programme has "food" in its name but is a different body.
Source: Food and Agriculture Organization

48. United Nations Day (24 October) marks the day in 1945 when:
(a) the UN Charter was signed (b) the UN Charter came into force (c) the Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted (d) the first UN General Assembly met
Answer: (b)
Solution: The UN Charter came into force on 24 October 1945. Trap: the Charter had been signed earlier, in June 1945; the Universal Declaration of Human Rights came in 1948.
Source: United Nations

49. International Literacy Day is observed on:
(a) 5 September (b) 8 March (c) 8 September (d) 11 November
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 5 September is Teachers' Day and 8 March is International Women's Day. UNESCO proclaimed International Literacy Day in 1966; it falls on 8 September.
Source: UNESCO

50. World Tourism Day is observed on:
(a) 25 January (b) 5 June (c) 18 April (d) 27 September
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 25 January is India's National Tourism Day. World Tourism Day, observed since 1980 by UN Tourism, falls on 27 September.
Source: United Nations (UN Tourism)
