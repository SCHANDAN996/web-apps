Set 3/6 – Important Days & Dates MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 51–75: Medium

51. World Wetlands Day (2 February) marks the signing of a convention on wetlands in 1971 at:
(a) Montreal, Canada (b) Stockholm, Sweden (c) Ramsar, Iran (d) Kyoto, Japan
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Ramsar Convention on wetlands was signed at Ramsar, Iran, on 2 February 1971. Trap: Montreal is linked to the Montreal Protocol (ozone, 16 September) and Stockholm to the 1972 environment conference.
Source: Ramsar Convention Secretariat

52. International Mother Language Day is observed on:
(a) 14 September (b) 21 February (c) 10 January (d) 23 April
Answer: (b)
Solution: Trap: 14 September is Hindi Diwas and 10 January is World Hindi Day. UNESCO proclaimed International Mother Language Day in November 1999 to remember students killed in Dhaka in 1952 while demanding recognition for Bangla; it falls on 21 February.
Source: UNESCO

53. World Wildlife Day (3 March) marks the signing of which agreement in 1973?
(a) Ramsar Convention (b) Montreal Protocol (c) Chicago Convention (d) CITES
Answer: (d)
Solution: CITES, the convention on international trade in endangered species, was signed on 3 March 1973. The Ramsar Convention (1971) is behind World Wetlands Day, not World Wildlife Day.
Source: United Nations (CITES Secretariat)

54. International Women's Day is observed on:
(a) 8 March (b) 8 May (c) 11 October (d) 24 January
Answer: (a)
Solution: International Women's Day falls on 8 March. Trap: 8 May is World Red Cross Day, 11 October is the International Day of the Girl Child and 24 January is India's National Girl Child Day.
Source: United Nations

55. World Consumer Rights Day is observed on:
(a) 24 December (b) 3 May (c) 15 March (d) 14 December
Answer: (c)
Solution: World Consumer Rights Day is 15 March. Trap: 24 December is India's National Consumer Day (Consumer Protection Act, 1986) — a look-alike pair.
Source: Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India

56. World Meteorological Day (23 March) marks the day in 1950 when:
(a) the India Meteorological Department was set up (b) the convention of the World Meteorological Organization came into force (c) the Montreal Protocol was signed (d) the first weather satellite was launched
Answer: (b)
Solution: The convention establishing the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) came into force on 23 March 1950. The Montreal Protocol (1987) is linked to the ozone day, 16 September.
Source: World Meteorological Organization

57. World Tuberculosis Day is observed on 24 March because on this day in 1882:
(a) the BCG vaccine was first used (b) Louis Pasteur made the rabies vaccine (c) Robert Koch announced the discovery of the TB germ (d) the World Health Organization was founded
Answer: (c)
Solution: On 24 March 1882 Robert Koch announced that he had discovered the bacterium that causes TB. Trap: WHO was founded much later, on 7 April 1948 — that is World Health Day.
Source: World Health Organization

58. The International Day of Forests is observed on:
(a) 22 April (b) 21 March (c) 5 June (d) 22 May
Answer: (b)
Solution: The International Day of Forests is 21 March, one day before World Water Day (22 March). 22 April is Earth Day, 5 June World Environment Day and 22 May the International Day for Biological Diversity.
Source: United Nations

59. Earth Day (22 April) was first held in the year:
(a) 1972 (b) 1987 (c) 1970 (d) 1993
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 1972 is the year of the Stockholm conference that gave us World Environment Day, and 1993 is when World Water Day was first observed. Earth Day was first held in 1970.
Source: United Nations

60. World Heritage Day, also called the International Day for Monuments and Sites, is observed on:
(a) 23 April (b) 27 September (c) 18 April (d) 3 May
Answer: (c)
Solution: 23 April is World Book and Copyright Day, 27 September World Tourism Day and 3 May World Press Freedom Day. World Heritage Day falls on 18 April.
Source: ICOMOS (International Council on Monuments and Sites)

61. World Book and Copyright Day, observed by UNESCO, falls on:
(a) 8 September (b) 23 April (c) 21 February (d) 18 April
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNESCO observes World Book and Copyright Day on 23 April. Trap: 8 September is International Literacy Day and 21 February is International Mother Language Day — both are also UNESCO days.
Source: UNESCO

62. 1 May is the foundation day of which two Indian states?
(a) Punjab and Haryana (b) Bihar and Odisha (c) Kerala and Karnataka (d) Maharashtra and Gujarat
Answer: (d)
Solution: Maharashtra and Gujarat were both formed on 1 May 1960, so 1 May is Maharashtra Day and Gujarat Day (and also International Labour Day). Kerala and Karnataka mark their formation on 1 November.
Source: Government of Maharashtra / Government of Gujarat

63. World Press Freedom Day is observed on:
(a) 3 May (b) 3 March (c) 3 December (d) 23 April
Answer: (a)
Solution: World Press Freedom Day is 3 May. 3 March is World Wildlife Day and 3 December is the International Day of Persons with Disabilities — same date number, different months.
Source: UNESCO

64. World Red Cross Day (8 May) is observed on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Florence Nightingale (b) Henry Dunant (c) Robert Koch (d) Karl Landsteiner
Answer: (b)
Solution: Henry Dunant, founder of the Red Cross, was born on 8 May. Trap: Florence Nightingale's birthday, 12 May, is International Nurses Day — four days later.
Source: International Committee of the Red Cross

65. International Nurses Day (12 May) honours the birth anniversary of:
(a) Mother Teresa (b) Henry Dunant (c) Florence Nightingale (d) Anandi Gopal Joshi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Florence Nightingale, the founder of modern nursing, was born on 12 May. Henry Dunant's birthday (8 May) is World Red Cross Day.
Source: World Health Organization

66. World Telecommunication and Information Society Day (17 May) marks the founding in 1865 of the:
(a) Universal Postal Union (b) UNESCO (c) International Telecommunication Union (d) Indian Space Research Organisation
Answer: (c)
Solution: The International Telecommunication Union (ITU) was founded on 17 May 1865. Trap: the Universal Postal Union (1874) is linked to World Post Day, 9 October.
Source: International Telecommunication Union

67. World No Tobacco Day, observed by WHO, falls on:
(a) 26 June (b) 7 April (c) 31 May (d) 1 December
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trap: 26 June is the International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking, and 7 April is World Health Day. WHO observes World No Tobacco Day on 31 May.
Source: World Health Organization

68. The International Day for Biological Diversity is observed on:
(a) 22 March (b) 22 April (c) 5 June (d) 22 May
Answer: (d)
Solution: The International Day for Biological Diversity is 22 May. Trap: three days share the number 22 — World Water Day (22 March), Earth Day (22 April) and Biodiversity Day (22 May); match the month carefully.
Source: United Nations

69. World Milk Day, observed by the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), falls on:
(a) 1 June (b) 26 November (c) 16 October (d) 1 July
Answer: (a)
Solution: World Milk Day is 1 June. Trap: 26 November is India's National Milk Day (Dr Verghese Kurien's birthday) and 16 October is World Food Day.
Source: Food and Agriculture Organization

70. World Oceans Day is observed on:
(a) 5 April (b) 8 June (c) 5 June (d) 22 March
Answer: (b)
Solution: World Oceans Day is 8 June. Trap: 5 April is India's National Maritime Day (SS Loyalty, 1919) and 5 June is World Environment Day.
Source: United Nations

71. World Blood Donor Day (14 June) is the birth anniversary of Karl Landsteiner, who discovered:
(a) the ABO blood groups (b) insulin (c) the TB germ (d) penicillin
Answer: (a)
Solution: Karl Landsteiner discovered the ABO blood groups, so his birthday, 14 June, is World Blood Donor Day. Insulin is linked to Frederick Banting (World Diabetes Day) and the TB germ to Robert Koch (World TB Day).
Source: World Health Organization

72. The International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking is observed on:
(a) 26 July (b) 31 May (c) 26 June (d) 21 June
Answer: (c)
Solution: This UN day falls on 26 June. Trap: 26 July is Kargil Vijay Diwas, and 21 June is the International Day of Yoga.
Source: United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime

73. International Tiger Day (29 July) was agreed at the Tiger Summit held in 2010 at:
(a) New Delhi (b) St Petersburg (c) Kathmandu (d) Stockholm
Answer: (b)
Solution: International Tiger Day was agreed at the Tiger Summit in St Petersburg, Russia, in 2010. Stockholm is linked to the 1972 environment conference, not to tigers.
Source: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (National Tiger Conservation Authority)

74. Hiroshima Day is observed on:
(a) 9 August (b) 6 August (c) 7 August (d) 14 August
Answer: (b)
Solution: The atomic bomb was dropped on Hiroshima on 6 August 1945. Trap: 9 August is Nagasaki Day (and also August Kranti Diwas in India).
Source: PYQ-style

75. International Youth Day, observed by the UN, falls on:
(a) 12 January (b) 11 July (c) 12 August (d) 1 October
Answer: (c)
Solution: The UN's International Youth Day is 12 August. Trap: 12 January is India's National Youth Day (Swami Vivekananda) — same date number, different month.
Source: United Nations
