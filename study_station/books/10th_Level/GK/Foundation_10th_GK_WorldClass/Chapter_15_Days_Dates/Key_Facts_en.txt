📋 Key Facts & Figures: Important Days & Dates

1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Republic Day	26 January — Constitution came into force, 1950.	"Constitution adopted on 26 January 1950" (adopted 26 November 1949).	Chronological Confusion
Constitution Day	26 November — adoption of the Constitution, 1949; declared Constitution Day in 2015.	Offering 26 January as Constitution Day.	Similar Name
Hindi Diwas	14 September — Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari as official language, 1949.	Offering 10 January (World Hindi Day).	Similar Name
World Hindi Day	10 January — first World Hindi Conference, Nagpur, 1975; observed since 2006.	Offering 14 September.	Similar Name
Teachers' Day	5 September — Dr S. Radhakrishnan's birth anniversary.	Offering 5 October (World Teachers' Day, UNESCO).	Similar Name
National Science Day	28 February — discovery of the Raman Effect, 1928.	"Raman's birthday" or offering 11 May.	Almost Correct Statement
National Technology Day	11 May — Pokhran tests, 1998.	Offering 28 February (Science Day).	Similar Name
National Youth Day	12 January — Swami Vivekananda; declared 1984, observed since 1985.	Offering 12 August (International Youth Day).	Similar Name
Parakram Diwas	23 January — Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose; declared 2021.	Offering 23 March (Bhagat Singh's martyrdom).	Similar Name
Martyrs' Day	30 January — Gandhi's assassination, 1948.	Offering 2 October (his birth anniversary).	Almost Correct Statement
National Voters' Day	25 January — ECI founded 1950; observed since 2011.	Offering 26 January.	Chronological Confusion
National Unity Day	31 October — Sardar Patel; observed since 2014.	Linking 31 October to a different leader.	Almost Correct Statement
National Education Day	11 November — Maulana Abul Kalam Azad; since 2008.	Offering 5 September (Teachers' Day).	Similar Name
Army / Air Force / Navy Day	15 January / 8 October / 4 December.	Swapping Navy (4 December) with Flag Day (7 December).	Similar Name
Vijay Diwas	16 December — victory in the 1971 war.	Offering 26 July (Kargil Vijay Diwas, 1999).	Chronological Confusion
World Health Day	7 April — WHO founded 1948.	Offering 7 April as World Heritage Day (18 April).	Similar Name
World Environment Day	5 June — decided at Stockholm, 1972.	Offering 22 April (Earth Day).	Almost Correct Statement
International Day of Yoga	21 June — UN resolution 11 December 2014; first observed 2015.	Offering 2014 as the first observance.	Chronological Confusion
Human Rights Day	10 December — UDHR adopted 1948.	Offering 24 October (UN Day).	Similar Name

2. Time Management (15/45-Sec Rule)
Question Type	Example	Time	Score Maximizer?
Direct date of a day	When is World Water Day?	15 sec	Yes
Whose birth anniversary	29 August honours whom?	15 sec	Yes
Defence day	When is Navy Day?	15 sec	Yes
Event behind the day	Why is 28 February Science Day?	15 sec	Yes
Look-alike pair	World Teachers' Day vs Teachers' Day	15–30 sec	Yes, after practice
Negative-phrased question	Which is NOT a birth anniversary?	45 sec	No (read carefully)
Arrange days in calendar order	Arrange four days from January to December	45 sec	No
Statement-based (2–3 statements)	Statements on Constitution Day and Republic Day	45 sec	No
Match the following (day–person)	Four days with four persons	45 sec	Yes, if two pairs are certain
