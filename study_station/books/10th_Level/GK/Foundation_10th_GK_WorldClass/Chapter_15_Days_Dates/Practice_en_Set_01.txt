Set 1/6 – Important Days & Dates MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Republic Day is celebrated in India on:
(a) 15 August (b) 26 November (c) 26 January (d) 2 October
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Constitution of India came into force on 26 January 1950, so Republic Day is 26 January. Trap: 26 November is Constitution Day, the day the Constitution was adopted in 1949.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Political Science (Democratic Politics I), Ch. Constitutional Design

2. Teachers' Day (5 September) is celebrated on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan (b) Dr Zakir Husain (c) Dr Rajendra Prasad (d) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dr S. Radhakrishnan, India's second President, was born on 5 September 1888. Maulana Azad's birth anniversary (11 November) is National Education Day, not Teachers' Day.
Source: Ministry of Education, Government of India

3. National Youth Day (12 January) is observed on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Bhagat Singh (b) Swami Vivekananda (c) Subhas Chandra Bose (d) Swami Dayanand Saraswati
Answer: (b)
Solution: Swami Vivekananda was born on 12 January 1863; the day was declared in 1984 and has been observed since 1985. Netaji's birth anniversary (23 January) is Parakram Diwas.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India

4. Children's Day (14 November) in India is the birth anniversary of:
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (c) Lal Bahadur Shastri (d) Jawaharlal Nehru
Answer: (d)
Solution: Jawaharlal Nehru, India's first Prime Minister, was born on 14 November 1889, and his birthday is celebrated as Bal Diwas. Sardar Patel's birth anniversary is 31 October.
Source: Government of India

5. National Science Day is celebrated in India on:
(a) 28 February (b) 11 May (c) 14 September (d) 5 June
Answer: (a)
Solution: C. V. Raman discovered the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928, so National Science Day is 28 February. Trap: 11 May is National Technology Day.
Source: Department of Science & Technology, Government of India

6. World Environment Day is observed every year on:
(a) 22 April (b) 22 March (c) 16 September (d) 5 June
Answer: (d)
Solution: World Environment Day was decided at the UN conference in Stockholm (1972) and is observed on 5 June. Trap: 22 April is Earth Day and 22 March is World Water Day.
Source: United Nations Environment Programme

7. The International Day of Yoga is observed on:
(a) 21 March (b) 21 June (c) 21 September (d) 21 December
Answer: (b)
Solution: 21 June, the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere, is the International Day of Yoga. 21 September is the International Day of Peace.
Source: United Nations

8. Army Day is celebrated in India on:
(a) 15 January (b) 8 October (c) 4 December (d) 26 July
Answer: (a)
Solution: Trap: 8 October is Air Force Day and 4 December is Navy Day. General K. M. Cariappa took over as the first Indian Commander-in-Chief on 15 January 1949, so Army Day is 15 January.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

9. National Sports Day (29 August) honours the birth anniversary of:
(a) Milkha Singh (b) Major Dhyan Chand (c) Sachin Tendulkar (d) Kapil Dev
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand was born on 29 August 1905. The other three are famous sportspersons but are not linked to this day.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of India

10. World Health Day is observed on:
(a) 1 December (b) 24 March (c) 7 April (d) 31 May
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Health Organization was founded on 7 April 1948, so World Health Day is 7 April. Trap: 1 December is World AIDS Day and 24 March is World TB Day — both health days, but not this one.
Source: World Health Organization

11. Hindi Diwas is celebrated in India on:
(a) 10 January (b) 21 February (c) 2 October (d) 14 September
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trap: 10 January is World Hindi Day, a different day. The Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari as the official language of the Union on 14 September 1949.
Source: Department of Official Language, Ministry of Home Affairs

12. The United Nations observes 2 October, Gandhi Jayanti, as the:
(a) International Day of Peace (b) International Day of Non-Violence (c) Human Rights Day (d) International Literacy Day
Answer: (b)
Solution: In 2007 the UN General Assembly declared Gandhi's birthday, 2 October, the International Day of Non-Violence. The International Day of Peace is 21 September.
Source: United Nations

13. Engineers' Day (15 September) is celebrated on the birth anniversary of:
(a) Homi J. Bhabha (b) Vikram Sarabhai (c) Sir M. Visvesvaraya (d) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sir Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya, the great civil engineer, was born on 15 September 1861. The other three are scientists, not linked to this day.
Source: Government of India

14. 30 January is observed as Martyrs' Day because on this day:
(a) Bhagat Singh was hanged (b) the Jallianwala Bagh massacre took place (c) Mahatma Gandhi was assassinated (d) the Quit India Movement began
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mahatma Gandhi was assassinated on 30 January 1948. Trap: Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev were hanged on 23 March 1931 — that date is also called Shaheed Diwas.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History (Themes in Indian History III), Ch. Mahatma Gandhi and the Nationalist Movement

15. India celebrates Independence Day on 15 August to mark its freedom in the year:
(a) 1945 (b) 1950 (c) 1949 (d) 1947
Answer: (d)
Solution: India became independent on 15 August 1947. 1950 is the year the Constitution came into force; India got freedom in 1947.
Source: NCERT Class 8 History

16. World Water Day is observed on:
(a) 22 March (b) 5 June (c) 22 May (d) 16 October
Answer: (a)
Solution: The UN observes World Water Day on 22 March (first observed in 1993). 22 May is the International Day for Biological Diversity.
Source: United Nations

17. Navy Day is celebrated in India on:
(a) 15 January (b) 8 October (c) 4 December (d) 1 February
Answer: (c)
Solution: Navy Day remembers Operation Trident, the attack on Karachi harbour during the 1971 war, and falls on 4 December. 1 February is Coast Guard Day.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

18. Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) is observed on:
(a) 26 January (b) 26 November (c) 15 August (d) 14 November
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on 26 November 1949; the government declared this day Constitution Day in 2015. Trap: 26 January is Republic Day.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Political Science (Democratic Politics I), Ch. Constitutional Design

19. World AIDS Day is observed on:
(a) 1 December (b) 7 April (c) 24 March (d) 10 October
Answer: (a)
Solution: 10 October is World Mental Health Day. World AIDS Day was first observed in 1988 and falls on 1 December.
Source: World Health Organization

20. Human Rights Day is observed on:
(a) 24 October (b) 1 December (c) 10 December (d) 26 June
Answer: (c)
Solution: The UN adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights on 10 December 1948, so Human Rights Day is 10 December. Trap: 24 October is United Nations Day.
Source: United Nations

21. National Technology Day (11 May) commemorates:
(a) the launch of the satellite Aryabhata (b) the Pokhran nuclear tests of 1998 (c) the launch of Chandrayaan-1 (d) the discovery of the Raman Effect
Answer: (b)
Solution: India carried out nuclear tests at Pokhran on 11 May 1998, which the day remembers. Trap: the Raman Effect (1928) is behind National Science Day, 28 February.
Source: Department of Science & Technology, Government of India

22. World Teachers' Day, observed by UNESCO, falls on:
(a) 5 September (b) 11 November (c) 5 October (d) 14 November
Answer: (c)
Solution: World Teachers' Day is 5 October. Trap: 5 September is India's own Teachers' Day (Dr Radhakrishnan's birthday) — same date number, different month.
Source: UNESCO

23. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) 23 January – National Youth Day (b) 11 November – Teachers' Day (c) 22 December – National Science Day (d) 31 October – National Unity Day
Answer: (d)
Solution: The others are wrong: 23 January is Parakram Diwas, 11 November is National Education Day and 22 December is National Mathematics Day. Sardar Patel's birth anniversary, 31 October, is National Unity Day.
Source: Government of India

24. National Voters' Day is observed on 25 January because on this day in 1950:
(a) the Constitution was adopted (b) the first Lok Sabha met (c) the Election Commission of India was founded (d) Panchayati Raj was introduced
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Election Commission of India was set up on 25 January 1950, one day before the Constitution came into force; National Voters' Day has been observed since 2011.
Source: Election Commission of India

25. Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed on:
(a) 16 December (b) 26 July (c) 15 January (d) 4 December
Answer: (b)
Solution: Trap: 16 December is Vijay Diwas, the 1971 war victory. Kargil Vijay Diwas marks Operation Vijay (1999) and is observed on 26 July.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India
