🃏 Flashcard Deck: Important Days & Dates (Foundation)

Card 1
Front: The Constitution was ready in 1949. Why did India wait two months to bring it into force on 26 January 1950?
Back: To honour 26 January 1930, when Purna Swaraj (complete independence) was celebrated across India after the Lahore Congress of 1929.

Card 2
Front: 26 November and 26 January — which is Constitution Day and which is Republic Day?
Back: 26 November = Constitution Day (adopted, 1949). 26 January = Republic Day (came into force, 1950).

Card 3
Front: National Science Day falls on 28 February. Is it C. V. Raman's birthday?
Back: No. It marks the discovery of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928. Declared in 1986, first celebrated in 1987.

Card 4
Front: Examiner's trap: "World Hindi Day — 14 September." What is wrong?
Back: 14 September is Hindi Diwas (Constituent Assembly decision, 1949). World Hindi Day is 10 January (first World Hindi Conference, Nagpur, 1975).

Card 5
Front: One teacher, two days. When is Teachers' Day and when is World Teachers' Day?
Back: Teachers' Day — 5 September (Dr S. Radhakrishnan's birthday). World Teachers' Day — 5 October (UNESCO).

Card 6
Front: Mnemonic check: what does "Youth 12, Bose 23, Gandhi 30" stand for?
Back: January's three person days — 12 Jan National Youth Day (Vivekananda), 23 Jan Parakram Diwas (Netaji), 30 Jan Martyrs' Day (Gandhi).

Card 7
Front: Mnemonic check: "Army Jan, Air Oct, Navy Dec — 15, 8, 4". Expand it.
Back: Army Day 15 January, Air Force Day 8 October, Navy Day 4 December.

Card 8
Front: Which nuclear tests gave India a "day", and what is it called?
Back: Pokhran tests of 11 May 1998 — 11 May is National Technology Day.

Card 9
Front: Why is 25 January National Voters' Day?
Back: The Election Commission of India was founded on 25 January 1950. The day has been observed since 2011.

Card 10
Front: Which world body was born on 7 April 1948, giving us a "day"?
Back: The World Health Organization (WHO) — 7 April is World Health Day.

Card 11
Front: Who proposed the International Day of Yoga, and when did the UN declare it?
Back: India proposed it; the UN General Assembly declared 21 June on 11 December 2014. First observed on 21 June 2015.

Card 12
Front: Two "victory days" — which war does each remember?
Back: Vijay Diwas, 16 December — 1971 war (Bangladesh born). Kargil Vijay Diwas, 26 July — Kargil, 1999.

Card 13
Front: Whose birthday is National Unity Day, and since when is it observed?
Back: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (31 October). Observed as Rashtriya Ekta Diwas since 2014.

Card 14
Front: Which day remembers the Swadeshi Movement of 7 August 1905?
Back: National Handloom Day (7 August), first celebrated in 2015.

Card 15
Front: What happened at Hot Springs, Ladakh, on 21 October 1959, and which day remembers it?
Back: Ten policemen were killed by Chinese troops. 21 October is Police Commemoration Day.

Card 16
Front: Mnemonic check: "Water 22, Weather 23, TB 24" — which month, which days?
Back: March — World Water Day (22), World Meteorological Day (23), World TB Day (24).

Card 17
Front: Examiner's trap: "Martyrs' Day is Gandhi's birth anniversary." Fix it.
Back: Martyrs' Day (30 January) is the day Gandhi was assassinated (1948). His birth anniversary is 2 October.

Card 18
Front: Which newest national day comes from the Moon?
Back: National Space Day, 23 August — Chandrayaan-3 landed on 23 August 2023; first observed in 2024.
