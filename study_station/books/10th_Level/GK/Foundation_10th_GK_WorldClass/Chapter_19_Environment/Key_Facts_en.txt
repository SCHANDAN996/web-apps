📋 Key Facts & Figures: Environment & Ecology (Basic)

1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Ecosystem (term)	Coined by A. G. Tansley in 1935.	Offering Ernst Haeckel, who coined "ecology".	Similar Name
Decomposers	Bacteria and fungi; return nutrients to the soil.	Calling fungi "producers" because they grow like plants (fungi cannot make their own food).	Almost True Statement
10% law	Only about 10% of energy passes to the next trophic level (Lindeman, 1942).	Offering 90% (that is the share lost, not passed on).	Reverse Causation
Energy vs nutrients	Energy flow is one-way; nutrients cycle.	"Energy is recycled by decomposers."	Almost True Statement
Pyramid of energy	Always upright.	"Pyramid of biomass is always upright" (it is inverted in the sea).	Almost True Statement
Pyramid of numbers	Inverted for a single tree with many insects.	Saying it is always upright.	Almost True Statement
Biological magnification	DDT/mercury concentration rises at higher trophic levels.	"Concentration is highest in producers."	Reverse Causation
Eutrophication	Excess nitrates/phosphates → algal bloom → oxygen loss → fish die.	Calling it "biomagnification".	Similar Name
Ozone layer	In the stratosphere; absorbs UV rays; depleted by CFCs.	Saying it is in the troposphere, or that ground-level ozone is useful.	Almost True Statement
Greenhouse gases	CO2, CH4, N2O, CFCs, water vapour.	Offering nitrogen (N2) — similar to nitrous oxide (N2O) but not a GHG.	Similar Name
Montreal vs Kyoto	Montreal 1987 = ozone; Kyoto 1997 = greenhouse gases.	Swapping the two.	Similar Name
Ramsar Convention	1971, Ramsar (Iran); wetlands; India joined 1982.	Saying it was signed in India or deals with tigers.	Almost True Statement
CITES	1973; trade in endangered wild species; India party 1976.	Saying it protects wetlands or the ozone layer.	Similar Name
Bhopal and EPA	Bhopal gas tragedy 1984 (MIC) → Environment (Protection) Act 1986.	Offering 1984 as the year of the Act.	Chronological Confusion
Article 51A(g) vs 48A	51A(g) = Fundamental Duty; 48A = Directive Principle; both by the 42nd Amendment (1976).	Swapping duty and directive, or giving the 44th Amendment.	Similar Name
In-situ vs ex-situ	National parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves = in-situ; zoos, seed banks = ex-situ.	"Which is NOT in-situ?" with a zoo hidden in the options.	Negative Phrasing
First national park	Hailey NP (1936), now Jim Corbett, Uttarakhand.	Offering Gir or Kaziranga.	Almost True Statement
Minamata vs Itai-itai	Mercury vs cadmium.	Swapping the two metals.	Similar Name
Blue baby syndrome	Nitrate in drinking water.	Offering fluoride (that causes fluorosis).	Similar Name
Chipko vs Appiko	Chipko 1973 (Uttarakhand region); Appiko 1983 (Karnataka).	Placing Appiko in Kerala.	Almost True Statement

2. Time Management (15/45-Sec Rule)
Question Type	Example	Time	Score Maximizer?
Term / coined-by one-liner	Who coined "ecosystem"?	15 sec	Yes
Pollutant–disease pair	Minamata disease is caused by?	15 sec	Yes
Park–animal pair	Gir is famous for?	15 sec	Yes
Day–date question	World Ozone Day?	15 sec	Yes
Act / convention year	Air Act was passed in?	15 sec	Yes
10% law calculation	Energy at the 3rd level if producers have 10,000 J	45 sec	No (write the steps)
Pyramid statements	Which pyramid can be inverted?	45 sec	No
Chronological order of Acts / treaties	Arrange Ramsar, CITES, Montreal, Kyoto	45 sec	No
Multi-statement on ozone or climate	Statements on Montreal and Kyoto	45 sec	No
Negative phrasing	Which is NOT a greenhouse gas?	45 sec	No (read NOT twice)
