Set 4/6 – Environment & Ecology MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Ramsar Convention on Wetlands of International Importance was signed in 1971 in which country?
(a) Iran (b) Switzerland (c) Kenya (d) France
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ramsar Convention was adopted on 2 February 1971 in the Iranian city of Ramsar on the Caspian Sea coast; 2 February is celebrated annually as World Wetlands Day.
Source: ramsar.org

77. Which was the first Ramsar wetland site designated in India in 1981, alongside Keoladeo National Park?
(a) Chilika Lake (b) Wular Lake (c) Vembanad Lake (d) Sambhar Lake
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chilika Lake in Odisha and Keoladeo National Park in Rajasthan were designated as India's first two Ramsar sites in 1981.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

78. The historic Chipko Movement to protect trees from commercial logging was launched in 1973 in:
(a) Chamoli district, Uttarakhand (b) Shimla district, Himachal Pradesh (c) Uttara Kannada district, Karnataka (d) Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Chipko Movement started in April 1973 in the village of Reni in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand under the grassroots leadership of Gaura Devi and Chandi Prasad Bhatt.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Political Science

79. The Appiko Movement, inspired by the northern Chipko movement to embrace trees, was started in 1983 in which state?
(a) Karnataka (b) Kerala (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pandurang Hegde spearheaded the Appiko movement in the Uttara Kannada district of Karnataka to protect the Western Ghats forests from clear-felling.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Sociology

80. In 1730, Amrita Devi and 362 other members of which community sacrificed their lives protecting Khejri trees in Rajasthan?
(a) Bishnoi community (b) Gujjar community (c) Meena community (d) Bhil community
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amrita Devi Bishnoi led villagers of Khejarli near Jodhpur in sacrificing their lives to protect sacred Khejri (Prosopis cineraria) trees from the Maharaja's soldiers.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Environmental Issues

81. Which National Park in West Bengal forms part of the world's largest contiguous mangrove forest and is a UNESCO World Heritage site?
(a) Sundarbans National Park (b) Jaldapara National Park (c) Gorumara National Park (d) Buxa National Park
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sundarbans National Park in the delta of the Ganga, Brahmaputra and Meghna rivers in West Bengal is the world's largest mangrove forest and home to the Royal Bengal tiger.
Source: UNESCO (whc.unesco.org)

82. The specialized roots of mangrove trees that grow vertically upward above water and mud to absorb atmospheric oxygen are called:
(a) Prop roots (b) Pneumatophores (breathing roots) (c) Haustoria (d) Epiphytic roots
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mangrove trees like Rhizophora and Avicennia grow in waterlogged, anaerobic mud; they develop pneumatophores (respiratory roots) that stick out of the mud to obtain oxygen.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology, Morphology of Flowering Plants

83. Pichavaram Mangrove Forest, one of the largest and most scenic mangrove forests in India and a designated Ramsar site, is located in:
(a) Kerala (b) Tamil Nadu (c) Andhra Pradesh (d) Odisha
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pichavaram Mangrove Forest is located near Chidambaram in the Cuddalore district of Tamil Nadu, situated between the Vellar and Coleroon estuaries.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

84. The Montreux Record is a register of Ramsar wetland sites that face ecological changes. Which two Indian wetlands are currently listed in the Montreux Record?
(a) Chilika Lake and Wular Lake (b) Keoladeo National Park and Loktak Lake (c) Sundarbans and Vembanad Lake (d) Sambhar Lake and Harike Wetland
Answer: (b)
Solution: Currently, Keoladeo National Park (Rajasthan) and Loktak Lake (Manipur) are in the Montreux Record. Chilika Lake was removed from the record in 2002 after successful ecological restoration.
Source: ramsar.org

85. India's largest Ramsar wetland site by surface area is:
(a) Vembanad-Kol Wetland (b) Sundarbans Wetland (c) Chilika Lake (d) Kolleru Lake
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Indian Sundarbans Wetland covers an area of over 4,230 square kilometres, making it the largest Ramsar site in India.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

86. Which famous environmentalist and environmental lawyer won landmark public interest litigations (PILs) to protect the Taj Mahal and clean the Ganga?
(a) Sunderlal Bahuguna (b) M. C. Mehta (c) Chandi Prasad Bhatt (d) Anil Agarwal
Answer: (b)
Solution: M. C. Mehta pioneered public interest environmental litigation in India, winning the Taj Trapezium case in 1996 and landmark orders against industrial pollution along the Ganga.
Source: PYQ-style

87. The "Narmada Bachao Andolan" (Save Narmada Movement) against large dams on the Narmada river was led primarily by:
(a) Arundhati Roy (b) Medha Patkar (c) Gaura Devi (d) Sunita Narain
Answer: (b)
Solution: Medha Patkar and Baba Amte led the Narmada Bachao Andolan starting in the 1980s to advocate for displaced tribal and farming communities facing submergence from the Sardar Sarovar Dam.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Political Science

88. The Silent Valley Movement in the 1970s was organized to prevent a hydroelectric dam project across which river in Kerala?
(a) Periyar river (b) Kunthipuzha river (c) Bharatapuzha river (d) Pamba river
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Silent Valley environmental movement protested a proposed hydroelectric dam across the pristine Kunthipuzha river, saving the contiguous rainforest from submergence.
Source: PYQ-style

89. Jadav Payeng, honoured with the Padma Shri and celebrated as the "Forest Man of India", created a 550-hectare man-made forest on a sandbar of which river?
(a) Ganga river (b) Godavari river (c) Brahmaputra river (d) Teesta river
Answer: (c)
Solution: Jadav "Molai" Payeng single-handedly planted and nurtured the Molai Forest on a barren sandbar of the Brahmaputra river near Majuli in Assam over four decades.
Source: PYQ-style

90. Dr. Salim Ali, revered as the "Birdman of India", played a key role in the creation of which famous bird sanctuary?
(a) Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary (b) Sultanpur Bird Sanctuary (c) Bharatpur Bird Sanctuary (Keoladeo) (d) Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dr. Salim Ali was instrumental in saving the Keoladeo wetland at Bharatpur from commercial exploitation and converting it into a world-renowned bird sanctuary and national park.
Source: PYQ-style

91. Coral reefs in India are majorly found in all of the following regions EXCEPT:
(a) Gulf of Mannar (b) Andaman and Nicobar Islands (c) Mouth of the Ganges in West Bengal (d) Lakshadweep Islands
Answer: (c)
Solution: Coral polyps require clear, warm, saline water with low sedimentation; massive freshwater and silt discharge at the mouth of the Ganges in Bengal prevents coral growth.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography, India: Physical Environment

92. Which coral island group of India is composed entirely of atolls and coral reefs in the Arabian Sea?
(a) Andaman Islands (b) Nicobar Islands (c) Lakshadweep (d) Rameswaram
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lakshadweep is an archipelago of 36 coral islands and atolls built up by the accumulation of coral skeletons over submarine volcanic ridges.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography, Physical Features of India

93. The smallest Ramsar wetland site in India by surface area (roughly 20 hectares) is:
(a) Chandertal Wetland (b) Pong Dam Lake (c) Renuka Wetland (d) Rudrasagar Lake
Answer: (c)
Solution: Renuka Wetland in the Sirmaur district of Himachal Pradesh is a natural freshwater lake covering roughly 20 hectares, making it the smallest Ramsar site in India.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

94. The annual "World Wetlands Day" is celebrated across the globe on:
(a) 22 March (b) 22 April (c) 2 February (d) 5 June
Answer: (c)
Solution: While World Water Day is on 22 March and Earth Day is on 22 April, World Wetlands Day is observed annually on 2 February.
Source: ramsar.org

95. Sunderlal Bahuguna popularized which famous slogan during the Chipko Movement: "Ecology is permanent economy"?
(a) "Save trees, save rainfall" (b) "Forest is green gold" (c) "Plant a tree for every child" (d) "Ecology is permanent economy"
Answer: (d)
Solution: Gandhian environmentalist Sunderlal Bahuguna gave the iconic message "Ecology is permanent economy" to emphasize that nature's ecological services far outweigh timber profits.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science

96. Which is the only active volcanic island in India and South Asia, located in the Andaman Sea?
(a) Narcondam Island (b) Ross Island (c) Havelock Island (d) Barren Island
Answer: (d)
Solution: Barren Island, located about 138 km northeast of Port Blair in the Andaman Sea, is the only confirmed active volcano in South Asia. Narcondam is a dormant volcano.
Source: Geological Survey of India (gsi.gov.in)

97. The National Wetland Conservation Programme (NWCP) in India provides financial and technical assistance to states for:
(a) Deep-sea trawling (b) Construction of hydroelectric reservoirs (c) Commercial mangrove cutting (d) Conservation and wise use of wetlands
Answer: (d)
Solution: NWCP operates under the MoEFCC to support the conservation, ecological restoration, and sustainable management of identified priority wetlands across India.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

98. Sunderlal Bahuguna was also a key leader who went on hunger strikes in the 1990s protesting the construction of which mega-dam?
(a) Bhakra Nangal Dam (b) Sardar Sarovar Dam (c) Hirakud Dam (d) Tehri Dam
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sunderlal Bahuguna vehemently opposed the Tehri Dam on the Bhagirathi river in Uttarakhand due to severe seismic hazards and ecological disruption in the fragile Himalayas.
Source: PYQ-style

99. The "Wildlife Week" is celebrated all over India every year during which period?
(a) 1 to 7 July (b) 5 to 11 June (c) 14 to 20 November (d) 2 to 8 October
Answer: (d)
Solution: National Wildlife Week is observed annually throughout India from 2 to 8 October to promote wildlife preservation, coinciding with Mahatma Gandhi's birth anniversary.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

100. The Government of India instituted the "Amrita Devi Bishnoi National Award" to honour outstanding contributions in:
(a) Solar panel manufacturing (b) Agricultural grain production (c) Organic fertilizer research (d) Wildlife protection and conservation
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Amrita Devi Bishnoi National Award for Wildlife Protection is given to individuals or communities from rural areas that have shown exemplary courage in protecting wildlife and flora.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)
