🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Mnemonic 1: Ecology vs Ecosystem
"Haeckel says Hello to Ecology; Tansley Ties the System."
Haeckel → Ecology (the subject) | Tansley (1935) → Ecosystem (the unit).

🧠 Mnemonic 2: The 10% law ladder
"Ten Thousand, Thousand, Hundred, Ten" — 10,000 J → 1,000 J → 100 J → 10 J.
Each step down the food chain, knock one zero off.

🧠 Mnemonic 3: Energy and nutrients
"Energy buys a one-way ticket; nutrients buy a return ticket."
Energy flow is unidirectional; nutrients are recycled by decomposers.

🧠 Mnemonic 4: Which pyramid flips?
"Energy never does a headstand. Numbers flip on a Tree. Biomass flips in the Sea."
Pyramid of energy — always upright | numbers — inverted for a tree | biomass — inverted in the sea.

🧠 Mnemonic 5: Japanese metal diseases
"Mercury Melts Minamata; Cadmium Cries Itai-itai."
Itai-itai literally means "it hurts, it hurts" — picture painful cadmium bones.

🧠 Mnemonic 6: Water poisons and their diseases
"Nitrate makes a Navy-blue baby; Fluoride Forms Fluorosis; Arsenic blackens the Ankle."
Nitrate → Blue baby syndrome | Fluoride → Fluorosis | Arsenic → Blackfoot disease.

🧠 Mnemonic 7: Ozone vs global warming
"Umbrella torn — UV is born. Blanket thick — Earth gets sick."
Ozone layer = umbrella (Montreal 1987). Greenhouse gases = blanket (Kyoto 1997, Paris 2015).

🧠 Mnemonic 8: Greenhouse gases
"Cows Make Noisy Clouds Warm" — Carbon dioxide, Methane, Nitrous oxide, CFCs, Water vapour.
Cows remind you that cattle give out methane. Trap check: Nitrogen (N2) is NOT in the list.

🧠 Mnemonic 9: Indian Acts in order
"Wild Water Forest Air Environment" — 72, 74, 80, 81, 86.
Story: wild animals drink water, live in the forest, breathe air — that is the environment.

🧠 Mnemonic 10: Conventions in order
"Real Clever Villagers Make Rich Kings Proud"
Ramsar 1971 → CITES 1973 → Vienna 1985 → Montreal 1987 → Rio 1992 → Kyoto 1997 → Paris 2015.

🧠 Mnemonic 11: Montreal vs Kyoto
"M for Mask of the sky (ozone); K for Kitchen smoke (greenhouse gases)."
Montreal = ozone layer; Kyoto = greenhouse gases.

🧠 Mnemonic 12: In-situ vs ex-situ
"IN-situ = IN the jungle; EX-situ = EXit the jungle."
In-situ: national park, sanctuary, biosphere reserve, sacred grove. Ex-situ: zoo, botanical garden, seed bank, gene bank.

🧠 Mnemonic 13: Green-days calendar
"Wet Wild Forest Water Earth Bio-Environment Ozone"
2 Feb Wetlands → 3 Mar Wildlife → 21 Mar Forests → 22 Mar Water → 22 Apr Earth → 22 May Biodiversity → 5 Jun Environment → 16 Sep Ozone.
Extra hook: "Triple 22" — 22 March Water, 22 April Earth, 22 May Biodiversity.

🧠 Mnemonic 14: Forest movements
"Chipko Climbs the hills (Uttarakhand, 1973); Appiko Arrives in Karnataka (1983); Bishnoi Bled for Khejri (1730)."

🧠 Mnemonic 15: Park–animal pairs
"Gir Growls (lion), Kaziranga Kicks with one horn (rhino), Dachigam Dances (Hangul), Keibul Lamjao floats with Sangai."

⏭️ Skip Strategy

1. If a question asks you to arrange five or more Acts or conventions by year and you cannot place even two of them with certainty, skip it in the first round — it eats 45 seconds or more and the options are built to confuse.

2. If a question asks for the exact present count of Ramsar sites, tiger reserves or biosphere reserves and you have not checked the latest official figure, do not guess between close numbers — these counts change during the year. Skip unless you are sure.

3. If a "match the following" lists four small sanctuaries or rare species you have never studied, use elimination only when you are sure about at least two pairs; otherwise skip.
