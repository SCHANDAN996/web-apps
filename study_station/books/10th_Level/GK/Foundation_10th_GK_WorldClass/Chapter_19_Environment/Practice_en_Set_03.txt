Set 3/6 – Environment & Ecology MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Project Tiger was launched in India on 1 April 1973 from which National Park?
(a) Jim Corbett National Park (b) Kaziranga National Park (c) Ranthambore National Park (d) Kanha National Park
Answer: (a)
Solution: Project Tiger was initiated on 1 April 1973 at Corbett National Park in Uttarakhand to ensure a viable population of Bengal tigers in their natural habitats.
Source: NTCA (ntca.gov.in)

52. Project Elephant was launched by the Government of India in which year?
(a) 1992 (b) 1973 (c) 1986 (d) 2002
Answer: (a)
Solution: While Project Tiger was launched in 1973, Project Elephant was launched to protect Asian elephants and their habitats in 1992.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

53. Which National Park in India is the world's only floating national park and the natural home of the endangered Sangai deer?
(a) Keibul Lamjao National Park (b) Kaziranga National Park (c) Silent Valley National Park (d) Namdapha National Park
Answer: (a)
Solution: Keibul Lamjao National Park, located on Loktak Lake in Manipur, is characterized by floating decomposed plant masses called 'phumdis' and houses the Sangai (brow-antlered deer).
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography, Natural Vegetation and Wildlife

54. The critically endangered Kashmiri stag or "Hangul" is protected exclusively in which National Park?
(a) Dachigam National Park (b) Hemis National Park (c) Kishtwar National Park (d) Jim Corbett National Park
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dachigam National Park near Srinagar in Jammu & Kashmir contains the last viable wild population of the Hangul (Cervus hanglu hanglu).
Source: PYQ-style

55. The Lion-tailed Macaque, an endangered arboreal primate endemic to the Western Ghats, is primarily protected in:
(a) Silent Valley National Park (b) Gir National Park (c) Sundarbans National Park (d) Kanha National Park
Answer: (a)
Solution: Silent Valley National Park in the Palakkad district of Kerala holds one of the largest remaining populations of the endangered Lion-tailed Macaque.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography, India: Physical Environment

56. The famous "Project Cheetah", which reintroduced African cheetahs to India in 2022, released the first batch of animals in:
(a) Kuno National Park (b) Ranthambore National Park (c) Bandhavgarh National Park (d) Sariska National Park
Answer: (a)
Solution: In September 2022, eight wild cheetahs brought from Namibia were released into quarantine bomas at Kuno National Park in Madhya Pradesh.
Source: NTCA (ntca.gov.in), 2022

57. Gir National Park in Gujarat is globally renowned as the only remaining wild sanctuary of:
(a) Bengal Tiger (b) Asiatic Lion (c) Snow Leopard (d) Clouded Leopard
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gir National Park and Wildlife Sanctuary in Saurashtra, Gujarat, is the sole natural home of the pure Asiatic Lion (Panthera leo persica).
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography, Natural Vegetation and Wildlife

58. Kaziranga National Park in Assam holds the world's largest population of which iconic mammal?
(a) Royal Bengal Tiger (b) Great Indian One-horned Rhinoceros (c) Asian Elephant (d) Asiatic Wild Buffalo
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kaziranga National Park along the Brahmaputra floodplains hosts over two-thirds of the world's great one-horned rhinoceroses.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography, Natural Vegetation and Wildlife

59. The National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA) was granted statutory status by amending which Act in 2006?
(a) Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 (b) Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 (c) Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (d) Biological Diversity Act, 2002
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Wildlife (Protection) Amendment Act, 2006 created the NTCA to strengthen tiger conservation and manage tiger reserves across India.
Source: NTCA (ntca.gov.in)

60. Nokrek Biosphere Reserve, renowned for preserving the wild gene pool of citrus species and the Red Panda, is located in:
(a) Sikkim (b) Meghalaya (c) Manipur (d) Mizoram
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nokrek Biosphere Reserve is located in the Garo Hills of Meghalaya; it protects the endangered wild gene sanctuary for citrus indica.
Source: UNESCO MAB (unesco.org)

61. Which National Park in Assam is famous for being home to rare species such as the Pygmy Hog and the Golden Langur?
(a) Orang National Park (b) Manas National Park (c) Nameri National Park (d) Dehing Patkai National Park
Answer: (b)
Solution: Manas National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site in Assam contiguous with Bhutan's Royal Manas, protects the pygmy hog, golden langur, and hispid hare.
Source: UNESCO (whc.unesco.org)

62. The Gulf of Mannar Marine Biosphere Reserve in Tamil Nadu is famous for protecting which herbivorous marine mammal?
(a) Gangetic Dolphin (b) Dugong (Sea cow) (c) Blue Whale (d) Olive Ridley Turtle
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Gulf of Mannar is home to the vulnerable Dugong (sea cow), which feeds on shallow seagrass beds along the Tamil Nadu coastline.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

63. The rare melanistic (black) tigers are found in the wild exclusively in which Tiger Reserve of India?
(a) Sundarbans Tiger Reserve (b) Simlipal Tiger Reserve (c) Bandipur Tiger Reserve (d) Periyar Tiger Reserve
Answer: (b)
Solution: Simlipal Tiger Reserve in the Mayurbhanj district of Odisha is the world's only natural habitat harboring melanistic tigers with thick, pseudo-melanistic stripes.
Source: NTCA (ntca.gov.in)

64. Namdapha National Park in Arunachal Pradesh is unique in India because it shelters four species of:
(a) Wild canids (b) Antelopes (c) Wild cats (Tiger, Leopard, Snow Leopard, Clouded Leopard) (d) Crocodilians
Answer: (c)
Solution: Namdapha National Park in the Changlang district of Arunachal Pradesh spans altitudinal zones from tropical to alpine, harboring four big cat species: tiger, leopard, snow leopard, and clouded leopard.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Hemis National Park, India's largest national park by area, is situated in which Union Territory/State and is a haven for which predator?
(a) Himachal Pradesh, Brown Bear (b) Uttarakhand, Himalayan Musk Deer (c) Ladakh, Snow Leopard (d) Jammu and Kashmir, Hangul
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hemis National Park in eastern Ladakh covers 4,400 sq km and possesses the highest density of snow leopards in any protected area in the world.
Source: PYQ-style

66. Which National Park in Madhya Pradesh is famous for successfully reintroducing the hard-ground Swamp Deer (Barasingha), its official mascot "Bhoorsingh"?
(a) Panna National Park (b) Pench National Park (c) Kanha National Park (d) Satpura National Park
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kanha National Park saved the hard-ground Barasingha (Rucervus duvaucelii branderi) from near extinction; Bhoorsingh the Barasingha is its official mascot.
Source: PYQ-style

67. Keoladeo National Park (formerly Bharatpur Bird Sanctuary) in Rajasthan is situated at the confluence of which two rivers?
(a) Chambal and Banas (b) Yamuna and Hindon (c) Gambhir and Banganga (d) Luni and Sukri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Keoladeo Ghana is a man-made wetland created at the confluence of the Gambhir and Banganga rivers, hosting thousands of wintering migratory birds.
Source: UNESCO (whc.unesco.org)

68. Dibru-Saikhowa National Park in Assam is recognized for its unique population of:
(a) Wild yaks (b) Snow leopards (c) Feral horses (wild horses) (d) Asiatic cheetahs
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dibru-Saikhowa National Park in Tinsukia and Dibrugarh districts of Assam is famous for its semi-wild feral horses and white-winged wood ducks.
Source: PYQ-style

69. Panna National Park in Madhya Pradesh was recently in the news due to the river-interlinking project between which two rivers?
(a) Narmada and Tapti (b) Godavari and Krishna (c) Ken and Betwa (d) Chambal and Son
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Ken-Betwa Link Project transfers surplus water from the Ken basin to the Betwa basin, partially submerging core areas of Panna Tiger Reserve.
Source: PIB (pib.gov.in)

70. The "MIKE" programme, established under CITES to track illegal poaching, specifically monitors which animal?
(a) Tigers (b) Rhinoceroses (c) Snow Leopards (d) Elephants
Answer: (d)
Solution: The MIKE (Monitoring the Illegal Killing of Elephants) programme was set up under CITES to provide information needed for elephant-range states to make conservation decisions.
Source: CITES (cites.org)

71. Great Himalayan National Park, inscribed as a UNESCO Natural World Heritage site in 2014, is located in which district of Himachal Pradesh?
(a) Shimla (b) Kangra (c) Lahaul and Spiti (d) Kullu
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Great Himalayan National Park Conservation Area is located in the Kullu district of Himachal Pradesh, conserving biodiversity in the Western Himalayas.
Source: UNESCO (whc.unesco.org)

72. Dehang-Debang Biosphere Reserve, featuring pristine sub-tropical and alpine forest ecosystems, is situated in:
(a) Nagaland (b) Assam (c) Mizoram (d) Arunachal Pradesh
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dehang-Debang Biosphere Reserve spans the Siang and Upper Dibang Valley districts of Arunachal Pradesh.
Source: MoEFCC (moef.gov.in)

73. The Indian Rhino Vision 2020 (IRV 2020) was a joint initiative aimed at translocating one-horned rhinos primarily into which National Park?
(a) Jim Corbett National Park (b) Dudhwa National Park (c) Valmiki National Park (d) Manas National Park
Answer: (d)
Solution: Under IRV 2020, wild rhinos were translocated from Kaziranga and Pobitora to re-establish a viable breeding population in Manas National Park.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Bhitarkanika National Park in Odisha is globally famous for supporting the largest population of which reptile in India?
(a) Gharial (b) Indian Rock Python (c) King Cobra (d) Saltwater Crocodile
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bhitarkanika National Park, within the Brahmani-Baitarani delta in Odisha, holds over 70% of India's estuarine or saltwater crocodiles (Crocodylus porosus).
Source: PYQ-style

75. Desert National Park, representing the delicate ecology of the Thar Desert and protecting the Great Indian Bustard, is located across which two districts of Rajasthan?
(a) Bikaner and Churu (b) Jodhpur and Nagaur (c) Udaipur and Rajsamand (d) Jaisalmer and Barmer
Answer: (d)
Solution: Desert National Park spans Jaisalmer and Barmer districts of Rajasthan and is the critical stronghold of the critically endangered Great Indian Bustard (Godawan).
Source: PYQ-style
