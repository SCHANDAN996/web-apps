126. The 10% law of energy transfer in ecological food chains, stating that only 10 percent of energy is passed to the next trophic level, was proposed by:
(a) Raymond Lindeman (b) Arthur Tansley (c) Charles Elton (d) Eugene Odum
Answer: (a)
Solution: Raymond Lindeman formulated the 10% Law of Energy Transfer in 1942, demonstrating that approximately 90% of energy is lost as metabolic heat between successive trophic levels.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Our Environment

127. In the ecological succession of a bare rock (xerarch succession), which organisms act as the "pioneer species"?
(a) Crustose lichens (b) Bryophyte mosses (c) Annual herbaceous grasses (d) Perennial woody shrubs
Answer: (a)
Solution: Crustose lichens secrete organic acids that weather the bare rock into fine soil particles, allowing foliose lichens, mosses, and later grasses to establish.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Ecosystem

128. Under the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD), the "Cartagena Protocol" specifically regulates:
(a) Safe handling, transfer, and transboundary movement of Living Modified Organisms (LMOs) (b) Fair and equitable sharing of benefits from genetic resources (c) Elimination of persistent organic pollutants (d) Regulation of international trade in endangered timber species
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety (2000) protects biological diversity from potential risks posed by living modified organisms resulting from modern biotechnology.
Source: CBD (cbd.int)

129. At the COP26 summit in Glasgow (2021), India announced its commitment to achieve Net-Zero carbon emissions by which target year?
(a) 2070 (b) 2050 (c) 2030 (d) 2047

Source: PIB (pib.gov.in), 2021

130. The "International Solar Alliance" (ISA), jointly launched by India and France in 2015, has its global headquarters located in:
(a) Gurugram, India (b) Paris, France (c) Geneva, Switzerland (d) Nairobi, Kenya
Answer: (a)
Solution: The International Solar Alliance is an intergovernmental treaty-based organization headquartered at the National Institute of Solar Energy (NISE) in Gurugram, Haryana.
Source: ISA (isolaralliance.org)

131. In biogeochemical cycles, which of the following is an example of a "sedimentary cycle" where the primary reservoir pool is Earth's crust?
(a) Phosphorus cycle (b) Nitrogen cycle (c) Carbon cycle (d) Oxygen cycle
Answer: (a)
Solution: In the phosphorus and sulphur cycles, the primary reservoirs are rocks and sediments in Earth's crust (sedimentary cycle). Carbon, nitrogen, and oxygen are gaseous cycles.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Ecosystem

132. The "Nagoya Protocol" under the Convention on Biological Diversity focuses on:
(a) Elimination of single-use plastics from oceans (b) Access to Genetic Resources and the Fair and Equitable Sharing of Benefits Arising from their Utilization (ABS) (c) Prevention of transboundary movement of hazardous nuclear wastes (d) International trade regulations for endangered migratory birds
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Nagoya Protocol (adopted in 2010) establishes a transparent legal framework for Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) arising from the utilization of traditional genetic resources.
Source: CBD (cbd.int)

133. The "Basel Convention" on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes and their Disposal was adopted in 1989 to prevent:
(a) Trade in endangered timber species (b) Dumping of hazardous chemical and industrial waste from developed to developing nations (c) Commercial whale hunting in international waters (d) Extraction of fossil fuels in the Antarctic continent
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Basel Convention was enacted to curb the toxic trade where industrialized countries exported dangerous industrial wastes to developing nations.
Source: UNEP (unep.org)

134. What is the designated global target year for the "Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework" (GBF) to protect at least 30% of the planet's land and oceans (30x30 target)?
(a) 2025 (b) 2030 (c) 2040 (d) 2050
Answer: (b)
Solution: Adopted at COP15 in December 2022, Target 3 of the Kunming-Montreal GBF commits nations to protect 30% of terrestrial, inland water, and marine areas by 2030.
Source: CBD (cbd.int)

135. The "Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure" (CDRI), an international partnership launched by India at the UN Climate Action Summit in 2019, is headquartered in:
(a) Tokyo, Japan (b) New Delhi, India (c) New York, USA (d) Bonn, Germany
Answer: (b)
Solution: CDRI is an international alliance of national governments, UN agencies, and banks headquartered in New Delhi to promote resilient infrastructure against climate risks.
Source: cdri.world

136. The maximum population size of a biological species that an environment can sustain indefinitely without degrading natural resources is termed:
(a) Biotic potential (b) Carrying capacity (c) Ecological niche (d) Trophic efficiency
Answer: (b)
Solution: The carrying capacity (denoted as K) represents the limit imposed by environmental resistance (food, space, water) on the exponential growth of a population.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Organisms and Populations

137. Which ecological pyramid is ALWAYS upright and can NEVER be inverted in any stable ecosystem?
(a) Pyramid of numbers (b) Pyramid of energy (c) Pyramid of biomass (d) Pyramid of volume
Answer: (b)
Solution: The pyramid of energy is always upright because according to thermodynamics, energy is continuously lost as heat at each trophic level (10% law), never increasing upward.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Ecosystem

138. The "Rotterdam Convention" operates an international regulatory procedure known as:
(a) Clean Development Mechanism (b) Prior Informed Consent (PIC) for hazardous chemicals and pesticides (c) Extended Producer Responsibility (d) Carbon Credit Trading Scheme
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Rotterdam Convention facilitates information exchange by requiring Prior Informed Consent (PIC) before any signatory nation exports banned or severely restricted hazardous pesticides.
Source: UNEP (unep.org)

139. In population interactions, which ecological relationship describes an interaction where one species benefits while the other species is neither helped nor harmed (+ / 0)?
(a) Mutualism (b) Amensalism (c) Commensalism (d) Parasitism
Answer: (c)
Solution: Commensalism benefits one partner without affecting the other (+/0), such as an orchid growing as an epiphyte on a mango branch, or barnacles on a whale.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Organisms and Populations

140. When cattle egrets forage in pastures alongside grazing cattle to catch insects stirred up by their movement, this interaction is a classic example of:
(a) Predation (b) Amensalism (c) Commensalism (d) Mutualism
Answer: (c)
Solution: The cattle egret benefits by easily finding insects flushed out by moving cattle, while the cattle remain unaffected (+/0), representing commensalism.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Organisms and Populations

141. The penicillin fungus inhibits the growth of neighbouring bacteria without deriving any direct nutritional benefit (- / 0). This interaction is termed:
(a) Commensalism (b) Competition (c) Amensalism (d) Mutualism
Answer: (c)
Solution: Amensalism is an ecological interaction where one species is harmed or inhibited while the other remains unaffected (-/0).
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Organisms and Populations

142. The term "Ecosystem" was first coined in 1935 by the British ecologist:
(a) Ernst Haeckel (b) Charles Elton (c) Arthur Tansley (d) Eugene Odum
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sir Arthur Tansley coined the term "Ecosystem" in 1935. Ernst Haeckel coined the word "Ecology" in 1866, and Charles Elton introduced ecological pyramids in 1927.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Ecosystem

143. Which schedule under the Wildlife (Protection) Amendment Act, 2022 provides the highest level of absolute protection to endangered animal species?
(a) Schedule IV (b) Schedule III (c) Schedule I (d) Schedule II
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 2022 Amendment rationalized the schedules: Schedule I gives highest protection to specially protected animals (tiger, elephant, rhino), and Schedule II covers other protected animals.
Source: PYQ-style

144. In ecosystem services classification by the Millennium Ecosystem Assessment, carbon sequestration and flood mitigation are categorized under:
(a) Provisioning services (b) Cultural services (c) Regulating services (d) Supporting services
Answer: (c)
Solution: Regulating services include climate regulation, carbon sequestration, disease control, water purification, and flood control performed by healthy ecosystems.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

147. Under the Wildlife (Protection) Amendment Act, 2022, which schedule is dedicated to specimens of protected plant species?
(a) Schedule I (b) Schedule II (c) Schedule IV (d) Schedule III
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Wildlife (Protection) Amendment Act, 2022 restructured schedules into four: Schedule I and II for animal species, Schedule III for protected plants, and Schedule IV for CITES specimens.
Source: PYQ-style

148. Which term defines the functional role and physical space occupied by an organism within its ecological community?
(a) Biome (b) Ecotone (c) Biosphere (d) Ecological Niche
Answer: (d)
Solution: Joseph Grinnell and Charles Elton defined the ecological niche as the unique functional role, resource requirements, and micro-habitat of a species in an ecosystem.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology, Organisms and Populations

149. "Bhopal Memorial Hospital and Research Centre" was established following the Bhopal Gas Tragedy caused by the accidental release of:
(a) Potassium cyanide (b) Phosgene gas (c) Sulphur mustard (d) Methyl isocyanate (MIC)
Answer: (d)
Solution: On the night of 2–3 December 1984, approximately 40 tonnes of toxic methyl isocyanate (MIC) gas leaked from the Union Carbide pesticide plant in Bhopal.
