126. Consider the following statements regarding the Preamble of the Indian Constitution:
1. It is a source of power to the legislature.
2. It is not enforceable in any court of law.
3. It cannot be amended by Parliament.
4. It states the basic objectives of the Constitution.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 2 only (b) 2 and 4 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 2, 3 and 4 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Preamble is non-justiciable (not enforceable in court) and outlines core objectives. It is neither a source of power nor a prohibition, and it can be amended subject to basic structure.
