26. The Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly was formally appointed on which date?
(a) 15 August 1947 (b) 29 August 1947 (c) 26 November 1947 (d) 9 December 1946
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Constituent Assembly set up the Drafting Committee under the chairmanship of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar on 29 August 1947.

33. Which of the following statements about Fundamental Duties in the Indian Constitution is INCORRECT?
(a) They were added by the 42nd Amendment in 1976. (b) They were recommended by the Swaran Singh Committee. (c) They can be directly enforced by courts like Fundamental Rights. (d) There are currently 11 Fundamental Duties.

35. Assertion [A]: The Constitution of India is described as a 'living document'.
Reason [R]: The Constitution provides mechanisms under Article 368 to adapt to changing societal needs through amendments.
Select the correct answer:
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A. (c) A is true but R is false. (d) A is false but R is true.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Because the Constitution contains flexible amendment procedures to adapt to changing times without losing its core principles, it is called a living document. Both A and R are true and R correctly explains A.
