51. Who was elected as the temporary President of the Constituent Assembly during its inaugural session on 9 December 1946?
(a) Dr. Rajendra Prasad (b) Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha (c) Jawaharlal Nehru (d) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Following the French practice of appointing the oldest member, Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha presided over the opening meeting on 9 December 1946.

52. How many types of prerogative writs can be issued by the Supreme Court under Article 32 for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights?
(a) 3 writs (b) 4 writs (c) 5 writs (d) 6 writs
Answer: (c)
Solution: Under Article 32, the Supreme Court is empowered to issue five types of writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, and Quo-Warranto.
