76. In India, the 'Right to Vote' guaranteed to adult citizens under Article 326 is a:
(a) Fundamental Right (b) Constitutional / Legal Right (c) Natural Right (d) Customary Right
Answer: (b)
Solution: Voting in India is a constitutional right granted under Article 326, not a Fundamental Right under Part III.

78. In which landmark case did the Supreme Court hold that Parliament cannot amend Fundamental Rights under Article 368 to take away or abridge them?
(a) Shankari Prasad case (1951) (b) Sajjan Singh case (1965) (c) Golaknath case (1967) (d) Minerva Mills case (1980)
Answer: (c)
Solution: In Golaknath v. State of Punjab (1967), an 11-judge bench ruled that Parliament could not curtail any Fundamental Right through constitutional amendments.
