1. The Constitution of India came into full force and effect on which date?
(a) 26 November 1949 (b) 15 August 1947 (c) 26 January 1950 (d) 9 December 1946
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Indian Constitution came into full legal force on 26 January 1950, celebrated nationwide as Republic Day. It was adopted earlier on 26 November 1949.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Social and Political Life-III

2. The first meeting of the Constituent Assembly of India was held on:
(a) 26 November 1946 (b) 9 December 1946 (c) 15 August 1947 (d) 26 January 1950
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Constituent Assembly held its first meeting on 9 December 1946 in New Delhi with Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha as its temporary President.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Social and Political Life-III

3. The words 'Socialist' and 'Secular' were added to the Preamble of the Indian Constitution by which amendment?
(a) 24th Amendment Act (b) 42nd Amendment Act (c) 44th Amendment Act (d) 52nd Amendment Act
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 42nd Amendment Act of 1976 added the terms 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity' to the Preamble.

4. Which Article of the Constitution was described by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar as the 'heart and soul of the Constitution'?
(a) Article 14 (b) Article 19 (c) Article 21 (d) Article 32
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dr. Ambedkar described Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies) as the heart and soul of the Constitution because it guarantees judicial protection of fundamental rights.

(a) Ireland (b) United States (c) United Kingdom (d) Canada

Solution: The Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV) were borrowed from the Constitution of Ireland.

6. Fundamental Duties are incorporated in which Part of the Indian Constitution?
(a) Part III (b) Part IV (c) Part IV-A (d) Part V
Answer: (c)

7. How many Schedules were there originally in the Indian Constitution when it was adopted in 1949?
(a) 8 Schedules (b) 10 Schedules (c) 12 Schedules (d) 14 Schedules
Answer: (a)
Solution: The original Constitution of India contained 395 Articles, 22 Parts, and 8 Schedules. Currently there are 12 Schedules.

Solution: Article 1 describes India as a 'Union of States' rather than a federation, signifying an indestructible union.

9. Which of the following is NOT a Fundamental Right under Part III of the Indian Constitution?

14. The 'Basic Structure Doctrine' of the Indian Constitution was propounded by the Supreme Court in which landmark case?
(a) Golaknath case (1967) (b) Kesavananda Bharati case (1973) (c) Minerva Mills case (1980) (d) Maneka Gandhi case (1978)
Answer: (b)
Solution: A historic 13-judge bench in Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973) held that Parliament cannot alter the basic structure of the Constitution.

(a) 42nd Amendment Act (b) 44th Amendment Act (c) 73rd Amendment Act (d) 86th Amendment Act
Answer: (a)
Solution: Due to extensive and sweeping changes across numerous Articles and the Preamble, the 42nd Amendment Act (1976) is called the Mini-Constitution.

17. Under which Article of the Constitution can the President impose President's Rule in a State upon failure of constitutional machinery?
(a) Article 352 (b) Article 356 (c) Article 360 (d) Article 365
Answer: (b)
Solution: Article 356 empowers the President to proclaim President's Rule in a state when its governance cannot be carried out in accordance with the Constitution. Hence state emergency is governed by Article 356.

Solution: The concept of Rule of Law (equality before the law) was borrowed from the British constitutional jurist A.V. Dicey's concept.

21. Which of the following statements regarding the Preamble of the Indian Constitution is correct?

Answer: (c)
