📝 Practice MCQs – Set 5 (25 Questions: 101–125)

101. (Medium) Who among the following was the Chairman of the Union Constitution Committee of the Constituent Assembly?
(a) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
(b) Jawaharlal Nehru
(c) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
(d) Dr. Rajendra Prasad

Answer: (b) Jawaharlal Nehru
Solution: Nehru chaired the Union Constitution Committee. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee, Patel the Provincial Constitution Committee, Rajendra Prasad was President of the Assembly.
Source: PYQ-style
Trap type: Similar names/roles

102. (Medium) Which of the following Articles of the Indian Constitution provides for the establishment of a Finance Commission?
(a) Article 280
(b) Article 324
(c) Article 263
(d) Article 148

Answer: (a) Article 280
Solution: Article 280 provides for the Finance Commission to recommend distribution of revenues between Centre and States. Article 324 is for Election Commission, 263 for Inter-State Council, 148 for CAG.
Source: PYQ-style

103. (Medium) Which part of the Constitution declares India as a “Union of States”?
(a) Part I
(b) Part II
(c) Part III
(d) Part IV

Answer: (a) Part I
Solution: Part I (Articles 1-4) deals with the Union and its territory. Article 1 states “India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States.”
Source: PYQ-style

104. (Medium) The expression “Procedure Established by Law” in Article 21 is borrowed from the Constitution of:
(a) Britain
(b) USA
(c) Japan
(d) Canada

Answer: (c) Japan
Solution: The phrase “Procedure established by law” is taken from the Japanese Constitution. In contrast, the American Constitution uses “due process of law.”
Source: PYQ-style

105. (Medium) Which of the following Fundamental Rights is not available to non-citizens?
(a) Equality before law (Article 14)
(b) Protection of life and personal liberty (Article 21)
(c) Freedom of assembly (Article 19)
(d) Prohibition of traffic in human beings (Article 23)

Answer: (c) Freedom of assembly (Article 19)
Solution: The six freedoms under Article 19 are restricted to citizens only. Articles 14, 21, 23 are available to all persons, citizens or not.
Source: PYQ-style
Trap type: Negative phrasing ("not") + common misconception

106. (Medium) The Preamble of the Indian Constitution was amended for the first and only time in:
(a) 1975
(b) 1976
(c) 1977
(d) 1978

Answer: (b) 1976
Solution: The 42nd Amendment Act, 1976, amended the Preamble to add “Socialist”, “Secular”, and “Integrity”. No other amendment has changed the Preamble.
Source: Banking PO 2021

107. (Medium) Which of the following is the correct description of the Constituent Assembly’s composition after partition?
(a) 389 members, all elected by provincial legislatures
(b) 299 members, 229 from British provinces and 70 from princely states
(c) 292 members, 229 from British provinces and 63 from princely states
(d) 324 members, 292 from provinces and 32 from princely states

Answer: (b) 299 members, 229 from British provinces and 70 from princely states
Solution: After partition, the strength of the Constituent Assembly was reduced from 389 to 299, comprising 229 representatives of provinces and 70 from princely states.
Source: PYQ-style

108. (Medium) The term “Democratic” in the Preamble signifies that:
(a) India has a presidential form of government
(b) The head of the state is elected
(c) The government derives its authority from the people
(d) Both (b) and (c)

Answer: (d) Both (b) and (c)
Solution: Democracy means the people elect their representatives, and the government is responsible to them. The head of the state (President) is also elected, unlike a monarchy. Option (a) is incorrect because India has a parliamentary system, not presidential.
Source: PYQ-style

109. (Medium) The Directive Principles of State Policy aim to establish:
(a) A theocratic state
(b) A welfare state
(c) A police state
(d) A capitalist state

Answer: (b) A welfare state
Solution: DPSPs direct the State to secure a social order with economic and social justice, aiming to make India a welfare state.
Source: PYQ-style

110. (Medium) Which schedule of the Constitution of India contains provisions regarding the administration and control of scheduled areas and scheduled tribes?
(a) Fourth Schedule
(b) Fifth Schedule
(c) Sixth Schedule
(d) Seventh Schedule

Answer: (b) Fifth Schedule
Solution: The Fifth Schedule deals with administration and control of scheduled areas and scheduled tribes in states other than Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram (which are under the Sixth Schedule).
Source: PYQ-style

111. (Medium) The Constitution of India guarantees which of the following to all persons?

Equality before law

Equal protection of laws

Equality of opportunity in public employment

Protection from discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth
Select the correct answer:
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1, 2 and 4
(c) 1, 2, 3 and 4
(d) 1 and 4 only

Answer: (b) 1, 2 and 4
Solution: Articles 14 (equality before law and equal protection of laws) and 15 (prohibition of discrimination) are available to all persons. Article 16 (equality of opportunity in public employment) is available only to citizens.
Source: PYQ-style

112. (Medium) The power to issue writs for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights is conferred on:
(a) Supreme Court only
(b) High Courts only
(c) Both Supreme Court and High Courts
(d) All courts in India

Answer: (c) Both Supreme Court and High Courts
Solution: Article 32 empowers the Supreme Court, and Article 226 empowers High Courts to issue writs for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.
Source: Banking PO 2022

113. (Medium) Which of the following is not a constitutional amendment that affected Fundamental Rights?
(a) 1st Amendment, 1951
(b) 24th Amendment, 1971
(c) 44th Amendment, 1978
(d) 73rd Amendment, 1992

Answer: (d) 73rd Amendment, 1992
Solution: The 73rd Amendment dealt with Panchayati Raj institutions and does not directly affect Fundamental Rights. The 1st, 24th, and 44th amendments significantly impacted Fundamental Rights.
Source: PYQ-style
Trap type: Negative phrasing ("not") + mixing amendment purposes

114. (Medium) The Supreme Court’s advisory jurisdiction is provided under which Article?
(a) Article 143
(b) Article 132
(c) Article 136
(d) Article 226

Answer: (a) Article 143
Solution: Article 143 empowers the President to seek the advisory opinion of the Supreme Court on any question of law or fact of public importance.
Source: PYQ-style

115. (Medium) Which one of the following statements regarding the President of India is incorrect?
(a) The President is the head of the State.
(b) The President is elected directly by the people.
(c) The President is the supreme commander of the armed forces.
(d) The President appoints the Prime Minister.

Answer: (b) The President is elected directly by the people.
Solution: The President is elected indirectly by an electoral college consisting of elected members of Parliament and state legislative assemblies.
Source: PYQ-style
Trap type: Almost correct (many assume direct election)

116. (Medium) The Fundamental Duties under Article 51A were added on the recommendation of which committee?
(a) Swaran Singh Committee
(b) Sarkaria Commission
(c) Balwant Rai Mehta Committee
(d) L.M. Singhvi Committee

Answer: (a) Swaran Singh Committee
Solution: The Swaran Singh Committee (1976) recommended the inclusion of Fundamental Duties. These were added by the 42nd Amendment Act.
Source: PYQ-style

117. (Medium) Which of the following provisions shows the secular character of the Indian Constitution?
(a) Preamble declares India as a Secular State
(b) Article 25-28 guarantee freedom of religion
(c) The State does not have any official religion
(d) All of the above

Answer: (d) All of the above
Solution: All these provisions collectively establish the secular nature of the Indian State.
Source: Banking PO 2021

118. (Medium) The term “Federal” is not mentioned in the Indian Constitution. Instead, which phrase is used?
(a) Federation of States
(b) Union of States
(c) Confederation of States
(d) Association of States

Answer: (b) Union of States
Solution: Article 1 uses the phrase “Union of States.” Dr. Ambedkar explained that the Union is indestructible and not based on an agreement among states.
Source: PYQ-style
Trap type: Negative phrasing (“not”) + almost correct (Federal is a popular term)

119. (Medium) Which Article of the Constitution deals with the impeachment of the President of India?
(a) Article 61
(b) Article 56
(c) Article 62
(d) Article 124

Answer: (a) Article 61
Solution: Article 61 provides for the impeachment of the President for violation of the Constitution. Article 56 deals with the term, 62 with vacancy, 124 with Supreme Court.
Source: PYQ-style

120. (Medium) The Right to Education under Article 21A applies to children in the age group of:
(a) 0-6 years
(b) 6-14 years
(c) 14-18 years
(d) 0-14 years

121. (Hard) Consider the following statements regarding the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976:

It added the words ‘Socialist’ and ‘Secular’ to the Preamble.

It inserted Fundamental Duties.

It transferred subjects from the State List to the Concurrent List.

It gave primacy to Fundamental Rights over Directive Principles.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 only
(b) 2, 3 and 4 only
(c) 1, 2 and 4 only
(d) 1 and 3 only

Answer: (a) 1, 2 and 3 only
Solution: Statement 4 is incorrect: the 42nd Amendment sought to give primacy to Directive Principles over Fundamental Rights (Article 31C), not the reverse. The other statements are correct.
Source: PYQ-style
Trap type: Reverse causation (FR and DPSP primacy reversed)

122. (Hard) Which of the following is not a part of the basic structure of the Constitution as identified by the Supreme Court?
(a) Secular character of the Constitution
(b) Federal character of the Constitution
(c) Fundamental Rights
(d) Right to Property

Answer: (d) Right to Property
Solution: The Supreme Court in various cases has held that secularism, federalism, judicial review, democracy, and Fundamental Rights are part of the basic structure. Right to Property, though once a Fundamental Right, is not part of the basic structure after the 44th Amendment.
Source: PYQ-style
Trap type: Negative phrasing + confusion with historical fundamental right

123. (Hard) Assertion (A): The Preamble is a part of the Constitution.
Reason (R): The Preamble can be amended under Article 368.
Choose the correct option:
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.

Answer: (b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
Solution: In Kesavananda Bharati (1973), the SC held that the Preamble is part of the Constitution and can be amended under Article 368, but the fact that it can be amended does not explain why it is a part; it is a part because it is the introductory statement. Thus R does not explain A.
Source: PYQ-style

124. (Hard) Match List I with List II and select the correct answer:
List I (Case)
A. Golaknath v. State of Punjab
B. Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala
C. Minerva Mills v. Union of India
D. Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India
List II (Outcome)

Due process interpretation of Article 21

FRs cannot be amended

Basic Structure Doctrine

Harmony between FR and DPSP
Choose the correct code:
(a) A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
(b) A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
(c) A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
(d) A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

Answer: (a) A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
Solution: Golaknath (1967) held FRs cannot be amended; Kesavananda (1973) propounded Basic Structure; Minerva Mills (1980) stressed harmony between FR and DPSP; Maneka Gandhi (1978) expanded Article 21 to include due process elements.
Source: PYQ-style

125. (Hard) Which of the following statements about the Constituent Assembly are correct?

Its first meeting was held on 9 December 1946.

Dr. Rajendra Prasad was its temporary Chairman.

It worked through various committees, including the Drafting Committee.

The Muslim League participated in all its meetings.
Select the correct answer:
(a) 1 and 3 only
(b) 1, 2 and 3
(c) 1, 3 and 4
(d) 2, 3 and 4

Answer: (a) 1 and 3 only
Solution: Statement 2 is incorrect because Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha was the temporary Chairman; Dr. Rajendra Prasad became permanent President later. Statement 4 is incorrect because the Muslim League boycotted the Constituent Assembly.
Source: PYQ-style
Trap type: Similar names (temporary vs permanent Chairman) + factually wrong statement about participation
