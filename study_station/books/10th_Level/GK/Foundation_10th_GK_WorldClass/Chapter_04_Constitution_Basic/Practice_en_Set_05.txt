101. Who among the following was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly?
(a) Dr. Rajendra Prasad (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (d) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

123. Assertion [A]: The Preamble can override an express provision of the Indian Constitution.
Reason [R]: The Preamble is an integral part of the Constitution and indicates its underlying philosophy.
Select the correct answer:
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A. (b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A. (c) A is true but R is false. (d) A is false but R is true.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Preamble is an integral part of the Constitution, but it is neither a source of power to legislature nor can it override any clear constitutional article. Thus A is false but R is true.
