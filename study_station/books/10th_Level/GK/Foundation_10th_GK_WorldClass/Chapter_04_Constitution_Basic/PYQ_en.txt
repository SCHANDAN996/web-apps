4. Top 10 High-Yield PYQ with Solutions & Trap Type

Q1. (UPSC 2023)
Which of the following is correct regarding the adoption and enforcement of the Indian Constitution?
(a) It was adopted on 26 January 1950 and enforced on 26 November 1949.
(b) It was adopted on 26 November 1949 and enforced on 26 January 1950.
(c) Both adoption and enforcement happened on 26 January 1950.
(d) Both adoption and enforcement happened on 26 November 1949.
Solution: (b) Adopted on 26 Nov 1949; enforced on 26 Jan 1950.
Trap type: Chronological Confusion (swapping dates).
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 15-sec fact-based; answer directly if known, skip if confused.

Q2. (SSC CGL 2022)
The words "Socialist" and "Secular" were added to the Preamble by which Constitutional Amendment?
(a) 42nd Amendment, 1976
(b) 44th Amendment, 1978
(c) 24th Amendment, 1971
(d) 26th Amendment, 1971
Solution: (a) 42nd Amendment, 1976.
Trap type: Similar Numbers (42, 44, 24, 26 – confusing amendment numbers).
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 15-sec fact-based; must recall the exact amendment number.

Q3. (UPSC 2021)
Which Article of the Indian Constitution is called the "heart and soul" by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar?
(a) Article 19
(b) Article 21
(c) Article 32
(d) Article 226
Solution: (c) Article 32 – Right to Constitutional Remedies.
Trap type: Similar Names/Articles (21 and 226 also deal with rights/remedies).
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 15-sec fact.

Q4. (SSC CHSL 2020)
"Right to Work" in India is a –
(a) Fundamental Right under Part III
(b) Directive Principle under Part IV
(c) Fundamental Duty under Part IV-A
(d) Constitutional Right under Part V
Solution: (b) Directive Principle under Article 41.
Trap type: Almost Correct Statement + Negative Phrasing if asked as "Which is not a Fundamental Right?"
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 15-sec fact; a classic DPSP vs FR confusion.

Q5. (Railway NTPC 2019)
The Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP) in the Indian Constitution are borrowed from the Constitution of –
(a) USA
(b) Ireland
(c) UK
(d) Canada
Solution: (b) Ireland.
Trap type: Similar Names/Sources (UK and USA are more prominent in FR borrowings).
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 15-sec fact.

Q6. (UPSC 2018)
In the Kesavananda Bharati case (1973), the Supreme Court laid down the doctrine of –
(a) Parliamentary Sovereignty
(b) Basic Structure of the Constitution
(c) Judicial Activism
(d) Principle of Natural Justice
Solution: (b) Basic Structure.
Trap type: Reverse Causation (confusing Parliament's power with Court's limit).
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 45-sec analytical – requires understanding of the doctrine.

Q7. (Banking PO 2021)
Which of the following is NOT a Fundamental Right?
(a) Right to Equality
(b) Right to Freedom of Religion
(c) Right to Work
(d) Right to Constitutional Remedies
Solution: (c) Right to Work (DPSP).
Trap type: Negative Phrasing ("NOT") + mixing DPSP with FRs.
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 15-sec fact if you know the classification.

Q8. (SSC CGL 2024)
How many Schedules did the original Constitution of India contain?
(a) 8
(b) 10
(c) 12
(d) 22
Solution: (a) 8. (Current 12)
Trap type: Almost Correct Statement (current number 12 presented as original).
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 15-sec fact.

Q9. (UPSC 2016)
The Constitution of India was enacted by the Constituent Assembly on –
(a) 26 November 1949
(b) 26 January 1950
(c) 15 August 1947
(d) 9 December 1946
Solution: (a) 26 November 1949.
Trap type: Chronological Confusion (multiple important dates).
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 15-sec fact.

Q10. (Railway NTPC 2022)
Which feature of the Indian Constitution is NOT borrowed from the UK?
(a) Parliamentary form of government
(b) Rule of Law
(c) Single Citizenship
(d) Written Constitution
Solution: (d) Written Constitution (UK has an unwritten constitution).
Trap type: Negative Phrasing ("NOT") + Similar Concepts.
⏱️ 15/45 sec rule: 45-sec analytical – compare features; understand that UK's is unwritten.

📊 Application of 15/45-Second Rule on Two Questions:

Q1 (Adoption/Enforcement dates): Fact-based → 15 seconds. Rule: if you recall the mnemonic "Adopt a Novel in Nov, Enforce Republic in Jan," answer immediately; else skip, as it's a simple retrieval with high negative marking risk if confused.

Q6 (Kesavananda Bharati): Analytical → 45 seconds. This question tests the concept behind the case, not just the name. Allocate time to reason: "Parliament wanted to amend anything, but Court limited it. That limit is Basic Structure." If unsure about the exact doctrine name, but understand the concept, you can still deduce. Do not spend more than 45 sec; flag for review if needed.
