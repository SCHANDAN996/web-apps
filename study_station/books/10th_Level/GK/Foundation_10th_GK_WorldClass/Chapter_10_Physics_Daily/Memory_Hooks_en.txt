🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Hook 1: Seven SI Base Units
Sentence: "My Kind Sister Always Keeps Many Candles."
My → metre | Kind → kilogram | Sister → second | Always → ampere | Keeps → kelvin | Many → mole | Candles → candela
Visual: Your sister lighting candles on a weighing scale (kg) with a clock (second) and a thermometer (kelvin).

🧠 Hook 2: Light Year Is Not a Year
Phrase: "Light YEAR is a light's YARD."
A yard is a length — so a light year is a length (distance), about 9.46 × 10^15 m.

🧠 Hook 3: The Electricity "Unit"
Phrase: "Kilo-Watt-HOUR is energy you pay for by the HOUR."
1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3.6 × 10^6 J. Horsepower (746 W) is power — the horse pulls, it does not store.

🧠 Hook 4: Newton's Three Laws
Phrase: "I Feel Alive."
I → Inertia (bus brakes) | F → F = ma (catch a ball, pull hands back) | A → Action–reaction (rocket, gun recoil)

🧠 Hook 5: Hygro, Hydro, Lacto
Story: "Hygro checks the Humid air, Hydro checks the liquid's density, Lacto checks the Lassi's milk."
Hygrometer → humidity | Hydrometer → relative density of liquids | Lactometer → purity of milk

🧠 Hook 6: Pressure Cooker vs Mountains
Rhyme: "Pressure UP, boiling UP; Mountain UP, boiling DOWN."
Cooker → boiling point above 100 °C. Hills → boiling point below 100 °C.

🧠 Hook 7: Pascal vs Archimedes
Phrase: "Pascal Pushes, Archimedes Afloat."
Pascal → hydraulic lift, brakes, press. Archimedes → floating, ships, buoyancy.

🧠 Hook 8: Water at 4 °C
Phrase: "Four degrees — Fish Free."
Water is densest at 4 °C, so lakes freeze from the top and fish survive below.

🧠 Hook 9: Steam Burns
Phrase: "Steam carries a Secret packet."
The secret packet is latent heat of vaporisation (about 22.5 × 10^5 J/kg), released on the skin.

🧠 Hook 10: Speed of Sound
Phrase: "Solid Speeds, Liquid Lags, Gas Goes slow — Vacuum: no show."
Steel about 5960 m/s > water about 1500 m/s > air about 346 m/s; vacuum → zero.

🧠 Hook 11: Infra and Ultra
Phrase: "INFRA is INFERIOR (below 20 Hz), ULTRA is ULTIMATE (above 20 kHz)."
Ultra users: Bats, Dolphins, SONAR, Scans — "BaD SoniCS".
Infra makers: earthquakes, whales, rhinoceroses, elephants.

🧠 Hook 12: Pitch and Loudness
Phrase: "Pitch = Frequency (both have an F-sound in 'Pitch-Freak'); Loud = Amplitude (A for Aloud)."

🧠 Hook 13: Mirrors in Daily Life
Phrase: "Convex looks Back, Concave Shaves and Shines."
Convex → rear-view, road turns. Concave → shaving, dentist, torch and headlight reflectors, solar furnace.

🧠 Hook 14: Eye Defects
Phrase: "MyopiA–concAve, HypermetropiA–conveX (Hyper needs eXtra power)."
Presbyopia → bifocal. Astigmatism → cylindrical.

🧠 Hook 15: Scattering Colours
Phrase: "Blue Bounces, Red Runs."
Blue scatters the most → blue sky. Red scatters the least → danger signals, red sunset.

🧠 Hook 16: Spectrum Order (longest to shortest wavelength)
Sentence: "Raju Met In Vizag Uncle X-ray Gupta."
Radio → Microwave → Infrared → Visible → Ultraviolet → X-ray → Gamma

🧠 Hook 17: House Wiring
Phrase: "Red Runs, Black Back, Green Ground."
Live red, neutral black, earth green (NCERT). The fuse sits on the runner — in series with the live wire.

🧠 Hook 18: Motor vs Generator
Phrase: "MOtor makes MOtion; GEnerator GEts current."
Motor: electrical → mechanical. Generator: mechanical → electrical (Faraday's induction).

⏭️ Skip Strategy

1. Instrument-matching questions with four pairs: If you are sure of two pairs and they eliminate three options, mark and move on. If you know none of the four pairs, skip at once — guessing a four-pair match rarely pays when there is negative marking.

2. Numericals with unfamiliar formulas: If the formula does not come to mind within 10 seconds (for example, a pendulum or gravitation problem with odd numbers), skip and return later. Spend your 45 seconds on familiar ones — echo, P = VI, kWh, temperature conversion.

3. Statement-based questions: If you can confirm one statement as definitely wrong, eliminate every option containing it. If you cannot judge any statement with confidence, skip — do not choose "both" just because it looks safe.
