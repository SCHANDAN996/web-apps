Set 1/6 – Physics in Everyday Life MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. What is the SI unit of force?
(a) joule (b) newton (c) watt (d) pascal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Force = mass × acceleration, so its unit is kg m/s², named newton. Joule is the unit of energy, watt of power and pascal of pressure.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

2. A light year is a unit of:
(a) time (b) speed (c) distance (d) intensity of light
Answer: (c)
Solution: A light year is the distance light travels in one year, about 9.46 × 10^15 m. The word "year" is the trap that makes students choose time.
Source: PYQ-style

3. When a moving bus stops suddenly, the passengers fall forward. This is due to:
(a) inertia of motion (b) inertia of rest (c) friction (d) gravity
Answer: (a)
Solution: The passengers' bodies tend to keep moving forward even after the bus stops — inertia of motion (Newton's first law). Inertia of rest explains falling backward when a bus starts suddenly.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

4. The working of a rocket is based on:
(a) Newton's first law of motion (b) Newton's second law of motion (c) Newton's third law of motion (d) Newton's law of gravitation
Answer: (c)
Solution: The rocket pushes hot gases backward (action) and the gases push the rocket forward (reaction) — Newton's third law.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

5. Which mirror is used as a rear-view mirror in vehicles?
(a) Plane mirror (b) Concave mirror (c) Cylindrical mirror (d) Convex mirror
Answer: (d)
Solution: A convex mirror always gives an erect, diminished image and a wider field of view. A concave mirror is used for shaving and in headlights.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

6. Sound cannot travel through:
(a) water (b) vacuum (c) steel (d) air
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sound is a mechanical wave and needs a material medium; there are no particles in vacuum to carry it.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

7. Bats fly in the dark without hitting obstacles by using:
(a) ultrasound (b) infrasound (c) light waves (d) radio waves
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bats emit ultrasound (frequency above 20 kHz) and detect its echoes from obstacles. Infrasound is below 20 Hz.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

8. Which instrument measures atmospheric pressure?
(a) Hygrometer (b) Hydrometer (c) Barometer (d) Lactometer
Answer: (c)
Solution: A barometer measures atmospheric pressure. A hygrometer measures humidity, a hydrometer the relative density of liquids and a lactometer the purity of milk.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Short-sightedness (myopia) is corrected by using a:
(a) convex lens (b) concave lens (c) cylindrical lens (d) bifocal lens
Answer: (b)
Solution: In myopia the image of a distant object forms in front of the retina; a concave (diverging) lens corrects it. A convex lens is used for hypermetropia.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

10. The sky appears blue because of:
(a) reflection of light (b) refraction of light (c) dispersion of light (d) scattering of light
Answer: (d)
Solution: Air molecules scatter blue light (shorter wavelength) much more than red light, so the sky looks blue.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

11. A fuse wire should have:
(a) a low melting point (b) a very high melting point (c) zero resistance (d) magnetic properties
Answer: (a)
Solution: A fuse must melt quickly when excess current flows, so it is made of a wire with a low melting point.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

12. The "unit" of electricity on a household electricity bill is:
(a) kilowatt (b) watt (c) kilowatt-hour (d) volt
Answer: (c)
Solution: The bill charges for energy; one unit = 1 kilowatt-hour = 3.6 × 10^6 J. Kilowatt and watt are units of power.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

13. At what temperature does water have its maximum density?
(a) 0 °C (b) 4 °C (c) 100 °C (d) -4 °C
Answer: (b)
Solution: Because of anomalous expansion, water contracts from 0 °C to 4 °C and expands after that, so it is densest at 4 °C.
Source: PYQ-style

14. Food cooks faster in a pressure cooker because:
(a) the boiling point of water increases (b) the boiling point of water decreases (c) pressure produces extra heat (d) steam cools the food quickly
Answer: (a)
Solution: The high pressure inside the cooker raises the boiling point of water above 100 °C, so the food gets more heat and cooks faster.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics

15. A lactometer is used to test the:
(a) humidity of air (b) atmospheric pressure (c) speed of wind (d) purity of milk
Answer: (d)
Solution: A lactometer measures the density of milk and so helps check its purity (whether water has been added).
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

16. Normal human body temperature is 37 °C. What is it on the Fahrenheit scale?
(a) 37 °F (b) 96.8 °F (c) 98.6 °F (d) 100.4 °F
Answer: (c)
Solution: °F = (9/5) × °C + 32 = (9/5) × 37 + 32 = 66.6 + 32 = 98.6 °F.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science

17. In which of the following does sound travel the fastest?
(a) Air (b) Steel (c) Water (d) Vacuum
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sound is fastest in solids, slower in liquids and slowest in gases; in vacuum it cannot travel at all. Steel is a solid (about 5960 m/s).
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

18. An electric motor converts:
(a) electrical energy into mechanical energy (b) mechanical energy into electrical energy (c) chemical energy into electrical energy (d) light energy into electrical energy
Answer: (a)
Solution: A motor uses electricity to produce rotation. The reverse — mechanical to electrical — is done by a generator.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

19. Ice floats on water because:
(a) ice is a solid (b) ice is colder than water (c) ice is less dense than water (d) ice has more mass than water
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ice (about 0.92 g/cm³) is less dense than water (1 g/cm³), so the buoyant force can balance its weight with part of it above water.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

20. According to NCERT, the insulation colour of the earth wire in Indian household wiring is:
(a) red (b) black (c) blue (d) green
Answer: (d)
Solution: In Indian household wiring the live wire has red insulation, the neutral black and the earth wire green.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

21. A boy claps near a cliff and hears the echo after 3 s. If the speed of sound in air is 340 m/s, how far is the cliff from him?
(a) 340 m (b) 510 m (c) 1020 m (d) 170 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sound travels to the cliff and back, so distance = (speed × time) ÷ 2 = (340 × 3) ÷ 2 = 1020 ÷ 2 = 510 m.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

22. Astronauts inside a spacecraft orbiting the Earth feel weightless because:
(a) there is no gravity in space (b) they are in free fall along with the spacecraft (c) the Moon's gravity cancels the Earth's gravity (d) their mass becomes zero in orbit
Answer: (b)
Solution: Earth's gravity still acts in orbit; the astronauts and the spacecraft fall freely together, so nothing presses on anything. "No gravity in space" is the almost-true trap.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

23. A 100 W bulb is used for 5 hours every day. How much electrical energy does it use in 30 days?
(a) 1.5 kWh (b) 15 kWh (c) 150 kWh (d) 1500 kWh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Energy = power × time = 100 W × (5 × 30) h = 15000 Wh = 15 kWh.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

24. Burns caused by steam at 100 °C are more severe than those caused by boiling water at 100 °C because:
(a) steam is hotter than 100 °C (b) steam has a higher specific heat (c) steam gives out extra latent heat when it condenses (d) steam moves faster than water
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both are at 100 °C, but steam releases its latent heat of vaporisation (about 22.5 × 10^5 J/kg) on condensing on the skin. "Steam is hotter" is the tempting wrong option.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

25. A man has a mass of 60 kg on the Earth. On the Moon, where g is one-sixth of that on the Earth, his mass and weight will be (take g on Earth = 9.8 m/s²):
(a) 10 kg and 98 N (b) 60 kg and 98 N (c) 60 kg and 588 N (d) 10 kg and 588 N
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mass does not change, so it stays 60 kg. Weight on the Moon = m × g/6 = 60 × 9.8 ÷ 6 = 98 N. Option (a) is the trap that wrongly divides the mass by 6.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science
