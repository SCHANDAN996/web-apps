Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type
Light year | Distance travelled by light in one year (about 9.46 × 10^15 m) | Offered as a unit of time | Similar Name

Newton's First Law | Law of inertia; mass is the measure of inertia | Passenger falling forward explained by "third law" | Almost True Statement

Catching a ball | Hands pulled back → more time → less force (second law) | Explained as "less momentum" | Reverse Causation
Rolling friction | Rolling < sliding < static friction | "Static friction is the least" | Almost True Statement

Weightlessness in orbit | Due to free fall; gravity is present | "No gravity in space" | Reverse Causation
g at poles and equator | Slightly more at poles | Reversed (more at equator) | Almost True Statement

Floating of ship | Archimedes' principle; hollow shape displaces more water | "Iron is lighter than water" | Almost True Statement

Water densest at 4 °C | Anomalous expansion; fish survive under frozen lakes | Given as 0 °C | Almost True Statement
Steam burns | Latent heat of vaporisation (about 22.5 × 10^5 J/kg) | "Steam is hotter than 100 °C" | Almost True Statement

−40° | Same reading on Celsius and Fahrenheit | Given as −32° or 0° | Almost True Statement
Sound in vacuum | Cannot travel; needs a medium | "Sound is faster in vacuum" | Almost True Statement
Speed of sound | Solid > liquid > gas | Order reversed | Reverse Causation
Audible range | 20 Hz to 20 kHz | Ultrasound and infrasound swapped | Similar Name
Bats, dolphins | Use ultrasound | "Bats use infrasound" | Similar Name
Echo | Reflector at least about 17.2 m away | Given as 17.2 cm or 34.4 m | Almost True Statement
Pitch vs loudness | Pitch ← frequency; loudness ← amplitude | Swapped | Similar Name
Rear-view mirror | Convex mirror (wide field, erect diminished image) | Concave mirror offered | Similar Name
Shaving mirror, headlight reflector | Concave mirror | Convex mirror offered | Similar Name
Blue sky | Scattering of light (blue scattered most) | "Reflection from sea" | Reverse Causation
Red danger signal | Red is scattered least, seen from far | "Red has highest frequency" | Almost True Statement
Twinkling of stars | Atmospheric refraction | Attributed to reflection or scattering | Similar Name
Diamond sparkle, optical fibre, mirage | Total internal reflection | Attributed to refraction alone or dispersion | Almost True Statement
Rainbow | Dispersion + refraction + internal reflection; seen opposite the Sun | "Seen towards the Sun" | Almost True Statement
Myopia | Corrected by concave lens | Convex lens offered | Similar Name
Hypermetropia | Corrected by convex lens | Concave lens offered | Similar Name
Persistence of vision | About 1/16 s; basis of cinema | Given as 1/16 minute | Almost True Statement
Fuse | Low melting point; in series with live wire | "Connected in parallel" or "in neutral wire" | Almost True Statement
Household wiring | Appliances in parallel; live red, neutral black, earth green | "Appliances in series" | Almost True Statement
Electric motor vs generator | Motor: electrical → mechanical; generator: mechanical → electrical | Swapped | Reverse Causation
Transformer | Works only on AC | "Works on DC" | Almost True Statement
