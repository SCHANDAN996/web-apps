Set 3/6 – World Geography (Basic) MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 51–75: Medium

51. In December 2020, Nepal and China jointly announced the revised height of Mount Everest as:
(a) 8,848 m (b) 8,848.86 m (c) 8,611 m (d) 8,586 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: 8,848 m was the older accepted height; 8,611 m is K2 and 8,586 m is Kangchenjunga. The December 2020 joint announcement gave 8,848.86 m.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Which is the highest peak of Africa?
(a) Aconcagua (b) Elbrus (c) Mount Kenya (d) Kilimanjaro
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kilimanjaro (Tanzania), a volcanic mountain, is the highest peak of Africa. Aconcagua is in South America and Elbrus in Europe.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Mount Aconcagua is the highest peak of which continent?
(a) North America (b) Europe (c) Africa (d) South America
Answer: (d)
Solution: Aconcagua, in the Andes of Argentina, is the highest peak of South America. Denali is the highest in North America.
Source: PYQ-style

54. The Andes mountains of South America are:
(a) block mountains (b) residual mountains (c) young fold mountains (d) old fold mountains
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Andes, like the Himalayas, Alps and Rockies, are young fold mountains. The Appalachians and Aravallis are old fold mountains.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

55. The Ural Mountains form a natural divide between:
(a) Asia and Africa (b) North America and South America (c) Europe and Africa (d) Europe and Asia
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Urals separate Europe from Asia. North and South America are joined by the Isthmus of Panama.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

56. The Mariana Trench, the deepest known part of the oceans, lies in the:
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Pacific Ocean (d) Arctic Ocean
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Mariana Trench is in the western Pacific Ocean, the largest and deepest ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

57. Which is the smallest ocean?
(a) Arctic Ocean (b) Southern Ocean (c) Indian Ocean (d) Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Arctic Ocean, around the North Pole, is the smallest ocean. It is joined to the Pacific by the Bering Strait.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

58. The Bering Strait separates:
(a) Europe and Africa (b) Asia and North America (c) India and Sri Lanka (d) Asia and Europe
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Bering Strait lies between Russia (Asia) and Alaska (North America) and joins the Pacific with the Arctic Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

59. The Strait of Gibraltar connects the Atlantic Ocean with the:
(a) Mediterranean Sea (b) Red Sea (c) Black Sea (d) Caspian Sea
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Strait of Gibraltar, between Spain and Morocco, joins the Atlantic with the Mediterranean Sea.
Source: PYQ-style

60. The Strait of Hormuz connects the Persian Gulf with the:
(a) Red Sea (b) Gulf of Aden (c) Mediterranean Sea (d) Gulf of Oman
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Strait of Hormuz joins the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman. The Red Sea is joined to the Gulf of Aden by the Bab-el-Mandeb strait.
Source: PYQ-style

61. Which line forms a large part of the boundary between the USA and Canada?
(a) 38th Parallel (b) 17th Parallel (c) 49th Parallel (d) Durand Line
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 49th Parallel forms much of the USA–Canada boundary. The 38th Parallel divides North and South Korea.
Source: PYQ-style

62. The 38th Parallel is associated with the boundary between:
(a) North Korea and South Korea (b) USA and Canada (c) India and China (d) Germany and Poland
Answer: (a)
Solution: The 38th Parallel is linked with the division of North and South Korea. The 49th Parallel is the USA–Canada line.
Source: PYQ-style

63. The McMahon Line is the boundary line between:
(a) India and China (b) India and Pakistan (c) Pakistan and Afghanistan (d) India and Nepal
Answer: (a)
Solution: The McMahon Line is the India–China boundary in the east. The Durand Line (Pakistan–Afghanistan) is the similar-name trap.
Source: PYQ-style

64. Which country is known as the "Land of the Midnight Sun"?
(a) Finland (b) Japan (c) Norway (d) Bhutan
Answer: (c)
Solution: In northern Norway, beyond the Arctic Circle, the Sun can be seen even at midnight in summer. Finland is the Land of a Thousand Lakes.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Which country is called the "Land of a Thousand Lakes"?
(a) Norway (b) Finland (c) Canada (d) Ireland
Answer: (b)
Solution: Finland is called the Land of a Thousand Lakes. Norway is the Land of the Midnight Sun and Ireland the Emerald Isle.
Source: PYQ-style

66. Which country is known as the "Land of the Thunder Dragon"?
(a) Bhutan (b) Nepal (c) China (d) Myanmar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bhutan is called the Land of the Thunder Dragon (Druk Yul). Nepal is a common wrong choice.
Source: PYQ-style

67. Which country is known as the "Sugar Bowl of the World"?
(a) Brazil (b) India (c) Cuba (d) Jamaica
Answer: (c)
Solution: Cuba is traditionally called the Sugar Bowl of the World because of its sugarcane production.
Source: PYQ-style

68. The temperate grasslands called "Velds" are found in:
(a) South Africa (b) Australia (c) Argentina (d) Russia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Velds are the temperate grasslands of South Africa. Australia has the Downs and Argentina the Pampas.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

69. The temperate grasslands called "Steppes" are found in:
(a) North America (b) Eurasia (c) Australia (d) South America
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Steppes stretch across Eurasia, especially Central Asia. North America has the Prairies.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

70. The Atacama Desert, one of the driest places on Earth, lies mainly in:
(a) Peru (b) Argentina (c) Chile (d) Mexico
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Atacama Desert lies mainly in northern Chile, on the west coast of South America.
Source: PYQ-style

71. The Kalahari Desert is located in:
(a) northern Africa (b) southern Africa (c) Australia (d) Central Asia
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Kalahari is in southern Africa (mainly Botswana). The Sahara is the desert of northern Africa.
Source: PYQ-style

72. The Gobi Desert, a cold desert, lies in:
(a) Mongolia and China (b) India and Pakistan (c) Chile and Peru (d) Egypt and Libya
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Gobi is a cold desert spread over Mongolia and northern China. The Thar lies in India and Pakistan.
Source: PYQ-style

73. London is situated on the bank of which river?
(a) Seine (b) Thames (c) Danube (d) Tiber
Answer: (b)
Solution: London stands on the Thames. Paris is on the Seine, Vienna on the Danube and Rome on the Tiber.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Paris is situated on the bank of which river?
(a) Rhine (b) Danube (c) Seine (d) Thames
Answer: (c)
Solution: Paris stands on the Seine. The Thames flows through London, a common swap in matching questions.
Source: PYQ-style

75. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Lake Baikal – deepest lake (b) Caspian Sea – largest lake (c) Lake Superior – largest freshwater lake by area (d) Dead Sea – largest freshwater lake
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Dead Sea is extremely salty, not a freshwater lake; its shore is the lowest land point on Earth. The other three pairs are correct.
Source: PYQ-style
