Set 1/6 – World Geography (Basic) MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which is the largest planet of the solar system?
(a) Mercury (b) Venus (c) Jupiter (d) Saturn
Answer: (c)
Solution: Jupiter is the largest planet. Saturn is famous for its rings but is smaller than Jupiter; Mercury is the smallest.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

2. Which planet is known as the "Red Planet"?
(a) Venus (b) Mars (c) Jupiter (d) Mercury
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mars looks reddish because of iron oxide on its surface, so it is called the Red Planet.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

3. Which planet is nearest to the Sun?
(a) Mercury (b) Venus (c) Earth (d) Mars
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mercury is the nearest planet to the Sun. Venus is the second planet and the hottest, which is a common confusion.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

4. Since the 2006 decision of the International Astronomical Union, how many planets does the solar system have?
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 10
Answer: (b)
Solution: Older books said 9 because they counted Pluto. In 2006 Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet, so the number is now 8.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

5. The Earth is called the "Blue Planet" because:
(a) its atmosphere is completely blue (b) about 71% of its surface is covered by water (c) its rocks are blue in colour (d) it reflects blue light from the Moon
Answer: (b)
Solution: About 71% of the Earth's surface is water, so it looks blue from space. The other options are not true.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

6. The Tropic of Cancer lies at which latitude?
(a) 66½°N (b) 23½°S (c) 23½°N (d) 0°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Tropic of Cancer is at 23½°N. 23½°S is the Tropic of Capricorn, 66½°N is the Arctic Circle and 0° is the Equator.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

7. The Prime Meridian (0° longitude) passes through:
(a) Paris (b) Greenwich (c) New York (d) Tokyo
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Prime Meridian passes through the Royal Observatory at Greenwich, near London.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

8. The Earth completes one rotation on its axis in about:
(a) 365 days (b) 30 days (c) 12 hours (d) 24 hours
Answer: (d)
Solution: About 365 days is the time for one revolution around the Sun, not one rotation. One rotation, which causes day and night, takes about 24 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

9. Which is the largest ocean in the world?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Arctic Ocean (d) Pacific Ocean
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean. The Atlantic is second, and the Arctic is the smallest.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

10. Which is the smallest continent?
(a) Europe (b) Antarctica (c) Australia (d) South America
Answer: (c)
Solution: Australia is the smallest continent and is called the island continent. Europe is the second smallest.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

11. Which is the largest continent?
(a) Asia (b) Africa (c) North America (d) Europe
Answer: (a)
Solution: Asia is the largest continent, covering about one-third of the Earth's land area. Africa is second.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

12. Which is the longest river in the world?
(a) Amazon (b) Nile (c) Mississippi (d) Yangtze
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Nile is the longest river. The Amazon is the largest by volume of water — a common trap.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

13. In which layer of the atmosphere do almost all weather phenomena such as rain and storms take place?
(a) Stratosphere (b) Mesosphere (c) Troposphere (d) Exosphere
Answer: (c)
Solution: The troposphere, the lowest layer, contains almost all clouds, rain and storms. The stratosphere above it is nearly free of clouds.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

14. Which gas forms the largest part of the air?
(a) Oxygen (b) Carbon dioxide (c) Argon (d) Nitrogen
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nitrogen makes up about 78% of the air; oxygen is about 21%.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

15. What is the innermost layer of the Earth called?
(a) Crust (b) Mantle (c) Core (d) Lithosphere
Answer: (c)
Solution: The core is the innermost layer, made mainly of nickel and iron (NIFE). The crust is the outermost layer.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

16. The Sahara Desert is located in which continent?
(a) Asia (b) Africa (c) Australia (d) South America
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Sahara lies in northern Africa and is the largest hot desert in the world.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

17. The temperate grasslands called "Prairies" are found in:
(a) North America (b) South America (c) Africa (d) Australia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Prairies are the temperate grasslands of North America. South America has the Pampas, Africa the Velds and Australia the Downs.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

18. Which country is known as the "Land of the Rising Sun"?
(a) China (b) South Korea (c) Japan (d) Thailand
Answer: (c)
Solution: Japan is called the Land of the Rising Sun because it lies at the eastern edge of Asia. Thailand is the Land of White Elephants.
Source: PYQ-style

19. The Suez Canal is located in which country?
(a) Egypt (b) Panama (c) Turkey (d) Israel
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Suez Canal is in Egypt and joins the Mediterranean Sea with the Red Sea. The Panama Canal is in Panama.
Source: PYQ-style

20. Which is the only ocean named after a country?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Pacific Ocean (d) Arctic Ocean
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Indian Ocean is named after India; it is the only ocean named after a country.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

21. Which is the hottest planet of the solar system?
(a) Mercury (b) Mars (c) Venus (d) Jupiter
Answer: (c)
Solution: Venus is the hottest because its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere traps heat. Mercury is nearest to the Sun but is not the hottest — an almost-true trap.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

22. On 21 June, the Sun's rays fall vertically on:
(a) the Equator (b) the Tropic of Capricorn (c) the Arctic Circle (d) the Tropic of Cancer
Answer: (d)
Solution: On 21 June (summer solstice) the Sun is overhead on the Tropic of Cancer. On 22 December it is overhead on the Tropic of Capricorn.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

23. Indian Standard Time is calculated from which longitude?
(a) 75°E (b) 82°30'E (c) 88°30'E (d) 90°E
Answer: (b)
Solution: IST is taken from 82°30'E, which passes near Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh; it makes IST 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of GMT.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India I)

24. Which continent is crossed by the Equator, the Tropic of Cancer and the Tropic of Capricorn?
(a) South America (b) Australia (c) Asia (d) Africa
Answer: (d)
Solution: Africa is crossed by all three lines. South America is crossed by the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn only, and Asia by the Equator and the Tropic of Cancer only.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

25. Consider the following statements:
Statement I: The Pacific is the largest ocean.
Statement II: The Mariana Trench lies in the Atlantic Ocean.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement I is correct. The Mariana Trench, the deepest known point on Earth, lies in the Pacific, not the Atlantic, so Statement II is wrong.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)
