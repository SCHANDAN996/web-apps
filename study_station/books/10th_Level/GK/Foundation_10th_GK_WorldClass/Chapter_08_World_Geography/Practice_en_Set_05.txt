Set 5/6 – World Geography (Basic) MCQs (Foundation)

101. In which direction does the Earth rotate on its axis?
(a) East to west (b) North to south (c) West to east (d) South to north
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Earth rotates from west to east. That is why the Sun appears to rise in the east and set in the west.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

102. Day and night on the Earth are caused by:
(a) revolution of the Earth (b) rotation of the Earth (c) tilt of the axis alone (d) revolution of the Moon
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rotation brings each place into sunlight and then into darkness, causing day and night. Revolution with the tilt causes seasons — a reverse-causation trap.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

103. We always see the same face of the Moon because:
(a) its rotation and revolution take the same time (b) the Moon does not rotate at all (c) the Earth blocks the other side (d) the Sun lights only one side
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Moon takes about 27 days both to rotate once and to go round the Earth once, so the same side always faces us. The Moon does rotate.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

104. Sunlight takes about how long to reach the Earth?
(a) 8 seconds (b) 8 hours (c) 1 second (d) 8 minutes
Answer: (d)
Solution: Light from the Sun travels about 150 million km and reaches the Earth in about 8 minutes.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

105. In which year was Pluto reclassified as a dwarf planet by the International Astronomical Union?
(a) 2000 (b) 2004 (c) 2006 (d) 2010
Answer: (c)
Solution: The IAU decided on the new definition of a planet and reclassified Pluto as a dwarf planet in 2006.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

106. Venus is often called:
(a) the Red Planet (b) the Blue Planet (c) the Ringed Planet (d) Earth's twin
Answer: (d)
Solution: Venus is called Earth's twin because its size is close to that of the Earth. Mars is the Red Planet and Earth the Blue Planet.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

107. The Antarctic Circle lies at:
(a) 23½°S (b) 66½°S (c) 66½°N (d) 90°S
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Antarctic Circle is at 66½°S. 23½°S is the Tropic of Capricorn and 90°S is the South Pole.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

108. The Torrid Zone, which receives the overhead Sun at least once a year, lies between:
(a) the Arctic Circle and the North Pole (b) the Tropic of Cancer and the Arctic Circle (c) the Tropic of Cancer and the Tropic of Capricorn (d) the Antarctic Circle and the South Pole
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Torrid Zone lies between the two Tropics. The zones between the Tropics and the Polar Circles are temperate; beyond the Polar Circles are frigid.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

109. According to NCERT, the crust under the ocean floor is about how thick?
(a) 5 km (b) 35 km (c) 100 km (d) 2,900 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The crust is about 35 km thick under continents but only about 5 km under the ocean floor; 2,900 km is the depth of the mantle. The answer is about 5 km.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

110. The oceanic crust, made mainly of silica and magnesium, is called:
(a) SIAL (b) NIFE (c) Mantle (d) SIMA
Answer: (d)
Solution: SIMA = silica + magnesium (oceanic crust). SIAL = silica + alumina (continental crust), and NIFE is the core.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

111. Slate is formed by the metamorphism of:
(a) limestone (b) sandstone (c) granite (d) shale
Answer: (d)
Solution: Shale (clay) changes into slate under heat and pressure. Limestone becomes marble and sandstone becomes quartzite.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

112. Fossils are mostly found in which type of rocks?
(a) Igneous (b) Metamorphic (c) Sedimentary (d) Volcanic
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sedimentary rocks form from layers of deposited material that can preserve remains of plants and animals. Molten magma in igneous rocks destroys such remains.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

113. Basalt, formed when lava cools quickly on the Earth's surface, is:
(a) an extrusive igneous rock (b) an intrusive igneous rock (c) a sedimentary rock (d) a metamorphic rock
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lava cooling on the surface forms extrusive igneous rocks such as basalt. Granite, which cools slowly inside the Earth, is intrusive.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

114. Mount Kilimanjaro is located in:
(a) Kenya (b) Tanzania (c) Uganda (d) Ethiopia

Solution: Kilimanjaro is in Tanzania, close to the Kenya border — which makes Kenya the tempting wrong option.

115. The Black Forest, a block mountain, is located in:
(a) France (b) Switzerland (c) Austria (d) Germany

Solution: The Black Forest is in Germany. The Vosges, the other block mountain beside the Rhine rift valley, is in France.

116. Which is the largest freshwater lake in the world by surface area?
(a) Caspian Sea (b) Lake Baikal (c) Lake Superior (d) Lake Victoria
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lake Superior in North America is the largest freshwater lake by area. The Caspian Sea is larger but salty, and Baikal is the deepest.

117. The lowest point of land on the Earth's surface lies on the shore of the:
(a) Dead Sea (b) Caspian Sea (c) Lake Baikal (d) Lake Titicaca
Answer: (a)
Solution: The shore of the Dead Sea, between Jordan and Israel, is the lowest land point on Earth. Lake Baikal is the deepest lake, which is a different record.
Source: PYQ-style

118. Which river crosses the Equator twice?
(a) Nile (b) Amazon (c) Congo (d) Niger
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Congo (Zaire) River crosses the Equator twice in its course. The Amazon flows close to the Equator but does not cross it twice.
Source: PYQ-style

119. Which river crosses the Tropic of Capricorn twice?

Solution: The Limpopo in southern Africa flows in a large arc and crosses the Tropic of Capricorn twice.

120. Which country is the largest archipelago (group of islands) in the world?
(a) Philippines (b) Japan (c) Maldives (d) Indonesia
Answer: (d)
Solution: Indonesia, made up of thousands of islands, is the largest archipelagic country. Greenland is the largest single island, a different record.
Source: PYQ-style

121. Place A is on 75°E and place B is on 15°W. When it is 6:00 p.m. at A, what is the local time at B?
(a) 12:00 noon (b) 12:00 midnight (c) 6:00 a.m. (d) 2:00 p.m.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Longitude difference = 75 + 15 = 90°, so time difference = 90 × 4 = 360 minutes = 6 hours. B is west of A, so B is behind: 6:00 p.m. − 6 hours = 12:00 noon.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

122. Consider the following statements:
Statement I: All meridians of longitude are of equal length.
Statement II: All parallels of latitude are of equal length.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(a) Both I and II (b) Neither I nor II (c) Only I (d) Only II
Answer: (c)
Solution: Every meridian is a half-circle from pole to pole, so all are equal. Parallels become smaller towards the poles, the Equator being the longest, so Statement II is wrong.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

123. A ship crosses the International Date Line travelling eastward, from the Asian side to the American side. Its calendar date will:
(a) move one day forward (b) go back by one day (c) remain the same (d) move two days forward
Answer: (b)
Solution: Crossing the Date Line eastward (Asia to America) sets the date back by one day, so the day is repeated. Going westward moves it one day forward — the reversed-direction trap.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

124. Match List I (grassland) with List II (region):
List I: P. Pampas, Q. Velds, R. Downs, S. Steppes
List II: 1. Australia, 2. Eurasia, 3. Argentina, 4. South Africa
Choose the correct answer:
(a) P-3, Q-4, R-1, S-2 (b) P-4, Q-3, R-2, S-1 (c) P-3, Q-1, R-4, S-2 (d) P-1, Q-4, R-3, S-2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pampas – Argentina, Velds – South Africa, Downs – Australia, Steppes – Eurasia. So the matching is P-3, Q-4, R-1, S-2.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

125. When it is 4:00 p.m. at Greenwich (0°), what is the local time at a place on 60°W longitude?
(a) 8:00 p.m. (b) 12:00 noon (c) 12:00 midnight (d) 4:00 a.m.
Answer: (b)
Solution: 60 × 4 = 240 minutes = 4 hours. The place is west of Greenwich, so it is behind: 4:00 p.m. − 4 hours = 12:00 noon. Adding instead of subtracting gives 8:00 p.m., the trap.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)
