🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Mnemonic 1: Planets in Order from the Sun
"My Very Efficient Mother Just Served Us Nuts"
M → Mercury, V → Venus, E → Earth, M → Mars, J → Jupiter, S → Saturn, U → Uranus, N → Neptune
Picture: Mother serves only 8 bowls of nuts. Pluto came late in 2006 and found no bowl — so it became a "dwarf" guest.

🧠 Mnemonic 2: Hottest vs Nearest
"Mercury is the Messenger, Venus is the Volcano-oven."
Mercury runs nearest to the Sun like a messenger; Venus is the oven that locks in heat with carbon dioxide.
Rule: Near ≠ Hot.

🧠 Mnemonic 3: Solstices
"June — Jump up north (Cancer); December — Dive down south (Capricorn)."
Picture: In June the Sun jumps onto a crab (Cancer) in the north; in December it dives onto a goat (Capricorn) in the south.

🧠 Mnemonic 4: Perihelion and Aphelion
"January Hug, July Goodbye."
3 January — Earth hugs the Sun (nearest). 4 July — Earth waves goodbye (farthest).
And still India is cold during the hug — so "Tilt makes the season, distance is no reason."

🧠 Mnemonic 5: Time and Longitude
"15 per hour, 4 per degree — East is Early, West is Late."
Picture: A rooster in the east crows early; a sleepy owl in the west wakes late.

🧠 Mnemonic 6: International Date Line
"East — repeat a day; West — skip a day."
Flying from Tokyo to Los Angeles (east), you may land "before" you took off by the calendar.

🧠 Mnemonic 7: IST Meridian
"Eighty-two and a half — Mirzapur's half-past."
82°30'E passes near Mirzapur and gives IST = GMT + 5:30 (the "half-past").

🧠 Mnemonic 8: Earth's Interior
"SIAL Sits on land, SIMA Swims under sea, NIFE is the Nickel-Iron heart."
Crust on continents = SIAL; ocean crust = SIMA; core = NIFE.

🧠 Mnemonic 9: Metamorphic Rocks
"Lime Marries Marble, Shale Sleeps as Slate, Sand Quarrels into Quartzite."
Limestone → marble, shale → slate, sandstone → quartzite.

🧠 Mnemonic 10: Continents by Size
"All Animals Need Space And Easy Air"
Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia.

🧠 Mnemonic 11: Oceans by Size
"Please Always Include South Arctic"
Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic.

🧠 Mnemonic 12: Layers of the Atmosphere
"The Strong Man Throws Eggs"
Troposphere (weather), Stratosphere (ozone, aeroplanes), Mesosphere (meteors burn), Thermosphere (ionosphere, radio waves), Exosphere.
Outrageous visual: A strong man throws eggs upward — the eggs fry in the meteor fire of the Mesosphere.

🧠 Mnemonic 13: Temperate Grasslands
"PaPa SteVe Down in Canterbury"
Prairies (North America), Pampas (Argentina), Steppes (Eurasia), Velds (South Africa), Downs (Australia), Canterbury (New Zealand).

🧠 Mnemonic 14: Warm vs Cold Currents
"Gulf Stream is a Geyser, Labrador is a Lab fridge."
Gulf Stream = warm; Labrador = cold. Where the geyser meets the fridge (Newfoundland), fish party — the Grand Banks.

🧠 Mnemonic 15: Boundary Lines
"Durand Divides Afghans, McMahon Meets China, Radcliffe Runs through Punjab."
Durand = Pakistan–Afghanistan; McMahon = India–China; Radcliffe = India–Pakistan.

⏭️ Skip Strategy

Tip 1: If a question gives two places' longitudes and asks the exact local time, and you cannot finish the degrees × 4 sum in about 45 seconds, mark it for review and move on. Come back only after all fact questions are done.

Tip 2: If a matching question has four pairs (rivers–cities, lines–countries) and you are sure of only one pair, check whether that one pair alone eliminates three options. If it does not, skip — guessing among two or more long combinations wastes time.

Tip 3: If a question asks for an exact record figure that you never studied (for example, a precise depth or height to the metre) and no option can be eliminated, skip it immediately. Spend that time on the records you know — longest, largest, deepest.
