Set 2/6 – General Chemistry MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 26–50: Medium

26. In his alpha-particle scattering experiment, Rutherford bombarded a thin foil of which metal?
(a) Silver (b) Aluminium (c) Copper (d) Gold
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rutherford used a very thin gold foil. Most alpha particles passed straight through, showing that most of the atom is empty space with a tiny, dense, positive nucleus.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

27. What is the maximum number of electrons that the M shell (n = 3) can hold?
(a) 2 (b) 8 (c) 18 (d) 32
Answer: (c)
Solution: The maximum number of electrons in a shell is 2n². For n = 3 this gives 2 × 9 = 18.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

28. What is the electronic configuration of sodium (atomic number 11)?
(a) 2, 8, 1 (b) 2, 9 (c) 2, 8, 2 (d) 2, 7, 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: K shell takes 2, L shell takes 8 and the remaining one electron goes to the M shell. An L shell cannot hold 9, so option (b) is wrong.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

29. Chlorine has the electronic configuration 2, 8, 7. What is its valency?
(a) 7 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Chlorine needs just one more electron to complete its octet, so its valency is 1. Writing the number of outer electrons as the valency is the trap.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

30. Which of the following pairs are isobars?
(a) Protium and deuterium (b) Carbon-12 and carbon-14 (c) Argon-40 and calcium-40 (d) Chlorine-35 and chlorine-37
Answer: (c)
Solution: Isobars are atoms of different elements with the same mass number. Argon (atomic number 18) and calcium (atomic number 20) both have mass number 40. The other three pairs are isotopes.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

31. Who discovered canal rays, which led to the discovery of the proton?
(a) E. Goldstein (b) J.J. Thomson (c) James Chadwick (d) Niels Bohr
Answer: (a)
Solution: In 1886 E. Goldstein discovered canal rays, positively charged radiations that led to the discovery of the proton. Thomson discovered the electron.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

32. Thomson's model of the atom is popularly compared to:
(a) The solar system (b) A Christmas pudding or watermelon (c) Fixed circular orbits (d) A hollow ball
Answer: (b)
Solution: Thomson described the atom as a positive sphere with electrons embedded in it, like dry fruits in a Christmas pudding or seeds in a watermelon. The solar-system picture belongs to Rutherford's nuclear model.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

33. An atom of sodium has atomic number 11 and mass number 23. How many neutrons does it have?
(a) 11 (b) 23 (c) 12 (d) 34
Answer: (c)
Solution: Neutrons = mass number − atomic number = 23 − 11 = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

34. The Modern Periodic Table arranges elements in order of increasing:
(a) Atomic mass (b) Number of neutrons (c) Density (d) Atomic number
Answer: (d)
Solution: Henry Moseley (1913) showed that atomic number is the more fundamental property. Arrangement by atomic mass was Mendeleev's basis — the almost-correct option.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Periodic Classification of Elements

35. How many groups and periods does the Modern Periodic Table have?
(a) 8 groups and 7 periods (b) 18 groups and 7 periods (c) 7 groups and 18 periods (d) 18 groups and 9 periods
Answer: (b)
Solution: There are 18 vertical columns called groups and 7 horizontal rows called periods. Option (c) swaps the two.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Periodic Classification of Elements

36. Which of the following is a Döbereiner triad?
(a) Li, Na, K (b) Na, Mg, Al (c) H, He, Li (d) C, N, O
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lithium, sodium and potassium have similar properties and the atomic mass of sodium is roughly the average of the other two. Na, Mg, Al are neighbours in a period, not a triad.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Periodic Classification of Elements

37. Newlands' Law of Octaves worked well only for elements up to:
(a) Oxygen (b) Iron (c) Calcium (d) Uranium
Answer: (c)
Solution: Newlands noticed that every eighth element resembled the first, like notes of music, but the pattern held only up to calcium.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Periodic Classification of Elements

38. Mendeleev predicted an element he called eka-aluminium. Which element was it found to be?
(a) Scandium (b) Germanium (c) Boron (d) Gallium
Answer: (d)
Solution: Eka-aluminium became gallium. Eka-boron became scandium and eka-silicon became germanium — the similar-name trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Periodic Classification of Elements

39. Moving from left to right across a period, the atomic radius generally:
(a) Decreases (b) Increases (c) Stays the same (d) First increases then decreases
Answer: (a)
Solution: Across a period the nuclear charge increases and pulls the electrons closer, so the atomic size decreases. Down a group it increases because new shells are added.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Periodic Classification of Elements

40. The element with atomic number 12 belongs to which group?
(a) Group 1 (b) Group 2 (c) Group 12 (d) Group 13
Answer: (b)
Solution: Magnesium has the configuration 2, 8, 2 — two valence electrons, so it is in group 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Periodic Classification of Elements

41. In the Modern Periodic Table, noble gases such as helium, neon and argon are placed in:
(a) Group 1 (b) Group 2 (c) Group 17 (d) Group 18
Answer: (d)
Solution: Noble gases have completely filled outer shells and are very unreactive; they are placed in group 18.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science, Periodic Classification of Elements

42. One atomic mass unit (u) is defined as one-twelfth of the mass of one atom of:
(a) Hydrogen-1 (b) Oxygen-16 (c) Carbon-12 (d) Helium-4
Answer: (c)
Solution: IUPAC uses the carbon isotope as the standard: 1 u is one-twelfth of the mass of one carbon-12 atom.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 3 Atoms and Molecules

43. An isotope of which element is used in the treatment of goitre?
(a) Cobalt (b) Uranium (c) Iodine (d) Carbon
Answer: (c)
Solution: An isotope of iodine is used to treat goitre. An isotope of cobalt is used in cancer treatment and an isotope of uranium as nuclear fuel.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

44. The atomic mass of chlorine is taken as 35.5 u because:
(a) A chlorine atom contains half a neutron (b) Natural chlorine is a mixture of chlorine-35 and chlorine-37 in about 3 : 1 ratio (c) Electrons add 0.5 u to the mass (d) It is a measuring error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The value 35.5 u is the average mass of the naturally occurring mixture of isotopes, not the mass of any single atom.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

45. Compared with a proton, the mass of an electron is about:
(a) Equal (b) Half (c) 1/2000 (d) 2000 times more
Answer: (c)
Solution: An electron's mass is about 1/2000 of a proton's mass, so it is usually treated as negligible. Option (d) reverses the relation.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

46. A temperature of 25 °C equals how many kelvin?
(a) 248 K (b) 298 K (c) 300 K (d) 273 K
Answer: (b)
Solution: Temperature in kelvin = temperature in °C + 273, so 25 + 273 = 298 K.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 1 Matter in Our Surroundings

47. The glow of fluorescent tubes and of stars is due to which state of matter?
(a) Bose–Einstein condensate (b) Plasma (c) Colloid (d) Liquid
Answer: (b)
Solution: Plasma is a state of super-energetic, ionised gas particles. A Bose–Einstein condensate forms only at extremely low temperatures.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 1 Matter in Our Surroundings

48. Which of the following DECREASES the rate of evaporation?
(a) Increase in surface area (b) Increase in temperature (c) Increase in wind speed (d) Increase in humidity
Answer: (d)
Solution: When humidity is high, the air already holds much water vapour, so evaporation slows down. The other three increase evaporation — read the capitalised word carefully.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 1 Matter in Our Surroundings

49. An element has the electronic configuration 2, 8, 6. What is its atomic number?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total electrons = 2 + 8 + 6, and in a neutral atom electrons equal protons, so the atomic number is 16 (sulphur).
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

50. An element has the electronic configuration 2, 8, 3. What is its valency?
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 8 (d) 13
Answer: (a)
Solution: The element (aluminium) easily loses its outer electrons to reach a stable octet, so its valency is 3.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science, Ch 4 Structure of the Atom
