1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Mercury – liquid metal	Only metal that is liquid at room temperature.	Offering gallium or caesium, which only melt on the palm.	Almost Correct Statement
Bromine – liquid non-metal	Only non-metal that is liquid at room temperature.	Offering mercury, which is a liquid but a metal.	Similar Name
Graphite – conducts electricity	Allotrope of carbon; a non-metal that conducts electricity.	"No non-metal conducts electricity." (False)	Negative Phrasing
Iodine – lustrous non-metal	Lustre is normally a metallic property; iodine is the exception.	Asking for a lustrous metal-like substance and listing iodine as a metal.	Almost Correct Statement
Isotopes vs isobars	Isotopes: same atomic number (C-12, C-14). Isobars: same mass number (Ar-40, Ca-40).	Calling Ar-40 and Ca-40 isotopes.	Similar Name
Discoverers	Electron – J.J. Thomson; canal rays – Goldstein; nucleus – Rutherford (gold foil); neutron – Chadwick (1932).	Giving the electron to Rutherford or the neutron to Thomson.	Similar Name
Modern Periodic Table	Based on atomic number (Moseley, 1913); 18 groups, 7 periods.	"Based on atomic mass" – that was Mendeleev.	Chronological Confusion
Mendeleev's predictions	Eka-boron = scandium; eka-aluminium = gallium; eka-silicon = germanium.	Swapping gallium and germanium.	Similar Name
Oxidation and reduction	Gain of oxygen (or loss of hydrogen) = oxidation; loss of oxygen = reduction.	In CuO + H2 → Cu + H2O, saying CuO is oxidised.	Reverse Causation
Electrolysis of water	Hydrogen : oxygen = 2 : 1 by volume.	Giving 1 : 8, which is the ratio by mass.	Almost Correct Statement
Natural acids	Tamarind – tartaric; tomato – oxalic; curd – lactic; vinegar – acetic; ant sting – methanoic.	Swapping tartaric (tamarind) and oxalic (tomato).	Similar Name
Indicators	Phenolphthalein: colourless in acid, pink in base. Methyl orange: red in acid, yellow in base.	Giving "yellow" for phenolphthalein in base.	Similar Name
Gypsum vs Plaster of Paris	Gypsum CaSO4·2H2O; PoP CaSO4·½H2O (gypsum heated at 373 K).	Writing CaSO4·2H2O as Plaster of Paris.	Almost Correct Statement
Bleaching powder	CaOCl2; made by chlorine on DRY SLAKED lime.	"Chlorine on quicklime."	Almost Correct Statement
Alloys	Brass Cu+Zn; bronze Cu+Sn; solder Pb+Sn; stainless steel Fe+Ni+Cr.	Brass ↔ bronze swap.	Similar Name
Aqua regia	Conc. HCl : conc. HNO3 = 3 : 1; dissolves gold.	Giving 1 : 3.	Reverse Causation
Reactivity series	K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > H > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au.	"Copper gives hydrogen with dilute HCl." (False – Cu is below H)	Almost Correct Statement
Flames of hydrocarbons	Saturated – clean blue flame; unsaturated – yellow sooty flame.	Reversing the two.	Reverse Causation
CNG vs LPG	CNG mainly methane; LPG mainly butane.	Answering butane for CNG.	Similar Name
2. Time Management (15/45-Sec Rule)
Fact / Topic	Type (15s Fact / 45s Analytical)	Score Maximizer?	Reason
Common names and formulas of salts	15s (Fact)	Yes	Direct one-to-one recall once the list is learnt.
Natural acids in fruits and stings	15s (Fact)	Yes	Asked very often; one table covers all.
Discoverers of electron, proton, neutron	15s (Fact)	Yes	Fixed pairs; no calculation.
Liquid metal / liquid non-metal / exceptions	15s (Fact)	Yes	Short exception list answers many questions.
Neutrons, protons, electrons from Z and A	45s (Analytical)	Yes	Simple subtraction, but check whether the particle is an ion.
Molecular mass calculation	45s (Analytical)	Yes	Add atomic masses carefully; multiply by subscripts.
Position of element from atomic number	45s (Analytical)	Yes	Write configuration, then shells = period, valence electrons = group.
Oxidised vs reduced in an equation	45s (Analytical)	Yes	Track who gains and who loses oxygen.
Statement-based questions (two or three statements)	45s (Analytical)	Moderate	Look for absolute words like "all", "only", "never".
Balancing equations	45s (Analytical)	Moderate	Balance oxygen and the metal first; skip if it takes longer.
