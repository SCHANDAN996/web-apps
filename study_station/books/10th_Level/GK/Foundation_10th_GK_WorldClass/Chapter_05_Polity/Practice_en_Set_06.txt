126. Which of the following statements regarding the nature of the Indian federal system is correct?
(a) It is a classical federation formed by agreement among sovereign states (b) It is described as a 'quasi-federation' with a strong unitary bias (c) States have the constitutional right to secede from the Union (d) There is dual citizenship for national and state levels
Answer: (b)
Solution: Prof. K.C. Wheare described the Indian Constitution as quasi-federal due to its combination of federal features with a strong central authority.
