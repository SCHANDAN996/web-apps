26. Which of the following statements regarding the Constituent Assembly of India is correct?
(a) The total membership of the Assembly after partition was 299 (b) All members were directly elected by universal adult franchise (c) Mahatma Gandhi was the President of the Drafting Committee (d) The Constitution was finally adopted on 15 August 1947
Answer: (a)
Solution: Following the partition of India in 1947, the membership of the Constituent Assembly was reduced from 389 to 299.

(a) The State treats all religions with equal respect and neutrality (b) The State promotes the majority religion (c) The State is completely anti-religious (d) Only minority religions are recognized
