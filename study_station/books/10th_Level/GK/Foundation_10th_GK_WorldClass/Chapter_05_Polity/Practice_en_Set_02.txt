Q50. In case of a conflict between the Union and States on a matter enumerated in the Concurrent List, which law prevails?
(a) The State law prevails.
(b) The Union law prevails.
(c) Both laws are void.
(d) The matter is referred to the Supreme Court.
Answer: (b) The Union law prevails.
Solution: Article 254 provides that in case of repugnancy between a law made by Parliament and a law made by the State Legislature on a Concurrent subject, the Parliamentary law shall prevail, and the State law to the extent of repugnancy shall be void. There is an exception if State law receives Presidential assent. But generally, Union law prevails.
Source: UPSC Prelims 2017
