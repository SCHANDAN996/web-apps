Top 10 High-Yield PYQs with Solutions & Trap Analysis

Q1 (UPSC 2020): Who among the following introduced the 'Dagh' system?
(a) Akbar
(b) Alauddin Khilji
(c) Sher Shah Suri
(d) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (b) Alauddin Khilji
Trap Type: Similar Names / Chronological Confusion – candidates often confuse it with Akbar's Mansabdari or Sher Shah's Chehra.
15/45-sec: Factual, solve in 15 sec.

Q2 (SSC CGL 2022): Din-i-Ilahi was founded in which year?
(a) 1575
(b) 1582
(c) 1585
(d) 1556
Answer: (b) 1582
Trap Type: Almost Correct Statement – 1575 is the year of Ibadat Khana, close to 1582.
15/45-sec: Factual, 15 sec.

Q3 (UPSC 2018): With reference to medieval India, which of the following is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Alai Darwaza – Alauddin Khilji
(b) Buland Darwaza – Akbar
(c) Moti Masjid – Aurangzeb
(d) Shalimar Bagh – Shah Jahan
Answer: (d) Shalimar Bagh was built by Jahangir, not Shah Jahan.
Trap Type: Negative Phrasing ("NOT") + Almost Correct – Shah Jahan built many gardens, but Shalimar Bagh in Kashmir is Jahangir's.
15/45-sec: Analytical, 45 sec (requires careful cross-check).

Q4 (Railway 2021): Who was the first sultan of Delhi to issue token currency?
(a) Alauddin Khilji
(b) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(d) Jalaluddin Khilji
Answer: (c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Trap Type: Almost Correct – many confuse with Alauddin's market reforms or Firoz's coinage.

Q5 (UPSC 2017): Consider the following pairs:

Harihara and Bukka – Vijayanagar

Hasan Gangu – Bahmani

Krishnadeva Raya – Maratha
Which of the pairs is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b) 1 and 2 only. Krishnadeva Raya was Vijayanagar, not Maratha.
Trap Type: Context Shift – mixing Krishnadeva Raya with Maratha kingdom.

Q6 (SSC CGL 2023): The treaty of Purandar (1665) was signed between Shivaji and:
(a) Aurangzeb
(b) Raja Jai Singh
(c) Shaista Khan
(d) Prince Muazzam
Answer: (b) Raja Jai Singh
Trap Type: Similar Names – many associate Aurangzeb directly, but the treaty was with his general Jai Singh.

Q7 (Banking 2019): Ibn Battuta visited India during the reign of:
(a) Akbar
(b) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(d) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Trap Type: Chronological Confusion – Akbar is much later.

Q8 (UPSC 2015): Which of the following Mughal emperors was known as 'Zinda Pir'?
(a) Babur
(b) Humayun
(c) Jahangir
(d) Aurangzeb
Answer: (d) Aurangzeb
Trap Type: Almost Correct – 'Zinda Pir' (living saint) was Aurangzeb, but his religious bigotry misleads some to think he wouldn't be called that.

Q9 (SSC CGL 2024): The famous 'Peacock Throne' was made for which Mughal emperor?
(a) Akbar
(b) Jahangir
(c) Shah Jahan
(d) Aurangzeb
Answer: (c) Shah Jahan
Trap Type: Almost Correct – many associate the throne with Akbar due to his grandeur.

Q10 (Railway 2020): Who among the following was the founder of the Khilji dynasty?
(a) Alauddin Khilji
(b) Jalaluddin Khilji
(c) Muhammad Khilji
(d) Mubarak Khilji
Answer: (b) Jalaluddin Khilji
Trap Type: Similar Names / Chronological Confusion – Alauddin is more famous, so trap.
15/45-sec: Factual, 15 sec. Use mnemonic: "Jalal first, Alauddin later."

15/45-Second Rule Application

Q1, Q2, Q10 → Fact-based, 15 seconds each. Straight recall; no analysis needed.

Q3, Q8 → Analytical, 45 seconds each. Need to verify multiple statements, check "NOT" phrasing, and rule out traps. For Q8, requires understanding of epithet and context.

Score Maximizer Tip: Invest time in Mughal administrative/revenue systems and Delhi Sultanate experiments; these yield high returns across exams.
