Set 2: Medieval Indian History – MCQs (26–50)
Medium: 26–50

26. Consider the following statements about Alauddin Khilji's market reforms:

He fixed prices of all commodities.

A separate department called Diwan-i-Riyasat was created to control markets.

The system collapsed immediately after his death.
Which of the above are correct?
(A) 1 and 2 only
(B) 1 and 3 only
(C) 2 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (A) 1 and 2 only
Solution: The market control department under Alauddin was Diwan-i-Riyasat (or Shahana-i-Mandi). The system did not collapse immediately; it was maintained for some time. So statement 3 is incorrect.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

27. Which of the following rulers introduced the 'Sijda' and 'Paibos' practices in the court?
(A) Iltutmish
(B) Balban
(C) Alauddin Khilji
(D) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (B) Balban
Solution: Balban enforced strict court etiquette; nobles had to perform Sijda (prostration) and Paibos (kissing the feet) before the Sultan.
Source: Railway NTPC 2019

28. Who among the following was NOT a Navratna in Akbar's court?
(A) Todar Mal
(B) Birbal
(C) Raja Man Singh
(D) Malik Kafur
Answer: (D) Malik Kafur
Solution: Malik Kafur was a general of Alauddin Khilji, not a jewel in Akbar's court.
Source: UPSC 2017

29. The 'Zabti' system of land revenue during Akbar's reign was introduced by:
(A) Birbal
(B) Abul Fazl
(C) Todar Mal
(D) Raja Man Singh
Answer: (C) Todar Mal
Solution: Raja Todar Mal, finance minister of Akbar, introduced the Zabti system based on measurement of land and estimation of produce.
Source: Banking 2020

30. Which Mughal emperor is known for the 'Chain of Justice' (Zanjir-e-Adl)?
(A) Akbar
(B) Jahangir
(C) Shah Jahan
(D) Aurangzeb
Answer: (B) Jahangir
Solution: Jahangir hung a golden chain from the palace to the riverbank, which anyone could pull to seek royal justice.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

31. Sir Thomas Roe visited the court of:
(A) Akbar
(B) Jahangir
(C) Shah Jahan
(D) Aurangzeb
Answer: (B) Jahangir
Solution: Thomas Roe, the English ambassador, came in 1615 during Jahangir's reign and obtained trading concessions.
Source: UPSC 2016

32. Who built the Buland Darwaza at Fatehpur Sikri?
(A) Akbar
(B) Jahangir
(C) Shah Jahan
(D) Babur
Answer: (A) Akbar
Solution: Akbar built the Buland Darwaza (Gate of Magnificence) in 1601 to commemorate his conquest of Gujarat.
Source: Railway Group D 2018

33. The 'Peacock Throne' was made during the reign of:
(A) Akbar
(B) Jahangir
(C) Shah Jahan
(D) Aurangzeb
Answer: (C) Shah Jahan
Solution: The famous jewel-studded Peacock Throne, later looted by Nadir Shah, was commissioned by Shah Jahan.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

34. Which of the following rulers reimposed Jizya after it had been abolished by Akbar?
(A) Jahangir
(B) Shah Jahan
(C) Aurangzeb
(D) Bahadur Shah I
Answer: (C) Aurangzeb
Solution: Akbar abolished Jizya in 1564; Aurangzeb reimposed it in 1679 as part of his orthodox policies.
Source: UPSC 2019

35. The 'Ashtapradhan' was a council of eight ministers introduced by:
(A) Akbar
(B) Sher Shah Suri
(C) Shivaji
(D) Krishnadeva Raya
Answer: (C) Shivaji
Solution: Shivaji's Ashtapradhan (eight ministers) each headed a department — Peshwa, Amatya, etc.
Source: SSC CHSL 2022

36. Which Maratha tax was one-fourth of the land revenue collected from non-Maratha territories?
(A) Sardeshmukhi
(B) Chauth
(C) Zabti
(D) Galla
Answer: (B) Chauth
Solution: Chauth was 25% of revenue, collected as protection tax. Sardeshmukhi was an additional 10%.
Source: Banking 2019

37. The Treaty of Purandar (1665) was signed between Shivaji and:
(A) Aurangzeb
(B) Jai Singh
(C) Shaista Khan
(D) Afzal Khan
Answer: (B) Jai Singh
Solution: Raja Jai Singh of Amber, sent by Aurangzeb, forced Shivaji to sign the Treaty of Purandar.
Source: UPSC 2021

38. Harihara and Bukka founded the Vijayanagar empire with the guidance of:
(A) Ramanujacharya
(B) Vidyaranya
(C) Shankaracharya
(D) Basavanna
Answer: (B) Vidyaranya
Solution: Saint Vidyaranya, a sage of the Sringeri monastery, guided the brothers in founding the empire in 1336.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

39. The Battle of Talikota (1565) resulted in the defeat of:
(A) Bahmani kingdom
(B) Vijayanagar empire
(C) Maratha kingdom
(D) Mughal empire
Answer: (B) Vijayanagar empire
Solution: The combined forces of the Deccan Sultanates decisively defeated Vijayanagar at Talikota.
Source: Railway NTPC 2020

40. Who was the greatest ruler of Vijayanagar known for his patronage of literature and architecture?
(A) Devaraya II
(B) Krishnadeva Raya
(C) Harihara I
(D) Bukka I
Answer: (B) Krishnadeva Raya
Solution: Krishnadeva Raya (1509-29) wrote Amuktamalyada in Telugu and built the Vitthala and Hazara Rama temples.
Source: UPSC 2015

41. The 'Grand Trunk Road' from Bengal to the Indus was built by:
(A) Ashoka
(B) Sher Shah Suri
(C) Akbar
(D) Shah Jahan
Answer: (B) Sher Shah Suri
Solution: Sher Shah built the Sadak-e-Azam (GT Road) from Sonargaon (Bengal) to Attock (Indus).
Source: SSC CGL 2023

42. 'Patta' and 'Kabuliyat' related to:
(A) Religious grants
(B) Land revenue records
(C) Military recruitment
(D) Trade contracts
Answer: (B) Land revenue records
Solution: Patta was the state's record of land rights and revenue demand; Kabuliyat was the peasant's acceptance deed. This was introduced by Sher Shah.
Source: Banking 2018

43. The autobiography of Babur, 'Tuzuk-i-Baburi', was originally written in:
(A) Persian
(B) Arabic
(C) Turkish
(D) Urdu
Answer: (C) Turkish
Solution: Babur wrote his memoirs in Chagatai Turkish; later translated to Persian as Baburnama.
Source: UPSC 2014

44. 'Ain-i-Akbari' was written by:
(A) Akbar
(B) Abul Fazl
(C) Faizi
(D) Badayuni
Answer: (B) Abul Fazl
Solution: Abul Fazl, one of Akbar's Navratnas, wrote Ain-i-Akbari, part of the Akbarnama, detailing administration.
Source: SSC CGL 2017

45. Which Sikh Guru compiled the Adi Granth and built the Harmandir Sahib?
(A) Guru Nanak
(B) Guru Angad
(C) Guru Arjan Dev
(D) Guru Tegh Bahadur
Answer: (C) Guru Arjan Dev
Solution: Guru Arjan Dev (fifth guru) compiled the Adi Granth, built the Golden Temple, and was executed by Jahangir.
Source: Railway 2021

46. The term 'Mansab' in the Mughal administration denoted:
(A) A piece of land
(B) A rank or position
(C) A type of coin
(D) A religious decree
Answer: (B) A rank or position
Solution: Mansab means rank. The Mansabdari system classified officers by Zat (personal status) and Sawar (cavalry).
Source: SSC CGL 2020

47. Which of the following monuments is NOT correctly matched with its builder?
(A) Alai Darwaza – Alauddin Khilji
(B) Shalimar Bagh – Shah Jahan
(C) Moti Masjid (Agra) – Shah Jahan
(D) Red Fort – Shah Jahan
Answer: (B) Shalimar Bagh – Shah Jahan
Solution: Shalimar Bagh in Kashmir was built by Jahangir, not Shah Jahan. Shah Jahan built Shalimar Gardens in Lahore.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct statement – Shalimar Bagh's builder is often mixed between Jahangir and Shah Jahan.
Source: UPSC 2018

48. Assertion (A): Muhammad bin Tughlaq transferred his capital from Delhi to Daulatabad. Reason (R): Daulatabad was centrally located and helped in better administration of the Deccan.
(A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation
(B) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation
(C) A is true, R is false
(D) A is false, R is true
Answer: (A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation
Solution: Muhammad bin Tughlaq did shift the capital; one reason was the central location for controlling the vast empire and the Mongol threat from the northwest. Reason is valid.
Source: UPSC 2022

49. Which of the following battles was fought between the Marathas and Ahmad Shah Abdali?
(A) First Battle of Panipat
(B) Second Battle of Panipat
(C) Third Battle of Panipat
(D) Battle of Khanwa
Answer: (C) Third Battle of Panipat
Solution: The Third Battle of Panipat (1761) was between the Marathas under Sadashivrao Bhau and Ahmad Shah Abdali.
Examiner's Trap: Chronological confusion – mixing up the three Panipat battles.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

50. Consider the following statements:

Alauddin Khilji was the founder of the Khilji dynasty.

Din-i-Ilahi was a new religion founded by Akbar.

The token currency of Muhammad bin Tughlaq was made of leather.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(A) None
(B) 1 only
(C) 2 only
(D) 3 only
Answer: (A) None
Solution: Jalaluddin founded Khilji dynasty, not Alauddin. Din-i-Ilahi was not a religion. Token currency was of copper/brass, not leather. All statements are false.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct statements – each statement has a subtle error.
Source: UPSC 2019
