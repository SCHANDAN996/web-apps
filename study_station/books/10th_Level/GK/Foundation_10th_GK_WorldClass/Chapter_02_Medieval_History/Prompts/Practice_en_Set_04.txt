76. Who among the following Sultans was the first to introduce the practice of 'Sijda' (prostration) and 'Paibos' (kissing feet) in the court?
(A) Iltutmish
(B) Balban
(C) Alauddin Khilji
(D) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (B) Balban
Solution: Balban enforced strict court rituals to enhance the prestige of the Sultan.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

77. The 'Diwan-i-Arz' department under the Delhi Sultanate dealt with:
(A) Revenue
(B) Military administration
(C) Religious affairs
(D) Justice
Answer: (B) Military administration
Solution: Diwan-i-Arz was the military department headed by Ariz-i-Mumalik.
Source: Railway NTPC 2020

78. Which of the following Sultans adopted the title of 'Niyabat-i-Khudai' (Vicegerent of God)?
(A) Balban
(B) Alauddin Khilji
(C) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(D) Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq
Answer: (A) Balban
Solution: Balban called himself 'Niyabat-i-Khudai' and 'Zil-e-Ilahi' to emphasize divine kingship.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

79. The famous 'Hauz Khas' complex in Delhi was built by:
(A) Alauddin Khilji
(B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(D) Sikandar Lodi
Answer: (C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Solution: Firoz Shah Tughlaq built Hauz Khas (royal tank) along with a madrasa and his own tomb.
Source: Banking 2021

80. Which of the following statements about the 'Madrasa' system under Delhi Sultanate is correct?
(A) It was exclusively for religious education
(B) Iltutmish established the first madrasa in Delhi
(C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq closed down all madrasas
(D) Madrasas were only open to Muslims
Answer: (B) Iltutmish established the first madrasa in Delhi
Solution: Iltutmish established the Madrasa-i-Muizzi in Delhi. The other statements are factually incorrect or misleading.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct – some options might seem plausible if one confuses later rulers' policies.
Source: UPSC 2017

(C) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(D) Firoz Shah Tughlaq

Source: Railway Group D 2019

83. Which of the following crops was NOT covered under the 'Zabti' system of Akbar?
(A) Wheat
(B) Rice
(C) Sugarcane
(D) Indigo
Answer: (C) Sugarcane
Solution: The Zabti system initially applied to areas growing wheat, rice, barley, gram, etc. Cash crops like sugarcane, cotton, indigo were taxed differently or were outside the standard system. However, variations exist; typical exam answer favors sugarcane. (Exact: Ain-i-Akbari mentions that zabti was for food grains, not cash crops. Many sources state that sugarcane was excluded from standard zabti; often a separate assessment.)
Source: UPSC 2014
