126. Consider the following pairs (Tax – Levied by):

Charai – Alauddin Khilji

Ghari – Muhammad bin Tughlaq

Chauth – Shivaji

Sardeshmukhi – Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(A) 1 only
(B) 1 and 3 only
(C) 2, 3 and 4 only
(D) 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: (B) 1 and 3 only
Solution: Charai (grazing tax) and Ghari (house tax) were both introduced by Alauddin Khilji, not Muhammad bin Tughlaq. Chauth and Sardeshmukhi were Maratha taxes under Shivaji. So 1 and 3 are correct. Pair 2 and 4 are incorrect.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct – mixing up rulers associated with similar taxes.
Source: UPSC 2017

127. Which of the following statements about the 'Madad-i-Maash' grants during the Sultanate period is correct?
(A) They were hereditary military assignments
(B) They were revenue-free land grants given to scholars and religious men
(C) They were given only to Hindu temples
(D) They could be sold or gifted by the holder
Answer: (B) They were revenue-free land grants given to scholars and religious men
Solution: Madad-i-Maash (also called Suyurghal) were non-hereditary, revenue-free grants, usually for life, to learned men, sufis, etc. Not hereditary, not transferable.
Source: UPSC 2019

128. Who among the following wrote 'Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi', a major source for the history of the Delhi Sultanate?
(A) Amir Khusrau
(B) Ziauddin Barani
(C) Ibn Battuta
(D) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Answer: (B) Ziauddin Barani
Source: SSC CGL 2021

129. The 'Khuts' and 'Muqaddams' during the Delhi Sultanate were:
(A) Military commanders
(B) Village headmen and landlords
(C) Revenue officials at the provincial level
(D) Market controllers
Answer: (B) Village headmen and landlords
Solution: Khuts were rich peasants/landlords and Muqaddams were village headmen who often resisted Alauddin's revenue demands.
Source: UPSC 2015

130. Which of the following architectural features was first introduced in the Alai Darwaza?
(A) Use of red sandstone
(B) True arch and dome
(C) Minaret construction
(D) Jali work
Answer: (B) True arch and dome
Solution: The Alai Darwaza (1311) is considered the first building to employ true Islamic arches and domes in India.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

131. The 'Amir-i-Kohi' department created by Muhammad bin Tughlaq dealt with:
(A) Military recruitment
(B) Agricultural loans and cultivation
(C) Foreign affairs
(D) Market regulation
Answer: (B) Agricultural loans and cultivation
Solution: Muhammad bin Tughlaq set up the Diwan-i-Amir-i-Kohi to extend loans (sondhar) and bring wasteland under cultivation.
Source: UPSC 2016

133. Consider the following statements about the 'Zabti' system:

It was based on land measurement and estimation of average yield per bigha.

It was primarily implemented in Gujarat and Bengal.

It used the 'Ilahi Gaz' as the standard unit of measurement.
Which of the statements are correct?
(A) 1 and 2 only
(B) 1 and 3 only
(C) 2 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (B) 1 and 3 only
Solution: The Zabti (Dahsala) system was applied in the core Mughal provinces like Delhi, Agra, Allahabad, etc. Gujarat and Bengal had different revenue arrangements. So 2 is incorrect.
Source: UPSC 2020

134. The 'Majmuah-e-Sultan' was a compilation of the sayings and teachings of:
(A) Alauddin Khilji
(B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq

Answer: (C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Source: Railway NTPC 2019

135. Arrange the following Mughal emperors in the correct chronological order of their reign:

Jahangir

Humayun

(A) 4-2-1-3
(B) 4-2-3-1

137. Which of the following statements about the 'Mansabdari' system is INCORRECT?
(A) The Sawar rank indicated the number of horsemen a mansabdar was required to maintain.
(B) The system was introduced by Akbar.
(C) Mansabdars could be transferred from one jagir to another.
(D) The mansab was always hereditary.
Answer: (D) The mansab was always hereditary.
Solution: The mansab was not hereditary; it was a personal rank that lapsed on death or dismissal. Jagirs were also transferable.
Source: UPSC 2021

(B) Maintain a large standing army on modest pay
(C) Please the religious class

Answer: (B) Maintain a large standing army on modest pay
Source: SSC CGL 2018

139. The autobiography 'Futuhat-i-Firoz Shahi' is written in:
(A) Persian
(B) Arabic
(C) Turkish
(D) Hindi
Answer: (A) Persian
Solution: Firoz Shah wrote it in Persian, though some claim he was not very learned; the court language was Persian.
Source: Banking 2020

140. Which of the following pairs (Battle – Year) is correctly matched?

Source: UPSC 2016

141. Consider the following statements about 'Din-i-Ilahi':

It was a syncretic religion with a holy book called 'Ilahi Granth'.

It had a formal priesthood and places of worship.

It did not survive after Akbar's death.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(A) 3 only
(B) 1 and 2 only
(C) 2 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (A) 3 only
Solution: Din-i-Ilahi was not a religion; it had no holy book, no priesthood, no church/mosque/temple. It faded after Akbar. So only 3 is correct.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct – tempting to think it had some book or formal structure.
Source: UPSC 2019

142. The 'Masir-i-Alamgiri' is a contemporary account of the reign of:
(A) Akbar
(B) Jahangir
(C) Shah Jahan
(D) Aurangzeb
Answer: (D) Aurangzeb
Solution: Written by Mustaid Khan, it covers Aurangzeb's reign.
Source: SSC CGL 2017

143. Which of the following statements about the Vijayanagar empire is correct?
(A) The capital Hampi was sacked by the Marathas in 1565.
(B) Krishnadeva Raya belonged to the Sangama dynasty.
(C) The empire was founded by Harihara and Bukka of the Tuluva dynasty.
(D) The Hazara Rama temple was built during the reign of Krishnadeva Raya.
Answer: (D) The Hazara Rama temple was built during the reign of Krishnadeva Raya.
Solution: Hampi was sacked in 1565 at Talikota by Deccan sultanates, not Marathas. Krishnadeva Raya belonged to the Tuluva dynasty, not Sangama. The founders were from Sangama dynasty. Hazara Rama temple is indeed built by Krishnadeva Raya.
Source: UPSC 2018

A. Amir Khusrau - 1. Khazain-ul-Futuh

D. Firoz Shah Tughlaq - 4. Futuhat-i-Firoz Shahi

Source: UPSC 2014

147. Which of the following is NOT an example of Indo-Islamic architecture?

148. Assertion (A): The Tughlaq dynasty was followed by the Sayyid dynasty.
Reason (R): The Sayyids were descendants of the Prophet Muhammad.
(A) Both A and R are true, R is the correct explanation of A
(B) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation
(C) A is true, R is false
(D) A is false, R is true
Answer: (B) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation
Solution: Tughlaqs were succeeded by the Sayyids (1414). The Sayyids claimed descent from the Prophet, but that is not the reason for their succession; it was due to the decline of the Tughlaqs and the vacuum of power.
Source: UPSC 2016

149. Consider the following statements about Sher Shah Suri:

He introduced a silver coin called 'Rupiya'.

He built the Grand Trunk Road connecting Sonargaon to Attock.

He introduced the 'Patta-Kabuliyat' system for land records.

He abolished Jizya completely.
Which of the statements are correct?
(A) 1, 2 and 3 only
(B) 1, 2 and 4 only
(C) 2, 3 and 4 only
(D) 1, 3 and 4 only
Answer: (A) 1, 2 and 3 only
Solution: Sher Shah did not abolish Jizya; Akbar did later. So 4 is incorrect. Rest all are correct.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct – many assume Sher Shah also abolished Jizya due to his secular image.
Source: UPSC 2022

150. Which of the following statements about the Maratha 'Ashtapradhan' is INCORRECT?
(A) The Peshwa was the prime minister.
(B) The Amatya was the revenue minister.
(C) The Mantri was the head of the intelligence department.
(D) The Sachiv was the foreign minister.
Answer: (D) The Sachiv was the foreign minister.
Solution: In Shivaji's Ashtapradhan, the Sachiv was the general secretary and in charge of official correspondence. The foreign minister was the Sumant/Dabir. So (D) is incorrectly matched.
Source: SSC CGL 2019
