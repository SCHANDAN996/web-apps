101. The Iqta system under the Delhi Sultanate was a transferable assignment of:
(A) Military command
(B) Religious endowment
(C) Land revenue
(D) Judicial authority
Answer: (C) Land revenue
Solution: Iqta was the assignment of the right to collect land revenue from a specified area in lieu of salary; it was non-hereditary and transferable.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Which of the following Sultans introduced the practice of 'Charai' and 'Ghari' (house and grazing taxes)?
(A) Iltutmish
(B) Alauddin Khilji
(C) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(D) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Answer: (B) Alauddin Khilji
Solution: Alauddin imposed house tax (Ghari) and grazing tax (Charai) on villages to increase state revenue.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Who was the Delhi Sultan when the famous poet Amir Khusrau lived?
(A) Qutb-ud-din Aibak
(B) Balban
(C) Jalaluddin Khilji
(D) Iltutmish
Answer: (B) Balban
Solution: Amir Khusrau (1253–1325) served under multiple Sultans from Balban to Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq, but his earliest patron was Balban. He lived through Balban's reign.
Source: PYQ-style

104. The 'Alai Darwaza' is an example of which architectural feature?
(A) True arch and dome
(B) Trabeate construction
(C) Pyramid structure
(D) Rock-cut architecture
Answer: (A) True arch and dome
Solution: The Alai Darwaza (1311) is one of the first structures in India to use a true Islamic arch and dome, a hallmark of the Indo-Islamic style.
Source: PYQ-style

Hard Level (121–125)

121. Consider the following statements about the Mansabdari system:

The 'Sawar' rank always equalled the 'Zat' rank.

The system was hereditary.

The salary of a mansabdar was paid in cash or jagir.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(A) 1 only
(B) 1 and 2 only
(C) 3 only
(D) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (C) 3 only
Solution: Sawar and Zat ranks were not always equal; the system was not hereditary; salary could be cash (naqd) or jagir assignment. Thus only statement 3 is correct.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct – statements 1 and 2 are commonly misinterpreted.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Which of the following events is NOT correctly matched with the year?
(A) First Battle of Panipat – 1526
(B) Battle of Talikota – 1565
(C) Din-i-Ilahi – 1582
(D) Reimposition of Jizya by Aurangzeb – 1707
Answer: (D) Reimposition of Jizya by Aurangzeb – 1707
Solution: Aurangzeb reimposed Jizya in 1679, not 1707 (1707 was his death).
Examiner's Trap: Chronological confusion – 1707 is Aurangzeb's death year, often mixed with his policies.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Consider the following pairs (Author – Work):

Abul Fazl – Tabakat-i-Akbari

Nizamuddin Ahmad – Akbarnama

Badauni – Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh
Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(A) 1 only
(B) 2 only
(C) 3 only
(D) None of the above
Answer: (C) 3 only
Solution: Abul Fazl wrote Akbarnama (not Tabakat-i-Akbari); Nizamuddin Ahmad wrote Tabakat-i-Akbari; Badauni wrote Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh. So only pair 3 is correct.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Which of the following statements is correct regarding the 'Token Currency' of Muhammad bin Tughlaq?
(A) It was made of silver and copper
(B) It was successful in controlling inflation
(C) It was inspired by the Chinese paper currency system
(D) It was made of leather
Answer: (C) It was inspired by the Chinese paper currency system
Solution: The token currency was brass/copper coins, not leather or silver; it failed miserably. The idea was influenced by the paper money system in China under Kublai Khan.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct – candidates often assume 'token' meant leather or paper, or they think it succeeded.
Source: PYQ-style
