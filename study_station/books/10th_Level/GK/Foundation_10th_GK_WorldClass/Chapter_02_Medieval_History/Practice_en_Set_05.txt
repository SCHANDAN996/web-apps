101. The Iqta system under the Delhi Sultanate was a transferable assignment of:
(a) Land revenue (b) Religious endowment (c) Military command (d) Judicial authority
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is military command, because iqta holders did maintain troops, but the iqta itself was the right to collect land revenue from a specified area in lieu of salary; it was non-hereditary and transferable, so the answer is land revenue.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

102. Which Sultan introduced the house tax (Ghari) and the grazing tax (Charai)?
(a) Iltutmish (b) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (d) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (d)
Solution: Firoz Shah Tughlaq is tempting because he levied many cesses, but the house tax and grazing tax on villages were imposed by Alauddin Khilji to maximise revenue for his large standing army, so the answer is Alauddin Khilji.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Amir Khusrau was a disciple of which Sufi saint?
(a) Nizamuddin Auliya (b) Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti (c) Baba Farid (d) Bahauddin Zakariya
Answer: (a)
Solution: Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti is tempting as the most famous Chishti saint, but he belonged to an earlier generation at Ajmer; Amir Khusrau was the devoted disciple of Nizamuddin Auliya of Delhi, so the answer is Nizamuddin Auliya.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 8 Devotional Paths to the Divine

104. The Alai Darwaza is an early example of which architectural feature in India?
(a) Pyramid structure (b) Trabeate construction (c) True arch and dome (d) Rock-cut architecture
Answer: (c)
Solution: Trabeate (beam-and-lintel) construction is tempting because earlier Sultanate buildings used it, but the Alai Darwaza (1311) is among the first structures in India built with a true Islamic arch and dome, so the answer is true arch and dome.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 5 Rulers and Buildings

105. Which Sultan founded the fortified city of Tughlaqabad near Delhi?
(a) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (b) Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq (c) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (d) Nasiruddin Mahmud Tughlaq
Answer: (b)
Solution: Muhammad bin Tughlaq is tempting because he built cities like Jahanpanah, but Tughlaqabad was founded by the dynasty's founder Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq, so the answer is Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

106. The Gaz-i-Sikandari, a standard yard for land measurement, was introduced by:
(a) Sher Shah Suri (b) Sikandar Lodi (c) Alauddin Khilji (d) Akbar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Akbar is tempting because of his famous Ilahi Gaz, but the Gaz-i-Sikandari was introduced by Sikandar Lodi; the similar names are the examiner's trap, so the answer is Sikandar Lodi.
Source: PYQ-style

107. Babur's autobiography Tuzuk-i-Baburi was translated into Persian (as Baburnama) during the reign of:
(a) Babur (b) Humayun (c) Jahangir (d) Akbar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Babur is tempting because he wrote the memoir, but he wrote it in Chagatai Turkish; Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan translated it into Persian under Akbar, so the answer is Akbar.
Source: PYQ-style

108. The Ain-i-Akbari provides detailed information mainly on:
(a) Military conquests only (b) Administration, revenue, and culture (c) Religious philosophy (d) Foreign relations
Answer: (b)
Solution: Military conquests is tempting because the Akbarnama narrates campaigns, but the Ain-i-Akbari, its third volume by Abul Fazl, is a statistical account of Akbar's administration, revenues, army, and society, so the answer is administration, revenue, and culture.
Source: PYQ-style

109. Which Mughal emperor built the city of Dinpanah at Delhi?
(a) Babur (b) Akbar (c) Humayun (d) Jahangir
Answer: (c)
Solution: Akbar is tempting because he built Fatehpur Sikri, but Dinpanah was the new city begun by Humayun at Delhi, where the Purana Qila stands, so the answer is Humayun.
Source: PYQ-style

110. In the Mughal Mansabdari system, the 'Zat' rank signified:
(a) Number of horses maintained (b) Personal rank and salary (c) Size of land assignment (d) Number of foot soldiers
Answer: (b)
Solution: Number of horses is tempting because that is what the Sawar rank indicated, but Zat fixed the officer's personal rank, status, and salary, so the answer is personal rank and salary.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 4 The Mughal Empire

111. The Pietra Dura technique (inlay of semi-precious stones) is prominently seen in:
(a) Qutub Minar (b) Alai Darwaza (c) Buland Darwaza (d) Taj Mahal
Answer: (d)
Solution: Buland Darwaza is tempting as a grand Mughal monument, but pietra dura — coloured stones inlaid in white marble — is the signature decoration of the Taj Mahal, so the answer is Taj Mahal.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 5 Rulers and Buildings

112. Which Rajput ruler, son of Raja Bharmal of Amber, became a trusted general of Akbar?
(a) Raja Man Singh (b) Rana Pratap (c) Chandrasen Rathore (d) Rana Sanga
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rana Pratap is tempting as the most famous Rajput of Akbar's era, but he fought Akbar at Haldighati; Raja Man Singh of Amber entered Akbar's service through matrimonial alliance and became a leading general, so the answer is Raja Man Singh.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 4 The Mughal Empire

113. The 'Chain of Justice' was a symbol of which emperor's commitment to justice?
(a) Akbar (b) Jahangir (c) Babur (d) Aurangzeb
Answer: (b)
Solution: Akbar is tempting because of his reputation for tolerance and fairness, but the golden chain with bells, which any petitioner could pull, was installed by Jahangir, so the answer is Jahangir.
Source: PYQ-style

114. Which Mughal emperor founded the city of Shahjahanabad and shifted the capital back to Delhi?
(a) Humayun (b) Shah Jahan (c) Jahangir (d) Akbar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Akbar is tempting because he moved capitals between Agra, Fatehpur Sikri, and Lahore, but Shahjahanabad (with the Red Fort and Jama Masjid) was founded by Shah Jahan in 1638–48, so the answer is Shah Jahan.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 5 Rulers and Buildings

115. The Jizya reimposed by Aurangzeb in 1679 had earlier been abolished by:
(a) Alauddin Khilji (b) Sher Shah Suri (c) Akbar (d) Jahangir
Answer: (c)
Solution: Jahangir is tempting as Akbar's successor who continued many of his policies, but it was Akbar who abolished Jizya in 1564, which Aurangzeb later reimposed, so the answer is Akbar.
Source: PYQ-style

116. The Khalsa was created by Guru Gobind Singh in 1699 in response to:
(a) Mughal persecution of Sikhs (b) Mughal religious tolerance (c) Maratha expansion (d) Afghan invasions
Answer: (a)
Solution: Maratha expansion is tempting because it was contemporary, but the Khalsa was a military-religious brotherhood formed amid Mughal persecution, including the execution of Guru Tegh Bahadur, so the answer is Mughal persecution of Sikhs.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 10 Eighteenth-Century Political Formations

117. The Maratha revenue term 'Chauth' literally means:
(a) One-fourth (b) One-tenth (c) One-third (d) Half
Answer: (a)
Solution: One-tenth is tempting because another Maratha levy, Sardeshmukhi, was an additional 10 per cent, but Chauth was one-fourth (25 per cent) of the revenue paid as protection money, so the answer is one-fourth.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 10 Eighteenth-Century Political Formations

118. The Bahmani kingdom eventually split into how many Deccan sultanates?
(a) Five (b) Four (c) Three (d) Six
Answer: (a)
Solution: Four is tempting because candidates often forget Berar, but the Bahmani kingdom broke into five sultanates — Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar, Berar, and Bidar — so the answer is five.
Source: PYQ-style

119. Hampi, the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire, is located on the banks of which river?
(a) Godavari (b) Krishna (c) Kaveri (d) Tungabhadra
Answer: (d)
Solution: Krishna is tempting because Vijayanagara fought battles near the Krishna river, but the ruins of Hampi lie on the southern bank of the Tungabhadra in Karnataka, so the answer is Tungabhadra.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 9 The Making of Regional Cultures

120. Who is credited with creating the Qawwali and enriching Hindustani classical music?
(a) Amir Khusrau (b) Baiju Bawra (c) Swami Haridas (d) Tansen
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tansen is tempting as the most famous musician of the era, but he belonged to Akbar's court centuries later; Amir Khusrau, the Sufi poet-musician, is regarded as the father of Qawwali, so the answer is Amir Khusrau.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 9 The Making of Regional Cultures

121. Consider the following statements about the Mansabdari system:
1. The Sawar rank always equalled the Zat rank.
2. The system was hereditary.
3. The salary of a mansabdar was paid in cash or through a jagir.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 3 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statements 1 and 2 are tempting because they sound plausible, but Sawar could be lower than or equal to Zat, and mansabs were not hereditary; only the payment mode in cash (naqd) or jagir is correct, so the answer is 3 only.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Which of the following events is NOT correctly matched with the year?
(a) First Battle of Panipat – 1526 (b) Battle of Talikota – 1565 (c) Reimposition of Jizya by Aurangzeb – 1707 (d) Din-i-Ilahi – 1582
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is chronological confusion: 1707 is the year of Aurangzeb's death, while he reimposed Jizya in 1679; the other three pairs are correct, so the answer is reimposition of Jizya – 1707.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Consider the following pairs (Author – Work):
1. Abul Fazl – Tabakat-i-Akbari
2. Nizamuddin Ahmad – Akbarnama
3. Badauni – Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh
Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) 3 only (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Pairs 1 and 2 are tempting because the authors and works are all real, but they are swapped: Abul Fazl wrote the Akbarnama and Nizamuddin Ahmad wrote Tabakat-i-Akbari; only Badauni's Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh is correctly matched, so the answer is 3 only.
Source: PYQ-style

124. Arrange the following battles in chronological order:
1. Battle of Khanwa
2. Battle of Chanderi
3. Battle of Ghaghra
4. Battle of Haldighati
Select the correct answer:
(a) 2-1-3-4 (b) 1-2-3-4 (c) 1-3-2-4 (d) 3-2-1-4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is placing Ghaghra before Chanderi, but the order is Khanwa (1527), Chanderi (1528), Ghaghra (1529), and Haldighati (1576), so the answer is 1-2-3-4.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Which statement is correct regarding the token currency of Muhammad bin Tughlaq?
(a) It was made of silver and copper (b) It succeeded in controlling inflation (c) It was made of leather (d) It was inspired by the paper currency of China
Answer: (d)
Solution: Leather or paper tokens are tempting guesses from the word 'token', but the coins were of brass and copper and the scheme failed; the idea was inspired by Kublai Khan's paper money in China, so the answer is that it was inspired by the paper currency of China.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans
