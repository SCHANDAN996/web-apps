Q: What did Qutb-ud-din Aibak start building but never finish?
A: Qutub Minar. He only built the first storey; Iltutmish completed it later.

Q: Who was the only Muslim woman to ever rule the Delhi Sultanate, and why did her rule end?
A: Razia Sultan. Turkish nobles (Chalisa) refused to accept a female monarch, leading to her downfall.

Q: Why is calling Din-i-Ilahi a “new religion” a classic exam trap?
A: Because it wasn’t a religion — it was a moral club with a few followers, no holy book, no worship, and no mass conversion.

Q: What material were Muhammad bin Tughlaq’s token coins actually made of? (Beware of paper-coin trap.)
A: Copper and brass — not leather, not paper. Forgeries exploded because people made identical coins at home.

Q: What mnemonic can you use to remember the five dynasties of the Delhi Sultanate in order?
A: “Gul Khil Tug Say Lo” — Gulam, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi.

Q: What is the simple “BHAJ SA” trick for recalling the Mughal emperors chronologically?
A: Babur, Humayun, Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb — BHAJ SA.

Q: How do you quickly differentiate the three Battles of Panipat by just using initials?
A: 1st → Babur (1526), 2nd → Akbar vs Hemu, 3rd → Marathas vs Abdali (1761).

Q: Which ruler issued the silver ‘Rupiya’ that eventually gave India’s currency its name?
A: Sher Shah Suri. He also built the Grand Trunk Road and introduced Patta-Kabuliyat.

Q: How does the Mansabdari system work like a modern government job with grade pay?
A: Every officer had a Zat (personal rank/salary) and Sawar (cavalry to maintain), just like a pay scale and a responsibility quota.

Q: What were Chauth and Sardeshmukhi, and which ruler collected them?
A: Chauth was 1/4th tax, Sardeshmukhi was an extra 10%. Shivaji levied these as protection money from non-Maratha territories.

Q: In which battle did the combined Deccan sultanates crush the Vijayanagar Empire?
A: Battle of Talikota (1565), long after Krishnadeva Raya’s golden reign.

Q: Which famous Moroccan traveller wrote ‘Rihla’ and in whose court did he stay?
A: Ibn Battuta, in the court of Muhammad bin Tughlaq.

Q: Who made Jizya a separate tax independent of land revenue, and why does it confuse students?
A: Firoz Shah Tughlaq. Many mistakenly think he introduced Jizya; he only separated it from the land tax.

Q: What was Shivaji’s council of eight ministers called?
A: Ashtapradhan. Each minister handled a specific department like finance, defence, and foreign affairs.

Q: When and where did Guru Gobind Singh found the Khalsa?
A: 1699, at Anandpur Sahib. He gave the Five Ks to make Sikhs a martial community.

Q: Who really founded the Vijayanagar Empire, and under whose spiritual guidance?
A: Harihara and Bukka, with the guidance of saint Vidyaranya. (Not Krishnadeva Raya — he was its greatest ruler later.)

Q: Which gateway in Delhi is considered the first true Islamic arch in India and who built it?
A: Alai Darwaza, built by Alauddin Khilji near Qutub complex.

Q: What’s the “double capital” trap: which ruler shifted the capital to Daulatabad, not Akbar?
A: Muhammad bin Tughlaq moved the entire population from Delhi to Daulatabad (Devagiri) and then back. Akbar later built a temporary capital at Fatehpur Sikri.
