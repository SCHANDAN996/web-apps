Set 1: Medieval Indian History – MCQs (1–25)
Easy: 1–20 | Medium: 21–25

1. Who started the construction of Qutub Minar?
(A) Iltutmish
(B) Qutb-ud-din Aibak
(C) Alauddin Khilji
(D) Balban
Answer: (B) Qutb-ud-din Aibak
Solution: Aibak, the first Sultan of Delhi, began building Qutub Minar but only completed the first storey. Iltutmish later finished it.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

2. The Iqta system was introduced by:
(A) Balban
(B) Alauddin Khilji
(C) Iltutmish
(D) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (C) Iltutmish
Solution: Iltutmish established the Iqta system – assignment of land revenue in lieu of salary to officers.
Source: Railway Group D 2019

3. Razia Sultan was the daughter of:
(A) Balban
(B) Iltutmish
(C) Qutb-ud-din Aibak
(D) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (B) Iltutmish
Solution: Razia was the daughter of Iltutmish; he nominated her as his successor.
Source: SSC CGL 2017

4. The silver coin 'Tanka' was introduced by:
(A) Alauddin Khilji
(B) Iltutmish
(C) Sher Shah Suri
(D) Akbar
Answer: (B) Iltutmish
Solution: Iltutmish issued silver Tanka and copper Jital.
Source: UPSC 2016

5. Who assumed the title 'Lakh Baksh'?
(A) Balban
(B) Qutb-ud-din Aibak
(C) Iltutmish
(D) Razia Sultan
Answer: (B) Qutb-ud-din Aibak
Solution: Aibak was called Lakh Baksh (giver of lakhs) due to his generosity.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

6. The 'Chalisa' or 'Forty' was a group of nobles during the reign of:
(A) Balban
(B) Iltutmish
(C) Alauddin Khilji
(D) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (B) Iltutmish
Solution: Iltutmish organized the Turkish nobles into a council of forty called Chalisa.
Source: Railway NTPC 2021

7. Alauddin Khilji's market control system was called:
(A) Iqta
(B) Mansab
(C) Shahana-i-Mandi
(D) Zabti
Answer: (C) Shahana-i-Mandi
Solution: A market controller, Shahana-i-Mandi, regulated prices under Alauddin.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

8. Who led Alauddin Khilji's southern campaigns?
(A) Zafar Khan
(B) Malik Kafur
(C) Ulugh Khan
(D) Nusrat Khan
Answer: (B) Malik Kafur
Solution: Malik Kafur, a eunuch general, conquered Devagiri, Warangal, Dwarasamudra, and Madurai.
Source: UPSC 2014

9. 'Token currency' experiment in Delhi Sultanate was introduced by:
(A) Alauddin Khilji
(B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(D) Sikandar Lodi
Answer: (B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Solution: Muhammad bin Tughlaq introduced brass and copper token coins with face value equal to silver.
Source: Banking 2020

10. Who shifted the capital from Delhi to Daulatabad?
(A) Akbar
(B) Alauddin Khilji
(C) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(D) Sher Shah Suri
Answer: (C) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Solution: Muhammad bin Tughlaq forcibly moved the entire population from Delhi to Devagiri (renamed Daulatabad) and later reversed the decision.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

11. The Moroccan traveler Ibn Battuta visited India during the reign of:
(A) Alauddin Khilji
(B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(D) Akbar
Answer: (B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Solution: Ibn Battuta arrived in the court of Muhammad bin Tughlaq and later wrote 'Rihla'.
Source: UPSC 2015

12. Who separated Jizya from land revenue for the first time?
(A) Akbar
(B) Aurangzeb
(C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(D) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Solution: Jizya existed earlier but was part of land tax; Firoz Shah Tughlaq made it a separate tax.
Source: Railway 2018

13. Which Lodi Sultan founded the city of Agra?
(A) Bahlul Lodi
(B) Sikandar Lodi
(C) Ibrahim Lodi
(D) Daulat Khan Lodi
Answer: (B) Sikandar Lodi
Solution: Sikandar Lodi established Agra in 1504.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

14. The First Battle of Panipat (1526) was fought between:
(A) Babur and Rana Sanga
(B) Babur and Ibrahim Lodi
(C) Akbar and Hemu
(D) Humayun and Sher Shah
Answer: (B) Babur and Ibrahim Lodi
Solution: Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi using gunpowder and Tulughma tactics.
Source: UPSC 2020

15. Who wrote 'Tuzuk-i-Baburi'?
(A) Babur
(B) Abul Fazl
(C) Jahangir
(D) Gulbadan Begum
Answer: (A) Babur
Solution: Babur's autobiography, originally in Turkish, is Tuzuk-i-Baburi.
Source: SSC CGL 2016

16. Sher Shah Suri issued the silver coin called:
(A) Tanka
(B) Rupiya
(C) Jital
(D) Dam
Answer: (B) Rupiya
Solution: Sher Shah's silver Rupiya and copper Dam laid the foundation for the modern rupee.
Source: Banking 2018

17. The second Battle of Panipat (1556) was fought between:
(A) Babur and Ibrahim Lodi
(B) Akbar and Hemu
(C) Akbar and Rana Pratap
(D) Sher Shah and Humayun
Answer: (B) Akbar and Hemu
Solution: Akbar, under the regency of Bairam Khan, defeated Hemu.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

18. Din-i-Ilahi was founded in the year:
(A) 1575
(B) 1582
(C) 1585
(D) 1605
Answer: (B) 1582
Solution: Akbar proclaimed Din-i-Ilahi, a socio-spiritual code, in 1582.
Source: UPSC 2019

19. Which Mughal emperor built the Taj Mahal?
(A) Akbar
(B) Jahangir
(C) Shah Jahan
(D) Aurangzeb
Answer: (C) Shah Jahan
Solution: Taj Mahal was built by Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.
Source: Railway NTPC 2020

20. Guru Gobind Singh founded the Khalsa in:
(A) 1665
(B) 1675
(C) 1699
(D) 1708
Answer: (C) 1699
Solution: On Baisakhi day 1699 at Anandpur Sahib, Guru Gobind Singh established the Khalsa Panth.
Source: SSC CGL 2023

Medium Level

21. Which of the following statements about Din-i-Ilahi is NOT correct?
(A) It was founded by Akbar
(B) It was a new religion with a holy book
(C) Birbal was one of its few followers
(D) It aimed at moral and spiritual upliftment
Answer: (B) It was a new religion with a holy book
Solution: Din-i-Ilahi had no holy book, no formal worship; it was a socio-ethical code, not a religion. Option (B) is false.
Examiner's Trap: Almost correct statement – candidates often mistakenly believe it was a full-fledged religion.
Source: UPSC 2018

22. Match List-I (Monument) with List-II (Builder):
(A) Alai Darwaza – 1. Shah Jahan
(B) Buland Darwaza – 2. Alauddin Khilji
(C) Moti Masjid (Agra) – 3. Akbar
(D) Red Fort – 4. Shah Jahan
Correct matching:
(1) A-2, B-3, C-4, D-1
(2) A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
(3) A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
(4) A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
Answer: (2) A-2 (Alai Darwaza – Alauddin Khilji), B-3 (Buland Darwaza – Akbar), C-1 (Moti Masjid – Shah Jahan), D-4 (Red Fort – Shah Jahan).
Solution: Alai Darwaza by Alauddin, Buland Darwaza at Fatehpur Sikri by Akbar, Moti Masjid in Agra Fort by Shah Jahan, Red Fort by Shah Jahan.
Source: UPSC 2021

23. Consider the following pairs:

Vijayanagar – Harihara and Bukka

Bahmani – Hasan Gangu

Maratha – Krishnadeva Raya
Which of the above pairs is/are correct?
(A) 1 only
(B) 1 and 2 only
(C) 2 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (B) 1 and 2 only
Solution: Krishnadeva Raya was the greatest ruler of Vijayanagar, not a Maratha. Maratha kingdom was founded by Shivaji. So pair 3 is wrong.
Examiner's Trap: Context shift – mixing Vijayanagar ruler with Maratha kingdom.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

24. Which of the following battles is correctly matched with the year?
(A) First Battle of Panipat – 1556
(B) Battle of Khanwa – 1527
(C) Battle of Talikota – 1605
(D) Second Battle of Panipat – 1526
Answer: (B) Battle of Khanwa – 1527
Solution: First Panipat – 1526; Khanwa – 1527 (Babur vs Rana Sanga); Talikota – 1565; Second Panipat – 1556. Only (B) is correct.
Source: Railway 2017

25. Assertion (A): Alauddin Khilji introduced the Dagh system. Reason (R): Dagh was a branding system for horses to prevent corruption in the cavalry.
(A) Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation of A
(B) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation
(C) A is true, R is false
(D) A is false, R is true
Answer: (A) Both A and R are true, R is correct explanation
Solution: Alauddin introduced Dagh (horse branding) and Chehra (descriptive roll of soldiers) to maintain a standing, well-equipped army and prevent fraudulent musters. Reason correctly explains the purpose.
Source: UPSC 2016
