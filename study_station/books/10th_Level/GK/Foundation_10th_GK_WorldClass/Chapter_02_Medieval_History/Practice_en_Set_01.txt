1. Who started the construction of the Qutub Minar in Delhi?
(a) Qutb-ud-din Aibak (b) Iltutmish (c) Alauddin Khilji (d) Balban
Answer: (a)
Solution: Qutb-ud-din Aibak laid the foundation and built the first storey of the Qutub Minar; it was later completed by Iltutmish.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

2. The Iqta system in the Delhi Sultanate was introduced by:
(a) Balban (b) Alauddin Khilji (c) Iltutmish (d) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (c)
Solution: Iltutmish organized the administration by dividing the empire into Iqtas (revenue assignments) given to nobles and military commanders.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

3. Razia Sultan, the first woman ruler of medieval India, was the daughter of:
(a) Balban (b) Qutb-ud-din Aibak (c) Iltutmish (d) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (c)
Solution: Iltutmish nominated his talented daughter Razia as his successor to the Delhi throne.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

4. The pure silver coin 'Tanka' and copper coin 'Jital' were introduced by:
(a) Alauddin Khilji (b) Sher Shah Suri (c) Akbar (d) Iltutmish
Answer: (d)
Solution: Iltutmish was the first Delhi Sultan to issue standardized Arabic coinage: silver Tanka (175 grains) and copper Jital.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

5. Which Sultan of Delhi was popularly known by the title 'Lakh Baksh' (giver of lakhs)?
(a) Qutb-ud-din Aibak (b) Balban (c) Iltutmish (d) Razia Sultan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Qutb-ud-din Aibak was called Lakh Baksh because of his generous donations and charity.

(a) Balban (b) Alauddin Khilji (c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (d) Iltutmish
Answer: (d)
Solution: Iltutmish created the corp of forty loyal Turkish slaves (Chahalgani) to consolidate his royal power.

(a) Diwan-i-Arz (b) Shahana-i-Mandi (c) Diwan-i-Insha (d) Wakil-i-Dar
Answer: (b)

Answer: (c)
Solution: Malik Kafur (Hazar Dinari) commanded the Khilji armies in successful expeditions against Devagiri, Warangal, Dwarasamudra, and Madurai.

9. The ambitious experiment of introducing 'token currency' made of copper and brass was undertaken by:
(a) Alauddin Khilji (b) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (c) Sikandar Lodi (d) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (d)
Solution: Muhammad bin Tughlaq issued bronze/copper token currency on par with silver coins, but widespread counterfeiting forced him to withdraw it.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

10. Which Sultan shifted his capital from Delhi to Daulatabad (Devagiri) in 1327?
(a) Akbar (b) Alauddin Khilji (c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (d) Sher Shah Suri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Muhammad bin Tughlaq moved the imperial court to Daulatabad to exercise tighter control over southern territories and avoid Mongol incursions.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

11. The Moroccan traveler Ibn Battuta visited India during the reign of which Sultan?
(a) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (b) Alauddin Khilji (c) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (d) Akbar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ibn Battuta reached Delhi in 1333 during Muhammad bin Tughlaq's reign and was appointed as the Qazi (judge) of Delhi for several years.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History Part II, Ch 5 Through the Eyes of Travellers

12. Which Sultan of Delhi established canals for irrigation and founded several new cities including Firozabad and Hisar?
(a) Akbar (b) Aurangzeb (c) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (d) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (c)
Solution: Firoz Shah Tughlaq constructed extensive irrigation canals in northern India and established cities like Jaunpur, Hisar, and Firozabad.

(a) Bahlul Lodi (b) Ibrahim Lodi (c) Sikandar Lodi (d) Daulat Khan Lodi

14. The First Battle of Panipat in 1526 was fought between:
(a) Babur and Rana Sanga (b) Akbar and Hemu (c) Humayun and Sher Shah (d) Babur and Ibrahim Lodi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi at Panipat on 21 April 1526 using artillery and the Tulughma tactic, founding the Mughal Empire.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 4 The Mughal Empire

15. The autobiography 'Tuzuk-i-Baburi' (Baburnama) was written by Babur in which language?
(a) Chagatai Turkish (b) Persian (c) Arabic (d) Urdu
Answer: (a)
Solution: Babur wrote his intimate memoirs 'Tuzuk-i-Baburi' in his mother tongue Chagatai Turkish, later translated into Persian by Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 4 The Mughal Empire

16. The standard silver coin 'Rupiya' (178 grains) was first introduced in medieval India by:
(a) Sher Shah Suri (b) Akbar (c) Iltutmish (d) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sher Shah Suri reformed the monetary system by introducing the silver Rupiya and copper Dam, which continued into the Mughal era.

(a) Babur and Ibrahim Lodi (b) Humayun and Sher Shah (c) Akbar and Hemu (d) Akbar and Maharana Pratap
Answer: (c)
Solution: Young Akbar's regent Bairam Khan defeated Hemu (Hemchandra Vikramaditya) at Panipat in 1556, re-establishing Mughal supremacy in North India.

21. Which of the following statements about Akbar's 'Din-i-Ilahi' is INCORRECT?
(a) It was introduced by Akbar in 1582
(b) Birbal was one of its few adherents
(c) It was an ethical-philosophical moral code without a prophet or scripture
(d) Most orthodox Muslims eagerly embraced and joined it
Answer: (d)
Solution: Din-i-Ilahi had very few followers (around 18-20 including Birbal); orthodox nobles and the Muslim clergy largely rejected it. Statement (d) is false.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 4 The Mughal Empire

25. Statement [A]: Alauddin Khilji introduced the Dagh and Chehra systems in his army.
Reason [R]: Dagh was the branding of horses to prevent substitution and corruption in the cavalry.
(a) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
(b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A
(c) A is true, but R is false
(d) A is false, but R is true
Answer: (a)
Solution: Alauddin instituted Dagh (horse branding) and Chehra (huliya or roll of soldiers) to maintain an effective standing cavalry and eliminate proxy musters. Both are true and R explains A.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans
