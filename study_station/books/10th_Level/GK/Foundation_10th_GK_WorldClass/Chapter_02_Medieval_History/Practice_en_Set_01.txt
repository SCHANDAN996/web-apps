1. Who started the construction of the Qutub Minar in Delhi?
(a) Iltutmish (b) Qutb-ud-din Aibak (c) Alauddin Khilji (d) Balban
Answer: (b)
Solution: Iltutmish only completed the upper storeys; the foundation and first storey were laid by Qutb-ud-din Aibak, so the answer is Qutb-ud-din Aibak.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

2. The Iqta system in the Delhi Sultanate was organized by:
(a) Iltutmish (b) Alauddin Khilji (c) Balban (d) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (a)
Solution: Balban and Alauddin only modified later administration; it was Iltutmish who divided the empire into Iqtas assigned to nobles and commanders, so the answer is Iltutmish.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

3. Razia Sultan, the first woman ruler of medieval India, was the daughter of:
(a) Iltutmish (b) Qutb-ud-din Aibak (c) Balban (d) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (a)
Solution: Aibak was her grandfather, not father; Iltutmish nominated his talented daughter Razia as his successor, so the answer is Iltutmish.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

4. The pure silver coin 'Tanka' and copper coin 'Jital' were introduced by:
(a) Alauddin Khilji (b) Sher Shah Suri (c) Akbar (d) Iltutmish
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sher Shah's Rupiya came centuries later; Iltutmish first issued the standardized silver Tanka and copper Jital, so the answer is Iltutmish.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

5. Which Sultan of Delhi was popularly known by the title 'Lakh Baksh' (giver of lakhs)?
(a) Qutb-ud-din Aibak (b) Balban (c) Iltutmish (d) Razia Sultan
Answer: (a)
Solution: The title reflects generosity, not conquest; Qutb-ud-din Aibak was called Lakh Baksh for his lavish charity, so the answer is Qutb-ud-din Aibak.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans

6. The group of forty powerful Turkish nobles known as 'Chalisa' or 'Chahalgani' was organized by:
(a) Balban (b) Iltutmish (c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (d) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (b)
Solution: Balban actually destroyed the Chalisa rather than creating it; Iltutmish formed the corps of forty loyal Turkish slaves, so the answer is Iltutmish.

(a) Diwan-i-Arz (b) Wakil-i-Dar (c) Diwan-i-Insha (d) Shahana-i-Mandi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Diwan-i-Arz headed the military department, not markets; the Shahana-i-Mandi supervised fixed prices and weights, so the answer is Shahana-i-Mandi.

(a) Malik Kafur (b) Ulugh Khan (c) Zafar Khan (d) Nusrat Khan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Zafar Khan fought the Mongols in the north; Malik Kafur led expeditions against Devagiri, Warangal, Dwarasamudra and Madurai, so the answer is Malik Kafur.

9. The ambitious experiment of introducing 'token currency' made of copper and brass was undertaken by:
(a) Alauddin Khilji (b) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (c) Sikandar Lodi (d) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (d)
Solution: Firoz Shah is known for canals, not coinage experiments; Muhammad bin Tughlaq issued token currency that failed due to counterfeiting, so the answer is Muhammad bin Tughlaq.

(a) Akbar (b) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (c) Alauddin Khilji (d) Sher Shah Suri
Answer: (b)
Solution: Akbar and Sher Shah belong to a later century; Muhammad bin Tughlaq moved the court to Daulatabad to control the Deccan, so the answer is Muhammad bin Tughlaq.

11. The Moroccan traveler Ibn Battuta visited India during the reign of which Sultan?
(a) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (b) Alauddin Khilji (c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (d) Akbar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Akbar's famous visitor was the Jesuit mission, not Ibn Battuta; Ibn Battuta reached Delhi in 1333 under Muhammad bin Tughlaq and served as Qazi, so the answer is Muhammad bin Tughlaq.
Source: NCERT Class 12 History Part II, Ch 5 Through the Eyes of Travellers

12. Which Sultan of Delhi established canals for irrigation and founded several new cities including Firozabad and Hisar?
(a) Akbar (b) Aurangzeb (c) Alauddin Khilji (d) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Answer: (d)
Solution: Alauddin focused on markets and conquest, not canals; Firoz Shah Tughlaq built irrigation canals and founded Hisar, Firozabad and Jaunpur, so the answer is Firoz Shah Tughlaq.

14. The First Battle of Panipat in 1526 was fought between:
(a) Babur and Ibrahim Lodi (b) Akbar and Hemu (c) Humayun and Sher Shah (d) Babur and Rana Sanga
Answer: (a)
Solution: Babur versus Rana Sanga was Khanwa in 1527; at Panipat in 1526 Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi using artillery, so the answer is Babur and Ibrahim Lodi.

21. Which of the following statements about Akbar's 'Din-i-Ilahi' is INCORRECT?
(a) Most orthodox Muslims eagerly embraced and joined it (b) Birbal was one of its few adherents (c) It was an ethical-philosophical moral code without a prophet or scripture (d) It was introduced by Akbar in 1582
Answer: (a)
Solution: Din-i-Ilahi had only a handful of followers such as Birbal, and orthodox nobles and clergy largely rejected it; the claim that most orthodox Muslims joined it is false, so the incorrect statement is the one about eager mass acceptance.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 4 The Mughal Empire

22. Match List-I (Monument) with List-II (Builder):
A. Alai Darwaza – 1. Akbar
B. Buland Darwaza – 2. Shah Jahan
C. Red Fort (Delhi) – 3. Alauddin Khilji
D. Qutub Minar (completion) – 4. Iltutmish
(a) A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4 (b) A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4 (c) A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4 (d) A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4

Solution: The trap is swapping the two Khilji and Mughal monuments; Alai Darwaza was built by Alauddin Khilji, Buland Darwaza by Akbar, the Red Fort of Delhi by Shah Jahan, and the Qutub Minar was completed by Iltutmish, so the correct matching is A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4.

25. Statement [A]: Alauddin Khilji introduced the Dagh and Chehra systems in his army.
Reason [R]: Dagh was the branding of horses to prevent substitution and corruption in the cavalry.
(a) A is false, but R is true (b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A (c) A is true, but R is false (d) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming the branding relates to soldiers; Dagh meant branding horses and Chehra meant descriptive rolls of soldiers, both introduced by Alauddin to stop proxy musters, so both statements are true and the reason correctly explains the statement.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History Our Pasts-II, Ch 3 The Delhi Sultans
