76. What is the motto of the Asian Games?
(a) Ever Onward (b) Citius, Altius, Fortius (c) Unity in Diversity (d) Play True
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Asian Games motto is "Ever Onward". Trap: "Citius, Altius, Fortius" is the Olympic motto.
Source: Olympic Council of Asia
