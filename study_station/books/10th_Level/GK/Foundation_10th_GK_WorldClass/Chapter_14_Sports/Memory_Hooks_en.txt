🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks

🧠 Mnemonic 1: Small team sizes
Sentence: "Polo has 4 legs, Basket has 5 fingers, Volley has 6 strings, Kabaddi has 7 days"
Polo 4 → a horse has 4 legs | Basketball 5 → 5 fingers on the hand that holds the ball | Volleyball 6 | Kabaddi 7 → 7 days of the week.

🧠 Mnemonic 2: The 11 club
"CFH = Cricket, Football, Hockey — Come For Hundred" — all three are played 11 a side.

🧠 Mnemonic 3: Badminton team cups
"TUS for shuttle" — Thomas (men), Uber (women), Sudirman (mixed).
Visual: a shuttlecock with "T-U-S" written on its three feathers.

🧠 Mnemonic 4: Football cups of India
"DSS kicks the ball" — Durand Cup (1888, Shimla), Santosh Trophy (state teams), Subroto Cup (schools).

🧠 Mnemonic 5: Hockey cups
"Beighton and Aga Khan hold hockey sticks" — both the Beighton Cup and the Aga Khan Cup are hockey.
Trap check: the Nehru Trophy of Kerala is a snake-boat race, not hockey.

🧠 Mnemonic 6: Tennis vs Table tennis team cups
"Davis Drives the tennis car; Swaythling Swings the table-tennis bat" — Davis Cup = tennis (men); Swaythling Cup = table tennis (men).

🧠 Mnemonic 7: National sports awards
"Guru gets Drona, Shishya gets Arjuna, the Ratna goes to the best, the University gets MAKA."
Years in birth order: "61–85–91" → Arjuna 1961, Dronacharya 1985, Khel Ratna 1991–92.

🧠 Mnemonic 8: India's Olympic "firsts"
"Pritchard Plays, Jadhav Jumps, Malleswari Moves, Bindra Bangs, Neeraj Nails it"
Pritchard 1900 (first medals) → Jadhav 1952 (first individual medal of independent India) → Malleswari 2000 (first woman) → Bindra 2008 (first individual gold) → Neeraj 2021 (first athletics gold).

🧠 Mnemonic 9: Eight hockey golds
"28-32-36, 48-52-56, 64, 80"
Read it as two runs of three Games (4 years apart), then two single shots: 1928, 1932, 1936 | 1948, 1952, 1956 | 1964 | 1980.

🧠 Mnemonic 10: Olympic ring colours
"Blue Yak Bites Green Rice" — Blue, Yellow, Black, Green, Red.

🧠 Mnemonic 11: Headquarters
"Zurich kicks, Dubai bats, Lausanne lights the flame" — FIFA in Zurich, ICC in Dubai, IOC in Lausanne.
"Kuala Lumpur smashes" — BWF (badminton) in Kuala Lumpur.

🧠 Mnemonic 12: Odd terms
"Chukker on a horse, Puck on the ice, Scrum in the mud, Snatch on the bar"
Polo, ice hockey, rugby, weightlifting.

🧠 Mnemonic 13: Mascots of Indian-hosted Games
"Appu the elephant came first, Shera the tiger came later" — Appu: 1982 Asian Games; Shera: 2010 Commonwealth Games.

🧠 Mnemonic 14: National Sports Day
"29 August — the Wizard's birthday" — Major Dhyan Chand, born 29 August 1905.

⏭️ Skip Strategy

1. If a "match the following" lists four trophies of sports you have never studied (rare regional cups) and you are not sure of at least two pairs, skip it in the first round — elimination works only with two sure pairs.

2. If a question asks for an exact medal count, distance or score of a recent event and the options are close to each other (for example 104, 106, 107, 109), skip unless you learnt that number with its year.

3. If a question asks "who won X in year Y" for a minor tournament you have never read about, do not spend more than 15 seconds — mark it for review and move on; the easy cup–sport questions are worth the same marks.
