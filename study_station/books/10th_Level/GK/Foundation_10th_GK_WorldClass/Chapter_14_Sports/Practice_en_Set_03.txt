51. Where is the headquarters of the International Olympic Committee (IOC)?
(a) Lausanne (b) Paris (c) Geneva (d) Athens
Answer: (a)
Solution: The IOC has its headquarters at Lausanne, Switzerland. Trap: Geneva is also in Switzerland, and Athens hosted the first modern Games, but neither is the IOC headquarters.
Source: Olympics.com

52. In which year was the International Olympic Committee founded?
(a) 1894 (b) 1896 (c) 1900 (d) 1920
Answer: (a)
Solution: The IOC was founded in 1894, two years before the first modern Games. Trap: 1896 is the year of the first modern Olympics (Athens). So the IOC dates from 1894.
Source: Olympics.com

53. The modern Olympic Games were started through the efforts of:
(a) Pierre de Coubertin (b) James Naismith (c) Guru Dutt Sondhi (d) William G. Morgan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pierre de Coubertin of France led the revival of the Olympics in 1896. Trap: Guru Dutt Sondhi is linked with the Asian Games; Naismith and Morgan invented basketball and volleyball.
Source: Olympics.com

54. The ancient Olympic Games were held at Olympia in honour of:
(a) Zeus (b) Apollo (c) Athena (d) Poseidon
Answer: (a)
Solution: The ancient Olympics were held at Olympia, Greece, from 776 BC in honour of Zeus.
Source: Olympics.com

55. In 2021 the IOC added a new word to the Olympic motto "Citius, Altius, Fortius". What does the added word "Communiter" mean?
(a) Together (b) Peace (c) Friendship (d) Unity in diversity
Answer: (a)
Solution: On 20 July 2021 the IOC added "Communiter", meaning "Together". The original three words mean Faster, Higher, Stronger.
Source: Olympics.com

56. Olympic Day is observed every year on:

57. Which of the following colours is NOT one of the five Olympic rings?
(a) Blue (b) Orange (c) Yellow (d) Green
Answer: (b)
Solution: The five rings are blue, yellow, black, green and red, on a white flag. Orange is not one of them.
Source: Olympics.com

58. The Olympic flag with five rings was first flown at the Olympic Games of:
(a) 1896 Athens (b) 1920 Antwerp (c) 1912 Stockholm (d) 1936 Berlin
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Olympic flag was first flown at the 1920 Antwerp Games. Trap: 1936 Berlin is remembered for the first Olympic torch relay, not the first flag.
Source: Olympics.com

59. The first Olympic torch relay was held for the Games of:
(a) 1908 London (b) 1936 Berlin (c) 1920 Antwerp (d) 1948 London
Answer: (b)
Solution: The first torch relay was held for the 1936 Berlin Games. The flame is lit at Olympia with the Sun's rays.
Source: Olympics.com

60. Women athletes took part in the modern Olympics for the first time at:
(a) 1896 Athens (b) 1900 Paris (c) 1908 London (d) 1924 Paris
Answer: (b)
Solution: Women first competed at the 1900 Paris Games. Trap: 1896 Athens was the first modern Games, but it had no women athletes.
Source: Olympics.com

61. The Summer Olympics were not held in some years because of the World Wars. In which one of the following years WERE the Games held?
(a) 1916 (b) 1948 (c) 1944 (d) 1940
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Games were cancelled in 1916, 1940 and 1944. They were held in 1948 at London.
Source: Olympics.com

62. Which was the first city to host the Summer Olympic Games three times?
(a) Paris (b) London (c) Athens (d) Los Angeles
Answer: (b)
Solution: London hosted in 1908, 1948 and 2012, becoming the first city to do so three times. Trap: Paris also hosted three times (1900, 1924, 2024), but reached three only in 2024.
Source: Olympics.com
