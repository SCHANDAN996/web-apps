🃏 Flashcard Deck: Sports & Competitions (Foundation)

Card 1
Front: India waited over a hundred years for an Olympic athletics gold. Who won it, where, and in which event?
Back: Neeraj Chopra — javelin throw, Tokyo 2020 Olympics (final on 7 August 2021).

Card 2
Front: Three popular games share the same team size. Which games, and what size?
Back: Cricket, football and hockey — 11 players a side.

Card 3
Front: The smallest common team in exam questions rides horses. Which game, and how many players?
Back: Polo — 4 players a side.

Card 4
Front: Mnemonic check: what does "TUS for shuttle" stand for?
Back: The three badminton team cups — Thomas Cup (men), Uber Cup (women), Sudirman Cup (mixed).

Card 5
Front: Mnemonic check: "Guru gets Drona, Shishya gets Arjuna" — what does it tell you?
Back: The Dronacharya Award is for coaches; the Arjuna Award is for players.

Card 6
Front: Examiner's trap: "Neeraj Chopra won India's first individual Olympic gold." True or false?
Back: False. The first individual gold was Abhinav Bindra's (2008, 10 m air rifle). Neeraj won the first athletics gold (Almost True Statement).

Card 7
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Tokyo 2020 Olympics were held in 2020." What is wrong?
Back: They were held in 2021 (because of COVID-19), but kept the name "Tokyo 2020" (Chronological Confusion).

Card 8
Front: Where and when did the modern Olympics begin, and who is called their father?
Back: Athens, 1896 — Pierre de Coubertin of France. The IOC (1894) is based at Lausanne.

Card 9
Front: Which word was added to the Olympic motto in 2021?
Back: "Communiter" — "Together". The motto is now "Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together".

Card 10
Front: How many times has India won Olympic gold in hockey, and when was the last one?
Back: 8 times — the last in 1980 at Moscow (the first in 1928 at Amsterdam).

Card 11
Front: Which city hosted the first Asian Games, and in which year?
Back: New Delhi, 1951. India hosted the Asian Games again in 1982 (mascot Appu).

Card 12
Front: Which is India's highest sporting honour, when did it start, and who received it first?
Back: Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna — started 1991–92; first awardee Viswanathan Anand. Renamed from Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna in August 2021.

Card 13
Front: Why is 29 August special in Indian sport?
Back: It is National Sports Day — the birthday of hockey wizard Major Dhyan Chand. The Fit India Movement was launched on this day in 2019.

Card 14
Front: Is hockey officially India's national game?
Back: No. In an RTI reply (January 2020), the Sports Ministry said no sport has been declared the national game.

Card 15
Front: Chukker, puck, scrum, raid — name the game for each word.
Back: Polo, ice hockey, rugby, kabaddi.

Card 16
Front: Durand Cup, Santosh Trophy, Subroto Cup — what do they have in common?
Back: All are football tournaments. The Durand Cup (1888, Shimla) is India's oldest football tournament.

Card 17
Front: Which Indian athlete won two medals at a single Olympic Games, and where?
Back: Manu Bhaker — two bronze medals in shooting at Paris 2024 (individual 10 m air pistol, and mixed team with Sarabjot Singh).

Card 18
Front: FIFA, ICC, IOC — where is the headquarters of each?
Back: FIFA – Zurich; ICC – Dubai; IOC – Lausanne.
