Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Arrange the following in the order in which they began:
I. Durand Cup
II. First FIFA World Cup
III. Wimbledon Championship
IV. Modern Olympic Games
(a) III, I, IV, II (b) I, III, IV, II (c) III, IV, I, II (d) IV, III, I, II
Answer: (a)
Solution: Wimbledon 1877, Durand Cup 1888, modern Olympics 1896, first FIFA World Cup 1930. Trap: the Olympics feel "oldest", but Wimbledon and the Durand Cup started before 1896.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Consider the following statements:
I. India has won the Olympic gold medal in hockey eight times.
II. India's last Olympic gold medal in hockey came in 1975.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (a)
Solution: India won eight hockey golds, the last in 1980 (Moscow). Statement II is wrong: 1975 is the year India won the Hockey World Cup, not an Olympic gold.
Source: Olympics.com

128. In which one of the following years did India NOT win the Olympic gold medal in hockey?
(a) 1928 (b) 1960 (c) 1964 (d) 1980
Answer: (b)
Solution: India's eight hockey golds came in 1928, 1932, 1936, 1948, 1952, 1956, 1964 and 1980. 1960 is not in the list. Trap: 1960 falls between two gold years, so it looks like part of the run.
Source: Olympics.com

129. Which of the following athletes did NOT win a medal for India at the Paris 2024 Olympics?
(a) Swapnil Kusale (b) Aman Sehrawat (c) Sarabjot Singh (d) Mirabai Chanu
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kusale (50 m rifle 3 positions), Sehrawat (wrestling) and Sarabjot Singh (mixed team pistol, with Manu Bhaker) won bronze at Paris 2024. Mirabai Chanu's Olympic medal was the silver at Tokyo 2020.
Source: Olympics.com

130. At the Paris 2024 Paralympics, India won 29 medals, its best ever. How many of these were gold?
(a) 5 (b) 7 (c) 9 (d) 13
Answer: (b)
Solution: India won 7 gold, 9 silver and 13 bronze = 29 medals. Trap: 9 is the number of silver and 13 of bronze medals. So the answer is 7 gold medals.
Source: PIB

134. In December 2024, D. Gukesh became the youngest world chess champion. Where was the World Championship match held?
(a) Singapore (b) Budapest (c) Chennai (d) Dubai

Solution: Gukesh won the title in Singapore in December 2024, becoming the second Indian world champion after Viswanathan Anand. Trap: Budapest hosted the 2024 Chess Olympiad, where India won both gold medals.
Source: FIDE (fide.com)

137. Consider the following statements about the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026:

138. In which year did India win the Thomas Cup (badminton) for the first time?

143. Which of the following golf terms is correctly matched with its meaning?
(a) Birdie – one stroke over par (b) Eagle – two strokes under par (c) Bogey – two strokes under par (d) Birdie – two strokes over par
Answer: (b)
Solution: Eagle = 2 under par, birdie = 1 under par, bogey = 1 over par. Trap: birdie and bogey are often swapped.
Source: PYQ-style

144. Which one of the following terms is used in cricket, football and hockey?

146. The game of chess developed in ancient India under which name?
(a) Chaupar (b) Chaturanga (c) Pachisi (d) Ganjifa

149. India hosted the Commonwealth Games at Delhi in 2010. What was India's result?
(a) 101 medals, second place (b) 107 medals, first place (c) 101 medals, third place (d) 66 medals, second place

Solution: India won 101 medals and finished second at Delhi 2010. Trap: 107 is India's tally at the Hangzhou Asian Games (held 2023).
Source: Commonwealth Sport

150. Consider the following statements:
I. Prakash Padukone was the first Indian to win the All England Badminton Championship (1980).
II. Bachendri Pal was the first Indian woman to climb Mount Everest (1984).
III. Mihir Sen was the first Indian to swim across the English Channel (1958).
Which of the statements given are correct?
(a) I and II only (b) II and III only (c) I and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (d)
Solution: All three statements are correct: Padukone 1980, Bachendri Pal 1984, Mihir Sen 1958. Trap: such "first Indian" lists tempt you to assume one must be wrong.
