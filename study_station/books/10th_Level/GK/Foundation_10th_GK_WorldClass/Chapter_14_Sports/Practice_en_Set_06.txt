126. Arrange the following in the order in which they began:
I. Durand Cup
II. First FIFA World Cup
III. Wimbledon Championship
IV. Modern Olympic Games
(a) I, III, IV, II (b) III, I, IV, II (c) III, IV, I, II (d) IV, III, I, II
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Olympics feel "oldest", but Wimbledon (1877) and the Durand Cup (1888) both started before the modern Olympics (1896); the first FIFA World Cup came in 1930. So the correct order is III, I, IV, II.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Consider the following statements:
I. India has won the Olympic gold medal in hockey eight times.
II. India's last Olympic gold medal in hockey came in 1975.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Neither I nor II (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Only I
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1975 is the tempting trap — that is the year India won the Hockey World Cup, not an Olympic gold. India's eighth and last Olympic hockey gold came at Moscow in 1980, so only statement I is correct.
Source: Olympics.com

128. In which one of the following years did India NOT win the Olympic gold medal in hockey?
(a) 1980 (b) 1928 (c) 1964 (d) 1960
Answer: (d)
Solution: India's eight hockey golds came in 1928, 1932, 1936, 1948, 1952, 1956, 1964 and 1980. The trap is that 1960 falls between two gold years, so it looks like part of the run — but at Rome 1960 India took silver. So the answer is 1960.
Source: Olympics.com

129. Which of the following athletes did NOT win a medal for India at the Paris 2024 Olympics?
(a) Aman Sehrawat (b) Mirabai Chanu (c) Sarabjot Singh (d) Swapnil Kusale
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kusale (50 m rifle 3 positions), Sehrawat (wrestling) and Sarabjot Singh (mixed team pistol, with Manu Bhaker) all won bronze at Paris 2024. The trap is Mirabai Chanu, a famous medallist — but her Olympic medal was the silver at Tokyo 2020, not Paris. So the answer is Mirabai Chanu.
Source: Olympics.com

130. At the Paris 2024 Paralympics, India won 29 medals, its best ever. How many of these were gold?
(a) 9 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 13
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap options are the other two counts from the same tally — 9 silver and 13 bronze. India's 29 medals at Paris 2024 broke down as 7 gold, 9 silver and 13 bronze, so the answer is 7 gold medals.
Source: PIB

131. Murlikant Petkar won India's first Paralympic gold medal in 1972 in:
(a) Athletics (b) Shooting (c) Swimming (d) Archery
Answer: (c)
Solution: Murlikant Petkar, an army veteran, won India's first Paralympic gold in swimming at Heidelberg 1972. Shooting is a tempting trap because later Indian Paralympic stars like Avani Lekhara won in shooting, but the answer is swimming.
Source: PIB

132. The first Paralympic Games were held in 1960 at:
(a) London (b) Seoul (c) Tokyo (d) Rome
Answer: (d)
Solution: London is the trap because the 1948 Stoke Mandeville Games in England were the forerunner of the Paralympics. But the first official Paralympic Games were held at Rome in 1960, so the answer is Rome.
Source: Olympics.com

133. The National Sports Awards 2024 were presented on 17 January 2025. Who among the following did NOT receive the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna in that list?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The Khel Ratna 2024 went to Manu Bhaker, D. Gukesh, Harmanpreet Singh and para-athlete Praveen Kumar. Neeraj Chopra is the trap — he is India's biggest track-and-field star, but he had already received the Khel Ratna in 2021. So the answer is Neeraj Chopra.

134. In December 2024, D. Gukesh became the youngest world chess champion. Where was the World Championship match held?
(a) Budapest (b) Singapore (c) Chennai (d) Dubai
Answer: (b)
Solution: Budapest is the trap — it hosted the 2024 Chess Olympiad, where India won both team gold medals. But Gukesh won the World Championship match against Ding Liren in Singapore in December 2024, so the answer is Singapore.

135. Who was the first Indian to win an individual Olympic gold medal?

Source: Olympics.com

138. In which year did India win the Thomas Cup (badminton) for the first time?
(a) 2022 (b) 1980 (c) 2014 (d) 2024
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1980 is the trap — that is when Prakash Padukone won the All England Championship. India won the Thomas Cup, the men's world team championship, for the first time in 2022, beating Indonesia, so the answer is 2022.

139. The Ranji Trophy, India's premier domestic first-class competition, is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Football (c) Cricket (d) Tennis
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Ranji Trophy, named after the cricketer Ranjitsinhji, is India's first-class cricket championship, begun in 1934–35. Football is a distractor because of the Santosh and Durand trophies, but the answer is cricket.

141. The Dronacharya Award is given to:
(a) Outstanding sportspersons for lifetime achievement (b) Sports journalists (c) Universities for promoting sports (d) Outstanding coaches of sportspersons and teams
Answer: (d)
Solution: Lifetime achievement is the trap — that describes the lifetime category of the Arjuna Award (and the Khel Ratna honours players). The Dronacharya Award, named after the guru of the Pandavas, honours outstanding coaches, so the answer is coaches.

143. Which of the following golf terms is correctly matched with its meaning?
(a) Birdie – one stroke over par (b) Bogey – two strokes under par (c) Eagle – two strokes under par (d) Birdie – two strokes over par
Answer: (c)

146. The game of chess developed in ancient India under which name?
(a) Chaturanga (b) Ganjifa (c) Pachisi (d) Chaupar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chaupar and Pachisi are the trap — they are ancient Indian board games too, but they are race games with dice, not the ancestor of chess. Chess developed in India as Chaturanga, around the Gupta period, so the answer is Chaturanga.
