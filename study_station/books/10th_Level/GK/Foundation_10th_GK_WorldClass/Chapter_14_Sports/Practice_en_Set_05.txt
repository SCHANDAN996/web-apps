Set 5/6 – Sports & Competitions MCQs (Foundation)
Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard

101. The Sports Authority of India (SAI) was set up in 1984, after which event?
(a) 1951 Asian Games (b) 1982 Asian Games (c) 1983 Cricket World Cup (d) 2010 Commonwealth Games
Answer: (b)
Solution: SAI was set up in 1984, after India hosted the 1982 Asian Games in New Delhi. Trap: 1951 is too early and 2010 is long after SAI was formed. So SAI came after the 1982 Asian Games.
Source: Sports Authority of India

102. The Netaji Subhas National Institute of Sports (NIS) is located at:
(a) Gwalior (b) Patiala (c) Bengaluru (d) Imphal
Answer: (b)
Solution: NIS is at Patiala, Punjab. Trap: Gwalior has LNIPE and Imphal has the National Sports University.
Source: Sports Authority of India

103. The Lakshmibai National Institute of Physical Education (LNIPE) is located at:
(a) Jhansi (b) Gwalior (c) Patiala (d) Bhopal
Answer: (b)
Solution: LNIPE is at Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh. Trap: the name "Lakshmibai" makes students think of Jhansi.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports

104. India's National Sports University is located in:
(a) Imphal, Manipur (b) Patiala, Punjab (c) Gandhinagar, Gujarat (d) Shillong, Meghalaya
Answer: (a)
Solution: The National Sports University is at Imphal, Manipur. Trap: Patiala has the NIS, not the university.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports

105. The headquarters of the Badminton World Federation (BWF) is in:
(a) Jakarta (b) Kuala Lumpur (c) Copenhagen (d) London
Answer: (b)
Solution: BWF is headquartered at Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Trap: London is the headquarters of the International Tennis Federation (ITF).
Source: BWF (bwfbadminton.com)

106. The headquarters of World Athletics is in:
(a) Monaco (b) Lausanne (c) Zurich (d) Paris
Answer: (a)
Solution: World Athletics is based in Monaco. Trap: Lausanne is home to the IOC, FIH and FIDE; Zurich to FIFA.
Source: World Athletics

107. Where is the headquarters of the International Cricket Council (ICC)?
(a) London (b) Mumbai (c) Melbourne (d) Dubai
Answer: (d)
Solution: The ICC is headquartered in Dubai. Trap: Mumbai is the BCCI headquarters, and Lord's in London is the "Home of Cricket" but not the ICC office.
Source: ICC (icc-cricket.com)

108. The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) was founded in 1928. Where is its headquarters?

121. Arrange these Olympic medal wins by Indians in chronological order:
I. Karnam Malleswari – bronze, weightlifting
II. K. D. Jadhav – bronze, wrestling
III. Rajyavardhan Singh Rathore – silver, shooting
IV. Leander Paes – bronze, tennis
(a) II, IV, I, III (b) II, I, IV, III (c) IV, II, I, III (d) II, IV, III, I
Answer: (a)
Solution: Jadhav 1952, Paes 1996, Malleswari 2000, Rathore 2004. Trap: Paes (1996) came before Malleswari (2000), even though she is remembered as a "first".
Source: Olympics.com

122. Consider the following statements:
I. Sushil Kumar won two Olympic medals at a single Olympic Games.
II. At Paris 2024, Manu Bhaker became the first athlete of independent India to win two medals at a single Olympic Games.
Which of the statements given is/are correct?
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both I and II (d) Neither I nor II
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sushil Kumar won two medals but at two different Games (bronze 2008, silver 2012), so I is wrong. Manu Bhaker won two bronzes at Paris 2024 (10 m air pistol and the mixed team event with Sarabjot Singh), so II is right.
Source: Olympics.com

Answer: (a)
Solution: Kapil Dev – "Straight from the Heart"; Mary Kom – "Unbreakable"; Abhinav Bindra – "A Shot at History"; Milkha Singh – "The Race of My Life". Start with the two pairs you are sure of and eliminate options.

124. Which of the following pairs of sports body and headquarters is NOT correctly matched?
(a) FIH (hockey) – Lausanne (b) FIDE (chess) – Lausanne (c) ITF (tennis) – London (d) Olympic Council of Asia – Doha
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Olympic Council of Asia is headquartered in Kuwait, not Doha. The other three pairs are correct.
Source: Olympic Council of Asia

125. Consider the following statements about the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna:
I. It was instituted in 1991–92, and its first recipient was Viswanathan Anand.
II. It received its present name on 6 August 2021.
III. It is the oldest of India's national sports awards.
Which of the statements given are correct?
(a) I and II only (b) II and III only (c) I and III only (d) I, II and III
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statements I and II are correct. Statement III is wrong: the Arjuna Award (1961) is older than the Khel Ratna (1991–92).
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports
