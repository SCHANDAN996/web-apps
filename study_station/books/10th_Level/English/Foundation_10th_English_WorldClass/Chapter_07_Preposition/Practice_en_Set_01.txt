1. The cat is sitting ___ the table.
(a) on (b) in (c) at (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In" suggests inside something and "at" suggests a point, which do not fit a surface. "On" is used for a surface, so the answer is on.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

2. She was born ___ 15th August 2005.
(a) in (b) on (c) at (d) by
Answer: (b)
Solution: "In" is used with months and years, which tempts many students, but a specific date always takes "on". So the answer is on.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

3. We go to school ___ bus.
(a) on (b) with (c) in (d) by
Answer: (d)
Solution: "With" shows company, not transport. For the means of transport we use "by" without an article, so the answer is by.

24. He is junior ___ me by two years.

Answer: (b)
Solution: "Than" is the strong trap because Hindi speakers translate "mujhse chhota" as a comparison with "than". Words like junior, senior, superior and inferior take "to", so the answer is to.

Answer: (c)
Solution: "Work" tempts students who treat "despite" like a conjunction, but "despite" is a preposition and must be followed by a noun or gerund. The gerund form is correct, so the answer is working.
