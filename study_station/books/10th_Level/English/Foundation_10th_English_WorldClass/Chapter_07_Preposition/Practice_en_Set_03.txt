51. She has been living in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) from (b) for (c) since (d) at
Answer: (c)
Solution: "For" is tempting because it also shows time, but "for" is used with a period of time (for ten years), while "since" is used with a point of time. 2015 is a fixed starting point, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. The cat is hiding ______ the table.
(a) on (b) under (c) in (d) at
Answer: (b)
Solution: "On" would mean the cat is on top of the table, which contradicts "hiding". "Under" correctly shows position below the table, so the answer is under.
Source: PYQ-style

53. He is fond ______ playing cricket.
(a) for (b) in (c) with (d) of
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Fond in" sounds possible to Hindi speakers because of "में रुचि", but the fixed adjective-preposition pair is "fond of". So the answer is of.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

54. The train arrived ______ time.
(a) on (b) in (c) at (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In time" means before the deadline (with time to spare), but "on time" means exactly at the scheduled time, which suits a train's arrival. So the answer is on.
Source: PYQ-style

55. We go to school ______ bus.
(a) on (b) with (c) by (d) in
Answer: (c)
Solution: "With bus" is wrong because the instrument of travel takes "by" when no article is used: by bus, by train, by air. So the answer is by.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

56. The book belongs ______ me.
(a) with (b) for (c) of (d) to
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Belong of" is a Hindi-speaker error (मेरा है); the verb "belong" is always followed by "to". So the answer is to.
Source: PYQ-style

57. He jumped ______ the river to save the child.
(a) into (b) in (c) on (d) upon
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In" shows position (already inside), but the sentence describes movement from outside to inside the river, which needs "into". So the answer is into.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

58. My birthday falls ______ Monday this year.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "In" is used with months and years, but days of the week always take "on". So the answer is on.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

59. She is afraid ______ spiders.
(a) from (b) by (c) with (d) of
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Afraid from" is a common Hindi-speaker error (डरती है से); the correct fixed pair is "afraid of". So the answer is of.
Source: PYQ-style

60. The meeting will start ______ 10 a.m.
(a) on (b) at (c) in (d) by
Answer: (b)
Solution: "On" is for days and "in" is for months or years; exact clock time always takes "at". So the answer is at.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

61. He has been suffering ______ fever since Monday.
(a) from (b) with (c) by (d) of
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Suffering with" tempts learners because "with" shows accompaniment, but the verb "suffer" takes "from" before a disease or problem. So the answer is from.
Source: PYQ-style

62. The teacher was angry ______ the boy for his misbehaviour.
(a) on (b) at (c) with (d) upon
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Angry at" is used with things or actions, but with a person the correct preposition is "with". The boy is a person, so the answer is with.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

63. Spot the error: Part P: He is junior / Part Q: than me / Part R: by two years. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "than", which sounds natural after a comparison, but "junior" is a Latin comparative and takes "to", not "than" — "junior to me". The error lies in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

64. I prefer tea ______ coffee.
(a) to (b) over (c) than (d) from
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Than" is the tempting wrong choice because "prefer" shows comparison, but "prefer" is followed by "to", never "than". So the answer is to.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

65. The police are looking ______ the case.
(a) into (b) after (c) at (d) for
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Looking for" means searching for something lost, which misleads many, but investigating a case is "look into". So the answer is into.
Source: PYQ-style

66. He apologized ______ his rude behaviour.
(a) about (b) on (c) at (d) for
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Apologized about" sounds conversational, but the standard pattern is "apologize for something" (and "apologize to somebody"). So the answer is for.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

67. The two brothers divided the property ______ themselves.
(a) among (b) between (c) in (d) within
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Among" tempts because the property is being shared, but "between" is used for two persons and "among" for more than two. There are two brothers, so the answer is between.
Source: PYQ-style

68. She congratulated me ______ my success.
(a) for (b) at (c) on (d) about
Answer: (c)
Solution: "For" is tempting because Hindi uses "के लिए", but the fixed pattern is "congratulate somebody on something". So the answer is on.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

69. Spot the error: Part P: Despite of / Part Q: the heavy rain, / Part R: the match continued. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "Despite of", which learners form by mixing "despite" with "in spite of". "Despite" never takes "of"; the correct phrase is "Despite the heavy rain". The error lies in Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

70. He is eligible ______ this post.
(a) to (b) of (c) in (d) for
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Eligible to" is used before a verb (eligible to apply), but before a noun like "this post" the correct preposition is "for". So the answer is for.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

71. The child was run ______ by a speeding car.
(a) over (b) down (c) into (d) after
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Run down" means to criticise or to knock down, but the standard phrasal verb for a vehicle knocking someone down is "run over". So the answer is over.
Source: PYQ-style

72. We should abide ______ the rules of the school.
(a) by (b) to (c) with (d) on
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Abide with" means to stay with someone, which misleads learners; "abide by" means to obey rules or decisions. So the answer is by.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

73. He died ______ cancer last year.
(a) from (b) by (c) of (d) with
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Died from" is used for external causes like injuries, but death due to a disease takes "of" — died of cancer. So the answer is of.
Source: PYQ-style

74. The manager dealt ______ the complaint firmly.
(a) in (b) with (c) about (d) on
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Dealt in" means to trade in goods (deals in cars), which is the trap here; handling a complaint is "deal with". So the answer is with.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

75. I have been waiting for you ______ two hours.
(a) since (b) for (c) from (d) at
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Since" is the tempting trap because the sentence is in perfect continuous tense, but "since" needs a point of time (since 5 o'clock) while "two hours" is a duration, which takes "for". So the answer is for.
Source: PYQ-style
