26. The train arrived ______ time.
(a) in (b) by (c) at (d) on
Answer: (d)
Solution: "In time" means before a deadline, which tempts many students, but the fixed phrase for punctuality is "on time". The train was punctual, so the answer is on.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

27. She has been living in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) since (b) from (c) for (d) at
Answer: (a)
Solution: "For" is used with a period of time, not a starting point, so it is the trap here. "Since" is used with a point of time like 2015, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

28. The cat is hiding ______ the table.
(a) on (b) above (c) under (d) over
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Above" and "over" suggest a higher position, which does not fit hiding. A cat hiding beneath a table takes "under", so the answer is under.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

29. He is fond ______ playing cricket.
(a) of (b) in (c) with (d) at
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Fond" is always followed by the fixed preposition "of"; "fond in" or "fond with" are common Hindi-influenced errors. The correct phrase is fond of, so the answer is of.

30. We go to school ______ bus.
(a) on (b) with (c) by (d) in
Answer: (c)
Solution: "On bus" tempts students who think of sitting on it, but for the mode of transport without an article we use "by". The correct phrase is by bus, so the answer is by.

31. The book belongs ______ me.
(a) with (b) for (c) to (d) of
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Belong" takes the fixed preposition "to"; "belong with" is a tempting but wrong choice in this sense of ownership. The correct phrase is belongs to me, so the answer is to.

32. He jumped ______ the river to save the child.
(a) into (b) in (c) on (d) at
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In" shows position, but the trap is that movement from outside to inside needs "into". He moved into the river, so the answer is into.

(a) in (b) on (c) at (d) by

35. The meeting will start ______ 10 a.m.
(a) at (b) in (c) on (d) by
Answer: (a)

(a) for (b) from (c) since (d) by

38. I am interested ______ learning French.
(a) for (b) at (c) in (d) with
Answer: (c)

39. The children are playing ______ the park.
(a) on (b) in (c) at (d) by
Answer: (b)
Solution: "At the park" is possible for a point location, but playing inside an open enclosed area naturally takes "in". The best answer is in, so the answer is in.

40. He died ______ cancer.
(a) from (b) of (c) by (d) with
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Die from" is used for external causes like injuries, which tempts students, but diseases take "die of". The correct phrase is died of cancer, so the answer is of.

41. Spot the error: He is senior (P) / than me (Q) / by two years. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "than", which feels natural after a comparative-sounding word, but "senior" is a Latin comparative that takes "to", not "than". Part Q should read "to me", so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

42. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is good in mathematics. (b) He is good with mathematics. (c) He is good at mathematics. (d) He is good for mathematics.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Good in" is a common Hindi-influenced error and the main trap. Skill at a subject takes "good at", so the answer is He is good at mathematics.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

43. The bridge was built ______ the river Ganga.
(a) on (b) above (c) over (d) upon
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Above" only means higher than, without covering, which is the trap. A bridge spanning across a river takes "over", so the answer is over.

44. She has been suffering ______ fever since Monday.
(a) from (b) by (c) with (d) of
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Suffering with" tempts students translating from Hindi, but the fixed verb-preposition pair is "suffer from". The correct phrase is suffering from fever, so the answer is from.

45. Spot the error: Despite of (P) / the heavy rain, (Q) / the match continued. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "despite of", which students form by mixing "despite" with "in spite of". "Despite" never takes "of", so Part P is wrong and the answer is Part P.

46. He travelled sixty kilometres ______ two hours.
(a) since (b) for (c) at (d) in
Answer: (d)
Solution: "For two hours" states duration, but the sentence expresses time taken to complete the journey, which needs "in". The correct preposition is in, so the answer is in.

47. The teacher was angry ______ the boy's behaviour.
(a) with (b) on (c) upon (d) at
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Angry with" is used for a person, which is the trap here because the object is a thing, "behaviour". For things we say "angry at", so the answer is at.

48. Choose the correctly framed question.
(a) What you are doing? (b) What you doing are? (c) What doing you are? (d) What are you doing?
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is keeping the Hindi word order "What you are doing?", which drops the inversion English questions need. In English the helping verb comes before the subject, so the answer is What are you doing?
Source: Linguistic Bridge — common Hindi-speaker error

49. He is junior ______ me in service.
(a) than (b) to (c) from (d) with
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Than" is the strong trap because "junior" sounds comparative, but Latin comparatives like junior, senior, superior and inferior take "to". The correct phrase is junior to me, so the answer is to.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

50. The flight departs ______ 6 o'clock ______ the morning.
(a) at, in (b) in, at (c) on, in (d) at, on
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is reversing the pair: "in" never goes with exact clock time and "at" never goes with parts of the day. Exact time takes "at" and "the morning" takes "in", so the answer is at, in.
