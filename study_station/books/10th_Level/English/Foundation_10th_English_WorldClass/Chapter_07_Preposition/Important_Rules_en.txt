# 📋 Important Grammar Rules & Tips — Preposition

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | **In** = months, years, seasons, long periods; **On** = days & dates; **At** = exact time. | in July, in 2024; on Monday, on 15 August; at 6 p.m. | "on 2024" ❌ — year always takes **in**. |
| 2 | **Since** = point of time; **For** = period of time. | since Monday / since 2010; for two years | "since two years" ❌ — a duration takes **for**. |
| 3 | **Between** = two persons/things; **Among** = more than two. | between Ram and Shyam; among the students | "between the three brothers" ❌ (use *among*). |
| 4 | **Beside** = next to; **Besides** = in addition to. | She sat beside me. / Besides tea, he took coffee. | Sound-alike trap — one 's' changes the meaning. |
| 5 | Verbs like *discuss, enter, reach, order, accompany, attack, resemble* take **no preposition**. | He entered the room. | "entered into the room" ❌ (enter into = only for agreements/discussions). |
| 6 | **Different from** (not *different than/to*). | My book is different from yours. | "different than" is American usage — exams prefer **from**. |
| 7 | **Aim at, look at, laugh at, wonder at** — fixed preposition *at*. | He laughed at the beggar. | "laughed on him" ❌. |
| 8 | **Good at, bad at, angry with (person), angry at (thing), pleased with, afraid of** — adjective + fixed preposition. | She is good at maths. He is angry with me. | "angry on him" ❌ — a person takes **with**. |
| 9 | **By** = agent/method; **With** = instrument. | killed by the robber; cut with a knife | "cut by a knife" ❌ — the tool takes **with**. |
| 10 | **In** = inside (static); **Into** = movement towards inside. | He is in the room. / He jumped into the well. | "jumped in the well" ❌ when motion is meant. |
| 11 | **Above/Below** = general level; **Over/Under** = direct vertical contact or covering. | Temperature fell below zero. / The bridge is over the river. | "below the fan" ❌ — directly beneath = **under**. |
| 12 | **Till/Until** = time; **To** = place/direction. | Wait till Monday. / Go to the station. | "He walked till the station" ❌ — place takes **to**. |

## (b) Time Management — 15/45-Second Rule

| Type | What it looks like | Time | Strategy |
|------|-------------------|------|----------|
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Fill-in-the-blank with a fixed preposition (good ___, since/for, in/on/at) | 15 sec | Recall the fixed pair instantly. If you know the rule, mark and move — never re-read. |
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Spot-the-error where a verb takes no preposition (entered into, reached at) | 15 sec | Scan for the 7 "no-preposition verbs" first. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Blank where two prepositions both look possible (beside/besides, in/into, between/among) | 45 sec | Read the full sentence, decide the *meaning* (motion? number? addition?), then choose. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Error hidden inside a long sentence with an intervening phrase | 45 sec | Strip the sentence to subject + verb + object, then check the preposition. |

**Rule of thumb:** If the answer is a memorised pair → 15 seconds. If you must judge meaning or context → allow 45 seconds, but never more.

## (c) Morphology — Roots & Affixes Behind "Preposition"

| Part | Meaning | Example words |
|------|---------|---------------|
| **pre-** (prefix, Latin) | before, in front | predict, prefix, preposition, preview |
| **posit / pon** (root, Latin *ponere*) | to place, to put | position, deposit, compose, opponent |
| **-tion** (suffix, Latin) | act/state of (makes a noun) | action, station, condition |
| **post-** (prefix, Latin) | after | postpone, postscript, post-mortem |
| **inter-** (prefix, Latin) | between | international, interrupt, interval |
| **sub-** (prefix, Latin) | under | submarine, subway, subscribe |

**Memory key:** *Preposition* = **pre** (before) + **posit** (placed) + **tion** → "a word *placed before* a noun." Most prepositions are themselves position-words: *in, on, under, over, between* — they tell **where** something is placed.
