76. He has been suffering from fever ______ Monday.
(a) by (b) for (c) from (d) since
Answer: (d)
Solution: "For" is tempting because it also shows time, but "for" is used with a period of time (for two days), while "since" is used with a point of time. Monday is a point of time, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

77. The train arrived ______ time despite the heavy fog.
(a) by (b) in (c) at (d) on
Answer: (d)
Solution: "In time" means before the deadline, but "on time" means exactly at the scheduled moment, which fits a train's arrival. The correct phrase is on time, so the answer is on.
Source: PYQ-style

78. She is junior ______ me by two years.
(a) to (b) with (c) from (d) than
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Than" is the trap because comparatives usually take "than", but Latin comparatives like junior, senior, superior, inferior and prefer take "to". The correct preposition is to, so the answer is to.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

79. He was accused ______ theft by the police.
(a) with (b) of (c) for (d) on
Answer: (b)
Solution: "For" looks natural after many verbs, but the fixed phrase is "accused of". The verb accuse always takes the preposition of, so the answer is of.

80. The children were playing ______ the garden.
(a) upon (b) at (c) on (d) in
Answer: (d)
Solution: "At" suggests a point location, but an enclosed area like a garden takes "in". The correct preposition is in, so the answer is in.

81. He died ______ cancer last year.
(a) from (b) by (c) of (d) with
Answer: (c)
Solution: "From" is tempting because "die from" is used for external causes like injuries, but diseases take "die of". Cancer is a disease, so the answer is of.

82. I am fond ______ classical music.
(a) for (b) about (c) in (d) of
Answer: (d)
Solution: "For" seems plausible after fond, but the fixed adjective phrase is "fond of". The correct preposition is of, so the answer is of.

83. The bridge was built ______ the river Ganga.
(a) over (b) on (c) above (d) upon
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Above" only means higher than, without contact or crossing; a bridge crosses over a river, touching both banks. The correct preposition is over, so the answer is over.

84. He has been living in Delhi ______ five years.
(a) since (b) from (c) for (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Since" is the trap, but it is used with a point of time (since 2019); "five years" is a period of time, which takes "for". The correct preposition is for, so the answer is for.

85. She was married ______ a rich businessman.
(a) to (b) with (c) by (d) from
Answer: (a)
Solution: "With" is a common Hindi-speaker error ("usse shaadi hui"), but in English "married" takes "to". The correct preposition is to, so the answer is to.

86. The teacher was angry ______ the boy for his misbehaviour.
(a) with (b) about (c) at (d) on
Answer: (a)
Solution: "At" is tempting, but when anger is directed at a person the fixed phrase is "angry with"; "angry at/about" is used for things or situations. Here the object is the boy, a person, so the answer is with.

87. He jumped ______ the well to save the child.
(a) into (b) in (c) inside (d) within
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In" shows position, but movement from outside to inside requires "into". Jumping is movement, so the answer is into.

88. We should abide ______ the rules of the school.
(a) with (b) on (c) to (d) by
Answer: (d)
Solution: "To" tempts learners because of "obey the rules", but "abide" is always followed by "by". The correct preposition is by, so the answer is by.

89. The meeting will be held ______ 10 a.m. ______ Monday.
(a) in, on (b) on, at (c) at, on (d) at, in
Answer: (c)
Solution: Reversing the pair is the trap; clock time takes "at" and days take "on". So it is "at 10 a.m. on Monday", and the answer is at, on.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

90. He is good ______ mathematics but weak ______ English.
(a) at, at (b) in, at (c) at, in (d) for, in
Answer: (c)
Solution: Using the same preposition twice is the trap; "good at" and "weak in" are the fixed phrases. The correct pair is at, in, so the answer is at, in.

91. Spot the error: Part P: He insisted / Part Q: to go / Part R: with me / Part S: to the market.
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q is wrong because "insist" is followed by "on" plus a gerund, not an infinitive; it should be "insisted on going". The error lies in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

92. He deals ______ electronic goods in this market.
(a) at (b) with (c) in (d) for
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Deal with" means to handle a person or problem, which is the trap; trading in goods takes "deal in". The correct preposition is in, so the answer is in.

93. The thief was caught ______ the police red-handed.
(a) by (b) from (c) with (d) through
Answer: (a)
Solution: "With" is tempting in Hindi translation, but in the passive voice the doer of the action is introduced by "by". The correct preposition is by, so the answer is by.

94. She congratulated me ______ my success.
(a) for (b) about (c) on (d) at

(a) to (b) from (c) with (d) than

96. Spot the error: Part P: Despite of / Part Q: the rain, / Part R: the match / Part S: continued.
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part P is wrong because "despite" never takes "of"; either use "despite the rain" or "in spite of the rain". The error lies in Part P, so the answer is Part P.

97. The two brothers divided the property ______ themselves.
(a) amongst (b) between (c) within (d) among
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Among" is the trap, but it is used for more than two; for two persons "between" is correct, even with "themselves". The correct preposition is between, so the answer is between.

98. He has been waiting for her ______ two o'clock.
(a) for (b) since (c) from (d) at
Answer: (b)
Solution: "For" is tempting with waiting, but "two o'clock" is a point of time, not a duration, so it takes "since". The correct preposition is since, so the answer is since.

99. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) He entered into the room quietly. (b) He entered the room quietly. (c) He entered in the room quietly. (d) He entered at the room quietly.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Entered into" is the trap; "enter" is never followed by a preposition when it means to go into a place. The correct sentence is "He entered the room quietly", so the answer is He entered the room quietly.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

100. Choose the sentence with the correct prepositions: "The manager agreed ______ my proposal and assured me ______ his full support."
(a) to, about (b) to, of (c) with, of (d) on, with
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Agree with" is the trap, but we agree with a person and agree to a proposal; "assure someone of something" is the fixed phrase. The correct pair is to, of, so the answer is to, of.
