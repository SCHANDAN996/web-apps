51. Choose the correct adjective: "She is a ___ girl."
(a) beauty (b) beautifully (c) beautiful (d) beautify
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Beautifully" is an adverb and "beautify" is a verb, so they cannot describe a noun; "beauty" is a noun, not a describing word. We need an adjective before the noun "girl", so the answer is beautiful.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

52. Identify the adjective in: "The old man walked slowly."
(a) man (b) slowly (c) walked (d) old
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Slowly" is an adverb describing the verb, and "man" is a noun. "Old" describes the noun "man", so the answer is old.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

53. Choose the correct form: "This is the ___ building in the city."
(a) tall (b) taller (c) tallest (d) most tall
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Most tall" is wrong because "tall" forms its superlative with -est, not with "most". Comparing one building against all in the city needs the superlative, so the answer is tallest.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

54. Fill in the blank: "My bag is ___ than yours."
(a) heavy (b) heavier (c) heaviest (d) more heavy
Answer: (b)
Solution: "More heavy" is a double comparative and is incorrect; "heaviest" is used for three or more. "Than" signals a comparison between two, so the answer is heavier.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

55. Which sentence uses the adjective correctly?
(a) He is more taller than me. (b) He is taller than me. (c) He is tallest than me. (d) He is much tallest than me.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "More taller" and "much tallest" are double comparisons, and "tallest" cannot take "than". A simple comparative with "than" is correct, so the answer is "He is taller than me."
Source: PYQ-style

56. Choose the correct adjective: "I have ___ money than my brother."
(a) less (b) few (c) lesser (d) littlest
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Few" is used with countable nouns, and "lesser" is not the standard comparative of "little" for quantity. "Money" is uncountable, so the answer is less.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

57. Identify the type of adjective in: "Each student got a prize."
(a) Adjective of quality (b) Adjective of quantity (c) Demonstrative adjective (d) Distributive adjective
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Each" does not show quality or quantity, and it does not point at a noun like "this" or "that". It refers to members of a group one by one, so the answer is distributive adjective.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

58. Fill in the blank: "___ book is mine." (pointing to one near you)
(a) Those (b) This (c) These (d) That
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Those" and "these" are plural, and "that" points to something far away. A single nearby thing takes "this", so the answer is This.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

59. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is senior to me. (b) He is senior than me. (c) He is senior from me. (d) He is senior with me.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior and inferior take "to", never "than", so options with "than", "from" or "with" are wrong. The correct preposition is "to", so the answer is "He is senior to me."
Source: PYQ-style

60. Fill in the blank: "There is ___ water in the glass." (some, but not much)
(a) few (b) a few (c) little (d) a little
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Few" and "a few" go with countable nouns, and bare "little" means almost none (negative sense). "Water" is uncountable and the sense is positive, so the answer is a little.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

61. Spot the error: "The Ganga (P)/ is one of the (Q)/ most longest rivers (R)/ in India. (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that "most longest" sounds grand and correct to many ears, but "longest" is already a superlative and adding "most" makes a double superlative. Part R should read "longest rivers", so the error is in Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

62. Choose the correct order of adjectives: "She bought a ___ saree."
(a) beautiful red silk (b) red beautiful silk (c) silk red beautiful (d) red silk beautiful
Answer: (a)
Solution: English follows the order opinion → colour → material; putting "silk" or "red" first breaks this natural order. "Beautiful" (opinion) comes before "red" (colour) and "silk" (material), so the answer is beautiful red silk.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

63. Fill in the blank: "Of the two brothers, Ravi is the ___."
(a) tall (b) most tall (c) tallest (d) taller
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "tallest", which many pick on seeing "the"; but the superlative needs three or more. When choosing between exactly two, English uses "the + comparative", so the answer is taller.
Source: PYQ-style

64. Spot the error: "He is (P)/ more wiser (Q)/ than his (R)/ younger brother. (S)"
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Wiser" is already the comparative of "wise", so "more wiser" is a double comparative — a classic examiner trap. Part Q should be just "wiser", so the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I prefer tea than coffee. (b) I prefer tea over than coffee. (c) I prefer tea to coffee. (d) I prefer tea from coffee.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Prefer" already contains the idea of comparison, so "than" after it is wrong, and "from" is never used with it. "Prefer" takes "to", so the answer is "I prefer tea to coffee."
Source: PYQ-style

66. Fill in the blank: "___ students passed the exam; almost everyone failed."
(a) A few (b) Few (c) The few (d) Many
Answer: (b)
Solution: "A few" is positive (some), and "many" contradicts "almost everyone failed". Bare "few" means hardly any, matching the negative sense, so the answer is Few.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

67. Choose the correct adjective: "This is a ___ problem; we must solve it today."
(a) seriousness (b) seriously (c) serious (d) more seriously
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Seriously" is an adverb and "seriousness" is a noun, neither of which can describe the noun "problem". We need an adjective of quality, so the answer is serious.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

68. Spot the error: "No sooner (P)/ did he arrive (Q)/ than the (R)/ meeting started. (S)"
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to suspect "than" after "no sooner", but "no sooner... than" is the correct correlative pair, and the inversion "did he arrive" is also correct. Every part is grammatically sound, so the answer is No error.
Source: PYQ-style

69. Fill in the blank: "Gold is ___ than silver."
(a) more precious (b) precious (c) most precious (d) preciouser
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Preciouser" is not a word, and "most precious" is a superlative needing three or more items. "Precious" is a long adjective, so its comparative uses "more"; with "than" between two metals, the answer is more precious.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

70. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is the better of the two players. (b) He is the best of the two players. (c) He is the good of the two players. (d) He is more good of the two players.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Best" is a superlative and needs three or more; "good" and "more good" are not comparatives. Between two players we use "the + comparative", so the answer is "He is the better of the two players."
Source: PYQ-style

71. Fill in the blank: "The ___ should be helped by society."
(a) poor (b) poors (c) poorly (d) poorness
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Poors" is wrong because adjectives used as nouns with "the" never take -s; "poorly" is an adverb and "poorness" is not used this way. "The + adjective" denotes a whole class of people, so the answer is poor.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

72. Spot the error: "My house (P)/ is bigger (Q)/ than (R)/ my friend. (S)"
(a) Part S (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to accept the sentence because it sounds natural in speech, but it wrongly compares a house with a person. Part S should be "my friend's" (house understood), so the error is in Part S.
Source: PYQ-style

73. Fill in the blank: "___ information you gave me was very useful."
(a) Many (b) A few (c) Much (d) Few
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Many", "a few" and "few" are used only with countable plural nouns. "Information" is uncountable, so the answer is Much.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

74. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) Delhi is bigger than any city in India. (b) Delhi is bigger than every cities in India. (c) Delhi is bigger than all city in India. (d) Delhi is bigger than any other city in India.
Answer: (d)
Solution: Without "other", Delhi would be compared with itself, which is illogical; "all city" and "every cities" break number agreement. "Any other" excludes Delhi from the comparison set, so the answer is "Delhi is bigger than any other city in India."
Source: PYQ-style

75. Fill in the blank: "He is ___ honest man."
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) much
Answer: (b)
Solution: "A" is wrong because "honest" begins with a vowel sound (the h is silent), and "much" cannot precede a singular countable noun. The vowel sound requires "an", so the answer is an.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar
