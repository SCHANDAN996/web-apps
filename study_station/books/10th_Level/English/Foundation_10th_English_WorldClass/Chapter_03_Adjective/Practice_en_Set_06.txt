126. Spot the error: The Ganges is / one of the longest / river in India. / No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting choice is "No error" because the sentence sounds natural in speech. But after "one of the + superlative", the noun must be plural — "one of the longest rivers". So the answer is Part R.

127. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) He is wiser than his brother. (b) He is more wiser than his brother. (c) He is wisest than his brother. (d) He is more wise than his brother.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Option (b) tempts learners who think "more" strengthens the comparison, but a double comparative (more + wiser) is wrong. "Wiser" alone is the correct comparative of "wise", so the answer is "He is wiser than his brother."

128. Spot the error: I prefer / tea more than / coffee in the morning. / No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Prefer" already contains the sense of comparison, so "more than" is redundant; the correct form is "I prefer tea to coffee". Verbs like prefer, senior, junior, superior take "to", not "than". So the answer is Part Q.
