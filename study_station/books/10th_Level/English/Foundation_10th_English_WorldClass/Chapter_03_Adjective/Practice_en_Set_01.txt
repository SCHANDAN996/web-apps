1. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) She is more taller than her sister. (b) She is much more taller than her sister. (c) She is most taller than her sister. (d) She is taller than her sister.
Answer: (d)
Solution: Option (a) is tempting but wrong because "more" and "-er" together make a double comparative, which is incorrect. Use only one comparative marker, so the answer is "She is taller than her sister."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

2. Identify the adjective in the sentence: "The brave soldier saved the child."
(a) brave (b) soldier (c) saved (d) child
Answer: (a)
Solution: An adjective describes a noun. Here "brave" describes the noun "soldier", so the answer is "brave".

3. Fill in the blank: "This is the ______ book I have ever read."
(a) good (b) better (c) best (d) most good
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Ever read" shows comparison among all books, so the superlative degree is needed. "Most good" is wrong because "good" has irregular forms: good–better–best, so the answer is "best".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

4. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is senior to me. (b) He is senior than me. (c) He is senior from me. (d) He is senior of me.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior and prefer take "to", not "than". Option (b) sounds right but is a classic examiner trap, so the answer is "He is senior to me."

5. Fill in the blank: "Gold is ______ than silver."

Solution: "Precious" is a long adjective, so it forms the comparative with "more". "Preciouser" does not exist, so the answer is "more precious".

7. Fill in the blank: "There is ______ milk in the jug." (very little, almost none)
(a) a little (b) little (c) the little (d) few
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Little" means almost none (negative sense), while "a little" means some. "Few" is used with countable nouns, and milk is uncountable, so the answer is "little".

Answer: (a)

9. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I have less money than you. (b) I have fewer money than you. (c) I have lesser money than you. (d) I have least money than you.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Fewer" is for countable nouns; money is uncountable, so "less" is correct. "Lesser" is not used for quantity comparisons like this, so the answer is "I have less money than you."

19. Choose the correct order of adjectives:
(a) a wooden old round table (b) a wooden round old table (c) a round wooden old table (d) an old round wooden table
Answer: (d)
Solution: The standard order is opinion–size–age–shape–colour–origin–material. Age (old) comes before shape (round), which comes before material (wooden), so the answer is "an old round wooden table".

25. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The weather of Shimla is better than Delhi. (b) The weather of Shimla is better than those of Delhi. (c) The weather of Shimla is more better than Delhi. (d) The weather of Shimla is better than that of Delhi.
Answer: (d)
Solution: We must compare weather with weather, not weather with Delhi. "That of" stands for "the weather of"; "those of" is wrong because weather is singular, so the answer is "The weather of Shimla is better than that of Delhi."
