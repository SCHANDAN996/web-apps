76. Choose the correctly spelt adjective.
(a) beautifull (b) beautiful (c) beutiful (d) beautifel
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Beautiful" keeps only one "l" at the end; the double "l" in "beautifull" is a common spelling trap. The correct spelling is beautiful.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

77. Select the correct sentence.
(a) He is senior to me. (b) He is senior than me. (c) He is more senior than me. (d) He is more senior to me.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior take "to", never "than", and they never take "more". So the answer is "He is senior to me."
Source: PYQ-style

78. Fill in the blank: Of the two brothers, Ravi is the ______.
(a) taller (b) tallest (c) more tall (d) most tall
Answer: (a)
Solution: When comparing only two persons, the comparative degree is used with "the", not the superlative. "Tallest" tempts because of "the", but it needs three or more. So the answer is taller.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

79. Spot the error: Part P: No sooner did I reach / Part Q: the station when / Part R: the train left. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than", not "when". Part Q should read "the station than". So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

80. Choose the correct option: She is ______ than her sister.
(a) more wiser (b) most wise (c) wiser (d) much wisest
Answer: (c)
Solution: "More wiser" is a double comparative, a classic examiner trap; "wiser" alone is correct. So the answer is wiser.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

81. Fill in the blank: This is the ______ book I have ever read.
(a) most interesting (b) more interesting (c) interesting (d) much interesting
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Ever read" shows comparison among all books, so the superlative "most interesting" is needed. So the answer is most interesting.

82. Select the correctly framed sentence.
(a) I have a five-years-old son. (b) I have a five-years old son. (c) I have a five year old son. (d) I have a five-year-old son.
Answer: (d)
Solution: In a hyphenated compound adjective before a noun, the noun stays singular: "five-year-old". So the answer is "I have a five-year-old son."

83. Spot the error: Part P: He is the best / Part Q: of the two / Part R: players in the team. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "two", the comparative "better" is required, not the superlative "best". Part P should read "He is the better". So the error is in Part P.

84. Choose the correct adjective order: She bought a ______ vase.
(a) beautiful old Chinese (b) old beautiful Chinese (c) Chinese beautiful old (d) old Chinese beautiful
Answer: (a)
Solution: Adjective order is opinion–age–origin: beautiful (opinion), old (age), Chinese (origin). So the answer is beautiful old Chinese.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

85. Fill in the blank: The ______ of the two plans was accepted.
(a) best (b) better (c) good (d) most good
Answer: (b)
Solution: Comparing two plans needs the comparative "better"; "best" is the tempting trap but needs three or more. So the answer is better.

86. Select the correct sentence.
(a) He is comparatively more richer today. (b) He is comparative richer today. (c) He is comparatively richer today. (d) He is comparatively most rich today.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Comparatively" already carries the sense of comparison, so adding "more" is a double comparative error. So the answer is "He is comparatively richer today."

87. Fill in the blank: Gold is ______ than silver.
(a) precious (b) most precious (c) more precious (d) much precious
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Precious" is a multi-syllable adjective, so its comparative is "more precious". So the answer is more precious.

88. Spot the error: Part P: The Ganga is / Part Q: one of the longest / Part R: river in India. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error

89. Choose the correct option: ______ students passed the exam this year.
(a) Less (b) Least (c) Lesser (d) Fewer
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Students" is countable, so "fewer" is correct; "less" is used with uncountable nouns. So the answer is Fewer.

90. Fill in the blank: There is ______ milk left in the fridge.
(a) several (b) a few (c) many (d) a little
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Milk" is uncountable, so "a little" (meaning some) fits; "a few", "many", "several" go with countable nouns. So the answer is a little.

91. Select the correct sentence.
(a) He is the most unique player. (b) He is more unique player. (c) He is a unique player. (d) He is the uniquest player.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Unique" is an absolute adjective and has no comparative or superlative degree. So the answer is "He is a unique player."

92. Fill in the blank: My ______ brother lives in Delhi.
(a) elder than (b) older (c) eldest than (d) elder
Answer: (d)
Solution: For family members, "elder/eldest" is used, and "elder" never takes "than". So the answer is elder.

93. Spot the error: Part P: He gave me / Part Q: an useful advice / Part R: on my project. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two errors hide in Part Q: "useful" begins with a "yu" sound, so it takes "a", and "advice" is uncountable, so it takes no article at all — it should be "useful advice". So the error is in Part Q.

94. Choose the correct option: This road is ______ than that one.
(a) more narrow (b) narrowest (c) most narrow (d) narrower
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Narrow" forms its comparative as "narrower"; "more narrow" is a double comparative trap. So the answer is narrower.

95. Fill in the blank: ______ information you gave me was wrong.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) Many
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable, so "a/an/many" cannot be used; the definite article "The" refers to specific information. So the answer is The.

96. Spot the error: Part P: Of all the students / Part Q: in the class, Sita is / Part R: more intelligent. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Of all the students" demands the superlative, so Part R should read "the most intelligent". The comparative "more intelligent" tempts because it sounds natural in speech. So the error is in Part R.

97. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The rich should helps the poor. (b) The rich should help the poor. (c) The riches should help the poor. (d) Rich should help poor.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "The rich" is an adjective used as a plural noun meaning "rich people", so it takes a plural verb "help". So the answer is "The rich should help the poor."

98. Spot the error: Part P: He is / Part Q: enough tall / Part R: to touch the ceiling. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Enough" comes after the adjective it qualifies: "tall enough". Placing it before the adjective is a frequent examiner trap. So the error is in Part Q.

99. Fill in the blank: No sooner had she finished her work ______ the lights went off.
(a) when (b) than (c) then (d) that
Answer: (b)
Solution: "No sooner" is always followed by "than"; "when" pairs with "hardly/scarcely". So the answer is than.
Source: PYQ-style

100. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is cleverer than any other boy in the class. (b) He is the cleverest of all other boys. (c) He is cleverer than any boy in the class. (d) He is the cleverest than all boys.
Answer: (a)
Solution: When a person is compared with the group he belongs to, "any other" is needed to exclude himself; option (c) wrongly compares him with himself. So the answer is "He is cleverer than any other boy in the class."
Source: PYQ-style
