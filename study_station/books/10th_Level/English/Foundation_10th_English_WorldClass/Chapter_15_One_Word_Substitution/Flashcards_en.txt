Card 1
Front: A person who speaks many languages?
Back: Polyglot (Greek: poly = many, glotta = tongue)

Card 2
Front: One who loves mankind?
Back: Philanthropist (phil = love, anthropos = man)

Card 3
Front: One who hates mankind?
Back: Misanthrope (misein = to hate, anthropos = man)

Card 4
Front: A word or law no longer in use?
Back: Obsolete

Card 5
Front: 🧠 MNEMONIC — How to remember "Ambidextrous"?
Back: "AMBI = BOTH" — think of an AMBIvalent person using BOTH hands equally well. Ambidextrous = one who can use both hands equally well.

Card 6
Front: Government by the people?
Back: Democracy (demos = people, kratos = rule)

Card 7
Front: ⚠️ EXAMINER'S TRAP — "One who looks at the bright side of things" — Optimist or Pessimist?
Back: Optimist. Trap: Pessimist looks at the DARK side. Remember: O = Opportunity (bright), P = Problem (dark).

Card 8
Front: A list of books available in a library?
Back: Catalogue

Card 9
Front: Murder of one's own brother?
Back: Fratricide (frater = brother, cide = killing)

Card 10
Front: One who eats human flesh?
Back: Cannibal (Trap: Carnivore eats flesh of animals, not humans)

Card 11
Front: 🧠 MNEMONIC — How to remember "Somnambulist"?
Back: "SOMN = SLEEP" (like in-SOMN-ia) + ambul = walk. A somnambulist walks in sleep — imagine an insomniac sleepwalking!

Card 12
Front: A place where dead bodies are kept for identification?
Back: Mortuary (Trap: Morgue is also correct, but Cemetery = burial ground — don't confuse)

Card 13
Front: That which cannot be avoided?
Back: Inevitable

Card 14
Front: ⚠️ EXAMINER'S TRAP — "One who pretends to be what he is not" — Hypocrite or Sycophant?
Back: Hypocrite. Trap: A Sycophant flatters others for personal gain; a Hypocrite fakes virtue/beliefs.

Card 15
Front: A speech delivered without preparation?
Back: Extempore (Impromptu is also accepted)

Card 16
Front: One who knows everything?
Back: Omniscient (omni = all, scient = knowing)

Card 17
Front: A person who lives at the same time?
Back: Contemporary

Card 18
Front: Writing on a wall, building or monument?
Back: Inscription (Trap: Epitaph = words written on a TOMB/grave only)

Card 19
Front: That which can be drunk?
Back: Potable (Trap: Edible = fit to be EATEN, not drunk)

Card 20
Front: An animal that lives both on land and in water?
Back: Amphibian (amphi = both, bios = life)
