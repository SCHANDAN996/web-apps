76. A person who speaks many languages
(a) Polyglot (b) Bilingual (c) Linguist (d) Multilingualist
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Linguist" tempts because it relates to language, but a linguist studies language scientifically and need not speak many. "Polyglot" (Greek poly = many, glotta = tongue) means one who speaks many languages, so the answer is Polyglot.
