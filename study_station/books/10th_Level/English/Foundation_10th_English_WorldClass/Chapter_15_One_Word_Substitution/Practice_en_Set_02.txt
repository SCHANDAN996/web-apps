26. A person who speaks many languages
(a) Bilingual (b) Polyglot (c) Linguist (d) Orator
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Bilingual" tempts because it involves languages, but it means one who speaks only two languages. "Poly" means many and "glot" means tongue/language, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: NCERT-based vocabulary list

27. One who loves mankind
(a) Philanthropist (b) Misanthrope (c) Optimist (d) Pessimist
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Misanthrope" is the trap — it means one who hates mankind, the exact opposite. "Phil" means love and "anthropos" means man, so the answer is Philanthropist.
Source: PYQ-style

28. A place where dead bodies are kept for identification
(a) Cemetery (b) Monastery (c) Mortuary (d) Sanatorium
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Cemetery" tempts because it is connected with the dead, but it is a burial ground, not a place for keeping bodies for identification. A mortuary is where dead bodies are kept temporarily, so the answer is Mortuary.
Source: SSC-pattern vocabulary

29. One who cannot read or write
(a) Literate (b) Scholar (c) Novice (d) Illiterate
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Novice" tempts as it suggests inexperience, but it means a beginner, not one unable to read or write. The prefix "il-" means not, so the answer is Illiterate.
