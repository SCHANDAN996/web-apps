51. A person who speaks many languages
(a) Multilingual speaker (b) Bilingual (c) Linguist (d) Polyglot
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Bilingual" is tempting but means only two languages, not many. "Linguist" studies language scientifically but need not speak many. One who speaks many languages is a polyglot, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

52. A place where dead bodies are kept for identification
(a) Mortuary (b) Cemetery (c) Crematorium (d) Mausoleum
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Cemetery" tempts because it relates to the dead, but it is a burial ground, not a place for keeping bodies for identification. That place is a mortuary, so the answer is Mortuary.

53. One who does not believe in the existence of God
(a) Agnostic (b) Ascetic (c) Atheist (d) Apostate
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Agnostic" is the classic trap — an agnostic is unsure whether God exists, while an atheist firmly denies God's existence. The question says "does not believe", so the answer is Atheist.
