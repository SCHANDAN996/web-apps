Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. One who loves mankind
(a) Misanthropist (b) Philanthropist (c) Anthropologist (d) Optimist
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Misanthropist" is the tempting trap because it looks similar, but it means one who hates mankind — the exact opposite. "Phil" means love and "anthropos" means man, so the answer is Philanthropist.
Source: NCERT-based vocabulary / PYQ-style

2. One who cannot read or write
(a) Literate (b) Scholar (c) Illiterate (d) Calligrapher
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Literate" is the trap — it means one who CAN read and write, the opposite of what is asked. The prefix "il-" means not, so the answer is Illiterate.
Source: PYQ-style

3. A place where dead bodies are kept
(a) Cemetery (b) Monastery (c) Mortuary (d) Sanatorium
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Cemetery" tempts because it is related to the dead, but it is a burial ground, not a place where bodies are kept temporarily. A mortuary is where dead bodies are kept before burial, so the answer is Mortuary.
Source: PYQ-style

4. One who speaks two languages
(a) Bilingual (b) Linguist (c) Polyglot (d) Orator
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Polyglot" is the trap — it means one who knows MANY languages, not two. "Bi" means two, so the answer is Bilingual.
Source: PYQ-style

5. A person who eats human flesh
(a) Herbivore (b) Carnivore (c) Omnivore (d) Cannibal
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Carnivore" tempts because it involves flesh-eating, but it means any flesh-eating animal, not specifically a human-eater. A cannibal eats human flesh, so the answer is Cannibal.
Source: PYQ-style

6. Government by the people
(a) Autocracy (b) Democracy (c) Monarchy (d) Bureaucracy
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Autocracy" is the trap — rule by one person with total power, the opposite of rule by the people. "Demos" means people and "cracy" means rule, so the answer is Democracy.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Civics

7. One who looks at the bright side of things
(a) Optimist (b) Pessimist (c) Idealist (d) Realist
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Pessimist" is the trap — it means one who sees the dark side, the exact opposite. An optimist sees the bright side, so the answer is Optimist.
Source: PYQ-style

8. A place where birds are kept
(a) Apiary (b) Aquarium (c) Aviary (d) Stable
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Apiary" is the classic trap — it sounds like "aviary" but is a place where BEES are kept. "Avis" means bird in Latin, so the answer is Aviary.
Source: PYQ-style

9. One who does not believe in the existence of God
(a) Atheist (b) Theist (c) Agnostic (d) Ascetic
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Agnostic" tempts, but an agnostic is unsure whether God exists, not a firm disbeliever. "A" means without and "theos" means God, so the answer is Atheist.
Source: PYQ-style

10. A list of books available in a library
(a) Glossary (b) Catalogue (c) Index (d) Dictionary
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Index" tempts, but an index lists topics inside one book, not all books in a library. A catalogue lists available books, so the answer is Catalogue.
Source: PYQ-style

11. One who lives on vegetables only
(a) Vegetarian (b) Herbivore (c) Vegan (d) Frugivore
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Herbivore" tempts, but it describes plant-eating animals, not a person by choice. A person who eats only vegetables is a vegetarian, so the answer is Vegetarian.
Source: PYQ-style

12. Murder of one's own brother
(a) Homicide (b) Patricide (c) Fratricide (d) Regicide
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Homicide" is the trap — it means murder of any human being, not specifically a brother. "Frater" means brother and "cide" means killing, so the answer is Fratricide.
Source: PYQ-style

13. A place where coins are made
(a) Bank (b) Mint (c) Treasury (d) Factory
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Treasury" tempts because it deals with money, but it is where money is kept, not made. Coins are manufactured at a mint, so the answer is Mint.
Source: PYQ-style

14. One who can use both hands equally well
(a) Versatile (b) Ambidextrous (c) Expert (d) Flexible
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Versatile" tempts as it means skilled in many things, but it says nothing about hands. "Ambi" means both and "dexter" means right hand, so the answer is Ambidextrous.
Source: PYQ-style

15. A speech delivered without preparation
(a) Monologue (b) Extempore (c) Soliloquy (d) Rhetoric
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Monologue" tempts, but it only means a speech by one person — it may be fully prepared. An unprepared speech is extempore, so the answer is Extempore.
Source: PYQ-style

16. One who knows everything
(a) Omnipotent (b) Optimist (c) Omnipresent (d) Omniscient
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Omnipotent" is the trap — it means all-POWERFUL, not all-knowing. "Omni" means all and "scient" means knowing, so the answer is Omniscient.
Source: PYQ-style

17. A place where weapons and ammunition are stored
(a) Arsenal (b) Archive (c) Granary (d) Warehouse
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Archive" tempts because it sounds similar, but it stores historical records and documents. Weapons are stored in an arsenal, so the answer is Arsenal.
Source: PYQ-style

18. One who pretends to be what he is not
(a) Sycophant (b) Imposter-scholar (c) Diplomat (d) Hypocrite
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Sycophant" tempts, but it means a flatterer who praises to gain favour, not a pretender of character. One who pretends virtue he does not have is a hypocrite, so the answer is Hypocrite.
Source: PYQ-style

19. A place where grains are stored
(a) Granary (b) Godown-cellar (c) Apiary (d) Hangar
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Hangar" tempts, but it is a shelter for aircraft. "Granum" means grain, so the answer is Granary.
Source: PYQ-style

20. One who is indifferent to pleasure and pain
(a) Cynic (b) Sadist (c) Masochist (d) Stoic
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Cynic" tempts, but a cynic distrusts human goodness — that is about belief, not about bearing pleasure and pain calmly. A stoic remains unmoved by both, so the answer is Stoic.
Source: PYQ-style

21. A person who speaks on behalf of others, especially in a court of law
(a) Auditor (b) Arbitrator (c) Advocate (d) Ambassador
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Arbitrator" tempts, but an arbitrator settles disputes OUTSIDE court between two parties. One who pleads for another in a law court is an advocate, so the answer is Advocate.
Source: PYQ-style

22. That which cannot be avoided
(a) Inevitable (b) Invincible (c) Invisible (d) Incredible
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Invincible" is the trap — it means that which cannot be DEFEATED, not avoided. "In" means not and "evitable" means avoidable, so the answer is Inevitable.
Source: PYQ-style

23. A person who collects stamps
(a) Archaeologist (b) Numismatist (c) Cartographer (d) Philatelist
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Numismatist" is the classic trap — it means a collector of COINS, not stamps. "Phil" means love and "ateleia" means tax-free (stamp), so the answer is Philatelist.
Source: PYQ-style

24. A word or law no longer in use
(a) Ancient (b) Obscure (c) Absolute (d) Obsolete
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Ancient" tempts, but ancient things may still be in use — old age alone does not mean disuse. Something fallen out of use is obsolete, so the answer is Obsolete.
Source: PYQ-style

25. One who does a thing for pleasure and not as a profession
(a) Novice (b) Professional (c) Amateur (d) Veteran
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Novice" is the trap — it means a beginner, who may still be training for a profession. One who does something purely for love of it is an amateur, so the answer is Amateur.
Source: PYQ-style
