# 📋 Important Grammar Rules & Tips — Articles

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use **A** before consonant *sounds*, **AN** before vowel *sounds* — sound matters, not spelling. | a university, an hour, an M.P., a one-rupee note | "an university" looks right but is wrong — 'u' here sounds like 'yu' (consonant). |
| 2 | Use **THE** for a specific/known thing (mentioned before or clear from context). | I saw a dog. The dog was black. | Second mention without 'the' is a classic error-spotting trap. |
| 3 | Use **THE** with unique things (only one exists). | the sun, the moon, the earth, the equator | "A sun rises in the east" — wrong; there is only one sun. |
| 4 | Use **THE** with superlatives and ordinals. | the best player, the first boy | "He is best player in the team" — missing 'the' before superlative. |
| 5 | Use **THE** with rivers, seas, oceans, mountain *ranges*, deserts, gulfs. | the Ganga, the Indian Ocean, the Himalayas, the Sahara | But single peaks/lakes take NO article: Mount Everest, Lake Baikal. |
| 6 | Use **THE** with plural country names, unions, republics, kingdoms. | the USA, the UK, the UAE, the Netherlands | Single-name countries take NO article: India, Japan, France. |
| 7 | NO article before proper nouns, languages, meals, games, and uncountable/abstract nouns used generally. | India, Hindi, breakfast, cricket, honesty, milk | "The honesty is the best policy" — wrong; abstract noun in general sense takes no 'the'. |
| 8 | NO article before plural countable nouns used in a general sense. | Dogs are faithful animals. | "The dogs are faithful animals" changes meaning to specific dogs. |
| 9 | Use **THE** before an adjective to denote a whole class of people. | the rich, the poor, the blind, the old | "The rich is not always happy" — wrong verb too; 'the rich' is plural → 'are'. |
| 10 | Use **THE** with holy/famous books, newspapers, monuments, historical events. | the Ramayana, the Times of India, the Taj Mahal, the French Revolution | Trap: names of magazines without 'the' in their official title still take 'the' in sentences. |
| 11 | NO article before names of subjects, diseases (mostly), and 'school/church/hospital/prison/bed' when used for their primary purpose. | He goes to school (to study). She is in hospital (as a patient). | "He goes to the school" = he visits the building, not to study — meaning changes! |
| 12 | Use **A/AN** in the sense of 'one' or 'per'; use **THE** when an adjective/noun represents a class or species. | a rupee a kilo; once a week; The cow is a useful animal. | "Rice sells at fifty rupees kilo" — missing 'a' before 'kilo'. |

## (b) ⏱️ Time Management — The 15/45-Second Rule

**15-Second Questions (Fact-based — answer instantly, don't overthink):**
- Fill in the blank with a/an/the before a single noun (sound test: university, hour, honest).
- Unique things (the sun, the moon) and superlatives (the best, the tallest).
- Fixed names: the Ganga, the Himalayas, the USA, the Taj Mahal.
- Zero-article cases: meals, games, languages, subjects.

**45-Second Questions (Inference-based — read the whole sentence twice):**
- Sentences where meaning changes with/without 'the' (go to school vs go to the school).
- 'The + adjective = class of people' with subject-verb agreement (the poor *are*…).
- Error-spotting with two blanks in one sentence (I saw ___ dog; ___ dog was black).
- Questions mixing countable/uncountable nouns (an information ❌ → a piece of information ✔).

**Strategy:** Attempt all 15-second questions first in one sweep. Mark 45-second ones, return with fresh eyes. Never spend more than 60 seconds on any article question — the rule either clicks or it doesn't.

## (c) 🔤 Morphology — Roots & Affixes (Word-Building for Articles Context)

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Example Words | Article Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **uni-** (Latin: one) | one | university, uniform, union, unique | 'u' = /yu/ sound → **a** university, **a** unique chance |
| **hon-** (Latin: honour) | honour | honest, honour, honorary | silent 'h' → **an** honest man |
| **heir-** (Latin: heres) | inherit | heir, heiress, heritage | silent 'h' in heir → **an** heir; but 'h' sounded in heritage → **a** heritage |
| **equi-** (Latin: equal) | equal | equator, equinox, equal | **the** equator (unique thing) |
| **-est** (superlative suffix) | most | tallest, fastest, best | always **the** tallest, **the** best |
| **-ian / -an** (suffix: belonging to) | person/people | the Indian Ocean, the Andaman Islands | oceans/seas take **the** |
| **re-** (Latin: again) | again | republic, reunion | **the** Republic of India (official name) |
| **mono-** (Greek: single) | one | monsoon, monolith | **the** monsoon (specific season) |

**Memory Hook:** *"Sound decides A/AN, uniqueness decides THE, generality decides ZERO."*
