Card 1
Front: When do we use "a" before a word?
Back: Before a consonant SOUND — a boy, a university (sounds "yu"), a one-rupee note (sounds "wa"), a European country.

Card 2
Front: When do we use "an" before a word?
Back: Before a vowel SOUND — an apple, an hour (h is silent), an honest man, an MBA (sounds "em").

Card 3
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: Which five letters remind you of the vowel sounds for "an"?
Back: "A-E-I-O-U" — but remember the rule is about SOUND, not spelling. "An hour" (silent h) vs "a university" (yu-sound).

Card 4
Front: When is "the" used before a noun?
Back: When the noun is specific, already mentioned, unique, or known to both speaker and listener — "The sun rises in the east."

Card 5
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "He is ___ honest man." — a or an?
Back: "an" — because "h" in honest is silent; the word begins with a vowel sound. Examiners test sound vs spelling here.

Card 6
Front: Which article goes before superlative degrees?
Back: "The" — the best, the tallest, the most beautiful girl in the class.

Card 7
Front: Name three things that always take "the" because they are unique.
Back: The sun, the moon, the earth (also: the sky, the equator, the internet).

Card 8
Front: Do we use "the" before names of rivers, oceans, and mountain ranges?
Back: Yes — the Ganga, the Indian Ocean, the Himalayas. But single peaks take no article: Mount Everest.

Card 9
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "___ gold is a precious metal." — Should "the" be used?
Back: No article. Materials/metals used in a general sense take no article. But "The gold of this ring is pure" (specific gold) takes "the".

Card 10
Front: When do we use NO article (zero article) before plural and uncountable nouns?
Back: When speaking generally — "Dogs are faithful animals", "Water is essential for life", "Honesty is the best policy."

Card 11
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember which places take "the"?
Back: "R-O-D-S" — Rivers, Oceans, Deserts, Seas (and ranges/gulfs) take "the". Single lakes, peaks, and most countries do not.

Card 12
Front: Which article is used before ordinal numbers (first, second, third)?
Back: "The" — the first boy, the second chapter, the third floor.

Card 13
Front: "I go to ___ school by bus." vs "I went to ___ school to meet the principal." — Fill in the articles.
Back: First: no article (school for its primary purpose — studying). Second: "the" (visiting the building for another purpose).

Card 14
Front: Which countries take "the" before their names?
Back: Countries with words like Republic, Kingdom, States, or plural names — the USA, the UK, the UAE, the Netherlands, the Republic of India.

Card 15
Front: What article is used before names of languages, meals, and games?
Back: No article — "Hindi is easy", "Lunch is ready", "We play cricket." (But "the Hindi language" is correct.)

Card 16
Front: "___ rich should help the poor." — Which article, and what does this structure mean?
Back: "The" — "the + adjective" represents a whole class of people: the rich = rich people, the blind, the elderly.

Card 17
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: "He is ___ best player in the team." — a, an, the, or no article?
Back: "the" — superlatives always take "the". Examiners often offer "a" as a tempting wrong option.

Card 18
Front: Which article is used before holy/famous books and newspapers?
Back: "The" — the Ramayana, the Quran, the Times of India, the Hindu.

Card 19
Front: "She plays ___ guitar very well." — Which article and why?
Back: "the" — musical instruments take "the" when talking about playing them: play the flute, the piano, the tabla.

Card 20
Front: Quick rule: a/an is used only with which kind of nouns?
Back: Singular COUNTABLE nouns. Uncountable nouns (water, advice, furniture, information) never take a/an — use "some" or no article.
