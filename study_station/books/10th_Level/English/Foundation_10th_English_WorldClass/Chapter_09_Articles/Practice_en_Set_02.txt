26. I saw ___ elephant at the zoo.
(a) an (b) no article (c) the (d) a
Answer: (a)
Solution: "A" is tempting because learners apply the rule loosely to spelling, but the choice depends on the first sound, and "elephant" begins with a vowel sound. So the answer is "an".
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

27. ___ sun rises in the east.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "A" is tempting because the sentence states a general fact, but the sun is a unique object and unique things always take the definite article. So the answer is "The".
