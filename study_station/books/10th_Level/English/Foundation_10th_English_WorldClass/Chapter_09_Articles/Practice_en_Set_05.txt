101. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is a university student. (b) He is an university student. (c) He is the university student. (d) He is university student.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting choice "an university" sounds right because the word starts with a vowel letter, but "university" begins with a consonant sound /juː/, so "a" is used. The correct answer is "He is a university student."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

102. Fill in the blank: Mount Everest is ___ highest peak in the world.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: Superlative adjectives always take "the". "A" and "an" cannot precede a superlative, and omitting the article is a common trap. The correct answer is "the".

Solution: Abstract nouns like "honesty" used in a general sense take no article. Learners often insert "the" by over-generalising, but that is wrong here. The correct answer is "No article".
