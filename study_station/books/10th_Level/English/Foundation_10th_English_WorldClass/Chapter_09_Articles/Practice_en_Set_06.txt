126. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is an university student. (b) He is the university student. (c) He is a university student. (d) He is university student.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is "an university" — "university" begins with a consonant sound /juː/, so "an" is wrong despite the vowel letter. "A" is used before consonant sounds, so the answer is "He is a university student."

127. Choose the correctly framed sentence.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "a useful advice" — "advice" is uncountable, so no article can be used with it at all. "An useful" is doubly wrong. Uncountable nouns take no article in general statements, so the answer is "She gave me useful advice."
