Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. He is ___ honest man.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the first sound is a vowel sound /ɒ/. The tempting choice "a" is wrong because we choose by sound, not spelling. So the answer is "an".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. ___ sun rises in the east.
(a) A (b) The (c) An (d) No article
Answer: (b)
Solution: Unique things of nature (sun, moon, earth, sky) take "the". "A" is tempting but wrong because there is only one sun. So the answer is "The".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

3. I saw ___ one-eyed man in the market.
(a) the (b) an (c) a (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "One" is pronounced /wʌn/ — it begins with a consonant sound /w/. "An" is the trap because the letter 'o' is a vowel; sound matters, not the letter. So the answer is "a".

4. She is ___ university student.
(a) the (b) an (c) a (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "University" begins with the sound /juː/ (a 'y' consonant sound), so we use "a". "An" tempts because of the vowel letter 'u'. So the answer is "a".
