51. He is ___ honest man.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the first sound is a vowel sound /ɒ/. The tempting choice "a" is wrong because we choose the article by sound, not by spelling. Since the sound is a vowel, the answer is "an".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. ___ sun rises in the east.
(a) The (b) An (c) A (d) No article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "A" and "an" are wrong because the sun is a unique object — there is only one. Unique things in the universe take "the", so the answer is "The".

(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "An" tempts because 'o' is a vowel letter, but "one" is pronounced /wʌn/ — it begins with a consonant sound /w/. Articles follow sound, so the answer is "a".

Solution: Part P tempts because learners think "the best" is the error, but "the + superlative" is correct. The real error is in Part R: "team" is a singular countable noun and needs an article — "in the team". So the answer is Part R.
