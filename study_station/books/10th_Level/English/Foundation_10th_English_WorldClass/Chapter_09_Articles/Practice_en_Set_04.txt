76. Choose the correctly punctuated sentence.
(a) He is a European traveller. (b) He is an European traveller. (c) He is the European traveller. (d) He is European traveller.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "European" begins with a vowel letter but a consonant sound (/j/), so "an" is wrong; "a" is correct. No specific traveller is meant, so "the" is not needed. The answer is "He is a European traveller."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

77. Fill in the blank: She goes to ___ church every Sunday to pray.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (b)
Solution: When a place like church, school, hospital or prison is visited for its primary purpose (praying, studying, treatment), no article is used. "The church" would mean merely visiting the building. The answer is "no article."

96. Spot the error: Part P: The teacher said that / Part Q: the earth revolves / Part R: around a sun. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part R is wrong because "sun" is a unique object and must take "the", not "a" — the trap is that "a" sounds natural after "around". Part Q is correct since universal truths stay in the present tense. The error is in Part R.

97. Spot the error: Part P: He is / Part Q: the taller / Part R: of the two brothers. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is to mark Part Q wrong because comparatives usually avoid "the"; but when one of two is selected ("of the two"), the comparative correctly takes "the". The sentence has no error.
