# Error Spotting (Basic) — Practice Set 2
Questions 26–50 | Easy: 26–40 | Medium: 41–50

26. Spot the error: One of my friend (P) / has gone (Q) / to Delhi. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting choice is Part Q because "has gone" sounds fine, but the real error is earlier: after "one of" we always use a plural noun, so it must be "one of my friends". Part Q and Part R are correct, so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Spot the error: He do not (P) / like tea (Q) / in the morning. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: With "he" (third person singular) we use "does", not "do". The correct sentence is "He does not like tea in the morning", so the answer is Part P.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

28. Spot the error: She is more taller (P) / than (Q) / her sister. (R)
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Taller" is already comparative; adding "more" makes a double comparative, which is wrong. Say "She is taller than her sister", so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Spot the error: I have seen (P) / that movie (Q) / yesterday. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Yesterday" is a definite past time, so we must use the simple past "saw", not the present perfect "have seen". The correct form is "I saw that movie yesterday", so the answer is Part P.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

30. Spot the error: The news (P) / are (Q) / very shocking. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "News" looks plural but is singular in English, so it takes "is". The tempting error seems to be Part P, but "the news" is fine; the verb is wrong, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

31. Spot the error: He is (P) / more wiser (Q) / than his brother. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Wiser" is already the comparative of "wise"; "more wiser" is a double comparative. Say "He is wiser than his brother", so the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

32. Spot the error: Each of the boys (P) / have done (Q) / his homework. (R)
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Each of" takes a singular verb, so it must be "has done", not "have done". The plural "boys" tempts you toward a plural verb, but the subject is "each", so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

33. Spot the error: She can (P) / sings (Q) / very well. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: After a modal like "can", we use the base form of the verb: "sing", not "sings". The correct sentence is "She can sing very well", so the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English

34. Spot the error: I am living (P) / in this city (Q) / since 2015. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: With "since 2015" (an action continuing up to now) we use the present perfect continuous: "I have been living in this city since 2015". The present continuous is wrong here, so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

35. Spot the error: He gave me (P) / many advices (Q) / on this matter. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Advice" is an uncountable noun and has no plural form; say "much advice" or "many pieces of advice". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

36. Spot the error: Neither Ram (P) / nor his friends (Q) / was present. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: With "neither…nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject, which is "his friends" (plural), so it must be "were present". The error is in Part R, so the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

37. Spot the error: She is (P) / a honest (Q) / girl. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a vowel sound (the h is silent), so the article must be "an": "an honest girl". The spelling tempts you toward "a", but sound decides the article, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

38. Spot the error: The teacher asked (P) / that why (Q) / he was late. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: In reported speech we do not use "that" before a wh-word; say "The teacher asked why he was late". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

39. Spot the error: He is (P) / senior than (Q) / me by two years. (R)
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Senior" is a Latin comparative and takes "to", not "than": "senior to me". Many learners pick Part R, but "me" is correct after "to"; the error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

40. Spot the error: There is (P) / many people (Q) / in the hall. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "People" is plural, so the verb must be "are": "There are many people in the hall". The error is in Part P, so the answer is Part P.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English

41. Spot the error: The Ganges (P) / is one of the (Q) / longest river in India. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "one of the" we need a plural noun: "one of the longest rivers". The singular "river" is the error, so the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Spot the error: No sooner did he reach (P) / the station (Q) / then the train left. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part R
Answer: (d)
Solution: "No sooner" is paired with "than", not "then": "No sooner did he reach the station than the train left". The error is in Part R, so the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

43. Spot the error: He as well as (P) / his brothers (Q) / were invited. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject, "He", so it must be "was invited". The nearby plural "brothers" is the trap; the error is in Part R, so the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Spot the error: I have been (P) / working here (Q) / since five years. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part R
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (since 2019); for a period we use "for": "for five years". The error is in Part R, so the answer is Part R.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

45. Spot the error: The principal, along with (P) / the teachers, (Q) / have left. (R)
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject, "the principal" (singular), so it must be "has left". The intervening phrase "the teachers" is the examiner's trap; the error is in Part R, so the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Spot the error: He is (P) / enough tall (Q) / to reach the shelf. (R)
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Enough" comes after an adjective: "tall enough", not "enough tall". The correct sentence is "He is tall enough to reach the shelf", so the answer is Part Q.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

47. Spot the error: Unless you do not (P) / work hard, (Q) / you will fail. (R)
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Unless" already carries a negative meaning, so "do not" makes a double negative; say "Unless you work hard, you will fail". The error is in Part P, so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Spot the error: The sceneries (P) / of Kashmir (Q) / are beautiful. (R)
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Scenery" is uncountable and has no plural form; say "The scenery of Kashmir is beautiful". The error is in Part P, so the answer is Part P.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

49. Spot the error: Hardly had he (P) / entered the room (Q) / when the phone rang. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Hardly had he entered… when…" is the correct inversion and pairing; all three parts are grammatically sound. Do not over-think and hunt for an error that is not there, so the answer is No error.
Source: PYQ-style

50. Spot the error: One should (P) / do his (Q) / duty honestly. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: When the subject is "one", the pronoun must also be "one's": "One should do one's duty honestly". Using "his" breaks pronoun consistency, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style
