101. Spot the error: Part P: One of my friends / Part Q: are going / Part R: to Delhi tomorrow. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting trap is choosing Part R, but the real error is in Part Q. After "One of + plural noun", the verb must be singular because the true subject is "One", so "are" should be "is". The answer is Part Q.

102. Spot the error: Part P: Neither of the two boys / Part Q: have done / Part R: his homework. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb, so "have" in Part Q must be "has". Learners often pick Part R thinking "his" is wrong, but the singular pronoun "his" is actually correct here. The answer is Part Q.

Answer: (a)
Solution: With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject "The teacher" (singular), so "were" should be "was". The trap is matching the verb with the nearer noun "students". The answer is Part Q.

Answer: (d)
