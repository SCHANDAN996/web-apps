1. Spot the error in the sentence: "He go (P) / to school (Q) / every day. (R)"
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to suspect the time expression "every day", but it is perfectly correct. With a singular subject "He" in the simple present tense, the verb must take "-s" and become "goes", so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: The trap is to check the pronoun "She", which is correct. "She" is third person singular, so the auxiliary must be "doesn't", not "don't", so the answer is Part Q.
