26. Choose the word closest in meaning to: ABANDON
(a) leave (b) keep (c) protect (d) repair
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Keep" and "protect" are tempting because they sound positive, but they are opposites of abandon. Abandon means to give up or leave completely, so the answer is leave.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (Beehive)

27. Choose the word closest in meaning to: BRAVE
(a) courageous (b) fearful (c) timid (d) weak
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Timid" and "fearful" are antonyms, not synonyms — a common trap when options mix opposites. Brave means showing courage, so the answer is courageous.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English (Honeydew)
