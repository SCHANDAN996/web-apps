101. Choose the word closest in meaning to ABANDON.
(a) keep (b) repair (c) forsake (d) cherish
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Keep" and "cherish" are antonyms and tempt those who confuse the word with "hold on to". Abandon means to give up or leave completely, so the answer is forsake.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (d)
Solution: "Rude" tempts because candid remarks can hurt, but candid simply means honest and open, not impolite. Frank is the exact synonym, so the answer is frank.
