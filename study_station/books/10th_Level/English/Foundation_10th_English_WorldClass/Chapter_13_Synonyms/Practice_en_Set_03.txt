51. Choose the synonym of ABANDON.
(a) Forsake (b) Keep (c) Hold (d) Protect
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Keep" and "hold" are tempting because they relate to possession, but they are opposites, not synonyms. Abandon means to give up or leave completely, so the answer is Forsake.
