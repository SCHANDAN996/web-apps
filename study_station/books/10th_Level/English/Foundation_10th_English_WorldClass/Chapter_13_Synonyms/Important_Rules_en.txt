# 📋 Important Rules & Tips — Synonyms

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | A synonym must match the **part of speech** of the given word. | "Abandon" (verb) → "Leave" (verb), not "Abandonment" (noun) | Options include the same root in a different form (noun/adjective) to confuse you. |
| 2 | Match the **shade of meaning**, not just the broad idea. | "Frugal" = careful with money (positive); "Stingy" = unwilling to spend (negative) | A near-synonym with the wrong emotional tone (positive vs negative) is the classic trap. |
| 3 | Learn words in **clusters**, not alone — one root gives 4–5 exam words. | Root *bene* (good): benefit, benevolent, beneficial, benediction | Examiner picks the least common word of a familiar family. |
| 4 | **Prefix reverses meaning** — check for un-, in-, dis-, mis- before answering. | "Hostile" = unfriendly; its opposite-family word "Hospitable" looks similar but means welcoming | Options pair a word with its look-alike antonym (e.g., "Hostile" vs "Hospitable"). |
| 5 | **Context decides the synonym** when a word has multiple meanings. | "Light" → "Bright" (illumination) or "Not heavy" (weight) | The question word is polysemous; only one option fits the intended sense. |
| 6 | **Formal vs informal** pairs are frequently tested. | "Commence" (formal) = "Begin" (informal) | A slangy or overly casual option is planted as a distractor. |
| 7 | **Degree matters**: synonyms differ in intensity. | "Angry" → "Furious" (stronger), "Annoyed" (weaker) | Options contain words of the same family but wrong intensity. |
| 8 | **Confusing pairs** (homophone-like or spelling-similar) are trap favourites. | "Elicit" (to draw out) vs "Illicit" (illegal) | The distractor differs by one letter and means the opposite. |
| 9 | **Foreign words** used in English appear often — learn their exact sense. | "Ad hoc" = for a specific purpose; "Bona fide" = genuine | Literal translation of the Latin/French phrase misleads. |
| 10 | **Phrasal-verb synonyms**: a single word can replace a phrase. | "Tolerate" = "Put up with"; "Postpone" = "Put off" | Options mix single words and phrases; both can be correct in meaning, so match the question's format. |
| 11 | **Eliminate antonyms first** — in 4 options, usually 1–2 are opposites or unrelated. | For "Candid" (frank): eliminate "Secretive", "Biased" first | The correct answer hides between two antonyms; elimination beats guessing. |
| 12 | When two options look equally close, pick the one with the **same connotation and register** as the question word. | "Request" → "Ask" (neutral), not "Demand" (forceful) | Over-thinking trap: the "fancy" word feels smarter but changes the meaning. |

## (b) Time Management — 15/45-Second Rule

| Type | What it looks like | Time | Strategy |
|------|-------------------|------|----------|
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Direct one-word synonym: "Synonym of BRAVE?" | 15 seconds | You either know it or you don't. Answer instantly or mark and move on. Never re-read. |
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Common root/prefix word: "Synonym of BENEVOLENT?" | 15 seconds | Decode via root (*bene* = good) and answer. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Word in a sentence: "His *candid* remarks hurt her." | 45 seconds | Read the sentence, guess meaning from context, then match options. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Two close options (e.g., "Frugal" vs "Stingy") | 45 seconds | Compare connotation and intensity; eliminate antonyms first. |

**Rule of thumb:** If you don't recognise the word in 15 seconds, switch to root-decoding + elimination. If that fails in another 30 seconds, skip — a blind guess costs negative marks.

## (c) Morphology — Root, Affix, Example

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Example Words | Synonym Link |
|--------------|---------|---------------|--------------|
| *bene* (Latin) | good | benevolent, benefit, beneficial | benevolent = kind |
| *mal* (Latin) | bad | malicious, malice, malfunction | malicious = spiteful |
| *dict* (Latin) | say/speak | predict, dictate, verdict | predict = foretell |
| *port* (Latin) | carry | transport, export, portable | transport = carry |
| *spect* (Latin) | look/see | inspect, spectator, prospect | inspect = examine |
| *cred* (Latin) | believe | credible, credit, incredible | credible = believable |
| *chron* (Greek) | time | chronic, chronology, synchronize | chronic = long-lasting |
| *phil* (Greek) | love | philanthropy, philosophy | philanthropy = charity |
| *phob* (Greek) | fear | phobia, claustrophobia | phobia = fear |
| *graph* (Greek) | write | biography, autograph, graphic | biography = life-story |
| Prefix *un-/in-/dis-* | not, opposite | unhappy, inactive, disagree | unhappy = sad |
| Prefix *pre-* | before | predict, preview, precaution | preview = forelook |
| Prefix *re-* | again | rebuild, recall, revise | recall = remember |
| Suffix *-ful / -less* | full of / without | hopeful / hopeless | hopeful = optimistic |
| Suffix *-ous* | full of (quality) | courageous, dangerous | courageous = brave |
