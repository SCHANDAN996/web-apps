# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Synonyms

## How Exams Test Synonyms

Synonyms are one of the most reliable scoring areas in SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD), RRB (NTPC, Group D), and Bank exams. Across these exams, vocabulary questions appear in every shift, and synonyms typically form a significant share of the vocabulary block. The pattern is remarkably stable:

**What examiners repeatedly ask:**

1. **Direct word-meaning recall** — A single word is given (often in capitals or bold), and you pick the closest meaning. This is the most common format. Words are drawn from a repeating pool: *abandon, ample, candid, diligent, obsolete, serene, vigilant, reluctant, fragile, genuine*.

2. **Synonym in a sentence** — The word appears inside a sentence, so context helps. Slightly easier, but the options are closer in meaning.

3. **Tricky pairs** — Options include a word that *sounds* similar but means something different (e.g., *eminent* vs *imminent*), or a word that is an antonym dressed up as a synonym.

**Sub-topics that repeat most often:**

- Common adjectives of character: *honest (candid), hardworking (diligent), brave (valiant), calm (serene)*
- Verbs of action: *begin (commence), stop (cease), forgive (pardon), delay (postpone)*
- Abstract nouns: *happiness (bliss), anger (wrath), fear (dread), shortage (scarcity)*
- Negative-tone words: *harmful (detrimental), unclear (vague), stubborn (obstinate)*

**The examiner's favourite traps:**

- **Sound-alike trap:** *adopt/adapt*, *elicit/illicit*, *eminent/imminent* — the wrong option looks like the question word.
- **Antonym trap:** One option is the exact opposite (*scarce* for *abundant*) placed to catch hasty readers.
- **Partial-meaning trap:** An option that is *related* but not the closest meaning (*sad* for *gloomy* is fine, but *angry* is not).
- **Over-thinking trap:** The simple, obvious answer is correct, but students reject it thinking "it can't be this easy."

## Top 10 High-Yield Questions (with Solutions)

**Q1.** Choose the synonym of **ABANDON**.
(a) keep (b) forsake (c) cherish (d) maintain
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Abandon* means to give up or leave completely — *forsake* matches exactly. (a), (c), (d) are near-antonyms. **Trap type:** Antonym trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style (repeated across SSC shifts)

**Q2.** Choose the synonym of **CANDID**.
(a) secretive (b) frank (c) clever (d) rude
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Candid* = truthful and straightforward = *frank*. (d) *rude* is the partial-meaning trap — candid speech can hurt, but rudeness is not its meaning. **Trap type:** Partial-meaning trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3.** Choose the synonym of **OBSOLETE**.
(a) modern (b) outdated (c) useful (d) rare
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Obsolete* = no longer in use = *outdated*. (d) *rare* is tempting because obsolete things become rare, but rare ≠ out of use. **Trap type:** Partial-meaning trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4.** Choose the synonym of **COMMENCE**.
(a) end (b) begin (c) delay (d) command
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Commence* = *begin*. (d) *command* is the sound-alike trap — it looks similar but means "to order." **Trap type:** Sound-alike trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q5.** Choose the synonym of **SERENE**.
(a) noisy (b) calm (c) serious (d) stormy
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Serene* = peaceful, calm. (c) *serious* is the sound-alike trap; (a) and (d) are antonyms. **Trap type:** Sound-alike trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6.** Choose the synonym of **RELUCTANT**.
(a) willing (b) unwilling (c) eager (d) lazy
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Reluctant* = hesitant, unwilling. (d) *lazy* is the partial-meaning trap — a lazy person may be reluctant, but the words are not synonyms. **Trap type:** Partial-meaning trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7.** Choose the synonym of **AMPLE**.
(a) scarce (b) sufficient (c) empty (d) narrow
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Ample* = more than enough = *sufficient*. (a) *scarce* is the antonym trap. **Trap type:** Antonym trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8.** Choose the synonym of **VIGILANT**.
(a) careless (b) watchful (c) violent (d) sleepy
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Vigilant* = alert and watchful. (c) *violent* is the sound-alike trap — similar spelling, totally different meaning. **Trap type:** Sound-alike trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9.** Choose the synonym of **GENUINE**.
(a) fake (b) authentic (c) generous (d) general
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Genuine* = real, authentic. (c) *generous* and (d) *general* are sound-alike traps; (a) is the antonym. **Trap type:** Sound-alike trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10.** Choose the synonym of **DETRIMENTAL**.
(a) helpful (b) harmful (c) determined (d) delicate
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** *Detrimental* = causing damage = *harmful*. (c) *determined* is the sound-alike trap. **Trap type:** Sound-alike trap.
**Source:** PYQ-style

## Applying the 15/45-Second Rule

**Q1 (ABANDON → forsake) — 15-second question.**
This is pure fact recall: you either know *abandon = give up* or you don't. Strategy: read the word, scan options, eliminate the three positive words (*keep, cherish, maintain*), mark (b), move on. Do not re-read. Target: under 15 seconds.

**Q6 (RELUCTANT → unwilling) — up to 45 seconds if needed.**
Here *lazy* creates genuine doubt. Use the 45-second budget: (1) recall the meaning — "not wanting to do something"; (2) test each option in a sentence — "He was reluctant to speak" → "He was unwilling to speak" ✓, "He was lazy to speak" ✗; (3) confirm and mark. If still unsure after 45 seconds, mark your best guess and move on — never leave it blank if there is no negative marking, and never burn 2 minutes on one word.

**Key takeaway:** About 4 out of 5 synonym questions are 15-second recall questions. Build your word bank so that the 45-second questions become rare exceptions, not the norm.
