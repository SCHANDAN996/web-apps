Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the word closest in meaning to ABANDON.
(a) Forsake (b) Keep (c) Protect (d) Repair
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Keep" is the tempting opposite — examiners place antonyms first to catch hasty readers. Abandon means to give up or leave completely, which matches "forsake", so the answer is Forsake.

2. Choose the word closest in meaning to BRAVE.
(a) Timid (b) Cowardly (c) Courageous (d) Weak
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Timid" and "cowardly" are antonyms placed to trap you. Brave means showing courage, so the answer is Courageous.

3. Choose the word closest in meaning to BEGIN.
(a) End (b) Finish (c) Commence (d) Close
Answer: (c)
Solution: "End", "finish" and "close" are all antonyms. Begin means to start, and "commence" is its formal synonym, so the answer is Commence.

4. Choose the word closest in meaning to HAPPY.
(a) Joyful (b) Angry (c) Sad (d) Tired
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Sad" is the direct opposite and a common trap. Happy means full of joy, so the answer is Joyful.

5. Choose the word closest in meaning to RAPID.
(a) Slow (b) Late (c) Lazy (d) Swift
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Slow" is the antonym trap. Rapid means very fast, which matches "swift", so the answer is Swift.

6. Choose the word closest in meaning to ANCIENT.
(a) Modern (b) New (c) Recent (d) Old
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Modern", "new" and "recent" are all opposites. Ancient means very old, so the answer is Old.

7. Choose the word closest in meaning to HONEST.
(a) Dishonest (b) Truthful (c) Cunning (d) Clever
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Clever" is the trap — a clever person is not necessarily honest. Honest means truthful, so the answer is Truthful.

8. Choose the word closest in meaning to ENORMOUS.
(a) Huge (b) Small (c) Tiny (d) Narrow
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Tiny" and "small" are antonyms. Enormous means extremely large, so the answer is Huge.

9. Choose the word closest in meaning to POLITE.
(a) Rude (b) Proud (c) Rough (d) Courteous
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Rude" is the opposite trap. Polite means well-mannered and courteous, so the answer is Courteous.

10. Choose the word closest in meaning to DIFFICULT.
(a) Tough (b) Simple (c) Easy (d) Smooth
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Easy" and "simple" are antonyms. Difficult means hard or tough, so the answer is Tough.

11. Choose the word closest in meaning to WEALTHY.
(a) Poor (b) Rich (c) Needy (d) Weak
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Poor" and "needy" are opposites. Wealthy means having much money, so the answer is Rich.

12. Choose the word closest in meaning to VACANT.
(a) Full (b) Occupied (c) Empty (d) Crowded
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Occupied" and "full" are antonyms. Vacant means unoccupied or empty, so the answer is Empty.

13. Choose the word closest in meaning to ASSIST.
(a) Hinder (b) Stop (c) Help (d) Block
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Hinder" is the antonym trap — it sounds similar in structure but means the opposite. Assist means to help, so the answer is Help.

14. Choose the word closest in meaning to FAMOUS.
(a) Unknown (b) Ordinary (c) Hidden (d) Renowned
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Unknown" is the opposite. Famous means well-known or renowned, so the answer is Renowned.

15. Choose the word closest in meaning to CALM.
(a) Peaceful (b) Noisy (c) Angry (d) Restless
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Restless" is the antonym trap. Calm means quiet and peaceful, so the answer is Peaceful.

16. Choose the word closest in meaning to PURCHASE.
(a) Sell (b) Lend (c) Return (d) Buy
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Sell" is the opposite action and a classic trap. Purchase means to buy, so the answer is Buy.

17. Choose the word closest in meaning to FATAL.
(a) Harmless (b) Deadly (c) Mild (d) Safe
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Harmless" and "safe" are antonyms. Fatal means causing death, so the answer is Deadly.

18. Choose the word closest in meaning to GRATITUDE.
(a) Thankfulness (b) Anger (c) Hatred (d) Pride
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Pride" is the trap — a proud person often lacks gratitude. Gratitude means thankfulness, so the answer is Thankfulness.

19. Choose the word closest in meaning to OBSERVE.
(a) Ignore (b) Miss (c) Watch (d) Avoid
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Ignore" is the antonym trap. Observe means to watch carefully, so the answer is Watch.

20. Choose the word closest in meaning to TRIUMPH.
(a) Defeat (b) Victory (c) Loss (d) Failure
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Defeat", "loss" and "failure" are all antonyms. Triumph means a great victory, so the answer is Victory.

21. Choose the word closest in meaning to CANDID.
(a) Secretive (b) Frank (c) Rude (d) Shy
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Rude" is the examiner's trap — candid speech can sound rude, but the word itself means honest and open, not impolite. Candid means frank, so the answer is Frank.

22. Choose the word closest in meaning to OBSOLETE.
(a) Modern (b) Costly (c) Useful (d) Outdated
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Costly" is the trap — old things are not always expensive. Obsolete means no longer in use or outdated, so the answer is Outdated.

23. Choose the word closest in meaning to FRAGILE.
(a) Brittle (b) Strong (c) Heavy (d) Flexible
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Heavy" is the trap — fragile things are often light, but weight is not the meaning. Fragile means easily broken, i.e. brittle, so the answer is Brittle.

24. Choose the word closest in meaning to RELUCTANT.
(a) Eager (b) Ready (c) Unwilling (d) Happy
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Eager" and "ready" are antonyms. Reluctant means not wanting to do something, so the answer is Unwilling.

25. Choose the word closest in meaning to AMPLIFY.
(a) Reduce (b) Magnify (c) Simplify (d) Weaken
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Simplify" is the examiner's trap — it looks similar to "amplify" but means to make easier, not bigger. Amplify means to increase or magnify, so the answer is Magnify.
