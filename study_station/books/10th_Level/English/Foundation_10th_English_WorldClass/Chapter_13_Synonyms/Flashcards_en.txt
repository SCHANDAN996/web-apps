Card 1
Front: What is a synonym?
Back: A word that has the same or nearly the same meaning as another word. Example: "happy" and "joyful".

Card 2
Front: Give the synonym of "abandon".
Back: Leave / forsake / desert. (To give up completely.)

Card 3
Front: Synonym of "brave"?
Back: Courageous, valiant, bold.

Card 4
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember "candid" = honest/frank?
Back: Think of a "candid camera" — it shows people as they truly are, with nothing hidden. Candid = open and honest.

Card 5
Front: Synonym of "begin"?
Back: Commence, start, initiate. ("Commence" is the formal exam favourite.)

Card 6
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: Are synonyms always interchangeable in every sentence?
Back: No. Synonyms match in meaning but not always in usage. We say "strong tea" but not "powerful tea", and "a powerful engine" but not "a strong engine". Always check which word fits the sentence.

Card 7
Front: Synonym of "end" (verb)?
Back: Conclude, finish, terminate.

Card 8
Front: What does the Latin root "bene" mean? Give two words from it.
Back: "Bene" = good/well. Words: benefit (a good result), benevolent (kind, wishing good).

Card 9
Front: Synonym of "difficult"?
Back: Hard, tough, arduous, strenuous.

Card 10
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember "enormous" = huge?
Back: Break it: e-NORM-ous — "beyond normal" size. Anything beyond normal in size is enormous = huge.

Card 11
Front: Synonym of "angry"?
Back: Furious, irate, enraged, annoyed (mild).

Card 12
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: The question asks for the synonym of "scarce". Options: (a) rare (b) plentiful (c) costly (d) common. Which trap is set here?
Back: Option (b) "plentiful" is the antonym placed to catch fast readers. Correct answer: (a) rare. Always check whether the question asks synonym or antonym.

Card 13
Front: What does the Latin root "port" mean? Give two words.
Back: "Port" = carry. Words: transport (carry across), export (carry out).

Card 14
Front: Synonym of "quick"?
Back: Rapid, swift, speedy, fast.

Card 15
Front: Synonym of "help" (verb)?
Back: Assist, aid, support. ("Assist" is common in SSC/Bank papers.)

Card 16
Front: What does the root "dict" mean? Give two words.
Back: "Dict" = say/speak. Words: predict (say before), dictate (speak for someone to write).

Card 17
Front: Synonym of "beautiful"?
Back: Pretty, lovely, attractive, gorgeous.

Card 18
Front: Synonym of "calm"?
Back: Peaceful, quiet, serene, tranquil.

Card 19
Front: What does the root "vid/vis" mean? Give two words.
Back: "Vid/Vis" = see. Words: visible (can be seen), vision (power of seeing).

Card 20
Front: Synonym of "true"?
Back: Genuine, real, authentic, accurate.
