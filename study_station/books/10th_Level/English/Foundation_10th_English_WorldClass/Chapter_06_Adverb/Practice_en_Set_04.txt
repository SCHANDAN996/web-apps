76. Choose the correctly spelt adverb.
(a) truely (b) trully (c) truily (d) truly
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option "truely" keeps the silent 'e' of "true", but when -ly is added to words ending in -ue, the 'e' is dropped: true → truly. So the answer is truly.
