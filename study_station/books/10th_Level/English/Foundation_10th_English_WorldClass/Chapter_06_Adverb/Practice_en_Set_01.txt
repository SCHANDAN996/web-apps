Adverb — Practice Set 1 (Questions 1–25)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the correct adverb: She sings very ______.
(a) beautiful (b) beauty (c) beautifully (d) beautify
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Beautiful" is an adjective and cannot modify the verb "sings" — that is the tempting trap. A verb is modified by an adverb, so the answer is beautifully.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

2. Identify the adverb in the sentence: He runs fast.
(a) He (b) runs (c) fast (d) None of these
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Runs" is the verb, not the adverb — do not confuse the two. "Fast" tells how he runs, so the answer is fast.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

3. Choose the correctly spelt adverb.
(a) truly (b) truely (c) trully (d) trewly
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students write "truely" by simply adding -ly to "true", but the silent 'e' is dropped before -ly. The correct spelling is truly.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

4. The adverb in "She always arrives on time" is:
(a) She (b) arrives (c) always (d) time
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Arrives" is the verb and "time" is a noun; neither shows frequency. "Always" tells how often she arrives, so the answer is always.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

5. Which word is an adverb of place?
(a) here (b) yesterday (c) quickly (d) very
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Quickly" shows manner and "yesterday" shows time, so both are traps. "Here" answers the question "where?", so the answer is here.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

6. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He did the work good. (b) He did the work well. (c) He did the work goodly. (d) He did the work best.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Good" is an adjective and cannot modify the verb "did" — this is the classic trap. The adverb form is "well", so the answer is "He did the work well."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

7. "Hardly" in "He hardly works" means:
(a) almost not (b) with great effort (c) very fast (d) always
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often think "hardly" comes from "hard" and means "with effort" — that is the trap. "Hardly" is a negative adverb meaning "almost not", so the answer is almost not.
Source: PYQ-style

8. Identify the adverb of time: "I will meet you tomorrow."
(a) will (b) tomorrow (c) you (d) meet
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Meet" is the verb and "will" is an auxiliary, not an adverb of time. "Tomorrow" tells when the meeting happens, so the answer is tomorrow.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

9. Choose the correct adverb: The baby slept ______ through the night.
(a) peaceful (b) peacefull (c) peace (d) peacefully
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Peaceful" is an adjective and "peace" is a noun; neither can modify "slept". The adverb is peacefully, so the answer is peacefully.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

10. Which sentence uses "enough" correctly as an adverb?
(a) He is enough tall to reach the shelf. (b) He is tall enough to reach the shelf. (c) He enough is tall to reach the shelf. (d) Enough he is tall to reach the shelf.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is placing "enough" before the adjective, as in option one. As an adverb, "enough" comes after the adjective it modifies, so the answer is "He is tall enough to reach the shelf."
Source: PYQ-style

11. The adverb in "The train arrived late" is:
(a) late (b) arrived (c) train (d) the
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Arrived" is the verb, not the adverb. "Late" tells when the train arrived, so the answer is late.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

12. Choose the correct word: She spoke ______ to the old man.
(a) kind (b) kindness (c) kindly (d) kinder
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Kind" is an adjective and "kindness" is a noun — both are traps here. The verb "spoke" needs an adverb, so the answer is kindly.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

13. "Never" is an adverb of:
(a) manner (b) place (c) frequency (d) degree
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Never" does not tell how or where, so manner and place are wrong. It tells how often (zero times), so the answer is frequency.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

14. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He drives very fastly. (b) He drives very fasting. (c) He drives very faster. (d) He drives very fast.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Fastly" does not exist in English — that is the common trap. "Fast" is both an adjective and an adverb, so the answer is "He drives very fast."
Source: PYQ-style

15. Identify the adverb of degree: "The soup is too hot."
(a) soup (b) is (c) hot (d) too
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Hot" is an adjective, not the adverb. "Too" tells to what degree the soup is hot, so the answer is too.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

16. Choose the correct adverb: The soldiers fought ______.
(a) bravely (b) brave (c) bravery (d) braver
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Brave" is an adjective and "bravery" is a noun; neither modifies "fought". The adverb is bravely, so the answer is bravely.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

17. In "She is quite intelligent", "quite" modifies:
(a) She (b) is (c) intelligent (d) none of these
Answer: (c)
Solution: Students often say "quite" modifies the verb "is" — that is the trap. "Quite" is an adverb of degree modifying the adjective "intelligent", so the answer is intelligent.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

18. Which word is an adverb of manner?
(a) slowly (b) soon (c) there (d) seldom
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Soon" shows time, "there" shows place, and "seldom" shows frequency. "Slowly" tells how an action is done, so the answer is slowly.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

19. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I have seen him yesterday. (b) I had saw him yesterday. (c) I seen him yesterday. (d) I saw him yesterday.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using the present perfect "have seen" with a definite past-time adverb like "yesterday", which is not allowed. The simple past is required, so the answer is "I saw him yesterday."
Source: PYQ-style

20. The adverb in "Please come in" is:
(a) please (b) in (c) come (d) none of these
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Come" is the verb and "please" is a polite marker, not an adverb here. "In" shows place/direction of the action, so the answer is in.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

21. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He works hardly to support his family. (b) He works hard to support his family. (c) He works hardily to support his family. (d) He works harderly to support his family.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The big trap is "hardly", which means "almost not" and reverses the meaning. The adverb meaning "with effort" is "hard" itself, so the answer is "He works hard to support his family."
Source: PYQ-style

22. Spot the error: Part P: She sings / Part Q: more sweeter / Part R: than her sister. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting choice is Part R, but "than" is correctly used with a comparative. The error is in Part Q: "sweeter" is already comparative, so "more" is wrong; it should be "more sweetly" or simply "sweeter". The error lies in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Choose the correct option: "______ did you arrive?" — "An hour ago."
(a) How (b) When (c) Where (d) Why
Answer: (b)
Solution: "How" asks about manner and "Where" about place, but the reply "An hour ago" gives time. The interrogative adverb for time is "when", so the answer is When.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

24. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is enough strong to lift the box. (b) Enough strong he is to lift the box. (c) He is strongly enough to lift the box. (d) He is strong enough to lift the box.
Answer: (d)
Solution: Option one wrongly places "enough" before the adjective, and option three wrongly uses the adverb "strongly" after "is". "Enough" follows the adjective "strong", so the answer is "He is strong enough to lift the box."
Source: PYQ-style

25. Spot the error: Part P: He returned / Part Q: back home / Part R: late at night. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students accept "returned back" because it sounds natural in speech — that is the sound-right trap. "Return" already means "come back", so "back" in Part Q is superfluous. The error lies in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style
