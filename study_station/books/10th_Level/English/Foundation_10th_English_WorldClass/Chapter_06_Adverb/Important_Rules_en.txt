# 📋 Important Grammar Rules & Tips — Adverb

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | An adverb modifies a **verb, adjective, or another adverb** — never a noun. | She sings **beautifully**. / He is **very** tall. | "She is a beautifully singer" ❌ — adjective *beautiful* needed before noun *singer*. |
| 2 | Use an **adjective** (not adverb) after linking verbs: *be, seem, look, feel, taste, smell, sound*. | The soup tastes **good**. | "The soup tastes **well**" ❌ — *well* is an adverb here; the soup isn't "tasting skilfully". |
| 3 | **Hard** and **hardly** are different words. *Hardly* = almost not. | He works **hard**. / He **hardly** works. | "He works hardly to pass" ❌ — reverses the meaning completely. |
| 4 | **Fast, hard, early, late, daily, straight** — same form as adjective and adverb. No "-ly". | The train arrived **late**. | "He came **lately**" ❌ — *lately* means "recently", not "late". |
| 5 | **Too** = more than needed (negative sense); **very** = to a high degree (neutral). | The tea is **too** hot to drink. / The tea is **very** hot. | "I am too happy" ❌ for positive feeling — use *very happy*. |
| 6 | **Enough** comes **after** adjectives/adverbs but **before** nouns. | He is tall **enough**. / He has **enough** money. | "He is enough tall" ❌ — classic word-order trap. |
| 7 | **Much** modifies comparatives and past participles; **very** modifies positive degree. | **Much** better. / **Very** good. | "Very better" ❌ — double comparative error. |
| 8 | Adverbs of frequency (*always, often, never, seldom*) go **before** the main verb but **after** "be". | She **always** comes on time. / She is **always** punctual. | "She comes always late" ❌ — wrong position. |
| 9 | **Seldom, never, hardly, scarcely, rarely** are negative in meaning — don't add another negative. | He **seldom** goes out. | "He seldom does **not** go out" ❌ — double negative. |
| 10 | **Hardly/Scarcely... when** and **No sooner... than** — fixed pairs. | **Hardly** had I reached **when** it rained. / **No sooner** had I reached **than** it rained. | "No sooner... when" ❌ — mixed-up pair. |
| 11 | **Only** must sit next to the word it modifies — position changes meaning. | **Only** Ravi passed. / Ravi passed **only** English. | "He only ate rice" (ambiguous) — did he *only eat* it or eat *only rice*? |
| 12 | **Ago** (with past simple) vs **before** (with past perfect / no reference point). | I met him two days **ago**. / I had met him two days **before**. | "I have met him two days ago" ❌ — *ago* never with present perfect. |

## (b) ⏱️ Time Management — the 15/45-Second Rule

**⚡ 15-Second Questions (Fact-based — spot the rule, mark, move on):**
- Adjective vs adverb choice after a verb (*sing beautiful/beautifully*).
- Linking verb + adjective (*tastes good*).
- Fixed pairs: *no sooner...than*, *hardly...when*.
- Position of *enough* and *only*.
- *Too* vs *very*, *much* vs *very*.

**🔍 45-Second Questions (Inference-based — read the full sentence twice):**
- Error-spotting where the adverb is far from the word it modifies (intervening phrase trap).
- Sentences with *hard/hardly, late/lately* — decide the intended meaning first.
- Double-negative errors hidden in long sentences (*seldom...not*).
- Improvement questions where two options look grammatically possible — check meaning, not just grammar.

**Rule of thumb:** If the answer depends on one word's form → 15 sec. If it depends on the sentence's meaning → 45 sec. Never spend 45 seconds on a 15-second question.

## (c) 🧬 Morphology — Roots & Affixes of Adverbs

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Example Words |
|---|---|---|
| **-ly** (suffix) | "in the manner of" — forms adverbs from adjectives | quick → quick**ly**, brave → brave**ly**, careful → careful**ly** |
| **-ward(s)** (suffix) | direction | for**ward**, back**ward**, home**ward** |
| **-wise** (suffix) | manner / direction | clock**wise**, other**wise**, like**wise** |
| **a-** (prefix) | "on / in" (forms adverbs) | **a**sleep, **a**live, **a**way, **a**part |
| **be-** (prefix) | "about / around" | **be**fore, **be**side, **be**low |
| **loc** (Latin root) | place | **loc**al → locally, al**loc**ate |
| **tempor** (Latin root) | time | **tempor**ary → temporarily, con**tempor**ary |
| **manu** (Latin root) | hand | **manu**al → manually, **manu**facture |
| **dict** (Latin root) | say | pre**dict**, contra**dict** → contradictorily |
| **chron** (Greek root) | time | **chron**ic → chronically, **chron**ology |

**Spelling alerts while adding "-ly":**
- true → tru**ly** (drop the *e*) | whole → whol**ly**
- happy → happi**ly** (*y* → *i*) | gentle → gent**ly** (drop *le*)
- full → ful**ly** (one *l* drops) | public → public**ly** (not *publically*)
