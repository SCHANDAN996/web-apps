126. सही passive form चुनें: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."
(a) The documents will signed by the manager tomorrow. (b) The documents would be signed by the manager tomorrow. (c) The documents will be sign by the manager tomorrow. (d) The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प past participle से पहले "be" गिरा देता है, लेकिन future passive के लिए "will be + V3" चाहिए। "will be" के बाद base form "sign" ही जाल है; तीसरा रूप "signed" आवश्यक है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

127. सही passive form चुनें: "Someone has stolen my wallet."
(a) My wallet is stolen. (b) My wallet had stolen. (c) My wallet was been stolen. (d) My wallet has been stolen.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present perfect active ("has stolen") passive में "has been + V3" बनता है। जाल विकल्प साधारण "is stolen" लगाता है, जो tense बदल देता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "My wallet has been stolen."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

128. सही active form चुनें: "The novel was written by her in just six months."
(a) She has written the novel in just six months. (b) She was writing the novel in just six months. (c) She wrote the novel in just six months. (d) She had wrote the novel in just six months.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Was written" simple past passive है, इसलिए active भी simple past "wrote" होना चाहिए। जाल विकल्प "had wrote" लगाता है, जो व्याकरणिक रूप से गलत है क्योंकि "had" के साथ तीसरा रूप "written" आता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She wrote the novel in just six months."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

129. सही passive form चुनें: "Open the window."
(a) Let the window been opened. (b) Let the window be opened. (c) The window should opened. (d) The window is opened.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Imperative वाक्य passive में "Let + object + be + V3" से बनते हैं। जाल विकल्प "let" के बाद "been" लगाता है, जो गलत है क्योंकि "let" के साथ bare form "be" आता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Let the window be opened."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

131. सही passive form चुनें: "The teacher is teaching the students grammar."
(a) Grammar has being taught to the students by the teacher. (b) Grammar is been taught to the students by the teacher. (c) Grammar is teaching to the students by the teacher. (d) Grammar is being taught to the students by the teacher.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present continuous passive "is being + V3" होता है। जाल विकल्प "is been" लगाता है — बहुत आम गलती; "been" कभी सीधे "is" के बाद नहीं आता; "being" आवश्यक है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Grammar is being taught to the students by the teacher."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

132. सही active form चुनें: "By whom was this letter written?"
(a) Who was wrote this letter? (b) Whom wrote this letter? (c) Who wrote this letter? (d) Who has wrote this letter?
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Passive का "By whom" active voice में subject बनकर "Who" बन जाता है। जाल विकल्प subject के रूप में "Whom" लगाता है, लेकिन "whom" केवल object form है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Who wrote this letter?"
स्रोत: PYQ-style

133. सही passive form चुनें: "They made him captain of the team."
(a) He is made captain of the team. (b) He was made captain of the team. (c) He was made the captain of the team by them. (d) He has been made captain of the team.

स्रोत: PYQ-style

134. सही passive form चुनें: "She gave me a gift." ("I" से शुरू करें)

स्रोत: PYQ-style

137. सही passive form चुनें: "Who taught you French?"
