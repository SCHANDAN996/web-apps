126. Choose the correct passive form: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."
(a) The documents will signed by the manager tomorrow. (b) The documents would be signed by the manager tomorrow. (c) The documents will be sign by the manager tomorrow. (d) The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option drops "be" before the past participle, but future passive needs "will be + V3". The base form "sign" after "will be" is the trap; the third form "signed" is required, so the answer is "The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow."
