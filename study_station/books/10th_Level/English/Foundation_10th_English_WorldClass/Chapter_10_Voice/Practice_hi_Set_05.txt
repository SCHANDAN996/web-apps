101. सही passive form चुनें: "She is writing a letter."
(a) A letter is been written by her. (b) A letter is being written by her. (c) A letter has being written by her. (d) A letter is wrote by her.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Present continuous passive में "is/are being + V3" बनता है। "Been" वाला विकल्प present continuous को present perfect से भ्रमित करता है, और "wrote" V2 है, V3 नहीं, इसलिए उत्तर "A letter is being written by her." है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English Grammar

102. सही passive form चुनें: "The teacher praised the boy."
(a) The boy was being praised by the teacher. (b) The boy is praised by the teacher. (c) The boy had praised by the teacher. (d) The boy was praised by the teacher.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Simple past passive में "was/were + V3" बनता है। "Was being praised" में गलती से continuous अर्थ जुड़ जाता है, और "had praised" passive नहीं है, इसलिए उत्तर "The boy was praised by the teacher." है।

104. सही passive form चुनें: "They will have completed the project by Monday."
(a) The project will have been completed by them by Monday. (b) The project will be completed by them by Monday. (c) The project will had been completed by them by Monday. (d) The project would have been completed by them by Monday.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Future perfect passive "will have been + V3" होता है। "Will be completed" में perfect aspect खो जाता है, और "will had been" व्याकरणिक रूप से असंभव है, इसलिए उत्तर "The project will have been completed by them by Monday." है।

105. सही passive form चुनें: "Open the door."

हल: Imperative वाक्यों में "Let + object + be + V3" लगता है। "Let the door opened" में आवश्यक "be" गायब है, इसलिए उत्तर "Let the door be opened." है।

106. सही passive form चुनें: "Someone has stolen my watch."

108. सही active form चुनें: "By whom was this poem composed?"

114. सही passive form चुनें: "Did she invite you to the party?"

उत्तर: (d)
