51. Choose the correct passive form: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter is written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option "was written" changes the tense to past, but the original is simple present. Simple present becomes "is/am/are + V3", so the answer is "A letter is written by her."
