1. Passive voice में बदलिए: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter has been written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter is written by her.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: लुभाने वाला विकल्प "is being written" गलत है क्योंकि मूल वाक्य simple present ("writes") में है, present continuous में नहीं। Simple present बनता है "is/am/are + V3", इसलिए उत्तर है "A letter is written by her."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English Grammar

2. Passive voice में बदलिए: "He plays football."
(a) Football was played by him. (b) Football is played by him. (c) Football is being played by him. (d) Football has played by him.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Has played" गलत है क्योंकि passive में "has" के बाद V3 से पहले "been" चाहिए। Simple present "plays" बदलता है "is + V3" में, इसलिए उत्तर है "Football is played by him."

3. Active voice में बदलिए: "The cake was baked by mother."

हल: "Bakes" गलत है क्योंकि passive वाक्य simple past ("was baked") में है, इसलिए active भी simple past में होना चाहिए। Simple past active में V2 होता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Mother baked the cake."

24. Passive voice में बदलिए: "People speak English here."
(a) English is spoken here by people. (b) English is spoken here. (c) English was spoken here. (d) English is being spoken here by people.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Is spoken here by people" परीक्षक का जाल है — "people", "someone", "they" जैसे अस्पष्ट subjects passive में हटा दिए जाते हैं, "by" के साथ नहीं रखे जाते। Simple present बनता है "is + V3", इसलिए उत्तर है "English is spoken here."
