# Active-Passive Voice — Practice Set 2 (Questions 26–50)
# Difficulty: Questions 26–40 Easy | Questions 41–50 Medium

26. Choose the correct passive form: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter is being written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The active sentence is in simple present tense, so the passive uses "is/am/are + V3". "Was written" is past tense and "is being written" is continuous, so both change the tense. The correct passive is "A letter is written by her."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

Answer: (a)

30. Choose the correct passive form: "She has finished the work."

Answer: (c)

Answer: (a)

32. Choose the correct passive form: "Someone stole my watch."
(a) My watch is stolen. (b) My watch was stolen. (c) My watch has stolen. (d) My watch was being stolen.
Answer: (b)
Solution: When the doer is vague ("someone"), we drop it in the passive. Simple past becomes "was/were + V3". The correct passive is "My watch was stolen."

42. Choose the correct passive form: "She gave me a book."
(a) A book is given to me by her. (b) A book was given to me by her. (c) I was given a book to her. (d) A book had given to me by her.
Answer: (b)
Solution: With two objects, either can become the subject; here the indirect object "me" needs the preposition "to" when the direct object leads. "I was given a book to her" wrongly attaches "to her". The correct passive is "A book was given to me by her."

Answer: (c)

47. Choose the correct passive form: "He made me laugh."
(a) I was made to laugh by him. (b) I was made laugh by him. (c) I am made to laugh by him. (d) I was being made laugh by him.
Answer: (a)
Solution: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: verbs like "make" drop "to" in the active ("made me laugh") but the passive restores it — "was made to laugh". "I was made laugh" copies the active pattern and is wrong. The correct passive is "I was made to laugh by him."

48. Choose the correct passive form: "They elected him president."
(a) He was elected president by them. (b) He was elected as president by them. (c) President was elected him by them. (d) He is elected president by them.
Answer: (a)
Solution: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: after "elect/appoint/make" in the passive, no "as" is added before the complement — "He was elected president". The tempting option with "as" is a classic over-correction. The correct passive is "He was elected president by them."
