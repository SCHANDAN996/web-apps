Card 1
Front: What is the difference between Active and Passive Voice?
Back: In Active Voice, the subject performs the action (Ram eats an apple). In Passive Voice, the subject receives the action (An apple is eaten by Ram).

Card 2
Front: What is the basic formula for converting Active to Passive?
Back: Object of active → Subject of passive; Verb → be + V3 (past participle); Subject of active → "by + object" (agent).

Card 3
Front: 🧠 MNEMONIC — What does "B-V3-BY" remind you of?
Back: Passive = **B**e form + **V3** + **BY** agent. "Be-V3-By" — the three B's of Passive Voice!

Card 4
Front: How does "She writes a letter" (Present Simple) change to passive?
Back: A letter is written by her. (is/am/are + V3)

Card 5
Front: ⚠️ EXAMINER'S TRAP — "The work has been completed by them." What tense is this, and what is the common error students make?
Back: Present Perfect Passive (has/have + been + V3). Trap: students forget "been" and write "has been complete" or "has completed by" — both wrong.

Card 6
Front: Convert to passive: "He is playing football."
Back: Football is being played by him. (Present Continuous: is/am/are + being + V3)

Card 7
Front: Convert to passive: "They built a bridge." (Past Simple)
Back: A bridge was built by them. (was/were + V3)

Card 8
Front: How do modals (can, should, must, may) change in passive voice?
Back: Modal + be + V3. Example: "You must obey the rules" → "The rules must be obeyed by you."

Card 9
Front: 🧠 MNEMONIC — What does "BING" stand for in continuous passive?
Back: **B**e + be**ING** + V3 — Continuous tenses always need "being" in passive. "He is teaching me" → "I am being taught by him."

Card 10
Front: ⚠️ EXAMINER'S TRAP — Can the sentence "He sleeps early" be changed into passive voice? Why or why not?
Back: No. Only transitive verbs (verbs with an object) can be made passive. "Sleep" is intransitive — there is no object to become the subject. Examiners love this trap!

Card 11
Front: Convert to passive: "Someone has stolen my watch."
Back: My watch has been stolen. (When the doer is unknown/unnecessary, drop "by + agent".)

Card 12
Front: What happens to pronouns when converting active to passive?
Back: Subject pronoun → object pronoun after "by": I→me, he→him, she→her, we→us, they→them. Object pronoun → subject pronoun: him→he, her→she, them→they.

Card 13
Front: Convert to passive: "Open the door." (Imperative sentence)
Back: Let the door be opened. (Imperatives use "Let + object + be + V3".)

Card 14
Front: Convert to passive: "Who taught you English?"
Back: By whom were you taught English? / Who were you taught English by? ("Who" becomes "By whom" in passive questions.)

Card 15
Front: What is the passive form of Future Simple: "She will help me"?
Back: I will be helped by her. (will/shall + be + V3)

Card 16
Front: Convert to passive: "The teacher gave me a book." (two objects)
Back: Either object can become the subject: "A book was given to me by the teacher" OR "I was given a book by the teacher."

Card 17
Front: Why is "The match was being watched by us" in Past Continuous Passive? Give the formula.
Back: was/were + being + V3. Active: "We were watching the match."

Card 18
Front: Which verbs are followed by a preposition other than "by" in passive? Give examples.
Back: Verbs with fixed prepositions keep them: known **to**, surprised **at**, annoyed **with**, interested **in**, pleased **with**. Example: "His behaviour surprised me" → "I was surprised at his behaviour."

Card 19
Front: Convert to passive: "People speak English all over the world."
Back: English is spoken all over the world. (General agents like "people/someone/they" are dropped in passive.)

Card 20
Front: Quick check — Active or Passive? "The cake was baked by my mother." How do you identify it in 5 seconds?
Back: Passive. Spot the signal: be-form (was) + V3 (baked) + by-agent. If you see "be + V3", it's passive.
