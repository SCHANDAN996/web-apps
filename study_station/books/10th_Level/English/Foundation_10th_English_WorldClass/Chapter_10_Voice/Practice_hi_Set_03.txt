51. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter is written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: लुभाने वाला विकल्प "was written" काल (tense) को भूतकाल में बदल देता है, जबकि मूल वाक्य simple present है। Simple present का passive "is/am/are + V3" बनता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "A letter is written by her."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

52. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "He teaches English."

58. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "Someone stole my watch."
(a) My watch is stolen. (b) My watch was stolen. (c) My watch has stolen. (d) My watch had stolen.

हल: "Has stolen" active है, passive नहीं, और "is stolen" काल बदल देता है। अज्ञात कर्ता होने पर "someone" हटा देते हैं और "was + V3" लगाते हैं, इसलिए उत्तर है "My watch was stolen."

60. सही कर्मणि वाच्य (passive) रूप चुनिए: "Who taught you English?"
(a) Who was taught you English? (b) By whom you were taught English? (c) By whom were you taught English? (d) By whom was you taught English?
