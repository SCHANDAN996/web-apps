76. सही passive form चुनें: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter is written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: लुभाने वाला विकल्प "is being written" गलत है क्योंकि मूल वाक्य simple present है, present continuous नहीं। Simple present active passive में "is/am/are + V3" बनता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "A letter is written by her."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English Grammar
