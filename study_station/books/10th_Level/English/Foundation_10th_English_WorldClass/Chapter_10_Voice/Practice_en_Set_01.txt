1. Change into passive voice: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter has been written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter is written by her.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option "is being written" is wrong because the original sentence is in simple present ("writes"), not present continuous. Simple present becomes "is/am/are + V3", so the answer is "A letter is written by her."

Answer: (b)
