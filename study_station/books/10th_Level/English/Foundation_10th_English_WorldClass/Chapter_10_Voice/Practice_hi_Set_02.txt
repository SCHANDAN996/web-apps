# क्रियात्मक-कर्मणि वाच्य (Active-Passive Voice) — अभ्यास सेट 2 (प्रश्न 26–50)
# कठिनाई: प्रश्न 26–40 आसान | प्रश्न 41–50 मध्यम

26. सही passive रूप चुनें: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter is being written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: क्रियात्मक वाक्य simple present tense में है, इसलिए passive में "is/am/are + V3" प्रयोग होता है। "Was written" past tense है और "is being written" continuous है, इसलिए दोनों tense बदल देते हैं। सही passive है "A letter is written by her."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

27. सही passive रूप चुनें: "He teaches English."
(a) English is taught by him. (b) English was taught by him. (c) English has taught by him. (d) English is being taught by him.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Simple present active, passive में "is/am/are + V3" बनता है। "Taught", "teach" का past participle है। इसलिए उत्तर है "English is taught by him."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

30. सही passive रूप चुनें: "She has finished the work."
(a) The work has finished by her. (b) The work is finished by her. (c) The work has been finished by her. (d) The work had been finished by her.
उत्तर: (c)

31. सही passive रूप चुनें: "The teacher will help the students."
(a) The students will be helped by the teacher. (b) The students will helped by the teacher. (c) The students will being helped by the teacher. (d) The students would be helped by the teacher.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Simple future active, passive में "will + be + V3" बनता है। "Will helped" में "will" के बाद सीधे V3 लगाना गलत है। सही passive है "The students will be helped by the teacher."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

32. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Someone stole my watch."
(a) My watch is stolen. (b) My watch was stolen. (c) My watch has stolen. (d) My watch was being stolen.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जब कर्ता अस्पष्ट हो ("someone"), तो passive में उसे हटा देते हैं। Simple past "was/were + V3" बनता है। सही passive है "My watch was stolen."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

33. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Open the door."
(a) Let the door opened. (b) The door is opened. (c) The door should opened. (d) Let the door be opened.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: आज्ञार्थक (imperative) वाक्य passive में "Let + object + be + V3" से बनते हैं। "Let the door opened" में "be" छूट जाता है। सही passive है "Let the door be opened."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

37. सही passive रूप चुनें: "He can solve this problem."

उत्तर: (c)

40. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Do not insult the poor."
(a) The poor are not insulted. (b) Let the poor not be insulted. (c) Let the poor not insulted. (d) The poor should not insulted.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: नकारात्मक आज्ञार्थक वाक्य "Let + object + not + be + V3" बनते हैं। "Let the poor not insulted" में "be" छूट जाता है। सही passive है "Let the poor not be insulted."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

41. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Who taught you English?"
(a) By whom was you taught English? (b) By whom were you taught English? (c) Who was taught you English? (d) By whom you were taught English?
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Passive में "Who" बन जाता है "By whom", और "you" के साथ "were" आता है, "was" नहीं — लुभाने वाला पहला विकल्प subject-verb agreement पर चूकता है। सही passive है "By whom were you taught English?"
स्रोत: PYQ-style

42. सही passive रूप चुनें: "She gave me a book."
(a) A book is given to me by her. (b) A book was given to me by her. (c) I was given a book to her. (d) A book had given to me by her.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: दो objects होने पर कोई भी subject बन सकता है; यहाँ indirect object "me" के लिए preposition "to" चाहिए जब direct object आगे आए। "I was given a book to her" गलती से "to her" जोड़ देता है। सही passive है "A book was given to me by her."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

43. सही passive रूप चुनें: "The manager will sign the cheque tomorrow."
(a) The cheque will be signed by the manager tomorrow. (b) The cheque will signed by the manager tomorrow. (c) The cheque would be signed by the manager tomorrow. (d) The cheque will being signed by the manager tomorrow.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Simple future "will + be + V3" बनता है। "Will signed" में "be" गिर जाता है, जो future-passive का मानक जाल है। सही passive है "The cheque will be signed by the manager tomorrow."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

44. सही passive रूप चुनें: "One should respect one's elders."

स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

45. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Why did he beat the boy?"
(a) Why was the boy beat by him? (b) Why the boy was beaten by him? (c) Why was the boy beaten by him? (d) Why is the boy beaten by him?
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Passive प्रश्नों में helping verb subject से पहले आता है: "Why was the boy…"। "Why the boy was beaten" में statement का क्रम रहता है, और "beat" यहाँ गलत participle रूप है। सही passive है "Why was the boy beaten by him?"
स्रोत: PYQ-style

उत्तर: (d)

47. सही passive रूप चुनें: "He made me laugh."
(a) I was made to laugh by him. (b) I was made laugh by him. (c) I am made to laugh by him. (d) I was being made laugh by him.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: ⚠️ परीक्षक का जाल: "make" जैसी क्रियाएँ active में "to" गिरा देती हैं ("made me laugh"), लेकिन passive में "to" लौट आता है — "was made to laugh"। "I was made laugh" active का पैटर्न नकल करता है और गलत है। सही passive है "I was made to laugh by him."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

48. सही passive रूप चुनें: "They elected him president."
(a) He was elected president by them. (b) He was elected as president by them. (c) President was elected him by them. (d) He is elected president by them.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: ⚠️ परीक्षक का जाल: passive में "elect/appoint/make" के बाद complement से पहले "as" नहीं लगता — "He was elected president"। "as" वाला लुभाने वाला विकल्प क्लासिक अति-सुधार (over-correction) है। सही passive है "He was elected president by them."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

50. सही passive रूप चुनें: "You must obey your parents."
