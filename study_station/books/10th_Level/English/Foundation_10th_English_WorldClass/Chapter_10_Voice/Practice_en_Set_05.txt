101. Choose the correct passive form: "She is writing a letter."
(a) A letter is been written by her. (b) A letter is being written by her. (c) A letter has being written by her. (d) A letter is wrote by her.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Present continuous becomes "is/are being + V3" in passive. Option with "been" confuses present continuous with present perfect, and "wrote" is V2, not V3, so the answer is "A letter is being written by her."
