# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Active & Passive Voice

## Explain It Like I'm 12

Imagine your mother asks: **"Who ate the mangoes?"**

You have two ways to answer:

**Way 1:** "Rohan ate the mangoes."
Here, Rohan (the doer) comes first. The sentence is all about **who did it**. This is **ACTIVE voice** — the doer is the hero of the sentence.

**Way 2:** "The mangoes were eaten by Rohan."
Here, the mangoes (the receiver of the action) come first. The sentence is all about **what happened to the mangoes**. This is **PASSIVE voice** — the receiver becomes the hero.

That's it. Voice is just a question of **who stands in the spotlight** — the doer or the receiver.

## The Daily-Life Example You'll Never Forget

Think of a cricket match:

- **Active:** "Dhoni hit the ball." → Camera on **Dhoni**.
- **Passive:** "The ball was hit by Dhoni." → Camera on **the ball**.

Same action, same people — only the camera angle changed!

## The Unforgettable Trick: "Object Becomes King" 👑

To convert Active → Passive, remember three steps:

1. **The object becomes the KING** (moves to the front of the sentence).
   *She wrote a letter → **A letter** …*
2. **The verb wears a costume** — "be" + V3 (is/am/are/was/were/been + third form).
   *…was **written**…*
3. **The old subject hides behind "by"** (or disappears if unimportant).
   *…**by her**.*

**Active:** She wrote a letter.
**Passive:** A letter **was written** by her.

## The Hardest Part Made Easy: Tense Never Changes!

Students panic: "The tense changed!" No — the tense **hides inside the "be" verb**:

| Active | Passive | What happened? |
|---|---|---|
| writes (present) | **is** written | "is" carries the present |
| wrote (past) | **was** written | "was" carries the past |
| will write (future) | **will be** written | "will be" carries the future |

The V3 (written) never changes. Only the little "be" helper dances according to the tense. 🕺

## One More Trap a 12-Year-Old Can Spot

Some verbs **cannot** become passive because they have no object — nothing to make king!
- "He **sleeps**." → Sleeps *what*? Nothing. ❌ No passive possible.
- "She **runs**." → Runs *what*? Nothing. ❌ No passive.

**Rule:** No object = No passive. (Intransitive verbs like sleep, go, come, die, run stay active forever.)
