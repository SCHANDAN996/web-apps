26. Choose the correct form: If it rains, we ___ at home.
(a) stayed (b) stays (c) will stay (d) staying
Answer: (c)
Solution: In a first conditional the main clause takes "will" + base form, so the answer is "will stay".
Source: NCERT Class 7–10 English Grammar

27. Fill in the blank with the correct form of "play": He ___ chess every day.
(a) plays (b) playing (c) play (d) played
Answer: (a)
Solution: A singular subject in the simple present takes -s/-es, so the answer is "plays".

28. Which verb is transitive in its sentence?
