126. Choose the correct form: They ___ football when it started to rain.
(a) were playing (b) was playing (c) plays (d) are play
Answer: (a)
Solution: An action in progress in the past with "they" takes "were" + -ing, so the answer is "were playing".

127. Choose the correct form: Look! The baby ___.

Solution: "Look!" shows an action happening now, so the answer is "is sleeping".
