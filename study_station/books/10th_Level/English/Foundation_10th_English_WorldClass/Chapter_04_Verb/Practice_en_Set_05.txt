101. Choose the correct form: We ___ here since 2015.
(a) lived (b) live (c) are living (d) have lived
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since" with a past starting point needs the present perfect, so the answer is "have lived".

102. Fill in the blank with the correct form of "sing": We are ___ a song right now.

Answer: (a)
Solution: "Are" with "right now" needs the -ing form, so the answer is "singing".
