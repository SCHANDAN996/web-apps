Card 1
Front: What does a verb show?
Back: An action (run, write) or a state (is, seem, belong).

Card 2
Front: What form follows a modal like can, must or will?
Back: The base form — "She can swim", never "She can swims".

Card 3
Front: What form follows has, have or had?
Back: The past participle (third form) — "has gone", "had eaten".

Card 4
Front: What does the mnemonic "Had, Has, Have — Participle Behave" mean?
Back: After had, has or have, use the past participle.

Card 5
Front: What is the trick "Look left before you choose"?
Back: The word just before the verb (modal, do, have, be) decides its form.

Card 6
Front: Give the three forms of "write".
Back: write – wrote – written.

Card 7
Front: Give the three forms of "begin".
Back: begin – began – begun.

Card 8
Front: "He go to school daily." What is wrong?
Back: With he/she/it in the present tense the verb takes -s: "He goes to school daily."

Card 9
Front: Each of the students ___ a book. (has / have)
Back: "has" — each takes a singular verb.

Card 10
Front: One of my friends ___ a doctor. (is / are)
Back: "is" — the verb agrees with "one".

Card 11
Front: How do you tell a transitive verb?
Back: Ask "what?" or "whom?" after it. If there is an answer (an object), it is transitive.

Card 12
Front: Is "slept" in "The baby slept" transitive or intransitive?
Back: Intransitive — there is no object.

Card 13
Front: What form follows did, does or do?
Back: The base form — "Did you go?", not "Did you went?"

Card 14
Front: Examiner's trap: "I have went there." How do you fix it?
Back: Use the past participle: "I have gone there."

Card 15
Front: Examiner's trap: "The news are good." Correct?
Back: No — "news" is singular: "The news is good."

Card 16
Front: Which helping verbs come before an -ing form?
Back: is, am, are, was, were — "They are playing."

Card 17
Front: What is the -ing form of "run" and "swim"?
Back: running, swimming — the last consonant doubles.

Card 18
Front: Ram and Shyam ___ brothers. (is / are)
Back: "are" — two subjects joined by "and" take a plural verb.
