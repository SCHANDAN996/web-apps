# Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Verb

**Box 1 — "Look left before you choose"**
> The word before the verb decides its form.

**Box 2 — "Modal = plain"**
> can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, would + base form.

**Box 3 — "Had, Has, Have — Participle Behave"**
> After these, use the third form: gone, written, eaten.

**Box 4 — "Do brings you home"**
> do, does, did + base form: Did you go? Does she play?

**Box 5 — "He, She, It — add an S to it"**
> Present tense: He plays, She watches, It rains.

**Box 6 — "Each and Every walk alone"**
> Each, every, either, neither, everyone → singular verb.

**Box 7 — "One of many, still one"**
> One of the boys is… (singular verb).

**Box 8 — "News is news"**
> News, mathematics, physics, economics → singular verb.

**Box 9 — "What? Whom? Transitive!"**
> If the verb answers "what/whom", it has an object — transitive.

**Box 10 — "Irregular gang"**
> go-went-gone, eat-ate-eaten, write-wrote-written, see-saw-seen, take-took-taken — learn them as songs.

## Skip Strategy

1. **15 seconds:** verb-form and identify-the-verb questions — answer instantly or move on.
2. **45 seconds:** long error-spotting sentences — find the subject, then the verb, then check the signal word.
3. **Use options:** strike out any option where the verb form breaks a signal rule.
