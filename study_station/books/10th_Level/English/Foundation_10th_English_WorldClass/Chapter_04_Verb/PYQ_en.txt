# PYQ Analysis — Verb

## 1. How Often It Appears

Verb-based questions — correct form, subject–verb agreement and error spotting — appear in almost every English section of SSC (MTS, CHSL, GD), railway and state-level exams, and in school board grammar papers. Exact yearly counts vary, so this analysis describes the pattern rather than quoting numbers.

## 2. Sub-topic Weightage (qualitative)

- **Fill in the blank with the correct verb form** — the most common type.
- **Subject–verb agreement in error spotting** — very common in SSC.
- **Past vs past participle** — common.
- **Modals** — common in board exams and SSC MTS.
- **Transitive / intransitive** — occasional.

## 3. Ten High-Yield Questions (PYQ-style, with solutions)

1. She has ___ (write) a letter. → **written**. Hook: "wrote".
2. He ___ (go) to school every day. → **goes**. Hook: "go".
3. Did you ___ (see) the match? → **see**. Hook: "saw".
4. Each of the boys ___ a bicycle. → **has**. Hook: "have".
5. One of my friends ___ a pilot. → **is**. Hook: "are".
6. You must ___ (wear) a helmet. → **wear**. Hook: "wears".
7. They ___ (play) when it started raining. → **were playing**.
8. The news ___ shocking. → **is**. Hook: "are".
9. Spot the error: "He can speaks three languages." → **"can speaks" → "can speak"**.
10. Identify the intransitive verb: (a) bought (b) slept (c) wrote (d) ate — in "The baby slept" → **slept**.

## 4. Skip Guidance (15-second / 45-second rule)

- Questions 1–6 and 8 should take under 15 seconds.
- Long error-spotting sentences: if the error is not found in 45 seconds, mark and return.

## 5. Examiner's Traps Seen Again and Again

1. Past form used after has/have/had.
2. -s form used after a modal or did.
3. Plural verb after "one of", "each", "every".
