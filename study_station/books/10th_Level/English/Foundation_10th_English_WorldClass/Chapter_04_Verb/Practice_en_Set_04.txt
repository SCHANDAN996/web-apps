Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Choose the correct verb: The news ___ very good today.
(a) is (b) are (c) have (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" is singular, so the answer is "is".

77. Fill in the blank with the correct form of "watch": We are ___ the match right now.
(a) watch (b) watches (c) watched (d) watching
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Are" with "right now" needs the -ing form, so the answer is "watching".
