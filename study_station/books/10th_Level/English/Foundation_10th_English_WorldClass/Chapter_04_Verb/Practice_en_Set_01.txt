1. Choose the correct form: You must ___ your homework on time.
(a) finish (b) finishing (c) finishes (d) finished
Answer: (a)
Solution: After a modal like "must" use the base form, so the answer is "finish".
Source: NCERT Class 7–10 English Grammar

2. Fill in the blank with the correct form of "begin": You have ___ the work.
(a) beginning (b) began (c) begin (d) begun
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "have" use the past participle, so the answer is "begun".

3. Choose the correct form: The thief ___ by the police last night.

Answer: (c)
Solution: The thief receives the action in the past, so the passive "was caught" is correct: the answer is "was caught".
