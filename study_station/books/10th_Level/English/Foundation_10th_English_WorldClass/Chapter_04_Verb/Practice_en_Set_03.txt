51. Which sentence uses "lay" correctly?
(a) She lay down on the bed after lunch. (b) She lied down on the bed after lunch. (c) She laid down on the bed after lunch. (d) She layed down on the bed after lunch.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The past of "lie" (to rest) is "lay", so the answer is "She lay down on the bed after lunch."
Source: NCERT Class 7–10 English Grammar

52. Spot the part with the error: "He is / knowing / the answer / very well."
(a) very well (b) He is (c) the answer (d) knowing
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Know" is a state verb and is not used in the continuous form: "He knows", so the answer is "knowing".
