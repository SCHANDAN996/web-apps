# Important Grammar Rules & Tips — Verb

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1. Every sentence needs a verb | The sun **rises** in the east. | A phrase with no verb is not a sentence |
| 2. -ing form needs a helping verb | She **is** reading. | "She reading" |
| 3. After has/have/had → past participle | He has **written**. | "He has wrote" |
| 4. After a modal → base form | She can **dance**. | "She can dances" |
| 5. He/she/it + present → -s/-es | He **watches** TV. | "He watch TV" |
| 6. did / does / do + base form | Did you **go**? | "Did you went?" |
| 7. Each / every / either / neither → singular | Each boy **has** a bag. | "Each boy have" |
| 8. One of the + plural noun → singular verb | One of my pens **is** lost. | "One of my pens are lost" |
| 9. News, mathematics, physics → singular | The news **is** true. | "The news are true" |
| 10. Two subjects joined by "and" → plural | Ram and Shyam **are** friends. | "Ram and Shyam is" |
| 11. Transitive verbs take an object | She **bought** a book. | Treating "slept" as transitive |
| 12. Irregular verbs have special forms | go – went – gone | "goed", "writed" |

## (b) Time Management (15/45-second rule)

| Question type | Time | Why |
|---------------|------|-----|
| Verb form (past / past participle) | 15 seconds | Fact-based: you know the form or not |
| Identify the verb | 15 seconds | Look for the action or state word |
| Subject–verb agreement | 15–30 seconds | Find the real subject first |
| Error spotting in a sentence | 45 seconds | Inference-based: check each part |
| Transitive / intransitive | 15 seconds | Ask "what?" after the verb |

## (c) Morphology — verb endings

| Affix | Meaning | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| -s / -es | present, he/she/it | runs, watches |
| -ed | past (regular) | played, jumped |
| -ing | continuous / gerund | reading, swimming |
| -en | past participle (some irregular verbs) | written, eaten, taken |
| -ify / -ise / -ize | makes a verb from a noun or adjective | simplify, modernise, realize |
| en- | makes a verb ("to cause to be") | enlarge, enable |
