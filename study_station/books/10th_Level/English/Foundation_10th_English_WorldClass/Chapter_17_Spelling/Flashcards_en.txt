Card 1
Front: What is the "i before e" rule, and when does it flip?
Back: "i before e, except after c" — believe, achieve, but receive, deceive. Also flip when the sound is "ay": neighbour, weigh.

Card 2
Front: Which is correct — "occured" or "occurred"?
Back: "Occurred" — double the final consonant when a stressed syllable ends in consonant-vowel-consonant (ocCUR → occurred, refer → referred).

Card 3
Front: Mnemonic — how do you remember the spelling of "necessary"?
Back: "One Collar, two Sleeves" — one C, two S's: neCeSSary.

Card 4
Front: Examiner's Trap — "definately" looks right. What is the correct spelling and why is it a trap?
Back: Correct: "definitely" (from "finite"). Trap: the spoken sound "def-nit-ly" tempts an "a"; remember it contains "finite".

Card 5
Front: What is the rule for adding "-ing" to words ending in silent "e"?
Back: Drop the silent "e" before "-ing": make → making, write → writing. Exception: keep "e" in dyeing/singeing to avoid confusion with dying/singing.

Card 6
Front: Which is correct — "seperate" or "separate"?
Back: "Separate" — remember "there's A RAT in sepARATe."

Card 7
Front: Mnemonic — how do you remember "accommodation"?
Back: "Two Cots and two Mattresses" — double C, double M: aCCoMModation.

Card 8
Front: What happens to words ending in consonant + "y" when adding "-es" or "-ed"?
Back: Change "y" to "i": baby → babies, carry → carried. But keep "y" after a vowel: play → played, boy → boys.

Card 9
Front: Examiner's Trap — "liason" vs "liaison": why do students get this wrong?
Back: Correct: "liaison" (two i's). Trap: pronunciation "lee-ay-zon" hides the first "i"; it comes from French.

Card 10
Front: Which is correct — "existance" or "existence"?
Back: "Existence" — ends in "-ence" (like the verb "exist" + -ence). Compare: independent → independence.

Card 11
Front: What is the plural of "phenomenon" and "criterion"?
Back: Phenomena, criteria — Greek-origin words where "-on" becomes "-a".

Card 12
Front: Which is correct — "maintainance" or "maintenance"?
Back: "Maintenance" — from "maintain" but spelled with "-tenance". Trap: the verb has "ai", the noun does not.

Card 13
Front: How do you spell the number-word often misspelled as "fourty"?
Back: "Forty" — no "u", even though "four" has one. (Fourteen keeps the "u".)

Card 14
Front: Which is correct — "begining" or "beginning"?
Back: "Beginning" — stressed final syllable with CVC pattern doubles the consonant: beGIN → beginning. Same: running, planning.

Card 15
Front: What is the difference between "principal" and "principle"?
Back: "Principal" = head of a school / main (the principal is your "pal"). "Principle" = a rule or truth.

Card 16
Front: Which is correct — "questionaire" or "questionnaire"?
Back: "Questionnaire" — double "n" (French origin). Mnemonic: a questionnaire has many questions — double the n's.

Card 17
Front: What is the rule for words ending in "-ful"?
Back: Only one "l": beautiful, careful, grateful. But "full" itself has two l's.

Card 18
Front: Which is correct — "priviledge" or "privilege"?
Back: "Privilege" — no "d". Trap: people insert "d" by analogy with "knowledge".

Card 19
Front: How do you decide between "-able" and "-ible"?
Back: If the root is a complete English word, usually "-able": read → readable, comfort → comfortable. If the root is Latin-only, usually "-ible": visible, possible, terrible.

Card 20
Front: Which is correct — "embarass" or "embarrass"?
Back: "Embarrass" — double R, double S. Mnemonic: "Really Red and Super Shy" — 2 R's, 2 S's.
