## 📖 Part 1: Why English Spelling Is Tricky (and Why That's Good News)

English borrowed words from Latin, Greek, French and German — so its spelling is not always phonetic. *Knight* has a silent 'k', *psychology* a silent 'p'. This frustrates casual learners, but for exam aspirants it is **good news**: examiners repeatedly test the *same* pool of tricky words. Master about 300–400 high-frequency words plus the rules below, and you are exam-ready.

🧠 **Active Recall:** Why is English spelling not fully phonetic? Name two words with silent letters.

## 📖 Part 2: The 6 Golden Spelling Rules

**Rule 1 — "I before E, except after C"**
bel**ie**ve, f**ie**ld, ach**ie**ve — but rec**ei**ve, dec**ei**ve, conc**ei**t.
⚠️ Exceptions: *seize, weird, height, neighbour, weigh*.

**Rule 2 — Silent 'e' drops before a vowel suffix**
hope → hop**ing**, write → writ**ing**; but keeps before consonant suffix: hope → hope**ful**, sure → sure**ly**.

**Rule 3 — Double the final consonant (CVC rule)**
One-syllable word ending consonant-vowel-consonant doubles the last letter: run → run**ning**, stop → stop**ped**, big → big**gest**. But: rain → raining (two vowels — no doubling).

**Rule 4 — 'y' changes to 'i'**
happy → happ**i**ly, beauty → beaut**i**ful, carry → carr**i**ed. But keep 'y' before -ing: carry → carry**ing**.

**Rule 5 — '-full' becomes '-ful' as a suffix**
beauti**ful**, grate**ful**, faith**ful** — always single 'l' at the end.

**Rule 6 — Common silent letters**
- Silent K: **k**nee, **k**now, **k**nife
- Silent P: **p**sychology, **p**neumonia, recei**p**t
- Silent B: clim**b**, thum**b**, dou**b**t
- Silent H: **h**onest, **h**our, g**h**ost

🧠 **Active Recall:** Spell the -ing forms of *write*, *carry*, *run*. Why does *grateful* have one 'l'?

## 📖 Part 4: Greek & Latin Roots — Your Spelling X-Ray

Roots tell you *why* a word is spelt the way it is. Learn these 8 roots and hundreds of words become predictable:

| Root | Meaning | Derived Words |
|---|---|---|
| **scrib/script** (Latin) | write | describe, prescription, manuscript |
| **port** (Latin) | carry | transport, portable, export |
| **dict** (Latin) | say | dictate, predict, dictionary |
| **spect** (Latin) | see/look | inspect, spectator, respect |
| **phon** (Greek) | sound | telephone, phonetics, symphony |
| **graph** (Greek) | write | biography, photograph, autograph |
| **chron** (Greek) | time | chronology, chronic, synchronize |
| **bio** (Greek) | life | biology, biography, antibiotic |

### 📰 Reading Passage — Roots in Context

*Dr. Meena is a **biographer** who loves to **describe** the lives of scientists. Every morning she checks her **chronological** notes, picks up her **portable** recorder, and travels to **inspect** old **manuscripts**. Her latest subject **predicted** climate change decades ago. She records interviews on the **telephone**, studies his **phonetic** notes, and hopes her book will earn the **respect** of readers. Her **autograph** sessions are always crowded!*

👉 Notice: every bold word comes from the table above. When you know *chron* = time, you will never write "cronology".

## ⚠️ Examiner's Trap Box

1. **The "Looks-Right" Trap:** *Seperate, Definately, Occured* look correct because we mispronounce them. Trust the rule, not your ear.
2. **The Double-Letter Trap:** Examiners swap single/double letters — *Comittee/Committee, Begining/Beginning*. Note: the CVC doubling rule works only for one-syllable words (run → running). For longer words like *committee* and *beginning*, simply memorise which letters double — use the mnemonic in Part 3.
3. **The "All Correct" Trap:** Sometimes ALL four options look wrong to a nervous mind. Stay calm — apply rules one option at a time; exactly one is correct.

## 🔁 Linguistic Bridge — Hindi Speaker's Corner

| हम हिंदी में सोचते हैं | Common Error | Correct English |
|---|---|---|
| "सरकार" — बोलते समय 'n' हल्का लगता है | Goverment | **Government** (the 'n' is written and must not be dropped!) |
| "अलग" — 'seperate' जैसा लगता है | Seperate | **Separate** (think: there's "a rat" in sep**a**rate) |
| "निश्चित रूप से" | Definately | **Definitely** (from *finite* — de-**fini**-tely) |
| "आवास / निवास" | Accomodation | **Accommodation** (double c, double m — a big room has space for both!) |
