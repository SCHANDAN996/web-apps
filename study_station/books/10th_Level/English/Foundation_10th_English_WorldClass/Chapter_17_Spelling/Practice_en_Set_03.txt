51. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Receeve (c) Riceive (d) Receive
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option "Recieve" breaks the classic rule "i before e except after c" — after the letter c, e comes before i. So the answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

52. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Beleive (b) Belive (c) Beleeve (d) Believe

Solution: "Beleive" wrongly puts e before i; the rule "i before e" applies here because there is no preceding c. So the answer is Believe.

53. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occured (b) Ocurred (c) Occurred (d) Occurrd
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Occured" forgets that a stressed final syllable with a single vowel plus consonant doubles the consonant — occur becomes occurred with double c and double r. So the answer is Occurred.
