# 📋 Important Rules & Tips — Spelling Check

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | **"i before e, except after c"** — when the sound is /ee/ | bel**ie**ve, rec**ei**ve, ach**ie**ve | *receive* is often misspelt as "recieve" — the most repeated trap in SSC/Railway papers |
| 2 | **Silent 'e' drops** before a vowel suffix (-ing, -ed, -able) | hope → hoping, write → writing, love → lovable | "writeing" ❌ — but keep 'e' before consonant suffix: hope**ful**, care**less** |
| 3 | **Double the final consonant** — one-syllable word ending consonant-vowel-consonant (CVC) before a vowel suffix (-ing, -ed, -er, -est) | run → running, stop → stopped, big → bigger, hot → hottest | "runing" ❌; but *open → opening* (stress not on last syllable, so no doubling) |
| 4 | **Words ending in 'y' after a consonant**: y → i before suffix | happy → happier, beauty → beautiful, carry → carried | "beautyful" ❌; but y stays after a vowel: play → played, enjoy → enjoying |
| 5 | **-ful has only ONE 'l'** | beautiful, helpful, grateful, successful | "beautifull" ❌ — the classic one-letter trap |
| 6 | **-ceed / -cede / -sede**: only *exceed, proceed, succeed* take -ceed; only *supersede* takes -sede; all others take -cede | precede, recede, concede, supersede | "preceed" ❌, "supercede" ❌ — examiners love these two |
| 7 | **Double letters that never change**: accommodate (2c, 2m), committee (2m, 2t, 2e), embarrass (2r, 2s), occurrence (2c, 2r) | accommodate, committee, embarrass | "accomodate" ❌, "embarass" ❌ — count the doubles before answering |
| 8 | **-able vs -ible**: full root word + -able (comfort → comfortable); broken root + -ible (possible, visible, terrible) | readable, washable; audible, divisible | "responsable" ❌ — when in doubt, check if the root stands alone |
| 9 | **Silent letters are real letters**: know (k), write (w), psychology (p), honest (h), doubt (b), island (s) | knight, pneumonia, Wednesday | "Wensday" ❌, "dout" ❌ — pronounce the word slowly to catch them |
| 10 | **-ent vs -ant / -ence vs -ance**: no sound difference, so memorise the high-frequency words | independent (-ent), important (-ant); existence (-ence), attendance (-ance) | "independant" ❌ — the single most tested -ent/-ant word |
| 11 | **British spellings are standard in Indian exams**: -our, -re, -ise | colour, honour, centre, metre, organise | "center" is American — in SSC/Bank papers the British form is treated as correct |
| 12 | **One word or two?** — a lot (two), all right (two), in spite of (three); but: nevertheless, moreover, cannot (one) | a lot, cannot, in spite of | "alot" ❌, "can not" ❌ — spacing errors appear as "correctly spelt" options |

## (b) Time Management — 15/45-Second Rule

| Type | What it looks like | Time | Strategy |
|------|-------------------|------|----------|
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | "Choose the correctly spelt word" / "Find the misspelt word" — pure recall of one word | 15 sec | Scan each option letter-by-letter for the classic traps (double letters, ie/ei, -ful, silent letters). If you know it, mark it; never re-read. |
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Spelling of high-frequency exam words (accommodate, committee, government, separate, definitely) | 15 sec | These are memorised items — answer instantly from your flashcard bank. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Four near-identical options differing by one letter (recieve / receive / riceive / receeve) | 45 sec | Apply the rule (here: "i before e except after c"), eliminate two options, then compare the remaining two letter by letter. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | "One part of the sentence has a spelling error" — error hidden inside a long sentence | 45 sec | Read once for meaning, then re-scan only the content words (nouns/verbs/adjectives); function words (the, of, is) are never the error. |

**Golden rule:** If a spelling question is not solved in 45 seconds, mark your best guess and move on — spelling is recall, not calculation; extra time rarely helps.

## (c) Morphology — Root, Affix, Example

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Example Words | Spelling Note |
|--------------|---------|---------------|----------------|
| **scrib/script** (Latin) | write | describe, prescription, manuscript | de**scrib**e — not "disscribe" |
| **port** (Latin) | carry | transport, portable, import | sup**port** — double p, single r |
| **dict** (Latin) | say/speak | dictionary, predict, verdict | pre**dict** — one 'd' in the root |
| **spect** (Latin) | look/see | inspect, respect, spectator | pro**spect** — not "prospet" |
| **un-** (prefix) | not | unhappy, unable, unknown | Prefix never changes the base word's spelling: un + necessary = unnecessary |
| **dis-** (prefix) | not/opposite | disappear, disappoint, disagree | dis + appear = dis**a**ppear (one s, two p) |
| **-tion / -sion** (suffix) | act/state of | education, permission, decision | Verb ending -mit → -ssion (permit → permission); -de → -sion (decide → decision) |
| **-ly** (suffix) | in the manner of | quickly, happily, truly | true → truly (drop the e); happy → happily (y → i) |
| **-ness** (suffix) | state/quality | happiness, darkness, kindness | happy → happi**ness** (y → i); if the base word ends in n, both n's stay: thin → thin**ness** |
| **-ous** (suffix) | full of | dangerous, famous, courageous | fame → famous (drop e); courage → courageous (keep e after soft g) |
