Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Receive (c) Riceive (d) Receeve
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Recieve" is the classic trap because we often write "ei" after C. Remember the rule: "i before e, except after c" — after C we write "ei", so the correct spelling is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

2. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Beleive (b) Believe (c) Belive (d) Beleeve
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Beleive" tempts because of the "except after c" rule, but there is no C here, so "ie" comes first. The correct spelling is Believe.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

3. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Goverment (b) Governmant (c) Government (d) Govarnment
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Goverment" drops the silent N, which is a very common error. The word is "govern" + "ment", so the correct spelling is Government.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

4. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Environment (b) Enviranment (c) Enviroment (d) Invironment
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Enviroment" misses the N before "ment", a frequent exam trap. The correct spelling is Environment.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

5. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occassion (b) Ocasion (c) Occasion (d) Occassiom
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Occassion" doubles the S wrongly; the word has double C but single S. The correct spelling is Occasion.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

6. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Accommodate (b) Acommodate (c) Accomodate (d) Acomodate
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Accomodate" with single M is the most common wrong form. Remember: two C's and two M's — "aCCoMModate". The correct spelling is Accommodate.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

7. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Begining (b) Beginninng (c) Beginning (d) Biginning
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Begining" forgets to double the N. When adding "-ing" to a short verb ending in consonant after a single vowel, double the consonant: begin → beginning. The correct spelling is Beginning.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

8. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Neccessary (b) Neccesary (c) Necessary (d) Necesary
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Neccessary" doubles the C wrongly. Trick: a shirt has one Collar and two Sleeves — one C, two S's. The correct spelling is Necessary.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

9. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Definately (b) Definitely (c) Definitly (d) Defenitely
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Definately" with A is the trap; the word comes from "finite", so it keeps the I. The correct spelling is Definitely.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

10. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Separate (b) Seperate (c) Seprate (d) Saparate
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Seperate" is the most misspelt version; remember "there is A RAT in sepARATe". The correct spelling is Separate.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

11. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Tommorrow (b) Tomorow (c) Tomorrow (d) Tommarrow
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Tommorrow" doubles the M wrongly; the word has single M and double R. The correct spelling is Tomorrow.

12. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Friend (b) Freind (c) Frend (d) Friand
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Freind" reverses the vowels; use "i before e" (no C here) — a FRiEND is with you till the END. The correct spelling is Friend.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

13. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Writting (b) Wreting (c) Writeing (d) Writing
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Writting" doubles the T wrongly because "write" already ends in a silent E, which is dropped before "-ing": write → writing. The correct spelling is Writing.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

14. Choose the correctly spelt word.

15. Choose the correctly spelt word.

(a) Entreprenur (b) Entrapreneur (c) Entrepreneur (d) Entreprenure

(a) Millenium (b) Milenium (c) Milennium (d) Millennium
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Millenium" with single N is the tempting error; like "annual" (from Latin "annus" = year), it needs double L and double N. The correct spelling is Millennium.
