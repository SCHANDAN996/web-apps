76. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Receive (b) Recieve (c) Riceive (d) Receeve
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Recieve" is the classic trap because we hear the "ee" sound first, but the rule is "i before e except after c" — after C, E comes before I. The correct spelling is Receive.
