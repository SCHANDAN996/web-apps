26. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Riceive (c) Receive (d) Receeve
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option "Recieve" breaks the famous rule "i before e except after c". After the letter c, e comes before i, so the correct spelling is "Receive".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

27. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Beleive (b) Belive (c) Beleeve (d) Believe
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Beleive" tempts students who misapply the i-before-e rule, but there is no c before this pair, so i comes first. The correct spelling is "Believe".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

28. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occured (b) Ocurred (c) Occurred (d) Occurrd
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Occured" is tempting because many verbs just add -ed, but "occur" has a stressed final syllable ending in a single consonant, so the r doubles. The correct spelling is "Occurred".
