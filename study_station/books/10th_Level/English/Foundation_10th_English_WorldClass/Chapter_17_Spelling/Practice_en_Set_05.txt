101. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occurence (b) Occurrance (c) Ocurrence (d) Occurrence
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Occurrence" doubles both the C and the R — a classic double-letter trap. "Occurence" drops one R and is the most common wrong choice. The correct spelling is Occurrence, so the answer is (d).
