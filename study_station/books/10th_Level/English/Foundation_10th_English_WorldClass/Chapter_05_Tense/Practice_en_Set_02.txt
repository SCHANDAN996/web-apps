26. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She go to school daily. (b) She gone to school daily. (c) She going to school daily. (d) She goes to school daily.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "She go" tempts learners who forget the third-person rule, but a singular subject in the simple present takes "goes". "Going" and "gone" cannot stand alone as the main verb, so the answer is "She goes to school daily."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. Fill in the blank: The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rises (b) rise (c) is rising (d) has risen
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Is rising" sounds natural in speech, but universal truths and permanent facts always take the simple present. The subject "the sun" is singular, so the answer is "rises".

Answer: (b)
Solution: "Have seen" is the examiner's favourite trap — the present perfect can never be used with a definite past time like "yesterday". Definite past time takes the simple past, so the answer is "I saw him yesterday."

49. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I am having a car. (b) I have a car. (c) I had a car always. (d) I having a car.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Am having" is the trap — when "have" shows possession it is stative and is not used in the continuous form (unlike "having lunch", where it means eating). So the answer is "I have a car."
