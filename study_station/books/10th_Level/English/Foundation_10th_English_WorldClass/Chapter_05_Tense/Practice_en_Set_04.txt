76. Choose the correctly framed sentence in the present perfect tense.
(a) I have finished my work just now. (b) I just have finished my work. (c) I have just finished my work. (d) I have finished just my work now.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Just now" is a past-time marker and forces simple past, so the first option is wrong. "Just" as an adverb of time sits between the auxiliary "have" and the past participle, so the answer is "I have just finished my work."

77. By the time we reached the station, the train ______.
(a) has left (b) left (c) had left (d) was leaving
Answer: (c)
Solution: "By the time" signals that one past action was completed before another past action, so the earlier action takes the past perfect. "Left" alone would suggest both actions happened together, which is the trap. The earlier action needs "had left", so the answer is "had left".
