101. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) I am knowing the answer since morning. (b) I know the answer since morning. (c) I have known the answer since morning. (d) I had known the answer since morning.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Know" is a stative verb and is not used in the continuous form, so option (a) is the tempting trap. With "since morning" showing duration up to now, the present perfect is required, so the answer is "I have known the answer since morning."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

102. By the time we reached the station, the train ______.
(a) has left (b) is leaving (c) leaves (d) had left
Answer: (d)
Solution: "By the time" with a past action signals that the earlier action must be in the past perfect; "has left" wrongly mixes present perfect with a past context. The train left before we reached, so the answer is "had left."

103. Spot the error: Part P: He said that / Part Q: the earth revolved / Part R: around the sun. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is applying backshift blindly: universal truths do not change tense in reported speech even after a past reporting verb. Part Q should read "the earth revolves," so the answer is Part Q.
