Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. She ______ to school every day.
(a) goes (b) go (c) going (d) gone
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Every day" signals a habitual action, so the simple present tense is used, and with the third-person singular subject "she" the verb takes -s. "Go" would agree only with plural subjects, so the answer is goes.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

2. They ______ football in the park yesterday.
(a) play (b) played (c) playing (d) plays
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Yesterday" is a clear marker of the simple past tense, so the verb must be in its past form. "Play" and "plays" are present forms and "playing" needs a helping verb, so the answer is played.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

3. I ______ my homework now.
(a) am doing (b) did (c) do (d) done
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Now" indicates an action happening at the moment of speaking, which takes the present continuous tense (is/am/are + verb-ing). "Do" is simple present and "did" is past, so the answer is am doing.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

4. He ______ Delhi last year.
(a) visits (b) visit (c) visited (d) visiting
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Last year" points to a finished past time, so the simple past is required. "Visits" and "visit" are present forms, so the answer is visited.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

5. We ______ a movie tomorrow.
(a) watching (b) watch (c) watched (d) will watch
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Tomorrow" shows future time, so we use "will + base verb". "Watch" is present and "watched" is past, so the answer is will watch.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

6. The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rise (b) rose (c) rises (d) rising
Answer: (c)
Solution: Universal truths are always expressed in the simple present tense, and "the sun" is singular, so the verb takes -s. "Rise" would need a plural subject, so the answer is rises.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

7. She ______ her lunch before I reached her house.
(a) finished (b) had finished (c) finishes (d) has finished
Answer: (b)
Solution: When two past actions occur, the earlier one takes the past perfect (had + V3) and the later one the simple past. Finishing lunch happened before reaching, so the answer is had finished.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

8. Look! The baby ______.
(a) cry (b) cries (c) cried (d) is crying
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Look!" signals an action in progress right now, which needs the present continuous tense. "Cries" is a habitual present form and does not fit an action happening at this moment, so the answer is is crying.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

9. I ______ this book twice.
(a) have read (b) am reading (c) read (d) had read
Answer: (a)
Solution: An action completed at an unspecified time with relevance to the present takes the present perfect (have/has + V3). "Read" as simple past would need a definite past time, so the answer is have read.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

10. He ______ TV when I entered the room.
(a) was watching (b) watches (c) watched (d) is watching
Answer: (a)
Solution: A longer action interrupted by a shorter past action takes the past continuous (was/were + verb-ing). "Watched" would suggest two sequential actions, not an ongoing one, so the answer is was watching.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

11. Water ______ at 100°C.
(a) boil (b) boils (c) boiled (d) boiling
Answer: (b)
Solution: Scientific facts are stated in the simple present tense, and "water" is singular, so the verb takes -s. "Boil" would suit only a plural subject, so the answer is boils.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

12. They ______ here since 2015.
(a) lived (b) live (c) are living (d) have lived
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since + point of time" requires the present perfect (or present perfect continuous) tense. "Live" and "lived" cannot combine with "since 2015", so the answer is have lived.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

13. My father ______ a letter just now.
(a) writes (b) has written (c) is writing (d) wrote
Answer: (d)
Solution: This is an examiner's trap: "just now" means "a moment ago" and counts as definite past time, so the present perfect "has written" is wrong and the simple past is required. "Writes" and "is writing" are present forms, so the answer is wrote.
Source: PYQ-style

14. The train ______ at 6 a.m. every morning.
(a) leave (b) left (c) leaves (d) leaving
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fixed timetables and schedules are expressed in the simple present tense. "The train" is singular, so the verb takes -s, making the answer leaves.

15. While she ______, the phone rang.
(a) was cooking (b) cooked (c) cooks (d) is cooking
Answer: (a)
Solution: "While" introduces a longer background action, which takes the past continuous when the interrupting action (rang) is in the simple past. "Cooked" would make both actions sequential, so the answer is was cooking.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

16. I ______ him last week.
(a) have met (b) am meeting (c) met (d) meet
Answer: (c)
Solution: This is an examiner's trap: "have met" looks attractive, but the present perfect can never be used with a definite past time like "last week". Definite past time demands the simple past, so the answer is met.

17. She ______ English very well.
(a) speak (b) spoke (c) speaking (d) speaks
Answer: (d)
Solution: A general ability or habit is expressed in the simple present tense. "She" is third-person singular, so the verb takes -s, making the answer speaks.

18. We ______ the match before it started raining.
(a) won (b) win (c) had won (d) have won
Answer: (c)
Solution: Of the two past actions, winning happened before the rain started, so the earlier action takes the past perfect. "Won" would wrongly place both actions at the same time, so the answer is had won.

19. Listen! Someone ______ at the door.
(a) is knocking (b) knocked (c) knocks (d) knock
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Listen!" signals an action happening at this very moment, requiring the present continuous tense. "Knocks" expresses habit, not an ongoing action, so the answer is is knocking.

20. He ______ to Mumbai next Monday.
(a) goes (b) will go (c) gone (d) went
Answer: (b)

21. By the time we reached the station, the train ______.
(a) had left (b) has left (c) left (d) leaves
Answer: (a)
Solution: "By the time" with a past action means the other action was already completed earlier, so the past perfect is needed. "Left" would suggest the train left at the same moment we reached, so the answer is had left.

22. I ______ in this city for ten years now.
(a) live (b) lived (c) am living (d) have been living
Answer: (d)
Solution: "For ten years now" shows an action that began in the past and continues up to the present, best expressed by the present perfect continuous. "Live" ignores the duration, so the answer is have been living.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

23. Spot the error: Part P: He has gone / Part Q: to Delhi / Part R: yesterday. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is an examiner's trap: the present perfect "has gone" cannot be used with a definite past time like "yesterday"; it must be replaced by the simple past "went". Parts Q and R are fine on their own, so the error lies in Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

24. When I ______ him, he was working in the garden.
(a) see (b) had seen (c) saw (d) have seen
Answer: (c)
Solution: The short interrupting action takes the simple past while the ongoing background action (was working) takes the past continuous. "Had seen" would wrongly make seeing the earlier of two completed actions, so the answer is saw.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

25. She ______ her project before the deadline tomorrow.
(a) will finish (b) will have finished (c) finished (d) finishes
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Before the deadline tomorrow" indicates completion by a future point of time, which takes the future perfect (will have + V3). "Will finish" only promises the action without stressing completion before the deadline, so the answer is will have finished.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
