51. Choose the correctly framed sentence in the simple present tense.
(a) She go to school daily (b) She going to school daily (c) She goes to school daily (d) She gone to school daily
Answer: (c)
Solution: With a third-person singular subject in the simple present, the verb takes -s/-es. Option one drops the -es, and the other two use non-finite forms that cannot stand alone. So the answer is "She goes to school daily".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. Fill in the blank: The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rise (b) rises (c) is rising (d) has risen
Answer: (b)
Solution: Universal truths are always expressed in the simple present tense, even though "is rising" may sound natural in speech. So the answer is "rises".

53. Fill in the blank: Look! The children ______ in the park.
(a) are playing (b) played (c) play (d) have played
Answer: (a)
Solution: Words like "Look!" and "Listen!" signal an action happening right now, which needs the present continuous. So the answer is "are playing".

61. Spot the error: Part P: I have seen / Part Q: that movie / Part R: last week. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that the sentence sounds perfectly natural in speech, but the present perfect can never be used with a definite past time like "last week"; it must be "I saw". So the error is in Part P.
