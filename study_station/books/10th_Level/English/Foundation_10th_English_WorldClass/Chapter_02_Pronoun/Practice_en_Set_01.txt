Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the correct pronoun: "Ravi is a good boy. ___ always helps others."
(a) Him (b) He (c) His (d) They
Answer: (b)
Solution: The blank is the subject of the verb "helps", so a subject pronoun is needed. "Him" and "His" are object and possessive forms, and "They" does not match the singular "Ravi", so the answer is He.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

2. Choose the correct pronoun: "This book belongs to me. It is ___."
(a) my (b) me (c) I (d) mine
Answer: (d)
Solution: When a possessive pronoun stands alone without a noun after it, we use "mine", not "my". "My" must be followed by a noun, so the answer is mine.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

3. Choose the correct pronoun: "Sita and Geeta are friends. ___ go to school together."
(a) They (b) She (c) He (d) It
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two persons joined by "and" make a plural subject, so the plural pronoun is required. "She" and "He" are singular, so the answer is They.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

4. Choose the correct pronoun: "The dog wagged ___ tail."
(a) it's (b) its (c) their (d) his
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "it's", which means "it is" and is wrong here; the possessive of "it" has no apostrophe. "Their" is plural and "his" is for male persons, so the answer is its.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

5. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ am a student of Class 10."
(a) Me (b) Mine (c) My (d) I
Answer: (d)
Solution: The blank is the subject of "am", and only the subject pronoun fits. "Me" is an object form and "Mine/My" are possessives, so the answer is I.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

6. Choose the correct pronoun: "Please give the pen to ___."
(a) I (b) mine (c) my (d) me
Answer: (d)
Solution: After the preposition "to", the object form of the pronoun is used. "I" is a subject form and "my/mine" are possessives, so the answer is me.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

7. Choose the correct pronoun: "The children did the homework ___."
(a) themself (b) themselves (c) theirself (d) itself
Answer: (b)
Solution: The plural reflexive pronoun of "they" is "themselves"; "themself" and "theirself" are not standard English forms. "Itself" is singular, so the answer is themselves.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

8. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ is raining heavily today."
(a) This (b) He (c) It (d) There
Answer: (c)
Solution: For weather, time and distance, English uses the dummy subject "It". "He" refers to a person, so the answer is It.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

9. Choose the correct pronoun: "Everyone should do ___ duty."
(a) his (b) their (c) her (d) its
Answer: (a)
Solution: In traditional exam grammar, indefinite pronouns like "everyone" take a singular pronoun, and "his" is the conventional choice in such questions. "Their" is plural, so the answer is his.
Source: PYQ-style

10. Choose the correct pronoun: "Between you and ___, this plan will fail."
(a) I (b) me (c) mine (d) myself
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "I", which sounds polite but is wrong because the preposition "between" needs an object pronoun. "Mine" and "myself" do not fit, so the answer is me.
Source: PYQ-style

11. Choose the correct pronoun: "This is the boy ___ won the first prize."
(a) which (b) whom (c) who (d) whose
Answer: (c)
Solution: The relative pronoun for a person acting as the subject of "won" is "who". "Which" is for things and "whom" is an object form, so the answer is who.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

12. Choose the correct pronoun: "The pen ___ I bought yesterday is lost."
(a) who (b) whom (c) whose (d) which
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Pen" is a thing, so the relative pronoun is "which" (or "that"). "Who" and "whom" refer to persons, so the answer is which.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

13. Choose the correct pronoun: "One should respect ___ parents."
(a) his (b) their (c) one's (d) its
Answer: (c)
Solution: When the subject is "one", the following pronoun must also be "one's" to keep consistency. "His" and "their" break this rule, so the answer is one's.
Source: PYQ-style

14. Choose the correct pronoun: "She hurt ___ while cutting vegetables."
(a) her (b) hers (c) she (d) herself
Answer: (d)
Solution: When the subject and object are the same person, a reflexive pronoun is used. "Her" would mean she hurt another person, so the answer is herself.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

15. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ of these two shirts do you like?"
(a) What (b) Who (c) Which (d) Whom
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Which" is used when choosing from a limited number of things, here two shirts. "What" is for open choice and "who/whom" are for persons, so the answer is Which.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

16. Choose the correct pronoun: "My father is a teacher. ___ works in a government school."
(a) He (b) She (c) It (d) They
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Father" is masculine singular, so the subject pronoun is "He". "She" is feminine and "It" is for things, so the answer is He.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

17. Choose the correct pronoun: "This house is ours, not ___."
(a) their (b) them (c) theirs (d) they
Answer: (c)
Solution: A possessive pronoun standing alone is needed to match "ours". "Their" needs a noun after it, so the answer is theirs.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

18. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ is my best friend, standing near the gate."
(a) These (b) That (c) Those (d) Them
Answer: (b)
Solution: For one person at a distance, the demonstrative is "That". "These/Those" are plural and "Them" is an object pronoun, so the answer is That.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

19. Choose the correct pronoun: "Neither of the two boys has done ___ homework."
(a) his (b) their (c) them (d) they
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Neither of" is singular in exam grammar, so it takes a singular pronoun. The trap is "their", which treats it as plural, so the answer is his.
Source: PYQ-style

20. Choose the correct pronoun: "We enjoyed ___ at the picnic."
(a) us (b) our (c) ourselves (d) ours
Answer: (c)
Solution: The verb "enjoyed" here needs a reflexive pronoun matching the subject "we". "Us" and "ours" do not fit the structure, so the answer is ourselves.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

21. Choose the correct pronoun: "It was I ___ called you last night."
(a) whom (b) who (c) which (d) whose
Answer: (b)
Solution: The relative pronoun refers to "I" and is the subject of "called", so the subject form is required. "Whom" is an object form and "which" is for things, so the answer is who.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Choose the correct pronoun: "Each of the students has brought ___ own book."
(a) his (b) their (c) them (d) they
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Each of" is always singular, so the pronoun must be singular. The trap is "their", which sounds natural in speech but is plural, so the answer is his.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Choose the correct pronoun: "He is one of those boys ___ work hard."
(a) who (b) which (c) whom (d) whose
Answer: (a)
Solution: The relative pronoun refers to "boys" (persons) and is the subject of "work", so "who" is correct. "Which" is for things and "whom" is an object form, so the answer is who.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Choose the correct pronoun: "You and ___ must finish this work today."
(a) I (b) me (c) mine (d) myself
Answer: (a)
Solution: The blank is part of the subject of "must finish", so the subject pronoun is needed. The trap is "me", which is an object form, so the answer is I.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Choose the correct pronoun: "The teacher praised the girl ___ answer was correct."
(a) who (b) whom (c) which (d) whose
Answer: (d)
Solution: The blank shows possession — the answer belongs to the girl — so the possessive relative pronoun is needed. "Who" and "whom" show no possession, so the answer is whose.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar
