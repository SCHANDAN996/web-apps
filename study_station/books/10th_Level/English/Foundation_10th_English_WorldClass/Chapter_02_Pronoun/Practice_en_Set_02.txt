26. Choose the correct pronoun: "Ravi lost ___ pen in the class."
(a) his (b) him (c) he (d) himself
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Him" is tempting because it refers to Ravi, but we need a possessive adjective before the noun "pen", not an object pronoun. "His" shows possession, so the answer is "his".
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

27. Choose the correct pronoun: "The dog wagged ___ tail."
(a) it's (b) itself (c) it (d) its
Answer: (d)
Solution: "It's" is the classic trap — it means "it is", not possession. The possessive form of "it" is "its" without an apostrophe, so the answer is "its".
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

28. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ am going to the market."
(a) I (b) Mine (c) Me (d) Myself
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Me" tempts Hindi speakers because of "main ja raha hoon" patterns, but the subject of a verb must be in the subjective case. "I" is the subject pronoun, so the answer is "I".
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

29. Choose the correct pronoun: "This book belongs to me. It is ___."
(a) my (b) mine (c) me (d) I
Answer: (b)
Solution: "My" is tempting but it is a possessive adjective and must come before a noun; here there is no noun after the blank. The possessive pronoun "mine" stands alone, so the answer is "mine".

30. Choose the correct pronoun: "She hurt ___ while cutting vegetables."
(a) herself (b) hers (c) she (d) her
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Her" is tempting as an object pronoun, but when the subject and object are the same person, a reflexive pronoun is needed. "Herself" matches "she", so the answer is "herself".

31. Choose the correct pronoun: "Between you and ___, this plan will fail."
(a) I (b) me (c) mine (d) myself
Answer: (b)
Solution: "I" sounds polite and educated, which is exactly the trap — but after a preposition ("between") the objective case is required. "Me" is the object form, so the answer is "me".

(a) didn't he (b) didn't they (c) did he (d) did they
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Didn't they" is tempting, but "nobody" is already negative, so the question tag must be positive; and "nobody" takes "they" in tags. So the answer is "did they".

41. Spot the error: "Each of the students (P) / have submitted (Q) / their assignment on time. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part R looks like the error because "their" is plural, but the primary error is in Part Q: "each" is singular and takes a singular verb, so "have" must be "has". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style (Examiner's Trap: Intervening Phrase)

47. Spot the error: "The Ganges is (P) / one of the longest rivers (Q) / which flows through India. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part Q looks suspicious but "one of the longest rivers" is correct. The error is in Part R: after "one of the + plural noun", the relative pronoun refers to the plural "rivers", so the verb must be plural — "flow", not "flows". The error is in Part R, so the answer is Part R.
Source: PYQ-style (Examiner's Trap: Over-thinking)
