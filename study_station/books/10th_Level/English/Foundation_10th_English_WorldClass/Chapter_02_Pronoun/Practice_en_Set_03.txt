51. Choose the correct pronoun: "Ravi is a good boy. ___ always helps others."
(a) His (b) Him (c) He (d) Himself
Answer: (c)
Solution: The blank is the subject of the verb "helps", so a subject pronoun is needed. "Him" is an object form and "His" is possessive, so they cannot be the subject. The correct answer is "He".
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

52. Choose the correct pronoun: "The girls have done ___ homework."
(a) his (b) her (c) its (d) their
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Girls" is plural, so the possessive pronoun must also be plural. "His", "her" and "its" are singular. The correct answer is "their".
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

53. Choose the correct pronoun: "This pen is mine. Where is ___?"
(a) your (b) yours (c) you (d) yourself
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "is" we need a possessive pronoun that stands alone, not a possessive adjective. "Your" must be followed by a noun, so it is wrong here. The correct answer is "yours".

54. Choose the correct pronoun: "I saw Sita and ___ mother at the market."
(a) his (b) its (c) their (d) her
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sita is one female person, so the singular feminine possessive is required. "Their" would need a plural antecedent. The correct answer is "her".

55. Choose the correct pronoun: "The dog wagged ___ tail."
(a) it's (b) his (c) its (d) their
Answer: (c)
Solution: "It's" means "it is" and is a common trap; the possessive of "it" has no apostrophe. "His" and "their" do not match "dog" here. The correct answer is "its".

56. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ am a student of Class Ten."
(a) Me (b) I (c) Mine (d) Myself
Answer: (b)
Solution: The blank is the subject of "am", and only "I" pairs with "am" as a subject. "Me" is an object form. The correct answer is "I".

57. Choose the correct pronoun: "Please give this book to ___."
(a) I (b) mine (c) me (d) myself
Answer: (c)
Solution: After the preposition "to", the object form is required. "I" is a subject form and cannot follow a preposition. The correct answer is "me".

(a) their (b) his (c) them (d) they
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Each" is singular, so the pronoun referring to it must be singular. "Their" looks natural but is plural and is the trap here. The correct answer is "his".

61. Choose the correct pronoun: "One should do ___ duty honestly."
(a) his (b) our (c) their (d) one's
Answer: (d)
Solution: When the subject is "one", the pronoun that follows must also be "one's" to keep consistency. "His" and "their" break the rule of agreement with "one". The correct answer is "one's".

62. Choose the correct option: "It was I ___ called you yesterday."
(a) who (b) which (c) whom (d) whose
Answer: (a)
Solution: The relative pronoun refers to "I", a person acting as subject of "called", so the subject form is required. "Whom" is an object form and "which" is for things. The correct answer is "who".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

63. Spot the error: "Between you and I, (P) / this plan (Q) / will never succeed. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: After the preposition "between", the object form "me" is required, not the subject form "I"; "between you and I" sounds polite but is grammatically wrong — this is a classic examiner trap. Parts Q and R are correct. The error is in Part P.

64. Choose the correct pronoun: "The teacher praised the boy ___ answer was correct."
(a) whose (b) whom (c) who (d) which
Answer: (a)
Solution: The blank shows possession — the answer belongs to the boy — so a possessive relative pronoun is needed. "Who" and "whom" do not show possession. The correct answer is "whose".

Solution: "Neither" is singular, so the pronoun must be singular. "Their" is the tempting wrong choice because "brothers" is plural, but the pronoun refers to "neither", not to "brothers". The correct answer is "his".

66. Choose the correct pronoun: "My house is bigger than ___."
(a) yours (b) your (c) you (d) yourself

67. Choose the correct pronoun: "The committee has submitted ___ report."
(a) its (b) their (c) his (d) them
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Committee" is a collective noun acting as one unit here (note the singular verb "has"), so the singular pronoun is required. "Their" would fit only if the members acted separately. The correct answer is "its".

68. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ of these two shirts do you like?"
(a) Who (b) Which (c) Whom (d) Whose
Answer: (b)
Solution: The choice is between things (shirts), so "which" is used; "who" and "whom" refer to persons. "Whose" shows possession, which is not the meaning here. The correct answer is "Which".

69. Spot the error: "Every student and every teacher (P) / have attended (Q) / the meeting yesterday. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: When two singular nouns are joined by "and" but each is preceded by "every", the subject is singular, so the verb must be "has attended", not "have attended". The plural-looking pair is the trap. The error is in Part Q.

70. Choose the correct pronoun: "He himself admitted ___ mistake."
(a) him (b) himself (c) his (d) he
Answer: (c)
Solution: Before the noun "mistake" we need a possessive adjective. "Himself" is reflexive and "him" is an object form, neither of which can show possession before a noun. The correct answer is "his".

71. Choose the correct pronoun: "Let you and ___ go to the library."
(a) me (b) I (c) mine (d) myself
Answer: (a)
Solution: After "let", the object form is used, even when two persons are joined by "and". "I" sounds formal but is the trap; "let" behaves like a verb taking an object. The correct answer is "me".

(a) whose (b) whom (c) who (d) which
Answer: (a)
Solution: The blank expresses that the car belongs to the man, so a possessive relative pronoun is needed. "Who" and "whom" cannot show possession. The correct answer is "whose".

73. Choose the correct pronoun: "The two sisters love ___ very much."
(a) one other (b) one another (c) themselves (d) each other
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Each other" is used for two persons, while "one another" is traditionally used for more than two; since there are two sisters, the first is correct. "Themselves" would change the meaning to self-love. The correct answer is "each other".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

74. Choose the correct pronoun: "Anyone can pass this exam if ___ works hard."
(a) they (b) one (c) he (d) it
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Anyone" is singular, and in traditional exam grammar the singular pronoun "he" is used for it. "They" is the tempting modern usage but is marked wrong in objective exams testing agreement. The correct answer is "he".
Source: PYQ-style

75. Choose the correct pronoun: "It is ___ who are responsible for this delay."
(a) ourselves (b) us (c) our (d) we
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "It is", the complement takes the subject form, and the verb "are" confirms a plural subject pronoun is needed. "Us" is the object form and is the common trap. The correct answer is "we".
