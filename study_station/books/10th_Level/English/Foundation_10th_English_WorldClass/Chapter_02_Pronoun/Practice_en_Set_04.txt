Practice Set 4 — Pronoun (Questions 76–100)
Difficulty: 20 Medium, 5 Hard

76. Each of the boys ______ done his homework.
(a) have (b) are (c) has (d) were
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "boys" is plural, but the subject is "Each," which is always singular and takes a singular verb. So the answer is "has."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

77. One should do ______ duty honestly.
(a) one's (b) his (c) their (d) our
Answer: (a)
Solution: "His" is the common trap, but when the subject is "one," the pronoun must also be "one's" throughout the sentence. So the answer is "one's."
Source: PYQ-style

78. Neither of the two brothers ______ passed the examination.
(a) have (b) are (c) has (d) were
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Have" attracts because "brothers" is plural, but "neither of" takes a singular verb. So the answer is "has."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

79. It is ______ who helped the poor man.
(a) me (b) myself (c) mine (d) I
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Me" sounds natural in speech, but after "It is," the pronoun acts as a complement of "is" and must be in the nominative case. So the answer is "I."
Source: PYQ-style

80. The teacher praised the boy ______ answer was correct.
(a) whose (b) whom (c) who (d) which
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Who" tempts because the boy is a person, but here we need a possessive relative pronoun showing that the answer belongs to the boy. So the answer is "whose."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

81. Spot the error: Part P: Everyone of the students / Part Q: have submitted / Part R: their assignments on time. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part R looks suspicious because of "their," but modern usage accepts it; the real error is in Part Q, since "everyone" is singular and needs "has," not "have." So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Between you and ______, this plan will fail.
(a) I (b) myself (c) mine (d) me
Answer: (d)
Solution: "I" is the classic sound-right trap, but after a preposition like "between," the objective case is required. So the answer is "me."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

83. He is one of those students who ______ always punctual.
(a) is (b) was (c) are (d) has been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Is" tempts because "one" is singular, but the relative pronoun "who" refers to "students" (plural), so the verb must be plural. So the answer is "are."
Source: PYQ-style

84. The Ganges is one of the holiest rivers ______ flow through India.
(a) whose (b) who (c) whom (d) that
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Who" is wrong because rivers are things, not persons; after a superlative ("holiest"), "that" is preferred over "which." So the answer is "that."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

85. My brother and ______ went to the market yesterday.
(a) me (b) myself (c) mine (d) I
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Me" is the everyday-speech trap, but the pronoun is part of the subject of "went," so the nominative case is needed. So the answer is "I."
Source: PYQ-style

86. Spot the error: Part P: Neither Ram nor his friends / Part Q: was present / Part R: at the meeting. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part P looks fine, but with "neither…nor," the verb agrees with the nearer subject "friends" (plural), so Part Q should be "were present." So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

87. This is the man ______ wallet was stolen in the bus.
(a) whose (b) whom (c) who (d) which
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Whom" tempts because it refers to a person, but the sentence needs a possessive link between the man and the wallet. So the answer is "whose."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

88. Each student and each teacher ______ given a certificate.
(a) were (b) was (c) have (d) are
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Were" tempts because two nouns are joined by "and," but when each noun is preceded by "each," the subject is singular. So the answer is "was."
Source: PYQ-style

89. ______ of the two sisters is a doctor?
(a) Who (b) Which (c) Whom (d) Whose
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Who" tempts because sisters are people, but when choosing between a limited number (two), "which" is correct. So the answer is "which."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

90. The committee ______ divided in their opinion on this issue.
(a) is (b) was (c) has been (d) are
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Is" tempts because "committee" is a collective noun, but "their" shows the members are acting separately, so a plural verb is needed. So the answer is "are."
Source: PYQ-style

91. He himself admitted ______ mistake.
(a) her (b) his (c) their (d) its
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Their" tempts as a gender-neutral choice, but the subject "he" is clearly masculine singular, so the possessive must match. So the answer is "his."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

92. Spot the error: Part P: It was me / Part Q: who called you / Part R: last night. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Parts Q and R are correct; the trap is in Part P, where "me" sounds natural in speech but the complement of "was" must be nominative "I." So the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

93. The dog wagged ______ tail happily.
(a) its (b) it's (c) his (d) their
Answer: (a)
Solution: "It's" is the spelling trap — it means "it is"; the possessive of "it" has no apostrophe. So the answer is "its."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

94. None of the milk ______ spilt on the floor.
(a) was (b) have been (c) were (d) are
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "none" can be plural with countable nouns, but "milk" is uncountable, so the verb is singular. So the answer is "was."
Source: PYQ-style

95. Hard (Examiner's Trap): It is I who ______ responsible for this decision.
(a) is (b) are (c) am (d) was
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Is" tempts because "It is" precedes, but the verb after "who" agrees with its antecedent "I," which takes "am." So the answer is "am."
Source: PYQ-style

96. Hard: The principal, along with the teachers, ______ present at the function.
(a) were (b) was (c) are (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "teachers" is nearer, but with "along with," the verb agrees with the first subject "principal" (singular). So the answer is "was."
Source: PYQ-style

97. Hard: Whom do you think ______ the prize yesterday?
(a) won (b) win (c) wins (d) winning
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "whom," which misleads learners into thinking the verb needs an object form; here "do you think" is parenthetical, and the sentence needs a simple past verb. So the answer is "won."
Source: PYQ-style

98. Hard: There goes Mohan and ______ friends to the stadium.
(a) her (b) his (c) its (d) their
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Their" tempts because "friends" follows, but the possessive refers to Mohan, who is masculine singular. So the answer is "his."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

99. Hard (Examiner's Trap): Spot the error: Part P: One should not boast / Part Q: of his wealth / Part R: in front of others. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part P is correct; the trap is in Part Q, because when the subject is "one," the pronoun must be "one's," not "his." So the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

100. Hard: The two brothers love ______ deeply.
(a) one another (b) one other (c) themselves (d) each other
Answer: (d)
Solution: "One another" tempts, but it is used for more than two persons; for exactly two, "each other" is correct. So the answer is "each other."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
