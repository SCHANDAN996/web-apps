Questions 76–100: Medium (20) and Hard (5)

76. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom: "To bite the dust"
(a) To suffer defeat or failure (b) To eat in a hurry (c) To clean the floor (d) To work in a mine
Answer: (a)
Solution: "To bite the dust" means to be defeated, fail, or come to an end. The image of dust misleads many into choosing a cleaning-related meaning, but the idiom comes from falling to the ground in defeat, so the answer is to suffer defeat or failure.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (Beehive)

77. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom: "A red herring"
(a) A rare fish dish (b) A lucky sign (c) An expensive gift (d) Something that diverts attention from the real issue
Answer: (d)
Solution: "A red herring" is a misleading clue or distraction that takes attention away from the main matter. The literal fish image tempts learners toward food-related options, but the idiom is about deception, so the answer is something that diverts attention from the real issue.
Source: PYQ-style

78. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom: "To burn the midnight oil"
(a) To waste electricity (b) To light lamps in a temple (c) To celebrate a festival (d) To work or study late into the night
Answer: (d)
Solution: The idiom refers to working or studying late at night, from the days of oil lamps. Choosing "to waste electricity" is a trap based on the word "burn", but the idiom praises hard work, so the answer is to work or study late into the night.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

79. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom: "At the drop of a hat"

Answer: (c)
Solution: "At the drop of a hat" means immediately and without any delay, from the old custom of starting a race or fight by dropping a hat. The ceremony option is a distractor built on the hat image, so the answer is instantly, without hesitation.

96. [Examiner's Trap] Choose the correct meaning of the idiom: "To read between the lines"
