101. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom: "To bite the dust"
(a) To eat in a hurry (b) To work in a mine (c) To clean the floor (d) To suffer defeat or failure
Answer: (d)
Solution: "To bite the dust" means to be defeated, fail, or come to an end — it has nothing to do with literally eating dust, which is the tempting literal trap. So the answer is "To suffer defeat or failure."
