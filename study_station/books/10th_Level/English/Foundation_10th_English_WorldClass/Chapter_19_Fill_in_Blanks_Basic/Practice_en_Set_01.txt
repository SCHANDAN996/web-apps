Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. She ______ to school every day.
(a) goes (b) go (c) going (d) gone
Answer: (a)
Solution: With a singular subject "she" in the simple present tense, the verb takes "-s". "Go" is tempting because it looks like the base form, but it is used only with I, you, we, they and plural nouns, so the answer is goes.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

2. I ______ my homework before dinner yesterday.
(a) finish (b) finishes (c) finishing (d) finished
Answer: (d)
Solution: The word "yesterday" signals simple past tense, so the past form is needed. "Finish" is tempting if you ignore the time marker, so the answer is finished.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

3. There ______ a book on the table.
(a) is (b) are (c) were (d) be
Answer: (a)
Solution: "A book" is singular, so it takes "is". "Are" is tempting because "there" often comes before plural verbs, but the verb agrees with the noun after it, so the answer is is.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

4. The children ______ playing in the park.
(a) is (b) was (c) has (d) are
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Children" is plural, so it needs a plural helping verb. "Is" is tempting because it sounds smooth, but it is singular, so the answer is are.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

5. He is ______ honest man.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent "h", so the sound is a vowel sound and takes "an". "A" is tempting because the spelling starts with a consonant letter — this is a classic sound-versus-spelling trap, so the answer is an.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

6. We ______ cricket every Sunday.
(a) plays (b) playing (c) play (d) played
Answer: (c)
Solution: "We" is plural and the sentence is simple present, so the base verb is used. "Plays" is tempting by habit with singular subjects, so the answer is play.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

7. The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rise (b) rose (c) rising (d) rises
Answer: (d)
Solution: Universal truths take the simple present tense, and "the sun" is singular, so the verb takes "-s". "Rise" is tempting as the base form, so the answer is rises.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

8. I have lived in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) for (b) at (c) from (d) since
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (a year, a date), while "for" is used with a period of time. "For" is tempting because learners mix the two, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

9. She has been waiting here ______ two hours.
(a) since (b) from (c) for (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Two hours" is a period of time, which takes "for". "Since" is tempting because the sentence is perfect continuous, but "since" needs a point of time, so the answer is for.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

10. The cat is sitting ______ the chair.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: We sit "on" a chair (a surface). "In" is tempting because we say "in a room", but a chair is a surface, so the answer is on.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

11. My birthday is ______ 15th August.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: Specific dates take "on". "In" is tempting because months take "in", but a full date needs "on", so the answer is on.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

12. He is good ______ mathematics.
(a) at (b) in (c) for (d) with
Answer: (a)
Solution: The fixed phrase is "good at" a subject or skill. "Good in" is tempting because Hindi speakers say "mein achha", but English uses "at", so the answer is at.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

13. ______ you like a cup of tea?
(a) Would (b) Will (c) Shall (d) Should
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Would you like…?" is the polite offer pattern. "Will" is tempting as a future marker, but it does not make polite offers, so the answer is Would.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

14. Neither Ravi nor his friends ______ present.
(a) was (b) is (c) has been (d) were
Answer: (d)
Solution: With "neither…nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject, which is "friends" (plural). "Was" is tempting because "Ravi" is singular and comes first — this is the proximity trap, so the answer is were.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

15. She sings very ______.
(a) beautiful (b) beautifully (c) beauty (d) beautify
Answer: (b)
Solution: The verb "sings" needs an adverb to describe how she sings. "Beautiful" is tempting because it describes her, but it is an adjective and cannot modify a verb, so the answer is beautifully.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

16. This is the ______ movie I have ever seen.
(a) good (b) best (c) better (d) well
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Ever seen" compares one movie against all others, so the superlative is needed. "Better" is tempting but compares only two things, so the answer is best.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

17. I ______ my keys. Can you help me find them?
(a) lose (b) have lost (c) lost (d) am losing
Answer: (b)
Solution: A past action with a present result (the keys are still missing) takes the present perfect. "Lost" is tempting as simple past, but the present connection demands "have lost", so the answer is have lost.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

18. Bread and butter ______ my favourite breakfast.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Bread and butter" is treated as one combined dish, so it takes a singular verb. "Are" is tempting because two nouns are joined by "and" — this is the compound-subject trap, so the answer is is.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

19. He ______ TV when I entered the room.
(a) was watching (b) watched (c) watches (d) is watching
Answer: (a)
Solution: An ongoing past action interrupted by another past action takes the past continuous. "Watched" is tempting because both events are past, but the ongoing action needs "was watching", so the answer is was watching.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

20. There is ______ water in the glass.
(a) many (b) few (c) much (d) a few
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Water" is uncountable, so it takes "much". "Many" is tempting as a quantity word, but it is used only with countable nouns, so the answer is much.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

21. Each of the boys ______ given a prize.
(a) was (b) were (c) have been (d) are
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Each of" is always followed by a singular verb, even though a plural noun follows it. "Were" is tempting because "boys" is plural — this is the intervening-phrase trap, so the answer is was.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

22. I look forward ______ meeting you soon.
(a) for (b) to (c) at (d) towards
Answer: (b)
Solution: In "look forward to", "to" is a preposition, not part of an infinitive, which is why it is followed by the "-ing" form. "For" is tempting because Hindi speakers translate "ke liye", so the answer is to.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

23. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) I have reached (c) I reached (d) had I reached
Answer: (d)
Solution: When a sentence begins with "Hardly", the subject and auxiliary are inverted, and the past perfect is used. "I had reached" is tempting because the words are correct, but the order is wrong after a negative adverb, so the answer is had I reached.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

24. One of my friends ______ a doctor.
(a) is (b) are (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: The real subject is "One", which is singular; "of my friends" is only an intervening phrase. "Are" is tempting because "friends" sits right before the verb — this is the intervening-phrase trap, so the answer is is.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

25. Unless you work hard, you ______ pass the exam.
(a) will (b) will not (c) would (d) can
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Unless" already means "if not", so the main clause needs a negative result. "Will" is tempting because the sentence sounds positive, but with "unless" it would reverse the meaning, so the answer is will not.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar
