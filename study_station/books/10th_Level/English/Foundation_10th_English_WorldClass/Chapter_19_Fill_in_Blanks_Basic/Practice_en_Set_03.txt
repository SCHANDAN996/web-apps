Questions 51–60: Easy | Questions 61–75: Medium

51. She ______ to school every day.
(a) go (b) going (c) goes (d) gone
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Goes" is tempting only if you forget the subject; with a third-person singular subject "she" in the simple present, the verb takes -s. "Go" would need a plural subject, so the answer is goes.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

52. I ______ my homework before dinner yesterday.
(a) finish (b) finished (c) finishes (d) finishing
Answer: (b)
Solution: The word "yesterday" signals simple past tense, so the past form is required. "Finish" and "finishes" are present forms, so the answer is finished.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

53. There ______ many books on the table.
(a) is (b) been (c) was (d) are
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Is" tempts because "there" feels singular, but the verb agrees with the real subject "many books", which is plural. So the answer is are.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

54. He is ______ honest man.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (d)
Solution: "A" tempts because "honest" starts with the letter h, but the h is silent and the word begins with a vowel sound, which takes "an". So the answer is an.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

55. The cat is sitting ______ the chair.
(a) on (b) in (c) at (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: We sit "on" a surface like a chair; "in" is used for enclosed spaces. So the answer is on.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

56. We ______ playing cricket now.
(a) is (b) are (c) am (d) was
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Now" shows present continuous, and the subject "we" takes "are". "Am" goes only with "I", so the answer is are.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

57. Ravi is taller ______ Mohan.
(a) then (b) that (c) than (d) to
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Then" is a sound-alike trap meaning time; comparisons always use "than". So the answer is than.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

58. She has lived here ______ 2015.
(a) for (b) from (c) since (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "For" tempts because it also shows duration, but "since" is used with a point of time like a year, while "for" takes a period. So the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

59. The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rises (b) rise (c) rose (d) rising
Answer: (a)
Solution: Universal truths take the simple present, and "the sun" is singular, so the verb takes -s. So the answer is rises.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

60. I saw ______ elephant at the zoo.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Elephant" begins with a vowel sound and is mentioned for the first time, so it takes the indefinite article "an". So the answer is an.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

61. Neither of the two boys ______ done his work.
(a) have (b) were (c) are (d) has
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "boys" is plural and sits next to the verb, but "neither of" is singular and takes a singular verb. So the answer is has.
Source: PYQ-style

62. He is one of the best players who ______ ever played for India.
(a) have (b) has (c) is (d) was
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Has" tempts because of "one of the best", but the relative pronoun "who" refers to "players" (plural), so the verb must be plural. So the answer is have.
Source: PYQ-style

63. The teacher, along with his students, ______ gone to the museum.
(a) has (b) have (c) are (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "students" comes just before the verb, but with "along with" the verb agrees with the first subject, "the teacher", which is singular. So the answer is has.
Source: PYQ-style

64. Bread and butter ______ my favourite breakfast.
(a) are (b) have (c) were (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Are" tempts because two nouns are joined by "and", but bread and butter together form one dish, so a singular verb is used. So the answer is is.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

65. I ______ this film twice already.
(a) see (b) have seen (c) saw (d) am seeing
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Saw" tempts because the action is finished, but "already" with an experience up to now needs the present perfect. So the answer is have seen.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

66. She is good ______ mathematics.
(a) in (b) for (c) on (d) at
Answer: (d)
Solution: The fixed phrase is "good at" a skill or subject; "good in" is a common Hindi-speaker error. So the answer is at.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

67. He has been working here ______ five years.
(a) for (b) since (c) from (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Since" tempts because the sentence is perfect continuous, but "five years" is a period of time, which takes "for"; "since" needs a starting point. So the answer is for.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

68. If it rains tomorrow, we ______ at home.
(a) stayed (b) would stay (c) will stay (d) stay
Answer: (c)
Solution: In a first conditional, "if" clause takes simple present and the main clause takes "will + verb". "Would stay" belongs to imaginary conditions, so the answer is will stay.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

69. The Ganga is one of the ______ rivers in India.
(a) long (b) longest (c) longer (d) most long
Answer: (b)
Solution: "One of the" is always followed by a superlative adjective and a plural noun. "Longer" is comparative and fits only two items, so the answer is longest.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

70. You should ______ your elders.
(a) respect (b) respects (c) respected (d) respecting
Answer: (a)
Solution: After a modal like "should", the base form of the verb is used without -s or -ed. So the answer is respect.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

71. Each of the girls ______ a prize.
(a) have won (b) win (c) has won (d) are winning
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Have won" tempts because "girls" is plural and nearest to the verb, but "each of" makes the subject singular. So the answer is has won.
Source: PYQ-style

72. He prevented me ______ going there.
(a) to (b) from (c) for (d) against
Answer: (b)
Solution: The verb "prevent" is followed by the preposition "from" plus an -ing form; "to" wrongly suggests an infinitive. So the answer is from.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

73. I am looking forward ______ meeting you.
(a) to (b) for (c) at (d) towards
Answer: (a)
Solution: "For" tempts because of "forward", but the fixed phrase is "look forward to" followed by an -ing form. So the answer is to.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

74. No sooner did she enter the room ______ the phone rang.
(a) when (b) than (c) then (d) that
Answer: (b)
Solution: "When" tempts because the sentence is about time, but "no sooner" is always paired with "than". So the answer is than.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

75. The news ______ very shocking.
(a) are (b) were (c) have been (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "news" ends in s and looks plural, but "news" is an uncountable singular noun and takes a singular verb. So the answer is is.
