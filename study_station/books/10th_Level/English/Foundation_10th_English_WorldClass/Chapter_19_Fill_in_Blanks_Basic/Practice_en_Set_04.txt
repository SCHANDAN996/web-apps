76. The teacher asked the students to ______ their notebooks before leaving the class.
(a) summit (b) submit (c) admit (d) permit
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Summit" (a mountain top) is the tempting wrong option because it sounds similar to "submit", but it makes no sense with notebooks. "Admit" and "permit" do not fit the context of handing in work. To hand in work for checking is "to submit", so the answer is submit.

Answer: (d)
