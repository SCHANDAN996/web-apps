76. Choose the best replacement for the underlined part: "He is working here since 2019."
(a) was working (b) has been working (c) is being working (d) No improvement
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Since 2019" with a point of time demands the present perfect continuous, not the simple present. The tempting option "was working" wrongly shifts the action to the past, so the answer is has been working.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Choose the best replacement for the underlined part: "Unless you do not work hard, you will fail."
(a) until you do not work hard (b) unless you work hard (c) if you not work hard (d) No improvement
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Unless" already carries a negative meaning, so adding "not" creates a double negative — the classic trap. "Until" refers to time, not condition, so the answer is unless you work hard.
Source: PYQ-style

78. Choose the best replacement for the underlined part: "One of my friend has gone to Delhi."
(a) one from my friend (b) one of friend (c) one of my friends (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "one of", a plural noun is required even though the verb stays singular. The trap is keeping "friend" singular because the verb "has" sounds singular, so the answer is one of my friends.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

79. Choose the best replacement for the underlined part: "The teacher as well as the students were present."
(a) No improvement (b) are present (c) have been present (d) was present
Answer: (d)
Solution: With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject, "the teacher", which is singular. The trap is matching the verb with the nearer plural noun "students", so the answer is was present.

80. Choose the best replacement for the underlined part: "I prefer tea more than coffee."
(a) tea than coffee (b) tea over coffee (c) No improvement (d) tea to coffee
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Prefer" is followed by the preposition "to", never "than" or "more than". The trap "over coffee" sounds modern but is not standard in exams, so the answer is tea to coffee.
Source: PYQ-style

81. Choose the best replacement for the underlined part: "He denied to help me."
(a) denied me to help (b) refused to help me (c) denied for helping me (d) No improvement
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Deny" takes a gerund or a that-clause, not an infinitive; "refuse to help" is the natural structure here. The trap "denied for helping me" only adds a wrong preposition, so the answer is refused to help me.

82. Choose the best replacement for the underlined part: "No sooner did he enter the room when the phone rang."
(a) then the phone rang (b) No improvement (c) as the phone rang (d) than the phone rang
Answer: (d)
Solution: The fixed correlative pair is "No sooner … than". The trap "when" is used with "hardly/scarcely", not with "no sooner", so the answer is than the phone rang.

Answer: (c)
Solution: In polite English the first person comes last, and as subject we need "I", not "me". The trap "Me and Ram" uses the object form as subject, so the answer is Ram and I.
