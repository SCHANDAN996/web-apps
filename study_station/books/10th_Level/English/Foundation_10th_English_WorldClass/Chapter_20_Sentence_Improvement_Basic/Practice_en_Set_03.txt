51. Choose the correctly spelled word.
(a) Recieve (b) Riceive (c) Receive (d) Receeve
Answer: (c)
Solution: The rule "i before e except after c" applies here, so it is re-ceive. The trap is "Recieve", the most common misspelling, which swaps i and e. The correct answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

52. Choose the correctly spelled word.

Solution: "Occurrence" doubles both the c and the r. The trap is "Occurence", which forgets the second r. The correct answer is Occurrence.
