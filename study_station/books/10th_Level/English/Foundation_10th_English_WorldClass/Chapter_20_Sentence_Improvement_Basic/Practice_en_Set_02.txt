26. Choose the correct improvement: "He is more taller than his brother."
(a) taller (b) most taller (c) much more taller (d) No improvement
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Most taller" is a double superlative and "much more taller" doubles the comparative — both tempting but wrong, because "taller" already shows comparison. The correct form is simply "taller".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

27. Choose the correct improvement: "She does not likes tea."
(a) do not like (b) does not like (c) does not liking (d) No improvement
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "does/do/did", the main verb always takes its base form, so "likes" is the trap. "Do not like" fails because the subject "she" needs "does". The correct form is "does not like".
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

28. Choose the correct improvement: "I have been living here since five years."
(a) from five years (b) since five years ago (c) for five years (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (since 2019, since Monday), while a period of time takes "for" — mixing them is the classic trap. "Five years" is a period, so the correct phrase is "for five years".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

Solution: "Suggest" is never followed by an object plus infinitive — "suggested me to go" is the classic Hindi-speaker trap. The verb takes a that-clause or a gerund, so the correct form is "suggested that I should go".
