126. Choose the best improvement for the underlined part: "He is one of the best players that has ever played for this club."
(a) that have ever played (b) who has ever played (c) which has ever played (d) No improvement
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting choice "No improvement" fails because after "one of the + plural noun", the relative pronoun refers to the plural noun "players", so the verb must be plural "have". The relative clause describes all the players, not just "one", so the answer is "that have ever played".

127. Choose the best improvement for the underlined part: "Unless you do not work hard, you will not pass the exam."
(a) If you do not hardly work (b) Until you do not work hard (c) Unless you work hard (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Unless" itself carries a negative meaning, so adding "not" creates a double negative — the trap in "No improvement". "Until" refers to time, not condition. Removing "not" gives the correct conditional, so the answer is "Unless you work hard".
