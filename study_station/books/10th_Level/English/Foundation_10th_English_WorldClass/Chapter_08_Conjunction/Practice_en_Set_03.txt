51. Choose the correct conjunction: "He was tired, ______ he kept working."
(a) and (b) so (c) but (d) because
Answer: (c)
Solution: The two clauses show contrast — being tired would normally stop work, yet he continued. "And" and "so" suggest agreement or result, which is the trap here. Contrast needs "but", so the answer is but.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. Fill in the blank: "She is rich ______ honest."
(a) but (b) yet (c) and (d) or
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both qualities go together without contrast, so an additive conjunction is needed. "But" and "yet" wrongly suggest opposition. The correct answer is and.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

53. Choose the correct option: "______ it was raining, we went out."
(a) Although (b) Because (c) Unless (d) So
Answer: (a)
Solution: The sentence shows contrast between rain and going out, so a concessive conjunction is required. "Because" would reverse the logic, which is the common trap. The correct answer is Although.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

54. Fill in the blank: "Work hard ______ you will fail."
(a) and (b) but (c) or (d) so
Answer: (c)
Solution: The sentence gives a warning with two alternatives — work hard or face failure. "And" would wrongly promise failure despite hard work. The correct answer is or.

61. Choose the correctly paired correlative conjunctions: "______ Ravi ______ his brother came to the party."
(a) Either … or (b) Neither … nor (c) Both … and (d) Not only … but also
Answer: (c)
Solution: The verb "came" with two joined subjects in a positive sense needs "Both … and". "Either … or" would need a singular-agreement logic and a choice meaning, which does not fit. The correct answer is Both … and.

(a) Unless (b) If (c) Until (d) Since
Answer: (a)
Solution: The sentence means "if you do not apologize", which is exactly what "unless" conveys. "If" alone would reverse the meaning, the classic trap. The correct answer is Unless.

(a) when (b) than (c) then (d) that

Answer: (d)
Solution: The structure "so + adjective + that + clause" shows result. "Too … to" cannot be followed by a full clause, which is the trap. The correct answer is so … that.
