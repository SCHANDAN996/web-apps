26. Choose the correct conjunction: "I was tired, ______ I went to bed early."
(a) but (b) or (c) so (d) yet
Answer: (c)
Solution: "But" and "yet" show contrast, which does not fit because going to bed early is a result of being tired, not a contrast. "So" shows result, so the answer is "so".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

27. Choose the correct conjunction: "He is poor ______ honest."
(a) and (b) so (c) but (d) because
Answer: (c)
Solution: "And" joins similar ideas, but poverty and honesty contrast here. "But" shows contrast, so the answer is "but".

28. Choose the correct conjunction: "Work hard ______ you will fail."
(a) and (b) or (c) so (d) but
Answer: (b)
Solution: "And" would wrongly suggest both happen together. "Or" here means "otherwise", giving a warning, so the answer is "or".

29. Choose the correct conjunction: "She stayed at home ______ she was ill."

30. Choose the correct conjunction: "______ it was raining, we went out."

Solution: "Because" and "since" would reverse the logic — rain did not cause us to go out. "Although" shows contrast, so the answer is "Although".

31. Choose the correct pair: "______ Ram ______ Shyam came to the party."

Answer: (a)
Solution: "Neither/nor" would make the sentence negative, but "came" is positive. "Both/and" joins two subjects with a plural verb, so the answer is "Both / and".

41. Spot the error: "No sooner did he (P)/ enter the room (Q)/ when the phone rang. (R)/ No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part P looks correct and tempts students to hunt elsewhere, but the fixed pair is "no sooner...than", not "no sooner...when". Part R must use "than", so the answer is Part R.

43. Choose the correctly joined sentence: "He was ill. He attended the meeting."

Answer: (d)
Solution: "So" and "because" wrongly make illness the cause of attending. The ideas contrast, so the answer is "Although he was ill, he attended the meeting."
