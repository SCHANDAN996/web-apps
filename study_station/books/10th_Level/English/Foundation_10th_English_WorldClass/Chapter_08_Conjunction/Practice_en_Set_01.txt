1. Choose the correct conjunction: He is poor ______ honest.
(a) and (b) or (c) but (d) so
Answer: (c)
Solution: "And" is tempting because two qualities are listed, but the sentence shows contrast between poverty and honesty, so a contrastive conjunction is needed. "But" expresses contrast, so the answer is but.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

2. Fill in the blank: Work hard ______ you will fail.
(a) or (b) but (c) and (d) because
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Because" is tempting as it gives a reason, but the sentence is a warning showing the result of not working hard. "Or" is used to show an alternative or warning, so the answer is or.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

3. Choose the correct pair: ______ he was tired, he kept working.
(a) Because (b) So (c) Unless (d) Although
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Because" is tempting, but it would reverse the logic — tiredness is not the reason he kept working. The sentence shows concession, so "Although" fits, and the answer is Although.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

4. Fill in the blank: She could not come ______ she was ill.
(a) so (b) because (c) but (d) or
Answer: (b)
Solution: "So" is tempting, but "so" introduces a result, while here the illness is the cause of her absence. "Because" introduces a reason, so the answer is because.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

5. Choose the correct conjunction: Hurry up ______ you will miss the train.
(a) and (b) or (c) lest (d) yet
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Lest" is tempting because it also expresses a warning, but "lest" must be followed by "should" or the subjunctive, not "will". "Or" correctly gives the warning, so the answer is or.

Answer: (a)
Solution: "Either … or" is tempting, but it shows a choice between two things, while the sentence praises both qualities together. "Not only … but also" adds two positive qualities, so the answer is not only … but also.

21. Spot the error: Part P: Scarcely he had reached the station / Part Q: when the train / Part R: left the platform. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part Q looks suspicious because "when" seems like the error, but "scarcely … when" is the correct pair; the real fault is word order. After "scarcely" at the start, inversion is required: "Scarcely had he reached". The error is in Part P, so the answer is Part P.

22. Choose the correctly joined sentence: He was ill. He attended the meeting.
(a) He was ill, so he attended the meeting. (b) He was ill, therefore he attended the meeting. (c) Because he was ill, he attended the meeting. (d) Despite being ill, he attended the meeting.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The options with "so", "because" and "therefore" are tempting, but they all wrongly make illness the cause of attending. The two ideas contrast, and "Despite being ill" expresses that contrast, so the answer is Despite being ill, he attended the meeting.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Spot the error: Part P: Neither of the two boys / Part Q: have done / Part R: his homework. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part P looks tempting because "neither" seems negative enough to be the error, but "neither of the two boys" is correct. "Neither" is singular, so the verb must be "has done", not "have done". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Fill in the blank: ______ had he closed his eyes ______ the phone rang.
(a) Scarcely … than (b) Hardly … then (c) No sooner … when (d) No sooner … than
Answer: (d)
Solution: "No sooner … when" is tempting because "when" feels natural with time, but the fixed pair is "no sooner … than"; "hardly/scarcely" take "when". The correct correlative pair is No sooner … than, so the answer is No sooner … than.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He not only plays cricket but also football. (b) He plays not only cricket but also football. (c) Not only he plays cricket but also football. (d) He plays cricket not only but also football.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The first option is tempting, but "not only … but also" must be placed just before the words they join; here they join "cricket" and "football", so they must come directly before those nouns. "He plays not only cricket but also football" places them correctly, so the answer is He plays not only cricket but also football.
Source: PYQ-style
