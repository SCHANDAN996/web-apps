76. Choose the correctly structured sentence.
(a) Hardly I had reached the station when the train left. (b) Hardly had I reached the station than the train left. (c) Hardly I reached the station when the train had left. (d) Hardly had I reached the station when the train left.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option uses "than" with "hardly", but "hardly" always pairs with "when", never "than" (that belongs to "no sooner"). With "hardly" at the start, inversion is required: "Hardly had I reached… when…", so the answer is option (d).
