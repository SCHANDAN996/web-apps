101. Choose the correctly structured sentence:
(a) Hardly I had reached the station when the train left. (b) Hardly had I reached the station than the train left. (c) Hardly had I reached the station when the train left. (d) Hardly I had reached the station than the train left.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option uses "than" with "hardly", but "hardly… when" is the fixed pair ("than" pairs with "no sooner"). Also, a negative adverb at the start needs inversion: "had I", not "I had", so the answer is "Hardly had I reached the station when the train left."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

103. Choose the correct sentence:

Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option uses "were" because "students" sits next to the verb, but with "as well as" the verb agrees with the first subject, "the teacher", which is singular, so the answer is "The teacher as well as the students was present."

113. Choose the correct sentence (confessing a fault):
(a) I, you and he are guilty of the mistake. (b) You, he and I are guilty of the mistake. (c) He, you and I are guilty of the mistake. (d) He, I and you are guilty of the mistake.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option uses the polite order 2-3-1 (second person, third person, first person), which is correct for pleasant or neutral actions, but for confessing faults or unpleasant acts English uses the order 1-2-3, with the first person first. Since the sentence admits guilt, the answer is "I, you and he are guilty of the mistake."
Source: PYQ-style
