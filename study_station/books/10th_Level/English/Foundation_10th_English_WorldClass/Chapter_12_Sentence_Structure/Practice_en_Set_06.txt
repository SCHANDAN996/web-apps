Practice Set 6 — Sentence Structure (Questions 126–150)
Difficulty: 10 Medium, 15 Hard

126. Identify the correctly structured sentence.
(a) Hardly he had reached the station when the train left. (b) Hardly had reached he the station when the train left. (c) Hardly he reached the station when had the train left. (d) Hardly had he reached the station when the train left.
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "Hardly" at the start of a sentence, inversion is required: auxiliary before subject ("had he"). Option (a) tempts because it sounds natural in speech, but it lacks inversion. So the answer is "Hardly had he reached the station when the train left."
Source: PYQ-style

127. Spot the error: P: No sooner did she enter the room / Q: when the lights / R: went off. / S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than", not "when" — Part Q is the trap because "when" feels natural after a time clause. Replace "when" with "than". So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is one of the best players in the team. (b) He is one of the best player in the team. (c) He is one of best player in the team. (d) He is one of the best players in team.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "One of the + superlative + plural noun" is the rule; "player" in option (b) tempts because the verb is singular, but the noun after "one of the" must be plural. So the answer is "He is one of the best players in the team."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

129. Spot the error: P: The teacher, along with his students, / Q: were present / R: at the function. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject ("the teacher" — singular), so it should be "was present". The plural "students" near the verb is the intervening-phrase trap. So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

130. Choose the correctly framed question.
(a) Why are you late today? (b) Why you are late today? (c) Why late you are today? (d) Why are late you today?
Answer: (a)
Solution: In wh-questions the auxiliary comes before the subject: "Why are you…". Option (b) is the classic Hindi-speaker error ("तुम क्यों…" word order). So the answer is "Why are you late today?"
Source: PYQ-style

131. Spot the error: P: Neither of the two boys / Q: have done / R: his homework. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb, so "have" should be "has". The plural "boys" before the verb tempts a plural verb. So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

132. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) Unless you work hard, you will fail. (b) Unless you do not work hard, you will fail. (c) Until you work hard, you will fail. (d) Unless you will work hard, you will fail.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Unless" is already negative, so "do not" in option (b) creates a double negative — the most common trap. Also, no future tense after "unless". So the answer is "Unless you work hard, you will fail."
Source: PYQ-style

133. Spot the error: P: Scarcely had he stepped out of the house / Q: than it began / R: to rain heavily. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Scarcely/Hardly" pairs with "when", not "than" — the reverse of the "no sooner…than" pair, which is why examiners mix them. Replace "than" with "when". So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

134. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The Ganges is one of longest river in India. (b) The Ganges is one of the longest river in India. (c) Ganges is one of longest rivers in India. (d) The Ganges is one of the longest rivers in India.
Answer: (d)
Solution: River names take "the", and "one of the" needs a plural noun ("rivers"). Option (b) tempts with correct article but wrong singular noun. So the answer is "The Ganges is one of the longest rivers in India."

135. Spot the error: P: Each of the students / Q: have submitted / R: the assignment on time. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Each of" takes a singular verb — "has submitted". The plural "students" sitting just before the verb is the intervening-phrase trap. So the error is in Part Q.

136. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He denied to help me. (b) He refused to help me. (c) He refused for helping me. (d) He denied for help me.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Refuse" is followed by the to-infinitive; "deny" takes a gerund ("denied helping"), not an infinitive — option (a) is the sound-right trap. So the answer is "He refused to help me."
Source: PYQ-style

137. Spot the error: P: It is high time / Q: we start / R: preparing for the exam. / S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: After "It is high time", the verb takes the past form ("we started") even though the meaning is present/future. Present tense "start" tempts because the meaning is about now. So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

138. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I am knowing the answer. (b) I am know the answer. (c) I know the answer. (d) I knowing the answer.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Know" is a stative verb and is not used in the continuous form; option (a) is the classic Hindi-speaker error ("मैं जान रहा हूँ"). So the answer is "I know the answer."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

139. Spot the error: P: The quality of the mangoes / Q: were not / R: up to the mark. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: The subject is "quality" (singular), not "mangoes", so the verb should be "was". The plural noun nearest the verb is the trap. So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is more wiser than his brother. (b) He is wiser then his brother. (c) He is most wiser than his brother. (d) He is wiser than his brother.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Wiser" is already comparative; adding "more" makes a double comparative — the trap in option (a). Also "than", not "then", is used in comparisons. So the answer is "He is wiser than his brother."
Source: PYQ-style

141. Spot the error: P: Not only he failed in English / Q: but also / R: in Mathematics. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: When "Not only" begins the clause, inversion is needed: "Not only did he fail in English". Option (a) tempts because the spoken order sounds fine. So the error is in Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

142. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) Bread and butter are my favourite breakfast. (b) Bread and butter is my favourite breakfast. (c) Bread and butter were my favourite breakfast. (d) Bread and butter have been my favourite breakfast.
Answer: (b)
Solution: When two nouns joined by "and" denote one combined dish/idea, the verb is singular. The plural-looking subject is the trap. So the answer is "Bread and butter is my favourite breakfast."
Source: PYQ-style

143. Spot the error: P: He told me / Q: that he will come / R: to the party the next day. / S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: In reported speech with a past reporting verb ("told"), "will" changes to "would". Keeping "will" tempts because the event is still future in real life. So the error is in Part Q.

144. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The police has arrested the thief. (b) The police arrested been the thief. (c) The police is arrest the thief. (d) The police have arrested the thief.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Police" is a plural noun and always takes a plural verb; option (a) tempts because "police" looks singular. So the answer is "The police have arrested the thief."
Source: PYQ-style

145. Spot the error: P: I prefer tea / Q: more than / R: coffee in the morning. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Prefer" is followed by "to", not "more than" — "prefer tea to coffee". Using "more than" tempts because comparison is implied. So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

146. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He as well as his friends were invited. (b) He as well as his friends have invited. (c) He as well as his friends are invited. (d) He as well as his friends was invited.
Answer: (d)
Solution: With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject ("He" — singular). The nearby plural "friends" is the intervening-phrase trap. So the answer is "He as well as his friends was invited."
Source: PYQ-style

147. Spot the error: P: No sooner had the bell rung / Q: when the students / R: rushed out of the class. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "No sooner" pairs with "than": "No sooner had the bell rung than the students rushed out". "When" tempts because the sentence is about time sequence. So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

148. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She is suffering from fever since Monday. (b) She is suffering from fever for Monday. (c) She has been suffering from fever since Monday. (d) She was suffering from fever since Monday.
Answer: (c)
Solution: An action starting in the past and continuing now takes the present perfect continuous with "since". Option (a) is the Hindi-speaker trap ("वह सोमवार से बीमार है"). So the answer is "She has been suffering from fever since Monday."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

149. Spot the error: P: Many a student / Q: have failed / R: in this examination. / S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Many a + singular noun" takes a singular verb — "has failed". The plural meaning of the phrase tempts a plural verb; this is a sound-right trap. So the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

150. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) If I had known the truth, I would told you. (b) Had I known the truth, I would have told you. (c) Had I known the truth, I would told you. (d) If I known the truth, I would have told you.
Answer: (b)
Solution: In the third conditional, "Had I known" (inversion without "if") pairs with "would have + past participle". Option (c) tempts with correct inversion but drops "have". So the answer is "Had I known the truth, I would have told you."
Source: PYQ-style
