26. Which of the following is a complete sentence?
(a) The boy runs fast in the morning (b) Running fast in the morning (c) Because he was late (d) Under the big tree
Answer: (a)
Solution: A complete sentence needs a subject and a finite verb expressing a full thought. "Running fast in the morning" has no subject, "Because he was late" is a dependent clause, and "Under the big tree" is only a phrase. Only the option with subject "The boy" and verb "runs" is complete, so the answer is "The boy runs fast in the morning."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

27. Identify the subject in the sentence: "The tall girl won the race."
(a) girl (b) tall (c) won (d) race
Answer: (a)
Solution: The subject is the person or thing the sentence is about. "Tall" is an adjective describing the subject, "won" is the verb, and "race" is the object. The sentence is about the girl, so the answer is "girl."

28. Identify the predicate in the sentence: "My father works in a bank."
(a) works in a bank (b) My father (c) in a bank (d) My
Answer: (a)
Solution: The predicate is everything said about the subject, including the verb. "My father" is the subject, so the rest of the sentence forms the predicate. The answer is "works in a bank."

29. Which sentence is in the correct Subject + Verb + Object order?
(a) Rohit plays cricket every Sunday (b) Plays cricket Rohit every Sunday (c) Cricket plays Rohit every Sunday (d) Every Sunday Rohit cricket plays
Answer: (a)
Solution: English follows the S-V-O pattern: subject first, then verb, then object. Only one option places Rohit (subject), plays (verb), and cricket (object) in that order, so the answer is "Rohit plays cricket every Sunday."

30. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She doesn't like tea. (b) She doesn't likes tea. (c) She don't like tea. (d) She not like tea.
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "doesn't," the main verb stays in its base form, so "doesn't likes" is wrong, and "don't" does not agree with "she." The correct form uses "doesn't" plus base verb "like," so the answer is "She doesn't like tea."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

31. Which of these is a declarative sentence?
(a) Close the door. (b) What a beautiful day! (c) The sun rises in the east. (d) Where are you going?
Answer: (c)
Solution: A declarative sentence states a fact and ends with a full stop. "Close the door" is imperative, "What a beautiful day!" is exclamatory, and "Where are you going?" is interrogative. The statement of fact is "The sun rises in the east," so that is the answer.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

Answer: (b)
Solution: An interrogative sentence asks a question and ends with a question mark. Only one option asks a question, so the answer is "Do you like mangoes?"

35. Which group of words is a phrase, not a clause?
(a) She sings well (b) They played cricket (c) He is a doctor (d) in the garden
Answer: (d)
Solution: A clause has a subject and a finite verb; a phrase does not. "In the garden" has neither a subject nor a verb, so it is a phrase. The answer is "in the garden."

39. Choose the correct question form.
(a) Why you are late? (b) Why are you late? (c) Why late you are? (d) You are why late?
Answer: (b)
Solution: In English questions, the helping verb comes before the subject. Hindi speakers often say "Why you are late?" by translating directly, which is wrong. The correct inversion is "Why are you late?" so that is the answer.

40. Which sentence has a subject and a verb but is still incomplete in meaning?
(a) She gave a gift. (b) She gave. (c) The baby slept. (d) He runs daily.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Gave" is a transitive verb that needs an object to complete its meaning, so "She gave" feels incomplete. The other sentences express complete thoughts. The answer is "She gave."

Answer: (c)
Solution: This is a classic examiner's trap: the phrase "of my friends" sits between the subject and the verb and tempts you toward "have." But the real subject is "One," which is singular, so the verb must be "has gone." The error is in Part Q.

44. Identify the type of sentence: "Although it was raining, we went out."
(a) Simple sentence (b) Complex sentence (c) Compound sentence (d) Exclamatory sentence
Answer: (b)
Solution: A complex sentence has one main clause and at least one subordinate clause joined by words like "although," "because," or "when." "Although it was raining" is the subordinate clause, so the answer is "Complex sentence."

Answer: (b)
Solution: Two independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction like "but," "and," or "or" form a compound sentence. Both parts here can stand alone, so the answer is "Compound sentence."

Solution: "Each" is always singular, even when followed by a plural noun in an intervening phrase — a favourite examiner trap. The verb must be "has," so the answer is "Each of the boys has a book."

50. Choose the correct exclamatory sentence.
(a) What beautiful flower it is! (b) What beautiful a flower it is! (c) What a beautiful flower is it! (d) What a beautiful flower it is!
Answer: (d)
Solution: In exclamatory sentences with "what," the order is "What + a/an + adjective + noun + subject + verb." The inversion "is it" is a trap borrowed from question structure. The correct form is "What a beautiful flower it is!" so that is the answer.
