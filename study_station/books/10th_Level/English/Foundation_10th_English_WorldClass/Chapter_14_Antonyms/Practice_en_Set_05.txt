101. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: TRANSPARENT
(a) Clear (b) Opaque (c) Fragile (d) Thin
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Clear" is a synonym, not an antonym — the classic trap of picking a similar word when the question asks for the opposite. "Opaque" means not allowing light through, the direct opposite of transparent, so the answer is Opaque.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English (Vocabulary)
