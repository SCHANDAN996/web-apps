🃏 Flashcards — Antonyms

Card 1
Front: What is an antonym?
Back: A word that means the opposite of another word. Example: Hot ↔ Cold.

Card 2
Front: Antonym of "Abundant"
Back: Scarce. (Abundant = plenty; Scarce = very little.)

Card 3
Front: Antonym of "Transparent"
Back: Opaque. (Transparent = see-through; Opaque = blocks light.)

Card 4
Front: Antonym of "Expand"
Back: Contract. (Expand = grow bigger; Contract = shrink.)

Card 5
Front: Antonym of "Ancient"
Back: Modern. (Ancient = very old; Modern = new, current.)

Card 6
Front: Antonym of "Courage"
Back: Cowardice. (Courage = bravery; Cowardice = fearfulness.)

Card 7
Front: Antonym of "Victory"
Back: Defeat. (Victory = winning; Defeat = losing.)

Card 8
Front: Antonym of "Genuine"
Back: Fake / Spurious. (Genuine = real; Fake = not real.)

Card 9
Front: Antonym of "Humble"
Back: Arrogant / Proud. (Humble = modest; Arrogant = full of pride.)

Card 10
Front: Antonym of "Permanent"
Back: Temporary. (Permanent = lasting forever; Temporary = for a short time.)

Card 11
Front: Antonym of "Accept"
Back: Reject. (Accept = say yes; Reject = say no.)

Card 12
Front: Antonym of "Optimistic"
Back: Pessimistic. (Optimistic = hopeful; Pessimistic = expecting the worst.)

Card 13
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember "Benevolent ↔ Malevolent"?
Back: "BENE = good (like Benefit), MALE = bad (like Malfunction)." Benevolent = kind; Malevolent = wishing harm.

Card 14
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember "Loquacious ↔ Taciturn"?
Back: "LOQU = talk (like eloQUent)." Loquacious = talkative; Taciturn = silent, says little.

Card 15
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: Is the antonym of "Polite" — "Impolite" or "Rude"?
Back: Both are correct in meaning, but in exams pick the option given. Trap: examiners put two near-correct options — choose the exact opposite in degree, not just a negative word.

Card 16
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: What is the antonym of "Flammable"?
Back: Non-flammable / Fire-resistant. Trap: "Inflammable" looks like an opposite but actually means the SAME as flammable!

Card 17
Front: Antonym of "Rigid"
Back: Flexible. (Rigid = stiff, unbending; Flexible = bends easily.)

Card 18
Front: Antonym of "Scarcity"
Back: Abundance / Plenty. (Scarcity = shortage; Abundance = more than enough.)

Card 19
Front: Antonym of "Guilty"
Back: Innocent. (Guilty = did the wrong; Innocent = did no wrong.)

Card 20
Front: Antonym of "Artificial"
Back: Natural. (Artificial = man-made; Natural = made by nature.)
