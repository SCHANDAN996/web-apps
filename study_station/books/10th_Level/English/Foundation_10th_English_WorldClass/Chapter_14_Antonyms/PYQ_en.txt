# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Antonyms (Pattern & Strategy)

## How Exams Test Antonyms

Antonyms are a **permanent fixture** in SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD), Banking (IBPS, SBI), Railway (NTPC, Group D) and State exams. The pattern is remarkably stable:

- **Direct format:** "Choose the word OPPOSITE in meaning to the given word." One word, four options.
- **Sentence format (harder):** The word is underlined in a sentence, so context can mislead you.
- **Where the marks come from:** Words are drawn from a repeating pool — adjectives of quality (*humble, generous, brave*), verbs of action (*expand, permit, abandon*), and abstract nouns (*prosperity, harmony, courage*).

## What Examiners Repeatedly Ask (Sub-topic Frequency in Words)

- **High-frequency zones:** Common adjectives (*ancient, vague, rigid, scarce*), everyday verbs (*accept, create, forbid, gather*), and trait words (*cruel, honest, timid, polite*).
- **Medium-frequency zones:** Slightly formal words from newspapers (*abundant, reluctant, obscure, genuine*).
- **Rare but scoring:** Words with prefixes where the antonym is also prefix-based (*legal → illegal, visible → invisible*).

## The Examiner's Four Traps (Trap Types)

| Trap Type | What It Looks Like | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Synonym Trap** | One option is a *synonym*, not antonym — placed to catch fast readers | Word: *Expand* → option "Enlarge" (synonym!) vs "Contract" ✓ |
| **Association Trap** | An option *related* to the word's field, but not its opposite | Word: *Guilty* → "Criminal" and "Accused" belong to the crime field, but the opposite is "Innocent" ✓ |
| **Degree Trap** | Option is opposite but wrong *intensity* | Word: *Warm* → "Cool" ✓ (not "Frozen" — too extreme) |
| **Over-thinking Trap** | The simple, obvious answer IS correct; students reject it as "too easy" | Word: *Accept* → "Reject" ✓ (students waste time doubting it) |

## ⏱️ The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**Fact-based (15 seconds):** Direct one-word antonyms of common words. You either know it or you don't — don't stare.

> **Q:** Choose the antonym of **ANCIENT**.
> (a) Old (b) Modern (c) Antique (d) Aged
>
> **15-second application:** (a), (c), (d) are all synonyms — eliminate instantly. Answer: **(b) Modern**. Done in under 15 seconds. *Trap type: Synonym Trap (three synonyms planted together).*

**Inference-based (45 seconds):** When the word is embedded in a sentence or is unfamiliar — use root-analysis and context.

> **Q:** Choose the antonym of **BENEVOLENT**.
> (a) Malevolent (b) Kind (c) Generous (d) Gentle
>
> **45-second application:** Don't know the word? Break it: *bene-* = good (benefit, beneficial), *vol-* = wish. So "good-wishing" = kind. The opposite must be "evil-wishing." *Mal-* = bad (malfunction, malicious) → **(a) Malevolent**. Root analysis converts an unknown word into a solved question. *Trap type: Synonym Trap — (b), (c), (d) are all synonyms of the word itself.*

## Top 10 High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions (with Solutions)

**Q1.** Antonym of **TRANSPARENT**
(a) Clear (b) Opaque (c) Thin (d) Obvious
**Answer: (b)** — Transparent = allowing light through; opaque = blocking light. (a) and (d) are synonym-traps; (c) is unrelated.
*Trap type: Synonym Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q2.** Antonym of **EXPAND**
(a) Extend (b) Enlarge (c) Contract (d) Spread
**Answer: (c)** — Three options are synonyms; only "contract" reverses the meaning.
*Trap type: Synonym Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q3.** Antonym of **HUMBLE**
(a) Modest (b) Meek (c) Proud (d) Simple
**Answer: (c)** — Humble = not proud; its opposite is proud/arrogant. (a), (b), (d) are near-synonyms.
*Trap type: Synonym Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q4.** Antonym of **SCARCE**
(a) Rare (b) Abundant (c) Costly (d) Precious
**Answer: (b)** — Scarce = in short supply; abundant = plentiful. (c) and (d) are association-traps (scarce things are often costly — but that is not the opposite).
*Trap type: Association Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q5.** Antonym of **PERMIT**
(a) Allow (b) Grant (c) Forbid (d) Consent
**Answer: (c)** — Permit = allow; forbid = prohibit. Classic verb pair asked repeatedly across exams.
*Trap type: Synonym Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q6.** Antonym of **VAGUE**
(a) Unclear (b) Definite (c) Confused (d) Hazy
**Answer: (b)** — Vague = not clear; definite = precise. (a), (c), (d) all echo the word's own meaning.
*Trap type: Synonym Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q7.** Antonym of **RIGID**
(a) Stiff (b) Hard (c) Flexible (d) Firm
**Answer: (c)** — Rigid = unbending; flexible = bendable. (a), (b), (d) are synonyms planted together.
*Trap type: Synonym Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q8.** Antonym of **GUILTY**
(a) Criminal (b) Innocent (c) Accused (d) Blameworthy
**Answer: (b)** — Guilty ↔ Innocent is one of the most repeated pairs in SSC/Bank exams. (a), (c), (d) all belong to the "crime" field to confuse you.
*Trap type: Association Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q9.** Antonym of **WARM** (as in "warm weather")
(a) Frozen (b) Cool (c) Boiling (d) Hot
**Answer: (b)** — "Frozen" is the degree-trap (too extreme); "cool" is the correct moderate opposite. (d) is a synonym.
*Trap type: Degree Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

**Q10.** Antonym of **RELUCTANT**
(a) Unwilling (b) Hesitant (c) Eager (d) Slow
**Answer: (c)** — Reluctant = unwilling; eager = keenly willing. (a) and (b) are direct synonyms — the classic trap layout.
*Trap type: Synonym Trap | Source: PYQ-style*

## Key Takeaways from the Pattern

1. **Three-synonym layout:** In most antonym questions, 2–3 options are synonyms of the given word. Spot them, eliminate them, and the answer reveals itself.
2. **The repeating pool:** Pairs like *accept–reject, expand–contract, guilty–innocent, ancient–modern, permit–forbid* recur across years and exams. Master the core 200 pairs and you cover the vast majority of what appears.
3. **Don't over-think easy words:** If the word is common (*happy, begin, strong*), the obvious opposite is almost always the answer. Save your 45 seconds for unfamiliar words — and attack those with Greek/Latin roots.
