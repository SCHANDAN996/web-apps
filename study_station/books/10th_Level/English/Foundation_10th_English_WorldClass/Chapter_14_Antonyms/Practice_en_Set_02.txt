26. Choose the word opposite in meaning to: ANCIENT
(a) Modern (b) Old (c) Antique (d) Aged
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Old", "antique" and "aged" are synonyms of ancient, not opposites — a classic trap where three options mean the same as the given word. The opposite of ancient is modern, so the answer is modern.
Source: PYQ-style
