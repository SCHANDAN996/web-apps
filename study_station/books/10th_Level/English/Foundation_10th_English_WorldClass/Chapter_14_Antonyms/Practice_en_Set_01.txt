1. Choose the word opposite in meaning to: HAPPY
(a) Joyful (b) Glad (c) Sad (d) Cheerful
Answer: (c)
Solution: Joyful, glad and cheerful are all synonyms of happy, so they are traps for a student who reads the question too fast. The opposite of happy is sad, so the answer is Sad.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English
