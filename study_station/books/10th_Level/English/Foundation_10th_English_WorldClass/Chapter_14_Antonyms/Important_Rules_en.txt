# 📋 Important Rules & Tips — Antonyms

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Prefix flips meaning**: un-, in-, im-, il-, ir-, dis-, non- usually make a word negative/opposite. | happy → **unhappy**; legal → **illegal** | Prefix changes with the starting letter: im- before p/b/m (im*possible), il- before l (il*legal), ir- before r (ir*regular). Don't apply "un-" blindly. |
| 2 | **Match the degree/intensity**, not just the direction. | Antonym of *frigid* (very cold) = **scorching** (very hot), not merely "warm". | Options often contain a weak opposite (warm) to trap you; pick the equally strong one. |
| 3 | **Match the part of speech.** Noun ↔ noun, verb ↔ verb, adjective ↔ adjective. | Antonym of *courage* (noun) = **cowardice** (noun), not "cowardly" (adjective). | A grammatically wrong-class option that "sounds right" is the classic trap. |
| 4 | **Antonyms must have opposite meaning — reject near-synonyms with a different shade.** | *frugal* (careful with money) ↔ **extravagant**; "stingy" is a near-synonym of frugal, not its antonym. | "Stingy" looks like an opposite because of its negative tone, but it means almost the same as frugal — reject it. |
| 5 | **Beware of partial synonyms disguised as antonyms.** | Antonym of *expand* = **contract**, not "shrink slightly" options like "reduce" when "contract" is present. | When two options look opposite-ish, choose the *exact* mirror word. |
| 6 | **One word can have different antonyms in different senses.** | *light* → dark (colour), heavy (weight). | Read the usage/context if given; without context, pick the most common sense. |
| 7 | **Learn antonym pairs as fixed exam pairs.** | abundant–scarce, ancient–modern, brave–cowardly, genuine–fake, rigid–flexible | Exams repeatedly draw from a common pool of well-known pairs; memorised pairs beat guesswork. |
| 8 | **"Near-antonyms" differ by one shade — check the shade.** | *proud* ↔ **humble** (not "shy"); *arrogant* ↔ **modest**. | Shy ≠ humble. Trap options are cousins of the real answer. |
| 9 | **Abstract nouns need abstract antonyms.** | *honesty* ↔ **dishonesty**; *freedom* ↔ **captivity/slavery** | Concrete options (e.g., "prison" for freedom) are wrong class — reject. |
| 10 | **Foreign/Latin words: decode via root, then flip.** | *benevolent* (bene = good) ↔ **malevolent** (mal = bad) | If you know the root, you don't need to memorise the pair. |
| 11 | **Eliminate synonyms first.** In 4 options, usually 1–2 are synonyms of the question word. | Q: *hostile* — options: friendly, **amiable**, warm, antagonistic → "antagonistic" is a synonym, eliminate it. | The synonym-option is planted to catch those who misread "antonym" as "synonym". Read the instruction twice. |
| 12 | **When unsure, guess by tone, not by length or difficulty of the option.** | Q word is harsh/negative → answer is likely the soft/positive option. | Over-thinking kills: your first root-based instinct is usually right. Don't switch answers without a reason. |

## (b) Time Management — 15/45-Second Rule

| Type | What it looks like | Time | Strategy |
|------|-------------------|------|----------|
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Direct antonym of a common word (happy, expand, ancient, brave) — pure recall of a memorised pair. | ≤ 15 sec | See word → recall pair → mark. No re-reading options. If not recalled in 15 sec, mark for review and move on. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Unfamiliar/tough word (ephemeral, obstinate, lucid) — must decode via root/prefix, match degree & part of speech, eliminate synonyms. | ≤ 45 sec | (1) Break word into root+affix, (2) fix its tone (+/–) and class, (3) eliminate synonym-traps, (4) match intensity. If still stuck at 45 sec, take the tone-based guess and move. |

**Exam tactic:** Attempt all 15-second questions first in one sweep; return to 45-second ones. Never let one hard word eat 2 minutes — an antonym question carries the same marks as an easy one.

## (c) Morphology — Root + Affix → Antonym Power

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Word | Antonym |
|---|---|---|---|
| **bene-** (Latin) | good | benevolent | malevolent (**mal-** = bad) |
| **mal-** (Latin) | bad | malevolent | benevolent |
| **cred-** (Latin) | believe | credible | incredible |
| **dict-** (Latin) | say | dictate (order) | — (no direct antonym; learn the root for word families: predict, verdict, contradict) |
| **port-** (Latin) | carry | export | import |
| **chron-** (Greek) | time | chronic (long-lasting) | acute (short, sharp) |
| **phil-** (Greek) | love | philanthropy | misanthropy (**mis-** = hate) |
| **un- / in- / im- / il- / ir-** | not | able → unable; possible → impossible | (removes the quality) |
| **dis-** | opposite of | agree → disagree; honest → dishonest | — |
| **-ful / -less** | full of / without | hopeful ↔ hopeless; careful ↔ careless | suffix itself creates the pair |

**Golden tip:** Learn 20 roots + 10 affixes and you unlock hundreds of antonym pairs without memorising each one separately.
