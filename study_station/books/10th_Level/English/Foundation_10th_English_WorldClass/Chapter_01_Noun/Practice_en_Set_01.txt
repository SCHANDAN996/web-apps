Set 1: Noun MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which is a Proper Noun?
(a) Ram (b) city (c) boy (d) river
Answer: (a)
Solution: Boy, city and river are common names of whole classes. Ram names one particular person, so the answer is Ram.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

2. Which is an Abstract Noun?
(a) beauty (b) gold (c) army (d) table
Answer: (a)
Solution: Table and gold can be touched, and army is a group name. Beauty can only be felt, not touched, so the answer is beauty.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

3. Which is a Collective Noun?
(a) honesty (b) Delhi (c) water (d) crowd
Answer: (d)
Solution: Honesty is abstract and water is a material. A crowd is one name for a group of people, so the answer is crowd.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English

4. Which is a Material Noun?
(a) iron (b) anger (c) family (d) London
Answer: (a)
Solution: Family is collective and anger is abstract. Iron is the material things are made of, so the answer is iron.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

5. The plural of "box" is:
(a) boxes (b) boxs (c) boxies (d) boxen
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nouns ending in x take es, not just s. So box becomes boxes, and the answer is boxes.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

6. The plural of "baby" is:
(a) babys (b) babyes (c) babies (d) baby
Answer: (c)
Solution: After a consonant plus y, the y changes to ies; "babys" keeps the y wrongly. So the answer is babies.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

7. The plural of "key" is:
(a) keies (b) kies (c) keys (d) keyes
Answer: (c)
Solution: After a vowel plus y, only s is added; changing to ies is the trap. So the answer is keys.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

8. Fill in the blank: I have ___ books on my shelf.
(a) much (b) many (c) little (d) a little
Answer: (b)
Solution: Much and little go with uncountable nouns, which is the trap here. Books are countable, so the answer is many.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English

9. Which word is uncountable?
(a) information (b) boy (c) apple (d) car
Answer: (a)
Solution: Apple, boy and car can be counted one by one. Information has no plural form, so the answer is information.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

10. The plural of "man" is:
(a) men (b) man (c) mans (d) mens
Answer: (a)
Solution: This is an irregular plural; adding s to man is wrong. The correct plural is men, so the answer is men.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

11. The plural of "sheep" is:
(a) sheeps (b) sheep (c) sheepies (d) sheepes
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sheep keeps the same form in singular and plural; adding s is the common mistake. So the answer is sheep.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

12. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She bought a furniture. (b) She bought many furnitures. (c) She bought much furniture. (d) She bought many furniture pieceses.
Answer: (c)
Solution: Furniture is uncountable, so it never takes a plural or a/an; "furnitures" is the trap. The correct sentence is "She bought much furniture."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

13. The possessive of "Ram" is:
(a) Rams (b) Ram's (c) Ram (d) Rams'
Answer: (b)
Solution: For one living being we add apostrophe plus s; "Rams" is only a plural, not a possessive. So the answer is Ram's.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English

14. The plural of "leaf" is:
(a) leafs (b) leafes (c) leaf (d) leaves
Answer: (d)
Solution: Words ending in f usually change f to v and add es; "leafs" is the tempting wrong form. So the answer is leaves.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

15. Which is a Common Noun?
(a) India (b) Ganga (c) country (d) Monday
Answer: (c)
Solution: India, Monday and Ganga are particular names, hence Proper Nouns. Country names a whole class, so the answer is country.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

16. The plural of "tooth" is:
(a) tooth (b) tooths (c) toothes (d) teeth
Answer: (d)
Solution: This is an irregular plural where the vowel changes; adding s is wrong. The correct plural is teeth, so the answer is teeth.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

17. How do you count an uncountable noun like advice?
(a) two advices (b) a piece of advice (c) an advice (d) many advice
Answer: (b)
Solution: Advice has no plural and cannot take a/an directly. We count it with "piece", so the answer is a piece of advice.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

18. Fill in the blank: There is not ___ milk left.
(a) many (b) few (c) several (d) much
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many, few and several go with countable nouns, which is the trap. Milk is uncountable, so the answer is much.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English

19. The plural of "hero" is:
(a) heros (b) hero (c) heroes (d) heroies
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many nouns ending in o take es in the plural; "heros" with only s is the trap. So the answer is heroes.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

20. Which of these words must always begin with a capital letter?
(a) Ganga (b) river (c) water (d) boat
Answer: (a)
Solution: River, water and boat are common nouns and stay small in the middle of a sentence. Ganga is a Proper Noun, so the answer is Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English

21. Fill in the blank: The team ___ arguing among themselves.
(a) was (b) has (c) is (d) are
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Themselves" shows the members act separately, so a singular verb like "is" is the trap. The plural verb fits, so the answer is are.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Choose the correct possessive.
(a) the students union (b) the studentses union (c) the students' union (d) the student's union
Answer: (c)
Solution: A plural already ending in s takes only an apostrophe; adding another s is the trap. So the answer is the students' union.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Which is correct?
(a) He gave me informations. (b) He gave me a piece of information. (c) He gave me an information. (d) He gave me many information.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Information is uncountable, so a/an, plurals and many are all wrong with it. We say a piece of information, so the answer is "He gave me a piece of information."
Source: PYQ-style

24. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The table's leg is broken. (b) The leg of the table is broken. (c) The tables leg is broken. (d) The leg's table is broken.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lifeless things normally take "of" for possession, not apostrophe s; "the table's leg" is the tempting trap. So the answer is "The leg of the table is broken."
Source: PYQ-style

25. Which of these is correct?
(a) a day work (b) a days work (c) a days' work (d) a day's work
Answer: (d)
Solution: Expressions of time take apostrophe s, and "day" here is singular, so the apostrophe comes before the s. The correct phrase is a day's work, so the answer is a day's work.
Source: PYQ-style
