126. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The sceneries of Kashmir are very beautiful. (b) The scenery of Kashmir is very beautiful. (c) The scenery of Kashmir are very beautiful. (d) The sceneries of Kashmir is very beautiful.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun, so it has no plural form and takes a singular verb; the tempting options use "sceneries" or the plural verb "are". The correct sentence is "The scenery of Kashmir is very beautiful."

Answer: (b)
Solution: "Furniture" is an uncountable noun and has no plural form, so Part P should read "The furniture"; students are tempted to blame the verb in Part R, but the verb only looks wrong because the noun is wrong. The error is in Part P.

Answer: (a)

130. Spot the error: One of my friend (P) / has gone (Q) / to Delhi yesterday. (R) / No error (S)
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "one of", we always use a plural noun, so Part P should be "One of my friends"; the trap is that the singular verb "has" in Part Q is actually correct and misleads students into checking the wrong part. The error is in Part P.
