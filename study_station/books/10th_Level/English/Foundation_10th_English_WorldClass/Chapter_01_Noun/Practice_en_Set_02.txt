Noun — Practice Set 2 (Questions 26–50)
Difficulty: Questions 26–40 Easy | Questions 41–50 Medium

26. Which of the following is a proper noun?
(a) city (b) river (c) mountain (d) Ganga
Answer: (d)
Solution: City, river and mountain are common nouns because they name a general class. Ganga names one specific river, so it is a proper noun and is capitalised. The answer is Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

27. Identify the abstract noun in the sentence: "Honesty is the best policy."
(a) Honesty (b) policy (c) best (d) is
Answer: (a)
Solution: Policy is a concrete common noun, and best is an adjective. Honesty is a quality that cannot be seen or touched, so it is an abstract noun. The answer is Honesty.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

28. The plural of "child" is:
(a) children (b) childes (c) childs (d) childrens
Answer: (a)
Solution: Childs and childrens are wrong forms; child does not take a simple -s. Child has an irregular plural, children. The answer is children.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

29. Which word is a collective noun?
(a) soldier (b) army (c) brave (d) fight
Answer: (b)
Solution: Soldier names one person, brave is an adjective, and fight is a verb. Army names a group of soldiers taken as one body, so it is a collective noun. The answer is army.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

30. The plural of "knife" is:
(a) knifes (b) knive (c) knives (d) knifies
Answer: (c)
Solution: Knifes is a common error; nouns ending in -fe usually change f to v and add -es. So knife becomes knives. The answer is knives.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

31. Which of the following is a material noun?
(a) beauty (b) ring (c) gold (d) crowd
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ring is a common noun, beauty is abstract, and crowd is collective. Gold names a substance or material, so it is a material noun. The answer is gold.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

32. The plural of "tooth" is:
(a) tooths (b) teethes (c) toothes (d) teeth
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tooths is wrong because tooth has an irregular plural formed by a vowel change. The correct plural is teeth. The answer is teeth.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

33. Identify the common noun: "The boy plays in the park."
(a) the (b) plays (c) boy (d) in
Answer: (c)
Solution: Plays is a verb, the is an article, and in is a preposition. Boy names a general class of persons, so it is a common noun. The answer is boy.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

34. Which noun is always used in the plural form?
(a) news (b) information (c) mathematics (d) scissors
Answer: (d)
Solution: News, mathematics and information look plural or uncountable but take singular verbs. Scissors is a plural-only noun (plurale tantum) and takes a plural verb. The answer is scissors.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

35. The plural of "ox" is:
(a) oxes (b) oxies (c) ox (d) oxen
Answer: (d)
Solution: Oxes is a tempting but wrong form. Ox has an old irregular plural, oxen. The answer is oxen.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

36. "Kindness" is which type of noun?
(a) Proper noun (b) Common noun (c) Collective noun (d) Abstract noun
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kindness is not a name, a class of things, or a group. It is a quality that we can only feel, so it is an abstract noun. The answer is abstract noun.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

37. The plural of "lady" is:
(a) ladys (b) ladyes (c) ladies (d) ladise
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ladys is wrong because a noun ending in consonant + y changes y to i and adds -es. So lady becomes ladies. The answer is ladies.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

38. Which sentence uses an uncountable noun correctly?
(a) She gave me a piece of advice. (b) She gave me many advices. (c) She gave me an advice. (d) She gave me advices.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Advice is uncountable, so an advice and advices are wrong — this is the examiner's favourite trap. We count it with "a piece of". The answer is "She gave me a piece of advice."
Source: PYQ-style

39. The plural of "mouse" (the animal) is:
(a) mouses (b) mices (c) mouse (d) mice
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mouses and mices are wrong forms. Mouse has an irregular plural, mice. The answer is mice.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

40. "Team" is which type of noun?
(a) Abstract noun (b) Proper noun (c) Collective noun (d) Material noun
Answer: (c)
Solution: Team is not a quality, a specific name, or a substance. It names a group of players acting as one unit, so it is a collective noun. The answer is collective noun.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

41. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The news are good. (b) The news is good. (c) The news were good. (d) The news have been good.
Answer: (b)
Solution: News looks plural, which tempts students toward are, were and have, but it is singular in meaning and takes a singular verb. The answer is "The news is good."
Source: PYQ-style

42. The plural of "analysis" is:
(a) analysises (b) analysies (c) analyses (d) analysis
Answer: (c)
Solution: Analysises is a common error. Words of Greek origin ending in -is change to -es in the plural, so analysis becomes analyses. The answer is analyses.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

43. Identify the error: "He gave me (P) / many informations (Q) / about the case. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q is wrong because information is uncountable and has no plural form; we say "much information" or "pieces of information". Parts P and R are correct. The answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

44. The feminine form of "king" is:
(a) queen (b) kingess (c) kingine (d) lady king
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kingess is not an English word. King and queen form a gender pair with entirely different words. The answer is queen.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

45. Choose the correctly spelt plural:
(a) potatos (b) potatoes (c) potatose (d) potatoies
Answer: (b)
Solution: Potatos is tempting but wrong; nouns ending in -o preceded by a consonant usually add -es. So potato becomes potatoes. The answer is potatoes.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

46. "A ______ of cattle was grazing in the field."
(a) herd (b) swarm (c) flock (d) pack
Answer: (a)
Solution: Swarm goes with insects, flock with sheep or birds, and pack with wolves or dogs — these are the confusing look-alikes. Cattle are counted in a herd. The answer is herd.
Source: PYQ-style

47. Identify the error: "The furniture in this room (P) / are very old (Q) / and needs repair. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part Q is wrong because furniture is uncountable and always takes a singular verb; it should be "is very old". This is a classic sound-right trap since furniture refers to many items. The answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

48. The plural of "criterion" is:
(a) criterions (b) criteria (c) criterias (d) criterion
Answer: (b)
Solution: Criterions and criterias are wrong forms. Greek words ending in -on change to -a, so criterion becomes criteria. The answer is criteria.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

49. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) Mathematics is difficult for me. (b) Mathematics are difficult for me. (c) Mathematics were difficult for me. (d) Mathematics have been difficult for me.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mathematics ends in -s, which tricks students into using plural verbs, but names of subjects are singular. The answer is "Mathematics is difficult for me."
Source: PYQ-style

50. The feminine form of "hero" is:
(a) heroess (b) heroine (c) heroina (d) she-hero
Answer: (b)
Solution: Heroess is not a standard word. The accepted feminine form of hero is heroine. The answer is heroine.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar
