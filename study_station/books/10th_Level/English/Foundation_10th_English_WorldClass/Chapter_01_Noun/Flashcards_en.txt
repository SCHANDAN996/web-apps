Front: What does Noun mean, in one line?
Back: The **name** of a person, place, thing or idea.

Front: What are the five types of Noun?
Back: Proper, Common, Collective, Material, Abstract.

Front: Difference between Proper and Common Noun?
Back: Proper = name of one particular thing (Delhi, Ram) — capital letter. Common = shared name of a whole class (city, boy) — small letter.

Front: What is a Collective Noun? Three examples.
Back: One name for a group — team, family, crowd, army.

Front: What is an Abstract Noun? Three examples.
Back: What you cannot touch, only feel — honesty, beauty, anger, freedom.

Front: What is a Material Noun? Three examples.
Back: The material things are made of — gold, water, iron, wood.

Front: What question identifies Countable vs Uncountable?
Back: "Can I count this as 1, 2, 3?" Yes → Countable, No → Uncountable.

Front: Five words that never take a plural.
Back: advice, information, furniture, luggage, news (also work, bread, hair, money, scenery).

Front: When do you use many and when much?
Back: **many** + Countable (many books); **much** + Uncountable (much water).

Front: Correct this: "He gave me many advices."
Back: "He gave me **much advice**" or "a piece of advice". Advice has no plural.

Front: How do you count an uncountable noun?
Back: Add piece/item — *a piece of advice*, *an item of furniture*.

Front: Why is it city → cities but boy → boys?
Back: Consonant + y → ies; vowel + y → just s. Look at the letter before the y.

Front: Plural of knife and leaf?
Back: knives, leaves (f/fe → ves).

Front: Plural of man, child, tooth, foot?
Back: men, children, teeth, feet (irregular).

Front: How is the possessive of a lifeless thing formed?
Back: With **of** — *the leg of the table*, not *table's leg*.

Front: Possessive of a plural already ending in s?
Back: Apostrophe only — *boys' school*, *students' union*.

Front: "The team ___ playing" — is or are?
Back: **is** — the group is acting as one unit.

Front: "The team ___ arguing among themselves" — is or are?
Back: **are** — the members are acting separately.

Front: What is the commonest exam trap with Nouns?
Back: Making an uncountable plural — advices, informations, furnitures (all wrong).

Front: Mnemonic for uncountables?
Back: "Advice-News-Luggage" — plus furniture and information.
