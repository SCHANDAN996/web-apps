101. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The scenery of Kashmir are very beautiful. (b) The sceneries of Kashmir are very beautiful. (c) The scenery of Kashmir is very beautiful. (d) The sceneries of Kashmir is very beautiful.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun, so it has no plural form and takes a singular verb. Options with "sceneries" or "are" are tempting but wrong, so the answer is "The scenery of Kashmir is very beautiful."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

102. Spot the error: Part P: He gave me / Part Q: many advices / Part R: on how to improve my English. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Advice" is an uncountable noun and has no plural form "advices"; the correct phrase is "much advice" or "many pieces of advice". The error is in Part Q.

106. Choose the correct possessive form.
(a) The childrens' toys are scattered. (b) The children toys' are scattered. (c) The childrens toys are scattered. (d) The children's toys are scattered.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Children" is already plural without "-s", so the possessive is formed by adding 's, giving "children's". "Childrens'" wrongly adds an apostrophe to a non-existent word, so the answer is "The children's toys are scattered."

112. Choose the correct possessive form for two brothers sharing one room.
(a) Ravi's and Mohan's room is big. (b) Ravi and Mohan's room is big. (c) Ravi and Mohans' room is big. (d) Ravi's and Mohan room is big.
Answer: (b)
Solution: When two people jointly own one thing, the apostrophe goes only with the second name: "Ravi and Mohan's room". "Ravi's and Mohan's" would mean two separate rooms, so the answer is "Ravi and Mohan's room is big."
