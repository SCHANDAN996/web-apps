51. Which of the following is a proper noun?
(a) city (b) Ganga (c) river (d) mountain
Answer: (b)
Solution: "City", "river" and "mountain" are common nouns naming general things, while "Ganga" names one specific river and is capitalised, so the answer is Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

52. Identify the abstract noun in the sentence: "Honesty is the best policy."
(a) Honesty (b) policy (c) best (d) the
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Policy" is a common noun for a thing one can state, but "Honesty" names a quality that cannot be seen or touched, so the answer is Honesty.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

53. The plural of "child" is:
(a) childs (b) childes (c) childrens (d) children
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Childs" and "childrens" tempt learners who add -s blindly, but "child" has an irregular plural, so the answer is children.
Source: NCERT Class 5 English Grammar

54. Which word is a collective noun?
(a) army (b) soldiers (c) brave (d) fight
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Soldiers" is a plural common noun and "brave" is an adjective, but "army" names a group as one unit, so the answer is army.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

55. The plural of "knife" is:
(a) knifes (b) knife (c) knives (d) knivies
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Knifes" looks natural but nouns ending in -fe change f to v and add -es, so the answer is knives.

56. Identify the material noun: "This ring is made of gold."
(a) gold (b) made (c) ring (d) This
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Ring" is a countable common noun, but "gold" names a substance or raw material, so the answer is gold.

57. Which sentence uses a countable noun correctly?
(a) He bought three chairs. (b) She gave me an advice. (c) I need some informations. (d) We drank many waters.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Information", "advice" and "water" are uncountable and cannot take plurals or "many", while "chair" is countable, so the answer is "He bought three chairs."

58. The plural of "tooth" is:
(a) teeth (b) tooths (c) toothes (d) tooth

59. In "The team won the match", the noun "team" is:
(a) abstract noun (b) proper noun (c) material noun (d) collective noun
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Team" is not a quality or a substance; it names a group of players acting as one body, so the answer is collective noun.

60. Which of the following is always singular and takes a singular verb?
(a) cattle (b) scissors (c) trousers (d) news
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Scissors", "trousers" and "cattle" take plural verbs, but "news" ends in -s yet is singular, so the answer is news.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

61. Spot the error: "The sceneries (P) / of Kashmir (Q) / are very beautiful. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that "sceneries" sounds like a normal plural, but "scenery" is uncountable and has no plural form; Part P should read "The scenery". The error is in Part P.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

62. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The furniture in the room is new. (b) The furnitures in the room are new. (c) The furniture in the room are new. (d) The furnitures in the room is new.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Furnitures" is a tempting but wrong plural since "furniture" is uncountable, and an uncountable noun takes a singular verb, so the answer is "The furniture in the room is new."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

63. Spot the error: "One of my friend (P) / has gone (Q) / to Delhi. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the singular verb "has" nearby, which makes "friend" sound right, but after "one of" the noun must be plural — "one of my friends". The error is in Part P.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap: intervening phrase)

Solution: The trap is treating "committee" as automatically singular, but "their opinions" shows the members act separately, so a plural verb is needed and the answer is were.

Solution: "Advices" sounds acceptable because we say "many suggestions", but "advice" is uncountable and should be "much good advice" or "pieces of advice". The error is in Part Q.

Answer: (b)
Solution: "Childrens'" tempts by adding -s before the apostrophe, but "children" is already plural, so we add only 's; the answer is children's.

Solution: The trap is the plural "birds" near the verb, but here the flock moves as one unit, so the collective noun takes a singular verb and the answer is was.
