Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. He said, "I am happy."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that he was happy. (c) He said that I was happy. (d) He said that he had been happy.
Answer: (b)
Solution: In reported speech the present simple "am" shifts to past simple "was", and "I" changes to "he" to match the subject. Option (a) wrongly keeps the present tense, so the answer is "He said that he was happy."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

2. She said to me, "I like tea."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) She told me that I liked tea. (b) She told me that she likes tea. (c) She said me that she liked tea. (d) She told me that she liked tea.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Said to me" becomes "told me" — "said me" in option (c) is a classic trap and is wrong English. The verb "like" shifts to "liked", so the answer is "She told me that she liked tea."

3. He said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) He said that the earth revolves around the sun. (b) He said that the earth revolved around the sun. (c) He said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (d) He said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Universal truths do not change tense in indirect speech, so option (b) with "revolved" is the trap. The tense stays present, so the answer is "He said that the earth revolves around the sun."

4. Ram said, "I will go tomorrow."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) Ram said that he will go tomorrow. (b) Ram said that he will go the next day. (c) Ram said that he would go tomorrow. (d) Ram said that he would go the next day.
Answer: (d)
Solution: Both the modal and the time word must shift: "will" becomes "would" and "tomorrow" becomes "the next day". Option (c) changes only the modal, so the answer is "Ram said that he would go the next day."
