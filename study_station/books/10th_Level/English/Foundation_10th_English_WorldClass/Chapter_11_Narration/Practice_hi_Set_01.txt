1. He said, "I am happy."
सही परोक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनें।
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that he was happy. (c) He said that I was happy. (d) He said that he had been happy.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: परोक्ष वाक्य में present simple "am" बदलकर past simple "was" हो जाता है, और "I" कर्ता के अनुसार "he" हो जाता है। विकल्प (a) गलती से present tense रखता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he was happy."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी

2. She said to me, "I like tea."
सही परोक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनें।
(a) She told me that I liked tea. (b) She told me that she likes tea. (c) She said me that she liked tea. (d) She told me that she liked tea.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Said to me" बदलकर "told me" हो जाता है — विकल्प (c) का "said me" एक क्लासिक जाल है और गलत अंग्रेज़ी है। क्रिया "like" बदलकर "liked" हो जाती है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She told me that she liked tea."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी

3. He said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
सही परोक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनें।
(a) He said that the earth revolves around the sun. (b) He said that the earth revolved around the sun. (c) He said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (d) He said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य (universal truths) परोक्ष वाक्य में tense नहीं बदलते, इसलिए "revolved" वाला विकल्प (b) जाल है। Tense present ही रहता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that the earth revolves around the sun."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी

4. Ram said, "I will go tomorrow."
सही परोक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनें।
(a) Ram said that he will go tomorrow. (b) Ram said that he will go the next day. (c) Ram said that he would go tomorrow. (d) Ram said that he would go the next day.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Modal और समय-शब्द दोनों बदलने चाहिए: "will" बनता है "would" और "tomorrow" बनता है "the next day"। विकल्प (c) केवल modal बदलता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Ram said that he would go the next day."

6. He said to me, "Do you like mangoes?"
सही परोक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनें।
(a) He asked me that did I like mangoes. (b) He asked me whether did I like mangoes. (c) He asked me if I liked mangoes. (d) He asked me do I like mangoes.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Yes/no प्रश्नों में "if/whether" के साथ statement word order आता है — "if" के बाद "did" नहीं आता, जो विकल्प (a) और (b) में जाल है। क्रिया "liked" में बदल जाती है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He asked me if I liked mangoes."

8. Mother said to me, "Open the door."
सही परोक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनें।
(a) Mother told me to open the door. (b) Mother told me that open the door. (c) Mother said me to open the door. (d) Mother told me that I open the door.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: आज्ञावाचक वाक्यों (imperatives) को "told/ordered + object + to-infinitive" से बदलते हैं। विकल्प (c) गलत क्रिया "said me" का प्रयोग करता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Mother told me to open the door."

(a) He said her to help him. (b) He requested her to help him. (c) He requested that help him. (d) He told her please help him.

14. He said, "Alas! I have lost my purse."
सही परोक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनें।
(a) He exclaimed with sorrow that he has lost his purse. (b) He exclaimed with joy that he had lost his purse. (c) He said alas that he lost his purse. (d) He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his purse.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Alas" दुख दर्शाता है, इसलिए reporting phrase "exclaimed with sorrow" होता है — "joy" वाला विकल्प (b) जाल है। Tense past perfect में बदल जाता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his purse."
