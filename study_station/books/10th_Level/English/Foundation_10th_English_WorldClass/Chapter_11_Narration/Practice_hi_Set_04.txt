76. He said, "I am writing a letter now."
(a) He said that he was writing a letter then. (b) He said that he is writing a letter then. (c) He said that he had been writing a letter now. (d) He said that he wrote a letter then.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) में Present Continuous, Past Continuous में बदल जाता है और "now" का "then" हो जाता है। लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प "is" को अपरिवर्तित रखता है, जो reporting verb के past होने पर backshift नियम का उल्लंघन है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he was writing a letter then."

77. She said to me, "Do you like tea?"
(a) She asked me did I like tea. (b) She asked me if I like tea. (c) She asked me that whether I liked tea. (d) She asked me if I liked tea.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: हाँ/नहीं वाले प्रश्नों में "if" या "whether" का प्रयोग होता है (दोनों एक साथ नहीं, इसलिए "that whether" वाला विकल्प गलत है), और प्रश्न-रूप statement order में बदल जाता है तथा "like" का backshift "liked" में होता है। इसलिए उत्तर है "She asked me if I liked tea."

80. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
(a) Mother told me to not go out in the rain. (b) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (c) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain. (d) Mother told me do not go out in the rain.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: नकारात्मक आदेश (negative imperatives) "forbade + to-infinitive" या "told... not to go" में बदलते हैं। "Forbade" में पहले से ही नकारात्मक भाव है, इसलिए यह साधारण infinitive लेता है। "Told me to not go" वाला विकल्प infinitive को अनौचित्यपूर्वक तोड़ता है और मानक रूप नहीं है। इसलिए उत्तर है "Mother forbade me to go out in the rain."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

81. He said, "Alas! I have lost my wallet."
(a) He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet. (b) He exclaimed with joy that he had lost his wallet. (c) He said alas that he lost his wallet. (d) He exclaimed with sorrow that he has lost his wallet.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Alas" दुख दर्शाता है, इसलिए यह "exclaimed with sorrow" बनता है, और Present Perfect, Past Perfect में बदल जाता है। "Joy" वाला विकल्प भाव को उलट देता है — यह एक क्लासिक जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet."

82. She said, "Hurrah! We have won the match."
(a) She exclaimed with sorrow that they had won the match. (b) She exclaimed with joy that they had won the match. (c) She said hurrah that they won the match. (d) She exclaimed with joy that they have won the match.

83. He said to her, "Please help me in this work."
(a) He said her please to help him in this work. (b) He requested her to help him in that work. (c) He requested her that help him in that work. (d) He ordered her to help him in that work.

(a) The stranger asked me if I could show him the way to the station. (b) The stranger asked me could I show him the way to the station. (c) The stranger requested me that show him the way to the station. (d) The stranger asked me if I can show him the way to the station.

97. अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य में त्रुटि पहचानिए: He told me (P) / that he will come (Q) / to my house (R) / the next day. (S)

100. सही अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनिए: The mother said, "My son has been ill since Monday."
