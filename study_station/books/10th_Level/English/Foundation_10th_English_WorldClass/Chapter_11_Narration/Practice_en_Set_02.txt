26. He said, "I am happy."
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that I was happy. (c) He said that he had been happy. (d) He said that he was happy.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The reporting verb "said" is past, so "am" backshifts to "was" and "I" becomes "he". The tempting option keeps "is", which breaks the backshift rule, so the answer is "He said that he was happy."

27. She said to me, "I will help you."
(a) She told me that she will help me. (b) She told me that I would help her. (c) She said me that she would help you. (d) She told me that she would help me.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Said to me" becomes "told me", "will" backshifts to "would", and "you" (the listener) becomes "me". The option keeping "will" ignores tense backshift, so the answer is "She told me that she would help me."
