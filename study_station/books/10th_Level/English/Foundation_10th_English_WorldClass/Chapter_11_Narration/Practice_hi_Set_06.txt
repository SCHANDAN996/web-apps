126. He said, "I was writing a letter when you called."
सही अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) चुनें।
(a) He said that he was writing a letter when I called. (b) He said that he had been writing a letter when I had called. (c) He said that he wrote a letter when I called. (d) He said that he has been writing a letter when I called.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: लुभाने वाला विकल्प "was writing" को अपरिवर्तित रखता है, लेकिन reported speech में past continuous, past perfect continuous में बदल जाता है और "called" past perfect में बदल जाता है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he had been writing a letter when I had called."
