126. He said, "I was writing a letter when you called."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) He said that he was writing a letter when I called. (b) He said that he had been writing a letter when I had called. (c) He said that he wrote a letter when I called. (d) He said that he has been writing a letter when I called.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option keeps "was writing" unchanged, but past continuous in reported speech shifts to past perfect continuous, and "called" shifts to past perfect. So the answer is "He said that he had been writing a letter when I had called."

127. The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) The teacher said that the earth revolved around the sun. (b) The teacher said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (c) The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun. (d) The teacher said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option applies the normal backshift, but universal truths and scientific facts never change tense in reported speech. So the answer is "The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun."

128. She said to me, "Do you know where the station is?"
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) She asked me if I knew where the station was. (b) She asked me whether do I know where the station is. (c) She asked me did I know where the station was. (d) She asked me that I knew where the station was.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option keeps the question word order "did I know", but in reported questions the subject comes before the verb, and yes/no questions take "if/whether" with backshifted tense. So the answer is "She asked me if I knew where the station was."

Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option uses "said with sorrow", but exclamatory sentences require "exclaimed with sorrow/joy"; "Alas" signals sorrow, and "has failed" backshifts to "had failed". So the answer is "He exclaimed with sorrow that his brother had failed the exam."

130. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward and capture the post."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) The captain requested the soldiers marching forward and capturing the post. (b) The captain ordered the soldiers that march forward and capture the post. (c) The captain said the soldiers to march forward and capture the post. (d) The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward and capture the post.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option keeps "that" before the command, but imperative sentences in reported speech use "ordered + object + to-infinitive", not a that-clause. So the answer is "The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward and capture the post."

131. She said, "I may go to Delhi tomorrow."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) She said that she might go to Delhi tomorrow. (b) She said that she may go to Delhi the next day. (c) She said that she might go to Delhi the next day. (d) She said that she may go to Delhi tomorrow.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option changes "may" but forgets "tomorrow"; both must change — "may" becomes "might" and "tomorrow" becomes "the next day". So the answer is "She said that she might go to Delhi the next day."

132. He said to her, "Let us go for a walk."

Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option uses "told", but "Let us" expresses a suggestion, which is reported with "suggested that + subject + should"; note "suggested to her", never "suggested her". So the answer is "He suggested to her that they should go for a walk."

Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option keeps "do not" unchanged, but negative imperatives become "forbade + object + to-infinitive" or "told + object + not to"; "said me" is always wrong. So the answer is "Mother forbade me to go out in the rain."

150. The commander said, "Fire at the enemy, but do not waste bullets."
Choose the correct indirect speech.
(a) The commander ordered his men that fire at the enemy but not waste bullets. (b) The commander ordered to fire at the enemy but not to waste bullets. (c) The commander said fire at the enemy but do not waste bullets. (d) The commander ordered his men to fire at the enemy but not to waste bullets.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option omits the object "his men", which "ordered" requires; commands are reported as "ordered + object + to-infinitive", with the negative command as "not to waste". So the answer is "The commander ordered his men to fire at the enemy but not to waste bullets."
