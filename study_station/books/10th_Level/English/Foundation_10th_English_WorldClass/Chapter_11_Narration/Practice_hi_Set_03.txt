51. He said, "I am happy."
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that I was happy. (c) He said that he was happy. (d) He said that he had been happy.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) में present simple "am" बदलकर past simple "was" हो जाता है, और "I" कर्ता के अनुसार "he" हो जाता है। लुभाने वाला विकल्प "is" को अपरिवर्तित रखता है, जो reporting verb past में होने पर गलत है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he was happy."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

52. She said to me, "I like your book."
(a) She told me that she liked my book. (b) She told me that she likes my book. (c) She told me that I liked my book. (d) She said me that she liked my book.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Said to me" बदलकर "told me" हो जाता है; "like" बदलकर "liked" और "your" बदलकर "my" हो जाता है। जाल वाला विकल्प "said me" अव्याकरणिक है क्योंकि "say" कभी प्रत्यक्ष personal object नहीं लेता। इसलिए उत्तर है "She told me that she liked my book."

53. The teacher said, "The sun rises in the east."
(a) The teacher said that the sun rose in the east. (b) The teacher said that the sun was rising in the east. (c) The teacher said that the sun had risen in the east. (d) The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य (universal truths) अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य में tense नहीं बदलते, इसलिए "rises" का "rises" ही रहता है। लुभाने वाला विकल्प इसे "rose" में बदल देता है, जो आम जाल है। इसलिए उत्तर है "The teacher said that the sun rises in the east."

(a) She said that she could swim. (b) She said that she can swim. (c) She said that she may swim. (d) She said that she should swim.

56. He said to me, "Do you like tea?"
(a) He asked me if I liked tea. (b) He asked me that did I like tea. (c) He asked me if I like tea. (d) He told me if I liked tea.

हल: हाँ/नहीं वाले प्रश्नों में "if" या "whether" लगता है, प्रश्न कथन बन जाता है ("I liked"), और "said to" की जगह "asked" आता है। जाल वाला विकल्प प्रश्न रूप "did I like" बरकरार रखता है, जो reported speech में गलत है। इसलिए उत्तर है "He asked me if I liked tea."

57. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
(a) Mother told me to not go out in the rain. (b) Mother asked me that do not go out in the rain. (c) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (d) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: नकारात्मक आदेशों में "forbade" या "told... not to" प्रयोग होता है। जाल वाला विकल्प "told me to not go" infinitive को अजीब ढंग से तोड़ता है; मानक रूप "not to go" या "forbade" है। इसलिए उत्तर है "Mother forbade me to go out in the rain."

68. The boy said, "Alas! My brother is dead."

69. She said to him, "Why did you come late?"
(a) She asked him that why he had come late. (b) She asked him why did he come late. (c) She asked him why he came late. (d) She asked him why he had come late.
