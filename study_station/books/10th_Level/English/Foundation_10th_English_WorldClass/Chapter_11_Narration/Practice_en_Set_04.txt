76. He said, "I am writing a letter now."
(a) He said that he was writing a letter then. (b) He said that he is writing a letter then. (c) He said that he had been writing a letter now. (d) He said that he wrote a letter then.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Present Continuous changes to Past Continuous in indirect speech, and "now" becomes "then". The tempting wrong option keeps "is" unchanged, which violates the backshift rule when the reporting verb is in the past. So the answer is "He said that he was writing a letter then."
