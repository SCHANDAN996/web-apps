101. He said to me, "Do you know where the station is?"
(a) He asked me did I know where the station was. (b) He asked me if I knew where the station was. (c) He asked me that if I knew where the station is. (d) He asked me whether do I know where the station was.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: लुभाने वाला गलत विकल्प प्रश्न-क्रम "did I know" को वैसा ही रखता है, लेकिन reported speech में clause एक statement बन जाता है, इसलिए subject verb से पहले आता है। "Do you know" एक yes/no प्रश्न है, इसलिए "if/whether" का प्रयोग होता है, और "know" बदलकर "knew", "is" बदलकर "was" हो जाता है। अतः सही उत्तर है "He asked me if I knew where the station was."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

102. She said, "I was cooking dinner when the lights went out."
(a) She said that she is cooking dinner when the lights went out. (b) She said that she was cooking dinner when the lights had gone out. (c) She said that she had been cooking dinner when the lights went out. (d) She said that she had cooked dinner when the lights went out.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Past continuous ("was cooking") reported speech में past perfect continuous ("had been cooking") बन जाता है, जबकि "when" के बाद simple past "went" वैसा ही रहता है। जो विकल्प "went" को "had gone" बदल देता है, वह tense को अनावश्यक रूप से आगे बढ़ा देता है। अतः सही उत्तर है "She said that she had been cooking dinner when the lights went out."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी

103. The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
(a) The teacher said that the earth revolved around the sun. (b) The teacher said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (c) The teacher said that the earth was revolving around the sun. (d) The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य और वैज्ञानिक तथ्य reported speech में tense नहीं बदलते, भले ही reporting verb past में हो। जाल वाला विकल्प "revolved" स्थायी तथ्य पर backshift नियम गलती से लागू करता है। अतः सही उत्तर है "The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी

104. He said to her, "Please lend me your pen."
(a) He told her please lend him her pen. (b) He requested her that lend him your pen. (c) He requested to her to lend him her pen. (d) He requested her to lend him her pen.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Please" वाली विनम्र request को "requested + object + to-infinitive" से report करते हैं; "your" श्रोता के अनुसार "her" बन जाता है। "Requested to her" गलत है क्योंकि "request" object को सीधे लेता है, बिना "to" के। अतः सही उत्तर है "He requested her to lend him her pen."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

105. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
(a) Mother told me do not go out in the rain. (b) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (c) Mother ordered me to not went out in the rain. (d) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Negative imperative को "forbade + object + to-infinitive" या "told + object + not to + verb" से report करते हैं। "Said me" अव्याकरणिक है (say के साथ व्यक्ति से पहले "to" चाहिए), और "to not went" infinitive को past tense से मिला देता है। अतः सही उत्तर है "Mother forbade me to go out in the rain."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी

106. He said, "Alas! I have lost my wallet."
(a) He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet. (b) He said alas that he lost his wallet. (c) He exclaimed with joy that he had lost his wallet. (d) He cried that he has lost his wallet.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Alas!" दुख दर्शाता है, इसलिए reporting verb "exclaimed with sorrow" होगा; present perfect "have lost" बदलकर past perfect "had lost" हो जाता है। जाल वाला विकल्प "with joy" प्रयोग करता है, जो "alas" की भावना के विपरीत है। अतः सही उत्तर है "He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

107. She said to me, "Will you help me with this work?"
(a) She asked me if I would help her with that work. (b) She asked me will I help her with that work. (c) She asked me whether will I help her with this work. (d) She told me if I would help her with that work.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Yes/no प्रश्न "if/whether" से शुरू होता है और statement word order लेता है; "will" बदलकर "would" और "this" बदलकर "that" हो जाता है। जाल वाला विकल्प उल्टा क्रम "will I help" रखता है, जो केवल direct प्रश्नों में सही है। अतः सही उत्तर है "She asked me if I would help her with that work."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी

108. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward and capture the post."
(a) The captain ordered the soldiers marched forward and capture the post. (b) The captain said the soldiers march forward and captured the post. (c) The captain requested the soldiers that they march forward and capture the post. (d) The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward and capture the post.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: वरिष्ठ की command को "ordered + object + to-infinitive" से report करते हैं; दोनों verbs "march" और "capture" "and" से जुड़े infinitive में ही रहते हैं। जाल वाला विकल्प "marched" प्रयोग करके infinitive संरचना तोड़ देता है। अतः सही उत्तर है "The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward and capture the post."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

109. He said, "I may go to Delhi tomorrow."
(a) He said that he may go to Delhi the next day. (b) He said that he might go to Delhi the next day. (c) He said that he might go to Delhi tomorrow. (d) He said that he would may go to Delhi the next day.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Reported speech में "may" बदलकर "might" और "tomorrow" बदलकर "the next day" हो जाता है। जाल वाला विकल्प "tomorrow" को वैसा ही रखता है, भूल जाता है कि समय-अभिव्यक्तियाँ भी बदलती हैं। अतः सही उत्तर है "He said that he might go to Delhi the next day."

110. She said, "What a beautiful garden this is!"
(a) She told that it was a beautiful garden. (b) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful garden. (c) She exclaimed that what a beautiful garden it is. (d) She said what a beautiful garden that was.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "What a" वाले exclamations को "exclaimed that" से report करते हैं और exclamatory भाव के स्थान पर "very" आता है; "is" बदलकर "was" और "this" बदलकर "it/that" हो जाता है। Reported clause के अंदर "what a" रखना आम जाल है। अतः सही उत्तर है "She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful garden."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

120. He said, "If I were a bird, I would fly to you."
