26. He said, "I am happy."
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that I was happy. (c) He said that he had been happy. (d) He said that he was happy.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Reporting verb "said" past tense में है, इसलिए "am" बदलकर "was" हो जाता है और "I" बदलकर "he" हो जाता है। लुभाने वाला विकल्प "is" को वैसा ही रखता है, जो backshift नियम तोड़ता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He said that he was happy."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

27. She said to me, "I will help you."
(a) She told me that she will help me. (b) She told me that I would help her. (c) She said me that she would help you. (d) She told me that she would help me.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Said to me" बदलकर "told me" हो जाता है, "will" बदलकर "would" हो जाता है, और "you" (श्रोता) बदलकर "me" हो जाता है। "will" को वैसा ही रखने वाला विकल्प tense backshift को नज़रअंदाज़ करता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "She told me that she would help me."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

28. The teacher said, "The sun rises in the east."
(a) The teacher said that the sun had risen in the east. (b) The teacher said that the sun rises in the east. (c) The teacher said that the sun rose in the east. (d) The teacher said that the sun was rising in the east.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य (universal truths) indirect speech में tense नहीं बदलते, इसलिए "rises" का "rises" ही रहता है। "rose" वाला विकल्प स्थायी तथ्य पर गलती से backshift लगाता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "The teacher said that the sun rises in the east."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

29. He said to her, "Do you like tea?"
(a) He asked her that did she like tea. (b) He asked her if she liked tea. (c) He asked her if she likes tea. (d) He told her if she liked tea.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Yes/no प्रश्न में "asked + if/whether" का प्रयोग होता है, प्रश्न का क्रम statement क्रम में बदल जाता है, और "like" बदलकर "liked" हो जाता है। "that did she like" वाला विकल्प गलती से प्रश्न का क्रम बनाए रखता है, इसलिए उत्तर है "He asked her if she liked tea."

30. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
(a) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain. (b) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (c) Mother told me do not go out in the rain. (d) Mother asked me that I should not go out in the rain.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Negative imperative बदलकर "forbade + to-infinitive" या "told me not to go" हो जाता है। "told me do not go" वाला विकल्प imperative रूप बनाए रखता है, जो अव्याकरणिक है, इसलिए उत्तर है "Mother forbade me to go out in the rain."
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

31. She said, "I wrote a letter yesterday."
(a) She said that she had written a letter the day before. (b) She said that she wrote a letter yesterday. (c) She said that she has written a letter the day before. (d) She said that she was writing a letter the day before.

32. He said to me, "Where do you live?"
(a) He asked me where did I live. (b) He asked me where do I live. (c) He asked me where I lived. (d) He told me where I lived.

33. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward."
(a) The captain asked the soldiers that march forward. (b) The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward. (c) The captain requested the soldiers marching forward. (d) The captain said the soldiers march forward.

46. He said, "If I were a bird, I would fly to you."
