126. In a certain code, "MASTER" is written as "OCUVGT". How will "PLANET" be written in the same code?
(a) RNCQGV (b) RNCPGV (c) QNCPGV (d) RNCPGU
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option RNCQGV comes from shifting only some letters by +2 and forgetting the rest. Each letter of MASTER moves +2: M→O, A→C, S→U, T→V, E→G, R→T. Applying +2 to PLANET: P→R, L→N, A→C, N→P, E→G, T→V, so the answer is RNCPGV.
