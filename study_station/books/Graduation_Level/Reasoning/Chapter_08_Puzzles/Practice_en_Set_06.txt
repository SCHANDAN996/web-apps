126. Eight persons L, M, N, O, P, Q, R and S sit in a row facing south. N sits second to the left of M. L sits between M and S (next to both). O sits third to the right of R. How many persons sit between N and R?
(a) 0 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. As we look at them (they face us), from our left to our right: S, L, M, O, N, Q, R, P. So the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style
