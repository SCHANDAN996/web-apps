126. Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing north. A sits at the extreme left end. C sits exactly in the middle of the row. B sits between A and C. D sits at the extreme right end. Who sits second from the right?
(a) C (b) E (c) D (d) B
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering D, who sits at the right end itself rather than second from it. The order from left is A, B, C, E, D, so the answer is E.

127. In a family of six members A, B, C, D, E and F, there are two married couples. D is the grandmother of A and the mother of B. C is the wife of B and the mother of F. F is the granddaughter of E. How is A related to C?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) Son (c) Granddaughter (d) Daughter
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming A must be a daughter because A is a child of B and C. A's gender is never stated anywhere in the clues, so A could be a son or a daughter. So the answer is Cannot be determined.

128. Five boxes A, B, C, D and E are stacked one above another. Box C is immediately above box D. Box A is above box B but not at the top. Box E is at the bottom. Which box is at the top?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) D
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is A, since "A is above B" is read first, but A is not at the top and E is at the bottom. The only arrangement fitting all clues is C–D–A–B–E from top to bottom, so the answer is C.

129. Six lectures P, Q, R, S, T and U are held from Monday to Saturday, one per day. P is on Monday. Q is on Thursday. R is held immediately before S. T is on Saturday. Which lecture is held on Wednesday?
(a) R (b) U (c) T (d) S
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing R and S on Friday–Saturday, but T already occupies Saturday. The only consecutive free pair for R–S is Tuesday–Wednesday, leaving U for Friday, so the answer is S.

130. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits opposite D. B sits second to the right of A. C sits opposite B. F is adjacent to A. Who sits opposite E?
(a) B (b) D (c) C (d) F
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering C, who is opposite B, not E. Fixing A at one seat: D is opposite A, B is two seats to A's right, C is opposite B, and F takes the free seat next to A. The only seat left for E is opposite F, so the answer is F.

133. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F are seated in two rows of three, facing each other. A faces D. B is to the left of A. E does not face B. C is at an end. Who faces B?
(a) C (b) F (c) D (d) E
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering E by ignoring the clue that E does not face B. Row 1 is B, A, C (B left of A, C at an end). In the facing row, D faces A, so D is in the middle; E does not face B, so E faces C and F faces B. So the answer is F.

134. Five cars — red, blue, green, black and white — are parked in a row. The red car is between the blue and green cars. The black car is at the left end. The white car is not next to the red car. Which car is at the right end?
(a) Green (b) Blue (c) White (d) Red
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is green, assuming red sits between blue and green at the right side. Black is at the left end, and red between blue and green forms a block of three in positions 2–4; since white is not next to red, white must take the far right end. So the answer is White.

136. A clock shows 4:15. Through what angle has the hour hand moved from 3 o'clock?
(a) 30° (b) 45° (c) 37.5° (d) 32.5°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering 45°, treating 4:15 as one and a half hours of 30° each. The hour hand moves 0.5° per minute: from 3:00 to 4:15 is 75 minutes, giving 75 × 0.5° = 37.5°, so the answer is 37.5°.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

Answer: (a)

138. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km, then turns left and walks 2 km. In which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) West (b) North (c) South (d) East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering South, since he walked 5 km north and then 5 km south, which cancel out. After cancellation, his net movement is 3 km east plus 2 km east (the left turn while facing south points east), totalling 5 km east, so the answer is East.

139. Five friends A, B, C, D and E like five different fruits — mango, apple, banana, grapes and orange. A likes mango. B does not like apple or banana. C likes the fruit that D does not like, and D likes grapes. E likes apple. Which fruit does B like?
(a) Orange (b) Apple (c) Banana (d) Grapes
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering banana by quick elimination while missing that B rejects banana too. A–mango, D–grapes, E–apple; B cannot take apple or banana, so B takes orange and C takes banana. So the answer is Orange.

140. In a row of 40 students, Ravi is 12th from the left and Kavi is 18th from the right. How many students sit between them?
(a) 12 (b) 11 (c) 10 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 18 − 12 = 6 or adding the positions wrongly. Kavi's position from the left is 40 − 18 + 1 = 23, and students between positions 12 and 23 = 23 − 12 − 1 = 10, so the answer is 10.

143. The letters of the word BRAIN are rearranged so that the two vowels always come together. How many arrangements are possible?
(a) 120 (b) 12 (c) 24 (d) 48
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 120, the total arrangements ignoring the condition. Treat the vowel block AI as one unit with the three consonants: 4! arrangements, and the vowels can swap within the block in 2! ways, giving 4! × 2! = 24 × 2 = 48, so the answer is 48.
