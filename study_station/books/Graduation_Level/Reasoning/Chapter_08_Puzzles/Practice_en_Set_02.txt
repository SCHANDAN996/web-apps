Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Five persons P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row facing north. P sits at the extreme left end. S sits to the immediate left of Q. R sits to the immediate right of Q. Who sits at the extreme right end?
(a) Q (b) R (c) S (d) T
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is R, who sits fourth, not last. Placing P at the left end and the block S, Q, R next to it gives the order P, S, Q, R, T, so the answer is T.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Five boxes P, Q, R, S, T are stacked one above another. P is immediately above Q. R is above P but not at the top. T is at the bottom. Which box is at the top?
(a) R (b) P (c) S (d) Q
Answer: (c)
Solution: T is at the bottom, P is directly above Q, and R is above P but not at the top, so the stack from top is S, R, P, Q, T. The tempting wrong choice is R, but R is barred from the top by the clue. The only box left for the top is S, so the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style

28. In a family of six, P is the son of Q. R is the sister of P. S is the mother of R. T is the husband of S. U is the daughter of T. How is U related to P?
(a) Mother (b) Sister (c) Aunt (d) Cousin
Answer: (b)
Solution: S is mother of R, and R is sister of P, so S is also mother of P; T, husband of S, is father of P. U is daughter of T, making U a sister of P. The tempting wrong choice is Cousin, but no second couple is given to create a cousin branch, so the answer is Sister.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Seven lectures on subjects A, B, C, D, E, F, G are held from Monday to Sunday. A is on Thursday. B is immediately before A. C is three days after B. E is on Sunday. F is immediately after D. Which subject is on Monday?
(a) F (b) C (c) D (d) G
Answer: (c)
Solution: B is on Wednesday, C is three days later on Saturday, and E is on Sunday. The remaining days Monday, Tuesday, Friday go to D, F, G; since F must be immediately after D, D is Monday and F is Tuesday, leaving G on Friday. The tempting wrong choice is G, but G cannot be paired with F as required, so the answer is D.
Source: PYQ-style

30. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T, U sit around a circular table facing the centre. P sits opposite S. Q sits to the immediate right of P. R sits to the immediate left of S. T sits to the immediate right of S. Who sits opposite Q?
(a) T (b) P (c) U (d) R
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fix P with S opposite; Q takes the seat immediately right of P, R the seat immediately left of S, and T the seat immediately right of S, leaving U in the last seat. Counting across the table, the seat opposite Q falls on T. The tempting wrong choice is R, but R sits next to T, not opposite Q, so the answer is T.
Source: PYQ-style

31. Five friends scored different marks. Ram scored more than Shyam but less than Mohan. Sohan scored more than Mohan. Geeta scored less than Shyam. Who scored the second highest?
(a) Shyam (b) Sohan (c) Ram (d) Mohan
Answer: (d)
Solution: The order from highest is Sohan, Mohan, Ram, Shyam, Geeta. The tempting wrong choice is Sohan, who is the highest, not the second highest. Second highest is Mohan, so the answer is Mohan.

32. In a row of students, Kiran is 12th from the left and 18th from the right. How many students are in the row?
(a) 28 (b) 30 (c) 31 (d) 29
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 12 + 18 − 1 = 29. The tempting wrong choice is 30, obtained by adding 12 and 18 without subtracting the double-counted person, so the answer is 29.

33. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T, U are seated in two rows of three facing each other. P faces Q. R is to the left of P. S faces R. T is to the right of Q. Who faces T?
(a) P (b) U (c) R (d) S
Answer: (b)
Solution: In the first row R sits left of P, so the row is R, P, U. In the facing row S faces R and Q faces P, so T, being to the right of Q, faces the remaining person U. The tempting wrong choice is S, but S faces R, so the answer is U.

34. A man points to a photo and says, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Aunt (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Daughter
Answer: (c)
Solution: The grandfather's only son is the man's own father, and the father's daughter is the man's sister. The tempting wrong choice is Aunt, which comes from misreading "grandfather's son" as an uncle, but "only son" fixes it as the father, so the answer is Sister.

35. Five cities A, B, C, D, E are located such that A is north of B, C is south of B, D is north of C, and E is south of C. Which city is the southernmost?
(a) C (b) D (c) E (d) B
Answer: (c)
Solution: A is north of B, C is south of B, D is north of C, and E is south of C, so E lies below every other city. The tempting wrong choice is C, which is south of B but still north of E, so the answer is E.

36. Four persons W, X, Y, Z like four different colours red, blue, green, yellow. W likes neither red nor yellow. X likes blue. Y does not like red. Who likes red?
(a) Z (b) X (c) Y (d) W
Answer: (a)
Solution: X likes blue; W cannot like red or yellow, so W likes green; Y cannot like red, so Y likes yellow. Red is left for Z. The tempting wrong choice is Y, but the clue directly bars Y from red, so the answer is Z.

37. Six books are kept on a shelf. History is to the right of Geography but to the left of Maths. English is at the extreme left. Science is between English and Geography. Civics is between History and Maths. Which book is at the extreme right?
(a) Civics (b) History (c) Maths (d) Geography
Answer: (c)
Solution: The order from left is English, Science, Geography, History, Civics, Maths, which satisfies every clue. The tempting wrong choice is Civics, but Civics must sit between History and Maths, so it cannot be last. The extreme right book is Maths, so the answer is Maths.

38. In a family, A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. D is the father of A. E is the son of D. How is E related to B?
(a) Cousin (b) Father (c) Uncle (d) Brother
Answer: (d)
Solution: D is father of A, and A is brother of B, so D is also father of B. E is son of D, hence E is brother of B. The tempting wrong choice is Cousin, which assumes E belongs to another branch, but all share father D, so the answer is Brother.

39. Seven persons stand in a queue. Ganesh is at the front. Mahesh is immediately behind Ganesh. Ramesh is immediately behind Mahesh. Suresh is immediately behind Ramesh. Dinesh is behind Suresh. Mukesh is behind Dinesh. Who is exactly in the middle of the queue?
(a) Suresh (b) Dinesh (c) Mahesh (d) Ramesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: The order from front is Ganesh, Mahesh, Ramesh, Suresh, Dinesh, Mukesh, and the seventh person at the rear. With seven persons the middle is the 4th position, which is Suresh. The tempting wrong choice is Ramesh, who is 3rd, not 4th, so the answer is Suresh.

40. Five friends A, B, C, D, E have birthdays in different months from January to May. A's birthday is in March. B's is before A's but not in January. C's is immediately after A's. D's is in January. In which month is E's birthday?
(a) April (b) May (c) February (d) January
Answer: (b)
Solution: D is January, B is February (before March but not January), A is March, and C is immediately after A, so C is April. The only month left for E is May. The tempting wrong choice is April, but April is fixed for C, so the answer is May.

41. In a circular arrangement of six people facing the centre, A is opposite D. B is to the immediate left of A. C is between D and E. Who is to the immediate right of D?
(a) E (b) F (c) C (d) B
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fix A with D opposite; B is immediate left of A. C sits between D and E, so C and E occupy the two seats on one side of D, leaving F on the other side, which is the immediate right of D. The tempting wrong choice is C, but C is separated from D's right side by the "between" clue, so the answer is F.

Solution: The only daughter of the woman's mother is the woman herself, so the man's mother is the woman speaking. The tempting wrong choice is Sister, which comes from thinking "only daughter" refers to someone else in the family, so the answer is Mother.

43. Six employees work on six different floors numbered 1 to 6. K works on floor 4. L is above K. M is below K but not on floor 1. N is on floor 1. O is immediately below L. On which floor does O work?
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: K is on 4 and N on 1; M is below K but not on 1, so M is on 2 or 3. L is above K on floor 5 or 6; if L were on 5, O would be on 4, but K is there, so L is on 6 and O is on 5. The tempting wrong choice is 6, which is L's floor, not O's, so the answer is 5.

44. In a row facing north, X is 7th from the left and Y is 9th from the right. If they interchange positions, X becomes 11th from the left. How many persons are in the row?
(a) 19 (b) 18 (c) 20 (d) 21
Answer: (a)
Solution: After the interchange X sits at Y's original seat, which is 11th from the left and 9th from the right. Total = 11 + 9 − 1 = 19. The tempting wrong choice is 20, obtained by adding without subtracting the double-counted seat, so the answer is 19.

47. Eight boxes are stacked. Box A is immediately above Box B. Box C is immediately below Box B. Box D is at the top. Box E is immediately below Box D. Box F is at the bottom. Box G is immediately above Box F. Where is Box H?
(a) Between B and C (b) Between G and F (c) Immediately above D (d) Between E and A
Answer: (d)
Solution: From the top the fixed order is D, E, then the block A, B, C, then G, F — that is seven boxes. The eighth box H must fit between E and A to complete the stack. The tempting wrong choice is between B and C, but A, B, C form a fixed consecutive block, so the answer is Between E and A.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The height order is P, Q, S, R, T from tallest to shortest. The tempting wrong choice is R, which forgets that T is shorter than R. The shortest is T, so the answer is T.

49. Six friends G, H, I, J, K, L sit in a row facing north. G is third to the left of H. I is to the immediate right of H. J is at the extreme right end. K sits between G and L. Who sits at the extreme left end?
(a) G (b) K (c) L (d) I
Answer: (a)
Solution: Facing north, left and right match the observer's left and right. G is three places left of H and I is immediately right of H, giving G, _, _, H, I with J at the extreme right; K between G and L fills the two gaps as K then L, giving G, K, L, H, I, J. The tempting wrong choice is L, but L sits between K and H, not at an end, so the answer is G.
Source: PYQ-style

50. A, B, C, D, E are five houses in a row. A is to the right of B. C is to the left of D. E is to the right of A. B is to the right of D. Which house is in the middle?
(a) A (b) B (c) D (d) E
Answer: (b)
Solution: Chaining the clues gives the left-to-right order C, D, B, A, E. The tempting wrong choice is A, which feels central but is actually fourth. The middle (third) house is B, so the answer is B.
