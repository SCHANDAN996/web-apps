26. Seven friends P, Q, R, S, T, U and V are of different heights. Rank 1 is the shortest and rank 7 is the tallest. U is just taller than V (next rank). Q has rank 6 from the shortest. U is shorter than R, with exactly 1 person(s) in height between them. V is taller than S, with exactly 1 person(s) in height between them. Who is just taller than Q?
(a) U (b) V (c) R (d) P
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one order. From 1 upwards: rank 1 – P, rank 2 – S, rank 3 – T, rank 4 – V, rank 5 – U, rank 6 – Q, rank 7 – R. The trap is U, which you get by counting from the wrong end or on the wrong side. So the answer is R.
