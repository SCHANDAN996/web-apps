51. Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F and G live on seven different floors of a building. The lowest floor is numbered 1 and the top floor is numbered 7. B lives on the top floor. E lives on floor 6. D lives on some floor above C. G lives on floor 5. Exactly 2 persons live between A and D. C lives on some floor below F. Who lives on the floor immediately below C?
(a) A (b) G (c) F (d) B
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one order. From 1 upwards: floor 1 – A, floor 2 – C, floor 3 – F, floor 4 – D, floor 5 – G, floor 6 – E, floor 7 – B. The trap is F, which you get by counting from the wrong end or on the wrong side. So the answer is A.
Source: PYQ-style
