│ 1. LINE UP! (linear arrangement)             │
│ "Line-Up = Lock the Ends First" — In line-ups, fix endpoints first. The two ends give you the first "end-locks".   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 2. BOX IT!                │
│ "Box It = Box Diagram" — For grid puzzles, draw boxes. "Box It"! — Draw the diagram!  │
