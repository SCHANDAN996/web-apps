101. Seven persons L, M, N, O, P, Q and R sit in a row facing north. R sits third to the right of Q. M sits to the immediate right of L. N sits to the immediate left of O. N sits second to the left of P. Who sits to the immediate left of L?
(a) O (b) Q (c) P (d) M
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: Q, L, M, R, N, O, P. The trap is M, who is on the other side of L. So the answer is Q.
Source: PYQ-style
