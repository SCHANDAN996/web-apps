76. Seven friends S, T, U, V, W, X and Y are of different heights. Rank 1 is the shortest and rank 7 is the tallest. W has rank 5 from the shortest. S has rank 3 from the shortest. S is just taller than U (next rank). How many persons are taller than one and shorter than the other of W and U?
(a) 3 (b) 0 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one order. From 1 upwards: rank 1 – T, rank 2 – U, rank 3 – S, rank 4 – Y, rank 5 – W, rank 6 – V, rank 7 – X. The trap is 3, which you get by counting from the wrong end or on the wrong side. So the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style
