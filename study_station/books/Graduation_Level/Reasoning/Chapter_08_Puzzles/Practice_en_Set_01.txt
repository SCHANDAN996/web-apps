1. Five lectures D, E, F, G and H are held on five consecutive days of one week, from Monday to Friday, one lecture a day. Exactly 2 lectures are held between D and H. F is held 2 days before D. E is held on the day immediately before H. Which lecture is held on Thursday?
(a) H (b) F (c) G (d) E
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one order. From 1 upwards: Monday – E, Tuesday – H, Wednesday – F, Thursday – G, Friday – D. The trap is H, which you get by counting from the wrong end or on the wrong side. So the answer is G.
Source: PYQ-style
