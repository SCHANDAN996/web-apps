1. Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing north. A sits at the extreme left end. B sits immediately to the right of A. C sits in the middle. D sits immediately to the left of E. Who sits at the extreme right end?
(a) E (b) D (c) C (d) B
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is D, who sits next to E but not at the end. The clues fix the order A, B, C, D, E from left to right, so the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Five boxes P, Q, R, S and T are stacked one above another. P is at the top. Q is immediately below P. R is immediately below Q. S is immediately below R. Which box is at the bottom?
(a) S (b) Q (c) R (d) T
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is S, the last box named in the clues, but one box still remains. The order from the top is P, Q, R, S, so the leftover box T takes the bottom place, so the answer is T.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G live on floors 1 to 7 of a building. A lives on floor 3. B lives immediately above A. C lives on floor 7. D lives immediately below C. E lives on floor 1. G lives immediately above E. Who lives on floor 5?
(a) F (b) G (c) D (d) B
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is G, but G sits on floor 2, immediately above E. Floors are fixed as E1, G2, A3, B4, D6, C7, leaving only floor 5 for F, so the answer is F.

4. Pointing to a woman, a man said, "She is the daughter of my mother's only son." How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Sister (b) Daughter (c) Niece (d) Aunt
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Sister, which would fit "my father's daughter", but the clue says the mother's only son, who is the man himself. The daughter of the man is his daughter, so the answer is Daughter.

5. Six friends A, B, C, D, E, F sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits opposite D. B sits immediately to the left of A. E sits opposite B. Who sits opposite C?
(a) E (b) F (c) D (d) B
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is E, but E is already fixed opposite B. With A opposite D and B opposite E, the only remaining pair is C with F, so the answer is F.

6. Five exams — Hindi, English, Maths, Science, GK — are held Monday to Friday, one per day. Maths is on Wednesday. English is before Maths but not on Monday. Science is immediately after GK. Hindi is not on Friday. Which exam is on Friday?
(a) Hindi (b) GK (c) English (d) Science
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing GK–Science on Monday–Tuesday, but Tuesday is already taken by English, the only day before Wednesday that is not Monday. So GK is Thursday and Science is Friday, with Hindi on Monday, so the answer is Science.

7. Six people P, Q, R, S, T, U are of different heights. P is taller than Q but shorter than R. S is taller than R. T is shorter than Q. U is taller than S. Who is the shortest?
(a) T (b) Q (c) P (d) S
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Q, which looks short in the first clue, but T is explicitly shorter than Q. The full chain is U > S > R > P > Q > T, so the answer is T.

8. In a row of students facing north, Ravi is 7th from the left and 12th from the right. How many students are in the row?
(a) 17 (b) 19 (c) 18 (d) 20
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding 7 + 12 = 19, which counts Ravi twice. Total = 7 + 12 − 1, so the answer is 18.

9. Five friends A, B, C, D, E each like a different colour: red, blue, green, yellow, pink. A likes red. C likes green. B likes neither blue nor yellow. D likes blue. What colour does E like?
(a) Pink (b) Blue (c) Green (d) Yellow
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Pink for E, but pink is the only colour left for B once red, green and blue are assigned and B rejects blue and yellow. With A red, C green, D blue and B pink, yellow remains for E, so the answer is Yellow.

10. Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman related to the man?

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Cousin, which would need the grandfather to have another son, but "only son" rules that out. Grandfather's only son is the man's father, and the father's daughter is the man's sister, so the answer is Sister.

12. A, B, C, D, E, F, G have birthdays in the same week (Monday to Sunday), one each day. A's birthday is on Thursday. B is three days before A. C is immediately after D. G is on Sunday. E is not on Friday or Saturday. On which day is C's birthday?
(a) Friday (b) Saturday (c) Tuesday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is placing the D–C pair on Tuesday–Wednesday, but then E would have only Friday or Saturday left, which the clue forbids. With A on Thursday, B on Monday and G on Sunday, the pair must be D on Friday and C on Saturday, so the answer is Saturday.

15. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 3 km West (b) 5 km North (c) 8 km East (d) 3 km East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 3 km West, which comes from misreading the second right turn; facing east, a right turn points south. The 5 km north and 5 km south cancel out, leaving only the 3 km eastward leg, so the answer is 3 km East.

(a) Chemistry (b) Physics (c) English (d) History
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is placing the Physics–Chemistry pair in periods 1–2, but then English would have no period before Maths. The pair must occupy periods 4–5 with Physics first, while English and History take periods 1 and 2, so the answer is Physics.

17. In a family, R is the mother of S. S is the brother of T. T is the mother of U. P is the father of S. How is P related to U?
(a) Uncle (b) Father (c) Grandfather (d) Brother
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Uncle, which describes S's relation to U, not P's. P is the father of S and hence of S's sibling T, and T is U's mother, making P the grandfather of U, so the answer is Grandfather.

18. Five girls Anita, Bina, Chitra, Deepa and Ela sit in a row facing south. Anita is second to the left of Bina. Chitra is at one end. Deepa is to the immediate right of Anita. Ela sits between Bina and Chitra. Who is at the extreme left end as seen by the girls?
(a) Chitra (b) Bina (c) Anita (d) Ela
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is reading "left" from the viewer's side; for a south-facing row the girls' left is the viewer's right. From the viewer's side the order is Chitra, Ela, Bina, Deepa, Anita, so the girls' extreme left is the viewer's extreme right, so the answer is Anita.

21. Eight persons sit around a square table, four at corners facing the centre and four at the middle of sides facing outward. A sits at a corner. B faces outward and is second to the right of A. C sits opposite A. D is to the immediate left of C and faces the centre. Which statement must be true?
(a) C faces outward (b) B sits opposite D (c) D sits at a corner (d) B and C are neighbours
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the claim that C faces outward, but C sits opposite corner-sitter A, so C is also at a corner facing the centre. D faces the centre and only corner seats face the centre, so D must sit at a corner, while outward-facing B sits at a side-middle and cannot be opposite D, so the answer is D sits at a corner.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Six employees P, Q, R, S, T, U are paid different salaries. P earns more than Q but less than R. S earns less than Q. T earns more than R but less than U. S does not earn the least. Who earns the least?
(a) The sixth person not compared with S (b) T (c) Q (d) S
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is picking S, ignoring the explicit clue that S is not the lowest earner. The chain U > T > R > P > Q > S covers only five people, so the least earner must be the sixth person never ranked against S, so the answer is the sixth person not compared with S.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Five couples (husbands A, B, C, D, E and wives P, Q, R, S, T) attend a party. A is married to R. B's wife is not Q or S. C is married to T. D's wife is S. Who is E's wife?
(a) P (b) Q (c) S (d) T

25. Four friends W, X, Y, Z play four different games — cricket, football, hockey, tennis — and each wears a different coloured jersey: red, blue, green, white. W plays cricket and does not wear red. X wears blue and does not play football or tennis. Y plays football and wears white. Z does not wear green. Which game does Z play and which jersey does Z wear?
(a) Tennis, red (b) Hockey, green (c) Tennis, white (d) Football, white
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Tennis with white, but white is already fixed on Y. X must play hockey, so Z plays tennis; W cannot wear red, blue or white, so W wears green, and red falls to Z, so the answer is Tennis, red.
