26. Statement: "Regular exercise reduces the risk of heart disease. Therefore, anyone who exercises regularly will never suffer from heart disease."
Which of the following best identifies the flaw in the argument?
(a) It confuses reduced risk with complete elimination of risk (b) It ignores the benefits of a balanced diet (c) It assumes exercise is the only factor affecting heart disease (d) It relies on an unrepresentative sample of patients
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is the first one, but the argument's core error is logical: reducing risk does not mean eliminating it. The conclusion "will never suffer" overstates the premise "reduces the risk", so the answer is that it confuses reduced risk with complete elimination of risk.
