1. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3:15?
(a) 0° (b) 15° (c) 7.5° (d) 22.5°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 0°, which assumes the hour hand stays exactly at 3. In 15 minutes the hour hand moves 15 × 0.5° = 7.5° ahead of 3, while the minute hand points exactly at 3. So the angle is 7.5°, so the answer is 7.5°.

2. At what time between 4 o'clock and 5 o'clock do the hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 20 minutes past 4 (b) 21 minutes past 4 (c) 22 minutes past 4 (d) 21 9/11 minutes past 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The hands coincide at (60 × H)/11 minutes past H. For H = 4, time = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes past 4. The rough guess of 20 minutes ignores the hour hand's movement, so the answer is 21 9/11 minutes past 4.

3. How many times are the hands of a clock at right angles in 12 hours?
(a) 11 (b) 24 (c) 22 (d) 44
Answer: (c)
Solution: In 12 hours the hands would form right angles 24 times, but the periods 2–3 and 8–9 produce only one right angle each instead of two, reducing the count by 2. So the answer is 22.

Answer: (a)
Solution: Counting odd days up to 1999 (400 × 4 + 99 years pattern) gives 0 odd days for complete 1600 years, plus 5 odd days for 300 years, plus odd days for 99 years (124 ÷ 7 remainder 5) — total 10, i.e. 3 odd days from Monday base gives... using the standard reference, 1 January 2000 fell on a Saturday, so the answer is Saturday.

6. Which of the following years is NOT a leap year?
(a) 1900 (b) 2000 (c) 1600 (d) 2024
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that 1900 is divisible by 4, but a century year must be divisible by 400 to be a leap year. 1900 ÷ 400 leaves a remainder, so it is not a leap year, while 1600 and 2000 are. So the answer is 1900.

7. How many odd days are there in 100 consecutive years?
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: 100 years contain 76 ordinary years and 24 leap years, giving 76 + 48 = 124 days extra; 124 ÷ 7 leaves remainder 5. So the answer is 5.

(a) Saturday (b) Friday (c) Sunday (d) Thursday

Solution: 61 ÷ 7 = 8 weeks and 5 odd days. Monday + 5 days = Saturday. Counting 61 mod 7 as 6 is the common slip, so the answer is Saturday.

10. At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock are the hands of a clock exactly opposite each other?
(a) 5 5/11 minutes past 7 (b) 5 minutes past 7 (c) 6 minutes past 7 (d) 10 minutes past 7
Answer: (a)
Solution: Opposite hands occur at (30H − 180) × 2/11 minutes past H when 30H > 180. For H = 7: (210 − 180) × 2/11 = 60/11 = 5 5/11 minutes past 7. So the answer is 5 5/11 minutes past 7.

11. A clock gains 2 minutes every hour. It is set right at 8 a.m. What time will it show when the actual time is 8 p.m. the same day?
(a) 8:12 p.m. (b) 8:20 p.m. (c) 8:24 p.m. (d) 8:30 p.m.
Answer: (c)
Solution: From 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. is 12 hours; gain = 12 × 2 = 24 minutes. The faulty clock runs fast, so it shows 8:24 p.m. Subtracting the gain is the trap, so the answer is 8:24 p.m.

23. On what day of the week was 15 August 2024?
(a) Tuesday (b) Wednesday (c) Friday (d) Thursday
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1 January 2024 was a Monday. Days up to 31 July 2024 (leap year): 31+29+31+30+31+30+31 = 213; adding 15 gives day 228. (228 − 1) mod 7 = 227 mod 7 = 3; Monday + 3 = Thursday. Forgetting that 2024 is a leap year gives Wednesday — the trap — so the answer is Thursday.
Source: PYQ-style

24. At what time between 3 and 4 o'clock will the hands of a clock first be at right angles after 3 o'clock?
(a) Exactly at 3:00 (b) 32 8/11 minutes past 3 (c) 30 minutes past 3 (d) 33 minutes past 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering 3:00, which is indeed a right angle but the question asks for the time between 3 and 4 after 3 o'clock. Right angle occurs at (30H + 90) × 2/11 = (90 + 90) × 2/11 = 360/11 = 32 8/11 minutes past 3. So the answer is 32 8/11 minutes past 3.
Source: PYQ-style

25. The calendar of the year 2024 will be the same as which of the following years?
(a) 2048 (b) 2052 (c) 2050 (d) 2056
Answer: (b)
Solution: A leap year's calendar repeats after 28 years within the same century (when no non-leap century year intervenes): 2024 + 28 = 2052. The trap is adding 28 to get 2052 but second-guessing toward 2048; cumulative odd days confirm 28 years give a multiple of 7, so the answer is 2052.
