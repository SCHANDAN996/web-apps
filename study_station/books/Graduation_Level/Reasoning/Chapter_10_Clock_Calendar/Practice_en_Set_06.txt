126. At what time between 4 o'clock and 5 o'clock do the two hands of a clock coincide?
(a) 20 minutes past 4 (b) 21 9/11 minutes past 4 (c) 22 minutes past 4 (d) 21 10/11 minutes past 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer 20 minutes past 4 ignores that the hour hand also moves while the minute hand travels. The minute hand must gain 20 minutes over the hour hand, and it gains 55 minutes in 60 minutes, so required time = 20 × 60/55 = 240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes past 4, so the answer is 21 9/11 minutes past 4.
Source: PYQ-style

127. At what time between 5 and 6 o'clock will the hands of a clock first be at right angles?
(a) 11 minutes past 5 (b) 10 10/11 minutes past 5 (c) 43 7/11 minutes past 5 (d) 10 minutes past 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: At 5 o'clock the hands are 25 minute-spaces apart; for a right angle the gap must be 15 minute-spaces, so the minute hand must gain 10 spaces. Time = 10 × 60/55 = 120/11 = 10 10/11 minutes past 5 (the other right angle at 43 7/11 comes later, so it is not the first), so the answer is 10 10/11 minutes past 5.
Source: PYQ-style

128. How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in 24 hours?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 23 (d) 21
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer 24 assumes one coincidence per hour, but the hands coincide every 65 5/11 minutes, not every 60 minutes. They coincide 11 times in 12 hours, hence 22 times in 24 hours, so the answer is 22.
Source: PYQ-style

129. A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. It is set right at 6 AM on Monday. What time will it show when the actual time is 6 PM on Tuesday?
(a) 9 PM (b) 8 PM (c) 9:30 PM (d) 8:30 PM
Answer: (a)
Solution: From 6 AM Monday to 6 PM Tuesday is 36 hours. The clock gains 5 × 36 = 180 minutes = 3 hours, so it shows 6 PM + 3 hours = 9 PM, so the answer is 9 PM.
Source: PYQ-style

130. A clock loses 10 minutes every hour. It is set right at 12 noon. If this clock shows 5 PM, what is the correct time?
(a) 6 PM (b) 5:50 PM (c) 5:10 PM (d) 6:10 PM
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer 5:50 PM wrongly adds the lost minutes to the shown time. In this clock, 50 minutes of its dial equal 60 real minutes, so 5 shown hours = 5 × 60/50 = 6 real hours; correct time = 12 noon + 6 hours = 6 PM, so the answer is 6 PM.
Source: PYQ-style

131. What is the angle between the two hands of a clock at 7:20?
(a) 90° (b) 110° (c) 100° (d) 95°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer 90° comes from ignoring the hour hand's movement past 7. Angle = |30 × 7 − 5.5 × 20| = |210 − 110| = 100°, so the answer is 100°.
Source: PYQ-style

132. What is the smaller angle between the hands of a clock at 2:45?
(a) 187.5° (b) 165° (c) 172.5° (d) 180°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is option 187.5°, which is the larger reflex angle, not the smaller one asked. Angle = |30 × 2 − 5.5 × 45| = |60 − 247.5| = 187.5°, and the smaller angle = 360° − 187.5° = 172.5°, so the answer is 172.5°.
Source: PYQ-style

133. What is the mirror image of the time 3:40 as seen in a plane mirror placed beside the clock?
(a) 9:40 (b) 9:20 (c) 8:40 (d) 8:20
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer 9:20 comes from subtracting from 12:00 instead of 11:60. Mirror image = 11:60 − 3:40 = 8:20, so the answer is 8:20.
Source: PYQ-style

134. What is the water image of the time 4:15?
(a) 7:45 (b) 13:45 (c) 14:15 (d) 2:15
Answer: (d)
Solution: For water image subtract the time from 18:30. 18:30 − 4:15 = 14:15, which on a 12-hour clock is read as 2:15, so the answer is 2:15.
Source: PYQ-style

135. How many times in 12 hours are the hands of a clock exactly opposite each other?
(a) 12 (b) 22 (c) 10 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer 12 assumes once per hour, but between 5 and 7 the opposition occurs only once (at 6 o'clock), so one case is lost. Hence the hands are opposite 11 times in 12 hours, so the answer is 11.
Source: PYQ-style

136. How many times in a day do the hands of a clock form a straight line (either together or opposite)?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 44 (d) 48
Answer: (c)
Solution: Coincidences happen 22 times and oppositions 22 times in 24 hours; together they form a straight line 22 + 22 = 44 times, so the answer is 44.
Source: PYQ-style

137. What day of the week was 15 August 1947?
(a) Saturday (b) Thursday (c) Wednesday (d) Friday
Answer: (d)
Solution: Counting odd days: 1600 years = 0, 300 years = 1, 46 years = 11 leap + 35 ordinary = 57 days ≡ 1 odd day; days from 1 Jan to 15 Aug 1947 = 227 ≡ 3 odd days. Total = 0 + 1 + 1 + 3 = 5 ≡ Friday, so the answer is Friday.
Source: PYQ-style

138. What day of the week was 26 January 1950?
(a) Wednesday (b) Thursday (c) Friday (d) Tuesday
Answer: (b)
Solution: Odd days: 1600 years = 0, 300 years = 1, 49 years = 12 leap + 37 ordinary = 61 ≡ 5 odd days; 26 days of January ≡ 5 odd days. Total = 0 + 1 + 5 + 5 = 11 ≡ 4 ≡ Thursday, so the answer is Thursday.
Source: PYQ-style

139. What day of the week was 1 January 2001?
(a) Monday (b) Tuesday (c) Sunday (d) Saturday
Answer: (a)
Solution: Odd days in 2000 years = 0 (multiple of 400), so 1 January 2001 falls on the same day as 1 January 1 AD convention plus 0 odd days, which is Monday, so the answer is Monday.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Which of the following years is a leap year?
(a) 1900 (b) 2000 (c) 2100 (d) 2200
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that 1900, 2100 and 2200 are divisible by 4 yet are NOT leap years, because century years must be divisible by 400. Only 2000 is divisible by 400, so the answer is 2000.
Source: PYQ-style

141. How many odd days are there in 300 consecutive years?
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: In 300 years there are 72 leap years (century year like 1900 is not leap) and 228 ordinary years: 72 × 2 + 228 = 372 days ≡ 372 mod 7 = 1 odd day, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

142. If 1 January 2007 was a Monday, what day was 1 January 2008?
(a) Sunday (b) Wednesday (c) Tuesday (d) Monday
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to add 2 days thinking of leap year 2008, but the shift depends on the year that has passed (2007), which is an ordinary year with 1 odd day. Monday + 1 = Tuesday, so the answer is Tuesday.
Source: PYQ-style

143. If today is Wednesday, what day will it be after 61 days?
(a) Tuesday (b) Sunday (c) Friday (d) Monday
Answer: (d)
Solution: 61 ÷ 7 gives remainder 5. Wednesday + 5 days = Monday (Thursday 1, Friday 2, Saturday 3, Sunday 4, Monday 5), so the answer is Monday.
Source: PYQ-style

144. The calendar of the year 2011 will be used again in which year?
(a) 2017 (b) 2033 (c) 2039 (d) 2022
Answer: (d)
Solution: From 2011 to 2021 the odd days are 11 (one per year) plus 3 extra for leap years 2012, 2016, 2020, totalling 14 ≡ 0 odd days, so the calendar repeats in 2022, so the answer is 2022.
Source: PYQ-style

145. The last day of a century can never be which of the following?
(a) Monday (b) Wednesday (c) Saturday (d) Friday
Answer: (c)
Solution: The last day of a century can only be Monday, Wednesday, Friday or Sunday, because century years give 0, 5, 3 or 1 odd days respectively. Saturday never occurs, so the answer is Saturday.
Source: PYQ-style

146. The first day of a century must be which of the following?
(a) Wednesday (b) Monday (c) Sunday (d) Thursday
Answer: (b)
Solution: The first day of a century can only be Monday, Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday; Sunday, Wednesday and Friday are impossible. Among the options only Monday is valid, so the answer is Monday.
Source: PYQ-style

147. A watch shows 8:30. What is the angle between its two hands?
(a) 90° (b) 60° (c) 75° (d) 105°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer 90° assumes the hour hand is exactly at 8, but at 8:30 it has moved halfway to 9. Angle = |30 × 8 − 5.5 × 30| = |240 − 165| = 75°, so the answer is 75°.
Source: PYQ-style

148. At what time between 9 and 10 o'clock are the hands of a clock exactly opposite each other?
(a) 15 minutes past 9 (b) 16 4/11 minutes past 9 (c) 16 2/11 minutes past 9 (d) 17 minutes past 9
Answer: (b)
Solution: At 9 o'clock the minute hand is 45 spaces behind; for opposition the gap must be 30 spaces, so it must gain 15 spaces. Time = 15 × 60/55 = 180/11 = 16 4/11 minutes past 9, so the answer is 16 4/11 minutes past 9.
Source: PYQ-style

149. How many times in 24 hours are the hands of a clock at right angles to each other?
(a) 44 (b) 24 (c) 48 (d) 22
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer 48 assumes twice per hour, but between 2–4 and 8–10 o'clock one right angle each is lost (shared at 3 and 9). Right angles occur 22 times in 12 hours, hence 44 times in 24 hours, so the answer is 44.
Source: PYQ-style

150. If 15 August 2020 was a Saturday, what day of the week was 15 August 2021?
(a) Sunday (b) Saturday (c) Monday (d) Friday
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to add 2 days because 2020 is a leap year, but February 2020 falls before August 2020, so the period August 2020 to August 2021 contains no 29 February and has only 1 odd day. Saturday + 1 = Sunday, so the answer is Sunday.
Source: PYQ-style
