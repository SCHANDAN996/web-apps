76. A clock gains 5 minutes every hour. If it is set right at 6 a.m., what time will it show when the correct time is 6 p.m. the same day?
(a) 6:55 p.m. (b) 7:05 p.m. (c) 7:00 p.m. (d) 6:05 p.m.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer 6:55 p.m. comes from subtracting the gain instead of adding it. From 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. is 12 hours, so the clock gains 12 × 5 = 60 minutes. It therefore shows 6:00 + 60 min = 7:00 p.m., so the answer is 7:00 p.m.
