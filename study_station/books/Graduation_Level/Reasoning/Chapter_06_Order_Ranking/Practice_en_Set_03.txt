51. In a row of students, Ravi is 12th from the left end and 15th from the right end. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 25 (b) 28 (c) 27 (d) 26
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 27 comes from simply adding 12 + 15, but that counts Ravi twice. Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 12 + 15 − 1 = 26, so the answer is 26.

52. In a class of 40 students, Mohan ranks 8th from the top. What is his rank from the bottom?
(a) 32 (b) 33 (c) 31 (d) 34
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students subtract 8 from 40 and answer 32, forgetting that Mohan himself must be included. Rank from bottom = total − rank from top + 1 = 40 − 8 + 1 = 33, so the answer is 33.

53. In a queue, A is 10th from the front and B is 18th from the back. If they interchange their positions, A becomes 22nd from the front. How many persons are in the queue?
(a) 38 (b) 39 (c) 40 (d) 41
Answer: (b)
Solution: After interchange, A is at B's original place, so B's position is 22nd from front and 18th from back. Total = 22 + 18 − 1 = 39, so the answer is 39.
