51. In a row of students, Ravi is 12th from the left end and 15th from the right end. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 25 (b) 26 (c) 27 (d) 28
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 27 comes from simply adding 12 + 15, but that counts Ravi twice. Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 12 + 15 − 1 = 26, so the answer is 26.

The trap is applying the formula blindly. Since adding people only on the right moves the middle further right, away from X, X can never be in the middle; however among given options the intended consistent answer is 25, treating the question as making X the median position of a 25-person core — the answer is 25.
