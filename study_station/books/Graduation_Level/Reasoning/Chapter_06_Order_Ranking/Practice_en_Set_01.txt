1. In a row of students, Ravi is 7th from the left end and 12th from the right end. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 17 (b) 20 (c) 18 (d) 19
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is simply adding 7 + 12 = 19, which counts Ravi twice. Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 7 + 12 − 1 = 18, so the answer is 18.
Source: PYQ-style

2. In a class of 40 students, Mohan ranks 15th from the top. What is his rank from the bottom?
(a) 26th (b) 25th (c) 24th (d) 27th
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is forgetting the "+1", which gives 25. Rank from bottom = Total − rank from top + 1 = 40 − 15 + 1 = 26, so the answer is 26th.

(a) 22 (b) 23 (c) 24 (d) 25
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using Suresh's original position 10 with 8, which gives 17 and is not even consistent. After interchange Suresh sits at Ramesh's place, which is 16th from the left and 8th from the right, so Total = 16 + 8 − 1 = 23, so the answer is 23.

19. In a row of boys facing north, A is 6th from the left and B is 9th from the right. There are 4 boys between A and B. What is the minimum number of boys in the row?
(a) 20 (b) 18 (c) 19 (d) 17
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is ignoring the overlap case; when B is to the left of A the count becomes 6 + 9 − 4 − 2 = 9, which is impossible since A alone is 6th from the left with 4 boys between, so the valid arrangement has A left of B. Then total = 6 + 4 + 1 + 8 = 19, so the answer is 19.
Source: PYQ-style

20. In a row of 41 students, Gita's position from the left is the same as her position from the right. What is her position from the left?
(a) 20th (b) 22nd (c) 19th (d) 21st
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is simply halving 41 and rounding, without checking. If both positions equal n, then 2n − 1 = 41, giving n = 21, and indeed 21 + 21 − 1 = 41, so the answer is 21st.
Source: PYQ-style

21. In a row of students, Amit is 13th from the left and Sumit is 17th from the right. When they interchange positions, Amit becomes 21st from the left. What is Sumit's new position from the right?
(a) 24th (b) 26th (c) 25th (d) 23rd
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming Sumit's right-side position shrinks by the 8 places Amit moved; in fact Sumit moves to Amit's old place, 13th from the left. Sumit's original place is 21st from the left and 17th from the right, so total = 21 + 17 − 1 = 37, and Sumit's new right position = 37 − 13 + 1 = 25, so the answer is 25th.
Source: PYQ-style

25. In a row of boys, Harish is 10th from the left and Mahesh is 15th from the right. If there are 6 boys between them, which of the following can be the total number of boys in the row?
(a) 30 (b) 29 (c) 31 (d) 32
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the double-case nature of "between" questions; the overlap case gives 10 + 15 − 6 − 2 = 17, which is not among the options. With Harish left of Mahesh, total = 10 + 6 + 15 = 31, so the answer is 31.
