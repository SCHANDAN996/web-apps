76. In a row of students, Ravi is 12th from the left end and 18th from the right end. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 28 (b) 31 (c) 29 (d) 30
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 12 + 18 − 1 = 29. The tempting answer 30 comes from simply adding 12 and 18, which double-counts Ravi, so the answer is 29.
Source: PYQ-style

77. In a class, Priya ranks 8th from the top and 35th from the bottom. How many students are in the class?

78. In a row of 40 boys, Aman is 15th from the left. What is his position from the right?
(a) 25th (b) 26th (c) 24th (d) 27th
Answer: (b)
Solution: Position from right = Total − position from left + 1 = 40 − 15 + 1 = 26. Forgetting the +1 gives the tempting wrong answer 25, so the answer is 26th.

79. In a queue, Suresh is 10th from the front and Mahesh is 15th from the back. If there are 30 people in the queue, how many people stand between them?
(a) 4 (b) 7 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mahesh's position from front = 30 − 15 + 1 = 16. People between 10th and 16th = 16 − 10 − 1 = 5. Subtracting directly (16 − 10 = 6) counts one endpoint, so the answer is 5.

85. In a row, A is 7th from the left and B is 9th from the right. They swap positions, after which A becomes 14th from the left. What is B's new position from the right?
(a) 15th (b) 16th (c) 14th (d) 17th
Answer: (b)
Solution: After the swap, A sits at B's old seat: 14th from left and 9th from right, so total = 14 + 9 − 1 = 22. B now sits at A's old seat, 7th from left, so from right = 22 − 7 + 1 = 16. Assuming B's new right position equals A's new left position (14) is the trap, so the answer is 16th.

88. In an examination, Ashok ranked 16th from the top and 29th from the bottom among the students who passed. 6 students failed and 4 were absent. What was the total number of students enrolled?
(a) 52 (b) 54 (c) 50 (d) 56
Answer: (b)
Solution: Passed students = 16 + 29 − 1 = 44. Total enrolled = 44 + 6 + 4 = 54. Stopping at 44 or adding only the failed (50) are the traps, so the answer is 54.
