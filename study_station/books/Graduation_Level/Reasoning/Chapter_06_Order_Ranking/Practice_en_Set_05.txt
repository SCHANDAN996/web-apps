101. In a row of 40 students, Ravi is 12th from the left end. What is his position from the right end?
(a) 28th (b) 30th (c) 27th (d) 29th
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 40 − 12 = 28, which forgets that Ravi himself must be counted. Position from right = Total − Position from left + 1 = 40 − 12 + 1 = 29, so the answer is 29th.

102. In a class, Sita ranks 8th from the top and 15th from the bottom. How many students are there in the class?
(a) 21 (b) 23 (c) 24 (d) 22
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding 8 + 15 = 23, which counts Sita twice. Total = 8 + 15 − 1 = 22, so the answer is 22.

103. In a row of boys, A is 10th from the left and B is 9th from the right. If they interchange their positions, A becomes 15th from the left. How many boys are there in the row?
(a) 25 (b) 24 (c) 22 (d) 23
Answer: (d)
Solution: After interchange, A sits at B's original seat, so that seat is 15th from the left and 9th from the right. The trap is using A's old rank 10 with 9, which mixes two different seats. Total = 15 + 9 − 1 = 23, so the answer is 23.

Answer: (c)
Solution: Removing 5 candidates above her moves her up to 25 − 5 = 20th, and inserting 2 above her pushes her to 20 + 2 = 22nd. The trap is adjusting for the 3 candidates removed below her, which does not change her rank, so the answer is 22nd.
