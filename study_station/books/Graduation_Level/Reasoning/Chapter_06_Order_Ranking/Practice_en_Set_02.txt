Questions 26–50: Medium

26. In a row of students, Ravi is 12th from the left end and 18th from the right end. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 29 (b) 28 (c) 30 (d) 31
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total = position from left + position from right − 1 = 12 + 18 − 1 = 29. The trap is adding 12 + 18 = 30 without subtracting the double-counted person, so the answer is 29.
Source: PYQ-style

27. In a class of 45 students, Mohan ranks 20th from the top. What is his rank from the bottom?
(a) 24th (b) 26th (c) 25th (d) 27th
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rank from bottom = total − rank from top + 1 = 45 − 20 + 1 = 26. The tempting wrong answer 25 comes from forgetting the +1 adjustment, so the answer is 26th.
Source: PYQ-style

28. In a queue, A is 7th from the front and B is 12th from the back. If they interchange their positions, A becomes 15th from the front. How many persons are in the queue?
(a) 25 (b) 28 (c) 27 (d) 26
Answer: (d)
Solution: After interchange, A stands at B's original place, so that place is 15th from front and 12th from back. Total = 15 + 12 − 1 = 26. The trap is using A's original 7th position instead of the new 15th, so the answer is 26.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Ram is taller than Shyam but shorter than Mohan. Sohan is taller than Ram but shorter than Mohan. Who is the shortest?
(a) Ram (b) Shyam (c) Sohan (d) Mohan
Answer: (b)
Solution: The order is Mohan > Sohan > Ram > Shyam. Shyam is below Ram, and everyone else is above Ram, so the answer is Shyam.

30. In a row of 40 boys, Suresh is 8th from the left and Ramesh is 9th from the right. How many boys are there between them?
(a) 21 (b) 22 (c) 23 (d) 24
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ramesh's position from left = 40 − 9 + 1 = 32. Boys between 8th and 32nd = 32 − 8 − 1 = 23. The trap is subtracting only (32 − 8 = 24) without removing one endpoint, so the answer is 23.

31. Five friends P, Q, R, S and T have different heights. P is taller than only T. R is taller than S but shorter than Q. Who is the tallest?
(a) S (b) P (c) R (d) Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: P is taller than only T, so P is 4th and T is 5th. Among the rest, Q > R > S, making Q the tallest. The trap is assuming R is tallest because it appears in two comparisons, so the answer is Q.

33. In a row of girls facing north, Anita is 10th from the left and Kavita is 14th from the right. If there are 6 girls between them, how many girls are in the row?
(a) 30 (b) 28 (c) 29 (d) 32
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total = 10 + 14 + 6 = 30, since the two positions do not overlap (Kavita is 17th from the left, consistent with 30 − 14 + 1 = 17). The trap is subtracting the 6 girls instead of adding them, so the answer is 30.

35. In a row of 35 students, the position of the middle student from the left is:
(a) 16th (b) 17th (c) 18th (d) 19th
Answer: (c)
Solution: Middle position = (35 + 1)/2 = 18th. The trap is taking 35/2 ≈ 17.5 and rounding down to 17, so the answer is 18th.

36. A is 3 ranks above B in a class of 50. If B is 20th from the bottom, what is A's rank from the top?
(a) 29th (b) 27th (c) 30th (d) 28th
Answer: (d)
Solution: B's rank from top = 50 − 20 + 1 = 31st. A is 3 ranks above B, so A is 31 − 3 = 28th from top. The trap is adding 3 instead of subtracting, since "above" means a better (smaller) rank, so the answer is 28th.

Answer: (b)
Solution: After interchange, Amit stands at Sumit's original place, which is 18th from front and 15th from back. Total = 18 + 15 − 1 = 32. The trap is using Amit's original 10th position (10 + 15 − 1 = 24) or forgetting the −1, so the answer is 32.

41. In a row, position of A from the left is equal to position of B from the right, and there are 4 persons between them. If A is 6th from the left, how many persons are in the row?
(a) 14 (b) 17 (c) 15 (d) 16
Answer: (d)
Solution: B is 6 + 4 + 1 = 11th from the left, and B is 6th from the right. Total = 11 + 6 − 1 = 16. The trap is miscounting the gap as 5 persons (giving 17), so the answer is 16.

46. In a row of students facing south, Manoj is 11th from the left end. What will be his position from the right end if the row has 30 students?

48. In a queue, the number of persons ahead of Vinod is equal to the number of persons behind him. If Vinod is 13th from the front, how many persons are in the queue?
(a) 24 (b) 25 (c) 26 (d) 27
Answer: (b)
Solution: Persons ahead of Vinod = 12, so persons behind = 12. Total = 12 + 1 + 12 = 25. The trap is computing 13 × 2 = 26, which counts Vinod twice, so the answer is 25.
