Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Statements: All pens are books. Some books are chairs. Conclusions: I. Some pens are chairs. II. Some chairs are books.
(a) Only I follows (b) Both follow (c) Only II follows (d) Neither follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is conclusion I: since pens are inside books and some books are chairs, it feels like pens must touch chairs — but the books that are chairs may lie entirely outside the pens. Conclusion II is just the converse of "Some books are chairs", which always holds, so the answer is Only II follows.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Statements: No dog is a cat. All cats are animals. Conclusions: I. No dog is an animal. II. Some animals are not dogs.
(a) Both follow (b) Only I follows (c) Only II follows (d) Neither follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is conclusion I — "no dog is a cat" does not push dogs outside animals, because dogs could be animals that are not cats. Since all cats are animals and no cat is a dog, at least the cat-animals are not dogs, so the answer is Only II follows.
