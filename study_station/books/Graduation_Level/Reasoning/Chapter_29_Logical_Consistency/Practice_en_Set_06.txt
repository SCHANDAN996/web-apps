126. Statements: All pens are books. Some books are chairs. All chairs are tables.
Conclusions: I. Some tables are books. II. Some chairs are pens.
(a) Neither follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Only I follows
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is the claim that some chairs are pens — but "some books are chairs" does not connect chairs to pens, since pens are only a part of books. However, all chairs are tables and some books are chairs, so those books which are chairs must be tables, making Conclusion I valid. So the answer is only I follows.
