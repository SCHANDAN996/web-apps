1. Statements: All pens are books. All books are chairs. Which conclusion logically follows?
(a) All chairs are pens (b) All pens are chairs (c) Some chairs are not pens (d) No pen is a chair
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims all chairs are pens, but "all A are B" never reverses. Since pens ⊆ books ⊆ chairs, every pen is a chair, so the answer is "All pens are chairs".
Source: PYQ-style

2. Statements: Some dogs are cats. Some cats are rats. Which conclusion definitely follows?
(a) None of these definitely follows (b) All dogs are rats (c) No dog is a rat (d) Some dogs are rats
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two "some" statements with no universal link give no definite relation between dogs and rats; the tempting first option is only a possibility, not a certainty. Hence no listed conclusion definitely follows.

3. Statements: No apple is a banana. All bananas are fruits. Which conclusion follows?
(a) No fruit is an apple (b) Some fruits are not apples (c) All fruits are bananas (d) Some apples are fruits
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is concluding "no fruit is an apple", but apples could still be fruits through another path. Since all bananas are fruits and no banana is an apple, those banana-fruits are not apples, so "Some fruits are not apples" follows.
