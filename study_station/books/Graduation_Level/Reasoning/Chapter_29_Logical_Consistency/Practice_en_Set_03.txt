51. Statements: All pens are books. Some books are chairs. Conclusions: I. Some pens are chairs. II. Some chairs are books.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Neither follows
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting trap is Conclusion I — "All pens are books" plus "Some books are chairs" does not force any pen to be a chair, because the books that are chairs may lie outside the pens circle. Conclusion II is just the converse of the second statement, so it definitely follows. Hence only II follows, so the answer is Only II follows.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC/Bank syllogism pattern)
