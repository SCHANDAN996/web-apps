# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Blood Relations (Coded/Complex)

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 1: "DRAW, Don't Think!"
**Rule:** Never solve blood relations in your head — always draw the family tree.
> **"A tree on paper beats ten thoughts in the brain."**
Circle = Female, Square = Male, Double line (=) = Marriage, Single vertical line = Parent–Child, Horizontal line = Siblings.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 2: "MOM is a Circle, DAD is a Square"
**Rule:** Gender symbols.
> **"MOM's face is Round (○), DAD's wallet is Square (□)."**
Never assume gender from a name — "Kiran" or "Suman" can be either!

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 3: "P-A-S-D — Plus Adds, Slash Divides… NO! Read the CODE!"
**Rule:** In coded relations (A + B means A is the father of B), the symbol's meaning is GIVEN in the question — never assume + means "son" or "brother."
> **"The code is the king — read it twice before you swing."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 4: "Right-to-Left Ride"
**Rule:** In coded chains like P + Q − R × S, solve from RIGHT to LEFT when the question asks "How is P related to S?" — build the chain backwards from the last person.
> **"Start where the question ends, and the answer bends to you."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 5: "Only Two Generations Up"
**Rule:** Grandfather = 2 levels up, Great-grandfather = 3 levels up. Exams rarely go beyond 3 generations.
> **"Up-Up-STOP: Grandpa's roof, no higher hop."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 6: "Sister-in-Law has TWO Doors"
**Trap-breaker:** "B is A's sister-in-law" can mean:
1. A's brother's wife, OR
2. A's spouse's sister.
> **"Sister-in-law enters through TWO doors — check both before you score."**
Same for brother-in-law: spouse's brother OR sister's husband.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 7: "Maternal = Mother-side, Paternal = Papa-side"
**Rule:** Maternal uncle = Mother's brother; Paternal uncle = Father's brother.
> **"M for Mummy's side, P for Papa's side — M-P, never collide."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 8: "Cousin = Child of Uncle/Aunt — C-U-A"
**Rule:** Your parent's sibling's child = cousin. Cousins share NO fixed gender.
> **"C-U-A: Child of Uncle or Aunt — that's the cousin chant."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 9: "Pointing Photo Trick — Replace 'My' with 'I'"
**Rule:** In "Pointing to a photo, he said, 'She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son'" — replace "my" with the speaker and simplify step by step.
> **"Grandfather's only son = my FATHER. Father's daughter = my SISTER."**
> **"Kill the 'only' first — it unlocks the chain."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 10: "The ONLY Bomb 💣"
**Rule:** Words like "only son," "only daughter," "only child" collapse the tree instantly.
> **"'Only' is a bomb — it blasts away all extra branches."**
"My mother's only brother" = exactly ONE maternal uncle. "Only son of my father" = ME (if male speaker) or my brother.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 11: "Gender Check Before You Wreck"
**Rule:** After solving, verify the gender demanded by the question. If asked "How is X related as a FEMALE?" and X's gender is unknown — answer "Cannot be determined."
> **"No gender clue? Don't guess — 'Cannot be determined' is your best."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 12: "Marriage = Equal (=), Blood = Line (|)"
**Rule:** In-law relations come ONLY through the = (marriage) lines, never through blood lines.
> **"Equals bring in-laws, lines bring blood — mix them up and you're done for good."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 13: "Back-to-Me Method"
**Rule:** For "How is A related to B?" — trace from B back to A, then FLIP the relation.
> **"Walk from B to A, then turn around — son becomes father, nephew becomes uncle, upside down!"**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 14: "Two-Family Puzzle? Two Trees!"
**Rule:** In complex puzzles with two married couples, draw TWO separate trees and connect them with the marriage line.
> **"Two families, two trees — one '=' bridge, and the answer breathes."**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 15: "Niece-Nephew = Sibling's Kids"
**Rule:** Brother's or sister's son = Nephew; daughter = Niece. (Gender of the connecting sibling doesn't matter.)
> **"Sibling's Son = Nephew, Sibling's Daughter = Niece — S-S-N, S-D-N, easy as peace."**

---

# ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to SKIP a Blood Relation Question

### 🚫 Skip Tip 1: "The 20-Second Tree Rule"
If after 20 seconds you cannot place even the FIRST two people of a coded chain on paper, **mark it and move on**. Coded chains with 4+ symbols (e.g., A + B − C × D ÷ E) eat 90+ seconds — attempt them in the second round, never the first.

### 🚫 Skip Tip 2: "Gender Fog = Danger Zone"
If the question's final answer depends on a gender that the statements never fix, and "Cannot be determined" is NOT among the options — **skip immediately**. The question is either flawed or hiding a clue you'll burn 2 minutes hunting.

### 🚫 Skip Tip 3: "Three-Generation Maze with Two Marriages"
If the puzzle has 3+ generations AND 2+ marriage links AND asks a cross-family relation (e.g., "How is the wife of X's grandson related to Y's niece?"), **attempt only if it's your strong suit** — otherwise skip. One wrong branch flips the answer, and negative marking punishes brave guesses.
