Questions 51–75: Medium

51. If A + B means A is the father of B, A − B means A is the mother of B, and A × B means A is the sister of B, then in the expression P × Q + R − S, how is P related to S?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Grandmother
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is "Sister", which only covers P × Q and ignores the rest of the chain. P × Q means P is the sister of Q; Q + R means Q is the father of R; R − S means R is the mother of S. So Q and R are the parents of S, and P is the sister of S's father, so the answer is Aunt.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to the man?
(a) Cousin (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Aunt
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is "Cousin", chosen by those who assume the grandfather has more than one son. "My grandfather's only son" must be the man's own father, so the daughter of his father is his sister, so the answer is Sister.

53. If A # B means A is the mother of B, A $ B means A is the brother of B, and A * B means A is the daughter of B, then which expression shows that M is the maternal grandmother of T?
(a) M # N $ T (b) M * N # T (c) M # N # T (d) M $ N # T
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the first option, where N is the brother of T, making M the mother of T's uncle, not the grandmother. For M to be the maternal grandmother of T, M must be the mother of T's mother: M # N means M is the mother of N, and N # T means N is the mother of T, so the answer is M # N # T.
Source: PYQ-style

54. Introducing a boy, a girl said, "He is the son of the daughter of the father of my uncle." How is the boy related to the girl?
(a) Brother (b) Nephew (c) Son (d) Cousin
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "Brother", which would require the boy's mother to be the girl's own mother. The father of the girl's uncle is her grandfather; the daughter of the grandfather is the girl's aunt (father's sister); the son of that aunt is the girl's cousin, so the answer is Cousin.

55. If M × N means M is the wife of N, M + N means M is the son of N, and M ÷ N means M is the daughter of N, then in K + L × M, how is K related to M?
(a) Husband (b) Brother (c) Son (d) Father
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is "Brother", from misreading L × M as L being the husband. L × M means L is the wife of M, and K + L means K is the son of L, so K is the son of L and hence also the son of her husband M, so the answer is Son.

56. A is the brother of B. B is the daughter of C. D is the father of A. E is the wife of D. How is E related to C?
(a) Cannot be determined (b) Daughter-in-law (c) Mother (d) Sister
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming E must be C's daughter or daughter-in-law. D is the father of A, and C is a parent of B (and hence of A, since A is B's brother). So C and D are the two parents, and E is the wife of D, but the gender of C is not given, so the exact relation of E to C cannot be fixed, so the answer is Cannot be determined.

57. If X @ Y means X is the husband of Y, X & Y means X is the sister of Y, and X % Y means X is the son of Y, then in P % Q @ R & S, how is P related to S?
(a) Son (b) Nephew (c) Brother (d) Uncle
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "Son", which ignores the last link R & S. P % Q means P is the son of Q; Q @ R means Q is the husband of R; R & S means R is the sister of S. So P is the son of R (Q's wife), and R is the sister of S, making P the nephew of S, so the answer is Nephew.

59. In a family, P is the father of Q, R is the sister of Q, S is the mother of R, and T is the brother of S. How is T related to Q?
(a) Father (b) Brother (c) Grandfather (d) Uncle
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "Grandfather", from adding an extra generation. S is the mother of R and hence of Q (R is Q's sister); T is the brother of S, so T is the brother of Q's mother, i.e., Q's maternal uncle, so the answer is Uncle.

60. If A − B means A is the mother of B, A + B means A is the brother of B, and A × B means A is the father of B, then which expression means "M is the paternal uncle of P"?
(a) M + N − P (b) M + N × P (c) M − N + P (d) M × N + P
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the second option, where M becomes the father, not the uncle. For M to be the paternal uncle of P, M must be the brother of P's father: M + N means M is the brother of N, and N × P means N is the father of P, so the answer is M + N × P.

61. K is the son of L. M is the daughter of N. L is the wife of N. O is the brother of K. How is O related to M?
(a) Cousin (b) Brother (c) Uncle (d) Nephew
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "Cousin", from assuming K and M belong to different families. L is the wife of N, so L and N are a couple; K is the son of L (and N), M is the daughter of N (and L), and O is the brother of K, so O and M share the same parents, so the answer is Brother.

63. If P > Q means P is the father of Q, P < Q means P is the wife of Q, and P = Q means P is the brother of Q, then in A > B = C < D, how is A related to D?

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "Father", which stops after the first symbol. A > B means A is the father of B; B = C means B is the brother of C; C < D means C is the wife of D. So A is the father of C, and C is D's wife, making A the father-in-law of D, so the answer is Father-in-law.

69. In a family of six members A, B, C, D, E and F, there are two married couples. B is the son of A. C is the wife of A. D is the daughter of F. E is the husband of D. F is the mother of B. How is E related to A?
(a) Father (b) Son (c) Brother (d) Son-in-law
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "Son", from placing E in the children's generation without the marriage link. A and C are one couple; F is the mother of B, and B is the son of A, so F and C are the same person. D is the daughter of F (hence of A), and E is the husband of D, so E is the husband of A's daughter, so the answer is Son-in-law.
