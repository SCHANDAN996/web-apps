126. In a certain code, 'A + B' means 'A is the father of B', 'A − B' means 'A is the sister of B', 'A × B' means 'A is the mother of B', and 'A ÷ B' means 'A is the brother of B'. Which expression means 'P is the maternal uncle of R'?
(a) P ÷ Q + R (b) P × Q ÷ R (c) P ÷ Q × R (d) P + Q ÷ R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option P ÷ Q + R fails because '+' makes Q the father of R, so P would be the brother of R's father — a paternal uncle, not maternal. In P ÷ Q × R: P is the brother of Q, and Q is the mother of R, so P is the brother of R's mother, so the answer is P ÷ Q × R.

Answer: (a)
Solution: K # L means K is the son of L; L @ P means L is the wife of P; P $ Q means P is the daughter of Q. So K is the son of P, and P is Q's daughter, making K the son of Q's daughter; the trap is treating K as Q's son directly, skipping one generation. So the answer is Grandson.
