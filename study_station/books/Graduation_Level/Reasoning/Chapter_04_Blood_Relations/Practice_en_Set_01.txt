1. If A + B means A is the father of B, A − B means A is the mother of B, and A × B means A is the brother of B, then in the expression P + Q − R, how is P related to R?
(a) Father (b) Brother (c) Grandfather (d) Uncle
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is "Father," which ignores the middle step. P + Q means P is the father of Q, and Q − R means Q is the mother of R, so P is the father of R's mother, i.e., the maternal grandfather. So the answer is Grandfather.
Source: PYQ-style

2. If P × Q means P is the sister of Q, P + Q means P is the mother of Q, and P − Q means P is the father of Q, then in M + N × O, how is N related to O?
(a) Mother (b) Sister (c) Aunt (d) Daughter
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "Aunt," chosen by those who combine both steps unnecessarily. M + N means M is the mother of N, and N × O means N is the sister of O; the question asks only about N and O. So the answer is Sister.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Pointing to a photograph, Ravi said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to Ravi?

Answer: (d)
Solution: "Aunt" is a trap for those who read "grandfather's son" as someone other than the father. Grandfather's only son is Ravi's father, and the daughter of Ravi's father is Ravi's sister. So the answer is Sister.

21. If A + B means A is the mother of B, A − B means A is the brother of B, A × B means A is the father of B, and A ÷ B means A is the sister of B, then which expression shows that P is the maternal uncle of R?
(a) P − Q × R (b) P − Q + R (c) P + Q − R (d) P × Q + R
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the expression P − Q × R, which makes Q the father and gives a paternal uncle instead. Maternal uncle means brother of the mother: in P − Q + R, P is the brother of Q and Q is the mother of R. So the answer is P − Q + R.
