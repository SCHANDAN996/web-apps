101. Pointing to a photograph, Ravi said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to Ravi?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Cousin
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is "cousin", chosen by those who assume the grandfather has many sons. Grandfather's only son is Ravi's father, and his daughter is Ravi's sister, so the answer is sister.
Source: PYQ-style

102. In a code, A + B means A is the father of B; A − B means A is the mother of B; A × B means A is the brother of B. If P + Q − R, how is P related to R?
(a) Grandfather (b) Uncle (c) Father (d) Father-in-law
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Father" tempts those who stop after the first symbol. P + Q means P is the father of Q; Q − R means Q is the mother of R, so P is the father of R's mother, i.e., R's maternal grandfather. The answer is grandfather.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: "Father" tempts because Q × R puts a father symbol near R. But Q × R makes Q the father of R, and R + S makes R the brother of S, so the answer is brother.

Solution: "Cannot be determined" tempts because S's gender is unknown — but grandmother does not depend on S's gender. P is the mother of Q; Q is the father of R; R is the sister of S, so R and S share father Q; hence P is the mother of S's father, i.e., S's grandmother. The answer is grandmother.
