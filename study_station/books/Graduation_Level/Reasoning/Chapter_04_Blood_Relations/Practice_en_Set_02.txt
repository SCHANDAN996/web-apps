Questions 26–50: Medium

26. If A + B means A is the father of B, A − B means A is the mother of B, and A × B means A is the sister of B, then how is P related to S in P + Q − R × S?
(a) Uncle (b) Father (c) Grandfather (d) Brother
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option "Father" ignores the middle link Q. P + Q means P is father of Q; Q − R means Q is mother of R; R × S means R is sister of S, so Q is mother of both R and S, making P the father of S's mother, so the answer is Grandfather.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to the man?
(a) Sister (b) Mother (c) Aunt (d) Cousin
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap "Aunt" comes from misreading "only son" as the man himself. Grandfather's only son is the man's father, and the father's daughter is the man's sister, so the answer is Sister.
Source: PYQ-style

28. If P × Q means P is the brother of Q, P ÷ Q means P is the mother of Q, and P + Q means P is the father of Q, which of the following shows that M is the maternal uncle of N?
(a) M ÷ K × N (b) M × K + N (c) M × K ÷ N (d) N ÷ K × M
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap "M ÷ K × N" makes M the mother of K, not the uncle. In M × K ÷ N, M is brother of K and K is mother of N, so M is the brother of N's mother, so the answer is M × K ÷ N.
Source: PYQ-style

29. A is the brother of B. B is the mother of C. D is the father of B. How is D related to C?
(a) Grandfather (b) Father (c) Uncle (d) Brother
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap "Uncle" fits A, not D. B is the mother of C and D is the father of B, so D is the father of C's mother, so the answer is Grandfather.

30. In a family of six members P, Q, R, S, T, U: P and Q are a married couple; T is the sister of Q; R is the son of P; S is the daughter of R; U is the brother of R. How is T related to R?
(a) Mother (b) Sister (c) Grandmother (d) Aunt
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap "Sister" links T directly to R's generation. T is the sister of Q, and Q is R's mother, so T is R's maternal aunt, so the answer is Aunt.

31. If A $ B means A is the wife of B, A # B means A is the son of B, and A @ B means A is the brother of B, then in the expression T # P @ Q $ R, how is T related to R?
(a) Nephew (b) Brother-in-law (c) Son (d) Son-in-law
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap "Son" stops at the first link T # P. T is son of P; P is brother of Q; Q is wife of R, so T is the son of R's wife's brother, so the answer is Nephew.

(a) Mother (b) Grandmother (c) Aunt (d) Sister

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap "Son" confuses P with N. N is parent of O and Q; M is N's brother and P is M's sister, so P is also N's sister, and Q, son of N, is the nephew of P, so the answer is Nephew.

34. If X + Y means X is the mother of Y, X − Y means X is the brother of Y, X × Y means X is the father of Y, which expression shows that R is the maternal grandmother of T?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap "R + P × T" makes P the father, giving a paternal grandmother. In R + P + T, R is mother of P and P is mother of T, so R is the maternal grandmother of T, so the answer is R + P + T.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap "Niece" treats A as B's child instead of B's sister. A is sister of B; B is daughter of C; C is husband of D, so A and B are both daughters of C and D, making A the daughter of D, so the answer is Daughter.

43. If M % N means M is the mother of N, M & N means M is the father of N, and M = N means M is the sister of N, which expression shows that W is the daughter of Z?
(a) Z & X = W (b) W = X % Z (c) W % X & Z (d) X = W & Z
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap "W = X % Z" makes X the mother of Z, reversing the generations. In Z & X = W, Z is father of X and X is sister of W, so W is also a child of Z, so the answer is Z & X = W.
