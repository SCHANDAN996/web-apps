101. You are a Block Development Officer. A contractor who completed a village road project requests early release of payment, but the mandatory quality inspection report is still pending. What should you do?
(a) Hold the payment until the quality inspection report is received (b) Release half the payment as a goodwill gesture (c) Release the payment since the work appears complete (d) Ask the contractor to get a written request from villagers
Answer: (a)
Solution: Releasing payment without the mandatory inspection violates procedure and could make you accountable for substandard work; partial payment is equally irregular. Rules require the quality report before any disbursement, so the answer is to hold the payment until the inspection report is received.

(a) Ask him to bring a guarantor and then allow it (b) Allow the overdraft because he is a trusted customer (c) Allow it for one week only (d) Refuse politely and suggest legitimate alternatives like a personal loan
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trust cannot override banking regulations; any informal exception risks audit action against you. The professional response is to refuse within rules while helping through permitted channels, so the answer is to refuse politely and suggest legitimate alternatives.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting options of investigation and waiting for seniors delay life-saving help; saving life takes precedence over all other duties. Investigation can follow once the victim is safe, so the answer is to take the injured man to hospital immediately.
