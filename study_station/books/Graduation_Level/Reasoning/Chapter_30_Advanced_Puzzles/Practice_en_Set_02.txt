Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a row facing north. P sits third to the left of S. S sits at the right end. Q sits at one of the extreme ends. T sits immediately to the right of P. U does not sit adjacent to Q. Who sits second to the right of R?
(a) P (b) S (c) T (d) U
Answer: (c)
Solution: S is at seat 6, so P is at seat 3 and T at seat 4, and Q takes the free end, seat 1. U cannot be next to Q at seat 2, so U is at seat 5 and R at seat 2. The trap is P, who is only one seat to R's right, so the answer is T.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Five boxes A, B, C, D and E are stacked one above another. Box C is immediately above box A. Box D is above box B but not at the top. Box E is at the bottom. Which box is at the top?
(a) C (b) B (c) D (d) A
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many pick D because "D is above B" sounds like the highest clue, but D is explicitly not at the top. E is at the bottom. The A–C pair must occupy two consecutive slots; the only arrangement that also lets D sit above B without being on top is B at 2, D at 3, A at 4 and C at 5. The topmost box is C, so the answer is C.
Source: PYQ-style

28. Five meetings on subjects Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology and English are held from Monday to Friday, one each day. Maths is on Wednesday. Physics is immediately before Maths. English is on Friday. Biology is not on Monday. Which subject is on Monday?
(a) Biology (b) Chemistry (c) English (d) Physics
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Biology, picked by those who forget its restriction and place it on the only free early day. Physics is Tuesday (immediately before Maths on Wednesday) and English is Friday. Biology cannot be on Monday, so Biology is Thursday and Monday is left for Chemistry, so the answer is Chemistry.
Source: PYQ-style

29. In a family, P is the son of Q. Q is the daughter of R. R is the wife of S. T is the son of S. How is T related to P?
(a) Father (b) Maternal uncle (c) Brother (d) Grandfather
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Brother, chosen because T and Q are children of the same parents and solvers equate T with P's generation. Q is P's mother; T is the son of R and S, hence Q's brother. A mother's brother is a maternal uncle, so the answer is Maternal uncle.
Source: PYQ-style

30. Eight friends A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits second to the right of B. C sits opposite A. F sits second to the left of C. Who sits opposite B?
(a) C (b) A (c) F (d) E
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is C, because "opposite" appears in the clue about C and solvers stop there. Fix B at seat 1; A second to the right of B is seat 7, so C opposite A is seat 3. F second to the left of C is seat 5, and seat 5 is exactly opposite seat 1, so the answer is F.
Source: PYQ-style

31. Five students scored different marks. Ravi scored more than Kiran but less than Mohan. Sohan scored more than Mohan. Tina scored less than Kiran. Who scored the second highest?
(a) Ravi (b) Mohan (c) Sohan (d) Kiran
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many mark Sohan, confusing "second highest" with "highest". The chain is: Sohan > Mohan > Ravi > Kiran > Tina. Highest is Sohan; second highest is Mohan, so the answer is Mohan.

32. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F are of different heights. C is taller than A but shorter than B. D is taller than B. F is shorter than E but taller than D. A is taller than nobody. Who is the second shortest?
(a) E (b) F (c) B (d) C
Answer: (d)
Solution: The order from tallest is E, F, D, B, C, A. The trap is B, who is third from the bottom, so the second shortest is C.

33. In a row of students facing north, Asha is 8th from the left and 12th from the right. How many students are in the row?
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 21 (d) 19
Answer: (d)
Solution: The common trap is adding 8 + 12 = 20, which counts Asha twice. Total = left position + right position − 1 = 8 + 12 − 1 = 19, so the answer is 19.
Source: PYQ-style

34. Four friends W, X, Y and Z like four different colours — red, blue, green and yellow. W likes red. X does not like blue. Y likes green. Which colour does X like?
(a) Blue (b) Yellow (c) Green (d) Red
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Blue, left over if you assign colours without applying X's restriction. W takes red and Y takes green, leaving blue and yellow for X and Z. X does not like blue, so X takes yellow and Z takes blue, so the answer is Yellow.
Source: PYQ-style

35. Six employees J, K, L, M, N and O work on six different floors (1 to 6, 1 being lowest). K works on floor 4. L works immediately above M. N works on an odd floor below K. O works above K but not on the top floor. On which floor does J work?
(a) 6 (b) 2 (c) 5 (d) 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is floor 5, taken by J if you misplace O on the top floor. K is on 4; O is above 4 but not on 6, so O is on 5. N is on an odd floor below 4, so N is on 1 or 3; the L–M consecutive pair fills the other two of floors 1, 2 and 3 either way. Floor 6 remains for J, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

36. Pointing to a photograph, Ramesh said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman in the photograph related to Ramesh?
(a) Cousin (b) Mother (c) Sister (d) Aunt
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Cousin, chosen by those who think the grandfather could have more sons. "My grandfather's only son" is Ramesh's father. The daughter of Ramesh's father is Ramesh's sister, so the answer is Sister.
Source: PYQ-style

37. Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing south. A sits at one extreme end. B sits second to the right of A. C sits immediately to the left of B. D sits at the other extreme end. Who sits in the middle of the row?
(a) A (b) C (c) E (d) B
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is C, placed in the middle by those who forget that facing south reverses left and right. Facing south, a person's right is towards the viewer's left, so A must be at the viewer's right end for B to fit second to A's right. That puts B at the viewer's position 3, C at position 4, D at position 1 and E at position 2. Position 3 is the middle, so the answer is B.
Source: PYQ-style

38. Five cars P, Q, R, S and T are parked in a row facing east. Car P is at the leftmost end. Car Q is immediately to the right of car P. Car R is at the rightmost end. Car S is to the left of car T. Which car is in the middle?
(a) Q (b) S (c) T (d) R
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is T, chosen by reading "S is to the left of T" and pushing T to the centre. Order from left: P at 1, Q at 2, then S left of T gives S at 3 and T at 4, and R at 5. The middle position is 3, so the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style

39. Six persons have birthdays in six different months of the same year: January, March, May, July, September and November. A's birthday is in July. B's birthday is before A's but not in January. C's birthday is immediately after B's in the given list of months. D's birthday is in November. E's birthday is after A's. Whose birthday is in January?
(a) F (b) E (c) C (d) B
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is B, since B is "before A" and January is the earliest month — but B is explicitly not in January. B before July and not January means B is March or May; C immediately after B in the list forces B = March and C = May, since May would push C into July, already taken by A. E after July with D in November gives E = September. January remains for F, so the answer is F.
Source: PYQ-style

40. A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the son of D. D is the wife of E. E is the son of F. How is A related to F?
(a) Brother (b) Son (c) Nephew (d) Grandson
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Son, picked by collapsing one generation. A, B and C are siblings; C is the son of D and E, so A is also a child of D and E. E is the son of F, making F the grandparent of A. A is male, so the answer is Grandson.
Source: PYQ-style

41. Six boxes are stacked one above another. The box with books is third from the bottom. The box with pens is immediately above the box with books. The box with files is at the top. The box with toys is at the bottom. Which position from the bottom is the box with pens?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 3, which is the position of the books, not the pens. Books are 3rd from the bottom; pens are immediately above books, so pens are 4th from the bottom. Files at the top and toys at the bottom do not disturb this, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

42. In a queue of 40 people, Ritu is 7th from the front and Sita is 15th from the back. How many people stand between Ritu and Sita?
(a) 17 (b) 19 (c) 18 (d) 16
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 40 − 7 − 15 = 18 and then subtracting 1 more to get 17. Sita's position from the front is 40 − 15 + 1 = 26. People between positions 7 and 26 = 26 − 7 − 1 = 18, so the answer is 18.
Source: PYQ-style

43. Five teams A, B, C, D and E played matches on five consecutive days, Monday to Friday, one each day. Team A played on Monday. Team D played immediately after Team A. Team B played two days after Team C. On which day did Team C play?
(a) Friday (b) Wednesday (c) Thursday (d) Monday
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Thursday, obtained by placing B two days after A instead of after C. A is Monday and D is Tuesday, leaving Wednesday, Thursday and Friday for B, C and E. The only pair exactly two days apart among these is Wednesday and Friday, so C is Wednesday and B is Friday, with E on Thursday, so the answer is Wednesday.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Six friends sit in two rows of three each; the north row faces south and the south row faces north, so each person faces exactly one person in the other row. P sits in the north row and faces Q. R sits to the immediate right of Q. T sits to the immediate left of P. Who faces R?
(a) T (b) S (c) P (d) Q
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is P, chosen because P faces Q and R sits next to Q, but facing pairs are column-wise, not diagonal. Q faces north, so Q's right is the viewer's right; R is one column to the viewer's right of Q. P faces south, so P's left is also the viewer's right; T is one column to the viewer's right of P. T and R are in the same column, so the answer is T.
Source: PYQ-style

45. In a family, X is the mother of Y. Y is the sister of Z. Z is the father of W. V is the son of W. How is X related to V?
(a) Mother (b) Aunt (c) Grandmother (d) Great grandmother
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Grandmother, counting only two generations down from X. X is the parent of Y and Z; Z is the parent of W; W is the parent of V. X is three generations above V, so the answer is Great grandmother.

46. Seven people earn different salaries. G earns more than H but less than I. J earns more than K but less than H. L earns the highest. M earns more than I. Who earns the second lowest?
(a) H (b) K (c) J (d) G
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is H, picked by reading the chain bottom-up incorrectly. The full chain is L > M > I > G > H > J > K. Lowest is K; second lowest is J, so the answer is J.

47. A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 3 km west (b) 5 km north (c) 8 km east (d) 3 km east
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 8 km east, from adding the two right-turn legs. North 5 km, then east 3 km, then south 5 km; the south leg exactly cancels the north leg. Net displacement is 3 km east, so the answer is 3 km east.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Six books of different subjects are arranged on a shelf. Physics is to the immediate left of Chemistry. Maths is at the rightmost position. Biology is to the left of Physics. English is between Biology and Physics. Hindi is at the leftmost position. Which book is third from the left?
(a) English (b) Chemistry (c) Physics (d) Biology
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Biology, placed second and then miscounted as third. The order is: Hindi at 1, Biology at 2, English at 3 (between Biology and Physics), Physics at 4, Chemistry at 5, Maths at 6. Third from the left is English, so the answer is English.

49. Four friends Anil, Bina, Chitra and Deepa each like a different fruit among mango, apple, banana and orange. Anil likes mango. Bina does not like apple or banana. Chitra likes apple. Which fruit does Bina like?
(a) Orange (b) Apple (c) Mango (d) Banana
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Banana, assumed as the leftover without applying Bina's restriction. Anil takes mango and Chitra takes apple, leaving banana and orange for Bina and Deepa. Bina dislikes banana, so Bina takes orange and Deepa takes banana, so the answer is Orange.

50. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. A sits fourth from the left. B sits at the right extreme end. C sits second to the left of A. D sits immediately to the left of B. F sits immediately to the right of C. Who sits at the left extreme end?
(a) F (b) D (c) E (d) C
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is F, guessed as the leftover without fixing every position. A is at position 4, B at 6, C second to the left of A at 2, D immediately left of B at 5, and F immediately right of C at 3. Only position 1 remains, which goes to E, so the answer is E.
