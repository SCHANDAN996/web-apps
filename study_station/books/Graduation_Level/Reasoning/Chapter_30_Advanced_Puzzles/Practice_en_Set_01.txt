1. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. A sits at one of the extreme ends. B sits second to the right of A. C sits to the immediate right of B. E sits to the immediate left of F. Who sits at the rightmost end?
(a) F (b) D (c) E (d) C
Answer: (a)
Solution: A cannot be at the right end, since B is to A's right, so A is at seat 1, B at seat 3 and C at seat 4. Seats 2, 5 and 6 are left, and only 5 and 6 are side by side, so E is at seat 5 and F at seat 6, with D at seat 2. The trap is D, who is fixed at the left side, so the answer is F.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Five boxes P, Q, R, S and T are stacked one above another. P is immediately above Q. R is above P but not at the top. T is at the bottom. Which box is at the top?
(a) S (b) P (c) R (d) Q
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students wrongly pick R because it is "above P", but the condition "not at the top" rules it out. From the bottom the order is T, Q, P, R, and the only remaining box S must occupy the top, so the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style

3. In a family, M is the father of N. N is the sister of O. O is the son of R. Q is the daughter of R. R is the wife of M. How is Q related to N?
(a) Sister (b) Cousin (c) Aunt (d) Mother
Answer: (a)
Solution: M and R are husband and wife, and N, O and Q are all their children. The trap is "Cousin", which needs different parents; Q and N share both parents, so the answer is Sister.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Seven lectures on subjects Hindi, English, Maths, Science, History, Geography and Art are held from Monday to Sunday, one each day. Maths is on Wednesday. English is immediately before Maths. History is on Friday. Hindi is immediately after History. Geography is on Sunday. Science is on Thursday. On which day is Art held?
(a) Tuesday (b) Thursday (c) Monday (d) Saturday
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Thursday, but Science is fixed there. English is on Tuesday, Maths on Wednesday, History on Friday, Hindi on Saturday and Geography on Sunday, leaving only Monday for Art, so the answer is Monday.

5. A, B, C, D and E are five friends of different heights. A is taller than B but shorter than C. D is taller than C. E is shorter than B. Who is the shortest?
(a) B (b) A (c) E (d) D
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is B, but E is explicitly shorter than B. The full chain is D > C > A > B > E, so the answer is E.

6. Six people P, Q, R, S, T and U sit around a circular table facing the centre. P sits opposite S. Q sits to the immediate right of P. R sits between S and T. Who sits opposite Q?
(a) R (b) U (c) T (d) S
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is T, which sits next to R, not opposite Q. Place P and S opposite each other; Q takes the seat immediately right of P, and R between S and T forces R into the seat facing Q, with T and U filling the last two seats, so the answer is R.
Source: PYQ-style

7. Five cities Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata and Jaipur are visited by a salesman in some order. He visits Delhi before Mumbai. He visits Chennai immediately after Kolkata. Jaipur is visited last. Mumbai is visited before Kolkata. Which city is visited first?
(a) Mumbai (b) Kolkata (c) Delhi (d) Chennai
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Mumbai, which is early but not first. Jaipur is last; Delhi before Mumbai, Mumbai before Kolkata, and Chennai immediately after Kolkata give the order Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Jaipur, so the answer is Delhi.
Source: PYQ-style

8. Four girls W, X, Y and Z like four different colours — red, blue, green and yellow. W does not like red, blue or yellow. X likes blue. Y likes red. Which colour does Z like?
(a) Green (b) Blue (c) Red (d) Yellow
Answer: (d)
Solution: W can only like green, X likes blue and Y likes red. The trap is green, which is already W's, so the only colour left for Z is yellow.

9. Eight persons sit in two rows of four facing each other. Row 1 (facing south): A, B, C, D. Row 2 (facing north): E, F, G, H. A faces E. B sits to the right of A. F sits to the left of E. G faces C. Who faces D?
(a) F (b) H (c) E (d) G
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is F, but F sits opposite B. Since row 1 faces south, B being to A's right places the row as D, C, B, A from the viewer's left; A faces E, G faces C, F is left of E, leaving H opposite D, so the answer is H.

12. P, Q, R, S, T and U are seated in a row facing north. P is at the extreme left. Q is second to the right of P. R is at the extreme right. S sits exactly between Q and T. Who is to the immediate left of R?
(a) S (b) T (c) Q (d) U
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is S, but S is two seats away from R. P is at position 1, Q at 3, R at 6; S exactly between Q and T gives S at 4 and T at 5, with U at 2, so the person immediately left of R is T, so the answer is T.

(a) Orange (b) Banana (c) Grapes (d) Apple
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bina can only like orange. Deepak cannot have grapes, and mango, apple and orange are taken, so Deepak likes banana. The trap is orange, which is Bina's, so Esha likes grapes.

14. Seven persons A to G are standing in a queue. A is behind B. C is in front of B. D is behind A but in front of E. F is between C and B. G is at the back. Who is at the front?
(a) F (b) A (c) B (d) C
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is F, who is second, not first. C is in front of B with F between them, and A, D, E, G follow behind B in that order, giving C, F, B, A, D, E, G, so the answer is C.

15. Five houses in a row are coloured white, black, red, blue and green. The blue house is at the left end and the black house is at the right end. The red house is immediately to the right of the white house. The green house is immediately to the left of the black house. Which colour is the middle house?
(a) Green (b) Red (c) White (d) Black
Answer: (b)
Solution: Blue is at position 1 and black at position 5, so green is at position 4. White and red must fill positions 2 and 3 in that order. The trap is white, which is second, so the middle house is red.

16. Six employees work on six different floors (1 to 6). K works on floor 4. L works immediately above K. M works on floor 1. N works above O but below L. On which floor does P work?
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is floor 5, but L works there. M is on 1, K on 4, L on 5; N above O and below L forces O on 2 and N on 3, leaving floor 6 for P, so the answer is 6.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is X, who sits fourth, not in the middle. V must be at the left end for W to sit second to its right, so V is at 1 and W at 3; X immediately left of Y forces X at 4 and Y at 5, leaving Z at 2, so the middle seat goes to W, so the answer is W.

20. Six friends P, Q, R, S, T and U have birthdays in different months: January, March, April, June, August and October. P's birthday is in June. Q's is immediately before P's. R's is in January. S's is after T's but before U's. In which month is S's birthday?
(a) April (b) August (c) October (d) March
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is March, which is T's month. R–January, Q–April (immediately before June) and P–June are fixed; the remaining months March, August, October go to T, S, U in that order, so the answer is August.

21. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F live on floors 1 to 6 of a building, one on each floor. A lives on floor 3. B lives immediately above A. C lives on floor 6. D lives below A but not on floor 1. E lives immediately below C. On which floor does F live?
(a) 1 (b) 5 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is floor 5, but E lives there, immediately below C. A is on 3, B on 4, C on 6, D must be on 2 since it is below A and not on floor 1, and E is on 5, leaving only floor 1 for F, so the answer is 1.

22. Five couples (husbands A, B, C, D, E and wives P, Q, R, S, T) attend a party. A is married to R. B is not married to P or Q. C is married to T. D is married to P. Who is E married to?
(a) Q (b) P (c) S (d) R

23. Seven boxes contain different items: pen, book, key, watch, ring, coin and card. The pen box is immediately above the book box. The key box is at the top. The watch box is above the ring box. The coin box is immediately below the book box. The card box is at the bottom. The ring box is third from the top. Which item is in the box second from the bottom?
(a) Book (b) Watch (c) Coin (d) Ring
Answer: (c)
Solution: From the top, key is first and ring third, so watch, being above ring, is second. Pen, book and coin must be three boxes in a row, which fit only at positions 4, 5 and 6, and card is seventh. The trap is book, which is third from the bottom, so the answer is coin.

24. Six persons M, N, O, P, Q and R are of different weights. M is heavier than N but lighter than O. P is heavier than O. Q is lighter than N. R is heavier than P. Who is the second heaviest?
(a) P (b) O (c) R (d) M
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is O, which is third; students who misread "heavier than O" as the top often pick O. The chain is R > P > O > M > N > Q, making P the second heaviest, so the answer is P.

25. Five friends Ajay, Vijay, Sanjay, Dhananjay and Abhay study in different classes: 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th and 10th. Ajay is in a higher class than Vijay but lower than Sanjay. Dhananjay is in 10th. Abhay is in a lower class than Vijay. In which class is Ajay?
(a) 7th (b) 6th (c) 9th (d) 8th
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 9th, which is Sanjay's class; note Sanjay is higher than Ajay. The order Sanjay > Ajay > Vijay > Abhay fills classes 9th, 8th, 7th and 6th below Dhananjay's 10th, so the answer is 8th.
