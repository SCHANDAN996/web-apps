76. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a row facing north. P sits third to the left of S. Q sits at one of the extreme ends. T sits immediately to the right of P. R is not adjacent to S. Who sits second to the right of U?
(a) P (b) T (c) Q (d) R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is T, because T sits immediately right of P and feels central. P third to the left of S with T right of P fixes P at position 2, T at 3 and S at 5. R cannot sit at 4 or 6 (both adjacent to S), so R is at 1; Q must then take the extreme end at 6, leaving U at 4. Second to the right of U is position 6, so the answer is Q.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Five boxes A, B, C, D and E are stacked one above another. A is immediately above C. B is above E but not at the top. D is below C. Which box is at the bottom?
(a) A (b) C (c) D (d) E
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is D, since "D is below C" sounds like D sinks to the bottom. A–C–D form a consecutive block; if the block sits at positions 2–4, the top must be E, but then B cannot be above E, and at positions 3–5 the same problem occurs. So the block is at the top: A, C, D, then B above E at positions 4 and 5, so the answer is E.

78. In a family of six members A, B, C, D, E and F, there are two married couples. A is the father of C. D is the daughter of B. E is the wife of C. F is the sister of C. How is B related to E?
(a) Mother (b) Mother-in-law (c) Sister (d) Grandmother
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Mother, because solvers stop at "B is C's mother" and forget the question asks about E. The two couples are A–B and C–E, so B is the mother of C, who is E's husband. The husband's mother is the mother-in-law, so the answer is Mother-in-law.

79. Seven lectures on subjects M, N, O, P, Q, R and S are held from Monday to Sunday. O is on Wednesday. M is held immediately before O. P is held after S but before R. N is held on neither Monday nor Sunday. Q is held on Sunday. On which day is P held?
(a) Saturday (b) Thursday (c) Friday (d) Monday
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Saturday, since "after S but before R" feels like P is pushed late in the week. M is on Tuesday, O on Wednesday and Q on Sunday, leaving Monday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday. The chain S–P–R needs three of these days in order; placing S on Thursday forces N onto Monday, which is barred, so S is on Monday, N on Thursday, P on Friday and R on Saturday, so the answer is Friday.

80. Eight friends sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits second to the left of B. C sits opposite A. D is an immediate neighbour of both B and C. E sits third to the right of C. Who sits opposite D?
(a) A (b) E (c) B (d) C
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is A, because A is defined relative to B just as the answer is defined relative to D. Number seats 1–8 clockwise with B at seat 1; second to the left (anticlockwise) puts A at seat 7, so C is opposite at seat 3. The only seat touching both B and C is seat 2, which is D. Third to the right (clockwise) of C is seat 6, which is E, and seat 6 is opposite seat 2, so the answer is E.

Solution: The trap is floor 4, because "M above N but below O" sounds like a high-floor chain. Floors 2, 3 and 6 remain for N, M and O in ascending order, placing M on floor 3, so the answer is 3.

100. In a coded statement puzzle: "All teachers are readers. Some readers are writers. No writer is a singer." Which conclusion definitely follows?
(a) Some teachers are writers (b) Some readers are not singers (c) All readers are teachers (d) No teacher is a singer
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "No teacher is a singer", because chaining the premises feels like it should connect teachers to singers — but no premise links them. "Some teachers are writers" fails since the writers may lie outside the teachers, and "All readers are teachers" reverses the first premise. Since some readers are writers and no writer is a singer, those readers are definitely not singers, so the answer is Some readers are not singers.
