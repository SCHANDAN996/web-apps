101. Six persons K, L, M, N, O and P sit in a row facing north. K sits at the extreme right end. L sits second to the left of K. M sits immediately to the left of L. N sits at the extreme left end. O sits between N and M. Who sits immediately to the right of O?
(a) P (b) L (c) M (d) N
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is L, who is placed near the middle and feels like O's neighbour. Fixing K at seat 6 gives L at seat 4 and M at seat 3; N takes seat 1, O sits between N and M at seat 2, and P fills seat 5. The seat immediately right of O (seat 2) is seat 3, which is M, so the answer is M.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Five boxes A, B, C, D and E are stacked one above another. Box C is immediately above box A. Box D is below box A but not at the bottom. Box B is above box C. Which box is at the bottom?
(a) D (b) E (c) A (d) B
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many pick D because "D is below A" sounds like the lowest — but D is explicitly not at the bottom. The order from top is B, C, A, D, E, so the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

103. In a family of six members A, B, C, D, E and F, there are two married couples. A is a doctor and is married to D who is a teacher. F, the engineer, is the brother of B. C is the granddaughter of D. E is the son of A. How is C related to E?
(a) Daughter (b) Niece (c) Sister (d) Cousin
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "niece", assuming C descends through some other branch. Since F is the brother of B, they cannot be a couple, so the second married couple must be E and B; C, the granddaughter of D, is then the child of E and B. C is therefore E's daughter, so the answer is Daughter.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Seven lectures on subjects M, N, O, P, Q, R and S are held from Monday to Sunday. O is on Wednesday. M is held immediately before O. P is held after S but before R. N is held on Sunday. Q is held before S. Which subject is held on Friday?
(a) P (b) S (c) Q (d) R
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is S, since "P after S" makes S feel like the Friday lecture. M is on Tuesday (immediately before O on Wednesday) and N is on Sunday, leaving Monday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday for Q, S, P, R in the fixed chain Q before S before P before R. That gives Q on Monday, S on Thursday, P on Friday and R on Saturday, so the answer is P.
Source: PYQ-style

105. Eight friends A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits opposite B. C sits opposite D. E sits between A and C. F sits between C and B. G sits between B and D. Who sits opposite G?
(a) E (b) A (c) C (d) H
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is C, since G is placed using B and D and C is also opposite D. Number seats 1 to 8 with A at seat 1; then B is at seat 5, C at seat 3 and D at seat 7. E sits between A and C at seat 2, F between C and B at seat 4, and G between B and D at seat 6, leaving H at seat 8. The seat opposite G (seat 6) is seat 2, which is E, so the answer is E.

106. Five persons A, B, C, D and E have different heights. A is taller than B but shorter than C. D is taller than C. E is shorter than B. Who is the second tallest?
(a) D (b) C (c) A (d) B
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is D, the tallest, when the question asks for the second tallest. The order is D > C > A > B > E, so the answer is C.

107. Six cities P, Q, R, S, T and U must each be visited once. P must be visited before Q and R. S must be visited immediately after T. U must be visited last. Which of the following is a valid order?
(a) P T S Q R U (b) P Q T R S U (c) T S P R U Q (d) T P S Q R U
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the first listed order, where T and S are split by P, breaking the "S immediately after T" rule; the second puts S after R instead of right after T, and the third does not end with U. Only the last order keeps P before Q and R, S immediately after T, and U last, so the answer is P T S Q R U.
Source: PYQ-style

108. In a row of students, Ravi is 12th from the left and 15th from the right. If 5 more students join the row at the right end, what is Ravi's new position from the right?
(a) 12th (b) 20th (c) 17th (d) 15th
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 15th — students joining at the right end do not change Ravi's left-side position, but they add 5 to his rank counted from the right. New position from right = 15 + 5 = 20, so the answer is 20th.
Source: PYQ-style

109. A, B, C, D, E and F are seated in two rows of three facing each other. A faces D. B is to the left of A. E faces B. C is not adjacent to A. Who faces C?
(a) E (b) D (c) F (d) B
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is D, since A faces D and C seems near A. Row 1 must be B, A, C (B left of A, and C not adjacent to A forces C to the far end). Row 2 has E facing B and D facing A, leaving F to face C, so the answer is F.

110. Five friends P, Q, R, S and T like five different colours — red, blue, green, yellow and black. P likes red. R likes blue. T likes yellow. Q does not like green. Which colour does S like?
(a) Black (b) Blue (c) Yellow (d) Green
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is black, which looks free but is actually forced on Q. Red, blue and yellow are taken by P, R and T; Q cannot take green, so Q takes black, leaving only green for S, so the answer is Green.

(a) July (b) February (c) April (d) June
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is July, which C takes as the 31-day month after May. Fixing A = March, B = February, C = July, D = May, E = January and F = April leaves only June for G, so the answer is June.

113. Four couples sit around a circular table. Each husband sits opposite his wife. No two women sit adjacent. If Mr. A sits between Mrs. C and Mrs. D, who sits opposite Mr. A?

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Mrs. C or Mrs. D, since they are named next to Mr. A — but neighbours are irrelevant here. The rule states each husband sits opposite his own wife, so the answer is Mrs. A.

115. A building has six floors numbered 1 (lowest) to 6 (highest). Six persons J, K, L, M, N and O live one per floor. K lives on floor 4. J lives immediately above K. L lives above K. N lives on floor 1. M lives immediately above N. On which floor does M live?
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is floor 3, assuming M sits just below J. J is on floor 5, L must take floor 6 (the only floor above K left), N is on floor 1, and M immediately above N puts M on floor 2, leaving O on floor 3, so the answer is 2.

116. Five teams A, B, C, D and E played matches. A won more matches than B. C won fewer than D but more than E. B won more than D. Which team won the fewest matches?
(a) C (b) E (c) D (d) B
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is D, since C won fewer than D and D feels low. The chain is A > B > D > C > E, so the fewest wins belong to E, so the answer is E.

118. In a queue of 40 people, Asha is 8th from the front and Beena is 12th from the back. How many people stand between Asha and Beena?
(a) 22 (b) 19 (c) 18 (d) 20
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 22, obtained by wrongly adding or subtracting the two given ranks directly. Beena is 12th from the back, i.e. 40 − 12 + 1 = 29th from the front; people between the 8th and 29th positions = 29 − 8 − 1 = 20, so the answer is 20.
Source: PYQ-style

119. Six friends R, S, T, U, V and W go to six different cities — Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Jaipur and Pune. R goes to Delhi. T goes to Kolkata. V goes to Jaipur. S does not go to Mumbai or Chennai. U goes to Mumbai. Where does S go?
(a) Pune (b) Delhi (c) Mumbai (d) Chennai
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Chennai, listed as an option but explicitly rejected by S. Delhi, Kolkata, Jaipur and Mumbai are taken by R, T, V and U; S cannot take Chennai either, leaving only Pune, so the answer is Pune.

120. A committee of 4 must be formed from 3 men (X, Y, Z) and 3 women (L, M, N) with these conditions: Z must be included; X and L cannot be together; at least two women must be included; if Y is selected, M must be selected. Which committee is valid?
(a) X, Y, Z, M (b) Y, Z, L, N (c) X, Z, L, M (d) X, Z, M, N
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the second listed committee, which looks balanced but selects Y without M, breaking the Y–M rule; the third pairs X with L, and the fourth has only one woman. The committee X, Z, M, N includes Z, avoids the X–L clash, has two women, and needs no M-condition since Y is absent, so the answer is X, Z, M, N.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Six events A, B, C, D, E and F occur in sequence. A occurs before B and C. D occurs after C but before E. F occurs last. B occurs before D. Which event occurs third?
(a) Cannot be uniquely determined (b) B (c) C (d) D
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is picking B or C by assuming one fixed order. Both A B C D E F and A C B D E F satisfy every condition, and the third position differs between them, so the answer is Cannot be uniquely determined.

(a) R (b) U (c) S (d) Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is S, since R and U are both defined around S. The full chain is V > T > P > Q > R > S > U, so the shortest is U, so the answer is U.

125. Eight diplomats from countries A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H sit at a round table facing the centre. A sits opposite B. C sits second to the right of A. F sits opposite C. D sits to the immediate right of B. E sits to the immediate left of A. G does not sit adjacent to A. Who sits to the immediate left of B?
(a) D (b) G (c) F (d) H
Answer: (b)
Solution: Number the seats clockwise with A at seat 0; facing the centre, left is clockwise. B is at 4, C at 6, F at 2, D at 3 and E at 1. Seats 5 and 7 remain, and seat 7 touches A, so G is at seat 5. The trap is D, who is on B's right, so the answer is G.
