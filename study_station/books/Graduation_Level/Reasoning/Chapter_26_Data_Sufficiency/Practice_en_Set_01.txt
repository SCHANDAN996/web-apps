Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Note for all questions: Each question is followed by two statements, I and II. Decide whether the data given in the statements are sufficient to answer the question.

1. What is the value of x?
I. 2x + 6 = 20
II. x² = 49
(a) Both statements together are needed (b) Statement II alone is sufficient (c) Statement I alone is sufficient (d) Either statement alone is sufficient
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming x² = 49 fixes x uniquely; it gives x = +7 or x = −7, two values, so Statement II is not sufficient. Statement I gives 2x = 14, so x = 7, so the answer is Statement I alone is sufficient.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Is the integer n even?
I. n + 4 is even
II. 3n is even
(a) Statement I alone is sufficient (b) Statement II alone is sufficient (c) Either statement alone is sufficient (d) Both statements together are needed
Answer: (c)
Solution: From Statement I, n + 4 even means n is even, since even minus even is even. From Statement II, 3n even with 3 odd forces n to be even. Each statement alone answers the question, so the answer is either statement alone is sufficient.
Source: PYQ-style

3. What is the age of Ravi?
I. Ravi is 5 years older than Mohan.
II. Mohan is 20 years old.
(a) Both statements together are needed (b) Statement II alone is sufficient (c) Statement I alone is sufficient (d) Either statement alone is sufficient
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is using Statement II alone, which gives Mohan's age but says nothing about Ravi. Statement I alone gives only a relation, not an actual age. Combining them: Ravi = 20 + 5 = 25 years, so the answer is both statements together are needed.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is trusting Statement I, but digit-sum 9 fits many numbers (18, 27, 36, 45, 54), so it is not sufficient. Statement II directly gives the number 45, so the answer is Statement II alone is sufficient.

7. Is x greater than y?
I. x − y = 5

Answer: (b)

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Statement I alone, where "sky" could be "ta" or "na". Comparing the two statements, "blue" is common, so "blue" = "ta" and hence "sky" = "na", so the answer is both statements together are needed.

(a) Both statements together are needed (b) Statement I alone is sufficient (c) Statement II alone is sufficient (d) Either statement alone is sufficient
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Statement II, which gives no reference day at all, so nothing can be counted from it. From Statement I, 26 January is 25 days after Sunday, and 25 mod 7 = 4, giving Thursday, so the answer is Statement I alone is sufficient.

14. What is the area of a rectangle?
I. Its perimeter is 40 cm.
II. Its length is twice its breadth.
(a) Both statements together are needed (b) Statement II alone is sufficient (c) Statement I alone is sufficient (d) Either statement alone is sufficient
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Statement I alone: l + b = 20 fits many rectangles with different areas, and the ratio in Statement II alone gives no size. Together: 3b = 20, so b = 20/3, l = 40/3, area = 800/9 cm², a unique value, so the answer is both statements together are needed.

17. Is triangle ABC a right-angled triangle?
I. AB = 3 cm, BC = 4 cm, CA = 5 cm.
II. The perimeter of the triangle is 12 cm.
(a) Statement II alone is sufficient (b) Both statements together are needed (c) Either statement alone is sufficient (d) Statement I alone is sufficient
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Statement II: a perimeter of 12 cm fits many triangles, for example 4-4-4, which is not right-angled, so it is not sufficient. From Statement I, 3² + 4² = 5², so by the converse of Pythagoras the triangle is right-angled, so the answer is Statement I alone is sufficient.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

20. Five friends P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row facing north. Who sits in the middle?
I. P sits at one end, and Q sits second from the left.
II. R sits exactly between Q and S, and T sits at the right end.
(a) Statement I alone is sufficient (b) Statement II alone is sufficient (c) Both statements together are needed (d) Either statement alone is sufficient
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement I leaves R, S, T unplaced, and Statement II alone does not fix positions from the ends. Together: Q is 2nd, R between Q and S forces R 3rd and S 4th, T is 5th and P is 1st, so R sits in the middle, so the answer is both statements together are needed.

25. What is the value of x + y?
I. x² − y² = 20
II. x − y = 4
(a) Both statements together are needed (b) Either statement alone is sufficient (c) Statement II alone is sufficient (d) Statement I alone is sufficient
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is trying to solve for x and y individually, which is unnecessary. Statement I factors as (x − y)(x + y) = 20, and with Statement II, 4(x + y) = 20 gives x + y = 5, so the answer is both statements together are needed.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
