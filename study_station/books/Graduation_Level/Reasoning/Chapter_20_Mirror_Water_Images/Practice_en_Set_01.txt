1. A mirror is placed vertically on the right side of the word "MOM". What will be its mirror image?
(a) WOW (b) WOM (c) MOW (d) MOM
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is WOM, which only reverses the letter order without checking each letter's symmetry. M and O are both laterally symmetric, so reversing the order of M-O-M still gives M-O-M, so the answer is MOM.
Source: PYQ-style

2. A mirror is placed vertically on the right of the number "1234". What will be its mirror image?
(a) 4321 with each digit laterally inverted (b) 2143 (c) 4321 (d) 1234
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students pick plain 4321, but that only reverses the order; in a true mirror image each digit is also laterally inverted, so digits like 2 and 4 appear flipped. The answer is 4321 with each digit laterally inverted.
Source: PYQ-style

3. A mirror is placed vertically on the right of the letter "A". Its mirror image will be:
(a) V (b) A rotated 180 degrees (c) A (d) A flipped top-to-bottom
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the top-to-bottom flip, which is what water does, not a vertical mirror. A vertical mirror flips only left and right, and A is symmetric about its vertical axis, so the image is unchanged, so the answer is A.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Which of the following capital letters looks exactly the same in a vertical mirror?
(a) E (b) H (c) B (d) F
Answer: (b)
Solution: B, E and F are tempting because they look balanced, but each is symmetric only about a horizontal axis, so a vertical mirror reverses them. H is symmetric about its vertical axis, so the answer is H.

6. A clock shows 3:00. What time will its image in a vertical mirror show?
(a) 3:00 (b) 9:00 (c) 6:00 (d) 12:00
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is 3:00, assuming the face is symmetric, but the minute hand stays at 12 while the hour hand at 3 flips to 9. Using the rule 11:60 minus the given time also gives 9:00, so the answer is 9:00.

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 6:30, since the minute hand at 6 is vertically aligned and looks unchanged. The hour hand between 6 and 7 flips to between 5 and 6, and 11:60 minus 6:30 gives 5:30, so the answer is 5:30.

15. If a mirror is placed on the left side of a figure instead of the right, the mirror image will:
(a) be different from the image with the mirror on the right (b) be the water image (c) be identical in form to the image with the mirror on the right (d) be the original figure unchanged
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming the side of the mirror changes the image. Lateral inversion is the same whether the mirror is on the left or right; only the position of the image changes, not its form, so the answer is that it is identical in form to the image with the mirror on the right.

16. Which of the following words reads exactly the same in a vertical mirror?
(a) TOOT (b) DAD (c) POD (d) MIX
Answer: (a)
Solution: DAD and POD are tempting palindromes, but D and P are not laterally symmetric, so they fail; MIX has symmetric letters but is not a palindrome, so its order reverses. T and O are both laterally symmetric and TOOT is a palindrome, so the answer is TOOT.

23. A clock shows 12:30. Its image in a vertical mirror will show:
(a) 6:30 (b) 12:30 (c) 1:30 (d) 11:30
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 12:30, since both hands lie near the vertical line and appear symmetric; but the hour hand sits between 12 and 1, which flips to between 11 and 12. Using 11:60 minus 12:30, treating 12 as 0, gives 11:30, so the answer is 11:30.
Source: PYQ-style

24. The water image of the word "CODE" is formed. What happens to the letters?
(a) The order stays the same and each letter flips top-to-bottom (b) The order reverses and each letter flips top-to-bottom (c) The order reverses and each letter flips left-to-right (d) The order stays the same and each letter flips left-to-right
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is any option that reverses the order, which confuses a water image with a vertical mirror or a rotation. A water image flips only top and bottom, so the letters keep their left-to-right order while each is vertically flipped, so the answer is that the order stays the same and each letter flips top-to-bottom.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A figure shows a flag on the left side of a pole, with the flag's star near the top. In the water image of this figure, the flag will appear:
(a) on the right of the pole with the star at the top (b) on the right of the pole with the star at the bottom (c) on the left of the pole with the star at the bottom (d) on the left of the pole with the star at the top
Answer: (c)
Solution: The double trap is changing both the side and the star position; a water image flips only top and bottom, never left and right. So the flag stays on the left of the pole while the star moves to the bottom, so the answer is on the left of the pole with the star at the bottom.
