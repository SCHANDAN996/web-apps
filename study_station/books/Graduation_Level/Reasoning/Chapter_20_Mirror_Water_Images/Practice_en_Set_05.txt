101. A mirror is placed vertically on the right side of the word "QUALITY". What will be its mirror image?
(a) YTILAUQ with each letter laterally inverted (b) QUALITY unchanged (c) YTILAUQ with letters in normal form (d) QYTLIAU
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option shows only the reversed order of letters, but a vertical mirror reverses the order AND laterally inverts each individual letter. So the answer is YTILAUQ with each letter laterally inverted.
Source: PYQ-style

102. A clock shows 4:30. What time will it show in a plane mirror?
(a) 7:30 (b) 8:00 (c) 8:30 (d) 7:00
Answer: (a)
Solution: The option 8:30 is a trap from wrongly subtracting from 12:00. For mirror images of clocks, subtract the given time from 11:60: 11:60 − 4:30 = 7:30. So the answer is 7:30.

103. The water image of the letter "F" will look like:
(a) F unchanged (b) F flipped left-right (c) F rotated 90° (d) F flipped top-bottom
Answer: (d)
Solution: Left-right flipping is the trap of confusing water image with mirror image. A water image is a reflection in a horizontal axis, so the top and bottom interchange while left and right stay the same. So the answer is F flipped top-bottom.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Which of the following letters looks the SAME in both mirror image and water image?
(a) A (b) M (c) H (d) T
Answer: (c)
Solution: A, M and T have only vertical symmetry, so they survive a mirror but change in water — that is the trap. H has both vertical and horizontal symmetry, so it is unchanged in both reflections. So the answer is H.

Answer: (d)

124. The word "CHIDE" is placed in front of a vertical mirror on its right, and the mirror image is then reflected in water. The final result is equivalent to:
(a) Only the water image of CHIDE (b) Only the mirror image of CHIDE (c) The original word rotated 180° (d) The original word unchanged
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming the two reflections cancel each other; they cancel only if applied along the same axis twice. A lateral inversion followed by a top-bottom inversion is equivalent to a 180° rotation of the original. So the answer is the original word rotated 180°.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Two clocks show the same time. Clock A is seen directly and reads 7:25. Clock B is seen in a mirror. What does Clock B appear to show, and what is the sum of the two displayed times?
(a) 4:35 and 12:00 (b) 4:35 and 11:60 (c) 5:35 and 12:00 (d) 4:25 and 11:50
Answer: (a)
Solution: The option 5:35 is the trap of subtracting from 12:60 incorrectly. Mirror image: 11:60 − 7:25 = 4:35, and the sum of the actual and mirror times is always 12:00 (7:25 + 4:35 = 12:00). So the answer is 4:35 and 12:00.
