Questions 51–75: Medium

51. If a mirror is placed on the right side of the word "QUALITY", which of the following will be its mirror image?
(a) QUALITY unchanged (b) YTILAUQ with each letter laterally inverted (c) YTILAUQ with letters upright (d) QUALITY flipped top to bottom
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is YTILAUQ with upright letters, but a mirror not only reverses the order of letters, it also flips each letter left-right. So the answer is YTILAUQ with each letter laterally inverted.
Source: PYQ-style

52. What is the water image of the digit "8"?
(a) A laterally inverted 8 (b) 0 (c) 8 unchanged (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is choosing 6, assuming a top-bottom flip turns 8 into something else, but 8 has both horizontal and vertical symmetry, so neither flip changes it. So the answer is 8 unchanged.
Source: PYQ-style

53. A clock shows 4:30. What time will its mirror image show?
(a) 7:60 (b) 8:30 (c) 8:00 (d) 7:30
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is subtracting only the hour from 12 and writing 8:30. The rule is mirror time = 11:60 − actual time = 11:60 − 4:30 = 7:30. So the answer is 7:30.
Source: PYQ-style

54. Which of the following capital letters looks the SAME in both a mirror image and a water image?
(a) T (b) H (c) M (d) A
Answer: (b)
Solution: A and T survive the mirror flip but change in a water image, and M changes in a water image too. H has both vertical and horizontal symmetry, so neither reflection alters it. So the answer is H.
Source: NCERT-based concept

55. The word "AMBULANCE" is printed in laterally inverted form on the front of an ambulance because:
(a) It is only a legal tradition (b) It saves printing cost (c) Drivers ahead see it correctly in their rear-view mirror (d) It looks decorative
Answer: (c)
Solution: A rear-view mirror laterally inverts whatever is printed on the vehicle behind; printing the word already inverted makes it read correctly to drivers ahead. The decorative and cost options are distractors with no scientific basis. So the answer is that drivers ahead see it correctly in their rear-view mirror.
Source: PYQ-style

56. The word "MOM" is written on a card. Its water image will show:
(a) WOW (b) MOM in the same order with each letter flipped top to bottom (c) MOM with letters reversed as MOM but upright (d) MOM unchanged
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming a water image reverses letter order like a mirror — it does not; only a top-bottom flip occurs and the order stays the same. So the answer is MOM in the same order with each letter flipped top to bottom.
Source: PYQ-style

57. A clock's mirror image shows 9:25. What is the actual time?
(a) 3:35 (b) 3:25 (c) 2:25 (d) 2:35
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting 3:35 comes from wrongly subtracting from 12:25. The correct rule: actual time = 11:60 − 9:25 = 2:35. So the answer is 2:35.

58. Which digit remains unchanged in a mirror image?
(a) 8 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 7
Answer: (a)
Solution: Among digits, 0, 1 and 8 have vertical symmetry and survive lateral inversion; 2, 4 and 7 do not. So the answer is 8.

59. The word "TAXI" is held in front of a mirror placed vertically on its left. The mirror image will show:
(a) IXAT with upright letters (b) TAXI unchanged (c) IXAT with each letter laterally inverted (d) TAXI flipped top to bottom
Answer: (c)
Solution: Whether the mirror is on the left or the right, it causes lateral inversion: the order reverses and each letter flips left-right. Upright IXAT is the common trap. So the answer is IXAT with each letter laterally inverted.
Source: PYQ-style

60. In a water image, which of these capital letters remains unchanged?
(a) C (b) F (c) A (d) L
Answer: (a)
Solution: A water image is a top-bottom flip, so only letters with horizontal symmetry survive. A has vertical symmetry only, and F and L have none; C is symmetric about its horizontal axis. So the answer is C.
Source: PYQ-style

61. A clock shows 10:10. Its mirror image will show:
(a) 11:50 (b) 2:50 (c) 1:10 (d) 1:50
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap 2:50 comes from subtracting 10:10 from 12:10 incorrectly. Mirror time = 11:60 − 10:10 = 1:50. So the answer is 1:50.

62. The mirror image of "12345" is:
(a) 12345 unchanged (b) 54321 upright (c) 54321 with each digit laterally inverted (d) 12345 flipped vertically
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lateral inversion reverses the order and flips each digit; digits like 2, 3, 4, 5 change shape under a mirror. Upright 54321 is the tempting wrong choice. So the answer is 54321 with each digit laterally inverted.

63. Which statement about water images is TRUE?
(a) Left and right interchange, top and bottom stay the same (b) Neither interchanges (c) Both interchange (d) Top and bottom interchange, left and right stay the same
Answer: (d)
Solution: A water image acts like reflection on a horizontal axis: only top and bottom swap. Confusing it with a mirror image, where left and right swap, is the classic trap. So the answer is that top and bottom interchange while left and right stay the same.
Source: NCERT-based concept

64. The water image of the letter "P" will look like:
(a) A vertically flipped P with the loop at the bottom (b) A mirror-reversed P (c) P unchanged (d) The letter b
Answer: (a)
Solution: A water image flips top-bottom, so the loop moves to the bottom while the letter still faces the same direction. Choosing a mirror-reversed P confuses the two reflection types. So the answer is a vertically flipped P with the loop at the bottom.
Source: PYQ-style

65. A clock's water image (reflection in water below it) shows 6:00. The actual time is:
(a) 6:00 (b) 12:00 (c) 3:00 (d) 9:00
Answer: (a)
Solution: At 6:00 the minute hand points up and the hour hand points down; a top-bottom flip swaps them, but both lie on the same vertical line, so the image still reads 6:00. So the answer is 6:00.
Source: PYQ-style

66. The mirror image of "BOX" is placed again in front of another mirror. The final image will be:
(a) BOX flipped vertically (b) XOB inverted (c) XOB upright (d) BOX normal
Answer: (d)
Solution: Two successive lateral inversions cancel each other and restore the original. The trap is thinking the reversal doubles. So the answer is BOX normal.
Source: PYQ-style

67. Which of the following words will read exactly the same in a mirror?
(a) MOM (b) TOT (c) WOW (d) DAD
Answer: (b)
Solution: A word reads the same in a mirror only if it is a palindrome AND every letter has vertical symmetry. M, W and D lack vertical symmetry, while T, O, T all have it and TOT is a palindrome. So the answer is TOT.
Source: PYQ-style

68. The water image of "CODE" will have:
(a) Letters reversed as EDOC (b) Letters in the same order, each flipped top to bottom (c) Letters reversed and flipped (d) CODE unchanged
Answer: (b)
Solution: A water image never reverses left-right order; only a vertical flip occurs. EDOC is the mirror-style trap. So the answer is letters in the same order, each flipped top to bottom.
Source: PYQ-style

70. In the mirror image of the figure "R7", the result will be:
(a) Laterally inverted R followed by inverted 7, in reversed order (b) R7 unchanged (c) 7R upright (d) R7 flipped vertically
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lateral inversion reverses the sequence and flips each character; neither R nor 7 is vertically symmetric. Upright 7R is the tempting trap. So the answer is laterally inverted R followed by inverted 7, in reversed order.

71. In the usual seven-segment display style, which pair of digits are water images of each other?
(a) 1 and 7 (b) 6 and 9 (c) 3 and 8 (d) 2 and 5
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 6 and 9, but those are related by a 180° rotation, not a top-bottom flip. In a seven-segment display a vertical flip maps the segments of 2 exactly onto 5 and vice versa. So the answer is 2 and 5.
Source: PYQ-style

72. A signboard reads "STOP". Its water image will show:
(a) POTS with inverted letters (b) POTS (c) STOP with each letter flipped top to bottom, order unchanged (d) STOP unchanged
Answer: (c)
Solution: A water reflection flips vertically only; S, T, O, P each flip top-bottom but the order stays STOP. POTS is the mirror trap. So the answer is STOP with each letter flipped top to bottom, order unchanged.
Source: PYQ-style

73. The mirror image of a clock showing 12:30 is:
(a) 11:30 (b) 12:30 (c) 11:60 (d) 6:30
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 12:30 assuming symmetry. Treat 12:30 as 0:30; mirror time = 11:60 − 0:30 = 11:30. So the answer is 11:30.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Which letter's mirror image and water image are DIFFERENT from each other and both different from the original?
(a) F (b) O (c) X (d) I
Answer: (a)
Solution: O, X and I are symmetric and remain unchanged in one or both reflections. F has no symmetry: its mirror image faces left while its water image flips the bars downward, giving two distinct new shapes. So the answer is F.
Source: PYQ-style

75. A paper with "2024" written on it is seen in a mirror. Which statement is correct?
(a) Only the digits flip, the order stays (b) Only the order reverses (c) The order reverses and each digit flips laterally (d) It reads 2024 unchanged
Answer: (c)
Solution: A mirror image always does both: sequence reversal and a lateral flip of each character. Saying only the order reverses is the standard examiner trap. So the answer is that the order reverses and each digit flips laterally.
Source: PYQ-style
