# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Mirror & Water Images (Explained Like You're 12)

## The Story of Ravi and the Lake

Imagine a boy named Ravi standing in front of a big mirror in his house, holding a cricket bat in his **right hand**. He looks at the mirror and shouts — "Mumma! The boy in the mirror is holding the bat in his **left hand**!"

Is the mirror boy a different person? No! It's Ravi himself. The mirror did only ONE thing — it **swapped left and right**. His right hand became the left hand in the image. But his head is still on top, his feet are still at the bottom. The mirror never touches **top and bottom**.

Now imagine Ravi goes to a lake. He bends down and looks at his reflection in the **water**. This time something different happens — his **head appears at the bottom** and his **feet at the top**! The water flipped him **upside down**. But his right hand is still on the right side. Water never touches **left and right**.

**That's the whole chapter in one story:**
- 🪞 **Mirror (kept on the side)** → swaps LEFT ↔ RIGHT, keeps TOP-BOTTOM same.
- 💧 **Water (below the object)** → swaps TOP ↔ BOTTOM, keeps LEFT-RIGHT same.

## The Hardest Part Made Easy: Letters and Numbers

Students get confused when a letter like **"b"** or a number like **"6"** comes in the exam. Just ask yourself two questions:

**Question 1: Where is the mirror/water?**
- Mirror on the **right side** of the object (most common in exams) → flip horizontally.
- Water **below** the object → flip vertically.

**Question 2: What changes?**
- In a mirror image of **"b"** → the belly moves to the other side → it looks like **"d"**.
- In a water image of **"b"** → the belly goes down → it looks like **"p"**.

See? One letter, two different answers — it all depends on **where the reflecting surface is**.

## ⚠️ The Trap Everyone Falls Into

The examiner's favourite trick: giving a question that says **"water image"** but putting an option that is actually the **mirror image**. Students who memorise "image = left-right flip" blindly pick it and lose marks. Always read: **mirror or water?** That one word decides everything.

Another trap: when the mirror is placed **on top** of the object (above it), it behaves like **water** — top and bottom swap! The mirror's *position* matters, not its name.

## 🎯 The Unforgettable Trick: "M-W Rule"

> **"Mirror flips the Middle (sides), Water flips the Width-wise (top-bottom)."**

Or even simpler — remember **"M = Left-Right, W = Up-Down"**.

**The 3-Second Exam Method:**
1. Read: Mirror or Water? (1 second)
2. Mirror → reverse the order of letters AND flip each letter left-right. Water → flip each letter top-bottom, order stays same. (2 seconds)
3. Eliminate options: any option where top-bottom changed in a mirror question = wrong instantly.

**Shortcut for letter-groups:** In a mirror image, the **last letter comes first** (order reverses). In a water image, the **order stays the same** — only each letter flips vertically. This one line kills 50% of options immediately.
