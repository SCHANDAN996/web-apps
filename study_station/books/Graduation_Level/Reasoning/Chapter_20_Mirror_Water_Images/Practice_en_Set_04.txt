76. If a mirror is placed on the right side of the word "MOM", what will be its mirror image?
(a) WOM (b) WOW (c) MOW (d) MOM
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice WOM only reverses the letter order, but M and O are vertically symmetric letters, so each letter looks unchanged in a mirror and the reversed order M-O-M still reads MOM. So the answer is MOM.
Source: PYQ-style

77. A clock shows 4:30. What time will its image in a plane mirror show?
(a) 8:30 (b) 7:30 (c) 7:00 (d) 8:00
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is subtracting from 12:00, which gives wrong results in most cases; the correct rule is to subtract the time from 11:60. 11:60 − 4:30 = 7:30. So the answer is 7:30.
Source: PYQ-style

78. Which of the following capital letters will look exactly the same in a plane mirror?
(a) B (b) C (c) H (d) G
Answer: (c)
Solution: Letters B, C and G lack a vertical axis of symmetry, so their mirror images look reversed and different. H has a vertical axis of symmetry, so it appears unchanged in a mirror. So the answer is H.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science (Light)

79. If a mirror is held vertically to the right of the number "25", what will the mirror image look like?
(a) 52 (b) reversed 5 followed by reversed 2 (c) 25 (d) reversed 2 followed by reversed 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is merely swapping the digits to get 52, but a mirror also reverses the shape of each digit. The digit nearer the mirror, 5, appears first in the image, and both digits are laterally inverted. So the answer is reversed 5 followed by reversed 2.
Source: PYQ-style

80. The water image of the letter "A" is:
(a) A flipped top-to-bottom (b) the same as A (c) A flipped left-to-right (d) A rotated by 90°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is choosing the left-to-right flip, which describes a mirror image, not a water image. A water image is a reflection in a horizontal surface, so the figure flips top-to-bottom. So the answer is A flipped top-to-bottom.
Source: PYQ-style

81. A clock shows 4:20. What will be the time shown by its water image?
(a) 4:10 (b) 2:10 (c) 1:50 (d) 2:50
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is applying the mirror-image rule of 11:60, which is meant for vertical mirrors. For water images of clocks, subtract the time from 18:30: 18:30 − 4:20 = 14:10, which is 2:10. So the answer is 2:10.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Which word will appear exactly the same in a plane mirror?
(a) TOOT (b) BOX (c) SUN (d) DAD
Answer: (a)
Solution: DAD fails because D is not vertically symmetric, BOX fails because of B, and SUN fails because of S and N. TOOT uses only the vertically symmetric letters T and O and is a palindrome, so it reads the same in a mirror. So the answer is TOOT.
Source: PYQ-style

83. If the mirror is placed on the left side of the letter "R", the mirror image will show:
(a) R as it is (b) R upside down (c) R rotated by 180° (d) a laterally inverted R
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is thinking the image is a 180° rotation, which would also flip the letter vertically — a plane mirror never does that. A plane mirror produces only lateral inversion, so R appears reversed left-to-right. So the answer is a laterally inverted R.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science (Light)

84. The number "8" is placed before a plane mirror. Its image will be:
(a) reversed 8 (b) 8 (c) 0 (d) an infinity symbol
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming every digit reverses like 2 or 5. The digit 8 is symmetric about the vertical axis, so lateral inversion leaves it unchanged. So the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

85. A clock shows 2:40. What time will its mirror image show?
(a) 9:20 (b) 9:40 (c) 10:40 (d) 10:20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice 10:20 comes from wrongly subtracting from 12:00 without borrowing minutes. The correct rule is 11:60 − 2:40 = 9:20. So the answer is 9:20.

86. Which of the following digits will look exactly the same in a plane mirror?
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The digits 2, 5 and 4 are not vertically symmetric, so their mirror images look reversed and different. The digit 8 has a vertical axis of symmetry, so it appears unchanged. So the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

87. The water image of the number "6" will most closely resemble:
(a) 6 (b) 0 (c) reversed 6 (d) 9
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing a left-right reversed 6, which is the mirror image, not the water image. A water image flips the figure top-to-bottom, and flipping 6 vertically turns it into a shape like 9. So the answer is 9.
Source: PYQ-style

88. A mirror is placed horizontally below the word "TAXI". The image seen is a:
(a) water image (b) inverted real image (c) rotated image (d) mirror image
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is calling it a mirror image, a term reserved for a vertical reflecting surface. When the reflecting surface is horizontal, below or above the object, the image flips top and bottom and is called a water image. So the answer is water image.
Source: PYQ-style

89. A clock shows 6:00. Its mirror image will show:
(a) 5:60 (b) 12:00 (c) 6:00 (d) 11:60
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is writing 5:60 directly without converting 60 minutes into one hour. Using the rule, 11:60 − 6:00 = 5:60, which converts to 6:00. So the answer is 6:00.
Source: PYQ-style

90. Which letter's water image is the same as the letter itself?
(a) B (b) K (c) E (d) H
Answer: (d)
Solution: A water image flips top-to-bottom, so the letter must be symmetric about a horizontal axis. B, K and E change when flipped vertically, while H has horizontal symmetry and stays the same. So the answer is H.
Source: PYQ-style

91. The word "AMBULANCE" is written laterally inverted on the front of the vehicle so that:
(a) it looks decorative (b) it saves paint (c) drivers ahead can read it correctly in their rear-view mirror (d) it confuses followers
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming the reversed writing is stylistic. Because a rear-view mirror laterally inverts whatever it reflects, writing the word in reverse makes it readable to drivers in front. So the answer is drivers ahead can read it correctly in their rear-view mirror.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science (Light)

92. A clock shows 10:10. What time will its mirror image show?
(a) 1:50 (b) 2:50 (c) 1:10 (d) 2:10
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice 2:50 comes from subtracting from 12:00, the most common error. The correct rule is 11:60 − 10:10 = 1:50. So the answer is 1:50.

93. If the mirror is placed on the right of the small letter "p", the mirror image will look like:
(a) p (b) b (c) d (d) q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing d, which is a 180° rotation of p, not a mirror reflection. Lateral inversion of p flips it left-to-right, producing the shape of q. So the answer is q.

94. The water image of the letter "M" will look like:
(a) M (b) W (c) reversed M (d) N
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is choosing M itself, because M is vertically symmetric and its mirror image is M — but a water image flips top and bottom, not left and right. Flipping M vertically gives W. So the answer is W.
Source: PYQ-style

95. A clock shows 3:00. The difference between the actual time and its mirror image time is:
(a) 6 hours (b) 3 hours (c) 9 hours (d) 12 hours
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing the mirror time wrongly as 8:00 by subtracting from 11:00 instead of 11:60. The mirror image of 3:00 is 11:60 − 3:00 = 8:60 = 9:00, and the difference between 3:00 and 9:00 is 6 hours. So the answer is 6 hours.
Source: PYQ-style

96. Which of the following digits has the same mirror image and the same water image as itself?
(a) only 0 (b) only 8 (c) both 0 and 8 (d) 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is picking only 8 and forgetting 0. Both 0 and 8 are symmetric about the vertical and the horizontal axes, so neither a mirror flip nor a water flip changes them. So the answer is both 0 and 8.
Source: PYQ-style

98. The mirror image of the word "QUALITY" will have which letter closest to the mirror if the mirror is on the right?
(a) Q (b) T (c) Y (d) L
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering Q, the first letter of the word, while ignoring the mirror's position. The letter nearest the mirror appears first in the image, and with the mirror on the right, the last letter Y is closest to it. So the answer is Y.

99. A clock shows 12:30. What time will its mirror image show?
(a) 12:30 (b) 11:00 (c) 11:30 (d) 6:30
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is subtracting 12:30 from 12:00 and getting a confusing negative result. Treat 12:30 as 0:30 and subtract from 11:60: 11:60 − 0:30 = 11:30. So the answer is 11:30.
Source: PYQ-style

100. In a plane mirror, the image of a left hand appears as a:
(a) right hand (b) rotated hand (c) upside-down hand (d) left hand
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is thinking the image is upside down, which never happens with a vertical plane mirror. Lateral inversion swaps left and right, so a left hand appears as a right hand. So the answer is right hand.
