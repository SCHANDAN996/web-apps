Card 1
Front: In a mirror image, which axis actually gets reversed — top-bottom or left-right?
Back: Left-right (lateral inversion). A mirror placed vertically swaps the left and right sides; top and bottom stay unchanged.

Card 2
Front: In a water image, which axis gets reversed?
Back: Top-bottom (vertical inversion). The water surface acts like a horizontal mirror, so the figure flips upside down; left and right stay the same.

Card 3
Front: What is the quick mnemonic to remember mirror vs water image reversal?
Back: "Mirror flips Sides, Water flips Heights" — mirror = left-right swap, water = top-bottom swap.

Card 4
Front: If the mirror is placed on the right side of a figure, where does the image appear and what changes?
Back: The image appears on the right of the mirror line, with left and right interchanged. The part of the figure nearest the mirror stays nearest in the image.

Card 5
Front: Which capital English letters look exactly the same in a mirror image?
Back: A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y — letters with vertical symmetry.

Card 6
Front: Which capital letters look the same in a water image?
Back: B, C, D, E, H, I, K (in most fonts), O, X — letters with horizontal symmetry. H, I, O, X survive both.

Card 7
Front: What is the mirror image of the time 3:00 on an analog clock?
Back: 9:00. Trick: subtract the given time from 11:60 (or 12:00). 11:60 − 3:00 = 8:60 = 9:00.

Card 8
Front: What is the water image of the time 4:30 on a clock?
Back: Subtract from 18:30 → 18:30 − 4:30 = 14:00, i.e., 2:00. (For water images of clocks, subtract from 18:30.)

Card 9
Front: Memory trick: how do you instantly recall the clock subtraction values for mirror and water images?
Back: "Mirror 11:60, Water 18:30" — mirror image: subtract from 11:60; water image: subtract from 18:30.

Card 10
Front: Examiner's trap: a question says "mirror placed below the figure." What does the image actually become?
Back: A mirror below the figure behaves like a water surface — the image is inverted top-bottom, NOT left-right. Trap: students apply left-right reversal out of habit.

Card 11
Front: Examiner's trap: in mirror-image questions of numbers like 25 or 68, what wrong option do examiners plant?
Back: They offer the number merely written in reverse order (52, 86) without flipping each digit's shape. Correct answer reverses both the order AND the shape of each digit.

Card 12
Front: What is the mirror image of the letter 'b'?
Back: 'd' — left-right reversal turns b into d. (Similarly p ↔ q.)

Card 13
Front: What is the water image of the letter 'b'?
Back: 'p' — top-bottom flip turns b into p. (Similarly d ↔ q in water.)

Card 14
Front: If an arrow points to the north-east in the original, where does it point in a mirror (placed vertically on the right)?
Back: North-west. Horizontal component reverses (east ↔ west), vertical component (north) stays.

Card 15
Front: If an arrow points north-east, where does it point in a water image?
Back: South-east. Vertical component reverses (north ↔ south), horizontal component (east) stays.

Card 16
Front: In a figure with a small dot in the top-left corner, where does the dot go in (a) mirror image, (b) water image?
Back: (a) Mirror: top-right corner. (b) Water: bottom-left corner.

Card 17
Front: What is the fastest elimination trick for mirror-image MCQs?
Back: Check one asymmetric detail (a dot, a slant, a letter like R or F). Any option where that detail hasn't swapped left-right is wrong — usually eliminates 2–3 options instantly.

Card 18
Front: Does the size or distance of the object change in mirror/water image questions in exams?
Back: No. Exam questions treat images as same-size, same-distance reflections. Options showing a shrunken or enlarged image are distractors.

Card 19
Front: What is the mirror image of the word "MOM"?
Back: MOM itself — M, O, M are all vertically symmetric, and reversing the order of this palindrome changes nothing.

Card 20
Front: When the mirror line is horizontal (above or below the figure), which rule applies?
Back: The water-image rule: top-bottom inversion. Mirror position decides the flip axis — vertical mirror = left-right flip, horizontal mirror = top-bottom flip.
