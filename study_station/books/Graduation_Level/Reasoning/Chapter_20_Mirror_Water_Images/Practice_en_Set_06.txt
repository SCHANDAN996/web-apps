126. If a mirror is placed on the right side of the word "QUALITY", which of the following is its correct mirror image?
(a) YTILAUQ with each letter laterally inverted (b) QUALITY with only Q and Y swapped (c) QUALITY written normally (d) YTILAUQ with letters unchanged
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option reverses only the letter order but keeps the letter shapes unchanged; a true mirror image reverses both the order and the shape of each asymmetric letter. A mirror on the right produces lateral inversion, so the answer is YTILAUQ with each letter laterally inverted.

127. A clock shows 4:40. What time will its image in a plane mirror show?
(a) 7:20 (b) 8:20 (c) 7:40 (d) 8:40
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 8:20 comes from subtracting 4:40 from 12:60 incorrectly. The rule is mirror time = 11:60 − actual time = 11:60 − 4:40 = 7:20, so the answer is 7:20.
