# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Mirror & Water Images

---

**Box 1 — The "FLIP" Rule**
**F**lip **L**eft-right, **I**t's a mirror; **I**nvert **P**erpendicular for water.
Mirror = sideways flip. Water = upside-down flip. Just remember: **"Mirror Moves Sideways, Water Washes Down."**

---

**Box 2 — "The Mirror Never Sweats"**
A mirror image never changes the TOP and BOTTOM of a figure — only left and right swap. If an option flips the top to the bottom, it's a **water image in disguise** — reject it instantly.

---

**Box 3 — "Water Doesn't Care About Sides"**
A water image never swaps LEFT and RIGHT — only top and bottom. If an option has swapped left-right, it's a mirror image wearing a water costume. Eliminate!

---

**Box 4 — The Clock Trick: "11:60 Minus"**
For mirror image of a clock time: subtract from **11:60** (or 12:00 if minutes = 00).
For water image of a clock: subtract from **17:90** (18:30 minus the time, adjusted).
Mnemonic: **"Mirror says 12, Water says 6-ish (18:30)."**

---

**Box 5 — "CAPITALS Confess"**
Capital letters that look the SAME in a mirror: **A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y** (vertical symmetry).
Memory line: **"AH! I MOVe TU VW XY"** — say it like a car brand list.

---

**Box 6 — "Water-Loving Letters"**
Capitals unchanged in water image (horizontal symmetry): **B, C, D, E, H, I, K, O, X**.
Mnemonic: **"Big Crocodiles Dive Elegantly, Hiding In Kitchens, Okay X?"**

---

**Box 7 — "Both-Symmetry Superstars"**
Letters identical in BOTH mirror and water: **H, I, O, X**.
Mnemonic: **"HIOX — the superhero squad that no reflection can touch."**

---

**Box 8 — The Number Villains**
Digits that survive a mirror: **0, 1, 8** (and 3 in some fonts). In water: **0, 1, 3, 8** (font-dependent — in exams usually only 0, 8 are safe).
Mnemonic: **"018 — the VIP pass for mirrors."**

---

**Box 9 — "Write It, Fold It"**
Stuck on a figure? Mentally draw a vertical line (mirror) or horizontal line (water) through the figure's edge, then "fold the page." Whatever touches the fold line stays touching it. **The fold line is sacred — distances from it never change.**

---

**Box 10 — "Arrow Betrays First"**
In any figure with an arrow, check the arrow FIRST. Mirror: arrow pointing right → points left. Water: arrow pointing up → points down. One glance at the arrow kills 2–3 wrong options in 5 seconds.

---

**Box 11 — "The Asymmetric Anchor"**
Find the most ASYMMETRIC part of the figure (a dot, a flag, a bent line). Track ONLY that part through the flip. Symmetric parts (circles, squares) are traps — they look identical in every option and waste your time.

---

**Box 12 — "Mirror on the RIGHT, Unless Told"**
Exam default: mirror is placed on the **right side** of the figure (vertical mirror). If the question says "mirror on left" or "mirror below," the answer changes! Mnemonic: **"Read the mirror's address before flipping."**

---

**Box 13 — "Water = Mirror + 180°? NO!"**
Common trap: students think water image = mirror image rotated 180°. WRONG. Water image is a pure top-bottom flip. Mnemonic: **"Water flips, it doesn't spin."**

---

**Box 14 — "Option Elimination Ladder"**
Step 1: Check orientation type (sideways vs upside-down) → kill 2 options.
Step 2: Check the asymmetric anchor → kill 1 more.
Step 3: Verify one small detail (dot position, line tilt) → answer found.
**Never compare all 4 options fully — climb the ladder.**

---

**Box 15 — "The 15-Second Scan"**
Before solving, scan the 4 options. Often 2 options are not even flipped (same as original) — instant elimination. **"Scan before you solve."**

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip

1. **The 15-Second Rule:** If after 15 seconds you cannot identify the asymmetric anchor or the flip direction, mark and skip. Mirror/water questions are meant to be 20–30 second questions — if it's taking longer, the figure is deliberately complex and will eat time meant for 3 easier questions.

2. **Skip "Double-Trap" Figures:** If the figure has BOTH letters/numbers AND a complex shape (e.g., a word inside a tilted figure), and the options differ only in tiny details, skip on first pass. Return only if time remains — these are designed as time-traps with near-identical options.

3. **Skip Ambiguous Font Cases:** If the question hinges on whether a digit like 3 or a letter like K "looks the same" after flipping, and two options both look plausible, don't gamble blindly — mark it, solve the rest, and return with fresh eyes. Never burn 90 seconds on a 1-mark coin toss.
