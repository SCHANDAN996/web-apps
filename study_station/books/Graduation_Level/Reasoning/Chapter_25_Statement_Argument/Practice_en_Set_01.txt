1. Statement: Should all government schools have a library in every building?
Arguments:
I. Yes, a library builds the reading habit and helps poor students who cannot buy books.
II. No, students these days only want to play games on phones.
(a) Only argument II is strong (b) Only argument I is strong (c) Both arguments I and II are strong (d) Neither argument I nor II is strong
Answer: (b)
Solution: Argument I gives a real, wide benefit to students. Argument II is a sweeping generalisation about all students, which makes it weak. So the answer is "Only argument I is strong".
