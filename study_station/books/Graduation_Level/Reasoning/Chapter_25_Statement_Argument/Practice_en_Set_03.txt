51. Statement: Should the government shut down all loss-making public sector units?
Arguments:
I. Yes, they are a continuous drain on public money.
II. No, closing them suddenly will throw many employees out of work.
(a) Only argument I is strong (b) Only argument II is strong (c) Both arguments I and II are strong (d) Neither argument I nor II is strong
Answer: (c)
Solution: Argument I raises a real fiscal concern; Argument II raises a real human cost. Both are strong. So the answer is "Both arguments I and II are strong".
