# Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Statement and Argument

**Box 1 — "SIMPLE-T is weak"**
> Superlative, Irrelevant, Mere example/tradition, Personal, Logic gap, Exaggeration, Tautology.

**Box 2 — "Only and All make it fall"**
> Arguments with only, all, every, never, always usually collapse.

**Box 3 — "The principal's notebook"**
> Would a sensible principal write it down? Health, safety, money, rights, environment — yes.

**Box 4 — "Hair, taste and looks never win"**
> Arguments about personal comfort or appearance are weak.

**Box 5 — "One uncle is not the nation"**
> A single example cannot support a policy for everyone.

**Box 6 — "Other countries are not a reason"**
> "X does it" or "it was always done" needs a real reason behind it.

**Box 7 — "Overnight is a red light"**
> Promises of instant, magical results are weak.

**Box 8 — "Two sides, two notes"**
> A real benefit and a real cost can both be strong — answer "Both".

**Box 9 — "Judge the reason, not the team"**
> Agreeing with a side never decides strength.

**Box 10 — "Repeat ≠ reason"**
> If the argument just repeats the statement, it is a tautology — weak.

## Skip Strategy

1. **15 seconds:** strike out arguments with SIMPLE-T words first.
2. **45 seconds:** if both arguments are serious but you are unsure, mark and return.
3. **Close options:** when "Only I" and "Both" both look possible, ask whether argument II gives a real, serious consequence.
