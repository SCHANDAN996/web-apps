76. Statement: Should the government ban the sale of tobacco products near schools?
Arguments:
I. Yes, it makes it harder for children to start using tobacco.
II. No, shopkeepers near schools are very friendly.
(a) Neither argument I nor II is strong (b) Only argument II is strong (c) Both arguments I and II are strong (d) Only argument I is strong
Answer: (d)
Solution: Argument I gives a real public-health reason. Argument II is irrelevant, so only I is strong. So the answer is "Only argument I is strong".
