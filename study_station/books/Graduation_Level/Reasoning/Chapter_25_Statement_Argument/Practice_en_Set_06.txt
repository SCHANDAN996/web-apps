126. Statement: Should India stop building large dams?
Arguments:
I. Yes, large dams displace many families and submerge forests.
II. No, large dams provide water for irrigation, drinking and electricity to millions.
(a) Only argument I is strong (b) Only argument II is strong (c) Neither argument I nor II is strong (d) Both arguments I and II are strong
Answer: (d)
Solution: Argument I gives a serious social and ecological cost; Argument II serious benefits. Both are strong. So the answer is "Both arguments I and II are strong".
