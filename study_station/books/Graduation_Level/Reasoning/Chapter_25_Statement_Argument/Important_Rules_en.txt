# Important Rules & Approaches — Statement and Argument

## 1. Key Rules / Approaches

| Rule Name | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Relevance | A strong argument talks about the exact step in the statement | Helmets → "reduce head injuries" (strong) |
| Importance | It concerns a serious matter: health, safety, education, economy, rights, environment | "Smoke harms non-smokers" (strong) |
| General truth | It rests on facts or widely accepted reasoning, not one example | "My uncle dropped his phone" (weak) |
| Logical link | The reason must actually lead to "Yes" or "No" | "Ban phones, then everyone tops exams" (weak) |
| Extreme words | only, all, every, always, never, overnight usually make it weak | "All students are lazy" (weak) |
| Personal/trivial | comfort, looks, liking of a few people | "Helmets spoil hairstyles" (weak) |
| Tradition/other countries | "It was always done" or "other countries do it" is weak by itself | "Other countries host such events" (weak) |
| Tautology | Repeating the statement as the reason | "Ban plastic because it should not be used" (weak) |
| Both can be strong | One side gives a real benefit, the other a real cost | Retirement age 65 — experience vs jobs for youth |
| Neither | Both arguments use weak patterns | "Everyone becomes rich" / "No one wants it" |
| Separate judgement | Judge I and II independently, never against each other | — |
| No personal opinion | An argument you disagree with can still be strong | — |

## 2. Time Management

| Rule | What to do |
|------|------------|
| 15-second rule | Scan for extreme or trivial words first — they decide most weak arguments within 15 seconds |
| 45-second rule | If both arguments look serious and you cannot decide in 45 seconds, mark the question and return |
| Options check | Once one argument is clearly weak, strike out "Both" and the option that keeps it strong |

## 3. Logical / Mathematical Formulas

No formulas for this chapter. Use the four tests: related, important, generally true, logical.
