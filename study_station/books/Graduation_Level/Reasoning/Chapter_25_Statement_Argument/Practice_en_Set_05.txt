101. Statement: Should the government ban mobile phones inside examination halls?
Arguments:
I. Yes, phones can be used to cheat.
II. No, the time shown on phones is very accurate.
(a) Both arguments I and II are strong (b) Only argument II is strong (c) Only argument I is strong (d) Neither argument I nor II is strong
Answer: (c)
Solution: Argument I gives a real reason. A wall clock can show the time, so Argument II is weak. So the answer is "Only argument I is strong".
