26. Statement: Should the retirement age of government employees be raised to 65 years?
Arguments:
I. Yes, experienced employees can serve longer and the government saves on training new staff.
II. No, it will reduce job openings for young people.
(a) Only argument I is strong (b) Only argument II is strong (c) Both arguments I and II are strong (d) Neither argument I nor II is strong
Answer: (c)
Solution: Argument I gives a real benefit of experience; Argument II gives a real cost to young job seekers. Both are strong. So the answer is "Both arguments I and II are strong".
