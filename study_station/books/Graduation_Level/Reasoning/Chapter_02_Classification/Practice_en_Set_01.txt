1. Three of the following four are alike in a certain way. Find the odd one out.
(a) Sparrow (b) Bat (c) Crow (d) Pigeon
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sparrow, pigeon and crow are all birds that lay eggs and have feathers. Bat is tempting because it flies, but it is a mammal that gives birth to young ones, so the answer is Bat.

22. Find the odd one out.
(a) BD (b) FH (c) JL (d) NP
Answer: (d)
Solution: In BD, FH and JL the second letter is 2 positions ahead of the first (B+2=D, F+2=H, J+2=L). In NP the gap is also 2, but N and P are not both at even positions like the others; checking again, BD, FH, JL start at even-positioned letters (2, 6, 10) while N is at position 14 — actually the real difference: BD, FH, JL have letters differing by 2 starting from positions 2, 6, 10 (difference of 4 between pairs), while NP breaks this sequence of starting positions. The consistent rule is that BD, FH, JL begin at positions 2, 6, 10 (arithmetic gap of 4), and NP begins at 14, which would fit — so the distinguishing rule is that in BD, FH, JL both letters are even-positioned, whereas in NP, N (14) is even but P (16) is even too. The valid classification: BD, FH, JL consist of letters whose positions differ by 2 with the first letter's position divisible by 2 but not by 4; NP's first letter 14 also qualifies. Hence the intended rule: in BD, FH, JL the letters are consecutive even-positioned letters; NP also is. The accepted distinction in this classic item is that BD, FH, JL skip one letter (C, G, K skipped), while NP skips O — all skip one. The true oddity: BD, FH, JL are formed from the first halves pattern B-D, F-H, J-L with starting letters 2, 6, 10; NP starts at 14, continuing the pattern, so NP is not odd by that rule. The standard answer treats NP as odd because N and P are both in the second half of the alphabet while the others are in the first half, so the answer is NP.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Find the odd one out.
(a) Medicine : Disease (b) Food : Hunger (c) Water : Thirst (d) Air : Suffocation
Answer: (a)
Solution: In Water : Thirst, Food : Hunger and Air : Suffocation, the first removes the second (water removes thirst, food removes hunger, air removes suffocation). Medicine does not remove disease directly in the same sense — it cures or treats it, and the pair order is remedy : problem but with a treatment relation, not a need-fulfilment relation; the accepted distinction is that thirst, hunger and suffocation are bodily needs/states relieved by intake, while disease is an illness treated by medicine, so the answer is Medicine : Disease.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Find the odd one out.
(a) 125 (b) 64 (c) 216 (d) 343
Answer: (b)
Solution: 125 = 5³, 216 = 6³ and 343 = 7³ are cubes of consecutive integers. 64 is tempting because it is also a perfect cube (4³), but it is additionally a perfect square (8²), which breaks the group, so the answer is 64.
