26. Four of the following five are alike in a certain way and so form a group. Which is the one that does not belong to that group?
(a) Brass (b) Zinc (c) Copper (d) Aluminium
Answer: (a)
Solution: Brass is tempting because it is a common metal, but it is an alloy of copper and zinc, not a pure element. Copper, zinc and aluminium are all pure metallic elements, so the answer is Brass.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Four of the following five are alike in a certain way. Find the odd one out.

Solution: Bat looks like a bird because it flies, which is the trap, but it is a mammal that gives birth to young ones. Sparrow, pigeon and parrot are birds, so the answer is Bat.
Source: PYQ-style

28. Choose the one which is different from the others.
(a) 121 (b) 144 (c) 169 (d) 156
Answer: (d)
Solution: 156 is close to 144 and may look like a square, but it is not a perfect square. 121 = 11², 144 = 12² and 169 = 13², so the answer is 156.
