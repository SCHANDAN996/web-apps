Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Four of the following five are alike in a certain way. Find the odd one out.
(a) Bat (b) Pigeon (c) Sparrow (d) Parrot
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bat looks like a bird and even flies, which tempts many students to group it with the others, but it is a mammal — it gives birth to young ones and suckles them. Sparrow, pigeon and parrot are all birds that lay eggs. So the answer is Bat.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Find the odd one out.
(a) 27 (b) 64 (c) 125 (d) 144
Answer: (d)
Solution: 144 is a perfect square (12²), and students who notice only "big numbers" may miss the real link. 27 = 3³, 64 = 4³ and 125 = 5³ are all perfect cubes, while 144 is not a cube of any integer. So the answer is 144.
Source: PYQ-style

78. Find the odd one out.
(a) Potato (b) Mango (c) Banana (d) Apple
Answer: (a)
Solution: Potato is sometimes wrongly clubbed with fruits because it is eaten as food, but it is a vegetable that grows underground as a tuber. Apple, mango and banana are all fruits that grow on plants or trees above the ground. So the answer is Potato.
Source: PYQ-style

79. Find the odd one out.
(a) 16 (b) 36 (c) 48 (d) 64
Answer: (c)
Solution: 48 may look harmless among even numbers, but it is not a perfect square. 16 = 4², 36 = 6² and 64 = 8² are all perfect squares of even numbers. So the answer is 48.
Source: PYQ-style

80. Find the odd one out.
(a) Engineer (b) Patient (c) Teacher (d) Doctor
Answer: (b)
Solution: Patient is linked to a doctor, which creates a double-relationship trap — some students reject "Doctor" thinking the others are independent professions. But doctor, engineer and teacher are all service-giving professions, while a patient is a receiver of service, not a profession. So the answer is Patient.
Source: PYQ-style

81. Find the odd one out.
(a) 121 (b) 169 (c) 225 (d) 196
Answer: (d)
Solution: 196 = 14² is a perfect square like the others, so the trap is to think all four belong together. But 121 = 11², 169 = 13² and 225 = 15² are squares of odd numbers, whereas 196 is the square of an even number. So the answer is 196.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Find the odd one out.
(a) Yamuna (b) Kaveri (c) Godavari (d) Krishna
Answer: (a)
Solution: Yamuna is a famous north-Indian river, so students hesitate to remove it, but the clean classification here is origin. Kaveri, Godavari and Krishna are peninsular rivers rising in the Western Ghats or the Deccan plateau, while Yamuna is a Himalayan river and a tributary of the Ganga. So the answer is Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

83. Find the odd one out.
(a) 27 (b) 100 (c) 64 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: 100 is a perfect square (10²), which misleads students into keeping it. But 8 = 2³, 27 = 3³ and 64 = 4³ are all perfect cubes, while 100 is not a perfect cube. So the answer is 100.
Source: PYQ-style

84. Find the odd one out.
(a) Lotus (b) Rose (c) Marigold (d) Mango
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mango is a familiar plant name, so it hides among the flowers. Rose, lotus and marigold are flowers, while mango is a fruit. So the answer is Mango.
Source: PYQ-style

85. Find the odd one out.
(a) 43 (b) 41 (c) 37 (d) 51
Answer: (d)
Solution: 51 looks like a prime because it is odd and not an obvious multiple, which is the classic trap. But 51 = 3 × 17 is composite, while 37, 41 and 43 are all prime numbers. So the answer is 51.
Source: PYQ-style

86. Find the odd one out.
(a) Cricket (b) Football (c) Hockey (d) Chess
Answer: (d)
Solution: Chess is a competitive sport, so students hesitate to remove it. But cricket, hockey and football are outdoor team games played on a field, while chess is an indoor board game between two individuals. So the answer is Chess.
Source: PYQ-style

87. Find the odd one out.
(a) 21 (b) 19 (c) 13 (d) 17
Answer: (a)
Solution: 21 sits naturally among odd numbers, which is the trap. But 21 = 3 × 7 is composite, while 13, 17 and 19 are all prime numbers. So the answer is 21.

88. Find the odd one out.
(a) Eyes (b) Ears (c) Hands (d) Nose
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hands are body parts like the others, so the grouping feels complete. But eyes, ears and nose are sense organs, while hands are organs of action, not a sense organ. So the answer is Hands.
Source: NCERT Class 5 EVS

89. Find the odd one out.
(a) 2 (b) 9 (c) 5 (d) 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: 9 is odd like 3 and 5, so students may keep it. But 2, 3 and 5 are prime numbers, while 9 = 3 × 3 is composite. So the answer is 9.
Source: PYQ-style

90. Find the odd one out.
(a) May (b) March (c) June (d) January
Answer: (c)
Solution: All four are months, so the trap is to stop thinking there. January, March and May have 31 days each, while June has only 30 days. So the answer is June.
Source: PYQ-style

91. Find the odd one out.
(a) Wood (b) Copper (c) Silver (d) Iron
Answer: (a)
Solution: Wood is a common material like the metals, so it blends in. Copper, iron and silver are metals and good conductors of heat and electricity, while wood is a non-metal and an insulator. So the answer is Wood.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

92. Find the odd one out.
(a) 49 (b) 132 (c) 121 (d) 81
Answer: (b)
Solution: 132 is close to 121, so it hides among the squares. But 49 = 7², 81 = 9² and 121 = 11² are squares of odd numbers, while 132 is not a perfect square at all. So the answer is 132.
Source: PYQ-style

93. Find the odd one out.
(a) Fox (b) Tiger (c) Leopard (d) Lion
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fox is a wild animal like the others, which is the trap. Lion, tiger and leopard belong to the cat family (Felidae), while the fox belongs to the dog family (Canidae). So the answer is Fox.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

94. Find the odd one out.
(a) 11 (b) 13 (c) 15 (d) 17
Answer: (c)
Solution: 15 is odd and sits between 13 and 17, so it looks like part of a prime sequence. But 15 = 3 × 5 is composite, while 11, 13 and 17 are consecutive prime numbers. So the answer is 15.
Source: PYQ-style

95. Find the odd one out.
(a) Triangle (b) Square (c) Circle (d) Rectangle
Answer: (c)
Solution: Circle is a basic shape like the others, so students keep it. But triangle, square and rectangle are polygons made of straight line segments, while a circle is a curve with no sides or angles. So the answer is Circle.

96. Find the odd one out.
(a) 730 (b) 512 (c) 729 (d) 343
Answer: (a)
Solution: 730 is just one more than 729, so it slips in unnoticed. But 343 = 7³, 512 = 8³ and 729 = 9³ are consecutive perfect cubes, while 730 is not a perfect cube. So the answer is 730.
Source: PYQ-style

97. Find the odd one out.
(a) Mumbai (b) Delhi (c) Chennai (d) Kolkata
Answer: (b)
Solution: Delhi is a famous metro city, so it seems to belong. But Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata are coastal port cities, while Delhi is landlocked and is also the national capital, not a port. So the answer is Delhi.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

98. Find the odd one out.
(a) 24 (b) 48 (c) 50 (d) 36
Answer: (c)
Solution: 50 is an even number like the rest, which is the trap. But 24, 36 and 48 are all multiples of 12, while 50 is not divisible by 12. So the answer is 50.
Source: PYQ-style

99. Find the odd one out.
(a) Gold (b) Silver (c) Platinum (d) Brass
Answer: (d)
Solution: Brass shines like a precious metal, so students keep it in the group. But gold, silver and platinum are pure metallic elements, while brass is an alloy of copper and zinc. So the answer is Brass.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

100. Find the odd one out.
(a) 89 (b) 91 (c) 83 (d) 79
Answer: (b)
Solution: 91 is the famous trap number — it looks prime because it is odd and not divisible by 3 or 5, and students routinely mark a different option. But 91 = 7 × 13 is composite, while 89, 83 and 79 are all prime numbers. So the answer is 91.
Source: PYQ-style
