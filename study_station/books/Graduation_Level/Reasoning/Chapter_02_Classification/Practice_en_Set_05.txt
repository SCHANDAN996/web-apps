101. Four of the following five are alike in a certain way and form a group. Which one does not belong to the group?
(a) Mercury (b) Venus (c) Moon (d) Mars
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to treat all four as members of the solar system and reject the question. Mercury, Venus and Mars are planets, while the Moon is a natural satellite of the Earth, so the answer is Moon.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

102. Find the odd one out.
(a) 121 (b) 169 (c) 144 (d) 150
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that 150 is a round number and looks harmless beside the others. 121 = 11², 144 = 12² and 169 = 13² are perfect squares, while 150 is not a perfect square, so the answer is 150.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

103. Which of the following does not belong to the group?
(a) Copper (b) Iron (c) Zinc (d) Brass
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that brass looks like a metal name in the same list. Copper, zinc and iron are pure metallic elements, while brass is an alloy of copper and zinc, so the answer is Brass.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

104. Find the odd pair of words.
(a) Doctor : Patient (b) Teacher : Student (c) Carpenter : Wood (d) Lawyer : Client
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that all four pairs seem to show a worker and something connected with work. In three pairs the second word is a person who receives a service; wood is a material, not a person, so the answer is Carpenter : Wood.
Source: PYQ-style

105. Four of the following are alike. Find the odd one.
(a) 100 (b) 27 (c) 64 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that 100 is a perfect square, which distracts from the real pattern. 8 = 2³, 27 = 3³ and 64 = 4³ are perfect cubes, while 100 is not a perfect cube, so the answer is 100.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

106. Which word does not belong to the group?

107. Find the odd one out.
(a) 51 (b) 23 (c) 37 (d) 17
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that 51 looks prime at a glance. 17, 23 and 37 are prime numbers, while 51 = 3 × 17 is composite, so the answer is 51.

109. Find the odd pair.
(a) 6 : 38 (b) 4 : 16 (c) 5 : 25 (d) 3 : 9
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that 38 is close to 36 and can be misread in a hurry. In three pairs the second number is the square of the first: 3² = 9, 4² = 16, 5² = 25; but 6² = 36, not 38, so the answer is 6 : 38.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

110. Which number does not belong to the group?
(a) 6 (b) 45 (c) 28 (d) 496
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to check only divisibility or size. 6, 28 and 496 are perfect numbers (each equals the sum of its proper divisors, e.g. 1+2+4+7+14 = 28), while 45 is not a perfect number, so the answer is 45.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

112. Which of the following does not belong to the group?
(a) Rectangle (b) Square (c) Triangle (d) Circle
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that all are geometrical figures. Triangle, square and rectangle are polygons bounded by straight line segments, while a circle is bounded by a curve, so the answer is Circle.

113. Find the odd pair of numbers.
(a) 5 : 20 (b) 4 : 16 (c) 6 : 36 (d) 3 : 9
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that 20 is a multiple of 5, which looks acceptable. In the other pairs the second number is the square of the first: 3² = 9, 4² = 16, 6² = 36; but 5² = 25, not 20, so the answer is 5 : 20.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

115. Find the odd one out.
(a) 482 (b) 264 (c) 132 (d) 396
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that all are three-digit even numbers. 132, 264 and 396 are divisible by 11 (alternating digit sums: 1−3+2 = 0, 2−6+4 = 0, 3−9+6 = 0), while 482 gives 4−8+2 = −2 and is not divisible by 11, so the answer is 482.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

120. Which of the following does not belong to the group?
(a) Gold (b) Diamond (c) Silver (d) Platinum
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that all four are precious and expensive. Gold, silver and platinum are precious metals (chemical elements), while diamond is an allotrope of carbon, a gemstone and not a metal, so the answer is Diamond.

121. Find the odd one out.
(a) 123 (b) 231 (c) 312 (d) 421
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that all are three-digit numbers with distinct digits. 123, 231 and 312 are formed by the same digits 1, 2 and 3 in different orders, while 421 contains the digit 4, so the answer is 421.
Source: PYQ-style

122. Which number does not belong to the group?
(a) 16 (b) 48 (c) 64 (d) 36
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that all are even composite numbers. 16 = 4², 36 = 6² and 64 = 8² are squares of even numbers, while 48 is not a perfect square, so the answer is 48.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

123. Find the odd pair.
(a) 3 : 12 (b) 2 : 6 (c) 5 : 25 (d) 4 : 20
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that 25 is a familiar multiple of 5. In three pairs the second number is the first multiplied by the next integer: 2 × 3 = 6, 3 × 4 = 12, 4 × 5 = 20; but 5 × 6 = 30, not 25, so the answer is 5 : 25.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

124. Which of the following does not belong to the group?
(a) 253 (b) 374 (c) 572 (d) 495
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that all are three-digit numbers divisible by 11. In 253, 374 and 495 the middle digit is the sum of the other two digits (2+3 = 5, 3+4 = 7, 4+5 = 9), while in 572 the middle digit 7 is not the sum of 5 and 2, so the answer is 572.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Find the odd one out.
(a) BD (b) MO (c) FH (d) JL
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that all pairs look like letters with a fixed gap. In BD, FH and JL the second letter is exactly 2 positions ahead of the first (B→D, F→H, J→L), while in MO the gap is also 2, so check further: B, F, J are at positions 2, 6, 10 forming an arithmetic progression of +4, and the next term should start at 14, i.e. NP, not MO; hence MO breaks the starting-letter series, so the answer is MO.
Source: PYQ-style
