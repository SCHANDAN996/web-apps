51. Choose the odd one out.
(a) Copper (b) Brass (c) Silver (d) Iron
Answer: (b)
Solution: Brass is tempting because it is a metal like the others, but it is an alloy of copper and zinc, not a pure element. Copper, iron and silver are all pure metallic elements, so the answer is Brass.

53. Choose the odd one out.
(a) 225 (b) 144 (c) 169 (d) 121
Answer: (a)
Solution: All four are perfect squares, so the trap is to stop checking there. 121 = 11², 144 = 12², 169 = 13² are squares of consecutive numbers, while 225 = 15² breaks the sequence (14² = 196 is missing), so the answer is 225.
