101. Doctor : Stethoscope :: Carpenter : ?
(a) Plough (b) Saw (c) Scalpel (d) Telescope
Answer: (b)
Solution: A stethoscope is the primary tool of a doctor; a saw is a primary tool of a carpenter. The tempting option is scalpel, but that belongs to a surgeon, not a carpenter, so the answer is Saw.

102. Book : Pages :: Wall : ?
(a) Bricks (b) Cement (c) Roof (d) Paint
Answer: (a)
Solution: A book is composed of pages; a wall is composed of bricks. Cement is only a binding material, not the constituent unit, so the answer is Bricks.

103. Thermometer : Temperature :: Barometer : ?
(a) Humidity (b) Wind speed (c) Atmospheric pressure (d) Rainfall
Answer: (c)
Solution: A thermometer measures temperature; a barometer measures atmospheric pressure. Humidity is measured by a hygrometer, which is the trap here, so the answer is Atmospheric pressure.

104. Bird : Nest :: Lion : ?
(a) Cave (b) Hive (c) Burrow (d) Den
Answer: (d)
Solution: A bird lives in a nest; a lion lives in a den. A burrow is for rabbits and a hive for bees, so the answer is Den.

105. Water : Thirst :: Food : ?

Answer: (c)

125. Virus : Disease :: Famine : ?
(a) Hunger (b) Drought (c) Poverty (d) Crop
Answer: (a)
Solution: A virus causes disease; famine causes hunger (starvation). Drought is a cause of famine, not its effect — the cause–effect reversal trap — so the answer is Hunger.
