1. Book : Pages :: Wall : ?
(a) Bricks (b) Cement (c) Roof (d) Paint
Answer: (a)
Solution: Cement is tempting because it is used in a wall, but it is only a binding material, not the unit that composes it. A book is made up of pages, and a wall is made up of bricks — the relation is "whole : constituent parts", so the answer is Bricks.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Doctor : Patient :: Teacher : ?
(a) Student (b) Classroom (c) Book (d) School
Answer: (a)
Solution: School and classroom are tempting because they are linked to teaching, but they are places, not recipients of service. A doctor treats a patient; a teacher teaches a student — the relation is "professional : recipient of service", so the answer is Student.

3. Bird : Nest :: Lion : ?
(a) Den (b) Cave (c) Burrow (d) Hive
Answer: (a)
Solution: Cave is tempting because lions are sometimes shown in caves, but a cave is generic and not the specific name of a lion's dwelling. The relation is "animal : its dwelling", and a lion lives in a den, so the answer is Den.

Solution: Pump is tempting because petrol comes from a pump, but a pump only supplies petrol; it is not the device that petrol powers. The relation is "consumable fuel : the working device it directly runs" — ink makes a pen write and petrol makes an engine run, so the answer is Engine.
