Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Doctor : Stethoscope :: Astronomer : ?
(a) Microscope (b) Periscope (c) Telescope (d) Stethometer
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is Microscope, because it is also a scientific instrument ending in "-scope", but a microscope is used by biologists, not astronomers. The relationship is "professional : characteristic instrument of observation". An astronomer observes distant celestial bodies with a telescope, so the answer is Telescope.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Ornithology : Birds :: Entomology : ?
(a) Reptiles (b) Fishes (c) Fossils (d) Insects
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Fossils, which sounds like a "-logy" subject (palaeontology), but the relationship is "branch of study : object studied". Ornithology is the study of birds; entomology is the study of insects. Reptiles are studied in herpetology and fishes in ichthyology, so the answer is Insects.

128. 8 : 72 :: 11 : ?
(a) 121 (b) 99 (c) 110 (d) 132
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 121, because 11 squared is 121 and candidates assume a squaring rule — but 72 is not 8 squared (64). The actual rule is n : n × (n + 1), since 8 × 9 = 72. Applying it, 11 × 12 = 132, so the answer is 132.

129. Thermometer : Temperature :: Barometer : ?
(a) Humidity (b) Atmospheric pressure (c) Wind speed (d) Earthquake intensity
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Humidity, because weather instruments are often memorised as a group and candidates confuse barometer with hygrometer. The relationship is "instrument : quantity it measures". A barometer measures atmospheric pressure; humidity is measured by a hygrometer, wind speed by an anemometer, and earthquake intensity by a seismograph, so the answer is Atmospheric pressure.

130. ACE : GIK :: BDF : ?
(a) GIK (b) HJL (c) HJK (d) IKM
Answer: (b)
Solution: Each letter of the first group is shifted forward by 6 positions: A→G, C→I, E→K. The tempting wrong choice is HJK, which results from shifting only the first letter correctly and then continuing carelessly. Applying +6 to B, D, F gives H, J, L, so the answer is HJL.

131. Moon : Satellite :: Earth : ?
(a) Star (b) Asteroid (c) Planet (d) Comet
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Star, because both the Sun and Earth are celestial bodies and candidates hastily pair "heavenly body : type" wrongly. The relationship is "celestial body : its classification". The Moon is a natural satellite; the Earth is a planet, so the answer is Planet.

132. 16 : 256 :: 12 : ?
(a) 124 (b) 96 (c) 1728 (d) 144
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 1728, which is 12 cubed, chosen by candidates who overthink and assume a cube relation. But 256 is 16 squared, so the rule is n : n². Hence 12² = 144, so the answer is 144.

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Case, because "lawyer handles a case" sounds parallel, but the relationship is "professional : the person who receives the service". A doctor treats a patient; a lawyer serves a client. Court is a place and judge is another professional, so the answer is Client.

134. Water : Thirst :: Food : ?
(a) Taste (b) Appetite (c) Energy (d) Hunger
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is Energy, since food gives energy, but the relationship is "thing : the need or sensation it removes". Water quenches thirst; food removes hunger. Appetite is a desire, not the exact counterpart of thirst, so the answer is Hunger.

135. 5 : 36 :: 7 : ?
(a) 64 (b) 49 (c) 56 (d) 42
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 49, which is simply 7 squared, but 36 is not 5 squared. The rule is n : (n + 1)², since (5 + 1)² = 36. Applying it, (7 + 1)² = 64, so the answer is 64.

(a) Hive (b) Cave (c) Burrow (d) Den
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is Cave, because lions are popularly shown in caves, but the standard term for a lion's shelter is a den. The relationship is "animal : its dwelling". A burrow belongs to rabbits and a hive to bees, so the answer is Den.

137. CAT : DBU :: DOG : ?
(a) EPG (b) FPH (c) EPH (d) DPH
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is FPH, which comes from shifting the first letter by 2 instead of 1. The rule is that every letter moves one step forward in the alphabet: C→D, A→B, T→U. Applying the same to DOG gives D→E, O→P, G→H, so the answer is EPH.

138. Pen : Write :: Knife : ?
(a) Paper (b) Cut (c) Sharp (d) Blade
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Blade, because a knife has a blade just as a pen has a nib, but the relationship is "tool : its function", not "tool : its part". A pen is used to write; a knife is used to cut. Sharp is a quality, not a function, so the answer is Cut.

(a) 65 (b) 60 (c) 63 (d) 64

140. Court : Justice :: School : ?
(a) Education (b) Student (c) Teacher (d) Book
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Teacher, because a school has teachers just as a court has judges, but the relationship is "institution : the abstract purpose it serves". A court delivers justice; a school delivers education. Teacher and student are persons, and book is an object, so the answer is Education.

141. 42 : 6 :: 56 : ?
(a) 7 (b) 9 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 7, because 42 ÷ 6 = 7 and candidates mechanically divide 56 by 8 to get 7, or pick the quotient of the first pair. The rule is "number : its factor such that the other factor is one more", since 42 = 6 × 7. For 56 = 7 × 8, the matching smaller factor is 7 and the larger is 8; since 6 was the smaller factor of 42, the parallel answer is the smaller factor 7 — but the option set pairs 56 with 8 as 56 ÷ 7 = 8 following the division reading 42 ÷ 7 = 6. Taking the consistent reading "first number divided by the answer's complement", 56 ÷ 7 = 8, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

142. Architect : Building :: Sculptor : ?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Chisel, because it is strongly associated with a sculptor, but the relationship is "creator : the thing created". An architect designs a building; a sculptor makes a statue. Stone is the raw material and museum is a place, so the answer is Statue.

143. Moon : Night :: Sun : ?

Answer: (c)

148. Preamble : Constitution :: Prologue : ?
(a) Index (b) Play (c) Chapter (d) Epilogue
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is Epilogue, because prologue and epilogue form a famous opposite pair, but the relationship is "introductory part : the whole it introduces", not opposites. A preamble introduces a constitution; a prologue introduces a play (or literary work). Chapter and index are other parts, so the answer is Play.

150. Odometer : Distance :: Ammeter : ?
(a) Voltage (b) Current (c) Resistance (d) Power
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is Voltage, because electrical instruments are often confused, but voltage is measured by a voltmeter. The relationship is "instrument : quantity measured". An odometer measures distance travelled; an ammeter measures electric current. Resistance is measured by an ohmmeter, so the answer is Current.
