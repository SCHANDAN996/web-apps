Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Doctor : Stethoscope :: Carpenter : ?
(a) Saw (b) Plough (c) Scalpel (d) Telescope
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is scalpel, because it is also a medical-looking tool and candidates confuse the two professions' instruments. The relationship is professional : primary tool. A doctor uses a stethoscope; a carpenter uses a saw, so the answer is Saw.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Book : Chapter :: Building : ?
(a) Brick (b) Cement (c) Storey (d) Architect
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is brick, which is a raw material, not a division. The relationship is whole : major internal division. A book is divided into chapters; a building is divided into storeys, so the answer is Storey.

78. Thermometer : Temperature :: Barometer : ?
(a) Humidity (b) Atmospheric pressure (c) Wind speed (d) Rainfall
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is humidity, because weather instruments are often lumped together; humidity is measured by a hygrometer. The relationship is instrument : quantity measured. A barometer measures atmospheric pressure, so the answer is Atmospheric pressure.
