51. Doctor : Patient :: Lawyer : ?
(a) Client (b) Accused (c) Customer (d) Magistrate
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is Accused, but a lawyer serves all clients, not only the accused — the relationship is professional : person served. A doctor serves a patient; a lawyer serves a client, so the answer is Client.
