Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Doctor : Patient :: Lawyer : ?
(a) Client (b) Accused (c) Customer (d) Magistrate
Answer: (a)
Solution: A doctor gives professional service to a patient; similarly a lawyer gives professional service to a client. "Accused" is tempting but a lawyer may also defend plaintiffs or advise companies, so the general professional relationship is with a client, so the answer is Client.
Source: PYQ-style (Verbal Analogy)

27. Book : Pages :: Wall : ?
(a) Bricks (b) Cement (c) Roof (d) House
Answer: (a)
Solution: A book is made up of pages; a wall is made up of bricks. Cement only joins bricks and a house is a larger whole, so the answer is Bricks.
Source: PYQ-style (Verbal Analogy)

28. Thermometer : Temperature :: Barometer : ?
(a) Wind speed (b) Rainfall (c) Humidity (d) Pressure
Answer: (d)
Solution: A thermometer measures temperature; a barometer measures atmospheric pressure. Humidity is measured by a hygrometer, so the answer is Pressure.
Source: PYQ-style (Instrument–Measurement)

29. Moon : Satellite :: Earth : ?
(a) Comet (b) Star (c) Asteroid (d) Planet
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Moon is a satellite (of Earth); the Earth is a planet (of the Sun). The category relationship gives Planet, so the answer is Planet.
Source: PYQ-style (Verbal Analogy)

30. Water : Thirst :: Food : ?
(a) Taste (b) Energy (c) Hunger (d) Weakness
Answer: (c)
Solution: Water removes thirst; food removes hunger. Energy is what food provides, not what it removes, so the answer is Hunger.
Source: PYQ-style (Verbal Analogy)

31. Bird : Nest :: Lion : ?
(a) Cave (b) Burrow (c) Den (d) Stable
Answer: (c)
Solution: A bird lives in a nest; a lion lives in a den. A cave is a tempting trap because lions may shelter in caves, but the standard dwelling term for a lion is a den, so the answer is Den.
Source: PYQ-style (Animal–Dwelling)

32. Pen : Write :: Knife : ?
(a) Cut (b) Steel (c) Blade (d) Sharp
Answer: (a)
Solution: A pen is used to write; a knife is used to cut. Sharp and blade are properties or parts, not functions, so the answer is Cut.
Source: PYQ-style (Tool–Function)

33. India : Tiger :: USA : ?
(a) Lion (b) Kangaroo (c) Bald Eagle (d) Panda
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tiger is the national animal of India; the bald eagle is the national bird and animal emblem of the USA. Kangaroo belongs to Australia and panda to China, so the answer is Bald Eagle.
Source: PYQ-style (Country–National Symbol)

34. Doctor : Stethoscope :: Carpenter : ?
(a) Needle (b) Plough (c) Hammer (d) Scalpel
Answer: (c)
Solution: A stethoscope is a tool of a doctor; a hammer is a tool of a carpenter. A scalpel belongs to a surgeon, so the answer is Hammer.
Source: PYQ-style (Person–Tool)

35. Ornithology : Birds :: Entomology : ?
(a) Insects (b) Fishes (c) Snakes (d) Fossils
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ornithology is the study of birds; entomology is the study of insects. Fishes are studied in ichthyology, so the answer is Insects.
Source: PYQ-style (Study–Subject)

36. Cow : Calf :: Dog : ?
(a) Puppy (b) Kitten (c) Cub (d) Foal
Answer: (a)
Solution: The young one of a cow is a calf; the young one of a dog is a puppy. Kitten is the young of a cat, so the answer is Puppy.
Source: PYQ-style (Animal–Young One)

37. 8 : 64 :: 7 : ?
(a) 42 (b) 49 (c) 48 (d) 56
Answer: (b)
Solution: The relation is n : n², since 8² = 64; hence 7² = 49. 56 is a trap for those who multiply 7 × 8, so the answer is 49.
Source: PYQ-style (Number Analogy)

38. 12 : 36 :: 15 : ?
(a) 40 (b) 50 (c) 45 (d) 60
Answer: (c)
Solution: The relation is n : 3n, since 12 × 3 = 36; hence 15 × 3 = 45. 60 is a trap from multiplying by 4, so the answer is 45.
Source: PYQ-style (Number Analogy)

39. 5 : 36 :: 8 : ?
(a) 100 (b) 81 (c) 64 (d) 72
Answer: (b)
Solution: The relation is n : (n+1)², since (5+1)² = 36; hence (8+1)² = 81. 64 is a trap for those who square 8 itself, so the answer is 81.
Source: PYQ-style (Number Analogy)

40. ACE : GIK :: MOQ : ?
(a) STW (b) SUW (c) SVX (d) RTV
Answer: (b)
Solution: Each letter moves +6: A→G, C→I, E→K. Applying +6 to M, O, Q gives S, U, W. SVX is a trap from adding 6, 7, 8, so the answer is SUW.
Source: PYQ-style (Letter Analogy)

41. DF : UW :: GI : ?
(a) RT (b) TR (c) QS (d) RP
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each letter is replaced by its reverse-position letter (positions summing to 27): D(4)→W(23), F(6)→U(21), and the pair is then written in reverse order, giving UW. So G(7)→T(20) and I(9)→R(18), reversed to give RT. TR is a trap from forgetting the order reversal seen in the first pair, so the answer is RT.
Source: PYQ-style (Letter Analogy)

42. CAT : FDW :: DOG : ?
(a) FQI (b) HRL (c) GRJ (d) GQJ
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each letter moves +3: C→F, A→D, T→W. Applying +3 to D, O, G gives G, R, J. GQJ is a trap from shifting the middle letter by only +2, so the answer is GRJ.
Source: PYQ-style (Letter Analogy)

43. Flower : Bouquet :: Ship : ?
(a) Sailor (b) Ocean (c) Harbour (d) Fleet
Answer: (d)
Solution: A bouquet is a collection of flowers; a fleet is a collection of ships. Harbour is a tempting trap but it is a place where ships stay, not a group of ships, so the answer is Fleet.
Source: PYQ-style (Unit–Collection)

44. Paddy : Rice :: Wheat : ?
(a) Bread (b) Flour (c) Biscuit (d) Grain
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rice is obtained directly by milling paddy; flour is obtained directly by grinding wheat. Bread is a tempting trap but it is a later processed product made from flour, so the answer is Flour.
Source: PYQ-style (Raw–Product)

45. Disease : Pathology :: Earthquake : ?
(a) Geology (b) Seismology (c) Volcanology (d) Oceanography
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pathology is the study of diseases; seismology is the study of earthquakes. Geology is a tempting trap but it studies the Earth in general, so the answer is Seismology.
Source: PYQ-style (Study–Subject)

46. 16 : 240 :: 20 : ?
(a) 380 (b) 400 (c) 360 (d) 420
Answer: (a)
Solution: The relation is n : n² − n, since 16² − 16 = 240; hence 20² − 20 = 380. 400 is a trap for those who simply square 20, so the answer is 380.
Source: PYQ-style (Number Analogy)

47. Optimist : Pessimist :: Zenith : ?
(a) Horizon (b) Nadir (c) Summit (d) Peak
Answer: (b)
Solution: Optimist and pessimist are antonyms; the antonym of zenith (highest point) is nadir (lowest point). Peak and summit are synonyms, which is the trap, so the answer is Nadir.
Source: PYQ-style (Antonym Analogy)

48. Fragile : Break :: Ductile : ?
(a) Bend (b) Melt (c) Rust (d) Draw into wire
Answer: (d)
Solution: A fragile thing easily breaks; a ductile thing can easily be drawn into wire. Bending relates to malleability or flexibility, which is the trap, so the answer is Draw into wire.
Source: PYQ-style (Property–Behaviour)

49. 3 : 28 :: 4 : ?
(a) 68 (b) 60 (c) 63 (d) 65
Answer: (d)
Solution: The relation is n : n³ + 1, since 3³ + 1 = 28; hence 4³ + 1 = 65. 63 is a trap from computing 4³ − 1, so the answer is 65.
Source: PYQ-style (Number Analogy)

50. Editor : Magazine :: Director : ?
(a) Studio (b) Theatre (c) Actor (d) Film
Answer: (d)
Solution: An editor produces and shapes a magazine; a director produces and shapes a film. Actor and studio are connected to films but are not the finished creation, so the answer is Film.
Source: PYQ-style (Person–Creation)
