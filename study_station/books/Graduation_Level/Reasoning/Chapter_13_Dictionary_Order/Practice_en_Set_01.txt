1. Which word will come first in a dictionary?
(a) Mango (b) Market (c) Magnet (d) Master
Answer: (c)
Solution: All words start with "Ma", so compare the third letter: n, r, s, g. The letter "g" comes earliest in the alphabet. The tempting option "Mango" looks similar, but its third letter "n" comes after "g". So the answer is Magnet.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Which word will come last in a dictionary?
(a) Pen (b) Pencil (c) Penny (d) People
Answer: (d)
Solution: All start with "Pe"; compare third letters: People has "o" while the other three have "n". Since "o" comes after "n", People comes last. The tempting option "Penny" feels longest among the "Pen" words, but its third letter "n" comes before "o". So the answer is People.

(a) Can, Cap, Car, Cat (b) Car, Cat, Can, Cap (c) Cat, Car, Cap, Can (d) Cap, Car, Cat, Can
Answer: (a)
Solution: All begin with "Ca"; compare third letters: t, r, p, n. Alphabetical order of the third letters is n, p, r, t. The trap is reading the words by familiarity instead of comparing the third letter. So the answer is Can, Cap, Car, Cat.
