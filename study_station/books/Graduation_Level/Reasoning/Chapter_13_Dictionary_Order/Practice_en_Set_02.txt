26. Arrange the following words in dictionary order: Pigeon, Pillar, Pillow, Pilot
(a) Pillow, Pillar, Pilot, Pigeon (b) Pillar, Pillow, Pilot, Pigeon (c) Pigeon, Pillar, Pillow, Pilot (d) Pilot, Pigeon, Pillar, Pillow
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is to compare only the third letters and put Pillar first, but the second letters decide first: 'g' in Pigeon comes before 'l' in the other three. Among the Pil- words, Pillar < Pillow < Pilot, so the answer is Pigeon, Pillar, Pillow, Pilot.
