101. Which word will come last in dictionary order?
(a) Superfluous (b) Superlative (c) Superintendent (d) Superficial
Answer: (b)
Solution: In dictionary order the words are Superficial, Superfluous, Superintendent, Superlative. The trap is Superintendent, which differs only a few letters later; the word in last place is Superlative, so the answer is Superlative.
