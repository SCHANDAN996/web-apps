1. Statements: A > B, B ≥ C, C < D
Conclusions: I. A > C  II. D > B
(a) Both I and II follow (b) Only II follows (c) Only I follows (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: From A > B and B ≥ C, we get A > C, so conclusion I follows. For II, C < D does not connect D with B, so D > B cannot be established. Hence the answer is Only I follows.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Statements: P = Q, Q < R, R ≤ S
Conclusions: I. P < S  II. S > Q
(a) Both I and II follow (b) Only II follows (c) Only I follows (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: P = Q < R ≤ S gives P < S, so I follows. Also S ≥ R > Q gives S > Q, so II follows. Hence the answer is Both I and II follow.
