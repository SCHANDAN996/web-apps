26. Statements: P ≥ Q = R > S; T < S ≤ U
Conclusions: I. P > T  II. R > U
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is conclusion II: R > S and S ≤ U give no fixed relation between R and U, since U could be greater than R. For I, P ≥ Q = R > S > T, so P > T is definite. Only conclusion I follows, so the answer is "Only I follows".
