76. Statements: P > Q ≥ R = S; S < T ≤ U
Conclusions: I. P > S  II. Q ≥ T
(a) Neither I nor II follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Only I follows
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is conclusion II: since Q ≥ R = S and S < T, we cannot link Q and T, so II does not follow. For I, P > Q ≥ R = S gives P > S directly, so the answer is that only I follows.
Source: PYQ-style
