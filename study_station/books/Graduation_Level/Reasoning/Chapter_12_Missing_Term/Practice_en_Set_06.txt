126. Find the missing term: 6, 5, 9, 10, 12, 20, ?
(a) 14 (b) 11 (c) 19 (d) 15
Answer: (d)
Solution: Two series are mixed: odd places 6, 9, 12 (+3) and even places 5, 10, 20 (×2). The 7th term is an odd place: 12 + 3 = 15. Test the rule on every term before choosing; so the answer is 15.
