76. Statement: "Buy our new smartphone X — it has the best camera in its price segment," an advertisement claims.
Assumptions:
I. Camera quality is an important factor for buyers in this price segment.
II. No other smartphone in this price segment has a comparable camera.
III. The advertisement will increase the sales of smartphone X.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only I and II are implicit (c) Only I and III are implicit (d) All I, II and III are implicit
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is III — increased sales is the hoped-for result of the advertisement, not something assumed in making the claim. Calling the camera the "best in its price segment" directly presupposes that rival phones in the segment lack a comparable camera (II), and highlighting the camera assumes buyers care about it (I), so the answer is that only I and II are implicit.
