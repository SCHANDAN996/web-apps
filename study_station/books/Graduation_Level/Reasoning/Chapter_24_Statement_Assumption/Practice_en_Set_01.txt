1. Statement: "Join our coaching institute to clear the SSC exam in your first attempt." — an advertisement.
Assumptions:
I. Coaching institutes help students clear exams.
II. It is possible to clear the SSC exam in the first attempt.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Neither I nor II is implicit (d) Both I and II are implicit
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing only one assumption; the advertisement exists because the institute believes coaching helps (I) and that first-attempt success is achievable (II). Both assumptions underlie the appeal, so the answer is both I and II are implicit.
Source: PYQ-style
