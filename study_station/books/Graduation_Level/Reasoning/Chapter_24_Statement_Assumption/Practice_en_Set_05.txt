101. Statement: "Buy two notebooks and get one pen free," an advertisement by a stationery shop.
Assumptions:
I. The offer will attract more customers to the shop.
II. Customers are interested in getting free items.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both I and II are implicit (d) Neither I nor II is implicit
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to think only one assumption holds because the ad mentions only one offer. But any promotional offer assumes both that it will draw customers and that customers value free items; without either, the offer is pointless. So the answer is that both I and II are implicit.

(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both I and II are implicit (d) Neither I nor II is implicit
