Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Statement: "Buy two, get one free" — an advertisement by a shoe company.
Assumptions:
I. The offer will attract customers.
II. The company has sufficient stock to support the offer.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Only II is implicit (c) Both I and II are implicit (d) Neither I nor II is implicit
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is to reject II as "too detailed", but any such offer presumes the company can actually supply the free item, otherwise the campaign collapses. An advertisement is made to attract customers, so I is implicit, and the offer's feasibility presumes adequate stock, so II is also implicit. Hence the answer is that both I and II are implicit.
Source: PYQ-style (Bank PO pattern)

52. Statement: The municipal corporation advised citizens to boil drinking water during the monsoon.

II. Boiling water makes it safe to drink.
(a) Only I is implicit (b) Both I and II are implicit (c) Only II is implicit (d) Neither I nor II is implicit
