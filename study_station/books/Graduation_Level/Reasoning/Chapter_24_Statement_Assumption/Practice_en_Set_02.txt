26. Statement: "Join our spoken English course and get a job in a multinational company," an advertisement claims.
Assumptions:
I. Spoken English skills help in getting jobs in multinational companies.
II. People who join the course will definitely get jobs.
(a) Only II is implicit (b) Only I is implicit (c) Both I and II are implicit (d) Neither I nor II is implicit
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting trap is II — advertisements exaggerate, and a guaranteed job is something no advertiser can logically assume; only the underlying belief that spoken English improves job prospects is needed for the ad to make sense. Hence only I is implicit, so the answer is "Only I is implicit".
