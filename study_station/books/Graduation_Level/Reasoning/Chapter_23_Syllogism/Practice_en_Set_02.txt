26. Statements: All pens are books. Some books are chairs.
Conclusions: I. Some pens are chairs. II. Some chairs are books.
(a) Only I follows (b) Neither follows (c) Only II follows (d) Both follow
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is Conclusion I — "All pens are books" plus "Some books are chairs" does not guarantee any overlap between pens and chairs, since the books that are chairs may lie outside the pens circle. Conclusion II is just the converse of the second statement, which always holds for "some". So the answer is Only II follows.
Source: PYQ-style
