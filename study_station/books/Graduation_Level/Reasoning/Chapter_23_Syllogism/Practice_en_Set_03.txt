51. Statements: All pens are books. Some books are chairs.
Conclusions: I. Some pens are chairs. II. Some chairs are books.
(a) Only I follows (b) Both follow (c) Only II follows (d) Neither follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is Conclusion I — "All pens are books" plus "Some books are chairs" does not guarantee any overlap between pens and chairs, since the books that are chairs may lie outside the pens circle. Conclusion II is just the converse of the second statement, so it definitely follows. Hence only II follows, so the answer is (c).
