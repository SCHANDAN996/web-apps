1. Statements: All pens are books. All books are chairs.
Conclusions: I. All pens are chairs. II. All chairs are pens.
(a) Only II follows (b) Only I follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option claims both follow, but "All chairs are pens" reverses the chain, which is never valid. Since pens ⊂ books ⊂ chairs, all pens are chairs, so the answer is Only I follows.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Statements: Some dogs are cats. All cats are animals.
Conclusions: I. Some dogs are animals. II. All dogs are animals.
(a) Neither follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both follow (d) Only I follows
Answer: (d)
Solution: "All dogs are animals" is the trap — "some" can never be stretched to "all". The dogs that are cats are animals, so some dogs are animals, and the answer is Only I follows.
