101. Which letter is 9th to the right of the letter which is 5th to the left of M in the English alphabet?
(a) O (b) Q (c) R (d) P
Answer: (b)
Solution: 5th to the left of M (13th) is H (8th), and 9th to the right of H is the 17th letter, Q. The trap is counting M itself while moving left, which wrongly gives I and then R. So the answer is Q.
Source: PYQ-style

102. If the alphabet is written in reverse order, which letter will be 5th to the right of the 8th letter from the left end?
(a) M (b) O (c) L (d) N
Answer: (d)
Solution: The reversed row begins Z, Y, X, W, V, U, T, S, so the 8th letter from the left is S. Moving 5 to the right gives R, Q, P, O, N. The trap is counting in the normal alphabet instead of the reversed one. So the answer is N.

103. How many pairs of letters in the word "MASTER" have as many letters between them in the word as in the English alphabet?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The matching pairs are M–R (positions 1 and 6, four letters between in both), S–T (adjacent in both, zero between) and T–R (positions 4 and 6, one letter between in both). The trap is checking only forward alphabetical pairs and missing backward ones like T–R. So the answer is 3.

105. Which letter is exactly midway between the 9th letter from the left and the 8th letter from the right in the English alphabet?
(a) O (b) M (c) L (d) N
Answer: (d)
Solution: 9th from the left is I (position 9), and 8th from the right is position 26 − 8 + 1 = 19, i.e., S. Midway between 9 and 19 is position 14, which is N. The trap is taking 8th from the right as position 18 by forgetting the +1. So the answer is N.

Solution: D (4) ↔ 27 − 4 = 23 = W; O (15) ↔ 12 = L; G (7) ↔ 20 = T, giving WLT. The trap is using 26 − n instead of 27 − n, which produces XMU-type wrong options. So the answer is WLT.

116. How many letters in the word "PSYCHOLOGY" have a vertical line of symmetry in capital form?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 3
Answer: (b)

The trap is counting C as vertically symmetric (it is only horizontally symmetric), which inflates the count to 6. So the answer is 5.

124. If each consonant in the alphabet is replaced by the next consonant and each vowel by the next vowel, how is "BOAT" coded?

Answer: (c)
Solution: B shifts to the next consonant C, O shifts to the next vowel U, A shifts to the next vowel E, and T shifts to the next consonant V, giving CUEV. The trap is shifting vowels to the next alphabet letter (O→P, A→B) instead of the next vowel. So the answer is CUEV.
