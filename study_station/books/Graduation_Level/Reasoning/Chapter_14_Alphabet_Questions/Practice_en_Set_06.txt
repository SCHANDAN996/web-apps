126. If the letters of the word "CORRESPONDENCE" are arranged in alphabetical order, how many letters will remain in the same position as in the original word?
(a) None (b) One (c) Two (d) Three
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is eyeballing instead of writing both sequences. Original: C O R R E S P O N D E N C E; sorted: C C D E E E N N O O P R R S. Only position 1 (C) and position 5 (E) match, so the answer is Two.

127. In the English alphabet, which letter is 7th to the right of the 15th letter from the left?

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is counting the 15th letter itself as the first step to the right. The 15th letter is O; moving 7 steps right gives P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, so the answer is V.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is arranging all 5 letters freely and ignoring the restriction. The odd positions are 1, 3 and 5; the 2 vowels E and O can be placed in them in 3 × 2 = 6 ways, and the 3 consonants fill the rest in 3! = 6 ways, giving 6 × 6 = 36, so the answer is 36.
