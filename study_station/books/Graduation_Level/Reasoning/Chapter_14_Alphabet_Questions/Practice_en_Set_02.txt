Questions 26–50: Medium

26. In the English alphabet, which letter is exactly midway between the 7th letter from the left and the 6th letter from the right?
(a) O (b) M (c) L (d) N
Answer: (d)
Solution: The 7th letter from the left is G (position 7) and the 6th from the right is at position 27 − 6 = 21, which is U. The midpoint is (7 + 21)/2 = 14, the 14th letter, N. The trap is averaging 7 and 6 and picking M without converting the right-side position first. The midway letter is N, so the answer is N.
Source: PYQ-style

27. How many pairs of letters in the word "COMPUTER" have as many letters between them in the word as there are in the English alphabet?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Checking pairs in both directions: M-O (one letter between them in both) ✓; O-P (consecutive in the alphabet, adjacent in the word) ✓; T-U (consecutive in the alphabet, adjacent in the word) ✓; R-U (two letters between them in both) ✓. The trap is stopping after the obvious pairs and missing T-U, which sits inside the word and is easy to overlook. There are 4 such pairs, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

28. If the letters of the word "LEADING" are arranged in alphabetical order, how many letters remain in the same position as in the original word?
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Original: L E A D I N G; alphabetical order: A D E G I L N. Comparing position by position, only position 5 has the same letter (I) in both. The trap is matching letters like E or G that appear in both arrangements but at different positions. Only one letter stays fixed, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

29. In a certain code, the alphabet is written in reverse order (Z=1, Y=2, …, A=26). What is the 13th letter in this new arrangement?
(a) L (b) N (c) M (d) O
Answer: (b)
Solution: In the reversed alphabet, position n corresponds to the normal letter at position 27 − n. So the 13th letter in reverse order is the normal 14th letter, N. The trap is taking the normal 13th letter M and forgetting the reversal. The 13th letter in the reversed order is N, so the answer is N.
Source: PYQ-style

30. Which of the following words will appear third in a dictionary?
(a) GRAPES (b) GRANITE (c) GRAVITY (d) GRAND
Answer: (a)
Solution: All four begin with GRA, so compare the 4th letter: V, P, N, N. The two N-words come first; between GRAND and GRANITE the 5th letter decides (D before I), giving the order GRAND, GRANITE, GRAPES, GRAVITY. The trap is stopping at the 4th letter and placing GRANITE before GRAND. The third word is GRAPES, so the answer is GRAPES.

31. If every alternate letter of the English alphabet starting from B is removed (B, D, F, …), which letter will be the 10th in the remaining series?
(a) Q (b) R (c) P (d) S
Answer: (d)
Solution: Removing the even-positioned letters leaves A, C, E, G, I, K, M, O, Q, S, U, W, Y. The 10th term is the letter at normal position 2 × 10 − 1 = 19, which is S. The trap is removing the odd letters instead, which gives R. The 10th remaining letter is S, so the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style

32. In the word "ESTABLISH", how many pairs of letters have as many letters between them in the word (in forward direction only) as in the alphabet?
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 3 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: In E S T A B L I S H, the pair S-T is consecutive in the alphabet and adjacent in the word, and the pair A-B is also consecutive in the alphabet and adjacent in the word. No other forward pair matches. The trap is counting H-I or the second S with T, neither of which works in the forward direction. There are 2 such pairs, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

33. If A = 1, B = 2, …, Z = 26, what is the sum of the letter values of the word "CAB"?
(a) 9 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: C = 3, A = 1, B = 2, so the sum is 3 + 1 + 2 = 6. The trap is counting C as 4 by starting the alphabet from 0, which gives 7. The sum is 6, so the answer is 6.

34. In the English alphabet, which letter is 7th to the right of the 10th letter from the left?
(a) O (b) P (c) R (d) Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The 10th letter from the left is J (position 10); moving 7 steps right gives position 10 + 7 = 17, which is Q. The trap is counting J itself as the first step, which lands on P. The required letter is Q, so the answer is Q.

35. How many meaningful English words can be formed using the letters E, A, T (using each letter only once)?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The possible arrangements are EAT, ATE, TEA, ETA, AET, TAE, of which EAT, ATE and TEA are meaningful words. The trap is counting ETA, which is an abbreviation, not a common word, to make 4. Exactly 3 meaningful words can be formed, so the answer is 3.

36. If the positions of two letters are interchanged — the 4th letter with the 24th, and the 7th with the 17th — which letter will now be at the 17th position?
(a) G (b) Q (c) D (d) X
Answer: (a)
Solution: Only the specified swaps occur: the 7th letter G and the 17th letter Q change places, so position 17 now holds G. The trap is applying both swaps to position 17 or assuming the whole alphabet is reversed, which would give X or D. After the swap, position 17 has G, so the answer is G.

37. If the word "KNOWLEDGE" is written with its letters in reverse order, which letter will be 4th to the left of the 3rd letter from the right end?
(a) G (b) E (c) D (d) L
Answer: (c)
Solution: The reversed word is E G D E L W O N K. The 3rd letter from the right is N (position 7 from the left), and 4 steps to its left lands on position 3, which is D. The trap is counting N itself as the first step, which lands on E. The letter is D, so the answer is D.

38. What should come in place of the question mark in the series: Z, X, V, T, ?
(a) S (b) R (c) Q (d) P
Answer: (b)
Solution: The series moves backward by 2 letters each time: Z(26), X(24), V(22), T(20), so the next letter is at position 18, which is R. The trap is assuming a step of −1 after seeing consecutive-looking letters, which gives S. The next letter is R, so the answer is R.

39. In the series: A, C, F, J, O, ? — which letter comes next?
(a) T (b) U (c) S (d) V
Answer: (b)
Solution: The gaps increase by 1 each time: A→C (+2), C→F (+3), F→J (+4), J→O (+5), so the next gap is +6: O(15) + 6 = 21 = U. The trap is repeating +5, which gives T. The next letter is U, so the answer is U.

40. Find the missing letter: B, E, I, N, ?
(a) S (b) T (c) R (d) U
Answer: (b)
Solution: The gaps are +3, +4, +5, so the next gap is +6: N(14) + 6 = 20 = T. The tempting wrong choice S comes from repeating the +5 gap instead of increasing it. The missing letter is T, so the answer is T.

41. If the first half of the English alphabet is written in reverse order, which letter will be 8th from the left?
(a) F (b) G (c) H (d) E
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first half A–M reversed gives M, L, K, J, I, H, G, F, E, D, C, B, A, followed by N to Z unchanged. Counting to the 8th position gives F. The trap is reversing the whole alphabet, which would give S. The 8th letter is F, so the answer is F.

42. If the second half of the English alphabet is written in reverse order, which letter will be 20th from the left?
(a) U (b) H (c) G (d) T
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first 13 letters A–M stay in place, and positions 14 onward hold Z, Y, X, W, V, U, T, … Position 20 is the 7th letter of this reversed second half, which is T. The trap is reversing the entire alphabet and computing 27 − 20 = 7 = G. With only the second half reversed, the 20th letter is T, so the answer is T.

43. In the word "RIGHTEOUS", if the first and fifth letters are interchanged, and the second and sixth letters are interchanged, which letter will be third from the right?
(a) E (b) U (c) S (d) O
Answer: (d)
Solution: Original: R I G H T E O U S. Swapping positions 1 and 5 gives T I G H R E O U S; then swapping positions 2 and 6 gives T E G H R I O U S. Counting from the right: S (1st), U (2nd), O (3rd). The trap is performing both swaps mentally at once and losing track, which gives E. The third letter from the right is O, so the answer is O.
Source: PYQ-style

44. How many letters in the word "DANGER" are at the same position in the word as their position in the alphabet (i.e., letter value equals word position)?

Solution: Checking each letter: D is at word position 1 but alphabet position 4; A at 2 vs 1; N at 3 vs 14; G at 4 vs 7; E at 5 vs 5 ✓; R at 6 vs 18. Only E matches. The trap is assuming D matches because it is the 4th letter of the alphabet, but it sits at position 1 in the word. Only one letter matches, so the answer is 1.

45. In the English alphabet, which letter is exactly midway between the 5th letter from the left and the 10th letter from the right?
(a) K (b) L (c) J (d) M
Answer: (a)
Solution: The 5th letter from the left is E (position 5), and the 10th from the right is at position 27 − 10 = 17, which is Q. The midpoint is (5 + 17)/2 = 11, the 11th letter, K. The trap is using 10 instead of converting the right-side position, which gives a midpoint near H. The midway letter is K, so the answer is K.

46. If the letters of the alphabet are assigned values A = 26, B = 25, …, Z = 1, what is the value of the word "BAD"?
(a) 76 (b) 72 (c) 74 (d) 70
Answer: (c)
Solution: Under reverse values, each letter is worth 27 minus its normal position: B = 25, A = 26, D = 23. The sum is 25 + 26 + 23 = 74. The trap is adding the normal values (2 + 1 + 4 = 7) or subtracting the normal total from 81 incorrectly. The value of the word is 74, so the answer is 74.

48. Find the next term: AZ, BY, CX, DW, ?

Answer: (c)

49. Find the missing term: CE, FH, IK, LN, ?
(a) OP (b) NP (c) PQ (d) OQ
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first letters of the pairs advance by +3: C, F, I, L, O; the second letters also advance by +3: E, H, K, N, Q. So the missing term is OQ. The trap is advancing only +2 within the pair pattern and writing OP. The missing term is OQ, so the answer is OQ.

50. If the word "EDUCATION" is arranged with vowels first (in their original order) followed by consonants (in their original order), which letter will be exactly in the middle of the new arrangement?
(a) O (b) I (c) D (d) A
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vowels in original order: E, U, A, I, O (5 vowels); consonants in original order: D, C, T, N (4 consonants). The new arrangement is E U A I O D C T N, which has 9 letters, so the middle is the 5th letter, O. The trap is taking the middle of the original word or stopping the vowel list early, which gives A or I. The middle letter is O, so the answer is O.
