Card 1
Front: What is the fastest way to find the position of any letter from the right end of the alphabet?
Back: Subtract its left-end position from 27. Example: K is 11th from the left, so 27 − 11 = 16th from the right.

Card 2
Front: Which five "anchor letters" should you memorise to locate any letter in under 5 seconds?
Back: E(5), J(10), O(15), T(20), Y(25) — the multiples of 5. From the nearest anchor, count forward or backward.

Card 3
Front: What does the mnemonic "EJOTY" help you remember?
Back: The letters at positions 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 — E, J, O, T, Y. It is the master key for all position-based alphabet questions.

Card 4
Front: How do you get the reverse (right-end) positions of E, J, O, T, Y instantly?
Back: Apply 27 − position: E→22, J→17, O→12, T→7, Y→2. No second list to memorise — the 27-minus trick covers everything.

Card 5
Front: In "which letter is 7th to the right of the 10th letter from the left," what is the one-line calculation?
Back: Same end, same direction → add: 10 + 7 = 17th from the left = Q.

Card 6
Front: In "which letter is 5th to the left of the 9th letter from the right," how do the directions combine?
Back: Opposite directions (left of a right-end position) → add: 9 + 5 = 14th from the right = 27 − 14 = 13th from the left = M.

Card 7
Front: Examiner's trap: "7th to the right of the 10th letter from the RIGHT" — why do most students get this wrong?
Back: They add 10 + 7 = 17 and answer Q. But both are from the right, so it is 10 − 7 = 3rd from the right = X. Same end + same direction means subtract, not add.

Card 8
Front: When the alphabet is written in reverse order, what is the shortcut for "the letter which is Nth from the left"?
Back: It is the (27 − N)th letter of the normal alphabet. Example: 5th from left in reversed series = 22nd letter = V.

Card 9
Front: How many letters of the English alphabet look the same in a mirror (vertical mirror image)?
Back: 11 letters: A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y.

Card 10
Front: Which letters read the same when seen in water (horizontal flip / water image)?
Back: 8 letters in standard exam usage: B, C, D, E, H, I, O, X. (K is sometimes included depending on font, but the standard exam list has 8.)

Card 11
Front: In letter-pair counting questions (e.g., "how many pairs in the word have as many letters between them as in the alphabet"), what must you always check?
Back: Both directions — forward and backward — and count each qualifying pair only once. Missing backward pairs is the most common error.

Card 12
Front: Examiner's trap: In "meaningful word" questions, why is forming ANY word not enough?
Back: The question usually demands a word using ALL the given letters (or specific positions like 1st, 3rd, 5th). A word using only some letters, or wrong positions, scores zero.

Card 13
Front: If every alternate letter is dropped from the alphabet starting with A, which letters remain?
Back: B, D, F, H, J, L, N, P, R, T, V, X, Z — 13 letters (the even-positioned letters).

Card 14
Front: What is the middle letter of the English alphabet, and why is the question itself a trap?
Back: There is no single middle letter — 26 is even, so M (13th) and N (14th) share the middle. Any option naming one letter alone is wrong.

Card 15
Front: In "arrange the letters to form a word, then find the middle letter" questions, what is the safe first step?
Back: First form the meaningful word and write it out fully; only then count to the middle. Guessing the middle from jumbled letters causes errors.

Card 16
Front: Which letter is exactly midway between the 4th and the 20th letters of the alphabet?
Back: (4 + 20)/2 = 12th letter = L. Midpoint formula: average the two positions; if the average is a fraction, there is no single middle letter.

Card 17
Front: In dictionary-order questions with words like APPLE, APPLY, APPROACH, what decides the order after a common prefix?
Back: Compare the first letter where the words differ, position by position. APPLE vs APPLY: differ at 5th letter (E < Y), so APPLE comes first.

Card 18
Front: What is the 15-second rule for alphabet position questions in the exam?
Back: If EJOTY + the 27-minus trick gives the answer in one step, attempt immediately; if it needs writing the full series or pair-counting in a long word, mark it and return after the quick wins.
