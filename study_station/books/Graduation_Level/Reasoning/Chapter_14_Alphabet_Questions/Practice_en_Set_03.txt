Questions 51–75: Medium

51. In the English alphabet, which letter is exactly midway between the 5th letter from the left and the 21st letter from the left?
(a) M (b) L (c) N (d) K
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option L comes from misreading position 13 as L; the 13th letter is actually M. Midway position = (5 + 21)/2 = 13, and the 13th letter is M, so the answer is M.
Source: PYQ-style

52. How many pairs of letters in the word "CREDIT" have as many letters between them in the word as there are in the English alphabet (in either direction)?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is counting only the obvious C–E pair and stopping. C–E has one letter between them in the word (R) and one in the alphabet (D), and E–D are adjacent in both the word and the alphabet (zero letters between); no other pair matches, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

53. If the letters of the word "DANGER" are arranged in alphabetical order, which letter will be third from the right?
(a) G (b) E (c) N (d) R
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is arranging the letters in reverse alphabetical order, which the question does not ask for. Alphabetical arrangement is A, D, E, G, N, R; counting from the right gives R first, N second and G third, so the answer is G.
Source: PYQ-style

54. In the word "ASSASSINATION", which letter occurs the maximum number of times?
(a) A (b) N (c) I (d) S
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is A, which appears early and often but only 3 times. Counting carefully: A appears 3 times, S appears 4 times, I appears 2 times and N appears 2 times, so the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style

55. If A = 1, B = 2, …, Z = 26, what is the sum of the letter values of the word "CAB"?
(a) 8 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 6
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is counting C as 4 by starting the count from 0; the alphabet starts at A = 1. C = 3, A = 1, B = 2, and 3 + 1 + 2 = 6, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

56. Which of the following words will appear second in a dictionary?
(a) PLASTER (b) PLASTIC (c) PLATEAU (d) PLATTER
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is comparing the fifth letter first; dictionary order compares letters left to right, and the fourth letter already separates the pairs. PLASTER and PLASTIC (fourth letter S) precede PLATEAU and PLATTER (fourth letter T); between PLASTER and PLASTIC the sixth letters E and I decide, and E comes first, so PLASTER is first and PLASTIC is second, so the answer is PLASTIC.
Source: PYQ-style

57. In the English alphabet, the 9th letter to the left of the 18th letter from the left is:
(a) H (b) G (c) I (d) J
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding 9 instead of subtracting, which gives a position beyond the alphabet. 18 − 9 = 9, and the 9th letter is I, so the answer is I.
Source: PYQ-style

58. How many meaningful English words can be formed using the letters E, A, T (each letter used exactly once)?
(a) 3 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is counting only EAT and missing the others, or counting TAE which is not a word. The valid words are EAT, ATE and TEA, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

59. If the alphabet is written in reverse order, which letter will be 10th from the left?
(a) P (b) R (c) O (d) Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is counting 10 from A in the normal order, which gives J. In reverse order the 10th letter corresponds to position 27 − 10 = 17 in the normal alphabet, and the 17th letter is Q, so the answer is Q.
Source: PYQ-style

60. If the alphabet is written in reverse order, which letter will be 5th to the right of the 12th letter from the left?
(a) I (b) K (c) J (d) H
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is converting only one of the two positions into the normal alphabet and mixing the directions. In the reversed alphabet the 12th letter from the left is at normal position 27 − 12 = 15 (O), and moving 5 further right in the reversed sequence reaches reversed position 17, which is normal position 27 − 17 = 10, the letter J, so the answer is J.
Source: PYQ-style

61. Which letter is the 7th to the right of the letter which is 12th from the right end of the English alphabet?
(a) U (b) V (c) T (d) S
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is mixing up the directions and moving towards A instead of Z. The 12th letter from the right is at position 27 − 12 = 15, which is O; moving 7 to the right gives 15 + 7 = 22, which is V, so the answer is V.
Source: PYQ-style

62. If the first half of the English alphabet is reversed, which letter will be 10th from the left?
(a) C (b) D (c) E (d) B
Answer: (b)
Solution: Reversing the first 13 letters gives M, L, K, J, I, H, G, F, E, D, C, B, A followed by N to Z. The tempting wrong option C comes from miscounting the reversed block; counting carefully, the 10th position holds D, so the answer is D.
Source: PYQ-style

63. The letters of the word "LISTEN" can be rearranged to form which word meaning "making no sound"?
(a) TINSEL (b) INLETS (c) SILENT (d) ENLIST
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that all four options are valid anagrams of LISTEN, so letters alone cannot decide; the meaning must be used. The word meaning "making no sound" is SILENT, so the answer is SILENT.
Source: PYQ-style

64. In a certain code, the letters of the alphabet are numbered such that A = 26, B = 25, …, Z = 1. What is the code value of the word "CAT"?
(a) 55 (b) 58 (c) 57 (d) 56
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using the normal values C = 3, A = 1, T = 20, which sum to 24. Under the rule value = 27 − normal position: C = 24, A = 26, T = 7, and 24 + 26 + 7 = 57, so the answer is 57.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Which group of letters is different from the other three?
(a) PRT (b) BDG (c) KMO (d) DFH
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is checking only the first gap and assuming all groups follow the same pattern. In DFH, KMO and PRT each letter is exactly 2 positions ahead of the previous one (+2, +2), but in BDG the gaps are +2 and +3, so the odd group is BDG.
Source: PYQ-style

66. If every alternate letter of the alphabet starting from B is removed (B, D, F, …), which letter will be the 8th in the remaining sequence?
(a) Q (b) M (c) O (d) K
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is counting 8 in the original alphabet, which gives H, or removing the odd letters instead. The remaining sequence is A, C, E, G, I, K, M, O, Q, …, and the 8th letter is O, so the answer is O.
Source: PYQ-style

67. In the word "IMAGINATION", how many different letters appear exactly twice?
(a) 4 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is counting I as a twice-appearing letter; I actually appears 3 times. Only A and N appear exactly twice, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

68. Which of the following is the 15th letter from the right end of the English alphabet?
(a) J (b) L (c) M (d) K
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is computing 27 − 15 = 12 and then misreading position 12 as K; the 12th letter is L. The 15th letter from the right is L, so the answer is L.
Source: PYQ-style

69. Arrange the following words in dictionary order and choose the one that comes last: 1. SOUND 2. SOUP 3. SOURCE 4. SOUTH
(a) SOUND (b) SOUTH (c) SOURCE (d) SOUP
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is comparing only up to the third letter, where all four words tie at SOU. The fourth letters are N, P, R and T respectively; since T is the largest, SOUTH comes last in dictionary order, so the answer is SOUTH.
Source: PYQ-style

70. If the word "PLANT" is written with its letters in reverse order, which letter will be in the middle?
(a) N (b) L (c) T (d) A
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is picking N by counting from the wrong end of the reversed word. Reversed, PLANT becomes T, N, A, L, P; with 5 letters the middle is the 3rd position, which holds A, so the answer is A.
Source: PYQ-style

71. How many letters of the English alphabet have the same position from the left as from the right when counted in a sequence of the first 25 letters (A to Y)?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 0 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming there must be a pair of such letters. In a sequence of 25 letters, only the middle letter — the 13th, which is M — is equidistant from both ends, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

72. In the word "STRONG", if each letter is replaced by the letter immediately following it in the alphabet, the new word is:
(a) TUSPOH (b) RSQMNF (c) TUSQOH (d) SUTPOH
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is shifting each letter one step backward, which gives RSQMNF. Shifting forward: S→T, T→U, R→S, O→P, N→O, G→H, giving TUSPOH, so the answer is TUSPOH.
Source: PYQ-style

73. Which letter in the word "WONDERFUL" has the same position in the word (from the left) as its position in the English alphabet?
(a) N (b) U (c) O (d) E
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is stopping at D, which is 4th in both the word and the alphabet but is not among the options. Checking the options: E is the 5th letter of the alphabet and also the 5th letter of WONDERFUL (W-O-N-D-E), so the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

74. If the letters of the alphabet are divided into two halves, A–M and N–Z, and the two halves are interleaved (N first, then A, then O, then B, …), which letter will be 5th in the new sequence?
(a) P (b) O (c) C (d) B
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is writing the whole second half first (N, O, P, Q, R, …), which would wrongly give R. Interleaving one letter from each half gives N, A, O, B, P, C, …, and the 5th letter is P, so the answer is P.
Source: PYQ-style

75. In the word "EDUCATION", how many vowels occupy positions (from the left) that are odd numbers?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is counting O at position 8, but 8 is even, or counting N at position 9, but N is a consonant. The odd positions 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 hold E, U, A, I, N; the vowels among them are E, U, A and I, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style
