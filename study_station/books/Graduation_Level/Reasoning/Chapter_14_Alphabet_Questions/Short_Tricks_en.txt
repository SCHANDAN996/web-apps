# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Alphabet Questions

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 1: "EJOTY" — The Position King
**E-J-O-T-Y = 5-10-15-20-25**

> "**E**very **J**udge **O**n **T**he **Y**acht" — five letters, five multiples of 5.

If you know EJOTY, you can find ANY letter's position in under 3 seconds:
- Need R? O is 15, O-P-Q-R → 18. Done.
- Need W? Y is 25, go back: Y-X-W → 23. Done.

**Box:** 📦 *Master EJOTY first — every other trick in this chapter stands on it.*

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 2: "CFILORUX" — The Multiples of 3
**C-F-I-L-O-R-U-X = 3-6-9-12-15-18-21-24**

> "**C**razy **F**rogs **I**nvade **L**ondon **O**n **R**ed **U**mbrellas, e**X**pecting rain."

Instant positions for every multiple of 3 — no counting on fingers.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 3: "AZ BY CX DW EV FU GT HS IR JQ KP LO MN" — Opposite Pairs
> "**A**dam and **Z**ebra **B**ought **Y**ellow **C**ars..."

Pairs sum to **27** (A=1, Z=26 → 1+26=27). Remember: **"Opposites always make 27."**
- Opposite of D (4)? 27 − 4 = 23 → W.
- Opposite of M (13)? 27 − 13 = 14 → N. (M-N is the famous middle pair!)

**Box:** 📦 *27 is the magic number of opposites. Subtract from 27, never count backwards.*

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 4: "M-N = 13-14, the Middle Twins"
> "**M**y **N**eighbour sits in the **middle** of the alphabet."

26 letters → the middle falls between M (13) and N (14). Any "middle letter" question starts here.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 5: "Left + Left = Subtract, Right + Right = Subtract, Mixed = Add"
> "**S**ame **S**ide **S**ubtracts, **O**pposite sides **O**pen up (add)."

- 5th to the **left** of 10th from the **left** → 10 − 5 = 5th from left.
- 5th to the **left** of 10th from the **right** → 10 + 5 = 15th from right.

**Box:** 📦 *Same direction → subtract. Opposite directions → add. Never solve these by writing the whole alphabet.*

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 6: "Right = 27 − Left"
> "The right hand owes the left hand 27."

To convert "kth from right" into "from left": **(27 − k)th from left**.
- 9th from right = 27 − 9 = 18th from left = R.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 7: "Vowel Vendetta — AEIOU = 1-5-9-15-21"
> "**A** **E**agle **I**s **O**ften **U**pset" — vowel positions 1, 5, 9, 15, 21.

Questions like "how many vowels between X and Y" become instant when vowel positions are memorised.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 8: "Backward Alphabet = ZY XW VU TS RQ PO NM LK JI HG FE DC BA"
> "Sing the alphabet **backwards in pairs**, like a song in reverse."

Practise Z→A in pairs (ZY, XW, VU...) — series questions with decreasing patterns (−2, −3) stop being scary.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 9: "Letter-Number Bridge: Squares Hide in the Alphabet"
> Perfect-square positions: **A(1), D(4), I(9), P(16), Y(25)** → "**A** **D**og **I**s **P**artl**y** **Y**ellow."

Series like A, D, I, P, ? → next square 36 > 26, so pattern ends or wraps — spotting squares instantly cracks "odd one out" questions.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 10: "Prime Positions: B-C-E-G-K-M-Q-S-W"
> Primes 2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23 → "**B**ig **C**ats **E**at **G**iant **K**angaroos **M**ostly **Q**uickly, **S**ir **W**atson."

If a series jumps B→E→K→Q, check primes before anything else.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 11: "Gap Trick for Series: Write the Jumps, Not the Letters"
> "Don't stare at letters — **listen to the footsteps between them**."

For A, C, F, J, ? — write gaps: +2, +3, +4 → next +5 → J+5 = O. The pattern lives in the **differences**, not the letters.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 12: "Word-Formation: Circle the Vowels First"
> "**V**owels are the **V**IP guests — seat them first."

In "how many meaningful words from the 3rd, 5th, 7th letters" questions, pick letters, spot vowels instantly, and build around them. 30 seconds saved every time.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 13: "Pair-Counting (Dictionary Order): Forward AND Backward"
> "A pair counts **both ways** — like a two-way street."

In "how many letter pairs in the word have as many letters between them as in the alphabet," always scan **left-to-right AND right-to-left**. Missing the backward scan is the examiner's favourite trap.

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Leave the Question

**1. The 15-Second Pattern Rule:**
If a letter series shows no pattern in the gaps within 15 seconds (check +1/+2, squares, primes, alternate letters — in that order), **mark and skip**. Alphabet series have infinite possible patterns; your time is finite. Return only if time remains.

**2. The Long-Word Pair Trap:**
"How many pairs in the word ESTABLISHMENT..." — if the word has 10+ letters, pair-counting takes 60–90 seconds with high error risk. **Attempt only if you're strong at it; otherwise skip without guilt** — it's worth the same 1 mark as a 5-second EJOTY question.

**3. The Double-Condition Jumble:**
Questions like "if the word is reversed AND vowels replaced by next letter AND consonants by previous, which is 4th from right?" — three operations stacked. If you can't set it up cleanly in 20 seconds, **skip first, solve in the second pass**. One misapplied operation = −0.25/−0.5 negative marking for nothing.

> 🎯 **Golden Rule:** Alphabet questions are the **fastest-scoring** area of reasoning — but only if you bank the easy ones (position, opposite, EJOTY) in under 10 seconds each and refuse to wrestle the traps.
