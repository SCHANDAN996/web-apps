126. Statement: The company has decided to increase the price of all its products by 15% from next month.
Conclusions:
I. The demand for the company's products will fall after the price increase.
II. The company expects to earn higher revenue after the price increase.
(a) Only I follows (b) Only II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting trap is Conclusion I — a price rise does not automatically mean demand will fall; demand depends on elasticity, brand loyalty and necessity, none of which is stated, so I is speculative. A firm raises prices expecting better revenue or margins, which is a reasonable implicit assumption behind the decision, so II follows. Hence only II follows, so the answer is (b).

(a) Both I and II follow (b) Only II follows (c) Only I follows (d) Neither I nor II follows
