76. Statement: The company has decided to increase the price of all its products by 10% from next month.
Conclusions:
I. The company's products will become costlier for customers.
II. The company's sales will definitely fall after the price increase.
(a) Both I and II follow (b) Only II follows (c) Only I follows (d) Neither I nor II follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is II — a price rise does not guarantee a fall in sales, since demand may be inelastic or customers may still buy. Only I is a direct and certain consequence of a price increase, so the answer is Only I follows.
