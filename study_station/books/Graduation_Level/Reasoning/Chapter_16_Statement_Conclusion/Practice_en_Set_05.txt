101. Statement: "All employees who complete the training program will receive a certificate."
Conclusions:
I. Ravi received a certificate, so he completed the training program.
II. Employees who do not complete the training program will not receive a certificate.
(a) Only II follows (b) Both I and II follow (c) Neither I nor II follows (d) Only I follows
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is assuming the statement works in reverse. The statement only says completers get certificates; it does not say certificates are given only to completers, so I does not follow, and non-completers may still get certificates through other routes, so II also fails. Hence the answer is Neither I nor II follows.
