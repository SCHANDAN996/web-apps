51. Statement: A large number of people in the city have fallen ill due to contaminated drinking water supplied by the municipal corporation.
Courses of Action:
I. The municipal corporation should immediately arrange for supply of clean drinking water through tankers in the affected areas.
II. The water supply pipelines in the affected areas should be inspected and repaired on priority.
III. The residents should be advised to permanently stop using municipal water.
(a) Only I follows (b) All follow (c) Only II and III follow (d) Only I and II follow
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option includes III, but advising people to permanently stop using municipal water is an extreme and impractical step; the supply system must be fixed, not abandoned. Immediate relief through tankers (I) and inspection-cum-repair of pipelines (II) address both the emergency and the root cause, so the answer is Only I and II follow.
