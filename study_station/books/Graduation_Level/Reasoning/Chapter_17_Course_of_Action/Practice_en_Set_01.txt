1. Statement: A large number of people in the city have fallen ill due to contaminated drinking water supplied by the municipal corporation.
Courses of Action:
I. The municipal corporation should immediately arrange for supply of clean drinking water through tankers in the affected areas.
II. The people should be advised to boil water before drinking.
III. The entire water supply system of the city should be shut down permanently.
(a) Only I and II follow (b) Only I follows (c) Only II and III follow (d) All follow
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is action III, which sounds like a strong response but permanently shutting the water supply is absurd and impractical — a course of action must be a logical, feasible remedy, not an extreme reaction. Action I addresses the immediate crisis and action II is a sensible precaution, so the answer is Only I and II follow.
