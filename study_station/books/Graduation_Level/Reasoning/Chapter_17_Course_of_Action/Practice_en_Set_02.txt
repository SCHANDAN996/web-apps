26. Statement: Many passengers of a city bus service have complained about buses frequently breaking down mid-route, causing long delays.
Courses of Action:
I. The transport department should immediately replace all buses in the fleet with new ones.
II. The transport department should set up a preventive maintenance schedule and inspect buses regularly.
(a) Only I follows (b) Neither I nor II follows (c) Both I and II follow (d) Only II follows
Answer: (d)
Solution: Replacing the entire fleet at once is an extreme, costly step and not justified when the problem may be poor upkeep; the trap is to treat "immediate replacement" as practical. Regular preventive maintenance directly addresses breakdowns, so the answer is Only II follows.
