26. In a class of 60 students, 35 play cricket, 28 play football and 12 play both. How many students play neither game?
(a) 7 (b) 12 (c) 15 (d) 9
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 12 comes from confusing "both" with "neither". Students playing at least one game = 35 + 28 − 12 = 51. Neither = 60 − 51 = 9, so the answer is 9.

27. In a survey of 100 people, 60 read newspaper A, 45 read newspaper B and 20 read both. How many read exactly one newspaper?
(a) 40 (b) 85 (c) 55 (d) 65
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 85, which is "at least one" (60 + 45 − 20). Exactly one = only A + only B = (60 − 20) + (45 − 20) = 40 + 25 = 65, so the answer is 65.
