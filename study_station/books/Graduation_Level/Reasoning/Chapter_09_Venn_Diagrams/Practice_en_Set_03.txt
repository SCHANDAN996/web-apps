51. In a class of 60 students, 35 play cricket, 28 play football and 12 play both. How many students play neither game?
(a) 11 (b) 7 (c) 9 (d) 5
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer comes from adding 35 + 28 = 63 and subtracting 60 to get 3, which confuses the overlap with the "neither" group. Correct method: students playing at least one game = 35 + 28 − 12 = 51, so neither = 60 − 51 = 9. So the answer is 9.

52. In a survey of 100 people, 60 read newspaper A, 45 read newspaper B and 20 read both. How many read exactly one newspaper?
(a) 65 (b) 75 (c) 55 (d) 85
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing "at least one" = 60 + 45 − 20 = 85 and stopping there. Exactly one = (60 − 20) + (45 − 20) = 40 + 25 = 65. So the answer is 65.
