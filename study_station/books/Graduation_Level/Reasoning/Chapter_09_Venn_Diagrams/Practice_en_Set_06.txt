126. In a class of 120 students, 60 play cricket, 50 play football and 40 play hockey. 20 play cricket and football, 15 play football and hockey, 10 play cricket and hockey, and 5 play all three. How many students play none of the three games?
(a) 15 (b) 10 (c) 20 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 15 comes from forgetting to add back those playing all three in the formula. By inclusion–exclusion, playing at least one game = 60 + 50 + 40 − 20 − 15 − 10 + 5 = 110, so none = 120 − 110, and the answer is 10.
Source: PYQ-style

127. In a survey of 200 people, 110 read newspaper A, 90 read newspaper B and 80 read newspaper C. 40 read A and B, 30 read B and C, 25 read A and C, and 15 read all three. How many read exactly one newspaper?
(a) 115 (b) 125 (c) 135 (d) 145
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to subtract the pairwise figures without adding back the all-three group, which gives smaller wrong values. Only A = 110 − 40 − 25 + 15 = 60; only B = 90 − 40 − 30 + 15 = 35; only C = 80 − 30 − 25 + 15 = 40; exactly one = 60 + 35 + 40, so the answer is 135.
Source: PYQ-style

128. In a group, 70% like tea, 60% like coffee, and 20% like neither. What percentage like both tea and coffee?
(a) 40% (b) 30% (c) 45% (d) 50%
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to compute 70 + 60 − 100 = 30%, which ignores the neither group. Since 20% like neither, 80% like at least one, so both = 70 + 60 − 80, and the answer is 50%.
Source: PYQ-style

129. In a class, 45 students passed in Mathematics, 40 in Science and 30 in both. If 15 students failed in both subjects, how many students are in the class?
(a) 75 (b) 65 (c) 70 (d) 80
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 85 comes from adding 45 + 40 + 15 without subtracting the overlap. Passed in at least one = 45 + 40 − 30 = 55; total = 55 + 15, so the answer is 70.

139. In a survey of 300 people, 180 like tea, 150 like coffee and 90 like both. How many like at most one of the two drinks?
(a) 210 (b) 240 (c) 180 (d) 200
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to answer 240, which is at least one; "at most one" means exactly one or neither. Exactly one = (180 − 90) + (150 − 90) = 150; neither = 300 − 240 = 60; at most one = 150 + 60, so the answer is 210.
