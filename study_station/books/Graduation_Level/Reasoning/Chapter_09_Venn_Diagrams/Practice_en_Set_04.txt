Questions 76–100: Medium

76. In a class of 60 students, 35 play cricket, 28 play football and 12 play both. How many students play neither game?
(a) 11 (b) 9 (c) 7 (d) 13
Answer: (b)
Solution: Students playing at least one game = 35 + 28 − 12 = 51. The tempting wrong answer 51 counts players, not non-players. Neither = 60 − 51 = 9, so the answer is 9.
Source: PYQ-style

77. In a survey of 100 people, 60 like tea, 45 like coffee and 25 like both. How many like exactly one of the two drinks?
(a) 35 (b) 80 (c) 45 (d) 55
Answer: (d)
Solution: Only tea = 60 − 25 = 35; only coffee = 45 − 25 = 20. Exactly one = 35 + 20 = 55. The trap is answering 80, which counts everyone who likes at least one drink, so the answer is 55.
Source: PYQ-style

78. In a group, 40% speak Hindi, 50% speak English and 15% speak both. What percentage speaks neither language?
(a) 20% (b) 15% (c) 25% (d) 30%
Answer: (c)
Solution: At least one language = 40 + 50 − 15 = 75%. Neither = 100 − 75 = 25%. The trap is 15%, which is merely the overlap, so the answer is 25%.
Source: PYQ-style

79. In a school, 50 students study Mathematics, 40 study Physics and 20 study both. If every student studies at least one of these subjects, how many students are there?
(a) 90 (b) 80 (c) 60 (d) 70
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total = 50 + 40 − 20 = 70. The trap is 90, obtained by forgetting to subtract the overlap of 20, so the answer is 70.
Source: PYQ-style

80. Out of 80 students, 45 passed in English, 50 passed in Hindi and 10 failed in both. How many passed in both subjects?
(a) 25 (b) 30 (c) 20 (d) 15
Answer: (a)
Solution: Passed at least one = 80 − 10 = 70. Both = 45 + 50 − 70 = 25. The trap is 95 from blind addition, so the answer is 25.

81. In a colony of 120 families, 70 read newspaper A, 60 read newspaper B and 20 read neither. How many read only newspaper A?

84. In a survey, 30 people play cricket, 25 play hockey, 20 play football, 10 play cricket and hockey, 8 play hockey and football, 6 play cricket and football, and 3 play all three. How many play at least one game?
(a) 75 (b) 54 (c) 60 (d) 51
Answer: (b)
Solution: At least one = 30 + 25 + 20 − 10 − 8 − 6 + 3 = 54. The trap is 75, the raw sum ignoring all overlaps, so the answer is 54.

85. Using the data: 30 play cricket, 25 hockey, 20 football; 10 play cricket and hockey, 8 hockey and football, 6 cricket and football, 3 play all three. How many play exactly two games?
(a) 15 (b) 24 (c) 12 (d) 18
Answer: (a)
Solution: Exactly two = (10 − 3) + (8 − 3) + (6 − 3) = 7 + 5 + 3 = 15. The trap is 24, which adds the pairwise figures without removing those who play all three, so the answer is 15.
Source: PYQ-style

86. In a group of 90 people, 50 drink tea, 40 drink coffee and 25 drink both. How many drink neither?
(a) 35 (b) 30 (c) 20 (d) 25
Answer: (d)
Solution: At least one = 50 + 40 − 25 = 65. Neither = 90 − 65 = 25. The trap is confusing this 25 with the overlap, which is also 25 here by coincidence; verify by regions: only tea 25, only coffee 15, both 25, neither 25, so the answer is 25.
Source: PYQ-style

97. In a college, 80 students play cricket, 60 play football and 30 play both. What is the ratio of students playing only cricket to those playing only football?

Solution: Only cricket = 80 − 30 = 50; only football = 60 − 30 = 30. Ratio = 50 : 30 = 5 : 3. The trap is 4 : 3, the raw ratio 80 : 60 ignoring the overlap, so the answer is 5 : 3.
