Questions 76–100: Medium

76. A square sheet is folded along both diagonals (one after the other) and a single hole is punched through all layers at a point away from the centre. After unfolding, how many holes appear?
(a) 2 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is 8, assuming each fold doubles holes twice more, but folding along both diagonals creates only 4 layers at any point away from the centre. One punch through 4 layers gives 4 holes, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

77. A rectangular sheet is folded once vertically (left half onto right half) and then once horizontally (top onto bottom). A triangular cut is made at the folded corner where the two fold lines meet. On unfolding, the cut produces which shape at the centre?
(a) A triangle (b) A rectangle (c) A circle (d) A square/diamond
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many pick "a triangle" forgetting that the cut at the double-fold corner is mirrored across both fold lines. A triangle mirrored across two perpendicular lines forms a four-sided figure (square or diamond), so the answer is a square/diamond.
Source: PYQ-style

78. A square paper is folded into half along a diagonal, then folded again into half. A hole is punched near the middle of the final folded triangle. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two folds create 4 layers, so one punch yields 4 holes. The trap answer 8 assumes three doublings, but only two folds were made, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

79. A sheet is folded along the vertical centre line, and a semicircular cut is made exactly on the fold line. After unfolding, the cut appears as:
(a) A circle (b) A semicircle (c) An ellipse (d) Two semicircles
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is choosing "two semicircles," but a cut made on the fold line mirrors into itself, joining the two halves into one complete circle, so the answer is a circle.
Source: PYQ-style

80. A square sheet is folded twice — first bottom edge to top edge, then left edge to right edge. Two holes are punched in the folded quarter. How many holes appear after unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 16
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each punch passes through 4 layers, giving 4 holes per punch; two punches give 8. The trap answer 4 counts only one punch's holes, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

81. A circular paper is folded into a semicircle, then into a quarter circle. A straight cut is made across the folded tip (the centre of the original circle). On unfolding, the centre shows:
(a) A circular hole (b) A triangular hole (c) A square hole (d) No hole
Answer: (c)
Solution: The folded tip corresponds to the centre, and the straight cut is mirrored across two perpendicular fold lines, producing a four-sided (square) hole. Choosing "triangular" ignores the second mirror, so the answer is a square hole.
Source: PYQ-style

82. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal only, and two holes are punched through the folded triangle. After unfolding, how many holes appear?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 6 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: One fold means 2 layers; each punch makes 2 holes, so two punches make 4. The trap answer 2 forgets the mirroring across the diagonal, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

83. A paper is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally, and a hole is punched exactly at the corner where the two fold edges intersect. After unfolding, the hole(s) appear:
(a) Four separate holes (b) Two holes (c) One hole at the centre (d) Eight holes
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is four separate holes, but a punch exactly on both fold lines maps onto itself under every reflection, so all four images coincide at the centre, giving one hole, so the answer is one hole at the centre.
Source: PYQ-style

84. A rectangular sheet is folded into thirds along its length (two parallel folds) and one hole is punched through all layers. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming each fold doubles the holes (giving 4), but folding into thirds stacks exactly 3 layers, so one punch gives 3 holes, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

85. A square sheet is folded along the horizontal centre line, then along the vertical centre line of the folded rectangle. A V-shaped cut is made at the outer free corner (not on any fold). After unfolding, how many V-shaped cuts appear?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 8 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The cut is away from fold lines, so it is mirrored across both folds, giving 4 copies. The trap answer 2 counts only one reflection, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

86. A sheet is folded once diagonally and a cut is made parallel to the fold, near the fold edge. After unfolding, the two cut lines are:
(a) Parallel to each other (b) Perpendicular to each other (c) Meeting at the centre (d) Curved
Answer: (a)
Solution: A line parallel to the fold mirrors to another line parallel to the fold on the other side, so both remain parallel. The trap "perpendicular" confuses reflection with rotation, so the answer is parallel to each other.
Source: PYQ-style

87. A square paper is folded in half twice (both folds parallel to the sides, in the same direction), creating a strip of 4 layers. One hole is punched. After unfolding, the holes are arranged:
(a) In a 2×2 square (b) At the corners (c) In a straight line of 4 (d) Randomly
Answer: (c)
Solution: Both folds are in the same direction, so all reflections occur along one axis only, placing the 4 holes in a straight line. The trap "2×2 square" applies only when folds are in perpendicular directions, so the answer is a straight line of 4.
Source: PYQ-style

88. A square sheet is folded along both diagonals and a hole is punched exactly at the centre of the folded figure. After unfolding, how many holes appear?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 8 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 4 assumes the punch multiplies, but the centre lies on both fold lines, so all reflections coincide at the centre, giving a single hole, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

89. A paper is folded in half vertically, then the folded paper is folded in half vertically again. A circular hole is punched away from the fold edges. After unfolding, the holes are:
(a) 4 holes in a horizontal row (b) 4 holes in a vertical column (c) 2 holes (d) 4 holes in a square
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two vertical folds reflect the punch horizontally twice, producing 4 holes spaced along a horizontal row. The trap "vertical column" mixes up the fold direction with the reflection direction, so the answer is 4 holes in a horizontal row.
Source: PYQ-style

90. A square sheet is folded along a diagonal, then folded again along the line joining the midpoint of the diagonal to the opposite corner of the triangle. A punch is made through all layers. The maximum number of holes after unfolding is:
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each fold at most doubles the layers: 2 then 4, so at most 4 holes. The trap answer 8 assumes a third doubling that never happened, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

91. A rectangular sheet is folded so that the bottom edge meets the top edge, and then the right edge meets the left edge. A semicircular cut is made on the horizontal fold edge only. After unfolding, the result is:
(a) One circle (b) Two circles (c) Four semicircles (d) One semicircle
Answer: (b)
Solution: The cut on the horizontal fold completes into one circle when that fold is opened, and the vertical fold then mirrors that circle to the other half, giving two circles. The trap "one circle" ignores the second fold's reflection, so the answer is two circles.
Source: PYQ-style

92. A square paper is folded into a smaller square by two folds (horizontal then vertical). A hole is punched at the corner of the folded square that lies on the vertical fold but not on the horizontal fold. After unfolding, how many holes appear?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 4 treats the punch as fully interior, but since it lies on the vertical fold, reflection across that fold maps it to itself; only the horizontal fold creates a second image, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

93. A circular sheet is folded into a semicircle and a hole is punched at the midpoint of the diameter (the fold edge). After unfolding, the hole appears as:
(a) One hole at the centre (b) Two holes (c) Four holes (d) A semicircular hole
Answer: (a)
Solution: The midpoint of the diameter is the centre of the circle and lies on the fold line, so its reflection coincides with itself, giving one hole. The trap "two holes" forgets that points on the fold do not duplicate, so the answer is one hole at the centre.
Source: PYQ-style

94. A sheet is folded in half horizontally, then the folded sheet is folded in half vertically. Two holes are punched: one on the vertical fold edge and one in the interior. Total holes after unfolding:
(a) 8 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The interior punch gives 4 holes; the punch on the vertical fold gives only 2 (it duplicates across the horizontal fold only). Total 6. The trap answer 8 counts both punches as interior, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

95. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal and a straight cut is made from one fold edge to the other, not passing through the centre. After unfolding, the cut divides the sheet into:
(a) 3 pieces (b) 2 pieces (c) 4 pieces (d) 1 piece
Answer: (b)
Solution: The cut mirrors across the diagonal into a single continuous line (or V) from edge to edge, splitting the sheet into 2 pieces. The trap "3 pieces" assumes two independent cuts, but they join at the fold, so the answer is 2 pieces.
Source: PYQ-style

96. A paper is folded twice (vertical then horizontal) and a small square is cut out at the corner where both fold edges meet. After unfolding, the hole at the centre is:
(a) A small square (b) A square four times the cut's area (c) A circle (d) Four separate small squares
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap "four separate small squares" ignores that the cut sits on both folds, so the four mirrored copies join seamlessly into one square with twice the side, i.e., four times the area, so the answer is a square four times the cut's area.
Source: PYQ-style

97. A square sheet is folded along both midlines (horizontal and vertical). A hole is punched at a point that is on the horizontal fold but not on the vertical fold. After unfolding, the holes are:
(a) 4 in a square (b) 2 in a vertical line (c) 2 in a horizontal line (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: Since the punch lies on the horizontal fold, reflection across that fold maps it to itself; only the vertical fold creates a mirror image, giving 2 holes aligned vertically. The trap "4 in a square" treats the point as interior, so the answer is 2 in a vertical line.
Source: PYQ-style

98. A rectangular sheet is folded into quarters by one vertical and one horizontal fold. Three holes are punched in the interior of the folded quarter. Total holes after unfolding:

Solution: Each interior punch passes through 4 layers, so 3 punches give 12 holes. The trap answer 6 halves the layers by mistake, so the answer is 12.

99. A square sheet is folded along a diagonal, then along the other diagonal of the original square (now folded). A hole is punched at a point lying on the second fold but not the first. After unfolding, how many holes appear?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 4 assumes the point is interior to all layers, but lying on the second fold means that reflection maps it to itself; only the first diagonal's reflection duplicates it, so the answer is 2.

100. A sheet is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally, then in half vertically again (8 layers). One hole is punched in the interior. After unfolding, the holes number:
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 16
Answer: (c)
Solution: Three folds stack 2×2×2 = 8 layers, so one interior punch gives 8 holes. The trap answer 4 counts only two folds, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style
