Questions 26–50: Medium

26. A square sheet of paper is folded once along its vertical midline and then once along its horizontal midline. A single circular hole is punched through all layers at the centre of the folded quarter. How many holes appear when the sheet is unfolded?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer 2 counts only one fold's reflection, but each fold doubles the layers. Two folds create 4 layers, so one punch passes through 4 layers and the hole is mirrored across both fold lines, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

27. A rectangular sheet is folded along its diagonal. The folded corner (the corner lying on the diagonal fold) is cut off by one straight cut. When unfolded, the cut-off piece will appear as:
(a) Two separate triangles (b) A single triangle (c) A circle (d) A quadrilateral
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming the piece stays a single triangle; a straight cut across a corner that lies on the fold line is mirrored across the fold, so two triangles join base-to-base into one kite-like quadrilateral. It also does not split into two separate pieces, since the cut crosses the fold, so the answer is a quadrilateral.
Source: PYQ-style

28. A square paper is folded into half vertically (left half over right), then the folded piece is folded horizontally (top over bottom). A cut is made at the corner where the two fold edges meet. After unfolding, where does the cut appear?
(a) Along one diagonal (b) At the centre of the sheet (c) At the midpoints of the sides (d) At the four corners
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is thinking four cuts appear; since the cut is on both fold lines, all reflections coincide at one point. The corner where both folded edges meet corresponds to the centre of the original sheet, so the answer is the centre of the sheet.
Source: PYQ-style

29. A sheet is folded twice — first along the vertical midline, then along the horizontal midline of the folded piece. Two holes are punched in the folded quarter, neither on any fold edge. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 2 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 4 counts only one punch's reflections. Two folds give 4 layers, each punch produces 4 holes, and two punches give 2 × 4 = 8 holes, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

30. A circular paper is folded into a semicircle, then the semicircle is folded again into a quarter circle. A small notch is cut on the curved edge of the quarter. How many notches appear on the rim of the unfolded circle?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 2 counts only one fold. Folding a circle into a semicircle and then a quarter gives 4 layers, and since the curved edge is not a fold line, each layer's notch appears separately on the rim, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

31. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal, then folded again along the other diagonal direction of the folded triangle. A hole is punched away from all fold edges. How many holes appear when unfolded?
(a) 8 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 8 assumes three doublings. The first diagonal fold gives 2 layers and the second fold of the triangle doubles it to 4 layers, so one punch yields 4 holes mirrored across the fold lines, so the answer is 4.

32. A rectangular sheet is folded into thirds along its length (two parallel folds, like a letter fold). A single hole is punched through all layers, away from fold edges. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is doubling for each fold and answering 4. A letter fold stacks 3 panels, creating 3 layers rather than 4, so one punch gives 3 holes, so the answer is 3.

33. A square paper is folded in half vertically, then a semicircular cut is made on the folded edge (the fold line itself). On unfolding, the cut produces:
(a) One full circle (b) Two semicircles (c) One semicircle (d) An ellipse
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is counting two semicircles as the final shape; they join along the fold into a single figure. A semicircular cut centred on the fold line mirrors into its other half when unfolded, so the answer is one full circle.
Source: PYQ-style

34. A sheet is folded along the vertical midline, then along the horizontal midline. A straight cut is made connecting the two folded edges of the small folded square, removing the corner opposite to the fold-meeting corner. After unfolding, the sheet has:
(a) Four corners cut off (b) A hole at the centre (c) One corner cut off (d) Two corners cut off
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is thinking the centre is cut, but the centre lies at the fold-meeting corner. The corner opposite it is the free corner where all four original corners of the sheet are stacked, so cutting it removes all four original corners at once, so the answer is four corners cut off.
Source: PYQ-style

35. A paper is folded once horizontally and a punch is made near the folded edge but not on it. On unfolding, the two holes will be:
(a) One above the other, symmetric about the fold line (b) Coincident (c) Diagonally placed (d) Side by side horizontally
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming side-by-side placement, which would result from a vertical fold. A horizontal fold mirrors points across a horizontal line, so the hole and its image lie one above the other, equidistant from the fold line, so the answer is one above the other, symmetric about the fold line.
Source: PYQ-style

36. A square sheet is folded along both diagonals (one after the other). A hole is punched through all layers at a point not on any fold. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 2 (b) 8 (c) 4 (d) 16
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 8 assumes three doublings or miscounts the diagonal folds. The first diagonal fold gives 2 layers and folding along the second diagonal direction doubles to 4 layers, so one punch gives 4 holes, so the answer is 4.

37. A strip of paper is folded in half lengthwise and a V-shaped cut is made on the folded long edge. On unfolding, the cut shape on the strip is:
(a) A diamond/rhombus-shaped hole (b) A V (c) Two separate V cuts (d) A rectangle
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is expecting two separate V shapes; cuts on the fold join into one symmetric figure. A V cut on the fold line mirrors into an inverted V, and together they form a diamond (rhombus) shaped hole, so the answer is a diamond/rhombus-shaped hole.
Source: PYQ-style

38. A square paper is folded in half vertically (right over left), then in half horizontally (bottom over top). A punch is made at the corner where the free (unfolded) edges meet. On unfolding, the holes appear at:
(a) Along the vertical midline (b) The centre (c) Midpoints of two sides (d) The four corners of the sheet
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is confusing this corner with the fold-meeting corner, which would give a centre hole. The corner where the free edges meet is where all four original corners are stacked together, so a punch there produces holes at all four corners, so the answer is the four corners of the sheet.
Source: PYQ-style

39. A circular sheet is folded into a semicircle. Two holes are punched in the folded semicircle, neither on the fold diameter. How many holes appear on unfolding?

Solution: The trap answer 2 forgets the mirroring across the fold. One fold gives 2 layers, each punch produces 2 holes mirrored across the diameter, and two punches give 4 holes, so the answer is 4.

40. A square sheet is folded along the vertical midline, then along the horizontal midline, and then along the diagonal of the small folded square. A hole is punched away from all edges. How many holes appear on full unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 16 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 4 counts only two folds. Three folds each double the layers: 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 layers, so one punch through 8 layers gives 8 holes on unfolding, so the answer is 8.

41. A rectangular sheet is folded in half along its length, and a half-square notch is cut at the midpoint of the folded edge. On unfolding, the notch becomes:
(a) A square hole at a corner (b) Two separate half-square notches (c) A triangular hole at the centre (d) A square hole at the centre of the sheet
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is expecting two separate notches, but the notch is cut on the fold edge, so the two halves join into one shape. The folded edge is the midline of the sheet and its midpoint is the sheet's centre, so the half-square notch opens into a full square hole there, so the answer is a square hole at the centre of the sheet.

42. A paper is folded twice (vertically, then horizontally). A punch is made exactly on the horizontal fold edge of the folded piece but away from the vertical fold edge. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 1 (d) 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 4 assumes both folds double the hole, but a hole on a fold line is not duplicated by that fold. The punch reflects only across the vertical fold, giving 2 holes, so the answer is 2.

43. In a folded-then-cut problem, a sheet is folded along its vertical midline and a triangle is cut with its base on the fold edge. On unfolding, the cut-out shape is:
(a) A triangle (b) Two triangles (c) A kite/rhombus-like quadrilateral (d) A pentagon
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering two triangles; they remain joined along the base on the fold line. A triangle with its base on the fold mirrors into an identical triangle sharing that base, forming a kite or rhombus-like quadrilateral, so the answer is a kite/rhombus-like quadrilateral.

44. A square sheet is folded into a smaller square by two folds (vertical then horizontal). Three punches are made in the folded square, all away from fold edges. How many holes appear on unfolding?

47. A square sheet is folded along the horizontal midline (top half down). A semicircular cut is made with its diameter on the bottom free edge of the folded piece. On unfolding, how many cut shapes appear and where?
(a) Two circles at top and bottom edges (b) One circle at the centre (c) Two semicircles at the top and bottom edges (d) One semicircle at the bottom edge
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is expecting a full circle, which would happen only if the cut were on the fold edge. The bottom free edge of the folded piece contains both the original bottom edge and the folded-down top edge, so the cut produces one semicircle on each, so the answer is two semicircles at the top and bottom edges.
Source: PYQ-style

(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 4 assumes four layers always give four holes, but coincident reflections merge. The intersection of both fold lines is the centre of the sheet, and a punch there lies on both fold lines, so all reflections coincide at one point, so the answer is 1.

49. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal. A straight cut is made parallel to the fold, near the folded edge, removing a strip. On unfolding, the sheet will have:
(a) A slit parallel to the diagonal on one half only (b) Two slits symmetric about the diagonal (c) A hole at the centre (d) The sheet cut into two pieces
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is expecting a single slit; only cuts on the fold line stay single. A cut parallel to the fold but not on it passes through 2 layers and mirrors across the diagonal, so the answer is two slits symmetric about the diagonal.
Source: PYQ-style

50. A sheet is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally. A punch is made on the vertical fold edge of the folded piece (but not at the corner where folds meet), and another punch is made in the interior away from all edges. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 8 (b) 5 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 8 doubles both punches twice, ignoring that the fold-edge punch mirrors onto itself across the vertical fold. The interior punch gives 4 holes and the fold-edge punch gives 2 holes, totalling 4 + 2 = 6, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style
