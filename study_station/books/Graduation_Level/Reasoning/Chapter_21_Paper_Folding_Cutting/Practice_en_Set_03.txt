Questions 51–75: Medium

51. A square sheet of paper is folded once along its vertical centre line and then once along its horizontal centre line. A single circular hole is punched through all layers at the folded corner. How many holes appear when the paper is unfolded?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is 8, but that would need three folds. Two folds create 2 × 2 = 4 layers, so one punch passes through 4 layers and produces 4 symmetric holes on unfolding, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A rectangular sheet is folded along its diagonal. A triangular cut is made near the fold. When unfolded, the cut appears as:
(a) Two separate triangles (b) One circle (c) One kite-shaped (rhombus-like) figure (d) Two circles
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many pick two separate triangles, forgetting that a cut made exactly on the fold line mirrors into a single joined figure. A triangle cut on a fold opens into a kite/rhombus-like symmetric shape, so the answer is one kite-shaped figure.
Source: PYQ-style

53. A square paper is folded into quarters (half, then half again). A hole is punched at the centre of the folded quarter. On unfolding, the holes are located:
(a) At the four corners of the sheet (b) At the centre only (c) Symmetrically around the centre, one in each quadrant (d) Along one diagonal only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Choosing "centre only" ignores that the punch goes through 4 layers. Each layer corresponds to one quadrant, so the holes appear symmetrically, one in each quadrant around the centre, so the answer is symmetrically around the centre, one in each quadrant.
Source: PYQ-style

54. A sheet is folded along the vertical centre line, then along the horizontal centre line, and then along the diagonal of the folded square. One punch is made. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 8 (b) 6 (c) 16 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 16 by doubling wrongly. Three folds give 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 layers, so one punch creates 8 holes, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

55. A circular paper is folded into a semicircle and then into a quarter circle. A straight cut is made across the folded tip (the centre of the original circle). On unfolding, the cut produces:
(a) No hole (b) A small square (diamond) hole at the centre (c) A hole at the edge (d) A cross-shaped slit at the centre
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is a cross-shaped slit, but a cross would need two separate cuts, one along each fold. A single straight snip across the doubly folded tip mirrors across both perpendicular fold lines, and the four mirrored straight edges join to enclose a small square (diamond) hole at the centre, so the answer is a small square hole at the centre.
Source: PYQ-style

56. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal and then the resulting triangle is folded along its axis of symmetry. A punch is made away from the centre. How many holes appear when unfolded?
(a) 2 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is 8, but two folds stack only 4 triangular layers, not 8. Hence one punch gives 4 holes arranged symmetrically, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

57. In a paper folding question, the paper is folded upward along a horizontal line and then rightward along a vertical line. A hole punched in the top-right of the folded piece will, on full unfolding, appear in:
(a) Only the top-right quadrant (b) All four quadrants, mirror-imaged (c) Only the two right quadrants (d) Only the two top quadrants
Answer: (b)
Solution: Choosing only two quadrants ignores that two perpendicular folds reflect the punch across both axes. The hole is mirrored across the vertical and horizontal fold lines, appearing in all four quadrants, so the answer is all four quadrants, mirror-imaged.
Source: PYQ-style

58. A strip of paper is folded in half lengthwise and a semicircular cut is made on the fold. On unfolding, the cut becomes:
(a) A square (b) A semicircle (c) An ellipse (d) A full circle
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering semicircle by forgetting the mirror effect. A semicircle cut on a fold line reflects into its mirror half, forming a complete circle, so the answer is a full circle.
Source: PYQ-style

59. A square paper is folded to form a triangle by folding along one diagonal, then folded again along the altitude of that triangle. A punch is made through all layers. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 8 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: Answering 8 assumes three folds; there are only two folds here. Two folds give 4 layers, so one punch yields 4 holes, so the answer is 4.

60. A sheet is folded once vertically. Two punches are made on the folded sheet. How many holes appear after unfolding?
(a) 8 (b) 2 (c) 6 (d) 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is 2, counting only the visible punches. Each punch passes through 2 layers, so 2 punches × 2 layers = 4 holes, so the answer is 4.

61. A square sheet is folded in half, then in half again (both folds parallel to each other). A punch is made. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 2 (b) 8 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is thinking parallel folds behave differently from perpendicular ones — the layer count is what matters. Two folds still create 4 layers, so one punch gives 4 holes in a row, so the answer is 4.

62. A paper is folded along the vertical centre line with the left half behind the right half. A cut is made on the folded edge. On unfolding, the pattern will be:
(a) Symmetric about the vertical centre line (b) Asymmetric (c) Symmetric about the horizontal centre line (d) Rotated by 90 degrees
Answer: (a)
Solution: Choosing horizontal symmetry is the common error; the fold line is vertical. Any cut on a fold mirrors across that fold, so the design is symmetric about the vertical centre line, so the answer is symmetric about the vertical centre line.
Source: PYQ-style

63. A square sheet is folded into quarters. A triangular punch is made at the corner where the two fold edges meet (the centre of the original sheet). On unfolding, the result is:
(a) Four triangles at the corners (b) One diamond/square hole at the centre (c) One triangle at the centre (d) Four holes along the edges
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is four triangles, but the punched corner is the meeting point of both folds — the sheet's centre. The mirrored triangles join into a single diamond-shaped hole at the centre, so the answer is one diamond/square hole at the centre.
Source: PYQ-style

64. A rectangular sheet is folded in half horizontally, then the folded sheet is folded in half vertically. Three punches are made on the final folded piece. Total holes on unfolding:

Solution: Answering 6 counts only one fold's doubling. Two folds create 4 layers, so 3 punches × 4 layers = 12 holes, so the answer is 12.

66. In a folding-and-cutting problem, a V-shaped cut is made on a single fold line. On unfolding, the cut becomes:
(a) An X shape (b) A diamond (rhombus) shape (c) A V shape (d) A straight line
Answer: (b)
Solution: Choosing an X is tempting, but an X needs crossing lines; a V mirrored across the fold forms a closed four-sided figure. The V and its mirror image join at the fold to form a diamond, so the answer is a diamond (rhombus) shape.

67. A square paper is folded along the vertical centre line, then along the horizontal centre line, then along the diagonal of the small folded square. Two punches are made. Total holes on unfolding:

Solution: The trap answer 24 wrongly triples instead of using layer count. Three folds give 8 layers; 2 punches × 8 layers = 16 holes, so the answer is 16.

68. A sheet is folded so that the bottom edge meets the top edge, and then the left edge meets the right edge. A hole punched near the free (open) corner appears, on unfolding:
(a) At the centre of the sheet (b) At the midpoints of the edges (c) Only at one corner (d) Near all four corners of the sheet
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing the centre, which is where the folded corner lies, not the open corner. The open corner corresponds to the four outer corners stacked together, so holes appear near all four corners, so the answer is near all four corners of the sheet.
Source: PYQ-style

69. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal only. A punch is made through the folded triangle, away from the fold. How many holes appear on unfolding, and where?
(a) Four holes in a square pattern (b) One hole at the centre (c) Two holes, mirror images across the diagonal (d) Two holes on the diagonal itself
Answer: (c)
Solution: Choosing four holes assumes two folds; there is only one. One fold gives 2 layers, so the punch makes 2 holes mirrored across the diagonal, so the answer is two holes, mirror images across the diagonal.
Source: PYQ-style

70. A paper is folded twice (in half, then in half again perpendicularly). A cut is made removing the corner where all folds meet. On unfolding, the sheet has:
(a) A hole at the centre (b) Corners cut off (c) A hole at one corner (d) No change
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer is cut-off corners, but the corner where the folds meet is the centre of the original sheet, not its outer corners. Removing it creates a hole at the centre, so the answer is a hole at the centre.
Source: PYQ-style

71. A square sheet is folded into quarters. A punch is made exactly on one fold edge (but not at the corner). On unfolding, the number of distinct holes is:
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 4, the layer count. A punch exactly on a fold edge is shared by the two halves across that fold, so pairs of holes merge; only 2 distinct holes appear, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

73. A sheet is folded along the vertical centre line, then along the horizontal centre line. A punch is made at the exact centre of the folded quarter. On unfolding, the four holes form:
