101. A square sheet is folded once along a vertical line so that the left half lies over the right half. A single circular hole is punched through both layers near the fold line. After unfolding, how many holes appear on the sheet?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: One fold creates two layers, so one punch passes through both layers and produces two holes that are mirror images across the fold line. The tempting answer 1 is wrong because the punch does not lie exactly on the fold line, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

102. A rectangular sheet is folded along its horizontal midline (bottom half up over the top half), and then a triangular cut is made at the folded edge. After unfolding, the cut appears as which shape?
(a) A rhombus/diamond (b) A triangle (c) A rectangle (d) A semicircle
Answer: (a)
Solution: A cut made exactly on a fold line mirrors itself when unfolded, so two congruent triangles join base-to-base along the fold. Choosing a triangle ignores this mirror effect, so the answer is a rhombus/diamond.
Source: PYQ-style

103. A square paper is folded along both diagonals (one after the other) into a small triangle. One hole is punched through all layers at a point away from the centre. How many holes appear after complete unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 6 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Folding along one diagonal gives 2 layers; folding along the second diagonal doubles it to 4 layers, so one punch gives 4 holes symmetric about both diagonals. The answer 8 is a trap from doubling once too often, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

104. A sheet is folded in half vertically, then the folded sheet is again folded in half horizontally. A punch is made at the corner where the two fold edges meet. After unfolding, where do the holes lie?
(a) Four holes at the four corners of the sheet (b) Four holes symmetric about the centre (c) Two holes on the vertical midline (d) One hole at the centre of the sheet
Answer: (d)
Solution: The corner where both folded edges meet corresponds to the intersection of the two fold lines, i.e., the centre of the original sheet, and all four layers share this single point. Assuming four separate holes ignores that the punched point lies on both folds, so the answer is one hole at the centre.
Source: PYQ-style

105. A square sheet is folded along a diagonal. A cut is made parallel to the fold, midway between the fold and the opposite corner. After unfolding, how many cut lines appear and how are they placed?
(a) One cut line on the diagonal (b) Two parallel cut lines on the same side (c) Two cut lines, mirror images across the diagonal (d) Four cut lines forming a square
Answer: (c)
Solution: The cut passes through the two layers created by the single diagonal fold, and on unfolding the two cuts are reflections of each other across the diagonal. Cuts on the same side are wrong because unfolding separates the layers to opposite sides, so the answer is two mirror-image cut lines.
Source: PYQ-style

106. A circular paper is folded into a semicircle, then folded again into a quarter circle. A small notch is cut at the midpoint of the curved (arc) edge. After unfolding fully, how many notches appear on the boundary of the circle?
(a) 2 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: Two folds create 4 layers, and the arc edge is common to all layers, so one notch cuts all 4 layers and produces 4 notches on the circle's boundary. The answer 8 wrongly doubles again, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

107. A sheet is folded in half (left over right). Two punches are made: one near the fold and one near the open edge. After unfolding, how many holes appear in total?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each punch passes through 2 layers, so each produces 2 holes after unfolding, giving 2 + 2 = 4 holes in mirrored pairs. Counting only the visible punches gives the trap answer 2, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

108. A square sheet is folded along the vertical midline, then along the horizontal midline, giving a small square. A punch is made at the corner of the small square that is free (not on any fold). After unfolding, the holes are located:
(a) At the centre of the sheet (b) At the four corners of the original sheet (c) Two holes on the horizontal midline (d) One hole at a corner
Answer: (b)
Solution: The free corner of the doubly folded small square corresponds to the four outer corners of the original sheet stacked together, so one punch through 4 layers yields holes at all four corners. The centre is the trap, since the centre corresponds to the folded corner, not the free one, so the answer is the four corners.
Source: PYQ-style

109. A paper is folded once horizontally. A straight cut is made from the folded edge to the open edge, perpendicular to the fold. After unfolding, the sheet becomes:
(a) Three separate pieces (b) One piece with a slit (c) Two separate pieces (d) One piece with a hole
Answer: (c)
Solution: The cut runs the full width of the folded sheet, from the folded edge straight across to the open edge, severing every layer along that line; on unfolding, the cut extends from the midline of the original sheet to its outer edge, splitting it into two separate pieces. The trap is thinking the fold keeps the sheet joined, but the cut begins at the fold itself and breaks that join, so the answer is two separate pieces.
Source: PYQ-style

110. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal into a triangle. A semicircular cut is made with its diameter on the fold line. After unfolding, the hole formed is:
(a) A full circle (b) An ellipse (c) A crescent (d) A semicircle
Answer: (a)
Solution: A semicircular cut whose diameter lies on the fold mirrors into an identical semicircle on the other side when unfolded, and two semicircles joined along the diameter form a full circle. The trap is answering semicircle by forgetting the mirror image, so the answer is a full circle.
Source: PYQ-style

111. A rectangular sheet is folded into thirds along its length (two parallel folds, like a letter fold). One hole is punched through all layers away from the folds. After unfolding, how many holes appear?
(a) 3 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Folding into thirds stacks 3 layers, so a single punch through 3 layers produces 3 holes spaced according to the two fold lines. The trap answer 4 assumes two folds double the layers, but a letter fold gives 3 layers, not 4, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

112. A sheet is folded in half vertically and then in half vertically again (both folds parallel). A punch is made away from all fold lines. How many holes appear after unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each fold doubles the layers: 2 layers after the first fold, 4 after the second, so one punch through 4 layers gives 4 holes arranged symmetrically about both fold lines. The answer 8 is a trap from over-doubling, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

113. A square paper is folded along a diagonal, and then the resulting triangle is folded in half again. A punch is made exactly at the vertex where the two fold edges meet. After unfolding, the hole(s) appear:
(a) At two opposite corners of the square (b) At the centre of the square as a single hole (c) At four points on the diagonals (d) At the midpoints of the four sides
Answer: (b)
Solution: The meeting point of the two fold edges corresponds to the centre of the original square, where the diagonal and the triangle's midline intersect, and all layers share this point, so one punch gives a single hole there. Four points is the trap for ignoring that the punch lies on both folds, so the answer is a single hole at the centre of the square.
Source: PYQ-style

114. A sheet is folded once along the horizontal midline. A V-shaped cut is made with its vertex on the fold line, removing a small triangular piece of paper. After unfolding, the hole formed in the sheet is:
(a) A triangle (b) A diamond (rhombus) (c) A V shape (d) A rectangle
Answer: (b)
Solution: The triangular piece removed has its apex on the fold, so on unfolding it mirrors into a congruent triangle on the other side, and two triangles joined base-to-base along the fold form a diamond. The trap is answering triangle by forgetting the mirror half, so the answer is a diamond (rhombus).
Source: PYQ-style

115. A circular sheet is folded along a diameter. A punch is made exactly on the fold line. After unfolding, how many holes appear?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: A punch exactly on the fold line passes through both layers at the same location, so the two holes coincide into one when unfolded. The trap answer 2 treats it like any other punch, but a point on the fold is its own mirror image, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

116. A square sheet is folded in half vertically, then a quarter-circle cut is made at the top corner of the folded edge. After unfolding, the shape cut out at the top edge is:
(a) A full circle (b) A quarter circle (c) A semicircle (d) A square notch
Answer: (c)
Solution: The quarter-circle cut at the folded edge mirrors across the fold, and two quarter circles combine into a semicircle centred on the fold at the top edge. A full circle would require two folds, which is the trap, so the answer is a semicircle.
Source: PYQ-style

117. A paper is folded along the vertical midline and then along the horizontal midline. Two punches are made on the folded quarter: one on the vertical fold edge and one in the interior. After unfolding, the total number of holes is:
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 8 (d) 6
Answer: (d)
Solution: The interior punch passes through 4 layers giving 4 holes, while the punch on the vertical fold edge lies on one fold, so its mirror across that fold coincides and gives only 2 holes; total = 4 + 2 = 6. The trap answer 8 counts both punches as 4 each, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

118. A sheet is folded in half (right half over left). A straight cut is made parallel to the fold, entirely in the interior (not touching any edge). After unfolding, the sheet has:
(a) Two holes (b) Two parallel slits (c) One hole (d) One slit
Answer: (b)
Solution: An interior straight cut through 2 layers produces two slits that are mirror images across the vertical fold, one on each half of the sheet. One slit is the trap for forgetting the second layer, so the answer is two parallel slits.
Source: PYQ-style

119. A square sheet is folded along both midlines into a small square. A punch is made at the centre of the small square (interior, not on any fold). After unfolding, the four holes are arranged:
(a) Along one diagonal (b) Along the horizontal midline only (c) At the vertices of a smaller square centred on the sheet (d) Randomly
Answer: (c)
Solution: The punch point reflects across both midlines, producing four holes at equal distances on either side of the centre — the vertices of a smaller square symmetric about the centre. Arrangement along one line is the trap from considering only one fold, so the answer is the vertices of a smaller square.
Source: PYQ-style

120. A rectangular sheet is folded in half lengthwise (top over bottom). A triangular cut is made at the open (free) edge, not touching the fold. After unfolding, how many triangular notches appear and where?
(a) One diamond-shaped hole in the interior (b) One notch on the top edge (c) Two notches side by side on the top edge (d) Two notches, one on the top edge and one on the bottom edge
Answer: (d)
Solution: The cut at the open edge passes through both free edges (the top and bottom edges of the original sheet), so unfolding gives one notch on the top edge and its mirror on the bottom edge. A single notch is the trap for ignoring the second layer, so the answer is two notches on opposite edges.
Source: PYQ-style

121. A square sheet is folded along the vertical midline, then along the horizontal midline, and finally along the diagonal of the resulting small square. A single punch is made in the interior of the final folded triangle, away from all edges. How many holes appear after complete unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 16 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each of the three folds doubles the layers: 2, then 4, then 8, and a punch in the interior passes through all 8 layers, giving 8 holes symmetric about all three fold lines. The trap answer 16 doubles once too many, and 4 ignores the diagonal fold, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

122. A sheet is folded in half vertically, then in half horizontally. A punch is made exactly at the midpoint of the horizontal fold edge of the folded quarter (on the horizontal fold, but not on the vertical fold). After unfolding, how many distinct holes appear?
(a) 3 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The punch lies on the horizontal fold, so reflection across that fold maps the hole onto itself; only reflection across the vertical fold creates a distinct second hole, giving 2 distinct holes from 4 layers. The trap answer 4 treats the punch as fully interior, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

123. A circular sheet is folded into a semicircle, then into a quarter, then once more into an eighth (three folds, all through the centre). A notch is cut on the curved edge of the final wedge. After full unfolding, how many notches appear on the circle's boundary?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 16 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: Three folds through the centre create 8 layers, and the arc is common to all of them, so one notch cuts all 8 layers and produces 8 equally spaced notches on the boundary. The trap answer 4 counts only two folds, so the answer is 8.

124. A square sheet is folded along one diagonal. Two punches are made: punch P on the fold line, and punch Q in the interior of the folded triangle. After unfolding, the total number of distinct holes is:
(a) 2 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: Punch P lies on the fold, so its two layer-holes coincide into 1 distinct hole, while punch Q is interior and gives 2 distinct mirror-image holes; total = 1 + 2 = 3. The trap answer 4 counts both punches as producing 2 holes each, ignoring the fold-line coincidence, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

125. A rectangular sheet is folded in half vertically (left over right), then the folded sheet is folded in half horizontally (top over bottom). A straight cut is made joining the midpoints of the two folded edges of the small rectangle, and the small corner piece containing the folded corner is removed. After unfolding, the sheet becomes:
(a) Four separate pieces (b) One piece with a diamond-shaped hole at the centre (c) Two separate rectangular pieces (d) One piece with a slit from edge to edge
Answer: (b)
Solution: The cut joins the two fold edges, and on unfolding it reflects across both folds to form a closed diamond-shaped cut line around the centre; removing the folded corner piece removes the central diamond, leaving the outer sheet as a single piece with a diamond hole. The trap answers of two or four pieces assume the cuts run to the outer edges, which they do not, so the answer is one piece with a diamond-shaped hole at the centre.
Source: PYQ-style
