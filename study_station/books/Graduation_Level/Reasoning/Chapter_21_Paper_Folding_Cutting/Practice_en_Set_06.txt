126. A square sheet is folded along its vertical centre line, then along its horizontal centre line. A single circular punch is made exactly at the corner of the folded quarter that lies at the centre of the original sheet. How many holes appear when the sheet is unfolded?
(a) 4 (b) 2 (c) 8 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is 4, from blindly doubling once for each fold. But the punched corner is the exact centre of the original sheet, which lies on both fold lines, so every reflection maps it onto itself and all layers share that single point, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

127. A rectangular sheet is folded once vertically (left half over right half) and then once horizontally (top over bottom). Two punches are made on the folded piece, both away from the fold edges. How many holes will the unfolded sheet show?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to count only the visible 2 punches and answer 2, or to double only once and answer 4. Each of the two folds doubles every punch, so each punch produces 2 × 2 = 4 holes; two punches give 4 × 2 = 8 holes, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style
