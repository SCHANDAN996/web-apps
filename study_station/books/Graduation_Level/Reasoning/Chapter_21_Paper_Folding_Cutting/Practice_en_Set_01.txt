1. A square sheet of paper is folded once along its vertical midline and a single hole is punched through the folded layers. How many holes appear when the sheet is unfolded?
(a) 1 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: One fold creates two layers, so one punch passes through both layers. On unfolding, the hole is mirrored across the fold line, giving two holes symmetric about the vertical midline. The tempting answer 4 assumes two folds, but there is only one fold here, so the answer is 2.
Source: PYQ-style

2. A sheet is folded twice — first along the vertical midline, then along the horizontal midline. One hole is punched through all layers. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: Each fold doubles the number of layers: 2 layers after the first fold, 4 after the second. One punch therefore makes 4 holes, mirrored across both fold lines. The tempting answer 8 wrongly doubles once more, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

3. A paper is folded along its diagonal and a cut is made near the fold. When unfolded, the cut pattern will be:
(a) Asymmetric about the diagonal (b) Randomly placed (c) Symmetric about the vertical midline (d) Symmetric about the diagonal
Answer: (d)
Solution: Any cut made on folded paper is reflected across the fold line when the paper is opened. Since the fold here is the diagonal, the resulting pattern must be mirror-symmetric about that diagonal, so the answer is symmetric about the diagonal.
Source: PYQ-style

4. A square sheet is folded into half vertically (left half over right half). A semicircular cut is made on the fold edge. What shape appears on the fold line after unfolding?
(a) A full circle (b) A semicircle (c) A square (d) A triangle
Answer: (a)
Solution: A semicircle cut on the fold edge is mirrored to the other half on unfolding, and two mirror-image semicircles join to form a full circle. The tempting answer is a semicircle, which ignores the mirroring, so the answer is a full circle.

6. A paper is folded twice along both midlines (vertical and horizontal). A hole is punched exactly at the corner where the two fold lines meet. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: A punch exactly on the intersection of both fold lines lies on both mirror axes, so all its reflections coincide at the same single point — the centre of the sheet. The tempting answer 4 applies the usual doubling rule without noticing the punch is on the fold intersection, so the answer is 1.

7. A sheet is folded once vertically and then once horizontally. A hole is punched on one fold line (but not at the intersection). How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: A punch lying on one fold line is mirrored only across the other fold line; its reflection across the fold it sits on coincides with itself. Hence only 2 distinct holes appear. The tempting answer 4 ignores that the punch lies on a fold, so the answer is 2.

8. In paper folding questions, the golden rule for counting holes is:
(a) Holes = 2 raised to the number of folds, adjusted for punches on fold lines (b) Holes = number of folds (c) Holes = number of cuts plus one (d) Holes are always 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: Each fold doubles the layers, so a punch through n folds normally gives 2^n holes; but punches lying on a fold line or at a fold intersection produce fewer distinct holes. The other options are fixed rules that fail in such cases, so the answer is 2 raised to the number of folds, adjusted for punches on fold lines.
Source: PYQ-style

16. While solving paper folding problems, the correct solving order is:
(a) Start from the first fold and move forward (b) Count only the visible holes (c) Start from the last fold and unfold step by step in reverse (d) Guess from the final folded figure
Answer: (c)
Solution: Unfolding in reverse order — undoing the last fold first — lets you mirror each hole or cut one step at a time without confusion. Starting forward or guessing leads to missed reflections, so the answer is start from the last fold and unfold step by step in reverse.
Source: PYQ-style

17. A paper is folded twice (vertical then horizontal). A hole is punched at the free corner of the folded quarter (the corner opposite to all folds). After unfolding, the holes are located:
(a) All at the centre (b) At the four corners of the sheet (c) Only at two corners (d) Along one edge only
Answer: (b)
Solution: The free corner of the folded quarter corresponds to all four outer corners of the original sheet stacked together. Unfolding mirrors the punch to all four corners. The tempting answer places holes at the centre, which would happen only if the punch were at the fold intersection, so the answer is at the four corners of the sheet.
Source: PYQ-style

18. A sheet is folded once vertically. Two holes are punched on the folded sheet, one on the fold line and one away from it. How many holes appear on unfolding?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: The punch away from the fold gives 2 holes after mirroring, while the punch on the fold line stays as a single hole because its reflection coincides with itself. Total = 2 + 1 = 3. The tempting answer 4 mirrors both punches, so the answer is 3.
