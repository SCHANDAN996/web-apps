# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Paper Folding & Cutting

---

**Box 1 — "MIRROR, MIRROR, EVERY FOLD!"**
Every fold is a mirror. Whatever is cut on top gets **reflected** across the fold line. One fold = one mirror image. Two folds = mirror of a mirror. Say it aloud: *"Fold = Mirror, Cut = Multiply!"*

---

**Box 2 — "DOUBLE TROUBLE"**
Holes multiply by **2 for every fold** the cut passes through.
- 1 fold → hole count × 2
- 2 folds → × 4
- 3 folds → × 8
Chant: *"One fold two, two folds four, three folds eight — don't ask for more!"*

---

**Box 3 — "LAST FOLD, FIRST OPEN"**
Unfolding happens in **reverse order** — like unwrapping a gift. The fold made LAST opens FIRST. Memory hook: **"LIFO — Last In, First Out"** (borrow it from your computer friends!).

---

**Box 4 — "CORNER CUT = CENTER PARTY"**
A cut made at the **folded corner** (where all folds meet) lands at the **center** of the opened sheet. A cut on a folded **edge** lands on a **line of symmetry**. Hook: *"Corner cuts go to the heart, edge cuts sit on the mirror line."*

---

**Box 5 — "ODD-EVEN POLICE"**
If the paper is folded **once** and you see an **odd** number of holes in an option — arrest that option! One fold can only give **even** hole counts (pairs). Odd count = impossible = eliminate instantly.

---

**Box 6 — "TRACE BACKWARD, WIN FORWARD"**
Never guess forward. Start from the **final folded figure**, take the last cut, and **reflect it back** step by step. Hook: *"Detectives solve crimes backwards — so do toppers."*

---

**Box 7 — "HALF-CUT HERO"**
A shape cut **half on the fold line** opens into a **full symmetric shape** (half-heart on the fold → full heart when opened). Remember the school craft heart? *"Cut half on the fold, get whole when unrolled."*

---

**Box 8 — "PUNCH = STAMP"**
Paper punching is just stamping: the punch **stamps itself** on every layer beneath it. Layers = 2^(folds). Hook: *"One punch, many stamps — count the layers, not the luck."*

---

**Box 9 — "ARROW FOLDS TRAVEL"**
When the fold arrow points **left**, the right part flips left — the design **travels in the arrow's direction and flips**. Hook: *"Follow the arrow, then flip like a pancake."*

---

**Box 10 — "SYMMETRY IS YOUR X-RAY"**
The final opened sheet is **always symmetric** about every fold line. If an option breaks symmetry even slightly — delete it. Hook: *"No symmetry, no entry."*

---

**Box 11 — "COUNT BEFORE YOU CUT"**
Before solving, count: (a) number of folds, (b) number of cuts/punches. Expected holes ≤ cuts × 2^(folds). Any option exceeding this is garbage. Hook: *"Math first, eyes later."*

---

**Box 12 — "DIAGONAL FOLD = DIAMOND MIND"**
A diagonal fold reflects across the **diagonal line itself** — the two corners **on** the fold line stay fixed, and the other two corners swap with each other. So a fold along the top-left to bottom-right diagonal swaps top-right with bottom-left; a fold along the top-right to bottom-left diagonal swaps top-left with bottom-right. Students forget this and reflect horizontally. Hook: *"Diagonal fold? Think diamond, not door — corners on the line stay put, the other two swap."*

---

**Box 13 — "TRANSPARENT PAPER TRICK"**
Imagine the folded paper is **transparent** — the cut shines through every layer, and each layer is a rotated/flipped copy. Hook: *"Be X-ray vision: see through the layers."*

---

**Box 14 — "ELIMINATE IN 3 PASSES"**
Pass 1: kill options with wrong **hole count**.
Pass 2: kill options breaking **symmetry**.
Pass 3: kill options with wrong **position** (corner vs edge vs center).
Hook: *"Count → Symmetry → Position. Three strikes and the wrong options are out."*

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip the Question

1. **The 15-Second Rule:** If after 15 seconds you cannot tell *how many folds* and *in which order* they were made, **mark and skip**. Folding order confusion = guaranteed trap. Return only if time remains.

2. **3+ Folds with Diagonal + Punch Combo:** If the figure has 3 or more folds **including a diagonal fold** AND multiple punches, the reflection chain is long and error-prone. If you can't eliminate at least 2 options in 20 seconds, **skip it** — this question is designed to eat 2 minutes.

3. **"Which paper was cut?" Reverse Questions:** When the question shows the *opened* sheet and asks for the *folding/cutting sequence*, and the options differ only subtly — skip unless you can verify one full fold-step quickly. These have the highest silly-mistake rate in the chapter.

**Golden Rule:** One skipped trap question = time saved for two sure-shot questions. Paper Folding questions are **scoring only when solved by method, never by hurry.**
