76. If '+' means '÷', '÷' means '×', '×' means '−' and '−' means '+', then the value of 24 + 4 × 5 − 2 ÷ 3 is:
(a) 5 (b) 7 (c) 9 (d) 11
Answer: (b)
Solution: Substituting the symbols: 24 ÷ 4 − 5 + 2 × 3. By BODMAS: 24 ÷ 4 = 6 and 2 × 3 = 6, so 6 − 5 + 6 = 7. The trap is computing left to right without BODMAS, which gives a wrong value. So the answer is 7.
Source: PYQ-style

77. If '×' means '+', '+' means '÷', '÷' means '−' and '−' means '×', then 8 × 6 + 3 ÷ 5 − 2 = ?
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: Substituting: 8 + 6 ÷ 3 − 5 × 2. By BODMAS: 6 ÷ 3 = 2 and 5 × 2 = 10, so 8 + 2 − 10 = 0. The trap is swapping the mappings of '÷' and '−', which leads to a wrong positive value. So the answer is 0.

78. If '+' means '×', '−' means '+', '×' means '÷' and '÷' means '−', then 7 + 3 − 20 × 4 ÷ 6 = ?

Answer: (c)
Solution: Substituting: 7 × 3 + 20 ÷ 4 − 6. By BODMAS: 7 × 3 = 21 and 20 ÷ 4 = 5, so 21 + 5 − 6 = 20. The trap is reading '20 × 4' as multiplication even after substitution; here '×' has become '÷'. So the answer is 20.

79. Select the correct combination of mathematical signs to replace * so that 18 * 3 * 2 * 3 = 24 is true.

Solution: Trying option (c): 18 × 3 ÷ 2 − 3 = 54 ÷ 2 − 3 = 27 − 3 = 24, which is true. The trap is option (a): 18 ÷ 3 × 2 + 3 = 15, which looks close to a plausible result but fails. So the answer is ×, ÷, −.

80. If 5 * 3 = 34, 7 * 2 = 53 and 4 * 6 = 52, then 8 * 5 = ?
(a) 98 (b) 89 (c) 94 (d) 104
Answer: (b)
Solution: The pattern is a * b = a² + b²: 25 + 9 = 34, 49 + 4 = 53, 16 + 36 = 52. The trap is assuming a² + b² + ab, which overshoots every example. Hence 8 * 5 = 64 + 25 = 89, so the answer is 89.

81. If 12 @ 4 = 8, 15 @ 5 = 10 and 21 @ 7 = 14, then 36 @ 9 = ?
(a) 27 (b) 20 (c) 24 (d) 30
Answer: (a)
Solution: The rule is simple subtraction: 12 − 4 = 8, 15 − 5 = 10, 21 − 7 = 14. The trap is over-complicating with division-based formulas when subtraction fits all three examples. Hence 36 @ 9 = 36 − 9 = 27, so the answer is 27.

82. Which interchange of signs will make the equation 5 + 3 × 8 − 12 ÷ 4 = 3 correct?

Answer: (b)
Solution: Interchanging − and ÷: 5 + 3 × 8 ÷ 12 − 4 = 5 + 2 − 4 = 3, which is correct. The trap is option (a): interchanging + and − gives 5 − 3 × 8 + 12 ÷ 4 = 5 − 24 + 3 = −16, which fails badly. So the answer is − and ÷.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The pattern is a % b = (a + b)²: 8² = 64, 9² = 81, 8² = 64. The trap is assuming a² + b², which fails on the very first example since 9 + 25 = 34 ≠ 64. Hence 5 % 6 = 11² = 121, so the answer is 121.

Solution: The rule is (sum of digits of one number)²: (1+1)² = 4, (2+2)² = 16, (3+3)² = 36. The trap is using the digit-sum of the actual product, which fails on the third example (1+0+8+9 = 18 ≠ 36). Hence (4+4)² = 64, so the answer is 64.
