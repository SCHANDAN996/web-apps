# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Mathematical Operations

---

**Box 1 — BODMAS Order**
🧠 *"Big Oxen March Ahead Slowly"*
**B**rackets → **O**f (multiplication) → **D**ivision → **M**ultiplication → **A**ddition → **S**ubtraction
Remember: 'Of' is a hidden multiplication and comes right after brackets — and never let Addition jump the queue before Multiplication!

---

**Box 2 — Bracket Priority**
🧠 *"Bar Opens, Braces Close, Brackets Last"*
Bar (vinculum) → ( ) → { } → [ ]
Innermost first, always!

---

**Box 3 — Symbol Substitution Questions**
🧠 *"Change the Costume, Not the Actor"*
When '+' means '×', only the symbol changes its dress — the numbers and the BODMAS rule stay the same. Rewrite the whole equation first, THEN solve. Never solve while translating!

---

**Box 4 — Interchanging Two Signs**
🧠 *"Swap the Twins, Check the Options"*
If '+' and '−' interchange, swap them in EVERY option, then test each option by BODMAS. Don't test the original equation — the twins have already exchanged places!

---

**Box 5 — Interchanging Two Numbers**
🧠 *"Numbers Play Musical Chairs"*
When digits/numbers interchange (e.g., 3 and 5 swap), rewrite each option with the swapped numbers. The equation that balances after the music stops is your answer.

---

**Box 6 — Balancing the Equation (which sign fits?)**
🧠 *"Feed the Hungry Gap One Sign at a Time"*
For "which sign makes 8 __ 4 __ 2 = 10 true?" — plug in options one by one. Start with × and ÷ (they change values fastest), because they usually crack the equation quicker than + and −.

---

**Box 7 — Correcting a Wrong Equation**
🧠 *"The Equation is Lying — Find the Honest Swap"*
Given a false equation, ask: which single interchange of signs/numbers makes it true? Test the swap that affects the BIGGEST numbers first — lies usually hide there.

---

**Box 8 — Division & Multiplication are Equal Partners**
🧠 *"× and ÷ are Twins — Left One Wins"*
Same priority for × and ÷ (and for + and −). Solve left to right. *"When twins fight, the elder (left) goes first."*
So 12 ÷ 4 × 3 = 3 × 3 = 9, NOT 12 ÷ 12 = 1.

---

**Box 9 — The 'Of' Trap**
🧠 *"OF is a Hidden Multiply"*
"4 of 3" = 4 × 3. *"Whenever you see OF, multiply — don't be shy!"* And 'of' is solved BEFORE division: 24 ÷ 4 of 2 = 24 ÷ 8 = 3.

---

**Box 10 — Sign-Code Words (÷ as 'P', × as 'Q'…)**
🧠 *"Decode the Secret Letter First"*
If P = +, Q = −, R = ×, S = ÷, then write the decoded equation on paper BEFORE touching any calculation. *"Decode fully, then compute — half-decoding is half-wrong."*

---

**Box 11 — Checking Options Fast**
🧠 *"Estimate Before You Calculate"*
If the RHS is small (like 2 or 3) but the LHS has big numbers, division must be involved somewhere. If RHS is huge, look for ×. *"The size of the answer whispers the sign."*

---

**Box 12 — Fraction Bars are Brackets in Disguise**
🧠 *"Every Fraction Wears an Invisible Bracket"*
(8 + 4)/(5 − 3) means (8+4) ÷ (5−3). Solve numerator and denominator fully BEFORE dividing. *"Clean both floors, then take the stairs."*

---

**Box 13 — Negative Results Warning**
🧠 *"Subtraction Can Go Below Zero — That's Legal!"*
In interchange questions, if a step gives a negative number, don't panic and don't reject the option. Negatives are allowed mid-calculation; only the final equality matters.

---

**Box 14 — One-Equation-One-Substitution Rule**
🧠 *"One Question, One Rewrite"*
Rewrite the substituted equation ONCE, neatly, on rough paper. Students who substitute "in their head" mix old and new signs — the #1 cause of silly errors in this chapter.

---

**Box 15 — The 30-Second Verdict**
🧠 *"Decode in 10, Solve in 20"*
A standard symbol-substitution question should take max 30 seconds: 10 sec to rewrite, 20 sec for BODMAS. If it's taking longer, you've misread a sign — re-read, don't re-calculate blindly.

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip

1. **Multi-blank balancing questions (3+ blanks):** If the equation needs 3 or more signs filled and trial-and-error isn't converging within 40–45 seconds, mark it and move on. Return only if time remains — these are time-traps disguised as easy questions.

2. **Double interchange (signs + numbers together):** If BOTH two signs AND two numbers interchange in the same question, the option-checking cost doubles. If the first option test takes over 30 seconds, skip — attempt it in the second pass.

3. **Pattern invisible in 15 seconds:** For "find the correct equation" types where none of the first two options balance and you can't spot why, don't brute-force all four. Flag it, bank your sure questions first, and revisit with fresh eyes — the misread sign usually reveals itself on the second look.
