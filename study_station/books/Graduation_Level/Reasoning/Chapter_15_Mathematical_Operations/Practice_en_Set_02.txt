26. If '+' means '÷', '÷' means '×', '×' means '−' and '−' means '+', then the value of 36 + 6 × 3 − 2 ÷ 4 is:
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 11
Answer: (d)
Solution: Replace the symbols: 36 ÷ 6 − 3 + 2 × 4. By BODMAS, 36 ÷ 6 = 6 and 2 × 4 = 8, giving 6 − 3 + 8 = 11. The trap is forgetting that '×' becomes '−', which would wrongly give 6 + 3 + 8 = 17, so the answer is 11.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Which interchange of signs makes the equation 5 + 3 × 8 − 12 ÷ 4 = 3 correct?
(a) + and − (b) − and ÷ (c) + and × (d) × and ÷
Answer: (b)
Solution: Interchanging − and ÷ gives 5 + 3 × 8 ÷ 12 − 4 = 5 + 2 − 4 = 3. The trap is trying + and − first, which gives 5 − 3 × 8 ÷ 12 + 4 = 5 − 2 + 4 = 7, not 3, so the answer is − and ÷.

30. If 7 * 5 = 24 and 9 * 4 = 26, where '*' is a defined operation, then 8 * 6 equals:
(a) 28 (b) 26 (c) 30 (d) 32
Answer: (a)
Solution: The rule is a * b = 2(a + b): 2 × 12 = 24 and 2 × 13 = 26, both fit. So 8 * 6 = 2 × 14 = 28. The trap is assuming a product-based rule like a × b − something, which fits neither example, so the answer is 28.
