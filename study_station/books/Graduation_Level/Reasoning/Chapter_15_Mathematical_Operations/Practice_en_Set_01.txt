1. If '+' means '÷', '÷' means '×', '×' means '−' and '−' means '+', then the value of 48 + 8 × 5 − 3 ÷ 2 is
(a) 11 (b) 9 (c) 5 (d) 7
Answer: (d)
Solution: Substitute the symbols: 48 ÷ 8 − 5 + 3 × 2. By BODMAS: 48 ÷ 8 = 6 and 3 × 2 = 6, giving 6 − 5 + 6 = 7. The trap option 9 comes from adding 5 instead of subtracting it, so the answer is 7.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (c)
Solution: Substitute: 6 × 2 + 9 − 4 ÷ 2. By BODMAS: 6 × 2 = 12 and 4 ÷ 2 = 2, giving 12 + 9 − 2 = 19. The trap option 21 comes from forgetting to subtract the 2, so the answer is 19.

4. Which interchange of signs makes the equation 5 + 3 × 8 − 12 ÷ 4 = 3 correct?
(a) + and − (b) × and ÷ (c) + and ÷ (d) − and ÷
Answer: (d)
Solution: Interchanging − and ÷ gives 5 + 3 × 8 ÷ 12 − 4 = 5 + 2 − 4 = 3, which is correct. The trap is interchanging + and −, which gives 5 − 3 × 8 − 12 ÷ 4, clearly not 3, so the answer is − and ÷.

5. If 4 * 3 = 25 and 5 * 2 = 29, where '*' is a defined operation, then 6 * 4 equals
(a) 48 (b) 50 (c) 52 (d) 40
Answer: (c)
Solution: The pattern is a * b = a² + b²: 4² + 3² = 16 + 9 = 25 and 5² + 2² = 25 + 4 = 29. The trap is assuming a×b + a + b, which fails the second check. So 6 * 4 = 36 + 16 = 52, so the answer is 52.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (a)
Solution: After interchanging 4↔9 and +↔×, the equation 4 × 9 + 3 = 21 becomes 9 + 4 × 3 = 9 + 12 = 21, which is correct. The other options give 39 or 31 after interchange, none matching their stated right side, so the answer is 4 × 9 + 3 = 21.

11. If 12 (×) 6 = 2, 15 (−) 5 = 10 and 8 (+) 4 = 12 follow normal meanings, find the value of 20 ÷ 5 + 3 × 2 − 4.
(a) 8 (b) 6 (c) 10 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a plain BODMAS check: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 and 3 × 2 = 6, so 4 + 6 − 4 = 6. The trap option 10 comes from adding before dividing, treating it as 20 ÷ (5 + 3), so the answer is 6.

25. In a certain code, if 8 # 2 = 4, 12 # 3 = 4 and 20 # 5 = 4, then 36 # 9 equals
(a) 4 (b) 27 (c) 9 (d) 45
Answer: (a)
Solution: The pattern is a # b = a ÷ b, since 8 ÷ 2 = 4, 12 ÷ 3 = 4 and 20 ÷ 5 = 4. The trap is assuming the constant result 4 must break for new numbers — but 36 ÷ 9 = 4 too, so the answer is 4.
