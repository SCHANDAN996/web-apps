# 🧒 The Feynman Way — Mathematical Operations, Explained Like You're 12

## The Story: The Secret Code of the Market

Imagine you're 12 years old, and your grandmother sends you to the market with a **secret shopping list**. But Grandma loves puzzles, so she doesn't write the list in normal maths. She writes it in a **code**:

> "Buy vegetables worth:  8 **Δ** 4 **Ω** 2 **+** 6"

She hands you a decoder card:
- **Δ** means **×** (multiply)
- **Ω** means **÷** (divide)
- **+** means... well, just **+**!

So the real sum is: **8 × 4 ÷ 2 + 6**

Now here's where most kids (and most exam candidates!) mess up. They solve it **left to right** like reading a comic:
- 8 × 4 = 32
- 32 ÷ 2 = 16
- 16 + 6 = **22** ✅

Lucky — this one worked! But what if Grandma wrote **6 + 8 Δ 4 Ω 2**? Reading left to right: 6 + 8 = 14, × 4 = 56, ÷ 2 = 28. **Wrong!** The real answer: multiplication and division are the "VIP customers" — they get served **first**: 8 × 4 ÷ 2 = 16, then 6 + 16 = **22**.

That, in one story, is the **entire chapter** of Mathematical Operations:
1. **Decode the symbols** (Grandma's code card = the question's instructions).
2. **Respect the VIP order** (BODMAS — Brackets, Orders, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction).

## The Three Disguises This Chapter Wears in Exams

**Disguise 1 — Symbol Swap:** "If '+' means '×', '×' means '−', '−' means '÷' and '÷' means '+', find the value of…"
👉 Rewrite the whole expression in real symbols **first**, on paper, then apply BODMAS. Never solve in your head while translating — that's where marks die.

**Disguise 2 — Which Equation Is Correct?:** Four equations with weird symbols; only one is true.
👉 Don't solve all four fully. Test the **VIP operations first** (× and ÷). If 6 × 2 is written as 6 + 2, the equation is dead — move on in 5 seconds.

**Disguise 3 — Interchange:** "If we interchange '+' and '×', and also interchange 3 and 5, which equation becomes correct?"
👉 Two changes at once! Rewrite each option with **both** swaps applied, then check. Slow, careful rewriting beats fast guessing every single time.

## The Unforgettable Trick: **"The VIP Queue"** 🎟️

Think of every expression as a queue at a cinema ticket counter:

> **B**rackets are **VIPs with passes** — they skip everyone.
> **O**rders (powers/roots) are **celebrities** — next in line.
> **D**ivision and **M**ultiplication are **twins** — served together, left to right.
> **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction are **ordinary folks** — last, left to right.

**Memory hook:** *"Beta, Orders Diye, Magar Ab Sabko"* — **B-O-D-M-A-S**.

And for symbol-swap questions, remember: **"Decode first, calculate second."** Write the decoded expression on your rough sheet before touching any numbers. One line of rewriting saves you from the examiner's favourite trap — a question that *looks* like 30 seconds but punishes mental translation.

## The Examiner's Trap (So You Never Fall In)

The examiner's favourite trick: making **two options differ only in the order of operations**. E.g., for "12 − 4 × 2", one option gives 16 (the left-to-right trap: 12−4=8, ×2=16) and another gives 4 (correct: 4×2=8, 12−8=4). The trap option is *always* there, smiling at you. BODMAS is your shield.
