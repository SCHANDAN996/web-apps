101. If 'A' means '+', 'B' means '−', 'C' means '×' and 'D' means '÷', then the value of 12 C 3 A 8 B 6 D 2 is:
(a) 41 (b) 39 (c) 43 (d) 37
Answer: (a)
Solution: Replacing the letters gives 12 × 3 + 8 − 6 ÷ 2. By BODMAS, 12 × 3 = 36 and 6 ÷ 2 = 3, so 36 + 8 − 3 = 41. The tempting wrong option 39 comes from subtracting 6 from 8 before dividing, so the answer is 41.
