126. If 'P' means '×', 'Q' means '÷', 'R' means '+' and 'S' means '−', then the value of 18 Q 3 P 2 R 5 S 4 is:
(a) 11 (b) 17 (c) 13 (d) 15
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is solving strictly left to right and ignoring that × and ÷ come before + and −. Replace the symbols: 18 ÷ 3 × 2 + 5 − 4 = 6 × 2 + 5 − 4 = 12 + 5 − 4 = 13, so the answer is 13.
