126. Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F and G sit in a row facing north. E sits between C and D (next to both). B sits third to the right of G. D sits third to the right of A. How many persons sit between A and E?
(a) 1 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: G, A, F, B, D, E, C. So the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style
