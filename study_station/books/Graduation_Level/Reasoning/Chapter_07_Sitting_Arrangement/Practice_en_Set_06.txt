126. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a row facing north. P sits second from the left end. Q is to the immediate right of P. R sits at the right end. S sits between Q and T. Who sits at the left end?
(a) U (b) S (c) T (d) Q
Answer: (a)
Solution: P is 2nd, Q is 3rd and R is 6th. S between Q and T gives S 4th and T 5th, leaving only seat 1 for U. The trap is placing T between Q and S, but the clue fixes S next to Q, so the answer is U.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H sit in a row facing north. A is third from the left end. B is to the immediate right of A. C is fourth to the right of B. D sits between A and E. G is second to the left of C. F sits at an end. Who sits at the right end?
(a) G (b) F (c) H (d) C

Solution: A is 3rd, B is 4th and C, fourth to the right of B, is 8th — the right end itself. G is 6th, D sits between A and E, and F takes the left end. The trap is placing A third from the right, which pushes C off the row; that inconsistency is the check, so the answer is C.
