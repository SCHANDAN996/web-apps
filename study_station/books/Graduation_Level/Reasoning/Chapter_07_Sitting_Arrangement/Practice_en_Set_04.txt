76. Six persons S, T, U, V, W and X sit in a row facing north. T sits third to the left of V. U sits third to the right of S. X sits between S and V (next to both). Who sits to the immediate right of V?
(a) U (b) W (c) S (d) X
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: T, S, X, V, U, W. The trap is X, who is on the other side of V. So the answer is U.
Source: PYQ-style
