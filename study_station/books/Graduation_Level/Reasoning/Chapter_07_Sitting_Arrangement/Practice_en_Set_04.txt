76. Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits second to the left of B. C sits opposite A. D is an immediate neighbour of both B and C. E sits third to the right of D. F is not adjacent to A. G sits immediately to the left of A. Who sits second to the right of F?
(a) G (b) H (c) E (d) B
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fix A; B is second to A's right and C is opposite A. D must touch both B and C, so D sits between them, E goes third to D's right, and F takes the only seat away from A. G, fixed immediately left of A, then lands exactly second to F's right. The trap is assuming D can sit on either side of the B–C pair; only one side keeps F non-adjacent to A, so the answer is G.

77. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a row facing north. P sits at the left end. Q sits second to the right of P. R sits immediately to the left of T. S sits at the right end. Who sits third from the left end?
(a) R (b) U (c) Q (d) T
Answer: (c)
Solution: P is at position 1, so Q is at position 3. The R–T pair fits at positions 4 and 5, S takes position 6, and U fills position 2. The trap is placing the R–T pair at positions 2–3, but position 3 is already Q's, so the answer is Q.

78. Eight persons sit around a circular table facing the centre. K sits opposite L. M sits second to the right of K. N sits second to the right of L. Who sits opposite M?

Answer: (a)
Solution: Fix K and place L opposite it. M is two seats to K's right and N is two seats to L's right; because L is opposite K, N lands exactly opposite M. The trap is measuring N from K instead of from L, which shifts it one seat off, so the answer is N.

Answer: (a)
Solution: Left and right reverse when facing direction flips while seats stay fixed. Second to the right for an outward-facing person becomes second to the left for a centre-facing person in the same seat. The trap is keeping "right" unchanged after the flip, so the answer is second to the left.

Solution: Fifth from the right in a row of nine means position 5 from the left (9 − 5 + 1 = 5). Three places left of position 5 is position 2. The trap is computing 9 − 5 = 4 and then shifting, which double-counts, so the answer is 2nd.
