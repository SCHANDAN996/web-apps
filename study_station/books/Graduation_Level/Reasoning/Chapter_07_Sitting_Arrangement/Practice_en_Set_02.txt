26. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. C sits to the immediate right of B. D sits third to the left of B. A sits at one of the ends. F sits second from the left end. Who sits to the immediate right of E?
(a) B (b) F (c) A (d) C
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: D, F, E, B, C, A. The trap is F, who is on the other side of E. So the answer is B.
Source: PYQ-style
