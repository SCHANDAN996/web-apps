1. Five persons L, M, N, O and P sit in a row facing south. N sits second to the left of P. L sits second to the left of N. M sits third to the left of P. Who sits to the immediate right of L?
(a) O (b) P (c) M (d) N
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. As we look at them (they face us), from our left to our right: P, O, N, M, L. So the answer is M.
Source: PYQ-style
