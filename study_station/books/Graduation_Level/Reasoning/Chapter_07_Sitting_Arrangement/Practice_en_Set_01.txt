1. Five persons P, Q, R, S and T sit in a row facing north. Q sits to the immediate left of R. P sits at the left end. S sits exactly between R and T. Who sits in the middle of the row?
(a) Q (b) S (c) R (d) T
Answer: (c)
Solution: The order from left to right is P, Q, R, S, T. The trap is picking Q because the block Q-R is built first, but the middle (third) seat is held by R, so the answer is R.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Six friends P, Q, R, S, T and U sit around a circular table facing the centre. P sits second to the right of Q. R sits opposite P. S is not adjacent to Q. T sits to the immediate left of R. Who sits opposite Q?
(a) S (b) U (c) T (d) R
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fix Q. P is second to the right of Q, R is opposite P, and T, immediately left of R, takes the seat second to the left of Q. The two remaining seats are the one opposite Q and the one adjacent to Q; S cannot be adjacent to Q, so S sits opposite Q. The trap is choosing R, but R is opposite P, so the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style

3. In a row of students facing north, Ravi is 12th from the left and Suresh is 16th from the right. If they interchange their positions, Ravi becomes 18th from the left. How many students are there in the row?
(a) 33 (b) 34 (c) 32 (d) 35
Answer: (a)
Solution: After interchanging, Ravi takes Suresh's seat, which is 18th from the left and 16th from the right. Total = 18 + 16 − 1 = 33. The trap is forgetting to subtract 1, which gives 34, so the answer is 33.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing south. B sits in the middle seat. A sits to the immediate left of B. D sits to the immediate right of B. C sits at the viewer's left end. Who sits at the viewer's right end?
(a) A (b) D (c) C (d) E
Answer: (d)
Solution: Facing south, a person's left is the viewer's right and vice versa. B is in the middle, so A is on the viewer's right of B and D is on the viewer's left of B. With C at the viewer's left end, the order from the viewer's left is C, D, B, A, E. The trap is ignoring the south-facing reversal, so the answer is E.
Source: PYQ-style

5. Eight boxes are stacked one above another. Box A is immediately above Box B. Box C is below Box B but not at the bottom. Box D is at the top. Box E is immediately below Box C. Box F is above Box D. Box G is between Box E and Box H. Which box is at the bottom?
(a) E (b) H (c) B (d) G
Answer: (b)
Solution: F is above D, so F is position 8 and D is position 7. A is immediately above B: A6, B5. C is below B but not at the bottom: C4. E is immediately below C: E3. G is between E and H: G2, H1. The trap is choosing E, which is low but not last, so the answer is H.

Answer: (b)

11. Eight persons sit around a square table, four at the corners and four at the middle of the sides. Those at corners face the centre; those at the sides face outward. A sits at a corner. B sits second to the right of A. C faces A. D sits to the immediate left of C. If B faces outward, where does B sit?
(a) Opposite D (b) At a corner (c) At the middle of a side (d) Adjacent to C
Answer: (c)
Solution: A is at a corner facing the centre. Second to the right of a corner sitter lands on a middle-of-side seat, and side sitters face outward, which matches the given condition that B faces outward. The trap is assuming all eight face the same way and placing B at a corner, so the answer is "At the middle of a side".

12. In a row of 40 students facing north, Anil is 15th from the left. What is his position from the right?
(a) 24th (b) 27th (c) 25th (d) 26th
Answer: (d)
Solution: Position from right = total − position from left + 1 = 40 − 15 + 1 = 26. The trap is computing 40 − 15 = 25 and forgetting the +1, so the answer is 26th.

13. Five couples (ten persons) sit around a circular table. Each husband sits opposite his wife. If Mr. X sits second to the right of Mrs. Y, where does Mrs. X sit relative to Mrs. Y?
(a) Immediate right (b) Second to the left (c) Opposite (d) Third to the right
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mrs. X sits opposite Mr. X. Mr. X is second to the right of Mrs. Y, so the seat opposite him is second to the left of Mrs. Y, because opposite seats mirror direction in a circle. The trap is answering "opposite", which confuses Mrs. X's relation to Mr. X with her relation to Mrs. Y, so the answer is second to the left.

19. Fifteen persons stand in a line facing north. K is 4th from the left and L is 6th from the right. M stands exactly in the middle of K and L. What is M's position from the left?
(a) 6th (b) 5th (c) 7th (d) 8th
Answer: (c)
Solution: L is 6th from the right in a line of 15, so L is 15 − 6 + 1 = 10th from the left. The middle of positions 4 and 10 is (4 + 10) ÷ 2 = 7. The trap is averaging without first converting L's position to the same side, so the answer is 7th.

22. In a row facing north, X is 10th from the left and Y is 12th from the right. When they swap seats, X becomes 15th from the left. What is Y's new position from the right?
(a) 17th (b) 10th (c) 15th (d) 12th
Answer: (a)
Solution: After the swap, X occupies Y's old seat, which is 15th from the left and 12th from the right, so the row has 15 + 12 − 1 = 26 persons. Y now occupies X's old seat, 10th from the left, which is 26 − 10 + 1 = 17th from the right. The trap is reporting 12th, which was Y's old position, so the answer is 17th.
Source: PYQ-style

25. A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H sit in a row facing north. B sits fourth to the right of A. C sits exactly between B and D. E is adjacent to neither A nor B. F sits second to the left of G. H sits at the right end. If A is at the left end, who sits fifth from the left?
(a) F (b) B (c) D (d) E
Answer: (b)
Solution: A is at seat 1, so B, fourth to the right, is at seat 5. C is exactly between B and D, giving C at seat 6 and D at seat 7, while H is at seat 8. F is second to the left of G among the free seats, so F is at seat 2 and G at seat 4, leaving E at seat 3. The trap is miscounting "fourth to the right" as seat 4, so the answer is B.
