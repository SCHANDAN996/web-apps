101. Seven persons S, T, U, V, W, X and Y sit around a circular table, facing the centre. U sits second to the right of S. X sits second to the left of T. W sits second to the left of X. U sits to the immediate left of Y. Who sits to the immediate right of V?
(a) T (b) Y (c) W (d) X
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. Going clockwise: T, S, X, V, W, Y, U (facing the centre, a person's left is clockwise). The trap is W, who is on the other side of V. So the answer is X.
Source: PYQ-style
