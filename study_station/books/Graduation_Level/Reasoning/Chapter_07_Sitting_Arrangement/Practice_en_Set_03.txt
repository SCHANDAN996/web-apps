51. Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit in a row facing north. B sits at the right end. C sits third from the left. A sits second to the left of C. D sits to the immediate right of A. E is not adjacent to B. Who sits at the left end?
(a) F (b) E (c) D (d) A
Answer: (d)
Solution: C third from the left fixes C at position 3, so A is at position 1 and D at position 2, with B at position 6. E cannot sit next to B, so E takes position 4 and F position 5. The trap is F, the last-placed person, but the left end belongs to A, so the answer is A.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a row facing north. P sits at the left end. Q sits second to the right of P. R sits to the immediate left of T. T sits at an extreme end. S is not adjacent to P. Who sits at the right end?
(a) T (b) S (c) R (d) U
Answer: (a)
Solution: P is at position 1 and Q at position 3. Since T is at an extreme end and R must be immediately left of T, T takes position 6 with R at position 5. S cannot sit next to P, so S takes position 4 and U position 2. The trap is S, who ends up near the end, but the right end is T, so the answer is T.

N faces outward and sits to the immediate left of M. O sits opposite M. P faces the centre and sits second to the right of O. Q sits between O and P. R sits third to the right of M. Who sits opposite N?
(a) O (b) Q (c) P (d) R
Answer: (d)
Solution: Place M at a corner; N, facing outward, takes the side seat immediately left of M. O is the corner opposite M, P is the corner second to O's right, and Q fills the side seat between them. R, third to the right of inward-facing M, lands exactly on the side seat across from N. The trap is O, who is opposite M rather than N, so the answer is R.

(a) G (b) D (c) F (d) B
Answer: (c)
Solution: Facing south, left means toward the diagram's right, so B sits at position 4 with A at position 1. C between B and D fixes D at 6 and C at 5, and E takes 7. F cannot touch A, so F is at 3 and G at 2. Second to the right of south-facing C means two seats toward the diagram's left, which is F; the trap is forgetting the south-facing reversal and picking D, so the answer is F.

55. Six persons X, Y, Z, W, V and U sit around a circular table facing the centre. X sits opposite Y. Z sits to the immediate right of X. W sits to the immediate left of Y. V sits to the immediate right of Y. Who sits to the immediate left of V?

Answer: (a)
Solution: Fix X and Y opposite each other. Z takes the seat right of X, W the seat left of Y, and V the seat right of Y, leaving U in the last seat. V's immediate left neighbour, counting clockwise from V, is Y. The trap is W, who sits on Y's other side and feels adjacent to V, so the answer is Y.

60. Nine persons sit in a row facing north. D sits fourth from the left. G sits exactly in the middle of the row. B sits second to the right of G. How many persons sit between D and G?

Answer: (c)
Solution: In a row of nine, the middle position is the 5th, so G sits 5th. D is 4th from the left, immediately beside G, with no seat between them. The trap is an off-by-one error that counts positions 4 and 5 as having one seat in between, so the answer is zero.

72. Six persons sit around a triangular table, one at each corner facing the centre and one at the middle of each side facing outward. P sits at a corner. Q sits at the middle of a side. R also sits at the middle of a side. Who among P, Q and R faces the centre?
