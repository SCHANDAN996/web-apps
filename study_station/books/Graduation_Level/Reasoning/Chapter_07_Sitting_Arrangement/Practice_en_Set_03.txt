51. Six persons S, T, U, V, W and X sit in a row facing north. T sits second to the left of V. W sits to the immediate left of V. U sits between T and X (next to both). Who sits third to the left of W?
(a) U (b) S (c) V (d) X
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fixing the clues one by one gives only one arrangement. From left to right: X, U, T, W, V, S. So the answer is X.
Source: PYQ-style
