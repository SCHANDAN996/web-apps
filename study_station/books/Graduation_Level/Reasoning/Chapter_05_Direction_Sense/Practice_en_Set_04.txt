76. A man walks 5 km towards the North, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 3 km (b) 13 km (c) 8 km (d) 5 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 8 km, obtained by adding 5 + 3 without cancelling the two 5 km legs. The two 5 km stretches (North then South) cancel each other, leaving only the 3 km Eastward leg, so the answer is 3 km.
Source: PYQ-style

77. A boy walks 4 km towards the East and then 3 km towards the South. What is the shortest distance between his final position and the starting point?
(a) 5 km (b) 6 km (c) 7 km (d) 12 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 7 km, the simple sum of the two legs. The legs are perpendicular, so the shortest distance is √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5, so the answer is 5 km.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

78. Ravi walks 10 m towards the East, turns left and walks 6 m, then turns left and walks 10 m. In which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) East (b) North (c) South (d) West
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering West, the direction of his final walk, instead of his position relative to start. The 10 m East and 10 m West legs cancel, leaving him 6 m to the North of the start, so the answer is North.

79. In a coded language, 'East' is called 'North', 'North' is called 'West', 'West' is called 'South' and 'South' is called 'East'. A man walks 5 km towards 'West' (as per the code). In which actual direction does he walk?
(a) South (b) East (c) North (d) West
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering South, applying the shift in the wrong direction. Since 'North' is called 'West', the coded word 'West' stands for actual North, so walking towards coded 'West' means walking North, so the answer is North.

80. A girl facing North-West turns 90° clockwise, then 180° anticlockwise. Which direction is she facing now?
(a) South-East (b) North-East (c) South-West (d) North-West
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is North-East, found by applying only the first turn. Net rotation = 90° clockwise − 180° anticlockwise = 90° anticlockwise from North-West, which gives South-West, so the answer is South-West.
Source: PYQ-style

81. Point A is 7 m East of point B. Point C is 24 m North of point A. What is the straight-line distance between B and C?
(a) 17 m (b) 31 m (c) 25 m (d) 30 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 31 m, the sum of 7 and 24, ignoring that the path is a right angle. Using Pythagoras, √(7² + 24²) = √(49 + 576) = √625 = 25, so the answer is 25 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

82. A man walks 6 km South, then 8 km East, then 6 km North. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 6 km (b) 14 km (c) 10 km (d) 8 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 10 km, wrongly applying Pythagoras to 6 and 8 even though the 6 km legs cancel. The South and North legs cancel, leaving only the 8 km Eastward displacement, so the answer is 8 km.

83. If 'A × B' means A is to the North of B, 'A + B' means A is to the South of B, and 'A − B' means A is to the East of B, then in 'P × Q − R', what is the position of P with respect to R?
(a) North-East (b) North-West (c) South-East (d) South-West
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is North-West, obtained by misreading 'Q − R' as R being East of Q. Actually Q − R means Q is East of R, so R is West of Q; P is North of Q, hence P lies to the North-East of R, so the answer is North-East.
Source: PYQ-style

84. Sunita walks 15 m towards the West, turns right and walks 8 m, then turns right and walks 15 m. How far and in which direction is she from the start?
(a) 8 m, South (b) 8 m, North (c) 15 m, West (d) 23 m, North
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 23 m, the sum of 15 and 8. Facing West, a right turn points North; the two 15 m legs cancel, leaving 8 m to the North, so the answer is 8 m, North.

85. A clock shows 3:00. The hour hand points East. In which direction does the minute hand point?
(a) North (b) East (c) South (d) West
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the minute hand also points East. At 3:00 the minute hand points to 12, which is 90° anticlockwise from the 3 (East), i.e. North, so the answer is North.

86. A man facing South turns 135° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) South-East (b) North-East (c) North-West (d) East

Solution: The trap is North-West, obtained by turning clockwise instead of anticlockwise. From South, 135° anticlockwise passes through East (90°) and ends 45° beyond it at North-East, so the answer is North-East.

87. Village P is 5 km North of village Q. Village R is 12 km East of village Q. What is the shortest distance between P and R?
(a) 17 km (b) 13 km (c) 7 km (d) 15 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 17 km, the simple sum of 5 and 12. The villages form a right triangle, so √(5² + 12²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13, so the answer is 13 km.

Solution: The trap is South-West, obtained by shifting anticlockwise instead of clockwise. Actual West shifted 45° clockwise gives North-West, so the answer is North-West.

89. Rohan walks 20 m North, turns left and walks 20 m, turns left and walks 20 m, then turns left and walks 20 m. Where is he with respect to the starting point?

Solution: The trap is 20 m West, counting only three sides of the square. Four equal sides with four left turns complete a closed square, bringing him back to the start, so the answer is at the starting point.

91. If South-East becomes North and North-East becomes West in a coded system, what does West become?
(a) South-East (b) South-West (c) South (d) North-East

95. Pointing towards a pole, Amit says its shadow falls to his right in the morning. If Amit faces the pole, in which direction is the pole from Amit?
(a) East (b) West (c) South (d) North
Answer: (c)

100. If 'P @ Q' means Q is 5 m South of P, and 'P # Q' means Q is 5 m East of P, then for 'A @ B # C', what is the distance between A and C?
(a) 5 m (b) 10 m (c) 5√2 m (d) 7.5 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 10 m, adding the two 5 m segments as if they were in a straight line. B is 5 m South of A and C is 5 m East of B, so A and C are at right-angle legs of 5 m each, giving √(25 + 25) = 5√2, so the answer is 5√2 m.
Source: PYQ-style
