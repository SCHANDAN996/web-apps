1. Ravi walks 5 km towards the North, then turns right and walks 3 km. How far is he from his starting point?
(a) 8 km (b) 2 km (c) 4 km (d) √34 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 8 km wrongly adds the two legs as if they were in a straight line. The two legs are perpendicular, so the distance is the hypotenuse: √(5² + 3²) = √34 km, so the answer is √34 km.
Source: PYQ-style

2. A man walks 4 km towards the East, then turns left and walks 3 km. In which direction is he from his starting point?
(a) South-East (b) North-West (c) North-East (d) South-West
Answer: (c)
Solution: South-East is the trap for those who turn right instead of left. Turning left from East means he goes North, so his final position is East and North of the start, so the answer is North-East.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Sita faces the North and turns 90° clockwise. Which direction is she facing now?
(a) South (b) West (c) East (d) North
Answer: (c)
Solution: West is the trap for those who turn anticlockwise instead of clockwise. In clockwise order North → East → South → West, a 90° clockwise turn from North points to East, so the answer is East.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Mohan walks 10 km towards the South and then 10 km towards the North. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 10 km (b) 0 km (c) 20 km (d) 5 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 20 km, which adds the distances without considering that the directions are opposite. Walking 10 km South and then 10 km North brings him exactly back to the start, so the answer is 0 km.

5. If South-East becomes North, then what will North-East become?
(a) West (b) South-East (c) South (d) North-West
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is North-West, which comes from rotating only 90°. South-East becoming North is a 135° anticlockwise shift, and applying the same shift to North-East gives West, so the answer is West.

6. A girl walks 6 km towards the West, then turns right and walks 8 km. What is the shortest distance between her starting and ending points?
(a) 12 km (b) 14 km (c) 2 km (d) 10 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 14 km, obtained by simply adding the legs. Turning right from West means she walks North, so the shortest distance is √(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10 km, so the answer is 10 km.

7. Ram is facing East. He turns 45° clockwise, then 90° anticlockwise, then 180° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North-West (b) South-West (c) South-East (d) North-East
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to add all turns as clockwise, which gives the wrong quadrant. Net rotation = 45° − 90° + 180° = 135° clockwise, and from East, 135° clockwise reaches South-West, so the answer is South-West.

8. In a coded direction problem, 'A @ B' means B is to the North of A. If P @ Q and Q @ R, then R is in which direction with respect to P?
(a) South (b) North (c) East (d) West
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to read the code in reverse and answer South. P @ Q puts Q North of P and Q @ R puts R North of Q, so R is North of P, so the answer is North.

9. A man walks 3 km North, then 4 km East, then 3 km South. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 7 km (b) 10 km (c) 6 km (d) 4 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 10 km, which adds all three distances. The 3 km North and 3 km South cancel out, leaving only the 4 km East displacement, so the answer is 4 km.

10. The shadow of a pole falls to the West in the morning. In which direction is the Sun?
(a) West (b) North (c) East (d) South
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is West, which assumes the shadow points towards the Sun. A shadow always falls opposite to the Sun's direction, so a West shadow means the Sun is in the East, which also matches sunrise, so the answer is East.

11. Neha walks 5 km towards the South, turns left and walks 5 km, then turns left again and walks 5 km. In which direction is she from the start?
(a) East (b) North (c) West (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is West, from treating left turns as right turns. From South, left faces East; after 5 km East, the next left faces North and the 5 km North cancels the South leg, leaving her 5 km East of the start, so the answer is East.

14. Point A is 4 km East of point B. Point C is 3 km North of point A. What is the distance between B and C?
(a) 5 km (b) 12 km (c) 1 km (d) 7 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 7 km from adding the two segments. B to A is 4 km East and A to C is 3 km North, forming a right triangle, so BC = √(4² + 3²) = 5 km, so the answer is 5 km.

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is North-West, which results from turning clockwise instead of anticlockwise. From South, moving anticlockwise: 45° reaches South-East, 90° reaches East, and 135° reaches North-East, so the answer is North-East.

21. A man walks 5 km North, then 5 km East, then 10 km South, then 5 km West. How far and in which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) At the starting point (b) 5 km North (c) 10 km South (d) 5 km South
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "at the starting point," which wrongly assumes all movements cancel. The 5 km East and 5 km West cancel, but the net vertical movement is 5 km North − 10 km South = 5 km South, so the answer is 5 km South.

Solution: The trap is South-East, from forgetting that the vertical movements cancel. B is 4 km North of A, C is 4 km East of B, and D is 4 km South of C; the North and South legs cancel, so D is exactly 4 km East of A, so the answer is 4 km East.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 14 km from adding the legs instead of applying Pythagoras. B is 6 km North of A and C is 8 km West of B, so C is 6 km North and 8 km West of A, giving √(6² + 8²) = 10 km, so the answer is 10 km.
