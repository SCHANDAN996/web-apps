Questions 51–75: Medium

51. A man walks 5 km towards the North, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 3 km (b) 5 km (c) 8 km (d) 2 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 8 km, obtained by adding all distances without tracking direction. After 5 km North, he goes 3 km East, then 5 km South, which cancels the Northward leg completely. Only the 3 km Eastward displacement remains, so the answer is 3 km.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Ravi starts from his house and walks 10 m towards the East, then turns left and walks 6 m, then turns left again and walks 10 m. In which direction is he from his house?
(a) North (b) West (c) East (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to answer West, thinking the final walk direction decides the position. His final walk is Westward, but his net position is 6 m North of the house since the East and West legs cancel. So the answer is North.
Source: PYQ-style

53. In a coded language of directions, 'North' is called 'East', 'East' is called 'South', 'South' is called 'West' and 'West' is called 'North'. A man walks 4 km towards what is called 'South'. In which actual direction does he walk?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to decode 'South' as West by reversing the chain. Here 'East' is called 'South', so when he walks towards what is called 'South', he actually walks East. So the answer is East.

54. Priya walks 8 m towards the South, then turns left and walks 6 m, then turns left again and walks 8 m. How far and in which direction is she from the starting point?
(a) 8 m, North (b) 6 m, West (c) 14 m, East (d) 6 m, East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 14 m, which adds the last two legs. Facing South, her left is East; she walks 6 m East, then left again means North for 8 m, cancelling the Southward leg. Net displacement is 6 m East, so the answer is 6 m, East.

55. A is 40 m South-West of B. C is 40 m South-East of B. In which direction is C with respect to A?
(a) North-East (b) West (c) South-East (d) East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to answer South-East by copying the description of C relative to B. A and C are both 40 m from B at the same depth below B, so they lie on the same horizontal line, with C to the right of A. So the answer is East.

56. A man facing North turns 135° clockwise, then 45° anti-clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) South-East (b) South (c) North-East (d) East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to compute 135° − 45° = 90° but then turn anti-clockwise from North, landing on West. Net rotation is 90° clockwise from North, which is East. So the answer is East.

57. Rohan walks 12 m towards the East, then 5 m towards the South. What is the shortest distance between his starting point and final position?
(a) 17 m (b) 13 m (c) 7 m (d) 12 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 17 m, the sum of the two legs. The shortest distance is the hypotenuse of the right triangle with legs 12 and 5: √(144 + 25) = √169 = 13. So the answer is 13 m.

58. If South-East becomes North, and North-East becomes West, and all directions change in the same manner, what will West become?
(a) North-West (b) South-East (c) South (d) North-East
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to rotate only 90° instead of the actual shift. South-East to North is a 135° anti-clockwise shift; applying the same shift to West (135° anti-clockwise) gives South-East. So the answer is South-East.

60. Point P is 9 m East of point Q. Point R is 12 m North of point P. What is the distance between Q and R?
(a) 21 m (b) 12 m (c) 15 m (d) 9 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 21 m, the sum of the two segments. Q, P, R form a right triangle with legs 9 and 12, so QR = √(81 + 144) = √225 = 15. So the answer is 15 m.

61. A clock shows 3:00. If the minute hand points towards the East, in which direction does the hour hand point?
(a) West (b) North (c) South (d) East
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to assume the hour hand points North as on a normal clock face. At 3:00 the hour hand is 90° clockwise from the minute hand; 90° clockwise from East is South. So the answer is South.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to answer 5 m by ignoring the final left turn. The North and South legs cancel; he moves 5 m East, then after facing South his left is East, adding another 5 m East. Total Eastward displacement is 10 m, so the answer is 10 m.

Solution: The trap is to answer North-East by mixing up the reference point. From A, village B is to the South, and C is further East of B, so C lies to the South-East of A. So the answer is South-East.

68. Facing South, a man turns 45° clockwise, then 90° anti-clockwise, then 45° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) East (b) West (c) South-West (d) South
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to add all turns as clockwise, giving 180° and answering North. Net rotation is 45° − 90° + 45° = 0°, so he still faces South. So the answer is South.

71. One morning, Ram and Shyam stand facing each other. Ram's shadow falls exactly to his left. In which direction is Shyam facing?
(a) North (b) West (c) East (d) South
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to forget that the two men face each other and give Ram's own direction as the answer. In the morning the Sun is in the East, so shadows fall West; if Ram's shadow is to his left, his left is West, which means Ram faces North, and Shyam, standing opposite him, faces South. So the answer is South.

(a) 25 km (b) 31 km (c) 17 km (d) 24 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 31 km, the simple sum of both legs. The two legs are perpendicular, so the shortest distance is √(49 + 576) = √625 = 25. So the answer is 25 km.

73. If North-West is called South, South-West is called East, and so on (each direction shifted by 135° clockwise in naming), what will be called North?
(a) South-West (b) North-East (c) West (d) South-East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to shift only 90° and answer South-West. The naming shifts each direction 135° clockwise: North-West (315°) becomes South (180°), a shift of −135°. To find what is called North (0°), the actual direction is 0° + 135° = 135°, i.e. South-East. So the answer is South-East.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Deepak walks 20 m towards the North, then turns right and walks 30 m, then turns right and walks 35 m, then turns left and walks 15 m, then turns left again and walks 15 m. In which direction and how far is he from the starting point?
(a) 15 m West (b) 15 m East (c) 45 m East (d) 30 m East
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to answer 15 m East by subtracting the fourth leg, but after the second right turn Deepak faces South, so his left is East and the fourth leg adds 15 m Eastward. Net North-South: 20 up, 35 down, 15 up = 0. Net East-West: 30 + 15 = 45 m East. So the answer is 45 m East.
Source: PYQ-style

75. A pole casts a shadow of 8 m towards the West in the morning. A man standing at the base of the pole walks along the shadow to its tip, then turns 90° to his right and walks 6 m. In which direction is he from the base of the pole?
(a) North-East (b) South-West (c) North-West (d) South-East
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to take the morning shadow towards the East; shadows fall opposite the Sun, so the shadow points West. He walks 8 m West, and facing West his right is North, so he walks 6 m North. His final position is North-West of the base, so the answer is North-West.
