126. A man walks 5 km North, turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) 8 km East (b) 3 km East (c) 3 km West (d) 5 km North
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 8 km East, obtained by wrongly adding the two 5 km legs. The two 5 km legs are in opposite directions (North then South), so they cancel out, leaving only the 3 km East leg, so the answer is 3 km East.

127. Point A is 4 m East of point B. Point C is 3 m North of point A. Point D is 4 m West of point C. Point E is 6 m North of point D. In which direction is point E with respect to point B?
(a) South (b) West (c) East (d) North
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to add the North movements of C and E and assume a North-East answer. Track coordinates: B = (0,0), A = (4,0), C = (4,3), D = (0,3), E = (0,9); E is directly above B, so the answer is North.

128. Ravi walks 10 m towards South, then turns left and walks 6 m, then turns left and walks 10 m. He then turns right and walks 4 m. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 6 m (b) 4 m (c) 10 m (d) 2 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to answer 4 m by ignoring the first left turn. South 10 m, left (East) 6 m, left (North) 10 m cancels the South leg, then right from North-facing is East 4 m; net displacement = 6 + 4 = 10 m East, so the answer is 10 m.

129. In a coded direction system, 'North' is called 'East', 'East' is called 'South', 'South' is called 'West' and 'West' is called 'North'. A man walks 5 km towards what is called 'South'. In which actual direction does he walk?
(a) West (b) East (c) South (d) North
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to answer South by taking the word literally. Since 'East' is called 'South', walking towards what is called 'South' means actually walking East, so the answer is East.

130. A clock shows 3:00. The hour hand points North. In which direction does the minute hand point?
(a) West (b) East (c) South (d) North
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to answer North, assuming both hands align at 3:00. At 3:00 the minute hand points to 12 while the hour hand points to 3; if the 3-position is North, the 12-position is 90° anti-clockwise from it, which is West, so the answer is West.

131. A man facing North-West turns 90° clockwise, then 180° anti-clockwise, then 45° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) West (b) North-East (c) South-West (d) North
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to add all turns as clockwise and get North. Net turn = 90° clockwise − 180° + 45° clockwise = 45° anti-clockwise; from North-West, 45° anti-clockwise gives West, so the answer is West.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to compute 10 km using a 6-8-10 triangle, but the 6 km legs cancel. Q = (0,0), P = (−6,0), R = (−6,−8), S = (0,−8); S is directly 8 km South of Q, so the answer is 8 km.

134. If South-East becomes North, and North-East becomes West, and all directions change accordingly, what will West become?
(a) South (b) South-East (c) North-East (d) North-West
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to rotate only 90° and answer North-East. South-East to North is a 135° anti-clockwise shift; applying the same shift, West moves 135° anti-clockwise to South-East, so the answer is South-East.

145. Town X is 40 km North of town Y. Town Z is 30 km East of town Y. A helicopter flies directly from X to Z. What distance does it cover?
(a) 50 km (b) 60 km (c) 70 km (d) 10 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to add the legs and answer 70 km. X and Z form a right triangle with legs 40 km and 30 km from Y; direct distance = √(40² + 30²) = √2500 = 50 km, so the answer is 50 km.
