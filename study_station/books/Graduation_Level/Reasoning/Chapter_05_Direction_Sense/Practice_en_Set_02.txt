26. A man walks 5 km towards the North, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 8 km (b) 5 km (c) 3 km (d) 13 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 8 km, obtained by adding 5 + 3 without tracking direction. Walking 5 km North and then 5 km South cancels out completely, leaving only the 3 km East displacement, so the answer is 3 km.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Ravi starts from his house and walks 10 m towards the East. He then turns left and walks 6 m, then turns left again and walks 10 m. In which direction is he now from his house?
(a) East (b) West (c) North (d) South
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to answer West, thinking only of the final leg. The two 10 m legs (East then West) cancel each other, so only the 6 m Northward movement remains, so the answer is North.
Source: PYQ-style

28. In a coded language of directions, 'North' is called 'East', 'East' is called 'South', 'South' is called 'West' and 'West' is called 'North'. A man walks 4 km towards what is called 'South'. In which actual direction does he walk?
(a) East (b) South (c) West (d) North
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to answer South by ignoring the code. Since 'East' is called 'South', walking towards 'South' means actually walking East, so the answer is East.

29. Priya walks 8 m towards the South, then turns left and walks 6 m, then turns left again and walks 8 m. What is the shortest distance between her starting point and final position?
(a) 8 m (b) 10 m (c) 6 m (d) 14 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 10 m, from wrongly applying Pythagoras to 8 and 6. The 8 m South and 8 m North cancel; turning left from South means facing East, so she ends 6 m East of the start, so the answer is 6 m.

30. A is 40 m South-West of B. C is 40 m South-East of B. In which direction is C with respect to A?
(a) South-East (b) West (c) East (d) North-East
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is South-East, which describes C relative to B, not relative to A. A and C are both 40 m south of B at equal angles, so they lie on the same horizontal line with C directly to the East of A, so the answer is East.
Source: PYQ-style

31. A man facing East turns 90° clockwise and then turns 45° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) South-East (b) South (c) East (d) North-East
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is South, obtained by forgetting the final 45° anticlockwise turn. Turning 90° clockwise from East gives South, and then 45° anticlockwise from South gives South-East, so the answer is South-East.

Solution: The trap is 9 km, from adding 6 + 3. The 4 km North and 4 km South cancel; of the westward 6 km, 3 km is retraced East, leaving a net 3 km West, so the answer is 3 km.

33. A clock shows 3:00. The hour hand points towards the North-East. In which direction does the minute hand point?

Solution: The trap is North, assuming the minute hand at 12 points North by default. At 3:00 the minute hand is 90° anticlockwise from the hour hand; 90° anticlockwise from North-East is North-West, so the answer is North-West.

37. Village P is to the North of village Q. Village R is to the East of village Q. Village S is to the South of village R. In which direction is S with respect to P?
(a) North-East (b) South-West (c) North-West (d) South-East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to say it cannot be determined because distances are unknown. S has the same easting as R, which is east of Q and hence east of P; and S is south of R, which is level with Q, so S is south of P, so the answer is South-East.

38. A man facing North turns 135° clockwise, then 90° anticlockwise, then 45° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) South-East (b) East (c) North-East (d) South
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is South-East, from adding all turns as clockwise. Net rotation = 135° − 90° + 45° = 90° clockwise from North, which is East, so the answer is East.

39. Deepak walks 7 m towards the South, then turns to his left and walks 4 m, then turns to his left and walks 7 m. In which direction is he facing at the end?

Solution: The trap is East, which is only the direction of the middle leg. Facing South, a left turn makes him face East; another left turn from East makes him face North for the last leg, so the answer is North.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 20√2 m, from adding all magnitudes blindly. After reaching 10 m North and 10 m East, he faces East; turning 45° clockwise makes him face South-East, and walking 10√2 m moves him 10 m South and 10 m East, ending 20 m East of the start, so the answer is 20 m.

42. A girl walks 3 km towards the West, turns right and walks 4 km. What is the shortest distance between her starting and ending points?
(a) 1 km (b) 5 km (c) 7 km (d) 12 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 7 km, from simply adding the two legs. The two legs are perpendicular, so by Pythagoras √(3² + 4²) = 5 km, so the answer is 5 km.

48. Town M is 15 km West of town N. Town O is 20 km South of town N. What is the shortest distance between M and O?
(a) 35 km (b) 25 km (c) 5 km (d) 30 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 35 km, from adding the two distances. M and O are at right angles from N, so distance = √(15² + 20²) = √625 = 25 km, so the answer is 25 km.
