101. A man walks 5 km towards the North, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 13 km (b) 5 km (c) 8 km (d) 3 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer 8 km comes from adding the legs, but distance from start is not the total path. The two 5 km legs (North then South) cancel each other, leaving only the 3 km East leg, so the answer is 3 km.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Ravi walks 10 m towards the East, turns left and walks 6 m, then turns left and walks 10 m. In which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) North (b) West (c) East (d) South
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer West treats the last leg as the resultant, but the 10 m East and 10 m West legs cancel out. The remaining 6 m was walked after turning left from East, which is North, so the answer is North.
Source: PYQ-style

103. In a coded direction system, 'A @ B' means B is 4 m North of A, and 'A # B' means B is 3 m East of A. If P @ Q and Q # R, how far is P from R?
(a) 5 m (b) 7 m (c) 12 m (d) 1 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer 7 m comes from simply adding 4 and 3, but the two legs are perpendicular. Q is 4 m North of P and R is 3 m East of Q, so PR = √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 m, so the answer is 5 m.
Source: PYQ-style

104. Sunita walks 8 km towards the South, then turns left and walks 6 km. How far and in which direction is she from the start?
(a) 2 km, South (b) 14 km, South-East (c) 10 km, South-East (d) 10 km, South-West
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer 14 km adds the legs, ignoring that they are perpendicular. After walking South, a left turn faces East, so distance = √(8² + 6²) = 10 km towards South-East, so the answer is 10 km, South-East.

105. A boy facing West turns 135° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) South-West (b) South-East (c) North-West (d) North-East
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer South-East comes from turning anticlockwise by mistake. From West, 90° clockwise gives North, and a further 45° gives North-East, so the answer is North-East.

109. A man walks 15 m towards the North-East. He then turns 90° anticlockwise and walks 15 m. In which direction is he from the start?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer North-West is the direction of the second leg, not the resultant. The two equal legs at 90° (North-East then North-West) produce a resultant exactly midway between them, i.e. North, so the answer is North.

111. A clock shows 3:00. If the minute hand points East, in which direction does the hour hand point?
(a) North (b) West (c) East (d) South
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer North assumes the hour hand is 90° anticlockwise from the minute hand. At 3:00 the hour hand is 90° clockwise from the minute hand, and 90° clockwise from East is South, so the answer is South.

Solution: The tempting answer South-East comes from adding the turns with wrong signs. Net turn = 45° − 90° + 135° = 90° clockwise, and 90° clockwise from South is West, so the answer is West.

121. A man walks 6 km North, then 8 km East, then 6 km South, then 3 km West. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 10 km (b) 23 km (c) 5 km (d) 8 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer 10 km applies Pythagoras to 6 and 8 without cancelling opposite legs. Net North–South = 0; net East–West = 8 − 3 = 5 km East, so the answer is 5 km.

122. In a coded system, 'A & B' means B is 5 m North of A, 'A ! B' means B is 5 m South of A, 'A ? B' means B is 12 m East of A. Given P & Q, Q ! R and R ? S, how far is P from S?
(a) 17 m (b) 13 m (c) 12 m (d) 22 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer 13 m uses 5 and 12 in Pythagoras, but the two 5 m legs cancel: Q is 5 m North of P and R is 5 m South of Q, so R coincides with P. Then S is 12 m East of R, which is P, so the answer is 12 m.

123. Two persons start from the same point. One walks 9 km North and then 12 km East; the other walks 9 km South and then 12 km West. What is the distance between their final positions?
(a) 30 km (b) 15 km (c) 42 km (d) 24 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer 24 km doubles only the East–West gap. The first ends at (12, 9) and the second at (−12, −9); distance = √(24² + 18²) = √900 = 30 km, so the answer is 30 km.

124. A man walks 10 m North, then 10 m East, then 10 m South, then 10 m West, and repeats this square cycle 3 times. He then walks 6 m North and 8 m East. How far is he from the start?
(a) 120 m (b) 14 m (c) 10 m (d) 2 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer 120 m adds up the entire path, but each complete square cycle brings him back to the start. After 3 cycles he is at the start, and the final legs give √(6² + 8²) = 10 m, so the answer is 10 m.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer 0 km assumes the whole journey cancels, but cancellation happens only within each complete cycle. After 5 complete cycles the drone is back at the start, and the final legs of 6 km East and 8 km North give √(6² + 8²) = 10 km, so the answer is 10 km.
