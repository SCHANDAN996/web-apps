Questions 26–50: Medium

26. In a figure series, a dot moves one corner clockwise in each step while a cross moves two corners anticlockwise. If the dot is at the top-left corner in step 1, where will it be in step 4?
(a) Bottom-left (b) Bottom-right (c) Top-left (d) Top-right
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer is bottom-right, obtained by counting only two moves from step 1. From step 1 to step 4 there are exactly three moves: top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left, and the cross's movement is irrelevant to the dot, a classic double-relationship trap. So the answer is bottom-left.
Source: PYQ-style

27. A series shows a triangle rotating 90° clockwise each step, with one extra line added inside at each step. Which figure comes next after a triangle pointing right with two internal lines?
(a) Triangle pointing up with three lines (b) Triangle pointing down with three lines (c) Triangle pointing down with two lines (d) Triangle pointing right with three lines
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the option with the triangle pointing up — candidates who rotate 180° by mistake pick it. Rotation is 90° clockwise: right → down, and lines increase by one: two → three. So the answer is triangle pointing down with three lines.

28. Find the odd figure out: four figures each contain a circle and a square. In three, the circle is inside the square; in one, the square is inside the circle.
(a) The third figure (b) The figure with square inside circle (c) The second figure (d) The first figure
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to look for size or shading differences, which are identical across all four. The only real difference is the inside-outside relationship of the two shapes. So the answer is the figure with the square inside the circle.

29. In a series, the number of sides of the outer figure increases by one each step (3, 4, 5, 6) while the number of inner dots decreases by one (6, 5, 4, 3). What comes next?
(a) Heptagon with 3 dots (b) Hexagon with 2 dots (c) Heptagon with 2 dots (d) Pentagon with 2 dots
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice keeps the hexagon, forgetting the outer figure must also advance. Outer: 6 → 7 sides (heptagon); inner: 3 → 2 dots. So the answer is heptagon with 2 dots.

30. A square is divided into 4 equal smaller squares. In each step, one more small square is shaded, moving clockwise from the top-left, and the pattern restarts once all four are shaded. How many small squares are shaded in step 5?
(a) 4 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer is 4, assuming shading keeps accumulating. With only 4 cells, after all four are shaded in step 4 the pattern cycles back, so step 5 restarts with one shaded square. So the answer is 1.

31. Five figures are given; four can be obtained from each other by rotation, one cannot. Which is the odd one?
(a) The largest figure (b) The figure with darkest shading (c) The figure that is a mirror image of the others (d) The first figure
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to classify by size or shading, which are irrelevant in rotation problems. A mirror image can never be produced by rotation alone, so the mirror-image figure is the odd one. So the answer is the figure that is a mirror image of the others.

32. In a series, an arrow rotates 45° clockwise in steps 1–2, 90° in steps 2–3, 135° in steps 3–4. By how much should it rotate next?
(a) 180° (b) 135° (c) 225° (d) 90°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 135°, repeating the last increment. The increments themselves grow by 45°: 45, 90, 135, so the next increment is 180°. So the answer is 180°.

36. A 3×3 grid of figures follows the rule: figure in column 3 = superimposition of columns 1 and 2, with common lines cancelled. If row 2 column 1 has a vertical line and a horizontal line, and column 2 has a horizontal line and a dot, what is column 3?
(a) Horizontal line and dot (b) Vertical line and dot (c) Vertical line only (d) Two horizontal lines and a dot
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is keeping the horizontal line, forgetting the cancellation rule. The horizontal line appears in both, so it cancels; the vertical line and dot remain. So the answer is vertical line and dot.

37. In a series, each figure has two elements: the left element of one step becomes the right element of the next, and a new element enters on the left. If step 3 has [triangle | circle], what is step 4?
(a) [new element | triangle] (b) [circle | triangle] (c) [triangle | new element] (d) [circle | circle]
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is swapping the two existing elements, which mimics a simple interchange rule. Actually the left element (triangle) shifts right, and a fresh element enters on the left. So the answer is new element on the left and triangle on the right.
