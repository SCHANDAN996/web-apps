1. In a figure series, a dot moves one corner clockwise in each step inside a square. If the dot is at the top-left corner in the first figure, where will it be in the fifth figure?
(a) Top-right (b) Bottom-right (c) Bottom-left (d) Top-left
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is bottom-right, reached by counting only three moves. The dot moves one corner per step, so after four moves (steps 2 to 5) it completes a full cycle and returns to the starting corner, so the answer is top-left.
Source: PYQ-style

2. A series shows triangles with 1, 2, 3, 4 … small lines inside them. How many lines will the sixth figure contain?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The number of inner lines increases by one in each successive figure, so the sixth figure has six lines, so the answer is 6.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Which figure does not belong to the group: circle, square, triangle, rectangle?
(a) Triangle (b) Circle (c) Square (d) Rectangle
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is triangle because it has the fewest sides, but the real basis is curves versus straight lines. Square, triangle and rectangle are made only of straight lines, while the circle is a curved figure, so the answer is circle.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Understanding Quadrilaterals)

4. In a series, an arrow rotates 45° clockwise each step. If it points up in the first figure, which direction does it point in the third figure?
(a) Right (b) Down (c) Up-right (d) Left
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer is up-right, which is only one step of rotation. Two steps of 45° give 90°, turning the arrow from up to right, so the answer is right.
Source: PYQ-style

5. A square is divided into 4 equal parts; in each successive figure one more part is shaded, moving clockwise. If one part is shaded in figure 1, how many parts are shaded in figure 4?
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 2 (d) 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: One additional part is shaded per step, so figure 4 has all four parts shaded, so the answer is 4.
Source: PYQ-style

6. Which of the following does not belong to the group: cube, cuboid, cone, sphere?
(a) Cuboid (b) Sphere (c) Cone (d) Cube
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is cone because it has a pointed top, but the classification basis is flat faces. Cube, cuboid and cone all have at least one flat face, while a sphere has no flat face at all, so the answer is sphere.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Visualising Solid Shapes)

7. In a series, the number of sides of the main figure increases by one: triangle, square, pentagon, … What is the fifth figure?
(a) Hexagon (b) Pentagon (c) Octagon (d) Heptagon
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is hexagon, which is only the fourth figure. Counting on: triangle (3), square (4), pentagon (5), hexagon (6), heptagon (7), so the answer is heptagon.
Source: PYQ-style

8. A figure series alternates between a shaded circle and an unshaded circle. If figure 1 is shaded, what is figure 8?
(a) Half-shaded (b) Shaded (c) Unshaded (d) Double circle
Answer: (c)
Solution: Odd-numbered figures are shaded and even-numbered figures are unshaded; 8 is even, so the answer is unshaded.

9. Which figure is the odd one out: figures made of 3, 4, 5 and 6 straight line segments respectively?
(a) 4-segment figure (b) 3-segment figure (c) 5-segment figure (d) 6-segment figure
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is the 5-segment figure because 5 is odd, but the basis is closed versus open figures as drawn: the 3-segment figure is open while the others are closed polygons, so the answer is the 3-segment figure.

10. In a series, a small square inside a bigger square moves one step anticlockwise along the four corners. Starting at top-right, where is it in the fourth figure?
(a) Top-left (b) Top-right (c) Bottom-left (d) Bottom-right
Answer: (d)
Solution: Moving anticlockwise from top-right: top-left, bottom-left, bottom-right across three moves, so the answer is bottom-right.

11. A series shows stars with 4, 5, 6, 7 points. How many points will the next star have?
(a) 7 (b) 9 (c) 8 (d) 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: The number of points increases by one each step, so the next star has 8 points, so the answer is 8.

12. Which does not belong: all figures are divided into equal halves except one. The odd figure is divided into —
(a) Two unequal parts (b) Two equal halves (c) Four equal parts (d) Three equal parts
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answers are the figures with more divisions, but the rule is equality of parts, not their number. A figure split into two unequal parts violates the equal-division rule, so the answer is two unequal parts.

13. In a series, each figure adds one more row of dots: 1 dot, 3 dots, 6 dots, 10 dots. How many dots in the next figure?
(a) 12 (b) 14 (c) 16 (d) 15
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting answer is 16, assuming doubling. The differences are +2, +3, +4, so the next difference is +5, giving 10 + 5 = 15, so the answer is 15.

14. A line segment inside a circle rotates 90° anticlockwise each step. If it is horizontal in figure 1, its position in figure 3 is —
(a) Vertical (b) Absent (c) Horizontal (d) Diagonal
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is vertical, which is the position after only one step. Two steps of 90° give 180°, returning the line to horizontal, so the answer is horizontal.

15. Which is the odd one out: pentagon, hexagon, heptagon, circle?
(a) Pentagon (b) Hexagon (c) Heptagon (d) Circle
Answer: (d)
Solution: Pentagon, hexagon and heptagon are polygons with straight sides; the circle has no sides or vertices, so the answer is circle.

16. In a series, a flag on a pole flips between pointing left and pointing right alternately. If figure 1 points right, figure 7 points —

Solution: Odd figures point right and even figures point left; 7 is odd, so the answer is right.

17. A series shows 2, 4, 8, 16 small squares. How many small squares will the next figure have?
(a) 32 (b) 24 (c) 36 (d) 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer is 24, assuming a constant addition. The count doubles each step, so 16 × 2 = 32, so the answer is 32.

18. Which figure does not belong: four figures each contain a triangle; in three the triangle points up, in one it points down. The odd one is —
(a) The first upward triangle (b) The largest triangle (c) The shaded triangle (d) The downward triangle
Answer: (d)
Solution: The classification basis given is orientation; three triangles point up and only one points down, so the answer is the downward triangle.

19. In a series, a cross (+) inside a square moves from the centre to the top edge, then to the right edge. Following the clockwise pattern, its next position is —
(a) Centre (b) Left edge (c) Bottom edge (d) Top edge
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is left edge, which would be anticlockwise movement. Moving clockwise: centre → top → right → bottom, so the answer is bottom edge.

20. A series shows rectangles whose length doubles each step while width stays constant. If the first rectangle is 1 cm long, the fourth is —
(a) 8 cm (b) 6 cm (c) 16 cm (d) 4 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer is 4 cm, assuming simple addition. Doubling: 1, 2, 4, 8, so the fourth rectangle is 8 cm, so the answer is 8 cm.

21. In a series, a dot moves two corners clockwise each step around a hexagon's six corners. Starting at corner 1, at which corner is the dot in figure 4?
(a) Corner 1 (b) Corner 3 (c) Corner 5 (d) Corner 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting answer is corner 5, reached by counting only two moves. Three moves of two corners each give six corners of travel — a full loop back to the start, so the answer is corner 1.

22. A figure contains two elements: a circle that rotates 90° clockwise each step and a square that rotates 45° anticlockwise each step. After how many steps will both elements simultaneously return to their original positions for the first time?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is 4, which is only the circle's cycle. The circle repeats every 4 steps and the square every 8 steps; the LCM of 4 and 8 is 8, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

23. In a classification question, four figures each show a large shape with a smaller shape inside. In three figures the inner shape has fewer sides than the outer shape; in one it has more. The odd figure is the one where —
(a) Inner shape has fewer sides (b) Inner shape has more sides (c) Both shapes are identical (d) Inner shape is shaded
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is the figure with identical shapes, but the stated rule concerns the side-count relationship. Three figures follow "inner sides < outer sides"; the one where the inner shape has more sides breaks the rule, so the answer is inner shape has more sides.
Source: PYQ-style

24. A series shows the number of intersections of two lines inside a box as 0, 1, 2, 1, 0, 1, 2, … What is the number of intersections in the ninth figure?
(a) 2 (b) 1 (c) 0 (d) 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is 2, assuming the count keeps rising. The pattern 0, 1, 2, 1 repeats with a cycle of 4; the ninth figure starts the third cycle, so the answer is 0.
Source: PYQ-style

25. In a series, a shaded sector of a circle grows by 45° clockwise each step while a dot moves one position anticlockwise among 8 positions numbered clockwise. In figure 1 the sector is at position 1 and the dot at position 1. In figure 5, the sector and dot are respectively at positions —
(a) 5 and 1 (b) 1 and 5 (c) 5 and 5 (d) 5 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is 5 and 1, which forgets that the dot also moves. The sector advances clockwise four places from position 1 to position 5; the dot moves anticlockwise four places from position 1 (1 → 8 → 7 → 6 → 5), also reaching position 5, so the answer is 5 and 5.
Source: PYQ-style
