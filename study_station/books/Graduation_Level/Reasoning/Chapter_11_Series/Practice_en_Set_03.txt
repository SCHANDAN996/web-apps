51. Find the missing term: 7, 12, 19, 28, 39, ?
(a) 50 (b) 56 (c) 52 (d) 54
Answer: (c)
Solution: The differences are 5, 7, 9, 11 — increasing by 2 each time. The next difference is 13, so 39 + 13 = 52. The tempting option 54 comes from wrongly adding 15, skipping a step in the difference pattern. So the answer is 52.

53. Find the missing term: 2, 5, 11, 23, 47, ?
