126. Find the missing term: 2, 3, 10, 39, 172, ?
(a) 865 (b) 875 (c) 895 (d) 885
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 875 comes from assuming a constant multiplier of 5 at the last step, but the multipliers themselves grow. The pattern is multiply by n and add n²: 2×1+1=3, 3×2+4=10, 10×3+9=39, 39×4+16=172, 172×5+25=885, so the answer is 885.
Source: PYQ-style
