1. Find the next term: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ?
(a) 129 (b) 95 (c) 111 (d) 127
Answer: (d)
Solution: Each term is double the previous term plus 1: 3×2+1=7, 7×2+1=15, and so on up to 63, so 63×2+1=127. The tempting option 129 comes from adding 64 instead of doubling and adding 1, so the answer is 127.
Source: PYQ-style
