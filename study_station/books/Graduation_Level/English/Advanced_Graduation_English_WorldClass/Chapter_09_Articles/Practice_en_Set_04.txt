76. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is an university student. (b) He is the university student. (c) He is a university student. (d) He is university student.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "University" begins with a consonant sound /juː/, so it takes "a", not "an". Sound, not spelling, decides the article.

77. Fill in the blank: She plays ___ flute very well.

Answer: (c)
Solution: Musical instruments take "the" when we talk about playing them: play the flute, the piano, the guitar.

78. Fill in the blank: ___ honesty is the best policy.
(a) A (b) No article (c) The (d) An
Answer: (b)
Solution: Abstract nouns used in a general sense (honesty, wisdom, courage) take no article. "The honesty" would refer to a specific instance.

80. Fill in the blank: He is ___ heir to a large fortune.
