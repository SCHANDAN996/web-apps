Questions 51–60: Easy | Questions 61–75: Medium

51. She is ___ honest woman.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the first sound is a vowel sound /ɒ/. We use "an" before vowel sounds, not vowel letters.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. ___ sun rises in the east.
(a) The (b) An (c) A (d) No article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The" is used before unique things of which there is only one — the sun, the moon, the earth.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

53. I saw ___ one-eyed man at the station.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "One-eyed" is pronounced with a /w/ sound (wun), a consonant sound, so "a" is correct. Sound, not spelling, decides a/an.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

54. He plays ___ guitar very well.
(a) the (b) an (c) a (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The" is used before musical instruments when we talk about playing them: play the guitar, the piano, the flute.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

55. ___ gold is a precious metal.
(a) A (b) An (c) No article (d) The
Answer: (c)
Solution: Material nouns used in a general sense take no article. "Gold" here means gold in general, not specific gold.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

56. Mount Everest is ___ highest peak in the world.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: Superlative adjectives (highest, best, largest) always take "the" before them.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

57. She goes to ___ school by bus every day.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (b)
Solution: When "school" is used for its primary purpose (studying), no article is used: go to school, go to bed, go to church.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

58. ___ Ganga is a holy river of India.
(a) A (b) An (c) No article (d) The
Answer: (d)
Solution: Names of rivers, seas, oceans and mountain ranges always take "the": the Ganga, the Himalayas, the Indian Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

59. He is ___ university student.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (d)
Solution: "University" begins with a /juː/ (y) sound, which is a consonant sound, so "a" is used. This is a classic examiner trap — spelling suggests "an", sound demands "a".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

60. ___ honesty is the best policy.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (d)
Solution: Abstract nouns (honesty, kindness, truth) used in a general sense take no article.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

61. ___ rich should help the poor.
(a) The (b) An (c) A (d) No article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The + adjective" denotes a whole class of people: the rich = rich people, the poor, the blind, the old.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

62. I have ___ few friends in Delhi, so I never feel lonely.
(a) no article (b) the (c) few (d) a
Answer: (d)
Solution: "A few" means some (positive sense); "few" alone means almost none (negative). Since he never feels lonely, the positive "a few" fits.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

63. ___ more you read, ___ more you learn.
(a) A, a (b) An, an (c) The, the (d) No article, no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: In double comparative structures ("the more… the more"), "the" is used before both comparatives.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

64. He is ___ best player in the team.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: Superlatives take "the". "Best" is the superlative of good, so "the best player" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

65. ___ United States of America is a powerful country.
(a) A (b) An (c) No article (d) The
Answer: (d)
Solution: Countries whose names are plural or contain words like "Republic", "Kingdom", "States" take "the": the USA, the UK, the Republic of India.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

66. Spot the error: Part P: He is / Part Q: an European / Part R: by birth. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part Q is wrong. "European" starts with a /j/ (y) sound, a consonant sound, so it should be "a European". Examiner's trap: the letter is a vowel but the sound is not.
Source: PYQ-style

67. She returned home after ___ hour.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Hour" has a silent 'h' and begins with a vowel sound /aʊ/, so "an hour" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

68. ___ Taj Mahal was built by Shah Jahan.
(a) A (b) The (c) An (d) No article
Answer: (b)
Solution: Names of famous buildings, monuments and historical structures take "the": the Taj Mahal, the Red Fort, the Great Wall of China.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

69. There is ___ little milk left in the fridge; you can make tea.
(a) a (b) the (c) little (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "A little" means some, enough for the purpose (positive); "little" means almost none (negative). Since tea can be made, "a little" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

70. Spot the error: Part P: The gold / Part Q: of this ring / Part R: is pure. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part R
Answer: (c)
Solution: No error. When a material noun is particularised (the gold of this ring, not gold in general), it takes "the". This is the exception to the "no article before material nouns" rule.
Source: PYQ-style

71. ___ Indian Ocean is the third largest ocean in the world.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (c)
Solution: Names of oceans, seas and rivers always take "the": the Indian Ocean, the Arabian Sea, the Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

72. He goes to ___ bed at 10 p.m. every night.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Go to bed" (for sleeping) is an idiomatic use with no article. But "the bed" is used when referring to the object: "She sat on the bed."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

73. ___ Ramayana is a sacred epic of the Hindus.
(a) The (b) An (c) A (d) No article
Answer: (a)
Solution: Names of holy books, epics and famous literary works take "the": the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, the Quran, the Bible.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

74. Of the two brothers, Ravi is ___ taller.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: When a comparative is used to select one out of two ("of the two"), it takes "the": the taller of the two, the better of the two.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

75. Spot the error: Part P: He is / Part Q: the same man / Part R: who helped me yesterday. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: No error. "The same" always takes "the" — "same" is never used without it. The sentence is grammatically correct throughout.
Source: PYQ-style
