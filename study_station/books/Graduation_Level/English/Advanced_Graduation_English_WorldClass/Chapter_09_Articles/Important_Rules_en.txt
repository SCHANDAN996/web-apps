# 📋 Important Grammar Rules & Tips — Articles

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | **A** before consonant *sound*, **An** before vowel *sound* — sound matters, not the letter. | a university, an hour, an M.P., a one-rupee note | "an university" / "a hour" — the letter tricks you; say the word aloud. |
| 2 | Use **the** when the noun is specific or already mentioned (second reference). | I saw a dog. The dog was black. | First mention = a/an; second mention = the. Questions test both in one sentence. |
| 3 | **The** before unique things (sun, moon, earth, sky, equator). | The sun rises in the east. | "Sun rises in east" — dropping 'the' before unique nouns is the classic error. |
| 4 | **The** before superlatives and ordinals (first, second, best, most). | She is the best player. He came the first. | "He is best player in class" — superlative without 'the' is wrong. |
| 5 | **The** before rivers, seas, oceans, mountain *ranges*, deserts, gulfs — but **no article** before single peaks and lakes. | the Ganga, the Himalayas, the Sahara; but Mount Everest, Lake Baikal | "The Mount Everest" is wrong; "Himalayas" without 'the' is wrong. |
| 6 | **No article** before proper nouns (names of persons, countries, cities) — except countries with plural/union/republic names. | India, Paris; but the USA, the UK, the Republic of Ireland | "The India" is wrong; "USA" without 'the' is wrong. |
| 7 | **No article** before uncountable/abstract nouns used in a general sense. | Honesty is the best policy. Milk is white. | "The honesty is the best policy" — general sense takes zero article. |
| 8 | **No article** before names of meals, languages, games, and academic subjects (general sense). | We have lunch at 1. He plays cricket. She teaches physics. | "The lunch is ready" (general) is wrong; but "the lunch we had yesterday" (specific) is right. |
| 9 | **A/An** means 'one' or 'per' with singular countable nouns; never use a/an with plurals or uncountables. | a boy, an apple, once a week, Rs. 50 a kilo | "an information", "a furnitures" — uncountables never take a/an. |
| 10 | **The + adjective** = a whole class of people (plural meaning). | The rich should help the poor. | "The rich is…" is wrong — it takes a plural verb: "The rich are…" |
| 11 | Same noun repeated with 'and' → one article = same person/thing; two articles = two different persons/things. | The poet and philosopher is dead. (one person) / The poet and the philosopher are dead. (two persons) | Verb agreement flips with the second 'the' — a favourite trap. |
| 12 | **The** before musical instruments, inventions, and holy/epic books; **no article** before 'man/mankind' meaning the whole race. | She plays the flute. The telephone was invented by Bell. The Ramayana; but Man is mortal. | "She plays flute" is wrong; "The man is mortal" (general sense) is wrong. |

## (b) Time Management — The 15/45-Second Rule

**⏱️ 15-Second Questions (Fact / Rule-Recall based)**
Attempt these instantly; do not re-read.

- Fill-in-the-blank with a/an/the before a single noun (Rule 1, 5, 6).
- Spot-the-error where the article before a unique noun, superlative, or country name is missing/extra (Rules 3, 4, 6).
- Sound-based a/an choice: hour, honest, university, European, one-day, M.A. (Rule 1).
- Fixed phrases: *in a hurry, at a loss, the whole day, by the dozen.*

**⏱️ 45-Second Questions (Inference / Context based)**
Read the full sentence twice; meaning decides the article.

- General vs. specific sense of uncountable/abstract nouns: "___ water of this well is sweet" (specific → the) vs. "___ water is essential" (general → zero) (Rules 7, 8).
- Same-person vs. two-person constructions with verb agreement (Rule 11).
- 'The + adjective' class nouns and their plural verbs (Rule 10).
- Error-spotting where the article error hides in the middle of a long sentence with an intervening phrase: "The life of the poor farmers of this region **is/are**…" — locate the real head noun first.

**Strategy:** In the exam, clear all 15-second article questions in the first pass. Mark 45-second ones, return in the second pass, and decide by asking: *"Is this noun general or specific?"*

## (c) Morphology — Roots & Affixes for Article-Related Vocabulary

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Example Words | Usage Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **uni-** (Latin: unus) | one | unique, university, union, uniform | Words starting with 'uni-' take **a** (consonant sound /juː/): *a unique chance*. |
| **hon-** (Latin: honos) | honour | honest, honour, honorary | Silent 'h' → vowel sound → **an**: *an honest man*. |
| **heir-** (Latin: heres) | one who inherits | heir, heiress, heritage | 'Heir' has silent 'h' → **an heir**; but 'heritage' has sounded 'h' → **a heritage site**. |
| **equi-** (Latin: aequus) | equal | equator, equinox, equilibrium | Unique object → **the equator**. |
| **-est / most** (superlative affix) | highest degree | tallest, most beautiful | Always **the**: *the tallest boy*. |
| **-th / -st / -nd / -rd** (ordinal affix) | order in series | fifth, first, second, third | Ordinals take **the**: *the fifth chapter*. |
| **-ism** (Greek: -ismos) | abstract doctrine/quality | idealism, patriotism, optimism | Abstract nouns in general sense → **zero article**: *Optimism helps.* |
| **-ity / -ness** (Latin/Old English) | abstract state | honesty, kindness, humanity | General sense → zero article: *Kindness costs nothing.* Specific sense → the: *the kindness she showed*. |

**Memory hook:** *"Sound decides A/AN, Sense decides THE, and General sense decides ZERO."*
