Questions 26–40: Easy | Questions 41–50: Medium

26. She is ___ honest woman.
(a) an (b) a (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the first sound is a vowel sound /ɒ/. Use "an" before vowel sounds, not vowel letters.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. ___ sun rises in the east.
(a) A (b) An (c) No article (d) The
Answer: (d)
Solution: "The" is used before unique things of which there is only one — the sun, the moon, the earth.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

28. I saw ___ one-eyed man at the station.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (d)
Solution: "One-eyed" is pronounced with a /w/ sound (wun), a consonant sound, so "a" is correct. This is a classic examiner trap — the letter is a vowel but the sound is not.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap)

29. He plays ___ guitar very well.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "The" is used before musical instruments when we talk about playing them: play the guitar, the piano, the flute.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

30. ___ Ganga is a holy river of India.
(a) A (b) An (c) No article (d) The
Answer: (d)
Solution: Names of rivers always take "the": the Ganga, the Yamuna, the Nile.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

31. Mount Everest is ___ highest peak in the world.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: Superlative adjectives (highest, best, largest) always take "the".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

32. He goes to ___ school by bus every day.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (a)
Solution: No article is used when school, college, church, hospital or prison is visited for its primary purpose (here, to study). "The school" would mean visiting the building for some other reason.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

33. ___ honesty is the best policy.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (d)
Solution: Abstract nouns used in a general sense (honesty, truth, kindness) take no article.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

34. I had ___ lunch at 2 p.m. today.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (a)
Solution: Names of meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) take no article when used in the ordinary sense.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

35. He is ___ M.P. from Rajasthan.
(a) an (b) a (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "M.P." is pronounced "em-pee", beginning with a vowel sound /e/, so "an" is used. Articles depend on sound, not spelling.
Source: PYQ-style

36. ___ poor deserve our help.
(a) A (b) The (c) An (d) No article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "The + adjective" denotes a whole class of people: the poor, the rich, the blind, the old.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

37. She bought ___ umbrella from the market.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Umbrella" begins with a vowel sound /ʌ/, so "an" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

38. ___ Taj Mahal is situated in Agra.
(a) A (b) An (c) No article (d) The
Answer: (d)
Solution: Names of famous buildings and monuments take "the": the Taj Mahal, the Red Fort, the Qutub Minar.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

39. He is ___ best player in the team.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: Superlative degree ("best") always takes "the" before it.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

40. ___ gold is a precious metal.
(a) A (b) No article (c) The (d) An
Answer: (b)
Solution: Material nouns used in a general sense (gold, water, milk, iron) take no article. "The gold" would refer to specific gold already mentioned.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

41. He is ___ university student who topped ___ university last year.
(a) the, a (b) an, the (c) a, the (d) a, a
Answer: (c)
Solution: "University" begins with a /j/ (yoo) consonant sound, so "a university student". The second blank refers to a specific university already identified, so "the".
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap — sound vs spelling)

42. ___ Ramayana is a sacred book of the Hindus.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (c)
Solution: Names of holy and famous books take "the": the Ramayana, the Quran, the Bible, the Gita.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

43. I first met him ___ year ago.
(a) the (b) an (c) a (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Year" is pronounced with a /j/ (y) consonant sound, so "a year ago", not "an year ago".
Source: PYQ-style

44. ___ more you read, ___ more you learn.
(a) The, the (b) A, a (c) An, an (d) No article, no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: In the double comparative structure "the + comparative … the + comparative", "the" is used in both parts.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

45. He returned home after ___ hour.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Hour" has a silent 'h' and begins with a vowel sound /aʊ/, so "an hour".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

46. ___ United States of America is a powerful country.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (c)
Solution: Countries whose names are plural or contain words like "republic", "kingdom" or "states" take "the": the USA, the UK, the Republic of India.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

47. What ___ beautiful scene it is!
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: In exclamatory sentences with a singular countable noun, "a/an" is used after "what": What a beautiful scene! "Beautiful" starts with a consonant sound, so "a".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

48. He is ___ heir to his uncle's property.
(a) a (b) no article (c) the (d) an
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Heir" has a silent 'h' and begins with a vowel sound /eə/, so "an heir". Compare: "a horse" (h is pronounced).
Source: PYQ-style

49. ___ Indian Ocean lies to the south of India.
(a) The (b) An (c) A (d) No article
Answer: (a)
Solution: Names of oceans, seas and gulfs take "the": the Indian Ocean, the Arabian Sea, the Bay of Bengal.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

50. He goes to ___ bed at 10 p.m. and gets up early in ___ morning.
(a) the, the (b) no article, the (c) a, the (d) no article, a
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Go to bed" (for sleeping) is a fixed phrase with no article. But "in the morning" is a fixed time expression that takes "the".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
