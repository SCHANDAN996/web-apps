Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. He is ___ honest man.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', so the first sound is a vowel sound /ɒ/. We use "an" before vowel sounds, not vowel letters.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. ___ sun rises in the east.
(a) The (b) An (c) A (d) No article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The" is used before unique things of which there is only one — the sun, the moon, the earth.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

3. She plays ___ guitar very well.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "The" is used before musical instruments: play the guitar, the piano, the flute.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

4. I saw ___ one-eyed man at the station.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "One" is pronounced with a /w/ sound (won), a consonant sound, so we use "a". This is a classic examiner trap — go by sound, not spelling.
Source: PYQ-style

5. He goes to ___ school by bus every day.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (a)
Solution: No article is used when school, college, church, hospital, or prison is visited for its primary purpose (a student going to study).
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

6. ___ Ganga is a holy river of India.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "The" is used before names of rivers, seas, oceans, and mountain ranges: the Ganga, the Himalayas, the Indian Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

7. Mount Everest is ___ highest peak in the world.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "The" is always used before superlative degrees: the highest, the best, the most beautiful.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

8. He is ___ university student.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "University" begins with a /juː/ (yoo) sound, which is a consonant sound, so "a" is correct. Examiner trap: do not choose "an" just because the letter is a vowel.
Source: PYQ-style

9. ___ honesty is the best policy.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (d)
Solution: Abstract nouns used in a general sense (honesty, wisdom, love) take no article.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

10. I had ___ European friend in college.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (d)
Solution: "European" starts with a /j/ (y) sound, a consonant sound, so "a" is used despite the vowel letter 'E'.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

11. ___ poor need our help.
(a) The (b) An (c) A (d) No article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The + adjective" denotes a whole class of people: the poor, the rich, the blind, the old.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

12. She is ___ M.A. in English.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "M" is pronounced /em/, beginning with a vowel sound, so "an" is used. Abbreviations follow the sound rule.
Source: PYQ-style

13. ___ Ramayana is a sacred epic of India.
(a) A (b) An (c) The (d) No article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "The" is used before names of holy and famous books: the Ramayana, the Quran, the Bible.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

14. What ___ beautiful flower this is!
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (d)
Solution: In exclamatory sentences with a singular countable noun, "a/an" is used after "what": What a beautiful flower!
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

15. He goes to ___ bed at 10 p.m. daily.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (a)
Solution: No article is used with bed, home, work, school when they express their normal purpose: go to bed (to sleep).
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

16. ___ gold is a precious metal.
(a) A (b) No article (c) The (d) An
Answer: (b)
Solution: Material nouns used in a general sense (gold, water, milk) take no article. "The gold" would mean some specific gold.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

17. He is ___ tallest boy in the class.
(a) a (b) an (c) no article (d) the
Answer: (d)
Solution: Superlative adjectives always take "the": the tallest, the fastest, the cleverest.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

18. I need ___ hour to finish this work.
(a) a (b) the (c) an (d) no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Hour" has a silent 'h' and begins with a vowel sound /aʊ/, so "an" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

19. ___ Himalayas lie to the north of India.
(a) A (b) An (c) No article (d) The
Answer: (d)
Solution: "The" is used before ranges of mountains (the Himalayas) but not before a single peak (Mount Everest).
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

20. He plays ___ cricket every Sunday.
(a) no article (b) an (c) the (d) a
Answer: (a)
Solution: No article is used before names of games and sports: play cricket, football, chess. (But musical instruments take "the".)
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

21. ___ more you read, ___ more you learn.
(a) A, a (b) An, an (c) The, the (d) No article, no article
Answer: (c)
Solution: In double comparative constructions ("the more... the more"), "the" is used before both comparatives.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

22. He is ___ best of the two brothers.
(a) a (b) an (c) no article (d) the
Answer: (d)
Solution: When one of only two persons or things is compared, the comparative is normally used (the better of the two), but when a superlative form appears with "of the two", it still takes "the". Here "the best of the two" is the accepted idiomatic form with "the".
Source: PYQ-style

23. Spot the error: Part P: "The gold" / Part Q: "of this ring" / Part R: "is pure." / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: The sentence is correct. When a material noun is particularised by an "of" phrase, it takes "the" — "the gold of this ring" means the specific gold in this ring.
Source: PYQ-style

24. ___ United States of America is a powerful country.
(a) A (b) The (c) An (d) No article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "The" is used before countries whose names include words like republic, kingdom, states, or union: the USA, the UK, the Republic of Ireland.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

25. Spot the error: Part P: "He is an" / Part Q: "unique player" / Part R: "in our team." / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part P has the error. "Unique" begins with a /juː/ (yoo) consonant sound, so it should be "a unique player", not "an unique player". Examiner trap: the vowel letter 'u' misleads many candidates.
Source: PYQ-style
