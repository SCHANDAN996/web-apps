Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Riceive (c) Receive (d) Receeve
Answer: (c)
Solution: The rule "i before e except after c" applies here: after "c" we write "ei". "Recieve" is the most common misspelling trap, so the answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

2. Choose the correct synonym of "Abundant".
(a) Scarce (b) Plentiful (c) Rare (d) Meagre
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Scarce", "rare" and "meagre" are antonyms meaning "in short supply" — a classic trap of offering opposite words. "Abundant" means existing in large quantity, so the answer is Plentiful.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

3. Choose the correct antonym of "Transparent".
(a) Clear (b) Obvious (c) Fragile (d) Opaque
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Clear" and "obvious" are near-synonyms, not antonyms — the trap is picking a similar word when the opposite is asked. "Transparent" means allowing light through; its opposite is Opaque, so the answer is Opaque.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

4. Fill in the blank: She has been living in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) since (b) for (c) from (d) at
Answer: (a)
Solution: "For" is the tempting trap, but it is used with a duration (for ten years), not a starting point. "Since" is used with a point of time like 2015, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

5. Choose the correctly punctuated sentence.
(a) It's raining heavily today. (b) Its raining heavily today. (c) Its' raining heavily today. (d) It raining's heavily today.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Its" without an apostrophe is a possessive pronoun and is the classic trap here. The sentence needs the contraction "It is", so the answer is It's raining heavily today.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

6. Choose the correct one-word substitution: "A person who speaks many languages."
(a) Polyglot (b) Bilingual (c) Linguist (d) Orator
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Linguist" is the trap — it means a person skilled in languages or the science of language, not specifically a speaker of many languages. "Polyglot" (poly = many, glot = tongue) is exact, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English

7. Fill in the blank: Neither of the two boys ______ done his homework.
(a) have (b) were (c) are (d) has
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "two boys" sounds plural, but "neither of" takes a singular verb. The subject is "neither" (not one), so the answer is has.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

8. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom "To bite the dust".
(a) To eat quickly (b) To work hard (c) To clean the floor (d) To suffer defeat
Answer: (d)
Solution: The literal reading "to eat quickly" is the trap; idioms must not be read literally. "To bite the dust" means to fail or be defeated, so the answer is To suffer defeat.
Source: PYQ-style

9. Choose the correct passive form: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter was written by her. (b) A letter is written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap options change the tense — the passive must keep the simple present of the active sentence. Simple present active becomes "is/are + V3", so the answer is A letter is written by her.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

10. Choose the correct indirect speech: He said, "I am tired."
(a) He said that he is tired. (b) He said that he had been tired. (c) He said that he was tired. (d) He said that he will be tired.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Is tired" is the trap — in reported speech the present tense shifts back one step. "Am" becomes "was", so the answer is He said that he was tired.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

11. Spot the error: Part P: One of my friend / Part Q: has gone / Part R: to Mumbai yesterday.
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q looks suspicious because of "has gone... yesterday", but "one of" must be followed by a plural noun — "friend" should be "friends". The error is in Part P, so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

12. Fill in the blank: The committee ______ divided in their opinion.
(a) was (b) has (c) is (d) were
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Was" is the trap because "committee" is a collective noun, but when members act separately (divided in their opinion), a plural verb is used. So the answer is were.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English

13. Choose the correct synonym of "Candid".
(a) Secretive (b) Frank (c) Rude (d) Clever
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Rude" is the trap — candid speech can feel rude, but the word itself means truthful and open. "Candid" means Frank, so the answer is Frank.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

14. Choose the correct antonym of "Expand".
(a) Enlarge (b) Extend (c) Spread (d) Contract
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Enlarge", "extend" and "spread" are all synonyms placed as traps. The opposite of "expand" is Contract, so the answer is Contract.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

15. Fill in the blank: He is senior ______ me by two years.
(a) than (b) from (c) to (d) of
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Than" is the sound-right trap — comparative feel suggests "than", but Latin comparatives (senior, junior, superior, inferior) take "to". So the answer is to.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English

16. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occurrence (b) Occurence (c) Ocurrence (d) Occurrance
Answer: (a)
Solution: The traps drop one "r" or one "c"; the verb "occur" doubles the "r" before "-ence" and keeps double "c". So the answer is Occurrence.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

17. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom "A red herring".
(a) A misleading distraction (b) A lucky catch (c) An expensive gift (d) A rare discovery
Answer: (a)
Solution: "A lucky catch" plays on the literal fish image — the trap. A red herring is something that diverts attention from the real issue, so the answer is A misleading distraction.
Source: PYQ-style

18. Fill in the blank: Each of the students ______ given a certificate.
(a) were (b) was (c) have (d) are
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Were" tempts because "students" is plural and sits next to the verb, but the true subject is "each", which is singular. So the answer is was.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

19. Choose the correct one-word substitution: "That which cannot be avoided."
(a) Invincible (b) Incredible (c) Inevitable (d) Infallible
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Invincible" (cannot be defeated) is the look-alike trap. "Inevitable" means certain to happen and unavoidable, so the answer is Inevitable.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English

20. Choose the correct article: He is ______ honest man.
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "A" is the trap because "honest" begins with the consonant letter h, but the h is silent and the word starts with a vowel sound. So the answer is an.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

21. Spot the error: Part P: The teacher, along with his students, / Part Q: were present / Part R: at the function.
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part P looks fine, and the plural "students" next to the verb makes "were" sound right — the intervening-phrase trap. With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject "the teacher" (singular), so it should be "was". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Choose the correct indirect speech: She said to me, "Do you like tea?"
(a) She asked me that did I like tea. (b) She told me if I like tea. (c) She asked me whether did I like tea. (d) She asked me if I liked tea.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap options keep the question form "did I like" — in reported speech the clause becomes a statement with backshifted tense. "Do you like" becomes "if I liked", so the answer is She asked me if I liked tea.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

23. Fill in the blank: Scarcely had he entered the room ______ the phone rang.
(a) than (b) then (c) when (d) that
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Than" is the sound-right trap because "scarcely" feels comparative, but the fixed pair is "scarcely/hardly... when" ("than" pairs with "no sooner"). So the answer is when.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English

24. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The news is very shocking. (b) The news were very shocking. (c) The news are very shocking. (d) The news have been very shocking.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" ends in "s" and sounds plural — the trap — but it is an uncountable singular noun and takes a singular verb. So the answer is The news is very shocking.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

25. Spot the error: Part P: No sooner did the bell ring / Part Q: when the students / Part R: rushed out of the class.
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part P is correct, and "when" sounds natural after "no sooner" — the trap. The correct correlative pair is "no sooner... than", so "when" must be replaced by "than". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style
