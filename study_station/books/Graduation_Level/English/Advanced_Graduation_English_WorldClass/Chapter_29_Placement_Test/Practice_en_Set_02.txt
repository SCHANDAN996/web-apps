26. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Receive (b) Recieve (c) Riceive (d) Receeve
Answer: (a)
Solution: The rule "i before e except after c" applies here: after "c" we write "ei". "Recieve" is the most common misspelling and tempts many candidates, so the answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

27. Choose the word closest in meaning to "Abundant".
(a) Scarce (b) Costly (c) Plentiful (d) Rare
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Scarce" and "rare" are antonyms and are placed to trap hasty readers. "Abundant" means existing in large quantity, so the answer is Plentiful.
Source: PYQ-style

28. Choose the word opposite in meaning to "Transparent".
(a) Clear (b) Obvious (c) Fragile (d) Opaque
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Clear" and "obvious" are near-synonyms, not opposites — a classic trap. The opposite of transparent is something that does not allow light through, so the answer is Opaque.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

29. Fill in the blank: She has been working here ______ 2015.
(a) since (b) for (c) from (d) at
Answer: (a)
Solution: "For" is used with a duration (for ten years), not a starting point. "Since" is used with a point of time like a year, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

30. Choose the correct passive form: "The teacher praised the boy."
(a) The boy is praised by the teacher. (b) The boy had praised by the teacher. (c) The boy has been praised by the teacher. (d) The boy was praised by the teacher.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The active sentence is in simple past, so the passive must be "was/were + past participle". Option with "is praised" wrongly shifts to present tense, so the answer is "The boy was praised by the teacher."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

31. Choose the correctly punctuated sentence.
(a) Its a beautiful day. (b) Its' a beautiful day. (c) It's a beautiful day. (d) It is' a beautiful day.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Its" without an apostrophe is possessive and is the classic trap here. The sentence needs the contraction of "it is", so the answer is "It's a beautiful day."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

32. Fill in the blank: Neither of the two boys ______ done his homework.
(a) have (b) were (c) are (d) has
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Have" tempts because "boys" is plural, but "neither of" takes a singular verb. So the answer is has.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

33. Choose the correct indirect speech: He said, "I am tired."
(a) He said that he is tired. (b) He said that I was tired. (c) He said that he was tired. (d) He said that he had tired.
Answer: (c)
Solution: In reported speech the present tense shifts to past and "I" becomes "he". Keeping "is" is the common trap, so the answer is "He said that he was tired."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

34. Choose the one-word substitute for "a person who speaks many languages".
(a) Polyglot (b) Bilingual (c) Linguist (d) Orator
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Linguist" tempts because it relates to language, but it means a student of language, not necessarily a speaker of many. "Poly" means many and "glot" means tongue, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: PYQ-style

35. Fill in the blank: The Ganges ______ one of the longest rivers in India.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Are" tempts because "rivers" is plural, but the subject is "The Ganges", a single river. So the answer is is.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

36. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occurence (b) Ocurrence (c) Occurrence (d) Occurrance
Answer: (c)
Solution: The verb "occur" doubles the "r" and the noun takes "-ence": oc-cur-rence. Single "r" or single "c" versions are traps, so the answer is Occurrence.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

37. Choose the word closest in meaning to "Candid".
(a) Frank (b) Secretive (c) Rude (d) Clever
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Rude" tempts because candid remarks can hurt, but candid means honest and open, not impolite. So the answer is Frank.

38. Fill in the blank: He is junior ______ me by two years.
(a) than (b) to (c) from (d) with
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Than" is the biggest trap because we say "younger than". But Latin comparatives like junior, senior, superior and inferior take "to", so the answer is to.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

39. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The news is shocking. (b) The news are shocking. (c) The news were shocking. (d) The news have shocked.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" looks plural and tempts "are", but it is an uncountable singular noun. So the answer is "The news is shocking."

40. Choose the word opposite in meaning to "Expand".
(a) Enlarge (b) Contract (c) Extend (d) Spread
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Enlarge", "extend" and "spread" are synonyms placed as traps. The opposite of expand is to shrink, so the answer is Contract.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

41. Spot the error: Part P: One of my friend / Part Q: has gone / Part R: to Delhi / Part S: yesterday.
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part Q tempts because "has gone yesterday" feels odd, but it is acceptable with a finished time in informal usage; the real error is in Part P — after "one of" we need a plural noun, "one of my friends". So the error is in Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Choose the correct meaning of the idiom "to burn the midnight oil".
(a) To work or study late into the night (b) To waste electricity (c) To light lamps at night (d) To stay awake doing nothing
Answer: (a)
Solution: The literal options about lamps and electricity are traps. The idiom means working hard late at night, so the answer is "To work or study late into the night."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

43. Fill in the blank: Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) had I reached (c) I reached (d) did I reached
Answer: (b)
Solution: "I had reached" tempts because the past perfect is correct, but after "hardly" at the start of a clause the subject and auxiliary invert. So the answer is "had I reached".
Source: PYQ-style

44. Spot the error: Part P: The principal, along with the teachers, / Part Q: were present / Part R: at the meeting / Part S: yesterday.
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q looks right because "teachers" is plural, but "along with" is an intervening phrase — the true subject is "the principal", which is singular, so the verb must be "was". The error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Choose the correct indirect speech: She said to me, "Do you like tea?"
(a) She asked me did I like tea. (b) She asked me if I liked tea. (c) She asked me that did I like tea. (d) She asked me whether I like tea.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Did I like tea" keeps the question form, which is wrong in reported speech; we use statement order with "if/whether" and backshift the tense. So the answer is "She asked me if I liked tea."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

46. Fill in the blank: The committee ______ divided in their opinion on the new policy.
(a) was (b) is (c) were (d) has been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Was" tempts because a committee is a collective noun, but when members act separately — shown by "their opinion" — a plural verb is used. So the answer is were.
Source: PYQ-style

47. Choose the word closest in meaning to "Obsolete".
(a) Modern (b) Ancient (c) Useful (d) Outdated
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Ancient" is the trap — something ancient is old but may still be in use; obsolete means no longer used or needed. So the answer is Outdated.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Choose the correctly framed question.
(a) Why are you late? (b) Why you are late? (c) Why late you are? (d) Why you late are?
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hindi speakers often say "Why you are late?" by translating directly, but English questions need inversion of subject and auxiliary. So the answer is "Why are you late?"
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

49. Spot the error: Part P: Each of the students / Part Q: have submitted / Part R: their assignments / Part S: on time.
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part Q tempts because "students" is plural, but "each of" takes a singular verb — "has submitted". The error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

50. Fill in the blank: No sooner did she enter the room ______ the phone rang.
(a) when (b) then (c) than (d) that
Answer: (c)
Solution: "When" is the trap because it sounds natural with time, but the fixed correlative pair is "no sooner ... than". So the answer is than.
