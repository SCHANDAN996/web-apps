# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Explaining the Hardest Concept Like You're 12

## The Concept: "Fact vs. Inference" — Why You Lose Marks on Questions You *Thought* You Understood

The single hardest thing in a placement test's English section is not grammar rules or vocabulary. It is this: **knowing when the answer is sitting in the passage (Fact) and when you must *conclude* it yourself (Inference).** Students mix these up, overthink easy questions, and underthink hard ones. Let's fix that forever.

---

## 🍕 The Pizza Story (A Daily-Life Example)

Imagine you come home and see an empty pizza box on the table, your brother wiping his mouth, and the smell of cheese in the air.

**Question 1 (FACT):** "Is there a pizza box on the table?"
→ You don't think. You *see* it. Answer in 2 seconds. ✅

**Question 2 (INFERENCE):** "Who probably ate the pizza?"
→ The box doesn't have a name tag. But the clues — empty box, brother wiping mouth, smell — point to your brother. You *detect* the answer from evidence. This takes 30–45 seconds of thinking. 🕵️

**That's the whole game:**
- **Fact question** = the answer is *written* in the passage. Find it, pick it, move on. **15 seconds.**
- **Inference question** = the answer is *hidden between the lines*. Collect clues, eliminate options, conclude. **45 seconds.**

---

## 🚨 Where 12-Year-Olds (and Toppers) Go Wrong

**Trap 1 — The "Sound-Right" Trap:** In inference questions, one option is *directly copied* from the passage. It feels safe, so students pick it. But an inference question never asks what is *stated* — it asks what is *suggested*. The copied line is the trap!

**Trap 2 — The "Over-thinking" Trap:** In fact questions, students imagine hidden meanings — "But maybe the author *meant* something else…" No! If it's written in black and white, just pick it. Don't cook a story.

**The Unforgettable Trick — 🕵️ "DETECTIVE vs. PHOTOCOPIER":**
> **Fact question? Be a PHOTOCOPIER** — copy exactly what's in the passage. No brain needed.
> **Inference question? Be a DETECTIVE** — gather clues, reject the photocopied option, conclude what the author *hinted* but never *said*.

Ask yourself one magic question before answering:
**"Can I point my finger at the exact line where this answer is written?"**
- YES → Fact → Photocopier mode → 15 seconds.
- NO → Inference → Detective mode → 45 seconds.

---

## 🧠 Try It Right Now

Passage snippet: *"Riya refreshed the result page for the tenth time, her palms sweaty, whispering 'please, please' under her breath."*

- **Q (Fact):** How many times did Riya refresh the page? → *Ten.* Point at the line. Done. 15 sec.
- **Q (Inference):** Riya is most likely feeling…? → *Anxious/nervous.* The passage never says "nervous" — but sweaty palms + whispering "please" are your clues. Detective mode. 45 sec.

See? You just solved the "hardest" concept in the placement test.
