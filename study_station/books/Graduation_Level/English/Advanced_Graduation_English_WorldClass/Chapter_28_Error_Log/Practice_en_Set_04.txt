76. Spot the error: Part P: Neither of the two candidates / Part Q: have submitted / Part R: their documents on time. / Part S: No error
(a) No error (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is Part R, where "their" looks wrong, but "their" is acceptable with "neither of the candidates" in modern usage. The real error is in Part Q: "neither of" takes a singular verb, so "have" must be "has". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.

77. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occurence (b) Ocurrence (c) Occurrence (d) Occurrance
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap options double the wrong letters: "occur" doubles the r before "-ence" and keeps double c. Only "occurrence" has cc plus rr, so the answer is Occurrence.

78. Spot the error: Part P: The Ganges is / Part Q: one of the longest river / Part R: in the Indian subcontinent. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to suspect the proper noun in Part P, which is correct since "the Ganges" rightly takes the article. After "one of the", a plural noun is required, so "river" must be "rivers". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.

81. Choose the correct sentence.

85. Choose the correct passive form: "The teacher praised the boy."
(a) The boy was praised by the teacher. (b) The boy had praised by the teacher. (c) The boy has been praised by the teacher. (d) The boy is praised by the teacher.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the present-tense option, which ignores that the active sentence is in simple past. Simple past active becomes "was/were + V3" in passive, so the answer is "The boy was praised by the teacher."

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to treat "his friends" as part of the subject. With "as well as", the verb agrees with the first subject only — "He" — so "were" must be "was". The error is in Part Q, so the answer is Part Q.
