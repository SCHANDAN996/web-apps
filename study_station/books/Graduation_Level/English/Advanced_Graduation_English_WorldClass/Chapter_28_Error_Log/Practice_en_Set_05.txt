101. Spot the error: Neither of the two candidates (P) / have submitted (Q) / the required documents (R) / before the deadline. (S)
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting choice is Part R, but "documents" is fine. "Neither of" takes a singular verb, so "have submitted" must be "has submitted". The error is in Part Q.

106. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is senior to me by two years. (b) He is more senior than me by two years. (c) He is senior than me by two years. (d) He is most senior to me by two years.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Senior than" is the classic trap; Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior take "to", never "than", and are not used with "more/most". The correct sentence is "He is senior to me by two years."

Answer: (c)
