51. What is the primary purpose of maintaining an Error Log during exam preparation?
(a) To record only the questions you answered correctly (b) To memorise answer keys of previous papers (c) To count the total number of mock tests attempted (d) To record, classify and revisit every mistake so it is never repeated
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice suggests logging correct answers, but a log of successes teaches nothing new. An Error Log exists to capture mistakes, classify their cause and schedule their review, so the answer is to record, classify and revisit every mistake so it is never repeated.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Which of the following is the MOST essential column in an Error Log entry?
(a) The reason why the error occurred (b) The date of the mock test (c) The colour of the pen used (d) The name of the book the question came from
Answer: (a)
Solution: Date and source are useful metadata, but they do not drive improvement. The single most essential field is the diagnosed cause of the mistake, because classification enables correction, so the answer is the reason why the error occurred.

53. In an Error Log, a "Sound-Right trap" error means the mistake happened because:
(a) the question was factually too difficult (b) the option that sounded natural to the ear was grammatically wrong (c) the candidate ran out of time (d) the question was misprinted
Answer: (b)
Solution: Running out of time is a pacing error, not a Sound-Right trap. A Sound-Right trap exploits the ear of Hindi-speaking learners, where the wrong option feels natural, so the answer is the option that sounded natural to the ear was grammatically wrong.
