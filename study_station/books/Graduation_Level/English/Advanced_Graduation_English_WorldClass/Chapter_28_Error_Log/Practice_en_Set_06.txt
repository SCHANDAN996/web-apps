126. Spot the error: The number of students (P) / who have failed in the examination (Q) / are surprisingly high this year. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting trap is Part Q, which looks fine, but the real issue is the verb. "The number of" takes a singular verb, so "are" must be "is". Part Q is correct because "who" refers to "students" (plural). The error is in Part R, so the answer is Part R.

Answer: (a)
Solution: "It's" is the contraction of "it is", and the tag "isn't it" needs an apostrophe and a question mark. Only one option satisfies all three. So the answer is "It's a beautiful day, isn't it?"
