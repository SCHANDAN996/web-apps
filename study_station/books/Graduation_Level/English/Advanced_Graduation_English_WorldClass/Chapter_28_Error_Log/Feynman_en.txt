🧒 Feynman Technique — Explaining the Error Log Like You're 12

**The Big Idea in One Line:**
An error log is a notebook where you write down every mistake you make in practice — so you never make the same mistake twice.

**Imagine This:**
You're playing cricket with your friends. Every time you bat, you get out the same way — caught at slip while chasing a wide ball. Your friend says, "Bro, you ALWAYS get out like that!" But you don't believe him... until he shows you a small diary where he wrote: *"Match 1: out chasing wide ball. Match 2: out chasing wide ball. Match 3: same."* Now you can't argue. The diary caught your pattern. Next match, you leave the wide ball — and score fifty.

**Your error log is that diary.** Your brain is a trickster — it forgets mistakes and remembers only victories. The log doesn't forget. After 20 entries, you'll see something shocking: you don't make 100 different mistakes. You make the **same 5–7 mistakes again and again** — maybe subject-verb agreement with intervening phrases, maybe confusing "fewer/less", maybe misreading "NOT/EXCEPT" questions. Fix those 5–7 patterns, and your score jumps.

**The Unforgettable Trick — "MISTAKE = MAP":**
Every entry must answer 4 questions, or it's useless:
1. **W**hat did I mark? (my wrong answer)
2. **W**hat was correct? (the right answer + rule)
3. **W**hy did I fall? (the trap: sound-right? over-thinking? didn't read "NOT"?)
4. **W**hen will I revisit? (a date — 2 days later)

Just writing "Q wrong, answer is (b)" is like writing "I got out" without writing *how*. Useless. The **WHY** is the gold.

**The 2-Day Rule:**
A mistake you never look at again is a mistake you'll repeat in the exam hall. Re-read your log after 2 days, then after a week. Each re-read takes 5 minutes but saves 5 marks.

---

📄 **Blurting Sheet Instruction:**
Close this book. Take a blank page and blurt out — from memory — the 4 questions every error log entry must answer, and why the "WHY" matters more than the "WHAT". Write until your brain is empty. Then check: did you remember the 2-day revisit rule?

**Now answer this:** If your log shows that 8 out of your last 10 errors came from questions containing the words "NOT" or "EXCEPT", what single habit should you build before your next mock test?
