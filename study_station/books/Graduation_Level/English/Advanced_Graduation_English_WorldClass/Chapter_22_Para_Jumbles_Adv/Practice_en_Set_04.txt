76. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P: Habits shape most of what we do every day.
Q: A habit forms when an action is repeated in the same situation.
R: Over time, the brain does it almost automatically.
S: That is why changing an old habit needs conscious effort at first.
(a) PQRS (b) PQSR (c) SRQP (d) SQPR
Answer: (a)
Solution: P opens the paragraph because it introduces the topic without pointing back to anything; Q ("A habit …") follows because it continues the idea before it; R ("Over time …") follows because it continues the idea before it; S ("That is …") follows because it continues the idea before it. The trap is an order that puts a pointing sentence first. So the answer is PQRS.
