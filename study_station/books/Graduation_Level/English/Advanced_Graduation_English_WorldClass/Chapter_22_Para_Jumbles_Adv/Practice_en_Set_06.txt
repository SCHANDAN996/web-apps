126. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P: Economists distinguish between needs and wants.
Q: Wants, by contrast, are things that make life more comfortable.
R: Needs are things essential for survival, such as food and shelter.
S: A sound budget meets needs first and wants only with what is left.
(a) PSQR (b) PQRS (c) PRQS (d) QRSP
Answer: (c)
Solution: P opens the paragraph because it introduces the topic without pointing back to anything; R ("Needs are …") follows because it continues the idea before it; Q ("Wants, by …") follows because it continues the idea before it; S ("A sound …") follows because it continues the idea before it. The trap is an order that puts a pointing sentence first. So the answer is PRQS.
