1. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P: Riya planted a mango sapling in her backyard.
Q: She watered it every morning before school.
R: Those mangoes were the sweetest she had ever tasted.
S: After four years, the tree gave its first fruit.
(a) PQRS (b) PSQR (c) PQSR (d) PRQS
Answer: (c)
Solution: P opens the paragraph because it introduces the topic without pointing back to anything; Q ("She watered …") follows because it continues the idea before it; S ("After four …") follows because it continues the idea before it; R ("Those mangoes …") follows because it continues the idea before it. The trap is an order that puts a pointing sentence first. So the answer is PQSR.
Source: PYQ-style
