# Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Advanced Para Jumbles

**Box 1 — "OPCO: Open the Parcel, Check the Order"**
> Opener → Pairs → Check options → Order and read.

**Box 2 — "Who is leaning?"**
> Pronouns, "this/these", and connectors lean on an earlier sentence. The one leaning on no one opens.

**Box 3 — "A meets, The greets"**
> "A" meets something new; "the" greets it again later. A-sentence before the-sentence.

**Box 4 — "However is a reply, never a hello"**
> Contrast words answer an earlier point — never the first line.

**Box 5 — "Rain first, flood next"**
> Cause comes before effect: "As a result", "so", "consequently" follow the cause.

**Box 6 — "General → Example"**
> "For example" and "for instance" sit right after the big idea they explain.

**Box 7 — "These need a crowd"**
> "These" and "such" need a plural or a full idea before them.

**Box 8 — "Finally means finally"**
> Thus, hence, therefore, in short — look for them at the end.

**Box 9 — "Pair first, options next"**
> Find one sure pair and strike out options that break it. Two options left? Check one more link.

**Box 10 — "Fixed ends? Build a bridge"**
> With S1 and S6 given, link the end of S1 to one sentence and the start of S6 to another.

**Box 11 — "Odd one wears different clothes"**
> In odd-one-out questions, the odd sentence changes topic or tone.

**Box 12 — "Read it like a story"**
> Your final order should sound like natural speech when read aloud in your head.

## Skip Strategy

1. **15 seconds:** for "first" or "last sentence" questions, decide in 15 seconds using the opener/conclusion tests.
2. **45 seconds:** for full arrangements, if you cannot find even one sure pair in 45 seconds, mark and move on.
3. **Long passages:** if a jumble has six or more movable sentences and the options are close, attempt it only in the second round.
