101. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P: Social media lets anyone share opinions instantly.
Q: At the same time, it allows rumours to spread quickly.
R: Users must therefore check facts before sharing posts.
S: This has given a voice to people who were rarely heard before.
(a) QSPR (b) PSRQ (c) PQSR (d) PSQR
Answer: (d)
Solution: P opens the paragraph because it introduces the topic without pointing back to anything; S ("This has …") follows because it continues the idea before it; Q ("At the …") follows because it continues the idea before it; R ("Users must …") follows because it continues the idea before it. The trap is an order that puts a pointing sentence first. So the answer is PSQR.
Source: PYQ-style
