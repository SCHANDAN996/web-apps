26. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P: This shift has reduced the need to carry cash.
Q: The Unified Payments Interface allows instant transfers through a mobile phone.
R: Digital payments have grown rapidly in India.
S: Even small vendors now display a QR code at their shops.
(a) RPSQ (b) RQSP (c) SQRP (d) SQPR
Answer: (b)
Solution: R opens the paragraph because it introduces the topic without pointing back to anything; Q ("The Unified …") follows because it continues the idea before it; S ("Even small …") follows because it continues the idea before it; P ("This shift …") follows because it continues the idea before it. The trap is an order that puts a pointing sentence first. So the answer is RQSP.
