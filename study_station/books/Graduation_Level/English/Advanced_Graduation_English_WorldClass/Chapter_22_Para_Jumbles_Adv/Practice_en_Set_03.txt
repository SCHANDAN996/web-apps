51. Rearrange the following sentences to form a coherent paragraph.
P: Some cities have therefore prepared heat action plans.
Q: Under such plans, water points and shaded rest areas are set up in summer.
R: Climate change is making heatwaves more frequent in India.
S: These heatwaves are especially dangerous for outdoor workers.
(a) SQPR (b) RSPQ (c) RQPS (d) QSRP
Answer: (b)
Solution: R opens the paragraph because it introduces the topic without pointing back to anything; S ("These heatwaves …") follows because it continues the idea before it; P ("Some cities …") follows because it continues the idea before it; Q ("Under such …") follows because it continues the idea before it. The trap is an order that puts a pointing sentence first. So the answer is RSPQ.
Source: PYQ-style
