76. One of the students (P)/ who was selected (Q)/ for the scholarship (R)/ have not submitted the documents. (S)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part S
Answer: (d)
Solution: The subject is "One of the students", which is singular, so the verb must be "has not submitted". The relative clause "who was selected" correctly agrees with "students" (plural), which is the examiner's trap here.
