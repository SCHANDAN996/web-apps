Card 1
Front: "One of my friends ___ (is/are) coming." Which verb is correct?
Back: "is" — after "one of + plural noun", the verb agrees with "one", not the plural noun. Trap: the plural noun right before the verb is a decoy.

Card 2
Front: What is the "Intervening Phrase" trap?
Back: A phrase placed between subject and verb (e.g., "The quality of apples was good") to make you match the verb with the nearest noun instead of the real subject.

Card 3
Front: Mnemonic: Which words always take a singular verb? (hint: S-E-A-N)
Back: SEAN — Someone, Everyone, Anyone, Nobody (and each, either, neither). All take singular verbs: "Everyone is ready."

Card 4
Front: "Neither of the two boys ___ (has/have) done his homework."
Back: "has" — "neither of / either of + plural noun" takes a singular verb.

Card 5
Front: "The committee ___ (has/have) taken its decision." Singular or plural?
Back: "has" — a collective noun acting as one unit takes a singular verb. Use plural only when members act individually ("The committee have divided opinions").

Card 6
Front: Examiner's Trap: "He is one of the best players that ___ (has/have) ever played."
Back: "have" — in "one of the + plural noun + who/that", the relative pronoun refers to the plural noun, so the verb is plural. Classic trap!

Card 7
Front: "Scissors, trousers, spectacles" — singular or plural verb?
Back: Always plural: "The scissors are on the table." But "a pair of scissors is…" takes singular.

Card 8
Front: "Bread and butter ___ (is/are) my favourite breakfast."
Back: "is" — two nouns joined by "and" that form one idea/dish take a singular verb.

Card 9
Front: Mnemonic: Which verbs are followed by a bare infinitive (no "to")? (hint: M-B-L)
Back: MBL — Make, Bid, Let (+ see, hear, watch, help): "He made me go," not "made me to go."

Card 10
Front: "No sooner had he arrived ___ (when/than) it started raining."
Back: "than" — "no sooner" pairs with "than"; "hardly/scarcely" pair with "when". Examiners love swapping these.

Card 11
Front: Examiner's Trap: "Tell me what are you doing." — spot the error.
Back: Word order: in indirect (embedded) questions, use statement order — "Tell me what you are doing." Inversion ("what are you doing") is correct only in direct questions. Hindi-speakers often invert wrongly in indirect questions.

Card 12
Front: "Each of the students ___ (was/were) given a book."
Back: "was" — "each of + plural noun" takes a singular verb. Don't be fooled by "students".

Card 13
Front: "I, you and he" or "You, he and I"? Which order is correct for positive actions?
Back: "You, he and I" (2-3-1 order) for good/positive actions; "I, you and he" (1-2-3) when confessing something bad.

Card 14
Front: "The news ___ (is/are) shocking." Why?
Back: "is" — news, physics, mathematics, politics, measles look plural but are singular.

Card 15
Front: "He is senior ___ (than/to) me by two years."
Back: "to" — senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior, prefer take "to", never "than".

Card 16
Front: Mnemonic: Adjectives ending in -ior take which preposition? (hint: "2-ior-2")
Back: All -ior comparatives (senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior, ulterior) take "to" — remember "2-ior-2" (to-ior-to).

Card 17
Front: "Hardly had I reached the station ___ (than/when) the train left."
Back: "when" — hardly/scarcely…when; no sooner…than. Mixing them is a favourite trap.

Card 18
Front: "One should do ___ (his/one's) duty."
Back: "one's" — the pronoun for "one" is "one/one's", never "his".

Card 19
Front: "The Ganges is one of the holiest river in India." Spot the error.
Back: "river" → "rivers" — after "one of the + superlative", the noun must be plural: "one of the holiest rivers".

Card 20
Front: "He as well as his brothers ___ (is/are) going."
Back: "is" — with "as well as, along with, together with, in addition to", the verb agrees with the first subject only.
