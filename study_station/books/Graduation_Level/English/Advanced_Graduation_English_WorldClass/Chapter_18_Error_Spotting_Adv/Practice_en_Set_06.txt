126. Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. If there is no error, choose "No error".
P: Scarcely had the meeting begun / Q: when the chairman, along with his two deputies, / R: were called away to attend / S: an urgent phone call from the ministry.
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part P
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Along with his two deputies" is an intervening phrase; the true subject is "the chairman" (singular), so the verb must be "was called away", not "were called away". This is the classic Intervening Phrase trap.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. If there is no error, choose "No error".
P: The number of students / Q: opting for online courses / R: have increased sharply / S: over the last three years.
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part R
Answer: (d)
Solution: "The number of" takes a singular verb, so it should be "has increased". "A number of" would take a plural verb — examiners exploit this contrast.

150. Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error. If there is no error, choose "No error".

Answer: (a)
