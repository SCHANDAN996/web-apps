126. Arrange the following parts into a coherent sentence:
P. that the government announced
Q. the new policy
R. it was only after much debate
S. to curb inflation
(a) R Q P S (b) P Q R S (c) R P Q S (d) Q S R P
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice starts with P, but beginning with "that the government announced" leaves the sentence without a main clause. The sentence must open with the introductory clause R ("it was only after much debate"), followed by P, then the object Q, then the purpose phrase S. So the answer is R P Q S.

127. Arrange the parts into a meaningful sentence:
P. not only did the team win
Q. the championship trophy
R. but it also broke
S. the national record
(a) P R Q S (b) Q P R S (c) P Q R S (d) P Q S R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing R ("but it also broke") immediately after P, which separates "not only" from its first achievement. With the correlative pair "not only... but also", the first achievement (P Q) must be completed before "but it also" introduces the second (R S). So the answer is P Q R S.
