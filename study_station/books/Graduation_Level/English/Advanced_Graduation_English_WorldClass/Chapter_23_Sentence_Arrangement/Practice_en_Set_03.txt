51. Arrange the parts P, Q, R, S to form a meaningful sentence.
P: to succeed in life
Q: one must work hard
R: and remain honest
S: in all circumstances
(a) PQRS (b) SRPQ (c) QRPS (d) QPRS
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting order PQRS starts with the infinitive phrase "to succeed in life", which leaves the sentence without a subject. The sentence must begin with the subject clause "one must work hard", followed by the purpose, then "and remain honest" and "in all circumstances", so the answer is QPRS.
