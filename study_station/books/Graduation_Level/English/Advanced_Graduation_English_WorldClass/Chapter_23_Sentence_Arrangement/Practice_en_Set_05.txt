101. Arrange the parts P, Q, R, S to form a meaningful sentence:
P: to understand the causes of the war
Q: the historian spent years
R: that had devastated the entire continent
S: examining letters, diaries and official records
(a) SQPR (b) QSRP (c) PQRS (d) QSPR
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option QSRP places the relative clause "that had devastated the entire continent" before "the war", leaving the clause with nothing to describe. The subject "the historian" (Part Q) opens the sentence, Part S says what he did, Part P gives the purpose, and Part R describes the war, so the answer is QSPR.
