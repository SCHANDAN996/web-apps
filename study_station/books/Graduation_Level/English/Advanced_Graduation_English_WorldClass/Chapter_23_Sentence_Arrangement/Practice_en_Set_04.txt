76. Arrange the following parts to form a meaningful sentence.
P: the committee decided
Q: after a long discussion
R: to postpone the meeting
S: until further notice
(a) PRQS (b) QPRS (c) PQRS (d) QPSR
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting choice PQRS reads "the committee decided after a long discussion to postpone the meeting until further notice," which splits the time phrase awkwardly between the verb and its object. Placing the adverbial phrase "after a long discussion" first gives a clean, emphatic sentence, so the answer is QPRS.

77. Arrange the parts to form a coherent sentence.
P: that the earth revolves
Q: Galileo was punished
R: around the sun
S: for asserting
(a) QSRP (b) SQPR (c) QSPR (d) PSQR
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is QSRP, which reads "Galileo was punished for asserting around the sun that the earth revolves," tearing the clause away from its object. The clause "that the earth revolves around the sun" must stay together after "asserting," so the answer is QSPR.
