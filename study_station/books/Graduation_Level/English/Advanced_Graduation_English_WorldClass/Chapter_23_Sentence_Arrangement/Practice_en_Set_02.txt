26. Rearrange the parts to form a meaningful sentence.
P: is the key
Q: hard work
R: to success
S: in life
(a) PQRS (b) QRPS (c) QPRS (d) RQPS
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option PQRS starts with the verb phrase "is the key", which cannot open a sentence before its subject. The subject "hard work" (Q) must come first, followed by "is the key" (P), "to success" (R) and "in life" (S). So the answer is QPRS.
