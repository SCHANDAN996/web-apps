1. Arrange the parts to form a meaningful sentence.
P: to school
Q: the children
R: walk
S: together every day
(a) RQPS (b) QPRS (c) QRPS (d) PQRS
Answer: (c)
Solution: "The children" (Part Q) is the subject, "walk" (Part R) the verb, "to school" (Part P) the destination, and S the time phrase at the end. The tempting option QPRS puts the destination before the verb, giving "the children to school walk", which is ungrammatical. So the answer is QRPS.
Source: PYQ-style

(a) QPSR (b) PRQS (c) QPRS (d) RQPS
Answer: (c)
