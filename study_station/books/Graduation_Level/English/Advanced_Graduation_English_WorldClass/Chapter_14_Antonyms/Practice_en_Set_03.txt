51. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: ABUNDANT
(a) Plentiful (b) Ample (c) Scarce (d) Copious
Answer: (c)
Solution: Abundant means existing in large quantities; its opposite is scarce (in short supply). Plentiful, ample and copious are synonyms, not antonyms.
