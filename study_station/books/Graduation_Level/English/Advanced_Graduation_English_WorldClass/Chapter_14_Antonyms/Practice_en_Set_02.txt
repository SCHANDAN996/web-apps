Antonyms — Practice Set 2 (Questions 26–50)
Difficulty: Questions 26–40 Easy | Questions 41–50 Medium

26. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: ANCIENT
(a) Modern (b) Old (c) Antique (d) Aged
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ancient means belonging to the distant past; its opposite is Modern. Old, Antique and Aged are near-synonyms, not opposites.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: EXPAND
(a) Enlarge (b) Contract (c) Extend (d) Spread
Answer: (b)
Solution: Expand means to become larger; Contract means to shrink or become smaller. Enlarge, Extend and Spread are synonyms of expand.
Source: PYQ-style
