Card 1
Front: What is an antonym?
Back: A word that has the opposite meaning of another word. Example: "generous" ↔ "stingy".

Card 2
Front: Antonym of "benevolent"?
Back: Malevolent (unkind, wishing harm). Root: bene = good, male = bad.

Card 3
Front: Antonym of "obsolete"?
Back: Modern / current / up-to-date. "Obsolete" means outdated or no longer in use.

Card 4
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: How to remember "lucid" (clear) vs its antonym?
Back: LUCID = "Light U Can See In Darkness" — clear, bright. Antonym: obscure / vague (dark, unclear).

Card 5
Front: Antonym of "frugal"?
Back: Extravagant / wasteful. "Frugal" means economical, careful with money.

Card 6
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: What is the antonym of "flammable"?
Back: Non-flammable. Trap: "inflammable" also means flammable (NOT its opposite!) — the prefix "in-" here means "into", not "not".

Card 7
Front: Antonym of "augment"?
Back: Diminish / reduce. "Augment" means to increase.

Card 8
Front: Antonym of "candid"?
Back: Evasive / secretive / guarded. "Candid" means frank and open.

Card 9
Front: 🧠 Mnemonic: Remember "ephemeral" and its antonym.
Back: E-PHEMeral = "Exists For A Moment" — short-lived. Antonym: permanent / eternal / enduring.

Card 10
Front: Antonym of "ambiguous"?
Back: Clear / unambiguous / explicit. "Ambiguous" means having more than one meaning.

Card 11
Front: ⚠️ Examiner's Trap: Antonym of "genuine" — is it "artificial" or "spurious"?
Back: Both can work, but in exams pick the closest formal opposite: "spurious" (fake, false). Trap: options often include a synonym like "authentic" to confuse you — read the question twice: ANTONYM, not synonym.

Card 12
Front: Antonym of "verbose"?
Back: Concise / brief / laconic. "Verbose" means using too many words.

Card 13
Front: Antonym of "humble"?
Back: Arrogant / haughty / proud.

Card 14
Front: Antonym of "transparent"?
Back: Opaque. (Also figuratively: secretive vs open.)

Card 15
Front: Antonym of "optimistic"?
Back: Pessimistic. Root: optimus = best (Latin), pessimus = worst.

Card 16
Front: Antonym of "scarce"?
Back: Abundant / plentiful.

Card 17
Front: Antonym of "rigid"?
Back: Flexible / pliable.

Card 18
Front: Antonym of "courage" (abstract noun)?
Back: Cowardice. Trap: exams test noun↔noun pairs — don't answer "cowardly" (adjective) if the question word is a noun.

Card 19
Front: Antonym of "expand"?
Back: Contract / shrink / compress.

Card 20
Front: Quick strategy: What should you check first before choosing an antonym?
Back: (1) Part of speech must match, (2) eliminate synonyms first, (3) beware of sound-alike words and tricky prefixes like "in-" (inflammable) and "im-" (impossible = true opposite).
