1. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: ABUNDANT
(a) Scarce (b) Plentiful (c) Ample (d) Copious
Answer: (a)
Solution: Abundant means existing in large quantity; its opposite is scarce, meaning in short supply. Plentiful, ample and copious are synonyms, not antonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: TRANSPARENT
(a) Clear (b) Obvious (c) Fragile (d) Opaque
Answer: (d)
Solution: Transparent means allowing light to pass through so objects can be seen; opaque blocks light completely. Clear and obvious are near-synonyms, and fragile relates to breakability, not visibility.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: HUMBLE
(a) Modest (b) Shy (c) Meek (d) Arrogant
Answer: (d)
Solution: Humble means having a modest view of one's importance; arrogant means having an exaggerated sense of one's own importance. Modest and meek are synonyms of humble.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: EXPAND
(a) Enlarge (b) Extend (c) Contract (d) Spread
Answer: (c)
Solution: Expand means to become larger; contract means to become smaller or shrink. Enlarge, extend and spread all carry the same sense as expand.

5. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: ANCIENT

Answer: (c)
Solution: Ancient means belonging to the very distant past; modern means relating to the present or recent times. Old, antique and aged are synonyms.

6. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: GENUINE
(a) Authentic (b) Spurious (c) Real (d) Sincere
Answer: (b)

21. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: OBSOLETE
(a) Outdated (b) Contemporary (c) Archaic (d) Ancient
Answer: (b)
Solution: Obsolete means no longer in use because something newer exists; contemporary means current and up to date. Outdated, archaic and ancient are synonyms — this is a classic examiner trap where three options look alike and the odd one out is the answer.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: FRUGAL

Answer: (b)
Solution: Frugal means careful with money and avoiding waste; extravagant means spending excessively. Thrifty and economical are synonyms, and careful is too general to be the antonym.

24. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: RELUCTANT

Answer: (b)
Solution: Reluctant means unwilling to do something; eager means keen and enthusiastic. Unwilling and hesitant are synonyms, and cautious is a trap option — being careful is not the opposite of being unwilling.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: MITIGATE
(a) Aggravate (b) Alleviate (c) Reduce (d) Lessen
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mitigate means to make something less severe; aggravate means to make it worse. Reduce, alleviate and lessen are synonyms of mitigate — a sound-right trap, since aggravate is often misused to mean irritate.
Source: PYQ-style
