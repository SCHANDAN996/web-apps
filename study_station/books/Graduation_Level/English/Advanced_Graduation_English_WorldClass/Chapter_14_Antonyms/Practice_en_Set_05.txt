101. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: OPAQUE
(a) cloudy (b) dull (c) rigid (d) transparent
Answer: (d)
Solution: Opaque means not allowing light through; transparent is its direct opposite. Cloudy and dull are near-synonyms, not antonyms.
