Antonyms — Practice Set 6 (Questions 126–150)
Difficulty: 10 Medium, 15 Hard | Examiner-trap questions: 131, 144

126. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: CANDID
(a) frank (b) evasive (c) honest (d) blunt
Answer: (b)
Solution: Candid means truthful and straightforward. Evasive means avoiding direct answers, which is the opposite. Frank, honest and blunt are near-synonyms, not antonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: OBSOLETE
(a) outdated (b) archaic (c) current (d) ancient
Answer: (c)
Solution: Obsolete means no longer in use. Current means in present use, the direct opposite. Outdated and archaic are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: FRUGAL
(a) thrifty (b) prudent (c) economical (d) extravagant
Answer: (d)
Solution: Frugal means careful with money. Extravagant means spending excessively, the opposite. Thrifty, economical and prudent are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

129. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: TRANSPARENT
(a) clear (b) lucid (c) obvious (d) opaque
Answer: (d)
Solution: Transparent means allowing light through or easily understood. Opaque means not allowing light through or unclear, the opposite. Clear, obvious and lucid are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

130. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: HUMBLE
(a) modest (b) meek (c) submissive (d) arrogant
Answer: (d)
Solution: Humble means having a modest view of oneself. Arrogant means having an exaggerated sense of self-importance, the opposite. Modest and meek are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

131. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: MITIGATE
(a) aggravate (b) alleviate (c) reduce (d) soothe
Answer: (a)
Solution: Examiner's trap — mitigate and aggravate look like a pair but candidates often confuse aggravate with irritate. Mitigate means to lessen severity; aggravate means to make worse, the true opposite. Alleviate, reduce and soothe are synonyms of mitigate.
Source: PYQ-style

132. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: BENEVOLENT
(a) kind (b) generous (c) charitable (d) malevolent
Answer: (d)
Solution: Benevolent means kindly and well-meaning. Malevolent means wishing harm to others, the exact opposite (bene = good, male = evil). Kind, generous and charitable are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

133. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: EPHEMERAL
(a) permanent (b) fleeting (c) transient (d) momentary
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ephemeral means lasting a very short time. Permanent means lasting forever, the opposite. Fleeting, transient and momentary are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

134. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: OPAQUE (in the sense of writing style)
(a) obscure (b) lucid (c) murky (d) vague
Answer: (b)
Solution: Opaque writing is hard to understand. Lucid means clear and easy to understand, the opposite. Obscure, murky and vague are synonyms in this context.
Source: PYQ-style

135. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: SPURIOUS
(a) fake (b) counterfeit (c) false (d) genuine
Answer: (d)
Solution: Spurious means false or fake. Genuine means authentic, the opposite. Fake, false and counterfeit are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

136. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: LACONIC
(a) brief (b) verbose (c) terse (d) concise
Answer: (b)
Solution: Laconic means using very few words. Verbose means using too many words, the opposite. Brief, terse and concise are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

137. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: PROSCRIBE
(a) forbid (b) ban (c) permit (d) prohibit
Answer: (c)
Solution: Proscribe means to forbid or ban. Permit means to allow, the opposite. Forbid, ban and prohibit are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

138. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: OBDURATE
(a) yielding (b) inflexible (c) stubborn (d) adamant
Answer: (a)
Solution: Obdurate means stubbornly refusing to change. Yielding means giving way, the opposite. Stubborn, inflexible and adamant are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

139. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: ENERVATE
(a) weaken (b) invigorate (c) exhaust (d) drain
Answer: (b)
Solution: Enervate means to drain of energy. Invigorate means to fill with energy, the opposite. Weaken, exhaust and drain are synonyms. Note: enervate does not mean energise — a classic trap pair.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: PARSIMONIOUS
(a) lavish (b) miserly (c) stingy (d) frugal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Parsimonious means extremely unwilling to spend. Lavish means giving or spending in large amounts, the opposite. Stingy, miserly and frugal are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

141. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: SANGUINE
(a) pessimistic (b) hopeful (c) optimistic (d) cheerful
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sanguine means optimistic, especially in a difficult situation. Pessimistic means expecting the worst, the opposite. Optimistic, hopeful and cheerful are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

142. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: TACITURN

143. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: AMELIORATE
(a) worsen (b) improve (c) enhance (d) better
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ameliorate means to make something bad better. Worsen is the direct opposite. Improve, enhance and better are synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

144. Choose the word most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to: FLAMMABLE
(a) inflammable (b) ignitable (c) combustible (d) fire-resistant
Answer: (d)
Solution: Examiner's trap — inflammable is a synonym of flammable, not its opposite (the prefix in- here means "into", not "not"). The true opposite is fire-resistant. Combustible and ignitable are also synonyms.
Source: PYQ-style

Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (c)
Solution: Lugubrious means looking or sounding sad and mournful. Joyful means full of happiness, the opposite. Mournful, doleful and melancholy are synonyms.
