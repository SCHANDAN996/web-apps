76. सही passive form चुनें: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."
(a) The documents will signed by the manager tomorrow. (b) The documents would be signed by the manager tomorrow. (c) The documents will be sign by the manager tomorrow. (d) The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Future simple passive = will + be + V3। "Sign" का "signed" बनता है, इसलिए "will be signed" सही है। पहले विकल्प में "be" गायब है और तीसरे में "be" के बाद base form आई है — दोनों गलत।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

77. सही passive form चुनें: "Someone has stolen my wallet."
(a) My wallet has stolen by someone. (b) My wallet is been stolen. (c) My wallet has been stolen. (d) My wallet had been steal.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Present perfect passive = has/have + been + V3। जब कर्ता ("someone") अज्ञात या महत्वहीन हो, तो उसे हटा दिया जाता है। "Is been" कभी भी मान्य संरचना नहीं है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

78. सही active form चुनें: "The novel was written by her in just three months."
(a) She was written the novel in just three months. (b) She has wrote the novel in just three months. (c) She wrote the novel in just three months. (d) She had wrote the novel in just three months.

हल: Passive "was written" past simple है, इसलिए active भी past simple "wrote" होगा। अंतिम दो विकल्पों में "has/had" के साथ "written" के बजाय गलती से "wrote" लगाया गया है।

79. सही passive form चुनें: "They are repairing the bridge."
(a) The bridge is being repaired by them. (b) The bridge has being repaired by them. (c) The bridge is being repair by them. (d) The bridge is repaired by them.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Present continuous passive = is/am/are + being + V3। पहला विकल्प tense को simple present में बदल देता है, और "has being" कोई मान्य संरचना नहीं है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

80. सही passive form चुनें: "You must obey the rules."

84. सही active form चुनें: "By whom was this letter written?"

100. सही passive form चुनें: "They told us that the flight had been delayed."
(a) We were been told that the flight had been delayed. (b) We are told that the flight had been delayed. (c) We were told that the flight had been delayed. (d) We had told that the flight had been delayed.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Told" past simple है, इसलिए पहला खंड "We were told" बनता है; reported clause "had been delayed" (पहले से past perfect passive) अपरिवर्तित रहता है। तीसरे विकल्प में auxiliary दोगुनी हो गई है — "were been" असंभव है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style
