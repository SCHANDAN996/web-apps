51. Choose the correct passive form: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter was written by her. (b) A letter is being written by her. (c) A letter is written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
Answer: (c)
Solution: The active sentence is in simple present tense, so the passive uses "is/are + V3". "A letter" is singular, hence "is written". Options with "was", "being" and "has been" change the tense.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

62. Choose the correct passive form: "She gave me a book."
(a) A book had given to me by her. (b) A book is given to me by her. (c) Me was given a book by her. (d) A book was given to me by her.
Answer: (d)
Solution: With two objects, either can become the subject; here the thing "a book" becomes subject with "to me". Simple past gives "was given". "Me" can never be a subject.
