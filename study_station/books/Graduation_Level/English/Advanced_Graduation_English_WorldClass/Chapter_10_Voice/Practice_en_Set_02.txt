26. Choose the correct passive form: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter has been written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter is written by her.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The active sentence is in simple present tense, so the passive uses "is/am/are + V3". "Writes" becomes "is written". The option with "was" wrongly shifts to past tense.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

Answer: (a)
Solution: Simple past "praised" becomes "was/were + V3" in passive. "The boy" is singular, so "was praised" is correct.
