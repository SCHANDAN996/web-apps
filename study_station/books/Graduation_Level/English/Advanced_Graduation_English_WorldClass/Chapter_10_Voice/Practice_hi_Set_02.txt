26. सही passive form चुनें: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter has been written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is being written by her. (d) A letter is written by her.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Active वाक्य simple present tense में है, इसलिए passive में "is/am/are + V3" प्रयोग होता है। "Writes" बन जाता है "is written"। "was" वाला विकल्प गलती से past tense में चला जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English Grammar

27. सही passive form चुनें: "The teacher praised the boy."
(a) The boy was praised by the teacher. (b) The boy is praised by the teacher. (c) The boy has been praised by the teacher. (d) The boy was being praised by the teacher.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Simple past "praised" passive में "was/were + V3" बनता है। "The boy" एकवचन है, इसलिए "was praised" सही है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

31. सही passive form चुनें: "Open the door."
(a) The door is opened. (b) Let the door be opened. (c) The door should open. (d) The door was opened.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Imperative (आदेशसूचक) वाक्यों का passive "Let + object + be + V3" से बनता है। अतः "Let the door be opened."
स्रोत: PYQ-style

32. सही passive form चुनें: "People speak English all over the world."
(a) English was spoken all over the world. (b) English is spoken all over the world. (c) English is being spoken all over the world. (d) English has spoken all over the world.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Simple present "speak" बनता है "is spoken"। जब कर्ता अस्पष्ट हो (people, someone, they), तो passive में "by" वाला भाग हटा दिया जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English Grammar

33. सही passive form चुनें: "The police arrested the thief."
(a) The thief was arrested by the police. (b) The thief is arrested by the police. (c) The thief has arrested by the police. (d) The thief was being arrested by the police.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Simple past "arrested" बनता है "was/were + V3"। "has arrested" वाला विकल्प व्याकरण की दृष्टि से टूटा हुआ है, क्योंकि passive में "has" के बाद V3 से पहले "been" चाहिए।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

34. सही active form चुनें: "A song is being sung by her."
(a) She is singing a song. (b) She sings a song. (c) She was singing a song. (d) She has sung a song.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Is being sung" present continuous passive है, इसलिए active "is singing" होगा। Tense बिल्कुल मेल खाना चाहिए।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

35. सही passive form चुनें: "Who taught you English?"
(a) By whom were you taught English? (b) By whom was you taught English? (c) Who was taught you English? (d) By whom you were taught English?
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Subject के रूप में "Who" passive में "By whom" बनता है, और प्रश्नवाचक संरचना में inversion चाहिए: "were you taught"। बिना inversion वाला विकल्प प्रश्न नहीं, कथन है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

36. सही passive form चुनें: "She will finish the project."

उत्तर: (a)

43. सही passive form चुनें: "You must obey the rules."
(a) The rules must obeyed. (b) The rules must being obeyed. (c) The rules must be obeyed. (d) The rules must been obeyed.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Modal verbs का passive "modal + be + V3" बनता है। "Must obeyed" और "must been" व्याकरण की दृष्टि से टूटी संरचनाएँ हैं — परीक्षक का क्लासिक जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

44. सही passive form चुनें: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."

स्रोत: PYQ-style

45. सही passive form चुनें: "Why did he beat the boy?"
(a) Why the boy was beaten by him? (b) Why is the boy beaten by him? (c) Why was the boy beat by him? (d) Why was the boy beaten by him?
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Did + V1" simple past है, इसलिए passive "was/were + V3" होगा और प्रश्न में inversion चाहिए। "Beat" यहाँ गलत है क्योंकि past participle "beaten" है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

46. सही passive form चुनें: "One should keep one's promises."
(a) Promises should kept. (b) Promises should be kept. (c) Promises should been kept. (d) Promises should be keep.
उत्तर: (b)

स्रोत: PYQ-style

49. सही passive form चुनें: "I know him."
(a) He was known to me. (b) He is known by me. (c) He is known with me. (d) He is known to me.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Know, surprise, annoy, contain जैसी क्रियाएँ passive में "by" के अलावा दूसरे preposition लेती हैं — "known to", न कि "known by"। यह परीक्षक का पसंदीदा जाल है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

50. सही passive form चुनें: "The loud noise frightened the child."

स्रोत: PYQ-style
