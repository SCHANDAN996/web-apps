प्रैक्टिस सेट 5 — सकर्मक-अकर्मक प्रयोग (Active-Passive Voice) (प्रश्न 101–125) | कठिनाई: 15 मध्यम, 10 कठिन

101. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."
(a) The documents will signed by the manager tomorrow. (b) The documents will be sign by the manager tomorrow. (c) The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow. (d) The documents will being signed by the manager tomorrow.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Future simple passive = will + be + V3 (signed)। "Will signed" और "will be sign" be + past participle की संरचना को तोड़ देते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

102. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "Someone has stolen my wallet."
(a) My wallet has been stolen. (b) My wallet is been stolen. (c) My wallet has stolen by someone. (d) My wallet had been steal.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Present perfect passive = has/have + been + V3। जब कर्ता अज्ञात या महत्वहीन हो ("someone"), तो agent को हटा दिया जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

103. सही सकर्मक (active) रूप चुनें: "The novel was written by her in just six months."
(a) She was written the novel in just six months. (b) She wrote the novel in just six months. (c) She has written the novel by six months. (d) She had wrote the novel in just six months.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Passive "was written" = past simple, इसलिए active "wrote" होगा। Agent "her" subject बनकर "she" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

104. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "They are repairing the bridge."
(a) The bridge is repaired by them. (b) The bridge has being repaired. (c) The bridge is been repaired. (d) The bridge is being repaired.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present continuous passive = is/am/are + being + V3। "Is been" एक क्लासिक गलत रूप है जिसे परीक्षक रखते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

105. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "You must obey the rules."
(a) The rules must obeyed by you. (b) The rules must being obeyed. (c) The rules must been obeyed. (d) The rules must be obeyed.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Modal passive = modal + be + V3। "Must obeyed" और "must been" दोनों इस निश्चित पैटर्न का उल्लंघन करते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

106. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "The teacher gave the students a difficult test."
(a) The students were gave a difficult test by the teacher. (b) A difficult test was given to the students by the teacher. (c) A difficult test is given the students by the teacher. (d) The students have been given a difficult test by the teacher.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: दो objects होने पर कोई भी subject बन सकता है, लेकिन tense past simple ही रहेगा: "was given"। "were gave" वाला विकल्प auxiliary को base form के साथ गलत तरीके से मिलाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

107. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "People speak English all over the world."
(a) English is spoken all over the world. (b) English is speak all over the world. (c) English spoken all over the world. (d) English has spoken all over the world.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Present simple passive = is + V3 (spoken)। "People" जैसे अस्पष्ट agents को passive में हटा दिया जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

108. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "One should keep one's promises."
(a) One's promises should be keep. (b) One's promises should be kept. (c) Promises should been kept by one. (d) One's promises should kept.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जाल: सर्वनाम "one" छात्रों को उसे agent के रूप में बनाए रखने के लिए लुभाता है। Modal passive = should + be + kept (keep का V3); "should be keep" में base form है, जो गलत है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

109. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "The committee was discussing the proposal at that time."
(a) The proposal was being discussed by the committee at that time. (b) The proposal was discussed being by the committee at that time. (c) The proposal had being discussed by the committee at that time. (d) The proposal is being discussed by the committee at that time.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Past continuous passive = was/were + being + V3। समय-सूचक "at that time" भूतकाल की पुष्टि करता है, जिससे present रूप निकल जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

110. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "They will have completed the project by March."
(a) The project would have been complete by March. (b) The project will have been completed by March. (c) The project will have completed by March. (d) The project will been completed by March.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Future perfect passive = will + have + been + V3। "Been" गिरा देना यहाँ सबसे आम गलती है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

111. सही सकर्मक (active) रूप चुनें: "The thief was caught by the police last night."
(a) The police were caught the thief last night. (b) The police caught the thief last night. (c) The police has caught the thief last night. (d) The police had been caught the thief last night.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Was caught" = past simple passive, इसलिए active = "caught"। "Last night" present perfect ("has caught") की अनुमति नहीं देता।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

112. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "Did she invite you to the party?"
(a) Were you invited to the party by her? (b) Was you invited to the party by her? (c) Did you invited to the party by her? (d) Were you invite to the party by her?
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Past simple interrogative passive = Was/Were + subject + V3। "You" के साथ "were" आता है और participle "invited" है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

113. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "The noise frightened the child."
(a) The child was frighten by the noise. (b) The child is frightened by the noise. (c) The child had frightened by the noise. (d) The child was frightened by the noise.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Past simple passive = was + V3 (frightened)। "Frighten" base form है, participle नहीं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

114. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "They made him captain of the team."
(a) He is made captain of the team. (b) He was made the captain by them of the team. (c) He was made captain of the team. (d) He was being make captain of the team.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: नियुक्ति वाली क्रियाएँ (make, elect, appoint) दोनों voices में complement से पहले article हटा देती हैं: "made him captain" बनता है "was made captain"।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

115. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "I know him."
(a) He is known with me. (b) He is known by me. (c) He was known to me. (d) He is known to me.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: जाल वाली क्रिया: "know" passive में "by" नहीं, "to" preposition लेती है। Tense present simple ही रहता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

116. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "His behaviour annoyed me."
(a) I am annoyed with his behaviour. (b) I was annoyed by his behaviour to me. (c) I was annoyed with his behaviour. (d) I had been annoyed with his behaviour.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Annoyed" passive में (चीज़ों के लिए) "with" लेता है। Past simple बना रहता है: "was annoyed"।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

117. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "Open the window."
(a) Let the window opened. (b) The window is open. (c) Let the window be opened. (d) Let the window being opened.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Imperative passive = Let + object + be + V3। "Let the window opened" में ज़रूरी "be" गायब है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

118. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "Who taught you French?"
(a) Whom were you taught French by him? (b) By who was you taught French? (c) By whom were you taught French? (d) By whom you were taught French?
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Who" passive में "By whom" बन जाता है; फिर inversion आता है: "were you taught"। "was you" वाले विकल्प में subject-verb agreement की गलती है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

119. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "The doctor is examining the patient."
(a) The patient is examined by the doctor being. (b) The patient is being examined by the doctor. (c) The patient has been examined by the doctor. (d) The patient is been examined by the doctor.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जाल: "is been examined" दिखने में सही लगता है पर व्याकरणिक रूप से असंभव है — continuous passive को "being" चाहिए, perfect passive को "been", और मूल वाक्य continuous है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

120. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "She likes people admiring her."
(a) She likes being admired. (b) She likes to being admired. (c) She likes been admired. (d) She is liked being admired.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Like" के बाद gerund passive = being + V3 होता है। यहाँ "likes" के बाद "been" नहीं आ सकता।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

121. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "I remember my father taking me to the fair."
(a) I remember been taken to the fair by my father. (b) I remember to be taken to the fair by my father. (c) I am remembered being taken to the fair. (d) I remember being taken to the fair by my father.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Remember" के बाद gerund passive = being + V3। अर्थ बीती हुई याद का है, इसलिए "being taken" ठीक है; "to be taken" भविष्य की व्यवस्था का भाव देगा।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

122. सही रूपांतरित वाक्य चुनें: "It is said that he is a spy."
(a) He is said to be a spy. (b) He is said being a spy. (c) He was said to be a spy. (d) He is say to be a spy.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "It is said that…" बदलकर subject + is said + to-infinitive हो जाता है। Tense present ही रहता है और infinitive "to be" है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

123. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "The fire has destroyed many houses."
(a) Many houses had destroyed by the fire. (b) Many houses has been destroyed by the fire. (c) Many houses have been destroyed by the fire. (d) Many houses have being destroyed by the fire.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Present perfect passive = have/has + been + V3। बहुवचन subject "houses" के साथ "have" आता है; "has been" agreement में असफल है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

124. सही अकर्मक (passive) रूप चुनें: "Don't insult the poor."
(a) Let the poor not be insulted. (b) The poor are not insulted by anyone. (c) Let the poor not insulted. (d) The poor should not insulted.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Negative imperative passive = Let + object + not + be + V3: "Let the poor not be insulted." अन्य विकल्प या तो "be" गिरा देते हैं या वाक्य को आदेश के बजाय साधारण कथन बना देते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

125. सही सकर्मक (active) रूप चुनें: "We were taught grammar by a strict teacher."
(a) A strict teacher had been teaching us grammar. (b) A strict teacher was taught us grammar. (c) A strict teacher has taught us grammar. (d) A strict teacher taught us grammar.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Were taught" = past simple passive, इसलिए active past simple "taught" होगा। अन्य विकल्प tense बदल देते हैं या passive auxiliary का गलत प्रयोग करते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style
