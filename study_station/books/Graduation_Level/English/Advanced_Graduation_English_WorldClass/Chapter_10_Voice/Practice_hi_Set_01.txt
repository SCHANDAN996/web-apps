सक्रिय-कर्मणि वाच्य (Active-Passive Voice) — अभ्यास सेट 1 (प्रश्न 1–25)
कठिनाई: प्रश्न 1–20 आसान | प्रश्न 21–25 मध्यम

1. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter is being written by her. (b) A letter was written by her. (c) A letter is written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: सक्रिय वाक्य simple present tense में है, इसलिए passive में "is/are + V3" प्रयोग होता है। "Writes" बन जाता है "is written"। अन्य विकल्पों के past रूप tense बदल देते हैं, जो उचित नहीं है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

2. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "He killed the snake."
(a) The snake is killed by him. (b) The snake has been killed by him. (c) The snake was killed by him. (d) The snake had been killed by him.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Simple past "killed" passive में "was/were + V3" बनता है। "The snake" एकवचन है, इसलिए "was killed" सही है। अन्य विकल्प tense बदल देते हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

3. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "They are playing football."
(a) Football is played by them. (b) Football was being played by them. (c) Football has been played by them. (d) Football is being played by them.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present continuous "are playing" passive में "is/are being + V3" बनता है। "Football" एकवचन है, इसलिए "is being played" सही है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

4. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "The teacher has praised the boy."
(a) The boy has praised by the teacher. (b) The boy was praised by the teacher. (c) The boy had been praised by the teacher. (d) The boy has been praised by the teacher.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present perfect "has praised" passive में "has/have been + V3" बनता है। विकल्प (a) में "been" गायब है — यह परीक्षक का प्रचलित जाल है; perfect passive में "been" हमेशा आवश्यक है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

5. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "Someone stole my watch."
(a) My watch is stolen. (b) My watch was stolen. (c) My watch has stolen. (d) My watch had stolen.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Simple past "stole" passive में "was + V3" बनता है। जब कर्ता अस्पष्ट हो (someone), तो "by" वाला भाग हटा दिया जाता है। विकल्प (c) और (d) passive नहीं, active रूप हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

6. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "Open the door."
(a) The door should opened. (b) The door is opened. (c) The door was opened. (d) Let the door be opened.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: आज्ञार्थक (imperative) वाक्य passive में "Let + object + be + V3" से बनते हैं। विकल्प (a) व्याकरण की दृष्टि से टूटा हुआ है (should + opened गलत है)।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

7. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "She will finish the work."
(a) The work will finished by her. (b) The work will being finished by her. (c) The work will be finished by her. (d) The work would be finished by her.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Simple future "will finish" passive में "will be + V3" बनता है। "will" के बाद मूल क्रिया "be" चाहिए, इसलिए विकल्प (a) और (b) गलत हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

8. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "The mother loves her child."
(a) Her child is being loved by the mother. (b) Her child was loved by the mother. (c) Her child is loved by the mother. (d) Her child has been loved by the mother.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Simple present "loves" passive में "is + V3" बनता है। Love, like, hate जैसी भाववाचक क्रियाएँ continuous passive में प्रयोग नहीं होतीं, इसलिए विकल्प (a) गलत है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

9. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "He was writing a letter."
(a) A letter was being written by him. (b) A letter was written by him. (c) A letter is being written by him. (d) A letter had been written by him.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Past continuous "was writing" passive में "was/were being + V3" बनता है। Tense past ही रहना चाहिए, इसलिए विकल्प (c) गलत है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

10. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "People speak English all over the world."
(a) English is speaking all over the world. (b) English was spoken all over the world. (c) English is spoken all over the world. (d) English has spoken all over the world.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Simple present "speak" passive में "is + V3" (spoken) बनता है। "People" जैसे सामान्य कर्ता के साथ "by" वाला भाग हटा दिया जाता है। विकल्प (a) और (d) passive संरचना ही नहीं हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

11. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "The police arrested the thief."
(a) The thief is arrested by the police. (b) The thief was arrested by the police. (c) The thief has arrested by the police. (d) The thief was being arrested by the police.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Simple past "arrested" passive में "was + V3" बनता है। विकल्प (c) में "been" गायब है और विकल्प (d) simple past को गलती से past continuous में बदल देता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

12. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "She can solve this problem."
(a) This problem can solved by her. (b) This problem could solved by her. (c) This problem can being solved by her. (d) This problem can be solved by her.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Modal + क्रिया passive में "modal + be + V3" बनता है। "can" के बाद मूल रूप "be" आवश्यक है, इसलिए विकल्प (a), (c) और (b) गलत हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

13. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "They had completed the project before the deadline."
(a) The project had completed before the deadline. (b) The project has been completed before the deadline. (c) The project was completed before the deadline. (d) The project had been completed before the deadline.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Past perfect "had completed" passive में "had been + V3" बनता है। विकल्प (a) active है, और विकल्प (b) तथा (c) tense बदल देते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

14. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "Do not insult the poor."
(a) The poor are not insulted. (b) Let the poor not be insulted. (c) The poor should not insulted. (d) Let not the poor insulted.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: नकारात्मक आज्ञार्थक वाक्य passive में "Let + object + not + be + V3" बनते हैं। विकल्प (d) में "be" गायब है और विकल्प (c) व्याकरण की दृष्टि से टूटा हुआ है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

15. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "The chef is cooking the food."
(a) The food is being cooked by the chef. (b) The food is cooked by the chef. (c) The food was being cooked by the chef. (d) The food has been cooked by the chef.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Present continuous "is cooking" passive में "is being + V3" बनता है। Tense present continuous ही रहना चाहिए, इसलिए विकल्प (c) गलत है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

16. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "Who taught you English?"
(a) By whom you were taught English? (b) By whom was you taught English? (c) Who was taught you English? (d) By whom were you taught English?
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Passive प्रश्नों में "Who" बन जाता है "By whom", और सहायक क्रिया subject से पहले आती है: "were you taught"। विकल्प (b) में agreement गलत है (was-you), और विकल्प (a) में inversion नहीं है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

17. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "She gave me a book."
(a) I was given a book by her. (b) I am given a book by her. (c) A book is given to me by her. (d) I had been given a book by her.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: दो objects वाले वाक्य में कोई भी subject बन सकता है; indirect object "me" बनता है "I was given"। Simple past "gave" के लिए "was + V3" चाहिए, इसलिए विकल्प (b) गलत है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

18. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "We should respect our elders."
(a) Our elders should respected by us. (b) Our elders should be respected by us. (c) Our elders should being respected by us. (d) Our elders should been respected by us.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Modal passive = modal + be + V3। केवल विकल्प (b) में सही "should be respected" है; बाकी में modal के बाद क्रिया का रूप गलत है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

19. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "The storm damaged the crops."
(a) The crops were damaged by the storm. (b) The crops are damaged by the storm. (c) The crops have damaged by the storm. (d) The crops had damaged by the storm.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Simple past "damaged" passive में "were + V3" बनता है (crops बहुवचन है)। विकल्प (c) और (d) passive नहीं, active संरचनाएँ हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

20. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "Please help me."
(a) I am helped by you. (b) You are requested to help me. (c) Let me helped. (d) I was helped please.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Please" से शुरू होने वाले आज्ञार्थक वाक्य विनम्रता से "You are requested to + V1" के रूप में बदले जाते हैं। विकल्प (c) में "be" गायब है और विकल्प (d) tense बदल देता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

21. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "The manager will sign the documents tomorrow."
(a) The documents would be signed by the manager tomorrow. (b) The documents will signed by the manager tomorrow. (c) The documents will be signed by the manager tomorrow. (d) The documents will being signed by the manager tomorrow.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Simple future passive में "will be + V3" बनता है। विकल्प (b) में "be" गायब है — परीक्षक का बार-बार आने वाला जाल — और विकल्प (a) "will" को "would" में बदल देता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

22. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "One should keep one's promises."
(a) Promises should kept. (b) Promises should be kept. (c) Promises should be keep. (d) Promises are should be kept.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "one" subject होने पर passive में कर्ता हटा दिया जाता है: "should be + V3 (kept)"। विकल्प (a) में "be" गायब है और विकल्प (c) में "be" के बाद मूल क्रिया आ गई है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

23. सही सक्रिय (active) रूप चुनें: "The novel was written by Premchand."
(a) Premchand wrote the novel. (b) Premchand writes the novel. (c) Premchand has written the novel. (d) Premchand was writing the novel.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Was written" simple past passive है, इसलिए active में simple past "wrote" होगा। अन्य विकल्प tense बदल देते हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

24. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "The children were flying kites."
(a) Kites were being flown by the children. (b) Kites were flown by the children. (c) Kites are being flown by the children. (d) Kites had been flown by the children.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Past continuous "were flying" passive में "were being + V3 (flown)" बनता है। विकल्प (b) इसे simple past में और विकल्प (c) present में बदल देता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

25. सही कर्मणि (passive) रूप चुनें: "Did he invite you to the party?"
(a) Were you invited by him to the party? (b) Was you invited by him to the party? (c) Did you invited by him to the party? (d) Were you invite by him to the party?
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Did + V1" (simple past प्रश्न) passive में "Was/Were + subject + V3" बनता है। "You" के साथ "were" आता है, इसलिए विकल्प (b) में agreement गलत है, और विकल्प (d) में past participle की जगह मूल क्रिया है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style
