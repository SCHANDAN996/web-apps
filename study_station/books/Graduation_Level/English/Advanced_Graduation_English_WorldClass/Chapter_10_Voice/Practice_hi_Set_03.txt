51. सही passive form चुनें: "She writes a letter."
(a) A letter was written by her. (b) A letter is being written by her. (c) A letter is written by her. (d) A letter has been written by her.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Active वाक्य simple present tense में है, इसलिए passive में "is/are + V3" लगता है। "A letter" एकवचन है, इसलिए "is written"। "was", "being" और "has been" वाले विकल्प tense बदल देते हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English Grammar

52. सही passive form चुनें: "The teacher praised the boy."

उत्तर: (a)

53. सही active form चुनें: "The cake was baked by my mother."

56. सही passive form चुनें: "Someone has stolen my watch."
(a) My watch has been stolen. (b) My watch has stolen. (c) My watch was stolen by someone. (d) My watch is being stolen.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Present perfect passive "has/have been + V3" होता है। "Has stolen" रूप से active है और गलत है; "someone" जैसे अस्पष्ट कर्ता को passive में आमतौर पर हटा दिया जाता है।

57. सही passive form चुनें: "Open the door."

61. सही passive form चुनें: "Who taught you English?"
(a) Who was taught you English? (b) By whom was you taught English? (c) By whom were you taught English? (d) By whom you were taught English?
उत्तर: (c)
हल: कर्ता के रूप में "Who" passive में "By whom" बनता है, और प्रश्न में inversion बना रहता है — "were you taught"। "You" के साथ "were" आता है, "was" नहीं, और प्रश्न का क्रम interrogative ही रहना चाहिए।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

62. सही passive form चुनें: "She gave me a book."

67. सही passive form चुनें: "The noise frightened the child."

70. सही passive form चुनें: "Do not insult the poor."
(a) The poor are not insulted. (b) Let the poor not be insulted. (c) The poor should not insulted. (d) Let the poor not insulted.
उत्तर: (b)

74. सही passive form चुनें: "It is said that he is a spy."
(a) He has been said to be a spy. (b) He was said to be a spy. (c) He is said being a spy. (d) He is said to be a spy.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "It is said that…" का रूपांतरण "subject + is said + to-infinitive" में होता है — "He is said to be a spy"। Tense present ही रहता है; "said being" व्याकरण की दृष्टि से गलत है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style
