126. सही passive रूप चुनें: "They made him sign the document."
(a) He was made sign the document. (b) He was made to sign the document. (c) He had been made sign the document. (d) He is made to sign the document.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "make" जैसी बाध्यता (compulsion) वाली क्रियाएँ active voice में "to" छोड़ देती हैं, लेकिन passive में "to + V1" चाहिए: "made him sign" बनता है "was made to sign"। पहला विकल्प क्लासिक जाल है — यह active की संरचना को जैसे-का-तैसे नकल करता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

127. सही passive रूप चुनें: "People speak English all over the world."
(a) English is being spoken all over the world. (b) English is spoken all over the world. (c) English has spoken all over the world. (d) English was spoken all over the world.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Simple present active, passive में "is/am/are + V3" बनता है। आदत-जैसे तथ्य के लिए simple present passive चाहिए, continuous या past नहीं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

128. त्रुटि पहचानें: The letter (P) / was wrote (Q) / by my grandfather (R) / before independence. (S)
(a) भाग Q (b) भाग P (c) भाग R (d) कोई त्रुटि नहीं
उत्तर: (a)
हल: भाग Q गलत है। Passive में past participle "written" चाहिए, past tense "wrote" नहीं: "was written"। यह Sound-Right जाल है क्योंकि "was" के बाद "wrote" सुनने में स्वाभाविक लगता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

129. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Someone has stolen my wallet."
(a) My wallet had been stolen. (b) My wallet has been stolen. (c) My wallet is stolen by someone. (d) My wallet was being stolen.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Present perfect active, passive में "has/have been + V3" बनता है। अस्पष्ट कर्ता "someone" को passive में हटा दिया जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

130. सही passive रूप चुनें: "The manager will sign the cheques tomorrow."
(a) The cheques would be signed tomorrow. (b) The cheques will signed tomorrow. (c) The cheques will be signed tomorrow. (d) The cheques will have signed tomorrow.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Simple future passive = "will be + V3"। दूसरा विकल्प "be" छोड़ देता है — परीक्षक का बार-बार आने वाला जाल।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

131. सही passive रूप चुनें: "One should keep one's promises."
(a) Promises should be kept. (b) Promises should kept. (c) Promises are should be kept. (d) Promises should have kept.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Modal passive = modal + be + V3। अनिश्चित कर्ता "one" हटा दिया जाता है। दूसरा विकल्प "be" छोड़ देता है, जो व्याकरणिक रूप से अधूरा है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

132. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Who taught you French?"
(a) By whom were you taught French? (b) By whom was you taught French? (c) Who was taught you French? (d) By whom you were taught French?
उत्तर: (a)
हल: कर्ता के रूप में "Who" बनता है "by whom", और प्रश्नवाचक passive में inversion बना रहता है: "were you taught"। आखिरी विकल्प inversion खो देता है; दूसरे में agreement गलत है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

133. त्रुटि पहचानें: The new bridge (P) / has been built (Q) / since two years (R) / by the municipal corporation. (S)
(a) कोई त्रुटि नहीं (b) भाग Q (c) भाग P (d) भाग R
उत्तर: (d)
हल: भाग R गलत है। "Since" समय के किसी बिंदु के साथ आता है ("since 2022"); अवधि के लिए "for" चाहिए: "for two years"। Since-for की उलझान परीक्षक का मानक जाल है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

134. सही passive रूप चुनें: "The teacher gave me a book."
(a) A book was given to me by the teacher. (b) A book was given me by the teacher. (c) I was given a book to the teacher. (d) Me was given a book by the teacher.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: दो कर्मों (objects) में से कोई भी subject बन सकता है, लेकिन बचे हुए indirect object के साथ "to" चाहिए: "a book was given to me"। तीसरा विकल्प देने वाले और पाने वाले को उलट देता है — over-thinking का जाल।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

135. सही passive रूप चुनें: "They were repairing the road when the accident happened."
(a) The road was repaired when the accident happened. (b) The road is being repaired when the accident happened. (c) The road was being repaired when the accident happened. (d) The road had been repairing when the accident happened.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Past continuous passive = "was/were being + V3"। पहला विकल्प aspect को simple past में बदल देता है, जिससे जारी-रहने का भाव खो जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

136. सही passive रूप चुनें: "You must obey the rules."
(a) The rules must be obeyed. (b) The rules must been obeyed. (c) The rules must obeyed. (d) The rules must being obeyed.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Modal passive = modal + be + V3। बाकी तीन विकल्प "be + V3" की ढाँचे को अलग-अलग तरीके से बिगाड़ते हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

137. सही passive रूप चुनें: "The committee is considering your proposal."
(a) Your proposal is considered by the committee. (b) Your proposal is being considered by the committee. (c) Your proposal has being considered by the committee. (d) Your proposal was considered by the committee.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Present continuous passive = "is/are being + V3"। पहला विकल्प simple present में चला जाता है, जिससे अर्थ "अभी इस समय" से बदलकर "आदतन" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

138. त्रुटि पहचानें: The thief (P) / has been (Q) / catched by (R) / the police. (S)
(a) कोई त्रुटि नहीं (b) भाग Q (c) भाग R (d) भाग P
उत्तर: (c)
हल: भाग R गलत है। "Catch" का past participle "caught" है, "catched" नहीं। Sound-Right जाल: सीखने वाले अनियमित क्रियाओं को नियमित बना देते हैं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

139. सही passive रूप चुनें: "They will have completed the project by March."
(a) The project will have completed by March. (b) The project would have been completed by March. (c) The project will have been completed by March. (d) The project will been completed by March.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Future perfect passive = "will have been + V3"। पहला विकल्प "been" छोड़ देता है, जिससे passive इरादे के साथ active-जैसी संरचना रह जाती है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

140. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Open the door."
(a) Let the door be opened. (b) Let the door opened. (c) The door is opened by you. (d) The door should opened.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: आज्ञावाचक (imperative) वाक्य passive में "Let + object + be + V3" से बनते हैं। दूसरा विकल्प "be" छोड़ देता है; तीसरा व्याकरणिक तो है लेकिन आज्ञा का भाव बदल देता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

141. सही passive रूप चुनें: "The noise frightened the child."
(a) The child was frightened with the noise. (b) The child is frightened by the noise. (c) The child had frightened by the noise. (d) The child was frightened by the noise.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Simple past passive = "was/were + V3", और "frightened" कर्ता के लिए "by" लेता है। Tense active वाक्य से तय है, इसलिए present-tense वाला विकल्प असफल है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

142. सही passive रूप चुनें: "The news alarmed her."
(a) She was alarmed at the news. (b) She was alarmed by the news. (c) She was alarmed with the news. (d) She was alarmed from the news.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Simple past passive = "was/were + V3", और "alarmed" कर्ता के लिए "by" लेता है: "alarmed by the news"। बाकी विकल्प ऐसे preposition लगाते हैं जो इस क्रिया के साथ नहीं आते — preposition का जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

143. त्रुटि पहचानें: It is believed (P) / that the treasure (Q) / is being hid (R) / somewhere in the fort. (S)

144. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Do not insult the poor."
(a) The poor should not insulted. (b) The poor are not insulted. (c) Let not the poor insulted. (d) Let the poor not be insulted.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: नकारात्मक आज्ञावाचक वाक्य बनते हैं "Let + object + not be + V3"। पहला और तीसरा विकल्प "be" छोड़ देते हैं — imperative passives में सबसे आम गलती।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English Grammar

145. सही passive रूप चुनें: "The doctor is examining the patient now."
(a) The patient is being examined by the doctor now. (b) The patient is examined by the doctor now. (c) The patient has been examined by the doctor now. (d) The patient was being examined by the doctor now.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Now" present continuous तय करता है; passive = "is being + V3"। आखिरी विकल्प "being" तो रखता है लेकिन past में चला जाता है, जो "now" से टकराता है — tense-mismatch का जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

146. सही passive रूप चुनें: "We know him to be an honest man."
(a) He is known by us an honest man. (b) He is being known to be an honest man. (c) He was known to be an honest man. (d) He is known to be an honest man.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Know" passive में "by" नहीं, "to" preposition लेता है — fixed-preposition का जाल। "Know" जैसी stative क्रियाएँ continuous रूप भी नहीं लेतीं, इसलिए दूसरा विकल्प भी कट जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

147. सही passive रूप चुनें: "She had written the letter before I arrived."
(a) The letter has been written before I arrived. (b) The letter was written before I arrived. (c) The letter had been written before I arrived. (d) The letter had written before I arrived.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Past perfect passive = "had been + V3", जो दो भूतकालीन घटनाओं में पहले-बाद का क्रम बनाए रखता है। दूसरा विकल्प क्रम को simple past में समतल कर देता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

148. त्रुटि पहचानें: The match (P) / was won (Q) / by our team (R) / with a huge margin. (S)
(a) भाग P (b) भाग Q (c) कोई त्रुटि नहीं (d) भाग R
उत्तर: (c)
हल: वाक्य सही है: simple past passive "was won", कर्ता के साथ "by" और तरीका बताने वाला वाक्यांश "with a huge margin"। Over-thinking का जाल — परीक्षक त्रुटि-रहित वाक्य भी रखते हैं ताकि अंधे अनुमान लगाने वाले फँसें।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

149. सही passive रूप चुनें: "Why did he refuse the offer?"
(a) Why the offer was refused by him? (b) Why was the offer refused by him? (c) Why was the offer refuse by him? (d) Why did the offer refused by him?
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Wh-प्रश्न का passive wh-शब्द रखता है, फिर inversion: "was the offer refused"। पहला विकल्प inversion खो देता है; तीसरा participle की जगह base form लगाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

150. सही active रूप चुनें: "The students are being taught grammar by the new teacher."
(a) The new teacher was teaching grammar to the students. (b) The new teacher teaches grammar to the students. (c) The new teacher has been teaching grammar to the students. (d) The new teacher is teaching grammar to the students.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Are being taught" present continuous passive है, इसलिए active भी present continuous होना चाहिए: "is teaching"। बाकी विकल्प tense बदल देते हैं — reverse-conversion का जाल, जहाँ passive दिया गया है और active का ठीक-ठीक मिलान करना है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style
