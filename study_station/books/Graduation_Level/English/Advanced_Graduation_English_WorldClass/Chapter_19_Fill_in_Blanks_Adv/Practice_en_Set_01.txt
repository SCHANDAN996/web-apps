Fill in the Blanks (Advanced) — Practice Set 1
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. She has been working in this company ______ 2015.
(a) since (b) for (c) from (d) at
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (2015), while "for" is used with a period of time (for ten years). "From" needs an end point (from 2015 to 2020).
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. He is good ______ mathematics but weak in English.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) with
Answer: (b)
Solution: The fixed preposition with "good" is "at" when talking about a skill or subject. "Good in" is a common Hindi-speaker error.
Source: PYQ-style

3. The train ______ before we reached the station.
(a) left (b) had left (c) has left (d) was leaving
Answer: (b)
Solution: When two past actions occur, the earlier action takes the past perfect (had left) and the later one takes the simple past (reached).
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

4. I ______ my homework before the teacher arrived.
(a) had finished (b) finished (c) have finished (d) finish
Answer: (a)
Solution: Finishing homework happened before the teacher's arrival, so the earlier action takes the past perfect tense.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

5. Neither of the two boys ______ done his work.
(a) have (b) has (c) are (d) were
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb. Examiner's trap: the plural noun "boys" sits next to the verb and tempts you toward "have".
Source: PYQ-style

6. The Ganges ______ one of the holiest rivers of India.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "The Ganges" is the name of one river, so it takes a singular verb despite ending in "s".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

7. He prevented me ______ going there.
(a) from (b) for (c) to (d) against
Answer: (a)
Solution: The verb "prevent" is always followed by the preposition "from" plus the -ing form of the verb.
Source: PYQ-style

8. She is ______ honest woman.
(a) an (b) the (c) no article (d) a
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a silent "h", so the sound is a vowel sound; hence "an" is used.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

9. He ______ TV when the lights went off.
(a) watches (b) watched (c) had watched (d) was watching
Answer: (d)
Solution: An action in progress in the past (was watching) is interrupted by a shorter action (went off) in the simple past.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

10. You should abide ______ the rules of the institution.
(a) by (b) with (c) to (d) on
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Abide by" is the fixed phrasal-prepositional combination meaning "to follow or obey".
Source: PYQ-style

11. Each of the students ______ given a certificate.
(a) were (b) are (c) was (d) have
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Each of" takes a singular verb. Examiner's trap: the plural "students" before the verb misleads many candidates.
Source: PYQ-style

12. I am looking forward ______ meeting you soon.
(a) towards (b) at (c) to (d) for
Answer: (c)
Solution: In "look forward to", "to" is a preposition, not an infinitive marker, so it is followed by the -ing form (meeting).
Source: PYQ-style

13. He has been suffering ______ fever since Monday.
(a) with (b) by (c) of (d) from
Answer: (d)
Solution: The fixed preposition with "suffer" when naming a disease is "from".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

14. Bread and butter ______ his daily breakfast.
(a) are (b) have been (c) is (d) were
Answer: (c)
Solution: When two nouns joined by "and" together denote one single dish or idea, the verb is singular.
Source: PYQ-style

15. Unless you work hard, you ______ not succeed.
(a) should (b) would (c) will (d) shall
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Unless" introduces a negative condition; the main clause takes "will not" for a future result. Note that "unless" itself already carries the negative sense.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

16. The teacher asked the students ______ noise.
(a) do not make (b) to not make (c) not to make (d) not make
Answer: (c)
Solution: In indirect speech with "asked", the correct structure is "asked + object + not to + verb". Splitting the infinitive as "to not make" is considered incorrect in exams.
Source: PYQ-style

17. He is one of the best players that ______ ever played for India.
(a) has (b) is (c) was (d) have
Answer: (d)
Solution: In "one of the + plural noun + that/who", the relative pronoun refers to the plural noun (players), so the verb is plural (have).
Source: PYQ-style

18. We must respect our ______.
(a) older (b) elder (c) eldest (d) elders
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Elders" (plural noun) means people older than us and is the correct word here; "elder" alone is an adjective and cannot stand as a noun in this sense.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

19. No sooner did she see the police ______ she ran away.
(a) when (b) then (c) that (d) than
Answer: (d)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than". "Hardly/Scarcely" pairs with "when" — do not mix the two pairs.
Source: PYQ-style

20. He deals ______ electronic goods.
(a) with (b) in (c) at (d) on
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Deal in" means to trade in something; "deal with" means to handle a person or problem. Trading in goods needs "in".
Source: PYQ-style

21. The jury ______ delivered its verdict unanimously.
(a) have (b) has (c) are (d) were
Answer: (b)
Solution: When a collective noun acts as a single unit (unanimous verdict, "its"), the verb is singular. The plural verb would be correct only if the members were divided.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) had I reached (b) I had reached (c) I reached (d) did I reach
Answer: (a)
Solution: When a sentence begins with "Hardly", inversion is required and the earlier action takes the past perfect: "Hardly had I reached... when the train left."
Source: PYQ-style

23. He is junior ______ me by two years.
(a) than (b) from (c) with (d) to
Answer: (d)
Solution: Latin comparatives like junior, senior, superior, inferior, prior and prefer take "to", never "than". Examiner's trap: "than" sounds natural after a comparative idea.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Scarcely had he stepped out of the house ______ it began to rain.
(a) when (b) than (c) then (d) that
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Scarcely/Hardly" pairs with "when"; "No sooner" pairs with "than". Mixing the pairs is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

25. The number of road accidents ______ increasing every year.
(a) were (b) are (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "The number of" takes a singular verb, while "a number of" takes a plural verb. The headword is "number", not "accidents".
Source: PYQ-style
