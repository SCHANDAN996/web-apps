51. The committee ______ reached its decision after a long discussion.
(a) have (b) has (c) are (d) were
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Committee" is a collective noun acting as a single unit here, so it takes a singular verb "has". Trap: "have" tempts because a committee has many members, but the unit acts as one.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

52. She is good ______ mathematics but weak in chemistry.
(a) at (b) in (c) for (d) with
Answer: (a)
Solution: The fixed preposition with "good" is "at" — good at something. "Good in" is a common Hindi-speaker error.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Neither of the two boys ______ done his homework.
(a) has (b) have (c) are (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb. Trap: the plural noun "boys" right before the verb misleads many into choosing "have".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

54. He has been working in this company ______ 2015.
(a) for (b) from (c) since (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (2015); "for" is used with a period of time (for ten years).
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

55. The teacher asked the students why they ______ late.
(a) are (b) will be (c) have been (d) were
Answer: (d)
Solution: In reported speech with a past reporting verb ("asked"), the tense shifts back: "are" becomes "were".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

56. I look forward ______ you at the conference.
(a) to meet (b) meeting (c) for meeting (d) to meeting
Answer: (d)
Solution: In "look forward to", "to" is a preposition, so it takes a gerund: "to meeting". Trap: "to meet" sounds right because "to" usually marks an infinitive.
Source: PYQ-style

57. The scenery of Kashmir ______ very beautiful.
(a) have been (b) are (c) were (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb; it has no plural form.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

58. He is one of the best players who ______ ever played for India.
(a) has (b) have (c) is (d) was
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "one of the + plural noun + who/that", the verb agrees with the plural noun ("players"), so "have". Trap: choosing "has" by matching with "one".
Source: PYQ-style

59. The principal, along with the teachers, ______ present at the function.
(a) was (b) were (c) are (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject ("the principal"), which is singular. The intervening phrase "along with the teachers" is the examiner's trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

60. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) had I reached (b) I had reached (c) I reached (d) did I reach
Answer: (a)
Solution: After "Hardly/Scarcely" at the start of a sentence, inversion is required with the past perfect: "had I reached".
Source: PYQ-style

61. Each of the students ______ given a certificate.
(a) was (b) were (c) have (d) are
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Each of" always takes a singular verb, regardless of the plural noun that follows.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

62. The government has ______ a new policy to curb inflation.
(a) adapted (b) adopted (c) adepped (d) adored
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Adopted" means accepted/put into effect; "adapted" means adjusted. Policies are adopted, not adapted.
Source: PYQ-style

63. No sooner did she enter the room ______ the phone rang.
(a) when (b) then (c) than (d) that
Answer: (c)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than"; "hardly/scarcely" are paired with "when". This pairing trap is a favourite of examiners.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

64. The number of accidents on this road ______ increasing every year.
(a) is (b) are (c) have been (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The number of" takes a singular verb; "a number of" takes a plural verb. Trap: the plural "accidents" nearby pulls candidates toward "are".
Source: PYQ-style

65. He denied ______ the money from the drawer.
(a) to take (b) taking (c) taken (d) take
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Deny" is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive: "denied taking".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

66. The furniture in the new office ______ been delivered yet.
(a) have (b) are (c) has (d) were
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Furniture" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb: "has been delivered". Trap: candidates think of many chairs and tables and pick a plural verb.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

67. Unless you ______ hard, you will not clear the examination.
(a) will work (b) work (c) worked (d) had worked
Answer: (b)
Solution: In conditional clauses with "unless/if", use the present simple for the future; "will" is not used in the condition clause.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

68. The manager made the workers ______ overtime without extra pay.
(a) to work (b) worked (c) working (d) work
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "make" (causative) in the active voice, the bare infinitive is used: "made the workers work".
Source: PYQ-style

69. Bread and butter ______ his daily breakfast.
(a) is (b) have been (c) were (d) are
Answer: (a)
Solution: When two nouns joined by "and" denote one combined dish or idea, the verb is singular. Trap: two nouns suggest a plural verb.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

70. She is senior ______ me by two years.
(a) from (b) than (c) to (d) over
Answer: (c)
Solution: Latin comparatives — senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior — take "to", not "than".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

71. The judge acquitted him ______ all charges.
(a) from (b) with (c) of (d) off
Answer: (c)
Solution: The fixed preposition with "acquit" is "of": acquitted of charges. Compare "accused of", "convicted of".
Source: PYQ-style

72. Not only the students but also the teacher ______ surprised by the result.
(a) were (b) are (c) have been (d) was
Answer: (d)
Solution: With "not only... but also", the verb agrees with the nearer subject ("the teacher"), so "was". Proximity agreement is the tested rule.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

73. He ______ his lunch before I reached his house.
(a) finished (b) finishes (c) has finished (d) had finished
Answer: (d)
Solution: Of two past actions, the earlier one takes the past perfect: he had finished lunch before I reached.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

74. The minister's speech had little effect ______ the audience.
(a) over (b) with (c) on (d) at
Answer: (c)
Solution: The fixed preposition with "effect" is "on": "effect on the audience". "Over" tempts because "influence over" exists, but that is a different collocation.
Source: PYQ-style

75. I wish I ______ a millionaire; I would donate half my wealth.
(a) was (b) were (c) am (d) had been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "I wish" expressing an unreal present desire takes the subjunctive "were" for all persons. Trap: "was" sounds natural in speech but is not the exam answer.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
