Sentence Improvement (Advanced) — Practice Set 2
Questions 26–50 | Easy: 26–40 | Medium: 41–50

26. Choose the option that best improves the underlined part: "He denied to help me in my difficulty."
(a) denied helping me (b) No improvement (c) denied for helping me (d) denied to helping me
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Deny" is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive, so "denied to help" is wrong and "denied helping me" is correct. The trap is to think "deny" behaves like "refuse," which does take the infinitive.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Choose the correct improvement: "Unless you do not work hard, you will fail."
(a) Until you do not work hard (b) No improvement (c) If you not work hard (d) Unless you work hard
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Unless" is already negative in meaning, so "do not" must be removed. "Unless you work hard" is the correct conditional form.
Source: NCERT-based grammar rule

28. Choose the correct improvement: "She is more wiser than her sister."
(a) wiser (b) No improvement (c) most wisest (d) much more wiser
Answer: (a)
Solution: Double comparatives are wrong. "Wiser" is already comparative, so "more" must be dropped.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Choose the correct improvement: "I have been living in Delhi since five years."
(a) from five years (b) for five years (c) since five years ago (d) No improvement
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (since 2019); "for" is used with a period of time (for five years).
Source: NCERT Class 9 English grammar

30. Choose the correct improvement: "He is one of the best player in the team."
(a) one of best player (b) one of the better player (c) one of the best players (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "one of the + superlative," the noun must be plural: "one of the best players."
Source: PYQ-style

31. Choose the correct improvement: "The teacher asked the students to not make a noise."
(a) to not making a noise (b) to not make noise making (c) not to make a noise (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: The negative infinitive places "not" before "to": "not to make a noise."
Source: PYQ-style

32. Choose the correct improvement: "He as well as his brothers were present at the meeting."
(a) No improvement (b) was present (c) are present (d) were presented
Answer: (b)
Solution: With "as well as," the verb agrees with the first subject ("He"), so singular "was present" is correct. This is a classic examiner trap — the nearby plural "brothers" tempts a plural verb.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap: intervening phrase)

33. Choose the correct improvement: "No sooner did he enter the room when the phone rang."
(a) then the phone rang (b) than the phone rang (c) as the phone rang (d) No improvement
Answer: (b)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than," not "when." "Hardly/Scarcely" take "when."
Source: PYQ-style

34. Choose the correct improvement: "She is senior than me by two years."
(a) No improvement (b) senior from me (c) senior of me (d) senior to me
Answer: (d)
Solution: Latin comparatives (senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior) take "to," not "than."
Source: PYQ-style

35. Choose the correct improvement: "I am looking forward to meet you."
(a) to met you (b) for meeting you (c) to meeting you (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: In "look forward to," "to" is a preposition, so it is followed by a gerund: "to meeting you."
Source: PYQ-style

36. Choose the correct improvement: "He prevented me to go there."
(a) me for going there (b) me from going there (c) me in going there (d) No improvement
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Prevent" takes the pattern "prevent somebody from doing something."
Source: NCERT-based grammar rule

37. Choose the correct improvement: "The furnitures in this room are very costly."
(a) The furnitures is (b) The furniture are (c) No improvement (d) The furniture in this room is
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Furniture" is an uncountable noun; it has no plural form and takes a singular verb.
Source: PYQ-style

38. Choose the correct improvement: "He is working here since 2015."
(a) is working here from 2015 (b) has been working here since 2015 (c) was working here since 2015 (d) No improvement
Answer: (b)
Solution: An action that began in the past and continues now takes the present perfect continuous with "since."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English grammar

39. Choose the correct improvement: "Each of the boys have done his homework."
(a) have done their homework (b) has done their homework (c) No improvement (d) has done his homework
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Each of" takes a singular verb and singular pronoun: "has done his homework."
Source: PYQ-style

40. Choose the correct improvement: "I prefer tea more than coffee."
(a) tea than coffee (b) tea over than coffee (c) tea to coffee (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Prefer" is followed by "to," not "than": "prefer tea to coffee."
Source: PYQ-style

41. Choose the correct improvement: "The committee has taken their decision unanimously."
(a) No improvement (b) have taken their decision (c) has took its decision (d) has taken its decision
Answer: (d)
Solution: When a collective noun acts as one body (shown by "unanimously"), it takes a singular verb and singular pronoun "its." The trap is that "their" sounds natural in speech.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap: collective noun)

42. Choose the correct improvement: "Hardly had he slept than the alarm rang."
(a) than the alarm had rung (b) then the alarm rang (c) when the alarm rang (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Hardly" and "Scarcely" pair with "when"; only "No sooner" pairs with "than."
Source: PYQ-style

43. Choose the correct improvement: "He is one of those students who always comes first."
(a) who always come first (b) No improvement (c) who is always coming first (d) which always comes first
Answer: (a)
Solution: The relative pronoun "who" refers to "students" (plural), not "one," so the verb is plural: "come." In "one of those who…" the verb agrees with the plural antecedent.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Choose the correct improvement: "Being a rainy day, I stayed at home."
(a) Being it a rainy day (b) As being a rainy day (c) It being a rainy day (d) No improvement
Answer: (c)
Solution: The original is a dangling participle — "being a rainy day" cannot describe "I." "It being a rainy day" gives the participle its proper subject.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Choose the correct improvement: "He insisted to go with me."
(a) insisted on going with me (b) insisted for going with me (c) No improvement (d) insisted in going with me
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Insist" is followed by "on + gerund": "insisted on going."
Source: NCERT-based grammar rule

46. Choose the correct improvement: "The Ganges is one of the longest river in India."
(a) longest rivers in India (b) longer river in India (c) most long rivers of India (d) No improvement
Answer: (a)
Solution: "One of the + superlative" requires a plural noun: "one of the longest rivers."
Source: PYQ-style

47. Choose the correct improvement: "Neither of the two boys have passed the examination."
(a) No improvement (b) has passed the examination (c) have been passing the examination (d) have passed their examination
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb: "has passed."
Source: PYQ-style

48. Choose the correct improvement: "He told me that he will come tomorrow."
(a) he will be coming tomorrow (b) he shall come tomorrow (c) No improvement (d) he would come the next day
Answer: (d)
Solution: In reported speech with a past reporting verb, "will" becomes "would" and "tomorrow" becomes "the next day."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English grammar

49. Choose the correct improvement: "The number of accidents are increasing every year."
(a) is increasing every year (b) are increased every year (c) have been increasing every year (d) No improvement
Answer: (a)
Solution: "The number of" takes a singular verb; "a number of" takes a plural verb. The plural noun "accidents" in between is the examiner's trap.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner trap: intervening phrase)

50. Choose the correct improvement: "He availed of the opportunity to study abroad."
(a) availed himself of the opportunity (b) availed for the opportunity (c) No improvement (d) availed the opportunity
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Avail" is used reflexively: "avail oneself of something." Simply "availed of" without the reflexive pronoun is incorrect.
Source: PYQ-style
