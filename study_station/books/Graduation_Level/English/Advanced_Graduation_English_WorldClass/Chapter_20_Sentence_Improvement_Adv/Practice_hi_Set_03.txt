51. सही सुधार चुनें: "He is working here since 2015."
(a) He is working here from 2015. (b) No improvement (c) He has been working here since 2015. (d) He was working here since 2015.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Since 2015" के साथ वर्तमान तक जारी कार्य के लिए present perfect continuous tense चाहिए, इसलिए "has been working" सही है। "Since" के साथ जारी कार्य के लिए simple present का प्रयोग नहीं होता।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

52. सही सुधार चुनें: "One of my friend lives in Delhi."
(a) One of my friends live in Delhi. (b) One of my friend live in Delhi. (c) One of my friends lives in Delhi. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "One of" के बाद संज्ञा बहुवचन ("friends") होती है, पर क्रिया एकवचन ("lives") रहती है क्योंकि कर्ता "one" है। यह परीक्षक की क्लासिक चाल है जो दोनों भागों को एक साथ परखती है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

53. सही सुधार चुनें: "She is more taller than her sister."
(a) She is taller than her sister. (b) No improvement (c) She is most taller than her sister. (d) She is much more taller than her sister.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Taller" पहले से ही comparative है; "more" जोड़ने से double comparative बनता है, जो गलत है। केवल "taller" का प्रयोग करें।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

54. सही सुधार चुनें: "I and Ravi went to the market."
(a) No improvement (b) Me and Ravi went to the market. (c) Ravi and me went to the market. (d) Ravi and I went to the market.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: कर्ता के रूप में सर्वनाम "I" होना चाहिए, और शिष्ट प्रयोग में दूसरे व्यक्ति को पहले रखा जाता है: "Ravi and I"। "Me" object pronoun है और कर्ता नहीं बन सकता।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 8 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

55. सही सुधार चुनें: "I am having two brothers."
(a) I am having two brother. (b) No improvement (c) I have two brothers. (d) I have been having two brothers.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Have" जब "पास होना" के अर्थ में आए तो यह stative verb है और continuous रूप में प्रयोग नहीं होता, इसलिए "I have two brothers" सही है। Continuous रूप तभी सही है जब "have" का अर्थ खाना या अनुभव करना हो, जैसे "I am having lunch"।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

56. सही सुधार चुनें: "The news are very shocking."
(a) The news were very shocking. (b) The news is very shocking. (c) The news have been very shocking. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "News" दिखने में बहुवचन लगता है, पर यह अगणनीय संज्ञा है और हमेशा एकवचन क्रिया लेता है। अंतिम "s" के कारण यह बार-बार पूछा जाने वाला sound-right जाल है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

57. सही सुधार चुनें: "Unless you do not work hard, you will fail."
(a) Unless you work hard, you will fail. (b) Until you do not work hard, you will fail. (c) Unless you will not work hard, you will fail. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Unless" में पहले से ही नकारात्मक अर्थ ("if not") छिपा है, इसलिए "do not" जोड़ने से double negative बन जाता है। "Unless" के बाद "not" हटाएँ।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

58. सही सुधार चुनें: "He is senior than me by two years."
(a) He is senior to me by two years. (b) He is senior from me by two years. (c) He is more senior than me by two years. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Senior, junior, superior, inferior और prefer जैसे Latin comparatives के साथ "than" नहीं, "to" लगता है। इनके साथ "more" भी कभी नहीं आता।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

59. सही सुधार चुनें: "I have seen him yesterday."
(a) I have seen him the day before. (b) I saw him yesterday. (c) I had seen him yesterday. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Yesterday" जैसे निश्चित भूतकाल के सूचक के साथ simple past का प्रयोग होता है, present perfect का नहीं। Present perfect केवल अनिश्चित समय के साथ प्रयोग होता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

60. सही सुधार चुनें: "Each of the boys have done his work."
(a) Each of the boys have done their work. (b) No improvement (c) Each of the boys has done their works. (d) Each of the boys has done his work.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Each of" के साथ संज्ञा बहुवचन पर क्रिया और सर्वनाम एकवचन होते हैं: "has done his work"। बीच में आया बहुवचन "boys" intervening-phrase जाल है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

61. सही सुधार चुनें: "The teacher, along with the students, were present in the hall."
(a) No improvement (b) The teacher, along with the students, have been present in the hall. (c) The teacher, along with the students, are present in the hall. (d) The teacher, along with the students, was present in the hall.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Along with" के साथ क्रिया पहले कर्ता "the teacher" के अनुसार लगती है, जो एकवचन है। उसके बाद का वाक्यांश intervening phrase है और उसे नज़रअंदाज़ करना चाहिए — यह परीक्षक की क्लासिक चाल है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

62. सही सुधार चुनें: "No sooner did he reach the station when the train left."
(a) No sooner did he reach the station than the train left. (b) No sooner did he reach the station then the train left. (c) No sooner he reached the station than the train left. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "No sooner" हमेशा "than" के साथ जुड़ता है, "when" या "then" के साथ कभी नहीं। Inversion "did he reach" पहले से सही है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

63. सही सुधार चुनें: "He is one of the best players that has ever played for India."
(a) No improvement (b) He is one of the best player that has ever played for India. (c) He is one of the best players that have ever played for India. (d) He is one of the best players who has ever played for India.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "One of the + plural noun + relative clause" में relative clause की क्रिया बहुवचन संज्ञा ("players") के अनुसार लगती है, इसलिए "have" सही है। एकवचन "one" कई अभ्यर्थियों को भ्रमित करता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

64. सही सुधार चुनें: "Scarcely he had entered the room when the phone rang."
(a) Scarcely he had entered the room than the phone rang. (b) No improvement (c) Scarcely did he entered the room when the phone rang. (d) Scarcely had he entered the room when the phone rang.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: जब "scarcely" जैसा नकारात्मक क्रिया-विशेषण वाक्य की शुरुआत में आए, तो inversion आवश्यक है: "Scarcely had he entered"। "Scarcely" के साथ "when" जुड़ता है, जो पहले से सही है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

65. सही सुधार चुनें: "The committee has taken their decision unanimously."
(a) No improvement (b) The committee has taken its decision unanimously. (c) The committee has took its decision unanimously. (d) The committee have taken their decision unanimously.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जब सामूहिक संज्ञा एक इकाई के रूप में कार्य करे (जैसा "unanimously" दर्शाता है), तो एकवचन सर्वनाम "its" लगता है। "Their" से सदस्यों में मतभेद का भाव आता, जो "unanimously" के विपरीत है — यह collective-noun जाल है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

66. सही सुधार चुनें: "He insisted to go with me."
(a) He insisted in going with me. (b) He insisted for going with me. (c) He insisted on going with me. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Insist" के बाद preposition "on" और gerund आता है: "insisted on going"। यह कभी bare infinitive नहीं लेता।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

67. सही सुधार चुनें: "Neither of the two boys have passed the examination."
(a) Neither of the two boy has passed the examination. (b) Neither of the two boys has passed the examination. (c) Neither of the two boys have been passed the examination. (d) No improvement

स्रोत: PYQ-style

68. सही सुधार चुनें: "I look forward to meet you soon."
(a) I look forward to meeting with you soon. (b) I look forward to meeting you soon. (c) I look forward for meeting you soon. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Look forward to" में "to" preposition है, infinitive का भाग नहीं, इसलिए इसके बाद gerund आता है: "meeting"। यह बहुत आम sound-right जाल है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

69. सही सुधार चुनें: "The Ganges is one of the longest river in India."
(a) The Ganges is one of the longest rivers in India. (b) The Ganges is one of the most longest rivers in India. (c) The Ganges is one of longest river in India. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "One of the + superlative" के बाद संज्ञा बहुवचन होनी चाहिए: "longest rivers"। Superlative "longest" के साथ "most" कभी नहीं लगता।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

70. सही सुधार चुनें: "Hardly had I reached home than it started raining."
(a) Hardly I had reached home when it started raining. (b) Hardly had I reached home when it started raining. (c) Hardly had I reached home then it started raining. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Hardly" के साथ "when" जुड़ता है, "than" नहीं ("than" "no sooner" के साथ आता है)। Inversion "had I reached" पहले से सही है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

71. सही सुधार चुनें: "He as well as his brothers were invited to the party."
(a) He as well as his brothers was invited to the party. (b) He as well as his brothers have been invited to the party. (c) He as well as his brothers are invited to the party. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "As well as" के साथ क्रिया पहले कर्ता "He" के अनुसार लगती है, इसलिए एकवचन "was" चाहिए। पास का बहुवचन "brothers" वही intervening-phrase जाल है जो परीक्षक बिछाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

72. सही सुधार चुनें: "Being a rainy day, I stayed at home."
(a) Being a rainy day, staying at home was done by me. (b) No improvement (c) As being a rainy day, I stayed at home. (d) It being a rainy day, I stayed at home.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Being a rainy day" "I" का वर्णन नहीं कर सकता — यह dangling modifier है। Dummy subject "It" जोड़ने से तर्क ठीक हो जाता है: "It being a rainy day, I stayed at home"।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

73. सही सुधार चुनें: "The principal and secretary have arrived for the meeting."
(a) The principal and secretary has arrived for the meeting. (b) The principal and the secretary has arrived for the meeting. (c) The principal and secretary is arriving for the meeting. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (a)
हल: दो जुड़ी संज्ञाओं से पहले एक ही article होने का अर्थ है कि एक ही व्यक्ति दोनों पदों पर है, इसलिए क्रिया एकवचन होगी: "has arrived"। "The" दोहराने पर दो व्यक्ति समझे जाएँगे — यह article जाल है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

74. सही सुधार चुनें: "He told me that he will come the next day."
(a) No improvement (b) He told me that he will be coming the next day. (c) He told me that he shall come the next day. (d) He told me that he would come the next day.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: भूतकालीन reporting verb ("told") वाले reported speech में "will" बदलकर "would" हो जाता है। यहाँ third-person कर्ता के साथ "shall" गलत है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

75. सही सुधार चुनें: "Many a student have failed in this examination."
(a) Many a students have failed in this examination. (b) Many a student have been failed in this examination. (c) Many a student has failed in this examination. (d) No improvement
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Many a" के बाद एकवचन संज्ञा और एकवचन क्रिया आती है: "Many a student has failed"। अर्थ बहुवचन लगता है, जो sound-right जाल है; व्याकरण पूरी तरह एकवचन है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style
