Tense — Practice Set 2 (Questions 26–50)
Difficulty: Questions 26–40 Easy | Questions 41–50 Medium

26. She ______ her homework before her mother returned.
(a) finished (b) had finished (c) has finished (d) finishes
Answer: (b)
Solution: Two past actions are given; the earlier action takes the past perfect. Finishing homework happened before the mother returned, so "had finished" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. Water ______ at 100°C at sea level.
(a) is boiling (b) boils (c) boiled (d) has boiled
Answer: (b)
Solution: Universal truths and scientific facts are always expressed in the simple present tense, regardless of the tense of the main clause.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

28. Look! The children ______ in the garden.
(a) play (b) played (c) are playing (d) have played
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Look!" signals an action happening right now, so the present continuous "are playing" is required.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

29. I ______ this movie three times.
(a) have seen (b) saw (c) am seeing (d) see
Answer: (a)
Solution: An action completed at an unspecified time in the past with relevance to the present takes the present perfect. "Three times" shows experience, not a fixed past time.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

30. He ______ to office every day by bus.
(a) has gone (b) is going (c) goes (d) went
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Every day" indicates a habitual action, which is expressed in the simple present tense.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

31. They ______ the match when it started raining.
(a) won (b) win (c) have won (d) had won
Answer: (d)
Solution: Winning happened before the rain started; the earlier of two past actions takes the past perfect "had won".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

32. We ______ in this city since 2015.
(a) have lived (b) are living (c) live (d) lived
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Since 2015" shows an action that began in the past and continues to the present, so the present perfect "have lived" is used.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

33. The train ______ at 6 a.m. tomorrow.
(a) leaves (b) is leaving (c) will leave (d) left
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixed timetables and schedules, even in the future, are expressed in the simple present tense.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

34. While I ______ a book, the phone rang.
(a) read (b) was reading (c) am reading (d) have read
Answer: (b)
Solution: A longer background action interrupted by a shorter one takes the past continuous; "rang" is the interrupting action.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

35. She ______ her lunch already.
(a) has finished (b) finished (c) finishes (d) is finishing
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Already" is a marker of the present perfect tense, showing a recently completed action with present relevance.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

36. My father ______ before I woke up.
(a) had left (b) left (c) has left (d) leaves
Answer: (a)
Solution: The father's departure happened before the waking up; the earlier past action takes the past perfect.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

37. Listen! Someone ______ at the door.
(a) knocks (b) is knocking (c) knocked (d) has knocked
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Listen!" indicates an action in progress at the moment of speaking, so the present continuous is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

38. He ______ here yesterday.
(a) comes (b) has come (c) is coming (d) came
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Yesterday" is a definite past time marker, so the simple past "came" is used. The present perfect is never used with definite past time.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

39. I ______ him for ten years.
(a) know (b) knew (c) am knowing (d) have known
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Know" is a stative verb and is not used in the continuous form; with "for ten years" the present perfect "have known" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

40. By next December, she ______ her degree.
(a) will have completed (b) will complete (c) completes (d) has completed
Answer: (a)
Solution: "By next December" points to an action that will be finished before a future deadline, so the future perfect is required.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

41. Spot the error: Part P: I have seen / Part Q: him yesterday / Part R: at the market.
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part P is wrong. The present perfect cannot be used with a definite past time like "yesterday"; it should be "I saw him yesterday". This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) I am knowing the answer. (b) I know the answer. (c) I have been knowing the answer. (d) I was knowing the answer.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Know" is a stative verb and never takes the continuous form; the simple present "I know the answer" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

43. When we reached the theatre, the film ______.
(a) already started (b) starts already (c) has already started (d) had already started
Answer: (d)
Solution: The film started before we reached; the earlier of two past actions takes the past perfect "had already started".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

44. Spot the error: Part P: He is working / Part Q: in this bank / Part R: since 2010.
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part P is wrong. With "since 2010", the present perfect or present perfect continuous is needed: "He has been working in this bank since 2010."
Source: PYQ-style

45. She ______ television when the lights went off.
(a) watched (b) watches (c) has watched (d) was watching
Answer: (d)
Solution: A continuing background action interrupted by another past event takes the past continuous "was watching".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

46. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I have been waiting here since two hours. (b) I am waiting here since two hours. (c) I have been waiting here for two hours. (d) I wait here for two hours.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "For" is used with a period of time (two hours); "since" is used with a point of time. The present perfect continuous fits an action still in progress.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

47. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) had I reached (c) I reached (d) did I reach
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "Hardly", inversion is used and the earlier action takes the past perfect: "Hardly had I reached the station when the train left."
Source: PYQ-style

48. By the time the police arrived, the thief ______.
(a) escaped (b) has escaped (c) escapes (d) had escaped
Answer: (d)
Solution: The escape happened before the police arrived; the earlier past action takes the past perfect "had escaped".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

49. Spot the error: Part P: No sooner she had entered / Part Q: the room / Part R: than the phone rang.
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part P is wrong. "No sooner" requires inversion with the past perfect: "No sooner had she entered the room than the phone rang." This is a frequent examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

50. He said that he ______ the letter the previous day.
(a) posted (b) has posted (c) had posted (d) posts
Answer: (c)
Solution: In reported speech, the simple past of direct speech shifts back to the past perfect; "the previous day" confirms the earlier action, so "had posted" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
