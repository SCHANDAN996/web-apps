Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. She ______ to school every day.
(a) go (b) goes (c) going (d) gone
Answer: (b)
Solution: A daily habit takes the simple present tense; with the third-person singular subject "she", the verb takes "-s", so "goes" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. They ______ football in the park yesterday evening.
(a) play (b) played (c) plays (d) playing
Answer: (b)
Solution: The time marker "yesterday" demands the simple past tense, so "played" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

3. I ______ my homework before my father returned.
(a) had finished (b) finished (c) have finished (d) finish
Answer: (a)
Solution: When two past actions occur, the earlier one takes the past perfect ("had finished") and the later one the simple past ("returned").
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

4. Look! The baby ______.
(a) cries (b) cried (c) is crying (d) has cried
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Look!" signals an action happening at the moment of speaking, which requires the present continuous tense.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

5. We ______ in this city since 2010.
(a) live (b) lived (c) are living (d) have lived
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since 2010" shows an action that began in the past and continues to the present, so the present perfect "have lived" is used.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

6. He ______ his lunch before I reached his house.
(a) has (b) had had (c) will have (d) is having
Answer: (b)
Solution: The earlier of two past actions takes the past perfect; "had had" is the past perfect of "have" (eat).
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

7. The train ______ at 6 a.m. tomorrow.
(a) leaves (b) is leaving (c) will leave (d) left
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fixed timetables and schedules are expressed in the simple present even for future time, so "leaves" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

8. While she ______ a book, the phone rang.
(a) read (b) was reading (c) reads (d) has read
Answer: (b)
Solution: A longer background action interrupted by a shorter one takes the past continuous ("was reading"); the interrupting action takes the simple past ("rang").
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

9. I ______ never ______ the Taj Mahal.
(a) am, seeing (b) had, seen (c) have, seen (d) was, seeing
Answer: (c)
Solution: Life experience up to the present without a definite past time takes the present perfect: "have seen".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

10. By next year, he ______ his degree.
(a) will have completed (b) will complete (c) completes (d) has completed
Answer: (a)
Solution: "By next year" indicates completion before a future point of time, which requires the future perfect tense.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

11. Water ______ at 100 degrees Celsius.
(a) is boiling (b) boiled (c) boils (d) has boiled
Answer: (c)
Solution: Universal truths and scientific facts are always expressed in the simple present tense.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

12. She ______ television when I entered the room.
(a) watches (b) watched (c) was watching (d) has watched
Answer: (c)
Solution: An action in progress at a past moment ("when I entered") takes the past continuous tense.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

13. We ______ the project already.
(a) have finished (b) finished (c) had finished (d) finish
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Already" with no definite past time signals the present perfect tense: "have finished".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

14. He ______ here tomorrow.
(a) will come (b) comes (c) came (d) has come
Answer: (a)
Solution: A simple future action with the marker "tomorrow" takes "will + base verb": "will come".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

15. Spot the error: Part P: I have seen / Part Q: that movie / Part R: last night. / Part S: No error
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part P is wrong because a definite past time ("last night") cannot be used with the present perfect; it should be "I saw that movie last night". This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

16. They ______ cricket every Sunday.
(a) plays (b) play (c) played (d) playing
Answer: (b)
Solution: A regular habit ("every Sunday") with a plural subject takes the simple present "play".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

17. She ______ in Delhi for five years before she moved to Mumbai.
(a) has lived (b) is living (c) lives (d) had lived
Answer: (d)
Solution: An action completed before another past action ("moved") takes the past perfect: "had lived".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

18. Listen! Someone ______ at the door.
(a) is knocking (b) knocked (c) knocks (d) has knocked
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Listen!" indicates an action happening right now, so the present continuous "is knocking" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

19. The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rise (b) is rising (c) rises (d) rose
Answer: (c)
Solution: A universal truth takes the simple present; with the singular subject "sun", the verb is "rises".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

20. I ______ my keys; I cannot find them anywhere.
(a) lose (b) lost (c) have lost (d) had lost
Answer: (c)
Solution: A past action with a present result ("I cannot find them now") takes the present perfect: "have lost".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

21. Spot the error: Part P: He is working / Part Q: in this factory / Part R: since 2015. / Part S: No error
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part P is wrong: "since 2015" shows an action continuing from the past to now, so it needs the present perfect continuous — "He has been working in this factory since 2015". Examiners often pair "since" with a continuous form to trap candidates.
Source: PYQ-style

22. By the time we reached the station, the train ______.
(a) had left (b) has left (c) left (d) leaves
Answer: (a)
Solution: "By the time + simple past" sets a past reference point; the action completed before it takes the past perfect "had left".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

23. She ______ English for two hours when her friends arrived.
(a) studied (b) studies (c) has been studying (d) had been studying
Answer: (d)
Solution: A duration ("for two hours") before another past event ("arrived") takes the past perfect continuous: "had been studying".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

24. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) I am knowing the answer. (b) I know the answer. (c) I was knowing the answer. (d) I have been knowing the answer.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Know" is a stative verb and is not used in continuous forms; the simple present "I know the answer" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

25. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) did I reach (c) I reached (d) had I reached
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "Hardly…when", the sentence uses inversion with the past perfect: "Hardly had I reached…when the train left."
Source: PYQ-style
