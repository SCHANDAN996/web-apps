📋 महत्वपूर्ण व्याकरण नियम और टिप्स — काल (Tense)

═══════════════════════════════════
(क) 12 हाई-यील्ड स्वर्ण नियम
═══════════════════════════════════

| क्र. | नियम | उदाहरण | परीक्षक का जाल |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Universal truth / Scientific fact हमेशा Simple Present में — भले मुख्य वाक्य Past में हो। | The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun. | "The teacher said that the earth revolved…" — revolved लिखकर गलत करना। |
| 2 | Since + point of time (Monday, 2010, childhood); For + period of time (two years, a decade) — दोनों Perfect/Perfect Continuous के साथ। | He has been working here since 2015. / for ten years. | "He is working here since 2015" — since के साथ Simple Present गलत। |
| 3 | Present Perfect में definite past time (yesterday, ago, last year) नहीं आता — Simple Past लगाओ। | I have finished the work. / I finished it yesterday. | "I have met him yesterday" — yesterday के साथ have met गलत। |
| 4 | By the time / Before + Simple Past → मुख्य क्रिया Past Perfect में (पहले हुई क्रिया)। | The train had left before I reached the station. | दोनों क्रियाओं को Simple Past में लिखना — क्रम भ्रमित होता है। |
| 5 | When + Simple Past, मुख्य clause Past Continuous — चल रही क्रिया में घटना घुसी। | I was sleeping when the phone rang. | "I slept when the phone was ringing" — अर्थ उलट जाता है। |
| 6 | If clause (Present) + Main clause (Future) — Conditional Type-1 में if-भाग में will नहीं। | If it rains, I will stay home. | "If it will rain, I will stay" — if के साथ will गलत। |
| 7 | Wish / If only / Would that → Past (Present की कल्पना) या Past Perfect (Past की कल्पना)। | I wish I were a bird. / I wish I had worked harder. | "I wish I was" — औपचारिक परीक्षा में were ही सही। |
| 8 | It is time / It is high time + Past Tense (काम अभी तक नहीं हुआ)। | It is high time we left. | "It is high time we leave" — leave गलत, left सही। |
| 9 | Hardly/Scarcely … when और No sooner … than — पहली क्रिया Past Perfect में। | No sooner had she arrived than the show began. | "No sooner she had arrived…" — inversion (had she) छोड़ना गलत। |
| 10 | Future की घोषित योजना/समय-सारणी (timetable) Simple Present में। | The train leaves at 6 a.m. tomorrow. | "The train will leave at 6" — timetable में will अनावश्यक। |
| 11 | Present Continuous = निकट भविष्य की तय योजना (व्यक्तिगत arrangement)। | I am meeting the manager tomorrow. | "I will meet him tomorrow" गलत नहीं, पर विकल्प में "am meeting" हो तो arrangement का संकेत चुनो। |
| 12 | Past Perfect के बिना before/after भी क्रम साफ़ कर देते हैं — पर दोनों Past में हों तो पहली क्रिया had + V3 सुरक्षित। | He had locked the door before he went out. | "After he had eaten, he has gone out" — has gone (Present Perfect) Past के साथ नहीं जुड़ता। |

═══════════════════════════════════
(ख) समय प्रबंधन — 15/45 सेकंड नियम
═══════════════════════════════════

⏱️ 15 सेकंड — तथ्यात्मक (Fact-based) प्रश्न
ये नियम-यादी प्रश्न हैं; देखते ही उत्तर आना चाहिए:
- since/for का चुनाव, yesterday/ago के साथ tense पहचानना।
- Universal truth वाले वाक्य (revolves/revolved)।
- It is high time + Past, wish + were जैसे fixed patterns।
- Timetable-Future (The train leaves at 6)।
👉 रणनीति: signal word (since, ago, by the time, no sooner) पकड़ो → नियम लगाओ → आगे बढ़ो। 15 सेकंड से ज़्यादा मत दो।

⏱️ 45 सेकंड — तार्किक (Inference-based) प्रश्न
ये संदर्भ-समझ माँगते हैं; जल्दबाज़ी में गलत होते हैं:
- दो क्रियाओं का कालक्रम तय करना (कौन-सी पहले हुई?) — Past Perfect बनाम Simple Past।
- Error-spotting जहाँ चारों underlined भाग देखने हैं।
- Conditional sentences (Type 1/2/3) जहाँ अर्थ से काल्पनिकता पहचाननी है।
- Reported speech में tense-shift वाले लंबे वाक्य।
👉 रणनीति: वाक्य को दो क्रियाओं में तोड़ो → समय-रेखा (timeline) मन में बनाओ → फिर विकल्प मिलाओ। पूरे 45 सेकंड लगाने में संकोच नहीं।

═══════════════════════════════════
(ग) रूपात्मक सूत्र (Morphology) — Tense की Verb-संरचना
═══════════════════════════════════

| काल | सूत्र (Formula) | उदाहरण |
|---|---|---|
| Simple Present | V1 (he/she/it के साथ V1+s/es) | She writes a letter. |
| Present Continuous | is/am/are + V1+ing | They are playing. |
| Present Perfect | has/have + V3 | He has eaten. |
| Present Perfect Continuous | has/have + been + V1+ing | She has been reading since morning. |
| Simple Past | V2 | I wrote a letter. |
| Past Continuous | was/were + V1+ing | We were sleeping. |
| Past Perfect | had + V3 | The train had left. |
| Past Perfect Continuous | had + been + V1+ing | He had been waiting for two hours. |
| Simple Future | will/shall + V1 | I will go tomorrow. |
| Future Continuous | will/shall + be + V1+ing | She will be travelling at this time. |
| Future Perfect | will/shall + have + V3 | They will have finished by 5 p.m. |
| Future Perfect Continuous | will/shall + have + been + V1+ing | By 2026, he will have been working here for a decade. |

🔑 याद रखने की कुंजी:
- Continuous = be + ing (चल रहा है)
- Perfect = have + V3 (पूरा हो चुका)
- Perfect Continuous = have + been + ing (समय से चला आ रहा)
- Past में have → had, Future में have → will have — बस यही तीन बदलाव पूरे 12 काल बना देते हैं।
