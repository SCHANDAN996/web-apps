Questions 51–60: Easy | Questions 61–75: Medium

51. She ______ her homework before the guests arrived.
(a) finished (b) finishes (c) has finished (d) had finished
Answer: (d)
Solution: Two past actions are shown; the earlier action takes the past perfect. Finishing homework happened before the guests arrived, so "had finished" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. Water ______ at 100°C at sea level.
(a) boiled (b) boils (c) is boiling (d) has boiled
Answer: (b)
Solution: Universal truths and scientific facts are expressed in the simple present tense, so "boils" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

53. Look! The children ______ in the garden.
(a) are playing (b) played (c) play (d) have played
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Look!" signals an action happening right now, which requires the present continuous tense.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

54. I ______ this city since 2015.
(a) live in (b) am living in (c) lived in (d) have lived in
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since 2015" indicates an action that began in the past and continues to the present, so the present perfect is used.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

55. The train ______ at 6 a.m. every day.
(a) is leaving (b) has left (c) leaves (d) left
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fixed timetables and daily routines take the simple present tense.

56. While I ______ TV, the lights went off.
(a) watch (b) watched (c) was watching (d) had watched
Answer: (c)
Solution: A longer background action interrupted by a shorter one takes the past continuous; the interrupting action takes the simple past.

57. He ______ his lunch already.
(a) ate (b) has eaten (c) eats (d) is eating
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Already" with a recently completed action whose result matters now calls for the present perfect.

58. We ______ to Shimla last summer.
(a) go (b) have gone (c) went (d) are going
Answer: (c)
Solution: A definite past time marker like "last summer" requires the simple past, never the present perfect.

59. By next year, she ______ her degree.
(a) completes (b) will have completed (c) completed (d) is completing
Answer: (b)
Solution: "By next year" points to an action that will be finished before a future point, so the future perfect is used.

60. My father ______ the newspaper every morning.
(a) read (b) is reading (c) has read (d) reads
Answer: (d)

61. Spot the error: Part P: I have been / Part Q: knowing him / Part R: for ten years. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Know" is a stative verb and is not normally used in continuous forms. Part Q should read "known him" — "I have known him for ten years."

62. Spot the error: Part P: He is working / Part Q: in this bank / Part R: since 2010. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: With "since 2010", the action started in the past and continues, so the present perfect continuous is needed: "He has been working". Part P is wrong.
Source: PYQ-style

63. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) I have had a car since 2018. (b) I have a car since 2018. (c) I am having a car since 2018. (d) I had a car since 2018.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Possession continuing from a past point to now takes the present perfect: "have had". "Have" in the sense of possession is stative, so the continuous form in the first option is wrong.
Source: PYQ-style

64. When I reached the station, the train ______.
(a) already left (b) already leaves (c) has already left (d) had already left
Answer: (d)
Solution: The train's departure happened before the reaching, so the earlier of two past actions takes the past perfect.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

65. Spot the error: Part P: No sooner did she enter the room / Part Q: when the phone rang. / Part R: / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: "No sooner" is paired with "than", not "when". Part Q should read "than the phone rang". This is a classic examiner trap testing correlative conjunctions.

66. By the time the police arrived, the thief ______.

67. She ______ here tomorrow at this time.
(a) will be working (b) will work (c) works (d) has worked

68. Spot the error: Part P: I wish / Part Q: I was / Part R: a millionaire. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "I wish" expressing an unreal present desire, the subjunctive "were" is used for all persons: "I wish I were a millionaire." Part Q is wrong.

69. Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) had I reached (c) I reached (d) did I reach
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "Hardly" at the start of a clause, inversion is required, and the earlier action takes the past perfect: "Hardly had I reached… when the train left."

70. He ______ English for five years before he moved to London.

Solution: "Unless" itself means "if not", so adding "do not" creates a double negative. Part P should read "Unless you work hard". This is a frequent examiner trap.

Solution: A definite past time adverb like "yesterday" cannot be used with the present perfect; the simple past is required.

74. The meeting ______ by the time we reach the office.

Answer: (b)

Answer: (d)
Solution: When "Scarcely" begins a sentence, inversion is compulsory: "Scarcely had he stepped out". Part P lacks inversion and is wrong.
