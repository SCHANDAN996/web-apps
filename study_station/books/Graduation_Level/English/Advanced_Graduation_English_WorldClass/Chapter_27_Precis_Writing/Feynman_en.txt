🧒 Feynman Technique — Precis Writing Explained to a 12-Year-Old

Imagine your mom sends you to the market with a long shopping list. She talks for five minutes: "Beta, first go to Sharma ji's shop, but if he's closed go to the other one, and remember last time the tomatoes were bad so check them properly, and oh, buy coriander only if it's fresh, and don't forget the milk — full cream, not toned — and also…"

You don't carry her five-minute speech in your head. You carry this: **milk (full cream), tomatoes (check quality), coriander (if fresh).**

That is exactly what a precis is. A long passage is your mom's five-minute speech. Your precis is the short shopping list — everything that matters, nothing that doesn't, in YOUR OWN words.

**The One Unforgettable Trick: "The WhatsApp Forward Test"**

Suppose you read a 300-word passage and want to forward its message to a friend who has only 10 seconds. You would:
1. Read the whole thing once (don't start writing yet!).
2. Ask: "What is the ONE thing the writer wants me to know?" — that's the **title**.
3. Ask: "What are the 3–4 big points supporting it?" — those are your **sentences**.
4. Delete: examples, stories, repetitions, quotes, figures of speech. These are decoration, not the message.
5. Write it in your own words, in about **one-third** the length, in a single connected paragraph.

If your friend reads your forward and understands the whole passage without reading it — you passed. If they ask "but what about the main point?" — you failed.

**The 3 Golden Don'ts (explained simply):**
- **Don't copy sentences** from the passage — that's like reading out mom's whole speech again. Use your own words.
- **Don't add your opinion** — "I think the writer is right" is NOT allowed. You are a messenger, not a judge.
- **Don't write in points or bullets** — a precis is one flowing paragraph, not a list.

**The Length Rule:** If the passage is 300 words, your precis should be roughly 100 words (one-third). Too short = you dropped a main point. Too long = you kept decoration.

📝 **Blurting Sheet Instruction:**
Close this book. Take a blank sheet and in 90 seconds blurt out everything you remember: the WhatsApp Forward Test steps, the 3 Don'ts, and the one-third length rule. Then open the book and check what you missed — that gap is exactly what you must revise tonight.

**Question for you:** A 240-word passage contains a story about a farmer, two statistics, and a repeated warning about water scarcity. How many words should your precis be, and which parts will you delete first? (Answer: ~80 words; delete the story, the statistics, and the repetition of the warning — the story and statistics are examples, and saying the same warning twice is repetition. Keep the warning itself once, because it is part of the message.)
