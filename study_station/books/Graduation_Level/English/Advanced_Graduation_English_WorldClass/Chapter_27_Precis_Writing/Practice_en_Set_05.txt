101. Which of the following is the most appropriate length of a precis of a 300-word passage?
(a) About 30 words (b) About 200 words (c) About 100 words (d) About 250 words
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 200 words, which many candidates assume because "two-thirds" sounds safe; but the standard rule is that a precis should be roughly one-third of the original passage. One-third of 300 words is about 100 words, so the answer is about 100 words.

102. A good precis title should be —
(a) a complete sentence summarising the whole passage (b) a long phrase covering every detail of the passage (c) the first line of the passage copied exactly (d) a short phrase expressing the central theme of the passage
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is choosing a complete sentence, which feels thorough but violates the rule that a title must be brief — a phrase, not a sentence. Copying the first line is also wrong because the opening line may not carry the central idea. A short phrase capturing the theme is correct, so the answer is a short phrase expressing the central theme of the passage.
Source: PYQ-style

103. While writing a precis, which of the following must be strictly avoided?
(a) Using your own words (b) Using indirect speech (c) Writing in the third person (d) Adding your personal opinion on the topic
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap lies in thinking "using your own words" is wrong; in fact, a precis must be in your own words. What is forbidden is inserting personal opinions, comments or criticism, because a precis is a faithful summary of the author's ideas only, so the answer is adding your personal opinion on the topic.

104. A passage is written in the first person ("I believe that hard work…"). In the precis, this should be written as —
(a) "I believe that hard work…" (b) "The author believes that hard work…" (c) "We believe that hard work…" (d) "You believe that hard work…"
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting error is retaining "I", but a precis is always written in the third person and in indirect (reported) speech. The writer of the passage becomes "the author" or "the writer", so the answer is "The author believes that hard work…".

105. Which of the following should normally be omitted in a precis?
(a) The main argument of the passage (b) Illustrations, examples and repetitions (c) The author's conclusion (d) The central theme
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is omitting the conclusion, which some candidates cut to save words; but the conclusion is essential to the summary. Illustrations, examples, quotations and repetitions are supporting material and must be dropped, so the answer is illustrations, examples and repetitions.

106. The tone of a passage that mocks human folly by pretending to praise it is best described as —
(a) laudatory (b) didactic (c) pessimistic (d) ironic
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is "laudatory" because the surface words sound like praise; but praise used to expose folly is irony. "Didactic" means intended to teach, which does not fit mockery, so the answer is ironic.

(a) false, because a precis must be plain, concise and free of borrowed ornament (b) true, because figures of speech improve style (c) true, because quotations add authority (d) false, because a precis must be longer than the original
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is believing quotations add authority; in a precis, quotations, metaphors and rhetorical flourishes are removed and expressed in plain language. The statement is false because a precis must be plain and concise, so the answer is that it is false since a precis must be plain, concise and free of borrowed ornament.

108. Which of these is the correct order of steps in writing a precis?

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the option that begins with writing the final draft; no summary can be written before understanding the passage. (a) acceptable if the passage is a speech (b) a serious error, as a precis must use reported speech (c) acceptable if the word limit permits (d) preferred in competitive examinations

Solution: The tempting wrong choice is that direct speech is acceptable for a speech passage; examiners, however, uniformly require indirect (reported) speech in a precis regardless of the original form. Answer: (a)

112. Which sentence is the best precis of: "Despite repeated warnings from doctors, Ramesh, who was a chain smoker, continued to smoke twenty cigarettes a day, and consequently developed a serious lung disease which ultimately proved fatal"?
(a) Ramesh smoked cigarettes and died (b) Ignoring medical advice, Ramesh's heavy smoking caused a fatal lung disease (c) Doctors warned Ramesh many times about smoking but he was stubborn and foolish (d) Smoking twenty cigarettes daily is always fatal for everyone
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the option "Ramesh smoked cigarettes and died", which is concise but loses the key causal links — the ignored warnings and the disease. The option with "stubborn and foolish" adds opinion, which is forbidden. The version retaining cause and consequence in compressed form is best, so the answer is "Ignoring medical advice, Ramesh's heavy smoking caused a fatal lung disease".
Source: PYQ-style

113. An examiner deducts marks in precis writing mainly when a candidate —

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is thinking simple vocabulary is penalised; clarity and simplicity are actually rewarded. Introducing outside ideas or personal views is a fundamental violation of precis writing, so the answer is includes ideas not found in the original passage.

Solution: The trap is choosing the example-filled passage, but examples and repetitions are exactly what get cut, making such passages easy to compress. 121. Which of the following best describes the "Detective Approach" to identifying the main idea of a passage?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is relying on the last line, since conclusions sometimes introduce a fresh remark. The main idea is the one that recurs and to which all other sentences are subordinate — like clues pointing to one culprit, so the answer is looking for the idea the author repeatedly returns to and which all other points support.
