51. Which of the following is a proper noun?
(a) Ganga (b) honesty (c) city (d) team
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Ganga" names a specific river, so it is a proper noun and is capitalised. "City" is common, "honesty" is abstract, and "team" is collective.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

52. Identify the abstract noun in: "Her kindness touched everyone."
(a) her (b) kindness (c) touched (d) everyone
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Kindness" is a quality that cannot be seen or touched, so it is an abstract noun. "Everyone" is a pronoun, not a noun.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

53. The plural of "child" is:
(a) childs (b) children (c) childes (d) childrens
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Child" has an irregular plural — "children". "Childrens" is doubly wrong because "children" is already plural.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

54. Which noun is uncountable?
(a) book (b) furniture (c) apple (d) chair
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Furniture" is uncountable in English; we say "a piece of furniture", never "furnitures". The others are countable.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

55. Identify the collective noun: "A ______ of wolves attacked the deer."
(a) swarm (b) flock (c) pack (d) bunch
Answer: (c)
Solution: Wolves move in a "pack". A "swarm" is of bees, a "flock" of sheep/birds, a "bunch" of keys or grapes.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

56. The plural of "knife" is:
(a) knifes (b) knife (c) knivs (d) knives
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nouns ending in -fe often change to -ves: knife → knives, wife → wives, life → lives.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

57. Which of these is a material noun?
(a) beauty (b) ring (c) gold (d) army
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Gold" is a material noun — the substance from which things are made. "Ring" is a common noun (a finished product), "beauty" is abstract, "army" is collective.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

58. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He gave me much advice. (b) He gave me many advice. (c) He gave me many advices. (d) He gave me an advice.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Advice" is uncountable, so it takes "much", not "many" or "an". The countable form is "a piece of advice".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

59. The plural of "phenomenon" is:
(a) phenomenons (b) phenomenas (c) phenomena (d) phenomenones
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Phenomenon" is a Greek word with the plural "phenomena". Similar pairs: criterion–criteria, medium–media.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

60. Identify the common noun in: "The teacher gave the boy a book."
(a) the (b) gave (c) teacher (d) All nouns here are proper
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Teacher", "boy" and "book" are all common nouns; "teacher" is the one listed. "Gave" is a verb and "the" is an article.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

61. Choose the correctly spelt plural:
(a) tomatos (b) heroes (c) potatos (d) echos
Answer: (b)
Solution: Most nouns ending in -o take -es: hero → heroes, potato → potatoes, tomato → tomatoes, echo → echoes. Only (b) is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

62. "The committee ______ divided in their opinion."
(a) was (b) has (c) is (d) were
Answer: (d)
Solution: When a collective noun's members act separately (divided in opinion), it takes a plural verb — "were". If the committee acted as one body, "was" would be correct.
Source: PYQ-style

63. Which sentence is correct?
(a) The scissor is on the table. (b) A scissors is on the table. (c) The scissors is on the table. (d) The scissors are on the table.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Scissors" is always plural (like trousers, spectacles, pliers) and takes a plural verb. To count it, say "a pair of scissors".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

64. "Mathematics ______ a difficult subject for many students."
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: Names of subjects ending in -ics (mathematics, physics, economics) are singular and take a singular verb. Examiner's trap: the -s ending tempts students to choose a plural verb.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Choose the correct possessive form:
(a) the men's room (b) the mens room (c) the mens' room (d) the men room
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Men" is already plural without -s, so the possessive is 's — "men's". For plurals ending in -s we add only an apostrophe (girls' school).
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

66. "A pair of shoes ______ been kept outside the door."
(a) have (b) were (c) are (d) has
Answer: (d)
Solution: The subject is "a pair" (singular), not "shoes", so the verb is singular — "has". Trap: the nearer word "shoes" misleads students into choosing "have".
Source: PYQ-style

67. Which of the following has an irregular plural?
(a) box (b) dog (c) pen (d) ox
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Ox" becomes "oxen" — an old English plural. The others take regular plurals: dogs, pens, boxes.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

68. "The news ______ shocking."
(a) is (b) were (c) are (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" looks plural but is uncountable and singular — it always takes a singular verb. Same with "innings" and "billiards".
Source: PYQ-style

69. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I need some informations. (b) I need an information. (c) I need some information. (d) I need many informations.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable; it has no plural and cannot take "an". Use "some information" or "a piece of information".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

70. "One of my friends ______ a doctor."
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: The real subject is "one" (singular); "of my friends" is only an intervening phrase. So the verb is "is". This is the classic intervening-phrase trap.
Source: PYQ-style

71. The feminine gender of "stag" is:
(a) hind (b) doe (c) mare (d) vixen
Answer: (a)
Solution: The feminine of "stag" is "hind". "Doe" pairs with buck, "mare" with horse, and "vixen" with fox.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

72. "The cattle ______ grazing in the field."
(a) is (b) was (c) has been (d) are
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Cattle" is a plural noun with no singular form in ordinary use; it takes a plural verb. Similar words: people, police, poultry.
Source: PYQ-style

73. Choose the correct form: "He is a man of few ______."
(a) word (b) words (c) wordings (d) word's
Answer: (b)
Solution: The idiom is "a man of few words" — someone who speaks little. "Words" here means speech, and the plural is required.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

74. "The jury ______ given its verdict."
(a) have (b) has (c) are (d) were
Answer: (b)
Solution: Here the jury acts as one body delivering a single verdict, so the collective noun takes a singular verb — "has". "Have" would fit only if members acted individually.
Source: PYQ-style

75. Which sentence uses the noun correctly?
(a) He took pains in his work. (b) He took pain in his work. (c) He took paining in his work. (d) He took the pain in his work.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "To take pains" (plural) means to make great effort — the fixed idiomatic form. Some nouns change meaning in the plural: pain (suffering) vs pains (effort).
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
