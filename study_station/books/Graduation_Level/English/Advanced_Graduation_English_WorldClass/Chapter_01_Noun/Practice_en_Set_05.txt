101. The committee ______ divided in their opinion on the new policy.
(a) was (b) were (c) is (d) has been
Answer: (b)
Solution: When a collective noun's members act individually (divided in opinion), it takes a plural verb. "Were" is correct. Option (a) tempts because "committee" looks singular, but division among members demands plural.
Source: PYQ-style

102. He gave me ______ useful piece of advice before the interview.
(a) many (b) an (c) a (d) few
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Piece" is countable, and "useful" begins with a consonant sound /juː/, so "a" is correct. The "an" trap relies on the spelling (vowel letter "u"), not the sound; "many/few" cannot precede a singular noun.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

103. The ______ of the building was completed in record time.
(a) constructed (b) construction (c) constructing (d) construct
Answer: (b)
Solution: The definite article "the" must be followed by a noun. "Construction" is the noun form; the others are verb forms. Examiner's trap: "constructing" looks noun-like but functions as a verb/gerund needing an object here.
Source: PYQ-style

104. ______ of the two boys has passed the examination.
(a) Both (b) Each (c) All (d) Every
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Each" is used for two or more and takes a singular verb. "Every" is used for more than two; "all" and "both" take plural verbs. Trap: "both" sounds natural but clashes with "has".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

105. The scenery of Kashmir ______ very beautiful.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Scenery" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb. Trap: its plural-sounding meaning lures students into "are".
Source: PYQ-style

106. She has bought two ______ of shoes.
(a) pair (b) pairs (c) pairing (d) paired
Answer: (b)
Solution: After a numeral greater than one, the noun "pair" takes the plural form: "two pairs of shoes". Trap: some nouns like "dozen" stay singular after numerals, but "pair" does not.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

107. The cattle ______ grazing in the field.
(a) is (b) was (c) has been (d) are
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Cattle" is a plural noun with no singular form in this sense; it always takes a plural verb. Trap: absence of "-s" makes it look singular.
Source: PYQ-style

108. His ______ in the matter is not known to anyone.
(a) interfere (b) interfered (c) interfering (d) interference
Answer: (d)
Solution: The possessive adjective "his" must be followed by a noun — "interference". The other options are verb forms. Trap: "interfering" is a gerund but does not fit the possessive-noun pattern here naturally.
Source: PYQ-style

109. Mathematics ______ a difficult subject for many students.
(a) have been (b) were (c) is (d) are
Answer: (c)
Solution: Names of subjects ending in "-ics" (Mathematics, Physics, Economics) take a singular verb when they denote a subject of study. Trap: the "-s" ending suggests plural.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

110. The ______ of the two sisters is a doctor.
(a) elder (b) older (c) eldest (d) oldest
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Elder" is used for members of the same family and is followed by "of" here, not "than". With only two sisters, comparative "elder" is correct; superlatives need three or more.
Source: PYQ-style

111. I need some ______ about the new scheme.
(a) information (b) an information (c) informations (d) the informations
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable — no plural form and no indefinite article. "Some information" is correct. Trap: Hindi speakers often pluralize it as "informations".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

112. The jury ______ given its verdict unanimously.
(a) has (b) have (c) are (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Unanimously" shows the jury acted as one body, so the collective noun takes a singular verb "has". Trap: "jury" often takes plural verbs when members disagree — read the context word.
Source: PYQ-style

113. He is one of the best ______ in the team.
(a) played (b) player (c) playing (d) players
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "one of the + superlative", the noun must be plural: "one of the best players". Trap: "one" misleads students into choosing the singular "player".
Source: PYQ-style

114. The news ______ broadcast at nine o'clock last night.
(a) was (b) are (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" is uncountable and singular despite its "-s" ending; past time ("last night") requires "was". Trap: the "-s" suggests plural.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

115. Many a student ______ failed in this examination.
(a) has (b) have (c) are (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Many a + singular noun" takes a singular verb: "many a student has failed". Trap: the meaning is plural, but the grammatical form is singular.
Source: PYQ-style

116. The ______ of the accident victims were taken to hospital.
(a) remaining (b) remain (c) remains (d) remained
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Remains" (noun, meaning what is left / the bodies) is always plural in form and takes a plural verb "were". Trap: "remains" as a verb would be singular, but here it is a noun.
Source: PYQ-style

117. She gave me ______ advice that proved very valuable.
(a) an (b) a piece of (c) many (d) few
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Advice" is uncountable; it cannot take "an" or be counted with "many/few". We count it as "a piece of advice". Trap: "an advice" is a classic Hindi-speaker error.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

118. The rich ______ not always happy.
(a) has been (b) was (c) is (d) are
Answer: (d)
Solution: "The + adjective" (the rich, the poor) denotes a whole class of people and takes a plural verb. Trap: no "-s" ending makes it look singular.
Source: PYQ-style

119. Neither of the two answers ______ correct.
(a) are (b) were (c) have been (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb in formal English: "neither of the answers is correct". Trap: the plural noun "answers" just before the verb pulls students toward "are" (intervening-phrase trap).
Source: PYQ-style

120. The furniture in this room ______ very old.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Furniture" is uncountable and always singular. The intervening phrase "in this room" must not affect the verb. Trap: students match the verb with "room" or treat furniture as countable items.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

121. He has a large ______ of books on history.
(a) collect (b) collected (c) collecting (d) collection
Answer: (d)
Solution: The article "a" and adjective "large" must be followed by a noun — "collection". The other options are verb forms. Trap: "collecting" is a gerund but cannot follow "a large".
Source: PYQ-style

122. The poet and philosopher ______ dead.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: A single article "the" before two nouns means one person holds both roles, so a singular verb "is" is used. Trap: two nouns joined by "and" usually take a plural verb — but only when both have articles.
Source: PYQ-style

123. All the ______ in the factory went on strike.
(a) workmen (b) workman (c) workmans (d) working men
Answer: (a)
Solution: The plural of "workman" is "workmen" (irregular plural like man–men). "Workmans" is a fabricated form — a common examiner's trap.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

124. The gentry of the town ______ invited to the function.
(a) is (b) were (c) was (d) has been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Gentry" is a plural collective noun (like cattle, people) and takes a plural verb "were". Trap: it has no "-s" and looks singular.
Source: PYQ-style

125. The number of road accidents ______ increasing every year.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "The number of" takes a singular verb (the number itself is rising); "a number of" would take a plural verb. Trap: the plural noun "accidents" right before the verb lures students to "are" (intervening-phrase trap).
Source: PYQ-style
