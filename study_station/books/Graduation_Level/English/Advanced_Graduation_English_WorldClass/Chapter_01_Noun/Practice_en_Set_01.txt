1. Which of the following is a proper noun?
(a) city (b) river (c) mountain (d) Ganga
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Ganga" names a specific river, so it is a proper noun and is capitalised. "City", "river" and "mountain" are common nouns.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

2. Identify the collective noun in: "A flock of birds flew over the field."
(a) birds (b) flew (c) field (d) flock
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Flock" denotes a group of birds, so it is a collective noun. "Birds" is a common noun, "flew" is a verb.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

3. Which of the following is an abstract noun?
(a) table (b) honesty (c) teacher (d) garden
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Honesty" is a quality that cannot be seen or touched, so it is an abstract noun. The others are concrete common nouns.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

4. The plural of "child" is:
(a) childs (b) childes (c) childrens (d) children
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Child" has an irregular plural — "children". "Childrens" is doubly wrong because "children" is already plural.
Source: NCERT Class 5 English Grammar

5. The plural of "knife" is:
(a) knifes (b) knives (c) knife (d) knivess
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nouns ending in -fe usually change to -ves: knife → knives, wife → wives. "Knifes" is a common error.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

6. Which noun is always used in the plural form?
(a) news (b) information (c) mathematics (d) scissors
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Scissors" (like trousers, spectacles) exists only in plural form and takes a plural verb. "News" and "mathematics" look plural but are singular.
Source: PYQ-style

7. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The news is good. (b) The news are good. (c) The news were good. (d) The news have been good.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" is singular in meaning despite ending in -s, so it takes "is". This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

8. Which of the following is an uncountable noun?
(a) water (b) pen (c) book (d) chair
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Water" cannot be counted (we say "some water", not "a water"). Book, pen and chair are countable.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

9. The plural of "ox" is:
(a) oxes (b) ox (c) oxen (d) oxens
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Ox" has an old irregular plural "oxen". "Oxes" and "oxens" are incorrect forms.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

10. Identify the material noun: "This ring is made of gold."
(a) ring (b) gold (c) made (d) this
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Gold" is a material noun — the name of a substance. "Ring" is a common noun; "made" is a verb.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

11. Which sentence uses "information" correctly?
(a) He gave me useful information. (b) He gave me an information. (c) He gave me many informations. (d) He gave me two informations.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable — no plural form and no "a/an" before it. We say "a piece of information" if counting is needed.
Source: PYQ-style

12. The feminine form of "king" is:
(a) queen (b) kingess (c) kingwoman (d) lady king
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Queen" is the feminine gender of "king" — a completely different word (like bull/cow, horse/mare).
Source: NCERT Class 5 English Grammar

13. "Team" in "Our team won the match" is a:
(a) proper noun (b) collective noun (c) abstract noun (d) material noun
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Team" names a group of players acting as one unit, so it is a collective noun.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

14. The plural of "tooth" is:
(a) tooths (b) toothes (c) teeth (d) tooth
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Tooth → teeth" is an irregular plural formed by vowel change (like foot → feet, goose → geese).
Source: NCERT Class 5 English Grammar

15. Which of the following is a countable noun?
(a) milk (b) apple (c) furniture (d) advice
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Apple" can be counted (one apple, two apples). Milk, furniture and advice are uncountable.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

16. Choose the correctly spelt plural:
(a) potatos (b) potato (c) potatoes (d) potaties
Answer: (c)
Solution: Nouns ending in -o preceded by a consonant usually add -es: potato → potatoes, hero → heroes. "Potatos" is a frequent exam trap.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

17. "Honesty is the best policy." Here "honesty" is:
(a) common noun (b) abstract noun (c) proper noun (d) collective noun
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Honesty" is a quality/idea that cannot be perceived by the senses — an abstract noun.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

18. The plural of "sheep" is:
(a) sheeps (b) shoop (c) sheepes (d) sheep
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Sheep" has the same form in singular and plural (like deer, fish). "Sheeps" is a common error.
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

19. Which word is a proper noun?
(a) country (b) boy (c) India (d) happiness
Answer: (c)
Solution: "India" names a specific country, so it is a proper noun. "Happiness" is abstract; "country" and "boy" are common nouns.
Source: NCERT Class 5 English Grammar

20. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I need some advices. (b) I need an advice. (c) I need some advice. (d) I need advices.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Advice" is uncountable — never "advices" or "an advice". Say "some advice" or "a piece of advice".
Source: PYQ-style

21. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
(a) The furniture in this room is new. (b) The furniture in this room are new. (c) The furnitures in this room are new. (d) The furnitures in this room is new.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Furniture" is uncountable and always singular — no plural form, singular verb "is". Options with "furnitures" or "are" are wrong.
Source: PYQ-style

22. "The jury ______ divided in their opinion."
(a) were (b) is (c) was (d) has
Answer: (a)
Solution: When a collective noun's members act separately (divided opinion), it takes a plural verb — "were". "Was" would be correct only if the jury acted as one unit. This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) My luggage was lost at the station. (b) He gave me many luggages. (c) My luggages were lost. (d) I have a luggage.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Luggage" is uncountable — no plural, no "a", and it takes a singular verb "was". (Note: "advice" is uncountable, but "advise" is the verb.)
Source: PYQ-style

24. "Mathematics ______ a difficult subject for many students."
(a) are (b) were (c) have been (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: Subjects ending in -ics (mathematics, physics, economics) are singular when they mean the subject of study, so "is" is correct. The -s ending is the examiner's trap.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Choose the correctly punctuated possessive: "The ______ toys are scattered everywhere." (the toys belong to two children)
(a) childrens' (b) childrens (c) children's (d) childs'
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Children" is already plural, so the possessive adds 's → "children's". "Childrens'" wrongly pluralises an already plural word — a favourite examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style
