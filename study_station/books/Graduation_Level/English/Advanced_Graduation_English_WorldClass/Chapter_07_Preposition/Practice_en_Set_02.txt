Questions 26–40: Easy | Questions 41–50: Medium

26. He has been living in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) since (b) for (c) from (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (2015, Monday, January); "for" is used with a period of time (two years). 2015 is a point of time, so "since" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. The cat is hiding ______ the table.
(a) on (b) in (c) under (d) at
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Under" shows position below something. The cat is below the table, so "under" fits. "On" would mean on top of it.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

28. She is afraid ______ spiders.
(a) by (b) from (c) with (d) of
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Afraid of" is the fixed prepositional phrase. "Afraid with" or "afraid from" are common Hindi-influenced errors.
Source: PYQ-style

29. We reached the station ______ time to catch the train.
(a) by (b) on (c) at (d) in
Answer: (d)
Solution: "In time" means early enough for something; "on time" means exactly at the scheduled time. Here the sense is "early enough to catch the train", so "in time" is correct.
Source: PYQ-style

30. He jumped ______ the river to save the child.
(a) in (b) on (c) into (d) upon
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Into" shows movement from outside to inside; "in" shows position. Jumping involves movement, so "into" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

31. The train arrives ______ 6 o'clock.
(a) at (b) in (c) on (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "At" is used with exact clock time. "In" is for months, years and parts of the day; "on" is for days and dates.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

32. My birthday falls ______ 15th August.
(a) in (b) on (c) at (d) by
Answer: (b)
Solution: "On" is used with specific dates and days. "In" would be used for the month alone (in August).
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

33. He is senior ______ me by two years.
(a) to (b) from (c) than (d) over
Answer: (a)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior and prefer take "to", not "than". This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

34. The book belongs ______ me.
(a) with (b) for (c) of (d) to
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Belong to" is the fixed phrase showing possession. Note that "belong" is also never used in the continuous form.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

35. She has been suffering ______ fever since Monday.
(a) from (b) of (c) with (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Suffer from" is used with diseases and ailments. "Suffer with" is a frequent error made by Hindi speakers.
Source: PYQ-style

36. He was killed ______ a knife.
(a) by (b) through (c) with (d) from
Answer: (c)
Solution: "With" is used for the instrument; "by" is used for the doer or agent. The knife is the instrument, so "with" is correct. Compare: killed by the robber.
Source: PYQ-style

37. The children are fond ______ chocolates.
(a) for (b) of (c) with (d) about
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Fond of" is the fixed adjective-preposition combination. No other preposition is idiomatic here.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

38. He apologized ______ his rude behaviour.
(a) on (b) at (c) about (d) for
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Apologize for" something is correct; "apologize to" a person. Here the object is the behaviour, so "for" is used.
Source: PYQ-style

39. There is a post office ______ the corner of this street.
(a) in (b) on (c) by (d) at
Answer: (d)
Solution: "At the corner" is the standard usage for a point or spot on a street. "In the corner" is used for the inside corner of a room, which does not fit a street.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

40. I am looking forward ______ meeting you.
(a) for (b) to (c) at (d) towards
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Look forward to" is a phrasal preposition, and it is followed by a gerund (meeting), not an infinitive. A classic trap.
Source: PYQ-style

41. Spot the error: Part P: He is good / Part Q: in mathematics / Part R: but weak in English. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part Q has the error. The correct phrase is "good at", not "good in". Part R correctly uses "weak in".
Source: PYQ-style

42. He insisted ______ going with me.
(a) for (b) on (c) in (d) at
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Insist on" is the standard fixed verb-preposition combination, followed by a gerund. "Insist for" and "insist in" are common Hindi-influenced errors.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

43. The manager did not comply ______ the worker's demands.
(a) to (b) with (c) for (d) by
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Comply with" means to act in accordance with rules or demands. "Comply to" is a common error.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Spot the error: Part P: Despite of / Part Q: heavy rain, / Part R: the match continued. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part R (c) No error (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part P has the error. "Despite" never takes "of"; either write "despite heavy rain" or "in spite of heavy rain". This is a favourite examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

45. He was accused ______ theft by the police.
(a) for (b) on (c) of (d) with
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Accused of" is the fixed combination for a charge or crime. Compare: "charged with" a crime — the preposition changes with the verb.
Source: PYQ-style

46. The two brothers divided the property ______ themselves.
(a) within (b) among (c) between (d) amongst
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Between" is used when something is shared by two persons; "among" and "amongst" are used for more than two. Since there are only two brothers, "between themselves" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

47. She is eligible ______ this post.
(a) for (b) to (c) of (d) in
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Eligible for" a post or benefit is the fixed combination. "Eligible to" is used before a verb (eligible to apply), not before a noun.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Spot the error: Part P: He ordered / Part Q: for a cup of tea / Part R: and a sandwich. / Part S: No error
(a) No error (b) Part P (c) Part Q (d) Part R
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part Q has the error. "Order" as a verb takes a direct object without "for": "He ordered a cup of tea". "Order for" is a Hindi-influenced error.
Source: PYQ-style

49. The flight was delayed ______ bad weather.
(a) owing (b) due to (c) because (d) since
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Due to" means "caused by" and fits before the noun phrase "bad weather". "Because" needs "of" before a noun; "owing" needs "to".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

50. He has been absent ______ class since Friday.
(a) from (b) of (c) in (d) at
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Absent from" is the fixed adjective-preposition combination. Its opposite is "present in" or "present at". "Absent in" is a common error.
Source: PYQ-style
