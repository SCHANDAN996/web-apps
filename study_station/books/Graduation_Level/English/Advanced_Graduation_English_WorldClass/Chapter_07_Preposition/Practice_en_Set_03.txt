Questions 51–60: Easy | Questions 61–75: Medium

51. He has been living in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) from (b) for (c) since (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (2015 is a specific year). "For" is used with a period of time, e.g., "for ten years".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. The cat is hiding ______ the table.
(a) on (b) over (c) above (d) under
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Under" means directly below and covered by something. "Below" would suggest merely at a lower level, not hidden beneath.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

53. She is afraid ______ spiders.
(a) with (b) from (c) of (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: The fixed adjective-preposition combination is "afraid of". "Afraid with" and "afraid from" do not exist in standard English.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

54. We travelled ______ train to Mumbai.
(a) by (b) on (c) with (d) in
Answer: (a)
Solution: "By + mode of transport" (by train, by bus, by air) shows the means of travel. Use "on the train" only with an article.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

55. The meeting will start ______ 10 o'clock.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) by
Answer: (b)
Solution: "At" is used with exact clock times. "In" is for months/years, "on" for days and dates.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

56. He is good ______ mathematics.
(a) in (b) for (c) at (d) with
Answer: (c)
Solution: The fixed combination is "good at" a subject or skill. "Good in" is a common Hindi-speaker error.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

57. The book belongs ______ me.
(a) to (b) for (c) with (d) of
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Belong to" is the correct verb-preposition pair showing possession. "Belong with" suggests suitability, not ownership.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

58. Diwali falls ______ October or November.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "In" is used with months and years. "On" is reserved for specific dates and days.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

59. Please listen ______ what the teacher says.
(a) to (b) at (c) for (d) on
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Listen to" is the fixed combination. "Listen" alone cannot take an object directly.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

60. The bridge goes ______ the river.
(a) over (b) above (c) on (d) upon
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Over" suggests crossing from one side to the other, which fits a bridge. "Above" only means at a higher level, without the sense of spanning.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

61. He has been suffering ______ fever since Monday.
(a) from (b) by (c) with (d) of
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Suffer from" is used for diseases and ailments. "Suffer with" is non-standard; this is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

62. I am looking forward ______ meeting you soon.
(a) for (b) at (c) to (d) towards
Answer: (c)
Solution: In "look forward to", "to" is a preposition, so it takes a gerund (meeting), not an infinitive. Candidates often wrongly write "to meet".
Source: PYQ-style

63. She was married ______ a doctor last year.
(a) with (b) by (c) to (d) from
Answer: (c)
Solution: The correct passive form is "married to". "Married with" is a frequent error among Hindi speakers ("usse shaadi hui").
Source: PYQ-style

64. The train arrived exactly ______ time.
(a) in (b) at (c) by (d) on
Answer: (d)
Solution: "On time" means punctually, at the scheduled moment. "In time" means early enough for a purpose — a subtle but tested difference.
Source: PYQ-style

65. He is junior ______ me by two years.
(a) than (b) to (c) from (d) with
Answer: (b)
Solution: Latin comparatives like junior, senior, superior, inferior and prefer take "to", never "than". This is one of the most repeated traps in SSC and banking exams.
Source: PYQ-style

66. Spot the error: Part P: He insisted / Part Q: to go / Part R: with me / Part S: to the market.
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part Q is wrong: "insist" takes the preposition "on" plus a gerund — "insisted on going". "Insist to go" is incorrect.
Source: PYQ-style

67. The manager was angry ______ his clerk for coming late.
(a) on (b) upon (c) with (d) at
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Angry with" is used for a person; "angry at" is used for a thing or situation. Since the object is a person (clerk), "with" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

68. He died ______ cancer after a long illness.
(a) from (b) with (c) by (d) of
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Die of" is used for disease, hunger and old age; "die from" is used for wounds or external injuries. Cancer is a disease, so "of" fits.
Source: PYQ-style

69. Spot the error: Part P: Despite of / Part Q: heavy rain, / Part R: the match / Part S: continued.
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part P is wrong: "despite" never takes "of" — say "despite heavy rain" or "in spite of heavy rain". This is a favourite examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

70. The children were prevented ______ playing in the rain.
(a) to (b) from (c) for (d) against
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Prevent from" plus gerund is the fixed pattern. The same pattern applies to "prohibit from" and "restrain from".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

71. I have no objection ______ your joining the team.
(a) to (b) for (c) on (d) against
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Objection to" is the correct noun-preposition pair, and "to" here is a preposition followed by a gerund or possessive plus gerund.
Source: PYQ-style

72. He is eligible ______ this post as he has the required degree.
(a) to (b) of (c) in (d) for
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Eligible for" a post or benefit is the fixed combination. "Eligible to" is used only before a verb, e.g., "eligible to apply".
Source: PYQ-style

73. The two brothers divided the property ______ themselves.
(a) among (b) between (c) in (d) within
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Between" is used for two persons; "among" for more than two. Since there are two brothers, "between" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

74. Spot the error: Part P: He is / Part Q: capable to handle / Part R: any difficult / Part S: situation.
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part Q is wrong: "capable" takes "of" plus a gerund — "capable of handling". "Capable to handle" is a common error tested in exams.
Source: PYQ-style

75. She congratulated me ______ my success in the examination.
(a) for (b) on (c) at (d) about
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Congratulate someone on" something is the fixed pattern. "Congratulate for" is a widespread Hindi-speaker error ("safalta ke liye badhai").
Source: PYQ-style
