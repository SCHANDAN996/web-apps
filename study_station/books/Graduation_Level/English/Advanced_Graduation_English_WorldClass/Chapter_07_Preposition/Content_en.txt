# Preposition — The Small Words That Carry Big Marks

Imagine losing a mark in SSC CGL not because you didn't know a difficult word, but because you wrote **"married with"** instead of **"married to"**. That is the power — and the danger — of prepositions. They are the smallest words in English grammar, yet they appear in almost every sentence of every competitive exam: error spotting, sentence improvement, fill in the blanks, cloze tests, and even comprehension.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

1. Identify and correctly use prepositions of **time, place, direction, and agency**.
2. Apply the **12 Golden Rules** of prepositions to error-spotting questions.
3. Eliminate **redundant prepositions** (a favourite examiner trap).
4. Master **fixed prepositional phrases** (verb + preposition, adjective + preposition, noun + preposition).
5. Distinguish confusing pairs: *in/into, on/upon, since/for, between/among, beside/besides*.

---

## 1. What Is a Preposition?

A **preposition** is a word placed **before** a noun or pronoun to show its **relationship** with another word in the sentence.

- The book is **on** the table. (relationship between *book* and *table*)
- She came **after** lunch. (relationship between *came* and *lunch*)

The word comes from Latin: *prae* (before) + *ponere* (to place) — literally, "placed before."

The noun/pronoun that follows a preposition is called its **object**, and together they form a **prepositional phrase**: *on the table, in the morning, with great care*.

## 2. Types of Prepositions

### (a) Prepositions of Time
| Preposition | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | exact time, festivals | at 5 p.m., at dawn, at Diwali |
| **on** | days and dates | on Monday, on 15th August |
| **in** | months, years, seasons, centuries, parts of day | in July, in 2024, in winter, in the morning |
| **since** | point of time (with perfect tenses) | since Monday, since 2010 |
| **for** | period/duration of time | for two hours, for five years |
| **by** | deadline (not later than) | Submit it by Friday. |
| **within** | inside a time limit | within a week |

### (b) Prepositions of Place & Direction
| Preposition | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | a point | at the bus stop, at the door |
| **in** | enclosed space, large areas | in the room, in India |
| **on** | a surface | on the wall, on the road |
| **into** | movement from outside to inside | He jumped into the river. |
| **onto** | movement to a surface | The cat leapt onto the roof. |
| **between** | two persons/things | between Ram and Shyam |
| **among** | more than two | among the students |
| **above / over** | higher than (over often implies contact/covering or movement) | The plane flew over the city. |
| **below / under** | lower than (under often implies contact/covering) | under the table |
| **beside** | next to | Sit beside me. |
| **besides** | in addition to | Besides Hindi, she knows French. |

### (c) Other Important Prepositions
- **Agency/Instrument:** The letter was written **by** him **with** a pen. (*by* = doer; *with* = instrument)
- **Manner:** He spoke **with** confidence.
- **Cause:** He died **of** hunger. (disease/hunger → *of*); He died **from** his injuries.

🧠 **Active Recall:** Fill in the blanks mentally: (1) I have lived here ___ 2015. (2) The thief jumped ___ the well. (3) Distribute the sweets ___ the two brothers. *(Answers: since, into, between)*

## 3. The 12 Golden Rules of Prepositions ⭐

| # | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Since** = point of time; **for** = period of time | since 2010 / for ten years |
| 2 | **In** = inside (static); **into** = movement inside | He is in the room. / He went into the room. |
| 3 | **Between** = two; **among** = more than two | between you and me / among the crowd |
| 4 | **Beside** = next to; **besides** = in addition to | beside the river / besides English |
| 5 | **By** = agent; **with** = instrument | killed by the robber with a knife |
| 6 | **On** time = exactly at the time; **in** time = before the deadline | The train arrived on time. / Reach in time for the exam. |
| 7 | No preposition after **transitive verbs** like *enter, reach, attack, resemble, accompany, order, request* | He entered the room. (NOT entered into) |
| 8 | No preposition before **home, here, there, abroad, upstairs** (adverbs of place) | Go home. (NOT go to home) |
| 9 | **Different from**, not "different than/to" (in standard exam English) | My book is different from yours. |
| 10 | **Prefer...to**, **senior to**, **junior to**, **superior to**, **inferior to**, **prior to** — Latin comparatives take *to*, not *than* | He is senior to me. |
| 11 | A preposition is followed by a **gerund (V-ing)**, not an infinitive | He insisted on going. (NOT on to go) |
| 12 | **Married to, angry with (person)/at (thing), good at, afraid of, fond of, proud of, interested in, depend on, consist of, look after, look into, agree with (person)/to (proposal)** — fixed phrases must be memorised | She is married to a doctor. |

## ⚠️ Examiner's Trap

> **Trap 1 — The Redundant Preposition:** "He entered **into** the room" / "She reached **to** Delhi" / "The child resembles **with** his father." All wrong. *Enter, reach, resemble, attack, accompany, order, request* never take a preposition. This is the single most repeated preposition trap in SSC and Banking exams.
>
> **Trap 2 — Since vs For:** "I know him **since** five years" ❌ → "I know him **for** five years" ✔. Examiners swap these constantly. Remember: **since + point (Monday, 2010), for + period (five years, two months)**.
>
> **Trap 3 — Sound-Right Errors:** "discuss about," "comprise of," "order for," "request for" *sound* natural because of Hindi influence, but all are wrong: *discuss the matter, comprises ten chapters, ordered a meal, requested help*.

## 🔁 Linguistic Bridge (Hindi → English)

| In Hindi we think... | Wrong English | Correct English |
|---|---|---|
| "वह मुझसे शादी करेगी" (से = with) | She will marry **with** me. | She will marry me. / She is married **to** me. |
| "मैंने इस विषय पर चर्चा की" | We discussed **about** the matter. | We discussed the matter. |
| "वह दिल्ली पहुँचा" (को = to) | He reached **to** Delhi. | He reached Delhi. |
| "मैं पाँच साल से यहाँ हूँ" | I am here **since** five years. | I have been here **for** five years. |
| "समिति दस सदस्यों से बनी है" | The committee comprises **of** ten members. | The committee comprises ten members. / is composed **of** ten members. |
| "मुझे रोटी की तुलना में मक्खन पसंद है" | I prefer butter **than** bread. | I prefer butter **to** bread. |

## 4. Fixed Prepositional Phrases — The Memory Bank

Exams test these as one-word blanks. Learn them in groups:

**Verb + Preposition:** abide *by*, account *for*, agree *with/to*, apologize *for*, believe *in*, blame *for*, care *for*, comply *with*, cope *with*, deal *in/with*, depend *on*, die *of/from*, dream *of*, insist *on*, laugh *at*, listen *to*, look *after/into/at*, object *to*, rely *on*, succeed *in*, wait *for*.

**Adjective + Preposition:** afraid *of*, angry *with/at*, anxious *about*, aware *of*, capable *of*, fond *of*, good *at*, interested *in*, jealous *of*, keen *on*, proud *of*, similar *to*, superior *to*, sure *of*, tired *of*.

**Noun + Preposition:** access *to*, alternative *to*, attitude *towards*, cause *of*, demand *for*, exception *to*, increase *in*, lack *of*, need *for*, reason *for*, solution *to*.

🧠 **Active Recall:** Cover the right column. Say aloud the preposition for: *insist, depend, afraid, superior, solution, access*.

## 5. Mnemonic 🧠

**"Aunt S.I.F. lives IN-ON-AT"** — for time:
- **IN** = the *biggest* container (months, years, centuries) — think of a big hall.
- **ON** = the *middle* shelf (days, dates).
- **AT** = the *pinpoint* (exact time) — the smallest dot.

Big → small: **IN > ON > AT**. The bigger the time unit, the "bigger" the preposition.

## 6. Summary Table

| Concept | Key Point | Classic Trap |
|---|---|---|
| Time | in/on/at, since/for, by/within | since + period ❌ |
| Place | in/into, on/onto, above/over, below/under | "entered into" ❌ |
| Number | between (2) / among (3+) | "between the three" ❌ |
| Agency | by (doer) / with (instrument) | "killed with the robber" ❌ |
| Fixed phrases | married to, prefer...to, consist of | "married with" ❌ |
| Redundancy | enter, reach, resemble, discuss, comprise — no preposition | "discuss about" ❌ |
