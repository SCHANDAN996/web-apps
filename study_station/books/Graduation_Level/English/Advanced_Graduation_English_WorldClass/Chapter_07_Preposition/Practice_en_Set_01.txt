Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. He has been living in Delhi ______ 2015.
(a) from (b) for (c) since (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (2015, Monday, January). "For" is used with a period of time (for five years), so it is the tempting wrong choice here.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. The train arrived ______ time.
(a) on (b) by (c) at (d) in
Answer: (a)
Solution: "On time" means punctually, at the scheduled time. "In time" means before a deadline, with time to spare, which does not fit the sense of a train's schedule.
Source: PYQ-style

3. She is afraid ______ spiders.
(a) from (b) with (c) of (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Afraid of" is the fixed preposition. Adjectives like afraid, fond, proud, and jealous all take "of".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

4. The book is ______ the table.
(a) in (b) on (c) at (d) by
Answer: (b)
Solution: "On" shows contact with a surface. "In" means inside something, and "by" means next to, neither of which fits a book resting on a table.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

5. He died ______ cancer.
(a) of (b) with (c) by (d) from
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Die of" is used for diseases (die of cancer, die of hunger). "Die from" is used for injuries or external causes, so it is the trap here.
Source: PYQ-style

6. We travelled ______ bus.
(a) with (b) on (c) in (d) by
Answer: (d)
Solution: "By" is used with means of transport when there is no article: by bus, by train, by air. With an article we say "in a bus" or "on the bus".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

7. The cat hid ______ the bed.
(a) below (b) across (c) under (d) beside
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Under" is the standard preposition for a position directly below something. "Below" suggests a lower level, not necessarily directly beneath.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

8. I will meet you ______ 5 o'clock.
(a) in (b) at (c) by (d) on
Answer: (b)
Solution: "At" is used with exact clock time (at 5 o'clock, at noon). "In" is for months and years, and "on" is for days and dates.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

9. She is good ______ mathematics.
(a) in (b) with (c) for (d) at
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Good at" is the fixed combination for skill in a subject or activity. "Good in" is a common Hindi-speaker error that examiners love to test.
Source: PYQ-style

10. The bridge was built ______ the river.
(a) over (b) on (c) inside (d) between
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Over" means across or spanning something, which fits a bridge over a river. "On" would wrongly suggest contact with the water's surface.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

11. He has been working here ______ five years.
(a) since (b) from (c) at (d) for
Answer: (d)
Solution: "For" is used with a duration or period (for five years, for two hours). "Since" needs a starting point, not a duration, so it is the trap.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

12. The children are fond ______ chocolates.
(a) with (b) for (c) of (d) about
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Fond of" is the fixed adjective-preposition pair. The other options do not collocate with "fond".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

13. Please listen ______ what the teacher says.
(a) at (b) for (c) on (d) to
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Listen" always takes "to" before its object. Omitting "to" is one of the most frequent errors tested in exams.
Source: PYQ-style

14. He jumped ______ the well to save the child.
(a) in (b) into (c) onto (d) upon
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Into" shows movement from outside to inside. "In" shows position, not motion, so it is wrong after "jumped".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

15. My birthday falls ______ Monday this year.
(a) in (b) at (c) on (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "On" is used with days of the week and dates (on Monday, on 15th August). "In" is for months and years only.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

16. He is senior ______ me by two years.
(a) from (b) to (c) than (d) over
Answer: (b)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior, and prefer take "to", never "than". Choosing "than" is the classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

17. The thief was caught ______ the police.
(a) with (b) by (c) through (d) from
Answer: (b)
Solution: In passive voice, the doer of the action is introduced by "by". "By the police" names the agent of the action.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

18. We should not laugh ______ others.
(a) at (b) upon (c) on (d) over
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Laugh at" means to mock or ridicule someone. "Laugh on" and "laugh upon" are not standard English.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

19. The shop opens ______ 9 a.m. and closes at 9 p.m.
(a) on (b) in (c) at (d) since
Answer: (c)
Solution: Exact clock times take "at". "Since" would need a continuing action up to the present, which is not the case here.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

20. He apologized ______ his rude behaviour.
(a) at (b) about (c) on (d) for
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Apologize for" something is the fixed pattern; "apologize to" a person. Here the object is the behaviour, so "for" is correct.
Source: PYQ-style

21. Spot the error: Part P — He has been / Part Q — working in this office / Part R — since five years. / Part S — No error
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part R has the error. "Five years" is a period of time, so it needs "for", not "since"; "since" is used only with a point of time like "since 2019". The trap is to assume the sentence is correct because "has been working" sounds right.
Source: PYQ-style

22. He is inferior ______ his brother in studies.
(a) than (b) with (c) from (d) to
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Inferior" is a Latin comparative and takes "to", not "than". Choosing "than" is the sound-right trap because ordinary comparatives use "than".
Source: PYQ-style

23. Despite ______ rich, he is not happy.
(a) being (b) been (c) be (d) to be
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Despite" is a preposition and must be followed by a noun or gerund, so "being" is correct. A bare verb form after "despite" is grammatically wrong.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Spot the error: Part P — The teacher asked / Part Q — the students to / Part R — discuss about the topic. / Part S — No error
(a) Part R (b) Part P (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part R has the error. "Discuss" is a transitive verb and never takes "about"; we discuss a topic directly. This is a very common examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

25. He entered ______ the room without knocking.
(a) no preposition needed (b) in (c) into (d) inside
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Enter" already means "go into", so no preposition follows it: "He entered the room." Adding "into" is a classic error tested repeatedly in SSC and banking exams.
Source: PYQ-style
