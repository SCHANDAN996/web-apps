# 🪄 Short Tricks + Skip Strategy — Preposition

## Mnemonic Boxes

**Box 1 — "IN-ON-AT" Time Ladder**
> **IN** the big, **ON** the day, **AT** the dot.
- IN → months, years, seasons, centuries (in 2024, in July, in winter)
- ON → days & dates (on Monday, on 15th August)
- AT → exact time (at 5 p.m., at noon, at midnight)

**Box 2 — "IN-ON-AT" Place Zoom**
> Zoom out = IN, surface = ON, point = AT.
- IN → large areas (in India, in the room)
- ON → surfaces (on the table, on the wall)
- AT → specific points (at the station, at the door)

**Box 3 — "Arrive" Rule**
> **Arrive AT** small, **arrive IN** large — never "arrive to".
- arrive at the station ✔ | arrive in Delhi ✔ | arrive to Delhi ✘

**Box 4 — "Since vs For"**
> **Since** = point (a dot on the timeline), **For** = period (a stretch of the timeline).
- since Monday / since 2010 | for two years / for a long time

**Box 5 — "Between vs Among"**
> **Between TWO, Among the CREW.**
- between Ram and Shyam | among the students

**Box 6 — "Beside vs Besides"**
> **Beside** = by the side; **Besides** = in addition (has an extra 's' = extra meaning).
- She sat beside me. | Besides English, she knows French.

**Box 7 — Fixed Pairs (Verb + Preposition)**
> **"DREAM of, DEPEND on, INSIST on, SUCCEED in, BELIEVE in, APOLOGIZE for."**
- Chant: *Dream-Of, Depend-On, Insist-On, Succeed-In, Believe-In, Apologize-For.*

**Box 8 — Adjective Pairs**
> **Good AT, Angry WITH (person) / ABOUT (thing), Afraid OF, Fond OF, Proud OF, Interested IN.**
- Mnemonic: **"GA-AWA-AFO-FFO-PO-II"** — or remember: *"Good At, Angry With, Afraid Of, Fond Of, Proud Of, Interested In."*

**Box 9 — "Different FROM, not Different THAN"**
> In Indian exams, **different from** is always the accepted form.

**Box 10 — "No Preposition After These Verbs"**
> **"REACH, ENTER, ATTACK, RESEMBLE, ORDER, ACCOMPANY"** — take NO preposition.
- He reached Delhi ✔ (not "reached to Delhi")
- She entered the room ✔ (not "entered into the room")
- Mnemonic: **"REAROA"** — *Reach, Enter, Attack, Resemble, Order, Accompany.*

**Box 11 — "By vs With" (Agent vs Instrument)**
> **By** the doer, **With** the tool.
- killed by the robber | killed with a knife

**Box 12 — "In spite of vs Despite"**
> **Despite** never takes "of" — *despite* = *in spite of* (the 'of' is already inside).
- Despite the rain ✔ | Despite of the rain ✘

**Box 13 — "Junior/Senior/Superior/Prefer + TO"**
> Latin comparatives take **TO**, not "than".
> Mnemonic: **"J-S-S-P go TO school"** — Junior, Senior, Superior, Prefer → TO.
- He is senior to me ✔ (not "senior than me")

**Box 14 — "On foot, By bus"**
> Transport: **by** + vehicle (by train, by car), but **on foot**.
- Exception trick: your feet are not a vehicle!

**Box 15 — "Married TO, Angry WITH, Listen TO, Look AT"**
> Mnemonic: **"M-A-L-L"** — *Married To, Angry With, Listen To, Look At.*
- Remember the "MALL": you get married to someone, get angry with the crowd, listen to music, and look at shops.

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy

**Tip 1 — Skip the "Double Preposition Trap" question if confused.**
If a sentence already has a correct preposition and an option adds another (e.g., "entered into the room"), don't overthink — recall Box 10 (REAROA). If you can't recall the list in 15 seconds, mark and move on; these are pure recall, not worth 45 seconds.

**Tip 2 — Skip long "fill-in-the-blank with 3 blanks" items first.**
Questions with three blanks and four similar option-sets (in/on/at combinations) consume the most time. Attempt all single-blank fixed-preposition questions first (they are 15-second Fact-based), then return to multi-blank ones with a fresh eye.

**Tip 3 — When two options "sound right," skip and return.**
Sound-right traps (e.g., "discuss about," "comprise of") prey on your ear, not your knowledge. If your ear says both are fine, flag it, finish the section, and re-attack with the rule list in mind — never guess between two sound-alikes under time pressure.
