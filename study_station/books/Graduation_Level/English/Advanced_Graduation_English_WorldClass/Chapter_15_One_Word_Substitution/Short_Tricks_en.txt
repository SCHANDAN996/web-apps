# 🪄 Short Tricks + Skip Strategy — One-word Substitution

## Memory Mnemonics (Learn these, never forget)

**📦 Trick 1 — The "PHIL" Family (Love)**
> **PHIL = LOVE** → Philanthropist (loves mankind), Bibliophile (loves books), Anglophile (loves England), Philatelist (loves stamps).
> *Memory hook: "PHIL loves PHIL-ately" — a man named Phil who loves everything!*

**📦 Trick 2 — The "PHOBIA" Family (Fear)**
> **PHOBIA = FEAR** → Claustrophobia (fear of closed spaces), Hydrophobia (fear of water), Xenophobia (fear of foreigners), Acrophobia (fear of heights).
> *Memory hook: "PHOBIA = FEAR-BIA" — anything ending in -phobia is a fear.*

**📦 Trick 3 — The "CIDE" Family (Killing)**
> **CIDE = KILL** → Homicide (killing a man), Fratricide (killing a brother), Suicide (killing oneself), Regicide (killing a king), Infanticide (killing an infant).
> *Memory hook: "CIDE rhymes with DIED" — someone died, someone killed.*

**📦 Trick 4 — The "CRACY" Family (Rule/Government)**
> **CRACY = RULE** → Democracy (rule by people), Autocracy (rule by one), Bureaucracy (rule by officials), Theocracy (rule by religious laws), Plutocracy (rule by the rich).
> *Memory hook: "CRACY = CROWN" — whoever holds the crown rules.*

**📦 Trick 5 — The "LOGY" Family (Study)**
> **LOGY = STUDY** → Biology (study of life), Zoology (study of animals), Etymology (study of word origins), Cardiology (study of the heart).
> *Memory hook: "LOG = LOGIC = learning" — every -logy is a subject to study.*

**📦 Trick 6 — The "MANIA" Family (Madness/Obsession)**
> **MANIA = MADNESS** → Kleptomania (mad urge to steal), Pyromania (mad urge to set fire), Megalomania (madness for power), Dipsomania (mad craving for alcohol).
> *Memory hook: "MANIAC" — a maniac has a mania.*

**📦 Trick 7 — The "GAMY" Family (Marriage)**
> **GAMY = MARRIAGE** → Monogamy (one marriage), Bigamy (two marriages), Polygamy (many marriages).
> *Memory hook: "GAME of marriage" — Mono = 1, Bi = 2, Poly = many.*

**📦 Trick 8 — The "VORE" Family (Eating)**
> **VORE = EAT** → Carnivore (eats flesh), Herbivore (eats plants), Omnivore (eats everything), Insectivore (eats insects).
> *Memory hook: "OMNI = ALL" — an omnivore devours all.*

**📦 Trick 9 — The "IST" Family (Person who does)**
> **IST = PERSON** → Novelist (writes novels), Pianist (plays piano), Optimist (sees the bright side), Pessimist (sees the dark side).
> *Memory hook: "The IST is the artist" — the suffix points to the person.*

**📦 Trick 10 — The "GRAPH" Family (Writing)**
> **GRAPH = WRITE** → Biography (life written by another), Autobiography (life written by oneself), Calligraphy (beautiful writing), Telegraph (writing from far).
> *Memory hook: "AUTO = SELF" — auto + bio + graphy = self-life-writing.*

**📦 Trick 11 — The "OMNI" Family (All)**
> **OMNI = ALL** → Omnipotent (all-powerful), Omniscient (all-knowing), Omnipresent (present everywhere).
> *Memory hook: "OMNI = ALL-MIGHTY" — God's three qualities in exams.*

**📦 Trick 12 — The "AMBUL" Family (Walk)**
> **AMBUL = WALK** → Somnambulist (walks in sleep), Ambulance (vehicle that moves/walks to patients), Perambulator (baby walker).
> *Memory hook: "SOMN = SLEEP" — sleep + walk = sleepwalker.*

**📦 Trick 13 — The "LOQU" Family (Speak)**
> **LOQU = SPEAK** → Loquacious (talkative), Soliloquy (speaking to oneself), Eloquent (speaking beautifully).
> *Memory hook: "SOLI = ALONE" — soliloquy = speaking alone.*

**📦 Trick 14 — The "MORT" Family (Death)**
> **MORT = DEATH** → Mortal (subject to death), Immortal (never dies), Mortuary (place for dead bodies), Post-mortem (examination after death).
> *Memory hook: "MORT = MORTAL" — mortals must die.*

**📦 Trick 15 — The "CHRON" Family (Time)**
> **CHRON = TIME** → Chronic (lasting a long time), Chronology (order of time), Anachronism (something out of its time), Synchronize (happen at the same time).
> *Memory hook: "CHRONO = CLOCK" — anything with chron- ticks with time.*

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy (Exam Hall Wisdom)

**Skip Tip 1 — The "Unknown Root" Skip:**
If a substitution question contains a root you have never seen (e.g., "one who can use both hands equally well" — ambidextrous) and no option looks familiar, **do not guess blindly**. Mark it, move on, and return only if time remains. Negative marking makes wild guesses costlier than blanks.

**Skip Tip 2 — The "Two Similar Options" Trap:**
When two options look almost identical in meaning (e.g., *Optimist* vs *Idealist*), the examiner is testing a fine distinction. If you cannot recall the exact difference within 45 seconds, **skip and return** — over-thinking here usually converts a right instinct into a wrong answer.

**Skip Tip 3 — The "Long Sentence, Short Answer" Rule:**
One-word substitution questions often hide behind long descriptive sentences. Read the **last 5 words first** ("...is called?"), then scan for the key noun (fear / killing / study / government). If the key noun matches a family you know (Tricks 1–15), answer in **15 seconds**; if not, flag it as a 45-second question or skip.
