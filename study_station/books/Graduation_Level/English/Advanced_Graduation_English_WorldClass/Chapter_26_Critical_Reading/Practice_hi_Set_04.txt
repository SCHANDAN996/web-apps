76. कथन पढ़ें: "Although the new policy was intended to reduce paperwork, it has, in practice, multiplied the forms citizens must fill." नीति के प्रति लेखक का दृष्टिकोण सबसे सही ढंग से किस रूप में वर्णित किया जा सकता है:
(a) wholehearted approval (b) critical of the gap between intention and outcome (c) neutral reporting of facts (d) admiration for its ambition
उत्तर: (b)
हल: जाल विकल्प (c) है: वाक्य तथ्य बताता है, लेकिन "although" और "in practice" जैसे विरोध-सूचक शब्द निर्णय दर्शाते हैं, तटस्थता नहीं। लेखक उजागर करता है कि नीति ने अपने उद्देश्य के विपरीत परिणाम दिया, जो इरादे और परिणाम के अंतर की आलोचना है, इसलिए उत्तर critical of the gap between intention and outcome है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

77. वाक्य "The committee's findings were met with scepticism, for its members had been appointed by the very agency under investigation," में "scepticism" शब्द का अर्थ सबसे निकट है:

78. गद्यांश: "Renewable energy now accounts for a growing share of India's power capacity, yet coal still supplies the majority of electricity actually generated." इस कथन से क्या निष्कर्ष निकाला जा सकता है?
(a) Coal has been completely phased out (b) India generates no solar power (c) Renewable energy is unreliable everywhere (d) Installed capacity and actual generation are not the same thing

79. वह विकल्प चुनें जो इस वाक्य की भावना (tone) को सबसे अच्छी तरह व्यक्त करता है: "One can only marvel at the committee's decision to solve water scarcity by printing yet another report about it."

स्रोत: PYQ-style

80. गद्यांश: "Unlike its predecessor, the revised curriculum emphasises projects over rote memorisation." "predecessor" शब्द किसका संदर्भ देता है:

81. त्रुटि पहचानें: "Neither the manager (P) / nor the employees (Q) / was informed about the change. (R) / No error (S)"

82. गद्यांश: "The study found that students who slept fewer than six hours scored lower on memory tests, though the researchers cautioned that other factors, such as stress, were not controlled." सबसे उचित निष्कर्ष है:
(a) Less sleep certainly causes poor memory (b) The study proves stress improves memory (c) The results are suggestive but not conclusive (d) Sleep has no relation to memory
उत्तर: (c)

स्रोत: PYQ-style

83. "The minister's speech was a masterclass in obfuscation, leaving reporters more confused than before," में "obfuscation" शब्द का अर्थ है:

84. गद्यांश: "Automation will not eliminate work; it will eliminate tasks. Jobs, which are bundles of tasks, will be reshaped rather than destroyed." लेखक का मुख्य तर्क है कि:

स्रोत: PYQ-style

86. गद्यांश: "Critics argue that the dam displaced thousands; supporters counter that it irrigates lakhs of hectares. Both claims are true, which is precisely why the debate refuses to die." लेखक का आशय है कि:

87. त्रुटि पहचानें: "The data collected (P) / from the survey (Q) / was published yesterday. (R) / No error (S)"

88. वाक्य "Her argument was cogent, and even her opponents conceded its force," में "cogent" शब्द का अर्थ है:

90. कौन-सा विकल्प इस वाक्य का सर्वोत्तम सार है: "Democracy depends not merely on elections but on what happens between them — a free press, independent courts, and citizens who can criticise power without fear."
