76. Read the statement: "Although the new policy was intended to reduce paperwork, it has, in practice, multiplied the forms citizens must fill." The author's attitude toward the policy is best described as:
(a) wholehearted approval (b) critical of the gap between intention and outcome (c) neutral reporting of facts (d) admiration for its ambition
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is option (c): the sentence reports facts, but the contrast words "although" and "in practice" signal judgement, not neutrality. The author highlights that the policy achieved the opposite of its aim, which is criticism of the intention–outcome gap, so the answer is critical of the gap between intention and outcome.
