26. Choose the correct conjunction: He is poor ______ honest.
(a) or (b) and (c) but (d) so
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Poor" and "honest" show contrast, so the adversative conjunction "but" is correct. "And" would join similar ideas, which is not the case here.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. Choose the correct conjunction: Work hard ______ you will fail.
(a) and (b) or (c) because (d) but
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Or" here means "otherwise" and warns of a consequence. "And" would wrongly suggest both actions happen together.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

28. Choose the correct conjunction: ______ it was raining, we went out.
(a) Because (b) As (c) Since (d) Although
Answer: (d)
Solution: The two clauses contrast with each other, so the concessive conjunction "Although" fits. "Because" would reverse the logic of the sentence.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

29. Choose the correct conjunction: She is ______ intelligent ______ hardworking.
(a) not only / but also (b) either / or (c) neither / nor (d) both / or
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Not only... but also" is the correct correlative pair for adding two positive qualities. "Both" pairs with "and", never with "or".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

30. Choose the correct conjunction: ______ you ______ your brother has broken the window.
(a) Both / and (b) Neither / nor (c) Not only / but also (d) Either / or
Answer: (d)
Solution: The singular verb "has" and the sense of one-of-two choice point to "Either... or". "Both... and" would need a plural verb.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

31. Choose the correct conjunction: He ran fast ______ he missed the train.
(a) yet (b) because (c) so (d) and
Answer: (a)
Solution: The result is unexpected given the effort, so the contrastive "yet" is correct. "So" would wrongly suggest missing the train was the intended result.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

32. Choose the correct conjunction: I will wait here ______ you come back.
(a) because (b) unless (c) until (d) although
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Until" expresses time up to a point, which the sentence needs. "Unless" means "if not" and would distort the meaning.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

33. Choose the correct conjunction: ______ he is rich, he is not happy.
(a) Because (b) As (c) Since (d) Though
Answer: (d)
Solution: Wealth and unhappiness contrast, so the concessive "Though" is correct. "Because" would make wealth the cause of unhappiness, which the sentence does not say.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

34. Choose the correct conjunction: Take an umbrella ______ it rains.
(a) although (b) so that (c) in case (d) until
Answer: (c)
Solution: "In case" expresses a precaution against a possible event. "So that" expresses purpose, not precaution, so it does not fit.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

35. Choose the correct conjunction: No sooner had she arrived ______ the meeting began.
(a) than (b) then (c) that (d) when
Answer: (a)
Solution: "No sooner" is always followed by "than", never by "when" or "then". This is a fixed correlative pair frequently tested in exams.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

36. Choose the correct conjunction: Hardly had he stepped out ______ it started raining.
(a) when (b) than (c) then (d) that
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Hardly" and "Scarcely" are followed by "when", while "No sooner" takes "than". Mixing these pairs is a classic examiner's trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

37. Choose the correct conjunction: He is ______ a fool ______ a knave.
(a) either / or (b) not only / but also (c) both / and (d) neither / nor
Answer: (d)
Solution: The sentence denies both qualities, so "neither... nor" is correct. "Either... or" would mean he is one of the two, changing the meaning.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

38. Choose the correct conjunction: ______ you work hard, you cannot succeed.
(a) If (b) Unless (c) Until (d) Provided
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Unless" means "if not" and fits the negative main clause "cannot succeed". "If" would require "you do not work hard" to make sense.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

39. Choose the correct conjunction: He spoke ______ softly ______ nobody could hear him.
(a) so / that (b) such / that (c) too / to (d) very / that
Answer: (a)
Solution: "So + adverb + that" expresses result. "Such" is used before a noun phrase, not before an adverb like "softly".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

40. Choose the correct conjunction: Walk carefully ______ you should fall.
(a) so that (b) because (c) until (d) lest
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Lest" means "for fear that" and is followed by "should". "So that" expresses a desired purpose, not a fear to be avoided.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

41. Spot the error: Part P: Scarcely he had reached the station / Part Q: when the train / Part R: left the platform. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part P (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part P is wrong. After "Scarcely" at the beginning, inversion is required: "Scarcely had he reached". The trap is that "when" in Part Q is correct with "scarcely", so candidates wrongly hunt for an error there.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) No sooner I entered the room than the phone rang. (b) No sooner had I entered the room when the phone rang. (c) No sooner had I entered the room than the phone rang. (d) No sooner I had entered the room than the phone rang.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "No sooner" demands inversion ("had I entered") and the pair "than". The other options either miss the inversion or wrongly use "when".
Source: PYQ-style

43. Choose the correct conjunction: ______ being rich, he is humble.
(a) Despite (b) Although (c) Unless (d) Because
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Despite" is a preposition followed by a noun or "-ing" form, which matches "being rich". "Although" is a conjunction and must be followed by a full clause with a finite verb.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

44. Spot the error: Part P: Neither the teacher / Part Q: nor the students / Part R: was present in the hall. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part P
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part R is wrong. With "neither... nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject, "students", so it should be "were present". This proximity rule is a favourite examiner's trap.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Choose the correct conjunction: He is ______ weak ______ he cannot walk.
(a) very / that (b) too / to (c) such / that (d) so / that
Answer: (d)
Solution: The second part is a full clause ("he cannot walk"), so "so... that" is needed. "Too... to" must be followed by an infinitive, not a clause.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

46. Choose the correct conjunction: ______ you apologize, I will not forgive you.
(a) If (b) Unless (c) Till (d) Until
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Unless" means "if... not" and fits: without an apology there will be no forgiveness. "Until" and "till" express time, not condition.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

47. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) He not only plays cricket but also football. (b) He plays not only cricket but also football. (c) Not only he plays cricket but also football. (d) He plays cricket not only but also football.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Correlatives must be placed just before the words they join; here they join "cricket" and "football", so "not only" and "but also" must precede those nouns. Misplacement of correlatives is a common trap.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Choose the correct conjunction: The more you read, ______ you learn.
(a) the most (b) most (c) the more (d) more
Answer: (c)
Solution: "The + comparative... the + comparative" is the fixed parallel structure: "The more you read, the more you learn." Dropping "the" breaks the idiom.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

49. Spot the error: Part P: He came late / Part Q: because of he missed / Part R: the school bus. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q is wrong. "Because of" is a preposition and cannot be followed by a clause; use the conjunction "because" before "he missed". Confusing "because" with "because of" is a classic trap.
Source: PYQ-style

50. Choose the correct conjunction: ______ had the bell rung ______ the students rushed out.
(a) Scarcely / than (b) Hardly / than (c) No sooner / than (d) No sooner / when
Answer: (c)
Solution: "No sooner... than" is the only fully correct pair among the options. "Scarcely" and "Hardly" take "when", not "than", and "No sooner" never takes "when", so the mixed pairs are wrong.
Source: PYQ-style
