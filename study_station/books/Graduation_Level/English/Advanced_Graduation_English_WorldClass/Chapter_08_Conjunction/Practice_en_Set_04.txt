76. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) Scarcely had he reached the station when the train left. (b) Scarcely he had reached the station when the train left. (c) Scarcely he had reached the station than the train left. (d) Scarcely had he reached the station than the train left.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Scarcely/Hardly" at the beginning of a sentence requires inversion (had he), and the paired conjunction is "when", not "than". "Than" pairs with "no sooner".

77. Choose the correct option to fill the blank: "_______ he is rich, he is not happy."
(a) Because (b) Since (c) Although (d) Unless
Answer: (c)
Solution: The two clauses show contrast (rich vs not happy), so the concessive conjunction "Although" is needed. "Because/Since" show reason and "Unless" shows condition, which do not fit.

78. Spot the error: "No sooner did the bell ring (P) / when the students (Q) / rushed out of the class. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part Q is wrong. "No sooner" is always paired with "than", not "when". It should be "No sooner did the bell ring than the students rushed out."

79. Choose the correct sentence:

Answer: (c)
Solution: "Not only... but also" must be placed immediately before the parallel words they join — here "a singer" and "a dancer". Option (c) keeps the correlative pair in the correct parallel position.

80. Fill in the blank: "Work hard _______ you will fail."

Solution: "Or" here means "otherwise" — it warns of the consequence of not working hard. "Lest" would need "should" and a negative sense ("Work hard lest you should fail").

81. Choose the correct sentence:

Answer: (b)

83. Fill in the blank: "_______ the rain stopped, the match resumed."

Solution: With "neither...nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject — "his friends" (plural), so "were" is correct. Also, "neither" pairs with "nor", never with "or".
