Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Choose the correct form of the verb: She _____ to school every day.
(a) goes (b) go (c) going (d) gone
Answer: (a)
Solution: With a third-person singular subject (she) in the simple present tense, the verb takes -s/-es. "Goes" is correct; "go" would need a plural subject.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

2. Identify the helping (auxiliary) verb: They have finished their work.
(a) they (b) have (c) finished (d) work
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Have" is the auxiliary verb helping the main verb "finished" to form the present perfect tense.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

3. Choose the correct past tense: He _____ a letter yesterday.
(a) writes (b) wrote (c) write (d) written
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Yesterday" signals simple past tense. The past tense of "write" is "wrote"; "written" is the past participle and needs an auxiliary.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

4. Fill in the blank: The sun _____ in the east.
(a) rise (b) rose (c) rises (d) rising
Answer: (c)
Solution: Universal truths take the simple present tense. With singular subject "the sun," the verb is "rises."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

5. Choose the correct option: I _____ my homework before dinner.
(a) has completed (b) completing (c) had complete (d) have completed
Answer: (d)
Solution: With "I," the present perfect uses "have + past participle." "Has" is used only with third-person singular subjects.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

6. Identify the main verb in: The children were playing in the park.
(a) the (b) were (c) playing (d) park
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Playing" is the main verb carrying the action; "were" is the auxiliary forming the past continuous tense.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

7. Choose the correct verb: Bread and butter _____ my favourite breakfast.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: When two nouns joined by "and" denote one single dish/idea, the verb is singular. Bread and butter is treated as one meal. (Examiner's trap: "are" looks right because of two nouns.)
Source: PYQ-style

8. Fill in the blank: We _____ a movie last night.
(a) watch (b) watches (c) watched (d) watching
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Last night" indicates simple past; the past tense of "watch" is "watched."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

9. Choose the correct form: She _____ singing since morning.
(a) is (b) was (c) have been (d) has been
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Since morning" with a continuing action needs present perfect continuous: has/have + been + V-ing. With "she," use "has been."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

10. Identify the type of verb in: The baby slept soundly.
(a) transitive (b) auxiliary (c) intransitive (d) modal
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Slept" does not take an object — the action does not pass to anything — so it is intransitive.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

11. Choose the correct option: Each of the boys _____ a pen.
(a) have (b) has (c) are having (d) having
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Each of" is followed by a plural noun but takes a singular verb. (Examiner's trap: "boys" tempts you to pick "have.")
Source: PYQ-style

12. Fill in the blank: They _____ cricket when it started raining.
(a) play (b) played (c) are playing (d) were playing
Answer: (d)
Solution: An ongoing past action interrupted by another past action uses past continuous: "were playing."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

13. Choose the correct past participle: The window was _____ by the storm.
(a) break (b) broke (c) breaking (d) broken
Answer: (d)
Solution: Passive voice needs "be + past participle." The past participle of "break" is "broken."
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

14. Identify the modal verb: You should respect your elders.
(a) should (b) you (c) respect (d) elders
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Should" is a modal auxiliary expressing duty/advice; "respect" is the main verb.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

15. Choose the correct verb: Neither Ram nor his friends _____ present.
(a) were (b) is (c) was (d) has been
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "neither...nor," the verb agrees with the nearer subject — "friends" (plural) — so "were" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

16. Fill in the blank: I _____ Delhi last year.
(a) visited (b) visit (c) have visited (d) visiting
Answer: (a)
Solution: A definite past time ("last year") takes simple past, not present perfect. "Visited" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

17. Choose the correct form: The teacher asked why he _____ late.
(a) comes (b) come (c) came (d) coming
Answer: (c)
Solution: In reported speech with a past reporting verb ("asked"), the verb shifts back: present "comes" becomes past "came."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

18. Identify the sentence with a transitive verb:
(a) The bird flew away. (b) They laughed loudly. (c) He slept early. (d) She sings a song.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Sings" takes the object "a song," making it transitive. The other verbs have no object.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

19. Choose the correct option: There _____ many students in the class today.
(a) are (b) was (c) is (d) has been
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "there," the verb agrees with the real subject that follows — "many students" (plural) — so "are."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

20. Fill in the blank: By next year, she _____ her degree.
(a) will complete (b) completes (c) will have completed (d) completed
Answer: (c)
Solution: "By next year" indicates an action completed before a future point — future perfect: "will have completed."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

21. Choose the correctly framed sentence:
(a) One of my friend is a doctor. (b) One of my friends is a doctor. (c) One of my friends are a doctor. (d) One of my friend are a doctor.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "One of" takes a plural noun ("friends") but a singular verb ("is"). Options (a) and (d) fail on the noun; (c) fails on the verb.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Spot the error: The quality of the mangoes / were not good / and so we did not buy them. / No error
(a) The quality of the mangoes (b) were not good (c) and so we did not buy them (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The subject is "quality" (singular), not "mangoes." The verb should be "was." (Examiner's trap: the intervening phrase "of the mangoes" misleads.)
Source: PYQ-style

23. Choose the correct verb: The committee _____ divided in their opinion.
(a) were (b) is (c) was (d) has been
Answer: (a)
Solution: When a collective noun's members act separately ("divided in their opinion"), a plural verb is used. "Were" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

24. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He denied to help me. (b) He refused to help me. (c) He refused helping me. (d) He denied helping me yesterday always.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Refuse" is followed by the to-infinitive ("refused to help"). "Deny" takes a gerund ("denied helping"), which makes (a) wrong; (c) and (d) misuse the verb patterns.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Fill in the blank: Hardly had he reached the station _____ the train left.
(a) then (b) than (c) that (d) when
Answer: (d)
Solution: The correlative pair is "Hardly/Scarcely... when." "Than" pairs with "No sooner," which is the common confusion here.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar
