26. Choose the correct form of the verb: She ______ to school every day.
(a) go (b) going (c) goes (d) gone
Answer: (c)
Solution: With a third-person singular subject (she) in the simple present tense, the verb takes -s/-es. "Goes" is correct; "go" would need a plural subject.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

27. Fill in the blank: They ______ playing cricket when it started raining.
(a) was (b) are (c) were (d) is
Answer: (c)
Solution: "They" is plural and the action was ongoing in the past, so past continuous "were playing" is needed. "Was" agrees only with singular subjects.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

28. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He don't like tea. (b) He doesn't like tea. (c) He doesn't likes tea. (d) He not like tea.
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "doesn't", the main verb stays in its base form — "like", not "likes". "Don't" clashes with the singular subject "he".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

29. Fill in the blank: I ______ my homework before the teacher arrived.
(a) finish (b) finished (c) have finished (d) had finished
Answer: (d)
Solution: When two past actions occur, the earlier one takes the past perfect ("had finished") and the later one the simple past ("arrived").
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

30. Choose the correct passive form: "The chef cooks the food."
(a) The food was cooked by the chef. (b) The food is cooked by the chef. (c) The food is cooking by the chef. (d) The food cooked by the chef.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Simple present active becomes "is/are + past participle" in passive. "Was cooked" wrongly shifts the tense to past.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

31. Fill in the blank: Neither of the boys ______ done his work.
(a) have (b) were (c) are (d) has
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Neither of" takes a singular verb, so "has" is correct. "Have" is a common trap because "boys" sits right before the verb.

32. Choose the correctly spelt past participle:
(a) written (b) wrote (c) writen (d) writted
Answer: (a)
Solution: The verb "write" has the forms write–wrote–written. "Written" is the past participle used with has/have/had.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

33. Fill in the blank: The train ______ at 6 a.m. tomorrow.
(a) leave (b) left (c) leaving (d) leaves
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fixed timetables and schedules take the simple present even for future time, so "leaves" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

34. Choose the correct modal: You ______ obey the law. (moral duty)
(a) ought to (b) might (c) could (d) would
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Ought to" expresses moral duty or obligation. "Might" and "could" show only possibility, and "would" shows habit or willingness.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

35. Fill in the blank: Bread and butter ______ his daily breakfast.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: When two nouns joined by "and" denote one single dish or idea, the verb is singular. Bread and butter here is one meal, so "is".
Source: PYQ-style

36. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I am knowing the answer. (b) I have been knowing the answer. (c) I was knowing the answer. (d) I know the answer.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Know" is a stative verb and is not used in continuous forms. The simple present "know" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

37. Fill in the blank: Each of the students ______ given a certificate.
(a) were (b) have (c) was (d) are
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Each of" is always followed by a singular verb, so "was". The plural noun "students" before the verb is the examiner's trap.
Source: PYQ-style

38. Choose the correct form: By next year, she ______ her degree.
(a) will have completed (b) will complete (c) completes (d) has completed
Answer: (a)
Solution: "By + future time" signals the future perfect tense — "will have completed" — showing an action finished before a future point.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

39. Fill in the blank: He ______ in this city since 2015.
(a) has been living (b) lives (c) is living (d) lived
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Since + point of time" with an action continuing up to now requires the present perfect continuous — "has been living".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

40. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The news is shocking. (b) The news are shocking. (c) The news were shocking. (d) The news have shocked.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "News" looks plural but is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb — "is".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

41. Spot the error: Part P: The quality of the apples / Part Q: were not good / Part R: so we returned them. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: The subject is "quality" (singular), not "apples"; the intervening phrase "of the apples" is the trap. Part Q should read "was not good".
Source: PYQ-style

42. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) One of my friend lives in Delhi. (b) One of my friends lives in Delhi. (c) One of my friends live in Delhi. (d) One of my friend live in Delhi.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "One of" needs a plural noun ("friends") but a singular verb ("lives"). Options with "friend" or "live" break one of these two rules.
Source: PYQ-style

43. Fill in the blank: Hardly ______ I reached the station when the train left.
(a) have (b) had (c) did (d) was
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Hardly/Scarcely... when" constructions use the past perfect with inversion — "Hardly had I reached". "Did" is the sound-right trap here.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Spot the error: Part P: The committee / Part Q: have submitted / Part R: its report yesterday. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Committee" acting as one body takes a singular verb, and "yesterday" demands simple past — Part Q should be "submitted". "Have submitted" mixes present perfect with a definite past time.
Source: PYQ-style

45. Choose the correct form: If he ______ harder, he would pass the exam.
(a) worked (b) works (c) had worked (d) will work
Answer: (a)
Solution: A second conditional (imaginary present/future) uses "If + simple past, ... would + base verb". "Had worked" belongs to the third conditional with "would have passed".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

46. Fill in the blank: The teacher, along with his students, ______ gone to the museum.
(a) have (b) are (c) has (d) were
Answer: (c)
Solution: With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject — "the teacher" (singular) — so "has". The nearby plural "students" is the examiner's trap.
Source: PYQ-style

47. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He denied to help me. (b) He denied helping me yesterday night at 9 pm sharp. (c) He refused helping me. (d) He refused to help me.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Refuse" is followed by the to-infinitive — "refused to help". "Deny" takes a gerund ("denied helping"), which makes the first option wrong.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

48. Spot the error: Part P: No sooner did she enter the room / Part Q: than the lights / Part R: went off. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: "No sooner... than" with inversion ("did she enter") is perfectly correct, and "went off" is the right simple past. All parts are error-free.
Source: PYQ-style

49. Fill in the blank: I wish I ______ a millionaire.
(a) am (b) was (c) were (d) will be
Answer: (c)
Solution: After "I wish" for an unreal present situation, use the subjunctive "were" for all persons. "Was" sounds right in speech but is not the standard exam answer.
Source: PYQ-style

50. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The Ganges is one of the longest river in India. (b) The Ganges is one of the longest rivers in India. (c) The Ganges is one of longest rivers in India. (d) The Ganges is one of the most longest rivers in India.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "One of the + superlative + plural noun" is the required pattern — "one of the longest rivers". A singular "river" and double superlative "most longest" are both wrong.
Source: PYQ-style
