126. Neither the manager nor the employees _____ aware of the new policy.
(a) was (b) were (c) is (d) has been
Answer: (b)
Solution: With "neither…nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject ("employees" — plural), so "were" is correct. Option (a) tempts those who agree the verb with "manager".
Source: PYQ-style

127. The quality of the apples _____ not good.
(a) were (b) was (c) are (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: The subject is "quality" (singular); "of the apples" is an intervening phrase. Hence "was". Trap type: Intervening Phrase.
Source: PYQ-style

128. He _____ here since 2019.
(a) is living (b) has been living (c) was living (d) lived
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Since + point of time" with an action continuing up to now takes the present perfect continuous: "has been living".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

129. By the time we reached the station, the train _____.
(a) left (b) has left (c) had left (d) was leaving
Answer: (c)
Solution: Of two past actions, the earlier one takes the past perfect: the train "had left" before we reached.
Source: PYQ-style

130. One of my friends _____ going to Delhi tomorrow.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "One of + plural noun" takes a singular verb — the real subject is "one". Trap type: Intervening Phrase.
Source: PYQ-style

131. I wish I _____ a millionaire.
(a) were (b) was (c) am (d) would be
Answer: (a)
Solution: Wishes contrary to present fact take the subjunctive "were" for all persons.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

132. The committee _____ divided in their opinion.
(a) were (b) was (c) is (d) has been
Answer: (a)
Solution: When a collective noun's members act separately (indicated by "divided in their opinion"), a plural verb is used. Trap type: Collective Noun.
Source: PYQ-style

133. Hardly _____ the station when the train left.
(a) I had reached (b) did I reach (c) I reached (d) had I reached
Answer: (d)
Solution: After "hardly" at the beginning of a sentence, inversion is required, and the earlier action takes the past perfect: "had I reached".
Source: PYQ-style

134. Each of the boys _____ given a prize.
(a) were (b) have been (c) was (d) are
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Each of" always takes a singular verb: "was".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

135. She suggested that he _____ a doctor.
(a) consults (b) consult (c) consulted (d) will consult
Answer: (b)
Solution: After "suggested that", the mandative subjunctive uses the base form of the verb: "consult" (no -s, no "should" needed).
Source: PYQ-style

136. It is high time we _____ the truth.
(a) accept (b) have accepted (c) accepted (d) will accept
Answer: (c)
Solution: "It is high time" is followed by the simple past to express a present necessity: "accepted". Trap type: Sound-Right — "accept" sounds natural but is wrong.
Source: PYQ-style

137. The number of accidents _____ increasing every year.
(a) are (b) were (c) have been (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: "The number of" takes a singular verb; "a number of" would take a plural verb. Trap type: Intervening Phrase.
Source: PYQ-style

138. No sooner had she entered the room _____ the phone rang.
(a) when (b) then (c) than (d) that
Answer: (c)
Solution: "No sooner" pairs with "than"; "hardly/scarcely" pair with "when". Trap type: Sound-Right.
Source: PYQ-style

139. He denied _____ the money.
(a) stealing (b) to steal (c) steal (d) having stole
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Deny" is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive: "denied stealing".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

140. Many a soldier _____ died for the country.
(a) has (b) have (c) are (d) were
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Many a + singular noun" takes a singular verb: "has".
Source: PYQ-style

141. The teacher, along with his students, _____ gone on a trip.
(a) has (b) were (c) are (d) have
Answer: (a)
Solution: With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject ("the teacher" — singular): "has". Trap type: Intervening Phrase.
Source: PYQ-style

142. I would rather you _____ home now.
(a) go (b) went (c) will go (d) have gone
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Would rather + subject" is followed by the simple past for a present/future preference: "went".
Source: PYQ-style

143. Bread and butter _____ his daily food.
(a) is (b) were (c) are (d) have been
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Bread and butter" is treated as one combined dish, so it takes a singular verb: "is". Trap type: Sound-Right — two nouns joined by "and" usually take a plural verb, but not when they form a single idea.
Source: PYQ-style

144. Unless you _____ hard, you will not pass.
(a) work (b) will work (c) worked (d) would work
Answer: (a)
Solution: In conditional clauses with "unless", the present simple replaces the future: "work", not "will work".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

145. The Ganges _____ one of the holiest rivers of India.
(a) are (b) is (c) have been (d) were
Answer: (b)
Solution: Names of rivers, though plural-looking, are singular: "is".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

146. He is one of those students who _____ always punctual.
(a) is (b) was (c) has been (d) are
Answer: (d)
Solution: In "one of those who…", the relative pronoun "who" refers to the plural "students", so the verb is plural: "are". Trap type: Over-thinking — many pick "is" by agreeing with "one".
Source: PYQ-style

147. Scarcely had he stepped out of the house _____ it began to rain.
(a) than (b) then (c) when (d) that
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Scarcely/hardly" pair with "when"; only "no sooner" pairs with "than".
Source: PYQ-style

148. The furniture in this room _____ very old.
(a) are (b) were (c) is (d) have been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Furniture" is an uncountable noun and always takes a singular verb: "is". Trap type: Sound-Right.
Source: PYQ-style

149. Had I known about the meeting, I _____ attended it.
(a) will have (b) would (c) had (d) would have
Answer: (d)
Solution: This is an inverted third conditional ("Had I known" = "If I had known"); the main clause takes "would have + past participle": "would have attended".
Source: PYQ-style

150. Ten thousand rupees _____ a big amount for him.
(a) are (b) were (c) have been (d) is
Answer: (d)
Solution: A sum of money regarded as a single amount takes a singular verb: "is". Trap type: Sound-Right — the plural-looking "rupees" misleads many.
Source: PYQ-style
