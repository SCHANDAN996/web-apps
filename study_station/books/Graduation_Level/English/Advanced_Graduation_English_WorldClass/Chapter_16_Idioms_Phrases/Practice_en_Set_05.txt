101. The manager told the team to stop beating around the bush and come to the point.
(a) To avoid the main topic (b) To discuss in detail (c) To argue loudly (d) To praise someone indirectly
Answer: (a)
Solution: "To beat around the bush" means to talk about irrelevant things and avoid the main point. Option (b) is the opposite of the idiom's meaning.

Solution: "To lie low" means to keep oneself hidden or avoid attention, especially after trouble. It has nothing to do with physical lying down.
