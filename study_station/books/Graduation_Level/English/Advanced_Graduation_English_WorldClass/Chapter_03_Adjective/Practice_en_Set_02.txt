26. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) She is more taller than her sister. (b) She is taller than her sister. (c) She is most taller than her sister. (d) She is tall than her sister.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Taller" is already the comparative form; adding "more" creates a double comparative. "Tall than" lacks the comparative marker.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

27. Identify the adjective in the sentence: "The hungry child ate quickly."
(a) hungry (b) child (c) ate (d) quickly
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Hungry" describes the noun "child", so it is an adjective. "Quickly" describes the verb "ate", so it is an adverb.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

28. Choose the correct option: "This is the ______ book I have ever read."
(a) interesting (b) most interesting (c) more interesting (d) much interesting
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Ever read" signals a comparison among all books, so the superlative "most interesting" is required.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is senior than me. (b) He is senior from me. (c) He is senior to me. (d) He is senior of me.
Answer: (c)
Solution: Latin comparatives (senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior, prefer) take "to", not "than". This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

30. "She has ______ friends in the city, so she often feels lonely."
(a) a few (b) many (c) few (d) several
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Few" (without "a") means "almost none", which matches the lonely context. "A few" would mean some friends exist.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

31. Choose the correct order of adjectives: "She bought a ______ dress."
(a) red beautiful silk (b) red silk beautiful (c) silk red beautiful (d) beautiful red silk
Answer: (d)
Solution: The standard order is Opinion → Colour → Material: beautiful (opinion), red (colour), silk (material).
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

32. Identify the type of adjective in: "Which book do you want?"
(a) Interrogative adjective (b) Demonstrative adjective (c) Adjective of quality (d) Possessive adjective
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Which" is used with the noun "book" to ask a question, making it an interrogative adjective.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

33. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The gold is a precious metal. (b) Gold is a precious metal. (c) A gold is a precious metal. (d) An gold is a precious metal.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Material nouns used in a general sense take no article. "The gold" would refer to specific gold.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

34. "There is ______ water left in the jug; you can drink it."
(a) little (b) the few (c) few (d) a little
Answer: (d)
Solution: "A little" means "some, enough" — positive sense, matching "you can drink it". "Little" would mean almost none. Also, water is uncountable, so "few" is wrong.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

35. Choose the correct comparative sentence:
(a) Delhi is bigger than any city in India.
(b) Delhi is bigger than any other city in India.
(c) Delhi is bigger than all city in India.
(d) Delhi is bigger than every cities in India.
Answer: (b)
Solution: When comparing a member with its own group, "any other" is needed to exclude the member itself. Option (a) illogically compares Delhi with itself — a frequent trap.
Source: PYQ-style

36. Identify the adjective of quantity:
(a) He showed much patience.
(b) He is a patient man.
(c) He waited patiently.
(d) His patience is known.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Much" shows quantity (how much patience), so it is an adjective of quantity. "Patient" in (b) is an adjective of quality.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

37. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I prefer tea than coffee. (b) I prefer tea to coffee. (c) I prefer tea over than coffee. (d) I prefer tea from coffee.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Prefer" is a Latin comparative and takes "to", never "than". Same family: senior, junior, superior, inferior.
Source: PYQ-style

38. "The ______ are respected everywhere." (Choose the correct adjective used as a noun)
(a) richness (b) riches (c) richly (d) rich
Answer: (d)
Solution: "The + adjective" (the rich, the poor, the blind) denotes a whole class of people and takes a plural verb.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

39. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is the best of the two brothers.
(b) He is the better of the two brothers.
(c) He is the most best of the two brothers.
(d) He is more better of the two brothers.
Answer: (b)
Solution: For two persons/things, use the comparative ("better"); the superlative ("best") needs three or more. Options (c) and (d) are double forms.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

40. Identify the demonstrative adjective:
(a) Those mangoes are sweet.
(b) Those are sweet.
(c) He is a sweet boy.
(d) The sweetness is natural.
Answer: (a)
Solution: In (a), "those" comes before the noun "mangoes" — an adjective. In (b), "those" stands alone as a pronoun. This word-before-noun test is the key.
Source: NCERT Class 7 English Grammar

41. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) No sooner did he arrive than the meeting started. (b) No sooner did he arrive when the meeting started. (c) No sooner he arrived than the meeting started. (d) No sooner did he arrive then the meeting started.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "No sooner" pairs with "than" (not "when/then"), and inversion (did he arrive) is required after it. Examiners swap "than/then" to trap candidates.
Source: PYQ-style

42. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is the most intelligent of all other boys. (b) He is the most intelligent of all boys. (c) He is more intelligent of all boys. (d) He is the most intelligent than all boys.
Answer: (b)
Solution: With the superlative, "other" is wrong because the person is included in the group. Superlative takes "of", not "than".
Source: PYQ-style

43. "______ information he gave was not reliable."
(a) The (b) Few (c) Many (d) Several
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable, so "many/few/several" are impossible. "The" specifies the particular information he gave.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

44. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) She is more wiser than her brother. (b) She is wisest than her brother. (c) She is wiser than her brother. (d) She is most wise than her brother.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Wiser" is already comparative; "more wiser" is a double comparative — the most common adjective trap in SSC/Bank exams.
Source: PYQ-style

45. "Of the two sisters, Rina is the ______."
(a) taller (b) tallest (c) most tall (d) more tall
Answer: (a)
Solution: Between exactly two, use the comparative: "the taller of the two". Superlative needs three or more.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

46. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He gave me an useful suggestion. (b) He gave me useful an suggestion. (c) He gave me the useful suggestion an. (d) He gave me a useful suggestion.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Useful" begins with a consonant sound (/juː/), so it takes "a", not "an". Sound, not spelling, decides the article — a classic trap.
Source: PYQ-style

47. "The scenery of Kashmir is ______ than that of Shimla."
(a) beautiful (b) most beautiful (c) more beautiful (d) beautifuller
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Beautiful" is a long adjective forming its comparative with "more". "Than" confirms the comparative is needed.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

48. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) Little knowledge is a dangerous thing. (b) A little knowledge is a dangerous thing. (c) The little knowledge is a dangerous thing. (d) Few knowledge is a dangerous thing.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The proverb uses "a little knowledge" (some knowledge, but incomplete). "Knowledge" is uncountable, so "few" is wrong.
Source: PYQ-style

49. "He is ______ honest man." (Examiner's trap)
(a) an (b) a (c) the (d) no article needed
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Honest" has a silent "h" and begins with a vowel sound (/ɒ/), so "an" is correct. Spelling misleads many candidates into choosing "a".
Source: PYQ-style

50. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) My house is near to the station than yours. (b) My house is more near to the station than yours. (c) My house is nearest to the station than yours. (d) My house is nearer to the station than yours.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Nearer" is the correct comparative of "near". "More near" is a double comparative, and "nearest" is superlative, which cannot pair with "than".
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar
