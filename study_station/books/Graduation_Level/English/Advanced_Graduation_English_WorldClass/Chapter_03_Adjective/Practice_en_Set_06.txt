126. Choose the correctly spelt and correctly used adjective: "The committee gave its _____ approval to the plan."
(a) unanimous (b) unanymous (c) unanamous (d) unenimous
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Unanimous" (una = one + animus = mind) means in complete agreement. The other spellings are incorrect.
Source: NCERT-based vocabulary; PYQ-style

127. Identify the part of the sentence containing an error: "The scenery (P) / of Kashmir (Q) / are very beautiful. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) Part R
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part R has the error. "Scenery" is an uncountable noun used as singular, so the verb must be "is", not "are". This is a classic examiner trap where a plural-looking idea takes a singular verb.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

128. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is more senior than me by two years. (b) He is senior than me by two years. (c) He is senior to me by two years. (d) He is more senior to me by two years.
Answer: (c)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior and posterior take "to", not "than", and they never take "more". Hence "senior to me".
Source: PYQ-style (SSC/Bank pattern)

129. Identify the error: "No sooner did (P) / the bell ring (Q) / when the students rushed out. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part R (b) Part P (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part R is wrong. "No sooner" is paired with "than", not "when". "When" pairs with "hardly/scarcely".
Source: PYQ-style

130. Choose the correct option: "_____ information you gave me was not reliable."
(a) Few (b) A few (c) The few (d) Little
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable, so "few/a few/the few" (used with countable nouns) are impossible. "Little" fits the negative sense of the sentence.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Grammar

131. Identify the error: "He is (P) / the most cleverest (Q) / boy in the class. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part Q contains a double superlative. Use either "most clever" or "cleverest", never both together.
Source: PYQ-style

132. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) I prefer tea to coffee. (b) I prefer tea more than coffee. (c) I prefer tea over than coffee. (d) I prefer tea from coffee.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Prefer" already contains the comparative sense, so it takes "to" and never "more than".
Source: PYQ-style (Bank pattern)

133. Identify the error: "The Ganges is (P) / one of the (Q) / longest river in India. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part R is wrong. After "one of the + superlative", the noun must be plural: "longest rivers".
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

134. Choose the correct option: "Of the two brothers, Ravi is the _____."
(a) tallest (b) taller (c) more tall (d) most tall
Answer: (b)
Solution: When only two persons are compared and one is selected, we use "the + comparative": "the taller of the two". Superlative needs three or more.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Grammar

135. Identify the error: "She is (P) / comparatively (Q) / more happier today. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part R has a double comparative. "Comparatively" is followed by the positive degree ("comparatively happy"), and "more happier" is itself a double comparative.
Source: PYQ-style

136. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He gave me many advices. (b) He gave me much advices. (c) He gave me a lot of advice. (d) He gave me an advice.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Advice" is uncountable; it has no plural "advices" in this sense and cannot take "an". "A lot of advice" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Grammar

137. Identify the error: "This is (P) / the most unique (Q) / painting in the gallery. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q is the trap. "Unique" is an absolute adjective — something is either unique or not — so it cannot take "most". Say "a unique painting". Examiners love this sound-right trap.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

138. Choose the correct option: "The _____ of the two sisters is a doctor."
(a) elder (b) older (c) eldest (d) oldest
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Elder/eldest" are used for members of the same family; "elder" for comparing two. "Older/oldest" are used for age of things or persons generally.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Grammar

139. Identify the error: "He is (P) / enough tall (Q) / to reach the shelf. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q is wrong. "Enough" as an adverb comes after the adjective: "tall enough".
Source: PYQ-style

140. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) The rich should helping the poor. (b) The rich should helps the poor. (c) The rich should helped the poor. (d) The rich should help the poor.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "The + adjective" (the rich, the poor, the blind) denotes a whole class and takes a plural verb: "the rich should help".
Source: NCERT Class 10 Grammar

141. Identify the error: "My house is (P) / farther than (Q) / yours from the station. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (d)
Solution: The sentence is correct. "Farther" is used for physical distance; "further" for additional/abstract sense. This is an over-thinking trap — the sentence has no error.
Source: PYQ-style

142. Choose the correct option: "He is _____ than any other boy in the class."
(a) more intelligent (b) intelligent (c) most intelligent (d) the most intelligent
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Comparative + than any other + singular noun" is the correct structure to express the superlative idea through the comparative degree.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Grammar

143. Identify the error: "The police (P) / has arrested (Q) / the thief yesterday. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part Q is wrong. "Police" is a plural noun and takes "have", not "has". (Also, "yesterday" would require simple past, but the first clear error is the verb agreement.)
Source: PYQ-style (SSC pattern)

144. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) Little knowledge is a dangerous thing. (b) A little knowledge is a dangerous thing. (c) The little knowledge is a dangerous thing. (d) Few knowledge is a dangerous thing.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The famous proverb uses "a little" (some, though small). "Little knowledge" would mean almost no knowledge, which changes the meaning. "Knowledge" is uncountable, so "few" is impossible.
Source: PYQ-style

145. Identify the error: "He is (P) / the best (Q) / of the two players. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (c)
Solution: Part Q is wrong. With "two", use the comparative: "the better of the two players". Superlative requires three or more.
Source: PYQ-style (Bank pattern)

146. Choose the correct option: "_____ students passed the test; the teacher was disappointed."
(a) A few (b) The few (c) Few (d) Many
Answer: (c)
Solution: The disappointment shows a negative sense — almost none passed — so "few" (hardly any) is correct. "A few" would be positive and would not disappoint the teacher.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Grammar

147. Identify the error: "No sooner had (P) / she finished her speech (Q) / then the audience started clapping. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part Q (b) Part R (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part R is wrong. "No sooner" pairs with "than", not "then". This sound-alike trap (then/than) is a favourite of examiners.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

148. Choose the correct sentence:
(a) He is wisest than strong. (b) He is wiser than strong. (c) He is more wiser than strong. (d) He is more wise than strong.
Answer: (d)
Solution: When two qualities of the same person are compared, we use "more + positive degree", not the comparative form: "more wise than strong". "Wiser than strong" wrongly mixes a comparative with a positive adjective.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Grammar

149. Identify the error: "The furniture in (P) / this room are (Q) / made of teak wood. (R) / No error (S)"
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q is wrong. "Furniture" is uncountable and always singular: "the furniture is made of teak wood".
Source: PYQ-style

150. Choose the correct option: "This is _____ book I have ever read."
(a) most interestingest (b) the more interesting (c) the most interesting (d) an interesting
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Ever read" implies comparison among all books read, so the superlative "the most interesting" is required. "Interestingest" is not a word.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC/Bank pattern)
