51. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She is more taller than her sister. (b) She is taller than her sister. (c) She is tallest than her sister. (d) She is much more taller than her sister.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Taller" is already comparative, so "more taller" is a double comparative error. The simple comparative "taller" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

52. Fill in the blank: This is the ______ movie I have ever seen.
(a) good (b) better (c) best (d) most good
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Ever seen" indicates comparison among all movies, so the superlative "best" is required. "Most good" is not a valid form.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

53. Choose the correctly spelt adjective.
(a) beautifull (b) beautifel (c) beutiful (d) beautiful
Answer: (d)
Solution: The correct spelling is "beautiful" — one "l" at the end. The suffix "-ful" always has a single "l".
Source: NCERT Class 7 English

54. Fill in the blank: There isn't ______ milk left in the fridge.
(a) many (b) few (c) much (d) several
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Milk" is uncountable, so "much" is used. "Many", "few" and "several" go with countable nouns.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

55. Identify the adjective in the sentence: "The hungry child ate quickly."
(a) child (b) hungry (c) ate (d) quickly
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Hungry" describes the noun "child", so it is the adjective. "Quickly" is an adverb modifying the verb "ate".
Source: NCERT Class 6 English Grammar

56. Choose the correct order of adjectives: She bought a ______ dress.
(a) red beautiful silk (b) silk red beautiful (c) beautiful red silk (d) red silk beautiful
Answer: (c)
Solution: The standard order is opinion → size → age → shape → colour → origin → material. "Beautiful" (opinion) comes before "red" (colour), which comes before "silk" (material).
Source: PYQ-style

57. Fill in the blank: He is ______ honest man.
(a) an (b) a (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Honest" begins with a vowel sound (the "h" is silent), so "an" is used before it.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

58. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) I have few friends in Delhi is wrong always. (b) I have few friend in Delhi. (c) I have a few friend in Delhi. (d) I have a few friends in Delhi.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "A few" means "some" (positive sense) and takes a plural countable noun — "a few friends". "Few" alone would mean "almost none".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

59. Fill in the blank: Mount Everest is the ______ peak in the world.
(a) high (b) highest (c) higher (d) most high
Answer: (b)
Solution: Comparison among all peaks of the world needs the superlative "highest". "Most high" is not the correct superlative of "high".
Source: NCERT Class 7 English

60. Choose the correct comparative form of "good".
(a) gooder (b) more good (c) best (d) better
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Good" has an irregular comparison: good – better – best. "Gooder" and "more good" do not exist.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

61. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is senior than me by two years. (b) He is senior to me by two years. (c) He is more senior than me. (d) He is senior from me by two years.
Answer: (b)
Solution: Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior and prefer take "to", not "than". This is a classic examiner trap.
Source: PYQ-style

62. Spot the error: Part P: Of the two brothers, / Part Q: Ramesh is / Part R: the tallest. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: When comparing only two persons or things, the comparative is used, not the superlative. Part R should read "the taller". The superlative "tallest" needs three or more.
Source: PYQ-style

63. Fill in the blank: The ______ of the two sisters is a doctor.
(a) elder (b) older (c) eldest (d) oldest
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Elder" is used for members of the same family and is followed by "of" here; "eldest" would need three or more. "Older" is used for things or non-family comparisons.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

64. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) No sooner had he arrived when the meeting started. (b) No sooner had he arrived then the meeting started. (c) No sooner he had arrived than the meeting started. (d) No sooner had he arrived than the meeting started.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "No sooner" is always paired with "than", and inversion (had he arrived) is required. "When" and "then" are common traps.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Spot the error: Part P: He is / Part Q: more wiser / Part R: than his brother. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: Part Q has a double comparative — "wiser" already carries the comparative sense, so "more" must be dropped. Examiners love this trap.
Source: PYQ-style

66. Fill in the blank: ______ information you gave me was very useful.
(a) Many (b) The few (c) Much (d) A few
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Information" is uncountable, so "much" is correct. "Many" and "few" are used only with countable nouns.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

67. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) Gold is a precious metal. (b) The gold is a precious metal. (c) A gold is precious metal. (d) The gold is precious metal always.
Answer: (a)
Solution: Material nouns used in a general sense take no article. "The gold" would refer to specific gold, not the metal in general.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English Grammar

68. Fill in the blank: She is ______ than any other girl in the class.
(a) tall (b) tallest (c) taller (d) most tall
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Than any other + singular noun" is a comparative structure meaning she outranks all others. The superlative "tallest" cannot be followed by "than".
Source: PYQ-style

69. Spot the error: Part P: The population of India / Part Q: is greater / Part R: than China. / Part S: No error
(a) Part R (b) Part Q (c) Part P (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: The comparison is illogical — population is being compared with a country. Part R should read "than that of China". This faulty-comparison trap appears frequently in exams.
Source: PYQ-style

70. Fill in the blank: He gave me ______ advice that solved my problem.
(a) an (b) a (c) many (d) a piece of
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Advice" is uncountable and never takes "a/an" or a plural form. We say "a piece of advice" or "some advice".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

71. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is the best of the two players. (b) He is the most better of the two players. (c) He is the better of the two players. (d) He is best of two players.
Answer: (c)
Solution: With "of the two", the comparative with "the" is used — "the better of the two". The superlative "best" needs three or more.
Source: PYQ-style

72. Fill in the blank: The ______ news made everyone sad.
(a) late (b) later (c) latter (d) latest
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Latest" means most recent, which fits "news". "Latter" means the second of two; "later" refers to time sequence, not freshness.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

73. Spot the error: Part P: Little knowledge / Part Q: that he possessed / Part R: proved dangerous. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Little knowledge" means almost no knowledge, which contradicts the sense that his knowledge proved dangerous. Part P should be "The little knowledge" — meaning the small amount he had.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Fill in the blank: Of all the students, Priya writes ______.
(a) most neatly (b) neater (c) neat (d) more neat
Answer: (a)
Solution: The verb "writes" needs an adverb, and comparison among all students needs the superlative — "most neatly". "Neat" and "neater" are adjectives, not adverbs.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

75. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is comparatively richer now. (b) He is comparatively more richer now. (c) He is comparatively richest now. (d) He is comparatively most rich now.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Comparatively" already signals comparison, so pairing it with "more richer" is a double comparative. "Richer" alone is correct.
Source: PYQ-style
