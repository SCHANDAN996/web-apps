51. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Receeve (c) Riceive (d) Receive
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Recieve" is the classic trap because we hear the "ee" sound first, but the rule is "i before e except after c" — after C, E comes before I. So the answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

52. Select the synonym of ABANDON.
(a) Keep (b) Cherish (c) Forsake (d) Protect
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Keep" and "cherish" are antonyms and tempt students who misread the question as asking for the opposite. To abandon means to give up completely, so the answer is Forsake.
Source: PYQ-style

53. He ___ to school every day.
(a) goes (b) go (c) going (d) gone
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Go" tempts because it is the base form, but with third-person singular "he" in simple present, the verb takes -s. So the answer is goes.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

54. Choose the antonym of TRANSPARENT.
(a) Clear (b) Obvious (c) Fragile (d) Opaque
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Clear" and "obvious" are near-synonyms and trap careless readers. The opposite of transparent is something that blocks light, so the answer is Opaque.
Source: PYQ-style

55. One word substitution: "A person who speaks many languages."
(a) Linguist (b) Bilingual (c) Polyglot (d) Orator
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Linguist" tempts because it relates to language, but a linguist studies language scientifically and need not speak many. "Poly" (many) + "glot" (tongue) fits exactly, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: PYQ-style

56. Spot the error: Part P: "Each of the boys" / Part Q: "have done" / Part R: "his homework."
(a) Part P (b) No error (c) Part R (d) Part Q
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the plural "boys" sitting right before the verb, but the subject is "Each," which is singular. Part Q must read "has done," so the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

57. Choose the correct passive form: "She wrote a letter."
(a) A letter is written by her. (b) A letter has been written by her. (c) A letter was written by her. (d) A letter had written by her.
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Is written" tempts students who forget to shift the tense; the original is simple past, so the passive must be "was written." So the answer is "A letter was written by her."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

58. Select the correctly punctuated sentence.
(a) Its a beautiful day. (b) It's a beautiful day. (c) Its' a beautiful day. (d) It is' a beautiful day.
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Its" without the apostrophe is the possessive form and is the most common trap. Here we need the contraction of "it is," so the answer is "It's a beautiful day."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

59. Idiom: "To bite the dust."
(a) To eat quickly (b) To clean the floor (c) To suffer defeat (d) To work hard
Answer: (c)
Solution: "To eat quickly" tempts because of the literal word "bite," but idioms are never literal. The idiom means to fail or be defeated, so the answer is To suffer defeat.
Source: PYQ-style

60. Choose the correct article: "He is ___ honest man."
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
Answer: (b)
Solution: "A" tempts because "honest" begins with the consonant letter H, but the H is silent and the word starts with a vowel sound. So the answer is an.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

61. Spot the error: Part P: "The Ganges is" / Part Q: "one of the longest" / Part R: "river in India."
(a) Part P (b) Part R (c) Part Q (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that the sentence sounds correct in casual speech, but after "one of the" the noun must be plural — "one of the longest rivers." So the error is in Part R.
Source: PYQ-style

62. Choose the correct indirect speech: He said, "I am tired."
(a) He said that he was tired. (b) He said that he is tired. (c) He said that he had been tired. (d) He said that I was tired.
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Is tired" tempts because the original quote uses "am," but in reported speech with a past reporting verb, present shifts to past and "I" becomes "he." So the answer is "He said that he was tired."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

63. Select the synonym of METICULOUS.
(a) Careless (b) Hasty (c) Superficial (d) Scrupulous
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Superficial" tempts because it sounds like a fancy word, but it means shallow — the opposite of meticulous. Meticulous means showing great attention to detail, so the answer is Scrupulous.
Source: PYQ-style

64. Neither the teacher nor the students ___ present in the hall.
(a) was (b) is (c) were (d) has been
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Was" tempts because "teacher" is singular, but with "neither…nor" the verb agrees with the nearer subject, which is the plural "students." So the answer is were.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Choose the word closest in meaning to EPHEMERAL.
(a) Eternal (b) Short-lived (c) Strong (d) Artificial
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Eternal" is the antonym and traps students who guess from the word's grand sound. "Ephemera" relates to a day (Greek epi + hemera), so the answer is Short-lived.
Source: PYQ-style

66. Spot the error: Part P: "Scarcely he had reached" / Part Q: "the station when" / Part R: "the train left."
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: The sentence sounds natural, which is the trap, but when a negative adverb like "scarcely" begins a clause, inversion is required: "Scarcely had he reached." So the error is in Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

67. One word substitution: "That which cannot be avoided."
(a) Inevitable (b) Invincible (c) Incredible (d) Infallible
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Invincible" tempts because it also starts with "in-" and sounds strong, but it means "cannot be defeated." "Cannot be avoided" is the meaning of Inevitable, so the answer is Inevitable.
Source: PYQ-style

68. Choose the correct preposition: "He has been suffering ___ fever since Monday."
(a) from (b) with (c) by (d) of
Answer: (a)
Solution: "With" tempts because Hindi speakers say "bukhar se," but the fixed English collocation is "suffer from" a disease. So the answer is from.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

69. Select the antonym of FRUGAL.
(a) Extravagant (b) Economical (c) Thrifty (d) Careful
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Thrifty" and "economical" are synonyms and trap students who skim. The opposite of frugal is wasteful spending, so the answer is Extravagant.
Source: PYQ-style

70. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occurrence (b) Occurence (c) Ocurrence (d) Occurrance
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Occurence" with one R is the most common misspelling and the main trap. The verb "occur" doubles the R before "-ence," giving double C and double R, so the answer is Occurrence.
Source: PYQ-style

71. Spot the error: Part P: "No sooner did she finish" / Part Q: "her speech then" / Part R: "the audience applauded."
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Then" sounds right because Hindi uses "tab," and that is exactly the trap. The correct correlative pair is "No sooner…than," so the error is in Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style

72. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The furniture in the room are new. (b) The furnitures in the room is new. (c) The furnitures in the room are new. (d) The furniture in the room is new.
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Furnitures" tempts because we count chairs and tables, but "furniture" is an uncountable noun with no plural and takes a singular verb. So the answer is "The furniture in the room is new."
Source: PYQ-style

73. Select the meaning of the idiom "To burn the midnight oil."
(a) To waste electricity (b) To study or work late into the night (c) To light lamps at night (d) To stay awake in fear
Answer: (b)
Solution: "To light lamps at night" is the literal trap. The idiom refers to working or studying late, as people once did by oil lamp, so the answer is To study or work late into the night.
Source: PYQ-style

74. Choose the correct form: "I wish I ___ a millionaire."
(a) am (b) was (c) will be (d) were
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Was" tempts because "I was" is correct in ordinary past sentences, but "I wish" expresses an unreal condition and takes the subjunctive "were" for all persons. So the answer is were.
Source: PYQ-style

75. Select the word most nearly opposite in meaning to CANDID.
(a) Frank (b) Honest (c) Evasive (d) Blunt
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Frank" and "blunt" are synonyms of candid and trap quick readers. Candid means open and truthful; its opposite is dodging the truth, so the answer is Evasive.
Source: PYQ-style
