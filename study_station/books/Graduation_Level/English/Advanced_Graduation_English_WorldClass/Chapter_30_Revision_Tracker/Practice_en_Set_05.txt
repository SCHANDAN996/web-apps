101. Spot the error: The number of students (P) / who have completed their revision tracker (Q) / are increasing every month. (R)
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) No error (d) Part R
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is to think "students" controls the verb, but the subject is "The number", which is singular and takes a singular verb. "A number of" would be plural, but "The number of" is always singular. Part R should read "is increasing", so the answer is Part R.

106. Choose the correct passive form: "The teacher praised the boy for his consistent revision."

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to keep the present tense or add a continuous form; the original is simple past, so the passive needs "was + past participle". Only the first option preserves simple past, so the answer is "The boy was praised by the teacher for his consistent revision."

Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is keeping "yesterday" unchanged or failing to backshift the tense. In reported speech, simple past becomes past perfect and "yesterday" becomes "the previous day", so the answer is "He said that he had revised the whole chapter the previous day."
