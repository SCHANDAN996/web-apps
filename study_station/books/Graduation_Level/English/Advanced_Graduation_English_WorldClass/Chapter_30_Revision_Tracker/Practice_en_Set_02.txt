26. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Receeve (c) Riceive (d) Receive
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option "Recieve" breaks the classic "i before e except after c" rule, which is why it looks plausible to many candidates. After "c", the order is "ei", so the answer is Receive.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

27. Choose the word closest in meaning to ABANDON.
(a) Keep (b) Forsake (c) Begin (d) Protect
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Protect" is tempting because it sounds positive, but it is nearly the opposite of giving something up. To abandon is to give up completely, so the answer is Forsake.
Source: PYQ-style

28. Fill in the blank: She has been working here ______ 2015.
(a) since (b) for (c) from (d) by
Answer: (a)
Solution: "For" is tempting because it also expresses duration, but it is used with a period of time (for ten years), not a point of time. "Since" is used with a specific starting point like 2015, so the answer is since.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

29. Choose the antonym of TRANSPARENT.
(a) Clear (b) Thin (c) Fragile (d) Opaque
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Clear" is tempting because it is related to transparency, but it is a synonym, not an antonym. The opposite of transparent is opaque, so the answer is Opaque.
Source: PYQ-style

30. Spot the error: Part P: One of my friend / Part Q: has gone / Part R: to Delhi yesterday. / Part S: No error
(a) Part P (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part R is tempting because "has gone" with "yesterday" is also faulty, but the clearest structural error is in Part P: after "one of", a plural noun is required. It should be "one of my friends", so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style (Examiner's Trap: Intervening Phrase)

31. Choose the correct passive form: "The teacher praised the boy."
(a) The boy is praised by the teacher. (b) The boy has been praised by the teacher. (c) The boy had praised by the teacher. (d) The boy was praised by the teacher.
Answer: (d)
Solution: The present-tense option is tempting because the object "the boy" is singular, but the original sentence is in the simple past ("praised"), so the passive must also be in the simple past. Hence the answer is "The boy was praised by the teacher."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

32. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Ocasion (b) Occassion (c) Occasion (d) Occasionn
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Occassion" is tempting because the double "s" feels natural, but the word has double "c" and a single "s". The correct spelling is Occasion.

33. Fill in the blank: Neither of the two boys ______ done his homework.
(a) have (b) are (c) has (d) were
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Have" is tempting because "boys" is plural and sits right before the verb, but the true subject is "neither", which is singular. The singular verb is required, so the answer is has.

34. Choose the word closest in meaning to CANDID.
(a) Frank (b) Secretive (c) Rude (d) Clever
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Rude" is tempting because candid remarks can sometimes hurt, but rudeness is not the meaning of the word. Candid means open and honest, so the answer is Frank.

35. Choose the correct indirect speech: He said, "I am tired."
(a) He said that I was tired. (b) He said that he was tired. (c) He said that he is tired. (d) He said that he had tired.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The present-tense option is tempting because the reporting feels immediate, but when the reporting verb is in the past, the reported clause shifts back: "am" becomes "was". The pronoun "I" also changes to "he", so the answer is "He said that he was tired."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

36. Choose the antonym of EXPAND.
(a) Spread (b) Enlarge (c) Contract (d) Extend
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Enlarge" and "spread" are tempting because they are close relatives of expand, but they are synonyms. The opposite of expand is contract, so the answer is Contract.
Source: PYQ-style

37. Fill in the blank: The Ganges ______ one of the longest rivers in India.
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Are" is tempting because "rivers" appears just before the blank, but the subject is "The Ganges", a single river. A singular subject takes a singular verb, so the answer is is.

38. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Maintainance (b) Mentainance (c) Maintenence (d) Maintenance

39. Choose the word closest in meaning to OBSOLETE.
(a) Modern (b) Outdated (c) Useful (d) Costly
Answer: (b)

40. Fill in the blank: I ______ this film twice already.
(a) saw (b) have seen (c) had seen (d) see
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Saw" is tempting because the action happened in the past, but "already" signals an experience up to the present moment, which needs the present perfect. Hence the answer is have seen.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

41. Spot the error: Part P: The committee / Part Q: have taken / Part R: its decision unanimously. / Part S: No error
(a) Part Q (b) Part P (c) Part R (d) No error
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part R is tempting because collective nouns often confuse candidates about pronouns, but "its" is correct here since the committee acted as one body ("unanimously"). The error is in Part Q: a singular pronoun "its" demands the singular verb "has taken", so the answer is Part Q.
Source: PYQ-style (Examiner's Trap: Collective Noun)

42. Choose the best meaning of the idiom: "To burn the midnight oil."
(a) To waste electricity (b) To celebrate all night (c) To work or study late into the night (d) To light lamps at dusk
Answer: (c)
Solution: The literal option about lamps is tempting because the idiom mentions oil, but idioms are figurative. It means working or studying late at night, so the answer is "To work or study late into the night."
Source: PYQ-style

43. Fill in the blank: Hardly ______ the station when the train left.
(a) had I reached (b) I had reached (c) I reached (d) did I reach
Answer: (a)
Solution: "I had reached" is tempting because the past perfect tense is right, but after "hardly" at the start of a clause, inversion of subject and auxiliary is compulsory. The correct inverted form is "had I reached", so the answer is had I reached.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He is senior to me. (b) He is more senior than me. (c) He is senior than me. (d) He is more senior to me.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The "than" options are tempting because comparative sentences usually take "than", but Latin comparatives like senior, junior, superior and inferior take "to" and never "more". The correct sentence is "He is senior to me."
Source: PYQ-style

45. Choose the word closest in meaning to METICULOUS.
(a) Nervous (b) Hasty (c) Careless (d) Thorough
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Nervous" is tempting because meticulous people may seem anxious, but the word describes attention to detail, not emotion. Meticulous means showing great care and precision, so the answer is Thorough.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Spot the error: Part P: Scarcely he had / Part Q: entered the room / Part R: when the phone rang. / Part S: No error
(a) No error (b) Part Q (c) Part R (d) Part P
Answer: (d)
Solution: Part R is tempting because learners often confuse "when" with "then", but "scarcely... when" is the correct pairing. The real error is in Part P: after "scarcely" the auxiliary must come before the subject, i.e. "Scarcely had he", so the answer is Part P.
Source: PYQ-style

47. Choose the correct one-word substitute: "A person who speaks many languages."
(a) Linguist (b) Bilingual (c) Polyglot (d) Orator
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Linguist" is tempting because it relates to languages, but a linguist studies language scientifically and need not speak many. A person who speaks many languages is a polyglot, so the answer is Polyglot.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Fill in the blank: The principal, along with the teachers, ______ present at the meeting.
(a) was (b) were (c) have been (d) are
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Were" is tempting because "teachers" stands just before the blank, but with "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject, "the principal", which is singular. Hence the answer is was.
Source: PYQ-style (Examiner's Trap: Intervening Phrase)

49. Choose the antonym of FRUGAL.
(a) Thrifty (b) Economical (c) Extravagant (d) Careful
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Thrifty" and "economical" are tempting because they are close in meaning to frugal, but the question asks for the opposite. The antonym of frugal is extravagant, so the answer is Extravagant.
Source: PYQ-style

50. Choose the correct indirect speech: She said to me, "Do you like tea?"
(a) She asked me that did I like tea. (b) She asked me if I liked tea. (c) She asked me if I like tea. (d) She told me whether I like tea.
Answer: (b)
Solution: The option with "that did I like" is tempting because it keeps the question form, but indirect yes-no questions use "if/whether" with statement word order and a backshifted tense. The correct form is "She asked me if I liked tea", so the answer is "She asked me if I liked tea."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English
