1. He said, "I am happy."
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) He said that he was happy. (b) He said that he is happy. (c) He said that I was happy. (d) He said that he had been happy.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Reported speech में present simple "am" बदलकर past simple "was" हो जाता है, और "I" subject के अनुसार "he" हो जाता है। विकल्प (b) में present tense गलती से वैसा ही रखा गया है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English Grammar

2. She said to me, "I will help you."
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) She told me that she will help me. (b) She told me that I would help her. (c) She said me that she would help you. (d) She told me that she would help me.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "Said to me" बदलकर "told me" होता है; "will" बदलकर "would" होता है; "I" बदलकर "she" और "you" बदलकर "me" होता है। विकल्प (a) में "will" का backshift नहीं हुआ है।

3. The teacher said, "The sun rises in the east."
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) The teacher said that the sun rose in the east. (b) The teacher said that the sun was rising in the east. (c) The teacher said that the sun had risen in the east. (d) The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य (universal truths) और habitual facts का tense reported speech में नहीं बदलता, इसलिए "rises" वैसा ही रहता है। विकल्प (a) में universal truth का गलत backshift किया गया है — यह परीक्षक की क्लासिक चाल है।

4. He said, "I have finished my work."
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) He said that he has finished his work. (b) He said that he finished his work. (c) He said that he had finished his work. (d) He said that he has finished my work.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Present perfect "have finished" बदलकर past perfect "had finished" हो जाता है, और "my" बदलकर "his" हो जाता है। विकल्प (a) में present perfect वैसा ही रखा गया है, जो past reporting verb के बाद गलत है।

5. She said, "I was reading a book."

उत्तर: (d)

6. Ram said, "I go to school daily."
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) Ram said that he goes to school daily. (b) Ram said that he went to school daily. (c) Ram said that I go to school daily. (d) Ram said that he had gone to school daily.
उत्तर: (b)

7. He said to her, "Do you like tea?"
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) He asked her if she liked tea. (b) He asked her that did she like tea. (c) He asked her if she likes tea. (d) He told her if she liked tea.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Yes/no प्रश्नों को "if/whether" से report किया जाता है, प्रश्न का रूप statement ("she liked") बन जाता है, और "said to" की जगह "asked" आता है। विकल्प (b) में प्रश्न का inverted क्रम गलती से बरकरार रखा गया है।

8. She said to me, "Where do you live?"
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) She asked me where I lived. (b) She asked me where did I live. (c) She asked me where do I live. (d) She asked me where I live.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Wh-questions में wh-word बना रहता है, पर क्रम statement जैसा ("I lived") हो जाता है और tense का backshift होता है। विकल्प (b) और (c) में प्रश्न का inversion गलती से रखा गया है — यह हिंदी-भाषियों की आम गलती है।

9. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward."
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) The captain ordered the soldiers that march forward. (b) The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward. (c) The captain said the soldiers to march forward. (d) The captain requested the soldiers to march forward.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: आदेशों को "ordered/commanded + object + to-infinitive" से report किया जाता है। विकल्प (d) अर्थ बदल देता है — सेना का कैप्टन आदेश देता है, request नहीं करता।

10. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) Mother told me to not go out in the rain. (b) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (c) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain. (d) Mother told me do not go out in the rain.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: नकारात्मक आदेशों के लिए "told + object + not to" या "forbade + object + to" का प्रयोग होता है। विकल्प (a) में "not" की स्थिति "to" से पहले गलत है; "forbade" सबसे सही रूप है।

11. He said, "Alas! I have lost my wallet."
सही indirect speech चुनिए।
(a) He exclaimed with joy that he had lost his wallet. (b) He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet. (c) He said alas that he lost his wallet. (d) He cried that he has lost his wallet.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Alas!" दुख व्यक्त करता है, इसलिए इसे "exclaimed with sorrow" से report किया जाता है; "have lost" बदलकर "had lost" हो जाता है। विकल्प (a) भाव को उलट देता है — यह अर्थ का जाल है।

25. She said, "I must finish this work today."
