51. He said, "I am happy."
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that he had been happy. (c) He said that I was happy. (d) He said that he was happy.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Indirect speech में present simple "am" बदलकर past simple "was" हो जाता है, और "I" reporting verb के subject के अनुसार "he" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

52. She said to me, "I like your dress."
(a) She said me that she liked my dress. (b) She told me that she liked my dress. (c) She told me that she likes my dress. (d) She told to me that she liked my dress.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Said to me" बदलकर "told me" हो जाता है (told के बाद "to" नहीं आता); "like" का "liked" और "your" का "my" हो जाता है।

53. The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun."

हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य (universal truths) का tense indirect speech में नहीं बदलता, इसलिए "revolves" जस का तस रहता है।

57. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
(a) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain. (b) Mother told me do not go out in the rain. (c) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (d) Mother told that I should not go out in rain.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Negative imperative को "forbade + to-infinitive" या "told me not to go" से बदलते हैं। "Forbade me to go" सही संक्षिप्त रूप है।

61. He said to me, "Why did you come late?"
(a) He asked me why I came late. (b) He asked me why did I come late. (c) He asked me why I had come late. (d) He asked me that why I had come late.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Wh-question में statement word order (बिना inversion) लगता है, और past simple "did come" बदलकर past perfect "had come" हो जाता है। Wh-word के साथ "asked" के बाद "that" नहीं लगता।

63. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward."

66. He said, "Alas! My brother is dead."

72. She said to me, "Did you see the film last night?"

73. He said, "Shall I open the window?"
(a) He asked if he shall open the window. (b) He ordered to open the window. (c) He offered to open the window. (d) He said that he would open the window.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: प्रस्ताव (offer) व्यक्त करने वाले "Shall I...?" को "offered to" से बदलते हैं। साधारण "asked if he should" केवल कर्तव्य से जुड़े वास्तविक प्रश्न के लिए उपयुक्त होता।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

74. The old man said, "May you live long!"
(a) The old man said that may I live long. (b) The old man exclaimed that I might live long. (c) The old man prayed that I might live long. (d) The old man wished that I may live long.

हल: आशीर्वाद व्यक्त करने वाले optative वाक्य को "prayed/wished that" से बदलते हैं; "may" का "might" हो जाता है और "you" object के अनुसार "I" हो जाता है।

75. He said to me, "I shall help you if you work hard."
(a) He told me that he should help me if I worked hard. (b) He told me that he would help me if I had worked hard. (c) He told me that he will help me if I work hard. (d) He told me that he would help me if I worked hard.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Conditional वाक्य में main clause का "shall" बदलकर "would" हो जाता है और if-clause का "work" बदलकर "worked" (past simple) हो जाता है, past perfect नहीं।
