76. He said, "I wrote a letter yesterday."
(a) He said that he wrote a letter yesterday. (b) He said that he was writing a letter yesterday. (c) He said that he has written a letter the day before. (d) He said that he had written a letter the previous day.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) में simple past, past perfect में बदल जाता है और "yesterday" बदलकर "the previous day" हो जाता है। विकल्प (a) में tense और समय-शब्द दोनों अपरिवर्तित हैं, जो गलत है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

77. She said to me, "Do you like coffee?"
(a) She asked me if I liked coffee. (b) She asked me did I like coffee. (c) She asked me whether did I like coffee. (d) She told me if I liked coffee.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: हाँ/नहीं वाले प्रश्नों में "if/whether" के साथ साधारण वाक्य-क्रम (statement order) आता है और present tense, past में बदल जाता है। विकल्प (b) में प्रश्नवाचक क्रम "did I like" गलती से वैसा ही रखा गया है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

78. The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
(a) The teacher said that the earth revolved around the sun. (b) The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun. (c) The teacher said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (d) The teacher said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य और वैज्ञानिक तथ्यों का tense अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य में नहीं बदलता। विकल्प (a), (c) और (d) में tense गलती से बदल दिया गया है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 English

79. He said to her, "Please help me with this work."
(a) He told her to help him with that work. (b) He requested her to help him with that work. (c) He said her to please help him with this work. (d) He asked that she helps him with that work.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "please" वाले वाक्यों को "requested" के बाद infinitive लगाकर बदलते हैं। विकल्प (a) में "told" आने से "please" का विनम्र भाव खो जाता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

80. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
(a) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain. (b) Mother told me do not go out in the rain. (c) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (d) Mother asked that I should not went out in the rain.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: निषेधात्मक आज्ञा (negative imperative) को "forbade + to-infinitive" या "told me not to go" से बदलते हैं। विकल्प (c) में "said me" गलत है; "said" के साथ सीधे object pronoun नहीं आता।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

81. He said, "Alas! I have lost my wallet."
(a) He cried that he has lost his wallet. (b) He said alas that he lost his wallet. (c) He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet. (d) He exclaimed with joy that he had lost his wallet.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Alas" दुख व्यक्त करता है, इसलिए "exclaimed with sorrow" आता है और present perfect, past perfect में बदल जाता है। विकल्प (d) में "joy" आया है, जो उल्टी भावना है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

82. She said, "What a beautiful garden this is!"
(a) She said what a beautiful garden that was. (b) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful garden. (c) She asked if it was a beautiful garden. (d) She told that the garden is beautiful.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: विस्मयादिबोधक वाक्य "exclaimed that" के साथ साधारण वाक्य बन जाते हैं और विस्मय-संरचना की जगह "very" आता है। विकल्प (a) में direct speech की विस्मयादिबोधक संरचना वैसी ही रखी गई है, जो गलत है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

83. He said to me, "Will you lend me your book?"
(a) He asked me if I will lend him my book. (b) He asked me will I lend him my book. (c) He told me to lend him my book. (d) He asked me whether I would lend him my book.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य में "will" बदलकर "would" हो जाता है और प्रश्न में "whether" के साथ साधारण वाक्य-क्रम आता है। विकल्प (a) में "will" अपरिवर्तित है — एक आम जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

84. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward."
(a) The captain said the soldiers to march forward. (b) The captain told that soldiers march forward. (c) The captain requested the soldiers marching forward. (d) The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: वरिष्ठ की आज्ञा को "ordered + to-infinitive" से बदलते हैं। विकल्प (a) में "said" के साथ object गलती से लगाया गया है; "said" के साथ सीधे "the soldiers" नहीं आ सकता।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

85. He said, "I may go to Delhi tomorrow."
(a) He said that he may go to Delhi the next day. (b) He said that he might go to Delhi tomorrow. (c) He said that he might go to Delhi the next day. (d) He said that he can go to Delhi the next day.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "may" बदलकर "might" और "tomorrow" बदलकर "the next day" हो जाता है। विकल्प (b) में modal तो बदला पर समय-शब्द भूल गए — परीक्षक का क्लासिक जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

86. She said to him, "Why did you come late?"
(a) She asked him why he had come late. (b) She asked him why did he come late. (c) She asked him why had he come late. (d) She asked him why he came late.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Wh-प्रश्नों में wh-शब्द वैसा रहता है, वाक्य-क्रम साधारण हो जाता है और simple past, past perfect में बदल जाता है। विकल्प (b) में उल्टा क्रम "did he come" गलती से बरकरार है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

87. He said, "I have been working here since 2015."
(a) He said that he has been working there since 2015. (b) He said that he had worked there since 2015. (c) He said that he was working here since 2015. (d) He said that he had been working there since 2015.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present perfect continuous, past perfect continuous में बदल जाता है और "here" बदलकर "there" हो जाता है। विकल्प (a) में tense नहीं बदला।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

88. The boy said, "Hurrah! We have won the match."
(a) The boy exclaimed with joy that they had won the match. (b) The boy said hurrah that they won the match. (c) The boy exclaimed with sorrow that they had won the match. (d) The boy told that they have won the match.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Hurrah" खुशी दर्शाता है, इसलिए "exclaimed with joy" आता है; present perfect, past perfect में बदल जाता है। विकल्प (c) में "sorrow" गलत भावना है — भावना-शब्दों पर आधारित जाल।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

89. She said to me, "Let us go for a walk."
(a) She told me to go for a walk. (b) She ordered me to go for a walk. (c) She suggested that we should go for a walk. (d) She asked me let us go for a walk.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Let us" सुझाव व्यक्त करता है, जिसे "suggested that... should" से बदलते हैं। विकल्प (b) में इसे गलती से आज्ञा मान लिया गया है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

90. He said, "I shall finish the work by Monday."
(a) He said that he should finish the work by Monday. (b) He said that he shall finish the work by Monday. (c) He said that he will finish the work by Monday. (d) He said that he would finish the work by Monday.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: जब "shall" साधारण भविष्य व्यक्त करे, तो reported speech में वह "would" बन जाता है। विकल्प (c) और (b) में modal अपरिवर्तित है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

91. The old man said, "Could you show me the way to the station?"
(a) The old man asked if could I show him the way to the station. (b) The old man asked me whether I could show him the way to the station. (c) The old man requested me to show him the way to the station. (d) The old man told me to could show him the way.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Could you...?" यहाँ विनम्र प्रश्न है, जिसे "asked whether + could" से बदलते हैं। विकल्प (c) लुभावना है, पर "requested to show" प्रश्न को निवेदन में बदल देता है; परीक्षा का मानक उत्तर प्रश्नवाचक रूप ही रखता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

92. She said, "I was cooking dinner when he arrived."
(a) She said that she had been cooking dinner when he arrived. (b) She said that she had been cooking dinner when he had arrived. (c) She said that she was cooking dinner when he arrived. (d) She said that she has been cooking dinner when he arrived.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Past continuous, past perfect continuous में बदल जाता है, पर समय-उपवाक्य का simple past ("when he arrived") सामान्यतः अपरिवर्तित रहता है। विकल्प (b) में दोनों क्रियाएँ ज़रूरत से ज़्यादा बदल दी गई हैं — अति-विचार का जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

93. He said to me, "I met your brother last week."
(a) He told me that he had met my brother the previous week. (b) He said me that he met my brother last week. (c) He told me that he has met my brother the week before. (d) He told me that he had met my brother last week.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Said to me" बदलकर "told me", simple past बदलकर past perfect और "last week" बदलकर "the previous week" हो जाता है। विकल्प (d) में tense बदला पर समय-अभिव्यक्ति नहीं।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

94. The traveller said, "How tired I am!"
(a) The traveller said how tired he was. (b) The traveller told that he is very tired. (c) The traveller asked how tired he was. (d) The traveller exclaimed that he was very tired.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: विस्मयादिबोधक वाक्य "exclaimed that... very" के साथ साधारण कथन बन जाते हैं। विकल्प (a) में direct speech की "how" वाली विस्मय-संरचना वैसी ही रखी गई है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

95. He said, "I must leave now."
(a) He said that he must leave then. (b) He said that he has to leave now. (c) He said that he had to leave then. (d) He said that he would leave now.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: बाध्यता व्यक्त करने वाला "must" बदलकर "had to" और "now" बदलकर "then" हो जाता है। विकल्प (a) कुछ किताबों में स्वीकार्य है, पर परीक्षा की answer key बाध्यता के लिए "had to" को प्राथमिकता देती है; विकल्प (d) अर्थ ही बदल देता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

96. She said to the children, "Never tell a lie."
(a) She told the children never tell a lie. (b) She advised the children never to tell a lie. (c) She said the children to never telling a lie. (d) She asked the children that never tell a lie.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: सामान्य सलाह वाली निषेधात्मक आज्ञा को "advised... never to" से बदलते हैं। विकल्प (a) में infinitive संरचना छूट गई है।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

97. He said, "If I were rich, I would help the poor."
(a) He said that if he had been rich, he would have helped the poor. (b) He said that if he was rich, he will help the poor. (c) He said that if he were rich, he would help the poor. (d) He said that if he is rich, he would help the poor.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: अवास्तविक शर्त वाले वाक्यों (subjunctive "were" + "would") का tense अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य में नहीं बदलता। विकल्प (a) में third conditional में अनावश्यक बदलाव किया गया है — अति-विचार का आम जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

98. The student said to the teacher, "Sir, may I come in?"
(a) The student asked the teacher if he might go in. (b) The student respectfully asked the teacher if he might come in. (c) The student told the teacher to come in. (d) The student asked the teacher may he come in.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: "Sir" सम्मान दर्शाता है, इसलिए "respectfully asked" आता है; "may" बदलकर "might" हो जाता है। विकल्प (d) में प्रत्यक्ष प्रश्न का रूप और modal दोनों अपरिवर्तित हैं।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 English

99. Choose the correctly converted indirect form: He said, "I don't know where she lives."
(a) He said that he didn't know where she lived. (b) He said that he doesn't know where she lives. (c) He said that he didn't know where did she live. (d) He said that he hadn't known where she lived.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: दोनों present tense, past में बदल जाते हैं और भीतर का प्रश्न "where she lives" साधारण वाक्य-क्रम रखकर "where she lived" बन जाता है। विकल्प (c) में भीतर के प्रश्न का क्रम गलती से उलट दिया गया है — एक बार-बार आने वाला जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style

100. Choose the correctly converted indirect form: "Who taught you English?" she said to me.
(a) She asked me who did teach me English. (b) She asked me who taught me English. (c) She asked me who had taught me English. (d) She asked me that who had taught me English.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Who taught" में "who" कर्ता है, इसलिए शब्द-क्रम वैसा रहता है, पर simple past, past perfect में बदल जाता है। विकल्प (d) में wh-शब्द से पहले "that" गलती से जोड़ दिया गया है — परीक्षक का क्लासिक जाल।
स्रोत: PYQ-style
