26. He said, "I am happy."
(a) He said that he is happy. (b) He said that he had been happy. (c) He said that I was happy. (d) He said that he was happy.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: अप्रत्यक्ष वाक्य (indirect speech) में present simple "am" बदलकर past simple "was" हो जाता है, और "I" reporting verb के subject के अनुसार "he" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

27. She said to me, "I will help you."
(a) She told me that she will help me. (b) She told me that she would help you. (c) She told me that she would help me. (d) She said me that she would help me.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Reported speech में "will" बदलकर "would" हो जाता है, और "said to me" बन जाता है "told me"। सर्वनाम "you" श्रोता के लिए है, इसलिए वह "me" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

28. The teacher said, "The earth revolves around the sun."
(a) The teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun. (b) The teacher said that the earth revolved around the sun. (c) The teacher said that the earth had revolved around the sun. (d) The teacher said that the earth was revolving around the sun.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: सार्वभौमिक सत्य और वैज्ञानिक तथ्य indirect speech में अपना tense नहीं बदलते, इसलिए "revolves" अपरिवर्तित रहता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

29. He said, "I wrote a letter yesterday."
(a) He said that he wrote a letter yesterday. (b) He said that he had written a letter the day before. (c) He said that he has written a letter the day before. (d) He said that he had written a letter tomorrow.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: Past simple "wrote" बदलकर past perfect "had written" हो जाता है, और "yesterday" बन जाता है "the day before" (या "the previous day")।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

30. She said, "I can swim very well."
(a) She said that she could swim very well. (b) She said that she can swim very well. (c) She said that she may swim very well. (d) She said that she will swim very well.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: जब reporting verb past tense में हो, तो modal "can" बदलकर "could" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

31. He said to me, "Do you like tea?"
(a) He asked me if I liked tea. (b) He asked me that did I like tea. (c) He asked me whether I like tea. (d) He told me if I liked tea.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Yes/no प्रश्नों को "if" या "whether" से report किया जाता है, प्रश्न का रूप कथन (statement) में बदल जाता है (inversion नहीं रहता), और "like" बदलकर "liked" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

32. Mother said to me, "Do not go out in the rain."
(a) Mother told me to not go out in the rain. (b) Mother said me not to go out in the rain. (c) Mother forbade me to go out in the rain. (d) Mother asked that I do not go out in the rain.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: नकारात्मक आज्ञा (negative imperative) को "forbade + to-infinitive" या "told/ordered + not to" से report किया जाता है। "Forbade me to go" सही संरचना है; "told me to not go" यहाँ दोषपूर्ण split-infinitive शैली है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

33. He said, "Alas! I have lost my wallet."
(a) He exclaimed with joy that he had lost his wallet. (b) He said alas that he lost his wallet. (c) He exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet. (d) He cried that he has lost his wallet.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: "Alas" दुख व्यक्त करता है, इसलिए इसे "exclaimed with sorrow" से report किया जाता है, और "have lost" बदलकर "had lost" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

34. She said, "What a beautiful garden!"
(a) She said what a beautiful garden it was. (b) She told that the garden is beautiful. (c) She asked if it was a beautiful garden. (d) She exclaimed that it was a very beautiful garden.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: "What a" वाले विस्मयादिबोधक वाक्यों को "exclaimed that" के साथ "very" जोड़कर report किया जाता है, और tense past में बदल जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

35. He said to her, "Where do you live?"
(a) He asked her where did she live. (b) He asked her that where she lived. (c) He asked her where she lives. (d) He asked her where she lived.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Wh-प्रश्नों में प्रश्न-शब्द ही connector बनता है, शब्द-क्रम कथन जैसा हो जाता है, और "live" बदलकर "lived" हो जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

36. The captain said to the soldiers, "March forward."
(a) The captain told the soldiers march forward. (b) The captain requested the soldiers to march forward. (c) The captain ordered the soldiers to march forward. (d) The captain asked the soldiers that march forward.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: वरिष्ठ द्वारा दी गई आज्ञा को "ordered + object + to-infinitive" से report किया जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

37. She said, "I have been waiting here since morning."
(a) She said that she has been waiting there since morning. (b) She said that she was waiting here since morning. (c) She said that she had been waiting there since morning. (d) She said that she had waited there since morning.
उत्तर: (c)
हल: Present perfect continuous "have been waiting" बदलकर past perfect continuous "had been waiting" हो जाता है, और "here" बन जाता है "there"।

38. He said, "Let us go for a walk."
(a) He proposed that they should go for a walk. (b) He said that they should go for a walk. (c) He told to go for a walk. (d) He ordered them to go for a walk.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Let us" से शुरू होने वाले वाक्य सुझाव या प्रस्ताव व्यक्त करते हैं और इन्हें "proposed/suggested that ... should" से report किया जाता है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 9 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

39. The boy said, "I may come tomorrow."

उत्तर: (d)
हल: Reported speech में "may" बदलकर "might" और "tomorrow" बदलकर "the next day" (या "the following day") हो जाता है।

40. She said to him, "Please lend me your pen."
(a) She requested him to lend her his pen. (b) She ordered him to lend her his pen. (c) She told him that lend her his pen. (d) She asked him that please lend her his pen.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: "Please" विनम्र निवेदन दर्शाता है, जिसे "requested + object + to-infinitive" से report किया जाता है; "me" और "your" क्रमशः "her" और "his" हो जाते हैं।

42. She said, "Hurrah! We have won the match."
(a) She said hurrah that they won the match. (b) She exclaimed with sorrow that they had won the match. (c) She exclaimed with joy that they had won the match. (d) She told that they have won the match.
उत्तर: (c)

43. He said to me, "Why did you come late?"
(a) He asked me why I had come late. (b) He asked me why did I come late. (c) He asked me why I came late. (d) He asked me that why I had come late.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: Reported wh-प्रश्नों में शब्द-क्रम कथन जैसा हो जाता है, और past simple "did come" बदलकर past perfect "had come" हो जाता है। प्रश्न-शब्द के बाद "that" का प्रयोग नहीं होता।

45. She said, "I shall have finished the work by evening."

46. He said, "I don't know where she lives."
(a) He said that he didn't know where she lived. (b) He said that he doesn't know where she lives. (c) He said that he didn't know where did she live. (d) He said that he hadn't known where she lived.
उत्तर: (a)
हल: दोनों clause पीछे की ओर बदलते हैं: "don't know" बन जाता है "didn't know" और "lives" बन जाता है "lived"। Embedded question में कथन-क्रम रहता है, इसलिए "where did she live" गलत है।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

47. The traveller said, "I have been walking since dawn, and I am exhausted."
(a) The traveller said that he has been walking since dawn and was exhausted. (b) The traveller said that he had walked since dawn and is exhausted. (c) The traveller said that he was walking since dawn and had been exhausted. (d) The traveller said that he had been walking since dawn and was exhausted.
उत्तर: (d)
हल: Present perfect continuous "have been walking" बदलकर past perfect continuous हो जाता है, और present "am" बदलकर "was" हो जाता है। दोनों क्रियाएँ एक साथ बदलनी चाहिए।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

48. She said to me, "You must finish this today, or you will lose the job."
(a) She told me that I must finish that that day, or I will lose the job. (b) She told me that I had to finish that that day, or I would lose the job. (c) She told me that I must finish this today, or I would lose the job. (d) She told me that I had to finish that that day, or I will lose the job.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: बाध्यता वाला "must" बन जाता है "had to", "this/today" बन जाते हैं "that/that day", और "will" बन जाता है "would"। तीनों बदलाव एक साथ होने चाहिए।
स्रोत: NCERT कक्षा 10 अंग्रेज़ी व्याकरण

49. He said, "If I were a bird, I would fly to you."
(a) He said that if he was a bird, he would fly to me. (b) He said that if he had been a bird, he would have flown to me. (c) He said that if he were a bird, he would have flown to me. (d) He said that if he had been a bird, he would fly to me.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: अवास्तविक past conditional एक कदम और पीछे जाता है: "were" बन जाता है "had been" और "would fly" बन जाता है "would have flown"। परीक्षक "were → was" से छात्रों को फँसाते हैं, जो इसे गलती से वास्तविक past मान लेता है।
स्रोत: PYQ-शैली

50. The manager said to the clerk, "Did you not submit the report that I asked for last week?"
(a) The manager asked the clerk whether he did not submit the report that he asked for last week. (b) The manager asked the clerk whether he had not submitted the report that he had asked for the week before. (c) The manager asked the clerk if he had not submitted the report that he has asked for the week before. (d) The manager asked the clerk that had he not submitted the report he had asked for the week before.
उत्तर: (b)
हल: नकारात्मक yes/no प्रश्न को "whether/if ... not" से report किया जाता है; "did submit" बदलकर "had submitted", "asked" बदलकर "had asked", और "last week" बदलकर "the week before" हो जाता है। जाल यह है कि "did not submit" को अपरिवर्तित रख दिया जाए।
स्रोत: PYQ-शैली
