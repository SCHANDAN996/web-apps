126. The word "antebellum" literally means:
(a) against war (b) before the war (c) after the war (d) beautiful war
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option "against war" confuses "ante-" (before) with "anti-" (against). "Bellum" is Latin for war, so antebellum means "before the war", often used for the period before the American Civil War. The answer is before the war.
Source: Latin root "ante-" (before) + "bellum" (war)

127. Which word is built from the root "cred" (to believe)?

Answer: (b)
Solution: "Creature" comes from "creare" (to create) and "crescent/crescendo" from "crescere" (to grow) — these are sound-alike traps. "Credible" means believable, from "credere" (to believe). The answer is credible.
Source: Latin root "cred" (believe)

128. "The anthropologist studied the misanthrope who avoided all human contact." A misanthrope is one who:
(a) loves humanity (b) fears animals (c) studies humanity (d) hates humanity
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Philanthropist" (loves humanity) is the tempting opposite trap. "Mis-" means hate and "anthropos" means human, so a misanthrope hates humanity. The answer is hates humanity.
Source: Greek roots "misos" (hate) + "anthropos" (human)

Solution: The trap is that "diagnose" sounds acceptable as a noun, but it is a verb; the noun is "diagnosis". Part S should read "before the diagnosis was confirmed". The error is in Part S.
Source: Greek root "gnosis" (knowledge); noun/verb morphology

130. Which word means "a government ruled by the people"?
(a) democracy (b) bureaucracy (c) autocracy (d) theocracy
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Autocracy" (rule by one self) and "theocracy" (rule by God/religion) are common confusions. "Demos" means people and "kratos" means rule, so democracy is rule by the people. The answer is democracy.
Source: Greek roots "demos" (people) + "kratos" (rule)
