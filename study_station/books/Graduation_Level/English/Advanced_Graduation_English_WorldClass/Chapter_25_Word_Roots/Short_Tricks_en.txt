# 🪄 Short Tricks + Skip Strategy — Word Roots & Morphology

## Mnemonic Boxes

**Box 1 — The "Body Parts" Family**
> **CORP** = body → corpse, corporation, incorporate
> **CAPIT** = head → capital, decapitate, captain
> **PED** = foot → pedal, pedestrian, expedite
> **MAN** = hand → manual, manufacture, manipulate
> *Trick: "The CORPse lost its CAPITal head, its PEDal feet and MANual hands."*

**Box 2 — The "Love/Hate" Pair**
> **PHIL** = love → philosophy, philanthropy, bibliophile
> **MIS** = hate → misanthrope, misogynist
> *Trick: PHIL loves, MIS hates — "Phil misses his love."*

**Box 3 — The "Speaking" Roots**
> **DICT** = say → predict, verdict, dictionary
> **LOG** = word/speech → dialogue, monologue, logic
> **LOQU** = speak → eloquent, loquacious, soliloquy
> *Trick: "The DICTator's LOG of LOQUacious speeches."*

**Box 4 — The "Life & Death" Pair**
> **VIV/VIT** = life → survive, vivid, vital
> **MORT** = death → mortal, immortal, mortuary
> *Trick: VIVid life vs MORTal death.*

**Box 5 — The "Time" Root**
> **CHRON** = time → chronology, chronic, synchronize, anachronism
> *Trick: "CHRONO-logy = time-logic."*

**Box 6 — The "Rule/Power" Roots**
> **CRACY** = rule → democracy, autocracy, bureaucracy
> **ARCH** = rule/chief → monarchy, anarchy, patriarch
> *Trick: CRACY = rule by people; ARCH = rule by a chief.*

**Box 7 — The "Seeing" Roots**
> **VID/VIS** = see → video, vision, invisible, revise
> **SPECT** = look → inspect, spectator, retrospect
> *Trick: "Watch the VIDeo, inSPECT it."*

**Box 8 — The "Carrying" Root**
> **PORT** = carry → transport, export, import, portable
> *Trick: A PORT is where goods are carried.*

**Box 9 — The "Writing" Roots**
> **SCRIB/SCRIPT** = write → describe, manuscript, inscription
> **GRAPH** = write → biography, autograph, photograph
> *Trick: SCRIBble and GRAPHite both write.*

**Box 10 — The "Belief" Root**
> **CRED** = believe → credible, incredible, credit, credulous
> *Trick: CREDit card = the bank believes you'll pay.*

**Box 11 — The "Breaking" Root**
> **RUPT** = break → interrupt, erupt, corrupt, bankrupt
> *Trick: "The bankRUPT firm was disRUPTed."*

**Box 12 — The "Turning" Root**
> **VERT/VERS** = turn → convert, reverse, versatile, introvert
> *Trick: An introVERT turns inward; an extroVERT turns outward.*

**Box 13 — The "Throwing" Root**
> **JECT** = throw → reject, inject, project, eject
> *Trick: "ReJECT = throw back; inJECT = throw in; eJECT = throw out."*

**Box 14 — The "Feeling" Root**
> **PATH** = feeling/suffering → sympathy, empathy, apathy, pathetic
> *Trick: SYM-pathy = feeling together; A-pathy = no feeling.*

**Box 15 — The "Knowing" Roots**
> **SCI** = know → science, conscious, omniscient
> **GNOS/COGN** = know → diagnosis, recognize, cognition
> *Trick: SCIence = knowing; reCOGNize = know again.*

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy

**Tip 1 — Skip "unknown-root + unknown-word" combos.**
If neither the root nor any option rings a bell (e.g., a rare root like *helios* with four unfamiliar options), don't burn 60+ seconds guessing. Mark it, move on, and return only if time remains. One skipped hard question costs less than three rushed easy ones.

**Tip 2 — Skip when two options share the same root.**
If two options both contain the target root (e.g., both have *chron-*) and you can't recall the exact meaning, the question is designed to trap partial knowledge. Eliminate the two root-less options, make a quick 50-50 call, and move on within 20 seconds — don't re-read the question repeatedly.

**Tip 3 — Never skip "word-in-context" questions.**
When the word appears in a sentence, the context almost always reveals the meaning even if you don't know the root. These are high-probability marks — always attempt them, even if the vocabulary looks unfamiliar.
