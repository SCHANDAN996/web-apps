26. Choose the synonym of ABANDON.
(a) forsake (b) keep (c) cherish (d) protect
Answer: (a)
Solution: Abandon means to give up or leave completely, so "forsake" is correct. "Keep" and "cherish" are near-antonyms.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

27. Choose the synonym of BRAVE.
(a) timid (b) cowardly (c) valiant (d) fearful
Answer: (c)
Solution: Brave means showing courage; "valiant" matches it. "Timid", "cowardly" and "fearful" are antonyms.
Source: PYQ-style
