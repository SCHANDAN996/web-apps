# 📋 Important Rules & Tips — Synonyms

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A synonym must match the **part of speech** of the given word. | "Abandon" (verb) → "forsake" (verb), not "abandonment" (noun) | Options include the same root in a different form (noun/adjective) to lure you. |
| 2 | Match the **shade of meaning**, not just the broad idea. | "Frugal" = careful with money (positive); "stingy" = miserly (negative) | A near-synonym with the wrong connotation (positive vs negative) is the classic trap. |
| 3 | Check the **context** if the word appears in a sentence — many words are polysemous. | "Bank" (river bank vs financial bank) | The option matching the *other* meaning of the word is planted. |
| 4 | Use **word roots** to decode unknown words. | "Bene-" = good → benevolent, beneficial, benediction | An option that *looks* similar in spelling (e.g., "malevolent" for "benevolent") reverses the meaning. |
| 5 | **Prefix = meaning changer.** Negative prefixes (un-, dis-, in-, mis-) flip the sense. | "Credible" = believable; "incredible" = unbelievable | Synonym of the *negated* form is offered for the positive word. |
| 6 | **Suffix = word-class marker.** -tion/-ness (noun), -ous/-ful (adjective), -ify/-ize (verb). | "Beauty" (n) → "beautiful" (adj) → "beautify" (v) | Options mix classes; eliminate by suffix first. |
| 7 | **Formal vs informal register** must match. | "Commence" (formal) ↔ "begin"; "kick off" (informal) | A slang/colloquial option is planted for a formal word. |
| 8 | **Collocation test:** the synonym must fit the same sentence frame. | "Commit a crime" — "perpetrate" fits; "perform" does not | A grammatically correct but non-collocating word is offered. |
| 9 | **Degree/intensity** matters: words on a scale are not interchangeable. | "Glad" < "delighted" < "ecstatic" | An option with a much stronger or weaker intensity is the trap. |
| 10 | **Eliminate antonyms first** — usually 1–2 options are opposites in disguise. | For "expand": "contract", "shrink" are antonyms | Antonyms are dressed as synonyms using similar-looking spellings. |
| 11 | **Phrasal/idiomatic words** need meaning-based, not literal, synonyms. | "Put off" = postpone (not "remove") | Literal word-by-word translation options are planted. |
| 12 | When two options seem correct, pick the one **closest in usage frequency and register** to the given word. | "Begin" vs "inaugurate" for "commence" → "inaugurate" (both formal) | The more common everyday word tempts you away from the register-matched answer. |

## (b) Time Management — The 15/45-Second Rule

| Type | What it looks like | Time | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Direct one-word synonym; word is familiar or decodable by root/prefix. | ≤ 15 seconds | Read word → recall/decode → match → move on. Never re-read. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Word given inside a sentence; polysemous word; two close options; connotation/register judgement needed. | ≤ 45 seconds | Read the sentence → fix part of speech & tone → eliminate antonyms → apply collocation test → choose. |

**Rule of thumb:** If you don't know the word in 15 seconds, mark it, skip, and return. Synonym questions are the fastest marks in the paper — never let one unknown word eat 2 minutes.

## (c) Morphology — Root, Affix, Example

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Derived Words (Synonym clue) |
|---|---|---|
| **bene-** (Latin) | good, well | benevolent (kind), beneficial (helpful), benign (harmless) |
| **mal-** (Latin) | bad, evil | malevolent (spiteful), malicious (vicious), malady (ailment) |
| **dict-** (Latin) | say, speak | predict (foretell), dictate (command), verdict (judgement) |
| **chron-** (Greek) | time | chronic (persistent), synchronize (coordinate), anachronism (misplacement in time) |
| **phil-** (Greek) | love | philanthropist (humanitarian), bibliophile (book-lover) |
| **-cide** (Latin) | kill | homicide (murder), pesticide, regicide |
| **un- / dis- / in-** (prefix) | not, opposite of | unhappy (sad), dishonest (deceitful), inactive (idle) |
| **-ous / -ful** (suffix) | full of (adjective) | courageous (brave), joyful (delighted) |
| **-tion / -ness** (suffix) | state/quality (noun) | hesitation (reluctance), kindness (compassion) |
| **-ify / -ize** (suffix) | to make (verb) | simplify (ease), modernize (update) |

**Memory hook:** *"Root gives the meaning, prefix flips it, suffix files it."* Decode any unknown word in that order — root first, prefix second, suffix last.
