51. Choose the word closest in meaning to ABANDON.
(a) keep (b) forsake (c) cherish (d) protect
Answer: (b)
Solution: Abandon means to give up or leave completely, so forsake is the correct synonym. Keep, cherish and protect are near-opposites.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Choose the word closest in meaning to BRAVE.

Answer: (a)
Solution: Brave means showing courage, so courageous is the synonym. Timid, cowardly and fearful all express fear, the opposite sense.
