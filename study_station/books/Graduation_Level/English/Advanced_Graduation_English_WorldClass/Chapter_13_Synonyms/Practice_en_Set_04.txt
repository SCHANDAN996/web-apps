76. Choose the word closest in meaning to LUCID.
(a) murky (b) rigid (c) clear (d) brief
Answer: (c)
Solution: LUCID means clear and easy to understand. Murky is its opposite; rigid and brief are unrelated.

77. Choose the word closest in meaning to OBSTINATE.

Answer: (b)
Solution: OBSTINATE means stubbornly refusing to change one's opinion. Flexible is the opposite.
