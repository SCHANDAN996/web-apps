101. Choose the word closest in meaning to: LUCID
(a) Confusing (b) Clear and easy to understand (c) Brightly coloured (d) Lengthy
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lucid means expressed clearly and easy to understand. "Brightly coloured" is a trap based on the word's look; lucid refers to clarity of thought or expression, not colour.
Source: PYQ-style

Answer: (d)
Solution: Frugal means careful and economical with money or resources. "Greedy" is a trap — a frugal person spends little, but not out of greed.
