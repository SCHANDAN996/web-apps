# Synonyms — Practice Set 1 (Questions 1–25)
# Difficulty: Questions 1–20 Easy | Questions 21–25 Medium

1. Choose the word closest in meaning to ABANDON.
(a) keep (b) forsake (c) cherish (d) protect
Answer: (b)
Solution: Abandon means to give up or leave completely, so forsake is the closest synonym. Keep, cherish and protect are near-opposites.
Source: NCERT-based vocabulary list

2. Choose the word closest in meaning to BRAVE.
(a) timid (b) fearful (c) weak (d) courageous
Answer: (d)
Solution: Brave means showing courage, so courageous is the synonym. Timid and fearful are antonyms.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English
