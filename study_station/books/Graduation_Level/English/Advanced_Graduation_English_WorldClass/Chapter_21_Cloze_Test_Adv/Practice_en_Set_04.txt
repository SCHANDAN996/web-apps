Directions (Q. 76–80): In the following passage, there are five blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank.

Passage: Artificial intelligence is no longer a distant dream; it has quietly __(76)__ itself into everyday life. From recommending films to detecting fraud, algorithms now __(77)__ decisions that once belonged solely to humans. Yet this convenience comes at a cost: the data that __(78)__ these systems often reflects old biases, and unless developers __(79)__ these flaws, machines will simply __(80)__ them at scale.

76. Choose the correct word for blank 76.
(a) withered (b) waved (c) woven (d) woken
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Woven itself into everyday life" is the correct idiom meaning integrated deeply. "Waved" and "woken" do not fit the sense of integration; "withered" means decayed, the opposite of the intended meaning.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Choose the correct word for blank 77.
(a) shatter (b) sharpen (c) shelter (d) shape
Answer: (d)
Solution: Algorithms "shape decisions" — they influence how decisions are made. "Shatter" (destroy) and "shelter" (protect) contradict the neutral tone; "sharpen" collocates with skills, not decisions.
Source: PYQ-style

78. Choose the correct word for blank 78.
(a) copies (b) starves (c) blocks (d) fuels
Answer: (d)
Solution: Data "fuels" AI systems — it powers them. "Starves" and "blocks" are negative and contradict the passage; "copies" does not express the powering relationship.
Source: PYQ-style

79. Choose the correct word for blank 79.
(a) address (b) ignore (c) invent (d) praise
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Address these flaws" means to deal with them — the logical requirement before deployment. "Ignore" would worsen the problem; "invent" and "praise" flaws are illogical.
Source: PYQ-style

80. Choose the correct word for blank 80.
(a) conceal (b) repair (c) question (d) amplify
Answer: (d)
Solution: The warning is that machines will "amplify" biases at scale — make them bigger. "Repair" and "question" contradict the negative warning; "conceal" (hide) is not the danger described.
Source: PYQ-style

Directions (Q. 81–85): In the following passage, there are five blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank.

Passage: The monsoon is the __(81)__ of the Indian economy. A good rainfall fills reservoirs, __(82)__ rural demand, and keeps food inflation in __(83)__. A failed monsoon, however, __(84)__ through every sector — farmers lose crops, factories lose orders, and the government loses revenue. It is no __(85)__ that economists watch the skies as anxiously as farmers do.

81. Choose the correct word for blank 81.
(a) backdrop (b) backfire (c) backbone (d) backlog
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Backbone of the economy" is the standard collocation meaning the chief support. "Backfire" (go wrong), "backdrop" (background setting) and "backlog" (pending work) do not fit.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Choose the correct word for blank 82.
(a) dampens (b) boosts (c) borrows (d) bans
Answer: (b)
Solution: Good rain "boosts" rural demand — a positive effect parallel to filling reservoirs. The trap is "dampens": it looks related to rain (damp means wet) but actually means "reduces", which contradicts the positive context.
Source: PYQ-style

83. Choose the correct word for blank 83.
(a) cheque (b) check (c) chaos (d) chains
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Keep inflation in check" is the fixed idiom meaning under control. "Cheque" is a sound-alike trap; "chaos" and "chains" are not idiomatic here.
Source: PYQ-style

84. Choose the correct word for blank 84.
(a) ripples (b) retreats (c) rejoices (d) remains
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Ripples through every sector" means spreads its effects outward, like ripples in water — matching the listed chain of losses. The other options do not express spreading impact.
Source: PYQ-style

85. Choose the correct word for blank 85.
(a) hurry (b) doubt (c) wonder (d) use
Answer: (c)
Solution: "It is no wonder that…" is the fixed expression meaning "it is not surprising". "No doubt" would need a different sentence structure; "no use" and "no hurry" are illogical here.
Source: PYQ-style

Directions (Q. 86–90): In the following passage, there are five blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank.

Passage: Sleep is not a luxury but a biological __(86)__. During deep sleep, the brain __(87)__ memories, clearing out irrelevant information and strengthening what matters. Chronic sleep deprivation has been __(88)__ to poor concentration, weakened immunity and even heart disease. Yet modern lifestyles __(89)__ late nights, and many people wear their sleeplessness as a badge of honour. Scientists warn that no amount of caffeine can __(90)__ for lost sleep.

86. Choose the correct word for blank 86.
(a) necessity (b) nuisance (c) novelty (d) narrative
Answer: (a)
Solution: The contrast "not a luxury but a…" demands a word meaning something essential — "necessity". "Nuisance" (annoyance) and "novelty" (newness) break the contrast; "narrative" is irrelevant.

87. Choose the correct word for blank 87.
(a) consolidates (b) conceals (c) confuses (d) deletes
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Consolidates memories" is the scientifically accurate term — strengthening and organising them, as the next clause explains. "Deletes" contradicts "strengthening what matters".
Source: PYQ-style

88. Choose the correct word for blank 88.
(a) linked (b) limited (c) listened (d) lent
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Has been linked to" is the standard collocation for showing a researched connection. "Limited to" would mean restricted to, which distorts the meaning.

89. Choose the correct word for blank 89.
(a) discourage (b) encourage (c) endanger (d) enlighten

Solution: Modern lifestyles "encourage" late nights — the passage then criticises this. "Discourage" reverses the meaning; "endanger" and "enlighten" do not collocate with "late nights".

90. Choose the correct word for blank 90.
(a) compensate (b) campaign (c) compare (d) complain

Directions (Q. 91–95): In the following passage, there are five blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank.

Passage: The gig economy promises freedom but often delivers __(91)__. Delivery riders and freelance workers enjoy flexible hours, yet they lack the safety net that __(92)__ employees take for granted — no paid leave, no pension, no insurance. Courts in several countries have begun to __(93)__ whether such workers should be reclassified as employees. Companies argue that flexibility would be __(94)__ if rigid rules were imposed. The debate, at its heart, is about who should __(95)__ the risks of a changing economy.

91. Choose the correct word for blank 91.
(a) security (b) prosperity (c) insecurity (d) certainty
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Promises freedom but delivers…" needs a negative contrast — "insecurity", confirmed by the list of missing benefits that follows. The positive options break the "but" contrast.
Source: PYQ-style

92. Choose the correct word for blank 92.
(a) irregular (b) regular (c) reluctant (d) redundant
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Regular employees" means permanent, salaried staff — the natural contrast with gig workers. "Irregular" reverses it; "reluctant" (unwilling) and "redundant" (no longer needed) are distractors.
Source: PYQ-style

93. Choose the correct word for blank 93.
(a) ignore (b) consider (c) condemn (d) conceal
Answer: (b)
Solution: Courts "consider whether…" — examine a question. "Condemn" would pre-judge the issue; "ignore" and "conceal" contradict the active role of courts described.
Source: PYQ-style

94. Choose the correct word for blank 94.
(a) celebrated (b) enhanced (c) sacrificed (d) simplified
Answer: (c)
Solution: The companies' argument is that flexibility would be lost — "sacrificed" — under rigid rules. "Enhanced" and "celebrated" reverse the argument; "simplified" is illogical.
Source: PYQ-style

95. Choose the correct word for blank 95.
(a) burn (b) bare (c) born (d) bear
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Bear the risks" is the correct collocation meaning carry or endure. The trap is "bare", a sound-alike homophone that means "uncovered"; "born" is the past participle of birth.

Directions (Q. 96–100): In the following passage, there are five blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank. (HARD)

Passage: History is rarely the tidy story that textbooks suggest. It is a __(96)__ of competing narratives, where the victors' version often __(97)__ the voices of the defeated. The historian's task, therefore, is not merely to record events but to __(98)__ the silences in the archive — to ask who was denied the chance to speak and why. This does not mean that all interpretations are equally __(99)__; evidence must remain the final arbiter. But a discipline that refuses to question its own assumptions risks becoming not a science of the past, but a __(100)__ to the present.

96. Choose the correct word for blank 96.
(a) monument (b) museum (c) mirror (d) battleground
Answer: (d)
Solution: "A battleground of competing narratives" captures the idea of struggle between versions, matching "victors" and "defeated" in the next sentence. The other options suggest passive display, not conflict.
Source: PYQ-style

97. Choose the correct word for blank 97.
(a) drowns (b) amplifies (c) records (d) honours
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Drowns the voices of the defeated" means overwhelms and silences them — consistent with "silences in the archive" later. "Amplifies" and "honours" reverse the meaning; "records" is neutral.
Source: PYQ-style

98. Choose the correct word for blank 98.
(a) ignore (b) imitate (c) inflate (d) interrogate
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Interrogate the silences" means to question them critically — exactly what the following infinitive clause ("to ask who was denied…") explains. "Ignore" contradicts the historian's duty described.
Source: PYQ-style

99. Choose the correct word for blank 99.
(a) vain (b) valid (c) vague (d) vivid
Answer: (b)
Solution: The sentence concedes that interpretations differ but insists they are not all equally "valid" — evidence decides. "Vague" (unclear), "vain" (conceited or futile) and "vivid" (bright) do not fit the logic of evidence-based judgement.
Source: PYQ-style

100. Choose the correct word for blank 100.
(a) guide (b) stranger (c) slave (d) witness
Answer: (c)
Solution: "A slave to the present" is the idiomatic phrase meaning completely controlled by current assumptions — completing the contrast with "science of the past" and matching "refuses to question its own assumptions". "Stranger to the present" reverses the meaning, and "guide" or "witness" carry no sense of captivity.
Source: PYQ-style
