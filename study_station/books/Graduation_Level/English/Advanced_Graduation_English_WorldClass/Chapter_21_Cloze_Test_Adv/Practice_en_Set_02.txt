Questions 26–40: Easy | Questions 41–50: Medium

26. The government has decided to _______ new measures to control inflation.
(a) complete (b) compliment (c) compete (d) implement
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Implement" means to put a plan into effect, which fits the context of government measures. "Compliment" (praise) and "compete" are sound-alike distractors that make no sense here.
Source: PYQ-style

27. Despite the heavy rain, the match continued _______ interruption.
(a) within (b) without (c) with (d) beside
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Despite" signals contrast, so the match went on "without interruption." The other prepositions break the logical contrast.
Source: PYQ-style

28. The scientist's discovery proved to be a _______ in the field of medicine.
(a) breakthrough (b) breakdown (c) breakout (d) breakup
Answer: (a)
Solution: A "breakthrough" is an important discovery or advance. "Breakdown" (failure), "breakout" (escape), and "breakup" (separation) do not fit a positive scientific context.
Source: PYQ-style

29. She was _______ tired that she fell asleep immediately.
(a) so (b) too (c) very (d) much
Answer: (a)
Solution: The structure "so + adjective + that" expresses result. "Too" takes an infinitive (too tired to…), not a "that" clause.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

30. The committee _______ its report to the ministry yesterday.
(a) submitted (b) subjugated (c) substituted (d) subscribed
Answer: (a)
Solution: Reports are "submitted" (presented formally). "Subjugated" (conquered), "substituted" (replaced), and "subscribed" (signed up) are contextually wrong.
Source: PYQ-style

31. His argument was so _______ that everyone in the room was convinced.
(a) feeble (b) vague (c) hollow (d) compelling
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Compelling" means powerfully convincing, matching the result clause. "Feeble," "hollow," and "vague" all suggest weakness, contradicting "everyone was convinced."
Source: PYQ-style

32. The new policy aims to _______ the gap between the rich and the poor.
(a) widen (b) bridge (c) deepen (d) enlarge
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Bridge the gap" is the standard collocation meaning to reduce a divide. "Widen" and "deepen" express the opposite of the policy's aim.
Source: PYQ-style

33. The teacher asked the students to _______ from making noise in the library.
(a) refrain (b) restrain (c) retain (d) regain
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Refrain from" means to avoid doing something and takes the preposition "from." "Restrain" takes an object (restrain someone), not "from."
Source: NCERT Class 9 English Grammar

34. The company suffered heavy losses _______ the economic slowdown.
(a) because (b) due to (c) despite (d) although
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Due to" is followed by a noun phrase ("the economic slowdown"). "Because" and "although" need a clause; "despite" reverses the logic.
Source: PYQ-style

35. The ancient manuscript was so _______ that only a few scholars could read it.
(a) legible (b) lucid (c) obscure (d) transparent
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Obscure" means unclear or difficult to understand, fitting "only a few scholars could read it." "Legible" and "lucid" mean clear — the opposite.
Source: PYQ-style

36. He has been working in this organisation _______ 2015.
(a) for (b) from (c) since (d) by
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Since" is used with a point of time (2015); "for" is used with a duration (for nine years). This is a classic sound-right trap where learners pick "for."
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

37. The soldiers fought _______ till their last breath.
(a) cowardly (b) timidly (c) valiantly (d) reluctantly
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Valiantly" (bravely) matches the heroic tone of "till their last breath." The other options carry negative connotations that clash with the context.
Source: PYQ-style

38. The manager asked me to _______ the matter personally.
(a) look after (b) look over (c) look into (d) look up
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Look into" means to investigate a matter. "Look after" (take care of), "look over" (examine quickly), and "look up" (search a reference) do not fit.
Source: PYQ-style

39. The flood victims were provided _______ food and shelter by the NGO.
(a) to (b) by (c) for (d) with
Answer: (d)
Solution: The correct structure is "provide somebody with something." Learners often wrongly write "provided food and shelter to" here because the word order is inverted — an examiner's trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English Grammar

40. Her speech had a profound _______ on the young audience.
(a) affect (b) effect (c) infect (d) defect
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Effect" is the noun meaning influence; "affect" is usually a verb. This affect/effect confusion is a favourite examiner's trap.
Source: PYQ-style

41. The negotiations between the two countries reached a _______ when neither side agreed to compromise.
(a) deadlock (b) landmark (c) milestone (d) benchmark
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Deadlock" means a stalemate where no progress is possible, matching "neither side agreed to compromise." The other options denote achievements, not failure.
Source: PYQ-style

42. The report was _______ with factual errors, so it had to be withdrawn.
(a) devoid (b) bereft (c) empty (d) replete
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Replete with" means full of, fitting "had to be withdrawn." "Devoid of" and "bereft of" mean lacking, which contradicts the consequence.
Source: PYQ-style

43. Although the plan seemed perfect on paper, it failed to _______ the practical difficulties on the ground.
(a) account for (b) account to (c) count for (d) count on
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Account for" means to take into consideration or explain. The contrast set up by "although" demands a phrase meaning "consider," and "account to" is not standard usage.
Source: PYQ-style

44. The minister's statement was deliberately _______, leaving journalists unsure of the government's real position.
(a) explicit (b) forthright (c) ambiguous (d) candid
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Ambiguous" means open to more than one interpretation, which explains the journalists' confusion. "Explicit," "candid," and "forthright" all mean clear and direct — the opposite.
Source: PYQ-style

45. The new evidence _______ the earlier theory, confirming that the site was indeed a trading post.
(a) refutes (b) negates (c) belies (d) corroborates
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Confirming" makes clear the evidence supports the theory, and "corroborates" means confirms. The trap is "belies," which looks like "believes" but actually means contradicts.
Source: PYQ-style

46. The court _______ the earlier verdict and ordered a fresh trial.
(a) set about (b) set aside (c) set off (d) set up
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Set aside" is the legal phrasal verb meaning to annul or quash a decision. "Set about" (begin), "set off" (start a journey), and "set up" (establish) do not fit a verdict.
Source: PYQ-style

47. His _______ remarks at the function offended many guests.
(a) caustic (b) diplomatic (c) courteous (d) tactful
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Caustic" means biting or sarcastic, which explains why guests were offended. The other options are positive traits inconsistent with the consequence.
Source: PYQ-style

48. The two sisters are so alike that it is difficult to _______ one from the other.
(a) extinguish (b) diminish (c) distinguish (d) admonish
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Distinguish one from the other" is the correct collocation meaning to tell apart. The other verbs are sound-alike distractors with unrelated meanings.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

49. The project was abandoned midway _______ paucity of funds.
(a) in case of (b) instead of (c) in spite of (d) owing to
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Owing to" (because of) correctly shows cause for abandoning the project. "In spite of" would reverse the logic, and "instead of" and "in case of" do not express reason.
Source: PYQ-style

50. The author's latest novel is a _______ of his earlier works, combining their best themes.
(a) departure (b) culmination (c) deviation (d) fragment
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Culmination" means the highest point combining earlier elements, matching "combining their best themes." "Departure" and "deviation" suggest moving away, contradicting the sentence.
Source: PYQ-style
