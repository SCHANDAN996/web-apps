# 📋 Important Grammar Rules & Tips — Advanced Cloze Test

## (a) 12 High-Yield Rules

| # | Rule | Example | Examiner's Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Read the whole passage first** — never fill blank 1 before knowing the theme and tone. | A passage about "economic slowdown" needs negative/neutral words, not celebratory ones. | Option that is grammatically perfect but opposite in tone to the passage. |
| 2 | **Collocation beats grammar** — choose the word that naturally pairs with its neighbour. | "___ a decision" → *take/make* a decision, never "do a decision". | Two grammatically correct options; only one collocates ("heavy rain", not "strong rain"). |
| 3 | **Preposition is fixed by the word before/after it**, not by meaning. | "depend ___" → *on*; "accused ___" → *of*. | Hindi-logic prepositions: "discuss about", "comprise of", "married with" — all wrong. |
| 4 | **Connector must match the logic** (contrast / cause / addition / result). | "He worked hard; ___, he failed." → *nevertheless/however*. | "Therefore" planted where the sentence shows contrast. |
| 5 | **Tense consistency** — the blank's verb must match the passage's time frame. | Passage in past tense → "The scientist ___ (discover)" → *discovered*. | A present-tense option that "sounds fine" in isolation. |
| 6 | **Subject–verb agreement across intervening phrases.** | "The quality of the products ___ poor." → *is* (subject = quality). | Verb agreeing with the nearest noun ("products are") instead of the real subject. |
| 7 | **Article choice depends on sound and specificity**, not spelling. | "___ honest man" → *an* (vowel sound); "___ European" → *a*. | "An university", "a hour" — sound-vs-spelling traps. |
| 8 | **Pronoun must agree with its antecedent** in number and gender. | "Each student must bring ___ book." → *his/her/their* (formal: his or her). | "Everyone… their" vs "Everyone… his" — exam expects formal singular unless modern usage is signalled. |
| 9 | **Parallel structure** — items in a list must share the same grammatical form. | "She likes reading, swimming and ___." → *dancing* (not "to dance"). | One option breaks the -ing / infinitive pattern mid-list. |
| 10 | **Vocabulary in context** — pick the word whose *shade of meaning* fits, not the dictionary synonym. | "The ___ smell of garbage" → *pungent/offensive*, not "fragrant". | Near-synonyms: "economic" vs "economical", "historic" vs "historical". |
| 11 | **Phrasal verbs are fixed units** — test the whole chunk. | "The meeting was called ___." → *off* (cancelled). | "called up / called out" — each changes the meaning completely. |
| 12 | **Eliminate, don't select** — cross out 2–3 wrong options first; the last survivor is usually right. | If two options are near-identical in meaning, both are usually wrong. | "Sound-right" option placed first (option a) to stop you reading further. |

## (b) Time Management — The 15/45-Second Rule

| Type | What it looks like | Time | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fact-based (15 sec)** | Prepositions, articles, subject–verb agreement, fixed collocations, phrasal verbs, tense of a single verb. | ⏱️ 15 seconds | Look at the words immediately around the blank. Apply the rule. Mark and move. Never re-read the whole passage for these. |
| **Inference-based (45 sec)** | Connectors (however/therefore), tone words, vocabulary-in-context, pronoun reference, theme-dependent blanks. | ⏱️ 45 seconds | Read the sentence before AND after the blank. Ask: "Is the author agreeing, contrasting, or concluding here?" Then choose. |

**Practical split for a 10-blank cloze:** roughly 6 blanks are fact-based (≈ 90 sec) and 4 are inference-based (≈ 3 min) → total ≈ 4.5–5 minutes. If a blank crosses 60 seconds, mark your best guess and move on — a cloze blank is worth the same as any other question.

## (c) Morphology — Root, Affix, Example

| Root / Affix | Meaning | Example words | Cloze signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **bene-** (Latin) | good, well | benefit, benevolent, beneficial | Positive tone blank |
| **mal-** (Latin) | bad | malnutrition, malfunction, malice | Negative tone blank |
| **cred-** (Latin) | believe | credible, incredible, credulous | "Hard to believe" context |
| **dict-** (Latin) | say, speak | predict, contradict, verdict | Speech/report context |
| **spect-** (Latin) | look, see | inspect, prospect, retrospect | Observation context |
| **-phobia** (Greek) | fear | claustrophobia, xenophobia | Fear/aversion context |
| **anti-** (Greek) | against | antibiotic, antidote, antisocial | Opposition context |
| **pre- / post-** (Latin) | before / after | pre-war, postpone, postscript | Time-sequence blanks |
| **-able / -ible** (Latin) | capable of | readable, visible, durable | Adjective blank after "is/was" |
| **un- / in- / dis-** | not, reverse | unhappy, inactive, disagree | Negative-tone adjective blanks |

**Morphology trick in cloze:** if the blank needs an adjective and one option is a noun or verb form of the same root ("success / successful / succeed / successfully"), the *form* alone eliminates three options before you even consider meaning.
