Advanced Cloze Test — Practice Set 6 (Questions 126–150)
Difficulty: 10 Medium, 15 Hard | Includes 2 Examiner-Trap Questions

Directions (Q. 126–135): In the following passage, there are TEN blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank from the options given.

Passage: The rise of artificial intelligence has ___(126)___ a fierce debate among economists. While some argue that automation will ___(127)___ millions of jobs, others believe it will ___(128)___ entirely new industries. History offers a ___(129)___ lesson: the industrial revolution destroyed many occupations, yet it ultimately ___(130)___ living standards across the world. However, the speed of the current transformation is ___(131)___, leaving workers little time to ___(132)___ new skills. Governments must therefore ___(133)___ policies that cushion the transition, such as retraining programmes and wage insurance. If they fail to act, inequality could ___(134)___ to dangerous levels, and social unrest may become ___(135)___.

126. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 126.
(a) avoided (b) extinguished (c) silenced (d) ignited
Answer: (d)
Solution: The debate is described as "fierce", so a word meaning "started/intensified" fits. "Ignited" collocates naturally with "debate". "Extinguished" and "silenced" mean the opposite; "avoided" contradicts the context.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

127. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 127.
(a) eliminate (b) create (c) protect (d) decorate
Answer: (a)
Solution: The sentence contrasts job destruction with new industries, so "eliminate" fits the first clause. "Create" belongs to the optimistic side of the contrast, not this one.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 128.
(a) destroy (b) shrink (c) spawn (d) forbid
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Spawn" means to give rise to, matching "entirely new industries". "Destroy" and "shrink" contradict the optimistic view; "forbid" is illogical here.
Source: PYQ-style

129. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 129.
(a) cautionary (b) misleading (c) worthless (d) confusing
Answer: (a)
Solution: The historical example warns as well as informs, so "cautionary" (serving as a warning) fits best. The lesson is presented as useful, ruling out "worthless" and "misleading".
Source: PYQ-style

130. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 130.
(a) lowered (b) ignored (c) froze (d) raised
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Yet" signals contrast with job destruction, so a positive outcome is needed: "raised living standards" is the standard collocation.
Source: PYQ-style

131. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 131.
(a) unprecedented (b) negligible (c) predictable (d) sluggish
Answer: (a)
Solution: The clause says workers have "little time", implying the change is faster than ever before — "unprecedented" (never known before) fits. "Sluggish" and "negligible" contradict the context.
Source: PYQ-style

132. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 132.
(a) forget (b) acquire (c) abandon (d) fake
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Acquire new skills" is the correct collocation for retraining. "Forget" and "abandon" reverse the meaning.
Source: PYQ-style

133. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 133.
(a) abandon (b) repeal (c) devise (d) delay
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Devise policies" means to design/formulate policies, which matches the call to action. "Repeal" and "abandon" contradict the sentence's purpose.
Source: PYQ-style

134. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 134.
(a) shrink (b) escalate (c) vanish (d) stabilise
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Escalate to dangerous levels" matches the warning about inequality. "Shrink" and "vanish" would be good news, contradicting "fail to act".
Source: PYQ-style

135. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 135.
(a) impossible (b) inevitable (c) irrelevant (d) amusing
Answer: (b)
Solution: If governments fail, unrest "may become inevitable" (unavoidable) — a logical warning. "Impossible" reverses the logic of the conditional warning.
Source: PYQ-style

Directions (Q. 136–140): In the following passage, there are FIVE blanks. Choose the most appropriate word for each blank.

Passage: Coral reefs, often called the rainforests of the sea, are ___(136)___ sensitive to temperature changes. Even a rise of one or two degrees can trigger bleaching, a process ___(137)___ corals expel the algae that provide them with food. If the stress is ___(138)___, the corals starve and die. Scientists warn that unless emissions are curbed ___(139)___, most of the world's reefs could disappear within decades, taking with them the livelihoods of millions who ___(140)___ on fishing and tourism.

136. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 136.
(a) hardly (b) never (c) exquisitely (d) scarcely
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Exquisitely sensitive" means extremely/delicately sensitive, matching the next sentence about a mere one-to-two-degree rise. "Hardly" and "scarcely" would contradict the passage.
Source: PYQ-style

137. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 137.
(a) whereby (b) wherever (c) whenever (d) whereas
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Whereby" means "by which", correctly linking the process to its mechanism. "Whereas" shows contrast and "whenever" shows time — neither fits.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner-trap: relative adverbs)

138. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 138.
(a) prolonged (b) mild (c) brief (d) imaginary
Answer: (a)
Solution: Corals die only if the stress continues for long, so "prolonged" fits. "Brief" or "mild" stress would allow recovery, contradicting "starve and die".
Source: PYQ-style

139. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 139.
(a) drastically (b) slowly (c) reluctantly (d) never
Answer: (a)
Solution: The warning is urgent, so emissions must be cut "drastically" (severely). "Slowly" or "reluctantly" would not prevent the predicted disappearance.
Source: PYQ-style

140. Choose the most appropriate word for blank 140.
(a) spy (b) insist (c) impose (d) rely
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Rely on" means depend on, fitting "livelihoods". "Insist on" and "impose on" have different meanings; "spy on" is absurd here.
Source: PYQ-style

141. In the following question, a sentence is given with a blank. Choose the most appropriate option. The committee's report was so ______ that even its harshest critics could find nothing to dispute.
(a) ambiguous (b) biased (c) superficial (d) meticulous
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Meticulous" (showing great attention to detail) explains why critics found nothing to dispute. "Ambiguous", "superficial" and "biased" would invite criticism, not silence it.
Source: PYQ-style

142. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. Despite the ______ evidence presented by the prosecution, the jury remained unconvinced.
(a) irrelevant (b) flimsy (c) compelling (d) fabricated
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Despite" signals contrast: the evidence was strong ("compelling"), yet the jury was unconvinced. The other options would make "despite" illogical.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner-trap: contrast connectors)

143. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. The manager's ______ remarks, though intended as humour, offended several employees.
(a) tactful (b) caustic (c) courteous (d) gracious
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Caustic" (biting, sarcastic) explains why people were offended despite humorous intent. "Tactful", "courteous" and "gracious" would not offend anyone.
Source: PYQ-style

144. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. The two sisters looked alike, but their temperaments could not have been more ______.
(a) similar (b) identical (c) divergent (d) harmonious
Answer: (c)
Solution: "But" demands contrast with "looked alike", so "divergent" (different) fits. "Similar" and "identical" would remove the contrast.
Source: PYQ-style

145. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. His argument, though ______ constructed, collapsed under cross-examination.
(a) poorly (b) ingeniously (c) never (d) weakly
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Though" requires a positive quality contrasted with collapse: "ingeniously constructed" (cleverly built) fits. "Poorly" and "weakly" would make "though" pointless.
Source: PYQ-style

146. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. The ancient manuscript was so ______ that only a handful of scholars in the world could read it.
(a) legible (b) lucid (c) popular (d) abstruse
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Abstruse" means difficult to understand, explaining why few could read it. "Legible" and "lucid" mean clear — the opposite of what the sentence needs.
Source: PYQ-style

147. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. Rather than ______ the problem, the new policy merely swept it under the carpet.
(a) hiding (b) ignoring (c) worsening (d) addressing
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Rather than" sets up a contrast with "swept under the carpet" (hid), so the blank needs the opposite action — "addressing" (dealing with). "Ignoring" and "hiding" are synonyms of the second action, not contrasts.
Source: PYQ-style (examiner-trap: rather than contrast)

148. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. The diplomat's ______ handling of the crisis prevented a war that had seemed all but certain.
(a) clumsy (b) adroit (c) reckless (d) timid
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Adroit" means skilful, which explains the prevention of war. "Clumsy" and "reckless" handling would have caused, not prevented, disaster.
Source: PYQ-style

149. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. The scientist's theory, once dismissed as ______, is now taught in every leading university.
(a) heretical (b) orthodox (c) proven (d) mainstream
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Once dismissed... now taught" shows reversal, so the blank needs a negative label: "heretical" (contrary to accepted belief). "Orthodox" and "mainstream" would not have been dismissed.
Source: PYQ-style

150. Choose the most appropriate word to fill the blank. The novel's ending was deliberately ______, leaving readers to decide the hero's fate for themselves.
(a) conclusive (b) definitive (c) ambiguous (d) predictable
Answer: (c)
Solution: If readers must "decide for themselves", the ending was "ambiguous" (open to more than one interpretation). "Conclusive" and "definitive" would settle the fate, contradicting the clue.
Source: PYQ-style
