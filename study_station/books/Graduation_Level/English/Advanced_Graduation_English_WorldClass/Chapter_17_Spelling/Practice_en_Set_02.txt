Questions 26–40: Easy | Questions 41–50: Medium

26. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Recieve (b) Riceive (c) Receive (d) Receeve
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Receive" follows the rule "i before e except after c" — after C we write EI, so it is re-CEI-ve. "Recieve" reverses the vowels and is the most common trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

27. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occured (b) Ocurred (c) Occurrd (d) Occurred
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Occur" has double C, and the final R doubles before -ed (stressed final syllable): oc-cur-red. Single R or single C forms are wrong.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

28. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Begining (b) Beginninng (c) Beginning (d) Biginning
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Begin" doubles the N before -ing because the stress falls on the last syllable: be-gin-ning. "Begining" with one N is a frequent error.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

29. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Seperate (b) Separate (c) Sepparate (d) Saparate
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Separate" has A in the middle — remember "there is A RAT in sep-A-rat-e". "Seperate" with E is the classic examiner's trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

30. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Definately (b) Defenitely (c) Definitly (d) Definitely
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Definitely" comes from "finite" — de-FINITE-ly, with I's, not A. "Definately" is the most commonly misspelt form in exams.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

31. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Accommodate (b) Acommodate (c) Accomodate (d) Accomadate
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Accommodate" has double C AND double M — think "two Cots and two Mattresses". Both "Accomodate" and "Acommodate" drop one double letter.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

32. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Necessary (b) Neccessary (c) Neccesary (d) Necesary
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Necessary" has one C and double S — "one Collar, two Sleeves" (a shirt is necessary). "Neccessary" wrongly doubles the C.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

33. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Believe (b) Beleive (c) Bilieve (d) Belive
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Believe" follows "i before e" (no C before it): be-LIE-ve. "Beleive" reverses the vowel pair.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

34. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Grammer (b) Grammar (c) Gramar (d) Grammmer
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Grammar" ends in -AR, not -ER. Mnemonic: "A grammARian uses AR". "Grammer" is a trap because many English words end in -er.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

35. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Environment (b) Enviroment (c) Envirnment (d) Enviromant
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Environment" contains an N before M: environ-ment. "Enviroment" drops that N and is a very common error.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

36. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Goverment (b) Governmant (c) Government (d) Govarnment
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Government" keeps the N of "govern": govern-ment. "Goverment" drops the N because we often swallow it in speech — a sound-based trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

37. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Freind (b) Frend (c) Friend (d) Frind
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Friend" is an exception to "i before e" — remember "a fri-END to the END". "Freind" wrongly applies the rule.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

38. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Writting (b) Writeing (c) Writing (d) Wreting
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Write" drops the silent E before -ing and does NOT double the T: writing. "Writting" wrongly doubles the T (compare: sit → sitting, where doubling is correct).
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

39. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Tommorrow (b) Tommrow (c) Tomorow (d) Tomorrow
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Tomorrow" has one M and double R: to-mor-row. "Tommorrow" doubles the wrong letter — a classic trap.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

40. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Wensday (b) Wednasday (c) Wednsday (d) Wednesday
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Wednesday" is spelt with D-N-E-S though pronounced "Wenz-day". Spell it as "Wed-nes-day" to remember the silent first D.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

41. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Questionaire (b) Questionnaire (c) Questionnair (d) Questionairre
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Questionnaire" has double N and ends in -aire (French origin). "Questionaire" with one N is the common trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

42. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Maintainance (b) Mentainance (c) Maintenence (d) Maintenance
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Maintenance" keeps -ten- from "maintain" but changes to -ance: main-TEN-ance. "Maintainance" wrongly keeps "main" intact — the examiner's favourite trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

43. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Pronounciation (b) Pronunciation (c) Pronunciaton (d) Prononciation
Answer: (b)
Solution: Although the verb is "pronounce", the noun is "pro-NUN-ciation" — the OU becomes U. "Pronounciation" is a sound-right trap that fools most candidates.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

44. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Embarass (b) Embaras (c) Embarrass (d) Emberrass
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Embarrass" has double R and double S — "so embarrassed you turn Red and Sweat twice". Single-letter versions are wrong.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

45. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Millenium (b) Millennium (c) Milennium (d) Milenium
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Millennium" has double L and double N (from Latin "mille" = thousand, "annus" = year). "Millenium" drops one N and is a frequent exam trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

46. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Conscientous (b) Conscentious (c) Consientious (d) Conscientious
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Conscientious" contains "science" inside it: con-SCIEN-tious. Dropping the first N or changing -tious is incorrect.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

47. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Liason (b) Liaison (c) Liaision (d) Liasonn
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Liaison" (French origin) has two I's: li-AI-son. "Liason" drops the second I and is the standard trap.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

48. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Perseverance (b) Perseverence (c) Persaverance (d) Persevarence
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Perseverance" ends in -ance (like "tolerance", "endurance"). The -ence ending is the tempting wrong choice.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

49. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Exhilarate (b) Exhilirate (c) Exhilerate (d) Exhillarate
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Exhilarate" has H after X, one L, and -arate ending (from Latin "hilaris" = cheerful). "Exhilerate" wrongly copies the sound of "accelerate".
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

50. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Idiosyncrasy (b) Idiosyncracy (c) Idiosincrasy (d) Idiosyncrassy
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Idiosyncrasy" ends in -sy (Greek "syn-krasis" = mixture), not -cy. "Idiosyncracy" is the trap because words like "democracy" end in -cy.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English
