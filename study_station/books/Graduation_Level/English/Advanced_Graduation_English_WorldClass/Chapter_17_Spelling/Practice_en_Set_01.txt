1. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Receive (b) Recieve (c) Riceive (d) Receeve
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Receive" follows the rule "i before e except after c". After the letter c, we write "ei", so "receive" is correct.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

2. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occured (b) Ocurred (c) Occurred (d) Occurrd
Answer: (c)
Solution: Verbs ending in a single consonant after a stressed vowel double the consonant: "occur" becomes "occurred" with double c and double r.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

3. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Begining (b) Beginning (c) Beginninng (d) Biginning
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Begin" doubles the final n before "-ing" because the stress falls on the last syllable: "beginning".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

4. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Seperate (b) Saparate (c) Separate (d) Seprate
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Separate" has "par" in the middle — remember "there is A RAT in separate". "Seperate" is the most common misspelling.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

5. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Definately (b) Defenitely (c) Definitly (d) Definitely
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Definitely" comes from "finite" — it contains "inite", not "inate". Link it to "finite" to fix the spelling.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

6. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Accomodate (b) Accommodate (c) Acommodate (d) Accomadate
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Accommodate" has double c and double m — remember it has enough room to accommodate two Cs and two Ms.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

7. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Necessary (b) Neccessary (c) Necesary (d) Neccesary
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Necessary" has one c and double s — remember "one Collar, two Sleeves" (one c, two s's).
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

8. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Embarass (b) Embarrass (c) Emberrass (d) Embaras
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Embarrass" takes double r and double s. Think: you are so embarrassed you turn Really Red and feel Super Shy.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

9. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Grammer (b) Gramar (c) Grammar (d) Grammmer
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Grammar" ends in "-ar", not "-er". Words like "scholar" and "dollar" also end in "-ar".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

10. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Enviroment (b) Environmant (c) Envirnment (d) Environment
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Environment" contains an n before the m: "environ + ment". Do not drop the n while writing quickly.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

11. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Government (b) Goverment (c) Governmant (d) Govarnment
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Government" keeps the n of "govern" before "-ment": govern + ment = government.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

12. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Tommorrow (b) Tommrow (c) Tomorow (d) Tomorrow
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Tomorrow" has one m and double r. Compare with "borrow" — both have a single consonant followed by double r.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

13. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Wensday (b) Wednsday (c) Wednesday (d) Wadnesday
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Wednesday" is spelt with a silent d after "Wednes" (from Woden's day). The d is written but not pronounced.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

14. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Freind (b) Frind (c) Frend (d) Friend
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Friend" has "ie" — remember "I am your friend till the END" (fri-END). The "i before e" rule applies here since there is no preceding c.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

15. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Beleive (b) Bilieve (c) Believe (d) Beleeve
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Believe" follows "i before e" because there is no c before it. Contrast with "receive", where c forces "ei".
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

16. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Writting (b) Writing (c) Writeing (d) Wreting
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Write" drops the silent e before "-ing" and does not double the t: writing. "Writting" wrongly doubles the t.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

17. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Forty (b) Fourty (c) Forti (d) Fourti
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Forty" drops the u of "four" — this is an exception students must memorise. "Fourteen" keeps the u, but "forty" does not.
Source: NCERT Class 8 English

18. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Truely (b) Trully (c) Truley (d) Truly
Answer: (d)
Solution: "True" drops the e before "-ly": truly. This is an exception to the usual rule of keeping the e before a consonant suffix.
Source: NCERT Class 9 English

19. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Arguement (b) Argument (c) Arguemant (d) Argumant
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Argue" drops the e before "-ment": argument. This is a famous exception — most words keep the e before "-ment" (like "movement").
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

20. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Existance (b) Existence (c) Existense (d) Exsistence
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Existence" ends in "-ence" because the related adjective is "existent". Words ending in "-ent" adjectives take "-ence" nouns.
Source: NCERT Class 10 English

21. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Questionnaire (b) Questionaire (c) Questionnair (d) Questionair
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Questionnaire" is a French borrowing with double n and "-aire" ending. French loanwords often keep their original spelling.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Millennium (b) Millenium (c) Milennium (d) Milenium
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Millennium" has double l and double n — from Latin "mille" (thousand) + "annus" (year). The double n comes from "annus", which is the trap: students often write only one n.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Conscientous (b) Conscentious (c) Consientious (d) Conscientious
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Conscientious" comes from "conscience" plus "-ious": con-sci-en-tious. The "sci" cluster and the "i" before "ous" are both needed.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Pronounciation (b) Pronuncation (c) Pronunciation (d) Prononciation
Answer: (c)
Solution: Examiner's trap: the verb is "pronounce" but the noun is "pronunciation" — the "ou" becomes "u". Do not copy the verb's spelling into the noun.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Maintenance (b) Maintainance (c) Maintenence (d) Mentainance
Answer: (a)
Solution: Examiner's trap: the verb is "maintain" but the noun is "maintenance" — "main" becomes "mainte". The noun does not keep the "-ain" of the verb.
Source: PYQ-style
