101. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occurence (b) Ocurrence (c) Occurrence (d) Occurrance
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Occurrence" doubles both the c and the r (occur + ence). Doubling only one consonant is the classic trap.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Choose the correctly spelt word.
(a) Millennium (b) Milennium (c) Millenium (d) Milenium
Answer: (a)
Solution: "Millennium" has double l and double n (from Latin mille + annus). Most candidates drop one n.

105. Choose the correctly spelt word.

Answer: (d)
