76. Choose the correctly structured sentence:
(a) Hardly had he reached the station when the train left. (b) Hardly he had reached the station when the train left. (c) Hardly he reached the station when had the train left. (d) Hardly had reached he the station when the train left.
Answer: (a)
Solution: After a negative adverbial like "Hardly" at the start of a sentence, inversion is required: Hardly + had + subject + past participle. The first option keeps normal statement order, which is the trap, so the answer is "Hardly had he reached the station when the train left."

77. Spot the error: P: The teacher, along with his students, / Q: were invited / R: to the annual function / S: held last week.

Answer: (d)
Solution: With "along with", the verb agrees with the first subject ("The teacher", singular), not with the noun nearest the verb. The plural "students" tempts a plural verb, but the error is in Part Q, which should read "was invited".
