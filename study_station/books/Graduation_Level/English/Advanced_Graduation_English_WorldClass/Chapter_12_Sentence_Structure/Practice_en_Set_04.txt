76. Choose the correctly structured sentence:
(a) Hardly he had reached the station when the train left. (b) Hardly had he reached the station when the train left. (c) Hardly he reached the station when had the train left. (d) Hardly had reached he the station when the train left.
Answer: (b)
Solution: After negative adverbials like "Hardly" at the start of a sentence, inversion is required: Hardly + had + subject + past participle. The other options fail to invert correctly.
