1. The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point 30 m away from its foot is 45°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 15 m (b) 60 m (c) 30√3 m (d) 30 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: tan 45° = height/30, so height = 30 × 1 = 30 m. The tempting option 30√3 comes from wrongly using tan 60° instead of tan 45°, so the answer is 30 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

2. If the angle of elevation of the Sun is 60°, the length of the shadow of a pole of height 12 m is:
(a) 12√3 m (b) 12 m (c) 6√3 m (d) 4√3 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: tan 60° = 12/shadow, so shadow = 12/√3 = 4√3 m. The option 12√3 results from multiplying by √3 instead of dividing, so the answer is 4√3 m.

21. From the top of a tower 60 m high, the angles of depression of the top and bottom of a building are 30° and 60° respectively. The height of the building is:
(a) 20 m (b) 30 m (c) 40 m (d) 45 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: From depression 60° to the base: tan 60° = 60/d, so d = 60/√3 = 20√3 m. From depression 30° to the top: height difference = d tan 30° = 20√3 × (1/√3) = 20 m, so building height = 60 − 20 = 40 m. The option 20 m is the height difference, not the building height, so the answer is 40 m.
Source: PYQ-style

22. The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point A is 30°. On walking 40 m towards the tower to point B, the angle becomes 60°. The height of the tower is:
(a) 40√3 m (b) 20√3 m (c) 20 m (d) 40 m
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let height h and distance from B be x. Then h = x√3 and h = (x + 40)/√3. Equating: 3x = x + 40, so x = 20 and h = 20√3 m. The option 40√3 doubles the correct value by misplacing the 40 m, so the answer is 20√3 m.

23. From a point on the ground, the angles of elevation of the bottom and top of a flagstaff on a building are 45° and 60°. If the building is 30 m high, the height of the flagstaff is:

Solution: tan 45° = 30/d gives d = 30 m. Total height = d tan 60° = 30√3 m, so flagstaff = 30√3 − 30 = 30(√3 − 1) m. The option 30√3 is the combined height, not the flagstaff alone, so the answer is 30(√3 − 1) m.

25. A vertical pole is divided by a point on the ground such that the angles of elevation of the top from two points on either side, at distances 9 m and 16 m from the foot, are complementary. The height of the pole is:
(a) 25 m (b) 144 m (c) 12 m (d) 7 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: If angles θ and 90° − θ, then h/9 = tan θ and h/16 = cot θ = 1/tan θ. Multiplying: h²/(9 × 16) = 1, so h² = 144 and h = 12 m. The option 144 m is h², not h, so the answer is 12 m.
Source: PYQ-style
