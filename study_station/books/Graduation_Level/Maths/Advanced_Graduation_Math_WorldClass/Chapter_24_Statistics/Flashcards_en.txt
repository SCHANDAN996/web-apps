Card 1
Front: What is the formula for the mean of grouped data (direct method)?
Back: Mean = Σfᵢxᵢ / Σfᵢ, where xᵢ is the class mark and fᵢ is the frequency.

Card 2
Front: What is the relationship between Mean, Median and Mode (empirical formula)?
Back: Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean.

Card 3
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the empirical relation Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean?
Back: "M3M2" — Mode needs 3 Medians minus 2 Means. Think: "Mode is 3–2 of Mean-Median."

Card 4
Front: What is the formula for the median of grouped data?
Back: Median = l + [(N/2 − cf) / f] × h, where l = lower limit of median class, cf = cumulative frequency before it, f = its frequency, h = class width.

Card 5
Front: What is the formula for the mode of grouped data?
Back: Mode = l + [(f₁ − f₀) / (2f₁ − f₀ − f₂)] × h, where f₁ = modal class frequency, f₀ and f₂ = frequencies of the classes before and after it.

Card 6
Front: What is variance and its formula?
Back: Variance is the mean of squared deviations from the mean: σ² = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / N.

Card 7
Front: What is standard deviation?
Back: The positive square root of variance: σ = √[Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / N]. It measures spread in the same units as the data.

Card 8
Front: What is the Coefficient of Variation (CV) and when is it used?
Back: CV = (σ / x̄) × 100. It compares variability of two datasets with different means or units — lower CV means more consistent.

Card 9
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember which measure is affected by extreme values?
Back: "Mean is MEAN to outliers" — the mean is pulled by extreme values; the median stays put. Remember: Median = Middle = immune.

Card 10
Front: What is the assumed mean (shortcut) method for the mean?
Back: Mean = a + Σfᵢdᵢ / Σfᵢ, where dᵢ = xᵢ − a and a is the assumed mean.

Card 11
Front: What is the step-deviation method formula?
Back: Mean = a + (Σfᵢuᵢ / Σfᵢ) × h, where uᵢ = (xᵢ − a)/h and h is the class width.

Card 12
Front: What are quartiles Q₁, Q₂, Q₃?
Back: Q₁ = 25th percentile (median of lower half), Q₂ = median (50th percentile), Q₃ = 75th percentile (median of upper half).

Card 13
Front: What is the interquartile range (IQR)?
Back: IQR = Q₃ − Q₁. It measures the spread of the middle 50% of data and is resistant to outliers.

Card 14
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the order of measures in a right-skewed distribution?
Back: "Mean chases the tail" — in right skew: Mean > Median > Mode. In left skew, the order reverses.

Card 15
Front: If every value in a dataset is increased by k, what happens to the mean and standard deviation?
Back: Mean increases by k; standard deviation stays unchanged (adding a constant doesn't change spread).

Card 16
Front: If every value in a dataset is multiplied by k, what happens to the mean and standard deviation?
Back: Both are multiplied by k (variance is multiplied by k²).

Card 17
Front: What is the range of a dataset?
Back: Range = Maximum value − Minimum value. It is the simplest measure of dispersion.

Card 18
Front: What is the combined mean of two groups with means x̄₁, x̄₂ and sizes n₁, n₂?
Back: Combined mean = (n₁x̄₁ + n₂x̄₂) / (n₁ + n₂).

Card 19
Front: Which average is most appropriate for open-ended class intervals?
Back: The median, because it does not depend on the extreme class limits, unlike the mean.

Card 20
Front: What is the mean deviation about the mean?
Back: Mean deviation = Σ|xᵢ − x̄| / N — the average of absolute deviations from the mean.
