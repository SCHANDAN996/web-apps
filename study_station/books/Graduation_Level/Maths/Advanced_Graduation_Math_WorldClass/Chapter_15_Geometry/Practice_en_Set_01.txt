1. The sum of the interior angles of a triangle is
(a) 90° (b) 360° (c) 270° (d) 180°
Answer: (d)
Solution: A common trap is 360°, which is the sum of exterior angles of any polygon, not the interior angles of a triangle. By the angle-sum property, the three interior angles of any triangle always add up to 180°, so the answer is 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

2. Two angles are complementary. If one angle is 35°, the other is
(a) 145° (b) 55° (c) 65° (d) 45°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 145°, which is the supplement of 35°, not the complement. Complementary angles add to 90°, so 90° − 35° = 55°, so the answer is 55°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

3. Two angles are supplementary. If one angle is 110°, the other is
(a) 80° (b) 60° (c) 70° (d) 90°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 80°, obtained by wrongly subtracting from 190° or mixing up with complementary angles. Supplementary angles add to 180°, so 180° − 110° = 70°, so the answer is 70°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

4. The number of sides of a regular polygon whose each interior angle is 120° is
(a) 6 (b) 5 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 5, which comes from using 108° (the pentagon's angle) by mistake. Each exterior angle = 180° − 120° = 60°, and number of sides = 360° ÷ 60° = 6, so the answer is 6.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

5. In a circle, the angle subtended by a diameter at any point on the circumference is
(a) 45° (b) 90° (c) 60° (d) 180°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 180°, which is the angle the diameter subtends at the centre, not on the circumference. By Thales' theorem, the angle in a semicircle is always a right angle, so the answer is 90°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

6. The area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 8 cm is
(a) 40 cm² (b) 80 cm² (c) 18 cm² (d) 36 cm²
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 80 cm², which is base × height without the factor of half. Area = ½ × 10 × 8 = 40 cm², so the answer is 40 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

7. The sum of the exterior angles of any convex polygon is
(a) 360° (b) 270° (c) 180° (d) 540°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 180°, which is the interior angle sum of a triangle, not the exterior sum of a polygon. Walking once around any convex polygon you turn through one full revolution, so the answer is 360°.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

8. In a right-angled triangle, the sides containing the right angle are 6 cm and 8 cm. The hypotenuse is
(a) 9 cm (b) 12 cm (c) 14 cm (d) 10 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 14 cm, obtained by simply adding 6 and 8 instead of applying Pythagoras. Hypotenuse = √(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10 cm, so the answer is 10 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

9. Vertically opposite angles formed by two intersecting lines are always
(a) equal (b) supplementary (c) complementary (d) unequal
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "supplementary", which applies to adjacent angles on a straight line, not vertically opposite ones. Vertically opposite angles are always equal, so the answer is equal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

10. The area of a circle of radius 7 cm is (take π = 22/7)
(a) 308 cm² (b) 44 cm² (c) 154 cm² (d) 77 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 44 cm², which is the circumference 2πr, not the area. Area = πr² = (22/7) × 49 = 154 cm², so the answer is 154 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

11. An equilateral triangle has each interior angle equal to
(a) 45° (b) 60° (c) 90° (d) 120°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 90°, which confuses equilateral with right-angled triangles. All three angles are equal and sum to 180°, so each is 180° ÷ 3 = 60°, so the answer is 60°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

12. The diagonals of a rhombus
(a) bisect each other at right angles (b) are always equal (c) never intersect (d) are parallel to each other
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is "are always equal", which is true for rectangles and squares but not for a general rhombus. The diagonals of every rhombus bisect each other at 90°, so the answer is bisect each other at right angles.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

13. The length of a tangent drawn from an external point 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm is
(a) 8 cm (b) 10 cm (c) 18 cm (d) 12 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 8 cm, obtained by subtracting 13 − 5 instead of using the right triangle. Tangent² = 13² − 5² = 169 − 25 = 144, so tangent = 12 cm, so the answer is 12 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

14. The sum of the interior angles of a hexagon is
(a) 720° (b) 540° (c) 900° (d) 360°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 360°, which is the exterior angle sum, not the interior. Interior sum = (n − 2) × 180° = 4 × 180° = 720°, so the answer is 720°.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

15. In an isosceles triangle, the angle between the two equal sides is 40°. Each base angle is
(a) 40° (b) 70° (c) 80° (d) 60°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 40°, assuming base angles equal the vertex angle. Base angles are equal: (180° − 40°) ÷ 2 = 70°, so the answer is 70°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

16. The perimeter of a square with area 144 cm² is
(a) 12 cm (b) 24 cm (c) 36 cm (d) 48 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 12 cm, which is only the side length, not the perimeter. Side = √144 = 12 cm, perimeter = 4 × 12 = 48 cm, so the answer is 48 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

17. The centroid of a triangle divides each median in the ratio
(a) 1 : 1 (b) 3 : 1 (c) 2 : 1 (d) 1 : 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 1 : 1, which would be a simple midpoint, but the centroid is not the midpoint of a median. The centroid divides each median in the ratio 2 : 1 from the vertex, so the answer is 2 : 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

18. Two circles of radii 5 cm and 3 cm touch each other externally. The distance between their centres is
(a) 2 cm (b) 15 cm (c) 8 cm (d) 4 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 2 cm, which is the difference of radii used for internal touching, not external. For external contact, distance = 5 + 3 = 8 cm, so the answer is 8 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

19. The area of an equilateral triangle of side 6 cm is
(a) 9√3 cm² (b) 12√3 cm² (c) 18√3 cm² (d) 36√3 cm²
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 36√3 cm², which forgets the division by 4 in the formula. Area = (√3/4) × 6² = (√3/4) × 36 = 9√3 cm², so the answer is 9√3 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

20. If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, each pair of alternate interior angles is
(a) supplementary (b) equal (c) complementary (d) unequal
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is "supplementary", which applies to co-interior (same-side interior) angles, not alternate interior angles. Alternate interior angles between parallel lines are equal, so the answer is equal.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

21. In triangle ABC, angle A = 50° and angle B = 60°. The exterior angle at C is
(a) 70° (b) 110° (c) 120° (d) 130°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 70°, which is the interior angle at C, not the exterior angle asked for. Exterior angle = sum of the two opposite interior angles = 50° + 60° = 110°, so the answer is 110°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

22. The angle subtended by an arc at the centre of a circle is 100°. The angle subtended by the same arc at any point on the remaining part of the circle is
(a) 100° (b) 200° (c) 50° (d) 25°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 200°, which doubles instead of halving the central angle. The angle at the circumference is half the angle at the centre: 100° ÷ 2 = 50°, so the answer is 50°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

23. The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. Its area is
(a) 192 cm² (b) 28 cm² (c) 48 cm² (d) 96 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 192 cm², which is the plain product of the diagonals without the factor of half. Area = ½ × 16 × 12 = 96 cm², so the answer is 96 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

24. In a triangle, the sides are 7 cm, 24 cm and 25 cm. The triangle is
(a) acute-angled (b) obtuse-angled (c) equilateral (d) right-angled
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is "acute-angled", chosen by assuming any triangle with unequal sides is acute. Check: 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25², satisfying Pythagoras, so the triangle is right-angled, so the answer is right-angled.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

25. A chord of length 16 cm is drawn in a circle of radius 10 cm. The perpendicular distance of the chord from the centre is
(a) 5 cm (b) 8 cm (c) 6 cm (d) 12 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 8 cm, which is half the chord length, not the distance from the centre. The perpendicular bisects the chord, giving a right triangle: distance = √(10² − 8²) = √36 = 6 cm, so the answer is 6 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics
