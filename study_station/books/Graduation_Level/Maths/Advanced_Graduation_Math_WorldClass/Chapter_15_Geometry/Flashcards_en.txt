Card 1
Front: What is the sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon?
Back: (n − 2) × 180°

Card 2
Front: What is the sum of exterior angles of any convex polygon?
Back: 360°

Card 3
Front: What is the measure of each interior angle of a regular polygon with n sides?
Back: [(n − 2) × 180°] ÷ n

Card 4
Front: What is the sum of the three interior angles of a triangle?
Back: 180°

Card 5
Front: What is the measure of an exterior angle of a triangle in terms of its opposite interior angles?
Back: It equals the sum of the two opposite (remote) interior angles.

Card 6
Front: What is the area of a triangle?
Back: ½ × base × height

Card 7
Front: What is the area of an equilateral triangle with side a?
Back: (√3 ÷ 4) × a²

Card 8
Front: What is the Pythagoras theorem?
Back: In a right-angled triangle, (hypotenuse)² = (perpendicular)² + (base)²

Card 9
Front: What is the area of a circle with radius r?
Back: πr²

Card 10
Front: What is the circumference of a circle with radius r?
Back: 2πr

Card 11
Front: What is the area of a rectangle?
Back: length × breadth

Card 12
Front: What is the area of a square with side a?
Back: a²

Card 13
Front: What is the diagonal of a square with side a?
Back: a√2

Card 14
Front: What is the area of a parallelogram?
Back: base × height

Card 15
Front: What is the area of a trapezium with parallel sides a and b and height h?
Back: ½ × (a + b) × h

Card 16
Front: What is the area of a rhombus with diagonals d₁ and d₂?
Back: ½ × d₁ × d₂

Card 17
Front: What is the volume of a cube with edge a?
Back: a³

Card 18
Front: What is the total surface area of a cube with edge a?
Back: 6a²

Card 19
Front: What is the volume of a cuboid with length l, breadth b and height h?
Back: l × b × h

Card 20
Front: What is the volume of a cylinder with radius r and height h?
Back: πr²h
