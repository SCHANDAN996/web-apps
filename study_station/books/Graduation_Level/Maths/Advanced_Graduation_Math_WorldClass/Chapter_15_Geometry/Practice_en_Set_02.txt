26. In a triangle ABC, the sides are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5 and its perimeter is 72 cm. What is the area of the triangle?
(a) 108 cm² (b) 216 cm² (c) 432 cm² (d) 144 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 108 cm², which comes from taking the sides as 3, 4, 5 directly instead of scaling them. Let the sides be 3x, 4x, 5x; then 12x = 72, so x = 6 and the sides are 18, 24, 30. Since 3-4-5 is a right triangle, area = ½ × 18 × 24 = 216 cm², so the answer is 216 cm².

27. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 7. The triangle is:
(a) Acute-angled (b) Right-angled (c) Obtuse-angled (d) Equilateral
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is choosing right-angled by assuming the largest angle is 90°. The angles are 2x, 3x, 7x with 12x = 180°, so x = 15° and the largest angle is 7 × 15° = 105°, which exceeds 90°. Hence the triangle is obtuse-angled, so the answer is obtuse-angled.

28. In triangle ABC, D and E are midpoints of AB and AC respectively. If BC = 14 cm, then DE equals:
(a) 28 cm (b) 7 cm (c) 3.5 cm (d) 14 cm

Solution: The tempting wrong option is 28 cm, which comes from doubling instead of halving. By the Midpoint Theorem, the segment joining the midpoints of two sides is parallel to the third side and half of it, so DE = 14/2 = 7 cm, so the answer is 7 cm.

29. Two circles of radii 8 cm and 3 cm touch each other externally. The distance between their centres is:
(a) 11 cm (b) 5 cm (c) 24 cm (d) 8 cm

30. The length of the tangent drawn from a point 13 cm away from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm is:
(a) 8 cm (b) 18 cm (c) 12 cm (d) 10 cm

Solution: The tempting wrong option is 8 cm, obtained by wrongly subtracting 13 − 5. The radius is perpendicular to the tangent, so tangent length = √(13² − 5²) = √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12 cm, so the answer is 12 cm.

(a) 48 cm² (b) 192 cm² (c) 96 cm² (d) 120 cm²

Solution: The trap is 192 cm², obtained by forgetting the factor of ½. Area = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height = ½ × (15 + 9) × 8 = ½ × 24 × 8 = 96 cm², so the answer is 96 cm².

35. An angle is 30° more than its complement. The angle is:
