26. A can do a piece of work in 12 days and B can do it in 18 days. They work together for 4 days, after which A leaves. In how many more days will B finish the remaining work?
(a) 8 days (b) 9 days (c) 6 days (d) 10 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to compute the remaining work but then divide by the combined rate instead of B's rate alone. Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 per day; in 4 days they complete 20/36 = 5/9, leaving 4/9. B alone does 1/18 per day, so time = (4/9) ÷ (1/18) = 8 days, so the answer is 8 days.
Source: PYQ-style

27. A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a work in 14 days. In how many days can A alone finish the work?
(a) 21 days (b) 18 days (c) 24 days (d) 28 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting error is to simply double 14 to get 28, ignoring that A contributes only 2/3 of the combined effort. Let B's rate be x, then A's rate is 2x; combined 3x = 1/14, so x = 1/42 and A's rate = 1/21, so the answer is 21 days.
Source: PYQ-style

28. 12 men can complete a work in 15 days. After working for 5 days, 4 men leave. In how many days will the remaining work be completed?
(a) 15 days (b) 12 days (c) 14 days (d) 13 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to assume the remaining work still takes 10 days because one-third of the time has passed. Total work = 12 × 15 = 180 man-days; done in 5 days = 60 man-days, leaving 120. With 8 men, time = 120/8 = 15 days, so the answer is 15 days.
Source: PYQ-style

29. A and B together can do a work in 10 days, B and C together in 15 days, and A and C together in 20 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 60 days (b) 40 days (c) 120 days (d) 30 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to average the three pair-times, which has no mathematical basis. C's rate = (1/15 + 1/20 − 1/10) ÷ 2 = (4/60 + 3/60 − 6/60) ÷ 2 = (1/60) ÷ 2 = 1/120 per day, so the answer is 120 days.
Source: PYQ-style

30. A can do a work in 20 days. He works for 5 days and then B alone finishes the remaining work in 15 days. In how many days can A and B together finish the whole work?
(a) 10 days (b) 15 days (c) 12 days (d) 18 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to treat B's 15 days as his time for the whole work. In 5 days A completes 5/20 = 1/4, leaving 3/4; B's rate = (3/4) ÷ 15 = 1/20 per day. Combined rate = 1/20 + 1/20 = 1/10, so the answer is 10 days.
Source: PYQ-style

31. A pipe can fill a tank in 6 hours and another pipe can empty it in 9 hours. If both pipes are opened together, in how many hours will the tank be filled?
(a) 16 hours (b) 15 hours (c) 18 hours (d) 20 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to subtract the times (9 − 6 = 3) instead of subtracting the rates. Net rate = 1/6 − 1/9 = 3/18 − 2/18 = 1/18 per hour, so the answer is 18 hours.
Source: PYQ-style

32. A and B can do a work in 10 days, B and C in 15 days, and A and C in 20 days. In how many days will A, B and C together complete the work?
(a) 9 days (b) 120/13 days (c) 15 days (d) 8 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to add the three pair-times and divide by 3. Using LCM 60 units: A+B = 6, B+C = 4, A+C = 3 units/day; sum = 13 = 2(A+B+C), so A+B+C = 6.5 units/day. Time = 60/6.5 = 120/13 days, so the answer is 120/13 days.
Source: PYQ-style

33. 8 men or 12 women can do a work in 25 days. In how many days can 6 men and 11 women do the same work?
(a) 12 days (b) 15 days (c) 20 days (d) 18 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to add 6 men and 11 women as if they were the same unit. 8 men = 12 women, so 1 man = 1.5 women; 6 men = 9 women, giving 9 + 11 = 20 women. Since 12 women take 25 days, 20 women take 12 × 25/20 = 15 days, so the answer is 15 days.
Source: PYQ-style

34. A can do a work in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work together, but A leaves 3 days before the work is completed. In how many days is the work finished?
(a) 8 days (b) 12 days (c) 10 days (d) 9 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to subtract 3 days from the combined time of 36/5 days, which ignores that B works alone at the end. Let total time be T days: (T − 3)/12 + T/18 = 1; multiplying by 36 gives 3(T − 3) + 2T = 36, so 5T = 45 and T = 9, so the answer is 9 days.
Source: PYQ-style

35. A is 50% more efficient than B. If B can do a work in 24 days, in how many days can A do it?
(a) 12 days (b) 14 days (c) 16 days (d) 18 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to take 50% of 24 (= 12) instead of dividing by 1.5. Efficiency ratio A : B = 3 : 2, so time ratio is 2 : 3 and A's time = 24 × 2/3 = 16 days, so the answer is 16 days.
Source: PYQ-style

36. Two pipes can fill a tank in 12 minutes and 16 minutes respectively. A third pipe can empty it in 8 minutes. If all three are opened together, in how many minutes will the tank be filled?
(a) 96 minutes (b) 24 minutes (c) 36 minutes (d) 48 minutes
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to mishandle the sign of the emptying pipe or to average the times. Net rate = 1/12 + 1/16 − 1/8 = 4/48 + 3/48 − 6/48 = 1/48 per minute, so the answer is 48 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

37. A can do a work in 9 days. B is 50% more efficient than A. In how many days will B do the same work?
(a) 4.5 days (b) 6 days (c) 7 days (d) 7.5 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to reduce 9 by 50% to get 4.5 days, but a 50% increase in efficiency means time divides by 1.5, not by 2. B's time = 9/1.5 = 6 days, so the answer is 6 days.

38. 15 men can complete a work in 20 days working 8 hours a day. In how many days can 20 men complete the same work working 10 hours a day?
(a) 16 days (b) 10 days (c) 14 days (d) 12 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to scale only by men and forget the hours per day. Total work = 15 × 20 × 8 = 2400 man-hours; with 20 men at 10 hours/day, days = 2400/(20 × 10) = 12, so the answer is 12 days.
Source: PYQ-style

39. A and B together can finish a work in 12 days. A alone can finish it in 20 days. If they work on alternate days starting with A, in how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 26 days (b) 23 days (c) 24 days (d) 25 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to take the combined 12 days and double it. B's rate = 1/12 − 1/20 = 1/30; in each 2-day cycle work done = 1/20 + 1/30 = 1/12. After 12 cycles, i.e. 24 days, the work is exactly complete, so the answer is 24 days.
Source: PYQ-style

40. A can do 1/3 of a work in 5 days and B can do 1/4 of the same work in 3 days. Working together, in how many days will they complete the work?
(a) 8 days (b) 6 2/3 days (c) 7 days (d) 6 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to compare 5 days and 3 days directly without scaling to the full work. A's full time = 5 × 3 = 15 days; B's full time = 3 × 4 = 12 days. Combined rate = 1/15 + 1/12 = 4/60 + 5/60 = 3/20, so time = 20/3 = 6 2/3 days, so the answer is 6 2/3 days.
Source: PYQ-style

41. A works twice as fast as B and together they finish a work in 8 days. B started the work alone and worked for 6 days; then A joined him. In how many total days was the work finished?
(a) 12 days (b) 11 days (c) 10 days (d) 14 days
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to add 6 days to the full combined time of 8 days. Let B's rate be x, A's rate 2x; 3x = 1/8, so x = 1/24. In 6 days B completes 6/24 = 1/4, leaving 3/4, which together they do in (3/4) ÷ (1/8) = 6 days; total = 12 days, so the answer is 12 days.
Source: PYQ-style

42. A pipe fills a tank in 4 hours. Due to a leak at the bottom, it takes 6 hours to fill the tank. In how many hours can the leak alone empty the full tank?
(a) 12 hours (b) 10 hours (c) 14 hours (d) 8 hours
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to take the difference 6 − 4 = 2 hours as the leak's time. Leak's rate = 1/4 − 1/6 = 3/12 − 2/12 = 1/12 per hour, so the answer is 12 hours.
Source: PYQ-style

43. 5 men or 8 women can earn ₹520 per day. How much will 7 men and 12 women earn per day?
(a) ₹1,640 (b) ₹1,508 (c) ₹1,560 (d) ₹1,400
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to treat men and women as equal earners. 1 man earns 520/5 = ₹104 and 1 woman earns 520/8 = ₹65; total = 7 × 104 + 12 × 65 = 728 + 780 = ₹1,508, so the answer is ₹1,508.
Source: PYQ-style

44. A can do a work in 25 days. B is 25% more efficient than A. They work together for 5 days, then C joins them and the work is finished in 5 more days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 25 days (b) 30 days (c) 40 days (d) 50 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to ignore C and assume A and B finish in 10 days. B's time = 25/1.25 = 20 days; in 10 days A does 10/25 = 2/5 and B does 10/20 = 1/2, together 9/10, leaving 1/10. C does this 1/10 in 5 days, so C's full time = 50 days, so the answer is 50 days.
Source: PYQ-style

45. A and B can do a work in 18 and 24 days respectively. They work together for 6 days and then A leaves. B continues and C joins him after 2 more days; the work is then finished in 4 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 36 days (b) 24 days (c) 48 days (d) 72 days
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to forget B's 2 solo days in the middle. A works 6 days = 6/18 = 1/3; B works 6 + 2 + 4 = 12 days = 12/24 = 1/2; together 5/6, leaving 1/6. C does 1/6 in 4 days, so C's full time = 4 × 6 = 24 days, so the answer is 24 days.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Three pipes A, B and C can fill a tank in 10, 15 and 30 hours respectively. If all three are opened together, in how many hours will the tank be filled?

Solution: The trap is to average the three times. Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 + 1/30 = 3/30 + 2/30 + 1/30 = 6/30 = 1/5 per hour, so the answer is 5 hours.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to treat B's 24 days as his full-work time. A completes 4/16 = 1/4 in 4 days, leaving 3/4; B does 3/4 of the work in 24 days, so B's full time = 24 × 4/3 = 32 days, so the answer is 32 days.

48. 10 men can build a wall in 12 days. After 4 days, 5 more men join. In how many total days will the wall be completed?

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to assume the work still takes 12 days total. Total work = 10 × 12 = 120 man-days; done in 4 days = 40, leaving 80. With 15 men, time = 80/15 = 5 1/3 days; total = 4 + 5 1/3 = 9 1/3 days, so the answer is 9 1/3 days.

49. A is 60% more efficient than B. Together they can complete a work in 10 days. In how many days can B alone complete the work?
(a) 24 days (b) 28 days (c) 26 days (d) 30 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to add 60% of 10 to 10, giving 16. Efficiency ratio A : B = 8 : 5, so combined = 13 parts; B's rate = (5/13) × (1/10) = 1/26 per day, so the answer is 26 days.

50. A and B working separately can do a work in 10 and 30 days respectively. They work together, but A leaves 2 days before completion. In how many days is the work completed?
(a) 8 days (b) 10 days (c) 9 days (d) 11 days
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to use the combined time of 7.5 days directly without adjusting for A's early exit. Let total time be T: (T − 2)/10 + T/30 = 1; multiplying by 30 gives 3(T − 2) + T = 30, so 4T = 36 and T = 9, so the answer is 9 days.
