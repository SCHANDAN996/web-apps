# Chapter 11: Time & Work (Comprehensive Graduation Level Foundation)

## 1. Fundamentals of Time, Work, and Efficiency
Time and Work problems are central to quantitative aptitude examinations at the graduation level (SSC CGL Tier-2, IBPS PO, CDS, State PCS). The subject is governed by the universal conservation of work:
$$\text{Work Done} = \text{Efficiency} \times \text{Time}$$

### Core Definitions:
- **Total Work (W):** The complete quantum of physical task, standardized either as 1 complete unit or as an integer multiple of units (LCM of individual time periods).
- **Time (T):** The duration (in days, hours, or minutes) required to execute the assigned work.
- **Efficiency (E):** The rate at which work is executed per unit time (units of work completed per day/hour).
  $$\text{Efficiency} = \frac{\text{Work}}{\text{Time}}$$
- **Inverse Proportionality:** When the total work is constant, Efficiency and Time are inversely proportional:
  $$E_1 \cdot T_1 = E_2 \cdot T_2 \implies \frac{E_1}{E_2} = \frac{T_2}{T_1}$$

---

## 2. The LCM Method (Unitary Efficiency Paradigm)
Instead of operating with cumbersome fractions ($1/a + 1/b$), the LCM method converts fractional rates into whole numbers.

### Step-by-Step LCM Protocol:
1. **Determine Total Work:** Find the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of the days taken by each individual worker.
   $$\text{Total Work (units)} = \text{LCM}(D_A, D_B, D_C)$$
2. **Compute Daily Efficiency:**
   $$E_A = \frac{\text{Total Work}}{D_A}, \quad E_B = \frac{\text{Total Work}}{D_B}, \quad E_C = \frac{\text{Total Work}}{D_C}$$
3. **Find Combined Efficiency:**
   $$E_{\text{combined}} = E_A + E_B + E_C$$
4. **Compute Combined Time:**
   $$T_{\text{combined}} = \frac{\text{Total Work}}{E_{\text{combined}}}$$

### Two-Worker Direct Formula:
If Worker A takes $x$ days and Worker B takes $y$ days:
$$T_{A+B} = \frac{x \cdot y}{x + y} \text{ days}$$

---

## 3. Dynamic Participation: Leaving and Joining
In competitive exams, workers often join or depart before completion.

### Case A: Leaving After Working for 'n' Days
- Work done together in $n$ days = $n \times (E_A + E_B)$.
- Remaining work = $\text{Total Work} - n \times (E_A + E_B)$.
- Time taken by the remaining worker to complete = $\frac{\text{Remaining Work}}{E_{\text{remaining}}}$.

### Case B: Leaving 'k' Days BEFORE Scheduled Completion (The Forward Addition Hack)
When worker A leaves $k$ days before completion, worker B works alone for the final $k$ days.
- **Standard Method:** Let total days be $T$. Then $(T - k)(E_A + E_B) + k(E_B) = W$.
- **Fast Exam Hack:** Pretend worker A did NOT leave! Force worker A to work those $k$ extra days.
  $$\text{Augmented Work} = \text{Total Work} + (k \times E_A)$$
  $$\text{Total Time} = \frac{\text{Total Work} + k \cdot E_A}{E_A + E_B}$$
This avoids algebra and yields the total duration in a single step.

---

## 4. Alternate Days Work Cycles
When workers operate on alternating days (e.g., A on Day 1, B on Day 2):
1. **Identify the Cycle Duration and Cycle Work:**
   $$\text{1 Cycle} = 2 \text{ days}, \quad W_{\text{cycle}} = E_A + E_B$$
2. **Determine Complete Cycles:**
   $$\text{Number of full cycles } q = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Total Work}}{W_{\text{cycle}}} \right\rfloor$$
   $$\text{Work completed} = q \times W_{\text{cycle}}, \quad \text{Days elapsed} = q \times (\text{days per cycle})$$
3. **Handle Residual Work:**
   $$\text{Residual Work} = \text{Total Work} - (q \times W_{\text{cycle}})$$
   Assign the remaining work sequentially to whoever's turn begins the next cycle.

---

## 5. Group Work and the Chain Rule (M-D-H-E Formula)
For heterogeneous workgroups (men, women, children, machines) under varying schedules:
$$\frac{M_1 \cdot D_1 \cdot H_1 \cdot E_1}{W_1} = \frac{M_2 \cdot D_2 \cdot H_2 \cdot E_2}{W_2}$$
Where:
- $M$: Number of workers
- $D$: Number of working days
- $H$: Daily working hours
- $E$: Efficiency factor of each worker
- $W$: Quantity of work accomplished or wages earned

### Equivalence Problems ('OR' vs 'AND'):
If $m$ men OR $w$ women OR $b$ boys can do a work in $D$ days:
$$m \cdot M = w \cdot W = b \cdot B$$
Establish individual efficiency ratios:
$$M : W : B = \frac{1}{m} : \frac{1}{w} : \frac{1}{b}$$
Then substitute into the mixed team ('AND' group).

---

## 6. Wages and Remuneration Distribution
- **Equal Working Duration:** When all workers are engaged for the exact same number of days, wages are distributed strictly in proportion to their **efficiencies**:
  $$\text{Wage}_A : \text{Wage}_B = E_A : E_B$$
- **Unequal Working Duration:** When workers work for different numbers of days, wages are distributed strictly in proportion to the **actual quantum of work performed**:
  $$\text{Wage}_A : \text{Wage}_B = (E_A \cdot T_A) : (E_B \cdot T_B)$$

---

## 7. Advanced Algebraic Work Relations
### The Classic Square Root Offset Shortcut:
If A and B together take $x$ days to complete a work, while A alone takes $(x + a)$ days and B alone takes $(x + b)$ days:
$$x = \sqrt{a \cdot b}$$
- Time for A alone = $\sqrt{ab} + a$
- Time for B alone = $\sqrt{ab} + b$
