# Flashcards: Time & Work

Front: What is the fundamental relation between Time and Efficiency when total work is constant?
Back: Efficiency is strictly inversely proportional to Time ($E_1 / E_2 = T_2 / T_1$).

---
Front: If A takes $x$ days and B takes $y$ days, what is the combined time formula?
Back: $T = (x \cdot y) / (x + y)$ days.

---
Front: What is the offset formula if A takes $(x + a)$ days, B takes $(x + b)$ days, and together they take $x$ days?
Back: $x = \sqrt{a \cdot b}$.

---
Front: State the Universal Chain Rule formula for heterogeneous groups.
Back: $(M_1 \cdot D_1 \cdot H_1 \cdot E_1) / W_1 = (M_2 \cdot D_2 \cdot H_2 \cdot E_2) / W_2$.

---
Front: How are wages divided when workers work for unequal number of days?
Back: In direct proportion to the actual quantum of work done ($E_A \cdot T_A : E_B \cdot T_B$).

---
Front: How do you solve problems where a worker leaves $k$ days BEFORE scheduled completion?
Back: Add $(k \times E_{\text{leaving}})$ to the Total Work, then divide by the combined efficiency of all workers.

---
Front: In alternate day work, what constitutes one complete operational cycle?
Back: Sum of days taken by each person in one round and the total units of work produced in that round.
