Card 1
Front: If A can do a work in 'x' days, what is A's one-day work?
Back: 1/x of the work. Efficiency and time are inversely related.

Card 2
Front: A does a work in 10 days, B in 15 days. Together, in how many days?
Back: Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6, so 6 days. Shortcut: (10×15)/(10+15) = 6.

Card 3
Front: What is the LCM method for Time & Work?
Back: Take total work = LCM of the days. Each person's efficiency = LCM ÷ their days. Then solve using efficiencies.

Card 4
Front: What is the mnemonic for the "work together" formula?
Back: "Product over Sum" — for two people: Time = (a × b)/(a + b). Remember: "Multiply on top, add below."

Card 5
Front: If A is twice as efficient as B, and together they finish in 12 days, how long does A alone take?
Back: Efficiency ratio 2:1 → A does 2/3 of work daily. Combined rate = 1/12, so A's rate = (2/3)(1/12) = 1/18 → 18 days.

Card 6
Front: Efficiency ratio of A:B is 3:2. What is the ratio of time taken by A:B for the same work?
Back: Inverse: 2:3. Time is inversely proportional to efficiency.

Card 7
Front: A can do a work in 20 days. He works for 5 days, then B finishes the rest in 15 days. How long would B alone take?
Back: A does 5/20 = 1/4 of work; remaining 3/4 done by B in 15 days → B alone takes 15 × (4/3) = 20 days.

Card 8
Front: Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours, pipe B empties it in 12 hours. Net fill time when both are open?
Back: Net rate = 1/6 − 1/12 = 1/12 → 12 hours. Outlet is treated as negative work.

Card 9
Front: What is the mnemonic for pipes with an outlet/leak?
Back: "Fill minus Spill" — always subtract the emptying rate. A leak turns your plus into a minus.

Card 10
Front: A and B together finish in 12 days; B and C in 15 days; A and C in 20 days. How long for all three together?
Back: Add the three rates: 2(A+B+C) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = 12/60 = 1/5 → A+B+C rate = 1/10 → 10 days.

Card 11
Front: If 'M1 men do W1 work in D1 days' and 'M2 men do W2 work in D2 days', what is the chain formula?
Back: M1 × D1 × H1 / W1 = M2 × D2 × H2 / W2 (include hours H if given). Work in the denominator.

Card 12
Front: 10 men finish a work in 8 days. How many days for 4 men (same efficiency)?
Back: Man-days constant: 10 × 8 = 80 man-days → 80/4 = 20 days. Fewer men, more days (inverse).

Card 13
Front: A works alone for some days, then leaves; B completes the rest. What is the standard approach?
Back: Compute work done by A (rate × days), subtract from 1, then divide remaining work by B's rate.

Card 14
Front: A is 50% more efficient than B. If B takes 30 days, how long does A take?
Back: A's efficiency = 1.5 × B's → A's time = 30/1.5 = 20 days.

Card 15
Front: What does "work done ∝ men × days × hours" assume?
Back: All workers have equal efficiency and work uniformly. If efficiencies differ, use individual rates instead.

Card 16
Front: Wages are shared between A and B who worked together. On what basis is the wage divided?
Back: In the ratio of work done = ratio of their efficiencies (if same time) — e.g., efficiencies 3:2 → wages split 3:2.

Card 17
Front: What is the mnemonic for the MDH/W chain rule?
Back: "Men-Days-Hours over Work stays equal" — remember "MaDHat over Work": M×D×H/W is constant across both situations.

Card 18
Front: A can do a work in 12 days, B in 18 days. They work alternately, A starting. When is the work finished?
Back: In 2 days they do 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36. After 14 days: 35/36 done. On day 15, A does the remaining 1/36 in (1/36)/(1/12) = 1/3 day → 14⅓ days.

Card 19
Front: If A completes 2/3 of a work in 8 days, how long for the whole work?
Back: Total time = 8 × (3/2) = 12 days. Scale up by the reciprocal of the fraction done.

Card 20
Front: Two pipes fill a tank in 4 h and 6 h; a leak empties it in 12 h. Time to fill with all three open?
Back: Net rate = 1/4 + 1/6 − 1/12 = 4/12 = 1/3 → 3 hours.
