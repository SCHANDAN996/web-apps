Questions 101–125: Hard

101. (Hard) A contractor undertook to complete a road in 100 days and employed 110 men. After 45 days, he found that only 1/4 of the work was completed. How many additional men must he employ to finish the work on time?
(A) 160 men  (B) 150 men  (C) 140 men  (D) 180 men
Answer: (A) 160 men
Solution: Remaining days = 100 - 45 = 55 days. Remaining work = 1 - 1/4 = 3/4. Using the formula (M1 * D1)/W1 = (M2 * D2)/W2: (110 * 45)/(1/4) = (M2 * 55)/(3/4) => 110 * 45 * 3 = M2 * 55 => M2 = (110 * 135)/55 = 270 men. Additional men required = 270 - 110 = 160 men. The required value is 160 men. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

102. (Hard) A contractor takes a contract to complete a project in 150 days and employs 80 men. After 90 days, only 1/2 of the project is finished. How many additional men must be employed so that the project is finished on time?
(A) 30 men  (B) 40 men  (C) 50 men  (D) 60 men
Answer: (B) 40 men
Solution: Remaining days = 150 - 90 = 60 days. Remaining work = 1/2. Equating: (80 * 90)/(1/2) = (M2 * 60)/(1/2) => 80 * 90 = M2 * 60 => M2 = 7200 / 60 = 120 men. Additional men = 120 - 80 = 40 men. The required value is 40 men. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

103. (Hard) 15 men can complete 1/3 of a work in 7 days working 8 hours a day. How many more men should be employed to complete the rest of the work in 14 days working 6 hours a day?
(A) 6 men  (B) 4 men  (C) 5 men  (D) 8 men
Answer: (C) 5 men
Solution: Using (M1 * D1 * H1)/W1 = (M2 * D2 * H2)/W2: (15 * 7 * 8)/(1/3) = (M2 * 14 * 6)/(2/3) => 15 * 7 * 8 * 2 = M2 * 14 * 6 => 1680 = 84 * M2 => M2 = 20 men. Additional men needed = 20 - 15 = 5 men. The required value is 5 men. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

104. (Hard) 4 men and 6 women can complete a work in 8 days, while 3 men and 7 women can complete it in 10 days. In how many days will 10 women complete the work?
(A) 35 days  (B) 45 days  (C) 50 days  (D) 40 days
Answer: (D) 40 days
Solution: Equating total work: 8 * (4M + 6W) = 10 * (3M + 7W) => 32M + 48W = 30M + 70W => 2M = 22W => 1M = 11W. Total work = 8 * (4*11W + 6W) = 8 * 50W = 400 woman-days. Time taken by 10 women = 400 / 10 = 40 days. The required value is 40 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

105. (Hard) 2 men, 3 women, and 4 boys can complete a work in 10 days. A man is twice as efficient as a woman, and a woman is twice as efficient as a boy. In how many days can 16 boys complete the same work?
(A) 11.25 days  (B) 10.50 days  (C) 12.00 days  (D) 12.50 days
Answer: (A) 11.25 days
Solution: Let efficiency of a boy = 1 unit/day. Then efficiency of a woman = 2 units/day and man = 4 units/day. Daily work of initial group = 2(4) + 3(2) + 4(1) = 8 + 6 + 4 = 18 units/day. Total work = 18 * 10 = 180 units. Daily efficiency of 16 boys = 16 * 1 = 16 units/day. Time taken = 180 / 16 = 11.25 days. The required value is 11.25 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

106. (Hard) A can do a piece of work in 20 days, and B in 30 days. They work together for 7 days, and then both leave. Then C alone finishes the remaining work in 10 days. If the total wages for the work is Rs. 1200, find C's share.
(A) Rs. 420  (B) Rs. 500  (C) Rs. 480  (D) Rs. 550
Answer: (B) Rs. 500
Solution: Total work = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units. Efficiency: A = 3, B = 2 units/day. In 7 days, A does 7 * 3 = 21 units, B does 7 * 2 = 14 units. Remaining work done by C = 60 - (21 + 14) = 25 units. Ratio of work done: A : B : C = 21 : 14 : 25. C's share = (25 / 60) * 1200 = Rs. 500. The required value is Rs. 500. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

107. (Hard) A garrison of 1200 men has provisions for 60 days. After 15 days, a reinforcement of 300 men arrives. For how many more days will the remaining provisions last?
(A) 35 days  (B) 32 days  (C) 36 days  (D) 40 days
Answer: (C) 36 days
Solution: Provisions left after 15 days can feed 1200 men for 60 - 15 = 45 days. New number of men = 1200 + 300 = 1500 men. Using M1 * D1 = M2 * D2: 1200 * 45 = 1500 * D2 => D2 = (1200 * 45) / 1500 = 36 days. The required value is 36 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

108. (Hard) A fort had provision of food for 150 men for 45 days. After 10 days, 25 men left the fort. The remaining food will last for how many days?
(A) 40 days  (B) 44 days  (C) 45 days  (D) 42 days
Answer: (D) 42 days
Solution: Food left after 10 days is sufficient for 150 men for 45 - 10 = 35 days. Remaining men = 150 - 25 = 125 men. Using M1 * D1 = M2 * D2: 150 * 35 = 125 * D => D = (150 * 35) / 125 = 42 days. The required value is 42 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

109. (Hard) If 6 engines consume 15 metric tonnes of coal when each is running 9 hours a day, how much coal (in metric tonnes) will be needed for 8 engines, each running 10 hours a day, given that 3 engines of the former type consume as much as 4 engines of the latter type?
(A) 16.67 tonnes  (B) 15.50 tonnes  (C) 18.00 tonnes  (D) 17.25 tonnes
Answer: (A) 16.67 tonnes
Solution: Consumption ratio: 3 E1 = 4 E2 => E2 = 3/4 E1. Using formula (N1 * H1 * E1) / W1 = (N2 * H2 * E2) / W2: (6 * 9 * 1) / 15 = (8 * 10 * 3/4) / W2 => 54 / 15 = 60 / W2 => W2 = (60 * 15) / 54 = 50 / 3 = 16.67 metric tonnes. The required value is 16.67 tonnes. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

110. (Hard) A can build a wall in 20 days, while B can demolish it in 30 days. If they work on alternate days starting with A, on which day will the wall be completed for the first time?
(A) 116 days  (B) 115 days  (C) 120 days  (D) 114 days
Answer: (B) 115 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units. Rate of A = +3 units/day, Rate of B = -2 units/day. In a 2-day cycle, net work = 3 - 2 = 1 unit. To avoid demolition after completion, target work = 60 - 3 = 57 units. Time for 57 units = 57 * 2 = 114 days. On day 115, A works and completes 57 + 3 = 60 units. Thus, the wall is completed in 115 days. The required value is 115 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

111. (Hard) 8 men can build a 100 m long wall in 12 days working 6 hours a day. How many days will 10 men take to build a 150 m long wall working 8 hours a day?
(A) 10.5 days  (B) 11.2 days  (C) 10.8 days  (D) 12.0 days
Answer: (C) 10.8 days
Solution: Using (M1 * D1 * H1) / W1 = (M2 * D2 * H2) / W2: (8 * 12 * 6) / 100 = (10 * D2 * 8) / 150 => 576 / 100 = 80 D2 / 150 => 5.76 = 8 D2 / 15 => D2 = (5.76 * 15) / 8 = 10.8 days. The required value is 10.8 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

112. (Hard) A works twice as fast as B, and B works twice as fast as C. If A and B together can complete a work in 4 days, in how many days can all three working together complete three times the same work?
(A) 9.75 days  (B) 10.50 days  (C) 11.20 days  (D) 10.29 days
Answer: (D) 10.29 days
Solution: Efficiency ratio: C = 1, B = 2, A = 4 units/day. Combined rate of (A + B) = 4 + 2 = 6 units/day. Total work = 6 * 4 = 24 units. Triple the work = 3 * 24 = 72 units. Combined rate of (A + B + C) = 4 + 2 + 1 = 7 units/day. Time taken = 72 / 7 = 10.29 days. The required value is 10.29 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

113. (Hard) A contractor employed 40 men to do a piece of work in 50 days. After 30 days, only 50% of the work was done. How many extra men must he employ to finish the work 5 days before the scheduled time?
(A) 40 men  (B) 30 men  (C) 50 men  (D) 35 men
Answer: (A) 40 men
Solution: Target completion time = 50 - 5 = 45 days. Time remaining = 45 - 30 = 15 days. Remaining work = 50% = 0.50. Equating: (40 * 30) / 0.50 = (M2 * 15) / 0.50 => 40 * 30 = M2 * 15 => M2 = 80 men. Additional men required = 80 - 40 = 40 men. The required value is 40 men. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

114. (Hard) A and B together can complete a work in 12 days. B and C can do it in 15 days. A is twice as efficient as C. In how many days can B alone complete the work?
(A) 24 days  (B) 20 days  (C) 18 days  (D) 25 days
Answer: (B) 20 days
Solution: Rates: A + B = 1/12, B + C = 1/15. Subtracting equations: A - C = 1/12 - 1/15 = 1/60. Since A = 2C, we have 2C - C = 1/60 => C = 1/60. Rate of B = (B + C) - C = 1/15 - 1/60 = (4 - 1)/60 = 3/60 = 1/20 per day. Time taken by B alone = 20 days. The required value is 20 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

115. (Hard) A and B can do a job in 15 days and 10 days respectively. They began the work together but after 2 days B left and A had to continue alone. After 1 day, C joined A and the work was completed in 5 more days. In how many days can C alone complete the entire job?
(A) 18.50 days  (B) 19.00 days  (C) 18.75 days  (D) 17.50 days
Answer: (C) 18.75 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(15, 10) = 30 units. Daily efficiency: A = 2, B = 3 units/day. In first 2 days, (A + B) do: 2 * (2 + 3) = 10 units. On day 3, A alone does: 1 * 2 = 2 units. Work completed so far = 10 + 2 = 12 units. Remaining work = 30 - 12 = 18 units. (A + C) finish 18 units in 5 days => 5 * (2 + C) = 18 => 10 + 5C = 18 => 5C = 8 => C = 1.6 units/day. Time taken by C alone = 30 / 1.6 = 18.75 days. The required value is 18.75 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

116. (Hard) 6 men and 8 women can reap a field in 10 days. 13 men and 24 women can reap it in 4 days. What will 1 man and 1 woman earn daily if the total contract is Rs. 5600 for the field, completed by 6 men and 8 women in 10 days?
(A) Rs. 80  (B) Rs. 90  (C) Rs. 78  (D) Rs. 84
Answer: (D) Rs. 84
Solution: Equating work: 10 * (6M + 8W) = 4 * (13M + 24W) => 60M + 80W = 52M + 96W => 8M = 16W => 1M = 2W. Workforce 6M + 8W = 6(2W) + 8W = 20 women. Daily wages of team = 5600 / 10 = Rs. 560/day. Daily wage of 1 woman = 560 / 20 = Rs. 28/day. Daily wage of 1 man = 2 * 28 = Rs. 56/day. Together, 1 man and 1 woman earn = 56 + 28 = Rs. 84/day. The required value is Rs. 84. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

117. (Hard) 18 women can complete a work in 12 days and 12 men can complete the same work in 9 days. In how many days will 8 men and 8 women complete the work?
(A) 9 days  (B) 8 days  (C) 10 days  (D) 12 days
Answer: (A) 9 days
Solution: Total work = 18 * 12 = 216 woman-days = 12 * 9 = 108 man-days => 216 W = 108 M => 1 M = 2 W. Target group: 8 M + 8 W = 8(2 W) + 8 W = 24 women. Time taken = 216 / 24 = 9 days. The required value is 9 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).

118. (Hard) A can do a piece of work in 36 days, B in 54 days, and C in 72 days. All three began the work together, but A left 8 days before the completion of the work and B left 12 days before the completion. For how many days did C work?
(A) 22 days  (B) 24 days  (C) 26 days  (D) 28 days
Answer: (B) 24 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(36, 54, 72) = 216 units. Daily efficiency: A = 6, B = 4, C = 3 units/day. Let total days be T (C works for all T days). Work done: 6(T - 8) + 4(T - 12) + 3T = 216 => 6T - 48 + 4T - 48 + 3T = 216 => 13T - 96 = 216 => 13T = 312 => T = 24 days. The required value is 24 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

119. (Hard) A and B can do a work in 12 days. B and C can do it in 16 days. If A works on it for 5 days, B for 7 days, and C completes the remaining work in 13 days, in how many days can B alone complete the work?
(A) 40 days  (B) 36 days  (C) 48 days  (D) 54 days
Answer: (C) 48 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(12, 16) = 48 units. (A + B) = 4, (B + C) = 3 units/day. Work = 5(A + B) + 2(B + C) + 11C = 48 => 5(4) + 2(3) + 11C = 48 => 26 + 11C = 48 => C = 2 units/day. Since (B + C) = 3 units/day, B's efficiency = 3 - 2 = 1 unit/day. Time taken by B alone = 48 / 1 = 48 days. The required value is 48 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

120. (Hard) A and B can do a piece of work in 40 days and 50 days respectively. They begin together, but A leaves after some time and B completes the remaining work in 23 days. For how many days did they work together?

Combined efficiency = 5 + 4 = 9 units/day. Number of days worked together = 108 / 9 = 12 days. The required value is 12 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).

122. (Hard) A, B, and C can do a work in 24, 30, and 40 days respectively. They began the work together, but C left 4 days before the completion of the work. In how many days was the work completed?

123. (Hard) A contractor employed 25 men to do a job in 24 days. After 16 days, he engaged 10 more men and the work was finished 2 days before the scheduled time. If he had not engaged any additional men, how many days behind schedule would the work have been finished?
(A) 0.5 days  (B) 1.0 day  (C) 0.4 days  (D) 0.8 days
Answer: (C) 0.4 days
Solution: Work in first 16 days = 25 * 16 = 400 man-days. Finished 2 days early => total 22 days => second period was 22 - 16 = 6 days with 35 men: 35 * 6 = 210 man-days. Total work = 400 + 210 = 610 man-days. If only 25 men worked: Time = 610 / 25 = 24.4 days. Days behind schedule = 24.4 - 24 = 0.4 days. The required value is 0.4 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

124. (Hard) A can do a piece of work in 10 days, B in 15 days, and C in 20 days. A and C worked together for two days and then A was replaced by B. In how many days, altogether, was the work finished?
(A) 7 days  (B) 9 days  (C) 10 days  (D) 8 days
Answer: (D) 8 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(10, 15, 20) = 60 units. Efficiency: A = 6, B = 4, C = 3 units/day. In first 2 days, (A + C) do: 2 * (6 + 3) = 18 units. Remaining work = 60 - 18 = 42 units. Then B and C work together: rate = 4 + 3 = 7 units/day. Time taken = 42 / 7 = 6 days. Total time = 2 + 6 = 8 days. The required value is 8 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

125. (Hard) 2 men and 3 boys can do a piece of work in 10 days while 3 men and 2 boys can do the same work in 8 days. In how many days can 2 men and 1 boy do the work?
(A) 12.0 days  (B) 12.5 days  (C) 11.5 days  (D) 13.0 days
Answer: (B) 12.5 days
Solution: Equating work: 10 * (2M + 3B) = 8 * (3M + 2B) => 20M + 30B = 24M + 16B => 4M = 14B => 1M = 3.5B. Total work = 10 * (2*3.5 + 3) = 10 * 10 = 100 boy-days. Target workforce = 2M + 1B = 2(3.5B) + 1B = 8 boys. Time taken = 100 / 8 = 12.5 days. The required value is 12.5 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
