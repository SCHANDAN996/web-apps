# Previous Year Exam Patterns: Time & Work

## Pattern 1: Combined Time with Three Workers
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern
- **Problem:** Worker P takes 12 days, Worker Q takes 15 days, and Worker R takes 20 days to complete a task. How many days will they take working together?
- **Analysis:** LCM of (12, 15, 20) = 60 units.
  Daily efficiencies: $E_P = 60/12 = 5$, $E_Q = 60/15 = 4$, $E_R = 60/20 = 3$.
  Combined efficiency = $5 + 4 + 3 = 12$ units/day.
  Combined time = $60 / 12 = 5$ days.

---

## Pattern 2: Worker Leaving After Fixed Days
- **Exam Pattern:** IBPS PO Mains Pattern
- **Problem:** Worker A can do a piece of work in 24 days and Worker B in 32 days. They work together for 6 days, after which Worker A leaves. How many days will Worker B take to finish the remaining work?
- **Analysis:** LCM(24, 32) = 96 units. $E_A = 4$, $E_B = 3$.
  Work in 6 days = $6 \times (4 + 3) = 42$ units.
  Remaining work = $96 - 42 = 54$ units.
  Time for B = $54 / 3 = 18$ days.

---

## Pattern 3: Worker Leaving Before Completion
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern
- **Problem:** A can do a work in 20 days and B in 30 days. They start together, but A leaves 5 days before the work is completed. Find the total time to finish the work.
- **Analysis:** LCM(20, 30) = 60 units. $E_A = 3$, $E_B = 2$.
  Virtual work added for A = $5 \times 3 = 15$ units.
  Total augmented work = $60 + 15 = 75$ units.
  Total time = $75 / (3 + 2) = 15$ days.

---

## Pattern 4: Alternate Day Work
- **Exam Pattern:** CDS Pattern
- **Problem:** P and Q can finish a work in 12 days and 18 days respectively. If they work on alternate days starting with P, in how many days is the work completed?
- **Analysis:** LCM(12, 18) = 36 units. $E_P = 3$, $E_Q = 2$.
  1 cycle (2 days) = $3 + 2 = 5$ units.
  7 full cycles = 14 days, doing $7 \times 5 = 35$ units.
  Remaining work = $36 - 35 = 1$ unit.
  Turn on day 15 is P's: time taken = $1/3$ day.
  Total time = $14 + 1/3 = 14\frac{1}{3}$ days.

---

## Pattern 5: Offset Square Root Relation
- **Exam Pattern:** State PCS Pattern
- **Problem:** A takes 9 days more than A and B together, while B takes 16 days more than A and B together. How many days do they take together?
- **Analysis:** $T_{A+B} = \sqrt{9 \times 16} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ days.

---

## Pattern 6: Chain Rule with Variable Working Hours
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern
- **Problem:** 25 workers working 8 hours a day can complete a project in 18 days. How many days will 30 workers take working 6 hours a day to complete double the work?
- **Analysis:** $(M_1 \cdot D_1 \cdot H_1) / W_1 = (M_2 \cdot D_2 \cdot H_2) / W_2$.
  $(25 \times 18 \times 8) / 1 = (30 \times D_2 \times 6) / 2$.
  $3600 = 90 \cdot D_2 \implies D_2 = 40$ days.

---

## Pattern 7: Men, Women Equivalence
- **Exam Pattern:** IBPS PO Mains Pattern
- **Problem:** 4 men or 6 women can build a wall in 22 days. In how many days can 5 men and 3 women build the same wall?
- **Analysis:** $4M = 6W \implies 1M = 1.5W$.
  $5M + 3W = 5(1.5W) + 3W = 10.5W$.
  Chain rule: $6 \times 22 = 10.5 \times D_2 \implies D_2 = 132 / 10.5 = 264 / 21 = 12\frac{4}{7}$ days.

---

## Pattern 8: Wage Sharing with Differing Durations
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern
- **Problem:** A can complete a task in 15 days and B in 20 days. They work together for 4 days, then B leaves and A finishes the remaining work in 8 more days. If total wage is Rs 4,500, find B's share.
- **Analysis:** LCM(15, 20) = 60 units. $E_A = 4$, $E_B = 3$.
  A worked for $4 + 8 = 12$ days $\implies 12 \times 4 = 48$ units.
  B worked for 4 days $\implies 4 \times 3 = 12$ units.
  Ratio of work done = $48 : 12 = 4 : 1$.
  B's share = $4500 \times (1 / 5) = \text{Rs } 900$.

---

## Pattern 9: Negative Efficiency (Destruction Factor)
- **Exam Pattern:** CDS Pattern
- **Problem:** Builder A can construct a wall in 12 days and Builder B in 15 days. A demolisher C can demolish it in 20 days. If all three work together, in how many days is the wall completed?
- **Analysis:** LCM(12, 15, 20) = 60 units. $E_A = +5$, $E_B = +4$, $E_C = -3$.
  Net efficiency = $5 + 4 - 3 = 6$ units/day.
  Time taken = $60 / 6 = 10$ days.

---

## Pattern 10: Efficiency Scaling with Variable Days
- **Exam Pattern:** SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern
- **Problem:** Worker A is 40% more efficient than Worker B. If B takes 35 days to complete a work alone, how many days will both take working together?
- **Analysis:** $E_A : E_B = 140 : 100 = 7 : 5$.
  Total work = $E_B \times 35 = 5 \times 35 = 175$ units.
  Combined efficiency = $7 + 5 = 12$ units/day.
  Time together = $175 / 12 = 14\frac{7}{12}$ days.
