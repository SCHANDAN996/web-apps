| Formula Name | Formula | One-line Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Work = Rate × Time | W = R × T | Basic relation between work, rate, and time |
| One day's work (efficiency) | 1/n | If a person finishes a job in n days, work done in 1 day = 1/n |
| Combined work rate | 1/A + 1/B | When two people work together, add their one-day works |
| Time taken together | (A × B)/(A + B) | Direct shortcut for two workers finishing a job together |
| Three workers together | 1/A + 1/B + 1/C | Add one-day works of all three; time = reciprocal of the sum |
| Work done in d days | d × (1/n) | Multiply one day's work by the number of days |
| Remaining work | 1 − (work done) | Subtract completed fraction from the whole work (1) |
| Efficiency ratio | E₁ : E₂ = (1/n₁) : (1/n₂) | Efficiency is inversely proportional to time taken |
| Men–Days–Work relation | (M₁ × D₁ × H₁)/W₁ = (M₂ × D₂ × H₂)/W₂ | Compare two situations with men, days, hours, and work |
| Wages share | Wages ∝ work done | Divide payment in the ratio of each person's work contribution |
| Pipe filling rate | 1/x | A pipe filling a tank in x hours fills 1/x of it in 1 hour |
| Pipe emptying rate | −1/y | Treat an outlet as negative work; subtract its rate |
| Net rate (fill + empty) | 1/x − 1/y | Time to fill = reciprocal of the net rate (if positive) |
