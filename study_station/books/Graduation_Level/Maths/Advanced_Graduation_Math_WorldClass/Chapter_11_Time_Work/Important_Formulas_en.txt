# Important Formulas: Time & Work

## 1. Fundamental Relations
- $\text{Work} = \text{Efficiency} \times \text{Time}$
- $\text{Efficiency} = \frac{\text{Work}}{\text{Time}}$
- $\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Work}}{\text{Efficiency}}$
- $E_1 : E_2 = T_2 : T_1$ (when total work is constant)

## 2. Combined Work Formulas
- Two workers A and B:
  $$T_{A+B} = \frac{x \cdot y}{x + y}$$
- Three workers A, B, and C:
  $$T_{A+B+C} = \frac{x \cdot y \cdot z}{xy + yz + zx}$$

## 3. Offset Square Root Formula
If A takes $(x + a)$ days and B takes $(x + b)$ days, while together they take $x$ days:
$$x = \sqrt{a \cdot b}$$

## 4. Chain Rule (Compound Proportion)
$$\frac{M_1 \cdot D_1 \cdot H_1 \cdot E_1}{W_1} = \frac{M_2 \cdot D_2 \cdot H_2 \cdot E_2}{W_2}$$

## 5. Work Leaving Before Completion Shortcut
If worker A leaves $k$ days before scheduled completion:
$$T_{\text{total}} = \frac{W_{\text{original}} + k \cdot E_A}{E_A + E_B}$$

## 6. Remuneration Rules
- Constant working days: $\text{Wage}_A : \text{Wage}_B = E_A : E_B$
- Variable working days: $\text{Wage}_A : \text{Wage}_B = (E_A \cdot T_A) : (E_B \cdot T_B)$
