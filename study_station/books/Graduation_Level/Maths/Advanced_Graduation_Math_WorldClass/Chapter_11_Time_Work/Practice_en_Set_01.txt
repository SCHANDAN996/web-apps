Questions 1–25: Easy

1. (Easy) A can do a piece of work in 10 days and B in 15 days. In how many days can they complete it working together?
(A) 6 days  (B) 5 days  (C) 7 days  (D) 8 days
Answer: (A) 6 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(10, 15) = 30 units. Daily efficiency of A = 30 / 10 = 3 units/day. Daily efficiency of B = 30 / 15 = 2 units/day. Combined efficiency = 3 + 2 = 5 units/day. Time required = 30 / 5 = 6 days. The required value is 6 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. (Easy) A and B together can do a piece of work in 12 days. If A alone can do it in 20 days, in how many days can B alone complete the work?
(A) 25 days  (B) 30 days  (C) 24 days  (D) 35 days
Answer: (B) 30 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(12, 20) = 60 units. Combined efficiency of A and B = 60 / 12 = 5 units/day. Efficiency of A = 60 / 20 = 3 units/day. Efficiency of B = 5 - 3 = 2 units/day. Time for B alone = 60 / 2 = 30 days. The required value is 30 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

3. (Easy) A can do a piece of work in 8 days, B in 12 days, and C in 24 days. In how many days can all three together complete the work?
(A) 5 days  (B) 6 days  (C) 4 days  (D) 3 days
Answer: (C) 4 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(8, 12, 24) = 24 units. Efficiencies: A = 3, B = 2, C = 1 unit/day. Combined efficiency = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 units/day. Time taken together = 24 / 6 = 4 days. The required value is 4 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

4. (Easy) A can finish a work in 18 days and B can do the same work in half the time taken by A. Working together, what fraction of the work can they finish in a day?
(A) 1/9  (B) 2/9  (C) 1/5  (D) 1/6
Answer: (D) 1/6
Solution: Time taken by B = 18 / 2 = 9 days. In 1 day, A completes 1/18 of the work and B completes 1/9 of the work. Together in 1 day, they complete 1/18 + 1/9 = 1/18 + 2/18 = 3/18 = 1/6 of the work. The required value is 1/6. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

5. (Easy) If 12 men can complete a piece of work in 20 days, in how many days can 15 men complete the same work?
(A) 16 days  (B) 15 days  (C) 18 days  (D) 14 days
Answer: (A) 16 days
Solution: Using M1 * D1 = M2 * D2: 12 * 20 = 15 * D2 => 240 = 15 * D2 => D2 = 240 / 15 = 16 days. The required value is 16 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

6. (Easy) A is twice as good a workman as B, and together they finish a piece of work in 14 days. In how many days can A alone finish the work?
(A) 28 days  (B) 21 days  (C) 35 days  (D) 18 days
Answer: (B) 21 days
Solution: Ratio of efficiencies E_A : E_B = 2 : 1. Combined daily efficiency = 2 + 1 = 3 units/day. Total work = 3 * 14 = 42 units. Time for A alone = 42 / 2 = 21 days. The required value is 21 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

7. (Easy) A and B can do a piece of work in 15 days and 10 days respectively. They began the work together, but B leaves after 2 days. In how many days will the remaining work be finished by A alone?
(A) 12 days  (B) 8 days  (C) 10 days  (D) 11 days
Answer: (C) 10 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(15, 10) = 30 units. E_A = 2 units/day, E_B = 3 units/day. Work done together in 2 days = (2 + 3) * 2 = 10 units. Remaining work = 30 - 10 = 20 units. Time taken by A to complete remaining work = 20 / 2 = 10 days. The required value is 10 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

8. (Easy) A can do a work in 25 days and B in 20 days. Both work together for 5 days and then A leaves. How many days will B take to complete the remaining work?
(A) 10 days  (B) 12 days  (C) 9 days  (D) 11 days
Answer: (D) 11 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(25, 20) = 100 units. E_A = 4 units/day, E_B = 5 units/day. Work done in 5 days = (4 + 5) * 5 = 45 units. Remaining work = 100 - 45 = 55 units. Time for B alone = 55 / 5 = 11 days. The required value is 11 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

9. (Easy) A, B, and C can complete a piece of work in 24, 6, and 12 days respectively. Working together, in how many days will they complete the work?
(A) 3.43 days  (B) 3.00 days  (C) 4.00 days  (D) 3.75 days
Answer: (A) 3.43 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(24, 6, 12) = 24 units. Efficiencies: E_A = 1, E_B = 4, E_C = 2 units/day. Combined efficiency = 1 + 4 + 2 = 7 units/day. Time required = 24 / 7 = 3.43 days (or 3 3/7 days). The required value is 3.43 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

10. (Easy) A can do a piece of work in 4 hours, B and C together in 3 hours, and A and C together in 2 hours. How long will B alone take to do it?
(A) 10 hours  (B) 12 hours  (C) 8 hours  (D) 15 hours
Answer: (B) 12 hours
Solution: Total work = LCM(4, 3, 2) = 12 units. E_A = 12 / 4 = 3 units/hr. E_B + E_C = 12 / 3 = 4 units/hr. E_A + E_C = 12 / 2 = 6 units/hr => E_C = 6 - 3 = 3 units/hr. Therefore, E_B = 4 - 3 = 1 unit/hr. Time for B alone = 12 / 1 = 12 hours. The required value is 12 hours. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

11. (Easy) 36 men can complete a piece of work in 18 days. In how many days will 27 men complete the same work?
(A) 22 days  (B) 26 days  (C) 24 days  (D) 28 days
Answer: (C) 24 days
Solution: M1 * D1 = M2 * D2 => 36 * 18 = 27 * D2 => D2 = (36 * 18) / 27 = 4 * 6 = 24 days. The required value is 24 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

12. (Easy) A can do a piece of work in 15 days and B in 20 days. If they work on it together for 4 days, what fraction of the work is left?
(A) 7/15  (B) 1/2  (C) 11/15  (D) 8/15
Answer: (D) 8/15
Solution: Total work = LCM(15, 20) = 60 units. E_A = 4, E_B = 3 units/day. In 4 days, they complete (4 + 3) * 4 = 28 units. Remaining work = 60 - 28 = 32 units. Fraction left = 32 / 60 = 8 / 15. The required value is 8/15. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (A) 24 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(8, 12) = 24 units. Combined efficiency of A and B = 3 units/day. Efficiency of A = 2 units/day. Efficiency of B = 3 - 2 = 1 unit/day. Time for B = 24 / 1 = 24 days. The required value is 24 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

14. (Easy) If A can do 1/3 of a work in 4 days and B can do 2/5 of the work in 8 days, in how many days can both A and B together do the work?
(A) 8.0 days  (B) 7.5 days  (C) 7.0 days  (D) 6.5 days
Answer: (B) 7.5 days
Solution: Time taken by A alone = 4 * 3 = 12 days. Time taken by B alone = 8 * (5/2) = 20 days. Combined time = (12 * 20) / (12 + 20) = 240 / 32 = 7.5 days. The required value is 7.5 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

15. (Easy) A, B, and C together can complete a piece of work in 6 days. A alone can do it in 12 days and B alone in 18 days. In how many days can C alone complete the work?
(A) 30 days  (B) 32 days  (C) 36 days  (D) 40 days
Answer: (C) 36 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(6, 12, 18) = 36 units. E_(A+B+C) = 6 units/day. E_A = 3, E_B = 2 units/day. Efficiency of C = 6 - (3 + 2) = 1 unit/day. Time for C alone = 36 / 1 = 36 days. The required value is 36 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

16. (Easy) If 20 men can complete a certain work in 10 days, how many men will be required to complete the same work in 5 days?
(A) 35 men  (B) 50 men  (C) 30 men  (D) 40 men
Answer: (D) 40 men
Solution: Using M1 * D1 = M2 * D2: 20 * 10 = M2 * 5 => M2 = 200 / 5 = 40 men. The required value is 40 men. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

17. (Easy) A is 50% more efficient than B. If B can complete a work in 30 days, in how many days can A alone complete the same work?
(A) 20 days  (B) 25 days  (C) 15 days  (D) 18 days
Answer: (A) 20 days
Solution: Ratio of efficiencies E_A : E_B = 150 : 100 = 3 : 2. Total work = Days of B * E_B = 30 * 2 = 60 units. Time for A alone = Total work / E_A = 60 / 3 = 20 days. The required value is 20 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

18. (Easy) A can do a piece of work in 20 days and B in 30 days. They work together and receive Rs 1,500 for the whole work. What is A's share of the wages?
(A) Rs 600  (B) Rs 900  (C) Rs 800  (D) Rs 1,000
Answer: (B) Rs 900
Solution: Ratio of efficiencies E_A : E_B = 30 : 20 = 3 : 2. Since both worked for the same duration, wages are divided in the ratio 3 : 2. Total ratio units = 3 + 2 = 5 units = Rs 1,500 => 1 unit = Rs 300. A's share = 3 * 300 = Rs 900. The required value is Rs 900. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (C) 4 days

20. (Easy) A and B can do a job together in 7 days. A is 1.75 times as efficient as B. The same job can be done by A alone in how many days?
(A) 14 days  (B) 12 days  (C) 10 days  (D) 11 days
Answer: (D) 11 days
Solution: Efficiency ratio E_A : E_B = 1.75 : 1 = 7/4 : 1 = 7 : 4. Combined daily efficiency = 7 + 4 = 11 units/day. Total work = 11 * 7 = 77 units. Time taken by A alone = 77 / 7 = 11 days. The required value is 11 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

21. (Medium) A can do a work in 14 days and B in 21 days. They begin together but 3 days before the completion of the work, A leaves. In how many days is the work completed?
(A) 10.2 days  (B) 10.0 days  (C) 11.0 days  (D) 9.8 days
Answer: (A) 10.2 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(14, 21) = 42 units. E_A = 3, E_B = 2 units/day. Since A leaves 3 days before completion, add A's potential work: Adjusted Total Work = 42 + (3 * 3) = 51 units. Total time taken = 51 / (3 + 2) = 51 / 5 = 10.2 days. The required value is 10.2 days. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

22. (Medium) A can do a piece of work in 10 days, B in 15 days, and C in 20 days. A and C work together for 2 days, and then A is replaced by B. In how many more days will the work be completed?
(A) 5 days  (B) 6 days  (C) 7 days  (D) 8 days
Answer: (B) 6 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(10, 15, 20) = 60 units. E_A = 6, E_B = 4, E_C = 3 units/day. Work done by (A + C) in 2 days = (6 + 3) * 2 = 18 units. Remaining work = 60 - 18 = 42 units. Combined efficiency of (B + C) = 4 + 3 = 7 units/day. Additional time required = 42 / 7 = 6 days. The required value is 6 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

23. (Medium) 10 women can complete a work in 7 days and 10 children take 14 days to complete the same work. How many days will 5 women and 10 children take to complete the work?
(A) 8 days  (B) 6 days  (C) 7 days  (D) 9 days
Answer: (C) 7 days
Solution: Total work = 10W * 7 = 70 W-days, or 10C * 14 = 140 C-days. Equating gives 70W = 140C => 1 Woman = 2 Children. Target group: 5 Women + 10 Children = 5(2C) + 10C = 20 Children. Time taken = 140 C-days / 20 Children = 7 days. The required value is 7 days. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

24. (Medium) A and B can do a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, C and A in 20 days. In how many days can A, B, and C together finish the work?
(A) 12 days  (B) 8 days  (C) 15 days  (D) 10 days
Answer: (D) 10 days
Solution: Total work = LCM(12, 15, 20) = 60 units. E_(A+B) = 5, E_(B+C) = 4, E_(C+A) = 3 units/day. Adding gives 2 * (E_A + E_B + E_C) = 5 + 4 + 3 = 12 => E_(A+B+C) = 6 units/day. Time required together = 60 / 6 = 10 days. The required value is 10 days. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

25. (Medium) A and B can do a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, C and A in 20 days. In how many days can A alone finish the work?
(A) 25 days  (B) 30 days  (C) 20 days  (D) 35 days
Answer: (B) 30 days
Solution: From the previous question, Total work = 60 units and E_(A+B+C) = 6 units/day. Since E_(B+C) = 4 units/day, E_A = 6 - 4 = 2 units/day. Time taken by A alone = 60 / 2 = 30 days. The required value is 30 days. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
