# 🪄 Short Tricks — Time & Work (Memory Hacks)

---

**🧠 TRICK 1 — "LCM is the Boss Cake"**
> Never work with fractions of a day. Take **LCM of all days = Total Work units** (the "cake"). Everyone's efficiency = slices per day.
> A = 10 days, B = 15 days → Cake = 30 units → A eats 3/day, B eats 2/day. Together 5/day → 30/5 = **6 days**.
> *Mantra: "Convert days to slices, and the problem melts."*

---

**🧠 TRICK 2 — "The Reciprocal Romance"**
> Two people together: **1/T = 1/A + 1/B**. Shortcut for two: **T = (A × B)/(A + B)** — "Product over Sum."
> *Memory hook: "Lovers multiply on top, add below."*
> 12 & 24 days → (12×24)/(12+24) = 288/36 = **8 days**.

---

**🧠 TRICK 3 — "MDH — Man-Days-Hours"**
> **M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂** — the one formula that rules ALL work problems.
> *Mnemonic: "My Dear Horse Works" — M, D, H over W.*
> More men → fewer days (inverse); more work → more days (direct). Men and Work sit on **opposite sides** of the see-saw.

---

**🧠 TRICK 4 — "Efficiency is Inverse Royalty"**
> If A is twice as efficient as B, A takes **half** the time. Efficiency ratio 2:1 → Time ratio 1:2.
> *Hook: "The faster king finishes the race in reverse order."*
> Always **flip the ratio** when converting efficiency ↔ time.

---

**🧠 TRICK 5 — "The Quitter's Leftover"**
> A works alone for some days, then leaves? Do: **Work done by A + Work done by both = 1 (the whole cake)**.
> *Story: "The quitter eats his slices first; the remaining cake is shared."*
> Set up: (A's rate × solo days) + (combined rate × joint days) = Total units.

---

**🧠 TRICK 6 — "Negative Nancy (Pipes & Leaks)"**
> Filling pipe = **+**, Emptying pipe/leak = **−**. Net rate = Fill − Leak.
> *Mnemonic: "Nancy the Leak steals slices from the cake."*
> Pipe fills in 6 hrs (+10/hr of a 60-unit tank), leak empties in 10 hrs (−6/hr) → Net 4/hr → **15 hrs**.

---

**🧠 TRICK 7 — "Alternate Days — Pair Them Up"**
> A and B work on alternate days? Combine **2 days = 1 pair**, find work per pair, count pairs, handle the leftover day carefully.
> *Hook: "Dance partners — count the duets, then check who dances the last solo."*
> ⚠️ Trap: the answer often ends mid-pair — check who works on the final day!

---

**🧠 TRICK 8 — "Wages Follow Sweat"**
> Wages are divided in the ratio of **work done = efficiency × days worked**, NOT equally.
> *Mnemonic: "No sweat, no sweet."*
> A:B efficiencies 3:2, both work full time → wages split **3:2**, never 1:1.

---

**🧠 TRICK 9 — "The Chain Rule Snap"**
> "a men or b women or c children do work in D days" → treat as: **aM = bW = cC** (equal efficiencies per day).
> Convert everyone into ONE type (say, men) and solve.
> *Hook: "Turn the whole zoo into one animal."*

---

**🧠 TRICK 10 — "X% More Efficient = Fraction Flip"**
> "A is 50% more efficient than B" → A:B = 3:2 efficiency → time ratio 2:3.
> "A is 25% less efficient" → A:B = 3:4.
> *Trick: Convert % to fraction FIRST (50% = 1/2), then build the ratio. Never guess.*

---

**🧠 TRICK 11 — "The 1-Day Detective"**
> Stuck? Always ask: **"What happens in ONE day?"** Write each person's 1-day work, then add/subtract.
> *Mantra: "When lost, go daily."*
> Most Time & Work problems become much simpler once you list each person's per-day work.

---

**🧠 TRICK 12 — "Double Trouble Halving"**
> Double the workers → half the time. Triple the workers → one-third the time.
> *Hook: "Hands multiply, days divide."*
> Quick check for options: if men doubled, any answer ≥ original time is instantly wrong.

---

**🧠 TRICK 13 — "The Leak Discovery Trick"**
> Tank filled in T₁ hrs alone, but T₂ hrs with a leak (T₂ > T₁)?
> Leak alone empties in **(T₁ × T₂)/(T₂ − T₁)** hrs.
> *Mnemonic: "Product over Difference — the leak's confession."*
> 6 hrs alone, 10 hrs with leak → (6×10)/(10−6) = **15 hrs** to empty.

---

**🧠 TRICK 14 — "Work Left = Days Left Mirror"**
> "A leaves x days before completion" → B alone does the **last x days of work**. Compute B's x-day work, subtract from total, the rest was done together.
> *Hook: "The finisher owns the tail of the cake."*

---

**🧠 TRICK 15 — "The Grand Master Key: W = E × T"**
> **Work = Efficiency × Time.** Every formula in this chapter is this one line in disguise.
> *Final mantra: "Cake = Appetite × Meals."*
> If you remember nothing else in the exam hall, remember this — and rebuild everything from it.
