Questions 26–50: Medium

26. (Medium) Two trains are running in opposite directions with the same speed. If the length of each train is 120 meters and they cross each other in 12 seconds, what is the speed of each train in km/h?
(A) 36 km/h  (B) 30 km/h  (C) 40 km/h  (D) 45 km/h
Answer: (A) 36 km/h
Solution: Total distance to cross = 120 + 120 = 240 meters. Relative speed in opposite direction = 240 / 12 = 20 m/s. Since speeds are equal, 2s = 20 => s = 10 m/s. Converting to km/h: 10 * (18/5) = 36 km/h. The required value is 36 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

27. (Medium) A train 125 meters long passes a man, running at 5 km/h in the same direction in which the train is going, in 10 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 45 km/h  (B) 50 km/h  (C) 55 km/h  (D) 48 km/h
Answer: (B) 50 km/h
Solution: Relative speed in same direction = 125 / 10 = 12.5 m/s = 12.5 * (18/5) = 45 km/h. Speed of train = Relative speed + Speed of man = 45 + 5 = 50 km/h. The required value is 50 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

28. (Medium) A train passes two bridges of lengths 800 meters and 400 meters in 100 seconds and 60 seconds respectively. What is the length of the train?
(A) 180 meters  (B) 240 meters  (C) 200 meters  (D) 250 meters
Answer: (C) 200 meters
Solution: Difference in length = 800 - 400 = 400 meters. Difference in time = 100 - 60 = 40 seconds. Speed of train = 400 / 40 = 10 m/s. Distance in 60 seconds = 10 * 60 = 600 meters. Length of train = 600 - 400 = 200 meters. The required value is 200 meters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

29. (Medium) A person travels 120 km by train at 60 km/h, 180 km by car at 90 km/h, and 100 km by bus at 50 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire journey?
(A) 65.00 km/h  (B) 70.00 km/h  (C) 68.50 km/h  (D) 66.67 km/h
Answer: (D) 66.67 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 120 + 180 + 100 = 400 km. Time for train = 120/60 = 2 hours. Time for car = 180/90 = 2 hours. Time for bus = 100/50 = 2 hours. Total time = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 hours. Average speed = 400 / 6 = 66.67 km/h. The required value is 66.67 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

30. (Medium) A student walks from his house at 2.5 km/h and reaches his school 6 minutes late. Next day, he increases his speed by 1 km/h (to 3.5 km/h) and reaches 6 minutes before time. How far is the school from his house?
(A) 1.75 km  (B) 2.00 km  (C) 1.50 km  (D) 2.25 km
Answer: (A) 1.75 km
Solution: Time difference Delta T = 6 + 6 = 12 minutes = 12/60 = 0.2 hours. Distance = (S1 * S2) / |S1 - S2| * Delta T = (2.5 * 3.5) / (3.5 - 2.5) * 0.2 = 8.75 * 0.2 = 1.75 km. The required value is 1.75 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

31. (Medium) A boat travels 24 km upstream in 6 hours and 20 km downstream in 4 hours. How much time will it take to cover a distance of 18 km in still water?
(A) 3.5 hours  (B) 4 hours  (C) 4.5 hours  (D) 5 hours
Answer: (B) 4 hours
Solution: Upstream speed U = 24 / 6 = 4 km/h. Downstream speed D = 20 / 4 = 5 km/h. Speed of boat in still water = (D + U) / 2 = (5 + 4) / 2 = 4.5 km/h. Time to cover 18 km in still water = 18 / 4.5 = 4 hours. The required value is 4 hours. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

32. (Medium) A thief running at 8 km/h is chased by a policeman running at 10 km/h. If the thief is 100 meters ahead, how long will the policeman take to catch the thief?

Answer: (C) 3 minutes
Solution: Relative speed of chase = 10 - 8 = 2 km/h = 2 * (5/18) = 5/9 m/s. Time to bridge 100 m = 100 / (5/9) = 180 seconds = 3 minutes. The required value is 3 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (C).

34. (Medium) Two cities A and B are 330 km apart. A train starts from A at 8:00 AM and travels towards B at 60 km/h. Another train starts from B at 9:00 AM and travels towards A at 75 km/h. At what time do they meet?
(A) 11:00 AM  (B) 10:30 AM  (C) 11:30 AM  (D) 12:00 PM
Answer: (A) 11:00 AM
Solution: Distance covered by Train A in first hour (8 to 9 AM) = 60 km. Remaining distance at 9:00 AM = 330 - 60 = 270 km. Relative speed = 60 + 75 = 135 km/h. Time to meet after 9:00 AM = 270 / 135 = 2 hours. Meeting time = 9:00 AM + 2 hours = 11:00 AM. The required value is 11:00 AM. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

35. (Medium) A man rows to a place 48 km distant and back in 14 hours. He finds that he can row 4 km with the stream in the same time as 3 km against the stream. What is the speed of the stream?
(A) 1.5 km/h  (B) 1 km/h  (C) 2 km/h  (D) 0.5 km/h
Answer: (B) 1 km/h
Solution: Ratio of downstream speed D to upstream speed U is 4 : 3. Let D = 4k and U = 3k. Total round trip time: 48 / (4k) + 48 / (3k) = 14 => 12/k + 16/k = 14 => 28/k = 14 => k = 2. Thus, D = 8 km/h and U = 6 km/h. Speed of stream = (D - U) / 2 = (8 - 6) / 2 = 1 km/h. The required value is 1 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

36. (Medium) A train running at 54 km/h takes 20 seconds to pass a platform. Next, it takes 12 seconds to pass a man walking at 6 km/h in the same direction. What is the length of the train?
(A) 150 meters  (B) 180 meters  (C) 160 meters  (D) 140 meters
Answer: (C) 160 meters
Solution: Relative speed with respect to man = 54 - 6 = 48 km/h = 48 * (5/18) = 40/3 m/s. Length of train = (40/3) * 12 = 160 meters. The required value is 160 meters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

37. (Medium) If a train runs at 40 km/h, it reaches its destination late by 11 minutes. But if it runs at 50 km/h, it is late by 5 minutes only. What is the correct scheduled time in minutes for the train to complete the journey?
(A) 20 minutes  (B) 18 minutes  (C) 22 minutes  (D) 19 minutes
Answer: (D) 19 minutes
Solution: Time difference Delta T = 11 - 5 = 6 minutes = 0.1 hour. Distance = (40 * 50) / (50 - 40) * 0.1 = 200 * 0.1 = 20 km. Time taken at 40 km/h = 20 / 40 = 0.5 hour = 30 minutes. Since this is 11 minutes late, scheduled time = 30 - 11 = 19 minutes. The required value is 19 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

38. (Medium) A monkey climbs up a greased pole 60 meters high. He climbs 3 meters in the first minute and slips down 2 meters in the alternate minute. In how many minutes will he reach the top of the pole?
(A) 115 minutes  (B) 120 minutes  (C) 118 minutes  (D) 116 minutes
Answer: (A) 115 minutes
Solution: Net climb in 2 minutes (1 cycle) = 3 - 2 = 1 meter. In the final minute, the monkey climbs 3 meters and reaches the top without slipping. Target before the final jump = 60 - 3 = 57 meters. Time to climb 57 meters = 57 * 2 = 114 minutes. On the 115th minute, he climbs the remaining 3 meters to reach 60 meters. Total time = 115 minutes. The required value is 115 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

39. (Medium) In a 500-meter race, the ratio of speeds of two contestants A and B is 3 : 4. If A has a head start of 140 meters, then A wins by how many meters?
(A) 25 meters  (B) 20 meters  (C) 15 meters  (D) 30 meters
Answer: (B) 20 meters
Solution: Distance A has to run = 500 - 140 = 360 meters. Time taken by A = 360 / 3 = 120 units. In 120 units of time, B runs 120 * 4 = 480 meters. Since the race is 500 meters long, B is at 480 meters when A crosses the finish line. A wins by 500 - 480 = 20 meters. The required value is 20 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Answer: (C) 68 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 80 * 2 + 60 * 3 = 160 + 180 = 340 km. Total time = 2 + 3 = 5 hours. Average speed = Total distance / Total time = 340 / 5 = 68 km/h. The required value is 68 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Answer: (A) 50 km
Solution: Let the time of travel be t hours. Then 14t - 10t = 20 => 4t = 20 => t = 5 hours. Actual distance covered = 10 * 5 = 50 km. The required value is 50 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).

43. (Medium) A boat takes 90 minutes less to travel 36 km downstream than to travel the same distance upstream. If the speed of the boat in still water is 10 km/h, what is the speed of the stream?
(A) 3 km/h  (B) 2 km/h  (C) 2.5 km/h  (D) 1.5 km/h
Answer: (B) 2 km/h
Solution: Equation: 36 / (10 - v) - 36 / (10 + v) = 1.5 hours. 36 * [2v / (100 - v^2)] = 1.5 => 72v / (100 - v^2) = 1.5 => 48v = 100 - v^2 => v^2 + 48v - 100 = 0 => (v + 50)(v - 2) = 0 => v = 2 km/h. The required value is 2 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

44. (Medium) A train leaves station P at 7:00 AM and reaches station Q at 11:00 AM. Another train leaves station Q at 8:00 AM and reaches station P at 11:30 AM. At what time do the two trains cross each other?
(A) 9:30 AM  (B) 9:15 AM  (C) 9:24 AM  (D) 9:36 AM
Answer: (C) 9:24 AM
Solution: Duration of Train 1 = 4 hours. Duration of Train 2 = 3.5 hours = 7/2 hours. Let distance = 28 km. Speed 1 = 28/4 = 7 km/h, Speed 2 = 28/(7/2) = 8 km/h. By 8:00 AM, Train 1 covers 7 km, leaving 21 km. Relative speed = 7 + 8 = 15 km/h. Time to meet = 21 / 15 = 1.4 hours = 1 hour 24 minutes. Crossing time = 8:00 AM + 1 hr 24 min = 9:24 AM. The required value is 9:24 AM. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

46. (Medium) A bus covers a distance of 400 km at a certain speed. If the speed is increased by 10 km/h, the journey takes 2 hours less. What was the original speed of the bus?
(A) 40 km/h  (B) 50 km/h  (C) 45 km/h  (D) 35 km/h

Solution: Equation: 400/s - 400/(s + 10) = 2 => 200/s - 200/(s + 10) = 1 => 2000 = s(s + 10). Since 40 * 50 = 2000, original speed s = 40 km/h. The required value is 40 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).

48. (Medium) Two travelers A and B start at the same time with speeds of 40 km/h and 50 km/h respectively. If in covering the journey A takes 15 minutes longer than B, what is the total distance of the journey?
(A) 45 km  (B) 55 km  (C) 50 km  (D) 60 km
Answer: (C) 50 km
Solution: Time difference Delta T = 15 minutes = 1/4 hour. Distance = (S1 * S2) / |S1 - S2| * Delta T = (40 * 50) / (50 - 40) * (1/4) = (2000 / 10) * 0.25 = 200 * 0.25 = 50 km. The required value is 50 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

50. (Medium) A person can row 7 1/2 km/h in still water and finds that it takes him twice as long to row up as to row down the river. What is the rate of the stream?
(A) 2.0 km/h  (B) 2.5 km/h  (C) 3.0 km/h  (D) 1.5 km/h
Answer: (B) 2.5 km/h
Solution: Let still water speed be u = 7.5 km/h and stream speed be v. Upstream time / Downstream time = (u + v) / (u - v) = 2 / 1 => u + v = 2u - 2v => 3v = u => v = 7.5 / 3 = 2.5 km/h. The required value is 2.5 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
