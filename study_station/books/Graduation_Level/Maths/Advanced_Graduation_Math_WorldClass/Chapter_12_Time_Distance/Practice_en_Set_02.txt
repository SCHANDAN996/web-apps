26. A train 240 m long crosses a pole in 12 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(a) 60 km/h (b) 80 km/h (c) 72 km/h (d) 90 km/h
Answer: (c)
Solution: Speed = distance/time = 240/12 = 20 m/s. Converting to km/h: 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h. The trap is forgetting the 18/5 conversion, which would leave the answer at 20. So the answer is 72 km/h.

27. A man walks at 5 km/h and reaches his office 10 minutes late. If he walks at 6 km/h, he reaches 5 minutes early. What is the distance to his office?
(a) 6 km (b) 10 km (c) 9 km (d) 7.5 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: Using the formula D = (S1 × S2 × time difference)/(S2 − S1) = (5 × 6 × 15/60)/(6 − 5) = 30 × 0.25 = 7.5 km. The trap is using 10 minutes instead of the total gap of 15 minutes (10 late + 5 early). So the answer is 7.5 km.

50. A and B start together from the same point on a 400 m circular track at speeds of 6 m/s and 4 m/s in the same direction. After how long will they meet again at the starting point?
(a) 100 s (b) 200 s (c) 150 s (d) 400 s
Answer: (b)
Solution: A completes a lap in 400/6 = 200/3 s and B in 400/4 = 100 s. They meet at the starting point at the LCM of 200/3 and 100, which is 200 s. The trap is using the relative speed formula 400/(6 − 4) = 200 s for meeting anywhere — here it coincides, but the correct reasoning is the LCM of lap times; note 100 s only brings B to the start, not A. So the answer is 200 s.
