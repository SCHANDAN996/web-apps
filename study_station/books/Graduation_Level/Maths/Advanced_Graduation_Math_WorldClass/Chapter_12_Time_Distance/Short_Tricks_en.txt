# Exam Short Tricks: Time, Speed and Distance

## Trick 1: Fast Early / Late Distance Calculation
When changing speeds from $S_1$ to $S_2$ alters arrival time by $\Delta T$:
$$\text{Distance} = \frac{S_1 \cdot S_2}{|S_1 - S_2|} \times \left( \frac{\Delta T \text{ (in minutes)}}{60} \right)$$
- **Example:** Walking at 4 km/h, late by 10 minutes. Walking at 5 km/h, early by 5 minutes.
  $\Delta T = 10 + 5 = 15$ minutes = $1/4$ hour.
  $$\text{Distance} = \frac{4 \times 5}{5 - 4} \times \frac{15}{60} = 20 \times \frac{1}{4} = 5 \text{ km}$$

---

## Trick 2: Meeting and Crossing Destinations
If two travelers meet and take $t_1$ and $t_2$ hours to finish their respective journeys:
$$\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{t_2}{t_1}}$$
- **Example:** A and B meet. A reaches destination in 4 hours, B in 9 hours.
  $$S_A : S_B = \sqrt{9 / 4} = 3 : 2$$

---

## Trick 3: Instant Stoppage Time per Hour
$$\text{Stoppage Time (minutes/hour)} = \frac{\text{Difference in Speeds}}{\text{Speed without Stoppage}} \times 60$$
- **Example:** Speed without stops = 54 km/h, with stops = 45 km/h.
  $$\text{Stoppage} = \frac{54 - 45}{54} \times 60 = \frac{9}{54} \times 60 = 10 \text{ minutes/hour}$$

---

## Trick 4: River Round-Trip Ratio
If a boat takes $n$ times as long to row upstream as downstream over the same distance:
$$\frac{u}{v} = \frac{n + 1}{n - 1}$$
- **Example:** A boat takes 3 times as long upstream as downstream ($n = 3$):
  $$u : v = \frac{3 + 1}{3 - 1} = \frac{4}{2} = 2 : 1$$
