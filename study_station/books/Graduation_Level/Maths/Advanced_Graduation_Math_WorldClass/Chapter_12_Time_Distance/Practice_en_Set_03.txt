Questions 51–75: Medium

51. (Medium) Two trains start at the same time from two stations A and B towards each other at 50 km/h and 60 km/h respectively. When they meet, it is found that the faster train has traveled 120 km more than the slower train. What is the distance between the two stations?
(A) 1320 km  (B) 1200 km  (C) 1250 km  (D) 1400 km
Answer: (A) 1320 km
Solution: Difference in speeds = 60 - 50 = 10 km/h. Time elapsed until meeting = 120 / 10 = 12 hours. Sum of speeds = 50 + 60 = 110 km/h. Total distance = 110 * 12 = 1320 km. The required value is 1320 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

52. (Medium) Two trains A and B start from station X to Y and station Y to X respectively at the same time. After meeting each other, train A takes 4 hours 48 minutes and train B takes 3 hours 20 minutes to reach their respective destinations. If train A is moving at 45 km/h, what is the speed of train B?
(A) 50 km/h  (B) 54 km/h  (C) 60 km/h  (D) 48 km/h
Answer: (B) 54 km/h
Solution: t1 = 4 hrs 48 min = 24/5 hours, t2 = 3 hrs 20 min = 10/3 hours. Using S1 / S2 = sqrt(t2 / t1) = sqrt((10/3) / (24/5)) = sqrt(50/72) = sqrt(25/36) = 5/6. Given S1 = 45: 45 / S2 = 5/6 => S2 = 45 * 6 / 5 = 54 km/h. The required value is 54 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

53. (Medium) In a 1000-meter race, Runner A beats Runner B by 100 meters or 10 seconds. What is the time taken by Runner A to finish the race?
(A) 100 seconds  (B) 85 seconds  (C) 90 seconds  (D) 95 seconds
Answer: (C) 90 seconds
Solution: Speed of Runner B = 100 m / 10 s = 10 m/s. Time taken by Runner B to finish 1000 m = 1000 / 10 = 100 seconds. Since A beats B by 10 seconds, time taken by Runner A = 100 - 10 = 90 seconds. The required value is 90 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

54. (Medium) A boat travels 32 km downstream and 14 km upstream in 6 hours. In another journey, it travels 40 km downstream and 21 km upstream in 8 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
(A) 7.0 km/h  (B) 8.0 km/h  (C) 6.5 km/h  (D) 7.5 km/h
Answer: (D) 7.5 km/h
Solution: Equations: 32/D + 14/U = 6 and 40/D + 21/U = 8. Testing D = 8: 32/8 + 14/U = 6 => 14/U = 2 => U = 7. For second: 40/8 + 21/7 = 5 + 3 = 8 (valid). Speed of boat in still water = (D + U) / 2 = (8 + 7) / 2 = 7.5 km/h. The required value is 7.5 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

55. (Medium) A train travels 20% faster than a car. Both start from point A at the same time and reach point B, 180 km away, at the same time. On the way, the train lost 30 minutes while stopping at stations. What is the speed of the car in km/h?
(A) 60 km/h  (B) 50 km/h  (C) 72 km/h  (D) 65 km/h
Answer: (A) 60 km/h
Solution: Let speed of car be S km/h. Speed of train = 1.2S = 6S/5. Time difference = 30 minutes = 0.5 hours. Equation: 180/S - 180/(1.2S) = 0.5 => 180/S * (1 - 5/6) = 0.5 => 30/S = 0.5 => S = 60 km/h. The required value is 60 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

56. (Medium) Two runners A and B run on a circular track of circumference 600 meters from the same starting point at speeds of 15 m/s and 10 m/s in opposite directions. After how many seconds will they meet for the first time?
(A) 20 seconds  (B) 24 seconds  (C) 30 seconds  (D) 25 seconds
Answer: (B) 24 seconds
Solution: Relative speed in opposite directions = 15 + 10 = 25 m/s. Time for first meeting = Track circumference / Relative speed = 600 / 25 = 24 seconds. The required value is 24 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (B).

57. (Medium) A man travels a distance of 61 km in 9 hours, partly on foot at 4 km/h and partly on bicycle at 9 km/h. What is the distance travelled on foot?
(A) 14 km  (B) 18 km  (C) 16 km  (D) 20 km
Answer: (C) 16 km
Solution: Let time on foot be t hours. Then 4t + 9(9 - t) = 61 => 4t + 81 - 9t = 61 => 5t = 20 => t = 4 hours. Distance on foot = 4 * 4 = 16 km. The required value is 16 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

58. (Medium) A train after traveling 50 km meets with an accident and then proceeds at 3/4 of its former speed and arrives at its destination 35 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 24 km further on, it would have arrived only 25 minutes late. What is the original speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 40 km/h  (B) 50 km/h  (C) 45 km/h  (D) 48 km/h
Answer: (D) 48 km/h
Solution: For the 24 km stretch, time saved = 35 - 25 = 10 minutes = 1/6 hour. At 3/4 of normal speed, time taken is 4/3 of normal, causing an extra delay of 1/3 of normal time: (1/3) * (24 / S) = 1/6 => 8 / S = 1/6 => S = 48 km/h. The required value is 48 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

59. (Medium) A thief steals a motorcycle and drives away at 45 km/h. The owner discovers the theft after 40 minutes and pursues in a car at 60 km/h. At what distance from the place of theft will the owner catch up with the thief?
(A) 120 km  (B) 100 km  (C) 140 km  (D) 150 km
Answer: (A) 120 km
Solution: Head start of thief in 40 minutes = 45 * (40/60) = 30 km. Relative speed = 60 - 45 = 15 km/h. Time to overtake = 30 / 15 = 2 hours. Distance from starting point = 60 * 2 = 120 km. The required value is 120 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

60. (Medium) A train crosses a pole in 8 seconds and a bridge 200 meters long in 24 seconds. In how many seconds will it cross a platform 300 meters long?
(A) 30 seconds  (B) 32 seconds  (C) 35 seconds  (D) 28 seconds
Answer: (B) 32 seconds
Solution: Speed of train = 200 / (24 - 8) = 200 / 16 = 12.5 m/s. Length of train = 12.5 * 8 = 100 meters. Total distance for 300 m platform = 100 + 300 = 400 meters. Time taken = 400 / 12.5 = 32 seconds. The required value is 32 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

61. (Medium) A boy goes to school at an average speed of 12 km/h and returns at an average speed of 8 km/h. If he takes a total of 50 minutes for the two-way journey, what is the distance between his home and school?
(A) 5 km  (B) 3.5 km  (C) 4 km  (D) 4.5 km
Answer: (C) 4 km
Solution: Average speed for round trip = (2 * 12 * 8) / (12 + 8) = 192 / 20 = 9.6 km/h. Total distance for two-way journey = 9.6 * (50/60) = 8 km. One-way distance = 8 / 2 = 4 km. The required value is 4 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

62. (Medium) In a 1 km race, Runner A beats Runner B by 100 meters, and in a 1 km race, Runner B beats Runner C by 50 meters. By how many meters does Runner A beat Runner C in a 1 km race?
(A) 150 meters  (B) 140 meters  (C) 155 meters  (D) 145 meters
Answer: (D) 145 meters
Solution: Ratio A : B = 1000 : 900 = 10 : 9. Ratio B : C = 1000 : 950 = 20 : 19. Ratio A : C = (10/9) * (20/19) = 200 / 171. When A runs 1000 m (scaled by 5), C runs 171 * 5 = 855 meters. A beats C by 1000 - 855 = 145 meters. The required value is 145 meters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

63. (Medium) A boat goes 12 km downstream and comes back to the starting point in 3 hours. If the speed of the current is 3 km/h, what is the speed of the boat in still water?
(A) 9 km/h  (B) 8 km/h  (C) 10 km/h  (D) 7.5 km/h
Answer: (A) 9 km/h
Solution: Equation: 12 / (u + 3) + 12 / (u - 3) = 3 => 4 / (u + 3) + 4 / (u - 3) = 1 => 4(2u) = u^2 - 9 => u^2 - 8u - 9 = 0 => (u - 9)(u + 1) = 0 => u = 9 km/h. The required value is 9 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

64. (Medium) Two stations A and B are 110 km apart on a straight line. One train starts from A at 7:00 AM and travels towards B at 20 km/h. Another train starts from B at 8:00 AM and travels towards A at 25 km/h. At what time will they meet?
(A) 10:30 AM  (B) 10:00 AM  (C) 9:45 AM  (D) 11:00 AM
Answer: (B) 10:00 AM
Solution: From 7:00 to 8:00 AM, Train A covers 20 km. Remaining distance at 8:00 AM = 110 - 20 = 90 km. Relative speed = 20 + 25 = 45 km/h. Time to meet after 8:00 AM = 90 / 45 = 2 hours. Meeting time = 8:00 AM + 2 hours = 10:00 AM. The required value is 10:00 AM. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

66. (Medium) A train running at 36 km/h crosses a standing person in 12 seconds. How many seconds will it take to cross a 280-meter long tunnel?
(A) 35 seconds  (B) 45 seconds  (C) 38 seconds  (D) 40 seconds
Answer: (D) 40 seconds
Solution: Speed in m/s = 36 * (5/18) = 10 m/s. Length of train = 10 * 12 = 120 meters. Total distance for tunnel = 120 + 280 = 400 meters. Time taken = 400 / 10 = 40 seconds. The required value is 40 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (D).

67. (Medium) A man travels from P to Q at a speed of 45 km/h and returns at a speed of 75 km/h. If the return journey took 16 minutes less than the forward journey, what is the distance between P and Q?
(A) 30 km  (B) 35 km  (C) 25 km  (D) 40 km
Answer: (A) 30 km
Solution: Time difference Delta T = 16 minutes = 16/60 = 4/15 hours. Distance = (45 * 75) / (75 - 45) * (4/15) = (3375 / 30) * (4/15) = 112.5 * (4/15) = 30 km. The required value is 30 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

68. (Medium) A train overtakes two persons walking in the same direction at 2 km/h and 4 km/h in 9 seconds and 10 seconds respectively. What is the length of the train in meters?
(A) 45 meters  (B) 50 meters  (C) 60 meters  (D) 55 meters
Answer: (B) 50 meters
Solution: Let speed of train be S m/s. S1 = 2 * (5/18) = 5/9 m/s, S2 = 4 * (5/18) = 10/9 m/s. L = (S - 5/9) * 9 = 9S - 5. Also L = (S - 10/9) * 10 = 10S - 100/9. Equating gives S = 55/9 m/s. Length L = 9*(55/9) - 5 = 55 - 5 = 50 meters. The required value is 50 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

69. (Medium) In a 100-meter race, A can give B 10 meters and C 28 meters. In the same race, how many meters head start can B give to C?
(A) 18 meters  (B) 22 meters  (C) 20 meters  (D) 25 meters
Answer: (C) 20 meters
Solution: When A runs 100 m, B runs 90 m and C runs 72 m. Ratio C / B = 72 / 90 = 4 / 5. In a 100 m race, when B runs 100 m, C runs 100 * (4/5) = 80 meters. Therefore, B can give C a start of 100 - 80 = 20 meters. The required value is 20 meters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

70. (Medium) A motorboat in still water travels at 15 km/h. It goes 30 km downstream and returns in a total of 4 hours 30 minutes. What is the speed of the stream?
(A) 4 km/h  (B) 6 km/h  (C) 4.5 km/h  (D) 5 km/h
Answer: (D) 5 km/h
Solution: Equation: 30 / (15 + v) + 30 / (15 - v) = 4.5 = 9/2 hours. 30 * [30 / (225 - v^2)] = 9/2 => 900 / (225 - v^2) = 9/2 => 100 / (225 - v^2) = 1/2 => 225 - v^2 = 200 => v^2 = 25 => v = 5 km/h. The required value is 5 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

72. (Medium) Two friends A and B start from point X at the same time to walk to point Y, 30 km away. A walks at 4 km/h and B at 6 km/h. B reaches Y, immediately turns back, and meets A at point Z. What is the distance from X to Z?

74. (Medium) A cyclist rides around a circular track of circumference 750 meters at 15 m/s, and a runner runs in the opposite direction at 10 m/s. If they start from the same point simultaneously, how many times will they cross each other in 6 minutes?
(A) 10 times  (B) 14 times  (C) 15 times  (D) 12 times
Answer: (D) 12 times
Solution: Relative speed in opposite direction = 15 + 10 = 25 m/s. Time for one crossing = 750 / 25 = 30 seconds. In 6 minutes (360 seconds), number of crossings = 360 / 30 = 12 times. The required value is 12 times. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

75. (Medium) A passenger train leaves a station at 6:00 AM at 40 km/h. An express train leaves the same station at 8:30 AM in the same direction at 60 km/h. At what time will the express train overtake the passenger train?
(A) 1:00 PM  (B) 1:30 PM  (C) 2:00 PM  (D) 12:30 PM
Answer: (B) 1:30 PM
Solution: Time lead of passenger train = 2.5 hours. Head start distance = 40 * 2.5 = 100 km. Relative speed = 60 - 40 = 20 km/h. Time to overtake = 100 / 20 = 5 hours after 8:30 AM. Overtake time = 8:30 AM + 5 hours = 1:30 PM. The required value is 1:30 PM. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
