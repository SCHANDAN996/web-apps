51. A train 240 m long crosses a pole in 12 seconds. How long will it take to cross a platform 360 m long?
(a) 24 s (b) 36 s (c) 28 s (d) 30 s
Answer: (d)
Solution: Speed of train = 240/12 = 20 m/s. To cross the platform it must cover 240 + 360 = 600 m, so time = 600/20 = 30 s. The tempting wrong option 24 s comes from forgetting to add the train's own length to the platform length, so the answer is 30 s.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A man covers a certain distance in 6 hours at 40 km/h. At what speed must he travel to cover the same distance in 5 hours?
(a) 48 km/h (b) 45 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 52 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Distance = 40 × 6 = 240 km. Required speed = 240/5 = 48 km/h. The trap is to guess a speed near 40 km/h instead of first fixing the distance, so the answer is 48 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept), PYQ-style

53. Two trains of lengths 120 m and 180 m run towards each other on parallel lines at 42 km/h and 48 km/h. In what time will they cross each other?
(a) 10 s (b) 18 s (c) 12 s (d) 15 s
Answer: (c)
Solution: Relative speed = 42 + 48 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Total distance = 120 + 180 = 300 m, so time = 300/25 = 12 s. The tempting wrong option 18 s comes from wrongly subtracting the speeds as if the trains moved in the same direction, so the answer is 12 s.
Source: PYQ-style

54. A car travels the first 160 km at 40 km/h and the next 160 km at 80 km/h. Its average speed for the whole journey is:
(a) 53⅓ km/h (b) 60 km/h (c) 56 km/h (d) 58 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Times are 160/40 = 4 h and 160/80 = 2 h. Average speed = total distance/total time = 320/6 = 53⅓ km/h. The tempting wrong option 60 km/h is the simple arithmetic mean of the two speeds, which is wrong when equal distances (not equal times) are covered, so the answer is 53⅓ km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

55. A boat goes 30 km downstream in 2 hours and returns in 3 hours. The speed of the stream is:
(a) 2 km/h (b) 2.5 km/h (c) 3 km/h (d) 3.5 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Downstream speed = 30/2 = 15 km/h; upstream speed = 30/3 = 10 km/h. Stream speed = (15 − 10)/2 = 2.5 km/h. The tempting wrong option comes from taking the plain difference of the two speeds and forgetting to halve it, so the answer is 2.5 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

56. Walking at 5/6 of his usual speed, a man reaches his office 10 minutes late. His usual time to reach the office is:
(a) 40 min (b) 45 min (c) 50 min (d) 60 min
Answer: (c)
Solution: At 5/6 of usual speed, time becomes 6/5 of usual time. Extra time = (6/5 − 1)T = T/5 = 10 min, so T = 50 min. The tempting wrong option 60 min comes from wrongly taking the extra fraction as 1/6 of T, so the answer is 50 min.
Source: PYQ-style

57. A train running at 72 km/h crosses a man standing on a platform in 10 seconds. The length of the train is:
(a) 200 m (b) 180 m (c) 220 m (d) 240 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s. Length = 20 × 10 = 200 m. The tempting wrong option 240 m comes from a slip in the km/h to m/s conversion, so the answer is 200 m.
Source: PYQ-style

58. A and B start together from the same point and walk in opposite directions at 4 km/h and 6 km/h. After how long will they be 25 km apart?
(a) 2 h (b) 2.5 h (c) 3 h (d) 3.5 h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Separation speed = 4 + 6 = 10 km/h. Time = 25/10 = 2.5 h. The tempting wrong option comes from using the difference of speeds (2 km/h), which applies only when both move in the same direction, so the answer is 2.5 h.
Source: PYQ-style

59. A man rows at 8 km/h in still water. If the stream flows at 2 km/h, how long will he take to go 40 km downstream and come back?
(a) 10 h (b) 11 h (c) 10 h 40 min (d) 12 h
Answer: (c)
Solution: Downstream speed = 10 km/h, time = 40/10 = 4 h. Upstream speed = 6 km/h, time = 40/6 = 6 h 40 min. Total = 10 h 40 min. The tempting wrong option 10 h assumes the still-water speed of 8 km/h for the whole round trip, ignoring the stream, so the answer is 10 h 40 min.
Source: PYQ-style

60. Two trains start from stations A and B, 300 km apart, at the same time and travel towards each other at 55 km/h and 65 km/h. After how many hours do they meet?
(a) 2 h (b) 2.5 h (c) 3 h (d) 3.5 h
Answer: (b)
Solution: Relative speed = 55 + 65 = 120 km/h. Time = 300/120 = 2.5 h. The tempting wrong option comes from using the difference of speeds (10 km/h), which would give an absurd 30 h, so the answer is 2.5 h.
Source: PYQ-style

61. A cyclist covers a distance of 750 m in 2 minutes 30 seconds. His speed in km/h is:
(a) 15 km/h (b) 21 km/h (c) 20 km/h (d) 18 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: Time = 150 s. Speed = 750/150 = 5 m/s = 5 × 18/5 = 18 km/h. The tempting wrong option 15 km/h comes from a slip in the m/s to km/h conversion (multiplying by 3 instead of 3.6), so the answer is 18 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

62. A train 150 m long is running at 54 km/h. In what time will it pass a man running at 6 km/h in the same direction as the train?
(a) 10 s (b) 12 s (c) 11.25 s (d) 12.5 s
Answer: (c)
Solution: Relative speed = 54 − 6 = 48 km/h = 48 × 5/18 = 40/3 m/s. Time = 150 ÷ (40/3) = 11.25 s. The tempting wrong option 10 s comes from adding the speeds (60 km/h), which applies only to opposite directions, so the answer is 11.25 s.
Source: PYQ-style

63. A man travels 600 km partly by train at 80 km/h and partly by car at 60 km/h, taking 8 hours in all. The distance covered by train is:
(a) 480 km (b) 400 km (c) 420 km (d) 500 km
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let train distance be x. Then x/80 + (600 − x)/60 = 8. Multiplying by 240: 3x + 4(600 − x) = 1920, so 2400 − x = 1920 and x = 480 km. The tempting wrong option 400 km comes from assuming equal times for both parts, so the answer is 480 km.
Source: PYQ-style

64. A bus covers a certain distance at 60 km/h without stoppage and at 45 km/h with stoppages. How many minutes per hour does the bus stop?
(a) 15 min (b) 10 min (c) 12 min (d) 18 min
Answer: (a)
Solution: In one hour at 60 km/h the bus would cover 60 km, but with stoppages it covers only 45 km, losing 15 km. Stoppage time = 15/60 h = 15 min. The tempting wrong option 12 min comes from wrongly computing 45/60 of an hour as the stoppage, so the answer is 15 min.
Source: PYQ-style

65. A thief steals a car at 1:00 pm and drives at 50 km/h. The owner discovers the theft at 2:00 pm and chases at 70 km/h. At what time does he catch the thief?
(a) 4:00 pm (b) 5:00 pm (c) 5:30 pm (d) 4:30 pm
Answer: (d)
Solution: By 2:00 pm the thief is 50 km ahead. Relative speed = 70 − 50 = 20 km/h, so catching time = 50/20 = 2.5 h and the catch time is 2:00 + 2:30 = 4:30 pm. The tempting wrong option 4:00 pm ignores the one-hour head start, so the answer is 4:30 pm.
Source: PYQ-style

66. A man can row 9 km/h in still water. He takes twice as long to row up as to row down the river. The speed of the current is:
(a) 3 km/h (b) 3.5 km/h (c) 2 km/h (d) 4 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Taking twice as long upstream means downstream speed = 2 × upstream speed. So 9 + c = 2(9 − c), giving 9 + c = 18 − 2c, hence 3c = 9 and c = 3 km/h. The tempting wrong option comes from setting the two speeds equal instead of in ratio 2:1, so the answer is 3 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

67. Two trains of equal length cross a pole in 18 s and 12 s respectively. In what time will they cross each other when moving in opposite directions?
(a) 14.4 s (b) 14 s (c) 15 s (d) 16 s
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let each length be L. Speeds are L/18 and L/12, so relative speed = L/18 + L/12 = 5L/36. Time to cross = 2L ÷ (5L/36) = 72/5 = 14.4 s. The tempting wrong option 15 s is the average of 18 and 12, which has no physical basis, so the answer is 14.4 s.
Source: PYQ-style

68. A person travels a certain distance. If he increases his speed by 10 km/h, he saves 1 hour; if he decreases his speed by 10 km/h, he takes 2 hours more. The distance is:
(a) 110 km (b) 120 km (c) 100 km (d) 130 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let speed be s and time t. Then st = (s + 10)(t − 1) gives 10t − s = 10, and st = (s − 10)(t + 2) gives 2s − 10t = 20. Adding: s = 30 km/h, then t = 4 h, so distance = 30 × 4 = 120 km. The tempting wrong option 100 km comes from solving only one of the two conditions, so the answer is 120 km.
Source: PYQ-style

69. A train crosses a bridge 200 m long in 25 seconds and a bridge 300 m long in 35 seconds. The length of the train is:
(a) 75 m (b) 100 m (c) 60 m (d) 50 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The extra 100 m of bridge takes 35 − 25 = 10 s extra, so speed = 100/10 = 10 m/s. Train length = 25 × 10 − 200 = 50 m. The tempting wrong option 100 m is just the difference of the bridge lengths, not the train's length, so the answer is 50 m.
Source: PYQ-style

70. A and B run a 1 km race. A gives B a start of 100 m and still beats him by 20 seconds. If A runs at 10 m/s, B's speed is:
(a) 8 m/s (b) 8.2 m/s (c) 7.5 m/s (d) 8.5 m/s
Answer: (c)
Solution: A runs 1000 m in 1000/10 = 100 s. B runs only 900 m and takes 100 + 20 = 120 s, so B's speed = 900/120 = 7.5 m/s. The tempting wrong option 8 m/s comes from letting B run the full 1000 m in 120 s, ignoring the 100 m start, so the answer is 7.5 m/s.
Source: PYQ-style

71. A man travels from A to B at 30 km/h and returns at 45 km/h. His average speed for the round trip is:
(a) 38 km/h (b) 36 km/h (c) 37.5 km/h (d) 40 km/h
Answer: (b)
Solution: For equal distances, average speed = 2 × 30 × 45/(30 + 45) = 2700/75 = 36 km/h. The tempting wrong option 37.5 km/h is the simple mean of 30 and 45, valid only for equal times, so the answer is 36 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

72. A goods train leaves a station at 6 am at 40 km/h. An express train leaves the same station at 8 am at 60 km/h in the same direction. At what distance from the station does the express overtake the goods train?
(a) 200 km (b) 240 km (c) 260 km (d) 220 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: By 8 am the goods train has an 80 km lead. Relative speed = 60 − 40 = 20 km/h, so catching time = 80/20 = 4 h and distance = 60 × 4 = 240 km. The tempting wrong option 200 km comes from using the goods train's speed for the catching distance, so the answer is 240 km.
Source: PYQ-style

73. A boat takes 8 hours to go 48 km downstream and 12 hours to return the same distance. The speed of the boat in still water is:
(a) 4 km/h (b) 7 km/h (c) 6 km/h (d) 5 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: Downstream speed = 48/8 = 6 km/h; upstream speed = 48/12 = 4 km/h. Boat speed in still water = (6 + 4)/2 = 5 km/h. The tempting wrong option 6 km/h is just the downstream speed, forgetting to average with the upstream speed, so the answer is 5 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

74. A train 180 m long is running at 90 km/h. How long will it take to cross a platform 270 m long?
(a) 20 s (b) 16 s (c) 18 s (d) 22 s
Answer: (c)
Solution: Speed = 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s. Total distance = 180 + 270 = 450 m, so time = 450/25 = 18 s. The tempting wrong option comes from using only the platform length or only the train length, so the answer is 18 s.
Source: PYQ-style

75. A man walks a certain distance and rides back, taking 37 minutes in all. He could walk both ways in 55 minutes. How long would he take to ride both ways?
(a) 18 min (b) 22 min (c) 20 min (d) 19 min
Answer: (d)
Solution: Walk one way = 55/2 = 27.5 min. Ride one way = 37 − 27.5 = 9.5 min, so ride both ways = 2 × 9.5 = 19 min. The tempting wrong option 18 min comes from wrongly halving 37 instead of first removing the walking leg, so the answer is 19 min.
Source: PYQ-style
