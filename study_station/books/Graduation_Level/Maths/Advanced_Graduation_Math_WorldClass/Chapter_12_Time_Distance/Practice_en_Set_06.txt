Questions 126–150: Very Hard

126. (Very Hard) A train leaves station A at 6:00 AM and reaches station B at 10:00 AM. Another train leaves station B at 8:00 AM and reaches station A at 11:30 AM. At what time do the two trains cross each other?
(A) 8:56 AM  (B) 9:00 AM  (C) 8:48 AM  (D) 9:12 AM
Answer: (A) 8:56 AM
Solution: Duration of Train 1 = 4 hours. Duration of Train 2 = 3.5 hours = 7/2 hours. Let distance = 28 km. Speed 1 = 28/4 = 7 km/h, Speed 2 = 28/(7/2) = 8 km/h. By 8:00 AM, Train 1 covers 7 * 2 = 14 km. Remaining distance at 8:00 AM = 28 - 14 = 14 km. Combined speed = 7 + 8 = 15 km/h. Time to meet after 8:00 AM = 14 / 15 hours = 56 minutes. Crossing time = 8:00 AM + 56 min = 8:56 AM. The required value is 8:56 AM. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

127. (Very Hard) Two guns were fired from the same place at an interval of 11 minutes 45 seconds, but a person who was approaching the place observed that 11 minutes elapsed between hearing the first and the second sound. If the speed of sound is 330 m/s, what is the speed of the approaching person in km/h?
(A) 75 km/h  (B) 81 km/h  (C) 84 km/h  (D) 78 km/h
Answer: (B) 81 km/h
Solution: Time difference = 11 min 45 s - 11 min = 45 seconds. The distance covered by sound in 45 seconds is covered by the approaching person in 11 minutes = 660 seconds. Distance = 330 * 45 = 14850 meters. Speed of person = 14850 / 660 = 22.5 m/s. Converting to km/h: 22.5 * (18/5) = 81 km/h. The required value is 81 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

128. (Very Hard) A, B, and C walk around a circular track 1200 meters long with speeds 2 m/s, 4 m/s, and 6 m/s respectively, starting from the same point at the same time in the same direction. How many seconds will elapse before all three meet together again for the first time anywhere on the track?
(A) 400 seconds  (B) 300 seconds  (C) 600 seconds  (D) 1200 seconds
Answer: (C) 600 seconds
Solution: Relative speed of B with respect to A = 4 - 2 = 2 m/s => Time for B to lap A = 1200 / 2 = 600 s. Relative speed of C with respect to A = 6 - 2 = 4 m/s => Time for C to lap A = 1200 / 4 = 300 s. Time when all three meet = LCM(600, 300) = 600 seconds. The required value is 600 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: CDS Pattern

129. (Very Hard) A man can row 8 km/h in still water. When the river flows at 4 km/h, it takes him 3 hours longer to row up than to row down a certain distance. What is the distance?
(A) 16 km  (B) 20 km  (C) 24 km  (D) 18 km
Answer: (D) 18 km
Solution: Downstream speed D = 8 + 4 = 12 km/h. Upstream speed U = 8 - 4 = 4 km/h. Equation: d/4 - d/12 = 3 => (3d - d) / 12 = 3 => 2d = 36 => d = 18 km. The required value is 18 km. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

130. (Very Hard) A train overtakes two boys walking in the same direction at 4 km/h and 5 km/h in 9 seconds and 10 seconds respectively. What is the length of the train in meters?
(A) 25 meters  (B) 30 meters  (C) 20 meters  (D) 35 meters
Answer: (A) 25 meters
Solution: Boy 1 speed = 4 * (5/18) = 10/9 m/s. Boy 2 speed = 5 * (5/18) = 25/18 m/s. Length L = 9(S - 10/9) = 9S - 10. Also L = 10(S - 25/18) = 10S - 125/9. Equating: 9S - 10 = 10S - 125/9 => S = 35/9 m/s. Train length L = 9(35/9) - 10 = 35 - 10 = 25 meters. The required value is 25 meters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

131. (Very Hard) Two points A and B are 100 km apart. One car starts from A and another from B at the same time. If they travel in the same direction, they meet in 5 hours. If they travel towards each other, they meet in 1 hour. What are the speeds of the two cars?
(A) 55 km/h and 45 km/h  (B) 60 km/h and 40 km/h  (C) 65 km/h and 35 km/h  (D) 70 km/h and 30 km/h
Answer: (B) 60 km/h and 40 km/h
Solution: Relative speed in same direction = 100 / 5 = 20 km/h => S1 - S2 = 20. Relative speed in opposite direction = 100 / 1 = 100 km/h => S1 + S2 = 100. Adding gives 2 S1 = 120 => S1 = 60 km/h and S2 = 40 km/h. The required value is 60 km/h and 40 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: State PCS Pattern

132. (Very Hard) A train leaves a station 30 minutes behind schedule. In order to reach its destination 1500 km away on time, it has to increase its speed by 250 km/h from its usual speed. What is its usual speed in km/h?
(A) 700 km/h  (B) 800 km/h  (C) 750 km/h  (D) 650 km/h
Answer: (C) 750 km/h
Solution: Equation: 1500/S - 1500/(S + 250) = 0.5 hours => 3000 * 250 = S(S + 250) => S(S + 250) = 750,000. Factoring: 750 * 1000 = 750,000 => Usual speed S = 750 km/h. The required value is 750 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

133. (Very Hard) In a 1000-meter race, A can beat B by 100 meters, and B can beat C by 100 meters. In a 500-meter race, by how many meters will A beat C?
(A) 100 meters  (B) 90 meters  (C) 85 meters  (D) 95 meters
Answer: (D) 95 meters
Solution: Ratios: A : B = 10 : 9 and B : C = 10 : 9 => A : C = 100 : 81. In a 500-meter race, when A covers 500 m, C covers 500 * (81/100) = 405 meters. Margin of victory = 500 - 405 = 95 meters. The required value is 95 meters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

Answer: (A) 4 hours
Solution: Let time on foot be t hours. Then 10t + 20(8 - t) = 120 => 10t + 160 - 20t = 120 => 10t = 40 => t = 4 hours. The required value is 4 hours. Hence, the correct option is (A).

135. (Very Hard) A train crosses two persons walking in the opposite direction at 3 km/h and 6 km/h in 6 seconds and 5 seconds respectively. What is the length of the train in meters?
(A) 30 meters  (B) 25 meters  (C) 20 meters  (D) 35 meters
Answer: (B) 25 meters
Solution: S1 = 3 * (5/18) = 5/6 m/s, S2 = 6 * (5/18) = 5/3 m/s. Opp direction: L = 6(S + 5/6) = 6S + 5. Also L = 5(S + 5/3) = 5S + 25/3. Equating gives 6S + 5 = 5S + 25/3 => S = 10/3 m/s. Train length L = 6(10/3) + 5 = 20 + 5 = 25 meters. The required value is 25 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

136. (Very Hard) A boat covers a round trip of 48 km downstream and 48 km upstream in 14 hours. If the speed of the current is 1 km/h, what is the speed of the boat in still water?
(A) 6 km/h  (B) 8 km/h  (C) 7 km/h  (D) 7.5 km/h
Answer: (C) 7 km/h
Solution: Equation: 48 / (u + 1) + 48 / (u - 1) = 14 => 96u = 14(u^2 - 1) => 7u^2 - 48u - 7 = 0 => (7u + 1)(u - 7) = 0 => u = 7 km/h. The required value is 7 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: CDS Pattern

137. (Very Hard) A motorboat went downstream for 28 km and immediately returned. It took 3 hours 12 minutes to complete the round trip. If the speed of the stream is 3 km/h, what is the speed of the motorboat in still water?
(A) 15 km/h  (B) 20 km/h  (C) 16 km/h  (D) 18 km/h
Answer: (D) 18 km/h
Solution: Time = 3 1/5 = 16/5 hours. Equation: 28/(u + 3) + 28/(u - 3) = 16/5 => 56u / (u^2 - 9) = 16/5 => 35u = 2u^2 - 18 => 2u^2 - 35u - 18 = 0 => (2u + 1)(u - 18) = 0 => u = 18 km/h. The required value is 18 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

138. (Very Hard) In a 100-meter race, Runner A covers the distance in 36 seconds and Runner B in 45 seconds. In this race, by how many meters does Runner A beat Runner B?
(A) 20 meters  (B) 25 meters  (C) 18 meters  (D) 22 meters
Answer: (A) 20 meters
Solution: When A finishes in 36 seconds, B has run for 36 seconds. Speed of B = 100 / 45 = 20/9 m/s. Distance covered by B in 36 seconds = 36 * (20/9) = 80 meters. A beats B by 100 - 80 = 20 meters. The required value is 20 meters. Hence, the correct option is (A).

139. (Very Hard) A thief steals a car at 2:30 PM and drives it at 60 km/h. The theft is discovered at 3:00 PM and the owner sets off in another car at 75 km/h. At what time will he overtake the thief?
(A) 4:30 PM  (B) 5:00 PM  (C) 5:30 PM  (D) 6:00 PM
Answer: (B) 5:00 PM
Solution: Head start of thief in 30 minutes = 60 * 0.5 = 30 km. Relative speed = 75 - 60 = 15 km/h. Time to overtake = 30 / 15 = 2 hours after 3:00 PM. Overtake time = 3:00 PM + 2 hours = 5:00 PM. The required value is 5:00 PM. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

140. (Very Hard) A train 150 meters long passes a platform 250 meters long in 20 seconds. How many seconds will it take to pass a person standing on the platform?
(A) 8.0 seconds  (B) 7.0 seconds  (C) 7.5 seconds  (D) 6.5 seconds
Answer: (C) 7.5 seconds
Solution: Total distance for platform = 150 + 250 = 400 meters. Speed of train = 400 / 20 = 20 m/s. Time taken to pass a stationary person = Train length / Speed = 150 / 20 = 7.5 seconds. The required value is 7.5 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (C).

Answer: (D) 50 km

(A) 120 meters  (B) 110 meters  (C) 130 meters  (D) 125 meters
Answer: (A) 120 meters
Solution: Relative speed in same direction = 56 - 29 = 27 km/h = 27 * (5/18) = 7.5 m/s. Total distance covered = 7.5 * 36 = 270 meters. Length of slower train = 270 - 150 = 120 meters. The required value is 120 meters. Hence, the correct option is (A).

143. (Very Hard) A boat can row 1 km upstream in 10 minutes and 1 km downstream in 6 minutes. What is the speed of the current in km/h?
(A) 2.5 km/h  (B) 2 km/h  (C) 1.5 km/h  (D) 3 km/h
Answer: (B) 2 km/h
Solution: Upstream speed U = 1 / (10/60) = 6 km/h. Downstream speed D = 1 / (6/60) = 10 km/h. Speed of current = (D - U) / 2 = (10 - 6) / 2 = 2 km/h. The required value is 2 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

144. (Very Hard) A man covers 1/3 of his journey at 25 km/h, 1/4 of his journey at 30 km/h, and the rest at 50 km/h. What is his average speed for the entire journey in km/h?
(A) 35 km/h  (B) 32 1/2 km/h  (C) 33 1/3 km/h  (D) 34 km/h
Answer: (C) 33 1/3 km/h
Solution: Let total distance = 600 km. Leg 1: 200 km at 25 km/h = 8 hours. Leg 2: 150 km at 30 km/h = 5 hours. Leg 3: 250 km at 50 km/h = 5 hours. Total time = 8 + 5 + 5 = 18 hours. Average speed = 600 / 18 = 100/3 = 33 1/3 km/h. The required value is 33 1/3 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

147. (Very Hard) A man goes downstream 60 km and upstream 40 km, taking 4 hours for each trip. What is the speed of the current in km/h?
(A) 2.0 km/h  (B) 2.5 km/h  (C) 3.0 km/h  (D) 1.5 km/h
Answer: (B) 2.5 km/h
Solution: Downstream speed D = 60 / 4 = 15 km/h. Upstream speed U = 40 / 4 = 10 km/h. Speed of current = (D - U) / 2 = (15 - 10) / 2 = 2.5 km/h. The required value is 2.5 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).

148. (Very Hard) A train leaves Station A at 7:00 AM at 50 km/h and another leaves Station B at 8:00 AM at 60 km/h towards each other. The distance between A and B is 380 km. At what time will they meet?
(A) 10:30 AM  (B) 11:30 AM  (C) 11:00 AM  (D) 12:00 PM
Answer: (C) 11:00 AM

(A) 50 km/h  (B) 48 km/h  (C) 45 km/h  (D) 52 km/h
Answer: (B) 48 km/h
Solution: Since both distance halves are equal (60 km each), Average Speed = (2 * 40 * 60) / (40 + 60) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h. The required value is 48 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
