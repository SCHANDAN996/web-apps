# Chapter 12: Time, Speed and Distance (Comprehensive Graduation Level Foundation)

## 1. Fundamental Principles and Unit Conversions
Time, Speed, and Distance (TSD) forms one of the most intellectually rigorous sections of quantitative examinations (SSC CGL Tier-2, IBPS PO, CDS, State PCS). The entire domain rests on the kinematics identity:
$$\text{Distance} (D) = \text{Speed} (S) \times \text{Time} (T)$$

### Metric Conversions:
- To convert kilometers per hour (km/h) to meters per second (m/s):
  $$1 \text{ km/h} = \frac{1000 \text{ m}}{3600 \text{ s}} = \frac{5}{18} \text{ m/s}$$
- To convert meters per second (m/s) to kilometers per hour (km/h):
  $$1 \text{ m/s} = \frac{18}{5} \text{ km/h}$$

### Invariance and Proportionality Laws:
1. **Constant Distance ($D$ is constant):** Speed and Time are strictly inversely proportional:
   $$S_1 \cdot T_1 = S_2 \cdot T_2 \implies \frac{S_1}{S_2} = \frac{T_2}{T_1}$$
2. **Constant Time ($T$ is constant):** Distance and Speed are directly proportional:
   $$\frac{D_1}{D_2} = \frac{S_1}{S_2}$$
3. **Constant Speed ($S$ is constant):** Distance and Time are directly proportional:
   $$\frac{D_1}{D_2} = \frac{T_1}{T_2}$$

---

## 2. Average Speed Mechanics
Average speed is defined as total distance traversed divided by total elapsed time. It is never the simple arithmetic mean of speeds unless the time intervals are identical.
$$\text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} = \frac{D_1 + D_2 + \dots + D_n}{T_1 + T_2 + \dots + T_n}$$

### Special Scenarios:
- **Two Equal Distance Segments ($D_1 = D_2$):** Harmonic mean of speeds:
  $$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 \cdot S_1 \cdot S_2}{S_1 + S_2}$$
- **Three Equal Distance Segments ($D_1 = D_2 = D_3$):**
  $$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{3 \cdot S_1 \cdot S_2 \cdot S_3}{S_1 S_2 + S_2 S_3 + S_3 S_1}$$
- **Equal Time Intervals ($T_1 = T_2$):** Arithmetic mean of speeds:
  $$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{S_1 + S_2}{2}$$

---

## 3. Early and Late Phenomena (Difference of Times)
When a journey between two fixed points is made at two different speeds resulting in early or late arrivals:
$$\text{Distance} = \frac{S_1 \cdot S_2}{|S_1 - S_2|} \times \Delta T$$
Where $\Delta T$ is the difference in arrival times:
- If one journey is $t_1$ late and the other is $t_2$ early: $\Delta T = t_1 + t_2$.
- If both journeys are late (or both early): $\Delta T = |t_1 - t_2|$.
- Ensure $\Delta T$ is converted into hours when speeds are in km/h.

---

## 4. Relative Speed Dynamics
Relative speed represents the rate at which the separation distance between two moving entities changes.
- **Opposite Directions (Approaching or Moving Apart):**
  $$S_{\text{rel}} = S_1 + S_2$$
- **Same Direction (Chasing or Overtaking):**
  $$S_{\text{rel}} = |S_1 - S_2|$$
- **Time to Meet or Overtake:**
  $$T = \frac{\text{Initial Separation Distance}}{S_{\text{rel}}}$$

---

## 5. Problems on Trains
A train is a body of significant physical length.
1. **Crossing a Stationary Point Object (Pole, Standing Person, Signal Post):**
   - Distance traversed = Length of train ($L_T$).
   - Time taken = $\frac{L_T}{S_T}$.
2. **Crossing a Stationary Extended Object (Platform, Bridge, Tunnel):**
   - Distance traversed = Length of train ($L_T$) + Length of platform ($L_P$).
   - Time taken = $\frac{L_T + L_P}{S_T}$.
3. **Crossing a Moving Person/Vehicle:**
   - Distance traversed = $L_T$.
   - Relative speed = $S_T \pm S_P$ (depending on relative direction).
4. **Two Trains Crossing Each Other:**
   - Distance traversed = $L_1 + L_2$.
   - Opposite directions: $T = \frac{L_1 + L_2}{S_1 + S_2}$.
   - Same direction: $T = \frac{L_1 + L_2}{|S_1 - S_2|}$.
5. **Person Inside Another Moving Train:**
   - The distance considered is purely the length of the passing train ($L_{\text{passing}}$), not the sum of both trains!

## 6. Boats and Streams (River Kinematics)
Let $u$ be the speed of the boat in still water and $v$ be the speed of the stream/current.
- **Downstream Speed ($D$):** Flow assists motion:
  $$D = u + v$$
- **Upstream Speed ($U$):** Flow opposes motion:
  $$U = u - v$$
- **Speed of Boat in Still Water:**
  $$u = \frac{D + U}{2}$$
- **Speed of Stream / Current:**
  $$v = \frac{D - U}{2}$$
- **Equal Distance Round Trip Time Ratio:**
  $$\frac{u}{v} = \frac{T_U + T_D}{T_U - T_D}$$

---

## 7. The Post-Meeting Travel Theorem
Two bodies start simultaneously from points X and Y toward each other. After meeting at an intermediate point, body 1 takes $t_1$ hours to reach Y, and body 2 takes $t_2$ hours to reach X.
$$\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{t_2}{t_1}}$$
- Time of meeting from start: $t_{\text{meet}} = \sqrt{t_1 \cdot t_2}$.
- Total distance between X and Y: $D = (S_1 + S_2) \cdot \sqrt{t_1 \cdot t_2}$.

---

## 8. Races and Linear Contests
In a race of length $L$:
- **Head Start in Distance:** "A gives B a start of $x$ meters": B starts $x$ meters ahead of the starting line. When A runs $L$ meters, B needs to run only $(L - x)$ meters.
- **Head Start in Time:** "A gives B a start of $t$ seconds": B starts running $t$ seconds before A.
- **Beating in Distance and Time:** "A beats B by $x$ meters or $t$ seconds": B was $x$ meters behind the finish line when A finished, and B would have taken $t$ seconds to cover those $x$ meters:
  $$\text{Speed of B} = \frac{x}{t}$$

---

## 9. Circular Tracks and Pursuit
For a circular track of circumference $C$, with runners speeds $S_1$ and $S_2$:
1. **Time for First Meeting Anywhere on the Track:**
   $$T = \frac{C}{S_{\text{rel}}}$$
2. **Time for First Meeting at the Exact Starting Point:**
   $$T_{\text{start}} = \text{LCM}\left(\frac{C}{S_1}, \frac{C}{S_2}\right)$$
3. **Number of Distinct Meeting Points on the Track:**
   Express $S_1 : S_2 = a : b$ in lowest irreducible form:
   - Opposite directions: $(a + b)$ distinct points.
   - Same direction: $|a - b|$ distinct points.

---

## 10. Stoppage and Rest Dynamics
When a transit vehicle stops along its journey:
$$\text{Stoppage Time per Hour} = \frac{\text{Speed excluding stoppages} - \text{Speed including stoppages}}{\text{Speed excluding stoppages}} \times 60 \text{ minutes}$$
