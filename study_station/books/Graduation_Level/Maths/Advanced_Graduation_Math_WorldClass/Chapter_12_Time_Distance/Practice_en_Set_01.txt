Questions 1–25: Easy

1. (Easy) A car travels at a speed of 72 km/h. What is its speed in meters per second?
(A) 20 m/s  (B) 25 m/s  (C) 18 m/s  (D) 15 m/s
Answer: (A) 20 m/s
Solution: To convert speed from km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18. Speed = 72 * (5/18) = 4 * 5 = 20 m/s. The required value is 20 m/s. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

2. (Easy) A cyclist covers a distance of 750 meters in 2 minutes 30 seconds. What is the speed of the cyclist in km/h?
(A) 15 km/h  (B) 18 km/h  (C) 20 km/h  (D) 16 km/h
Answer: (B) 18 km/h
Solution: Total time in seconds = 2 * 60 + 30 = 150 seconds. Speed in m/s = 750 / 150 = 5 m/s. Converting to km/h: 5 * (18/5) = 18 km/h. The required value is 18 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

3. (Easy) A person drives from City A to City B at 60 km/h and returns along the same route at 40 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire round trip?
(A) 50 km/h  (B) 45 km/h  (C) 48 km/h  (D) 52 km/h
Answer: (C) 48 km/h
Solution: For equal distances covered at speeds x and y, Average Speed = (2xy) / (x + y). Average Speed = (2 * 60 * 40) / (60 + 40) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h. The required value is 48 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

4. (Easy) The speeds of two cars are in the ratio 4 : 5. If the first car takes 45 minutes to cover a certain distance, how many minutes will the second car take to cover the same distance?
(A) 35 minutes  (B) 40 minutes  (C) 32 minutes  (D) 36 minutes
Answer: (D) 36 minutes
Solution: Since distance is constant, time is inversely proportional to speed: T1 : T2 = S2 : S1 = 5 : 4. 5 units correspond to 45 minutes, so 1 unit = 9 minutes. Therefore, time for the second car = 4 * 9 = 36 minutes. The required value is 36 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

5. (Easy) A train 240 meters long is running at a speed of 72 km/h. In how many seconds will it pass a telephone pole?
(A) 12 seconds  (B) 10 seconds  (C) 15 seconds  (D) 14 seconds
Answer: (A) 12 seconds
Solution: Speed in m/s = 72 * (5/18) = 20 m/s. To pass a pole, the train covers its own length: Distance = 240 m. Time = 240 / 20 = 12 seconds. The required value is 12 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

6. (Easy) A train 180 meters long is traveling at a speed of 54 km/h. In how many seconds will it pass a railway platform of length 270 meters?
(A) 25 seconds  (B) 30 seconds  (C) 35 seconds  (D) 28 seconds
Answer: (B) 30 seconds
Solution: Speed = 54 * (5/18) = 15 m/s. Total distance to cross platform = Train length + Platform length = 180 + 270 = 450 meters. Time = 450 / 15 = 30 seconds. The required value is 30 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

7. (Easy) A train running at uniform speed passes a 120-meter long platform in 10 seconds and a telegraph post in 6 seconds. What is the length of the train?
(A) 150 meters  (B) 200 meters  (C) 180 meters  (D) 160 meters
Answer: (C) 180 meters
Solution: Time difference to cover the 120 m platform = 10 - 6 = 4 seconds. Speed of train = 120 / 4 = 30 m/s. Length of train = Speed * Time to cross post = 30 * 6 = 180 meters. The required value is 180 meters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

8. (Easy) Two runners start from the same point at the same time and run in opposite directions at speeds of 6 m/s and 9 m/s. After how many seconds will they be 450 meters apart?
(A) 25 seconds  (B) 35 seconds  (C) 28 seconds  (D) 30 seconds
Answer: (D) 30 seconds
Solution: Since they run in opposite directions, relative speed = 6 + 9 = 15 m/s. Time to reach 450 m separation = 450 / 15 = 30 seconds. The required value is 30 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

9. (Easy) A thief is spotted by a policeman from a distance of 200 meters. The thief starts running and the policeman chases him. The thief and policeman run at speeds of 10 km/h and 11 km/h respectively. What is the distance between them after 6 minutes?
(A) 100 meters  (B) 120 meters  (C) 80 meters  (D) 150 meters
Answer: (A) 100 meters
Solution: Relative speed of policeman = 11 - 10 = 1 km/h = 1000 m / 60 min. In 6 minutes, the policeman gains (1000 / 60) * 6 = 100 meters. Remaining distance between them = 200 - 100 = 100 meters. The required value is 100 meters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

10. (Easy) A boat can travel with a speed of 13 km/h in still water. If the speed of the stream is 4 km/h, find the time taken by the boat to go 68 km downstream.
(A) 5 hours  (B) 4 hours  (C) 3.5 hours  (D) 4.5 hours
Answer: (B) 4 hours
Solution: Downstream speed = Speed in still water + Stream speed = 13 + 4 = 17 km/h. Time taken = Distance / Downstream speed = 68 / 17 = 4 hours. The required value is 4 hours. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

11. (Easy) A boat goes 40 km upstream in 8 hours and 36 km downstream in 6 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
(A) 6.0 km/h  (B) 5.0 km/h  (C) 5.5 km/h  (D) 6.5 km/h
Answer: (C) 5.5 km/h
Solution: Upstream speed U = 40 / 8 = 5 km/h. Downstream speed D = 36 / 6 = 6 km/h. Speed of boat in still water = (D + U) / 2 = (6 + 5) / 2 = 5.5 km/h. The required value is 5.5 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

12. (Easy) A man can row downstream at 14 km/h and upstream at 8 km/h. What is the speed of the stream?
(A) 4 km/h  (B) 2.5 km/h  (C) 3.5 km/h  (D) 3 km/h
Answer: (D) 3 km/h
Solution: Speed of the stream = (Downstream speed - Upstream speed) / 2 = (14 - 8) / 2 = 6 / 2 = 3 km/h. The required value is 3 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

13. (Easy) Walking at 3 km/h, a student reaches school 15 minutes late. Walking at 4 km/h, he reaches 15 minutes early. What is the distance from his house to school?
(A) 6 km  (B) 5 km  (C) 7 km  (D) 8 km
Answer: (A) 6 km
Solution: Time difference Delta T = 15 + 15 = 30 minutes = 0.5 hours. Distance = (S1 * S2) / |S1 - S2| * Delta T = (3 * 4) / (4 - 3) * 0.5 = 12 * 0.5 = 6 km. The required value is 6 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

14. (Easy) Excluding stoppages, the speed of a bus is 54 km/h, and including stoppages, it is 45 km/h. For how many minutes does the bus stop per hour?
(A) 12 minutes  (B) 10 minutes  (C) 8 minutes  (D) 15 minutes
Answer: (B) 10 minutes
Solution: Stoppage time per hour = [(Speed without stops - Speed with stops) / Speed without stops] * 60 = [(54 - 45) / 54] * 60 = (9 / 54) * 60 = 10 minutes. The required value is 10 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

15. (Easy) Walking at 3/4 of his usual speed, a man reaches his office 20 minutes later than usual. What is his usual time to reach the office?
(A) 50 minutes  (B) 45 minutes  (C) 60 minutes  (D) 80 minutes
Answer: (C) 60 minutes
Solution: Speed ratio = 3/4 => Time ratio = 4/3. The extra time is 4/3 - 1 = 1/3 of usual time. Given (1/3) * T = 20 minutes, usual time T = 20 * 3 = 60 minutes (1 hour). The required value is 60 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

16. (Easy) A man travels for 5 hours. He covers the first half of the journey at 21 km/h and the second half at 24 km/h. What is the total distance covered?
(A) 108 km  (B) 115 km  (C) 120 km  (D) 112 km
Answer: (D) 112 km
Solution: Let half the distance be d km. Equation: d/21 + d/24 = 5 => (8d + 7d)/168 = 5 => 15d / 168 = 5 => 3d / 168 = 1 => d = 56 km. Total distance = 2d = 112 km. The required value is 112 km. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

17. (Easy) Two trains of lengths 130 meters and 120 meters are running on parallel lines in opposite directions at 42 km/h and 30 km/h. In what time will they pass each other?
(A) 12.5 seconds  (B) 12.0 seconds  (C) 13.0 seconds  (D) 11.5 seconds
Answer: (A) 12.5 seconds
Solution: Total distance to cross = 130 + 120 = 250 meters. Relative speed in opposite direction = 42 + 30 = 72 km/h = 72 * (5/18) = 20 m/s. Time = 250 / 20 = 12.5 seconds. The required value is 12.5 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

18. (Easy) A train running at 90 km/h crosses a platform in 36 seconds. If the length of the train is 300 meters, what is the length of the platform?
(A) 550 meters  (B) 600 meters  (C) 650 meters  (D) 500 meters
Answer: (B) 600 meters
Solution: Speed = 90 * (5/18) = 25 m/s. Total distance covered in 36 s = 25 * 36 = 900 meters. Length of platform = 900 - Length of train = 900 - 300 = 600 meters. The required value is 600 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

19. (Easy) An airplane covers a certain distance at a speed of 240 km/h in 5 hours. To cover the same distance in 1 2/3 hours, at what speed must it travel?
(A) 700 km/h  (B) 750 km/h  (C) 720 km/h  (D) 680 km/h
Answer: (C) 720 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 240 * 5 = 1200 km. Required time = 1 2/3 = 5/3 hours. Required speed = 1200 / (5/3) = 1200 * 3 / 5 = 720 km/h. The required value is 720 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

20. (Easy) A car covers four successive three-kilometer stretches at speeds of 10 km/h, 20 km/h, 30 km/h and 60 km/h respectively. What is its average speed over this distance?
(A) 25 km/h  (B) 18 km/h  (C) 24 km/h  (D) 20 km/h
Answer: (D) 20 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 4 * 3 = 12 km. Total time = 3/10 + 3/20 + 3/30 + 3/60 = 3 * (6 + 3 + 2 + 1) / 60 = 3 * 12 / 60 = 36 / 60 = 0.6 hours. Average speed = 12 / 0.6 = 20 km/h. The required value is 20 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

21. (Easy) Two men A and B start walking towards each other from two points 36 km apart at speeds of 4 km/h and 5 km/h respectively. After how many hours will they meet?
(A) 4 hours  (B) 3.5 hours  (C) 4.5 hours  (D) 5 hours
Answer: (A) 4 hours
Solution: Relative speed of approach = 4 + 5 = 9 km/h. Distance = 36 km. Time taken to meet = 36 / 9 = 4 hours. The required value is 4 hours. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

22. (Easy) A train 150 meters long is traveling at 68 km/h. How many seconds will it take to pass a man who is running at 8 km/h in the same direction?
(A) 8 seconds  (B) 9 seconds  (C) 10 seconds  (D) 12 seconds
Answer: (B) 9 seconds
Solution: Relative speed in same direction = 68 - 8 = 60 km/h = 60 * (5/18) = 50/3 m/s. Distance to cross = 150 m. Time = 150 / (50/3) = 150 * 3 / 50 = 9 seconds. The required value is 9 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

23. (Easy) In a 100-meter race, Runner A beats Runner B by 20 meters. If Runner A finishes the race in 10 seconds, what is Runner B's speed in m/s?
(A) 7.5 m/s  (B) 8.5 m/s  (C) 8 m/s  (D) 9 m/s
Answer: (C) 8 m/s
Solution: When Runner A runs 100 m in 10 s, Runner B runs 100 - 20 = 80 m in the same 10 s. Speed of Runner B = 80 / 10 = 8 m/s. The required value is 8 m/s. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

25. (Easy) A man can walk a certain distance in 6 hours. If he decreases his speed by 1 km/h, he takes 9 hours to cover the same distance. What is his original walking speed?
(A) 4 km/h  (B) 3 km/h  (C) 2.5 km/h  (D) 3.5 km/h
Answer: (B) 3 km/h
Solution: Let original speed be s km/h. Distance = s * 6 = (s - 1) * 9 => 6s = 9s - 9 => 3s = 9 => s = 3 km/h. The required value is 3 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
