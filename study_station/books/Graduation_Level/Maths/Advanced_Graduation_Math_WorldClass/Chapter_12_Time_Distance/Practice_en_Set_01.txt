1. A train covers 240 km in 4 hours. What is its speed?
(a) 60 km/h (b) 50 km/h (c) 70 km/h (d) 80 km/h
Answer: (a)
Solution: Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 240 ÷ 4 = 60 km/h. The tempting option 50 km/h comes from dividing 240 by 4.8 or misreading the distance, so the answer is 60 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

3. Convert 72 km/h into m/s.

14. A car covers the first 100 km at 50 km/h and the next 100 km at 100 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?
(a) 80 km/h (b) 70 km/h (c) 75 km/h (d) 66.67 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total distance = 200 km. Time = 100/50 + 100/100 = 2 + 1 = 3 hours. Average speed = 200/3 ≈ 66.67 km/h. The big trap is 75 km/h, the simple average of the two speeds, which is wrong because times differ, so the answer is 66.67 km/h.
Source: PYQ-style

15. A man travels 240 km. He covers half the distance at 40 km/h and the remaining half at 60 km/h. What is his average speed?
(a) 45 km/h (b) 52 km/h (c) 50 km/h (d) 48 km/h
Answer: (d)
Solution: For equal distances, average speed = 2xy/(x+y) = 2×40×60/(40+60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h. The trap is 50 km/h, the arithmetic mean of the speeds, so the answer is 48 km/h.

Answer: (d)

23. A man covers a distance in 6 hours when travelling at 40 km/h. If he increases his speed by 20%, how much time will he save?
(a) 30 minutes (b) 45 minutes (c) 1 hour (d) 1.5 hours
Answer: (c)
Solution: Distance = 40 × 6 = 240 km. New speed = 40 × 1.2 = 48 km/h. New time = 240 ÷ 48 = 5 hours. Time saved = 6 − 5 = 1 hour. The trap is 1.5 hours, from assuming time falls by 20% of 6 hours plus extra, so the answer is 1 hour.
Source: PYQ-style

24. A and B start together from the same point and walk in the same direction at 5 km/h and 8 km/h. After how many hours will B be 12 km ahead of A?
(a) 4 hours (b) 3 hours (c) 5 hours (d) 6 hours
Answer: (a)
Solution: Relative speed = 8 − 5 = 3 km/h. Time = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 hours. The trap is 3 hours, from dividing 12 by 4 (a wrong speed gap), so the answer is 4 hours.
