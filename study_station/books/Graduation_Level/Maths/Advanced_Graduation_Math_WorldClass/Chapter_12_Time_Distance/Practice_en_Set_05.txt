Questions 101–125: Hard

101. (Hard) Two guns are fired from the same place at an interval of 6 minutes, but a person in a train approaching the place hears the second sound 5 minutes 52 seconds after the first. If the speed of sound is 330 m/s, what is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 27 km/h  (B) 25 km/h  (C) 30 km/h  (D) 24 km/h
Answer: (A) 27 km/h
Solution: Time difference = 6 min - 5 min 52 s = 8 seconds. The distance covered by sound in 8 seconds is covered by the train in 5 min 52 s = 352 seconds. Distance = 330 * 8 = 2640 meters. Speed of train = 2640 / 352 = 7.5 m/s. Converting to km/h: 7.5 * (18/5) = 27 km/h. The required value is 27 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

102. (Hard) A person can walk up a stalled escalator in 90 seconds. When standing on the same moving escalator, he is carried up in 60 seconds. How many seconds would it take him to walk up the moving escalator?
(A) 30 seconds  (B) 36 seconds  (C) 40 seconds  (D) 45 seconds
Answer: (B) 36 seconds
Solution: Let length of escalator be L. Walking speed = L / 90. Escalator speed = L / 60. Combined speed when walking up moving escalator = L / 90 + L / 60 = 5L / 180 = L / 36. Time taken = 36 seconds. The required value is 36 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

103. (Hard) A train after traveling 120 km meets with a mechanical fault and its speed drops to 3/4 of its original speed, causing it to arrive at its destination 2 hours late. Had the fault occurred 90 km further on, it would have arrived only 1.5 hours late. What is the original speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 50 km/h  (B) 72 km/h  (C) 60 km/h  (D) 65 km/h
Answer: (C) 60 km/h
Solution: For the 90 km stretch, time saved = 2 - 1.5 = 0.5 hours. At 3/4 speed, the extra time taken is 1/3 of the normal time for 90 km: (1/3) * (90 / S) = 0.5 => 30 / S = 0.5 => S = 60 km/h. The required value is 60 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

104. (Hard) Three runners A, B, and C run in the same direction on a circular track of circumference 1200 meters with speeds of 6 m/s, 4 m/s, and 2 m/s respectively from the same point simultaneously. After how many seconds will all three runners meet together for the first time at the starting point?
(A) 400 seconds  (B) 500 seconds  (C) 800 seconds  (D) 600 seconds
Answer: (D) 600 seconds
Solution: Time taken by each runner for one complete lap: T_A = 1200 / 6 = 200 s, T_B = 1200 / 4 = 300 s, T_C = 1200 / 2 = 600 s. Time of first meeting at starting point = LCM(200, 300, 600) = 600 seconds (10 minutes). The required value is 600 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: CDS Pattern

105. (Hard) A man rows 24 km upstream and 36 km downstream in 6 hours. Also, he rows 36 km upstream and 24 km downstream in 6.5 hours. What is the speed of the current in km/h?
(A) 2 km/h  (B) 3 km/h  (C) 2.5 km/h  (D) 1.5 km/h
Answer: (A) 2 km/h
Solution: Equations: 24/U + 36/D = 6 and 36/U + 24/D = 6.5. Solving gives U = 8 km/h and D = 12 km/h. Speed of current = (D - U) / 2 = (12 - 8) / 2 = 2 km/h. The required value is 2 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

106. (Hard) Two trains of lengths 160 meters and 140 meters are running towards each other on parallel tracks at 77 km/h and 67 km/h respectively. In how many seconds will they completely cross each other?
(A) 8.0 seconds  (B) 7.5 seconds  (C) 7.0 seconds  (D) 8.5 seconds
Answer: (B) 7.5 seconds
Solution: Total distance = 160 + 140 = 300 meters. Relative speed in opposite direction = 77 + 67 = 144 km/h = 144 * (5/18) = 40 m/s. Time to cross = 300 / 40 = 7.5 seconds. The required value is 7.5 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: State PCS Pattern

107. (Hard) A man walking at 5 km/h notices that a 225-meter long train coming from behind passes him completely in 9 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 90 km/h  (B) 100 km/h  (C) 95 km/h  (D) 85 km/h
Answer: (C) 95 km/h
Solution: Relative speed in same direction = 225 / 9 = 25 m/s = 25 * (18/5) = 90 km/h. Speed of train = Relative speed + Man speed = 90 + 5 = 95 km/h. The required value is 95 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

108. (Hard) A dog sees a hare 100 meters away and starts chasing it. The hare runs at 8 m/s and the dog runs at 10 m/s. But every 10 seconds, the dog stops for 2 seconds. How many seconds after the chase began will the dog catch the hare?
(A) 240 seconds  (B) 260 seconds  (C) 245 seconds  (D) 250 seconds
Answer: (D) 250 seconds
Solution: In one 12-second cycle (10 s running, 2 s resting): Dog covers 10 * 10 = 100 m; Hare covers 12 * 8 = 96 m. Net gain of dog per cycle = 100 - 96 = 4 meters. After 20 cycles (240 seconds), net gain = 20 * 4 = 80 meters. Remaining distance = 100 - 80 = 20 meters. In the 21st cycle, relative speed during running = 10 - 8 = 2 m/s. Time to bridge 20 m = 20 / 2 = 10 seconds. Total time = 240 + 10 = 250 seconds. The required value is 250 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

109. (Hard) A man travels 180 km in 4 hours, partly by car at 60 km/h and partly by train at 40 km/h. How much distance in km did he actually cover by train?
(A) 120 km  (B) 100 km  (C) 140 km  (D) 110 km
Answer: (A) 120 km
Solution: Let time by car be t1 and by train be t2. Then t1 + t2 = 4 and 60 t1 + 40 t2 = 180 => 3 t1 + 2 t2 = 9. Since 2 t1 + 2 t2 = 8, subtracting yields t1 = 1 hour, t2 = 3 hours. Distance by train = 40 * 3 = 120 km. The required value is 120 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

110. (Hard) In a 400-meter race, Runner A beats Runner B by 20 meters and beats Runner C by 40 meters. If Runner B and Runner C run a race of 380 meters, by how many meters will Runner B beat Runner C?
(A) 18 meters  (B) 20 meters  (C) 22 meters  (D) 25 meters
Answer: (B) 20 meters
Solution: When A runs 400 m, B runs 380 m and C runs 360 m. Therefore, when B runs 380 meters, C runs 360 meters. In a 380-meter race, Runner B beats Runner C by 380 - 360 = 20 meters. The required value is 20 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

111. (Hard) Two trains X and Y start from two points A and B at the same time and travel towards each other. After passing each other, they take 9 hours and 16 hours to reach B and A respectively. What is the ratio of the speed of train X to that of train Y?
(A) 3 : 4  (B) 16 : 9  (C) 4 : 3  (D) 9 : 16
Answer: (C) 4 : 3
Solution: Using the post-meeting theorem: S_X / S_Y = sqrt(t2 / t1) = sqrt(16 / 9) = 4 / 3 = 4 : 3. The required value is 4 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

112. (Hard) A person can row 5 km/h in still water. If the velocity of current is 1 km/h and it takes him 1 hour to row to a place and come back, how far is the place?
(A) 2.5 km  (B) 2.0 km  (C) 2.8 km  (D) 2.4 km
Answer: (D) 2.4 km
Solution: Upstream speed U = 5 - 1 = 4 km/h. Downstream speed D = 5 + 1 = 6 km/h. Total round trip time = d/4 + d/6 = 1 => 5d / 12 = 1 => d = 12 / 5 = 2.4 km. The required value is 2.4 km. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: State PCS Pattern

113. (Hard) A train traveling at 48 km/h crosses another train, having half its length and traveling in opposite direction at 42 km/h, in 12 seconds. It also passes a railway platform in 45 seconds. What is the length of the platform?
(A) 400 meters  (B) 450 meters  (C) 350 meters  (D) 380 meters
Answer: (A) 400 meters
Solution: Relative speed in opposite direction = 48 + 42 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Sum of lengths = L + 0.5L = 1.5L = 25 * 12 = 300 m => L = 200 m (first train length). Speed of first train = 48 * (5/18) = 40/3 m/s. Distance covered across platform in 45 s = (40/3) * 45 = 600 m. Platform length = 600 - 200 = 400 meters. The required value is 400 meters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

114. (Hard) A cyclist moves around a circular track of radius 140 meters. If he completes one full round in 44 seconds, what is his speed in km/h? (Use pi = 22/7)
(A) 65 km/h  (B) 72 km/h  (C) 75 km/h  (D) 68 km/h
Answer: (B) 72 km/h
Solution: Circumference of circular track = 2 * (22/7) * 140 = 880 meters. Speed in m/s = 880 / 44 = 20 m/s. Converting to km/h: 20 * (18/5) = 72 km/h. The required value is 72 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: CDS Pattern

115. (Hard) A man covers 300 km: 60 km by train and the rest by car in 4 hours. If he travels 100 km by train and the rest by car, he takes 4 hours 10 minutes. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 50 km/h  (B) 70 km/h  (C) 60 km/h  (D) 65 km/h
Answer: (C) 60 km/h
Solution: Equations: 60/T + 240/C = 4 => 1/T + 4/C = 1/15. Also 100/T + 200/C = 25/6 => 1/T + 2/C = 1/24. Subtracting gives 2/C = 1/15 - 1/24 = 3/120 = 1/40 => C = 80 km/h. Substituting: 1/T + 4/80 = 1/15 => 1/T = 1/15 - 1/20 = 1/60 => T = 60 km/h. The required value is 60 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

116. (Hard) A train running at 50 km/h is 100 meters long. In what time in seconds will it pass a train 150 meters long running at 40 km/h in the same direction?
(A) 80 seconds  (B) 100 seconds  (C) 85 seconds  (D) 90 seconds
Answer: (D) 90 seconds
Solution: Total distance to cross = 100 + 150 = 250 meters. Relative speed in same direction = 50 - 40 = 10 km/h = 10 * (5/18) = 25/9 m/s. Time taken = 250 / (25/9) = 250 * 9 / 25 = 90 seconds. The required value is 90 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (D).

118. (Hard) Walking at 4 km/h, a person reaches his office 5 minutes late. If he walks at 5 km/h, he reaches 10 minutes early. What is the distance to his office in km?
(A) 4.5 km  (B) 5 km  (C) 6 km  (D) 5.5 km
Answer: (B) 5 km
Solution: Time difference Delta T = 5 + 10 = 15 minutes = 0.25 hours. Distance = (4 * 5) / (5 - 4) * 0.25 = 20 * 0.25 = 5 km. The required value is 5 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).

119. (Hard) A boat travels 16 km upstream in 4 hours and 24 km downstream in 3 hours. What is the speed of the current in km/h?
(A) 2.5 km/h  (B) 1.5 km/h  (C) 2 km/h  (D) 3 km/h
Answer: (C) 2 km/h
Solution: Upstream speed U = 16 / 4 = 4 km/h. Downstream speed D = 24 / 3 = 8 km/h. Speed of current = (D - U) / 2 = (8 - 4) / 2 = 2 km/h. The required value is 2 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

120. (Hard) A train passes a standing man in 10 seconds and passes a platform 260 meters long in 23 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
(A) 65 km/h  (B) 75 km/h  (C) 68 km/h  (D) 72 km/h
Answer: (D) 72 km/h
Solution: Speed = Platform length / (Platform time - Man time) = 260 / (23 - 10) = 260 / 13 = 20 m/s. Converting to km/h: 20 * (18/5) = 72 km/h. The required value is 72 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

121. (Hard) Two stations A and B are 450 km apart. A train starts from A at 4:00 PM towards B at 60 km/h. Another train starts from B at 3:20 PM towards A at 80 km/h. At what time will the two trains meet?
(A) 6:50 PM  (B) 7:00 PM  (C) 6:40 PM  (D) 7:10 PM
Answer: (A) 6:50 PM
Solution: From 3:20 to 4:00 PM (40 min = 2/3 hr), Train B covers 80 * (2/3) = 160/3 km. Remaining distance at 4:00 PM = 450 - 160/3 = 1190/3 km. Combined speed = 60 + 80 = 140 km/h. Time to meet after 4:00 PM = (1190/3) / 140 = 17/6 hours = 2 hours 50 minutes. Meeting time = 4:00 PM + 2 hr 50 min = 6:50 PM. The required value is 6:50 PM. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

122. (Hard) A thief is noticed by a policeman from a distance of 300 meters. The thief starts running and the policeman chases him. The thief and policeman run at speeds of 11 km/h and 13 km/h respectively. What is the distance between them after 3 minutes?
(A) 180 meters  (B) 200 meters  (C) 220 meters  (D) 250 meters
Answer: (B) 200 meters
Solution: Relative speed of pursuit = 13 - 11 = 2 km/h = 2000 m / 60 min. In 3 minutes, policeman gains (2000 / 60) * 3 = 100 meters. Distance between them after 3 minutes = 300 - 100 = 200 meters. The required value is 200 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

123. (Hard) A motor car does a journey in 10 hours, the first half at 21 km/h and the second half at 24 km/h. Find the total distance in km.
(A) 220 km  (B) 230 km  (C) 224 km  (D) 216 km
Answer: (C) 224 km
Solution: Let half the distance be d km. Equation: d/21 + d/24 = 10 => 15d / 168 = 10 => 3d / 168 = 2 => d = 112 km. Total distance = 2d = 224 km. The required value is 224 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: State PCS Pattern

124. (Hard) A boat moves downstream at the rate of 1 km in 5 minutes and upstream at the rate of 1 km in 10 minutes. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
(A) 8 km/h  (B) 10 km/h  (C) 7.5 km/h  (D) 9 km/h
Answer: (D) 9 km/h
Solution: Downstream speed D = 1 / (5/60) = 12 km/h. Upstream speed U = 1 / (10/60) = 6 km/h. Speed of boat in still water = (D + U) / 2 = (12 + 6) / 2 = 9 km/h. The required value is 9 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

125. (Hard) A train 108 meters long moving at a speed of 50 km/h crosses another train 112 meters long coming from the opposite direction in 6 seconds. What is the speed of the second train in km/h?
(A) 80 km/h  (B) 82 km/h  (C) 85 km/h  (D) 78 km/h
Answer: (B) 82 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 108 + 112 = 220 meters. Relative speed in opposite direction = 220 / 6 = 110/3 m/s = (110/3) * (18/5) = 132 km/h. Speed of second train = 132 - 50 = 82 km/h. The required value is 82 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
