Questions 76–100: Hard

76. (Hard) A train 150 meters long moving at 45 km/h overtakes another train 100 meters long moving in the same direction at 36 km/h. How many seconds does a passenger sitting in the first train observe the second train passing by?
(A) 40 seconds  (B) 100 seconds  (C) 50 seconds  (D) 60 seconds
Answer: (A) 40 seconds
Solution: The passenger in the first train is a point observer. Therefore, the distance to be covered is purely the length of the second train = 100 meters. Relative speed in same direction = 45 - 36 = 9 km/h = 9 * (5/18) = 2.5 m/s. Time taken = 100 / 2.5 = 40 seconds. The required value is 40 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

77. (Hard) An escalator moves downwards at a constant speed. A boy walks down the escalator taking 50 steps to reach the bottom. When he runs down at 3 times his walking speed, he takes 75 steps to reach the bottom. How many steps would he take if the escalator was stationary?
(A) 90 steps  (B) 100 steps  (C) 120 steps  (D) 80 steps
Answer: (B) 100 steps
Solution: Let walking speed = 1 step/s, running speed = 3 steps/s, and escalator speed = e steps/s. Walking time = 50 / 1 = 50 s => Total escalator length = 50 + 50e. Running time = 75 / 3 = 25 s => Total escalator length = 75 + 25e. Equating: 50 + 50e = 75 + 25e => 25e = 25 => e = 1 step/s. Total steps on stationary escalator = 50 + 50(1) = 100 steps. The required value is 100 steps. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

78. (Hard) A boat travels upstream from P to Q and downstream from Q to P in 3 hours. If the speed of the boat in still water is 9 km/h and the speed of the current is 3 km/h, what is the distance between P and Q?
(A) 10 km  (B) 15 km  (C) 12 km  (D) 14 km
Answer: (C) 12 km
Solution: Upstream speed U = 9 - 3 = 6 km/h. Downstream speed D = 9 + 3 = 12 km/h. Let distance be d. Equation: d/6 + d/12 = 3 => (2d + d)/12 = 3 => 3d = 36 => d = 12 km. The required value is 12 km. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

79. (Hard) Two runners A and B run on a circular track of length 1200 meters from the same starting point in opposite directions with speeds of 18 km/h and 27 km/h. At how many distinct points on the track will they cross each other?
(A) 6 points  (B) 4 points  (C) 3 points  (D) 5 points
Answer: (D) 5 points
Solution: Ratio of speeds in lowest terms: 18 : 27 = 2 : 3. For motion in opposite directions on a circular ring, the number of distinct meeting points is a + b = 2 + 3 = 5 distinct points. The required value is 5 points. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: CDS Pattern

80. (Hard) A and B run a 1 km race. If A gives B a head start of 50 meters, A wins by 14 seconds. But if A gives B a head start of 22 seconds, B wins by 20 meters. What is the time taken by A to run 1 km?
(A) 100 seconds  (B) 120 seconds  (C) 90 seconds  (D) 110 seconds
Answer: (A) 100 seconds
Solution: Let time for A be T_A. In Case 1: B covers 950 m in (T_A + 14) s => 1/S_B = (T_A + 14)/950. In Case 2: B covers 1000 m while A runs 980 m in 0.98 T_A, so B runs for (0.98 T_A + 22) s. Equating 1000/S_B: 1000 * (T_A + 14)/950 = 0.98 T_A + 22 => (20/19)(T_A + 14) = 0.98 T_A + 22 => 20 T_A + 280 = 18.62 T_A + 418 => 1.38 T_A = 138 => T_A = 100 seconds. The required value is 100 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

81. (Hard) Two trains start simultaneously from stations L and M towards each other. They meet after 4 hours. The train from L continues to M and reaches in 5 hours after meeting. How many hours after meeting will the train from M take to reach L?
(A) 3.5 hours  (B) 3.2 hours  (C) 3.0 hours  (D) 4.0 hours
Answer: (B) 3.2 hours
Solution: Using the post-meeting relation t_meet = sqrt(t1 * t2), where t_meet = 4 hours and t1 = 5 hours: 4 = sqrt(5 * t2) => 16 = 5 * t2 => t2 = 16 / 5 = 3.2 hours (3 hours 12 minutes). The required value is 3.2 hours. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

82. (Hard) A car travels from A to B at 60 km/h and returns from B to A at a different speed. The average speed for the whole journey is 48 km/h. What was the return speed from B to A?
(A) 42 km/h  (B) 36 km/h  (C) 40 km/h  (D) 45 km/h
Answer: (C) 40 km/h
Solution: Average speed = 2 * S1 * S2 / (S1 + S2). 48 = (2 * 60 * S2) / (60 + S2) => 48(60 + S2) = 120 S2 => 2880 + 48 S2 = 120 S2 => 72 S2 = 2880 => S2 = 40 km/h. The required value is 40 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

83. (Hard) A train 200 meters long moving at 60 km/h crosses a platform in 30 seconds. A man running on the platform in the direction of the train at 6 km/h will be passed by the train in how many seconds?
(A) 12 1/2 seconds  (B) 14 seconds  (C) 15 seconds  (D) 13 1/3 seconds
Answer: (D) 13 1/3 seconds
Solution: Relative speed with respect to the running man = 60 - 6 = 54 km/h = 54 * (5/18) = 15 m/s. The distance to pass the man is the length of the train = 200 meters. Time taken = 200 / 15 = 40/3 = 13 1/3 seconds. The required value is 13 1/3 seconds. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

84. (Hard) A steamer goes downstream from port A to port B in 4 hours and upstream from B to A in 5 hours. If the stream flows at 2 km/h, what is the distance between port A and port B?
(A) 80 km  (B) 75 km  (C) 85 km  (D) 90 km
Answer: (A) 80 km
Solution: Let speed in still water be u. Downstream distance = (u + 2) * 4. Upstream distance = (u - 2) * 5. Equating distances: 4u + 8 = 5u - 10 => u = 18 km/h. Distance = (18 + 2) * 4 = 80 km. The required value is 80 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: State PCS Pattern

85. (Hard) Two runners start together from the same point on a 400-meter circular track running in the same direction at 8 m/s and 5 m/s. When the faster runner completes 4 laps, how many linear meters behind is the slower runner?
(A) 500 meters  (B) 600 meters  (C) 400 meters  (D) 700 meters
Answer: (B) 600 meters
Solution: Time taken by faster runner to complete 4 laps (1600 m) = 1600 / 8 = 200 seconds. In 200 seconds, the slower runner covers 200 * 5 = 1000 meters. Linear distance behind = 1600 - 1000 = 600 meters. The required value is 600 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: CDS Pattern

86. (Hard) A train leaves station A at 5:00 AM and reaches station B at 9:00 AM. Another train leaves station B at 7:00 AM and reaches station A at 10:30 AM. At what time do they cross each other?
(A) 8:00 AM  (B) 7:45 AM  (C) 7:56 AM  (D) 8:10 AM
Answer: (C) 7:56 AM
Solution: Duration of Train 1 = 4 hours. Duration of Train 2 = 3.5 hours = 7/2 hours. Let distance = 28 km. Speed 1 = 7 km/h, Speed 2 = 8 km/h. By 7:00 AM, Train 1 covers 7 * 2 = 14 km, leaving 14 km. Relative speed = 7 + 8 = 15 km/h. Time to meet after 7:00 AM = 14 / 15 hours = 56 minutes. Crossing time = 7:00 AM + 56 min = 7:56 AM. The required value is 7:56 AM. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

87. (Hard) A man walks at 6 km/h for 1 hour, then rests for 20 minutes, then walks at 6 km/h for 1 hour, and so on. How long will he take to cover a total distance of 30 km?
(A) 6 hours 40 minutes  (B) 6 hours  (C) 7 hours  (D) 6 hours 20 minutes
Answer: (D) 6 hours 20 minutes
Solution: In 1 hour of walking, he covers 6 km. To cover 30 km, he needs 30 / 6 = 5 walking segments of 1 hour each. Between 5 walking segments, there are 4 rest breaks of 20 minutes each. Total rest time = 4 * 20 = 80 minutes = 1 hour 20 minutes. Total time = 5 hours + 1 hour 20 min = 6 hours 20 minutes. The required value is 6 hours 20 minutes. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: State PCS Pattern

88. (Hard) A train travels 300 km at a uniform speed. If the speed had been 5 km/h more, the journey would have taken 2 hours less. What is the original speed of the train?
(A) 25 km/h  (B) 30 km/h  (C) 20 km/h  (D) 35 km/h
Answer: (A) 25 km/h
Solution: Equation: 300/s - 300/(s + 5) = 2 => 150/s - 150/(s + 5) = 1 => 750 = s(s + 5). Since 25 * 30 = 750, original speed s = 25 km/h. The required value is 25 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

89. (Hard) A man rows 15 km upstream and 22 km downstream in 5 hours. He also rows 20 km upstream and 27.5 km downstream in 6.5 hours. What is the speed of the stream?
(A) 2.5 km/h  (B) 3 km/h  (C) 3.5 km/h  (D) 2 km/h
Answer: (B) 3 km/h
Solution: Equations: 15/U + 22/D = 5 and 20/U + 27.5/D = 6.5. Testing D = 11: 22/11 = 2 => 15/U = 3 => U = 5. Checking second: 20/5 + 27.5/11 = 4 + 2.5 = 6.5 (valid). Speed of stream = (D - U) / 2 = (11 - 5) / 2 = 3 km/h. The required value is 3 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

92. (Hard) Two cars X and Y start at the same time from two cities 150 km apart and travel towards each other. If they meet after 1.5 hours and car X travels 10 km/h faster than car Y, what is the speed of car X?

93. (Hard) A boat's speed in still water is 15 km/h, and the current's speed is 3 km/h. It travels a certain distance downstream and immediately returns. If the total time taken is 10 hours, what is the one-way distance?
(A) 70 km  (B) 72 km  (C) 75 km  (D) 68 km
Answer: (B) 72 km
Solution: Downstream speed D = 15 + 3 = 18 km/h. Upstream speed U = 15 - 3 = 12 km/h. Equation: d/18 + d/12 = 10 => (2d + 3d) / 36 = 10 => 5d = 360 => d = 72 km. The required value is 72 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: State PCS Pattern

94. (Hard) A train passes a telegraph pole in 7 seconds and passes another train of equal length traveling in the opposite direction in 10 seconds. If the speed of the first train is 72 km/h, what is the speed of the second train in km/h?
(A) 30.0 km/h  (B) 25.2 km/h  (C) 28.8 km/h  (D) 32.4 km/h
Answer: (C) 28.8 km/h
Solution: Speed of first train S1 = 72 * (5/18) = 20 m/s. Length of first train = 20 * 7 = 140 meters. Both trains have equal length = 140 meters. Total distance to cross = 140 + 140 = 280 meters. Relative speed = 280 / 10 = 28 m/s. S1 + S2 = 28 => 20 + S2 = 28 => S2 = 8 m/s = 8 * (18/5) = 28.8 km/h. The required value is 28.8 km/h. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: CDS Pattern

95. (Hard) A person sets out on a journey of 150 km. He travels the first 60 km at 30 km/h and the remaining distance at 45 km/h. What is his average speed for the entire journey?
(A) 35.0 km/h  (B) 40.0 km/h  (C) 38.5 km/h  (D) 37.5 km/h

96. (Hard) A man covered a certain distance at some speed. Had he moved 3 km/h faster, he would have taken 40 minutes less. If he had moved 2 km/h slower, he would have taken 40 minutes more. What is the distance in km?
(A) 40 km  (B) 36 km  (C) 45 km  (D) 50 km
Answer: (A) 40 km
Solution: Distance formula: d = S(S + 3)/3 * (40/60) = S(S - 2)/2 * (40/60). Equating gives (S + 3)/3 = (S - 2)/2 => 2S + 6 = 3S - 6 => S = 12 km/h. Distance d = 12 * 15 / 3 * (2/3) = 40 km. The required value is 40 km. Hence, the correct option is (A).

100. (Hard) In a 200-meter race, A can beat B by 31 meters and C by 18 meters. In a race of 350 meters, by how many meters will C beat B?
(A) 28 meters  (B) 25 meters  (C) 20 meters  (D) 30 meters
Answer: (B) 25 meters
Solution: When A runs 200 m, B runs 200 - 31 = 169 m and C runs 200 - 18 = 182 m. Ratio B / C = 169 / 182 = 13 / 14. In a race of 350 m, when C runs 350 m, B runs 350 * (13/14) = 25 * 13 = 325 meters. C beats B by 350 - 325 = 25 meters. The required value is 25 meters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern
