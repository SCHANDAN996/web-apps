# Important Formulas: Time, Speed and Distance

## 1. Fundamentals and Conversions
- $\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$
- $1 \text{ km/h} = \frac{5}{18} \text{ m/s}$
- $1 \text{ m/s} = \frac{18}{5} \text{ km/h}$
- Constant Distance: $\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \frac{T_2}{T_1}$
- Constant Time: $\frac{D_1}{D_2} = \frac{S_1}{S_2}$

## 2. Average Speed
- General: $S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}}$
- Two equal distances: $S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 S_1 S_2}{S_1 + S_2}$
- Three equal distances: $S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{3 S_1 S_2 S_3}{S_1 S_2 + S_2 S_3 + S_3 S_1}$
- Equal time intervals: $S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{S_1 + S_2}{2}$

## 3. Early / Late Shortcut
$$\text{Distance} = \frac{S_1 \cdot S_2}{|S_1 - S_2|} \times \Delta T$$

## 4. Relative Speed
- Opposite direction: $S_{\text{rel}} = S_1 + S_2$
- Same direction: $S_{\text{rel}} = |S_1 - S_2|$

## 5. Trains Formulas
- Passing a pole/person: $T = \frac{L_T}{S_T}$
- Passing a platform: $T = \frac{L_T + L_P}{S_T}$
- Two trains crossing: $T = \frac{L_1 + L_2}{S_{\text{rel}}}$

## 6. Boats and Streams
- Downstream: $D = u + v$
- Upstream: $U = u - v$
- Speed of boat in still water: $u = \frac{D + U}{2}$
- Speed of stream: $v = \frac{D - U}{2}$
- Ratio relation: $\frac{u}{v} = \frac{T_U + T_D}{T_U - T_D}$

## 7. Meeting Offset Formula
$$\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{t_2}{t_1}}, \quad t_{\text{meet}} = \sqrt{t_1 \cdot t_2}$$

## 8. Stoppage Time
$$\text{Stoppage min/hr} = \frac{\text{Speed without stop} - \text{Speed with stop}}{\text{Speed without stop}} \times 60$$
