Card 1
Front: What is the basic relation between speed, distance and time?
Back: Speed = Distance ÷ Time. Hence Distance = Speed × Time, and Time = Distance ÷ Speed.

Card 2
Front: How do you convert km/h to m/s?
Back: Multiply by 5/18. (km/h × 5/18 = m/s)

Card 3
Front: How do you convert m/s to km/h?
Back: Multiply by 18/5. (m/s × 18/5 = km/h)

Card 4
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the conversion factors 5/18 and 18/5?
Back: "Small to Big, 18 up" — m/s is the smaller unit, so to go to the bigger unit (km/h) put 18 on top: ×18/5. Reverse for km/h → m/s: ×5/18.

Card 5
Front: If a distance is covered at speeds a and b for equal distances, what is the average speed?
Back: Average speed = 2ab/(a+b) — the harmonic mean of the two speeds.

Card 6
Front: If a person travels for equal times at speeds a and b, what is the average speed?
Back: Average speed = (a+b)/2 — the simple arithmetic mean.

Card 7
Front: Two objects move towards each other at speeds a and b. What is their relative speed?
Back: a + b (relative speeds add in opposite directions).

Card 8
Front: Two objects move in the same direction at speeds a and b (a > b). What is the relative speed?
Back: a − b (relative speeds subtract in the same direction).

Card 9
Front: A train of length L crosses a pole at speed S. Time taken?
Back: Time = L/S — the train must cover only its own length.

Card 10
Front: A train of length L crosses a platform of length P at speed S. Time taken?
Back: Time = (L + P)/S — the train covers its own length plus the platform's length.

Card 11
Front: Two trains of lengths L₁ and L₂ cross each other moving in opposite directions at speeds a and b. Time taken?
Back: Time = (L₁ + L₂)/(a + b).

Card 12
Front: What is the "DST triangle" mnemonic?
Back: Draw a triangle with D on top, S and T at the bottom. Cover what you want: D = S×T, S = D/T, T = D/S. "Dad Sits on Top" — Distance sits on top.

Card 13
Front: In a boat-and-stream problem, what are downstream and upstream speeds?
Back: Downstream = boat speed + stream speed; Upstream = boat speed − stream speed.

Card 14
Front: If downstream speed is D and upstream speed is U, how do you find boat speed and stream speed?
Back: Boat speed = (D + U)/2; Stream speed = (D − U)/2.

Card 15
Front: A walks at x% of B's speed. In the same time, what is the ratio of distances covered by A and B?
Back: Distances are in the same ratio as speeds: A : B = x : 100 (distance ∝ speed when time is constant).

Card 16
Front: If speed increases by x%, by what percentage does time decrease for a fixed distance?
Back: Time decreases by [x/(100 + x)] × 100%.

Card 17
Front: Two runners on a circular track of length L run in opposite directions at speeds a and b. When do they first meet?
Back: After L/(a + b) time — they meet when their combined distance equals one full lap.

Card 18
Front: A man reaches office x minutes late at speed a and y minutes early at speed b. What is the formula for the correct distance?
Back: Distance = (ab/(b − a)) × (x + y)/60 km, when speeds are in km/h and times in minutes.

Card 19
Front: What is the mnemonic for relative speed rules?
Back: "OppOSite → Add, Same → Subtract" — remember "O-A-S-S": Opposite Add, Same Subtract.

Card 20
Front: A train passes a man in t₁ seconds and a platform of length P in t₂ seconds. What is the train's length?
Back: Length = P × t₁/(t₂ − t₁). The extra time (t₂ − t₁) is spent covering just the platform.
