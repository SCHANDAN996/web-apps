1. Find the next term: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?
(a) 36 (b) 30 (c) 48 (d) 42
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 36, which assumes a constant difference of 6, but the differences here are 3, 6, 12 — each term is doubled. So 24 × 2 = 48, so the answer is 48.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics
