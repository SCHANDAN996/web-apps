101. Find the missing term: 4, 9, 25, 49, 121, ?
(a) 144 (b) 169 (c) 196 (d) 225
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting choice 144 assumes squares of consecutive integers, but the terms are squares of primes: 2², 3², 5², 7², 11², so the next is 13² = 169. The answer is 169.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Find the next term: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
(a) 36 (b) 40 (c) 44 (d) 42
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 40, obtained by adding 10 to 30 without checking the difference pattern. Differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, so the next difference is 12, giving 30 + 12 = 42 (also n² + n with n = 6). The answer is 42.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (pattern recognition)
