76. Find the missing term: 4, 9, 25, 49, 121, ?
(a) 144 (b) 225 (c) 196 (d) 169
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting choice 144 assumes squares of consecutive integers, but the terms are squares of primes: 2², 3², 5², 7², 11². The next prime is 13, so the answer is 169.

78. Find the wrong term in the series: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, 127, 256

Answer: (b)
Solution: Each term follows the rule "double and add 1": 3→7→15→31→63→127→255. The tempting choice 127 fits the rule perfectly, so it is not wrong; the last term should be 255, not 256. Hence the wrong term is 256.
