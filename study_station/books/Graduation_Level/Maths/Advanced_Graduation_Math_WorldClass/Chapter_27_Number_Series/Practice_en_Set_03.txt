51. Find the missing term: 4, 9, 25, 49, 121, 169, ?
(a) 225 (b) 256 (c) 289 (d) 196
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option 256 looks like a square continuation, but the series is squares of consecutive primes: 2², 3², 5², 7², 11², 13², so the next is 17² = 289. The trap is treating it as squares of odd numbers, so the answer is 289.
Source: PYQ-style

52. Find the missing term: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, 42, ?
(a) 56 (b) 54 (c) 52 (d) 60
Answer: (a)
Solution: Differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, so the next difference is 14, giving 42 + 14 = 56. The tempting option 54 comes from wrongly adding 12 again, so the answer is 56.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (pattern recognition)

57. Find the wrong term: 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55

Answer: (d)
Solution: This is the Fibonacci series where each term is the sum of the previous two: 2+3 = 5, 3+5 = 8, and so on up to 34+21 = 55. Every term fits, so the answer is that all terms are correct.
