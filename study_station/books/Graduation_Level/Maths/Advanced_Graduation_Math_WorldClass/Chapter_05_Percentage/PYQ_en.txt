# Pattern Analysis: Percentage (Graduation Level)

Recurring patterns in SSC CGL Tier-2, IBPS PO, and State PCS:
1. Product-constancy expenditure consumption adjustments with price shifts.
2. Two-candidate and three-candidate election systems with invalid votes and non-voting populations.
3. Income, expenditure, and savings percentage shifts modeled as weighted averages.
4. Geometric dimension changes affecting volume and surface area.

---

Q1. (Medium) If the price of petrol increases by 25%, by what percentage must a motorist reduce petrol consumption so that expenditure remains unchanged?
(A) 20%  (B) 25%  (C) 16 2/3%  (D) 33 1/3%
Answer: (A)
Solution: Price increases by 25% = +1/4. To keep expenditure constant, consumption must decrease by 1 / (4 + 1) = 1/5 = 20%. Therefore, the correct value is 20%, corresponding to option (A).
Psychological Trap: Assuming consumption must decrease by the same 25%.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q2. (Medium) In an election between two candidates, 10% of voters did not cast their votes, and 10% of the votes cast were declared invalid. The winning candidate received 54% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 1620 votes. Find the total number of voters enrolled on the voter list.
(A) 25000  (B) 20000  (C) 22500  (D) 30000
Answer: (A)
Solution: Let total enrolled voters be V. Polled = 0.9V. Valid = 0.9 * 0.9V = 0.81V. Winning margin = 54% - 46% = 8% of valid votes. So 0.08 * 0.81V = 1620 => 0.0648V = 1620 => V = 1620 / 0.0648 = 25000. Therefore, the correct value is 25000, corresponding to option (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q3. (Medium) A person spends 75% of their income. If their income increases by 20% and expenditure increases by 10%, find the percentage increase in their savings.
(A) 50%  (B) 40%  (C) 60%  (D) 30%
Answer: (A)
Solution: Let initial Income = 100. Expenditure = 75, Savings = 25. New Income = 120. New Expenditure = 75 * 1.10 = 82.5. New Savings = 120 - 82.5 = 37.5. Increase in savings = 37.5 - 25 = 12.5. Percentage increase = (12.5 / 25) * 100 = 50%. Therefore, the correct value is 50%, corresponding to option (A).
Source: State PCS Pattern

Q4. (Hard) If the radius of a sphere is increased by 20%, what is the percentage increase in its volume?
(A) 72.8%  (B) 60.0%  (C) 68.4%  (D) 70.0%
Answer: (A)
Solution: Volume is proportional to r^3. With r increasing by 20% (multiplier 1.2), the volume multiplier becomes (1.2)^3 = 1.728. The percentage increase is (1.728 - 1) * 100 = 72.8%. Therefore, the correct value is 72.8%, corresponding to option (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q5. (Hard) A reduction of 20% in the price of sugar enables a purchaser to obtain 4 kg more sugar for 160. What was the original price of sugar per kg?
(A) 10 per kg  (B) 8 per kg  (C) 12 per kg  (D) 9 per kg
Answer: (A)
Solution: Money saved on 160 due to 20% price drop = 20% of 160 = 32. This 32 buys 4 kg at the reduced price, so Reduced Price = 32 / 4 = 8 per kg. Since Reduced Price is 80% of original, Original Price = 8 / 0.80 = 10 per kg. Therefore, the correct value is 10 per kg, corresponding to option (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q6. (Medium) Two numbers are respectively 20% and 50% more than a third number. What percentage is the first number of the second number?
(A) 80%  (B) 70%  (C) 85%  (D) 90%
Answer: (A)
Solution: Let the third number be 100. First number = 120, second number = 150. Percentage = (120 / 150) * 100 = (4/5) * 100 = 80%. Therefore, the correct value is 80%, corresponding to option (A).
Source: IBPS PO Pattern

Q7. (Medium) In an examination, 35% of candidates failed in Mathematics and 25% in English. If 10% failed in both Mathematics and English, what percentage of candidates passed in both subjects?
(A) 50%  (B) 40%  (C) 60%  (D) 55%
Answer: (A)
Solution: Failed in at least one subject = n(M) + n(E) - n(M and E) = 35% + 25% - 10% = 50%. Percentage passing in both subjects = 100% - 50% = 50%. Therefore, the correct value is 50%, corresponding to option (A).
Source: State PCS Pattern

Q8. (Hard) The population of a town increases by 5% annually. If its present population is 92610, what was its population 3 years ago?
(A) 80000  (B) 85000  (C) 75000  (D) 82000
Answer: (A)
Solution: Multiplier per year = 1 + 5/100 = 21/20. Population 3 years ago P_0 satisfies P_0 * (21/20)^3 = 92610. Since 21^3 = 9261 and 20^3 = 8000: P_0 * (9261 / 8000) = 92610 => P_0 = 10 * 8000 = 80000. Therefore, the correct value is 80000, corresponding to option (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q9. (Medium) A student scored 30% marks and failed by 15 marks, while another student scored 40% marks and obtained 35 marks more than the minimum passing marks. Find the maximum marks of the examination.
(A) 500  (B) 400  (C) 450  (D) 600
Answer: (A)
Solution: Difference in percentage = 40% - 30% = 10%. Difference in absolute marks = 35 - (-15) = 50 marks. 10% of maximum marks = 50 => Maximum marks = 500. Therefore, the correct value is 500, corresponding to option (A).
Source: IBPS PO Pattern

Q10. (Hard) If a number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%, what is the net percentage change in the number?
(A) 4% decrease  (B) 0% change  (C) 4% increase  (D) 2% decrease
Answer: (A)
Solution: Net change = +20 - 20 - (20 * 20)/100 = -4%, which is a 4% decrease. Therefore, the correct value is 4% decrease, corresponding to option (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
