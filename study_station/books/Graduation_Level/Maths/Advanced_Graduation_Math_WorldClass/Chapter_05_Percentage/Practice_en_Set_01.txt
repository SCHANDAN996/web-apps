1. What is 25% of 480?
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 140 (d) 160
Answer: (b)
Solution: 25% means one-fourth, so divide 480 by 4: 480 ÷ 4 = 120. The trap is multiplying 480 by 25 and forgetting to divide by 100, which gives 12000. So the answer is 120.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

2. 40% of 250 = ?
(a) 100 (b) 95 (c) 90 (d) 110
Answer: (a)
Solution: 40% = 40/100, so 250 × 40/100 = 100. The trap is computing 250 × 40 = 10000 and forgetting to divide by 100. So the answer is 100.
