# Competitive Exam Short Tricks: Percentage

1. The Fractional Multiplier Chain:
   Memorize standard fractions:
   - 1/6 = 16.67%, 1/7 = 14.28%, 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/9 = 11.11%
   - 1/11 = 9.09%, 1/12 = 8.33%, 1/13 = 7.69%, 1/14 = 7.14%
   - 1/15 = 6.67%, 1/16 = 6.25%, 1/17 = 5.88%, 1/19 = 5.26%

2. Instant Reduced Price Formula:
   When r% price reduction enables purchasing 'k' extra kg for total cost 'T':
   - Reduced Price = (r% of T) / k
   - Original Price = Reduced Price / (1 - r/100)

3. Rapid Alligation for Income-Expenditure:
   Ratio of Expenditure to Savings = (Change in Savings - Change in Income) : (Change in Income - Change in Expenditure).

4. Constant Expenditure Shift (AB Rule):
   +1/n increase in price => -1/(n + 1) decrease in consumption.
   -1/n decrease in price => +1/(n - 1) increase in consumption.
