Q26. (Medium) The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percentage must a household reduce its consumption so that the total expenditure remains unchanged?
(A) 20%  (B) 25%  (C) 16 2/3%  (D) 22.5%

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Using the formula for constant expenditure: Reduction = [r / (100 + r)] * 100% = [25 / 125] * 100% = 20%. Psychological Trap: Students often mistakenly answer 25% thinking consumption reduction equals price increase. Therefore, the correct value is 20%, corresponding to option (A).
