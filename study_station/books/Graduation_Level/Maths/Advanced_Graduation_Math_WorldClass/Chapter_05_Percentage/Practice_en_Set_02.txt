26. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The net change in the number is:
(a) No change (b) 4% decrease (c) 4% increase (d) 2% decrease
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is "no change", but successive equal percentage changes never cancel out. Net change = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%. So the number decreases by 4%, so the answer is 4% decrease.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

27. If the price of sugar rises by 25%, by what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that the expenditure remains the same?
(a) 25% (b) 30% (c) 15% (d) 20%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students pick 25%, assuming the reduction equals the rise — that is the trap. Required reduction = 25/(100+25) × 100 = 20%. So the answer is 20%.
