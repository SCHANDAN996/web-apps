Card 1
Front: What does "per cent" literally mean?
Back: "Per hundred" — x% means x out of 100, i.e., x/100.

Card 2
Front: Convert 3/8 into a percentage.
Back: 3/8 × 100 = 37.5%

Card 3
Front: Convert 62.5% into a fraction.
Back: 62.5% = 62.5/100 = 5/8

Card 4
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the fraction-percentage pairs 1/3, 1/6, 1/9?
Back: "Thirds family": 1/3 = 33.33%, 1/6 = 16.66%, 1/9 = 11.11% — denominator ×3 pattern: 3→33, 6→16, 9→11 (each roughly half of the previous).

Card 5
Front: If A's salary is 25% more than B's, by what percent is B's salary less than A's?
Back: 25/(100+25) × 100 = 20% less.

Card 6
Front: If a number is increased by x% and then decreased by x%, what is the net change?
Back: Net decrease of x²/100 % (e.g., 10% up then 10% down → 1% net decrease).

Card 7
Front: What is the successive percentage change formula?
Back: Net % change = a + b + (ab/100), where a and b are the two successive changes (use negative sign for decreases).

Card 8
Front: Price of an item rises 20%. By what % must consumption fall to keep expenditure constant?
Back: 20/(100+20) × 100 = 16.66% reduction.

Card 9
Front: What is the mnemonic for increase/decrease reversal problems?
Back: "Up x, Down x/(100+x); Down x, Up x/(100−x)" — remember: "Same base? No! Flip the hundred."

Card 10
Front: A population grows 10% per year. What is the population after 2 years if it starts at P?
Back: P × (1.1)² = 1.21P — a 21% total increase.

Card 11
Front: If the side of a square increases by 10%, by what % does its area increase?
Back: 10 + 10 + (10×10)/100 = 21%

Card 12
Front: What % is 45 of 60?
Back: 45/60 × 100 = 75%

Card 13
Front: A student scores 30% and fails by 15 marks; pass mark is 40%. What are the maximum marks?
Back: 10% of max = 15 → Maximum marks = 150.

Card 14
Front: What is the quick trick to find 15% of any number?
Back: 10% + 5% (half of 10%). E.g., 15% of 240 = 24 + 12 = 36.

Card 15
Front: In an election, winner gets 55% of votes and wins by 1200 votes. How many valid votes were cast?
Back: Margin = 55% − 45% = 10% → 10% = 1200 → Total valid votes = 12,000.

Card 16
Front: What is the mnemonic for converting percentage to decimal?
Back: "Drop the %, jump two left" — 7.5% → 0.075 (decimal point moves two places left).

Card 17
Front: If A is 20% of B and B is 50% of C, what % is A of C?
Back: A = 0.20 × 0.50 × C = 10% of C.

Card 18
Front: A number increased by 20% becomes 360. What is the original number?
Back: 360 × 100/120 = 300.

Card 19
Front: Fresh fruit has 68% water; dry fruit has 20% water. If dry fruit is 25 kg, what was the fresh weight?
Back: Pulp is constant: 80% of 25 = 20 kg pulp = 32% of fresh → Fresh = 20/0.32 = 62.5 kg.

Card 20
Front: What is the key trap in "percentage of a percentage" questions?
Back: Students add instead of multiply — 20% of 50% is 0.20 × 0.50 = 10%, not 70%.
