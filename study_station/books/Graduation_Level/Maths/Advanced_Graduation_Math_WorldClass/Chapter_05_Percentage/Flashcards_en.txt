Q: If price increases by a/b, what fractional decrease in consumption maintains constant expenditure?
A: Consumption must decrease by a / (b + a).

Q: If a quantity increases by x% and then decreases by x%, what is the net percentage change?
A: An overall decrease of (x^2 / 100)%.

Q: What is the single equivalent percentage change for successive increases of 20% and 30%?
A: 20 + 30 + (20 * 30)/100 = 56% increase.

Q: In an election between two candidates, Winner gets 58% of valid votes and wins by 3200 votes. What are the total valid votes?
A: Margin is 58% - 42% = 16%. Total valid votes = 3200 / 0.16 = 20,000.

Q: If a person's income increases by 20%, expenditure increases by 10%, and initial savings were 30% of income, what is the percentage increase in savings?
A: 43 1/3%, calculated via Income increase = Expenditure increase + Savings increase.

Q: If the radius of a cylinder increases by 10% and height decreases by 20%, what is the net percentage change in volume?
A: Volume varies as r^2 * h: 1.1 * 1.1 * 0.8 = 0.968, representing a 3.2% net decrease.
