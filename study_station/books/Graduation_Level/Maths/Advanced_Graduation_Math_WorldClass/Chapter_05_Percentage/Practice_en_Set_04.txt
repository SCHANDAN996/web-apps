76. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The net change in the number is:
(a) No change (b) 4% decrease (c) 4% increase (d) 2% decrease
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is "no change", but the base changes: after a 20% rise the value is 1.2 times, and the 20% fall applies to this larger value. Net multiplier = 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96, i.e. a 4% decrease. So the answer is 4% decrease.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

77. If the price of sugar rises by 25%, by what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that the expenditure remains the same?
(a) 20% (b) 15% (c) 22.5% (d) 25%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many pick 25%, assuming rise and reduction are equal, but the reduction is on the new higher price. Required reduction = 25/125 × 100 = 20%. So the answer is 20%.

78. In an election, a candidate who got 42% of the votes lost by 1600 votes. The total number of votes polled was:
(a) 16000 (b) 8000 (c) 12000 (d) 10000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The loser got 42%, so the winner got 58%; the trap is treating 1600 as 42% of votes. The margin is 58% − 42% = 16% of total votes, so total = 1600/0.16 = 10000. So the answer is 10000.

79. The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the population at the end of the second year is 49500, the initial population was:
(a) 49500 (b) 50000 (c) 50500 (d) 49000
Answer: (b)
Solution: Choosing 49500 ignores the compounding; the net multiplier is 1.1 × 0.9 = 0.99, not 1. Initial population = 49500/0.99 = 50000. So the answer is 50000.

80. A student scored 30% marks and failed by 15 marks. Another student scored 40% marks and got 35 marks more than the pass marks. The maximum marks are:
(a) 400 (b) 600 (c) 500 (d) 450
Answer: (c)
Solution: The gap between 40% and 30% scores is 35 + 15 = 50 marks, which equals 10% of maximum; the trap is adding only 35 or only 15. Maximum = 50/0.10 = 500. So the answer is 500.

81. If A's salary is 25% more than B's salary, then B's salary is what percent less than A's?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 22.5%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 25%, but the base for "less" is A's salary, not B's. Let B = 100, then A = 125; B is less by 25/125 × 100 = 20%. So the answer is 20%.

82. A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 25%. His profit percentage is:
(a) 15% (b) 10% (c) 5% (d) 8%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Subtracting 40 − 25 = 15% is the common trap, since the discount applies to the marked price. Selling price = 1.40 × 0.75 = 1.05 of cost price, i.e. 5% profit. So the answer is 5%.

83. The value of a machine depreciates at 10% per annum. If its present value is ₹81000, its value 2 years ago was:
(a) ₹102000 (b) ₹90000 (c) ₹100000 (d) ₹98000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding 20% to 81000 gives 97200, a trap, because depreciation compounds. Value 2 years ago = 81000/(0.9 × 0.9) = 81000/0.81 = 100000. So the answer is ₹100000.

84. In a class, 60% of the students are boys. If 20% of the boys and 30% of the girls play cricket, what percentage of the whole class plays cricket?
(a) 24% (b) 25% (c) 26% (d) 22%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Averaging 20% and 30% to get 25% is the trap, since boys and girls are not equal in number. Cricket players = 0.20 × 60 + 0.30 × 40 = 12 + 12 = 24%. So the answer is 24%.

85. A number is first increased by 10% and then the result is increased by 20%. The total percentage increase is:
(a) 35% (b) 30% (c) 28% (d) 32%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Simply adding 10 + 20 = 30% is the trap; the second increase acts on the increased value. Net multiplier = 1.1 × 1.2 = 1.32, i.e. a 32% increase. So the answer is 32%.

86. If 30% of a number is subtracted from the number, the result is 91. The number is:
(a) 120 (b) 130 (c) 140 (d) 150

Solution: The trap is computing 91 + 30% of 91; the 30% must be of the original number. Remaining 70% = 91, so the number = 91/0.7 = 130. So the answer is 130.

88. In an examination, 65% of the students passed in Mathematics, 55% passed in English and 40% passed in both. What percentage of students failed in both subjects?
(a) 30% (b) 25% (c) 20% (d) 15%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is ignoring the overlap of 40% who passed in both. Passed in at least one = 65 + 55 − 40 = 80%, so failed in both = 100 − 80 = 20%. So the answer is 20%.

89. A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%. His savings increase by:
(a) 50% (b) 40% (c) 45% (d) 55%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is taking 20% − 10% = 10% as the savings rise. Let income = 100, expenditure = 75, savings = 25. New income = 120, new expenditure = 82.5, new savings = 37.5; increase = 12.5/25 × 100 = 50%. So the answer is 50%.

90. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and the denominator is decreased by 20%, the fraction becomes 3/2. The original fraction is:
(a) 3/4 (b) 1 (c) 5/4 (d) 4/5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming the original fraction equals 3/2 directly. Let the fraction be x/y; then (1.2x)/(0.8y) = 3/2, so x/y = (3/2) × (0.8/1.2) = 1. So the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

91. Fresh fruit contains 68% water and dry fruit contains 20% water. How much dry fruit can be obtained from 100 kg of fresh fruit?
(a) 50 kg (b) 32 kg (c) 45 kg (d) 40 kg
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is taking 32% of 100 kg as the answer; the pulp mass, not the water, stays constant. Pulp in fresh fruit = 32 kg, which is 80% of dry fruit, so dry fruit = 32/0.8 = 40 kg. So the answer is 40 kg.

94. In a factory, 40% of the workers are skilled and the rest unskilled. If 60% of the skilled workers and 30% of the unskilled workers are permanent, what percentage of all workers are temporary?
(a) 58% (b) 60% (c) 62% (d) 55%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is averaging 40% and 70% temporary rates without weighting. Temporary = 0.40 × 40 + 0.70 × 60 = 16 + 42 = 58%. So the answer is 58%.

97. If 15% of x is equal to 20% of y, then x : y is:
(a) 2 : 3 (b) 3 : 4 (c) 1 : 1 (d) 4 : 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is writing the ratio as 15 : 20 = 3 : 4, which inverts it. From 0.15x = 0.20y, x/y = 20/15 = 4/3. So the answer is 4 : 3.
