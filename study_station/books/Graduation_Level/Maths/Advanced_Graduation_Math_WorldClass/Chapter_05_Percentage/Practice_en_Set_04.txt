Q77. (Hard) The population of a city was 160,000 two years ago. If it increased by 10% in the first year and decreased by 10% in the second year, what is its present population?
(A) 160,000  (B) 158,400  (C) 156,800  (D) 162,000

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Net multiplier = (1 + 0.10) * (1 - 0.10) = 1.10 * 0.90 = 0.99. Present population = 160,000 * 0.99 = 158,400. Psychological Trap: Students often assume equal percentage increase and decrease cancel each other out to leave the population unchanged at 160,000. Therefore, the correct value is 158,400, corresponding to option (B).

Q80. (Hard) In an election between two candidates, 10% of voters did not cast their votes and 10% of the votes cast were declared invalid. A candidate got 54% of the valid votes and won by a majority of 1,620 votes. Find the total number of voters on the electoral roll.
(A) 25,000  (B) 20,000  (C) 30,000  (D) 22,500

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Let total enrolled voters be x. Votes cast = 0.90x. Valid votes = 0.90x * 0.90 = 0.81x. Winner got 54% and loser got 46% of valid votes. Winning margin = 54% - 46% = 8% of valid votes. Thus, 0.08 * 0.81x = 1,620 => 0.0648x = 1,620 => x = 1,620 / 0.0648 = 25,000. Psychological Trap: A critical trap is applying the 8% margin directly to the total enrolled votes or subtracting 10% + 10% = 20% additively instead of successively. Therefore, the correct value is 25,000, corresponding to option (A).
