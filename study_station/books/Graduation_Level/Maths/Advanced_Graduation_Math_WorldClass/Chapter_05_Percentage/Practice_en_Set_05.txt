101. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The net change in the number is:
(a) No change (b) 2% decrease (c) 4% increase (d) 4% decrease
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is "no change", since +20% and −20% look like they cancel. But the 20% decrease applies to the increased value: net change = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%, so the answer is a 4% decrease.

102. If the price of sugar rises by 25%, by what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that the expenditure remains the same?
(a) 25% (b) 20% (c) 30% (d) 22.5%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 25%, assuming the reduction equals the rise. Required reduction = r/(100+r) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

103. In an election between two candidates, the winner got 62% of the votes and won by 7200 votes. The total number of votes polled was:
(a) 32000 (b) 28000 (c) 36000 (d) 30000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using 62% directly as the margin. The loser got 38%, so the margin is 62 − 38 = 24% of total votes; 24% of total = 7200 gives total = 7200 × 100/24 = 30000, so the answer is 30000.

104. The population of a town increases by 10% every year. If the present population is 121000, what was it 2 years ago?
(a) 105000 (b) 100000 (c) 110000 (d) 99000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is subtracting 20% of 121000, which uses the wrong base. Population 2 years ago = 121000/(1.1)² = 121000/1.21 = 100000, so the answer is 100000.

105. A student scored 32% marks and failed by 40 marks. Another student scored 42% marks and got 30 marks more than the pass mark. The maximum marks are:
(a) 500 (b) 600 (c) 800 (d) 700
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is taking 40 − 30 = 10 marks for the 10% gap. The difference of 10% actually corresponds to 40 + 30 = 70 marks, so 10% = 70 and maximum marks = 700, so the answer is 700.

Solution: The trap is adding 20% to 81000. Value 2 years ago = 81000/(0.9)² = 81000/0.81 = ₹100000, so the answer is ₹100000.

108. In a class, 60% of the students are boys. If 25% of the boys and 40% of the girls play cricket, what percentage of the whole class plays cricket?
(a) 31% (b) 30% (c) 32.5% (d) 28%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is averaging 25% and 40% directly. Boys playing = 25% of 60% = 15% and girls playing = 40% of 40% = 16%, giving 15 + 16 = 31%, so the answer is 31%.

109. A man spends 75% of his income. His income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 10%. His savings increase by:
(a) 40% (b) 50% (c) 30% (d) 25%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 20 − 10 = 10%. Let income = 100, expenditure = 75, savings = 25; new income = 120, new expenditure = 82.5, new savings = 37.5, an increase of 12.5/25 × 100 = 50%, so the answer is 50%.

Solution: The trap is 50 − 20 = 30%. Let the third number be 100; then first = 120 and second = 150, and 120/150 × 100 = 80%, so the answer is 80%.

112. If 30% of a number is subtracted from 91, the result is the number itself. The number is:
(a) 75 (b) 65 (c) 80 (d) 70
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is setting 91 − 0.3x equal to 0.3x instead of x. The correct equation is 91 − 0.3x = x, so 91 = 1.3x and x = 70, so the answer is 70.

113. In an examination, 35% of students failed in Hindi, 45% failed in English and 20% failed in both. What percentage of students passed in both subjects?
(a) 40% (b) 35% (c) 45% (d) 30%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding all three figures. Failed in at least one subject = 35 + 45 − 20 = 60%, so passed in both = 100 − 60 = 40%, so the answer is 40%.

117. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and the denominator is decreased by 20%, the fraction becomes 3/2. The original fraction is:
(a) 4/5 (b) 5/4 (c) 1 (d) 3/4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is inverting the ratio of the multipliers. Here (1.2N)/(0.8D) = 3/2 gives N/D = (3/2) × (0.8/1.2) = (3/2) × (2/3) = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

118. A's income is 60% of B's income, and A's expenditure is 70% of B's expenditure. If A's income is 75% of B's expenditure, find the ratio of A's savings to B's savings.
(a) 3 : 4 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 1 : 5 (d) 1 : 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is comparing incomes instead of savings. Let B's expenditure = 100; then A's income = 75, A's expenditure = 70, so A's savings = 5, while B's income = 75 × 100/60 = 125 and B's savings = 25. Ratio = 5 : 25 = 1 : 5, so the answer is 1 : 5.

120. In a school, 20% of the students are below 8 years of age. The number of students above 8 years is 2/3 of the number of students of exactly 8 years, which is 48. The total number of students is:
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 110 (d) 90
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating 48 as 80% of the total directly. Students above 8 = (2/3) × 48 = 32, so students of 8 years and above = 48 + 32 = 80, which is 80% of total; total = 80 × 100/80 = 100, so the answer is 100.

121. A solution contains 10% salt. After some water evaporates, the salt concentration becomes 20%. If the original solution weighed 50 kg, the weight of water evaporated is:
(a) 30 kg (b) 20 kg (c) 25 kg (d) 15 kg
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming 10 kg evaporates because the concentration doubled. Salt = 10% of 50 = 5 kg stays constant; new total = 5 × 100/20 = 25 kg, so water evaporated = 50 − 25 = 25 kg, so the answer is 25 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

122. In an election between two candidates, the winner got 55% of the valid votes and won by a margin of 1500 votes. The number of valid votes polled was:
(a) 12000 (b) 10000 (c) 18000 (d) 15000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is dividing 1500 by 55% or by 45%. The loser got 45%, so the margin is 55 − 45 = 10% of valid votes; 10% = 1500 gives valid votes = 15000, so the answer is 15000.

124. The population of a town was 160000 three years ago. It increased by 3%, 2.5% and 5% in the three successive years. The present population is approximately:
(a) 177000 (b) 176800 (c) 178000 (d) 177366
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding 3 + 2.5 + 5 = 10.5% and computing 176800. Successive multiplication gives 160000 × 1.03 × 1.025 × 1.05 = 160000 × 1.1085375 = 177366, so the answer is 177366.
Source: PYQ-style

125. A vessel contains 60 litres of milk. 12 litres of milk is taken out and replaced by water. This process is repeated once more. The quantity of milk left is:
(a) 36 litres (b) 38.4 litres (c) 42 litres (d) 40 litres
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is subtracting 12 litres twice to get 36 litres. Milk left = 60 × (1 − 12/60)² = 60 × (0.8)² = 60 × 0.64 = 38.4 litres, so the answer is 38.4 litres.
Source: PYQ-style
