51. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. The net change in the number is:
(a) No change (b) 2% decrease (c) 4% increase (d) 4% decrease
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is "no change", since +20% and −20% look like they cancel. But the decrease applies to the increased value: net change = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%. So the answer is 4% decrease.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

52. If the price of sugar rises by 25%, by what percentage must a family reduce its consumption so that expenditure remains the same?
(a) 25% (b) 15% (c) 22.5% (d) 20%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many pick 25%, assuming the reduction equals the rise — that is the trap, because the base changes. Required reduction = 25/(100+25) × 100 = 20%. So the answer is 20%.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC CGL pattern)

53. In an election, 75% of voters cast their votes, out of which 2% were invalid. The winner got 60% of valid votes and won by 10,290 votes. How many voters were enrolled?
(a) 70,000 (b) 75,000 (c) 80,000 (d) 85,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is applying 60% to total enrolled voters instead of valid votes. Valid votes = 0.75 × 0.98 = 0.735 of enrolled. Winner's margin = (60 − 40)% of valid = 0.20 × 0.735 = 0.147 of enrolled. Enrolled = 10,290/0.147 = 70,000. So the answer is 70,000.
Source: PYQ-style (SSC/Bank election pattern)

54. A's salary is 40% more than B's. By what percentage is B's salary less than A's?

Solution: The trap is answering 40%, ignoring that the base is now A's salary. Let B = 100, so A = 140. B is less than A by 40/140 × 100 = 28.57%. So the answer is 28.57%.

55. The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the population at the end of the second year is 49,500, what was it initially?
(a) 49,000 (b) 50,000 (c) 51,000 (d) 52,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming the population returns to the initial value after equal rise and fall. Net multiplier = 1.1 × 0.9 = 0.99. Initial population = 49,500/0.99 = 50,000. So the answer is 50,000.

56. A student scored 32% marks and failed by 40 marks. Another student scored 42% marks and got 30 marks more than the pass mark. What are the maximum marks?

Solution: The trap is taking the difference of marks (70) as 10% of the pass mark instead of the maximum. Difference of 10% of maximum = 40 + 30 = 70, so maximum = 700. So the answer is 700.

58. The length of a rectangle is increased by 30% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. The percentage change in its area is:

59. In a factory, 40% of the workers are men and the rest women. If 25% of the men and 15% of the women are skilled, what percentage of all workers are skilled?

60. A man spends 80% of his income. His income increases by 25% and his expenditure increases by 20%. What is the percentage increase in his savings?
(a) 40% (b) 45% (c) 35% (d) 50%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is taking 25% − 20% = 5% as the savings increase. Let income = 100: expenditure = 80, savings = 20. New income = 125, new expenditure = 96, new savings = 29. Increase = 9/20 × 100 = 45%. So the answer is 45%.

61. The value of a machine depreciates by 15% every year. If its present value is ₹1,00,000, its value after 2 years will be:
(a) ₹70,000 (b) ₹72,250 (c) ₹72,000 (d) ₹73,500
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is deducting 30% at once, giving ₹70,000. Depreciation is successive: 1,00,000 × 0.85 × 0.85 = ₹72,250. So the answer is ₹72,250.

62. If x% of y is equal to y% of 200, and y = 50, then x equals:
(a) 100 (b) 250 (c) 150 (d) 200

Solution: The trap is assuming x must equal y since x% of y = y% of x. Here x% of 50 = 50% of 200 = 100, so x/100 × 50 = 100, giving x = 200. So the answer is 200.

63. A number is first increased by 10% and then the result is increased by 20%. The overall percentage increase is:
(a) 30% (b) 35% (c) 28% (d) 32%

Solution: The trap is adding 10 + 20 = 30%. Successive change = 10 + 20 + (10×20)/100 = 32%. So the answer is 32%.

66. A village has 5,000 residents. 60% are literate, and 30% of the literate are women. How many literate men are there in the village?

67. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 25% and the denominator is decreased by 20%, the fraction becomes 5/4. The original fraction is:
(a) 5/6 (b) 4/5 (c) 3/4 (d) 6/5

Solution: The trap is reversing the changes (multiplying by 1.25 and dividing by 0.8 again). Original = (5/4) × (0.8/1.25) = (5/4) × (16/25) = 4/5. So the answer is 4/5.

68. A salesman gets a commission of 5% on sales up to ₹10,000 and 8% on sales above ₹10,000. If his total sales are ₹25,000, his commission is:
