126. The population of a town increases by 20% in the first year, decreases by 20% in the second year and again increases by 10% in the third year. What is the net percentage change in the population over the three years?
(a) 10% increase (b) 4% decrease (c) 8% increase (d) 5.6% increase
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is adding the changes: 20 − 20 + 10 = 10%, which ignores the changing base. Multiply the factors: 1.20 × 0.80 × 1.10 = 1.056, i.e. a 5.6% net increase, so the answer is 5.6% increase.
