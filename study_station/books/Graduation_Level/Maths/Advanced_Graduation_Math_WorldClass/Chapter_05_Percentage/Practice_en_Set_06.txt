Q126. (Very Hard) In a college, 20% of the students failed in Mathematics, 15% failed in English, and 10% failed in both. A student is selected at random. If he failed in English, what is the probability that he also failed in Mathematics?
(A) 2/3  (B) 1/2  (C) 3/4  (D) 1/3

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Conditional probability P(Math | English) = P(Math and English) / P(English) = 10% / 15% = 10 / 15 = 2/3. Psychological Trap: Students often divide 10% by total students (100%) or fail to recognize this as a conditional percentage problem. Therefore, the correct value is 2/3, corresponding to option (A).
