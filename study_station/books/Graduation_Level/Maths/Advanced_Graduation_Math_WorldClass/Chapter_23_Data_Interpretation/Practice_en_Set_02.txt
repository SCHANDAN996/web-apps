26. The table below shows the production (in tonnes) of wheat in five states in 2022 and 2023.

State | 2022 | 2023
P | 1200 | 1500
Q | 900 | 1080
R | 1500 | 1650
S | 800 | 1040
T | 1100 | 1210

Which state recorded the highest percentage increase in production from 2022 to 2023?
(a) P (b) S (c) R (d) Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is P because its absolute increase of 300 tonnes is the largest, but percentage increase depends on the base. P: 300/1200 = 25%; Q: 180/900 = 20%; R: 150/1500 = 10%; S: 240/800 = 30%; T: 110/1100 = 10%. The highest percentage increase is 30% for S, so the answer is S.
Source: PYQ-style

27. A pie chart shows the monthly expenditure of a family: Food 30%, Rent 25%, Education 15%, Transport 10%, Savings 20%. If the total monthly income is ₹60,000 and the entire income is distributed as shown, what is the amount spent on Education and Transport together?
(a) ₹12,000 (b) ₹15,000 (c) ₹18,000 (d) ₹21,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many students compute only Education (15% = ₹9,000) and then guess. Education + Transport = 15% + 10% = 25% of ₹60,000 = 60,000 × 0.25 = ₹15,000, so the answer is ₹15,000.
Source: PYQ-style

28. The bar graph shows the number of employees in a company from 2019 to 2023: 400, 460, 500, 580, 650 respectively. What is the average of the yearly percentage growth rates from 2019 to 2023 (approximately)?
(a) 12.9% (b) 10.2% (c) 15.6% (d) 62.5%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Option 62.5% is the total growth over 4 years (250/400), a common trap for those who forget to average. Yearly growth rates: 60/400 = 15%, 40/460 ≈ 8.7%, 80/500 = 16%, 70/580 ≈ 12.1%; average = (15 + 8.7 + 16 + 12.1)/4 ≈ 12.9%, so the answer is 12.9%.
Source: PYQ-style

(a) April (b) February (c) May (d) June
Answer: (b)
Solution: May shows the biggest absolute jump (₹10 lakh), which tempts many, but percentage increase depends on the base. Feb: 15/40 = 37.5%; Apr: 15/45 ≈ 33.3%; May: 10/60 ≈ 16.7%; Mar and Jun are decreases. The highest percentage increase is in February, so the answer is February.

31. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 5:4. The pie chart of total 1800 people is divided into Boys, Girls and Staff. If staff are 200, how many boys are there?
(a) 889 (b) 900 (c) 1000 (d) 800
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is applying the ratio to all 1800, giving 1000 boys. But staff must be removed first: students = 1800 − 200 = 1600. Boys = 5/9 × 1600 ≈ 888.9 ≈ 889, so the answer is 889.

35. A company's revenue grew from ₹80 crore in 2020 to ₹125 crore in 2023. What is the approximate compound annual growth rate (CAGR)?
(a) 12% (b) 16% (c) 18.75% (d) 25%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is dividing total growth 56.25% by 3 to get 18.75% — that is simple average, not CAGR. CAGR = (125/80)^(1/3) − 1 = (1.5625)^(1/3) − 1 ≈ 1.16 − 1 = 16%, so the answer is 16%.
Source: PYQ-style

36. Study the data of runs scored by a batsman in 6 innings: 45, 78, 0, 102, 55, 62. What is his average score?
(a) 57 (b) 62 (c) 60 (d) 55
Answer: (a)
Solution: Some students drop the duck (0) and divide by 5, getting 68.4 — wrong, since all innings count. Sum = 45 + 78 + 0 + 102 + 55 + 62 = 342. Average = 342/6 = 57, so the answer is 57.

48. In a survey of 500 people, 60% read newspaper X, 45% read newspaper Y, and 25% read both. How many read neither?
(a) 150 (b) 75 (c) 125 (d) 100
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is adding 60% + 45% = 105% and concluding a negative number, or forgetting to subtract the overlap. Reading at least one = 60 + 45 − 25 = 80% = 400 people. Neither = 500 − 400 = 100, so the answer is 100.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

49. The bar graph shows profit margin % of a firm: 2020: 10%, 2021: 12%, 2022: 8%, 2023: 15%. If revenue each year is ₹200 crore, what is the total profit over 4 years (₹ crore)?
(a) 80 (b) 45 (c) 100 (d) 90
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is averaging margins (11.25%) and applying to one year's revenue only, giving 22.5. Total profit = 200 × (0.10 + 0.12 + 0.08 + 0.15) = 200 × 0.45 = ₹90 crore, so the answer is 90.
