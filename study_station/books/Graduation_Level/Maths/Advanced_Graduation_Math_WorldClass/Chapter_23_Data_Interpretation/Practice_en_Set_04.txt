76. The table shows the production of wheat (in lakh tonnes) in four states: Punjab 150, Haryana 110, UP 180, MP 90. What percentage of the total production comes from UP?
(a) 34.5% (b) 32.8% (c) 33.96% (d) 35.2%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 34.5% comes from dividing 180 by 522, a mis-added total. Total production = 150 + 110 + 180 + 90 = 530; UP's share = 180/530 × 100 = 33.96%, so the answer is 33.96%.
Source: PYQ-style

77. A pie chart shows the expenditure of a family: Food 90°, Rent 72°, Education 54°, Savings 36°, Others 108°. If the total monthly income is ₹60,000, how much is spent on education?
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹7,500 (c) ₹10,000 (d) ₹9,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option ₹10,000 comes from treating 54° as 60°, one-sixth of the circle. Education's share = 54/360 × 60,000 = 0.15 × 60,000 = ₹9,000, so the answer is ₹9,000.
Source: PYQ-style

78. A bar graph shows sales (in ₹ thousand) of a company: 2019: 240, 2020: 300, 2021: 270, 2022: 360. What is the percentage increase in sales from 2019 to 2022?
(a) 40% (b) 45% (c) 50% (d) 55%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option 45% comes from dividing the increase 120 by 270, the 2021 value, instead of the base year value. Increase = 360 − 240 = 120; percentage increase = 120/240 × 100 = 50%, so the answer is 50%.
Source: PYQ-style

79. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 5:4. In 2023 the total number of students is 1,800. How many girls were there in 2023?
(a) 720 (b) 900 (c) 800 (d) 1,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 720 comes from swapping the ratio and taking the boys' share formula for girls with parts reversed. Girls' share = 4/(5+4) × 1,800 = 4/9 × 1,800 = 800, so the answer is 800.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

(a) 68% (b) 70% (c) 72% (d) 75%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 72% comes from misreading SST as 60 and adding an extra 10. Total = 80 + 70 + 60 + 90 + 50 = 350; average = 350/5 = 70, so the answer is 70%.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option ₹16,000 comes from treating 45° as 60°, one-sixth of the circle. Transport = 45/360 × 96,000 = 1/8 × 96,000 = ₹12,000, so the answer is ₹12,000.

82. A company's profit rose from ₹2.4 crore in 2021 to ₹3.0 crore in 2022 and then fell to ₹2.7 crore in 2023. What is the net percentage change from 2021 to 2023?
(a) 10% increase (b) 12.5% increase (c) 15% increase (d) 12.5% decrease
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 15% comes from adding the yearly changes 25% and −10% directly, ignoring the change of base. Net change = (2.7 − 2.4)/2.4 × 100 = 0.3/2.4 × 100 = 12.5%, so the answer is 12.5% increase.
Source: PYQ-style

83. The table shows employees in three departments: HR 40, Sales 120, Tech 200. If 20% of Tech employees are shifted to Sales, what is the new ratio of Sales to Tech?
(a) 1:1 (b) 5:4 (c) 4:5 (d) 3:2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option 5:4 comes from shifting only 20 employees instead of 20%. Shifted = 20% of 200 = 40; new Sales = 120 + 40 = 160, new Tech = 200 − 40 = 160; ratio 160:160 = 1:1, so the answer is 1:1.
Source: PYQ-style

84. A line graph shows the population of a town: 50,000 in 2018, growing by 10% every year. What was the population in 2021?
(a) 68,000 (b) 65,000 (c) 67,500 (d) 66,550
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option 65,000 comes from simple-interest thinking, adding 5,000 three times. Compound growth: 50,000 × 1.1³ = 50,000 × 1.331 = 66,550, so the answer is 66,550.

87. The average of five years' rainfall values is 840 mm. Four values are 800, 860, 900 and 780 mm. What is the fifth value?
(a) 860 mm (b) 840 mm (c) 880 mm (d) 820 mm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 840 assumes the missing value equals the average, but the four given values average only 835. Total = 5 × 840 = 4,200; fifth = 4,200 − (800 + 860 + 900 + 780) = 4,200 − 3,340 = 860, so the answer is 860 mm.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

Solution: The tempting option 37.5% uses Plant C as the base instead of Plant A. Increase = 4,000 − 2,500 = 1,500; percentage = 1,500/2,500 × 100 = 60%, so the answer is 60%.

94. A company's revenue grew from ₹80 lakh to ₹92 lakh in one year. Expenses grew from ₹60 lakh to ₹75 lakh. By what percentage did profit change?
(a) 10% decrease (b) No change (c) 15% increase (d) 15% decrease
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 15% increase looks only at revenue growth and ignores expenses. Old profit = 80 − 60 = ₹20 lakh; new profit = 92 − 75 = ₹17 lakh; change = −3/20 × 100 = −15%, so the answer is 15% decrease.

97. In a pie chart, savings form an angle of 54° of a monthly income of ₹45,000. If savings increase by ₹1,500 with income unchanged, what is the new savings angle?
(a) 60° (b) 64° (c) 66° (d) 72°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 60° comes from rounding the new share to one-sixth of the circle. Old savings = 54/360 × 45,000 = ₹6,750; new savings = ₹8,250; new angle = 8,250/45,000 × 360 = 66°, so the answer is 66°.
Source: PYQ-style

98. A table shows two products: Product X costs ₹250 and sells at ₹300; Product Y costs ₹400 and sells at ₹460. Which statement about profit percentage is true?
(a) X gives 20% and Y gives 15% (b) X gives 15% and Y gives 20% (c) Both give 20% (d) X gives 25% and Y gives 15%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting claim that X gives 25% divides X's profit ₹50 by the selling price ₹300 instead of the cost price. X: 50/250 × 100 = 20%; Y: 60/400 × 100 = 15%, so the answer is X gives 20% and Y gives 15%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

99. A bar graph shows exports (₹ crore): 2020: 500, 2021: 650, 2022: 585. What is the percentage change in exports from 2020 to 2022 measured over the whole period?
(a) 17% (b) 8.5% (c) 15% (d) 10%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option 8.5% halves the total change assuming it is per year, but the question asks for the change over the whole period. Change = (585 − 500)/500 × 100 = 85/500 × 100 = 17%, so the answer is 17%.
Source: PYQ-style

100. In a class of 60 students, a table shows: 24 play cricket, 20 play football, 10 play both. How many students play neither game?
(a) 24 (b) 28 (c) 26 (d) 30
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option 24 comes from an addition slip that makes 36 students play at least one game. Students playing at least one game = 24 + 20 − 10 = 34; neither = 60 − 34 = 26, so the answer is 26.
