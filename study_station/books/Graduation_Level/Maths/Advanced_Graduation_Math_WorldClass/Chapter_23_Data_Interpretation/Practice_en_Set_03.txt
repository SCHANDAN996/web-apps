51. The table shows the production of wheat (in lakh tonnes) in four states: Punjab 150, Haryana 110, UP 180, MP 120. What percentage of the total production is contributed by UP?
(a) 32.14% (b) 30% (c) 33.33% (d) 35%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total production = 150 + 110 + 180 + 120 = 560 lakh tonnes. UP's share = 180/560 × 100 = 32.14%. The tempting option 33.33% comes from wrongly taking the total as 540 (forgetting one state), so the answer is 32.14%.
Source: PYQ-style

52. A company's revenue grew from ₹240 crore in 2021 to ₹300 crore in 2022 and ₹360 crore in 2023. What is the percentage growth of revenue from 2021 to 2023?
(a) 50% (b) 45% (c) 40% (d) 60%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Growth = (360 − 240)/240 × 100 = 120/240 × 100 = 50%. A common trap is adding the two yearly growth rates (25% + 20% = 45%), which ignores compounding, so the answer is 50%.
Source: PYQ-style

53. In a pie chart, the angle for expenditure on education is 72° out of a total expenditure of ₹5,00,000. How much is spent on education?
(a) ₹1,00,000 (b) ₹90,000 (c) ₹1,20,000 (d) ₹72,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amount = (72/360) × 5,00,000 = (1/5) × 5,00,000 = ₹1,00,000. The trap option ₹72,000 comes from treating degrees directly as rupees in thousands, so the answer is ₹1,00,000.
Source: PYQ-style

54. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 5:4. If 20% of boys and 25% of girls play cricket, what percentage of the total students play cricket?
(a) 20% (b) 22.22% (c) 25% (d) 24%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let boys = 500, girls = 400. Cricketers = 20% of 500 + 25% of 400 = 100 + 100 = 200. Percentage = 200/900 × 100 = 22.22%. Taking the average of 20% and 25% gives 22.5%, which is wrong because the groups are unequal, so the answer is 22.22%.
Source: PYQ-style

55. A bar graph shows sales (in units): Jan 400, Feb 500, Mar 450, Apr 600. What is the average monthly sale?
(a) 487.5 (b) 475 (c) 490 (d) 500
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total = 400 + 500 + 450 + 600 = 1950. Average = 1950/4 = 487.5. The trap is rounding 450 to 500 first, which inflates the total, so the answer is 487.5.

56. The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the initial population was 1,00,000, what is the population after two years?
(a) 1,00,000 (b) 98,000 (c) 1,01,000 (d) 99,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: After year 1: 1,00,000 × 1.10 = 1,10,000. After year 2: 1,10,000 × 0.90 = 99,000. The trap is assuming +10% and −10% cancel out to give 1,00,000, but the base changes, so the answer is 99,000.
Source: PYQ-style

57. In a table, the imports of a country are ₹800 crore and exports are ₹600 crore. What is the ratio of exports to imports?
(a) 4:3 (b) 3:4 (c) 2:3 (d) 3:2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Exports : Imports = 600 : 800 = 3 : 4. The tempting option 4:3 reverses the order (imports to exports), so the answer is 3:4.

58. A line graph shows the profit of a firm: 2019 ₹50 lakh, 2020 ₹70 lakh, 2021 ₹65 lakh, 2022 ₹90 lakh. In which year was the percentage increase over the previous year the highest?
(a) 2022 (b) 2021 (c) 2020 (d) Equal in 2020 and 2022
Answer: (c)
Solution: 2020 increase = 20/50 = 40%; 2021 is a decrease; 2022 increase = 25/65 ≈ 38.5%. The trap is picking 2022 because its absolute increase (₹25 lakh) is largest, but percentage-wise 2020 wins, so the answer is 2020.

59. Out of 800 employees, 60% are men. If 30% of men and 40% of women are graduates, how many employees are graduates?
(a) 240 (b) 256 (c) 280 (d) 272
Answer: (d)
Solution: Men = 480, women = 320. Graduates = 30% of 480 + 40% of 320 = 144 + 128 = 272. The trap is applying 30% or 40% to all 800 employees, so the answer is 272.
Source: PYQ-style

60. The average of five numbers shown in a table is 46. If one number, 60, is removed, what is the average of the remaining four numbers?
(a) 42 (b) 42.5 (c) 43 (d) 44
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sum of five = 5 × 46 = 230. Remaining sum = 230 − 60 = 170. New average = 170/4 = 42.5. The trap is subtracting 60 from 46 directly, so the answer is 42.5.
Source: PYQ-style

61. A pie chart shows a family's monthly budget of ₹36,000: Food 120°, Rent 90°, Savings 60°, Others 90°. How much more is spent on food than on savings?
(a) ₹4,000 (b) ₹6,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹3,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: Difference in angles = 120° − 60° = 60°. Amount = (60/360) × 36,000 = ₹6,000. The trap is computing food (₹12,000) and savings (₹6,000) and reporting savings instead of the difference, so the answer is ₹6,000.
Source: PYQ-style

(a) 72% (b) 80% (c) 78% (d) 75%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total = 80 + 70 + 60 + 90 = 300 out of 400. Percentage = 300/400 × 100 = 75%. The trap is averaging only the highest and lowest marks, so the answer is 75%.

63. A company's expenditure in 2022 was ₹450 lakh, which was 125% of its income. What was its income?

64. In a survey of 1,200 people, 45% liked tea, 35% liked coffee and the rest liked milk. How many people liked milk?
(a) 240 (b) 220 (c) 200 (d) 260
Answer: (a)
Solution: Milk lovers = 100% − 45% − 35% = 20%. Number = 20% of 1,200 = 240. The trap is adding 45% and 35% and reporting 80% of 1,200, so the answer is 240.

(a) 12% (b) 18% (c) 15% (d) 13.04%

66. A table gives the number of students in three classes: X = 40, Y = 50, Z = 60, with pass percentages 80%, 70% and 75% respectively. What is the overall pass percentage?
(a) 74% (b) 74.67% (c) 75% (d) 76%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Passed = 32 + 35 + 45 = 112 out of 150. Percentage = 112/150 × 100 = 74.67%. The trap is taking the simple average of 80, 70 and 75, which gives 75%, ignoring class sizes, so the answer is 74.67%.

67. A shopkeeper's sales in four quarters are in the ratio 2:3:4:3. If total annual sales are ₹24 lakh, what were the sales in the third quarter?
(a) ₹6 lakh (b) ₹7 lakh (c) ₹8 lakh (d) ₹9 lakh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sum of ratio parts = 2 + 3 + 4 + 3 = 12. Third quarter = (4/12) × 24 = ₹8 lakh. The trap is dividing 24 by 4 equally (₹6 lakh), ignoring the ratio, so the answer is ₹8 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

68. The angle for "Transport" in an expenditure pie chart is 45°. If the amount spent on transport is ₹15,000, what is the total expenditure?
(a) ₹1,00,000 (b) ₹1,50,000 (c) ₹1,20,000 (d) ₹90,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: 45° is 1/8 of 360°, so total = 15,000 × 8 = ₹1,20,000. The trap is multiplying by 45/100 as if it were a percentage, so the answer is ₹1,20,000.

69. A bar graph shows exports (₹ crore): 2019 = 200, 2020 = 250, 2021 = 300, 2022 = 350. What is the average annual growth in absolute terms over 2019–2022?
(a) ₹40 crore (b) ₹45 crore (c) ₹55 crore (d) ₹50 crore
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total growth = 350 − 200 = 150 over 3 years. Average = 150/3 = ₹50 crore. The trap is dividing by 4 (the number of years shown), giving ₹37.5 crore, so the answer is ₹50 crore.

70. In a college, the ratio of students in Arts, Science and Commerce is 3:4:5. If there are 600 Commerce students, what is the total number of students?
(a) 1,200 (b) 1,320 (c) 1,500 (d) 1,440
Answer: (d)
Solution: 5 parts = 600, so 1 part = 120. Total parts = 12, hence total = 12 × 120 = 1,440. The trap is computing 600 × 2 = 1,200 by assuming Commerce is half the total, so the answer is 1,440.

72. The income of A is 20% more than that of B. By what percentage is B's income less than A's?
(a) 20% (b) 16.67% (c) 25% (d) 15%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let B = 100, then A = 120. B is less than A by 20/120 × 100 = 16.67%. The classic trap is answering 20%, forgetting the base changes from B to A, so the answer is 16.67%.

73. A pie chart represents 1,800 voters. The angle for Party X is 140° and for Party Y is 100°. How many more votes did Party X get than Party Y?
(a) 200 (b) 180 (c) 150 (d) 220
Answer: (a)
Solution: Difference in angles = 40°. Votes = (40/360) × 1,800 = 200. The trap is computing Party X's total (700) or Party Y's total (500) and stopping there, so the answer is 200.

74. The table shows the number of units produced and sold by a firm: produced 5,000, sold 4,200. If the selling price is ₹50 per unit and the cost is ₹40 per produced unit, what is the profit?
(a) ₹42,000 (b) ₹12,000 (c) ₹10,000 (d) ₹50,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Revenue = 4,200 × 50 = ₹2,10,000. Cost = 5,000 × 40 = ₹2,00,000. Profit = ₹10,000. The trap is computing profit only on sold units (4,200 × 10 = ₹42,000), ignoring the cost of unsold units, so the answer is ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

75. In a city, rainfall (in mm) over six months was: 50, 80, 120, 200, 150, 100. The rainfall in the wettest month is what percentage of the total rainfall?
(a) 28.57% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 33.33%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total = 50 + 80 + 120 + 200 + 150 + 100 = 700 mm. Wettest month = 200 mm. Percentage = 200/700 × 100 = 28.57%. The trap is dividing 200 by 600 (dropping one month), which gives 33.33%, so the answer is 28.57%.
Source: PYQ-style
