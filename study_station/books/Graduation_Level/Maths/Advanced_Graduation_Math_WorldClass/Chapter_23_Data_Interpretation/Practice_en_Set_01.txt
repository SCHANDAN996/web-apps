1. The table shows the number of students in four classes: Class A = 40, Class B = 50, Class C = 60, Class D = 30. What is the total number of students?
(a) 180 (b) 170 (c) 190 (d) 200
Answer: (a)
Solution: A common slip is adding only three classes and getting 150, but all four must be included. Total = 40 + 50 + 60 + 30 = 180, so the answer is 180.
Source: PYQ-style

2. A bar graph shows sales of a shop over four months: Jan = 200, Feb = 250, Mar = 300, Apr = 350 (units). In which month were sales the highest?
(a) April (b) February (c) March (d) January
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is picking March because the jump from Feb to Mar looks big, but the tallest bar is April with 350 units, so the answer is April.
Source: PYQ-style

3. In a pie chart, the angle for Rent is 90°. What percentage of total expenditure is spent on Rent?
(a) 25% (b) 20% (c) 30% (d) 15%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students wrongly divide 90 by 100 to get 90%. Percentage = (90/360) × 100 = 25%, so the answer is 25%.

4. The table shows marks of a student: Hindi = 70, English = 80, Maths = 90, Science = 60. What is the average marks?
(a) 70 (b) 75 (c) 72 (d) 80
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dividing by 5 instead of 4 gives 60, a common error. Average = (70 + 80 + 90 + 60)/4 = 300/4 = 75, so the answer is 75.

5. A line graph shows population of a town: 2019 = 10,000; 2020 = 12,000. What is the percentage increase from 2019 to 2020?
(a) 10% (b) 15% (c) 20% (d) 25%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 2000/12000 = 16.67% by using the new value as base. Increase = (2000/10000) × 100 = 20%, so the answer is 20%.

6. The table shows production of wheat (in tonnes): Year 1 = 500, Year 2 = 600, Year 3 = 700. What is the ratio of production in Year 1 to Year 3?

Solution: Reversing the order gives 7 : 5, the tempting wrong choice. Ratio = 500 : 700 = 5 : 7, so the answer is 5 : 7.

7. A pie chart shows expenses: Food = 40%, Rent = 30%, Travel = 20%, Savings = 10%. If total income is ₹50,000, how much is spent on Food?
(a) ₹15,000 (b) ₹25,000 (c) ₹20,000 (d) ₹10,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Confusing Food with Rent gives ₹15,000. Food = 40% of 50,000 = 0.40 × 50,000 = ₹20,000, so the answer is ₹20,000.

9. The table gives the number of employees in a company: 2018 = 200, 2019 = 240, 2020 = 300. What is the average number of employees over the three years?
(a) 240 (b) 246.67 (c) 250 (d) 260
Answer: (b)
Solution: Picking the middle value 240 ignores the rising trend. Average = (200 + 240 + 300)/3 = 740/3 ≈ 246.67, so the answer is 246.67.

10. In a pie chart, the sector for Savings is 36°. If total income is ₹1,00,000, what is the amount saved?
(a) ₹36,000 (b) ₹3,600 (c) ₹10,000 (d) ₹1,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Treating 36° as 36% gives ₹36,000, the classic trap. Amount = (36/360) × 1,00,000 = 10% of 1,00,000 = ₹10,000, so the answer is ₹10,000.

21. The table shows production of two items (in units): Item A — 2019: 400, 2020: 500; Item B — 2019: 600, 2020: 550. What is the percentage change in total production from 2019 to 2020?
(a) 5% decrease (b) 5% increase (c) 10% increase (d) No change
Answer: (b)
Solution: Looking only at Item B suggests a decrease, the trap. Total 2019 = 1000; Total 2020 = 1050; change = (50/1000) × 100 = 5% increase, so the answer is 5% increase.

22. A pie chart shows a company's expenditure: Salaries = 120°, Raw material = 90°, Marketing = 60°, Others = 90°. If total expenditure is ₹24 lakh, how much more is spent on Salaries than on Marketing?
(a) ₹8 lakh (b) ₹6 lakh (c) ₹2 lakh (d) ₹4 lakh
Answer: (d)
Solution: Taking the difference of angles (60°) as ₹6 lakh directly confuses degrees with money. Salaries = (120/360) × 24 = ₹8 lakh; Marketing = (60/360) × 24 = ₹4 lakh; difference = ₹4 lakh, so the answer is ₹4 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

23. The table shows exports (₹ crore): 2018 = 250, 2019 = 300, 2020 = 360. What is the average annual percentage growth from 2018 to 2020 (using overall growth over the period divided by number of years)?
(a) 20% (b) 22% (c) 44% (d) 11%
Answer: (b)
Solution: Taking total growth 44% as the answer ignores the two-year span. Overall growth = (360 − 250)/250 × 100 = 44%; average per year = 44/2 = 22%, so the answer is 22%.

25. A bar graph shows the number of vehicles sold: Cars = 240, Bikes = 360, Scooters = 180, Trucks = 120. If these data are shown in a pie chart, what will be the angle for Bikes?
(a) 120° (b) 96° (c) 144° (d) 108°
Answer: (c)
Solution: Using 360 as the total (confusing the value 360 with degrees) gives wrong angles. Total = 240 + 360 + 180 + 120 = 900; angle = (360/900) × 360 = 144°, so the answer is 144°.
