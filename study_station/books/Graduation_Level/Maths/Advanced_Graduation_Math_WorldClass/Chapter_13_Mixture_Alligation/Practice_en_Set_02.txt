Questions 26–50: Medium

26. (Medium) A grocer mixes two varieties of tea costing Rs 150 per kg and Rs 200 per kg respectively. He sells the mixture at Rs 216 per kg, thereby making a profit of 20%. In what ratio did he mix the two varieties?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 3 : 2  (C) 1 : 2  (D) 3 : 4
Answer: (A) 2 : 3
Solution: Selling price of mixture is Rs 216 at 20% profit. Mean CP of mixture = 216 / 1.20 = Rs 180 per kg. Applying alligation with CP of 150 and 200: Ratio of cheaper to dearer = (200 - 180) / (180 - 150) = 20 / 30 = 2 : 3. The required value is 2 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

27. (Medium) A milkman adds water to milk costing Rs 45 per liter and sells the mixture at Rs 50 per liter, making a profit of 25%. What is the ratio of water to milk in the mixture?
(A) 1 : 9  (B) 1 : 8  (C) 1 : 7  (D) 2 : 9
Answer: (B) 1 : 8
Solution: Mean CP of mixture = 50 / 1.25 = Rs 40 per liter. Cost of water = 0, milk = 45. Ratio of water to milk = (45 - 40) / (40 - 0) = 5 / 40 = 1 / 8, or 1 : 8. The required value is 1 : 8. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

28. (Medium) Two vessels Vessel P and Vessel Q contain mixtures of milk and water. In Vessel P, the ratio of milk to water is 4 : 3, and in Vessel Q, it is 2 : 3. In what ratio should liquids from both vessels be mixed to obtain a new mixture in which milk and water are in the ratio 1 : 1?
(A) 5 : 7  (B) 3 : 4  (C) 7 : 5  (D) 4 : 3
Answer: (C) 7 : 5
Solution: Fraction of milk in Vessel P = 4/7. Fraction of milk in Vessel Q = 2/5. Target fraction = 1/2. By Alligation on milk: (1/2 - 2/5) / (4/7 - 1/2) = (1/10) / (1/14) = 14 / 10 = 7 / 5. The ratio is 7 : 5. The required value is 7 : 5. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

29. (Medium) A vessel of 80 liters is filled with milk and water. 70% of milk and 30% of water is taken out. It is found that 55% of the vessel is vacated. Find the initial quantity of milk in the vessel.
(A) 45 liters  (B) 48 liters  (C) 52 liters  (D) 50 liters
Answer: (D) 50 liters
Solution: By Alligation on percentage taken out: Milk (70%) and Water (30%) with Mean (55%). Ratio of initial milk to water = (55 - 30) / (70 - 55) = 25 / 15 = 5 / 3. Total parts = 5 + 3 = 8. Initial milk = 80 * (5/8) = 50 liters. The required value is 50 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: IBPS PO Prelims Pattern

30. (Medium) A container has 60 liters of a mixture of wine and water in the ratio 2 : 1. How much water should be added to the container so that the ratio of wine to water becomes 1 : 2?
(A) 60 liters  (B) 40 liters  (C) 50 liters  (D) 80 liters
Answer: (A) 60 liters
Solution: Initial wine = 60 * (2/3) = 40 liters, water = 20 liters. In the new mixture, wine remains 40 liters, which corresponds to 1 part. Thus 2 parts of water = 2 * 40 = 80 liters. Water to be added = 80 - 20 = 60 liters. The required value is 60 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

31. (Medium) A merchant mixes two varieties of rice costing Rs 36 per kg and Rs 48 per kg in the ratio 3 : 1. He sells the mixture at Rs 46.80 per kg. What is his percentage profit?
(A) 18%  (B) 20%  (C) 22%  (D) 25%
Answer: (B) 20%
Solution: CP of mixture = (3 * 36 + 1 * 48) / 4 = (108 + 48) / 4 = 156 / 4 = Rs 39 per kg. SP of mixture = Rs 46.80 per kg. Profit = 46.80 - 39 = Rs 7.80. Profit% = (7.80 / 39) * 100 = 20%. The required value is 20%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

32. (Medium) In a 72-liter mixture, the ratio of milk to water is 7 : 2. How much water must be added to make the ratio of milk to water 7 : 3?
(A) 6 liters  (B) 10 liters  (C) 8 liters  (D) 12 liters
Answer: (C) 8 liters
Solution: Initial total = 72 liters. Milk = 72 * (7/9) = 56 liters, Water = 16 liters. For ratio 7 : 3, milk (7 parts) = 56 liters, so 1 part = 8 liters. Water needed (3 parts) = 24 liters. Water to be added = 24 - 16 = 8 liters. The required value is 8 liters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

33. (Medium) A person travels a total distance of 150 km in 5 hours, partly by bus at 20 km/h and partly by train at 40 km/h. How much distance did he cover by bus?
(A) 75 km  (B) 60 km  (C) 40 km  (D) 50 km
Answer: (D) 50 km
Solution: Average speed = 150 / 5 = 30 km/h. Alligation on speed: (40 - 30) / (30 - 20) = 10 / 10 = 1 : 1. Note: Alligation on speed yields the ratio of TIME taken, not distance! Time by bus = 5 * (1/2) = 2.5 hours. Distance covered by bus = 20 * 2.5 = 50 km. The required value is 50 km. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

34. (Medium) A beaker contains an acid solution of 45% concentration. Another beaker contains an acid solution of 75% concentration. How many liters of the 75% solution must be mixed with 30 liters of the 45% solution to produce a solution of 55% concentration?
(A) 15 liters  (B) 18 liters  (C) 20 liters  (D) 12 liters
Answer: (A) 15 liters
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of 45% solution to 75% solution = (75 - 55) / (55 - 45) = 20 / 10 = 2 / 1. Quantity of 45% solution is 30 liters (2 parts), so 1 part (75% solution) = 30 / 2 = 15 liters. The required value is 15 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: CDS Pattern

35. (Medium) A mixture of 120 gallons of wine and water contains 20% wine. How much wine must be added to make wine 25% of the total mixture?
(A) 10 gallons  (B) 8 gallons  (C) 6 gallons  (D) 12 gallons
Answer: (B) 8 gallons
Solution: Initial wine = 120 * 0.20 = 24 gallons, water = 96 gallons. In the new mixture, water is 75% of total. Total volume = 96 / 0.75 = 128 gallons. Wine to add = 128 - 120 = 8 gallons. The required value is 8 gallons. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: IBPS PO Prelims Pattern

36. (Medium) A sum of Rs 10,000 was lent in two parts, one at 9% per annum and the other at 11% per annum. If the total annual interest from both parts was Rs 980, how much was lent at 9%?
(A) Rs 5,000  (B) Rs 4,000  (C) Rs 6,000  (D) Rs 5,500
Answer: (C) Rs 6,000
Solution: Overall interest rate = (980 / 10,000) * 100 = 9.8%. By Alligation: Ratio of part at 9% to part at 11% = (11 - 9.8) / (9.8 - 9) = 1.2 / 0.8 = 3 / 2. Sum lent at 9% = 10,000 * (3 / 5) = Rs 6,000. The required value is Rs 6,000. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

37. (Medium) Two vessels Vessel P and Vessel Q contain milk and water in the ratio 5 : 2 and 8 : 5 respectively. In what ratio should the contents of Vessel P and Vessel Q be mixed to obtain a new mixture containing milk and water in the ratio 9 : 4?
(A) 5 : 2  (B) 2 : 7  (C) 3 : 5  (D) 7 : 2
Answer: (D) 7 : 2
Solution: Milk fractions: Vessel P = 5/7 = 65/91, Vessel Q = 8/13 = 56/91. Target = 9/13 = 63/91. By Alligation on milk: Ratio of P to Q = (63/91 - 56/91) / (65/91 - 63/91) = 7 / 2 = 7 : 2. The required value is 7 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

38. (Medium) A container has 54 liters of pure milk. One-third of the milk is replaced with water. Then, again one-third of the mixture is replaced with water. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(A) 4 : 5  (B) 5 : 4  (C) 3 : 5  (D) 2 : 3
Answer: (A) 4 : 5
Solution: Fraction of milk remaining after 2 operations = (1 - 1/3)^2 = (2/3)^2 = 4/9. Remaining milk = 54 * (4/9) = 24 liters. Water = 54 - 24 = 30 liters. Ratio of milk to water = 24 : 30 = 4 : 5. The required value is 4 : 5. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

39. (Medium) A merchant blends two types of sugar, one costing Rs 38 per kg and the other costing Rs 46 per kg. In what ratio should he blend them so that by selling the blend at Rs 52.80 per kg, he earns a profit of 20%?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 1 : 3  (C) 3 : 1  (D) 1 : 2
Answer: (B) 1 : 3
Solution: Mean CP of blend = 52.80 / 1.20 = Rs 44 per kg. By Alligation: Ratio of cheaper to dearer = (46 - 44) / (44 - 38) = 2 / 6 = 1 / 3, which is 1 : 3. The required value is 1 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

40. (Medium) In an alloy, the ratio of copper to zinc is 5 : 3. If 16 kg of zinc is melted and mixed with 64 kg of this alloy, what will be the ratio of copper to zinc in the new alloy?
(A) 5 : 4  (B) 4 : 5  (C) 1 : 1  (D) 3 : 2
Answer: (C) 1 : 1
Solution: In 64 kg of alloy: Copper = 64 * (5/8) = 40 kg, Zinc = 24 kg. When 16 kg of zinc is added: New Zinc = 24 + 16 = 40 kg, New Copper = 40 kg. Ratio of copper to zinc = 40 : 40 = 1 : 1. The required value is 1 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: CDS Pattern

41. (Medium) The ratio of milk and water in a 45-liter mixture is 4 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?
(A) 12 liters  (B) 10 liters  (C) 18 liters  (D) 15 liters
Answer: (D) 15 liters
Solution: Initial milk = 45 * (4/5) = 36 liters, water = 9 liters. In the new mixture, milk is unchanged at 36 liters, representing 3 parts. Each part = 12 liters. Water required (2 parts) = 24 liters. Water to add = 24 - 9 = 15 liters. The required value is 15 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

42. (Medium) A trader has 120 kg of wheat. He sells a part at 12% profit and the rest at 20% profit. His total gain on the transaction is 15%. How much wheat was sold at 20% profit?
(A) 45 kg  (B) 50 kg  (C) 75 kg  (D) 40 kg
Answer: (A) 45 kg
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of part at 12% to part at 20% = (20 - 15) / (15 - 12) = 5 / 3. Total parts = 8. Quantity sold at 20% profit = 120 * (3 / 8) = 45 kg. The required value is 45 kg. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: IBPS PO Prelims Pattern

43. (Medium) A chemist has 10 liters of a 60% alcohol solution. How much pure alcohol must he add to increase the concentration to 75%?
(A) 5 liters  (B) 6 liters  (C) 4 liters  (D) 8 liters
Answer: (B) 6 liters
Solution: Water quantity = 10 * 40% = 4 liters. In the new solution, water represents 100% - 75% = 25% of total. Total volume = 4 / 0.25 = 16 liters. Pure alcohol to add = 16 - 10 = 6 liters. The required value is 6 liters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

44. (Medium) A vessel contains 60 liters of pure acid. 12 liters of acid is drawn off and replaced with water. Next, 12 liters of the mixture is drawn off and again replaced with water. How much acid is left in the vessel?
(A) 36.0 liters  (B) 39.6 liters  (C) 38.4 liters  (D) 40.2 liters
Answer: (C) 38.4 liters
Solution: Using formula: Remaining pure liquid = x * (1 - y/x)^n. Here x = 60, y = 12, n = 2. Remaining acid = 60 * (1 - 12/60)^2 = 60 * (4/5)^2 = 60 * (16/25) = 38.4 liters. The required value is 38.4 liters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

45. (Medium) In what ratio must an alloy containing 80% copper and 20% zinc be mixed with an alloy containing 65% copper and 35% zinc to produce an alloy containing 70% copper?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 2 : 1  (C) 3 : 4  (D) 1 : 2
Answer: (D) 1 : 2
Solution: By Alligation on copper: (70 - 65) / (80 - 70) = 5 / 10 = 1 / 2. The ratio of the first alloy to the second alloy is 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: State PCS Pattern

46. (Medium) A merchant bought a horse and a carriage for Rs 40,000. He sold the horse at a profit of 10% and the carriage at a loss of 5%. If he gained 1% on the entire transaction, find the cost price of the horse.
(A) Rs 16,000  (B) Rs 18,000  (C) Rs 24,000  (D) Rs 20,000
Answer: (A) Rs 16,000
Solution: By Alligation: Profit on horse = +10%, Carriage = -5%, Overall = +1%. Ratio of CP of horse to carriage = (1 - (-5)) / (10 - 1) = 6 / 9 = 2 / 3. Cost of horse = 40,000 * (2 / 5) = Rs 16,000. The required value is Rs 16,000. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

47. (Medium) A drum contains 80 liters of ethanol. 20 liters of ethanol is removed and replaced with water. Then 20 liters of the mixture is removed and replaced with water. What is the final percentage of ethanol in the drum?
(A) 50.00%  (B) 56.25%  (C) 62.50%  (D) 52.50%
Answer: (B) 56.25%
Solution: Remaining ethanol = 80 * (1 - 20/80)^2 = 80 * (3/4)^2 = 80 * (9/16) = 45 liters. Percentage of ethanol = (45 / 80) * 100% = 56.25%. The required value is 56.25%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

48. (Medium) Two vessels contain spirit and water. In Vessel 1, the ratio of spirit to water is 5 : 3. In Vessel 2, the ratio of spirit to water is 3 : 5. In what ratio must the contents of Vessel 1 and Vessel 2 be mixed to form a mixture containing equal amounts of spirit and water?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 3 : 2  (C) 1 : 1  (D) 3 : 4
Answer: (C) 1 : 1
Solution: Spirit fraction: Vessel 1 = 5/8, Vessel 2 = 3/8. Target fraction = 1/2 = 4/8. By Alligation on spirit: Ratio = (4/8 - 3/8) / (5/8 - 4/8) = (1/8) / (1/8) = 1 : 1. The required value is 1 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

49. (Medium) A dishonest milkman claims to sell milk at its cost price, but he mixes water with it and earns a profit of 11.11% (1/9). If the cost of milk is Rs 54 per liter, how much water does he mix with 36 liters of pure milk?
(A) 3 liters  (B) 5 liters  (C) 6 liters  (D) 4 liters
Answer: (D) 4 liters
Solution: When selling at cost price, Profit% = (Water / Milk) * 100%. Given profit is 1/9, we have Water / Milk = 1 / 9. With 36 liters of milk, Water = 36 * (1/9) = 4 liters. The required value is 4 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

50. (Medium) A mixture of 64 liters contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3. If 16 liters of this mixture is removed and replaced with 16 liters of water, what is the new ratio of milk to water?
(A) 17 : 15  (B) 15 : 17  (C) 5 : 7  (D) 7 : 5
Answer: (B) 15 : 17
Solution: Remaining mixture = 64 - 16 = 48 liters. In this 48 liters, milk = 48 * (5/8) = 30 liters, water = 18 liters. Adding 16 liters of water gives Water = 18 + 16 = 34 liters, while Milk remains 30 liters. Ratio of milk to water = 30 : 34 = 15 : 17. The required value is 15 : 17. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern
