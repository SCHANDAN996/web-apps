# Flashcards: Mixture & Alligation

Front: What is the fundamental ratio formula in the Rule of Alligation?
Back: $\frac{\text{Quantity of Cheaper}}{\text{Quantity of Dearer}} = \frac{d - m}{m - c}$.

---
Front: What must you do before using a mixture Selling Price (SP) with profit in Alligation?
Back: Convert the Selling Price to Cost Price ($m = \text{SP} \times 100 / (100 + P\%)$).

---
Front: State the repeated dilution formula for remaining pure liquid.
Back: $\text{Remaining Liquid} = x \left( 1 - \frac{y}{x} \right)^n$.

---
Front: If pure milk to water ratio after 2 replacements is 16 : 9, what is the remaining fraction of milk?
Back: $16 / (16 + 9) = 16 / 25$, so $(1 - y/x)^2 = 16/25 \implies 1 - y/x = 4/5$.

---
Front: When alligation is applied to speeds S1 and S2 with average speed S_avg, what ratio is obtained?
Back: The ratio of times spent traveling at each speed ($T_1 : T_2$).

---
Front: When alligation is applied to interest rates R1 and R2 with net rate R_net, what ratio is obtained?
Back: The ratio of principal investments ($P_1 : P_2$).

---
Front: If a mixture of liquids A and B in ratio 7 : 5 has 9 liters drawn off, in what ratio are A and B removed?
Back: Strictly in the ratio 7 : 5.
