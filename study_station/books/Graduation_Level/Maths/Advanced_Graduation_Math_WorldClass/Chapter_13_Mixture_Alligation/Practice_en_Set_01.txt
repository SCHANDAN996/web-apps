Questions 1–25: Easy

1. (Easy) In what ratio must a grocer mix two varieties of pulses costing Rs 60 per kg and Rs 85 per kg respectively so as to get a mixture worth Rs 75 per kg?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 3 : 2  (C) 1 : 2  (D) 3 : 4
Answer: (A) 2 : 3
Solution: By the Rule of Alligation: Quantity of cheaper / Quantity of dearer = (85 - 75) / (75 - 60) = 10 / 15 = 2 / 3. The required value is 2 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

2. (Easy) A shopkeeper blends tea worth Rs 240 per kg with tea worth Rs 300 per kg to obtain a blend costing Rs 280 per kg. What is the ratio of dearer tea to cheaper tea in the blend?
(A) 1 : 2  (B) 2 : 1  (C) 3 : 2  (D) 2 : 3
Answer: (B) 2 : 1
Solution: By Alligation: Cheaper (240) to Dearer (300) ratio = (300 - 280) / (280 - 240) = 20 / 40 = 1 : 2. The question asks for the ratio of Dearer to Cheaper tea, which is the reciprocal: 2 : 1. The required value is 2 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

3. (Easy) In what ratio must water be mixed with milk costing Rs 48 per liter so that the resulting mixture is worth Rs 36 per liter (assume cost of water is Rs 0)?
(A) 1 : 4  (B) 1 : 2  (C) 1 : 3  (D) 2 : 3
Answer: (C) 1 : 3
Solution: By Alligation: Cost of water = 0, Cost of milk = 48, Mean cost = 36. Ratio of water to milk = (48 - 36) / (36 - 0) = 12 / 36 = 1 / 3, or 1 : 3. The required value is 1 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

4. (Easy) A container contains a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 7 : 3. What percentage of the mixture is pure milk?
(A) 65%  (B) 75%  (C) 60%  (D) 70%
Answer: (D) 70%
Solution: Total parts = 7 + 3 = 10. Fraction of milk = 7 / 10. Percentage of milk = (7 / 10) * 100% = 70%. The required value is 70%. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

5. (Easy) A solution of 40 liters contains acid and water in the ratio 3 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio of acid to water 3 : 2?
(A) 10 liters  (B) 12 liters  (C) 8 liters  (D) 15 liters
Answer: (A) 10 liters
Solution: Initial total = 40 liters. Acid = 40 * (3/4) = 30 liters, Water = 40 * (1/4) = 10 liters. If w liters of water is added: 30 / (10 + w) = 3 / 2 implies 10 + w = 20, so w = 10 liters. The required value is 10 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

6. (Easy) In what ratio should coffee powder costing Rs 320 per kg be blended with chicory costing Rs 120 per kg to make a blend worth Rs 200 per kg?
(A) 3 : 5  (B) 2 : 3  (C) 1 : 2  (D) 3 : 4
Answer: (B) 2 : 3
Solution: By Alligation: Coffee (320) and Chicory (120) with Mean (200). Ratio of Coffee to Chicory = (200 - 120) / (320 - 200) = 80 / 120 = 2 / 3, which is 2 : 3. The required value is 2 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: IBPS Clerk Pattern

7. (Easy) A merchant has 100 kg of sugar, part of which he sells at 7% profit and the rest at 17% profit. He gains 10% on the whole. How much sugar was sold at 7% profit?
(A) 60 kg  (B) 80 kg  (C) 70 kg  (D) 75 kg
Answer: (C) 70 kg
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of quantity at 7% to quantity at 17% = (17 - 10) / (10 - 7) = 7 / 3. Sugar sold at 7% profit = 100 * (7 / 10) = 70 kg. The required value is 70 kg. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

8. (Easy) A dishonest milkman professes to sell his milk at cost price but mixes water with it and thereby gains 25%. What is the ratio of water to milk in the mixture?
(A) 1 : 5  (B) 1 : 3  (C) 4 : 1  (D) 1 : 4
Answer: (D) 1 : 4
Solution: When selling at cost price, the entire profit is due to water added. Gain% = (Quantity of water / Quantity of milk) * 100 = 25%. Thus, Water / Milk = 25 / 100 = 1 / 4. The ratio of water to milk is 1 : 4. The required value is 1 : 4. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CPO Pattern

9. (Easy) Two alloys contain copper and tin in the ratios 1 : 3 and 2 : 5 respectively. If equal weights of both alloys are melted together, what will be the ratio of copper to tin in the resulting alloy?
(A) 15 : 41  (B) 13 : 43  (C) 11 : 45  (D) 17 : 39
Answer: (A) 15 : 41
Solution: Total parts are 4 and 7. LCM of 4 and 7 is 28. Let each alloy weigh 28 units. Alloy 1: Copper = 28 * (1/4) = 7, Tin = 21. Alloy 2: Copper = 28 * (2/7) = 8, Tin = 20. Combined Copper = 7 + 8 = 15, Combined Tin = 21 + 20 = 41. The ratio is 15 : 41. The required value is 15 : 41. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: CDS Pattern

10. (Easy) How many kilograms of wheat costing Rs 30 per kg must be mixed with 36 kg of wheat costing Rs 45 per kg so that the mixture is worth Rs 40 per kg?
(A) 20 kg  (B) 18 kg  (C) 24 kg  (D) 16 kg
Answer: (B) 18 kg
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of 30 Rs/kg to 45 Rs/kg wheat = (45 - 40) / (40 - 30) = 5 / 10 = 1 / 2. Let quantity of Rs 30 wheat be q. Then q / 36 = 1 / 2 implies q = 18 kg. The required value is 18 kg. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

11. (Easy) A mixture of 60 liters of wine and water contains 20% water. How much water must be added to make water 25% of the new mixture?
(A) 5 liters  (B) 6 liters  (C) 4 liters  (D) 8 liters
Answer: (C) 4 liters
Solution: Initial wine = 60 * 80% = 48 liters, Water = 12 liters. In the new mixture, wine remains 48 liters and represents 75% of total. Total new mixture = 48 / 0.75 = 64 liters. Water added = 64 - 60 = 4 liters. The required value is 4 liters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: IBPS Clerk Pattern

12. (Easy) The average salary of all staff in an office is Rs 15,000. The average salary of officers is Rs 45,000 and that of non-officers is Rs 10,000. If the number of officers is 15, find the number of non-officers.
(A) 75  (B) 100  (C) 80  (D) 90
Answer: (D) 90
Solution: By Alligation: Officers (45,000) and Non-officers (10,000) with Mean (15,000). Ratio of Officers to Non-officers = (15,000 - 10,000) / (45,000 - 15,000) = 5,000 / 30,000 = 1 / 6. Number of non-officers = 15 * 6 = 90. The required value is 90. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

13. (Easy) In what ratio should a 30% alcohol solution be mixed with a 50% alcohol solution to obtain a 45% alcohol solution?
(A) 1 : 3  (B) 1 : 4  (C) 2 : 3  (D) 3 : 4
Answer: (A) 1 : 3
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of 30% solution to 50% solution = (50 - 45) / (45 - 30) = 5 / 15 = 1 / 3, which is 1 : 3. The required value is 1 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

14. (Easy) A sum of Rs 4,000 is lent out in two parts, one at 8% simple interest and the other at 10% simple interest. If the annual total interest is Rs 352, find the sum lent at 8%.
(A) Rs 2,000  (B) Rs 2,400  (C) Rs 1,600  (D) Rs 2,200
Answer: (B) Rs 2,400
Solution: Overall interest rate = (352 / 4,000) * 100 = 8.8%. By Alligation: Ratio of part at 8% to part at 10% = (10 - 8.8) / (8.8 - 8) = 1.2 / 0.8 = 3 / 2. Sum at 8% = 4,000 * (3 / 5) = Rs 2,400. The required value is Rs 2,400. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

15. (Easy) A container holds 50 liters of pure milk. From this, 10 liters of milk is taken out and replaced with water. What is the ratio of milk to water in the container now?
(A) 3 : 1  (B) 5 : 1  (C) 4 : 1  (D) 2 : 1
Answer: (C) 4 : 1
Solution: Remaining milk = 50 - 10 = 40 liters. Water added = 10 liters. Ratio of milk to water = 40 : 10 = 4 : 1. The required value is 4 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

16. (Easy) A vessel contains 90 liters of mixture of milk and water in the ratio 5 : 1. How much water must be added to the mixture so that the ratio of milk to water becomes 3 : 1?
(A) 8 liters  (B) 12 liters  (C) 15 liters  (D) 10 liters
Answer: (D) 10 liters
Solution: Total = 90 liters. Milk = 90 * (5/6) = 75 liters, Water = 15 liters. For new ratio 3 : 1, Water required = 75 / 3 = 25 liters. Water to be added = 25 - 15 = 10 liters. The required value is 10 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

17. (Easy) In a zoo, there are rabbits and pigeons. If heads are counted, there are 200, and if legs are counted, there are 560. How many pigeons are there in the zoo?
(A) 120  (B) 140  (C) 80  (D) 110
Answer: (A) 120
Solution: Pigeons have 2 legs, rabbits have 4 legs. If all 200 were rabbits, legs = 800. If all were pigeons, legs = 400. Mean legs = 560. Ratio of pigeons to rabbits = (800 - 560) / (560 - 400) = 240 / 160 = 3 / 2. Number of pigeons = 200 * (3 / 5) = 120. The required value is 120. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

18. (Easy) Gold is 19 times as heavy as water and copper is 9 times as heavy as water. In what ratio should these metals be mixed so that the mixture may be 15 times as heavy as water?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 3 : 2  (C) 1 : 2  (D) 3 : 4
Answer: (B) 3 : 2
Solution: By Alligation: Gold (19) and Copper (9) with Mean (15). Ratio of Gold to Copper = (15 - 9) / (19 - 15) = 6 / 4 = 3 / 2, or 3 : 2. The required value is 3 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: CDS Pattern

19. (Easy) A trader mixes 26 kg of rice at Rs 20 per kg with 30 kg of rice of another variety at Rs 36 per kg. What is the cost price per kg of the mixture?
(A) Rs 27.50  (B) Rs 29.20  (C) Rs 28.57  (D) Rs 28.00
Answer: (C) Rs 28.57
Solution: Total cost = (26 * 20) + (30 * 36) = 520 + 1,080 = Rs 1,600. Total quantity = 26 + 30 = 56 kg. Cost per kg = 1,600 / 56 = 200 / 7 = Rs 28.57 per kg. The required value is Rs 28.57. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

20. (Easy) A merchant has 50 kg of pulses. He sells a part at 14% profit and the remaining at 6% loss. On the whole, he gains 4%. What quantity did he sell at 14% profit?
(A) 30 kg  (B) 20 kg  (C) 35 kg  (D) 25 kg
Answer: (D) 25 kg
Solution: By Alligation: Note that 6% loss is -6. Ratio = (4 - (-6)) / (14 - 4) = 10 / 10 = 1 : 1. Quantity at 14% profit = 50 * (1 / 2) = 25 kg. The required value is 25 kg. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: IBPS PO Prelims Pattern

Answer: (A) 1 : 3
Solution: Cost of water = 0, Spirit = 60, Mixture = 45. Ratio of water to spirit = (60 - 45) / (45 - 0) = 15 / 45 = 1 / 3, which is 1 : 3. The required value is 1 : 3. Hence, the correct option is (A).

22. (Easy) Two vessels contain milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2 and 7 : 3 respectively. If both vessels contain equal volumes and are mixed into a third vessel, what will be the ratio of milk to water in the third vessel?
(A) 11 : 7  (B) 13 : 7  (C) 12 : 7  (D) 14 : 9
Answer: (B) 13 : 7
Solution: Total parts are 5 and 10. LCM = 10. Let volume of each vessel be 10 units. Vessel 1: Milk = 10 * (3/5) = 6, Water = 4. Vessel 2: Milk = 10 * (7/10) = 7, Water = 3. Combined Milk = 6 + 7 = 13, Combined Water = 4 + 3 = 7. The ratio is 13 : 7. The required value is 13 : 7. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

23. (Easy) A mixture contains 80% acid and the rest water. What part of the mixture should be replaced with water so that the resulting mixture contains 50% acid?
(A) 1/4  (B) 1/2  (C) 3/8  (D) 2/5
Answer: (C) 3/8
Solution: By Alligation: Solution acid = 80%, Pure water acid = 0%, Target acid = 50%. Ratio of original solution retained to water added = (50 - 0) / (80 - 50) = 50 / 30 = 5 / 3. Total parts = 5 + 3 = 8. Part of mixture replaced = 3 / 8. The required value is 3/8. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

24. (Easy) The average weight of boys in a class is 50 kg and that of girls is 40 kg. If the average weight of the whole class is 46 kg, what is the ratio of boys to girls in the class?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 3 : 4  (C) 4 : 3  (D) 3 : 2
Answer: (D) 3 : 2
Solution: By Alligation: Boys (50) and Girls (40) with Mean (46). Ratio of boys to girls = (46 - 40) / (50 - 46) = 6 / 4 = 3 / 2. The required value is 3 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: State PCS Pattern

25. (Easy) A container contains 40 liters of milk. From this container 4 liters of milk was taken out and replaced by water. This process was repeated once more. How much milk is now contained by the container?
(A) 31.5 liters  (B) 32.4 liters  (C) 30.8 liters  (D) 33.2 liters
Answer: (B) 32.4 liters
Solution: Formula: Remaining milk = x * (1 - y/x)^n. Here x = 40, y = 4, n = 2. Remaining milk = 40 * (1 - 4/40)^2 = 40 * (9/10)^2 = 40 * (81/100) = 32.4 liters. The required value is 32.4 liters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern
