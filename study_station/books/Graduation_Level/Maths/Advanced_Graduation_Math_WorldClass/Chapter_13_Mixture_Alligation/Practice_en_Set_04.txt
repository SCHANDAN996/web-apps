Questions 76–100: Hard

76. (Hard) Three vessels whose capacities are in the ratio 3 : 2 : 1 are completely filled with milk and water mixtures in the ratios 5 : 2, 4 : 1, and 4 : 1 respectively. If the contents of all three vessels are emptied into a large container, what will be the ratio of milk to water in the container?
(A) 53 : 17  (B) 51 : 19  (C) 49 : 21  (D) 55 : 15
Answer: (A) 53 : 17
Solution: Sum of parts are 7, 5, 5. LCM = 35. Scale capacities 3, 2, 1 by 35: Volume 1 = 105, Volume 2 = 70, Volume 3 = 35. In Vessel 1: Milk = 105 * (5/7) = 75, Water = 30. In Vessel 2: Milk = 70 * (4/5) = 56, Water = 14. In Vessel 3: Milk = 35 * (4/5) = 28, Water = 7. Total Milk = 75 + 56 + 28 = 159. Total Water = 30 + 14 + 7 = 51. Ratio = 159 : 51 = 53 : 17. The required value is 53 : 17. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

77. (Hard) A milkman mixes 25% water (at zero cost) into pure milk and marks up the price of the mixture by 20% above the cost price of pure milk. What is his overall profit percentage?
(A) 45%  (B) 50%  (C) 52%  (D) 48%
Answer: (B) 50%
Solution: Let the cost price of 100 liters of pure milk be Rs 100 (Rs 1/L). Adding 25% water yields 125 liters of mixture. The marked selling price per liter is Rs 1.20. Total selling price = 125 * 1.20 = Rs 150. Total profit = 150 - 100 = Rs 50. Profit percentage = (50 / 100) * 100 = 50%. The required value is 50%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

78. (Hard) A container contains 90 liters of pure milk. 18 liters is taken out and replaced with water. Then 30 liters of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water. How much pure milk remains in the container?
(A) 45 liters  (B) 50 liters  (C) 48 liters  (D) 52 liters
Answer: (C) 48 liters
Solution: After 1st replacement: Fraction left = (90 - 18)/90 = 72/90 = 4/5. Milk remaining = 90 * (4/5) = 72 liters. After 2nd replacement: Fraction left = (90 - 30)/90 = 60/90 = 2/3. Pure milk remaining = 72 * (2/3) = 48 liters. The required value is 48 liters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

79. (Hard) In what ratio must three varieties of wheat costing Rs 24, Rs 30, and Rs 42 per kg be mixed to produce a mixture worth Rs 32 per kg?
(A) 2 : 2 : 3  (B) 1 : 2 : 1  (C) 2 : 1 : 1  (D) 1 : 1 : 1
Answer: (D) 1 : 1 : 1
Solution: Mean price = 32. Cheaper varieties: 24, 30. Dearer variety: 42. Pair (24, 42): (42 - 32) : (32 - 24) = 10 : 8 = 5 : 4. Pair (30, 42): (42 - 32) : (32 - 30) = 10 : 2 = 5 : 1. Combining both pairs: Variety at 24 = 5, Variety at 30 = 5, Variety at 42 = 4 + 1 = 5. The ratio is 5 : 5 : 5 = 1 : 1 : 1. The required value is 1 : 1 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: CDS Pattern

80. (Hard) A vessel contains 120 liters of a mixture of milk and water containing 20% water. How many liters of water must be added so that the water content becomes 36% of the mixture?
(A) 30 liters  (B) 25 liters  (C) 35 liters  (D) 40 liters
Answer: (A) 30 liters
Solution: Initial milk = 120 * 0.80 = 96 liters, water = 24 liters. In the new mixture, milk represents 100% - 36% = 64% of total. Total volume = 96 / 0.64 = 150 liters. Water to be added = 150 - 120 = 30 liters. The required value is 30 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

81. (Hard) Two alloys of gold and copper are prepared by mixing metals in the proportions 7 : 2 and 7 : 11 respectively. If equal quantities of these two alloys are melted to form a third alloy Alloy C, what is the proportion of gold and copper in Alloy C?
(A) 5 : 7  (B) 7 : 5  (C) 3 : 4  (D) 4 : 3
Answer: (B) 7 : 5
Solution: Parts sums are 9 and 18. LCM = 18. Scale alloy 1 to 18 units: Gold = 14, Copper = 4. Alloy 2 has 18 units: Gold = 7, Copper = 11. Combined Gold = 14 + 7 = 21, Combined Copper = 4 + 11 = 15. Ratio in Alloy C = 21 : 15 = 7 : 5. The required value is 7 : 5. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

82. (Hard) A trader buys two types of rice: Type A at Rs 40 per kg and Type B at Rs 60 per kg. In what ratio must he mix them so that by selling the mixture at Rs 66 per kg, he earns a profit of 32%?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 3 : 2  (C) 1 : 1  (D) 1 : 2
Answer: (C) 1 : 1
Solution: Mean CP of mixture = 66 / 1.32 = Rs 50 per kg. By Alligation: Ratio of Type A to Type B = (60 - 50) / (50 - 40) = 10 / 10 = 1 : 1. The required value is 1 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

83. (Hard) A jar was full of honey. A person drew out 25% of the honey from the jar and replaced it with sugar syrup. He repeated this process 3 more times (total 4 times). If there were 405 grams of pure honey left in the jar, what was the initial quantity of honey in the jar?
(A) 1,200 grams  (B) 1,500 grams  (C) 1,440 grams  (D) 1,280 grams
Answer: (D) 1,280 grams
Solution: Fraction of honey remaining after 4 operations = (1 - 1/4)^4 = (3/4)^4 = 81 / 256. Thus, Initial quantity * (81 / 256) = 405 grams. Initial quantity = 405 * (256 / 81) = 5 * 256 = 1,280 grams. The required value is 1,280 grams. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

84. (Hard) A can contains a mixture of two liquids Liquid P and Liquid Q in the ratio 5 : 3. When 16 liters of mixture is drawn off and the can is filled with Liquid Q, the ratio of P and Q becomes 3 : 5. How many liters of Liquid P were in the can initially?
(A) 25 liters  (B) 30 liters  (C) 20 liters  (D) 35 liters
Answer: (A) 25 liters
Solution: Let initial quantities be 5x and 3x (total 8x). Quantity of P removed in 16 L mixture = 16 * (5/8) = 10 liters. Pure P remaining = 5x - 10. In final mixture (total volume 8x), P is 3/8 of 8x = 3x. Equating: 5x - 10 = 3x implies 2x = 10, so x = 5. Initial quantity of P = 5 * 5 = 25 liters. The required value is 25 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

85. (Hard) A motorist travels for 6 hours. During the first part of the journey, his average speed was 40 km/h, and during the second part, his average speed was 60 km/h. If his average speed for the entire journey was 52 km/h, find the distance he covered at 60 km/h.
(A) 180 km  (B) 216 km  (C) 144 km  (D) 192 km
Answer: (B) 216 km
Solution: Alligation on speed gives ratio of times: (60 - 52) / (52 - 40) = 8 / 12 = 2 / 3. Time spent at 60 km/h = 6 * (3 / 5) = 3.6 hours. Distance covered at 60 km/h = 60 * 3.6 = 216 km. The required value is 216 km. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

86. (Hard) A container has 100 liters of pure alcohol. A person replaces 10 liters of alcohol with water, then replaces 20 liters of the mixture with water, and finally replaces 30 liters of the mixture with water. What is the remaining amount of pure alcohol in the container?
(A) 48.6 liters  (B) 52.8 liters  (C) 50.4 liters  (D) 45.0 liters
Answer: (C) 50.4 liters
Solution: Formula for variable removals: Remaining liquid = 100 * (1 - 10/100) * (1 - 20/100) * (1 - 30/100) = 100 * 0.90 * 0.80 * 0.70 = 50.4 liters. The required value is 50.4 liters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: CDS Pattern

87. (Hard) A sum of Rs 15,000 is invested in two schemes. Scheme 1 gives 8% per annum compound interest (compounded annually) for 2 years, and Scheme 2 gives 10% per annum simple interest for 2 years. If the total interest earned after 2 years is Rs 2,748, how much was invested in Scheme 1?
(A) Rs 8,000  (B) Rs 6,500  (C) Rs 7,000  (D) Rs 7,500
Answer: (D) Rs 7,500
Solution: Effective 2-year CI rate for Scheme 1 = 8 + 8 + (8*8)/100 = 16.64%. 2-year SI rate for Scheme 2 = 2 * 10 = 20%. Overall return = (2,748 / 15,000) * 100 = 18.32%. By Alligation: Ratio = (20 - 18.32) / (18.32 - 16.64) = 1.68 / 1.68 = 1 : 1. Amount invested in Scheme 1 = 15,000 / 2 = Rs 7,500. The required value is Rs 7,500. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: IBPS PO Mains Pattern

88. (Hard) An alloy contains 40% zinc and 60% copper. Another alloy contains 25% zinc and 75% copper. If both alloys are mixed to obtain a new alloy with 30% zinc, in what ratio were the two alloys mixed?
(A) 1 : 2  (B) 2 : 1  (C) 2 : 3  (D) 3 : 2
Answer: (A) 1 : 2
Solution: By Alligation on zinc percentage: Ratio of first alloy to second alloy = (30 - 25) / (40 - 30) = 5 / 10 = 1 / 2, or 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).

89. (Hard) A milkman buys milk at Rs 50 per liter. He mixes water in it such that water constitutes 20% of the mixture. If he sells the mixture at Rs 55 per liter, find his profit percentage.
(A) 35.0%  (B) 37.5%  (C) 40.0%  (D) 32.5%
Answer: (B) 37.5%
Solution: Consider 100 liters of mixture: it contains 80 liters of milk and 20 liters of water. Total CP = 80 * 50 = Rs 4,000. Total SP = 100 * 55 = Rs 5,500. Profit = 5,500 - 4,000 = Rs 1,500. Profit percentage = (1,500 / 4,000) * 100 = 37.5%. The required value is 37.5%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

90. (Hard) A vessel contains 60 liters of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1. How much of this mixture must be withdrawn and replaced with water so that the resulting mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 1 : 1?
(A) 12 liters  (B) 18 liters  (C) 15 liters  (D) 20 liters
Answer: (C) 15 liters
Solution: Initial: Milk = 40 liters, Water = 20 liters. Target: In 60 liters of 1 : 1 mixture, Milk = 30 liters. Milk must reduce by 40 - 30 = 10 liters. Each liter of mixture removed contains 2/3 liter of milk. Quantity of mixture to withdraw = 10 / (2/3) = 15 liters. The required value is 15 liters. Hence, the correct option is (C).

92. (Hard) A vessel is completely filled with pure milk. 15 liters of milk is replaced with water. This process is repeated once more. The final ratio of milk to water is 9 : 16. What was the capacity of the vessel?
(A) 37.5 liters  (B) 40.0 liters  (C) 35.0 liters  (D) 45.0 liters
Answer: (A) 37.5 liters
Solution: Fraction of milk left = 9 / (9 + 16) = 9 / 25. Thus (1 - 15/x)^2 = (3/5)^2 implies 1 - 15/x = 3/5, giving 15/x = 2/5, so 2x = 75, which yields x = 37.5 liters. The required value is 37.5 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

93. (Hard) A chemist mixes two solutions of acid of concentrations 20% and 50% in the ratio 2 : 3. What is the concentration of the resulting mixture?
(A) 35%  (B) 38%  (C) 36%  (D) 40%
Answer: (B) 38%
Solution: Weighted average concentration = (2 * 20 + 3 * 50) / (2 + 3) = (40 + 150) / 5 = 190 / 5 = 38%. The required value is 38%. Hence, the correct option is (B).

94. (Hard) A merchant has two varieties of tea: one costing Rs 210 per kg and the other costing Rs 280 per kg. He mixes them in the ratio 4 : 3 and sells the mixture at Rs 260 per kg. What is his profit percentage?
(A) 7.50%  (B) 9.09%  (C) 8.33%  (D) 10.00%
Answer: (C) 8.33%
Solution: CP of mixture = (4 * 210 + 3 * 280) / 7 = (840 + 840) / 7 = 1,680 / 7 = Rs 240 per kg. SP = Rs 260 per kg. Profit% = ((260 - 240) / 240) * 100 = (20 / 240) * 100 = 8.33%. The required value is 8.33%. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

95. (Hard) In what ratio must water be mixed with concentrated acid costing Rs 120 per liter so that selling the mixture at Rs 100 per liter gives a profit of 25%?
(A) 1 : 3  (B) 2 : 3  (C) 3 : 4  (D) 1 : 2
Answer: (D) 1 : 2
Solution: Mean CP of mixture = 100 / 1.25 = Rs 80 per liter. Water cost = 0, Acid cost = 120. By Alligation: Ratio of water to acid = (120 - 80) / (80 - 0) = 40 / 80 = 1 / 2, or 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

97. (Hard) A seller blends two qualities of rice: one priced at Rs 32 per kg and another at Rs 45 per kg. If 40 kg of the first quality is mixed with 60 kg of the second quality, what should be the selling price per kg of the mixture to yield a profit of 20%?

99. (Hard) Two vessels contain mixtures of milk and water. Vessel 1 has 75% milk and Vessel 2 has 45% milk. How many liters from Vessel 1 must be mixed with 15 liters from Vessel 2 to obtain a mixture with 65% milk?
(A) 25 liters  (B) 20 liters  (C) 35 liters  (D) 30 liters
Answer: (D) 30 liters
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of Vessel 1 to Vessel 2 = (65 - 45) / (75 - 65) = 20 / 10 = 2 / 1. Vessel 2 quantity is 15 liters (1 part), so Vessel 1 quantity (2 parts) = 2 * 15 = 30 liters. The required value is 30 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

100. (Hard) A trader mixes 3 varieties of oil costing Rs 100 per liter, Rs 120 per liter, and Rs 150 per liter in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. What is the average cost price per liter of the mixture?
(A) Rs 128  (B) Rs 131  (C) Rs 135  (D) Rs 130
Answer: (B) Rs 131
Solution: Weighted average cost = (2 * 100 + 3 * 120 + 5 * 150) / (2 + 3 + 5) = (200 + 360 + 750) / 10 = 1,310 / 10 = Rs 131 per liter. The required value is Rs 131. Hence, the correct option is (B).
