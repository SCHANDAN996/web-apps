Questions 51–75: Medium

51. (Medium) A container contains 80 liters of milk. From this container, 8 liters of milk is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated 2 more times. How much milk is left in the container?
(A) 58.32 liters  (B) 56.40 liters  (C) 60.00 liters  (D) 54.88 liters
Answer: (A) 58.32 liters
Solution: Total operations n = 3. Remaining milk = 80 * (1 - 8/80)^3 = 80 * (9/10)^3 = 80 * (729/1000) = 58.32 liters. The required value is 58.32 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

52. (Medium) Two containers Container P and Container Q contain milk and water in the ratios 4 : 1 and 3 : 2 respectively. In what ratio should the contents of containers P and Q be mixed so that the resulting mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7 : 3?
(A) 2 : 1  (B) 1 : 1  (C) 3 : 2  (D) 1 : 2
Answer: (B) 1 : 1
Solution: Milk fractions: P = 4/5 = 8/10, Q = 3/5 = 6/10. Mean = 7/10. By Alligation on milk: Ratio of P to Q = (7/10 - 6/10) / (8/10 - 7/10) = (1/10) / (1/10) = 1 : 1. The required value is 1 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

53. (Medium) A merchant has 1,000 kg of sugar, part of which he sells at 8% profit and the rest at 18% profit. He gains 14% on the whole. What is the quantity sold at 18% profit?
(A) 400 kg  (B) 500 kg  (C) 600 kg  (D) 650 kg
Answer: (C) 600 kg
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of quantity at 8% to quantity at 18% = (18 - 14) / (14 - 8) = 4 / 6 = 2 / 3. Quantity at 18% profit = 1,000 * (3 / 5) = 600 kg. The required value is 600 kg. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: IBPS PO Prelims Pattern

54. (Medium) A vessel contains 100 liters of pure wine. 20 liters of wine is removed and replaced with water. Again 20 liters of the mixture is removed and replaced with water. What is the ratio of wine to water in the final mixture?
(A) 16 : 25  (B) 4 : 1  (C) 9 : 16  (D) 16 : 9
Answer: (D) 16 : 9
Solution: Remaining wine = 100 * (1 - 20/100)^2 = 100 * (4/5)^2 = 64 liters. Water present = 100 - 64 = 36 liters. Ratio of wine to water = 64 : 36 = 16 : 9. The required value is 16 : 9. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

55. (Medium) A sum of Rs 6,000 is lent partly at 5% and partly at 7% per annum simple interest. If the total annual interest earned is Rs 340, what amount was lent at 5%?
(A) Rs 4,000  (B) Rs 3,500  (C) Rs 2,000  (D) Rs 4,500
Answer: (A) Rs 4,000
Solution: Overall interest rate = (340 / 6,000) * 100 = 17/3 %. By Alligation: Ratio of sum at 5% to sum at 7% = (7 - 17/3) / (17/3 - 5) = (4/3) / (2/3) = 2 / 1. Sum lent at 5% = 6,000 * (2 / 3) = Rs 4,000. The required value is Rs 4,000. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

56. (Medium) The population of a town was 50,000. In one year, male population increased by 10% and female population increased by 15%, so the total population became 56,000. What was the female population of the town initially?
(A) 25,000  (B) 20,000  (C) 30,000  (D) 18,000
Answer: (B) 20,000
Solution: Net percentage increase = (6,000 / 50,000) * 100 = 12%. By Alligation: Ratio of males to females = (15 - 12) / (12 - 10) = 3 / 2. Female population initially = 50,000 * (2 / 5) = 20,000. The required value is 20,000. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

57. (Medium) Two vessels contain spirit and water in the ratio 7 : 5 and 5 : 3 respectively. If the contents of both vessels are mixed in the ratio 3 : 2, what will be the ratio of spirit to water in the new mixture?
(A) 7 : 5  (B) 5 : 4  (C) 3 : 2  (D) 4 : 3
Answer: (C) 3 : 2
Solution: Sum of parts are 12 and 8. LCM = 24. Let vessel 1 volume be 3 * 24 = 72, vessel 2 volume be 2 * 24 = 48. In vessel 1: Spirit = 42, Water = 30. In vessel 2: Spirit = 30, Water = 18. Combined: Spirit = 42 + 30 = 72, Water = 30 + 18 = 48. Ratio = 72 : 48 = 3 : 2. The required value is 3 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: CDS Pattern

58. (Medium) In what ratio should water and 90% pure wine be mixed to produce a mixture of 60% wine concentration?
(A) 2 : 3  (B) 2 : 1  (C) 3 : 2  (D) 1 : 2
Answer: (D) 1 : 2
Solution: Water has 0% wine. By Alligation: Ratio of water to 90% wine = (90 - 60) / (60 - 0) = 30 / 60 = 1 / 2, or 1 : 2. The required value is 1 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: State PCS Pattern

59. (Medium) A milkman mixes 20 liters of water with 80 liters of milk. He sells one-fourth of this mixture and adds 15 liters of water to the remaining mixture. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(A) 2 : 1  (B) 3 : 1  (C) 5 : 2  (D) 3 : 2
Answer: (A) 2 : 1
Solution: Initial: 80 milk, 20 water (total 100 liters, ratio 4 : 1). Selling 1/4 leaves 75 liters: milk = 60 liters, water = 15 liters. Adding 15 liters water: water becomes 15 + 15 = 30 liters. Final ratio of milk to water = 60 : 30 = 2 : 1. The required value is 2 : 1. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

60. (Medium) A trader buys 500 meters of cloth. He sells 200 meters at 10% profit and the remaining 300 meters at 25% profit. What is his overall profit percentage on the transaction?
(A) 18%  (B) 19%  (C) 20%  (D) 17.5%
Answer: (B) 19%
Solution: Weighted average profit = (200 * 10 + 300 * 25) / 500 = (2,000 + 7,500) / 500 = 9,500 / 500 = 19%. The required value is 19%. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

61. (Medium) From a container full of pure milk, 9 liters is taken out and replaced with water. This operation is performed one more time. If the ratio of milk to water now is 16 : 9, what was the initial capacity of the container?
(A) 40 liters  (B) 50 liters  (C) 45 liters  (D) 36 liters
Answer: (C) 45 liters
Solution: Fraction of milk left / Total volume = 16 / (16 + 9) = 16 / 25. Thus (1 - 9/x)^2 = (4/5)^2 implies 1 - 9/x = 4/5, so 9/x = 1/5, giving x = 45 liters. The required value is 45 liters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

62. (Medium) A vessel contains a mixture of two liquids P and Q in the ratio 7 : 5. When 9 liters of mixture are drawn off and the vessel is filled with liquid Q, the ratio of P and Q becomes 7 : 9. How many liters of liquid P were in the vessel initially?
(A) 28 liters  (B) 24 liters  (C) 18 liters  (D) 21 liters
Answer: (D) 21 liters
Solution: Let initial quantities be 7x and 5x. When 9 liters is removed, quantity of P removed is 9 * (7/12) = 21/4 liters. Since only liquid Q is added, P in final mixture is 7x - 21/4. In final mixture, P is 7/16 of total volume (12x): 7x - 21/4 = (7/16) * 12x = 21x/4. Thus 7x - 21x/4 = 21/4, so 7x/4 = 21/4, which gives x = 3. Initial liquid P = 7 * 3 = 21 liters. The required value is 21 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

63. (Medium) A chemist has 30 liters of a 10% hydrochloric acid solution. How many liters of a 30% hydrochloric acid solution must be added to make a 15% solution?
(A) 10 liters  (B) 12 liters  (C) 15 liters  (D) 8 liters
Answer: (A) 10 liters
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of 10% solution to 30% solution = (30 - 15) / (15 - 10) = 15 / 5 = 3 / 1. Since 10% solution is 30 liters (3 parts), 1 part = 10 liters. The required value is 10 liters. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: RRB NTPC Pattern

64. (Medium) A grocer bought 80 kg of sugar at Rs 30 per kg and mixed it with 120 kg of sugar at Rs 35 per kg. At what price per kg should he sell the mixture to gain 16%?
(A) Rs 37.50  (B) Rs 38.28  (C) Rs 39.10  (D) Rs 36.80
Answer: (B) Rs 38.28
Solution: CP of mixture = (80 * 30 + 120 * 35) / 200 = (2,400 + 4,200) / 200 = 6,600 / 200 = Rs 33 per kg. Selling price for 16% gain = 33 * 1.16 = Rs 38.28 per kg. The required value is Rs 38.28. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

65. (Medium) A mixture of 40 liters of milk and water contains 10% water. How much water should be added to this so that water may be 20% in the new mixture?
(A) 4 liters  (B) 6 liters  (C) 5 liters  (D) 8 liters
Answer: (C) 5 liters
Solution: Initial milk = 40 * 0.90 = 36 liters. In the new mixture, milk represents 80% of the total. New total = 36 / 0.80 = 45 liters. Water to be added = 45 - 40 = 5 liters. The required value is 5 liters. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

66. (Medium) In a 50-liter mixture of glycerin and water, the percentage of glycerin is 80%. What quantity of water should be added to make it 50%?
(A) 25 liters  (B) 20 liters  (C) 35 liters  (D) 30 liters
Answer: (D) 30 liters
Solution: Glycerin quantity = 50 * 0.80 = 40 liters. In the target mixture, glycerin is 50%, meaning the total volume is 40 / 0.50 = 80 liters. Water to add = 80 - 50 = 30 liters. The required value is 30 liters. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

67. (Medium) A merchant mixes two varieties of pulses costing Rs 50 per kg and Rs 75 per kg. If the resulting mixture is sold at Rs 72 per kg with a 20% profit, what is the ratio of the two varieties in the mixture?
(A) 3 : 2  (B) 2 : 3  (C) 1 : 2  (D) 3 : 4
Answer: (A) 3 : 2
Solution: Mean CP of mixture = 72 / 1.20 = Rs 60 per kg. By Alligation: Ratio of cheaper to dearer = (75 - 60) / (60 - 50) = 15 / 10 = 3 : 2. The required value is 3 : 2. Hence, the correct option is (A).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

68. (Medium) A merchant sold 100 kg of rice. He sold a portion at 10% loss and the rest at 15% profit, earning an overall profit of 5%. What quantity was sold at 10% loss?
(A) 50 kg  (B) 40 kg  (C) 60 kg  (D) 45 kg
Answer: (B) 40 kg
Solution: Loss is -10% and profit is +15%. Mean is +5%. By Alligation: Ratio of quantity at loss to quantity at profit = (15 - 5) / (5 - (-10)) = 10 / 15 = 2 / 3. Quantity at 10% loss = 100 * (2 / 5) = 40 kg. The required value is 40 kg. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-2 Pattern

69. (Medium) Two glasses of equal volume are filled with a mixture of alcohol and water in the ratios 2 : 1 and 3 : 2 respectively. If both glasses are emptied into a vessel, what will be the ratio of alcohol to water in the vessel?
(A) 17 : 13  (B) 13 : 17  (C) 19 : 11  (D) 11 : 19
Answer: (C) 19 : 11
Solution: Sum of parts are 3 and 5. Equal volume = LCM(3, 5) = 15 units each. Glass 1: Alcohol = 10, Water = 5. Glass 2: Alcohol = 9, Water = 6. Combined: Alcohol = 19, Water = 11. Ratio = 19 : 11. The required value is 19 : 11. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: CDS Pattern

70. (Medium) How many kilograms of salt costing Rs 28 per kg must be mixed with 60 kg of salt costing Rs 16 per kg to make a mixture worth Rs 20 per kg?
(A) 25 kg  (B) 20 kg  (C) 35 kg  (D) 30 kg
Answer: (D) 30 kg
Solution: By Alligation: Ratio of salt at Rs 28 to salt at Rs 16 = (20 - 16) / (28 - 20) = 4 / 8 = 1 / 2. Since 60 kg is the quantity of Rs 16 salt (2 parts), 1 part = 30 kg. The required value is 30 kg. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

72. (Medium) A vessel contains 63 liters of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 4 : 3. If 7 liters of water is added to the mixture, what will be the new ratio of milk to water?
(A) 16 : 15  (B) 18 : 17  (C) 17 : 18  (D) 15 : 14
Answer: (B) 18 : 17
Solution: Initial milk = 63 * (4/7) = 36 liters, water = 27 liters. Adding 7 liters of water gives water = 27 + 7 = 34 liters. Ratio of milk to water = 36 : 34 = 18 : 17. The required value is 18 : 17. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC MTS Pattern

73. (Medium) A man travels 360 km in 8 hours partly by car at 60 km/h and partly by bicycle at 20 km/h. How many hours did he spend traveling by car?
(A) 4 hours  (B) 3 hours  (C) 5 hours  (D) 6 hours
Answer: (C) 5 hours
Solution: Average speed = 360 / 8 = 45 km/h. By Alligation on speed: Ratio of time by car to bicycle = (45 - 20) / (60 - 45) = 25 / 15 = 5 / 3. Total time = 8 hours, so time by car = 8 * (5 / 8) = 5 hours. The required value is 5 hours. Hence, the correct option is (C).
Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 Pattern

74. (Medium) An alloy contains copper, zinc, and nickel in the ratio 5 : 3 : 2. If 10 kg of nickel is added to 100 kg of this alloy, what will be the new percentage of nickel in the alloy?
(A) 25.00%  (B) 28.57%  (C) 30.00%  (D) 27.27%
Answer: (D) 27.27%
Solution: In 100 kg: Nickel = 100 * (2/10) = 20 kg. Adding 10 kg nickel gives 30 kg nickel in a total weight of 110 kg. Percentage of nickel = (30 / 110) * 100% = 27.27%. The required value is 27.27%. Hence, the correct option is (D).
Source: CDS Pattern

75. (Medium) A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 1. On adding 5 liters of water, the ratio becomes 5 : 2. What was the initial quantity of milk in the mixture?
(A) 20 liters  (B) 25 liters  (C) 30 liters  (D) 15 liters
Answer: (B) 25 liters
Solution: Let milk be 5x and water be x. When 5 liters water is added: 5x / (x + 5) = 5 / 2. This implies 2x = x + 5, so x = 5. Initial milk = 5x = 25 liters. The required value is 25 liters. Hence, the correct option is (B).
Source: SSC CGL Pattern
