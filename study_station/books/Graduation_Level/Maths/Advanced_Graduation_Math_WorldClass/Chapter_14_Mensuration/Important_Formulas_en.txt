# Important Formulas — Mensuration

## 1. Plane Figures (2D)

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Area of Triangle | ½ × base × height | When base & height known |
| Heron's Formula | √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)], s = (a+b+c)/2 | When all 3 sides known |
| Area of Equilateral Triangle | (√3/4) a² | Side a given |
| Height of Equilateral Triangle | (√3/2) a | Also used in hexagon problems |
| Area of Isosceles Triangle | (b/4)√(4a² − b²) | a = equal sides, b = base |
| Area of Square | a² ; also d²/2 | d = diagonal |
| Diagonal of Square | a√2 | Quick link between side & diagonal |
| Area of Rectangle | l × b | Perimeter = 2(l+b) |
| Diagonal of Rectangle | √(l² + b²) | Pythagoras |
| Area of Parallelogram | base × height | Height ⊥ base |
| Area of Rhombus | ½ × d₁ × d₂ | Diagonals bisect at 90° |
| Side of Rhombus | ½√(d₁² + d₂²) | From half-diagonals |
| Area of Trapezium | ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height | Height = distance between parallels |
| Area of Quadrilateral | ½ × d × (h₁ + h₂) | d = diagonal, h₁,h₂ = offsets |
| Area of Regular Hexagon | (3√3/2) a² | = 6 equilateral triangles |
| Area of Regular Octagon | 2(1+√2) a² | Rare, but asked in CAT |
| Circumference of Circle | 2πr | π ≈ 22/7 or 3.14 |
| Area of Circle | πr² | Base for ring, sector, segment |
| Area of Ring (Annulus) | π(R² − r²) | R = outer, r = inner radius |
| Area of Sector | (θ/360°) × πr² | θ in degrees |
| Length of Arc | (θ/360°) × 2πr | Same fraction of circumference |
| Area of Segment | Area of sector − area of triangle | Minor segment |
| Perimeter of Semicircle | πr + 2r | Don't forget the diameter! |
| Area of Semicircle | ½πr² | Half of circle |

## 2. Solid Figures (3D)

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Volume of Cuboid | l × b × h | Basic brick shape |
| Surface Area of Cuboid (TSA) | 2(lb + bh + hl) | All 6 faces |
| LSA of Cuboid | 2h(l + b) | 4 walls only (room painting) |
| Diagonal of Cuboid | √(l² + b² + h²) | Longest rod inside a room |
| Volume of Cube | a³ | a = edge |
| TSA of Cube | 6a² | 6 equal faces |
| LSA of Cube | 4a² | 4 side faces |
| Diagonal of Cube | a√3 | Body diagonal |
| Volume of Cylinder | πr²h | Base area × height |
| CSA of Cylinder | 2πrh | Curved part only (label of a tin) |
| TSA of Cylinder | 2πr(h + r) | CSA + 2 circular ends |
| Volume of Hollow Cylinder | π(R² − r²)h | Pipe problems |
| Volume of Cone | (1/3)πr²h | One-third of cylinder |
| CSA of Cone | πrl | l = slant height |
| TSA of Cone | πr(l + r) | CSA + base |
| Slant Height of Cone | l = √(r² + h²) | Pythagoras on r, h |
| Volume of Frustum of Cone | (1/3)πh(R² + r² + Rr) | Bucket shape |
| CSA of Frustum | π(R + r)l, l = √(h² + (R−r)²) | Lampshade shape |
| Volume of Sphere | (4/3)πr³ | Ball |
| Surface Area of Sphere | 4πr² | Only one surface |
| Volume of Hemisphere | (2/3)πr³ | Half sphere |
| CSA of Hemisphere | 2πr² | Curved dome |
| TSA of Hemisphere | 3πr² | Dome + flat base |
| Volume of Spherical Shell | (4/3)π(R³ − r³) | Hollow ball |
| Volume of Prism | Base area × height | Any uniform cross-section |
| Volume of Pyramid | (1/3) × Base area × height | One-third rule again |

## 3. Conversion & Combination Rules

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Melting/Recasting | Volume before = Volume after | n small spheres → 1 big: n·(4/3)πr³ = (4/3)πR³ |
| Ratio of Volumes (similar solids) | (ratio of sides)³ | If sides in ratio a:b, volumes a³:b³ |
| Ratio of Surface Areas (similar solids) | (ratio of sides)² | Areas scale with square |
| Cylinder ⊃ Cone (same base & height) | V_cyl : V_cone = 3 : 1 | Classic exam ratio |
| Sphere in Cylinder (fitting) | V_sphere = (2/3) V_cylinder | Archimedes' result |
| Path around rectangle (outside) | (l+2w)(b+2w) − lb | w = width of path |
| Path inside rectangle | lb − (l−2w)(b−2w) | Garden with inner path |
| Carpet/Road crossing | w(l + b − w) | Two crossing paths of width w |
| Wheel revolutions | Distance / 2πr | No. of rotations = distance ÷ circumference |
| Cubes cut from a cube | (N)³ small cubes, N = big edge/small edge | Painted-cube problems |
| 3-face painted cubes | Always 8 (corners) | Painted cube rule |
| 2-face painted cubes | 12(N − 2) | Edges minus corners |
| 1-face painted cubes | 6(N − 2)² | Face centres |
| 0-face painted cubes | (N − 2)³ | Inner core |

## 4. Unit Conversions

| Formula Name | Formula | Use-Hint |
|---|---|---|
| Area | 1 m² = 10,000 cm² = 100 dm² | Square the length factor |
| Volume | 1 m³ = 10⁶ cm³ = 1000 litres | 1 litre = 1000 cm³ |
| Land | 1 hectare = 10,000 m² | Field-area problems |
