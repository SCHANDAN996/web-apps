1. The area of a rectangle is 240 cm² and its length is 20 cm. What is its breadth?
(a) 10 cm (b) 16 cm (c) 14 cm (d) 12 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 10 cm comes from dividing 240 by 24, a slip in reading the length. Area = length × breadth, so breadth = 240 ÷ 20 = 12 cm, so the answer is 12 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

2. The perimeter of a square is 64 cm. What is its area?
(a) 128 cm² (b) 196 cm² (c) 256 cm² (d) 512 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 128 cm² comes from doubling the perimeter instead of finding the side. Side = 64 ÷ 4 = 16 cm, so area = 16² = 256 cm², so the answer is 256 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

3. The radius of a circle is 7 cm. What is its circumference? (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 44 cm (b) 22 cm (c) 88 cm (d) 154 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 154 cm is the area (πr²), not the circumference. Circumference = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 7 = 44 cm, so the answer is 44 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

4. The area of a triangle is 84 cm² and its base is 14 cm. What is its height?
(a) 12 cm (b) 10 cm (c) 6 cm (d) 24 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 6 cm comes from forgetting the factor 2 in the area formula. Area = (1/2) × base × height, so height = (2 × 84) ÷ 14 = 12 cm, so the answer is 12 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

5. The area of a circle is 154 cm². What is its radius? (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 14 cm (b) 7 cm (c) 21 cm (d) 3.5 cm
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 14 cm is the diameter, not the radius. πr² = 154 gives r² = 154 × 7/22 = 49, so r = 7 cm, so the answer is 7 cm.

6. The sides of a triangle are 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. What is its area?
(a) 12 cm² (b) 10 cm² (c) 6 cm² (d) 15 cm²
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 12 cm² comes from multiplying 3 × 4 without halving. Since 3² + 4² = 5², it is a right triangle, so area = (1/2) × 3 × 4 = 6 cm², so the answer is 6 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 3080 cm³ comes from using 2πr²h, doubling the correct formula. Volume = πr²h = (22/7) × 49 × 10 = 1540 cm³, so the answer is 1540 cm³.

10. The area of a parallelogram is 180 cm² and its base is 15 cm. What is its corresponding height?
(a) 12 cm (b) 10 cm (c) 6 cm (d) 24 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 6 cm comes from wrongly halving, as if it were a triangle. Area of parallelogram = base × height, so height = 180 ÷ 15 = 12 cm, so the answer is 12 cm.

11. The radius of a sphere is 3 cm. What is its volume? (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 36 cm³ (b) 113.14 cm³ (c) 108 cm³ (d) 226.28 cm³
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 108 cm³ comes from using 4πr³ instead of (4/3)πr³. Volume = (4/3) × (22/7) × 27 = 792/7 ≈ 113.14 cm³, so the answer is 113.14 cm³.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

12. The perimeter of a rectangle is 50 cm and its length is 15 cm. What is its breadth?
(a) 20 cm (b) 12.5 cm (c) 10 cm (d) 35 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 35 cm comes from subtracting the length from the perimeter without halving. 2(l + b) = 50 gives l + b = 25, so b = 25 − 15 = 10 cm, so the answer is 10 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

13. The area of a rhombus is 96 cm² and one diagonal is 16 cm. What is the other diagonal?
(a) 12 cm (b) 6 cm (c) 24 cm (d) 48 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 6 cm comes from dividing 96 by 16 directly, forgetting the factor 2. Area = (1/2) × d1 × d2, so d2 = (2 × 96) ÷ 16 = 12 cm, so the answer is 12 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

14. The total surface area of a cube is 150 cm². What is the length of its edge?
(a) 25 cm (b) 6 cm (c) 10 cm (d) 5 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 25 cm comes from dividing 150 by 6 but forgetting the square root. 6a² = 150 gives a² = 25, so a = 5 cm, so the answer is 5 cm.

Answer: (b)

16. The parallel sides of a trapezium are 10 cm and 14 cm, and the distance between them is 6 cm. What is its area?
(a) 36 cm² (b) 72 cm² (c) 144 cm² (d) 84 cm²
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 144 cm² comes from forgetting the factor 1/2 in the formula. Area = (1/2) × (10 + 14) × 6 = 72 cm², so the answer is 72 cm².

17. The slant height of a cone is 13 cm and its base radius is 5 cm. What is its height?
(a) 8 cm (b) 10 cm (c) 12 cm (d) 18 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 18 cm comes from adding 13 and 5 instead of using Pythagoras. h = √(l² − r²) = √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12 cm, so the answer is 12 cm.

18. The volume of a cuboid is 360 cm³, its length is 10 cm and breadth is 6 cm. What is its height?
(a) 4 cm (b) 5 cm (c) 8 cm (d) 6 cm
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 5 cm comes from dividing 360 by 72, a slip in multiplying 10 × 6. Height = 360 ÷ (10 × 6) = 360 ÷ 60 = 6 cm, so the answer is 6 cm.

19. The area of an equilateral triangle with side 4 cm is:
(a) 2√3 cm² (b) 8√3 cm² (c) 16√3 cm² (d) 4√3 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 16√3 cm² comes from squaring the side but dropping the √3/4 factor. Area = (√3/4) × 4² = (√3/4) × 16 = 4√3 cm², so the answer is 4√3 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

20. The curved surface area of a cylinder is 440 cm² and its radius is 7 cm. What is its height? (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 5 cm (b) 14 cm (c) 10 cm (d) 20 cm
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 5 cm comes from using πr instead of 2πr in the formula. 2πrh = 440 gives h = 440 ÷ (2 × (22/7) × 7) = 440 ÷ 44 = 10 cm, so the answer is 10 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

21. A wire is bent into a square of side 22 cm. If the same wire is bent into a circle, what is the radius of the circle? (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 14 cm (b) 11 cm (c) 7 cm (d) 22 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 11 cm comes from dividing the perimeter by 8 instead of using 2πr. Wire length = 4 × 22 = 88 cm; 2πr = 88 gives r = 88 × 7/44 = 14 cm, so the answer is 14 cm.

22. The length and breadth of a rectangle are in the ratio 5 : 3 and its perimeter is 64 cm. What is its area?
(a) 120 cm² (b) 300 cm² (c) 180 cm² (d) 240 cm²
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 120 cm² comes from using 5x and 3x with x = 4 but forgetting that the perimeter gives 16x = 64. 2(5x + 3x) = 64 gives x = 4, so sides are 20 cm and 12 cm, and area = 240 cm², so the answer is 240 cm².
Source: PYQ-style

23. The radius of a circle is increased by 50%. By what percentage does its area increase?
(a) 50% (b) 125% (c) 100% (d) 225%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 100% comes from doubling the percentage, ignoring that area scales with the square of the radius. New area = (1.5r)²π = 2.25πr², an increase of 125%, so the answer is 125%.
Source: PYQ-style

24. A right circular cone and a cylinder have the same base radius and the same height. What is the ratio of the volume of the cone to that of the cylinder?
(a) 1 : 2 (b) 2 : 3 (c) 1 : 4 (d) 1 : 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 1 : 2 confuses the cone's one-third factor with a half. Cone volume = (1/3)πr²h and cylinder volume = πr²h, so the ratio is 1 : 3, so the answer is 1 : 3.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

25. A path 2 m wide runs around a square park of side 30 m, outside it. What is the area of the path?
(a) 256 m² (b) 124 m² (c) 240 m² (d) 120 m²
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 240 m² comes from computing 4 × 30 × 2 and forgetting the four corner squares. Outer side = 30 + 4 = 34 m, so path area = 34² − 30² = 1156 − 900 = 256 m², so the answer is 256 m².
Source: PYQ-style
