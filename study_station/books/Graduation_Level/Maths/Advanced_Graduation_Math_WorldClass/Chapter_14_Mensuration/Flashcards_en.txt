Card 1
Front: What is the area of a triangle with base b and height h?
Back: Area = ½ × b × h

Card 2
Front: State Heron's formula for the area of a triangle.
Back: Area = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)], where s = (a+b+c)/2 is the semi-perimeter.

Card 3
Front: What is the area of an equilateral triangle of side a?
Back: Area = (√3/4) × a²

Card 4
Front: What is the area of a trapezium?
Back: Area = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × distance between them = ½ × (a + b) × h

Card 5
Front: What is the area of a rhombus with diagonals d₁ and d₂?
Back: Area = ½ × d₁ × d₂

Card 6
Front: What is the area of a circle and its circumference?
Back: Area = πr²; Circumference = 2πr

Card 7
Front: What is the area of a sector of a circle with angle θ (in degrees)?
Back: Area = (θ/360) × πr²; Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr

Card 8
Front: What is the curved surface area (CSA) and total surface area (TSA) of a cylinder?
Back: CSA = 2πrh; TSA = 2πr(r + h); Volume = πr²h

Card 9
Front: What is the curved surface area of a cone with radius r and slant height l?
Back: CSA = πrl, where l = √(r² + h²); TSA = πr(l + r); Volume = (1/3)πr²h

Card 10
Front: What is the surface area and volume of a sphere?
Back: Surface area = 4πr²; Volume = (4/3)πr³

Card 11
Front: What is the volume of a hemisphere and its total surface area?
Back: Volume = (2/3)πr³; TSA = 3πr² (curved part 2πr² + base πr²)

Card 12
Front: What is the volume of a frustum of a cone with radii R and r and height h?
Back: Volume = (1/3)πh(R² + r² + Rr)

Card 13
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember that a cone's volume is one-third of a cylinder's?
Back: "Cone is the poor cousin — it holds only ONE-THIRD of the cylinder's meal." Same base, same height: V(cone) = ⅓ V(cylinder). Similarly, pyramid = ⅓ prism.

Card 14
Front: What is the mnemonic for the surface area of a sphere (4πr²)?
Back: "A sphere wears FOUR circle-shirts" — its surface equals 4 flat circles of the same radius: 4πr².

Card 15
Front: If the side of a square is doubled, what happens to its area?
Back: Area becomes 4 times (area scales with the square of the linear dimension).

Card 16
Front: If the radius of a sphere is doubled, what happens to its volume?
Back: Volume becomes 8 times (volume scales with the cube of the radius: 2³ = 8).

Card 17
Front: What is the area of a regular hexagon of side a?
Back: Area = (3√3/2) × a² (six equilateral triangles of side a)

Card 18
Front: What is the diagonal of a cube of side a, and its surface area?
Back: Space diagonal = a√3; face diagonal = a√2; surface area = 6a²; volume = a³

Card 19
Front: What is the area of a parallelogram?
Back: Area = base × height (perpendicular height, not the slant side)

Card 20
Front: A path of width w runs around a rectangular field (L × B) outside it. What is the path's area?
Back: Area = (L + 2w)(B + 2w) − LB = 2w(L + B) + 4w². For a path inside: 2w(L + B) − 4w².
