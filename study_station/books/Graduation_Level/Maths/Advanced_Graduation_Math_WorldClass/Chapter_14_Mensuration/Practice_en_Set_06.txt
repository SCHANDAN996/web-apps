126. A sector of a circle of radius 12 cm and central angle 120° is folded to form a cone. What is the volume of the cone?
(a) 128√2π/3 cm³ (b) 64√2π/3 cm³ (c) 128π/3 cm³ (d) 96√2π cm³

Solution: The trap is taking the sector radius 12 as the cone's base radius; it is actually the slant height. Arc length = (120/360) × 2π × 12 = 8π, so base radius r = 4 cm. Then h = √(12² − 4²) = √128 = 8√2. Volume = (1/3)π × 16 × 8√2 = 128√2π/3 cm³, so the answer is 128√2π/3 cm³.
