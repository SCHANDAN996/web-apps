26. The length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its breadth is decreased by 20%. What is the percentage change in its area?
(a) No change (b) 4% increase (c) 4% decrease (d) 2% decrease
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting answer is "no change", assuming +20% and −20% cancel out — but percentage changes multiply, not add. Net change = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%. So the area decreases by 4%.
