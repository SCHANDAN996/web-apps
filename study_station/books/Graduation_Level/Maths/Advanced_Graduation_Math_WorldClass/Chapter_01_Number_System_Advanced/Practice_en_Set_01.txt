Q1. (Easy) What is the remainder when (17 * 23 * 29) is divided by 6?
(A) 5  (B) 3  (C) 1  (D) 4

Answer: (A)

Solution:
In modulo 6 arithmetic, 17 ≡ -1, 23 ≡ -1, and 29 ≡ -1. Multiplying these gives (-1)*(-1)*(-1) = -1 ≡ 5 (mod 6). Psychological Trap: Students often multiply large numbers directly instead of using negative remainders. Therefore, the correct value is 5, corresponding to option (A).

Q2. (Easy) A number when divided by 899 gives a remainder of 63. If the same number is divided by 29, what will be the remainder?
(A) 4  (B) 5  (C) 2  (D) 1

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Since 899 = 29 * 31, 899 is an exact multiple of 29. Therefore, the new remainder is simply 63 mod 29 = 5. Therefore, the correct value is 5, corresponding to option (B).

Q3. (Easy) What is the units digit of (234)^102 + (234)^103?
(A) 4  (B) 6  (C) 0  (D) 8

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Powers of 4 have cyclicity 2: 4^even ends in 6 and 4^odd ends in 4. The sum of units digits is 6 + 4 = 10, which ends in 0. Therefore, the correct value is 0, corresponding to option (C).

Q4. (Easy) Which of the following numbers is completely divisible by 99?

Answer: (D)

Answer: (D)

Solution:
A number divisible by both 3 and 5 must be divisible by lcm(3, 5) = 15. The number of multiples is floor(199 / 15) = 13. Psychological Trap: Including the endpoints incorrectly when 'between' is specified. Therefore, the correct value is 13, corresponding to option (D).

Q10. (Easy) What is the units digit of (7^95 - 3^58)?
(A) 0  (B) 4  (C) 6  (D) 7

Answer: (B)

Solution:
For 7^95, 95 mod 4 = 3, so 7^3 ends in 3. For 3^58, 58 mod 4 = 2, so 3^2 ends in 9. Since 3 < 9, borrow 10: 13 - 9 = 4. Psychological Trap: Forgetting to borrow 10 when the first unit digit is smaller than the second. Therefore, the correct value is 4, corresponding to option (B).

Q15. (Easy) What is the units digit of (1! + 2! + 3! + 4! + ... + 100!)?
(A) 1  (B) 5  (C) 3  (D) 7

Answer: (C)

Solution:
For all n >= 5, n! contains 2 and 5, so its units digit is 0. Only the first 4 terms contribute: 1 + 2 + 6 + 24 = 33, which ends in 3. Therefore, the correct value is 3, corresponding to option (C).

Solution:
Prime factorization of 100 is 2^2 * 5^2. Total divisors τ(100) = (2 + 1)(2 + 1) = 3 * 3 = 9. Therefore, the correct value is 9, corresponding to option (D).

Q17. (Easy) The expression (a^n - b^n) is always divisible by (a + b) when n is:
(A) Even positive integer  (B) Odd positive integer  (C) Any prime number  (D) Any integer

Answer: (A)

Solution:
By algebraic identity, a^n - b^n = (a + b)(a^(n-1) - a^(n-2)b + ... - b^(n-1)) only when n is an even integer. Therefore, the correct value is Even positive integer, corresponding to option (A).

Q18. (Easy) Find the smallest 5-digit number that is exactly divisible by 41.
(A) 10025  (B) 10004  (C) 10041  (D) 10000

Answer: (B)

Solution:
The smallest 5-digit number is 10000. 10000 / 41 = 243 with remainder 37. To make it divisible, add (41 - 37) = 4: 10000 + 4 = 10004. Therefore, the correct value is 10004, corresponding to option (B).

Solution:
In mod 8 arithmetic, 9 ≡ 1. Therefore, 9^6 - 11 ≡ 1^6 - 11 = 1 - 11 = -10. In mod 8, -10 = -2 * 8 + 6 ≡ 6. Therefore, the correct value is 6, corresponding to option (A).

Q24. (Medium) What is the remainder when (35^37 + 37^37) is divided by 36?
(A) 1  (B) 35  (C) 37  (D) 0

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Modulo 36 gives 35 ≡ -1 and 37 ≡ 1. Therefore, (-1)^37 + (1)^37 = -1 + 1 = 0. Therefore, the correct value is 0, corresponding to option (D).

Q25. (Medium) A number N leaves a remainder of 3 when divided by 4, and a remainder of 4 when divided by 5. What is the remainder when N is divided by 20?
(A) 7  (B) 19  (C) 11  (D) 15

Answer: (B)

Solution:
N ≡ 3 (mod 4) and N ≡ 4 (mod 5). Testing values of 5k + 4: for k=3, 5(3)+4 = 19. Notice 19 mod 4 = 3 and 19 mod 5 = 4. Hence N ≡ 19 (mod 20). Therefore, the correct value is 19, corresponding to option (B).
