# Flashcards: Ratio & Proportion Mastery

## Card 1: Mean Proportional
- **Front:** What is the formula for the Mean Proportional between two numbers a and b?
- **Back:** Mean Proportional = sqrt(a * b).

## Card 2: Third Proportional
- **Front:** What is the third proportional to two quantities a and b?
- **Back:** Third proportional x = b^2 / a.

## Card 3: Fourth Proportional
- **Front:** What is the fourth proportional to three quantities a, b, and c?
- **Back:** Fourth proportional x = (b * c) / a.

## Card 4: Ratio Merging Rule
- **Front:** If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5, what is a : b : c?
- **Back:** a : b : c = (2*4) : (3*4) : (3*5) = 8 : 12 : 15.

## Card 5: Componendo and Dividendo
- **Front:** If a / b = c / d, state the Componendo and Dividendo formula.
- **Back:** (a + b) / (a - b) = (c + d) / (c - d).

## Card 6: Duplicate and Sub-duplicate Ratio
- **Front:** What are duplicate and sub-duplicate ratios of a : b?
- **Back:** Duplicate = a^2 : b^2; Sub-duplicate = sqrt(a) : sqrt(b).
