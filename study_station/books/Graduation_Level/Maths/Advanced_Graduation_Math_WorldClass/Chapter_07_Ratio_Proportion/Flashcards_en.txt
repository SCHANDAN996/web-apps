Card 1
Front: What is a ratio?
Back: A comparison of two quantities of the same kind by division, written as a:b = a/b. It has no unit.

Card 2
Front: What is a proportion?
Back: An equality of two ratios: a:b = c:d. Here a and d are extremes, b and c are means, and a×d = b×c.

Card 3
Front: State the mnemonic for remembering the rule of proportion.
Back: "Product of Extremes = Product of Means" — remember "PEPM" or "Outside × Outside = Inside × Inside" (the X-cross trick).

Card 4
Front: If a:b = 2:3 and b:c = 4:5, what is a:c?
Back: a:c = (2×4):(3×5) = 8:15. Make the common term b equal (LCM method) when chaining ratios.

Card 5
Front: What is a duplicate ratio?
Back: The ratio of squares: duplicate of a:b is a²:b². Sub-duplicate is √a:√b.

Card 6
Front: What are triplicate and sub-triplicate ratios?
Back: Triplicate of a:b is a³:b³; sub-triplicate is ∛a:∛b.

Card 7
Front: What is the componendo rule?
Back: If a/b = c/d, then (a+b)/b = (c+d)/d.

Card 8
Front: What is the dividendo rule?
Back: If a/b = c/d, then (a−b)/b = (c−d)/d.

Card 9
Front: State componendo-dividendo and its mnemonic.
Back: If a/b = c/d, then (a+b)/(a−b) = (c+d)/(c−d). Mnemonic: "CD = Combine & Divide" — add on top, subtract below.

Card 10
Front: If a/b = c/d = e/f, what does (a+c+e)/(b+d+f) equal?
Back: It equals each individual ratio a/b (= c/d = e/f). This is the "addendo" or equal-ratios property.

Card 11
Front: What is the fourth proportional to a, b, c?
Back: The number x such that a:b = c:x, so x = bc/a.

Card 12
Front: What is the third proportional to a and b?
Back: The number x such that a:b = b:x, so x = b²/a.

Card 13
Front: What is the mean proportional between a and b?
Back: x = √(ab), since a:x = x:b gives x² = ab.

Card 14
Front: How do you divide ₹N between A and B in the ratio a:b?
Back: A gets N × a/(a+b); B gets N × b/(a+b). Mnemonic: "Share = Total × Part/Sum of parts."

Card 15
Front: If A:B = 3:4 and B:C = 5:6, find A:B:C.
Back: Make B equal: A:B = 15:20, B:C = 20:24, so A:B:C = 15:20:24.

Card 16
Front: If x is added to both terms of a ratio a:b (a<b), does the ratio increase or decrease?
Back: It increases (moves toward 1). For a proper fraction, adding the same number to numerator and denominator increases its value.

Card 17
Front: What is an inverse ratio?
Back: The inverse of a:b is b:a (also written 1/a : 1/b simplified). Used in work-time and speed-time problems.

Card 18
Front: Two numbers are in ratio 5:7 and their sum is 96. Find them.
Back: Numbers = 96 × 5/12 = 40 and 96 × 7/12 = 56.

Card 19
Front: What is the quick trick for "income ratio × expenditure ratio" savings problems?
Back: Use actual values = ratio × common multiplier (say x). Set Income − Expenditure = Savings, solve for x, then compute the asked quantity.

Card 20
Front: If a:b = c:d, list all valid rearrangements (alternendo & invertendo).
Back: Alternendo: a/c = b/d. Invertendo: b/a = d/c. Both follow from ad = bc.
