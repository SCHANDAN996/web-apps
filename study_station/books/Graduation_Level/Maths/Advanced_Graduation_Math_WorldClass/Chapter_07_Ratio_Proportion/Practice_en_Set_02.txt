26. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7. If 9 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 2 : 3. Find the larger number.
(a) 70 (b) 56 (c) 49 (d) 63
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let the numbers be 5x and 7x. Then (5x − 9)/(7x − 9) = 2/3, giving 15x − 27 = 14x − 18, so x = 9. The tempting error is inverting the new ratio to 3/2, which yields a negative x and signals the mistake. Larger number = 7 × 9, so the answer is 63.
Source: PYQ-style

27. The ratio of incomes of A and B is 4 : 5 and the ratio of their expenditures is 3 : 4. If each saves ₹4,000, find A's income.
(a) ₹24,000 (b) ₹20,000 (c) ₹16,000 (d) ₹12,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let incomes be 4x and 5x, expenditures 3y and 4y. Then 4x − 3y = 4000 and 5x − 4y = 4000; subtracting gives x = y, and then 4x − 3x = 4000 gives x = 4000. A common trap is assuming the savings ratio equals the income ratio, which is not given. A's income = 4 × 4000, so the answer is ₹16,000.
Source: PYQ-style

28. If a : b = 3 : 4 and b : c = 6 : 7, then a : b : c is
(a) 9 : 12 : 14 (b) 3 : 4 : 7 (c) 6 : 8 : 14 (d) 3 : 6 : 7
Answer: (a)
Solution: Make b common using the LCM of 4 and 6, which is 12: a : b = 9 : 12 and b : c = 12 : 14. The trap is simply writing 3 : 4 : 7 without adjusting b to a common value. Combining, so the answer is 9 : 12 : 14.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept)

29. ₹6,200 is divided among A, B and C so that A gets half of what B gets, and B gets twice what C gets. Find B's share.
(a) ₹1,550 (b) ₹3,100 (c) ₹2,067 (d) ₹3,550
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let C get x. Then B gets 2x and A gets x, so the total is 4x = 6200 and x = 1550. The tempting mistake is miscounting the parts as 1 : 2 : 2 instead of 1 : 2 : 1. B's share = 2 × 1550, so the answer is ₹3,100.
Source: PYQ-style

30. The ratio of two numbers is 7 : 11. If 7 is added to each, the ratio becomes 2 : 3. Find the smaller number.
(a) 49 (b) 77 (c) 56 (d) 63
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let the numbers be 7x and 11x. Then (7x + 7)/(11x + 7) = 2/3, giving 21x + 21 = 22x + 14, so x = 7. A common slip is cross-multiplying correctly but mis-solving to a negative x. Smaller number = 7 × 7, so the answer is 49.
Source: PYQ-style

31. If (3a + 5b) : (3a − 5b) = 5 : 1, then a : b equals
(a) 5 : 2 (b) 3 : 5 (c) 5 : 3 (d) 2 : 5
Answer: (a)
Solution: Cross-multiplying: 3a + 5b = 5(3a − 5b) = 15a − 25b, so 30b = 12a and a : b = 30 : 12 = 5 : 2. The trap is reaching 12a = 30b and then writing the ratio inverted as 2 : 5. Check: with a = 5, b = 2, (15 + 10) : (15 − 10) = 25 : 5 = 5 : 1, so the answer is 5 : 2.
Source: PYQ-style

32. The seats in three departments are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. If the seats are increased by 20%, 25% and 40% respectively, the new ratio is
(a) 36 : 50 : 70 (b) 18 : 25 : 35 (c) 18 : 20 : 35 (d) 9 : 12 : 15
Answer: (b)
Solution: New values: 3 × 1.2 = 3.6, 4 × 1.25 = 5, 5 × 1.4 = 7; multiplying by 5 clears decimals to give 18 : 25 : 35. The trap is adding the percentages to the ratio terms directly or assuming the ratio stays unchanged. So the answer is 18 : 25 : 35.
Source: PYQ-style

33. A bag contains ₹1, 50-paise and 25-paise coins in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. If the total value is ₹312.50, the number of 50-paise coins is
(a) 100 (b) 150 (c) 200 (d) 250
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let the coins be 3x, 4x and 5x. Total value = 3x(1) + 4x(0.5) + 5x(0.25) = 6.25x = 312.50, so x = 50. The common trap is treating the coin-count ratio as the value ratio, ignoring the denominations. Number of 50-paise coins = 4 × 50, so the answer is 200.
Source: PYQ-style

34. If x : y = 2 : 3, then (5x + 2y) : (7x − y) equals
(a) 11 : 16 (b) 16 : 11 (c) 16 : 9 (d) 9 : 16
Answer: (b)
Solution: Substitute x = 2k, y = 3k: numerator = 10k + 6k = 16k and denominator = 14k − 3k = 11k. The trap is an arithmetic slip computing 7x − y as 17k, or inverting the final ratio. So the answer is 16 : 11.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept)

35. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 7 : 8. If 20 boys and 20 girls are admitted, the ratio becomes 8 : 9. The original number of girls was
(a) 280 (b) 240 (c) 160 (d) 400
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let boys = 7x and girls = 8x. Then (7x + 20)/(8x + 20) = 8/9, giving 63x + 180 = 64x + 160, so x = 20. The trap is cross-multiplying with the new ratio inverted, which produces a negative x and signals the error. Original number of girls = 8 × 20, so the answer is 160.
Source: PYQ-style

36. ₹2,340 is divided among P, Q and R in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. Q's share is
(a) ₹630 (b) ₹540 (c) ₹720 (d) ₹420
Answer: (c)
Solution: Multiply the fractions by LCM 12 to get the whole-number ratio 6 : 4 : 3, with 13 total parts. The trap is dividing the money using the fractions 1/2, 1/3, 1/4 as if they were whole-number parts. Q's share = (4/13) × 2340, so the answer is ₹720.
Source: PYQ-style

37. The fourth proportional to 6, 8 and 15 is
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 24 (d) 12
Answer: (b)
Solution: The fourth proportional x satisfies 6 : 8 :: 15 : x, so x = (8 × 15)/6 = 20. The trap is computing the third proportional 8²/6 instead, which mixes up the two concepts. So the answer is 20.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept)

38. The third proportional to 12 and 18 is
(a) 36 (b) 24 (c) 21 (d) 27
Answer: (d)
Solution: The third proportional x satisfies 12 : 18 :: 18 : x, so x = 18²/12 = 324/12 = 27. The tempting error is finding the mean proportional √(12 × 18) or misordering the terms. So the answer is 27.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept)

39. The mean proportional between 0.08 and 0.18 is
(a) 0.12 (b) 0.13 (c) 0.0144 (d) 0.144
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mean proportional = √(0.08 × 0.18) = √0.0144 = 0.12. The trap is stopping at the product 0.0144 and choosing it, forgetting the square root. So the answer is 0.12.
Source: PYQ-style

40. If a/b = c/d = e/f = 2/3, then (3a + 2c + 4e)/(3b + 2d + 4f) equals
(a) 3/2 (b) 2/3 (c) 4/9 (d) 9/4
Answer: (b)
Solution: By the property of equal ratios, each numerator term is 2/3 of its denominator term, so the combined ratio is also 2/3. The trap is squaring the ratio to get 4/9 or inverting it to 3/2. So the answer is 2/3.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept)

41. A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3. How much water must be added to 40 litres of the mixture so that the ratio becomes 5 : 7?
(a) 20 litres (b) 16 litres (c) 10 litres (d) 12 litres
Answer: (a)
Solution: Milk = (5/8) × 40 = 25 litres and water = 15 litres. If x litres of water are added, milk stays 25, so 25/(15 + x) = 5/7, giving 15 + x = 35 and x = 20. The trap is increasing both terms of the ratio or changing the milk quantity too. So the answer is 20 litres.
Source: PYQ-style

42. The ratio of the ages of a father and son is 7 : 2. After 10 years the ratio will be 9 : 4. The father's present age is
(a) 35 years (b) 42 years (c) 49 years (d) 56 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let the ages be 7x and 2x. Then (7x + 10)/(2x + 10) = 9/4, giving 28x + 40 = 18x + 90, so 10x = 50 and x = 5. The trap is inverting the future ratio to 4/9 while setting up the equation. Father's age = 7 × 5, so the answer is 35 years.
Source: PYQ-style

43. If (a + b) : (b + c) : (c + a) = 6 : 7 : 8 and a + b + c = 21, then c equals
(a) 5 (b) 7 (c) 11 (d) 9
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let a + b = 6k, b + c = 7k, c + a = 8k. Their sum is 2(a + b + c) = 21k = 42, so k = 2. Then c = (a + b + c) − (a + b) = 21 − 12 = 9. The trap is taking c = 8k = 16 directly, forgetting that 8k is c + a, not c. So the answer is 9.
Source: PYQ-style

44. The ratio of copper to zinc in alloy A is 5 : 3 and in alloy B is 3 : 5. Equal weights of both alloys are melted together. The ratio of copper to zinc in the new alloy is
(a) 8 : 8 (b) 4 : 3 (c) 1 : 1 (d) 3 : 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: Take 8 kg of each alloy: copper = 5 + 3 = 8 kg and zinc = 3 + 5 = 8 kg, giving 8 : 8 = 1 : 1. The trap is averaging the ratios without using equal weights, which works here only because the weights are equal. So the answer is 1 : 1.
Source: PYQ-style

45. What number must be added to each term of the ratio 11 : 17 so that it becomes 3 : 4?
(a) 7 (b) 5 (c) 9 (d) 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let x be added to each term: (11 + x)/(17 + x) = 3/4, giving 44 + 4x = 51 + 3x, so x = 7. The trap is adding x only to the smaller term or subtracting instead of adding. Check: (11 + 7)/(17 + 7) = 18/24 = 3/4, so the answer is 7.
Source: PYQ-style

46. A, B and C start a business with capitals in the ratio 4 : 5 : 6. If the total profit is ₹45,000 and profits are shared in the capital ratio, B's share exceeds A's share by
(a) ₹9,000 (b) ₹6,000 (c) ₹4,500 (d) ₹3,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: Total parts = 15, so each part = 45000/15 = 3000, and B − A = (5 − 4) × 3000. The trap is reporting B's full share of ₹15,000 or A's ₹12,000 instead of the difference. So the answer is ₹3,000.
Source: PYQ-style

47. If 2a = 3b = 4c, then a : b : c is
(a) 2 : 3 : 4 (b) 4 : 3 : 2 (c) 6 : 4 : 3 (d) 3 : 4 : 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let 2a = 3b = 4c = k. Then a = k/2, b = k/3, c = k/4, and multiplying by 12 gives 6 : 4 : 3. The classic trap is writing 2 : 3 : 4 directly from the coefficients, which is inverted logic. So the answer is 6 : 4 : 3.
Source: PYQ-style

48. The ratio of the areas of two squares is 16 : 25. The ratio of their perimeters is
(a) 8 : 12.5 (b) 16 : 25 (c) 256 : 625 (d) 4 : 5
Answer: (d)
Solution: Side ratio = √(16 : 25) = 4 : 5, and the perimeter ratio equals the side ratio. The trap is keeping the area ratio 16 : 25 for perimeters, forgetting that perimeter scales with side, not area. So the answer is 4 : 5.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (concept)

49. ₹840 is divided between A and B such that for every ₹3 that A gets, B gets ₹4. If A gives ₹45 to B, the new ratio of their amounts is
(a) 5 : 7 (b) 3 : 5 (c) 1 : 2 (d) 2 : 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: A gets (3/7) × 840 = ₹360 and B gets ₹480. After the transfer, A = 315 and B = 525, and 315 : 525 = 3 : 5. The trap is subtracting ₹45 from A but forgetting to add it to B, which distorts the ratio. So the answer is 3 : 5.
Source: PYQ-style

50. The prices of a scooter and a TV are in the ratio 9 : 4. If the scooter costs ₹45,000 more than the TV, the price of the TV is
(a) ₹32,000 (b) ₹40,000 (c) ₹45,000 (d) ₹36,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The difference of parts is 9 − 4 = 5 parts = ₹45,000, so 1 part = ₹9,000 and the TV costs 4 × 9000. The trap is dividing 45,000 by 9, the scooter's parts, instead of by the difference 5. So the answer is ₹36,000.
Source: PYQ-style
