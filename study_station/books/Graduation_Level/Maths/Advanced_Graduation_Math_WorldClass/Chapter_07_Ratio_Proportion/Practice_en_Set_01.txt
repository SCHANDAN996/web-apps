1. If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 5 : 6, then A : C is
(a) 3 : 6 (b) 1 : 2 (c) 4 : 5 (d) 5 : 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is simply joining 3 and 6 to get 1 : 2, ignoring the middle term. Make B common: A : B = 3 : 4 = 15 : 20 and B : C = 5 : 6 = 20 : 24, so A : C = 15 : 24 = 5 : 8, so the answer is 5 : 8.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

2. The ratio of 25 paise to ₹2 is
(a) 1 : 8 (b) 8 : 1 (c) 1 : 4 (d) 25 : 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice 25 : 2 forgets to convert units. ₹2 = 200 paise, so the ratio is 25 : 200 = 1 : 8, so the answer is 1 : 8.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

3. If x : y = 7 : 3, then (5x + 2y) : (5x − 2y) is
(a) 29 : 41 (b) 41 : 29 (c) 35 : 6 (d) 7 : 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Substituting x = 7, y = 3: numerator = 35 + 6 = 41, denominator = 35 − 6 = 29. The trap is reversing the order to get 29 : 41, so the answer is 41 : 29.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Divide ₹1200 between Ram and Shyam in the ratio 3 : 5. Shyam gets
(a) ₹450 (b) ₹600 (c) ₹750 (d) ₹720
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total parts = 8. The trap is giving Ram's share (3/8 × 1200 = 450) when Shyam's is asked. Shyam's share = 5/8 × 1200 = 750, so the answer is ₹750.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

5. If 4 : x :: x : 25, then x equals
(a) 12.5 (b) 10 (c) 20 (d) 100
Answer: (b)
Solution: Product of means = product of extremes, so x² = 4 × 25 = 100, giving x = 10. The trap is computing 100 and stopping there, so the answer is 10.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

6. The third proportional to 9 and 12 is
(a) 15 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 10.5
Answer: (b)
Solution: Third proportional x satisfies 9 : 12 :: 12 : x, so x = 12 × 12 / 9 = 16. The trap is finding the mean proportional (√108 ≈ 10.4) instead, so the answer is 16.
Source: PYQ-style

7. The mean proportional between 4 and 36 is
(a) 20 (b) 12 (c) 16 (d) 18
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mean proportional = √(4 × 36) = √144 = 12. The trap is taking the arithmetic mean (4 + 36)/2 = 20, so the answer is 12.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

8. If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5, then a : b : c is
(a) 8 : 12 : 15 (b) 2 : 3 : 5 (c) 8 : 12 : 20 (d) 4 : 6 : 5
Answer: (a)
Solution: Make b common: a : b = 2 : 3 = 8 : 12 and b : c = 4 : 5 = 12 : 15. The trap is writing 2 : 3 : 5 by dropping the middle adjustment, so the answer is 8 : 12 : 15.

9. The ratio of two numbers is 5 : 7 and their sum is 60. The smaller number is
(a) 35 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 20
Answer: (b)
Solution: Total parts = 12, each part = 60/12 = 5. Smaller number = 5 × 5 = 25. The trap is reporting the larger number 35, so the answer is 25.

10. If 15% of A = 20% of B, then A : B is
(a) 15 : 20 (b) 3 : 4 (c) 5 : 4 (d) 4 : 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: 0.15A = 0.20B gives A/B = 20/15 = 4/3. The trap is inverting to 3 : 4, so the answer is 4 : 3.

11. The ages of two brothers are in the ratio 4 : 5. If the elder is 20 years old, the younger is
(a) 16 years (b) 15 years (c) 18 years (d) 25 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: 5 parts = 20, so 1 part = 4, and younger = 4 × 4 = 16. The trap is treating the elder as 4 parts, giving 25, so the answer is 16 years.

13. ₹840 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 7. C's share is
(a) ₹490 (b) ₹210 (c) ₹140 (d) ₹420
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total parts = 12, each part = 70. C's share = 7 × 70 = 490. The trap is computing half the amount (420) instead of 7/12 of it, so the answer is ₹490.

14. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 7 : 5. If there are 35 boys, the number of girls is
(a) 20 (b) 30 (c) 49 (d) 25
Answer: (d)
Solution: 7 parts = 35, so 1 part = 5, and girls = 5 × 5 = 25. The trap is mis-scaling as 35 × 7/5 = 49 by inverting the ratio, so the answer is 25.

15. If x : y = 2 : 5, then (3x + y) : (x + 3y) is
(a) 1 : 2 (b) 17 : 11 (c) 7 : 8 (d) 11 : 17

16. The fourth proportional to 3, 5 and 21 is
(a) 35 (b) 15 (c) 12.6 (d) 45
Answer: (a)
Solution: 3 : 5 :: 21 : x gives x = 5 × 21 / 3 = 35. The trap is computing 3 × 21 / 5 = 12.6 by misplacing terms, so the answer is 35.

17. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 4. If 6 is added to each, the ratio becomes 5 : 6. The numbers are
(a) 15 and 20 (b) 12 and 16 (c) 9 and 12 (d) 6 and 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let the numbers be 3k, 4k. Then (3k + 6)/(4k + 6) = 5/6, so 18k + 36 = 20k + 30, giving k = 3 and numbers 9, 12. The trap is picking 12 and 16, which give 18/22 = 9/11, not 5/6, so the answer is 9 and 12.
Source: PYQ-style

18. If A : B = 1 : 2, B : C = 3 : 4 and C : D = 5 : 6, then A : D is
(a) 3 : 8 (b) 1 : 6 (c) 5 : 16 (d) 15 : 48
Answer: (c)
Solution: A/D = (1/2) × (3/4) × (5/6) = 15/48 = 5/16. The trap is joining the ends directly as 1 : 6, so the answer is 5 : 16.

19. The duplicate ratio of 3 : 5 is
(a) 27 : 125 (b) 6 : 10 (c) 9 : 25 (d) 3 : 10
Answer: (c)
Solution: Duplicate ratio means squaring each term: 3² : 5² = 9 : 25. The trap is 27 : 125, which is the triplicate (cubed) ratio, so the answer is 9 : 25.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Mathematics

20. If 2A = 3B = 4C, then A : B : C is
(a) 2 : 3 : 4 (b) 6 : 4 : 3 (c) 4 : 3 : 2 (d) 3 : 4 : 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: Let 2A = 3B = 4C = 12 (the LCM). Then A = 6, B = 4, C = 3. The trap is writing 2 : 3 : 4 directly from the coefficients, so the answer is 6 : 4 : 3.

21. The ratio of incomes of A and B is 5 : 4 and the ratio of their expenditures is 3 : 2. If each saves ₹1600, A's income is
(a) ₹4800 (b) ₹3200 (c) ₹3600 (d) ₹4000
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let incomes be 5k, 4k and expenditures 3m, 2m. Then 5k − 3m = 1600 and 4k − 2m = 1600. From the second, m = 2k − 800; substituting gives 5k − 6k + 2400 = 1600, so k = 800 and A's income = 5 × 800 = 4000. The trap is reporting B's income 3200, so the answer is ₹4000.
Source: PYQ-style

22. A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 1. How much water must be added to 60 litres of the mixture so that the ratio becomes 5 : 3?
(a) 15 litres (b) 24 litres (c) 20 litres (d) 12 litres
Answer: (c)
Solution: Milk = 5/6 × 60 = 50 litres, water = 10 litres. Milk stays fixed: 50/(10 + w) = 5/3 gives 150 = 50 + 5w, so w = 20. The trap is scaling the whole mixture instead of keeping milk constant, which suggests 24, so the answer is 20 litres.
Source: PYQ-style

23. The ratio of two numbers is 4 : 7. If each number is increased by 4, the ratio becomes 3 : 5. The larger number is
(a) 56 (b) 32 (c) 48 (d) 40
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let the numbers be 4k, 7k. Then (4k + 4)/(7k + 4) = 3/5, so 20k + 20 = 21k + 12, giving k = 8 and larger number = 56. The trap is reporting the smaller number 32, so the answer is 56.
Source: PYQ-style

24. ₹782 is divided among A, B and C so that A gets 2/3 of what B gets, and B gets 1/4 of what C gets. B's share is
(a) ₹138 (b) ₹92 (c) ₹552 (d) ₹46
Answer: (a)
Solution: A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 1 : 4 = 3 : 12, so A : B : C = 2 : 3 : 12. Total = 17 parts = ₹782, so 1 part = ₹46 and B = 3 × 46 = 138. The trap is using B : C = 1 : 4 without making B common, which distorts all shares, so the answer is ₹138.
Source: PYQ-style

25. The seats for Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry in a college are in the ratio 5 : 7 : 8. The seats are increased by 40%, 50% and 75% respectively. The new ratio is
(a) 4 : 7 : 14 (b) 6 : 7 : 8 (c) 7 : 10.5 : 14 (d) 2 : 3 : 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: New seats: 5 × 1.4 = 7, 7 × 1.5 = 10.5, 8 × 1.75 = 14, giving 7 : 10.5 : 14 = 2 : 3 : 4 on dividing by 3.5. The trap is stopping at 7 : 10.5 : 14 without simplifying to integers, so the answer is 2 : 3 : 4.
Source: PYQ-style
