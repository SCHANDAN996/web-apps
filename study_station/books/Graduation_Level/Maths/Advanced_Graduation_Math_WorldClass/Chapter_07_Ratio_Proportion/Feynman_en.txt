# The Feynman Technique: Ratio as Recipe Scaling & Proportional Balancing

Imagine baking cookies using a master recipe:
To bake 12 cookies, you need 2 cups of flour and 3 eggs. The ratio of flour to eggs is **2 : 3**.

### Scaling the Recipe
If you want to bake 60 cookies (5 times the batch), you must scale both ingredients by 5:
Flour = 2 * 5 = 10 cups, Eggs = 3 * 5 = 15 eggs.
The ratio 10 : 15 is identical to 2 : 3 because the underlying 'flavour' balance hasn't shifted.

### The Balancing Beam of Componendo & Dividendo
Think of a fraction a / b = c / d as two identical balance scales.
If 7 / 3 = 14 / 6:
Add the denominator to numerator: (7 + 3) = 10; subtract denominator: (7 - 3) = 4.
The ratio of sum to difference on the left scale is 10 / 4 = 5 / 2.
Do the same on the right scale: (14 + 6) / (14 - 6) = 20 / 8 = 5 / 2!
They remain perfectly equal. This powerful insight (Componendo & Dividendo) collapses complex fractional equations into simple arithmetic in seconds.
