Q51. (Medium) The monthly incomes of two persons are in the ratio 5 : 3 and their monthly expenditures are in the ratio 9 : 5. If they save Rs 1,300 and Rs 900 respectively, find the monthly income of the first person.
(A) Rs 4,000  (B) Rs 3,600  (C) Rs 4,500  (D) Rs 3,200

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Using cross multiplication: (5 * 5 - 3 * 9)x = 5 * (-1300) - 9 * (-900) => (25 - 27)x = -6500 + 8100 => -2x = 1600... absolute difference: |25 - 27|x = |8100 - 6500| => 2x = 1600 => x = 800. Monthly income of first person = 5 * 800 = Rs 4,000. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 4,000, corresponding to option (A).
