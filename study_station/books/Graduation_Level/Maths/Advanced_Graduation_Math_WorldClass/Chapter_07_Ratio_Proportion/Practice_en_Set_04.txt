Q76. (Hard) Two vessels A and B contain mixtures of milk and water in the ratio 7 : 5 and 17 : 7 respectively. In what ratio should the contents of these two vessels be mixed so as to result in a mixture containing milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3?
(A) 2 : 1  (B) 1 : 2  (C) 3 : 2  (D) 2 : 3

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Milk concentration in vessel A is 7/12 = 14/24. Milk concentration in vessel B is 17/24. The target milk concentration is 5/8 = 15/24. Applying the rule of alligation: Ratio of A to B = (17/24 - 15/24) : (15/24 - 14/24) = 2/24 : 1/24 = 2 : 1. Therefore, the correct value is 2 : 1, corresponding to option (A).

Q77. (Hard) A container contains 80 litres of milk. From this container, 8 litres of milk is taken out and replaced by water. This process was repeated further two times. How much milk is now contained by the container?
(A) 56.48 litres  (B) 58.32 litres  (C) 60.24 litres  (D) 54.72 litres

Solution:
The fraction of liquid replaced in each cycle is 8/80 = 1/10. The process is carried out a total of 3 times. Remaining quantity of pure milk = 80 * (1 - 1/10)^3 = 80 * (9/10)^3 = 80 * (729 / 1000) = 58.32 litres. Therefore, the correct value is 58.32 litres, corresponding to option (B).
