1. What is the value of 3/5 + 1/5?
(a) 4/5 (b) 2/5 (c) 4/10 (d) 3/25
Answer: (a)
Solution: Since the denominators are equal, add the numerators directly: 3 + 1 = 4, keeping the denominator 5. The tempting wrong choice 4/10 comes from wrongly adding the denominators too, which is never done. So the answer is 4/5.

2. Which of the following fractions is the largest?
(a) 3/7 (b) 1/2 (c) 5/11 (d) 4/9
Answer: (b)
Solution: Convert to decimals: 3/7 ≈ 0.428, 4/9 ≈ 0.444, 5/11 ≈ 0.454, and 1/2 = 0.5. The tempting choice 5/11 looks big because of the larger numerator, but its decimal value is smaller than 0.5. So the answer is 1/2.

3. The decimal form of 7/8 is:
(a) 0.785 (b) 0.857 (c) 0.778 (d) 0.875
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dividing 7 by 8: 8 × 0.8 = 6.4, remainder 0.6; continuing gives 0.875 exactly, since 7/8 = 7/(2³) terminates. The tempting choice 0.785 just swaps the digits, a common slip. So the answer is 0.875.

5. What is 2/3 of 45?
(a) 15 (b) 30 (c) 25 (d) 35
Answer: (b)
Solution: 2/3 × 45 = (2 × 45)/3 = 90/3 = 30. The tempting choice 15 is only 1/3 of 45, which results from forgetting to multiply by the numerator 2. So the answer is 30.

6. Which fraction is equivalent to 12/18?
(a) 3/4 (b) 2/3 (c) 4/5 (d) 5/6
Answer: (b)
Solution: Divide numerator and denominator by their HCF 6: 12 ÷ 6 = 2 and 18 ÷ 6 = 3, giving 2/3. The tempting choice 3/4 comes from dividing only the numerator by 4 and denominator by 4.5 inconsistently. So the answer is 2/3.

7. The value of 0.25 × 0.4 is:
(a) 0.01 (b) 1.0 (c) 0.1 (d) 0.64
Answer: (c)
Solution: 25 × 4 = 100; total decimal places = 2 + 1 = 3, so place the decimal three places from the right: 0.100 = 0.1. The tempting choice 1.0 results from misplacing the decimal point by one place. So the answer is 0.1.

10. Evaluate: 3/4 − 1/6
(a) 2/2 (b) 1/2 (c) 7/12 (d) 5/12
Answer: (c)
Solution: LCM of 4 and 6 is 12: 3/4 = 9/12 and 1/6 = 2/12, so 9/12 − 2/12 = 7/12. The tempting choice 1/2 comes from wrongly subtracting numerators and denominators separately (2/2 = 1, then guessing). So the answer is 7/12.

11. Which of the following is a terminating decimal?
(a) 3/8 (b) 5/6 (c) 1/3 (d) 2/7
Answer: (a)
Solution: A fraction in lowest terms terminates only if its denominator has no prime factors other than 2 and 5. Here 8 = 2³, so 3/8 = 0.375 terminates; 3, 6 and 7 contain other primes. The tempting choice 5/6 fails because 6 = 2 × 3 contains the prime 3. So the answer is 3/8.

13. Arrange in ascending order: 2/5, 3/8, 1/4

Answer: (a)
Solution: Convert to decimals: 1/4 = 0.25, 3/8 = 0.375, 2/5 = 0.4. Ascending order: 0.25 < 0.375 < 0.4. The tempting choice with 2/5 first assumes the largest denominator gives the smallest value, which is false. So the answer is 1/4, 3/8, 2/5.

18. Which is greater: 0.9 or 0.89?
(a) 0.89 (b) 0.9 (c) Both equal (d) Cannot be determined
Answer: (b)
Solution: Write 0.9 as 0.90; comparing 0.90 and 0.89 digit by digit, the tenths are equal but 0 > 9 is false — rather, hundredths: 0 vs 9 would mislead; correctly, 0.90 > 0.89 since 90 > 89 hundredths. The tempting choice 0.89 fools those who think more digits means a bigger number. So the answer is 0.9.

21. The value of 0.333… + 0.666… is:

22. If 1/3 of a number exceeds its 1/4 by 5, the number is:
(a) 20 (b) 45 (c) 30 (d) 60
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let the number be x. Then x/3 − x/4 = 5, so (4x − 3x)/12 = 5, giving x/12 = 5 and x = 60. The tempting choice 20 comes from wrongly computing 5 × 4 instead of 5 × 12. So the answer is 60.

24. A student was asked to multiply a number by 3/5, but by mistake divided it by 3/5 and got 45. The correct answer is:
(a) 27 (b) 75 (c) 16.2 (d) 45
Answer: (c)
Solution: The number = 45 × 3/5 = 27. The correct operation gives 27 × 3/5 = 81/5 = 16.2. The tempting choice 27 is only the original number, not the final correct result — a classic half-done trap. So the answer is 16.2.

25. Which of the following is the correct value of 1/2 + 1/6 + 1/12 + 1/20?
(a) 4/5 (b) 5/6 (c) 3/4 (d) 7/10
Answer: (a)
Solution: Use the telescoping form: 1/2 = 1 − 1/2, 1/6 = 1/2 − 1/3, 1/12 = 1/3 − 1/4, 1/20 = 1/4 − 1/5. All middle terms cancel, leaving 1 − 1/5 = 4/5. The tempting choice 5/6 comes from adding only the first two or three terms and approximating. So the answer is 4/5.
Source: PYQ-style
