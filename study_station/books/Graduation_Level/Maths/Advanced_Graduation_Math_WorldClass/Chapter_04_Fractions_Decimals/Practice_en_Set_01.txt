Q1. (Easy) Convert the decimal 0.75 into a simple fraction in lowest terms.
(A) 3/4  (B) 7/10  (C) 4/5  (D) 75/10

Answer: (A)

Solution:
0.75 = 75 / 100. Dividing numerator and denominator by 25 gives 3/4. Therefore, the correct value is 3/4, corresponding to option (A).
