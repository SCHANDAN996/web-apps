Q124. (Hard) How many pairs of positive integers (u, v) with u <= v satisfy the Diophantine equation 1/u + 1/v = 1/6?
(A) 9  (B) 6  (C) 4  (D) 5

Answer: (D)

Solution:
(u - 6)(v - 6) = 36. For u <= v, (u - 6) takes the 5 factors of 36 that are <= 6: {1, 2, 3, 4, 6}, yielding exactly 5 pairs. Psychological Trap: Counting all 9 divisor pairs and forgetting the condition u <= v, or missing the symmetric solution u = v = 12. Therefore, the correct value is 5, corresponding to option (D).
