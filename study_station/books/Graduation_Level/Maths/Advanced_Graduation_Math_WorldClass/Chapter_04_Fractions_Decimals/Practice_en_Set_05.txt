101. What is the value of 3/7 + 2/5 − 1/3?
(a) 52/105 (b) 71/105 (c) 62/105 (d) 47/105
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 62/105 comes from adding all three numerators after conversion instead of subtracting the last. LCM of 7, 5, 3 is 105: 3/7 = 45/105, 2/5 = 42/105, 1/3 = 35/105, so 45 + 42 − 35 = 52, giving 52/105, so the answer is 52/105.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics (Fractions and Decimals)

102. A fraction becomes 3/4 when 1 is added to both numerator and denominator, and becomes 2/3 when 1 is subtracted from both. The fraction is:
(a) 5/7 (b) 3/5 (c) 7/11 (d) 4/7
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 4/7 satisfies the second condition but fails the first, which students who check only one condition pick. Let the fraction be x/y: 4(x+1) = 3(y+1) gives 4x − 3y = −1, and 3(x−1) = 2(y−1) gives 3x − 2y = 1. Solving, x = 5 and y = 7; check: 6/8 = 3/4 and 4/6 = 2/3, so the answer is 5/7.
Source: PYQ-style

103. The value of 0.2̅ + 0.3̅ (recurring decimals) is:
(a) 5/9 (b) 1/2 (c) 0.55 (d) 0.5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 0.55, chosen by treating the sum as a terminating decimal. Since 0.2̅ = 2/9 and 0.3̅ = 3/9, the sum is 5/9 = 0.555…, so the answer is 5/9.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics (Number Systems)

104. Which of the following fractions is the largest: 7/9, 11/15, 13/17, 19/25?
(a) 19/25 (b) 11/15 (c) 13/17 (d) 7/9
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 19/25, which looks big because of large numbers but equals 0.76. Converting: 7/9 ≈ 0.7778, 11/15 ≈ 0.7333, 13/17 ≈ 0.7647, 19/25 = 0.76, so the answer is 7/9.
Source: PYQ-style

105. If 3/5 of a number exceeds its 2/7 by 44, the number is:
(a) 120 (b) 105 (c) 140 (d) 175
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 105 comes from using LCM 35 but misplacing the difference. Let the number be N: 3N/5 − 2N/7 = 44 gives (21N − 10N)/35 = 44, so 11N = 1540 and N = 140; check: 84 − 40 = 44, so the answer is 140.
Source: PYQ-style

106. The value of 4.036 ÷ 0.04 is:
(a) 100.9 (b) 10.09 (c) 1.009 (d) 1009
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 10.09, obtained by shifting the decimal only one place. Multiply numerator and denominator by 100: 403.6 ÷ 4 = 100.9, so the answer is 100.9.

107. A student was asked to multiply a number by 3/5 but divided it by 3/5 instead. The result was 64 more than the correct answer. The number is:
(a) 60 (b) 100 (c) 80 (d) 120
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 120, from doubling the correct value while solving. Wrong result − correct result = 5N/3 − 3N/5 = (25N − 9N)/15 = 16N/15 = 64, so N = 60; check: 100 − 36 = 64, so the answer is 60.
Source: PYQ-style

108. The sum of the reciprocals of two numbers is 7/12 and their product is 12. The numbers are:
(a) 2 and 6 (b) 3 and 4 (c) 1 and 12 (d) 3 and 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 2 and 6, whose product is 12 but whose reciprocals sum to 1/2 + 1/6 = 2/3, not 7/12. For 3 and 4: product = 12 and 1/3 + 1/4 = 7/12, so the answer is 3 and 4.
Source: PYQ-style

109. Express 0.123̅ (only 3 repeating, i.e. 0.12333…) as a fraction:
(a) 41/333 (b) 37/300 (c) 123/999 (d) 61/495
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 123/999, which treats all three digits as repeating. Let x = 0.12333…; then 100x = 12.333… and 1000x = 123.333…, so 900x = 111 and x = 111/900 = 37/300, so the answer is 37/300.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics (Number Systems)

110. If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 100% and the denominator is increased by 200%, the fraction becomes 3/8. The original fraction is:
(a) 3/4 (b) 9/16 (c) 2/3 (d) 5/8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 3/4, from reversing the percentage increases. New numerator = 2x and new denominator = 3y, so 2x/3y = 3/8 gives x/y = 9/16; check: 18/48 = 3/8, so the answer is 9/16.
Source: PYQ-style

111. The value of (0.75 × 0.75 − 0.25 × 0.25) / (0.75 − 0.25) is:
(a) 0.5 (b) 1 (c) 0.25 (d) 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 0.5, which is just the denominator value. Using a² − b² = (a−b)(a+b), the expression equals a + b = 0.75 + 0.25 = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: PYQ-style

112. Which fraction lies exactly midway between 2/5 and 3/7?
(a) 5/12 (b) 14/35 (c) 29/70 (d) 29/35
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 5/12, obtained by adding numerators and denominators (the mediant, which is not the midpoint). Midpoint = (2/5 + 3/7)/2 = (29/35)/2 = 29/70, so the answer is 29/70.
Source: PYQ-style

113. The value of 1/(1×2) + 1/(2×3) + 1/(3×4) + … + 1/(9×10) is:
(a) 10/11 (b) 1/10 (c) 8/9 (d) 9/10
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 10/11, from assuming the series runs to 1/(10×11). Each term 1/(n(n+1)) = 1/n − 1/(n+1), so the sum telescopes to 1 − 1/10 = 9/10, so the answer is 9/10.
Source: PYQ-style

114. A man spends 3/8 of his money on food and 2/5 of the remainder on rent. If he is left with ₹900, his original money was:
(a) ₹2000 (b) ₹1800 (c) ₹2400 (d) ₹3000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is ₹2000, from applying 2/5 to the whole amount instead of the remainder. After food the remainder is 5/8, and after rent he has 5/8 × 3/5 = 3/8 of the original; so 3M/8 = 900 and M = 2400, so the answer is ₹2400.
Source: PYQ-style

115. The decimal 0.0009 expressed in standard scientific form is:
(a) 9 × 10⁻³ (b) 9 × 10⁴ (c) 0.9 × 10⁻³ (d) 9 × 10⁻⁴
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 9 × 10⁻³, from counting only three decimal places. Since 0.0009 = 9/10000 = 9 × 10⁻⁴, so the answer is 9 × 10⁻⁴.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Exponents and Powers)

116. If 4/9 of a pole is painted red, 1/3 of the remainder white, and the rest is 10 m green, the length of the pole is:
(a) 27 m (b) 22.5 m (c) 30 m (d) 18 m
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 22.5 m, from taking 1/3 of the whole pole as white. Remainder after red = 5/9; white = 5/27; green = 5/9 − 5/27 = 10/27. So (10/27)L = 10 and L = 27 m, so the answer is 27 m.
Source: PYQ-style

117. The value of 2.5 × 0.4 × 0.08 is:
(a) 0.8 (b) 8 (c) 0.08 (d) 0.008
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 0.8, from dropping a decimal place. Since 2.5 × 0.4 = 1.0 and 1.0 × 0.08 = 0.08, so the answer is 0.08.

118. The difference between a fraction and its reciprocal is 9/20. The fraction (less than 1) is:
(a) 5/6 (b) 3/4 (c) 4/5 (d) 2/3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 3/4, whose reciprocal difference is 4/3 − 3/4 = 7/12, not 9/20. For 4/5: 5/4 − 4/5 = (25 − 16)/20 = 9/20, so the answer is 4/5.

119. Arrange in ascending order: 5/8, 7/12, 3/5, 11/18:
(a) 7/12 < 11/18 < 3/5 < 5/8 (b) 3/5 < 7/12 < 5/8 < 11/18 (c) 7/12 < 3/5 < 11/18 < 5/8 (d) 3/5 < 7/12 < 11/18 < 5/8
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the arrangement that misplaces 3/5 below 7/12. Decimals: 5/8 = 0.625, 7/12 ≈ 0.5833, 3/5 = 0.6, 11/18 ≈ 0.6111, so ascending order is 7/12 < 3/5 < 11/18 < 5/8, so the answer is 7/12 < 3/5 < 11/18 < 5/8.
Source: PYQ-style

120. The value of 0.1 + 0.01 + 0.001 + 0.0001 up to 10 terms is closest to:
(a) 0.1 (b) 0.1111 (c) 1/9 (d) 0.1111111111
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 1/9 = 0.1111…, which is the infinite sum, but the question asks for only 10 terms. The finite sum is 0.1111111111 (ten 1s), slightly less than 1/9, so the answer is 0.1111111111.
Source: PYQ-style

121. The sum 1/2 + 1/6 + 1/12 + 1/20 + 1/30 + 1/42 + 1/56 equals:
(a) 6/7 (b) 7/8 (c) 8/9 (d) 5/6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 6/7, from stopping the telescoping one term early. The terms are 1/(1×2), 1/(2×3), …, 1/(7×8), and each equals 1/n − 1/(n+1), so the sum telescopes to 1 − 1/8 = 7/8, so the answer is 7/8.
Source: PYQ-style

122. A fraction is such that if its numerator is multiplied by 3 and denominator reduced by 3, it becomes 18/11; but if the numerator is increased by 8 and denominator doubled, it becomes 2/5. The fraction is:
(a) 8/25 (b) 12/25 (c) 12/19 (d) 8/19
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 8/25, which fails the first condition since 24/22 = 12/11, not 18/11. Let the fraction be x/y: 3x/(y−3) = 18/11 gives 11x = 6y − 18, and (x+8)/(2y) = 2/5 gives 5x + 40 = 4y. Solving, x = 12 and y = 25; check: 36/22 = 18/11 and 20/50 = 2/5, so the answer is 12/25.
Source: PYQ-style

123. The value of (1.5)³ + (0.5)³ + 3 × 1.5 × 0.5 × 2 is:
(a) 9 (b) 6.5 (c) 7.5 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 6.5, from computing only the cubes and ignoring the identity. Using a³ + b³ + 3ab(a+b) = (a+b)³ with a = 1.5 and b = 0.5, the expression equals (1.5 + 0.5)³ = 2³ = 8, so the answer is 8.
Source: PYQ-style

124. The recurring decimal 0.2̅3̅ (i.e. 0.232323…) added to 0.1̅2̅ (i.e. 0.121212…) gives:
(a) 36/99 (b) 7/20 (c) 0.35 (d) 35/99
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 0.35, from treating the sum as a terminating decimal. Since 0.2323… = 23/99 and 0.1212… = 12/99, the sum is 35/99 = 0.3535…, so the answer is 35/99.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics (Number Systems)

125. A vessel contains a mixture in which milk is 5/8 of the whole. After removing 16 litres of mixture and adding 16 litres of water, milk becomes 3/8 of the whole. The capacity of the vessel is:
(a) 40 litres (b) 32 litres (c) 48 litres (d) 64 litres
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 32 litres, from ignoring that the removed mixture also removes milk proportionally. Milk removed = 16 × 5/8 = 10 litres, so 5V/8 − 10 = 3V/8, giving 2V/8 = 10 and V = 40; check: 25 − 10 = 15 = 3/8 × 40, so the answer is 40 litres.
Source: PYQ-style
