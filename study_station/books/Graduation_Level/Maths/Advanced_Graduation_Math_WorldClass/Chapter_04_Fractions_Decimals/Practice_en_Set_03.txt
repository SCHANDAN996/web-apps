51. What is the value of 3/7 + 2/5 − 1/35?
(a) 32/35 (b) 30/35 (c) 28/35 (d) 34/35
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 30/35 comes from adding the numerators without converting to a common denominator. LCM of 7, 5 and 35 is 35: 3/7 = 15/35, 2/5 = 14/35, so 15/35 + 14/35 − 1/35 = 28/35, so the answer is 28/35.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

52. Which of the following fractions is the largest?
(a) 11/18 (b) 5/8 (c) 7/12 (d) 3/5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is picking 11/18 because it has the biggest numerator. Convert to decimals: 7/12 ≈ 0.5833, 11/18 ≈ 0.6111, 5/8 = 0.625, 3/5 = 0.6. The greatest is 0.625, so the answer is 5/8.
Source: PYQ-style

53. If 0.4 of a number is 56, what is 0.75 of the same number?
(a) 95 (b) 100 (c) 110 (d) 105
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 100 comes from treating 0.75 as three-fourths of a wrongly computed base. The number = 56 ÷ 0.4 = 140, and 0.75 × 140 = 105, so the answer is 105.
Source: PYQ-style

54. The value of 2.5 × 0.04 ÷ 0.002 is:
(a) 5 (b) 500 (c) 0.5 (d) 50
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 5 comes from misplacing the decimal in the division. 2.5 × 0.04 = 0.1, and 0.1 ÷ 0.002 = 100/2 = 50, so the answer is 50.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

55. What is the HCF of 3/4, 9/16 and 15/8?
(a) 3/4 (b) 3/8 (c) 1/16 (d) 3/16
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 3/8 comes from taking the HCF of numerators over the LCM of only two denominators. HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators ÷ LCM of denominators = HCF(3, 9, 15) ÷ LCM(4, 16, 8) = 3/16, so the answer is 3/16.
Source: PYQ-style

56. Express 0.363636… as a fraction in lowest terms.
(a) 9/25 (b) 36/99 (c) 4/11 (d) 6/17
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is leaving 36/99 unreduced. Let x = 0.3636…; then 100x = 36.3636…, so 99x = 36 and x = 36/99 = 4/11 in lowest terms, so the answer is 4/11.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

57. A man spends 2/5 of his salary on rent and 1/4 of the remainder on food. What fraction of his salary is left?
(a) 3/5 (b) 9/20 (c) 11/20 (d) 7/20
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 7/20 comes from subtracting 1/4 of the whole salary instead of the remainder. After rent, remainder = 3/5; food = 1/4 × 3/5 = 3/20; left = 3/5 − 3/20 = 12/20 − 3/20 = 9/20, so the answer is 9/20.
Source: PYQ-style

58. The value of (0.75 ÷ 0.15) + (1.2 × 0.5) is:
(a) 4.6 (b) 5.6 (c) 6.5 (d) 5.06
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 5.06 comes from adding 0.06 instead of 0.6. 0.75 ÷ 0.15 = 5 and 1.2 × 0.5 = 0.6, giving 5 + 0.6 = 5.6, so the answer is 5.6.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

59. Which fraction lies exactly midway between 1/3 and 1/2?
(a) 2/5 (b) 5/12 (c) 7/12 (d) 5/6
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 2/5 comes from averaging numerators and denominators separately. Midpoint = (1/3 + 1/2) ÷ 2 = (5/6) ÷ 2 = 5/12, so the answer is 5/12.

60. If 3/8 of a tank is filled in 12 minutes, how long will it take to fill the whole tank at the same rate?
(a) 32 minutes (b) 28 minutes (c) 30 minutes (d) 36 minutes
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 36 comes from multiplying 12 by 3 instead of dividing by the fraction. Time for the full tank = 12 ÷ (3/8) = 12 × 8/3 = 32 minutes, so the answer is 32 minutes.
Source: PYQ-style

61. The sum of 0.2 + 0.22 + 0.222 + 0.2222 is:
(a) 0.8662 (b) 0.8888 (c) 0.8622 (d) 0.8642
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 0.8662 comes from a column-addition slip in the thousandths place. Adding step by step: 0.2 + 0.22 = 0.42; 0.42 + 0.222 = 0.642; 0.642 + 0.2222 = 0.8642, so the answer is 0.8642.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

62. A fraction becomes 3/4 when 2 is added to both its numerator and its denominator. Which of the following fractions satisfies this condition?
(a) 7/10 (b) 5/8 (c) 4/7 (d) 9/13
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is picking an option without testing it. Test each: 7/10 → 9/12 = 3/4 ✓; 5/8 → 7/10 ✗; 4/7 → 6/9 = 2/3 ✗; 9/13 → 11/15 ✗. Only one option works, so the answer is 7/10.
Source: PYQ-style

63. The value of 1/2 + 1/6 + 1/12 + 1/20 + 1/30 is:
(a) 6/7 (b) 4/5 (c) 5/6 (d) 7/8
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding numerators and denominators directly. Use telescoping: 1/2 = 1 − 1/2, 1/6 = 1/2 − 1/3, 1/12 = 1/3 − 1/4, 1/20 = 1/4 − 1/5, 1/30 = 1/5 − 1/6; the sum collapses to 1 − 1/6 = 5/6, so the answer is 5/6.
Source: PYQ-style

64. What is 0.125 expressed as a percentage of 0.5?
(a) 25% (b) 30% (c) 20% (d) 40%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 20% comes from using the wrong base in the division. Required percentage = (0.125 ÷ 0.5) × 100 = 0.25 × 100 = 25%, so the answer is 25%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

65. The LCM of 2/3, 3/4 and 4/5 is:
(a) 12/60 (b) 24 (c) 12 (d) 1/5
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap answer 12/60 comes from applying the HCF formula instead. LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators ÷ HCF of denominators = LCM(2, 3, 4) ÷ HCF(3, 4, 5) = 12 ÷ 1 = 12, so the answer is 12.
Source: PYQ-style

66. If x = 0.666… and y = 0.333…, then x² − y² equals:
(a) 1/9 (b) 1/3 (c) 2/9 (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 1/9 comes from squaring the difference directly without using the identity. x = 2/3 and y = 1/3, so x² − y² = (x − y)(x + y) = (1/3)(1) = 1/3, so the answer is 1/3.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

67. A student was asked to multiply a number by 3/5 but divided it by 3/5 instead. The result was 64 more than the correct answer. The number is:
(a) 100 (b) 75 (c) 90 (d) 60
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is setting up the difference with the operations reversed. Let the number be N: N × 5/3 − N × 3/5 = 64, so N × (25 − 9)/15 = 64, giving N × 16/15 = 64 and N = 60. Check: 60 × 5/3 = 100 and 60 × 3/5 = 36, difference = 64 ✓, so the answer is 60.
Source: PYQ-style

68. The value of 3.6 × 2.5 − 1.8 × 2.5 is:
(a) 5.0 (b) 4.0 (c) 4.5 (d) 3.6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing each product separately and making a decimal slip. Factor out 2.5: 2.5 × (3.6 − 1.8) = 2.5 × 1.8 = 4.5, so the answer is 4.5.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

69. Which of the following is in ascending order?
(a) 3/7 < 4/9 < 5/11 < 6/13 (b) 3/7 < 5/11 < 4/9 < 6/13 (c) 4/9 < 3/7 < 5/11 < 6/13 (d) 5/11 < 3/7 < 4/9 < 6/13
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming bigger numerators mean bigger fractions. As decimals: 3/7 ≈ 0.4286, 4/9 ≈ 0.4444, 5/11 ≈ 0.4545, 6/13 ≈ 0.4615 — already ascending, so the answer is 3/7 < 4/9 < 5/11 < 6/13.

70. The denominator of a fraction is 5 more than its numerator. If 3 is added to both, the fraction becomes 5/6. The original fraction is:
(a) 22/27 (b) 12/17 (c) 27/32 (d) 17/22
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is picking an option whose denominator exceeds the numerator by 5 without testing the second condition. Let the fraction be x/(x+5): (x+3)/(x+8) = 5/6 gives 6x + 18 = 5x + 40, so x = 22 and the fraction is 22/27. Check: 25/30 = 5/6 ✓, while 17/22 → 20/25 = 4/5 ✗, so the answer is 22/27.
Source: PYQ-style

71. The value of 0.1 + 0.01 + 0.001 + … up to 10 terms is closest to:
(a) 0.1111111109 (b) 0.1111111112 (c) 0.1111111011 (d) 0.1111111111
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is a place-value slip in the last digits. The sum is a geometric series: 0.1(1 − 0.1¹⁰)/(1 − 0.1) = (1/9)(1 − 10⁻¹⁰) = 0.1111111111…, which to 10 decimal places is 0.1111111111, so the answer is 0.1111111111.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

72. If 5/8 of a number exceeds its 3/8 by 24, the number is:
(a) 96 (b) 108 (c) 120 (d) 84
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 120 comes from dividing 24 by 1/5 instead of 2/8. Difference of fractions = 5/8 − 3/8 = 2/8 = 1/4, so the number = 24 ÷ 1/4 = 96, so the answer is 96.
Source: PYQ-style

73. The value of (1.5)² − (0.5)² is:
(a) 2.25 (b) 2.0 (c) 1.5 (d) 2.5
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap answer 2.25 comes from just squaring 1.5 and forgetting the subtraction. Use a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b) = (2.0)(1.0) = 2.0, so the answer is 2.0.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

74. A rope of length 13.5 m is cut into pieces of 0.75 m each. How many pieces are obtained?
(a) 18 (b) 16 (c) 17 (d) 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap answer 17 comes from a division slip treating 0.75 as 0.8. Number of pieces = 13.5 ÷ 0.75 = 1350/75 = 18, so the answer is 18.

75. The sum of the reciprocals of 0.2, 0.25 and 0.5 is:
(a) 12 (b) 13 (c) 11 (d) 14
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is adding the decimals first and then taking the reciprocal. Reciprocals: 1/0.2 = 5, 1/0.25 = 4, 1/0.5 = 2; sum = 5 + 4 + 2 = 11, so the answer is 11.
