Q: What is the necessary and sufficient condition for a reduced fraction p/q to be a terminating decimal?
A: The prime factorization of denominator q must be of the form 2^m * 5^n with no other prime factors.

Q: How many decimal digits does the terminating decimal of 7 / (2^3 * 5^5) contain?
A: Exactly max(3, 5) = 5 decimal digits.

Q: How is the mixed recurring decimal 0.ab(bar(cde)) converted into a rational fraction?
A: (abcde - ab) / 99900 (3 nines for the 3 repeating digits, 2 zeros for the 2 non-repeating digits).

Q: If a/b < 1 and k > 0, what is the inequality relationship between a/b and (a+k)/(b+k)?
A: (a+k)/(b+k) > a/b (adding equal positive amounts increases a proper fraction).

Q: What is the Mediant of two fractions a/b and c/d?
A: (a + c) / (b + d), which always lies strictly between a/b and c/d.

Q: What is the rational fraction representation of 0.bar(27)?
A: 27 / 99 = 3 / 11.
