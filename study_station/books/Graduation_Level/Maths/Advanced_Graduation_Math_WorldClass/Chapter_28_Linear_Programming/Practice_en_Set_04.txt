76. Maximise Z = 2x + 5y subject to 3x + 4y ≤ 12, x + y ≤ 20, x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0. What is the maximum value of Z?
(a) 8 (b) 0 (c) 17 (d) 15
Answer: (d)
Solution: The feasible region is bounded; check Z at each corner point: (0, 0) → 0; (0, 3) → 15; (4, 0) → 8. The largest value is 15, at (0, 3). So the answer is 15.
