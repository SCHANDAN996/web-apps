51. Maximise Z = 6x + 6y subject to 4x + 2y ≤ 24, 2x + 4y ≤ 8, x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0. What is the maximum value of Z?
(a) 24 (b) 30 (c) 12 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: The feasible region is bounded; check Z at each corner point: (0, 0) → 0; (0, 2) → 12; (4, 0) → 24. The largest value is 24, at (4, 0). So the answer is 24.
