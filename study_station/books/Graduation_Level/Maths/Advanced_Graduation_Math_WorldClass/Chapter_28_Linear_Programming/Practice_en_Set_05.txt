101. Maximise Z = 5x + 4y subject to 3x + 2y ≤ 24, 4x + 2y ≤ 20, x ≥ 0, y ≥ 0. What is the maximum value of Z?
(a) 40 (b) 44 (c) 25 (d) 39
Answer: (a)
Solution: The feasible region is bounded; check Z at each corner point: (0, 0) → 0; (0, 10) → 40; (5, 0) → 25. The largest value is 40, at (0, 10). So the answer is 40.
