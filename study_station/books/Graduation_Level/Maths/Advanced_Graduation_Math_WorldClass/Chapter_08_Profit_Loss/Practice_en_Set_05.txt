101. A shopkeeper marks an article 40% above the cost price and gives a discount of 10%. What is his profit percentage?
(a) 28% (b) 30% (c) 24% (d) 26%
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let CP = 100. Marked price = 140. Selling price after 10% discount = 140 × 0.9 = 126. Profit = 126 − 100 = 26, so profit % = 26%. The tempting answer 30% comes from wrongly subtracting 40 − 10, ignoring that discount applies to the marked price, not the cost price. So the answer is 26%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

102. A trader sells an article at ₹720 and gains 20%. What is the cost price?
(a) ₹576 (b) ₹620 (c) ₹600 (d) ₹640
Answer: (c)
Solution: SP = CP × 1.2, so CP = 720 / 1.2 = 600. The tempting answer 576 comes from wrongly computing 720 × 0.8, which treats the gain as if it were on the selling price. So the answer is ₹600.

122. A dishonest milkman mixes water with milk and sells the mixture at the cost price of pure milk, gaining 25%. The ratio of milk to water in the mixture is:
(a) 5 : 1 (b) 4 : 1 (c) 3 : 1 (d) 7 : 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gain of 25% means 1 litre of mixture contains milk worth 1 litre's price: if CP of 1 litre milk = ₹1, then SP of 1 litre mixture = ₹1 while its cost = ₹0.80, so milk = 0.8 litre and water = 0.2 litre, ratio 4 : 1. The tempting answer 5 : 1 comes from misreading 25% as 20%. So the answer is 4 : 1.

123. A trader sells an article at a profit of 20%. If both the cost price and selling price were ₹100 lower, the profit would be 4% more (i.e. 24%). Find the original cost price.
(a) ₹600 (b) ₹500 (c) ₹400 (d) ₹700
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let CP = x, SP = 1.2x. New profit % = (1.2x − 100 − (x − 100))/(x − 100) = 0.2x/(x − 100) = 0.24. So 0.2x = 0.24x − 24, giving 0.04x = 24, x = 600. The tempting answer 500 comes from setting the difference as 5% instead of 4%. So the answer is ₹600.
Source: PYQ-style

124. A man bought an article and sold it at a gain of 10%. If he had bought it at 20% less and sold it for ₹10 more, he would have gained 40%. The original cost price is:
(a) ₹600 (b) ₹400 (c) ₹500 (d) ₹450
Answer: (c)
Solution: Let CP = x, SP = 1.1x. New CP = 0.8x, new SP = 1.1x + 10. Gain 40%: 1.1x + 10 = 1.4 × 0.8x = 1.12x, so 10 = 0.02x, x = 500. The tempting answer 400 comes from an algebra slip when moving terms. So the answer is ₹500.
Source: PYQ-style
