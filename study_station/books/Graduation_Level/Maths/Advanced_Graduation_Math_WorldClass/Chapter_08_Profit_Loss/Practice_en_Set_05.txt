Q101. (Hard) A man sold two watches for Rs 2,400 each. On one he gained 20% and on the other he lost 25%. What was his net profit or loss percentage on the whole transaction?
(A) 7 9/13% loss  (B) 7 9/13% gain  (C) 8% loss  (D) 6 2/3% loss

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Cost Price of first watch = 2400 / 1.20 = Rs 2000. Cost Price of second watch = 2400 / 0.75 = Rs 3200. Total Cost Price = 2000 + 3200 = Rs 5200. Total revenue = 2400 + 2400 = Rs 4800. Net Loss = 5200 - 4800 = Rs 400. Net Loss percentage = (400 / 5200) * 100 = 100 / 13 = 7 9/13% loss. Therefore, the correct value is 7 9/13% loss, corresponding to option (A).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q102. (Hard) A merchant buys 40 articles for Rs 2,000. He sells 25 articles at a profit of 20% and the remaining 15 articles at a loss of 10%. What is his overall profit percentage?
(A) 8.5%  (B) 8.75%  (C) 9.0%  (D) 8.25%

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Cost Price per article = 2000 / 40 = Rs 50. Cost of 25 articles = 25 * 50 = Rs 1250, profit = 20% of 1250 = Rs 250. Cost of 15 articles = 15 * 50 = Rs 750, loss = 10% of 750 = Rs 75. Net Profit = 250 - 75 = Rs 175. Overall profit percentage = (175 / 2000) * 100 = 8.75%. Therefore, the correct value is 8.75%, corresponding to option (B).

Q103. (Hard) A shopkeeper bought 150 calculators at Rs 250 each. He spent Rs 2,500 on transportation and packaging. He marked the price of each calculator at Rs 320 and gave a discount of 5% on the marked price. What will be his percentage profit?
(A) 15%  (B) 12.5%  (C) 14%  (D) 16%

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Total purchase cost = 150 * 250 = Rs 37500. Total Cost Price including overheads = 37500 + 2500 = Rs 40000. Selling Price per calculator = 320 * (1 - 0.05) = Rs 304. Total Selling Price = 150 * 304 = Rs 45600. Profit = 45600 - 40000 = Rs 5600. Profit percentage = (5600 / 40000) * 100 = 14%. Therefore, the correct value is 14%, corresponding to option (C).

Q104. (Hard) A tradesman marks his goods at such a price that he can make 16% profit after giving a discount of 15%. However, a customer gets an additional 5% off the discounted price for paying cash. What is the merchant's actual profit percentage on cash sales?
(A) 11.0%  (B) 9.8%  (C) 10.5%  (D) 10.2%

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Using MP / CP = 116 / 85. Standard discounted price = MP * 0.85 = (116/85 * CP) * 0.85 = 1.16 * CP. Cash discounted price = 1.16 * CP * (1 - 0.05) = 1.16 * 0.95 * CP = 1.102 * CP. Profit on cash sales = (1.102 - 1) * 100 = 10.2%. Therefore, the correct value is 10.2%, corresponding to option (D).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q107. (Hard) A dealer allows a discount of 10% on the marked price of an article and still makes 17% profit. If the cost price of the article increases by 10%, how much discount percentage should he now offer on the original marked price so as to earn the same percentage of profit?
(A) 2%  (B) 1.5%  (C) 1%  (D) 0.5%

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Let initial Cost Price = 100. Selling Price = 117. Since discount is 10%, Marked Price = 117 / 0.90 = Rs 130. New Cost Price = 100 * 1.10 = 110. To maintain 17% profit, new Selling Price = 110 * 1.17 = Rs 128.70. Discount from original MP of 130 = 130 - 128.70 = Rs 1.30. New discount percentage = (1.30 / 130) * 100 = 1%. Psychological Trap: Examinees frequently estimate that no discount can be given or guess 5%, underestimating the exact geometric margin sensitivity. Therefore, the correct value is 1%, corresponding to option (C).

Q108. (Hard) A person buys 120 shirts at the rate of Rs 400 each. He sells 40 shirts at a gain of 10% and 50 shirts at a gain of 20%. At what gain percentage must he sell the remaining shirts so as to gain 15% on the entire transaction?

Q112. (Hard) An article is marked at a price which is 25% higher than its cost price. The shopkeeper gives a discount of x% on marked price and still makes a profit of 10%. Find the value of x.

Q114. (Hard) A wholesale dealer claims to sell grain at cost price, but by using false weights he gains 25%. How many grams does he actually give for a 1 kg weight?

Solution:
Let delivered weight be W grams. Gain percentage equation: [(1000 - W) / W] * 100 = 25 => (1000 - W) / W = 1 / 4 => 4000 - 4W = W => 5W = 4000 => W = 800 grams. Psychological Trap: Many examinees incorrectly take 1000 - 25% of 1000 = 750 grams, failing to recognize that the 25% gain is earned on the weight delivered. Therefore, the correct value is 800 grams, corresponding to option (B).

Q121. (Hard) If the selling price of an article is doubled, the profit triples. What is the original profit percentage?
(A) 100%  (B) 50%  (C) 150%  (D) 200%

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Let original Cost Price be C and Selling Price be S. Original profit = S - C. When Selling Price becomes 2S, new profit = 2S - C. Given 2S - C = 3(S - C) => 2S - C = 3S - 3C => S = 2C. Original profit percentage = [(2C - C) / C] * 100 = 100%. Therefore, the correct value is 100%, corresponding to option (A).
