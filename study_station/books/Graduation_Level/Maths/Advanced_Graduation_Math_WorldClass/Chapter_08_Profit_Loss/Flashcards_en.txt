Card 1
Front: What is the basic formula for Profit %?
Back: Profit % = (Profit / Cost Price) × 100. Always calculated on CP, never on SP.

Card 2
Front: What is the formula for Selling Price when Profit % is given?
Back: SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) / 100

Card 3
Front: What is the formula for Selling Price when Loss % is given?
Back: SP = CP × (100 − Loss%) / 100

Card 4
Front: If two items are sold at the same SP, one at x% profit and one at x% loss, what is the overall result?
Back: Always a loss of x²/100 %. (Mnemonic: "Same SP, opposite % — loss squares in!")

Card 5
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember that Profit/Loss % is always on CP?
Back: "CP is the Boss" — every percentage in Profit & Loss bows to the Cost Price, unless the question says otherwise.

Card 6
Front: What is the relationship between Marked Price (MP), Discount, and SP?
Back: SP = MP × (100 − Discount%) / 100. Discount is always calculated on MP.

Card 7
Front: What is the formula for successive discounts of a% and b%?
Back: Equivalent single discount = a + b − (ab/100) %

Card 8
Front: A trader marks goods 40% above CP and gives 10% discount. What is the profit %?
Back: Profit = 40 − 10 − (40×10/100) = 26%. (Use the successive-change formula: a + b + ab/100, with discount as negative.)

Card 9
Front: What is the formula linking MP and CP when discount d% and profit p% are given?
Back: MP/CP = (100 + p) / (100 − d)

Card 10
Front: What is a dishonest dealer's profit % when he uses false weights (sells 900 g as 1 kg)?
Back: Profit % = (Error / True weight sold) × 100 = (100/900) × 100 = 11.11%

Card 11
Front: What is the mnemonic for the false-weight profit formula?
Back: "Cheat on top, Truth below" — Profit % = (cheated amount / actual quantity delivered) × 100.

Card 12
Front: If SP of x articles equals CP of y articles, what is the profit %?
Back: Profit % = (y − x)/x × 100 (profit when y > x; loss when y < x)

Card 13
Front: A sells to B at 20% profit, B sells to C at 25% profit. What is C's price if A's CP is ₹100?
Back: ₹100 × 1.20 × 1.25 = ₹150. Chain transactions: multiply the factors.

Card 14
Front: What is the difference between Profit and Profit %?
Back: Profit is an absolute amount (SP − CP); Profit % is that amount expressed as a percentage of CP.

Card 15
Front: If CP of 12 articles = SP of 9 articles, what is the profit %?
Back: Profit % = (12 − 9)/9 × 100 = 33.33%

Card 16
Front: By selling at ₹X a man gains p%; what is his CP?
Back: CP = SP × 100 / (100 + p). Reverse the percentage — divide, don't subtract.

Card 17
Front: What happens to profit % if both CP and SP increase by the same amount?
Back: Profit % decreases, because the same absolute profit is now measured on a larger CP base.

Card 18
Front: A shopkeeper sells at 10% profit but uses 800 g weight for 1 kg. What is his total profit %?
Back: Effective SP factor = 1.10 × (1000/800) = 1.375 → Profit = 37.5%

Card 19
Front: What is the mnemonic for successive discount order?
Back: "Order doesn't matter, multiplication does" — 20% then 10% off gives the same result as 10% then 20% off (0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72).

Card 20
Front: If an article sold at 25% profit had been sold for ₹40 more, profit would be 45%. What is the CP?
Back: 20% of CP = ₹40 → CP = ₹200. The price gap equals the percentage-point gap applied to CP.
