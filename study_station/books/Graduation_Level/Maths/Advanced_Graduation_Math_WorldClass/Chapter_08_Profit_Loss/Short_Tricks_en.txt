# 🪄 Short Tricks & Mnemonics — Profit & Loss

---

**🎯 TRICK 1 — "CP is the King"**

> **"Every percentage bows to the COST PRICE."**

Profit %, Loss %, Discount % confusion? Remember: **Profit% and Loss% are ALWAYS calculated on CP** — never on SP. Only **Discount% lives on MP (Marked Price)**.

📌 *Memory hook:* The King (CP) collects the taxes (profit/loss %). The Shop Signboard (MP) only gives discounts.

---

**🎯 TRICK 2 — The "×1.x / ×0.x" Multiplier Trick**

> **Profit of 20% → multiply CP by 1.2**
> **Loss of 20% → multiply CP by 0.8**

Never write CP + 20% of CP. Just **one multiplication**:
- SP = CP × (1 + p/100) for profit
- SP = CP × (1 − p/100) for loss

📌 *Memory hook:* **"Profit ADDS a head (1.something), Loss CUTS the head (0.something)."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 3 — "DISH" for the Dishonest Dealer**

> **D**ishonest **I**s **S**tealing **H**eight (weight)!

A shopkeeper using false weights:
**Gain% = (Error ÷ (True weight − Error)) × 100**

Example: sells 900 g instead of 1000 g → Gain% = 100/900 × 100 = 11.11%

📌 *Memory hook:* The error sits on TOP, the honest leftover sits BELOW.

---

**🎯 TRICK 4 — "Same % Profit & Loss? You ALWAYS Lose!"**

> If an item is sold at **x% profit** and another at **x% loss** (same SP), the net result is **ALWAYS a loss** = **x²/100 %**

Example: 10% profit + 10% loss → Loss = 100/100 = **1% loss**

📌 *Memory hook:* **"Twin deals, twin pain — square the number, feel the strain."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 5 — "MP is the Umbrella"**

> Chain: **CP → MP → SP**
> **MP = SP × 100/(100 − d%)**

📌 *Memory hook:* The **Umbrella (MP)** is always BIGGER than what you actually pay (SP). Rain (discount) falls from the umbrella.

---

**🎯 TRICK 6 — "CP-SP Same Letter = Profit"**

> **P**rofit → SP **P**lus side (SP > CP)
> **L**oss → SP **L**ower side (SP < CP)

📌 *Memory hook:* **"Profit Pushes Price up, Loss Lowers it."** — the P's and L's alliterate!

---

**🎯 TRICK 7 — The 20% ↔ 25% Flip**

> If you must convert between CP-basis and SP-basis:
> - Profit 25% on CP = Profit 20% on SP
> - Loss 20% on CP = Loss 25% on SP

Why: CP : SP = 4 : 5 for a 25% profit on CP (gain 1 on 4 = 1/5 of SP = 20%). For a 20% loss on CP, CP : SP = 5 : 4 (loss 1 on 5 = 1/4 of SP = 25%).

⚠️ *Trap alert:* Loss 25% on CP is **NOT** 20% on SP — it is 33.33% on SP (CP : SP = 4 : 3, loss 1 on 4 = 1/3 of SP). Profit and loss do NOT flip symmetrically!

📌 *Memory hook:* **"Quarter on cost, fifth on sale"** — 1/4 vs 1/5.

---

**🎯 TRICK 8 — "Successive Discounts SHRINK, Never Add"**

> Two successive discounts of a% and b% ≠ (a+b)%
> **Net discount = a + b − (ab/100)**

Example: 20% + 10% = 20 + 10 − 2 = **28%** (not 30%!)

📌 *Memory hook:* **"Discounts are shy — together they give LESS than they promise."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 9 — "The 100-Rupee Shop" (Assumption Method)**

> Whenever CP is unknown and only percentages are given — **assume CP = ₹100.**

Everything becomes mental math: 20% profit → SP = ₹120. Done.

📌 *Memory hook:* **"When in doubt, open the ₹100 shop."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 10 — "Marked-Up, Marked-Down" Formula**

> If a trader marks goods **m% above CP** and gives **d% discount**:
> **Profit% = m − d − (md/100)**

Example: mark up 40%, discount 10% → 40 − 10 − 4 = **26% profit**

📌 *Memory hook:* Same formula as successive change — **"Up minus Down minus their product/100."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 11 — "SP Twins" Ratio Trick**

> Two articles sold at the **same SP**, one at x% profit, one at x% loss:
> **CP ratio = (100 − x) : (100 + x)**

Example: 25% profit & 25% loss → CPs are 75 : 125 = **3 : 5**

📌 *Memory hook:* **"Same selling price? Costs stand on opposite sides of 100."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 12 — "Fraction Friends" (Percentage ↔ Fraction)**

> Memorize these instant conversions:
> - 12.5% = 1/8 | 16.66% = 1/6 | 14.28% = 1/7
> - 11.11% = 1/9 | 9.09% = 1/11 | 8.33% = 1/12

If profit = 1/8 of CP → CP = 8 parts, profit = 1 part, SP = 9 parts. **Ratio method unlocked!**

📌 *Memory hook:* **"8-9, 6-7, 11-12 — fractions make P&L a breeze."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 13 — "Profit on SP? Convert First!"**

> If profit is given **on SP** (rare trap!), convert to CP basis:
> **Profit% on CP = [p/(100 − p)] × 100**

Example: 20% on SP → 20/80 × 100 = **25% on CP**

📌 *Memory hook:* **"SP-based profit is a liar — it always understates. Divide by (100 − p) to hear the truth."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 14 — "The Dishonest Double Dip"**

> A dealer cheats **twice**: marks up m% AND gives e% **less weight**.

Step 1 — Gain from false weight alone: **w = [e/(100 − e)] × 100** (Trick 3).
Step 2 — Combine with the mark-up using the successive formula:
**Total Gain% = m + w + (mw/100)**

Example: marks up 20% and gives 10% less weight → w = 10/90 × 100 = 11.11% → Total = 20 + 11.11 + (20 × 11.11)/100 ≈ **33.33% gain**

⚠️ *Trap alert:* Do NOT plug the "less weight %" directly into m + e + me/100 — first convert it to the actual gain% (divide by 100 − e).

📌 *Memory hook:* **"Two cheats = two successive profits — but convert the weight cheat first."**

---

**🎯 TRICK 15 — "Break-Even Bridge"**

> To find CP when SP and profit% are known:
> **CP = SP × 100/(100 + p)** — profit
> **CP = SP × 100/(100 − p)** — loss

📌 *Memory hook:* **"Cross the bridge backwards: profit → divide by MORE than 100; loss → divide by LESS than 100."**
