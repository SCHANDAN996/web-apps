101. Simplify: 48 ÷ 8 × 3 + 12 − 5 = ?
(a) 25 (b) 23 (c) 19 (d) 31
Answer: (a)
Solution: By BODMAS, division and multiplication go left to right: 48 ÷ 8 = 6, then 6 × 3 = 18, and 18 + 12 − 5 = 25. The trap is computing 8 × 3 = 24 first and then 48 ÷ 24 = 2, so the answer is 25.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Simplify: 7.5 × 8 + 15% of 240 = ?
(a) 90 (b) 84 (c) 96 (d) 102
Answer: (c)
Solution: 7.5 × 8 = 60 and 15% of 240 = 36, giving 60 + 36 = 96. The trap is computing 15% of 240 as 24 (confusing it with 10%), which gives 84, so the answer is 96.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Simplify: (3/5) of (7/9) of 675 = ?
(a) 325 (b) 305 (c) 315 (d) 295
Answer: (c)
Solution: (7/9) of 675 = 7 × 75 = 525, then (3/5) of 525 = 3 × 105 = 315. The trap is cancelling the wrong factors while combining 3/5 × 7/9, so the answer is 315.

Answer: (b)

120. Simplify: (17² − 15²) ÷ 8 = ?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 10 (d) 8
Answer: (d)
Solution: Using a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b): (17 + 15)(17 − 15) = 32 × 2 = 64, and 64 ÷ 8 = 8. The trap is computing 17² − 15² as (17 − 15)² = 4, so the answer is 8.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

125. Simplify: [(3/4 of 2/3 of 5/6 of 720) + 60] ÷ 9 = ?
(a) 45 (b) 30 (c) 35 (d) 40
Answer: (d)
Solution: 5/6 of 720 = 600, 2/3 of 600 = 400, and 3/4 of 400 = 300, then (300 + 60) ÷ 9 = 360 ÷ 9 = 40. The trap is applying the fractions to 720 one after another without reducing at each step, so the answer is 40.
