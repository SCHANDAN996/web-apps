Q101. (Medium) Find the value of (10^4 + 4 * 5^4) / (10^2 + 2 * 5^2 + 2 * 10 * 5).
(A) 50  (B) 100  (C) 25  (D) 75

Answer: (A)

Solution:
By Sophie Germain identity: (a^4 + 4b^4) / (a^2 + 2b^2 + 2ab) = a^2 + 2b^2 - 2ab. With a = 10, b = 5: 100 + 50 - 100 = 50. Therefore, the correct value is 50, corresponding to option (A).
