Card 1
Front: What is the correct order of operations in simplification?
Back: BODMAS — Brackets, Of (multiplication by/of), Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction. Division and Multiplication are done left to right; so are Addition and Subtraction.

Card 2
Front: Which mnemonic helps you remember the order of operations, and what does each letter stand for?
Back: "BODMAS" — B: Brackets, O: Of, D: Division, M: Multiplication, A: Addition, S: Subtraction. Memory line: "Big Old Dogs Make Awesome Snacks."

Card 3
Front: In what order are the three types of brackets removed?
Back: First the bar/vinculum (—), then small brackets ( ), then curly braces { }, then square brackets [ ]. Mnemonic: "Bar-Small-Curly-Square" (innermost to outermost).

Card 4
Front: What is the rule of signs when a minus sign precedes a bracket?
Back: Every sign inside the bracket flips: −(a − b + c) = −a + b − c. The most common exam trap is forgetting to flip the middle terms.

Card 5
Front: How do you quickly compute 48 × 25 without long multiplication?
Back: Since 25 = 100/4, divide by 4 and multiply by 100: 48 ÷ 4 = 12 → 1200. Same trick: ×50 = ÷2 ×100; ×125 = ÷8 ×1000.

Card 6
Front: What is the digit-sum (casting out nines) check, and when is it used?
Back: Add digits repeatedly until one digit remains; the digit sum of the answer must equal the digit sum of the computed expression (with 9 ≡ 0). Used to verify simplification answers in seconds.

Card 7
Front: When is approximation (rounding) acceptable in exams?
Back: When the question says "approximately equal to (≈)" or the options are widely spaced. Round each number to 1–2 significant figures, then compute. Never approximate when options differ by less than ~5%.

Card 8
Front: What is the quick trick to square a number ending in 5, e.g., 85²?
Back: Multiply the tens digit by (itself + 1), then append 25: 8 × 9 = 72 → 85² = 7225.

Card 9
Front: Which mnemonic remembers the fraction–percentage conversions 1/2 to 1/12?
Back: "Half-Third-Quarter-Fifth-Sixth…" chain: 50%, 33.33%, 25%, 20%, 16.66%, 14.28%, 12.5%, 11.11%, 10%, 9.09%, 8.33%. Trick line: "Every extra 1 in the denominator shrinks the share."

Card 10
Front: How do you simplify a surd expression like 1/(√3 + √2)?
Back: Rationalise: multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate (√3 − √2). Result: (√3 − √2)/(3 − 2) = √3 − √2.

Card 11
Front: What is the rule for comparing fractions like 7/9 and 5/7 quickly?
Back: Cross-multiply: 7×7 = 49 vs 5×9 = 45. Since 49 > 45, 7/9 > 5/7. The larger cross-product sits with the larger fraction.

Card 12
Front: What mnemonic recalls the squares you must memorise for simplification speed?
Back: "Sweet Sixteen to Thirty" — memorise squares 16² to 30² (256 … 900) and cubes 1³ to 15³. Trick: "Squares till 30, cubes till 15 — no calculator needed."

Card 13
Front: How do you compute 999 × 997 using the base method?
Back: Base 1000: deficiencies are −1 and −3. Cross-subtract: 999 − 3 = 996; multiply deficiencies: 1 × 3 = 003. Answer: 996003.

Card 14
Front: In a continued fraction like 2 + 1/(3 + 1/4), where do you start solving?
Back: Always from the bottom (deepest denominator) upward: 3 + 1/4 = 13/4 → 1/(13/4) = 4/13 → 2 + 4/13 = 30/13.

Card 15
Front: What is the trap in "a% of b" versus "b% of a"?
Back: There is none — they are always equal (a×b/100). Use this to pick the easier form: 8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2.

Card 16
Front: How do you approximate √85 without a calculator?
Back: Nearest perfect squares: 81 (9²) and 100 (10²). √85 ≈ 9 + (85−81)/(2×9) ≈ 9 + 4/18 ≈ 9.22. Formula: √(a² + x) ≈ a + x/2a.

Card 17
Front: What does the mnemonic "V-BODMAS" add to plain BODMAS?
Back: V = Vinculum (bar bracket), solved before all other brackets. Order: Vinculum → Brackets → Of → Division → Multiplication → Addition → Subtraction.

Card 18
Front: How do you quickly divide by 5, 25, or 125?
Back: ÷5 = ×2 then ÷10; ÷25 = ×4 then ÷100; ÷125 = ×8 then ÷1000. Example: 340 ÷ 25 = 1360 ÷ 100 = 13.6.

Card 19
Front: When simplifying (a + b)² − (a − b)², what is the instant result?
Back: 4ab. Memorising this identity turns a 30-second expansion into a 3-second answer.

Card 20
Front: What is the "unit digit" shortcut for checking large multiplications?
Back: Multiply only the unit digits; the unit digit of the product must match the unit digit of the answer. Eliminates wrong options instantly in MCQs.
