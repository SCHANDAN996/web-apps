51. What is 48% of 625 + 36% of 450?
(a) 452 (b) 482 (c) 472 (d) 462
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 452 comes from computing 36% of 450 as 152 instead of 162. 48% of 625 = (48 × 625)/100 = 300, and 36% of 450 = (36 × 450)/100 = 162. Sum = 300 + 162, so the answer is 462.
