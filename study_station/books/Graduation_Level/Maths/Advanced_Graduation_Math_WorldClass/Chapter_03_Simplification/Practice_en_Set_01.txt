1. Simplify: 48 ÷ 8 × 3 + 12 − 5
(a) 13 (b) 19 (c) 25 (d) 7
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students add 12 − 5 first or divide 48 by (8 × 3) = 24, getting 7, but division and multiplication go left to right. 48 ÷ 8 = 6; 6 × 3 = 18; 18 + 12 − 5 = 25, so the answer is 25.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

2. Simplify: 15 + 5 × 4 − 10 ÷ 2
(a) 70 (b) 35 (c) 30 (d) 25
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is solving left to right blindly: (15 + 5) × 4 = 80, which is wrong because BODMAS puts multiplication and division first. 5 × 4 = 20 and 10 ÷ 2 = 5; then 15 + 20 − 5 = 30, so the answer is 30.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

3. Simplify: 36 ÷ 6 + 4 × 5 − 8
(a) 26 (b) 22 (c) 14 (d) 18
Answer: (d)
Solution: Doing addition before multiplication gives 36 ÷ 6 + 4 = 10, then 10 × 5 − 8 = 42, which is the trap. Correctly: 36 ÷ 6 = 6, 4 × 5 = 20, so 6 + 20 − 8 = 18, so the answer is 18.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

4. Simplify: 2/3 of 90 + 1/5 of 75
(a) 75 (b) 70 (c) 65 (d) 80
Answer: (a)
Solution: A common slip is computing 2/3 of 90 = 60 and then adding 1/5 of 90 = 18, giving 78, by using the wrong base for the second fraction. Correctly: 60 + 15 = 75, so the answer is 75.

5. Simplify: 144 ÷ 12 × 2 − 6
(a) 6 (b) 18 (c) 30 (d) 12
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is dividing 144 by (12 × 2) = 24, giving 6, but division and multiplication are done left to right. 144 ÷ 12 = 12; 12 × 2 = 24; 24 − 6 = 18, so the answer is 18.

6. Simplify: 25% of 240 + 10% of 150
(a) 70 (b) 65 (c) 75 (d) 85
Answer: (c)
Solution: Some students take 25% of 240 as 50 by halving incorrectly, or add the percentages first (35% of 240 = 84), which mixes bases. 25% of 240 = 60 and 10% of 150 = 15; 60 + 15 = 75, so the answer is 75.

9. Simplify: 5/6 + 1/4 − 1/3
(a) 2/3 (b) 5/12 (c) 7/12 (d) 3/4
Answer: (d)
Solution: Adding numerators and denominators directly (5 + 1 − 1)/(6 + 4 − 3) = 5/7 is the classic trap. LCM of 6, 4, 3 is 12: 10/12 + 3/12 − 4/12 = 9/12 = 3/4, so the answer is 3/4.

10. Simplify: 96 ÷ 4 ÷ 2 × 3
(a) 48 (b) 16 (c) 24 (d) 36
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 96 ÷ (4 ÷ 2 × 3) = 96 ÷ 6 = 16 by grouping wrongly. Left to right: 96 ÷ 4 = 24; 24 ÷ 2 = 12; 12 × 3 = 36, so the answer is 36.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

11. Simplify: 18 − [6 − {4 − (8 − 6)}]
(a) 14 (b) 16 (c) 12 (d) 10
Answer: (a)
Solution: Removing brackets without sign care gives 18 − 6 − 4 − 8 + 6 = 6, the trap. Innermost first: (8 − 6) = 2; {4 − 2} = 2; [6 − 2] = 4; 18 − 4 = 14, so the answer is 14.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

12. Simplify: 45 × 12 ÷ 9 + 15
(a) 75 (b) 60 (c) 90 (d) 70
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dividing 45 by 9 first and then multiplying 12 wrongly ordered, or adding 15 before dividing, gives 90, the trap. 45 × 12 = 540; 540 ÷ 9 = 60; 60 + 15 = 75, so the answer is 75.

13. Simplify: 0.5 × 0.4 + 0.3 × 0.2

Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is adding before multiplying: 0.5 × (0.4 + 0.3) × 0.2 = 0.07, or misplacing decimals to get 2.6. 0.5 × 0.4 = 0.20 and 0.3 × 0.2 = 0.06; 0.20 + 0.06 = 0.26, so the answer is 0.26.

Solution: Using 64 as the base for both fractions (3/8 + 5/6 of 64) gives a non-integer mess, the trap of mixing bases. 3/8 of 64 = 24 and 5/6 of 36 = 30; 24 + 30 = 54, so the answer is 54.

25. Simplify: (13² − 12²) ÷ 5 + 7
(a) 14 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 8
Answer: (c)
Solution: Computing (13 − 12)² = 1, then 1 ÷ 5 + 7, is the trap of misusing the difference of squares. 13² − 12² = (13 − 12)(13 + 12) = 25; 25 ÷ 5 = 5; 5 + 7 = 12, so the answer is 12.
