Q1. (Easy) Find the value of 48 / 12 * (9 - 5) + 3.
(A) 19  (B) 4  (C) 16  (D) 22

Answer: (A)

Solution:
First evaluate parentheses: 9 - 5 = 4. Next division: 48 / 12 = 4. Then multiplication: 4 * 4 = 16. Finally addition: 16 + 3 = 19. Therefore, the correct value is 19, corresponding to option (A).
