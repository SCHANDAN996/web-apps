26. Simplify: 48 ÷ 8 × 3 + 12 − 5 × 2 = ?
(a) 26 (b) 22 (c) 24 (d) 20
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer 26 comes from adding before subtracting at the end. By BODMAS, division and multiplication go left to right first: 48 ÷ 8 = 6, then 6 × 3 = 18, and 5 × 2 = 10. Now 18 + 12 − 10 = 20, so the answer is 20.
