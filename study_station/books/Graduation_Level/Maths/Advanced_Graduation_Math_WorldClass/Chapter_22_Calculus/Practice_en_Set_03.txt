51. If y = x³ − 3x² + 5, then dy/dx at x = 2 is
(a) 0 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: Differentiate term by term: dy/dx = 3x² − 6x. At x = 2, this is 3(4) − 6(2) = 12 − 12 = 0. The tempting wrong choice 8 comes from substituting x = 2 into x³ only, ignoring the derivative step. So the answer is 0.

52. The derivative of sin x · cos x with respect to x is
(a) sin²x − cos²x (b) cos²x − sin²x (c) 2 sin x cos x (d) cos²x + sin²x

Solution: By the product rule, d/dx(sin x cos x) = cos x·cos x + sin x·(−sin x) = cos²x − sin²x. The tempting wrong choice 2 sin x cos x is the derivative of sin²x, not of sin x cos x. So the answer is cos²x − sin²x.
