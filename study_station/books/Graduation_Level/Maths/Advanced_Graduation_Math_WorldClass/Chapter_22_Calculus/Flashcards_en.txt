Card 1
Front: What is the derivative of xⁿ?
Back: n·xⁿ⁻¹ (Power Rule)

Card 2
Front: What is the derivative of sin x?
Back: cos x

Card 3
Front: What is the derivative of cos x?
Back: −sin x

Card 4
Front: What is the derivative of eˣ?
Back: eˣ (it is its own derivative)

Card 5
Front: What is the derivative of ln x?
Back: 1/x, for x > 0

Card 6
Front: State the Product Rule.
Back: d/dx(u·v) = u·v′ + v·u′

Card 7
Front: State the Quotient Rule.
Back: d/dx(u/v) = (v·u′ − u·v′) / v²

Card 8
Front: State the Chain Rule.
Back: d/dx[f(g(x))] = f′(g(x)) · g′(x) — differentiate the outer function, then multiply by the derivative of the inner one.

Card 9
Front: What is the mnemonic for the Quotient Rule?
Back: "Low D-High minus High D-Low, over Low squared" — (v·u′ − u·v′)/v². Remember: LOW comes first on top and gets squared at the bottom.

Card 10
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember which trig derivative gets a minus sign?
Back: "C goes negative" — derivatives of functions starting with C (cos x, cot x, cosec x) carry a minus sign: cos′ = −sin, cot′ = −cosec², cosec′ = −cosec·cot.

Card 11
Front: What does f′(x) = 0 at a point tell you?
Back: The tangent is horizontal there — the point is a stationary point (possible local max, min, or point of inflection).

Card 12
Front: How do you distinguish a local maximum from a local minimum using the second derivative?
Back: If f′(a) = 0 and f″(a) < 0 → local maximum; if f″(a) > 0 → local minimum.

Card 13
Front: What is ∫ xⁿ dx (n ≠ −1)?
Back: xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1) + C

Card 14
Front: What is ∫ (1/x) dx?
Back: ln|x| + C

Card 15
Front: State the First Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
Back: If F′(x) = f(x), then ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a) — definite integration is the reverse of differentiation.

Card 16
Front: What is the integration by parts formula, and its mnemonic for choosing u?
Back: ∫ u dv = u·v − ∫ v du. Choose u by ILATE: Inverse, Log, Algebraic, Trig, Exponential (in that priority order).

Card 17
Front: What is the area under a curve y = f(x) from a to b?
Back: ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx (taking absolute value where the curve dips below the x-axis).

Card 18
Front: What does a positive derivative say about a function?
Back: The function is increasing on that interval; negative derivative means decreasing.

Card 19
Front: What is the derivative of tan x?
Back: sec²x

Card 20
Front: What is ∫ eˣ dx and ∫ aˣ dx?
Back: ∫ eˣ dx = eˣ + C; ∫ aˣ dx = aˣ/ln a + C
