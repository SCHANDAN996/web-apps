Questions 76–100: Medium

76. In how many ways can 6 different books be arranged on a shelf so that two particular books are never together?
(a) 720 (b) 480 (c) 600 (d) 360
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to compute only the "together" cases and forget to subtract. Total arrangements = 6! = 720. Treat the two books as one unit: 5! × 2! = 240 arrangements where they are together. Required = 720 − 240 = 480, so the answer is 480.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

77. How many words can be formed from the letters of the word "BANANA"?
(a) 720 (b) 360 (c) 120 (d) 60
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is answering 6! = 720 while ignoring repeated letters. BANANA has 6 letters with A repeated 3 times and N repeated 2 times. Arrangements = 6!/(3! × 2!) = 720/12 = 60, so the answer is 60.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

78. A committee of 4 is to be formed from 6 men and 4 women such that it contains at least one woman. How many ways?
(a) 195 (b) 210 (c) 180 (d) 200
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing C(4,1) × C(9,3), which double-counts committees with more than one woman. Use complement: total committees C(10,4) = 210; all-men committees C(6,4) = 15. Required = 210 − 15 = 195, so the answer is 195.
Source: PYQ-style

79. In how many ways can 5 boys and 5 girls be seated in a row so that no two girls sit together?
(a) 10! / 2 (b) 5! × 5! (c) 6! × 5! × 2 (d) 5! × 6!
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing girls first, which leaves no guarantee of separation. Seat the 5 boys first: 5! ways. This creates 6 gaps (including ends); choose 5 gaps for the girls and arrange them: C(6,5) × 5! = 6 × 5!. Total = 5! × 6 × 5! = 5! × 6!, so the answer is 5! × 6!.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

80. How many diagonals does a polygon with 12 sides have?
(a) 48 (b) 66 (c) 60 (d) 54
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is using C(12,2) = 66, which counts the 12 sides as well. Diagonals = C(n,2) − n = 66 − 12 = 54, so the answer is 54.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

81. From 5 consonants and 4 vowels, how many words of 3 consonants and 2 vowels can be formed?
(a) 1440 (b) 3600 (c) 7200 (d) 720
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is stopping after selection: C(5,3) × C(4,2) = 60, forgetting arrangement. The 5 chosen letters can be arranged in 5! = 120 ways. Total = 60 × 120 = 7200, so the answer is 7200.

82. In how many ways can 8 people be seated around a circular table if two particular persons must sit together?
(a) 5040 (b) 1440 (c) 720 (d) 10080
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using linear arrangement 8! instead of circular. Treat the pair as one unit: 7 units around a circle give (7−1)! = 720 arrangements; the pair can swap internally in 2 ways. Total = 720 × 2 = 1440, so the answer is 1440.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

83. How many 4-digit numbers can be formed using the digits 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 without repetition?
(a) 360 (b) 180 (c) 240 (d) 300
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 6 × 5 × 4 × 3 = 360, which counts "numbers" starting with 0 that are not really 4-digit numbers. Thousands digit: 5 choices (1–5); then the remaining three places from the leftover 5 digits: 5 × 4 × 3 = 60. Total = 5 × 60 = 300, so the answer is 300.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

84. In how many ways can the letters of "MISSISSIPPI" be arranged?
(a) 34650 (b) 39916800 (c) 69300 (d) 3465
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 11! while ignoring repetitions. MISSISSIPPI has 11 letters: I ×4, S ×4, P ×2, M ×1. Arrangements = 11!/(4! × 4! × 2!) = 39916800/1152 = 34650, so the answer is 34650.

85. A box contains 4 red and 5 blue balls. In how many ways can 3 balls be drawn so that at least one is red?
(a) 74 (b) 84 (c) 64 (d) 80
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing C(4,1) × C(8,2) = 112, which double-counts draws with two red balls. Use complement: total C(9,3) = 84; all-blue C(5,3) = 10. Required = 84 − 10 = 74, so the answer is 74.

86. How many numbers between 100 and 1000 have all distinct digits?
(a) 504 (b) 648 (c) 729 (d) 576
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 9 × 9 × 9 = 729, which allows the tens digit to repeat the hundreds digit. Hundreds digit: 9 choices (1–9); tens digit: 9 choices (0–9 except the one used); units digit: 8 choices. Total = 9 × 9 × 8 = 648, so the answer is 648.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

87. In how many ways can 10 identical pens be distributed among 4 children so that each child gets at least one pen?
(a) 120 (b) 84 (c) 286 (d) 210
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is using C(13,3) = 286, which allows children to get zero pens. Give each child one pen first, then distribute the remaining 6 freely: C(6+4−1, 4−1) = C(9,3) = 84, so the answer is 84.
Source: PYQ-style

88. How many ways are there to select a cricket team of 11 from 15 players, if a particular player must be included?
(a) C(14,10) (b) C(15,11) (c) C(14,11) (d) C(15,10)
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is choosing C(15,11) = 1365, which ignores the fixed player. With one player fixed, choose the remaining 10 from the other 14: C(14,10) = 1001, so the answer is C(14,10).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

89. In how many ways can 4 men and 4 women sit around a round table so that men and women alternate?
(a) 144 (b) 576 (c) 288 (d) 72
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is using linear alternating arrangements 4! × 4! × 2 = 1152, which overcounts rotations. Fix the 4 men around the circle: (4−1)! = 6 ways. The 4 women go into the 4 gaps between men: 4! = 24 ways. Total = 6 × 24 = 144, so the answer is 144.
Source: PYQ-style

90. How many 5-letter words (meaningful or not) can be formed from the letters of "EQUATION" if each word must contain exactly 3 vowels and 2 consonants?
(a) 1800 (b) 7200 (c) 3600 (d) 5400
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is stopping at selection C(5,3) × C(3,2) = 30 and forgetting arrangement. EQUATION has 5 vowels and 3 consonants. Select: C(5,3) × C(3,2) = 10 × 3 = 30. Arrange the 5 letters: 5! = 120. Total = 30 × 120 = 3600, so the answer is 3600.
Source: PYQ-style

91. In how many ways can 7 different prizes be given to 4 students if each student can receive any number of prizes?
(a) 16384 (b) 2401 (c) 840 (d) 4096
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is computing 7^4 = 2401, which assigns students to prizes the wrong way round. Each of the 7 prizes can go to any of the 4 students independently: 4^7 = 16384, so the answer is 16384.

92. How many triangles can be formed from 10 points in a plane, of which 4 are collinear?
(a) 120 (b) 116 (c) 110 (d) 114
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is answering C(10,3) = 120, which counts degenerate selections from the collinear points. Subtract the C(4,3) = 4 selections that lie on one line: 120 − 4 = 116, so the answer is 116.

93. In how many ways can the letters of "ARRANGE" be arranged so that the two R's are never together?
(a) 360 (b) 1260 (c) 900 (d) 720
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing total arrangements 7!/(2!×2!) = 1260 and stopping. Arrangements with both R's together: treat RR as one unit, giving 6 units with A repeated twice: 6!/2! = 360. Required = 1260 − 360 = 900, so the answer is 900.

94. A group has 8 members. In how many ways can a president, a secretary and a treasurer be chosen if no person can hold two posts?
(a) 512 (b) 672 (c) 336 (d) 56
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is using 8^3 = 512, which allows one person to hold multiple posts. Since posts are distinct and holders must differ: 8 × 7 × 6 = 336, so the answer is 336.

95. How many 6-digit numbers can be formed from digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 without repetition that are divisible by 5?
(a) 120 (b) 240 (c) 720 (d) 600
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is answering 6! = 720, ignoring divisibility. A number is divisible by 5 only if its last digit is 5 (0 is unavailable). Fix 5 at the units place; arrange the remaining 5 digits in 5! = 120 ways, so the answer is 120.

96. In how many ways can 5 different rings be worn on 4 fingers of one hand, if each finger can hold any number of rings and the order of rings on a finger matters?
(a) 625 (b) 1024 (c) 6720 (d) 840
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing 4^5 = 1024, which ignores the order of rings on each finger. Place rings one by one: the first ring has 4 choices, and each subsequent ring adds one new position (it can sit above or below existing rings on any finger), giving 4 × 5 × 6 × 7 × 8 = 6720, so the answer is 6720.
Source: PYQ-style

97. From a group of 7 men and 6 women, a committee of 5 is to be formed with at least 3 men. How many ways?
(a) 7560 (b) 812 (c) 1287 (d) 756
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing C(7,3) × C(10,2) = 1575, which double-counts committees with more than 3 men. Count cases: 3 men 2 women: C(7,3)×C(6,2) = 35×15 = 525; 4 men 1 woman: 35×6 = 210; 5 men: 21. Total = 525 + 210 + 21 = 756, so the answer is 756.
Source: PYQ-style

98. How many words can be formed using all letters of "DAUGHTER" so that vowels always occupy odd positions?
(a) 4320 (b) 2880 (c) 1440 (d) 5760
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is arranging the vowels in any 3 of the 8 positions instead of only the odd ones. DAUGHTER has 3 vowels (A, U, E) and 5 consonants; the odd positions are 1, 3, 5, 7 — four positions. Place the vowels in P(4,3) = 24 ways and the consonants in the remaining 5 positions in 5! = 120 ways. Total = 24 × 120 = 2880, so the answer is 2880.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

99. In how many ways can 9 people be divided into three groups of 3 each?
(a) 84 (b) 1680 (c) 280 (d) 560
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is computing C(9,3) × C(6,3) × C(3,3) = 1680, which treats the groups as labelled. Since the groups are identical in size and unlabelled, divide by 3!: 1680/6 = 280, so the answer is 280.
Source: PYQ-style

100. How many 4-digit even numbers can be formed using digits 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 without repetition?
(a) 180 (b) 120 (c) 144 (d) 156
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is a single-case count that mishandles zero in the thousands place. Split by units digit. Units digit 0: thousands 5 ways, then 4 × 3 for the middle: 5 × 4 × 3 = 60. Units digit 2 or 4 (2 choices): thousands digit 4 choices (non-zero, not the used digit), then the middle two from the remaining 4 digits: 4 × 3 = 12, giving 2 × 4 × 12 = 96. Total = 60 + 96 = 156, so the answer is 156.
Source: PYQ-style
