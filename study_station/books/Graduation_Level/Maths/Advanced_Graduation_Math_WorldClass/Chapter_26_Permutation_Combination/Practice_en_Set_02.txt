26. In how many ways can 6 boys and 4 girls be seated in a row so that no two girls sit together?
(a) 10! − 6!4! (b) 6! × ⁶P₄ (c) 6! × ⁷P₄ (d) 7! × 4!
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 10! − 6!4! wrongly assumes only the "all girls together" case needs removal, ignoring partial adjacency. First seat the 6 boys in 6! ways; this creates 7 gaps (including ends). Choose and arrange 4 girls in these gaps in ⁷P₄ ways, so the answer is 6! × ⁷P₄.

27. How many words can be formed using all the letters of the word "BANANA"?

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap answer 720 treats all 6 letters as distinct, but BANANA has 3 A's and 2 N's. Total arrangements = 6!/(3! × 2!) = 720/12 = 60, so the answer is 60.
