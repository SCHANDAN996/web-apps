1. In how many different ways can the letters of the word "CAT" be arranged?
(a) 3 (b) 9 (c) 6 (d) 12
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 3 comes from simply counting the letters instead of arranging them. The word has 3 distinct letters, so the number of arrangements is 3! = 3 × 2 × 1 = 6, so the answer is 6.

2. What is the value of 5! ?
(a) 25 (b) 60 (c) 720 (d) 120
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 25 comes from wrongly multiplying 5 by 5. By definition 5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120, so the answer is 120.
