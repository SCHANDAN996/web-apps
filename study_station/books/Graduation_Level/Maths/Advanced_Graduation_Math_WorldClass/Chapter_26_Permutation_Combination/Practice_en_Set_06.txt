126. In how many distinct ways can the letters of the word "MISSISSIPPI" be arranged?
(a) 39916800 (b) 34650 (c) 69300 (d) 17325
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 39916800 is simply 11!, which wrongly treats all letters as distinct. The word has 11 letters with I repeated 4 times, S repeated 4 times, and P repeated 2 times, so the count is 11!/(4! × 4! × 2!) = 39916800/1152 = 34650, so the answer is 34650.

127. Eight persons are to be seated around a circular table. In how many ways can they be seated if two particular persons must never sit together?
(a) 5040 (b) 3600 (c) 1440 (d) 4320
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to subtract the "together" case from 8! instead of from the circular total 7!. Circular arrangements of 8 persons = 7! = 5040. Arrangements with the two together = 2 × 6! = 1440. Required = 5040 − 1440 = 3600, so the answer is 3600.

128. Four letters are placed into four addressed envelopes at random. In how many ways can this be done so that no letter goes into its correct envelope?
(a) 6 (b) 11 (c) 9 (d) 24
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students guess 6 by trial and miss cases. This is a derangement problem: D4 = 4! × (1 − 1/1! + 1/2! − 1/3! + 1/4!) = 24 × (9/24) = 9, so the answer is 9.

129. How many onto (surjective) functions can be defined from a set of 5 elements to a set of 3 elements?
(a) 243 (b) 150 (c) 93 (d) 60
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option 243 is just 3^5, the count of all functions, ignoring the "onto" condition. By inclusion–exclusion: 3^5 − C(3,1)·2^5 + C(3,2)·1^5 = 243 − 96 + 3 = 150, so the answer is 150.

130. A committee of 5 is to be formed from 5 men and 4 women such that it contains at least 3 women. In how many ways can this be done?
(a) 45 (b) 126 (c) 40 (d) 81
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to compute only the "exactly 3 women" case (40 ways) and forget the "4 women" case. Exactly 3 women and 2 men: C(4,3) × C(5,2) = 4 × 10 = 40. Exactly 4 women and 1 man: C(4,4) × C(5,1) = 5. Total = 40 + 5 = 45, so the answer is 45.

131. How many diagonals does a convex polygon with 12 sides have?
