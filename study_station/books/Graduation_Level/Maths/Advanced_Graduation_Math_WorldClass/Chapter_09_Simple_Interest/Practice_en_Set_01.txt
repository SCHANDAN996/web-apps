1. Find the simple interest on ₹5,000 at 8% per annum for 3 years.
(a) ₹1,000 (b) ₹1,600 (c) ₹1,400 (d) ₹1,200
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is computing 8% of 5000 = 400 and stopping there, forgetting the 3 years. SI = PRT/100 = (5000 × 8 × 3)/100 = 1200, so the answer is ₹1,200.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

2. A sum of ₹8,000 is lent at 5% per annum simple interest. What is the interest earned in 4 years?
(a) ₹1,600 (b) ₹1,400 (c) ₹1,200 (d) ₹1,800
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting error is using 5 × 4 = 20 and writing ₹200 instead of 20% of the principal. SI = (8000 × 5 × 4)/100 = 1600, so the answer is ₹1,600.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

3. At what rate percent per annum will ₹6,000 earn a simple interest of ₹900 in 3 years?
(a) 4% (b) 6% (c) 5% (d) 8%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students divide 900 by 6000 and report 15%, ignoring the time factor. R = (100 × SI)/(P × T) = (100 × 900)/(6000 × 3) = 5, so the answer is 5%.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

4. In how many years will ₹4,500 amount to ₹5,400 at 5% per annum simple interest?
(a) 4 years (b) 3 years (c) 5 years (d) 6 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is using the amount 5400 as the interest; interest is only 5400 − 4500 = 900. T = (100 × 900)/(4500 × 5) = 4, so the answer is 4 years.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

5. What sum will amount to ₹7,200 in 5 years at 8% per annum simple interest?
(a) ₹5,000 (b) ₹5,143 (c) ₹5,500 (d) ₹6,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The common mistake is subtracting 40% of 7200; the 40% applies to the principal, not the amount. P = (100 × 7200)/(100 + 8 × 5) = 720000/140 ≈ 5143, so the answer is ₹5,143.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

6. The simple interest on a sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is ₹800. Find the sum.
(a) ₹3,600 (b) ₹4,800 (c) ₹4,400 (d) ₹4,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: A frequent slip is computing 800 × 10/2 = 4000 wrongly inverted as 800 × 2/10 × 100 confusion; use P = (100 × SI)/(R × T) = (100 × 800)/(10 × 2) = 4000, so the answer is ₹4,000.

Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is rounding 2.5 years to 3 years, which gives ₹14,160. SI = (12000 × 6 × 2.5)/100 = 1800; Amount = 12000 + 1800 = 13800, so the answer is ₹13,800.

Solution: Many assume doubling means 20% rate; doubling means interest equals principal, so R × T = 100. R = 100/10 = 10, so the answer is 10%.

15. A sum becomes ₹9,200 in 3 years and ₹10,400 in 6 years at simple interest. The sum is:
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹7,600 (c) ₹8,400 (d) ₹8,800
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is treating the growth as compound. Interest for 3 extra years = 10400 − 9200 = 1200, so yearly interest = 400; interest for 3 years = 1200; principal = 9200 − 1200 = 8000, so the answer is ₹8,000.

20. A person deposits ₹20,000 in a bank at 6% per annum simple interest. After how many years will the interest be ₹6,000?
(a) 5 years (b) 4 years (c) 6 years (d) 8 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting slip is 6000/20000 = 30% reported as 30 years. T = (100 × 6000)/(20000 × 6) = 5, so the answer is 5 years.

21. A sum of money triples itself in 16 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 5 times itself at the same rate?
(a) 24 years (b) 28 years (c) 32 years (d) 40 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is proportionality of amounts (16 × 5/3), which is wrong because interest, not amount, grows linearly. Tripling means interest = 2P in 16 years, so P of interest takes 8 years; five times means interest = 4P, needing 4 × 8 = 32, so the answer is 32 years.
Source: PYQ-style

22. A sum was invested at simple interest at a certain rate for 3 years. Had it been invested at 2% higher rate, it would have fetched ₹720 more. The sum is:
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹15,000 (c) ₹14,000 (d) ₹12,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: Extra interest = P × 2 × 3/100 = 720, so P = 720 × 100/6 = 12000. The trap is dividing 720 by 2 only, ignoring the 3 years, so the answer is ₹12,000.
Source: PYQ-style

23. A lent ₹5,000 to B for 2 years and ₹3,000 to C for 4 years at the same rate of simple interest, and received ₹2,200 in all as interest. The rate percent per annum is:
(a) 8% (b) 9% (c) 10% (d) 12%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Total interest = (5000 × 2 × R + 3000 × 4 × R)/100 = 220R = 2200, giving R = 10. The trap is averaging the principals or times wrongly, so the answer is 10%.
Source: PYQ-style

24. A sum of ₹1,550 was lent partly at 5% and partly at 8% per annum simple interest. The total interest received after 3 years was ₹300. The ratio of money lent at 5% to that at 8% is:
(a) 5 : 8 (b) 8 : 5 (c) 15 : 16 (d) 16 : 15
Answer: (d)
Solution: Let x be at 5%; then (x × 5 × 3 + (1550 − x) × 8 × 3)/100 = 300, giving 15x + 37200 − 24x = 30000, so x = 800 and the rest is 750; ratio 800 : 750 = 16 : 15. The trap is inverting the ratio, so the answer is 16 : 15.
Source: PYQ-style

25. The simple interest on a sum of money is 4/9 of the principal, and the number of years is equal to the rate percent per annum. The rate percent per annum is:
(a) 5% (b) 6% (c) 6⅔% (d) 7½%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Given SI/P = 4/9 and R = T, so R²/100 = 4/9, giving R² = 400/9 and R = 20/3 = 6⅔. The trap is taking R = 4/9 × 100 directly, so the answer is 6⅔%.
Source: PYQ-style
