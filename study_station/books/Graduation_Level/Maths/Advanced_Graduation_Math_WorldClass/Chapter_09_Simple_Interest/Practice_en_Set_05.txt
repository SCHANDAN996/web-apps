101. What is the simple interest on ₹8,000 at 6% per annum for 3 years?
(a) ₹1,240 (b) ₹1,340 (c) ₹1,540 (d) ₹1,440
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer ₹1,540 comes from adding ₹100 extra through a slip in multiplication. SI = (P × R × T)/100 = (8000 × 6 × 3)/100 = 1440, so the answer is ₹1,440.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

102. A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 10% (b) 15% (c) 12.5% (d) 8%
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students pick 10% by wrongly dividing 100 by 10 instead of using the correct relation. If the sum doubles, SI = P, so R = (SI × 100)/(P × T) = 100/8 = 12.5, so the answer is 12.5%.
Source: PYQ-style

103. Find the amount when ₹6,000 is invested at 5% per annum simple interest for 4 years.
(a) ₹7,000 (b) ₹7,400 (c) ₹7,200 (d) ₹6,800
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option ₹6,800 uses only 2 years of interest instead of 4. SI = (6000 × 5 × 4)/100 = 1200, and Amount = 6000 + 1200 = 7200, so the answer is ₹7,200.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics (Comparing Quantities)

104. The simple interest on a sum at 5% per annum for 6 years is ₹1,500. Find the sum.
(a) ₹4,500 (b) ₹6,000 (c) ₹5,500 (d) ₹5,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: Choosing ₹4,500 results from dividing 1500 by 30 and then multiplying by 90 incorrectly. P = (SI × 100)/(R × T) = (1500 × 100)/(5 × 6) = 150000/30 = 5000, so the answer is ₹5,000.

105. In what time will ₹5,000 earn a simple interest of ₹900 at 6% per annum?

Answer: (a)
Solution: The distractor 2.5 years comes from using 720 instead of 900 in the numerator. T = (SI × 100)/(P × R) = (900 × 100)/(5000 × 6) = 90000/30000 = 3, so the answer is 3 years.

108. A sum of money amounts to ₹11,500 in 5 years and ₹13,000 in 8 years at simple interest. Find the sum.
(a) ₹8,500 (b) ₹10,000 (c) ₹9,500 (d) ₹9,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap ₹10,000 comes from subtracting only one year's interest from ₹11,500. Interest for 3 years = 13000 − 11500 = 1500, so yearly interest = 500; interest for 5 years = 2500; principal = 11500 − 2500 = 9000, so the answer is ₹9,000.

109. Using the data that a sum amounts to ₹11,500 in 5 years and ₹13,000 in 8 years, find the rate of interest.
(a) 5% (b) 50/9% (c) 6% (d) 25/4%
Answer: (b)
Solution: The neat-looking 5% is a trap because the yearly interest of ₹500 divided by ₹10,000 gives it, but the principal is ₹9,000, not ₹10,000. R = (500 × 100)/9000 = 50/9, so the answer is 50/9%.

110. The simple interest on a sum at 12% per annum for 4 years is ₹800 more than that at 10% per annum for 4 years. Find the sum.
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹8,000 (c) ₹12,000 (d) ₹16,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The option ₹8,000 is tempting if one treats the rate difference as 10% instead of 2%. The difference in rates is 2% over 4 years, i.e. 8% of the principal equals 800, so P = 800 × 100/8 = 10000, so the answer is ₹10,000.

111. A sum lent at 8% per annum for 3 years earns ₹540 more simple interest than the same sum at 6% per annum for 3 years. Find the sum.
(a) ₹8,000 (b) ₹10,000 (c) ₹9,000 (d) ₹12,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: Choosing ₹10,000 comes from dividing 540 by 5.4 incorrectly. The rate gap is 2% over 3 years, i.e. 6% of P = 540, giving P = 540 × 100/6 = 9000, so the answer is ₹9,000.

112. ₹5,000 is lent in two parts, one at 4% and the other at 6% per annum simple interest. The total interest after 2 years is ₹520. How much was lent at 4%?
(a) ₹1,500 (b) ₹2,000 (c) ₹2,500 (d) ₹3,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap ₹3,000 is the amount lent at 6%, not at 4%. Let x be lent at 4%: [4x + 6(5000 − x)] × 2/100 = 520, so 30000 − 2x = 26000, giving x = 2000, so the answer is ₹2,000.
Source: PYQ-style

113. A sum of money triples itself in 15 years at simple interest. The rate of interest per annum is:
(a) 13⅓% (b) 12% (c) 15% (d) 20%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The option 20% is tempting because 100/5 = 20, but tripling means interest equals twice the principal, not the principal itself. SI = 2P, so R = (2P × 100)/(P × 15) = 200/15 = 13⅓, so the answer is 13⅓%.
Source: PYQ-style

114. The simple interest on ₹9,600 at a certain rate for 3 years equals the simple interest on ₹7,200 at 8% per annum for 4 years. Find the rate.
(a) 6% (b) 7% (c) 8% (d) 9%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The option 9% comes from rounding errors while dividing. SI on the second sum = (7200 × 8 × 4)/100 = 2304; then R = (2304 × 100)/(9600 × 3) = 230400/28800 = 8, so the answer is 8%.

117. A sum amounts to ₹7,200 in 2 years and to ₹8,400 in 5 years at simple interest. Find the rate of interest.

119. Two sums are in the ratio 2:3, invested at rates in the ratio 3:4 for times in the ratio 4:5. The ratio of their simple interests is:
(a) 3:5 (b) 2:5 (c) 1:2 (d) 5:8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap 3:5 comes from multiplying only rates and times, ignoring the principals. SI ratio = (2 × 3 × 4) : (3 × 4 × 5) = 24 : 60 = 2 : 5, so the answer is 2:5.

120. A sum at simple interest becomes 5/4 of itself in 4 years. The rate of interest per annum is:
(a) 6.25% (b) 5% (c) 8% (d) 10%
Answer: (a)
Solution: The option 5% comes from dividing 20 by 4 directly without noting the interest is one-fourth of the principal. SI = P/4, so R = (P/4 × 100)/(P × 4) = 25/4 = 6.25, so the answer is 6.25%.

121. ₹12,000 is lent partly at 8% and the rest at 10% per annum simple interest. The total interest earned in 3 years is ₹3,240. How much was lent at 8%?
(a) ₹4,000 (b) ₹5,000 (c) ₹6,000 (d) ₹8,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap ₹8,000 comes from assuming more money sits at the lower rate without computing. Average rate = (3240 × 100)/(12000 × 3) = 9%, which is exactly midway between 8% and 10%, so the parts are equal: 6000 each, so the answer is ₹6,000.
Source: PYQ-style

122. A sum doubles itself in 10 years at simple interest. In how many years will it triple itself at the same rate?
(a) 15 years (b) 20 years (c) 18 years (d) 25 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting 15 years comes from assuming time scales directly with the amount multiple. Doubling needs interest equal to P in 10 years, so rate = 10%; tripling needs interest of 2P, which takes 2 × 10 = 20 years, so the answer is 20 years.
Source: PYQ-style

124. A man borrowed ₹25,000 at simple interest and repaid ₹31,000 after 3 years. The rate of interest per annum was:

125. Equal sums are invested at 5% per annum for 4 years and at 7% per annum for 6 years, both at simple interest. The total interest earned is ₹6,200. Find each sum.
(a) ₹10,000 (b) ₹9,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹12,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap ₹8,000 comes from averaging the rates and times incorrectly. Total interest = P × (5 × 4 + 7 × 6)/100 = P × 62/100 = 6200, so P = 6200 × 100/62 = 10000, so the answer is ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style
