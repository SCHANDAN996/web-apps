Q126. (Very Hard) A person borrowed a sum of Rs 18,900 at 10% per annum simple interest. He agreed to repay the loan in two equal annual installments, the first installment being paid at the end of the first year and the second at the end of the second year. Find the amount of each installment under standard simple interest rules.
(A) Rs 10,800  (B) Rs 10,500  (C) Rs 11,000  (D) Rs 10,200

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Under the standard simple interest debt model: Total debt due at the end of 2 years on Rs 18,900 = 18900 + (18900 * 10 * 2) / 100 = 18900 + 3780 = Rs 22,680. If x is the annual installment: Installment 1 (paid at year 1 end) earns interest for 1 year = x + 0.10x = 1.10x. Installment 2 (paid at year 2 end) earns interest for 0 years = x. Total discharged = 1.10x + x = 2.10x. Equating to debt: 2.10x = 22680 => x = 22680 / 2.10 = 226800 / 21 = Rs 10,800. Psychological Trap: A common error is treating the loan as compound interest EMI or ignoring the interest accrued on the first installment. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 10,800, corresponding to option (A).

Q130. (Very Hard) The banker's gain on a bill due 2 years hence at 5% per annum simple interest is Rs 40. Find the face value (amount) of the bill.
(A) Rs 4,400  (B) Rs 4,000  (C) Rs 4,200  (D) Rs 4,500

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Time T = 2 years, Rate R = 5%. Banker's Gain BG = (TD * R * T) / 100 => 40 = (TD * 5 * 2) / 100 = 10*TD / 100 = TD / 10 => True Discount TD = 40 * 10 = Rs 400. Present Worth PW = (TD * 100) / (R * T) = (400 * 100) / 10 = Rs 4,000. Face Value (Amount) = PW + TD = 4000 + 400 = Rs 4,400. Psychological Trap: A common mistake is mistaking Present Worth (Rs 4,000) for the Face Value, neglecting the addition of True Discount. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 4,400, corresponding to option (A).
