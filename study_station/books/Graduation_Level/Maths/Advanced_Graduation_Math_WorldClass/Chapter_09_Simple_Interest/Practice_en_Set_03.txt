51. Find the simple interest on ₹8000 at 6% per annum for 3 years.
(a) ₹1200 (b) ₹1800 (c) ₹1600 (d) ₹1440
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option ₹1600 comes from wrongly using 4 years instead of 3. SI = (P × R × T)/100 = (8000 × 6 × 3)/100 = ₹1440, so the answer is 1440.

52. A sum of money doubles itself in 8 years at simple interest. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(a) 10% (b) 12% (c) 12.5% (d) 15%
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is answering 8% by simply reading the time. If the sum doubles, SI = P, so R = (SI × 100)/(P × T) = 100/8 = 12.5%, so the answer is 12.5%.
