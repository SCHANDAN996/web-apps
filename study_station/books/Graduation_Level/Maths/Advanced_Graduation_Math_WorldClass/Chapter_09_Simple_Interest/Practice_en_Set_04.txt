Q76. (Hard) What annual installment will discharge a debt of Rs 6,450 due in 4 years at 5% simple interest per annum?
(A) Rs 1,500  (B) Rs 1,600  (C) Rs 1,450  (D) Rs 1,550

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Let the annual installment be x. The 4 installments paid at the ends of years 1, 2, 3, and 4 accumulate interest for 3, 2, 1, and 0 years respectively. Psychological Trap: Candidates often mistakenly divide 6,450 by 4 = 1612.50, completely neglecting the interest earned on early installments. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 1,500, corresponding to option (A).
