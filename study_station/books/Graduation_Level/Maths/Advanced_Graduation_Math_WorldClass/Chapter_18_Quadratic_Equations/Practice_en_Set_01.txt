1. What are the roots of the equation x² − 5x + 6 = 0?
(a) 2 and 3 (b) 1 and 6 (c) −2 and −3 (d) 5 and 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students pick 1 and 6 because they multiply to 6, but their sum is 7, not 5. Factorising: x² − 5x + 6 = (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0, since 2 + 3 = 5 and 2 × 3 = 6, so the answer is 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

2. The discriminant of the quadratic equation 2x² + 3x + 1 = 0 is:
(a) −1 (b) 1 (c) 17 (d) 9
Answer: (b)
Solution: A common slip is computing b² + 4ac = 9 + 8 = 17, but the discriminant is b² − 4ac. Here D = 9 − 4(2)(1) = 9 − 8 = 1, so the answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

3. If the roots of x² − 8x + k = 0 are equal, then k equals:
(a) 16 (b) 8 (c) 4 (d) 64
Answer: (a)
Solution: Equal roots need D = 0. Students often halve 8 and square wrongly to get 8 or 4. Here 64 − 4k = 0 gives k = 16, so the answer is 16.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

4. The sum of the roots of 3x² − 12x + 7 = 0 is:
(a) −4 (b) 4 (c) 12 (d) 7/3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is forgetting the sign: sum of roots = −b/a, not b/a, so −4 is wrong. Sum = −(−12)/3 = 12/3 = 4, so the answer is 4.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

5. The product of the roots of 5x² + 2x − 3 = 0 is:
(a) 3/5 (b) 2/5 (c) −3/5 (d) −2/5
Answer: (c)
Solution: Product of roots = c/a = −3/5. Choosing 3/5 ignores the negative sign of the constant term, so the answer is −3/5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

6. Which of the following is a quadratic equation?
(a) x² + 3x + 2 = 0 (b) x³ − x = 0 (c) x + 1/x = 2 (d) √x + x = 5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The equation x + 1/x = 2 looks quadratic after multiplying by x, but as written it is not a polynomial equation; x³ − x is cubic. Only x² + 3x + 2 = 0 is a polynomial of degree 2, so the answer is x² + 3x + 2 = 0.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

7. The nature of the roots of x² + 2x + 5 = 0 is:
(a) Imaginary (b) Real and distinct (c) Real and equal (d) Rational
Answer: (a)
Solution: D = 4 − 20 = −16 < 0. Students who miscompute D as 4 + 20 = 24 pick "real and distinct", but a negative discriminant means no real roots, so the answer is imaginary.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

8. The 10th term of the AP 3, 7, 11, 15, … is:
(a) 43 (b) 37 (c) 40 (d) 39
Answer: (d)
Solution: Using a + nd instead of a + (n−1)d gives 3 + 40 = 43, the tempting wrong option. Correctly, T₁₀ = 3 + 9×4 = 39, so the answer is 39.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

9. The sum of the first 20 natural numbers is:
(a) 200 (b) 190 (c) 210 (d) 220
Answer: (c)
Solution: Using n(n+1)/2 with n = 20 gives 20×21/2 = 210. Forgetting to divide by 2 gives 420, and using n = 19 gives 190, so the answer is 210.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

10. The common difference of the AP 10, 7, 4, 1, … is:
(a) −3 (b) 3 (c) 7 (d) −7
Answer: (a)
Solution: d = 7 − 10 = −3. Taking the absolute value gives 3, which is the trap, so the answer is −3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

11. The 5th term of the GP 2, 6, 18, … is:
(a) 54 (b) 486 (c) 162 (d) 108
Answer: (c)
Solution: T₅ = ar⁴ = 2 × 3⁴ = 2 × 81 = 162. Using ar⁵ gives 486, the off-by-one trap, so the answer is 162.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

12. The sum of the infinite GP 1, 1/2, 1/4, … is:
(a) 3/2 (b) 2 (c) 1 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: S = a/(1 − r) = 1/(1 − 1/2) = 2. Using a/(1 + r) gives 2/3 and stopping after a few terms gives 3/2, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

13. If α and β are roots of x² − 7x + 12 = 0, then α² + β² equals:
(a) 24 (b) 49 (c) 25 (d) 73
Answer: (c)
Solution: α² + β² = (α + β)² − 2αβ = 49 − 24 = 25. Forgetting to subtract 2αβ gives 49, the trap, so the answer is 25.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

14. The quadratic equation whose roots are 3 and −4 is:
(a) x² − 7x − 12 = 0 (b) x² − x − 12 = 0 (c) x² + x + 12 = 0 (d) x² + x − 12 = 0
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sum = −1, product = −12, so the equation is x² − (sum)x + product = x² + x − 12 = 0. Taking the sum as +1 gives x² − x − 12, the sign trap, so the answer is x² + x − 12 = 0.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

15. How many terms of the AP 5, 9, 13, … must be taken to give a sum of 230?
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 12 (d) 10
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sₙ = n/2 [10 + (n−1)4] = 2n² + 3n = 230 ⇒ 2n² + 3n − 230 = 0 ⇒ (n − 10)(2n + 23) = 0, so n = 10. Guessing 8 or 9 without solving the quadratic is the trap, so the answer is 10.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

16. The values of x satisfying x² = 81 are:
(a) −9 only (b) 9 and −9 (c) 9 only (d) 81
Answer: (b)
Solution: A quadratic has two roots; x = ±9. Choosing only 9 forgets the negative root, so the answer is 9 and −9.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

17. The common ratio of the GP 5, −10, 20, −40, … is:
(a) 2 (b) −5 (c) −2 (d) 1/2
Answer: (c)
Solution: r = −10/5 = −2. Ignoring the alternating signs gives 2, the trap, so the answer is −2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

18. If the sum of two numbers is 15 and their product is 56, the numbers are:
(a) 7 and 8 (b) 6 and 9 (c) 5 and 10 (d) 4 and 11
Answer: (a)
Solution: They are roots of x² − 15x + 56 = 0 ⇒ (x − 7)(x − 8) = 0. The pair 6 and 9 sums to 15 but multiplies to 54, the near-miss trap, so the answer is 7 and 8.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

19. The 15th term of the AP whose first term is 2 and common difference is 3 is:
(a) 45 (b) 44 (c) 47 (d) 42
Answer: (b)
Solution: T₁₅ = 2 + 14×3 = 44. Using 15×3 instead of 14×3 gives 47, the off-by-one trap, so the answer is 44.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

20. For what value of p does x² + px + 9 = 0 have equal roots?
(a) 3 (b) 6 (c) 9 (d) ±6
Answer: (d)
Solution: D = p² − 36 = 0 ⇒ p = ±6. Taking only the positive value misses half the answer, so the answer is ±6.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

21. If one root of x² − kx + 18 = 0 is twice the other, then k equals:
(a) ±9 (b) 6 (c) 9 (d) 12
Answer: (a)
Solution: Let the roots be r and 2r. Then 2r² = 18 ⇒ r = ±3, and k = 3r = ±9. Taking only positive roots gives 9, the trap, so the answer is ±9.
Source: PYQ-style

22. The sum of the series 1 + 3 + 5 + … up to 25 terms is:
(a) 600 (b) 625 (c) 650 (d) 576
Answer: (b)
Solution: The sum of the first n odd numbers is n² = 25² = 625. Using n(n+1) = 650 applies the wrong formula, so the answer is 625.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

23. If α and β are roots of 2x² − 5x + 3 = 0, the value of 1/α + 1/β is:
(a) 3/5 (b) 5/2 (c) −5/3 (d) 5/3
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1/α + 1/β = (α + β)/αβ = (5/2)/(3/2) = 5/3. Inverting the ratio gives 3/5, the trap, so the answer is 5/3.
Source: PYQ-style

24. The third term of a GP is 12 and the sixth term is 96. The first term is:
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: ar² = 12 and ar⁵ = 96 ⇒ r³ = 8 ⇒ r = 2, so a = 12/4 = 3. Dividing 96 by 12 and calling the quotient r (getting 8) is the trap, so the answer is 3.
Source: PYQ-style

25. The number of real roots of (x − 2)² + 4 = 0 is:
(a) Infinite (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 0
Answer: (d)
Solution: (x − 2)² = −4 has no real solution since a square cannot be negative. Expanding and miscomputing the discriminant as positive tempts the answer 2, so the answer is 0.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics
