Card 1
Front: What is the formula for average of a set of numbers?
Back: Average = Sum of values / Number of values

Card 2
Front: The average of 5 numbers is 20. What is the sum of the numbers?
Back: Sum = Average × Number of values = 20 × 5 = 100

Card 3
Front: What is the average of the first n natural numbers?
Back: (n + 1) / 2

Card 4
Front: What is the average of the first n even numbers?
Back: n + 1

Card 5
Front: What is the average of the first n odd numbers?
Back: n

Card 6
Front: What is the average of consecutive numbers from 1 to 10?
Back: (1 + 10) / 2 = 5.5 — for an arithmetic series, average = (first term + last term) / 2

Card 7
Front: If each value in a set is increased by 5, what happens to the average?
Back: The average also increases by 5

Card 8
Front: If each value in a set is multiplied by 3, what happens to the average?
Back: The average is also multiplied by 3

Card 9
Front: The average of 8 numbers is 15. One number, 30, is removed. What is the new average?
Back: New sum = (8 × 15) − 30 = 90; new average = 90 / 7 ≈ 12.86

Card 10
Front: The average age of 10 students is 14 years. When the teacher is included, the average becomes 15 years. What is the teacher's age?
Back: Teacher's age = (11 × 15) − (10 × 14) = 165 − 140 = 25 years

Card 11
Front: What is the formula for weighted average?
Back: Weighted average = (w₁x₁ + w₂x₂ + … + wₙxₙ) / (w₁ + w₂ + … + wₙ)

Card 12
Front: The average of 4 numbers is 12 and the average of 6 other numbers is 18. What is the combined average?
Back: Combined average = (4 × 12 + 6 × 18) / 10 = (48 + 108) / 10 = 15.6

Card 13
Front: A batsman scores an average of 40 runs in 9 innings. How many runs must he score in the 10th innings to raise his average to 42?
Back: Required runs = (10 × 42) − (9 × 40) = 420 − 360 = 60 runs

Card 14
Front: What is the average of the squares of the first n natural numbers?
Back: (n + 1)(2n + 1) / 6

Card 15
Front: If the average of a, b and c is M, what is the value of a + b + c?
Back: a + b + c = 3M

Card 16
Front: The average of two numbers is 25 and one number is 30. What is the other number?
Back: Other number = (2 × 25) − 30 = 20

Card 17
Front: In an exam, the average marks of passed students is 60 and of failed students is 20. If the overall average is 50, what fraction of students passed?
Back: Using alligation: (50 − 20) : (60 − 50) = 30 : 10 = 3 : 1, so 3/4 of the students passed

Card 18
Front: What is the quickest way to find the average of numbers equally spaced (arithmetic progression)?
Back: Average = (first term + last term) / 2 — no need to add all terms

Card 19
Front: The average of 11 results is 50. The average of the first 6 is 49 and of the last 6 is 52. What is the 6th result?
Back: 6th result = (6 × 49 + 6 × 52) − (11 × 50) = (294 + 312) − 550 = 56

Card 20
Front: A number is replaced by a larger number in a set. What happens to the average?
Back: The average increases by (new number − old number) / total count
