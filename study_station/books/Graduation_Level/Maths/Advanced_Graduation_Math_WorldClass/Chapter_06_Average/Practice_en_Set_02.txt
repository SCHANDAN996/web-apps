26. The average of 11 numbers is 45. If each number is increased by 7, the new average is:
(a) 52 (b) 56 (c) 45 (d) 38
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is 45, which ignores that adding a constant to every term shifts the average by the same constant. New average = 45 + 7 = 52, so the answer is 52.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Statistics

27. The average of 5 consecutive even numbers is 24. The largest of these numbers is:
(a) 26 (b) 24 (c) 28 (d) 30
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students pick 26 by adding only 2 to the average, forgetting the largest term is two steps above the middle term. For 5 consecutive even numbers, the average equals the middle (3rd) number = 24, so the largest = 24 + 4 = 28, so the answer is 28.
Source: PYQ-style

28. The average age of 30 students is 14 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average becomes 15 years. The teacher's age is:
(a) 40 years (b) 50 years (c) 45 years (d) 35 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to simply add 1 year to 14, giving 15, which ignores the 30 students each gaining 1 year. Teacher's age = 15 + 30 × (15 − 14) = 15 + 30 = 45 years, so the answer is 45 years.
Source: PYQ-style

29. The average of 20 numbers is 30. Later it was found that one number, 56, was wrongly read as 36. The correct average is:
(a) 30 (b) 31 (c) 29 (d) 32
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting error is to add the full difference 20 to the average, giving 50, instead of spreading it over all 20 numbers. Correction = (56 − 36)/20 = 1, so correct average = 30 + 1 = 31, so the answer is 31.
Source: PYQ-style

30. The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one of them weighing 65 kg. The weight of the new person is:
(a) 85 kg (b) 80 kg (c) 75 kg (d) 90 kg
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often compute 65 + 2.5 = 67.5 kg, forgetting the increase applies to all 8 persons. New person's weight = 65 + 8 × 2.5 = 65 + 20 = 85 kg, so the answer is 85 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

31. The average of the first 50 natural numbers is:
(a) 25 (b) 24.5 (c) 26 (d) 25.5
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to take n/2 = 25, which gives only the middle position, not the mean. Average of first n natural numbers = (n + 1)/2 = 51/2 = 25.5, so the answer is 25.5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Statistics

32. The average of the first 10 multiples of 7 is:
(a) 35 (b) 38.5 (c) 42 (d) 35.5
Answer: (b)
Solution: A common slip is taking 7 × 5 = 35, the 5th multiple, instead of the mean of the 5th and 6th multiples. Average = 7 × (10 + 1)/2 = 7 × 5.5 = 38.5, so the answer is 38.5.
Source: PYQ-style

33. The average of 6 numbers is 20. If one number is removed, the average of the remaining numbers is 15. The removed number is:
(a) 40 (b) 35 (c) 45 (d) 50
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong step is 20 − 15 = 5, which ignores the change in count. Removed number = 6 × 20 − 5 × 15 = 120 − 75 = 45, so the answer is 45.
Source: PYQ-style

34. The average score of a class of 40 students is 60. The average score of the boys is 55 and that of the girls is 65. The number of boys in the class is:
(a) 28 (b) 24 (c) 16 (d) 20
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to guess 24 or 16 without checking that the overall average 60 lies exactly midway between 55 and 65, which forces equal groups. By allegation, boys : girls = (65 − 60) : (60 − 55) = 5 : 5 = 1 : 1, so boys = 40/2 = 20, so the answer is 20.
Source: PYQ-style

35. The average of 8 readings is 24. The average of the first three is 20 and that of the next three is 26. The seventh reading is 2 more than the eighth. The eighth reading is:
(a) 24 (b) 28 (c) 26 (d) 30
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to average 20 and 26 to guess the last two readings, ignoring the grand total. Sum of all 8 = 192; sum of first six = 60 + 78 = 138; so the last two sum to 54. With seventh = eighth + 2: 2x + 2 = 54, giving x = 26, so the answer is 26.
Source: PYQ-style

36. A batsman makes a score of 87 runs in the 17th innings and thus increases his average by 3. His average after 17 innings is:
(a) 36 (b) 42 (c) 39 (d) 33
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting error is 87 − 3 × 17 = 36, which gives the old average, not the new one. Let the new average be x: 17x = 16(x − 3) + 87, so x = 87 − 48 = 39, so the answer is 39.
Source: PYQ-style

37. The average of three numbers is 77. The first number is twice the second and the second is twice the third. The first number is:
(a) 132 (b) 121 (c) 110 (d) 99
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often set the first number as x instead of the third, tangling the ratios. Let third = x; then second = 2x, first = 4x. Sum = 7x = 231, x = 33, first = 4 × 33 = 132, so the answer is 132.
Source: PYQ-style

38. The average temperature of Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday was 40°C, and that of Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday was 41°C. If the temperature on Thursday was 42°C, the temperature on Monday was:
(a) 40°C (b) 39°C (c) 38°C (d) 41°C
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to assume Monday equals Thursday at 42°C, ignoring the 1°C rise across three days. Mon + Tue + Wed = 120; Tue + Wed + Thu = 123; subtracting, Thu − Mon = 3, so Mon = 42 − 3 = 39°C, so the answer is 39°C.
Source: PYQ-style

39. The average of 5 numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 25. The excluded number is:
(a) 35 (b) 30 (c) 25 (d) 27
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 27 − 25 = 2, which ignores the totals. Excluded number = 5 × 27 − 4 × 25 = 135 − 100 = 35, so the answer is 35.
Source: PYQ-style

40. The average salary of all workers in a factory is ₹8,000. The average salary of 7 technicians is ₹12,000 and that of the rest is ₹6,000. The total number of workers is:
(a) 20 (b) 21 (c) 22 (d) 23
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many compute 7 plus some guessed number instead of using the total-sum equation. Let total = n: 8000n = 7 × 12000 + (n − 7) × 6000, so 2000n = 42000 and n = 21, so the answer is 21.
Source: PYQ-style

41. The average of 11 results is 50. The average of the first six results is 49 and that of the last six is 52. The sixth result is:
(a) 50 (b) 60 (c) 46 (d) 56
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to average 49 and 52 to get 50.5, forgetting the sixth result is counted twice. Sixth result = 6 × 49 + 6 × 52 − 11 × 50 = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56, so the answer is 56.
Source: PYQ-style

42. The average age of a husband and wife was 23 years at their marriage. After 5 years they have a one-year-old child. The average age of the family now is:
(a) 23 years (b) 20 years (c) 21 years (d) 19 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting error is to keep 23 or add 5 to it, ignoring the child as a third member. Sum at marriage = 46; after 5 years the couple's sum = 56; plus child (1 year) = 57; average = 57/3 = 19 years, so the answer is 19 years.
Source: PYQ-style

43. The average of the squares of the first 10 natural numbers is:
(a) 38.5 (b) 35.5 (c) 30.25 (d) 42.5
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is squaring the average 5.5 to get 30.25, which is not the average of squares. Average = (n + 1)(2n + 1)/6 = (11 × 21)/6 = 231/6 = 38.5, so the answer is 38.5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Statistics

44. A cricketer's average for 10 innings was 32 runs. How many runs must he score in the next innings to raise his average to 36?
(a) 70 (b) 72 (c) 76 (d) 80
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 36 + 4 = 40, ignoring that the 4-run rise applies to all 11 innings. Required runs = 11 × 36 − 10 × 32 = 396 − 320 = 76, so the answer is 76.
Source: PYQ-style

45. The average of 7 consecutive numbers is 20. The smallest of these numbers is:
(a) 16 (b) 17 (c) 18 (d) 15
Answer: (b)
Solution: Students often subtract 4 instead of 3, forgetting the middle term of 7 numbers is the 4th. Middle (4th) number = 20, so smallest = 20 − 3 = 17, so the answer is 17.
Source: PYQ-style

46. The average marks of 120 candidates were 35. If the average of passed candidates was 39 and that of failed candidates was 15, the number of candidates who passed is:
(a) 80 (b) 90 (c) 110 (d) 100
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is splitting 120 equally at 60, ignoring the weighted average. By allegation, passed : failed = (35 − 15) : (39 − 35) = 20 : 4 = 5 : 1, so passed = (5/6) × 120 = 100, so the answer is 100.
Source: PYQ-style

47. The average of two numbers is 62. If 2 is added to the smaller number, the ratio of the numbers becomes 1 : 2. The smaller number is:
(a) 42 (b) 44 (c) 38 (d) 40
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting slip is applying the ratio to the original numbers directly. Sum = 124. Let smaller = x, larger = 124 − x; then 2(x + 2) = 124 − x, so 3x = 120 and x = 40, so the answer is 40.
Source: PYQ-style

48. The average weight of A, B and C is 45 kg. If the average weight of A and B is 40 kg and that of B and C is 43 kg, the weight of B is:
(a) 31 kg (b) 35 kg (c) 29 kg (d) 33 kg
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is averaging 40 and 43 to guess B's weight. B = (A + B) + (B + C) − (A + B + C) = 80 + 86 − 135 = 31 kg, so the answer is 31 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

49. The average of 50 numbers is 38. If two numbers, 45 and 55, are discarded, the average of the remaining numbers is:
(a) 37.5 (b) 36.5 (c) 37 (d) 38
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 38, assuming discarding two numbers changes nothing — but their mean is 50, above 38. Remaining sum = 1900 − 100 = 1800 over 48 numbers, average = 1800/48 = 37.5, so the answer is 37.5.
Source: PYQ-style

50. The average age of 24 students and their principal is 15 years. When the principal's age is excluded, the average drops by 1 year. The age of the principal is:
(a) 38 years (b) 39 years (c) 40 years (d) 37 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 15 + 1 = 16, ignoring that the drop of 1 year applies to all 24 students. Principal's age = 15 + 24 × 1 = 39 years, so the answer is 39 years.
Source: PYQ-style
