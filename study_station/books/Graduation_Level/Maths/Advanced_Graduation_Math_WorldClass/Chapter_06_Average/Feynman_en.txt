# The Feynman Technique: Average as Balancing Weights on a See-Saw

Imagine a seesaw with a fulcrum in the exact center. If you place unequal weights on different positions along the board, the seesaw tips. The **Average** is the single balance point where all positive deviations exactly cancel out all negative deviations.

### The Fulcrum Principle (Zero Deviation)
If five students score 82, 85, 90, 93, and 100 on a test:
Instead of adding 82 + 85 + 90 + 93 + 100 = 450 and dividing by 5:
Guess a central point, say 90.
- 82 is -8
- 85 is -5
- 90 is 0
- 93 is +3
- 100 is +10

Sum the deviations: (-8 - 5 + 0 + 3 + 10) = 0!
Because the net deviation is 0, the balance point is exactly 90.

### The Heavy Passenger Rule (Inclusion)
Imagine a boat with 10 people whose average weight is 60 kg.
A new passenger boards, and the average weight increases by 2 kg (to 62 kg).
How heavy is the new passenger?
He must bring his own new share (62 kg), PLUS he must donate 2 kg to each of the 10 existing passengers to raise them to 62 kg!
Passenger's weight = 62 + (10 * 2) = 82 kg.
No long multiplication needed.
