76. The average of 11 numbers is 50. If the average of the first six numbers is 49 and that of the last six is 52, find the sixth number.
(a) 60 (b) 58 (c) 56 (d) 62
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong approach is to take the difference of the two averages (52 − 49 = 3) and stop there. Sum of first six = 6 × 49 = 294; sum of last six = 6 × 52 = 312; total of these = 606, which counts the sixth number twice. Sum of all 11 = 11 × 50 = 550. Sixth number = 606 − 550 = 56, so the answer is 56.
Source: PYQ-style

77. The average weight of 8 men increases by 1.5 kg when one of them weighing 65 kg is replaced by a new man. What is the weight of the new man?
(a) 70 kg (b) 77 kg (c) 74 kg (d) 80 kg
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many candidates add only 1.5 kg to 65 kg and pick 66.5 kg, forgetting the increase applies to all 8 men. Total increase = 8 × 1.5 = 12 kg. New man's weight = 65 + 12 = 77 kg, so the answer is 77 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

78. The average of five consecutive even numbers is 24. What is the largest of these numbers?
(a) 26 (b) 28 (c) 30 (d) 32
Answer: (b)
Solution: A common slip is to take 24 as the largest number instead of the middle one. For consecutive even numbers, the average equals the middle (third) number, so the numbers are 20, 22, 24, 26, 28. The largest is 28, so the answer is 28.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Statistics (concept)

79. The average age of a class of 40 students is 16 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average rises by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?
(a) 61 years (b) 51 years (c) 45 years (d) 57 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to compute 16 + 1 = 17 and stop. New average of 41 persons = 17 years, total = 41 × 17 = 697. Students' total = 40 × 16 = 640. Teacher's age = 697 − 640 = 57 years, so the answer is 57 years.
Source: PYQ-style

80. The average of 20 numbers is zero. Of them, at the most, how many may be greater than zero?
(a) 1 (b) 19 (c) 10 (d) 0
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting answer is 10, assuming half must be positive and half negative. But if 19 numbers are positive, the 20th can be a single negative number equal in magnitude to their sum, keeping the total zero. Hence at most 19 numbers can be positive, so the answer is 19.
Source: PYQ-style

81. A batsman makes a score of 87 runs in the 17th innings and thus increases his average by 3. Find his average after the 17th innings.
(a) 39 (b) 36 (c) 42 (d) 45
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many solve 87 − 3 = 84 and divide wrongly. Let the new average be x. Then 16(x − 3) + 87 = 17x, giving 16x − 48 + 87 = 17x, so x = 39. The average after the 17th innings is 39, so the answer is 39.
Source: PYQ-style

82. The average of 7 consecutive numbers is 20. What is the smallest of these numbers?
(a) 16 (b) 17 (c) 15 (d) 18
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to subtract 7 from 20 and get 13. The average of consecutive numbers is the middle (4th) term, so the numbers run from 20 − 3 = 17 to 20 + 3 = 23. The smallest is 17, so the answer is 17.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Statistics (concept)

83. The average salary of all workers in a workshop is Rs 8,000. The average salary of 7 technicians is Rs 12,000 and that of the rest is Rs 6,000. How many workers are there in the workshop?
(a) 21 (b) 20 (c) 22 (d) 23
Answer: (a)
Solution: A common error is to average 12,000 and 6,000 to get 9,000 and compare with 8,000 directly. Let total workers be n: 8000n = 7 × 12000 + (n − 7) × 6000, so 8000n = 84000 + 6000n − 42000, giving 2000n = 42000 and n = 21, so the answer is 21.
Source: PYQ-style

84. The average of three numbers is 60. The first number is one-fourth of the sum of the other two. What is the first number?
(a) 30 (b) 42 (c) 45 (d) 36
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting mistake is to take one-fourth of the total (180/4 = 45). Total = 3 × 60 = 180. If the first number is x, the other two sum to 4x, so x + 4x = 180, giving x = 36, so the answer is 36.
Source: PYQ-style

85. The average marks of 100 students were found to be 40. Later it was discovered that a score of 53 was misread as 83. Find the correct average.
(a) 39.3 (b) 40.7 (c) 39.7 (d) 40.3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many subtract 30 from the average directly, getting 10, which is wrong. The total was overstated by 83 − 53 = 30, so the correct total = 4000 − 30 = 3970. Correct average = 3970/100 = 39.7, so the answer is 39.7.
Source: PYQ-style

86. The average age of 30 boys in a class is 14 years. When one boy aged 16 years leaves the class and a new boy joins in his place, the average becomes 13.8 years. What is the age of the new boy?
(a) 10 years (b) 12 years (c) 8 years (d) 14 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to assume the new boy's age equals the new average, 13.8 years. Original total = 30 × 14 = 420; new total = 30 × 13.8 = 414, a drop of 6 years. New boy's age = 16 − 6 = 10 years, so the answer is 10 years.
Source: PYQ-style

87. The average of 50 numbers is 38. If two numbers, 45 and 55, are discarded, find the average of the remaining numbers.
(a) 36.5 (b) 39.5 (c) 37.5 (d) 38.5
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting error is to keep the average at 38, assuming discarding two numbers changes nothing. Total = 50 × 38 = 1900; after discarding, total = 1900 − 100 = 1800 over 48 numbers. New average = 1800/48 = 37.5, so the answer is 37.5.
Source: PYQ-style

88. The average of the first 100 positive integers is:
(a) 50.5 (b) 50 (c) 49.5 (d) 51
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many pick 50, taking half of 100 directly. Sum of first n natural numbers = n(n + 1)/2 = 5050; average = 5050/100 = 50.5, so the answer is 50.5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Arithmetic Progressions

89. A cricketer has a certain average for 10 innings. In the 11th innings he scores 108 runs, thereby increasing his average by 6 runs. What is his new average?
(a) 48 (b) 52 (c) 42 (d) 54
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to compute 108 − 6 = 102 and divide by 11. Let the new average be x: 10(x − 6) + 108 = 11x, so 10x + 48 = 11x, giving x = 48, so the answer is 48.
Source: PYQ-style

90. The average of 8 readings is 24.2. The average of the first three is 21.5 and that of the next two is 27.8. If the sixth reading is 5 less than the seventh and 7 less than the eighth, find the sixth reading.
(a) 24.5 (b) 22.5 (c) 18.5 (d) 20.5
Answer: (d)
Solution: A common slip is to divide the leftover sum by 3 directly, ignoring the differences. Total = 8 × 24.2 = 193.6; first five sum to 3 × 21.5 + 2 × 27.8 = 64.5 + 55.6 = 120.1; last three sum to 193.6 − 120.1 = 73.5. Let the sixth be x: x + (x + 5) + (x + 7) = 73.5, so 3x = 61.5 and x = 20.5, so the answer is 20.5.
Source: PYQ-style

91. The average weight of A, B and C is 45 kg. If the average weight of A and B is 40 kg and that of B and C is 43 kg, find the weight of B.
(a) 36 kg (b) 21 kg (c) 26 kg (d) 31 kg
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to average 40 and 43 to estimate B. A + B + C = 3 × 45 = 135; A + B = 80; B + C = 86. Then B = (A + B) + (B + C) − (A + B + C) = 80 + 86 − 135 = 31, so the answer is 31 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

92. The average of five numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 25. What is the excluded number?
(a) 35 (b) 27 (c) 30 (d) 25
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many take the difference 27 − 25 = 2 and stop. Sum of five = 135; sum of remaining four = 100. Excluded number = 135 − 100 = 35, so the answer is 35.
Source: PYQ-style

93. The average age of a husband and wife was 23 years at their marriage. After 5 years they have a 1-year-old child. What is the average age of the family now?
(a) 23 years (b) 20 years (c) 19 years (d) 21 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to add 5 years to 23 and get 28, ignoring the child. At marriage, combined age = 46. Five years later, parents' combined age = 46 + 10 = 56; plus the 1-year-old child = 57 over 3 members. Average = 57/3 = 19 years, so the answer is 19 years.
Source: PYQ-style

94. The average of 11 results is 60. If the average of the first six results is 58 and that of the last six is 63, find the sixth result.
(a) 68 (b) 66 (c) 70 (d) 72
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting shortcut is 63 − 58 = 5 added to 60, giving 65. Sum of first six = 348; sum of last six = 378; combined = 726 (sixth counted twice). Total of 11 = 660. Sixth result = 726 − 660 = 66, so the answer is 66.
Source: PYQ-style

95. A library has an average of 510 visitors on Sundays and 240 on other days. What is the average number of visitors per day in a month of 30 days beginning with a Sunday?
(a) 300 (b) 290 (c) 295 (d) 285
Answer: (d)
Solution: The common error is to take 4 Sundays; a 30-day month starting on Sunday has 5 Sundays. Total visitors = 5 × 510 + 25 × 240 = 2550 + 6000 = 8550. Average = 8550/30 = 285, so the answer is 285.
Source: PYQ-style

96. The average of 9 observations was found to be 35. Later it was found that an observation of 81 was misread as 18. Find the correct average.
(a) 40 (b) 41 (c) 42 (d) 43
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many add 63 to 35, getting 98, forgetting the correction spreads over 9 observations. The total was understated by 81 − 18 = 63, so the average rises by 63/9 = 7. Correct average = 35 + 7 = 42, so the answer is 42.
Source: PYQ-style

97. The average temperature for Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday was 40°C. The average for Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday was 41°C. If the temperature on Thursday was 42°C, find the temperature on Monday.
(a) 38°C (b) 40°C (c) 41°C (d) 39°C
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to assume Monday equals Thursday minus 1, giving 41. Mon + Tue + Wed = 120; Tue + Wed + Thu = 123. Subtracting: Thu − Mon = 3, so Mon = 42 − 3 = 39°C, so the answer is 39°C.
Source: PYQ-style

98. The average marks obtained by 120 candidates in an examination were 35. If the average marks of the passed candidates was 39 and that of the failed candidates was 15, how many candidates passed?
(a) 100 (b) 90 (c) 110 (d) 115
Answer: (a)
Solution: A common slip is to split 120 in the ratio of the averages directly. Let passed = p: 39p + 15(120 − p) = 120 × 35, so 24p + 1800 = 4200, giving 24p = 2400 and p = 100, so the answer is 100.
Source: PYQ-style

99. The average of 6 numbers is 3.95. The average of two of them is 3.4, while the average of the other two is 3.85. What is the average of the remaining two numbers?
(a) 4.5 (b) 4.6 (c) 4.7 (d) 4.8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting error is to average 3.4 and 3.85 and compare with 3.95. Total = 6 × 3.95 = 23.7; sum of the four known = 2 × 3.4 + 2 × 3.85 = 6.8 + 7.7 = 14.5. Remaining two sum to 9.2, average = 4.6, so the answer is 4.6.
Source: PYQ-style

100. The average age of 24 students and their principal is 15 years. When the principal's age is excluded, the average falls by 1 year. What is the principal's age?
(a) 38 years (b) 40 years (c) 39 years (d) 41 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many compute 15 + 1 = 16 and stop, forgetting the drop of 1 year applies to all 24 students. Total of 25 persons = 25 × 15 = 375; students' total = 24 × 14 = 336. Principal's age = 375 − 336 = 39 years, so the answer is 39 years.
Source: PYQ-style
