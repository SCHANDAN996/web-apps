Q1. (Easy) Find the average of the first 50 natural numbers.
(A) 25.5  (B) 25  (C) 26  (D) 24.5

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Average of first n natural numbers is given by (n + 1) / 2. Here n = 50, so average = (50 + 1) / 2 = 51 / 2 = 25.5. Therefore, the correct value is 25.5, corresponding to option (A).

Q3. (Easy) Find the average of the first 35 odd natural numbers.
(A) 36  (B) 34  (C) 35  (D) 35.5

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Average of first n odd natural numbers is exactly n. Therefore, for n = 35, the average is 35. Psychological Trap: Students often confuse the odd numbers rule (n) with the even numbers rule (n + 1) and calculate 36. Therefore, the correct value is 35, corresponding to option (C).

Q4. (Easy) Find the average of all multiples of 7 between 1 and 50.
(A) 21  (B) 24.5  (C) 35  (D) 28

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Multiples of 7 between 1 and 50 are 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49. In an arithmetic progression, average = (first term + last term) / 2 = (7 + 49) / 2 = 56 / 2 = 28. Therefore, the correct value is 28, corresponding to option (D).

Q5. (Easy) The average of 5 numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 25. Find the excluded number.
(A) 35  (B) 32  (C) 30  (D) 28

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Sum of 5 numbers = 5 * 27 = 135. Sum of remaining 4 numbers = 4 * 25 = 100. Excluded number = 135 - 100 = 35. Therefore, the correct value is 35, corresponding to option (A).

Q7. (Easy) The average score of 8 students in a test is 72. If a ninth student scores 90 marks, what is the new average score?
(A) 73.5  (B) 75  (C) 74  (D) 72.5

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Net surplus brought by the new student = 90 - 72 = 18. This surplus is distributed across all 9 students: 18 / 9 = 2. New average = 72 + 2 = 74. Therefore, the correct value is 74, corresponding to option (C).

Q9. (Easy) The average of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 61. What is the difference between the highest and lowest numbers?
(A) 8  (B) 10  (C) 6  (D) 12

Answer: (A)

Solution:
For any n consecutive odd numbers, the difference between the largest and smallest is always 2 * (n - 1). Here 2 * (5 - 1) = 8. (The numbers are 57, 59, 61, 63, 65; 65 - 57 = 8). Therefore, the correct value is 8, corresponding to option (A).

Q10. (Easy) The average of 9 numbers is 50. If the average of the first 5 numbers is 54 and that of the last 5 numbers is 52, find the fifth number.
(A) 75  (B) 80  (C) 70  (D) 85

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Sum of all 9 numbers = 9 * 50 = 450. Sum of first 5 numbers = 5 * 54 = 270. Sum of last 5 numbers = 5 * 52 = 260. The fifth number is included in both sums: Fifth number = (270 + 260) - 450 = 530 - 450 = 80. Therefore, the correct value is 80, corresponding to option (B).

Q11. (Easy) The average age of 24 students and their teacher is 15 years. If the teacher's age is excluded, the average decreases by 1 year. Find the age of the teacher.
(A) 38 years  (B) 40 years  (C) 39 years  (D) 36 years

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Total persons initially = 25. Teacher's age = Old Average + (Total remaining) * (Decrease) = 15 + 24 * 1 = 39 years. Therefore, the correct value is 39 years, corresponding to option (C).

Q12. (Easy) The average weight of a group of 15 members is 40 kg. If a new member weighing 56 kg joins the group, what is the new average weight?
(A) 42 kg  (B) 40.5 kg  (C) 41.5 kg  (D) 41 kg

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Surplus weight brought by the new member = 56 - 40 = 16 kg. New total members = 16. Increase per member = 16 / 16 = 1 kg. New average = 40 + 1 = 41 kg. Therefore, the correct value is 41 kg, corresponding to option (D).

Q15. (Easy) A car covers the first 60 km at a speed of 30 km/h and the next 60 km at a speed of 60 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey?
(A) 45 km/h  (B) 42 km/h  (C) 40 km/h  (D) 38 km/h

Answer: (C)

Solution:
For equal distances, average speed is the harmonic mean: (2 * 30 * 60) / (30 + 60) = 3600 / 90 = 40 km/h. Psychological Trap: A classic trap is calculating the arithmetic mean (30 + 60) / 2 = 45 km/h, which is wrong because more time is spent at the slower speed. Therefore, the correct value is 40 km/h, corresponding to option (C).

Q16. (Easy) If the average of x, y, and z is 14, and the average of y and z is 10, find the value of x.

Solution:
Sum of x, y, and z = 3 * 14 = 42. Sum of y and z = 2 * 10 = 20. Therefore, x = 42 - 20 = 22. Therefore, the correct value is 22, corresponding to option (D).

Solution:

Q23. (Easy) Find the average of squares of the first 5 natural numbers.
(A) 10  (B) 12  (C) 11  (D) 11.5

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Average of squares of first n natural numbers = (n + 1)(2n + 1) / 6. For n = 5: (5 + 1)(10 + 1) / 6 = (6 * 11) / 6 = 11. Therefore, the correct value is 11, corresponding to option (C).

Q25. (Easy) A batsman has a certain average for 11 innings. In the 12th inning, he scores 90 runs and his average increases by 5 runs. What is his new average?
(A) 30  (B) 35  (C) 40  (D) 45

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Let old average be A. Then 12 * (A + 5) = 11 * A + 90 => 12A + 60 = 11A + 90 => A = 30. New average = A + 5 = 35. Therefore, the correct value is 35, corresponding to option (B).
