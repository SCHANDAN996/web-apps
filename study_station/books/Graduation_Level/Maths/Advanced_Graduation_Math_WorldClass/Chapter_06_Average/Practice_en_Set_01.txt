1. Find the average of the first 10 natural numbers.
(a) 5 (b) 4.5 (c) 6 (d) 5.5
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 5 comes from taking the middle of 1 and 10 incorrectly as 5. Average of first n natural numbers is (n+1)/2 = 11/2 = 5.5, so the answer is 5.5.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

2. The average of 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 is:
(a) 10 (b) 11 (c) 12 (d) 14
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 10 comes from picking the second term instead of the middle term. Sum = 60, and 60/5 = 12; also for an arithmetic progression the average equals the middle term, so the answer is 12.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Mathematics

3. The average of 5 numbers is 20. What is their sum?
(a) 4 (b) 100 (c) 25 (d) 80
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 4 comes from dividing 20 by 5 instead of multiplying. Sum = average × number of terms = 20 × 5 = 100, so the answer is 100.

21. The average of 11 numbers is 50. The average of the first 6 numbers is 49 and that of the last 6 numbers is 52. The 6th number is:
(a) 56 (b) 54 (c) 58 (d) 60
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 54 comes from averaging 49 and 52 and adding nothing. The 6th number is counted twice: 6 × 49 + 6 × 52 − 11 × 50 = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56, so the answer is 56.

22. The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one weighing 65 kg. The weight of the new person is:

24. The average marks of 30 students is 60. If the marks of two students were wrongly taken as 35 and 45 instead of 53 and 54, the correct average is:
(a) 60.6 (b) 61.5 (c) 61.2 (d) 60.9
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 61.2 comes from adding the full difference 36 without adjusting the pair correctly. Correction = (53 + 54) − (35 + 45) = 107 − 80 = 27; new average = 60 + 27/30 = 60.9, so the answer is 60.9.
Source: PYQ-style

25. The average age of a class of 40 students is 15 years. When the teacher is included, the average becomes 16 years. The age of the teacher is:
(a) 50 years (b) 54 years (c) 60 years (d) 56 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 56's rival 50 comes from adding only 40 × 1 − some slip; compute exactly: teacher's age = 41 × 16 − 40 × 15 = 656 − 600 = 56 years, so the answer is 56 years.
Source: PYQ-style
