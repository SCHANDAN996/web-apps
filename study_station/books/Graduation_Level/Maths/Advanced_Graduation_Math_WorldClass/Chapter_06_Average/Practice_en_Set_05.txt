101. The average of 11 numbers is 50. If the average of the first six numbers is 49 and that of the last six numbers is 52, what is the sixth number?
(a) 46 (b) 50 (c) 56 (d) 60
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong approach is to assume the sixth number equals the overall average 50, but it is counted twice. Sum of all 11 numbers = 11 × 50 = 550; sum of first six = 294; sum of last six = 312. Sixth number = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56, so the answer is 56.
Source: PYQ-style

102. The average age of 30 students of a class is 14 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average increases by 1 year. What is the teacher's age?
(a) 44 years (b) 35 years (c) 45 years (d) 31 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students wrongly add just 1 year to 14 and get 15, forgetting the increase applies to all 31 people. Total age of students = 30 × 14 = 420; new total = 31 × 15 = 465. Teacher's age = 465 − 420 = 45, so the answer is 45 years.
Source: PYQ-style

103. The average of five consecutive even numbers is 24. What is the largest of these numbers?
(a) 28 (b) 30 (c) 24 (d) 26
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to take 24 as the largest number, but the average of consecutive numbers is the middle one. The numbers are 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, so the answer is 28.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

104. A batsman has a certain average after 16 innings. In the 17th innings he scores 85 runs, raising his average by 3 runs. What is his new average?
(a) 34 (b) 37 (c) 40 (d) 31
Answer: (b)
Solution: The common error is to divide 85 by 17 directly. Let the old average be x; then 16x + 85 = 17(x + 3), giving x = 34. New average = 34 + 3 = 37, so the answer is 37.
Source: PYQ-style

105. The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one of them weighing 65 kg. What is the weight of the new person?
(a) 80 kg (b) 75 kg (c) 85 kg (d) 90 kg
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to add only 2.5 kg to 65 kg, ignoring that all 8 persons' average rises. Total increase = 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg, so the new person weighs 65 + 20 = 85 kg, so the answer is 85 kg.
Source: PYQ-style

106. The average of 20 numbers is zero. At most, how many of them can be greater than zero?
(a) 1 (b) 19 (c) 10 (d) 0
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 10, assuming half must be positive and half negative. But 19 numbers can be positive while one sufficiently large negative number balances their total, so the answer is 19.
Source: PYQ-style

107. The average of three numbers is 77. The first number is twice the second, and the second is twice the third. What is the first number?
(a) 99 (b) 66 (c) 121 (d) 132
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many candidates wrongly stop at 77, which is only the average. Let the third number be x; then the numbers are 4x, 2x and x, so 7x = 231 and x = 33. First number = 4 × 33 = 132, so the answer is 132.
Source: PYQ-style

108. The average marks of 100 students were 40. Later it was found that a score of 53 was misread as 83. What is the correct average?
(a) 39.7 (b) 40.6 (c) 39.4 (d) 40.3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to subtract the full difference 30 from the average instead of distributing it over 100 students. Correction = (53 − 83)/100 = −0.3, so the correct average = 40 − 0.3 = 39.7, so the answer is 39.7.
Source: PYQ-style

109. The average age of a husband and wife was 23 years at their marriage. After 5 years they have a 1-year-old child. What is the average age of the family now?
(a) 19 years (b) 20 years (c) 21 years (d) 18 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: The common mistake is to add 5 years to 23 and divide by 3, forgetting the child is only 1 year old. Parents' total age now = 46 + 10 = 56; family total = 57. Average = 57/3 = 19, so the answer is 19 years.
Source: PYQ-style

110. The average of 7 consecutive numbers is 20. What is the average of the first and the last of these numbers?
(a) 19 (b) 20 (c) 21 (d) 23
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to compute the first and last numbers separately, which is unnecessary work. For any arithmetic progression, the average of the first and last terms equals the overall average, so the answer is 20.

111. A grocer has sales of ₹6435, ₹6927, ₹6855, ₹7230 and ₹6562 for 5 consecutive months. How much must he sell in the sixth month to get an average of ₹6500?
(a) ₹6001 (b) ₹5991 (c) ₹6991 (d) ₹4991
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to average the five given sales and compare with 6500 directly. Required total for 6 months = ₹39000; sum of 5 months = ₹34009. Sixth month sale = 39000 − 34009 = ₹4991, so the answer is ₹4991.
Source: PYQ-style

112. The average of 50 numbers is 38. If two numbers, 45 and 55, are discarded, what is the average of the remaining numbers?
(a) 38 (b) 37 (c) 36.5 (d) 37.5
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 38, but the discarded pair averages 50, which is above the group average. Remaining sum = 1900 − 100 = 1800 over 48 numbers, giving 1800/48 = 37.5, so the answer is 37.5.
Source: PYQ-style

113. The average of x and y is 30, the average of y and z is 40, and the average of x and z is 50. What is the value of y?
(a) 40 (b) 30 (c) 10 (d) 20
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to average 30, 40 and 50 to get 40 and call it y. From the data: x + y = 60, y + z = 80, x + z = 100; adding gives x + y + z = 120. Then y = 120 − 100 = 20, so the answer is 20.
Source: PYQ-style

114. In a class of 45 students, the average weight of boys is 64 kg and that of girls is 55 kg. If the average weight of the whole class is 60 kg, how many boys are there?
(a) 25 (b) 30 (c) 20 (d) 27
Answer: (a)
Solution: The common error is to split 45 into two equal halves, but the overall average is not midway between 64 and 55. By allegation, boys : girls = (60 − 55) : (64 − 60) = 5 : 4, so boys = 45 × 5/9 = 25, so the answer is 25.
Source: PYQ-style

115. The average of five numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, the average becomes 25. What is the excluded number?
(a) 27 (b) 35 (c) 30 (d) 25
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to take the difference 27 − 25 = 2 as the excluded number. Sum of five numbers = 135; sum of remaining four = 100. Excluded number = 135 − 100 = 35, so the answer is 35.
Source: PYQ-style

116. A cricketer's average for 11 matches is 23 runs. In the next 4 matches he scores 40, 52, 37 and 23 runs. What is his average for all 15 matches?
(a) 26 (b) 27 (c) 25 (d) 28
Answer: (b)
Solution: The common error is to average 23 with the average of the four new scores, giving unequal weights. Total runs = 11 × 23 + 152 = 253 + 152 = 405, and 405/15 = 27, so the answer is 27.
Source: PYQ-style

117. The average temperature for Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday was 40°C, and for Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday it was 41°C. If Thursday's temperature was 42°C, what was Monday's temperature?
(a) 39°C (b) 40°C (c) 38°C (d) 41°C
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to assume Monday equals Thursday at 42°C. Mon + Tue + Wed = 120 and Tue + Wed + Thu = 123; subtracting gives Thu − Mon = 3, so Mon = 42 − 3 = 39, so the answer is 39°C.
Source: PYQ-style

118. The average salary of all workers in a factory is ₹8000. The average salary of 7 technicians is ₹12000 and that of the rest is ₹6000. How many workers are there in the factory?
(a) 22 (b) 20 (c) 21 (d) 23
Answer: (c)
Solution: The common mistake is to set up the equation with the wrong group sizes. Let total workers be n; then 8000n = 84000 + 6000(n − 7), giving 2000n = 42000 and n = 21, so the answer is 21.
Source: PYQ-style

119. The average of 9 observations is 9, that of the first 5 observations is 10, and that of the last 5 is 8. What is the 5th observation?
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 9 (d) 7
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to overlook that the 5th observation is counted in both groups of five. Sum of first 5 = 50 and sum of last 5 = 40, while the total of all 9 is 81. Fifth observation = 50 + 40 − 81 = 9, so the answer is 9.
Source: PYQ-style

120. A man buys 3 shirts at ₹500 each, 4 shirts at ₹600 each and 5 shirts at ₹400 each. What is the average price per shirt?
(a) ₹491.67 (b) ₹500 (c) ₹490 (d) ₹475
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to average 500, 600 and 400 directly to get 500, ignoring the different quantities. Total cost = 1500 + 2400 + 2000 = ₹5900 for 12 shirts, and 5900/12 ≈ 491.67, so the answer is ₹491.67.
Source: PYQ-style

121. The average age of a family of 10 members is 20 years. If the age of the youngest member is 10 years, what was the average age of the family just before the birth of the youngest member?
(a) 10 years (b) 13.5 years (c) 12 years (d) 11.1 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 10, obtained by simply subtracting 10 from 20. Ten years ago the family had 9 members with total age 200 − 100 = 100 years, giving 100/9 ≈ 11.1, so the answer is 11.1 years.
Source: PYQ-style

122. The average of 11 results is 60. If the average of the first 6 results is 58 and that of the last 6 is 62, and the 6th result is excluded, what is the average of the remaining 10 results?
(a) 59.5 (b) 61 (c) 60.5 (d) 60
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to assume the average must change after exclusion. Sixth result = 6 × 58 + 6 × 62 − 11 × 60 = 348 + 372 − 660 = 60; remaining sum = 600 over 10 results, giving 60, so the answer is 60.
Source: PYQ-style

123. In an examination, the average marks of all students was 60. The average marks of students who passed was 68 and of those who failed was 25. If 175 students passed, how many students appeared in the examination?
(a) 258 (b) 200 (c) 215 (d) 180
Answer: (c)
Solution: The common error is to use simple averaging of 68 and 25 instead of weighted allegation. By allegation, passed : failed = (60 − 25) : (68 − 60) = 35 : 8, so failed = 175 × 8/35 = 40. Total = 175 + 40 = 215, so the answer is 215.
Source: PYQ-style

124. The average of the first 100 positive integers is 50.5. If each of the numbers 1 to 100 is multiplied by 2 and then 5 is added to each, what is the new average?
(a) 106 (b) 101 (c) 105.5 (d) 110
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to multiply the average by 2 and forget the added 5, getting 101. Doubling every term doubles the average to 101, and adding 5 to every term raises it by 5 more, giving 106, so the answer is 106.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

125. A team of 8 persons joins a shooting competition. The best marksman scored 85 points. Had he scored 92 points, the average score of the team would have been 84. What was the team's actual average score?
(a) 83 (b) 83.125 (c) 82.5 (d) 84
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer is 84, which is the hypothetical average, not the actual one. With 92 points the total would be 8 × 84 = 672; actual total = 672 − 7 = 665, and 665/8 = 83.125, so the answer is 83.125.
Source: PYQ-style
