Q101. (Hard) The average weight of 3 men A, B, and C is 84 kg. Another man D joins them and the average becomes 80 kg. If another man E, whose weight is 3 kg more than that of D, replaces A, then the average weight of B, C, D, and E becomes 78 kg. What is the weight of A?
(A) 79 kg  (B) 75 kg  (C) 80 kg  (D) 78 kg

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Weight of A + B + C = 3 * 84 = 252 kg. Weight of A + B + C + D = 4 * 80 = 320 kg => D = 320 - 252 = 68 kg. Then E = 68 + 3 = 71 kg. Weight of B + C + D + E = 4 * 78 = 312 kg. Subtracting (B + C + D + E) from (A + B + C + D): A - E = 320 - 312 = 8 kg => A = E + 8 = 71 + 8 = 79 kg. Therefore, the correct value is 79 kg, corresponding to option (A).

Q102. (Hard) A car travels from city X to city Y at a speed of 60 km/h and returns from Y to X at 40 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the round trip?
(A) 50 km/h  (B) 48 km/h  (C) 45 km/h  (D) 52 km/h

Answer: (B)

Solution:
For equal distances covered at speeds u and v, average speed = (2 * u * v) / (u + v) = (2 * 60 * 40) / (60 + 40) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h. Psychological Trap: A classic trap is calculating the arithmetic mean (60 + 40) / 2 = 50 km/h. Therefore, the correct value is 48 km/h, corresponding to option (B).

Q103. (Hard) In a class of 30 students, the average age of girls is 12 years and the average age of boys is 15 years. If the average age of the entire class is 13.8 years, find the number of boys in the class.
(A) 12  (B) 15  (C) 18  (D) 20

Answer: (C)

Solution:
By rule of alligation: Ratio of Girls to Boys = (15 - 13.8) : (13.8 - 12) = 1.2 : 1.8 = 2 : 3. Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5. Number of boys = 30 * (3 / 5) = 18. Therefore, the correct value is 18, corresponding to option (C).

Q104. (Hard) The average of five consecutive positive integers is n. If the next two integers are added, the new average becomes:
(A) n + 2  (B) n + 1.5  (C) n + 0.5  (D) n + 1

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Adding each subsequent consecutive integer increases the mean by 0.5. Adding 2 consecutive integers increases the average by 2 * 0.5 = 1. Thus the new average is n + 1. Therefore, the correct value is n + 1, corresponding to option (D).
