Q51. (Medium) A cricketer has an average of 32 runs in 10 innings. How many runs must he score in the next inning so as to increase his average by 4 runs?
(A) 76  (B) 72  (C) 70  (D) 80

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Target average for 11 innings = 32 + 4 = 36 runs. Total runs required = 11 * 36 = 396. Runs in first 10 innings = 10 * 32 = 320. Runs needed in 11th inning = 396 - 320 = 76. Therefore, the correct value is 76, corresponding to option (A).

Q52. (Medium) A batsman in his 17th inning makes a score of 85 runs, and thereby increases his average by 3 runs. What is his average after the 17th inning?
(A) 34  (B) 37  (C) 40  (D) 36

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Let new average after 17th inning be A. The score in 17th inning = A + (17 - 1) * 3 = A + 48 = 85. Therefore, A = 85 - 48 = 37. Psychological Trap: A frequent trap is providing the old average (34) instead of the new average after the 17th inning (37). Therefore, the correct value is 37, corresponding to option (B).

Q53. (Medium) A bowler has a bowling average of 12.4 runs per wicket. In his next match, he takes 5 wickets for 26 runs, thereby decreasing his average by 0.4 runs per wicket. How many wickets had he taken before this match?
(A) 80  (B) 90  (C) 85  (D) 75

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Performance in current match = 26 / 5 = 5.2 runs/wicket. New overall average = 12.4 - 0.4 = 12.0. By alligation: (12.4 - 12.0) : (12.0 - 5.2) = 0.4 : 6.8 = 1 : 17. The current match represents 1 part (5 wickets), so the previous matches represent 17 parts = 17 * 5 = 85 wickets. Psychological Trap: Students often misinterpret a decrease in bowling average as a negative outcome rather than an improvement. Therefore, the correct value is 85, corresponding to option (C).
