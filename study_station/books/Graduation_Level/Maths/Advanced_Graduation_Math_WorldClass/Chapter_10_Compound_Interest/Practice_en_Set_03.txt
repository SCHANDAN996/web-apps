Q51. (Medium) A sum of money invested at compound interest amounts to Rs 4,800 in 4 years and to Rs 6,000 in 8 years. What was the original sum invested?
(A) Rs 3,840  (B) Rs 3,600  (C) Rs 4,000  (D) Rs 3,750

Answer: (A)

Solution:
When the two time intervals are equal (4 years and 8 years = 2 * 4 years), the multiplier squared relation holds: P = (A1^2) / A2 = (4800 * 4800) / 6000 = 4800 * 0.8 = Rs 3,840. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 3,840, corresponding to option (A).
