76. A sum of ₹8,000 is invested at 10% per annum compound interest, compounded annually. What is the amount after 2 years?
(a) ₹9,600 (b) ₹9,620 (c) ₹9,680 (d) ₹9,700
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting option ₹9,600 comes from simple interest (8000 × 10% × 2 = 1600 added), which ignores interest on interest. Amount = 8000 × (1.1)² = 8000 × 1.21 = 9680, so the answer is ₹9,680.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

77. The compound interest on ₹10,000 for 2 years at 8% per annum is:
(a) ₹1,664 (b) ₹1,600 (c) ₹1,640 (d) ₹1,680
Answer: (a)
Solution: ₹1,600 is the simple interest trap. CI = 10000 × [(1.08)² − 1] = 10000 × (1.1664 − 1) = 10000 × 0.1664 = 1664, so the answer is ₹1,664.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Mathematics

78. The difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹5,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years is:
(a) ₹100 (b) ₹75 (c) ₹25 (d) ₹50
Answer: (d)
Solution: A common slip is computing CI − SI with the rate not squared, giving ₹500 or ₹100. Difference = P × (r/100)² = 5000 × (10/100)² = 5000 × 0.01 = 50, so the answer is ₹50.
Source: PYQ-style

79. At what rate per annum will ₹4,000 amount to ₹4,840 in 2 years, interest compounded annually?
(a) 10% (b) 12% (c) 8% (d) 15%
Answer: (a)
Solution: Guessing 12% fails because 4000 × (1.12)² = 5017.6, not 4840. We need (1 + r/100)² = 4840/4000 = 1.21, so 1 + r/100 = 1.1, giving r = 10, so the answer is 10%.
Source: PYQ-style

80. A sum becomes ₹6,050 in 2 years at 10% per annum compound interest. The sum is:
(a) ₹5,000 (b) ₹5,500 (c) ₹4,800 (d) ₹5,200
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dividing 6050 by 1.1 only once gives 5500, the trap for forgetting two years of compounding. Principal = 6050 / (1.1)² = 6050 / 1.21 = 5000, so the answer is ₹5,000.

83. A sum of money doubles itself in 4 years at compound interest. In how many years will it become 8 times itself?
(a) 8 years (b) 12 years (c) 16 years (d) 10 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is linear thinking: 8 times = 4 × 2 = 8 years, but growth is multiplicative. 8 = 2³, so it needs 3 doubling periods = 3 × 4 = 12 years, so the answer is 12 years.

86. The difference between CI and SI on ₹8,000 at 5% per annum for 3 years is:
(a) ₹64 (b) ₹60 (c) ₹62 (d) ₹61
Answer: (d)
Solution: Using the 2-year formula P(r/100)² = 20 is the trap; for 3 years use P(r/100)²(3 + r/100). Difference = 8000 × 0.0025 × 3.05 = 61, so the answer is ₹61.

Solution: Saying 10% ignores the extra compounding within the year. Effective rate = (1.05)² − 1 = 0.1025 = 10.25%, so the answer is 10.25%.

93. If the compound interest on a sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is ₹2,100, the simple interest on the same sum for the same period is:
(a) ₹2,000 (b) ₹1,900 (c) ₹2,050 (d) ₹1,950
Answer: (a)
Solution: Halving 2100 gives 1050, a meaningless trap. SI : CI for 2 years = 2r : (2r + r²/100) = 20 : 21, so SI = 2100 × 20/21 = 2000, so the answer is ₹2,000.

97. The simple interest on a sum for 2 years at 8% per annum is ₹1,600. The compound interest on the same sum for the same period is:

Answer: (b)
Solution: Assuming CI equals SI (₹1,600) is the trap; CI always exceeds SI for 2 or more years. Principal = 1600/0.16 = 10000; CI = 10000 × (1.1664 − 1) = 1664, so the answer is ₹1,664.

98. A sum of ₹4,200 is split into two parts, both invested at 10% per annum compound interest, one for 2 years and the other for 3 years. If both yield equal amounts, the part invested for 2 years is:
(a) ₹2,100 (b) ₹2,000 (c) ₹2,200 (d) ₹2,400
Answer: (c)
Solution: Splitting equally (₹2,100) is the trap; the 2-year part must be larger to catch up. Equal amounts need Principal(2 yr) × (1.1)² = Principal(3 yr) × (1.1)³, so the parts are in ratio 1.1 : 1 = 11 : 10, and the 2-year part = 4200 × 11/21 = 2200, so the answer is ₹2,200.
Source: PYQ-style

100. A sum invested at compound interest amounts to ₹4,800 in 2 years and ₹5,760 in 4 years. The sum is:
(a) ₹3,800 (b) ₹3,600 (c) ₹4,200 (d) ₹4,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: Averaging the two amounts is a trap; growth over the next 2 years is the same factor. Growth factor over 2 years = 5760/4800 = 1.2, so Principal = 4800/1.2 = 4000, so the answer is ₹4,000.
Source: PYQ-style
