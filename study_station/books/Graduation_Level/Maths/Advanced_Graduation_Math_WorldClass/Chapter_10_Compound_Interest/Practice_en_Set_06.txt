126. The difference between compound interest and simple interest on ₹8,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum is:
(a) ₹60 (b) ₹160 (c) ₹100 (d) ₹80
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is ₹160, which is the simple interest of one full year and ignores that the CI–SI gap for 2 years comes only from interest on the first year's interest. Difference = P × (r/100)² = 8000 × (10/100)² = 8000 × 0.01 = 80, so the answer is ₹80.

127. A sum of money doubles itself in 5 years at compound interest. In how many years will it become 8 times itself at the same rate?
(a) 15 years (b) 12 years (c) 10 years (d) 20 years
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to think 8 times means 4 times the doubling period, giving 20 years. But 8 = 2³, so the money must double three times: 3 × 5 = 15, so the answer is 15 years.
