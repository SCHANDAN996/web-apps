Q1. (Easy) Find the compound interest on Rs 10,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum, compounded annually.
(A) Rs 2,100  (B) Rs 2,000  (C) Rs 2,200  (D) Rs 2,050

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Using the effective rate formula for 2 years at 10%: Effective Rate = 10 + 10 + (10 * 10) / 100 = 21%. Compound Interest = 21% of 10000 = Rs 2,100. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 2,100, corresponding to option (A).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q2. (Easy) What will be the amount of Rs 8,000 in 2 years at 5% per annum compound interest, compounded annually?
(A) Rs 8,800  (B) Rs 8,820  (C) Rs 8,850  (D) Rs 8,750

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Amount = P * (1 + R/100)^2 = 8000 * (1 + 5/100)^2 = 8000 * (21/20)^2 = 8000 * (441 / 400) = 20 * 441 = Rs 8,820. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 8,820, corresponding to option (B).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q3. (Easy) Find the compound interest on Rs 12,000 for 3 years at 10% per annum, compounded annually.
(A) Rs 3,900  (B) Rs 4,000  (C) Rs 3,972  (D) Rs 3,960

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Using the 3 : 3 : 1 multiplier ratio: First tier = 10% of 12000 = Rs 1200. Second tier = 10% of 1200 = Rs 120. Third tier = 10% of 120 = Rs 12. Total CI = (3 * 1200) + (3 * 120) + (1 * 12) = 3600 + 360 + 12 = Rs 3,972. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 3,972, corresponding to option (C).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q5. (Easy) Find the compound interest on Rs 16,000 for 1 year at 10% per annum, compounded half-yearly.
(A) Rs 1,640  (B) Rs 1,600  (C) Rs 1,680  (D) Rs 1,620

Answer: (A)

Solution:
When interest is compounded half-yearly: Rate per cycle = 10 / 2 = 5%. Number of cycles in 1 year = 1 * 2 = 2 periods. Effective rate for 2 periods at 5% = 5 + 5 + (5 * 5)/100 = 10.25%. Compound Interest = 10.25% of 16000 = 160 * 10.25 = Rs 1,640. Psychological Trap: A frequent trap is using annual rate 10% directly for 2 periods or using 5% for only 1 period, leading to 1,600. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 1,640, corresponding to option (A).

Q6. (Easy) At what rate percent per annum will Rs 2,000 amount to Rs 2,420 in 2 years, compounded annually?
(A) 8%  (B) 10%  (C) 12%  (D) 9%

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Ratio of Amount to Principal = A / P = 2420 / 2000 = 121 / 100. Since time is 2 years: (1 + R/100)^2 = 121 / 100 = (11 / 10)^2. Taking square root: 1 + R/100 = 11/10 => R/100 = 1/10 => R = 10%. Therefore, the correct value is 10%, corresponding to option (B).

Q12. (Easy) A sum of money doubles itself in 4 years at compound interest. In how many years will it become 8 times of itself at the same rate?
(A) 16 years  (B) 8 years  (C) 10 years  (D) 12 years

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Under compound interest, growth follows an exponential power law: If sum becomes 2 times in 4 years, it becomes 8 = 2^3 times in 3 * 4 = 12 years. Psychological Trap: A classic trap is applying simple interest logic (8 - 1 = 7 units => 7 * 4 = 28 years) or multiplying 4 * 4 = 16 years. Therefore, the correct value is 12 years, corresponding to option (D).

Q13. (Easy) If the compound interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 5% per annum is Rs 328, what is the simple interest on the same sum at the same rate and for the same period?
(A) Rs 320  (B) Rs 310  (C) Rs 300  (D) Rs 325

Answer: (A)

Solution:
Effective CI rate for 2 years at 5% = 5 + 5 + 0.25 = 10.25%. Effective SI rate for 2 years at 5% = 2 * 5% = 10.00%. Ratio CI / SI = 10.25 / 10 = 41 / 40. Given CI = Rs 328 => 41 units = 328 => 1 unit = 8. SI = 40 * 8 = Rs 320. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 320, corresponding to option (A).

Q14. (Easy) Find the compound interest on Rs 50,000 for 1.5 years at 8% per annum, compounded half-yearly.
(A) Rs 6,000.00  (B) Rs 6,243.20  (C) Rs 6,200.00  (D) Rs 6,300.00

Solution:

Total CI = (3 * 2000) + (3 * 80) + (1 * 3.20) = 6000 + 240 + 3.20 = Rs 6,243.20. Therefore, the correct value is Rs 6,243.20, corresponding to option (B).

Q15. (Easy) A sum of money amounts to Rs 4,840 in 2 years and to Rs 5,324 in 3 years at compound interest, compounded annually. What is the rate of interest per annum?
(A) 8%  (B) 12%  (C) 10%  (D) 9%

Answer: (C)

Solution:
The increase from year 2 to year 3 is solely the interest on the amount at the end of year 2: Interest for the 3rd year = 5324 - 4840 = Rs 484. Rate of interest R = (Interest / Principal for that year) * 100 = (484 / 4840) * 100 = 10%. Therefore, the correct value is 10%, corresponding to option (C).
