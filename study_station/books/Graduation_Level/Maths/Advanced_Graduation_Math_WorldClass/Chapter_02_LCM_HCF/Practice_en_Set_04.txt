Q76. (Medium) Six bells commence tolling together and toll at intervals of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12 seconds respectively. In 30 minutes, how many times do they toll together?

Answer: (A)

Solution:
LCM(2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12) = 120 seconds = 2 minutes. In 30 minutes, they toll (30 / 2) + 1 = 15 + 1 = 16 times (including the initial toll at t = 0).
