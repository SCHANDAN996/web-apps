Q101. (Medium) Find the HCF of the fractions 9/10, 12/25, 18/35, and 21/40.
(A) 3/1400  (B) 9/1400  (C) 3/700  (D) 1/1400

Answer: (A)

Solution:
HCF of fractions = HCF(9, 12, 18, 21) / LCM(10, 25, 35, 40) = 3 / 1400. Therefore, the correct value is 3/1400, corresponding to option (A).

Answer: (C)

By identity gcd(a^m - 1, a^n - 1) = a^gcd(m, n) - 1: gcd(45, 35) = 5. So 3^5 - 1 = 243 - 1 = 242. Therefore, the correct value is 242, corresponding to option (C).

Solution:

Q124. (Hard) If p is a prime number greater than 3, what is the greatest integer that always divides (p^2 - 1)?
(A) 12  (B) 6  (C) 48  (D) 24

Answer: (D)

Solution:
Every prime p > 3 is of form 6k ± 1. (6k ± 1)^2 - 1 = 36k^2 ± 12k = 12k(3k ± 1). Since k or (3k ± 1) is even, this expression is always divisible by 24. Psychological Trap: Assuming 12 is the answer because 12 divides it, without realizing 24 is the maximum divisor. Therefore, the correct value is 24, corresponding to option (D).
