Q1. (Easy) Find the HCF of 2^3 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^4 and 2^2 * 3^5 * 5^2 * 7^3.
(A) 2^2 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3  (B) 2^3 * 3^5 * 5^2 * 7^4  (C) 2^2 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7^3  (D) 2^3 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3

Answer: (A)

Solution:
HCF takes the minimum power of each common prime factor: 2^min(3,2) * 3^min(2,5) * 5^min(1,2) * 7^min(4,3) = 2^2 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3. Therefore, the correct value is 2^2 * 3^2 * 5 * 7^3, corresponding to option (A).

Q2. (Easy) Find the LCM of 2^3 * 3^2 * 5 and 2^4 * 3 * 5^2 * 7.

Answer: (B)

Solution:
LCM takes the highest power of all prime factors appearing in either number: 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7. Therefore, the correct value is 2^4 * 3^2 * 5^2 * 7, corresponding to option (B).

Q3. (Easy) The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 12 and 336 respectively. If one number is 84, find the other number.
(A) 36  (B) 56  (C) 48  (D) 42

Answer: (C)

Solution:
Product of two numbers = LCM * HCF. Other number = (12 * 336) / 84 = 336 / 7 = 48. Therefore, the correct value is 48, corresponding to option (C).

Q6. (Easy) What is the HCF of any two consecutive natural numbers n and n + 1?
(A) 2  (B) 1  (C) n  (D) n + 1

Answer: (B)

Solution:
Consecutive natural numbers never share any common factor greater than 1 because (n + 1) - n = 1. Hence, gcd(n, n + 1) = 1. Therefore, the correct value is 1, corresponding to option (B).

Q18. (Easy) The LCM of two numbers is 495 and their HCF is 5. If the sum of the numbers is 100, find their difference.
(A) 15  (B) 10  (C) 20  (D) 25

Answer: (B)

Solution:
A + B = 100, A * B = 495 * 5 = 2475. (A - B)^2 = (A + B)^2 - 4AB = 10000 - 4(2475) = 10000 - 9900 = 100. Thus A - B = 10. Therefore, the correct value is 10, corresponding to option (B).

Q21. (Medium) Find the smallest four-digit number which is exactly divisible by 12, 18, 21, and 28.
(A) 1008  (B) 1024  (C) 1080  (D) 1050

Answer: (A)

Solution:
LCM(12, 18, 21, 28): 12 = 2^2*3, 18 = 2*3^2, 21 = 3*7, 28 = 2^2*7. LCM = 4 * 9 * 7 = 252. Smallest 4-digit number is 1000. 1000 / 252 = 3 rem 244. Required number = 1000 + (252 - 244) = 1008. Therefore, the correct value is 1008, corresponding to option (A).

Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Q22. (Medium) Find the greatest number of four digits which is divisible by 15, 25, 40, and 75.

Solution:
LCM(15, 25, 40, 75) = 600. Largest 4-digit number is 9999. 9999 / 600 = 16 rem 399. Required number = 9999 - 399 = 9600. Therefore, the correct value is 9600, corresponding to option (B).

Q24. (Medium) How many pairs of positive integers have an HCF of 15 and an LCM of 630?
(A) 2  (B) 3  (C) 1  (D) 4

Answer: (D)

Solution:
LCM = H * x * y => 630 = 15 * x * y => x * y = 42. Coprime pairs for 42 = 2 * 3 * 7: (1, 42), (2, 21), (3, 14), (6, 7). Exactly 4 pairs. Therefore, the correct value is 4, corresponding to option (D).

Q25. (Medium) The sum of two numbers is 84 and their HCF is 12. How many such pairs of numbers exist?
(A) 4  (B) 3  (C) 2  (D) 1

Answer: (B)

Solution:
12x + 12y = 84 => x + y = 7. Coprime pairs summing to 7: (1, 6), (2, 5), (3, 4). Exactly 3 pairs exist. Therefore, the correct value is 3, corresponding to option (B).
