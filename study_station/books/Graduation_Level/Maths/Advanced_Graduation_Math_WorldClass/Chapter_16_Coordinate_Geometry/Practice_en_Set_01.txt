1. The distance between the points (3, 4) and (0, 0) is
(a) 12 (b) 7 (c) 5 (d) 25
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students square the coordinates and forget the square root, getting 25. Distance = √((3−0)² + (4−0)²) = √(9 + 16) = √25, so the answer is 5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

2. The coordinates of the origin are
(a) (1, 1) (b) (0, 0) (c) (0, 1) (d) (1, 0)
Answer: (b)
Solution: The origin is the point where the x-axis and y-axis meet, so both coordinates are zero. The answer is (0, 0).
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

3. The point (−4, 3) lies in which quadrant?
(a) First (b) Fourth (c) Third (d) Second
Answer: (d)
Solution: A negative x with positive y places the point in the second quadrant; students often confuse this with the third quadrant where both are negative. The answer is Second.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

4. The distance of the point (5, −12) from the origin is
(a) 7 (b) 17 (c) 13 (d) 60
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong value 17 comes from simply adding 5 and 12. Distance = √(25 + 144) = √169, so the answer is 13.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

5. The midpoint of the segment joining (2, 6) and (8, 10) is
(a) (3, 2) (b) (10, 16) (c) (5, 8) (d) (6, 8)
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adding without dividing by 2 gives the trap (10, 16). Midpoint = ((2+8)/2, (6+10)/2), so the answer is (5, 8).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

6. The slope of the line passing through (1, 2) and (3, 6) is
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 1/2 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: Subtracting the y-values without dividing by the run gives the trap 4. Slope = (6−2)/(3−1) = 4/2, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

7. A point on the x-axis has its y-coordinate equal to
(a) 0 (b) x (c) 1 (d) −1
Answer: (a)
Solution: Every point on the x-axis lies at zero height above or below it, so its y-coordinate is 0. The answer is 0.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

8. The distance between (a, 0) and (0, b) is
(a) a + b (b) √(a² + b²) (c) a² + b² (d) √(a + b)
Answer: (b)
Solution: Adding a and b directly is the common trap. By the distance formula, distance = √(a² + b²), so the answer is √(a² + b²).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

9. The slope of a line parallel to the x-axis is
(a) 0 (b) −1 (c) 1 (d) undefined
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often pick "undefined", which applies to vertical lines. A horizontal line has zero rise, so the answer is 0.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

10. The slope of a line parallel to the y-axis is
(a) undefined (b) 1 (c) −1 (d) 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: Zero is the trap here, but that is the slope of a horizontal line. A vertical line has zero run, making the ratio undefined, so the answer is undefined.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

11. The point (0, −7) lies on
(a) the x-axis (b) the y-axis (c) the first quadrant (d) the third quadrant
Answer: (b)
Solution: Any point with x-coordinate zero lies on the y-axis, regardless of the sign of y. The answer is the y-axis.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Mathematics

12. The area of the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (4, 0) and (0, 3) is
(a) 6 (b) 12 (c) 7 (d) 24
Answer: (a)
Solution: Forgetting the factor 1/2 gives the trap 12. Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 4 × 3, so the answer is 6.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

13. If the points (2, 3), (4, k) and (6, 3) are collinear, then k equals
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 6 (d) 0
Answer: (b)
Solution: For collinear points the area of the triangle is zero: ½|2(k−3) + 4(3−3) + 6(3−k)| = 0 gives 2k − 6 = 0 after simplification, so k = 3. The answer is 3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

14. The equation of the x-axis is
(a) x = 0 (b) y = 0 (c) x = y (d) y = 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap x = 0 is actually the y-axis. On the x-axis every point has y = 0, so the answer is y = 0.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

15. The distance between the points (−1, −1) and (2, 3) is
(a) √7 (b) 7 (c) 5 (d) 25
Answer: (c)
Solution: Distance = √((2+1)² + (3+1)²) = √(9 + 16) = √25; stopping at 25 without the root is the trap. The answer is 5.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

16. The line y = 3x + 5 has y-intercept equal to
(a) 5 (b) 3 (c) −5 (d) 8
Answer: (a)
Solution: In y = mx + c, c is the y-intercept; 3 is the slope, which is the trap. The answer is 5.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

17. Two lines with slopes 2 and −1/2 are
(a) perpendicular (b) parallel (c) coincident (d) intersecting at 45°
Answer: (a)
Solution: Equal slopes would mean parallel, which is the trap. Since the product of slopes is 2 × (−1/2) = −1, the lines are perpendicular, so the answer is perpendicular.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

18. The centroid of the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (6, 0) and (0, 6) is
(a) (3, 3) (b) (4, 4) (c) (2, 2) (d) (6, 6)
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dividing by 2 instead of 3 gives the trap (3, 3). Centroid = ((0+6+0)/3, (0+0+6)/3), so the answer is (2, 2).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

19. The point which divides the segment joining (1, 2) and (5, 6) in the ratio 1 : 1 is
(a) (4, 5) (b) (2, 3) (c) (3, 4) (d) (6, 8)
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ratio 1 : 1 means the midpoint: ((1+5)/2, (2+6)/2). The answer is (3, 4).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

20. The slope of the line making an angle of 45° with the positive x-axis is
(a) 0 (b) 1/√3 (c) √3 (d) 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: Slope = tan θ, and tan 45° = 1; √3 is tan 60°, the trap. The answer is 1.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

21. The point P divides the segment joining A(2, −5) and B(5, 1) in the ratio 2 : 1 internally. The coordinates of P are
(a) (7/2, −2) (b) (3, −3) (c) (4, 1) (d) (4, −1)
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mixing up the ratio order gives the trap (3, −3). P = ((2×5 + 1×2)/3, (2×1 + 1×(−5))/3) = (12/3, −3/3), so the answer is (4, −1).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

22. The area of the triangle with vertices (1, 2), (3, 4) and (5, 6) is
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 0
Answer: (d)
Solution: Computing mechanically without checking gives traps like 2 or 4. The three points lie on the line y = x + 1, so they are collinear and the area is 0. The answer is 0.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

23. The distance between the parallel lines 3x + 4y + 5 = 0 and 3x + 4y + 15 = 0 is
(a) 10 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: Taking the difference of constants as 10 without dividing is the trap. Distance = |15 − 5|/√(9 + 16) = 10/5, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

24. If the slope of the line joining (2, k) and (4, 8) is 3, then k equals
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: (8 − k)/(4 − 2) = 3 gives 8 − k = 6, so k = 2. Solving 8 − k = 3 directly gives the trap 5. The answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

25. The equation of the line passing through (1, 1) with slope 2 is
(a) y = 2x + 1 (b) y = 2x − 1 (c) y = x + 2 (d) y = 2x − 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Using y = 2x + 1 ignores the given point, the common trap. Point-slope form: y − 1 = 2(x − 1), i.e. y = 2x − 1, so the answer is y = 2x − 1.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics
