51. The distance between the points (3, –2) and (–1, 2) is
(a) 5√2 (b) 2√5 (c) 4√2 (d) 6
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option 2√5 comes from squaring only one difference. Distance = √[(3+1)² + (–2–2)²] = √(16+16) = √32 = 4√2, so the answer is 4√2.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

52. The midpoint of the segment joining (–4, 6) and (8, –2) is
(a) (4, 4) (b) (6, 2) (c) (2, 4) (d) (2, 2)
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option (4, 4) comes from adding coordinates without dividing by 2. Midpoint = ((–4+8)/2, (6–2)/2) = (2, 2), so the answer is (2, 2).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

53. The point dividing the segment joining (2, 3) and (6, 7) internally in the ratio 1 : 3 is
(a) (5, 6) (b) (4, 5) (c) (3, 4) (d) (3, 5)
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option (4, 5) is the midpoint, which applies only for ratio 1:1. Using the section formula: x = (1×6 + 3×2)/4 = 3, y = (1×7 + 3×3)/4 = 4, so the answer is (3, 4).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

54. The area of the triangle with vertices (0, 0), (4, 0) and (0, 6) is
(a) 24 sq units (b) 12 sq units (c) 10 sq units (d) 6 sq units
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 24 comes from forgetting the factor 1/2. Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 4 × 6 = 12 sq units, so the answer is 12 sq units.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

55. The slope of the line passing through (2, 5) and (6, 13) is
(a) 1/2 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 1/2 comes from inverting the ratio. Slope = (13–5)/(6–2) = 8/4 = 2, so the answer is 2.

56. The slope of a line perpendicular to the line 3x + 4y = 7 is
(a) –3/4 (b) 3/4 (c) 4/3 (d) –4/3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option –3/4 is the slope of the given line itself, not its perpendicular. Given slope = –3/4; perpendicular slope = negative reciprocal = 4/3, so the answer is 4/3.

57. The distance of the point (3, 4) from the line 3x + 4y – 10 = 0 is
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 3 (d) 15
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option 15 comes from forgetting to divide by √(a²+b²). Distance = |3×3 + 4×4 – 10|/√(9+16) = 15/5 = 3, so the answer is 3.

58. The equation of the line with slope 2 and y-intercept –3 is
(a) y = 2x + 3 (b) y = –3x + 2 (c) 2x + y = 3 (d) y = 2x – 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option y = 2x + 3 flips the sign of the intercept. Using y = mx + c with m = 2 and c = –3 gives y = 2x – 3, so the answer is y = 2x – 3.

59. The coordinates of the centroid of the triangle with vertices (2, 4), (6, 10) and (4, 1) are
(a) (4, 4) (b) (3, 5) (c) (4, 5) (d) (5, 5)
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option (3, 5) comes from an arithmetic slip in adding the x-coordinates. Centroid = ((2+6+4)/3, (4+10+1)/3) = (12/3, 15/3) = (4, 5), so the answer is (4, 5).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

60. If the points (1, 2), (3, k) and (5, 6) are collinear, then k equals
(a) 4 (b) 3 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 6 comes from assuming k equals the last y-coordinate. Collinearity gives equal slopes: (k–2)/(3–1) = (6–k)/(5–3), so k–2 = 6–k, giving k = 4, so the answer is 4.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

61. The equation of the line passing through (1, 2) and parallel to 2x – 3y + 5 = 0 is
(a) 3x + 2y – 7 = 0 (b) 2x – 3y – 4 = 0 (c) 2x + 3y – 8 = 0 (d) 2x – 3y + 4 = 0
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option 3x + 2y – 7 = 0 is perpendicular, not parallel. A parallel line has form 2x – 3y + c = 0; substituting (1, 2): 2 – 6 + c = 0 gives c = 4, so the answer is 2x – 3y + 4 = 0.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

62. The angle between the lines y = x and y = √3 x is
(a) 15° (b) 30° (c) 45° (d) 60°
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 45° comes from misreading the slopes as 1 and 0. Slopes are 1 (45°) and √3 (60°); angle between them = 60° – 45° = 15°, so the answer is 15°.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

63. The radius of the circle x² + y² – 6x + 4y – 12 = 0 is
(a) 5 (b) 12 (c) 25 (d) √13
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 12 is just the constant term, not the radius. Centre = (3, –2); radius = √(9 + 4 + 12) = √25 = 5, so the answer is 5.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

64. The centre of the circle x² + y² + 8x – 10y + 5 = 0 is
(a) (4, –5) (b) (–8, 10) (c) (8, –10) (d) (–4, 5)
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option (–8, 10) forgets to halve the coefficients. Centre = (–g, –f) = (–8/2, 10/2) = (–4, 5), so the answer is (–4, 5).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

65. The distance between the parallel lines 3x + 4y + 5 = 0 and 3x + 4y + 15 = 0 is
(a) 2 (b) 5 (c) 10 (d) 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option 10 is the difference of constants without dividing. Distance = |15 – 5|/√(9+16) = 10/5 = 2, so the answer is 2.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

66. The point on the x-axis equidistant from (2, 3) and (4, 1) is
(a) (4, 0) (b) (2, 0) (c) (3, 0) (d) (1, 0)
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option (3, 0) is the midpoint's x-coordinate, which is not equidistant here. Let the point be (x, 0): (x–2)² + 9 = (x–4)² + 1, so x² – 4x + 13 = x² – 8x + 17, giving 4x = 4 and x = 1, so the answer is (1, 0).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

67. The equation of the line passing through (3, –1) and perpendicular to x + 2y = 4 is
(a) x + 2y – 1 = 0 (b) 2x – y – 7 = 0 (c) x – 2y – 5 = 0 (d) 2x – y + 7 = 0
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option x – 2y – 5 = 0 keeps the original slope structure instead of the perpendicular one. Given slope = –1/2, so perpendicular slope = 2; line: y + 1 = 2(x – 3), i.e. 2x – y – 7 = 0, so the answer is 2x – y – 7 = 0.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

68. The area of the triangle formed by the line 3x + 4y = 12 with the coordinate axes is
(a) 24 sq units (b) 6 sq units (c) 12 sq units (d) 3 sq units
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 12 is the product of intercepts without the factor 1/2. Intercepts are 4 and 3; area = ½ × 4 × 3 = 6 sq units, so the answer is 6 sq units.

69. If the line 2x + ky = 6 passes through (1, 2), then k equals
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option 1 comes from substituting the coordinates in the wrong order. Substituting: 2(1) + k(2) = 6 gives 2k = 4, so k = 2, so the answer is 2.

(a) (–3, –5) (b) (3, 5) (c) (–3, 5) (d) (5, –3)
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option (3, 5) is the reflection in the x-axis, not the y-axis. Reflection in the y-axis changes the sign of the x-coordinate only, giving (–3, –5), so the answer is (–3, –5).

71. The locus of a point equidistant from (1, 0) and (–1, 0) is
(a) x = 1 (b) y = 0 (c) x = 0 (d) y = x
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap option y = 0 is the line joining the two points, not the perpendicular bisector. Points equidistant from (1, 0) and (–1, 0) lie on the perpendicular bisector of the segment, which is the y-axis, x = 0, so the answer is x = 0.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics

73. The coordinates of the foot of the perpendicular from (2, 3) to the line x + y = 5 are
(a) (1, 4) (b) (2, 3) (c) (3, 2) (d) (4, 1)
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap option (3, 2) swaps the coordinates without checking. Since 2 + 3 = 5, the point (2, 3) already lies on the line, so the foot of the perpendicular is the point itself, so the answer is (2, 3).

74. Three vertices of a parallelogram are (1, 2), (3, 4) and (5, 2). The fourth vertex (opposite to (3, 4)) is
(a) (7, 4) (b) (4, 0) (c) (3, 2) (d) (3, 0)
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap option (7, 4) treats (3, 4) as adjacent rather than opposite. Diagonals bisect each other: midpoint of (1, 2)–(5, 2) is (3, 2); if the fourth vertex is (x, y), midpoint of (3, 4)–(x, y) = (3, 2) gives x = 3, y = 0, so the answer is (3, 0).
Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics

75. The equation of the circle with centre (2, –3) and passing through (5, 1) is
(a) x² + y² – 4x + 6y – 12 = 0 (b) x² + y² – 4x + 6y + 12 = 0 (c) x² + y² + 4x – 6y – 12 = 0 (d) x² + y² – 4x – 6y – 12 = 0
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap option with +12 has the wrong sign of the constant term. Radius² = (5–2)² + (1+3)² = 9 + 16 = 25; equation: (x–2)² + (y+3)² = 25, expanding gives x² + y² – 4x + 6y – 12 = 0, so the answer is x² + y² – 4x + 6y – 12 = 0.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Mathematics
