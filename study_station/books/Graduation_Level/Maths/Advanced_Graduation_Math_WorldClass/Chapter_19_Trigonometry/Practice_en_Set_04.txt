76. If sin θ + cos θ = √2, then the value of θ (0° ≤ θ ≤ 90°) is
(a) 30° (b) 60° (c) 45° (d) 90°
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 60°, chosen by those who guess the largest standard angle. Squaring both sides: sin²θ + cos²θ + 2 sin θ cos θ = 2, so 1 + sin 2θ = 2, giving sin 2θ = 1, hence 2θ = 90° and θ = 45°. So the answer is 45°.

77. The value of tan 1° · tan 2° · tan 3° … tan 89° is
(a) 1 (b) 0 (c) √3 (d) undefined
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 0, chosen by those who think tan 90° appears in the product — it does not, the product stops at 89°. Pair terms: tan θ · tan(90° − θ) = tan θ · cot θ = 1. There are 44 such pairs and the middle term tan 45° = 1, so the whole product is 1. So the answer is 1.
