Card 1
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the signs of trig functions in the four quadrants?
Back: "All Students Take Calculus" (ASTC) — Quadrant I: All positive; II: Sin positive; III: Tan positive; IV: Cos positive.

Card 2
Front: What is the value of sin 30°?
Back: 1/2

Card 3
Front: What is the value of cos 60°?
Back: 1/2

Card 4
Front: What is the value of tan 45°?
Back: 1

Card 5
Front: What is the fundamental Pythagorean identity?
Back: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

Card 6
Front: What is 1 + tan²θ equal to?
Back: sec²θ

Card 7
Front: What is 1 + cot²θ equal to?
Back: cosec²θ

Card 8
Front: What is the mnemonic for remembering sin, cos, tan ratios in a right triangle?
Back: "SOH-CAH-TOA" — Sin = Opposite/Hypotenuse, Cos = Adjacent/Hypotenuse, Tan = Opposite/Adjacent.

Card 9
Front: What is the pattern to remember sin values for 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°?
Back: √(0/4), √(1/4), √(2/4), √(3/4), √(4/4) → 0, 1/2, 1/√2, √3/2, 1. Cos values are the same in reverse.

Card 10
Front: What is sin(90° − θ) equal to?
Back: cos θ

Card 11
Front: What is tan(90° − θ) equal to?
Back: cot θ

Card 12
Front: What is the formula for sin(A + B)?
Back: sin A cos B + cos A sin B

Card 13
Front: What is the formula for cos(A + B)?
Back: cos A cos B − sin A sin B

Card 14
Front: What is sin 2θ equal to?
Back: 2 sin θ cos θ

Card 15
Front: What are the three forms of cos 2θ?
Back: cos²θ − sin²θ = 2cos²θ − 1 = 1 − 2sin²θ

Card 16
Front: What is tan 2θ equal to?
Back: 2 tan θ / (1 − tan²θ)

Card 17
Front: What is the maximum value of sin θ and cos θ?
Back: 1 (minimum value is −1)

Card 18
Front: If the angle of elevation of the sun is 45°, how does a pole's height compare to its shadow?
Back: They are equal, because tan 45° = 1 = height/shadow.

Card 19
Front: What is the value of sin 0° and cos 90°?
Back: Both are 0.

Card 20
Front: What is the mnemonic for the complementary angle pairs?
Back: "Co-functions swap at 90°": sin↔cos, tan↔cot, sec↔cosec — each function of θ equals its co-function of (90° − θ).
