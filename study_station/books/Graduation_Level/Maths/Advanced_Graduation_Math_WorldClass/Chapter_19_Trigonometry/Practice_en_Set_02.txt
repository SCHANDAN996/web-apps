26. If sin θ + cos θ = √2, then the value of sin θ · cos θ is
(a) 1/4 (b) 1/2 (c) √2/2 (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: Squaring both sides: (sin θ + cos θ)² = sin²θ + cos²θ + 2 sin θ cos θ = 1 + 2 sin θ cos θ = 2. So 2 sin θ cos θ = 1, giving sin θ cos θ = 1/2. The tempting wrong option is 1, which comes from forgetting to divide by 2 after subtracting 1 from 2. So the answer is 1/2.

27. The value of tan 1° · tan 2° · tan 3° … tan 89° is
