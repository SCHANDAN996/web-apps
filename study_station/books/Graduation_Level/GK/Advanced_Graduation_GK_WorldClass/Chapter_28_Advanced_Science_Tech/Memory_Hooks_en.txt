# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Advanced Science & Tech

---

**Hook 1 — ISRO's Workhorse Rockets**
> **"Papa Gave Lovely Sweets"** → **P**SLV, **G**SLV, **L**VM3 (GSLV Mk III), **S**SLV
Order of lifting power: SSLV (small) → PSLV → GSLV → LVM3 (heaviest).
*Trap alert:* PSLV = **Polar** Satellite Launch Vehicle; GSLV = **Geo**synchronous. Examiners swap the full forms!

---

**Hook 2 — NavIC's 7 Satellites**
> **"Seven Brothers Never Leave Indian Ground"** → NavIC has **7** satellites: **3** in Geostationary orbit + **4** in Geosynchronous orbit.
*Trap alert:* GPS (USA) = 24+ satellites; NavIC = 7. Don't mix the counts.

---

**Hook 3 — The Four Global Navigation Systems**
> **"Global Giants: G-G-G-B"** → **G**PS (USA), **G**LONASS (Russia), **G**alileo (EU), **B**eiDou (China) — these four cover the whole globe.
*Trap alert:* **NavIC/IRNSS (India) is a REGIONAL system**, not global — it covers India and about 1,500 km beyond its borders. Examiners love to slip NavIC into the "global" list!

---

**Hook 4 — Chandrayaan Missions**
> **"One Orbits, Two Crashes, Three Lands"** →
Chandrayaan-**1** (2008): orbiter, found water molecules.
Chandrayaan-**2** (2019): orbiter works, lander Vikram crashed.
Chandrayaan-**3** (2023): soft landing near south pole (23 Aug 2023).
*Trap alert:* The lander of C-3 = **Vikram**, rover = **Pragyan** — same names as C-2!

---

**Hook 5 — DNA vs RNA**
> **"DNA is Double, RNA is Running solo"** → DNA = double-stranded, **T**hymine; RNA = single-stranded, **U**racil replaces Thymine.
*Trick:* "**U** ran away with RNA" — Uracil is in RNA only.

---

**Hook 6 — COVID Vaccine Makers (India)**
> **"Bharat's Serum Saved Us"** → **C**ovaxin = **B**harat Biotech; **C**ovishield = **S**erum Institute of India.
*Trap alert:* Examiners swap the two companies constantly. Covaxin = indigenous; Covishield = Oxford-AstraZeneca licensed.

---

**Hook 7 — Supercomputer Family**
> **"PARAM's Pratyush Predicts the Weather"** → PARAM series (C-DAC built, first = PARAM 8000, 1991); **Pratyush & Mihir** = weather/monsoon supercomputers (MoES).
*Trap alert:* PARAM = C-DAC; Pratyush = IITM Pune (weather). Different ministries!

---

**Hook 8 — Missile Series under IGMDP**
> **"Agni Paces, Prithvi Punches, Akash Aims up, Nag Nips tanks, Trishul Targets low"** →
**Agni** = ballistic (long range), **Prithvi** = surface-to-surface, **Akash** = surface-to-air, **Nag** = anti-tank, **Trishul** = short-range SAM.
*Trap alert:* Agni is ballistic; BrahMos is a **cruise** missile (India-Russia JV) — don't classify BrahMos as ballistic.

---

**Hook 9 — Generations of Computers**
> **"Vacuum Tubes Transistor-ed Into Very Large Chips"** →
1st = **V**acuum tubes, 2nd = **T**ransistors, 3rd = **I**ntegrated Circuits, 4th = **V**LSI/microprocessors, 5th = AI/ULSI.

---

**Hook 10 — Internet & Web Inventors**
> **"Tim Berned the Web, Vinton Cerf-ed the Net"** →
**WWW** = Tim Berners-Lee (1989, CERN); **Internet/TCP-IP** = Vint Cerf & Bob Kahn.
*Trap alert:* The Internet ≠ the Web. Berners-Lee invented the Web, NOT the Internet.

---

**Hook 11 — Nuclear vs Thermal Power Sites**
> **"Rawatbhata's Atoms, Singrauli's Steam"** →
Nuclear: Rawatbhata (Rajasthan), Tarapur (Maharashtra), Kalpakkam (TN), Kudankulam (TN — with Russia), Narora (UP), Kakrapar (Gujarat), Kaiga (Karnataka).
*Trick:* "**K**udankulam has a **K**remlin connection" (Russian reactors).

---

**Hook 12 — BARC, ISRO, DRDO Headquarters**
> **"Mumbai Bombs, Bengaluru Birds, Delhi Defends"** →
BARC = **Mumbai** (nuclear), ISRO = **Bengaluru** (space), DRDO = **New Delhi** (defence).
*Trap alert:* ISRO launches from **Sriharikota** (Andhra Pradesh), but HQ is Bengaluru — the classic swap trap.

---

**Hook 13 — Cryogenic Engine = India's Hard-Won Tech**
> **"Cryo = Cold + Costly + Conquered late"** → Cryogenic engines use liquid hydrogen + liquid oxygen at extreme cold; India mastered them after the 1990s denial of Russian tech — used in GSLV/LVM3 upper stages.
*Trap alert:* PSLV does **not** use a cryogenic stage; GSLV/LVM3 do.

---

**Hook 14 — Stem Cell Types**
> **"Toti can make a Total baby, Pluri makes Parts"** →
**Toti**potent → whole organism (zygote); **Pluri**potent → almost all body cells (embryonic stem cells); **Multi**potent → limited cell types (adult stem cells).

---

**Hook 15 — 5G vs 4G in One Line**
> **"5G = 5 times Faster, Fraction of the lag"** → 5G offers far higher speed and ultra-low latency (~1 ms target) vs 4G LTE. India's 5G rollout began in **October 2022**.
*Trap alert:* 5G is a telecom standard, not a satellite technology — examiners love that confusion.

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

1. **The "One Unknown Founder/Year" Rule:** If a question asks you to match four missions/scientists/institutes with their years or founders and you are blank on even **one** pair, skip immediately. Matching questions are all-or-nothing — partial knowledge gives a false sense of confidence and eats 90+ seconds.

2. **The "Latest Number" Trap:** If a question asks for a current figure (number of satellites, supercomputer rank, vaccine doses, 5G cities) and the options are close together (e.g., 104 vs 108), skip unless you have read that exact figure recently. These change yearly and are the highest-risk guesses in Science & Tech.

3. **The "Statement Soup" Rule:** If a question has 4 long statements about a technology you only vaguely know (e.g., quantum computing, blockchain), and two statements *both* sound plausible, skip. In S&T, examiners hide one wrong word ("all" instead of "some", "first" instead of "second") — vague familiarity loses marks to negative marking. Attempt only when you can kill at least two options with certainty.
