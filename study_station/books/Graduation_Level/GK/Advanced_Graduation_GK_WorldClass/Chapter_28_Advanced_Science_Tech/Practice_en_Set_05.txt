101. Which of the following is the primary fuel used in India's Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR) at Kalpakkam?
(a) Enriched uranium-235 (b) Heavy water (c) Thorium-232 directly (d) Mixed oxide (MOX) of plutonium and uranium
Answer: (d)
Solution: Thorium-232 is tempting because India's three-stage programme is thorium-based, but thorium is only a blanket material that gets converted to U-233; it is not the direct fuel. The PFBR uses mixed oxide fuel of plutonium and uranium, so the answer is mixed oxide (MOX) of plutonium and uranium.
Source: Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India

102. The 'Gaganyaan' mission's human-rated launch vehicle is a modified version of which rocket?
(a) LVM3 (b) GSLV Mk II (c) PSLV (d) SSLV
Answer: (a)
Solution: GSLV Mk II is a tempting distractor because of its cryogenic stage, but the human-rated vehicle chosen for Gaganyaan is the LVM3 (formerly GSLV Mk III), designated HLVM3. PSLV has no cryogenic upper stage and insufficient lift capacity, so the answer is LVM3.
Source: ISRO official website

103. In CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, the role of the guide RNA (gRNA) is to:
(a) Cut both strands of DNA
(b) Direct the Cas9 protein to the target DNA sequence
(c) Repair the DNA after cutting
(d) Copy the edited gene into RNA
Answer: (b)
Solution: Cutting DNA is done by the Cas9 protein itself, which makes the first option a tempting trap. The guide RNA provides sequence complementarity that leads Cas9 to the correct target site, so the answer is that it directs the Cas9 protein to the target DNA sequence.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology (Biotechnology)

104. India's first indigenously developed aircraft carrier, commissioned in September 2022, is:
(a) INS Viraat (b) INS Vikramaditya (c) INS Vikrant (d) INS Vishal
Answer: (c)
Solution: INS Vikramaditya is the common trap because it is also in service, but it is a refurbished Russian carrier, not indigenous. INS Viraat was decommissioned in 2017. The indigenous carrier commissioned in 2022 is INS Vikrant.
Source: Indian Navy, Government of India

105. Which of the following correctly describes 'quantum supremacy'?
(a) A quantum computer that never makes errors (b) A quantum computer solving a problem infeasible for classical supercomputers (c) A quantum computer with more than 1000 qubits (d) A quantum network connecting two continents
Answer: (b)
Solution: The qubit-count option is a trap because there is no fixed qubit threshold defining supremacy. Quantum supremacy means demonstrating a computation that a classical supercomputer cannot perform in any reasonable time, so the answer is solving a problem infeasible for classical supercomputers.
Source: PYQ-style

106. The 'NavIC' satellite navigation system of India consists of a constellation of how many operational satellites in its original configuration?
(a) 7 (b) 5 (c) 9 (d) 24
Answer: (a)
Solution: 24 is a tempting figure because it is the GPS constellation size, not NavIC's. NavIC (IRNSS) was designed with 7 satellites — three in geostationary and four in geosynchronous inclined orbits, so the answer is 7.
Source: ISRO official website

107. Which Indian institute developed the 'Param' series of supercomputers?
(a) BARC (b) CSIR (c) DRDO (d) C-DAC
Answer: (d)
Solution: BARC is tempting because it also builds supercomputers (like Anupam), but the Param series, starting with PARAM 8000 in 1991, was developed by the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing, so the answer is C-DAC.
Source: C-DAC, Ministry of Electronics & IT

108. In the context of 5G technology, 'network slicing' refers to:
(a) Creating multiple virtual networks on shared physical infrastructure (b) Physically dividing optical fibre cables (c) Splitting spectrum among telecom companies (d) Reducing tower height to avoid interference
Answer: (a)
Solution: Spectrum splitting is tempting because spectrum is indeed auctioned, but that is allocation, not slicing. Network slicing creates independent virtual networks with different characteristics over one physical network, so the answer is creating multiple virtual networks on shared physical infrastructure.
Source: PYQ-style

109. The 'Bharatiya Antariksh Station' (Bharatiya Space Station) announced by India is planned to be established by which target year?
(a) 2028 (b) 2035 (c) 2030 (d) 2040
Answer: (b)
Solution: 2040 is the trap because it is the target year for an Indian crewed lunar landing, not the space station. The government announced the Bharatiya Antariksh Station target as 2035, so the answer is 2035.
Source: Government of India announcements (2023)

110. Which of the following diseases is targeted by the world's first DNA vaccine approved for human use, ZyCoV-D, developed in India?
(a) COVID-19 (b) Dengue (c) Hepatitis B (d) Rabies
Answer: (a)
Solution: Hepatitis B is tempting because its vaccine is recombinant and gene-based, but it is a protein subunit vaccine, not DNA. ZyCoV-D by Zydus is a plasmid DNA vaccine against COVID-19, so the answer is COVID-19.
Source: DBT, Government of India

111. The 'Agni-V' missile of India is best classified as:
(a) A cruise missile (b) An anti-tank guided missile (c) A short-range tactical missile (d) An intercontinental-range ballistic missile
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cruise missile is the trap because BrahMos is India's famous cruise missile, but Agni-V is a ballistic missile with a range above 5,000 km, placing it in the intercontinental class, so the answer is an intercontinental-range ballistic missile.
Source: DRDO, Government of India

112. Which of the following is NOT a greenhouse gas?
(a) Methane (b) Nitrous oxide (c) Carbon monoxide (d) Sulphur hexafluoride
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sulphur hexafluoride is tempting because it sounds industrial, but it is actually one of the most potent greenhouse gases listed under the Kyoto Protocol. Carbon monoxide is an air pollutant but not a direct greenhouse gas, so the answer is carbon monoxide.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

113. The 'Chandrayaan-3' mission's lander was named:
(a) Pragyan (b) Vikram (c) Shiv Shakti (d) Tiranga
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pragyan is the classic trap because it flew on the same mission, but Pragyan is the rover, not the lander. Shiv Shakti is the name of the landing point. The lander was named Vikram, so the answer is Vikram.
Source: ISRO official website

114. 'Blockchain' technology ensures data integrity primarily through:
(a) Centralised server verification (b) Frequent deletion of old records (c) Cryptographic hashing and distributed consensus (d) Encryption by a single private key holder
Answer: (c)
Solution: Centralised verification is the trap because it sounds secure, but blockchain is specifically decentralised. Integrity comes from cryptographic hashes linking blocks and consensus among distributed nodes, so the answer is cryptographic hashing and distributed consensus.
Source: PYQ-style

115. India's 'National Quantum Mission' was approved with an outlay of approximately:
(a) ₹1,000 crore (b) ₹500 crore (c) ₹10,372 crore (d) ₹6,003 crore
Answer: (d)
Solution: ₹10,372 crore is the trap because it is the outlay of the IndiaAI Mission, not the quantum mission. The National Quantum Mission (2023) was approved with about ₹6,003.65 crore, so the answer is ₹6,003 crore.
Source: Cabinet approval, Government of India (2023)

116. Which technique is used in DNA fingerprinting to amplify small DNA samples?
(a) Gel electrophoresis (b) Polymerase Chain Reaction (c) Southern blotting (d) Centrifugation
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gel electrophoresis is tempting because it is also used in fingerprinting, but it separates DNA fragments rather than amplifying them. Amplification of minute samples is done by the Polymerase Chain Reaction, so the answer is Polymerase Chain Reaction.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

117. The 'LCA Tejas' is powered by which engine in its current operational variants?
(a) Kaveri (b) AL-31F (c) GE F404 (d) RD-33
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kaveri is the well-known trap because it is India's indigenous engine programme, but it did not meet thrust requirements and Tejas does not fly with it operationally. Current Tejas variants use the GE F404 engine, so the answer is GE F404.
Source: HAL / DRDO, Government of India

118. In the context of artificial intelligence, 'deep learning' is based on:
(a) Multi-layered artificial neural networks (b) Rule-based expert systems (c) Simple linear regression (d) Decision trees only
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rule-based expert systems are tempting because they are an older form of AI, but they are not deep learning. Deep learning uses artificial neural networks with many hidden layers, so the answer is multi-layered artificial neural networks.
Source: PYQ-style

119. The 'NISAR' satellite is a joint mission between ISRO and which space agency?
(a) ESA (b) JAXA (c) NASA (d) Roscosmos
Answer: (c)
Solution: Roscosmos is tempting because of India's long space cooperation with Russia, but NISAR (NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar) is a joint Earth-observation mission with NASA, so the answer is NASA.
Source: ISRO / NASA official websites

120. Which of the following is the correct order of electromagnetic waves in increasing wavelength?
(a) Radio waves < Microwaves < X-rays < Gamma rays (b) X-rays < Gamma rays < Radio waves < Microwaves (c) Gamma rays < X-rays < Microwaves < Radio waves (d) Microwaves < Radio waves < Gamma rays < X-rays
Answer: (c)
Solution: The first option is the trap because it lists the waves in a familiar-sounding order but reverses the wavelength relationship of X-rays and gamma rays with radio waves. Wavelength increases from gamma rays to radio waves, so the answer is gamma rays < X-rays < microwaves < radio waves.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

121. Consider the following statements about India's three-stage nuclear power programme:
1. Stage 1 uses Pressurised Heavy Water Reactors with natural uranium.
2. Stage 2 uses Fast Breeder Reactors fuelled by plutonium obtained from Stage 1.
3. Stage 3 uses thorium-232 directly as fissile fuel.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 3 is the examiner's trap: thorium-232 is fertile, not fissile, so Stage 3 first converts thorium into fissile uranium-233 rather than burning thorium directly. Statements 1 and 2 correctly describe the PHWR and FBR stages, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India

122. Arrange the following Indian space milestones in correct chronological order:
1. Launch of Aryabhata
2. Launch of Chandrayaan-1
3. First flight of SLV-3
4. Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan)
(a) 1, 3, 2, 4 (b) 3, 1, 4, 2 (c) 1, 3, 4, 2 (d) 3, 1, 2, 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap is placing SLV-3 before Aryabhata: Aryabhata was launched in 1975 by a Soviet rocket, while India's own SLV-3 flew in 1980. Chandrayaan-1 (2008) preceded Mangalyaan (2013), so the answer is 1, 3, 2, 4.
Source: ISRO official website

123. Consider the following statements about mRNA vaccines:
1. They introduce a weakened form of the virus into the body.
2. They carry instructions for cells to produce a viral protein that triggers immunity.
3. The mRNA enters the cell nucleus and alters human DNA.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 1 is the trap because it describes traditional live-attenuated vaccines, not mRNA vaccines; statement 3 is false because mRNA does not enter the nucleus or alter DNA. Only statement 2 is correct, so the answer is 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology / PYQ-style

124. Match the following defence systems with their categories:
1. Akash — P. Ballistic missile defence interceptor
2. Prithvi Air Defence — Q. Surface-to-air missile
3. BrahMos — R. Supersonic cruise missile
(a) 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R (b) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R (c) 1-Q, 2-R, 3-P (d) 1-R, 2-P, 3-Q
Answer: (a)
Solution: The similar-names trap is confusing Akash with Prithvi Air Defence: Akash is a surface-to-air missile, while Prithvi Air Defence (PAD) is the exo-atmospheric interceptor of India's ballistic missile defence programme. BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile, so the answer is 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R.
Source: DRDO, Government of India

125. Consider the following statements about the 'Semiconductor' ecosystem in India:
1. The India Semiconductor Mission was launched under the Ministry of Electronics and IT.
2. Silicon is the most widely used semiconductor material because it is a good conductor of electricity at all temperatures.
3. Doping silicon with phosphorus creates an n-type semiconductor.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 2 is the almost-correct trap: silicon is a semiconductor precisely because its conductivity is poor at low temperatures and rises with temperature — it is not a good conductor at all temperatures. Statements 1 and 3 are correct (phosphorus is pentavalent, giving n-type), so the answer is 1 and 3 only.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics / MeitY, Government of India
