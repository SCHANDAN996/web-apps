101. Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the amendment procedure?
(a) Article 356 (b) Article 360 (c) Article 370 (d) Article 368
Answer: (d)
Solution: Article 356 (President's Rule) and Article 360 (financial emergency) are tempting because they sit in the same Part, but they deal with emergencies, not amendment. Article 368 in Part XX lays down the power and procedure of Parliament to amend the Constitution, so the answer is Article 368.

123. Consider the following statements about the Preamble:
1. In the Berubari case (1960), the Supreme Court held that the Preamble is a part of the Constitution.
2. In the Kesavananda Bharati case (1973), the Supreme Court held that the Preamble is a part of the Constitution.
3. The Preamble has been amended only once so far.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 1 is the classic trap — in Berubari (1960) the Court held the Preamble is NOT a part of the Constitution, the opposite of what is stated. Kesavananda Bharati (1973) reversed this view, and the Preamble has been amended only once, by the 42nd Amendment, so statements 2 and 3 are correct and the answer is 2 and 3 only.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — an amendment bill can be introduced in either House, not only the Lok Sabha, unlike a money bill. Article 368 governs amendment, and a constitutional amendment bill must be passed by each House separately with no joint sitting, so statements 1 and 3 are correct and the answer is 1 and 3 only.
