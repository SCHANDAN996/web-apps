126. Which of the following statements about the Preamble of the Indian Constitution is correct?
(a) It is a source of power to the legislature (b) It is enforceable in courts of law (c) It is part of the Constitution but not a source of power (d) It can override the express provisions of the Constitution
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is the claim that the Preamble is enforceable in courts; in the Berubari Union case (1960) the Supreme Court held it is not a source of power nor enforceable, and in Kesavananda Bharati (1973) it was held to be part of the Constitution. So the answer is that it is part of the Constitution but not a source of power.
