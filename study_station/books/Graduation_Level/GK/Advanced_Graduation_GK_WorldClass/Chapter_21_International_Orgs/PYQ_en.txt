# 📊 PYQ Analysis — International Organizations

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from This Chapter

International Organizations is one of the most **reliable, high-frequency static GK areas** across UPSC (Prelims), SSC (CGL/CHSL/MTS), Banking (IBPS/SBI PO-Clerk), Railways (NTPC/Group D) and State PCS prelims. Questions are almost always **direct, fact-based and one-liner** — the examiner's real weapon is not depth but **confusion between similar-looking facts**.

### Recurring sub-topics (in rough order of how often they appear)

1. **Headquarters of organizations** — the single most repeated theme. Geneva (WHO, WTO, ILO, WIPO, UNHCR, ITU), Washington D.C. (IMF, World Bank), New York (UN, UNICEF, UNDP), Paris (UNESCO, OECD), Rome (FAO, WFP), Vienna (IAEA, OPEC, UNIDO), The Hague (ICJ, OPCW), Brussels (NATO), Kathmandu (SAARC), Jakarta (ASEAN).
2. **Founding years and parent conferences** — UN (1945, San Francisco), IMF & World Bank (1944, Bretton Woods), WTO (1995, successor of GATT), NATO (1949), SAARC (1985), ASEAN (1967), NAM (1961, Belgrade), BRICS (first summit 2009).
3. **Heads / Chiefs of organizations** — asked in current-linked static form; the post (e.g., "WHO is headed by a Director-General") is static, the name changes and must be checked as of the exam year.
4. **UN structure** — six principal organs, official languages (6), permanent members of UNSC (P5), non-permanent members (10, 2-year terms), ICJ judges (15, 9-year terms).
5. **Membership numbers and India's role** — UN member states (193), India as a founding member of UN, NAM, G-20; India's contributions to peacekeeping.
6. **Specialized agencies vs. funds/programmes** — WHO/ILO/FAO (specialized agencies) vs. UNICEF/UNDP/UNHCR (funds and programmes) — a classic UPSC-style distinction.
7. **Reports and indices published by organizations** — Human Development Report (UNDP), World Development Report (World Bank), World Economic Outlook (IMF). **Caution:** some famous indices are NOT published by international organizations — e.g., the Global Hunger Index is published by the NGOs Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe, not by any UN body; examiners exploit this confusion.

---

## 2. Exam-wise Pattern (in words, not numbers)

- **UPSC Prelims:** Statement-based questions ("Which of the following is/are correct?"), matching lists (organization ↔ headquarters ↔ function), and conceptual traps (e.g., "IMF gives loans for poverty reduction" — that is the World Bank's mandate).
- **SSC (CGL/CHSL/MTS):** Pure one-liners — headquarters, founding year, full forms, current chief. Speed matters more than depth.
- **Banking (IBPS/SBI):** Headquarters + chief + India's membership status; often linked to financial bodies (IMF, World Bank, ADB, AIIB, NDB).
- **Railways (NTPC/Group D):** Simplest level — headquarters, full forms, founding years.
- **State PCS:** Mix of SSC-style one-liners plus India-specific angles (India and UNSC reforms, India's role in SAARC/BRICS).

---

## 3. The Examiner's Traps (जाल) — Know Them Before the Exam

| Trap type | How it looks | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Almost Correct** | One word changed in an otherwise true statement | "UNESCO headquarters — Geneva" (it is Paris; Geneva hosts WHO/WTO/ILO) |
| **Chronological Confusion** | Swapped years of similar events | UN 1945 vs. IMF/World Bank 1944 vs. WTO 1995 (GATT 1947) |
| **Similar Names** | Bodies with overlapping names | UNHCR (refugees) vs. UNICEF (children); ICJ (The Hague) vs. ICC (also The Hague, but different body, est. 2002) |
| **Negative Phrasing** | "Which is NOT…", "All EXCEPT…" | "Which of the following is NOT a specialized agency of the UN?" (UNICEF is a fund, not a specialized agency) |
| **Headquarters Cluster Trap** | Many Geneva bodies shuffled | WTO–Geneva, ILO–Geneva, but UNESCO–Paris, FAO–Rome |
| **Membership Trap** | Wrong member count or wrong member | "SAARC has 7 members" (it has 8 — Afghanistan joined in 2007) |
| **Publisher Trap** | Famous index attributed to a UN body | "Global Hunger Index is published by FAO" (it is published by NGOs Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe) |

## 4. Top 10 High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions with Solutions

**Q1. The headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO) is located at:**
(a) Paris (b) Geneva (c) Rome (d) New York
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** ILO, founded in 1919 (the only League of Nations body still active), is headquartered in Geneva. **Trap: Almost Correct** — Paris (UNESCO) and Rome (FAO) are offered because they are also UN-agency capitals.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q2. Which of the following is NOT a specialized agency of the United Nations?**
(a) WHO (b) FAO (c) UNICEF (d) UNESCO
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** UNICEF is a **fund/programme** of the UN General Assembly, not a specialized agency. WHO, FAO and UNESCO are specialized agencies with their own charters. **Trap: Negative Phrasing** — read the "NOT" twice before answering.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3. The Bretton Woods Conference (1944) led to the creation of:**
(a) UN and UNESCO (b) IMF and World Bank (c) WTO and GATT (d) FAO and WHO
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** The 1944 Bretton Woods (USA) conference created the IMF and the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (World Bank). **Trap: Chronological Confusion** — WTO came only in 1995; GATT (1947) was an agreement, not an organization.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4. The International Court of Justice is headquartered at:**
(a) Geneva (b) New York (c) The Hague (d) Vienna
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** The ICJ, the principal judicial organ of the UN, sits at The Hague (Netherlands) — the only principal UN organ not in New York. It has 15 judges elected for 9-year terms. **Trap: Similar Names** — do not confuse with the International Criminal Court (ICC, est. 2002), also at The Hague but NOT a UN organ.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q5. The headquarters of SAARC is located at:**
(a) New Delhi (b) Colombo (c) Kathmandu (d) Dhaka
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** SAARC (est. 8 December 1985, Dhaka) has its secretariat in Kathmandu, Nepal. It has 8 members; Afghanistan joined in 2007. **Trap: Almost Correct** — Dhaka is offered because the first summit was held there.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6. How many permanent members does the UN Security Council have?**
(a) 5 (b) 10 (c) 15 (d) 6
**Answer: (a)**
**Solution:** The UNSC has 5 permanent members (China, France, Russia, UK, USA — the P5, with veto power) and 10 non-permanent members elected for 2-year terms. **Trap: Almost Correct** — 15 is the *total* strength; 10 is the non-permanent count.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7. The World Trade Organization (WTO) came into existence on:**
(a) 1 January 1947 (b) 24 October 1945 (c) 1 January 1995 (d) 1 January 2001
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** WTO was established on 1 January 1995 under the Marrakesh Agreement, replacing GATT (1947). Headquarters: Geneva. **Trap: Chronological Confusion** — 1947 is GATT's year; 1945 is the UN's.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8. Which organization publishes the Human Development Report?**
(a) World Bank (b) UNDP (c) IMF (d) WHO
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** The Human Development Report (with the HDI) has been published by the UNDP since 1990. **Trap: Similar Names** — the World Bank publishes the *World Development Report*; the names are deliberately close.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9. The headquarters of OPEC is located at:**
(a) Riyadh (b) Vienna (c) Geneva (d) Doha
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** OPEC (est. 1960, Baghdad) has been headquartered in Vienna, Austria since 1965. Vienna also hosts IAEA and UNIDO. **Trap: Almost Correct** — Riyadh/Doha are offered because OPEC is an oil body, but its HQ is in Europe.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?**
(a) UNESCO — Geneva (b) FAO — Paris (c) UNICEF — New York (d) IMF — Geneva
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** UNICEF is headquartered in New York. UNESCO is in Paris, FAO in Rome, and the IMF in Washington D.C. **Trap: Headquarters Cluster Trap** — every wrong option swaps a body into Geneva or swaps Paris/Rome.
**Source:** PYQ-style

## 5. The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**Rule:** If a question is a pure recall one-liner (headquarters, year, full form) and you know it — answer in **15 seconds** and move on. If it is statement-based or has a negative phrasing — give it up to **45 seconds** to re-read carefully; never more.

**Applied to Q4 (ICJ — The Hague):** Pure recall. If you know "ICJ = The Hague", mark it in 15 seconds. Do NOT waste time re-reading the options — Geneva and New York are decoys for the hesitant.

**Applied to Q2 (NOT a specialized agency):** Negative phrasing + conceptual distinction. This deserves the full 45 seconds: underline "NOT", then classify each option (agency vs. fund). Rushing this question type is where most marks are lost — the 15-second instinct says "all are UN bodies", which is exactly the trap.

**Skip signal:** If a question asks for a *current chief's name* you have not revised recently, skip and return — guessing between two similar foreign names has negative expected value in negative-marking exams.

## 6. One-Line Revision Bank (Most-Tested Facts)

- UN: est. 24 Oct 1945 (San Francisco Conference); HQ New York; 193 members; 6 official languages.
- UNSC: 5 permanent + 10 non-permanent (2-year terms).
- ICJ: The Hague; 15 judges; 9-year terms.
- IMF & World Bank: 1944, Bretton Woods; both HQ Washington D.C.
- WTO: 1 Jan 1995; Geneva; succeeded GATT (1947).
- WHO: 1948; Geneva; 7 April = World Health Day.
- UNESCO: 1945; Paris. | UNICEF: 1946; New York. | FAO: 1945; Rome.
- ILO: 1919; Geneva; Nobel Peace Prize 1969.
- NATO: 1949; Brussels. | SAARC: 1985; Kathmandu; 8 members. | ASEAN: 1967; Jakarta; 10 members.
- NAM: 1961, Belgrade. | OPEC: 1960; Vienna. | Amnesty International: 1961; London.
- ADB: 1966; Manila. | AIIB: 2016; Beijing. | New Development Bank (BRICS): 2015; Shanghai.
- Human Development Report → UNDP; World Development Report → World Bank; World Economic Outlook → IMF.
- Global Hunger Index → NGOs Concern Worldwide & Welthungerhilfe (NOT a UN body — a favourite trap).
