Questions 51–75: Medium

51. The headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO) is located in:
(a) Paris (b) Vienna (c) Geneva (d) Rome
Answer: (c)
Solution: Paris is a trap because UNESCO and the OECD are headquartered there, but the ILO, founded in 1919 and now a UN specialized agency, is based in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: ILO official website (ilo.org)

52. Which of the following is NOT a principal organ of the United Nations?
(a) Trusteeship Council (b) Economic and Social Council (c) International Monetary Fund (d) International Court of Justice
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Trusteeship Council is a tempting wrong pick because it suspended operations in 1994, yet it remains a principal organ; the IMF is a specialized agency, not a principal organ, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.
Source: UN Charter, Chapter III

53. The International Court of Justice consists of how many judges?
(a) 15 (b) 11 (c) 9 (d) 21
Answer: (a)
Solution: The number 9 is a trap borrowed from the US Supreme Court; the ICJ at The Hague has 15 judges elected for nine-year terms by the UNGA and UNSC, so the answer is 15.
Source: ICJ official website (icj-cij.org)

54. Which UN specialized agency is headquartered in Montreal, Canada?
(a) IMO (b) ICAO (c) WIPO (d) ITU
Answer: (b)
Solution: IMO is a trap because it also deals with transport, but it is based in London; the International Civil Aviation Organization is headquartered in Montreal, so the answer is ICAO.
Source: ICAO official website (icao.int)

55. The World Trade Organization (WTO) replaced GATT in which year?
(a) 1991 (b) 1993 (c) 1997 (d) 1995
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1993 is a trap because the Uruguay Round concluded with the Marrakesh Agreement in 1994 and many confuse the signing year; the WTO officially came into existence on 1 January 1995, so the answer is 1995.
Source: WTO official website (wto.org)

56. The headquarters of the World Health Organization is in:
(a) New York (b) Geneva (c) Paris (d) Washington D.C.
Answer: (b)
Solution: New York is a trap since the UN headquarters is there; WHO, established in 1948, is headquartered in Geneva, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: WHO official website (who.int)

57. Which of the following countries is NOT a permanent member of the UN Security Council?
(a) France (b) Russia (c) United Kingdom (d) Germany
Answer: (d)
Solution: France is a trap because it is sometimes mistakenly thought to have given up its seat to the EU; the five permanent members are China, France, Russia, the UK and the USA, so the answer is Germany.
Source: UN Charter, Article 23

58. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) is headquartered in:
(a) Geneva (b) Washington D.C. (c) New York (d) London
Answer: (b)
Solution: New York is a trap because the UN and many financial bodies are associated with it; both the IMF and the World Bank are headquartered in Washington D.C., so the answer is Washington D.C.
Source: IMF official website (imf.org)

59. UNESCO, the UN Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, is headquartered in:
(a) Paris (b) Rome (c) Geneva (d) Vienna
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rome is a trap because FAO is headquartered there; UNESCO, founded in 1945, is based in Paris, so the answer is Paris.
Source: UNESCO official website (unesco.org)

60. The Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the UN is headquartered in:
(a) Geneva (b) Paris (c) Vienna (d) Rome
Answer: (d)
Solution: Paris is a trap since UNESCO is there and both were founded around 1945; FAO, established in 1945, is headquartered in Rome, so the answer is Rome.
Source: FAO official website (fao.org)

61. How many non-permanent members does the UN Security Council have?
(a) 5 (b) 15 (c) 10 (d) 20
Answer: (c)
Solution: 15 is a trap because it is the total membership of the Council; the UNSC has 5 permanent and 10 non-permanent members elected for two-year terms, so the answer is 10.
Source: UN Charter, Article 23

62. The headquarters of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) is in:
(a) Geneva (b) Vienna (c) Paris (d) The Hague
Answer: (b)
Solution: Geneva is a trap because many UN agencies cluster there; the IAEA, established in 1957, is headquartered in Vienna, so the answer is Vienna.
Source: IAEA official website (iaea.org)

63. Which organization is known as the "World Bank"?
(a) IBRD (b) IMF (c) ADB (d) WTO
Answer: (a)
Solution: IMF is a trap because it was created alongside the World Bank at Bretton Woods in 1944; the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development is the original World Bank institution, so the answer is IBRD.
Source: World Bank official website (worldbank.org)

64. The headquarters of the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC) is in:
(a) Riyadh (b) Vienna (c) Geneva (d) Baghdad
Answer: (b)
Solution: Riyadh is a trap because Saudi Arabia is OPEC's largest producer; OPEC, founded in Baghdad in 1960, has been headquartered in Vienna since 1965, so the answer is Vienna.
Source: OPEC official website (opec.org)

65. The International Criminal Court (ICC) is located in:
(a) The Hague (b) New York (c) Geneva (d) Brussels
Answer: (a)
Solution: Geneva is a trap as the seat of many international bodies; the ICC, established by the Rome Statute of 1998 and functioning since 2002, sits in The Hague, Netherlands, so the answer is The Hague.
Source: ICC official website (icc-cpi.int)

66. Which of the following is the oldest among these organizations?
(a) International Labour Organization (b) United Nations (c) World Health Organization (d) UNESCO
Answer: (a)
Solution: The United Nations is a trap because it feels like the parent body, but it dates to 1945; the ILO was founded in 1919 under the League of Nations, so the answer is International Labour Organization.
Source: ILO official website (ilo.org)

67. The headquarters of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) is in:
(a) London (b) Paris (c) Vienna (d) Geneva
Answer: (d)
Solution: London is a trap because IMO is headquartered there; the ITU, founded in 1865 and the oldest UN agency, is based in Geneva, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: ITU official website (itu.int)

68. The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is headquartered in:
(a) Bangkok (b) Tokyo (c) Singapore (d) Manila
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tokyo is a trap because Japan is a leading member and the AIIB confusion adds noise; the ADB, established in 1966, is headquartered in Manila, Philippines, so the answer is Manila.
Source: ADB official website (adb.org)

69. Which UN agency is responsible for refugees worldwide?
(a) UNICEF (b) WFP (c) UNHCR (d) UNDP
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNICEF is a trap because it also does humanitarian relief, but its mandate is children; the UN High Commissioner for Refugees handles refugee protection, so the answer is UNHCR.
Source: UNHCR official website (unhcr.org)

70. The World Meteorological Organization (WMO) is headquartered in:
(a) Geneva (b) Paris (c) Rome (d) Nairobi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nairobi is a trap because UNEP is headquartered there; the WMO, which became a UN specialized agency in 1950, is based in Geneva, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: WMO official website (wmo.int)

71. The headquarters of the International Maritime Organization (IMO) is in:
(a) Geneva (b) London (c) Montreal (d) The Hague
Answer: (b)
Solution: Montreal is a trap because ICAO, the aviation counterpart, is based there; the IMO is headquartered in London, so the answer is London.
Source: IMO official website (imo.org)

72. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) UNICEF – Paris (b) WFP – Geneva (c) UNDP – New York (d) UNIDO – Rome
Answer: (c)
Solution: The UNICEF–Paris pairing is a trap since UNESCO is in Paris, but UNICEF is in New York; UNDP is correctly headquartered in New York, so the answer is UNDP – New York.
Source: UNDP official website (undp.org)

73. The New Development Bank (NDB), established by BRICS countries, is headquartered in:
(a) Beijing (b) New Delhi (c) Shanghai (d) Moscow
Answer: (c)
Solution: Beijing is a trap because the AIIB is headquartered there; the NDB, set up in 2015, is headquartered in Shanghai, so the answer is Shanghai.
Source: NDB official website (ndb.int)

74. The term of a non-permanent member of the UN Security Council is:
(a) 1 year (b) 5 years (c) 3 years (d) 2 years
Answer: (d)
Solution: 5 years is a trap because ICJ judges serve nine years and some confuse UNSC terms with other bodies; non-permanent UNSC members serve two-year terms, so the answer is 2 years.
Source: UN Charter, Article 23

75. The headquarters of the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is in:
(a) Nairobi (b) Geneva (c) Vienna (d) Paris
Answer: (a)
Solution: Geneva is a trap because most UN agencies are based there; UNEP, established in 1972, is headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, so the answer is Nairobi.
Source: UNEP official website (unep.org)
