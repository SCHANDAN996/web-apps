# Key Facts & Figures — International Organizations

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| UN founded | 24 October 1945 (UN Charter entered into force); Charter signed 26 June 1945 at the San Francisco Conference (April–June 1945) | 26 June (signing) vs 24 Oct (entry into force) confusion | Date swap |
| UN Headquarters | New York, USA | Geneva/Vienna/Paris given as distractors | Similar names |
| UN official languages | 6 — English, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Arabic | Hindi often inserted as 7th language | Extra item |
| UN principal organs | 6 — GA, SC, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ, Secretariat | ICJ is the only organ NOT in New York (The Hague) | Location trap |
| UN Security Council | 15 members: 5 permanent (US, Russia, UK, France, China) + 10 non-permanent (2-yr term) | Germany/Japan/India shown as permanent members | False inclusion |
| Veto power | Only P5 hold veto; veto applies to substantive matters, not procedural | "GA members have veto" — false | Concept reversal |
| UNGA membership | 193 member states (South Sudan, 2011, is the 193rd) | 192/195 figures; Vatican & Palestine are observers, not members | Number trap |
| ICJ | The Hague, Netherlands; 15 judges, 9-year term | Confused with ICC (also The Hague) — ICJ settles state disputes, ICC tries individuals | Similar names |
| WHO | HQ Geneva; founded 7 April 1948 (World Health Day) | 1945/1950 dates; HQ shown as New York | Date/location swap |
| UNESCO | HQ Paris; 1945 | Confused with UNICEF (New York, 1946) | Similar names |
| UNICEF | HQ New York; 1946; Nobel Peace Prize 1965 | HQ shown as Geneva | Location trap |
| ILO | HQ Geneva; 1919 (oldest, League of Nations era); Nobel Peace Prize 1969; only tripartite UN agency | "ILO founded 1945" — false | Date trap |
| FAO | HQ Rome; 1945; motto "Fiat Panis" | HQ shown as Geneva/Paris | Location trap |
| IMF | HQ Washington D.C.; 1944 Bretton Woods; SDR basket | Confused with World Bank functions | Function swap |
| World Bank Group | HQ Washington D.C.; 1944; 5 institutions (IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID) | "World Bank = single body" — false | Structure trap |
| WTO | HQ Geneva; 1 January 1995; succeeded GATT (1947) | "WTO founded 1947" — GATT date used | Date swap |
| NATO | HQ Brussels; 1949 (Washington Treaty); Article 5 collective defence | HQ shown as Washington; founding shown as 1945 | Location/date trap |
| EU | HQ Brussels; Maastricht Treaty 1993; Eurozone ≠ EU (not all EU states use euro) | "All EU members use euro" — false | Overgeneralization |
| SAARC | HQ Kathmandu; 8 members; founded 8 December 1985, Dhaka | Myanmar/China shown as members; HQ shown as New Delhi | Membership trap |
| ASEAN | HQ Jakarta; 1967 Bangkok Declaration; 10 members | India shown as member (India is dialogue partner) | False inclusion |
| BRICS | Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa (2010); expanded 2024 (Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE) | Saudi Arabia membership status confusion | Recent-change trap |
| OPEC | HQ Vienna; 1960, Baghdad | HQ shown as Riyadh/Dubai | Location trap |
| Amnesty International | HQ London; 1961; Nobel Peace Prize 1977 | Shown as a UN body — it is an NGO | Category trap |
| Red Cross (ICRC) | HQ Geneva; 1863; three Nobel Peace Prizes | Confused with UN agency | Category trap |
| Commonwealth | HQ London; 56 members; mostly former British territories | "All former British colonies are members" — false (e.g., USA, Myanmar not members) | Overgeneralization |
| NAM | Founded 1961, Belgrade; 120 members; India a founder | HQ trap — NAM has NO permanent HQ/secretariat | Structure trap |
| G20 | Founded 1999; no permanent HQ; India hosted 2023 summit (New Delhi); African Union joined 2023 | "G20 HQ in New York" — false | Structure trap |
| UN Secretary-General | António Guterres (Portugal), since 2017 | Trygve Lie (first SG) shown as current | Person swap |
| First UN Secretary-General | Trygve Lie (Norway) | Dag Hammarskjöld shown as first | Person swap |
| India & UN | Founding member (1945, even before independence); among the largest contributors of peacekeeping troops historically | "India joined UN in 1947" — false | Date trap |
| Trusteeship Council | Suspended operations 1994 (Palau's independence) | Shown as fully active organ | Status trap |
| ECOSOC | 54 members, 3-year terms | Confused with SC's 15 members | Number trap |
| UN Charter | Chapter VII — enforcement action; Article 51 — self-defence | Article numbers swapped | Number trap |
| ADB | HQ Manila, Philippines; 1966 | HQ shown as Tokyo | Location trap |
| AIIB | HQ Beijing; 2016; India is a founding member | Confused with NDB (Shanghai) | Similar names |
| NDB (BRICS Bank) | HQ Shanghai; 2015 | HQ shown as New Delhi | Location trap |
| Interpol | HQ Lyon, France; 1923 | HQ shown as Paris/The Hague | Location trap |
| UNHCR | HQ Geneva; 1950; Nobel Peace Prizes 1954 & 1981 | Confused with UNRWA (Palestine refugees only) | Similar names |
| IAEA | HQ Vienna; 1957; Nobel Peace Prize 2005 | Shown as disarmament body — it promotes peaceful nuclear use + safeguards | Function trap |
| WIPO, WMO, ITU | All HQ Geneva | Geneva-cluster confusion with Rome (FAO) / Paris (UNESCO, OECD) | Location trap |
| OPCW | HQ The Hague; Nobel Peace Prize 2013 | Confused with IAEA | Similar names |
| SCO | HQ Beijing; 2001; India & Pakistan joined 2017 | "India is a founder" — false | Membership trap |
| BIMSTEC | HQ Dhaka; 7 members (Bay of Bengal) | Maldives/Pakistan shown as members | Membership trap |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Fact Area | Type | Time | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| HQ of organizations (UN, IMF, WTO, NATO, SAARC…) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes — pure recall, highest ROI |
| Founding years & places | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Membership numbers (UN 193, SC 15, SAARC 8, ASEAN 10) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Official languages of UN | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Nobel Peace Prize-winning organizations | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Heads of organizations (current) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ⚠️ Only if recently verified |
| Veto power & SC procedure | Analytical | 45 sec | ✅ Yes — statement questions frequent |
| UN Charter articles & chapters | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
| GATT vs WTO, League vs UN evolution | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
| ICJ vs ICC jurisdiction | Analytical | 45 sec | ✅ Yes — classic trap zone |
| Specialized agency vs UN organ classification | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
| Grouping membership logic (G20, BRICS, SCO expansion) | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
| Matching: organization ↔ HQ ↔ year (match-the-following) | Analytical | 45 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Chronological ordering of foundings | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
