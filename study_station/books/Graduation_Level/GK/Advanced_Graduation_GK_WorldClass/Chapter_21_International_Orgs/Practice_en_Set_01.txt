1. Where is the headquarters of the United Nations located?
(a) New York (b) Geneva (c) Paris (d) Vienna
Answer: (a)
Solution: Geneva is a tempting option because it hosts many UN offices and agencies, but the principal headquarters of the UN is in New York City, USA, established in 1945, so the answer is New York.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

2. How many permanent members does the UN Security Council have?
(a) 10 (b) 5 (c) 15 (d) 7
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Security Council has 15 members in total, which makes 15 a tempting wrong choice, but only 5 are permanent — the USA, UK, France, Russia and China — while 10 are non-permanent, so the answer is 5.

21. Consider the following statements about the UN Security Council:
1. It has 10 non-permanent members elected for two-year terms.
2. Each permanent member holds veto power over substantive resolutions.
3. Non-permanent members are elected by the Security Council itself.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — it sounds plausible, but non-permanent members are elected by the General Assembly, not by the Security Council; statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.

23. Arrange the following events in chronological order:
1. Establishment of the League of Nations
2. Establishment of the United Nations
3. Replacement of GATT by the WTO
4. Establishment of the IMF
(a) 1-2-4-3 (b) 1-2-3-4 (c) 2-1-4-3 (d) 1-4-2-3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing the UN (1945) before the IMF (1944) — the IMF was created at Bretton Woods in 1944, a year before the UN; the correct order is League of Nations (1920), IMF (1944), UN (1945), WTO (1995), so the answer is 1-4-2-3.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)
