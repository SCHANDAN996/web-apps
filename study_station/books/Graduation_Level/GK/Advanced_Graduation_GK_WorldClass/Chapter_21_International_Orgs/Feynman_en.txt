# 🧒 Feynman Technique: The IMF vs World Bank Confusion — Finally Solved

## The Concept That Trips Everyone Up

Ask any exam aspirant: *"What is the difference between the IMF and the World Bank?"* — and watch them freeze. Both were born at **Bretton Woods (1944)**, both are headquartered in **Washington, D.C.**, both are called the "Bretton Woods twins," and both lend money to countries. Examiners *love* this confusion — they swap the functions in options and catch thousands of students every year.

Let's fix it forever, the Feynman way.

---

## Explain It Like You're 12

Imagine your neighbourhood has **two kinds of helpers**:

**🏥 The Emergency Doctor (IMF)**
Your family suddenly can't pay its bills this month. You're about to default on everything. You rush to the emergency doctor. He gives you **quick, short-term medicine** (a loan) to survive the crisis — but with strict instructions: "Stop eating junk food, cut your expenses, exercise daily." (These are the famous IMF *conditions* — cut subsidies, devalue currency, reduce government spending.)

**🏗️ The House Builder (World Bank)**
Your family is fine day-to-day, but your house has no proper roof, no toilet, no road to the market. The house builder gives you **long-term money** to *build things* — schools, dams, highways, irrigation. No emergency here; this is about becoming stronger over 20–30 years.

**That's it. That's the whole difference:**

| | IMF (Emergency Doctor) | World Bank (House Builder) |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem it solves** | Balance of Payments crisis (country can't pay foreign bills) | Poverty & underdevelopment |
| **Loan type** | Short-term, crisis loans | Long-term, project loans |
| **Money comes from** | Member quotas (like a club fund) | Bonds sold in world markets + member contributions |
| **Famous condition** | "Structural adjustment" — strict economic reforms | Project must be completed as planned |
| **Head (traditionally)** | A European | An American |

---

## The Unforgettable Trick

> **"IMF = I Manage Fire 🔥 | World Bank = We Build 🏗️"**

- **I**MF → **I**nstant crisis → **fire-fighting** (short-term)
- **W**orld **B**ank → **W**e **B**uild → development (long-term)

Second trick for the leadership convention:
> **"Europe runs the Fund, America runs the Bank"** — the IMF Managing Director is traditionally European; the World Bank President is traditionally American.

---

## Where the Examiner Sets the Trap

1. **"The World Bank gives loans to fix Balance of Payments crises"** — ❌ WRONG. That's the IMF's job.
2. **"The IMF funds dam and road construction"** — ❌ WRONG. That's the World Bank.
3. **"Both were created at the San Francisco Conference"** — ❌ WRONG. Bretton Woods, 1944. San Francisco (1945) created the **UN Charter**.
4. **"The World Bank is a single institution"** — ❌ It's a **Group of 5** (IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID).
