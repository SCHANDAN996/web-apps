101. The headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO) is located in:
(a) New York (b) Vienna (c) Paris (d) Geneva
Answer: (d)
Solution: New York is tempting because many UN bodies are based there, but the ILO, founded in 1919, has its headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

102. How many permanent members does the UN Security Council have?

103. The World Trade Organization (WTO) came into existence on:
(a) 1 January 1991 (b) 1 January 1995 (c) 1 January 2000 (d) 1 January 1948
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1948 is tempting because GATT was signed in 1947 and came into force in 1948, but the WTO replaced GATT on 1 January 1995 after the Uruguay Round, so the answer is 1 January 1995.

121. Consider the following statements:
1. The UN Charter was signed in San Francisco.
2. The UN officially came into existence on 26 June 1945.
3. The UN headquarters is in New York.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — it is almost correct, but 26 June 1945 is when the Charter was signed, not when the UN came into existence (24 October 1945); statements 1 and 3 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.

124. Arrange the following organizations in chronological order of their establishment:
1. WTO
2. United Nations
3. League of Nations
4. IMF
(a) 4-3-2-1 (b) 3-2-4-1 (c) 3-4-2-1 (d) 2-3-4-1
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing the UN before the IMF — the League of Nations came first (1920), then the IMF was conceived at Bretton Woods (1944), then the UN (1945), and finally the WTO (1995), so the answer is 3-4-2-1.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Contemporary World Politics)

125. Consider the following statements:
1. India is a founding member of the United Nations.
2. India has been a permanent member of the UN Security Council.
3. India signed the UN Charter before its independence.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — India has served multiple times as a non-permanent member but has never been a permanent member; India was a founding member and signed the Charter in 1945, before independence in 1947, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.
