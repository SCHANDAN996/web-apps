126. Which of the following statements about the International Court of Justice (ICJ) is correct?
(a) It is headquartered in The Hague and has 15 judges elected for 9-year terms (b) It is headquartered in Geneva and has 15 judges elected for 9-year terms (c) It is headquartered in The Hague and has 9 judges elected for 15-year terms (d) It is headquartered in New York and its judges are appointed by the Security Council alone
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Geneva, which hosts many UN bodies and tempts candidates, but the ICJ is the only principal UN organ not located in New York; it sits at The Hague, Netherlands. It has 15 judges elected for 9-year terms by the General Assembly and Security Council voting independently, so the answer is The Hague with 15 judges and 9-year terms.
Source: ICJ official website (icj-cij.org)

127. Arrange the following UN Secretary-Generals in correct chronological order: 1. Trygve Lie 2. Dag Hammarskjöld 3. U Thant 4. Kurt Waldheim
(a) 1-2-3-4 (b) 2-1-3-4 (c) 1-3-2-4 (d) 2-1-4-3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is swapping the first two — Hammarskjöld is more famous (he died in a 1961 plane crash), so candidates assume he came first. Trygve Lie of Norway was the first Secretary-General (1946–52), followed by Hammarskjöld (1953–61), U Thant (1961–71) and Kurt Waldheim (1972–81), so the answer is 1-2-3-4.
Source: UN official website (un.org)

128. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) ILO — Geneva — 1919 (b) FAO — Rome — 1945 (c) UNESCO — Paris — 1945 (d) IMF — Geneva — 1944
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that the IMF was indeed created at Bretton Woods in 1944, so the whole pair feels right — but its headquarters is Washington, D.C., not Geneva. The other three pairs are correct, so the answer is the IMF — Geneva — 1944 pair.
Source: IMF official website (imf.org)

129. The UN Charter was signed on 26 June 1945 at San Francisco, but the United Nations officially came into existence on:
(a) 1 January 1946 (b) 26 June 1945 (c) 24 October 1945 (d) 10 December 1948
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 26 June 1945, the signing date, which is celebrated as UN Charter Day — but an organisation begins only when its charter is ratified. The UN came into being on 24 October 1945 after ratification by the five permanent members and a majority of other signatories, and this date is celebrated as UN Day, so the answer is 24 October 1945.
Source: UN official website (un.org)

130. Which of the following is NOT a principal organ of the United Nations?
(a) Trusteeship Council (b) United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) (c) International Court of Justice (d) Economic and Social Council
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that UNDP is a very well-known UN body, so candidates hesitate to mark it. The six principal organs are the General Assembly, Security Council, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ and Secretariat; UNDP is a fund and programme, not a principal organ, so the answer is UNDP.
Source: UN Charter, Chapter III

131. The headquarters of the World Trade Organization (WTO), which replaced GATT on 1 January 1995, is located at:
(a) Geneva (b) Washington, D.C. (c) New York (d) Paris
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Washington, D.C., because candidates confuse the WTO with the IMF and World Bank, both of which are in Washington. The WTO, successor to GATT (1947), is headquartered at Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: WTO official website (wto.org)

132. Which country is NOT a permanent member of the UN Security Council?
(a) United Kingdom (b) Germany (c) France (d) Russia
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that Germany is a major economy and a leading G4 candidate (with India, Japan and Brazil) for permanent membership, which makes it feel like a member. The five permanent members with veto power are the USA, UK, France, Russia and China, so the answer is Germany.
Source: UN Charter, Article 23

133. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank were both conceived at which conference?
(a) Bretton Woods Conference, 1944 (b) Yalta Conference, 1945 (c) San Francisco Conference, 1945 (d) Dumbarton Oaks Conference, 1944
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the San Francisco Conference, strongly associated with the UN's founding in 1945 — but that conference produced the UN Charter, not the financial institutions. The IMF and World Bank were designed at the Bretton Woods Conference (New Hampshire, USA) in July 1944, so the answer is Bretton Woods, 1944.
Source: IMF official website (imf.org)

134. Which of the following statements about the UN General Assembly is correct?
(a) Its resolutions on substantive matters are legally binding on all members (b) Each member state has voting weight proportional to its population (c) Each member state has one vote, and important questions require a two-thirds majority (d) It can impose economic sanctions on member states on its own
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the first statement — candidates overestimate the General Assembly because it is the UN's main deliberative body; in reality its resolutions are recommendations, not binding law. The Assembly follows the one-nation-one-vote principle, with important questions needing a two-thirds majority, so the answer is the one-vote and two-thirds-majority statement.
Source: UN Charter, Articles 9–18

135. The headquarters of the International Criminal Court (ICC), established by the Rome Statute of 1998, is at:
(a) Geneva (b) Vienna (c) Brussels (d) The Hague
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Geneva, the default guess for any international legal body. The ICC, which began functioning in 2002 after the Rome Statute entered into force, is seated at The Hague, Netherlands — the same city as the ICJ, though the two courts are entirely separate, so the answer is The Hague.
Source: ICC official website (icc-cpi.int)

136. Which of the following countries is NOT a member of the G20?
(a) Argentina (b) Saudi Arabia (c) South Africa (d) Spain
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that Spain is a large European economy and attends G20 summits regularly as a permanent guest, which makes it feel like a member. The G20 comprises 19 countries plus the European Union and the African Union; Spain is only a permanent invitee, so the answer is Spain.
Source: G20 official website (g20.org)

137. Amnesty International, which won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1977, is headquartered in:
(a) London (b) Geneva (c) New York (d) Paris
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Geneva, since most human-rights bodies such as the UN Human Rights Council are based there. Amnesty International, founded in 1961 by Peter Benenson, is a non-governmental organisation headquartered in London, so the answer is London.
Source: Amnesty International official website

138. Which of the following pairs of organisation and founding year is correctly matched?
(a) Warsaw Pact — 1949 (b) SEATO — 1949 (c) OPEC — 1949 (d) NATO — 1949
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the year 1949 appearing in every option, forcing recall of exact years. NATO was founded by the Washington Treaty on 4 April 1949, while SEATO came in 1954, the Warsaw Pact in 1955 and OPEC in 1960, so the answer is NATO — 1949.
Source: NATO official website (nato.int)

139. The headquarters of the Asian Development Bank (ADB), established in 1966, is located at:
(a) Tokyo (b) Bangkok (c) Singapore (d) Manila
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Tokyo, because Japan is the ADB's largest shareholder alongside the USA and every ADB president has been Japanese. The bank's headquarters, however, is in Manila, Philippines, so the answer is Manila.
Source: ADB official website (adb.org)

140. Which of the following statements about the World Health Organization (WHO) is correct?
(a) It was founded in 1945 and is headquartered in New York (b) It was founded on 7 April 1948 and is headquartered in Geneva (c) It was founded in 1950 and is headquartered in Paris (d) It is a principal organ of the United Nations
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the 1945–New York combination, which borrows the UN's own founding year and city. The WHO is a specialised agency, not a principal organ; its constitution came into force on 7 April 1948 — now World Health Day — and it is headquartered in Geneva, so the answer is the 7 April 1948 and Geneva statement.
Source: WHO official website (who.int)

141. The New Development Bank (NDB), established by the BRICS countries, is headquartered at:
(a) Shanghai (b) Beijing (c) New Delhi (d) Moscow
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Beijing, since China hosts the bank and candidates confuse it with the AIIB, which is indeed in Beijing. The NDB, agreed at the 2012 BRICS summit and operational since 2015, is headquartered in Shanghai, so the answer is Shanghai.
Source: NDB official website (ndb.int)

142. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of establishment?
(a) UN — IMF — WTO — NATO (b) UN — NATO — IMF — WTO (c) IMF — UN — NATO — WTO (d) IMF — NATO — UN — WTO
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing the UN first because it feels like the oldest post-war body; in fact the IMF and World Bank were created at Bretton Woods in 1944, before the UN in 1945. The correct sequence is IMF (1944), UN (1945), NATO (1949), WTO (1995), so the answer is IMF — UN — NATO — WTO.
Source: Official websites of IMF, UN, NATO and WTO

143. The headquarters of the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC), founded in Baghdad in 1960, is located at:
(a) Baghdad (b) Vienna (c) Riyadh (d) Geneva
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Baghdad, the founding city named in the question itself — a classic misdirection. OPEC was founded in Baghdad in September 1960 but its secretariat has been in Vienna, Austria, since 1965, so the answer is Vienna.
Source: OPEC official website (opec.org)

144. Which of the following statements about the veto power in the UN Security Council is correct?
(a) A negative vote by a permanent member on a substantive (non-procedural) resolution defeats it, even if 9 or more members vote in favour (b) Abstention by a permanent member counts as a veto (c) A veto can be exercised by any of the 15 members of the Security Council (d) The General Assembly can override a veto by a simple majority
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the abstention statement — candidates assume any non-yes vote by a permanent member blocks a resolution; in practice, abstention does not count as a veto. A substantive resolution needs 9 of 15 votes including the concurring votes of all five permanent members, so one permanent member's negative vote defeats it, and the answer is the negative-vote statement.
Source: UN Charter, Article 27

145. The headquarters of the International Labour Organization (ILO), the only tripartite UN agency and winner of the 1969 Nobel Peace Prize, is at:
(a) Rome (b) Geneva (c) Paris (d) Vienna
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Paris, because the ILO was created in 1919 by the Treaty of Versailles, which is associated with Paris. The ILO — founded in 1919 and the first specialised agency of the UN — is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: ILO official website (ilo.org)

146. Which of the following organisations has its headquarters in Washington, D.C.?
(a) World Trade Organization (b) International Court of Justice (c) World Bank (d) UNESCO
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the WTO, which candidates lump with the World Bank and IMF as economic bodies in Washington; the WTO is actually in Geneva. The World Bank (IBRD, 1944) and the IMF are both headquartered in Washington, D.C., while the ICJ is at The Hague and UNESCO in Paris, so the answer is the World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website (worldbank.org)

147. The Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) was founded in 1967 by the Bangkok Declaration. Its secretariat is located at:
(a) Bangkok (b) Kuala Lumpur (c) Singapore (d) Jakarta
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Bangkok, the founding city named in the question — the same misdirection used with OPEC. ASEAN's five founding members were Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand, and its secretariat is in Jakarta, Indonesia, so the answer is Jakarta.
Source: ASEAN official website (asean.org)

148. Which of the following statements about the Commonwealth of Nations is correct?
(a) All members must accept the British monarch as their head of state (b) Membership is open only to countries that were British colonies (c) It is a voluntary association of 56 independent countries, most of which were formerly under British rule (d) Its headquarters is at the United Nations, New York
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the first statement — candidates assume every Commonwealth member has the British monarch as head of state; in fact most members are republics like India, and only the Commonwealth realms share the monarch. The Commonwealth is a voluntary association of 56 countries headquartered at Marlborough House, London, and even non-former-colonies like Mozambique and Rwanda have joined, so the answer is the voluntary-association statement.
Source: Commonwealth official website (thecommonwealth.org)

149. The headquarters of the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), a two-time Nobel Peace Prize winner (1954, 1981), is at:
(a) New York (b) Vienna (c) Geneva (d) Rome
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is New York, since the UN's main headquarters is there and UNHCR is a UN programme. UNHCR was established in 1950 by the UN General Assembly and is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, so the answer is Geneva.
Source: UNHCR official website (unhcr.org)

150. Which of the following pairs of organisation and headquarters is NOT correctly matched?
(a) INTERPOL — Lyon (b) World Economic Forum — Davos (c) Red Cross (ICRC) — Geneva (d) International Olympic Committee — Lausanne
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is Davos, because the World Economic Forum's famous annual meeting is held there, so candidates accept the pair as correct. The WEF is actually headquartered at Cologny in the canton of Geneva, Switzerland — Davos is only its meeting venue — while INTERPOL (Lyon, France), the IOC (Lausanne) and the ICRC (Geneva) are exact matches, so the answer is the World Economic Forum — Davos pair.
Source: Official websites of INTERPOL, IOC, WEF and ICRC
