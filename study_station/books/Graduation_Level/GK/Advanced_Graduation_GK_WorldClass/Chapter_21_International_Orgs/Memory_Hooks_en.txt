# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — International Organizations

---

**Hook 1 — The Big Five of the UN Security Council (P5)**
> **"F-R-U-U-C" — "FruUCy juice is permanent!"**
> **F**rance, **R**ussia, **U**K, **U**SA, **C**hina — the five permanent members with veto power.
> Trap alert: The order in options is often shuffled to hide a fake member like Germany or Japan. If it's not in "FruUCy," it's not permanent.

---

**Hook 2 — UN's Six Principal Organs**
> **"GA-SC-ECOSOC-TC-ICJ-S" → "Grandpa Sam Eats Tasty Ice-cream Sandwiches"**
> **G**eneral **A**ssembly, **S**ecurity **C**ouncil, **EC**onomic and **S**ocial **C**ouncil, **T**rusteeship **C**ouncil, **I**nternational **C**ourt of **J**ustice, **S**ecretariat.
> Remember: Trusteeship Council is the "sleeping organ" — suspended since 1994 after Palau's independence.

---

**Hook 3 — UN Headquarters Cities**
> **"New York rules, Geneva heals, Vienna drugs, Nairobi greens, Hague judges."**
> New York = UN HQ | Geneva = WHO, ILO, UNHCR | Vienna = IAEA, UNODC | Nairobi = UNEP, UN-Habitat | The Hague = ICJ, ICC.
> Examiner's trap: ICJ is in **The Hague (Netherlands)** — the ONLY principal organ NOT in New York.

---

**Hook 4 — Founding Years That Get Swapped**
> **"45 UN, 44 Bretton, 57 Rome, 95 Trade."**
> UN = 1945 | IMF & World Bank = 1944 (Bretton Woods) | EU roots (Treaty of Rome) = 1957 | WTO = 1995.
> Trap: WTO replaced GATT (1947) — examiners love asking "WTO established?" and offering 1947 as bait.

---

**Hook 5 — NATO vs Warsaw Pact**
> **"NATO 49, Warsaw 55 — the reply came 6 years late."**
> NATO = 1949 (Washington), Warsaw Pact = 1955 (dissolved 1991). NATO still exists; Warsaw Pact is history.

---

**Hook 6 — SAARC's Eight Members**
> **"A Big Indian Man Never Says Bad Politics"**
> **A**fghanistan, **B**angladesh, **I**ndia, **M**aldives, **N**epal, **S**ri Lanka, **B**hutan, **P**akistan.
> HQ: Kathmandu, Nepal. Founded: 1985 (Dhaka). Trap: Myanmar is NOT in SAARC — it's in ASEAN.

---

**Hook 7 — ASEAN's Ten Members**
> **"B-C-I-L-M-P-S-T-V-M" → "Big Cats In Laos Make People Sing; Tigers Visit Myanmar"**
> **B**runei, **C**ambodia, **I**ndonesia, **L**aos, **M**alaysia, **P**hilippines, **S**ingapore, **T**hailand, **V**ietnam, **M**yanmar — each of the 10 members appears exactly once.
> HQ: Jakarta, Indonesia. Founded: 1967 (Bangkok Declaration). Trap: HQ is Jakarta, but founding city is Bangkok!

---

**Hook 8 — BRICS Expansion**
> **"B-R-I-C-S + 4 new guests (2024): E-U-E-I"**
> Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa + Egypt, UAE, Ethiopia, Iran (joined 2024).
> Trick: "BRICS" coined by Goldman Sachs economist Jim O'Neill (2001); South Africa joined in 2010 — the "S" came last.

---

**Hook 9 — OPEC's Home**
> **"Oil sleeps in Vienna."**
> OPEC HQ = Vienna, Austria (since 1965). Founded 1960 in Baghdad by 5: Iran, Iraq, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Venezuela.
> Trap: Venezuela is the only founding member from the Americas; Russia is NOT an OPEC member (it's OPEC+).

---

**Hook 10 — World Bank's Five Institutions**
> **"I-D-I-M-I" → "IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID — India's Development Includes Many Institutions"**
> **IBRD**, **IDA**, **IFC**, **MIGA**, **ICSID** — the five institutions of the World Bank Group.
> Simpler version: "IBRD lends to middle-income countries, IDA lends interest-free to the poorest — IDA = 'Interest-free Donation Arm'."

---

**Hook 11 — IMF vs World Bank (The Classic Confusion)**
> **"IMF = International Monetary Firefighter (puts out currency crises); World Bank = Builder (long-term development)."**
> Both born 1944 at Bretton Woods, both in Washington DC. IMF gives short-term bailout loans; World Bank funds dams, roads, schools.
> Trap: IMF does NOT fund projects; World Bank does NOT handle exchange-rate crises.

---

**Hook 12 — UN Secretary-Generals Sequence (Recent)**
> **"Kofi → Ban → António: 'Kofi Banned Antonio' — but Antonio won!"**
> Kofi Annan (Ghana, 1997–2006) → Ban Ki-moon (South Korea, 2007–2016) → António Guterres (Portugal, 2017–present).
> Trap: Guterres is from Portugal (Europe), breaking the informal regional-rotation expectation examiners test.

---

**Hook 13 — India's Group Memberships**
> **"India is in G20, BRICS, SCO, QUAD — but NOT in UNSC (permanent), NOT in ASEAN, NOT in OPEC, NOT in NATO."**
> Chant it: "G-B-S-Q yes; U-A-O-N no!"
> This single line kills most "Which of the following is India a member of?" traps.

---

**Hook 14 — Amnesty, Red Cross & the Nobel Peace Club**
> **"ICRC won the Peace Prize THREE times (1917, 1944, 1963) — the only hat-trick in Nobel history."**
> ICRC founded 1863, Geneva. Amnesty International founded 1961, London (Nobel 1977). UNHCR won twice (1954, 1981).

---

**Hook 15 — The "City Swap" Master Trick**
> **"Paris = UNESCO & OECD; Rome = FAO; Geneva = WHO/ILO/WTO; Washington = IMF/World Bank."**
> Memory image: "In Paris they teach culture (UNESCO), in Rome they eat (FAO), in Geneva they heal and trade (WHO/WTO), in Washington they count money (IMF/WB)."
> Trap: WTO is in Geneva, NOT New York or Washington.

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

**Tip 1 — The "One Unknown Founder" Rule:**
If a question asks to match 4–5 organizations with their founding years or founders and you are unsure of even ONE pair, skip immediately. These match-the-following questions are designed so that one wrong pair flips the answer. Come back only if time remains — never guess between two plausible pairs.

**Tip 2 — The "Current Officeholder" Trap:**
Questions asking for the *current* Secretary-General / Director-General / President of a body are time-sensitive. If the exam is more than a year old relative to your preparation, or you haven't revised officeholders in the last month, skip. A stale memory (e.g., remembering Ban Ki-moon instead of Guterres) is worse than no attempt, especially with negative marking.

**Tip 3 — The "Member-Count" Bluff:**
If asked "How many members does X have?" and the options are clustered (e.g., 27, 28, 30, 32), skip unless you are certain. Examiners deliberately place the pre-expansion and post-expansion numbers side by side (e.g., EU 27 vs 28 pre-Brexit). A vague "around 30" feeling is a guaranteed negative mark — skip and bank your time for factual questions you own.
