1. A person standing in a moving bus falls forward when the bus suddenly stops. This is due to:
(a) Inertia of rest (b) Inertia of motion (c) Inertia of direction (d) Gravitational force
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting choice is inertia of rest, but the body was moving with the bus, so its upper part continues moving forward when the lower part stops with the bus. This is inertia of motion, so the answer is inertia of motion.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Force and Laws of Motion)

2. It is easier to roll a barrel than to pull it along the road because:
(a) Rolling friction is greater than sliding friction (b) The surface area increases while rolling (c) The barrel has less weight when rolling (d) Rolling friction is much smaller than sliding friction
Answer: (d)
Solution: Weight does not change while rolling, so the third option is a trap. Rolling friction is the smallest among static, sliding and rolling friction, which makes rolling easier, so the answer is rolling friction is much smaller than sliding friction.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science (Friction)

3. A sudden fall in barometer reading indicates:
(a) Calm weather (b) Stormy weather (c) Dry weather (d) Cold wave
Answer: (b)
Solution: A slow, steady fall suggests rain, but a sudden sharp fall means a rapid drop in atmospheric pressure associated with storms. Calm weather is linked with a steady high reading, so the answer is stormy weather.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Mechanical Properties of Fluids)

4. An iron needle sinks in water but an iron ship floats. This is explained by:
(a) Pascal's law (b) Archimedes' principle (c) Bernoulli's theorem (d) Surface tension
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bernoulli's theorem, a tempting option, deals with moving fluids, not floating bodies. The ship's hollow shape displaces water equal to its weight, giving enough buoyant force as per Archimedes' principle, so the answer is Archimedes' principle.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Gravitation)

5. The sky appears blue because of:
(a) Scattering of light (b) Refraction of light (c) Reflection of light (d) Dispersion of light
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dispersion, the tempting wrong option, splits white light into colours as in a rainbow, not the blue sky. Shorter blue wavelengths are scattered most by air molecules (Rayleigh scattering), so the answer is scattering of light.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Human Eye and the Colourful World)

6. While climbing a hill, a bent forward posture helps a person because:
(a) It reduces body weight (b) It keeps the line of centre of gravity within the base of support (c) It increases friction (d) It reduces air resistance
Answer: (b)
Solution: Body weight cannot change by bending, so the first option is a trap. Bending forward shifts the vertical line through the centre of gravity over the feet, maintaining stability, so the answer is it keeps the line of centre of gravity within the base of support.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Systems of Particles and Rotational Motion)

7. A pressure cooker cooks food faster because:
(a) Increased pressure raises the boiling point of water (b) Increased pressure lowers the boiling point of water (c) Steam absorbs more heat (d) The metal conducts heat faster
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first option reverses the relationship and is a common trap. Higher pressure inside raises water's boiling point above 100°C, so food cooks at a higher temperature, so the answer is increased pressure raises the boiling point of water.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Thermodynamics)

8. The colour of a star indicates its:
(a) Distance (b) Size (c) Age (d) Temperature
Answer: (d)
Solution: Distance affects brightness, not colour, so the first option is a trap. By Wien's displacement law, hotter stars emit shorter wavelengths (blue) and cooler stars appear red, so the answer is temperature.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Kinetic Theory / Thermal Radiation)

9. A rainbow is formed due to:
(a) Only refraction of sunlight (b) Only total internal reflection (c) Scattering of sunlight by dust (d) Refraction, internal reflection and dispersion of sunlight in water droplets
Answer: (d)
Solution: Options mentioning only one phenomenon are incomplete traps. A rainbow needs dispersion plus refraction at entry and exit and internal reflection inside raindrops, so the answer is refraction, internal reflection and dispersion of sunlight in water droplets.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Human Eye and the Colourful World)

10. When a running car suddenly takes a turn, passengers are thrown outward. This is an example of:
(a) Centrifugal tendency due to inertia (b) Centripetal force (c) Gravitational pull (d) Frictional force
Answer: (a)
Solution: Centripetal force acts inward on the car, not outward on passengers, so the first option is a trap. Passengers' bodies tend to continue in a straight line due to inertia, appearing to move outward, so the answer is centrifugal tendency due to inertia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Laws of Motion)

11. The handle of a pressure cooker or pan is made of plastic or wood because these materials are:
(a) Good conductors of heat (b) Bad conductors of heat (c) Good radiators of heat (d) Cheap and light
Answer: (b)
Solution: Good conductors like metals would burn the hand, so the first option is a trap. Plastic and wood are insulators, preventing heat from reaching the hand, so the answer is bad conductors of heat.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science (Heat)

12. A black surface is a:
(a) Good absorber and good radiator of heat (b) Poor absorber but good radiator of heat (c) Good absorber but poor radiator of heat (d) Poor absorber and poor radiator of heat
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first option is a tempting half-truth; good absorbers are also good emitters (Kirchhoff's law of radiation). A black body both absorbs and radiates heat best, so the answer is good absorber and good radiator of heat.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Thermal Properties of Matter)

13. The working of a hydraulic brake in vehicles is based on:
(a) Archimedes' principle (b) Pascal's law (c) Bernoulli's principle (d) Stoke's law
Answer: (b)
Solution: Archimedes' principle concerns buoyancy, not pressure transmission, so it is a trap here. Pascal's law states pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted equally in all directions, which multiplies braking force, so the answer is Pascal's law.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Mechanical Properties of Fluids)

14. An aeroplane flies because of the lift produced by:
(a) Newton's third law only (b) Archimedes' principle (c) Bernoulli's principle — pressure difference over the wings (d) Surface tension of air
Answer: (c)
Solution: Buoyancy from Archimedes' principle is far too small to lift a plane, so that option is a trap. Faster airflow over the curved upper wing surface lowers pressure there, creating lift, so the answer is Bernoulli's principle — pressure difference over the wings.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Mechanical Properties of Fluids)

15. In winter, water pipes sometimes burst because:
(a) Water contracts on freezing (b) Metal expands on cooling (c) Water expands on freezing (d) Ice is heavier than water
Answer: (c)
Solution: The first option is a trap since most liquids contract, but water is anomalous. Water expands below 4°C and on freezing, exerting pressure that bursts pipes, so the answer is water expands on freezing.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Thermal Properties of Matter)

16. A convex mirror is used as a rear-view mirror in vehicles because:
(a) It gives a magnified image (b) It converges light (c) It gives a real image (d) It gives an erect image with a wider field of view
Answer: (d)
Solution: A convex mirror always gives a diminished, not magnified, image, so the first option is a trap. It forms erect, virtual images and covers a wide field of view, so the answer is it gives an erect image with a wider field of view.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Light — Reflection and Refraction)

17. The twinkling of stars is caused by:
(a) Atmospheric refraction of starlight (b) Reflection of light (c) Dispersion of light (d) Total internal reflection
Answer: (a)
Solution: Total internal reflection causes mirages and sparkling diamonds, not twinkling, so it is a trap. Continuously changing air density refracts starlight variably, making stars appear to twinkle, so the answer is atmospheric refraction of starlight.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Human Eye and the Colourful World)

18. A fuse wire used in household circuits is made of:
(a) Copper (b) Tungsten (c) An alloy of tin and lead with low melting point (d) Nichrome
Answer: (c)
Solution: Tungsten, used in bulb filaments, has a very high melting point — the opposite of what a fuse needs, so it is a trap. A fuse must melt quickly on excess current, requiring a low-melting-point tin-lead alloy, so the answer is an alloy of tin and lead with low melting point.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Electricity)

19. Sound travels fastest in:
(a) Steel (b) Water (c) Air (d) Vacuum
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vacuum is a trap because sound, being a mechanical wave, cannot travel through it at all. Sound speed increases with the medium's elasticity, being greatest in solids like steel, so the answer is steel.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Sound)

20. The ozone layer protects life on Earth by absorbing:
(a) Infrared radiation (b) Radio waves (c) Visible light (d) Ultraviolet radiation
Answer: (d)
Solution: Infrared absorption relates to greenhouse gases, not ozone, so the first option is a trap. The stratospheric ozone layer absorbs harmful ultraviolet radiation from the Sun, so the answer is ultraviolet radiation.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Our Environment)

21. Consider the following statements about a geostationary satellite:
1. Its orbital period is 24 hours.
2. It revolves from west to east above the equator.
3. It can be placed over any latitude.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — a geostationary satellite must orbit directly above the equator (latitude zero), not any latitude. Its period matches Earth's rotation and it moves west to east, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics (Gravitation)

22. A piece of ice is floating in a glass of water. When the ice melts completely, the water level will:
(a) Rise (b) Remain unchanged (c) Fall (d) First rise then fall
Answer: (b)
Solution: The rise option is a trap based on assuming melting adds extra volume. Floating ice displaces water equal to its own weight, and meltwater occupies exactly that displaced volume, so the answer is remain unchanged.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Gravitation)

23. Consider the following statements:
1. A concave lens is used to correct myopia (short-sightedness).
2. A convex lens is used to correct hypermetropia (long-sightedness).
3. A cylindrical lens is used to correct astigmatism.
Which of the above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: A common trap is swapping the lenses for myopia and hypermetropia; myopia needs a diverging concave lens and hypermetropia a converging convex lens, and astigmatism needs a cylindrical lens. All three statements are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Human Eye and the Colourful World)

24. A man weighs 60 kg on Earth. His weight on the Moon, where gravity is about one-sixth of Earth's, will be approximately:
(a) 60 kg (b) 360 kg (c) 10 kg (d) 6 kg
Answer: (c)
Solution: The first option is a trap confusing mass with weight; mass stays 60 kg everywhere, but weight changes with gravity. One-sixth of 60 kg-weight is about 10 kg-weight, so the answer is 10 kg.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Gravitation)

25. Consider the following statements about total internal reflection:
1. Light must travel from a denser to a rarer medium.
2. The angle of incidence must be greater than the critical angle.
3. It explains the sparkling of diamonds.
Which of the above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is reversing the first condition — light must go from denser to rarer medium, not the reverse. Both conditions are required, and diamonds sparkle due to total internal reflection at their small critical angle, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics (Ray Optics and Optical Instruments)
