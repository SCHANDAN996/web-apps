126. A ray of light passes from glass (refractive index 1.5) into water (refractive index 1.33). Which statement is correct?
(a) The ray bends towards the normal and its speed decreases (b) The ray bends away from the normal and its speed decreases (c) The ray bends towards the normal and its speed increases (d) The ray bends away from the normal and its speed increases
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice assumes light always bends towards the normal when entering water, but that rule applies only when entering a denser medium from air. Here light goes from optically denser glass to rarer water, so it bends away from the normal, and since speed is inversely proportional to refractive index, its speed increases, so the answer is that the ray bends away from the normal and its speed increases.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Light – Reflection and Refraction)

127. A convex lens of focal length 20 cm forms a real image of an object placed 30 cm from it. The image distance is:
(a) 60 cm (b) 12 cm (c) 50 cm (d) 40 cm
Answer: (a)
Solution: A common trap is to add object distance and focal length, which has no physical basis. Using the lens formula 1/f = 1/v − 1/u with u = −30 cm and f = +20 cm: 1/v = 1/20 − 1/30 = 1/60, giving v = 60 cm, so the answer is 60 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Light – Reflection and Refraction)

128. Two resistors of 6 Ω and 3 Ω are connected in parallel across a 12 V battery. The total current drawn from the battery is:
(a) 6 A (b) 4 A (c) 1.33 A (d) 2 A
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer comes from treating the resistors as if in series (9 Ω, giving 1.33 A). In parallel, equivalent resistance is (6 × 3)/(6 + 3) = 2 Ω, so current = 12/2 = 6 A, so the answer is 6 A.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Electricity)

129. An electric bulb rated 100 W at 220 V is operated at 110 V. Assuming resistance is constant, the power consumed is:
(a) 50 W (b) 25 W (c) 100 W (d) 200 W
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer halves the power to 50 W, but power varies with the square of voltage since P = V²/R. Halving the voltage quarters the power: 100/4 = 25 W, so the answer is 25 W.

130. A body is thrown vertically upward with velocity 20 m/s. Taking g = 10 m/s², the maximum height reached is:
(a) 10 m (b) 40 m (c) 20 m (d) 30 m
Answer: (c)
Solution: A frequent trap is using h = v²/g instead of h = v²/2g, which doubles the result to 40 m. Correctly, h = (20)²/(2 × 10) = 400/20 = 20 m, so the answer is 20 m.

131. Which of the following correctly matches the phenomenon with its cause?
(a) Blue colour of the sky — dispersion of light (b) Rainbow formation — scattering of light (c) Twinkling of stars — atmospheric refraction (d) Red colour of the Sun at sunset — total internal reflection
Answer: (c)
Solution: This question uses the almost-correct-statement trap: the blue sky is due to scattering (not dispersion), the rainbow is due to dispersion and internal reflection (not scattering), and the red sunset is due to scattering (not total internal reflection). Only the twinkling of stars, caused by atmospheric refraction of starlight through varying air layers, is correctly matched, so the answer is twinkling of stars — atmospheric refraction.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Human Eye and the Colourful World)

132. A concave mirror of focal length 15 cm has an object placed at 10 cm from it. The image formed is:
(a) Virtual, erect and magnified (b) Real, inverted and magnified (c) Real, erect and diminished (d) Virtual, inverted and diminished
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to assume a concave mirror always forms a real, inverted image. When the object lies between the pole and the focus, the concave mirror forms a virtual, erect and magnified image behind the mirror — the principle of shaving and make-up mirrors, so the answer is virtual, erect and magnified.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Light – Reflection and Refraction)

133. The commercial unit of electrical energy, one kilowatt-hour, is equal to:
(a) 3.6 × 10⁶ J (b) 3.6 × 10⁵ J (c) 3.6 × 10⁴ J (d) 3.6 × 10⁷ J
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option confuses the power of ten. One kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3,600,000 J = 3.6 × 10⁶ J, so the answer is 3.6 × 10⁶ J.

134. A current-carrying straight conductor is placed in a uniform magnetic field. It experiences maximum force when the angle between the conductor and the field is:
(a) 0° (b) 90° (c) 45° (d) 180°
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to think the force is maximum when the conductor is aligned with the field; in fact F = BIL sinθ, which is zero at 0° and 180° and maximum at 90°, so the answer is 90°.

135. An object of mass 2 kg moving at 3 m/s collides with and sticks to a stationary object of mass 1 kg. The combined velocity after collision is:
(a) 1 m/s (b) 1.5 m/s (c) 3 m/s (d) 2 m/s
Answer: (d)
Solution: A common error is to average the velocities or divide momentum by only one mass. By conservation of momentum: (2 × 3) = (2 + 1) × v, giving v = 6/3 = 2 m/s, so the answer is 2 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Force and Laws of Motion)

136. Which mirror is used as a rear-view mirror in vehicles and why?
(a) Concave mirror, because it gives a magnified image (b) Convex mirror, because it gives an erect image and a wider field of view (c) Plane mirror, because it gives an image of the same size (d) Concave mirror, because it gives a wider field of view
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option credits the concave mirror with a wide field of view, but a concave mirror actually has a narrow field and forms inverted images of distant objects. A convex mirror always forms an erect, diminished image and covers a wider field of view, so the answer is convex mirror, because it gives an erect image and a wider field of view.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Light – Reflection and Refraction)

137. The power of a lens is +2.5 D. Its focal length and nature are:
(a) 40 cm, convex (b) 25 cm, concave (c) 40 cm, concave (d) 2.5 m, convex
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to invert the power value directly (1/2.5 m misread as 25 cm) or to miss that positive power means a converging lens. Focal length f = 1/P = 1/2.5 m = 0.4 m = 40 cm, and positive power indicates a convex lens, so the answer is 40 cm, convex.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Human Eye and the Colourful World)

138. A fuse wire is characterised by:
(a) High melting point and high resistance (b) High melting point and low resistance (c) Low melting point and low resistance (d) Low melting point and high resistance
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option pairs low melting point with low resistance, but a fuse must offer appreciable resistance so that excessive current heats it quickly (H = I²Rt) and melts it to break the circuit. Hence a fuse wire has a low melting point and relatively high resistance, so the answer is low melting point and high resistance.

139. Sound waves cannot travel through:
(a) Vacuum (b) Water (c) Air (d) Steel
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to pick air, thinking sound needs a dense medium; in fact sound travels in all material media, fastest in solids like steel. Being a mechanical wave, sound requires a material medium and cannot propagate through a vacuum, so the answer is vacuum.

140. An electric heater draws 5 A from a 220 V supply for 2 hours. The energy consumed is:
(a) 1.1 kWh (b) 2.2 kWh (c) 4.4 kWh (d) 0.55 kWh
Answer: (b)
Solution: A common slip is to forget the time factor or misplace the decimal. Power = V × I = 220 × 5 = 1100 W = 1.1 kW; energy = 1.1 kW × 2 h = 2.2 kWh, so the answer is 2.2 kWh.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Electricity)

141. The phenomenon of total internal reflection occurs when light travels:
(a) From a rarer to a denser medium at any angle of incidence (b) From a denser to a rarer medium at an angle of incidence less than the critical angle (c) From a denser to a rarer medium at an angle of incidence greater than the critical angle (d) From a rarer to a denser medium at an angle greater than the critical angle
Answer: (c)
Solution: The almost-correct trap option reverses the medium direction, but total internal reflection is impossible when going from rarer to denser media. It requires travel from a denser to a rarer medium with the angle of incidence exceeding the critical angle — the principle behind optical fibres and the brilliance of diamonds, so the answer is from a denser to a rarer medium at an angle of incidence greater than the critical angle.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science (Light – Reflection and Refraction)

142. A person with myopia (near-sightedness) has a far point at 2 m. The corrective lens needed has power approximately:
(a) +0.5 D (b) −0.5 D (c) −2 D (d) +2 D
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is to choose a positive power, but myopia is corrected with a concave (diverging) lens of negative power. Required focal length = −2 m (to bring infinity to the far point), so P = 1/f = −0.5 D, so the answer is −0.5 D.

143. Which of the following statements about Fleming's rules is correct?
(a) Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of induced current in a generator (b) Fleming's right-hand rule gives the direction of force on a current-carrying conductor in a motor (c) Fleming's right-hand rule applies to electric motors (d) Fleming's left-hand rule gives the direction of force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field
Answer: (d)
Solution: This is a classic similar-names trap: the left-hand and right-hand rules are frequently swapped. The left-hand rule applies to motors (force on a conductor), while the right-hand rule applies to generators (induced current). Only the statement that the left-hand rule gives the direction of force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field is correct, so the answer is that option.

(a) 170 m/s (b) 300 m/s (c) 735 m/s (d) 340 m/s
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer divides frequency by wavelength instead of multiplying. Speed v = f × λ = 500 × 0.68 = 340 m/s, which is also the speed of sound in air, so the answer is 340 m/s.

145. Three resistors of 2 Ω, 4 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in series with a 12 V battery. The potential difference across the 4 Ω resistor is:
(a) 2 V (b) 6 V (c) 4 V (d) 12 V
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is to assume the full 12 V appears across each resistor, which is true only in parallel. Total resistance = 12 Ω, current = 1 A, so voltage across the 4 Ω resistor = 1 × 4 = 4 V, so the answer is 4 V.

146. The image formed by a plane mirror is always:
(a) Real, erect and of the same size (b) Virtual, erect and of the same size (c) Virtual, inverted and diminished (d) Real, inverted and magnified
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option calls the image real, but a plane mirror's image cannot be obtained on a screen. A plane mirror always forms a virtual, erect, laterally inverted image of the same size as the object, so the answer is virtual, erect and of the same size.

(a) ₹900 (b) ₹225 (c) ₹450 (d) ₹150
Answer: (c)

148. When white light passes through a glass prism, the colour that deviates the most is:
(a) Violet (b) Green (c) Yellow (d) Red
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to pick red, confusing deviation with scattering (red scatters least and deviates least). In dispersion through a prism, refractive index is highest for violet, so violet bends the most and red the least, so the answer is violet.

149. A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 10 s. The distance covered in this time is:
(a) 200 m (b) 50 m (c) 400 m (d) 100 m
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong answer multiplies final velocity by time (20 × 10 = 200 m), ignoring that the car was accelerating from rest. Acceleration = 2 m/s²; distance = ½ × 2 × 10² = 100 m (or average velocity 10 m/s × 10 s), so the answer is 100 m.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science (Motion)

150. Which of the following devices works on the principle of electromagnetic induction?
(a) Electric motor (b) Electric heater (c) Electric generator (d) Electric bell
Answer: (c)
Solution: The similar-principle trap is the electric motor, which works on the force experienced by a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field — the reverse process. The electric generator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy through electromagnetic induction (induced current in a coil rotating in a magnetic field), so the answer is electric generator.
