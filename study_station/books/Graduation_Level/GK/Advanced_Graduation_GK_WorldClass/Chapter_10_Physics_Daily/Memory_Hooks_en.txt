# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Physics in Everyday Life

---

**Box 1 — Primary Colours of Light**
🎨 **"RGB = Really Good Bulbs"**
Red, Green, Blue are the primary colours of *light* (additive). Mix all three → **White** light.
Trap: Primary colours of *paint/pigments* are different (subtractive) — don't confuse the two!

---

**Box 2 — Why the Sky is Blue**
🌌 **"BLUE scatters Best — Little Unlucky Electrons"**
Blue light has a **shorter wavelength**, so it scatters **most** (Rayleigh scattering ∝ 1/λ⁴). Violet scatters even more, but our eyes are more sensitive to blue — so the sky looks blue, not violet.

---

**Box 3 — Concave vs Convex Mirror**
🪞 **"CAVE goes IN — Concave CAVES in"**
Concave mirror curves **inward** (like a cave) → converging → used in shaving mirrors, headlights, dentists' mirrors.
Convex bulges **out** → diverging → rear-view mirrors (wider field of view, "objects are closer than they appear").

---

**Box 4 — Fuse Wire Facts**
⚡ **"Fuse = Low Melt, High Resist"**
Fuse wire = **low melting point + high resistance** (tin-lead alloy). It melts and breaks the circuit when current exceeds the limit. Always connected in **series** with the live wire.

---

**Box 5 — Series vs Parallel at Home**
🏠 **"Home is a PARALLEL universe"**
Domestic wiring is **parallel** — each appliance gets full 220 V and works independently. In series, one fused bulb would kill the whole house!

---

**Box 6 — Heat Transfer Trio**
🔥 **"Conduction needs Contact, Convection needs Current (of fluid), Radiation needs nothing — it Radiates through space"**
- Conduction → solids (spoon in hot tea)
- Convection → fluids (sea breeze, boiling water)
- Radiation → vacuum OK (Sun's heat reaches Earth)

---

**Box 7 — Black Body Logic**
⚫ **"Black Absorbs Best, Black Radiates Best"**
Black surfaces are the best absorbers AND best emitters of heat. That's why desert coolers and radiators are painted black, and why we wear white in summer.

---

**Box 8 — Hydraulic Machines**
🚗 **"Pascal Pushes with Pressure — Brakes and Lifts obey him"**
Hydraulic brakes and hydraulic lifts work on **Pascal's Law**: pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted equally in all directions. Small force on small area → big force on big area.

---

**Box 9 — Density & Floating**
🧊 **"Ice is Nice — it floats at 0.9"**
Ice floats because its density (~0.9 g/cm³) is less than water (1 g/cm³) — water is densest at **4°C**. That's why ponds freeze from the top, saving aquatic life.

---

**Box 10 — Sound Speed Ladder**
🔊 **"Solid > Liquid > Gas — Sound Sprints in Solids"**
Sound travels fastest in **solids** (~5000 m/s in steel), slower in liquids (~1500 m/s in water), slowest in air (~332 m/s at 0°C). And it **cannot** travel in vacuum — no medium, no music.

---

**Box 11 — Inertia Types**
🚌 **"REST wants Rest, MOTION wants Motion"**
When a bus suddenly starts, you fall **backward** (inertia of rest). When it suddenly stops, you lurch **forward** (inertia of motion). Newton's First Law in every bus ride!

---

**Box 12 — AC vs DC**
🔌 **"India Alternates at 50"**
Indian domestic supply = **220 V, 50 Hz AC**. AC is used because transformers can step it up/down easily, cutting transmission losses. Batteries give DC.

---

**Box 13 — Pressure Cooker Magic**
🍲 **"Pressure Up → Boiling Up → Cooking Quick"**
Inside a cooker, pressure rises → water's boiling point rises above 100°C → food cooks faster. On mountains, pressure is low → water boils below 100°C → cooking is slow (so cookers help even more there!).

---

**Box 14 — Mirage vs Rainbow**
🌈 **"Mirage = Refraction's trick on the Road; Rainbow = Refraction + Reflection in Raindrops"**
Mirage (hot roads) → **total internal reflection** through heated air layers. Rainbow → dispersion + internal reflection inside raindrops, sun behind you, rain in front.

---

**Box 15 — Capillary & Surface Tension**
💧 **"Mercury is a Snob — it won't wet the glass"**
Water climbs **up** in a capillary tube (adhesion > cohesion, concave meniscus); mercury dips **down** (cohesion > adhesion, convex meniscus). Mercury is the classic exception question!

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

1. **Numerical with twisted units:** If a question mixes units (e.g., km/h with m/s, or calories with joules) and you can't convert in 10 seconds, **skip and return later**. Unit-conversion traps eat 2+ minutes and invite silly errors.

2. **"Which of the following is NOT correct" with 4 similar statements:** If two statements look almost identical and you can't recall the exact fact (e.g., exact boiling point at a given altitude, exact refractive index), **skip immediately** — guessing between near-identical statements has the worst risk-reward ratio with negative marking.

3. **Multi-concept assertion-reason questions:** If the assertion itself is unfamiliar (e.g., an obscure application of Bernoulli's principle), don't burn time decoding the reason. **Mark it, move on** — everyday-physics questions you *do* know (mirrors, fuses, pressure cookers) are faster marks. Attempt these only in the final pass.
