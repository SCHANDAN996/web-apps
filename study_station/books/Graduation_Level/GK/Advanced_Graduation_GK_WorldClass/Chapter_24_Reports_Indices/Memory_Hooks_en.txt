# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Reports & Indices

---

**Box 1 — Who publishes what? The "W-W-W" Gang**
> **W**orld Bank → **W**orld Development Report
> **W**EF → **W**orld Competitiveness... no wait — **Global Competitiveness Report**
> **W**HO → **W**orld Health Statistics
> Trick: *"The W-family loves the World — but WEF is the rebel, it says GLOBAL, not World!"*
> Exam trap: WEF publishes **Global** Competitiveness Report, NOT "World Competitiveness Report" (that's IMD's!).

---

**Box 2 — UNDP's HDI: "Humans Develop Incomes"**
> **H**ealth (life expectancy), **D**... no — remember **H-E-I**:
> **H**ealth + **E**ducation + **I**ncome = HDI's three dimensions.
> Mnemonic: *"A Healthy Educated Indian tops HDI."*
> Trap: HDI does NOT measure poverty or inequality directly — that's MPI and IHDI.

---

**Box 3 — IMF's twin reports: "Fiscal Outlook & Economic Outlook"**
> IMF publishes **World Economic Outlook (WEO)** and **Fiscal Monitor**.
> Mnemonic: *"The IMF looks OUT at the economy and MONITORS your fiscal habits — like a strict loan officer."*
> (Makes sense — IMF gives loans!)

---

**Box 4 — World Bank's twins: "Ease of Doing Business is DEAD"**
> **Doing Business Report was DISCONTINUED (2021)** after data irregularities!
> Mnemonic: *"Doing Business did dirty data — so it died."*
> Trap: Any question treating Doing Business 2023/2024 as current = trap. World Bank replaced it with **B-READY (Business Ready)**.

---

**Box 5 — Press Freedom: "RSF = Reporters Sans Frontières"**
> **Reporters Without Borders (RSF)** → World Press Freedom Index.
> Mnemonic: *"Reporters cross borders freely — that's press freedom."*
> Trap: It is NOT published by the UN or UNESCO — it's an NGO based in Paris.

---

**Box 6 — Transparency International: "CPI = Corruption, not Consumer Price!"**
> **Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI)** → Transparency International (Berlin).
> Mnemonic: *"Transparent people don't take bribes."*
> Trap: CPI here is NOT Consumer Price Index (that's India's inflation measure by NSO/Labour Bureau). Same letters, different worlds!

---

**Box 7 — Happiness: "Finns are FINe"**
> **World Happiness Report** → UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN), based on Gallup data.
> **Finland** has topped it repeatedly (2018–2024).
> Mnemonic: *"FINland FINishes first in happiness."*
> Trap: Published by SDSN, NOT directly by UNDP.

---

**Box 8 — Global Hunger Index: "The Irish-German hunger fighters"**
> Published jointly by **Concern Worldwide (Ireland)** and **Welthungerhilfe (Germany)**.
> Mnemonic: *"Concerned Germans (Welt-hunger-hilfe = 'world hunger help') fight hunger."*
> Trap: NOT published by FAO or WFP! FAO publishes **SOFI** (State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World).

---

**Box 9 — Henley Passport Index: "Henley counts your visa-free doors"**
> **Henley & Partners** → Passport Index (visa-free destinations).
> Mnemonic: *"Henley the travel agent counts how many doors your passport opens."*
> Trap: India's rank fluctuates every release — always quote with the year.

---

**Box 10 — Global Peace Index: "IEP = Imagine Eternal Peace"**
> **Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP), Sydney** → Global Peace Index.
> **Iceland** tops it consistently.
> Mnemonic: *"ICEland is the coolest, most peaceful place."*
> Trap: IEP also publishes the **Global Terrorism Index** — same publisher, two indices!

---

**Box 11 — NITI Aayog's Indian indices: "NITI = India's Index Factory"**
> NITI Aayog publishes: **SDG India Index, Composite Water Management Index, School Education Quality Index (SEQI), Export Preparedness Index, State Energy & Climate Index**.
> Mnemonic: *"NITI's kids: SDG, Water, School, Export, Energy — five fingers of one hand."*
> Trap: **India Innovation Index** is ALSO NITI Aayog (earlier with DIPP); but **Global Innovation Index** is WIPO — don't mix!

---

**Box 12 — WIPO's GII: "Swiss innovate, WIPO counts"**
> **Global Innovation Index** → **WIPO** (World Intellectual Property Organization, Geneva).
> **Switzerland** tops it year after year.
> Mnemonic: *"Swiss watches = precision innovation; WIPO in Geneva watches the innovators."*

---

**Box 13 — Environmental Performance Index: "Yale & Columbia walk into a forest"**
> **EPI** → Yale University + Columbia University (with WEF).
> Mnemonic: *"Two Ivy League professors grade the planet's homework."*
> Trap: NOT a UNEP publication, despite the environment theme.

---

**Box 14 — Climate Change Performance Index: "GermanWatch watches the climate"**
> **CCPI** → **Germanwatch** (NGO).
> Mnemonic: *"Germans watch the climate like hawks."*
> Trap: CCPI famously leaves its **top 3 ranks EMPTY** (no country does enough) — a favourite quirky exam fact!

---

**Box 15 — The "Index vs Publisher" lightning table (chant it!)**
> HDI → **UNDP** | WEO → **IMF** | GCI → **WEF** | GII → **WIPO** | EPI → **Yale/Columbia** | CPI → **Transparency Intl.** | Press Freedom → **RSF** | Hunger → **Concern + Welthungerhilfe** | Peace → **IEP** | Passport → **Henley** | Happiness → **SDSN**
> Mnemonic: *"UNDP develops Humans, IMF looks at Economies, WEF Competes, WIPO Innovates, Yale grades Earth, Transparency sees Corruption, Reporters guard Press, Concern feeds Hunger, IEP keeps Peace, Henley opens Passports, SDSN spreads Happiness."*

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

1. **The "current rank" trap:** If a question asks *India's exact rank* in a recent index (e.g., "India's rank in GII 2024") and you only remember the publisher or an older rank — **skip it**. Ranks change every edition; guessing between 39, 40, 42 is a coin toss with negative marking. Attempt only if you verified that specific year's figure.

2. **The "four publishers" match:** If a matching question pairs 4 indices with 4 publishers and you're blank on even **one** pair (say, EPI's publisher), don't gamble — partial knowledge in match-the-following usually means a wrong answer. **Skip and bank the time.**

3. **The "first edition / base year" question:** Questions like "In which year was the Global Hunger Index first published?" are pure memory with no logic to fall back on. If the year isn't in your memory hooks, **skip immediately** — no amount of thinking will recover a date you never learned.
