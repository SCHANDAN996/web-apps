Card 1
Front: Which organisation publishes the Human Development Index (HDI), and what are its three dimensions?
Back: UNDP (United Nations Development Programme). Three dimensions: health (life expectancy), education (mean and expected years of schooling), and standard of living (GNI per capita, PPP).

Card 2
Front: Which index is topped most often by Nordic countries and measures "how happy nations are"?
Back: The World Happiness Report, published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network. Finland has topped it repeatedly in recent years (including 2024).

Card 3
Front: Which Indian institute releases the "India State of Forest Report" (ISFR), and how frequently?
Back: Forest Survey of India (FSI), Dehradun — biennially (every two years), since 1987.

Card 4
Front: Which two indices are both published by the World Economic Forum and often confused in exams?
Back: Global Gender Gap Report and Global Competitiveness Report (the latter discontinued after 2019). Trap: the Global Hunger Index is NOT by WEF — it is by Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.

Card 5
Front: Which index ranks countries on perceived corruption, and who publishes it?
Back: Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI), published by Transparency International. Denmark has consistently ranked at or near the top.

Card 6
Front: Mnemonic trick: How do you remember the three dimensions of HDI?
Back: "HES" — Health, Education, Standard of living. Or in Hindi order: "स्वास्थ्य, शिक्षा, आय" (Health, Education, Income).

Card 7
Front: Which organisation releases the Global Hunger Index, and what is a common examiner trap about it?
Back: Published jointly by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany). Trap: students wrongly attribute it to FAO or the World Food Programme.

Card 8
Front: Which report by the World Bank was discontinued in 2021 after data irregularities?
Back: The Ease of Doing Business Report. It was discontinued in September 2021 following an investigation into data manipulation in earlier editions.

Card 9
Front: Which index measures press freedom worldwide, and who publishes it?
Back: World Press Freedom Index, published by Reporters Without Borders (RSF). Norway has frequently topped it.

Card 10
Front: Which Indian body publishes the SDG India Index, and what does it track?
Back: NITI Aayog — it tracks the progress of Indian states and UTs on the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Card 11
Front: Mnemonic trick: How to remember the publisher of the Global Innovation Index?
Back: "WIPO invents innovation" — WIPO (World Intellectual Property Organization) publishes the Global Innovation Index, along with Cornell University and INSEAD. Switzerland has topped it for many consecutive years.

Card 12
Front: Examiner's trap: The Henley Passport Index ranks what — and what do students wrongly assume it measures?
Back: It ranks passports by the number of destinations their holders can access visa-free (or with visa on arrival). Trap: it does NOT measure a country's power, economy, or tourism quality — only travel freedom.

Card 13
Front: Which report by the IMF gives GDP growth forecasts for the world and individual countries?
Back: The World Economic Outlook (WEO), published twice a year (April and October, with updates).

Card 14
Front: Which index, published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, ranks countries on peacefulness?
Back: The Global Peace Index (GPI). Iceland has topped it every year since 2008.

Card 15
Front: Which organisation publishes the Global Gender Gap Report, and what are its four sub-indexes?
Back: World Economic Forum. Four sub-indexes: Economic Participation and Opportunity, Educational Attainment, Health and Survival, Political Empowerment.

Card 16
Front: Examiner's trap: "India's rank improved in the latest index" — why should you always check the year and the number of countries?
Back: Ranks change with every edition, and the total number of ranked countries also changes. A "better" rank may simply reflect fewer countries being ranked. Always note the edition year and total count before trusting a rank.

Card 17
Front: Which UN agency publishes the State of the World's Children report?
Back: UNICEF (United Nations Children's Fund).

Card 18
Front: Which report by UNEP assesses the gap between pledged emission cuts and what is needed for climate targets?
Back: The Emissions Gap Report, published annually by the UN Environment Programme.

Card 19
Front: Which index ranks countries on food security, and who publishes it?
Back: The Global Food Security Index, published by the Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU).

Card 20
Front: Which Indian ministry releases the "Economic Survey", and when is it tabled?
Back: Ministry of Finance (Department of Economic Affairs) — tabled in Parliament a day before the Union Budget, traditionally around 31 January.
