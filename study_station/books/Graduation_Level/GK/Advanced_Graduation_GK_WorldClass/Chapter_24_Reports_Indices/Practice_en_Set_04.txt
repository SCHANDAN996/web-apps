76. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published annually by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) United Nations Development Programme (c) World Economic Forum (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, not HDI — a common confusion. HDI has been published by the UNDP since 1990, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: UNDP official website

77. Which of the following is NOT a dimension of the Human Development Index?
(a) Life expectancy at birth (b) Gender equality (c) Education (d) Gross National Income per capita
Answer: (b)
Solution: Gender equality is measured by the Gender Inequality Index, a separate UNDP index — this is the tempting trap. HDI has three dimensions: health (life expectancy), education, and standard of living (GNI per capita), so the answer is Gender equality.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

78. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which organisations?
(a) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (b) FAO and WFP (c) UNICEF and WHO (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (a)
Solution: FAO and WFP are UN food agencies but do not publish this index — a frequent trap. The Global Hunger Index is published by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

79. The Corruption Perceptions Index is released by:
(a) Transparency International (b) Amnesty International (c) Human Rights Watch (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amnesty International and Human Rights Watch work on human rights, not corruption rankings. The Corruption Perceptions Index, ranking countries on perceived public-sector corruption, is published by Transparency International, headquartered in Berlin, so the answer is Transparency International.
Source: Transparency International official website

80. The Global Innovation Index is published by:
(a) UNESCO (b) World Intellectual Property Organization (c) OECD (d) World Trade Organization
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNESCO handles education and culture, not innovation rankings. The Global Innovation Index is published by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO), a UN specialised agency based in Geneva, so the answer is World Intellectual Property Organization.
Source: WIPO official website

81. The World Happiness Report is published by:
(a) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (b) UNDP (c) WHO (d) World Bank
Answer: (a)
Solution: UNDP publishes the Human Development Report, which is the common confusion here. The World Happiness Report is published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, using Gallup survey data, so the answer is UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network.
Source: World Happiness Report official website

82. Which index measures the ease of doing business and was discontinued by the World Bank in 2021?
(a) Global Competitiveness Index (b) Economic Freedom Index (c) Ease of Doing Business Index (d) Logistics Performance Index
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Global Competitiveness Index belongs to the World Economic Forum, not the World Bank. The World Bank's Ease of Doing Business report was discontinued in 2021 after data irregularities, so the answer is Ease of Doing Business Index.
Source: World Bank official statements

83. The Global Gender Gap Report is published by:
(a) UN Women (b) World Bank (c) UNDP (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (d)
Solution: UN Women works on gender issues but does not publish this report — a classic trap. The Global Gender Gap Report, covering economic, educational, health and political dimensions, is published by the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

84. The Environmental Performance Index is prepared by which institutions?
(a) UNEP and WWF (b) Oxford University and MIT (c) Yale University and Columbia University (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNEP and WWF work on environment but do not prepare this index. The Environmental Performance Index is a biennial report prepared by Yale University and Columbia University in collaboration with the World Economic Forum, so the answer is Yale University and Columbia University.
Source: EPI official website

85. The Global Peace Index is published by:
(a) United Nations (b) Institute for Economics and Peace (c) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (d) Amnesty International
Answer: (b)
Solution: SIPRI publishes data on arms and military expenditure, not the Global Peace Index — a similar-name trap. The Global Peace Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, headquartered in Sydney, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace official website

86. In the Human Development Index, the education dimension is measured by:
(a) Mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling (b) Literacy rate only (c) Number of universities (d) Government expenditure on education
Answer: (a)
Solution: Literacy rate alone is not used — that is the tempting oversimplification. HDI's education dimension combines mean years of schooling for adults and expected years of schooling for children, so the answer is Mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

87. The Global Competitiveness Report is published by:
(a) World Bank (b) International Monetary Fund (c) OECD (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (d)
Solution: The World Bank and IMF publish economic outlooks, not this report. The Global Competitiveness Report is the flagship publication of the World Economic Forum, based in Geneva, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

88. The Press Freedom Index is published annually by:
(a) Reporters Without Borders (b) UNESCO (c) Committee to Protect Journalists (d) International Press Institute
Answer: (a)
Solution: UNESCO promotes press freedom but does not publish this ranking. The World Press Freedom Index is published by Reporters Without Borders (Reporters Sans Frontières), headquartered in Paris, so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: Reporters Without Borders official website

89. The Multidimensional Poverty Index is jointly developed by UNDP and:
(a) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (b) World Bank (c) UNICEF (d) Asian Development Bank
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Bank measures income poverty, not this index. The global Multidimensional Poverty Index is developed by UNDP together with the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI), so the answer is Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: UNDP-OPHI official website

90. Which organisation publishes the World Development Report?
(a) IMF (b) UNDP (c) WTO (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The IMF publishes the World Economic Outlook — a frequently confused pair. The World Development Report is the annual flagship publication of the World Bank, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

91. The Global Terrorism Index is published by:
(a) United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (b) Interpol (c) Institute for Economics and Peace (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNODC deals with drugs and crime reports, not this index. The Global Terrorism Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, the same body behind the Global Peace Index, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace official website

92. The Henley Passport Index ranks countries based on:
(a) Number of embassies abroad (b) Number of international airports (c) Size of the economy (d) Number of destinations accessible without a prior visa
Answer: (d)
Solution: Embassies and airports are irrelevant to this ranking — a distractor trap. The Henley Passport Index ranks passports by the number of destinations their holders can access without a prior visa, based on IATA data, so the answer is Number of destinations accessible without a prior visa.
Source: Henley & Partners official website

93. The State of World Population report is published by:
(a) WHO (b) UNICEF (c) UNFPA (d) World Bank
Answer: (c)
Solution: UNICEF focuses on children and WHO on health — neither publishes this report. The State of World Population report is the annual flagship publication of the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA), so the answer is UNFPA.
Source: UNFPA official website

94. The Global Food Security Index is published by:

Solution: FAO and WFP are UN food agencies but do not publish this index — a strong trap. The Global Food Security Index is published by Economist Impact (Economist Intelligence Unit), so the answer is Economist Impact.

96. The Logistics Performance Index is published by:
(a) World Trade Organization (b) World Bank (c) IMF (d) UNCTAD

97. The Global Climate Risk Index was published by which organisation?
(a) Germanwatch (b) UNEP (c) Greenpeace (d) IPCC
Answer: (a)
Solution: The IPCC publishes assessment reports on climate science, not country risk rankings — a common confusion. The Global Climate Risk Index was published by Germanwatch, a Bonn-based environmental organisation, so the answer is Germanwatch.

98. The Inequality-adjusted Human Development Index (IHDI) adjusts HDI for:
(a) Gender disparity (b) Political freedom (c) Environmental degradation (d) Distribution of achievements across the population
Answer: (d)
Solution: Gender disparity is handled by the Gender Inequality Index, not IHDI — the classic trap. IHDI discounts each HDI dimension according to its inequality in distribution among the population, so the answer is Distribution of achievements across the population.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

99. The World Economic Outlook is published by:
(a) World Bank (b) OECD (c) World Economic Forum (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (d)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the Global Economic Prospects report — a near-identical name trap. The World Economic Outlook, released twice a year, is published by the International Monetary Fund, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.
Source: IMF official website

100. The Global Liveability Index, ranking cities on quality of life, is published by:
(a) UN-Habitat (b) Mercer (c) Economist Intelligence Unit (d) World Bank
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mercer publishes a Quality of Living survey for expatriates — a similar-name trap. The Global Liveability Index is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU), assessing stability, healthcare, culture, education and infrastructure, so the answer is Economist Intelligence Unit.
Source: EIU official website
