Questions 126–150: Hard

126. Consider the following statements about the Human Development Index (HDI):
1. It is published annually by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP).
2. It combines life expectancy, education, and Gross National Income per capita.
3. The education dimension uses mean years of schooling and expected years of schooling.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting trap is to think HDI uses GDP per capita; it actually uses GNI per capita (PPP US$), so statement 2 is correct as written. All three statements are accurate: UNDP publishes the Human Development Report, and the education dimension combines mean and expected years of schooling, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report (official methodology)

127. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which organisations?
(a) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (b) FAO and World Food Programme (c) WHO and UNICEF (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is choosing FAO/WFP because they publish the "State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World" report — a similar-name confusion. The Global Hunger Index has long been a joint publication of Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

128. Which of the following correctly matches the index with its publishing body?
(a) Global Innovation Index — World Economic Forum (b) Ease of Doing Business — World Bank (c) Global Competitiveness Report — World Intellectual Property Organization (d) Corruption Perceptions Index — Amnesty International
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap options swap publishers: the Global Innovation Index is by WIPO (not WEF), the Global Competitiveness Report is by WEF (not WIPO), and the Corruption Perceptions Index is by Transparency International (not Amnesty). The Ease of Doing Business report was published by the World Bank (discontinued in 2021 after irregularities), so the answer is Ease of Doing Business — World Bank.
Source: Official websites of WIPO, WEF, World Bank, Transparency International

129. Consider the following statements about the Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI):
1. It is published by Transparency International, headquartered in Berlin.
2. It scores countries on a scale of 0 to 100, where 0 means highly corrupt.
3. It measures actual corruption incidents reported by citizens.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: the CPI measures *perceived* corruption in the public sector based on expert assessments and surveys, not actual reported incidents. Statements 1 and 2 are correct — Transparency International is Berlin-based and uses a 0–100 scale where 0 is highly corrupt, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Transparency International official methodology

130. The World Press Freedom Index is published annually by:
(a) UNESCO (b) Reporters Without Borders (RSF) (c) Committee to Protect Journalists (d) Amnesty International
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is UNESCO, which is associated with World Press Freedom Day (3 May) but does not publish this index. The World Press Freedom Index is published by Reporters Without Borders (Reporters Sans Frontières), headquartered in Paris, so the answer is Reporters Without Borders (RSF).
Source: RSF official website

131. Which of the following reports is published by the International Labour Organization (ILO)?
(a) World Development Report (b) World Economic Outlook (c) Global Financial Stability Report (d) World Employment and Social Outlook
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap options are similar-sounding flagship reports: the World Development Report is by the World Bank, while the Global Financial Stability Report and World Economic Outlook are by the IMF. The World Employment and Social Outlook is the ILO's flagship report, so the answer is World Employment and Social Outlook.
Source: ILO official website

132. Consider the following statements about the Global Gender Gap Report:
1. It is published by the World Economic Forum.
2. It measures gaps in economic participation, educational attainment, health and survival, and political empowerment.
3. It ranks countries on the absolute level of women's development rather than the gap between men and women.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 3 is the classic trap: the index measures the *gap* between women and men, not absolute development levels — a poor country with equality can outrank a rich country with a wide gap. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: World Economic Forum, Global Gender Gap Report methodology

133. The 'State of the World's Children' report is published by:
(a) UNICEF (b) WHO (c) UNESCO (d) Save the Children
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Save the Children, an NGO with a similar child-welfare focus, or WHO which handles health. The State of the World's Children is UNICEF's flagship annual report, so the answer is UNICEF.
Source: UNICEF official website

134. Which organisation publishes the Global Peace Index?
(a) United Nations Security Council (b) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (c) International Peace Bureau (d) Institute for Economics and Peace
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is SIPRI, which is famous for arms and military expenditure data, not the Global Peace Index. The GPI is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, a Sydney-based think tank, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace official website

135. Consider the following about the Henley Passport Index:
1. It ranks passports based on the number of destinations their holders can access without a prior visa.
2. It is based on data from the International Air Transport Association (IATA).
3. It is published by the United Nations World Tourism Organization.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: the index is published by Henley & Partners, a residence and citizenship advisory firm, not by UNWTO. It uses IATA's Timatic data and counts visa-free/visa-on-arrival destinations, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Henley & Partners official website

136. The 'World Inequality Report' is associated with which institution?
(a) World Inequality Lab, Paris School of Economics (b) International Monetary Fund (c) OECD (d) World Bank
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the World Bank, which publishes poverty and shared prosperity data but not this report. The World Inequality Report is produced by the World Inequality Lab (associated with the Paris School of Economics and economists like Thomas Piketty and Lucas Chancel), so the answer is World Inequality Lab, Paris School of Economics.
Source: World Inequality Lab official website

137. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of the first publication of these indices/reports?
1. Human Development Index
2. Global Gender Gap Report
3. Corruption Perceptions Index
Select the correct order (earliest first):
(a) 1, 3, 2 (b) 3, 1, 2 (c) 1, 2, 3 (d) 2, 3, 1
Answer: (a)
Solution: This is a chronological-confusion trap. The HDI was first published in 1990, the Corruption Perceptions Index in 1995, and the Global Gender Gap Report in 2006. The correct earliest-first order is HDI, CPI, Gender Gap, so the answer is 1, 3, 2.
Source: Official histories of UNDP, Transparency International, WEF

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: the Export Preparedness Index is also released by NITI Aayog (in partnership with the Institute for Competitiveness), not by the Ministry of Commerce. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.

139. The 'Global Terrorism Index' is published by:
(a) United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (b) Interpol (c) Institute for Economics and Peace (d) RAND Corporation
Answer: (c)

140. Which organisation releases the 'Global Financial Stability Report'?
(a) International Monetary Fund (b) Bank for International Settlements (c) World Bank (d) Financial Stability Board
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the Financial Stability Board, whose name matches the report title almost exactly — a deliberate similar-name confusion. The Global Financial Stability Report is a flagship IMF publication released twice a year, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.

141. Consider the following statements about the World Happiness Report:
1. It is published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network.
2. Its rankings are based primarily on Gallup World Poll life-evaluation data.
3. GDP per capita is the sole determinant of a country's ranking.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: rankings rest on six factors — GDP per capita, social support, healthy life expectancy, freedom, generosity, and perceptions of corruption — not GDP alone. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: World Happiness Report official methodology

142. The 'Global Climate Risk Index' has been published by which organisation?
(a) Germanwatch (b) UNEP (c) Greenpeace International (d) IPCC
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is IPCC, which produces assessment reports on climate science but does not rank countries by climate risk. The Global Climate Risk Index is published by Germanwatch, a Bonn-based environmental NGO, so the answer is Germanwatch.
Source: Germanwatch official website

143. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Global Liveability Index — Mercer (b) Global Food Security Index — Economist Impact (c) Logistics Performance Index — World Economic Forum (d) Human Freedom Index — Freedom House
Answer: (b)
Solution: The traps are near-misses: the Logistics Performance Index is by the World Bank, the Global Liveability Index is by the Economist Intelligence Unit (Mercer publishes a separate Quality of Living survey), and the Human Freedom Index is by the Cato Institute and Fraser Institute. The Global Food Security Index is by Economist Impact, so the answer is Global Food Security Index — Economist Impact.
Source: Official websites of the respective publishers

144. Consider the following statements about the Environmental Performance Index (EPI):
1. It is a biennial index developed by Yale and Columbia Universities in collaboration with the World Economic Forum.
2. It ranks countries on climate change performance, environmental health, and ecosystem vitality.
3. It is published every year without exception.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: the EPI is released once every two years (biennial), not annually — a frequency-confusion trap common in exams. Statements 1 and 2 correctly describe its developers and pillars, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Yale Center for Environmental Law & Policy, EPI official website

145. The 'Global Multidimensional Poverty Index' is jointly produced by:
(a) UNICEF and WHO (b) World Bank and IMF (c) UNDP and Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (d) ADB and UNDP
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the World Bank, which publishes its own monetary poverty estimates but not this index. The global MPI is a joint product of UNDP and the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI), measuring deprivations in health, education, and living standards, so the answer is UNDP and Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: UNDP–OPHI official website

146. Which of the following statements about the 'Ease of Doing Business' report is correct?
(a) It is still published annually by the World Bank (b) It was discontinued by the World Bank in 2021 after data irregularities, with a replacement 'Business Ready' project announced (c) It is published by the World Economic Forum (d) It ranks countries on human development parameters
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the first option: many aspirants still assume the report continues. The World Bank discontinued Doing Business in 2021 after an investigation found data irregularities, and announced the Business Ready (B-READY) project as its successor, so the answer is that it was discontinued in 2021 with Business Ready announced as replacement.
Source: World Bank official website

147. Consider the following about India's NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework):

Which of the statements given above are correct?

148. The 'State of World Population' report is published by:
(a) WHO (b) UN Population Division, Department of Economic and Social Affairs (c) World Bank (d) UNFPA

Solution: The trap is the UN Population Division, which produces the World Population Prospects data — a near-identical name confusion. The State of World Population is the flagship annual report of UNFPA (United Nations Population Fund), so the answer is UNFPA.

149. Consider the following statements about the Global Innovation Index (GII):
1. It is published by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO).
2. WIPO is a specialised agency of the United Nations headquartered in Geneva.
3. The GII ranks countries solely on the number of patents filed.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: the GII uses around 80 indicators across innovation inputs (institutions, human capital, infrastructure, market and business sophistication) and outputs (knowledge, technology, creative outputs) — not patents alone. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: WIPO official website

150. Which of the following correctly arranges these reports by their publishing bodies — World Bank, IMF, WTO — in that order?
(a) World Economic Outlook, World Development Report, Global Financial Stability Report (b) World Development Report, World Economic Outlook, World Trade Report (c) World Trade Report, Global Financial Stability Report, World Development Report (d) Global Financial Stability Report, World Trade Report, World Economic Outlook
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap options shuffle the flagship reports among the three bodies. The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, the IMF publishes the World Economic Outlook (and the Global Financial Stability Report), and the WTO publishes the World Trade Report, so the answer is World Development Report, World Economic Outlook, World Trade Report.
Source: Official websites of World Bank, IMF, WTO
