101. The Human Development Report is published annually by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) World Economic Forum (c) United Nations Development Programme (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, which is the tempting confusion here, but the Human Development Report has been published by the UNDP since 1990, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: UNDP official website

102. The Human Development Index (HDI) combines which three dimensions?
(a) Income, trade, employment (b) Health, wealth, happiness (c) Health, education, standard of living (d) Education, environment, income
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many candidates add "happiness" or "environment" because of confusion with Bhutan's Gross National Happiness or the Environment Performance Index, but HDI uses life expectancy (health), schooling (education) and GNI per capita (standard of living), so the answer is health, education, standard of living.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

103. The Global Gender Gap Report is released by which body?
(a) UN Women (b) World Bank (c) World Economic Forum (d) UNICEF
Answer: (c)
Solution: UN Women sounds plausible because of the subject, but the Global Gender Gap Report is an annual publication of the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

104. The World Press Freedom Index is published by which organisation?
(a) Reporters Without Borders (b) Amnesty International (c) UNESCO (d) Committee to Protect Journalists
Answer: (a)
Solution: UNESCO is associated with press freedom days and awards, which creates confusion, but the annual World Press Freedom Index is compiled by Reporters Without Borders (RSF), so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: RSF official website

105. The Corruption Perceptions Index is released annually by which organisation?

Source: Transparency International official website

106. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which organisations?
(a) FAO and WFP (b) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (c) UNICEF and WHO (d) IFAD and World Bank
Answer: (b)
Solution: FAO and WFP are UN food agencies and seem natural, but the Global Hunger Index is published by the NGOs Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

121. Consider the following statements about the Human Development Index:
1. It was created by Pakistani economist Mahbub ul Haq with Amartya Sen's contributions.
2. The first Human Development Report was published in 1990.
3. Income in HDI is measured by GDP per capita at current prices.
Which of the above statements are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the almost-correct trap: HDI uses GNI per capita (PPP, US dollars), not GDP per capita at current prices. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

122. Arrange the following reports in the correct chronological order of their first publication:
1. Human Development Report
2. Global Gender Gap Report
3. Corruption Perceptions Index
4. World Development Report
(a) 1, 4, 3, 2 (b) 4, 1, 3, 2 (c) 4, 3, 1, 2 (d) 1, 3, 4, 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The chronological confusion trap is placing the Human Development Report first; the World Development Report began in 1978, HDR in 1990, CPI in 1995, and the Global Gender Gap Report in 2006, so the answer is 4, 1, 3, 2.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Consider the following pairs of index and publishing body:
1. Global Innovation Index — WIPO
2. Global Peace Index — Institute for Economics and Peace
3. World Press Freedom Index — UNESCO
4. Global Hunger Index — Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe
Which pairs are correctly matched?
(a) 1, 2 and 4 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pair 3 is the trap: the World Press Freedom Index is published by Reporters Without Borders, not UNESCO. Pairs 1, 2 and 4 are correctly matched, so the answer is 1, 2 and 4 only.
Source: PYQ-style

Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: the CPI is published by Transparency International, not the World Bank. Statements 1 and 3 correctly describe the index, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.
