26. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published annually by which organisation?
(a) United Nations Development Programme (b) World Bank (c) World Economic Forum (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, not HDI — a common confusion since both are UN-system bodies. HDI has been published by the United Nations Development Programme since 1990, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: PYQ-style

27. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which two organisations?
(a) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (b) FAO and WFP (c) UNICEF and WHO (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many candidates pick FAO and WFP because they are UN food agencies, but the Global Hunger Index is prepared by the NGOs Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: PYQ-style

28. The three dimensions of the Human Development Index are:
(a) Income, trade and employment (b) Health, education and standard of living (c) Health, environment and income (d) Education, gender and governance
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option with "environment" is wrong because environment is not an HDI dimension; it appears in other indices. HDI measures a long and healthy life, knowledge, and a decent standard of living (GNI per capita), so the answer is health, education and standard of living.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

29. The Global Gender Gap Report is released by:
(a) World Economic Forum (b) UN Women (c) UNDP (d) World Bank
Answer: (a)
Solution: UN Women sounds plausible because of the word "gender", but the Global Gender Gap Report, with its four sub-indices (economic, educational, health, political), is published by the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum

30. The Corruption Perceptions Index is published by:
(a) Amnesty International (b) World Bank (c) Transparency International (d) OECD
Answer: (c)
Solution: Amnesty International works on human rights, not corruption rankings — a frequent mix-up. The Corruption Perceptions Index, which scores countries on a 0–100 scale, is published by Berlin-based Transparency International, so the answer is Transparency International.
Source: Transparency International

31. In the Corruption Perceptions Index scoring scale, a score of 0 indicates:
(a) Very clean (b) Moderate corruption (c) Highly corrupt (d) No data available
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming 0 means "clean" as in many indices where low is good; here the scale runs from 0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean), so the answer is highly corrupt.
Source: Transparency International

32. The World Happiness Report is published by:
(a) World Health Organisation (b) UNESCO (c) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (d) Gallup Inc. alone
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gallup supplies the survey data but does not publish the report itself — a classic data-provider versus publisher trap. The report is published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, so the answer is UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network.
Source: World Happiness Report

33. The Global Innovation Index is published by:
(a) World Intellectual Property Organization (b) World Economic Forum (c) OECD (d) UNESCO
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Economic Forum publishes the Global Competitiveness Report, which confuses many aspirants. The Global Innovation Index is published by WIPO, a UN specialised agency based in Geneva, so the answer is World Intellectual Property Organization.
Source: WIPO

34. The Ease of Doing Business report, discontinued in 2021, was published by:
(a) World Economic Forum (b) World Bank (c) IMF (d) WTO
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Economic Forum is a distractor because it publishes competitiveness rankings, but the Ease of Doing Business report was a flagship World Bank publication, discontinued after data irregularities, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank

35. The Global Peace Index is released by:
(a) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (b) Institute for Economics and Peace (c) United Nations Peacekeeping (d) Amnesty International
Answer: (b)
Solution: SIPRI is tempting because it works on peace and conflict data, but it publishes military expenditure and arms transfer data, not the Global Peace Index. The GPI is released by the Institute for Economics and Peace, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace

36. The Human Development Report was first published in which year?
(a) 1990 (b) 1985 (c) 1995 (d) 2000
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1995 is a trap because the Gender Development Index was introduced that year; the first Human Development Report itself came out in 1990 under Mahbub ul Haq's leadership, so the answer is 1990.
Source: UNDP

37. The concept of human development underlying the HDI was developed primarily by:
(a) Joseph Stiglitz and Paul Krugman (b) John Maynard Keynes and Milton Friedman (c) Adam Smith and David Ricardo (d) Amartya Sen and Mahbub ul Haq
Answer: (d)
Solution: Keynes and Friedman are macroeconomists unrelated to HDI — a name-similarity trap. The capability approach of Amartya Sen and the work of Pakistani economist Mahbub ul Haq shaped the HDI, so the answer is Amartya Sen and Mahbub ul Haq.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

38. The World Press Freedom Index is published by:
(a) Committee to Protect Journalists (b) Reporters Without Borders (c) UNESCO (d) International Press Institute
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Committee to Protect Journalists is a similar-sounding media-freedom body and a common trap, but the annual World Press Freedom Index ranking 180 countries is published by Reporters Without Borders (RSF), so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: Reporters Without Borders

39. The Global Multidimensional Poverty Index is jointly released by UNDP and:
(a) Asian Development Bank (b) World Bank (c) Oxfam International (d) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative
Answer: (d)
Solution: Oxfam works on inequality reports and misleads many candidates, but the global MPI is a joint product of UNDP and the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI), so the answer is Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: UNDP–OPHI

40. The Global Competitiveness Report is published by:
(a) World Bank (b) UNCTAD (c) IMF (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (d)
Solution: The World Bank is tempting because of its business rankings, but the Global Competitiveness Report, based in Geneva, is the World Economic Forum's flagship publication, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum

41. The Henley Passport Index ranks countries based on:
(a) Bilateral trade agreements (b) Strength of the national currency (c) Number of embassies abroad (d) Number of destinations accessible without a prior visa
Answer: (d)
Solution: The number of embassies is a plausible-sounding trap, but the Henley Passport Index, based on IATA data, ranks passports by how many destinations their holders can enter without a prior visa, so the answer is number of destinations accessible without a prior visa.
Source: Henley & Partners

42. The Global Terrorism Index is published by:
(a) Institute for Economics and Peace (b) United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (c) Interpol (d) RAND Corporation
Answer: (a)
Solution: Interpol is a policing body and does not publish this index — a frequent confusion. The Global Terrorism Index is published by the Institute for Economics and Peace, the same body behind the Global Peace Index, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace

43. The World Development Report is an annual flagship publication of:
(a) IMF (b) WTO (c) World Bank (d) Asian Development Bank
Answer: (c)
Solution: The IMF publishes the World Economic Outlook, which sounds similar and traps many aspirants. The World Development Report is published annually by the World Bank, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank

44. The World Economic Outlook report is published by:
(a) World Bank (b) OECD (c) World Economic Forum (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (d)
Solution: Despite its name, the World Economic Forum does not publish the World Economic Outlook — a deliberate name trap. The report is published twice a year by the International Monetary Fund, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.
Source: IMF

45. The Environmental Performance Index is prepared by Yale and Columbia universities in collaboration with:
(a) UNEP (b) World Economic Forum (c) World Bank (d) Greenpeace
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNEP seems natural for an environment index, but the EPI is a biennial product of Yale and Columbia universities in collaboration with the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: Environmental Performance Index

46. The Global Food Security Index is published by:
(a) World Food Programme (b) FAO (c) Economist Impact (d) IFPRI
Answer: (c)
Solution: FAO is the obvious trap since it is the UN food agency, but the Global Food Security Index is published by Economist Impact (Economist Intelligence Unit), so the answer is Economist Impact.
Source: Economist Impact

47. The Logistics Performance Index is released by:
(a) UNCTAD (b) World Trade Organization (c) IMF (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: UNCTAD deals with trade and development and misleads some candidates, but the Logistics Performance Index, covering customs, infrastructure and shipments, is a World Bank publication, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank

48. The Global Liveability Index, ranking cities on quality of life, is published by:
(a) Mercer (b) Economist Intelligence Unit (c) UN-Habitat (d) World Bank
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mercer publishes a Quality of Living survey, which is a near-identical trap, but the Global Liveability Index ranking cities worldwide is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit, so the answer is Economist Intelligence Unit.
Source: Economist Intelligence Unit

49. The Inequality-adjusted Human Development Index (IHDI) differs from HDI because it:
(a) Covers only developing countries (b) Includes environmental sustainability (c) Discounts each dimension's average value according to its inequality (d) Uses GDP instead of GNI
Answer: (c)
Solution: The environmental option is a trap — that describes the planetary pressures-adjusted HDI. The IHDI discounts the HDI's dimension averages for inequality in their distribution, so the answer is discounts each dimension's average value according to its inequality.
Source: UNDP Human Development Report

50. The Global Cybersecurity Index is published by:
(a) International Telecommunication Union (b) Interpol (c) World Economic Forum (d) NATO
Answer: (a)
Solution: Interpol again tempts candidates as a security body, but the Global Cybersecurity Index is published by the International Telecommunication Union, the UN agency for information and communication technologies, so the answer is International Telecommunication Union.
Source: International Telecommunication Union
