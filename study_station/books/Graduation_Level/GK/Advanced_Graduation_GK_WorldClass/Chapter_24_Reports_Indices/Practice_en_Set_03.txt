51. Which organisation publishes the World Development Report?
(a) International Monetary Fund (b) World Bank (c) United Nations Development Programme (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Economic Forum publishes the Global Competitiveness Report, not the World Development Report — a classic similar-name trap. The World Development Report is the flagship annual publication of the World Bank, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

52. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published by which body?
(a) World Bank (b) World Health Organization (c) United Nations Development Programme (d) UNICEF
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many students confuse HDI with the World Bank because both deal with development data, but the World Bank publishes the World Development Report. The Human Development Report, containing the HDI, has been published by the UNDP since 1990, so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: UNDP official website

53. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which organisations?
(a) FAO and WFP (b) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (c) UNICEF and WHO (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (b)
Solution: FAO and WFP are UN food agencies and sound plausible, but they do not publish the GHI. The Global Hunger Index is a peer-reviewed report published jointly by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: Global Hunger Index official website

54. The Global Gender Gap Report is released by which organisation?
(a) UN Women (b) World Bank (c) UNDP (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (d)
Solution: UN Women sounds like the natural publisher of a gender report, which is the trap here. The Global Gender Gap Report, benchmarking countries on economic, educational, health and political dimensions, is published annually by the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

55. The Corruption Perceptions Index is published by which body?
(a) Transparency International (b) World Bank (c) Amnesty International (d) OECD
Answer: (a)
Solution: Amnesty International works on human rights, not corruption rankings — a similar-sounding trap. The Corruption Perceptions Index, ranking countries by perceived levels of public-sector corruption, is published annually by Transparency International, headquartered in Berlin, so the answer is Transparency International.
Source: Transparency International official website

56. The World Happiness Report is published under which framework?
(a) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (b) UNDP Human Development Report Office (c) World Health Organization (d) OECD Better Life Initiative
Answer: (a)
Solution: The UNDP publishes the Human Development Report, which students often mix up with the Happiness Report. The World Happiness Report is a publication of the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, using Gallup World Poll data, so the answer is UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network.
Source: World Happiness Report official website

57. The Ease of Doing Business report, which was discontinued in 2021, was published by which institution?
(a) World Bank (b) International Monetary Fund (c) World Economic Forum (d) World Trade Organization
Answer: (a)
Solution: The World Economic Forum publishes the Global Competitiveness Report, which is often confused with Ease of Doing Business. The Doing Business report was a World Bank flagship, discontinued in 2021 after data-irregularity findings, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

58. The Global Innovation Index is co-published by WIPO along with which partners?
(a) World Bank and IMF (b) UNESCO and UNICEF (c) Oxford University and MIT (d) Cornell University and INSEAD
Answer: (d)
Solution: Oxford and MIT are famous universities and form a tempting distractor, but they are not the co-publishers. The Global Innovation Index is published by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) in partnership with Cornell University and INSEAD, so the answer is Cornell University and INSEAD.
Source: WIPO official website

59. The Global Peace Index is published by which organisation?
(a) United Nations Security Council (b) International Court of Justice (c) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (d) Institute for Economics and Peace
Answer: (d)
Solution: SIPRI is a famous peace research institute and is the trap option, but it publishes military expenditure data, not the Global Peace Index. The GPI is produced by the Institute for Economics and Peace, headquartered in Sydney, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace official website

60. The Henley Passport Index ranks passports based on which criterion?
(a) Security features of the passport (b) Economic strength of the issuing country (c) Number of countries a passport holder can access without a prior visa (d) Population of the issuing country
Answer: (c)
Solution: Economic strength correlates with passport power but is not the ranking criterion — a conceptual trap. The Henley Passport Index ranks passports by the number of destinations their holders can enter without a prior visa, based on IATA data, so the answer is number of countries accessible without a prior visa.
Source: Henley & Partners official website

61. The State of World Population report is published by which UN agency?
(a) UNICEF (b) WHO (c) UNFPA (d) UN DESA
Answer: (c)
Solution: UN DESA produces population estimates and projections, which makes it a tempting choice, but it does not publish this flagship report. The State of World Population report is the annual flagship publication of the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA), so the answer is UNFPA.
Source: UNFPA official website

62. The World Economic Outlook report is published by which institution?
(a) World Bank (b) International Monetary Fund (c) World Economic Forum (d) Bank for International Settlements
Answer: (b)
Solution: The World Bank publishes Global Economic Prospects, a similarly named report — a classic similar-name trap. The World Economic Outlook, presenting IMF staff analysis of global economic developments, is published by the International Monetary Fund, usually twice a year, so the answer is International Monetary Fund.
Source: IMF official website

63. The Global Terrorism Index is published by which body?
(a) United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (b) RAND Corporation (c) Interpol (d) Institute for Economics and Peace
Answer: (d)
Solution: Interpol deals with international policing and sounds plausible, but it publishes no such index. The Global Terrorism Index is published annually by the Institute for Economics and Peace, the same body behind the Global Peace Index, so the answer is Institute for Economics and Peace.
Source: Institute for Economics and Peace official website

64. The State of the World's Children report is published by which agency?
(a) UNICEF (b) UNESCO (c) WHO (d) Save the Children
Answer: (a)
Solution: Save the Children is a well-known child welfare NGO and forms the trap, but it is not a UN agency publishing this report. The State of the World's Children is the flagship annual publication of UNICEF, so the answer is UNICEF.
Source: UNICEF official website

65. The Global Competitiveness Report is published by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) International Monetary Fund (c) World Economic Forum (d) OECD
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Bank published the Ease of Doing Business report, which students routinely confuse with competitiveness rankings. The Global Competitiveness Report, based in Geneva, is published by the World Economic Forum, so the answer is World Economic Forum.
Source: World Economic Forum official website

66. The World Inequality Report is associated with which institution?
(a) World Inequality Lab (b) World Bank (c) Oxfam International (d) London School of Economics
Answer: (a)
Solution: Oxfam publishes a well-known inequality report each year at Davos, making it a strong trap, but that is a separate advocacy report. The World Inequality Report is produced by the World Inequality Lab, associated with economists like Thomas Piketty, so the answer is World Inequality Lab.
Source: World Inequality Lab official website

67. The Global Climate Risk Index has been published by which organisation?
(a) UNEP (b) Germanwatch (c) Greenpeace (d) IPCC
Answer: (b)
Solution: The IPCC assesses climate science but does not publish a country-wise climate risk index — a common confusion. The Global Climate Risk Index, analysing impacts of extreme weather events, has been published by the German environmental organisation Germanwatch, so the answer is Germanwatch.
Source: Germanwatch official website

68. The Press Freedom Index (World Press Freedom Index) is published by which body?
(a) Committee to Protect Journalists (b) UNESCO (c) Amnesty International (d) Reporters Without Borders
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Committee to Protect Journalists works on journalist safety and is a tempting distractor, but it does not publish this index. The World Press Freedom Index is published annually by Reporters Without Borders (Reporters Sans Frontières), headquartered in Paris, so the answer is Reporters Without Borders.
Source: Reporters Without Borders official website

69. The Global Food Security Index is published by which organisation?
(a) FAO (b) World Food Programme (c) International Fund for Agricultural Development (d) Economist Impact
Answer: (d)
Solution: FAO is the UN's food agency and the obvious trap, but it publishes The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World, not this index. The Global Food Security Index is published by Economist Impact (Economist Intelligence Unit), so the answer is Economist Impact.
Source: Economist Impact official website

70. The Environmental Performance Index is jointly produced by which institutions?
(a) UNEP and WWF (b) Yale University and Columbia University (c) Oxford University and Cambridge University (d) World Bank and IMF
Answer: (b)
Solution: Oxford and Cambridge form a plausible-sounding pair, which is the trap, but they are not involved. The Environmental Performance Index is a biennial joint product of Yale University and Columbia University in collaboration with the World Economic Forum, so the answer is Yale University and Columbia University.
Source: Environmental Performance Index official website

71. The Global Multidimensional Poverty Index is jointly released by UNDP and which other body?
(a) World Bank (b) UNICEF (c) Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (d) Asian Development Bank
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Bank publishes monetary poverty estimates, which makes it a tempting choice, but it is not the co-publisher of this index. The global Multidimensional Poverty Index is released jointly by UNDP and the Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI), so the answer is Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative.
Source: UNDP official website

72. The Logistics Performance Index is published by which institution?
(a) World Trade Organization (b) World Bank (c) International Maritime Organization (d) UNCTAD
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNCTAD publishes maritime transport reviews and sounds plausible, but it does not publish this index. The Logistics Performance Index, benchmarking trade logistics across countries, is a World Bank publication, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: World Bank official website

73. The Global Liveability Index, ranking cities on quality of life, is published by which body?
(a) Economist Intelligence Unit (b) Mercer (c) UN-Habitat (d) World Economic Forum
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mercer publishes a well-known Quality of Living city ranking, which is the trap — two similar city rankings exist. The Global Liveability Index is published by the Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU), so the answer is Economist Intelligence Unit.
Source: Economist Intelligence Unit official website

74. The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World (SOFI) report is led by FAO along with which set of partners?
(a) UNEP, UNESCO and UNIDO (b) World Bank, IMF and WTO (c) WFP, UNICEF, WHO and IFAD (d) OECD, WEF and ILO
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Bank-IMF-WTO trio deals with finance and trade, not hunger monitoring — a thematic trap. The SOFI report is jointly produced by FAO, IFAD, UNICEF, WFP and WHO, the five UN agencies tracking SDG hunger targets, so the answer is WFP, UNICEF, WHO and IFAD.
Source: FAO official website

75. The Global Gender Gap Index measures gaps across how many dimensions?
(a) Four (b) Three (c) Five (d) Six
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often guess three by recalling only health, education and economic participation, forgetting the fourth pillar. The index measures four dimensions: Economic Participation and Opportunity, Educational Attainment, Health and Survival, and Political Empowerment, so the answer is four.
Source: World Economic Forum official website
