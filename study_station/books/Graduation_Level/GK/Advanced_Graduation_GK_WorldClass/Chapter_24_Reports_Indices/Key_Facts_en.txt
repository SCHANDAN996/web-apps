# Key Facts & Figures — Reports & Indices

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human Development Index (HDI) | Published by UNDP since 1990; dimensions: health, education, standard of living | Confusing publisher with World Bank; confusing with "Human Development Report Office" vs "World Economic Forum" | Similar-name confusion |
| Global Hunger Index (GHI) | Published jointly by Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (Germany/Ireland NGOs) | Trap: "published by FAO/WFP" — it is NOT a UN body report | Wrong publisher |
| World Happiness Report | Published by UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN); uses Gallup data | Trap: "published by UNDP" | Wrong publisher |
| Global Gender Gap Report | Published by World Economic Forum (WEF); 4 sub-indices (economic, education, health, political) | Trap: attributing it to UN Women | Wrong publisher |
| Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI) | Published by Transparency International (Berlin-based NGO); scale 0–100 | Trap: "published by World Bank"; also 0 = highly corrupt, 100 = very clean (direction trap) | Wrong publisher + scale reversal |
| World Press Freedom Index | Published by Reporters Without Borders (RSF), Paris | Trap: "published by UNESCO" | Wrong publisher |
| Global Innovation Index (GII) | Published by WIPO (World Intellectual Property Organization), Geneva | Trap: "published by WEF" | Wrong publisher |
| Ease of Doing Business Report | Was published by World Bank; DISCONTINUED in 2021 after data irregularities | Trap: asking "latest EoDB rank" — report no longer exists | Outdated fact trap |
| Global Peace Index | Published by Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP), Sydney | Trap: "published by UN" | Wrong publisher |
| Henley Passport Index | Published by Henley & Partners; based on IATA data; ranks passports by visa-free access | Trap: confusing with "Passport Index by Arton Capital" | Similar-name confusion |
| Environmental Performance Index (EPI) | Published by Yale & Columbia Universities with WEF; biennial | Trap: "published by UNEP" | Wrong publisher |
| Global Competitiveness Report | Published by World Economic Forum (WEF) | Trap: confusing with IMD World Competitiveness Ranking | Similar-name confusion |
| Human Capital Index | Published by World Bank (part of Human Capital Project) | Trap: attributing to UNDP | Wrong publisher |
| Global Terrorism Index | Published by Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP) | Trap: "published by UNODC" | Wrong publisher |
| World Development Report | Annual flagship report of the World Bank | Trap: confusing with UNDP's Human Development Report | Similar-name confusion |
| Global Financial Stability Report | Published by IMF (twice a year) | Trap: "published by World Bank" | Wrong publisher |
| World Economic Outlook | Published by IMF (twice a year) | Trap: "published by WEF" — WEF is a forum, IMF is a fund | Similar-name confusion |
| Global Food Security Index | Published by Economist Impact (Economist Intelligence Unit) | Trap: "published by FAO" | Wrong publisher |
| State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World (SOFI) | Published by FAO (with IFAD, UNICEF, WFP, WHO) | Trap: confusing with Global Hunger Index | Similar-name confusion |
| Human Development Report — India's HDI | India falls in the "medium human development" category | Trap: options claiming "high human development" category | Category trap |
| Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) | Published by UNDP & Oxford Poverty and Human Development Initiative (OPHI); India also releases national MPI via NITI Aayog | Trap: confusing global MPI (UNDP) with India's national MPI (NITI Aayog) | Dual-body confusion |
| NITI Aayog indices | SDG India Index, Export Preparedness Index, School Education Quality Index, State Energy & Climate Index | Trap: attributing these to Finance Commission or CSO | Wrong publisher |
| Logistics Performance Index (LPI) | Published by World Bank; component: customs, infrastructure, shipments, tracking | Trap: "published by WTO" | Wrong publisher |
| Global Liveability Index | Published by Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU) | Trap: confusing with Mercer's Quality of Living Survey | Similar-name confusion |
| Democracy Index | Published by Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU); categories: full, flawed, hybrid, authoritarian | Trap: "published by Freedom House" (Freedom House publishes "Freedom in the World") | Similar-name confusion |
| Global Cybersecurity Index | Published by ITU (International Telecommunication Union) | Trap: "published by INTERPOL" | Wrong publisher |
| Climate Change Performance Index (CCPI) | Published by Germanwatch, NewClimate Institute & Climate Action Network | Trap: "published by UNFCCC" | Wrong publisher |
| World Inequality Report | Published by World Inequality Lab (Paris School of Economics) | Trap: "published by Oxfam" (Oxfam releases its own inequality briefings) | Wrong publisher |
| Global Slavery Index | Published by Walk Free Foundation (Australia) | Trap: "published by ILO" | Wrong publisher |
| Travel & Tourism Development Index | Published by World Economic Forum (earlier Travel & Tourism Competitiveness Index) | Trap: using old name vs new name | Name-change trap |
| India's rank changes yearly | Ranks in HDI, GHI, CPI, Press Freedom etc. are revised annually | Trap: quoting a previous year's rank as current | Outdated-rank trap |
| Denmark/Finland/Norway pattern | Nordic countries top most governance/happiness/press-freedom indices | Trap: swapping which Nordic country tops which index | Topper-swap trap |
| Somalia/South Sudan pattern | These frequently appear at the bottom of CPI and fragile-state lists | Trap: swapping bottom-ranked countries | Bottom-swap trap |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Fact Type | Classification | Time | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Who publishes index X?" | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes — pure recall, highest ROI in this chapter |
| "Which country tops index X?" | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes — but verify year before answering |
| "India's rank in index X (year given)" | Fact-based | 15 sec | ⚠️ Only if year is specified; else skip |
| Scale/direction questions (e.g., CPI 0–100 meaning) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| "Match the index with the publishing body" (4 pairs) | Analytical | 45 sec | ✅ Yes — elimination works well |
| Statement-based: "Which of the following indices are published by UN bodies?" | Analytical | 45 sec | ✅ Yes — recall NGO vs UN list |
| Chronology: "Arrange reports by year of first publication" | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt only if 2+ dates are certain |
| "Which index was discontinued?" (EoDB) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes — favourite trap question |
| Multi-statement with rank + publisher + topper combined | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ One wrong element kills it — attempt only if confident on all |
| India's national indices (NITI Aayog) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes — frequently asked in State PCS |

## Part 3: Data & Formulas

| Item | Data | Note |
|---|---|---|
| HDI components | Life expectancy at birth; expected & mean years of schooling; GNI per capita (PPP) | 3 dimensions, 4 indicators |
| HDI scale | 0 to 1 | Higher = better; categories: low, medium, high, very high |
| GHI components | Undernourishment; child stunting; child wasting; child mortality | 4 indicators; scale 0–100, lower = better |
| GHI severity bands | Low ≤9.9; Moderate 10–19.9; Serious 20–34.9; Alarming 35–49.9; Extremely alarming ≥50 | Direction trap: lower score is better |
| CPI scale | 0–100 | 0 = highly corrupt; 100 = very clean (reversed vs GHI) |
| Global Gender Gap sub-indices | Economic participation; educational attainment; health & survival; political empowerment | 4 sub-indices; scale 0–1 |
| Global Peace Index | Lower score = more peaceful | Direction trap |
| EPI frequency | Biennial (every 2 years) | Yale + Columbia |
| Henley Passport Index basis | Number of destinations accessible without prior visa | Based on IATA data |
| Democracy Index categories | Full democracy; flawed democracy; hybrid regime; authoritarian regime | EIU classification |
| MPI dimensions | Health, education, standard of living (10 indicators) | Same 3 dimensions as HDI — classic trap |
| World Economic Outlook frequency | Twice yearly (April & October, with updates) | IMF |
| Global Financial Stability Report frequency | Twice yearly | IMF |
| NITI Aayog national MPI | Baseline report released 2021 | Uses NFHS data |
| Formula note | No numerical formulas in this chapter; only index scales, component counts, and publication frequencies | Memorise scale direction (higher-better vs lower-better) for each index |
