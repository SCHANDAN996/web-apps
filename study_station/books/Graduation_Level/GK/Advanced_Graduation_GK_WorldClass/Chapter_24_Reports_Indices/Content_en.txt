# Reports & Indices

## The Hook: One Number That Moved a Nation

In 2019, India jumped 14 places in a single year on the World Bank's Ease of Doing Business ranking — from 77th to 63rd. Newspapers ran front-page stories. Ministers held press conferences. Opposition leaders questioned the methodology. All this drama over... one number published by one institution.

That is the power of **Reports and Indices**. They are not just statistics — they are verdicts. They tell us which country is happiest, which is most corrupt, which is hungriest, which is most peaceful. And for your exam, they are among the most predictable, high-yield questions: *"Which organisation publishes the Global Hunger Index?"* — a 15-second question, if you know it.

This chapter will make sure you always know it.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

1. Name the **publishing organisation** of every major report/index asked in exams.
2. Recall the **latest edition's key facts** — India's rank, the topper country, and the theme (where relevant).
3. Distinguish between **confusing pairs** (e.g., Human Development Index vs. Human Development Report; WEF vs. World Bank).
4. Spot and defeat the **examiner's traps** built around similar names and outdated ranks.

---

## Part 1: The Big Idea — What Is an Index, Really?

An **index** is a composite score built from multiple indicators, used to rank countries (or states, or cities). A **report** is the document that presents and explains those rankings.

Three questions decode any index for exam purposes:

- **WHO** publishes it? (the organisation)
- **WHAT** does it measure? (the indicators)
- **WHERE does India stand?** (rank + score, with the year)

🧠 **Active Recall:** Before reading further, try to answer — Who publishes the Human Development Index? (Don't peek. Answer: UNDP. If you knew it, good. If not, you'll never forget it by the end of this chapter.)

## Part 2: The UN Family of Reports

### 2.1 Human Development Index (HDI)

- **Publisher:** United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)
- **Since:** 1990 (first Human Development Report)
- **Created by:** Pakistani economist **Mahbub ul Haq**, with Indian economist **Amartya Sen** playing a key role in the concept.
- **Three dimensions:** Health (life expectancy), Education (mean and expected years of schooling), Standard of living (GNI per capita).
- **Scale:** 0 to 1. Countries are grouped into Very High / High / Medium / Low human development.
- **India (2023-24 report, released 2024):** Rank **134** out of 193 countries, in the "medium human development" category. Switzerland topped.

**Memory hook:** *"Haq se HDI"* — Mahbub ul **Haq** gave us HDI.

### 2.2 Other UN Reports You Must Know

| Report/Index | Publisher |
|---|---|
| World Happiness Report | UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN) |
| Global Gender Gap Report | World Economic Forum (WEF) — *not* a UN body! |
| World Population Prospects | UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs (UN DESA) |
| Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Index | UN SDSN |
| Multidimensional Poverty Index (Global MPI) | UNDP + Oxford Poverty & Human Development Initiative (OPHI) |
| State of World Population Report | UNFPA |
| World Economic Situation and Prospects | UN DESA |

🧠 **Active Recall:** Which two organisations jointly publish the Global MPI? Which report does UNFPA publish?

## Part 3: The Economic Heavyweights

### 3.1 World Bank

- **World Development Report** — flagship annual publication.
- **Global Economic Prospects** — growth forecasts.
- **Ease of Doing Business** — *discontinued* (last edition 2020; India ranked 63rd). The World Bank scrapped it after data irregularities and is developing a replacement (B-READY). **This is a classic trap** — if an option says "latest Ease of Doing Business 2023," it's wrong.
- **Logistics Performance Index (LPI)** — India ranked **38th** out of 139 (2023 report).

### 3.2 International Monetary Fund (IMF)

- **World Economic Outlook (WEO)** — published twice a year (April and October), with updates in between.
- **Global Financial Stability Report**
- **Fiscal Monitor**

### 3.3 World Economic Forum (WEF)

- **Global Gender Gap Report** — India ranked **129th** out of 146 (2024 report). Iceland has topped for over a decade.
- **Global Competitiveness Report** — *discontinued* after 2019 (another trap!).
- **Global Risks Report** — released every January before the Davos meeting.

**Mnemonic — "WEF's GGG":** WEF publishes **G**ender **G**ap and **G**lobal Risks. (Competitiveness is gone — "WEF lost its competitiveness!")

🧠 **Active Recall:** Which two famous indices have been *discontinued*? Why does that matter for exams?

## Part 4: Hunger, Poverty, and Health

### 4.1 Global Hunger Index (GHI)

- **Publisher:** **Concern Worldwide** (Ireland) and **Welthungerhilfe** (Germany) — two NGOs. Earlier, IFPRI was involved.
- **Four indicators:** Undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting, child mortality.
- **India (2024):** Rank **105** out of 127 countries, score 27.3 — "serious" category.
- **Trap alert:** GHI is **NOT** published by the UN, FAO, or WHO. This is the single most-tested trap in this chapter.

### 4.2 Global Food Security Index

- **Publisher:** Economist Impact (Economist Intelligence Unit), sponsored by Corteva.

### 4.3 Health-related

- **World Health Statistics** — WHO.
- **Global TB Report** — WHO.
- **World Malaria Report** — WHO.

**Mnemonic — "Hungry NGOs":** Hunger is handled by **NGOs** (Concern + Welthungerhilfe), not the UN. *"Bhookh NGO ki, khushhaali UN ki"* — Hunger Index by NGOs, Happiness Report by UN (SDSN).

🧠 **Active Recall:** Name the four indicators of the Global Hunger Index.

## Part 5: Peace, Corruption, Press Freedom, and Democracy

| Index | Publisher | Key Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Global Peace Index | Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP), Sydney | Iceland tops; India 116th (2024) |
| Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI) | Transparency International, Berlin | Denmark tops; India 93rd (2023 report) |
| World Press Freedom Index | Reporters Without Borders (RSF), Paris | Norway tops; India 159th out of 180 (2024) |
| Democracy Index | Economist Intelligence Unit (EIU) | Norway tops; India classified as "flawed democracy" |
| Henley Passport Index | Henley & Partners | Based on visa-free access; India's passport allows visa-free/visa-on-arrival access to around 60 destinations (2024) |
| Global Terrorism Index | Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP) | India 14th (2024) |

**Mnemonic — "Peace and Terror live together":** Both the Global **Peace** Index and Global **Terrorism** Index come from the same house — **IEP**.

## Part 6: Innovation, Competitiveness, and Environment

- **Global Innovation Index (GII):** Published by **WIPO** (World Intellectual Property Organization, a UN agency) with partners. India ranked **39th** out of 133 (2024); Switzerland tops.
- **Global Competitiveness Index (IMD version):** IMD World Competitiveness Ranking — separate from WEF's discontinued one. India 39th (2024).
- **Environmental Performance Index (EPI):** Yale and Columbia Universities, with WEF. India ranked **176th** out of 180 (2024).
- **Climate Change Performance Index (CCPI):** Germanwatch, NewClimate Institute, CAN International. India **7th** (2024) — a rare high rank for India; note that the top 3 slots are always left vacant because no country does enough.
- **Global Liveability Index:** EIU — Vienna tops.

**Mnemonic — "WIPO Innovates":** **W**IPO = **W**orld **I**ntellectual **P**roperty **O**rganization → Innovation Index. Intellectual property and innovation go hand in hand.

🧠 **Active Recall:** Which index leaves its top three positions vacant, and why?

## 🔗 Static-Dynamic Link

**NITI Aayog (static) ↔ Indices (dynamic):** NITI Aayog was created on **1 January 2015**, replacing the Planning Commission (1950). Its role as India's "index factory" (SDG India Index, Innovation Index, Export Preparedness Index) is the modern expression of its mandate of cooperative federalism — ranking states to spark competition. If you know *why* NITI Aayog exists, you can guess *which* indices it publishes.

**Mahbub ul Haq (static) ↔ HDI (dynamic):** The HDI's 1990 origin is static history; India's rank changes every edition. Exams test both — the creator (static) and the latest rank (dynamic).

## ⚠️ Caution: Examiner's Trap

**Trap 1 — "लगभग सही कथन" (Almost-True Statement):**
*"The Global Hunger Index is published by the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)."*
Sounds 90% plausible — FAO deals with food, after all. **Wrong.** GHI is published by **Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe** (NGOs). FAO publishes the *State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World (SOFI)*. The examiner swaps the publisher while keeping everything else correct.

**Trap 2 — "समान नाम/वर्ष" (Similar Names/Outdated Rank):**
*"India ranked 63rd in the Ease of Doing Business 2023 report."*
India did rank 63rd — but in the **2020** report, which was the **last one ever published**. The index was discontinued. The examiner pairs a *real* rank with a *fake* year. Similarly, WEF's Global Competitiveness Report ended in 2019 — any "latest" edition is a trap.

**Trap 3 — "कालानुक्रमिक भ्रम" (Chronological Confusion):**
Questions like *"Which index did NITI Aayog launch first?"* or options mixing up HDI's creator (Mahbub ul Haq) with Amartya Sen as the *sole* creator. Sen contributed to the concept, but the HDI was **Mahbub ul Haq's** creation. The examiner exploits half-remembered associations.

**Trap 4 — "नकारात्मक वाक्यांश" (Hidden Negative):**
*"Which of the following is NOT published by a UN body?"* — with options like WEF's Gender Gap Report mixed among genuine UN publications. Under time pressure, students miss the "NOT" and pick the first UN-linked option. Circle the negative word before reading options.

## 📅 Revision Planner

**After 2 days (Blurt Test):** Take a blank sheet. Write every index you remember with its publisher — no peeking. Target: at least 12 correct pairs. Check against the Master Table. Mark the ones you missed; those go on your flashcards.

**Weekly (Trap Drill):** Re-read only the "Examiner's Trap" box and the discontinued indices (Ease of Doing Business, WEF Competitiveness). Then quiz yourself: *"Who publishes GHI? Who publishes SOFI?"* — the confusing pairs are where marks are lost.

**Monthly (Full Blurt + Update):** Reproduce the entire Master Table from memory — publisher, India's rank, topper. Then spend 10 minutes checking whether any new edition has been released (most indices update annually; GHI in October, HDI around March-May, Happiness in March). Update your notes with the year attached: *"GHI 2024: India 105."* A rank without a year is a trap waiting to happen.

**Golden rule:** In this chapter, the **publisher** is asked more often than the rank. When in doubt, revise publishers first.
