# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Reports & Indices

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from This Chapter

Reports and Indices is a **high-frequency, low-effort** chapter across UPSC (Prelims), SSC CGL/CHSL, IBPS/SBI PO-Clerk, RRB NTPC, and State PCS prelims. The questions are almost always **one-liner factual MCQs**, but examiners earn their money through *traps*, not difficulty.

**The four question styles that dominate:**

1. **"Who publishes it?"** — The single most repeated pattern. The report name is given; you must pick the publishing body (e.g., Human Development Report → UNDP, not World Bank).
2. **"Which index, which organisation?"** — Reverse matching: organisation is given, you pick the index/report it releases (e.g., IMF → World Economic Outlook).
3. **"India's rank / top country"** — Rank-based questions, usually on India's position or the country ranked first (e.g., Denmark/Finland-type toppers in happiness, press freedom, corruption indices).
4. **Statement-based questions (UPSC/PCS style)** — Two or three statements about a report's publisher, frequency, or theme; you must identify the correct combination. This is where traps multiply.

**Sub-topics that recur most often (in words, not numbers):**

- **UN family reports** (UNDP's HDR, UNICEF's State of the World's Children, UNESCO's GEM Report, UNEP's Emissions Gap Report) — asked in nearly every exam cycle.
- **Economic reports** (World Bank's World Development Report & Ease of Doing Business — now discontinued, a classic trap; IMF's World Economic Outlook; WEF's Global Competitiveness & Gender Gap Reports).
- **Indices with India's rank** (HDI, Global Hunger Index, Corruption Perceptions Index, World Press Freedom Index, Henley Passport Index, Global Peace Index).
- **Indian reports** (NITI Aayog's SDG India Index, Economic Survey, RBI reports, NFHS) — increasingly asked in SSC and Banking exams.

## 2. Examiner's Trap Types (जाल-प्रकार)

| Trap Type | How It Works | Classic Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Almost Correct** | Option names a real organisation that publishes *other* famous reports | "Global Gender Gap Report — published by UNDP" ❌ (It is WEF; UNDP publishes HDR) |
| **Chronological Confusion** | Swapping years of first publication or mixing annual/biennial frequency | "World Economic Outlook is published annually" ❌ (It is published twice a year) |
| **Negative Phrasing** | "Which of the following is NOT published by the World Bank?" — students misread and pick the one that IS | Always underline NOT/EXCEPT before answering |
| **Similar-Sounding Names** | World Economic Forum vs World Bank vs IMF; Global Hunger Index vs Global Food Security Index | GHI → Concern Worldwide & Welthungerhilfe; GFSI → Economist Impact |
| **Discontinued/Changed Reports** | Ease of Doing Business was **discontinued by the World Bank in 2021** — yet options still use it as bait | Treat any "latest Doing Business ranking" option with suspicion |
| **Rank Swap** | India's rank in one index is planted as the option for another index | Verify rank *and* index *and* year together |

## 3. Top 10 High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions with Solutions

**Q1.** The Human Development Report is published by —
(a) World Bank (b) UNDP (c) UNESCO (d) IMF
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** The Human Development Report, with the HDI, is published by the **UNDP** (since 1990). Trap type: *Almost Correct* — World Bank is the most-picked wrong answer because it publishes many development reports.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q2.** The Global Gender Gap Report is released by —
(a) UN Women (b) UNDP (c) World Economic Forum (d) World Bank
**Answer:** (c)
**Solution:** The Global Gender Gap Report is a **World Economic Forum** publication. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — UN Women and UNDP both work on gender, making them tempting distractors.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3.** Which of the following is NOT a publication of the World Bank?
(a) World Development Report (b) Global Economic Prospects
(c) World Economic Outlook (d) Ease of Doing Business (discontinued)
**Answer:** (c)
**Solution:** The **World Economic Outlook is the IMF's** flagship report, published twice a year. Trap type: *Negative Phrasing* + *Similar-Sounding Names* — "World Economic Outlook" and "Global Economic Prospects" are easily confused.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4.** The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by —
(a) FAO and WFP (b) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe
(c) UNICEF and WHO (d) IFAD and World Bank
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** GHI is published by **Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany)**. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — FAO/WFP deal with hunger, so they are the standard bait.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q5.** The Corruption Perceptions Index is released by —
(a) Transparency International (b) World Economic Forum
(c) Amnesty International (d) OECD
**Answer:** (a)
**Solution:** CPI is published by **Transparency International**, headquartered in Berlin. Trap type: *Similar-Sounding Names* — Amnesty International works on human rights, not corruption rankings.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6.** The World Press Freedom Index is published by —
(a) UNESCO (b) Reporters Without Borders (RSF)
(c) Committee to Protect Journalists (d) United Nations
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** It is published by **Reporters Without Borders (RSF)**, based in Paris. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — UNESCO is associated with press freedom days and awards, so it misleads many.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7.** The 'Emissions Gap Report' is an annual publication of —
(a) IPCC (b) UNEP (c) WMO (d) UNFCCC Secretariat
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** The Emissions Gap Report is released by the **UN Environment Programme (UNEP)**. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — IPCC publishes Assessment Reports, not the Emissions Gap Report.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8.** Which Indian institution releases the SDG India Index?
(a) Ministry of Statistics (b) NITI Aayog (c) RBI (d) CSO
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** The SDG India Index, ranking states and UTs on Sustainable Development Goals, is released by **NITI Aayog**. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — MoSPI handles statistics but not this index.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9.** The Henley Passport Index ranks countries based on —
(a) visa-free access of their passports (b) tourism revenue
(c) diplomatic missions abroad (d) immigration rates
**Answer:** (a)
**Solution:** The Henley Passport Index ranks passports by the number of destinations their holders can access **without a prior visa**. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — the other options sound plausible but are not the criterion.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10.** The 'Global Peace Index' is published by —
(a) United Nations (b) Institute for Economics and Peace
(c) Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (d) World Bank
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** GPI is published by the **Institute for Economics and Peace (IEP)**, Sydney. Trap type: *Similar-Sounding Names* — SIPRI is famous for arms/military expenditure data, not the GPI.
**Source:** PYQ-style

## 4. The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**⏱ 15-Second Question — Q1 (HDR → UNDP):**
This is a pure recall question. You either know "Human Development Report = UNDP" or you don't. **Do not spend more than 15 seconds.** If the answer doesn't surface immediately, mark it and move on — re-reading the options will not create knowledge you don't have. Such publisher-matching questions are decided in the first glance.

**⏱ 45-Second Question — Q3 (NOT published by World Bank):**
Negative-phrasing questions deserve the full 45 seconds. The method: (1) underline "NOT" mentally, (2) check each option against the World Bank list — WDR ✔, Global Economic Prospects ✔, Doing Business ✔ (discontinued but still a World Bank product), (3) the odd one out is World Economic Outlook → IMF. Rushing this question type is the single biggest cause of silly errors in this chapter.

**Strategy summary:** Publisher-matching and rank-recall questions = 15 seconds. Statement-based, NOT/EXCEPT, and multi-report matching questions = up to 45 seconds. Never cross 45 seconds on this chapter — every extra minute here is stolen from reasoning or quantitative sections.

---

## 5. One-Line Exam Wisdom

In Reports & Indices, **the examiner never tests depth — only precision**. Master the publisher of each report, India's rank with the year attached, and the habit of hunting for NOT/EXCEPT, and this chapter becomes the fastest scoring zone in the GK paper.
