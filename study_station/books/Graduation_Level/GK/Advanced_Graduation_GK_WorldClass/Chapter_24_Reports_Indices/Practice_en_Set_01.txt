1. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published annually by which organisation?
(a) World Bank (b) World Economic Forum (c) United Nations Development Programme (d) International Monetary Fund
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Bank publishes the World Development Report, not HDI — a common confusion. HDI is released by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), so the answer is United Nations Development Programme.
Source: PYQ-style

2. The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by which organisations?
(a) FAO and WFP (b) World Bank and IMF (c) UNICEF and WHO (d) Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe
Answer: (d)
Solution: FAO and WFP are UN food agencies but do not publish this index — a tempting trap. The Global Hunger Index is published by Concern Worldwide (Ireland) and Welthungerhilfe (Germany), so the answer is Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe.
Source: PYQ-style

8. The Human Development Index combines which three dimensions?
(a) Health, wealth and freedom (b) Health, education and standard of living (c) Education, employment and equality (d) Income, trade and literacy
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Freedom" and "equality" sound plausible but are not HDI dimensions. HDI uses life expectancy (health), schooling (education) and GNI per capita (standard of living), so the answer is health, education and standard of living.

21. Consider the following statements about the Human Development Report:
1. It is published by the United Nations Development Programme.
2. The first Human Development Report was published in 1990.
3. It is published once every five years.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — the report is annual, not five-yearly; the IPCC assessment cycle is roughly five to seven years, which causes confusion. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
