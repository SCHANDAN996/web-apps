26. Which of the following is the correct order of the Earth's layers from the outermost to the innermost?
(a) Mantle, Crust, Outer Core, Inner Core (b) Crust, Mantle, Outer Core, Inner Core (c) Crust, Outer Core, Mantle, Inner Core (d) Mantle, Outer Core, Crust, Inner Core
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option reverses crust and mantle; the crust is the thin outermost shell, not the mantle. Below the crust lies the mantle, then the liquid outer core, and finally the solid inner core, so the answer is Crust, Mantle, Outer Core, Inner Core.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

46. Which of the following statements about the International Date Line is correct?
(a) It roughly follows the 180° meridian with deviations (b) It follows the 0° meridian exactly (c) It passes through the Atlantic Ocean (d) It coincides with the Equator
Answer: (a)
Solution: The 0° meridian is the trap because it sounds like the natural date divider, but that is the Prime Meridian at Greenwich; the Date Line roughly follows 180° longitude, zigzagging to keep island groups in the same date, so the answer is that it roughly follows the 180° meridian with deviations.
