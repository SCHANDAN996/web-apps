Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Which of the following rivers crosses the Equator twice?
(a) Congo (b) Amazon (c) Nile (d) Mekong
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Amazon is a tempting choice because of its size, but it flows almost entirely south of the Equator and never crosses it. The Congo (Zaire) river flows in a great arc that crosses the Equator once going north and again going south, making it the only major river to do so. Hence the answer is Congo.

77. The 'Ring of Fire' is associated with which of the following?
(a) Volcanoes and earthquakes around the Pacific Ocean (b) Coral reefs of the Pacific (c) Deserts of Africa (d) Trade winds of the Atlantic
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ring of Fire is not a coral formation; it is the horseshoe-shaped belt of intense seismic and volcanic activity encircling the Pacific Ocean, where several tectonic plates meet. About 75% of the world's active volcanoes lie here. Hence the answer is volcanoes and earthquakes around the Pacific Ocean.

78. Which strait separates Asia from North America?
(a) Bering Strait (b) Davis Strait (c) Bosphorus Strait (d) Malacca Strait
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Bosphorus is a trap — it separates Europe from Asia (Turkey), not Asia from North America. The Bering Strait separates Russia (Asia) from Alaska (North America) and connects the Arctic Ocean with the Pacific. Hence the answer is Bering Strait.

79. The Great Barrier Reef is located off the coast of which country?
(a) Brazil (b) South Africa (c) Australia (d) Indonesia
Answer: (c)
Solution: Indonesia has large coral areas, which makes it a tempting option, but the Great Barrier Reef — the world's largest coral reef system — lies off the north-eastern coast of Queensland, Australia, in the Coral Sea. Hence the answer is Australia.

80. Which of the following is the largest desert in the world by area?
(a) Sahara (b) Antarctic Desert (c) Arabian Desert (d) Gobi Desert
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Sahara is the classic trap — it is the largest *hot* desert, but a desert is defined by low precipitation, not heat. The Antarctic Desert, a cold polar desert, is the largest desert on Earth by area. Hence the answer is Antarctic Desert.

81. The Tropic of Capricorn does NOT pass through which of the following countries?
(a) Brazil (b) Australia (c) South Africa (d) India
Answer: (d)
Solution: India is crossed by the Tropic of Cancer, not the Tropic of Capricorn — this is the chronological/latitudinal confusion examiners exploit. The Tropic of Capricorn passes through Brazil, Australia and South Africa among others. Hence the answer is India.

82. Which ocean is the deepest in the world?
(a) Pacific Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Arctic Ocean (d) Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Pacific is not only the largest but also the deepest ocean; the Mariana Trench (about 11,000 m) lies in it. The Arctic is the shallowest, which is the reverse trap. Hence the answer is Pacific Ocean.

83. The Suez Canal connects which two water bodies?
(a) Red Sea and Persian Gulf (b) Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea (c) Mediterranean Sea and Black Sea (d) Red Sea and Arabian Sea directly
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Persian Gulf option is a trap — that route involves the Strait of Hormuz, not the Suez Canal. The Suez Canal (opened 1869) links the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea, shortening the Europe–Asia sea route. Hence the answer is Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea.

84. Which country has the largest number of time zones?
(a) Russia (b) USA (c) France (d) China
Answer: (c)
Solution: Russia (11 zones) is the tempting answer, but France, counting its overseas territories spread across the globe, has 12 time zones — the most of any country. China, despite its width, uses a single time zone. Hence the answer is France.

85. The 'Midnight Sun' phenomenon is best observed in which region?
(a) Equatorial region (b) Regions within the Arctic and Antarctic Circles (c) Tropical deserts (d) Temperate grasslands
Answer: (b)
Solution: The equatorial region has nearly equal day and night throughout the year, so it is a trap. The Midnight Sun — the sun visible at midnight — occurs in summer within the polar circles, e.g., Norway. Hence the answer is regions within the Arctic and Antarctic Circles.

86. Which is the longest mountain range in the world above sea level?
(a) Himalayas (b) Rockies (c) Andes (d) Alps
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Himalayas are the *highest* range, which makes them the classic trap, but the Andes of South America (about 7,000 km) form the longest continental mountain range. The mid-ocean ridge is longer but lies underwater. Hence the answer is Andes.

87. Lake Baikal, the world's deepest freshwater lake, is located in which country?
(a) Canada (b) Kazakhstan (c) Mongolia (d) Russia
Answer: (d)
Solution: Canada has the most lakes, which is the tempting distractor, but Lake Baikal — the deepest (about 1,642 m) and largest by volume freshwater lake — lies in Siberia, Russia. Hence the answer is Russia.

88. Which of the following countries is landlocked?
(a) Thailand (b) Vietnam (c) Laos (d) Myanmar
Answer: (c)
Solution: All the other options have coastlines on the South China Sea or the Andaman Sea, which is the trap in this similar-names cluster of Southeast Asian countries. Laos is the only landlocked country in Southeast Asia. Hence the answer is Laos.

89. The Panama Canal connects which two oceans?
(a) Atlantic and Indian (b) Pacific and Indian (c) Arctic and Atlantic (d) Atlantic and Pacific
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Indian Ocean options are traps — no canal links the Indian Ocean directly to another ocean. The Panama Canal (opened 1914) joins the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans across the Isthmus of Panama. Hence the answer is Atlantic and Pacific.

90. Which of the following statements about the Equator is correct?
(a) It passes through Brazil (b) It passes through India (c) It passes through Egypt (d) It passes through China
Answer: (a)
Solution: India and Egypt lie in the Northern Hemisphere tropics and China is entirely in the northern temperate zone — all traps based on vague familiarity. The Equator passes through Brazil, Ecuador, Colombia, Congo, Uganda, Kenya and Indonesia, among others. Hence the answer is Brazil.

91. The 'Roaring Forties' refer to which of the following?
(a) Cyclones in the Bay of Bengal (b) A gold rush period in Australia (c) Warm ocean currents near 40°N (d) Strong westerly winds in the Southern Hemisphere
Answer: (d)
Solution: The gold-rush association is a tempting historical trap. The Roaring Forties are powerful westerly winds blowing between 40°S and 50°S latitudes, unobstructed by large landmasses. Hence the answer is strong westerly winds in the Southern Hemisphere.

92. Which is the smallest continent by land area?
(a) Europe (b) Australia (c) Antarctica (d) South America
Answer: (b)
Solution: Europe is the trap — it is the second smallest. Australia (about 7.7 million sq km) is the smallest continent, while Europe is larger in area. Hence the answer is Australia.

93. The Strait of Gibraltar connects the Mediterranean Sea with which water body?
(a) Red Sea (b) Black Sea (c) Atlantic Ocean (d) North Sea
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Black Sea is a trap — it connects to the Mediterranean via the Bosphorus and Dardanelles, not Gibraltar. The Strait of Gibraltar links the Mediterranean Sea to the Atlantic Ocean and separates Spain from Morocco. Hence the answer is Atlantic Ocean.

94. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Pampas — North America (b) Steppes — Eurasia (c) Prairies — South America (d) Veld — Australia
Answer: (b)
Solution: Each wrong option swaps the grassland's continent — the classic matching trap. Pampas are in South America (Argentina), Prairies in North America, and Veld in South Africa. Steppes are the temperate grasslands of Eurasia. Hence the answer is Steppes — Eurasia.

95. Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest peak, is located in which country?
(a) Kenya (b) Ethiopia (c) Tanzania (d) Uganda
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kenya is the frequent trap because Kilimanjaro is best viewed from the Kenyan side and lies near the border, but the mountain itself is in Tanzania. It is a dormant volcano with three cones. Hence the answer is Tanzania.

96. Which of the following is the correct order of the Earth's layers from outermost to innermost?
(a) Crust, Mantle, Core (b) Core, Mantle, Crust (c) Mantle, Crust, Core (d) Crust, Core, Mantle
Answer: (a)
Solution: The other sequences are chronological-style ordering traps that shuffle the same three terms. From the surface inward, the order is Crust, then Mantle, then Core (outer and inner). Hence the answer is Crust, Mantle, Core.

97. The 'Dead Sea' is bordered by which of the following countries?
(a) Egypt and Sudan (b) Saudi Arabia and Yemen (c) Syria and Lebanon (d) Jordan and Israel
Answer: (d)
Solution: The other pairs are nearby Middle Eastern countries chosen as geographic-confusion traps. The Dead Sea, the lowest point on Earth's land surface, lies between Jordan to the east and Israel and the West Bank to the west. Hence the answer is Jordan and Israel.

98. Which country is known as the 'Land of the Rising Sun'?
(a) China (b) South Korea (c) Thailand (d) Japan
Answer: (d)
Solution: China is a trap because of its size and eastern location, but Japan's name (Nippon, 'sun origin') reflects its position east of the Asian mainland, where the sun rises first. Hence the answer is Japan.

99. Which of the following rivers flows through the Grand Canyon?
(a) Mississippi (b) Colorado (c) Missouri (d) Columbia
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Mississippi is the tempting choice as the USA's most famous river, but it flows far to the east. The Colorado River carved the Grand Canyon in Arizona over millions of years. Hence the answer is Colorado.

100. Which of the following statements about the International Date Line is correct?
(a) It roughly follows 180° longitude with deviations to avoid dividing countries (b) It is a perfectly straight line along 180° longitude (c) It coincides exactly with the Prime Meridian (d) It passes through the middle of the Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (a)
Solution: The 'perfectly straight' option is the almost-correct trap — the Date Line zigzags around island groups like Kiribati and the Aleutians so that a single country does not fall into two dates. The Prime Meridian (0°) is its opposite, another trap. Hence the answer is that it roughly follows 180° longitude with deviations to avoid dividing countries.
