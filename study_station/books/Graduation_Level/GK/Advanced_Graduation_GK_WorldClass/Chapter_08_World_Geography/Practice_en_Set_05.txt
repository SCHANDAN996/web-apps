101. Which is the largest desert in the world by area?
(a) Sahara Desert (b) Antarctic Desert (c) Arabian Desert (d) Gobi Desert
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is the Sahara, which is only the largest *hot* desert. Deserts are defined by low precipitation, not heat, and the Antarctic Desert covers about 14 million sq km, larger than the Sahara's roughly 9 million sq km, so the answer is the Antarctic Desert.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

102. The Tropic of Capricorn passes through which of the following continents?
(a) South America, Africa, Australia (b) Asia, Africa, Australia (c) North America, Africa, Asia (d) Europe, Africa, South America
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many candidates confuse the Tropic of Cancer with the Tropic of Capricorn; the Tropic of Cancer passes through Asia, but Capricorn does not. The Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S) crosses South America, southern Africa and Australia, so the answer is South America, Africa, Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

103. Which strait separates Asia from North America?
(a) Davis Strait (b) Bering Strait (c) Bosphorus Strait (d) Malacca Strait
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Bosphorus is a trap because it also separates two continents (Europe and Asia within Turkey), but it does not touch North America. The Bering Strait separates Russia (Asia) from Alaska (North America), so the answer is the Bering Strait.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

104. The Great Barrier Reef is located off the coast of which country?
(a) Brazil (b) Australia (c) South Africa (d) Indonesia
Answer: (b)
Solution: Indonesia is tempting because it lies in the coral-rich Indo-Pacific, but the Great Barrier Reef lies in the Coral Sea off the north-eastern coast of Queensland, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

105. Which is the deepest oceanic trench in the world?
(a) Java Trench (b) Puerto Rico Trench (c) Peru-Chile Trench (d) Mariana Trench
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Peru-Chile Trench is a trap as it is the longest trench off South America, but not the deepest. The Mariana Trench in the western Pacific, with the Challenger Deep at about 11,000 metres, is the deepest point on Earth, so the answer is the Mariana Trench.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

106. The river Nile drains into which sea?
(a) Red Sea (b) Mediterranean Sea (c) Black Sea (d) Arabian Sea
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Red Sea is a tempting trap because it borders Egypt, but the Nile flows northward through Egypt and empties through its delta into the Mediterranean Sea, so the answer is the Mediterranean Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

107. Which country is known as the "Land of the Rising Sun"?
(a) China (b) Japan (c) South Korea (d) Thailand
Answer: (b)
Solution: China is a trap because it is also an East Asian nation, but the name comes from Japan's position east of the Asian mainland where the sun appears to rise first; "Nippon" itself means "origin of the sun", so the answer is Japan.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Social Science (General Knowledge reference)

108. The Pampas grasslands are found in which continent?
(a) South America (b) Africa (c) North America (d) Australia
Answer: (a)
Solution: North America is a trap because its temperate grasslands (Prairies) are similar, but the Pampas are the temperate grasslands of Argentina and Uruguay, so the answer is South America.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

109. Which is the largest island in the world?
(a) Borneo (b) Greenland (c) New Guinea (d) Madagascar
Answer: (b)
Solution: New Guinea is a trap as it is the second-largest island and often confused in rankings, but Greenland, with about 2.16 million sq km, is the world's largest island (Australia is treated as a continent), so the answer is Greenland.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

110. The Suez Canal connects which two water bodies?
(a) Mediterranean Sea and Black Sea (b) Red Sea and Persian Gulf (c) Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea (d) Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Mediterranean–Black Sea link is a trap because that route exists through the Bosphorus and Dardanelles, not the Suez. The Suez Canal, opened in 1869 in Egypt, joins the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea, so the answer is Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

111. Which of the following countries does the Equator NOT pass through?
(a) India (b) Kenya (c) Brazil (d) Indonesia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Indonesia is a trap because it is an Asian country and candidates assume India too lies on the Equator; in fact India lies entirely north of it. The Equator crosses Indonesia, Kenya and Brazil among others, so the answer is India.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

112. The "Ring of Fire" is associated with which ocean?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Pacific Ocean (c) Arctic Ocean (d) Indian Ocean
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Indian Ocean is a trap due to the 2004 tsunami, but the Ring of Fire is the horseshoe-shaped belt of volcanoes and earthquake zones around the margins of the Pacific Ocean, so the answer is the Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

113. Which is the longest river in Europe?
(a) Danube (b) Volga (c) Rhine (d) Thames
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Danube is a strong trap because it is the longest river of the European Union and passes through the most countries, but the Volga in Russia, about 3,530 km long, is Europe's longest river, so the answer is the Volga.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

114. The Atacama Desert, one of the driest places on Earth, is located in which country?
(a) Chile (b) Peru (c) Argentina (d) Bolivia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Peru is a trap because the desert's aridity extends near its border and the Peru Current causes the dryness, but the Atacama Desert lies mainly in northern Chile, so the answer is Chile.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

115. Which mountain range separates Europe from Asia?
(a) Alps (b) Andes (c) Carpathians (d) Urals
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Alps are a trap as they are Europe's most famous range, but they lie entirely within Europe. The Ural Mountains in Russia form the conventional boundary between Europe and Asia, so the answer is the Urals.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

116. The Strait of Gibraltar connects the Mediterranean Sea with which water body?
(a) Red Sea (b) Black Sea (c) North Sea (d) Atlantic Ocean
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Black Sea is a trap because it is also connected to the Mediterranean, but via the Bosphorus, not Gibraltar. The Strait of Gibraltar links the Mediterranean Sea to the Atlantic Ocean between Spain and Morocco, so the answer is the Atlantic Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

117. Which is the largest lake in the world by surface area?
(a) Lake Superior (b) Lake Victoria (c) Caspian Sea (d) Lake Baikal
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lake Superior is a trap because it is the largest *freshwater* lake by area, and Lake Baikal is the deepest and largest by volume, but the Caspian Sea, a saltwater lake of about 371,000 sq km, is the largest lake overall, so the answer is the Caspian Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

118. The country known as the "Land of a Thousand Lakes" is:
(a) Finland (b) Sweden (c) Norway (d) Denmark
Answer: (a)
Solution: Norway is a trap because of its famous fjords and water bodies, but Finland, with around 1,88,000 lakes, is officially called the Land of a Thousand Lakes, so the answer is Finland.

119. Which African country was formerly known as Abyssinia?
(a) Sudan (b) Somalia (c) Ethiopia (d) Eritrea
Answer: (c)
Solution: Eritrea is a trap because it was once part of the same region and gained independence from it in 1993, but the ancient name Abyssinia refers to Ethiopia, so the answer is Ethiopia.
Source: General Knowledge reference (standard atlas)

120. The Prime Meridian passes through which of the following cities?
(a) Paris (b) Greenwich (London) (c) Rome (d) Berlin
Answer: (b)
Solution: Paris is a trap because France historically used its own Paris Meridian for maps, but the internationally accepted Prime Meridian (0° longitude) passes through the Royal Observatory at Greenwich, London, so the answer is Greenwich (London).
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

121. Consider the following statements:
1. The Tropic of Cancer passes through the Bahamas.
2. The Tropic of Cancer passes through Saudi Arabia.
3. The Tropic of Cancer passes through Madagascar.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap: Madagascar is crossed by the Tropic of Capricorn, not the Tropic of Cancer — a classic north-south confusion. The Tropic of Cancer does pass through the Bahamas and Saudi Arabia, so statements 1 and 2 only are correct, and the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

122. Arrange the following straits from west to east:
1. Strait of Gibraltar
2. Bosphorus Strait
3. Strait of Hormuz
4. Strait of Malacca
Select the correct order:
(a) 1-3-2-4 (b) 2-1-3-4 (c) 1-2-3-4 (d) 2-1-4-3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing Hormuz before Bosphorus; Hormuz (Persian Gulf, about 56°E) lies east of the Bosphorus (about 29°E). West to east the order is Gibraltar, Bosphorus, Hormuz, Malacca, so the answer is 1-2-3-4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

123. Consider the following pairs:
1. Prairies — North America
2. Steppes — Eurasia
3. Velds — South Africa
4. Downs — South America
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1, 2, 3 and 4 (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Pair 4 is the trap: the Downs are the temperate grasslands of Australia, while South America's grasslands are the Pampas — a classic similar-name confusion. Prairies (North America), Steppes (Eurasia) and Velds (South Africa) are correctly matched, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3 only.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Our Environment)

124. Which of the following statements about Lake Baikal is correct?
(a) It is the deepest lake in the world and holds the largest volume of unfrozen fresh water. (b) It is the largest lake in the world by surface area. (c) It is located entirely within Mongolia. (d) It is a saltwater lake formed by tectonic uplift.
Answer: (a)
Solution: The first option is the trap: the Caspian Sea is the largest lake by surface area, not Baikal. Lake Baikal in Siberia, Russia, is the world's deepest lake (about 1,642 m) and holds the largest volume of unfrozen fresh water, so the answer is that it is the deepest lake holding the largest volume of unfrozen fresh water.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

125. Consider the following statements:
1. The Panama Canal connects the Atlantic Ocean with the Pacific Ocean.
2. The Panama Canal lies in South America.
3. The Panama Canal uses locks to raise and lower ships.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: Panama lies in Central America (North America), not South America, though it borders Colombia. The canal does connect the Atlantic and Pacific and uses a lock system to lift ships over the isthmus, so statements 1 and 3 only are correct, and the answer is 1 and 3 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)
