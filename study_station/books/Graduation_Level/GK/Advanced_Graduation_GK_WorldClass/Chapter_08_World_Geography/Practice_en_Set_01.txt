1. Which is the largest continent by area?
(a) Africa (b) North America (c) Asia (d) Europe
Answer: (c)
Solution: Africa is the second-largest continent and is a tempting wrong choice because it contains the most countries. Asia is the largest continent, covering about 30% of Earth's land area, so the answer is Asia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

2. The Prime Meridian (0° longitude) passes through which city?
(a) Paris (b) Greenwich (c) Rome (d) Berlin
Answer: (b)
Solution: Paris had its own historical meridian, which confuses many candidates. The internationally accepted Prime Meridian passes through the Royal Observatory at Greenwich, London, so the answer is Greenwich.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

3. Which is the deepest oceanic trench in the world?
(a) Java Trench (b) Mariana Trench (c) Puerto Rico Trench (d) Peru-Chile Trench
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Puerto Rico Trench is the deepest in the Atlantic and is often picked by mistake. The Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean is the deepest, with the Challenger Deep at about 10,900 m, so the answer is Mariana Trench.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

4. The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which of the following continents?
(a) Asia (b) Africa (c) North America (d) South America
Answer: (d)
Solution: Candidates often confuse the Tropic of Cancer with the Tropic of Capricorn, which does cross South America. The Tropic of Cancer passes through Africa, Asia and North America only, so the answer is South America.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

5. Which is the largest desert in the world (by area, including cold deserts)?
(a) Antarctic Desert (b) Gobi (c) Sahara (d) Arabian Desert
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sahara is the classic trap because it is the largest *hot* desert, and most books mention only that. Counting cold deserts, the Antarctic Desert is the largest desert on Earth, so the answer is Antarctic Desert.
Source: PYQ-style

6. The Great Barrier Reef is located off the coast of which country?
(a) Australia (b) South Africa (c) Japan (d) Brazil
Answer: (a)
Solution: Brazil has long coastlines that mislead some candidates, but the Great Barrier Reef lies off the north-eastern coast of Queensland, Australia, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

7. Which strait separates Asia from North America?
(a) Palk Strait (b) Strait of Malacca (c) Bering Strait (d) Davis Strait
Answer: (c)
Solution: Davis Strait is a similar-sounding name that separates Greenland and Canada, a classic similar-name trap. The Bering Strait separates Russia (Asia) from Alaska (North America), so the answer is Bering Strait.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

8. The longest river in the world by length is generally considered to be:
(a) Amazon (b) Yangtze (c) Nile (d) Mississippi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Amazon is the trap here — it carries the largest volume of water and some measurements dispute the length ranking, but the conventionally accepted longest river is the Nile (about 6,650 km), so the answer is Nile.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

9. Which of the following countries is landlocked?
(a) Thailand (b) Vietnam (c) Myanmar (d) Laos
Answer: (d)
Solution: All four are Southeast Asian neighbours, which creates confusion. Thailand, Vietnam and Myanmar all have coastlines; Laos is the only landlocked country in Southeast Asia, so the answer is Laos.
Source: PYQ-style

10. Mount Kilimanjaro, the highest peak of Africa, is located in:
(a) Tanzania (b) Kenya (c) Ethiopia (d) Uganda
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kenya is the tempting wrong answer because Kilimanjaro is best viewed from the Kenyan side and lies near the border. The mountain itself is entirely in Tanzania, so the answer is Tanzania.
Source: PYQ-style

11. The International Date Line roughly follows which longitude?
(a) 0° (b) 90°E (c) 90°W (d) 180°
Answer: (d)
Solution: 0° is the trap because candidates mix up the Prime Meridian with the Date Line. The International Date Line roughly follows the 180° longitude, so the answer is 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

12. Which is the smallest continent by area?
(a) Europe (b) South America (c) Antarctica (d) Australia
Answer: (d)
Solution: Europe feels small and is a common wrong pick, but it is larger than Australia. Australia is the smallest continent by land area, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

13. The Suez Canal connects:
(a) Red Sea and Mediterranean Sea (b) Red Sea and Persian Gulf (c) Mediterranean Sea and Black Sea (d) Arabian Sea and Red Sea
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Mediterranean–Black Sea link is the Bosporus/Dardanelles, a frequent confusion. The Suez Canal joins the Mediterranean Sea with the Red Sea, so the answer is Red Sea and Mediterranean Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

14. Which ocean is the deepest on average?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Pacific Ocean (d) Arctic Ocean
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Arctic Ocean is actually the shallowest, a reverse trap. The Pacific Ocean is both the largest and the deepest ocean on average, so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

15. The country known as the "Land of the Rising Sun" is:
(a) China (b) South Korea (c) Thailand (d) Japan
Answer: (d)
Solution: China is sometimes guessed due to its eastern position in Asia, but the name Japan (Nippon) literally means "sun origin". The answer is Japan.
Source: PYQ-style

16. Which of the following lakes is the largest freshwater lake in the world by surface area?
(a) Lake Superior (b) Lake Baikal (c) Lake Victoria (d) Caspian Sea
Answer: (a)
Solution: Two traps here: the Caspian Sea is larger but saline, and Baikal is the largest by volume, not area. By surface area among freshwater lakes, Lake Superior is the largest, so the answer is Lake Superior.
Source: PYQ-style

17. The equator passes through which of the following countries?
(a) Brazil (b) India (c) Egypt (d) Mexico
Answer: (a)
Solution: India is a trap because it lies close to the equator but entirely north of it. The equator passes through Brazil in South America, so the answer is Brazil.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (The Earth: Our Habitat)

18. Which mountain range separates Europe from Asia?
(a) Alps (b) Andes (c) Urals (d) Rockies
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Alps are a famous European range and mislead many, but they lie wholly within Europe. The Ural Mountains form the conventional Europe–Asia boundary, so the answer is Urals.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

19. The Panama Canal connects which two oceans?
(a) Atlantic and Pacific (b) Pacific and Indian (c) Atlantic and Indian (d) Atlantic and Arctic
Answer: (a)
Solution: Candidates sometimes confuse it with the Suez Canal's seas. The Panama Canal links the Atlantic Ocean (via the Caribbean) with the Pacific Ocean, so the answer is Atlantic and Pacific.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

20. Which is the largest island in the world?
(a) Borneo (b) Greenland (c) New Guinea (d) Madagascar
Answer: (b)
Solution: New Guinea and Borneo are huge and tempting, but Greenland (a Danish territory) is the world's largest island — Australia is treated as a continent, so the answer is Greenland.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

21. Consider the following statements:
1. The Tropic of Capricorn passes through Australia.
2. The equator passes through Australia.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Neither 1 nor 2 (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) 1 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — Australia looks close to the equator on maps, but it lies entirely in the Southern Hemisphere, so the equator does not cross it. The Tropic of Capricorn does pass through Australia, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Arrange the following straits from west to east:
1. Strait of Gibraltar
2. Bosphorus
3. Strait of Hormuz
4. Strait of Malacca
(a) 2-1-3-4 (b) 1-2-3-4 (c) 1-3-2-4 (d) 2-1-4-3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The chronological/geographical confusion trap is placing Bosphorus before Gibraltar; Gibraltar is at the western entrance of the Mediterranean, Bosphorus at its eastern end, then Hormuz in the Gulf, then Malacca in Southeast Asia, so the answer is 1-2-3-4.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) Victoria Falls — Zambezi River (b) Angel Falls — Orinoco basin (c) Iguazu Falls — Paraná basin (d) Niagara Falls — St. Lawrence River
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first two pairs are correct and lull candidates into picking "all correct". Niagara Falls lies on the Niagara River connecting Lake Erie and Lake Ontario, not on the St. Lawrence River, so the answer is Niagara Falls — St. Lawrence River.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Consider the following statements about the Dead Sea:
1. It is the lowest point on Earth's land surface.
2. It lies between Israel and Jordan.
3. It is a freshwater lake.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — the Dead Sea is one of the saltiest water bodies on Earth, not freshwater. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Match the following deserts with their continents:
1. Atacama — South America
2. Kalahari — Africa
3. Great Victoria — Australia
4. Sonoran — North America
How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?
(a) Only one (b) All four (c) Only three (d) Only two
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming at least one pair must be wrong in such matching questions. Atacama (Chile/Peru), Kalahari (southern Africa), Great Victoria (Australia) and Sonoran (USA/Mexico) are all correctly matched, so the answer is All four.
Source: PYQ-style
