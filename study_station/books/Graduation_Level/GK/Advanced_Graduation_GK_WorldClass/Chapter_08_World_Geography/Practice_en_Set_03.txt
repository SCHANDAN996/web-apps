51. Which of the following is the correct order of the Earth's atmospheric layers from the surface upward?
(a) Troposphere, Mesosphere, Stratosphere, Thermosphere, Exosphere (b) Stratosphere, Troposphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere, Exosphere (c) Troposphere, Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere, Exosphere (d) Troposphere, Stratosphere, Thermosphere, Mesosphere, Exosphere
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option swaps the first two layers, but the troposphere (where weather occurs) is the lowest layer, not the stratosphere. The correct sequence from the surface upward is troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, thermosphere, exosphere, so the answer is Troposphere, Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere, Exosphere.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Fundamentals of Physical Geography)

52. The Great Barrier Reef, the world's largest coral reef system, is located off the coast of which country?
(a) Australia (b) South Africa (c) Brazil (d) Indonesia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Indonesia is tempting because it has vast coral reefs in the Coral Triangle, but the Great Barrier Reef lies in the Coral Sea off the north-east coast of Queensland, Australia, so the answer is Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

53. Which of the following rivers does NOT flow into the Mediterranean Sea?
(a) Nile (b) Rhone (c) Ebro (d) Danube
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Danube is tempting because it is a major European river like the Rhone, but it flows eastward and empties into the Black Sea, not the Mediterranean. The Nile, Rhone and Ebro all drain into the Mediterranean, so the answer is Danube.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (World Physical Features)

54. The Tropic of Capricorn passes through which of the following continents?
(a) Africa, Asia and Australia (b) Africa, South America and Australia (c) Asia, South America and Africa (d) North America, Africa and Australia
Answer: (b)
Solution: Asia is the tempting wrong choice because the Tropic of Cancer passes through it, and students often confuse the two tropics. The Tropic of Capricorn (23.5°S) crosses South America, southern Africa and Australia, so the answer is Africa, South America and Australia.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (Globe: Latitudes and Longitudes)

55. Which ocean current is a cold current?
(a) Gulf Stream (b) Kuroshio Current (c) Brazil Current (d) Labrador Current
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Gulf Stream is tempting because it flows in the same North Atlantic region as the Labrador Current, but it is a warm current. The Labrador Current carries cold water southward from the Arctic along the Canadian coast, so the answer is Labrador Current.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Movements of Ocean Water)

56. The world's deepest oceanic trench, the Mariana Trench, is located in which ocean?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Arctic Ocean (d) Pacific Ocean
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Indian Ocean is tempting because it contains the Java (Sunda) Trench, but the Mariana Trench, with the Challenger Deep as the deepest known point, lies in the western Pacific Ocean near the Mariana Islands, so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Distribution of Oceans and Continents)

57. Which of the following pairs of countries is separated by the Bering Strait?
(a) Russia and the United States (b) Russia and Japan (c) Canada and Greenland (d) Norway and Russia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Russia and Japan is tempting because they are close neighbours in the same region, but they are separated by the Sea of Okhotsk and the La Perouse Strait. The Bering Strait separates Siberia (Russia) from Alaska (United States), so the answer is Russia and the United States.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

58. The Pampas, temperate grasslands known for wheat and cattle rearing, are found in which country?
(a) Kazakhstan (b) Argentina (c) South Africa (d) Canada
Answer: (b)
Solution: South Africa is tempting because it has the temperate grasslands called the Velds, which are similar in character. The Pampas, however, are the temperate grasslands of Argentina and Uruguay in South America, so the answer is Argentina.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Life in the Temperate Grasslands)

59. Which of the following is the largest desert in the world by area?
(a) Antarctic Desert (b) Arabian Desert (c) Sahara Desert (d) Gobi Desert
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Sahara is the classic tempting wrong answer because it is the largest hot desert, but deserts are defined by low precipitation, not heat. The Antarctic Desert, a polar desert, is the largest desert in the world by area, so the answer is Antarctic Desert.
Source: PYQ-style

60. The Prime Meridian (0° longitude) passes through which of the following countries?
(a) United Kingdom, Belgium and Libya (b) Germany, Italy and Egypt (c) Portugal, Morocco and Nigeria (d) France, Spain and Algeria
Answer: (d)
Solution: The United Kingdom option is tempting because the Prime Meridian passes through Greenwich in London, but it does not pass through Belgium or Libya. The meridian runs through the United Kingdom, France, Spain, Algeria, Mali, Burkina Faso, Togo and Ghana, so the answer is France, Spain and Algeria.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (Globe: Latitudes and Longitudes)

61. Which of the following lakes is the deepest in the world?
(a) Lake Superior (b) Caspian Sea (c) Lake Baikal (d) Lake Tanganyika
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Caspian Sea is tempting because it is the world's largest lake by area, but area and depth are different measures. Lake Baikal in Siberia, Russia, is the deepest lake in the world and also holds the largest volume of fresh water, so the answer is Lake Baikal.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

62. The Strait of Malacca connects which two water bodies?
(a) The Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman (b) The Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden (c) The Andaman Sea and the South China Sea (d) The Mediterranean Sea and the Black Sea
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman option is tempting because that passage (the Strait of Hormuz) is also a famous oil chokepoint. The Strait of Malacca, between Malaysia and Sumatra, links the Andaman Sea with the South China Sea, so the answer is the Andaman Sea and the South China Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

63. Which of the following is an example of a fold mountain?
(a) Vosges Mountains (b) Harz Mountains (c) Black Forest Mountains (d) Himalayas
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Vosges is tempting because it is a well-known European mountain, but it is a block (fault-block) mountain, as are the Black Forest and Harz ranges. The Himalayas were formed by the folding of sediments due to the collision of the Indian and Eurasian plates, so the answer is Himalayas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Landforms and their Evolution)

64. The country known as the "Land of the Rising Sun" is:
(a) China (b) Japan (c) South Korea (d) Thailand
Answer: (b)
Solution: China is tempting because it is also an East Asian country, but the name refers to Japan's position east of the Asian mainland, where the sun appears to rise first; the Japanese name Nippon means "origin of the sun", so the answer is Japan.
Source: PYQ-style

65. Which of the following rivers forms part of the border between the United States and Mexico?
(a) Colorado River (b) Columbia River (c) Mississippi River (d) Rio Grande
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Colorado River is tempting because it also flows through the south-western United States and into Mexico, but it does not form the international border. The Rio Grande forms a long stretch of the boundary between the United States and Mexico, so the answer is Rio Grande.

66. The "Ring of Fire", a zone of frequent earthquakes and volcanic eruptions, is associated with which ocean?
(a) Atlantic Ocean (b) Indian Ocean (c) Pacific Ocean (d) Arctic Ocean
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Atlantic Ocean is tempting because it has the Mid-Atlantic Ridge with volcanic islands like Iceland, but that is a divergent boundary, not the Ring of Fire. The Circum-Pacific belt around the Pacific Ocean accounts for most of the world's earthquakes and active volcanoes, so the answer is Pacific Ocean.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Distribution of Oceans and Continents)

67. Which of the following African countries does the Equator pass through?
(a) Kenya (b) Nigeria (c) Ethiopia (d) Ghana
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nigeria and Ghana are tempting because they lie close to the Equator along the Gulf of Guinea, but the Equator passes just south of them. In Africa it crosses Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda, Kenya and Somalia, so the answer is Kenya.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Geography (Globe: Latitudes and Longitudes)

68. The world's largest producer of coffee among the following is:
(a) Brazil (b) Vietnam (c) Colombia (d) Ethiopia
Answer: (a)
Solution: Colombia is tempting because of its strong association with premium coffee, and Vietnam is a leading producer of robusta, but Brazil has long been the world's largest coffee producer overall, so the answer is Brazil.

69. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of the formation of these features, from oldest to youngest?
(a) Aravalli, Deccan Plateau, Himalayas (b) Himalayas, Aravalli, Deccan Plateau (c) Deccan Plateau, Aravalli, Himalayas (d) Himalayas, Deccan Plateau, Aravalli
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Himalayas-first option is tempting because they are the most prominent mountains, but they are actually the youngest fold mountains. The Aravalli range is among the oldest fold mountains, the Deccan Plateau formed from ancient lava flows, and the Himalayas rose most recently, so the answer is Aravalli, Deccan Plateau, Himalayas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (Structure and Physiography)

70. The Suez Canal connects the Mediterranean Sea with which of the following?
(a) The Persian Gulf (b) The Black Sea (c) The Red Sea (d) The Arabian Sea directly
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Arabian Sea is tempting because ships from the Suez route do reach it, but they do so via the Red Sea and the Bab-el-Mandeb strait, not directly. The Suez Canal, opened in 1869, joins the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea, so the answer is the Red Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

71. Which of the following climate types is characterised by hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters?
(a) Mediterranean climate (b) Tropical monsoon climate (c) Tundra climate (d) Equatorial climate
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tropical monsoon climate is tempting because it also has a marked wet season, but its rain falls in summer, not winter. Winter rainfall with dry summers is the defining feature of the Mediterranean climate, found around the Mediterranean Sea and on the western margins of continents, so the answer is Mediterranean climate.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (World Climate)

72. The country with the largest number of time zones in the world (including overseas territories) is:
(a) Russia (b) United States (c) France (d) United Kingdom
Answer: (c)
Solution: Russia is the tempting wrong answer because it spans 11 time zones on its mainland alone, the most of any single contiguous territory. However, counting overseas territories, France covers the most time zones (12), so the answer is France.
Source: PYQ-style

73. Which of the following is the largest island in the world?
(a) Borneo (b) Greenland (c) New Guinea (d) Madagascar
Answer: (b)

74. The Atacama Desert, one of the driest places on Earth, is located primarily in which country?
(a) Peru (b) Chile (c) Bolivia (d) Argentina
Answer: (b)
Solution: Peru is tempting because the coastal desert extends near its southern border and the Peru Current influences the whole region's aridity. The Atacama Desert lies mainly in northern Chile, between the Andes and the Pacific coast, so the answer is Chile.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography

75. Which of the following pairs is INCORRECTLY matched?
(a) Prairies — North America (b) Downs — South Africa (c) Steppes — Eurasia (d) Savanna — Africa
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a negative question, and the trap is that all four grassland names sound correctly placed. The Downs are the temperate grasslands of Australia, not South Africa, whose grasslands are called the Velds; the Prairies, Steppes and Savanna are correctly matched, so the answer is Downs — South Africa.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography (Life in the Temperate Grasslands)
