# Key Facts & Figures — World Geography (Basic)

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Largest continent | Asia (~44.6 million km², ~30% of Earth's land) | Confusing with Africa (2nd largest) | Near-correct option |
| Smallest continent | Australia (~7.7 million km²) | Confusing with Europe | Near-correct option |
| Largest ocean | Pacific Ocean (~165 million km², ~1/3 of Earth's surface) | Confusing with Atlantic | Near-correct option |
| Deepest point on Earth | Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep, ~10,935 m), Pacific Ocean | Confusing with Puerto Rico Trench (Atlantic) | Similar-name trap |
| Longest river | Nile (~6,650 km, Africa); Amazon is largest by discharge | Nile vs Amazon confusion (length vs volume) | Concept swap |
| Largest desert (hot) | Sahara (Africa, ~9.2 million km²) | Antarctica is the largest desert overall (cold desert) | Negative/exception trap |
| Largest lake (by area) | Caspian Sea (~371,000 km²) — a lake, not a sea | Confusing with Lake Superior (largest freshwater lake) | Category trap |
| Deepest lake | Lake Baikal (Russia, ~1,642 m); also largest freshwater by volume | Confusing with Caspian Sea | Similar-name trap |
| Highest peak | Mount Everest (8,849 m), Nepal–China border | Confusing with K2 (2nd highest, Karakoram) | Near-correct option |
| Highest peak outside Asia | Aconcagua (6,961 m, Andes, Argentina) | Confusing with Kilimanjaro (Africa's highest) | Continent confusion |
| Longest mountain range (land) | Andes (~7,000 km, South America) | Mid-ocean ridge system is longest overall (~65,000 km, underwater) | Category trap |
| Largest island | Greenland (~2.16 million km²) | Australia is a continent, not an island | Definition trap |
| Largest country (area) | Russia (~17.1 million km², spans Europe + Asia) | Confusing with Canada (2nd) | Near-correct option |
| Smallest country | Vatican City (~0.49 km²) | Confusing with Monaco (2nd smallest) | Near-correct option |
| Country with most neighbours | China and Russia (14 each) | Options often give only one of the two | Incomplete-option trap |
| Equator passes through | 13 countries; in South America: Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil | Claiming it passes through India/Australia | False-statement trap |
| Tropic of Cancer passes through | 16 countries incl. India (8 states) | Miscounting Indian states (8: Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, WB, Tripura, Mizoram) | Counting trap |
| Prime Meridian | 0° longitude, passes through Greenwich (UK) | Confusing with International Date Line (≈180°) | Concept swap |
| International Date Line | ≈180° longitude, zigzags through Pacific | Assuming it is a straight line | Assumption trap |
| Largest archipelago country | Indonesia (~17,000+ islands) | Confusing with Philippines or Japan | Near-correct option |
| Ring of Fire | Pacific Ocean rim — ~75% of world's volcanoes, ~90% of earthquakes | Associating it with Atlantic | Location swap |
| Largest coral reef | Great Barrier Reef (Australia, ~2,300 km) | Confusing with Belize Barrier Reef (2nd) | Near-correct option |
| Largest hot-spring/geyser region | Yellowstone (USA) | — | — |
| Dead Sea | Lowest point on land (~430 m below sea level); bordered by Jordan, Israel, West Bank | Confusing with Death Valley (lowest in N. America) | Similar-name trap |
| Largest delta | Sundarbans (Ganga–Brahmaputra, India–Bangladesh) | Confusing with Nile delta | Near-correct option |
| Largest rainforest | Amazon (South America, mostly Brazil) | Confusing with Congo rainforest (2nd) | Near-correct option |
| Driest place (hot desert) | Atacama Desert (Chile) | Confusing with Sahara | Near-correct option |
| Coldest inhabited place | Oymyakon (Russia, Siberia) | Confusing with Verkhoyansk | Similar-name trap |
| Windiest region on Earth | Antarctica (katabatic winds) | — | — |
| Largest peninsula | Arabian Peninsula | Confusing with Indian peninsula | Near-correct option |
| Largest gulf | Gulf of Mexico | Confusing with Persian Gulf | Near-correct option |
| Largest bay | Bay of Bengal | Confusing with Hudson Bay (by some measures) | Category trap |
| Strait between Asia & N. America | Bering Strait (also where Date Line bends) | Confusing with Bosphorus (Europe–Asia) | Similar-name trap |
| Strait of Gibraltar | Connects Mediterranean Sea ↔ Atlantic Ocean; separates Spain & Morocco | Confusing with Strait of Hormuz (Persian Gulf ↔ Gulf of Oman) | Location swap |
| Suez Canal | Connects Mediterranean ↔ Red Sea (193 km, opened 1869) | Confusing with Panama Canal (Atlantic ↔ Pacific, opened 1914) | Pair confusion |
| Palk Strait | Between India and Sri Lanka | Confusing with Malacca Strait (Malaysia–Indonesia) | Location swap |
| Largest landlocked country | Kazakhstan | Confusing with Mongolia (2nd largest landlocked) | Near-correct option |
| Country crossed by both Equator and Tropic of Capricorn | Brazil | Options claiming a country crossed by all three lines (Equator + both tropics) — none exists | Exaggeration trap |
| Earth's shape | Oblate spheroid (geoid) — flattened at poles | "Perfect sphere" option | False-statement trap |
| Latitude of Arctic Circle | 66.5° N; Antarctic Circle 66.5° S | Swapping N/S or 23.5°/66.5° | Number swap |
| Tropics | Cancer 23.5° N, Capricorn 23.5° S | Swapping hemispheres | Number swap |
| Longest day in Northern Hemisphere | 21 June (Summer Solstice) | Confusing with 22 December (shortest day in N. Hemisphere) | Date swap |
| Equinoxes | ~21 March and ~23 September (equal day-night) | Confusing with solstices | Concept swap |
| Time zones | 1 hour per 15° longitude; Earth has 24 standard zones | Calculation errors with Date Line | Calculation trap |
| Largest producer of coffee | Brazil | Confusing with Vietnam (2nd, largest robusta) | Near-correct option |
| Largest producer of rice & wheat | China (rice); China also leads wheat | Confusing with India (2nd in both) | Near-correct option |
| Largest gold producer | China | Confusing with Australia/Russia | Near-correct option |
| Country with longest coastline | Canada (~202,000 km) | Confusing with Indonesia/Russia | Near-correct option |
| Only city spanning two continents | Istanbul (Turkey — Europe & Asia) | Options may name Turkey as a "two-continent city" — it is a country; the city is Istanbul | Category trap |
| Continent with no desert | Europe | "Every continent has a desert" option | False-statement trap |
| Continent crossed by all lines of longitude | Antarctica | Confusing with Arctic Ocean | Location swap |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Category | Question Type | Time Rule | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Superlatives (largest, longest, deepest, highest) | Direct fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes — high frequency, zero calculation |
| Straits, canals, isthmuses | Fact recall + map memory | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Rivers, lakes, deserts | Fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Latitude/longitude & time-zone calculation | Analytical (degree-to-time conversion) | 45 sec | ⚠️ Attempt only if formula is clear |
| Solstice/equinox, day-length questions | Conceptual recall | 15–30 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Statement-based questions (multiple statements) | Analytical — each statement verified | 45 sec | ⚠️ Trap-heavy; attempt with caution |
| Match-the-following (country–capital–currency, strait–countries) | Fact recall | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Climate/vegetation region questions | Conceptual | 30–45 sec | ⚠️ Moderate |
| "Which is NOT correct" questions | Analytical — negative framing | 45 sec | ⚠️ Read twice; negative words are the trap |

## Part 3: Data & Formulas

| Item | Value / Formula | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Earth's equatorial radius | ~6,378 km | Polar radius ~6,357 km (difference proves oblate shape) |
| Earth's surface: water vs land | ~71% water, ~29% land | Oceans hold ~97% of Earth's water |
| Time-zone conversion | 15° longitude = 1 hour; 1° = 4 minutes | East = add time, West = subtract |
| Longitude degrees | 180° E + 180° W = 360° total | Latitude: 0° to 90° N/S |
| Tropic of Cancer / Capricorn | 23.5° N / 23.5° S | Earth's axial tilt = 23.5° |
| Arctic / Antarctic Circle | 66.5° N / 66.5° S | 90° − 23.5° = 66.5° |
| Mount Everest height | 8,849 m (revised 2020, Nepal–China joint survey) | Older books say 8,848 m |
| Mariana Trench depth | ~10,935 m (Challenger Deep) | Often rounded to ~11,000 m |
| Nile length | ~6,650 km | Amazon ~6,400 km but largest by discharge |
| Sahara area | ~9.2 million km² | Antarctica ~14 million km² (largest cold desert) |
| Caspian Sea area | ~371,000 km² | Lake Superior ~82,100 km² (largest freshwater by area) |
| Lake Baikal depth | ~1,642 m | Holds ~20% of world's unfrozen fresh water |
| Dead Sea elevation | ~430 m below sea level | Lowest land point on Earth |
| Andes length | ~7,000 km | Longest continental mountain range |
| Great Barrier Reef length | ~2,300 km | Off Queensland, Australia |
| Suez Canal | 193 km, opened 1869 | Panama Canal: 82 km, opened 1914 |
| Countries on Equator | 13 | Incl. Ecuador, Brazil, Kenya, Indonesia |
| Tropic of Cancer: Indian states | 8 | Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram |
| Standard meridian of India | 82.5° E (Mirzapur, UP) | IST = UTC +5:30 |
