76. Which of the following best defines an ecosystem?
(a) A group of organisms of the same species living together (b) The physical environment excluding all living organisms (c) The total area occupied by a community of plants (d) A functional unit of nature where living organisms interact among themselves and with the physical environment
Answer: (d)
Solution: Option (a) describes a population, not an ecosystem — a common trap. An ecosystem includes both biotic and abiotic components interacting as a functional unit, so the answer is a functional unit of nature where living organisms interact among themselves and with the physical environment.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

77. In a food chain, the maximum concentration of a non-biodegradable pesticide like DDT is found in:
(a) Top carnivores (b) Primary consumers (c) Producers (d) Decomposers
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many students pick decomposers because DDT "ends up" in soil, but biomagnification means the concentration rises at each successive trophic level. The highest accumulation occurs in top carnivores, so the answer is top carnivores.

78. The 10% law of energy transfer in food chains was proposed by:
(a) Raymond Lindeman (b) Charles Elton (c) E.P. Odum (d) Arthur Tansley
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tansley coined the term "ecosystem" and Elton gave the ecological pyramid concept — both are tempting distractors. The 10% energy transfer law was given by Raymond Lindeman (1942), so the answer is Raymond Lindeman.

79. Which ecological pyramid is always upright?
(a) Pyramid of biomass in a pond ecosystem (b) Pyramid of energy (c) Pyramid of numbers in a parasitic food chain (d) Pyramid of biomass in a grassland
Answer: (b)
Solution: The pyramid of biomass can be inverted in aquatic ecosystems and the pyramid of numbers can be inverted in parasitic chains — these are classic traps. The pyramid of energy is always upright because energy decreases at every trophic level, so the answer is pyramid of energy.

(a) The water is pure and clean (b) Dissolved oxygen increases (c) Organic pollution in the water increases (d) The temperature of water decreases
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming high BOD means high oxygen availability; actually high BOD means microbes consume more oxygen to decompose organic waste. BOD rises with organic pollution, so the answer is that organic pollution in the water increases.
