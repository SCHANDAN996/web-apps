# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Environment & Ecology (Basic)

## The Most Confusing Concept: Food Chain vs Food Web vs Trophic Level — and the 10% Energy Law

Most students mix these three up, and examiners love it. Let's fix it forever, the way you'd explain it to a 12-year-old.

### Imagine a Pizza Party 🍕

Think of energy in nature as **pizza slices** being passed along a line of hungry friends.

- **The Sun** is the pizza shop — the only real source of energy.
- **Plants (producers)** are the first friends who get the whole pizza. They "cook" sunlight into food through photosynthesis.
- **The deer (herbivore)** eats the plant — it gets the pizza next.
- **The tiger (carnivore)** eats the deer — last in line.

Now here's the catch: **every time the pizza passes to the next friend, 90% of it is gone!** Each friend uses most of the energy to run, breathe, stay warm, and digest — only about **10% is stored in the body** and passed on when eaten.

So if plants capture 10,000 units of energy:
- Deer gets only **1,000 units**
- Tiger gets only **100 units**
- An eagle eating the tiger would get just **10 units**

This is the **10% Law** given by **Lindeman (1942)**.

### Now the three terms become easy:

1. **Food Chain** = ONE straight line of who-eats-whom.
   *Grass → Deer → Tiger.* Simple, single path.

2. **Food Web** = MANY food chains tangled together, like a spider's web.
   In reality, a deer eats many plants, and a tiger eats deer, rabbits, and birds. Real nature is a **web**, not a chain — that's why removing one species shakes the whole web.

3. **Trophic Level** = each *step* on the energy staircase.
   - T1: Producers (plants)
   - T2: Primary consumers (herbivores)
   - T3: Secondary consumers (carnivores)
   - T4: Tertiary consumers (top carnivores)

### Why can't food chains be very long?

Because energy runs out! After 4–5 trophic levels, so little energy is left that it can't support another level. That's why there are no "super-top" predators eating tigers in large numbers.

### 🪄 Unforgettable Trick: **"PIZZA-10"**

> **P**lants make it → **I**t passes on → **Z**apped 90% at each step → **Z**ero left after 4–5 levels → **A**lways a web, never just a chain → **10%** is all that moves up.

And remember the pyramid: **Energy pyramid is ALWAYS upright** (can never be inverted), because energy must decrease at every level. Number and biomass pyramids *can* be inverted (e.g., one tree supporting thousands of insects), but energy — never.

### ⚠️ Examiner's Trap

- "10% energy is **lost**" — ❌ Wrong. 90% is lost/used; **10% is transferred**.
- "Food chain is more realistic than food web" — ❌ Wrong. The **web** is the real picture of nature.
- "Energy pyramid can be inverted in a pond ecosystem" — ❌ Wrong. Energy pyramids are **never** inverted, anywhere.

## ❓ Question for You

If producers in an ecosystem store 1,00,000 kcal of energy, how much energy will reach the **tertiary consumer (T4)**? And why does this explain why top carnivores like tigers are always few in number?

*(Answer: 1,00,000 → 10,000 (T2) → 1,000 (T3) → **100 kcal (T4)**. So little energy reaches the top that only a few tigers can survive in a forest — which is also why their conservation is so critical.)*
