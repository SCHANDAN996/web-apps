# Key Facts & Figures

## 1. High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Indus Valley Civilization | Mature Harappan era c. 2600–1900 BCE; Early Harappan c. 3300–2600 BCE; Late Harappan c. 1900–1300 BCE | Exams swap Early and Mature phase dates — remember Mature = 2600–1900 BCE |
| Discovery | Harappa excavated in 1921 (Daya Ram Sahni); Mohenjo-daro in 1922 (R. D. Banerji), under Director-General John Marshall | Harappa was found first, but Mohenjo-daro is the larger site — questions mix up "first discovered" vs "largest" |
| Location | Along the Indus river system and its tributaries; covered parts of present-day Pakistan, India (Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Gujarat) and Afghanistan | It is also called the Harappan Civilization after Harappa, the first site excavated — not after Mohenjo-daro |
| Town planning | Grid-pattern streets, baked-brick houses, covered drains, the Great Bath (Mohenjo-daro) and granaries | The Great Bath is at Mohenjo-daro, not Harappa — a classic trap |
| Dockyard | Lothal (Gujarat) had a dockyard, showing maritime trade | Lothal = dockyard; Kalibangan = ploughed field; Dholavira = water reservoirs — sites and their specialties are frequently swapped |
| Script | Pictographic script, still undeciphered; written mostly right to left | Any option claiming the script "has been deciphered" is wrong |
| Religion | Worship of a Mother Goddess and a deity resembling Pashupati (proto-Shiva); no temples found | "Large temples were found" is a false statement — no clear temple structures exist |
| Metals | People knew copper, bronze, gold and silver, but not iron | Iron was unknown to the Harappans — iron belongs to the later Vedic period |
| Agriculture | Wheat and barley were the main crops; evidence of cotton cultivation (among the earliest in the world) | Rice was not a main Harappan crop — a common distractor |
| Decline | Civilization declined around 1900 BCE; causes debated (climate change, drying of rivers, floods) — the Aryan invasion theory is no longer widely accepted | Options stating a single proven cause of decline are traps; the exact cause remains uncertain |

## 2. Important Sites and Findings

| Site | River | Known for |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Harappa | Ravi | Granaries, cemetery |
| Mohenjo-daro | Indus | Great Bath, large granary, bronze "Dancing Girl" |
| Lothal | Bhogava (Gujarat) | Dockyard, bead-making |
| Kalibangan | Ghaggar (Rajasthan) | Ploughed field, fire altars |
| Dholavira | Gujarat (Rann of Kutch) | Water management, reservoirs; UNESCO World Heritage Site (2021) |
| Chanhudaro | Indus (Sindh) | Bead-making factory; no citadel |
| Banawali | Ghaggar (Haryana) | Barley evidence, terracotta plough model |

## 3. Quick Numbers

- Mature Harappan period: **c. 2600–1900 BCE**
- Year of Harappa's excavation: **1921**
- Year of Mohenjo-daro's excavation: **1922**
- Director-General of ASI at the time of discovery: **John Marshall**
- Dholavira declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site: **2021**
