26. The 'Dashavatara' panels depicting the ten incarnations of Vishnu are a famous sculptural feature of which temple?
(a) Lingaraja Temple, Bhubaneswar (b) Shore Temple, Mamallapuram (c) Deogarh Temple, Uttar Pradesh (d) Kandariya Mahadeva Temple, Khajuraho
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Shore Temple is Pallava, Lingaraja is Kalinga style, and Khajuraho is Chandela — none is known as the Dashavatara temple, which is the tempting confusion. The Gupta-period Vishnu Temple at Deogarh is renowned for its large relief panels of Vishnu's incarnations, including the Sheshashayi Vishnu panel, so the answer is Deogarh Temple, Uttar Pradesh.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

27. Which of the following pairs of classical dance and its state of origin is correctly matched?
(a) Mohiniyattam — Tamil Nadu (b) Sattriya — Assam (c) Kuchipudi — Odisha (d) Kathakali — Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that Mohiniyattam and Kathakali are often swapped between Tamil Nadu and Kerala; both actually belong to Kerala, and Kuchipudi belongs to Andhra Pradesh, not Odisha. Sattriya, introduced by the Vaishnavite saint Srimanta Sankaradeva, is the classical dance of Assam, so the answer is Sattriya — Assam.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

28. The Gandhara school of art is distinguished from the Mathura school primarily because Gandhara art:
(a) used only red sandstone (b) depicted only Jain themes (c) showed strong Greco-Roman influence and used bluish-grey schist (d) flourished exclusively under the Guptas
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option reverses the facts — red sandstone was the hallmark of the Mathura school, not Gandhara. Gandhara art, under Indo-Greek and Kushana patronage, blended Buddhist themes with Greco-Roman realism and used bluish-grey schist stone, so the answer is that it showed strong Greco-Roman influence and used bluish-grey schist.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

29. The 'Gita Govinda', which inspired the Odissi dance tradition and the Pahari school of painting, was composed by:
(a) Jayadeva (b) Bilhana (c) Banabhatta (d) Kalidasa
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kalidasa is a tempting distractor because of his fame, but he wrote Abhijnanashakuntalam and Meghaduta, not the Gita Govinda. The 12th-century poet Jayadeva composed the Gita Govinda celebrating Radha and Krishna, so the answer is Jayadeva.
Source: NCERT Class 12, Themes in Indian History

30. Which Mughal emperor's reign is regarded as the golden age of Mughal painting, with artists like Bishan Das and Mansur?
(a) Jahangir (b) Shah Jahan (c) Akbar (d) Aurangzeb
Answer: (a)
Solution: Akbar is the tempting wrong answer because he founded the Mughal atelier and commissioned the Hamzanama, but painting reached its refined peak — especially natural history studies by Mansur — under Jahangir, so the answer is Jahangir.
Source: NCERT Class 12, Themes in Indian History Part II

31. The 'Chaar Baagh' (four-part garden) layout in Mughal architecture is best exemplified in India by the:
(a) Buland Darwaza (b) Humayun's Tomb (c) Qutub Minar complex (d) Jami Masjid, Fatehpur Sikri
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Buland Darwaza and Jami Masjid tempt students because both are at Fatehpur Sikri, but they are a gateway and a mosque, not garden tombs. Humayun's Tomb in Delhi is set in a classic charbagh divided by water channels, so the answer is Humayun's Tomb.
Source: NCERT Class 12, Themes in Indian History Part II

32. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of these architectural monuments?
(a) Sanchi Stupa → Ajanta Caves → Brihadeeswara Temple → Qutub Minar (b) Ajanta Caves → Sanchi Stupa → Qutub Minar → Brihadeeswara Temple (c) Sanchi Stupa → Brihadeeswara Temple → Ajanta Caves → Qutub Minar (d) Qutub Minar → Sanchi Stupa → Ajanta Caves → Brihadeeswara Temple
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is chronological confusion: Sanchi's Great Stupa under Ashoka (3rd century BCE) predates the main Ajanta activity, and the Brihadeeswara Temple (1010 CE, Rajaraja I) precedes the Qutub Minar (begun 1199 CE). The correct sequence is Sanchi Stupa → Ajanta Caves → Brihadeeswara Temple → Qutub Minar, so the answer is Sanchi Stupa → Ajanta Caves → Brihadeeswara Temple → Qutub Minar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 & 12, An Introduction to Indian Art / Themes in Indian History

33. The 'Kalamkari' art form, involving hand-painting or block-printing on cotton textiles with a 'kalam' (pen), is traditionally associated with:
(a) Rajasthan (b) Gujarat (c) Andhra Pradesh (d) West Bengal
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gujarat and Rajasthan tempt students because both are famous for textile printing traditions like Bandhani and Sanganer block prints, but Kalamkari — centred at Srikalahasti and Machilipatnam — belongs to Andhra Pradesh, so the answer is Andhra Pradesh.
Source: Ministry of Textiles, Government of India (handicraft documentation)

34. In Hindustani classical music, a 'gharana' refers to:
(a) the opening section of a raga (b) a type of percussion instrument (c) a musical composition in Sanskrit (d) a stylistic school or lineage of music
Answer: (d)
Solution: The opening section of a raga is the 'alap', which is the tempting wrong option; students often confuse the two terms. A gharana is a hereditary stylistic school of Hindustani music, such as Gwalior, Kirana or Jaipur, so the answer is a stylistic school or lineage of music.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

35. The famous 'Dancing Girl' bronze statuette of the Indus Valley Civilization was made using which technique?
(a) Sand casting (b) Terracotta moulding (c) Repoussé work (d) Lost-wax (cire perdue) casting
Answer: (d)
Solution: Terracotta moulding is tempting because the Harappans made many terracotta figurines, but the Dancing Girl from Mohenjo-daro is a bronze piece cast by the lost-wax process, so the answer is lost-wax (cire perdue) casting.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

36. Which of the following temples is NOT built in the Nagara style of architecture?
(a) Kandariya Mahadeva Temple (b) Lingaraja Temple (c) Brihadeeswara Temple, Thanjavur (d) Sun Temple, Konark
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap lies in assuming all famous large temples are Nagara; the Brihadeeswara Temple of Thanjavur, built by Rajaraja Chola I, is a prime example of the Dravida (South Indian) style with its vimana, while the other three are Nagara temples, so the answer is Brihadeeswara Temple, Thanjavur.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

37. The 'Phad' scroll painting tradition, depicting folk deities like Pabuji and Devnarayan, belongs to:
(a) Madhya Pradesh (b) Bihar (c) Rajasthan (d) Odisha
Answer: (c)
Solution: Madhubani of Bihar and Pattachitra of Odisha are the tempting distractors since they are also famous folk painting styles, but Phad scroll painting is practised by the Bhopa singers of Rajasthan, so the answer is Rajasthan.
Source: Ministry of Culture, Government of India

38. The Sangeet Natak Akademi, the national academy for music, dance and drama, was established in which year?
(a) 1953 (b) 1955 (c) 1950 (d) 1952
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 1953, the year the Akademi was formally inaugurated and began functioning, which many sources loosely cite; however, it was established by a Government of India resolution in 1952 as the first of the national academies. The Sahitya Akademi and Lalit Kala Akademi followed in 1954, so the answer is 1952.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi, official website

39. Which classical dance form originated in the monasteries (sattras) of Assam as a medium of Vaishnavite worship?
(a) Manipuri (b) Sattriya (c) Odissi (d) Kathak
Answer: (b)
Solution: Manipuri is tempting because it is also a Vaishnavite dance from the North-East, but the monastic origin in the sattras established by Sankaradeva identifies Sattriya, so the answer is Sattriya.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

40. The 'Vesara' style of temple architecture, a hybrid of Nagara and Dravida styles, is chiefly associated with the:
(a) Chalukyas of Badami (b) Pallavas of Kanchi (c) Cholas of Thanjavur (d) Chandellas of Khajuraho
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Pallavas and Cholas are tempting because they were great southern builders, but their work is firmly Dravida. The Vesara (Deccan hybrid) style is associated with the Chalukyas of Badami and later the Rashtrakutas and Hoysalas, so the answer is Chalukyas of Badami.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

41. The 'Bharata Natyam' dance in its modern stage form was systematised by:
(a) Rukmini Devi Arundale (b) Balasaraswati (c) The Tanjore Quartet (d) Yamini Krishnamurthy
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rukmini Devi Arundale is the tempting wrong answer because she revived and popularised the dance and founded Kalakshetra, but the modern repertoire (margam) was codified in the early 19th century by the four brothers known as the Tanjore Quartet, so the answer is the Tanjore Quartet.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

42. The Elephanta Caves, famous for the 'Trimurti' sculpture of Shiva, are located in:
(a) Karnataka (b) Gujarat (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Maharashtra
Answer: (d)
Solution: Karnataka is tempting because of the Badami cave temples which also have Shaiva sculptures, but the Elephanta Caves lie on an island in Mumbai harbour, Maharashtra, so the answer is Maharashtra.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

43. Which of the following folk theatres is correctly matched with its state?
(a) Jatra — Punjab (b) Tamasha — Maharashtra (c) Nautanki — Kerala (d) Bhavai — Assam
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is regional shuffling: Jatra belongs to West Bengal and Odisha, Nautanki to Uttar Pradesh, and Bhavai to Gujarat. Tamasha is the traditional folk theatre of Maharashtra, so the answer is Tamasha — Maharashtra.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

44. The 'Pattachitra' painting tradition, done on cloth with mythological themes, is native to:
(a) Telangana (b) Goa (c) Himachal Pradesh (d) Odisha
Answer: (d)
Solution: Telangana tempts students because of Cheriyal scroll paintings, which are also narrative cloth paintings, but Pattachitra — especially of Raghurajpur village — is the classical cloth painting of Odisha, so the answer is Odisha.
Source: Ministry of Culture, Government of India

45. The 'Khajuraho' group of temples, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, were built by the rulers of which dynasty?
(a) Chandella (b) Pratihara (c) Paramara (d) Solanki
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Paramaras are tempting because they also ruled parts of central India and built at places like Ujjain and Dhar, but the Khajuraho temples (c. 950–1050 CE) were built by the Chandella dynasty, so the answer is Chandella.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

46. In Carnatic music, the 'Trinity' refers to Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar and:
(a) Purandara Dasa (b) Syama Sastri (c) Swati Tirunal (d) Annamacharya
Answer: (b)
Solution: Purandara Dasa is the tempting wrong answer because he is called the 'father of Carnatic music', but he predates the Trinity. The Carnatic Trinity comprises Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar and Syama Sastri, so the answer is Syama Sastri.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

47. The 'Lion Capital' of Ashoka, adopted as the National Emblem of India, was originally erected at:
(a) Bodh Gaya (b) Kushinagar (c) Sanchi (d) Sarnath
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sanchi is the tempting wrong answer because of its famous Ashokan-era stupa and gateways, but the four-lion capital crowned the Ashokan pillar at Sarnath, where the Buddha gave his first sermon, so the answer is Sarnath.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

48. Which of the following statements about the Ajanta paintings is correct?
(a) They are frescoes painted on wet plaster (b) They are tempera paintings on dry plaster depicting Jataka tales (c) They are oil paintings from the Gupta court (d) They depict only scenes from the Ramayana
Answer: (b)
Solution: The fresco option is the classic trap: Ajanta paintings are often loosely called frescoes, but technically they were executed in tempera on dry plaster, and their themes are Buddhist Jataka tales, not the Ramayana, so the answer is that they are tempera paintings on dry plaster depicting Jataka tales.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

49. The 'Kuchipudi' dance-drama tradition takes its name from a village in which present-day state?
(a) Andhra Pradesh (b) Karnataka (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Telangana
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tamil Nadu is tempting because of the proximity and shared classical dance heritage, but Kuchipudi village lies in the Krishna district region of Andhra Pradesh, where the dance-drama tradition developed, so the answer is Andhra Pradesh.
Source: NCERT Class 11, An Introduction to Indian Art

50. The 'Modhera Sun Temple' in Gujarat, known for its Surya Kund stepwell, was built during the rule of which dynasty?
(a) Solanki (Chaulukya) (b) Vaghela (c) Rashtrakuta (d) Maitraka
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Vaghelas are tempting because they succeeded the Solankis in Gujarat and built at places like Dabhoi, but the Modhera Sun Temple was built in the 11th century under Bhima I of the Solanki (Chaulukya) dynasty, so the answer is Solanki (Chaulukya).
Source: Archaeological Survey of India
