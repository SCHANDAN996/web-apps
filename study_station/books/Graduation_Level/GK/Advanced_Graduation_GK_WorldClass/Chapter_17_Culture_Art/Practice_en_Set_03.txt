51. The famous 'Dashavatara' panel, depicting the ten incarnations of Vishnu, is carved in which cave of Ellora?
(a) Cave 10 (b) Cave 15 (c) Cave 16 (d) Cave 21
Answer: (b)
Solution: Cave 16 (Kailasa temple) is the tempting wrong answer because it is the most famous Ellora cave, but the Dashavatara panel is carved in Cave 15, the Dashavatara cave. Cave 10 is a Buddhist chaitya hall. So the answer is Cave 15.

52. Which of the following pairs of classical dance and its traditional region is correctly matched?

Solution: The trap is Kuchipudi–Tamil Nadu, which looks plausible because Bharatanatyam is from Tamil Nadu; Kuchipudi actually belongs to Andhra Pradesh. Sattriya is from Assam, not Manipur, and Kathak is from North India. Mohiniyattam is the classical dance of Kerala, so the answer is Mohiniyattam – Kerala.
