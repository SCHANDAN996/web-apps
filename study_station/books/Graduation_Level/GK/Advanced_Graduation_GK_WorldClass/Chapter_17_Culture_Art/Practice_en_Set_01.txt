1. The famous rock-cut Kailasa temple at Ellora was built during the reign of which dynasty?
(a) Chalukyas (b) Cholas (c) Pallavas (d) Rashtrakutas
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kailasa temple (Cave 16) at Ellora, Maharashtra, was carved out of a single rock under the Rashtrakuta king Krishna I in the 8th century CE. The Chalukyas are associated with Pattadakal and Badami, not Ellora's Kailasa temple, so the answer is Rashtrakutas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

2. Bharatanatyam, one of the oldest classical dance forms of India, originated in which state?
(a) Kerala (b) Andhra Pradesh (c) Karnataka (d) Tamil Nadu
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bharatanatyam originated in the temples of Tamil Nadu and was earlier known as Sadir or Dasi Attam. Kerala is associated with Kathakali and Mohiniyattam, so the answer is Tamil Nadu.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

3. The Ajanta cave paintings mainly depict stories from which texts?
(a) Puranas (b) Upanishads (c) Vedas (d) Jatakas
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Ajanta murals (mostly 2nd century BCE to 6th century CE) illustrate the Jataka tales, which narrate the previous lives of the Buddha. The Puranas belong to a later Hindu tradition, so the answer is Jatakas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

4. Which of the following is a stringed musical instrument?
(a) Tabla (b) Santoor (c) Shehnai (d) Mridangam
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Santoor is a stringed instrument played with wooden mallets, associated with Kashmiri music. Tabla and Mridangam are percussion instruments and Shehnai is a wind instrument, so the answer is Santoor.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

5. The Sanchi Stupa, famous for its gateways (toranas), was originally commissioned by which ruler?
(a) Kanishka (b) Ashoka (c) Harshavardhana (d) Samudragupta
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Great Stupa at Sanchi was originally built by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE; the stone casing and toranas were added later under the Shungas and Satavahanas. Kanishka is linked with the Gandhara school, so the answer is Ashoka.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

6. Madhubani painting, characterised by geometric patterns and natural dyes, is a folk art of which region?
(a) Bundelkhand, Uttar Pradesh (b) Mithila, Bihar (c) Marwar, Rajasthan (d) Vidarbha, Maharashtra
Answer: (b)
Solution: Madhubani (Mithila) painting comes from the Mithila region of Bihar and is traditionally done by women on walls and paper. It is not from Rajasthan or Maharashtra, so the answer is Mithila, Bihar.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Themes in Indian History

7. The Gandhara school of art shows strong influence of which foreign artistic tradition?
(a) Chinese (b) Greco-Roman (c) Persian (d) Egyptian
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Gandhara school (1st–5th century CE) blended Indian themes with Greco-Roman techniques, producing realistic Buddha images with wavy hair and draped robes. The Mathura school was purely indigenous, so the answer is Greco-Roman.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

8. Which classical dance form is famous for its elaborate costumes, face masks and the use of 'Mudras' with stories from the Ramayana and Mahabharata?
(a) Kathak (b) Kathakali (c) Odissi (d) Manipuri
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kathakali of Kerala is known for its heavy make-up, colourful costumes and enactment of epics. Kathak is a North Indian form known for spins and footwork, so the answer is Kathakali.

9. The 'Natya Shastra', the foundational Sanskrit text on performing arts, is attributed to which sage?
(a) Bharata Muni (b) Panini (c) Patanjali (d) Kalidasa
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Natya Shastra, dealing with drama, dance and music, is attributed to Bharata Muni. Panini wrote grammar (Ashtadhyayi) and Patanjali the Mahabhashya and Yoga Sutras, so the answer is Bharata Muni.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

10. The Sun Temple at Konark, built in the shape of a colossal chariot, is located in which state?
(a) Odisha (b) West Bengal (c) Tamil Nadu (d) Gujarat
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Konark Sun Temple was built by Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty in the 13th century in Odisha. The Modhera Sun Temple is in Gujarat, which is a common confusion, so the answer is Odisha.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

11. Which of the following is a classical dance form of Andhra Pradesh?
(a) Mohiniyattam (b) Kuchipudi (c) Sattriya (d) Chhau
Answer: (b)
Solution: Kuchipudi originated in the village of Kuchipudi in Andhra Pradesh and combines dance with dialogue. Sattriya is from Assam and Mohiniyattam from Kerala, so the answer is Kuchipudi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

12. The famous bronze 'Nataraja' (dancing Shiva) sculptures reached their peak under which dynasty?
(a) Pallavas (b) Pandyas (c) Cholas (d) Cheras
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chola dynasty (9th–13th century) perfected the lost-wax (cire perdue) bronze casting technique, producing the iconic Nataraja images. The Pallavas are known for rock-cut architecture at Mamallapuram, so the answer is Cholas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

13. 'Phad' painting, a scroll painting tradition depicting folk deities, belongs to which state?
(a) Rajasthan (b) Gujarat (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Odisha
Answer: (a)
Solution: Phad is a long horizontal scroll painting of Rajasthan depicting deities like Pabuji and Devnarayan. Odisha's scroll tradition is Pattachitra, which is the tempting confusion, so the answer is Rajasthan.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Themes in Indian History

14. The 'Dhrupad' and 'Khayal' are styles associated with which tradition of Indian music?
(a) Hindustani classical music (b) Carnatic music (c) Folk music (d) Sufi music
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dhrupad is the oldest and Khayal the most popular vocal style of Hindustani (North Indian) classical music. Carnatic music uses forms like Kriti and Varnam, so the answer is Hindustani classical music.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

15. The Elephanta Caves, famous for the 'Trimurti' sculpture of Shiva, are located near which city?
(a) Kochi (b) Chennai (c) Kolkata (d) Mumbai
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Elephanta Caves lie on Elephanta Island in Mumbai Harbour, Maharashtra, and date mainly to the 5th–6th century CE. The Trimurti depicts Shiva as creator, preserver and destroyer, so the answer is Mumbai.

16. Which Mughal emperor is regarded as the greatest patron of painting, under whom the Mughal miniature school flourished?
(a) Akbar (b) Babur (c) Aurangzeb (d) Bahadur Shah Zafar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Akbar established the imperial painting atelier with artists like Basawan and Daswanth, and works like the Hamzanama were produced. Aurangzeb discouraged painting, so the answer is Akbar.

(a) Punjab (b) Odisha (c) Assam (d) Gujarat
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bihu is the most popular folk dance of Assam, performed during the Bihu festivals marking the agricultural cycle. Punjab's famous folk dance is Bhangra, so the answer is Assam.

18. The Brihadeeswara Temple at Thanjavur, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, was built by which ruler?
(a) Rajaraja Chola I (b) Rajendra Chola I (c) Krishnadevaraya (d) Pulakeshin II
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Brihadeeswara (Rajarajeshwara) Temple was completed in 1010 CE by Rajaraja Chola I. Rajendra Chola I built a similar temple at Gangaikonda Cholapuram, which is the common confusion, so the answer is Rajaraja Chola I.

20. The Sattriya dance form, recognised as classical in 2000, originated in the monasteries (sattras) of which state?
(a) Manipur (b) Tripura (c) Assam (d) West Bengal
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sattriya was created by the Vaishnavite saint Srimanta Sankardeva in the sattras (monasteries) of Assam in the 15th–16th century. Manipur has its own classical form, Manipuri, so the answer is Assam.

21. Consider the following statements about the Mathura and Gandhara schools of art:
1. The Mathura school used spotted red sandstone.
2. The Gandhara school primarily used blue-grey schist stone.
3. The Mathura school was influenced by Greco-Roman art.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is statement 3, which reverses the facts: it was the Gandhara school, not Mathura, that absorbed Greco-Roman influence; Mathura remained indigenous in style. Statements 1 and 2 correctly describe the materials of the two schools, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.

22. Arrange the following monuments in correct chronological order of their construction, from earliest to latest:
1. Sanchi Stupa (original brick structure)
2. Kailasa temple, Ellora
3. Brihadeeswara Temple, Thanjavur
4. Sun Temple, Konark
(a) 1-2-3-4 (b) 2-1-3-4 (c) 1-3-2-4 (d) 2-1-4-3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap lies in swapping Ellora and Sanchi: Sanchi's original stupa dates to Ashoka's time (3rd century BCE), the Kailasa temple to the 8th century CE, Brihadeeswara to 1010 CE, and Konark to the 13th century CE. Hence the correct sequence is 1-2-3-4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

23. Consider the following pairs of classical dances and their states of origin:

24. Consider the following statements about the Ajanta and Ellora caves:
1. Ajanta caves are exclusively Buddhist.
2. Ellora caves include Buddhist, Hindu and Jain monuments.
3. Both Ajanta and Ellora are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: All three statements are correct: Ajanta's 29-odd caves are all Buddhist, while Ellora's 34 caves span Buddhist (1–12), Hindu (13–29) and Jain (30–34) traditions, and both sites were inscribed by UNESCO in 1983. The tempting error is to assume Ellora is only Hindu because of the Kailasa temple, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art

25. Consider the following statements about Hindustani and Carnatic music:
1. Hindustani music was influenced by Persian and Islamic traditions.
2. Carnatic music remained largely free of Persian influence.
3. The 'Kriti' is a principal compositional form of Hindustani music.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 1 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is statement 3: the Kriti is the central compositional form of Carnatic music (perfected by Tyagaraja), not Hindustani music, whose main vocal forms are Dhrupad and Khayal. Statements 1 and 2 correctly describe the historical divergence after the medieval period, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art
