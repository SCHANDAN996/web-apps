101. The Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan and Padma Shri awards are announced every year on which occasion?
(a) Independence Day (b) Constitution Day (c) Gandhi Jayanti (d) Republic Day
Answer: (d)
Solution: Independence Day is the tempting trap, but the Padma awards are announced on the eve of Republic Day (25 January) and later conferred by the President at Rashtrapati Bhavan, so the answer is Republic Day.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

102. The Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, was instituted in which year?
(a) 1954 (b) 1950 (c) 1947 (d) 1952
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 1950, the year of the first Republic Day; the Bharat Ratna was actually instituted on 2 January 1954, along with the Padma Vibhushan, so the answer is 1954.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

103. The Jnanpith Award is given for outstanding contribution in which field?
(a) Medicine (b) Science (c) Sports (d) Literature
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Jnanpith Award, instituted by the Bharatiya Jnanpith in 1961, honours outstanding creative writing in Indian languages; the first recipient was G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam, 1965), so the answer is Literature.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith official

104. The Arjuna Award is related to which field?
(a) Literature (b) Sports (c) Social service (d) Gallantry
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Arjuna Award, instituted in 1961, is given for outstanding achievement in sports; the Dronacharya Award for coaches is a common confusion, so the answer is Sports.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports

105. Who was the first Indian to receive the Nobel Prize?
(a) Rabindranath Tagore (b) Mother Teresa (c) C. V. Raman (d) Amartya Sen
Answer: (a)
Solution: C. V. Raman is the trap — he was the first Indian scientist to win the Nobel (Physics, 1930), but Rabindranath Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: Nobel Prize official website

106. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is given for contribution to which field?
(a) Cinema (b) Music (c) Theatre (d) Literature
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is India's highest honour in cinema, instituted in 1969, and the first recipient was Devika Rani; the trap is confusing it with Sangeet Natak Akademi honours, so the answer is Cinema.
Source: Ministry of Information and Broadcasting

107. The Param Vir Chakra is awarded for:
(a) Wartime gallantry (b) Peacetime gallantry (c) Distinguished service (d) Sports
Answer: (a)
Solution: The classic trap is peacetime gallantry, which is the domain of the Ashoka Chakra; the Param Vir Chakra is the highest award for conspicuous bravery in the face of the enemy during war, so the answer is Wartime gallantry.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

108. Who among the following was NOT a recipient of the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Martin Luther King Jr (b) Nelson Mandela (c) Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (d) Mother Teresa
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mother Teresa (1980), Nelson Mandela (1990) and Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987) all received the Bharat Ratna; Martin Luther King Jr never did, so the answer is Martin Luther King Jr.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs

109. The Ramon Magsaysay Award is instituted in which country?
(a) India (b) Japan (c) Philippines (d) USA
Answer: (c)
Solution: The award was instituted in 1957 by the Rockefeller Brothers Fund in honour of Philippine President Ramon Magsaysay and is presented in Manila, so the answer is Philippines.
Source: Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation

110. The Sahitya Akademi Award is given in how many languages?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 18 (d) 26
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 22, the number of Eighth Schedule languages; the Sahitya Akademi Award is given in those 22 languages plus English and Rajasthani, so the answer is 24.
Source: Sahitya Akademi official

111. Who was the first woman to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Mother Teresa (b) Indira Gandhi (c) M. S. Subbulakshmi (d) Aruna Asaf Ali
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mother Teresa (1980) and M. S. Subbulakshmi (1998) are traps, but Indira Gandhi received the Bharat Ratna in 1971, earlier than both, so the answer is Indira Gandhi.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs

112. The Kalinga Prize for the popularisation of science is awarded by which organisation?
(a) UNESCO (b) WHO (c) World Bank (d) UNICEF
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Kalinga Prize was instituted in 1951 by UNESCO with a donation from Biju Patnaik of Odisha; UNICEF and WHO deal with children and health, not science popularisation, so the answer is UNESCO.
Source: UNESCO official

113. The Dronacharya Award is given to:
(a) Sportspersons (b) Scientists (c) Coaches (d) Journalists
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is sportspersons, who receive the Arjuna Award; the Dronacharya Award, instituted in 1985, honours outstanding coaches, so the answer is Coaches.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports

114. Arundhati Roy won the Booker Prize in 1997 for which novel?
(a) Midnight's Children (b) The Inheritance of Loss (c) The White Tiger (d) The God of Small Things
Answer: (d)
Solution: Midnight's Children is the trap — it won Salman Rushdie the Booker in 1981; Arundhati Roy won in 1997 for The God of Small Things, so the answer is The God of Small Things.
Source: Booker Prize official

115. The Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted by the Government of India in which year?
(a) 1985 (b) 1995 (c) 2000 (d) 1990
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted in 1995 on the occasion of the 125th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi, so the answer is 1995.
Source: Ministry of Culture, Government of India

116. The Vyas Samman is given for contribution in which field?
(a) Sports (b) Music (c) Literature (d) Science
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Vyas Samman, instituted by the K. K. Birla Foundation in 1991, honours an outstanding literary work in Hindi published in the preceding ten years, so the answer is Literature.
Source: K. K. Birla Foundation

117. Who among the following was the first person to receive the Bharat Ratna posthumously?
(a) C. V. Raman (b) C. Rajagopalachari (c) S. Radhakrishnan (d) Lal Bahadur Shastri
Answer: (d)
Solution: Raman, Radhakrishnan and Rajagopalachari all received the award in 1954 during their lifetimes; Lal Bahadur Shastri was awarded posthumously in 1966 after his death at Tashkent, so the answer is Lal Bahadur Shastri.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs

118. The Pulitzer Prize is associated with which country?
(a) UK (b) USA (c) France (d) India
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Pulitzer Prize is an American award for journalism, literature and music, established under the will of Joseph Pulitzer and administered by Columbia University, so the answer is USA.
Source: Pulitzer Prize official

119. Which is India's highest peacetime gallantry award?
(a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Maha Vir Chakra (c) Ashoka Chakra (d) Kirti Chakra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Param Vir Chakra is the trap — it is the highest wartime gallantry award; the Ashoka Chakra is the highest peacetime gallantry award, so the answer is Ashoka Chakra.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

120. The Sangeet Natak Akademi, the national academy for music, dance and drama, was established in which year?
(a) 1954 (b) 1952 (c) 1956 (d) 1958
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Sangeet Natak Akademi was established by a Government of India resolution in 1952 and formally inaugurated in 1953, which makes 1953 a tempting trap, so the answer is 1952.
Source: Sangeet Natak Akademi official

121. India's highest sporting honour, earlier called the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna, was renamed in 2021 after which sportsperson?
(a) Dronacharya (b) Milkha Singh (c) Major Dhyan Chand (d) Kapil Dev
Answer: (c)
Solution: Milkha Singh is a tempting trap due to his legendary status, but in August 2021 the Khel Ratna was renamed the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award after the hockey wizard, so the answer is Major Dhyan Chand.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports

122. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Padma Vibhushan – 1954 (b) Param Vir Chakra – 1954 (c) Bharat Ratna – 1950 (d) Jnanpith Award – 1965
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Bharat Ratna was instituted in 1954 (not 1950), the Param Vir Chakra traces to 1947, and the Jnanpith Award was instituted in 1961 (first given in 1965); only the Padma Vibhushan pairing is right, so the answer is Padma Vibhushan – 1954.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs

123. Who received the Ramon Magsaysay Award in 1962 for her work among the poor of Kolkata?
(a) Kiran Bedi (b) Aruna Roy (c) Ela Bhatt (d) Mother Teresa
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kiran Bedi is the trap — she won the Magsaysay Award in 1994 for government service; Mother Teresa received it in 1962, so the answer is Mother Teresa.
Source: Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation

124. What is the maximum number of Bharat Ratna awards that can be given in a single year?
(a) Two (b) Four (c) Five (d) Three
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming there is no limit or a higher cap; the Bharat Ratna is restricted to a maximum of three recipients in a year, so the answer is Three.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs

125. Which award is given for excellence in the field of journalism?
(a) Booker Prize (b) Kalinga Prize (c) Pulitzer Prize (d) Jnanpith Award
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Booker Prize is for fiction and the Kalinga Prize for science popularisation; the Pulitzer Prize honours journalism, literature and music, so the answer is Pulitzer Prize.
Source: Pulitzer Prize official
