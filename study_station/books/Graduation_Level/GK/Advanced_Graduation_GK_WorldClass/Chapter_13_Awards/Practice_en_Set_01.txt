1. The Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, was instituted in which year?
(a) 1954 (b) 1950 (c) 1947 (d) 1956
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is 1950, the year the Constitution came into force, which many candidates confuse with the award's institution. The Bharat Ratna was actually instituted on 2 January 1954, along with the Padma Vibhushan, so the answer is 1954.
Source: PYQ-style

2. Who was the first recipient of the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (c) C. Rajagopalachari (d) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many candidates pick Dr. S. Radhakrishnan because he was the first Vice-President, but the very first Bharat Ratna (1954) went to C. Rajagopalachari, the last Governor-General of India; Radhakrishnan and C. V. Raman also received it the same year, so the answer is C. Rajagopalachari.
Source: PYQ-style

3. The Nobel Prize is awarded in memory of Alfred Nobel, who belonged to which country?
(a) Norway (b) Sweden (c) Denmark (d) Switzerland
Answer: (b)
Solution: Norway is the trap because the Nobel Peace Prize ceremony is held in Oslo, Norway. Alfred Nobel himself was a Swedish chemist and inventor of dynamite, so the answer is Sweden.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

4. Who was the first Indian to win the Nobel Prize?
(a) C. V. Raman (b) Amartya Sen (c) Mother Teresa (d) Rabindranath Tagore
Answer: (d)
Solution: C. V. Raman is the tempting wrong choice as the first Indian Nobel laureate in science (1930), but Rabindranath Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913 for Gitanjali, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: PYQ-style

5. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is given for contribution to which field?
(a) Cinema (b) Classical music (c) Literature (d) Sports
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Dadasaheb Phalke Award, named after the father of Indian cinema, is India's highest award in cinema, given for lifetime contribution to Indian films, so the answer is Cinema.

6. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award is India's highest honour in which field?
(a) Literature (b) Science (c) Sports (d) Social service
Answer: (c)
Solution: Renamed in 2021 after hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand (earlier the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna), it is India's highest sporting honour, so the answer is Sports.

7. The Jnanpith Award is given for excellence in which field?
(a) Journalism (b) Indian literature (c) Medicine (d) Architecture
Answer: (b)
Solution: Instituted in 1961 by the Bharatiya Jnanpith, the award honours outstanding contribution to Indian literature; the first recipient was G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam) in 1965, so the answer is Indian literature.
Source: PYQ-style

8. Which is the highest gallantry award in India during wartime?
(a) Ashoka Chakra (b) Maha Vir Chakra (c) Param Vir Chakra (d) Kirti Chakra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ashoka Chakra is the trap — it is the highest peacetime gallantry award, not wartime. The Param Vir Chakra is the highest wartime gallantry decoration, so the answer is Param Vir Chakra.
Source: PYQ-style

9. The Pulitzer Prize is associated with which fields?
(a) Peace and economics (b) Sports and cinema (c) Science and medicine (d) Journalism, literature and music
Answer: (d)
Solution: Established by Joseph Pulitzer and administered by Columbia University, the Pulitzer Prizes honour excellence in journalism, literature and musical composition in the USA, so the answer is Journalism, literature and music.

10. Who was the first woman to receive the Bharat Ratna?

Answer: (c)
Solution: Mother Teresa (1980) is a common wrong guess, but Indira Gandhi received the Bharat Ratna in 1971, making her the first woman recipient, so the answer is Indira Gandhi.

11. The Arjuna Award is given for outstanding performance in which field?
(a) Films (b) Sports (c) Literature (d) Public administration
Answer: (b)
Solution: Instituted in 1961, the Arjuna Award recognises consistent outstanding performance in sports over the preceding four years, so the answer is Sports.

12. The Nobel Peace Prize is awarded in which city?
(a) Stockholm (b) Oslo (c) Geneva (d) Copenhagen
Answer: (b)
Solution: Stockholm is the trap because all other Nobel Prizes are awarded there. By Alfred Nobel's will, the Peace Prize alone is awarded in Oslo, Norway, so the answer is Oslo.
Source: PYQ-style

13. Which award is known as the 'Nobel Prize of Asia'?
(a) Booker Prize (b) Pulitzer Prize (c) Ramon Magsaysay Award (d) Templeton Prize
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Ramon Magsaysay Award, instituted in 1957 in memory of the Philippine President, is often called Asia's Nobel Prize; Vinoba Bhave was among its early Indian recipients, so the answer is Ramon Magsaysay Award.
Source: PYQ-style

14. The Booker Prize is awarded for excellence in which field?
(a) Journalism (b) Scientific research (c) Fiction writing in English (d) Peace activism
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Booker Prize is awarded annually for the best novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland; Arundhati Roy won it in 1997 for The God of Small Things, so the answer is Fiction writing in English.
Source: PYQ-style

15. The Padma Shri is which rank among India's civilian awards?
(a) Fourth highest (b) Second highest (c) Third highest (d) Highest
Answer: (a)
Solution: The order is Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri — many candidates wrongly place Padma Shri third, forgetting the Bharat Ratna, so the answer is Fourth highest.
Source: PYQ-style

16. C. V. Raman won the Nobel Prize in 1930 for his work in which subject?
(a) Chemistry (b) Peace (c) Medicine (d) Physics
Answer: (d)
Solution: He won the Nobel Prize in Physics for the discovery of the Raman Effect (scattering of light), making him the first Asian to win a Nobel in science, so the answer is Physics.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (related concept)

21. Consider the following statements about the Bharat Ratna:
1. It can be awarded posthumously.
2. A maximum of three persons can be awarded in a year.
3. It is conferred only on Indian citizens.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — the Bharat Ratna is not restricted to Indian citizens; Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan and Nelson Mandela received it. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.

Solution: 1980 is the trap — that is the year she received the Bharat Ratna, not the Nobel. She won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1979 for her work among the poor of Kolkata, so the answer is 1979.

24. Consider the following statements about the Nobel Prizes:

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — the Economics prize was created later (1968) by Sweden's central bank and was not in Nobel's will. Statements 1 and 3 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.

25. Who among the following was the first sportsperson to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Sachin Tendulkar (b) Milkha Singh (c) Dhyan Chand (d) Viswanathan Anand
Answer: (a)
Solution: Dhyan Chand is the tempting wrong answer because of his legendary status, but he never received the Bharat Ratna. Sachin Tendulkar became the first sportsperson (and youngest recipient) to get it, in 2014, so the answer is Sachin Tendulkar.
