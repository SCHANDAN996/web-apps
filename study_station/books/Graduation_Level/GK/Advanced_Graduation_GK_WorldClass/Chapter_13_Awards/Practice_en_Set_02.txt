Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, was instituted in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1950 (c) 1956 (d) 1954
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many candidates pick 1950 because the Constitution came into force that year, but the Bharat Ratna was instituted on 2 January 1954, along with the Padma Shri. The Padma Vibhushan and Padma Bhushan were also created in 1954, so the answer is 1954.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

27. Who was the first woman to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Indira Gandhi (b) Mother Teresa (c) Aruna Asaf Ali (d) M.S. Subbulakshmi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mother Teresa is a tempting choice because she received it in 1980, but Indira Gandhi was awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1971, making her the first woman recipient. Aruna Asaf Ali received it posthumously in 1997, so the answer is Indira Gandhi.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

28. The Padma Vibhushan is ranked at which position in the order of precedence of Indian civilian awards?
(a) Second (b) First (c) Third (d) Fourth
Answer: (a)
Solution: Candidates often confuse Padma Vibhushan with Padma Bhushan. The order is Bharat Ratna (first), Padma Vibhushan (second), Padma Bhushan (third) and Padma Shri (fourth), so the answer is Second.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

29. The Nobel Prize is awarded in all of the following fields EXCEPT:
(a) Mathematics (b) Peace (c) Physics (d) Literature
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap here is assuming all major sciences are covered. Alfred Nobel's will provided for Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature and Peace; Economics was added later by Sweden's central bank. Mathematics has never been a Nobel category, so the answer is Mathematics.
Source: Nobel Foundation (nobelprize.org)

30. The Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences was first awarded in which year?
(a) 1901 (b) 1969 (c) 1948 (d) 1975
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1901 is tempting because the original five Nobel Prizes began that year, but the Economics prize was established by Sveriges Riksbank in 1968 and first awarded in 1969 to Ragnar Frisch and Jan Tinbergen, so the answer is 1969.
Source: Nobel Foundation (nobelprize.org)

31. Who was the first Indian to win the Nobel Prize?
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Har Gobind Khorana (c) Rabindranath Tagore (d) Mother Teresa
Answer: (c)
Solution: C.V. Raman is a common wrong pick because his 1930 Physics Nobel is famous, but Rabindranath Tagore won the Literature Nobel in 1913 for Gitanjali, making him the first Indian and first non-European laureate, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: Nobel Foundation (nobelprize.org)

32. The Jnanpith Award is given for outstanding contribution in which field?
(a) Classical music (b) Indian literature (c) Social service (d) Science and technology
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Jnanpith Award, instituted in 1961 by the Bharatiya Jnanpith, honours Indian literature; the first award went to G. Sankara Kurup for Malayalam literature, so the answer is Indian literature.
Source: Bharatiya Jnanpith official records

33. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is associated with which field?
(a) Theatre (b) Cinema (c) Painting (d) Journalism
Answer: (b)
Solution: Theatre is a tempting distractor because Phalke was a pioneer of performing arts, but the award is India's highest honour in cinema, instituted in 1969 and first given to Devika Rani, so the answer is Cinema.
Source: Directorate of Film Festivals, Government of India

34. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award was earlier known by which name?
(a) Arjuna Award (b) Dronacharya Award (c) Tenzing Norgay Award (d) Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is chronological confusion: the award began in 1991–92 as the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna and was renamed the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna in 2021. Arjuna and Dronacharya are separate awards, so the answer is Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

35. The Dronacharya Award is given to:
(a) Outstanding sportspersons (b) Sports coaches (c) Adventure sports performers (d) Sports journalists
Answer: (b)
Solution: Candidates often mix up the Khel Ratna and Dronacharya awards. Instituted in 1985, the Dronacharya Award honours eminent coaches who have trained medal-winning athletes, so the answer is Sports coaches.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

36. The Arjuna Award was instituted in which year?
(a) 1961 (b) 1956 (c) 1985 (d) 1991
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1985 is tempting because the Dronacharya Award began then, but the Arjuna Award for outstanding sportspersons was instituted in 1961, so the answer is 1961.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

37. The Tenzing Norgay National Adventure Award is given for achievements in:
(a) Mountaineering only (b) Wildlife conservation (c) Adventure sports on land, water and air (d) Polar research
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Mountaineering only" is the trap, since Tenzing Norgay was a mountaineer. The award actually covers adventure achievements on land, at sea and in the air, plus lifetime achievement, so the answer is Adventure sports on land, water and air.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

38. The Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted by the Government of India in which year?
(a) 1995 (b) 1992 (c) 1998 (d) 2001
Answer: (a)
Solution: The award was instituted in 1995 on the occasion of the 125th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi, and the first recipient was Julius Nyerere of Tanzania, so the answer is 1995.
Source: Ministry of Culture, Government of India

39. Which of the following is India's highest peacetime gallantry award?
(a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Maha Vir Chakra (c) Ashoka Chakra (d) Kirti Chakra
Answer: (c)
Solution: Param Vir Chakra is the classic trap — it is the highest wartime gallantry award, not peacetime. The Ashoka Chakra tops the peacetime gallantry series (Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra, Shaurya Chakra), so the answer is Ashoka Chakra.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

40. The Param Vir Chakra was designed by:
(a) Nandalal Bose (b) Savitri Khanolkar (c) Satyajit Ray (d) Amrita Sher-Gil
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nandalal Bose is tempting because he decorated the original Constitution, but the Param Vir Chakra medal was designed by Savitri Khanolkar, whose son-in-law Major Somnath Sharma was its first recipient, so the answer is Savitri Khanolkar.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

41. Who was the first recipient of the Param Vir Chakra?
(a) Second Lieutenant Arun Khetarpal (b) Lance Naik Albert Ekka (c) Captain Vikram Batra (d) Major Somnath Sharma
Answer: (d)
Solution: Vikram Batra is a popular but wrong pick — he received it for Kargil 1999. Major Somnath Sharma was the first recipient, awarded posthumously for the Battle of Badgam in 1947, so the answer is Major Somnath Sharma.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

42. The Sahitya Akademi Award is given in how many languages?
(a) 22 (b) 18 (c) 24 (d) 26
Answer: (c)
Solution: 22 is the trap because the Eighth Schedule lists 22 languages. The Sahitya Akademi additionally honours English and Rajasthani, taking the total to 24 languages, so the answer is 24.
Source: Sahitya Akademi official records

43. The Pulitzer Prize is primarily associated with which fields?
(a) Science and medicine (b) Peace and diplomacy (c) Journalism, literature and music (d) Cinema and theatre
Answer: (c)
Solution: Established by Joseph Pulitzer's will and first awarded in 1917, the prizes honour excellence in journalism, letters (fiction, drama, poetry, history) and music in the United States, so the answer is Journalism, literature and music.
Source: Pulitzer Prizes official records (pulitzer.org)

44. The Booker Prize is awarded for:
(a) The best novel written in English (b) The best poem collection (c) The best biography (d) The best short story
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Booker Prize, instituted in 1969, is awarded annually for the best novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland; the International Booker covers translated fiction, so the answer is The best novel written in English.
Source: Booker Prize Foundation official records

45. Which Indian-origin author won the Booker Prize for "Midnight's Children"?
(a) V.S. Naipaul (b) Vikram Seth (c) Arundhati Roy (d) Salman Rushdie
Answer: (d)
Solution: Arundhati Roy is the tempting wrong option — she won the Booker in 1997 for "The God of Small Things". Salman Rushdie won in 1981 for "Midnight's Children", which also won the Booker of Bookers, so the answer is Salman Rushdie.
Source: Booker Prize Foundation official records

46. The Ramon Magsaysay Award is often described as Asia's equivalent of which prize?
(a) Booker Prize (b) Oscar (c) Pulitzer Prize (d) Nobel Prize
Answer: (d)
Solution: Instituted in 1957 in memory of Philippine President Ramon Magsaysay, the award honours selfless service in Asia and is popularly called Asia's Nobel Prize, so the answer is Nobel Prize.
Source: Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation official records

47. Who among the following Indians received the Ramon Magsaysay Award in 1958, the year it was first awarded?
(a) Vinoba Bhave (b) Jayaprakash Narayan (c) Mother Teresa (d) Verghese Kurien
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mother Teresa is tempting but she received it in 1962. Vinoba Bhave was among the very first recipients in 1958 for community leadership through the Bhoodan movement, so the answer is Vinoba Bhave.
Source: Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation official records

48. The Bharat Ratna has been awarded to how many non-Indian citizens so far?
(a) One (b) Three (c) Two (d) Four
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is counting Mother Teresa, who was an Indian citizen when awarded in 1980. The two foreign recipients are Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987) and Nelson Mandela (1990), so the answer is Two.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

49. The Grammy Awards are presented for excellence in which field?
(a) Film direction (b) Recorded music (c) Fashion design (d) Architecture
Answer: (b)
Solution: Presented by the Recording Academy of the United States since 1959, the Grammy Awards honour achievement in the recording industry, so the answer is Recorded music.
Source: Recording Academy official records (grammy.com)

50. The Abel Prize, often called the "Nobel of Mathematics", is awarded by which country?
(a) Sweden (b) Denmark (c) Finland (d) Norway
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sweden is the trap because the Nobel Prizes are Swedish. The Abel Prize, named after mathematician Niels Henrik Abel and first awarded in 2003, is given by the Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters, so the answer is Norway.
Source: Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters (abelprize.no)
