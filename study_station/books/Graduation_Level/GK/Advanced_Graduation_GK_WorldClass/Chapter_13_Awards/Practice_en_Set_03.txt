51. The Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, was instituted in which year?
(a) 1954 (b) 1947 (c) 1950 (d) 1956
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is 1950, the year the Constitution came into force, which many candidates confuse with the award's institution. The Bharat Ratna was actually instituted on 2 January 1954, along with the Padma awards, so the answer is 1954.
Source: Official website of Padma Awards, Ministry of Home Affairs

52. Who was the first recipient of the Bharat Ratna in 1954 along with C. Rajagopalachari and Dr. S. Radhakrishnan?
(a) C.V. Raman (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Bhagwan Das (d) Govind Ballabh Pant
Answer: (a)
Solution: Jawaharlal Nehru is a tempting wrong choice because he received the Bharat Ratna in 1955, not in the inaugural year. The first three recipients in 1954 were C. Rajagopalachari, Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan and Dr. C.V. Raman, so the answer is C.V. Raman.
Source: Official website of Padma Awards, Ministry of Home Affairs

53. Which of the following is the correct order of India's civilian awards from highest to lowest?
(a) Bharat Ratna, Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Shri (b) Padma Vibhushan, Bharat Ratna, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri (c) Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri (d) Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan, Bharat Ratna
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the option that swaps the second and third positions — a classic near-correct statement. The correct hierarchy is Bharat Ratna, then Padma Vibhushan, then Padma Bhushan, and finally Padma Shri, so the answer is Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri.
Source: Official website of Padma Awards, Ministry of Home Affairs

54. The Nobel Prize is NOT awarded in which of the following fields?
(a) Physics (b) Peace (c) Literature (d) Mathematics
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many candidates wrongly pick Literature or Peace thinking they were added later, but both were in Alfred Nobel's original will. Nobel Prizes cover Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature and Peace, with Economics added later by Sweden's central bank; Mathematics has never been included, so the answer is Mathematics.
Source: Official website of the Nobel Prize

55. The Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences was established by which institution?
(a) Norwegian Parliament (b) Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (c) Sveriges Riksbank (Sweden's central bank) (d) Nobel Foundation
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences is tempting because it selects the Economics laureates, but selecting is not the same as establishing. The prize was established in 1968 by Sveriges Riksbank, Sweden's central bank, in memory of Alfred Nobel, so the answer is Sveriges Riksbank.
Source: Official website of the Nobel Prize

56. Who was the first Indian to receive the Nobel Prize?
(a) Har Gobind Khorana (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Mother Teresa (d) C.V. Raman
Answer: (b)
Solution: C.V. Raman is the common trap because he was the first Indian scientist to win a Nobel (Physics, 1930). However, Rabindranath Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913, making him the first Indian Nobel laureate, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: Official website of the Nobel Prize

57. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is given for outstanding contribution to which field?
(a) Cinema (b) Classical music (c) Literature (d) Theatre
Answer: (a)
Solution: Theatre is a tempting distractor because Phalke was also associated with stage arts early in life. The award, instituted in 1969, honours lifetime contribution to Indian cinema and is named after the father of Indian cinema, so the answer is Cinema.
Source: Directorate of Film Festivals, Ministry of Information and Broadcasting

58. The Jnanpith Award is conferred for excellence in which field?
(a) Journalism (b) Science (c) Indian literature (d) Social service
Answer: (c)
Solution: Journalism is the trap because the award is presented by the Bharatiya Jnanpith trust founded by the Sahu Jain family of the Times of India group. The Jnanpith Award, instituted in 1961, is India's highest literary honour, so the answer is Indian literature.
Source: Official website of Bharatiya Jnanpith

59. Who was the first recipient of the Jnanpith Award in 1965?
(a) Sumitranandan Pant (b) Kuvempu (c) G. Sankara Kurup (d) Ashapurna Devi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kuvempu is tempting because he was the first Kannada writer to win it, but that was in 1967. The first Jnanpith Award (1965) went to Malayalam poet G. Sankara Kurup for his work 'Odakkuzhal', so the answer is G. Sankara Kurup.
Source: Official website of Bharatiya Jnanpith

60. The Arjuna Award is associated with which field?
(a) Bravery in armed forces (b) Outstanding achievement in sports (c) Scientific research (d) Public administration
Answer: (b)
Solution: Bravery in armed forces is the trap because the name Arjuna evokes the warrior of the Mahabharata. Instituted in 1961, the Arjuna Award recognises outstanding achievement in sports, so the answer is outstanding achievement in sports.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports

61. India's highest award for sports, now named after a hockey legend, is the:
(a) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (b) Dronacharya Award (c) Arjuna Award (d) Dhyan Chand Award for Lifetime Achievement
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Dhyan Chand Award is the trap — it is a separate lifetime achievement award, not the highest honour. The Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna was renamed the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award in 2021 and remains India's highest sporting honour, so the answer is Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports

62. The Dronacharya Award is given to:
(a) Sportspersons for lifetime achievement (b) Coaches for excellence in sports training (c) Universities promoting sports (d) Referees and umpires
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lifetime achievement in sports is tempting because the Dhyan Chand Award covers that category and the names are often confused. The Dronacharya Award, instituted in 1985, honours coaches who produce outstanding sportspersons, so the answer is coaches for excellence in sports training.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports

63. Which is the highest peacetime gallantry award of India?
(a) Maha Vir Chakra (b) Param Vir Chakra (c) Kirti Chakra (d) Ashoka Chakra
Answer: (d)
Solution: Param Vir Chakra is the classic trap — it is the highest gallantry award overall but is a wartime award. The highest peacetime gallantry award is the Ashoka Chakra, so the answer is Ashoka Chakra.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

64. The correct order of wartime gallantry awards from highest to lowest is:
(a) Maha Vir Chakra, Param Vir Chakra, Vir Chakra (b) Param Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra, Vir Chakra (c) Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra, Param Vir Chakra (d) Param Vir Chakra, Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra
Answer: (b)
Solution: The last option is the trap — it swaps the second and third awards, an almost-correct sequence. The correct wartime hierarchy is Param Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra, then Vir Chakra, so the answer is Param Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra, Vir Chakra.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

65. The Pulitzer Prize is awarded for excellence in which fields?
(a) Science and technology (b) Peace and diplomacy (c) Cinema and theatre (d) Journalism, literature and music
Answer: (d)
Solution: Peace and diplomacy is tempting because the Pulitzer is often mentioned alongside the Nobel Peace Prize in GK questions. Established under the will of Joseph Pulitzer and administered by Columbia University, it honours journalism, literature and musical composition, so the answer is journalism, literature and music.
Source: Official website of the Pulitzer Prizes

66. The Booker Prize is awarded for:
(a) A novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland (b) Poetry written in any language (c) Journalistic reporting (d) Scientific writing
Answer: (a)
Solution: Poetry is the trap because many literary awards cover verse, but the Booker is strictly for fiction. The Booker Prize is awarded annually to the best novel written in English and published in the United Kingdom or Ireland, so the answer is a novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland.
Source: Official website of the Booker Prize

67. The Sahitya Akademi Award recognises books in how many languages?
(a) 22 (b) 24 (c) 18 (d) 26
Answer: (b)
Solution: 22 is the trap because the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution lists 22 languages, and candidates often apply that number here. The Sahitya Akademi honours books in 24 languages — the 22 scheduled languages plus English and Rajasthani, so the answer is 24.
Source: Official website of Sahitya Akademi

68. Which eminent jurist and social reformer was awarded the Bharat Ratna posthumously in 1990?
(a) B.R. Ambedkar (b) M.G. Ramachandran (c) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (d) Lal Bahadur Shastri
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lal Bahadur Shastri is the trap because he was indeed the first person to receive the Bharat Ratna posthumously, but that was in 1966, not 1990. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, the chief architect of the Constitution, was honoured posthumously in 1990, so the answer is B.R. Ambedkar.
Source: Official website of Padma Awards, Ministry of Home Affairs

69. The Gandhi Peace Prize is instituted by:
(a) United Nations (b) UNESCO (c) Gandhi Smriti and Darshan Samiti independently (d) Government of India
Answer: (d)
Solution: The United Nations is tempting because the prize has an international character and past recipients include global figures. The Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted by the Government of India in 1995, on the 125th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi, so the answer is Government of India.
Source: Ministry of Culture, Government of India

70. Which Indian won the Nobel Peace Prize in 2014?
(a) Mother Teresa (b) Amartya Sen (c) Dalai Lama (d) Kailash Satyarthi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mother Teresa is the trap — she won the Nobel Peace Prize too, but in 1979, a chronological confusion. The 2014 Peace Prize went jointly to Kailash Satyarthi and Malala Yousafzai for the struggle against suppression of children, so the answer is Kailash Satyarthi.
Source: Official website of the Nobel Prize

71. Amartya Sen received the Nobel Prize in 1998 for his work in:
(a) Literature (b) Economic Sciences (c) Physics (d) Peace
Answer: (b)
Solution: Peace is tempting because Sen's work on famine and welfare sounds humanitarian, but the Nobel categories are strict. He received the 1998 prize in Economic Sciences for contributions to welfare economics, so the answer is Economic Sciences.
Source: Official website of the Nobel Prize

72. The Ramon Magsaysay Award, often called Asia's Nobel Prize, is presented in which country?
(a) Thailand (b) Japan (c) Philippines (d) Indonesia
Answer: (c)
Solution: Japan is the trap because the Kyoto Prize and other Japanese honours are also called 'Asia's Nobel'. The Ramon Magsaysay Award is presented in Manila, Philippines, named after a former Philippine president, so the answer is Philippines.
Source: Official website of the Ramon Magsaysay Award Foundation

73. Which of the following statements about the Bharat Ratna is correct?
(a) It can be conferred only on Indian citizens (b) It carries a cash prize of one crore rupees (c) A maximum of five persons can receive it in a year (d) It has been conferred on non-Indians as well
Answer: (d)
Solution: The 'only Indian citizens' statement is the trap — it sounds plausible but is false, since Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987) and Nelson Mandela (1990) received the award. The Bharat Ratna carries no cash component and the annual limit is three, not five, so the answer is that it has been conferred on non-Indians as well.
Source: Official website of Padma Awards, Ministry of Home Affairs

74. The Bharat Ratna was conferred on Sachin Tendulkar in 2014. What was special about this?
(a) He was the first cricketer to receive any Padma award (b) He was the first person to receive it while alive (c) He was the first sportsperson and the youngest recipient (d) He received it jointly with a scientist for the first time in history
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 'first person to receive it while alive' option is the trap — most Bharat Ratnas have gone to living persons. Tendulkar was the first sportsperson and, at 40, the youngest recipient of the Bharat Ratna, so the answer is that he was the first sportsperson and the youngest recipient.
Source: Official website of Padma Awards, Ministry of Home Affairs

75. The Vyas Samman is awarded for outstanding literary work in which language?
(a) Hindi (b) Urdu (c) Sanskrit (d) Bengali
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sanskrit is the trap because the name 'Vyas' evokes the Sanskrit tradition of the Mahabharata. The Vyas Samman, instituted by the K.K. Birla Foundation, honours outstanding Hindi literary work published in the preceding ten years, so the answer is Hindi.
Source: K.K. Birla Foundation official announcements
