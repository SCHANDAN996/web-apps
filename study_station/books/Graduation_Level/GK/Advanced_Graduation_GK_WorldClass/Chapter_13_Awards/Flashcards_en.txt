Card 1
Front: Which is India's highest civilian award, and in which year was it instituted?
Back: Bharat Ratna — instituted in 1954. It is awarded for exceptional service of the highest order in any field, including art, literature, science, and public service.

Card 2
Front: What is the correct order of precedence among India's four civilian awards?
Back: Bharat Ratna → Padma Vibhushan → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri. Mnemonic: "Bhaiya, Very Big Salute" (Bharat, Vibhushan, Bhushan, Shri).

Card 3
Front: Which award is given for gallantry in the face of the enemy, and which is its peacetime equivalent?
Back: Wartime: Param Vir Chakra (highest). Peacetime: Ashoka Chakra (highest peacetime gallantry award). Examiner's trap: papers often swap these two — remember "Param = war, Ashoka = peace."

Card 4
Front: Who was the first sportsperson to receive the Bharat Ratna?
Back: Sachin Tendulkar (2014). He was also, at the time, the youngest recipient of the award.

Card 5
Front: The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award was earlier known by what name?
Back: Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award — renamed in 2021. It is India's highest sporting honour.

Card 6
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the wartime gallantry awards in order?
Back: "PVC-MVC-VC" — Param Vir Chakra, Maha Vir Chakra, Vir Chakra. Trick: "Param Maha Vir" — think of descending degrees of "vir" (bravery).

Card 7
Front: Which award is given by the Government of India for excellence in literature, and who gives it?
Back: Sahitya Akademi Award — given by the Sahitya Akademi (India's National Academy of Letters) for outstanding books in Indian languages. Trap: it is NOT given by the Ministry of Culture directly.

Card 8
Front: Which Indian award honours lifetime contribution to cinema, and after whom is it named?
Back: Dadasaheb Phalke Award — named after the father of Indian cinema; instituted in 1969, first given to Devika Rani.

Card 9
Front: Examiner's trap: Is the Bharat Ratna restricted to Indian citizens only?
Back: No. It can be awarded to foreigners too — e.g., Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987) and Nelson Mandela (1990) received it. Don't fall for the "only Indians" statement.

Card 10
Front: Which Nobel laureate was the first Asian to win the Nobel Prize, and in which field?
Back: Rabindranath Tagore — Nobel Prize in Literature, 1913, for Gitanjali. He was the first non-European Nobel laureate in literature.

Card 11
Front: What is the difference between the Arjuna Award and the Dronacharya Award?
Back: Arjuna Award — for outstanding performance by sportspersons. Dronacharya Award — for coaches. Mnemonic: Arjuna was the player (archer), Dronacharya was the teacher (guru).

Card 12
Front: Which award is given for excellence in public administration, and what is its trap?
Back: There is no single "highest public administration award" — but the trap is confusing Padma awards (civilian, any field) with gallantry awards (military/police bravery). Padma awards are NOT gallantry awards.

Card 13
Front: Who was the first Indian to win the Nobel Prize in Physics, and for what discovery?
Back: C.V. Raman (1930) — for the Raman Effect (scattering of light). He was the first Asian to win a Nobel in any science.

Card 14
Front: Which international award is called the "Nobel Prize of Asia"?
Back: The Ramon Magsaysay Award (Philippines) — often called Asia's Nobel Prize. Several Indians, including Vinoba Bhave (1958) and Mother Teresa (1962), have received it.

Card 15
Front: Examiner's trap: Can the Padma awards be used as titles before or after one's name?
Back: No. Article 18(1) of the Constitution abolishes titles; Bharat Ratna and Padma awards cannot be used as prefixes or suffixes to the recipient's name.

Card 16
Front: Which award recognizes contributions to sports coaching at the grassroots/lifetime level, distinct from the regular Dronacharya?
Back: The Dronacharya Award itself has two categories: regular (recent outstanding coaching) and lifetime. The Dhyan Chand Award is for lifetime achievement in sports and games by sportspersons.

Card 17
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the peacetime gallantry awards in order?
Back: "Ashoka Kirti Shaurya" — Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra, Shaurya Chakra. Trick: "AKS — A Kind Soldier" in descending order.

Card 18
Front: Which Indian won the Nobel Peace Prize for work against child labour?
Back: Kailash Satyarthi (2014), shared with Malala Yousafzai. Trap: don't confuse with Mother Teresa (Peace, 1979) — different years, different causes.

Card 19
Front: What is the Jnanpith Award, and who was its first recipient?
Back: India's highest literary award for an author's overall contribution to literature; first awarded in 1965 to G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam). Trap: it is for a body of work, unlike the Sahitya Akademi Award which is for a specific book.

Card 20
Front: Which gallantry award series is open to civilians as well as military personnel?
Back: The peacetime series — Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra, Shaurya Chakra — can be awarded to civilians. The wartime series (Param Vir, Maha Vir, Vir Chakra) is for armed forces only.
