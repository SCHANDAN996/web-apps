# 🧒 The Feynman Corner — Awards & Honours

## The Most Confusing Concept: Bharat Ratna vs Padma Awards — Who Gets What, and Why the Order Matters

Most students memorise the list "Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Shri..." and then lose marks because they can't tell *who* is eligible for *which* award, or they mix up the order of precedence. Let's fix that the Feynman way.

---

## Explain It Like I'm 12

Imagine your school gives prizes on Annual Day.

- There is **one big trophy** — the "Student of the Year" award. Only **3 students maximum** in the whole school can get it in a year, and it can be given for *any* field — studies, sports, music, social work, anything. That is the **Bharat Ratna** — India's highest civilian award, limited to a **maximum of 3 recipients per year**, given for exceptional service in **any field of human endeavour**.

- Below that, the school gives **medals in three grades** — Gold, Silver, Bronze — but *only* for students who did something special **in a specific field** (art, science, sports, public service, etc.). That is the **Padma family**:
  - **Padma Vibhushan** = Gold (exceptional and distinguished service)
  - **Padma Bhushan** = Silver (distinguished service of a high order)
  - **Padma Shri** = Bronze (distinguished service)

So the whole system is just: **1 super-trophy on top, and 3 grades of medals below it.** Four levels total. That's it.

---

## The Daily-Life Example

Think of a pizza menu:

- **Bharat Ratna** = the "Chef's Special" — only 3 plates a day, any flavour.
- **Padma Vibhushan / Bhushan / Shri** = Large, Medium, Small — same pizza (civilian service in a field), different sizes of honour.

If someone asks, "Is Padma Bhushan higher than Padma Vibhushan?" — just ask yourself: *is Medium bigger than Large?* No. **Vibhushan > Bhushan > Shri.**

---

## The Examiner's Traps (Where Students Actually Lose Marks)

1. **Order trap:** Options shuffle Vibhushan/Bhushan/Shri. Remember: **V → B → S** (Very Big Service).
2. **Number trap:** Bharat Ratna cap is **3 per year** — not 2, not 5.
3. **Field trap:** Bharat Ratna = *any* field; Padma awards = *specific fields* (art, literature, science, public affairs, medicine, trade, sports, social work, etc.).
4. **"Posthumous" trap:** Both Bharat Ratna and Padma awards **can** be given posthumously.
5. **Cash trap:** These awards carry **no cash prize** — a sanad (certificate) and a medallion only. And the titles cannot be used as prefixes/suffixes with one's name (Article 18(1) spirit — they are not "titles" to be attached to names).
6. **Instituted year trap:** Bharat Ratna and Padma Vibhushan were instituted in **1954**; Padma Bhushan and Padma Shri also trace to 1954 (Padma Vibhushan was briefly split into three classes that year, then reorganised). The awards were **suspended twice** (1977–1980 and 1992–1995) — a favourite statement-based question.

---

## The Unforgettable Trick

> **"Bhaiya, Very Big Shout!"**
> **B**harat Ratna → **V**ibhushan → **B**hushan → **S**hri
> Highest to lowest, in one shout. And Bharat Ratna = **3** a year — think of a **trident (trishul)** on top of the pyramid.
