126. Who was the first Indian to receive the Bharat Ratna posthumously?
(a) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (b) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (c) Subhas Chandra Bose (d) Lal Bahadur Shastri
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is Sardar Patel, who did receive the Bharat Ratna posthumously, but only in 1991 — decades after his death. Lal Bahadur Shastri was awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1966, the year he died, making him the first posthumous recipient, so the answer is Lal Bahadur Shastri.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Which of the following statements about the Bharat Ratna is correct?
(a) A maximum of five persons can receive it in a year (b) It carries a monetary grant of ₹25 lakh (c) It can be awarded only to Indian citizens (d) It was instituted in 1954
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the "only Indian citizens" claim — foreigners like Nelson Mandela and Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan have received it. There is no monetary grant, and the annual cap is three, not five. The award was instituted on 2 January 1954, so the answer is it was instituted in 1954.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Who among the following received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913?

129. C.V. Raman won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930 for his work on:
(a) Photoelectric effect (b) Scattering of light (c) Quantum statistics (d) Nuclear fission
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is quantum statistics, which is associated with S.N. Bose (Bose-Einstein statistics), not C.V. Raman. Raman was honoured for the discovery of the Raman Effect, concerning the scattering of light, so the answer is scattering of light.

132. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of institution of these awards?

Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing the Jnanpith Award before the Padma Awards. Bharat Ratna and Padma Awards were both instituted in 1954, Jnanpith in 1961 (first awarded 1965), and the Gandhi Peace Prize in 1995, so the answer is Bharat Ratna – Padma Awards – Jnanpith Award – Gandhi Peace Prize.
