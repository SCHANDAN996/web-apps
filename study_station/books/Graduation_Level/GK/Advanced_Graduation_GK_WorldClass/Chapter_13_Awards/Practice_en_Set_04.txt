76. The Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, was instituted in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1950 (c) 1954 (d) 1956
Answer: (c)
Solution: Many candidates pick 1950 because the Constitution came into force that year and several national honours are associated with it — that is the chronological trap. The Bharat Ratna was actually instituted on 2 January 1954, along with the Padma Vibhushan, so the answer is 1954.
Source: PYQ-style

77. Who was the first recipient of the Bharat Ratna in the field of science?
(a) Homi J. Bhabha (b) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (c) Vikram Sarabhai (d) C. V. Raman
Answer: (d)
Solution: Homi Bhabha and Vikram Sarabhai are tempting because of their fame, but neither received the Bharat Ratna. C. V. Raman, the Nobel laureate physicist, was among the first three Bharat Ratna recipients in 1954, so the answer is C. V. Raman.
Source: PYQ-style

78. Which of the following is the correct order of precedence of India's civilian awards, from highest to lowest?
(a) Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan, Bharat Ratna (b) Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri (c) Bharat Ratna, Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Shri (d) Padma Vibhushan, Bharat Ratna, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the option that places Padma Bhushan above Padma Vibhushan — the names sound similar and are often confused. The correct hierarchy is Bharat Ratna, then Padma Vibhushan, then Padma Bhushan, then Padma Shri, so the answer is Bharat Ratna, Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri.
Source: PYQ-style

79. The Padma awards are announced every year on the occasion of which day?
(a) Gandhi Jayanti (b) Republic Day (c) Independence Day (d) Constitution Day
Answer: (b)
Solution: Independence Day is the tempting wrong choice because gallantry awards like the Kirti Chakra are announced then. Padma awards, however, are announced annually on the eve of Republic Day, so the answer is Republic Day.
Source: PYQ-style

80. Who was the first foreigner to receive the Bharat Ratna?
(a) Nelson Mandela (b) Mikhail Gorbachev (c) Mother Teresa (d) Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nelson Mandela is the classic trap — he did receive the Bharat Ratna, but in 1990, three years after Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan, who was honoured in 1987. Mother Teresa was a naturalised Indian citizen, so the answer is Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan.
Source: PYQ-style

81. The Jnanpith Award is given for outstanding contribution in which field?
(a) Literature (b) Classical music (c) Social service (d) Science
Answer: (a)
Solution: Social service tempts candidates who confuse it with the Magsaysay or Gandhi Peace Prize. The Jnanpith Award, instituted in 1961, is India's premier literary honour, so the answer is literature.
Source: PYQ-style

82. Who was the first recipient of the Jnanpith Award in 1965?
(a) Mahadevi Verma (b) Kuvempu (c) Ashapurna Devi (d) G. Sankara Kurup
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kuvempu is the trap because he was the first Kannada writer to win it, but that was in 1967. The very first Jnanpith Award went to Malayalam poet G. Sankara Kurup for his work "Odakkuzhal", so the answer is G. Sankara Kurup.
Source: PYQ-style

83. The Dadasaheb Phalke Award is associated with which field?
(a) Cinema (b) Theatre (c) Painting (d) Journalism
Answer: (a)
Solution: Theatre is the tempting distractor since Phalke worked in a performing art, but the award — instituted in 1969 — is India's highest honour in cinema, named after the father of Indian cinema, so the answer is cinema.
Source: PYQ-style

84. Who was the first recipient of the Dadasaheb Phalke Award?
(a) Prithviraj Kapoor (b) Devika Rani (c) Satyajit Ray (d) B. N. Sircar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Satyajit Ray is the trap — he received it in 1984, much later. The first award in 1969 went to actress Devika Rani, so the answer is Devika Rani.
Source: PYQ-style

85. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award is given for excellence in which domain?
(a) Sports (b) Military service (c) Literature (d) Public administration
Answer: (a)
Solution: Military service tempts some because Dhyan Chand served in the Indian Army, but the award named after the hockey legend is India's highest sporting honour, so the answer is sports.

86. The Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award was renamed the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award in which year?
(a) 2019 (b) 2020 (c) 2021 (d) 2022
Answer: (c)
Solution: 2020 is the trap because the renaming decision was widely discussed around that time, but the official renaming was announced in August 2021, so the answer is 2021.
Source: PYQ-style

87. Which award is given to coaches for excellence in sports training?
(a) Arjuna Award (b) Dhyan Chand Award (c) Dronacharya Award (d) Tenzing Norgay Award
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Dhyan Chand Award is the trap — it honours lifetime achievement by sportspersons, not coaches. The Dronacharya Award, named after the guru of the Pandavas, is meant for coaches, so the answer is Dronacharya Award.
Source: PYQ-style

88. The Tenzing Norgay National Adventure Award is named after the famous mountaineer who climbed Mount Everest with whom?
(a) George Mallory (b) Eric Shipton (c) Edmund Hillary (d) Reinhold Messner
Answer: (c)
Solution: George Mallory tempts candidates because of his famous Everest attempts, but he never reached the summit. Tenzing Norgay and Edmund Hillary made the first confirmed ascent of Everest in 1953, so the answer is Edmund Hillary.
Source: NCERT Class 8 (general knowledge reference)

89. Which of the following is the highest peacetime gallantry award of India?
(a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Ashoka Chakra (c) Kirti Chakra (d) Shaurya Chakra
Answer: (b)
Solution: Param Vir Chakra is the classic trap — it is the highest gallantry award overall, but it is a wartime award. The highest peacetime gallantry award is the Ashoka Chakra, so the answer is Ashoka Chakra.
Source: PYQ-style

90. The Param Vir Chakra is awarded for acts of valour in which context?
(a) Peacetime (b) Counter-insurgency only (c) Disaster relief (d) In the presence of the enemy
Answer: (d)
Solution: Peacetime is the trap — that describes the Ashoka Chakra series. The Param Vir Chakra is awarded for the most conspicuous bravery in the presence of the enemy, so the answer is in the presence of the enemy.
Source: PYQ-style

91. The Nobel Prize is awarded in memory of Alfred Nobel, who was a citizen of which country?
(a) Norway (b) Denmark (c) Sweden (d) Switzerland
Answer: (c)
Solution: Norway is the trap because the Nobel Peace Prize is awarded in Oslo, Norway. Alfred Nobel himself was a Swedish chemist and the inventor of dynamite, so the answer is Sweden.
Source: PYQ-style

92. Who was the first Indian to receive the Nobel Prize?
(a) C. V. Raman (b) Rabindranath Tagore (c) Mother Teresa (d) Har Gobind Khorana
Answer: (b)
Solution: C. V. Raman is the trap as the first Indian Nobel winner in science (1930). Rabindranath Tagore won the Literature Nobel in 1913, making him the first Indian laureate, so the answer is Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: PYQ-style

93. The Nobel Peace Prize is awarded in which city?
(a) Geneva (b) Stockholm (c) Oslo (d) Copenhagen
Answer: (c)
Solution: Stockholm is the tempting wrong answer because all other Nobel Prizes are awarded there. By Alfred Nobel's will, the Peace Prize alone is awarded in Oslo, Norway, so the answer is Oslo.
Source: PYQ-style

94. The Pulitzer Prize is primarily associated with which fields?
(a) Journalism, literature and music (b) Peace and diplomacy (c) Science and medicine (d) Sports and adventure
Answer: (a)
Solution: Peace and diplomacy tempts candidates who confuse it with the Nobel Peace Prize. The Pulitzer Prize, established by Joseph Pulitzer, honours excellence in journalism, literature and musical composition in the USA, so the answer is journalism, literature and music.
Source: PYQ-style

95. The Ramon Magsaysay Award is named after a former president of which country?
(a) Indonesia (b) Philippines (c) Thailand (d) Malaysia
Answer: (b)
Solution: Indonesia is a common confusion because the award is often called "Asia's Nobel Prize" and covers all of Asia. Ramon Magsaysay was the third President of the Philippines, and the award is presented in Manila, so the answer is Philippines.
Source: PYQ-style

96. Which Indian was the first recipient of the Ramon Magsaysay Award in 1958?
(a) Vinoba Bhave (b) Jayaprakash Narayan (c) Verghese Kurien (d) M. S. Subbulakshmi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Jayaprakash Narayan is the trap — he won it in 1965 for public service. Vinoba Bhave, the Bhoodan movement leader, was the first Indian recipient in 1958, so the answer is Vinoba Bhave.
Source: PYQ-style

97. The Booker Prize is awarded for excellence in which field?
(a) Fiction written in English (b) Biography (c) Drama (d) Poetry
Answer: (a)
Solution: Poetry tempts some because of the prize's literary nature, but the Booker Prize is specifically awarded for the best novel written in English and published in the UK or Ireland, so the answer is fiction written in English.
Source: PYQ-style

98. Who was the first Indian citizen to win the Booker Prize?
(a) Salman Rushdie (b) Kiran Desai (c) Aravind Adiga (d) Arundhati Roy
Answer: (d)
Solution: Salman Rushdie is the famous trap — he won in 1981 for "Midnight's Children", but he was a British citizen at the time, not an Indian citizen. Arundhati Roy won in 1997 for "The God of Small Things", making her the first Indian citizen to win, so the answer is Arundhati Roy.
Source: PYQ-style

99. The Gandhi Peace Prize was instituted by the Government of India in which year?
(a) 2005 (b) 1990 (c) 2000 (d) 1995
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1990 is the trap because it sounds close to the 125th birth anniversary period of Gandhi, but the prize was instituted in 1995 to mark that anniversary, so the answer is 1995.
Source: PYQ-style

100. The Sahitya Akademi Award is given for books written in how many recognised languages?
(a) 24 (b) 22 (c) 18 (d) 26
Answer: (a)
Solution: 22 is the classic trap — that is the number of languages in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution. The Sahitya Akademi Award covers those 22 plus English and Rajasthani, totalling 24 languages, so the answer is 24.
