# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Science & Technology (Basic)

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 1: Vitamins — Fat-Soluble vs Water-Soluble

> **"A DEK of cards is FAT"**
> **A, D, E, K** are **Fat-soluble** vitamins. Everything else (B-complex, C) is water-soluble.
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* Vitamin C is NOT fat-soluble — examiners love swapping it in.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 2: Vitamin Deficiency Diseases

> **"A-Night, B1-Beri, C-Scurvy, D-Rickets"**
> - **A** → **Night** blindness
> - **B1** (Thiamine) → **Beri-beri**
> - **C** → **Scurvy**
> - **D** → **Rickets**
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* Rickets is Vitamin **D**, not Calcium deficiency alone — both appear as options.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 3: Electromagnetic Spectrum (increasing frequency)

> **"Raging Martians Invaded Venus Using X-ray Guns"**
> **R**adio → **M**icrowave → **I**nfrared → **V**isible → **U**ltraviolet → **X**-ray → **G**amma
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* Questions reverse the order (decreasing wavelength = increasing frequency). Radio has the LONGEST wavelength, Gamma the SHORTEST.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 4: Ozone Layer Absorbs…

> **"UV = Ultra-Villain, O3 = Our shield"**
> Ozone (O₃) absorbs **ultraviolet (UV)** rays — NOT infrared, NOT X-rays.
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* "Ozone absorbs infrared" is the classic wrong option.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 5: pH Scale

> **"7 is Heaven (Neutral); Below = Battery acid; Above = Bleach (Basic)"**
> pH < 7 → Acidic | pH = 7 → Neutral | pH > 7 → Basic
> Human blood pH ≈ **7.4** (slightly basic) — a favourite one-liner question.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 6: Common Acids in Daily Life

> **"Citrus CIT, Milk LAC, Vinegar ACE, Ant FOR"**
> - Citrus fruits → **Cit**ric acid
> - Milk/Curd → **Lac**tic acid
> - Vinegar → **Ace**tic acid
> - Ant sting → **For**mic acid
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* Ant sting = formic (methanoic) acid; the remedy is a BASE like baking soda, not more acid.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 7: Newton's Laws in One Line

> **"1-Inertia, 2-Force (F=ma), 3-Action-Reaction"**
> - 1st Law → Inertia (body resists change)
> - 2nd Law → F = ma
> - 3rd Law → Every action has an equal & opposite reaction
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* Rocket propulsion = **3rd law**, not the 1st.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 8: Units of Physics

> **"NewtON pushes, JouLE works, WatT races"**
> - Force → **Newton**
> - Work/Energy → **Joule**
> - Power → **Watt** (Joule/second)
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* Power is NOT Joule; Energy is NOT Watt. kWh is a unit of **energy**, not power.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 9: Human Body Numbers

> **"206 bones, 32 teeth, 46 chromosomes, 5-6 litres of blood"**
> Adult skeleton = **206** bones; adult teeth = **32**; human chromosomes = **46** (23 pairs); blood ≈ **5–6 L**.
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* A baby has ~300 bones — if the question says "adult," answer 206.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 10: Glands — Master & Largest

> **"Pituitary is the MASTER, Liver is the LARGEST, Thyroid is the LARGEST ENDOCRINE"**
> - Master gland → **Pituitary**
> - Largest gland (overall) → **Liver**
> - Largest endocrine gland → **Thyroid**
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* "Largest gland" vs "largest endocrine gland" — read the question twice.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 11: Nuclear Reactions

> **"FISSION splits (reactors, atom bomb); FUSION joins (Sun, hydrogen bomb)"**
> - Fission → heavy nucleus splits → nuclear power plants, atom bomb
> - Fusion → light nuclei join → Sun's energy, hydrogen bomb
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* The Sun runs on FUSION — examiners offer "fission" as the tempting wrong answer.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 12: Computer Memory Units

> **"KB-MB-GB-TB: King, Minister, General, Tiger — each 1024 times bigger"**
> 1 KB = 1024 Bytes; 1 MB = 1024 KB; 1 GB = 1024 MB; 1 TB = 1024 GB.
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* The multiplier is **1024**, not 1000 — a classic trap in "1 KB = ?" questions.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 13: Inventions Quick-Pair

> **"Bell TELEphoned, Edison LIT the bulb, Marconi RADIOed, Wrights FLEW"**
> - Telephone → Alexander Graham **Bell**
> - Electric bulb → Thomas **Edison**
> - Radio → **Marconi**
> - Aeroplane → **Wright brothers**
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* Bulb = Edison, not Tesla; Radio = Marconi, not Bell.

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 14: Blood Groups — Universal Rules

> **"O is the Generous Giver, AB is the Greedy Grabber"**
> - Universal **Donor** → **O negative**
> - Universal **Recipient** → **AB positive**
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* Donor and Recipient get swapped in options — remember "O Gives, AB Takes."

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic 15: Greenhouse Gases

> **"COW-Methane-NO" → CO₂, Water vapour, CH₄, N₂O, O₃**
> Major greenhouse gases: Carbon dioxide, Water vapour, Methane, Nitrous oxide, Ozone.
> 🎯 *Trap alert:* **Oxygen (O₂) and Nitrogen (N₂) are NOT greenhouse gases** — the most repeated trap in this topic.

# ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

### Tip 1: The "Half-Known List" Rule
If a question asks you to match **four** scientists with inventions (or four vitamins with deficiency diseases) and you are sure of only two or three, **skip immediately**. In S&T match-the-following questions, one unknown pair makes the whole question a coin toss — the negative marking will cost more than the guess earns.

### Tip 2: The "Numeric Precision" Rule
If a question demands an exact figure you only vaguely remember (e.g., "normal human body temperature in °F," "pH of blood," "speed of light in km/s") and two options differ only slightly (98.4°F vs 98.6°F), **skip it**. Vague memory + close options = trap territory. Attempt only if you can recall the exact value with confidence.

### Tip 3: The "Statement Jugglery" Rule
In multi-statement questions ("Which of the above statements is/are correct?"), if even **one** statement contains an absolute word you cannot verify — *always, never, only, all* — and you are unsure about it, **skip**. Examiners hide one wrong absolute statement among three true ones; guessing here is statistically a losing bet.
