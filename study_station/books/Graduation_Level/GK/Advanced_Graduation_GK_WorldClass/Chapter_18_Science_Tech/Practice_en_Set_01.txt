1. Which vitamin is synthesized in the human skin on exposure to sunlight?
(a) Vitamin D (b) Vitamin C (c) Vitamin A (d) Vitamin K
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vitamin A is obtained from diet (retinol), Vitamin C from citrus fruits, and Vitamin K is made by gut bacteria — these are tempting but wrong. Ultraviolet rays convert 7-dehydrocholesterol in the skin into Vitamin D, so the answer is Vitamin D.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

2. The SI unit of electric current is:
(a) Volt (b) Ohm (c) Ampere (d) Watt
Answer: (c)
Solution: Volt measures potential difference, Ohm measures resistance, and Watt measures power — all related to electricity, which is the trap. The SI unit of current itself is the Ampere, so the answer is Ampere.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

3. Which of the following is a non-renewable source of energy?
(a) Solar energy (b) Wind energy (c) Natural gas (d) Hydropower
Answer: (c)
Solution: Solar, wind and hydropower are naturally replenished, so they are renewable. Natural gas is a fossil fuel formed over millions of years and gets exhausted, so the answer is Natural gas.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

4. The chemical formula of common salt is:
(a) Na₂CO₃ (b) NaHCO₃ (c) NaCl (d) KCl
Answer: (c)
Solution: Na₂CO₃ is washing soda and NaHCO₃ is baking soda — similar-looking formulas are the examiner's trap. Common salt is sodium chloride, so the answer is NaCl.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

5. Which organ of the human body produces insulin?
(a) Liver (b) Pancreas (c) Kidney (d) Spleen
Answer: (b)
Solution: The liver produces bile, which confuses many candidates, but insulin is secreted by the beta cells of the islets of Langerhans in the pancreas, so the answer is Pancreas.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

6. The speed of light in vacuum is approximately:
(a) 3 × 10⁸ m/s (b) 3 × 10⁶ m/s (c) 3 × 10¹⁰ m/s (d) 3 × 10⁴ m/s
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap lies in the power of ten; candidates often misremember the exponent. The accepted value is about 300,000 km/s, i.e. 3 × 10⁸ m/s, so the answer is 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

7. Which gas is most abundant in the Earth's atmosphere?
(a) Oxygen (b) Carbon dioxide (c) Hydrogen (d) Nitrogen
Answer: (d)
Solution: Oxygen feels like the obvious answer because we breathe it, but it forms only about 21% of air. Nitrogen makes up about 78%, so the answer is Nitrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science

8. The pH value of a neutral solution at 25°C is:
(a) 0 (b) 7 (c) 14 (d) 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: Values below 7 are acidic and above 7 are basic; 0 and 14 are the extremes, not neutrality. Pure water is neutral with pH 7, so the answer is 7.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

9. Which blood cells help in clotting of blood?
(a) Platelets (b) White blood cells (c) Red blood cells (d) Plasma cells
Answer: (a)
Solution: RBCs carry oxygen and WBCs fight infection — functions often swapped in the exam. Platelets (thrombocytes) are responsible for clotting, so the answer is Platelets.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

10. The device used to measure atmospheric pressure is:
(a) Barometer (b) Thermometer (c) Ammeter (d) Odometer
Answer: (a)
Solution: A thermometer measures temperature and an ammeter measures current; the similar-sounding names are the trap. A barometer measures atmospheric pressure, so the answer is Barometer.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

11. Which of the following diseases is caused by a virus?
(a) Tuberculosis (b) Cholera (c) Typhoid (d) Measles
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tuberculosis, cholera and typhoid are all bacterial diseases — a classic trap grouping. Measles is caused by a virus, so the answer is Measles.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

12. The process by which plants make their food is called:
(a) Respiration (b) Transpiration (c) Photosynthesis (d) Fermentation
Answer: (c)
Solution: Respiration breaks down food and transpiration is water loss — both plant processes, hence confusing. Food synthesis using sunlight, CO₂ and water is photosynthesis, so the answer is Photosynthesis.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science

13. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?
(a) Sodium (b) Aluminium (c) Mercury (d) Gallium only in solid form
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sodium is soft but solid, and aluminium is solid; gallium melts near 30°C but is solid at normal room temperature. Mercury is liquid at room temperature, so the answer is Mercury.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

14. The full form of DNA is:
(a) Dual Nucleic Acid (b) Dinucleic Acid (c) Deoxyribose Nucleic Atom (d) Deoxyribonucleic Acid
Answer: (d)
Solution: The wrong options mimic the real expansion with small word changes — a typical trap. DNA stands for Deoxyribonucleic Acid, so the answer is Deoxyribonucleic Acid.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

15. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
(a) Mars (b) Jupiter (c) Venus (d) Mercury
Answer: (a)
Solution: Venus is called the morning/evening star, which confuses candidates. Mars appears red due to iron oxide on its surface, so the answer is Mars.

16. The unit of frequency is:
(a) Hertz (b) Joule (c) Newton (d) Pascal
Answer: (a)
Solution: Joule is energy, Newton is force and Pascal is pressure — all SI units, which is the trap. Frequency is measured in Hertz, so the answer is Hertz.

17. Which of the following is the hardest natural substance?
(a) Quartz (b) Diamond (c) Graphite (d) Corundum
Answer: (b)
Solution: Graphite is chemically the same element (carbon) as diamond but is soft — a famous trap. Diamond is the hardest naturally occurring substance, so the answer is Diamond.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

18. The instrument used to see distant objects in space is:
(a) Microscope (b) Telescope (c) Periscope (d) Endoscope
Answer: (b)
Solution: A microscope magnifies tiny objects and an endoscope views inside the body; the "-scope" suffix is the trap. A telescope is used for distant celestial objects, so the answer is Telescope.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

19. Which vitamin deficiency causes night blindness?
(a) Vitamin D (b) Vitamin B₁ (c) Vitamin C (d) Vitamin A
Answer: (d)
Solution: Vitamin C deficiency causes scurvy and Vitamin D deficiency causes rickets — frequently interchanged. Night blindness results from Vitamin A deficiency, so the answer is Vitamin A.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

20. The gas released during photosynthesis is:
(a) Carbon dioxide (b) Oxygen (c) Nitrogen (d) Hydrogen
Answer: (b)
Solution: Plants use carbon dioxide, so candidates often pick it — but that is the input, not the output. Oxygen is released as a by-product of photosynthesis, so the answer is Oxygen.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Science

21. Consider the following statements:
1. Sound travels faster in solids than in gases.
2. Sound can travel through vacuum.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — it sounds plausible because light travels through vacuum, but sound is a mechanical wave and needs a medium. Sound does travel fastest in solids, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

22. Match the following scientists with their discoveries:
1. Alexander Fleming — Penicillin
2. Edward Jenner — Smallpox vaccine
3. Louis Pasteur — Rabies vaccine
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming one pair must be wrong because exams usually include a mismatch; here all three are historically correct. Fleming discovered penicillin (1928), Jenner developed the smallpox vaccine (1796) and Pasteur the rabies vaccine, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

23. Which of the following statements about acids is correct?
(a) Acids turn red litmus blue (b) Acids turn blue litmus red (c) Acids have pH greater than 7 (d) Acids taste bitter
Answer: (b)
Solution: Turning red litmus blue, pH above 7 and bitter taste are all properties of bases — the options deliberately swap acid-base traits. Acids turn blue litmus red, so the answer is Acids turn blue litmus red.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

24. Arrange the following electromagnetic waves in increasing order of wavelength:
1. X-rays 2. Visible light 3. Radio waves
(a) 1, 3, 2 (b) 3, 2, 1 (c) 2, 1, 3 (d) 1, 2, 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is reversing the order — radio waves have the longest wavelength, not the shortest. X-rays are shortest, then visible light, then radio waves, so the answer is 1, 2, 3.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

25. Consider the following statements about the human heart:
1. It has four chambers.
2. The right side of the heart pumps oxygenated blood to the body.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 only (b) 1 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — the right side pumps deoxygenated blood to the lungs; it is the left side that sends oxygenated blood to the body. The heart does have four chambers, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science
