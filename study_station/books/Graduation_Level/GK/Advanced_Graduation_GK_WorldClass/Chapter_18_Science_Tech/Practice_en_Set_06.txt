126. Which of the following statements about the pH scale is correct?
(a) pH of a neutral solution at 25°C is 0 (b) pH of human blood is slightly alkaline, around 7.4 (c) pH of gastric juice is around 7 (d) Lower pH always means weaker acid
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims neutrality means pH 0 — that is a trap; pH 0 is strongly acidic, and neutrality at 25°C is pH 7. Gastric juice is highly acidic (pH around 1.5–2), not 7. Human blood is maintained slightly alkaline at about 7.35–7.45, so the answer is pH of human blood is slightly alkaline, around 7.4.

150. Which of the following correctly states Ohm's law and its formula?
(a) V = I/R, where V is voltage, I is current, R is resistance (b) V = R/I, where V is voltage, I is current, R is resistance (c) V = I²R, where V is voltage, I is current, R is resistance (d) V = IR, where V is voltage, I is current, R is resistance

Solution: The trap is the formula I²R, which is the expression for electrical power dissipated as heat (P = I²R), not Ohm's law.
