Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Which part of the human eye controls the amount of light entering it?
(a) Cornea (b) Iris (c) Retina (d) Lens
Answer: (b)
Solution: The cornea only refracts light and the retina forms the image, so they are tempting but wrong. The iris is the muscular diaphragm that adjusts the pupil size and thus controls the light entering the eye, so the answer is Iris.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

52. The SI unit of electric resistance is:
(a) Ampere (b) Volt (c) Ohm (d) Watt
Answer: (c)
Solution: Ampere measures current and volt measures potential difference, which often confuse students. Resistance is measured in ohms, named after Georg Ohm, so the answer is Ohm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

53. Which of the following acids is present in vinegar?
(a) Citric acid (b) Tartaric acid (c) Lactic acid (d) Acetic acid
Answer: (d)
Solution: Citric acid is found in citrus fruits and lactic acid in curd, which are common traps. Vinegar contains 4–8% acetic acid (ethanoic acid), so the answer is Acetic acid.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

54. The phenomenon of light bending as it passes from one medium to another is called:
(a) Reflection (b) Dispersion (c) Diffraction (d) Refraction
Answer: (d)
Solution: Dispersion is the splitting of white light into colours, a near-synonym trap. The bending of light due to change in speed between media is refraction, so the answer is Refraction.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

55. Which blood cells are primarily responsible for fighting infections?
(a) White blood cells (b) Red blood cells (c) Platelets (d) Plasma cells
Answer: (a)
Solution: Platelets help in clotting and RBCs carry oxygen, so both are traps. White blood cells (leucocytes) form the body's defence against pathogens, so the answer is White blood cells.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

56. The chemical formula of washing soda is:
(a) NaHCO₃ (b) CaOCl₂ (c) NaOH (d) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
Answer: (d)
Solution: NaHCO₃ is baking soda, the classic similar-name trap. Washing soda is sodium carbonate decahydrate, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O, so the answer is Na₂CO₃·10H₂O.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

57. Which planet has the largest number of confirmed moons as of 2023?
(a) Saturn (b) Jupiter (c) Uranus (d) Neptune
Answer: (a)
Solution: Jupiter long held the record, which makes it the tempting wrong choice. As of 2023, Saturn leads with over 140 confirmed moons, so the answer is Saturn.
Source: PYQ-style

58. The pH value of a neutral solution at 25°C is:
(a) 0 (b) 14 (c) 7 (d) 1
Answer: (c)
Solution: Values 0 and 14 are the extremes of the scale, not neutrality. A neutral solution like pure water has pH 7 at 25°C, so the answer is 7.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

59. Which of the following is a non-renewable source of energy?
(a) Solar energy (b) Wind energy (c) Natural gas (d) Hydropower
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hydropower seems industrial but is renewable, which traps many. Natural gas is a fossil fuel that cannot be replenished on a human timescale, so the answer is Natural gas.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

60. The instrument used to measure atmospheric pressure is:
(a) Thermometer (b) Barometer (c) Ammeter (d) Anemometer
Answer: (b)
Solution: An anemometer measures wind speed, a similar-sounding trap. A barometer measures atmospheric pressure, so the answer is Barometer.
Source: NCERT Class 8 Science

61. Which vitamin is synthesised in the human skin on exposure to sunlight?
(a) Vitamin D (b) Vitamin C (c) Vitamin A (d) Vitamin K
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vitamin K is made by gut bacteria, which confuses students. Vitamin D is synthesised in the skin under ultraviolet sunlight, so the answer is Vitamin D.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

62. The process by which plants lose water through their leaves is called:
(a) Photosynthesis (b) Respiration (c) Translocation (d) Transpiration
Answer: (d)
Solution: Translocation is the movement of food through phloem, a similar-word trap. Loss of water vapour through stomata of leaves is transpiration, so the answer is Transpiration.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

63. Which of the following metals is liquid at room temperature?
(a) Sodium (b) Aluminium (c) Mercury (d) Gallium only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gallium melts near 30°C, but it is solid at standard room temperature, making it a trap. Mercury is the metal that is liquid at room temperature, so the answer is Mercury.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

64. The full form of 'DNA' is:
(a) Deoxyribose Nucleic Acid (b) Deoxyribonucleic Acid (c) Dinucleic Acid (d) Deoxyribonuclear Acid
Answer: (b)
Solution: "Deoxyribose Nucleic Acid" looks almost correct and is the examiner's favourite trap. The correct expansion is Deoxyribonucleic Acid, so the answer is Deoxyribonucleic Acid.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

65. Which gas is commonly known as 'laughing gas'?
(a) Nitrogen dioxide (b) Carbon monoxide (c) Nitrous oxide (d) Sulphur dioxide
Answer: (c)
Solution: Nitrogen dioxide is a similar-name trap and is a pollutant, not an anaesthetic. Nitrous oxide (N₂O) is called laughing gas, so the answer is Nitrous oxide.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

66. The speed of light in vacuum is approximately:
(a) 3 × 10⁶ m/s (b) 3 × 10¹⁰ m/s (c) 3 × 10⁸ m/s (d) 3 × 10⁴ m/s
Answer: (c)
Solution: The powers of ten are the trap here; students often misremember the exponent. Light travels at about 3 × 10⁸ m/s in vacuum, so the answer is 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

67. Which organ in the human body produces insulin?
(a) Pancreas (b) Liver (c) Kidney (d) Spleen
Answer: (a)
Solution: The liver regulates blood sugar by storing glycogen, which makes it a tempting trap. Insulin is secreted by the beta cells of the islets of Langerhans in the pancreas, so the answer is Pancreas.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

68. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of these space missions of India?
(a) Chandrayaan-1 – Aryabhata – Mangalyaan – Chandrayaan-3 (b) Aryabhata – Chandrayaan-1 – Mangalyaan – Chandrayaan-3 (c) Aryabhata – Mangalyaan – Chandrayaan-1 – Chandrayaan-3 (d) Mangalyaan – Aryabhata – Chandrayaan-1 – Chandrayaan-3
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a chronological-confusion trap: Mangalyaan (2013) came after Chandrayaan-1 (2008), not before. Aryabhata (1975), Chandrayaan-1 (2008), Mangalyaan (2013), Chandrayaan-3 (2023) is correct, so the answer is Aryabhata – Chandrayaan-1 – Mangalyaan – Chandrayaan-3.
Source: ISRO official website

69. The unit of power is:
(a) Joule (b) Newton (c) Pascal (d) Watt
Answer: (d)
Solution: Joule measures energy, and power is energy per unit time — a classic confusion. Power is measured in watts, so the answer is Watt.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

70. Which of the following diseases is caused by a virus?
(a) Measles (b) Cholera (c) Tuberculosis (d) Typhoid
Answer: (a)
Solution: Tuberculosis, cholera and typhoid are all bacterial, which is the trap in this list. Measles is caused by a virus, so the answer is Measles.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

71. The filament of an electric bulb is made of:
(a) Tungsten (b) Copper (c) Aluminium (d) Nichrome
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nichrome is used in heaters, not bulbs, and is the common trap. The bulb filament is made of tungsten because of its very high melting point, so the answer is Tungsten.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

72. Which of the following statements about sound is correct?
(a) Sound travels fastest in solids (b) Sound travels fastest in gases (c) Sound travels fastest in vacuum (d) Sound travels at equal speed in all media
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the idea that sound can travel in vacuum — it cannot, since it needs a medium. Sound travels fastest in solids because particles are closely packed, so the answer is Sound travels fastest in solids.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

73. The chemical symbol 'Fe' stands for:
(a) Fluorine (b) Zinc (c) Lead (d) Iron
Answer: (d)
Solution: Lead (Pb) tempts students because its symbol also differs from its English name. Fe comes from the Latin 'ferrum' and stands for iron, so the answer is Iron.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Science

74. Which of the following is used as a moderator in a nuclear reactor?
(a) Uranium-235 (b) Heavy water (c) Cadmium rods (d) Graphite-free water
Answer: (b)
Solution: Cadmium rods are control rods that absorb neutrons, the classic trap; uranium-235 is the fuel. Heavy water (D₂O) slows down fast neutrons as a moderator, so the answer is Heavy water.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

75. The ozone layer is mainly found in which layer of the atmosphere?
(a) Troposphere (b) Stratosphere (c) Mesosphere (d) Thermosphere
Answer: (b)
Solution: The troposphere is where weather occurs and is the tempting first layer, but ozone there is a pollutant. The protective ozone layer lies in the stratosphere, so the answer is Stratosphere.
Source: NCERT Class 7 Geography
