Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Montreal Protocol was signed in which year?
(a) 1987 (b) 1985 (c) 1989 (d) 1992
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Vienna Convention (1985) is the tempting wrong choice — it only created the framework for ozone protection, while the actual protocol with binding controls on ozone-depleting substances was signed in 1987, so the answer is 1987.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

77. The Kigali Amendment to the Montreal Protocol deals with the phase-down of which substances?
(a) Chlorofluorocarbons (b) Methyl bromide (c) Halons (d) Hydrofluorocarbons
Answer: (d)
Solution: CFCs and halons were already controlled by the original Montreal Protocol, so they are traps here; the Kigali Amendment (2016) targets HFCs, which are powerful greenhouse gases though not ozone-depleting, so the answer is Hydrofluorocarbons.
Source: PYQ-style

78. The Basel Convention is concerned with the control of:
(a) Persistent organic pollutants (b) Trade in endangered species (c) Ozone-depleting substances (d) Transboundary movements of hazardous wastes
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Stockholm Convention (POPs) and CITES (endangered species) are similar-sounding traps; the Basel Convention (1989) specifically regulates transboundary movement of hazardous wastes and their disposal, so the answer is transboundary movements of hazardous wastes.
Source: PYQ-style

79. The Stockholm Convention (2001) originally listed how many chemicals as the "dirty dozen"?
(a) 10 (b) 16 (c) 14 (d) 12
Answer: (d)
Solution: The number 10 is a tempting round-number trap; the Stockholm Convention initially targeted 12 persistent organic pollutants, popularly called the "dirty dozen", so the answer is 12.
Source: PYQ-style

80. The United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) was adopted at:
(a) Stockholm, 1972 (b) Kyoto, 1997 (c) Rio de Janeiro, 1992 (d) Paris, 2015
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kyoto and Paris are traps because they are protocols/agreements under the UNFCCC, not the convention itself; the UNFCCC was adopted at the Earth Summit in Rio de Janeiro in 1992, so the answer is Rio de Janeiro, 1992.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Environment and Natural Resources)

81. The Kyoto Protocol committed developed countries to reduce greenhouse gas emissions with reference to the base year:
(a) 1985 (b) 2000 (c) 1995 (d) 1990
Answer: (d)
Solution: The year 1995 is a trap because the first Conference of Parties (COP-1) was held in Berlin in 1995; the Kyoto Protocol's emission reduction targets were measured against 1990 levels, so the answer is 1990.
Source: PYQ-style

82. The Paris Agreement aims to limit global warming to well below:
(a) 1°C above pre-industrial levels (b) 2°C above pre-industrial levels (c) 3°C above pre-industrial levels (d) 4°C above pre-industrial levels
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1.5°C is a tempting trap, but it is only the "pursue efforts" aspirational target, not the main limit; the agreement's central goal is well below 2°C, so the answer is 2°C above pre-industrial levels.
Source: PYQ-style

83. The Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) was opened for signature at:
(a) Rio Earth Summit, 1992 (b) Stockholm Conference, 1972 (c) Johannesburg Summit, 2002 (d) Nairobi, 1982
Answer: (a)
Solution: Stockholm 1972 is a chronological-confusion trap — it was the first UN environment conference but produced no biodiversity treaty; the CBD was one of the two conventions opened for signature at Rio in 1992, so the answer is Rio Earth Summit, 1992.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology (Biodiversity and Conservation)

84. The Nagoya Protocol is associated with:
(a) Phase-out of POPs (b) Liability for biosafety damage (c) Access and benefit-sharing of genetic resources (d) Wetland conservation
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Cartagena Protocol (biosafety) is the similar-name trap here; the Nagoya Protocol (2010) deals with access to genetic resources and fair sharing of benefits arising from their use, so the answer is access and benefit-sharing of genetic resources.
Source: PYQ-style

85. The Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety primarily regulates:
(a) Ozone-depleting substances (b) Hazardous chemicals (c) Nuclear waste (d) Living modified organisms (LMOs)
Answer: (d)
Solution: Hazardous chemicals fall under the Rotterdam Convention, which is the trap; the Cartagena Protocol (2000) governs the safe transfer and handling of living modified organisms, so the answer is living modified organisms (LMOs).
Source: PYQ-style

86. The Ramsar Convention (1971) is related to the conservation of:
(a) Wetlands (b) Mangroves (c) Coral reefs (d) Rainforests
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mangroves are a tempting trap because they are often wetland ecosystems, but the convention covers wetlands of international importance broadly; Ramsar is a city in Iran where it was signed, so the answer is wetlands.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology (Biodiversity and Conservation)

87. CITES (Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species) was signed in:
(a) 1971 (b) 1973 (c) 1975 (d) 1980
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1971 is a chronological trap because the Ramsar Convention was signed that year; CITES, also called the Washington Convention, was signed in 1973 and entered into force in 1975, so the answer is 1973.
Source: PYQ-style

88. The Rotterdam Convention is based on which principle for trade in hazardous chemicals?
(a) Polluter Pays Principle (b) Prior Informed Consent (PIC) (c) Precautionary Principle (d) Common but Differentiated Responsibilities
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Polluter Pays Principle is a plausible-sounding trap but belongs to general environmental law (Rio Declaration); the Rotterdam Convention operates through the Prior Informed Consent procedure for listed hazardous chemicals, so the answer is Prior Informed Consent (PIC).
Source: PYQ-style

89. The Minamata Convention (2013) addresses pollution caused by:
(a) Lead (b) Arsenic (c) Cadmium (d) Mercury
Answer: (d)
Solution: Cadmium is a trap because it caused Itai-itai disease in Japan, creating a similar-name confusion; Minamata disease in Japan was caused by mercury poisoning, and the convention targets mercury, so the answer is mercury.
Source: PYQ-style

90. The concept of "Common but Differentiated Responsibilities" (CBDR) is a principle of:
(a) Montreal Protocol (b) UNFCCC (c) Basel Convention (d) Ramsar Convention
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Montreal Protocol is a trap because it also gave developing countries grace periods, but CBDR as a formal principle belongs to the climate regime; it is enshrined in the UNFCCC, so the answer is UNFCCC.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Environment and Natural Resources)

91. The first United Nations Conference on the Human Environment (1972) was held at:
(a) Geneva (b) Nairobi (c) Stockholm (d) New York
Answer: (c)
Solution: Nairobi is a trap because UNEP, created after this conference, is headquartered there; the 1972 conference itself was held in Stockholm, Sweden, so the answer is Stockholm.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Environment and Natural Resources)

92. The Vienna Convention (1985) is related to:
(a) Protection of the ozone layer (b) Climate change (c) Biodiversity (d) Desertification
Answer: (a)
Solution: Climate change is a trap because the Vienna Convention is often confused with the UNFCCC; the Vienna Convention is the framework treaty for the protection of the ozone layer, under which the Montreal Protocol was made, so the answer is protection of the ozone layer.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

93. The UN Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD) is particularly relevant to which Indian programme context?
(a) Land degradation neutrality (b) Coastal zone regulation (c) Tiger conservation (d) River interlinking
Answer: (a)
Solution: River interlinking is a trap because it sounds land-related but is a water-resource scheme; the UNCCD's central modern goal is achieving land degradation neutrality, which India has also pledged, so the answer is land degradation neutrality.
Source: PYQ-style

94. The "Earth Summit" of 1992 is formally known as:
(a) UN Conference on the Human Environment (b) World Summit on Sustainable Development (c) UN Conference on Environment and Development (d) Rio+20 Conference
Answer: (c)
Solution: The World Summit on Sustainable Development is a trap — that was Johannesburg 2002; the 1992 Rio Earth Summit was formally the UN Conference on Environment and Development (UNCED), so the answer is UN Conference on Environment and Development.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Environment and Natural Resources)

95. Under the Paris Agreement, countries submit their climate action plans called:
(a) National Action Plans (b) Nationally Determined Contributions (c) National Adaptation Programmes (d) Intended Mitigation Goals
Answer: (b)
Solution: National Action Plans is a trap because India has a National Action Plan on Climate Change (2008), but that is a domestic document; under the Paris Agreement the submissions are called Nationally Determined Contributions, so the answer is Nationally Determined Contributions.
Source: PYQ-style

96. The Kyoto Protocol's first commitment period covered the years:
(a) 2005–2012 (b) 2008–2012 (c) 2013–2020 (d) 2000–2008
Answer: (b)
Solution: 2005–2012 is a tempting trap because the protocol entered into force in 2005, but the first commitment period officially ran from 2008 to 2012, so the answer is 2008–2012.
Source: PYQ-style

97. Which of the following is NOT a greenhouse gas covered under the Kyoto Protocol basket?
(a) Sulphur dioxide (b) Methane (c) Carbon dioxide (d) Nitrous oxide
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nitrous oxide is a trap because it sounds like an ordinary pollutant but is indeed a Kyoto greenhouse gas; sulphur dioxide is an air pollutant causing acid rain but is not in the Kyoto basket, so the answer is sulphur dioxide.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

98. The "Rio+20" Conference (2012) produced which outcome document?
(a) Agenda 21 (b) Rio Declaration (c) The Future We Want (d) Johannesburg Plan of Implementation
Answer: (c)
Solution: Agenda 21 is a chronological trap — it was an outcome of the original 1992 Rio Summit, not Rio+20; the 2012 conference adopted the document "The Future We Want", so the answer is The Future We Want.
Source: PYQ-style

99. The Bonn Convention is associated with the conservation of:
(a) World heritage sites (b) Wetlands (c) Migratory species of wild animals (d) Marine pollution
Answer: (c)
Solution: Wetlands is a trap because Ramsar is the more famous convention of that era; the Bonn Convention (CMS, 1979) deals with the conservation of migratory species of wild animals, so the answer is migratory species of wild animals.
Source: PYQ-style

100. The Doha Amendment relates to the second commitment period of:
(a) Kyoto Protocol (b) Montreal Protocol (c) Paris Agreement (d) Basel Convention
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Paris Agreement is a trap because it is the more recent climate treaty, but the Doha Amendment (2012) established the Kyoto Protocol's second commitment period (2013–2020), so the answer is Kyoto Protocol.
Source: PYQ-style
