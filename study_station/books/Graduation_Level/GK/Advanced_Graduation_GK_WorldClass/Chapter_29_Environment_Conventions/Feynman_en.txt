# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Environment Conventions

## The Most Confusing Concept: "Montreal vs Kyoto — Both Are 'Protocols', So Both Must Be About Climate Change… Right?"

This is the single biggest trap in this chapter. Almost every exam (SSC CGL, IBPS, State PCS) has asked some version of: *"Which protocol deals with ozone depletion?"* — and lakhs of students confidently tick **Kyoto**. Wrong. Let me explain it like you're 12.

### The Simple Explanation

Imagine your house has **two different problems**:

1. **The roof has a hole** — sunlight (harmful UV rays) is pouring in and burning everyone's skin.
2. **The house is getting too hot** — because everyone inside is cooking on too many stoves at once, and the heat can't escape.

Now, would you call the **same repairman** for both? No!

- For the **hole in the roof**, you call the man who fixes roofs → that's the **Montreal Protocol (1987)**. It banned the chemicals (CFCs — from old fridges and sprays) that were punching a hole in the **ozone layer**.
- For the **overheating house**, you call the man who controls the stoves → that's the **Kyoto Protocol (1997)** and later the **Paris Agreement (2015)**. They control **greenhouse gases** (CO₂, methane) that trap heat and cause **global warming**.

**Ozone hole ≠ Global warming.** Two different problems, two different treaties, two different cities. The exam-setter's whole game is to make you mix them up.

### One More Twist (The Advanced Part)

Here's where it gets spicy. The chemicals that *replaced* CFCs (called HFCs) saved the ozone layer — but turned out to be powerful **greenhouse gases**! So in **2016**, the world signed the **Kigali Amendment** — and guess which treaty it amended? **Montreal**, not Kyoto! So today, the Montreal Protocol fights *both* ozone depletion *and* (through Kigali) some climate warming. That's why Kigali questions are the new favourite trap.

### The Unforgettable Trick 🪄

**"M-O-N-T = Mera Ozone Neemrana Treaty"** — just remember:

> **M**ontreal = **O**zone (the roof)
> **K**yoto = **K**arbon (the heat)
> **K**igali = **K**icks HFCs out (amendment to Montreal, 2016)
> **P**aris = **P**romise of 1.5–2°C (replaces Kyoto's system, 2015)

Or even shorter: **"Montreal mends the sky, Kyoto cools the pie."** 🥧

### Quick Self-Test Table (Say It Aloud)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Hole in ozone layer? | Montreal Protocol, 1987 |
| Greenhouse gas reduction targets? | Kyoto Protocol, 1997 |
| HFC phase-down? | Kigali Amendment, 2016 (to Montreal!) |
| 1.5°C / 2°C goal, NDCs? | Paris Agreement, 2015 |
