1. The United Nations Conference on the Human Environment (Stockholm Conference) was held in which year?
(a) 1972 (b) 1982 (c) 1992 (d) 2002
Answer: (a)
Solution: The 1992 date is a trap — that was the Rio Earth Summit, not Stockholm. The Stockholm Conference was the first major UN conference on environmental issues, held in June 1972, so the answer is 1972.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

2. The Rio Earth Summit (UNCED) of 1992 was held in which city?
(a) Rio de Janeiro (b) Stockholm (c) Geneva (d) Nairobi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Stockholm hosted the 1972 conference, which is the common confusion. The 1992 UN Conference on Environment and Development took place in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, so the answer is Rio de Janeiro.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

3. The Montreal Protocol deals with the phase-out of substances that deplete which layer?
(a) Troposphere (b) Ozone layer (c) Ionosphere (d) Mesosphere
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Montreal Protocol (1987) targets ozone-depleting substances like CFCs and halons. It is often confused with the Kyoto Protocol, which deals with greenhouse gases, so the answer is the ozone layer.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

4. The Kyoto Protocol was adopted in which year?
(a) 1987 (b) 1992 (c) 1997 (d) 2015
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1987 is the Montreal Protocol year and 2015 is the Paris Agreement year — both are frequent traps. The Kyoto Protocol was adopted in December 1997 at Kyoto, Japan, so the answer is 1997.
Source: UNFCCC official records

5. The Paris Agreement on climate change was adopted in which year?
(a) 2009 (b) 2012 (c) 2015 (d) 2019
Answer: (c)
Solution: The 2009 Copenhagen conference is the tempting wrong option, but it produced only a non-binding accord. The Paris Agreement was adopted at COP21 in December 2015, so the answer is 2015.
Source: UNFCCC official records

6. The Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) was opened for signature at which conference?
(a) Stockholm Conference, 1972 (b) Johannesburg Summit, 2002 (c) Rio Earth Summit, 1992 (d) Paris COP21, 2015
Answer: (c)
Solution: Stockholm 1972 predates the CBD by two decades. The CBD, along with UNFCCC and UNCCD, was opened for signature at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit, so the answer is Rio Earth Summit, 1992.
Source: Convention on Biological Diversity official records

7. The Basel Convention is concerned with the control of:
(a) Persistent organic pollutants (b) Trade in endangered species (c) Ozone-depleting substances (d) Transboundary movements of hazardous wastes
Answer: (d)
Solution: Trade in endangered species is CITES and persistent organic pollutants fall under the Stockholm Convention — both are classic name-confusion traps. The Basel Convention (1989) regulates transboundary movement of hazardous wastes, so the answer is transboundary movements of hazardous wastes.
Source: Basel Convention official records

8. CITES is an international agreement related to:
(a) Climate change (b) Wetland conservation (c) International trade in endangered species of wild fauna and flora (d) Marine pollution
Answer: (c)
Solution: Wetland conservation belongs to the Ramsar Convention, a frequent mix-up. CITES (1973, Washington D.C.) regulates international trade in endangered species, so the answer is international trade in endangered species of wild fauna and flora.
Source: CITES official records

9. The Ramsar Convention (1971) is associated with the conservation of:
(a) Deserts (b) Coral reefs (c) Wetlands (d) Tropical forests
Answer: (c)
Solution: Desertification is covered by UNCCD, not Ramsar. The Ramsar Convention, signed in the Iranian city of Ramsar in 1971, focuses on wetlands of international importance, so the answer is wetlands.
Source: Ramsar Convention official records

10. The Stockholm Convention (2001) deals with:
(a) Hazardous waste movement (b) Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs) (c) Greenhouse gas reduction (d) Biodiversity conservation
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hazardous waste movement is the Basel Convention — the similar city names (Basel/Stockholm) are a deliberate trap. The Stockholm Convention of 2001 targets Persistent Organic Pollutants such as DDT and dioxins, so the answer is Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs).
Source: Stockholm Convention official records

11. The headquarters of the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is located at:
(a) Geneva (b) Nairobi (c) New York (d) Paris
Answer: (b)
Solution: Geneva hosts many UN bodies, which makes it the tempting wrong choice. UNEP, established after the 1972 Stockholm Conference, is headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, so the answer is Nairobi.
Source: UNEP official records

12. The Kigali Amendment (2016) is related to which environmental treaty?
(a) Kyoto Protocol (b) Montreal Protocol (c) Paris Agreement (d) Basel Convention
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Kigali Amendment is often wrongly linked to the Paris Agreement because both concern climate. It actually amends the Montreal Protocol to phase down hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), so the answer is the Montreal Protocol.
Source: UNEP Ozone Secretariat

13. The concept of "sustainable development" was popularised by which report?
(a) Kyoto Protocol (b) Agenda 21 (c) Rio Declaration (d) Brundtland Report (Our Common Future)
Answer: (d)
Solution: Agenda 21 and the Rio Declaration came later, at the 1992 summit, so they are chronological traps. The Brundtland Commission's 1987 report "Our Common Future" defined and popularised sustainable development, so the answer is the Brundtland Report.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Environment and Natural Resources)

14. The United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) came into force in which year?
(a) 1994 (b) 1992 (c) 1997 (d) 2005
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1992 is when UNFCCC was adopted at Rio — adoption and entry into force are deliberately confused here. UNFCCC entered into force in March 1994, so the answer is 1994.
Source: UNFCCC official records

15. The Nagoya Protocol is associated with:
(a) Ozone protection (b) Access to genetic resources and benefit-sharing (c) Hazardous chemicals (d) Climate finance
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Nagoya Protocol (2010) is a supplementary agreement to the Convention on Biological Diversity, dealing with access to genetic resources and fair sharing of benefits (ABS), so the answer is access to genetic resources and benefit-sharing.
Source: Convention on Biological Diversity official records

16. The Rotterdam Convention deals with:
(a) Prior informed consent for trade in hazardous chemicals and pesticides (b) Trade in wildlife (c) Dumping of waste at sea (d) Nuclear safety
Answer: (a)
Solution: Wildlife trade is CITES, the standard confusion. The Rotterdam Convention (1998) establishes the Prior Informed Consent (PIC) procedure for hazardous chemicals and pesticides in international trade, so the answer is prior informed consent for trade in hazardous chemicals and pesticides.
Source: Rotterdam Convention official records

17. The World Environment Day is celebrated every year on:
(a) 22 April (b) 5 June (c) 16 September (d) 11 July
Answer: (b)
Solution: 22 April is Earth Day and 16 September is World Ozone Day — both are frequent traps. World Environment Day, established by the UN in 1972, is observed on 5 June, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: UNEP official records

18. The UNCCD (1994) is a convention to combat:
(a) Climate change (b) Desertification (c) Marine pollution (d) Deforestation
Answer: (b)
Solution: UNCCD is often confused with UNFCCC because both emerged from the Rio process. UNCCD stands for the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification, so the answer is desertification.
Source: UNCCD official records

19. The Minamata Convention is related to which pollutant?
(a) Lead (b) Cadmium (c) Arsenic (d) Mercury
Answer: (d)
Solution: The convention is named after Minamata city in Japan, where mercury poisoning caused the infamous Minamata disease. Adopted in 2013, it controls mercury emissions and use, so the answer is mercury.
Source: Minamata Convention official records

20. Agenda 21, a comprehensive action plan for sustainable development, was adopted at:
(a) Stockholm Conference, 1972 (b) Johannesburg Summit, 2002 (c) Kyoto Conference, 1997 (d) Rio Earth Summit, 1992
Answer: (d)
Solution: Stockholm 1972 produced only a declaration of principles, not Agenda 21. Agenda 21 was a key outcome document of the 1992 Rio Earth Summit, so the answer is Rio Earth Summit, 1992.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Environment and Natural Resources)

21. Consider the following statements:
1. The Kyoto Protocol was based on the principle of "common but differentiated responsibilities".
2. The first commitment period of the Kyoto Protocol ran from 2008 to 2012.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Some candidates reject statement 2 thinking the protocol started in 1997, but 1997 was only the adoption year. The Kyoto Protocol applied the CBDR principle and its first commitment period was 2008–2012, so the answer is Both 1 and 2.
Source: UNFCCC official records

22. Match the conventions with their subjects:
1. Basel Convention — Hazardous wastes
2. Rotterdam Convention — Hazardous chemicals (PIC)
3. Stockholm Convention — Persistent Organic Pollutants
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is swapping Basel and Stockholm because both are city-named treaties on pollutants. Basel covers hazardous waste movement, Rotterdam covers prior informed consent for chemicals, and Stockholm covers POPs — all three are correctly matched, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: Official records of the Basel, Rotterdam and Stockholm Conventions

23. Consider the following statements about the Paris Agreement:
1. It aims to limit global warming to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels.
2. It requires all countries to follow a single, uniform, legally fixed emission cut decided by the UN.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — the Paris Agreement works through Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) chosen by each country, not a uniform UN-imposed cut. Statement 1 correctly states its temperature goal, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: UNFCCC official records

24. Arrange the following events in chronological order:
1. Stockholm Conference on Human Environment
2. Brundtland Report
3. Rio Earth Summit
4. Paris Agreement
Select the correct order:
(a) 2-3-1-4 (b) 2-1-3-4 (c) 1-3-2-4 (d) 1-2-3-4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The common error is placing the Brundtland Report (1987) before Stockholm (1972). The correct sequence is Stockholm 1972, Brundtland 1987, Rio 1992, Paris 2015, so the answer is 1-2-3-4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Environment and Natural Resources)

25. Consider the following statements:
1. The Montreal Protocol is considered one of the most successful environmental treaties, with universal ratification.
2. The Vienna Convention (1985) provided the framework under which the Montreal Protocol was adopted.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Both 1 and 2 (b) 2 only (c) 1 only (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: A frequent trap is assuming the Montreal Protocol came directly out of the Rio Summit; in fact it was adopted in 1987 under the 1985 Vienna Convention framework. It is also the first treaty to achieve universal ratification, so the answer is Both 1 and 2.
Source: UNEP Ozone Secretariat
