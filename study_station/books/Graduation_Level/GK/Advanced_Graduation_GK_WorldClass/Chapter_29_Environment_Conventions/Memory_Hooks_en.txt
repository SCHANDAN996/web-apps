# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Environment Conventions

---

**BOX 1 — The Rio Trio (1992 Earth Summit)**
> 🎵 *"At the RIO carnival, three dancers arrived: Climate, Bio, and Dusty Desert."*
UNFCCC (climate), CBD (biodiversity), UNCCD (desertification) — all three born at Rio 1992. If a question says "Convention X was adopted at the Earth Summit," check if it's one of these three. Montreal (1987) and Ramsar (1971) were NOT at Rio — classic trap!

---

**BOX 2 — Montreal = O-Zone = "MO"**
> 🧊 *"MONtreal MEnds the Ozone MONth by MONth."*
Montreal Protocol (1987) → Ozone layer → phasing out CFCs. Both start with "MO". Kigali Amendment (2016) added HFCs — remember: **"Kigali Kills HFCs"** (K-K-H).

---

**BOX 3 — Kyoto's Two Commitment Periods**
> 🏯 *"Kyoto the Japanese city made TWO promises: 2008–12, then 2013–20. Then Paris took over the job."*
Kyoto Protocol (1997, in force 2005): 1st period 2008–2012, 2nd (Doha Amendment) 2013–2020. Paris Agreement (2015) replaced it from 2020 onward. Trap: Kyoto was *signed* 1997 but *came into force* 2005 — examiners love this 8-year gap.

---

**BOX 4 — Stockholm's Double Identity**
> 🇸🇪 *"Stockholm met TWICE: 1972 for humans, 2001 for poisons."*
1972 = Stockholm Conference on Human Environment (first big environment summit, UNEP born). 2001 = Stockholm Convention on **POPs** (Persistent Organic Pollutants). Same city, 29 years apart — the #1 confusion trap in this chapter.

---

**BOX 5 — Basel, Rotterdam, Stockholm = The Chemical Trio**
> ☠️ *"BRS — Big Rotten Substances: Basel Blocks waste movement, Rotterdam Requires prior consent, Stockholm Stops POPs."*
- **B**asel (1989): transboundary movement of hazardous **waste**
- **R**otterdam (1998): **PIC** — Prior Informed Consent for trade in hazardous chemicals
- **S**tockholm (2001): **POPs** elimination
Trick: **B-Waste, R-Consent, S-POPs.**

---

**BOX 6 — Ramsar = Wetlands = Iran**
> 🦆 *"A RAM (male duck!) splashing in an Iranian wetland, 1971."*
Ramsar Convention (1971, Iran) — the ONLY convention on wetlands, and the oldest modern environmental treaty. Trap: Ramsar is a *city in Iran*, not an acronym, and it's about wetlands — NOT deserts (that's UNCCD).

---

**BOX 7 — CITES vs CBD — The Trade Twin Trap**
> 🐘 *"CITES CITES the price tag — it's about TRADE. CBD Cares for BioDiversity — the whole ecosystem."*
CITES (1973, Washington) = international **trade** in endangered species. CBD (1992, Rio) = **conservation** of biodiversity overall. If the question mentions "trade/appendices/species listing" → CITES. If "ecosystems/genetic resources/benefit sharing" → CBD.

---

**BOX 8 — Paris Agreement Numbers**
> 🗼 *"Paris says: keep it WELL BELOW 2, dream of 1.5, and every 5 years show your homework."*
Paris (2015, in force 2016): hold warming well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C; NDCs (Nationally Determined Contributions) updated every 5 years. Trap: Paris has NO binding emission targets — NDCs are voluntary. Kyoto had binding targets for developed countries.

---

**BOX 9 — Vienna Before Montreal**
> 🎻 *"First Vienna composed the FRAMEWORK (1985), then Montreal played the actual TUNE (1987)."*
Vienna Convention (1985) = framework for protecting ozone; Montreal Protocol (1987) = the actual binding phase-out targets. Vienna has no specific targets — if a question asks "which treaty set binding CFC reduction targets," the answer is Montreal, never Vienna.

---

**BOX 10 — Nagoya & Cartagena = CBD's Two Children**
> 🧬 *"CBD had two kids: Cartagena the cautious one (biosafety, 2000) and Nagoya the fair one (benefit sharing, 2010)."*
- **C**artagena Protocol (2000): **C**aution with GMOs/LMOs — biosafety
- **N**agoya Protocol (2010): **N**ice and fair — Access & Benefit Sharing (ABS) of genetic resources
Trick: **C-Caution, N-Nice (fair sharing).**

---

**BOX 11 — Minamata = Mercury = Japan's Pain**
> 🐟 *"Minamata: the Japanese fishing town where MERCURY poisoned the fish and the people. The 2013 convention carries its name so we never forget."*
Minamata Convention (2013) → Mercury. The city suffered mercury poisoning (Minamata disease) in the 1950s. Memory chain: **Japan → fish → mercury → Minamata.**

---

**BOX 12 — UNEP Headquarters**
> 🌍 *"The UN's environment brain sits in NAIROBI — the only major UN programme HQ in the developing world (Africa)."*
UNEP HQ: Nairobi, Kenya (since 1972, after Stockholm Conference). Trap options: Geneva, New York, Paris — all wrong for UNEP.

---

**BOX 13 — Convention Years Speed-Chain**
> ⏳ *"71-72-73-85-87-89-92-97-13-15: Ramsar-Stockholm-CITES-Vienna-Montreal-Basel-Rio-Kyoto-Minamata-Paris."*
Chant it like a phone number. Covers 80% of "which year" questions in one line.

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

**Tip 1 — The "Exact Number of Parties/Amendments" Trap:**
If a question asks for the *precise current number* of member countries or ratifications of a convention (e.g., "How many parties does the Basel Convention have as of now?"), and you don't know it cold — **skip immediately**. These numbers change yearly, options are usually within 2–3 of each other, and guessing odds are poor. Never burn 45 seconds here.

**Tip 2 — The "Which COP / Which City" Deep Cut:**
If asked about a *specific COP number's host city* (e.g., "COP-27 was held at…") and you can't recall it within 15 seconds, skip. COP venues rotate and there are dozens of them — the cost of recall is too high unless it's a famous one (Paris COP-21, Glasgow COP-26, Sharm el-Sheikh COP-27, Dubai COP-28).

**Tip 3 — The "Statement Soup" Question:**
In 4-statement questions mixing conventions (e.g., "Statement 1: Montreal deals with HFCs; Statement 2: Ramsar covers mangroves…"), if TWO or more statements look uncertain, skip rather than partially guess. But if you can confidently eliminate even ONE statement as false, use option-elimination — one confirmed false statement usually kills two options and makes the question worth attempting.
