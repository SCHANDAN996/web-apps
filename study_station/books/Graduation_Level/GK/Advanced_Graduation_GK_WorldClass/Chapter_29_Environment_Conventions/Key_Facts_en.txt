# Key Facts & Figures — Environment Conventions

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stockholm Conference, 1972 | First UN Conference on Human Environment; led to UNEP; 5 June = World Environment Day | Confused with Rio 1992 (Earth Summit) | Similar-name confusion |
| UNEP Headquarters | Nairobi, Kenya (only UN agency HQ in Africa) | Trap options: Geneva, New York, Paris | Near-correct distractor |
| Ramsar Convention, 1971 | Wetlands of international importance; signed in Ramsar, Iran; in force 1975 | Trap: called a "biodiversity" or "forest" convention | Wrong-theme trap |
| World Wetlands Day | 2 February (adoption date of Ramsar Convention) | Trap: 5 June (Environment Day) | Date-swap trap |
| Montreux Record | Register of Ramsar sites under ecological threat | Trap: said to be a "blacklist of polluting countries" | Concept distortion |
| CITES, 1973 | Washington Convention; regulates international trade in endangered species; in force 1975 | Trap: said to ban all wildlife trade (it regulates, not bans) | Absolute-statement trap |
| CITES Appendices | Appendix I = trade banned; II = regulated; III = country-requested protection | Trap: Appendix III shown as "most endangered" | Reversed-order trap |
| Vienna Convention, 1985 | Framework convention for ozone layer protection; no binding targets | Trap: credited with fixing ODS phase-out targets (that was Montreal) | Credit-swap trap |
| Montreal Protocol, 1987 | Phase-out of ODS (CFCs, halons); in force 1989; universally ratified | Trap: called a "climate change" treaty (it is ozone, though it helps climate) | Theme-swap trap |
| Kigali Amendment, 2016 | Amendment to Montreal Protocol to phase down HFCs | Trap: linked to Kyoto Protocol instead of Montreal | Wrong-parent trap |
| Basel Convention, 1989 | Control of transboundary movement of hazardous wastes; in force 1992 | Trap: confused with Rotterdam (chemicals trade) or Stockholm (POPs) | Similar-name confusion |
| Rotterdam Convention, 1998 | Prior Informed Consent (PIC) for trade in hazardous chemicals & pesticides | Trap: PIC procedure attributed to Basel | Mechanism-swap trap |
| Stockholm Convention, 2001 | Eliminate/restrict Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs); in force 2004 | Trap: confused with 1972 Stockholm Conference | Same-city trap |
| "Dirty Dozen" | Original 12 POPs under Stockholm Convention (incl. DDT, PCBs, dioxins) | Trap: number quoted as 10 or 15 | Number distortion |
| Minamata Convention, 2013 | Mercury control; named after Minamata disease, Japan | Trap: said to cover lead or arsenic | Wrong-pollutant trap |
| UNFCCC, 1992 | Adopted at Rio Earth Summit; in force 1994; HQ secretariat Bonn, Germany | Trap: said to set binding emission cuts (that was Kyoto) | Credit-swap trap |
| Rio Earth Summit, 1992 | Produced UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD; Agenda 21; Rio Declaration | Trap: Stockholm 1972 credited with these outcomes | Chronology confusion |
| Kyoto Protocol, 1997 | Binding emission cuts for developed (Annex I) countries; first commitment period 2008–2012 | Trap: said to bind developing countries like India | Scope distortion |
| Kyoto flexibility mechanisms | Emissions Trading, Clean Development Mechanism (CDM), Joint Implementation (JI) | Trap: CDM shown as between two developed countries (it is developed→developing) | Mechanism-swap trap |
| Paris Agreement, 2015 | Adopted at COP21; in force 2016; NDCs by all countries; goal well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C | Trap: called "legally binding emission targets" (NDCs are nationally determined) | Absolute-statement trap |
| CBD, 1992 | Convention on Biological Diversity; objectives: conservation, sustainable use, benefit-sharing | Trap: confused with CITES (trade) | Similar-name confusion |
| Cartagena Protocol, 2000 | Biosafety — safe transfer of living modified organisms (LMOs); under CBD | Trap: parent convention given as UNFCCC | Wrong-parent trap |
| Nagoya Protocol, 2010 | Access & Benefit-Sharing (ABS) of genetic resources; under CBD; in force 2014 | Trap: confused with Cartagena (biosafety) | Same-family confusion |
| Kunming-Montreal GBF, 2022 | Global Biodiversity Framework; "30×30" target — protect 30% of land & oceans by 2030 | Trap: target quoted as 50% or year 2050 | Number distortion |
| UNCCD, 1994 | Only legally binding convention on desertification/land degradation; HQ Bonn | Trap: called a "forest" convention | Theme-swap trap |
| IPCC, 1988 | Set up by WMO & UNEP; assesses climate science; HQ Geneva | Trap: said to conduct its own research (it assesses published science) | Concept distortion |
| IPCC Nobel Peace Prize | 2007, shared with Al Gore | Trap: year given as 2001 or 2010 | Date-swap trap |
| Agenda 21 | Non-binding action plan from Rio 1992 for sustainable development | Trap: called a legally binding treaty | Legal-status trap |
| COP meaning | Conference of the Parties — supreme decision body of a convention | Trap: expanded as "Conference of Protocols" | Expansion trap |
| India & Montreal Protocol | India ratified in 1992 | Trap: India shown as non-party | Membership trap |
| Ramsar sites in India | India has the highest number of Ramsar sites in South Asia; count keeps rising (80 as of 2024) | Trap: exact number quoted from an outdated year | Outdated-data trap |
| World Environment Day host theme | Celebrated 5 June since 1973; theme changes yearly | Trap: date given as 22 April (Earth Day) | Date-swap trap |
| Earth Day | 22 April (since 1970) | Trap: swapped with 5 June | Date-swap trap |
| Bonn Convention (CMS), 1979 | Conservation of Migratory Species of Wild Animals | Trap: confused with Bonn-based UNFCCC secretariat | Same-city trap |
| COP13 of CMS (2020) | Held in Gandhinagar, India | Trap: venue given as New Delhi | Venue trap |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Fact / Question Type | Category | Time | Score Maximizer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Convention name ↔ year ↔ city (Stockholm 1972, Rio 1992, Kyoto 1997, Paris 2015) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Headquarters (UNEP–Nairobi, UNFCCC–Bonn, IPCC–Geneva) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Convention ↔ theme match (Ramsar–wetlands, Basel–hazardous waste, Minamata–mercury) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Protocol ↔ parent convention (Kigali–Montreal, Nagoya–CBD, Cartagena–CBD) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Environment days (5 June, 2 Feb, 22 April) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Statement-based questions on Kyoto mechanisms (ETS/CDM/JI) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Paris Agreement legal nature & NDC statements | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| CITES Appendix I/II/III classification statements | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Chronology ordering of conventions (arrange 1972→2015) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Multi-statement questions mixing Ramsar + Montreux Record + India sites | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
