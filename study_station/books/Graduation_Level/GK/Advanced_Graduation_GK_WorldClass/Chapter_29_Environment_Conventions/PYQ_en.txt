# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Environment Conventions & Protocols

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from This Chapter

Environment Conventions is a **high-certainty, low-syllabus** chapter: the facts are fixed (years, cities, targets), so examiners cannot invent new content — they can only twist the old content. Across UPSC (Prelims), SSC (CGL/CHSL), Banking (IBPS/SBI), Railway (NTPC/Group D) and State PCS, questions from this chapter fall into a small number of repeatable moulds:

**Mould 1 — "Match the Convention with the Year/City"**
The single most common pattern. Rio 1992, Kyoto 1997, Paris 2015, Montreal 1987, Stockholm 1972, Ramsar 1971, Basel 1989, Cartagena 2000, Nagoya 2010, Minamata 2013. The examiner swaps one pair to create the wrong options.

**Mould 2 — "Which convention deals with which pollutant/issue?"**
Ozone → Montreal; greenhouse gases → Kyoto/Paris; hazardous waste movement → Basel; mercury → Minamata; persistent organic pollutants → Stockholm (2001); wetlands → Ramsar; biodiversity → CBD; biosafety → Cartagena; access & benefit-sharing → Nagoya; desertification → UNCCD. The trap: **Stockholm appears twice** (1972 = first environment conference; 2001 = POPs convention) — examiners exploit this confusion heavily.

**Mould 3 — "Statement-based: which is/are correct?" (UPSC & Banking style)**
Two or three statements, one of them *almost correct* — e.g., "Paris Agreement requires all countries to cut emissions by the same percentage" (false — NDCs are nationally determined). These demand conceptual clarity, not just memory.

**Mould 4 — "Chronology / sequencing"**
Arrange Stockholm → Rio → Kyoto → Paris in order, or "which came first — Montreal or Basel?" Chronological confusion is the examiner's favourite low-effort trap.

**Mould 5 — "India-specific linkage"**
When did India ratify? Which Indian sites/cities are linked (Kigali amendment ratified by India in 2021; India's Panchamrit targets announced at Glasgow COP26, 2021)? Increasingly asked in SSC and State PCS.

**Mould 6 — "Negative phrasing"**
"Which of the following is **NOT** a greenhouse gas covered under the Kyoto Protocol?" or "Which convention does **NOT** deal with…" — the word NOT is the trap itself.

## 2. Sub-topics That Recur Most Often (in words, not numbers)

- **The Big Four years** — Stockholm 1972, Rio 1992, Kyoto 1997, Paris 2015 — appear in some form in nearly every exam cycle. Master these before anything else.
- **Montreal Protocol (1987)** and its **Kigali Amendment (2016, HFCs)** — asked repeatedly because it is called the "most successful environmental treaty."
- **Paris Agreement details** — 2°C / 1.5°C goal, NDCs, entry into force (4 November 2016), adopted at COP21.
- **Rio 1992 outputs** — the "Rio trio" (UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD) plus Agenda 21 — a classic multi-statement question base.
- **Waste & chemical conventions** — Basel (hazardous waste), Stockholm 2001 (POPs), Rotterdam (prior informed consent), Minamata (mercury) — examiners love mixing these four.
- **Ramsar & wetlands** — often merged with Indian Ramsar sites in State PCS papers.
- **Kyoto mechanisms** — Clean Development Mechanism (CDM), Joint Implementation, Emissions Trading; and the gases basket (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, SF₆; NF₃ added later).

| Exam | What it stresses |
|---|---|
| **UPSC Prelims** | Statement-based conceptual questions; Paris/NDC mechanics; convention-vs-issue matching with 3–4 statements |
| **SSC CGL/CHSL** | Direct year-city matching; "which protocol for ozone"; India's ratification facts |
| **Banking (IBPS/SBI)** | Recent COP host cities, India's targets, clubbed with current affairs |
| **Railway NTPC/Group D** | One-liner facts: full forms, headquarters (UNFCCC — Bonn; CBD — Montreal; UNCCD — Bonn) |
| **State PCS** | Ramsar sites of that state + convention basics |

## 4. Top 10 High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions (with Trap Analysis)

**Q1.** The Montreal Protocol (1987) is related to:
(a) Control of transboundary movement of hazardous wastes
(b) Phasing out of ozone-depleting substances
(c) Reduction of greenhouse gas emissions
(d) Conservation of wetlands
**Answer: (b)**
**Trap type:** *Similar-name confusion* — all four options are real conventions (Basel, Montreal, Kyoto/Paris, Ramsar). The examiner banks on you mixing up the "which does what" grid.
**Solution:** Montreal = Ozone layer (ODS like CFCs). Basel = hazardous waste, Ramsar = wetlands, Kyoto/Paris = climate.

**Q2.** Consider the following statements about the Paris Agreement:
1. It was adopted at COP21 in 2015.
2. It aims to limit warming to well below 2°C, pursuing efforts towards 1.5°C.
3. It imposes legally binding, uniform emission-cut targets on all countries.
Which are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a)**
**Trap type:** *Almost Correct* — statement 3 sounds plausible but is false: targets are **Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs)**, chosen by each country, not uniform or legally binding in amount.
**Solution:** Paris = bottom-up pledges + binding *reporting*, not binding *targets*.

**Q3.** Which of the following pairs is/are correctly matched?
1. Stockholm Convention — Persistent Organic Pollutants
2. Basel Convention — Hazardous wastes
3. Minamata Convention — Mercury
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d)**
**Trap type:** *Reverse of the usual trap* — here all three are correct; candidates conditioned to expect one wrong statement often reject (d) without checking.
**Solution:** All three pairs are standard. Remember: Stockholm-2001 (POPs) ≠ Stockholm-1972 (first environment conference).

**Q4.** The Kigali Amendment relates to the phase-down of:
(a) CFCs (b) HFCs (c) Methane (d) Sulphur hexafluoride
**Answer: (b)**
**Trap type:** *Chronological confusion* — CFCs were the *original* Montreal target; Kigali (2016) added **HFCs**, which are greenhouse gases, not ozone-depleters.
**Solution:** Kigali Amendment (2016) to the Montreal Protocol → HFC phase-down. India ratified it in 2021.

**Q5.** The "Rio trio" of conventions born at the 1992 Earth Summit does **NOT** include:
(a) UNFCCC (b) CBD (c) UNCCD (d) Ramsar Convention
**Answer: (d)**
**Trap type:** *Negative phrasing* — the word NOT. Ramsar (1971) predates Rio by two decades.
**Solution:** Rio 1992 gave UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD (+ Agenda 21). Ramsar = 1971, Iran.

**Q6.** Arrange in chronological order:
1. Kyoto Protocol 2. Stockholm Conference 3. Paris Agreement 4. Rio Earth Summit
(a) 2-4-1-3 (b) 4-2-1-3 (c) 2-1-4-3 (d) 4-1-2-3
**Answer: (a)**
**Trap type:** *Chronological confusion* — the classic sequence 1972 → 1992 → 1997 → 2015.
**Solution:** Mnemonic: **"72-92-97-15"** — Stockholm, Rio, Kyoto, Paris.

**Q7.** Which of the following is **NOT** a greenhouse gas covered under the Kyoto Protocol basket?
(a) Methane (b) Nitrous oxide (c) Sulphur dioxide (d) Sulphur hexafluoride
**Answer: (c)**
**Trap type:** *Negative phrasing + near-miss names* — SO₂ vs SF₆ look alike in a hurry. SO₂ causes acid rain; it is not a Kyoto GHG.
**Solution:** Kyoto basket: CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, SF₆ (NF₃ added in Doha amendment).

**Q8.** The Cartagena Protocol deals with:
(a) Access and benefit-sharing of genetic resources
(b) Biosafety and transboundary movement of living modified organisms
(c) Prior informed consent for hazardous chemicals trade
(d) Mercury emissions
**Answer: (b)**
**Trap type:** *Sibling-protocol swap* — Cartagena (2000, biosafety) and Nagoya (2010, access & benefit-sharing) are both under the CBD; examiners swap them constantly. Option (c) is Rotterdam.
**Solution:** Cartagena = Biosafety/LMOs; Nagoya = ABS of genetic resources.

**Q9.** The headquarters of the UNFCCC Secretariat is located at:
(a) Geneva (b) Bonn (c) Montreal (d) Paris
**Answer: (b)**
**Trap type:** *Almost Correct* — Montreal is the CBD Secretariat's home; Geneva hosts many UN bodies. The names feel interchangeable.
**Solution:** UNFCCC & UNCCD → **Bonn (Germany)**; CBD → **Montreal (Canada)**; UNEP → **Nairobi (Kenya)**.

**Q10.** The first-ever United Nations Conference on the Human Environment was held in 1972 at:
(a) Rio de Janeiro (b) Geneva (c) Stockholm (d) Nairobi
**Answer: (c)**
**Trap type:** *Similar-name confusion* — Rio is the more famous "Earth Summit" (1992), so hurried candidates pick (a).
**Solution:** Stockholm 1972 = first global environment conference; it also created UNEP. 5 June (its opening date) is World Environment Day.

## 5. The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**Rule:** If a question is pure recall (year/city/matching), decide within **15 seconds** — you either know it or you don't; flag and move. Reserve **45 seconds** for statement-based or chronology questions where elimination genuinely works.

**Applied to Q6 (chronology):** This is a 15-second question *if* the sequence 72-92-97-15 is memorised. If not memorised, do not burn 90 seconds reconstructing it — eliminate options starting with 4 (Rio first is impossible, since Stockholm 1972 is the earliest), which removes (b) and (d), guess between (a) and (c), flag, move on. **Time cap: 20 seconds.**

**Applied to Q2 (statement-based):** This deserves the full **45 seconds**. Read each statement separately. Statement 3 contains the word "uniform" — a red-flag absolute. Paris is built on nationally determined (i.e., *non-uniform*) pledges, so 3 is false → only option (a) survives. The 45 seconds are spent hunting for the absolute word, not on recalling facts.

**Applied to Q7 (negative phrasing):** 15 seconds. Circle the word **NOT** first — most errors here are reading errors, not knowledge errors. SO₂ vs SF₆: the acid-rain gas is the odd one out. Answer and move.

## 6. Examiner's Trap Summary (memorise this table)

| Trap type | How it looks in this chapter | Defence |
|---|---|---|
| **Almost Correct** | "Uniform binding targets under Paris" | Watch absolute words: *all, uniform, mandatory, only* |
| **Chronological Confusion** | Swapping Montreal/Basel, Kyoto/Paris years | Fix the spine: 72-85(Vienna)-87-89-92-97-2015 |
| **Negative Phrasing** | "NOT a Kyoto gas", "does NOT include" | Circle NOT before reading options |
| **Similar Names** | Stockholm 1972 vs Stockholm 2001; Cartagena vs Nagoya; SO₂ vs SF₆ | Learn pairs as pairs, never singly |
| **Headquarters Swap** | Bonn vs Montreal vs Geneva vs Nairobi | UNFCCC/UNCCD-Bonn, CBD-Montreal, UNEP-Nairobi |

**Source:** Pattern analysis of publicly available UPSC/SSC/Banking/Railway question trends; individual questions above are PYQ-style reconstructions based on NCERT (Class 11–12 Geography/Biology), UNEP and convention secretariat (unfccc.int, ozone.unep.org, cbd.int) facts.
