# Key Facts & Figures — Advanced Indian Polity

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Article 368 | Amendment procedure of the Constitution | Confused with Article 356 (President's Rule) | Similar numbers |
| Article 74 | Council of Ministers to aid and advise President | 42nd Amendment made advice binding; 44th allowed one-time reconsideration | Amendment confusion |
| Article 110 | Definition of Money Bill | Speaker's certification is final; confused with Financial Bill (Art. 117) | Near-identical concepts |
| Article 123 vs 213 | Ordinance by President vs Governor | Maximum life: 6 months + 6 weeks (both) | Centre–State mix-up |
| Article 352 | National Emergency — grounds: war, external aggression, armed rebellion | "Internal disturbance" replaced by "armed rebellion" (44th Amendment, 1978) | Outdated wording |
| Article 356 | President's Rule; max 3 years with conditions | S.R. Bommai case (1994) — judicial review applies | Case-law trap |
| Article 360 | Financial Emergency — never proclaimed so far | Falsely stated as "used during 1991 crisis" | Fabricated fact |
| 52nd Amendment (1985) | Anti-Defection Law — Tenth Schedule | 91st Amendment (2003) deleted split provision (1/3rd); merger needs 2/3rd | Amendment mix-up |
| 73rd & 74th Amendments (1992) | Panchayati Raj (Part IX, Art. 243–243O) & Municipalities (Part IXA, Art. 243P–243ZG) | 11th Schedule = 29 subjects; 12th Schedule = 18 subjects | Number swap |
| 86th Amendment (2002) | Article 21A — Right to Education (6–14 yrs) | Confused with Article 45 (now early childhood care) | Article swap |
| 101st Amendment (2016) | GST — Article 246A, GST Council (Art. 279A) | Wrongly cited as 100th or 122nd Amendment | Number confusion |
| 42nd Amendment (1976) | "Mini-Constitution"; added Socialist, Secular, Integrity to Preamble | Words added only once, via 42nd — not by original framers | Chronology trap |
| Fundamental Rights | Articles 12–35; 6 rights after 44th Amendment removed property right (Art. 300A) | Property right is now a legal right, not FR | Status change |
| Article 32 | Writ jurisdiction of SC — "heart and soul" (Ambedkar) | Habeas, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto — SC: Art. 32, HC: Art. 226 (wider) | Scope reversal |
| DPSP | Part IV, Articles 36–51; borrowed from Ireland | Non-justiciable; confused with Fundamental Duties (Part IVA) | Part confusion |
| Fundamental Duties | Article 51A; originally 10 (42nd Amendment), 11th added by 86th Amendment (2002) | Based on Swaran Singh Committee recommendations | Committee trap |
| President's election | Article 54 — electoral college: elected MPs + elected MLAs of states & UTs (Delhi, Puducherry) | Nominated members do NOT vote; J&K assembly no longer part (post-2019) | Membership trap |
| Vice-President | Article 63; ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha (Art. 64) | Elected by both Houses' members (including nominated) — unlike President | Contrast trap |
| PM & Council of Ministers | Total strength ≤ 15% of Lok Sabha (91st Amendment) | PM need not be from Lok Sabha; must become MP within 6 months | Assumption trap |
| Lok Sabha max strength | 550 (530 states + 20 UTs); Anglo-Indian nomination abolished by 104th Amendment (2019) | Old figure 552 still quoted in outdated books | Outdated data |
| Rajya Sabha max strength | 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated) | Present strength 245; members retire 1/3rd every 2 years | Static vs current |
| Speaker | Vacates office only when Lok Sabha dissolves AND new Speaker elected; removal needs 14 days' notice + effective majority | "Effective majority" vs "simple majority" confusion | Majority-type trap |
| Joint Sitting (Art. 108) | Summoned by President; presided by Speaker | Not applicable to Money Bills & Constitutional Amendment Bills | Exception trap |
| Supreme Court | Art. 124; judges: CJI + 33 (sanctioned strength 34) | Retirement age 65 (SC) vs 62 (HC) | Age swap |
| High Court | Art. 214; common HC possible (Art. 231) | HC judges appointed by President, NOT Governor | Appointing authority |
| CAG | Article 148; single-member body; audits Centre & States | Not eligible for further office under GoI/State after retirement | Post-retirement rule |
| Attorney General | Article 76; right of audience in all courts; can speak in either House (no vote) | Not a member of Cabinet; not a full-time government servant | Status trap |
| Election Commission | Article 324; CEC + 2 ECs; tenure 6 years or 65 years | CEC removal = like SC judge; other ECs on CEC's recommendation | Removal asymmetry |
| UPSC | Articles 315–323; Chairman/members: 6 years or 65 years | State PSC members: 6 years or 62 years | Age swap |
| Finance Commission | Article 280; constituted every 5th year by President | 15th FC (2021–26) chaired by N.K. Singh | Tenure/chairman trap |
| NITI Aayog | Formed 1 January 2015 by executive resolution; replaced Planning Commission | NOT a constitutional or statutory body | Body-type trap |
| NHRC | Statutory body — Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 | Wrongly called constitutional body | Body-type trap |
| CVC / CBI | CVC: statutory (2003); CBI: set up under DSPE Act, 1946 | CBI is NOT statutory in origin of powers debate — exam expects DSPE Act | Origin trap |
| Lokpal | Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013; first Lokpal: Justice Pinaki Chandra Ghose (2019) | Confused with Lokayukta (state-level) | Level confusion |
| Scheduled & Tribal Areas | Fifth Schedule (general), Sixth Schedule (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram) | Sixth Schedule states list — 4 states only | List trap |
| Official Language | Article 343 — Hindi in Devanagari; English continued (Official Languages Act, 1963) | No "national language" in Constitution | Common misconception |
| Schedules | Originally 8; now 12 (1st–12th) | 9th Schedule added by 1st Amendment (1951) | Chronology trap |
| Preamble | Borrowed "idea" from USA; amended once (42nd) | Preamble part of Constitution — Kesavananda Bharati (1973) | Case-law trap |
| Citizenship | Part II, Articles 5–11; single citizenship | CAA 2019 — cut-off date 31 December 2014 | Date trap |
| Article 370 | Abrogated August 2019; J&K & Ladakh UTs from 31 October 2019 | J&K has legislature; Ladakh does not | UT-type trap |
