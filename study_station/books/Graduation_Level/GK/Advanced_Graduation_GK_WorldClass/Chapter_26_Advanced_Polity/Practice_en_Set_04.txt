76. Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the appointment of the Prime Minister?
(a) Article 74 (b) Article 75 (c) Article 76 (d) Article 78
Answer: (b)
Solution: Article 74 deals with the Council of Ministers to aid and advise the President, which is the tempting wrong choice. Article 76 concerns the Attorney General, so the answer is Article 75.
