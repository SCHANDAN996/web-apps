Card 1
Front: Which is India's oldest paramilitary force, raised in 1835?
Back: Assam Rifles — raised as "Cachar Levy" in 1835; it functions under the Ministry of Home Affairs but is officered by the Army.

Card 2
Front: Which force guards the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders?
Back: Border Security Force (BSF), raised in 1965 after the Indo-Pak war.

Card 3
Front: Which force guards the India–China border, and when was it raised?
Back: Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), raised in 1962 after the Sino-Indian war.

Card 4
Front: Which force guards the India–Nepal and India–Bhutan borders?
Back: Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

Card 5
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember the four border-guarding forces and their borders?
Back: "B-I-S-A" → BSF (Pakistan/Bangladesh), ITBP (China/Tibet), SSB (Nepal/Bhutan), Assam Rifles (Myanmar).

Card 6
Front: Examiner's trap: "Assam Rifles comes under the Ministry of Defence." True or false?
Back: False. Administrative control is with the Ministry of Home Affairs; operational control in the Northeast is with the Army. Examiners swap the ministries to trap candidates.

Card 7
Front: Where is the headquarters of the Integrated Defence Staff (IDS)?
Back: New Delhi. It was created after the Kargil Review Committee recommendations to integrate the three services.

Card 8
Front: Who was India's first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS), and who succeeded him?
Back: General Bipin Rawat was the first CDS, appointed on 1 January 2020 (post created December 2019); he died in a helicopter crash in December 2021. General Anil Chauhan was appointed as the second CDS in September 2022.

Card 9
Front: Which is India's first indigenous aircraft carrier, commissioned in 2022?
Back: INS Vikrant (IAC-1), commissioned on 2 September 2022 at Kochi. Built by Cochin Shipyard Limited.

Card 10
Front: What is the difference between INS Vikrant (2022) and the earlier INS Vikrant?
Back: The first INS Vikrant (R11, ex-HMS Hercules) was a British-built carrier that served in the 1971 war and was decommissioned in 1997. The 2022 Vikrant is India's first indigenously built carrier.

Card 11
Front: Which missile series is India's nuclear-capable ballistic missile family, and what does "Agni-V" add?
Back: The Agni series (Agni-I to Agni-V). Agni-V, with a range of over 5,000 km, gives India long-range (ICBM-class) strike capability.

Card 12
Front: Examiner's trap: "Prithvi is a cruise missile and BrahMos is a ballistic missile." What is wrong with this statement?
Back: It is reversed. Prithvi is a surface-to-surface ballistic missile; BrahMos is a supersonic cruise missile (Indo-Russian joint venture). Examiners swap the categories.

Card 13
Front: What is the mnemonic to remember India's missile types under IGMDP?
Back: "A-P-A-N-T" → Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Nag, Trishul — the five missiles of the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (launched in 1983).

Card 14
Front: Which organisation developed the Agni and Prithvi missiles, and where is it headquartered?
Back: DRDO (Defence Research and Development Organisation), headquartered in New Delhi; established in 1958.

Card 15
Front: What is India's nuclear triad, and which submarine completed the sea leg?
Back: The ability to launch nuclear weapons from land (Agni missiles), air (fighter aircraft), and sea. INS Arihant, India's first indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN), completed the sea leg.

Card 16
Front: Which is the highest peacetime gallantry award and the highest wartime gallantry award of India?
Back: Param Vir Chakra is the highest wartime gallantry award; Ashoka Chakra is the highest peacetime gallantry award. Examiners often swap these two.

Card 17
Front: Where are the training academies of the three services located?
Back: Army — Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun; Navy — Indian Naval Academy (INA), Ezhimala (Kerala); Air Force — Air Force Academy, Dundigal (Hyderabad). Tri-services: National Defence Academy (NDA), Khadakwasla (Pune).

Card 18
Front: What does "theatre command" mean in Indian defence reforms?
Back: An integrated command where Army, Navy, and Air Force assets in a geographical theatre operate under a single commander, replacing the current single-service commands. It is part of the CDS-led jointness reforms.

Card 19
Front: Which exercise is a bilateral naval exercise between India and the USA, and which one is the multilateral one with the Quad?
Back: Malabar began as an India–USA bilateral naval exercise (1992) and now includes Japan and Australia (Quad members). Do not confuse it with Varuna (India–France) or SIMBEX (India–Singapore).

Card 20
Front: What is the trick to remember the order of precedence of wartime gallantry awards?
Back: "PVC-MVC-VC" → Param Vir Chakra > Maha Vir Chakra > Vir Chakra. Trick: "Param (supreme), Maha (great), Vir (brave)" — descending order of valour.
