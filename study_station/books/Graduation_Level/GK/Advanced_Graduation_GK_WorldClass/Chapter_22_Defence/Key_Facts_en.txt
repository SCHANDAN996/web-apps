# Indian Defence & Security — Key Facts & Figures

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supreme Commander of Armed Forces | President of India (Article 53) | PM or Defence Minister marked as Supreme Commander | Constitutional confusion |
| Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) | Post created in 2019; first CDS — Gen. Bipin Rawat | Confusing CDS with Service Chiefs; CDS heads Dept. of Military Affairs | Similar-name trap |
| Integrated Defence Staff (HQ IDS) | Headquartered in New Delhi; supports CDS | Confusing with Ministry of Defence | Institutional mix-up |
| Army Commands | 7 commands (e.g., Western — Chandimandir, Eastern — Kolkata, Northern — Udhampur, Southern — Pune, Central — Lucknow, South-Western — Jaipur, ARTRAC — Shimla) | ARTRAC is a training command, not operational; HQ swaps | Near-correct statement |
| Navy Commands | 3 commands: Western (Mumbai), Eastern (Visakhapatnam), Southern (Kochi — training command) | Southern Command called "operational" | Functional trap |
| Air Force Commands | 7 commands; Western — New Delhi, Eastern — Shillong, Central — Prayagraj, South-Western — Gandhinagar, Southern — Thiruvananthapuram, Training — Bengaluru, Maintenance — Nagpur | HQ city swaps (e.g., Central at Lucknow — that's Army) | Cross-service confusion |
| DRDO | Established 1958; HQ New Delhi; works under Ministry of Defence | Year given as 1947/1962; said to be under PMO directly | Date + ministry trap |
| Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) | Launched 1983 under Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam; missiles: Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Nag, Trishul | Trishul replaced with BrahMos in list; year given as 1973 | List-substitution trap |
| Agni series | Agni-V — ICBM-class, range over 5,000 km; developed by DRDO | Agni-V called "cruise missile" (it is ballistic) | Missile-type trap |
| Prithvi series | India's first indigenously developed ballistic missile family (surface-to-surface) | Prithvi called air-to-air missile | Missile-type trap |
| Akash | Surface-to-air missile (SAM) | Confused with Nag (anti-tank) | Pair swap |
| Nag | Third-generation anti-tank guided missile | Confused with Akash | Pair swap |
| BrahMos | Indo-Russian joint venture supersonic cruise missile; named after Brahmaputra + Moskva rivers | Called "joint venture with France/USA"; called ballistic missile | Country + type trap |
| INS Vikrant (IAC-1) | India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier; commissioned September 2022; built at Cochin Shipyard | Confused with INS Vikramaditya (Russian-origin, ex-Admiral Gorshkov) | Similar-name trap |
| INS Vikramaditya | Acquired from Russia; commissioned 2013 | Called "indigenously built" | Origin trap |
| INS Arihant | India's first indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN) | Called "diesel-electric" or "attack submarine (SSK)" | Submarine-class trap |
| Project 75 | Conventional submarines — Kalvari class (Scorpène, built with France at Mazagon Dock) | Mazagon Dock swapped with Garden Reach (GRSE, Kolkata) | Shipyard swap |
| HAL | Hindustan Aeronautics Limited; HQ Bengaluru; makes Tejas, Dhruv | HQ given as Hyderabad/New Delhi | HQ trap |
| Tejas | Indigenous Light Combat Aircraft (LCA), single-engine, developed by ADA, built by HAL | Called "twin-engine" (that's Su-30 MKI class) | Specification trap |
| Rafale | French-origin (Dassault) twin-engine fighter; inducted into IAF (first squadron at Ambala — 17 Sqn 'Golden Arrows') | Country of origin given as USA/Russia | Country trap |
| S-400 Triumf | Long-range air defence system procured from Russia | Called "American/Israeli system" | Country trap |
| Indian Coast Guard | Established 1978 (Coast Guard Act); under Ministry of Defence | Said to be under Ministry of Home Affairs / established 1962 | Ministry + date trap |
| BSF | Raised 1965 (after Indo-Pak war); guards India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders | Year given as 1962; assigned India–China border | Date + border trap |
| ITBP | Raised 1962; guards India–China (LAC) border | Swapped with BSF's role | Force-role swap |
| SSB | Guards India–Nepal and India–Bhutan borders | Assigned India–Myanmar border (that's Assam Rifles) | Border swap |
| Assam Rifles | Oldest paramilitary force (origin 1835, Cachar Levy); guards India–Myanmar border; under MHA, officered by Army | Called "youngest force"; placed fully under Army | Age + control trap |
| CISF | Raised 1969; protects airports, metros, vital installations | Confused with NSG (counter-terror) | Role trap |
| NSG | Raised 1984; counter-terrorism force ("Black Cats"); under MHA | Year given as 1986; placed under Army | Date + control trap |
| Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) | BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB — under Ministry of Home Affairs | Assam Rifles excluded/included wrongly; CAPF said to be under MoD | Classification trap |
| CRPF | Largest CAPF; raised 1939 (as Crown Representative's Police) | Called "raised 1947" | Date trap |
| Nuclear doctrine | No First Use (NFU); credible minimum deterrence; civilian control | "First Use" attributed to India; NFU attributed to Pakistan | Doctrine reversal |
| Pokhran-I | 1974 — "Smiling Buddha", first nuclear test | Year given as 1998 | Date trap |
| Pokhran-II (Operation Shakti) | May 1998 — series of nuclear tests | Confused with 1974 test | Date trap |
| CTBT / NPT | India has NOT signed either; calls them discriminatory | Statement says "India signed NPT in 1968" | Negative-statement trap |
| Missile Technology Control Regime (MTCR) | India joined in 2016 | Confused with NSG (India is not an NSG member) | Membership trap |
| Wassenaar Arrangement | India joined in 2017 | Year/membership swapped with Australia Group (2018) | Membership trap |
| Nuclear Suppliers Group (NSG) | India is NOT a member | Statement claims India is a member | Negative-statement trap |
| Theatre commands | Reform proposal for integrated tri-service commands; CDS driving integration | Presented as "already fully implemented" | Status trap |
| Agnipath scheme (2022) | Short-service recruitment of "Agniveers" (4 years); ~25% retained | Retention figure altered; called "permanent commission scheme" | Figure trap |
| Defence Corridors | Uttar Pradesh and Tamil Nadu defence industrial corridors | States swapped (e.g., Gujarat/Maharashtra) | State swap |
| iDEX | Innovations for Defence Excellence — defence innovation/start-up framework | Expanded form garbled | Acronym trap |
| Chief of Army Staff | Four-star General; senior-most service chief by convention | Called "five-star" (Field Marshal is five-star, honorary) | Rank trap |
| Field Marshal | Five-star rank; held by Sam Manekshaw and K.M. Cariappa | Attributed to other officers | Name trap |
| Param Vir Chakra | Highest wartime gallantry award | Confused with Ashoka Chakra (peacetime) | Award swap |
| Ashoka Chakra | Highest peacetime gallantry award | Confused with PVC | Award swap |
| Siachen Glacier | World's highest battlefield; Indian Army deployed since Operation Meghdoot (1984) | Operation year given as 1971/1999 | Date trap |
| Operation Vijay (1999) | Kargil conflict — eviction of intruders | Confused with 1971 war operations | Operation-name trap |
| Integrated Theatre Commands vs current | Currently 17 single-service commands; integration ongoing | Number quoted wrongly | Number trap |

**Strategy:** Attempt all 15-second fact questions first — this chapter is heavily fact-loaded and is a high-scoring area. Reserve 45-second slots only for statement/matching questions; skip if two statements look equally plausible.
