101. Which of the following is the first indigenously built aircraft carrier of the Indian Navy?
(a) INS Viraat (b) INS Vikrant (c) INS Vikramaditya (d) INS Vishal
Answer: (b)
Solution: INS Vikramaditya is a tempting wrong choice, but it is a refurbished Russian carrier (formerly Admiral Gorshkov), not indigenously built. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), built at Cochin Shipyard, was commissioned in September 2022 as India's first indigenous aircraft carrier, so the answer is INS Vikrant.
Source: PYQ-style

102. The Integrated Defence Staff (IDS) headquarters is located at:
(a) South Block, New Delhi (b) Kashmir House, New Delhi (c) Sena Bhawan, New Delhi (d) Vayu Bhawan, New Delhi
Answer: (b)
Solution: South Block houses the Ministry of Defence, which is the common trap. The Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff functions from Kashmir House, New Delhi, so the answer is Kashmir House.
Source: PYQ-style

103. The Agni-V missile of India has an approximate range of:
(a) 700 km (b) 2,500 km (c) Over 5,000 km (d) 3,500 km
Answer: (c)
Solution: 3,500 km is the trap because it is the range of Agni-IV. Agni-V is an intercontinental-range ballistic missile with a range of over 5,000 km, so the answer is over 5,000 km.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

104. Which of the following is a beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile developed by DRDO?
(a) Astra (b) Nag (c) Akash (d) Prithvi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Akash is the trap since it is also a DRDO missile, but it is a surface-to-air missile, not air-to-air. Astra is India's indigenous beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile, so the answer is Astra.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

105. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India also heads which department?
(a) Department of Defence (b) Department of Military Affairs (c) Department of Defence Production (d) Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare
Answer: (b)
Solution: Department of Defence is the trap; it is headed by the Defence Secretary, not the CDS. The CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs created in 2019, so the answer is Department of Military Affairs.
Source: Ministry of Defence (mod.gov.in)

106. The first Chief of Defence Staff of India was:
(a) General Manoj Mukund Naravane (b) General Bipin Rawat (c) Admiral Karambir Singh (d) Air Chief Marshal R.K.S. Bhadauria
Answer: (b)
Solution: General Naravane is the trap because he became Army Chief around the same time the CDS post was created. General Bipin Rawat took charge as the first CDS on 1 January 2020, so the answer is General Bipin Rawat.
Source: Ministry of Defence (mod.gov.in)

107. Which command of the Indian Armed Forces is a tri-services command?
(a) Andaman and Nicobar Command (b) Southern Command (c) Western Command (d) Northern Command
Answer: (a)
Solution: The other three are single-service Army commands and are the usual distractors. The Andaman and Nicobar Command, established in 2001 at Port Blair, is India's only tri-services theatre command, so the answer is Andaman and Nicobar Command.
Source: Ministry of Defence (mod.gov.in)

108. The 'Surya Kiran' exercise is conducted between India and:
(a) Sri Lanka (b) Bangladesh (c) Nepal (d) Myanmar
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bangladesh is the trap because India conducts 'Sampriti' with it, and the names are often confused. Surya Kiran is the joint military exercise between India and Nepal, so the answer is Nepal.
Source: PYQ-style

109. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was formed in:
(a) 1972 (b) 1947 (c) 1962 (d) 1958
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1947 is the trap because the Technical Development Establishment dates to that era. DRDO was formed in 1958 by merging the Technical Development Establishment with the Directorate of Technical Development and Production, so the answer is 1958.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

110. INS Arihant is:
(a) A nuclear-powered aircraft carrier (b) A nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (c) A diesel-electric attack submarine (d) A guided missile destroyer
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is calling it an aircraft carrier because of its fame. INS Arihant is India's first indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN), commissioned in 2016, so the answer is a nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine.
Source: PYQ-style

111. The 'Prithvi' missile series of India is:
(a) Surface-to-surface (b) Air-to-air (c) Surface-to-air (d) Anti-tank
Answer: (a)
Solution: Surface-to-air is the trap because Prithvi is often confused with Akash. Prithvi is a short-range surface-to-surface ballistic missile developed under the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, so the answer is surface-to-surface.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

112. Who is the Supreme Commander of the Indian Armed Forces?
(a) The President of India (b) The Chief of Defence Staff (c) The Prime Minister (d) The Defence Minister
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Prime Minister is the tempting trap since real executive power rests there. Constitutionally, the President of India is the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces, so the answer is the President of India.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Indian Constitution at Work)

113. The 'Varuna' naval exercise is held between India and:
(a) France (b) Australia (c) Japan (d) USA
Answer: (a)
Solution: USA is the trap because of the well-known Malabar exercise. Varuna is the bilateral naval exercise between India and France, so the answer is France.
Source: PYQ-style

114. The 'Nag' missile is designed primarily to destroy:
(a) Tanks (b) Aircraft (c) Ships (d) Submarines
Answer: (a)
Solution: Aircraft is the trap since Nag is often listed alongside Akash. Nag is a third-generation fire-and-forget anti-tank guided missile, so the answer is tanks.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

115. The National Security Advisor (NSA) of India heads which of the following?
(a) Ministry of Home Affairs (b) Cabinet Secretariat (c) Prime Minister's Office (d) National Security Council Secretariat
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Prime Minister's Office is the trap because the NSA works closely with the PM. The NSA heads the National Security Council Secretariat and advises the National Security Council, so the answer is National Security Council Secretariat.
Source: PYQ-style

116. The 'Tejas' aircraft is a:
(a) Heavy transport aircraft (b) Helicopter (c) Light combat aircraft (d) Unmanned aerial vehicle
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is treating Tejas as a heavy fighter like the Su-30. Tejas, developed by HAL and ADA, is an indigenous single-engine light combat aircraft, so the answer is light combat aircraft.
Source: HAL (hal-india.co.in)

117. The 'Malabar' naval exercise originally began as a bilateral exercise between India and:
(a) Japan (b) USA (c) France (d) Australia
Answer: (b)
Solution: Japan is the trap because it is now a regular Malabar participant. Malabar began in 1992 as a bilateral India–USA exercise; Japan and Australia joined later, so the answer is USA.
Source: PYQ-style

118. Which of the following is India's first indigenously developed anti-radiation missile?
(a) Rudram-1 (b) Helina (c) QRSAM (d) Pinaka
Answer: (a)
Solution: Helina is the trap as it is also a DRDO missile, but it is a helicopter-launched anti-tank missile. Rudram-1 is India's first indigenous anti-radiation missile, designed to destroy enemy radar, so the answer is Rudram-1.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

119. The 'Pinaka' system developed by DRDO is a:
(a) Ballistic missile (b) Radar system (c) Torpedo system (d) Multi-barrel rocket launcher
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ballistic missile is the trap because Pinaka is often listed with Prithvi and Agni. Pinaka is a multi-barrel rocket launcher system used by the Indian Army, so the answer is multi-barrel rocket launcher.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

120. The 'Garuda' special forces belong to the:
(a) Indian Army (b) Indian Navy (c) Indian Air Force (d) Coast Guard
Answer: (c)
Solution: Indian Army is the trap because its Para (SF) is the most famous special force. Garuda is the special forces unit of the Indian Air Force, while MARCOS belongs to the Navy, so the answer is Indian Air Force.
Source: PYQ-style

121. Consider the following statements about the Agni missile series:
1. Agni-I has a longer range than Agni-II.
2. Agni missiles are surface-to-surface ballistic missiles.
3. Agni-P is a new-generation canisterised missile.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 1 is the trap: Agni-I (about 700–1,200 km) has a shorter range than Agni-II (about 2,000 km), so it is false. Statements 2 and 3 are correct, so the answer is 2 and 3 only.
Source: DRDO (drdo.gov.in)

122. Arrange the following in chronological order of their establishment:
1. National Cadet Corps (NCC)
2. Border Security Force (BSF)
3. Central Reserve Police Force (as Crown Representative's Police)
4. Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)
(a) 3, 1, 2, 4 (b) 1, 3, 2, 4 (c) 1, 3, 4, 2 (d) 3, 1, 4, 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing BSF before ITBP; ITBP was raised in 1962 after the Sino-Indian war, while BSF came in 1965. CRPF traces its origin to the Crown Representative's Police of 1939 and NCC was raised in 1948, so the correct order is 3, 1, 4, 2.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) MARCOS — Indian Navy (b) Para (SF) — Indian Army (c) NSG — Counter-terrorism operations (d) Garuda — Indian Coast Guard
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is that all four sound plausible because Garuda is a real special force. Garuda belongs to the Indian Air Force, not the Coast Guard, so the incorrectly matched pair is Garuda — Indian Coast Guard.
Source: PYQ-style

124. Consider the following statements about India's nuclear doctrine:
1. India follows a policy of 'No First Use' of nuclear weapons.
2. India's nuclear doctrine was formalised after the 1998 Pokhran-II tests.
3. The doctrine envisages massive retaliation to a nuclear attack on Indian territory.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is doubting statement 3, but India's doctrine does envisage massive retaliation designed to inflict unacceptable damage. All three statements are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PYQ-style

125. Which of the following statements about INS Vikramaditya is correct?
(a) It was built indigenously at Cochin Shipyard (b) It was decommissioned in 2017 (c) It was purchased from Russia and commissioned in 2013 (d) It is a nuclear-powered carrier
Answer: (c)
Solution: The first option is the trap because it describes INS Vikrant, and the similar names cause confusion. INS Vikramaditya is the refurbished Russian carrier Admiral Gorshkov, commissioned into the Indian Navy in November 2013, so the answer is that it was purchased from Russia and commissioned in 2013.
Source: PYQ-style
