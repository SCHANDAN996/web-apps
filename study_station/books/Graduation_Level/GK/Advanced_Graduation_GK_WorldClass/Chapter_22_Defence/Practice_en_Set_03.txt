Questions 51–75: Medium

51. The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) was launched in which year under the leadership of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam?
(a) 1974 (b) 1998 (c) 1988 (d) 1983
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many candidates pick 1974, confusing it with India's first nuclear test (Smiling Buddha), but that was a separate event. IGMDP was sanctioned in 1983 and developed Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Nag and Trishul missiles, so the answer is 1983.
Source: DRDO official records

52. Which of the following is a beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile developed indigenously by DRDO?
(a) Astra (b) Akash (c) Nag (d) Prithvi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Akash is a tempting wrong choice, but it is a surface-to-air missile, not air-to-air. Astra is India's indigenous beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile, so the answer is Astra.
Source: DRDO official website

53. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) also heads which department of the Ministry of Defence?
(a) Department of Defence Production (b) Department of Defence Research (c) Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare (d) Department of Military Affairs
Answer: (d)
Solution: Department of Defence Production is a common wrong pick, but it is headed by a separate Secretary. The CDS functions as Secretary of the Department of Military Affairs, created in 2019, so the answer is Department of Military Affairs.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

54. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, was commissioned in which year?
(a) 2013 (b) 2019 (c) 2024 (d) 2022
Answer: (d)
Solution: 2013 is a trap because that is when Vikrant was launched, not commissioned — examiners exploit this launch-versus-commissioning confusion. It was commissioned in September 2022, so the answer is 2022.
Source: Indian Navy official records

55. The Agni-V missile has an approximate range of:
(a) 700 km (b) 2,500 km (c) 3,500 km (d) over 5,000 km
Answer: (d)
Solution: 3,500 km is the tempting wrong option because it matches Agni-IV's range, a classic similar-name confusion. Agni-V is an intercontinental-range ballistic missile with a range exceeding 5,000 km, so the answer is over 5,000 km.
Source: DRDO official website

56. Which command of the Indian Armed Forces is the only tri-services theatre command currently in existence?
(a) Andaman and Nicobar Command (b) Western Command (c) Strategic Forces Command (d) Northern Command
Answer: (a)
Solution: Strategic Forces Command is a frequent wrong answer, but it handles nuclear delivery assets and is not a geographic theatre command. The Andaman and Nicobar Command, set up in 2001, is India's only tri-services theatre command, so the answer is Andaman and Nicobar Command.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

57. The Kargil War between India and Pakistan was fought in which year?
(a) 1965 (b) 1971 (c) 1999 (d) 2001
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1971 is a trap due to chronological confusion with the Bangladesh Liberation War. The Kargil conflict (Operation Vijay) occurred in May–July 1999, so the answer is 1999.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science

58. Which of the following is India's first indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine?
(a) INS Chakra (b) INS Arihant (c) INS Vikramaditya (d) INS Kalvari
Answer: (b)
Solution: INS Chakra is a tempting wrong option since it is also nuclear-powered, but it is an attack submarine leased from Russia, not an indigenous SSBN. INS Arihant is India's first indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine, so the answer is INS Arihant.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

59. Operation Meghdoot (1984) is associated with which area?
(a) Siachen Glacier (b) Kargil Heights (c) Sri Lanka (d) Maldives
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kargil Heights is a trap because of confusion with the 1999 Operation Vijay. Operation Meghdoot was launched in April 1984 to secure the Siachen Glacier, so the answer is Siachen Glacier.
Source: Indian Army official records

60. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was formed in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1962 (c) 1958 (d) 1972
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1947 is a tempting wrong choice because DRDO's predecessor, the Technical Development Establishment, dates to that era. DRDO was formally established in 1958 by merging existing bodies, so the answer is 1958.
Source: DRDO official website

61. Which missile of India is a submarine-launched ballistic missile (SLBM)?
(a) Prithvi-II (b) BrahMos (c) Shaurya (d) K-4
Answer: (d)
Solution: Shaurya is a tempting wrong option as it is a canister-launched hypersonic missile, but it is land-based. The K-4 is an intermediate-range SLBM developed for the Arihant-class submarines, so the answer is K-4.
Source: DRDO official records

62. The BrahMos missile is a joint venture between India and which country?
(a) Russia (b) France (c) Israel (d) United States
Answer: (a)
Solution: Israel is a frequent wrong pick because India jointly develops the Barak-8 missile with Israel — a similar-partnership trap. BrahMos is a joint venture between India's DRDO and Russia's NPO Mashinostroyeniya, so the answer is Russia.
Source: BrahMos Aerospace official website

63. India's first nuclear test in 1974 was conducted at which site?
(a) Chandipur (b) Wheeler Island (c) Pokhran (d) Sriharikota
Answer: (c)
Solution: Chandipur is a trap since it is a missile test range, not a nuclear test site. The 1974 test (Smiling Buddha) was conducted at Pokhran, Rajasthan, so the answer is Pokhran.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science

64. Which of the following correctly matches the missile with its type?
(a) Nag — surface-to-air missile (b) Prithvi — surface-to-surface missile (c) Akash — anti-tank missile (d) Trishul — submarine-launched missile
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Nag option is a trap because Nag is actually an anti-tank guided missile, not surface-to-air; Akash is the surface-to-air one — the two are deliberately swapped. Prithvi is correctly a surface-to-surface ballistic missile, so the answer is Prithvi — surface-to-surface missile.
Source: DRDO official website

65. The Tejas Light Combat Aircraft is developed by which organisation?
(a) Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (b) Bharat Electronics Limited (c) Bharat Dynamics Limited (d) Mishra Dhatu Nigam
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bharat Dynamics Limited is a tempting wrong choice, but it manufactures missiles, not aircraft. Tejas was designed by the Aeronautical Development Agency and is manufactured by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited, so the answer is Hindustan Aeronautics Limited.
Source: HAL official website

66. The National Security Guard (NSG) was raised in which year following which legislation?
(a) 1984, under the NSG Act (b) 1962, after the Sino-Indian War (c) 1999, after the Kargil War (d) 2008, after the Mumbai attacks
Answer: (a)
Solution: 2008 is a strong trap because NSG hubs were expanded after the Mumbai attacks, but the force itself is much older. The NSG was raised in 1984 under the National Security Guard Act, so the answer is 1984, under the NSG Act.
Source: NSG official website

67. Which of the following statements about INS Vikramaditya is correct?
(a) It is an indigenously built aircraft carrier (b) It is a nuclear-powered carrier (c) It is a modified Kiev-class carrier acquired from Russia (d) It was commissioned in 2022
Answer: (c)
Solution: The indigenous option is a trap because it describes INS Vikrant, not Vikramaditya — a classic similar-name confusion. INS Vikramaditya is the refurbished Admiral Gorshkov, a Kiev-class carrier acquired from Russia and commissioned in 2013, so the answer is that it is a modified Kiev-class carrier acquired from Russia.
Source: Indian Navy official records

68. The Strategic Forces Command of India is responsible for:
(a) Coastal security (b) Border guarding along the LoC (c) Management of nuclear weapon delivery systems (d) Cyber warfare
Answer: (c)
Solution: Cyber warfare is a tempting wrong option, but that falls under the Defence Cyber Agency. The Strategic Forces Command, created in 2003, manages and operationalises India's nuclear delivery assets, so the answer is management of nuclear weapon delivery systems.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

69. Which paramilitary force guards the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders?
(a) ITBP (b) BSF (c) SSB (d) CISF
Answer: (b)
Solution: ITBP is a trap because it guards a border too, but its assignment is the India–China frontier. The Border Security Force guards the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders, so the answer is BSF.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

70. The S-400 air defence system procured by India is of which country's origin?
(a) United States (b) Israel (c) Russia (d) France
Answer: (c)
Solution: Israel is a tempting wrong option because India buys the Barak-8 and SPYDER systems from Israel. The S-400 Triumf is a Russian long-range surface-to-air missile system, so the answer is Russia.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

71. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of these events?
P. Pokhran-II nuclear tests
Q. Kargil War
R. Formation of the post of CDS
S. Commissioning of INS Arihant
(a) Q, P, S, R (b) P, Q, S, R (c) P, S, Q, R (d) Q, P, R, S
Answer: (b)
Solution: The option starting with Q is a trap exploiting the closeness of the 1998 tests and the 1999 war — Pokhran-II (May 1998) came before Kargil (1999). INS Arihant was commissioned in 2016 and the CDS post was created in December 2019, so the answer is P, Q, S, R.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science; Ministry of Defence

72. The 'Mission Shakti' (2019) demonstrated India's capability in which domain?
(a) Hypersonic cruise missile (b) Anti-satellite weapon (c) Ballistic missile defence (d) Cyber attack
Answer: (b)
Solution: Ballistic missile defence is a tempting wrong choice since both involve interceptor technology, but BMD tests target incoming missiles within the atmosphere. Mission Shakti was an anti-satellite test that destroyed a live satellite in low Earth orbit, so the answer is anti-satellite weapon.
Source: DRDO official records

73. Which of the following statements about the Agni missile series is NOT correct?
(a) Agni-I is a short-range ballistic missile (b) Agni missiles are cruise missiles (c) Agni-V has intercontinental range (d) Agni missiles are developed under IGMDP lineage
Answer: (b)
Solution: The intercontinental-range statement is a trap because it sounds exaggerated, but Agni-V's range above 5,000 km does qualify it as intercontinental-class. The incorrect statement is that Agni missiles are cruise missiles — they are ballistic missiles, so the answer is that Agni missiles are cruise missiles.
Source: DRDO official website

74. The Indian Coast Guard functions under which ministry?
(a) Ministry of Defence (b) Ministry of Home Affairs (c) Ministry of Shipping (d) Ministry of External Affairs
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ministry of Home Affairs is a strong trap because most Central Armed Police Forces work under it. The Indian Coast Guard, established in 1978, functions under the Ministry of Defence, so the answer is Ministry of Defence.
Source: Indian Coast Guard official website

75. The Rafale fighter aircraft inducted into the Indian Air Force is manufactured by which company?
(a) BAE Systems (b) Lockheed Martin (c) Sukhoi (d) Dassault Aviation
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sukhoi is a tempting wrong option because the Su-30 MKI is the IAF's most numerous fighter, creating a familiarity trap. The Rafale is built by the French company Dassault Aviation, so the answer is Dassault Aviation.
Source: Indian Air Force official records
