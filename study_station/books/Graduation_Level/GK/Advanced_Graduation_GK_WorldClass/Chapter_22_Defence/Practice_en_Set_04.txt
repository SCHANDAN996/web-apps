Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) was launched in which year under the leadership of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam?
(a) 1983 (b) 1974 (c) 1991 (d) 1998
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is 1974, which was the year of India's first nuclear test (Smiling Buddha), not the missile programme. IGMDP was launched in 1983 and gave India the Prithvi, Agni, Akash, Nag and Trishul missiles, so the answer is 1983.
Source: DRDO official records

77. Which of the following is India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier?
(a) INS Vikramaditya (b) INS Viraat (c) INS Vikrant (d) INS Jalashwa
Answer: (c)
Solution: INS Vikramaditya is a tempting wrong option because it is currently in service, but it is a refurbished Russian carrier (Admiral Gorshkov), not indigenous. INS Vikrant (IAC-1), commissioned in 2022, was built at Cochin Shipyard, so the answer is INS Vikrant.
Source: Indian Navy official website

78. The Agni-V missile has an approximate range of:
(a) 700 km (b) Over 5,000 km (c) 3,500 km (d) 2,500 km
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 3,500 km option is a trap because that is the range of Agni-IV, not Agni-V. Agni-V is an intercontinental-range ballistic missile with a range exceeding 5,000 km, so the answer is over 5,000 km.
Source: DRDO official records

79. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was formed in which year?
(a) 1947 (b) 1958 (c) 1962 (d) 1971
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 1962 option tempts students who link DRDO's expansion to the Sino-Indian war, but the organisation itself was created earlier. DRDO was formed in 1958 by merging the Technical Development Establishments with the Directorate of Technical Development and Production, so the answer is 1958.
Source: DRDO official website

80. Which command of the Indian Armed Forces is the only tri-services theatre command currently in existence?
(a) Western Command (b) Strategic Forces Command (c) Andaman and Nicobar Command (d) Northern Command
Answer: (c)
Solution: Strategic Forces Command is a trap because it handles nuclear delivery assets of all services but is a functional command, not a geographic theatre command. The Andaman and Nicobar Command, established in 2001 and headquartered at Port Blair, is India's only tri-services theatre command, so the answer is Andaman and Nicobar Command.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

81. The 'Sagar Kavach' exercise is conducted primarily to test:
(a) Anti-submarine warfare capability (b) Naval aviation skills (c) Deep-sea mining readiness (d) Coastal security preparedness
Answer: (d)
Solution: Anti-submarine warfare is a tempting distractor because it is a naval activity, but Sagar Kavach is specifically a coastal security exercise involving the Navy, Coast Guard, marine police and other agencies, conducted after the 26/11 Mumbai attacks exposed coastal gaps, so the answer is coastal security preparedness.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

82. India's first nuclear-powered submarine, INS Arihant, belongs to which category?
(a) SSK (b) SSN (c) SSBN (d) Aircraft carrier escort vessel
Answer: (c)
Solution: SSN is the tempting wrong option because Arihant is nuclear-powered, and students confuse propulsion with role. An SSBN is a nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine, which is what Arihant is, forming the sea leg of India's nuclear triad, so the answer is SSBN.
Source: Indian Navy official records

83. The 'Tejas' aircraft is developed by which organisation?
(a) Bharat Electronics Limited (b) Bharat Dynamics Limited (c) Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (d) Mishra Dhatu Nigam
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bharat Dynamics Limited is a trap because it sounds similar and works on missiles like Akash and Astra, not aircraft. Tejas, the light combat aircraft, was developed by the Aeronautical Development Agency and is manufactured by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited, so the answer is Hindustan Aeronautics Limited.
Source: HAL official website

84. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India also heads which department?
(a) Department of Defence Production (b) Department of Military Affairs (c) Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare (d) Department of Defence Research
Answer: (b)
Solution: Department of Defence Production is tempting because the CDS deals with procurement priorities, but that department is headed by a separate secretary. The post of CDS was created in 2019, and the CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs, so the answer is Department of Military Affairs.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

85. Which of the following missiles is a surface-to-air missile?
(a) Akash (b) Agni (c) Prithvi (d) Nag
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nag is a tempting wrong option because it is also a tactical missile, but it is an anti-tank guided missile, not surface-to-air. Akash is a medium-range surface-to-air missile developed under IGMDP, so the answer is Akash.
Source: DRDO official records

86. The 'BrahMos' missile is a joint venture between India and which country?
(a) France (b) Israel (c) United States (d) Russia
Answer: (d)
Solution: Israel is a tempting distractor because India has major defence ties with Israel (Barak missiles, drones), but BrahMos is a joint venture between India's DRDO and Russia's NPO Mashinostroyeniya, named after the Brahmaputra and Moskva rivers, so the answer is Russia.
Source: BrahMos Aerospace official website

87. The 'Mountain Strike Corps' of the Indian Army, raised for the China border, is designated as:
(a) XVII Corps (b) XV Corps (c) XIV Corps (d) XXI Corps
Answer: (a)
Solution: XIV Corps is a trap because it is the famous Ladakh-based corps (Fire and Fury), but it is not the strike corps raised later. The XVII Corps, headquartered at Panagarh, was raised as India's first Mountain Strike Corps, so the answer is XVII Corps.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

88. The 'Dhanush' artillery gun is an indigenously upgraded version of which foreign gun?
(a) M777 Howitzer (b) Bofors FH-77B (c) K9 Vajra (d) Soltam mortar
Answer: (b)
Solution: K9 Vajra is tempting because it is a modern gun in Indian service, but it is a South Korean self-propelled gun assembled by L&T, not the basis of Dhanush. Dhanush is the indigenised and upgraded version of the Swedish Bofors FH-77B, whose technology transfer India received in the 1980s, so the answer is Bofors FH-77B.
Source: Ordnance Factory Board / Ministry of Defence

89. Which of the following is India's first indigenously developed anti-tank guided missile?
(a) Nag (b) Helina (c) Amogha (d) Milan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Milan is the classic trap — it is an anti-tank missile in Indian service but is of French origin, built under licence. Nag, developed under IGMDP, is India's first indigenous third-generation anti-tank guided missile, so the answer is Nag.
Source: DRDO official records

90. The 'Project Seabird' is associated with the development of which naval base?
(a) Visakhapatnam (b) Port Blair (c) Kochi (d) Karwar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Visakhapatnam is tempting because it hosts the Eastern Naval Command and submarine base (INS Varsha), but Project Seabird specifically refers to the development of the Karwar naval base (INS Kadamba) in Karnataka, so the answer is Karwar.
Source: Indian Navy official records

91. The 'S-400 Triumf' air defence system purchased by India is of which origin?
(a) Russia (b) France (c) Israel (d) United States
Answer: (a)
Solution: The United States is a tempting wrong option because India buys significant US equipment (C-17, Apache, P-8I), but the S-400 is a Russian long-range surface-to-air missile system, contracted in 2018, so the answer is Russia.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

92. The 'Garud' is the special forces unit of which service?
(a) Indian Army (b) Indian Air Force (c) Indian Navy (d) Indian Coast Guard
Answer: (b)
Solution: Indian Navy is a trap because its MARCOS commandos are well known, and students mix up the special forces names. Garud is the special forces unit of the Indian Air Force, raised in 2004, so the answer is Indian Air Force.
Source: Indian Air Force official records

93. The 'Shakti' series of nuclear tests conducted in May 1998 took place at:
(a) Balasore (b) Sriharikota (c) Wheeler Island (d) Pokhran
Answer: (d)
Solution: Balasore and Wheeler Island (now Abdul Kalam Island) are tempting because they are missile test ranges, but nuclear tests are conducted underground at the Pokhran test range in Rajasthan, both in 1974 and 1998, so the answer is Pokhran.
Source: Government of India records

94. The 'Arjun' is the name of India's indigenous:
(a) Main battle tank (b) Fighter aircraft (c) Submarine (d) Aircraft carrier
Answer: (a)
Solution: Fighter aircraft is a tempting wrong option because Tejas and Arjun are often mentioned together in indigenisation lists, but Arjun is the main battle tank developed by DRDO's CVRDE and built at Avadi, so the answer is main battle tank.
Source: DRDO official records

95. The 'National Security Guard' (NSG) was raised in which year?
(a) 1965 (b) 1999 (c) 1984 (d) 2008
Answer: (c)
Solution: 2008 is a trap because the NSG's role in the 26/11 Mumbai operation made headlines that year, but the force itself was raised much earlier. The NSG was raised in 1984 under the NSG Act, following Operation Blue Star, as a federal counter-terrorism force, so the answer is 1984.
Source: NSG official records

96. The 'Astra' missile developed by DRDO is a:
(a) Anti-tank missile (b) Anti-ship cruise missile (c) Surface-to-surface ballistic missile (d) Beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile
Answer: (d)
Solution: Anti-ship cruise missile is tempting because BrahMos dominates that category in Indian service, but Astra is India's indigenous beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile meant for fighter aircraft like Su-30 MKI and Tejas, so the answer is beyond-visual-range air-to-air missile.
Source: DRDO official records

97. The 'Central Industrial Security Force' (CISF) primarily guards:
(a) International borders with Pakistan (b) Forest reserves (c) VIP residences only (d) Vital industrial and infrastructure installations
Answer: (d)
Solution: VIP residences is a tempting wrong option because CISF is sometimes seen at airports and metro stations, but its core mandate is guarding vital installations such as airports, atomic plants, space centres, refineries and major PSUs, so the answer is vital industrial and infrastructure installations.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

98. The 'Sashastra Seema Bal' (SSB) guards India's borders with which countries?
(a) Pakistan and Bangladesh (b) Nepal and Bhutan (c) China and Myanmar (d) Bangladesh and Myanmar
Answer: (b)
Solution: China and Myanmar is a trap because ITBP guards the China border and Assam Rifles guards Myanmar, and students often swap these. SSB guards the open borders with Nepal and Bhutan, so the answer is Nepal and Bhutan.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

99. The 'Mission Shakti' of March 2019 demonstrated India's capability in:
(a) Nuclear submarine launch (b) Hypersonic cruise missile (c) Anti-satellite weapon test (d) Laser weapon system
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hypersonic cruise missile is tempting because DRDO tested the HSTDV later, but Mission Shakti was the anti-satellite (A-SAT) test that destroyed a live satellite in low earth orbit, making India the fourth nation with this capability, so the answer is anti-satellite weapon test.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

100. The 'Defence Acquisition Council' (DAC) is chaired by:
(a) Union Minister of Defence (b) Defence Secretary (c) Chief of Defence Staff (d) National Security Advisor
Answer: (a)
Solution: Chief of Defence Staff is a tempting wrong option because the CDS heads the Department of Military Affairs and deals with procurement, but the DAC, the highest decision-making body for defence procurement, is chaired by the Union Minister of Defence, so the answer is Union Minister of Defence.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India
