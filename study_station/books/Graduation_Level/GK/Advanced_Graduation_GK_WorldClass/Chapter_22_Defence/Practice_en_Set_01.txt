1. The Supreme Commander of the Indian Armed Forces is the
(a) Prime Minister of India (b) President of India (c) Chief of Defence Staff (d) Defence Minister
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many candidates pick the Prime Minister because day-to-day decisions are taken by the executive, but Article 53(2) of the Constitution vests the supreme command of the Armed Forces in the President, so the answer is the President of India.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

2. The Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) of India also heads which department?
(a) Department of Military Affairs (b) Department of Defence Production (c) Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare (d) Department of Defence Research
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is Department of Defence Production, which is headed by a Secretary, not the CDS. The CDS functions as Secretary of the Department of Military Affairs, so the answer is Department of Military Affairs.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

3. The first Chief of Defence Staff of India was
(a) General Bipin Rawat (b) General Manoj Mukund Naravane (c) Admiral Karambir Singh (d) Air Chief Marshal B.S. Dhanoa
Answer: (a)
Solution: General Bipin Rawat assumed office as the first CDS on 1 January 2020; the other officers were service chiefs, not CDS, so the answer is General Bipin Rawat.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

4. The headquarters of the Integrated Defence Staff is located in
(a) Mumbai (b) New Delhi (c) Pune (d) Bengaluru
Answer: (b)
Solution: All tri-service headquarters, including the Integrated Defence Staff, are located in the national capital, so the answer is New Delhi.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

5. The Indian Army's highest peacetime gallantry award is
(a) Param Vir Chakra (b) Maha Vir Chakra (c) Ashoka Chakra (d) Kirti Chakra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Param Vir Chakra, which is the highest wartime gallantry award, not peacetime. The highest peacetime gallantry award is the Ashoka Chakra, so the answer is Ashoka Chakra.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

6. The motto of the Indian Army is
(a) Nation First (b) Touch the Sky with Glory (c) Shano Varuna (d) Service Before Self
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Touch the Sky with Glory" belongs to the Air Force and "Shano Varuna" to the Navy — a classic similar-name trap. The Army's motto is "Service Before Self", so the answer is Service Before Self.
Source: Indian Army official website

7. INS Vikrant, commissioned in 2022, is India's first
(a) nuclear-powered submarine (b) anti-submarine corvette (c) stealth frigate (d) indigenously built aircraft carrier
Answer: (d)
Solution: INS Vikrant (IAC-1) was built at Cochin Shipyard and commissioned in September 2022 as India's first indigenous aircraft carrier, so the answer is indigenously built aircraft carrier.
Source: Indian Navy official website

8. The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) was established in
(a) 1958 (b) 1947 (c) 1962 (d) 1972
Answer: (a)
Solution: DRDO was formed in 1958 by merging the Technical Development Establishment and the Directorate of Technical Development and Production, so the answer is 1958.
Source: DRDO official website

9. The Agni-V missile developed by DRDO is a
(a) short-range ballistic missile (b) cruise missile (c) intercontinental-range ballistic missile (d) anti-tank guided missile
Answer: (c)
Solution: Agni-V has a range of over 5,000 km, placing it in the intercontinental/long-range ballistic missile category, so the answer is intercontinental-range ballistic missile.
Source: DRDO official website

10. The BrahMos missile is a joint venture between India and
(a) France (b) Israel (c) the United States (d) Russia
Answer: (d)
Solution: BrahMos Aerospace is a joint venture between India's DRDO and Russia's NPO Mashinostroyeniya, so the answer is Russia.
Source: BrahMos Aerospace official website

11. India's first nuclear test, conducted in 1974, was code-named
(a) Operation Shakti (b) Pokhran-II (c) Operation Vijay (d) Smiling Buddha
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Operation Shakti, which was the code name for the 1998 Pokhran-II tests, not 1974. The 1974 test was code-named Smiling Buddha, so the answer is Smiling Buddha.
Source: Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India

12. The Strategic Forces Command, which manages India's nuclear arsenal, was formed in
(a) 2003 (b) 1998 (c) 2008 (d) 2014
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Strategic Forces Command was created in 2003 as part of the nuclear command structure announced after the 1998 tests, so the answer is 2003.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

13. The Andaman and Nicobar Command is significant because it is India's only
(a) submarine base (b) naval air station (c) tri-service theatre command (d) coast guard district
Answer: (c)
Solution: Established in 2001, the Andaman and Nicobar Command is India's first and only integrated tri-service command, so the answer is tri-service theatre command.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

14. The Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) functions under the
(a) Ministry of Home Affairs (b) Ministry of Defence (c) Ministry of External Affairs (d) Cabinet Secretariat
Answer: (a)
Solution: A common trap is the Ministry of Defence, but the Central Armed Police Forces such as CRPF and BSF work under the Ministry of Home Affairs, so the answer is Ministry of Home Affairs.
Source: Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

15. The Border Security Force (BSF) was raised in
(a) 1962 (b) 1971 (c) 1969 (d) 1965
Answer: (d)
Solution: The BSF was raised on 1 December 1965 after the 1965 war highlighted the need for a dedicated border guarding force, so the answer is 1965.
Source: BSF official website

16. The Indian Coast Guard functions under the
(a) Ministry of Home Affairs (b) Ministry of Shipping (c) Ministry of Defence (d) Ministry of Earth Sciences
Answer: (c)
Solution: Unlike other Central Armed Police Forces, the Indian Coast Guard operates under the Ministry of Defence, so the answer is Ministry of Defence.
Source: Indian Coast Guard official website

17. The rank of Field Marshal in the Indian Army has been held by
(a) only K.M. Cariappa (b) Sam Manekshaw and K.M. Cariappa only among five-star officers of the Army (c) only Sam Manekshaw (d) K.M. Cariappa and Sam Manekshaw
Answer: (d)
Solution: Both Sam Manekshaw (1973) and K.M. Cariappa (1986) were conferred the rank of Field Marshal, so the answer is K.M. Cariappa and Sam Manekshaw.
Source: Indian Army official website

18. The National Security Guard (NSG) was established in
(a) 1999 (b) 1986 (c) 1984 (d) 2008
Answer: (c)
Solution: The NSG was raised in 1984 following Operation Blue Star, as a federal counter-terrorism force, so the answer is 1984.
Source: NSG official website

19. The 'Make in India' defence project for light combat aircraft produced which fighter?
(a) Tejas (b) Rafale (c) Sukhoi-30 MKI (d) Mirage-2000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rafale and Mirage-2000 are French and Sukhoi-30 is of Russian origin; the indigenously developed light combat aircraft is the HAL Tejas, so the answer is Tejas.
Source: Hindustan Aeronautics Limited official website

20. The Siachen Glacier, where Indian forces are deployed, is located in
(a) Himachal Pradesh (b) Ladakh (c) Uttarakhand (d) Arunachal Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Siachen Glacier lies in the eastern Karakoram range in the Union Territory of Ladakh, so the answer is Ladakh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

21. Consider the following statements about the Param Vir Chakra:
Statement 1: It is India's highest military decoration awarded for valour during wartime.
Statement 2: It was instituted on 26 January 1950 with retrospective effect from 15 August 1947.
Statement 3: It can be awarded only to officers of the Indian Army.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — the Param Vir Chakra is open to all ranks of all three services, not only Army officers. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

22. Arrange the following events in chronological order:
1. First nuclear test at Pokhran
2. Kargil conflict
3. Raising of the Border Security Force
4. Commissioning of INS Vikrant (indigenous)
(a) 1, 2, 3, 4 (b) 1, 3, 2, 4 (c) 3, 2, 1, 4 (d) 3, 1, 2, 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap swaps the 1974 test and the 1965 raising of BSF. The correct order is BSF (1965), Pokhran-I (1974), Kargil (1999), INS Vikrant (2022), so the answer is 3, 1, 2, 4.
Source: Ministry of Defence, Government of India

23. Consider the following statements about India's nuclear doctrine:
Statement 1: India follows a policy of 'No First Use' of nuclear weapons.
Statement 2: India's nuclear doctrine envisages credible minimum deterrence.
Statement 3: The doctrine permits the use of nuclear weapons against non-nuclear weapon states.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 3 is the almost-correct trap — India's doctrine includes non-use against non-nuclear weapon states, not permission to use against them. Statements 1 and 2 reflect the official doctrine, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India

24. Match the following missiles with their type:
1. Prithvi — Surface-to-surface ballistic missile
2. Akash — Surface-to-air missile
3. Nag — Anti-tank guided missile
4. Trishul — Submarine-launched ballistic missile
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 4 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 only (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is pair 4 — Trishul was a short-range surface-to-air missile under the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, not a submarine-launched missile. Pairs 1, 2 and 3 are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3 only.
Source: DRDO official website

25. Consider the following statements about the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP):
Statement 1: It was conceived under the leadership of Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.
Statement 2: It covered the development of Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Nag and Trishul missiles.
Statement 3: It was launched in 1998 after the Pokhran-II tests.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 3 is the chronological trap — the IGMDP was launched in 1983, well before the 1998 tests. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: DRDO official website
