126. Consider the following statements about the Economic Survey of India:
1. It is prepared by the Department of Economic Affairs under the guidance of the Chief Economic Adviser.
2. It is presented in Parliament one day before the Union Budget.
3. It is a constitutional document mandated under Article 112.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — the Economic Survey has no constitutional backing; Article 112 mandates the Annual Financial Statement (Budget), not the Survey. Statements 1 and 2 correctly describe its preparation and timing, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

127. Under Article 112 of the Constitution, the estimates of expenditure in the Annual Financial Statement must distinguish between:
(a) Expenditure on revenue account and other expenditure (b) Plan and Non-Plan expenditure (c) Voted expenditure and discretionary expenditure (d) Defence expenditure and civil expenditure
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Plan/Non-Plan distinction is the trap — it was an administrative classification abolished from Budget 2017-18, not a constitutional requirement. Article 112(2) requires the estimates to distinguish expenditure on revenue account from other expenditure, so the answer is expenditure on revenue account and other expenditure.
Source: Constitution of India, Article 112

128. Which of the following is NOT charged on the Consolidated Fund of India?
(a) Salary and allowances of the President (b) Salaries of Ministers of the Union Government (c) Salaries of the Comptroller and Auditor General (d) Salary and allowances of the Speaker of the Lok Sabha
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Speaker's salary is the common trap — it IS charged on the Consolidated Fund, along with the President's emoluments, judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts, and the CAG. Ministers' salaries are voted by Parliament, so the answer is salaries of Ministers of the Union Government.
Source: Constitution of India, Article 112(3)

129. The practice of presenting the Railway Budget separately from the General Budget was discontinued from which year?
(a) 2016-17 (b) 2017-18 (c) 2018-19 (d) 2015-16
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 2016-17, the year the decision was announced on the Bibek Debroy Committee's recommendation, but the merged Budget was first presented for 2017-18. The separate Railway Budget had existed since 1924, so the answer is 2017-18.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

130. The Union Budget presentation date was advanced to 1 February from which Budget onwards?
(a) Budget 2017-18 (b) Budget 2016-17 (c) Budget 2019-20 (d) Budget 2015-16
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 2016-17 — the decision to advance the date was taken in 2016, but the first Budget actually presented on 1 February was for 2017-18, ending the practice of presenting it on the last working day of February, so the answer is Budget 2017-18.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

131. Which committee recommended the abolition of the Plan and Non-Plan expenditure classification in the Budget?
(a) N.K. Singh Committee (b) C. Rangarajan Committee (c) Bimal Jalan Committee (d) Vijay Kelkar Committee
Answer: (b)
Solution: The N.K. Singh Committee is the trap — it reviewed the FRBM framework, not the Plan/Non-Plan classification. The C. Rangarajan Committee (2011) on efficient management of public expenditure recommended ending the Plan/Non-Plan distinction, implemented from Budget 2017-18, so the answer is C. Rangarajan Committee.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

132. Under the FRBM Act, 2003, the Central Government was originally required to eliminate the revenue deficit by which year?
(a) 2008-09 (b) 2007-08 (c) 2006-07 (d) 2009-10
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 2007-08, which was the original target for reducing the fiscal deficit to 3% of GDP. The revenue deficit was to be eliminated by 31 March 2009, i.e., by the end of 2008-09 (targets were later revised), so the answer is 2008-09.
Source: FRBM Act, 2003

133. Which of the following documents is NOT mandated to be presented along with the Union Budget under the FRBM Act, 2003?
(a) Medium-term Fiscal Policy Statement (b) Fiscal Policy Strategy Statement (c) Medium-term Expenditure Framework Statement (d) Macroeconomic Framework Statement
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Medium-term Expenditure Framework Statement is the trap — it was added later by the FRBM amendment of 2012 and presented from Budget 2014-15, not by the original 2003 Act. The original Act mandated three statements: Medium-term Fiscal Policy, Fiscal Policy Strategy, and Macroeconomic Framework, so the answer is Medium-term Expenditure Framework Statement.
Source: FRBM Act, 2003

134. The 'Vote on Account' in the Indian budgetary process refers to:
(a) Voting on the Finance Bill in the Lok Sabha (b) Voting on the Appropriation Bill (c) Grant in advance for expenditure pending detailed discussion of demands (d) Approval of the Railway Budget
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is voting on the Appropriation Bill — that happens after demands are passed, whereas Vote on Account is an advance grant (usually for two months) to meet expenditure until the full Budget is approved, so the answer is grant in advance for expenditure pending detailed discussion of demands.
Source: Indian Parliamentary Practice

135. Which Article of the Constitution provides that no tax shall be levied or collected except by authority of law?
(a) Article 110 (b) Article 266 (c) Article 112 (d) Article 265
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Article 112, which deals with the Annual Financial Statement, not taxation. Article 265 embodies the principle that taxation requires legislative authority, so the answer is Article 265.
Source: Constitution of India

136. The Contingency Fund of India is placed at the disposal of:
(a) The Prime Minister (b) The Finance Minister (c) The Comptroller and Auditor General (d) The President
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Finance Minister is the tempting trap since the Fund is operated by the Finance Ministry on the President's behalf, but constitutionally the Contingency Fund under Article 267 is placed at the disposal of the President, so the answer is the President.
Source: Constitution of India, Article 267

137. The corpus of the Contingency Fund of India was enhanced to ₹30,000 crore in which year?
(a) 2005 (b) 2020 (c) 2023 (d) 2010
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 2005, when the corpus was raised from ₹500 crore to ₹5,000 crore. Through the Finance Act 2020, the corpus was increased to ₹30,000 crore, so the answer is 2020.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

138. Which of the following correctly describes the 'Gender Budget' in India?
(a) A separate budget presented for women-related ministries (b) A statement in the Budget reporting allocations for women-oriented schemes (c) A constitutional requirement under Article 112 (d) A budget prepared by the Ministry of Women and Child Development alone
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the idea of a separate budget for women — India presents no such document. Since 2005-06, the Budget includes a Gender Budget Statement (Part A: 100% women-specific schemes; Part B: schemes with 30-99% allocation for women), so the answer is a statement in the Budget reporting allocations for women-oriented schemes.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Gender Budget Statement

139. The first Economic Survey of India was presented in which year?
(a) 1955-56 (b) 1947-48 (c) 1950-51 (d) 1960-61
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 1947-48, the year of independence, which sounds plausible. The first Economic Survey was presented in 1950-51; until 1964 it was presented along with the Budget, and thereafter separately, so the answer is 1950-51.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

140. The Economic Survey began to be presented in two volumes from which year?
(a) 2017-18 (b) 2015-16 (c) 2016-17 (d) 2014-15
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 2017-18, when the Survey famously appeared in a pink cover. The two-volume format — Volume 1 with analytical chapters and Volume 2 with the standard review of the economy — began with the Economic Survey 2014-15, so the answer is 2014-15.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Economic Survey

141. Which Article of the Constitution deals with the Annual Financial Statement of a State?
(a) Article 280 (b) Article 112 (c) Article 266 (d) Article 202
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Article 112, which applies only to the Union. For States, the corresponding provision is Article 202, while Article 266 covers Consolidated Funds and Article 280 the Finance Commission, so the answer is Article 202.
Source: Constitution of India

142. The 'Halwa Ceremony' associated with the Union Budget marks:
(a) The presentation of the Budget in Parliament (b) The beginning of the printing of Budget documents (c) The signing of the Budget by the President (d) The passage of the Appropriation Bill
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the presentation of the Budget — the ceremony happens days before presentation. The Halwa Ceremony, held in the basement of North Block, marks the start of printing Budget documents, after which officials involved remain in lock-in until presentation, so the answer is the beginning of the printing of Budget documents.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

143. Who presented the first Budget of independent India?
(a) C.D. Deshmukh (b) John Mathai (c) Liaquat Ali Khan (d) R.K. Shanmukham Chetty
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is John Mathai, who presented the first Budget of the Republic (1950-51). R.K. Shanmukham Chetty presented independent India's first Budget on 26 November 1947, so the answer is R.K. Shanmukham Chetty.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

144. Which Finance Minister has presented the maximum number of Union Budgets?
(a) P. Chidambaram (b) Morarji Desai (c) Yashwant Sinha (d) Pranab Mukherjee
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is P. Chidambaram, who presented nine Budgets. Morarji Desai presented ten Budgets (including two interim Budgets) between 1959 and 1969, the highest by any Finance Minister, so the answer is Morarji Desai.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

145. The 'Output-Outcome Monitoring Framework' in the Union Budget was introduced from:
(a) Budget 2017-18 (b) Budget 2016-17 (c) Budget 2015-16 (d) Budget 2018-19
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 2016-17, when outcome budgeting was announced as a reform direction. The Output-Outcome Monitoring Framework, linking expenditure to measurable outputs and outcomes for each scheme, was introduced with Budget 2017-18, so the answer is Budget 2017-18.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India

146. Which of the following statements about the Finance Bill is correct?
1. It is a Money Bill under Article 110.
2. It can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha.
3. The Rajya Sabha can amend it.
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — the Rajya Sabha can only make recommendations on a Money Bill, which the Lok Sabha may accept or reject; it cannot amend it. The Finance Bill, containing taxation proposals, is a Money Bill introduced only in the Lok Sabha, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Constitution of India, Articles 109-110

147. 'Guillotine' in the budgetary process refers to:
(a) Rejection of a cut motion (b) Withdrawal of the Finance Bill (c) Adjournment of the Budget session (d) Putting all outstanding demands to vote together without discussion
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is rejection of a cut motion — cut motions are separate devices to reduce demands. When the time allotted for discussion on Demands for Grants expires, the Speaker puts all remaining demands to vote at once; this device is called the Guillotine, so the answer is putting all outstanding demands to vote together without discussion.
Source: Indian Parliamentary Practice

148. Which of the following is a 'Capital Receipt' for the Government of India?
(a) Income tax collections (b) Disinvestment proceeds (c) Dividends from public sector enterprises (d) Interest receipts on loans given to states
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is dividends from PSUs — despite sounding like investment income, dividends are non-tax revenue receipts. Disinvestment proceeds reduce government assets and are classified as capital receipts, so the answer is disinvestment proceeds.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Budget documents

149. Effective Revenue Deficit, introduced in Budget 2011-12, is defined as:
(a) Revenue deficit minus grants for creation of capital assets (b) Fiscal deficit minus revenue deficit (c) Revenue deficit minus interest payments (d) Fiscal deficit minus capital expenditure
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is fiscal deficit minus capital expenditure, which confuses the concept with primary deficit (primary deficit equals fiscal deficit minus interest payments). Effective Revenue Deficit equals revenue deficit minus grants-in-aid to states for creation of capital assets, so the answer is revenue deficit minus grants for creation of capital assets.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Budget 2011-12

150. The 'bahi-khata' (red cloth ledger) replacing the leather briefcase for carrying Budget documents was first used by Nirmala Sitharaman in:
(a) Budget 2017-18 (b) Budget 2018-19 (c) Budget 2019-20 (d) Budget 2020-21
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is 2020-21 — that was the first paperless (tablet-based) Budget due to the pandemic. The traditional bahi-khata replaced the briefcase first in Budget 2019-20, Nirmala Sitharaman's first Budget as Finance Minister, so the answer is Budget 2019-20.
Source: Ministry of Finance, Government of India
