51. The Union Budget of India is presented under which Article of the Constitution?
(a) Article 110 (b) Article 112 (c) Article 114 (d) Article 116
Answer: (b)
Solution: Article 110 defines a Money Bill, which is the tempting wrong option because the Budget is often loosely called a Money Bill; however, the constitutional requirement to lay the Annual Financial Statement before Parliament is in Article 112, so the answer is Article 112.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

52. The Economic Survey of India is prepared by which body?
(a) NITI Aayog (b) Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (c) Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance (d) Reserve Bank of India
Answer: (c)
Solution: NITI Aayog is a common trap since it deals with economic planning documents, but the Economic Survey is prepared by the Economic Division of the Department of Economic Affairs under the Ministry of Finance, so the answer is Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance.
Source: PYQ-style

53. Which of the following correctly describes 'fiscal deficit'?
(a) Total expenditure minus total revenue receipts (b) Fiscal deficit minus interest payments (c) Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts (d) Total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first option is tempting but wrong because it ignores capital receipts like disinvestment; fiscal deficit equals total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings, so the answer is total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

54. The 'primary deficit' is obtained by subtracting which item from the fiscal deficit?
(a) Revenue deficit (b) Capital expenditure (c) Interest payments (d) Tax revenue
Answer: (c)
Solution: Revenue deficit is the tempting wrong choice as both are budget deficit concepts, but primary deficit is fiscal deficit minus interest payments, showing borrowing needs excluding past debt servicing, so the answer is interest payments.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

55. The Economic Survey is traditionally presented in Parliament on which day relative to the Union Budget?
(a) On the same day as the Budget (b) One day after the Budget (c) One day before the Budget (d) One week before the Budget
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Same day" is a tempting trap because both documents are closely linked, but by convention the Economic Survey is tabled one day before the Union Budget, so the answer is one day before the Budget.
Source: PYQ-style

56. Which of the following is a capital receipt of the Government of India?
(a) Dividends from public sector enterprises (b) Disinvestment proceeds (c) Income tax collection (d) GST collection
Answer: (b)
Solution: Dividends from PSUs are tempting because they come from government assets, but they are non-tax revenue receipts; disinvestment reduces government assets and is a capital receipt, so the answer is disinvestment proceeds.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

57. The 'revenue deficit' implies that the government is:
(a) Spending more on revenue account than it earns on revenue account (b) Running a trade deficit (c) Borrowing to finance asset creation (d) Unable to pay interest on debt
Answer: (a)
Solution: Trade deficit is a tempting distractor as it sounds similar, but it relates to imports and exports; revenue deficit means revenue expenditure exceeds revenue receipts, so the answer is spending more on revenue account than it earns on revenue account.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

58. The FRBM Act, 2003 primarily deals with:
(a) Fiscal discipline and deficit targets (b) Foreign exchange management (c) Monetary policy targets (d) Banking regulation
Answer: (a)
Solution: Foreign exchange management is a trap because FEMA sounds similar to FRBM, but the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act sets targets for deficits and government debt, so the answer is fiscal discipline and deficit targets.

59. The GST framework in India was designed and steered primarily by which body?
(a) National Development Council (b) Sarkaria Commission (c) Empowered Committee of State Finance Ministers (d) Inter-State Council
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Sarkaria Commission is tempting because it dealt with Centre-State relations, but it examined administrative ties, not taxation; the GST design was steered by the Empowered Committee of State Finance Ministers, so the answer is Empowered Committee of State Finance Ministers.

60. In the Union Budget, the 'plan expenditure' and 'non-plan expenditure' classification was discontinued from which budget?
(a) Budget 2017-18 (b) Budget 2016-17 (c) Budget 2018-19 (d) Budget 2015-16
Answer: (a)
Solution: 2015-16 is tempting because the Planning Commission was replaced by NITI Aayog in 2015, but the plan/non-plan classification was actually removed from Budget 2017-18, so the answer is Budget 2017-18.

62. The Union Budget presentation date was advanced to 1 February from which budget year?
(a) 2017-18 (b) 2016-17 (c) 2018-19 (d) 2015-16
Answer: (a)
Solution: 2018-19 is tempting as many budget reforms cluster in that period, but the shift from the last day of February to 1 February happened with Budget 2017-18, so the answer is 2017-18.

63. Which of the following is NOT a component of the Union Budget documents?
(a) Monetary Policy Statement (b) Annual Financial Statement (c) Demands for Grants (d) Finance Bill
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Finance Bill is tempting as a wrong answer because it sounds separate, but it is a core budget document; the Monetary Policy Statement is issued by the RBI, not with the Budget, so the answer is Monetary Policy Statement.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

64. 'Effective revenue deficit' is defined as:
(a) Revenue deficit plus capital expenditure (b) Fiscal deficit minus revenue deficit (c) Primary deficit minus interest payments (d) Revenue deficit minus grants for creation of capital assets
Answer: (d)
Solution: Fiscal deficit minus revenue deficit is tempting as it sounds technical, but effective revenue deficit equals revenue deficit minus grants-in-aid for creation of capital assets, so the answer is revenue deficit minus grants for creation of capital assets.
Source: PYQ-style

65. The 'Vote on Account' in the budgetary process refers to:
(a) Voting on the Finance Bill (b) Approval of the Appropriation Bill (c) Voting on Demands for Grants (d) Grant to meet expenditure for a short period pending full budget approval
Answer: (d)
Solution: Voting on Demands for Grants is tempting because both involve parliamentary approval of spending, but a Vote on Account is an interim grant for a short period, usually in election years, so the answer is grant to meet expenditure for a short period pending full budget approval.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

66. Which Article of the Constitution deals with the 'Appropriation Bill'?
(a) Article 114 (b) Article 113 (c) Article 112 (d) Article 115
Answer: (a)
Solution: Article 112 is tempting because it concerns the Budget itself, but the Appropriation Bill, which authorises withdrawal from the Consolidated Fund, falls under Article 114, so the answer is Article 114.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

68. 'Disguised unemployment' as discussed in economic surveys and NCERT texts is most commonly found in which sector in India?
(a) Manufacturing (b) Information technology (c) Agriculture (d) Banking
Answer: (c)
Solution: Manufacturing is tempting because of surplus labour in factories, but disguised unemployment, where more people work than needed, is characteristic of Indian agriculture, so the answer is agriculture.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Economics

69. The 'twin balance sheet problem' highlighted in the Economic Survey refers to stress in:
(a) Government and RBI balance sheets (b) Banks and corporate sector balance sheets (c) Centre and State budgets (d) Imports and exports
Answer: (b)
Solution: Centre and State budgets is a tempting trap due to the word "twin," but the twin balance sheet problem refers to stressed balance sheets of public sector banks and over-leveraged corporates, so the answer is banks and corporate sector balance sheets.
Source: PYQ-style

72. In budget terminology, 'cess' differs from a tax because:
(a) Cess is voluntary (b) Cess is levied only by States (c) Cess is shared fully with States (d) Cess proceeds are earmarked for a specific purpose
Answer: (d)
Solution: "Shared fully with States" is a tempting trap, but cess proceeds are not shared with States and are earmarked for a specific purpose like health or education, so the answer is cess proceeds are earmarked for a specific purpose.

74. Which of the following best describes 'disinvestment'?
(a) Government borrowing from the market (b) Sale of government equity in public sector enterprises (c) Printing of new currency (d) Transfer of funds to States
Answer: (b)
Solution: Government borrowing is tempting because both raise resources for the government, but disinvestment specifically means selling government equity in PSUs, so the answer is sale of government equity in public sector enterprises.

75. The 'Output-Outcome Framework' in recent Union Budgets is intended to:
(a) Replace the Finance Bill (b) Link budgetary allocations with measurable outcomes of schemes (c) Merge the Railway Budget (d) Fix GST rates
Answer: (b)
Solution: Merging the Railway Budget is tempting as it was another budget reform of the same era, but the Output-Outcome Framework links scheme allocations with measurable outcomes, so the answer is link budgetary allocations with measurable outcomes of schemes.
