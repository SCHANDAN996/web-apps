101. The Union Budget of India is presented under which Article of the Constitution?
(a) Article 110 (b) Article 114 (c) Article 112 (d) Article 116
Answer: (c)
Solution: Article 110 defines a Money Bill, which is the tempting wrong option because the Budget is often confused with it; Article 112 mandates the Annual Financial Statement (the Budget), Article 114 covers Appropriation Bills and Article 116 covers votes on account, so the answer is Article 112.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

102. The Economic Survey of India is presented in Parliament by whom?
(a) The President of India (b) The RBI Governor (c) The Finance Minister (d) The Chief Economic Adviser
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chief Economic Adviser is the tempting wrong option because the CEA's office prepares the Survey, but it is formally tabled in Parliament by the Union Finance Minister, usually a day before the Budget, so the answer is the Finance Minister.

105. Fiscal deficit is best described as:
(a) Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts (b) Total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings (c) Fiscal deficit minus interest payments (d) Total expenditure minus revenue receipts
Answer: (b)
Solution: Option describing revenue deficit is the tempting wrong choice, but fiscal deficit equals total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings, i.e., the government's total borrowing requirement, so the answer is total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings.

106. Primary deficit is calculated as:
(a) Fiscal deficit plus interest payments (b) Total expenditure minus tax revenue (c) Revenue deficit minus interest payments (d) Fiscal deficit minus interest payments
Answer: (d)
Solution: Adding interest payments is the tempting wrong step, but primary deficit removes the interest burden to show the current year's borrowing for non-interest purposes, so the answer is fiscal deficit minus interest payments.

121. Consider the following statements about the Budget:

2. The Finance Bill can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha.
3. The Rajya Sabha can amend the Finance Bill.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 1 is the tempting trap — the Budget (Annual Financial Statement) is not itself a Money Bill; the Finance Bill is treated as a Money Bill and can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha, while the Rajya Sabha can only recommend, not amend, so the answer is 2 only.

(a) It is a constitutional document presented under Article 112 (b) It is prepared by the Reserve Bank of India (c) It reviews the state of the economy and is presented before the Union Budget (d) It must be approved by the Cabinet before presentation

Which of the above pairs is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Pair 3 is the tempting trap — fiscal deficit is not simply revenue deficit plus capital expenditure, since capital receipts also matter; pairs 1 and 2 are standard definitions, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
