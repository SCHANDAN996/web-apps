# Budget & Economic Survey — Key Facts & Figures

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Article 112 | Annual Financial Statement (Budget) is mandated by Article 112 of the Constitution | Confusing with Article 110 (Money Bill) or Article 266 (Consolidated Fund) | Similar-article confusion |
| Word "Budget" not in Constitution | The Constitution uses "Annual Financial Statement"; "Budget" is a conventional term (from French 'bougette' = leather bag) | Statement "Budget is mentioned in Article 112" is FALSE | Near-correct statement |
| Budget presented on 1 February | Since 2017, the Union Budget is presented on 1 February (earlier: last working day of February) | Trap: "Budget has always been presented on 1 February" | Chronology confusion |
| Railway Budget merged (2017) | Railway Budget merged with General Budget from 2017–18; separate Railway Budget existed since 1924 (Acworth Committee) | Trap: merger year given as 2016 or 2018 | Chronology confusion |
| First Budget of India | First Budget of British India: James Wilson, 1860; First Budget of Independent India: R. K. Shanmukham Chetty, 26 November 1947 | Confusing Wilson (1860) with Liaquat Ali Khan (1947–48 interim budget presenter) | Similar-name confusion |
| Economic Survey presented before Budget | Economic Survey is tabled one day before the Union Budget (31 January); prepared by the Chief Economic Adviser's office, Ministry of Finance | Trap: "Economic Survey is a constitutional document" — it is NOT | Near-correct statement |
| Economic Survey — two volumes | In recent years the Survey has come in two volumes; Volume 1 = macroeconomic review, Volume 2 = thematic chapters | Trap: reversed volume contents | Reversal trap |
| Money Bill — Article 110 | Budget's taxation/expenditure proposals pass as Finance Bill (a Money Bill); Speaker certifies a Money Bill | Confusing Finance Bill with Appropriation Bill | Similar-bill confusion |
| Appropriation Bill | Authorises withdrawal of money from Consolidated Fund of India; follows passage of Demands for Grants | Trap: "Appropriation Bill can be amended by Rajya Sabha" — RS can only recommend within 14 days | Power-confusion trap |
| Vote on Account | Interim grant for short period (usually 2 months) before full budget passage; in election years an Interim Budget is presented | Confusing Vote on Account with Interim Budget | Similar-concept confusion |
| Fiscal Deficit formula | Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (excluding borrowings) | Trap: including borrowings in receipts | Formula trap |
| Revenue Deficit formula | Revenue Deficit = Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts | Confusing with Effective Revenue Deficit (Revenue Deficit − grants for capital asset creation) | Formula trap |
| Primary Deficit formula | Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments | Trap: adding interest payments instead of subtracting | Formula trap |
| FRBM Act, 2003 | Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act; targets fiscal deficit and debt reduction; escape clause for national security/calamity | Trap: year given as 2000 or 2005; NK Singh Committee (2017) recommended debt-to-GDP anchor | Chronology confusion |
| Fiscal deficit target (2024–25) | Budget 2024–25 set fiscal deficit at 4.9% of GDP; glide path aims to reach 4.5% by 2025–26 | Trap: confusing with revised estimates of previous year | Figure-swap trap |
| GST — Article 246A & 101st Amendment | GST introduced via 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016; implemented 1 July 2017; GST Council under Article 279A | Confusing 101st Amendment with 122nd Amendment Bill (the bill number) | Number-swap trap |
| Consolidated Fund — Article 266(1) | All revenues and loans received by Government of India; no withdrawal without parliamentary authorisation | Confusing with Contingency Fund (Article 267) and Public Account (Article 266(2)) | Similar-fund confusion |
| Contingency Fund — Article 267 | Corpus of ₹30,000 crore (raised from ₹500 crore by the Contingency Fund of India (Amendment) Act, 2021); at the disposal of the President | Trap: "Parliament authorises withdrawal from Contingency Fund" — recouped later from Consolidated Fund | Power-confusion trap |
| Public Account — Article 266(2) | Provident funds, small savings, deposits; no parliamentary authorisation needed | Trap: treating Public Account like Consolidated Fund | Similar-fund confusion |
| Charged vs Voted expenditure | Charged expenditure (President's salary, judges, CAG, debt interest) is NOT voted by Parliament but can be discussed | Trap: "Charged expenditure is voted in Lok Sabha" | Near-correct statement |
| Cut Motions | Policy Cut (₹1), Economy Cut (specified amount), Token Cut (₹100); moved only in Lok Sabha during Demands for Grants | Confusing the three cut amounts | Number-swap trap |
| Guillotine | On the last day of discussion, all outstanding Demands for Grants are voted together | Trap: "Guillotine applies to Rajya Sabha" | Institution-confusion trap |
| Halwa Ceremony | Traditional ceremony before Budget printing; marks start of 'lock-in' of officials in North Block | Trap: ceremony held at Rashtrapati Bhavan | Place-confusion trap |
| Paperless Budget | Since 2021–22, Budget presented in paperless (digital tablet) form | Trap: year given as 2019 or 2020 | Chronology confusion |
| Longest Budget speech | Nirmala Sitharaman, 2020 (about 2 hours 40+ minutes); shortest: Hirubhai M. Patel, 1977 | Confusing longest/shortest speakers | Reversal trap |
| Most Budgets presented | Morarji Desai presented the maximum number of Budgets (10) | Trap: P. Chidambaram or Pranab Mukherjee given as record holder | Person-confusion trap |
| Interim Budget vs Full Budget | Interim Budget in election year via Vote on Account; no major policy announcements; full budget by new government | Trap: "Interim Budget cannot include tax proposals" — it can, but conventionally avoids them | Near-correct statement |
| Disinvestment vs Privatisation | Disinvestment = partial sale of government equity; Strategic disinvestment = transfer of management control | Confusing the two terms | Similar-concept confusion |
| Fiscal deficit vs Debt | Deficit is a flow concept (annual gap); Debt is a stock concept (accumulated borrowings) | Trap: treating deficit as stock | Concept trap |
| Budget documents | Key documents: Annual Financial Statement, Finance Bill, Demands for Grants, Appropriation Bill, Economic Survey (separate), Medium-term Fiscal Policy Statement (FRBM) | Trap: "Economic Survey is part of Budget documents" | Inclusion trap |
| Gender Budget | Gender Budget Statement introduced in 2005–06; part of Budget documents | Trap: year given as 2000–01 | Chronology confusion |
| Outcome Budget | Links outlays to outputs/outcomes; first presented 2005–06 | Confusing Outcome Budget with Gender Budget | Similar-concept confusion |
| CEA vs Finance Minister | Chief Economic Adviser authors Economic Survey; Finance Minister presents Budget | Trap: "Economic Survey presented by Finance Minister" — it is tabled by FM but authored by CEA's team | Role-confusion trap |
| Budget 2024–25 themes | Employment, skilling, MSMEs, middle class; nine priorities including agriculture, infrastructure, manufacturing | Trap: confusing with 2023–24 'Saptarishi' seven priorities | Year-swap trap |
| Capital expenditure trend | Capex raised to ₹11.11 lakh crore (3.4% of GDP) in 2024–25 Budget | Trap: confusing capex with total expenditure figure | Figure-swap trap |

| Fact/Question Type | Category | Time | Score Maximizer? |

| Deficit formulas (Fiscal/Revenue/Primary) | Fact-based (recall) | 15 sec | Yes |
| Budget history (first budget, merger year, records) | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
| Cut motion amounts | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
| Charged vs voted expenditure | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
| Statement-based questions on FRBM targets | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
| Multi-statement questions mixing Budget + Survey facts | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
| Chronology ordering (Budget reforms timeline) | Analytical | 45 sec | No |
| Deficit calculation with given numbers | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
| Matching funds (Consolidated/Contingency/Public) with features | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
| Distinguishing Interim Budget / Vote on Account / Appropriation Bill | Analytical | 45 sec | Yes |
| Current-year figures (fiscal deficit %, capex) | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
