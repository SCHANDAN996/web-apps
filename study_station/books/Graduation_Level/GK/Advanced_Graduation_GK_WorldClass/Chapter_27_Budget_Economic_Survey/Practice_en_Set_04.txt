76. The Union Budget of India is presented under which Article of the Constitution?
(a) Article 112 (b) Article 110 (c) Article 114 (d) Article 116
Answer: (a)
Solution: Article 110 defines a Money Bill, which is the tempting wrong option because the Budget is often confused with it. Article 112 mandates the laying of the Annual Financial Statement (the Budget) before Parliament, so the answer is Article 112.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

77. Which of the following is NOT a part of the Union Budget documents?
(a) Annual Financial Statement (b) Finance Bill (c) Economic Survey (d) Demands for Grants
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Annual Financial Statement, Finance Bill and Demands for Grants are all presented with the Budget, so they look like valid members of the list. The Economic Survey is tabled separately before the Budget and is prepared by the Department of Economic Affairs; it is not a Budget document, so the answer is Economic Survey.
Source: PYQ-style

78. The Economic Survey of India is prepared by which body?
(a) NITI Aayog (b) Reserve Bank of India (c) Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (d) Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance
Answer: (d)
Solution: NITI Aayog is a tempting option because it handles policy think-tank work, but it does not prepare the Survey. The Survey is prepared by the Economic Division of the Department of Economic Affairs under the Chief Economic Adviser's guidance, so the answer is Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance.
Source: Ministry of Finance (indiabudget.gov.in)

79. Fiscal deficit is best described as:
(a) Fiscal deficit minus interest payments (b) Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts (c) Total receipts minus total expenditure (d) Total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings
Answer: (d)
Solution: Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts defines revenue deficit, and fiscal deficit minus interest payments defines primary deficit — both are classic traps. Fiscal deficit equals total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings, so the answer is total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

80. The Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act was enacted in which year?
(a) 2005 (b) 2008 (c) 2003 (d) 2000
Answer: (c)
Solution: 2005 is a trap because the FRBM rules came into force in 2004 and targets were phased around 2005–08. The Act itself was passed by Parliament in 2003, so the answer is 2003.
Source: Ministry of Finance

81. Which committee recommended the merger of the Railway Budget with the Union Budget?
(a) N.K. Singh Committee (b) Chelliah Committee (c) Bibek Debroy Committee (d) Kelkar Committee
Answer: (c)
Solution: The N.K. Singh Committee dealt with the FRBM review, which is the trap here. The Bibek Debroy Committee recommended merging the Railway Budget with the General Budget, so the answer is Bibek Debroy Committee.
Source: PYQ-style

82. From which year was the Railway Budget merged with the Union Budget?
(a) 2018–19 (b) 2017–18 (c) 2015–16 (d) 2016–17
Answer: (b)
Solution: 2016–17 is a trap because the decision was announced in 2016. The first merged Budget was actually presented for 2017–18, so the answer is 2017–18.
Source: Ministry of Finance

83. The Budget presentation date was advanced to 1 February from which year?
(a) 2015 (b) 2017 (c) 2016 (d) 2018
Answer: (b)
Solution: Traditionally the Budget was presented on the last working day of February, and 2016 tempts because the change was announced then. From 2017 the Budget was advanced to 1 February to allow implementation from 1 April, so the answer is 2017.
Source: Ministry of Finance

84. 'Vote on Account' in the budgetary process refers to:
(a) Voting on the Finance Bill (b) Voting on charged expenditure (c) Approval of Demands for Grants (d) Grant in advance for expenditure pending passage of the Budget
Answer: (d)
Solution: Approval of Demands for Grants is a separate stage of the Budget and is the tempting trap. A Vote on Account is a grant in advance under Article 116 allowing the government to meet expenditure until the Budget is passed, so the answer is grant in advance for expenditure pending passage of the Budget.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

85. Which of the following is 'charged' expenditure on the Consolidated Fund of India?
(a) Salaries of civil servants (b) Defence capital outlay (c) Subsidies on food (d) Salary of the President of India
Answer: (d)
Solution: Civil servants' salaries are voted expenditure and form the trap. The President's salary, judges' salaries and debt charges are charged on the Consolidated Fund and are not voted by Parliament, so the answer is Salary of the President of India.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

86. In the election year 2024, the full Economic Survey for 2023–24 was presented in Parliament on which date?
(a) 31 January 2024 (b) 22 July 2024 (c) 1 February 2024 (d) 30 January 2024
Answer: (b)
Solution: 31 January is the usual pre-Budget date and is the trap; but 2024 was an election year, so only an Interim Budget was presented on 1 February 2024 and no Survey was tabled then. The full Economic Survey 2023–24 was tabled on 22 July 2024, just before the full Budget, so the answer is 22 July 2024.
Source: Ministry of Finance (indiabudget.gov.in)

87. Primary deficit is calculated as:
(a) Fiscal deficit minus interest payments (b) Revenue deficit minus interest payments (c) Fiscal deficit plus interest payments (d) Total expenditure minus revenue receipts
Answer: (a)
Solution: Adding interest payments to the fiscal deficit reverses the formula and is the trap. Primary deficit equals fiscal deficit minus interest payments, showing borrowing needs excluding past debt servicing, so the answer is fiscal deficit minus interest payments.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

88. The 'Halwa Ceremony' associated with the Budget marks:
(a) The start of the Budget printing process (b) The passage of the Finance Bill (c) The tabling of the Economic Survey (d) The presentation of the Budget
Answer: (a)
Solution: The ceremony does not mark presentation or passage, which is the common misconception. It is held in the basement of North Block before printing begins, after which officials stay in lock-in, so the answer is the start of the Budget printing process.
Source: PYQ-style

89. Which Article deals with the Consolidated Fund of India?
(a) Article 265 (b) Article 266 (c) Article 280 (d) Article 112
Answer: (b)
Solution: Article 112, the Annual Financial Statement, is the trap because it is Budget-related. Article 266 establishes the Consolidated Fund, Contingency Fund and Public Account, so the answer is Article 266.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

90. The Finance Commission is constituted under which Article?
(a) Article 270 (b) Article 282 (c) Article 275 (d) Article 280
Answer: (d)
Solution: Article 270 deals with the distribution of taxes between the Centre and States and is the trap. Article 280 provides for the Finance Commission every five years, so the answer is Article 280.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

91. The 15th Finance Commission, chaired by N.K. Singh, recommended what share of the divisible pool of taxes for States?
(a) 43% (b) 41% (c) 40% (d) 42%
Answer: (b)
Solution: 42% is the trap because it was the 14th Finance Commission's recommendation. The 15th Commission reduced the share to 41% after Jammu & Kashmir became a Union Territory, so the answer is 41%.
Source: 15th Finance Commission Report

92. An Interim Budget is presented when:
(a) The government faces a fiscal crisis (b) General elections are due and a full Budget cannot be passed (c) The Finance Bill is rejected (d) The President so directs
Answer: (b)
Solution: A fiscal crisis does not require an Interim Budget — that is the trap. In an election year the outgoing government presents an Interim Budget with a Vote on Account, so the answer is general elections are due and a full Budget cannot be passed.
Source: PYQ-style

93. The Contingency Fund of India is placed at the disposal of:
(a) The Parliament (b) The Finance Minister (c) The President (d) The Prime Minister
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Finance Minister administers it in practice, which is the trap. Constitutionally the Contingency Fund under Article 267 is placed at the disposal of the President, so the answer is the President.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

94. Which of the following correctly matches the deficit concept?
(a) Primary deficit = revenue deficit − capital receipts (b) Fiscal deficit = revenue deficit + interest payments (c) Budget deficit = fiscal deficit + primary deficit (d) Revenue deficit = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts
Answer: (d)
Solution: The other three identities mix up the standard definitions and are traps. Revenue deficit is simply revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts, so the answer is revenue deficit = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

95. The 'Gender Budget' and 'Child Budget' statements in the Union Budget are examples of:
(a) Thematic/statement-based budgeting disclosures (b) Voted capital expenditure (c) Charged expenditure (d) Extra-budgetary resources
Answer: (a)
Solution: They are not separate funds or charged heads — that is the trap. They are analytical statements within Budget documents showing allocations for specific groups, so the answer is thematic/statement-based budgeting disclosures.
Source: Ministry of Finance (indiabudget.gov.in)

96. Who presented the first Union Budget of independent India?
(a) R.K. Shanmukham Chetty (b) Liaquat Ali Khan (c) C.D. Deshmukh (d) John Mathai
Answer: (a)
Solution: Liaquat Ali Khan presented the Budget of undivided India in 1947 and is the trap. R.K. Shanmukham Chetty presented independent India's first Budget on 26 November 1947, so the answer is R.K. Shanmukham Chetty.
Source: PYQ-style

97. The 'Guillotine' in the budgetary process refers to:
(a) Rejection of the Finance Bill (b) Adjournment of the Budget session (c) Putting all remaining Demands for Grants to vote together without discussion (d) Cut motion moved by the opposition
Answer: (c)
Solution: A cut motion is a separate parliamentary device and is the trap. When time runs out, the Speaker applies the guillotine and all outstanding Demands are voted together, so the answer is putting all remaining Demands for Grants to vote together without discussion.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

98. Which of the following is a capital receipt for the government?
(a) Interest receipts on loans (b) GST collection (c) Disinvestment proceeds (d) Income tax collection
Answer: (c)
Solution: Interest receipts are non-tax revenue receipts and form the trap. Disinvestment reduces government assets and hence is a capital receipt, so the answer is disinvestment proceeds.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

99. The Economic Survey is traditionally presented in Parliament:
(a) One day before the Union Budget (b) After the Union Budget (c) Along with the Finance Bill (d) After the passage of the Appropriation Bill
Answer: (a)
Solution: Presenting it after the Budget is the trap based on its analytical nature. The Survey reviews the past year and is tabled a day before the Budget, so the answer is one day before the Union Budget.
Source: Ministry of Finance (indiabudget.gov.in)

100. 'Effective Revenue Deficit' was introduced as a concept in which Union Budget?
(a) 2011–12 (b) 2009–10 (c) 2013–14 (d) 2015–16
Answer: (a)
Solution: 2013–14 is a trap because the FRBM amendment around that time discussed new targets. Effective Revenue Deficit, meaning revenue deficit minus grants for creation of capital assets, was introduced in Budget 2011–12, so the answer is 2011–12.
Source: Ministry of Finance
