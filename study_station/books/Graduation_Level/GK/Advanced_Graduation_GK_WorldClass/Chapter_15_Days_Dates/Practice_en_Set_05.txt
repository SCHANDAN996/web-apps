101. World Environment Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 5 May (b) 21 June (c) 16 June (d) 5 June
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is 21 June, which is International Yoga Day — a frequent confusion because both fall in June. World Environment Day was established by the UN General Assembly in 1972 and has been celebrated since 1974, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: United Nations official website (un.org)

102. International Women's Day is celebrated on which date?
(a) 8 February (b) 8 May (c) 8 April (d) 8 March
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 8 February, which has no such association; the number 8 is correct but the month is often confused. The day has roots in early 20th-century labour movements and was adopted by the UN in 1975, so the answer is 8 March.
Source: United Nations official website (un.org)

103. National Science Day in India is observed on 28 February to commemorate which event?
(a) Launch of Aryabhata satellite (b) First nuclear test at Pokhran (c) Establishment of ISRO (d) Discovery of the Raman Effect
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is the Aryabhata launch (1975), but National Science Day marks C.V. Raman's discovery of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928, for which he won the Nobel Prize in 1930, so the answer is the discovery of the Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT / Government of India (DST)

104. World Health Day is observed on 7 April because it marks which of the following?
(a) Founding of the Red Cross (b) Establishment of WHO in 1948 (c) Eradication of smallpox (d) Signing of the Alma-Ata Declaration
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the smallpox eradication, which WHO declared in 1980, not on 7 April. World Health Day marks the coming into force of the WHO Constitution on 7 April 1948, so the answer is the establishment of WHO in 1948.
Source: WHO official website (who.int)

105. Human Rights Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 10 October (b) 10 December (c) 10 November (d) 10 January
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 10 October (World Mental Health Day) — the day-and-month repetition confuses candidates. Human Rights Day marks the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights by the UN General Assembly on 10 December 1948, so the answer is 10 December.
Source: United Nations official website (un.org)

106. National Voters' Day in India is celebrated on 25 January to mark which occasion?
(a) Foundation day of the Election Commission of India (b) Adoption of the Constitution (c) First general election (d) Introduction of EVMs
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is the adoption of the Constitution (26 November 1949, now Constitution Day). National Voters' Day, observed since 2011, marks the foundation of the Election Commission on 25 January 1950, so the answer is the foundation day of the Election Commission of India.
Source: Election Commission of India (eci.gov.in)

107. World Water Day is observed on which date?
(a) 22 April (b) 22 March (c) 22 May (d) 22 June
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 22 April, which is Earth Day — the shared date "22" causes confusion. World Water Day was recommended at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit and first observed in 1993, so the answer is 22 March.
Source: United Nations official website (un.org)

108. International Day of Yoga is celebrated on 21 June because this day is significant as which of the following?
(a) Birthday of Patanjali (b) Longest day in the Northern Hemisphere (c) Foundation day of AYUSH Ministry (d) Summer solstice in the Southern Hemisphere
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the Southern Hemisphere solstice — 21 June is the summer solstice (longest day) in the Northern Hemisphere, which is why PM Modi proposed it at the UN in 2014, so the answer is the longest day in the Northern Hemisphere.
Source: United Nations official website (un.org)

109. Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) in India is observed on which date?
(a) 26 January (b) 15 August (c) 26 November (d) 26 December
Answer: (c)
Solution: The classic trap is 26 January — that is Republic Day, when the Constitution came into force (1950). Constitution Day marks its adoption by the Constituent Assembly on 26 November 1949, so the answer is 26 November.
Source: Government of India / NCERT Class 11 Political Science

110. World AIDS Day is observed on which date?
(a) 1 October (b) 1 December (c) 1 November (d) 1 January
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 1 October, which sounds plausible but has no such link. World AIDS Day has been observed since 1988 as the first-ever global health day, so the answer is 1 December.
Source: UNAIDS / WHO official websites

111. National Unity Day (Rashtriya Ekta Diwas) is observed on 31 October to commemorate the birth anniversary of which leader?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Lal Bahadur Shastri (c) Subhas Chandra Bose (d) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is Lal Bahadur Shastri, whose birth anniversary is 2 October (shared with Gandhi). National Unity Day, observed since 2014, honours Sardar Patel, born 31 October 1875, so the answer is Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

112. World Population Day is observed on 11 July. This date was chosen because it marks which event?
(a) Establishment of UNFPA (b) World population reaching 6 billion (c) First World Population Conference (d) World population reaching 5 billion
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is 6 billion, reached in 1999. World Population Day was inspired by the "Day of Five Billion" on 11 July 1987, when world population crossed 5 billion, so the answer is world population reaching 5 billion.
Source: United Nations (UNFPA official website)

113. Hindi Diwas is celebrated in India on which date?
(a) 14 October (b) 14 September (c) 10 January (d) 26 January
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 10 January, which is World Hindi Day (Vishwa Hindi Diwas) — a classic similar-names trap. Hindi Diwas on 14 September marks the Constituent Assembly's 1949 decision adopting Hindi in Devanagari script as an official language, so the answer is 14 September.
Source: Government of India (Rajbhasha Vibhag)

114. International Literacy Day is observed on which date?
(a) 2 October (b) 5 September (c) 14 November (d) 8 September
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is 5 September, which is Teachers' Day in India — both are education-related and fall in September. International Literacy Day was proclaimed by UNESCO in 1966, so the answer is 8 September.
Source: UNESCO official website (unesco.org)

115. National Sports Day in India is celebrated on 29 August to mark the birth anniversary of which sportsperson?
(a) Milkha Singh (b) Dhyan Chand (c) P.T. Usha (d) Sachin Tendulkar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is Milkha Singh, but National Sports Day honours hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand, born 29 August 1905, and the day has been observed since 2012, so the answer is Dhyan Chand.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

116. World Red Cross Day is observed on 8 May, which is the birth anniversary of whom?
(a) Henry Dunant (b) Florence Nightingale (c) Mother Teresa (d) Louis Pasteur
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Florence Nightingale — her birthday, 12 May, is International Nurses Day, a near-identical confusion. World Red Cross Day honours Henry Dunant, founder of the Red Cross and first Nobel Peace Prize winner, so the answer is Henry Dunant.
Source: ICRC official website (icrc.org)

117. Anti-Terrorism Day in India is observed on 21 May because it marks which event?
(a) Death anniversary of Rajiv Gandhi (b) Adoption of POTA (c) Kargil Vijay Diwas (d) Mumbai attacks anniversary
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Kargil Vijay Diwas, which is 26 July. Anti-Terrorism Day marks the assassination of former PM Rajiv Gandhi on 21 May 1991, so the answer is the death anniversary of Rajiv Gandhi.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

118. International Day of Non-Violence is observed on 2 October because it is the birth anniversary of which leader?
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) Martin Luther King Jr. (c) Nelson Mandela (d) Dalai Lama
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting options are Mandela and King, both associated with non-violence, but the UN declared 2 October — Gandhi Jayanti — as the International Day of Non-Violence in 2007, so the answer is Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: United Nations official website (un.org)

119. World Tourism Day is observed on which date?
(a) 27 October (b) 27 August (c) 27 September (d) 27 July
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the month confusion — the day 27 is constant, so candidates must fix the month. World Tourism Day marks the adoption of the UNWTO Statutes on 27 September 1970, so the answer is 27 September.
Source: UNWTO official website (unwto.org)

120. Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed in India on which date?
(a) 26 July (b) 16 December (c) 7 December (d) 15 January
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is 16 December, which is Vijay Diwas marking the 1971 war victory — a classic similar-name trap. Kargil Vijay Diwas marks the end of Operation Vijay on 26 July 1999, so the answer is 26 July.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

121. Consider the following pairs of days and dates:
1. World Earth Day — 22 April
2. World Ozone Day — 16 September
3. World Wildlife Day — 3 March
Which of the above pairs are correctly matched?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap in such questions is that one pair is usually wrong; here all three are correct. Earth Day is 22 April (since 1970), Ozone Day is 16 September (Montreal Protocol signed 1987), and World Wildlife Day is 3 March (CITES signed 1973), so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: United Nations official website (un.org)

122. Arrange the following national days of India in correct chronological order through the calendar year:
1. National Voters' Day
2. National Science Day
3. National Unity Day
4. Constitution Day
(a) 1, 2, 4, 3 (b) 2, 1, 4, 3 (c) 1, 2, 3, 4 (d) 2, 1, 3, 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is swapping National Unity Day (31 October) and Constitution Day (26 November). The correct order is Voters' Day (25 January), Science Day (28 February), Unity Day (31 October), Constitution Day (26 November), so the answer is 1, 2, 3, 4.
Source: Government of India (PIB)

123. Consider the following statements:
1. International Nurses Day is observed on 12 May, the birthday of Florence Nightingale.
2. World Red Cross Day is observed on 8 May, the birthday of Henry Dunant.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming only one of these two similar May observances can be correct. Both are correct: Nurses Day on 12 May honours Nightingale, and Red Cross Day on 8 May honours Dunant, so the answer is both 1 and 2.
Source: ICRC / ICN official websites

124. Match the following international days with their establishing/proclaiming bodies:
1. International Yoga Day — proclaimed by the UN General Assembly in 2014
2. World Environment Day — established by the UN General Assembly in 1972
3. International Literacy Day — proclaimed by UNESCO in 1966
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is doubting the Yoga Day year — it was indeed proclaimed by UNGA resolution in December 2014 and first observed in 2015. All three matches are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: United Nations / UNESCO official websites

125. Consider the following statements about Indian national days:
1. Good Governance Day is observed on 25 December, the birth anniversary of Atal Bihari Vajpayee.
2. National Education Day is observed on 11 November, the birth anniversary of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad.
3. National Farmers' Day is observed on 23 December, the birth anniversary of Charan Singh.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the date proximity of 23 and 25 December, which tempts candidates to mark one wrong. All three are correct: Good Governance Day (25 December, Vajpayee), Education Day (11 November, Azad), and Farmers' Day (23 December, Charan Singh), so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: Government of India (PIB)
