76. International Women's Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 21 March (b) 8 April (c) 10 March (d) 8 March
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is 10 March, which some confuse with the date of the first US National Woman's Day observance history, but the United Nations began celebrating International Women's Day on 8 March from 1975 (International Women's Year). The date traces back to the 1917 women's protest in Russia, so the answer is 8 March.
Source: UN official website (un.org)

77. World Environment Day, celebrated on 5 June, is led by which UN body?
(a) UNESCO (b) FAO (c) WHO (d) UNEP
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students pick UNESCO because of its environmental programmes, but World Environment Day was established by the UN General Assembly in 1972 (Stockholm Conference) and is led by the United Nations Environment Programme, so the answer is UNEP.
Source: UNEP official website

78. National Science Day in India is celebrated on 28 February to commemorate which event?
(a) Launch of Aryabhata satellite (b) Establishment of ISRO (c) Discovery of the Raman Effect (d) First nuclear test at Pokhran
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Pokhran test (1974) is a tempting distractor, but National Science Day marks C.V. Raman's announcement of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928, for which he won the Nobel Prize in 1930, so the answer is the discovery of the Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT / Government of India (DST)

Answer: (a)
Solution: Smallpox eradication (declared 1980) is a common confusion, but 7 April 1948 is the date the WHO Constitution came into force, and the day has been celebrated as World Health Day since 1950, so the answer is the founding of WHO in 1948.
