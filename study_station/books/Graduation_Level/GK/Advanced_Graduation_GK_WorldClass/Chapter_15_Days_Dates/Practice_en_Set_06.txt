Questions 126–150: Hard

126. World Environment Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 5 May (b) 5 June (c) 16 June (d) 22 April
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is 22 April, but that is Earth Day, not World Environment Day. World Environment Day was established by the UN General Assembly in 1972 during the Stockholm Conference and has been celebrated annually since 1974, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry (Environmental Chemistry)

127. Human Rights Day is observed on 10 December because on this day:
(a) The UN Charter was signed (b) The International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights came into force (c) The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted (d) The UN Human Rights Council was formed
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the UN Charter, which was signed on 26 June 1945 — that date is observed as UN Charter Day, not Human Rights Day. The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted by the UN General Assembly on 10 December 1948 in Paris, so the answer is the Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Political Theory)

128. Which day is observed on 21 June, a date chosen because it is the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere?
(a) World Yoga Day (b) International Olympic Day (c) World Health Day (d) International Day of Yoga
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is World Yoga Day — the official UN name is "International Day of Yoga", and exams often test this exact wording. It was first celebrated in 2015 after a UN resolution moved by India, and 23 June is Olympic Day, so the answer is International Day of Yoga.
Source: PYQ-style

129. National Science Day in India is observed on 28 February to commemorate:
(a) C.V. Raman's discovery of the Raman Effect (b) The founding of CSIR (c) Homi Bhabha's birth anniversary (d) The launch of Aryabhata satellite
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Homi Bhabha's birth anniversary — Bhabha was born on 30 October, and his name is linked with atomic energy, not 28 February. On 28 February 1928, C.V. Raman announced the discovery of the Raman Effect, for which he won the Nobel Prize in 1930, so the answer is C.V. Raman's discovery of the Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

148. Consider the following statements about World Diabetes Day:
P. It is observed on 14 November.
Q. The date marks the birth anniversary of Frederick Banting, co-discoverer of insulin.
R. It was created by WHO alone in 1991.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(a) P and R only (b) Q and R only (c) P and Q only (d) P, Q and R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is Part R — World Diabetes Day was created jointly by the International Diabetes Federation and WHO in 1991, not by WHO alone; "alone" is the classic almost-correct word examiners insert. Parts P and Q are correct: 14 November is Frederick Banting's birthday (1891), so the answer is P and Q only.
Source: PYQ-style

149. Match the days with their dates: 1. World Press Freedom Day, 2. International Labour Day, 3. World Telecommunication Day, 4. International Museum Day — with dates: W. 1 May, X. 3 May, Y. 17 May, Z. 18 May.
(a) 1-X, 2-W, 3-Z, 4-Y (b) 1-W, 2-X, 3-Z, 4-Y (c) 1-X, 2-W, 3-Y, 4-Z (d) 1-W, 2-X, 3-Y, 4-Z
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is swapping 17 and 18 May — World Telecommunication Day (17 May, marking the founding of ITU in 1865) and International Museum Day (18 May) fall on consecutive days, a deliberate chronological confusion. Press Freedom Day is 3 May and Labour Day is 1 May, so the answer is 1-X, 2-W, 3-Y, 4-Z.
Source: PYQ-style

150. Consider the following statements about days observed in December:
P. World Soil Day is observed on 5 December.
Q. International Human Solidarity Day is observed on 20 December.
R. National Consumer Day in India is observed on 24 December.
Which of the above statements are correct?
(a) P and Q only (b) Q and R only (c) P and R only (d) P, Q and R
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is assuming one statement must be wrong — examiners also use fully-correct sets to punish guesswork. World Soil Day (5 December, honouring the King of Thailand's birthday), International Human Solidarity Day (20 December, UN-designated), and National Consumer Day (24 December, marking the President's assent to the Consumer Protection Act 1986) are all correctly stated, so the answer is P, Q and R.
