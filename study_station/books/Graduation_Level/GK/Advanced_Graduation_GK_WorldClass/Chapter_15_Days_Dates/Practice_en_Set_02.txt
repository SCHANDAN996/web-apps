Questions 26–50: Medium

26. World Environment Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 22 April (b) 5 June (c) 16 September (d) 11 July
Answer: (b)
Solution: 22 April is Earth Day and 16 September is Ozone Day, which are common confusions. World Environment Day, led by UNEP since 1973, is observed on 5 June, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: UNEP official website

27. International Yoga Day is celebrated on 21 June because it marks:
(a) The birthday of Patanjali (b) The longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere (c) The adoption of the UN Charter (d) The winter solstice
Answer: (b)
Solution: Patanjali's birthday is not fixed on 21 June — that is a fabricated trap. PM Modi proposed 21 June at the UN (2014) as it is the summer solstice, the longest day in the Northern Hemisphere, so the answer is the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere.
Source: UN official website

28. National Voters' Day in India is observed on 25 January to commemorate:
(a) The first general election of 1951–52 (b) The foundation day of the Election Commission of India (c) The adoption of the Constitution (d) Universal adult franchise coming into force
Answer: (b)
Solution: The first general election is a tempting but wrong association. The Election Commission of India was established on 25 January 1950, and National Voters' Day (since 2011) marks its foundation day, so the answer is the foundation day of the Election Commission of India.
Source: Election Commission of India official website

29. World Health Day is observed on 7 April because on this day:
(a) Smallpox was declared eradicated (b) The first World Health Assembly was held (c) The WHO Constitution came into force in 1948 (d) The Alma-Ata Declaration was signed
Answer: (c)
Solution: The first World Health Assembly (1948) only decided to observe the day; it did not itself fall on 7 April. The WHO Constitution came into force on 7 April 1948, so the answer is the WHO Constitution came into force in 1948.
Source: WHO official website

30. National Science Day in India is observed on 28 February to mark:
(a) The birth anniversary of C.V. Raman (b) The launch of India's first satellite (c) The establishment of CSIR (d) The discovery of the Raman Effect
Answer: (d)
Solution: C.V. Raman's birthday (7 November) is the classic trap. National Science Day marks the announcement of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928, for which he won the Nobel Prize in 1930, so the answer is the discovery of the Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Physics / Government of India

31. Which day is observed as World Water Day?
(a) 5 June (b) 22 April (c) 22 March (d) 21 March
Answer: (c)
Solution: 21 March is the International Day of Forests, a near-identical date trap. World Water Day, recommended at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit, is observed on 22 March, so the answer is 22 March.
Source: UN official website

32. Human Rights Day is observed on 10 December to commemorate:
(a) The founding of the UN in 1945 (b) The end of World War II (c) The adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948 (d) The establishment of the UN Human Rights Council
Answer: (c)
Solution: The UN founding (24 October 1945) is a chronological trap. The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was adopted on 10 December 1948, so the answer is the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948.
Source: UN official website

33. National Unity Day (Rashtriya Ekta Diwas) on 31 October marks the birth anniversary of:
(a) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Lal Bahadur Shastri (d) Subhas Chandra Bose
Answer: (a)
Solution: Shastri shares 2 October with Gandhi, which confuses many. National Unity Day, observed since 2014, marks Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's birth anniversary, so the answer is Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.
Source: Government of India

34. International Women's Day is observed on:
(a) 12 February (b) 8 March (c) 10 December (d) 11 October
Answer: (b)
Solution: 11 October is the International Day of the Girl Child, a similar-theme trap. International Women's Day is observed on 8 March, so the answer is 8 March.
Source: UN official website

35. World Population Day, observed on 11 July, commemorates:
(a) The adoption of Agenda 21 (b) The first World Population Conference (c) The establishment of UNFPA (d) The world population crossing five billion in 1987
Answer: (d)
Solution: UNFPA was established in 1969, unrelated to this date. The day marks the "Day of Five Billion" — 11 July 1987 — when world population crossed five billion, so the answer is the world population crossing five billion in 1987.
Source: UN official website

36. Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed in India on:
(a) 26 July (b) 16 December (c) 7 December (d) 15 January
Answer: (a)
Solution: 16 December is Vijay Diwas (1971 war victory), the most common confusion. Kargil Vijay Diwas marks the end of Operation Vijay on 26 July 1999, so the answer is 26 July.
Source: Government of India

37. World AIDS Day is observed on:
(a) 7 April (b) 1 July (c) 1 December (d) 24 March
Answer: (c)
Solution: 24 March is World Tuberculosis Day, a similar health-theme trap. World AIDS Day has been observed on 1 December since 1988, so the answer is 1 December.
Source: UNAIDS official website

38. National Education Day in India on 11 November marks the birth anniversary of:
(a) Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan (b) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (c) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (d) Swami Vivekananda
Answer: (b)
Solution: Radhakrishnan's birthday (5 September) is Teachers' Day — the classic trap. National Education Day marks the birth anniversary of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, India's first Education Minister, so the answer is Maulana Abul Kalam Azad.
Source: Government of India

39. International Day of Non-Violence on 2 October marks the birth anniversary of:
(a) Nelson Mandela (b) Martin Luther King Jr. (c) Mother Teresa (d) Mahatma Gandhi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mandela's birthday (18 July) is Nelson Mandela International Day, a similar-theme trap. The UN declared 2 October, Gandhi Jayanti, as the International Day of Non-Violence in 2007, so the answer is Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: UN official website

40. World Tourism Day is observed on 27 September because on this day:
(a) The UN was founded (b) The first international tourism conference was held (c) The UNWTO statutes were adopted in 1970 (d) The Manila Declaration was signed
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Manila Declaration (1980) is a chronological trap. The UNWTO statutes were adopted on 27 September 1970, and the day has been observed since 1980, so the answer is the UNWTO statutes were adopted in 1970.
Source: UNWTO official website

41. National Youth Day in India on 12 January marks the birth anniversary of:
(a) Swami Vivekananda (b) Bhagat Singh (c) Subhas Chandra Bose (d) Chandrashekhar Azad
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bose's birthday (23 January, Parakram Diwas) is a frequent confusion. National Youth Day marks Swami Vivekananda's birth anniversary, so the answer is Swami Vivekananda.
Source: Government of India

42. World Red Cross Day on 8 May marks the birth anniversary of:
(a) Florence Nightingale (b) Mother Teresa (c) Clara Barton (d) Henry Dunant
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nightingale's birthday (12 May) is International Nurses Day — a near-date trap. World Red Cross Day marks the birth anniversary of Henry Dunant, founder of the Red Cross, so the answer is Henry Dunant.
Source: ICRC official website

43. Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) in India is observed on 26 November because on this day:
(a) The Constitution was adopted by the Constituent Assembly (b) The Constitution came into force (c) The first session of the Constituent Assembly was held (d) The Constitution was signed by all members
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950 — the most common trap. It was adopted on 26 November 1949, so the answer is the Constitution was adopted by the Constituent Assembly.
Source: Government of India

44. International Labour Day is observed on 1 May in memory of:
(a) The Russian Revolution (b) The Haymarket affair in Chicago (c) The founding of the ILO (d) The first trade union in Britain
Answer: (b)
Solution: The ILO was founded in 1919, unrelated to the date's origin. May Day commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago during the struggle for an eight-hour workday, so the answer is the Haymarket affair in Chicago.
Source: NCERT Class 10 History

45. World Hindi Day (Vishwa Hindi Diwas) is observed on:
(a) 14 September (b) 8 March (c) 26 January (d) 10 January
Answer: (d)
Solution: 14 September is Hindi Diwas (national), the classic similar-name trap. World Hindi Day on 10 January marks the first World Hindi Conference held in Nagpur in 1975, so the answer is 10 January.
Source: Government of India

46. World Ozone Day on 16 September commemorates:
(a) The signing of the Montreal Protocol in 1987 (b) The discovery of the ozone hole (c) The Kyoto Protocol (d) The Rio Earth Summit
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Kyoto Protocol (1997) deals with climate change, not ozone. The Montreal Protocol on ozone-depleting substances was signed on 16 September 1987, so the answer is the signing of the Montreal Protocol in 1987.
Source: UN official website

47. National Sports Day in India on 29 August marks the birth anniversary of:
(a) Dhyan Chand (b) Milkha Singh (c) P.T. Usha (d) Sachin Tendulkar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Milkha Singh is a tempting name in sports but unrelated to this date. National Sports Day marks the birth anniversary of hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand, so the answer is Dhyan Chand.
Source: Government of India

48. International Day of Peace is observed on:
(a) 2 October (b) 24 October (c) 21 September (d) 10 December
Answer: (c)
Solution: 2 October is the International Day of Non-Violence, a similar-theme trap. The International Day of Peace is observed on 21 September, so the answer is 21 September.
Source: UN official website

49. Good Governance Day (Sushasan Diwas) in India on 25 December marks the birth anniversary of:
(a) Atal Bihari Vajpayee (b) Madan Mohan Malaviya (c) Lal Bahadur Shastri (d) Morarji Desai
Answer: (a)
Solution: Malaviya shares the same birth date and is honoured alongside, but the day was instituted in 2014 specifically for Vajpayee. Good Governance Day marks Atal Bihari Vajpayee's birth anniversary, so the answer is Atal Bihari Vajpayee.
Source: Government of India

50. World Book and Copyright Day on 23 April is linked to the death anniversary of:
(a) Rabindranath Tagore (b) Charles Dickens (c) Leo Tolstoy (d) William Shakespeare
Answer: (d)
Solution: Tagore's death anniversary (7 August) is unrelated to this date. UNESCO chose 23 April as it marks the death of William Shakespeare (and Cervantes) in 1616, so the answer is William Shakespeare.
Source: UNESCO official website
