## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Category | Fact Type | Time Rule | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct date recall (e.g., "When is World Water Day?") | Fact-based | 15 sec — answer instantly or skip | ✅ Yes |
| Person–day linkage (birth/death anniversaries) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Organisation–day linkage (WHO, FAO, UPU founding days) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| National vs International day pairs (Youth Day, Teachers' Day, Consumer Day) | Fact-based with trap | 15 sec — read "National/International/World" carefully | ✅ Yes |
| Two events on one date (1 May, 1 July, 2 October, 7 December) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| "Which day is observed on the LAST week/date of month" (e.g., World Maritime Day) | Semi-analytical | 45 sec — verify wording | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Chronology questions (arrange days/events in order) | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Statement-based questions ("Which of these pairs is/are correctly matched?") | Analytical | 45 sec — check each pair | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Reason-based questions (Why 21 June for Yoga Day?) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ Low frequency |

**Strategy:** Days & Dates is a pure memory chapter — most questions are 15-second fact recalls. Attempt all direct questions first; never spend 45 seconds guessing an unknown date — mark and move on.
