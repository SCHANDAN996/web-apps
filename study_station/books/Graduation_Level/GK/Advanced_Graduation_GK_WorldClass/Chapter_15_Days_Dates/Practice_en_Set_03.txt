51. World Environment Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 5 June (b) 22 April (c) 16 September (d) 11 July
Answer: (a)
Solution: 22 April is Earth Day and 16 September is Ozone Day, which are common confusions. World Environment Day, led by UNEP since 1973, is observed on 5 June, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: UNEP official website

52. International Women's Day is celebrated on which date?
(a) 10 December (b) 12 February (c) 24 January (d) 8 March
Answer: (d)
Solution: 24 January is National Girl Child Day in India, a tempting distractor. International Women's Day, recognised by the UN since 1977, falls on 8 March, so the answer is 8 March.
Source: UN official website

53. World Health Day, marking the founding of the WHO, is observed on which date?
(a) 12 May (b) 1 December (c) 31 May (d) 7 April
Answer: (d)
Solution: 1 December is World AIDS Day and 31 May is World No Tobacco Day — both WHO-related but wrong here. WHO was founded on 7 April 1948, and World Health Day is observed on 7 April, so the answer is 7 April.
Source: WHO official website

54. Human Rights Day is observed every year on which date?
(a) 10 December (b) 24 October (c) 2 October (d) 26 November
Answer: (a)

Source: UN official website

55. National Science Day in India is observed on 28 February to commemorate which event?
(a) The founding of ISRO (b) The launch of Aryabhata satellite (c) The discovery of the Raman Effect (d) Homi Bhabha's birth anniversary
Answer: (c)
Solution: Homi Bhabha's birth anniversary (30 October) is a plausible trap, but National Science Day marks C.V. Raman's announcement of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928, for which he won the Nobel Prize, so the answer is the discovery of the Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT / Government of India (DST)

(a) 15 August (b) 26 January (c) 26 November (d) 30 January
Answer: (c)
Solution: 26 January is the classic chronological trap — the Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950, but it was adopted on 26 November 1949, which is observed as Constitution Day, so the answer is 26 November.
Source: Government of India
