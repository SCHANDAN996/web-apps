1. On which date is International Women's Day celebrated every year?
(a) 8 February (b) 8 April (c) 8 March (d) 8 May
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is 8 February, which sounds plausible but has no UN observance attached. The United Nations began observing International Women's Day in 1977, and the date commemorates the 1908 women workers' march in New York, so the answer is 8 March.
Source: United Nations official observances (un.org)

2. World Environment Day is observed annually on which date?
(a) 5 July (b) 5 June (c) 16 September (d) 22 April
Answer: (b)
Solution: 22 April is a trap because it is Earth Day, a different observance often confused with World Environment Day. World Environment Day was established by the UN General Assembly in 1972 during the Stockholm Conference, so the answer is 5 June.
Source: United Nations Environment Programme (unep.org)

3. National Science Day in India is celebrated on 28 February to commemorate which event?
(a) Discovery of the Raman Effect (b) Launch of Aryabhata satellite (c) Establishment of ISRO (d) First nuclear test at Pokhran
Answer: (a)
Solution: The launch of Aryabhata is a trap since it is a famous Indian science milestone, but it occurred in 1975 and is unrelated to this date. National Science Day marks C. V. Raman's discovery of the Raman Effect on 28 February 1928, for which he won the Nobel Prize in 1930, so the answer is the discovery of the Raman Effect.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry; Government of India (DST)

4. World Health Day is observed on 7 April every year because it marks which of the following?
(a) Founding of the Red Cross (b) Entry into force of the WHO Constitution in 1948 (c) Eradication of smallpox (d) First World Health Assembly in 1950
Answer: (b)
Solution: The First World Health Assembly is a trap because the Assembly decided to observe the day, but the date itself marks the WHO Constitution coming into force on 7 April 1948, so the answer is the entry into force of the WHO Constitution in 1948.
Source: World Health Organization (who.int)

5. International Labour Day (May Day) is celebrated on which date?
(a) 1 May (b) 1 June (c) 1 April (d) 1 March
Answer: (a)
Solution: The other first-of-month dates are distractors with no labour significance. International Labour Day commemorates the 1886 Haymarket affair in Chicago and the struggle for the eight-hour workday, so the answer is 1 May.
Source: International Labour Organization (ilo.org)

6. In India, National Youth Day is celebrated on 12 January to mark the birth anniversary of which personality?
(a) Subhas Chandra Bose (b) Bhagat Singh (c) Swami Vivekananda (d) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam
Answer: (c)
Solution: Subhas Chandra Bose is a trap because his birth anniversary, 23 January, is also observed nationally as Parakram Diwas, but National Youth Day on 12 January marks Swami Vivekananda's birth in 1863, so the answer is Swami Vivekananda.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Youth Affairs)

7. World Book and Copyright Day is observed on which date?
(a) 23 May (b) 23 March (c) 23 April (d) 23 June
Answer: (c)
Solution: The other months are simple date distractors. UNESCO chose 23 April because it is the date of death of William Shakespeare, Miguel de Cervantes, and Inca Garcilaso de la Vega in 1616, so the answer is 23 April.
Source: UNESCO official observances (unesco.org)

8. Human Rights Day is observed every year on 10 December because it marks which event?
(a) Adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948 (b) Signing of the UN Charter (c) Founding of Amnesty International (d) End of World War II
Answer: (a)
Solution: The signing of the UN Charter is a trap since it is also a landmark UN event, but that occurred on 26 June 1945. The UN General Assembly adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights on 10 December 1948 in Paris, so the answer is the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948.
Source: United Nations official observances (un.org)

9. National Voters' Day in India is celebrated on 25 January to mark which occasion?
(a) Adoption of the Constitution (b) First general election (c) Foundation day of the Election Commission of India (d) Passing of the Representation of the People Act
Answer: (c)
Solution: The adoption of the Constitution is a trap because it is linked to 26 January, just one day later. The Election Commission of India was established on 25 January 1950, and National Voters' Day has been observed since 2011 to mark this, so the answer is the foundation day of the Election Commission of India.
Source: Election Commission of India (eci.gov.in)

10. World Water Day is observed annually on which date?
(a) 22 May (b) 22 April (c) 22 March (d) 22 June
Answer: (c)
Solution: 22 April is a trap because it is Earth Day, and the identical day-of-month makes the two easy to swap. World Water Day was recommended at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit and first observed in 1993, so the answer is 22 March.
Source: United Nations official observances (un.org)

11. In India, 26 January is celebrated as Republic Day because on this date in 1950:
(a) India gained independence (b) The Indian National Congress was founded (c) The Constituent Assembly first met (d) The Constitution of India came into force
Answer: (d)
Solution: India's independence is a trap because it is the most famous national date, but independence came on 15 August 1947. The Constitution of India came into force on 26 January 1950, replacing the Government of India Act 1935, so the answer is that the Constitution of India came into force.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Indian Constitution at Work)

12. World Red Cross Day is celebrated on 8 May, which is the birth anniversary of whom?
(a) Florence Nightingale (b) Louis Pasteur (c) Mother Teresa (d) Henry Dunant
Answer: (d)
Solution: Florence Nightingale is a trap because her birthday, 12 May, is observed as International Nurses Day, just four days later. Henry Dunant, founder of the Red Cross and first Nobel Peace Prize laureate, was born on 8 May 1828, so the answer is Henry Dunant.
Source: International Committee of the Red Cross (icrc.org)

13. International Yoga Day is observed on 21 June. Why was this date chosen?
(a) It is the birthday of Patanjali (b) It is the summer solstice in the Southern Hemisphere (c) It marks the first UN resolution on health (d) It is the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere
Answer: (d)
Solution: The summer solstice in the Southern Hemisphere is a trap because the solstice is the correct concept, but 21 June is the longest day in the Northern Hemisphere, not the Southern. Prime Minister Narendra Modi proposed the date at the UN in 2014 for this reason, so the answer is that it is the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere.
Source: United Nations official observances (un.org)

14. National Education Day in India is observed on 11 November to commemorate the birth anniversary of which leader?
(a) Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan (b) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar (c) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (d) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan is a trap because his birthday, 5 September, is Teachers' Day, and both are education-linked observances. National Education Day marks the birth of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, India's first Education Minister, born on 11 November 1888, so the answer is Maulana Abul Kalam Azad.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Education)

15. World Population Day is observed on 11 July. This date commemorates which milestone?
(a) World population reaching one billion (b) Founding of the UN Population Fund (c) The first World Population Conference (d) The "Day of Five Billion" in 1987
Answer: (d)
Solution: The founding of the UN Population Fund is a trap since UNFPA administers population programmes, but it was founded in 1969. World Population Day was inspired by 11 July 1987, when world population was estimated to reach five billion, so the answer is the "Day of Five Billion" in 1987.
Source: United Nations Population Fund (unfpa.org)

16. Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed in India on which date?
(a) 16 December (b) 26 July (c) 7 December (d) 15 August
Answer: (b)
Solution: 16 December is a trap because it is also a military victory observance — Vijay Diwas marking the 1971 war victory. Kargil Vijay Diwas marks 26 July 1999, when Operation Vijay successfully ended with the recapture of posts in Kargil, so the answer is 26 July.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Defence)

17. International Day of Non-Violence is observed on 2 October because it is the birth anniversary of which leader?
(a) Martin Luther King Jr. (b) Mahatma Gandhi (c) Nelson Mandela (d) Dalai Lama
Answer: (b)
Solution: Martin Luther King Jr. is a trap because he championed non-violence and was inspired by Gandhi, but his birthday is 15 January. The UN General Assembly designated 2 October, Mahatma Gandhi's birthday (1869), as the International Day of Non-Violence in 2007, so the answer is Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: United Nations official observances (un.org)

18. National Unity Day (Rashtriya Ekta Diwas) is observed on 31 October to mark the birth anniversary of which leader?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (c) Lal Bahadur Shastri (d) Subhas Chandra Bose
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lal Bahadur Shastri is a trap because his birthday, 2 October, is shared with Gandhi Jayanti and often confused in date questions. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, the Iron Man of India, was born on 31 October 1875, and the day has been observed as National Unity Day since 2014, so the answer is Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs)

19. World AIDS Day is observed annually on which date?
(a) 1 November (b) 1 December (c) 10 December (d) 7 April
Answer: (b)
Solution: 10 December is a trap because it is Human Rights Day, another high-profile December observance. World AIDS Day has been observed on 1 December since 1988, the first-ever global health day, so the answer is 1 December.
Source: World Health Organization (who.int)

20. Hindi Diwas in India is celebrated on 14 September because on this date in 1949:
(a) The Constitution was adopted (b) Hindi was declared the national language (c) The first Hindi newspaper was published (d) The Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari script as the official language of the Union
Answer: (d)
Solution: The declaration of Hindi as the national language is a trap because India has no national language — a classic examiner's trap. On 14 September 1949, the Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari script as the official language of the Union under Article 343, so the answer is that the Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari script as the official language of the Union.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science; Constitution of India, Article 343

21. Consider the following pairs of observances and dates:
1. World Ozone Day — 16 September
2. International Literacy Day — 8 September
3. World Tourism Day — 27 September
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap in such questions is that one September date is usually swapped, but here all three are correct: World Ozone Day marks the Montreal Protocol signing on 16 September 1987, International Literacy Day has been observed on 8 September since 1967, and World Tourism Day falls on 27 September, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: United Nations official observances (un.org)

22. Which of the following correctly arranges these observances in chronological order through the calendar year?
1. World Environment Day
2. Earth Day
3. World Water Day
(a) 3, 1, 2 (b) 2, 3, 1 (c) 1, 2, 3 (d) 3, 2, 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is confusing Earth Day and World Water Day because both fall on the 22nd of their months. World Water Day is 22 March, Earth Day is 22 April, and World Environment Day is 5 June, giving the order 3, 2, 1, so the answer is 3, 2, 1.
Source: United Nations official observances (un.org)

23. Consider the following statements about National Panchayati Raj Day:
1. It is observed on 24 April.
2. It marks the coming into force of the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Both 1 and 2 (b) 2 only (c) 1 only (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming the date relates to the 73rd Amendment's passage in Parliament, which happened in December 1992. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act came into force on 24 April 1993, and this date is observed as National Panchayati Raj Day, so the answer is both 1 and 2.
Source: Government of India (Ministry of Panchayati Raj)

1. International Day of Peace — P. 21 September
2. World Teachers' Day — Q. 5 October
3. International Migrants Day — R. 18 December

Source: United Nations official observances (un.org)

25. Consider the following statements about Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) in India:
1. It is observed on 26 November.
2. It marks the day the Constitution of India came into force.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Both 1 and 2 (b) 2 only (c) 1 only (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 2 is the classic trap: the Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950, not 26 November. Constitution Day on 26 November marks the adoption of the Constitution by the Constituent Assembly in 1949, so only statement 1 is correct and the answer is 1 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Political Science (Indian Constitution at Work)
