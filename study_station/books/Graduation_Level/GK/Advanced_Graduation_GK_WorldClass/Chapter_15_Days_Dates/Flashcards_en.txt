Card 1
Front: Which day is celebrated as National Youth Day in India, and whose birth anniversary does it mark?
Back: 12 January — birth anniversary of Swami Vivekananda (born 1863). Declared National Youth Day by the Government of India in 1984.

Card 2
Front: Army Day (15 January) commemorates which historic first?
Back: It marks the day in 1949 when General (later Field Marshal) K.M. Cariappa took over as the first Indian Commander-in-Chief of the Indian Army, succeeding General Sir Francis Butcher.

Card 3
Front: Why is 26 January celebrated as Republic Day, and what is its link to 26 January 1930?
Back: The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950. The date was chosen because the Indian National Congress had observed 26 January 1930 as Purna Swaraj (Complete Independence) Day.

Card 4
Front: World Environment Day is on 5 June — but which UN conference does it commemorate?
Back: The UN Conference on the Human Environment held in Stockholm in 1972, which led to the creation of UNEP.

Card 5
Front: Examiner's trap: Is National Science Day (28 February) linked to C.V. Raman's birth or to something he did on that date?
Back: It marks the discovery of the Raman Effect, announced on 28 February 1928 — not his birthday (7 November). Exams often swap these two dates.

Card 6
Front: Which day is observed as Hindi Diwas, and why that date?
Back: 14 September — on this date in 1949 the Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari script as the official language of the Union. (Note: World Hindi Day is different — 10 January.)

Card 7
Front: Mnemonic trick: How can you remember that World Health Day falls on 7 April?
Back: "WHO was born on 7-4" — the World Health Organization was established on 7 April 1948, and the day marks its founding.

Card 8
Front: What is the difference between Kargil Vijay Diwas (26 July) and Vijay Diwas (16 December)?
Back: Kargil Vijay Diwas marks India's victory in the 1999 Kargil War; Vijay Diwas (16 December) marks the 1971 victory over Pakistan that led to the creation of Bangladesh.

Card 9
Front: Examiner's trap: Teachers' Day in India is 5 September — but when is World Teachers' Day?
Back: World Teachers' Day is 5 October (UNESCO, since 1994). India's 5 September marks Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan's birthday. The two dates are frequently swapped in options.

Card 10
Front: Which day is observed as National Unity Day (Rashtriya Ekta Diwas), and whose birth anniversary is it?
Back: 31 October — birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (born 1875). The Statue of Unity was unveiled on this day in 2018.

Card 11
Front: International Yoga Day is 21 June — why was this specific date proposed?
Back: It is the summer solstice, the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere, holding significance in many traditions. Proposed by PM Narendra Modi at the UN in 2014; first observed in 2015.

Card 12
Front: Which day is celebrated as National Sports Day, and which legendary player does it honour?
Back: 29 August — birth anniversary of hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand (born 1905). The National Sports Awards are presented on this day.

Card 13
Front: Mnemonic trick: How to remember the three armed forces days — Army (15 Jan), Navy (4 Dec), Air Force (8 Oct)?
Back: Remember them in calendar order through the year: Army comes first (15 January), then Air Force (8 October), then Navy (4 December). Dates: 15 – 8 – 4.

Card 14
Front: What is observed on 26 November, and why was it chosen?
Back: Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) — the Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on 26 November 1949. It came into force two months later, on 26 January 1950.

Card 15
Front: Examiner's trap: Is Human Rights Day on 10 December linked to the UN's founding or to a specific document?
Back: It marks the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR) by the UN General Assembly on 10 December 1948 — not the UN Charter (24 October 1945, which is UN Day).

Card 16
Front: Which day is observed as National Voters' Day, and what does it commemorate?
Back: 25 January — the foundation day of the Election Commission of India (established 25 January 1950).

Card 17
Front: World Population Day (11 July) marks which milestone?
Back: The "Day of Five Billion" — 11 July 1987, when the world population was estimated to have crossed five billion. First observed in 1989.

Card 18
Front: Which day is celebrated as National Girl Child Day in India, and how does it differ from the international observance?
Back: National Girl Child Day (India) is 24 January (started 2008). The International Day of the Girl Child (UN) is 11 October. Exams often mix these two.

Card 19
Front: What is observed on 23 January, and which freedom fighter does it honour?
Back: Parakram Diwas — birth anniversary of Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose (born 1897). The Government of India announced this observance in 2021.

Card 20
Front: Anti-Terrorism Day is observed on 21 May — whose death anniversary is it?
Back: Former Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi, who was assassinated on 21 May 1991. (Trap: Martyrs' Day is 30 January — Mahatma Gandhi's death anniversary.)
