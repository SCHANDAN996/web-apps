76. Which of the following is the correct order of increasing atomic radius?
(a) Na < Mg < Al < Si (b) Si < Al < Mg < Na (c) Mg < Na < Si < Al (d) Al < Si < Mg < Na
Answer: (b)
Solution: Atomic radius decreases across a period from left to right due to increasing effective nuclear charge. The tempting first option reverses the trend, a classic trap, so the answer is Si < Al < Mg < Na.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

77. The pH of a 0.001 M HCl solution is:
(a) 1 (b) 11 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: HCl is a strong acid and dissociates completely, so [H⁺] = 10⁻³ M and pH = −log(10⁻³) = 3. The trap value 11 comes from confusing pH with pOH (14 − 3 = 11), so the answer is 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

78. Which gas is produced when zinc reacts with dilute sulphuric acid?
(a) Oxygen (b) Hydrogen (c) Sulphur dioxide (d) Chlorine
Answer: (b)
Solution: Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂. Sulphur dioxide is the trap — it is released only when metals react with hot concentrated sulphuric acid, not dilute acid, so the answer is Hydrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

79. The oxidation number of sulphur in H₂SO₄ is:
(a) +6 (b) +4 (c) +2 (d) −2
Answer: (a)
Solution: 2(+1) + x + 4(−2) = 0 gives x = +6. The trap value +4 is sulphur's oxidation state in H₂SO₃ (sulphurous acid), a similar-name confusion, so the answer is +6.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

80. Which of the following is an amphoteric oxide?
(a) Al₂O₃ (b) SO₂ (c) Na₂O (d) CaO
Answer: (a)
Solution: Al₂O₃ reacts with both acids and bases, making it amphoteric. Na₂O and CaO are basic oxides and SO₂ is acidic, so the answer is Al₂O₃.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

81. In the electrolysis of brine (concentrated NaCl solution), the gas evolved at the cathode is:
(a) Hydrogen (b) Oxygen (c) Nitrogen (d) Chlorine
Answer: (a)
Solution: At the cathode, water is reduced preferentially to Na⁺, releasing H₂; chlorine is released at the anode. The trap is swapping the electrodes — chlorine at the cathode is wrong, so the answer is Hydrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

82. The IUPAC name of CH₃–CH(OH)–CH₃ is:
(a) Propanal (b) Propan-1-ol (c) Propan-2-ol (d) Propanone
Answer: (c)
Solution: The –OH group is on the second carbon of a three-carbon chain, giving propan-2-ol. Propan-1-ol is the trap — numbering must give the functional group the lowest locant, so the answer is Propan-2-ol.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

83. Which of the following has the highest electronegativity?
(a) Oxygen (b) Nitrogen (c) Fluorine (d) Chlorine
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fluorine is the most electronegative element (3.98 on the Pauling scale). Oxygen is a close second and is the common trap, so the answer is Fluorine.

84. The number of moles in 11 g of CO₂ (molar mass 44 g/mol) is:
(a) 4 mol (b) 0.5 mol (c) 1 mol (d) 0.25 mol
Answer: (d)
Solution: Moles = mass/molar mass = 11/44 = 0.25 mol. The trap value 4 mol comes from dividing molar mass by mass instead of mass by molar mass, so the answer is 0.25 mol.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

85. Which indicator turns pink in a basic solution?
(a) Methyl orange (b) Blue litmus (c) Phenolphthalein (d) Starch solution
Answer: (c)
Solution: Phenolphthalein is colourless in acid and pink in base. Methyl orange is the trap — it turns red in acid and yellow in base, never pink, so the answer is Phenolphthalein.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

86. The hybridisation of carbon in methane (CH₄) is:
(a) sp (b) sp² (c) sp³ (d) sp³d
Answer: (c)
Solution: Carbon forms four sigma bonds with tetrahedral geometry, requiring sp³ hybridisation. The trap sp² applies to ethene with a double bond, so the answer is sp³.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

87. Which of the following salts gives a basic solution when dissolved in water?
(a) NH₄Cl (b) NaCl (c) K₂SO₄ (d) CH₃COONa
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sodium acetate is a salt of a strong base (NaOH) and weak acid (CH₃COOH), so its solution is basic due to anion hydrolysis. NH₄Cl is the trap — it is acidic (weak base + strong acid), while NaCl and K₂SO₄ are neutral, so the answer is CH₃COONa.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

88. The metal used in the thermite process for welding railway tracks is:
(a) Copper (b) Zinc (c) Aluminium (d) Iron
Answer: (c)
Solution: Thermite is a mixture of aluminium powder and Fe₂O₃; aluminium reduces iron oxide in a highly exothermic reaction. Iron is the trap — it is the product, not the reducing metal, so the answer is Aluminium.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

89. Which quantum number describes the shape of an orbital?
(a) Principal quantum number (b) Azimuthal quantum number (c) Magnetic quantum number (d) Spin quantum number
Answer: (b)
Solution: The azimuthal (angular momentum) quantum number l determines orbital shape (s, p, d, f). The magnetic quantum number is the trap — it fixes orientation, not shape, so the answer is Azimuthal quantum number.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

90. In the reaction 2H₂S + SO₂ → 3S + 2H₂O, SO₂ acts as:
(a) An oxidising agent (b) A reducing agent (c) A catalyst (d) A dehydrating agent
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sulphur in SO₂ goes from +4 to 0, gaining electrons, so SO₂ is reduced and acts as the oxidising agent. The trap is assuming SO₂ is always a reducing agent (as with KMnO₄); here H₂S is a stronger reductant, so the answer is An oxidising agent.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

91. The general formula of alkenes is:
(a) CₙHₙ (b) CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (c) CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ (d) CₙH₂ₙ
Answer: (d)
Solution: Alkenes have one double bond and the formula CₙH₂ₙ. The trap CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ belongs to alkanes, so the answer is CₙH₂ₙ.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

92. Which of the following is used as a moderator in a nuclear reactor?
(a) Cadmium rods (b) Uranium-235 (c) Heavy water (d) Plutonium
Answer: (c)
Solution: Heavy water (D₂O) slows down fast neutrons, acting as a moderator. Cadmium rods are the trap — they absorb neutrons as control rods, not moderate them, so the answer is Heavy water.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Physics

93. The bond angle in a water molecule is approximately:
(a) 109.5° (b) 120° (c) 180° (d) 104.5°
Answer: (d)
Solution: Two lone pairs on oxygen compress the H–O–H angle from the tetrahedral 109.5° to about 104.5°. The value 109.5° is the trap — that is methane's angle, so the answer is 104.5°.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

94. Which of the following is a Lewis acid?
(a) BF₃ (b) H₂O (c) NH₃ (d) OH⁻
Answer: (a)
Solution: BF₃ is electron-deficient (boron has only six electrons) and accepts an electron pair, making it a Lewis acid. NH₃ is the trap — it donates a lone pair and is a Lewis base, so the answer is BF₃.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

95. The number of electrons in an O²⁻ ion (atomic number of O = 8) is:
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 16 (d) 6
Answer: (b)
Solution: A neutral oxygen atom has 8 electrons; the 2− charge adds 2 more, giving 10. The trap value 16 is the mass number of the common isotope, not the electron count, so the answer is 10.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

96. Which of the following colloids has a liquid dispersed in a gas?
(a) Smoke (b) Fog (c) Cheese (d) Milk
Answer: (b)
Solution: Fog is liquid droplets dispersed in gas (an aerosol of liquid). Smoke is the trap — it is solid dispersed in gas, so the answer is Fog.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Chemistry

97. Which element was first detected in the Sun's spectrum before being found on Earth?
(a) Helium (b) Neon (c) Argon (d) Caesium
Answer: (a)
Solution: Helium was detected in the Sun's spectrum in 1868 (Janssen and Lockyer) before being found on Earth in 1895. Caesium is the trap — it was the first element discovered by spectroscopy on Earth (Bunsen, 1860), so the answer is Helium.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

98. Which of the following statements about catalysts is correct?
(a) A catalyst is consumed during the reaction (b) A catalyst lowers the activation energy of the reaction (c) A catalyst increases the equilibrium constant of a reaction (d) A catalyst shifts the equilibrium towards products
Answer: (b)
Solution: A catalyst provides an alternative path with lower activation energy and speeds up both forward and backward reactions equally. The tempting wrong options claim it shifts equilibrium or changes K — a catalyst does neither, so the answer is A catalyst lowers the activation energy of the reaction.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Chemistry

99. The chemical formula of washing soda is:
(a) NaHCO₃ (b) NaOH (c) Na₂CO₃ (d) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
Answer: (d)
Solution: Washing soda is hydrated sodium carbonate, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O. The trap is plain Na₂CO₃, which is soda ash (anhydrous); NaHCO₃ is baking soda, so the answer is Na₂CO₃·10H₂O.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

100. In the Modern Periodic Table, elements are arranged in increasing order of:
(a) Atomic number (b) Number of neutrons (c) Atomic mass (d) Density
Answer: (a)
Solution: Moseley's work established atomic number as the basis of the Modern Periodic Law. Atomic mass is the trap — it was Mendeleev's basis, which caused anomalies like Ar–K and Co–Ni, so the answer is Atomic number.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry
