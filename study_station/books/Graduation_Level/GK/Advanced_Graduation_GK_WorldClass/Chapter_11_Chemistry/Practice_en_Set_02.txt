26. Which of the following has the highest electronegativity?
(a) Oxygen (b) Nitrogen (c) Chlorine (d) Fluorine
Answer: (d)
Solution: Oxygen is a tempting choice because it is highly electronegative and more common in compounds, but electronegativity increases across a period and fluorine is the rightmost halogen with the smallest size. Fluorine has the highest electronegativity (3.98 on the Pauling scale), so the answer is Fluorine.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

27. The pH of a solution is 3. Its hydrogen ion concentration is:
(a) 10⁻¹¹ mol/L (b) 10⁻³ mol/L (c) 3 mol/L (d) 10³ mol/L
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is 10⁻¹¹, which is the hydroxide ion concentration, not the hydrogen ion concentration. By definition pH = −log[H⁺], so [H⁺] = 10⁻ᵖᴴ = 10⁻³ mol/L, so the answer is 10⁻³ mol/L.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

28. Which gas is known as 'laughing gas'?
(a) Nitric oxide (b) Nitrogen dioxide (c) Nitrous oxide (d) Dinitrogen pentoxide
Answer: (c)
Solution: Nitric oxide is a common trap because its name sounds similar, but it is a colourless gas involved in vasodilation, not anaesthesia. Nitrous oxide (N₂O) is called laughing gas and is used as a mild anaesthetic, so the answer is Nitrous oxide.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Chemistry

29. The oxidation number of sulphur in H₂SO₄ is:
(a) +4 (b) +2 (c) +6 (d) −2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The value +4 is a trap because that is sulphur's oxidation state in H₂SO₃, a similarly named acid. In H₂SO₄: 2(+1) + x + 4(−2) = 0, giving x = +6, so the answer is +6.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

30. Which of the following is used as a moderator in a nuclear reactor?
(a) Cadmium (b) Heavy water (c) Uranium-235 (d) Plutonium
Answer: (b)
Solution: Cadmium is the classic trap — it is used in control rods to absorb neutrons, not to slow them down. Heavy water (D₂O) slows down fast neutrons and acts as a moderator, so the answer is Heavy water.

31. The chemical formula of washing soda is:
(a) NaHCO₃ (b) Na₂CO₃ (c) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O (d) NaOH
Answer: (c)
Solution: Na₂CO₃ alone is a trap because it is only anhydrous soda ash; washing soda is specifically the hydrated form. Washing soda is sodium carbonate decahydrate, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O, so the answer is Na₂CO₃·10H₂O.

32. Which of the following is an amphoteric oxide?
(a) Na₂O (b) CaO (c) SO₂ (d) Al₂O₃
Answer: (d)
Solution: CaO is a tempting wrong choice as it is a metal oxide, but it is purely basic. Aluminium oxide reacts with both acids and bases, making it amphoteric, so the answer is Al₂O₃.

33. The number of electrons in ¹⁸O²⁻ ion is:
(a) 8 (b) 16 (c) 10 (d) 18
Answer: (c)
Solution: The number 18 is a trap because it is the mass number, which counts protons plus neutrons, not electrons. Oxygen has 8 electrons, and the 2− charge adds 2 more, giving 10 electrons, so the answer is 10.

34. Which acid is present in vinegar?
(a) Acetic acid (b) Citric acid (c) Lactic acid (d) Tartaric acid
Answer: (a)
Solution: Citric acid is a trap because it is found in sour citrus fruits, which students confuse with the sourness of vinegar. Vinegar is a 4–8% solution of acetic acid (ethanoic acid), so the answer is Acetic acid.

35. The bond present in a nitrogen molecule (N₂) is:
(a) Triple bond (b) Double bond (c) Single bond (d) Ionic bond
Answer: (a)
Solution: A double bond is a tempting answer since oxygen (O₂) has one, and students often generalise across diatomic molecules. Each nitrogen atom shares three electron pairs, forming a triple bond, so the answer is Triple bond.

36. Which of the following elements is a liquid at room temperature?
(a) Iodine (b) Sodium (c) Mercury (d) Aluminium
Answer: (c)
Solution: Iodine is a trap because it sublimes easily and appears volatile, but it is a solid at room temperature. Mercury is the only metal that is liquid at room temperature, so the answer is Mercury.

37. The process of coating iron with zinc to prevent rusting is called:
(a) Electroplating (b) Alloying (c) Anodising (d) Galvanisation
Answer: (d)
Solution: Electroplating is a trap because it also deposits a metal layer, but it is a general electrolytic process, not specifically zinc coating for rust protection. Coating iron with zinc is called galvanisation, so the answer is Galvanisation.

38. Which of the following is the lightest element?
(a) Hydrogen (b) Helium (c) Lithium (d) Carbon
Answer: (a)
Solution: Helium is a common trap because it is used in balloons and feels 'light', but its atomic mass is about 4 u. Hydrogen, with atomic mass about 1 u, is the lightest element, so the answer is Hydrogen.

39. The common name of calcium sulphate hemihydrate is:
(a) Plaster of Paris (b) Gypsum (c) Limestone (d) Quicklime
Answer: (a)
Solution: Gypsum is the classic trap because it is also calcium sulphate, but it is the dihydrate (CaSO₄·2H₂O), not the hemihydrate. Calcium sulphate hemihydrate (CaSO₄·½H₂O) is Plaster of Paris, so the answer is Plaster of Paris.

40. Which of the following is a noble gas?
(a) Nitrogen (b) Oxygen (c) Chlorine (d) Argon
Answer: (d)
Solution: Nitrogen is a trap because it is quite unreactive due to its triple bond, but it is not a noble gas. Argon belongs to Group 18 with a complete octet, so the answer is Argon.

41. The atomic number of an element is 17. It belongs to which group of the periodic table?
(a) Group 15 (b) Group 17 (c) Group 16 (d) Group 18
Answer: (b)
Solution: Group 18 is a trap because the element has 7 valence electrons and is 'almost' a noble gas configuration, tempting students to overshoot. With configuration 2, 8, 7, the element (chlorine) is in Group 17, so the answer is Group 17.

42. Which of the following is used in the manufacture of bleaching powder?
(a) Slaked lime and chlorine (b) Quicklime and water (c) Limestone and acid (d) Soda ash and lime
Answer: (a)
Solution: Quicklime and water is a trap because that reaction only produces slaked lime, an intermediate, not bleaching powder. Bleaching powder, CaOCl₂, is made by passing chlorine over dry slaked lime, so the answer is Slaked lime and chlorine.

43. The isotopes of an element differ in the number of:
(a) Protons (b) Neutrons (c) Electrons (d) Protons and electrons
Answer: (b)
Solution: Protons is a trap because students associate atomic identity with proton number, but isotopes of the same element must have identical proton numbers. Isotopes differ only in the number of neutrons, so the answer is Neutrons.

44. Which metal is extracted from bauxite ore?

45. A solution turns red litmus blue. The solution is:
(a) Acidic (b) Amphoteric (c) Neutral (d) Basic
Answer: (d)
Solution: Acidic is a trap arising from reversed memory — acids turn blue litmus red, the opposite change. A substance that turns red litmus blue is basic, so the answer is Basic.

46. The gas evolved when zinc reacts with dilute sulphuric acid is:
(a) Oxygen (b) Carbon dioxide (c) Sulphur dioxide (d) Hydrogen
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sulphur dioxide is a trap because sulphuric acid contains sulphur, but dilute acid with an active metal does not release SO₂. Zinc displaces hydrogen: Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂, so the answer is Hydrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

47. Which of the following has the maximum number of atoms?
(a) 1 g of oxygen (b) 1 g of hydrogen (c) 1 g of carbon (d) 1 g of nitrogen
Answer: (b)
Solution: Oxygen is a trap because its molecule is diatomic and students may double-count, but its molar mass is large, giving fewer moles per gram. Number of atoms per gram is inversely proportional to atomic mass, and hydrogen has the smallest atomic mass, so the answer is 1 g of hydrogen.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Chemistry

48. The IUPAC name of CH₃CHO is:
(a) Ethanal (b) Methanal (c) Ethanol (d) Ethanoic acid
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ethanol is a trap because the names differ by only one letter, but ethanol is the alcohol CH₃CH₂OH. CH₃CHO is a two-carbon aldehyde, named ethanal, so the answer is Ethanal.

49. Which of the following is a greenhouse gas?
(a) Oxygen (b) Methane (c) Nitrogen (d) Argon
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nitrogen is a trap because it dominates the atmosphere, but it is transparent to infrared radiation and does not trap heat. Methane absorbs infrared radiation and is a potent greenhouse gas, so the answer is Methane.

(a) Sodium carbonate (b) Sodium hydroxide (c) Sodium bicarbonate (d) Sodium chloride
Answer: (c)
