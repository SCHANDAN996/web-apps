# 🧒 The Feynman Corner — Indian Economy (Basic)

## The Concept That Confuses Everyone: **Repo Rate and Inflation**

Ask any aspirant: *"If inflation rises, what does the RBI do to the repo rate — and why?"* Most will memorise "repo rate up, inflation down" without understanding it. In the exam, the moment the question is twisted ("What happens to EMIs?" / "Why does a rate hike reduce demand?"), they fall into the trap. Let's fix that forever.

---

## Explain It Like You're 12

Imagine the whole economy is a **giant water tank**, and money is the water.

- **Too much water (money)** flowing around → everyone has cash, everyone wants to buy the same 10 mangoes → the mango seller raises prices. That's **inflation**.
- **Too little water** → nobody buys, shops shut, jobs vanish. That's a **slowdown**.

Now, who controls the tap? **The RBI.** And the tap's handle is the **Repo Rate**.

### The daily-life example

Think of your neighbourhood bank as a **shopkeeper** and the RBI as the **wholesaler**.

- The **repo rate** is the price at which the wholesaler (RBI) lends goods (money) to the shopkeeper (your bank).
- If the wholesaler **raises his price** (repo rate ↑), the shopkeeper's cost rises → he raises **his** prices (loan interest rates) → your home loan EMI, car loan, business loan all become **costlier** → people borrow less, spend less → demand cools → **prices stop rising**. Inflation tamed. ✅
- If the wholesaler **cuts his price** (repo rate ↓), loans become cheap → people borrow and spend more → the economy speeds up. Used when growth is slow, not when prices are rising.

**One line to remember:**
> Costly money → less borrowing → less spending → lower inflation.
> Cheap money → more borrowing → more spending → higher growth (but risk of inflation).

---

## The Unforgettable Trick: **"RBI = Rate Battles Inflation"**

- **R**epo **B**oosts **I**nflation when cut... no wait — flip it with the arrow rule:

**🔼 Repo Rate = 🔽 Inflation** (they move in **opposite** directions)

And the twin trap — **Reverse Repo**:
- Reverse repo = rate at which banks **park** their extra money with the RBI.
- Reverse repo is always **lower than** repo rate (the wholesaler never pays you more than he charges you — he'd go bankrupt!).
- Trick: **"Reverse is Reserved (smaller)"** — the word "reverse" itself hints it sits *below* repo.

**Exam trap alert:** Questions love swapping the direction — "To control inflation, RBI *decreases* repo rate" ❌. If you remember the opposite-arrows picture, you'll never fall for it.
