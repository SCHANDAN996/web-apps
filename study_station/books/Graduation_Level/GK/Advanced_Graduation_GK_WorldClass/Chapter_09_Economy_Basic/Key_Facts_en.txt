# Key Facts & Figures — Indian Economy (Basic)

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Father of Indian Economic Planning | Sir M. Visvesvaraya (book: *Planned Economy for India*, 1934) | Confused with Dadabhai Naoroji or M.N. Roy | Similar names |
| First attempt at national income estimation | Dadabhai Naoroji (1868), in *Poverty and Un-British Rule in India* | Credited to V.K.R.V. Rao instead | Chronology confusion |
| First scientific estimate of national income | V.K.R.V. Rao (1931–32) | Swapped with Naoroji's estimate | Chronology confusion |
| National income now calculated by | National Statistical Office (NSO), under MoSPI | Stated as RBI or Finance Ministry | Institution swap |
| Base year of GDP series (current) | 2011–12 | Old base years (2004–05) given as options | Outdated data |
| GDP at market price formula | GDP at factor cost + Indirect taxes − Subsidies | Signs of taxes/subsidies reversed | Formula reversal |
| GNP formula | GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) | NFIA added instead of netted, or depreciation confused | Formula reversal |
| NNP at factor cost | Called "National Income" of the country | GDP at market price called national income | Concept swap |
| Planning Commission established | 15 March 1950 (by executive resolution, non-statutory) | Called a constitutional body | Body-type trap |
| NITI Aayog established | 1 January 2015 (replaced Planning Commission) | Called a statutory body; year given as 2014 | Body-type + year trap |
| NITI Aayog full form | National Institution for Transforming India | Wrong expansions offered | Acronym trap |
| Vice-Chairperson of NITI Aayog | De facto executive head; PM is ex-officio Chairperson | Vice-Chairperson called Chairperson | Role swap |
| First Five-Year Plan | 1951–56, based on Harrod–Domar model, focus: agriculture | Model swapped with Mahalanobis | Model-plan mismatch |
| Second Five-Year Plan | 1956–61, based on P.C. Mahalanobis model, focus: heavy industry | Attributed to First Plan | Model-plan mismatch |
| Plan holiday | 1966–69 (three annual plans) due to wars and drought | Placed after 1979 or 1991 | Chronology confusion |
| Longest plan duration | All FYPs were 5 years; 12th FYP (2012–17) was the last | 13th FYP claimed to exist | Negative statement trap |
| Green Revolution | Mid-1960s (1966–67), M.S. Swaminathan; HYV seeds, Punjab–Haryana–western UP | Spread to all India claimed; father confused with Norman Borlaug (world) | Overgeneralisation |
| White Revolution (Operation Flood) | Verghese Kurien, 1970, Anand (Gujarat) — Amul | Confused with Green Revolution leaders | Similar-name trap |
| New Economic Policy (LPG) | 24 July 1991 — Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation; PM: P.V. Narasimha Rao, FM: Manmohan Singh | Year given as 1990 or 1992; Rao–Singh roles swapped | Year + role trap |
| Rupee devaluations | 1966, 1991 (twice in July 1991) | 1949 devaluation omitted or 1991 called single | Chronology confusion |
| RBI established | 1 April 1935, under RBI Act 1934; nationalised 1 January 1949 | Called nationalised in 1935; HQ confusion (Kolkata → Mumbai, 1937) | Year trap |
| RBI functions | Note issue (except ₹1 note/coin — issued by Ministry of Finance), banker to government, banker's bank, controller of credit | ₹1 note said to be issued by RBI | Function swap |
| First Governor of RBI | Sir Osborne Smith (Indian: C.D. Deshmukh) | Deshmukh called the first Governor | Person swap |
| Nationalisation of 14 banks | 19 July 1969 (Indira Gandhi govt); 6 more in 1980 | 14 and 6 swapped; year given as 1968 | Number swap |
| SBI formed | 1 July 1955 (from Imperial Bank of India, est. 1921) | Imperial Bank origin year confused | Chronology confusion |
| NABARD established | 12 July 1982 (Shivaraman Committee) | Confused with SIDBI (1990) or EXIM Bank (1982) | Institution swap |
| SEBI established | 1988; statutory status 1992 (SEBI Act) | Statutory year given as 1988 | Year trap |
| IRDAI established | 1999 (Malhotra Committee), HQ Hyderabad | HQ given as Mumbai | Location trap |
| Poverty line in India | Based on consumption expenditure; Tendulkar Committee (2009), Rangarajan Committee (2014) | Committees' years/recommendations swapped | Committee mismatch |
| First poverty estimation committee | V.M. Dandekar & N. Rath (1971) | Attributed to Planning Commission's later groups | Chronology confusion |
| Fiscal deficit formula | Total expenditure − Total receipts excluding borrowings | Borrowings included in receipts | Formula reversal |
| Primary deficit | Fiscal deficit − Interest payments | Interest added instead of subtracted | Formula reversal |
| Revenue deficit | Revenue expenditure − Revenue receipts | Confused with fiscal deficit | Concept swap |
| GST launched | 1 July 2017; 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016 | Amendment number given as 100th or 122nd | Number trap |
| GST Council | Article 279A; chaired by Union Finance Minister | Called a statutory (not constitutional) body | Body-type trap |
| GST slabs | 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% (+ cess on some goods) | 4% or 26% offered as slabs | Number trap |
| Goods outside GST | Alcohol for human consumption; petroleum products (crude, petrol, diesel, ATF, natural gas) — for now | Petrol said to be under GST | Negative statement trap |
| Direct vs Indirect tax | Direct: income tax, corporation tax; Indirect: GST, customs duty | Corporation tax called indirect | Classification swap |
| FRBM Act | 2003 — targets for fiscal & revenue deficit | Year given as 2000 or 2005 | Year trap |
| Demonetisation | ₹500 & ₹1000 notes withdrawn, 8 November 2016 | Earlier demonetisations (1946, 1978) ignored in "only time" statements | Negative statement trap |
| UPI launched | 2016, by NPCI (est. 2008) | NPCI confused with RBI | Institution swap |
| Jan Dhan Yojana | 28 August 2014 — financial inclusion, zero-balance accounts | Year given as 2015 | Year trap |
| MGNREGA | 2005 (Act), launched 2 Feb 2006; 100 days wage employment; renamed from NREGA in 2009 | 100 days given as 150; year as 2004 | Number + year trap |
| Pre-Tendulkar calorie norm (1979 Task Force, 1973–74 basis) | 2400 kcal rural / 2100 kcal urban per person per day | Rural–urban figures swapped | Number swap |
| HDI published by | UNDP (since 1990); three dimensions: health, education, standard of living | Credited to World Bank | Institution swap |
| WTO established | 1 January 1995 (replaced GATT, 1948); HQ Geneva | GATT called WTO's successor | Chronology confusion |
| IMF & World Bank | Both 1944 Bretton Woods; IMF HQ Washington DC; World Bank = IBRD + IDA | HQs swapped with Geneva | Location trap |
| India's currency system | Managed float; RBI issues under Minimum Reserve System (₹200 crore: ₹115 cr gold + ₹85 cr foreign securities) | Called fixed exchange rate | Concept swap |
| Inflation indices | WPI (base 2011–12, by DPIIT) and CPI (base 2012, by NSO); RBI targets CPI (4% ± 2%) | WPI said to be RBI's target | Index swap |
| Monetary Policy Committee | 6 members (3 RBI + 3 government nominees), RBI Governor chairs; formed 2016 | Called 5 or 7 members | Number trap |
| Repo vs Reverse repo | Repo: RBI lends to banks; Reverse repo: banks park funds with RBI | Definitions swapped | Concept swap |
| Stagflation | High inflation + high unemployment + stagnant growth | Confused with deflation or recession | Concept swap |
| Phillips curve | Inverse relation between inflation and unemployment | Stated as direct relation | Relation reversal |

## Part 3: Data & Formulas

| Item | Value / Formula |
|---|---|
| GDP at Market Price | GDP at Factor Cost + Indirect Taxes − Subsidies |
| GNP | GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) |
| NNP | GNP − Depreciation |
| National Income | NNP at Factor Cost |
| Personal Income | National Income − Corporate taxes − Undistributed profits − Social security contributions + Transfer payments |
| Disposable Income | Personal Income − Personal taxes |
| Fiscal Deficit | Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (excluding borrowings) |
| Primary Deficit | Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments |
| Revenue Deficit | Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts |
| Effective Revenue Deficit | Revenue Deficit − Grants for creation of capital assets |
| Base year — GDP series | 2011–12 |
| Base year — WPI | 2011–12 |
| Base year — CPI (combined) | 2012 |
| Base year — IIP | 2011–12 |
| CPI inflation target (RBI) | 4% ± 2% (i.e., 2%–6% band) |
| MPC composition | 6 members; 3 RBI + 3 external; Governor = ex-officio Chair |
| Minimum Reserve System | RBI must hold ₹200 crore (₹115 cr gold + ₹85 cr foreign securities) |
| MGNREGA wage employment | 100 days per rural household per year |
| Bank nationalisation | 14 banks (1969) + 6 banks (1980) = 20 |
| GST slabs | 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% |
| GST constitutional amendment | 101st Amendment Act, 2016 (Article 246A, 279A) |
| HDI dimensions | Life expectancy, education (mean + expected years of schooling), GNI per capita |
| Poverty calorie norm (1979 Task Force, 1973–74 basis) | 2400 kcal (rural), 2100 kcal (urban) per person per day |
| Repo/CRR/SLR rates | Change with RBI policy — check latest MPC resolution before exam; do not memorise stale values |
