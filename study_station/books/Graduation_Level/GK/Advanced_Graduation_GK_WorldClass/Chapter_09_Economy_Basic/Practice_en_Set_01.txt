Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which sector contributes the largest share to India's Gross Domestic Product (GDP)?
(a) Agriculture (b) Services (c) Industry (d) Mining
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mining is a tempting option because it is often discussed in news, but it is only a small part of industry. The services sector (tertiary sector) contributes more than half of India's GDP, so the answer is Services.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

2. Who is known as the father of the Green Revolution in India?
(a) Verghese Kurien (b) Norman Borlaug (c) M. S. Swaminathan (d) C. Subramaniam
Answer: (c)
Solution: Norman Borlaug is a trap — he is called the father of the Green Revolution worldwide, not specifically in India. M. S. Swaminathan led the Green Revolution in India, so the answer is M. S. Swaminathan.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

3. The Reserve Bank of India was nationalised in which year?
(a) 1935 (b) 1949 (c) 1947 (d) 1950
Answer: (b)
Solution: 1935 is a trap — that is the year the RBI was established (as a private shareholders' bank), not nationalised. Nationalisation happened on 1 January 1949, so the answer is 1949.
Source: RBI official website (rbi.org.in)

4. Which of the following is a direct tax?
(a) Goods and Services Tax (b) Customs duty (c) Income tax (d) Excise duty
Answer: (c)
Solution: GST, customs duty and excise duty are all indirect taxes because their burden can be shifted to others. Income tax is paid directly by the person on whom it is imposed, so the answer is Income tax.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

5. NITI Aayog replaced which institution in 2015?
(a) Finance Commission (b) Planning Commission (c) National Development Council (d) Inter-State Council
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Finance Commission is a trap — it is a constitutional body that still exists. NITI Aayog was formed on 1 January 2015 replacing the Planning Commission, so the answer is Planning Commission.
Source: NITI Aayog official website (niti.gov.in)

6. What does GDP stand for?
(a) General Domestic Price (b) Gross Domestic Product (c) Gross Development Plan (d) General Development Product
Answer: (b)
Solution: The other options are invented phrases that sound plausible. GDP is the total money value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders in a given period, so the answer is Gross Domestic Product.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Understanding Economic Development

7. Which bank is called the 'banker to the government' in India?
(a) State Bank of India (b) Punjab National Bank (c) Reserve Bank of India (d) NABARD
Answer: (c)
Solution: SBI is a trap because it is the largest public sector bank, but it is a commercial bank. The RBI acts as banker, agent and adviser to the central and state governments, so the answer is Reserve Bank of India.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Understanding Economic Development

8. The first Five-Year Plan of India was launched in which year?
(a) 1951 (b) 1950 (c) 1947 (d) 1956
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1950 is a trap — the Planning Commission was set up in March 1950, but the First Five-Year Plan itself ran from 1951 to 1956. So the answer is 1951.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

9. Which of the following is an example of the tertiary sector?
(a) Banking (b) Fishing (c) Manufacturing (d) Mining
Answer: (a)
Solution: Manufacturing is a trap — it belongs to the secondary sector, while fishing and mining are primary activities. Banking is a service, so the answer is Banking.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Understanding Economic Development

10. The Goods and Services Tax (GST) was implemented in India on which date?
(a) 1 July 2017 (b) 1 April 2016 (c) 15 August 2017 (d) 1 January 2018
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1 April 2016 is a trap — it sounds like a financial-year start, but GST actually came into force at midnight on 1 July 2017 after the 101st Constitutional Amendment. So the answer is 1 July 2017.
Source: GST Council / CBIC official website

11. Who presents the Union Budget in the Parliament of India?
(a) Prime Minister (b) Finance Minister (c) President (d) RBI Governor
Answer: (b)
Solution: The RBI Governor is a trap — he presents monetary policy, not the budget. Under Article 112 the Annual Financial Statement is laid before Parliament by the Finance Minister, so the answer is Finance Minister.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

12. Which of the following measures inflation at the wholesale level in India?
(a) Consumer Price Index (b) Index of Industrial Production (c) Wholesale Price Index (d) Human Development Index
Answer: (c)
Solution: CPI is a trap — it measures retail-level inflation faced by consumers. The Wholesale Price Index tracks price changes at the wholesale (bulk) level, so the answer is Wholesale Price Index.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

13. NABARD was established in which year?
(a) 1969 (b) 1975 (c) 1982 (d) 1991
Answer: (c)
Solution: 1982 is correct; 1975 is a trap because regional rural banks were set up that year. NABARD, the apex institution for agricultural and rural credit, was established on 12 July 1982, so the answer is 1982.
Source: NABARD official website (nabard.org)

14. How many banks were nationalised in India in 1969?
(a) 6 (b) 14 (c) 20 (d) 12
Answer: (b)
Solution: 20 is a trap — it is the total after the second round of nationalisation in 1980 (6 more banks). In July 1969, 14 major commercial banks were nationalised, so the answer is 14.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

15. The New Economic Policy of liberalisation, privatisation and globalisation was introduced in India in which year?
(a) 1991 (b) 1990 (c) 1985 (d) 1995
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1990 is a trap — the balance-of-payments crisis peaked then, but the reforms were announced in July 1991 under P. V. Narasimha Rao's government with Manmohan Singh as Finance Minister. So the answer is 1991.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

16. Which of the following is NOT a function of the Reserve Bank of India?
(a) Accepting deposits from the general public (b) Issuing currency notes (c) Controlling credit (d) Acting as lender of last resort
Answer: (a)
Solution: Issuing currency is a trap for those who think the government prints notes — RBI does issue notes (except the one-rupee note). RBI does not deal directly with the general public; accepting public deposits is the job of commercial banks, so the answer is Accepting deposits from the general public.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Understanding Economic Development

17. The headquarters of the Reserve Bank of India is located in which city?
(a) Mumbai (b) New Delhi (c) Kolkata (d) Chennai
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kolkata is a trap — the RBI was originally established in Kolkata in 1935 but its headquarters moved to Mumbai in 1937. So the answer is Mumbai.
Source: RBI official website (rbi.org.in)

18. Which sector is also known as the 'service sector'?
(a) Primary sector (b) Secondary sector (c) Quaternary sector (d) Tertiary sector
Answer: (d)
Solution: The secondary sector is a trap — it covers manufacturing and construction, not services. The tertiary sector provides services such as transport, banking and education, so the answer is Tertiary sector.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Understanding Economic Development

19. What is the full form of FDI?
(a) Foreign Debt Instrument (b) Foreign Deposit Insurance (c) Federal Development Index (d) Foreign Direct Investment
Answer: (d)
Solution: The other options are invented terms designed to confuse. FDI means investment made directly by a foreign entity into business or production in India, so the answer is Foreign Direct Investment.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

20. The Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act was passed in which year?
(a) 2000 (b) 2005 (c) 2003 (d) 2008
Answer: (c)
Solution: 2005 is a trap — the FRBM rules came into force in July 2004 and targets were revised later, but the Act itself was passed by Parliament in 2003. So the answer is 2003.
Source: Ministry of Finance official website

21. Consider the following statements about GDP and GNP:
1. GDP measures production within the geographical boundaries of a country.
2. GNP includes net factor income earned from abroad.
3. GNP of India is always greater than its GDP.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — it says GNP is "always" greater, but India's net factor income from abroad is generally negative, so India's GNP is usually slightly less than GDP. Statements 1 and 2 are correct definitions, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Introductory Macroeconomics

22. Consider the following statements about the Finance Commission:
1. It is a constitutional body under Article 280.
2. It is constituted every five years.
3. It recommends the distribution of tax revenues between the Union and the States.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is to confuse the Finance Commission with the Planning Commission, which was neither constitutional nor fixed at five-year intervals. All three statements correctly describe the Finance Commission, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Constitution at Work

23. Match the following revolutions with their related products:
1. White Revolution — P. Milk
2. Blue Revolution — Q. Fish
3. Yellow Revolution — R. Oilseeds
4. Golden Revolution — S. Horticulture/Honey
Which of the following is correctly matched?
(a) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-S, 4-R (b) 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R, 4-S (c) 1-P, 2-R, 3-Q, 4-S (d) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R, 4-S
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is swapping Blue and Yellow — Blue relates to fish (fisheries) and Yellow to oilseeds, not the reverse. All four pairings in the first option are correct, so the answer is 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R, 4-S.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development

24. Consider the following statements about the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC):
1. It was constituted in 2016.
2. It has six members, three from the RBI and three appointed by the Government.
3. The RBI Governor has a casting vote in case of a tie.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is to assume the committee has an odd number of members or that the Governor's vote is ordinary — in fact it has six members and the Governor's casting vote decides ties. All three statements are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: RBI official website (rbi.org.in)

25. Which of the following correctly arranges the events in chronological order?
1. Nationalisation of 14 banks
2. Establishment of NABARD
3. New Economic Policy (LPG reforms)
4. Implementation of GST
(a) 1, 2, 4, 3 (b) 2, 1, 3, 4 (c) 1, 3, 2, 4 (d) 1, 2, 3, 4
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is placing NABARD (1982) before bank nationalisation (1969) or GST (2017) before the 1991 reforms. The correct order is 1969 → 1982 → 1991 → 2017, so the answer is 1, 2, 3, 4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economic Development
