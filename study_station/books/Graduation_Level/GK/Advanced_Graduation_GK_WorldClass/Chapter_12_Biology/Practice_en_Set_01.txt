Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which is the largest gland in the human body?
(a) Liver (b) Pancreas (c) Thyroid (d) Pituitary
Answer: (a)
Solution: The pituitary is often picked because it is called the "master gland", but it is the size of a pea; "master" refers to its control over other glands, not its size. The liver, weighing about 1.5 kg, is the largest gland, so the answer is Liver.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

2. How many bones are there in an adult human skeleton?
(a) 206 (b) 208 (c) 300 (d) 186
Answer: (a)
Solution: Babies are born with about 300 bones, which tempts many to choose 300, but many of these fuse during growth. An adult skeleton has 206 bones, so the answer is 206.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

3. Which blood cells help in clotting of blood?
(a) Red blood cells (b) White blood cells (c) Plasma cells (d) Platelets
Answer: (d)
Solution: White blood cells are a common wrong pick because they defend the body, but defence against germs is not clotting. Platelets (thrombocytes) initiate clot formation, so the answer is Platelets.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

4. The normal human body temperature is approximately:
(a) 37°C (b) 35°C (c) 39°C (d) 40°C
Answer: (a)
Solution: 35°C is actually a sign of hypothermia, not normal temperature. The average normal body temperature is 37°C (98.6°F), so the answer is 37°C.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

5. Which organ produces insulin in the human body?
(a) Pancreas (b) Liver (c) Kidney (d) Spleen
Answer: (a)
Solution: The liver is tempting because it stores glycogen and regulates blood sugar, but it does not secrete insulin. Insulin is secreted by the beta cells of the islets of Langerhans in the pancreas, so the answer is Pancreas.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

6. Which is the smallest bone in the human body?
(a) Stapes (b) Femur (c) Tibia (d) Humerus
Answer: (a)
Solution: The femur is the longest bone, and some students confuse "longest" with "smallest" while hurrying. The stapes in the middle ear is the smallest bone, so the answer is Stapes.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

7. The functional unit of the kidney is:
(a) Neuron (b) Nephron (c) Alveolus (d) Villus
Answer: (b)
Solution: Neuron is a trap because it sounds similar to nephron, but a neuron is the unit of the nervous system. The nephron filters blood in the kidney, so the answer is Nephron.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

8. Which vitamin is synthesized in human skin on exposure to sunlight?
(a) Vitamin A (b) Vitamin C (c) Vitamin K (d) Vitamin D
Answer: (d)
Solution: Vitamin A is linked with eyes and carrots, which misleads some, but it comes from diet. Vitamin D is produced in the skin by sunlight, so the answer is Vitamin D.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

9. The exchange of gases in the lungs takes place in the:
(a) Bronchi (b) Bronchioles (c) Trachea (d) Alveoli
Answer: (d)
Solution: Bronchioles are tempting because they are tiny air passages, but they only conduct air. Actual gas exchange occurs across the thin walls of the alveoli, so the answer is Alveoli.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

10. Which blood group is called the universal donor?
(a) AB positive (b) O negative (c) A positive (d) B negative
Answer: (b)
Solution: AB positive is the classic trap — it is the universal recipient, not the donor. O negative blood can be given to any group, so the answer is O negative.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

11. The largest artery in the human body is:
(a) Aorta (b) Pulmonary artery (c) Carotid artery (d) Coronary artery
Answer: (a)
Solution: The pulmonary artery is often chosen because it is the only artery carrying deoxygenated blood, a famous fact, but it is not the largest. The aorta carries oxygenated blood from the left ventricle to the whole body, so the answer is Aorta.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

12. Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination of the body?
(a) Cerebrum (b) Cerebellum (c) Medulla oblongata (d) Hypothalamus
Answer: (b)
Solution: The cerebrum is tempting as the largest part of the brain, but it handles thinking and voluntary actions. The cerebellum maintains posture and balance, so the answer is Cerebellum.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

13. Deficiency of iron in the diet causes:
(a) Scurvy (b) Anaemia (c) Rickets (d) Goitre
Answer: (b)
Solution: Goitre is a trap because it is also a deficiency disease, but it is caused by iodine deficiency, not iron. Iron deficiency reduces haemoglobin and causes anaemia, so the answer is Anaemia.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

14. Which enzyme present in saliva begins the digestion of starch?
(a) Pepsin (b) Trypsin (c) Lipase (d) Amylase
Answer: (d)
Solution: Pepsin is a common wrong choice because it is a famous digestive enzyme, but it acts on proteins in the stomach. Salivary amylase (ptyalin) breaks down starch in the mouth, so the answer is Amylase.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

15. The normal number of chromosomes in a human body cell is:
(a) 23 (b) 44 (c) 46 (d) 48
Answer: (c)
Solution: 23 is the trap — that is the number of pairs, or the number in a gamete, not the total in a body cell. A human body cell has 46 chromosomes, so the answer is 46.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

16. Which organ is primarily responsible for filtering blood and removing urea?
(a) Liver (b) Kidney (c) Heart (d) Lungs
Answer: (b)
Solution: The liver is tempting because it detoxifies blood and produces urea, but it does not excrete it. The kidneys filter urea out of the blood into urine, so the answer is Kidney.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

17. Which hormone is known as the "fight or flight" hormone?
(a) Insulin (b) Thyroxine (c) Estrogen (d) Adrenaline
Answer: (d)
Solution: Thyroxine is a distractor because it controls metabolism and is secreted in the neck region, but it does not act in emergencies. Adrenaline from the adrenal medulla prepares the body for sudden action, so the answer is Adrenaline.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

18. The average resting heart rate of a healthy adult is about:
(a) 72 beats per minute (b) 40 beats per minute (c) 100 beats per minute (d) 120 beats per minute
Answer: (a)
Solution: 100 beats per minute is tempting because it is the upper limit of the normal range, but it is not the average. The average resting rate is about 72 beats per minute, so the answer is 72 beats per minute.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

19. Which of the following carries oxygen in the blood?
(a) Plasma (b) Platelets (c) Haemoglobin (d) Antibodies
Answer: (c)
Solution: Plasma is a trap because it carries many substances like nutrients and hormones, but oxygen is bound to a pigment, not dissolved in plasma. Haemoglobin in red blood cells carries oxygen, so the answer is Haemoglobin.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

20. The longest bone in the human body is:
(a) Femur (b) Humerus (c) Tibia (d) Fibula
Answer: (a)
Solution: The humerus is often picked because it is a long bone of the arm, but arm bones are shorter than the thigh bone. The femur (thigh bone) is the longest and strongest bone, so the answer is Femur.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

21. Consider the following statements about the human heart:
1. The right atrium receives oxygenated blood from the lungs.
2. The left ventricle pumps blood into the aorta.
3. The pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 1 is the examiner's trap — it sounds plausible, but the right atrium receives deoxygenated blood from the body; it is the left atrium that receives oxygenated blood from the lungs. Statements 2 and 3 are correct, so the answer is 2 and 3 only.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

22. Match the vitamins with their deficiency diseases:
1. Vitamin A — Night blindness
2. Vitamin C — Scurvy
3. Vitamin D — Rickets
4. Vitamin B1 — Beriberi
Which of the above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2, 3 and 4 (c) 2, 3 and 4 only (d) 1, 2 and 3 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap here is assuming at least one pair must be wrong, as is common in match questions, but all four pairs are standard and correct. Vitamin A deficiency causes night blindness, Vitamin C scurvy, Vitamin D rickets, and Vitamin B1 beriberi, so the answer is 1, 2, 3 and 4.
Source: NCERT Class 6 Science

23. Which of the following statements about blood pressure is correct?
(a) Normal blood pressure is about 120/80 mm Hg, where 120 is systolic pressure (b) Normal blood pressure is about 120/80 mm Hg, where 120 is diastolic pressure (c) Normal blood pressure is about 80/120 mm Hg (d) Blood pressure is measured with a thermometer
Answer: (a)
Solution: Option with 120 as diastolic is the trap — the two numbers are easily swapped; systolic (contraction) pressure is the higher value 120, and diastolic is 80. Blood pressure is measured with a sphygmomanometer, so the answer is "Normal blood pressure is about 120/80 mm Hg, where 120 is systolic pressure".
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

24. Arrange the following parts of the digestive tract in the correct order in which food passes through them:
1. Oesophagus
2. Stomach
3. Small intestine
4. Large intestine
(a) 1-3-2-4 (b) 2-1-3-4 (c) 1-2-3-4 (d) 2-1-4-3
Answer: (c)
Solution: The order 1-3-2-4 is a trap based on the confusion that food reaches the intestine directly after swallowing; food must pass through the stomach before entering the small intestine. The correct sequence is oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, so the answer is 1-2-3-4.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

25. Consider the following statements about the nervous system:
1. The medulla oblongata controls involuntary actions like heartbeat and breathing.
2. The spinal cord is protected by the vertebral column.
3. Reflex actions are controlled by the cerebrum.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — reflex actions feel instant and "automatic", tempting students to credit the brain, but simple reflexes are controlled by the spinal cord, not the cerebrum. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science
