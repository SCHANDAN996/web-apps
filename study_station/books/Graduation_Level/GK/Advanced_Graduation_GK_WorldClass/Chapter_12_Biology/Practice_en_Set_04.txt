Questions 76–100: Medium

76. Which enzyme present in saliva begins the digestion of starch?
(a) Ptyalin (b) Pepsin (c) Lipase (d) Trypsin
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pepsin acts in the stomach on proteins, not in the mouth — a common trap. Ptyalin (salivary amylase) in saliva starts starch digestion in the mouth, so the answer is Ptyalin.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

77. The functional unit of the kidney is:
(a) Neuron (b) Alveolus (c) Nephron (d) Glomerulus
Answer: (c)
Solution: Glomerulus is only a part of the filtering unit, not the whole unit — the tempting wrong choice. The complete structural and functional unit of the kidney is the nephron, so the answer is Nephron.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

78. Which blood vessel carries oxygenated blood from the lungs to the heart?
(a) Pulmonary artery (b) Pulmonary vein (c) Aorta (d) Vena cava
Answer: (b)
Solution: The pulmonary artery is a trap because arteries usually carry oxygenated blood, but it carries deoxygenated blood to the lungs. The pulmonary vein carries oxygenated blood from lungs to the left atrium, so the answer is Pulmonary vein.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

79. The normal resting heart rate of a healthy adult human is approximately:
(a) 32 beats/min (b) 72 beats/min (c) 120 beats/min (d) 180 beats/min
Answer: (b)
Solution: 120 beats/min is a trap as it resembles a systolic blood pressure value. The average resting heart rate is about 72 beats per minute, so the answer is 72 beats/min.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

80. Which hormone is secreted by the adrenal glands in response to stress?
(a) Adrenaline (b) Thyroxine (c) Insulin (d) Oestrogen
Answer: (a)
Solution: Thyroxine regulates metabolism and is a distractor here. Adrenaline (epinephrine) from the adrenal medulla prepares the body for "fight or flight" during stress, so the answer is Adrenaline.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

81. The exchange of gases in the lungs takes place in the:
(a) Bronchi (b) Bronchioles (c) Alveoli (d) Trachea
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bronchioles only conduct air and are a common confusion. Actual diffusion of oxygen and carbon dioxide occurs across the thin walls of the alveoli, so the answer is Alveoli.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

82. Which vitamin deficiency causes night blindness?
(a) Vitamin D (b) Vitamin C (c) Vitamin A (d) Vitamin B12
Answer: (c)
Solution: Vitamin C deficiency causes scurvy, a frequent mix-up. Night blindness (nyctalopia) results from deficiency of Vitamin A, needed for rhodopsin in the retina, so the answer is Vitamin A.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

83. The largest gland in the human body is:
(a) Liver (b) Thyroid (c) Pancreas (d) Pituitary
Answer: (a)
Solution: The thyroid is the largest endocrine gland, which tempts many candidates, but the question asks about the largest gland overall. The liver is the largest gland of the human body, so the answer is Liver.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

84. Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination of the body?
(a) Cerebrum (b) Cerebellum (c) Medulla oblongata (d) Hypothalamus
Answer: (b)
Solution: The medulla controls involuntary actions like heartbeat, a tempting distractor. The cerebellum maintains posture, balance and coordination, so the answer is Cerebellum.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

85. The universal blood donor group is:
(a) AB positive (b) B positive (c) AB negative (d) O negative
Answer: (d)
Solution: AB positive is the universal recipient, the classic trap in this question. O negative lacks A, B and Rh antigens, making it the universal donor, so the answer is O negative.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

86. Insulin is produced by which cells of the pancreas?
(a) Beta cells of Islets of Langerhans (b) Alpha cells (c) Delta cells (d) Acinar cells
Answer: (a)
Solution: Alpha cells secrete glucagon, which raises blood sugar — the opposite action and a common trap. Insulin is secreted by the beta cells of the Islets of Langerhans, so the answer is Beta cells of Islets of Langerhans.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

87. The average pH of human arterial blood is approximately:
(a) 6.4 (b) 7.4 (c) 7.0 (d) 8.2
Answer: (b)
Solution: 7.0 is tempting because it is "neutral", but blood is slightly alkaline. Normal arterial blood pH is about 7.4, so the answer is 7.4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

88. Which of the following is the master gland of the endocrine system?
(a) Pituitary (b) Adrenal (c) Thyroid (d) Pineal
Answer: (a)
Solution: The hypothalamus controls the pituitary, which confuses some, but among glands the pituitary regulates other endocrine glands through its tropic hormones. Hence the master gland is the Pituitary, so the answer is Pituitary.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

89. The oxygen-carrying pigment in human red blood cells is:
(a) Chlorophyll (b) Keratin (c) Myosin (d) Haemoglobin
Answer: (d)
Solution: Chlorophyll is the plant pigment, an easy distractor. Haemoglobin, an iron-containing protein in RBCs, binds and transports oxygen, so the answer is Haemoglobin.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

90. Which structure connects the embryo to the placenta?
(a) Amnion (b) Fallopian tube (c) Chorion (d) Umbilical cord
Answer: (d)
Solution: The amnion is a protective fluid-filled sac, a tempting confusion. The umbilical cord carries blood vessels linking the embryo to the placenta, so the answer is Umbilical cord.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

91. Bile, which helps in fat digestion, is stored in the:
(a) Liver (b) Gall bladder (c) Pancreas (d) Duodenum
Answer: (b)
Solution: The liver produces bile, which is the trap — production and storage are different. Bile is stored and concentrated in the gall bladder, so the answer is Gall bladder.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

92. Which of the following statements about human chromosomes is correct?
(a) Normal human cells have 23 chromosomes (b) Gametes have 46 chromosomes (c) Chromosome number varies daily (d) Normal somatic cells have 46 chromosomes
Answer: (d)
Solution: "23 chromosomes" is the trap — that is the number of pairs, not total chromosomes, in somatic cells. Normal human somatic cells contain 46 chromosomes (23 pairs), so the answer is Normal somatic cells have 46 chromosomes.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

93. The pacemaker of the human heart is the:
(a) AV node (b) Bundle of His (c) SA node (d) Purkinje fibres
Answer: (c)
Solution: The AV node is a trap because it also generates impulses, but only as a backup. The sino-atrial (SA) node initiates each heartbeat and is the natural pacemaker, so the answer is SA node.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

94. Which disease is caused by deficiency of iodine in the diet?
(a) Beriberi (b) Anaemia (c) Rickets (d) Goitre
Answer: (d)
Solution: Anaemia tempts many, but it is due to iron deficiency. Iodine deficiency enlarges the thyroid gland causing goitre, so the answer is Goitre.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

95. The site of fertilization in the human female reproductive system is usually the:
(a) Uterus (b) Fallopian tube (c) Ovary (d) Cervix
Answer: (b)
Solution: The uterus is the trap — it is the site of implantation, not fertilization. Fertilization normally occurs in the ampulla of the fallopian tube, so the answer is Fallopian tube.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Biology

96. Which cells of the blood are primarily responsible for immunity?
(a) Red blood cells (b) Platelets (c) White blood cells (d) Plasma cells of blood only
Answer: (c)
Solution: Platelets help in clotting, a common confusion. White blood cells (leucocytes) defend the body against pathogens and form the basis of immunity, so the answer is White blood cells.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

97. The longest bone in the human body is the:
(a) Tibia (b) Fibula (c) Humerus (d) Femur
Answer: (d)
Solution: The humerus is the longest bone of the arm, which misleads some candidates. The femur (thigh bone) is the longest and strongest bone in the body, so the answer is Femur.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

98. Which of the following correctly gives the order of structures through which light passes to reach the retina?
(a) Lens → Cornea → Pupil → Retina (b) Pupil → Cornea → Retina → Lens (c) Cornea → Pupil → Lens → Retina (d) Retina → Lens → Pupil → Cornea
Answer: (c)
Solution: Placing the lens before the cornea is the chronological trap in the first option. Light enters through the cornea, passes the pupil, is focused by the lens, and falls on the retina, so the answer is Cornea → Pupil → Lens → Retina.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Science

99. Which hormone regulates the calcium level in the blood?
(a) Parathyroid hormone (b) Insulin (c) Adrenaline (d) Progesterone
Answer: (a)
Solution: Calcitonin also affects calcium, but the chief regulator raising blood calcium is from the parathyroid glands. Parathyroid hormone (PTH) maintains blood calcium levels, so the answer is Parathyroid hormone.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

100. The total number of bones in an adult human skeleton is:
(a) 206 (b) 300 (c) 186 (d) 226
Answer: (a)
Solution: 300 is the trap — infants have about 300 bones, many of which fuse later. An adult human skeleton has 206 bones, so the answer is 206.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology
