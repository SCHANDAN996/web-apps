126. Which of the following statements about the human heart is correct?
(a) The bundle of His conducts impulses from the AV node to the ventricles (b) The sinoatrial node is located in the wall of the left atrium (c) The mitral valve guards the opening between the right atrium and right ventricle (d) Deoxygenated blood enters the left atrium through the pulmonary veins
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is the first one — the SA node is in the wall of the RIGHT atrium, not the left, a classic almost-correct trap. The mitral (bicuspid) valve is on the left side, and pulmonary veins bring oxygenated blood to the left atrium. The bundle of His does carry impulses from the AV node down to the ventricles, so the answer is the bundle of His conducts impulses from the AV node to the ventricles.

127. The oxygen-haemoglobin dissociation curve shifts to the right when:
(a) pH increases and CO₂ decreases (b) temperature decreases (c) CO₂ concentration and temperature increase (Bohr effect) (d) 2,3-BPG concentration decreases
Answer: (c)
Solution: A right shift means haemoglobin releases oxygen more easily. Rising CO₂, falling pH, rising temperature and rising 2,3-BPG all cause a right shift — the opposite conditions shift it left. The Bohr effect describes exactly this CO₂/pH influence, so the answer is CO₂ concentration and temperature increase (Bohr effect).
