# Key Facts & Figures — Biology: Human Body

**Score Maximizer subjects (attempt first, high accuracy):** superlative facts (largest/smallest), vitamin–disease pairs, hormone–gland pairs, blood facts, cell organelles. These are pure recall and take under 15 seconds each.
