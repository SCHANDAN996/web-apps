# 🧒 The Feynman Corner — Biology: Human Body

## The Most Confusing Concept: How Does Blood Actually Flow Through the Heart?

Ask any exam aspirant to trace blood through the heart, and most will mix up which side carries oxygen-rich blood and which carries oxygen-poor blood. Let's fix this forever — the way Richard Feynman would: so simply that a 12-year-old could teach it back.

---

## Think of Your Heart as a Double Water Pump in a Housing Colony

Imagine a housing colony with **two pumps sitting side by side** inside one machine room. They never mix their water.

**Pump 1 (Right side of the heart) — the "Dirty Water" pump:**
- All the used, dirty water (oxygen-poor blood) from every house in the colony (your body) flows back into a collection tank — the **right atrium**.
- It drops through a one-way door (the **tricuspid valve**) into a stronger chamber — the **right ventricle**.
- The right ventricle squeezes and sends this dirty water through a pipe (the **pulmonary artery**) to the **water-cleaning plant — your lungs**.
- In the lungs, the water is cleaned: carbon dioxide is thrown out, oxygen is loaded in.

**Pump 2 (Left side of the heart) — the "Clean Water" pump:**
- Fresh, clean water (oxygen-rich blood) returns from the lungs through pipes (the **pulmonary veins**) into the **left atrium**.
- It drops through another one-way door (the **mitral/bicuspid valve**) into the **left ventricle** — the strongest, thickest-walled chamber of the heart.
- The left ventricle squeezes hard and pushes clean water into the colony's main supply line — the **aorta** — which delivers it to every house (every cell of your body).

**Why is the left ventricle wall the thickest?** Because Pump 2 must push water to the *entire colony* — even the farthest house (your toes) — while Pump 1 only sends it next door to the lungs. More distance = more muscle.

---

## The One Rule That Solves Every Exam Question

> **"Arteries carry blood AWAY from the heart. Veins carry blood TOWARDS the heart."**

Notice — the rule says nothing about oxygen! That's the trap. The **pulmonary artery** carries oxygen-*poor* blood (heart → lungs), and the **pulmonary vein** carries oxygen-*rich* blood (lungs → heart). These two are the only exceptions to "artery = pure, vein = impure," and examiners love testing exactly this.

---

## The Unforgettable Trick: "RATS before CATS, and LORD"

- **RATS before CATS** — the dirty-water route: **R**ight **A**trium → **T**ricuspid valve → right ventricle → lungs. Clean route: **C**ollection in **L**eft **A**trium → **M**itral valve → left ventricle → **A**orta.
- **LORD** — **L**eft side = **O**xygen-**R**ich, **D**elivered to body. Whatever is LEFT is pure and goes out to the body. Whatever is RIGHT is impure and goes to the lungs.

Say it once: *"Right is Rubbish (goes to lungs), Left is Lovely (goes to body)."*

---

## Quick Self-Check Path (Trace It With Your Finger)

Body → Vena cava → Right atrium → Tricuspid valve → Right ventricle → Pulmonary artery → **LUNGS** → Pulmonary veins → Left atrium → Mitral valve → Left ventricle → Aorta → Body.

One full loop = **double circulation** — blood passes through the heart **twice** in one complete round of the body. That's why humans are said to have double circulation.
