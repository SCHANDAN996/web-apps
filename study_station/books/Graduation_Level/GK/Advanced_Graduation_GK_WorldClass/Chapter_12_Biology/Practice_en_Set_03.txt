51. Which enzyme present in saliva begins the digestion of starch?
(a) Ptyalin (b) Pepsin (c) Lipase (d) Trypsin
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pepsin is the tempting wrong choice, but it acts on proteins and is found in the stomach, not in saliva. Salivary amylase, also called ptyalin, converts starch into maltose in the mouth, so the answer is Ptyalin.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

52. The functional unit of the kidney is:
(a) Neuron (b) Alveolus (c) Nephron (d) Villus
Answer: (c)
Solution: Neuron is a trap because of its similar-sounding name, but it is the structural unit of the nervous system. Each kidney contains about a million nephrons that filter blood and form urine, so the answer is Nephron.

53. Which blood group is called the universal donor?
(a) O negative (b) AB positive (c) AB negative (d) B positive
Answer: (a)
Solution: AB positive is the classic trap — it is the universal recipient, not the donor. O negative blood has neither A/B antigens nor the Rh antigen, so it can be given to anyone, so the answer is O negative.

54. The exchange of gases in the lungs takes place in the:
(a) Bronchi (b) Trachea (c) Alveoli (d) Bronchioles
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bronchioles are a tempting option because they are the finest air passages, but they only conduct air. The thin-walled air sacs called alveoli, surrounded by capillaries, are the actual site of gas exchange, so the answer is Alveoli.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Biology

55. Which hormone regulates blood calcium levels by increasing them?
(a) Calcitonin (b) Parathyroid hormone (c) Insulin (d) Thyroxine
Answer: (b)
Solution: Calcitonin is the trap here — it is also a calcium-related hormone but it lowers blood calcium, the opposite action. Parathyroid hormone (PTH) raises blood calcium by acting on bones, kidneys and intestine, so the answer is Parathyroid hormone.

56. The largest gland in the human body is:
(a) Pancreas (b) Pituitary (c) Thyroid (d) Liver
Answer: (d)
Solution: Thyroid is a trap because it is the largest purely endocrine gland, but the question asks about the largest gland overall. The liver, weighing about 1.5 kg, is the largest gland of the body, so the answer is Liver.

57. Which part of the brain controls balance and coordination of the body?
(a) Cerebrum (b) Cerebellum (c) Medulla oblongata (d) Hypothalamus
Answer: (b)
Solution: Medulla oblongata is the tempting wrong answer, but it controls involuntary actions like heartbeat and breathing, not balance. The cerebellum coordinates muscular movement and maintains posture and balance, so the answer is Cerebellum.

58. The normal resting heart rate of a healthy adult human is approximately:
(a) 40–50 beats per minute (b) 140–160 beats per minute (c) 110–130 beats per minute (d) 60–100 beats per minute
Answer: (d)
Solution: The 40–50 range is a trap because trained athletes can have such low rates, but it is not the general adult norm. A healthy adult at rest has a heart rate of about 60–100 beats per minute (average 72), so the answer is 60–100 beats per minute.

59. Which vitamin deficiency causes night blindness?
(a) Vitamin C (b) Vitamin A (c) Vitamin D (d) Vitamin B12
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vitamin D is a trap because it is also a fat-soluble vitamin, but its deficiency causes rickets, not eye disease. Vitamin A is needed to form rhodopsin, the visual pigment of rod cells, so the answer is Vitamin A.

60. Bile, which helps in fat digestion, is stored in the:
(a) Liver (b) Duodenum (c) Pancreas (d) Gall bladder
Answer: (d)
Solution: Liver is the classic trap — bile is produced in the liver, but the question asks where it is stored. Bile is stored and concentrated in the gall bladder and released into the duodenum, so the answer is Gall bladder.

61. Which of the following carries oxygenated blood from the lungs to the heart?
(a) Pulmonary vein (b) Pulmonary artery (c) Aorta (d) Vena cava
Answer: (a)
Solution: Pulmonary artery is the trap because of the word "pulmonary", but it carries deoxygenated blood from the heart to the lungs — an exception among arteries. The pulmonary vein returns oxygenated blood from the lungs to the left atrium, so the answer is Pulmonary vein.

62. The master gland of the human endocrine system is the:
(a) Thyroid (b) Pituitary gland (c) Adrenal gland (d) Pineal gland
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hypothalamus would be a conceptual trap (it controls the pituitary), but among the given options the pituitary is correct because it secretes hormones that regulate other endocrine glands. Hence the pituitary is called the master gland, so the answer is Pituitary gland.

63. Which blood cells are primarily responsible for fighting infections?
(a) Red blood cells (b) Platelets (c) White blood cells (d) Plasma cells
Answer: (c)
Solution: Platelets are a trap because they are also formed elements of blood, but their role is clotting, not defence. White blood cells (leucocytes) produce antibodies and engulf pathogens, so the answer is White blood cells.

64. The protein digesting enzyme pepsin works best in:
(a) Acidic medium (b) Neutral medium (c) Alkaline medium (d) Absence of water

Solution: Alkaline medium is the trap because intestinal enzymes like trypsin work in alkaline conditions, creating confusion. Pepsin is secreted in the stomach where hydrochloric acid provides the acidic medium it needs, so the answer is Acidic medium.

(a) 35°C (b) 37°C (c) 39°C (d) 41°C

67. Which part of the nephron is mainly responsible for ultrafiltration of blood?
(a) Loop of Henle (b) Distal convoluted tubule (c) Collecting duct (d) Glomerulus
Answer: (d)
Solution: Loop of Henle is the trap because it is vital for concentrating urine, but it handles reabsorption, not filtration. The glomerulus, a capillary tuft inside Bowman's capsule, filters blood under pressure, so the answer is Glomerulus.

68. Insulin is secreted by which cells of the pancreas?
(a) Beta cells (b) Alpha cells (c) Delta cells (d) Acinar cells
Answer: (a)
Solution: Alpha cells are the trap — they belong to the same islets of Langerhans but secrete glucagon, which raises blood sugar, the opposite of insulin. Beta cells of the islets of Langerhans secrete insulin, so the answer is Beta cells.

69. The smallest bone in the human body is the:
(a) Femur (b) Incus (c) Stapes (d) Malleus
Answer: (c)
Solution: Incus and malleus are traps because they are also tiny ear ossicles, but both are larger than the stapes. The stapes (stirrup bone) of the middle ear, about 3 mm long, is the smallest bone, so the answer is Stapes.

70. Which of the following is the correct sequence of the passage of food through the digestive tract?
(a) Mouth → Oesophagus → Stomach → Small intestine → Large intestine (b) Mouth → Stomach → Oesophagus → Small intestine → Large intestine (c) Mouth → Oesophagus → Small intestine → Stomach → Large intestine (d) Mouth → Stomach → Small intestine → Oesophagus → Large intestine
Answer: (a)
Solution: Option with stomach before oesophagus is the chronological trap — food cannot reach the stomach without first passing through the oesophagus. The correct order is mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, then large intestine, so the answer is the sequence in option (a).

71. Which component of blood is responsible for clotting?
(a) Plasma (b) Lymphocytes (c) Red blood cells (d) Platelets
Answer: (d)
Solution: Plasma is a trap because it contains clotting proteins like fibrinogen, but the cellular component that initiates clot formation is different. Platelets (thrombocytes) aggregate at injury sites and trigger the clotting cascade, so the answer is Platelets.

(a) Cornea (b) Retina (c) Iris (d) Lens
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lens is the tempting wrong answer because it focuses light, but focusing is different from regulating the amount of light. The iris adjusts the size of the pupil to control light entry, so the answer is Iris.

74. Which statement about arteries is correct?
(a) All arteries carry oxygenated blood (b) Arteries carry blood away from the heart (c) Arteries have valves to prevent backflow (d) Arteries have thinner walls than veins
Answer: (b)
Solution: "All arteries carry oxygenated blood" is the classic almost-correct trap — the pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood. The defining feature of arteries is that they carry blood away from the heart, so the answer is "Arteries carry blood away from the heart".

75. The hormone adrenaline is secreted by the:
(a) Adrenal cortex (b) Anterior pituitary (c) Adrenal medulla (d) Thyroid gland
Answer: (c)
Solution: Adrenal cortex is the trap because it is part of the same gland, but it secretes corticoids like cortisol, not adrenaline. Adrenaline (epinephrine), the emergency hormone, is secreted by the adrenal medulla, so the answer is Adrenal medulla.
