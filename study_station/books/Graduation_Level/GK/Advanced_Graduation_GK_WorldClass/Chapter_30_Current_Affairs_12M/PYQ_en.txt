# PYQ Analysis — Current Affairs (October 2025 – October 2026)

*(How SSC CGL/CHSL, Banking (IBPS/SBI PO & Clerk), RRB NTPC, CDS/NDA, CAPF and State PCS exams ask current affairs. We describe the pattern of questions; we do not quote invented counts.)*

## 1. How exams ask current affairs

- **One-liners:** "Who won…", "Where was … held", "Who has been appointed…".
- **Number questions:** medal tallies, ranks, percentages, counts (Ramsar sites, Padma awards).
- **Firsts and records:** "first Indian to…", "record … time".
- **Pair-matching:** event ↔ host city, award ↔ winner, exercise ↔ partner country.

## 2. Themes examiners return to

- Awards and honours, appointments, sports events, defence tests and exercises, space missions, economy data, environment and heritage, government schemes, international summits.

## 3. Ten high-yield questions (PYQ-style, with answers)

**Q1.** Dhanush Srikanth set a world record while winning air rifle gold at which event in November 2025?
**Answer:** Deaflympics 2025, Tokyo
**Why:** Dhanush Srikanth won the air rifle gold with a world record 252.2 at the 2025 Deaflympics in Tokyo.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q2.** Which country won its fourth Davis Cup title in November 2025?
**Answer:** Italy
**Why:** Italy beat Spain 2-0 for a third straight title, its fourth overall.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3.** The Golden Peacock for Best Feature Film at the 56th IFFI (Goa, November 2025) went to a film from which country?
**Answer:** Vietnam
**Why:** The 56th IFFI concluded in Goa, with the Golden Peacock awarded to a Vietnamese film.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q4.** Suruchi Singh won gold with a junior world record at the ISSF World Cup Final 2025, held in which city?
**Answer:** Doha
**Why:** Suruchi Singh won the women's 10 m air pistol gold at the season-ending ISSF World Cup Final in Doha.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q5.** With Deepavali's inscription in December 2025, how many Indian elements are on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list?
**Answer:** 16
**Why:** Deepavali became the 16th Indian element on the list.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6.** Who took charge as the youngest National Working President of the BJP in December 2025?
**Answer:** Nitin Nabin
**Why:** Nitin Nabin, MLA from Bankipur, Bihar, became the youngest BJP National Working President and the first from Bihar.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7.** In December 2025 India was re-elected to the Council of which international organisation 'with one of the highest mandates'?
**Answer:** ICAO
**Why:** Civil Aviation Minister Ram Mohan Naidu said India was re-elected to the International Civil Aviation Organisation Council.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8.** What was flight-tested by DRDO in January 2026 with 'top attack' capability against a moving target?
**Answer:** Third-generation Man Portable Anti-Tank Guided Missile (MPATGM)
**Why:** DRDO flight-tested the third-generation fire-and-forget MPATGM at the KK Ranges, Ahilya Nagar, Maharashtra.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9.** Who was selected for the Subhas Chandra Bose National Award for Disaster Management 2026 (individual category)?
**Answer:** Lt Col Sita Ashok Shelke
**Why:** Lt Col Sita Ashok Shelke was selected for her outstanding work in disaster management.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q10.** How many Padma Vibhushan awards were announced for 2026?
**Answer:** 5
**Why:** The 131 awards comprised 5 Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan and 113 Padma Shri.
**Source:** PYQ-style

## 4. 15-second / 45-second rule

- Name/place questions: 15 seconds — you know it or you skip it.
- Number questions: up to 45 seconds — eliminate outdated figures first.
- Never spend more than 45 seconds on a single current-affairs question.
