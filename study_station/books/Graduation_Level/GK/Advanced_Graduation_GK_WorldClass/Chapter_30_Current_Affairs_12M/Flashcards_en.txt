# Flashcards — Current Affairs (October 2025 – October 2026)

Card 1
Front: Who was sworn in as the 50th Chief Justice of the Bombay High Court in September 2026?
Back: Justice Mahesh Chandra Tripathi — Maharashtra Governor Jishnu Dev Varma administered the oath to Justice Mahesh Chandra Tripathi, the 50th Chief Justice of the Bombay High Court.

Card 2
Front: Where was the 37th International Biology Olympiad 2026 held, at which India won one gold and three silver medals?
Back: Vilnius, Lithuania — India won 1 gold (Bhavyaa Gunwal) and 3 silver medals at IBO 2026 in Vilnius.

Card 3
Front: Indian space start-up Skyroot Aerospace signed a launch contract in October 2026 with which US start-up that plans to reflect sunlight at night?
Back: Reflect Orbital — California-based Reflect Orbital signed with Skyroot Aerospace for two launches of orbital mirrors to provide sunlight at night.

Card 4
Front: What was flight-tested by DRDO in January 2026 with 'top attack' capability against a moving target?
Back: Third-generation Man Portable Anti-Tank Guided Missile (MPATGM) — DRDO flight-tested the third-generation fire-and-forget MPATGM at the KK Ranges, Ahilya Nagar, Maharashtra.

Card 5
Front: Which country won its fourth Davis Cup title in November 2025?
Back: Italy — Italy beat Spain 2-0 for a third straight title, its fourth overall.

Card 6
Front: Who took charge as the youngest National Working President of the BJP in December 2025?
Back: Nitin Nabin — Nitin Nabin, MLA from Bankipur, Bihar, became the youngest BJP National Working President and the first from Bihar.

Card 7
Front: What was India's position in the overall medal table of the Asian Games 2026?
Back: Fourth — India finished fourth with 85 medals, including 21 gold.

Card 8
Front: Which entity was chosen for the Rashtriya Khel Protsahan Puruskar in the National Sports Awards 2025?
Back: Army Paralympic Node, Pune — Army Paralympic Node, Pune was named for the Rashtriya Khel Protsahan Puruskar, given for promoting sports.

Card 9
Front: BrahMos Aerospace's revenue for 2025-26 crossed what amount?
Back: ₹5,200 crore — DRDO said BrahMos Aerospace revenue crossed ₹5,200 crore; its new Lucknow facility rolled out its first missiles.

Card 10
Front: The Pinaka Long Range Guided Rocket was flight-tested in July 2026 for a user-defined minimum range of how many kilometres?
Back: 60 km — DRDO tested the Pinaka LRGR at the Integrated Test Range, Chandipur, for a minimum range of 60 km.

Card 11
Front: Angh, which won the Platform Prize 2026 at the Toronto International Film Festival, was directed by a filmmaker from which state?
Back: Nagaland — Angh, directed by Naga filmmaker Theja Rio, won TIFF's Platform Prize 2026 — a historic first.

Card 12
Front: What was RBI's Financial Inclusion Index (FI-Index) for March 2026?
Back: 70.0 — The FI-Index rose to 70.0 in March 2026 from 67.0 in March 2025.

Card 13
Front: Which two chemists won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2026?
Back: Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai — French chemist Henri B. Kagan and Japanese chemist Kenso Soai won for non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis.

Card 14
Front: With Deepavali's inscription in December 2025, how many Indian elements are on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list?
Back: 16 — Deepavali became the 16th Indian element on the list.

Card 15
Front: The nationwide Annual Health Check-Up initiative launched in May 2026 under the new Labour Codes covers workers of what age?
Back: 40 years and above — Labour Minister Dr Mansukh Mandaviya launched annual health check-ups for workers aged 40 and above.

Card 16
Front: Who was India's flag-bearer at the closing ceremony of the Asian Games 2026?
Back: Neeru Dhanda — Shooter Neeru Dhanda, who won two gold medals and a silver at the Games, carried the Indian flag at the closing ceremony.

Card 17
Front: The Common Landing Portal launched in May 2026 by the Department of Financial Services helps people search for what?
Back: Unclaimed financial assets — The portal, developed with the Public Sector Bank Alliance, gives easier access to information on unclaimed financial assets.

Card 18
Front: Who was elected President of Hungary by its parliament in August 2026?
Back: Andras Baka — Former Supreme Court chief Andras Baka was elected to the largely ceremonial post.

Card 19
Front: ICGS Achal, commissioned in May 2026, belongs to which series of Fast Patrol Vessels?
Back: Adamya class — ICGS Achal is the latest of the new-generation Adamya-class Fast Patrol Vessels, built at Goa Shipyard.

Card 20
Front: Who was sworn in as the elected President of Myanmar in April 2026?
Back: Min Aung Hlaing — Min Aung Hlaing, who took power in 2021, was sworn in as President after a widely criticised election.
