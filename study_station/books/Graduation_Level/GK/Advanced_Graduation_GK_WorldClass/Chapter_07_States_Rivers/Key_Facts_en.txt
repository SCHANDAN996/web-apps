## 1. High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap |
|---|---|---|
| Longest river of India | Ganga (about 2,525 km) | Do not confuse with the Indus or Brahmaputra, which are longer in total length but flow mostly outside India |
| Longest river entirely within India | Godavari (about 1,465 km) | Ganga is longest overall; Godavari is the longest peninsular river |
| "Sorrow of Bihar" | Kosi River | Kosi is a river's name; "Sorrow of Bihar" is its nickname due to frequent floods and course changes |
| "Sorrow of Bengal" | Damodar River | Do not swap with Kosi — Damodar = Bengal, Kosi = Bihar |
| "Sorrow of Assam" | Brahmaputra River | Floods in Assam are linked to the Brahmaputra, not the Ganga |
| Longest river of the world | Nile (Africa) | Amazon carries the most water, but the Nile is longest — examiners swap these two |
| Largest river by water discharge | Amazon (South America) | "Largest" by volume ≠ "longest" |
| Longest river of the U.S. | Mississippi–Missouri river system | Mississippi alone is shorter than the combined Mississippi–Missouri system |
| Longest river of Europe | Volga (Russia) | Danube is the longest in the European Union, not in all of Europe |
| Longest river of Asia | Yangtze (China) | Do not confuse with the Yellow River (Huang He), also in China |
| Ganga's origin | Gangotri Glacier (as Bhagirathi), Uttarakhand | The name "Ganga" applies only after Devprayag, where Bhagirathi meets Alaknanda |
| Yamuna's origin | Yamunotri Glacier, Uttarakhand | Yamuna is the longest tributary of the Ganga |
| Brahmaputra's origin | Near Lake Mansarovar, Tibet (as Tsangpo) | Enters India through Arunachal Pradesh; called Jamuna in Bangladesh |
| Indus's origin | Near Lake Mansarovar, Tibet | Flows mainly through Pakistan; India, Pakistan share it under the Indus Waters Treaty (1960) |
| Triveni Sangam | Prayagraj — confluence of Ganga, Yamuna and (mythical) Saraswati | Saraswati here is mythical, not a visible river |
| Only major west-flowing rivers of peninsular India | Narmada and Tapi (Tapti) | Most peninsular rivers flow east into the Bay of Bengal; these two flow into the Arabian Sea |
| Narmada flows through | A rift valley between Vindhya and Satpura ranges | Narmada does not form a delta — it forms an estuary |
| Largest river island in India | Majuli (in the Brahmaputra, Assam) | Majuli is a river island, not a sea island |
| "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South) | Godavari | Nickname belongs to Godavari, not Kaveri |
| Kaveri's origin | Talakaveri, Brahmagiri Hills, Karnataka | Kaveri water dispute is between Karnataka and Tamil Nadu |
| Chambal's characteristic | Known for ravines (badland topography) | Chambal is a tributary of the Yamuna, not of the Ganga directly |
| Bhakra Nangal Dam | On the Sutlej River (Himachal Pradesh/Punjab) | Do not confuse with Hirakud, which is on the Mahanadi (Odisha) |
| Hirakud Dam | On the Mahanadi, Odisha — one of the longest dams in the world | Longest dam ≠ highest dam; Tehri (Bhagirathi) is among the highest in India |
| City on the banks of the Ganga | Varanasi, Kanpur, Patna, Haridwar | Kolkata is on the Hooghly (a distributary of the Ganga), not the main Ganga stream |
| Delhi is situated on | Yamuna | Agra and Mathura are also on the Yamuna |
