76. Which river joins the Ganga near Chhapra in Bihar?
(a) Ghaghara (b) Son (c) Punpun (d) Phalgu
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Son joins the Ganga near Patna, while the Punpun and Phalgu are smaller south-bank tributaries joining further downstream, so they are traps. The Ghaghara (Sarayu), the largest tributary of the Ganga by volume, joins it near Chhapra, so the answer is Ghaghara.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

77. The river Kosi is notorious for frequently changing its course because it
(a) flows through a deep rocky gorge (b) carries a heavy silt load onto a flat alluvial plain (c) is a rain-fed river with no glaciers (d) flows through a rift valley
Answer: (b)
Solution: A deep gorge or rift valley would confine a river, not let it shift, so those options are traps. The Kosi brings enormous sediment from the Nepal Himalaya and deposits it on the flat plains of north Bihar, raising its own bed and spilling into new channels — hence it is called the "Sorrow of Bihar", so the answer is that it carries a heavy silt load onto a flat alluvial plain.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

78. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Betwa (b) Chambal (c) Brahmaputra (d) Ken
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chambal (Janapav hills, MP), Betwa (Vindhyas, MP) and Ken (Kaimur range) all rise within India, so they are tempting but wrong. The Brahmaputra rises as the Tsangpo from the Chemayungdung glacier near Lake Mansarovar in Tibet, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

79. The Tungabhadra river is formed by the confluence of which two streams?
(a) Tunga and Kabini (b) Tunga and Koyna (c) Tunga and Bhadra (d) Bhadra and Hemavati
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Koyna, Hemavati and Kabini are unrelated tributaries of the Krishna and Kaveri systems and are included as distractors. The Tunga and Bhadra rise in the Western Ghats of Karnataka and unite at Koodli to form the Tungabhadra, a major tributary of the Krishna, so the answer is Tunga and Bhadra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

80. Which river flows through the Union Territory of Delhi?
(a) Hindon (b) Sahibi (c) Chambal (d) Yamuna
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Hindon and Sahibi are small seasonal streams of the NCR region and the Chambal never approaches Delhi, so they are traps. The Yamuna flows along the eastern edge of Delhi and is the city's principal river, so the answer is Yamuna.

81. The largest river island in India, Majuli, lies in which river?
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Ganga (c) Godavari (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ganga, Godavari and Mahanadi have deltas and sandbars but no island comparable to Majuli, so they are distractors. Majuli in Assam, formed by the Brahmaputra and its channels, is recognised as the world's largest river island, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

82. Which of the following is a west-flowing river of peninsular India?
(a) Pennar (b) Tapi (c) Vamsadhara (d) Nagavali
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Pennar, Nagavali and Vamsadhara all drain eastward into the Bay of Bengal, which makes them attractive but wrong choices. The Tapi (Tapti) flows west through a rift valley between the Satpura and Ajanta ranges into the Gulf of Khambhat, so the answer is Tapi.

83. Which river flows through a rift valley along with the Narmada and Tapi?
(a) Luni (b) Mahi (c) Sabarmati (d) Damodar
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Mahi, Sabarmati and Luni flow west but not through true rift valleys, so they mislead. The Damodar flows through a fault-guided rift valley in Jharkhand–West Bengal, like the Narmada and Tapi systems, so the answer is Damodar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

84. Which river is known as the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Kaveri (b) Godavari (c) Krishna (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Kaveri is sacred but much shorter, and the Krishna and Mahanadi are smaller in basin area, so they are traps. The Godavari, the longest peninsular river with the largest peninsular basin, is called the Dakshin Ganga, so the answer is Godavari.

85. The Chambal river originates from
(a) Aravalli hills (b) Amarkantak plateau (c) Mahabaleshwar (d) Janapav hills near Mhow
Answer: (d)
Solution: Amarkantak gives rise to the Narmada and Son, and Mahabaleshwar to the Krishna, so those options are origin confusions. The Chambal rises in the Janapav hills of the Vindhyas near Mhow in Madhya Pradesh, so the answer is Janapav hills near Mhow.

86. Which of the following rivers joins the Ganga from the south (right bank)?
(a) Gandak (b) Kosi (c) Son (d) Ghaghara
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Gandak, Kosi and Ghaghara are all Himalayan rivers joining the Ganga from the north (left bank), which is the classic trap. The Son rises at Amarkantak and joins the Ganga from the south near Patna, so the answer is Son.

87. The Indus river enters India (Ladakh) after flowing in which general direction in Tibet?
(a) South-east (b) Due east (c) Due south (d) North-west
Answer: (d)
Solution: Flowing due south or east would take the Indus into the Brahmaputra's course, so those options are wrong. The Indus rises near Lake Mansarovar and flows north-west between the Ladakh and Zanskar ranges before entering Ladakh, so the answer is north-west.

88. Which river forms the famous Dhuandhar (Marble Rocks) waterfall near Jabalpur?
(a) Son (b) Tapi (c) Narmada (d) Betwa
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Son, Tapi and Betwa have no such marble gorge, so they are distractors. The Narmada plunges through marble rocks at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur, forming the Dhuandhar falls, so the answer is Narmada.

(a) Sutlej (b) Beas (c) Ravi (d) Chenab

90. Which of the following rivers is associated with the city of Nashik?
(a) Krishna (b) Bhima (c) Tapi (d) Godavari
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Krishna rises at Mahabaleshwar and the Bhima at Bhimashankar, so neither touches Nashik. The Godavari rises at Trimbakeshwar near Nashik, which lies on its banks, so the answer is Godavari.

91. Consider the following rivers from north to south: 1. Mahanadi 2. Godavari 3. Krishna 4. Kaveri. Which is the correct order?
(a) 1-2-3-4 (b) 2-1-3-4 (c) 1-3-2-4 (d) 2-3-1-4
Answer: (a)
Solution: Placing the Godavari north of the Mahanadi is the commonest error, but the Mahanadi drains Odisha, clearly north of the Godavari's mouth in Andhra Pradesh. The correct north-to-south sequence of east-flowing peninsular rivers is Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, so the answer is 1-2-3-4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

92. The river Luni, which disappears into the Rann of Kutch, originates in the
(a) Aravalli range (b) Vindhya range (c) Satpura range (d) Western Ghats
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Vindhyas and Satpuras feed the Ganga and Tapi systems, and the Western Ghats rivers reach the sea, so they are traps. The Luni rises in the Aravalli range near Ajmer and ends in the salty marshes of the Rann of Kutch, so the answer is Aravalli range.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

93. Which tributary of the Indus flows through the Kashmir valley?
(a) Chenab (b) Ravi (c) Jhelum (d) Beas
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Chenab, Ravi and Beas flow through the outer Himalayan ranges of Himachal and Jammu, not through the Kashmir valley, so they mislead. The Jhelum rises near Verinag and flows through the Kashmir valley past Srinagar before cutting a gorge through the Pir Panjal, so the answer is Jhelum.

97. Which of the following rivers does NOT form a delta at its mouth?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Krishna (c) Narmada (d) Godavari
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Mahanadi, Krishna and Godavari all build large deltas on the east coast, so they are wrong choices. The Narmada flows west through a rift valley and forms an estuary at the Gulf of Khambhat instead of a delta, so the answer is Narmada.

98. The Teesta river, a tributary of the Brahmaputra system, flows through which Indian state before entering West Bengal?
(a) Sikkim (b) Meghalaya (c) Arunachal Pradesh (d) Assam
Answer: (a)
Solution: Assam and Arunachal Pradesh lie on the Brahmaputra main stem, and Meghalaya drains to the Barak and Brahmaputra separately, so they are traps. The Teesta rises in Sikkim (from the Zemu glacier region) and flows through Sikkim into West Bengal, so the answer is Sikkim.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

99. Which river's basin covers the largest area in India?
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Indus (c) Godavari (d) Ganga
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Brahmaputra and Indus are mighty rivers but much of their basins lies outside India, which is the trap here. The Ganga has the largest basin area within India, covering about a quarter of the country's area, so the answer is Ganga.

100. The confluence of the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi, after which the river is called the Ganga, takes place at
(a) Devprayag (b) Karnaprayag (c) Vishnuprayag (d) Rudraprayag
Answer: (a)
Solution: Rudraprayag (Alaknanda–Mandakini), Vishnuprayag (Alaknanda–Dhauliganga) and Karnaprayag (Alaknanda–Pindar) are other prayags and classic similar-name traps. At Devprayag the Alaknanda meets the Bhagirathi and the combined stream takes the name Ganga, so the answer is Devprayag.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)
