# 🧒 Feynman Technique — States & Rivers

## The Hardest Concept: Why do some Indian rivers flow EAST and some flow WEST?

Students lose marks here every year. "Narmada flows west, Godavari flows east" — you memorise it, and two weeks later it flips in your head. Let's fix it permanently, the Feynman way.

### Explain it like you're 12

Imagine India as a **sloping dinner tray**. The tray is tilted: the **west side is high** (Western Ghats, like a wall running along the coast) and the **east side slopes down** gently toward the Bay of Bengal.

Now pour water on the tray:

- **Most water rolls eastward** — down the long, gentle slope — and falls into the **Bay of Bengal**. That's why the Ganga, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri and Mahanadi all flow **east**. They are born in or near the Western Ghats / Himalayas and travel across the whole country to the Bay of Bengal.
- **But a little water gets trapped on the high western edge** and spills off the short, steep side into the **Arabian Sea**. That's the **Narmada** and **Tapi** — they rise in central India (Amarkantak / Satpura region) and flow **west** through rift valleys (cracks in the earth) into the Gulf of Khambhat.

So the rule is not random memorisation — it's just **gravity + slope**:

> **Peninsular India's slope decides the river's direction. Main slope → east (Bay of Bengal). Rift valleys near the west coast → west (Arabian Sea).**

### The exam trap this kills

The examiner swaps one river to break your pattern memory:
- "Narmada flows east and joins the Bay of Bengal" ❌
- "Kaveri is a west-flowing river" ❌
- "Luni joins the Bay of Bengal" ❌ (Luni ends in the Rann of Kutch — it never reaches the sea!)

If you remember the **sloping tray**, you don't need to memorise each river separately — you can *derive* the answer.

### The Unforgettable Trick

**"EAST is BIG, WEST is Na-Ta-Lu"**

- **EAST (Bay of Bengal)** = the big boys: **G**anga, **G**odavari, **K**rishna, **K**averi, **M**ahanadi → *"Ganga-Godavari-Krishna-Kaveri-Mahanadi, sab Poorab ki ore"*
- **WEST (Arabian Sea)** = **Na**rmada, **Ta**pi (+ **Lu**ni which dies in the desert) → *"Na-Ta-Lu went West"*

Only **two major** peninsular rivers flow west: Narmada and Tapi. Everything else big flows east. If an option says a third big river flows west, it's almost certainly the trap.
