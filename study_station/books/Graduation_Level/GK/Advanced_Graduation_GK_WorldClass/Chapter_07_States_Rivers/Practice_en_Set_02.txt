Questions 26–50: Medium

26. Which river forms the boundary between Uttar Pradesh and Bihar for a considerable stretch before joining the Ganga?
(a) Gandak (b) Son (c) Kosi (d) Ghaghara
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Gandak and Kosi also flow through this region, which tempts many candidates, but the Ghaghara (Sarayu) runs along the UP–Bihar boundary for a long stretch before meeting the Ganga near Chhapra. The Kosi lies entirely east of this boundary zone, so the answer is Ghaghara.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

27. The Tungabhadra river is formed by the confluence of which two streams?
(a) Bhadra and Kabini (b) Tunga and Hemavati (c) Tunga and Bhadra (d) Netravati and Kumaradhara
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Hemavati and Kabini are tributaries of the Kaveri, a classic similar-names trap. The Tungabhadra is literally formed where the Tunga and Bhadra meet at Koodli in Karnataka, so the answer is Tunga and Bhadra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

28. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in the Amarkantak plateau region?
(a) Narmada (b) Son (c) Johilla (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Son is a tempting wrong choice because it joins the Ganga, but it does rise near Amarkantak. The Narmada, Son and Johilla all originate in the Amarkantak region of Madhya Pradesh, while the Mahanadi rises in the Sihawa hills of Chhattisgarh, so the answer is Mahanadi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

29. The river known as the "Sorrow of Bengal" before the construction of the Damodar Valley Project is:
(a) Hooghly (b) Damodar (c) Ajay (d) Mayurakshi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Hooghly is a distributary of the Ganga and is often picked by confusion with the "Sorrow of Bihar" (Kosi). The Damodar earned the title "Sorrow of Bengal" due to devastating floods in West Bengal, so the answer is Damodar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

30. Which state is drained by the maximum length of the Godavari river?
(a) Telangana (b) Maharashtra (c) Andhra Pradesh (d) Chhattisgarh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Andhra Pradesh tempts candidates because the Godavari delta lies there, but the river rises at Trimbakeshwar in Maharashtra and flows its longest stretch through that state before entering Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, so the answer is Maharashtra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

31. The Indus Water Treaty of 1960 allocated the waters of the eastern rivers — Ravi, Beas and Sutlej — to:
(a) Pakistan (b) Joint control of both countries (c) India (d) The World Bank
Answer: (c)
Solution: "Joint control" is an almost-correct-sounding trap since the treaty was brokered by the World Bank, but the Bank was only a mediator, not a beneficiary. Under the treaty, the three eastern rivers were allocated to India and the three western rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) to Pakistan, so the answer is India.
Source: PYQ-style

32. Which river flows through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges?
(a) Tapi (b) Mahi (c) Narmada (d) Chambal
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Tapi also flows in a rift valley, which makes it a tempting option, but it lies south of the Satpuras between the Satpura and Ajanta ranges. The Narmada flows westward in the rift valley between the Vindhyas to the north and the Satpuras to the south, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

33. The Chambal river is a tributary of the:
(a) Ganga (b) Son (c) Betwa (d) Yamuna
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many candidates mark Ganga because the Chambal's waters ultimately reach it, but the direct confluence matters here. The Chambal joins the Yamuna in Etawah district of Uttar Pradesh, so the answer is Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

34. Which of the following pairs of river and its place of origin is correctly matched?
(a) Kaveri — Mahabaleshwar (b) Krishna — Talakaveri (c) Narmada — Mahabaleshwar (d) Godavari — Trimbakeshwar
Answer: (d)
Solution: The options deliberately swap origins: the Kaveri rises at Talakaveri and the Krishna at Mahabaleshwar, so both of those pairs are reversed traps. The Godavari rises at Trimbakeshwar near Nashik in Maharashtra, so the answer is Godavari — Trimbakeshwar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

35. The largest river island in the world, Majuli, is located in which river?
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Ganga (c) Barak (d) Subansiri
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Subansiri is a tributary of the Brahmaputra and tempts those who recall it in Assam-related questions, but Majuli, the river island in Assam, lies in the Brahmaputra itself, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

36. Which peninsular river flows northward to join the Yamuna?
(a) Ken (b) Tapi (c) Sabarmati (d) Periyar
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Betwa and Ken both flow northward from the Bundelkhand region, but only the Ken is listed; the Tapi flows west and the Periyar is a Kerala river, which are distractors. The Ken joins the Yamuna near Chilla in Uttar Pradesh, so the answer is Ken.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

37. The Teesta river, a major tributary of the Brahmaputra system, originates from:
(a) Zemu glacier (b) Chemayungdung glacier (c) Gangotri glacier (d) Milam glacier
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Gangotri glacier is a tempting trap because of its fame, but it is the source of the Bhagirathi (Ganga). The Teesta rises from the Zemu glacier region (Tso Lhamo lake area) in Sikkim, so the answer is Zemu glacier.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

38. Which river is also known by the name "Tsangpo" in Tibet?
(a) Indus (b) Sutlej (c) Karnali (d) Brahmaputra
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Sutlej also originates near Mansarovar in Tibet, creating a similar-origin confusion, but its Tibetan stretch is not called Tsangpo. The Brahmaputra flows through Tibet as the Tsangpo before entering Arunachal Pradesh as the Siang/Dihang, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

39. The Hirakud Dam, one of the longest dams in the world, is built on the:
(a) Godavari (b) Mahanadi (c) Krishna (d) Damodar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Damodar tempts candidates who recall the Damodar Valley Corporation's dams, but Hirakud is in Odisha across the Mahanadi near Sambalpur, so the answer is Mahanadi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

40. Which of the following rivers forms an estuary and NOT a delta?
(a) Krishna (b) Narmada (c) Kaveri (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (b)
Solution: All four are major rivers, so the trap is assuming every large river builds a delta. West-flowing rivers like the Narmada and Tapi form estuaries because they flow through hard-rock rift valleys with steep gradients and carry less sediment, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

41. The city of Jabalpur is situated on the banks of which river?
(a) Betwa (b) Chambal (c) Narmada (d) Wainganga
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Wainganga flows through the same region of Madhya Pradesh and misleads many, but Jabalpur, famous for the Marble Rocks at Bhedaghat, stands on the Narmada, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

42. Which river is called the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Godavari (b) Kaveri (c) Krishna (d) Tungabhadra
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Kaveri is the tempting wrong answer because of its sacred status in the south, but the title "Dakshin Ganga" belongs to the Godavari owing to its great length — the longest among peninsular rivers — so the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

43. The Bhakra Nangal project is built across which river?
(a) Beas (b) Sutlej (c) Ravi (d) Chenab
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Beas is a strong distractor because the Beas–Sutlej Link project connects the two rivers, but the Bhakra and Nangal dams stand on the Sutlej in Himachal Pradesh and Punjab, so the answer is Sutlej.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

44. Which of the following statements about the Kosi river is correct?
(a) It is a left-bank tributary of the Yamuna (b) It originates in the Amarkantak plateau (c) It is known for frequently changing its course and is called the "Sorrow of Bihar" (d) It joins the Ganga at Patna
Answer: (c)
Solution: Option (d) is an almost-correct trap — the Kosi joins the Ganga near Kursela, upstream of Patna, not at Patna itself. The Kosi is notorious for shifting its westward course and causing floods, earning the name "Sorrow of Bihar", so the answer is the statement about course-changing.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

45. The Luni river of Rajasthan drains into:
(a) The Rann of Kutch (b) The Arabian Sea near Dwarka (c) The Gulf of Khambhat (d) The Chambal river
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Gulf of Khambhat tempts candidates who think of Gujarat's west-flowing rivers like the Sabarmati and Mahi, but the Luni is an inland-drainage river that ends in the marshy Rann of Kutch without reaching the sea, so the answer is the Rann of Kutch.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

46. Which river passes through the maximum number of Indian states?
(a) Brahmaputra (b) Godavari (c) Narmada (d) Ganga
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Godavari is tempting as the longest peninsular river crossing several states, but the Ganga basin and main stem cover Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand and West Bengal — more states than any other single river's course — so the answer is Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

47. The Sardar Sarovar Dam is located in which state?
(a) Gujarat (b) Maharashtra (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Rajasthan
Answer: (a)
Solution: Madhya Pradesh is the classic trap because most of the Narmada valley and the submergence area lie there, but the Sardar Sarovar Dam itself is built at Kevadia in Gujarat, so the answer is Gujarat.
Source: PYQ-style

48. Which tributary of the Indus flows entirely within India before joining the Chenab in Pakistan?
(a) Jhelum (b) Ravi (c) Zanskar (d) Shyok
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Jhelum and Chenab originate in India but flow mostly through Pakistan-administered territory, which confuses candidates. The Ravi rises in Himachal Pradesh, flows along the border and joins the Chenab in Pakistan, so the answer is Ravi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

49. Arrange the following rivers from west to east as they join the Ganga: 1. Son 2. Gandak 3. Kosi 4. Chambal (via Yamuna)
(a) 4, 1, 2, 3 (b) 1, 4, 2, 3 (c) 4, 2, 1, 3 (d) 1, 2, 4, 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: This is a chronological/positional confusion trap: candidates often place the Son west of the Yamuna system. The Chambal joins via the Yamuna (westernmost), then the Son, then the Gandak, and the Kosi is the easternmost, so the answer is 4, 1, 2, 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

50. The Nagarjuna Sagar Dam is built on the Krishna river between which two states?
(a) Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh (b) Maharashtra and Karnataka (c) Telangana and Andhra Pradesh (d) Telangana and Karnataka
Answer: (c)
Solution: Karnataka–Andhra Pradesh is a tempting pair because the Krishna links both, but the Tungabhadra dam lies on that border, not Nagarjuna Sagar. Nagarjuna Sagar stands on the Krishna between Telangana (Nalgonda) and Andhra Pradesh (Palnadu), so the answer is Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
Source: PYQ-style
