Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Which river forms the boundary between Uttar Pradesh and Bihar for a considerable stretch?
(a) Gandak (b) Ghaghara (c) Kosi (d) Son
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Gandak is tempting because it is a major Bihar river, but it flows entirely within Bihar after entering India. The Ghaghara flows along the UP–Bihar border before joining the Ganga near Chhapra, so the answer is Ghaghara.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

52. The Triveni Sangam at Prayagraj is the confluence of the Ganga, the Yamuna and which mythical river?
(a) Gandaki (b) Kaveri (c) Narmada (d) Saraswati
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kaveri and Narmada are real rivers but have no connection with Prayagraj. The third stream at the Sangam is the invisible, mythical Saraswati, so the answer is Saraswati.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

53. Which is the largest river island in India, located in the Brahmaputra in Assam?
(a) Majuli (b) Srirangapatna (c) Sagar Island (d) Diu
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sagar Island is tempting as it lies in the Hooghly delta, but it is a sea island, not a river island. Majuli in the Brahmaputra is India's largest river island, so the answer is Majuli.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

54. The Chambal river is a tributary of which river?
(a) Yamuna (b) Ganga (c) Son (d) Betwa
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ganga is tempting since the Chambal's water ultimately reaches it, but the Chambal joins the Yamuna directly in Etawah district of Uttar Pradesh, so the answer is Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

55. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar"?
(a) Gandak (b) Kosi (c) Bagmati (d) Mahananda
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Bagmati and Gandak also flood Bihar, which makes them tempting, but the Kosi's frequent course changes and devastating floods earned it the title "Sorrow of Bihar", so the answer is Kosi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

56. The Narmada river flows into which of the following?
(a) Bay of Bengal (b) Arabian Sea (c) Indian Ocean directly (d) Gulf of Mannar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Bay of Bengal is tempting because most major Indian rivers drain eastward, but the Narmada flows west through a rift valley and empties into the Gulf of Khambhat in the Arabian Sea, so the answer is Arabian Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

57. Which river forms the famous Jog (Gersoppa) Falls?
(a) Kali (b) Netravati (c) Sharavati (d) Tungabhadra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kali is tempting as it is another major Karnataka river, but the Jog Falls, among India's highest plunge waterfalls, are on the Sharavati river, so the answer is Sharavati.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

58. The Indus Water Treaty (1960) between India and Pakistan was brokered by which organisation?
(a) United Nations (b) IMF (c) Commonwealth (d) World Bank
Answer: (d)
Solution: The United Nations is tempting because it mediates many international disputes, but the Indus Waters Treaty of 1960 was negotiated and brokered by the World Bank, so the answer is World Bank.
Source: Official text of the Indus Waters Treaty, 1960

59. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Godavari (b) Mahanadi (c) Brahmaputra (d) Krishna
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Godavari is tempting because of its great length, but it rises at Trimbakeshwar in Maharashtra. The Brahmaputra rises near Lake Mansarovar in Tibet as the Tsangpo, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

60. The Bhakra Nangal dam is built on which river?
(a) Sutlej (b) Beas (c) Ravi (d) Chenab
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Beas is tempting because the Bhakra project also uses Beas water through the Beas–Sutlej link, but the Bhakra dam itself stands on the Sutlej in Himachal Pradesh, so the answer is Sutlej.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

61. Which river is called the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Krishna (b) Godavari (c) Kaveri (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Kaveri is tempting because of its sacred status in the South, but the Godavari, being the longest peninsular river, is called the Dakshin Ganga, so the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

62. The Teesta river, a source of water-sharing talks between India and Bangladesh, is a tributary of which river?
(a) Ganga (b) Barak (c) Brahmaputra (d) Mahananda
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Ganga is tempting because most India–Bangladesh water disputes concern it, but the Teesta rises in Sikkim and joins the Brahmaputra (Jamuna) in Bangladesh, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

63. Which state is drained by the maximum number of east-flowing peninsular rivers among the following?
(a) Rajasthan (b) Gujarat (c) Punjab (d) Odisha
Answer: (d)
Solution: Gujarat is tempting due to its long coastline, but its major rivers like the Narmada and Tapi are west-flowing. Odisha is drained by the Mahanadi, Brahmani, Baitarani and Subarnarekha, all flowing east into the Bay of Bengal, so the answer is Odisha.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

64. The Sardar Sarovar Dam is located in which state?
(a) Rajasthan (b) Maharashtra (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Gujarat
Answer: (d)
Solution: Madhya Pradesh is tempting because most of the Narmada valley and the project's submergence area lie there, but the Sardar Sarovar Dam itself is at Kevadia in Gujarat, so the answer is Gujarat.
Source: Official site, Sardar Sarovar Narmada Nigam Ltd.

65. Which river's main stem passes through the maximum number of Indian states?
(a) Ganga (b) Godavari (c) Narmada (d) Krishna
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Godavari is tempting as the longest peninsular river crossing several states, but the Ganga's main stem traverses Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand and West Bengal, the most among these, so the answer is Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

66. The Barak river, after entering Assam, flows through which valley?
(a) Surma valley (b) Brahmaputra valley (c) Kangra valley (d) Kullu valley
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Brahmaputra valley is tempting since Assam is synonymous with it, but the Barak flows through the Surma (Barak) valley of southern Assam, covering Cachar, Hailakandi and Karimganj, so the answer is Surma valley.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

67. Which of the following is a west-flowing river of peninsular India?
(a) Pennar (b) Vaigai (c) Vamsadhara (d) Tapi
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Pennar is tempting as a lesser-known peninsular river, but it flows east into the Bay of Bengal. The Tapi flows west through a rift valley into the Gulf of Khambhat, so the answer is Tapi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

68. The Hirakud Dam, one of the longest earthen dams in the world, is built on which river?
(a) Baitarani (b) Brahmani (c) Mahanadi (d) Subarnarekha
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Brahmani is tempting as another major Odisha river, but the Hirakud Dam near Sambalpur is built across the Mahanadi, so the answer is Mahanadi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

69. Which river originates from the Amarkantak plateau in Madhya Pradesh?
(a) Son (b) Mahanadi (c) Narmada (d) Tapi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Son is tempting because it too rises close to the Amarkantak region, but the Narmada specifically originates from the Amarkantak plateau in Anuppur district of Madhya Pradesh, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

70. The "Dhuandhar" Falls are located on which river?
(a) Narmada (b) Chambal (c) Betwa (d) Ken
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Chambal is tempting as another Madhya Pradesh river with gorges, but the Dhuandhar Falls at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur are on the Narmada, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

71. The Almatti (Lal Bahadur Shastri) dam is built on which river?
(a) Tungabhadra (b) Bhima (c) Kaveri (d) Krishna
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Bhima is tempting because it is a major tributary in the same basin, but the Almatti dam in Vijayapura district of Karnataka stands on the Krishna, so the answer is Krishna.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

72. The Loktak Lake, famous for phumdis, is fed and drained by rivers of which state?
(a) Manipur (b) Mizoram (c) Nagaland (d) Tripura
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mizoram is tempting as it has many lakes and rivers, but Loktak, the largest freshwater lake in the North-East, lies in Manipur and is drained by the Manipur river system, so the answer is Manipur.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

73. Which of the following rivers is associated with the city of Nashik?
(a) Krishna (b) Godavari (c) Tapi (d) Bhima
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Tapi is tempting because it also flows through northern Maharashtra, but Nashik stands on the banks of the Godavari, which rises nearby at Trimbakeshwar, so the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

74. The Ghaghara, Gandak and Kosi rivers originate in which country/region before entering India?
(a) Aravalli range (b) Myanmar hills (c) Tibet/Nepal Himalayas (d) Satpura range
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Satpura range is tempting because rivers like the Narmada rise there, but the Ghaghara, Gandak and Kosi are all Himalayan tributaries of the Ganga rising in Tibet or Nepal, so the answer is Tibet/Nepal Himalayas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 India Physical Environment

75. Which river's water dispute among Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Puducherry led to a tribunal whose award was modified by the Supreme Court in 2018?
(a) Krishna (b) Kaveri (c) Godavari (d) Pennar
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Krishna is tempting because it too has an inter-state tribunal, but the 2018 Supreme Court judgment modifying the tribunal award concerned the Kaveri dispute, so the answer is Kaveri.
Source: Supreme Court of India judgment, Kaveri water dispute (2018)
