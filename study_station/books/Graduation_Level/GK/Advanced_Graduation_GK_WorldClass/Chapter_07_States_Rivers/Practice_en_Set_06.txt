126. Which of the following statements about the Godavari river is correct?
(a) It originates from Trimbakeshwar near Nashik and is the longest river of Peninsular India (b) It is the only peninsular river that flows through a rift valley (c) It originates from the Amarkantak plateau and flows into the Bay of Bengal (d) It forms an estuary instead of a delta at its mouth
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong options describe the Narmada, which rises at Amarkantak, flows through a rift valley and ends in an estuary. The Godavari rises at Trimbakeshwar in the Western Ghats near Nashik and, at about 1,465 km, is the longest peninsular river, forming a delta in Andhra Pradesh, so the answer is that it originates from Trimbakeshwar near Nashik and is the longest river of Peninsular India.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

127. The Narmada and Tapi rivers are unique among major Indian rivers because they:
(a) flow westward through rift valleys and form estuaries (b) originate from the same glacier in Uttarakhand (c) form the largest deltas on the east coast (d) are entirely rain-fed seasonal rivers
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming all major Indian rivers form deltas; east-flowing rivers like the Mahanadi and Godavari do, but the Narmada and Tapi flow west through fault-created rift valleys between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges and enter the Arabian Sea through estuaries, so the answer is that they flow westward through rift valleys and form estuaries.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

128. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar" due to its frequent floods caused by heavy siltation?
(a) Gandak (b) Kosi (c) Son (d) Bagmati
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is the Gandak, which is also a flood-prone Himalayan tributary of the Ganga in Bihar, but the Kosi is specifically called the "Sorrow of Bihar" because its enormous silt load makes it shift course westward dramatically, so the answer is Kosi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

129. The Chambal river, famous for its ravines (badland topography), is a tributary of which river?
(a) Ganga directly (b) Son (c) Yamuna (d) Betwa
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is assuming the Chambal joins the Ganga directly; in fact it rises near Mhow in the Vindhyas, flows through Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan, and joins the Yamuna in Uttar Pradesh, so the answer is Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

130. Which of the following correctly matches the river with its place of origin?
(a) Kaveri – Nashik (b) Mahanadi – Amarkantak plateau (c) Krishna – Mahabaleshwar (d) Tapi – Talakaveri
Answer: (c)
Solution: The traps involve swapped origins: the Mahanadi rises near Sihawa in Chhattisgarh (Amarkantak is the Narmada's source), the Kaveri rises at Talakaveri in Karnataka, and the Tapi rises near Multai in Madhya Pradesh. The Krishna correctly rises near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra, so the answer is Krishna – Mahabaleshwar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

131. The Indus Water Treaty of 1960 between India and Pakistan allocated which three eastern rivers primarily to India?
(a) Ravi, Beas, Sutlej (b) Jhelum, Beas, Ravi (c) Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej (d) Indus, Jhelum, Chenab
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option lists the three western rivers, which were allocated primarily to Pakistan; the treaty gave India unrestricted use of the eastern rivers Ravi, Beas and Sutlej, so the answer is Ravi, Beas, Sutlej.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (India: People and Economy)

132. Which river flows through the Bundelkhand region and joins the Yamuna near Hamirpur in Uttar Pradesh?
(a) Sindh (b) Chambal (c) Betwa (d) Son
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the Chambal, which also joins the Yamuna but much further downstream near Etawah; the Betwa, rising in the Vindhyas near Bhopal, flows through Bundelkhand and meets the Yamuna near Hamirpur, so the answer is Betwa.

133. The Brahmaputra is known by which name when it flows through Tibet?
(a) Siang (b) Dihang (c) Tsangpo (d) Lohit
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong options Siang and Dihang are names used in Arunachal Pradesh after the river enters India; in Tibet the Brahmaputra is called the Tsangpo, so the answer is Tsangpo.

134. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Sutlej (b) Chenab (c) Beas (d) Ravi
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is assuming all Indus-system tributaries rise in India; the Beas, Chenab and Ravi originate in Himachal Pradesh, but the Sutlej rises near Lake Rakshastal in Tibet close to the Mansarovar region, so the answer is Sutlej.

137. The Sardar Sarovar Dam is built on which river?
(a) Tapi (b) Chambal (c) Mahi (d) Narmada
Answer: (d)

138. Which of the following statements about the Kaveri river is INCORRECT?
(a) It originates at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Karnataka (b) It is a west-flowing river that empties into the Arabian Sea (c) It forms the Shivanasamudra falls (d) It flows through Karnataka and Tamil Nadu
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is that the first three statements are all true, which tempts candidates to pick one of them; the incorrect statement is the claim about westward flow, because the Kaveri flows eastward and empties into the Bay of Bengal in Tamil Nadu, so the answer is the statement that it is a west-flowing river emptying into the Arabian Sea.

139. The Teesta river, a major tributary of the Brahmaputra system, originates from which state/region?
(a) Arunachal Pradesh (b) Sikkim (c) Assam (d) West Bengal
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is Arunachal Pradesh, where many Brahmaputra tributaries rise, but the Teesta originates from the Tso Lhamo lake region in Sikkim and flows through West Bengal before entering Bangladesh, so the answer is Sikkim.

140. Which river valley project, one of the earliest multipurpose projects of independent India, is built on the Damodar river?
(a) Damodar Valley Corporation (b) Rihand Project (c) Bhakra Nangal Project (d) Hirakud Project
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is Hirakud, which is also a major multipurpose project but lies on the Mahanadi in Odisha; the Damodar Valley Corporation, modelled on the Tennessee Valley Authority, was set up in 1948 on the Damodar river across Jharkhand and West Bengal, so the answer is Damodar Valley Corporation.

141. The Bhakra Nangal dam, one of the highest gravity dams in the world, is built on which river?
(a) Beas (b) Ravi (c) Chenab (d) Sutlej
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option is the Beas, because the Beas-Sutlej link project connects the two rivers, but the Bhakra Nangal dam is built on the Sutlej in Himachal Pradesh, creating the Gobind Sagar reservoir, so the answer is Sutlej.

142. Which of the following rivers forms a boundary between India and Nepal for part of its course?

144. Which of the following is the correct order of the Himalayan rivers of the Indus system from west to east?
(a) Indus – Jhelum – Chenab – Ravi – Beas – Sutlej (b) Sutlej – Beas – Ravi – Chenab – Jhelum – Indus (c) Indus – Chenab – Jhelum – Ravi – Sutlej – Beas (d) Jhelum – Indus – Ravi – Chenab – Beas – Sutlej
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is the reversed order, which reads the rivers from east to west; moving from west to east the correct sequence is Indus, Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas and Sutlej, so the answer is Indus – Jhelum – Chenab – Ravi – Beas – Sutlej.

146. Which of the following correctly arranges the peninsular rivers from north to south?

147. The Ganga is formed at Devprayag (Uttarakhand) by the confluence of which two rivers?
(a) Alaknanda and Mandakini (b) Alaknanda and Bhagirathi (c) Bhagirathi and Yamuna (d) Alaknanda and Dhauliganga

148. Which river was known as the "Sorrow of Bengal" before the construction of the Damodar Valley Project?
(a) Ajay (b) Hooghly (c) Damodar (d) Mayurakshi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is the Hooghly, which is Bengal's most famous river but is a distributary of the Ganga, not the holder of this title; the Damodar was called the "Sorrow of Bengal" for its devastating floods before the DVC tamed it, so the answer is Damodar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

149. Which of the following statements about the Luni river is correct?
(a) It is a perennial river of the Thar desert that reaches the Arabian Sea (b) It flows through Madhya Pradesh and joins the Chambal (c) It is a tributary of the Narmada (d) It originates in the Aravalli hills and its water turns saline, disappearing into the Rann of Kutch
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims the Luni is perennial and reaches the sea; in fact the Luni rises in the Aravalli near Ajmer, flows through the Thar, turns increasingly saline (hence its name, from "lavan" meaning salt) and is lost in the marshes of the Rann of Kutch, so the answer is that it originates in the Aravalli hills and disappears into the Rann of Kutch.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

150. The Farakka Barrage, built to divert water into the Hooghly river to flush silt from Kolkata port, is located on which river and in which state?
(a) Bhagirathi, Jharkhand (b) Mahananda, West Bengal (c) Padma, Bihar (d) Ganga, West Bengal
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the Bhagirathi, which is the distributary fed by the barrage, not the river on which it stands; the Farakka Barrage is built across the main Ganga river in Murshidabad district of West Bengal to divert water into the Bhagirathi-Hooghly, so the answer is Ganga, West Bengal.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Geography (India: People and Economy)
