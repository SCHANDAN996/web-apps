Card 1
Front: Which two major Indian rivers flow westward through rift valleys into the Arabian Sea?
Back: The Narmada and the Tapi (Tapti). Both flow between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges into the Gulf of Khambhat — the Narmada north of the Satpuras, the Tapi south of them.

Card 2
Front: Which river is called the "Sorrow of Bihar," and which river holds the same title for West Bengal?
Back: The Kosi is the "Sorrow of Bihar" (frequent course changes and floods); the Damodar was historically called the "Sorrow of Bengal."

Card 3
Front: Which is the longest river that flows entirely within India (not entering from another country)?
Back: The Godavari (about 1,465 km) — the longest peninsular river, also called "Dakshin Ganga." The Ganga is longer overall but originates partly fed by Himalayan sources near the border region.

Card 4
Front: Memory trick: How can you quickly recall the order of the four major Himalayan rivers from east to west?
Back: Use "B-G-I-S" → Brahmaputra, Ganga, Indus, Satluj (east to west). Mnemonic: "Big Giants In Snow."

Card 5
Front: Which river forms the famous Dhuandhar Falls and the Marble Rocks gorge near Jabalpur?
Back: The Narmada. It cuts through marble rocks at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh.

Card 6
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Ganga originates from Gangotri glacier as the river Ganga." What is wrong with this statement?
Back: The stream emerging from the Gangotri glacier (Gaumukh) is the Bhagirathi. The river is called the Ganga only after the Bhagirathi meets the Alaknanda at Devprayag.

Card 7
Front: Which is the largest river island in India (and among the largest in the world), and on which river does it lie?
Back: Majuli Island, on the Brahmaputra in Assam.

Card 8
Front: Which river is known as the "Tsangpo" in Tibet, and what is it called in Bangladesh?
Back: The Brahmaputra — called Tsangpo in Tibet and Jamuna in Bangladesh (where it joins the Ganga/Padma).

Card 9
Front: Memory trick: How to remember the left-bank vs right-bank tributaries of the Ganga?
Back: Left bank (from the east/northeast): "RGGK" — Ramganga, Gomti, Ghaghara, Kosi. Right bank (from the south): Yamuna, Son. Trick: "Left = R-G-G-K, Right = Yamuna-Son."

Card 10
Front: Which two rivers meet at Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam) along with the mythical Saraswati?
Back: The Ganga and the Yamuna.

Card 11
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Chambal is a tributary of the Ganga." True or false — and why do students get this wrong?
Back: False. The Chambal is a tributary of the Yamuna (joins it in Uttar Pradesh). Trap: Chambal, Betwa, and Ken all belong to the Yamuna system, not directly to the Ganga.

Card 12
Front: On which river is the Hirakud Dam — one of the world's longest earthen dams — built, and which states does its basin mainly cover?
Back: The Mahanadi. Its basin lies mainly in Chhattisgarh and Odisha, and the river flows into the Bay of Bengal. It is the major river of Odisha.

Card 13
Front: Which is the largest tributary of the Indus by volume, and how is it formed?
Back: The Chenab — formed by the confluence of the Chandra and Bhaga streams at Tandi in Himachal Pradesh (Lahaul region). It then flows through Jammu & Kashmir into Pakistan.

Card 14
Front: Under the Indus Waters Treaty (1960), which three rivers were allocated to India?
Back: The eastern rivers: Ravi, Beas, and Satluj. The western rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) were allocated to Pakistan, with limited use rights for India.

Card 15
Front: Which river is called the "Lifeline of Sikkim," and into which river does it merge?
Back: The Teesta — it flows through Sikkim and West Bengal and joins the Brahmaputra (Jamuna) in Bangladesh.

Card 16
Front: Which peninsular river is famous for the Jog (Gersoppa) Falls, one of India's highest plunge waterfalls?
Back: The Sharavati (Karnataka) — Jog Falls drops about 253 m.

Card 17
Front: Examiner's trap: "The Luni is a west-flowing river that reaches the Arabian Sea." What is the catch?
Back: The Luni (Rajasthan) flows southwest but does NOT reach the sea — it ends in the marshy Rann of Kutch. Its water turns saline, hence the name (from Sanskrit "lavan" = salt).

Card 18
Front: Which river is known as the "Handmaid of the Narmada," and where does it join it?
Back: The Tawa — the largest tributary of the Narmada, joining it in Madhya Pradesh (Hoshangabad region).

Card 19
Front: Which is the southernmost of the three major east-flowing peninsular rivers (Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri), and what is its traditional name in Tamil literature?
Back: The Kaveri — called "Ponni" in Tamil tradition; it rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills (Karnataka) and flows through Tamil Nadu.

Card 20
Front: Quick recall: Which river passes through the maximum number of Indian states?
Back: The Ganga — it flows through Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand, and West Bengal (5 states), the most for any single Indian river.
