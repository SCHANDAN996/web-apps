Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

1. Which is the longest river in India?
(a) Ganga (b) Godavari (c) Brahmaputra (d) Yamuna
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Godavari is the longest river of peninsular India, which tempts many students, but the question asks about the whole of India. The Ganga, about 2,525 km long, is the longest river in India, so the answer is Ganga.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

2. The Godavari river originates from which place?
(a) Amarkantak (b) Mahabaleshwar (c) Trimbakeshwar, Nashik (d) Gangotri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Amarkantak is the source of the Narmada, a common confusion. The Godavari rises at Trimbakeshwar near Nashik in Maharashtra, so the answer is Trimbakeshwar, Nashik.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

3. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar"?
(a) Son (b) Gandak (c) Kosi (d) Damodar
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Damodar is called the "Sorrow of Bengal", which is the tempting trap here. The Kosi, notorious for frequent floods and shifting course, is called the "Sorrow of Bihar", so the answer is Kosi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

4. The Narmada river flows into which water body?
(a) Bay of Bengal (b) Arabian Sea (c) Indian Ocean directly (d) Gulf of Mannar
Answer: (b)
Solution: Most major Indian rivers drain into the Bay of Bengal, which misleads students. The Narmada flows westward through a rift valley and empties into the Gulf of Khambhat in the Arabian Sea, so the answer is Arabian Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

5. Which is the largest tributary of the Ganga by volume of water?
(a) Ghaghara (b) Yamuna (c) Kosi (d) Son
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Yamuna is the longest tributary, which is the usual trap, but by discharge the Ghaghara carries more water. Hence the answer is Ghaghara.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

6. The Chambal river is a tributary of which river?
(a) Yamuna (b) Ganga (c) Narmada (d) Betwa
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Chambal rises near Mhow in Madhya Pradesh and joins the Yamuna in Uttar Pradesh. It does not join the Ganga directly, so the answer is Yamuna.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

7. Which river forms the famous Jog (Gersoppa) Falls?
(a) Kaveri (b) Krishna (c) Sharavati (d) Tungabhadra
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kaveri forms the Shivanasamudra Falls, a similar-sounding trap. The Jog Falls in Karnataka are formed by the Sharavati river, so the answer is Sharavati.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

8. The Tapi (Tapti) river flows through which of the following states?
(a) Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand (b) Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra (c) Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu (d) Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Tapi rises in the Satpura range (Betul district, Madhya Pradesh), flows through Maharashtra, and enters the Arabian Sea in Gujarat. It does not flow through Rajasthan, so the answer is Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

9. Which river is also known as the "Dakshin Ganga" (Ganga of the South)?
(a) Krishna (b) Kaveri (c) Godavari (d) Mahanadi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kaveri is sometimes loosely called the Ganga of the South in popular writing, which is the trap, but in standard geography texts the title "Dakshin Ganga" belongs to the Godavari, the longest peninsular river. So the answer is Godavari.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

10. The Brahmaputra enters India through which state?
(a) Assam (b) Sikkim (c) Arunachal Pradesh (d) Nagaland
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Brahmaputra is most associated with Assam, which tempts students, but it first enters Indian territory in Arunachal Pradesh (as the Dihang/Siang) before reaching Assam. So the answer is Arunachal Pradesh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

11. Which river originates from the Amarkantak plateau?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Narmada (c) Godavari (d) Kaveri
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Mahanadi rises in Chhattisgarh's highlands, a nearby-source confusion. The Narmada rises from the Amarkantak plateau in Madhya Pradesh, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

12. The Kaveri river originates in which state?
(a) Tamil Nadu (b) Kerala (c) Karnataka (d) Andhra Pradesh
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Kaveri is most associated with Tamil Nadu, which is the trap, but it rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Kodagu district, Karnataka. So the answer is Karnataka.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography

13. Which of the following rivers flows through a rift valley?
(a) Ganga (b) Narmada (c) Krishna (d) Kosi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Narmada flows westward between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges through a rift (graben) valley, unlike the delta-building east-flowing rivers. So the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

14. The Indus river originates near which lake/mansarovar region?
(a) Rakshastal (b) Mansarovar (Tibet) (c) Dal Lake (d) Wular Lake
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Indus rises from a glacier near Lake Mansarovar in Tibet (Bokhar Chu, Kailash range). Dal and Wular lakes are in Kashmir and unrelated to its source, so the answer is Mansarovar (Tibet).
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

15. Which is the longest west-flowing river of peninsular India?
(a) Narmada (b) Tapi (c) Sabarmati (d) Mahi
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Tapi is also west-flowing and is the tempting wrong choice, but the Narmada (about 1,312 km) is the longest west-flowing river of peninsular India. So the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

16. The Teesta river is a tributary of which major river?
(a) Ganga (b) Indus (c) Mahanadi (d) Brahmaputra
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Teesta rises in Sikkim, flows through West Bengal, and joins the Brahmaputra (Jamuna) in Bangladesh. It is not part of the Ganga system, so the answer is Brahmaputra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

17. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bengal"?
(a) Hooghly (b) Teesta (c) Kosi (d) Damodar
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Kosi is the "Sorrow of Bihar", the classic similar-name trap. The Damodar, once notorious for devastating floods in West Bengal, is called the "Sorrow of Bengal", so the answer is Damodar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

18. The Krishna river originates from which place?
(a) Mahabaleshwar (b) Trimbakeshwar (c) Amarkantak (d) Talakaveri
Answer: (a)
Solution: Trimbakeshwar is the source of the Godavari, a frequent mix-up since both are Maharashtra sources. The Krishna rises near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra, so the answer is Mahabaleshwar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

19. Which river forms the boundary between Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh for a considerable stretch?
(a) Son (b) Betwa (c) Ken (d) Chambal
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Chambal flows along the border of Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh (and also forms part of the MP–Rajasthan boundary) before joining the Yamuna. So the answer is Chambal.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

20. The Mahanadi flows mainly through which two states?
(a) Jharkhand and West Bengal (b) Chhattisgarh and Odisha (c) Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra (d) Bihar and Jharkhand
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Mahanadi rises in Chhattisgarh and flows through Odisha to the Bay of Bengal, forming a large delta near Cuttack. So the answer is Chhattisgarh and Odisha.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

21. Consider the following statements:
1. The Narmada and Tapi are the only major peninsular rivers that flow westward into the Arabian Sea.
2. The Narmada forms a large delta at its mouth.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: because the Narmada flows through a rift valley with a steep gradient and estuarine mouth, it forms an estuary, not a delta. Statement 1 is correct as these two are the major west-flowing peninsular rivers draining into the Arabian Sea, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

22. Arrange the following rivers from north to south based on their mouths on the east coast:
1. Mahanadi 2. Godavari 3. Krishna 4. Kaveri
(a) 2-1-3-4 (b) 1-2-3-4 (c) 1-3-2-4 (d) 2-3-1-4
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is swapping Godavari and Krishna, whose deltas lie close together in Andhra Pradesh. Moving south along the coast: Mahanadi (Odisha), Godavari, Krishna (both Andhra Pradesh), then Kaveri (Tamil Nadu), so the answer is 1-2-3-4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

23. The Indus Water Treaty (1960) between India and Pakistan was brokered by which organisation?
(a) United Nations (b) International Court of Justice (c) World Bank (d) Asian Development Bank
Answer: (c)
Solution: The United Nations is the tempting guess for any international treaty, but the Indus Waters Treaty of 1960 was negotiated and brokered by the World Bank (then IBRD). So the answer is World Bank.
Source: Official text of the Indus Waters Treaty, 1960

24. Consider the following pairs:
River — Tributary
1. Ganga — Gandak
2. Godavari — Manjira
3. Krishna — Bhima
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: All three pairs are correct: the Gandak joins the Ganga near Patna, the Manjira is a major tributary of the Godavari, and the Bhima is a major tributary of the Krishna. The trap is assuming one pair must be wrong in such questions, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

25. Which of the following rivers does NOT originate in India?
(a) Sutlej (b) Beas (c) Chenab (d) Ravi
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is that all four are Indus-system rivers flowing through India, so they seem Indian in origin. The Beas (Beas Kund, Himachal), Chenab (Lahaul, Himachal) and Ravi (Himachal) rise in India, while the Sutlej rises from Rakshastal near Mansarovar in Tibet, so the answer is Sutlej.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)
