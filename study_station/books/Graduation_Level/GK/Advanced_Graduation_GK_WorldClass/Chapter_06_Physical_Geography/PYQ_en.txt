# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Physical Geography of India

## 1. How Exams Use This Chapter

Physical Geography of India is one of the most dependable static GK areas across SSC CGL/CHSL, IBPS/SBI, RRB NTPC and State PCS prelims. Questions are almost always **fact-recall with a twist** — the examiner rarely asks a plain definition; instead, he wraps a true fact inside a nearly-true statement, a reversed pair, or a negative sentence.

**Sub-topics that appear again and again:**

- **Himalayan divisions** — Trans-Himalaya vs Great/Lesser/Outer Himalaya; which peak lies in which range (K2, Kangchenjunga, Nanda Devi, Dhaulagiri, Annapurna).
- **Pass–state matching** — Zoji La, Shipki La, Nathu La, Bomdi La, Banihal, Lipulekh.
- **River systems** — origin (source glacier), left/right bank tributaries, east-flowing vs west-flowing rivers, river–tributary mismatch questions.
- **Plateaus and passes of Peninsular India** — Deccan, Chotanagpur, Malwa; highest peaks of the Western/Eastern Ghats (Anamudi vs Mahendragiri).
- **Coastal plains** — Konkan, Malabar, Coromandel, Northern Circars: which stretch belongs to which name.
- **Islands** — Andaman–Nicobar (volcanic Barren Island, 10° Channel) vs Lakshadweep (coral origin, 8° and 9° Channels).
- **Soils and natural vegetation** — alluvial vs black (regur) vs laterite; soil–crop and soil–region matching.
- **Climate** — monsoon mechanism, retreating monsoon and Tamil Nadu rainfall, western disturbances.

**Exam-wise flavour (in words, not numbers):**

- **State PCS:** statement-based questions ("Which of the statements given above is/are correct?"), location sequencing (arrange peaks/passes east to west), and conceptual traps (why west-flowing rivers form estuaries, not deltas).
- **SSC / Banking:** one-liner factual MCQs — highest peak, longest river, source of a river, soil of a region.
- **Railway (NTPC/Group D):** direct matching — pass–state, river–origin, island–channel.

## 2. The Examiner's Traps (with examples)

### Trap 1 — Almost Correct Statement
One word is changed in an otherwise true sentence.
> "K2, the highest peak of India, lies in the Great Himalaya range."
K2 (Godwin-Austen, 8,611 m) is real and is India's highest peak, but it lies in the **Karakoram (Trans-Himalaya)**, not the Great Himalaya. Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) is the highest peak *within the Himalaya proper* on the Indian side.

### Trap 2 — Chronological / Sequence Confusion
Arrange peaks, passes or rivers in east-to-west or north-to-south order. The examiner shuffles one item.
> Arrange west to east: Nanga Parbat – Dhaulagiri – Everest – Kangchenjunga. (Correct order.) Swapping Dhaulagiri and Everest is the classic wrong option.

### Trap 3 — Negative Phrasing
"Which of the following is **NOT** a tributary of the Ganga?" Students in a hurry miss the NOT. Typical answer sets include one Brahmaputra tributary (e.g., Teesta) hidden among Ganga tributaries (Yamuna, Gandak, Kosi, Son).

### Trap 4 — Similar-Sounding Names
- **Zoji La** (Ladakh, Srinagar–Leh) vs **Shipki La** (Himachal, Sutlej enters India) vs **Nathu La** (Sikkim).
- **Mahi** (flows into the Gulf of Khambhat) vs **Sabarmati**; **Luni** (inland drainage, ends in Rann of Kutch) presented as a Bay of Bengal river.
- **Doddabetta** (Nilgiri, Tamil Nadu) vs **Anamudi** (Anaimalai, Kerala — highest in South India).

### Trap 5 — Reversed Pairs
"The Narmada flows through a rift valley between the Vindhyas (north) and Satpuras (south)" is true; the reversed statement (Vindhyas south, Satpuras north) is the trap.

### Trap 6 — Delta vs Estuary
East-flowing rivers (Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri) form **deltas**; west-flowing Narmada and Tapi form **estuaries**. Options often swap these.

## 3. Top 10 High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions with Solutions

**Q1.** K2 (Mount Godwin-Austen) is located in which range?
(a) Great Himalaya (b) Karakoram (c) Zanskar (d) Pir Panjal
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** K2 (8,611 m) lies in the Karakoram, part of the Trans-Himalaya. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — "Great Himalaya" tempts because K2 is India's highest peak.
**Source:** NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

**Q2.** Which of the following is NOT a tributary of the Ganga?
(a) Gandak (b) Kosi (c) Teesta (d) Son
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** Teesta joins the Brahmaputra system. Gandak, Kosi and Son are Ganga tributaries. Trap type: *Negative Phrasing*.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q3.** The Narmada river flows between which two ranges?
(a) Vindhya (north) and Satpura (south) (b) Satpura (north) and Vindhya (south)
(c) Aravalli and Vindhya (d) Satpura and Mahadeo
**Answer: (a)**
**Solution:** The Narmada flows west through a rift valley between the Vindhyas and Satpuras. Trap type: *Reversed Pair*.
**Source:** NCERT Class 11 Geography

**Q4.** Which is the highest peak of South India?
(a) Doddabetta (b) Anamudi (c) Mahendragiri (d) Kalsubai
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Anamudi (2,695 m, Kerala, Anaimalai Hills) is the highest; Doddabetta (2,637 m) is the highest of the Nilgiris — the classic distractor. Trap type: *Similar Names*.
**Source:** NCERT Class 11 Geography

**Q5.** Barren Island, India's only active volcano, is located in—
(a) Lakshadweep (b) Andaman and Nicobar Islands (c) Gulf of Mannar (d) Gulf of Khambhat
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Barren Island lies in the Andaman Sea, east of the Andamans. Lakshadweep islands are coral in origin. Trap type: *Almost Correct* (both are island groups).
**Source:** NCERT Class 11 Geography

**Q6.** The Ten Degree Channel separates—
(a) Andaman from Nicobar (b) India from Sri Lanka
(c) Lakshadweep from Maldives (d) Little Andaman from Car Nicobar
**Answer: (a)**
**Solution:** The 10° Channel separates the Andaman group from the Nicobar group. The 8° Channel separates Minicoy from Maldives; the 9° Channel separates Minicoy from the main Lakshadweep group. Trap type: *Similar Names/Numbers*.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q7.** Which soil is best suited for cotton cultivation?
(a) Alluvial soil (b) Black (regur) soil (c) Laterite soil (d) Red soil
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Black soil, formed from Deccan basalt, retains moisture and is ideal for cotton. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — alluvial is the most fertile overall, but not the cotton answer.
**Source:** NCERT Class 10 Geography

**Q8.** Shipki La pass is located in which state?
(a) Jammu & Kashmir (b) Himachal Pradesh (c) Uttarakhand (d) Sikkim
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** Shipki La is in Himachal Pradesh; the Sutlej enters India near it. Zoji La (Ladakh), Nathu La (Sikkim) and Lipulekh (Uttarakhand) are the distractors. Trap type: *Similar Names*.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q9.** Which of the following rivers forms an estuary instead of a delta?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Godavari (c) Narmada (d) Krishna
**Answer: (c)**
**Solution:** West-flowing Narmada (and Tapi) form estuaries; the east-flowing rivers listed form deltas. Trap type: *Delta–Estuary Swap*.
**Source:** NCERT Class 11 Geography

**Q10.** Tamil Nadu receives most of its rainfall from—
(a) South-west monsoon (b) Retreating (north-east) monsoon
(c) Western disturbances (d) Pre-monsoon showers
**Answer: (b)**
**Solution:** The Coromandel coast gets rain in October–December from the retreating monsoon picking up moisture over the Bay of Bengal. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — most of India rains from the SW monsoon.
**Source:** NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India)

## 4. The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**Rule:** If a question is pure recall (one fact), decide within **15 seconds** — you either know it or you don't; flag and move. If it is statement-based or sequence-based, allow up to **45 seconds** to eliminate options; beyond that, mark and return later.

**Example A — 15-second question (Q4 above, Anamudi):**
This is single-fact recall. If you know "Anamudi = Kerala = highest in South India," answer in under 15 seconds. If you hesitate between Anamudi and Doddabetta, do **not** burn 2 minutes — flag it. Extra time will not create the memory.

**Example B — 45-second question (Q2 above, NOT a tributary of Ganga):**
Negative-phrasing questions deserve the full 45 seconds: (1) underline "NOT" mentally, (2) verify each option one by one — Gandak ✓ Ganga, Kosi ✓ Ganga, Son ✓ Ganga, Teesta ✗ (Brahmaputra), (3) only then mark. Rushing a NOT question in 10 seconds is the single biggest cause of avoidable errors in this chapter.

---

## 5. Preparation Takeaways

1. Master **peak–range**, **pass–state**, **river–origin**, **channel–island** and **soil–crop** matching tables — these five cover most questions from this chapter.
2. Practise reading every question twice for the words **NOT / EXCEPT / INCORRECT**.
3. For sequence questions, fix two anchor points (e.g., westernmost = Nanga Parbat, easternmost = Kangchenjunga) and eliminate options around them.
4. Revise the delta–estuary, Himalayan division, and monsoon-mechanism concepts — exams repeatedly convert these into statement questions.
