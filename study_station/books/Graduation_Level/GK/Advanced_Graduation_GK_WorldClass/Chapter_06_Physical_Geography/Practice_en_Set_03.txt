51. Which of the following statements about the Deccan Plateau is correct?
(a) It slopes from west to east (b) It is composed mainly of alluvial deposits (c) It is bounded by the Satpura range in the south (d) Its highest peak is Anamudi
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims the Satpura bounds the plateau in the south, but the Satpura lies to the north of the Deccan Plateau; the southern boundary is formed by the Western and Eastern Ghats. The plateau is made of ancient crystalline and volcanic (Deccan Trap) rocks, not alluvium, and Anamudi is in the Western Ghats, not the plateau proper. Rivers like the Godavari and Krishna flow eastward, proving the general slope is from west to east, so the answer is "It slopes from west to east."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

52. The Barren Island, India's only active volcano, is located in which of the following?
(a) Palk Strait (b) Lakshadweep Sea (c) Gulf of Mannar (d) Andaman Sea
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students confuse Barren Island with the coral islands of Lakshadweep, but Lakshadweep islands are coral in origin, not volcanic. Barren Island lies in the Andaman Sea, east of the Andaman group of islands, and has erupted several times in recent decades. Therefore, the answer is the Andaman Sea.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

53. Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bengal"?
(a) Damodar (b) Hooghly (c) Kosi (d) Teesta
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Kosi is a common trap here because it is called the "Sorrow of Bihar," not Bengal. The Damodar earned the title "Sorrow of Bengal" due to its devastating floods in the West Bengal plains before the Damodar Valley Corporation dams were built. Hence, the answer is Damodar.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

54. The Nanda Devi peak is situated in which part of the Himalayas?
(a) Kumaon Himalayas (b) Nepal Himalayas (c) Kashmir Himalayas (d) Assam Himalayas
Answer: (a)
Solution: Students often place Nanda Devi in the Kashmir Himalayas because it is India's second-highest peak, but it actually lies in Uttarakhand, which falls in the Kumaon Himalayas region between the Satluj and Kali rivers. The Nepal Himalayas contain Everest and Kanchenjunga lies on the Nepal–Sikkim border. Thus, the answer is Kumaon Himalayas.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

55. Which of the following pairs of rivers and their origin is correctly matched?
(a) Godavari — Amarkantak (b) Mahanadi — Sihawa (Chhattisgarh) (c) Narmada — Trimbakeshwar (d) Krishna — Nasik
Answer: (b)
Solution: The options deliberately swap origins: the Godavari rises at Trimbakeshwar near Nasik, and the Narmada rises at Amarkantak, so both those pairs are reversed. The Krishna rises near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra, not Nasik. The Mahanadi rises near Sihawa in the Raipur district of Chhattisgarh, so the answer is "Mahanadi — Sihawa (Chhattisgarh)."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

56. The "Duns" are associated with which of the following?
(a) Sand dunes of the Thar desert (b) Glacial lakes of the Greater Himalaya (c) Deltaic plains of peninsular rivers (d) Longitudinal valleys between the Lesser Himalaya and Shiwaliks
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is confusing "Duns" with sand "dunes" of the Thar because the words sound similar. Duns are longitudinal valleys such as Dehradun, Kotli Dun and Patli Dun, lying between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwalik ranges, formed by Himalayan rivers. Therefore, the answer is "Longitudinal valleys between the Lesser Himalaya and Shiwaliks."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

57. Which of the following statements about the Western Coastal Plain is correct?
(a) It is a submerged coastal plain (b) It has well-developed deltas of major rivers (c) It is wider than the Eastern Coastal Plain (d) It extends from Kanyakumari to the Sundarbans
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims it is wider than the Eastern Coastal Plain, but in fact the Western Coastal Plain is narrow while the Eastern Coastal Plain is broader and has large deltas of the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri. The Western Coastal Plain is a submerged plain, which is why it provides natural conditions for ports and harbours. Hence, the answer is "It is a submerged coastal plain."

58. The Guru Shikhar peak, the highest point of the Aravalli range, is located in which state?
(a) Rajasthan (b) Gujarat (c) Haryana (d) Madhya Pradesh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Although the Aravalli range passes through Gujarat, Rajasthan, Haryana and Delhi, its highest peak Guru Shikhar (1,722 m) is on Mount Abu in Rajasthan. Students sometimes pick Gujarat because the range begins there, but the answer is Rajasthan.

59. Which river forms the boundary between the Central Highlands and the Deccan Plateau?
(a) Chambal (b) Son (c) Betwa (d) Narmada
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Chambal, Betwa and Son are tributaries that flow within the northern plains or highlands, but the Narmada, flowing westward through a rift valley, clearly demarcates the Central Highlands to its north from the Deccan Plateau to its south. Therefore, the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

60. The Loktak Lake, famous for its floating phumdis, is located in which state?
(a) Meghalaya (b) Assam (c) Manipur (d) Nagaland
Answer: (c)
Solution: Assam is a tempting choice because of its many wetlands, but Loktak Lake — the largest freshwater lake in Northeast India and home to the Keibul Lamjao National Park — lies in Manipur. Hence, the answer is Manipur.

61. Arrange the following Himalayan peaks from west to east: 1. Nanga Parbat 2. Nanda Devi 3. Kanchenjunga 4. Namcha Barwa
(a) 1, 3, 2, 4 (b) 2, 1, 3, 4 (c) 1, 2, 3, 4 (d) 2, 3, 1, 4
Answer: (c)
Solution: This is a spatial-order trap: students often swap Nanda Devi and Nanga Parbat. Nanga Parbat is in the westernmost Kashmir Himalaya, Nanda Devi in the Kumaon Himalaya, Kanchenjunga on the Nepal–Sikkim border, and Namcha Barwa at the eastern syntaxial bend in Arunachal Pradesh. The correct west-to-east order is 1, 2, 3, 4, so the answer is "1, 2, 3, 4."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

62. Which of the following is the largest brackish water lagoon in India?
(a) Pulicat Lake (b) Chilika Lake (c) Vembanad Lake (d) Kolleru Lake
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vembanad is a trap because it is the longest lake in India, but it is a freshwater-to-brackish lake of Kerala, not the largest brackish lagoon. Chilika Lake in Odisha, at the mouth of the Daya river, is India's largest brackish water lagoon and a Ramsar site. Thus, the answer is Chilika Lake.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

63. The Peninsular Plateau is composed mainly of which type of rocks?
(a) Sedimentary rocks (b) Igneous and metamorphic rocks (c) Alluvial deposits (d) Coral limestone
Answer: (b)
Solution: Sedimentary rocks are a tempting answer because they are widespread in India, but they dominate the northern plains and Himalayan foothills, not the plateau. The Peninsular Plateau is one of the oldest landmasses, made of ancient crystalline, igneous and metamorphic rocks, with the Deccan Trap basalt in its northwest. Therefore, the answer is "Igneous and metamorphic rocks."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

64. Which of the following rivers does NOT form a delta?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Godavari (c) Narmada (d) Krishna
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting trap is to think all large rivers form deltas, but east-flowing rivers like the Mahanadi, Godavari and Krishna form deltas on the Bay of Bengal coast, while the west-flowing Narmada forms an estuary because it flows through a rift valley with a steep gradient and tidal influence. Hence, the river that does not form a delta is the Narmada, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

65. The "Karewas" of Kashmir are famous for the cultivation of which crop?
(a) Walnut (b) Apple (c) Saffron (d) Barley
Answer: (c)
Solution: Apple and walnut are also grown in Kashmir, which makes them tempting options, but karewas — the lacustrine (lake-deposited) terraces of the Kashmir Valley — are specifically renowned for zafran (saffron) cultivation. Therefore, the answer is Saffron.

66. Which pass connects the Kashmir Valley with the Ladakh region?
(a) Banihal (b) Nathu La (c) Shipki La (d) Zoji La
Answer: (d)
Solution: Banihal is the trap because it also concerns Kashmir, but the Banihal pass connects the Kashmir Valley with the Jammu region (outer Himalaya), not Ladakh. Zoji La, on the Srinagar–Leh highway, links the Kashmir Valley with Ladakh. Hence, the answer is Zoji La.

67. The Malwa Plateau is drained mainly by which river system?
(a) Narmada (b) Chambal (c) Tapi (d) Mahi
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Narmada is tempting because it borders the Malwa Plateau to the south, but it flows in a rift valley and does not drain the plateau surface. The Chambal, along with its tributaries like the Kali Sindh and Parbati, drains most of the Malwa Plateau northward into the Yamuna system. Thus, the answer is Chambal.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

68. Which of the following statements about the Shiwalik range is correct?
(a) It is the oldest and highest range of the Himalayas (b) It contains the highest Himalayan peaks (c) It is composed of unconsolidated sediments brought by rivers (d) It lies north of the Lesser Himalaya
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is calling it the oldest and highest range — that description fits the Greater Himalaya (Himadri), which contains the highest peaks. The Shiwaliks are the youngest and outermost range, lying south of the Lesser Himalaya, and are made of unconsolidated gravel and alluvium deposited by rivers descending from the higher ranges. Therefore, the answer is "It is composed of unconsolidated sediments brought by rivers."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

69. The Kaveri river originates at which of the following places?
(a) Talakaveri (Brahmagiri hills) (b) Nasik (c) Amarkantak (d) Mahabaleshwar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mahabaleshwar is a tempting wrong option because the Krishna originates there, and students often confuse the two major peninsular rivers. The Kaveri rises at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of the Western Ghats in Kodagu district, Karnataka. Hence, the answer is Talakaveri (Brahmagiri hills).

70. Which island group of India is of coral origin?
(a) New Moore Island (b) Nicobar Islands (c) Andaman Islands (d) Lakshadweep Islands
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are a trap because they are also island groups, but they are the elevated portions of submarine mountains, not coral formations. The Lakshadweep Islands in the Arabian Sea are built of coral deposits (atolls and reefs). Therefore, the answer is Lakshadweep Islands.

71. The "Bhangar" and "Khadar" refer to which of the following?
(a) Coastal landforms of Kerala (b) Old and new alluvium of the northern plains (c) Hill ranges of the northeast (d) Two types of black soil
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is associating these terms with black soil, but black soil types are called regur soil, not bhangar or khadar. Bhangar is the older alluvium lying above the flood level (often with kankar nodules), while khadar is the newer alluvium renewed by annual floods in the floodplains. Hence, the answer is "Old and new alluvium of the northern plains."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

72. Which of the following is the correct sequence of physiographic divisions from north to south?
(a) Northern Plains — Peninsular Plateau — Himalayas — Coastal Plains (b) Northern Plains — Himalayas — Peninsular Plateau — Coastal Plains (c) Himalayas — Peninsular Plateau — Northern Plains — Coastal Plains (d) Himalayas — Northern Plains — Peninsular Plateau — Coastal Plains
Answer: (d)
Solution: This is a sequence trap: placing the Northern Plains north of the Himalayas is a common error, but the plains lie south of the mountains, formed by their rivers. The correct north-to-south order is the Himalayas, then the Northern Plains, then the Peninsular Plateau, and finally the Coastal Plains and islands. Therefore, the answer is "Himalayas — Northern Plains — Peninsular Plateau — Coastal Plains."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

73. The Tapi river flows through a rift valley between which two ranges?
(a) Vindhya and Satpura (b) Satpura and Ajanta (c) Aravalli and Vindhya (d) Western Ghats and Eastern Ghats
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Vindhya–Satpura pair is the trap because the Narmada flows between these two ranges, and students mix up the two rift-valley rivers. The Tapi flows in a rift valley between the Satpura range to its north and the Ajanta range to its south. Hence, the answer is "Satpura and Ajanta."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

74. Which of the following statements about the Eastern Ghats is correct?
(a) They are higher and more continuous than the Western Ghats (b) They run parallel to the Arabian Sea coast (c) They are discontinuous and dissected by rivers (d) Their highest peak is Anamudi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims they are higher and more continuous than the Western Ghats, but the reverse is true — the Western Ghats are higher and continuous, and Anamudi (their highest peak) is in the Western Ghats, not the Eastern Ghats. The Eastern Ghats run along the Bay of Bengal coast and are broken into discontinuous blocks by the Mahanadi, Godavari and Krishna. Therefore, the answer is "They are discontinuous and dissected by rivers."
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

75. The Brahmaputra is known as the "Tsangpo" in which region?
(a) Tibet (b) Arunachal Pradesh (c) Bangladesh (d) Assam
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap options are the river's other local names: in Arunachal Pradesh it is called the Dihang (Siang), and in Bangladesh it is known as the Jamuna. The Brahmaputra originates near Lake Mansarovar and flows eastward in Tibet under the name Tsangpo before taking a U-turn at Namcha Barwa. Hence, the answer is Tibet.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)
