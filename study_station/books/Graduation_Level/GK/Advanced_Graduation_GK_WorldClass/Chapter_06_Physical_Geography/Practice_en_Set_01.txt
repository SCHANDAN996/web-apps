1. Which is the highest peak of the Himalayas located entirely within India?
(a) Kanchenjunga (b) Mount Everest (c) Nanda Devi (d) Kamet
Answer: (c)
Solution: Kanchenjunga is higher but lies on the India–Nepal border, so it is not entirely within India — that is the trap. Nanda Devi (7,816 m) in Uttarakhand lies completely inside Indian territory, so the answer is Nanda Devi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

2. The Himalayas were formed due to the collision of which two plates?
(a) Indian Plate and Eurasian Plate (b) Indian Plate and African Plate (c) Eurasian Plate and Pacific Plate (d) Indian Plate and Australian Plate
Answer: (a)
Solution: The northward-moving Indian Plate collided with the Eurasian Plate, folding the sediments of the Tethys Sea into the Himalayas. The African and Pacific plates were not involved, so the answer is Indian Plate and Eurasian Plate.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

3. Which of the following is the oldest landmass of India?
(a) The Himalayas (b) The Peninsular Plateau (c) The Indo-Gangetic Plain (d) The Coastal Plains
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Himalayas are young fold mountains and the plains are recent alluvial deposits, so both are geologically young. The Peninsular Plateau is part of the ancient Gondwana landmass, so the answer is the Peninsular Plateau.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India)

4. The Deccan Trap was formed by which geological activity?
(a) Volcanic eruptions (b) Folding (c) Glacial deposition (d) River erosion
Answer: (a)
Solution: Folding created the Himalayas, not the Deccan. The Deccan Trap is a vast basaltic region formed by fissure-type volcanic eruptions around 66 million years ago, so the answer is volcanic eruptions.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

5. Which river flows through a rift valley?
(a) Ganga (b) Yamuna (c) Godavari (d) Narmada
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Ganga and Yamuna flow through alluvial plains, not rift valleys. The Narmada flows westward through a rift valley between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges, so the answer is Narmada.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

6. The northernmost range of the Himalayas is known as:
(a) Himachal (b) Himadri (c) Shiwalik (d) Purvanchal
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Shiwalik is the southernmost range, which is the common confusion. The Himadri (Greater Himalaya) is the northernmost and highest range, so the answer is Himadri.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India)

7. Which of the following hills is NOT part of the Purvanchal (Eastern Hills)?
(a) Patkai Bum (b) Nilgiri Hills (c) Mizo Hills (d) Naga Hills
Answer: (b)
Solution: Patkai Bum, Naga Hills and Mizo Hills all lie in the north-east and form the Purvanchal. The Nilgiri Hills are in the south where the Eastern and Western Ghats meet, so the answer is Nilgiri Hills.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India)

8. The Malwa Plateau is bounded by which mountain range to the south?
(a) Aravalli (b) Satpura (c) Vindhya (d) Western Ghats
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Aravalli lies to the north-west of Malwa, not the south. The Malwa Plateau is bounded by the Vindhya Range to the south, so the answer is Vindhya.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

9. Which is the largest physiographic division of India by area?
(a) The Himalayan Mountains (b) The Northern Plains (c) The Coastal Plains (d) The Peninsular Plateau
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Himalayas and plains are large but smaller in extent. The Peninsular Plateau, covering roughly 16 lakh sq km, is the largest physiographic division, so the answer is the Peninsular Plateau.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

10. The Guru Shikhar peak is located in which range?
(a) Vindhya (b) Satpura (c) Aravalli (d) Nilgiri
Answer: (c)
Solution: Dhupgarh is the highest peak of the Satpura, which students often confuse here. Guru Shikhar (1,722 m) on Mount Abu is the highest peak of the Aravalli Range, so the answer is Aravalli.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

11. Which plain is formed by the deposition of alluvium brought by the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra?
(a) Northern Plain (b) Deccan Plain (c) Coastal Plain (d) Malwa Plain
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Deccan and Malwa are plateaus, not alluvial plains. The Great Northern Plain was formed by alluvium deposited by the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra systems, so the answer is Northern Plain.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India)

12. The 'Bhabar' belt of the Northern Plain is characterised by:
(a) Pebbles and disappearing streams (b) Fine alluvium and fertile soil (c) Swampy marshy land (d) Older alluvium with calcareous deposits
Answer: (a)
Solution: Swampy land describes the Terai, which lies south of Bhabar — a frequent trap. In the Bhabar belt, streams deposit pebbles and disappear underground, so the answer is pebbles and disappearing streams.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

13. Which of the following is a west-flowing river of India?
(a) Mahanadi (b) Kaveri (c) Krishna (d) Tapi
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mahanadi, Kaveri and Krishna all flow eastward into the Bay of Bengal. The Tapi (Tapti) flows westward through a rift valley into the Arabian Sea, so the answer is Tapi.
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India)

14. The Lakshadweep Islands are of which origin?
(a) Volcanic (b) Coral (c) Tectonic (d) Alluvial
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are partly of volcanic/tectonic origin, which is the tempting confusion. The Lakshadweep Islands are coral atolls built by coral polyps, so the answer is coral.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

15. Which pass connects Srinagar with Leh?
(a) Shipki La (b) Zoji La (c) Nathu La (d) Bara Lacha La
Answer: (b)
Solution: Nathu La connects Sikkim with Tibet, not Srinagar with Leh. Zoji La lies on the Srinagar–Leh highway in the Zaskar region, so the answer is Zoji La.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

16. The 'Duns' are:
(a) Longitudinal valleys between the Lesser Himalaya and Shiwaliks (b) Glacial lakes of Ladakh (c) Sand dunes of Rajasthan (d) Deep gorges of the Brahmaputra
Answer: (a)
Solution: Sand dunes are a desert feature and have no link with Duns. Duns are longitudinal valleys such as Dehradun and Kotli Dun lying between the Lesser Himalaya and the Shiwaliks, so the answer is longitudinal valleys between the Lesser Himalaya and Shiwaliks.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

17. Which is the highest peak of the Western Ghats?
(a) Doddabetta (b) Mahendragiri (c) Anai Mudi (d) Kalsubai
Answer: (c)
Solution: Doddabetta is the highest peak of the Nilgiri Hills, not the entire Western Ghats — the classic trap. Anai Mudi (2,695 m) in Kerala is the highest peak of the Western Ghats, so the answer is Anai Mudi.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

18. The Eastern Coastal Plain between the Mahanadi and the Krishna is known as:
(a) Coromandel Coast (b) Konkan Coast (c) Northern Circars (d) Malabar Coast
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Coromandel Coast lies further south along Tamil Nadu, which is the common confusion. The stretch between the Mahanadi and Krishna deltas is called the Northern Circars, so the answer is Northern Circars.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

19. Which soil covers the largest area of the Deccan Plateau and is ideal for cotton?
(a) Black soil (b) Red soil (c) Alluvial soil (d) Laterite soil
Answer: (a)
Solution: Alluvial soil dominates the northern plains, not the Deccan. Black (regur) soil, formed from Deccan Trap basalt, retains moisture and is ideal for cotton, so the answer is black soil.
Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography (Contemporary India)

20. The Karakoram Range lies in which part of the Himalayan system?
(a) South of the Greater Himalaya (b) In the Purvanchal (c) Between Himachal and Shiwalik (d) North of the Greater Himalaya
Answer: (d)
Solution: The zone between Himachal and Shiwalik contains the Duns, not the Karakoram. The Karakoram lies to the north of the Greater Himalaya, beyond the Indus, so the answer is north of the Greater Himalaya.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

21. Consider the following statements:
1. The Shiwalik range is composed of unconsolidated sediments brought down by Himalayan rivers.
2. The Shiwaliks are the oldest range of the Himalayas.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Both 1 and 2 (b) 2 only (c) 1 only (d) Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap — the Shiwaliks are the youngest, not the oldest, range of the Himalayas; the Greater Himalaya is the oldest. Statement 1 is correct as the Shiwaliks consist of unconsolidated river-borne sediments, so the answer is 1 only.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

22. Arrange the following hills of the Peninsular region from north to south:
1. Nilgiri Hills
2. Aravalli Hills
3. Satpura Range
4. Cardamom Hills
(a) 3, 2, 1, 4 (b) 2, 3, 1, 4 (c) 2, 3, 4, 1 (d) 3, 2, 4, 1
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap lies in swapping the Aravalli and Satpura — the Aravalli (Rajasthan) lies north of the Satpura (Madhya Pradesh–Maharashtra). The correct north-to-south order is Aravalli, Satpura, Nilgiri, Cardamom, so the answer is 2, 3, 1, 4.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

23. Consider the following statements about the Brahmaputra river system:
1. The Brahmaputra enters India through Arunachal Pradesh under the name Dihang.
2. In Tibet, the Brahmaputra is known as the Tsangpo.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Neither 1 nor 2 (d) Both 1 and 2
Answer: (d)
Solution: Some students wrongly assume Tsangpo refers to the Indus, but the Indus in Tibet is called Singi Khamban (Lion's mouth). The Brahmaputra is the Tsangpo in Tibet and enters India as the Dihang in Arunachal Pradesh, so the answer is both 1 and 2.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Geography (India Physical Environment)

24. Which of the following statements about the Thar Desert is NOT correct?
(a) It lies to the north-west of the Aravalli Range (b) It receives very low rainfall (c) It is an extension of the Peninsular Plateau (d) The Luni is the only major river of this region
Answer: (c)
Solution: The first three statements are all correct descriptions of the Thar, so the trap is to pick one of them. The Thar is part of the Great Indian Desert region of the northern plains' western margin, not an extension of the Peninsular Plateau, so the answer is "It is an extension of the Peninsular Plateau".
Source: NCERT Class 9 Geography (Contemporary India)

25. Match the following peaks with their ranges:
1. K2 — P. Karakoram
2. Kanchenjunga — Q. Greater Himalaya
3. Doddabetta — R. Nilgiri Hills
4. Dhupgarh — S. Satpura
(a) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-S, 4-R (b) 1-Q, 2-P, 3-R, 4-S (c) 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R, 4-S (d) 1-Q, 2-P, 3-S, 4-R
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing K2 in the Greater Himalaya — K2 (8,611 m) actually lies in the Karakoram Range. Kanchenjunga is in the Greater Himalaya, Doddabetta in the Nilgiris and Dhupgarh in the Satpura, so the answer is 1-P, 2-Q, 3-R, 4-S.
