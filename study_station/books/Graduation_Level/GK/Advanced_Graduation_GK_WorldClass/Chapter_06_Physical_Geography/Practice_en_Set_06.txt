126. Consider the following statements about the Barak River:
1. It originates in the Manipur hills.
2. It flows into Bangladesh where it joins the Meghna system.
3. It is a tributary of the Brahmaputra within Indian territory.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is statement 3, which sounds plausible because the Barak flows through Assam like the Brahmaputra, but the Barak is part of the Meghna basin, not the Brahmaputra system. It rises in the Manipur hills, flows through Assam's Barak Valley, and enters Bangladesh to join the Meghna. So the answer is 1 and 2 only.
