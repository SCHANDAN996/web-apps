# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Economic Terms & Concepts

---

**Hook 1 — GDP vs GNP: "P for Place, N for Nation"**
- GD**P** = produced within the **P**lace (geographical boundary of India) — doesn't matter who produces it.
- GN**P** = produced by the **N**ationals (Indian citizens/companies) — doesn't matter where they produce it.
- Formula chain: GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA).

---

**Hook 2 — Repo vs Reverse Repo: "Repo = RBI lends, Reverse = RBI borrows"**
- **Repo** rate: RBI **lends** to banks (banks Repurchase their securities later). Higher repo → costlier loans → less inflation.
- **Reverse Repo**: RBI **borrows** from banks (banks park extra cash with RBI). Higher reverse repo → banks park more → less money in market.
- Trick: "REpo = RBI Earns (interest from banks); REVErse = REVErsed roles."

---

**Hook 3 — Inflation types: "Demand Pulls, Cost Pushes"**
- **Demand-Pull** inflation: too much money chasing too few goods (demand pulls prices UP).
- **Cost-Push** inflation: rising production costs push prices UP (oil, wages, raw materials).
- Remember: "Demand Pulls, Cost Pushes."
- Separate concept (not a type of inflation): the **Phillips curve** shows an inverse relationship between inflation and unemployment — don't let examiners club it with inflation types.

---

**Hook 4 — Monetary vs Fiscal Policy: "M for Money, F for Finance Ministry"**
- **M**onetary policy → RBI controls **M**oney supply (repo, CRR, SLR, OMO).
- **F**iscal policy → **F**inance Ministry controls taxation & government spending (Budget).
- Exam trap: CRR/SLR are RBI tools, NOT government tools.

---

**Hook 5 — CRR vs SLR: "Cash Reserve vs Statutory Liquidity"**
- **CRR**: banks keep **Cash** with RBI (no interest earned).
- **SLR**: banks keep **Liquid** assets (gold, govt securities) with themselves.
- Trick: "CRR goes to RBI's door; SLR stays in bank's own store."

---

**Hook 6 — Direct vs Indirect Tax: "Direct hits your pocket Directly"**
- **Direct tax**: paid directly by the person on whom it's imposed — Income Tax, Corporate Tax. (Wealth Tax was abolished by the Finance Act, 2015, effective from assessment year 2016-17.)
- **Indirect tax**: can be shifted to others — GST, Customs Duty.
- Trick: "Direct = can't Dodge; Indirect = Included in price."

---

**Hook 7 — Balance of Payments: "CAT"**
- **C**urrent Account: trade in goods & services, remittances, gifts.
- **A**ccount of Capital (Capital Account): FDI, FII, loans, external borrowing.
- **T**otal must balance (with errors & omissions + reserves).
- Trick: "Current = daily Chai (trade); Capital = big Capital investments."

---

**Hook 8 — Poverty lines: "Tendulkar & Rangarajan — the Cricket Connection"**
- **Tendulkar** Committee (2009): like Sachin — came FIRST, lower poverty line.
- **Rangarajan** Committee (2014): came later, HIGHER poverty line (more people counted poor).
- Trick: "Sachin batted first; Rangarajan raised the score."

---

**Hook 9 — Types of Unemployment: "DSS"**
- **D**isguised: more people working than needed (Indian farms — looks employed, adds nothing).
- **S**easonal: jobless between seasons (agriculture, tourism).
- **S**tructural: skills don't match available jobs.
- Trick: "Farmers are Disguised, Seasons come and go, Structure needs new Skills."

---

**Hook 10 — GST: "One Nation, One Tax — 101st Amendment, 1 July 2017"**
- GST launched: **1 July 2017** via **101st Constitutional Amendment** (2016).
- Article 246A gave powers; GST Council under Article 279A.
- Trick: "101 dalmatians brought GST on 1/7" (1st of July).

---

**Hook 11 — Inflation measurement: "WPI is Wholesale, CPI pinches the Consumer"**
- **WPI**: wholesale prices, no services, base for producers.
- **CPI**: retail prices, includes services, used by RBI for inflation targeting (4% ± 2%).
- Trick: "RBI watches CPI because Consumers vote, Wholesalers just supply."

---

**Hook 12 — Budget terms: "FRBM = Fiscal Responsibility"**
- **FRBM Act 2003**: targets to cut fiscal & revenue deficit.
- Fiscal Deficit = Total expenditure − Total receipts (excluding borrowings).
- Trick: "Fiscal Deficit = how much the government must Borrow."

---

**Hook 13 — National Income methods: "P-I-E"**
- **P**roduct method (value added)
- **I**ncome method (wages, rent, interest, profit)
- **E**xpenditure method (C + I + G + (X−M))
- Trick: "Bake the national income PIE three ways."

---

**Hook 14 — Money supply: "M1 to M4 — the Wider the Number, the Wider the Net"**
- M1 = currency + demand deposits (most liquid)
- M2 = M1 + post office savings
- M3 = M1 + time deposits (called **Broad Money** — most used)
- M4 = M3 + post office deposits
- Trick: "M3 is the Broad Boss — RBI's favourite."

---

**Hook 15 — Base Year & Index: "2011-12 is the Base Camp"**
- Current base year for GDP/IIP/WPI series: **2011-12**.
- CPI base years differ (2012 for CPI combined).
- Trick: "Base camp 2011-12 — everything is measured from there."

---

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

1. **Committee-year traps**: If a question asks to match 4 committees with years (e.g., Tendulkar, Rangarajan, Lakdawala, Alagh) and you're unsure of even one pair — **skip immediately**. One wrong match usually means a wrong answer, and negative marking costs more than the blank.

2. **"Current" data questions**: Questions asking "What is the current repo rate / CRR / fiscal deficit target?" change every few months. If your preparation data is older than the exam date and you can't verify — **skip rather than guess** between two close numbers (e.g., 6.25% vs 6.50%).

3. **Formula-chain questions with 4 similar options**: If a question asks GNP at factor cost vs GNP at market price with four nearly identical formulas and you can't recall whether depreciation or net indirect tax adjusts first — **skip**. These are designed so that partial memory produces a confident wrong answer.
