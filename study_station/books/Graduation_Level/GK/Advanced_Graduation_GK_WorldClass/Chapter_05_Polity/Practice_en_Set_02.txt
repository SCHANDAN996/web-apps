26. Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the appointment of the Prime Minister?
(a) Article 74 (b) Article 78 (c) Article 76 (d) Article 75
Answer: (d)
Solution: Article 74 deals with the Council of Ministers to aid and advise the President, which is the tempting wrong choice. Article 75 specifically provides that the Prime Minister shall be appointed by the President and other ministers on the PM's advice, so the answer is Article 75.

Answer: (a)
Solution: The joint sitting option is a trap because joint sittings under Article 108 never apply to Money Bills. Under Article 109 a Money Bill can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha, so the answer is the Lok Sabha.
