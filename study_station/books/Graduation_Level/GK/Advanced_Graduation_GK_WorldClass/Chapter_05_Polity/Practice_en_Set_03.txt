Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the impeachment of the President?
(a) Article 56 (b) Article 74 (c) Article 72 (d) Article 61
Answer: (d)
Solution: Article 56 is a tempting trap because it deals with the President's term of office and removal by impeachment in general terms, but the detailed procedure for impeachment is laid down in Article 61. Article 72 covers the pardoning power and Article 74 the Council of Ministers, so the answer is Article 61.

52. The idea of a Concurrent List in the Indian Constitution was borrowed from which country?
(a) Canada (b) Ireland (c) United States (d) Australia
Answer: (d)
Solution: Canada is a common trap because federalism with a strong centre was borrowed from Canada, but the Concurrent List specifically came from Australia. Ireland gave Directive Principles and the US gave fundamental rights and judicial review, so the answer is Australia.

53. Which of the following schedules was added to the Constitution by the 52nd Amendment Act, 1985?
(a) Tenth Schedule (b) Ninth Schedule (c) Eleventh Schedule (d) Twelfth Schedule
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ninth Schedule is a trap because it too was added by an amendment, but it came through the very First Amendment of 1951, not the 52nd. The 52nd Amendment added the Tenth Schedule containing the anti-defection law, so the answer is the Tenth Schedule.

54. The power of the President to promulgate ordinances under Article 123 is subject to which limitation?
(a) Ordinance must be approved by the Supreme Court (b) Ordinance ceases to operate six weeks after the reassembly of Parliament (c) Ordinance can be issued only during a national emergency (d) Ordinance requires prior recommendation of the Council of States
Answer: (b)
Solution: National emergency is a tempting trap because ordinances are often confused with emergency powers, but an ordinance can be issued whenever Parliament is not in session and immediate action is needed. It lapses six weeks after Parliament reassembles unless approved earlier, so the answer is the six-week limit after reassembly.

55. Who among the following presides over a joint sitting of the two Houses of Parliament summoned under Article 108?
(a) The Speaker of the Lok Sabha (b) The Vice-President (c) The President (d) The Chief Justice of India
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Vice-President is a trap because he presides over the Rajya Sabha, but a joint sitting is presided over by the Speaker of the Lok Sabha; in the Speaker's absence the Deputy Speaker presides. The President only summons the joint sitting, so the answer is the Speaker of the Lok Sabha.

56. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 added which Part to the Constitution?
(a) Part IX (b) Part IX-A (c) Part X (d) Part XI
Answer: (a)
Solution: Part IX-A is the classic trap because it was added by the very next amendment, the 74th Amendment of 1992, and deals with municipalities. The 73rd Amendment added Part IX dealing with Panchayats, so the answer is Part IX.

57. Which of the following is NOT a condition for the Parliament to legislate on a subject in the State List under Article 249?
(a) A resolution of the Rajya Sabha (b) Resolution passed by two-thirds of members present and voting (c) Declaration that it is necessary in the national interest (d) Prior consent of at least half of the state legislatures
Answer: (d)
Solution: The two-thirds requirement is a trap that makes students think all listed conditions are real, but Article 249 needs only a Rajya Sabha resolution passed by two-thirds of members present and voting in the national interest. Consent of state legislatures is required under Article 252, a different provision, so the answer is prior consent of state legislatures.

58. The concept of 'procedure established by law' in Article 21 was borrowed from the Constitution of which country?
(a) Japan (b) United States (c) United Kingdom (d) Germany
Answer: (a)
Solution: The United States is the strongest trap because the US follows 'due process of law', which sounds similar but is a different doctrine allowing wider judicial review. The expression 'procedure established by law' was taken from the Japanese Constitution, so the answer is Japan.

59. Under which Article can the Supreme Court issue writs for the enforcement of fundamental rights?
(a) Article 13 (b) Article 32 (c) Article 226 (d) Article 368
Answer: (b)
Solution: Article 226 is a deliberate trap because it also concerns writs, but it empowers High Courts, not the Supreme Court, and covers other legal rights too. Article 32 empowers the Supreme Court and is itself a fundamental right, so the answer is Article 32.

60. The Finance Commission is constituted by the President under which Article of the Constitution?
(a) Article 263 (b) Article 280 (c) Article 292 (d) Article 312
Answer: (b)
Solution: Article 263 is a trap because it also provides for a body constituted by the President, but it creates the Inter-State Council, not the Finance Commission. Article 280 provides for the Finance Commission every five years, so the answer is Article 280.

61. Which of the following statements about the Attorney General of India is correct?
(a) He has the right to speak and take part in proceedings of either House without a right to vote (b) He has the right to vote in Parliament (c) He is a member of the Union Cabinet (d) He is appointed by the Chief Justice of India
Answer: (a)
Solution: The right to vote is a tempting trap because the Attorney General can participate in parliamentary proceedings, but participation does not include voting since he is not a member. He is appointed by the President under Article 76, so the answer is the right to speak and take part without a right to vote.

62. The 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976 added which of the following words to the Preamble?
(a) Democratic, Secular, Integrity (b) Socialist, Secular, Fraternity (c) Socialist, Secular, Integrity (d) Sovereign, Socialist, Republic
Answer: (c)
Solution: Fraternity is a trap because it appears in the Preamble, but it was there from the original 1950 text, not added in 1976. The 42nd Amendment inserted Socialist, Secular and Integrity, so the answer is Socialist, Secular, Integrity.

63. A Money Bill can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha, and its certification as a Money Bill is done by whom?
(a) The President (b) The Speaker of the Lok Sabha (c) The Chairman of the Rajya Sabha (d) The Finance Minister
Answer: (b)
Solution: The President is a trap because a Money Bill requires the President's prior recommendation for introduction, but recommendation is not certification. Under Article 110 the Speaker's decision on whether a bill is a Money Bill is final, so the answer is the Speaker of the Lok Sabha.

64. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Article 352 — Financial Emergency (b) Article 356 — National Emergency (c) Article 360 — Financial Emergency (d) Article 368 — President's Rule
Answer: (c)
Solution: Article 352 is a trap because it is the most famous emergency article, but it deals with National Emergency, not financial emergency. Article 356 covers President's Rule and Article 368 covers amendment, so the answer is Article 360 — Financial Emergency.

65. The minimum age to become a member of the Rajya Sabha is:
(a) 21 years (b) 30 years (c) 25 years (d) 35 years
Answer: (b)
Solution: Twenty-five years is the classic trap because it is the minimum age for the Lok Sabha, and students often apply it to both Houses. The Rajya Sabha requires a minimum age of 30 years under Article 84, so the answer is 30 years.

66. Which committee of Parliament examines the report of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India regarding public undertakings?
(a) Public Accounts Committee (b) Estimates Committee (c) Committee on Government Assurances (d) Committee on Public Undertakings
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Public Accounts Committee is a strong trap because it does examine CAG reports, but only those relating to government departments and appropriation accounts. Reports concerning public undertakings go to the Committee on Public Undertakings, so the answer is the Committee on Public Undertakings.

67. The Fundamental Duties were incorporated into the Constitution on the recommendation of which committee?
(a) Sarkaria Commission (b) Shah Commission (c) Swaran Singh Committee (d) Punchhi Commission
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Sarkaria Commission is a trap because it is a well-known commission, but it examined Centre-State relations, not fundamental duties. The Swaran Singh Committee's recommendations led to the 42nd Amendment inserting Article 51A, so the answer is the Swaran Singh Committee.

68. Which of the following fundamental rights is available only to citizens of India and not to foreigners?
(a) Protection against discrimination under Article 15 (b) Equality before law under Article 14 (c) Right to life and personal liberty under Article 21 (d) Protection in respect of conviction for offences under Article 20
Answer: (a)
Solution: Article 14 is a trap because equality sounds like a citizen-centric right, but it is available to all persons including foreigners. Articles 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30 are restricted to citizens, so the answer is protection against discrimination under Article 15.

69. The Vice-President of India can be removed from office by:
(a) A resolution of the Lok Sabha agreed to by the Rajya Sabha (b) Impeachment by Parliament (c) A resolution of the Rajya Sabha passed by a majority of all its then members and agreed to by the Lok Sabha (d) An order of the President on the advice of the Supreme Court
Answer: (c)
Solution: Impeachment is a trap because the President is removed by impeachment, and students extend the same process to the Vice-President. The Vice-President is removed by a Rajya Sabha resolution passed by an effective majority and agreed to by the Lok Sabha, so the answer is the Rajya Sabha resolution agreed to by the Lok Sabha.

70. Which Article empowers the Parliament to amend the Constitution?
(a) Article 356 (b) Article 360 (c) Article 370 (d) Article 368
Answer: (d)
Solution: Article 370 is a trap because of its fame, but it dealt with the special status of Jammu and Kashmir, not the amendment power. Article 368 lays down the power and procedure of constitutional amendment, so the answer is Article 368.

71. In which case did the Supreme Court propound the 'basic structure' doctrine of the Constitution?
(a) Golaknath v. State of Punjab (b) Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (c) Minerva Mills v. Union of India (d) S.R. Bommai v. Union of India
Answer: (b)
Solution: Golaknath is a trap because it was the earlier case that restricted Parliament's amending power, but it did not propound the basic structure doctrine. That doctrine was laid down in Kesavananda Bharati in 1973, so the answer is Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala.

72. The 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002 is related to:
(a) Anti-defection law (b) Reservation in promotions (c) Right to education as a fundamental right (d) Goods and Services Tax
Answer: (c)
Solution: Reservation in promotions is a trap because it too came through amendments around that period, but those were the 77th and 85th Amendments. The 86th Amendment inserted Article 21A making education a fundamental right for children aged 6 to 14, so the answer is right to education as a fundamental right.

73. Which of the following statements about a caretaker government is correct?
(a) It is a government appointed by the President after dissolution of the Lok Sabha (b) It is a government headed by the Chief Justice of India (c) It is a government formed by the second largest party (d) It is the outgoing government that continues in office until the new government is sworn in
Answer: (d)
Solution: A President-appointed government is a trap because dissolution of the Lok Sabha is involved, but the President does not appoint a fresh caretaker government. The outgoing Council of Ministers simply continues until the new one assumes office, so the answer is the outgoing government continuing until the new government is sworn in.

74. The total number of ministers, including the Prime Minister, in the Council of Ministers shall not exceed what percentage of the total strength of the Lok Sabha?
(a) 15 per cent (b) 12 per cent (c) 10 per cent (d) 20 per cent
Answer: (a)
Solution: Ten per cent is a trap because the same 91st Amendment fixed a 10 per cent limit for some smaller state assemblies, which students confuse with the Union figure. For the Lok Sabha the ceiling under Article 75(1A) is 15 per cent, so the answer is 15 per cent.

75. Which of the following is the correct chronological order of the constitutional amendments?
P. Anti-defection law
Q. Fundamental Duties added
R. Right to property removed from fundamental rights
S. Right to education made a fundamental right
(a) Q, P, R, S (b) R, Q, P, S (c) Q, R, P, S (d) R, P, Q, S
Answer: (c)
Solution: The order R, Q is a trap because both events are associated with the late 1970s, but the 42nd Amendment adding Fundamental Duties came in 1976 while the 44th Amendment removing the right to property came in 1978. The anti-defection law came in 1985 and right to education in 2002, so the answer is Q, R, P, S.
