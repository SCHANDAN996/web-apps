# Key Facts & Figures — Indian Polity (Advanced)

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constitution adopted | 26 November 1949 (Constituent Assembly) | Swapped with 26 January 1950 (enforcement) | Date confusion |
| Constitution enforced | 26 January 1950 | Confused with adoption date | Date confusion |
| Drafting Committee Chairman | Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (7 members, set up 29 Aug 1947) | Confused with Constituent Assembly President (Dr. Rajendra Prasad) | Similar names/roles |
| Constituent Assembly President | Dr. Rajendra Prasad | Confused with Ambedkar | Similar names/roles |
| Constitutional Advisor | B.N. Rau | Confused with Drafting Committee | Role confusion |
| Total Articles (original) | 395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules | Current count (~448 Articles, 25 Parts, 12 Schedules) quoted as original | Number confusion |
| Schedules added later | 9th (1st Amdt 1951), 10th (52nd Amdt 1985), 11th (73rd Amdt 1992), 12th (74th Amdt 1992) | Asked which amendment added which schedule | Chronology trap |
| Preamble source | Based on Objectives Resolution by Nehru (13 Dec 1946) | "Borrowed from USA" — only the idea of Preamble is American | Source confusion |
| 42nd Amendment 1976 | Added "Socialist, Secular, Integrity" to Preamble; called "Mini Constitution" | Words attributed to original Preamble | Chronology trap |
| Preamble amendable | Yes (Kesavananda Bharati 1973), but not basic structure | "Preamble is part of Constitution" — settled in Kesavananda, not Berubari (1960) | Case confusion |
| Fundamental Rights | Part III, Articles 12–35; inspired by USA | Confused with DPSP (Part IV, Ireland) | Source confusion |
| Article 32 | Right to Constitutional Remedies; Ambedkar: "heart and soul" | Confused with Article 226 (High Courts — wider but not FR) | Near-correct trap |
| Article 21 | Right to Life and Personal Liberty | "Absolute right" — wrong; subject to procedure established by law | Absolute-word trap |
| Article 19 | Six freedoms (after 44th Amdt removed property) | Seven freedoms quoted (pre-1978 position) | Chronology trap |
| Right to Property | Now legal right under Article 300A (44th Amdt 1978) | Still listed as Fundamental Right | Outdated-fact trap |
| DPSP | Part IV, Articles 36–51; non-justiciable; from Ireland | "Enforceable by courts" — wrong | Negative-statement trap |
| Fundamental Duties | Article 51A, Part IVA; added by 42nd Amdt 1976 (Swaran Singh Committee); originally 10, now 11 (86th Amdt 2002) | Count quoted as 10 | Number confusion |
| President's election | Article 54; electoral college = elected MPs + elected MLAs of states & UTs (Delhi, Puducherry, J&K) | Nominated members included — wrong | Inclusion trap |
| President's term | 5 years (Art. 56); eligible for re-election | "Fixed till successor" confusion; impeachment under Art. 61 | Process confusion |
| Vice-President | Ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha (Art. 64); elected by both Houses' members (incl. nominated) | Electoral college confused with President's | Inclusion trap |
| PM & Council of Ministers | Art. 74 (aid & advice); 42nd Amdt made advice binding; 44th Amdt allowed one reconsideration | "President must always act on advice" — nuance of 44th Amdt missed | Near-correct trap |
| Total Ministers cap | 15% of Lok Sabha strength (91st Amdt 2003, Art. 75(1A)) | Quoted as 10% or 20% | Number confusion |
| Lok Sabha max strength | 550 (530 states + 20 UTs); Anglo-Indian nomination abolished by 104th Amdt 2020 | 552 quoted (old figure with 2 Anglo-Indians) | Outdated-fact trap |
| Rajya Sabha max strength | 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated by President) | Nominated = 12 from fields of Art. 80 | Number confusion |
| Money Bill | Art. 110; Speaker's decision final (Art. 110(3)); only Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha can amend — wrong; only recommend (14 days) | Process confusion |
| Joint Sitting | Art. 108; summoned by President; presided by Speaker | Not applicable to Money Bills & Constitutional Amendment Bills | Exception trap |
| Constitutional Amendment | Art. 368; some need half the states' ratification | "All amendments need state ratification" — wrong | Absolute-word trap |
| Supreme Court | Art. 124; judges appointed by President; collegium system (NJAC struck down 2015) | "NJAC is current system" — wrong | Outdated-fact trap |
| CJI appointment | President appoints; senior-most judge convention | Confused with collegium details | Process confusion |
| High Court | Art. 214; common HC possible (Art. 231) | Judges' retirement age: HC 62, SC 65 | Number confusion |
| Writs | Art. 32 (SC) & Art. 226 (HC); Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto | Prohibition vs Certiorari (before vs after judgment) | Similar-terms trap |
| CAG | Art. 148; single member; audits Union & States | "CAG audits private companies" — only govt-funded bodies | Scope trap |
| Attorney General | Art. 76; appointed by President; right of audience in all courts | Confused with Solicitor General (statutory, not constitutional) | Similar-roles trap |
| Election Commission | Art. 324; CEC + other ECs; CEC removal like SC judge | "All ECs have same removal protection" — only CEC | Near-correct trap |
| UPSC | Art. 315–323; Chairman/members: 6 years or 65 years | Age confused with SC judge (65) — same number, different body | Number confusion |
| Finance Commission | Art. 280; every 5th year; constituted by President | "Permanent body" — wrong | Nature trap |
| NITI Aayog | 2015, replaced Planning Commission; PM is Chairperson; non-statutory | "Constitutional body" — wrong | Nature trap |
| 73rd Amendment | Panchayati Raj; Part IX; 11th Schedule (29 subjects) | Confused with 74th (Municipalities, 12th Schedule, 18 subjects) | Similar-numbers trap |
| 74th Amendment | Urban local bodies; Part IXA; 12th Schedule | Swapped with 73rd | Similar-numbers trap |
| Emergency (National) | Art. 352; on war/external aggression/armed rebellion (44th Amdt changed "internal disturbance") | "Internal disturbance" still quoted | Outdated-fact trap |
| President's Rule | Art. 356; max 3 years with parliamentary approval every 6 months | Confused with Art. 365 | Article confusion |
| Financial Emergency | Art. 360; never declared | "Declared in 1991" — wrong | Fabricated-fact trap |
| Art. 370 | Special status of J&K; made inoperative Aug 2019; J&K Reorganisation Act 2019 | "Art. 370 abolished by constitutional amendment" — done via Presidential order route | Process confusion |
| Citizenship | Part II, Art. 5–11; Parliament's power under Art. 11 (CAA 1955) | "Citizenship is a Fundamental Right" — wrong | Nature trap |
| Official Language | Hindi in Devanagari (Art. 343); English continued (Official Languages Act 1963) | "National language" — India has none | Terminology trap |
| Amendment of FRs | Parliament can amend, but not basic structure (Kesavananda 1973) | "FRs cannot be amended" — wrong | Absolute-word trap |
| Reservation | Art. 15(4), 16(4); EWS 10% via 103rd Amdt 2019 (Art. 15(6), 16(6)) | EWS counted within 50% ceiling — it is above it | Near-correct trap |
| Anti-Defection | 10th Schedule, 52nd Amdt 1985; 91st Amdt removed split exemption | "1/3rd split allowed" — removed in 2003 | Outdated-fact trap |
| Panchayat tenure | 5 years from first meeting; election within 6 months of dissolution | Confused with Assembly dissolution rules | Process confusion |
| Gram Sabha | Art. 243A; foundation of Panchayati Raj | Confused with Gram Panchayat | Similar-terms trap |

| Topic | Type | Time | Score Maximizer |

| Federalism & Centre-State relations (Lists, Art. 245–263) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Amendment procedure with state ratification cases | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Emergency provisions (352/356/360 comparison) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Parliamentary procedures (bills, motions, privileges) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Basic Structure doctrine evolution | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Statement-based questions (I, II, III correct?) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |

**Strategy:** Attempt all 15-second fact questions first (Articles, amendments, bodies — these are pure recall and high accuracy). Reserve 45-second analytical questions for the second pass. Polity's fact-based share is a Score Maximizer — do not skip direct Article/amendment questions.
