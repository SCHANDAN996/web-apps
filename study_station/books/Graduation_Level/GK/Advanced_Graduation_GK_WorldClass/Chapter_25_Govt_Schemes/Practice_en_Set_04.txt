76. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the overdraft facility available to eligible account holders?
(a) Up to ₹5,000 (b) Up to ₹20,000 (c) Up to ₹15,000 (d) Up to ₹10,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting figure ₹5,000 was the overdraft limit in the scheme's initial phase, which creates chronological confusion; it was later raised. Eligible PMJDY account holders can avail overdraft up to ₹10,000, so the answer is ₹10,000.

77. Which ministry implements the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)?
(a) Ministry of Rural Development (b) Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (c) Ministry of Panchayati Raj (d) Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
Answer: (a)

80. Which of the following statements about the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) is correct?
(a) It guarantees 150 days of employment per individual per year (b) It guarantees 100 days of wage employment per household per year in rural areas (c) It applies only to Below Poverty Line families (d) It guarantees employment only during agricultural off-season
Answer: (b)
Solution: The "150 days" option is a trap — that enhanced figure applies only in specific notified circumstances, not as the general guarantee, and the guarantee is per rural household, not per individual, with no BPL condition. MGNREGA guarantees 100 days of wage employment per rural household per year, so the answer is the first statement.
Source: MGNREGA 2005, Ministry of Rural Development

81. The Stand Up India scheme facilitates bank loans for setting up greenfield enterprises to which categories of borrowers?
(a) Only SC/ST entrepreneurs (b) Only women entrepreneurs (c) All MSME entrepreneurs without restriction (d) SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs
Answer: (d)
Solution: Choosing only one category is the common trap; the scheme covers both. Stand Up India provides loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch, so the answer is SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs.

85. The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) prescribes a maximum premium payable by farmers for Kharif food and oilseed crops of:
(a) 1.5% of the sum insured (b) 2% of the sum insured (c) 5% of the sum insured (d) 3% of the sum insured
Answer: (b)
Solution: The 1.5% figure is the trap — it applies to Rabi crops, not Kharif, and 5% applies to commercial/horticultural crops. For Kharif food and oilseed crops the farmer's premium is capped at 2% of the sum insured, so the answer is 2%.
