51. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), which of the following is a correct feature of a basic savings account opened under the scheme?
(a) No overdraft facility is available to any account holder (b) Overdraft facility of up to ₹10,000 is available to eligible account holders (c) A minimum balance of ₹1,000 is compulsory (d) Accident insurance cover of ₹2 lakh is available on all RuPay cards regardless of usage
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting wrong option claims accident insurance of ₹2 lakh for all cards, but the ₹2 lakh cover applies only to RuPay cards issued on accounts opened after 28 August 2018; earlier accounts carry ₹1 lakh, and card usage conditions apply. PMJDY accounts are zero-balance accounts, and eligible holders can avail overdraft up to ₹10,000, so the answer is overdraft facility of up to ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

52. The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G) provides financial assistance for house construction. What is the unit assistance in plain areas under the revised norms?
(a) ₹1,20,000 (b) ₹1,00,000 (c) ₹70,000 (d) ₹1,50,000

54. Which of the following statements about the PM Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is correct?
(a) It covers only farmers owning less than 2 hectares of land (b) It provides ₹6,000 per month to small and marginal farmers only (c) It provides a one-time grant of ₹6,000 at the time of sowing (d) It provides ₹6,000 per year to all landholding farmer families in three equal instalments

Solution: The tempting wrong option restricts the scheme to farmers below 2 hectares, but the landholding ceiling was removed in June 2019, extending the scheme to all landholding farmer families (subject to exclusion criteria). 56. Consider the following statements about the Atal Pension Yojana (APY):
