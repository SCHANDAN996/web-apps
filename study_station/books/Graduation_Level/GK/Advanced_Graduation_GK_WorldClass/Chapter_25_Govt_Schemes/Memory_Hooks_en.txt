# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Government Schemes & Initiatives

---

## 🧠 Mnemonics (Memory Hooks)

---

**Box 1 — PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat) Cover Amount**

> **"JAY gives you FIVE a day!"**

PM-JAY = **₹5 lakh** per family per year for secondary & tertiary hospitalization. Picture a giant hand giving you a "high five" worth ₹5 lakh every time you enter an empanelled hospital. Launched **23 September 2018** from Ranchi, Jharkhand.

---

**Box 2 — PM-KISAN Installment**

> **"KISAN's 6-2-2-2: Six thousand, three kisses of two!"**

PM-KISAN = **₹6,000/year** in **three equal installments of ₹2,000** each, paid every four months via DBT. Imagine a farmer getting three "kisses" (installments) of ₹2,000 each from the government. Launched **24 February 2019** (Gorakhpur, UP); effective retrospectively from 1 December 2018.

---

**Box 3 — Ujjwala Connection Target**

> **"UJJWALA lit 8 CRORE stoves before 2020!"**

PM Ujjwala Yojana (launched **1 May 2016**, Ballia, UP) originally targeted **8 crore** LPG connections to women of BPL households — later crossed and extended under Ujjwala 2.0. Picture 8 crore blue flames lighting up at once. Deposit-free connection; first refill and stove free under Ujjwala 2.0.

---

**Box 4 — PMAY Deadline Trick**

> **"Housing for ALL by 2022 — the year the promise was due!"**

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban: June 2015; Gramin: restructured from Indira Awaas Yojana in **2016**) targeted "Housing for All" by **2022** (75th year of Independence). Remember: **75 years of freedom = roof for everyone**. PMAY-G unit assistance: ₹1.20 lakh (plains), ₹1.30 lakh (hilly/NE/difficult areas).

---

**Box 5 — MUDRA Loan Categories**

> **"Baby MUDRA grows: SHISHU crawls, KISHORE walks, TARUN runs!"**

PM MUDRA Yojana (April 2015) has three loan slabs:
- **Shishu** — up to ₹50,000 (a baby = shishu)
- **Kishore** — ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh (a teenager)
- **Tarun** — ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh (a young man)

The loan grows exactly like a child grows. (Note: Tarun Plus up to ₹20 lakh was announced in Budget 2024 for successful past borrowers.)

---

**Box 6 — Jan Dhan's Zero Balance Hook**

> **"JAN DHAN opened India's wallet on 28-8-14."**

PM Jan Dhan Yojana launched **28 August 2014** — the world's largest financial inclusion drive. Zero-balance accounts, RuPay debit card, and **₹2 lakh accident insurance** (for accounts opened after 28.08.2018; earlier ₹1 lakh). Hook: "28-8-14 — the day banking reached the last man."

---

**Box 7 — Swachh Bharat Launch Date**

> **"Gandhi's glasses got cleaned on his birthday!"**

Swachh Bharat Mission launched **2 October 2014** — Mahatma Gandhi's birth anniversary — aiming for an Open Defecation Free (ODF) India by **2 October 2019** (Gandhi's 150th birth anniversary). Both dates = Gandhi Jayanti. Impossible to forget once you picture Bapu's spectacles being wiped clean.

---

**Box 8 — Jal Jeevan Mission Target**

> **"HAR GHAR JAL by 2024 — water in every wall!"**

Jal Jeevan Mission (announced **15 August 2019**) aims to provide **Functional Household Tap Connections (FHTC)** to every rural household by **2024**. Hook: "Har Ghar Jal" rhymes with the goal itself — tap water, 55 litres per person per day, to every rural home.

---

**Box 9 — Stand-Up India Composition**

> **"STAND UP: One SC/ST + One Woman per branch — the 1+1 rule!"**

Stand-Up India (launched **5 April 2016**) mandates bank loans of **₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore** to at least **one SC/ST borrower and one woman borrower per bank branch** for greenfield enterprises. Hook: every branch must "stand up" two chairs — one for SC/ST, one for a woman entrepreneur.

---

**Box 10 — Beti Bachao Beti Padhao Launch Place**

> **"BBB was born in PANIPAT — where battles were fought, now for girls!"**

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao launched **22 January 2015** from **Panipat, Haryana** (the state with one of the worst child sex ratios then). Panipat is famous for three historic battles — the fourth battle is for the girl child. Ministries involved: **Women & Child Development + Health + HRD (Education)** — a tri-ministry scheme.

---

**Box 11 — Sukanya Samriddhi Numbers**

> **"SUKANYA: 21 to mature, 8+ to earn, 1.5 lakh to pour!"**

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (under BBBP, January 2015): account for a girl child below **10 years**, matures at **21 years** from opening, max deposit **₹1.5 lakh/year**, deposits for 15 years, and it enjoys **EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt)** tax status. Hook: "A girl's account matures when she's ready for college — 21."

---

**Box 12 — Atal Pension vs. PMJJBY vs. PMSBY (The Insurance Trio)**

> **"ATAL pays you monthly, JEEVAN JYOTI pays on death, SURAKSHA pays on accident — and it costs less than a cup of tea!"**

All three launched **9 May 2015** (Kolkata):
- **APY** — pension ₹1,000–₹5,000/month after 60; entry age 18–40
- **PMJJBY** — ₹2 lakh life cover; premium **₹330/year at launch, revised to ₹436/year from June 2022**; age 18–50
- **PMSBY** — ₹2 lakh accidental death/disability cover; premium **₹12/year at launch, revised to ₹20/year from June 2022**; age 18–70

Hook: "PMSBY = ₹20 — cheaper than a plate of momos for a full year's accident cover!" (Exam tip: if the question asks for the *launch* premium, pick ₹330 / ₹12; if it asks for the *current* premium, pick ₹436 / ₹20.)

---

**Box 13 — Startup India vs. Stand-Up India (The Classic Trap)**

> **"STARTUP = innovation & tax holidays; STAND-UP = loans for SC/ST & women."**

Examiners swap these two constantly. Startup India (**16 January 2016**) = DPIIT recognition, 3-year tax holiday, self-certification. Stand-Up India (**April 2016**) = bank loans ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore for SC/ST/women. Hook: "Start**up** rises on ideas; Stand-**Up** rises the marginalized."

---

**Box 14 — PM SVANidhi for Street Vendors**

> **"SVANidhi = S(treet) V(endor) + Nidhi (fund) — ₹10,000 first, then it doubles and triples!"**

PM SVANidhi (June 2020, post-COVID) gives street vendors collateral-free working capital: **₹10,000 → ₹20,000 → ₹50,000** in successive cycles on timely repayment, plus 7% interest subsidy and cashback on digital transactions. Hook: "10-20-50 — the vendor's ladder."

---

**Box 15 — Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (Free Grain)**

> **"PMGKAY: 5 kg FREE — the pandemic's food shield, now merged into NFSA's free run."**

Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (March 2020) gave **5 kg free foodgrains per person per month** over and above NFSA entitlement to ~80 crore beneficiaries. From **January 2023**, NFSA foodgrains themselves were made free (PMGKAY subsumed). Hook: "5 kg free — the COVID ration everyone remembers."

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

**Tip 1 — The "Current Minister/Secretary" Trap:**
If a question asks who *currently* heads a scheme's ministry or a mission's CEO and you haven't revised current affairs in the last 3 months, **skip immediately**. These change with every cabinet reshuffle — guessing between two plausible names is a pure coin toss with negative marking. Never gamble on names that change.

**Tip 2 — The "Exact Allocation/Outlay" Question:**
If asked for the precise budgetary allocation of a scheme (e.g., "What was the outlay of Scheme X in Budget 20XX-XX?") and you only remember the approximate range, **skip**. Examiners deliberately place options like ₹4,800 cr / ₹5,200 cr / ₹5,800 cr / ₹6,200 cr — all "close" numbers. Unless you recall the exact figure with confidence, the expected value of guessing is negative.

**Tip 3 — The "Four-Statement Match" with One Unknown:**
If a question asks you to match four schemes with their launch years/ministries and you are sure of only two, **don't guess the whole combination**. In match-the-following questions, one unknown pair usually leaves two options alive — a 50-50 with negative marking. Skip unless you can eliminate down to one option. Exception: if even one confirmed mismatch kills three options, answer it — that's a 15-second win, not a gamble.
