26. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the overdraft facility available to eligible account holders?
(a) Up to ₹5,000 (b) Up to ₹10,000 (c) Up to ₹15,000 (d) Up to ₹20,000
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many aspirants pick ₹5,000 because the scheme originally offered an overdraft of ₹5,000 at launch in 2014, but it was later enhanced. The tempting figure is the old limit. The overdraft facility under PMJDY was raised to up to ₹10,000 for eligible account holders, so the answer is ₹10,000.

(a) Rajiv Awas Yojana (b) Valmiki Ambedkar Awas Yojana (c) Indira Awas Yojana (d) Jawahar Rozgar Yojana
