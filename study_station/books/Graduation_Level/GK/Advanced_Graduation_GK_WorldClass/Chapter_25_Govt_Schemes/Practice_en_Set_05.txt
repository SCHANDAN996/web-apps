101. Which ministry is the nodal ministry for the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)?
(a) Ministry of Finance (b) Ministry of Rural Development (c) Ministry of Commerce and Industry (d) NITI Aayog
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting wrong choice is the Ministry of Rural Development, because PMJDY targets the unbanked rural poor, but financial inclusion schemes are administered by the Department of Financial Services under the Ministry of Finance. NITI Aayog is only a policy think tank, not an implementing ministry. So the answer is Ministry of Finance.
Source: PYQ-style

102. Under the Ayushman Bharat – PM Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY), the health cover per family per year is:
(a) ₹5 lakh (b) ₹2 lakh (c) ₹1 lakh (d) ₹10 lakh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many aspirants pick ₹2 lakh by confusing PM-JAY with state-level schemes or older insurance covers like RSBY's ₹30,000. PM-JAY provides a defined cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation. So the answer is ₹5 lakh.
Source: pmjay.gov.in

103. The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) provides eligible farmer families an annual income support of:
(a) ₹4,000 (b) ₹12,000 (c) ₹8,000 (d) ₹6,000
Answer: (d)
Solution: ₹12,000 is a trap arising from doubling the instalment figure; the scheme pays ₹2,000 every four months in three instalments, totalling ₹6,000 per year, transferred directly to bank accounts. So the answer is ₹6,000.
Source: pmkisan.gov.in

104. Which of the following schemes was launched first?
(a) Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (b) Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (c) Stand Up India Scheme (d) PM SVANidhi
Answer: (b)
Solution: This is a chronological-confusion trap: all four are financial-inclusion schemes of the same government. PMJDY was launched in August 2014, Mudra in April 2015, Stand Up India in April 2016, and PM SVANidhi in June 2020. So the answer is Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana.
Source: PYQ-style

105. The Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana classifies loans into Shishu, Kishore and Tarun. The maximum loan under the 'Tarun' category is:
(a) ₹10 lakh (b) ₹5 lakh (c) ₹50,000 (d) ₹20 lakh
Answer: (a)
Solution: ₹5 lakh is the tempting wrong answer because it is the upper limit of the Kishore category, not Tarun. Shishu covers up to ₹50,000, Kishore ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh, and Tarun ₹5,00,001 to ₹10 lakh. So the answer is ₹10 lakh.
Source: mudra.org.in

106. The Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is targeted primarily at workers in the:
(a) Organised sector (b) Central government services (c) Unorganised sector (d) Defence services
Answer: (c)
Solution: The organised sector is the trap because pension schemes are usually associated with formal employment; APY was specifically designed in 2015 to bring unorganised-sector workers into the pension net, guaranteeing a fixed pension of ₹1,000–₹5,000 per month after age 60. So the answer is the unorganised sector.
Source: PYQ-style

107. Under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin), the unit assistance for house construction in plain areas is:
(a) ₹1.00 lakh (b) ₹1.50 lakh (c) ₹1.30 lakh (d) ₹1.20 lakh
Answer: (d)
Solution: ₹1.30 lakh is the trap — it is the assistance for hilly/difficult areas and IAP districts, not plain areas. Plain-area assistance under PMAY-G is ₹1.20 lakh per unit. So the answer is ₹1.20 lakh.
Source: pmayg.nic.in

108. The 'One Nation One Ration Card' scheme is implemented under which Act/framework?
(a) National Food Security Act, 2013 (b) Essential Commodities Act, 1955 (c) Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (d) Public Distribution System (Control) Order, 2001
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Essential Commodities Act is a tempting distractor since it governs PDS commodities generally, but portability of ration cards under One Nation One Ration Card operates within the National Food Security Act, 2013 framework, allowing beneficiaries to lift entitlements anywhere in India. So the answer is the National Food Security Act, 2013.
Source: nfsa.gov.in

109. The PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) provides free foodgrains at the rate of:
(a) 3 kg per person per month (b) 5 kg per person per month (c) 7 kg per family per month (d) 10 kg per family per month
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the family-based option, because NFSA entitlement is often quoted as 35 kg per Antyodaya family; PMGKAY's free entitlement is 5 kg per person per month to NFSA beneficiaries. So the answer is 5 kg per person per month.
Source: nfsa.gov.in

110. Which scheme aims to provide tap water connection to every rural household?
(a) Atal Bhujal Yojana (b) Jal Jeevan Mission (c) Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (d) Namami Gange Programme
Answer: (b)
Solution: Atal Bhujal Yojana is the trap — it deals with groundwater management in select water-stressed areas, not household tap connections. The Jal Jeevan Mission (2019) aims at Functional Household Tap Connections to every rural household. So the answer is Jal Jeevan Mission.
Source: jaljeevanmission.gov.in

111. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana is associated with:
(a) Free electricity connections (b) Solar lanterns to students (c) LPG connections to women of poor households (d) CNG supply to cities
Answer: (c)
Solution: Free electricity connections is the trap, describing the Saubhagya scheme, which is frequently confused with Ujjwala because both are household-energy schemes. Ujjwala (2016) provides deposit-free LPG connections to adult women of poor households. So the answer is LPG connections to women of poor households.
Source: pmuy.gov.in

112. The 'Stand Up India' scheme mandates bank loans for greenfield enterprises to at least one borrower from SC/ST and one woman per bank branch, in the range of:
(a) ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh (b) ₹1 crore to ₹10 crore (c) ₹50 lakh to ₹5 crore (d) ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore
Answer: (d)
Solution: ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh is the trap, overlapping with Mudra's range; Stand Up India specifically covers composite loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs setting up greenfield enterprises. So the answer is ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore.

113. The National Health Authority (NHA) is the implementing agency for:
(a) PM-KISAN (b) PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat) (c) PMAY-Urban (d) PM POSHAN
Answer: (b)
Solution: The other options are traps from different sectors: PM-KISAN is run by the Ministry of Agriculture, PMAY-Urban by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, and PM POSHAN by the Ministry of Education. The National Health Authority implements Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY. So the answer is PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat).

114. The PM SVANidhi scheme, launched in 2020, provides collateral-free working capital loans to:
(a) Small farmers (b) Street vendors (c) Handloom weavers (d) Self-help groups
Answer: (b)
Solution: Self-help groups is the tempting wrong answer because micro-loans are commonly associated with SHGs under DAY-NRLM; PM SVANidhi is specifically for street vendors affected by the COVID-19 lockdown, offering an initial working capital loan of ₹10,000. So the answer is street vendors.
Source: pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in

115. Which of the following correctly pairs the scheme with its launch year?
(a) Digital India – 2015 (b) Swachh Bharat Mission – 2016 (c) Skill India – 2014 (d) Make in India – 2013
Answer: (a)
Solution: This is a chronological trap: Swachh Bharat Mission was launched in 2014 (not 2016), Skill India's National Skill Development Mission came in 2015 (not 2014), and Make in India in September 2014 (not 2013). Digital India was launched on 1 July 2015. So the answer is Digital India – 2015.

116. The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) fixes the maximum premium payable by farmers for Kharif food and oilseed crops at:
(a) 2% of sum insured (b) 1.5% of sum insured (c) 5% of sum insured (d) 10% of sum insured
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1.5% is the trap — that is the rate for Rabi crops, not Kharif. Under PMFBY, farmers pay a maximum of 2% of the sum insured for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5% for commercial/horticultural crops. So the answer is 2% of sum insured.
Source: pmfby.gov.in

117. The 'Beti Bachao Beti Padhao' scheme is a joint initiative of which ministries?
(a) Ministry of Home Affairs, Ministry of Law, Ministry of Social Justice (b) Ministry of Women and Child Development, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Ministry of Education (c) Ministry of Rural Development, Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Ministry of Tribal Affairs (d) Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Labour, Ministry of Skill Development
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap options list plausible social-sector ministries, but BBBP (2015) is a tri-ministerial initiative of the Ministry of Women and Child Development with the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and the then Ministry of Human Resource Development (now Ministry of Education), addressing declining child sex ratio and girls' education. So the answer is the WCD, Health and Education ministries.
Source: wcd.nic.in

118. Under the National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM), the core institutional platform for poor rural women is the:
(a) Gram Sabha (b) Cooperative Society (c) Self-Help Group (d) Producer Company
Answer: (c)
Solution: Producer Company is the trap, since NRLM does promote producer collectives at a later stage; however, the foundational institution of DAY-NRLM is the Self-Help Group of rural poor women, federated into village and cluster-level organisations. So the answer is Self-Help Group.

119. The 'PM POSHAN' scheme (formerly Mid-Day Meal) covers children studying in:
(a) Classes 1 to 5 only (b) Classes 1 to 8 in government and government-aided schools (c) Classes 1 to 10 in all private schools (d) Classes 6 to 12 in government schools
Answer: (b)
Solution: Classes 1 to 5 only is the trap, reflecting the scheme's original 1995 coverage before extension to upper primary. PM POSHAN provides hot cooked meals to children of Classes 1 to 8 (Bal Vatika/pre-primary children were also included) in government and government-aided schools. So the answer is Classes 1 to 8 in government and government-aided schools.
Source: education.gov.in

120. The Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme, announced from 2020 onwards, primarily aims to:
(a) Subsidise farm exports (b) Fund state infrastructure projects (c) Provide income support to artisans (d) Boost domestic manufacturing in identified sectors
Answer: (d)
Solution: Subsidising farm exports is the trap, as PLI sounds like a trade-subsidy scheme; in fact it offers incentives on incremental sales of goods manufactured in India across identified sectors such as electronics, pharmaceuticals and automobiles, to boost domestic manufacturing. So the answer is boosting domestic manufacturing in identified sectors.

121. Consider the following statements about the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana:
1. Accounts opened under it carry zero-balance facility.
2. It provides an overdraft facility of up to ₹10,000 to eligible account holders.
3. The RuPay debit card issued under it carries an inbuilt accident insurance cover.
Which of the above statements are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

123. Which of the following statements about the Atal Pension Yojana is NOT correct?
(a) It is open to Indian citizens aged 18 to 40 years (b) It guarantees a minimum monthly pension between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000 after age 60 (c) It is administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (d) It is open to income-tax payers without any restriction
Answer: (d)
Solution: The first three statements are all correct features, which tempts candidates to pick one of them as the wrong one. So the answer is the statement about income-tax payers.

(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

125. Which of the following statements about the National Education Policy 2020's related initiatives is correct?
(a) NEP 2020 replaced the 10+2 structure with 5+3+3+2 only in private schools (b) PM SHRI schools are exclusively for higher education (c) NIPUN Bharat is a mission for foundational literacy and numeracy under Samagra Shiksha (d) Academic Bank of Credits applies only to school education
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap options reverse the facts: PM SHRI schools are school-level exemplar institutions, the 5+3+3+4 structure applies across schools (and the option misquotes it as 5+3+3+2), and the Academic Bank of Credits applies to higher education, not school education. NIPUN Bharat (2021) is indeed the national mission on foundational literacy and numeracy under Samagra Shiksha. So the answer is the statement about NIPUN Bharat.
Source: education.gov.in
