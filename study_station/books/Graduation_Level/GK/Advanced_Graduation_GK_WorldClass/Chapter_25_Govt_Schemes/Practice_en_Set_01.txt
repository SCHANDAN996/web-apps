1. Under the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the overdraft facility available to eligible account holders?
(a) Up to ₹10,000 (b) Up to ₹5,000 (c) Up to ₹15,000 (d) Up to ₹20,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many aspirants pick ₹5,000 because the scheme originally offered an overdraft of ₹5,000 at launch in 2014; it was later doubled. The current overdraft limit under PMJDY is up to ₹10,000, so the answer is ₹10,000.
Source: PYQ-style

2. The Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana was launched on which date?
(a) 28 August 2014 (b) 15 August 2014 (c) 2 October 2014 (d) 26 January 2015
Answer: (a)
Solution: The scheme was announced in the Independence Day speech of 2014, which tempts many to choose 15 August, but the formal launch was on 28 August 2014. So the answer is 28 August 2014.
Source: PYQ-style

3. Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) provides a health cover of how much per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation?
(a) ₹1 lakh (b) ₹5 lakh (c) ₹2 lakh (d) ₹10 lakh
Answer: (b)
Solution: The figure of ₹1 lakh belongs to some state-level insurance schemes and confuses aspirants. PM-JAY provides a cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year, so the answer is ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

4. Which scheme aims to provide a pucca house with basic amenities to all rural households by the target year 2024 (extended)?
(a) Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (b) Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (c) Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gram Jyoti Yojana (d) Swachh Bharat Mission – Gramin
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Gram Sadak Yojana deals with rural roads, not housing, which is the common confusion. Housing for the rural poor is the goal of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin, so the answer is Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin.
Source: PYQ-style

5. The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) provides income support of how much per year to eligible farmer families?
(a) ₹4,000 (b) ₹8,000 (c) ₹6,000 (d) ₹10,000
Answer: (c)
Solution: The instalment of ₹2,000 sometimes misleads aspirants into picking ₹4,000 or ₹8,000. PM-KISAN gives ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000, so the answer is ₹6,000.
Source: PYQ-style

6. The Swachh Bharat Mission was launched on the birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi in which year?
(a) 2014 (b) 2012 (c) 2015 (d) 2016
Answer: (a)
Solution: The mission was launched on 2 October 2014, Gandhi Jayanti. The year 2015 is tempting because many targets were set for later years, but the launch year is 2014, so the answer is 2014.
Source: PYQ-style

7. Which ministry implements the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS)?
(a) Ministry of Rural Development (b) Ministry of Labour and Employment (c) Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (d) Ministry of Panchayati Raj
Answer: (a)
Solution: Because it is an employment scheme, the Ministry of Labour and Employment is the tempting wrong choice. MGNREGS is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development, so the answer is Ministry of Rural Development.
Source: PYQ-style

8. Under MGNREGA, the guaranteed wage employment per household per financial year is:
(a) 50 days (b) 75 days (c) 150 days (d) 100 days
Answer: (d)
Solution: The figure of 150 days applies only in certain notified drought-affected areas as an additional entitlement, which confuses aspirants. The standard guarantee is 100 days per household per year, so the answer is 100 days.
Source: PYQ-style

9. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is primarily associated with:
(a) Free electricity connections (b) Free piped water supply (c) LPG connections to women from poor households (d) Subsidised fertilisers
Answer: (c)
Solution: Electricity connections belong to the Saubhagya scheme, which is the common mix-up. PMUY provides deposit-free LPG connections to adult women of poor households, so the answer is LPG connections to women from poor households.
Source: PYQ-style

10. The 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' scheme was launched in 2015 from which place?
(a) New Delhi (b) Panipat, Haryana (c) Jaipur, Rajasthan (d) Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
Answer: (b)
Solution: New Delhi is tempting because most national launches happen there. The scheme was launched from Panipat in Haryana, a state with a low child sex ratio, so the answer is Panipat, Haryana.
Source: PYQ-style

11. The Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana provides loans under three categories. Which of the following is NOT one of them?
(a) Shishu (b) Kishore (c) Tarun (d) Yuva
Answer: (d)
Solution: The three categories are Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,001–₹5 lakh) and Tarun (₹5–10 lakh). 'Yuva' is not a Mudra category, so the answer is Yuva.
Source: PYQ-style

12. The 'Digital India' programme was launched in which year?
(a) 2014 (b) 2015 (c) 2016 (d) 2017
Answer: (b)
Solution: Although the government took office in 2014, which tempts that option, the Digital India programme was launched on 1 July 2015. So the answer is 2015.
Source: PYQ-style

13. The Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is primarily targeted at workers in the:
(a) Unorganised sector (b) Organised sector (c) Government sector (d) Defence services
Answer: (a)
Solution: Government employees are covered under the National Pension System, which creates confusion. APY targets unorganised sector workers, guaranteeing a fixed pension after age 60, so the answer is unorganised sector.
Source: PYQ-style

14. The 'Make in India' initiative was launched in which year?
(a) 2016 (b) 2015 (c) 2014 (d) 2018
Answer: (c)
Solution: Make in India was launched on 25 September 2014 to boost manufacturing. The year 2015 is tempting due to Digital India, but the correct year is 2014, so the answer is 2014.
Source: PYQ-style

15. The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is related to:
(a) Life insurance of farmers (b) Livestock insurance (c) Crop insurance (d) Health insurance of farmers
Answer: (c)
Solution: Life insurance is covered under PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana, the tempting confusion. PMFBY is the flagship crop insurance scheme, so the answer is crop insurance.
Source: PYQ-style

16. The 'Stand Up India' scheme facilitates bank loans to:
(a) All salaried employees (b) SC/ST and women entrepreneurs (c) Only large industries (d) Only exporters
Answer: (b)
Solution: The scheme is often confused with Mudra, which is open to all small borrowers. Stand Up India mandates loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and one woman borrower per bank branch, so the answer is SC/ST and women entrepreneurs.
Source: PYQ-style

17. The 'Jal Jeevan Mission' aims to provide:
(a) Free electricity to rural homes (b) Free LPG connections (c) Irrigation to all farmland (d) Functional household tap connections to every rural household
Answer: (d)
Solution: Irrigation schemes fall under PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana, which is the common confusion. Jal Jeevan Mission aims at piped tap water to every rural household, so the answer is functional household tap connections to every rural household.
Source: PYQ-style

18. The 'Skill India' mission is implemented primarily through which scheme?
(a) Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (b) Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (c) Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (d) Mid-Day Meal Scheme
Answer: (b)
Solution: The other options relate to housing, roads and school meals. Skill training under Skill India is delivered mainly through the Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana, so the answer is Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana.
Source: PYQ-style

19. The 'Saubhagya' scheme (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana) is related to:
(a) Rural roads (b) Solar pumps for farmers (c) Street lighting (d) Household electrification
Answer: (d)
Solution: Solar pumps fall under PM-KUSUM, which tempts aspirants. Saubhagya aims at electricity connections to all willing households, so the answer is household electrification.
Source: PYQ-style

20. The 'POSHAN Abhiyaan' (National Nutrition Mission) primarily targets:
(a) Malnutrition among children, adolescents and women (b) School dropout rates (c) Rural unemployment (d) Digital literacy
Answer: (a)
Solution: The word 'Abhiyaan' misleads some into linking it with literacy campaigns. POSHAN Abhiyaan targets reduction of stunting, under-nutrition and anaemia, so the answer is malnutrition among children, adolescents and women.

21. Consider the following statements about PM-JAY:
1. It is completely paperless and cashless at the point of service.
2. It covers pre-existing diseases from day one.
3. It is an entitlement-based scheme with no cap on family size.
Which of the above statements are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1, 2 and 3 (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 2 and 3 only
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is statement 3 — many assume a family-size cap exists because most insurance products have one, but PM-JAY has no restriction on family size or age. All three statements are correct features of the scheme, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PYQ-style

22. Arrange the following schemes in chronological order of their launch:
1. Swachh Bharat Mission
2. Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
3. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana
4. Jal Jeevan Mission
(a) 3, 1, 4, 2 (b) 1, 3, 2, 4 (c) 3, 1, 2, 4 (d) 1, 3, 4, 2
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is placing Swachh Bharat (October 2014) before Jan Dhan (August 2014) because Swachh Bharat is more famous; Jan Dhan actually came first. The correct order is PMJDY (Aug 2014), SBM (Oct 2014), PM-JAY (2018), Jal Jeevan (2019), so the answer is 3, 1, 2, 4.
Source: PYQ-style

23. Under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana, the 'Tarun' category covers loans of:
(a) Up to ₹50,000 (b) ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh (c) ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore (d) ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is the ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore range, which belongs to Stand Up India, not Mudra. Tarun covers loans from ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh, so the answer is ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

24. Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?
(a) PM-KUSUM – Solar energy for farmers (b) PM SVANidhi – Street vendors (c) PM SHRI – Sports infrastructure in rural areas (d) PM Vishwakarma – Traditional artisans and craftspeople
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is that PM SHRI sounds like a welfare scheme but is actually about schools. PM SHRI (PM Schools for Rising India) upgrades schools as model schools, not sports infrastructure, so the answer is PM SHRI – Sports infrastructure in rural areas.
Source: PYQ-style

25. Consider the following statements about the National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM):
1. It is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development.
2. It promotes Self-Help Groups (SHGs) of rural poor women.
3. It was earlier known as the Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana (SGSY).
Which of the above statements are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is statement 3 — aspirants often confuse SGSY with Sampoorna Grameen Rozgar Yojana, but DAY-NRLM is indeed the restructured SGSY. All three statements are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: PYQ-style
