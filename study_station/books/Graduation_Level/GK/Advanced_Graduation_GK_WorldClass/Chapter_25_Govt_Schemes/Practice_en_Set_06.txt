126. Consider the following statements about PM-KISAN:
1. It provides ₹6,000 per year to eligible farmer families in three equal instalments.
2. It is a centrally sponsored scheme with cost shared between Centre and States.
3. Institutional landholders are excluded from the scheme.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (b)
Solution: Statement 2 is the tempting trap: PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme, fully funded by the Government of India, not a centrally sponsored scheme with cost sharing. Statements 1 and 3 are correct — ₹6,000 is paid in three instalments of ₹2,000, and institutional landholders are excluded, so the answer is 1 and 3 only.
Source: PYQ-style

127. With reference to Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY, which of the following is correct?
(a) It covers only families holding BPL cards issued before 2011 (b) It provides health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization (c) It is an insurance scheme where beneficiaries pay an annual premium of ₹2,000 (d) It provides ₹10 lakh per family per year covering only tertiary care
Answer: (b)
Solution: The ₹10 lakh figure is an inflated almost-correct trap; the actual cover is ₹5 lakh per family per year. The scheme is fully government-funded with no beneficiary premium, and eligibility rests on SECC 2011 deprivation criteria, so the answer is health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
Source: PYQ-style

128. Arrange the following schemes in chronological order of their launch:
1. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana
2. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin)
3. Swachh Bharat Mission
4. Atal Pension Yojana
Select the correct order:
(a) 1-3-4-2 (b) 3-1-4-2 (c) 1-4-3-2 (d) 3-4-1-2
Answer: (a)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap lies in swapping Swachh Bharat (2 October 2014) and Jan Dhan (28 August 2014) — Jan Dhan came first. Atal Pension Yojana followed in May 2015, and PMAY-Gramin (restructured from Indira Awaas Yojana) in 2016, so the answer is 1-3-4-2.
Source: PYQ-style

129. Which of the following statements about the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) is NOT correct?
(a) It guarantees 100 days of wage employment per rural household per financial year (b) It provides guaranteed employment to both rural and urban households (c) It initially covered 200 most backward districts in its first phase (d) It was enacted in 2005 and implemented in phases starting February 2006
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is the extension to urban households — MGNREGA applies only to rural households whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work. The other statements are correct: 100 days guarantee, enacted 2005, first phase from February 2006 in 200 districts, so the answer is that it provides guaranteed employment to both rural and urban households.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Indian Economy

Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

131. With reference to the National Education Policy 2020, the new school curricular structure proposed is:
(a) 10+2 (b) 5+3+3+4 (c) 4+4+3+3 (d) 6+3+3+2
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap options rearrange the same digits; NEP 2020 replaces the 10+2 structure with 5+3+3+4, covering ages 3–18 (foundational, preparatory, middle and secondary stages), so the answer is 5+3+3+4.

132. Which of the following statements about the PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) is correct?

Answer: (c)
Solution: The 10 kg per family figure is the trap — the entitlement is 5 kg per person over and above NFSA entitlements, covering all NFSA beneficiaries (both Priority Households and AAY). (a) Ministry of Rural Development (b) NITI Aayog (c) Ministry of Home Affairs (d) Ministry of Panchayati Raj

135. Under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana, the maximum loan limit under the 'Kishor' category is:
(a) ₹5 lakh (b) ₹20 lakh (c) ₹10 lakh (d) ₹50,000
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is ₹10 lakh, which is the ceiling for the 'Tarun' category, not Kishor. The three categories are Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishor (₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh) and Tarun (₹5,00,001 to ₹10 lakh), so the answer is ₹5 lakh.
Source: PYQ-style

136. Which of the following correctly describes the 'One Nation One Ration Card' initiative?
(a) It provides free LPG connections to ration card holders (b) It merges all ration cards into a single national card issued by the President of India (c) It allows portability of ration card benefits across all States and Union Territories under NFSA (d) It replaces the Public Distribution System with direct cash transfers
Answer: (c)
Solution: The single national card and cash transfer options are exaggeration traps — the initiative does not abolish PDS or create a single physical card; it enables migrant beneficiaries to lift their entitled foodgrains from any fair price shop in the country using their existing ration card, so the answer is portability of ration card benefits across all States and Union Territories under NFSA.
Source: PYQ-style

Source: PYQ-style

138. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana primarily aims to:
(a) Provide free electricity connections to rural households (b) Provide piped natural gas to all urban households (c) Provide solar lanterns to off-grid villages (d) Provide LPG connections to women from poor households
Answer: (d)
Solution: The electricity option is the similar-scheme trap — free electricity connections belong to Saubhagya (PM Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana), not Ujjwala. Ujjwala, launched in 2016 from Ballia, provides deposit-free LPG connections to adult women of poor households, so the answer is LPG connections to women from poor households.
Source: PYQ-style

(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

145. Which of the following statements about the Jal Jeevan Mission is correct?

148. Which of the following correctly matches the scheme with its launch year?

149. Consider the following statements about the National Food Security Act, 2013:
1. It covers up to 75% of the rural population and 50% of the urban population.
2. It provides 5 kg of foodgrains per person per month at subsidized prices to priority households.
3. The eldest woman of the household is deemed the head of the household for ration cards.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap would be reversing the rural-urban coverage percentages (75% rural, 50% urban is correct, not the reverse). All three statements accurately reflect the Act, including the provision designating the eldest woman (18 years or above) as head of household, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.

150. Which of the following statements about the 'PM Internship Scheme' announced in 2024 is correct?
(a) It replaces the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (b) It guarantees government jobs to all interns after completion (c) It is open only to post-graduate degree holders (d) It aims to provide internship opportunities to youth in top companies
Answer: (d)
Solution: The job guarantee and replacement claims are exaggeration traps — the scheme neither guarantees jobs nor replaces NAPS; it offers 12-month internships in top companies with monthly assistance, targeting youth including ITI and diploma holders, so the answer is that it aims to provide internship opportunities to youth in top companies.
Source: PYQ-style
