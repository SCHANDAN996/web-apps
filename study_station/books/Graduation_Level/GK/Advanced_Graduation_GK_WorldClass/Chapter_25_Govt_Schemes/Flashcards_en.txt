Card 1
Front: Which scheme, launched in 2015, provides life insurance cover of ₹2 lakh for a premium of just ₹436 per year (revised 2022)?
Back: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) — for ages 18–50, renewable yearly. Don't confuse it with PMSBY (accident insurance, ₹20 premium).

Card 2
Front: What is the annual accident insurance premium under PMSBY, and what cover does it give?
Back: ₹20 per year (revised 2022) for ₹2 lakh accidental death/full disability cover, ages 18–70. Trap: exams swap PMJJBY and PMSBY premiums.

Card 3
Front: Which scheme gives ₹6,000 per year to small and marginal farmers, paid in three instalments?
Back: PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi), launched February 2019 — ₹2,000 every four months via DBT. Initially for landholding up to 2 hectares; later extended to all landholding farmers.

Card 4
Front: Ayushman Bharat–PMJAY provides health cover of how much per family per year, and for what kind of treatment?
Back: ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation. Trap: it does NOT cover OPD/outpatient expenses.

Card 5
Front: Mnemonic trick: how do you remember the three 2015 social-security schemes launched together (PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY)?
Back: "Jyoti–Suraksha–Atal" = Life (PMJJBY), Accident (PMSBY), Pension (APY) — all launched on 9 May 2015 from Kolkata. Remember: JSA = "Jeevan, Suraksha, Atal".

Card 6
Front: Under Atal Pension Yojana, what is the guaranteed monthly pension range and the eligible entry age?
Back: ₹1,000–₹5,000 per month after age 60; entry age 18–40 years. Named after Atal Bihari Vajpayee.

Card 7
Front: Which scheme provides collateral-free loans up to ₹10 lakh to micro/small enterprises, and what are its three categories?
Back: Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (2015): Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,001–₹5 lakh), Tarun (₹5–10 lakh). Mnemonic: "SKT — Small, Kicking, Towering".

Card 8
Front: Examiner's trap: MUDRA Bank gives loans directly to borrowers — true or false?
Back: False. MUDRA is a refinancing institution; loans are given through banks, NBFCs and MFIs. This "direct lending" statement is a classic wrong option.

Card 9
Front: What is the target of the Jal Jeevan Mission, and by when was it originally set?
Back: Functional household tap connection (Har Ghar Jal) to every rural household — original target year 2024 (later extended). Launched 2019.

Card 10
Front: Which mission aims at "Housing for All", and how do its Urban and Rural versions differ in launch year?
Back: Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana: PMAY-Urban launched June 2015; PMAY-Gramin (restructured Indira Awas Yojana) from 2016. Trap: exams swap the two years.

Card 11
Front: Under PMAY-Gramin, what is the unit assistance in plain areas and hilly/difficult areas?
Back: ₹1.20 lakh in plains and ₹1.30 lakh in hilly/difficult areas (including IAP districts).

Card 12
Front: Which scheme gives free LPG connections to women of poor households, and what was its initial deposit-free cylinder provision?
Back: Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (2016, launched from Ballia, UP) — deposit-free LPG connection in the woman's name; Ujjwala 2.0 added the first refill and stove free.

Card 13
Front: Mnemonic trick: how to remember the nodal ministries of Ujjwala, MUDRA and PMJAY?
Back: "Oil–Finance–Health": Ujjwala → Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas; MUDRA → Ministry of Finance; PMJAY → Ministry of Health & Family Welfare.

Card 14
Front: What does the Stand-Up India scheme mandate every bank branch to do?
Back: Each bank branch must give loans of ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and one woman borrower for a greenfield enterprise. Launched 2016.

Card 15
Front: Examiner's trap: "Beti Bachao Beti Padhao is a cash-transfer scheme for girl children" — true or false?
Back: False. BBBP (2015, launched from Panipat, Haryana) is an awareness/advocacy scheme for child sex ratio and girls' education — it gives no direct cash benefit. The cash-linked scheme is Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana.

Card 16
Front: What is special about the Sukanya Samriddhi Account regarding the depositor and maturity?
Back: Opened in a girl child's name (below age 10) by a guardian; deposits for 15 years, matures at 21 years from opening; enjoys EEE (exempt-exempt-exempt) tax status.

Card 17
Front: Which 2014 mission built household and community toilets to make India open-defecation free, and on whose anniversary was it launched?
Back: Swachh Bharat Mission, launched 2 October 2014 (Gandhi Jayanti); rural India declared ODF on 2 October 2019. Phase II focuses on ODF Plus / solid-liquid waste management.

Card 18
Front: What are the two components of the National Rural Livelihoods Mission's interest-subvention feature often tested, and what is the scheme now commonly called?
Back: DAY-NRLM (Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana–NRLM, earlier Aajeevika): women SHGs get loans at 7% interest, with an extra 3% subvention for prompt repayment (effective 4%).

Card 19
Front: Which scheme provides 100 days of guaranteed wage employment, and under which Act does it operate?
Back: MGNREGA — Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 (implemented from 2006). Guarantee is a legal right; unemployment allowance payable if work not given within 15 days.

Card 20
Front: Quick recall: match launch years — Digital India, Skill India, Make in India, Startup India.
Back: Digital India & Skill India: 2015; Make in India: 2014; Startup India: 2016. Trap: exams frequently swap Make in India and Startup India years.
