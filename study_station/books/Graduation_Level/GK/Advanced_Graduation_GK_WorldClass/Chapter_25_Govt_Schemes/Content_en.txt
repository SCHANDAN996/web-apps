# Government Schemes & Initiatives

## The Hook: A Bank Account That Changed 50 Crore Lives

In August 2014, a daily-wage worker in rural Uttar Pradesh walked into a bank for the first time in her life. She had no documents except an Aadhaar card, no minimum balance, and no idea what a "zero-balance account" meant. Within 15 minutes, she had a **Jan Dhan account** — and with it, a direct pipeline to every government benefit she was entitled to. No middleman. No cut. No bribe.

That single scheme — **Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), launched 28 August 2014** — became the backbone of the world's largest Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) architecture. When COVID-19 struck in 2020, the government pushed relief money directly into crores of women's Jan Dhan accounts within days. That is the power of a well-designed scheme — and that is exactly why examiners love this chapter.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

1. Recall the **launch year, ministry, and target beneficiary** of India's flagship schemes.
2. Distinguish between **Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS)** and **Central Sector Schemes**.
3. Spot the examiner's favourite traps: swapped years, swapped ministries, and "almost-correct" statements.
4. Link schemes to the **static GK** they rest on (Constitution, banking, health, agriculture).

## 1. How to Study Schemes: The 5-Point Framework

Never memorise a scheme as a random fact. For every scheme, fix **five anchors**:

| Anchor | Question to Ask |
|---|---|
| **When** | Launch year (and date, if famous) |
| **Who runs it** | Nodal Ministry |
| **For whom** | Target beneficiary |
| **What** | Core benefit (one line) |
| **How funded** | Central Sector or Centrally Sponsored (funding ratio) |

**Funding pattern — the most-tested static fact in this chapter:**
- **Central Sector Scheme**: 100% funded by the Union Government (e.g., PM-KISAN).
- **Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS)**: shared funding — typically **60:40** (Centre:State) for general states, and **90:10** for Himalayan/NE states and UTs with legislature; 100% for UTs without legislature.

⚠️ **Note:** Some major schemes have their **own specific funding patterns** and should not be assumed to follow the typical 60:40 ratio. For example, under **MGNREGA**, the Centre bears 100% of the wage cost of unskilled workers and 75% of the material cost. Similarly, **PMAY** assistance-sharing varies by component and state category. Learn the general CSS rule first, then memorise the exceptions separately.

🧠 **Active Recall:** Without looking back — what are the five anchors? What is the CSS funding ratio for a general state like Bihar?

## 2. Financial Inclusion & Economic Schemes

### Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) — 2014
- Launched **28 August 2014**; Ministry of Finance.
- Zero-balance bank account, RuPay debit card, accident insurance cover, overdraft facility.
- Foundation of the **JAM trinity** — **J**an Dhan + **A**adhaar + **M**obile — which powers Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

### PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) — 2019
- Launched **February 2019** (effective retrospectively from December 2018); Ministry of Agriculture.
- **₹6,000 per year** to eligible landholding farmer families, in **three instalments of ₹2,000**, paid via DBT.
- Central Sector Scheme — 100% Centre-funded.

### MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) — 2015
- Launched **8 April 2015**; loans to micro/small enterprises under three categories:
  - **Shishu**: up to ₹50,000
  - **Kishore**: ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh
  - **Tarun**: ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh
- *(Note: In the Union Budget 2024, the loan limit was enhanced to ₹20 lakh as "Tarun Plus" for entrepreneurs who repaid earlier loans — verify current limits as of your exam year.)*

### Stand-Up India — 2016
- Launched **5 April 2016**; bank loans of **₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore** for **SC/ST and women entrepreneurs** setting up greenfield enterprises.

🧠 **Active Recall:** Arrange Shishu–Kishore–Tarun in order with their limits. Which scheme gives ₹6,000/year, and in how many instalments?

**🧩 Mnemonic 1 — MUDRA ladder:** *"Shishu is a baby (50K), Kishore is a teen (5 Lakh), Tarun is a young adult (10 Lakh)."* The Hindi names themselves tell the story — child → adolescent → youth.

## 3. Rural Development & Employment

### MGNREGA — 2005
- Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, passed **2005**, implemented from **2 February 2006**.
- Guarantees **100 days of wage employment per household per year** to rural households willing to do unskilled manual work.
- Ministry of Rural Development. Unemployment allowance payable if work is not given within 15 days of demand.

### Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)
- **PMAY-Gramin (rural)**: launched 2016 (restructured from Indira Awaas Yojana); pucca houses with basic amenities for the rural homeless.
- **PMAY-Urban**: launched **25 June 2015**; "Housing for All" mission; Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

### Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – NRLM (DAY-NRLM)
- Restructured from Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana in **2011**; organises rural poor women into **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)**.

🧠 **Active Recall:** MGNREGA — Act year vs. implementation year. Why do examiners love this pair? (Because 2005 and 2006 are both "almost correct.")

## 4. Health & Social Security

### Ayushman Bharat — PM-JAY — 2018
- Launched **23 September 2018** from Ranchi, Jharkhand.
- World's largest government-funded health insurance scheme: **₹5 lakh per family per year** for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation, covering the bottom ~40% of population.
- Nodal: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare; implemented by the National Health Authority (NHA).
- The other pillar: **Health and Wellness Centres** (now Ayushman Arogya Mandirs).

### PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) & PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) — 2015
- Both launched **9 May 2015**:
  - **PMJJBY**: life insurance, ₹2 lakh cover, ages 18–50.
  - **PMSBY**: accident insurance, ₹2 lakh cover, ages 18–70.
- **Atal Pension Yojana (APY)**, launched the same day: guaranteed pension of ₹1,000–₹5,000/month after age 60, for unorganised-sector workers.

### POSHAN Abhiyaan — 2018
- Launched **March 2018** from Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan; Ministry of Women and Child Development; targets malnutrition, stunting and anaemia.

**🧩 Mnemonic 2 — The 9 May 2015 trio:** *"Jeevan, Suraksha, Atal — Life, Safety, Old age."* One date, three schemes, three stages of life. If the exam asks "which was NOT launched on 9 May 2015," the odd one out is usually Jan Dhan (2014).

🧠 **Active Recall:** Ayushman Bharat's insurance cover per family? Which ministry runs PM-JAY — Health or Finance?

## 5. Sanitation, Water & Environment

### Swachh Bharat Mission — 2014
- Launched **2 October 2014** (Gandhi Jayanti); target: eliminate open defecation by 2 October 2019 (150th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi).
- Two arms: **SBM-Gramin** (Ministry of Jal Shakti) and **SBM-Urban** (Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs).

### Jal Jeevan Mission — 2019
- Announced in **2019**; goal: **Functional Household Tap Connection (FHTC)** to every rural household by 2024 ("Har Ghar Jal"). Ministry of Jal Shakti.

### PM Ujjwala Yojana — 2016
- Launched **1 May 2016** from Ballia, Uttar Pradesh; LPG connections to women of BPL households; Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas.

**🧩 Mnemonic 3 — Launch cities:** *"Ujjwala was born in Ballia, Ayushman in Ranchi, POSHAN in Jhunjhunu."* Examiners occasionally ask the launch location — these three are the famous ones.

🧠 **Active Recall:** Which ministry handles Swachh Bharat (Gramin)? What does FHTC stand for?

## 6. Digital & Governance Initiatives

- **Digital India** — launched **1 July 2015**; Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY); three pillars: digital infrastructure, governance-on-demand, digital empowerment.
- **Make in India** — launched **25 September 2014**; boost manufacturing; DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce.
- **Skill India / PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)** — 2015; Ministry of Skill Development.
- **Startup India** — launched **16 January 2016**; tax benefits, easier compliance for recognised startups.
- **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** — launched **22 January 2015** from Panipat, Haryana; joint initiative of Ministries of WCD, Health, and Education.

## 🔗 Static–Dynamic Link Box

- **Article 41 (DPSP)** — "right to work" — is the constitutional soul behind MGNREGA. When a scheme question feels dynamic, ask: which Directive Principle does it serve?
- **NABARD** (est. 1982, static GK) refinances many rural schemes; **SIDBI** (est. 1990) anchors MUDRA. Schemes change yearly; their parent institutions are static — revise them together.
- The **JAM trinity** rests on Aadhaar, whose legal base is the **Aadhaar Act, 2016** — a favourite static-dynamic crossover question.

## ⚠️ Caution: Examiner's Trap

**Trap 1 — Chronological Confusion (कालानुक्रमिक भ्रम):** *"MGNREGA was launched in 2005."* The **Act** was passed in 2005; **implementation began in 2006**. Both options appear together — read whether the question says "Act passed" or "scheme launched."

**Trap 2 — Almost-Correct Statement (लगभग सही कथन):** *"PM-KISAN provides ₹6,000 per month to farmers."* Everything is right except one word — it is ₹6,000 **per year**, in three instalments. Similarly: "Ayushman Bharat gives ₹5 lakh per **person**" — wrong, it is per **family**.

**Trap 3 — Similar Names / Swapped Ministries (समान नाम):** *"Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) is run by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs."* That ministry runs SBM-**Urban**; SBM-**Gramin** belongs to the **Ministry of Jal Shakti**. Also beware: PMJJBY (life) vs. PMSBY (accident) — one word swapped flips the answer.

## 📅 Revision Planner

**After 2 days (Blurt Test):** Close the book. Write from memory: (a) all schemes launched in 2015, (b) the three MUDRA categories with limits, (c) the ministry of each scheme in the summary table. Check against the table — every miss goes on a flashcard.

**Weekly (Trap Drill):** Re-read only the "Examiner's Trap" box and the summary table's Year and Ministry columns. Write 5 "almost-correct" statements yourself and correct them — this builds the exact muscle the exam tests.

**Monthly (Full Blurt):** On a blank sheet, draw the 5-anchor framework (When / Who runs / For whom / What / Funding) and fill it for 10 schemes chosen at random. Then update yourself on schemes launched or modified in the current year via official sources (pib.gov.in, ministry websites) — this chapter is dynamic, and your notes must be too.
