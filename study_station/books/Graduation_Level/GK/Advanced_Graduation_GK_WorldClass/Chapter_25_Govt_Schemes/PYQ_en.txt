# 📊 PYQ Analysis — Government Schemes & Initiatives

## 1. How Exams Ask Questions from This Chapter

Government schemes appear in every major exam, but the *style* differs:

- **UPSC (Prelims):** Statement-based questions — "Which of the following statements about PM-KISAN is/are correct?" Tests ministry, beneficiary definition, funding pattern (centrally sponsored vs central sector), and launch year together.
- **SSC (CGL/CHSL/MTS):** Direct one-liners — nodal ministry, launch year, target beneficiary, or "which scheme is associated with X sector?" Increasingly asks about schemes' *sub-components*.
- **Banking (IBPS/SBI PO/Clerk):** Schemes linked to financial inclusion — Jan Dhan, Mudra, Stand-Up India, PMJJBY/PMSBY/APY — plus budget allocations and recent extensions.
- **Railway (NTPC/Group D):** Factual recall — launch year, ministry, slogan, and flagship scheme–ministry matching.
- **State PCS:** Central schemes *plus* that state's own schemes; questions often mix the two to confuse.

## 2. Sub-Topics That Recur Most Often

- **Financial inclusion trio:** PMJDY, PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY — premiums, age bands, and insurance cover amounts are asked repeatedly.
- **MUDRA loan categories:** Shishu / Kishore / Tarun slabs — a perennial favourite across SSC and Banking.
- **PM-KISAN:** instalment amount, number of instalments per year, who is *excluded* (income-tax payers, institutional landholders).
- **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY):** cover per family per year, and the difference between the Health & Wellness Centres component and the insurance component.
- **Housing:** PMAY-U vs PMAY-G — rural/urban nodal ministries are swapped in options.
- **Employment & skills:** MGNREGA (days of work, demand-driven nature), Skill India, Stand-Up India (borrower category — SC/ST and women).
- **Sanitation & water:** Swachh Bharat Mission phases, Jal Jeevan Mission (tap water to every rural household).
- **Old vs new names:** Exams love schemes that were renamed or restructured (e.g., rural employment and rural development programmes).

## 3. The Examiner's Traps (जाल)

1. **Almost Correct:** "PM-KISAN gives ₹6,000 per year in *four* instalments" — everything right except the number (it is three instalments of ₹2,000).
2. **Chronological Confusion:** Swapping launch years of similar schemes (PMJDY 2014 vs PM-KISAN 2019), or asking which scheme came first.
3. **Ministry Swap:** PMAY-G under Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs (wrong — it is Rural Development); Ayushman Bharat under Ministry of AYUSH (wrong — Health & Family Welfare).
4. **Negative Phrasing:** "Which of the following is NOT a component of…", "Which scheme does NOT provide…" — students misread and pick the correct-sounding feature.
5. **Similar Names:** PMJJBY vs PMSBY (life insurance vs accident insurance), PMAY-U vs PMAY-G, Stand-Up India vs Start-up India (loan vs ecosystem).
6. **Amount Inflation:** Insurance cover of PMSBY quoted as ₹4 lakh instead of ₹2 lakh; MUDRA Tarun limit quoted as ₹20 lakh instead of ₹10 lakh.
7. **Beneficiary Shift:** "PM-KISAN covers all farmers including institutional landholders" — the exclusion clause is the trap.

## 4. High-Yield PYQ-Style Questions with Solutions

**Q1.** Under the Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana, the 'Kishore' category covers loans of:
(a) Up to ₹50,000 (b) ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh (c) ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh (d) Above ₹10 lakh
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** Shishu = up to ₹50,000; Kishore = ₹50,001–₹5 lakh; Tarun = ₹5–10 lakh. Trap type: *Amount Inflation* — option (c) is the Tarun slab, placed to catch those who remember only two slabs.
**Source:** PYQ-style (based on official PMMY guidelines)

**Q2.** Which of the following is NOT correct about PM-KISAN?
(a) ₹6,000 per year to eligible farmer families
(b) Paid in three equal instalments
(c) Institutional landholders are eligible
(d) It is a central sector scheme
**Answer:** (c)
**Solution:** Institutional landholders, income-tax payers, and serving/retired government employees (except Group D/MTS) are excluded. Trap type: *Negative Phrasing* + *Almost Correct* — (a), (b), (d) are all true, so a hurried reader picks one of them.
**Source:** PYQ-style (based on pmkisan.gov.in)

**Q3.** The Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) provides:
(a) Accident insurance of ₹2 lakh (b) Life insurance of ₹2 lakh
(c) Life insurance of ₹4 lakh (d) Health insurance of ₹5 lakh
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** PMJJBY = life cover ₹2 lakh (age 18–50); PMSBY = accident cover ₹2 lakh. Trap type: *Similar Names* — option (a) is PMSBY's feature.
**Source:** PYQ-style (based on jansuraksha.gov.in)

**Q4.** Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY provides health cover of up to ______ per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation.
(a) ₹1 lakh (b) ₹2 lakh (c) ₹5 lakh (d) ₹10 lakh
**Answer:** (c)
**Solution:** ₹5 lakh per family per year, on a family-floater basis. Trap type: *Amount Inflation* — ₹2 lakh is the PMJJBY/PMSBY figure, planted to confuse.
**Source:** PYQ-style (based on pmjay.gov.in)

**Q5.** Arrange these schemes in chronological order of launch:
1. Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana 2. MGNREGA 3. PM-KISAN 4. Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
(a) 2-1-4-3 (b) 1-2-3-4 (c) 2-1-3-4 (d) 1-2-4-3
**Answer:** (a)
**Solution:** MGNREGA (2005) → PMJDY (2014) → PM-JAY (2018) → PM-KISAN (2019). Trap type: *Chronological Confusion* — options (c) and (d) swap the last two, the most common error.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q6.** Stand-Up India scheme provides bank loans for setting up greenfield enterprises to:
(a) All women entrepreneurs only (b) SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs
(c) Only SC entrepreneurs (d) All MSMEs without restriction
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** Loans of ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore to at least one SC/ST borrower and one woman borrower per bank branch. Trap type: *Beneficiary Shift* — option (a) drops the SC/ST component.
**Source:** PYQ-style (based on standupmitra.in)

**Q7.** Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G) is implemented by:
(a) Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (b) Ministry of Rural Development
(c) Ministry of Panchayati Raj (d) Ministry of Urban Development
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** PMAY-G → Rural Development; PMAY-U → Housing and Urban Affairs. Trap type: *Ministry Swap* — option (a) is correct for the *urban* version.
**Source:** PYQ-style

**Q8.** Under MGNREGA, the guaranteed wage employment per rural household per financial year is:
(a) 90 days (b) 100 days (c) 120 days (d) 150 days
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** 100 days of unskilled manual work on demand. Trap type: *Almost Correct* — 150 days applies only in notified drought areas as an additional provision, not the base guarantee.
**Source:** PYQ-style (based on nrega.nic.in)

**Q9.** Which scheme aims to provide functional household tap connections to every rural household?
(a) Swachh Bharat Mission (b) Jal Jeevan Mission
(c) PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (d) Atal Bhujal Yojana
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** Jal Jeevan Mission (2019) — "Har Ghar Jal". Trap type: *Similar Names* — PMKSY is about irrigation ("Har Khet Ko Pani"), not household taps.
**Source:** PYQ-style (based on jaljeevanmission.gov.in)

**Q10.** The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) annual premium is approximately:
(a) ₹12 (b) ₹20 (c) ₹330 (d) ₹436
**Answer:** (b)
**Solution:** PMSBY ≈ ₹20/year (accident cover ₹2 lakh); PMJJBY ≈ ₹436/year. Trap type: *Similar Names* + *Amount Inflation* — option (d) is PMJJBY's premium.
**Source:** PYQ-style (based on jansuraksha.gov.in)

## 5. The 15/45-Second Rule Applied

**Q1 (MUDRA Kishore slab) — 15 seconds:** This is pure recall of a fixed slab table. If you have memorised Shishu/Kishore/Tarun, mark (b) instantly and move on. Do not re-read the options twice — the trap (option c) only catches the unprepared, and hesitation gains nothing.

**Q5 (chronological order) — 45 seconds:** Worth the time. Anchor the two extremes first: MGNREGA (2005, oldest) and PM-KISAN (2019, newest). That alone eliminates (b) and (d). Then decide between PM-JAY (2018) and PM-KISAN (2019) — 45 seconds of structured elimination converts a guess into a certainty.

**Rule of thumb:** Direct recall (amounts, ministries, years) = 15 seconds, answer or skip. Ordering/matching/NOT-type questions = up to 45 seconds, because elimination genuinely improves odds. Never spend 45 seconds on a pure-recall amount you simply don't know — flag it and return.

## 6. Preparation Takeaways

- Master the **amount tables** (MUDRA slabs, PM-KISAN instalments, PM-JAY cover, PMJJBY/PMSBY premiums) — these generate the most questions.
- Prepare a **ministry–scheme matching list**; ministry swaps are the cheapest trap to set and the easiest to fall for.
- Learn **launch years in clusters** (2005: MGNREGA; 2014: PMJDY, SBM; 2015: PMJJBY/PMSBY/APY, PMMY; 2016: Stand-Up India; 2018: PM-JAY; 2019: PM-KISAN, JJM).
- For NOT-type questions, physically underline "NOT" before reading options.
- Since this chapter is dynamic, verify current-year extensions, renamed schemes, and new budget allocations from official sources before the exam.
