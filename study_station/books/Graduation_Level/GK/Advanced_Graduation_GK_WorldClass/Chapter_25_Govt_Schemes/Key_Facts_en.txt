## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Fact / Topic | Type | Time Target | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch year + launch place of flagship schemes (Ujjwala–Ballia, BBBP–Panipat, PM-JAY–Ranchi) | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
| Ministry in charge of a scheme | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
| Fixed figures: PM-KISAN ₹6,000, PM-JAY ₹5 lakh, MUDRA category limits, PMJJBY/PMSBY covers | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
| Scheme-to-nodal-ministry matching questions | Fact-based | 15 sec | Yes |
| Chronology ordering of schemes (e.g., Ganga Action Plan → Namami Gange) | Analytical | 45 sec | No |
| Statement-based questions ("Which statements about PMAY-U and PMAY-G are correct?") | Analytical | 45 sec | No |
| Scheme objective vs target group analysis (e.g., Stand-Up India eligibility) | Analytical | 45 sec | No |
| Budget allocation trends and scheme restructuring (e.g., NRLM from SGSY) | Analytical | 45 sec | No |
| Negative-worded questions ("Which scheme is NOT related to financial inclusion?") | Analytical | 45 sec | No |
