Questions 76–100: Medium

76. The Durand Cup, one of the oldest football tournaments in the world, is associated with which country?
(a) England (b) Spain (c) Brazil (d) India
Answer: (d)
Solution: Many students pick England because the FA Cup is the oldest national football competition, but the Durand Cup (started 1888 in Shimla) is India's tournament and among the oldest in the world, so the answer is India.
Source: PYQ-style

77. In which sport is the term 'Deuce' used when the score reaches 40–40?
(a) Badminton (b) Table Tennis (c) Lawn Tennis (d) Squash
Answer: (c)
Solution: Deuce occurs in badminton too at 20–20, which tempts students, but the classic 40–40 deuce terminology belongs to lawn tennis scoring, so the answer is Lawn Tennis.
Source: NCERT-based GK

78. The Thomas Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Men's Hockey (b) Women's Badminton (c) Men's Tennis (d) Men's Badminton
Answer: (d)
Solution: Students often confuse Thomas Cup with Uber Cup; the Uber Cup is the women's world team badminton championship, while the Thomas Cup is the men's team championship, so the answer is Men's Badminton.
Source: PYQ-style

79. Who was the first Indian to win an individual Olympic gold medal?
(a) Milkha Singh (b) Leander Paes (c) Abhinav Bindra (d) Karnam Malleswari
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hockey golds tempt students, but those were team events; Abhinav Bindra won the 10m air rifle gold at Beijing 2008, India's first individual Olympic gold, so the answer is Abhinav Bindra.
Source: PYQ-style

80. The term 'LBW' is used in which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Baseball (c) Football (d) Cricket
Answer: (d)
Solution: LBW stands for Leg Before Wicket, a mode of dismissal in cricket where the ball strikes the batsman's body in line with the stumps, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: NCERT-based GK
