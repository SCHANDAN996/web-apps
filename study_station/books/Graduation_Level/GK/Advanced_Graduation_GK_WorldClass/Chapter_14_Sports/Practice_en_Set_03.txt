Questions 51–75: Medium

51. Which country has won the FIFA World Cup the most number of times?
(a) Germany (b) Argentina (c) Brazil (d) Italy
Answer: (c)
Solution: Germany and Italy (4 titles each) are tempting because of their strong records, but Brazil leads with 5 titles (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002). Argentina has 3. So the answer is Brazil.
Source: FIFA official records

52. The term "LBW" is associated with which sport?
(a) Cricket (b) Football (c) Hockey (d) Tennis
Answer: (a)
Solution: LBW (Leg Before Wicket) is a mode of dismissal in cricket, governed by Law 36 of the Laws of Cricket. It is not used in hockey or football, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: MCC Laws of Cricket

53. Where were the first modern Olympic Games held in 1896?
(a) Paris (b) London (c) Rome (d) Athens
Answer: (d)
Solution: Paris hosted in 1900 and is a common trap because the modern Olympics were revived by Frenchman Pierre de Coubertin, but the first Games were held in Athens, Greece, in 1896. So the answer is Athens.
Source: International Olympic Committee (olympics.com)

54. How many players are there in a standard volleyball team on the court?
(a) 5 (b) 7 (c) 6 (d) 9
Answer: (c)
Solution: 5 is the basketball number and a frequent confusion; 7 belongs to kabaddi. A volleyball team fields 6 players on court. So the answer is 6.
Source: FIVB official rules

55. Who was the first Indian to win an individual Olympic gold medal?
(a) Milkha Singh (b) Abhinav Bindra (c) Neeraj Chopra (d) Leander Paes
Answer: (b)
Solution: Neeraj Chopra (Tokyo 2020 javelin gold) is the tempting recent answer, but Abhinav Bindra won India's first individual Olympic gold in 10m air rifle shooting at Beijing 2008. So the answer is Abhinav Bindra.
Source: International Olympic Committee (olympics.com)

56. The Durand Cup is associated with which sport in India?
(a) Cricket (b) Hockey (c) Football (d) Badminton
Answer: (c)
Solution: Hockey is tempting because India has many historic hockey trophies (Aga Khan Cup, Beighton Cup), but the Durand Cup, started in 1888, is a football tournament — one of the oldest in the world. So the answer is Football.
Source: Durand Cup official records

57. In which city were the 2010 Commonwealth Games held?
(a) Melbourne (b) Glasgow (c) New Delhi (d) Gold Coast
Answer: (c)
Solution: Glasgow (2014) and Gold Coast (2018) cause chronological confusion, but the 2010 edition was hosted by New Delhi, India. So the answer is New Delhi.
Source: Commonwealth Games Federation

58. What is the maximum number of sets in a men's singles match at a Grand Slam tennis tournament?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 7 (d) 5
Answer: (d)
Solution: 3 is tempting because women's singles and most tour matches are best-of-3, but men's singles at Grand Slams is best-of-5 sets. So the answer is 5.
Source: ITF Grand Slam rulebook

59. The Ranji Trophy is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Football (c) Tennis (d) Cricket
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Ranji Trophy, started in 1934–35 and named after Ranjitsinhji, is India's premier domestic first-class cricket championship. So the answer is Cricket.
Source: BCCI official records

60. Which country won the ICC Cricket World Cup in 2011?
(a) Sri Lanka (b) Australia (c) India (d) Pakistan
Answer: (c)
Solution: Sri Lanka is the trap because it was the runner-up in the 2011 final at Mumbai; India defeated Sri Lanka to win its second World Cup (first in 1983). So the answer is India.
Source: ICC official records

61. The Thomas Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Table Tennis (b) Badminton (c) Lawn Tennis (d) Squash
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lawn Tennis is tempting due to the similar-sounding Davis Cup, but the Thomas Cup is the world men's team championship in badminton (women's equivalent: Uber Cup). So the answer is Badminton.
Source: Badminton World Federation (BWF)

62. How many rings are there in the Olympic symbol?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 7
Answer: (b)
Solution: The five interlaced rings represent the five inhabited continents united by Olympism; the colours plus white background cover all national flags. So the answer is 5.
Source: International Olympic Committee (olympics.com)

63. Who is known as the "Flying Sikh" of India?
(a) Milkha Singh (b) P.T. Usha (c) Gurbachan Singh Randhawa (d) Makhan Singh
Answer: (a)
Solution: P.T. Usha is a sprinting great but is called the "Payyoli Express"; the title "Flying Sikh" belongs to Milkha Singh, the 400m runner who finished fourth at Rome 1960. So the answer is Milkha Singh.
Source: Athletics Federation of India records

64. In which year did India win its first Olympic hockey gold medal?
(a) 1928 (b) 1932 (c) 1936 (d) 1948
Answer: (a)
Solution: 1948 is the classic trap — it was India's first gold as an independent nation — but India's first Olympic hockey gold came at Amsterdam 1928, where Jaipal Singh Munda was the initial captain. So the answer is 1928.
Source: International Olympic Committee (olympics.com)

65. The term "Deuce" is used in which sport?
(a) Tennis (b) Cricket (c) Hockey (d) Boxing
Answer: (a)
Solution: Deuce denotes a 40–40 score in tennis, after which a player must win two consecutive points to take the game. The term also appears in badminton and table tennis scoring contexts, but among the given sports it belongs to tennis. So the answer is Tennis.
Source: ITF Rules of Tennis

66. Which Indian woman won a bronze medal in boxing at the London 2012 Olympics?
(a) Nikhat Zareen (b) Mary Kom (c) Lovlina Borgohain (d) Sarita Devi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Lovlina Borgohain is the trap — she won bronze at Tokyo 2020, not London. M.C. Mary Kom won the flyweight bronze at London 2012, where women's boxing debuted. So the answer is Mary Kom.
Source: International Olympic Committee (olympics.com)

67. The headquarters of the International Cricket Council (ICC) is located in which city?
(a) Dubai (b) London (c) Melbourne (d) Mumbai
Answer: (a)
Solution: London is tempting because the ICC (then Imperial Cricket Conference) was founded there in 1909 and was long based at Lord's, but its headquarters moved to Dubai in 2005. So the answer is Dubai.
Source: ICC official website

68. How many players does a kabaddi team field on the court?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 7
Answer: (d)
Solution: 6 (volleyball) is a common confusion; a kabaddi team has 7 players on court with up to 5 substitutes. So the answer is 7.
Source: Amateur Kabaddi Federation of India rules

69. Who was the first Indian to win the All England Badminton Championship?
(a) Syed Modi (b) Pullela Gopichand (c) Prakash Padukone (d) Saina Nehwal
Answer: (c)
Solution: Gopichand is the trap — he won it in 2001, but Prakash Padukone was the first Indian champion, winning the title in 1980. So the answer is Prakash Padukone.
Source: All England Badminton official records

70. The Santosh Trophy is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Volleyball (c) Cricket (d) Football
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Santosh Trophy, started in 1941, is India's premier inter-state football championship for senior men; hockey trophies like the Rangaswamy Cup create confusion. So the answer is Football.
Source: All India Football Federation (AIFF)

71. In which sport did India win its first medal at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics (held in 2021)?
(a) Wrestling (b) Weightlifting (c) Badminton (d) Hockey
Answer: (b)
Solution: Hockey is tempting because the men's bronze was historic, but India's first medal of those Games was Mirabai Chanu's silver in women's 49kg weightlifting on the opening day of medal events. So the answer is Weightlifting.
Source: International Olympic Committee (olympics.com)

72. The Ryder Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Golf (b) Tennis (c) Polo (d) Sailing
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Ryder Cup is a biennial men's golf team competition between the United States and Europe, first played in 1927. So the answer is Golf.
Source: Ryder Cup official records

73. Which Grand Slam tennis tournament is played on clay courts?
(a) French Open (b) US Open (c) Wimbledon (d) Australian Open
Answer: (a)
Solution: Wimbledon is the trap for those who recall only "grass vs hard"; the French Open (Roland Garros) is played on red clay, while the US and Australian Opens use hard courts. So the answer is French Open.
Source: Roland Garros official website

74. Dronacharya Award in India is given for excellence in which field?
(a) Sports playing performance (b) Sports coaching (c) Sports journalism (d) Sports administration
Answer: (b)
Solution: Playing performance is the trap — that is honoured by the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna and Arjuna Award; the Dronacharya Award honours outstanding coaches. So the answer is Sports coaching.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

75. Which country hosted the 2022 FIFA World Cup?
(a) Russia (b) United States (c) Brazil (d) Qatar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Russia is the chronological trap — it hosted the 2018 edition; the 2022 World Cup was held in Qatar, the first in the Middle East, won by Argentina. So the answer is Qatar.
Source: FIFA official records
