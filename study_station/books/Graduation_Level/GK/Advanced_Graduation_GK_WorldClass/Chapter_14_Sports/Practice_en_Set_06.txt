126. With reference to the Thomas Cup and Uber Cup, consider the following statements:
1. Both are international team championships in badminton.
2. India won its first Thomas Cup title in 2022.
3. The Uber Cup is the men's team championship while the Thomas Cup is the women's.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — it reverses the two trophies: the Thomas Cup is the men's world team championship and the Uber Cup is the women's. India indeed won its maiden Thomas Cup in 2022, defeating Indonesia 3–0 in Bangkok. Statements 1 and 2 are correct, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
Source: Badminton World Federation (BWF) official records

128. Which of the following correctly arranges these events in chronological order of their first occurrence?
1. First modern Olympic Games
2. First FIFA World Cup
3. First Cricket World Cup (men's ODI)
4. First Asian Games
(a) 2-1-4-3 (b) 1-4-2-3 (c) 1-2-4-3 (d) 1-2-3-4
Answer: (c)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap lies between the FIFA World Cup (1930, Uruguay) and the Asian Games (1951, New Delhi) — many candidates swap them. Source: IOC, FIFA and Olympic Council of Asia official records

130. India won its first individual Olympic gold medal in which event and through which athlete?

Solution: The tempting trap is Neeraj Chopra, whose Tokyo javelin gold is fresher in memory, but it was India's second individual gold. 132. The term "Duckworth-Lewis-Stern (DLS) method" is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Baseball (c) Tennis (d) Cricket
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is baseball, which also has rain interruptions and statistical methods, but the DLS method is a mathematical formulation used to set revised targets in rain-affected limited-overs cricket matches. It was devised by Frank Duckworth and Tony Lewis and later updated by Steven Stern, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: International Cricket Council (ICC) playing conditions

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 and 2 only

150. Consider the following statements about Indian Olympic history:
1. Norman Pritchard won two silver medals for India at the 1900 Paris Olympics.
2. India first participated in the Olympic Games in 1900.
3. The Indian men's hockey team won its first Olympic gold in 1928 at Amsterdam.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1, 2 and 3 (d) 1 only
Answer: (c)
Solution: The trap is questioning Norman Pritchard's status, but the IOC credits his two silvers (200m and 200m hurdles, Paris 1900) to India, making 1900 India's Olympic debut. The hockey team's first gold came at Amsterdam 1928 under Jaipal Singh Munda, beginning a run of six consecutive titles. All three statements are correct, so the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: International Olympic Committee official records
