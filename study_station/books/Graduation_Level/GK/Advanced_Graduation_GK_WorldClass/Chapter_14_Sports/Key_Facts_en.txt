# Key Facts & Figures — Sports & Competitions

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| First Modern Olympics | Athens, 1896; revived by Pierre de Coubertin | Paris 1900 given as "first" | Near-correct year |
| Olympic Rings | 5 rings, 5 continents; designed by Coubertin (1913) | "6 rings" or "one ring per country" | Number distortion |
| Olympic Motto | "Citius, Altius, Fortius – Communiter" (Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together); "Communiter" added 2021 | Older motto without "Communiter" | Outdated fact |
| Winter Olympics first held | Chamonix, France, 1924 | Same year/city as Summer Games | Chronology confusion |
| IOC Headquarters | Lausanne, Switzerland; founded 1894 | Geneva (other sports bodies are there) | Similar-name city |
| FIFA founded | 1904, Paris; HQ Zurich | Founded in Zurich (HQ city) | City swap |
| First FIFA World Cup | Uruguay, 1930; Uruguay won | Italy 1934 as first | Near-correct year |
| Cricket World Cup (ODI) first | 1975, England; West Indies won | 1983 (India's win) as first | Famous-event confusion |
| India's first Olympic medal | Norman Pritchard, 2 silvers, Paris 1900 (athletics) | 1928 hockey gold as "first medal" | Team vs individual confusion |
| India's first individual Olympic gold | Abhinav Bindra, shooting, Beijing 2008 | Rajyavardhan Rathore (silver 2004) as gold | Medal-colour swap |
| India's first hockey Olympic gold | Amsterdam 1928 (as British India team) | 1948 London as first | Chronology confusion |
| Dhyan Chand | "Wizard of Hockey"; 3 Olympic golds (1928, 1932, 1936); National Sports Day = 29 August (his birthday) | 15 August or 26 January as Sports Day | Date confusion |
| Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award | India's highest sporting honour; renamed from Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna in 2021 | Old name in current-affairs questions | Outdated name |
| Arjuna Award | Instituted 1961; for consistent outstanding performance | "Highest sports award" claim | Rank confusion |
| Dronacharya Award | For coaches; instituted 1985 | Given to players | Category confusion |
| Dhyan Chand Award | Lifetime achievement in sports; instituted 2002 | Confused with Khel Ratna | Similar-name award |
| Wimbledon | Oldest tennis tournament (1877); grass court; London | "Clay court" or "hard court" | Surface swap |
| Grand Slam surfaces | Australian Open & US Open = hard; French Open = clay; Wimbledon = grass | French Open as grass | Surface swap |
| French Open venue | Roland Garros, Paris | Confused with stadium names of other Slams | Venue swap |
| Thomas Cup | Men's team badminton (world) | Confused with Uber Cup (women) | Gender swap |
| Uber Cup | Women's team badminton | Confused with Thomas Cup | Gender swap |
| Davis Cup | Men's team tennis | Confused with Fed Cup/Billie Jean King Cup (women) | Gender swap |
| Santosh Trophy | Indian football (inter-state) | Confused with Durand Cup | Tournament swap |
| Durand Cup | Oldest football tournament in Asia (1888), India | "Oldest in the world" | Degree exaggeration |
| Ranji Trophy | Domestic cricket (first-class), since 1934–35 | Confused with Irani/Duleep Trophy format | Format confusion |
| Commonwealth Games first | 1930, Hamilton, Canada (as British Empire Games) | 1954 or London as first | Chronology confusion |
| Asian Games first | 1951, New Delhi, India | Tokyo or Bangkok as first host | Venue confusion |
| Asian Games motto | "Ever Onward" | Olympic motto applied here | Motto swap |
| SAF/South Asian Games first | 1984, Kathmandu | Delhi as first host | Venue confusion |
| Paralympics | Held same year & city as Olympics since Seoul 1988 (formally) | "Separate year" claim | Chronology confusion |
| Chess Olympiad 2022 | Held in Chennai, India (44th edition) | "First time in India" applied to other events | Event confusion |
| Viswanathan Anand | India's first Grandmaster (1988); 5-time World Champion | "First Indian GM" given to another player | Person swap |
| Neeraj Chopra | Javelin gold, Tokyo 2020 Olympics — India's first Olympic gold in athletics | "First individual gold" (that is Bindra) | Category confusion |
| Mithali Raj | Leading run-scorer in women's international cricket (ODI) | Confused with T20 records | Format confusion |
| Sachin Tendulkar | 100 international centuries; first ODI double century (2010) | "First double century" given to others | Person swap |
| Bharat Ratna in sports | Sachin Tendulkar (2014) — first sportsperson | Dhyan Chand as recipient (he is not) | Common myth trap |
| National Games of India | First held 1924 (as Indian Olympic Games), Lahore | Post-independence year as first | Chronology confusion |
| Khelo India | Launched 2018; youth & university games | "Khelo India = professional league" | Scheme-type confusion |
| IPL founded | 2008; BCCI's T20 league | 2007 (T20 World Cup year) as start | Near-correct year |
| Hockey India League / ISL | ISL (football) started 2014 | Confused with I-League (2007, older) | League-age confusion |
| Mary Kom | 6-time World Boxing Champion; Olympic bronze 2012 | "Olympic gold" claim | Medal-colour swap |
| P.V. Sindhu | Badminton: silver Rio 2016, bronze Tokyo 2020 | "Gold at Rio" | Medal-colour swap |
| Sushil Kumar | Only Indian with two individual Olympic medals in wrestling (2008 bronze, 2012 silver) | "Two golds" | Medal-colour swap |
| Milkha Singh | "Flying Sikh"; 4th in 400m, Rome 1960 | "Won Olympic medal" | Myth trap |
| P.T. Usha | 4th in 400m hurdles, Los Angeles 1984; "Queen of Indian track" | Confused with Milkha Singh's event | Event swap |
| Karnam Malleswari | First Indian woman Olympic medallist (weightlifting bronze, Sydney 2000) | "First woman gold medallist" | Medal-colour swap |
| Manu Bhaker | Two bronze medals, Paris 2024 (shooting) — first Indian since independence to win two medals in a single Olympics edition | Norman Pritchard comparison ignored | Era confusion |
| Chess: Gukesh D | Youngest World Chess Champion (2024) | Anand as youngest | Person swap |
| Olympic 2024 host | Paris; 2028 = Los Angeles; 2032 = Brisbane | Order shuffled | Sequence confusion |
| FIFA World Cup 2022 | Qatar; Argentina won; 2026 = USA-Canada-Mexico | France as 2022 winner (runner-up) | Winner/runner-up swap |
| Cricket ODI World Cup 2023 | India host; Australia won (beat India in final) | India as winner | Winner/runner-up swap |
| T20 World Cup 2024 | India won (beat South Africa) | 2007 win mixed with 2024 | Edition confusion |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Topic | Type | Time | Score Maximizer? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Olympic facts (host cities, years, motto) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Sports awards (Khel Ratna, Arjuna, Dronacharya) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Trophies & associated sports (Thomas Cup, Ranji, Durand) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Firsts in Indian sports (Pritchard, Bindra, Malleswari) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Grand Slam surfaces & venues | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Tournament chronology (which came first) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Statement-based questions (multiple correct facts) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ No |
| Match-the-following (player–sport–award) | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ If well-revised |
| Medal-colour & edition traps (Sindhu, Sushil, Mary Kom) | Fact-based with trap | 15–30 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Sports bodies' HQs & founding years (IOC, FIFA, BCCI) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
