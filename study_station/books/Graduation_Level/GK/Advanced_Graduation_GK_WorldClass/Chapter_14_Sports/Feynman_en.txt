# 🧒 Feynman Technique — Sports & Competitions

## The Most Confusing Concept: "Which Games happen in which year — and who can play in them?"

Students mix up the **Olympics, Commonwealth Games, and Asian Games** all the time. The confusion is not about the years — it is about *who is allowed to play*. Let me explain it like you're 12.

### Think of it as three birthday parties 🎂

Imagine three birthday parties happening in your city:

1. **The Olympics = The Whole World's Party.**
   Everyone on Earth is invited — about 200+ countries. It happens every **4 years**, and it alternates: Summer Olympics, then 2 years later Winter Olympics, then 2 years later Summer again. So a Summer Olympics and a Winter Olympics are never in the same year anymore (they were, until 1992).

2. **The Commonwealth Games = The "Old Family" Party.**
   Only countries that were once part of the **British Empire** (now the Commonwealth of Nations) are invited — about 70+ nations/territories. India, England, Australia, Canada, Kenya come. **The USA does NOT come** — because it left the empire by war, not as a Commonwealth member. **China does NOT come** — it was never a British colony. This is the trap examiners love!

3. **The Asian Games (Asiad) = The Neighbourhood Party.**
   Only countries of **Asia** come — organised by the Olympic Council of Asia (OCA). So China, Japan, Korea, India all play here. Australia does **not** (it's not in Asia). Here India faces *much tougher* competition than in the Commonwealth Games, because China and Japan are Asian giants.

### The one-line memory trick 🪄

> **"Olympics = Earth, Commonwealth = Empire, Asiad = Asia."**
> Say it as: **"E-E-A"** — Earth, Empire, Asia.

And for the years: all three follow a **4-year cycle**, but staggered — so in most 4-year blocks, at least one big multi-sport event happens. (E.g., 2018 had both Commonwealth Games *and* Asian Games; 2020/21 had the Olympics.)

### The examiner's favourite traps 🎯

- "USA won X medals at the Commonwealth Games" — **impossible**, USA doesn't participate.
- "China topped the Commonwealth Games medal tally" — **impossible**, China isn't a member.
- "The Winter and Summer Olympics are held in the same year" — **was true till 1992, false since 1994**.
- "Asian Games are organised by the IOC" — **false**, they are organised by the **OCA** (Olympic Council of Asia); only the Olympics belong to the IOC.
