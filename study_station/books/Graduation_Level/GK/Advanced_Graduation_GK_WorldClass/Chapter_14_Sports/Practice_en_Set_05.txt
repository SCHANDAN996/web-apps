101. The Thomas Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Badminton (b) Lawn Tennis (c) Table Tennis (d) Squash
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Thomas Cup is the world men's team championship in badminton, first held in 1948–49. The tempting wrong option is Lawn Tennis, whose equivalent team trophy is the Davis Cup, so the answer is Badminton.

121. Consider the following statements about the Olympic motto:
P. The original Olympic motto was 'Citius, Altius, Fortius'.
Q. The word 'Communiter' (Together) was added to the motto in 2021.
Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) Only P (b) Both P and Q (c) Only Q (d) Neither P nor Q
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming the motto has never changed; the IOC amended it in 2021 to 'Citius, Altius, Fortius – Communiter' (Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together). Both statements are correct, so the answer is Both P and Q.
