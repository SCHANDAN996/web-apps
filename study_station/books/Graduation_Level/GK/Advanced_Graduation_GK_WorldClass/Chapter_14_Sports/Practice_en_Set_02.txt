Questions 26–50: Medium

26. The Durand Cup, one of the oldest football tournaments in the world, is associated with which country?
(a) India (b) England (c) Spain (d) Brazil
Answer: (a)
Solution: Many aspirants pick England assuming the oldest tournaments must be European, but the Durand Cup was started in 1888 in Shimla by Sir Mortimer Durand and is India's oldest football tournament, so the answer is India.
Source: PYQ-style

27. In which sport is the term "googly" used?
(a) Hockey (b) Tennis (c) Badminton (d) Cricket
Answer: (d)
Solution: A googly is a leg-spinner's delivery that turns the opposite way to a normal leg-break; it belongs to cricket, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

28. The Thomas Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Badminton (b) Table Tennis (c) Lawn Tennis (d) Squash
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap here is confusing Thomas Cup with the Davis Cup, which is for lawn tennis; the Thomas Cup is the world men's team championship in badminton, so the answer is Badminton.
Source: PYQ-style

29. Who was the first Indian to win an individual Olympic gold medal?
(a) Milkha Singh (b) Abhinav Bindra (c) Leander Paes (d) Neeraj Chopra
Answer: (b)
Solution: Neeraj Chopra is the tempting wrong choice because his javelin gold came in 2021, but Abhinav Bindra won the 10m air rifle gold at Beijing 2008, making him the first Indian individual Olympic gold medallist, so the answer is Abhinav Bindra.
Source: Official Olympic records

30. The Ranji Trophy is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey (b) Football (c) Cricket (d) Kabaddi
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Ranji Trophy, named after Ranjitsinhji, is India's premier domestic first-class cricket championship, started in 1934–35, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: PYQ-style

31. How many players are there in a standard hockey team on the field?
(a) 9 (b) 10 (c) 11 (d) 12
Answer: (c)
Solution: A hockey team fields 11 players including the goalkeeper, the same number as cricket and football, so the answer is 11.
Source: International Hockey Federation rules

32. The term "deuce" is used in which sport?
(a) Chess (b) Lawn Tennis (c) Boxing (d) Shooting
Answer: (b)
Solution: Deuce describes a 40–40 score in lawn tennis, after which a player must win two consecutive points; it is also used in badminton and table tennis scoring contexts, but classically it is a tennis term, so the answer is Lawn Tennis.
Source: PYQ-style

33. Which country hosted the first modern Olympic Games in 1896?
(a) France (b) England (c) Italy (d) Greece
Answer: (d)
Solution: France is tempting because the 1900 Games were in Paris and Pierre de Coubertin was French, but the first modern Olympics were held in Athens, Greece, in 1896, so the answer is Greece.
Source: Official Olympic records

34. The Santosh Trophy is associated with which sport?
(a) Cricket (b) Hockey (c) Volleyball (d) Football
Answer: (d)
Solution: The Santosh Trophy is India's premier inter-state football championship for senior men's teams, so the answer is Football.
Source: PYQ-style

35. In which year did India win its first Cricket World Cup?
(a) 1975 (b) 1983 (c) 1987 (d) 2011
Answer: (b)
Solution: 2011 is the tempting wrong answer because of recency, but India's first World Cup win came under Kapil Dev in 1983 at Lord's, defeating the West Indies, so the answer is 1983.
Source: Official cricket records

36. The term "checkmate" originates from which language?
(a) Persian (b) Latin (c) Greek (d) French
Answer: (a)
Solution: Checkmate comes from the Persian phrase "shah mat," meaning the king is helpless or defeated, reflecting chess's journey from India through Persia, so the answer is Persian.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

37. Which Indian woman won a bronze medal in boxing at the London 2012 Olympics?
(a) Mary Kom (b) Nikhat Zareen (c) Lovlina Borgohain (d) Sarita Devi
Answer: (a)
Solution: Lovlina Borgohain is the trap because she won bronze at Tokyo 2020, not London; M.C. Mary Kom won the bronze in the flyweight category at London 2012, so the answer is Mary Kom.
Source: Official Olympic records

38. The Dronacharya Award is given for excellence in which field?
(a) Sports coaching (b) Sports playing (c) Sports journalism (d) Sports administration
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is confusing it with the Arjuna Award or Khel Ratna, which honour players; the Dronacharya Award honours outstanding coaches, so the answer is Sports coaching.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

39. How often are the Summer Olympic Games held?
(a) Every 2 years (b) Every 3 years (c) Every 4 years (d) Every 5 years
Answer: (c)
Solution: The Summer Olympics follow a four-year cycle called an Olympiad, though the 2020 Games were postponed to 2021 due to the pandemic, so the answer is Every 4 years.
Source: Official Olympic records

40. The term "LBW" is associated with which sport?
(a) Baseball (b) Hockey (c) Cricket (d) Rugby
Answer: (c)
Solution: LBW stands for "leg before wicket," a mode of dismissal in cricket when the ball strikes the batter's pad in line with the stumps, so the answer is Cricket.
Source: PYQ-style

41. Which stadium is known as the "Mecca of Indian Cricket"?
(a) Wankhede Stadium (b) M. Chinnaswamy Stadium (c) Eden Gardens (d) Feroz Shah Kotla
Answer: (c)
Solution: Wankhede is tempting because the 2011 World Cup final was played there, but Eden Gardens in Kolkata, established in 1864, is traditionally called the Mecca of Indian cricket, so the answer is Eden Gardens.
Source: PYQ-style

42. In badminton, what is the minimum points margin needed to win a game?
(a) 1 point (b) 4 points (c) 3 points (d) 2 points
Answer: (d)
Solution: A badminton game is played to 21 points, but if the score reaches 20–20, a player must lead by 2 points (up to a cap of 30), so the answer is 2 points.
Source: Badminton World Federation rules

43. The Agha Khan Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Cricket (b) Hockey (c) Football (d) Polo
Answer: (b)
Solution: Polo is the tempting wrong answer because of the Agha Khan's association with equestrian sports, but the Agha Khan Cup is a hockey tournament, so the answer is Hockey.
Source: PYQ-style

44. Who is known as the "Flying Sikh" of India?
(a) P.T. Usha (b) Milkha Singh (c) Gurbachan Singh Randhawa (d) Makhan Singh
Answer: (b)
Solution: P.T. Usha is the trap as she is called the "Payyoli Express" and "Queen of Indian track," but the title "Flying Sikh" belongs to Milkha Singh, the 400m sprinter who finished fourth at Rome 1960, so the answer is Milkha Singh.
Source: NCERT-based general knowledge

45. The term "knockout" (KO) is primarily associated with which sport?
(a) Wrestling (b) Judo (c) Boxing (d) Fencing
Answer: (c)
Solution: A knockout occurs when a boxer is unable to rise within the referee's count of ten; while wrestling and judo have falls and ippon, KO is the classic boxing term, so the answer is Boxing.
Source: PYQ-style

46. Which of the following is the highest sports honour of India?
(a) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (b) Dronacharya Award (c) Arjuna Award (d) Dhyan Chand Award
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Arjuna Award is the tempting wrong choice as it is older and more famous, but the highest honour is the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award (renamed from Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna in 2021), so the answer is Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award.
Source: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India

47. In which sport did India win its first Olympic team gold medal in 1928?
(a) Football (b) Kabaddi (c) Cricket (d) Hockey
Answer: (d)
Solution: India's first Olympic gold came in field hockey at Amsterdam 1928, where Dhyan Chand starred; India went on to win six consecutive hockey golds from 1928 to 1956, so the answer is Hockey.
Source: Official Olympic records

48. The term "slam dunk" is used in which sport?
(a) Volleyball (b) Baseball (c) Handball (d) Basketball
Answer: (d)
Solution: A slam dunk is a forceful shot in basketball where the player jumps and scores by putting the ball directly through the hoop, so the answer is Basketball.
Source: PYQ-style

49. Which Grand Slam tennis tournament is played on clay courts?
(a) Wimbledon (b) French Open (c) US Open (d) Australian Open
Answer: (b)
Solution: Wimbledon is the tempting wrong answer because it is the most famous Slam, but it is played on grass; the French Open at Roland Garros is played on clay, so the answer is French Open.
Source: Official tournament records

50. The Rovers Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Football (b) Cricket (c) Hockey (d) Tennis
Answer: (a)
Solution: The Rovers Cup, historically held in Mumbai, is one of India's oldest football tournaments, started in 1890, so the answer is Football.
Source: PYQ-style
