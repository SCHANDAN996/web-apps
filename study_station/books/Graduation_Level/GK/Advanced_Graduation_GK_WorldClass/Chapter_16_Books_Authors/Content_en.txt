# Books & Authors

## 1. Why This Chapter Matters

Questions on famous books and their authors are among the most frequently asked static GK items in SSC, Railway, Banking and State exams. The good news: the facts in this chapter do not change. A book written in 1859 will have the same author forever. Learn this chapter once, revise it with the flashcards, and these become "free marks" in the exam.

---

## 2. A Short History of Books and Printing

- The earliest "books" were handwritten manuscripts on palm leaves, birch bark, cloth and later paper.
- **Johannes Gutenberg** (Germany) is credited with inventing the **movable-type printing press** in Europe around **1440–1450**. The **Gutenberg Bible** was printed around **1455** and is considered the first major book printed with movable type in Europe.
- Printing reached India with the Portuguese; the first printing press in India was set up at **Goa in 1556**.
- **James Augustus Hicky** started India's first newspaper, the **Bengal Gazette (1780)** — a newspaper, not a book, but examiners often club these facts together.

**Exam tip:** Do not confuse "first printed book in Europe" (Gutenberg Bible) with "first newspaper in India" (Bengal Gazette). This is a classic trap.

## 7. PYQ Pattern Analysis (How Exams Ask This Chapter)

Based on the pattern of questions asked in SSC, Railway, Bank and State exams:

1. **Direct match questions** — "Who wrote *The Guide*?" This is the most common format. The tables above cover the vast majority of such questions.
2. **Award-linked questions** — Booker Prize and Nobel Prize winning books by Indian authors are asked again and again. Remember the year with the award.
3. **Confusing-pair traps** — Examiners love near-correct options:
   - *Akbarnama* — Abul Fazl (not Faizi)
   - *1984* and *Animal Farm* — both George Orwell (not Aldous Huxley)
   - *Gitanjali* — Tagore (not Sarojini Naidu)
4. **Ancient texts** — *Arthashastra* (Kautilya), *Rajatarangini* (Kalhana) and *Indica* (Megasthenes) appear very frequently, often mixed with history questions.
5. **Autobiographies** — *My Experiments with Truth* (Gandhi), *Wings of Fire* (Kalam), *Long Walk to Freedom* (Mandela) are evergreen questions.
6. **Negative questions** — "Which of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched?" Read all four pairs fully before answering; three will be obviously correct, and the wrong pair usually swaps authors of two famous books.

**Strategy:** In the exam, a books-and-authors question should take under 15 seconds. If you have revised the tables, answer instantly and move on — do not second-guess a fact you have memorised.
