101. Who wrote the novel "The Guide", later adapted into an acclaimed film?
(a) Mulk Raj Anand (b) R. K. Narayan (c) Raja Rao (d) Khushwant Singh
Answer: (b)
Solution: Mulk Raj Anand wrote "Untouchable" and "Coolie", which tempts many students, but "The Guide" (1958) is by R. K. Narayan, set in the fictional town of Malgudi. It won him the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1960, so the answer is R. K. Narayan.
Source: NCERT Class 11 English (Indian Writing in English)

102. "The God of Small Things", winner of the Booker Prize in 1997, was written by:
(a) Arundhati Roy (b) Kiran Desai (c) Anita Desai (d) Jhumpa Lahiri
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kiran Desai also won the Booker (2006, "The Inheritance of Loss"), which creates confusion, but the 1997 Booker went to Arundhati Roy for her debut novel "The God of Small Things", so the answer is Arundhati Roy.
Source: Booker Prize official records (thebookerprizes.com)

103. Who is the author of the Sanskrit play "Abhijnanashakuntalam"?
(a) Kalidasa (b) Bhasa (c) Shudraka (d) Vishakhadatta
Answer: (a)
Solution: Vishakhadatta wrote "Mudrarakshasa" and Shudraka wrote "Mrichchhakatika", which are common distractors, but "Abhijnanashakuntalam" is Kalidasa's masterpiece about Shakuntala and King Dushyanta, so the answer is Kalidasa.
Source: NCERT Class 11 (An Introduction to Indian Art)

104. "My Experiments with Truth" is the autobiography of:
(a) M. K. Gandhi (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) B. R. Ambedkar (d) Sardar Patel
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nehru's autobiography is "An Autobiography" (also called "Toward Freedom"), which is the tempting wrong option, but "The Story of My Experiments with Truth" was written by M. K. Gandhi, originally in Gujarati, so the answer is M. K. Gandhi.
Source: NCERT Class 8 History

105. Who wrote the epic poem "Meghaduta" (The Cloud Messenger)?

Answer: (b)
Solution: Banabhatta wrote "Harshacharita" and "Kadambari", which confuses students, but "Meghaduta", where a yaksha sends a message through a cloud, is by Kalidasa, so the answer is Kalidasa.
Source: NCERT Class 11 (An Introduction to Indian Art)

121. Consider the following statements about the book "Hind Swaraj":
1. It was written by Mahatma Gandhi in 1909.
2. It was originally written in English.
3. It was written during Gandhi's return voyage from London to South Africa.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 1 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1 and 3 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: Statement 2 is the trap: "Hind Swaraj" was originally written in Gujarati (in "Indian Opinion"), not English, though Gandhi later translated it. It was written in 1909 aboard the ship Kildonan Castle while returning from London to South Africa, so statements 1 and 3 are correct, and the answer is 1 and 3 only.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Political Science (Political Theory / Gandhi)

122. Match the following authors with their works:
1. Jhumpa Lahiri — Interpreter of Maladies
2. Rohinton Mistry — A Fine Balance
3. Amitav Ghosh — The Glass Palace
4. Chitra Banerjee Divakaruni — The Palace of Illusions
How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?
(a) Only one (b) All four (c) Only three (d) Only two
Answer: (b)
Solution: The trap is assuming one pair must be wrong in such questions, but all four are correct: Lahiri's "Interpreter of Maladies" won the 2000 Pulitzer, Mistry wrote "A Fine Balance", Ghosh wrote "The Glass Palace", and Divakaruni retold the Mahabharata in "The Palace of Illusions", so the answer is all four.
Source: PYQ-style

123. Which of the following books was written first?
(a) "Gitanjali" (English) by Rabindranath Tagore (b) "Untouchable" by Mulk Raj Anand (c) "The Guide" by R. K. Narayan (d) "Train to Pakistan" by Khushwant Singh
Answer: (a)
Solution: The chronological trap: "Untouchable" (1935) feels oldest because of its theme, but the English "Gitanjali" appeared in 1912, "The Guide" in 1958, and "Train to Pakistan" in 1956, so the earliest is "Gitanjali", and the answer is "Gitanjali" (English) by Rabindranath Tagore.
Source: Nobel Prize official site (nobelprize.org)

124. Consider the following statements:
1. "Rajatarangini" is a historical chronicle of Kashmir written by Kalhana.
2. "Mudrarakshasa" by Vishakhadatta deals with the rise of Chandragupta Maurya.
3. "Kadambari" was completed by Banabhatta's son Bhushanabhatta.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is statement 3, which sounds invented but is true: Banabhatta died leaving "Kadambari" unfinished, and his son Bhushanabhatta completed it. Statements 1 and 2 are standard facts, so all three are correct, and the answer is 1, 2 and 3.
Source: NCERT Class 11 (An Introduction to Indian Art) / NCERT Class 6 History

125. Who wrote "Annihilation of Caste", originally prepared as a presidential address for the Jat-Pat Todak Mandal in 1936?
(a) Jyotiba Phule (b) Mahatma Gandhi (c) Periyar E. V. Ramasamy (d) B. R. Ambedkar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Jyotiba Phule wrote "Gulamgiri" (1873), the tempting wrong option for caste-critique questions, but "Annihilation of Caste" was written by B. R. Ambedkar; the Mandal cancelled the invitation after reading the text, and Ambedkar published it himself, so the answer is B. R. Ambedkar.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Sociology (Indian Society)
