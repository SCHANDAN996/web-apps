51. Who wrote the novel "The Guide", which was also adapted into a famous Hindi film?
(a) Mulk Raj Anand (b) Raja Rao (c) R. K. Narayan (d) Khushwant Singh
Answer: (c)
Solution: Mulk Raj Anand wrote "Untouchable" and "Coolie", not "The Guide" — a common confusion among Indian English novelists. "The Guide" (1958) is by R. K. Narayan, set in the fictional town of Malgudi, and was adapted into the 1965 film "Guide" starring Dev Anand, so the answer is R. K. Narayan.
Source: PYQ-style

52. "An Autobiography: The Story of My Experiments with Truth" was originally written by Mahatma Gandhi in which language?
(a) Hindi (b) Gujarati (c) English (d) Marathi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Many assume Gandhi wrote it in English because the famous translation is in English — that is the trap. Gandhi wrote it in Gujarati (serialised in Navajivan), and Mahadev Desai translated it into English, so the answer is Gujarati.
Source: NCERT Class 10 History

54. Who is the author of the epic poem "Meghaduta" (The Cloud Messenger)?
(a) Kalidasa (b) Bhasa (c) Bharavi (d) Magha
Answer: (a)
Solution: Bharavi wrote "Kiratarjuniya" and Magha wrote "Shishupalavadha" — similar-sounding classical Sanskrit poets form the trap. "Meghaduta", a lyric poem about a yaksha sending a message through a cloud, is by Kalidasa, so the answer is Kalidasa.
Source: NCERT Class 11 (An Introduction to Indian Art / Sanskrit literature)
