# Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Books & Authors

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 1: "GOD of Small Things — ARUNDHATI"

**Hook:** *"Arundhati Roy saw GOD in SMALL things."*
Arundhati Roy → **The God of Small Things** (Booker Prize 1997).
Trap: Don't confuse with Kiran Desai's *The Inheritance of Loss* (Booker 2006).

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 2: "MIDNIGHT's Children were RUSHED"

**Hook:** *"At MIDNIGHT, children were RUSHED to the hospital."*
Salman **RUSHDIE** → **Midnight's Children** (Booker 1981, "Booker of Bookers" 1993).

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 3: "GITA-njali won the NOBEL"

**Hook:** *"Tagore sang the GITA-njali and the NOBEL came calling."*
Rabindranath Tagore → **Gitanjali** → Nobel Prize in Literature **1913** (first Asian Nobel laureate in Literature).

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 4: "AMITAV sailed the IBIS"

**Hook:** *"AMITAV sailed the IBIS across the SEA of POPPIES."*
Amitav Ghosh → *Sea of Poppies*, *River of Smoke*, *Flood of Fire* (the **Ibis trilogy**).

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 5: "PREMCHAND's final gift — GODAN"

**Hook:** *"Premchand's GODAN (gift of a cow) was his last gift to literature."*
Munshi Premchand → **Godan** (1936), his last complete novel. Also: *Gaban*, *Nirmala*, *Karmabhoomi*.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 6: "MEGHADUTA — KALIDASA's cloud messenger"

**Hook:** *"KALIDASA sent a MEGHA (cloud) as a DUTA (messenger)."*
Kalidasa → **Meghaduta**, **Abhijnanashakuntalam**, **Kumarasambhava**, **Raghuvamsha**.
Trap: *Abhijnanashakuntalam* is a play; *Meghaduta* is a lyric poem.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 7: "Discovery of INDIA — written in JAIL"

**Hook:** *"NEHRU DISCOVERED INDIA while locked inside it."*
Jawaharlal Nehru wrote **The Discovery of India** in Ahmednagar Fort prison (1942–46).
Also Nehru: *Glimpses of World History*, *An Autobiography*.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 8: "MY EXPERIMENTS with TRUTH — GANDHI's lab"

**Hook:** *"GANDHI's laboratory was TRUTH itself."*
M.K. Gandhi → **The Story of My Experiments with Truth** (autobiography).
Trap: *Hind Swaraj* is also Gandhi — don't attribute it to Nehru.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 9: "ANANDAMATH — the BANKIM's Vande Mataram"

**Hook:** *"BANKIM's monks sang VANDE MATARAM in ANANDAMATH."*
Bankim Chandra Chatterjee → **Anandamath** (1882), which contains the song *Vande Mataram*.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 10: "Train to PAKISTAN — KHUSHWANT drove it"

**Hook:** *"KHUSHWANT SINGH drove the TRAIN TO PAKISTAN."*
Khushwant Singh → **Train to Pakistan** (1956). Also: *Delhi: A Novel*, *The Company of Women*.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 11: "Guide R.K. NARAYAN to MALGUDI"

**Hook:** *"R.K. NARAYAN is the GUIDE to MALGUDI town."*
R.K. Narayan → **The Guide**, **Malgudi Days**, **Swami and Friends** — all set in fictional **Malgudi**.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 12: "WINGS of FIRE — the MISSILE MAN's autobiography"

**Hook:** *"KALAM's missiles had WINGS OF FIRE."*
A.P.J. Abdul Kalam → **Wings of Fire** (autobiography), *Ignited Minds*, *India 2020*.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 13: "The WHITE TIGER roared to the BOOKER"

**Hook:** *"ARAVIND ADIGA's WHITE TIGER hunted down the BOOKER."*
Aravind Adiga → **The White Tiger** (Booker Prize **2008**, debut novel).

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 14: "Mahabharata = VYASA; Ramayana = VALMIKI"

**Hook:** *"VYASA's war (Mahabharata), VALMIKI's journey (Ramayana)."*
Ved Vyasa → **Mahabharata**; Valmiki → **Ramayana** (Sanskrit). Tulsidas → **Ramcharitmanas** (Awadhi).
Trap: Ramcharitmanas ≠ Valmiki Ramayana — different authors, different languages.

---

## 🪄 Mnemonic 15: "ARTHASHASTRA — CHANAKYA's economics class"

**Hook:** *"CHANAKYA taught ARTHA (economics) and SHASTRA (science) to Chandragupta."*
Kautilya (Chanakya/Vishnugupta) → **Arthashastra**.
Trap: Don't confuse with Kalidasa (poet) — both linked to ancient courts.

---

# ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

**Tip 1 — The "one blank in a list" rule:**
If a question asks you to match **four authors with four books** and you are sure of only two or three, **skip immediately**. Matching questions are all-or-nothing; one wrong pairing flips the answer. Don't burn 60 seconds hoping the fourth pair "feels right."

**Tip 2 — The "year + prize" double trap:**
Questions like *"In which year did X win the Booker?"* with options 1993 / 1997 / 2006 / 2008 are pure memory gambles. If you don't recall the year within **15 seconds**, mark and skip — the options are deliberately clustered around famous Booker years (Roy 1997, Desai 2006, Adiga 2008) to lure guessers.

**Tip 3 — The "original language" trap:**
If asked the original language of a work (e.g., *Gitanjali* — Bengali, not English; *Ramcharitmanas* — Awadhi, not Sanskrit) and you have any doubt, **skip**. Examiners deliberately place the "translated-into" language as an option. Attempt only if you can recall the original with certainty.
