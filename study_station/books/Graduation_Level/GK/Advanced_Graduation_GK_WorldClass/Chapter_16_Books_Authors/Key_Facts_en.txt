## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Fact Type | Category | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct "Who wrote X?" questions | Fact-based | 15 sec | Pure recall — answer instantly, never overthink |
| Award–book–author match (Booker, Nobel, Jnanpith) | Fact-based | 15 sec | **Score Maximizer** — high frequency, zero calculation |
| Autobiography identification | Fact-based | 15 sec | **Score Maximizer** — small fixed list, easy to memorise |
| Ancient/classical texts (Kalidasa, Kautilya, Kalhana) | Fact-based | 15 sec | Fixed list; revise once, score always |
| Statement-based questions ("Which of the following pairs is/are correctly matched?") | Analytical | 45 sec | Check each pair; one wrong pair eliminates options |
| Chronology questions (order of publication, sequence of a trilogy) | Analytical | 45 sec | Verify first/last items first — often enough to eliminate |
| "First" claims (first Indian Booker, first Hindi International Booker) | Analytical | 45 sec | Trap-dense; read qualifiers (International vs original Booker) carefully |
| Negative questions ("Which is NOT written by…") | Analytical | 45 sec | Underline NOT; check all four options before answering |
| Language-of-origin questions (Baburnama, Gitanjali, Gandhi's autobiography) | Fact-based | 15 sec | Small fixed list — memorise as a group |
| Film-adaptation links (Guide, 3 Idiots, Pather Panchali) | Fact-based | 15 sec | Recall-based; skip if unseen — low ROI on guessing |

**Score Maximizer subjects:** Award–author matching, autobiographies, classical Sanskrit texts, and Indian English novelists — these are pure-recall, appear repeatedly across SSC/Bank/Railway/PCS papers, and can be answered in under 15 seconds each.
