1. Who is the author of the book "The Discovery of India"?
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) B. R. Ambedkar (c) Rabindranath Tagore (d) Jawaharlal Nehru
Answer: (d)
Solution: Mahatma Gandhi wrote "Hind Swaraj" and "My Experiments with Truth", which is a tempting wrong choice because both leaders wrote books during imprisonment. "The Discovery of India" was written by Jawaharlal Nehru during his imprisonment at Ahmednagar Fort (1942–46), so the answer is Jawaharlal Nehru.
Source: PYQ-style

2. It was originally written in English.

(a) Neither 1 nor 2 (b) 2 only (c) Both 1 and 2 (d) 1 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: The trap is statement 2, which sounds plausible because Gandhi wrote for a wide audience, but "Hind Swaraj" was originally written in Gujarati in 1909 aboard a ship during his return from London to South Africa. Only statement 1 is correct, so the answer is 1 only.
