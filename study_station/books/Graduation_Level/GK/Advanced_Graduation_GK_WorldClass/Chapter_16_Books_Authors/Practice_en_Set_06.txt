126. Which book, written by Jawaharlal Nehru during his imprisonment in Ahmednagar Fort (1942–1945), presents a sweeping view of India's history, culture and philosophy?
(a) The Discovery of India (b) Glimpses of World History (c) An Autobiography (d) Letters from a Father to His Daughter
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is Glimpses of World History, but it was written earlier (1930–1933) in Naini and other prisons as letters to Indira Gandhi and deals with world, not Indian, history. The Discovery of India was written at Ahmednagar Fort between 1944 and 1946, so the answer is The Discovery of India.
Source: PYQ-style

127. Who among the following wrote the Sanskrit play 'Mrichchhakatika' (The Little Clay Cart)?
(a) Kalidasa (b) Bhasa (c) Shudraka (d) Vishakhadatta
Answer: (c)
Solution: Vishakhadatta is a trap because he wrote the political drama Mudrarakshasa, whose title sounds similar to Mrichchhakatika. Bhasa wrote Swapnavasavadatta and Kalidasa wrote Abhijnanashakuntalam, so the answer is Shudraka.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

128. 'The Guide', later adapted into a famous Hindi film, was written by which author?
(a) R. K. Narayan (b) Mulk Raj Anand (c) Raja Rao (d) Khushwant Singh
Answer: (a)
Solution: Mulk Raj Anand is tempting because he too was a pioneer of Indian English fiction, but his famous works are Untouchable and Coolie. The Guide (1958), set in the fictional town of Malgudi, won R. K. Narayan the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1960, so the answer is R. K. Narayan.
Source: PYQ-style

129. Which of the following books was written by Kautilya (Chanakya)?
(a) Rajatarangini (b) Arthashastra (c) Harshacharita (d) Kadambari
Answer: (b)
Solution: Rajatarangini is a trap as it is also a classic Sanskrit text, but it is Kalhana's chronicle of the kings of Kashmir. Harshacharita and Kadambari are by Banabhatta. Kautilya's treatise on statecraft is the Arthashastra, so the answer is Arthashastra.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

130. 'Wings of Fire', the autobiography of Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, was co-authored with whom?
(a) Roddam Narasimha (b) Arun Tiwari (c) Y. S. Rajan (d) Satish Dhawan
Answer: (b)
Solution: Y. S. Rajan is the trap because he co-authored 'India 2020' with Kalam, and examiners exploit this confusion. Satish Dhawan was Kalam's mentor at ISRO, not a co-author. Wings of Fire (1999) was written with Arun Tiwari, so the answer is Arun Tiwari.
Source: PYQ-style

131. Who wrote the novel 'The God of Small Things', which won the Booker Prize in 1997?
(a) Kiran Desai (b) Jhumpa Lahiri (c) Anita Desai (d) Arundhati Roy
Answer: (d)
Solution: Kiran Desai is the tempting wrong option because she also won the Booker Prize (2006, for The Inheritance of Loss), and examiners pair the two. Jhumpa Lahiri won the Pulitzer, not the Booker, for Interpreter of Maladies. The 1997 Booker went to Arundhati Roy, so the answer is Arundhati Roy.
Source: PYQ-style

132. 'Meghaduta' (The Cloud Messenger), a lyric poem, was composed by which classical Sanskrit poet?
(a) Bharavi (b) Magha (c) Kalidasa (d) Dandin
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bharavi (Kiratarjuniya) and Magha (Shishupalavadha) are traps because they too wrote Sanskrit mahakavyas. Meghaduta, in which an exiled yaksha sends a message through a cloud, is one of Kalidasa's finest works, so the answer is Kalidasa.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

133. Which book by Amartya Sen discusses the relationship between economic development and freedom?
(a) The Argumentative Indian (b) Development as Freedom (c) Poverty and Famines (d) Identity and Violence
Answer: (b)
Solution: The Argumentative Indian is tempting as it is Sen's most popular essay collection, but it deals with India's traditions of public debate. His 1999 work arguing that development should be seen as the expansion of human freedoms is Development as Freedom, so the answer is Development as Freedom.
Source: PYQ-style

134. 'Ain-i-Akbari', which details the administration of Akbar's empire, was written by whom?
(a) Nizamuddin Ahmad (b) Abul Fazl (c) Badauni (d) Faizi
Answer: (b)
Solution: Badauni is the trap because he too wrote about Akbar's reign, but his Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh was secretly critical of the emperor. Ain-i-Akbari is the third volume of Abul Fazl's Akbarnama, so the answer is Abul Fazl.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

135. Who is the author of the novel 'Midnight's Children', which won the Booker of Bookers?
(a) Vikram Seth (b) Salman Rushdie (c) Amitav Ghosh (d) Rohinton Mistry
Answer: (b)
Solution: Vikram Seth is tempting because his A Suitable Boy is another landmark Indian English novel, but it never won the Booker. Midnight's Children (1981) won the Booker Prize and later the Booker of Bookers (1993) and Best of the Booker (2008), so the answer is Salman Rushdie.
Source: PYQ-style

136. 'Hind Swaraj', a critique of modern civilisation written in 1909, was authored by whom?
(a) Bal Gangadhar Tilak (b) Aurobindo Ghosh (c) Mahatma Gandhi (d) Rabindranath Tagore
Answer: (c)
Solution: Aurobindo Ghosh is a trap because he also wrote political-philosophical works in the same era, such as The Life Divine. Hind Swaraj was written by Gandhi aboard the ship Kildonan Castle during his return from London to South Africa, so the answer is Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Themes in Indian History

137. Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
(a) Gitanjali — Sarat Chandra Chatterjee (b) Nil Darpan — Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar (c) Anandamath — Bankim Chandra Chatterjee (d) Godan — Harishankar Parsai
Answer: (c)
Solution: The other pairs are traps: Nil Darpan was written by Dinabandhu Mitra, Gitanjali by Rabindranath Tagore, and Godan by Munshi Premchand. Anandamath (1882), which contains the song Vande Mataram, is by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee, so the answer is Anandamath — Bankim Chandra Chatterjee.
Source: NCERT Class 10 India and the Contemporary World

138. 'The Inheritance of Loss', winner of the Man Booker Prize 2006, was written by whom?
(a) Aravind Adiga (b) Kiran Desai (c) Anita Desai (d) Jeet Thayil
Answer: (b)
Solution: Aravind Adiga is the tempting option because he won the Booker in 2008 for The White Tiger, and examiners swap the two years. Anita Desai, Kiran Desai's mother, was shortlisted thrice but never won. The 2006 winner was Kiran Desai, so the answer is Kiran Desai.
Source: PYQ-style

139. Who wrote 'Poverty and Un-British Rule in India', propounding the drain of wealth theory?
(a) Dadabhai Naoroji (b) R. C. Dutt (c) M. G. Ranade (d) Gopal Krishna Gokhale
Answer: (a)
Solution: R. C. Dutt is the trap because his Economic History of India also analysed colonial economic exploitation. The systematic drain of wealth theory, however, was presented by Dadabhai Naoroji in Poverty and Un-British Rule in India (1901), so the answer is Dadabhai Naoroji.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Themes in Indian History

140. 'Train to Pakistan', a novel about the Partition, was written by which author?
(a) Bhisham Sahni (b) Saadat Hasan Manto (c) Khushwant Singh (d) Amrita Pritam
Answer: (c)
Solution: Bhisham Sahni is tempting because his novel Tamas is also a celebrated Partition narrative, and Amrita Pritam wrote Pinjar on the same theme. Train to Pakistan (1956), set in the fictional village Mano Majra, is by Khushwant Singh, so the answer is Khushwant Singh.
Source: PYQ-style

141. Which ancient Tamil epic, attributed to Ilango Adigal, tells the story of Kannagi and Kovalan?
(a) Manimekalai (b) Tirukkural (c) Tolkappiyam (d) Silappadikaram
Answer: (d)
Solution: Manimekalai is the classic trap because it is the twin epic of Silappadikaram, but it was written by Sattanar and deals with Kovalan's daughter Manimekalai. The story of Kannagi's quest for justice is Silappadikaram, so the answer is Silappadikaram.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

142. 'My Experiments with Truth' is the autobiography of which national leader?
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Subhas Chandra Bose (d) B. R. Ambedkar
Answer: (a)
Solution: Nehru is tempting because his An Autobiography (1936) is equally famous, and examiners exploit this pairing. Gandhi's autobiography, originally written in Gujarati as Satya na Prayogo, covers his life up to 1921, so the answer is Mahatma Gandhi.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Themes in Indian History

143. Who authored 'The White Tiger', winner of the Man Booker Prize 2008?
(a) Aravind Adiga (b) Kiran Desai (c) Salman Rushdie (d) Vikram Seth
Answer: (a)
Solution: Kiran Desai is the trap because she won the Booker just two years earlier (2006), and examiners test whether candidates confuse consecutive Indian winners. The White Tiger, narrated by Balram Halwai, won in 2008 for Aravind Adiga, so the answer is Aravind Adiga.
Source: PYQ-style

144. 'Kitab-ul-Hind', an account of Indian sciences, religion and society in the 11th century, was written by whom?
(a) Ibn Battuta (b) Al-Masudi (c) Firdausi (d) Al-Biruni
Answer: (d)
Solution: Ibn Battuta is the tempting wrong option because he too wrote a famous travel account of India, but he came in the 14th century and wrote the Rihla. Al-Biruni accompanied Mahmud of Ghazni and composed Kitab-ul-Hind in Arabic, so the answer is Al-Biruni.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

145. Which of the following books was NOT written by Munshi Premchand?

146. 'The Discovery of India' was written by Jawaharlal Nehru during his imprisonment at which place?
(a) Ahmednagar Fort (b) Naini Central Prison (c) Cellular Jail, Andaman (d) Yerwada Jail
Answer: (a)
Solution: Naini Central Prison is the trap because Nehru wrote Glimpses of World History there, and examiners swap the two prison-book pairs. The Discovery of India was composed at Ahmednagar Fort during 1944–1946, so the answer is Ahmednagar Fort.

147. Who wrote the Hindi novel 'Maila Anchal', a landmark of the Anchalik (regional) movement?
(a) Bhisham Sahni (b) Srilal Shukla (c) Nagarjun (d) Phanishwar Nath Renu
Answer: (d)
Solution: Srilal Shukla is tempting because his Raag Darbari is another great regional Hindi novel, but it is a satire on village politics. Maila Anchal (1954), depicting the village Maryganj in Bihar, is by Phanishwar Nath Renu, so the answer is Phanishwar Nath Renu.
Source: PYQ-style

148. 'Annihilation of Caste', originally written as an undelivered presidential address in 1936, was authored by whom?
(a) Jyotiba Phule (b) Mahatma Gandhi (c) Periyar E. V. Ramasamy (d) B. R. Ambedkar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Jyotiba Phule is the trap because he too wrote against caste, but his work is Gulamgiri (1873). Ambedkar wrote Annihilation of Caste for the Jat-Pat Todak Mandal, which cancelled the invitation after reading the text, so the answer is B. R. Ambedkar.
Source: NCERT Class 12 Themes in Indian History

149. Which Mughal prince translated the Upanishads into Persian under the title 'Sirr-i-Akbar'?
(a) Aurangzeb (b) Shah Jahan (c) Dara Shikoh (d) Murad
Answer: (c)
Solution: Aurangzeb is the tempting option because he was Dara Shikoh's brother and rival, but he was orthodox and opposed such syncretic projects. Dara Shikoh translated about fifty Upanishads into Persian in 1657, so the answer is Dara Shikoh.
Source: NCERT Class 11 Themes in Indian History

150. 'The Argumentative Indian' is a collection of essays by which Nobel laureate economist?
(a) Manmohan Singh (b) Abhijit Banerjee (c) Jagdish Bhagwati (d) Amartya Sen
Answer: (d)
Solution: Abhijit Banerjee is the trap because he is also an Indian Nobel laureate economist (2019), but his famous book is Poor Economics, co-written with Esther Duflo. The Argumentative Indian (2005) is by Amartya Sen, Nobel laureate of 1998, so the answer is Amartya Sen.
Source: PYQ-style
