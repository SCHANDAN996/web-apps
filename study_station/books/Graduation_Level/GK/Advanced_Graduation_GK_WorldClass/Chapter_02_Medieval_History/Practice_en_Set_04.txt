76. Which Delhi Sultan is credited with introducing the Iqta system, under which land revenue assignments were given to officers?
(a) Iltutmish (b) Qutbuddin Aibak (c) Balban (d) Alauddin Khalji
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is Qutbuddin Aibak because he founded the Sultanate, but it was Iltutmish who organised the iqta system, granting revenue of territories to officers in return for military service. So the answer is Iltutmish.
Source: NCERT Class 7 History

Answer: (b)
Solution: The tempting option is Alauddin Khalji because of his strong rule, but it was Balban who introduced Sijda and Paibos to raise the Sultan's prestige above the nobles. So the answer is Balban.

96. Consider the following statements about the iqta system:
1. Iqtadars collected revenue from their assigned territories.
2. Iqtadars were required to maintain troops for the Sultan.
3. Under the early Sultans, iqta assignments were always hereditary.
Which of the above statements are correct?
(a) 1, 2 and 3 (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting option is "1, 2 and 3", but statement 3 is false because early iqtas were transferable assignments, not hereditary property. Statements 1 and 2 correctly describe the iqtadar's duties, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.

100. Consider the following statements about Balban:
1. He strengthened the Sultanate by breaking the power of the Chahalgani (Group of Forty).
2. He claimed divine status for kingship through the theory of Zil-i-Ilahi.
3. He introduced token currency of bronze.
Which of the above statements are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: The tempting option is "1, 2 and 3", but token currency was introduced by Muhammad bin Tughlaq, not Balban. Balban crushed the Chahalgani and propounded the divine theory of kingship, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.
