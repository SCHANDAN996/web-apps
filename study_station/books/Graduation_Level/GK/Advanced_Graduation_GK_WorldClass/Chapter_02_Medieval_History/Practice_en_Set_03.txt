51. Who among the following was the first Sultan of Delhi to introduce the system of branding of horses (dagh) and descriptive rolls of soldiers (chehra)?
(a) Balban (b) Alauddin Khilji (c) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (d) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Answer: (b)
Solution: Balban is the tempting wrong option because he strengthened the army and introduced the Persian court customs of sijda and paibos, but the dagh and chehra reforms were military-market measures of Alauddin Khilji, aimed at preventing fraud in the cavalry. So the answer is Alauddin Khilji.

52. The famous traveller Ibn Battuta, who visited India during the reign of Muhammad bin Tughlaq, belonged to which country?
(a) Persia (b) Afghanistan (c) Turkey (d) Morocco
Answer: (d)
Solution: Persia is a common trap because many medieval travellers such as Al-Biruni came from the Persian-Central Asian region, but Ibn Battuta was a Moroccan traveller whose account is recorded in the Rihla. So the answer is Morocco.

(a) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (b) Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq (c) Alauddin Khilji (d) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Answer: (a)
Solution: Firoz Shah Tughlaq is tempting because he founded several new cities like Firozabad and Hisar, but the transfer of the capital to Daulatabad around 1327 was the controversial decision of Muhammad bin Tughlaq. So the answer is Muhammad bin Tughlaq.

54. The 'Iqta system' under the Delhi Sultanate referred to:
(a) A tax on non-Muslims (b) Land revenue assignments given to officers in lieu of salary (c) A market regulation policy (d) A system of hereditary kingship
Answer: (b)
Solution: The tax on non-Muslims is the trap because jizya is often confused with iqta in exam questions; iqta was actually a territorial assignment whose holder (iqtadar) collected revenue to maintain troops and draw his salary. So the answer is land revenue assignments given to officers in lieu of salary.

(a) Isami (b) Minhaj-us-Siraj (c) Amir Khusrau (d) Ziauddin Barani
Answer: (d)
Solution: Minhaj-us-Siraj is the tempting option because he also wrote a famous chronicle, but his work was the Tabaqat-i-Nasiri, whereas the Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi was composed by Ziauddin Barani. So the answer is Ziauddin Barani.

56. The First Battle of Panipat (1526) was fought between Babur and:
(a) Rana Sanga (b) Sikandar Lodi (c) Ibrahim Lodi (d) Mahmud Lodi
Answer: (c)
Solution: Rana Sanga is the classic chronological trap because Babur fought him at Khanwa in 1527, one year after Panipat; at the First Battle of Panipat Babur defeated and killed the Delhi Sultan Ibrahim Lodi. So the answer is Ibrahim Lodi.

57. Which Mughal emperor introduced the 'Dahsala' (Zabti) system of land revenue with the help of Raja Todar Mal?
(a) Babur (b) Humayun (c) Jahangir (d) Akbar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Sher Shah Suri's earlier revenue reforms make candidates hesitate, but the Dahsala system of 1580, based on the average produce and prices of ten years, was finalised under Akbar by his revenue minister Raja Todar Mal. So the answer is Akbar.

58. The 'Din-i-Ilahi', propounded by Akbar in 1582, was:
(a) A syncretic ethical code drawing on several faiths (b) A new religion with its own scriptures and priests (c) A sect of Shia Islam (d) A Sufi order founded at Fatehpur Sikri
Answer: (a)
Solution: The trap is calling it a full-fledged new religion; Din-i-Ilahi had no scriptures, no priesthood and very few followers, and was essentially an ethical-spiritual fellowship based on sulh-i-kul (universal peace). So the answer is a syncretic ethical code drawing on several faiths.

59. The Battle of Haldighati (1576) was fought between Akbar's forces led by Man Singh and:
(a) Amar Singh of Mewar (b) Rana Sanga of Mewar (c) Udai Singh of Mewar (d) Rana Pratap of Mewar
Answer: (d)
Solution: Rana Sanga is the similar-name trap because he was also a famous Mewar ruler, but he fought Babur at Khanwa in 1527; Haldighati was fought by his grandson Rana Pratap against the Mughal army. So the answer is Rana Pratap of Mewar.

(a) Mughal and Rajput armies (b) Maratha chiefs (c) Portuguese and French forces (d) Deccan Sultanates

69. The 'Mansabdari system' of the Mughals primarily determined:
(a) The religious rank of nobles (b) The succession to the throne (c) The rank, salary and military obligations of officers (d) The land revenue of peasants
Answer: (c)
Solution: The land revenue option is tempting because mansabdars were often paid through jagirs, but the mansab itself was a dual rank (zat and sawar) fixing an officer's status, pay and the number of cavalrymen he had to maintain. So the answer is the rank, salary and military obligations of officers.

73. Which of the following statements about the Bhakti saint Kabir is correct?
