# Key Facts & Figures — Modern Indian History

## Part 1: High-Yield Facts

| Fact | Description | Examiner's Trap | Trap Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battle of Plassey – 1757 | Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah; Mir Jafar's betrayal decided it | Confusing Plassey (1757) with Buxar (1764) | Chronology confusion |
| Battle of Buxar – 1764 | British defeated combined forces of Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daulah and Shah Alam II | Saying Buxar came before Plassey | Chronology confusion |
| Diwani Rights – 1765 | Treaty of Allahabad; Company got Diwani of Bengal, Bihar, Orissa from Shah Alam II | Attributing Diwani grant to Plassey | Cause–event mix-up |
| Regulating Act – 1773 | First parliamentary control; Governor of Bengal became Governor-General of Bengal (Warren Hastings) | Calling it the Act that created "Governor-General of India" (that was 1833) | Near-identical names |
| Pitt's India Act – 1784 | Board of Control created; dual control of Company and Crown | Confusing Board of Control (1784) with Board of Directors | Similar-name trap |
| Charter Act 1833 | Governor-General of Bengal became Governor-General of India (William Bentinck first) | Naming Cornwallis as first GG of India | Near-identical names |
| Charter Act 1853 | Open competition for civil services; separate legislative council | Attributing open competition to 1858 Act | Chronology confusion |
| First War of Independence – 1857 | Began at Meerut (10 May 1857); Mangal Pandey at Barrackpore (29 March 1857) | Saying revolt began at Barrackpore as the main uprising | Detail swap |
| Government of India Act 1858 | Crown rule began; Viceroy replaced Governor-General (Canning first Viceroy) | Calling Canning "last Governor-General" only — he was both last GG and first Viceroy | Dual-role trap |
| Queen Victoria's Proclamation – 1858 | Read at Allahabad durbar by Canning (1 November 1858) | Dating it 1877 (Delhi Durbar when she became Kaiser-i-Hind) | Chronology confusion |
| Indigo Revolt – 1859–60 | Bengal peasants against European planters; Dinabandhu Mitra's 'Neel Darpan' | Placing it in Champaran (1917) | Similar-event trap |
| Vernacular Press Act – 1878 | Lytton's Act against Indian-language press; repealed by Ripon (1882) | Attributing it to Curzon or Ripon | Person swap |
| Ilbert Bill – 1883 | Ripon's bill to let Indian judges try Europeans; withdrawn amid white protests | Crediting it to Lytton | Person swap |
| Indian National Congress – 1885 | Founded by A.O. Hume; first session Bombay (W.C. Bonnerjee president, 72 delegates) | Saying first session was at Calcutta (1886 was Calcutta) | Place swap |
| Partition of Bengal – 1905 | By Curzon; announced July, effective 16 October 1905; annulled 1911 | Saying annulment happened in 1905 itself | Chronology confusion |
| Swadeshi Movement – 1905 | Boycott resolution at Calcutta session 1906; Bengal partition sparked it | Confusing Swadeshi (1905) with Non-Cooperation (1920) methods | Similar-event trap |
| Muslim League – 1906 | Founded at Dacca; Aga Khan, Salimullah of Dacca key figures | Placing founding at Lucknow | Place swap |
| Surat Split – 1907 | Congress split into Moderates and Extremists at Surat session | Saying split happened at Calcutta 1906 (that was only tension) | Place/chronology trap |
| Morley-Minto Reforms – 1909 | Separate electorates for Muslims introduced | Attributing separate electorates to 1919 Act | Act swap |
| Gandhi's return to India – 1915 | Arrived 9 January 1915 (Pravasi Bharatiya Divas marks this) | Dating his first satyagraha in India to 1915 (Champaran was 1917) | Chronology confusion |
| Lucknow Pact – 1916 | Congress–League agreement; Tilak and Jinnah key architects | Confusing with Poona Pact (1932) | Similar-name trap |
| Champaran Satyagraha – 1917 | Gandhi's first satyagraha in India; tinkathia system | Calling Kheda (1918) his first | Order trap |
| Rowlatt Act – 1919 | "No vakil, no daleel, no appeal"; protest led to Jallianwala Bagh | Dating Jallianwala Bagh to 1918 | Chronology confusion |
| Jallianwala Bagh – 13 April 1919 | Dyer fired on unarmed crowd; Hunter Commission inquiry | Confusing Hunter Commission with Simon Commission | Similar-name trap |
| Non-Cooperation Movement – 1920–22 | Launched after Khilafat; withdrawn after Chauri Chaura (5 Feb 1922) | Saying it ended in 1923 | Chronology confusion |
| Swaraj Party – 1923 | Formed by C.R. Das and Motilal Nehru to enter councils | Crediting Gandhi with forming it | Person swap |
| Simon Commission – 1928 | All-white commission; boycotted; Lala Lajpat Rai died after lathi charge | Saying Simon came in 1927 (appointed 1927, arrived 1928) | Date trap |
| Lahore Session – 1929 | Purna Swaraj resolution; 26 January 1930 observed as Independence Day | Attributing Purna Swaraj to Karachi 1931 | Session swap |
| Dandi March – 1930 | 12 March–6 April 1930; Sabarmati to Dandi, ~240 miles, 78 followers | Saying 71 followers or march to Surat | Number/place trap |
| First Round Table Conference – 1930 | Congress absent; Gandhi attended only the Second (1931) | Saying Gandhi attended the First RTC | Attendance trap |
| Gandhi-Irwin Pact – 1931 | Congress suspended CDM, agreed to join Second RTC | Confusing with Poona Pact | Similar-name trap |
| Communal Award – 1932 | By Ramsay MacDonald; separate electorates for Depressed Classes | Attributing it to the British Parliament Act of 1935 | Source trap |
| Poona Pact – 1932 | Gandhi–Ambedkar agreement; reserved seats instead of separate electorates | Saying it gave separate electorates (it replaced them) | Reversed-fact trap |
| Government of India Act 1935 | Provincial autonomy, dyarchy at centre (proposed), Federal Court, RBI | Saying dyarchy was introduced in provinces (that was 1919) | Reversed-fact trap |
| August Offer – 1940 | Linlithgow's offer; first acceptance of Indian constitution-making | Confusing with Cripps Offer (1942) | Similar-offer trap |
| Cripps Mission – 1942 | Offered Dominion Status after war; Gandhi called it "post-dated cheque" | Saying Cripps offered full independence immediately | Exaggeration trap |
| Quit India Movement – 1942 | Launched 8 August 1942 (Bombay, Gowalia Tank); "Do or Die" | Dating the slogan to Non-Cooperation | Slogan swap |
| INA / Azad Hind Fauj | Founded by Mohan Singh (1942); revived by Subhas Chandra Bose (1943); earlier idea by Rash Behari Bose | Crediting S.C. Bose as original founder | Person swap |
| Cabinet Mission – 1946 | Three members (Pethick-Lawrence, Cripps, Alexander); rejected Pakistan, proposed grouping | Saying it accepted Pakistan | Reversed-fact trap |
| Direct Action Day – 16 August 1946 | Called by Muslim League; Great Calcutta Killings | Dating it to 1947 | Chronology confusion |
| Interim Government – 1946 | Formed 2 September 1946; Nehru as Vice-President of Executive Council | Calling Nehru "Prime Minister" in 1946 | Title trap |
| Mountbatten Plan – 3 June 1947 | Basis of partition; Indian Independence Act July 1947 | Confusing June 3 Plan with Wavell Plan | Plan swap |
| Indian Independence Act – 1947 | Passed by British Parliament July 1947; two dominions from 15 August 1947 | Saying the Constituent Assembly passed it | Source trap |
| Radcliffe Line – 1947 | Boundary drawn by Cyril Radcliffe; award announced 17 August 1947 | Saying borders were announced before independence | Chronology confusion |

## Part 2: Time Management (15/45-Second Rule)

| Topic | Question Type | Time Rule | Score Maximizer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governor-Generals & their events | Fact-based (match the following) | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Battles, treaties, dates (Plassey, Buxar, Allahabad) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Congress sessions (year–place–president–resolution) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Acts & reforms (1773–1947) | Fact-based with traps | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Revolutionary organisations & founders | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Newspapers/journals & founders | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Books & authors (Neel Darpan, Anandamath, Gita Rahasya) | Fact-based | 15 sec | ✅ Yes |
| Movement chronology (NCM → CDM → Quit India) | Analytical (arrange in order) | 45 sec | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Statement-based questions on Gandhi's strategy | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Comparing Acts (1919 vs 1935 features) | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Causes/effects of 1857, partition of Bengal | Analytical | 45 sec | ⚠️ Moderate |
| Ideology questions (Moderates vs Extremists, Gandhi vs Bose) | Analytical | 45 sec | ❌ Skip if unsure |

**Strategy:** In this chapter, ~70% of exam questions are pure recall (15-sec type). Attempt all fact-based questions first; return to statement-based/analytical ones only after securing these.
