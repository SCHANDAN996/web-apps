51. The Regulating Act of 1773 designated the Governor of Bengal as the Governor-General of Bengal. Who was the first to hold this office?
(a) Warren Hastings (b) Lord Cornwallis (c) Lord Clive (d) Lord Wellesley
Answer: (a)
Solution: Robert Clive is the tempting wrong choice because he won Plassey and served as Governor of Bengal, but he never held the new title created by the Regulating Act. The Act of 1773 made Warren Hastings the first Governor-General of Bengal, so the answer is Warren Hastings.

52. With reference to the Pitt's India Act, 1784, consider the following statements:
1. It established a Board of Control to supervise political affairs.
2. It separated the commercial and political functions of the East India Company.
3. It abolished the Court of Directors of the Company.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 only (d) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (a)
Solution: Statement 3 is the trap — the Court of Directors was retained to manage commercial affairs; it was not abolished. The Act created a Board of Control for political matters and thus separated political from commercial functions, so the answer is 1 and 2 only.

53. The 'Doctrine of Lapse', under which several princely states were annexed, is associated with which Governor-General?
(a) Lord Wellesley (b) Lord Hastings (c) Lord Dalhousie (d) Lord Canning
Answer: (c)
Solution: Lord Wellesley is the tempting distractor because he too expanded British territory, but through the Subsidiary Alliance system, not annexation by lapse. The Doctrine of Lapse, annexing states like Satara, Jhansi and Nagpur where rulers died without a natural male heir, was applied by Lord Dalhousie, so the answer is Lord Dalhousie.

54. Arrange the following events in chronological order:
1. Battle of Buxar
2. Battle of Plassey
3. Third Battle of Panipat
4. Battle of Wandiwash
(a) 2, 3, 4, 1 (b) 2, 3, 1, 4 (c) 3, 2, 4, 1 (d) 2, 4, 3, 1
Answer: (d)
Solution: The chronological-confusion trap is placing Buxar before Wandiwash or Panipat. The correct sequence is Plassey (1757), Wandiwash (1760), Third Battle of Panipat (1761), and Buxar (1764), so the answer is 2, 4, 3, 1.

55. The Treaty of Allahabad (1765) granted the East India Company which of the following rights in Bengal?
(a) Right to mint coins in Bombay (b) Right to maintain a standing army in Awadh (c) Right to appoint the Nawab of Bengal (d) Right to collect land revenue (Diwani)
Answer: (d)
Solution: The tempting wrong options confuse the treaty with military or appointment powers. By the Treaty of Allahabad, the Mughal Emperor Shah Alam II granted the Company the Diwani — the right to collect revenue — of Bengal, Bihar and Orissa, so the answer is the right to collect land revenue (Diwani).
