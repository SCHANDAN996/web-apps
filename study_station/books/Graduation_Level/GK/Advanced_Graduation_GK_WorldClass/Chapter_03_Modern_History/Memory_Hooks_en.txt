# 🪄 Memory Hooks & Short Tricks — Modern Indian History

---

**Hook 1 — Order of British Governor-Generals' Big Wars**

> **"Plassey Buxar, Mysore Maratha, Sikh Sinks"**
> 1757 Plassey → 1764 Buxar → Anglo-Mysore Wars → Anglo-Maratha Wars → Anglo-Sikh Wars (1845–49).
> Remember: **P-B-M-M-S** = *"Please Bring More Mango Shakes."*

---

**Hook 2 — Founders of Early Political Associations (pre-Congress)**

> **"Bengal's Surendranath, Madras's Mahajan Sabha, Poona's Ranade"**
> - Indian Association (1876) → **Surendranath Banerjee**
> - Madras Mahajan Sabha (1884) → **Viraraghavachari, Subramania Aiyar, Anandacharlu**
> - Poona Sarvajanik Sabha (1870) → **M.G. Ranade**
> Trick: **S**urendranath = **S**eventy-six (1876).

---

**Hook 3 — Congress Sessions Everyone Confuses**

> **"Surat Split, Lucknow Pact, Calcutta Swaraj, Lahore Purna"**
> - **1907 Surat** → Congress SPLIT (Moderates vs Extremists)
> - **1916 Lucknow** → Congress-League PACT + reunion
> - **1920 Calcutta (Special)** → Non-Cooperation adopted
> - **1929 Lahore** → Purna Swaraj (President: Jawaharlal Nehru)
> Trick: **S**urat = **S**plit; **L**ucknow = **L**eague; **L**ahore = **L**iberation demand.

---

**Hook 4 — Gandhi's Three Mass Movements**

> **"20-30-42: No, Salt, Quit"**
> - 19**20** → **No**n-Cooperation
> - 19**30** → **Salt** Satyagraha (Dandi, 12 March 1930)
> - 19**42** → **Quit** India ("Do or Die")
> Trick: decades rhyme — *Twenty said No, Thirty tasted Salt, Forty-two said Quit.*

---

**Hook 5 — Revolutionary Organisations**

> **"Bengal Anushilan, Punjab Ghadar, Abroad HSRA"**
> - Anushilan Samiti (1902, Bengal) → Barindra Ghosh, Jatindranath Banerjee
> - Ghadar Party (1913, San Francisco) → Lala Har Dayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna
> - HSRA (1928, Delhi) → Chandrashekhar Azad, Bhagat Singh
> Trick: **Ghadar = G**o abroad (founded in the **USA**, not India — classic trap!).

---

**Hook 6 — Newspapers & Their Founders**

> **"Tilak's Kesari roared, Gandhi's Harijan healed, Naoroji's Rast Goftar spoke"**
> - **Kesari** (Marathi) & Maratha (English) → Tilak
> - **Young India, Harijan** → Gandhi
> - **Rast Goftar** → Dadabhai Naoroji
> - **New India / Commonweal** → Annie Besant
> - **Al-Hilal / Al-Balagh** → Abul Kalam Azad
> Trick: **B**esant = **B**oth start with "New/Common" English papers; **Azad = Al-** (both begin with Al).

---

**Hook 7 — Viceroys at Key Events (the favourite trap zone)**

> **"Curzon Partitioned, Minto Morley-ed, Irwin Pacted, Mountbatten Freed"**
> - Partition of Bengal (1905) → **Curzon**
> - Morley-Minto Reforms (1909) → **Minto**
> - Gandhi-Irwin Pact (1931) → **Irwin**
> - Independence (1947) → **Mountbatten**
> Trap alert: Simon Commission arrived (1928) under **Irwin**, NOT Reading.

---

**Hook 8 — The Three Round Table Conferences**

> **"Gandhi went only to the SECOND — like a sequel, he skipped 1 and 3"**
> RTC-I (1930): Congress absent. RTC-II (1931): Gandhi attended (failed on minorities). RTC-III (1932): Congress absent again.
> Trick: **2 = Gandhi's number** (he was the sole Congress representative).

---

**Hook 9 — Acts of Parliament Sequence**

> **"73-13-92-09-19-35: Regulate, Charter, Council, Divide, Dyarchy, Federation"**
> - 1773 Regulating Act → 1813 Charter Act (ended trade monopoly) → 1892 Councils Act → 1909 Morley-Minto (separate electorates) → 1919 Montagu-Chelmsford (dyarchy) → 1935 Act (provincial autonomy, federal proposal).
> Trick: **"My Cat Catches Mice Daily, Friend"** — Monopoly(1813), Council(1892), Communal electorate(1909), Dyarchy(1919), Federation(1935).

---

**Hook 10 — Cabinet Mission's Three Members**

> **"PLC — Please Leave, Cripps!"**
> **P**ethick-**L**awrence, Stafford **C**ripps, A.V. **Alexander**.
> Trick: **PLC** = *"Plan for Leaving Country"* (1946).

---

**Hook 11 — INA & Subhas Chandra Bose**

> **"Bose: Delhi Chalo, Jai Hind, Azad Hind — 1943 Singapore"**
> Azad Hind Government formed **21 October 1943, Singapore**. INA first raised by **Mohan Singh** (1942), revived by Bose (1943).
> Trap: INA's idea/origin → **Mohan Singh**, not Bose. Bose = revival + leadership.

---

**Hook 12 — Famous Slogans**

> **"Tilak owns Swaraj, Bhagat owns Inquilab, Bose owns Jai Hind, Gandhi owns Do-or-Die"**
> - "Swaraj is my birthright" → **Tilak**
> - "Inquilab Zindabad" → coined by **Hasrat Mohani**, popularised by **Bhagat Singh**
> - "Jai Hind" / "Dilli Chalo" → **Bose**
> - "Do or Die" → **Gandhi** (1942)
> Trap: Inquilab Zindabad's *coiner* vs *populariser* — examiners love this split.

---

**Hook 13 — Women of the Freedom Struggle**

> **"Annie presided 1917, Sarojini 1925, Madam Cama unfurled abroad"**
> - First woman Congress President → **Annie Besant** (1917, Calcutta)
> - First *Indian* woman President → **Sarojini Naidu** (1925, Kanpur)
> - First to hoist flag on foreign soil (1907, Stuttgart) → **Madam Bhikaji Cama**
> Trick: **A**nnie = **A**ny woman (first overall); **S**arojini = **S**wadeshi (first Indian).

---

**Hook 14 — Cripps, Wavell, Cabinet, Mountbatten (Endgame Plans)**

> **"42 Offer, 45 Plan, 46 Mission, 47 Mount"**
> - 1942 Cripps Offer ("post-dated cheque" — Gandhi)
> - 1945 Wavell Plan (Shimla Conference)
> - 1946 Cabinet Mission
> - 1947 Mountbatten Plan (3 June) → Independence Act (July 1947)
> Trick: even years climb the ladder — **42-45-46-47**.

---

**Hook 15 — Hunter Commission vs Hunter Committee (classic trap!)**

> **"Education 1882, Jallianwala 1919 — same surname, different century of pain"**
> - **Hunter Commission (1882)** → Education, under **Ripon**
> - **Hunter Committee (1919)** → Jallianwala Bagh inquiry
> Trick: **E**ducation came **E**arlier (1882 < 1919).

## ⏭️ Skip Strategy — When to Skip a Question

1. **Four-founder / four-member lists:** If a question asks "who founded X" with four similar-sounding names (e.g., founders of Ghadar Party, members of Cabinet Mission) and you can recall only three confidently, **skip immediately**. These are designed so that the fourth name is a near-identical distractor (e.g., "Sohan Singh Bhakna" vs "Bhai Parmanand"). Guessing here is a coin toss with negative marking.

2. **Exact-date questions outside the golden list:** Memorise only the high-yield dates (1857, 1885, 1905, 1919, 1930, 1942, 1947, etc.). If a question demands an obscure date like "on which day was the Poona Pact signed?" and it isn't in your golden list, **skip** — the options will differ by only a day or two, and logic cannot rescue you.

3. **"Which Viceroy…" with overlapping tenures:** If the event sits near a changeover year (e.g., 1926 — Reading/Irwin; 1931 — Irwin/Willingdon) and you cannot place the exact month, **skip**. Examiners deliberately pick boundary events where two Viceroys both look correct.
