Chapter Notes

Mathematical Operations

🧠
Suppose in a code '→ means multiply (×) and '×' means add (+). Then what is the value of 4 + 3 × 2? Change the signs: 4 × 3 + 2 = 12 + 2 = 14. Mathematical-operations questions rest on this 'sign-change' game.

Definition: In these questions, the meanings of the mathematical signs (+, −, ×, ÷) are changed, or swapped with each other; we must find the value of the expression using the new meanings. The BODMAS rule still applies.

🧠 Recall: Which rule still applies even after the signs are changed?

The Basics: Remember BODMAS

When solving after changing the signs, the order stays the same:

B — Brackets, O — Of/Order, D — Divide, M — Multiply, A — Add, S — Subtract.

(Divide-multiply left to right; add-subtract left to right.)

Main Types

1. Symbol Substitution

"If + means ×, − means ÷" — apply the given meanings and solve.

Example: if + = ×, then 6 + 2 = 6 × 2 = 12.

2. Interchange of Signs

"Swap + and −": 8 + 4 − 2 → 8 − 4 + 2 = 6.

3. Balance the Equation

Which signs, when changed, make the equation correct.

4. Identify the True Statement

Which equation is correct (True) under the given rule.

🧠 Recall: If '×' means '→, what is the value of 5 × 3?

The Examiner's Trap

⚠️ Trap 1 — forgetting BODMAS: Even after changing signs, do divide-multiply first, then add-subtract — don't forget the order.

⚠️ Trap 2 — changing only one sign: If "swap both + and −" is said, change both places, not one.

⚠️ Trap 3 — using the original sign: Ignoring the new meaning and solving with the old sign is the most common mistake.

Review this after 2 days.

In Simple Words

Feynman Technique: Mathematical Operations in Simple Words

Tell your friend: "Let's make a secret code — wherever you see '→, we will multiply. So '4 + 5' actually means 4 × 5 = 20! Just give the sign its new meaning, then solve like normal maths."

Change the signs, but keep the solving order (BODMAS) the same.

The Biggest Trick: Change Signs First, Then BODMAS

Expression: 4 + 3 × 2 (where + = ×, × = +)

  • Step 1: change the signs → 4 × 3 + 2
  • Step 2: BODMAS → multiply first (4×3=12), then add (12+2=14).

If you change the signs and then solve straight left-to-right, you'll be wrong — BODMAS is essential.

Short Tricks

Short Tricks: Mathematical Operations

🪄
Trick: First give each sign its new meaning, then solve by BODMAS — that is the whole method.
🪄
Trick: BODMAS order — Brackets → Of/Order → Divide → Multiply → Add → Subtract.
🪄
Trick: In an "interchange", swap both signs — like + to − and − to + together.
🪄
Trick: After changing signs, never just add left to right; finish divide-multiply first.
🪄
Trick: In option-checking questions, apply each option's signs and see if both sides become equal.
🪄
Trick: If confused, rewrite the expression cleanly with the new signs, then solve.

Skip Strategy: If a very long expression (5+ signs) takes time, do the short 2-sign questions first.

💡Mnemonic: "Change the meaning, run BODMAS" — the two steps of mathematical operations.
