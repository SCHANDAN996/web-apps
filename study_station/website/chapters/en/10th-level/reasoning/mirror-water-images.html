Chapter Notes

Mirror & Water Images

🧠
Stand before a mirror and raise your right hand — in the mirror your left hand appears raised! In a mirror image, left and right are reversed. And a reflection in water? There, top and bottom are reversed. This chapter tests these two reversals.

Definition: (1) Mirror Image: the left-right (horizontal) reversed form of an object. (2) Water Image: the top-bottom (vertical) reversed form of an object.

🧠 Recall: In a mirror, which direction is reversed — left-right, or top-bottom?

Part 1: Mirror Image

  • Rule: left ↔ right changes; top-bottom stays the same.
  • Letters that look the same (symmetric): A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y — same even in a mirror.
  • Mirror time of a clock: mirror time = 11:60 − actual time (or 12:00 − time).

Example: mirror time of 3:00 = 12:00 − 3:00 = 9:00.

Part 2: Water Image

  • Rule: top ↔ bottom changes; left-right stays the same.
  • Letters that look the same: C, D, E, H, I, O, X (horizontally symmetric) look almost the same in water.
  • The digits 0, 1, 8 also look almost the same in a water image.
🧠 Recall: What is the mirror time of 3:00 on a clock?

The Examiner's Trap

⚠️ Trap 1 — mixing the two: Mirror = left-right; water = top-bottom. Don't confuse them.

⚠️ Trap 2 — clock time: For mirror time, subtract from 11:60 (or 12:00); don't just reverse it directly.

⚠️ Trap 3 — symmetric letters: Some letters look the same even when reversed — don't change them wrongly.

Review this after 2 days.

In Simple Words

Feynman Technique: Mirror & Water Images in Simple Words

Tell your younger brother: "Hold a paper with 'b' written on it before a mirror. In the mirror it looks like 'd' — left-right flipped! Now hold the same paper over water — there top-bottom flips. That is all the two images are."

Mirror = flip left-right. Water = flip top-bottom.

The Biggest Trick: The Mirror Time of a Clock

Mirror time of a clock = 11:60 − actual time.

  • 3:00 → 11:60 − 3:00 = 8:60 = 9:00.
  • 2:30 → 11:60 − 2:30 = 9:30.

(If the minutes are 00, subtracting from 12:00 gives the same.)

Short Tricks

Short Tricks: Mirror & Water Images

🪄
Trick: Mirror = flip left-right. Water = flip top-bottom. Just remember this much.
🪄
Trick: Mirror time of a clock = 11:60 − actual time. E.g. 3:00 → 9:00, 2:30 → 9:30.
🪄
Trick: Letters symmetric in a mirror (look the same): A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y.
🪄
Trick: Letters symmetric in a water image: C, D, E, H, I, O, X. Digits 0, 1, 8 also look the same.
🪄
Trick: Mirror image of a word = write the whole word reversed, then flip each letter left-right.
🪄
Trick: Check the options — immediately cut any option that shows a symmetric letter as changed.
