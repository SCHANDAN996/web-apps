Chapter Notes

Sitting Arrangement

🧠
At a wedding dinner, 6 people sit around a round table, all facing the centre. Someone asks, "Who is immediately to Ram's left?" — here is the twist: for a person facing the table, their left hand is on our (the observer's) right! Sitting arrangement is a test of exactly this reversal.

Definition: In these questions, several people are seated in a line, around a round table, or a square table, some conditions are given, and we must work out their exact positions.

🧠 Recall: For a person facing the centre of a round table, on which side of the observer is their left hand?

The Golden Rule: Draw the Arrangement

Draw the arrangement (line or circle) on paper and fill in the conditions one by one. Apply the definite conditions first, the uncertain ones later.

Main Types

1. Linear Arrangement (single row)

All facing the same direction → left/right is like ours.

Two rows facing each other → one row faces north, the other faces south.

2. Circular Arrangement

  • Facing the centre → a person's left = our right (reversed).
  • Facing outward → a person's left = our left (same).

3. Square/Rectangular Table

People at corners and along the sides; the facing direction is given in the conditions.

🧠 Recall: In a circle where everyone faces outward, on which side of the observer is a person's right hand?

The Examiner's Trap

⚠️ Trap 1 — facing direction: Facing inward vs outward reverses left and right. Decide the facing direction first.

⚠️ Trap 2 — "immediately beside" vs "somewhere on a side": "immediately to the left" (the very next neighbour) and "to the left" (anywhere on the left) are different.

⚠️ Trap 3 — incomplete information: Often one condition gives two possibilities; fix the final picture only after applying all conditions.

Review this after 2 days.

In Simple Words

Feynman Technique: Sitting Arrangement in Simple Words

Tell your friend: "Stand facing me. Now raise your right hand. Look — your right hand is on my left side! Because we face each other." That is the biggest secret of sitting arrangement — when someone faces us, their left and right are reversed from ours.

On a round table everyone faces the centre, which is in a way towards us. So their left = our right.

The Biggest Trick: Draw the Circle

Draw a circle on paper and mark 6 dots. Read the conditions one by one:

  • "A is to the right of B" → first place B, then A to B's right (taking facing-centre into account).
  • Seat everyone one at a time.

With the picture before your eyes, no condition is missed.

Short Tricks

Short Tricks: Sitting Arrangement

🪄
Trick: Always solve by drawing the arrangement (line/circle) on paper — don't keep it in your head.
🪄
Trick: Round table, facing centre → a person's left = our right (reversed). Facing outward → same as ours.
🪄
Trick: Apply the fixed conditions first — like "A sits exactly in the middle facing north". Uncertain conditions later.
🪄
Trick: In two rows facing each other, one row's left matches the other row's right. Match carefully.
🪄
Trick: "Immediately left/right" = the very next neighbour. Just "left/right" = anywhere on that side. Read the words carefully.
🪄
Trick: When counting the gap between two people in a circle, count both ways — choose the shorter path.

Skip Strategy: Puzzles with 8+ people take a lot of time; solve the smaller (5-6 people) and linear ones first.

💡Mnemonic: "See the face, then left-right" — the first step of sitting arrangement.

Previous Year Questions

Q1. Five friends P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row facing north. R is at the extreme left, T at the extreme right. Q is immediately right of P, and S is immediately right of Q. Who is in the middle?
A P
B Q
C S
D R
Show Solution
✅ Answer: (b) Q
💡 R _ _ _ T. P, Q, S form a block (P,Q,S consecutive) in the middle three seats: R, P, Q, S, T. The middle (3rd) seat is Q.
📚 SSC CGL
Q2. P faces the centre of a round table. Q is to the immediate right of P. In your drawing, Q is placed which way from P?
A Clockwise
B Anticlockwise
C Opposite
D Same seat
Show Solution
✅ Answer: (b) Anticlockwise
💡 For a person facing the centre, their right hand is on the observer's left, i.e. anticlockwise on the drawing.
📚 SSC CHSL
Q3. A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing north. C is exactly in the middle, A is at the left end, and D is immediately left of E. Where does B sit?
A 2nd from left
B right end
C middle
D 4th from left
Show Solution
✅ Answer: (a) 2nd from left
💡 A _ C _ _. D and E are adjacent → seats 4 and 5. So B takes seat 2: A, B, C, D, E. B is 2nd from left.
📚 RRB NTPC
Q4. Six friends A, B, C, D, E, F sit around a circle facing the centre, arranged clockwise in alphabetical order from A. Who sits opposite A?
A C
B D
C E
D F
Show Solution
✅ Answer: (b) D
💡 With 6 seats, the opposite seat is 3 places away. From Back: A→B→C→D. So D is opposite A.
📚 SSC CGL
Q5. Seven boys sit in a row facing north. D is 3rd from the left. Who is 5th from the right?
A D
B the boy left of D
C the boy right of D
D cannot say
Show Solution
✅ Answer: (a) D
💡 In a row of 7, the 5th from the right = 7 − 5 + 1 = 3rd from the left = D.
📚 SSC MTS
Q6. Two people stand facing each other. A's right hand is towards B's which side?
A Right
B Left
C Front
D Back
Show Solution
✅ Answer: (b) Left
💡 When two people face each other, one's right side is opposite the other's left. So A's right is towards B's left.
📚 SSC CHSL
Q7. Five people A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing north. B is immediately right of A, C is immediately right of B, and D is at the extreme right. Who is at the extreme left?

B B
C C
D E
Show Solution
✅ Answer: (a) A
💡 A, B, C are consecutive from the left (seats 1,2,3); D is at seat 5; E takes seat 4. The left end is A.
📚 SSC CGL
Q8. Eight friends A–H sit around a circle facing the centre, clockwise in alphabetical order from A. Who sits opposite E?

Q10. Eight people sit around a circle facing the centre. The person immediately to A's left is on which side in our clockwise view?
