School Facilities

The school has its own water supply and stand by generators to ensure uninterrupted power supply even for the air-conditioning units. Fire fighting equipments as per norms fixed by the U.P. Government have been installed. The building has a unique Gothic architecture. The Furniture is specially designed to cater to the needs of growing children of ever age group. The aesthetic layout and furnishing of each classroom makes it a beautiful home away from home. A specious elegant eruption hall and the lush green playground provide a very welcoming ambience the school.

Classroom

Properly ventilated classrooms, laboratories; activity rooms etc. are designed for greater efficiency, and to protect children from air and noise pollution. A class student ratio of 1:40 ensures individual attention to the pupils. Our pupils are encouraged and guided to develop their analytical and problem solving skills.

Special/remedial teaching both during and after school hours will be arranged according to the need of each child.

Play Ground

Growing children need to be provided with avenues to channelize their abundant energy resources. Sports and games provide appropriate learning platform to imbibe essential life skills. School has a large playground for outdoor games and provides facilities such as tennis courts, semi-Olympic size swimming pool (Coming-up), basketball court, skating rink, volley ball, soccer and cricket field. Besides these facilities for indoor games like Table tennis, chess, carom, badminton and squash are also provided.

Activity Room

The school has aesthetically designed well equipped rooms for activities such as Art and Craft, Theatre, Indian dance, Western dance, Instrumental music, vocal, yoga, aerobics and Gymnasium.

Computer Lab

Computer science is a compulsory subject in the school and computer labs offer a lot of scope for all children to acquire hands-on training.

Computer education begins right from class LKG. Computer is also used as a means to learn other subjects.

Infirmary

A well equipped medical room with a doctor and a nurse caters to medical needs of students during school hours. The nurse will maintain health record of all students & conducts regular health checkups. The school is having a tie-up with leading nursing homes to cater to emergency services for our students.

Library

The school is coming up with a state- of- the art computerized library which will stock a wide collection of books and CDs on a variety of subjects. Each student-computer station will have access to the internet for reference work to prepare projects which are a regular feature of our research based learning system. Transport

The school owns a fleet of buses running as per the directives of U.P Govt. A GPS wireless system is followed round the clock for the tracking of the buses. In case of any delay or mishap, a message will be flashed to the school which informs the parents and send a relief bus if necessary.

Cafeteria

The School Cafeteria is managed by a reputed caterer. It provides nourishing wholesome snacks and lunch in the most hygienic conditions. Students will come and eat in the cafeteria during their respective breaks.

Security

The school is well guarded round-the- clock by security guards, not only at the entry gate but also across the entire campus. Every corner of the campus is on surveillance by close circuit camera round the clock.
