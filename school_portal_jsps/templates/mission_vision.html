Mission & Vision

Our Mission: J S Public School is committed to provide a liberal and diverse environment for students that nurtures their creativity and help them to realise their full potential. J S Public School strives to develop best faculty and facility to encourage enquiry and excellence, faster values of courage, fair play, humility, sensitivity and fellowship among the students.

Our Vision: It is our endeavour to mold our students to be compassionate, disciplined, progressive and professionally successful human beings responsive to global culture and Indian heritage, in an environment of sharing and caring. We believe that every child has a talent, which is fostered in condusive environment, will enhance his/her overall development. We strive to provide a lively, warm and purposeful atmosphere for persuit of excellence.
