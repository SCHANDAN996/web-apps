About Us

“Education is the most powerful weapon you can use to change the world.” - Nelson Mandela

People with education, vision and knowledge succeed in the battle of life. It can be same with your child. An educated child will always have greater opportunity to make life’s dreams come true. At J S Public School, we realise the full potential of your child, because we believe in complete education. We make each day count.

Under the flagship of Jhamar Singh Educational Trust, J S Public School was established in the year 2013. This institution is the brain child of the noted social worker and public figure of Eastern UP, Late Shri Jhamar Singh.

This institution is now a Senior Secondary CBSE affiliated school and is committed to provide the most economical education with the best faculties and best facilities across the whole district of Chandauli and adjoining areas of Mirzapur and Varanasi.
