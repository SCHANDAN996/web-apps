📝 Set 3/6 — Direction Sense (Medium Level)

Instructions: Each question has four answer choices. Select the correct option and read the detailed solution.

51. In the morning, Geeta is standing facing the sun. She takes a left turn and walks 15 m. Then she takes a right turn and walks 20 m. After that, she turns 90° to her left and walks 10 m. In which direction is she now from her starting point?

A) North-East
B) North-West
C) South-East
D) South-West

Answer: A) North-East
Solution: Morning sun ⇒ East. Facing East. Left → North, walk 15 m. Right → East, walk 20 m. Left 90° → North, walk 10 m. Net: East = 20 m, North = 25 m ⇒ North-East.
Source: IBPS Clerk 2020

52. If 'East' is called 'North', 'South' is called 'East', 'West' is called 'South', and 'North' is called 'West', then what will 'South-West' be called?

A) North-West
B) North-East
C) South-East
D) South-West

Answer: C) South-East
Solution: E→N, S→E, W→S, N→W. SW = S+W → East + South = South-East.
Source: RRB NTPC 2019

53. A man walks 6 m North, turns left and walks 8 m, turns left and walks 6 m, turns right and walks 8 m. How far is he from the start?

A) 16 m
B) 8 m
C) 10 m
D) 14 m

Answer: A) 16 m
Solution: North 6 (face N). Left → West 8 (face W). Left → South 6 (face S). Right → West 8 (face W). Net: North–South cancel, West = 16 m. Distance = 16 m.
Source: SSC MTS 2022

54. At 5 PM, a man walking on a straight road sees his shadow exactly to his left. After walking 50 m, he takes a right turn and walks 30 m. In which direction is he now from his starting point?

A) North-East
B) South-East
C) North-West
D) South-West

Answer: D) South-West
Solution: 5 PM ⇒ Sun in West, shadow East. Shadow left ⇒ East on left ⇒ he faces South. Walks 50 m South, right turn → West 30 m. Net: South 50, West 30 ⇒ South-West.
Source: IBPS PO 2022

55. In a certain code, 'East' means 'North-East', 'West' means 'South-West', 'North' means 'North-West', and 'South' means 'South-East'. What does 'North-East' mean in that code?

A) North
B) East
C) West
D) South

Answer: A) North
Solution: Each direction shifts by −45° (anti-clockwise): E (90°)→NE (45°), W (270°)→SW (225°), N (0°)→NW (315°), S (180°)→SE (135°). So NE (45°) −45° = 0° (North).
Source: SSC CGL 2021

56. ⚠️ Examiner’s Trap
A boy starts from his house and walks 10 m North. He turns right and walks 5 m. Then he turns about and walks 5 m. He then turns right and walks 10 m. In which direction is he facing now?

A) North
B) South
C) East
D) West

Answer: A) North
Solution: Start North. Walk North 10 (face N). Right → East, walk 5 (face E). About turn (180°) → West, walk 5 (face W). Right → North (face N). Final facing North.
*Trap: ‘Turn about’ is a 180° reversal; many candidates forget and continue with left/right logic.*
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

57. A is 30 m to the South of B. C is 30 m to the East of B. D is 30 m to the South of C. E is 30 m to the West of D. F is 30 m to the North of E. Where is F with respect to B?

A) 30 m East
B) 30 m West
C) Same point
D) 30 m North

Answer: C) Same point
Solution: B(0,0). A(0,–30). C(30,0). D(30,–30). E(0,–30). F(0,0). Hence F is at B.
Source: IBPS RRB 2021

58. ⚠️ Examiner’s Trap
One evening, a man is standing in a field. His friend is coming from the opposite direction. The man observes that his friend’s shadow is falling to the right of the man. In which direction is the friend facing?

A) East
B) West
C) North
D) South

Answer: C) North
Solution: Evening ⇒ Sun West, shadow East. Friend’s shadow (East) falls to the man’s right ⇒ East on man’s right ⇒ man facing South. Friend from opposite direction ⇒ friend faces North.
Trap: Whose shadow is being observed? Read carefully; it’s the friend’s shadow relative to the man.
Source: SSC CGL Tier I 2023

59. A woman walks 4 m North, 8 m West, 4 m South, and 10 m East. How far is she from the starting point?

A) 10 m
B) 2 m
C) 8 m
D) 6 m

Answer: B) 2 m
Solution: North–South: 4 – 4 = 0. East–West: 10 – 8 = 2 m East. Distance = 2 m.
Source: RRB Group D 2020

60. If 'South-West' is coded as 'North', then what is 'West' coded as? (Assume same rotation)

A) North-East
B) South-East
C) East
D) North

Answer: A) North-East
Solution: SW (225°) → North (0°) ⇒ rotation +135°. West (270°) +135° = 405° ≡ 45° ⇒ North-East.
Source: SSC CPO 2021

61. A man faces West. He turns 135° clockwise, then 90° anti-clockwise, then 90° clockwise. In which direction is he facing?

A) North-West
B) South-West
C) South-East
D) North-East

Answer: D) North-East
Solution: West (270°) +135° = 405° ≡ 45° (NE). –90° = 315° (NW). +90° = 405° ≡ 45° (NE).
Source: IBPS Clerk 2019

62. At noon, a boy is standing facing the sun. He turns left and walks 20 m. Then he turns right and walks 10 m. In which direction is his shadow falling? (Northern hemisphere)

A) North
B) South
C) East
D) West

Answer: A) North
Solution: Noon ⇒ sun South, boy faces South. Left → East, walk 20 m. Right → South, walk 10 m. Final facing South. Shadow falls North (opposite sun).
Source: SSC Steno 2022

63. B is 8 m North of A. C is 6 m East of B. D is 8 m South of C. What is the shortest distance between A and D?

A) 6 m
B) 8 m
C) 10 m
D) 14 m

Answer: A) 6 m
Solution: A(0,0). B(0,8). C(6,8). D(6,0). Distance AD = √(6² + 0²) = 6 m.
Source: SSC MTS 2021

64. ⚠️ Examiner’s Trap
A man walks 10 m towards East, then takes a left turn and walks 10 m. He then takes a right turn and walks 10 m. He then takes a right turn and walks 10 m. Finally he takes a left turn and walks 10 m. How far is he from his starting point?

A) 10 m
B) 20 m
C) 0 m
D) 30 m

Answer: D) 30 m
Solution: East 10 (face E). Left → North 10 (face N). Right → East 10 (face E). Right → South 10 (face S). Left → East 10 (from South, left is East). Net: East = 30 m, North–South cancel. Distance = 30 m.
Trap: The last left turn after facing South is East, not West. Many candidates mistakenly turn West.
Source: IBPS SO 2019

65. One morning, Ravi and Shashi are standing in a field. Ravi’s shadow is falling exactly to Shashi’s left. If they are facing each other, which direction is Shashi facing?

A) East
B) West
C) North
D) South

Answer: D) South
Solution: Morning ⇒ sun East, shadow West. Ravi’s shadow (West) is to Shashi’s left ⇒ Shashi’s left is West ⇒ Shashi faces South. (Facing each other, Ravi faces North.)
Source: SSC CGL 2018

66. If the directions are rotated 90° anti-clockwise, then what was originally North-West will now be called?

A) North-East
B) South-West
C) North-West
D) South-East

Answer: B) South-West
Solution: 90° anti-clockwise shift: NW (315°) – 90° = 225° (SW).
Source: RRB ALP 2021

67. A man walks 5 m South, 12 m East, 5 m North, and 9 m West. The shortest distance from start is:

A) 3 m
B) 5 m
C) 4 m
D) 6 m

Answer: A) 3 m
Solution: South–North: 5–5 = 0. East–West: 12–9 = 3 m East. Distance = 3 m.
Source: IBPS Clerk 2022

68. A girl walks 20 m towards East. She then turns right and walks 20 m, then turns right and walks 30 m, then turns right and walks 20 m. In which direction is she now from her starting point?

A) East
B) West
C) North
D) South

Answer: B) West
Solution: East 20. Right → South 20. Right → West 30. Right → North 20. Net East–West: 20 East – 30 West = 10 m West. North–South cancel. So West.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

69. If 'North' means 'South-West', then what does 'West' mean? (Assume uniform rotation)

A) South-East
B) North-East
C) North-West
D) East

Answer: A) South-East
Solution: North (0°) → SW (225°) implies rotation +225°. West (270°) +225° = 495° ≡ 135° (SE).
Source: SSC CGL 2022

70. A boy walks 6 m North, turns left and walks 8 m, turns left and walks 6 m, turns right and walks 8 m. How far is he from the start?

A) 16 m West
B) 8 m East
C) 10 m North
D) 0 m

Answer: A) 16 m West
Solution: North 6 (N). Left → West 8 (W). Left → South 6 (S). Right → West 8 (W). Net: N–S cancel, West = 16 m.
Source: RRB NTPC 2022

71. At sunrise, a man starts walking facing the sun. After walking 50 m, he turns right and walks 100 m. He then turns right and walks 50 m. Finally, he turns left and walks 50 m. In which direction is his shadow falling now?

A) East
B) West
C) North
D) South

Answer: B) West
Solution: Sunrise ⇒ sun East. Start facing East. East 50, right (South) 100, right (West) 50, left (South) 50. Final facing South. Shadow always opposite sun ⇒ West.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

72. In a code, directions are rotated such that 'North-East' becomes 'East', and 'South-West' becomes 'West'. What does 'North' become?

A) North-West
B) North-East
C) South-West
D) South-East

Answer: B) North-East
Solution: NE (45°) → East (90°) = +45°. SW (225°) → West (270°) = +45°. So +45° rotation. North (0°) +45° = North-East.
Source: SSC CPO 2019

73. A woman walks 7 m North, then 5 m East, then 2 m South, then 4 m West, then 1 m North. Where is she from the start?

A) 1 m North, 1 m East
B) 6 m North, 1 m East
C) 6 m North, 9 m East
D) 7 m North, 1 m East

Answer: B) 6 m North, 1 m East
Solution: North: 7 – 2 + 1 = 6 m North. East: 5 – 4 = 1 m East.
Source: SSC MTS 2020

74. ⚠️ Examiner’s Trap
A man stands facing West. He turns 90° towards his right, then 180° towards his left, then again 90° towards his right. In which direction is he facing?

A) East
B) North
C) South
D) West

Answer: D) West
Solution: West (270°). Right 90° → North (0°). Left 180° (about turn) → South (180°). Right 90° → West (270°).
Trap: The 180° left turn is an about‑turn. Many treat it as a simple left and miscalculate.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

75. A is 4 m West of B. C is 5 m North of B. D is 3 m East of C. E is 10 m South of D. F is 8 m West of E. What is the direction of F from B?

A) North-West
B) South-West
C) South-East
D) North-East

Answer: B) South-West
Solution: B(0,0). A(–4,0). C(0,5). D(3,5). E(3,–5). F(–5,–5). F is 5 m West, 5 m South ⇒ South-West.
Source: IBPS RRB 2020
