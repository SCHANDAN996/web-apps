126. A man is facing West. He turns 135° clockwise, then 90° anti‑clockwise, then 45° clockwise, then 180° anti‑clockwise. In which direction is he facing now?

A) North
B) South
C) East
D) West

Answer: B) South
Solution: West = 270°. +135° → 405° ≡ 45° (NE). –90° → 315° (NW). +45° → 360° (N). –180° → 180° (S).
Source: SSC CGL Tier II 2023

127. At 7 AM, two persons A and B stand 100 m apart, facing each other. A’s shadow falls exactly to the left of B. In which direction is A facing?

A) North
B) South
C) East
D) West

Answer: A) North
Solution: Morning sun East, shadow West. A’s shadow (West) is to B’s left ⇒ B’s left is West ⇒ B faces South. Facing each other ⇒ A faces North.
Source: IBPS PO Mains 2022

128. A man walks 8 m West, turns left and walks 6 m, turns right and walks 8 m, turns right and walks 6 m, turns left and walks 8 m, turns right and walks 6 m. In which direction is he from his starting point?

A) North-East
B) North-West
C) South-West
D) South-East

Answer: B) North-West
Solution: Track net: West 8, left South 6, right West 8, right North 6, left West 8, right North 6. Total West = 8+8+8 = 24 m, North = 6+6 = 12 m. Direction North‑West.
Source: SSC CGL Tier I 2022

129. In a certain code, directions are rotated 135° clockwise. If ‘South’ is coded as ‘North‑West’, then how is ‘East’ coded?

Solution: Rotation +135°. East (90°) +135° = 225° (South‑West).

131. Point A is 10 m North of B. C is 5 m East of B. D is 10 m South of C. E is 5 m West of D. F is 5 m North of E. G is 10 m East of F. Where is G with respect to A?

132. A boy is facing North. He turns 45° clockwise and walks 10 m, then turns 135° anti‑clockwise and walks 20 m, then turns 90° clockwise and walks 10 m, then turns 180° anti‑clockwise and walks 15 m. In which direction is he from his starting point?

A) North-East
B) North-West
C) South-East
D) South-West

Answer: B) North-West
Solution: Start (0,0) N. 45° cw → NE (45°), walk 10 (7.07,7.07). 135° anti‑cw → W (270°), walk 20 (-12.93,7.07). 90° cw → N (0°), walk 10 (-12.93,17.07). 180° anti‑cw → S (180°), walk 15 (-12.93,2.07). Net West 12.93, North 2.07 ⇒ North‑West.
Source: SSC CPO 2022

135. In a code, directions are rotated by a fixed angle. ‘North’ becomes ‘South‑East’, ‘East’ becomes ‘South‑West’. How is ‘South’ written?

A) North-West
B) North-East
C) South-East
D) South-West

Answer: A) North-West
Solution: N(0°)→SE(135°) is +135°. E(90°)→SW(225°) is +135°. So +135° rotation. S(180°)+135° = 315° (NW).
Source: SSC CGL 2019

136. A man walks 8 m East, turns left and walks 6 m, turns right and walks 7 m, turns left and walks 2 m, turns left and walks 2 m, turns right and walks 5 m, turns left and walks 2 m, turns right and walks 5 m. How far is he from his starting point?

A) 13 m
B) 15 m
C) 17 m
D) 19 m

Answer: C) 17 m
Solution: East: 8+7+5 = 20, minus West 5 = 15 m East. North: 6+2-2+2 = 8 m North. Net (15,8). Distance = √(15²+8²) = 17 m.
Source: IBPS RRB 2020

137. A man is facing North. He turns right and walks 10 m, then turns left and walks 20 m, then turns right and walks 10 m, then turns about and walks 15 m, then turns left and walks 20 m. In which direction is he facing now?

Solution: Start N. Right → W. Forward → N. Left → E. Backward → S. Right → W.
