# Feynman Technique: Direction Sense in Simple Words

Tell your friend: "A game — you are standing facing North. Now raise your right hand. Which direction is it pointing?" They will think — East! Why? Because when you face North, your right hand always points East.

That is all direction sense is. **Every time, ask — 'Which way am I facing right now?' — only then will left and right come out correct.**

## The Biggest Trick: Walk on Paper

As you read the question, start walking from a point on paper:
- "3 km east" → an arrow of length 3 to the right.
- "then 4 km north" → an arrow of length 4 upward from there.
- Now the straight line between start and end = √(3²+4²) = 5 km.

The moment you draw the arrows, all the confusion in your head clears.

## Blurting Sheet (Write from Memory)

Close the book and write:
1. In which direction does the sun rise? Which way is the morning shadow?
2. Facing North, which direction after turning right?
3. What is the formula for the shortest distance?
4. What is the most common trap?

Whatever you cannot write, read again.
