# Direction Sense

Early in the morning, which way does your shadow fall? Towards the west — because the sun rises in the east, and a shadow always falls opposite to the sun. Direction-sense questions rest on this idea — a person starts somewhere, turns a few times, and ends up somewhere.

**Definition:** In direction-sense questions, a person's movement (how far, in which direction, how many turns) is given, and we must find their final position, direction, or distance from the starting point.

🧠 Recall: In which direction does the sun rise, and which way does the morning shadow fall?

## The Basics: Eight Directions

- Four main: North (N), South (S), East (E), West (W)
- Four corner: North-East (NE), North-West (NW), South-East (SE), South-West (SW)

On an imagined map, always: top = North, bottom = South, right = East, left = West.

## The Golden Rule: Draw Arrows

Draw each step as an arrow on paper. Where the person turns, start a new arrow. At the end, look at the start and end points.

**Left–right rule:**
- Facing North → turning right gives East, turning left gives West.
- Facing South → turning right gives West, turning left gives East (reversed!).

## Main Types

**1. Final Direction**
After several turns, which direction is the person facing.

**2. Shortest Distance**
If a person walks 3 km east, then 4 km north, the straight distance from the start = √(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 km (Pythagoras).

**3. Shadow Based**
In the morning the shadow falls west, in the evening east. At noon the shadow is shortest.

🧠 Recall: A person walks 6 km east, then 8 km south — what is the straight distance from the start?

## The Examiner's Trap

⚠️ **Trap 1 — forgetting the facing direction:** Left and right always depend on the person's current facing, not on top/bottom of the map.

⚠️ **Trap 2 — shadow direction:** "His shadow fell to his right" tells you which way he is facing. Morning and evening give different results.

⚠️ **Trap 3 — distance vs displacement:** Total distance walked and the straight (shortest) distance are different. Read the question carefully.
