Set 1: Analogy MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Q1. Cow : Calf :: Dog : ?
(a) Kitten (b) Puppy (c) Cub (d) Foal
Answer: (b) Puppy
Solution: The young one of a cow is a calf; the young one of a dog is a puppy.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. Finger : Hand :: Leaf : ?
(a) Root (b) Tree (c) Branch (d) Petal
Answer: (c) Branch
Solution: A finger is a part of a hand; a leaf is a part of a branch. Part–whole relationship.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Teacher : School :: Doctor : ?
(a) Medicine (b) Patient (c) Hospital (d) Nurse
Answer: (c) Hospital
Solution: A teacher works in a school; a doctor works in a hospital. Worker–workplace relationship.
Source: RRB Group D

Q4. 3 : 9 :: 5 : ?
(a) 15 (b) 20 (c) 25 (d) 10
Answer: (c) 25
Solution: 3² = 9, so 5² = 25. The rule is squaring.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q5. Hot : Cold :: Big : ?
(a) Huge (b) Small (c) Large (d) Tall
Answer: (b) Small
Solution: Hot and cold are opposites; big and small are opposites. Antonym relationship.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. Bird : Fly :: Fish : ?
(a) Water (b) Fin (c) Swim (d) Scale
Answer: (c) Swim
Solution: A bird flies; a fish swims. This is a creature–action relationship.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. AB : CD :: PQ : ?
(a) RS (b) QR (c) ST (d) RT
Answer: (a) RS
Solution: Each pair is two consecutive letters, and the next pair starts right after. After PQ comes RS.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Author : Book :: Poet : ?
(a) Story (b) Poem (c) Pen (d) Rhyme
Answer: (b) Poem
Solution: An author writes a book; a poet writes a poem. Creator–creation relationship.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. Sun : Day :: Moon : ?
(a) Star (b) Sky (c) Night (d) Light
Answer: (c) Night
Solution: The sun is associated with the day; the moon with the night.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q10. Wheel : Cycle :: Sail : ?
(a) Wind (b) Boat (c) Sea (d) Rope
Answer: (b) Boat
Solution: A wheel is a part of a cycle; a sail is a part of a (sail) boat. Part–whole.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q11. 2 : 8 :: 3 : ?
(a) 9 (b) 18 (c) 27 (d) 12
Answer: (c) 27
Solution: 2³ = 8, so 3³ = 27. The rule is cubing.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q12. Doctor : Stethoscope :: Painter : ?
(a) Wall (b) Brush (c) Colour (d) Art
Answer: (b) Brush
Solution: A doctor uses a stethoscope; a painter uses a brush. Worker–tool relationship.
Source: SSC MTS

Q13. Lion : Roar :: Dog : ?
(a) Growl (b) Bark (c) Howl (d) Bite
Answer: (b) Bark
Solution: A lion roars; a dog barks. Animal–sound relationship.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q14. Water : Thirst :: Food : ?
(a) Taste (b) Cook (c) Hunger (d) Health
Answer: (c) Hunger
Solution: Water removes thirst; food removes hunger. Remedy–need relationship.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q15. India : Delhi :: Japan : ?
(a) Beijing (b) Tokyo (c) Seoul (d) Bangkok
Answer: (b) Tokyo
Solution: Delhi is the capital of India; Tokyo is the capital of Japan.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q16. Eye : See :: Ear : ?
(a) Nose (b) Sound (c) Hear (d) Listen
Answer: (c) Hear
Solution: We see with the eye; we hear with the ear. Organ–function relationship.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q17. 4 : 16 :: 6 : ?
(a) 30 (b) 36 (c) 42 (d) 24
Answer: (b) 36
Solution: 4² = 16, so 6² = 36. The rule is squaring.
Source: SSC MTS

Q18. Carpenter : Wood :: Blacksmith : ?
(a) Fire (b) Hammer (c) Iron (d) Tool
Answer: (c) Iron
Solution: A carpenter works with wood; a blacksmith works with iron. Worker–material relationship.
Source: RRB Group D

Q19. Book : Reading :: Ball : ?
(a) Round (b) Play (c) Bat (d) Ground
Answer: (b) Play
Solution: A book is used for reading; a ball is used for playing. Object–use relationship.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q20. Z : A :: Y : ?
(a) B (b) C (c) X (d) W
Answer: (a) B
Solution: Z is the reverse of A (1st and last); Y is the second-last, and its reverse-pair position matches B (2nd). Reverse-pair relationship.
Source: SSC CGL

Q21. (Medium) BEAD : CFBE :: KNOW : ?
(a) LMPX (b) LOPX (c) LPQX (d) MOPX
Answer: (b) LOPX
Solution: Each letter moves +1: B→C, E→F, A→B, D→E gives CFBE. Apply to KNOW: K→L, N→O, O→P, W→X = LOPX.
Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) 6 : 42 :: 7 : ?
(a) 49 (b) 54 (c) 56 (d) 63
Answer: (c) 56
Solution: 6 × 7 = 42, so 7 × 8 = 56. The rule is n × (n+1).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q23. (Medium) Flow : Wolf :: 4123 : ?
(a) 4321 (b) 1234 (c) 3214 (d) 2143
Answer: (a) 4321
Solution: "Flow" reversed is "Wolf". Reversing the digit sequence of 4123... treat as reversing order: reverse of position pattern gives 4321. The relationship is full reversal.
Source: Bank PO

Q24. (Medium) 11 : 121 :: 13 : ?
(a) 156 (b) 169 (c) 143 (d) 133
Answer: (b) 169
Solution: 11² = 121, so 13² = 169. The rule is squaring.
Source: SSC CGL

Q25. (Medium) Triangle : 3 :: Hexagon : ?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: A triangle has 3 sides; a hexagon has 6 sides. Figure–number of sides relationship.
Source: RRB NTPC
