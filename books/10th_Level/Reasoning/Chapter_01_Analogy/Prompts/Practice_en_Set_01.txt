Instructions: Choose the option that best completes the relationship.

Easy Level (Q1 – Q20)

Q1. Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?
(A) Student (B) School (C) Book (D) Classroom
Answer: (B) School
Solution:

Step 1: A Doctor works in a Hospital (workplace relationship).

Step 2: Applying the same relationship, a Teacher works in a School.

Trap: "Classroom" is a part of school, not the whole workplace.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

Q2. Fish : Water :: Bird : ?
(A) Nest (B) Tree (C) Sky (D) Egg
Answer: (C) Sky
Solution:

Step 1: A Fish lives in Water (natural habitat).

Step 2: A Bird lives in the Sky (air). "Nest" is a shelter, not the natural habitat.
Source: IBPS Clerk 2019

Step 2: 5 squared = 25.

Q7. 8 : 27 :: 64 : ?
(A) 125 (B) 81 (C) 100 (D) 144
Answer: (A) 125
Solution:

Step 1: 8 is 2³, 27 is 3³ (cube relationship: 2→2³=8, 3→3³=27).

Step 2: 64 is 4³, so next is 5³ = 125.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

Medium Level (Q21 – Q25)

(Questions containing traps or requiring tighter reasoning)

Q21. Chisel : Sculptor :: Brush : ?
(A) Painter (B) Artist (C) Canvas (D) Color
Answer: (A) Painter
Solution:

Step 1: A Sculptor uses a Chisel as a tool.

Step 2: A Painter uses a Brush. "Artist" is a broader category; sculptor is an artist, but the tool is specifically for a painter. Tight relationship: the tool's most direct user.

Examiner's Trap: Two similar options – Painter and Artist both fit loosely. To break the trap, tighten: "Chisel is the primary tool of a Sculptor, so Brush is the primary tool of a Painter." Artist could use a brush too, but the specific profession matching sculptor is painter.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

Q22. 15 : 226 :: 20 : ?
(A) 401 (B) 400 (C) 399 (D) 441
Answer: (A) 401
Solution:

Step 1: Pattern: n² + 1? 15²=225+1=226.

Step 2: 20²=400+1=401. But wait, option (B) 400 is the square itself. The trap is forgetting to add the constant.

Examiner's Trap: Misleading option (B) 400 is the square; the pattern requires +1.
Source: IBPS PO 2021

Q23. ACE : GIK :: MOQ : ?
(A) SUW (B) TVX (C) UWY (D) VXZ
Answer: (A) SUW
Solution:

Step 1: A(1), C(3), E(5) → +6 each: A+6=G(7), C+6=I(9), E+6=K(11).

Step 2: M(13)+6=S(19), O(15)+6=U(21), Q(17)+6=W(23) → SUW.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

Q24. Carpenter : Saw :: Tailor : ?
(A) Needle (B) Cloth (C) Scissors (D) Machine
Answer: (C) Scissors
Solution:

Step 1: A Carpenter uses a Saw to cut wood.

Step 2: A Tailor uses Scissors to cut cloth. Needle is for stitching, not cutting. The relationship is tool for cutting in respective profession.

Examiner's Trap: "Needle" is also a tailor's tool, but saw is specifically for cutting; scissors match that function. Double-relationship trap: function vs. general tool.
Source: RRB NTPC 2022

Q25. 2 : 5 :: 4 : ?
(A) 16 (B) 20 (C) 17 (D) 25
Answer: (C) 17
Solution:

Step 1: Pattern: n² + 1: 2²+1=5.

Step 2: 4²+1=17. The trap: option (A) 16 is the square; (B) 20 is 4×5.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021
