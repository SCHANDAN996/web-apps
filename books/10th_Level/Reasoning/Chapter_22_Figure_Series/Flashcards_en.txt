Front: In a figure series, what is the pattern about?
Back: Visuals (shape/direction/rotation) — not numbers.

Front: What is the golden rule to solve a figure series?
Back: Pick one element, see its change, apply the same rule to the next figure.

Front: What can change in a figure? (4 types)
Back: Rotation, addition/removal, change of direction/position, flip/mirror.

Front: Arrow up, right, down — which way next?
Back: Left (90° clockwise each time).

Front: If a full turn takes 4 steps, what is the rotation angle?
Back: 90° (360° ÷ 4).

Front: If a full turn takes 8 steps, what is the angle?
Back: 45° (360° ÷ 8).

Front: Sides: triangle, square, pentagon — next?
Back: Hexagon (3, 4, 5, 6 sides).

Front: Dots 1, 2, 3, 4 — next?
Back: 5 (add 1 each time).

Front: Difference between clockwise and anticlockwise?
Back: Clockwise is the clock direction; anticlockwise is the reverse.

Front: What to do when there are many elements?
Back: Track each element separately, then combine.

Front: Turning 90° clockwise from North gives which direction?
Back: East.

Front: Why watch small changes (a dot/line) carefully?
Back: Because they are easily overlooked.

Front: How do you eliminate options?
Back: Match element by element — cut any that breaks even one rule.

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Missing one of two simultaneous changes, or reversing the rotation direction.

Front: What is the figure-series mnemonic?
Back: "One element, one rule".
