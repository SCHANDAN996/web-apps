Question 26 (Medium) – ⚠️ Contains Examiner's Trap (Alternating Pattern)
Type: Figure Series
Problem Figures:
(i) A circle containing a single dot at the centre.
(ii) Two concentric circles, no dot.
(iii) A circle with a dot at the centre again, but the circle is now a square.
(iv) Two concentric squares, no dot.
(v) ?

Options:
(A) A triangle with a dot at the centre
(B) Two concentric triangles, no dot
(C) A square with a dot at the centre
(D) A circle with a dot at the centre

Answer: (A)
Solution: Odd positions (1,3,5): The outer shape changes (circle → square → triangle) and always contains a central dot. Even positions (2,4): The same shape as the previous odd figure, but doubled into two concentric versions and no dot. Position 5 is odd, so we need the next shape in the sequence (circle→square→triangle) with a central dot. The trap: students might follow the concentric-no-dot pattern from position 4 and choose (B).
Source: SSC CGL 2022, Shift 1 (adapted)
