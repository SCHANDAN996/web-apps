Question 101 (Medium)
Type: Figure Series
Problem Figures:
(i) A small black dot at the centre of a circle.
(ii) The circle is now a square; the black dot has moved to the top-left corner of the square.
(iii) The square changes back to a circle; the black dot is now at the centre again.
(iv) A square with the black dot at the top-right corner.
(v) ?

Options:
(A) Circle with black dot at centre
(B) Square with black dot at bottom-right corner
(C) Circle with black dot at top-left corner
(D) Square with black dot at centre

Answer: (A)
Solution: Odd steps (1,3,5): outer shape is a circle with the black dot at the centre. Even steps (2,4): outer shape is a square with the black dot at a corner that moves clockwise (top-left → top-right → bottom-right). Step 5 is odd, so circle with dot at centre.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

Question 102 (Medium) – ⚠️ Contains Examiner's Trap (Two Rules Alternating)
Type: Figure Series
Problem Figures:
(i) A right-pointing arrow (→) with a small white triangle at the tail.
(ii) A left-pointing arrow (←) with the triangle now at the head (the arrow points left, triangle on the left side).
(iii) A down-pointing arrow (↓) with triangle at the tail again (top of arrow).
(iv) An up-pointing arrow (↑) with triangle at the head (bottom of arrow).
(v) ?

Options:
(A) Right-pointing arrow (→), triangle at tail
(B) Left-pointing arrow (←), triangle at head
(C) Down-pointing arrow (↓), triangle at tail
(D) Up-pointing arrow (↑), triangle at head

Answer: (B)
Solution: Odd positions (1,3,5): arrow rotates 90° clockwise (right → down → left). The triangle remains at the tail (on the non-pointed end). Even positions (2,4): arrow is the mirror image of the previous odd arrow (so left, then up), and the triangle moves to the head (pointed end). Position 5 is odd: left-pointing arrow with triangle at tail. The trap: students may assume the triangle always stays at tail and choose (A) or (C), but the rule alternates the triangle position.
Source: SSC CGL 2022 (adapted)
