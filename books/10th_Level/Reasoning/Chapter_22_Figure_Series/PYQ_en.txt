PYQ Analysis: Figure Series (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Figure series gives 2–4 questions per exam. Rotation and add/remove types are most common. The trap is missing one of two changes and the rotation direction.

Q1. An arrow points up, then right, then down. Which way does it point next?
(a) up (b) right (c) down (d) left
Answer: (d) left
Solution: The arrow turns 90° clockwise each time: down → left.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. If a figure completes a full turn in 4 steps, how much does it rotate each step?
(a) 45° (b) 90° (c) 120° (d) 180°
Answer: (b) 90°
Solution: 360° ÷ 4 = 90°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. The number of sides increases: triangle, square, pentagon, ? What is next?
(a) pentagon (b) hexagon (c) heptagon (d) octagon
Answer: (b) hexagon
Solution: Sides go 3, 4, 5, 6 — a hexagon.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Dots in each figure: 1, 2, 3, 4, ? How many next?
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 5
Solution: One dot is added each time: 4 + 1 = 5.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. A figure series is a pattern of:
(a) numbers (b) shapes/directions (c) letters only (d) words
Answer: (b) shapes/directions
Solution: Figure series is about visual patterns.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. An arrow points up. After a 90° clockwise turn, it points:
(a) up (b) right (c) down (d) left
Answer: (b) right
Solution: 90° clockwise from up is right.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. If a figure rotates 45° each step, how many steps complete a full turn?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 12
Answer: (c) 8
Solution: 360° ÷ 45° = 8.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. The number of lines: 1, 2, 3, ? What is next?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 2
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: One line is added each time: 3 + 1 = 4.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. Turning 90° clockwise from North gives:
(a) South (b) East (c) West (d) North
Answer: (b) East
Solution: 90° clockwise from North is East.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. Turning 90° anticlockwise from East gives:
(a) North (b) South (c) West (d) East
Answer: (a) North
Solution: 90° anticlockwise from East is North.
Source: SSC CGL
