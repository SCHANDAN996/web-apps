PYQ Analysis: Paper Folding & Cutting (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Paper folding & cutting gives 1–2 questions per exam. The trap is copying holes instead of mirroring, and the fold order.

Q1. A paper is folded once and one hole is punched. How many holes appear when opened?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: One fold doubles the hole: 1 × 2 = 2.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. A paper is folded twice and one hole is punched. How many holes appear when opened?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 8
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: Two folds: 1 × 2² = 4.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. When opened, each hole is formed:
(a) as a direct copy (b) as a mirror across the fold line (c) at the centre (d) at random
Answer: (b) as a mirror across the fold line
Solution: Holes are mirrored across the fold line.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. A paper is folded three times and one hole is punched. How many holes appear?
(a) 3 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (c) 8
Solution: 1 × 2³ = 8.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. Each fold does what to the number of holes?
(a) halves (b) doubles (c) keeps same (d) triples
Answer: (b) doubles
Solution: Each fold doubles the number of holes.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. A paper is folded once and two holes are punched. How many holes when opened?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 6
Answer: (c) 4
Solution: 2 × 2 = 4.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. In what order should a folded paper be opened?
(a) same order as folded (b) reverse order (c) any order (d) from the middle
Answer: (b) reverse order
Solution: The last fold is opened first.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. A paper is folded four times and one hole is punched. How many holes appear?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 16
Answer: (d) 16
Solution: 1 × 2⁴ = 16.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. If a hole is near the fold line, its mirror hole is:
(a) far away (b) at the same distance on the other side (c) at the centre (d) at the edge
Answer: (b) at the same distance on the other side
Solution: The mirror hole is equidistant on the other side of the fold line.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. A paper is folded twice and two holes are punched. How many holes appear?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 16
Answer: (c) 8
Solution: 2 × 2² = 8.
Source: SSC CGL
