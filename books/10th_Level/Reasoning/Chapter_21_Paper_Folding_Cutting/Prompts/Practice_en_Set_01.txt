Q1. (Easy)
A square paper is folded once along the vertical centre line. A small circle is cut out on the folded paper at a point touching the fold line. How will the paper appear when opened?

A. Two separate circles
B. One large circle
C. A single circle cut out from the centre
D. Two circles that join to form a single hole

Correct Answer: D
Step-by-step:

The fold line acts as a mirror.

The cut is a small circle that touches the fold line.

Upon opening, the circle is reflected across the fold. Because the cut touches the fold line, the two mirrored circles merge into one larger continuous shape – effectively a single elongated hole formed by two overlapping circles.

Among the options, D describes this correctly: “Two circles that join to form a single hole.” (Visual: a figure‑8 like hole).

Source: SSC CHSL 2018

Q4. (Easy)

Fold line is horizontal. Notch cut at the folded edge, protruding beyond fold.

Q8. (Easy)
A triangular paper (equilateral) is folded along its altitude. A circle is cut out on the folded paper, with its centre on the fold line. When opened, the hole appears as:

A. A circle touching the tip
B. A figure‑8 like hole
C. Two separate circles
D. A single circle exactly at the centre of the triangle

Correct Answer: B
Step-by-step:

Fold is altitude → fold line through vertex and base midpoint.

Cut a circle whose centre lies on the fold line.

The circle will be mirrored and the two circles overlap partly (since the cut straddles the fold). The resulting hole is a shape like a figure‑8, joined in the middle.

Thus a single continuous hole shaped like two overlapping circles.

Q9. (Easy)
A paper is folded twice: first vertically, then horizontally, to form a small square. A single punch is made exactly at the corner where the two fold lines meet. On fully opening, how many holes are there?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 4
D. 8

Correct Answer: A
Step-by-step:

Two folds, punch at the corner where both folds converge.

That point lies on both fold lines. When the paper is unfolded, the hole is mirrored across each fold, but because it is on the fold lines, the four mirrored holes all coincide at the same central location.

Thus only one hole is visible, in the centre of the paper.

Q10. (Easy)

Fold line is vertical. Cut a square whose side exactly on fold.

Q12. (Easy)

Q21. (Medium)
A square paper is folded as follows: first, it is folded along the vertical midline. Then, it is folded again along the horizontal midline. Now a small circle is cut out from the folded paper at the corner that is completely open (no fold line passes through it). After fully opening, how many circles will be seen?

B. 4
C. 8
D. 16

Correct Answer: B
Step-by-step:

Q22. (Medium – Examiner’s Trap: Rotation vs Reflection)
A square paper is folded once along the vertical line. On the folded paper, a cut is made in the shape of the letter ‘L’ placed such that the short arm of the L is horizontal and touches the fold line, while the long arm extends to the right. Which of the following correctly shows the unfolded shape of the hole?

A. A shape formed by the L and its mirror image, appearing as a wide ‘T’ with a long vertical stem
B. Two separate L shapes, one rotated 90°
C. A U‑shaped cut
D. A plus‑shaped hole

Correct Answer: A
Step-by-step:

The L cut has its short arm touching the fold. Mirror across vertical fold reflects the L, producing a shape where the two short arms meet at the fold line, forming the crossbar of a T, and the two long arms extend left and right, forming the top of the T. The overall hole looks like a T with a wide top and a central vertical stem.

Option B suggests a rotated L, which is a common trap. Rotation does not occur; only mirroring.

Option C and D are unrelated.

Source: SSC CGL 2022 (Medium)

Q23. (Medium)
A paper is folded first along the vertical centre, then along the horizontal centre. Now, a cut is made in the shape of a quarter circle from the corner that lies on both fold lines. After fully opening, the hole will be:

A. A full circle at the centre
B. Four quarter‑circle cuts at the four corners
C. A circle divided into four equal parts
D. A shape like a clover leaf

Correct Answer: A
Step-by-step:

Two folds. The cut is a quarter circle exactly at the corner where the two folds meet.

Unfold last (horizontal) fold: the quarter circle is mirrored, forming a semicircle at the top (or bottom) of the vertical fold.

Unfold first (vertical) fold: the semicircle is mirrored, creating a full circle at the centre of the original paper.

Since the cut was on the fold lines, all parts join, making a perfect circular hole in the centre.

Source: RRB NTPC 2021 (Medium)

Q24. (Medium – Examiner’s Trap: Multiple punches on fold and off fold)

C. 4
