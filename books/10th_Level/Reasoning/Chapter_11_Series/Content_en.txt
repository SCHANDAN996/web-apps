# Series

In a row, numbers or letters move forward by a hidden rule: 2, 5, 8, 11, ? — each time 3 is added, so the next is 14. Series questions test your ability to catch this hidden rule.

**Definition:** In a series, a sequence of numbers, letters, or both (alphanumeric) is given that follows some rule; we must find the next term, the missing term, or the wrong term.

🧠 Recall: What is our real job in a series — memorising terms, or catching the pattern?

## The Golden Rule: Look at the Difference

Find the difference (or ratio) between consecutive terms — the rule is often hidden there.

## Main Types

**1. Number Series**
- Constant add/subtract: 2, 5, 8, 11 (+3)
- Multiply/divide: 3, 6, 12, 24 (×2)
- Squares/cubes: 1, 4, 9, 16 (squares); 1, 8, 27 (cubes)
- Increasing difference: 2, 3, 5, 8, 12 (+1, +2, +3, +4)

**2. Letter Series**
A pattern of letters in the alphabet: A, C, E, G, ? → +2 each time, so I.
Remember: A=1, B=2 … Z=26 (mnemonic EJOTY).

**3. Alphanumeric Series**
Letters and numbers together: A1, B2, C3, ? → D4.

**4. Mixed / Alternating Series**
Two separate patterns alternating: 1, 2, 4, 3, 7, 4 → (odd positions: 1,4,7 +3; even positions: 2,3,4 +1).

🧠 Recall: Which letter comes after A, C, E, G?

## The Examiner's Trap

⚠️ **Trap 1 — assuming one rule:** If a simple difference doesn't work, try ratio, squares, or an alternating pattern.

⚠️ **Trap 2 — wrong term:** In "find the wrong term" questions, first catch the full rule, then pick the odd term.

⚠️ **Trap 3 — forgetting letter positions:** In letter series, the pattern is missed without writing the position numbers.

Review this after 2 days.
