Answer: (A) 195
Solution: ×2 − 1, then ×2 − 3 consistently: 8×2-1=15, 15×2-3=27, 27×2-3=51, 51×2-3=99, 99×2-3=195.
Source: IBPS PO Mains 2017

45. S, N, J, G, E, ?
(A) B (B) C (C) D (D) A
Answer: (C) D
Solution: Forward positions: S(19)→N(14) -5, N→J(10) -4, J→G(7) -3, G→E(5) -2, next -1 → 4=D.
Source: SSC CGL 2016

46. 0, 4, 18, 48, 100, ?
(A) 144 (B) 160 (C) 180 (D) 196
Answer: (C) 180
Solution: n³ − n²: 1³-1²=0, 2³-2²=4, 3³-3²=18, 4³-4²=48, 5³-5²=100, 6³-6²=180.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

47. 5, 25, 7, 49, 9, 81, 11, ?
(A) 121 (B) 110 (C) 99 (D) 100
Answer: (A) 121
Solution: Odd positions: 5,7,9,11; even positions are squares of those: 5²=25, 7²=49, 9²=81, 11²=121.
Source: Railway Group D 2020

48. Find the wrong term: 2, 6, 15, 20, 30, 42
(A) 6 (B) 15 (C) 20 (D) 30
Answer: (B) 15
Solution: Pattern n(n+1): 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42. So 15 should be 12.
Source: IBPS Clerk 2019

49. E, J, N, S, ?
(A) W (B) X (C) Y (D) Z
Answer: (A) W
Solution: Positions: E(5)→J(10) +5, J→N(14) +4, N→S(19) +5, next +4 → 23=W.
Source: SSC MTS 2017

50. ⚠️ Trap: 5, 7, 10, 15, 22, ?
(A) 31 (B) 33 (C) 35 (D) 37
Answer: (B) 33
Solution: Differences: +2, +3, +5, +7 (consecutive primes) → next +11 → 33.
Trap: If one misreads differences as odd numbers (2,3,5,7,9), they would get 31 (option A).
Source: SSC CGL 2019
