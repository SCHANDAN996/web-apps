Set 1: Alphabet Questions MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. Which is the 5th letter from the left?
(a) D (b) E (c) F (d) C
Answer: (b) E
Solution: E = 5th position.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. Which is the 26th letter from the left?
(a) Y (b) Z (c) X (d) W
Answer: (b) Z
Solution: Z = 26th position.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Which is the 1st letter from the right?
(a) Y (b) Z (c) A (d) X
Answer: (b) Z
Solution: The last letter Z is 1st from the right.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. How many letters are there between B and F?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
Answer: (b) 3
Solution: 6 − 2 − 1 = 3 letters (C, D, E).
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. Which is the 2nd letter to the right of D?
(a) E (b) F (c) G (d) H
Answer: (b) F
Solution: D(4) + 2 = 6 = F.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. Which is the 13th letter from the left?

Answer: (b) M
Solution: M = 13th position.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. Which is the 3rd letter from the right?
(a) W (b) X (c) Y (d) V
Answer: (b) X
Solution: From right = 27 − 3 = 24th from left = X.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. What is the position of P?
(a) 15 (b) 16 (c) 17 (d) 14
Answer: (b) 16
Solution: P = 16th position.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q9. Which letter is exactly in the middle of C and I?
(a) E (b) F (c) G (d) D
Answer: (b) F
Solution: Middle = (3 + 9) ÷ 2 = 6 = F.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. Which is the 4th letter to the left of K?

Solution: K(11) − 4 = 7 = G.
