📝 Practice Set 3/6: Statement & Conclusion (Basic)

Total Questions: 25 (Q51–Q75, All Medium)
Instructions: In each question, one or more statements are given followed by two conclusions I and II. Assume the statements to be true, even if they conflict with commonly known facts. Decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s). Mark:

(A) Only conclusion I follows

(B) Only conclusion II follows

(C) Both I and II follow

(D) Neither I nor II follows

51. Statement: Some tables are chairs. All chairs are furniture.
Conclusions:
I. Some tables are furniture.
II. Some furniture are not tables.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (A) Only I follows
Solution: I follows because tables that are chairs must be furniture. II is not definite; furniture could all be tables.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

52. Statement: No cloud is silver. Some silver things are linings.
Conclusions:
I. Some linings are not clouds.
II. No lining is a cloud.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (A) Only I follows
Solution: The silver things that are linings are not clouds, so some linings (those) are not clouds. II is not necessarily true for all linings.
Source: Bank PO 2019

53. Statement: All kings are rich. Some rich people are humble.
Conclusions:
I. Some kings are humble.
II. No king is humble.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Either I or II (D) Neither I nor II
Answer: (C) Either I or II follows
Solution: Kings are a subset of rich. The humble rich may include all, some, or no kings. The possibilities are exhaustive, so either some kings are humble (I) or no king is humble (II). The pair is complementary.
Source: SSC CGL 2018 (Examiner's Trap: complementary pair)

54. Statement: Some poets are dreamers. No dreamer is practical.
Conclusions:
I. Some poets are not practical.
II. Some practical people are poets.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (A) Only I follows
Solution: Poets who are dreamers cannot be practical, so some poets are not practical. II cannot be concluded; we don't know if any poet is practical.
Source: RRB NTPC 2021

55. Statement: Only a few bottles are glass. All glass is transparent.
Conclusions:
I. Some bottles are transparent.
II. Some bottles are not transparent.
Options: (A) Only I (B) Only II (C) Both (D) None
Answer: (A) Only I follows
Solution: "Only a few" implies some bottles are glass. Those bottles are transparent, so I follows. For II, we cannot say that some bottles are not transparent because the non-glass bottles might still be transparent.
Source: IBPS PO 2022 (Examiner's Trap: "Only a few" doesn't guarantee the negative about the predicate)

58. Statement: Some apples are green. All green things are natural.
