Front: What do opposite faces of a standard die sum to?
Back: 7 (like 1↔6, 2↔5, 3↔4).

Front: How many pieces have 3 painted faces in a painted cube?
Back: Always 8 (the 8 corners of the cube).

Front: What is the formula for 2 painted faces (edges)?
Back: 12 × (n − 2).

Front: Formula for 1 painted face (face-centre)?
Back: 6 × (n − 2)².

Front: Formula for 0 painted faces (inner)?
Back: (n − 2)³.

Front: How many small cubes in total?
Back: n³.

Front: In a 3×3×3 cube, how many pieces have 1 painted face?
Back: 6 × (3−2)² = 6.

Front: In a 3×3×3 cube, how many inner (0 painted) pieces?
Back: (3−2)³ = 1.

Front: How do you find the opposite number on a die?
Back: 7 − the given number (standard die).

Front: Which number is opposite 1?
Back: 6 (1+6=7).

Front: Which number is opposite 3?
Back: 4 (3+4=7).

Front: How many faces does a cube have?
Back: 6 faces.

Front: In a 4×4×4 cube, how many corner (3 painted) pieces?
Back: 8 (corners are always 8).

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Forgetting the die "sum 7" or the cube "(n−2)".

Front: What is the cubes-dice mnemonic?
Back: "Corner 3, edge 2, face 1, inner 0" and "the magic of 7 on a die".
