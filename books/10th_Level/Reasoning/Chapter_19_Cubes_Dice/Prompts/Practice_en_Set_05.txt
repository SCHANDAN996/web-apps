Solution: Net1: 2↔4 opposite, can’t meet. Net2: 2↔4 opposite. Net3: opposites 5↔6, 1↔4, 2↔3 – 2,4,6 all mutually adjacent. Net4: check opposite pairs; 6 opposite 5, 2 and 4 likely opposite, so invalid. Only Net3 works.

Question (Hard): A standard dice initially shows 1 on top, 2 in front, 3 on right. It is rotated 90° clockwise about the vertical axis, then 90° forward (front becomes top). What is the new top face?
Options: A) 1 B) 2 C) 3 D) 4
Answer: D) 4
Solution: Initial: top=1, front=2, right=3 ⇒ left=4, back=5, bottom=6. First rotation (clockwise about vertical): top stays 1, front becomes old left=4, right becomes old front=2. Second rotation (forward): new top = old front = 4.

Question (Hard): A dice has opposite pairs (1,4), (2,5), (3,6). Initial: top 1, front 2, right 3. It is rolled to the right (right becomes bottom), then rolled forward (front becomes bottom). What is the final top face?
Options: A) 1 B) 5 C) 6 D) 2
Answer: B) 5
Solution: Initial: top=1, front=2, right=3 ⇒ left=6, back=5, bottom=4. Roll right: top becomes old left=6, front=2, right=1, bottom=3. Then roll forward: new top = old back of current state. After roll right, back is old back=5? Let's track: After roll right, back remains old back=5. Then roll forward: top becomes old back = 5. So final top = 5.
