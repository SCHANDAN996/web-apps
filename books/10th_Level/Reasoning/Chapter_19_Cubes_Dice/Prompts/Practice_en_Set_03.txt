Question: Two positions: Pos1 – top 1, front 3, right 5. Pos2 – top 3, front 6, right 2. Opposite of 3?
Options: A) 1 B) 4 C) 5 D) 6
Answer: B) 4
Solution: 3 in both. Pos1: 3 adjacent to 1,5. Pos2: 3 adjacent to 6,2. Adjacent to 3: 1,5,6,2. Only 4 remains, opposite 3.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

Question: A cross net: top 6, middle row L-R: 1,2,3,4, bottom 5. After folding, opposite of 4?
Options: A) 1 B) 2 C) 3 D) 5
Answer: B) 2
Solution: Top↔bottom (6↔5). Middle row: 1st (1)↔3rd (3), 2nd (2)↔4th (4). So 4 opposite 2.
Source: SSC CHSL 2023 (Medium)
