Set 1: Cubes & Dice MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. How many corner pieces (3 painted faces) does any painted cube have?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 12
Answer: (c) 8
Solution: Corners are always 8.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. On a standard die, which number is opposite 6?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 5
Answer: (a) 1
Solution: 7 − 6 = 1.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. On a standard die, which number is opposite 4?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (b) 3
Solution: 7 − 4 = 3.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. How many small cubes are there in a 4×4×4 cube?
(a) 48 (b) 56 (c) 64 (d) 72
Answer: (c) 64
Solution: n³ = 4³ = 64.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. In a 4×4×4 painted cube, how many pieces have 3 painted faces?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 24
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: Corners are always 8.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. In a 4×4×4 painted cube, how many pieces have 1 painted face?
(a) 12 (b) 16 (c) 24 (d) 8
Answer: (c) 24
Solution: 6 × (n−2)² = 6 × 2² = 24.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. In a 4×4×4 painted cube, how many pieces have no painted face?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: (n−2)³ = 2³ = 8.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. On a standard die, opposite faces sum to:
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 7
Solution: 1+6, 2+5, 3+4 all equal 7.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q9. In a 4×4×4 painted cube, how many pieces have 2 painted faces?
(a) 12 (b) 24 (c) 8 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 24
Solution: 12 × (n−2) = 12 × 2 = 24.
Source: RRB Group D

Q10. On a standard die, which number is opposite 5?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: 7 − 5 = 2.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q11. How many small cubes are there in a 3×3×3 cube?
(a) 9 (b) 18 (c) 27 (d) 36
Answer: (c) 27
Solution: n³ = 3³ = 27.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q12. In a 3×3×3 painted cube, how many pieces have 2 painted faces?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 6 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: 12 × (n−2) = 12 × 1 = 12.
Source: SSC MTS

Q13. How many faces does a cube have?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 12
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: A cube has 6 faces.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q14. On a standard die, the opposite number is found by:
(a) adding 1 (b) 7 minus the number (c) doubling (d) 6 minus the number
Answer: (b) 7 minus the number
Solution: Opposite faces sum to 7.
Source: SSC CGL

Q15. In a 5×5×5 painted cube, how many pieces have 3 painted faces?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 27 (d) 54
Answer: (a) 8
Solution: Corners are always 8.
Source: SSC MTS

Q16. How many small cubes are there in a 5×5×5 cube?
(a) 100 (b) 125 (c) 75 (d) 150
Answer: (b) 125
Solution: n³ = 5³ = 125.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q17. (Medium) On a standard die, which number is opposite 1?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
Answer: (d) 6
Solution: 7 − 1 = 6.
Source: SSC CGL

Q18. (Medium) In a 5×5×5 painted cube, how many pieces have no painted face?
(a) 9 (b) 18 (c) 27 (d) 36
Answer: (c) 27
Solution: (n−2)³ = 3³ = 27.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q19. (Medium) In a 5×5×5 painted cube, how many pieces have 1 painted face?
(a) 27 (b) 54 (c) 36 (d) 45
Answer: (b) 54
Solution: 6 × (n−2)² = 6 × 3² = 54.
Source: SSC CGL

Q20. (Medium) The corner pieces of a painted cube always number:
(a) 6 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) depends on n
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: A cube always has 8 corners, so 8 pieces with 3 painted faces.
Source: SSC MTS
