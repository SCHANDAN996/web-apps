Set 1: Missing Term MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. 2, 4, ?, 8
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 9
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: Add 2 each time: 4 + 2 = 6.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. 5, ?, 15, 20
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 11
Answer: (b) 10
Solution: Add 5 each time: 5 + 5 = 10.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. A, B, ?, D
(a) C (b) E (c) F (d) B
Answer: (a) C
Solution: Consecutive letters: after B comes C.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. 3, 6, 12, ?
(a) 18 (b) 24 (c) 20 (d) 15
Answer: (b) 24
Solution: Multiply by 2 each time: 12 × 2 = 24.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. 1, 4, 9, ?, 25
(a) 12 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 20
Answer: (b) 16
Solution: Perfect squares: 4² = 16.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. 10, 20, ?, 40
(a) 25 (b) 30 (c) 35 (d) 32
Answer: (b) 30
Solution: Add 10 each time: 20 + 10 = 30.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. In a matrix, rows are (1, 2, 3), (4, 5, 9), (2, 3, ?). Find ?.
(a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 7
Answer: (b) 5
Solution: Third column = first + second: 2 + 3 = 5.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Z, X, ?, T
(a) W (b) V (c) U (d) Y
Answer: (b) V
Solution: Go back 2 letters each time: X → V.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q9. 7, 14, ?, 28
(a) 20 (b) 21 (c) 22 (d) 24
Answer: (b) 21
Solution: Add 7 each time: 14 + 7 = 21.
Source: RRB Group D

Q10. 2, 3, 5, 8, ?
(a) 11 (b) 12 (c) 13 (d) 10
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: Differences 1, 2, 3, then 4: 8 + 4 = 12.
Source: SSC CGL

Q11. B, D, F, ?
(a) G (b) H (c) I (d) J
Answer: (b) H
Solution: Add 2 positions each time: F → H.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q12. 81, 27, ?, 3
(a) 6 (b) 9 (c) 12 (d) 18
Answer: (b) 9
Solution: Divide by 3 each time: 27 ÷ 3 = 9.

Q13. 1, 1, 2, ?, 5
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 2 (d) 6
Answer: (a) 3
Solution: Fibonacci — sum of the previous two: 1 + 2 = 3.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q14. 4, 8, 16, ?

Answer: (b) 32
Solution: Multiply by 2 each time: 16 × 2 = 32.

Q15. 2, 5, 8, ?, 14

Q16. In a matrix, rows are (2, 4, 6), (3, 6, 9), (4, 8, ?). Find ?.
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: Each column = first × (1, 2, 3): 4 × 3 = 12.
Source: SSC MTS

Q17. (Medium) A, C, E, ?, I
