Front: Which direction is reversed in a mirror image?
Back: Left ↔ right (horizontal); top-bottom stays the same.

Front: Which direction is reversed in a water image?
Back: Top ↔ bottom (vertical); left-right stays the same.

Front: How do you find a clock's mirror time?
Back: Mirror time = 11:60 − actual time.

Front: What is the mirror time of 3:00?
Back: 9:00 (11:60 − 3:00 = 8:60 = 9:00).

Front: What is the mirror time of 2:30?
Back: 9:30 (11:60 − 2:30).

Front: Which letters look the same in a mirror?
Back: A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y.

Front: Which letters look the same in a water image?
Back: C, D, E, H, I, O, X.

Front: Which digits look almost the same in a water image?
Back: 0, 1, 8.

Front: What is the mirror time of 9:00?
Back: 3:00 (11:60 − 9:00 = 2:60 = 3:00).

Front: What is the mirror time of 6:00?
Back: 6:00 (11:60 − 6:00 = 5:60 = 6:00).

Front: How do you make the mirror image of a word?
Back: Write the whole word reversed, then flip each letter left-right.

Front: What does the mirror image of 'b' look like?
Back: Like 'd' (flipped left-right).

Front: What is the main difference between mirror and water images?
Back: Mirror flips left-right; water flips top-bottom.

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Mixing up the directions of mirror and water images.

Front: What is this chapter's mnemonic?
Back: "Mirror left-right, water top-bottom".
