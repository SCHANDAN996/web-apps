PYQ Analysis: Mirror & Water Images (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Mirror & water images give 1–3 questions per exam. The trap is mixing the two directions and the clock mirror-time formula.

Q1. A mirror image reverses which direction?
(a) top-bottom (b) left-right (c) both (d) none
Answer: (b) left-right
Solution: A mirror reverses left and right.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. A water image reverses which direction?
(a) left-right (b) top-bottom (c) both (d) none
Answer: (b) top-bottom
Solution: A water image reverses top and bottom.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. What is the mirror time of 3:00?
(a) 6:00 (b) 9:00 (c) 3:00 (d) 12:00
Answer: (b) 9:00
Solution: 11:60 − 3:00 = 8:60 = 9:00.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Which letter looks the same in a mirror image?
(a) A (b) B (c) G (d) P
Answer: (a) A
Solution: A is symmetric about the vertical axis, so it looks the same in a mirror.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. What is the mirror time of 9:00?
(a) 3:00 (b) 6:00 (c) 9:00 (d) 12:00
Answer: (a) 3:00
Solution: 11:60 − 9:00 = 2:60 = 3:00.
Source: SSC MTS

Q6. In a water image, the object is flipped:
(a) left to right (b) top to bottom (c) diagonally (d) not at all
Answer: (b) top to bottom
Solution: Water images flip top-bottom.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q7. What is the mirror time of 6:00?
(a) 5:00 (b) 6:00 (c) 7:00 (d) 12:00
Answer: (b) 6:00
Solution: 11:60 − 6:00 = 5:60 = 6:00.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Which digit looks the same in a water image?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 8 (d) 6
Answer: (c) 8
Solution: 8 is symmetric about the horizontal axis, so it looks the same in water.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. What is the mirror time of 2:30?
(a) 8:30 (b) 9:30 (c) 10:30 (d) 3:30
Answer: (b) 9:30
Solution: 11:60 − 2:30 = 9:30.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. Mirror flips left-right; water flips:
(a) left-right (b) top-bottom (c) both (d) neither
Answer: (b) top-bottom
Solution: Water images flip top-bottom.
Source: SSC CGL
