Set 1: Mirror & Water Images MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. A mirror image reverses:
(a) top-bottom (b) left-right (c) both (d) none
Answer: (b) left-right
Solution: A mirror reverses left and right.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. A water image reverses:
(a) left-right (b) top-bottom (c) both (d) none
Answer: (b) top-bottom
Solution: A water image reverses top and bottom.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. What is the mirror time of 4:00?
(a) 7:00 (b) 8:00 (c) 9:00 (d) 6:00
Answer: (b) 8:00
Solution: 11:60 − 4:00 = 7:60 = 8:00.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Which letter looks the same in a mirror?
(a) H (b) B (c) F (d) G
Answer: (a) H
Solution: H is symmetric about the vertical axis.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. What is the mirror time of 8:00?

Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q20. (Medium) Which word looks the same in a mirror (all its letters are symmetric)?

Answer: (c) MOM
Solution: M, O, M are all symmetric about the vertical axis and the word reads the same reversed, so MOM looks the same in a mirror.
Source: SSC MTS
