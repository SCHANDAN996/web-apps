Set 1: Coding-Decoding MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Q1. If BOOK is coded as CPPL, then WORD is coded as?
(a) XPSE (b) XPSF (c) WPSE (d) XQSE
Answer: (a) XPSE
Solution: Each letter +1. W→X, O→P, R→S, D→E = XPSE.
Source: SSC MTS

Q2. If STOP is coded as POTS, then STAR is coded as?
(a) RATS (b) RATES (c) TRAS (d) ARTS
Answer: (a) RATS
Solution: The word is reversed. STAR reversed = RATS.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. If A=1, B=2, …, then the sum of the letters of DOG is?
(a) 24 (b) 25 (c) 26 (d) 27
Answer: (c) 26
Solution: D=4, O=15, G=7. Sum = 4+15+7 = 26.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q4. If FISH is coded as GJTI, then BIRD is coded as?
(a) CJSE (b) CJSF (c) CKSE (d) CJTE
Answer: (a) CJSE
Solution: Each letter +1. B→C, I→J, R→S, D→E = CJSE.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. If LAMP is coded as PMAL, then HAND is coded as?

Q6. If MAP is coded as OCR, then CAR is coded as?

Q21. (Medium) If in ABCD odd-position letters move +1 and even-position letters move −1, giving BADC, then WXYZ is coded as?
(a) XWZY (b) XWYZ (c) XVZY (d) XWZX
Answer: (a) XWZY
Solution: W→X (+1), X→W (−1), Y→Z (+1), Z→Y (−1) = XWZY.
Source: SSC CGL
