Front: With what does comparison in dictionary order begin?
Back: With each word's first letter; if the same, the next letter.

Front: What to do if the first letter is the same?
Back: Look at the second letter; if that's also the same, the third, and so on.

Front: Between 'car' and 'card', which comes first?
Back: 'car' — because the shorter word comes first.

Front: Which comes first among Book, Bag, Ball, Bat?
Back: Bag (after Ba, g < l < t; Book has 'o' which comes last).

Front: Between a shorter and a longer word, which comes first?
Back: The shorter word (like love, lover).

Front: Why remember alphabet positions?
Back: So "which letter comes first" is settled instantly (A=1…Z=26).

Front: Correct order of Cat, Car, Call, Cap?
Back: Call, Cap, Car, Cat (third letter l, p, r, t).

Front: Correct order of Sun, Sit, Sad, Set?
Back: Sad, Set, Sit, Sun (second letter a, e, i, u).

Front: How to find reverse dictionary order?
Back: First arrange normally, then reverse.

Front: Between 'love' and 'lover', which comes first?
Back: 'love' (the shorter word).

Front: Correct order of Dog, Doll, Door, Dot?
Back: Dog, Doll, Door, Dot (after Do, g, l, o, t).

Front: A quick trick to arrange words?
Back: Group by the first 2-3 letters, then order within each group.

Front: What does the EJOTY mnemonic help with?
Back: Finding letter positions fast (E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25).

Front: What is the most common trap?
Back: Looking only at the first letter and not checking the following letters.

Front: What is the dictionary-order mnemonic?
Back: "First letter, then the next".
