Arrange the given words in dictionary order and select the correct sequence.
Words: Apple, Banana, Cherry, Grape
(A) Apple, Banana, Cherry, Grape
(B) Apple, Cherry, Banana, Grape
(C) Banana, Apple, Cherry, Grape
(D) Apple, Banana, Grape, Cherry

Arrange the given words in dictionary order and choose the correct sequence.
Words: Butter, Button, But, Butterfly, Butler
(A) But, Butler, Butter, Butterfly, Button
(B) But, Butter, Butler, Butterfly, Button
(C) Butler, But, Butter, Butterfly, Button
(D) But, Butler, Butterfly, Butter, Button
Answer: (A)
Solution: All begin ‘But’. ‘But’ ends, so first. Fourth letters: Butler (l), Butter (t), Butterfly (t), Button (t). l < t, so Butler second. Among t-words: Butter (e), Butterfly (e), Button (o). e < o, so Butter & Butterfly before Button. Butter ends after ‘r’, Butterfly continues ‘f’, so Butter before Butterfly. Order: But, Butler, Butter, Butterfly, Button.
Examiner’s Trap: Many rush to place ‘Butter’ first, ignoring the small-word rule; also miss that ‘l’ (Butler) comes before ‘t’.

Arrange the given words in dictionary order.
Words: Conservation, Conversation, Conversion, Convenient, Convention
(A) Conservation, Convenient, Convention, Conversation, Conversion
(B) Conservation, Conversation, Conversion, Convenient, Convention
(C) Conservation, Convenient, Convention, Conversion, Conversation
(D) Conservation, Convention, Convenient, Conversation, Conversion
Answer: (A)
Solution: All begin ‘Con’. Fourth letter: Conservation (s) < all others (v). Among v-words, fifth letter all e; sixth: Conversation (r), Conversion (r), Convenient (n), Convention (n). n < r, so Convenient & Convention first. Convenient (i) < Convention (t), so Convenient, Convention. Conversation vs Conversion: after ‘Convers’, ation (a) vs ion (i); a < i, so Conversation then Conversion. Final: Conservation, Convenient, Convention, Conversation, Conversion.
Examiner’s Trap: Words appear nearly identical; skipping the sixth‑letter split leads to random guessing.

Arrange the given words in dictionary order (ignore hyphens).
Words: X-ray, Xerox, X-mas, Xenon
(A) Xenon, Xerox, X-mas, X-ray
(B) X-mas, Xenon, Xerox, X-ray
(C) Xenon, X-mas, Xerox, X-ray
(D) Xerox, Xenon, X-mas, X-ray
Answer: (A)
Solution: Remove hyphens: Xray, Xerox, Xmas, Xenon. Second letters: X-ray (r), Xerox (e), X-mas (m), Xenon (e). e < m < r. Among e-words: Xenon (n) < Xerox (r). So Xenon, Xerox, Xmas, Xray. Restoring hyphens: Xenon, Xerox, X-mas, X-ray.
Examiner’s Trap: Treating hyphen as a letter or ignoring it altogether can misplace X-mas. Always remove hyphens before comparing.
Source: Banking (IBPS PO) 2023
