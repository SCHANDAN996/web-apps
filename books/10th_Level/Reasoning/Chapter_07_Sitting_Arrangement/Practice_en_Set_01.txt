Set 1: Sitting Arrangement MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing north. A is at the left end, E at the right end, C in the middle. B is immediately right of A, D is immediately left of E. Who sits between B and D?
(a) A (b) C (c) E (d) none
Answer: (b) C
Solution: The order is A, B, C, D, E. C sits between B and D.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. P faces the centre of a round table. Q is to the immediate right of P. In the drawing, Q is placed which way?
(a) Clockwise (b) Anticlockwise (c) Opposite (d) Same seat
Answer: (b) Anticlockwise
Solution: Facing the centre, a person's right is the observer's left = anticlockwise.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. In a row of 6 facing north, P is 2nd from the left and Q is immediately right of P. What is Q's position from the left?
(a) 2nd (b) 3rd (c) 4th (d) 1st
Answer: (b) 3rd
Solution: Q is one seat right of P (2nd), so Q is 3rd from the left.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Four friends A, B, C, D sit around a circle facing the centre, clockwise in alphabetical order. Who is opposite B?
(a) A (b) C (c) D (d) none
Answer: (c) D
Solution: With 4 seats, the opposite seat is 2 places away. From B: B→C→D. So D is opposite B.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. Two people face each other. A's left hand is towards B's which side?
(a) Left (b) Right (c) Front (d) Back
Answer: (b) Right
Solution: Facing each other, one's left is opposite the other's right. So A's left is towards B's right.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. In a row of 7 facing north, M is 4th from the left. What is M's position from the right?
(a) 3rd (b) 4th (c) 5th (d) 2nd
Answer: (b) 4th
Solution: From the right = 7 − 4 + 1 = 4th.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q7. A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing north. A is at the left end, E at the right end. The order from B onward is B, C, D. Who is 4th from the left?
(a) B (b) C (c) D (d) E
Answer: (c) D
Solution: The order is A, B, C, D, E. The 4th from the left is D.
Source: SSC MTS

Q8. In a circle where everyone faces outward, a person's left is on which side of the observer?
(a) Left (b) Right (c) Front (d) Opposite
Answer: (a) Left
Solution: Facing outward (towards us), left and right match ours. So the person's left is on our left.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q9. Six friends A–F sit around a circle facing the centre, clockwise in alphabetical order. Who is 2nd to the right of A?
(a) C (b) D (c) E (d) F
Answer: (c) E
Solution: Right = anticlockwise. From A anticlockwise: F (1st), E (2nd). So E is 2nd to A's right.
Source: SSC CGL

Q10. In a row of 8 facing north, D is 3rd from the right. What is D's position from the left?
(a) 5th (b) 6th (c) 7th (d) 4th
Answer: (b) 6th
Solution: From the left = 8 − 3 + 1 = 6th.
Source: RRB Group D

Q11. In a row of 6 facing north, X is 5th from the left. What is X's position from the right?
(a) 2nd (b) 3rd (c) 4th (d) 1st
Answer: (a) 2nd
Solution: From the right = 6 − 5 + 1 = 2nd.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q12. Eight friends A–H sit around a circle facing the centre, clockwise in alphabetical order. Who is opposite C?
(a) F (b) G (c) H (d) A
Answer: (b) G
Solution: With 8 seats, opposite is 4 places away. C is 3rd; 3 + 4 = 7 = G.
Source: SSC CGL

Q13. P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row facing north. T is at the left end, P at the right end. Q is immediately right of T, R is immediately left of P. Who is in the middle?
(a) Q (b) R (c) S (d) T
Answer: (c) S
Solution: T, Q, _, R, P — the remaining person S takes the middle seat: T, Q, S, R, P.
Source: SSC MTS

Q14. Six people sit around a circle. How many neighbours does each person have?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: In a circle, each person has exactly two neighbours — one on each side.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q15. Nine people sit in a row facing north. E is exactly in the middle. What is E's position from the left?
(a) 4th (b) 5th (c) 6th (d) 3rd
Answer: (b) 5th
Solution: The middle of 9 is the (9+1)/2 = 5th position.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q16. Five friends A–E sit around a circle facing the centre, clockwise in alphabetical order. Who is immediately to the left of A?
(a) B (b) C (c) D (d) E
Answer: (a) B
Solution: Facing the centre, the immediate left is the next person clockwise. After A comes B.
Source: SSC CGL

Q17. (Medium) In a row of 7 facing north, G is 2nd from the right. What is G's position from the left?
(a) 5th (b) 6th (c) 7th (d) 4th
Answer: (b) 6th
Solution: From the left = 7 − 2 + 1 = 6th.
Source: SSC CGL

Q18. (Medium) Two rows of 3 people each face each other. How many people are there in total?
(a) 3 (b) 6 (c) 9 (d) 5
Answer: (b) 6
Solution: 3 + 3 = 6 people in total.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q19. (Medium) Six friends A–F sit around a circle facing the centre, clockwise in alphabetical order. Who is 3rd to the left of A?
(a) B (b) C (c) D (d) E
Answer: (c) D
Solution: Left = clockwise. From A clockwise: B (1st), C (2nd), D (3rd). So D is 3rd to A's left.
Source: SSC CGL

Q20. (Medium) A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing north. A is at the left end. B is immediately right of A, C immediately right of B, D immediately left of E. Who is at the right end?
(a) C (b) D (c) E (d) B
Answer: (c) E
Solution: A, B, C fill seats 1–3; D and E are adjacent at seats 4 and 5, so E is at the right end.
Source: SSC MTS
