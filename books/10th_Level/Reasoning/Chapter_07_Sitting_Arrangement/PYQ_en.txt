PYQ Analysis: Sitting Arrangement (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Sitting arrangement gives 2–5 questions per exam (often a set on one arrangement). Linear and circular (facing centre) are most common. The trap is the facing direction and "immediate" vs "any" side.

Q1. Five friends P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row facing north. R is at the extreme left, T at the extreme right. Q is immediately right of P, and S is immediately right of Q. Who is in the middle?
(a) P (b) Q (c) S (d) R
Answer: (b) Q
Solution: R _ _ _ T. P, Q, S form a block (P,Q,S consecutive) in the middle three seats: R, P, Q, S, T. The middle (3rd) seat is Q.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. P faces the centre of a round table. Q is to the immediate right of P. In your drawing, Q is placed which way from P?
(a) Clockwise (b) Anticlockwise (c) Opposite (d) Same seat
Answer: (b) Anticlockwise
Solution: For a person facing the centre, their right hand is on the observer's left, i.e. anticlockwise on the drawing.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing north. C is exactly in the middle, A is at the left end, and D is immediately left of E. Where does B sit?
(a) 2nd from left (b) right end (c) middle (d) 4th from left
Answer: (a) 2nd from left
Solution: A _ C _ _. D and E are adjacent → seats 4 and 5. So B takes seat 2: A, B, C, D, E. B is 2nd from left.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q4. Six friends A, B, C, D, E, F sit around a circle facing the centre, arranged clockwise in alphabetical order from A. Who sits opposite A?
(a) C (b) D (c) E (d) F
Answer: (b) D
Solution: With 6 seats, the opposite seat is 3 places away. From A: A→B→C→D. So D is opposite A.
Source: SSC CGL

Q5. Seven boys sit in a row facing north. D is 3rd from the left. Who is 5th from the right?
(a) D (b) the boy left of D (c) the boy right of D (d) cannot say
Answer: (a) D
Solution: In a row of 7, the 5th from the right = 7 − 5 + 1 = 3rd from the left = D.
Source: SSC MTS

Q6. Two people stand facing each other. A's right hand is towards B's which side?
(a) Right (b) Left (c) Front (d) Back
Answer: (b) Left
Solution: When two people face each other, one's right side is opposite the other's left. So A's right is towards B's left.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q7. Five people A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing north. B is immediately right of A, C is immediately right of B, and D is at the extreme right. Who is at the extreme left?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) E
Answer: (a) A
Solution: A, B, C are consecutive from the left (seats 1,2,3); D is at seat 5; E takes seat 4. The left end is A.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Eight friends A–H sit around a circle facing the centre, clockwise in alphabetical order from A. Who sits opposite E?
(a) A (b) B (c) H (d) C
Answer: (a) A
Solution: With 8 seats, the opposite seat is 4 places away. E is 5th; 5 + 4 = 9 → back to seat 1 = A.
Source: Bank PO

Q9. In a row of 6 people facing north, X is 4th from the left. What is X's position from the right?
(a) 2nd (b) 3rd (c) 4th (d) 5th
Answer: (b) 3rd
Solution: From the right = 6 − 4 + 1 = 3rd.
Source: RRB Group D

Q10. Eight people sit around a circle facing the centre. The person immediately to A's left is on which side in our clockwise view?
(a) Clockwise (b) Anticlockwise (c) Opposite (d) Same
Answer: (a) Clockwise
Solution: For a person facing the centre, the left hand points clockwise around the circle, so the immediate-left neighbour is the next one clockwise.
Source: SSC CGL
