PYQ Analysis: Puzzles (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Puzzles give 3–5 questions per exam (often a set on one arrangement). Floor and scheduling puzzles are most common. The trap is guessing before filling definite clues.

Q1. A, B, C live on 3 floors. A lives on top, C at the bottom. Who lives on the middle floor?
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) none
Answer: (b) B
Solution: A on top, C at the bottom, so the only person left, B, is on the middle floor.
Source: SSC MTS

Q2. P, Q, R, S, T live on floors 1–5. P is on 5, Q on 1, R on 3. S lives above T. On which floor is S?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 1
Answer: (b) 4
Solution: Floors 2 and 4 remain for S and T. S is above T, so S = 4 and T = 2.
Source: SSC CGL

Q3. Five people have birthdays on Monday–Friday (one each). A's is on Monday, and B's is immediately after A's. When is B's birthday?
(a) Monday (b) Tuesday (c) Wednesday (d) Friday
Answer: (b) Tuesday
Solution: Immediately after Monday is Tuesday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Four boxes W, X, Y, Z are stacked. W is above X, X above Y, Y above Z. Which box is at the bottom?
(a) W (b) X (c) Y (d) Z
Answer: (d) Z
Solution: The order top to bottom is W, X, Y, Z, so Z is at the bottom.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. While solving a puzzle, which clue should you fill first?
(a) The negative clue (b) The definite clue (c) The relative clue (d) Any random clue
Answer: (b) The definite clue
Solution: Definite clues are certain and fix positions, from which the rest follow.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. Three friends like three colours: red, blue, green. Ram likes red, Mohan likes blue. Shyam does not like blue. What does Shyam like?
(a) Red (b) Blue (c) Green (d) Cannot say
Answer: (c) Green
Solution: Red and blue are taken by Ram and Mohan, so Shyam likes green.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. On a 5-floor building: A is on 2, B on 4, C on 5, D on 1, E on 3. Who lives immediately above A?
(a) B (b) C (c) D (d) E
Answer: (d) E
Solution: A is on floor 2, so the floor immediately above (floor 3) is E.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. In a floor puzzle, "immediately below" means:
(a) any lower floor (b) the very next lower floor (c) the ground floor (d) two floors down
Answer: (b) the very next lower floor
Solution: "Immediately below" refers to the floor directly beneath, not any lower floor.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q9. An exam is on Wednesday. What day is 3 days after Wednesday?
(a) Friday (b) Saturday (c) Sunday (d) Thursday
Answer: (b) Saturday
Solution: Thursday, Friday, Saturday — 3 days after Wednesday is Saturday.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. In a 6-floor building (top = 6), X lives on floor 4. How many people live above X?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: Floors 5 and 6 are above floor 4, so 2 people live above X.
Source: RRB Group D
