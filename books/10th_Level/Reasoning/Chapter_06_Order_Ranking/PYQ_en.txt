PYQ Analysis: Order & Ranking (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Order & ranking gives 1–2 questions per exam. Total-number and rank-conversion questions are most common. The trap is the missing "−1" and mixing directions.

Q1. A is 7th from the top and 18th from the bottom in a class. How many students are there?
(a) 24 (b) 25 (c) 23 (d) 26
Answer: (a) 24
Solution: Total = 7 + 18 − 1 = 24.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. In a class of 40, A is 12th from the top. What is A's rank from the bottom?
(a) 28 (b) 29 (c) 30 (d) 27
Answer: (b) 29
Solution: Rank from bottom = 40 − 12 + 1 = 29.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. In a row, A is 5th and B is 9th from the same end. How many persons are between them?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 2
Answer: (a) 3
Solution: Between = 9 − 5 − 1 = 3.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Rohit is 15th from the top and 20th from the bottom. How many students are in the class?
(a) 34 (b) 35 (c) 33 (d) 36
Answer: (a) 34
Solution: Total = 15 + 20 − 1 = 34.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. In a row of 25, a boy is 10th from the left. What is his position from the right?
(a) 15 (b) 16 (c) 14 (d) 17
Answer: (b) 16
Solution: From right = 25 − 10 + 1 = 16.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. In a class of 30, A is 11th from the top and B is 11th from the bottom. How many students are between them?
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 7 (d) 10
Answer: (a) 8
Solution: B's rank from top = 30 − 11 + 1 = 20. Between 11 and 20 = 20 − 11 − 1 = 8.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. Ravi ranks 18th from the top and 22nd from the bottom. How many students are in the class?
(a) 39 (b) 40 (c) 38 (d) 41
Answer: (a) 39
Solution: Total = 18 + 22 − 1 = 39.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q8. In a queue of 40 people, a man is 16th from the front. What is his position from the back?
(a) 24 (b) 25 (c) 26 (d) 23
Answer: (b) 25
Solution: From back = 40 − 16 + 1 = 25.
Source: RRB Group D

Q9. In a row, A is 8th and B is 15th from the same end. How many persons are between them?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 5 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: Between = 15 − 8 − 1 = 6.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. In a class of 45, Sita is 12th from the top. What is her rank from the bottom?
(a) 33 (b) 34 (c) 35 (d) 32
Answer: (b) 34
Solution: From bottom = 45 − 12 + 1 = 34.
Source: SSC CGL

Q11. In a row of 15 persons, A is 6th from the left and B is 4th from the right. How many persons are between them?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 4 (d) 7
Answer: (a) 5
Solution: Between = 15 − 6 − 4 = 5.
Source: SSC MTS

Q12. Amit is 10th from the top. After Amit and Sohan interchange seats, Amit becomes 15th from the top. What was Sohan's original rank from the top?
(a) 15 (b) 10 (c) 14 (d) 16
Answer: (a) 15
Solution: On interchange, Amit takes Sohan's position, which is 15th from the top. So Sohan was originally 15th.
Source: SSC CGL
