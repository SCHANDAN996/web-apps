126. In a row, A is 10th from the left and B is 12th from the right. They interchange positions, after which A becomes 18th from the left. Then C is placed exactly in the middle of the new positions of A and B. If C is 8th from the right, what is the total number of persons in the row?

(a) 28
(b) 29
(c) 30
(d) Cannot be determined

Answer: (d) Cannot be determined

Solution:
From interchange: A’s new left = 18 = B’s original left. B’s original right = 12 → total = 18 + 12 – 1 = 29.
Thus new positions: A at 18 (left), B at A’s original left = 10.
C is exactly midway between 10 and 18 → C left = (10 + 18) / 2 = 14.
If total = 29, C right = 29 – 14 + 1 = 16. But given C is 8th from right → contradiction. Hence data inconsistent.

Psychological Trap (⚠️): Students often compute total from interchange and then force the middle condition, assuming consistency. The examiner expects the candidate to detect inconsistency.

Source: Adapted from SBI PO Mains 2021

C is 5th from right → total = C left + C right – 1 = 12 + 5 – 1 = 16.
Now B is 14th from right → B left = 16 – 14 + 1 = 3.
D is 3rd to the right of B → D left = 3 + 3 = 6.
But we also have A left=18, which is impossible since total is only 16. Contradiction. Thus data inconsistent, cannot be determined.

⚠️ Trap: Many will sequentially compute D left as 6 and select an option, missing that A’s given position violates the total derived from C.
