Q1. In a row of 40 students, Anil is 18th from the left end. What is his position from the right end?

(a) 21st
(b) 22nd
(c) 23rd
(d) 24th

Answer: (c) 23rd

Solution:
Position from right = Total – Left + 1 = 40 – 18 + 1 = 23rd.

Source: SSC CGL Tier-1 2019
