# Short Tricks: Classification

Trick: Always first find the shared property of the "majority" — the one linking 3-4 items. The odd one shows up by itself.

Trick: In number classification, check this order — prime, perfect square, perfect cube, even/odd, multiples of a number, square ±1.

Trick: In letter classification, write the gap between the letters of each pair/triple. The pair with a different gap is the odd one.

Trick: In word classification, think "category" — domestic/wild, flower/fruit, metal/non-metal, living/non-living, country/city.

Trick: If even one option does not "fully" fit the shared property, that is the answer — even if the other three share several properties.

Skip Strategy: In number classification, if you cannot find the property within 30 seconds, move to the next question; they are often easier. Return later.

💡 Mnemonic: "P-S-C-E" — Prime, Square, Cube, Even — check these four first in number classification.
