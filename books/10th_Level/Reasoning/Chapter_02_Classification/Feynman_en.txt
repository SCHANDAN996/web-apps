# Feynman Technique: Classification in Simple Words

Tell your younger brother: "Let's play a game. I name four things — chair, table, bed, and banana. Which one is different?" He will say — "Banana!" Why? Because the other three are furniture, and a banana is something to eat.

That is all classification is. **First think — which family do most of the things belong to? The one that is not in that family is the answer.**

## The Biggest Trick: Find the "Family"

For every question, ask yourself — "How are 3 (or 4) of these the same?"
- Rose, Lotus, Jasmine, Mango → three are flowers → Mango is out.
- 2, 4, 6, 9, 8 → four are even numbers → 9 (odd) is out.

As soon as you catch the "family" (shared property), the odd one shows up by itself.
