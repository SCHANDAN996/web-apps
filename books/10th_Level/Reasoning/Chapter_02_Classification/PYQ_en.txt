PYQ Analysis: Classification (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Classification (odd-one-out) gives 1–3 questions per exam. Number classification is the most common, followed by word classification. The trap is usually a hidden property or two possible bases.

Q1. Find the odd one: Rose, Lotus, Lily, Potato
(a) Rose (b) Lotus (c) Lily (d) Potato
Answer: (d) Potato
Solution: Rose, Lotus and Lily are flowers; a potato is a vegetable.
Source: SSC MTS

Q2. Find the odd one: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11
(a) 5 (b) 7 (c) 9 (d) 11
Answer: (c) 9
Solution: All are prime except 9 (9 = 3×3, composite).
Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. Find the odd one: Lion, Tiger, Leopard, Cow
(a) Lion (b) Tiger (c) Leopard (d) Cow
Answer: (d) Cow
Solution: Lion, tiger and leopard are wild cats; the cow is a domestic animal.
Source: RRB Group D

Q4. Find the odd one: 4, 9, 16, 25, 30
(a) 9 (b) 16 (c) 25 (d) 30
Answer: (d) 30
Solution: 4, 9, 16, 25 are perfect squares; 30 is not.
Source: SSC CGL

Q5. Find the odd one: AB, CD, EF, GI
(a) AB (b) CD (c) EF (d) GI
Answer: (d) GI
Solution: AB, CD, EF are consecutive letters (gap 1); GI has a gap of 2 (G, H, I). Odd = GI.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. Find the odd one: 8, 27, 64, 125, 150
(a) 27 (b) 64 (c) 125 (d) 150
Answer: (d) 150
Solution: 8, 27, 64, 125 are perfect cubes (2³,3³,4³,5³); 150 is not.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q7. Find the odd one: India, China, Paris, Japan
(a) India (b) China (c) Paris (d) Japan
Answer: (c) Paris
Solution: India, China and Japan are countries; Paris is a city.
Source: SSC MTS

Q8. Find the odd one: 11, 13, 17, 19, 21
(a) 13 (b) 17 (c) 19 (d) 21
Answer: (d) 21
Solution: All are prime except 21 (21 = 3×7, composite).
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. Find the odd one: Triangle, Square, Circle, Rectangle
(a) Triangle (b) Square (c) Circle (d) Rectangle
Answer: (c) Circle
Solution: Triangle, square and rectangle have straight sides and corners; a circle does not.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. Find the odd one: Copper, Iron, Gold, Rubber
(a) Copper (b) Iron (c) Gold (d) Rubber
Answer: (d) Rubber
Solution: Copper, iron and gold are metals; rubber is a non-metal.
Source: RRB Group D

Q11. Find the odd one: 2, 3, 5, 7, 8
(a) 3 (b) 5 (c) 7 (d) 8
Answer: (d) 8
Solution: 2, 3, 5, 7 are prime; 8 is not.
Source: SSC CGL

Q12. Find the odd one: 10, 20, 30, 44, 50
(a) 20 (b) 30 (c) 44 (d) 50
Answer: (c) 44
Solution: 10, 20, 30, 50 are multiples of 10; 44 is not.
Source: SSC MTS
