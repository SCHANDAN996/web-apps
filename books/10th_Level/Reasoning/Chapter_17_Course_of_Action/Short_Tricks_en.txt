# Short Tricks: Course of Action

Trick: Apply three checks — the action must be (1) related to the problem, (2) practical, (3) not extreme.

Trick: Actions with harsh words like "shut down completely", "remove everyone", "ban forever" are usually wrong.

Trick: Prevention/solution actions are usually correct; punishment-only actions are often incomplete.

Trick: Immediately cut any action not directly related to the problem.

Trick: Check each action separately — one may be correct, another wrong.

Trick: Think about practicality — can this step actually be carried out?

Skip Strategy: For a tangled two-action situation, run the "three checks" on each; don't rush.

💡 Mnemonic: "Related, practical, moderate" — the three qualities of a correct action.
