Set 2: Statement & Course of Action (26–50)
Foundation Level • Verbal Reasoning • All Medium

Instructions: In each question below is given a statement followed by two courses of action numbered I and II. A course of action is a step or administrative decision to be taken for improvement, follow‑up, or further action in regard to the problem, policy, etc. On the basis of the information given in the statement, you have to assume everything in the statement to be true and then decide which of the suggested courses of action logically follow(s) for pursuing.

Give answer:
(a) if only I follows
(b) if only II follows
(c) if both I and II follow
(d) if neither I nor II follows

26.
Statement: The number of child labourers in the district has risen sharply in the last two years, despite a legal ban.
Courses of Action:
I. The labour department should conduct raids and ensure strict enforcement of the ban with rehabilitation measures.
II. All factories in the district should be closed until child labour is completely eradicated.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) only I follows
Solution: I is a direct, practical enforcement action that targets the problem. II is an extreme, blanket measure that would disrupt the economy and punish compliant factories without addressing root causes. Hence only I follows.
Source: IBPS PO 2020

38. (Examiner’s Trap – Dual Connection / Both Follow)

Courses of Action:

II. A nutritional awareness campaign should be launched for parents and school staff.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows

(d) neither I nor II follows

Solution: Both are complementary and practical steps. I provides immediate medical intervention; II addresses long‑term dietary habits. Neither is extreme, and both directly target the problem.
