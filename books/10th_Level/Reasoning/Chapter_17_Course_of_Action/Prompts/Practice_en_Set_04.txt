Set 4: Statement & Course of Action (76–100)
Foundation Level • Verbal Reasoning • All Medium

Instructions: In each question below is given a statement followed by two courses of action numbered I and II. A course of action is a step or administrative decision to be taken for improvement, follow‑up, or further action in regard to the problem, policy, etc. On the basis of the information given in the statement, you have to assume everything in the statement to be true and then decide which of the suggested courses of action logically follow(s) for pursuing.

Give answer:
(a) if only I follows
(b) if only II follows
(c) if both I and II follow
(d) if neither I nor II follows

76.
Statement: Cyber frauds targeting senior citizens have increased significantly.
Courses of Action:
I. A dedicated cyber crime helpline and awareness campaign for the elderly should be launched.
II. All online transactions by senior citizens should be blocked.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (a) only I follows
Solution: I is a targeted, practical administrative response. II is an extreme, patronising restriction that removes digital access entirely and fails to address the root crime. Only I follows.
Source: IBPS PO 2022

78. (Examiner’s Trap – Dual Connection / Both Follow)
Statement: A massive oil spill off the coast has severely affected marine life and the livelihood of fishermen.
Courses of Action:
I. Immediate containment and clean‑up operations along with compensation to affected fishermen should be launched.
II. A high‑level inquiry should be ordered to review offshore drilling safety protocols.

(a) only I follows
(b) only II follows
(c) both I and II follow
(d) neither I nor II follows

Answer: (c) both I and II follow
Solution: I addresses the immediate environmental and economic damage; II is a necessary preventive step to avoid recurrence. Both are valid, independent administrative actions and can proceed simultaneously. The trap lies in dismissing II as a future step when it is a standard immediate follow‑up.
Source: UPSC CSAT 2018 (adapted)
