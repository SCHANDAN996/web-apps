# Feynman Technique: Course of Action in Simple Words

Tell your younger brother: "Suppose your room has too many mosquitoes. What's the right step? 'Put up a net, don't let water collect' — that's sensible. But 'demolish the whole room' — that's crazy! Course-of-action questions just ask this — which step is sensible."

**A course of action is correct only if it is related to the problem, practical, and not extreme.**

## The Biggest Trick: Cut the "Extreme" Immediately

Any option with harsh words like "shut down completely", "remove everyone", "ban forever" — is usually wrong. Such steps are not practical in the real world.

A good course of action solves or prevents the problem, not just punishes.
