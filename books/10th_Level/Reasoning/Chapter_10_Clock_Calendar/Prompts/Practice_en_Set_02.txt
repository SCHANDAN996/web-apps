### Q27. (Medium)
**Q:** A watch gains 5 seconds in 3 minutes and was set right at 8 AM. What time will it show at 10 PM on the same day?
एक घड़ी 3 मिनट में 5 सेकंड तेज़ हो जाती है और सुबह 8 बजे सही सेट की गई। उसी दिन रात 10 बजे यह कितना समय दिखाएगी?
(A) 10:23:20 PM (B) 10:24:00 PM (C) 10:22:40 PM (D) 10:25:00 PM

**Answer:** (A) 10:23:20 PM
**Solution:** From 8 AM to 10 PM = 14 hours = 840 minutes. Gain rate = 5 seconds per 3 minutes = 5/3 seconds per minute. Total gain = (5/3) × 840 = 1400 seconds = 23 minutes 20 seconds. So clock shows 10:00:00 + 00:23:20 = 10:23:20 PM.
