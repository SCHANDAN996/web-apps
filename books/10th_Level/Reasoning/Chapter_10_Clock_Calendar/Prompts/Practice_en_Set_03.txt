### Q53. (Medium) 🧨 **Examiner's Trap**
**Q:** A watch shows 8:30. If the minute hand points towards North-East, in which direction does the hour hand point?
एक घड़ी में 8:30 बज रहे हैं। यदि मिनट की सुई उत्तर-पूर्व दिशा की ओर इशारा करती है, तो घंटे की सुई किस दिशा में इशारा करेगी?
(A) North / उत्तर (B) South / दक्षिण (C) East / पूर्व (D) South-East / दक्षिण-पूर्व

**Answer:** (D) South-East / दक्षिण-पूर्व
**Solution:** At 8:30, minute hand at 180° (pointing 6), hour hand at 255° (between 8 and 9). If minute hand points NE (45°), then the clock face is rotated. Determine relative position: hour hand is 75° clockwise from minute hand. So from NE (45°), add 75° → 120°, which is South-East.
**Trap:** Students might assume standard orientation and guess. This type of question is rare and often confuses.
**Source:** SSC CGL 2019
