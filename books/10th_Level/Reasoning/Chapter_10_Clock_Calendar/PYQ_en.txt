PYQ Analysis: Clock & Calendar (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Clock & calendar gives 1–3 questions per exam. Angle-between-hands and leap-year/odd-day questions are most common. The trap is forgetting the hour hand moves, and century leap years.

Q1. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 3 o'clock?
(a) 90° (b) 180° (c) 60° (d) 45°
Answer: (a) 90°
Solution: angle = |30 × 3 − 5.5 × 0| = 90°.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. What is the angle between the hands at 6 o'clock?
(a) 90° (b) 180° (c) 120° (d) 150°
Answer: (b) 180°
Solution: angle = |30 × 6| = 180° (a straight line).
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Each hour on a clock represents how many degrees?
(a) 15° (b) 30° (c) 60° (d) 6°
Answer: (b) 30°
Solution: 360° ÷ 12 hours = 30° per hour.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q4. How many odd days are there in an ordinary year?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 1
Solution: 365 = 52 weeks + 1 day, so 1 odd day.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q5. Is 2024 a leap year?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot say (d) Only if a century
Answer: (a) Yes
Solution: 2024 is divisible by 4 and is not a century year, so it is a leap year.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. What is the angle between the hands at 4 o'clock?
(a) 90° (b) 120° (c) 150° (d) 100°
Answer: (b) 120°
Solution: angle = |30 × 4| = 120°.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. Is 1900 a leap year?
(a) Yes (b) No (c) Cannot say (d) Always
Answer: (b) No
Solution: 1900 is a century year and is not divisible by 400, so it is not a leap year.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. What is the angle between the hands at 3:30?
(a) 75° (b) 90° (c) 60° (d) 80°
Answer: (a) 75°
Solution: angle = |30 × 3 − 5.5 × 30| = |90 − 165| = 75°.
Source: SSC CGL

Q9. How many odd days are there in a leap year?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 0 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 2
Solution: 366 = 52 weeks + 2 days, so 2 odd days.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q10. How many degrees does the minute hand move in one minute?
(a) 1° (b) 6° (c) 0.5° (d) 30°
Answer: (b) 6°
Solution: 360° ÷ 60 minutes = 6° per minute.
Source: RRB Group D
