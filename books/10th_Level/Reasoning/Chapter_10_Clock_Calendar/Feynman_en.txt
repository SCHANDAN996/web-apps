# Feynman Technique: Clock & Calendar in Simple Words

Tell your younger brother: "Think of the clock as a pizza — 12 slices, each of 30°. At 3 o'clock the hour hand is at 3, the minute hand at 12 — 3 slices between them, that is 3 × 30 = 90°! That's all."

**In a clock, each hour means 30° — that is the biggest key.**

## The Biggest Trick: The Hand Keeps Moving

At 3:30 the hour hand is not at 3 — it has moved half an hour forward (between 3 and 4). That is why the formula subtracts 5.5 per minute:
- angle = |30 × 3 − 5.5 × 30| = |90 − 165| = 75°.

For the calendar: an ordinary year means the same date next year shifts 1 day forward (52 weeks + 1 odd day).
