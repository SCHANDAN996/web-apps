PYQ Analysis: Blood Relations (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Blood relations give 2–3 questions per exam. Direct relations and photo puzzles are most common; coded relations appear in banking. The trap is generation direction and assumed gender.

Q1. A is the brother of B. B is the mother of C. How is A related to C?
(a) Father (b) Brother (c) Maternal uncle (d) Grandfather
Answer: (c) Maternal uncle
Solution: A is B's brother, and B is C's mother, so A is the mother's brother of C = maternal uncle.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. A man pointing to a lady said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the lady related to the man?
(a) Mother (b) Sister (c) Aunt (d) Cousin
Answer: (b) Sister
Solution: Grandfather's only son = the man's father. The father's daughter = the man's sister.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. P is the husband of Q. Q is the mother of R. R is the sister of S. How is P related to S?
(a) Brother (b) Uncle (c) Father (d) Grandfather
Answer: (c) Father
Solution: R and S are siblings (children of Q). P is Q's husband, so P is the father of S.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q4. If 'A + B' means A is the father of B, and 'A − B' means A is the wife of B, then in 'P − Q', how is P related to Q?
(a) Husband (b) Wife (c) Mother (d) Sister
Answer: (b) Wife
Solution: 'A − B' means A is the wife of B, so P is the wife of Q.
Source: Bank PO

Q5. Looking at a portrait, Ram said, "His mother is the wife of my father's son. I have no brothers or sisters." Whose portrait is it?
(a) His son (b) His father (c) His nephew (d) His cousin
Answer: (a) His son
Solution: "My father's son" = Ram himself (no siblings). So the portrait's mother is Ram's wife, meaning the portrait is Ram's son.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. X is the son of Y. Y is the daughter of Z. How is X related to Z?
(a) Son (b) Grandson (c) Nephew (d) Brother
Answer: (b) Grandson
Solution: Y is the daughter of Z, and X is the son of Y, so X is the grandson of Z.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the brother of C. E is the son of D. How is E related to B?
(a) Brother (b) Cousin (c) Uncle (d) Nephew
Answer: (b) Cousin
Solution: C is father of A and B. D is C's brother (uncle of A, B). E is D's son, so E is the cousin of B.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. Pointing to a girl, Meena said, "She is the daughter of the only child of my father." How is the girl related to Meena?
(a) Sister (b) Daughter (c) Niece (d) Cousin
Answer: (b) Daughter
Solution: "The only child of my father" = Meena herself. So the girl is Meena's daughter.
Source: RRB Group D

Q9. If 'A × B' means A is the mother of B and 'A + B' means A is the father of B, then in 'P + Q × R', how is P related to R?
(a) Father (b) Maternal grandfather (c) Paternal grandfather (d) Uncle
Answer: (b) Maternal grandfather
Solution: Q × R: Q is the mother of R. P + Q: P is the father of Q. So P is the father of R's mother = maternal grandfather.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q10. Ravi is the son of Ram. Sita is the daughter of Ram. How is Sita related to Ravi?
(a) Mother (b) Sister (c) Aunt (d) Niece
Answer: (b) Sister
Solution: Ravi and Sita are both children of Ram, so Sita is the sister of Ravi.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q11. M is the sister of N. N is the son of O. How is O related to M?
(a) Son (b) Brother (c) Father or Mother (d) Uncle
Answer: (c) Father or Mother
Solution: M is the sister of N, so M is also a child of O. Thus O is the parent (father or mother) of M.
Source: SSC MTS

Q12. A woman pointing to a man said, "He is the brother of the daughter of my mother." How is the man related to the woman?
(a) Father (b) Brother (c) Uncle (d) Son
Answer: (b) Brother
Solution: "The daughter of my mother" = the woman herself (or her sister). The brother of that daughter is the woman's brother.
Source: SSC CGL
