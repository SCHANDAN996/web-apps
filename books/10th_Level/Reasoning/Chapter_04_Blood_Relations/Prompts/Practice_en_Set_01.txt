1.  
**A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. How is A related to C?**  
(A) Sister  
(B) Brother  
(C) Cannot be determined  
(D) Cousin  
**Answer: (B) Brother**  
**Solution:** A is brother of B → A male. B is sister of C → C and B are siblings. So A, B, C are siblings. A is brother of C.  
**Source:** SSC CGL 2020

2.  
**Pointing to a boy, a man said, “He is my father’s only son.” How is the boy related to the man?**  
(A) Son  
(B) Brother  
(C) Himself  
(D) Cousin  
**Answer: (C) Himself**  
**Solution:** The man is the speaker. Father’s only son = speaker himself (since no other brother). So the boy is himself.  
**Source:** IBPS PO 2019

3.  
**Introducing a girl, Raj said, “She is the daughter of my grandfather’s only son.” How is the girl related to Raj?**  
(A) Mother  
(B) Sister  
(C) Aunt  
(D) Cousin  
**Answer: (B) Sister**  
**Solution:** Grandfather’s only son = Raj’s father. His daughter = Raj’s sister.  
**Source:** SSC CHSL 2021

4.  
**A is the mother of B. B is the brother of C. How is A related to C?**  
(A) Mother  
(B) Aunt  
(C) Sister  
(D) Grandmother  
**Answer: (A) Mother**  
**Solution:** B is brother of C ⇒ B and C are siblings. A is mother of B, so A is also mother of C.  
**Source:** RRB NTPC 2019

5.  
**If ‘A + B’ means ‘A is the father of B’, ‘A – B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’, then P + Q – R means:**  
(A) P is father of R  
(B) P is brother of R  
(C) P is uncle of R  
(D) P is grandfather of R  
**Answer: (A) P is father of R**  
**Solution:** P + Q ⇒ P father of Q. Q – R ⇒ Q sister of R. So Q and R are siblings, P is father of both. So P is father of R.  
**Source:** SSC CGL 2018

6.  
**A man said while looking at a photograph, “I have no brother or sister, and that man’s father is my father’s son.” Whose photograph is it?**  
(A) His son  
(B) His nephew  
(C) His father  
(D) Himself  
**Answer: (A) His son**  
**Solution:** The man has no siblings ⇒ my father’s son = himself. So “that man’s father is my father’s son” ⇒ that man’s father = himself ⇒ that man is his son.  
**Source:** UPSC CSAT 2017

7.  
**If ‘P × Q’ means ‘P is brother of Q’, ‘P ÷ Q’ means ‘P is mother of Q’, then A × B ÷ C means:**  
(A) A is uncle of C  
(B) A is father of C  
(C) A is nephew of C  
(D) A is brother of C  
**Answer: (A) A is uncle of C**  
**Solution:** A × B ⇒ A brother of B. B ÷ C ⇒ B mother of C. So A is maternal uncle (mama) of C.  
**Source:** IBPS PO 2020

8.  
**A woman introduces a man as “He is the only son of my mother’s mother.” How is the man related to the woman?**  
(A) Brother  
(B) Uncle  
(C) Cousin  
(D) Father  
**Answer: (B) Uncle**  
**Solution:** Mother’s mother = maternal grandmother. Her only son = mother’s only brother = maternal uncle.  
**Source:** SSC CGL 2016

9.  
**A is B’s sister. C is B’s mother. D is C’s father. E is D’s mother. How is A related to D?**  
(A) Daughter  
(B) Granddaughter  
(C) Sister  
(D) Mother  
**Answer: (B) Granddaughter**  
**Solution:** A is sister of B ⇒ both siblings. C is mother of B ⇒ C is mother of A as well. D is father of C ⇒ D is grandfather of A. So A is granddaughter of D.  
**Source:** RRB NTPC 2021

11.  
**If ‘A + B’ means ‘A is the son of B’, ‘A – B’ means ‘A is the daughter of B’, then M + N – O means:**  
(A) M is grandson of O  
(B) M is granddaughter of O  
(C) M is son of O  
(D) Cannot be determined  
**Answer: (A) M is grandson of O**  
**Solution:** M + N ⇒ M son of N. N – O ⇒ N daughter of O. So M is son of N, N is daughter of O ⇒ M is grandson (son’s son) of O. (M gender known as male from ‘son’)  
**Source:** IBPS PO 2017

12.  
**Pointing to a man, a woman said, “His mother is the only daughter of my father.” How is the woman related to the man?**  
(A) Mother  
(B) Aunt  
(C) Sister  
(D) Daughter  
**Answer: (A) Mother**  
**Solution:** The woman’s father’s only daughter = herself (if she is only daughter). So “his mother is the only daughter of my father” ⇒ his mother = the woman herself. So the man is her son.  
**Source:** SSC CGL 2021

13.  
**A is uncle of B. B is daughter of C. C is daughter-in-law of D. How is A related to D?**  
(A) Son  
(B) Son-in-law  
(C) Brother  
(D) Nephew  
**Answer: (A) Son**  
**Solution:** A is uncle of B ⇒ A is brother of B’s father (or mother). B is daughter of C, so C is B’s parent. C is daughter-in-law of D ⇒ C’s husband is D’s son. So B’s father is D’s son. A is brother of B’s father ⇒ A is also D’s son.  
**Source:** UPSC CSAT 2015

14.  
**Six members A, B, C, D, E, F. A and B are a married couple. A is father of C. C is brother of D. D is daughter of E. Who is E?**  
(A) A  
(B) B  
(C) C  
(D) F  
**Answer: (B) B**  
**Solution:** A father of C, C brother of D ⇒ C and D are siblings, A is father of both. D is daughter of E. Since A is father, E must be the mother. The only married female is B. So E = B.  
**Source:** RRB NTPC 2018

15.  
**If ‘P # Q’ means ‘P is wife of Q’, ‘P @ Q’ means ‘P is son of Q’, then A # B @ C means:**  
(A) A is daughter-in-law of C  
(B) A is mother of C  
(C) A is daughter of C  
(D) A is sister of C  
**Answer: (A) A is daughter-in-law of C**  
**Solution:** A # B ⇒ A wife of B. B @ C ⇒ B son of C. So A is wife of C’s son ⇒ daughter-in-law of C.  
**Source:** IBPS PO 2022

17.  
**‘A $ B’ means A is brother of B, ‘A & B’ means A is mother of B, ‘A * B’ means A is daughter of B. M $ N & O * P ⇒ How is M related to P?**  
(A) Son  
(B) Brother-in-law  
(C) Father  
(D) Uncle  
**Answer: (B) Brother-in-law**  
**Solution:** M brother of N; N mother of O; O daughter of P. So N and P are married (P is father of O). M is brother of N, so M is brother-in-law of P.  
**Source:** SSC CGL 2020

(A) Father

19.  
**Pointing to a woman, Raj said, “She is the only daughter of my mother.” How is Raj related to the woman?**  
(A) Brother  
(B) Sister  
(C) Daughter  
(D) Son  
**Answer: (A) Brother**  
**Solution:** “Only daughter of my mother” – If Raj is male, then the only daughter is his sister. If Raj were female, the statement would refer to herself, but she’s pointing to someone else, so Raj is male, making the woman his sister.  
**Source:** SSC CHSL 2022

20.  
**In a family, P is the son of Q. Q is the sister of R. R is the daughter of S. How is P related to S?**  
(A) Grandson  
(B) Son  
(C) Nephew  
(D) Brother  
**Answer: (A) Grandson**  
**Solution:** Q sister of R ⇒ both are daughters of S. P is son of Q ⇒ P is S’s grandson.  
**Source:** RRB NTPC 2020

21.  
**A man points to a woman and says, “She is the wife of the grandson of my mother.” How is the woman related to the man?**  
(A) Daughter-in-law  
(B) Sister-in-law  
(C) Niece  
(D) Cannot be determined  
**Answer: (D) Cannot be determined**  
**Solution:** The grandson of his mother could be his own son (if he has a son) or his brother’s son (nephew). So the woman could be daughter-in-law or niece-in-law. No information to decide. Classic trap.  
**Source:** UPSC CSAT 2014

22.  
**A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?**  
(A) Uncle  
(B) Father  
(C) Brother  
(D) Grandfather  
**Answer: (A) Uncle**  
**Solution:** A and B are siblings. B sister of C ⇒ C is brother of B, so A, B, C all siblings (C male). C father of D ⇒ A is D’s paternal uncle.  
**Source:** SSC CGL 2019

23.  
**If ‘A % B’ means ‘A is wife of B’, ‘A # B’ means ‘A is son of B’, ‘A @ B’ means ‘A is brother of B’, then P % Q @ R # S. How is P related to S?**  
(A) Mother  
(B) Daughter-in-law  
(C) Sister  
(D) Aunt  
**Answer: (B) Daughter-in-law**  
**Solution:** P wife of Q; Q brother of R ⇒ Q and R are brothers. R son of S ⇒ S is parent of R, hence parent of Q as well. P is wife of Q ⇒ P is daughter-in-law of S.  
**Source:** IBPS PO 2021

24.  
**A man points to a girl and says, “She is the daughter of my only brother’s father’s only daughter-in-law.” How is the girl related to the man?**  
(A) Daughter  
(B) Niece  
(C) Sister  
(D) Cannot be determined  
**Answer: (D) Cannot be determined**  
**Solution:** “My only brother’s father” = my father. “Father’s only daughter-in-law” could be the man’s wife (if he is married and brother unmarried) or the brother’s wife (if brother is married and man unmarried). So the girl could be his daughter or his niece. Trap: dual possibility due to “only”.  
**Source:** SSC CGL 2018

25.  
**A is father of B. B is sister of C. C is mother of D. E is brother of A. How is E related to D?**  
(A) Uncle  
(B) Grandfather  
(C) Great-uncle  
(D) Brother  
**Answer: (C) Great-uncle**  
**Solution:** A father of B, B sister of C ⇒ A grandfather of D (since C is mother of D). E is brother of A ⇒ E is great-uncle (grandfather’s brother) of D.  
**Source:** UPSC CSAT 2016
