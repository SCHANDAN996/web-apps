Back: Always draw the symbols one step at a time; never solve mentally. Translate each code to a visual link.

Front: What is the "Lego Tower Trick" in Blood Relations?
Back: Build a relationship chain by adding one person at a time, clicking each new person to the last person you drew, just like stacking Lego bricks. Never skip or try to build the whole tower in your head at once.

Front: When a question says "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son," why does the word "only" act like a superpower?
Back: Because it collapses possibilities. "Only son" means there is exactly one son, so grandfather's only son = my father. Without "only", there could be multiple sons, making it ambiguous.

Front: In a coded relation puzzle like "P Q means P is the father of Q; P # Q means P is the sister of Q", what is the most common mistake students make while decoding the chain? **Back:** They mix up the direction. Front: You're solving a puzzle and the gender of a key person named "Kamal" is not revealed. The options list "Brother", "Sister", and "Cannot be determined". Which one should you choose and why?
Back: "Cannot be determined." Names do not guarantee gender in competitive exams. Never assume; wait for a gender marker like "he", "she", "brother", "wife".

Front: A statement says "A is the only brother of B and also the son of C." How many roles does A play in this tree, and why is this a classic examiner's trap?
Back: Two roles: brother to B and son to C. The trap is dual relations. If you miss that A is both, you'll incorrectly answer questions about B's relation to C.

Front: What is the "Generation Row Rule" when drawing a family tree, and why does it save you from cross-generation confusion?
Back: Place every person of the same generation on the same horizontal row. This visually prevents mixing up someone's uncle with their cousin, etc.

Front: In a coded relation puzzle, you see "A % B means A is the mother of B". You have the expression "X % Y % Z". What two-step visual action will stop you from getting lost?
Back: First draw X (female) with vertical line down to Y, then Y (female) with vertical line down to Z. So X is grandmother of Z. Draw immediately, don't mentally process the chain.
