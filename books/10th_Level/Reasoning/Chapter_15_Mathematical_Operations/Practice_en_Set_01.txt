Set 1: Mathematical Operations MCQs (English)
Questions 1–16: Easy | Questions 17–20: Medium

Q1. If '+' means '×', then 3 + 4 = ?
(a) 7 (b) 12 (c) 1 (d) 34
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: 3 × 4 = 12.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q2. If '×' means '+', then 6 × 2 = ?
(a) 12 (b) 8 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: 6 + 2 = 8.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. If '−' means '÷', then 12 − 4 = ?
(a) 8 (b) 3 (c) 16 (d) 48
Answer: (b) 3
Solution: 12 ÷ 4 = 3.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. If '÷' means '−', then 10 ÷ 4 = ?
(a) 6 (b) 14 (c) 40 (d) 2.5
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: 10 − 4 = 6.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. If '+' means '×', then 5 + 5 = ?
(a) 10 (b) 25 (c) 1 (d) 55
Answer: (b) 25
Solution: 5 × 5 = 25.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q6. If '+' and '−' are interchanged, then 9 + 3 − 1 = ?
(a) 7 (b) 11 (c) 5 (d) 13
Answer: (a) 7
Solution: 9 − 3 + 1 = 7.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. If '×' means '+' and '+' means '×', then 2 × 3 + 4 = ?
(a) 14 (b) 10 (c) 20 (d) 24
Answer: (a) 14
Solution: 2 + 3 × 4. By BODMAS: 3×4 = 12, then 2 + 12 = 14.
Source: SSC CGL

Q8. If '−' means '+', then 7 − 2 = ?
(a) 9 (b) 5 (c) 14 (d) 3
Answer: (a) 9
Solution: 7 + 2 = 9.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q9. If '÷' means '×', then 6 ÷ 3 = ?

Answer: (b) 18
Solution: 6 × 3 = 18.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. In BODMAS, which operation comes first?
(a) Addition (b) Brackets (c) Division (d) Subtraction
Answer: (b) Brackets
Solution: Brackets are solved first in BODMAS.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q11. If '+' means '−', then 15 + 5 = ?

Solution: 15 − 5 = 10.

Q12. If '×' and '÷' are interchanged, then 12 × 6 ÷ 2 = ?
(a) 4 (b) 36 (c) 1 (d) 16
Answer: (a) 4
Solution: 12 ÷ 6 × 2 = 2 × 2 = 4.
Source: SSC CGL

Q13. If '×' means '−', then 8 × 3 = ?

Q14. If '+' means '×' and '−' means '+', then 4 + 2 − 3 = ?

Solution: 4 × 2 + 3. By BODMAS: 4×2 = 8, then 8 + 3 = 11.

Q15. If '÷' means '+', then 9 ÷ 3 = ?

Q16. If '−' means '×' and '×' means '−', then 6 − 2 × 4 = ?

Answer: (a) 8
Solution: 6 × 2 − 4. By BODMAS: 6×2 = 12, then 12 − 4 = 8.

Q19. (Medium) If '×' means '+' and '−' means '×', then 5 × 2 − 3 = ?
(a) 11 (b) 21 (c) 13 (d) 7
Answer: (a) 11
Solution: 5 + 2 × 3. By BODMAS: 2×3 = 6, then 5 + 6 = 11.
Source: SSC CGL

Q20. (Medium) If '÷' means '−' and '−' means '÷', then 12 ÷ 4 − 2 = ?
(a) 10 (b) 8 (c) 6 (d) 4
Answer: (a) 10
Solution: 12 − 4 ÷ 2. By BODMAS: 4÷2 = 2, then 12 − 2 = 10.
Source: SSC MTS
