PYQ Analysis: Mathematical Operations (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Mathematical operations give 1–3 questions per exam. Symbol substitution and interchange types are most common. The trap is forgetting BODMAS after changing signs.

Q1. If '+' means '×', then 6 + 2 = ?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 4 (d) 3
Answer: (b) 12
Solution: + means ×, so 6 × 2 = 12.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. If '×' means '+', then 5 × 3 = ?
(a) 15 (b) 8 (c) 2 (d) 53
Answer: (b) 8
Solution: × means +, so 5 + 3 = 8.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. If '+' means '×' and '×' means '+', then 4 + 3 × 2 = ?
(a) 14 (b) 20 (c) 11 (d) 24
Answer: (a) 14
Solution: 4 × 3 + 2. By BODMAS: 4×3 = 12, then 12 + 2 = 14.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. If '+' and '−' are interchanged, then 8 + 4 − 2 = ?
(a) 6 (b) 10 (c) 2 (d) 14
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: 8 − 4 + 2 = 6.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. If '÷' means '+', then 12 ÷ 4 = ?
(a) 3 (b) 16 (c) 8 (d) 48
Answer: (b) 16
Solution: ÷ means +, so 12 + 4 = 16.
Source: SSC MTS

Q6. If '−' means '×', then 5 − 3 = ?
(a) 2 (b) 15 (c) 8 (d) 53
Answer: (b) 15
Solution: − means ×, so 5 × 3 = 15.
Source: SSC CGL

Q7. After changing signs, which rule must you still follow?
(a) Left to right (b) BODMAS (c) Right to left (d) Any order
Answer: (b) BODMAS
Solution: BODMAS order must be applied even after the signs are changed.
Source: NCERT Reasoning

Q8. If '+' and '−' are interchanged, then 10 + 5 − 3 = ?
(a) 8 (b) 12 (c) 2 (d) 18
Answer: (a) 8
Solution: 10 − 5 + 3 = 8.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. If '×' and '÷' are interchanged, then 8 × 4 ÷ 2 = ?
(a) 4 (b) 16 (c) 1 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 4
Solution: 8 ÷ 4 × 2 = 2 × 2 = 4.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q10. If '+' means '÷' and '÷' means '+', then 16 + 4 ÷ 2 = ?
(a) 6 (b) 10 (c) 12 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 6
Solution: 16 ÷ 4 + 2 = 4 + 2 = 6.
Source: SSC CGL
