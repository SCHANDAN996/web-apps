Set 1: Noun MCQs (English)
Questions 1–20: Easy | Questions 21–25: Medium

Q1. Which is a Proper Noun?
(a) boy (b) Ram (c) city (d) river
Answer: (b) Ram
Solution: Ram is the name of one particular person — a Proper Noun.
Source: NCERT English

Q2. Which is an Abstract Noun?
(a) table (b) gold (c) beauty (d) army
Answer: (c) beauty
Solution: Beauty cannot be touched, only felt — an Abstract Noun.
Source: SSC MTS

Q3. Which is a Collective Noun?
(a) crowd (b) water (c) Delhi (d) honesty
Answer: (a) crowd
Solution: A crowd is one name for a group of people.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q4. Which is a Material Noun?
(a) family (b) iron (c) anger (d) London
Answer: (b) iron
Solution: Iron is a material things are made of.
Source: NCERT English

Q5. The plural of "box" is:
(a) boxs (b) boxes (c) boxies (d) box
Answer: (b) boxes
Solution: Nouns ending in x take *es*: box → boxes.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q6. The plural of "baby" is:
(a) babys (b) babies (c) babyes (d) baby
Answer: (b) babies
Solution: Consonant + y → ies: baby → babies.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. The plural of "key" is:
(a) keies (b) keys (c) kies (d) key
Answer: (b) keys
Solution: Vowel + y → just s: key → keys.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q8. Fill in the blank: I have ___ books on my shelf.
(a) much (b) many (c) little (d) a little
Answer: (b) many
Solution: Books are countable, so *many* is used.
Source: NCERT English

Q9. Which word is uncountable?
(a) apple (b) information (c) boy (d) car
Answer: (b) information
Solution: Information has no plural and takes *much*.
Source: SSC CGL

Q10. The plural of "man" is:
(a) mans (b) mens (c) men (d) man
Answer: (c) men
Solution: An irregular plural — man → men.
Source: NCERT English

Q11. The plural of "sheep" is:
(a) sheeps (b) sheepes (c) sheep (d) sheepies
Answer: (c) sheep
Solution: Sheep stays the same in singular and plural.
Source: SSC MTS

Q12. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) She bought many furnitures. (b) She bought much furniture. (c) She bought a furniture. (d) She bought furnitures.
Answer: (b) She bought much furniture.
Solution: Furniture is uncountable — no plural, no a/an, takes *much*.
Source: SSC CGL

Q13. The possessive of "Ram" is:
(a) Rams (b) Ram's (c) Rams' (d) Ram
Answer: (b) Ram's
Solution: For a living being, add 's — Ram's book.
Source: NCERT English

Q14. The plural of "leaf" is:
(a) leafs (b) leaves (c) leafes (d) leaf
Answer: (b) leaves
Solution: f/fe → ves: leaf → leaves.
Source: RRB Group D

Q15. Which is a Common Noun?
(a) India (b) Monday (c) country (d) Ganga
Answer: (c) country
Solution: Country is the shared name of a whole class — a Common Noun.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q16. The plural of "tooth" is:
(a) tooths (b) toothes (c) teeth (d) tooth
Answer: (c) teeth
Solution: An irregular plural — tooth → teeth.
Source: NCERT English

Q17. How do you count an uncountable noun like advice?
(a) two advices (b) a piece of advice (c) an advice (d) many advice
Answer: (b) a piece of advice
Solution: Use "piece/item" with uncountables — a piece of advice.
Source: SSC MTS

Q18. Fill in the blank: There is not ___ milk left.
(a) many (b) much (c) few (d) several
Answer: (b) much
Solution: Milk is uncountable, so *much* is used.
Source: SSC CGL

Q19. The plural of "hero" is:
(a) heros (b) heroes (c) heroies (d) hero
Answer: (b) heroes
Solution: Nouns ending in o often take *es*: hero → heroes.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q20. Which sentence is correct?
(a) ram went to delhi. (b) Ram went to Delhi. (c) Ram went to delhi. (d) ram went to Delhi.
Answer: (b) Ram went to Delhi.
Solution: Proper Nouns (Ram, Delhi) always begin with a capital letter.
Source: NCERT English

Q21. (Medium) Fill in the blank: The team ___ arguing among themselves.
(a) is (b) are (c) was (d) has
Answer: (b) are
Solution: The members are acting separately, so the collective noun takes a plural verb.
Source: SSC CGL

Q22. (Medium) Choose the correct possessive.
(a) the students union (b) the students' union (c) the student's union (d) the studentses union
Answer: (b) the students' union
Solution: A plural already ending in s takes only an apostrophe.
Source: SSC CGL

Q23. (Medium) Which is correct?
(a) He gave me an information. (b) He gave me informations. (c) He gave me a piece of information. (d) He gave me many information.
Answer: (c) He gave me a piece of information.
Solution: Information is uncountable — count it with "a piece of".
Source: SSC CHSL

Q24. (Medium) Choose the correct sentence.
(a) The table's leg is broken. (b) The leg of the table is broken. (c) The tables leg is broken. (d) The leg's table is broken.
Answer: (b) The leg of the table is broken.
Solution: Lifeless things use *of* for the possessive, not 's.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q25. (Medium) Which of these is correct?
(a) a day's work (b) a days work (c) a days' work (d) a day work
Answer: (a) a day's work
Solution: Expressions of time take 's — a day's work, a mile's distance.
Source: SSC CGL
