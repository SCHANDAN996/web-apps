PYQ Analysis: Noun (SSC, Banking, Railway — last 10 years)

Noun gives 2–4 questions per exam. Uncountable-plural errors and many/much are the most common. The trap is almost always *advices/informations/furnitures*.

Q1. Which of the following is an Abstract Noun?
(a) team (b) gold (c) honesty (d) Delhi
Answer: (c) honesty
Solution: Honesty cannot be touched, only felt — that is an Abstract Noun. Team = Collective, gold = Material, Delhi = Proper.
Source: SSC CGL

Q2. Choose the correct sentence.
(a) He gave me many advices. (b) He gave me much advice. (c) He gave me many advice. (d) He gave me an advice.
Answer: (b) He gave me much advice.
Solution: "Advice" is uncountable — no plural, no a/an, and it takes *much*, not *many*.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q3. What type of Noun is "team"?
(a) Proper (b) Common (c) Collective (d) Abstract
Answer: (c) Collective
Solution: A team is one name for a group of people — a Collective Noun.
Source: SSC MTS

Q4. The plural of "knife" is:
(a) knifes (b) knives (c) knifees (d) knife
Answer: (b) knives
Solution: Nouns ending in f/fe change to *ves*: knife → knives.
Source: RRB NTPC

Q5. Fill in the blank: There is ___ water in the glass.
(a) many (b) much (c) a few (d) several
Answer: (b) much
Solution: Water is uncountable, so it takes *much*, not *many*.
Source: SSC CGL

Q6. Which is a Proper Noun?
(a) city (b) river (c) Ganga (d) country
Answer: (c) Ganga
Solution: Ganga is one particular river — a Proper Noun, always capitalised. The rest are Common Nouns.
Source: SSC MTS

Q7. The plural of "child" is:
(a) childs (b) childes (c) children (d) child
Answer: (c) children
Solution: An irregular plural — child → children.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q8. Choose the correct possessive form.
(a) the table's leg (b) the leg of the table (c) the tables leg (d) the leg's table
Answer: (b) the leg of the table
Solution: For lifeless things the possessive uses *of*, not 's.
Source: Bank Clerk

Q9. Which word is uncountable?
(a) book (b) furniture (c) chair (d) pen
Answer: (b) furniture
Solution: Furniture has no plural and takes *much*; the rest are countable.
Source: SSC CGL

Q10. The plural of "city" is:
(a) citys (b) cities (c) cityes (d) city
Answer: (b) cities
Solution: Consonant + y → ies: city → cities.
Source: RRB Group D

Q11. Fill in the blank: The team ___ playing well today.
(a) is (b) are (c) were (d) have
Answer: (a) is
Solution: Here the team acts as one unit, so the verb is singular — *is*.
Source: SSC CHSL

Q12. The correct possessive of a plural noun ending in s:
(a) boys's school (b) boys' school (c) boy's school (d) boys school
Answer: (b) boys' school
Solution: A plural already ending in s takes only an apostrophe — boys'.
Source: SSC CGL
