Q25. If 1/3 of a number is 5 more than 1/4 of the number, find the number.
(a) 60 (b) 30 (c) 20 (d) 15

Correct Answer: (a) 60
Solution:
Let the number be x.
Equation: (1/3)x – (1/4)x = 5
⇒ (4/12 – 3/12)x = 5
⇒ (1/12)x = 5
⇒ x = 5 × 12 = 60.
Source: Exam Pattern (SSC CHSL)
