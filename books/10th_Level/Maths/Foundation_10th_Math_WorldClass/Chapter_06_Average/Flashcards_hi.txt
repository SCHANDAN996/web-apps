Flashcard 1Q: If 5 friends have 100 rupees and share equally, what is each one’s share called?A: Average. It’s the equal-share value.Flashcard 2Q: What is the single golden formula for average?A: Average = Total Sum ÷ Number of items. (A = S ÷ N)Flashcard 3Q: If you know Average and Number of items, how do you find the Total Sum?A: Sum = Average × Number. (S = A × N)Flashcard 4Q: If you know Total Sum and Average, how do you find how many items there are?A: Number = Total Sum ÷ Average. (N = S ÷ A)Flashcard 5Q: After 10 innings, a batsman’s average is 45. He wants to raise it to 50 after 11 innings. How many runs must he score in the 11th inning?A: 100 runs, not 50. Because (11×50) – (10×45) = 550 – 450 = 100.Flashcard 6Q: In an inclusion problem, what is the one-step formula for the new entry’s value?A: New value = (New Avg × New Count) – (Old Avg × Old Count).Flashcard 7Q: 4 boys average 50 kg, 6 girls average 40 kg. Why is the combined average NOT 45 kg?A: Because the groups are unequal in size. The correct weighted average is 44 kg.Flashcard 8Q: What is the formula for Weighted Average?A: Weighted Average = (w₁·x₁ + w₂·x₂ + …) ÷ (w₁ + w₂ + …), where w is the weight (count) and x is the average of each group.Flashcard 9Q: Find the average of numbers 1,2,3,…,100 without adding them all.A: (First + Last) ÷ 2 = (1+100) ÷ 2 = 50.5. Works for any consecutive numbers!Flashcard 10Q: A student wrote 65 instead of 56 in a sum of 20 numbers, getting an average of 40. What is the correct average?A: First fix the sum: Wrong sum = 800. Correct sum = 800 – 65 + 56 = 791. New average = 791 ÷ 20 = 39.55.Flashcard 11Q: What is the mnemonic “SAD” for computing an average?A: Sum all values, note the Amount (how many numbers), then Divide. SAD = Sum, Amount, Divide.Flashcard 12Q: Why is the panda in the mnemonic “SAD Panda” sad?A: Because when a student forgets to Divide at the end, the panda cries. Always finish with the Division step to make the panda happy.Flashcard 13Q: A class of 15 students has an average score of 72. A new student joins, and the average rises to 74. What was the new student’s score?A: New score = (74 × 16) – (72 × 15) = 1184 – 1080 = 104. The new student scored 104.Flashcard 14Q: How do you quickly find the average of an odd number of consecutive integers?A: The average is the middle number. E.g., 11,12,13,14,15 → average is 13.Flashcard 15Q: What’s the trap when adding one item to a group? “Just add the difference” — is it correct?A: No. The new item must cover the gap for all items. Correct formula: New value = (Target Avg + number of old items × change in average).Flashcard 16Q: A batsman has 8 innings with average 30. What should he score in the 9th innings to bring average to 35?A: 9 × 35 = 315, 8 × 30 = 240, difference = 75 runs.Flashcard 17Q: What is the shortcut formula for the effect of one new value on the average?A: New Average = Old Average + (New Value – Old Average) ÷ New Total Count.Flashcard 18Q: In the “SAD” mnemonic, what happens if you multiply Average and Amount?A: You get the Sum. The panda hugs his bamboo because you did it backwards perfectly.Flashcard 19Q: The average of 10 numbers is 25. If one number is removed, the average of the remaining 9 is 24. What was the removed number?A: Total 10 numbers = 250, total 9 numbers = 216. Removed number = 250 – 216 = 34.Flashcard 20Q: A fruit seller’s average profit over 4 days is ₹30. How much profit on day 5 to make the 5-day average ₹40?A: 5 × 40 = 200, 4 × 30 = 120, so day 5 = ₹80. He must earn ₹80, not ₹40.
