After 10 innings, his batting average is 45 runs. He wonders, “How many runs must I score in the next innings to raise my average to 50?”Is the answer 50? 95% of students think so.The correct answer is 100 runs.Surprising, isn’t it? Let’s understand this shocking game — Average.Average simply means “equal sharing.”If you have a group of different numbers and you want to split them into exactly equal parts, the value in each part is the average.👉 5 friends have a total of 100 rupees. Divide equally — each gets 20 rupees. That’s the average.🧠 Without looking up, tell me — what is the very first formula for Average?The Golden Formula:textCopyDownloadAverage = (Sum of all values) / (Number of values)Remember it like this: SUM divided by NUMBER.This formula has three forms that are most useful in exams:Sum = Average × NumberNumber = Sum / AverageAverage = Sum / Number🧠 If Average and Number are given, how will you find the Sum?Let’s crack Rahul’s puzzle.Old Sum (10 innings) = 10 × 45 = 450New Average needed = 50, and innings become 11New Sum (11 innings) = 11 × 50 = 550So runs required in the 11th innings = 550 – 450 = 100This is called an Inclusion problem — always use “New Sum – Old Sum”.⚡ Fun Mnemonic Trick“SAD — Sum, Amount (number), Divide”Step 1: Find the Sum of all values.Step 2: Count the Amount (number) of values.Step 3: Divide Sum by number.Reverse it: If you know Average and Number, Multiply them to get the Sum.Major Types Seen in Exams1. Combined average:textCopyDownload= (4×50 + 6×40) / (4+6) = (200 + 240)/10 = 44 kg⚠️ Trap: (50+40)/2 = 45 is wrong — never take the simple middle path.3.
