## Set 1: Simple Interest (Foundation) – English

**Instructions:** Each question has four options. Select the correct answer and then check the solution provided. Questions 1 to 20 are Easy; 21 to 25 are Medium.

**1. What is the simple interest on ₹200 at 5% per annum for 2 years?**
(A) ₹10 (B) ₹20 (C) ₹25 (D) ₹40
**Correct Answer: (B) ₹20**
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
SI = (P × R × T) / 100 = (200 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 2000 / 100 = ₹20.
**Source:** NCERT Class 7

**2. Find the amount if ₹500 is invested at 10% per annum simple interest for 3 years.**
(A) ₹550 (B) ₹600 (C) ₹650 (D) ₹700
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹650**
**Solution:**
SI = (500 × 10 × 3) / 100 = ₹150. Amount = P + SI = 500 + 150 = ₹650.
**Source:** NCERT Class 7

**3. At what rate per annum will ₹800 yield ₹160 as simple interest in 2 years?**
(A) 5% (B) 8% (C) 10% (D) 12%
**Correct Answer: (C) 10%**
**Solution:**
SI = (P × R × T)/100 → 160 = (800 × R × 2)/100 → 160 = 16R → R = 10%.
**Source:** NCERT Class 8

**7. A sum of money becomes ₹900 after 2 years and ₹1050 after 4 years at simple interest. Find the rate of interest.**
(A) 8% (B) 10% (C) 12% (D) 15%
**Correct Answer: (B) 10%**
**Solution:**
Interest for 2 years = 1050 – 900 = ₹150. So SI for 1 year = ₹75. For first 2 years SI = ₹150. Thus P = 900 – 150 = ₹750. Now for first year: 75 = (750 × R × 1)/100 → R = (75 × 100)/750 = 10%.
**Source:** SSC CGL

**8. At simple interest, a sum doubles itself in 5 years. The rate of interest per annum is:**

**Correct Answer: (C) 20%**
**Solution:**
If sum doubles, n = 2, SI = (2 – 1)P = P. So P = (P × R × 5)/100 → R = 100/5 = 20%.
**Source:** NCERT Class 8

**21. (Medium) A person invests ₹12,000 as a fixed deposit at 8% per annum simple interest. However, he has to pay 10% tax on the interest earned at the end of each year. Find the net amount he gets after 2 years.**
(A) ₹13,280 (B) ₹13,728 (C) ₹13,920 (D) ₹13,824
**Correct Answer: (B) ₹13,728**
**Solution:**
Annual SI = (12000 × 8 × 1)/100 = ₹960. Tax = 10% of 960 = ₹96, so net interest per year = ₹864. For 2 years, total net interest = 864 × 2 = ₹1728. Net amount = 12000 + 1728 = ₹13,728.
**Source:** SBI Clerk (Mains)

**22. (Medium) The simple interest on a certain sum for 3 years at 8% per annum is half the compound interest on ₹4000 for 2 years at 10% per annum compounded annually. Find the sum placed on simple interest.**
(A) ₹1750 (B) ₹1500 (C) ₹2000 (D) ₹2500
**Correct Answer: (A) ₹1750**
**Solution:**
CI on ₹4000 for 2 years at 10% = 4000 × (1+10/100)^2 – 4000 = 4000 × 1.21 – 4000 = 4840 – 4000 = ₹840. Half of CI = ₹420. So SI = 420. Now 420 = (P × 8 × 3)/100 = (24P)/100 → P = (420 × 100)/24 = 42000/24 = 1750.
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier 2

**23. (Medium) Two equal sums were lent at 4% and 6% per annum simple interest for 5 years. If the difference in interest is ₹150, find the sum.**
(A) ₹1500 (B) ₹2000 (C) ₹2500 (D) ₹3000
**Correct Answer: (A) ₹1500**
**Solution:**
Let each sum = P. SI1 = (P × 4 × 5)/100 = 20P/100 = P/5. SI2 = (P × 6 × 5)/100 = 30P/100 = 3P/10. Difference = 3P/10 – P/5 = (3P – 2P)/10 = P/10 = 150 → P = ₹1500.
**Source:** IBPS PO Prelims

**25. (Medium) A sum of Rs. 10,500 is lent at two rates of simple interest – Rs. 6,500 at 8% p.a. and the rest at 5% p.a. What is the average rate of interest on the whole sum?**
(A) 6.5% (B) 6.86% (C) 7.14% (D) 7.5%
**Correct Answer: (B) 6.86%**
**Solution:**
Total interest for 1 year = (6500 × 8)/100 + (4000 × 5)/100 = 520 + 200 = 720. Average rate = (720 / 10500) × 100 = 6.857% ≈ 6.86%.
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier 1
