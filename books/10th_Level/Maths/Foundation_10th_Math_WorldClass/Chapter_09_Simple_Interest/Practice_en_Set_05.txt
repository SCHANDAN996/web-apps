## Set 5: Simple Interest (Foundation) – English
**Questions 101–120: Medium | Questions 121–125: Hard**

---

**101. The simple interest on a sum for 5 years at 12% per annum is ₹ 360 less than the principal. Find the principal.**
(A) ₹ 800 (B) ₹ 900 (C) ₹ 1,000 (D) ₹ 1,200
**Correct Answer: (B) ₹ 900**
**Solution:**
SI = (P × 12 × 5)/100 = 0.6P.
Given P – SI = 360 → P – 0.6P = 0.4P = 360 → P = 900.
**Source:** SSC CHSL

---

**102. A sum of ₹ 8,000 at simple interest amounts to ₹ 10,400 in 3 years. In how many years will it amount to ₹ 12,000 at the same rate?**
(A) 4 years (B) 5 years (C) 6 years (D) 7 years
**Correct Answer: (B) 5 years**
**Solution:**
SI in 3 years = 10400 – 8000 = 2400.
Rate = (2400 × 100) / (8000 × 3) = 10% p.a.
For amount 12000, SI needed = 4000.
T = (4000 × 100) / (8000 × 10) = 5 years.
**Source:** SSC CGL

**103. The simple interest on a certain sum for 3 years at 8% p.a. exceeds the simple interest on the same sum for 2 years at 5% p.a. by ₹ 140. Find the sum.**
(A) ₹ 800 (B) ₹ 1,000 (C) ₹ 1,200 (D) ₹ 1,500
**Correct Answer: (B) ₹ 1,000**
**Solution:**
SI₁ = (P × 8 × 3)/100 = 0.24P.
SI₂ = (P × 5 × 2)/100 = 0.10P.
Difference = 0.24P – 0.10P = 0.14P = 140 → P = 1000.
**Source:** SSC MTS

**104. A sum of money becomes 2.5 times in 10 years at simple interest. Find the rate of interest per annum.**
(A) 10% (B) 12% (C) 15% (D) 18%
**Correct Answer: (C) 15%**
**Solution:**
n = 2.5, SI = (2.5 – 1)P = 1.5P.
1.5P = (P × R × 10)/100 → 1.5 = R/10 → R = 15%.
**Source:** IBPS Clerk

**105. A sum of ₹ 10,000 is lent at 10% p.a. simple interest. After 2 years, the borrower pays ₹ 2,000. How much should he pay at the end of 4 years (from the start) to clear the loan?**
(A) ₹ 10,800 (B) ₹ 11,200 (C) ₹ 12,000 (D) ₹ 12,400
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 12,000**
**Solution:**
After 2 years: Amount = 10000 + (10000 × 10 × 2)/100 = 12000.
Payment of 2000 reduces balance to 10000 (new principal).
Next 2 years: SI on 10000 = 10000 × 10 × 2/100 = 2000.
Final amount = 10000 + 2000 = 12000.
**Source:** Bank Clerk

**106. The simple interest on a sum for 4 years at 7% p.a. is ₹ 112 more than the simple interest on the same sum for 3 years at 6% p.a. Find the sum.**
(A) ₹ 1,000 (B) ₹ 1,120 (C) ₹ 1,200 (D) ₹ 1,250
**Correct Answer: (B) ₹ 1,120**
**Solution:**
SI₁ = (P × 7 × 4)/100 = 0.28P, SI₂ = (P × 6 × 3)/100 = 0.18P.
Difference = 0.10P = 112 → P = 1120.
**Source:** SSC CHSL

**107. A person invests ₹ 6,000 at 5% p.a. simple interest for 5 years. After 2 years, he withdraws ₹ 2,000 from the investment. Find the total interest earned by him at the end of 5 years.**
(A) ₹ 1,200 (B) ₹ 1,290 (C) ₹ 1,350 (D) ₹ 1,500
**Correct Answer: (B) ₹ 1,290**
**Solution:**
First 2 years: SI = 6000 × 5 × 2/100 = 600. Amount = 6600.
After withdrawing 2000, remaining principal = 4600.
Next 3 years: SI = 4600 × 5 × 3/100 = 690.
Total interest = 600 + 690 = 1290.
**Source:** SSC CGL

**108. A sum of money doubles itself in 12 years at simple interest. In how many years will it become 8 times?**
(A) 72 years (B) 84 years (C) 96 years (D) 108 years
**Correct Answer: (B) 84 years**
**Solution:**
Doubling (n=2) → SI = P in 12 years.
For 8 times (n=8) → SI = 7P.
Since SI is directly proportional to time, time = 7 × 12 = 84 years.
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier 2

**109. The difference between the simple interest on a sum for 6 years at 10% p.a. and the simple interest on the same sum for 4 years at 12% p.a. is ₹ 240. Find the sum.**
(A) ₹ 1,500 (B) ₹ 1,800 (C) ₹ 2,000 (D) ₹ 2,400
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 2,000**
**Solution:**
SI₁ = (P × 10 × 6)/100 = 0.6P, SI₂ = (P × 12 × 4)/100 = 0.48P.
Difference = 0.12P = 240 → P = 2000.
**Source:** IBPS Clerk

**110. A sum of ₹ 4,500 amounts to ₹ 5,850 in 4 years at simple interest. What will it amount to in 6 years if the rate of interest is increased by 2%?**
(A) ₹ 6,750 (B) ₹ 6,900 (C) ₹ 7,065 (D) ₹ 7,200
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 7,065**
**Solution:**
SI = 5850 – 4500 = 1350.
Rate R = (1350 × 100)/(4500 × 4) = 7.5%.
New rate = 9.5%. New SI = 4500 × 9.5 × 6/100 = 2565.
Amount = 4500 + 2565 = 7065.
**Source:** SSC CGL

**112. A sum was put at simple interest at a certain rate for 3 years. Had it been put at 2% higher rate, it would have fetched ₹ 360 more. Find the sum.**
(A) ₹ 5,000 (B) ₹ 5,500 (C) ₹ 6,000 (D) ₹ 6,500
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 6,000**
**Solution:**
Increase in SI = P × (increase in rate) × T / 100 = P × 2 × 3 / 100 = 0.06P = 360 → P = 6000.
**Source:** SSC CGL

**113. The simple interest on a sum for 7 months at 6% p.a. is ₹ 84. Find the sum.**
(A) ₹ 2,000 (B) ₹ 2,200 (C) ₹ 2,400 (D) ₹ 2,800
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 2,400**
**Solution:**
Time = 7/12 year.
SI = (P × 6 × 7/12)/100 = (P × 42/12)/100 = (3.5P)/100 = 84 → P = 84 × 100 / 3.5 = 2400.
**Source:** SSC MTS

**114. A sum of ₹ 8,000 is lent at 8% p.a. simple interest for 5 years. After 3 years, the rate is increased to 10% for the remaining period. Find the total interest.**
(A) ₹ 3,200 (B) ₹ 3,400 (C) ₹ 3,520 (D) ₹ 3,600
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 3,520**
**Solution:**
First 3 years: SI = 8000 × 8 × 3/100 = 1920.
Next 2 years: SI = 8000 × 10 × 2/100 = 1600.
Total = 1920 + 1600 = 3520.
**Source:** SSC CHSL

**115. A sum of ₹ 5,000 becomes ₹ 6,200 in 3 years at simple interest. If the rate of interest is increased by 4%, in how many years will it become ₹ 8,600?**
(A) 4 years (B) 5 years (C) 6 years (D) 7 years
**Correct Answer: (C) 6 years**
**Solution:**
Current SI = 6200 – 5000 = 1200 → R = (1200 × 100)/(5000 × 3) = 8%.
New rate = 12%. New SI required = 8600 – 5000 = 3600.
T = (3600 × 100)/(5000 × 12) = 6 years.
**Source:** IBPS PO

**116. The simple interest on a sum for 4 years at 5% p.a. is one-third the simple interest on another sum for 6 years at 4% p.a. If the first sum is ₹ 2,400, find the second sum.**
(A) ₹ 5,000 (B) ₹ 5,500 (C) ₹ 6,000 (D) ₹ 6,500
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 6,000**
**Solution:**
SI₁ = 2400 × 5 × 4/100 = 480.
SI₂ = 3 × 480 = 1440.
For second sum: 1440 = (P₂ × 4 × 6)/100 = 24P₂/100 → P₂ = 1440 × 100 / 24 = 6000.
**Source:** SSC CGL

**117. A sum of money becomes 7/4 of itself in 6 years at simple interest. Find the rate per annum.**
(A) 10% (B) 12% (C) 12.5% (D) 15%
**Correct Answer: (C) 12.5%**
**Solution:**
SI = (7/4 – 1)P = 3/4 P.
SI = (P × R × 6)/100 → 6PR/100 = 3P/4 → R = (3/4) × (100/6) = 12.5%.
**Source:** SSC CHSL

**121. (Hard) The simple interest on a certain sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is equal to the compound interest on ₹ 2,000 for 2 years at 5% per annum compounded annually. Find the sum.**
(A) ₹ 1,000 (B) ₹ 1,025 (C) ₹ 1,050 (D) ₹ 1,100
**Correct Answer: (B) ₹ 1,025**
**Solution:**
CI on ₹ 2000 at 5% for 2 years = 2000(1.05² – 1) = 2000 × 0.1025 = 205.
SI on required sum = (P × 10 × 2)/100 = 0.2P = 205 → P = 205 / 0.2 = 1025.
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier 2

**122. (Hard) A sum of ₹ 50,000 is divided into two parts. One part is lent at 6% p.a. simple interest for 4 years, and the other at 10% p.a. simple interest for 3 years. The total interest earned is ₹ 13,200. Find the sum lent at 6% p.a.**
(A) ₹ 20,000 (B) ₹ 25,000 (C) ₹ 30,000 (D) ₹ 35,000
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 30,000**
**Solution:**
Let sum at 6% = x, then sum at 10% = 50000 – x.
Interest = (x × 6 × 4)/100 + ((50000 – x) × 10 × 3)/100 = 0.24x + 0.3(50000 – x)
= 0.24x + 15000 – 0.3x = 15000 – 0.06x = 13200 → 0.06x = 1800 → x = 30000.
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier 1

**123. (Hard) A person lends a certain sum at 12% p.a. simple interest for 5 years. The amount received is then lent at 15% p.a. simple interest for another 5 years. If the final amount after 10 years is ₹ 2,80,000, find the original sum.**
(A) ₹ 80,000 (B) ₹ 90,000 (C) ₹ 1,00,000 (D) ₹ 1,10,000
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 1,00,000**
**Solution:**
Let original sum = P.
After 5 years at 12%: Amount = P + (P × 12 × 5)/100 = P + 0.6P = 1.6P.
This amount is lent at 15% for next 5 years: Final amount = 1.6P + (1.6P × 15 × 5)/100 = 1.6P + 1.2P = 2.8P.
Given 2.8P = 2,80,000 → P = 1,00,000.
**Source:** IBPS PO Mains

**124. (Hard) The simple interest on a sum for 10 years at 7.5% p.a. is ₹ 180 less than the simple interest on the same sum for 12 years at 7% p.a. Find the sum.**
(A) ₹ 1,500 (B) ₹ 1,800 (C) ₹ 2,000 (D) ₹ 2,200
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 2,000**
**Solution:**
SI₁ = (P × 7.5 × 10)/100 = 0.75P.
SI₂ = (P × 7 × 12)/100 = 0.84P.
Given SI₂ – SI₁ = 0.84P – 0.75P = 0.09P = 180 → P = 2000.
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier 2

**125. (Hard) A sum of money at simple interest amounts to ₹ 4,800 in 4 years and to ₹ 5,600 in 8 years. Another sum, which is double the first, is lent at the same rate for 5 years. Find the simple interest on the second sum.**
(A) ₹ 1,500 (B) ₹ 1,800 (C) ₹ 2,000 (D) ₹ 2,400
**Correct Answer: (C) ₹ 2,000**
**Solution:**
Increase in amount from 4 to 8 years = 5600 – 4800 = 800, which is SI for 4 years.
Thus annual SI = 200. SI for first 4 years = 800.
Principal of first sum = 4800 – 800 = 4000.
Rate = (200 × 100)/(4000 × 1) = 5% p.a.
Second sum = 2 × 4000 = 8000. SI on it for 5 years = 8000 × 5 × 5/100 = 2000.
**Source:** SSC CGL Tier 2
