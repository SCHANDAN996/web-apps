Trick 12: Cube Roots of Perfect Cubes — The 1,4,5,6,9,0 Club

text
Copy
Download
For cubes, the unit digit repeats itself for 1,4,5,6,9,0 (like a mirror).
But for 2,3,7,8, they swap: 2↔8 and 3↔7.
Imagine two dancers: 2 and 8 swapping wigs; 3 and 7 swapping hats.

Trick 13: HCF × LCM = Product — The Gift Wrap

text
Copy

Picture two numbers as gifts. Their HCF is the big box, and LCM is the wrapping paper.
The product of the two gifts = (Box) × (Wrapping paper).

Trick 14: Rationalising Denominator — The Conjugate Sword

text
Copy
Download
When a denominator has a surd like (√a + √b), you attack it with its CONJUGATE SWORD (√a - √b) from both top and bottom.
The denominator becomes (a - b), a rational number, slashing the surd away.
Example: 1/(√2+1) multiply by (√2-1), denominator becomes (2-1)=1, sword wins!
