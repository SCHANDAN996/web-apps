Q126. A vessel contains a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 7 : 5. 24 litres of the mixture is withdrawn and replaced with water. Then 32 litres of the new mixture is withdrawn and replaced with water. The ratio of milk to water in the final mixture becomes 7 : 13. Find the initial quantity of the mixture.
(A) 96 litres
(B) 108 litres
(C) 120 litres
(D) 144 litres

Answer: (C) 120 litres
Solution:
Let initial quantity = 12x (milk = 7x, water = 5x).
First replacement (24 L):
Milk left = 7x – (7/12)×24 = 7x – 14. Water left = 5x – (5/12)×24 + 24 = 5x – 10 + 24 = 5x + 14.
Second replacement (32 L): New total is still 12x. Milk fraction = (7x – 14)/(12x).
Milk left after second = (7x – 14) – (7x – 14)/(12x) × 32 = (7x – 14) [1 – 32/(12x)] = (7x – 14) (1 – 8/(3x)) = (7x – 14)((3x – 8)/(3x)).
Final milk = (7x – 14)(3x – 8)/(3x). Final water = 12x – final milk.
Given final ratio milk : water = 7 : 13, so milk fraction = 7/20.
Thus (7x – 14)(3x – 8)/(3x) = (7/20)×12x = (21x/5).
Multiply both sides by 3x: (7x – 14)(3x – 8) = (21x/5) × 3x = (63x²)/5.
Factor left: 7(x – 2)(3x – 8) = 7[3x² – 8x – 6x + 16] = 7(3x² – 14x + 16) = 21x² – 98x + 112.
Set equal to (63/5)x² → 5(21x² – 98x + 112) = 63x² → 105x² – 490x + 560 = 63x² → 42x² – 490x + 560 = 0 → divide 14: 3x² – 35x + 40 = 0.
Solve: (3x – 5)(x – 8) = 0 → x = 8 (positive). Initial quantity = 12×8 = 120 litres.

Q137. Three vessels contain milk and water mixtures in the ratios 1:1, 2:1, and 3:1 respectively. They are mixed in the ratio 2:3:5 by volume. Find the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture.
(A) 13 : 7
(B) 7 : 13
(C) 27 : 13
(D) 13 : 27

Answer: (C) 27 : 13
Solution: Milk fractions = 1/2, 2/3, 3/4. Total parts = 2+3+5=10. Milk = 2×1/2 + 3×2/3 + 5×3/4 = 1+2+3.75 = 6.75. Water = 10 - 6.75 = 3.25. Ratio = 6.75 : 3.25 = 27:13.
