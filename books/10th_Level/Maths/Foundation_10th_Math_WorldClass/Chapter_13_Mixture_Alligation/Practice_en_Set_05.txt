Set 5: Mixture & Alligation (Medium 20 + Hard 5)

Q101. In what ratio must water (cost ₹0) be mixed with milk costing ₹48 per litre to obtain a mixture worth ₹32 per litre?
(A) 1 : 2
(B) 2 : 1
(C) 1 : 3
(D) 3 : 1

Answer: (A) 1 : 2
Solution:
Alligation: water = ₹0, milk = ₹48, mean = ₹32.
(48 – 32) = 16, (32 – 0) = 32 → ratio of water : milk = 16:32 = 1:2.
Source: SSC CGL (Adapted)

Q102. A trader mixes 10 kg of rice at ₹30 per kg with 20 kg of rice at ₹45 per kg and sells the mixture at ₹48 per kg. What is his profit percentage?
(A) 15%
(B) 20%
(C) 25%
(D) 30%

Answer: (B) 20%
Solution:
Total CP = 10×30 + 20×45 = 300 + 900 = 1200. Total weight = 30 kg, CP/kg = ₹40.
SP = ₹48/kg, profit = ₹8/kg. Profit% = (8/40)×100 = 20%.
Source: IBPS Clerk

Q103. A vessel contains 60 litres of milk. 12 litres is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated once more. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(A) 16 : 9
(B) 9 : 16
(C) 25 : 16
(D) 16 : 25

Answer: (A) 16 : 9
Solution:
Milk left = 60 × (1 – 12/60)² = 60 × (4/5)² = 60 × 16/25 = 38.4 L. Water = 60 – 38.4 = 21.6 L.
Ratio = 38.4 : 21.6 = 384 : 216 = 16 : 9.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier I)

Q104. How many kilograms of sugar costing ₹18 per kg must be mixed with 30 kg of sugar costing ₹24 per kg so that by selling the mixture at ₹25.30 per kg a profit of 15% is earned?
(A) 10 kg
(B) 15 kg
(C) 20 kg
(D) 25 kg

Answer: (B) 15 kg
Solution:
SP = ₹25.30, Profit = 15% → CP = 25.30 / 1.15 = ₹22 per kg.
Alligation: (24 – 22) = 2, (22 – 18) = 4 → ratio of ₹18 sugar : ₹24 sugar = 2:4 = 1:2.
Given 30 kg of ₹24 sugar, quantity of ₹18 sugar required = (1/2)×30 = 15 kg.
Source: SSC CGL

Q105. A 40-litre mixture of alcohol and water contains 30% alcohol. How much water must be added so that the alcohol becomes 20%?
(A) 20 litres
(B) 25 litres
(C) 30 litres
(D) 35 litres

Answer: (A) 20 litres
Solution:
Alcohol = 30% of 40 = 12 L. Let x L water be added.
12 / (40 + x) = 20/100 = 1/5 → 60 = 40 + x → x = 20 L.
Source: NCERT

Q106. A container contains a mixture of two liquids A and B in the ratio 5 : 3. When 8 litres of the mixture is drawn off and the container is filled with B, the ratio of A to B becomes 3 : 5. Find the capacity of the container.
(A) 16 litres
(B) 20 litres
(C) 24 litres
(D) 32 litres

Answer: (B) 20 litres
Solution:
Let capacity = 8x (A = 5x, B = 3x). After removal and addition, A = 5x – 5, B = 3x – 3 + 8 = 3x + 5.
Given (5x – 5) / (3x + 5) = 3/5 → 25x – 25 = 9x + 15 → 16x = 40 → x = 2.5, capacity = 20 L.
Source: SSC CGL

Q107. Two equal vessels contain mixtures of milk and water in the ratios 5 : 4 and 4 : 5 respectively. The contents are poured into a third vessel. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(A) 1 : 1
(B) 5 : 4
(C) 4 : 5
(D) 9 : 10

Answer: (A) 1 : 1
Solution:

Total milk = 5+4=9, water = 4+5=9 → ratio = 1:1.

Q108. A vessel contains 120 litres of a mixture of acid and water, with 75% acid. How much water must be evaporated so that the acid becomes 90%?
(A) 15 litres
(B) 20 litres
(C) 25 litres
(D) 30 litres

Answer: (B) 20 litres
Solution:
Acid = 75% of 120 = 90 L, water = 30 L. After evaporation, acid = 90 L = 90% of new total.
New total = 90/0.9 = 100 L. Water evaporated = 120 – 100 = 20 L.
Source: IBPS PO

Q112. A container contains 50 litres of pure milk. 10 litres is removed and replaced with water. Then 20 litres of the mixture is removed and replaced with water. What is the quantity of milk left?
(A) 20 litres
(B) 24 litres
(C) 28 litres
(D) 32 litres

Answer: (B) 24 litres
Solution:
After first: milk = 40 L, water = 10 L.
Second removal (20 L): milk out = (40/50)×20 = 16 L, water out = 4 L.
Milk left = 40 – 16 = 24 L. (Water remains unchanged after replacement with water).
Source: SSC CGL

Q114. A solution of 80 litres of acid and water contains 25% acid. How much acid must be added to make the solution 40% acid?
(A) 15 litres
(B) 20 litres
(C) 25 litres
(D) 30 litres

Answer: (B) 20 litres
Solution:
Acid = 25% of 80 = 20 L, water = 60 L. Let x L acid be added.
(20 + x)/(80 + x) = 40/100 = 2/5 → 5(20+x) = 2(80+x) → 100+5x=160+2x → 3x=60 → x=20 L.
Source: NCERT

Q115. Two vessels contain milk and water in the ratios 3 : 1 and 2 : 3 respectively. In what ratio must these mixtures be mixed to obtain a mixture with milk and water in the ratio 1 : 1?
(A) 2 : 5
(B) 5 : 2
(C) 1 : 1
(D) 3 : 4

Answer: (A) 2 : 5
Solution:
Milk fractions: first = 3/4, second = 2/5, target = 1/2.
Alligation: (3/4 – 1/2) = 1/4, (1/2 – 2/5) = 1/10.
Ratio of second (cheaper) to first (dearer) = (1/4) : (1/10) = 5:2, so first:second = 2:5.
Source: SSC CGL

Q116. A vessel contains 60 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3. How much of the mixture must be taken out and replaced with water so that the ratio of milk to water becomes 5 : 7?
(A) 15 litres
(B) 20 litres
(C) 25 litres
(D) 30 litres

Answer: (B) 20 litres
Solution:
Milk = 37.5 L, water = 22.5 L. Let x L be replaced.
Milk left = 37.5 – (5/8)x, water left = 22.5 – (3/8)x + x = 22.5 + (5/8)x.
For 5:7 → (37.5 – 5x/8) / (22.5 + 5x/8) = 5/7 → cross-multiply: 7(37.5 – 5x/8) = 5(22.5 + 5x/8)
→ 262.5 – 35x/8 = 112.5 + 25x/8 → 150 = 60x/8 = 7.5x → x = 20 L.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II)

Q118. Two alloys contain gold and copper in the ratios 3 : 1 and 5 : 3 respectively. Equal weights of the two are melted together. What is the ratio of gold to copper in the new alloy?

Q121. (Hard) Vessel A contains milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2, and vessel B contains them in the ratio 5 : 4. These two vessels are mixed in the ratio 5 : 3 (A : B) by volume. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?
(A) 7 : 5
(B) 5 : 7
(C) 8 : 7
(D) 7 : 8

Answer: (A) 7 : 5
Solution:
Milk fraction A = 3/5, B = 5/9. Take 5 units of A and 3 units of B.
Milk = 5×(3/5) + 3×(5/9) = 3 + 5/3 = 14/3. Total volume = 8 units.
Water = 8 – 14/3 = 10/3. Ratio milk:water = 14:10 = 7:5.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II)

Q122. (Hard) Three varieties of tea costing ₹15, ₹20 and ₹30 per kg are mixed. The ratio of the first two is 2 : 3. The mixture is sold at ₹27 per kg making a profit of 20%. What is the ratio in which the three varieties are mixed?
(A) 2 : 3 : 3
(B) 2 : 3 : 5
(C) 3 : 2 : 3
(D) 3 : 3 : 2

Answer: (A) 2 : 3 : 3
Solution:
SP = ₹27, Profit = 20% → CP = 27/1.2 = ₹22.5 per kg.
CP of mixture of first two (₹15 and ₹20) in ratio 2:3 = (2×15 + 3×20)/5 = (30+60)/5 = ₹18 per kg.
Now alligate this ₹18/kg mixture with the third variety at ₹30/kg to get mean ₹22.5.
(30 – 22.5) = 7.5, (22.5 – 18) = 4.5 → ratio of ₹18 mix : ₹30 = 7.5 : 4.5 = 5 : 3.
Thus the mixture of first two is 5 parts, third is 3 parts. Since first two are in ratio 2:3 (total 5 parts), overall ratio of first : second : third = 2 : 3 : 3.
Source: SSC CGL (Mains)

Q123. (Hard) A vessel contains 100 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3. 20% of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water. This process is repeated two more times (i.e., three times in total). How many litres of pure milk must be added to the resulting mixture so that the ratio of milk to water becomes 1 : 1?
(A) 30
(B) 32
(C) 34
(D) 36

Answer: (D) 36
Solution:
Initial milk = (5/8)×100 = 62.5 L.
After three replacements of 20% with water, milk = 62.5 × (0.8)³ = 62.5 × 0.512 = 32 L.
Water = 100 – 32 = 68 L. Let x L milk be added.
Then 32 + x = 68 → x = 36 L.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II, Adapted)

Q124. (Hard) A vessel contains a mixture of alcohol and water in the ratio 3 : 1. 20% of the mixture is withdrawn and replaced with water. Then 25% of the resulting mixture is withdrawn and replaced with an alcohol-water solution having alcohol and water in the ratio 2 : 3. What is the ratio of alcohol to water in the final mixture?
(A) 13 : 11
(B) 11 : 9
(C) 5 : 4
(D) 7 : 5

Answer: (B) 11 : 9
Solution:
Let initial volume = 100 units (alcohol 75, water 25).
First replacement (20%): alcohol = 75×0.8 = 60, water = 25×0.8 + 20 = 20+20=40. Total 100.
Second replacement: remove 25% (25 units) from mixture (60 alcohol, 40 water).
Alcohol out = 25×0.6 = 15, water out = 25×0.4 = 10.
Remaining: alcohol = 60–15 = 45, water = 40–10 = 30.
Add 25 units of 2:3 alcohol-water solution: alcohol added = (2/5)×25 = 10, water added = 15.
Final: alcohol = 45+10=55, water = 30+15=45. Ratio = 55:45 = 11:9.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II)

Q125. (Hard) A vessel contains 100 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2. 20% of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water. Then 25% of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water. Finally, 50% of the mixture is taken out and replaced with water. What is the final ratio of milk to water?
(A) 9 : 41
(B) 9 : 16
(C) 9 : 25
(D) 9 : 34

Answer: (A) 9 : 41
Solution:
Initial milk = 60 L, water = 40 L.
First replacement (20%, i.e., 20 L): milk = 60×0.8 = 48, water = 40×0.8 + 20 = 32+20=52.
Second replacement (25%, i.e., 25 L): milk out = 25% of 48 = 12, water out = 25% of 52 = 13.
Milk = 48–12 = 36, water = 52–13+25 = 64.
Third replacement (50%, i.e., 50 L): milk out = 50% of 36 = 18, water out = 50% of 64 = 32.
Milk = 36–18 = 18, water = 64–32+50 = 82. Ratio = 18:82 = 9:41.
Source: SSC CGL (Tier II)
