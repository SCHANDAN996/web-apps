Q126. A and B can do a piece of work in 12 days and 15 days respectively. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. B works for 4 more days and then B also leaves. C alone completes the remaining work in 2 days. In how many days can C alone do the whole work?
(a) 12 days (b) 15 days (c) 20 days (d) 24 days

Answer: (b) 15 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(12, 15) = 60 units. A eff = 5, B eff = 4.
First 4 days (A+B): (5+4)×4 = 36. Remaining = 24.
Next 4 days (B alone): 4×4 = 16. Remaining = 8.
C does 8 units in 2 days → C eff = 4.
C's time = 60/4 = 15 days.
Source: SSC CPO PYQ

Q127. A is 20% more efficient than B, and B is 25% more efficient than C. C alone can do a work in 40 days. They all start together, but after 3 days A leaves, and after another 3 days B also leaves. In how many total days will the work be completed?
(a) 26 days (b) 28 days (c) 30 days (d) 32 days

Answer: (b) 28 days

Solution:
Efficiency ratio: A:B = 120:100 = 6:5, B:C = 125:100 = 5:4 → A:B:C = 6:5:4.
C's eff = 4, so total work = 4 × 40 = 160 units. A=6, B=5.
First 3 days (all): (6+5+4)×3 = 45. Remaining = 115.
Next 3 days (B+C): (5+4)×3 = 27. Remaining = 88.
C alone: 88/4 = 22 days. Total = 3+3+22 = 28 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q128. A certain number of men can complete a piece of work in 60 days. If there were 8 more men, the work could be finished in 10 days less. How many men were originally there?
(a) 32 (b) 36 (c) 40 (d) 44

Answer: (c) 40

Solution:
Let original men = M. Work = 60M.
With M+8 men, time = 50 days, work = 50(M+8).
Equate: 60M = 50(M+8) → 60M = 50M + 400 → 10M = 400 → M = 40.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q129. The efficiency ratio of A, B and C is 3 : 2 : 1. They undertake a work for ₹2800. A works for the entire duration, B works for 2/3 of the duration, and C works for 1/3 of the duration. What is A's share?
(a) ₹1500 (b) ₹1800 (c) ₹2000 (d) ₹2100

Answer: (b) ₹1800

Solution:
Let total duration = T days. Work done: A = 3T, B = 2 × (2T/3) = 4T/3, C = 1 × (T/3) = T/3.
Work ratio = 3T : 4T/3 : T/3 = 9 : 4 : 1. Total parts = 14.
A's share = (9/14) × 2800 = ₹1800.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q130. Pipe A can fill a tank in 8 hours, pipe B in 12 hours. A leak C can empty half the tank in 6 hours. If A and B are opened for 2 hours, then C is also opened. In how many total hours will the tank be full?
(a) 5⅓ hrs (b) 6 hrs (c) 6⅔ hrs (d) 7½ hrs

Answer: (c) 6⅔ hrs

Solution:
A's rate = 1/8, B's = 1/12. Combined fill = 1/8 + 1/12 = 5/24 tank/hr.
In 2 hours, filled = 5/24 × 2 = 5/12 tank. Remaining = 7/12.
Leak C empties half in 6 hrs → full empty rate = 1/12 per hr.
Net rate when all three open = 5/24 – 1/12 = 3/24 = 1/8 tank/hr.
Time to fill remaining 7/12 = (7/12) / (1/8) = 56/12 = 14/3 hrs = 4⅔ hrs.
Total time = 2 + 4⅔ = 6⅔ hrs.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q131. 3 men, 2 women and 1 boy can complete a work in 10 days. 2 men, 3 women and 1 boy can complete it in 12 days. 1 man, 2 women and 3 boys can complete it in 15 days. In how many days will 1 man, 1 woman and 1 boy together complete the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 22 days (c) 24 days (d) 26 days

Answer: (c) 24 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(10,12,15) = 60 units.
(3M+2W+1B) × 10 = 60 → 3M+2W+B = 6 …(i)
(2M+3W+1B) × 12 = 60 → 2M+3W+B = 5 …(ii)
(1M+2W+3B) × 15 = 60 → M+2W+3B = 4 …(iii)
Subtract (ii) from (i): M – W = 1 → M = W+1.
Subtract (iii) from (ii): M + W – 2B = 1 → (W+1)+W –2B = 1 → 2W –2B = 0 → W = B.
Put W = B and M = B+1 into (i): 3(B+1)+2B+B = 6 → 3B+3+3B = 6 → 6B = 3 → B=0.5, W=0.5, M=1.5.
1M+1W+1B = 2.5 units/day. Time = 60/2.5 = 24 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q132. A, B and C can complete a work in 12, 15 and 20 days respectively. They all start together. After 1 day, A leaves. B and C work for 3 more days and then B leaves. In how many total days will the remaining work be completed?
(a) 12 days (b) 13 days (c) 14 days (d) 15 days

Answer: (b) 13 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(12,15,20) = 60 units. Eff: A=5, B=4, C=3.
Day 1 (all): 5+4+3 = 12. Remaining = 48.
Next 3 days (B+C): (4+3)×3 = 21. Remaining = 27.
C alone: 27/3 = 9 days. Total days = 1+3+9 = 13 days.
Source: SSC CHSL PYQ

Q133. A and B together can do a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, C and A in 20 days. They work in a cycle: A alone on first day, B alone on second day, C alone on third day, and then repeating. In how many days will the work be finished?
(a) 27 days (b) 30 days (c) 33 days (d) 36 days

Answer: (b) 30 days

Solution:
2(A+B+C) = 1/12+1/15+1/20 = (5+4+3)/60 = 12/60 = 1/5 → A+B+C = 1/10.
Individual rates: A = 1/10 – 1/15 = 1/30, B = 1/10 – 1/20 = 1/20, C = 1/10 – 1/12 = 1/60.
Total work = 60 units. A=2, B=3, C=1.
3-day cycle = 2+3+1 = 6 units. 10 cycles (30 days) = 60 units exactly.
Source: SSC CPO PYQ

Q134. A and B can do a piece of work in 24 days and 30 days respectively. They start working together. After some days, A leaves. B works alone for 1 day, then C joins B and they complete the remaining work in 5 days. If C alone takes 40 days to do the work, after how many days from the start did A leave?
(a) 7 days (b) 8 days (c) 9 days (d) 10 days

Answer: (c) 9 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(24,30,40) = 120 units. A=5, B=4, C=3.
Let A worked for x days together with B. Work done together = (5+4)x = 9x.
B alone 1 day = 4. B+C for 5 days = (4+3)×5 = 35.
Total work equation: 9x + 4 + 35 = 120 → 9x = 81 → x = 9 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q135. A is 20% more efficient than B. B is 25% more efficient than C. Working together they can finish a work in 15 days. In how many days will A and C together finish the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 21½ days (c) 22½ days (d) 24 days

Answer: (c) 22½ days

Solution:
Efficiency A:B = 120:100 = 6:5, B:C = 125:100 = 5:4 → A:B:C = 6:5:4.
Combined eff = 15. Total work = 15 × 15 = 225 units.
A+C eff = 6+4 = 10. Time = 225/10 = 22.5 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q136. A certain number of men can complete a piece of work in some days. If 2 men leave, the work takes 8 more days to complete. If 4 men join, the work is completed 4 days earlier. How many men are there initially?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6

Answer: (b) 4

Solution:
Let initial men = M, initial days = D. Work = MD.
Case 1: (M–2)(D+8) = MD → –2D + 8M – 16 = 0 → 8M – 2D = 16 → 4M – D = 8.
Case 2: (M+4)(D–4) = MD → 4D – 4M – 16 = 0 → –M + D = 4 → D = M + 4.
Substitute: 4M – (M+4) = 8 → 3M = 12 → M = 4.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q137. A, B and C can do a work in 10, 15 and 20 days respectively. They all start together. After 2 days A leaves. B and C continue for 3 more days, then B leaves and C finishes the work. If total wages for the work is ₹2100, what is B's share?
(a) ₹500 (b) ₹600 (c) ₹700 (d) ₹800

Answer: (c) ₹700

Solution:
Total work = LCM(10,15,20) = 60 units. A=6, B=4, C=3.
First 2 days (all): (6+4+3)×2 = 26. Remaining = 34.
Next 3 days (B+C): (4+3)×3 = 21. Remaining = 13.
C alone: 13/3 days. B worked total 2+3 = 5 days, work = 4×5 = 20 units.
C worked 2+3+13/3 = 28/3 days, work = 3×(28/3) = 28 units. A worked 2 days = 12 units.
Ratio A:B:C = 12:20:28 = 3:5:7. B's share = (5/15)×2100 = ₹700.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q138. A and B can do a work in 12 days and 15 days respectively. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. B works alone for 4 days, then A rejoins and they complete the work. For how many days did A work in total?
(a) 4⅔ days (b) 4⁸⁄₉ days (c) 5⅓ days (d) 5⁵⁄₉ days

Answer: (b) 4⁸⁄₉ days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(12,15) = 60 units. A=5, B=4.
First 4 days (A+B): (5+4)×4 = 36. Remaining = 24.
Next 4 days (B alone): 4×4 = 16. Remaining = 8.
A rejoins: (A+B) eff = 9, time = 8/9 days.
A worked = 4 + 8/9 = 4⁸⁄₉ days.
Source: SSC CHSL PYQ

Q139. Pipes A, B and C can fill a tank in 6 hours, 8 hours and 12 hours respectively. They are opened simultaneously. After 2 hours, B is closed. After another 1 hour, C is also closed. In how many total hours is the tank filled?
(a) 2½ hrs (b) 3 hrs (c) 3⅓ hrs (d) 4 hrs

Answer: (b) 3 hrs

Solution:
Rates: A=1/6, B=1/8, C=1/12. Combined first 2 hrs = (1/6+1/8+1/12) = (4+3+2)/24 = 9/24 = 3/8 tank/hr.
In 2 hrs: 2 × 3/8 = 6/8 = 3/4 tank. Remaining = 1/4.
Next 1 hr (A+C): rate = 1/6+1/12 = 3/12 = 1/4 tank/hr. Fills exactly 1/4. Tank full in 2+1=3 hrs.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q140. A and B can do a work in 12 days and 15 days respectively. They work on alternate days, starting with A. After some days, B leaves, and the remaining work is finished by A alone in 3 days. For how many days did they work alternately before B left?
(a) 8 days (b) 10 days (c) 12 days (d) 14 days

Answer: (b) 10 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(12,15) = 60 units. A=5, B=4.
Alternate days starting A: 2‑day cycle = 5+4 = 9 units.
Let k cycles (2k days) of alternate work, then A alone 3 days.
Work done = 9k + 5×3 = 9k+15 = 60 → 9k = 45 → k = 5 cycles.
Days of alternate work = 2×5 = 10 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q141. A is 25% less efficient than B. C is 20% more efficient than A. Together they can complete a work in 10 days. In how many days will B alone complete the work?
(a) 25 days (b) 26½ days (c) 28 days (d) 30 days

Answer: (b) 26½ days

Solution:
A = 0.75 B, C = 1.2 A = 1.2 × 0.75 B = 0.9 B.
Efficiency ratio A:B:C = 0.75:1:0.9 = 15:20:18. Combined = 53.
Total work = 53 × 10 = 530. B's time = 530/20 = 26.5 days.

Q142. A and B together can do a piece of work in 12 days. A alone can do it in 24 days. They start together for 4 days, then B leaves. A works alone for 4 days, then C joins A and they finish the remaining work in 6 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 20 days (b) 24 days (c) 28 days (d) 30 days

Answer: (b) 24 days

Solution:
Work = LCM(12,24) = 24 units. A+B = 2, A=1 → B=1.
First 4 days (A+B): 2×4 = 8, remaining 16.
Next 4 days (A alone): 1×4 = 4, remaining 12.
A+C 6 days: eff = 12/6 = 2. Since A=1, C=1.
C alone time = 24/1 = 24 days.
Source: SSC CHSL PYQ

Q143. The efficiency ratio of A and B is 5:4. Together they can complete 2/3 of a work in 8 days. In how many days will A alone complete the full work?
(a) 20 days (b) 21⅗ days (c) 24 days (d) 25 days

Answer: (b) 21⅗ days

Solution:
Combined eff = 5+4 = 9. In 8 days, work done = 9×8 = 72 = 2/3 of total.
Total work = 72 × (3/2) = 108 units. A's eff = 5, time = 108/5 = 21.6 days = 21⅗ days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q144. 18 men or 27 women can complete a work in 20 days. In how many days will 12 men and 18 women complete the same work?
(a) 12 days (b) 15 days (c) 18 days (d) 20 days

Answer: (b) 15 days

Solution:
18M = 27W → M = 1.5W. Total work = 18M × 20 = 360M = 540W.
12M+18W = 12×1.5W+18W = 18W+18W = 36W. Time = 540/36 = 15 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q145. A and B can do a piece of work in 30 days, B and C in 12 days, A and C in 15 days. They all start together. After 5 days, A leaves. B and C continue for 3 more days, then B leaves. In how many days will C alone complete the remaining work?
(a) 3 days (b) 4 days (c) 5 days (d) 6 days

Answer: (c) 5 days

Solution:
2(A+B+C) = 1/30+1/12+1/15 = (2+5+4)/60 = 11/60 → A+B+C = 11/120.
Individual: A = 11/120 – 1/12 = 1/120, B = 11/120 – 1/15 = 3/120, C = 11/120 – 1/30 = 7/120.
Total work = 120 units. A=1, B=3, C=7.
First 5 days (all): (1+3+7)×5 = 55. Remaining = 65.
Next 3 days (B+C): (3+7)×3 = 30. Remaining = 35.
C alone: 35/7 = 5 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q146. A can do a work in 20 days, B in 30 days. They work together for 4 days. Then C, whose efficiency is the average of A and B, joins them. In how many total days will the work be finished?
(a) 8⅓ days (b) 9 days (c) 9⅓ days (d) 10 days

Answer: (c) 9⅓ days

Solution:
A=1/20, B=1/30. Combined = 1/20+1/30 = 1/12. In 4 days: 4/12 = 1/3 work. Remaining = 2/3.
C's eff = (1/20+1/30)/2 = (1/12)/2 = 1/24. All three = 1/12+1/24 = 3/24 = 1/8.
Time for remaining = (2/3) / (1/8) = 16/3 = 5⅓ days. Total = 4 + 5⅓ = 9⅓ days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q147. A and B can do a work in 12 days and 18 days respectively. They work on alternate days, starting with A. On the days B works, he is assisted by C who is half as efficient as B. In how many days will the work be completed?

Solution:

6 cycles (12 days) = 36 units exactly.
Source: SSC CHSL PYQ

Q148. The efficiency ratio of A, B and C is 7 : 5 : 4. They work together to complete a work for ₹8400. A and B work on all days, but C works only on every alternate day. What is C's share?
(a) ₹1000 (b) ₹1200 (c) ₹1400 (d) ₹1600

Solution:

C's share = (2/14) × 8400 = ₹1200.

Q149. A and B together can do a work in 25 days, B and C in 20 days, C and A in 15 days. They work in a cyclic order: A alone on day 1, B alone on day 2, C alone on day 3, repeating. In how many days will the work be completed?
(a) 36 days (b) 37½ days (c) 38¹²⁄₂₃ days (d) 40 days

Answer: (c) 38¹²⁄₂₃ days

Solution:
2(A+B+C) = 1/25+1/20+1/15 = (12+15+20)/300 = 47/300 → A+B+C = 47/600.
Individual: A = 47/600 – 1/20 = 17/600, B = 47/600 – 1/15 = 7/600, C = 47/600 – 1/25 = 23/600.
Total work = 600 units. A=17, B=7, C=23. 3‑day cycle = 17+7+23 = 47.
12 full cycles (36 days) = 12×47 = 564. Remaining = 36.
Day 37 (A): 17 done, remaining 19. Day 38 (B): 7 done, remaining 12. Day 39 (C): 12/23 day.
Total = 38 + 12/23 = 38¹²⁄₂₃ days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ

Q150. A certain number of men can complete a work in 24 days. They start working, but after 10 days, 5 men leave. After another 10 days, 5 more men leave, and the remaining work is completed by the remaining men in the next 10 days. Find the initial number of men.
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 30 (d) 35

Answer: (b) 25

Solution:
Let initial men = N. Total work = 24N.
First 10 days: work done = 10N. Remaining = 14N.
Next 10 days (N–5 men): work done = 10(N–5) = 10N–50. Remaining = 14N – (10N–50) = 4N+50.
Last 10 days (N–10 men): work = 10(N–10) = 4N+50 → 10N – 100 = 4N + 50 → 6N = 150 → N = 25.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ
