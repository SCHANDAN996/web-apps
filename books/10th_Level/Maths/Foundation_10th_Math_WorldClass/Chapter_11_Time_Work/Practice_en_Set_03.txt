Q51. A can do a piece of work in 8 days, B in 12 days. With the help of C, they finish the work in 3 days. In how many days can C alone do the work?
(a) 6 days (b) 8 days (c) 9 days (d) 12 days

Answer: (b) 8 days

Solution:
Total work = LCM(8,12) = 24 units.
A's eff = 3, B's eff = 2. (A+B+C)'s eff = 24/3 = 8.
C's eff = 8 – 3 – 2 = 3.
C's time = 24/3 = 8 days.
Source: SSC CGL PYQ
