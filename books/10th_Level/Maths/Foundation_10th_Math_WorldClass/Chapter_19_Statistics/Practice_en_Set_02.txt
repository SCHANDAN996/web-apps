Q29. If the mean of 10, 12, 13, 15, x, 17, 20 is 15, then the value of x is:
A. 15
B. 16
C. 17
D. 18

✅ Correct Answer: D. 18

Q37. In a moderately asymmetrical distribution, mean = 24 and mode = 30. The median is:
A. 26
B. 27
C. 28
D. 25

✅ Correct Answer: A. 26
Step-by-step: Using Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean → 30 = 3Mdn – 2×24 → 30 = 3Mdn – 48 → 3Mdn = 78 → Mdn = 26.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

Q38. For a given data, mean = 35, median = 40. The approximate value of mode is:
A. 45
B. 50
C. 55
D. 60

✅ Correct Answer: B. 50
Step-by-step: Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean = 3×40 – 2×35 = 120 – 70 = 50.
Source: Bank PO 2019

Q39. If the ratio of mean and median of a certain data is 2:3, then the ratio of mode and mean is:
A. 5:2
B. 3:5
C. 5:3
D. 3:2

✅ Correct Answer: A. 5:2
Step-by-step: Let mean = 2k, median = 3k. Mode = 3×3k – 2×2k = 9k – 4k = 5k. Ratio Mode:Mean = 5k:2k = 5:2.
Source: SSC CPO 2018
