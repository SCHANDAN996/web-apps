Q101. The mean weight of 20 boys is 50 kg, and the mean weight of 30 girls is 40 kg. The combined mean weight of all 50 students is:
A. 42 kg
B. 44 kg
C. 45 kg
D. 46 kg

✅ Correct Answer: B. 44 kg
Step-by-step: Total weight of boys = 20 × 50 = 1000 kg. Total weight of girls = 30 × 40 = 1200 kg. Combined total = 1000 + 1200 = 2200 kg. Total students = 50. Combined mean = 2200 ÷ 50 = 44 kg.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q102. The mean of 15 numbers is 20. If each number is increased by 4 and then multiplied by 2, the new mean is:
A. 40
B. 44
C. 48
D. 52

✅ Correct Answer: C. 48
Step-by-step: Adding 4 to each number increases the mean by 4 → 24. Multiplying each number by 2 then multiplies the mean by 2 → 24 × 2 = 48.
Source: SSC MTS 2019
