Q52. The mean of 20 observations is 18. If each observation is increased by 2, the new mean is:
A. 18
B. 19
C. 20
D. 22

✅ Correct Answer: C. 20
Step-by-step: If a constant is added to each observation, the mean increases by that constant. New mean = 18 + 2 = 20.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q53. The empirical relationship among mean, median and mode is valid only when the distribution is:
A. Symmetrical
B. Moderately skewed
C. Highly skewed
D. Always valid

✅ Correct Answer: B. Moderately skewed
Step-by-step: Karl Pearson’s empirical relation, Mode ≈ 3 Median – 2 Mean, holds approximately for moderately skewed (asymmetrical) distributions.
Source: NCERT Class 10

Q54. The class mark of the interval 25–35 is:
A. 25
B. 30
C. 35
D. 10

✅ Correct Answer: B. 30
Step-by-step: Class mark = (Lower limit + Upper limit) / 2 = (25+35)/2 = 30.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q55. The mean of five numbers is 27. If one number is excluded, the mean becomes 25. The excluded number is:

✅ Correct Answer: C. 35

Q63. In a frequency distribution, the mid‑value of a class is 15 and the class width is 10. The lower limit of the class is:
A. 5
B. 10
C. 12
D. 20

✅ Correct Answer: B. 10
Step-by-step: Mid-value = (Lower + Upper)/2. Upper – Lower = 10. Let lower = x, then upper = x+10. Mid = (2x+10)/2 = x+5 = 15 → x = 10.
Source: NCERT Class 9

Q64. The mean height of 25 boys is 160 cm. The mean height of 15 girls is 150 cm. The combined mean height of all 40 students is:
A. 155 cm
B. 156.25 cm
C. 157.5 cm
D. 158 cm

✅ Correct Answer: B. 156.25 cm
Step-by-step: Total height of boys = 25×160 = 4000 cm. Total height of girls = 15×150 = 2250 cm. Combined sum = 6250 cm. Combined n = 40. Combined mean = 6250/40 = 156.25 cm.
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

Q65. If the mode of a distribution is 18 and the mean is 24, then its median is:
A. 20
B. 21
C. 22
D. 23

✅ Correct Answer: C. 22
Step-by-step: Using Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean → 18 = 3 Median – 48 → 3 Median = 66 → Median = 22.
Source: Bank PO 2019

Q70. In a moderately skewed distribution, the mean is 50 and the mode is 44. The median is:
A. 46
B. 47
C. 48
D. 49

✅ Correct Answer: C. 48
Step-by-step: Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean → 44 = 3 Med – 100 → 3 Med = 144 → Med = 48.
Source: SSC CGL 2021
