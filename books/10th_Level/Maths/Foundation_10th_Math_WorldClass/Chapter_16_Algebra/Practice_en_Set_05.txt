101. Solve the system: 2x + 3y = 13, 5x – 4y = –2.
(A) x = 2, y = 3
(B) x = 3, y = 2
(C) x = 1, y = 4
(D) x = 4, y = 1
Answer: (A) x = 2, y = 3
Solution:
Multiply first equation by 4: 8x + 12y = 52. Multiply second by 3: 15x – 12y = –6. Add: 23x = 46 → x = 2. Substitute: 2(2) + 3y = 13 → 4 + 3y = 13 → 3y = 9 → y = 3.
Source: NCERT Class 10

102. If a + b = 8 and a² + b² = 40, find the value of ab.
(A) 12
(B) 14
(C) 16
(D) 10
Answer: (A) 12
Solution:
(a + b)² = a² + b² + 2ab → 64 = 40 + 2ab → 2ab = 24 → ab = 12.

103. The sum of the ages of a father and his son is 45 years. Five years ago, the product of their ages was 124. Find the son’s present age.
(A) 9 years
(B) 8 years
(C) 10 years
(D) 12 years
Answer: (A) 9 years
Solution:
Let father = f, son = s. f + s = 45. Five years ago: (f–5)(s–5)=124. Substitute f = 45–s: (40–s)(s–5)=124 → 40s – 200 – s² + 5s = 124 → –s² + 45s – 324 = 0 → s² – 45s + 324 = 0 → (s–9)(s–36)=0. s=9 or 36, but son is younger so s=9.
Source: NCERT Class 10
