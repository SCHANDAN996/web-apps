26. If x + 1/x = 4, then x² + 1/x² is equal to:
(A) 14
(B) 16
(C) 12
(D) 18
Answer: (A) 14
Solution:
Square both sides: (x + 1/x)² = 4² ⇒ x² + 2 + 1/x² = 16 ⇒ x² + 1/x² = 14.

48. The sum of two numbers is 15 and the sum of their reciprocals is 3/10. Find the numbers.
(A) 5 and 10
(B) 6 and 9
(C) 7 and 8
(D) 4 and 11
Answer: (A) 5 and 10
Solution:
Let numbers be x, y. x+y=15, 1/x+1/y = (x+y)/(xy) = 15/xy = 3/10 → xy = 50. So numbers are roots of t² – 15t + 50 = 0 → (t–5)(t–10)=0. So 5 and 10.

50. A boat goes 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 hours. In 13 hours, it can go 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream. The speed of the boat in still water (in km/h) is:
(A) 8
(B) 10
(C) 6
(D) 12
Answer: (A) 8
Solution:
Let speed in still water = x, stream speed = y. Upstream speed = x–y, downstream = x+y.
First condition: 30/(x–y) + 44/(x+y) = 10.
Second: 40/(x–y) + 55/(x+y) = 13.
Let 1/(x–y) = a, 1/(x+y) = b. Then: 30a + 44b = 10 …(1), 40a + 55b = 13 …(2). Multiply (1) by 4: 120a + 176b = 40; (2) by 3: 120a + 165b = 39. Subtract: 11b = 1 → b=1/11. Substitute in (1): 30a + 44×(1/11) = 10 → 30a + 4 = 10 → 30a = 6 → a = 1/5. So x–y = 5, x+y = 11. Add: 2x = 16 → x = 8 km/h.
Source: SSC CGL (adapted)
