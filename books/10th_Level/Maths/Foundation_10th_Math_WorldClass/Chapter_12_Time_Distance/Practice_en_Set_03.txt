Questions 1–20 (Easy), Questions 21–25 (Medium)

1. A car travels at a constant speed of 60 km/h. What distance does it cover in 3 hours?
a) 120 km b) 150 km c) 180 km d) 200 km
Answer: c) 180 km
Solution: Distance = Speed × Time = 60 × 3 = 180 km.
Source: NCERT Class 7

2. A cyclist covers 144 km in 8 hours. What is his speed in km/h?
a) 16 km/h b) 18 km/h c) 20 km/h d) 24 km/h
Answer: b) 18 km/h
Solution: Speed = Distance / Time = 144/8 = 18 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7

3. Convert 72 km/h into m/s.
a) 15 m/s b) 18 m/s c) 20 m/s d) 24 m/s
Answer: c) 20 m/s
Solution: 72 × (5/18) = 20 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 11 (Units)

4. Convert 10 m/s into km/h.
a) 30 km/h b) 36 km/h c) 42 km/h d) 50 km/h
Answer: b) 36 km/h
Solution: 10 × (18/5) = 36 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 11

5. A boy walks at 5 km/h for 2 hours and then at 4 km/h for 3 hours. Find the total distance covered.
a) 20 km b) 22 km c) 25 km d) 28 km
Answer: b) 22 km
Solution: First part: 5×2 = 10 km; second part: 4×3 = 12 km; total = 10+12 = 22 km.
Source: NCERT Class 7

6. A train 150 m long is running at 54 km/h. How much time will it take to cross a pole?
a) 5 sec b) 8 sec c) 10 sec d) 12 sec
Answer: c) 10 sec
Solution: Speed = 54 × (5/18) = 15 m/s; Time = Distance/Speed = 150/15 = 10 s.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

7. A man runs at 9 km/h. How far does he run in 20 seconds?
a) 40 m b) 50 m c) 60 m d) 80 m
Answer: b) 50 m
Solution: Speed = 9 × (5/18) = 2.5 m/s; Distance = 2.5 × 20 = 50 m.
Source: NCERT Class 7

8. A car covers a certain distance in 5 hours at 40 km/h. How much time will it take to cover the same distance at 50 km/h?
a) 3.5 hours b) 4 hours c) 4.5 hours d) 5 hours
Answer: b) 4 hours
Solution: Distance = 40×5 = 200 km; Time at 50 km/h = 200/50 = 4 hours.
Source: NCERT Class 7

9. Two trains moving in opposite directions have speeds of 60 km/h and 40 km/h. Their relative speed is:
a) 20 km/h b) 50 km/h c) 80 km/h d) 100 km/h
Answer: d) 100 km/h
Solution: Opposite direction relative speed = 60+40 = 100 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 9

10. A train crosses a man standing on the platform in 12 seconds. If the speed of the train is 60 km/h, what is its length?
a) 150 m b) 180 m c) 200 m d) 220 m
Answer: c) 200 m
Solution: Speed = 60×(5/18) = 50/3 m/s; Length = (50/3)×12 = 200 m.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

11. A person walks 360 metres in 4 minutes. His speed in km/h is:
a) 5.4 km/h b) 6.0 km/h c) 6.5 km/h d) 7.2 km/h
Answer: a) 5.4 km/h
Solution: Speed in m/s = 360/(4×60) = 1.5 m/s; in km/h = 1.5×(18/5) = 5.4 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7

12. A bus travels at 45 km/h for 2 hours and then at 60 km/h for 1 hour. What is its average speed for the entire journey?
a) 48 km/h b) 50 km/h c) 52 km/h d) 54 km/h
Answer: b) 50 km/h
Solution: Total distance = 45×2 + 60×1 = 150 km; total time = 3 h; average = 150/3 = 50 km/h.
Source: NCERT Class 7

13. A car moving at 54 km/h crosses a platform in 20 seconds. If the car’s length is 5 m, what is the length of the platform?
a) 245 m b) 295 m c) 300 m d) 305 m
Answer: b) 295 m
Solution: Speed = 54×(5/18)=15 m/s; total distance = 15×20 = 300 m; platform = 300−5 = 295 m.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 2 2019

14. A train 300 m long is running at a speed of 90 km/h. How much time will it take to cross a bridge 200 m long?

15. A runner completes a 400-metre race in 50 seconds. What is his speed in km/h?

17. A car goes from town A to town B at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?
a) 45 km/h b) 48 km/h c) 50 km/h d) 52 km/h
Answer: b) 48 km/h
Solution: Equal distance average = (2×60×40)/(60+40) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h.

18. A bus covers a distance of 240 km in 4 hours. What is its speed in m/s?
a) 12.5 m/s b) 15.2 m/s c) 16.67 m/s d) 18.0 m/s

Solution: Speed = 240/4 = 60 km/h = 60×(5/18) ≈ 16.67 m/s.
Source: NCERT Class 11

19. A man walks at 6 km/h and covers a certain distance in 40 min. How much time would he take if he walks at 8 km/h?

Source: NCERT Class 7

20. A train 250 m long passes a platform 350 m long in 30 seconds. The speed of the train (in km/h) is:
a) 60 km/h b) 72 km/h c) 80 km/h d) 90 km/h

Solution: Total distance = 250+350=600 m; speed = 600/30=20 m/s = 20×(18/5)=72 km/h.
Source: SSC CGL 2016

21. A train crosses a pole in 10 seconds and a 180 m long platform in 22 seconds. Find the length of the train.
a) 120 m b) 150 m c) 180 m d) 200 m
Answer: b) 150 m
Solution: Let length L, speed v. L = v×10; L+180 = v×22. Subtract: 180 = v×12 => v=15 m/s; L=15×10=150 m.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

22. A car travels the first half of a journey at 40 km/h and the second half at 60 km/h. The average speed for the whole journey is:
a) 45 km/h b) 48 km/h c) 50 km/h d) 52 km/h
Answer: b) 48 km/h
Solution: Equal distances: average = (2×40×60)/(40+60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

Answer: c) 5 hours
Solution: Relative speed = 50+70=120 km/h; time to meet = 600/120 = 5 hours.
