101. A car travels the first 50 km at 25 km/h and the next 50 km at 75 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole journey?
A) 35 km/h
B) 37.5 km/h
C) 40 km/h
D) 45 km/h
Correct Answer: B) 37.5 km/h
Solution:
Time first part = 50/25 = 2 h.
Time second part = 50/75 = 2/3 h.
Total distance = 100 km, total time = 2 + 2/3 = 8/3 h.
Average speed = 100 / (8/3) = 100 × 3/8 = 37.5 km/h.
Source: NCERT Exemplar (Class 7)
