Q27. How many 4-digit numbers can be formed using the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 without repetition such that the number is divisible by 4?
(1) 24 (2) 36 (3) 48 (4) 60

Answer: (1) 24
Solution: A number is divisible by 4 if its last two digits form a number divisible by 4. From {1,2,3,4,5}, the two-digit endings divisible by 4 are 12, 24, 32, 52 → 4 choices. For each ending, the first two digits are arranged from the remaining 3 digits in 3P2 = 6 ways. Total = 4 × 6 = 24.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

Q30. In how many ways can 5 persons be seated in a row if two particular persons never sit together?
(1) 72 (2) 96 (3) 120 (4) 48

Solution: Total arrangements = 5! = 120. Treat the two as a block: 4! × 2! = 48 arrangements where they are together. So arrangements with them not together = 120 – 48 = 72.

Q31. A bag contains 5 red and 4 blue balls. In how many ways can 3 balls be drawn such that at least one ball is red?

Solution: Total ways to draw 3 balls from 9 = 9C3 = 84. Ways with no red ball (all blue) = 4C3 = 4. Therefore, at least one red = 84 – 4 = 80.

Q32. How many 4-digit numbers divisible by 5 can be formed using the digits 0,1,2,3,4,5 without repetition?
(1) 108 (2) 120 (3) 96 (4) 84

Answer: (1) 108
Solution: A number divisible by 5 ends in 0 or 5.
Case 1: last digit 0 → thousands place: 5 choices (1-5), hundreds: 4, tens: 3 → 5×4×3 = 60.
Case 2: last digit 5 → thousands place: 4 choices (1,2,3,4, cannot be 0 or 5), hundreds: 4 (0 allowed), tens: 3 → 4×4×3 = 48.
Total = 60 + 48 = 108.
Source: SSC CGL 2018 (adapted)

Q43. A polygon has 44 diagonals. The number of sides is:
