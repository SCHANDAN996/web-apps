Solution: Perimeter ratio = side ratio (since P = 4s). Sides 3:5. Smaller side 6 cm → 3 parts = 6 → 1 part = 2 → larger side = 5×2 = 10 cm.

Q71. Two numbers are in the ratio 5:8. If 9 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 2:3. The larger number is:
A) 45
B) 54
C) 63
D) 72

Answer: D) 72
Solution: Numbers: 5x, 8x. (5x-9)/(8x-9) = 2/3 → 15x-27 = 16x-18 → x = 9. Larger = 8×9 = 72.

Q73. A and B have money in the ratio 4:3. A gives ₹10 to B, and the ratio becomes 5:4. How much did A have initially?
A) ₹240
B) ₹300
C) ₹360
D) ₹400

Answer: C) ₹360
Solution: Initial: A = 4x, B = 3x. After: (4x-10)/(3x+10) = 5/4 → 16x-40 = 15x+50 → x = 90. A = 4×90 = 360.

Q74. The ratio of the circumferences of two circles is 2:5. If the diameter of the smaller circle is 14 cm, the diameter of the larger circle is:

Solution: Circumference ∝ diameter → diameter ratio = 2:5. 2 parts = 14 → 1 part = 7 → larger diameter = 5×7 = 35 cm.

Q75. The number of sweets with X and Y is in the ratio 9:7. X gives 8 sweets to Y, and the ratio becomes 7:9. How many sweets did Y have initially?
A) 28
B) 32

Answer: A) 28
Solution: Initial: X = 9k, Y = 7k. After: (9k-8)/(7k+8) = 7/9 → 81k-72 = 49k+56 → 32k = 128 → k = 4. Y = 7×4 = 28.
