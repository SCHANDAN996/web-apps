101. Simplify: 24 ÷ 8 × 3 + 5 – 2
(a) 12 (b) 10 (c) 8 (d) 14
Answer: (a) 12
Solution:
Left to right: 24 ÷ 8 = 3, 3 × 3 = 9, then 9 + 5 – 2 = 12.
Source: NCERT Class 7

102. Simplify: (18 – 4 of 3) × 2 + 2
(a) 14 (b) 22 (c) 10 (d) 20
Answer: (a) 14
Solution:
"Of" first: 4 of 3 = 12. Inside bracket: 18 – 12 = 6. Then 6 × 2 = 12, 12 + 2 = 14.
Source: SSC CHSL Pattern

103. Simplify: 3.5 + 1/4 of 16 – 0.5
(a) 7 (b) 8 (c) 6.5 (d) 7.5
Answer: (a) 7
Solution:
1/4 of 16 = 4. Then 3.5 + 4 = 7.5, 7.5 – 0.5 = 7.
Source: NCERT Class 7

104. Simplify: [45 – {15 + (18 ÷ 3)}] ÷ 3
(a) 8 (b) 10 (c) 6 (d) 12
Answer: (a) 8
Solution:
Innermost: 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Then 15 + 6 = 21. Next 45 – 21 = 24. Finally 24 ÷ 3 = 8.
Source: Railway (RRB NTPC) Pattern

111. Simplify: (5/6 of 24) ÷ (1/3 of 18) + 2

117. Simplify: (4/7 of 35) ÷ (2/5 of 15) + 1/2

Answer: (a) 3 5/6
Solution:

Source: IBPS Clerk Pattern

118. Simplify: (0.027)¹⁄³ + (0.008)¹⁄³ × (0.25)¹⁄²

Answer: (a) 0.4
Solution:
∛0.027 = 0.3, ∛0.008 = 0.2, √0.25 = 0.5. Then 0.3 + 0.2 × 0.5 = 0.3 + 0.1 = 0.4.

119. Simplify: 16 2/3% of 240 + 33 1/3% of 180

120. Simplify: [12 + {3 × (4 + 2) ÷ 3} – 4] ÷ 2
(a) 7 (b) 6 (c) 5 (d) 8
Answer: (a) 7
Solution:
Innermost: 4 + 2 = 6, 3 × 6 = 18, 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Then 12 + 6 – 4 = 14. Finally 14 ÷ 2 = 7.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

Hard Level (Q121–Q125)

121. Simplify: 3 3/4 ÷ 2 1/2 of 1 1/5 + (1/4 – 1/6) × 12
(a) 9/4 (b) 7/3 (c) 11/4 (d) 5/2
Answer: (a) 9/4
Solution:
Mixed to improper: 15/4, 5/2, 6/5. "Of" first: 5/2 of 6/5 = 3. 15/4 ÷ 3 = 15/4 × 1/3 = 5/4.
(1/4 – 1/6) = 1/12. 1/12 × 12 = 1. Sum = 5/4 + 1 = 9/4.
Source: SSC CGL Tier-II Pattern

122. Simplify: [ { (3 1/2 – 2 1/3) ÷ 1 1/6 } of 2 1/4 ] + 1/2
(a) 11/4 (b) 3 (c) 7/2 (d) 9/4
Answer: (a) 11/4
Solution:
7/2 – 7/3 = (21 – 14)/6 = 7/6. Divide by 7/6 → 7/6 ÷ 7/6 = 1. 1 of 9/4 = 9/4. 9/4 + 1/2 = 11/4.
Source: IBPS PO Pattern

123. Simplify: (0.83̅ + 0.16̅) ÷ 0.3 + 0.25 × 4
(a) 13/3 (b) 4 (c) 14/3 (d) 5
Answer: (a) 13/3
Solution:
0.83̅ = 5/6, 0.16̅ = 1/6. Sum = 1. 1 ÷ 0.3 = 10/3. 0.25 × 4 = 1. Total = 10/3 + 1 = 13/3.
Source: SSC CGL Pattern

124. Simplify: 1 + 1/(1 + 1/(1 + 1/2))
(a) 8/5 (b) 5/3 (c) 7/5 (d) 4/3
Answer: (a) 8/5
Solution:
Innermost: 1 + 1/2 = 3/2. 1/(3/2) = 2/3. 1 + 2/3 = 5/3. 1/(5/3) = 3/5. Finally 1 + 3/5 = 8/5.
Source: Banking/Railway (Contd Fraction)

125. Simplify: (5/6 of 3/7 + 2/9 ÷ 1/3) × (1.25 × 0.8)
(a) 43/42 (b) 41/42 (c) 1 (d) 2
Answer: (a) 43/42
Solution:
5/6 × 3/7 = 15/42 = 5/14. 2/9 ÷ 1/3 = 2/9 × 3 = 6/9 = 2/3.
Sum = 5/14 + 2/3 = 15/42 + 28/42 = 43/42.
1.25 × 0.8 = 1. Result = 43/42.
Source: SSC CHSL Tier-II Pattern
