In a triangle, the sum of all interior angles is:
(A) 90° (B) 180° (C) 270° (D) 360°
Answer: (B) 180°
Solution: By the Angle Sum Property of a triangle, the three interior angles always add up to 180°. This property is true for every triangle.
Source: NCERT Class 7 (Triangle and its Properties)

A right-angled triangle has legs of 3 cm and 4 cm. Its hypotenuse is:
(A) 4 cm (B) 5 cm (C) 6 cm (D) 7 cm
Answer: (B) 5 cm
Solution: Using Pythagoras theorem, hypotenuse = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 10 (Pythagoras Theorem)

Which triangle has all three sides equal?
(A) Isosceles (B) Scalene (C) Equilateral (D) Right-angled
Answer: (C) Equilateral
Solution: An equilateral triangle has all sides equal and all angles equal to 60°. The name itself comes from “equal” (equi) and “side” (lateral).
Source: NCERT Class 6 (Basic Geometrical Ideas)

When two lines intersect, vertically opposite angles are:
(A) Complementary (B) Supplementary (C) Equal (D) Always 90°
Answer: (C) Equal
Solution: Vertically opposite angles have equal measures. This is a fundamental property of intersecting lines.
Source: NCERT Class 9 (Lines and Angles)

A line that intersects two or more lines at distinct points is called a:
(A) Parallel line (B) Perpendicular line (C) Transversal (D) Ray
Answer: (C) Transversal
Solution: A transversal is defined as a line that cuts across two or more lines (usually parallel) at different points, forming various pairs of angles.
Source: NCERT Class 9 (Lines and Angles)

In a circle, the distance from the centre to any point on the circle is called its:
(A) Chord (B) Diameter (C) Radius (D) Arc
Answer: (C) Radius
Solution: The radius is the line segment joining the centre and any point on the circumference. It is half the diameter.
Source: NCERT Class 6 (Circle)

A quadrilateral with all sides equal and each interior angle 90° is a:
(A) Rhombus (B) Rectangle (C) Square (D) Parallelogram
Answer: (C) Square
Solution: A square satisfies both conditions: all sides equal and all angles right angles. A rhombus has equal sides but not necessarily 90° angles; a rectangle has 90° angles but not necessarily equal sides.
Source: NCERT Class 8 (Understanding Quadrilaterals)

The area of a rectangle with length 10 cm and breadth 5 cm is:
(A) 50 cm² (B) 30 cm² (C) 15 cm² (D) 25 cm²
Answer: (A) 50 cm²
Solution: Area = length × breadth = 10 × 5 = 50 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 6 (Mensuration)

Which of the following is a Pythagorean triple?
(A) 2, 3, 4 (B) 3, 4, 5 (C) 4, 5, 6 (D) 5, 6, 7
Answer: (B) 3, 4, 5
Solution: 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5², so it forms a right-angled triangle. The other sets do not satisfy a² + b² = c².
Source: NCERT Class 10 (Pythagorean Triples)

A triangle with one angle equal to 90° is called a/an:
(A) Acute-angled triangle (B) Obtuse-angled triangle (C) Right-angled triangle (D) Equilateral triangle
Answer: (C) Right-angled triangle
Solution: By definition, a triangle containing a right angle (90°) is a right-angled triangle. The side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse.
Source: NCERT Class 7 (Triangles)

The sum of exterior angles of any convex polygon, taken one at each vertex (in order), is:
(A) 180° (B) 360° (C) 540° (D) depends on number of sides
Answer: (B) 360°
Solution: Regardless of the number of sides, the sum of exterior angles (one per vertex) of any convex polygon is always 360°.
Source: NCERT Class 8 (Understanding Quadrilaterals)

In a parallelogram, opposite angles are always:
(A) Supplementary (B) Complementary (C) Equal (D) 90°
Answer: (C) Equal
Solution: A parallelogram has opposite sides parallel and equal; its opposite angles are also equal. Adjacent angles are supplementary.
Source: NCERT Class 9 (Quadrilaterals)

A quadrilateral has how many diagonals?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4
Answer: (B) 2
Solution: A quadrilateral has 4 vertices. Number of diagonals = n(n-3)/2 = 4×1/2 = 2. You can also draw them directly.
Source: NCERT Class 6 (Basic Geometrical Ideas)

In an equilateral triangle, each interior angle measures:
(A) 45° (B) 60° (C) 90° (D) 120°
Answer: (B) 60°
Solution: All three angles are equal, and their sum is 180°, so each angle = 180°/3 = 60°.
Source: NCERT Class 7 (Triangles)

A transversal cuts two parallel lines. The sum of interior angles on the same side of the transversal is:
(A) 90° (B) 180° (C) 270° (D) 360°
Answer: (B) 180°
Solution: Interior angles on the same side of the transversal are supplementary (sum to 180°). This is known as the co-interior angle property.
Source: NCERT Class 9 (Lines and Angles)

The perimeter of a square whose side is 8 cm is:
(A) 16 cm (B) 24 cm (C) 32 cm (D) 64 cm
Answer: (C) 32 cm
Solution: Perimeter of a square = 4 × side = 4 × 8 = 32 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6 (Mensuration)

The area of a circle with radius 7 cm (use π = 22/7) is:
(A) 44 cm² (B) 154 cm² (C) 308 cm² (D) 616 cm²
Answer: (B) 154 cm²
Solution: Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 22 × 7 = 154 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 7 (Perimeter and Area)

The volume of a cube with side ‘a’ is given by:
(A) a² (B) a³ (C) 6a² (D) 4a³
Answer: (B) a³
Solution: Volume of cube = side × side × side = a³.
Source: NCERT Class 8 (Mensuration)

Which of the following is NOT a classification of triangles based on angles?
(A) Acute-angled (B) Right-angled (C) Obtuse-angled (D) Scalene
Answer: (D) Scalene
Solution: Scalene is a classification based on sides (no equal sides). The other three are based on the nature of angles.
Source: NCERT Class 7 (Triangles)

A straight angle measures exactly:
(A) 90° (B) 180° (C) 270° (D) 360°
Answer: (B) 180°
Solution: A straight angle forms a straight line and measures 180°.
Source: NCERT Class 6 (Understanding Elementary Shapes)

The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. The smallest angle is:
(A) 20° (B) 30° (C) 40° (D) 50°
Answer: (C) 40°
Solution: Let the angles be 2x, 3x, 4x. Their sum = 9x = 180° ⇒ x = 20°. Smallest angle = 2x = 40°.
Source: NCERT Class 9 (Lines and Angles – application) / SSC CGL 2021

A chord of a circle of radius 10 cm is at a distance of 6 cm from the centre. The length of the chord is:
(A) 8 cm (B) 12 cm (C) 16 cm (D) 18 cm
Answer: (C) 16 cm
Solution: The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects it. Let half chord = √(r² – d²) = √(10² – 6²) = √64 = 8 cm. Full chord = 2 × 8 = 16 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 9 (Circles)

The perimeter of a rectangle is 30 cm. If its length is 8 cm, then its area is:
(A) 48 cm² (B) 56 cm² (C) 60 cm² (D) 64 cm²
Answer: (B) 56 cm²
Solution: Perimeter = 2(l + b) = 30 ⇒ l + b = 15 ⇒ 8 + b = 15 ⇒ b = 7 cm. Area = l × b = 8 × 7 = 56 cm².
Source: SSC CHSL 2020

The diagonal of a square of side 10 cm is:
(A) 10√2 cm (B) 20 cm (C) 10√3 cm (D) 15 cm
Answer: (A) 10√2 cm
Solution: Diagonal of a square = side × √2 = 10 × √2 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8 (Square and Square Roots) / SSC CPO 2019

A ladder 13 m long leans against a vertical wall. The foot of the ladder is 5 m away from the wall. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?
(A) 11 m (B) 12 m (C) 13 m (D) 14 m
Answer: (B) 12 m
Solution: By Pythagoras theorem, height = √(13² – 5²) = √(169 – 25) = √144 = 12 m.
Source: NCERT Class 10 (Application of Pythagoras theorem) / SSC CGL 2022
