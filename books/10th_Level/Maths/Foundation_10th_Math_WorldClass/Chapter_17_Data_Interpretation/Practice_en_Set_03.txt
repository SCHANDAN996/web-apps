Q51.
The table below gives the monthly income (₹) and expenditure (₹) of five families.

Family	Income	Expenditure
A	50,000	30,000
B	60,000	40,000
C	45,000	35,000
D	70,000	50,000
E	55,000	35,000

Which family has the highest savings percentage? (Savings = Income – Expenditure)
(a) A
(b) B
(c) D
(d) E

Correct Answer: (a) A
Solution:
Savings: A = 20,000 → % = (20000/50000)×100 = 40%
B: 20,000/60,000 = 33.33%
C: 10,000/45,000 = 22.22%
D: 20,000/70,000 = 28.57%
E: 20,000/55,000 = 36.36%
Highest is A with 40%.
Source: NCERT Class 10

Q53.

Q67.
Data sufficiency:
What is the total number of books sold by a shopkeeper?
Statement I: The number of fiction books sold is 40% of the total.
Statement II: The number of non-fiction books sold is 120.
(a) Statement I alone is sufficient
(b) Statement II alone is sufficient
(c) Both together are sufficient
(d) Neither is sufficient

Correct Answer: (c) Both together are sufficient
Solution:
From I: fiction = 40%, non-fiction = 60%.
From II: non-fiction = 120.
Combining: 60% of total = 120 → total = 120/0.60 = 200. So both together are needed.
Source: UPSC CSAT

Q74.
Data sufficiency:
What is the ratio of boys to girls in a class?
Statement I: There are 20 boys in the class.
Statement II: The number of girls is 60% of the number of boys.
(a) I alone sufficient
(b) II alone sufficient
(c) Both together sufficient
(d) Neither sufficient

Correct Answer: (b) II alone sufficient
Solution:
From II: girls = 0.6 × boys, so ratio boys:girls = 1:0.6 = 5:3. I gives absolute number but not ratio alone. So II alone is sufficient to find ratio.
Source: UPSC CSAT
