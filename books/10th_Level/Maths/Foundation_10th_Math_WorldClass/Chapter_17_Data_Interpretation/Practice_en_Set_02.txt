Q37.
A company's revenue (in lakh ₹) for five years is shown: 2016:40, 2017:48, 2018:54, 2019:60, 2020:72.
What is the compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of revenue from 2016 to 2020? (Approximate)
(a) 12%
(b) 14%
(c) 16%
(d) 18%

Correct Answer: (c) 16%
Solution:
CAGR = [(72/40)^(1/4) - 1] × 100.
72/40 = 1.8. 1.8^(1/4). √1.8 ≈ 1.3416, then √1.3416 ≈ 1.1583. So 1.1583 - 1 = 0.1583 => 15.83% ≈ 16%. So (c).
Source: IBPS PO

Q42.
Data Sufficiency:
What is the total number of students in a class?
Statement I: 40% of the students are girls, and the number of boys is 30.
Statement II: The ratio of boys to girls is 3:2.
(a) I alone sufficient, II not
(b) II alone sufficient, I not
(c) Both together sufficient
(d) Neither sufficient

Solution:
From I: if 40% girls, then 60% boys. Given boys=30, total = 30/0.60 = 50. So I alone sufficient.
II gives ratio but no absolute number, not sufficient. So (a).

Q50.
Data Sufficiency:
What is the percentage of marks obtained by a student?
Statement I: The student scored 80 marks in Mathematics and 70 in Science.
Statement II: The maximum marks in each subject is 100 and there are 5 subjects.
(a) I alone sufficient
(b) II alone sufficient
(c) Both together sufficient
(d) Neither sufficient

Correct Answer: (d) Neither sufficient
Solution:
We need total marks obtained and total maximum marks to find percentage. Statement I gives marks in two subjects only, not all. II gives number of subjects and max each, but no actual marks. So even together we don't know marks in other three subjects, thus percentage cannot be determined. So (d).
