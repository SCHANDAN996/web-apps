🍕 Pizza Is PIE, Eat In
P(Event) = Pizza = Probability = Interested (Favorable) ÷ Every (Total outcome)
Imagine a pizza where only your favorite toppings (Interested) count out of all slices (Every). "Eat In" reminds you that the formula lives Inside the Sample Space. It’s deliciously unforgettable.

🦀 The Complement Crab
P(not E) = 1 – P(E)
Visualize a crab with one giant claw. The crab represents the whole probability universe (1). The smaller claw is the event E. Whatever is left over is the complement. Crabby says: “I pinch away E, what remains is not E!”

🙅 “ME? Just ADD!” (Mutually Exclusive)
If events can’t occur together, they’re Mutually Exclusive. Then P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).
Picture two sumo wrestlers who cannot occupy the same ring at once. To find the chance of either showing up, just ADD their individual chances. No overlapping belly fat.

🎩 Indy-Multiply (Independent Events)
For independent events: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)
Imagine Indiana Jones whipping two separate events into a multiplication sign. He shouts, “Independent? Multiply, don’t add!” The whip cracks the ‘×’ symbol.

🔦 The “One Minus None” Flashlight
P(at least one success) = 1 – P(all failures)
You’re in a dark room. The monster “None” (total failure) is the only thing that scares you. Shine the flashlight on None’s probability, then subtract from 1. Everything else is “at least one” – safe!

🤯 Two’s a Party, n’s the Power (Coins)
Number of outcomes for n coins = 2ⁿ
Picture a coin doubling party: With 1 coin, 2 people show up; with 2 coins, 4 people crash in; with n coins, 2ⁿ party animals. The host screams “2 to the n!” as the room overflows.

🎲 Dice Duo – 36 Ways to WOW
Two dice produce 6 × 6 = 36 equally likely outcomes.
Imagine two dice in love. The first die says, “I have 6 moods.” The second replies, “So do I!” Together they create 36 romantic possibilities. Sum 7 is their favorite child – born in 6 ways (1+6,2+5,3+4,4+3,5+2,6+1).

🃏 The 52 Pickup Gang
Standard deck: 52 cards = 4 suits of 13.
Face cards: 12 (Jack, Queen, King × 4). Aces: 4.
Visualize a gang of 52 thieves. The faces (12) wear masks; the aces (4) are the bosses. When you draw, calculate: favorable cards / 52. “Black queen” means two specific gang members (♣Q, ♠Q), so 2/52.

🚪 “Given is the Gate” (Conditional Probability)
P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)
Imagine entering a garden through a gate named B. Inside, you count those who also belong to A. So the probability is (People in both) ÷ (People in B). The “given” event restricts your universe.

📦 Sample Space – Your Universe Box
Always write Sample Space S first: all possible outcomes.
Think of a box containing every possible toy. If you don’t list the toys, you’ll grab the wrong one. S is your map; without it you’re blind. Respect the box.

🌡️ Zero the Hero, One the Sun
Probability = 0 means impossible (ice cream on the sun).
Probability = 1 means certain (sun rising tomorrow).
Zero is a frozen hero who never arrives; One is the blazing sun that always shows up. Every event lives between these two extremes.

🧠 Coin Memory: A Goldfish, Not an Elephant
A fair coin has no memory. Past tosses don’t change future odds.
The coin is a goldfish; after every toss, it forgets. Don’t be the gambler who thinks “tails is due.” The coin’s brain resets instantly.

🔄 Replacement – The Reset Button
With replacement: events are independent, probability stays same.
Without replacement: the pool shrinks, odds change.
Picture a bag of marbles. If you put it back (reset button), everything’s fresh. If not, the bag remembers and odds get quirky.

🔁 The “One Minus None” Jingle
When you see “at least one,” sing this jingle:
🎵 “Find the chance of NONE happening,
Take ONE minus NONE, and start dancing!”
It’s the catchiest remedy against counting nightmares. Use it and dance past the traps.
