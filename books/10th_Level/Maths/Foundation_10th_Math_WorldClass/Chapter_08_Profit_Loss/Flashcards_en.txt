Flashcard 3
Q: Two identical pens sell for $120 each. One yields 20% profit, the other 20% loss. Has the seller gained, lost, or broken even?
A: Overall loss. Loss % = (20²/100)% = 4%. The loss item’s CP is higher, so the rupees lost exceed the rupees gained.

Flashcard 4
Q: What’s the “Piggy bank” rule for remembering where profit % is calculated?
A: “Percent always on the Piggy bank (CP), not on the Show‑off (SP).” Piggy bank = your original savings (CP). Show‑off price = SP.

Flashcard 6
Q: A dishonest dealer claims to sell at cost price but uses a weight of 900 gm instead of 1 kg. What is his profit %?
A: ≈11.11%. Profit % = (Error / True weight − Error)×100 = (100 / 900)×100 = 11 1/9%.

Flashcard 9
Q: True or False: If an article is sold at two different selling prices and the profit in one case equals the loss in the other, the cost price is the average of the two selling prices.
A: True. CP = (SP1 + SP2)/2, because SP1 = CP + x, SP2 = CP – x.

Flashcard 10
Q: Recite the rhyme that helps remember the overall loss when two items are sold at the same price with equal profit and loss %.
A: “Profit and loss same percent, money spent won’t be what you get. Square the rate, divide by a hundred, that’s the loss that always blunders.”

Flashcard 13
Q: If the cost price of 15 articles equals the selling price of 10 articles, what is the profit %?
A: 50%. 15 × CP = 10 × SP → SP/CP = 15/10 = 1.5 → 50% profit.

Flashcard 14
Q: A shopkeeper gives a discount of 20% on marked price and still makes a profit of 12%. What is the ratio of CP to MP?
A: CP : MP = 5 : 7. SP = 0.8 MP = 1.12 CP → CP/MP = 0.8/1.12 = 5/7.

Flashcard 15
Q: After a 10% discount, a shopkeeper gains 17%. By what % is the marked price above the cost price?
A: 30%. 0.9 MP = 1.17 CP → MP/CP = 1.17/0.9 = 1.3 → MP = 130% of CP.

Flashcard 16
Q: What’s the “MP discount CP” rule? (Hint: a balloon and a piggy bank)
A: “Discount De‑tags the MP, but Profit measures on CP.” MP is like a balloon pricked by discount to become SP; profit is still measured on CP (the piggy bank).

Flashcard 17
Q: A trader sells one item at a 15% loss. To gain 15% overall on two items with equal cost prices, what should be the profit % on the second item?
A: 45%. Let each CP = 100, total CP = 200, desired SP = 230. First SP = 85, second SP = 145 → profit = 45 → 45%.
