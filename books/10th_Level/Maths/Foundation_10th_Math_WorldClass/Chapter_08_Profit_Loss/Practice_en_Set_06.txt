127. A shopkeeper marks his goods 50% above the cost price. He allows a discount of 10% on half of the stock and a discount of 20% on the remaining half. What is his overall profit percentage?
(A) 25%
(B) 27.5%
(C) 30%
(D) 32.5%

✅ Answer: (B) 27.5%
Solution: Let CP per unit = 100, MP = 150. For 2 units: SP₁ = 150×0.9 = 135; SP₂ = 150×0.8 = 120. Total SP = 255, total CP = 200. Profit% = (55/200)×100 = 27.5%.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 2 2019

128. A dishonest milkman mixes water with pure milk in the ratio 1:4 (water:milk). He sells the mixture at the price of pure milk, but uses a false measure that shows 1 litre while actually giving only 900 ml. What is his overall profit percentage?
(A) 38.89%
(B) 40%
(C) 42.5%
(D) 45%

✅ Answer: (A) 38.89%
Solution: In 900 ml mixture, milk = (4/5)×900 = 720 ml. CP = cost of 720 ml pure milk. SP = price of 1000 ml pure milk. Profit% = (280/720)×100 = 38.89%.
Source: IBPS PO Mains 2018

135. A trader marks his goods 80% above cost price. He sells three-fifths of the stock at the marked price, one-fifth at a discount of 25% on marked price, and the remaining at a discount of 50% on marked price. What is his overall profit percentage?
(A) 50%
(B) 55%
(C) 53%
(D) 60%

✅ Answer: (C) 53%
Solution: CP per unit = 100, MP = 180. For 5 units: SP = 3×180 + 1×(180×0.75) + 1×(180×0.5) = 540 + 135 + 90 = 765. Total CP = 500. Profit% = (265/500)×100 = 53%.

(A) 0%
(B) 5%

142. A shopkeeper allows a discount of 20% on the marked price and earns a profit of 16%. If he allows a discount of 25% on the same marked price, what will be his profit percent (approx)?
(A) 8.75%
(B) 10%
(C) 5%
(D) 12.5%

✅ Answer: (A) 8.75%
Solution: MP = 100. SP₁ = 80, CP = 80/1.16 ≈ 68.9655. SP₂ = 75. Profit% = (75 − 68.9655)/68.9655 × 100 ≈ 8.75%.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 2 2020

(A) 37.5%
(B) 30%
(C) 32%
(D) 40%

147. A person buys goods for $5000. He sells one-fourth of them at a loss of 10%. At what profit percentage must he sell the remaining goods so that he makes an overall profit of 20%?
(A) 30%
(B) 33.33%
(C) 25%
(D) 28%

✅ Answer: (A) 30%
Solution: Total desired SP = 5000×1.2 = 6000. SP from 1/4 = (1250)×0.9 = 1125. Required SP from rest = 6000−1125 = 4875. CP of rest = 3750. Profit% = (1125/3750)×100 = 30%.
Source: SBI Clerk Mains 2020

148. The selling price of an article is increased by 20%. By what percent should the cost price be increased so that the profit percentage remains unchanged?
(A) 20%
(B) 16.67%
(C) 25%
(D) Depends on original profit%

✅ Answer: (A) 20%
Solution: Let CP = x, profit% = p, SP = x(1 + p/100). New SP = 1.2·x(1 + p/100). New CP must be SP/(1 + p/100) = 1.2·x, so increase = 20%.
Source: SSC CGL 2018
