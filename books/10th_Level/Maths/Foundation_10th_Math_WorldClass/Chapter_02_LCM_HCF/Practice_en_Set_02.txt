26. The LCM of two numbers is 182 and their HCF is 13. If one number is 26, find the other.
(a) 91 (b) 78 (c) 104 (d) 65

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 91<br> <b>Solution:</b> Other number = (LCM × HCF) ÷ given number = (182 × 13) ÷ 26 = 2366 ÷ 26 = 91.<br> <b>Source:</b> SSC MTS Pattern </details>

28. The greatest number which divides 70 and 125, leaving remainders 5 and 8 respectively, is:
(a) 13 (b) 65 (c) 15 (d) 17

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 13<br> <b>Solution:</b> Required number = HCF of (70−5)=65 and (125−8)=117. 65=5×13, 117=3²×13, HCF=13. <b>Source:</b> NCERT Class 6 / Banking Exam </details>

29. The LCM of two numbers is 12 times their HCF. The sum of LCM and HCF is 403. If one number is 93, find the other.
(a) 124 (b) 186 (c) 155 (d) 217

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 124<br> <b>Solution:</b> Let HCF=h, then LCM=12h. 12h+h=403⇒13h=403⇒h=31, LCM=372. Other = (372×31)÷93 =11532÷93=124. <b>Source:</b> SSC CGL Memory Based </details>

30. Find the HCF of 2/3, 4/5, and 6/7.
(a) 2/105 (b) 1/105 (c) 2/35 (d) 1/35

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 2/105<br> <b>Solution:</b> HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators. Numerators: 2,4,6 → HCF=2. Denominators: 3,5,7 → LCM=105. So 2/105. <b>Source:</b> SSC CHSL Pattern </details>

31. What is the smallest number which when divided by 15, 20, and 25 leaves remainder 8 in each case?
(a) 300 (b) 308 (c) 292 (d) 608

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (b) 308<br> <b>Solution:</b> LCM of 15,20,25 = 300. Required number = 300 + 8 = 308. (If remainder is same and less than divisor, it's LCM + remainder). <b>Source:</b> NCERT Class 6 / Railway Pattern </details>

32. Two numbers have their LCM = 231 and HCF = 11. If the first number is 33, what is the second?
(a) 77 (b) 55 (c) 99 (d) 88

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 77<br> <b>Solution:</b> Second = (LCM×HCF)/first = (231×11)/33 = 2541/33 = 77. <b>Source:</b> Banking Clerk Pattern </details>

33. The least number which is a perfect square and exactly divisible by each of 8, 12, 15, and 20 is:
(a) 3600 (b) 14400 (c) 2400 (d) 4800

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 3600<br> <b>Solution:</b> LCM of 8,12,15,20: 8=2³,12=2²×3,15=3×5,20=2²×5. LCM=2³×3×5=120. Now to make it a perfect square, multiply by 2×3×5=30: 120×30=3600, which is 60². <b>Source:</b> SSC CGL Tier-II Pattern </details>

34. Four bells toll at intervals of 8, 12, 16, and 28 seconds respectively. They all toll together at 1:00 AM. When will they toll together again?
(a) 1:05:36 AM (b) 1:06:12 AM (c) 1:04:48 AM (d) 1:05:12 AM

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 1:05:36 AM<br> <b>Solution:</b> LCM of 8,12,16,28: 16=2⁴, 28=2²×7, others. LCM=2⁴×3×7=336 sec = 5 min 36 sec. Next time: 1:05:36 AM. <b>Source:</b> RRB Group D Pattern </details>

35. The HCF of two numbers is 11 and their LCM is 7700. If one number is 275, the other is:
(a) 308 (b) 280 (c) 292 (d) 260

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 308<br> <b>Solution:</b> Other = (7700×11)/275 = 84700/275 = 308. <b>Source:</b> IBPS PO Pattern </details>

36. Find the least number which when divided by 12, 16, 24, and 36 leaves a remainder 7 in each case.

37. The ratio of two numbers is 4∶5 and their LCM is 140. Find the numbers.

38. What is the HCF of 0.63, 1.05, and 2.1?

43. A rectangular field of length 120 m and width 75 m is to be paved with square tiles. The minimum number of tiles required is:

48. The LCM of two numbers is 4 times their HCF. The product of the numbers is 1024. Find their HCF.
(a) 16 (b) 8 (c) 32 (d) 4

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 16<br> <b>Solution:</b> Let HCF = h, LCM = 4h. Product = h × 4h = 4h² = 1024 ⇒ h² = 256 ⇒ h = 16. <b>Source:</b> Bank Clerk Pattern </details>

49. A, B, and C start running on a circular track of length 3600 m at speeds of 12 m/s, 18 m/s, and 20 m/s respectively. After what time will they meet for the first time at the starting point again?
(a) 30 min (b) 20 min (c) 25 min (d) 40 min

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 30 min<br> <b>Solution:</b> Time to complete one lap: A=3600/12=300 sec, B=3600/18=200 sec, C=3600/20=180 sec. They meet at start at LCM of 300,200,180. 300=2²×3×5², 200=2³×5², 180=2²×3²×5. LCM=2³×3²×5²=8×9×25=1800 sec = 30 min. <b>Source:</b> SSC CGL / RRB </details>

50. Find the largest number that will divide 148, 246, and 623 leaving remainders 4, 6, and 11 respectively.
(a) 12 (b) 16 (c) 18 (d) 24

<details> <summary><strong>Answer & Solution</strong></summary> <b>Answer:</b> (a) 12<br> <b>Solution:</b> Subtract remainders: 148−4=144, 246−6=240, 623−11=612. HCF of 144,240,612. 144=2⁴×3², 240=2⁴×3×5, 612=2²×3²×17. Common: 2²×3=12. <b>Source:</b> NCERT Class 6 / SSC </details>
