(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 0.5 (D) 4

(A) 30 s (B) 48 s (C) 60 s (D) 120 s

92. The largest number that divides 124, 187, and 250, leaving remainders 4, 7, and 10 respectively, is:
(A) 30 (B) 40 (C) 50 (D) 60

93. The LCM of two numbers is 15 times their HCF. If the sum of the LCM and HCF is 256, then the product of the numbers is:
(A) 2048 (B) 2560 (C) 3840 (D) 4096

94. The smallest number which, when increased by 5, is exactly divisible by 8, 12, and 18 is:
(A) 67 (B) 72 (C) 77 (D) 80

95. Two co‑prime numbers have a product of 253. Their LCM is:
(A) 23 (B) 253 (C) 11 (D) 276

96. Three traffic lights change every 48 s, 72 s, and 108 s. They change together at 8:00:00 AM. When will they next change together?
(A) 8:06:48 AM (B) 8:07:12 AM (C) 8:08:24 AM (D) 8:09:00 AM

97. The LCM of 0.12, 0.18, and 0.24 is:
(A) 0.24 (B) 0.36 (C) 0.48 (D) 0.72

98. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 6. If their HCF is 8, their LCM is:
(A) 120 (B) 180 (C) 240 (D) 360

99. The greatest 4‑digit number exactly divisible by 15, 25, and 40 is:
(A) 9600 (B) 9800 (C) 9900 (D) 9950

(A) 6 (B) 7 (C) 14 (D) 21
