MCQ Set 3 (Medium) – Questions 51 to 75

51. The LCM of two numbers is 252 and their HCF is 6. If one number is 42, find the other.
(A) 36 (B) 48 (C) 28 (D) 30

52. Three numbers are in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5 and their LCM is 2400. Their HCF is:
(A) 20 (B) 30 (C) 40 (D) 60

53. The greatest number that divides 85 and 153, leaving remainders 5 and 9 respectively, is:
(A) 13 (B) 16 (C) 17 (D) 19

54. The product of two numbers is 2028 and their HCF is 13. The LCM of the numbers is:
(A) 156 (B) 169 (C) 144 (D) 132

55. The smallest number which when divided by 24, 36, and 48 leaves a remainder of 8 in each case is:
(A) 144 (B) 152 (C) 136 (D) 160

56. The ratio of two numbers is 5 : 6 and their LCM is 480. The numbers are:
(A) 50 and 60 (B) 60 and 72 (C) 80 and 96 (D) 90 and 108

57. The LCM of two numbers is 14 times their HCF. The sum of the LCM and HCF is 600. If one of the numbers is 280, the other is:
(A) 80 (B) 100 (C) 120 (D) 72

59. The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 23 and 1449 respectively. If one number is 161, the other is:
(A) 207 (B) 207 (repeated intentionally or replace) - let's set (A) 207, (B) 217, (C) 184, (D) 230. Actually 207 is answer. So options: (A) 207, (B) 217, (C) 184, (D) 230.

60. The least perfect square which is exactly divisible by 9, 15, and 20 is:
(A) 900 (B) 3600 (C) 1800 (D) 2025

61. Four bells toll at intervals of 10, 15, 20, and 30 seconds respectively. They toll together at 10:00 AM. At what time will they toll together again?
(A) 10:01 AM (B) 10:02 AM (C) 10:00:30 AM (D) 10:10 AM

62. The sum of two numbers is 216 and their HCF is 27. How many such pairs of numbers exist?
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4

63. A rectangular field 90 m by 60 m is to be paved with identical square tiles. The minimum number of tiles required is:
(A) 10 (B) 6 (C) 15 (D) 12

64. What is the LCM of 0.24, 0.36, and 0.48?
(A) 1.44 (B) 0.72 (C) 2.88 (D) 1.20

65. The smallest number which when divided by 7, 8, and 9 leaves a remainder of 4 in each case is:
(A) 504 (B) 508 (C) 500 (D) 512

66. The LCM of two numbers is 5 times their HCF. If the product of the numbers is 1620, then their HCF is:
(A) 18 (B) 15 (C) 20 (D) 12

67. Find the greatest number which divides 121, 226, and 259, leaving the same remainder in each case.
(A) 3 (B) 7 (C) 9 (D) 11

68. Two numbers are in the ratio 8 : 9. If their HCF is 6, their LCM is:
(A) 432 (B) 408 (C) 386 (D) 412

69. In a school, students can be arranged in rows of 12, 15, or 24, with 3 students left out each time. The minimum number of students is:
(A) 120 (B) 123 (C) 117 (D) 243

70. The HCF of 136, 170, and 255 is:
(A) 17 (B) 34 (C) 51 (D) 68

71. Two co-prime numbers have an LCM of 391. The numbers are:
(A) 13 and 31 (B) 17 and 23 (C) 7 and 53 (D) 11 and 35

72. The least number that must be subtracted from 1936 so that the result is exactly divisible by 9, 10, and 15 is:
(A) 46 (B) 36 (C) 56 (D) 16

73. Three machines start sounding alarms at intervals of 5 minutes, 8 minutes, and 12 minutes, respectively. If they all sound together at 9:00 AM, when will they next sound together?
(A) 11:00 AM (B) 10:00 AM (C) 10:20 AM (D) 11:40 AM

74. The smallest 4‑digit number exactly divisible by 18, 24, and 32 is:
(A) 1152 (B) 1248 (C) 1008 (D) 1296

75. The LCM of two numbers is 28 times their HCF. The sum of the LCM and HCF is 1740. If one number is 480, the other number is:
(A) 210 (B) 180 (C) 240 (D) 300
