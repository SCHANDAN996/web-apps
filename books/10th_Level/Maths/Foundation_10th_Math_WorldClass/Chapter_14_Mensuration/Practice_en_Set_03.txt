Set 3 — Mensuration MCQs (All Medium: 51–75)

51. A rectangle has area 96 cm² and one of its sides is 12 cm. A square has the same perimeter as this rectangle. The side of the square is
(A) 10 cm
(B) 12 cm
(C) 8 cm
(D) 14 cm

Answer: (A) 10 cm
Solution: Breadth of rectangle = Area / length = 96/12 = 8 cm. Perimeter = 2(12+8) = 40 cm. Perimeter of square = 40 cm → side = 40/4 = 10 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6

52. The area of a rhombus is 216 cm² and one diagonal is 24 cm. Its perimeter is
(A) 60 cm
(B) 50 cm
(C) 72 cm
(D) 48 cm

Answer: (A) 60 cm
Solution: Area = (1/2) d₁ d₂ → (1/2)×24×d₂ = 216 → 12 d₂ = 216 → d₂ = 18 cm. Diagonals bisect at right angles, so side = √((d₁/2)² + (d₂/2)²) = √(12²+9²) = √225 = 15 cm. Perimeter = 4×15 = 60 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8

53. The length and breadth of a rectangle are in the ratio 4 : 3. If its area is 300 cm², then its perimeter is
(A) 70 cm
(B) 60 cm
(C) 80 cm
(D) 50 cm

Answer: (A) 70 cm
Solution: Let length=4x, breadth=3x. Area=12x²=300 → x²=25 → x=5. Length=20 cm, breadth=15 cm. Perimeter=2(20+15)=70 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 6

54. A circular park of radius 21 m has a 7 m wide road all around it. The area of the road (π=22/7) is
(A) 1078 m²
(B) 1386 m²
(C) 1540 m²
(D) 616 m²

Answer: (A) 1078 m²
Solution: Outer radius = 21+7 = 28 m. Area of road = π(R²−r²) = (22/7)(28²−21²) = (22/7)(784−441) = (22/7)×343 = 22×49 = 1078 m².
Source: NCERT Class 7

55. The perimeter of a sector of a circle of radius 7 cm is 25 cm. The area of the sector is
(A) 38.5 cm²
(B) 77 cm²
(C) 19.25 cm²
(D) 154 cm²

Answer: (A) 38.5 cm²
Solution: Perimeter = 2r + arc = 14 + l = 25 → arc l = 11 cm. l = (θ/360)×2πr → 11 = (θ/360)×2×(22/7)×7 → 11 = (θ/360)×44 → θ/360 = 1/4 → θ=90°. Area = (θ/360)×πr² = (1/4)×(22/7)×49 = 154/4 = 38.5 cm².
Source: NCERT Class 10

56. A cuboid of dimensions 9 cm × 12 cm × 16 cm is melted and recast into a cube. The surface area of the cube is
(A) 864 cm²
(B) 1728 cm²
(C) 432 cm²
(D) 648 cm²

Answer: (A) 864 cm²
Solution: Volume of cuboid = 9×12×16 = 1728 cm³. This equals a³ for cube → a = ∛1728 = 12 cm. Surface area of cube = 6a² = 6×144 = 864 cm².
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1

57. The area of a trapezium is 180 cm² and its height is 12 cm. If one parallel side is 20 cm, the other parallel side is
(A) 10 cm
(B) 15 cm
(C) 25 cm
(D) 5 cm

Answer: (A) 10 cm
Solution: Area = (1/2)(a+b)h → 180 = (1/2)(20+b)×12 → 180 = 6(20+b) → 20+b=30 → b=10 cm.
Source: NCERT Class 8

58. The volume of a cylinder is 1540 cm³ and its radius is 7 cm (π=22/7). Its total surface area is
(A) 748 cm²
(B) 880 cm²
(C) 616 cm²
(D) 1232 cm²

Answer: (A) 748 cm²
Solution: Volume = πr²h → (22/7)×49×h = 1540 → 154h = 1540 → h = 10 cm. TSA = 2πr(r+h) = 2×(22/7)×7×(7+10) = 44×17 = 748 cm².

63. A solid metal sphere of radius 9 cm is melted and drawn into a wire of diameter 2 mm. The length of the wire (in metres) is (π=22/7)
(A) 972 m
(B) 243 m
(C) 486 m
(D) 108 m

Answer: (A) 972 m
Solution: Volume of sphere = (4/3)π×9³ = 972π cm³. Wire radius = 1 mm = 0.1 cm, cross‑section area = π×(0.1)² = 0.01π cm². Length = Volume / area = 972π / 0.01π = 97200 cm = 972 m.
Source: SSC CGL

65. The circumference of a circle is 88 cm. The area of its quadrant (one‑fourth of the circle) is (π=22/7)

66. A room is 8 m long, 6 m wide, and 5 m high. The cost of whitewashing its four walls and ceiling at ₹12 per m² is
(A) ₹2256
(B) ₹1880
(C) ₹2400

Answer: (A) ₹2256
Solution: Area of four walls = 2h(l+b) = 2×5×(8+6) = 140 m². Ceiling area = 8×6 = 48 m². Total area = 188 m². Cost = 188×12 = ₹2256.

67. A conical vessel of radius 6 cm and height 8 cm is filled with water. The water is poured into a cylindrical vessel of radius 4 cm. The height of water in the cylinder is
(A) 6 cm

Answer: (A) 6 cm
Solution: Volume of cone = (1/3)π×6²×8 = 96π cm³. Cylinder base area = π×4² = 16π cm². Height = Volume / base area = 96π / 16π = 6 cm.

71. The length, breadth, and height of a cuboid are in the ratio 5:4:2 and its volume is 2560 cm³. Its total surface area is

73. A triangle and a parallelogram share the same base and have equal area. If the altitude of the parallelogram is 8 cm, the altitude of the triangle is
(A) 16 cm
(B) 8 cm
(C) 4 cm
(D) 12 cm

Solution: Area of parallelogram = base × 8. Area of triangle = (1/2) × base × h. Equating: (1/2)bh = 8b → h/2 = 8 → h = 16 cm.

75. A cylindrical iron rod of length 14 cm and base radius 7 cm is melted and recast into small spheres of radius 3.5 cm. How many spheres are obtained? (π=22/7)
(A) 12
(B) 8
(C) 6
(D) 10

Answer: (A) 12
Solution: Cylinder volume = π×7²×14 = 686π cm³. Sphere volume = (4/3)π×(3.5)³ = (4/3)π×(7/2)³ = (4/3)π×343/8 = (343/6)π cm³. Number = 686π / (343/6)π = 686 × 6 / 343 = 2×6 = 12.
Source: SSC CGL
