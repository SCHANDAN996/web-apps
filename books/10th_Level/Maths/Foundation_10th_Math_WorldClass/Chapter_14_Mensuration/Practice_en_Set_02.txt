26. A wire is bent into a square of side 22 cm. If the same wire is re-bent into a circle, the area of the circle is (Take π = 22/7)
(A) 616 cm²
(B) 484 cm²
(C) 308 cm²
(D) 154 cm²

Answer: (A) 616 cm²
Solution: Perimeter of square = 4×22=88 cm = circumference of circle. 2πr = 88 → r = 88/(2×22/7) = 14 cm. Area = πr² = (22/7)×14² = 616 cm².
Source: SSC CGL Tier 1, 2019

Answer: (A) 200 cm²
Solution: Square perimeter = 4×15 = 60 cm = rectangle perimeter. 2(l+b)=60 → l+b=30 → 20+b=30 → b=10 cm. Area = l×b = 20×10 = 200 cm².
