126. A solid metallic sphere of radius 10 cm is melted and recast into a cone of radius equal to the sphere's radius. The height of the cone is
(A) 40 cm
(B) 20 cm
(C) 30 cm
(D) 10 cm

Answer: (A) 40 cm
Solution: Volume of sphere = (4/3)πr³ = (4/3)π×1000 = (4000/3)π cm³. Volume of cone = (1/3)πR²h = (1/3)π×100×h = (100π/3)h. Set equal: (100π/3)h = (4000π/3) → h = 40 cm.
Source: SSC CGL Tier 2

127. A cone, a hemisphere, and a cylinder stand on equal bases and have the same height. The ratio of their volumes is
(A) 1 : 2 : 3
(B) 2 : 1 : 3
(C) 1 : 1 : 1
(D) 1 : 3 : 2

Answer: (A) 1 : 2 : 3
Solution: For a given radius r and height r (common), cone volume = (1/3)πr³, hemisphere = (2/3)πr³, cylinder = πr³. Ratio = 1 : 2 : 3.
Source: NCERT Class 10

128. A hollow sphere of internal and external radii 3 cm and 6 cm respectively is melted to form a solid cylinder of height 7 cm. The radius of the cylinder is (π=22/7)
(A) 6 cm
(B) 3 cm
(C) 4 cm
(D) 5 cm

Answer: (A) 6 cm
Solution: Volume of hollow sphere = (4/3)π(R³ − r³) = (4/3)π(216 − 27) = (4/3)π×189 = 252π cm³. Volume of cylinder = πR² h = π R² ×7. Equate: 7πR² = 252π → R² = 36 → R = 6 cm.
Source: SSC CHSL

(A) 440 cm²

133. The perimeter of a sector of a circle of radius 10 cm is 40 cm. The area of the sector is
(A) 100 cm²
(B) 200 cm²
(C) 150 cm²
(D) 50 cm²

Answer: (A) 100 cm²
Solution: Sector perimeter = 2r + arc length = 20 + l = 40 → l = 20 cm. Arc length = (θ/360)×2πr = 20 → (θ/360)×2π×10 = 20 → (θ/360) = 1/π → θ = (360/π)°. Sector area = (θ/360)×πr² = (1/π)×π×100 = 100 cm². (Note: θ is not a nice number, but area simplifies.)
Source: NCERT Class 10 (application)
