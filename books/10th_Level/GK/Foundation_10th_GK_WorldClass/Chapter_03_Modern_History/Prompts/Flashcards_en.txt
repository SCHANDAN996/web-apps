🃏 Flashcards – Modern Indian History

Front: A company came to India to trade in spices but ended up ruling it for 200 years. Which company, and what was its first Indian factory?
Back: The English East India Company; first factory at Surat (1613).

Front: What sparked the Revolt of 1857, turning a military mutiny into a nationwide uprising?
Back: Rumour that rifle cartridges were greased with cow and pig fat, offensive to Hindu and Muslim soldiers.

Front: Who was the last Mughal emperor, and what role did he play in 1857?
Back: Bahadur Shah Zafar; proclaimed the symbolic leader of the revolt from Delhi.

Front: What is the “Doctrine of Lapse,” and which Governor-General used it like a legal weapon?
Back: Lord Dalhousie’s policy: if a ruler died without a natural male heir, his state would lapse to the Company. Annexed Satara, Jhansi, Nagpur, etc.

Front: “Back to the Vedas” and the Shuddhi movement – which reform society is this?
Back: Arya Samaj, founded by Swami Dayanand Saraswati in 1875.

Front: Raja Ram Mohan Roy fought against which inhuman practice, leading to its abolition in 1829?
Back: Sati (widow burning). He founded the Brahmo Samaj in 1828.

Front: Why was the Partition of Bengal in 1905 a turning point? Who ordered it?
Back: Lord Curzon partitioned Bengal, sparking the Swadeshi and Boycott Movement, shifting the national movement from prayers to protest.

Front: The Congress split into two groups in 1907. At which session, and what were the groups called?
Back: Surat session; split into Moderates (Naram Dal) and Extremists (Garam Dal).

Front: Mnemonic Trick: How can you remember the chronological order of Gandhi’s three mass movements?
Back: Non-Cooperation (1920), Civil Disobedience (1930), Quit India (1942) → NCQ (think of Gandhi filing a Non-Compliance Query to the British Empire).

Front: Mnemonic Trick: What’s a simple trick to recall the key social reformers in order?
Back: Rammohan Roy, Dayanand, Vivekananda, Ahmad Khan, Phule → RaDaViAFu — like a radio signal for reform.

Front: The slogan “Do or Die” belongs to which movement? Who gave it?
Back: Quit India Movement (1942), given by Mahatma Gandhi.

Front: Who threw a bomb in the Central Legislative Assembly in 1929, and why did he say it was “to make the deaf hear”?
Back: Bhagat Singh (along with Batukeshwar Dutt); to protest the repressive Public Safety Bill and Trade Disputes Act.

Front: The Indian National Army was formed by Subhas Chandra Bose, but where did he establish the Provisional Government of Free India?
Back: Singapore (1943). He gave the call “Dilli Chalo.”

Front: What was the Communal Award (1932), and why did it create a controversy?
Back: It granted separate electorates to the Depressed Classes. Gandhi opposed it, leading to the Poona Pact.

Front: Examiner’s Trap: “The Poona Pact of 1932 gave separate electorates to the Depressed Classes.” True or False?
Back: False. The Poona Pact replaced the separate electorate with reserved seats in joint electorates. The Communal Award had proposed the separate electorate.

Front: Examiner’s Trap: “The Subsidiary Alliance was used to annex Indian states without a natural heir.” What’s wrong with this statement?
Back: It’s a reverse causation trap. Annexation without a natural heir was the Doctrine of Lapse, not the Subsidiary Alliance. The Alliance was about stationing troops and controlling foreign affairs.

Front: Which Act first introduced separate electorates in India, and for which community?
Back: Indian Councils Act 1909 (Morley-Minto Reforms); separate electorate for Muslims.

Front: “Purna Swaraj” was declared at which Congress session, and what significant event was celebrated on 26 January 1930 as a result?
Back: Lahore session (1929); the first Independence Day was observed on 26 January 1930.
