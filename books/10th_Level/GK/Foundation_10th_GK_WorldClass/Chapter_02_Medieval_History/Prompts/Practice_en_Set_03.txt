Set 3: Medieval Indian History – MCQs (51–75)
Medium: 51–75

51. Consider the following statements about the Iqta system:

It was introduced by Iltutmish.

It was a hereditary assignment of land.

The iqtadar collected revenue and maintained troops.
Which of the above are correct?
(A) 1 and 2 only
(B) 1 and 3 only
(C) 2 and 3 only
(D) 1, 2 and 3
Answer: (B) 1 and 3 only
Solution: Iqta was a non-hereditary revenue assignment, transferable at the Sultan's will. Statement 2 is false.
Source: UPSC 2019

52. Who among the following Sultans is known for the policy of 'Blood and Iron'?
(A) Iltutmish
(B) Balban
(C) Alauddin Khilji
(D) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Answer: (B) Balban
Solution: Balban adopted a ruthless approach to crush rebellions and consolidate the Sultanate, famously termed the 'Blood and Iron' policy.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

53. Which ruler built the Tughlaqabad Fort?
(A) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(B) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(C) Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq
(D) Alauddin Khilji
Answer: (C) Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq
Solution: Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq, founder of the Tughlaq dynasty, constructed the massive Tughlaqabad Fort near Delhi.
Source: Railway NTPC 2021

54. Who is the author of 'Futuhat-i-Firoz Shahi'?
(A) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(B) Barani
(C) Amir Khusrau
(D) Ibn Battuta
Answer: (A) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
Solution: Firoz Shah Tughlaq himself wrote his autobiography 'Futuhat-i-Firoz Shahi'.
Source: Banking 2019

55. The practice of 'Dagh' (branding of horses) and 'Chehra' (descriptive roll of soldiers) was introduced by:
(A) Sher Shah Suri
(B) Akbar
(C) Alauddin Khilji
(D) Balban
Answer: (C) Alauddin Khilji
Solution: Alauddin introduced Dagh and Chehra to prevent false musters and maintain a disciplined standing army.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021

56. Which of the following rulers shifted the capital from Delhi to Daulatabad and back?
(A) Alauddin Khilji
(B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(C) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(D) Akbar
Answer: (B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
Solution: Muhammad bin Tughlaq ordered the mass transfer to Daulatabad (Devagiri), then reversed it after facing administrative problems.
Source: UPSC 2016

57. Who was the Sultan of Delhi when the Mongol invader Timur (Tamerlane) sacked Delhi in 1398?
(A) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq
(C) Nasir-ud-din Mahmud Tughlaq
(D) Sikandar Lodi
Answer: (C) Nasir-ud-din Mahmud Tughlaq
Solution: Timur invaded during the reign of the weak Tughlaq ruler Nasir-ud-din Mahmud.
Source: Railway 2018

58. Which of the following pairs (Battle – Participants) is INCORRECT?
(A) First Battle of Panipat – Babur and Ibrahim Lodi
(B) Battle of Khanwa – Babur and Rana Sanga
(C) Battle of Chanderi – Babur and Medini Rai
(D) Battle of Ghaghra – Babur and Sher Shah Suri
Answer: (D) Battle of Ghaghra – Babur and Sher Shah Suri
Solution: The Battle of Ghaghra (1529) was between Babur and the Afghan chiefs of Bihar and Bengal; Sher Shah was not involved.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

59. The Mansabdari system was a grading system based on:
(A) Land holdings
(B) Rank and military responsibility
(C) Religious merit
(D) Hereditary nobility
Answer: (B) Rank and military responsibility
Solution: Mansabdars were ranked by Zat (personal status) and Sawar (cavalry to maintain), responsible for a specific number of troops.
Source: UPSC 2017

60. 'Jaziya' was abolished as a tax on non-Muslims by:
(A) Alauddin Khilji
(B) Firoz Shah Tughlaq
(C) Akbar
(D) Jahangir
Answer: (C) Akbar
Source: SSC CGL 2016

61. Who built the city of Fatehpur Sikri?
(A) Babur
(B) Humayun
(C) Akbar
(D) Shah Jahan
Answer: (C) Akbar
Solution: Akbar built Fatehpur Sikri (1571–85) as his capital, later abandoned due to water shortage.
Source: Railway Group D 2020

62. The 'Ibadat Khana' at Fatehpur Sikri was built for:
(A) Royal court sessions
(B) Religious debates
(C) Public audience
(D) Music performances
Answer: (B) Religious debates
Solution: Ibadat Khana (House of Worship) was built in 1575 where Akbar hosted discussions among religious scholars of different faiths.
Source: UPSC 2018

63. Which of the following was a prominent musician in Akbar's court?
(A) Amir Khusrau
(B) Tansen
(C) Baiju Bawra
(D) Haridas Swami
Answer: (B) Tansen
Solution: Tansen, one of the Navratnas, was the greatest musician of Akbar's era, credited with ragas like Miyan ki Malhar.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

64. The 'Jama Masjid' at Delhi was built by:
(A) Akbar
(B) Jahangir
(C) Shah Jahan
(D) Aurangzeb
Answer: (C) Shah Jahan
Solution: Shah Jahan constructed the Jama Masjid (1650–56) in Shahjahanabad, one of the largest mosques in India.
Source: Banking 2020

65. Which Sikh Guru was executed by Aurangzeb?
(A) Guru Ram Das
(B) Guru Arjan Dev
(C) Guru Tegh Bahadur
(D) Guru Gobind Singh
Answer: (C) Guru Tegh Bahadur
Solution: Aurangzeb ordered the execution of Guru Tegh Bahadur in 1675 for refusing to convert to Islam.
Source: SSC CGL 2021

66. The Maratha king Shivaji was crowned at:
(A) Satara
(B) Raigarh
(C) Pune
(D) Kolhapur
Answer: (B) Raigarh
Solution: Shivaji's grand coronation took place in 1674 at Raigarh fort.
Examiner's Trap: Similar names – candidates often mark Satara, which later became the Maratha capital.
Source: UPSC 2015

67. 'Sardeshmukhi' was a tax of what percentage on the revenue?
(A) 5%
(B) 10%
(C) 15%
(D) 25%
Answer: (B) 10%
Solution: Sardeshmukhi was an additional 10% levy, while Chauth was 25%.
Source: Railway NTPC 2019

68. Which of the following foreign travellers visited the Vijayanagar empire?
(A) Ibn Battuta
(B) Nicolo Conti
(C) Al-Biruni
(D) Marco Polo
Answer: (B) Nicolo Conti
Solution: Nicolo Conti, an Italian traveller, visited Vijayanagar in the 15th century and left vivid accounts. Ibn Battuta visited under Muhammad bin Tughlaq.
Source: UPSC 2020

69. The 'Bahmani kingdom' was founded by:
(A) Mahmud Gawan
(B) Hasan Gangu
(C) Firuz Shah Bahmani
(D) Ahmad Shah Wali
Answer: (B) Hasan Gangu
Solution: Hasan Gangu, also known as Bahman Shah, founded the Bahmani Sultanate in 1347.
Source: SSC CGL 2018

70. The famous madrasa at Bidar was built by:
(A) Alauddin Bahman Shah
(B) Mahmud Gawan
(C) Firuz Shah Bahmani
(D) Yusuf Adil Shah
Answer: (B) Mahmud Gawan
Solution: Mahmud Gawan, the Persian wazir of the Bahmani kingdom, built a grand madrasa at Bidar in the 15th century.
Source: Banking 2017

71. Which of the following statements about the Third Battle of Panipat (1761) is correct?
(A) It was fought between the Marathas and the British
(B) It resulted in a decisive victory for the Marathas
(C) Ahmad Shah Abdali led the Afghan forces
(D) It took place during the reign of Aurangzeb
Answer: (C) Ahmad Shah Abdali led the Afghan forces
Solution: The battle was between the Marathas (Sadashivrao Bhau) and Ahmad Shah Abdali, in which the Marathas were defeated. It occurred long after Aurangzeb's death.
Examiner's Trap: Chronological confusion – Aurangzeb died in 1707; the battle was in 1761.
Source: SSC CGL 2017

72. Which Mughal emperor built the Shalimar Bagh in Kashmir?
(A) Akbar
(B) Jahangir
(C) Shah Jahan
(D) Aurangzeb
Answer: (B) Jahangir
Solution: Jahangir, known for his love of nature, built the Shalimar Bagh in Srinagar. Shah Jahan built the Shalimar Gardens in Lahore.
Source: UPSC 2018

73. Assertion (A): The token currency experiment of Muhammad bin Tughlaq failed. Reason (R): People began minting counterfeit copper and brass coins at home.
(A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation
(B) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation
(C) A is true, R is false
(D) A is false, R is true
Answer: (A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation
Solution: The experiment collapsed because the government could not prevent private minting of base metal coins that looked exactly like the official ones.
Source: UPSC 2021

74. Which of the following is correct about the Delhi Sultanate dynasties?
(A) Gulam – Khilji – Tughlaq – Sayyid – Lodi
(B) Gulam – Tughlaq – Khilji – Lodi – Sayyid
(C) Khilji – Gulam – Sayyid – Tughlaq – Lodi
(D) Tughlaq – Gulam – Khilji – Lodi – Sayyid
Answer: (A) Gulam – Khilji – Tughlaq – Sayyid – Lodi
Solution: The correct chronological order is: Mamluk (Gulam) 1206–90, Khilji 1290–1320, Tughlaq 1320–1414, Sayyid 1414–51, Lodi 1451–1526.
Source: SSC CGL 2022

75. Which of the following pairs (Book – Author) is NOT correctly matched?
(A) Tuzuk-i-Baburi – Babur
(B) Akbarnama – Abul Fazl
(C) Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri – Jahangir
(D) Rihla – Al-Biruni
Answer: (D) Rihla – Al-Biruni
Solution: Rihla was written by Ibn Battuta, not Al-Biruni. Al-Biruni wrote Kitab-ul-Hind.
Examiner's Trap: Similar names – Al-Biruni and Ibn Battuta are often confused.
Source: UPSC 2020
