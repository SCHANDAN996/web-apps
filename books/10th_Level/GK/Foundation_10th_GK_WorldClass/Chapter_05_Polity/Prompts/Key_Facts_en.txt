Fact	Description	Examiner’s Trap	Trap Type
Constituent Assembly President	Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the permanent President; Dr. B.R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee.	Stating Ambedkar as President of the Constituent Assembly.	Similar Name/Year Confusion
Presidential vs Vice‑Presidential Electoral College	President elected by elected members of Parliament + elected members of State Assemblies. VP elected by all MPs (elected + nominated).	Mixing the two colleges—asking who votes for whom, especially nominated members.	Almost Correct Statement
Preamble Amendment	Can be amended (Kesavananda Bharati, 1973) but basic structure cannot be destroyed. Amended only once via 42nd Amendment, 1976.	Saying Preamble cannot be amended at all.	Overgeneralization
Article 32 vs 226	Article 32 – only for Fundamental Rights in SC. Article 226 – for Fundamental Rights and other rights in HC.	Claiming Article 32 covers all legal rights.	Almost Correct Statement
Suspension of Fundamental Rights during National Emergency	Only Article 19 suspended automatically; Articles 20 and 21 remain enforceable.	Stating all Fundamental Rights are suspended.	Overgeneralization
Fundamental Duties Count	Originally 10 added by 42nd Amendment (1976); 11th added by 86th Amendment (2002).	Asking “How many duties were added by 42nd Amendment?” and giving 11 as option.	Chronological Confusion
Money Bill	Can only be introduced in Lok Sabha; Rajya Sabha can only recommend changes within 14 days; otherwise deemed passed.	Suggesting Rajya Sabha can reject a Money Bill.	Almost Correct Statement
Anglo-Indian Nomination	Discontinued by 104th Amendment, 2019.	Offering questions on current Anglo-Indian seats in Lok Sabha.	Chronological Confusion
DPSP Enforceability	Non-justiciable, but fundamental in governance.	Asking which court enforces DPSPs.	Negative Phrasing (court)
Writs	5 writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto.	Mixing up writs’ names or their purposes.	Similar Name Confusion
