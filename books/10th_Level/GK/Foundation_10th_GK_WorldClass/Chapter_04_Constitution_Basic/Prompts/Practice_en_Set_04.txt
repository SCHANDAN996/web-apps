📝 Practice MCQs – Set 4 (25 Questions: 76–100)

76. (Medium) The "Right to Vote" in India is a:
(a) Fundamental Right under Article 19
(b) Constitutional Right under Article 326
(c) Legal Right given by the Representation of the People Act
(d) Directive Principle under Article 40

Answer: (b) Constitutional Right under Article 326
Solution: Article 326 provides for elections to the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies based on adult suffrage. However, the right to vote is not a Fundamental Right; it is a constitutional right, and its regulation is provided by the Representation of the People Act, 1951.
Source: UPSC 2017
Trap type: Almost correct statement (option (a) seems plausible but is incorrect)

77. (Medium) Which one of the following amendments introduced the term "Socialist" into the Preamble?
(a) 24th Amendment, 1971
(b) 42nd Amendment, 1976
(c) 44th Amendment, 1978
(d) 38th Amendment, 1975

Answer: (b) 42nd Amendment, 1976
Solution: The 42nd Amendment added the words "Socialist", "Secular", and "Integrity" to the Preamble. It did not originally contain these words.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

78. (Medium) The Supreme Court held that Fundamental Rights cannot be amended to destroy the basic structure of the Constitution in which of the following cases?
(a) Golaknath case, 1967
(b) Kesavananda Bharati case, 1973
(c) Minerva Mills case, 1980
(d) Waman Rao case, 1981

Answer: (b) Kesavananda Bharati case, 1973
Solution: The Kesavananda Bharati case established the Basic Structure Doctrine, limiting Parliament's amending power. Golaknath had earlier held that FRs cannot be amended, but it was overturned. Minerva Mills later reinforced the Basic Structure.
Source: Railway NTPC 2021

79. (Medium) The writ of Certiorari is issued by a higher court to:
(a) Command a person to perform a public duty
(b) Quash an order of a lower court or tribunal
(c) Prevent a lower court from exceeding its jurisdiction
(d) Produce a detained person in court

Answer: (b) Quash an order of a lower court or tribunal
Solution: Certiorari is a corrective writ to quash an order passed by a lower court or quasi-judicial body. Prohibition prevents excess of jurisdiction, Mandamus commands performance, Habeas Corpus secures release from illegal detention.
Source: SSC CHSL 2019

80. (Medium) Which of the following is not a Constitutional Amendment Act related to the Fundamental Rights?
(a) 1st Amendment Act, 1951
(b) 24th Amendment Act, 1971
(c) 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
(d) 52nd Amendment Act, 1985

Answer: (d) 52nd Amendment Act, 1985
Solution: The 52nd Amendment dealt with anti-defection (10th Schedule). The 1st Amendment restricted FRs (Article 19), 24th gave Parliament power to amend FRs, 42nd added words to Preamble and curtailed FRs. So (d) is not related to FRs.
Source: Banking PO 2022
Trap type: Negative phrasing ("not") + mixing amendment purposes

81. (Medium) The concept of "due process of law" is a feature of which country's Constitution?
(a) United Kingdom
(b) United States of America
(c) India
(d) Ireland

Answer: (b) United States of America
Solution: In the US, "due process of law" gives courts the power to examine both procedural and substantive aspects of law. In India, Article 21 uses "procedure established by law" (taken from Japan), which originally only checked procedural legality but later expanded to include substantive fairness (Maneka Gandhi case).
Source: UPSC 2016

82. (Medium) Under the Indian Constitution, who is the custodian of the Contingency Fund of India?
(a) President
(b) Prime Minister
(c) Finance Minister
(d) Comptroller and Auditor General

Answer: (a) President
Solution: The Contingency Fund of India (Article 267) is held by the President on behalf of the Union, and withdrawals require the President’s authorization, later approved by Parliament.
Source: SSC CGL 2023 (adapted)

83. (Medium) The power to declare a law passed by a state legislature unconstitutional lies with:
(a) The Governor
(b) The President
(c) The Supreme Court and High Courts
(d) The Parliament

Answer: (c) The Supreme Court and High Courts
Solution: Judicial review is exercised by the Supreme Court and High Courts. They can declare a state law void if it violates the Constitution. The Governor can reserve a bill for President's consideration, but cannot declare it unconstitutional.
Source: SSC CHSL 2022

84. (Medium) Which of the following schedules was added by the 52nd Amendment Act, 1985?
(a) Ninth Schedule
(b) Tenth Schedule
(c) Eleventh Schedule
(d) Twelfth Schedule

Answer: (b) Tenth Schedule
Solution: The 52nd Amendment added the Tenth Schedule (anti-defection). The Ninth Schedule was added by the 1st Amendment, Eleventh and Twelfth by the 73rd and 74th respectively.
Source: Railway Group D 2021

85. (Medium) Which Article empowers Parliament to form new States and alter boundaries of existing States?
(a) Article 2
(b) Article 3
(c) Article 4
(d) Article 1

Answer: (b) Article 3
Solution: Article 3 deals with the formation of new States and alteration of areas, boundaries, or names of existing States. Article 2 admits new States into the Union.
Source: Banking PO 2020

86. (Medium) In India, the "Residuary Powers" (subjects not listed in any of the three lists) are vested with:
(a) The States
(b) The Union
(c) Both Union and States jointly
(d) The Supreme Court

Answer: (b) The Union
Solution: Article 248 vests residuary powers of legislation exclusively in Parliament. This is a feature borrowed from the Canadian Constitution.
Source: SSC CGL 2019

87. (Medium) The term "Secular" in the Preamble means:
(a) The State has its own religion
(b) The State is anti-religion
(c) The State treats all religions equally and has no official religion
(d) The State promotes a particular religion

Answer: (c) The State treats all religions equally and has no official religion
Solution: Secularism in the Indian context means equal respect and protection to all religions, with no state-sponsored religion.
Source: Railway NTPC 2019

88. (Medium) The "Doctrine of Colourable Legislation" is related to:
(a) Division of legislative powers between Centre and States
(b) Judicial review of constitutional amendments
(c) Interpretation of Fundamental Rights
(d) Procedure for impeachment of the President

Answer: (a) Division of legislative powers between Centre and States
Solution: The doctrine applies when a legislature passes a law that it is not competent to make by dressing it up as a law on a subject within its competence. It is thus related to the distribution of legislative powers.
Source: UPSC 2015

90. (Medium) Which Fundamental Right is considered as the "soul of the Constitution" by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar?
(a) Right to Equality
(b) Right to Life and Personal Liberty
(c) Right to Constitutional Remedies
(d) Right against Exploitation

Answer: (c) Right to Constitutional Remedies
Solution: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar referred to Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies) as the "heart and soul of the Constitution" because it makes all other rights enforceable.
Source: UPSC 2018

91. (Medium) Which of the following Constitutional Amendments deleted the Right to Property from the list of Fundamental Rights?
(a) 42nd Amendment, 1976
(b) 44th Amendment, 1978
(c) 52nd Amendment, 1985
(d) 86th Amendment, 2002

Answer: (b) 44th Amendment, 1978
Solution: The 44th Amendment Act removed Article 31 and placed the right to property under Article 300A as a legal right. The 42nd Amendment had earlier amended Article 31C, but not deleted property right.
Source: Railway Group D 2020

92. (Medium) The "Doctrine of Harmonious Construction" is applied by courts to:
(a) Interpret statutes to avoid conflict between two provisions
(b) Strike down unconstitutional laws
(c) Determine the validity of constitutional amendments
(d) Decide disputes between Centre and States

Answer: (a) Interpret statutes to avoid conflict between two provisions
Solution: Harmonious construction means that when two provisions of a statute or the Constitution appear to conflict, they should be interpreted in a way that both can coexist and no provision is rendered ineffective.
Source: SSC CGL 2022 (adapted)

93. (Medium) Which of the following statements regarding the Preamble is incorrect?
(a) The Preamble is non-justiciable.
(b) The Preamble can be amended under Article 368.
(c) The Preamble was amended by the 44th Amendment.
(d) The Preamble declares the source of the Constitution.

Answer: (c) The Preamble was amended by the 44th Amendment.
Solution: The Preamble was amended only once, by the 42nd Amendment in 1976, not the 44th. All other statements are correct.
Source: UPSC 2019
Trap type: Almost correct (44th is a major amendment, easily confused with 42nd)

94. (Medium) Which schedule of the Constitution deals with the administration of tribal areas in the states of Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram?
(a) Fifth Schedule
(b) Sixth Schedule
(c) Seventh Schedule
(d) Eighth Schedule

Answer: (b) Sixth Schedule
Solution: The Sixth Schedule provides for the administration of tribal areas in the four northeastern states of Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram. The Fifth Schedule deals with scheduled areas and tribes in other states.
Source: UPSC 2017

95. (Medium) "All persons are equally entitled to freedom of conscience and the right freely to profess, practise and propagate religion" is stated under which Article?
(a) Article 25
(b) Article 26
(c) Article 27
(d) Article 28

Answer: (a) Article 25
Solution: Article 25 guarantees the freedom of conscience and free profession, practice, and propagation of religion to all persons, subject to public order, morality, and health. Article 26 deals with the right to manage religious affairs, Article 27 prohibits forced taxation for religion, Article 28 relates to religious instruction in educational institutions.
Source: Banking PO 2021

96. (Medium) Which one of the following is a correct statement about the Directive Principles of State Policy?
(a) They are enforceable in a court of law.
(b) They are fundamental in the governance of the country.
(c) They override Fundamental Rights.
(d) They can only be implemented by the states.

Answer: (b) They are fundamental in the governance of the country.
Solution: Article 37 says DPSPs are fundamental in the governance of the country and it is the duty of the State to apply them in making laws, though they are non-justiciable. They cannot override FRs.
Source: SSC CGL 2023

97. (Medium) The Union Territory of Puducherry is mentioned under which Part of the Constitution?
(a) Part VII
(b) Part VIII
(c) Part IX
(d) Part X

Answer: (b) Part VIII
Solution: Part VIII (Articles 239-241) deals with Union Territories. Puducherry, Delhi, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, etc., fall under this Part.
Source: Railway NTPC 2022

98. (Medium) The "Minerva Mills case" (1980) is significant because it:
(a) Upheld the unlimited power of Parliament to amend the Constitution
(b) Struck down clauses that gave primacy to DPSP over Fundamental Rights
(c) Introduced the Basic Structure doctrine
(d) Upheld the validity of the 42nd Amendment entirely

Answer: (b) Struck down clauses that gave primacy to DPSP over Fundamental Rights
Solution: In Minerva Mills, the Supreme Court struck down the clauses 4 and 5 of Article 368 (inserted by 42nd Amendment) that sought to give absolute primacy to DPSP over FRs, holding that harmony between FR and DPSP is part of the basic structure.
Source: UPSC 2016

99. (Medium) The 61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1989, is famous for:
(a) Adding the Ninth Schedule
(b) Reducing the voting age from 21 to 18 years
(c) Introducing the anti-defection law
(d) Providing reservation for women in local bodies

Answer: (b) Reducing the voting age from 21 to 18 years
Solution: The 61st Amendment amended Article 326, lowering the voting age for Lok Sabha and state legislative assembly elections from 21 to 18 years.
Source: SSC CHSL 2022

100. (Medium) Assertion (A): The Indian Constitution is called a 'living document'.
Reason (R): The Constitution can be amended to adapt to changing circumstances.
Choose the correct option:
(a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(b) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(c) A is true, but R is false.
(d) A is false, but R is true.

Answer: (a) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
Solution: A living document is one that can be altered; the amendment procedure (Article 368) enables the Constitution to evolve over time, making R the proper explanation.
Source: UPSC 2015
