📋 Key Facts & Figures – Indian Constitution (Basic)

1. High-Yield Facts
Fact	Description	Examiner's Trap	Trap Type
Adoption vs Enforcement	Constitution adopted on 26 Nov 1949; enforced on 26 Jan 1950	"Constitution was enforced on 26 Nov 1949" – a statement often presented as true	Chronological Confusion
42nd Amendment (1976)	Added 'Socialist', 'Secular' to Preamble, Fundamental Duties, extended emergency provisions	"The original Preamble contained the words Socialist and Secular"	Almost True Statement (90% correct)
Kesavananda Bharati Case (1973)	Established the Basic Structure Doctrine – Parliament cannot destroy the core of the Constitution	"Parliament has unlimited power to amend the Constitution" – a common reverse logic	Reverse Causation
Article 32 – Heart and Soul	Right to Constitutional Remedies; directly enforceable by Supreme Court	Question: "Which Article is the 'Heart and Soul'?" Options may include Art. 19, 21, 32, 226	Similar Numbers/Articles
Fundamental Rights vs DPSP	FRs are justiciable (Part III), DPSP are non-justiciable (Part IV)	"Right to Work is a Fundamental Right" – actually a DPSP (Art. 41)	Negative Phrasing ('not', 'except')
Single Citizenship	India provides single citizenship; borrowed from UK	"India has dual citizenship like the USA" – common confusion with federal features	Similar Names/Concepts
Drafting Committee Chairperson	Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (Chairman), Drafting Committee formed on 29 Aug 1947	Confusing with President of Constituent Assembly (Dr. Rajendra Prasad)	Similar Names/Roles
12 Schedules (Current)	Original 8, now 12; 9th Schedule added by 1st Amendment (1951) for land reforms	Listing 11 or 13 schedules as correct – trick numbers	Almost True Statement
Preamble Keywords	Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic (in order)	Rearranging the order or omitting one	Chronological Confusion
Source of DPSP	Directive Principles borrowed from Irish Constitution	"DPSP borrowed from Britain/USA" – popular trap	Similar Names/Sources
