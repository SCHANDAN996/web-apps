Solution: From south to north, the ranges are Outer (Shivalik), Middle (Himachal), and Greater (Himadri).
Source: SSC CGL 2019

(b) Flowing through rift valleys with a sudden drop in gradient
Solution: West-flowing peninsular rivers like Narmada and Tapi flow through rift valleys and have steep gradients near the coast, forming estuaries rather than deltas.
Source: UPSC 2020

(b) 2 and 3 only
Solution: Statement 1 is incorrect: Luni drains into the Rann of Kutch, not the Arabian Sea. Statement 2 is correct (rain-shadow of Aravallis). Statement 3 is correct (barchans are crescent-shaped sand dunes).
Source: UPSC 2020 (Trap: Almost Correct Statement – Luni’s mouth)

(b) Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Madhya Pradesh
Solution: The Deccan Trap is a vast basaltic province covering Maharashtra, parts of Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, and northern Karnataka.
Source: SSC CHSL 2018

(b) Bhabar
Solution: Bhabar is the narrow belt of boulders and pebbles at the foothills where streams disappear underground.
Source: Railway NTPC 2020

(d) Mahi
Solution: Mahi flows through a faulted region but not a true rift valley like Narmada, Tapi, and Damodar (Damodar is often considered to flow through a rift valley).
Source: SSC CGL 2021

(c) Mahendragiri
Solution: Mahendragiri (1501 m) in Odisha is the highest peak of the Eastern Ghats. Anaimudi is in the Western Ghats, Doddabetta in the Nilgiris, Guru Shikhar in the Aravallis.
Source: UPSC 2019

(a) Minicoy and Maldives
Solution: The 8° Channel separates Minicoy (India) from Maldives. 9° Channel separates Minicoy from the main Lakshadweep group.
Source: SSC CGL 2017 (Trap: Similar Numbers – 8°, 9°, 10°)

(a) Pre-monsoon showers in Kerala and coastal Karnataka
Solution: Mango showers are pre-monsoon rains that help in the early ripening of mangoes along the western coastal region.
Source: Railway Group D 2019

(c) Laterite soil
Solution: Laterite soil forms under high rainfall and high temperature, with intense leaching leaving behind iron and aluminium oxides.
Source: IBPS SO 2020

(a) Indus, Satluj, Ganga, Brahmaputra
Solution: From west to east: Indus, Satluj (Indus tributary), Ganga, Brahmaputra.
Source: SSC CPO 2018

(a) Glacial deposits in the Kashmir valley
Solution: Karewas are lacustrine deposits of the Pleistocene age found in the Kashmir valley, important for saffron cultivation.
Source: UPSC 2016

(c) Wardha
Solution: Wardha is a tributary of the Godavari (via the Pranhita). Ghaghra, Son, and Ramganga are Ganga tributaries.
Source: SSC MTS 2020

(b) Confluence of the Ganga and Brahmaputra and their distributaries
Solution: The Sundarbans is the world’s largest delta formed by the deposition of sediment by the Ganga, Brahmaputra, and their numerous distributaries.
Source: UPSC 2017

(a) 1 only
Solution: Chilika is in Odisha (correct). Pulicat is on the Tamil Nadu–Andhra Pradesh border, not Kerala. Vembanad is in Kerala, not Karnataka.
Source: SSC CHSL 2021 (Trap: Similar Names/Locations – lakes swapped)

(b) Andaman Sea
Solution: Barren Island, an active volcano, lies in the Andaman Sea, east of the Andaman Islands.
Source: RRB NTPC 2021

(c) They have a steep gradient and flow through rift valleys
Solution: Narmada and Tapi flow through rift valleys with a steep slope towards the Arabian Sea, preventing delta formation and leading to estuaries.
Source: UPSC 2019

(b) New alluvium deposited by annual floods
Solution: Khadar is the fresh alluvium deposited by annual flooding, highly fertile and granular. Bhangar is old alluvium. Bhabar is boulders, Terai is marshy.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

(a) Sahyadri
Solution: The Western Ghats are locally known as Sahyadri in Maharashtra and Karnataka. Nilgiri, Annamalai, Cardamom are hill ranges within the Ghats.
Source: Banking PO 2021

(b) Coromandel coast
Solution: The retreating southwest monsoon (October–December) brings rainfall to the Coromandel coast (Tamil Nadu and southern Andhra Pradesh).
Source: SSC CGL 2019

(c) Their valleys are deep and U-shaped
Solution: Peninsular rivers have shallow, broad valleys (graded profiles) and are in their mature stage. Deep U-shaped valleys are characteristic of Himalayan rivers (glacial erosion). Thus, (c) is incorrect.
Source: UPSC 2018 (Trap: Almost Correct Statement – mixing Himalayan and Peninsular features)

(b) Coal deposits
Solution: The Gondwana rocks contain about 98% of India's coal reserves, particularly in the Damodar, Mahanadi, and Godavari valleys.
Source: SSC CGL 2020

(b) Acting as a rain-shadow for the southwest monsoon
Solution: The Aravallis lie parallel to the moisture-laden winds and do not cause orographic uplift; instead, the region to the west is a rain-shadow area, contributing to the Thar Desert.
Source: UPSC 2021

(a) Antecedent drainage
Solution: Mahi is a west-flowing river that has maintained its course despite tectonic uplift, making it an example of antecedent drainage.
Source: SSC JE 2019

(b) Continuing convergence of the Indian plate with the Eurasian plate
Solution: The Himalayas are still rising (~5 mm/year) due to the ongoing collision of the Indian plate with the Eurasian plate.
Source: UPSC 2017
