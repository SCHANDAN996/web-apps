6. Channel Degrees (8°, 9°, 10°)
"L8 M9 A10"

L8 = Lakshadweep (Minicoy) & Maldives separated by 8° Channel

M9 = Minicoy & main Lakshadweep separated by 9° Channel

A10 = Andaman & Nicobar separated by 10° Channel
(Think: "Late (8) Marriage (9) Anniversary (10)")

(Also includes Mahanadi, but for main ones, "Go Krishna, Go Mahanadi Kaveri" can be: "Guddu Krishna Mom's Cooked Kheer" – Godavari, Krishna, Mahanadi, Cauvery)

8. Deccan Trap ↔ Regur (Black Cotton Soil)
"Trap the Black Cotton in Deccan"

Deccan Trap (volcanic basalt) → Black (Regur) soil → Cotton crop.
(Visualize a mousetrap made of black cotton in the Deccan.)

9. Highest Peaks of Ghats (Anaimudi West, Mahendragiri East)
"Anni West, Maahi East"

Maahi = Mahendragiri (1501 m) in Eastern Ghats
(Think of two friends Anni living in the west and Maahi in the east.)

11. Ganga Right Bank Tributaries (Yamuna + Sons)
"Yamuna Chambal Sind Betwa Ken Son"

Yamuna, Chambal, Sind, Betwa, Ken, Son.
Mnemonic: "Yamuna's Cham-cham Sindoor Beti Ken se Son" (Yamuna's shiny sindoor, daughter named Ken, with son) – wild but memorable.

12. Largest & Oldest
"Ara Old, Siachen Long, Chili Lagoon, Sunder Delta"

Aravalli = oldest mountains

Siachen = longest glacier (76 km)

Chilika = largest saltwater lagoon

Sunderbans = largest delta

13. Rain-Shadow Areas (Thar & Eastern side of Western Ghats)
"Thar is Thirsty, Leeward is Lean" – Thar Desert lies in rain-shadow of Aravallis/Western Ghats; the Deccan side east of Western Ghats is dry.

14. Remembering which side of Ghats gets more rain
"West Wets, East Eats dry"

Western Ghats block southwest monsoon → heavy rain on windward (west) side.

Eastern Ghats are lower and fragmented → less rain, dry interior.

15. Coal Valleys of Gondwana
"Damodar Mahanadi Godavari: DaMaGo" – like 'damage' but 'Damagoo' for coal.

📉 Skip Strategy for Physical Geography of India

When they ask to arrange unknown minor peaks or obscure minor rivers by height/length: If you can't recall all four options (e.g., "Arrange the following peaks of Western Ghats from north to south: Vavul Mala, Chembra Peak, Banasura, etc.") – skip immediately. Such questions are low-yield time sinks.

If a question throws a confusing 'Match the Following' with channel degrees and islands but you mix up 8°, 9°, 10°: Even if you recall one, if the second match is shaky, skip. The chance of getting the full set right is low unless you have the mnemonic solidly.

Statement-based trap questions requiring multi-step reasoning (e.g., "Consider the following statements about Peninsular drainage and choose the correct ones using the code given below:"): If the first read doesn't give you at least two certain true/false assignments within 15 seconds, mark for review and move on. Never wrestle with a 45-second analytical puzzle unless you've finished the easy factual MCQs first.
